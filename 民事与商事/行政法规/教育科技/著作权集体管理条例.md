# 著作权集体管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2013-12-07  
**生效日期**：2005-03-01

> (2004年12月28日中华人民共和国国务院令第429号公布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2013年12月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范著作权集体管理活动，便于著作权人和与著作权有关的权利人(以下简称权利人)行使权利和使用者使用作品，根据《中华人民共和国著作权法》(以下简称著作权法)制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称著作权集体管理，是指著作权集体管理组织经权利人授权，集中行使权利人的有关权利并以自己的名义进行的下列活动： (一)与使用者订立著作权或者与著作权有关的权利许可使用合同(以下简称许可使用合同)； (二)向使用者收取使用费； (三)向权利人转付使用费； (四)进行涉及著作权或者与著作权有关的权利的诉讼、仲裁等。 第三条　本条例所称著作权集体管理组织，是指为权利人的利益依法设立，根据权利人授权、对权利人的著作权或者与著作权有关的权利进行集体管理的社会团体。 著作权集体管理组织应当依照有关社会团体登记管理的行政法规和本条例的规定进行登记并开展活动。 第四条　著作权法规定的表演权、放映权、广播权、出租权、信息网络传播权、复制权等权利人自己难以有效行使的权利，可以由著作权集体管理组织进行集体管理。 第五条　国务院著作权管理部门主管全国的著作权集体管理工作。 第六条　除依照本条例规定设立的著作权集体管理组织外，任何组织和个人不得从事著作权集体管理活动。 第二章　著作权集体管理组织的设立 第七条　依法享有著作权或者与著作权有关的权利的中国公民、法人或者其他组织，可以发起设立著作权集体管理组织。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范著作权集体管理活动，便于著作权人和与著作权有关的权利人(以下简称权利人)行使权利和使用者使用作品，根据《中华人民共和国著作权法》(以下简称著作权法)制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of regulating copyright collective management activities, facilitating copyright owners and copyright-related right holders (hereinafter referred to as right holders) to exercise their rights and users to use works, in accordance with the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Copyright Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称著作权集体管理，是指著作权集体管理组织经权利人授权，集中行使权利人的有关权利并以自己的名义进行的下列活动：  
(一)与使用者订立著作权或者与著作权有关的权利许可使用合同(以下简称许可使用合同)；  
(二)向使用者收取使用费；  
(三)向权利人转付使用费；  
(四)进行涉及著作权或者与著作权有关的权利的诉讼、仲裁等。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Regulations, copyright collective management means the following activities conducted by a copyright collective management organization, upon authorization by right holders, to centrally exercise the relevant rights of right holders in its own name:  
(1) concluding contracts with users for licensing the copyright or copyright-related rights (hereinafter referred to as licensing contracts);  
(2) collecting royalties from users;  
(3) distributing royalties to right holders;  
(4) engaging in lawsuits, arbitration, etc., involving copyright or copyright-related rights.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称著作权集体管理组织，是指为权利人的利益依法设立，根据权利人授权、对权利人的著作权或者与著作权有关的权利进行集体管理的社会团体。  
著作权集体管理组织应当依照有关社会团体登记管理的行政法规和本条例的规定进行登记并开展活动。

**Article 3** For the purpose of these Regulations, copyright collective management organization means a social organization established according to law for the interests of right holders, which, upon authorization by right holders, collectively manages the copyright or copyright-related rights of right holders.  
A copyright collective management organization shall undergo registration and conduct activities in accordance with the relevant administrative regulations on the registration and management of social organizations and the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　著作权法规定的表演权、放映权、广播权、出租权、信息网络传播权、复制权等权利人自己难以有效行使的权利，可以由著作权集体管理组织进行集体管理。

**Article 4** The rights provided for in the Copyright Law that are difficult for right holders to effectively exercise on their own, such as the right of performance, the right of projection, the right of broadcasting, the right of rental, the right of information network dissemination, and the right of reproduction, may be collectively managed by a copyright collective management organization.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院著作权管理部门主管全国的著作权集体管理工作。

**Article 5** The copyright administration department of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of copyright collective management nationwide.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　除依照本条例规定设立的著作权集体管理组织外，任何组织和个人不得从事著作权集体管理活动。

**Article 6** No organization or individual may engage in copyright collective management activities except for copyright collective management organizations established in accordance with these Regulations.

### 第二章　著作权集体管理组织的设立

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　依法享有著作权或者与著作权有关的权利的中国公民、法人或者其他组织，可以发起设立著作权集体管理组织。  
设立著作权集体管理组织，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)发起设立著作权集体管理组织的权利人不少于50人；  
(二)不与已经依法登记的著作权集体管理组织的业务范围交叉、重合；  
(三)能在全国范围代表相关权利人的利益；  
(四)有著作权集体管理组织的章程草案、使用费收取标准草案和向权利人转付使用费的办法(以下简称使用费转付办法)草案。

**Article 7** Chinese citizens, legal persons, or other organizations that enjoy copyright or copyright-related rights according to law may initiate the establishment of a copyright collective management organization.  
The establishment of a copyright collective management organization shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) the number of right holders initiating the establishment of the copyright collective management organization shall be not less than 50;  
(2) the business scope shall not overlap or coincide with that of any copyright collective management organization already registered according to law;  
(3) it shall be able to represent the interests of relevant right holders nationwide;  
(4) there shall be a draft charter of the copyright collective management organization, a draft standard for royalty collection, and a draft method for distributing royalties to right holders (hereinafter referred to as the royalty distribution method).

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　著作权集体管理组织章程应当载明下列事项：  
(一)名称、住所；  
(二)设立宗旨；  
(三)业务范围；  
(四)组织机构及其职权；  
(五)会员大会的最低人数；  
(六)理事会的职责及理事会负责人的条件和产生、罢免的程序；  
(七)管理费提取、使用办法；  
(八)会员加入、退出著作权集体管理组织的条件、程序；  
(九)章程的修改程序；  
(十)著作权集体管理组织终止的条件、程序和终止后资产的处理。

**Article 8** The charter of a copyright collective management organization shall specify the following:  
(1) name and domicile;  
(2) purpose of establishment;  
(3) business scope;  
(4) organizational structure and its functions and powers;  
(5) minimum number of members in the general meeting of members;  
(6) duties of the board of directors, and the qualifications, procedures for election and removal of the person in charge of the board;  
(7) methods for collecting and using administrative fees;  
(8) conditions and procedures for members to join or withdraw from the copyright collective management organization;  
(9) procedures for amending the charter;  
(10) conditions and procedures for termination of the copyright collective management organization and disposal of its assets after termination.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请设立著作权集体管理组织，应当向国务院著作权管理部门提交证明符合[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定的条件的材料。国务院著作权管理部门应当自收到材料之日起60日内，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。批准的，发给著作权集体管理许可证；不予批准的，应当说明理由。

**Article 9** An applicant for the establishment of a copyright collective management organization shall submit to the copyright administration department of the State Council materials proving that it meets the conditions provided for in Article 7 of these Regulations. The copyright administration department of the State Council shall, within 60 days from the date of receipt of the materials, make a decision on approval or disapproval. If approved, it shall issue a copyright collective management license; if not approved, it shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　申请人应当自国务院著作权管理部门发给著作权集体管理许可证之日起30日内，依照有关社会团体登记管理的行政法规到国务院民政部门办理登记手续。

**Article 10** The applicant shall, within 30 days from the date the copyright administration department of the State Council issues the copyright collective management license, go through the registration formalities with the civil affairs department of the State Council in accordance with the relevant administrative regulations on the registration and management of social organizations.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　依法登记的著作权集体管理组织，应当自国务院民政部门发给登记证书之日起30日内，将其登记证书副本报国务院著作权管理部门备案；国务院著作权管理部门应当将报备的登记证书副本以及著作权集体管理组织章程、使用费收取标准、使用费转付办法予以公告。

**Article 11** A copyright collective management organization that has been registered according to law shall, within 30 days from the date the civil affairs department of the State Council issues the registration certificate, submit a duplicate of the registration certificate to the copyright administration department of the State Council for archival filing; the copyright administration department of the State Council shall announce the filed duplicate of the registration certificate, as well as the charter of the copyright collective management organization, the royalty collection standard, and the royalty distribution method.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　著作权集体管理组织设立分支机构，应当经国务院著作权管理部门批准，并依照有关社会团体登记管理的行政法规到国务院民政部门办理登记手续。经依法登记的，应当将分支机构的登记证书副本报国务院著作权管理部门备案，由国务院著作权管理部门予以公告。

**Article 12** The establishment of a branch by a copyright collective management organization shall be subject to the approval of the copyright administration department of the State Council, and the organization shall go through the registration formalities with the civil affairs department of the State Council in accordance with the relevant administrative regulations on the registration and management of social organizations. If registered according to law, the duplicate of the registration certificate of the branch shall be submitted to the copyright administration department of the State Council for archival filing, and shall be announced by the copyright administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　著作权集体管理组织应当根据下列因素制定使用费收取标准：  
(一)使用作品、录音录像制品等的时间、方式和地域范围；  
(二)权利的种类；  
(三)订立许可使用合同和收取使用费工作的繁简程度。

**Article 13** A copyright collective management organization shall establish royalty collection standards based on the following factors:  
(1) the time, manner, and geographical scope of the use of works, sound recordings, video recordings, etc.;  
(2) the types of rights;  
(3) the complexity of concluding licensing contracts and collecting royalties.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　著作权集体管理组织应当根据权利人的作品或者录音录像制品等使用情况制定使用费转付办法。

**Article 14** A copyright collective management organization shall establish a method for distributing royalties based on the usage of right holders' works, sound recordings, video recordings, etc.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　著作权集体管理组织修改章程，应当依法经国务院民政部门核准后，由国务院著作权管理部门予以公告。

**Article 15** Any amendment to the charter of a copyright collective management organization shall be approved by the civil affairs department of the State Council according to law, and thereafter announced by the copyright administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　著作权集体管理组织被依法撤销登记的，自被撤销登记之日起不得再进行著作权集体管理业务活动。

**Article 16** If a copyright collective management organization has its registration revoked according to law, it shall not engage in copyright collective management business activities from the date of revocation of registration.

### 第三章　著作权集体管理组织的机构

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　著作权集体管理组织会员大会(以下简称会员大会)为著作权集体管理组织的权力机构。  
会员大会由理事会依照本条例规定负责召集。理事会应当于会员大会召开60日以前将会议的时间、地点和拟审议事项予以公告；出席会员大会的会员，应当于会议召开30日以前报名。报名出席会员大会的会员少于章程规定的最低人数时，理事会应当将会员大会报名情况予以公告，会员可以于会议召开5日以前补充报名，并由全部报名出席会员大会的会员举行会员大会。  
会员大会行使下列职权：  
(一)制定和修改章程；  
(二)制定和修改使用费收取标准；  
(三)制定和修改使用费转付办法；  
(四)选举和罢免理事；  
(五)审议批准理事会的工作报告和财务报告；  
(六)制定内部管理制度；  
(七)决定使用费转付方案和著作权集体管理组织提取管理费的比例；  
(八)决定其他重大事项。  
会员大会每年召开一次；经10%以上会员或者理事会提议，可以召开临时会员大会。会员大会作出决定，应当经出席会议的会员过半数表决通过。

**Article 17** The general meeting of members of a copyright collective management organization (hereinafter referred to as the general meeting of members) shall be the organ of power of the copyright collective management organization.  
The general meeting of members shall be convened by the board of directors in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations. The board of directors shall, 60 days prior to the convening of the general meeting of members, announce the time, place, and matters to be considered; members attending the general meeting of members shall register 30 days prior to the convening of the meeting. Where the number of members registered to attend the general meeting of members is less than the minimum number prescribed in the charter, the board of directors shall announce the registration status of the general meeting of members, and members may make supplementary registrations 5 days prior to the convening of the meeting, and the general meeting of members shall be held with all registered members attending. The general meeting of members shall exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) to formulate and amend the charter;  
(2) to formulate and amend royalty collection standards;  
(3) to formulate and amend the royalty distribution method;  
(4) to elect and remove directors;  
(5) to examine and approve the work reports and financial reports of the board of directors;  
(6) to formulate internal management systems;  
(7) to decide on royalty distribution plans and the proportion of administrative fees to be deducted by the copyright collective management organization;  
(8) to decide on other major matters.  
The general meeting of members shall be held once a year; an interim general meeting of members may be convened upon the proposal of more than 10% of the members or the board of directors. Decisions of the general meeting of members shall be adopted by a majority vote of more than half of the members present at the meeting.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　著作权集体管理组织设立理事会，对会员大会负责，执行会员大会决定。理事会成员不得少于9人。  
理事会任期为4年，任期届满应当进行换届选举。因特殊情况可以提前或者延期换届，但是换届延期不得超过1年。

**Article 18** A copyright collective management organization shall establish a board of directors, which shall be responsible to the general meeting of members and implement the decisions of the general meeting of members. The board of directors shall have not less than 9 members.  
The term of office of the board of directors shall be 4 years, and reelection shall be conducted upon expiration of the term. Under special circumstances, the reelection may be advanced or postponed, but the postponement shall not exceed one year.

### 第四章　著作权集体管理活动

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　权利人可以与著作权集体管理组织以书面形式订立著作权集体管理合同，授权该组织对其依法享有的著作权或者与著作权有关的权利进行管理。权利人符合章程规定加入条件的，著作权集体管理组织应当与其订立著作权集体管理合同，不得拒绝。  
权利人与著作权集体管理组织订立著作权集体管理合同并按照章程规定履行相应手续后，即成为该著作权集体管理组织的会员。

**Article 19** A right holder may conclude a copyright collective management contract in writing with a copyright collective management organization, authorizing the organization to manage the copyright or copyright-related rights that the right holder enjoys according to law. If the right holder meets the conditions for joining as prescribed in the charter, the copyright collective management organization shall conclude a copyright collective management contract with the right holder and shall not refuse.  
A right holder shall become a member of the copyright collective management organization after concluding a copyright collective management contract with the organization and fulfilling the corresponding procedures in accordance with the charter.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　权利人与著作权集体管理组织订立著作权集体管理合同后，不得在合同约定期限内自己行使或者许可他人行使合同约定的由著作权集体管理组织行使的权利。

**Article 20** After concluding a copyright collective management contract with a copyright collective management organization, a right holder shall not, within the term agreed in the contract, exercise the rights that are agreed in the contract to be exercised by the copyright collective management organization, or license others to exercise such rights.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　权利人可以依照章程规定的程序，退出著作权集体管理组织，终止著作权集体管理合同。但是，著作权集体管理组织已经与他人订立许可使用合同的，该合同在期限届满前继续有效；该合同有效期内，权利人有权获得相应的使用费并可以查阅有关业务材料。

**Article 21** A right holder may, in accordance with the procedures prescribed in the articles of association, withdraw from a copyright collective management organization and terminate the copyright collective management contract. However, where the copyright collective management organization has already concluded a license contract with another person, the contract shall remain valid until the expiry of its term; during the term of the contract, the right holder is entitled to obtain the corresponding royalties and may consult relevant business materials.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　外国人、无国籍人可以通过与中国的著作权集体管理组织订立相互代表协议的境外同类组织，授权中国的著作权集体管理组织管理其依法在中国境内享有的著作权或者与著作权有关的权利。  
前款所称相互代表协议，是指中国的著作权集体管理组织与境外的同类组织相互授权对方在其所在国家或者地区进行集体管理活动的协议。  
著作权集体管理组织与境外同类组织订立的相互代表协议应当报国务院著作权管理部门备案，由国务院著作权管理部门予以公告。

**Article 22** Foreigners and stateless persons may, through an overseas similar organization that has concluded a mutual representation agreement with a Chinese copyright collective management organization, authorize the Chinese copyright collective management organization to administer their copyright or copyright-related rights that they enjoy in China according to law.  
The mutual representation agreement referred to in the preceding paragraph means an agreement under which a Chinese copyright collective management organization and an overseas similar organization mutually authorize each other to conduct collective administration activities in their respective countries or regions.  
The mutual representation agreement concluded between a copyright collective management organization and an overseas similar organization shall be submitted to the copyright administrative department of the State Council for recordation, and shall be announced by the copyright administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　著作权集体管理组织许可他人使用其管理的作品、录音录像制品等，应当与使用者以书面形式订立许可使用合同。  
著作权集体管理组织不得与使用者订立专有许可使用合同。  
使用者以合理的条件要求与著作权集体管理组织订立许可使用合同，著作权集体管理组织不得拒绝。  
许可使用合同的期限不得超过2年；合同期限届满可以续订。

**Article 23** A copyright collective management organization that licenses another person to use the works, sound recordings, video recordings, etc. under its administration shall conclude a license contract with the user in written form.  
A copyright collective management organization shall not conclude an exclusive license contract with a user.  
Where a user requests, on reasonable terms, to conclude a license contract with a copyright collective management organization, the copyright collective management organization shall not refuse.  
The term of a license contract shall not exceed two years; the contract may be renewed upon expiration of its term.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　著作权集体管理组织应当建立权利信息查询系统，供权利人和使用者查询。权利信息查询系统应当包括著作权集体管理组织管理的权利种类和作品、录音录像制品等的名称、权利人姓名或者名称、授权管理的期限。  
权利人和使用者对著作权集体管理组织管理的权利的信息进行咨询时，该组织应当予以答复。

**Article 24** A copyright collective management organization shall establish a right information inquiry system for right holders and users to inquire. The right information inquiry system shall include the types of rights administered by the copyright collective management organization, the names of works, sound recordings, video recordings, etc., the names of right holders, and the term of authorized administration.  
When a right holder or user consults the copyright collective management organization about the information on the rights administered by it, the organization shall respond.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　除[著作权法第二十三条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-23)、第三十三条第二款、第四十条第三款、第四十三条第二款和第四十四条规定应当支付的使用费外，著作权集体管理组织应当根据国务院著作权管理部门公告的使用费收取标准，与使用者约定收取使用费的具体数额。

**Article 25** Except for the royalties payable as provided in Articles 23, paragraph 2 of Article 33, paragraph 3 of Article 40, paragraph 2 of Article 43, and Article 44 of the Copyright Law, a copyright collective management organization shall, in accordance with the standards for collection of royalties announced by the copyright administrative department of the State Council, agree with users on the specific amount of royalties to be collected.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　两个或者两个以上著作权集体管理组织就同一使用方式向同一使用者收取使用费，可以事先协商确定由其中一个著作权集体管理组织统一收取。统一收取的使用费在有关著作权集体管理组织之间经协商分配。

**Article 26** Where two or more copyright collective management organizations collect royalties from the same user for the same method of use, they may, through prior negotiation, determine that one of them shall collect the royalties uniformly. The uniformly collected royalties shall be distributed among the relevant copyright collective management organizations through negotiation.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　使用者向著作权集体管理组织支付使用费时，应当提供其使用的作品、录音录像制品等的名称、权利人姓名或者名称和使用的方式、数量、时间等有关使用情况；许可使用合同另有约定的除外。  
使用者提供的有关使用情况涉及该使用者商业秘密的，著作权集体管理组织负有保密义务。

**Article 27** When a user pays royalties to a copyright collective management organization, the user shall provide relevant information on the use, including the names of the works, sound recordings, video recordings, etc. used, the names of the right holders, and the methods, quantity, and time of use, unless otherwise agreed in the license contract.  
Where the information on use provided by the user involves the user's trade secrets, the copyright collective management organization shall have an obligation of confidentiality.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　著作权集体管理组织可以从收取的使用费中提取一定比例作为管理费，用于维持其正常的业务活动。  
著作权集体管理组织提取管理费的比例应当随着使用费收入的增加而逐步降低。

**Article 28** A copyright collective management organization may deduct a certain proportion from the royalties collected as administrative fees to maintain its normal business activities.  
The proportion of administrative fees deducted by a copyright collective management organization shall gradually decrease with the increase in royalty income.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　著作权集体管理组织收取的使用费，在提取管理费后，应当全部转付给权利人，不得挪作他用。  
著作权集体管理组织转付使用费，应当编制使用费转付记录。使用费转付记录应当载明使用费总额、管理费数额、权利人姓名或者名称、作品或者录音录像制品等的名称、有关使用情况、向各权利人转付使用费的具体数额等事项，并应当保存10年以上。

**Article 29** After deducting administrative fees, a copyright collective management organization shall transfer all the royalties collected to the right holders, and shall not misappropriate them for other purposes.  
When transferring royalties, a copyright collective management organization shall prepare a record of royalty transfers. The record of royalty transfers shall contain the total amount of royalties, the amount of administrative fees, the names of the right holders, the names of the works or sound recordings, video recordings, etc., relevant information on use, the specific amounts of royalties transferred to each right holder, and other items, and shall be kept for more than ten years.

### 第五章　对著作权集体管理组织的监督

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　著作权集体管理组织应当依法建立财务、会计制度和资产管理制度，并按照国家有关规定设置会计账簿。

**Article 30** A copyright collective management organization shall, according to law, establish financial, accounting, and asset management systems, and shall set up accounting books in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　著作权集体管理组织的资产使用和财务管理受国务院著作权管理部门和民政部门的监督。  
著作权集体管理组织应当在每个会计年度结束时制作财务会计报告，委托会计师事务所依法进行审计，并公布审计结果。

**Article 31** The use of assets and financial management of a copyright collective management organization shall be subject to the supervision of the copyright administrative department of the State Council and the civil affairs department.  
A copyright collective management organization shall, at the end of each fiscal year, prepare a financial accounting report, entrust an accounting firm to conduct an audit according to law, and publish the audit results.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　著作权集体管理组织应当对下列事项进行记录，供权利人和使用者查阅：  
(一)作品许可使用情况；  
(二)使用费收取和转付情况；  
(三)管理费提取和使用情况。  
权利人有权查阅、复制著作权集体管理组织的财务报告、工作报告和其他业务材料；著作权集体管理组织应当提供便利。

**Article 32** A copyright collective management organization shall keep records of the following matters for inspection by right holders and users:  
(1) the licensing of works;  
(2) the collection and transfer of royalties;  
(3) the deduction and use of administrative fees.  
A right holder has the right to consult and reproduce the financial reports, work reports, and other business materials of the copyright collective management organization; the copyright collective management organization shall provide convenience.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　权利人认为著作权集体管理组织有下列情形之一的，可以向国务院著作权管理部门检举：  
(一)权利人符合章程规定的加入条件要求加入著作权集体管理组织，或者会员依照章程规定的程序要求退出著作权集体管理组织，著作权集体管理组织拒绝的；  
(二)著作权集体管理组织不按照规定收取、转付使用费，或者不按照规定提取、使用管理费的；  
(三)权利人要求查阅[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)规定的记录、业务材料，著作权集体管理组织拒绝提供的。

**Article 33** Where a right holder considers that a copyright collective management organization has any of the following circumstances, the right holder may report to the copyright administrative department of the State Council:  
(1) the right holder meets the conditions for admission prescribed in the articles of association and requests to join the copyright collective management organization, or a member requests to withdraw from the copyright collective management organization in accordance with the procedures prescribed in the articles of association, and the copyright collective management organization refuses;  
(2) the copyright collective management organization fails to collect or transfer royalties in accordance with the provisions, or fails to deduct or use administrative fees in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) the right holder requests to consult the records and business materials provided for in Article 32 of these Regulations, and the copyright collective management organization refuses to provide them.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　使用者认为著作权集体管理组织有下列情形之一的，可以向国务院著作权管理部门检举：  
(一)著作权集体管理组织违反[本条例第二十三条](#art-23)规定拒绝与使用者订立许可使用合同的；  
(二)著作权集体管理组织未根据公告的使用费收取标准约定收取使用费的具体数额的；  
(三)使用者要求查阅[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)规定的记录，著作权集体管理组织拒绝提供的。

**Article 34** Where a user considers that a copyright collective management organization has any of the following circumstances, the user may report to the copyright administrative department of the State Council:  
(1) the copyright collective management organization, in violation of Article 23 of these Regulations, refuses to conclude a license contract with the user;  
(2) the copyright collective management organization fails to agree on the specific amount of royalties to be collected in accordance with the announced standards for collection of royalties;  
(3) the user requests to inspect the records provided for in Article 32 of these Regulations, and the copyright collective management organization refuses to provide them.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　权利人和使用者以外的公民、法人或者其他组织认为著作权集体管理组织有违反本条例规定的行为的，可以向国务院著作权管理部门举报。

**Article 35** Citizens, legal persons, or other organizations other than right holders and users who consider that a copyright collective management organization has violated the provisions of these Regulations may report to the copyright administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国务院著作权管理部门应当自接到检举、举报之日起60日内对检举、举报事项进行调查并依法处理。

**Article 36** The copyright administrative department of the State Council shall, within 60 days from the date of receiving a report or complaint, investigate the reported or complained matters and handle them according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国务院著作权管理部门可以采取下列方式对著作权集体管理组织进行监督，并应当对监督活动作出记录：  
(一)检查著作权集体管理组织的业务活动是否符合本条例及其章程的规定；  
(二)核查著作权集体管理组织的会计账簿、年度预算和决算报告及其他有关业务材料；  
(三)派员列席著作权集体管理组织的会员大会、理事会等重要会议。

**Article 37** The copyright administrative department of the State Council may supervise a copyright collective management organization by the following methods, and shall make records of the supervision activities:  
(1) inspecting whether the business activities of the copyright collective management organization comply with the provisions of these Regulations and its articles of association;  
(2) examining the accounting books, annual budget and final accounts reports, and other relevant business materials of the copyright collective management organization;  
(3) sending personnel to attend as non-voting participants important meetings such as the general assembly of members and the board of directors of the copyright collective management organization.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　著作权集体管理组织应当依法接受国务院民政部门和其他有关部门的监督。

**Article 38** A copyright collective management organization shall, according to law, accept the supervision of the civil affairs department of the State Council and other relevant departments.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　著作权集体管理组织有下列情形之一的，由国务院著作权管理部门责令限期改正：  
(一)违反[本条例第二十二条](#art-22)规定，未将与境外同类组织订立的相互代表协议报国务院著作权管理部门备案的；  
(二)违反[本条例第二十四条](#art-24)规定，未建立权利信息查询系统的；  
(三)未根据公告的使用费收取标准约定收取使用费的具体数额的。  
著作权集体管理组织超出业务范围管理权利人的权利的，由国务院著作权管理部门责令限期改正，其与使用者订立的许可使用合同无效；给权利人、使用者造成损害的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 39** Where a copyright collective management organization has any of the following circumstances, the copyright administrative department of the State Council shall order it to make corrections within a specified period:  
(1) in violation of Article 22 of these Regulations, it fails to submit the mutual representation agreement concluded with an overseas similar organization to the copyright administrative department of the State Council for recordation;  
(2) in violation of Article 24 of these Regulations, it fails to establish a right information inquiry system;  
(3) it fails to agree on the specific amount of royalties to be collected in accordance with the announced standards for collection of royalties.  
Where a copyright collective management organization administers the rights of right holders beyond its business scope, the copyright administrative department of the State Council shall order it to make corrections within a specified period, and the license contracts concluded by it with users shall be void; if it causes damage to right holders or users, it shall bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　著作权集体管理组织有下列情形之一的，由国务院著作权管理部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令会员大会或者理事会根据本条例规定的权限罢免或者解聘直接负责的主管人员：  
(一)违反[本条例第十九条](#art-19)规定拒绝与权利人订立著作权集体管理合同的，或者违反[本条例第二十一条](#art-21)的规定拒绝会员退出该组织的要求的；  
(二)违反[本条例第二十三条](#art-23)规定，拒绝与使用者订立许可使用合同的；  
(三)违反[本条例第二十八条](#art-28)规定提取管理费的；  
(四)违反[本条例第二十九条](#art-29)规定转付使用费的；  
(五)拒绝提供或者提供虚假的会计账簿、年度预算和决算报告或者其他有关业务材料的。

**Article 40** Where a copyright collective management organization has any of the following circumstances, the copyright administrative department of the State Council shall order it to make corrections within a specified period; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, it shall order the general assembly of members or the board of directors to remove or dismiss the directly responsible persons in charge in accordance with the powers provided in these Regulations:  
(1) in violation of Article 19 of these Regulations, it refuses to conclude a copyright collective management contract with a right holder, or in violation of Article 21 of these Regulations, it refuses a member's request to withdraw from the organization;  
(2) in violation of Article 23 of these Regulations, it refuses to conclude a license contract with a user;  
(3) it deducts administrative fees in violation of Article 28 of these Regulations;  
(4) it transfers royalties in violation of Article 29 of these Regulations;  
(5) it refuses to provide or provides false accounting books, annual budget and final accounts reports, or other relevant business materials.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　著作权集体管理组织自国务院民政部门发给登记证书之日起超过6个月无正当理由未开展著作权集体管理活动，或者连续中止著作权集体管理活动6个月以上的，由国务院著作权管理部门吊销其著作权集体管理许可证，并由国务院民政部门撤销登记。

**Article 41** Where a copyright collective management organization fails to carry out copyright collective management activities for more than six months without justifiable reasons as of the date the civil affairs department of the State Council issues the registration certificate, or suspends its copyright collective management activities for more than six consecutive months, the copyright administration department of the State Council shall revoke its copyright collective management license, and the civil affairs department of the State Council shall cancel its registration.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　著作权集体管理组织从事营利性经营活动的，由工商行政管理部门依法予以取缔，没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 42** Where a copyright collective management organization engages in for-profit business activities, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall, according to law, ban it and confiscate its illegal gains; if the act constitutes a crime, the criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反[本条例第二十七条](#art-27)的规定，使用者能够提供有关使用情况而拒绝提供，或者在提供有关使用情况时弄虚作假的，由国务院著作权管理部门责令改正；著作权集体管理组织可以中止许可使用合同。

**Article 43** Where a user, in violation of the provisions of Article 27 of these Regulations, is able to provide information on the use but refuses to do so, or makes false reports when providing such information, the copyright administration department of the State Council shall order it to make corrections; the copyright collective management organization may suspend the license contract.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　擅自设立著作权集体管理组织或者分支机构，或者擅自从事著作权集体管理活动的，由国务院著作权管理部门或者民政部门依照职责分工予以取缔，没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 44** Where a copyright collective management organization or branch is established without authorization, or where a person engages in copyright collective management activities without authorization, the copyright administration department of the State Council or the civil affairs department shall, according to their respective functions and duties, ban the same and confiscate illegal gains; if the act constitutes a crime, the criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　依照本条例规定从事著作权集体管理组织审批和监督工作的国家行政机关工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 45** Where any functionary of a state administrative organ who, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, is engaged in the examination and approval and supervision of copyright collective management organizations, neglects his duty, abuses his power, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, if the act constitutes a crime, the criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本条例施行前已经设立的著作权集体管理组织，应当自本条例生效之日起3个月内，将其章程、使用费收取标准、使用费转付办法及其他有关材料报国务院著作权管理部门审核，并将其与境外同类组织订立的相互代表协议报国务院著作权管理部门备案。

**Article 46** Any copyright collective management organization that has been established before the coming into force of these Regulations shall, within three months from the date on which these Regulations take effect, submit its articles of association, standards for collection of royalties, methods for distribution of royalties, and other relevant materials to the copyright administration department of the State Council for examination, and shall report the mutual representation agreements concluded with similar organizations outside China to the copyright administration department of the State Council for record.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　依照[著作权法第二十三条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-23)、第三十三条第二款、第四十条第三款的规定使用他人作品，未能依照[《中华人民共和国著作权法实施条例》第三十二条](../../../民事与商事/行政法规/教育科技/中华人民共和国著作权法实施条例.md#art-32)的规定向权利人支付使用费的，应当将使用费连同邮资以及使用作品的有关情况送交管理相关权利的著作权集体管理组织，由该著作权集体管理组织将使用费转付给权利人。  
负责转付使用费的著作权集体管理组织应当建立作品使用情况查询系统，供权利人、使用者查询。  
负责转付使用费的著作权集体管理组织可以从其收到的使用费中提取管理费，管理费按照会员大会决定的该集体管理组织管理费的比例减半提取。除管理费外，该著作权集体管理组织不得从其收到的使用费中提取其他任何费用。

**Article 47** Where a person uses another's work in accordance with the provisions of Article 23, paragraph 2 of Article 33, and paragraph 3 of Article 40 of the Copyright Law, but fails to pay royalties to the right holder in accordance with the provisions of Article 32 of the Regulations for the Implementation of the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China, he shall deliver the royalties together with postage and the relevant information on the use of the work to the copyright collective management organization that administers the relevant rights, and such organization shall distribute the royalties to the right holder.  
  
The copyright collective management organization responsible for distributing royalties shall establish a query system for the use of the work, which shall be accessible to right holders and users.  
  
The copyright collective management organization responsible for distributing royalties may deduct administrative fees from the royalties it receives, and the administrative fees shall be deducted at half of the proportion of administrative fees decided by the general assembly of members of such organization. Except for administrative fees, such copyright collective management organization shall not deduct any other fees from the royalties it receives.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本条例自2005年3月1日起施行。

**Article 48** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 2005.
