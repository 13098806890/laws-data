# 中华人民共和国专利法实施细则

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2023-12-11  
**生效日期**：2024-01-20

> （2001年6月15日中华人民共和国国务院令第306号公布　根据2002年12月28日《国务院关于修改〈中华人民共和国专利法实施细则〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2010年1月9日《国务院关于修改〈中华人民共和国专利法实施细则〉的决定》第二次修订　根据2023年12月11日《国务院关于修改〈中华人民共和国专利法实施细则〉的决定》第三次修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国专利法》（以下简称专利法），制定本细则。 第二条　专利法和本细则规定的各种手续，应当以书面形式或者国务院专利行政部门规定的其他形式办理。以电子数据交换等方式能够有形地表现所载内容，并可以随时调取查用的数据电文（以下统称电子形式），视为书面形式。 第三条　依照专利法和本细则规定提交的各种文件应当使用中文；国家有统一规定的科技术语的，应当采用规范词；外国人名、地名和科技术语没有统一中文译文的，应当注明原文。 依照专利法和本细则规定提交的各种证件和证明文件是外文的，国务院专利行政部门认为必要时，可以要求当事人在指定期限内附送中文译文；期满未附送的，视为未提交该证件和证明文件。 第四条　向国务院专利行政部门邮寄的各种文件，以寄出的邮戳日为递交日；邮戳日不清晰的，除当事人能够提出证明外，以国务院专利行政部门收到日为递交日。 以电子形式向国务院专利行政部门提交各种文件的，以进入国务院专利行政部门指定的特定电子系统的日期为递交日。 国务院专利行政部门的各种文件，可以通过电子形式、邮寄、直接送交或者其他方式送达当事人。当事人委托专利代理机构的，文件送交专利代理机构；未委托专利代理机构的，文件送交请求书中指明的联系人。 国务院专利行政部门邮寄的各种文件，自文件发出之日起满15日，推定为当事人收到文件之日。当事人提供证据能够证明实际收到文件的日期的，以实际收到日为准。 根据国务院专利行政部门规定应当直接送交的文件，以交付日为送达日。 文件送交地址不清，无法邮寄的，可以通过公告的方式送达当事人。自公告之日起满1个月，该文件视为已经送达。 国务院专利行政部门以电子形式送达的各种文件，以进入当事人认可的电子系统的日期为送达日。 第五条　专利法和本细则规定的各种期限开始的当日不计算在期限内，自下一日开始计算。期限以年或者月计算的，以其最后一月的相应日为期限届满日；该月无相应日的，以该月最后一日为期限届满日；期限届满日是法定休假日的，以休假日后的第一个工作日为期限届满日。 第六条　当事人因不可抗拒的事由而延误专利法或者本细则规定的期限或者国务院专利行政部门指定的期限，导致其权利丧失的，自障碍消除之日起2个月内且自期限届满之日起2年内，可以向国务院专利行政部门请求恢复权利。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国专利法》（以下简称专利法），制定本细则。

**Article 1** These Rules are formulated in accordance with the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Patent Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　专利法和本细则规定的各种手续，应当以书面形式或者国务院专利行政部门规定的其他形式办理。以电子数据交换等方式能够有形地表现所载内容，并可以随时调取查用的数据电文（以下统称电子形式），视为书面形式。

**Article 2** All procedures prescribed in the Patent Law and these Rules shall be handled in written form or in such other forms as may be prescribed by the patent administrative department under the State Council. A data message that can visibly represent the content it carries through electronic data interchange or other means and can be retrieved and consulted at any time (hereinafter collectively referred to as electronic form) shall be deemed as written form.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　依照专利法和本细则规定提交的各种文件应当使用中文；国家有统一规定的科技术语的，应当采用规范词；外国人名、地名和科技术语没有统一中文译文的，应当注明原文。  
依照专利法和本细则规定提交的各种证件和证明文件是外文的，国务院专利行政部门认为必要时，可以要求当事人在指定期限内附送中文译文；期满未附送的，视为未提交该证件和证明文件。

**Article 3** Various documents submitted in accordance with the Patent Law and these Rules shall be in Chinese; where the State has uniform provisions on scientific and technical terms, the standardized terms shall be used; where there is no uniform Chinese translation for foreign names, places or scientific and technical terms, the original language shall be indicated.  
Where various certificates and supporting documents submitted in accordance with the Patent Law and these Rules are in a foreign language, the patent administrative department under the State Council may, when it deems it necessary, require the party to submit a Chinese translation within a specified time limit; if the translation is not submitted upon expiry of the time limit, the certificates and supporting documents shall be deemed not to have been submitted.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　向国务院专利行政部门邮寄的各种文件，以寄出的邮戳日为递交日；邮戳日不清晰的，除当事人能够提出证明外，以国务院专利行政部门收到日为递交日。  
以电子形式向国务院专利行政部门提交各种文件的，以进入国务院专利行政部门指定的特定电子系统的日期为递交日。  
国务院专利行政部门的各种文件，可以通过电子形式、邮寄、直接送交或者其他方式送达当事人。当事人委托专利代理机构的，文件送交专利代理机构；未委托专利代理机构的，文件送交请求书中指明的联系人。  
国务院专利行政部门邮寄的各种文件，自文件发出之日起满15日，推定为当事人收到文件之日。当事人提供证据能够证明实际收到文件的日期的，以实际收到日为准。  
根据国务院专利行政部门规定应当直接送交的文件，以交付日为送达日。  
文件送交地址不清，无法邮寄的，可以通过公告的方式送达当事人。自公告之日起满1个月，该文件视为已经送达。  
国务院专利行政部门以电子形式送达的各种文件，以进入当事人认可的电子系统的日期为送达日。

**Article 4** Where various documents are sent by mail to the patent administrative department under the State Council, the date of posting indicated by the postmark shall be the date of filing; if the postmark is unclear, the date of receipt by the patent administrative department under the State Council shall be the date of filing, unless the party can provide evidence to the contrary.  
Where various documents are submitted in electronic form to the patent administrative department under the State Council, the date on which they enter the specific electronic system designated by the patent administrative department under the State Council shall be the date of filing.  
Various documents of the patent administrative department under the State Council may be served on the party by electronic form, mail, direct delivery or other means. Where a party has appointed a patent agency, the documents shall be delivered to the patent agency; where no patent agency has been appointed, the documents shall be delivered to the contact person indicated in the request.  
Where various documents are sent by mail by the patent administrative department under the State Council, they shall be presumed to have been received by the party on the fifteenth day after the date of dispatch. Where the party provides evidence proving the actual date of receipt, the actual date of receipt shall prevail.  
Where documents are required by the patent administrative department under the State Council to be delivered directly, the date of delivery shall be the date of service.  
Where the address for delivery of documents is unclear and cannot be mailed, the documents may be served on the party by public announcement. One month after the date of the announcement, the documents shall be deemed to have been served.  
Where various documents are served in electronic form by the patent administrative department under the State Council, the date on which they enter the electronic system recognized by the party shall be the date of service.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　专利法和本细则规定的各种期限开始的当日不计算在期限内，自下一日开始计算。期限以年或者月计算的，以其最后一月的相应日为期限届满日；该月无相应日的，以该月最后一日为期限届满日；期限届满日是法定休假日的，以休假日后的第一个工作日为期限届满日。

**Article 5** The day on which any time limit prescribed in the Patent Law and these Rules begins shall not be counted in the time limit, which shall be counted from the following day. Where the time limit is calculated by year or by month, the corresponding day of the last month shall be the expiration day; if there is no corresponding day in that month, the last day of that month shall be the expiration day; if the expiration day falls on a legal holiday, the first working day after the holiday shall be the expiration day.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　当事人因不可抗拒的事由而延误专利法或者本细则规定的期限或者国务院专利行政部门指定的期限，导致其权利丧失的，自障碍消除之日起2个月内且自期限届满之日起2年内，可以向国务院专利行政部门请求恢复权利。  
除前款规定的情形外，当事人因其他正当理由延误专利法或者本细则规定的期限或者国务院专利行政部门指定的期限，导致其权利丧失的，可以自收到国务院专利行政部门的通知之日起2个月内向国务院专利行政部门请求恢复权利；但是，延误复审请求期限的，可以自复审请求期限届满之日起2个月内向国务院专利行政部门请求恢复权利。  
当事人依照本条第一款或者第二款的规定请求恢复权利的，应当提交恢复权利请求书，说明理由，必要时附具有关证明文件，并办理权利丧失前应当办理的相应手续；依照本条第二款的规定请求恢复权利的，还应当缴纳恢复权利请求费。  
当事人请求延长国务院专利行政部门指定的期限的，应当在期限届满前，向国务院专利行政部门提交延长期限请求书，说明理由，并办理有关手续。  
本条第一款和第二款的规定不适用[专利法第二十四条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-24)、第二十九条、第四十二条、第七十四条规定的期限。

**Article 6** Where a party, due to force majeure, fails to observe a time limit prescribed in the Patent Law or these Rules or designated by the patent administrative department under the State Council, thereby causing the loss of its rights, it may, within two months from the date on which the obstacle is removed and within two years from the date on which the time limit expires, request the patent administrative department under the State Council to restore its rights.  
Except for the circumstances provided in the preceding paragraph, where a party, due to other justifiable reasons, fails to observe a time limit prescribed in the Patent Law or these Rules or designated by the patent administrative department under the State Council, thereby causing the loss of its rights, it may, within two months from the date of receiving the notification from the patent administrative department under the State Council, request the patent administrative department under the State Council to restore its rights; however, if the time limit for requesting reexamination is not observed, the party may, within two months from the date on which the time limit for requesting reexamination expires, request the patent administrative department under the State Council to restore its rights.  
Where a party requests restoration of its rights in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 or paragraph 2 of this Article, it shall submit a request for restoration of rights, state the reasons, attach relevant supporting documents where necessary, and complete the corresponding formalities that should have been completed before the loss of rights; where the request is made in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of this Article, it shall also pay the fee for requesting restoration of rights.  
Where a party requests an extension of a time limit designated by the patent administrative department under the State Council, it shall, before the expiry of the time limit, submit a request for extension of the time limit to the patent administrative department under the State Council, state the reasons, and complete the relevant formalities.  
The provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article shall not apply to the time limits prescribed in Articles 24, 29, 42 and 74 of the Patent Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　专利申请涉及国防利益需要保密的，由国防专利机构受理并进行审查；国务院专利行政部门受理的专利申请涉及国防利益需要保密的，应当及时移交国防专利机构进行审查。经国防专利机构审查没有发现驳回理由的，由国务院专利行政部门作出授予国防专利权的决定。  
国务院专利行政部门认为其受理的发明或者实用新型专利申请涉及国防利益以外的国家安全或者重大利益需要保密的，应当及时作出按照保密专利申请处理的决定，并通知申请人。保密专利申请的审查、复审以及保密专利权无效宣告的特殊程序，由国务院专利行政部门规定。

**Article 7** Where a patent application involves national defense interests and needs to be kept confidential, it shall be accepted and examined by the national defense patent authority; where a patent application accepted by the patent administrative department under the State Council involves national defense interests and needs to be kept confidential, it shall be promptly transferred to the national defense patent authority for examination. Where no grounds for rejection are found after examination by the national defense patent authority, the patent administrative department under the State Council shall make a decision to grant a national defense patent.  
Where the patent administrative department under the State Council considers that an invention or utility model patent application it has accepted involves national security or major interests other than national defense interests and needs to be kept confidential, it shall promptly make a decision to treat the application as a confidential patent application and notify the applicant. The special procedures for the examination, reexamination, and invalidation declaration of confidential patent rights shall be prescribed by the patent administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　[专利法第十九条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-19)所称在中国完成的发明或者实用新型，是指技术方案的实质性内容在中国境内完成的发明或者实用新型。  
任何单位或者个人将在中国完成的发明或者实用新型向外国申请专利的，应当按照下列方式之一请求国务院专利行政部门进行保密审查：  
（一）直接向外国申请专利或者向有关国外机构提交专利国际申请的，应当事先向国务院专利行政部门提出请求，并详细说明其技术方案；  
（二）向国务院专利行政部门申请专利后拟向外国申请专利或者向有关国外机构提交专利国际申请的，应当在向外国申请专利或者向有关国外机构提交专利国际申请前向国务院专利行政部门提出请求。  
向国务院专利行政部门提交专利国际申请的，视为同时提出了保密审查请求。

**Article 8** For the purpose of Article 19 of the Patent Law, an invention or utility model completed in China means an invention or utility model whose substantive content of the technical solution is completed within the territory of China.  
Where any entity or individual files a patent application abroad for an invention or utility model completed in China, it shall request the patent administrative department under the State Council to conduct a confidentiality examination in one of the following manners:  
(1) where it intends to directly file a patent application abroad or submit an international patent application to a relevant foreign authority, it shall first submit a request to the patent administrative department under the State Council and provide a detailed description of its technical solution;  
(2) where, after filing a patent application with the patent administrative department under the State Council, it intends to file a patent application abroad or submit an international patent application to a relevant foreign authority, it shall submit a request to the patent administrative department under the State Council before filing the patent application abroad or submitting the international patent application to the relevant foreign authority.  
Where an international patent application is submitted to the patent administrative department under the State Council, it shall be deemed that a request for confidentiality examination has been made simultaneously.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院专利行政部门收到依照本细则第八条规定递交的请求后，经过审查认为该发明或者实用新型可能涉及国家安全或者重大利益需要保密的，应当在请求递交日起2个月内向申请人发出保密审查通知；情况复杂的，可以延长2个月。  
国务院专利行政部门依照前款规定通知进行保密审查的，应当在请求递交日起4个月内作出是否需要保密的决定，并通知申请人；情况复杂的，可以延长2个月。

**Article 9** After receiving a request submitted in accordance with Article 8 of these Rules, if the patent administrative department under the State Council, upon examination, considers that the invention or utility model may involve national security or major interests and needs to be kept confidential, it shall issue a notice of confidentiality examination to the applicant within two months from the date of submission of the request; where the circumstances are complex, the time limit may be extended by two months.  
Where the patent administrative department under the State Council notifies a confidentiality examination in accordance with the preceding paragraph, it shall make a decision on whether confidentiality is needed and notify the applicant within four months from the date of submission of the request; where the circumstances are complex, the time limit may be extended by two months.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　[专利法第五条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-5)所称违反法律的发明创造，不包括仅其实施为法律所禁止的发明创造。

**Article 10** An invention-creation that violates any law as mentioned in Article 5 of the Patent Law shall not include an invention-creation the implementation of which is merely prohibited by law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请专利应当遵循诚实信用原则。提出各类专利申请应当以真实发明创造活动为基础，不得弄虚作假。

**Article 11** An application for a patent shall follow the principle of good faith. All types of patent applications shall be based on genuine inventive-creation activities and shall not be fraudulent or false.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　除[专利法第二十八条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-28)和第四十二条规定的情形外，专利法所称申请日，有优先权的，指优先权日。  
本细则所称申请日，除另有规定的外，是指[专利法第二十八条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-28)规定的申请日。

**Article 12** Except for the circumstances provided for in Articles 28 and 42 of the Patent Law, the application date mentioned in the Patent Law shall, if there is a priority right, refer to the priority date.  
The application date mentioned in these Rules shall, unless otherwise provided, refer to the application date as provided for in Article 28 of the Patent Law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　[专利法第六条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-6)所称执行本单位的任务所完成的职务发明创造，是指：  
（一）在本职工作中作出的发明创造；  
（二）履行本单位交付的本职工作之外的任务所作出的发明创造；  
（三）退休、调离原单位后或者劳动、人事关系终止后1年内作出的，与其在原单位承担的本职工作或者原单位分配的任务有关的发明创造。  
[专利法第六条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-6)所称本单位，包括临时工作单位；[专利法第六条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-6)所称本单位的物质技术条件，是指本单位的资金、设备、零部件、原材料或者不对外公开的技术信息和资料等。

**Article 13** The service invention-creation made in the execution of the tasks of the entity as referred to in Article 6 of the Patent Law shall mean:  
(1) an invention-creation made in the course of performing the inventor’s own duties;  
(2) an invention-creation made in the execution of any task assigned to the inventor by the entity beyond the scope of the inventor’s own duties; and  
(3) an invention-creation made within one year after the inventor’s retirement, transfer from the original entity, or termination of the labor or personnel relationship with the entity, which relates to the inventor’s own duties or any task assigned to the inventor by the original entity.  
The entity as referred to in Article 6 of the Patent Law includes the temporary entity; the material and technical conditions of the entity as referred to in Article 6 of the Patent Law shall mean the entity’s funds, equipment, parts and components, raw materials, or technical information and data not disclosed to the public, etc.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　专利法所称发明人或者设计人，是指对发明创造的实质性特点作出创造性贡献的人。在完成发明创造过程中，只负责组织工作的人、为物质技术条件的利用提供方便的人或者从事其他辅助工作的人，不是发明人或者设计人。

**Article 14** The inventor or designer referred to in the Patent Law shall mean the person who has made creative contributions to the substantive features of the invention-creation. Any person who, during the course of completing the invention-creation, is only responsible for organizational work, facilitates the use of material and technical conditions, or performs other auxiliary work shall not be considered as an inventor or designer.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　除依照[专利法第十条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-10)规定转让专利权外，专利权因其他事由发生转移的，当事人应当凭有关证明文件或者法律文书向国务院专利行政部门办理专利权转移手续。  
专利权人与他人订立的专利实施许可合同，应当自合同生效之日起3个月内向国务院专利行政部门备案。  
以专利权出质的，由出质人和质权人共同向国务院专利行政部门办理出质登记。

**Article 15** Except for the assignment of patent rights in accordance with Article 10 of the Patent Law, where patent rights are transferred due to other causes, the parties shall, on the strength of relevant certification documents or legal instruments, go through the formalities for the transfer of patent rights with the administrative department for patent affairs under the State Council.  
A patent exploitation license contract concluded between the patentee and any other person shall, within three months from the date the contract takes effect, be submitted to the administrative department for patent affairs under the State Council for record.  
Where a patent right is pledged, the pledgor and the pledgee shall jointly go through the registration of pledge with the administrative department for patent affairs under the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　专利工作应当贯彻党和国家知识产权战略部署，提升我国专利创造、运用、保护、管理和服务水平，支持全面创新，促进创新型国家建设。  
国务院专利行政部门应当提升专利信息公共服务能力，完整、准确、及时发布专利信息，提供专利基础数据，促进专利相关数据资源的开放共享、互联互通。

**Article 16** The patent work shall implement the strategic deployment of intellectual property of the Party and the State, enhance the capacity of China in patent creation, utilization, protection, administration and services, support all-round innovation, and promote the building of an innovative country.  
The administrative department for patent affairs under the State Council shall enhance the capacity of public services for patent information, publish patent information completely, accurately and in a timely manner, provide basic patent data, and promote the open sharing, interconnection and intercommunication of patent-related data resources.

### 第二章　专利的申请

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　申请专利的，应当向国务院专利行政部门提交申请文件。申请文件应当符合规定的要求。  
申请人委托专利代理机构向国务院专利行政部门申请专利和办理其他专利事务的，应当同时提交委托书，写明委托权限。  
申请人有2人以上且未委托专利代理机构的，除请求书中另有声明的外，以请求书中指明的第一申请人为代表人。

**Article 17** A person applying for a patent shall submit patent application documents to the administrative department for patent affairs under the State Council. The application documents shall conform to the prescribed requirements.  
Where an applicant entrusts a patent agency to apply for a patent and handle other patent matters with the administrative department for patent affairs under the State Council, a power of attorney shall be submitted at the same time, specifying the entrusted scope of authority.  
Where there are two or more applicants and no patent agency is entrusted, unless otherwise stated in the request, the first applicant specified in the request shall be the representative.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　依照[专利法第十八条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-18)第一款的规定委托专利代理机构在中国申请专利和办理其他专利事务的，涉及下列事务，申请人或者专利权人可以自行办理：  
（一）申请要求优先权的，提交第一次提出的专利申请（以下简称在先申请）文件副本；  
（二）缴纳费用；  
（三）国务院专利行政部门规定的其他事务。

**Article 18** Where, in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 18 of the Patent Law, a patent agency is entrusted to apply for a patent and handle other patent matters in China, the applicant or patentee may personally handle the following matters:  
(1) submitting a copy of the patent application first filed (hereinafter referred to as the earlier application) for a claim to priority;  
(2) paying fees; and  
(3) other matters as prescribed by the administrative department for patent affairs under the State Council.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　发明、实用新型或者外观设计专利申请的请求书应当写明下列事项：  
（一）发明、实用新型或者外观设计的名称；  
（二）申请人是中国单位或者个人的，其名称或者姓名、地址、邮政编码、统一社会信用代码或者身份证件号码；申请人是外国人、外国企业或者外国其他组织的，其姓名或者名称、国籍或者注册的国家或者地区；  
（三）发明人或者设计人的姓名；  
（四）申请人委托专利代理机构的，受托机构的名称、机构代码以及该机构指定的专利代理师的姓名、专利代理师资格证号码、联系电话；  
（五）要求优先权的，在先申请的申请日、申请号以及原受理机构的名称；  
（六）申请人或者专利代理机构的签字或者盖章；  
（七）申请文件清单；  
（八）附加文件清单；  
（九）其他需要写明的有关事项。

**Article 19** The request for a patent application for invention, utility model or design shall state the following:  
(1) the title of the invention, utility model or design;  
(2) if the applicant is a Chinese entity or individual, its or his/her name, address, postal code, unified social credit code or identity document number; if the applicant is a foreigner, foreign enterprise or other foreign organization, his/her/its name, nationality or the country or region of registration;  
(3) the name of the inventor or designer;  
(4) if the applicant entrusts a patent agency, the name of the entrusted agency, the agency code, and the name of the patent agent designated by the agency, the patent agent qualification certificate number, and the contact phone number;  
(5) if priority is claimed, the filing date and application number of the earlier application, and the name of the original receiving institution;  
(6) the signature or seal of the applicant or the patent agency;  
(7) a list of the application documents;  
(8) a list of the additional documents; and  
(9) any other relevant matters that need to be stated.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　发明或者实用新型专利申请的说明书应当写明发明或者实用新型的名称，该名称应当与请求书中的名称一致。说明书应当包括下列内容：  
（一）技术领域：写明要求保护的技术方案所属的技术领域；  
（二）背景技术：写明对发明或者实用新型的理解、检索、审查有用的背景技术；有可能的，并引证反映这些背景技术的文件；  
（三）发明内容：写明发明或者实用新型所要解决的技术问题以及解决其技术问题采用的技术方案，并对照现有技术写明发明或者实用新型的有益效果；  
（四）附图说明：说明书有附图的，对各幅附图作简略说明；  
（五）具体实施方式：详细写明申请人认为实现发明或者实用新型的优选方式；必要时，举例说明；有附图的，对照附图。  
发明或者实用新型专利申请人应当按照前款规定的方式和顺序撰写说明书，并在说明书每一部分前面写明标题，除非其发明或者实用新型的性质用其他方式或者顺序撰写能节约说明书的篇幅并使他人能够准确理解其发明或者实用新型。  
发明或者实用新型说明书应当用词规范、语句清楚，并不得使用“如权利要求……所述的……”一类的引用语，也不得使用商业性宣传用语。  
发明专利申请包含一个或者多个核苷酸或者氨基酸序列的，说明书应当包括符合国务院专利行政部门规定的序列表。  
实用新型专利申请说明书应当有表示要求保护的产品的形状、构造或者其结合的附图。

**Article 20** The description of a patent application for invention or utility model shall state the title of the invention or utility model, which shall be consistent with the title in the request. The description shall include the following:  
(1) technical field: specifying the technical field to which the technical solution sought to be protected pertains;  
(2) background art: specifying the background art that is useful for understanding, searching and examining the invention or utility model; where possible, citing documents that reflect such background art;  
(3) summary of the invention: specifying the technical problem to be solved by the invention or utility model and the technical solution adopted to solve the technical problem, and stating the beneficial effects of the invention or utility model in comparison with the prior art;  
(4) description of drawings: if the description has drawings, briefly describing each drawing in the drawings;  
(5) specific mode of implementation: describing in detail the preferred mode of carrying out the invention or utility model as contemplated by the applicant; where necessary, giving examples; and if there are drawings, referring to them.  
An applicant for a patent for invention or utility model shall draft the description in the manner and order prescribed in the preceding paragraph, and shall state the heading before each part of the description, unless the nature of the invention or utility model allows a different manner or order that would save the length of the description and enable a person to understand the invention or utility model accurately.  
The description of an invention or utility model shall use standard wording and clear statements, and shall not use expressions such as “as described in claim ...” or similar references, nor shall it use commercial advertising language.  
Where the patent application for invention contains one or more nucleotide or amino acid sequences, the description shall include a sequence table conforming to the provisions of the administrative department for patent affairs under the State Council.  
The description of a patent application for utility model shall include drawings showing the shape, structure or combination thereof of the product sought to be protected.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　发明或者实用新型的几幅附图应当按照“图1，图2，……”顺序编号排列。  
发明或者实用新型说明书文字部分中未提及的附图标记不得在附图中出现，附图中未出现的附图标记不得在说明书文字部分中提及。申请文件中表示同一组成部分的附图标记应当一致。  
附图中除必需的词语外，不应当含有其他注释。

**Article 21** The drawings of an invention or utility model shall be numbered consecutively as "Figure 1, Figure 2, ...".  
Reference signs not mentioned in the descriptive part of the specification of the invention or utility model shall not appear in the drawings, and reference signs not appearing in the drawings shall not be mentioned in the descriptive part of the specification. Reference signs indicating the same component in the application documents shall be consistent.  
The drawings shall not contain any other annotations except for the necessary words.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　权利要求书应当记载发明或者实用新型的技术特征。  
权利要求书有几项权利要求的，应当用阿拉伯数字顺序编号。  
权利要求书中使用的科技术语应当与说明书中使用的科技术语一致，可以有化学式或者数学式，但是不得有插图。除绝对必要的外，不得使用“如说明书……部分所述”或者“如图……所示”的用语。  
权利要求中的技术特征可以引用说明书附图中相应的标记，该标记应当放在相应的技术特征后并置于括号内，便于理解权利要求。附图标记不得解释为对权利要求的限制。

**Article 22** The claims shall state the technical features of the invention or utility model.  
Where there are several claims, they shall be numbered consecutively in Arabic numerals.  
The technical terms used in the claims shall be consistent with those used in the description. Chemical formulas or mathematical formulas may be included, but no illustrations. Except where absolutely necessary, expressions such as "as described in part ... of the description" or "as shown in Figure ..." shall not be used.  
Technical features in the claims may quote corresponding reference signs from the drawings of the description, which shall be placed after the corresponding technical features and in parentheses, for the purpose of facilitating understanding of the claims. Reference signs shall not be construed as limiting the claims.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　权利要求书应当有独立权利要求，也可以有从属权利要求。  
独立权利要求应当从整体上反映发明或者实用新型的技术方案，记载解决技术问题的必要技术特征。  
从属权利要求应当用附加的技术特征，对引用的权利要求作进一步限定。

**Article 23** The claims shall contain an independent claim and may contain dependent claims.  
The independent claim shall reflect the technical solution of the invention or utility model as a whole and record the essential technical features necessary for solving the technical problem.  
A dependent claim shall further limit the claim it refers to by additional technical features.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　发明或者实用新型的独立权利要求应当包括前序部分和特征部分，按照下列规定撰写：  
（一）前序部分：写明要求保护的发明或者实用新型技术方案的主题名称和发明或者实用新型主题与最接近的现有技术共有的必要技术特征；  
（二）特征部分：使用“其特征是……”或者类似的用语，写明发明或者实用新型区别于最接近的现有技术的技术特征。这些特征和前序部分写明的特征合在一起，限定发明或者实用新型要求保护的范围。  
发明或者实用新型的性质不适于用前款方式表达的，独立权利要求可以用其他方式撰写。  
一项发明或者实用新型应当只有一个独立权利要求，并写在同一发明或者实用新型的从属权利要求之前。

**Article 24** The independent claim of an invention or utility model shall comprise a preamble portion and a characterizing portion, and shall be drafted in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) Preamble portion: stating the title of the subject matter of the technical solution of the invention or utility model for which protection is sought and the essential technical features that are common to the subject matter of the invention or utility model and the closest prior art;  
(2) Characterizing portion: using expressions such as "characterized in that..." or similar wording, stating the technical features that distinguish the invention or utility model from the closest prior art. These features, together with the features stated in the preamble portion, define the scope of protection sought for the invention or utility model.  
Where the nature of the invention or utility model is not suitable for being expressed in the manner prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the independent claim may be drafted in another manner.  
An invention or utility model shall have only one independent claim, which shall be written before the dependent claims of the same invention or utility model.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　发明或者实用新型的从属权利要求应当包括引用部分和限定部分，按照下列规定撰写：  
（一）引用部分：写明引用的权利要求的编号及其主题名称；  
（二）限定部分：写明发明或者实用新型附加的技术特征。  
从属权利要求只能引用在前的权利要求。引用两项以上权利要求的多项从属权利要求，只能以择一方式引用在前的权利要求，并不得作为另一项多项从属权利要求的基础。

**Article 25** The dependent claim of an invention or utility model shall comprise a reference portion and a limiting portion, and shall be drafted in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) Reference portion: stating the number of the claim referred to and the title of its subject matter;  
(2) Limiting portion: stating the additional technical features of the invention or utility model.  
A dependent claim may only refer to a preceding claim. A multiple dependent claim referring to two or more claims may only refer to the preceding claims in the alternative and shall not serve as a basis for another multiple dependent claim.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　说明书摘要应当写明发明或者实用新型专利申请所公开内容的概要，即写明发明或者实用新型的名称和所属技术领域，并清楚地反映所要解决的技术问题、解决该问题的技术方案的要点以及主要用途。  
说明书摘要可以包含最能说明发明的化学式；有附图的专利申请，还应当在请求书中指定一幅最能说明该发明或者实用新型技术特征的说明书附图作为摘要附图。摘要中不得使用商业性宣传用语。

**Article 26** The abstract of the description shall state a summary of the disclosure as set forth in the patent application for invention or utility model, i.e., the title of the invention or utility model and the technical field to which it pertains, and shall clearly reflect the technical problem to be solved, the gist of the technical solution to that problem, and the main use or uses.  
The abstract of the description may contain a chemical formula that best illustrates the invention; where the patent application contains drawings, the request shall also designate one drawing from the drawings of the description that best illustrates the technical features of the invention or utility model as the abstract drawing. No commercial advertising language shall be used in the abstract.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　申请专利的发明涉及新的生物材料，该生物材料公众不能得到，并且对该生物材料的说明不足以使所属领域的技术人员实施其发明的，除应当符合专利法和本细则的有关规定外，申请人还应当办理下列手续：  
（一）在申请日前或者最迟在申请日（有优先权的，指优先权日），将该生物材料的样品提交国务院专利行政部门认可的保藏单位保藏，并在申请时或者最迟自申请日起4个月内提交保藏单位出具的保藏证明和存活证明；期满未提交证明的，该样品视为未提交保藏；  
（二）在申请文件中，提供有关该生物材料特征的资料；  
（三）涉及生物材料样品保藏的专利申请应当在请求书和说明书中写明该生物材料的分类命名（注明拉丁文名称）、保藏该生物材料样品的单位名称、地址、保藏日期和保藏编号；申请时未写明的，应当自申请日起4个月内补正；期满未补正的，视为未提交保藏。

**Article 27** Where an invention for which a patent is applied for concerns a new biological material which is not available to the public, and the description of the biological material is insufficient for a person skilled in the art to carry out the invention, in addition to complying with the relevant provisions of the Patent Law and these Rules, the applicant shall also go through the following procedures:  
(1) before the date of filing or at the latest on the date of filing (where priority is claimed, the date of priority), deposit a sample of the biological material with a depositary institution recognized by the patent administration department under the State Council, and at the time of filing or within 4 months from the date of filing at the latest, submit a deposit certificate and a viability certificate issued by the depositary institution; if no such certificate is submitted by the expiration of the time limit, the sample shall be deemed not to have been deposited;  
(2) in the application documents, provide information concerning the characteristics of the biological material;  
(3) where the patent application involves the deposit of a sample of biological material, the request and the description shall state the taxonomic designation (with its Latin name) of the biological material, the name and address of the depositary institution where the sample of the biological material is deposited, the date of deposit, and the deposit number; if it is not stated at the time of filing, it shall be corrected within 4 months from the date of filing; if no correction is made by the expiration of the time limit, it shall be deemed that no deposit has been made.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　发明专利申请人依照本细则第二十七条的规定保藏生物材料样品的，在发明专利申请公布后，任何单位或者个人需要将该专利申请所涉及的生物材料作为实验目的使用的，应当向国务院专利行政部门提出请求，并写明下列事项：  
（一）请求人的姓名或者名称和地址；  
（二）不向其他任何人提供该生物材料的保证；  
（三）在授予专利权前，只作为实验目的使用的保证。

**Article 28** Where the applicant for a patent for invention has deposited a sample of biological material in accordance with Article 27 of these Rules, after the publication of the application for patent for invention, any entity or individual that needs to use the biological material involved in the patent application for the purpose of experiment shall make a request to the patent administration department under the State Council and state the following:  
(1) the name or title and address of the requester;  
(2) an undertaking not to make the biological material available to any other person; and  
(3) an undertaking to use it only for experimental purposes before the grant of the patent right.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　专利法所称遗传资源，是指取自人体、动物、植物或者微生物等含有遗传功能单位并具有实际或者潜在价值的材料和利用此类材料产生的遗传信息；专利法所称依赖遗传资源完成的发明创造，是指利用了遗传资源的遗传功能完成的发明创造。  
就依赖遗传资源完成的发明创造申请专利的，申请人应当在请求书中予以说明，并填写国务院专利行政部门制定的表格。

**Article 29** For the purposes of the Patent Law, "genetic resources" refer to materials taken from the human body, animals, plants, or microorganisms that contain genetic functional units and have actual or potential value, as well as genetic information generated from the use of such materials; for the purposes of the Patent Law, "invention-creation accomplished by relying on genetic resources" refers to an invention-creation accomplished by utilizing the genetic functions of genetic resources.  
Where a patent application is filed for an invention-creation accomplished by relying on genetic resources, the applicant shall indicate this in the request and fill in the form formulated by the patent administration department under the State Council.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　申请人应当就每件外观设计产品所需要保护的内容提交有关图片或者照片。  
申请局部外观设计专利的，应当提交整体产品的视图，并用虚线与实线相结合或者其他方式表明所需要保护部分的内容。  
申请人请求保护色彩的，应当提交彩色图片或者照片。

**Article 30** The applicant shall submit relevant drawings or photographs for the content for which protection is sought for each design product.  
Where a patent is applied for a partial design, a view of the whole product shall be submitted, and the content of the part for which protection is sought shall be indicated by a combination of broken lines and solid lines or by other means.  
Where the applicant requests protection of a color, color drawings or photographs shall be submitted.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　外观设计的简要说明应当写明外观设计产品的名称、用途，外观设计的设计要点，并指定一幅最能表明设计要点的图片或者照片。省略视图或者请求保护色彩的，应当在简要说明中写明。  
对同一产品的多项相似外观设计提出一件外观设计专利申请的，应当在简要说明中指定其中一项作为基本设计。  
申请局部外观设计专利的，应当在简要说明中写明请求保护的部分，已在整体产品的视图中用虚线与实线相结合方式表明的除外。  
简要说明不得使用商业性宣传用语，也不得说明产品的性能。

**Article 31** The brief description of the design shall state the name and purpose of the product incorporating the design, and the design features, and shall designate one drawing or photograph that best shows the design features. If views are omitted or color protection is claimed, this shall be stated in the brief description.  
Where a design patent application is filed for multiple similar designs of the same product in a single application, one of them shall be designated in the brief description as the basic design.  
Where a design patent application is filed for a partial design, the part for which protection is sought shall be stated in the brief description, unless it has been indicated in the views of the overall product by a combination of dashed lines and solid lines.  
The brief description shall not contain any commercial advertising language, nor shall it state the performance of the product.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院专利行政部门认为必要时，可以要求外观设计专利申请人提交使用外观设计的产品样品或者模型。样品或者模型的体积不得超过30厘米×30厘米×30厘米，重量不得超过15公斤。易腐、易损或者危险品不得作为样品或者模型提交。

**Article 32** If the patent administration department under the State Council deems it necessary, it may require the applicant for a design patent to submit a sample or model of the product incorporating the design. The volume of the sample or model shall not exceed 30 cm × 30 cm × 30 cm, and the weight shall not exceed 15 kg. Perishable, fragile, or dangerous articles shall not be submitted as samples or models.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　[专利法第二十四条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-24)第（二）项所称中国政府承认的国际展览会，是指国际展览会公约规定的在国际展览局注册或者由其认可的国际展览会。  
[专利法第二十四条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-24)第（三）项所称学术会议或者技术会议，是指国务院有关主管部门或者全国性学术团体组织召开的学术会议或者技术会议，以及国务院有关主管部门认可的由国际组织召开的学术会议或者技术会议。  
申请专利的发明创造有[专利法第二十四条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-24)第（二）项或者第（三）项所列情形的，申请人应当在提出专利申请时声明，并自申请日起2个月内提交有关发明创造已经展出或者发表，以及展出或者发表日期的证明文件。  
申请专利的发明创造有[专利法第二十四条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-24)第（一）项或者第（四）项所列情形的，国务院专利行政部门认为必要时，可以要求申请人在指定期限内提交证明文件。  
申请人未依照本条第三款的规定提出声明和提交证明文件的，或者未依照本条第四款的规定在指定期限内提交证明文件的，其申请不适用[专利法第二十四条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-24)的规定。

**Article 33** The term "international exhibition recognized by the Chinese Government" as mentioned in Article 24(2) of the Patent Law means an international exhibition registered with or recognized by the International Exhibitions Bureau as provided for in the Convention on International Exhibitions.  
The term "academic or technical conference" as mentioned in Article 24(3) of the Patent Law means an academic or technical conference organized by the competent department of the State Council or a national academic organization, and an academic or technical conference organized by an international organization recognized by the competent department of the State Council.  
Where an invention-creation for which a patent is applied for falls under the circumstances set forth in Article 24(2) or (3) of the Patent Law, the applicant shall make a declaration when filing the patent application, and shall, within two months from the filing date, submit documentary evidence that the invention-creation has been exhibited or published, as well as the date of such exhibition or publication.  
Where an invention-creation for which a patent is applied for falls under the circumstances set forth in Article 24(1) or (4) of the Patent Law, the patent administration department under the State Council may, if it deems necessary, require the applicant to submit documentary evidence within a specified time limit.  
If the applicant fails to make the declaration and submit the documentary evidence as provided in paragraph 3 of this Article, or fails to submit the documentary evidence within the specified time limit as provided in paragraph 4 of this Article, the provisions of Article 24 of the Patent Law shall not apply to his application.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　申请人依照[专利法第三十条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-30)的规定要求外国优先权的，申请人提交的在先申请文件副本应当经原受理机构证明。依照国务院专利行政部门与该受理机构签订的协议，国务院专利行政部门通过电子交换等途径获得在先申请文件副本的，视为申请人提交了经该受理机构证明的在先申请文件副本。要求本国优先权，申请人在请求书中写明在先申请的申请日和申请号的，视为提交了在先申请文件副本。  
要求优先权，但请求书中漏写或者错写在先申请的申请日、申请号和原受理机构名称中的一项或者两项内容的，国务院专利行政部门应当通知申请人在指定期限内补正；期满未补正的，视为未要求优先权。  
要求优先权的申请人的姓名或者名称与在先申请文件副本中记载的申请人姓名或者名称不一致的，应当提交优先权转让证明材料，未提交该证明材料的，视为未要求优先权。  
外观设计专利申请人要求外国优先权，其在先申请未包括对外观设计的简要说明，申请人按照本细则第三十一条规定提交的简要说明未超出在先申请文件的图片或者照片表示的范围的，不影响其享有优先权。

**Article 34** Where an applicant claims foreign priority in accordance with Article 30 of the Patent Law, the copy of the earlier application document submitted by the applicant shall be certified by the authority that originally received the application. Where, in accordance with an agreement concluded between the patent administration department under the State Council and the said receiving authority, the patent administration department under the State Council obtains a copy of the earlier application document through electronic exchange or other means, the applicant shall be deemed to have submitted a copy of the earlier application document certified by the said receiving authority. Where an applicant claims domestic priority and states in the request the filing date and application number of the earlier application, he shall be deemed to have submitted a copy of the earlier application document.  
Where a priority claim is made, but the request omits or incorrectly states one or two of the following: the filing date, the application number of the earlier application, and the name of the authority that originally received the application, the patent administration department under the State Council shall notify the applicant to make the correction within a specified time limit; if the correction is not made within the time limit, the priority claim shall be deemed not to have been made.  
Where the name or title of the applicant claiming priority is inconsistent with that recorded in the copy of the earlier application document, the applicant shall submit documentary evidence for the assignment of the priority right; if such documentary evidence is not submitted, the priority claim shall be deemed not to have been made.  
Where an applicant for a design patent claims foreign priority and the earlier application does not include a brief description of the design, if the brief description submitted by the applicant in accordance with Article 31 of these Implementing Regulations does not go beyond the scope of the drawings or photographs in the earlier application document, it shall not affect his enjoyment of the priority right.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　申请人在一件专利申请中，可以要求一项或者多项优先权；要求多项优先权的，该申请的优先权期限从最早的优先权日起计算。  
发明或者实用新型专利申请人要求本国优先权，在先申请是发明专利申请的，可以就相同主题提出发明或者实用新型专利申请；在先申请是实用新型专利申请的，可以就相同主题提出实用新型或者发明专利申请。外观设计专利申请人要求本国优先权，在先申请是发明或者实用新型专利申请的，可以就附图显示的设计提出相同主题的外观设计专利申请；在先申请是外观设计专利申请的，可以就相同主题提出外观设计专利申请。但是，提出后一申请时，在先申请的主题有下列情形之一的，不得作为要求本国优先权的基础：  
（一）已经要求外国优先权或者本国优先权的；  
（二）已经被授予专利权的；  
（三）属于按照规定提出的分案申请的。  
申请人要求本国优先权的，其在先申请自后一申请提出之日起即视为撤回，但外观设计专利申请人要求以发明或者实用新型专利申请作为本国优先权基础的除外。

**Article 35** In a patent application, the applicant may claim one or more priorities; where multiple priorities are claimed, the priority period for the application shall be calculated from the earliest priority date.  
Where an applicant for a patent for invention or utility model claims domestic priority, if the earlier application is an invention patent application, he may file an invention or utility model patent application for the same subject matter; if the earlier application is a utility model patent application, he may file a utility model or invention patent application for the same subject matter. Where an applicant for a design patent claims domestic priority, if the earlier application is an invention or utility model patent application, he may file a design patent application for the same subject matter as the design shown in the drawings; if the earlier application is a design patent application, he may file a design patent application for the same subject matter. However, when filing the later application, if the subject matter of the earlier application is under any of the following circumstances, it shall not be used as the basis for claiming domestic priority:  
(1) it has already been the subject of a claim for foreign or domestic priority;  
(2) it has already been granted a patent right;  
(3) it is a divisional application filed as prescribed.  
Where an applicant claims domestic priority, his earlier application shall be deemed to be withdrawn as of the filing date of the later application, except where an applicant for a design patent claims an invention or utility model patent application as the basis for domestic priority.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　申请人超出[专利法第二十九条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-29)规定的期限，向国务院专利行政部门就相同主题提出发明或者实用新型专利申请，有正当理由的，可以在期限届满之日起2个月内请求恢复优先权。

**Article 36** Where an applicant files an invention or utility model patent application with the patent administration department under the State Council for the same subject matter beyond the time limit prescribed in Article 29 of the Patent Law, if there is a legitimate reason, he may request restoration of the priority right within two months from the date of expiry of the time limit.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　发明或者实用新型专利申请人要求了优先权的，可以自优先权日起16个月内或者自申请日起4个月内，请求在请求书中增加或者改正优先权要求。

**Article 37** Where an applicant for a patent for invention or utility model has claimed priority, he may, within sixteen months from the priority date or within four months from the filing date, request to add or correct a priority claim in the request.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　在中国没有经常居所或者营业所的申请人，申请专利或者要求外国优先权的，国务院专利行政部门认为必要时，可以要求其提供下列文件：  
（一）申请人是个人的，其国籍证明；  
（二）申请人是企业或者其他组织的，其注册的国家或者地区的证明文件；  
（三）申请人的所属国，承认中国单位和个人可以按照该国国民的同等条件，在该国享有专利权、优先权和其他与专利有关的权利的证明文件。

**Article 38** Where an applicant who has no habitual residence or place of business in China files a patent application or claims foreign priority, the patent administration department under the State Council may, if it deems necessary, require him to provide the following documents:  
(1) if the applicant is an individual, a certificate of his nationality;  
(2) if the applicant is an enterprise or other organization, a document certifying the country or region in which it is registered;  
(3) a document certifying that the country to which the applicant belongs recognizes that Chinese entities and individuals may, on the same conditions as its nationals, enjoy patent rights, priority rights, and other rights relating to patents in that country.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　依照[专利法第三十一条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-31)第一款规定，可以作为一件专利申请提出的属于一个总的发明构思的两项以上的发明或者实用新型，应当在技术上相互关联，包含一个或者多个相同或者相应的特定技术特征，其中特定技术特征是指每一项发明或者实用新型作为整体，对现有技术作出贡献的技术特征。

**Article 39** Two or more inventions or utility models belonging to a single general inventive concept that may be filed as a single patent application in accordance with Article 31(1) of the Patent Law shall be technically interrelated and shall contain one or more same or corresponding special technical features. A "special technical feature" means a technical feature that defines a contribution which each of those inventions or utility models, considered as a whole, makes over the prior art.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　依照[专利法第三十一条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-31)第二款规定，将同一产品的多项相似外观设计作为一件申请提出的，对该产品的其他设计应当与简要说明中指定的基本设计相似。一件外观设计专利申请中的相似外观设计不得超过10项。  
[专利法第三十一条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-31)第二款所称同一类别并且成套出售或者使用的产品的两项以上外观设计，是指各产品属于分类表中同一大类，习惯上同时出售或者同时使用，而且各产品的外观设计具有相同的设计构思。  
将两项以上外观设计作为一件申请提出的，应当将各项外观设计的顺序编号标注在每件外观设计产品各幅图片或者照片的名称之前。

**Article 40** Where multiple similar designs of the same product are filed as a single application in accordance with Article 31(2) of the Patent Law, the other designs of that product shall be similar to the basic design designated in the brief description. The number of similar designs in a design patent application shall not exceed 10.  
The term "two or more designs for products belonging to the same class and sold or used as a set" as mentioned in Article 31(2) of the Patent Law means that the products belong to the same major class in the classification table, are customarily sold or used simultaneously, and the designs of the products share the same design concept.  
Where two or more designs are filed as a single application, the serial number of each design shall be indicated before the title of each drawing or photograph of each design product.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　申请人撤回专利申请的，应当向国务院专利行政部门提出声明，写明发明创造的名称、申请号和申请日。  
撤回专利申请的声明在国务院专利行政部门做好公布专利申请文件的印刷准备工作后提出的，申请文件仍予公布；但是，撤回专利申请的声明应当在以后出版的专利公报上予以公告。

**Article 41** Where an applicant withdraws an application for a patent, he/she shall submit a declaration to the patent administration department under the State Council, stating the title of the invention-creation, the application number and the date of filing.  
If the declaration to withdraw the patent application is submitted after the patent administration department under the State Council has completed the printing preparations for publishing the patent application documents, the application documents shall still be published; however, the declaration to withdraw the patent application shall be announced in a later issue of the Patent Gazette.

### 第三章　专利申请的审查和批准

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　在初步审查、实质审查、复审和无效宣告程序中，实施审查和审理的人员有下列情形之一的，应当自行回避，当事人或者其他利害关系人可以要求其回避：  
（一）是当事人或者其代理人的近亲属的；  
（二）与专利申请或者专利权有利害关系的；  
（三）与当事人或者其代理人有其他关系，可能影响公正审查和审理的；  
（四）复审或者无效宣告程序中，曾参与原申请的审查的。

**Article 42** In the procedures of preliminary examination, substantive examination, reexamination and invalidation, the personnel conducting examination and adjudication shall withdraw in any of the following circumstances, and a party or any other interested person may request his/her withdrawal:  
(1) being a close relative of the party or his/her agent;  
(2) having an interest in the patent application or patent right;  
(3) having other relations with the party or his/her agent which may affect the impartiality of the examination and adjudication;  
(4) having previously participated in the examination of the original application in the reexamination or invalidation procedure.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国务院专利行政部门收到发明或者实用新型专利申请的请求书、说明书（实用新型必须包括附图）和权利要求书，或者外观设计专利申请的请求书、外观设计的图片或者照片和简要说明后，应当明确申请日、给予申请号，并通知申请人。

**Article 43** After receiving the request, the description (in the case of a utility model, drawings must be included) and the claims of an application for a patent for invention or utility model, or the request, the drawings or photographs and a brief description of the design of an application for a patent for design, the patent administration department under the State Council shall determine the date of filing, grant an application number and notify the applicant.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　专利申请文件有下列情形之一的，国务院专利行政部门不予受理，并通知申请人：  
（一）发明或者实用新型专利申请缺少请求书、说明书（实用新型无附图）或者权利要求书的，或者外观设计专利申请缺少请求书、图片或者照片、简要说明的；  
（二）未使用中文的；  
（三）申请文件的格式不符合规定的；  
（四）请求书中缺少申请人姓名或者名称，或者缺少地址的；  
（五）明显不符合[专利法第十七条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-17)或者第十八条第一款的规定的；  
（六）专利申请类别（发明、实用新型或者外观设计）不明确或者难以确定的。

**Article 44** The patent administration department under the State Council shall not accept an application for a patent under any of the following circumstances, and shall notify the applicant:  
(1) an application for a patent for invention or utility model lacks a request, a description (a utility model without drawings) or claims, or an application for a patent for design lacks a request, drawings or photographs, or a brief description;  
(2) the Chinese language is not used;  
(3) the format of the application documents does not comply with the provisions;  
(4) the request lacks the name or title of the applicant, or lacks the address;  
(5) it clearly does not comply with the provisions of Article 17 or Article 18(1) of the Patent Law;  
(6) the category of the patent application (invention, utility model or design) is unclear or cannot be determined.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　发明或者实用新型专利申请缺少或者错误提交权利要求书、说明书或者权利要求书、说明书的部分内容，但申请人在递交日要求了优先权的，可以自递交日起2个月内或者在国务院专利行政部门指定的期限内以援引在先申请文件的方式补交。补交的文件符合有关规定的，以首次提交文件的递交日为申请日。

**Article 45** Where an application for a patent for invention or utility model lacks or incorrectly submits the claims, description or part of the claims or description, but the applicant has claimed priority at the date of filing, the applicant may, within two months from the date of filing or within a time limit specified by the patent administration department under the State Council, make a supplemental submission by incorporating the prior application documents by reference. If the documents as supplemented comply with the relevant provisions, the date of filing of the first submission shall be the date of filing.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　说明书中写有对附图的说明但无附图或者缺少部分附图的，申请人应当在国务院专利行政部门指定的期限内补交附图或者声明取消对附图的说明。申请人补交附图的，以向国务院专利行政部门提交或者邮寄附图之日为申请日；取消对附图的说明的，保留原申请日。

**Article 46** Where the description contains a description of the drawings but lacks the drawings or lacks part of the drawings, the applicant shall, within a time limit specified by the patent administration department under the State Council, submit the drawings or declare the cancellation of the description of the drawings. If the applicant submits the drawings, the date on which the drawings are submitted or mailed to the patent administration department under the State Council shall be the date of filing; if the description of the drawings is cancelled, the original date of filing shall be retained.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　两个以上的申请人同日（指申请日；有优先权的，指优先权日）分别就同样的发明创造申请专利的，应当在收到国务院专利行政部门的通知后自行协商确定申请人。  
同一申请人在同日（指申请日）对同样的发明创造既申请实用新型专利又申请发明专利的，应当在申请时分别说明对同样的发明创造已申请了另一专利；未作说明的，依照[专利法第九条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-9)第一款关于同样的发明创造只能授予一项专利权的规定处理。  
国务院专利行政部门公告授予实用新型专利权，应当公告申请人已依照本条第二款的规定同时申请了发明专利的说明。  
发明专利申请经审查没有发现驳回理由，国务院专利行政部门应当通知申请人在规定期限内声明放弃实用新型专利权。申请人声明放弃的，国务院专利行政部门应当作出授予发明专利权的决定，并在公告授予发明专利权时一并公告申请人放弃实用新型专利权声明。申请人不同意放弃的，国务院专利行政部门应当驳回该发明专利申请；申请人期满未答复的，视为撤回该发明专利申请。  
实用新型专利权自公告授予发明专利权之日起终止。

**Article 47** Where two or more applicants respectively apply for a patent for the same invention-creation on the same day (meaning the date of filing; in the case of priority, the date of priority), they shall, after receiving the notice from the patent administration department under the State Council, determine the applicant through consultation.  
Where the same applicant applies for both a utility model patent and an invention patent for the same invention-creation on the same day (meaning the date of filing), the applicant shall state separately at the time of filing that another patent has already been applied for the same invention-creation; if no such statement is made, the provisions of Article 9(1) of the Patent Law that only one patent right may be granted for the same invention-creation shall apply.  
Where the patent administration department under the State Council announces the grant of the utility model patent right, it shall announce the statement of the applicant that he/she has simultaneously applied for the invention patent in accordance with paragraph 2 of this Article.  
If, after examination, no grounds for rejection are found in the invention patent application, the patent administration department under the State Council shall notify the applicant to declare the abandonment of the utility model patent right within a prescribed time limit. If the applicant makes the declaration of abandonment, the patent administration department under the State Council shall make a decision to grant the invention patent right, and shall announce the declaration of abandonment of the utility model patent right by the applicant at the same time as announcing the grant of the invention patent right. If the applicant does not agree to abandon the utility model patent right, the patent administration department under the State Council shall reject the invention patent application; if the applicant fails to respond within the time limit, the invention patent application shall be deemed to have been withdrawn.  
The utility model patent right shall terminate as from the date of announcement of the grant of the invention patent right.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　一件专利申请包括两项以上发明、实用新型或者外观设计的，申请人可以在本细则第六十条第一款规定的期限届满前，向国务院专利行政部门提出分案申请；但是，专利申请已经被驳回、撤回或者视为撤回的，不能提出分案申请。  
国务院专利行政部门认为一件专利申请不符合[专利法第三十一条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-31)和本细则第三十九条或者第四十条的规定的，应当通知申请人在指定期限内对其申请进行修改；申请人期满未答复的，该申请视为撤回。  
分案的申请不得改变原申请的类别。

**Article 48** Where a patent application includes two or more inventions, utility models or designs, the applicant may, before the expiration of the time limit prescribed in Article 60(1) of these Rules, file a divisional application with the patent administration department under the State Council; however, if the patent application has been rejected, withdrawn or deemed to have been withdrawn, no divisional application may be filed.  
Where the patent administration department under the State Council considers that a patent application does not comply with the provisions of Article 31 of the Patent Law and Article 39 or Article 40 of these Rules, it shall notify the applicant to amend the application within a specified time limit; if the applicant fails to respond within the time limit, the application shall be deemed to have been withdrawn.  
A divisional application shall not change the category of the original application.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　依照本细则第四十八条规定提出的分案申请，可以保留原申请日，享有优先权的，可以保留优先权日，但是不得超出原申请记载的范围。  
分案申请应当依照专利法及本细则的规定办理有关手续。  
分案申请的请求书中应当写明原申请的申请号和申请日。

**Article 49** A divisional application filed in accordance with Article 48 of these Rules may retain the original date of filing; if priority is claimed, the date of priority may be retained, but it shall not go beyond the scope of the original application as recorded.  
A divisional application shall go through the relevant formalities in accordance with the Patent Law and these Rules.  
The request of a divisional application shall state the application number and the date of filing of the original application.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　[专利法第三十四条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-34)和第四十条所称初步审查，是指审查专利申请是否具备[专利法第二十六条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-26)或者第二十七条规定的文件和其他必要的文件，这些文件是否符合规定的格式，并审查下列各项：  
（一）发明专利申请是否明显属于[专利法第五条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-5)、第二十五条规定的情形，是否不符合[专利法第十七条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-17)、第十八条第一款、第十九条第一款或者本细则第十一条、第十九条、第二十九条第二款的规定，是否明显不符合[专利法第二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-2)第二款、第二十六条第五款、第三十一条第一款、第三十三条或者本细则第二十条至第二十四条的规定；  
（二）实用新型专利申请是否明显属于[专利法第五条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-5)、第二十五条规定的情形，是否不符合[专利法第十七条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-17)、第十八条第一款、第十九条第一款或者本细则第十一条、第十九条至第二十二条、第二十四条至第二十六条的规定，是否明显不符合[专利法第二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-2)第三款、第二十二条、第二十六条第三款、第二十六条第四款、第三十一条第一款、第三十三条或者本细则第二十三条、第四十九条第一款的规定，是否依照[专利法第九条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-9)规定不能取得专利权；  
（三）外观设计专利申请是否明显属于[专利法第五条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-5)、第二十五条第一款第（六）项规定的情形，是否不符合[专利法第十七条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-17)、第十八条第一款或者本细则第十一条、第十九条、第三十条、第三十一条的规定，是否明显不符合[专利法第二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-2)第四款、第二十三条第一款、第二十三条第二款、第二十七条第二款、第三十一条第二款、第三十三条或者本细则第四十九条第一款的规定，是否依照[专利法第九条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-9)规定不能取得专利权；  
（四）申请文件是否符合本细则第二条、第三条第一款的规定。  
国务院专利行政部门应当将审查意见通知申请人，要求其在指定期限内陈述意见或者补正；申请人期满未答复的，其申请视为撤回。申请人陈述意见或者补正后，国务院专利行政部门仍然认为不符合前款所列各项规定的，应当予以驳回。

**Article 50** The preliminary examination referred to in Articles 34 and 40 of the Patent Law means examining whether a patent application is provided with the documents specified in Article 26 or Article 27 of the Patent Law and other necessary documents, whether such documents are in the prescribed form, and also examining the following:  
(1) whether the application for a patent for invention clearly falls under the circumstances specified in Article 5 or Article 25 of the Patent Law, whether it does not comply with the provisions of Article 17, Article 18(1), Article 19(1) of the Patent Law or Article 11, Article 19, Article 29(2) of these Rules, and whether it clearly does not comply with the provisions of Article 2(2), Article 26(5), Article 31(1), Article 33 of the Patent Law or Articles 20 to 24 of these Rules;  
(2) whether the application for a patent for utility model clearly falls under the circumstances specified in Article 5 or Article 25 of the Patent Law, whether it does not comply with the provisions of Article 17, Article 18(1), Article 19(1) of the Patent Law or Articles 11, 19 to 22, 24 to 26 of these Rules, whether it clearly does not comply with the provisions of Article 2(3), Article 22, Article 26(3), Article 26(4), Article 31(1), Article 33 of the Patent Law or Articles 23, 49(1) of these Rules, and whether patent right cannot be granted pursuant to Article 9 of the Patent Law;  
(3) whether the application for a patent for design clearly falls under the circumstances specified in Article 5 or Article 25(1)(6) of the Patent Law, whether it does not comply with the provisions of Article 17, Article 18(1) of the Patent Law or Articles 11, 19, 30, 31 of these Rules, whether it clearly does not comply with the provisions of Article 2(4), Article 23(1), Article 23(2), Article 27(2), Article 31(2), Article 33 of the Patent Law or Article 49(1) of these Rules, and whether patent right cannot be granted pursuant to Article 9 of the Patent Law;  
(4) whether the application documents comply with the provisions of Articles 2 and 3(1) of these Rules.  
The patent administration department under the State Council shall notify the applicant of the examination opinion, requesting him/her to state observations or make corrections within a specified time limit; if the applicant fails to respond within the time limit, the application shall be deemed to have been withdrawn. After the applicant states observations or makes corrections, if the patent administration department under the State Council still considers that the application does not comply with any of the provisions listed in the preceding paragraph, it shall reject the application.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　除专利申请文件外，申请人向国务院专利行政部门提交的与专利申请有关的其他文件有下列情形之一的，视为未提交：  
（一）未使用规定的格式或者填写不符合规定的；  
（二）未按照规定提交证明材料的。  
国务院专利行政部门应当将视为未提交的审查意见通知申请人。

**Article 51** Except for the patent application documents, other documents related to the patent application submitted by the applicant to the patent administrative department of the State Council in any of the following circumstances shall be deemed not to have been submitted:  
(1) the prescribed format is not used or the filling does not comply with the provisions; or  
(2) the certifying materials are not submitted in accordance with the provisions.  
The patent administrative department of the State Council shall notify the applicant of the examination opinion that the documents are deemed not to have been submitted.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　申请人请求早日公布其发明专利申请的，应当向国务院专利行政部门声明。国务院专利行政部门对该申请进行初步审查后，除予以驳回的外，应当立即将申请予以公布。

**Article 52** Where an applicant requests early publication of the patent application for invention, it shall make a declaration to the patent administrative department of the State Council. After conducting preliminary examination of the application, the patent administrative department of the State Council shall immediately publish the application, unless the application is to be rejected.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　申请人写明使用外观设计的产品及其所属类别的，应当使用国务院专利行政部门公布的外观设计产品分类表。未写明使用外观设计的产品所属类别或者所写的类别不确切的，国务院专利行政部门可以予以补充或者修改。

**Article 53** Where the applicant specifies the product adopting the design and its classification, it shall use the classification table for design products published by the patent administrative department of the State Council. If the classification of the product adopting the design is not specified or the specified classification is inaccurate, the patent administrative department of the State Council may supplement or modify it.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　自发明专利申请公布之日起至公告授予专利权之日止，任何人均可以对不符合专利法规定的专利申请向国务院专利行政部门提出意见，并说明理由。

**Article 54** From the date of publication of the patent application for invention to the date of announcement of the grant of patent right, any person may submit observations to the patent administrative department of the State Council on a patent application that does not comply with the provisions of the Patent Law, and shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　发明专利申请人因有正当理由无法提交[专利法第三十六条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-36)规定的检索资料或者审查结果资料的，应当向国务院专利行政部门声明，并在得到有关资料后补交。

**Article 55** Where the applicant for a patent for invention is unable, for legitimate reasons, to submit the search materials or the examination result materials as provided in Article 36 of the Patent Law, it shall declare this to the patent administrative department of the State Council, and shall supplement the materials after obtaining them.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国务院专利行政部门依照[专利法第三十五条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-35)第二款的规定对专利申请自行进行审查时，应当通知申请人。  
申请人可以对专利申请提出延迟审查请求。

**Article 56** Where the patent administrative department of the State Council examines a patent application on its own initiative in accordance with the provisions of Article 35, paragraph 2 of the Patent Law, it shall notify the applicant.  
The applicant may file a request for deferment of examination of the patent application.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　发明专利申请人在提出实质审查请求时以及在收到国务院专利行政部门发出的发明专利申请进入实质审查阶段通知书之日起的3个月内，可以对发明专利申请主动提出修改。  
实用新型或者外观设计专利申请人自申请日起2个月内，可以对实用新型或者外观设计专利申请主动提出修改。  
申请人在收到国务院专利行政部门发出的审查意见通知书后对专利申请文件进行修改的，应当针对通知书指出的缺陷进行修改。  
国务院专利行政部门可以自行修改专利申请文件中文字和符号的明显错误。国务院专利行政部门自行修改的，应当通知申请人。

**Article 57** The applicant for a patent for invention may, when filing a request for substantive examination and within three months from the date of receipt of the notice issued by the patent administrative department of the State Council indicating that the patent application for invention has entered the substantive examination phase, make voluntary amendments to the patent application for invention.  
The applicant for a patent for utility model or design may, within two months from the date of filing, make voluntary amendments to the patent application for utility model or design.  
Where the applicant amends the patent application documents after receiving the examination opinion notice issued by the patent administrative department of the State Council, the amendments shall be made in response to the defects pointed out in the notice.  
The patent administrative department of the State Council may, on its own initiative, correct obvious errors in the text and symbols in the patent application documents. If the patent administrative department of the State Council makes such corrections on its own initiative, it shall notify the applicant.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　发明或者实用新型专利申请的说明书或者权利要求书的修改部分，除个别文字修改或者增删外，应当按照规定格式提交替换页。外观设计专利申请的图片或者照片的修改，应当按照规定提交替换页。

**Article 58** For amendments to the description or claims of a patent application for invention or utility model, replacement pages shall be submitted in the prescribed format, unless the amendments consist only of individual word changes or additions or deletions. For amendments to the drawings or photographs of a patent application for design, replacement pages shall be submitted as prescribed.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　依照[专利法第三十八条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-38)的规定，发明专利申请经实质审查应当予以驳回的情形是指：  
（一）申请属于[专利法第五条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-5)、第二十五条规定的情形，或者依照[专利法第九条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-9)规定不能取得专利权的；  
（二）申请不符合[专利法第二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-2)第二款、第十九条第一款、第二十二条、第二十六条第三款、第二十六条第四款、第二十六条第五款、第三十一条第一款或者本细则第十一条、第二十三条第二款规定的；  
（三）申请的修改不符合[专利法第三十三条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-33)规定，或者分案的申请不符合本细则第四十九条第一款的规定的。

**Article 59** In accordance with Article 38 of the Patent Law, the circumstances under which a patent application for invention shall be rejected after substantive examination mean:  
(1) the application falls under the circumstances specified in Article 5 or Article 25 of the Patent Law, or the patent right may not be granted in accordance with Article 9 of the Patent Law;  
(2) the application does not comply with the provisions of Article 2, paragraph 2, Article 19, paragraph 1, Article 22, Article 26, paragraph 3, Article 26, paragraph 4, Article 26, paragraph 5, Article 31, paragraph 1 of the Patent Law, or Article 11 or Article 23, paragraph 2 of these Rules; or  
(3) the amendment to the application does not comply with Article 33 of the Patent Law, or the divisional application does not comply with the provisions of Article 49, paragraph 1 of these Rules.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国务院专利行政部门发出授予专利权的通知后，申请人应当自收到通知之日起2个月内办理登记手续。申请人按期办理登记手续的，国务院专利行政部门应当授予专利权，颁发专利证书，并予以公告。  
期满未办理登记手续的，视为放弃取得专利权的权利。

**Article 60** After the patent administrative department of the State Council issues the notice of grant of patent right, the applicant shall go through the registration formalities within two months from the date of receipt of the notice. If the applicant goes through the registration formalities on time, the patent administrative department of the State Council shall grant the patent right, issue the patent certificate, and announce the grant.  
If the applicant fails to go through the registration formalities upon expiration of the time limit, it shall be deemed to have abandoned the right to obtain the patent right.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　保密专利申请经审查没有发现驳回理由的，国务院专利行政部门应当作出授予保密专利权的决定，颁发保密专利证书，登记保密专利权的有关事项。

**Article 61** If no reason for rejection is found after examination of a confidential patent application, the patent administration department under the State Council shall make a decision to grant the confidential patent right, issue a confidential patent certificate, and register the relevant matters of the confidential patent right.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　授予实用新型或者外观设计专利权的决定公告后，[专利法第六十六条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-66)规定的专利权人、利害关系人、被控侵权人可以请求国务院专利行政部门作出专利权评价报告。申请人可以在办理专利权登记手续时请求国务院专利行政部门作出专利权评价报告。  
请求作出专利权评价报告的，应当提交专利权评价报告请求书，写明专利申请号或者专利号。每项请求应当限于一项专利申请或者专利权。  
专利权评价报告请求书不符合规定的，国务院专利行政部门应当通知请求人在指定期限内补正；请求人期满未补正的，视为未提出请求。

**Article 62** After the announcement of the decision to grant a utility model or design patent right, the patentee, interested parties, and alleged infringer as specified in Article 66 of the Patent Law may request the patent administration department under the State Council to make a patent right evaluation report. The applicant may request the patent administration department under the State Council to make a patent right evaluation report when going through the formalities for patent registration.  
A request for a patent right evaluation report shall be accompanied by a written request for a patent right evaluation report, stating the patent application number or patent number. Each request shall be limited to one patent application or patent right.  
If the written request for a patent right evaluation report does not comply with the provisions, the patent administration department under the State Council shall notify the requestor to make corrections within a specified time limit; if the requestor fails to make corrections within the time limit, the request shall be deemed not to have been made.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国务院专利行政部门应当自收到专利权评价报告请求书后2个月内作出专利权评价报告，但申请人在办理专利权登记手续时请求作出专利权评价报告的，国务院专利行政部门应当自公告授予专利权之日起2个月内作出专利权评价报告。  
对同一项实用新型或者外观设计专利权，有多个请求人请求作出专利权评价报告的，国务院专利行政部门仅作出一份专利权评价报告。任何单位或者个人可以查阅或者复制该专利权评价报告。

**Article 63** The patent administration department under the State Council shall make a patent right evaluation report within two months from the date of receipt of the written request for a patent right evaluation report; however, if the applicant requests the evaluation report when going through the formalities for patent registration, the patent administration department under the State Council shall make the evaluation report within two months from the date of announcement of the grant of the patent right.  
If multiple requestors request a patent right evaluation report for the same utility model or design patent right, the patent administration department under the State Council shall make only one patent right evaluation report. Any entity or individual may consult or reproduce the patent right evaluation report.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　国务院专利行政部门对专利公告、专利单行本中出现的错误，一经发现，应当及时更正，并对所作更正予以公告。

**Article 64** The patent administration department under the State Council shall, upon discovering any errors in the patent announcements or patent specifications, promptly correct them and announce the corrections.

### 第四章　专利申请的复审与专利权的无效宣告

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　依照[专利法第四十一条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-41)的规定向国务院专利行政部门请求复审的，应当提交复审请求书，说明理由，必要时还应当附具有关证据。  
复审请求不符合[专利法第十八条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-18)第一款或者第四十一条第一款规定的，国务院专利行政部门不予受理，书面通知复审请求人并说明理由。  
复审请求书不符合规定格式的，复审请求人应当在国务院专利行政部门指定的期限内补正；期满未补正的，该复审请求视为未提出。

**Article 65** A request for reexamination made to the patent administration department under the State Council in accordance with Article 41 of the Patent Law shall be accompanied by a written request for reexamination, stating the reasons, and if necessary, also attaching relevant evidence.  
If the request for reexamination does not comply with the provisions of Article 18, paragraph 1, or Article 41, paragraph 1, of the Patent Law, the patent administration department under the State Council shall not accept it, and shall notify the person requesting reexamination in writing and state the reasons.  
If the written request for reexamination does not comply with the prescribed format, the person requesting reexamination shall make corrections within the time limit specified by the patent administration department under the State Council; if no corrections are made within the time limit, the request for reexamination shall be deemed not to have been made.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　请求人在提出复审请求或者在对国务院专利行政部门的复审通知书作出答复时，可以修改专利申请文件；但是，修改应当仅限于消除驳回决定或者复审通知书指出的缺陷。

**Article 66** When filing a request for reexamination or responding to the notice of reexamination from the patent administration department under the State Council, the requestor may amend the patent application documents; however, the amendment shall be limited to eliminating the defects pointed out in the decision of rejection or the notice of reexamination.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　国务院专利行政部门进行复审后，认为复审请求不符合专利法和本细则有关规定或者专利申请存在其他明显违反专利法和本细则有关规定情形的，应当通知复审请求人，要求其在指定期限内陈述意见。期满未答复的，该复审请求视为撤回；经陈述意见或者进行修改后，国务院专利行政部门认为仍不符合专利法和本细则有关规定的，应当作出驳回复审请求的复审决定。  
国务院专利行政部门进行复审后，认为原驳回决定不符合专利法和本细则有关规定的，或者认为经过修改的专利申请文件消除了原驳回决定和复审通知书指出的缺陷的，应当撤销原驳回决定，继续进行审查程序。

**Article 67** After conducting the reexamination, if the patent administration department under the State Council considers that the request for reexamination does not comply with the relevant provisions of the Patent Law and these Rules, or that the patent application has other obvious violations of the relevant provisions of the Patent Law and these Rules, it shall notify the person requesting reexamination and require him to state his opinions within a specified time limit. If no response is made within the time limit, the request for reexamination shall be deemed withdrawn; after the opinions are stated or amendments are made, if the patent administration department under the State Council still considers that it does not comply with the relevant provisions of the Patent Law and these Rules, it shall make a reexamination decision rejecting the request for reexamination.  
After conducting the reexamination, if the patent administration department under the State Council considers that the original decision of rejection does not comply with the relevant provisions of the Patent Law and these Rules, or that the amended patent application documents have eliminated the defects pointed out in the original decision of rejection and the notice of reexamination, it shall revoke the original decision of rejection and continue the examination procedure.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　复审请求人在国务院专利行政部门作出决定前，可以撤回其复审请求。  
复审请求人在国务院专利行政部门作出决定前撤回其复审请求的，复审程序终止。

**Article 68** Before the patent administration department under the State Council makes a decision, the person requesting reexamination may withdraw the request for reexamination.  
If the person requesting reexamination withdraws the request for reexamination before the patent administration department under the State Council makes a decision, the reexamination procedure shall terminate.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　依照[专利法第四十五条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-45)的规定，请求宣告专利权无效或者部分无效的，应当向国务院专利行政部门提交专利权无效宣告请求书和必要的证据一式两份。无效宣告请求书应当结合提交的所有证据，具体说明无效宣告请求的理由，并指明每项理由所依据的证据。  
前款所称无效宣告请求的理由，是指被授予专利的发明创造不符合[专利法第二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-2)、第十九条第一款、第二十二条、第二十三条、第二十六条第三款、第二十六条第四款、第二十七条第二款、第三十三条或者本细则第十一条、第二十三条第二款、第四十九条第一款的规定，或者属于[专利法第五条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-5)、第二十五条规定的情形，或者依照[专利法第九条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-9)规定不能取得专利权。

**Article 69** To request invalidation or partial invalidation of a patent right in accordance with Article 45 of the Patent Law, a written request for invalidation of the patent right and necessary evidence shall be submitted to the patent administration department under the State Council in duplicate. The written request for invalidation shall, in conjunction with all the evidence submitted, specify the reasons for the invalidation request and indicate the evidence on which each reason is based.  
The reasons for the invalidation request referred to in the preceding paragraph refer to the circumstances where the granted invention-creation does not comply with Article 2, Article 19, paragraph 1, Article 22, Article 23, Article 26, paragraph 3, Article 26, paragraph 4, Article 27, paragraph 2, or Article 33 of the Patent Law, or Article 11, Article 23, paragraph 2, or Article 49, paragraph 1 of these Rules, or falls under the circumstances specified in Article 5 or Article 25 of the Patent Law, or cannot be granted a patent right in accordance with Article 9 of the Patent Law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　专利权无效宣告请求不符合[专利法第十八条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-18)第一款或者本细则第六十九条规定的，国务院专利行政部门不予受理。  
在国务院专利行政部门就无效宣告请求作出决定之后，又以同样的理由和证据请求无效宣告的，国务院专利行政部门不予受理。  
以不符合[专利法第二十三条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-23)第三款的规定为理由请求宣告外观设计专利权无效，但是未提交证明权利冲突的证据的，国务院专利行政部门不予受理。  
专利权无效宣告请求书不符合规定格式的，无效宣告请求人应当在国务院专利行政部门指定的期限内补正；期满未补正的，该无效宣告请求视为未提出。

**Article 70** If a request for invalidation of a patent right does not comply with Article 18, paragraph 1 of the Patent Law or Article 69 of these Rules, the patent administration department under the State Council shall not accept it.  
After the patent administration department under the State Council has made a decision on the invalidation request, if a request for invalidation is filed again on the same grounds and with the same evidence, the patent administration department under the State Council shall not accept it.  
If a request for invalidation of a design patent right is filed on the ground of non-compliance with the provisions of Article 23, paragraph 3 of the Patent Law, but no evidence proving the conflict of rights is submitted, the patent administration department under the State Council shall not accept it.  
If the written request for invalidation of a patent right does not comply with the prescribed format, the person requesting invalidation shall make corrections within the time limit specified by the patent administration department under the State Council; if no corrections are made within the time limit, the request for invalidation shall be deemed not to have been made.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　在国务院专利行政部门受理无效宣告请求后，请求人可以在提出无效宣告请求之日起1个月内增加理由或者补充证据。逾期增加理由或者补充证据的，国务院专利行政部门可以不予考虑。

**Article 71** After the patent administrative department of the State Council accepts a request for invalidation declaration, the requester may add reasons or supplement evidence within one month from the date of filing the request for invalidation declaration. If the reasons are added or evidence is supplemented beyond the time limit, the patent administrative department of the State Council may disregard them.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　国务院专利行政部门应当将专利权无效宣告请求书和有关文件的副本送交专利权人，要求其在指定的期限内陈述意见。  
专利权人和无效宣告请求人应当在指定期限内答复国务院专利行政部门发出的转送文件通知书或者无效宣告请求审查通知书；期满未答复的，不影响国务院专利行政部门审理。

**Article 72** The patent administrative department of the State Council shall serve a copy of the request for invalidation declaration of patent right and relevant documents to the patentee, requiring it to present its opinions within a specified time limit.  
The patentee and the requester for invalidation declaration shall reply to the notification of forwarding documents or the notification of examination of the request for invalidation declaration issued by the patent administrative department of the State Council within the specified time limit; failure to reply within the time limit shall not affect the examination by the patent administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　在无效宣告请求的审查过程中，发明或者实用新型专利的专利权人可以修改其权利要求书，但是不得扩大原专利的保护范围。国务院专利行政部门在修改后的权利要求基础上作出维持专利权有效或者宣告专利权部分无效的决定的，应当公告修改后的权利要求。  
发明或者实用新型专利的专利权人不得修改专利说明书和附图，外观设计专利的专利权人不得修改图片、照片和简要说明。

**Article 73** During the examination of the request for invalidation declaration, the patentee of an invention or utility model patent may amend its claims, but shall not expand the protection scope of the original patent. If the patent administrative department of the State Council makes a decision to maintain the patent right valid or declare the patent right partially invalid based on the amended claims, it shall announce the amended claims.  
The patentee of an invention or utility model patent shall not amend the patent specification and drawings, and the patentee of a design patent shall not amend the pictures, photographs, and brief description.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　国务院专利行政部门根据当事人的请求或者案情需要，可以决定对无效宣告请求进行口头审理。  
国务院专利行政部门决定对无效宣告请求进行口头审理的，应当向当事人发出口头审理通知书，告知举行口头审理的日期和地点。当事人应当在通知书指定的期限内作出答复。  
无效宣告请求人对国务院专利行政部门发出的口头审理通知书在指定的期限内未作答复，并且不参加口头审理的，其无效宣告请求视为撤回；专利权人不参加口头审理的，可以缺席审理。

**Article 74** The patent administrative department of the State Council may, at the request of a party or as required by the circumstances of the case, decide to conduct an oral hearing for the request for invalidation declaration.  
If the patent administrative department of the State Council decides to conduct an oral hearing for the request for invalidation declaration, it shall issue a notice of oral hearing to the parties, informing them of the date and place of the oral hearing. The parties shall make a reply within the time limit specified in the notice.  
If the requester for invalidation declaration fails to reply to the notice of oral hearing issued by the patent administrative department of the State Council within the specified time limit and does not participate in the oral hearing, its request for invalidation declaration shall be deemed withdrawn; if the patentee does not participate in the oral hearing, the case may be heard by default.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　在无效宣告请求审查程序中，国务院专利行政部门指定的期限不得延长。

**Article 75** During the examination procedure of the request for invalidation declaration, the time limit specified by the patent administrative department of the State Council shall not be extended.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　国务院专利行政部门对无效宣告的请求作出决定前，无效宣告请求人可以撤回其请求。  
国务院专利行政部门作出决定之前，无效宣告请求人撤回其请求或者其无效宣告请求被视为撤回的，无效宣告请求审查程序终止。但是，国务院专利行政部门认为根据已进行的审查工作能够作出宣告专利权无效或者部分无效的决定的，不终止审查程序。

**Article 76** Before the patent administrative department of the State Council makes a decision on the request for invalidation declaration, the requester for invalidation declaration may withdraw its request.  
If, before the patent administrative department of the State Council makes a decision, the requester for invalidation declaration withdraws its request or its request for invalidation declaration is deemed withdrawn, the examination procedure of the request for invalidation declaration shall terminate. However, if the patent administrative department of the State Council considers that it can make a decision to declare the patent right invalid or partially invalid based on the examination work that has been conducted, the examination procedure shall not terminate.

### 第五章　专利权期限补偿

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　依照[专利法第四十二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-42)第二款的规定请求给予专利权期限补偿的，专利权人应当自公告授予专利权之日起3个月内向国务院专利行政部门提出。

**Article 77** Where a request is made for patent term compensation in accordance with the provisions of Article 42, paragraph 2 of the Patent Law, the patentee shall file a request with the patent administrative department of the State Council within three months from the date of announcement of the grant of the patent right.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　依照[专利法第四十二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-42)第二款的规定给予专利权期限补偿的，补偿期限按照发明专利在授权过程中不合理延迟的实际天数计算。  
前款所称发明专利在授权过程中不合理延迟的实际天数，是指自发明专利申请日起满4年且自实质审查请求之日起满3年之日至公告授予专利权之日的间隔天数，减去合理延迟的天数和由申请人引起的不合理延迟的天数。  
下列情形属于合理延迟：  
（一）依照本细则第六十六条的规定修改专利申请文件后被授予专利权的，因复审程序引起的延迟；  
（二）因本细则第一百零三条、第一百零四条规定情形引起的延迟；  
（三）其他合理情形引起的延迟。  
同一申请人同日对同样的发明创造既申请实用新型专利又申请发明专利，依照本细则第四十七条第四款的规定取得发明专利权的，该发明专利权的期限不适用[专利法第四十二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-42)第二款的规定。

**Article 78** Where patent term compensation is granted in accordance with the provisions of Article 42, paragraph 2 of the Patent Law, the compensation term shall be calculated based on the actual number of days of unreasonable delay during the granting process of the invention patent.  
The actual number of days of unreasonable delay during the granting process of the invention patent as mentioned in the preceding paragraph refers to the number of days from the date when four years have elapsed from the date of filing of the invention patent application and three years have elapsed from the date of filing the request for substantive examination to the date of announcement of the grant of the patent right, minus the number of days of reasonable delay and the number of days of unreasonable delay caused by the applicant.  
The following circumstances shall be considered reasonable delay:  
(1) delay caused by the re-examination procedure where the patent is granted after amendment of the patent application documents in accordance with Article 66 of these Rules;  
(2) delay caused by the circumstances specified in Article 103 and Article 104 of these Rules;  
(3) delay caused by other reasonable circumstances.  
Where the same applicant files both a utility model patent application and an invention patent application for the same invention on the same day, and obtains the invention patent right in accordance with Article 47, paragraph 4 of these Rules, the term of the invention patent right shall not be subject to the provisions of Article 42, paragraph 2 of the Patent Law.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　[专利法第四十二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-42)第二款规定的由申请人引起的不合理延迟包括以下情形：  
（一）未在指定期限内答复国务院专利行政部门发出的通知；  
（二）申请延迟审查；  
（三）因本细则第四十五条规定情形引起的延迟；  
（四）其他由申请人引起的不合理延迟。

**Article 79** The unreasonable delay caused by the applicant as provided in Article 42, paragraph 2 of the Patent Law includes the following circumstances:  
(1) failure to reply to a notice issued by the patent administrative department of the State Council within the specified time limit;  
(2) requesting delayed examination;  
(3) delay caused by the circumstances specified in Article 45 of these Rules;  
(4) other unreasonable delay caused by the applicant.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　[专利法第四十二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-42)第三款所称新药相关发明专利是指符合规定的新药产品专利、制备方法专利、医药用途专利。

**Article 80** The new drug-related invention patent as mentioned in Article 42, paragraph 3 of the Patent Law refers to patents for new drug products, preparation methods, and pharmaceutical uses that comply with the provisions.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　依照[专利法第四十二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-42)第三款的规定请求给予新药相关发明专利权期限补偿的，应当符合下列要求，自该新药在中国获得上市许可之日起3个月内向国务院专利行政部门提出：  
（一）该新药同时存在多项专利的，专利权人只能请求对其中一项专利给予专利权期限补偿；  
（二）一项专利同时涉及多个新药的，只能对一个新药就该专利提出专利权期限补偿请求；  
（三）该专利在有效期内，且尚未获得过新药相关发明专利权期限补偿。

**Article 81** A request for patent term compensation for a new drug-related invention patent in accordance with the provisions of Article 42(3) of the Patent Law shall meet the following requirements and shall be submitted to the patent administrative department of the State Council within three months from the date the new drug obtains marketing authorization in China:  
(1) where the new drug is covered by multiple patents, the patentee may only request patent term compensation for one of those patents;  
(2) where a patent covers multiple new drugs, only one request for patent term compensation may be filed for that patent in respect of one new drug;  
(3) the patent is within its term and has not previously been granted patent term compensation for a new drug-related invention.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　依照[专利法第四十二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-42)第三款的规定给予专利权期限补偿的，补偿期限按照该专利申请日至该新药在中国获得上市许可之日的间隔天数减去5年，在符合[专利法第四十二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-42)第三款规定的基础上确定。

**Article 82** Where patent term compensation is granted in accordance with the provisions of Article 42(3) of the Patent Law, the compensation term shall be determined by subtracting five years from the number of days between the patent application date and the date on which the new drug obtains marketing authorization in China, on the basis of compliance with the provisions of Article 42(3) of the Patent Law.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　新药相关发明专利在专利权期限补偿期间，该专利的保护范围限于该新药及其经批准的适应症相关技术方案；在保护范围内，专利权人享有的权利和承担的义务与专利权期限补偿前相同。

**Article 83** During the patent term compensation period for a new drug-related invention patent, the scope of protection of the patent shall be limited to the technical solutions related to the new drug and its approved indications; within the scope of protection, the rights enjoyed and obligations assumed by the patentee shall be the same as before the patent term compensation.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　国务院专利行政部门对依照[专利法第四十二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-42)第二款、第三款的规定提出的专利权期限补偿请求进行审查后，认为符合补偿条件的，作出给予期限补偿的决定，并予以登记和公告；不符合补偿条件的，作出不予期限补偿的决定，并通知提出请求的专利权人。

**Article 84** After examining a request for patent term compensation filed in accordance with the provisions of Article 42(2) and (3) of the Patent Law, the patent administrative department of the State Council shall, if it considers that the compensation conditions are met, make a decision to grant term compensation, and shall register and announce it; if the compensation conditions are not met, it shall make a decision not to grant term compensation, and shall notify the patentee who filed the request.

### 第六章　专利实施的特别许可

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　专利权人自愿声明对其专利实行开放许可的，应当在公告授予专利权后提出。  
开放许可声明应当写明以下事项：  
（一）专利号；  
（二）专利权人的姓名或者名称；  
（三）专利许可使用费支付方式、标准；  
（四）专利许可期限；  
（五）其他需要明确的事项。  
开放许可声明内容应当准确、清楚，不得出现商业性宣传用语。

**Article 85** Where a patentee voluntarily declares an open license for its patent, it shall make the declaration after the announcement of the grant of the patent right.  
The open license declaration shall specify the following:  
(1) the patent number;  
(2) the name of the patentee;  
(3) the method and standard for payment of the patent license fee;  
(4) the term of the patent license;  
(5) other matters that need to be specified.  
The content of the open license declaration shall be accurate and clear, and shall not contain commercial promotional language.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　专利权有下列情形之一的，专利权人不得对其实行开放许可：  
（一）专利权处于独占或者排他许可有效期限内的；  
（二）属于本细则第一百零三条、第一百零四条规定的中止情形的；  
（三）没有按照规定缴纳年费的；  
（四）专利权被质押，未经质权人同意的；  
（五）其他妨碍专利权有效实施的情形。

**Article 86** A patentee shall not offer an open license for a patent under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the patent is within the term of an exclusive or sole license;  
(2) the patent falls under the suspension situations provided for in Articles 103 and 104 of these Rules;  
(3) the annual fee has not been paid as required;  
(4) the patent right is pledged without the consent of the pledgee;  
(5) other circumstances that hinder the effective exploitation of the patent right.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　通过开放许可达成专利实施许可的，专利权人或者被许可人应当凭能够证明达成许可的书面文件向国务院专利行政部门备案。

**Article 87** Where a patent exploitation license is concluded through an open license, the patentee or the licensee shall file with the patent administrative department of the State Council by presenting written documents proving the conclusion of the license.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　专利权人不得通过提供虚假材料、隐瞒事实等手段，作出开放许可声明或者在开放许可实施期间获得专利年费减免。

**Article 88** A patentee shall not, by means such as providing false materials or concealing facts, make an open license declaration or obtain a reduction of annual patent fees during the period of implementing the open license.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　[专利法第五十三条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-53)第（一）项所称未充分实施其专利，是指专利权人及其被许可人实施其专利的方式或者规模不能满足国内对专利产品或者专利方法的需求。  
[专利法第五十五条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-55)所称取得专利权的药品，是指解决公共健康问题所需的医药领域中的任何专利产品或者依照专利方法直接获得的产品，包括取得专利权的制造该产品所需的活性成分以及使用该产品所需的诊断用品。

**Article 89** The term "has not sufficiently exploited its patent" as used in Item (1) of Article 53 of the Patent Law means that the manner or scale of exploitation of the patent by the patentee or its licensee cannot satisfy the domestic demand for the patented product or patented process.  
The term "patented drug" as used in Article 55 of the Patent Law means any patented product in the pharmaceutical field needed to solve public health problems, or any product directly obtained by a patented process, including the patented active ingredient required for manufacturing such product and the diagnostic supplies required for using such product.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　请求给予强制许可的，应当向国务院专利行政部门提交强制许可请求书，说明理由并附具有关证明文件。  
国务院专利行政部门应当将强制许可请求书的副本送交专利权人，专利权人应当在国务院专利行政部门指定的期限内陈述意见；期满未答复的，不影响国务院专利行政部门作出决定。  
国务院专利行政部门在作出驳回强制许可请求的决定或者给予强制许可的决定前，应当通知请求人和专利权人拟作出的决定及其理由。  
国务院专利行政部门依照[专利法第五十五条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-55)的规定作出给予强制许可的决定，应当同时符合中国缔结或者参加的有关国际条约关于为了解决公共健康问题而给予强制许可的规定，但中国作出保留的除外。

**Article 90** Where a request for a compulsory license is made, a written request for a compulsory license shall be submitted to the patent administrative department of the State Council, stating the reasons and attaching relevant supporting documents.  
The patent administrative department of the State Council shall serve a copy of the written request for a compulsory license on the patentee, who shall present its opinions within the time limit specified by the patent administrative department of the State Council; failure to reply within the time limit shall not affect the decision of the patent administrative department of the State Council.  
Before making a decision to reject the request for a compulsory license or to grant a compulsory license, the patent administrative department of the State Council shall notify the requester and the patentee of the intended decision and the reasons therefor.  
Where the patent administrative department of the State Council makes a decision to grant a compulsory license in accordance with the provisions of Article 55 of the Patent Law, it shall also comply with the provisions of relevant international treaties concluded or acceded to by China concerning the grant of compulsory licenses for solving public health problems, unless China has made a reservation.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　依照[专利法第六十二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-62)的规定，请求国务院专利行政部门裁决使用费数额的，当事人应当提出裁决请求书，并附具双方不能达成协议的证明文件。国务院专利行政部门应当自收到请求书之日起3个月内作出裁决，并通知当事人。

**Article 91** Where, in accordance with the provisions of Article 62 of the Patent Law, a party requests the patent administrative department under the State Council to adjudicate the amount of royalties, the party shall submit a request for adjudication and attach evidence that the parties cannot reach an agreement. The patent administrative department under the State Council shall make an adjudication within three months from the date of receipt of the request and shall notify the parties.

### 第七章　对职务发明创造的发明人或者设计人的奖励和报酬

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　被授予专利权的单位可以与发明人、设计人约定或者在其依法制定的规章制度中规定[专利法第十五条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-15)规定的奖励、报酬的方式和数额。鼓励被授予专利权的单位实行产权激励，采取股权、期权、分红等方式，使发明人或者设计人合理分享创新收益。  
企业、事业单位给予发明人或者设计人的奖励、报酬，按照国家有关财务、会计制度的规定进行处理。

**Article 92** A unit that is granted a patent right may agree with the inventor or designer, or stipulate in its rules and regulations formulated according to law, the methods and amounts of rewards and remunerations as prescribed in Article 15 of the Patent Law. Units that are granted patent rights are encouraged to implement equity incentives, adopting methods such as stock rights, options, and profit sharing, so that the inventor or designer may reasonably share the innovation benefits.  
Rewards and remunerations given by enterprises and public institutions to inventors or designers shall be handled in accordance with the relevant state financial and accounting systems.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　被授予专利权的单位未与发明人、设计人约定也未在其依法制定的规章制度中规定[专利法第十五条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-15)规定的奖励的方式和数额的，应当自公告授予专利权之日起3个月内发给发明人或者设计人奖金。一项发明专利的奖金最低不少于4000元；一项实用新型专利或者外观设计专利的奖金最低不少于1500元。  
由于发明人或者设计人的建议被其所属单位采纳而完成的发明创造，被授予专利权的单位应当从优发给奖金。

**Article 93** Where a unit that is granted a patent right has neither agreed with the inventor or designer nor stipulated in its rules and regulations formulated according to law the methods and amounts of rewards as prescribed in Article 15 of the Patent Law, it shall, within three months from the date of announcement of the grant of the patent right, pay a bonus to the inventor or designer. The minimum bonus for an invention patent shall be no less than 4,000 yuan; the minimum bonus for a utility model patent or a design patent shall be no less than 1,500 yuan.  
For an invention-creation completed as a result of the adoption of a suggestion made by the inventor or designer by the unit to which he or she belongs, the unit that is granted the patent right shall give a higher bonus.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　被授予专利权的单位未与发明人、设计人约定也未在其依法制定的规章制度中规定[专利法第十五条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-15)规定的报酬的方式和数额的，应当依照《中华人民共和国促进科技成果转化法》的规定，给予发明人或者设计人合理的报酬。

**Article 94** Where a unit that is granted a patent right has neither agreed with the inventor or designer nor stipulated in its rules and regulations formulated according to law the methods and amounts of remunerations as prescribed in Article 15 of the Patent Law, it shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Promoting the Transformation of Scientific and Technological Achievements, give reasonable remuneration to the inventor or designer.

### 第八章　专利权的保护

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理专利工作的部门以及专利管理工作量大又有实际处理能力的地级市、自治州、盟、地区和直辖市的区人民政府管理专利工作的部门，可以处理和调解专利纠纷。

**Article 95** The patent administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, as well as the patent administration departments of the people's governments of prefecture-level cities, autonomous prefectures, leagues, prefectures, and districts of municipalities directly under the Central Government that have a heavy workload of patent administration and actual handling capacity, may handle and mediate patent disputes.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　有下列情形之一的，属于[专利法第七十条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-70)所称的在全国有重大影响的专利侵权纠纷：  
（一）涉及重大公共利益的；  
（二）对行业发展有重大影响的；  
（三）跨省、自治区、直辖市区域的重大案件；  
（四）国务院专利行政部门认为可能有重大影响的其他情形。  
专利权人或者利害关系人请求国务院专利行政部门处理专利侵权纠纷，相关案件不属于在全国有重大影响的专利侵权纠纷的，国务院专利行政部门可以指定有管辖权的地方人民政府管理专利工作的部门处理。

**Article 96** Any of the following circumstances shall constitute a patent infringement dispute that has a major impact nationwide as referred to in Article 70 of the Patent Law:  
(1) involving major public interests;  
(2) having a major impact on the development of the industry;  
(3) major cases spanning provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(4) other circumstances that the patent administrative department under the State Council deems may have a major impact.  
Where a patentee or an interested party requests the patent administrative department under the State Council to handle a patent infringement dispute, and the case is not a patent infringement dispute that has a major impact nationwide, the patent administrative department under the State Council may designate the local patent administration department of the people's government that has jurisdiction to handle it.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　当事人请求处理专利侵权纠纷或者调解专利纠纷的，由被请求人所在地或者侵权行为地的管理专利工作的部门管辖。  
两个以上管理专利工作的部门都有管辖权的专利纠纷，当事人可以向其中一个管理专利工作的部门提出请求；当事人向两个以上有管辖权的管理专利工作的部门提出请求的，由最先受理的管理专利工作的部门管辖。  
管理专利工作的部门对管辖权发生争议的，由其共同的上级人民政府管理专利工作的部门指定管辖；无共同上级人民政府管理专利工作的部门的，由国务院专利行政部门指定管辖。

**Article 97** Where a party requests handling of a patent infringement dispute or mediation of a patent dispute, the matter shall be under the jurisdiction of the patent administration department at the location of the respondent or the place where the infringing act occurred.  
Where two or more patent administration departments have jurisdiction over a patent dispute, the party may submit a request to any one of them; where the party submits requests to two or more patent administration departments that have jurisdiction, the matter shall be under the jurisdiction of the patent administration department that first accepts the request.  
Where a dispute arises over jurisdiction between patent administration departments, the matter shall be designated to the patent administration department of their common superior people's government; where there is no common superior people's government, the patent administrative department under the State Council shall designate the jurisdiction.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　在处理专利侵权纠纷过程中，被请求人提出无效宣告请求并被国务院专利行政部门受理的，可以请求管理专利工作的部门中止处理。  
管理专利工作的部门认为被请求人提出的中止理由明显不能成立的，可以不中止处理。

**Article 98** In the process of handling a patent infringement dispute, where the respondent files a request for invalidation and it is accepted by the patent administrative department under the State Council, the respondent may request the patent administration department to suspend the handling.  
Where the patent administration department considers that the reasons for suspension raised by the respondent are obviously untenable, it may not suspend the handling.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　专利权人依照[专利法第十六条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-16)的规定，在其专利产品或者该产品的包装上标明专利标识的，应当按照国务院专利行政部门规定的方式予以标明。  
专利标识不符合前款规定的，由县级以上负责专利执法的部门责令改正。

**Article 99** Where a patentee, in accordance with Article 16 of the Patent Law, marks a patent indication on a patented product or the packaging of such product, the indication shall be made in the manner prescribed by the patent administrative department under the State Council.  
Where a patent indication does not comply with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the patent law enforcement department at or above the county level shall order correction.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　申请人或者专利权人违反本细则第十一条、第八十八条规定的，由县级以上负责专利执法的部门予以警告，可以处10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 100** Where an applicant or a patentee violates the provisions of Articles 11 and 88 of these Rules, the patent law enforcement department at or above the county level shall issue a warning and may impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　下列行为属于[专利法第六十八条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-68)规定的假冒专利的行为：  
（一）在未被授予专利权的产品或者其包装上标注专利标识，专利权被宣告无效后或者终止后继续在产品或者其包装上标注专利标识，或者未经许可在产品或者产品包装上标注他人的专利号；  
（二）销售第（一）项所述产品；  
（三）在产品说明书等材料中将未被授予专利权的技术或者设计称为专利技术或者专利设计，将专利申请称为专利，或者未经许可使用他人的专利号，使公众将所涉及的技术或者设计误认为是专利技术或者专利设计；  
（四）伪造或者变造专利证书、专利文件或者专利申请文件；  
（五）其他使公众混淆，将未被授予专利权的技术或者设计误认为是专利技术或者专利设计的行为。  
专利权终止前依法在专利产品、依照专利方法直接获得的产品或者其包装上标注专利标识，在专利权终止后许诺销售、销售该产品的，不属于假冒专利行为。  
销售不知道是假冒专利的产品，并且能够证明该产品合法来源的，由县级以上负责专利执法的部门责令停止销售。

**Article 101** The following acts constitute acts of passing off patents as prescribed in Article 68 of the Patent Law:  
(1) affixing a patent mark on a product or its packaging that has not been granted a patent right, continuing to affix a patent mark on the product or its packaging after the patent right has been declared invalid or has ceased, or affixing another person's patent number on a product or its packaging without permission;  
(2) selling the product described in item (1);  
(3) in product manuals or other materials, calling a technology or design that has not been granted a patent right a patented technology or patented design, calling a patent application a patent, or using another person's patent number without permission, thereby causing the public to mistake the involved technology or design for a patented technology or patented design;  
(4) forging or altering a patent certificate, patent document, or patent application document;  
(5) other acts that confuse the public and cause them to mistake a technology or design that has not been granted a patent right for a patented technology or patented design.  
Where, before the patent right ceases, the patent mark is affixed according to law on a patented product, a product directly obtained by a patented method, or its packaging, and after the patent right ceases, the product is offered for sale or sold, such act shall not be considered an act of passing off a patent.  
If a person sells a product that is an act of passing off a patent without knowledge thereof, and can prove that the product has a legal source, the department responsible for patent law enforcement at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the sale.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　除[专利法第六十五条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-65)规定的外，管理专利工作的部门应当事人请求，可以对下列专利纠纷进行调解：  
（一）专利申请权和专利权归属纠纷；  
（二）发明人、设计人资格纠纷；  
（三）职务发明创造的发明人、设计人的奖励和报酬纠纷；  
（四）在发明专利申请公布后专利权授予前使用发明而未支付适当费用的纠纷；  
（五）其他专利纠纷。  
对于前款第（四）项所列的纠纷，当事人请求管理专利工作的部门调解的，应当在专利权被授予之后提出。

**Article 102** In addition to the circumstances prescribed in Article 65 of the Patent Law, the patent administration department may, upon the request of a party, mediate the following patent disputes:  
(1) dispute over the ownership of the right to apply for a patent and the patent right;  
(2) dispute over the qualification of the inventor or designer;  
(3) dispute over the reward and remuneration for the inventor or designer of a service invention-creation;  
(4) dispute over the use of an invention without paying appropriate fees after the publication of the invention patent application but before the grant of the patent right;  
(5) other patent disputes.  
With respect to the dispute listed in item (4) of the preceding paragraph, a party requesting mediation by the patent administration department shall file such request after the grant of the patent right.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　当事人因专利申请权或者专利权的归属发生纠纷，已请求管理专利工作的部门调解或者向人民法院起诉的，可以请求国务院专利行政部门中止有关程序。  
依照前款规定请求中止有关程序的，应当向国务院专利行政部门提交请求书，说明理由，并附具管理专利工作的部门或者人民法院的写明申请号或者专利号的有关受理文件副本。国务院专利行政部门认为当事人提出的中止理由明显不能成立的，可以不中止有关程序。  
管理专利工作的部门作出的调解书或者人民法院作出的判决生效后，当事人应当向国务院专利行政部门办理恢复有关程序的手续。自请求中止之日起1年内，有关专利申请权或者专利权归属的纠纷未能结案，需要继续中止有关程序的，请求人应当在该期限内请求延长中止。期满未请求延长的，国务院专利行政部门自行恢复有关程序。

**Article 103** Where a party has a dispute over the ownership of the right to apply for a patent or the patent right, and has requested mediation by the patent administration department or filed a prosecution with a People's Court, the party may request the patent administrative department under the State Council to suspend the relevant procedures.  
Where a request is made to suspend the relevant procedures in accordance with the preceding paragraph, a written request shall be submitted to the patent administrative department under the State Council, stating the reasons, and shall be accompanied by a copy of the relevant acceptance document issued by the patent administration department or the People's Court that specifies the application number or patent number. If the patent administrative department under the State Council considers that the reasons for suspension put forward by the party are obviously untenable, it may not suspend the relevant procedures.  
After the mediation statement made by the patent administration department or the judgment made by the People's Court takes effect, the party shall go through the formalities with the patent administrative department under the State Council for the resumption of the relevant procedures. If, within one year from the date of the request for suspension, the dispute over the ownership of the right to apply for a patent or the patent right has not been concluded and it is necessary to continue the suspension, the requesting person shall, within that period, request an extension of the suspension. If no extension is requested upon the expiry of the time limit, the patent administrative department under the State Council shall resume the relevant procedures on its own.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　人民法院在审理民事案件中裁定对专利申请权或者专利权采取保全措施的，国务院专利行政部门应当在收到写明申请号或者专利号的裁定书和协助执行通知书之日中止被保全的专利申请权或者专利权的有关程序。保全期限届满，人民法院没有裁定继续采取保全措施的，国务院专利行政部门自行恢复有关程序。

**Article 104** Where a People's Court, in hearing a civil case, rules to take preservation measures against the right to apply for a patent or the patent right, the patent administrative department under the State Council shall, on the date of receiving the written ruling specifying the application number or patent number and the notice of assistance in enforcement, suspend the relevant procedures for the preserved right to apply for a patent or patent right. If the preservation period expires and the People's Court has not ruled to continue the preservation measures, the patent administrative department under the State Council shall resume the relevant procedures on its own.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　国务院专利行政部门根据本细则第一百零三条和第一百零四条规定中止有关程序，是指暂停专利申请的初步审查、实质审查、复审程序，授予专利权程序和专利权无效宣告程序；暂停办理放弃、变更、转移专利权或者专利申请权手续，专利权质押手续以及专利权期限届满前的终止手续等。

**Article 105** The suspension of the relevant procedures by the patent administrative department under the State Council in accordance with Articles 103 and 104 of these Rules means the suspension of the preliminary examination, substantive examination, reexamination procedures for patent applications, the procedure for granting patent rights and the procedure for invalidation of patent rights; and the suspension of the handling of formalities for abandonment, change, or transfer of a patent right or patent application right, the formalities for pledge of a patent right, and the formalities for termination before the expiry of the patent term, etc.

### 第九章　专利登记和专利公报

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　国务院专利行政部门设置专利登记簿，登记下列与专利申请和专利权有关的事项：  
（一）专利权的授予；  
（二）专利申请权、专利权的转移；  
（三）专利权的质押、保全及其解除；  
（四）专利实施许可合同的备案；  
（五）国防专利、保密专利的解密；  
（六）专利权的无效宣告；  
（七）专利权的终止；  
（八）专利权的恢复；  
（九）专利权期限的补偿；  
（十）专利实施的开放许可；  
（十一）专利实施的强制许可；  
（十二）专利权人的姓名或者名称、国籍和地址的变更。

**Article 106** The patent administrative department under the State Council shall set up a patent register for registering the following matters related to patent applications and patent rights:  
(1) grant of patent rights;  
(2) transfer of the right to apply for a patent and the patent right;  
(3) pledge, preservation, and release of patent rights;  
(4) recordal of patent exploitation license contracts;  
(5) declassification of national defense patents and confidential patents;  
(6) invalidation of patent rights;  
(7) cessation of patent rights;  
(8) restoration of patent rights;  
(9) compensation for the patent term;  
(10) open license for patent exploitation;  
(11) compulsory license for patent exploitation;  
(12) change of the name, nationality and address of the patentee.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　国务院专利行政部门定期出版专利公报，公布或者公告下列内容：  
（一）发明专利申请的著录事项和说明书摘要；  
（二）发明专利申请的实质审查请求和国务院专利行政部门对发明专利申请自行进行实质审查的决定；  
（三）发明专利申请公布后的驳回、撤回、视为撤回、视为放弃、恢复和转移；  
（四）专利权的授予以及专利权的著录事项；  
（五）实用新型专利的说明书摘要，外观设计专利的一幅图片或者照片；  
（六）国防专利、保密专利的解密；  
（七）专利权的无效宣告；  
（八）专利权的终止、恢复；  
（九）专利权期限的补偿；  
（十）专利权的转移；  
（十一）专利实施许可合同的备案；  
（十二）专利权的质押、保全及其解除；  
（十三）专利实施的开放许可事项；  
（十四）专利实施的强制许可的给予；  
（十五）专利权人的姓名或者名称、国籍和地址的变更；  
（十六）文件的公告送达；  
（十七）国务院专利行政部门作出的更正；  
（十八）其他有关事项。

**Article 107** The patent administrative department under the State Council shall periodically publish the Patent Gazette, publishing or announcing the following contents:  
(1) bibliographic data and abstract of the specification of invention patent applications;  
(2) request for substantive examination of invention patent applications and the decision of the patent administrative department under the State Council to conduct substantive examination on its own;  
(3) rejection, withdrawal, deemed withdrawal, deemed abandonment, restoration and transfer of invention patent applications after publication;  
(4) grant of patent rights and bibliographic data of patent rights;  
(5) abstract of the specification of utility model patents, and one drawing or photograph of a design patent;  
(6) declassification of national defense patents and confidential patents;  
(7) invalidation of patent rights;  
(8) cessation and restoration of patent rights;  
(9) compensation for the patent term;  
(10) transfer of patent rights;  
(11) recordal of patent exploitation license contracts;  
(12) pledge, preservation and release of patent rights;  
(13) matters concerning open license for patent exploitation;  
(14) grant of compulsory license for patent exploitation;  
(15) change of the name, nationality and address of the patentee;  
(16) service of documents by publication;  
(17) corrections made by the patent administrative department under the State Council;  
(18) other relevant matters.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　国务院专利行政部门应当提供专利公报、发明专利申请单行本以及发明专利、实用新型专利、外观设计专利单行本，供公众免费查阅。

**Article 108** The patent administrative department under the State Council shall provide the Patent Gazette, separate fascicles of invention patent applications, and separate fascicles of invention patents, utility model patents, and design patents for the public to consult free of charge.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　国务院专利行政部门负责按照互惠原则与其他国家、地区的专利机关或者区域性专利组织交换专利文献。

**Article 109** The patent administrative department under the State Council shall be responsible for exchanging patent documents with patent authorities or regional patent organizations of other countries or regions on the basis of the principle of reciprocity.

### 第十章　费用

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　向国务院专利行政部门申请专利和办理其他手续时，应当缴纳下列费用：  
（一）申请费、申请附加费、公布印刷费、优先权要求费；  
（二）发明专利申请实质审查费、复审费；  
（三）年费；  
（四）恢复权利请求费、延长期限请求费；  
（五）著录事项变更费、专利权评价报告请求费、无效宣告请求费、专利文件副本证明费。  
前款所列各种费用的缴纳标准，由国务院发展改革部门、财政部门会同国务院专利行政部门按照职责分工规定。国务院财政部门、发展改革部门可以会同国务院专利行政部门根据实际情况对申请专利和办理其他手续应当缴纳的费用种类和标准进行调整。

**Article 110** When filing a patent application with the patent administrative department under the State Council or going through other formalities, the following fees shall be paid:  
(1) application fee, application surcharge, publication printing fee, and priority claim fee;  
(2) substantive examination fee for invention patent applications, and reexamination fee;  
(3) annual fee;  
(4) fee for request for restoration of rights, fee for request for extension of time limit;  
(5) fee for change of bibliographic data, fee for request for patent evaluation report, fee for request for invalidation, and fee for certification of copies of patent documents.  
The payment standards for the various fees listed in the preceding paragraph shall be prescribed by the development and reform department, the finance department under the State Council jointly with the patent administrative department under the State Council in accordance with their respective functions and duties. The finance department and the development and reform department under the State Council may, jointly with the patent administrative department under the State Council, adjust the types and standards of fees to be paid for filing patent applications and going through other formalities in light of the actual circumstances.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　专利法和本细则规定的各种费用，应当严格按照规定缴纳。  
直接向国务院专利行政部门缴纳费用的，以缴纳当日为缴费日；以邮局汇付方式缴纳费用的，以邮局汇出的邮戳日为缴费日；以银行汇付方式缴纳费用的，以银行实际汇出日为缴费日。  
多缴、重缴、错缴专利费用的，当事人可以自缴费日起3年内，向国务院专利行政部门提出退款请求，国务院专利行政部门应当予以退还。

**Article 111** Various fees prescribed by the Patent Law and these Rules shall be paid strictly in accordance with the provisions.  
Where fees are paid directly to the patent administrative department of the State Council, the date of payment shall be the date of payment; where fees are paid by postal remittance, the date of the postmark stamped by the post office shall be the date of payment; where fees are paid by bank remittance, the actual date of remittance by the bank shall be the date of payment.  
Where patent fees are overpaid, paid twice, or wrongly paid, the party may, within three years from the date of payment, file a request for refund with the patent administrative department of the State Council, and the patent administrative department of the State Council shall make a refund.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　申请人应当自申请日起2个月内或者在收到受理通知书之日起15日内缴纳申请费、公布印刷费和必要的申请附加费；期满未缴纳或者未缴足的，其申请视为撤回。  
申请人要求优先权的，应当在缴纳申请费的同时缴纳优先权要求费；期满未缴纳或者未缴足的，视为未要求优先权。

**Article 112** The applicant shall pay the application fee, publication printing fee and necessary additional application fee within two months from the date of application or within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the notice of acceptance; if the fees are not paid or not paid in full upon expiration of the time limit, the application shall be deemed to be withdrawn.  
Where the applicant claims priority, it shall pay the priority claim fee at the same time as paying the application fee; if the fee is not paid or not paid in full upon expiration of the time limit, the claim for priority shall be deemed not to have been made.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　当事人请求实质审查或者复审的，应当在专利法及本细则规定的相关期限内缴纳费用；期满未缴纳或者未缴足的，视为未提出请求。

**Article 113** Where a party requests substantive examination or reexamination, it shall pay the fees within the relevant time limit prescribed by the Patent Law and these Rules; if the fees are not paid or not paid in full upon expiration of the time limit, the request shall be deemed not to have been made.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　申请人办理登记手续时，应当缴纳授予专利权当年的年费；期满未缴纳或者未缴足的，视为未办理登记手续。

**Article 114** When going through the registration formalities, the applicant shall pay the annual fee for the year in which the patent right is granted; if the fee is not paid or not paid in full upon expiration of the time limit, the registration formalities shall be deemed not to have been completed.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　授予专利权当年以后的年费应当在上一年度期满前缴纳。专利权人未缴纳或者未缴足的，国务院专利行政部门应当通知专利权人自应当缴纳年费期满之日起6个月内补缴，同时缴纳滞纳金；滞纳金的金额按照每超过规定的缴费时间1个月，加收当年全额年费的5%计算；期满未缴纳的，专利权自应当缴纳年费期满之日起终止。

**Article 115** The annual fee for the years after the year in which the patent right is granted shall be paid before the expiration of the preceding year. Where the patentee fails to pay or fails to pay the full amount, the patent administrative department of the State Council shall notify the patentee to make up the payment within six months from the date on which the annual fee falls due, and at the same time pay a surcharge; the amount of the surcharge shall be calculated at the rate of 5% of the full annual fee for the current year for each month beyond the prescribed payment period; if the payment is not made upon expiration of the time limit, the patent right shall cease as from the date on which the annual fee falls due.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　恢复权利请求费应当在本细则规定的相关期限内缴纳；期满未缴纳或者未缴足的，视为未提出请求。  
延长期限请求费应当在相应期限届满之日前缴纳；期满未缴纳或者未缴足的，视为未提出请求。  
著录事项变更费、专利权评价报告请求费、无效宣告请求费应当自提出请求之日起1个月内缴纳；期满未缴纳或者未缴足的，视为未提出请求。

**Article 116** The fee for requesting restoration of a right shall be paid within the relevant time limit prescribed by these Rules; if the fee is not paid or not paid in full upon expiration of the time limit, the request shall be deemed not to have been made.  
The fee for requesting extension of a time limit shall be paid before the expiry of the corresponding time limit; if the fee is not paid or not paid in full upon expiration of the time limit, the request shall be deemed not to have been made.  
The fee for change of bibliographic data, the fee for requesting a patent evaluation report, and the fee for requesting invalidation shall be paid within one month from the date of making the request; if the fees are not paid or not paid in full upon expiration of the time limit, the request shall be deemed not to have been made.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　申请人或者专利权人缴纳本细则规定的各种费用有困难的，可以按照规定向国务院专利行政部门提出减缴的请求。减缴的办法由国务院财政部门会同国务院发展改革部门、国务院专利行政部门规定。

**Article 117** Where the applicant or patentee has difficulty in paying the various fees prescribed by these Rules, it may, in accordance with the provisions, file a request for reduction with the patent administrative department of the State Council. The measures for reduction shall be prescribed by the finance department of the State Council jointly with the development and reform department of the State Council and the patent administrative department of the State Council.

### 第十一章　关于发明、实用新型国际申请的特别规定

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　国务院专利行政部门根据[专利法第十九条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-19)规定，受理按照专利合作条约提出的专利国际申请。  
按照专利合作条约提出并指定中国的专利国际申请（以下简称国际申请）进入国务院专利行政部门处理阶段（以下称进入中国国家阶段）的条件和程序适用本章的规定；本章没有规定的，适用专利法及本细则其他各章的有关规定。

**Article 118** The patent administrative department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 19 of the Patent Law, accept international patent applications filed under the Patent Cooperation Treaty.  
The conditions and procedures for an international patent application filed under the Patent Cooperation Treaty and designating China (hereinafter referred to as the international application) to enter the processing stage of the patent administrative department of the State Council (hereinafter referred to as entering the Chinese national phase) shall be governed by the provisions of this Chapter; where this Chapter does not provide, the relevant provisions of the Patent Law and other Chapters of these Rules shall apply.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　按照专利合作条约已确定国际申请日并指定中国的国际申请，视为向国务院专利行政部门提出的专利申请，该国际申请日视为[专利法第二十八条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-28)所称的申请日。

**Article 119** An international application for which an international filing date has been determined under the Patent Cooperation Treaty and which designates China shall be deemed to be a patent application filed with the patent administrative department of the State Council, and the international filing date shall be deemed to be the filing date as referred to in Article 28 of the Patent Law.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　国际申请的申请人应当在专利合作条约第二条所称的优先权日（本章简称优先权日）起30个月内，向国务院专利行政部门办理进入中国国家阶段的手续；申请人未在该期限内办理该手续的，在缴纳宽限费后，可以在自优先权日起32个月内办理进入中国国家阶段的手续。

**Article 120** The applicant of an international application shall, within thirty months from the priority date as referred to in Article 2 of the Patent Cooperation Treaty (hereinafter referred to as the priority date in this Chapter), go through the formalities for entering the Chinese national phase with the patent administrative department of the State Council; where the applicant fails to go through the formalities within that time limit, it may, after paying a grace fee, go through the formalities for entering the Chinese national phase within thirty-two months from the priority date.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　申请人依照本细则第一百二十条的规定办理进入中国国家阶段的手续的，应当符合下列要求：  
（一）以中文提交进入中国国家阶段的书面声明，写明国际申请号和要求获得的专利权类型；  
（二）缴纳本细则第一百一十条第一款规定的申请费、公布印刷费，必要时缴纳本细则第一百二十条规定的宽限费；  
（三）国际申请以外文提出的，提交原始国际申请的说明书和权利要求书的中文译文；  
（四）在进入中国国家阶段的书面声明中写明发明创造的名称，申请人姓名或者名称、地址和发明人的姓名，上述内容应当与世界知识产权组织国际局（以下简称国际局）的记录一致；国际申请中未写明发明人的，在上述声明中写明发明人的姓名；  
（五）国际申请以外文提出的，提交摘要的中文译文，有附图和摘要附图的，提交附图副本并指定摘要附图，附图中有文字的，将其替换为对应的中文文字；  
（六）在国际阶段向国际局已办理申请人变更手续的，必要时提供变更后的申请人享有申请权的证明材料；  
（七）必要时缴纳本细则第一百一十条第一款规定的申请附加费。  
符合本条第一款第（一）项至第（三）项要求的，国务院专利行政部门应当给予申请号，明确国际申请进入中国国家阶段的日期（以下简称进入日），并通知申请人其国际申请已进入中国国家阶段。  
国际申请已进入中国国家阶段，但不符合本条第一款第（四）项至第（七）项要求的，国务院专利行政部门应当通知申请人在指定期限内补正；期满未补正的，其申请视为撤回。

**Article 121** An applicant who undergoes the procedures for entering the Chinese national phase in accordance with Article 120 of these Rules shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) submitting a written declaration in Chinese for entering the Chinese national phase, specifying the international application number and the type of patent right requested;  
(2) paying the application fee and the publication printing fee as prescribed in Paragraph 1 of Article 110 of these Rules, and, where necessary, paying the grace fee as prescribed in Article 120 of these Rules;  
(3) where the international application is filed in a foreign language, submitting a Chinese translation of the description and claims of the original international application;  
(4) stating in the written declaration for entering the Chinese national phase the title of the invention-creation, the name or title and address of the applicant, and the name of the inventor, and the above content shall be consistent with the records of the International Bureau of the World Intellectual Property Organization (hereinafter referred to as the International Bureau); where the international application does not specify the inventor, specifying the inventor's name in the said declaration;  
(5) where the international application is filed in a foreign language, submitting a Chinese translation of the abstract; if there are drawings and an abstract drawing, submitting copies of the drawings and designating the abstract drawing; if there is text in the drawings, replacing it with corresponding Chinese characters;  
(6) where the applicant has undergone the formalities of change of applicant with the International Bureau during the international phase, providing, where necessary, evidence that the changed applicant enjoys the right to apply;  
(7) paying, where necessary, the additional application fee as prescribed in Paragraph 1 of Article 110 of these Rules.  
Where the requirements set forth in Items (1) to (3) of the first paragraph are met, the patent administration department under the State Council shall grant an application number, specify the date on which the international application enters the Chinese national phase (hereinafter referred to as the entry date), and notify the applicant that the international application has entered the Chinese national phase.  
Where the international application has entered the Chinese national phase but does not meet the requirements set forth in Items (4) to (7) of the first paragraph, the patent administration department under the State Council shall notify the applicant to make corrections within a specified time limit; if no corrections are made upon expiration of the time limit, the application shall be deemed to be withdrawn.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　国际申请有下列情形之一的，其在中国的效力终止：  
（一）在国际阶段，国际申请被撤回或者被视为撤回，或者国际申请对中国的指定被撤回的；  
（二）申请人未在优先权日起32个月内按照本细则第一百二十条规定办理进入中国国家阶段手续的；  
（三）申请人办理进入中国国家阶段的手续，但自优先权日起32个月期限届满仍不符合本细则第一百二十一条第（一）项至第（三）项要求的。  
依照前款第（一）项的规定，国际申请在中国的效力终止的，不适用本细则第六条的规定；依照前款第（二）项、第（三）项的规定，国际申请在中国的效力终止的，不适用本细则第六条第二款的规定。

**Article 122** An international application shall cease to have effect in China under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) during the international phase, the international application is withdrawn or deemed to be withdrawn, or the designation of China in the international application is withdrawn;  
(2) the applicant fails to undergo the procedures for entering the Chinese national phase in accordance with Article 120 of these Rules within 32 months from the priority date;  
(3) the applicant undergoes the procedures for entering the Chinese national phase, but by the expiration of the 32-month period from the priority date, still fails to meet the requirements set forth in Items (1) to (3) of Article 121 of these Rules.  
Where the international application ceases to have effect in China in accordance with Item (1) of the preceding paragraph, Article 6 of these Rules shall not apply; where the international application ceases to have effect in China in accordance with Items (2) or (3) of the preceding paragraph, Paragraph 2 of Article 6 of these Rules shall not apply.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　国际申请在国际阶段作过修改，申请人要求以经修改的申请文件为基础进行审查的，应当自进入日起2个月内提交修改部分的中文译文。在该期间内未提交中文译文的，对申请人在国际阶段提出的修改，国务院专利行政部门不予考虑。

**Article 123** Where an international application has been amended during the international phase and the applicant requests that examination be conducted on the basis of the amended application documents, the applicant shall submit a Chinese translation of the amended parts within 2 months from the entry date. If no Chinese translation is submitted within that time limit, the amendments made by the applicant during the international phase shall not be taken into consideration by the patent administration department under the State Council.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　国际申请涉及的发明创造有[专利法第二十四条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-24)第（二）项或者第（三）项所列情形之一，在提出国际申请时作过声明的，申请人应当在进入中国国家阶段的书面声明中予以说明，并自进入日起2个月内提交本细则第三十三条第三款规定的有关证明文件；未予说明或者期满未提交证明文件的，其申请不适用[专利法第二十四条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-24)的规定。

**Article 124** Where an invention-creation involved in an international application falls under any of the circumstances listed in Item (2) or (3) of Article 24 of the Patent Law, and a declaration was made at the time of filing the international application, the applicant shall state this in the written declaration for entering the Chinese national phase and shall, within 2 months from the entry date, submit the relevant certifying documents as prescribed in Paragraph 3 of Article 33 of these Rules; if no such statement is made or no certifying documents are submitted upon expiration of the time limit, Article 24 of the Patent Law shall not apply to the application.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　申请人按照专利合作条约的规定，对生物材料样品的保藏已作出说明的，视为已经满足了本细则第二十七条第（三）项的要求。申请人应当在进入中国国家阶段声明中指明记载生物材料样品保藏事项的文件以及在该文件中的具体记载位置。  
申请人在原始提交的国际申请的说明书中已记载生物材料样品保藏事项，但是没有在进入中国国家阶段声明中指明的，应当自进入日起4个月内补正。期满未补正的，该生物材料视为未提交保藏。  
申请人自进入日起4个月内向国务院专利行政部门提交生物材料样品保藏证明和存活证明的，视为在本细则第二十七条第（一）项规定的期限内提交。

**Article 125** Where an applicant has made a statement on the deposit of a biological material sample in accordance with the provisions of the Patent Cooperation Treaty, the applicant shall be deemed to have satisfied the requirements set forth in Item (3) of Article 27 of these Rules. The applicant shall indicate in the declaration on entering the Chinese national phase the document containing the information on the deposit of the biological material sample and the specific location of the information in that document.  
Where the applicant has recorded the information on the deposit of the biological material sample in the description of the originally filed international application but fails to indicate it in the declaration on entering the Chinese national phase, the applicant shall make corrections within 4 months from the entry date. If no correction is made upon expiration of the time limit, the biological material shall be deemed not to have been deposited.  
Where the applicant submits the deposit certificate and the viability certificate of the biological material sample to the patent administration department under the State Council within 4 months from the entry date, the applicant shall be deemed to have submitted them within the time limit specified in Item (1) of Article 27 of these Rules.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条　国际申请涉及的发明创造依赖遗传资源完成的，申请人应当在国际申请进入中国国家阶段的书面声明中予以说明，并填写国务院专利行政部门制定的表格。

**Article 126** Where the invention-creation involved in an international application is completed by relying on genetic resources, the applicant shall state this in the written declaration for entering the Chinese national phase and shall fill in the form prescribed by the patent administration department under the State Council.

<a id="art-127"></a>第一百二十七条　申请人在国际阶段已要求一项或者多项优先权，在进入中国国家阶段时该优先权要求继续有效的，视为已经依照[专利法第三十条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-30)的规定提出了书面声明。  
申请人应当自进入日起２个月内缴纳优先权要求费；期满未缴纳或者未缴足的，视为未要求该优先权。  
申请人在国际阶段已依照专利合作条约的规定，提交过在先申请文件副本的，办理进入中国国家阶段手续时不需要向国务院专利行政部门提交在先申请文件副本。申请人在国际阶段未提交在先申请文件副本的，国务院专利行政部门认为必要时，可以通知申请人在指定期限内补交；申请人期满未补交的，其优先权要求视为未提出。

**Article 127** Where an applicant has claimed one or more priorities during the international phase, and the priority claim remains valid upon entering the Chinese national phase, the applicant shall be deemed to have filed a written declaration in accordance with Article 30 of the Patent Law.  
The applicant shall pay the priority claim fee within 2 months from the entry date; if the fee is not paid or not paid in full upon expiration of the time limit, the priority shall be deemed not to have been claimed.  
Where the applicant has submitted a copy of the earlier application document in accordance with the Patent Cooperation Treaty during the international phase, the applicant is not required to submit a copy of the earlier application document to the patent administration department under the State Council when undergoing the procedures for entering the Chinese national phase. Where the applicant fails to submit a copy of the earlier application document during the international phase, the patent administration department under the State Council may, if it deems necessary, notify the applicant to submit it within a specified time limit; if the applicant fails to submit it upon expiration of the time limit, the priority claim shall be deemed not to have been made.

<a id="art-128"></a>第一百二十八条　国际申请的申请日在优先权期限届满之后2个月内，在国际阶段受理局已经批准恢复优先权的，视为已经依照本细则第三十六条的规定提出了恢复优先权请求；在国际阶段申请人未请求恢复优先权，或者提出了恢复优先权请求但受理局未批准，申请人有正当理由的，可以自进入日起2个月内向国务院专利行政部门请求恢复优先权。

**Article 128** Where the filing date of an international application is within 2 months after the expiration of the priority period, and the receiving Office has approved the restoration of the priority right during the international phase, it shall be deemed that a request for restoration of the priority right has been made in accordance with Article 36 of these Rules; where the applicant did not request restoration of the priority right during the international phase, or made such a request but the receiving Office did not approve it, the applicant may, if there are justified reasons, request the patent administration department under the State Council to restore the priority right within 2 months from the entry date.

<a id="art-129"></a>第一百二十九条　在优先权日起30个月期满前要求国务院专利行政部门提前处理和审查国际申请的，申请人除应当办理进入中国国家阶段手续外，还应当依照专利合作条约第二十三条第二款规定提出请求。国际局尚未向国务院专利行政部门传送国际申请的，申请人应当提交经确认的国际申请副本。

**Article 129** Where an applicant requests the patent administration department under the State Council to process and examine an international application before the expiration of 30 months from the priority date, the applicant shall, in addition to undergoing the procedures for entering the Chinese national phase, also file a request in accordance with Article 23(2) of the Patent Cooperation Treaty. If the International Bureau has not yet transmitted the international application to the patent administration department under the State Council, the applicant shall submit a certified copy of the international application.

<a id="art-130"></a>第一百三十条　要求获得实用新型专利权的国际申请，申请人可以自进入日起2个月内对专利申请文件主动提出修改。  
要求获得发明专利权的国际申请，适用本细则第五十七条第一款的规定。

**Article 130** For an international application requesting the grant of a utility model patent right, the applicant may, within 2 months from the entry date, voluntarily make amendments to the patent application documents.  
For an international application requesting the grant of an invention patent right, the provisions of Paragraph 1 of Article 57 of these Rules shall apply.

<a id="art-131"></a>第一百三十一条　申请人发现提交的说明书、权利要求书或者附图中的文字的中文译文存在错误的，可以在下列规定期限内依照原始国际申请文本提出改正：  
（一）在国务院专利行政部门做好公布发明专利申请或者公告实用新型专利权的准备工作之前；  
（二）在收到国务院专利行政部门发出的发明专利申请进入实质审查阶段通知书之日起3个月内。  
申请人改正译文错误的，应当提出书面请求并缴纳规定的译文改正费。  
申请人按照国务院专利行政部门的通知书的要求改正译文的，应当在指定期限内办理本条第二款规定的手续；期满未办理规定手续的，该申请视为撤回。

**Article 131** Where the applicant discovers that the Chinese translation of the text of the description, claims or drawings as submitted contains errors, the applicant may, within the following prescribed time limits, make corrections in accordance with the original international application text:  
(1) before the patent administration department of the State Council completes the preparations for publishing the invention patent application or announcing the utility model patent right;  
(2) within three months from the date of receipt of the notice issued by the patent administration department of the State Council regarding the entry of the invention patent application into the substantive examination stage.  
Where the applicant corrects errors in the translation, the applicant shall submit a written request and pay the prescribed fee for translation correction.  
Where the applicant corrects the translation in accordance with the requirements of the notice of the patent administration department of the State Council, the applicant shall handle the procedures specified in the second paragraph of this Article within the specified time limit; if the procedures are not handled within the time limit, the application shall be deemed to be withdrawn.

<a id="art-132"></a>第一百三十二条　对要求获得发明专利权的国际申请，国务院专利行政部门经初步审查认为符合专利法和本细则有关规定的，应当在专利公报上予以公布；国际申请以中文以外的文字提出的，应当公布申请文件的中文译文。  
要求获得发明专利权的国际申请，由国际局以中文进行国际公布的，自国际公布日或者国务院专利行政部门公布之日起适用[专利法第十三条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-13)的规定；由国际局以中文以外的文字进行国际公布的，自国务院专利行政部门公布之日起适用[专利法第十三条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-13)的规定。  
对国际申请，[专利法第二十一条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-21)和第二十二条中所称的公布是指本条第一款所规定的公布。

**Article 132** For an international application requesting the grant of an invention patent right, where the patent administration department of the State Council, after preliminary examination, finds that it complies with the relevant provisions of the Patent Law and these Rules, it shall publish the application in the Patent Gazette; where the international application is filed in a language other than Chinese, the Chinese translation of the application documents shall be published.  
For an international application requesting the grant of an invention patent right, where the International Bureau has made an international publication in Chinese, the provisions of Article 13 of the Patent Law shall apply as of the date of international publication or the date of publication by the patent administration department of the State Council; where the International Bureau has made an international publication in a language other than Chinese, the provisions of Article 13 of the Patent Law shall apply as of the date of publication by the patent administration department of the State Council.  
With respect to an international application, the term "publication" used in Articles 21 and 22 of the Patent Law refers to the publication provided for in the first paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-133"></a>第一百三十三条　国际申请包含两项以上发明或者实用新型的，申请人可以自进入日起，依照本细则第四十八条第一款的规定提出分案申请。  
在国际阶段，国际检索单位或者国际初步审查单位认为国际申请不符合专利合作条约规定的单一性要求时，申请人未按照规定缴纳附加费，导致国际申请某些部分未经国际检索或者未经国际初步审查，在进入中国国家阶段时，申请人要求将所述部分作为审查基础，国务院专利行政部门认为国际检索单位或者国际初步审查单位对发明单一性的判断正确的，应当通知申请人在指定期限内缴纳单一性恢复费。期满未缴纳或者未足额缴纳的，国际申请中未经检索或者未经国际初步审查的部分视为撤回。

**Article 133** Where an international application contains two or more inventions or utility models, the applicant may, from the date of entry into the Chinese national phase, file a divisional application in accordance with the provisions of Article 48(1) of these Rules.  
Where, in the international phase, the international searching authority or the international preliminary examining authority considers that the international application does not meet the requirement of unity of invention under the Patent Cooperation Treaty, and the applicant fails to pay the additional fee as prescribed, resulting in certain parts of the international application not having been searched or subjected to international preliminary examination, and upon entry into the Chinese national phase, the applicant requests to take the said parts as the basis for examination, if the patent administration department of the State Council considers that the judgment of the international searching authority or the international preliminary examining authority on unity of invention is correct, it shall notify the applicant to pay a unity restoration fee within a specified time limit. If the fee is not paid or is not paid in full within the time limit, the parts of the international application that were not searched or subjected to international preliminary examination shall be deemed to be withdrawn.

<a id="art-134"></a>第一百三十四条　国际申请在国际阶段被有关国际单位拒绝给予国际申请日或者宣布视为撤回的，申请人在收到通知之日起2个月内，可以请求国际局将国际申请档案中任何文件的副本转交国务院专利行政部门，并在该期限内向国务院专利行政部门办理本细则第一百二十条规定的手续，国务院专利行政部门应当在接到国际局传送的文件后，对国际单位作出的决定是否正确进行复查。

**Article 134** Where an international application is refused an international filing date or declared deemed withdrawn by the relevant international unit in the international phase, the applicant may, within two months from the date of receipt of the notice, request the International Bureau to transmit copies of any documents in the international application file to the patent administration department of the State Council, and within that time limit, handle the procedures prescribed in Article 120 of these Rules with the patent administration department of the State Council. The patent administration department of the State Council shall, after receiving the documents transmitted by the International Bureau, review whether the decision made by the international unit is correct.

<a id="art-135"></a>第一百三十五条　基于国际申请授予的专利权，由于译文错误，致使依照[专利法第六十四条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-64)规定确定的保护范围超出国际申请的原文所表达的范围的，以依据原文限制后的保护范围为准；致使保护范围小于国际申请的原文所表达的范围的，以授权时的保护范围为准。

**Article 135** Where a patent right is granted on the basis of an international application and, due to errors in the translation, the scope of protection determined in accordance with Article 64 of the Patent Law exceeds the scope expressed in the original text of the international application, the scope of protection shall be the scope as limited according to the original text; where the scope of protection is narrower than the scope expressed in the original text of the international application, the scope of protection at the time of grant shall prevail.

### 第十二章　关于外观设计国际申请的特别规定

<a id="art-136"></a>第一百三十六条　国务院专利行政部门根据[专利法第十九条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-19)第二款、第三款规定，处理按照工业品外观设计国际注册海牙协定（1999年文本）（以下简称海牙协定）提出的外观设计国际注册申请。  
国务院专利行政部门处理按照海牙协定提出并指定中国的外观设计国际注册申请（简称外观设计国际申请）的条件和程序适用本章的规定；本章没有规定的，适用专利法及本细则其他各章的有关规定。

**Article 136** The patent administration department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 19(2) and (3) of the Patent Law, handle international registration applications for industrial designs filed under the Hague Agreement Concerning the International Registration of Industrial Designs (1999 Act) (hereinafter referred to as the Hague Agreement).  
The conditions and procedures for the patent administration department of the State Council to handle international registration applications for industrial designs filed under the Hague Agreement and designating China (hereinafter referred to as international design applications) shall be governed by the provisions of this Chapter; for matters not provided for in this Chapter, the relevant provisions of the Patent Law and other chapters of these Rules shall apply.

<a id="art-137"></a>第一百三十七条　按照海牙协定已确定国际注册日并指定中国的外观设计国际申请，视为向国务院专利行政部门提出的外观设计专利申请，该国际注册日视为[专利法第二十八条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-28)所称的申请日。

**Article 137** An international design application for which an international registration date has been determined under the Hague Agreement and which designates China shall be deemed to be an application for a design patent filed with the patent administration department of the State Council, and the international registration date shall be deemed to be the filing date referred to in Article 28 of the Patent Law.

<a id="art-138"></a>第一百三十八条　国际局公布外观设计国际申请后，国务院专利行政部门对外观设计国际申请进行审查，并将审查结果通知国际局。

**Article 138** After the International Bureau publishes an international design application, the patent administration department of the State Council shall examine the international design application and notify the International Bureau of the examination result.

<a id="art-139"></a>第一百三十九条　国际局公布的外观设计国际申请中包括一项或者多项优先权的，视为已经依照[专利法第三十条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-30)的规定提出了书面声明。  
外观设计国际申请的申请人要求优先权的，应当自外观设计国际申请公布之日起3个月内提交在先申请文件副本。

**Article 139** Where an international design application published by the International Bureau includes one or more priority claims, it shall be deemed that a written declaration has been made in accordance with the provisions of Article 30 of the Patent Law.  
Where the applicant of an international design application claims priority, the applicant shall submit a copy of the earlier application documents within three months from the date of publication of the international design application.

<a id="art-140"></a>第一百四十条　外观设计国际申请涉及的外观设计有[专利法第二十四条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-24)第（二）项或者第（三）项所列情形的，应当在提出外观设计国际申请时声明，并自外观设计国际申请公布之日起2个月内提交本细则第三十三条第三款规定的有关证明文件。

**Article 140** Where the design involved in an international design application falls under the circumstances listed in Article 24(2) or (3) of the Patent Law, a declaration shall be made at the time of filing the international design application, and the relevant supporting documents prescribed in Article 33(3) of these Rules shall be submitted within two months from the date of publication of the international design application.

<a id="art-141"></a>第一百四十一条　一件外观设计国际申请包括两项以上外观设计的，申请人可以自外观设计国际申请公布之日起2个月内，向国务院专利行政部门提出分案申请，并缴纳费用。

**Article 141** Where an international application for design patent includes two or more designs, the applicant may, within two months from the date of publication of the international application for design patent, file a divisional application with the patent administration department under the State Council and pay the fee.

<a id="art-142"></a>第一百四十二条　国际局公布的外观设计国际申请中包括含设计要点的说明书的，视为已经依照本细则第三十一条的规定提交了简要说明。

**Article 142** Where the international application for design patent published by the International Bureau includes a specification containing design essentials, it shall be deemed that a brief description has been submitted in accordance with Article 31 of these Rules.

<a id="art-143"></a>第一百四十三条　外观设计国际申请经国务院专利行政部门审查后没有发现驳回理由的，由国务院专利行政部门作出给予保护的决定，通知国际局。  
国务院专利行政部门作出给予保护的决定后，予以公告，该外观设计专利权自公告之日起生效。

**Article 143** Where no grounds for rejection are found after examination of the international application for design patent by the patent administration department under the State Council, the patent administration department under the State Council shall make a decision to grant protection and notify the International Bureau thereof.  
After the patent administration department under the State Council makes the decision to grant protection, it shall make an announcement. The design patent right shall take effect as of the date of announcement.

<a id="art-144"></a>第一百四十四条　已在国际局办理权利变更手续的，申请人应当向国务院专利行政部门提供有关证明材料。

**Article 144** Where the formalities for change of rights have been completed with the International Bureau, the applicant shall provide relevant supporting materials to the patent administration department under the State Council.

### 第十三章　附则

<a id="art-145"></a>第一百四十五条　经国务院专利行政部门同意，任何人均可以查阅或者复制已经公布或者公告的专利申请的案卷和专利登记簿，并可以请求国务院专利行政部门出具专利登记簿副本。  
已视为撤回、驳回和主动撤回的专利申请的案卷，自该专利申请失效之日起满2年后不予保存。  
已放弃、宣告全部无效和终止的专利权的案卷，自该专利权失效之日起满3年后不予保存。

**Article 145** With the consent of the patent administration department under the State Council, any person may inspect or make copies of the files of published or announced patent applications and the patent register, and may request the patent administration department under the State Council to issue a copy of the patent register.  
The files of patent applications that have been deemed to be withdrawn, rejected, or voluntarily withdrawn shall not be preserved after the expiration of two years from the date on which such patent applications cease to have effect.  
The files of patent rights that have been abandoned, declared entirely invalid, or terminated shall not be preserved after the expiration of three years from the date on which such patent rights cease to have effect.

<a id="art-146"></a>第一百四十六条　向国务院专利行政部门提交申请文件或者办理各种手续，应当由申请人、专利权人、其他利害关系人或者其代表人签字或者盖章；委托专利代理机构的，由专利代理机构盖章。  
请求变更发明人姓名、专利申请人和专利权人的姓名或者名称、国籍和地址、专利代理机构的名称、地址和专利代理师姓名的，应当向国务院专利行政部门办理著录事项变更手续，必要时应当提交变更理由的证明材料。

**Article 146** Any submission of application documents or handling of various procedures with the patent administration department under the State Council shall be signed or sealed by the applicant, patentee, other interested party, or their representative; where a patent agency is entrusted, the patent agency shall affix its seal.  
Where a request is made to change the name of the inventor, the name or title, nationality and address of the patent applicant or patentee, or the name, address of the patent agency and the name of the patent agent, the formalities for change of bibliographic data shall be completed with the patent administration department under the State Council, and where necessary, supporting materials for the reason of change shall be submitted.

<a id="art-147"></a>第一百四十七条　向国务院专利行政部门邮寄有关申请或者专利权的文件，应当使用挂号信函，不得使用包裹。  
除首次提交专利申请文件外，向国务院专利行政部门提交各种文件、办理各种手续的，应当标明申请号或者专利号、发明创造名称和申请人或者专利权人姓名或者名称。  
一件信函中应当只包含同一申请的文件。

**Article 147** Documents concerning an application or a patent right sent to the patent administration department under the State Council by mail shall be sent by registered letter and shall not be sent by parcel.  
Except for the first submission of patent application documents, all kinds of documents submitted and various procedures handled with the patent administration department under the State Council shall indicate the application number or patent number, the title of the invention-creation, and the name or title of the applicant or patentee.  
A single letter shall contain only documents relating to the same application.

<a id="art-148"></a>第一百四十八条　国务院专利行政部门根据专利法和本细则制定专利审查指南。

**Article 148** The patent administration department under the State Council shall formulate the Patent Examination Guidelines in accordance with the Patent Law and these Rules.

<a id="art-149"></a>第一百四十九条　本细则自2001年7月1日起施行。1992年12月12日国务院批准修订、1992年12月21日中国专利局发布的《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》同时废止。

**Article 149** These Rules shall come into force as of July 1, 2001. The Implementing Regulations of the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China, as revised upon approval by the State Council on December 12, 1992, and promulgated by the China Patent Office on December 21, 1992, shall be abolished simultaneously.
