### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了支持普惠金融发展，促进资金融通，规范融资担保公司的行为，防范风险，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of supporting the development of inclusive finance, promoting financing, regulating the conduct of financing guarantee companies, and preventing risks.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称融资担保，是指担保人为被担保人借款、发行债券等债务融资提供担保的行为；所称融资担保公司，是指依法设立、经营融资担保业务的有限责任公司或者股份有限公司。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "financing guarantee" refers to the act of a guarantor providing guarantee for debt financing such as borrowing or bond issuance of the guaranteed party; "financing guarantee company" refers to a limited liability company or joint stock company limited established according to law that engages in financing guarantee business.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　融资担保公司开展业务，应当遵守法律法规，审慎经营，诚实守信，不得损害国家利益、社会公共利益和他人合法权益。

**Article 3** A financing guarantee company shall, in carrying out its business, comply with laws and regulations, operate prudently, be trustworthy, and shall not harm the national interest, public interest, or the legitimate rights and interests of others.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的部门(以下称监督管理部门)负责对本地区融资担保公司的监督管理。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责制定促进本地区融资担保行业发展的政策措施、处置融资担保公司风险，督促监督管理部门严格履行职责。  
国务院建立融资性担保业务监管部际联席会议，负责拟订融资担保公司监督管理制度，协调解决融资担保公司监督管理中的重大问题，督促指导地方人民政府对融资担保公司进行监督管理和风险处置。融资性担保业务监管部际联席会议由国务院银行业监督管理机构牵头，国务院有关部门参加。

**Article 4** The department designated by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government (hereinafter referred to as the regulatory department) shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of financing guarantee companies within its region.  
The people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for formulating policies and measures to promote the development of the financing guarantee industry in its region, handling risks of financing guarantee companies, and urging the regulatory department to strictly perform its duties.  
The State Council shall establish an inter-ministerial joint conference for the regulation of financing guarantee business, which shall be responsible for drafting the supervision and administration system for financing guarantee companies, coordinating and resolving major issues in the supervision and administration of financing guarantee companies, and urging and guiding local people's governments in the supervision and administration and risk disposal of financing guarantee companies. The inter-ministerial joint conference for the regulation of financing guarantee business shall be led by the banking regulatory authority under the State Council, with relevant departments of the State Council participating.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家推动建立政府性融资担保体系，发展政府支持的融资担保公司，建立政府、银行业金融机构、融资担保公司合作机制，扩大为小微企业和农业、农村、农民提供融资担保业务的规模并保持较低的费率水平。  
各级人民政府财政部门通过资本金投入、建立风险分担机制等方式，对主要为小微企业和农业、农村、农民服务的融资担保公司提供财政支持，具体办法由国务院财政部门制定。

**Article 5** The State shall promote the establishment of a government-led financing guarantee system, develop financing guarantee companies supported by the government, and establish a cooperation mechanism among the government, banking financial institutions, and financing guarantee companies, to expand the scale of financing guarantee services provided to small and micro enterprises, agriculture, rural areas, and farmers, and maintain a relatively low fee level.  
The finance departments of people's governments at all levels shall provide financial support to financing guarantee companies that primarily serve small and micro enterprises, agriculture, rural areas, and farmers, through capital injection, establishment of risk-sharing mechanisms, and other means. The specific measures shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council.

### 第二章　设立、变更和终止

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　设立融资担保公司，应当经监督管理部门批准。  
融资担保公司的名称中应当标明融资担保字样。  
未经监督管理部门批准，任何单位和个人不得经营融资担保业务，任何单位不得在名称中使用融资担保字样。国家另有规定的除外。

**Article 6** The establishment of a financing guarantee company shall be subject to the approval of the regulatory department.  
The name of a financing guarantee company shall clearly indicate the words "financing guarantee".  
Without the approval of the regulatory department, no unit or individual shall engage in financing guarantee business, and no unit shall use the words "financing guarantee" in its name, unless otherwise provided by the State.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　设立融资担保公司，应当符合《中华人民共和国公司法》的规定，并具备下列条件：  
(一)股东信誉良好，最近3年无重大违法违规记录；  
(二)注册资本不低于人民币2000万元，且为实缴货币资本；  
(三)拟任董事、监事、高级管理人员熟悉与融资担保业务相关的法律法规，具有履行职责所需的从业经验和管理能力；  
(四)有健全的业务规范和风险控制等内部管理制度。  
省、自治区、直辖市根据本地区经济发展水平和融资担保行业发展的实际情况，可以提高前款规定的注册资本最低限额。

**Article 7** The establishment of a financing guarantee company shall comply with the provisions of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China and meet the following conditions:  
(1) The shareholders have good credit standing and have no record of material illegal or non-compliant conduct in the most recent three years;  
(2) The registered capital shall not be less than RMB 20 million yuan and shall be paid-in monetary capital;  
(3) The proposed directors, supervisors, and senior officers are familiar with the laws and regulations related to financing guarantee business, and possess the professional experience and management capabilities required for performing their duties;  
(4) There are sound internal management systems such as business standards and risk control.  
Provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may raise the minimum registered capital requirement prescribed in the preceding paragraph based on the local economic development level and the actual circumstances of the development of the financing guarantee industry.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请设立融资担保公司，应当向监督管理部门提交申请书和证明其符合[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定条件的材料。  
监督管理部门应当自受理申请之日起30日内作出批准或者不予批准的决定。决定批准的，颁发融资担保业务经营许可证；不予批准的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
经批准设立的融资担保公司由监督管理部门予以公告。

**Article 8** An applicant applying for the establishment of a financing guarantee company shall submit to the regulatory department a written application and materials proving that it meets the conditions prescribed in Article 7 of these Regulations.  
The regulatory department shall, within 30 days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision of approval or disapproval. If the decision is to approve, it shall issue a financing guarantee business permit; if the decision is disapproval, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
A financing guarantee company established with approval shall be announced by the regulatory department.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　融资担保公司合并、分立或者减少注册资本，应当经监督管理部门批准。  
融资担保公司在住所地所在省、自治区、直辖市范围内设立分支机构，变更名称，变更持有5%以上股权的股东或者变更董事、监事、高级管理人员，应当自分支机构设立之日起或者变更相关事项之日起30日内向监督管理部门备案；变更后的相关事项应当符合[本条例第六条](#art-6)第二款、第七条的规定。

**Article 9** A financing guarantee company shall obtain the approval of the regulatory department for merger, division, or reduction of registered capital.  
If a financing guarantee company establishes a branch within the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where its domicile is located, changes its name, changes a shareholder holding more than 5% of the equity, or changes directors, supervisors, or senior officers, it shall file for record with the regulatory department within 30 days from the date of establishment of the branch or from the date of change of the relevant matters; the changed matters shall comply with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 6 and Article 7 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　融资担保公司跨省、自治区、直辖市设立分支机构，应当具备下列条件，并经拟设分支机构所在地监督管理部门批准：  
(一)注册资本不低于人民币10亿元；  
(二)经营融资担保业务3年以上，且最近2个会计年度连续盈利；  
(三)最近2年无重大违法违规记录。  
拟设分支机构所在地监督管理部门审批的程序和期限，适用[本条例第八条](#art-8)的规定。  
融资担保公司应当自分支机构设立之日起30日内，将有关情况报告公司住所地监督管理部门。  
融资担保公司跨省、自治区、直辖市设立的分支机构的日常监督管理，由分支机构所在地监督管理部门负责，融资担保公司住所地监督管理部门应当予以配合。

**Article 10** A financing guarantee company that establishes a branch across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall meet the following conditions and obtain the approval of the regulatory department of the place where the proposed branch is to be located:  
(1) The registered capital shall not be less than RMB 1 billion yuan;  
(2) It has been engaged in financing guarantee business for more than three years and has been profitable for the last two consecutive accounting years;  
(3) There is no record of material illegal or non-compliant conduct in the most recent two years.  
The procedures and time limit for the examination and approval by the regulatory department of the place where the proposed branch is to be located shall be governed by the provisions of Article 8 of these Regulations.  
A financing guarantee company shall, within 30 days from the date of establishment of the branch, report the relevant information to the regulatory department of the place where its domicile is located.  
The routine supervision and administration of a branch established by a financing guarantee company across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be the responsibility of the regulatory department of the place where the branch is located, and the regulatory department of the place where the financing guarantee company's domicile is located shall cooperate.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　融资担保公司解散的，应当依法成立清算组进行清算，并对未到期融资担保责任的承接作出明确安排。清算过程应当接受监督管理部门的监督。  
融资担保公司解散或者被依法宣告破产的，应当将融资担保业务经营许可证交监督管理部门注销，并由监督管理部门予以公告。

**Article 11** Where a financing guarantee company is dissolved, it shall establish a liquidation group according to law to carry out liquidation and make clear arrangements for the undertaking of unmatured financing guarantee liabilities. The liquidation process shall be subject to the supervision of the regulatory department.  
Where a financing guarantee company is dissolved or is declared bankrupt according to law, it shall surrender the financing guarantee business permit to the regulatory department for cancellation, and the regulatory department shall make an announcement.

### 第三章　经营规则

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　除经营借款担保、发行债券担保等融资担保业务外，经营稳健、财务状况良好的融资担保公司还可以经营投标担保、工程履约担保、诉讼保全担保等非融资担保业务以及与担保业务有关的咨询等服务业务。

**Article 12** In addition to engaging in financing guarantee business such as loan guarantee and bond issuance guarantee, a financing guarantee company with sound operation and good financial condition may also engage in non-financing guarantee businesses such as bid guarantee, project performance guarantee, and litigation preservation guarantee, as well as service businesses such as consulting related to guarantee business.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　融资担保公司应当按照审慎经营原则，建立健全融资担保项目评审、担保后管理、代偿责任追偿等方面的业务规范以及风险管理等内部控制制度。  
政府支持的融资担保公司应当增强运用大数据等现代信息技术手段的能力，为小微企业和农业、农村、农民的融资需求服务。

**Article 13** A financing guarantee company shall, in accordance with the principle of prudent operation, establish and improve business standards for financing guarantee project evaluation, post-guarantee management, recovery of compensation liabilities, and other aspects, as well as internal control systems such as risk management.  
A financing guarantee company supported by the government shall enhance its ability to utilize modern information technology means such as big data, to serve the financing needs of small and micro enterprises, agriculture, rural areas, and farmers.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　融资担保公司应当按照国家规定的风险权重，计量担保责任余额。

**Article 14** A financing guarantee company shall measure the balance of guarantee liabilities in accordance with the risk weights prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　融资担保公司的担保责任余额不得超过其净资产的10倍。  
对主要为小微企业和农业、农村、农民服务的融资担保公司，前款规定的倍数上限可以提高至15倍。

**Article 15** The balance of guarantee liabilities of a financing guarantee company shall not exceed 10 times its net assets.  
For a financing guarantee company that primarily serves small and micro enterprises, agriculture, rural areas, and farmers, the cap on the multiple prescribed in the preceding paragraph may be increased to 15 times.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　融资担保公司对同一被担保人的担保责任余额与融资担保公司净资产的比例不得超过10%，对同一被担保人及其关联方的担保责任余额与融资担保公司净资产的比例不得超过15%。

**Article 16** The ratio of the balance of guarantee liabilities of a financing guarantee company for the same guaranteed party to its net assets shall not exceed 10%, and the ratio of the balance of guarantee liabilities for the same guaranteed party and its related parties to its net assets shall not exceed 15%.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　融资担保公司不得为其控股股东、实际控制人提供融资担保，为其他关联方提供融资担保的条件不得优于为非关联方提供同类担保的条件。  
融资担保公司为关联方提供融资担保的，应当自提供担保之日起30日内向监督管理部门报告，并在会计报表附注中予以披露。

**Article 17** A financing guarantee company shall not provide financing guarantee for its controlling shareholder or de facto controller, and the conditions for providing financing guarantee for other related parties shall not be more favorable than the conditions for providing similar guarantee for non-related parties.  
Where a financing guarantee company provides financing guarantee for a related party, it shall report to the regulatory department within 30 days from the date of providing the guarantee, and disclose it in the notes to the accounting statements.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　融资担保公司应当按照国家有关规定提取相应的准备金。

**Article 18** A financing guarantee company shall set aside corresponding provisions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　融资担保费率由融资担保公司与被担保人协商确定。  
纳入政府推动建立的融资担保风险分担机制的融资担保公司，应当按照国家有关规定降低对小微企业和农业、农村、农民的融资担保费率。

**Article 19** The financing guarantee fee rate shall be determined through negotiation between the financing guarantee company and the guaranteed party.  
A financing guarantee company that is included in the financing guarantee risk-sharing mechanism established by the government shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, lower the financing guarantee fee rate for small and micro enterprises, agriculture, rural areas, and farmers.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　被担保人或者第三人以抵押、质押方式向融资担保公司提供反担保，依法需要办理登记的，有关登记机关应当依法予以办理。

**Article 20** Where the guaranteed party or a third party provides counter-guarantee to a financing guarantee company by way of mortgage or pledge, and registration is required according to law, the relevant registration authority shall handle the registration according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　融资担保公司有权要求被担保人提供与融资担保有关的业务活动和财务状况等信息。  
融资担保公司应当向被担保人的债权人提供与融资担保有关的业务活动和财务状况等信息。

**Article 21** A financing guarantee company shall have the right to request the guaranteed party to provide information on business activities and financial status related to the financing guarantee.  
A financing guarantee company shall provide to the creditors of the guaranteed party information on business activities and financial status related to the financing guarantee.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　融资担保公司自有资金的运用，应当符合国家有关融资担保公司资产安全性、流动性的规定。

**Article 22** The utilization of a financing guarantee company's own funds shall comply with the provisions of the State concerning the safety and liquidity of assets of financing guarantee companies.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　融资担保公司不得从事下列活动：  
(一)吸收存款或者变相吸收存款；  
(二)自营贷款或者受托贷款；  
(三)受托投资。

**Article 23** A financing guarantee company shall not engage in any of the following activities:  
(1) Absorbing deposits or absorbing deposits in disguised form;  
(2) Making proprietary loans or entrusted loans;  
(3) Making entrusted investments.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　监督管理部门应当建立健全监督管理工作制度，运用大数据等现代信息技术手段实时监测风险，加强对融资担保公司的非现场监管和现场检查，并与有关部门建立监督管理协调机制和信息共享机制。

**Article 24** The regulatory department shall establish and improve the supervision and administration work system, use modern information technology means such as big data to monitor risks in real time, strengthen off-site supervision and on-site inspection of financing guarantee companies, and establish supervision and administration coordination mechanisms and information sharing mechanisms with relevant departments.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　监督管理部门应当根据融资担保公司的经营规模、主要服务对象、内部管理水平、风险状况等，对融资担保公司实施分类监督管理。

**Article 25** The regulatory department shall implement categorized supervision and administration of financing guarantee companies based on factors such as their business scale, main service targets, internal management level, and risk status.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　监督管理部门应当按照国家有关融资担保统计制度的要求，向本级人民政府和国务院银行业监督管理机构报送本地区融资担保公司统计数据。

**Article 26** The regulatory department shall, in accordance with the requirements of the State's statistical system for financing guarantees, submit statistical data on financing guarantee companies in its region to the people's government at the corresponding level and the banking regulatory authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　监督管理部门应当分析评估本地区融资担保行业发展和监督管理情况，按年度向本级人民政府和国务院银行业监督管理机构报告，并向社会公布。

**Article 27** The regulatory department shall analyze and evaluate the development of the financing guarantee industry and the supervision and administration situation in its region, report annually to the people's government at the corresponding level and the banking regulatory authority under the State Council, and make it public to the society.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　监督管理部门进行现场检查，可以采取下列措施：  
(一)进入融资担保公司进行检查；  
(二)询问融资担保公司的工作人员，要求其对有关检查事项作出说明；  
(三)检查融资担保公司的计算机信息管理系统；  
(四)查阅、复制与检查事项有关的文件、资料，对可能被转移、隐匿或者毁损的文件、资料、电子设备予以封存。  
进行现场检查，应当经监督管理部门负责人批准。检查人员不得少于2人，并应当出示合法证件和检查通知书。

**Article 28** When conducting on-site inspection, the regulatory department may take the following measures:  
(1) Entering a financing guarantee company for inspection;  
(2) Inquiring the staff of the financing guarantee company and requesting them to give explanations on relevant inspection matters;  
(3) Inspecting the computer information management system of the financing guarantee company;  
(4) Examining and copying documents and materials related to the inspection matters, and sealing up documents, materials, and electronic devices that may be transferred, concealed, or damaged.  
On-site inspection shall be approved by the person in charge of the regulatory department. There shall be at least two inspectors, and they shall present their legal credentials and an inspection notice.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　监督管理部门根据履行职责的需要，可以与融资担保公司的董事、监事、高级管理人员进行监督管理谈话，要求其就融资担保公司业务活动和风险管理的重大事项作出说明。  
监督管理部门可以向被担保人的债权人通报融资担保公司的违法违规行为或者风险情况。

**Article 29** The regulatory department may, as required for performing its duties, conduct supervision and administration talks with the directors, supervisors, and senior officers of a financing guarantee company, and request them to make explanations on major matters concerning the business activities and risk management of the financing guarantee company.  
The regulatory department may notify the creditors of the guaranteed party of the illegal or non-compliant conduct or risk situation of the financing guarantee company.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　监督管理部门发现融资担保公司的经营活动可能形成重大风险的，经监督管理部门主要负责人批准，可以区别情形，采取下列措施：  
(一)责令其暂停部分业务；  
(二)限制其自有资金运用的规模和方式；  
(三)责令其停止增设分支机构。  
融资担保公司应当及时采取措施，消除重大风险隐患，并向监督管理部门报告有关情况。经监督管理部门验收，确认重大风险隐患已经消除的，监督管理部门应当自验收完毕之日起3日内解除前款规定的措施。

**Article 30** Where the regulatory department discovers that the business activities of a financing guarantee company may create material risks, it may, subject to approval of the principal responsible person of the regulatory department and based on the circumstances, take the following measures:  
(1) Ordering it to suspend part of its business;  
(2) Restricting the scale and manner of utilization of its own funds;  
(3) Ordering it to cease establishing additional branches.  
The financing guarantee company shall promptly take measures to eliminate material risk hazards and report the relevant information to the regulatory department. Where, upon verification by the regulatory department, it is confirmed that the material risk hazards have been eliminated, the regulatory department shall lift the measures prescribed in the preceding paragraph within three days from the date of completion of the verification.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　融资担保公司应当按照要求向监督管理部门报送经营报告、财务报告以及注册会计师出具的年度审计报告等文件和资料。  
融资担保公司跨省、自治区、直辖市开展业务的，应当按季度向住所地监督管理部门和业务发生地监督管理部门报告业务开展情况。

**Article 31** A financing guarantee company shall, as required, submit to the regulatory department documents and materials such as business reports, financial reports, and annual audit reports issued by certified public accountants.  
Where a financing guarantee company conducts business across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, it shall report quarterly to the regulatory department of the place of its domicile and the regulatory department of the place where the business is conducted on the status of business operations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　融资担保公司对监督管理部门依法实施的监督检查应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻碍。

**Article 32** A financing guarantee company shall cooperate with the regulatory department in the supervision and inspection conducted according to law, and shall not refuse or obstruct.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　监督管理部门应当建立健全融资担保公司信用记录制度。融资担保公司信用记录纳入全国信用信息共享平台。

**Article 33** The regulatory department shall establish and improve a credit record system for financing guarantee companies. The credit records of financing guarantee companies shall be incorporated into the national credit information sharing platform.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　监督管理部门应当会同有关部门建立融资担保公司重大风险事件的预警、防范和处置机制，制定融资担保公司重大风险事件应急预案。  
融资担保公司发生重大风险事件的，应当立即采取应急措施，并及时向监督管理部门报告。监督管理部门应当及时处置，并向本级人民政府、国务院银行业监督管理机构和中国人民银行报告。

**Article 34** The regulatory department shall, together with relevant departments, establish mechanisms for early warning, prevention, and handling of material risk events of financing guarantee companies, and formulate contingency plans for material risk events of financing guarantee companies.  
Where a financing guarantee company experiences a material risk event, it shall immediately take emergency measures and promptly report to the regulatory department. The regulatory department shall promptly handle the situation and report to the people's government at the corresponding level, the banking regulatory authority under the State Council, and the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　监督管理部门及其工作人员对监督管理工作中知悉的商业秘密，应当予以保密。

**Article 35** The regulatory department and its staff shall keep confidential the trade secrets learned during supervision and administration work.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本条例规定，未经批准擅自设立融资担保公司或者经营融资担保业务的，由监督管理部门予以取缔或者责令停止经营，处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本条例规定，未经批准在名称中使用融资担保字样的，由监督管理部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 36** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a financing guarantee company is established or financing guarantee business is operated without approval, the regulatory department shall ban it or order it to cease operations, impose a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 yuan but not more than RMB 1 million yuan, and confiscate any illegal gains; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, the words "financing guarantee" are used in the name without approval, the regulatory department shall order rectification within a specified time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed, and any illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　融资担保公司有下列情形之一的，由监督管理部门责令限期改正，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；逾期不改正的，责令停业整顿，情节严重的，吊销其融资担保业务经营许可证：  
(一)未经批准合并或者分立；  
(二)未经批准减少注册资本；  
(三)未经批准跨省、自治区、直辖市设立分支机构。

**Article 37** Where a financing guarantee company has any of the following circumstances, the regulatory department shall order rectification within a specified time limit, impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan, and confiscate any illegal gains; if rectification is not made within the time limit, it shall order suspension of business for rectification, and if the circumstances are serious, revoke its financing guarantee business permit:  
(1) Merging or dividing without approval;  
(2) Reducing registered capital without approval;  
(3) Establishing a branch across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government without approval.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　融资担保公司变更相关事项，未按照本条例规定备案，或者变更后的相关事项不符合本条例规定的，由监督管理部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款，情节严重的，责令停业整顿。

**Article 38** Where a financing guarantee company changes relevant matters without filing for record in accordance with these Regulations, or the changed matters do not comply with the provisions of these Regulations, the regulatory department shall order rectification within a specified time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed, and if the circumstances are serious, it shall order suspension of business for rectification.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　融资担保公司受托投资的，由监督管理部门责令限期改正，处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；逾期不改正的，责令停业整顿，情节严重的，吊销其融资担保业务经营许可证。  
融资担保公司吸收公众存款或者变相吸收公众存款、从事自营贷款或者受托贷款的，依照有关法律、行政法规予以处罚。

**Article 39** Where a financing guarantee company makes entrusted investments, the regulatory department shall order rectification within a specified time limit, impose a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 yuan but not more than RMB 1 million yuan, and confiscate any illegal gains; if rectification is not made within the time limit, it shall order suspension of business for rectification, and if the circumstances are serious, revoke its financing guarantee business permit.  
Where a financing guarantee company absorbs public deposits or absorbs deposits in disguised form, or engages in proprietary loans or entrusted loans, it shall be punished in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　融资担保公司有下列情形之一的，由监督管理部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得，并可以责令停业整顿，情节严重的，吊销其融资担保业务经营许可证：  
(一)担保责任余额与其净资产的比例不符合规定；  
(二)为控股股东、实际控制人提供融资担保，或者为其他关联方提供融资担保的条件优于为非关联方提供同类担保的条件；  
(三)未按照规定提取相应的准备金；  
(四)自有资金的运用不符合国家有关融资担保公司资产安全性、流动性的规定。

**Article 40** Where a financing guarantee company has any of the following circumstances, the regulatory department shall order rectification within a specified time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, any illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it may order suspension of business for rectification; if the circumstances are serious, revoke its financing guarantee business permit:  
(1) The ratio of the balance of guarantee liabilities to its net assets does not meet the requirements;  
(2) Providing financing guarantee for its controlling shareholder or de facto controller, or providing financing guarantee for other related parties on conditions more favorable than those for providing similar guarantee for non-related parties;  
(3) Failing to set aside corresponding provisions in accordance with the provisions;  
(4) The utilization of its own funds does not comply with the provisions of the State concerning the safety and liquidity of assets of financing guarantee companies.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　融资担保公司未按照要求向监督管理部门报送经营报告、财务报告、年度审计报告等文件、资料或者业务开展情况，或者未报告其发生的重大风险事件的，由监督管理部门责令限期改正，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停业整顿，情节严重的，吊销其融资担保业务经营许可证。

**Article 41** Where a financing guarantee company fails to submit documents and materials such as business reports, financial reports, annual audit reports, or the status of business operations to the regulatory department as required, or fails to report the material risk events it experiences, the regulatory department shall order rectification within a specified time limit and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan; if rectification is not made within the time limit, it shall order suspension of business for rectification; if the circumstances are serious, revoke its financing guarantee business permit.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　融资担保公司有下列情形之一的，由监督管理部门责令限期改正，处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停业整顿，情节严重的，吊销其融资担保业务经营许可证；构成违反治安管理行为的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)拒绝、阻碍监督管理部门依法实施监督检查；  
(二)向监督管理部门提供虚假的经营报告、财务报告、年度审计报告等文件、资料；  
(三)拒绝执行监督管理部门依照[本条例第三十条](#art-30)第一款规定采取的措施。

**Article 42** Where a financing guarantee company has any of the following circumstances, the regulatory department shall order rectification within a specified time limit, impose a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan; if rectification is not made within the time limit, it shall order suspension of business for rectification; if the circumstances are serious, revoke its financing guarantee business permit; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, it shall be punished in accordance with the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Refusing or obstructing the regulatory department from conducting supervision and inspection according to law;  
(2) Providing false documents and materials such as business reports, financial reports, annual audit reports to the regulatory department;  
(3) Refusing to implement the measures taken by the regulatory department in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 30 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　依照本条例规定对融资担保公司处以罚款的，根据具体情形，可以同时对负有直接责任的董事、监事、高级管理人员处5万元以下的罚款。  
融资担保公司违反本条例规定，情节严重的，监督管理部门对负有直接责任的董事、监事、高级管理人员，可以禁止其在一定期限内担任或者终身禁止其担任融资担保公司的董事、监事、高级管理人员。

**Article 43** Where a fine is imposed on a financing guarantee company in accordance with these Regulations, depending on the specific circumstances, a fine of not more than RMB 50,000 yuan may also be imposed on the directors, supervisors, and senior officers directly responsible.  
Where a financing guarantee company violates these Regulations and the circumstances are serious, the regulatory department may prohibit the directors, supervisors, and senior officers directly responsible from serving as directors, supervisors, or senior officers of a financing guarantee company for a specified period or for life.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　监督管理部门的工作人员在融资担保公司监督管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 44** Where a staff member of the regulatory department abuses power, neglects duties, or engages in irregularities for personal gain in the supervision and administration of financing guarantee companies, he or she shall be given disciplinary sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　融资担保行业组织依照法律法规和章程的规定，发挥服务、协调和行业自律作用，引导融资担保公司依法经营，公平竞争。

**Article 45** Financing guarantee industry organizations shall, in accordance with laws, regulations, and their articles of association, play a role in service, coordination, and industry self-discipline, and guide financing guarantee companies to operate according to law and compete fairly.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　政府性基金或者政府部门为促进就业创业等直接设立运营机构开展融资担保业务，按照国家有关规定执行。  
农村互助式融资担保组织开展担保业务、林业经营主体间开展林权收储担保业务，不适用本条例。

**Article 46** Where government funds or government departments directly establish and operate institutions to conduct financing guarantee business for the purpose of promoting employment and entrepreneurship, the relevant provisions of the State shall apply.  
Rural mutual-aid financing guarantee organizations conducting guarantee business and forest management entities carrying out forest rights acquisition and storage guarantee business shall not be subject to these Regulations.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　融资再担保公司的管理办法，由国务院银行业监督管理机构会同国务院有关部门另行制定，报国务院批准。

**Article 47** The measures for the administration of financing re-guarantee companies shall be separately formulated by the banking regulatory authority under the State Council together with the relevant departments of the State Council, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本条例施行前设立的融资担保公司，不符合本条例规定条件的，应当在监督管理部门规定的期限内达到本条例规定的条件；逾期仍不符合规定条件的，不得开展新的融资担保业务。

**Article 48** A financing guarantee company established before these Regulations come into force that does not meet the conditions prescribed in these Regulations shall meet the conditions prescribed in these Regulations within the time limit prescribed by the regulatory department; if it still fails to meet the conditions after the time limit expires, it shall not conduct any new financing guarantee business.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　本条例自2017年10月1日起施行。

**Article 49** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 1, 2017.
