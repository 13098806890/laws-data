# 证券期货行政执法当事人承诺制度实施办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2021-10-26  
**生效日期**：2022-01-01

> 当事人提交的材料以及在沟通协商时所作的陈述，只能用于实施行政执法当事人承诺。 第十一条　国务院证券监督管理机构与当事人进行沟通协商的期限为6个月。经国务院证券监督管理机构主要负责人或者其授权的其他负责人批准，可以延长沟通协商的期限，但延长的期限最长不超过6个月。 国务院证券监督管理机构与当事人沟通协商应当当面进行，并制作笔录。国务院证券监督管理机构与当事人进行沟通协商的工作人员不得少于2人，并应当向当事人出示执法证件。 国务院证券监督管理机构与当事人进行沟通协商的工作人员不得违反规定会见当事人及其委托的人。 第十二条　国务院证券监督管理机构相关工作人员与案件有直接利害关系或者有其他关系，可能影响公正执法的，应当回避。 当事人认为国务院证券监督管理机构相关工作人员有前款规定情形的，有权申请其回避。当事人提出回避申请，应当说明理由。国务院证券监督管理机构应当自当事人提出回避申请之日起3日内作出决定，并书面告知当事人。决定作出之前，被申请回避的人员不停止相关工作。 第十三条　国务院证券监督管理机构经与当事人沟通协商，认可当事人作出的承诺的，应当与当事人签署承诺认可协议。承诺认可协议应当载明下列事项： （一）申请适用行政执法当事人承诺的事由； （二）当事人涉嫌违法行为的主要事实； （三）当事人承诺采取的纠正涉嫌违法行为、赔偿有关投资者损失、消除损害或者不良影响的具体措施； （四）承诺金数额及交纳方式； （五）当事人履行承诺的期限； （六）保障当事人商业秘密、个人隐私等权利的措施； （七）需要载明的其他事项。 本办法所称承诺金，是指当事人为适用行政执法当事人承诺而交纳的资金。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院证券监督管理机构与当事人进行沟通协商的期限为6个月。经国务院证券监督管理机构主要负责人或者其授权的其他负责人批准，可以延长沟通协商的期限，但延长的期限最长不超过6个月。  
国务院证券监督管理机构与当事人沟通协商应当当面进行，并制作笔录。国务院证券监督管理机构与当事人进行沟通协商的工作人员不得少于2人，并应当向当事人出示执法证件。  
国务院证券监督管理机构与当事人进行沟通协商的工作人员不得违反规定会见当事人及其委托的人。

**Article 11** The period for the securities regulatory authority of the State Council to communicate and consult with the party shall be six months. With the approval of the principal person in charge of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council or another person in charge authorized thereby, the period for communication and consultation may be extended, but the extended period shall not exceed six months at maximum.  
The securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall conduct communication and consultation with the party in person and prepare written records. The number of staff members of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council who conduct communication and consultation with the party shall not be less than two, and they shall present their law enforcement credentials to the party.  
Staff members of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council who conduct communication and consultation with the party shall not meet with the party or persons entrusted by the party in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院证券监督管理机构相关工作人员与案件有直接利害关系或者有其他关系，可能影响公正执法的，应当回避。  
当事人认为国务院证券监督管理机构相关工作人员有前款规定情形的，有权申请其回避。当事人提出回避申请，应当说明理由。国务院证券监督管理机构应当自当事人提出回避申请之日起3日内作出决定，并书面告知当事人。决定作出之前，被申请回避的人员不停止相关工作。

**Article 12** Where a relevant staff member of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council has a direct interest in the case or has any other relationship that may affect impartial law enforcement, that staff member shall withdraw.  
If a party believes that a relevant staff member of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council falls under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, the party shall have the right to apply for that staff member's withdrawal. The party applying for withdrawal shall state the reasons therefor. The securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall make a decision within three days from the date of receiving the application for withdrawal and shall notify the party in writing. Before the decision is made, the person whose withdrawal is applied for shall not cease the relevant work.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院证券监督管理机构经与当事人沟通协商，认可当事人作出的承诺的，应当与当事人签署承诺认可协议。承诺认可协议应当载明下列事项：  
（一）申请适用行政执法当事人承诺的事由；  
（二）当事人涉嫌违法行为的主要事实；  
（三）当事人承诺采取的纠正涉嫌违法行为、赔偿有关投资者损失、消除损害或者不良影响的具体措施；  
（四）承诺金数额及交纳方式；  
（五）当事人履行承诺的期限；  
（六）保障当事人商业秘密、个人隐私等权利的措施；  
（七）需要载明的其他事项。  
本办法所称承诺金，是指当事人为适用行政执法当事人承诺而交纳的资金。

**Article 13** Where the securities regulatory authority of the State Council, after communication and consultation with the party, accepts the commitments made by the party, it shall sign a commitment recognition agreement with the party. The commitment recognition agreement shall specify the following matters:  
(1) the grounds for applying for the commitment in administrative law enforcement;  
(2) the main facts of the illegal act suspected of being committed by the party;  
(3) the specific measures that the party commits to take to rectify the suspected illegal act, compensate relevant investors for losses, and eliminate damage or adverse effects;  
(4) the amount of the commitment deposit and the method of payment;  
(5) the time limit for the party to perform the commitments;  
(6) measures to protect the party's rights such as trade secrets and personal privacy;  
(7) other matters that need to be specified.  
For the purpose of these Measures, "commitment deposit" means the funds paid by the party for the application of the commitment in administrative law enforcement.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院证券监督管理机构确定承诺金数额应当综合考虑下列因素：  
（一）当事人因涉嫌违法行为可能获得的收益或者避免的损失；  
（二）当事人涉嫌违法行为依法可能被处以罚款、没收违法所得的金额；  
（三）投资者因当事人涉嫌违法行为所遭受的损失；  
（四）签署承诺认可协议时案件所处的执法阶段；  
（五）需要考虑的其他因素。  
国务院证券监督管理机构可以就适用行政执法当事人承诺涉及的专业问题征求证券期货交易场所、证券登记结算机构、投资者保护机构等相关机构或者专家学者的意见。

**Article 14** When determining the amount of the commitment deposit, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall comprehensively consider the following factors:  
(1) the profits that the party may have obtained or the losses that the party may have avoided as a result of the suspected illegal act;  
(2) the amount of fines or confiscation of illegal gains that may be imposed on the party according to law for the suspected illegal act;  
(3) the losses suffered by investors due to the suspected illegal act of the party;  
(4) the law enforcement stage of the case at the time of signing the commitment recognition agreement;  
(5) other factors that need to be considered.  
The securities regulatory authority of the State Council may solicit opinions from relevant institutions such as securities and futures trading venues, securities registration and settlement institutions, investor protection institutions, or experts and scholars on the professional issues involved in the application of the commitment in administrative law enforcement.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院证券监督管理机构与当事人签署承诺认可协议后，应当中止案件调查，向当事人出具中止调查决定书，并予以公告。  
当事人完全履行承诺认可协议后，国务院证券监督管理机构应当终止案件调查，向当事人出具终止调查决定书，并予以公告。国务院证券监督管理机构出具终止调查决定书后，对当事人涉嫌实施的同一个违法行为不再重新调查。  
申请适用行政执法当事人承诺的当事人为《证券法》等法律、行政法规和国务院证券监督管理机构规定的信息披露义务人的，应当依法履行信息披露义务。

**Article 15** After the securities regulatory authority of the State Council signs the commitment recognition agreement with the party, it shall suspend the investigation of the case, issue a decision to suspend the investigation to the party, and make a public announcement.  
After the party fully performs the commitment recognition agreement, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall terminate the investigation of the case, issue a decision to terminate the investigation to the party, and make a public announcement. After the securities regulatory authority of the State Council issues the decision to terminate the investigation, it shall not reinvestigate the same illegal act suspected of being committed by the party.  
A party that applies for the commitment in administrative law enforcement and is an information disclosure obligor as stipulated by the Securities Law, other laws and administrative regulations, and the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall perform its information disclosure obligations according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　有下列情形之一的，国务院证券监督管理机构应当终止适用行政执法当事人承诺：  
（一）当事人在签署承诺认可协议前撤回适用行政执法当事人承诺的申请；  
（二）未能在[本办法第十一条](#art-11)规定的期限内签署承诺认可协议；  
（三）承诺认可协议签署后，当事人因自身原因未履行或者未完全履行承诺；  
（四）承诺认可协议履行完毕前，发现当事人提交的材料存在虚假记载或者重大遗漏；  
（五）承诺认可协议履行完毕前，当事人因涉嫌证券期货犯罪被依法立案。  
发生前款规定情形的，国务院证券监督管理机构应当向当事人出具终止适用行政执法当事人承诺通知书；发生前款第一项至第四项情形的，还应当及时恢复案件调查。国务院证券监督管理机构在决定终止适用行政执法当事人承诺前，应当听取当事人的意见。

**Article 16** Under any of the following circumstances, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall terminate the application of the commitment in administrative law enforcement:  
(1) the party withdraws its application for the commitment in administrative law enforcement before signing the commitment recognition agreement;  
(2) the commitment recognition agreement fails to be signed within the period specified in Article 11 of these Measures;  
(3) after the signing of the commitment recognition agreement, the party fails to perform or fully perform the commitments due to its own reasons;  
(4) before the completion of the performance of the commitment recognition agreement, it is found that the materials submitted by the party contain false records or major omissions;  
(5) before the completion of the performance of the commitment recognition agreement, the party is placed on file for investigation according to law for suspected securities or futures crimes.  
Upon the occurrence of the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall issue a notice of termination of the application of the commitment in administrative law enforcement to the party; upon the occurrence of the circumstances specified in items (1) to (4) of the preceding paragraph, it shall also promptly resume the investigation of the case. Before deciding to terminate the application of the commitment in administrative law enforcement, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall hear the opinions of the party.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院证券监督管理机构应当建立集体决策制度，讨论决定行政执法当事人承诺实施过程中的申请受理、签署承诺认可协议、中止或者终止案件调查等重大事项，并经国务院证券监督管理机构主要负责人或者其授权的其他负责人批准后执行。

**Article 17** The securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall establish a collective decision-making system to discuss and decide on major matters in the implementation of the commitment in administrative law enforcement, such as accepting applications, signing commitment recognition agreements, and suspending or terminating the investigation of cases, which shall be executed after approval by the principal person in charge of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council or another person in charge authorized thereby.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　投资者因当事人涉嫌违法行为遭受损失的，可以向承诺金管理机构申请合理赔偿，也可以通过依法对当事人提起民事赔偿诉讼等其他途径获得赔偿。承诺金管理机构向投资者支付的赔偿总额不得超过涉及案件当事人实际交纳并用于赔偿的承诺金总额。投资者已通过其他途径获得赔偿的，不得就已获得赔偿的部分向承诺金管理机构申请赔偿。  
承诺金管理和使用的具体办法由国务院证券监督管理机构会同国务院财政部门另行制定。

**Article 18** Investors who suffer losses due to the suspected illegal act of the party may apply to the commitment deposit management institution for reasonable compensation, or may obtain compensation through other channels such as filing a civil compensation lawsuit against the party according to law. The total amount of compensation paid by the commitment deposit management institution to investors shall not exceed the total amount of commitment deposits actually paid by the party in the relevant case and used for compensation. Investors who have already obtained compensation through other channels shall not apply to the commitment deposit management institution for compensation for the part already compensated.  
The specific measures for the management and use of the commitment deposit shall be separately formulated by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council in conjunction with the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　当事人有下列情形之一的，由国务院证券监督管理机构记入证券期货市场诚信档案数据库，纳入全国信用信息共享平台，按照国家规定实施联合惩戒：  
（一）因自身原因未履行或者未完全履行承诺；  
（二）提交的材料存在虚假记载或者重大遗漏；  
（三）违背诚信原则的其他情形。

**Article 19** Where a party falls under any of the following circumstances, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall record it in the securities and futures market integrity archive database, incorporate it into the national credit information sharing platform, and implement joint disciplinary actions in accordance with state regulations:  
(1) failing to perform or fully perform the commitments due to its own reasons;  
(2) the submitted materials contain false records or major omissions;  
(3) other circumstances that violate the principle of good faith.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院证券监督管理机构工作人员违反规定适用行政执法当事人承诺，或者泄露履行职责中知悉的商业秘密、个人隐私的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 20** Where a staff member of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council applies the commitment in administrative law enforcement in violation of regulations, or discloses trade secrets or personal privacy learned in the performance of duties, the staff member shall be given disciplinary action according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本办法自2022年1月1日起施行。

**Article 21** These Measures shall come into force on January 1, 2022.
