# 机动车交通事故责任强制保险条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2006-07-01

> (2006年3月21日中华人民共和国国务院令第462号公布　根据2012年3月30日《国务院关于修改〈机动车交通事故责任强制保险条例〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2012年12月17日《国务院关于修改〈机动车交通事故责任强制保险条例〉的决定》第二次修订　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第四次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了保障机动车道路交通事故受害人依法得到赔偿，促进道路交通安全，根据《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法》、《中华人民共和国保险法》，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内道路上行驶的机动车的所有人或者管理人，应当依照《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法》的规定投保机动车交通事故责任强制保险。 机动车交通事故责任强制保险的投保、赔偿和监督管理，适用本条例。 第三条　本条例所称机动车交通事故责任强制保险，是指由保险公司对被保险机动车发生道路交通事故造成本车人员、被保险人以外的受害人的人身伤亡、财产损失，在责任限额内予以赔偿的强制性责任保险。 第四条　国务院保险监督管理机构依法对保险公司的机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务实施监督管理。 公安机关交通管理部门、农业(农业机械)主管部门(以下统称机动车管理部门)应当依法对机动车参加机动车交通事故责任强制保险的情况实施监督检查。对未参加机动车交通事故责任强制保险的机动车，机动车管理部门不得予以登记，机动车安全技术检验机构不得予以检验。 公安机关交通管理部门及其交通警察在调查处理道路交通安全违法行为和道路交通事故时，应当依法检查机动车交通事故责任强制保险的保险标志。 第二章　投保 第五条　保险公司可以从事机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务。 为了保证机动车交通事故责任强制保险制度的实行，国务院保险监督管理机构有权要求保险公司从事机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务。 除保险公司外，任何单位或者个人不得从事机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务。 第六条　机动车交通事故责任强制保险实行统一的保险条款和基础保险费率。国务院保险监督管理机构按照机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务总体上不盈利不亏损的原则审批保险费率。 国务院保险监督管理机构在审批保险费率时，可以聘请有关专业机构进行评估，可以举行听证会听取公众意见。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障机动车道路交通事故受害人依法得到赔偿，促进道路交通安全，根据《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法》、《中华人民共和国保险法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Road Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and the Insurance Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of ensuring that victims of road traffic accidents involving motor vehicles receive compensation according to law and promoting road traffic safety.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内道路上行驶的机动车的所有人或者管理人，应当依照《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法》的规定投保机动车交通事故责任强制保险。  
机动车交通事故责任强制保险的投保、赔偿和监督管理，适用本条例。

**Article 2** The owner or manager of a motor vehicle running on roads within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Road Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, effect compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability.  
The insurance, compensation, and supervision and administration of compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability shall be governed by these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称机动车交通事故责任强制保险，是指由保险公司对被保险机动车发生道路交通事故造成本车人员、被保险人以外的受害人的人身伤亡、财产损失，在责任限额内予以赔偿的强制性责任保险。

**Article 3** Compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability as mentioned in these Regulations means the compulsory liability insurance under which an insurance company shall, within the liability limit, compensate for the personal injury or death and property damage of victims other than the occupants of the insured motor vehicle and the insured caused by a road traffic accident involving the insured motor vehicle.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院保险监督管理机构依法对保险公司的机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务实施监督管理。  
公安机关交通管理部门、农业(农业机械)主管部门(以下统称机动车管理部门)应当依法对机动车参加机动车交通事故责任强制保险的情况实施监督检查。对未参加机动车交通事故责任强制保险的机动车，机动车管理部门不得予以登记，机动车安全技术检验机构不得予以检验。  
公安机关交通管理部门及其交通警察在调查处理道路交通安全违法行为和道路交通事故时，应当依法检查机动车交通事故责任强制保险的保险标志。

**Article 4** The insurance regulatory authority under the State Council shall, according to law, exercise supervision and administration over the compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability business of insurance companies.  
The traffic administrative department of the public security organ and the agriculture (agricultural machinery) department (hereinafter collectively referred to as the motor vehicle administrative department) shall, according to law, carry out supervision and inspection over the participation of motor vehicles in compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability. No motor vehicle that has not participated in compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability shall be registered by the motor vehicle administrative department, nor shall it be inspected by the motor vehicle safety technical inspection institution.  
The traffic administrative department of the public security organ and its traffic police shall, according to law, inspect the insurance mark for compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability when investigating and handling road traffic safety violations and road traffic accidents.

### 第二章　投保

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　保险公司可以从事机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务。  
为了保证机动车交通事故责任强制保险制度的实行，国务院保险监督管理机构有权要求保险公司从事机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务。  
除保险公司外，任何单位或者个人不得从事机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务。

**Article 5** Insurance companies may engage in the business of compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability.  
For the purpose of ensuring the implementation of the compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability system, the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council has the right to require insurance companies to engage in the business of compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability.  
No entity or individual other than an insurance company shall engage in the business of compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　机动车交通事故责任强制保险实行统一的保险条款和基础保险费率。国务院保险监督管理机构按照机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务总体上不盈利不亏损的原则审批保险费率。  
国务院保险监督管理机构在审批保险费率时，可以聘请有关专业机构进行评估，可以举行听证会听取公众意见。

**Article 6** Compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability shall adopt uniform insurance clauses and basic premium rates. The insurance regulatory authority under the State Council shall approve the premium rates in accordance with the principle that the compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability business shall, as a whole, be neither profitable nor loss-making.  
When approving the premium rates, the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council may engage relevant professional institutions to conduct assessments and may hold hearings to solicit public opinions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　保险公司的机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务，应当与其他保险业务分开管理，单独核算。  
国务院保险监督管理机构应当每年对保险公司的机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务情况进行核查，并向社会公布；根据保险公司机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务的总体盈利或者亏损情况，可以要求或者允许保险公司相应调整保险费率。  
调整保险费率的幅度较大的，国务院保险监督管理机构应当进行听证。

**Article 7** The compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability business of an insurance company shall be managed separately from other insurance businesses and accounted for independently.  
The insurance regulatory authority under the State Council shall annually verify the situation of the compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability business of insurance companies and announce it to the public; based on the overall profit or loss situation of the compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability business of insurance companies, it may require or permit the insurance companies to correspondingly adjust the premium rates.  
If the adjustment range of the premium rates is relatively large, the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council shall hold a hearing.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　被保险机动车没有发生道路交通安全违法行为和道路交通事故的，保险公司应当在下一年度降低其保险费率。在此后的年度内，被保险机动车仍然没有发生道路交通安全违法行为和道路交通事故的，保险公司应当继续降低其保险费率，直至最低标准。被保险机动车发生道路交通安全违法行为或者道路交通事故的，保险公司应当在下一年度提高其保险费率。多次发生道路交通安全违法行为、道路交通事故，或者发生重大道路交通事故的，保险公司应当加大提高其保险费率的幅度。在道路交通事故中被保险人没有过错的，不提高其保险费率。降低或者提高保险费率的标准，由国务院保险监督管理机构会同国务院公安部门制定。

**Article 8** If the insured motor vehicle has not experienced any road traffic safety violation or road traffic accident, the insurance company shall reduce its premium rate for the next year. In subsequent years, if the insured motor vehicle still has not experienced any road traffic safety violation or road traffic accident, the insurance company shall continue to reduce its premium rate until the minimum standard is reached. If the insured motor vehicle experiences a road traffic safety violation or a road traffic accident, the insurance company shall increase its premium rate for the next year. If the insured motor vehicle experiences multiple road traffic safety violations or road traffic accidents, or experiences a major road traffic accident, the insurance company shall increase the extent of the increase in its premium rate. If the insured is not at fault in a road traffic accident, its premium rate shall not be increased. The standards for reducing or increasing premium rates shall be formulated by the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council jointly with the public security department under the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院保险监督管理机构、国务院公安部门、国务院农业主管部门以及其他有关部门应当逐步建立有关机动车交通事故责任强制保险、道路交通安全违法行为和道路交通事故的信息共享机制。

**Article 9** The insurance regulatory authority under the State Council, the public security department under the State Council, the agricultural department under the State Council, and other relevant departments shall gradually establish an information sharing mechanism concerning compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability, road traffic safety violations, and road traffic accidents.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　投保人在投保时应当选择从事机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务的保险公司，被选择的保险公司不得拒绝或者拖延承保。  
国务院保险监督管理机构应当将从事机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务的保险公司向社会公示。

**Article 10** The policyholder shall, when applying for insurance, select an insurance company that engages in the business of compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability, and the selected insurance company shall not refuse or delay underwriting.  
The insurance regulatory authority under the State Council shall publicize to the public the insurance companies that engage in the business of compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　投保人投保时，应当向保险公司如实告知重要事项。  
重要事项包括机动车的种类、厂牌型号、识别代码、牌照号码、使用性质和机动车所有人或者管理人的姓名(名称)、性别、年龄、住所、身份证或者驾驶证号码(组织机构代码)、续保前该机动车发生事故的情况以及国务院保险监督管理机构规定的其他事项。

**Article 11** When applying for insurance, the applicant shall truthfully disclose material matters to the insurance company.  
Material matters include the type, brand and model, identification code, license plate number, nature of use of the motor vehicle, and the name (title), gender, age, domicile, identity card or driver's license number (organization code) of the owner or manager of the motor vehicle, the circumstances of accidents involving the motor vehicle prior to renewal, and other matters prescribed by the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　签订机动车交通事故责任强制保险合同时，投保人应当一次支付全部保险费；保险公司应当向投保人签发保险单、保险标志。保险单、保险标志应当注明保险单号码、车牌号码、保险期限、保险公司的名称、地址和理赔电话号码。  
被保险人应当在被保险机动车上放置保险标志。  
保险标志式样全国统一。保险单、保险标志由国务院保险监督管理机构监制。任何单位或者个人不得伪造、变造或者使用伪造、变造的保险单、保险标志。

**Article 12** When concluding a compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance contract, the applicant shall pay the full insurance premium in a lump sum; the insurance company shall issue an insurance policy and insurance mark to the applicant. The insurance policy and insurance mark shall indicate the policy number, license plate number, insurance period, name, address and claims telephone number of the insurance company.  
The insured shall place the insurance mark on the insured motor vehicle.  
The design of the insurance mark shall be uniform nationwide. The insurance policy and insurance mark shall be supervised and manufactured by the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council. No unit or individual may forge, alter, or use forged or altered insurance policies or insurance marks.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　签订机动车交通事故责任强制保险合同时，投保人不得在保险条款和保险费率之外，向保险公司提出附加其他条件的要求。  
签订机动车交通事故责任强制保险合同时，保险公司不得强制投保人订立商业保险合同以及提出附加其他条件的要求。

**Article 13** When concluding a compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance contract, the applicant shall not propose additional conditions to the insurance company beyond the insurance clauses and insurance premium rates.  
When concluding a compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance contract, the insurance company shall not compel the applicant to conclude a commercial insurance contract or propose additional conditions.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　保险公司不得解除机动车交通事故责任强制保险合同；但是，投保人对重要事项未履行如实告知义务的除外。  
投保人对重要事项未履行如实告知义务，保险公司解除合同前，应当书面通知投保人，投保人应当自收到通知之日起5日内履行如实告知义务；投保人在上述期限内履行如实告知义务的，保险公司不得解除合同。

**Article 14** The insurance company shall not dissolve the compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance contract; except where the applicant fails to perform the obligation of truthful disclosure of material matters.  
If the applicant fails to perform the obligation of truthful disclosure of material matters, before dissolving the contract, the insurance company shall notify the applicant in writing, and the applicant shall perform the obligation of truthful disclosure within 5 days from the date of receipt of the notice; if the applicant performs the obligation of truthful disclosure within the aforesaid period, the insurance company shall not dissolve the contract.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　保险公司解除机动车交通事故责任强制保险合同的，应当收回保险单和保险标志，并书面通知机动车管理部门。

**Article 15** If the insurance company dissolves the compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance contract, it shall retrieve the insurance policy and insurance mark, and notify the motor vehicle administrative department in writing.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　投保人不得解除机动车交通事故责任强制保险合同，但有下列情形之一的除外：  
(一)被保险机动车被依法注销登记的；  
(二)被保险机动车办理停驶的；  
(三)被保险机动车经公安机关证实丢失的。

**Article 16** The applicant shall not dissolve the compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance contract, except under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The insured motor vehicle is deregistered according to law;  
(2) The insured motor vehicle is suspended from operation;  
(3) The insured motor vehicle is confirmed by the public security organ to be lost.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　机动车交通事故责任强制保险合同解除前，保险公司应当按照合同承担保险责任。  
合同解除时，保险公司可以收取自保险责任开始之日起至合同解除之日止的保险费，剩余部分的保险费退还投保人。

**Article 17** Before the dissolution of the compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance contract, the insurance company shall bear the insurance liability in accordance with the contract.  
Upon dissolution of the contract, the insurance company may collect the insurance premium from the commencement of the insurance liability to the date of dissolution of the contract, and the remaining portion of the insurance premium shall be refunded to the applicant.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　被保险机动车所有权转移的，应当办理机动车交通事故责任强制保险合同变更手续。

**Article 18** If the ownership of the insured motor vehicle is transferred, the formalities for modification of the compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance contract shall be completed.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　机动车交通事故责任强制保险合同期满，投保人应当及时续保，并提供上一年度的保险单。

**Article 19** Upon expiration of the compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance contract, the applicant shall renew the insurance in a timely manner and provide the insurance policy of the preceding year.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　机动车交通事故责任强制保险的保险期间为1年，但有下列情形之一的，投保人可以投保短期机动车交通事故责任强制保险：  
(一)境外机动车临时入境的；  
(二)机动车临时上道路行驶的；  
(三)机动车距规定的报废期限不足1年的；  
(四)国务院保险监督管理机构规定的其他情形。

**Article 20** The insurance period for compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance shall be one year, except that under any of the following circumstances, the applicant may purchase short-term compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance:  
(1) A motor vehicle from outside the territory is temporarily entering the country;  
(2) A motor vehicle is temporarily driven on the road;  
(3) The motor vehicle has less than one year until the prescribed scrap deadline;  
(4) Other circumstances prescribed by the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council.

### 第三章　赔偿

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　被保险机动车发生道路交通事故造成本车人员、被保险人以外的受害人人身伤亡、财产损失的，由保险公司依法在机动车交通事故责任强制保险责任限额范围内予以赔偿。  
道路交通事故的损失是由受害人故意造成的，保险公司不予赔偿。

**Article 21** Where an insured motor vehicle causes personal injury or death or property damage to a victim other than the personnel of the vehicle and the insured in a road traffic accident, the insurance company shall make compensation according to law within the liability limits of the compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance.  
Where the loss in a road traffic accident is intentionally caused by the victim, the insurance company shall not make compensation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　有下列情形之一的，保险公司在机动车交通事故责任强制保险责任限额范围内垫付抢救费用，并有权向致害人追偿：  
(一)驾驶人未取得驾驶资格或者醉酒的；  
(二)被保险机动车被盗抢期间肇事的；  
(三)被保险人故意制造道路交通事故的。  
有前款所列情形之一，发生道路交通事故的，造成受害人的财产损失，保险公司不承担赔偿责任。

**Article 22** Under any of the following circumstances, the insurance company shall advance the rescue expenses within the liability limits of the compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance and shall have the right to claim reimbursement from the injuring party:  
(1) the driver does not have the driving qualification or is intoxicated;  
(2) the insured motor vehicle causes an accident during the theft or robbery thereof;  
(3) the insured intentionally causes a road traffic accident.  
Where a road traffic accident occurs under any of the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph and causes property damage to the victim, the insurance company shall not bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　机动车交通事故责任强制保险在全国范围内实行统一的责任限额。责任限额分为死亡伤残赔偿限额、医疗费用赔偿限额、财产损失赔偿限额以及被保险人在道路交通事故中无责任的赔偿限额。  
机动车交通事故责任强制保险责任限额由国务院保险监督管理机构会同国务院公安部门、国务院卫生主管部门、国务院农业主管部门规定。

**Article 23** The compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance shall implement uniform liability limits across the country. The liability limits shall be divided into death and disability compensation limit, medical expense compensation limit, property damage compensation limit, and the compensation limit for the insured who has no liability in the road traffic accident.  
The liability limits for compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance shall be prescribed by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council jointly with the public security department of the State Council, the health administrative department of the State Council, and the agricultural administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家设立道路交通事故社会救助基金(以下简称救助基金)。有下列情形之一时，道路交通事故中受害人人身伤亡的丧葬费用、部分或者全部抢救费用，由救助基金先行垫付，救助基金管理机构有权向道路交通事故责任人追偿：  
(一)抢救费用超过机动车交通事故责任强制保险责任限额的；  
(二)肇事机动车未参加机动车交通事故责任强制保险的；  
(三)机动车肇事后逃逸的。

**Article 24** The State shall establish a road traffic accident social assistance fund (hereinafter referred to as the Assistance Fund). Under any of the following circumstances, the funeral expenses and part or all of the rescue expenses for personal injury or death of a victim in a road traffic accident shall be advanced by the Assistance Fund, and the administrative agency of the Assistance Fund shall have the right to claim reimbursement from the person liable for the road traffic accident:  
(1) the rescue expenses exceed the liability limits of the compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance;  
(2) the motor vehicle causing the accident is not covered by the compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance;  
(3) the motor vehicle escapes after causing an accident.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　救助基金的来源包括：  
(一)按照机动车交通事故责任强制保险的保险费的一定比例提取的资金；  
(二)对未按照规定投保机动车交通事故责任强制保险的机动车的所有人、管理人的罚款；  
(三)救助基金管理机构依法向道路交通事故责任人追偿的资金；  
(四)救助基金孳息；  
(五)其他资金。

**Article 25** The sources of the Assistance Fund shall include:  
(1) funds drawn at a certain proportion of the insurance premiums for compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance;  
(2) fines imposed on the owners or managers of motor vehicles that fail to take out compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance in accordance with the regulations;  
(3) funds recovered by the administrative agency of the Assistance Fund from the persons liable for road traffic accidents according to law;  
(4) interest generated from the Assistance Fund;  
(5) other funds.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　救助基金的具体管理办法，由国务院财政部门会同国务院保险监督管理机构、国务院公安部门、国务院卫生主管部门、国务院农业主管部门制定试行。

**Article 26** The specific administrative measures for the Assistance Fund shall be formulated and implemented on a trial basis by the finance department of the State Council jointly with the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, the public security department of the State Council, the health administrative department of the State Council, and the agricultural administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　被保险机动车发生道路交通事故，被保险人或者受害人通知保险公司的，保险公司应当立即给予答复，告知被保险人或者受害人具体的赔偿程序等有关事项。

**Article 27** Where an insured motor vehicle is involved in a road traffic accident and the insured or the victim notifies the insurance company, the insurance company shall immediately respond and inform the insured or the victim of matters such as the specific compensation procedures.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　被保险机动车发生道路交通事故的，由被保险人向保险公司申请赔偿保险金。保险公司应当自收到赔偿申请之日起1日内，书面告知被保险人需要向保险公司提供的与赔偿有关的证明和资料。

**Article 28** Where an insured motor vehicle is involved in a road traffic accident, the insured shall apply to the insurance company for compensation insurance money. The insurance company shall, within one day from the date of receipt of the application for compensation, inform the insured in writing of the certificates and materials related to compensation that need to be provided to the insurance company.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　保险公司应当自收到被保险人提供的证明和资料之日起5日内，对是否属于保险责任作出核定，并将结果通知被保险人；对不属于保险责任的，应当书面说明理由；对属于保险责任的，在与被保险人达成赔偿保险金的协议后10日内，赔偿保险金。

**Article 29** The insurance company shall, within five days from the date of receipt of the certificates and materials provided by the insured, verify whether the case falls within the insurance liability and notify the insured of the result; where the case does not fall within the insurance liability, it shall state the reasons in writing; where the case falls within the insurance liability, it shall pay the insurance money within ten days after reaching an agreement with the insured on the compensation insurance money.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　被保险人与保险公司对赔偿有争议的，可以依法申请仲裁或者向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 30** Where the insured and the insurance company have a dispute over compensation, they may apply for arbitration or institute a prosecution with the People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　保险公司可以向被保险人赔偿保险金，也可以直接向受害人赔偿保险金。但是，因抢救受伤人员需要保险公司支付或者垫付抢救费用的，保险公司在接到公安机关交通管理部门通知后，经核对应当及时向医疗机构支付或者垫付抢救费用。  
因抢救受伤人员需要救助基金管理机构垫付抢救费用的，救助基金管理机构在接到公安机关交通管理部门通知后，经核对应当及时向医疗机构垫付抢救费用。

**Article 31** An insurance company may pay insurance compensation to the insured, or may directly pay insurance compensation to the victim. However, if the insurance company needs to pay or advance expenses for the rescue of an injured person, the insurance company shall, upon receiving notice from the traffic administrative department of the public security organ and after verification, timely pay or advance the rescue expenses to the medical institution.  
If the rescue fund management institution needs to advance rescue expenses for the rescue of an injured person, the rescue fund management institution shall, upon receiving notice from the traffic administrative department of the public security organ and after verification, timely advance the rescue expenses to the medical institution.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　医疗机构应当参照国务院卫生主管部门组织制定的有关临床诊疗指南，抢救、治疗道路交通事故中的受伤人员。

**Article 32** A medical institution shall, by reference to the relevant clinical diagnosis and treatment guidelines formulated by the health authority of the State Council, rescue and treat the injured persons in road traffic accidents.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　保险公司赔偿保险金或者垫付抢救费用，救助基金管理机构垫付抢救费用，需要向有关部门、医疗机构核实有关情况的，有关部门、医疗机构应当予以配合。

**Article 33** Where an insurance company pays insurance compensation or advances rescue expenses, or a rescue fund management institution advances rescue expenses, and it is necessary to verify the relevant information with the relevant departments or medical institutions, the relevant departments and medical institutions shall cooperate.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　保险公司、救助基金管理机构的工作人员对当事人的个人隐私应当保密。

**Article 34** The staff members of an insurance company or a rescue fund management institution shall keep confidential the personal privacy of the parties.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　道路交通事故损害赔偿项目和标准依照有关法律的规定执行。

**Article 35** The items and criteria for compensation for damages in road traffic accidents shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws.

### 第四章　罚则

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　保险公司以外的单位或者个人，非法从事机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务的，由国务院保险监督管理机构予以取缔；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由国务院保险监督管理机构没收违法所得，违法所得20万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足20万元的，处20万元以上100万元以下罚款。

**Article 36** Where a unit or individual other than an insurance company illegally engages in the compulsory insurance business for motor vehicle traffic accident liability, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall ban it; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall confiscate the illegal gains, and in addition, if the illegal gains are 200,000 yuan or more, impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the amount of the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 200,000 yuan, impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than one million yuan.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　保险公司违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由国务院保险监督管理机构责令改正，处5万元以上30万元以下罚款；情节严重的，可以限制业务范围、责令停止接受新业务或者吊销经营保险业务许可证：  
(一)拒绝或者拖延承保机动车交通事故责任强制保险的；  
(二)未按照统一的保险条款和基础保险费率从事机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务的；  
(三)未将机动车交通事故责任强制保险业务和其他保险业务分开管理，单独核算的；  
(四)强制投保人订立商业保险合同的；  
(五)违反规定解除机动车交通事故责任强制保险合同的；  
(六)拒不履行约定的赔偿保险金义务的；  
(七)未按照规定及时支付或者垫付抢救费用的。

**Article 37** Where an insurance company violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it may restrict its business scope, order it to cease accepting new business, or revoke its insurance business license:  
(1) Refusing or delaying to underwrite compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability;  
(2) Engaging in compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability not in accordance with the unified insurance clauses and basic premium rates;  
(3) Failing to manage the compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability business separately from other insurance businesses and to keep separate accounts;  
(4) Compelling the policyholder to enter into a commercial insurance contract;  
(5) Dissolving a compulsory insurance contract for motor vehicle traffic accident liability in violation of regulations;  
(6) Refusing to perform the agreed obligation to pay insurance compensation;  
(7) Failing to pay or advance rescue expenses in a timely manner according to regulations.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　机动车所有人、管理人未按照规定投保机动车交通事故责任强制保险的，由公安机关交通管理部门扣留机动车，通知机动车所有人、管理人依照规定投保，处依照规定投保最低责任限额应缴纳的保险费的2倍罚款。  
机动车所有人、管理人依照规定补办机动车交通事故责任强制保险的，应当及时退还机动车。

**Article 38** Where the owner or manager of a motor vehicle fails to take out compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability in accordance with regulations, the traffic administrative department of the public security organ shall detain the motor vehicle, notify the owner or manager to take out insurance in accordance with regulations, and impose a fine of two times the insurance premium payable for the minimum liability coverage according to regulations.  
Where the owner or manager of a motor vehicle subsequently takes out compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability in accordance with regulations, the motor vehicle shall be promptly returned.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　上道路行驶的机动车未放置保险标志的，公安机关交通管理部门应当扣留机动车，通知当事人提供保险标志或者补办相应手续，可以处警告或者20元以上200元以下罚款。  
当事人提供保险标志或者补办相应手续的，应当及时退还机动车。

**Article 39** Where a motor vehicle driven on the road does not display an insurance mark, the traffic administrative department of the public security organ shall detain the motor vehicle, notify the party to provide an insurance mark or complete the relevant procedures, and may impose a warning or a fine of not less than 20 yuan but not more than 200 yuan.  
Where the party provides an insurance mark or completes the relevant procedures, the motor vehicle shall be promptly returned.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　伪造、变造或者使用伪造、变造的保险标志，或者使用其他机动车的保险标志，由公安机关交通管理部门予以收缴，扣留该机动车，处200元以上2000元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
当事人提供相应的合法证明或者补办相应手续的，应当及时退还机动车。

**Article 40** Where anyone forges, alters, or uses forged or altered insurance marks, or uses the insurance mark of another motor vehicle, the traffic administrative department of the public security organ shall confiscate the mark, detain the motor vehicle, and impose a fine of not less than 200 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where the party provides the corresponding legal proof or completes the relevant procedures, the motor vehicle shall be promptly returned.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
(一)投保人，是指与保险公司订立机动车交通事故责任强制保险合同，并按照合同负有支付保险费义务的机动车的所有人、管理人。  
(二)被保险人，是指投保人及其允许的合法驾驶人。  
(三)抢救费用，是指机动车发生道路交通事故导致人员受伤时，医疗机构参照国务院卫生主管部门组织制定的有关临床诊疗指南，对生命体征不平稳和虽然生命体征平稳但如果不采取处理措施会产生生命危险，或者导致残疾、器官功能障碍，或者导致病程明显延长的受伤人员，采取必要的处理措施所发生的医疗费用。

**Article 41** For the purpose of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) "Policyholder" means the owner or manager of a motor vehicle who concludes a compulsory insurance contract for motor vehicle traffic accident liability with an insurance company and who is obligated to pay insurance premiums under the contract.  
(2) "Insured" means the policyholder and the lawful driver permitted by the policyholder.  
(3) "Rescue expenses" means the medical expenses incurred when a motor vehicle causes personal injury in a road traffic accident, for which a medical institution takes necessary treatment measures for an injured person whose vital signs are unstable or, although vital signs are stable, failure to take treatment measures would cause life-threatening danger, or result in disability, organ dysfunction, or a significantly prolonged course of the disease, with reference to the relevant clinical diagnosis and treatment guidelines formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　挂车不投保机动车交通事故责任强制保险。发生道路交通事故造成人身伤亡、财产损失的，由牵引车投保的保险公司在机动车交通事故责任强制保险责任限额范围内予以赔偿；不足的部分，由牵引车方和挂车方依照法律规定承担赔偿责任。

**Article 42** A trailer shall not be covered by compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability. Where a road traffic accident occurs causing personal injury or death or property damage, the insurance company of the towing vehicle shall make compensation within the liability limit of the compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability; the part in excess thereof shall be borne by the towing vehicle party and the trailer party in accordance with the provisions of laws.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　机动车在道路以外的地方通行时发生事故，造成人身伤亡、财产损失的赔偿，比照适用本条例。

**Article 43** Where an accident occurs while a motor vehicle is traveling in a place other than a road, causing personal injury or death or property damage, these Regulations shall apply mutatis mutandis to the compensation.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队在编机动车参加机动车交通事故责任强制保险的办法，由中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队另行规定。

**Article 44** Measures for motor vehicles on active service of the People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force to participate in compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability shall be separately prescribed by the People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　机动车所有人、管理人自本条例施行之日起3个月内投保机动车交通事故责任强制保险；本条例施行前已经投保商业性机动车第三者责任保险的，保险期满，应当投保机动车交通事故责任强制保险。

**Article 45** A motor vehicle owner or manager shall, within three months from the date on which these Regulations come into force, take out compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability; where a commercial third-party liability insurance for motor vehicles has been taken out before these Regulations come into force, upon expiration of such insurance, the motor vehicle owner or manager shall take out compulsory insurance for motor vehicle traffic accident liability.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本条例自2006年7月1日起施行。

**Article 46** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 2006.
