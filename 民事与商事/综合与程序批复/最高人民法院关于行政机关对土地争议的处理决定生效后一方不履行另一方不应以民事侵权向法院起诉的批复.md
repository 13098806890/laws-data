### 最高人民法院关于行政机关对土地争议的处理决定生效后一方不履行另一方不应以民事侵权向法院起诉的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最 高 人 民 法 院  
  
关于行政机关对土地争议的处理决定生效后  
  
一方不履行另一方不应以民事侵权向法院起诉的批复  
  
（90）法民字第2号  
  
河北省高级人民法院：  
  
你省《关于人民政府对土地所有权和使用权争议做出处理决定后，一方当事人不服而又不向法院起诉，也不执行，期满后对方当事人可否以侵权案向人民法院起诉，以及如何处理的请示报告》收悉。经研究认为：行政机关对土地争议的处理决定生效后，一方当事人不履行的，对方当事人不应以民事侵权案向法院起诉，可向行政机关提出申请执行，该行政机关依照[行政诉讼法第六十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-66)的规定，可以申请人民法院强制执行，或依法强制执行。  
  
1991年7月24日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply Concerning the Issue That Where One Party Fails to Comply with an Effective Decision on a Land Dispute Made by an Administrative Authority, the Other Party Shall Not Prosecute in Court on the Ground of Civil Tort  
(1990) Fa Min Zi No. 2  
Higher People's Court of Hebei Province:  
We have received your Request for Instructions and Report concerning the issue that after the People's Government makes a decision on a dispute over land ownership or right of use, where one party disagrees with the decision and neither files a prosecution with the court nor performs the decision, whether the other party may file a prosecution with the People's Court on the ground of tort upon expiration of the time limit, and how to handle such a case. Upon examination, it is considered that: after an administrative authority's decision on a land dispute takes effect, if one party fails to perform it, the other party shall not file a prosecution with the court on the ground of civil tort, but may apply to the administrative authority for enforcement; the said administrative authority may, in accordance with Article 66 of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution, or conduct compulsory execution according to law.  
July 24, 1991
