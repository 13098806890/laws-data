# 北京市新技术产业开发试验区暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：1988-05-19

> (1988年5月10日国务院批准　1988年5月20日北京市人民政府发布) 第一条　为促进科学技术和生产直接结合，科学技术和其他生产要素优化组合，推动技术、经济的发展，扶植新技术产业开发试验区创建，制定本条例。 第二条　以中关村地区为中心，在北京市海淀区划出一百平方公里左右的区域，建立外向型、开放型的新技术产业开发试验区(以下简称试验区)。 试验区的具体范围，由北京市人民政府规划。 第三条　本条例适用于研究、开发、生产、经营一种或多种新技术及其产品的技术密集、智力密集的经济实体。 新技术及其产品的范围，由北京市人民政府根据国家科学技术委员会制订的目录另行规定。 新技术企业的技术性收入、研究开发经费、新产品产值等比例标准，由北京市人民政府商国家科学技术委员会制定。 第四条　试验区内的新技术企业，经北京市人民政府指定的部门认定后，按照国家有关规定，到工商行政管理部门登记。 第五条　对试验区的新技术企业，实行下列减征或免征税收的优惠： (一)减按15%税率征收所得税。企业出口产品的产值达到当年总产值40%以上的，经税务部门核定，减按10%税率征收所得税。 (二)新技术企业自开办之日起，三年内免征所得税。经北京市人民政府指定的部门批准，第四至六年可按前项规定的税率，减半征收所得税。 (三)经北京市人民政府批准，可以免购国家重点建设债券。 (四)以自筹资金新建技术开发的生产、经营性用房，自1988年起，五年内免征建筑税。 试验区内设立的外商投资企业，符合新技术企业标准的，适用以上减征或者免征税收的优惠。 第六条　试验区内新技术企业的生产、经营性基本建设项目，按照统一规划安排建设，不纳入固定资产投资规模，并简化审批手续，优先安排施工。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为促进科学技术和生产直接结合，科学技术和其他生产要素优化组合，推动技术、经济的发展，扶植新技术产业开发试验区创建，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of promoting the direct integration of science and technology with production, optimizing the combination of science and technology with other factors of production, advancing the development of technology and economy, and supporting the establishment of new technology industry development experiment zones.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　以中关村地区为中心，在北京市海淀区划出一百平方公里左右的区域，建立外向型、开放型的新技术产业开发试验区(以下简称试验区)。  
试验区的具体范围，由北京市人民政府规划。

**Article 2** An export-oriented and open-type new technology industry development experiment zone (hereinafter referred to as the Experiment Zone) shall be established in the Haidian District of Beijing Municipality, centering on the Zhongguancun area, with an area of approximately one hundred square kilometers delineated.  
The specific scope of the Experiment Zone shall be planned by the People's Government of Beijing Municipality.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例适用于研究、开发、生产、经营一种或多种新技术及其产品的技术密集、智力密集的经济实体。  
新技术及其产品的范围，由北京市人民政府根据国家科学技术委员会制订的目录另行规定。  
新技术企业的技术性收入、研究开发经费、新产品产值等比例标准，由北京市人民政府商国家科学技术委员会制定。

**Article 3** These Regulations shall apply to technology-intensive and knowledge-intensive economic entities that research, develop, produce, and operate one or more new technologies and their products.  
The scope of new technologies and their products shall be separately prescribed by the People's Government of Beijing Municipality in accordance with the catalogue formulated by the State Science and Technology Commission.  
The proportional standards for technology-based income, research and development expenditures, and new product output value of new technology enterprises shall be formulated by the People's Government of Beijing Municipality in consultation with the State Science and Technology Commission.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　试验区内的新技术企业，经北京市人民政府指定的部门认定后，按照国家有关规定，到工商行政管理部门登记。

**Article 4** New technology enterprises within the Experiment Zone shall, after being recognized by the department designated by the People's Government of Beijing Municipality, register with the administrative department for industry and commerce in accordance with the relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对试验区的新技术企业，实行下列减征或免征税收的优惠：  
(一)减按15%税率征收所得税。企业出口产品的产值达到当年总产值40%以上的，经税务部门核定，减按10%税率征收所得税。  
(二)新技术企业自开办之日起，三年内免征所得税。经北京市人民政府指定的部门批准，第四至六年可按前项规定的税率，减半征收所得税。  
(三)经北京市人民政府批准，可以免购国家重点建设债券。  
(四)以自筹资金新建技术开发的生产、经营性用房，自1988年起，五年内免征建筑税。  
试验区内设立的外商投资企业，符合新技术企业标准的，适用以上减征或者免征税收的优惠。

**Article 5** The following preferential tax reduction or exemption policies shall be implemented for new technology enterprises in the Experiment Zone:  
(1) Income tax shall be levied at a reduced rate of 15%. Where the value of exported products of an enterprise accounts for more than 40% of its total annual output value, upon verification by the tax authorities, income tax shall be levied at a reduced rate of 10%.  
(2) New technology enterprises shall be exempted from income tax for three years commencing from the date of establishment. With the approval of the department designated by the People's Government of Beijing Municipality, income tax may be levied at half the rate specified in the preceding paragraph from the fourth to the sixth year.  
(3) With the approval of the People's Government of Beijing Municipality, an enterprise may be exempted from purchasing state key construction bonds.  
(4) For production and operational premises newly built for technology development with self-raised funds, the construction tax shall be exempted for five years commencing from 1988.  
Foreign-invested enterprises established within the Experiment Zone that meet the standards for new technology enterprises shall be eligible for the above preferential tax reduction or exemption policies.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　试验区内新技术企业的生产、经营性基本建设项目，按照统一规划安排建设，不纳入固定资产投资规模，并简化审批手续，优先安排施工。

**Article 6** Production and operational capital construction projects of new technology enterprises within the Experiment Zone shall be constructed in accordance with the unified plan, shall not be included in the scale of fixed asset investment, and the approval procedures shall be simplified, with priority given to construction.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　试验区内的新技术企业生产出口产品所需的进口原材料和零部件，免领进口许可证，海关凭合同和北京市人民政府指定部门的批准文件验收。经海关批准，在试验区内可以设立保税仓库、保税工厂，海关按照进料加工，对进口的原材料和零部件进行监管；按实际加工出口数量，免征进口关税和进口环节产品税或增值税。出口产品免征出口关税。保税货物转为内销，必须经原审批部门批准和海关许可，并照章纳税。属于国家限制进口或者实行进口许可证管理的产品，需按国家有关规定补办进口批件或进口许可证。  
新技术企业用于新技术开发，进口国内不能生产的仪器和设备，凭审批部门的批准文件，经海关审核后，五年内免征进口关税。  
海关可在试验区内设置机构或派驻监督小组。

**Article 7** The import of raw materials and spare parts required for the production of export products by new technology enterprises in the Experiment Zone shall be exempted from the requirement for import licenses, and the Customs shall release them upon the basis of the contract and the approval document issued by the department designated by the People's Government of Beijing Municipality. With the approval of the Customs, bonded warehouses and bonded factories may be established within the Experiment Zone, and the Customs shall supervise the imported raw materials and spare parts in accordance with the processing of imported materials; the import duties and product tax or value-added tax at the import stage shall be exempted according to the actual quantity processed and exported. Export products shall be exempted from export duties. The conversion of bonded goods for domestic sale must be subject to the approval of the original approving department and the permission of the Customs, and taxes shall be paid in accordance with the regulations. For products that are subject to state import restrictions or import license management, import approval documents or import licenses shall be obtained retroactively in accordance with relevant state provisions.  
Instruments and equipment that cannot be manufactured domestically and are imported by new technology enterprises for new technology development shall, upon the basis of the approval document of the approving department and after examination by the Customs, be exempted from import duties within five years.  
The Customs may set up offices or dispatch supervisory teams within the Experiment Zone.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　所有减免的税款，作为“国家扶植基金”，由企业专项用于新技术开发和生产的发展，不得用于集体福利和职工分配。

**Article 8** All taxes reduced or exempted shall be used as the "State Support Fund" and shall be earmarked by the enterprise for the development of new technologies and production, and shall not be used for collective welfare or distribution to employees.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　银行对试验区内的新技术企业予以贷款支持，并每年从收回的技术改造贷款中，划出一定数额用于新技术开发。对外向型的新技术企业，优先提供外汇贷款。  
自本条例实施起三年内，银行每年提供一定数额的专项贷款，用于试验区内新技术企业的发展和建设(包括基本建设)，专款专用，由银行周转使用。银行每年给试验区安排发行长期债券的一定额度，用于向社会筹集资金，支持新技术开发。  
新技术企业所用贷款，经税务部门批准，可以税前还贷。使用贷款进行基本建设的，不受存足半年才能使用等规定的限制。  
试验区内的银行可从利息收入中提取一定比例，建立贷款风险基金。试验区内可设立中外合资的风险投资公司。

**Article 9** Banks shall provide loan support to new technology enterprises within the Experiment Zone, and shall allocate each year a certain amount from the recovered technical transformation loans for new technology development. Preferential foreign exchange loans shall be provided to export-oriented new technology enterprises.  
Within three years from the date of implementation of these Regulations, banks shall provide each year a certain amount of special loans for the development and construction (including capital construction) of new technology enterprises in the Experiment Zone. Such loans shall be used for designated purposes and shall be rotated and used by the banks. Banks shall each year arrange a certain amount for the Experiment Zone to issue long-term bonds to raise funds from society to support new technology development.  
Loans used by new technology enterprises may be repaid with pre-tax profits upon approval by the tax authorities. The use of loans for capital construction shall not be subject to the restriction that the funds must be deposited for six months before use.  
Banks within the Experiment Zone may set aside a certain proportion from their interest income to establish a loan risk fund. Chinese-foreign joint venture venture capital companies may be established within the Experiment Zone.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　试验区设立新技术产品进出口公司。有条件的新技术企业，由北京市人民政府授予外贸经营权，自负盈亏，承担出口计划；经国家有关部门批准，可以在国外设立分支机构。新技术企业出口所创外汇，三年内全额留给企业；从第四年起，地方和创汇企业二八分成。

**Article 10** An import and export company for new technology products shall be established in the Experiment Zone. Qualified new technology enterprises shall be granted the right to operate foreign trade by the People's Government of Beijing Municipality, shall be responsible for their own profits and losses, and shall undertake export plans; with the approval of the relevant state departments, they may establish branches abroad. The foreign exchange earned from exports by new technology enterprises shall be entirely retained by the enterprise within three years; from the fourth year onward, the local government and the foreign exchange-earning enterprise shall share it in the ratio of twenty to eighty.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　试验区内，对外经济技术交流和产品出口业务较多的新技术企业，其商务、技术人员一年内多次出国的，第一次由北京市人民政府审批，以后由企业自行审批。

**Article 11** For new technology enterprises within the Experiment Zone that have extensive foreign economic and technological exchange and product export business, if their business and technical personnel need to go abroad multiple times within one year, the first trip shall be approved by the People's Government of Beijing Municipality, and subsequent trips shall be approved by the enterprise itself.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　试验区内的新技术企业，用于新技术和新技术产品开发的仪器、设备，可以实行快速折旧。

**Article 12** New technology enterprises within the Experiment Zone may adopt accelerated depreciation for instruments and equipment used for the development of new technologies and new technology products.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　试验区内新技术企业开发的新产品，可自行制定试销价格。经营国家没有统一定价的新技术产品，可以自行定价。

**Article 13** New products developed by new technology enterprises within the Experiment Zone may set trial sale prices on their own. They may set prices on their own for new technology products that are not subject to uniform state pricing.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　鼓励科研单位、学校和企业中的科技人员在试验区内的新技术企业中兼职，兴办、领办、承包各种形式的新技术企业，或离职到新技术企业任职。有关部门要积极支持并提供方便，保障他们的合法权益。  
允许新技术企业招聘大专毕业生、大学毕业生、研究生、留学生和国外专家。

**Article 14** Scientific and technical personnel from research institutions, schools, and enterprises shall be encouraged to take part-time jobs in new technology enterprises within the Experiment Zone, to establish, lead, or contract various forms of new technology enterprises, or to leave their posts to take up positions in new technology enterprises. Relevant departments shall actively support and provide convenience to safeguard their legitimate rights and interests.  
New technology enterprises shall be permitted to recruit graduates from junior colleges, universities, postgraduates, returned students, and foreign experts.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　试验区内的新技术企业，免缴奖金税。企业从业人员的收入达到个人收入调节税纳税标准的，照章纳税。

**Article 15** New technology enterprises within the Experiment Zone shall be exempted from bonus tax. If the income of enterprise employees reaches the tax threshold for the personal income adjustment tax, tax shall be paid in accordance with the regulations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　试验区内新技术企业所缴各项税款，以1987年税款为基数，新增部分五年内全部返还给海淀区，用于试验区的开发建设，由市财政、税务部门监督使用。

**Article 16** With the tax paid in 1987 as the base figure, the increased portion of various taxes paid by new technology enterprises within the Experiment Zone shall be wholly returned to Haidian District within five years for the development and construction of the Experiment Zone, and shall be used under the supervision of the municipal finance and tax departments.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　北京市人民政府可以根据本条例制定实施办法和单行规定。

**Article 17** The People's Government of Beijing Municipality may formulate implementing measures and separate regulations in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 18** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
