# 最高人民法院关于审理食品安全民事纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕14号  
**公布日期**：2020-10-19  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理食品安全民事纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释（一）》已于2020年10月19日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1813次会议通过，现予公布，自2021年1月1日起施行。 2020年12月8日 最高人民法院 关于审理食品安全民事纠纷案件 适用法律若干问题的解释（一）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　消费者因不符合食品安全标准的食品受到损害，依据[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)第一款规定诉请食品生产者或者经营者赔偿损失，被诉的生产者或者经营者以赔偿责任应由生产经营者中的另一方承担为由主张免责的，人民法院不予支持。属于生产者责任的，经营者赔偿后有权向生产者追偿；属于经营者责任的，生产者赔偿后有权向经营者追偿。

**Article 1** If a consumer suffers damage due to food that does not meet the food safety standards, and requests compensation for losses from the food producer or operator pursuant to the first paragraph of Article 148 of the Food Safety Law, and the sued producer or operator claims exemption on the ground that the compensation liability shall be borne by the other party in the production or operation, the People's Court shall not support such claim. If the liability pertains to the producer, the operator, after making compensation, shall have the right to seek recourse from the producer; if the liability pertains to the operator, the producer, after making compensation, shall have the right to seek recourse from the operator.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　电子商务平台经营者以标记自营业务方式所销售的食品或者虽未标记自营但实际开展自营业务所销售的食品不符合食品安全标准，消费者依据[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)规定主张电子商务平台经营者承担作为食品经营者的赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
电子商务平台经营者虽非实际开展自营业务，但其所作标识等足以误导消费者让消费者相信系电子商务平台经营者自营，消费者依据[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)规定主张电子商务平台经营者承担作为食品经营者的赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 2** If an e-commerce platform operator sells food that does not meet the food safety standards by marking it as self-operated business, or sells food not marked as self-operated but actually carrying out self-operated business, and a consumer claims that the e-commerce platform operator shall bear compensation liability as a food operator pursuant to Article 148 of the Food Safety Law, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
If an e-commerce platform operator is not actually carrying out self-operated business, but its markings or other acts are sufficient to mislead consumers into believing that the food is self-operated by the e-commerce platform operator, and a consumer claims that the e-commerce platform operator shall bear compensation liability as a food operator pursuant to Article 148 of the Food Safety Law, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　电子商务平台经营者违反[食品安全法第六十二条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-62)和第一百三十一条规定，未对平台内食品经营者进行实名登记、审查许可证，或者未履行报告、停止提供网络交易平台服务等义务，使消费者的合法权益受到损害，消费者主张电子商务平台经营者与平台内食品经营者承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 3** If an e-commerce platform operator violates the provisions of Article 62 and Article 131 of the Food Safety Law by failing to conduct real-name registration or review licenses for food operators on the platform, or failing to perform obligations such as reporting or ceasing to provide online trading platform services, thereby damaging the legitimate rights and interests of consumers, and a consumer claims that the e-commerce platform operator shall bear joint and several liability together with the food operator on the platform, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　公共交通运输的承运人向旅客提供的食品不符合食品安全标准，旅客主张承运人依据[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)规定承担作为食品生产者或者经营者的赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持；承运人以其不是食品的生产经营者或者食品是免费提供为由进行免责抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 4** If a carrier of public transport provides food to passengers that does not meet the food safety standards, and a passenger claims that the carrier shall bear compensation liability as a food producer or operator pursuant to Article 148 of the Food Safety Law, the People's Court shall support such claim; if the carrier makes a defense for exemption on the ground that it is not a food producer or operator or that the food is provided free of charge, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　有关单位或者个人明知食品生产经营者从事[食品安全法第一百二十三条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-123)第一款规定的违法行为而仍为其提供设备、技术、原料、销售渠道、运输、储存或者其他便利条件，消费者主张该单位或者个人依据[食品安全法第一百二十三条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-123)第二款的规定与食品生产经营者承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 5** If a relevant entity or individual, knowing that a food producer or operator engages in illegal acts as specified in the first paragraph of Article 123 of the Food Safety Law, still provides equipment, technology, raw materials, sales channels, transportation, storage, or other convenience conditions, and a consumer claims that such entity or individual shall bear joint and several liability together with the food producer or operator pursuant to the second paragraph of Article 123 of the Food Safety Law, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　食品经营者具有下列情形之一，消费者主张构成[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)规定的“明知”的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）已过食品标明的保质期但仍然销售的；  
（二）未能提供所售食品的合法进货来源的；  
（三）以明显不合理的低价进货且无合理原因的；  
（四）未依法履行进货查验义务的；  
（五）虚假标注、更改食品生产日期、批号的；  
（六）转移、隐匿、非法销毁食品进销货记录或者故意提供虚假信息的；  
（七）其他能够认定为明知的情形。

**Article 6** If a food operator falls under any of the following circumstances, and a consumer claims that it constitutes "knowingly" as provided in Article 148 of the Food Safety Law, the People's Court shall support such claim:  
(1) selling food that has passed the marked shelf life;  
(2) failing to provide the legal source of the sold food;  
(3) purchasing at an obviously unreasonable low price without reasonable cause;  
(4) failing to perform the obligation of purchase inspection according to law;  
(5) falsely labeling or changing the production date or batch number of food;  
(6) transferring, concealing, or illegally destroying purchase and sale records of food, or intentionally providing false information;  
(7) other circumstances that may be deemed as knowing.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　消费者认为生产经营者生产经营不符合食品安全标准的食品同时构成欺诈的，有权选择依据[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)第二款或者[消费者权益保护法第五十五条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法.md#art-55)第一款规定主张食品生产者或者经营者承担惩罚性赔偿责任。

**Article 7** If a consumer considers that the food produced or operated by a producer or operator does not meet the food safety standards and also constitutes fraud, the consumer shall have the right to choose to claim punitive damages from the food producer or operator pursuant to the second paragraph of Article 148 of the Food Safety Law or the first paragraph of Article 55 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　经营者经营明知是不符合食品安全标准的食品，但向消费者承诺的赔偿标准高于[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)规定的赔偿标准，消费者主张经营者按照承诺赔偿的，人民法院应当依法予以支持。

**Article 8** If an operator knowingly operates food that does not meet the food safety standards, but the compensation standard promised to consumers is higher than the compensation standard provided in Article 148 of the Food Safety Law, and a consumer claims that the operator shall make compensation according to the promise, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　食品符合食品安全标准但未达到生产经营者承诺的质量标准，消费者依照民法典、消费者权益保护法等法律规定主张生产经营者承担责任的，人民法院应予支持，但消费者主张生产经营者依据[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)规定承担赔偿责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 9** If food meets the food safety standards but does not meet the quality standards promised by the producer or operator, and a consumer claims that the producer or operator shall bear liability pursuant to the Civil Code, the Law on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests, or other laws, the People's Court shall support such claim; however, if the consumer claims that the producer or operator shall bear compensation liability pursuant to Article 148 of the Food Safety Law, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　食品不符合食品安全标准，消费者主张生产者或者经营者依据[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)第二款规定承担惩罚性赔偿责任，生产者或者经营者以未造成消费者人身损害为由抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 10** If food does not meet the food safety standards, and a consumer claims that the producer or operator shall bear punitive damages liability pursuant to the second paragraph of Article 148 of the Food Safety Law, and the producer or operator makes a defense on the ground that no personal injury has been caused to the consumer, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　生产经营未标明生产者名称、地址、成分或者配料表，或者未清晰标明生产日期、保质期的预包装食品，消费者主张生产者或者经营者依据[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)第二款规定承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持，但法律、行政法规、食品安全国家标准对标签标注事项另有规定的除外。

**Article 11** If a producer or operator produces or operates prepackaged food without indicating the name, address, ingredients or ingredient list of the producer, or without clearly indicating the production date and shelf life, and a consumer claims that the producer or operator shall bear punitive damages liability pursuant to the second paragraph of Article 148 of the Food Safety Law, the People's Court shall support such claim, unless otherwise provided by laws, administrative regulations, or national food safety standards regarding labeling matters.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　进口的食品不符合我国食品安全国家标准或者国务院卫生行政部门决定暂予适用的标准，消费者主张销售者、进口商等经营者依据[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)规定承担赔偿责任，销售者、进口商等经营者仅以进口的食品符合出口地食品安全标准或者已经过我国出入境检验检疫机构检验检疫为由进行免责抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 12** If imported food does not meet the national food safety standards of China or the standards decided to be temporarily applicable by the health administrative department of the State Council, and a consumer claims that a seller, importer, or other operator shall bear compensation liability pursuant to Article 148 of the Food Safety Law, and the seller, importer, or other operator makes a defense for exemption solely on the ground that the imported food meets the food safety standards of the exporting country or has passed inspection and quarantine by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine authorities of China, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　生产经营不符合食品安全标准的食品，侵害众多消费者合法权益，损害社会公共利益，民事诉讼法、消费者权益保护法等法律规定的机关和有关组织依法提起公益诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 13** If the production or operation of food that does not meet the food safety standards infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of numerous consumers and damages the social public interest, and the authorities and relevant organizations provided for by the Civil Procedure Law, the Law on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests, and other laws initiate a public interest lawsuit according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本解释自2021年1月1日起施行。  
本解释施行后人民法院正在审理的一审、二审案件适用本解释。  
本解释施行前已经终审，本解释施行后当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本解释。  
最高人民法院以前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 14** This Interpretation shall come into force on January 1, 2021.  
Cases of first instance and second instance being tried by the People's Court after the coming into force of this Interpretation shall be governed by this Interpretation.  
Cases that have already become final before the coming into force of this Interpretation and for which parties apply for retrial or which are decided to be retried according to the trial supervision procedure after the coming into force of this Interpretation shall not be governed by this Interpretation.  
If any previous judicial interpretations issued by the Supreme People's Court are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
