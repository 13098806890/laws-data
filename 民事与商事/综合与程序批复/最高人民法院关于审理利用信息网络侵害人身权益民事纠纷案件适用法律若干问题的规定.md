# 最高人民法院关于审理利用信息网络侵害人身权益民事纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2014〕11号  
**公布日期**：2014-08-21  
**生效日期**：2014-10-10

> 最高人民法院《关于审理利用信息网络侵害人身权益民事纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的规定》已于2014年6月23日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1621次会议通过，现予公布，自2014年10月10日起施行。 2014年8月21日 最高人民法院 关于审理利用信息网络侵害人身权益民事纠纷 案件适用法律若干问题的规定 法释〔2014〕11号 为正确审理利用信息网络侵害人身权益民事纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》、《中华人民共和国侵权责任法》、《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于加强网络信息保护的决定》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律的规定，结合审判实践，制定本规定。 第一条　本规定所称的利用信息网络侵害人身权益民事纠纷案件，是指利用信息网络侵害他人姓名权、名称权、名誉权、荣誉权、肖像权、隐私权等人身权益引起的纠纷案件。 第二条　利用信息网络侵害人身权益提起的诉讼，由侵权行为地或者被告住所地人民法院管辖。 侵权行为实施地包括实施被诉侵权行为的计算机等终端设备所在地，侵权结果发生地包括被侵权人住所地。 第三条　原告依据侵权责任法第三十六条第二款、第三款的规定起诉网络用户或者网络服务提供者的，人民法院应予受理。 原告仅起诉网络用户，网络用户请求追加涉嫌侵权的网络服务提供者为共同被告或者第三人的，人民法院应予准许。 原告仅起诉网络服务提供者，网络服务提供者请求追加可以确定的网络用户为共同被告或者第三人的，人民法院应予准许。 第四条　原告起诉网络服务提供者，网络服务提供者以涉嫌侵权的信息系网络用户发布为由抗辩的，人民法院可以根据原告的请求及案件的具体情况，责令网络服务提供者向人民法院提供能够确定涉嫌侵权的网络用户的姓名（名称）、联系方式、网络地址等信息。 网络服务提供者无正当理由拒不提供的，人民法院可以依据民事诉讼法第一百一十四条的规定对网络服务提供者采取处罚等措施。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所称的利用信息网络侵害人身权益民事纠纷案件，是指利用信息网络侵害他人姓名权、名称权、名誉权、荣誉权、肖像权、隐私权等人身权益引起的纠纷案件。

**Article 1** For the purposes of these Provisions, civil dispute cases involving infringement upon personal rights and interests through the use of information networks refer to dispute cases arising from the infringement upon a natural or legal person's rights to name, title, reputation, honor, portrait, privacy, and other personal rights and interests through the use of information networks.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　利用信息网络侵害人身权益提起的诉讼，由侵权行为地或者被告住所地人民法院管辖。  
侵权行为实施地包括实施被诉侵权行为的计算机等终端设备所在地，侵权结果发生地包括被侵权人住所地。

**Article 2** Actions instituted for infringement upon personal rights and interests through the use of information networks shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the infringing act was committed or where the defendant has his domicile.  
The place where the infringing act was committed includes the place where the computer or other terminal equipment used to commit the alleged infringing act is located, and the place where the consequences of the infringement occurred includes the domicile of the infringed party.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　原告依据侵权责任法第三十六条第二款、第三款的规定起诉网络用户或者网络服务提供者的，人民法院应予受理。  
原告仅起诉网络用户，网络用户请求追加涉嫌侵权的网络服务提供者为共同被告或者第三人的，人民法院应予准许。  
原告仅起诉网络服务提供者，网络服务提供者请求追加可以确定的网络用户为共同被告或者第三人的，人民法院应予准许。

**Article 3** Where a plaintiff prosecutes a network user or a network service provider pursuant to Article 36, paragraphs 2 and 3 of the Tort Liability Law, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
Where the plaintiff prosecutes only the network user, and the network user requests to join the network service provider allegedly involved in the infringement as a co-defendant or a third party, the People's Court shall grant the request.  
Where the plaintiff prosecutes only the network service provider, and the network service provider requests to join the identifiable network user as a co-defendant or a third party, the People's Court shall grant the request.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　原告起诉网络服务提供者，网络服务提供者以涉嫌侵权的信息系网络用户发布为由抗辩的，人民法院可以根据原告的请求及案件的具体情况，责令网络服务提供者向人民法院提供能够确定涉嫌侵权的网络用户的姓名（名称）、联系方式、网络地址等信息。  
网络服务提供者无正当理由拒不提供的，人民法院可以依据[民事诉讼法第一百一十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-114)的规定对网络服务提供者采取处罚等措施。  
原告根据网络服务提供者提供的信息请求追加网络用户为被告的，人民法院应予准许。

**Article 4** Where a plaintiff prosecutes a network service provider, and the network service provider defends on the ground that the allegedly infringing information was published by a network user, the People's Court may, based on the plaintiff's request and the specific circumstances of the case, order the network service provider to provide to the People's Court the name, contact information, network address, and other information sufficient to identify the allegedly infringing network user.  
If the network service provider, without legitimate reasons, refuses to provide such information, the People's Court may, pursuant to Article 114 of the Civil Procedure Law, impose penalties or take other measures against the network service provider.  
Where the plaintiff requests to join the network user as a defendant based on the information provided by the network service provider, the People's Court shall grant the request.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　依据侵权责任法第三十六条第二款的规定，被侵权人以书面形式或者网络服务提供者公示的方式向网络服务提供者发出的通知，包含下列内容的，人民法院应当认定有效：  
（一）通知人的姓名（名称）和联系方式；  
（二）要求采取必要措施的网络地址或者足以准确定位侵权内容的相关信息；  
（三）通知人要求删除相关信息的理由。  
被侵权人发送的通知未满足上述条件，网络服务提供者主张免除责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 5** A notice sent by the infringed party to the network service provider in writing or in the manner published by the network service provider, which contains the following contents, shall be deemed effective by the People's Court:  
(1) the name and contact information of the notifying party;  
(2) the network address or sufficient relevant information to accurately locate the infringing content for which necessary measures are requested;  
(3) the reasons for the notifying party's request to delete the relevant information.  
If the notice sent by the infringed party does not satisfy the above conditions, and the network service provider claims exemption from liability, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院适用侵权责任法第三十六条第二款的规定，认定网络服务提供者采取的删除、屏蔽、断开链接等必要措施是否及时，应当根据网络服务的性质、有效通知的形式和准确程度、网络信息侵害权益的类型和程度等因素综合判断。

**Article 6** When applying Article 36, paragraph 2 of the Tort Liability Law, the People's Court shall comprehensively determine whether the necessary measures such as deletion, blocking, or disconnection of links taken by the network service provider were timely, based on factors including the nature of the network service, the form and accuracy of the effective notice, and the type and degree of the infringement of rights and interests by the network information.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　其发布的信息被采取删除、屏蔽、断开链接等措施的网络用户，主张网络服务提供者承担违约责任或者侵权责任，网络服务提供者以收到通知为由抗辩的，人民法院应予支持。  
被采取删除、屏蔽、断开链接等措施的网络用户，请求网络服务提供者提供通知内容的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 7** Where a network user whose published information has been subjected to measures such as deletion, blocking, or disconnection of links claims that the network service provider shall bear liability for breach of contract or tort liability, and the network service provider defends on the ground of having received a notice, the People's Court shall support the network service provider's defense.  
Where a network user whose information has been subjected to measures such as deletion, blocking, or disconnection of links requests the network service provider to provide the content of the notice, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　因通知人的通知导致网络服务提供者错误采取删除、屏蔽、断开链接等措施，被采取措施的网络用户请求通知人承担侵权责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
被错误采取措施的网络用户请求网络服务提供者采取相应恢复措施的，人民法院应予支持，但受技术条件限制无法恢复的除外。

**Article 8** Where a network service provider erroneously takes measures such as deletion, blocking, or disconnection of links as a result of the notifying party's notice, and the network user subjected to such measures requests the notifying party to bear tort liability, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where a network user who has been erroneously subjected to measures requests the network service provider to take corresponding restoration measures, the People's Court shall support such request, unless restoration is impossible due to technical limitations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院依据侵权责任法第三十六条第三款认定网络服务提供者是否“知道”，应当综合考虑下列因素：  
（一）网络服务提供者是否以人工或者自动方式对侵权网络信息以推荐、排名、选择、编辑、整理、修改等方式作出处理；  
（二）网络服务提供者应当具备的管理信息的能力，以及所提供服务的性质、方式及其引发侵权的可能性大小；  
（三）该网络信息侵害人身权益的类型及明显程度；  
（四）该网络信息的社会影响程度或者一定时间内的浏览量；  
（五）网络服务提供者采取预防侵权措施的技术可能性及其是否采取了相应的合理措施；  
（六）网络服务提供者是否针对同一网络用户的重复侵权行为或者同一侵权信息采取了相应的合理措施；  
（七）与本案相关的其他因素。

**Article 9** When determining whether a network service provider "knew" pursuant to Article 36, paragraph 3 of the Tort Liability Law, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider the following factors:  
(1) whether the network service provider processed the infringing network information manually or automatically by means such as recommendation, ranking, selection, editing, collation, or modification;  
(2) the capacity of the network service provider to manage information, as well as the nature and manner of the services it provides and the likelihood of infringement arising therefrom;  
(3) the type and obviousness of the infringement upon personal rights and interests by the network information;  
(4) the degree of social impact of the network information or its browsing volume within a certain period;  
(5) the technical feasibility for the network service provider to take preventive measures against infringement and whether it has taken corresponding reasonable measures;  
(6) whether the network service provider has taken corresponding reasonable measures against repeated infringement by the same network user or against the same infringing information;  
(7) other factors relevant to the case.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院认定网络用户或者网络服务提供者转载网络信息行为的过错及其程度，应当综合以下因素：  
（一）转载主体所承担的与其性质、影响范围相适应的注意义务；  
（二）所转载信息侵害他人人身权益的明显程度；  
（三）对所转载信息是否作出实质性修改，是否添加或者修改文章标题，导致其与内容严重不符以及误导公众的可能性。

**Article 10** When determining the fault and its degree of a network user or network service provider in republishing network information, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider the following factors:  
(1) the duty of care borne by the republishing party commensurate with its nature and scope of influence;  
(2) the obviousness of the infringement upon another's personal rights and interests by the republished information;  
(3) whether substantial modifications were made to the republished information, whether the article title was added or altered, resulting in a serious mismatch with the content and the likelihood of misleading the public.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　网络用户或者网络服务提供者采取诽谤、诋毁等手段，损害公众对经营主体的信赖，降低其产品或者服务的社会评价，经营主体请求网络用户或者网络服务提供者承担侵权责任的，人民法院应依法予以支持。

**Article 11** Where a network user or network service provider, by means such as defamation or slander, damages the public's trust in a business entity or reduces the social evaluation of its products or services, and the business entity requests the network user or network service provider to bear tort liability, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　网络用户或者网络服务提供者利用网络公开自然人基因信息、病历资料、健康检查资料、犯罪记录、家庭住址、私人活动等个人隐私和其他个人信息，造成他人损害，被侵权人请求其承担侵权责任的，人民法院应予支持。但下列情形除外：  
（一）经自然人书面同意且在约定范围内公开；  
（二）为促进社会公共利益且在必要范围内；  
（三）学校、科研机构等基于公共利益为学术研究或者统计的目的，经自然人书面同意，且公开的方式不足以识别特定自然人；  
（四）自然人自行在网络上公开的信息或者其他已合法公开的个人信息；  
（五）以合法渠道获取的个人信息；  
（六）法律或者行政法规另有规定。  
网络用户或者网络服务提供者以违反社会公共利益、社会公德的方式公开前款第四项、第五项规定的个人信息，或者公开该信息侵害权利人值得保护的重大利益，权利人请求网络用户或者网络服务提供者承担侵权责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
国家机关行使职权公开个人信息的，不适用本条规定。

**Article 12** Where a network user or network service provider uses a network to disclose the genetic information, medical records, health examination data, criminal records, home address, private activities, and other personal privacy and personal information of a natural person, causing harm to others, and the infringed party requests the network user or network service provider to bear tort liability, the People's Court shall support such request, except in the following circumstances:  
(1) the disclosure is with the written consent of the natural person and within the agreed scope;  
(2) the disclosure is for the promotion of public interest and within the necessary scope;  
(3) schools, scientific research institutions, or the like, for the purpose of academic research or statistics based on public interest, with the written consent of the natural person, and the manner of disclosure is insufficient to identify a specific natural person;  
(4) information voluntarily disclosed by the natural person on the network or other personal information that has been lawfully made public;  
(5) personal information obtained through lawful channels;  
(6) otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.  
Where a network user or network service provider discloses personal information specified in items (4) and (5) of the preceding paragraph in a manner that violates social public interest or social morality, or the disclosure infringes upon a significant interest of the right holder worthy of protection, and the right holder requests the network user or network service provider to bear tort liability, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where a state organ discloses personal information in the exercise of its functions, this Article shall not apply.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　网络用户或者网络服务提供者，根据国家机关依职权制作的文书和公开实施的职权行为等信息来源所发布的信息，有下列情形之一，侵害他人人身权益，被侵权人请求侵权人承担侵权责任的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）网络用户或者网络服务提供者发布的信息与前述信息来源内容不符；  
（二）网络用户或者网络服务提供者以添加侮辱性内容、诽谤性信息、不当标题或者通过增删信息、调整结构、改变顺序等方式致人误解；  
（三）前述信息来源已被公开更正，但网络用户拒绝更正或者网络服务提供者不予更正；  
（四）前述信息来源已被公开更正，网络用户或者网络服务提供者仍然发布更正之前的信息。

**Article 13** Where a network user or network service provider publishes information based on sources such as documents prepared by state organs in the exercise of their functions and official acts publicly performed, and any of the following circumstances exists, causing infringement upon another's personal rights and interests, and the infringed party requests the infringer to bear tort liability, the People's Court shall support such request:  
(1) the information published by the network user or network service provider is inconsistent with the content of the aforementioned sources;  
(2) the network user or network service provider causes misunderstanding by adding insulting content, defamatory information, improper titles, or by adding or deleting information, adjusting the structure, or altering the order;  
(3) the aforementioned sources have been publicly corrected, but the network user refuses to correct or the network service provider fails to correct;  
(4) the aforementioned sources have been publicly corrected, but the network user or network service provider still publishes the information before the correction.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　被侵权人与构成侵权的网络用户或者网络服务提供者达成一方支付报酬，另一方提供删除、屏蔽、断开链接等服务的协议，人民法院应认定为无效。  
擅自篡改、删除、屏蔽特定网络信息或者以断开链接的方式阻止他人获取网络信息，发布该信息的网络用户或者网络服务提供者请求侵权人承担侵权责任的，人民法院应予支持。接受他人委托实施该行为的，委托人与受托人承担连带责任。

**Article 14** Where an infringed party enters into an agreement with a network user or network service provider who has committed infringement, whereby one party pays remuneration and the other party provides services such as deletion, blocking, or disconnection of links, the People's Court shall deem such agreement void.  
Where a network user or network service provider that published the information requests the infringer to bear tort liability for unauthorized alteration, deletion, blocking of specific network information, or preventing others from accessing network information by disconnecting links, the People's Court shall support such request. If a person engages in such conduct upon the commission of another, the principal and the agent shall bear joint and several liability.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　雇佣、组织、教唆或者帮助他人发布、转发网络信息侵害他人人身权益，被侵权人请求行为人承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 15** Where a person employs, organizes, instigates, or assists another in publishing or republishing network information that infringes upon another's personal rights and interests, and the infringed party requests the actor to bear joint and several liability, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院判决侵权人承担赔礼道歉、消除影响或者恢复名誉等责任形式的，应当与侵权的具体方式和所造成的影响范围相当。侵权人拒不履行的，人民法院可以采取在网络上发布公告或者公布裁判文书等合理的方式执行，由此产生的费用由侵权人承担。

**Article 16** Where the People's Court renders a judgment ordering the infringer to assume forms of liability such as making an apology, eliminating the effects, or restoring reputation, such liability shall be commensurate with the specific manner of the infringement and the scope of the effects caused. If the infringer refuses to perform, the People's Court may enforce the judgment by reasonable means such as publishing an announcement on the network or publishing the adjudicative document, and the costs incurred therefrom shall be borne by the infringer.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　网络用户或者网络服务提供者侵害他人人身权益，造成财产损失或者严重精神损害，被侵权人依据侵权责任法第二十条和第二十二条的规定请求其承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 17** Where a network user or network service provider infringes upon another's personal rights and interests, causing property loss or serious mental harm, and the infringed party requests the infringer to bear compensation liability pursuant to Articles 20 and 22 of the Tort Liability Law, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　被侵权人为制止侵权行为所支付的合理开支，可以认定为侵权责任法第二十条规定的财产损失。合理开支包括被侵权人或者委托代理人对侵权行为进行调查、取证的合理费用。人民法院根据当事人的请求和具体案情，可以将符合国家有关部门规定的律师费用计算在赔偿范围内。  
被侵权人因人身权益受侵害造成的财产损失或者侵权人因此获得的利益无法确定的，人民法院可以根据具体案情在50万元以下的范围内确定赔偿数额。  
精神损害的赔偿数额，依据最高人民法院《关于确定民事侵权精神损害赔偿责任若干问题的解释》第十条的规定予以确定。

**Article 18** The reasonable expenses paid by the infringed party to stop the infringing act may be determined as the property loss under Article 20 of the Tort Liability Law. Reasonable expenses include the reasonable costs incurred by the infringed party or the authorized agent in investigating and collecting evidence of the infringing act. Upon the party's request and based on the specific circumstances of the case, the People's Court may include attorneys' fees that comply with the provisions of the relevant state authorities within the scope of compensation.  
If the property loss suffered by the infringed party due to the infringement of personal rights and interests, or the benefits obtained by the infringer therefrom, cannot be determined, the People's Court may determine the amount of compensation within the range of not more than RMB 500,000 yuan based on the specific circumstances of the case.  
The amount of compensation for mental harm shall be determined pursuant to Article 10 of the Supreme People's Court's Interpretation on Several Issues Concerning the Determination of Compensation Liability for Mental Harm in Civil Torts.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本规定施行后人民法院正在审理的一审、二审案件适用本规定。  
本规定施行前已经终审，本规定施行后当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本规定。

**Article 19** These Provisions shall apply to cases of first instance and second instance that are being tried by the People's Court after these Provisions come into force.  
These Provisions shall not apply to cases that have already been finally adjudicated before these Provisions come into force and for which a party applies for a retrial after these Provisions come into force, or for which a retrial is decided according to the trial supervision procedure.
