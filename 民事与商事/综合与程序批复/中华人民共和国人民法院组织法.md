### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范人民法院的设置、组织和职权，保障人民法院依法履行职责，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of regulating the establishment, organization, and functions and powers of the People's Courts, and ensuring that the People's Courts perform their duties according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院是国家的审判机关。  
人民法院通过审判刑事案件、民事案件、行政案件以及法律规定的其他案件，惩罚犯罪，保障无罪的人不受刑事追究，解决民事、行政纠纷，保护个人和组织的合法权益，监督行政机关依法行使职权，维护国家安全和社会秩序，维护社会公平正义，维护国家法制统一、尊严和权威，保障中国特色社会主义建设的顺利进行。

**Article 2** The People's Courts are the adjudicatory organs of the State.  
The People's Courts, through trying criminal, civil, administrative, and other cases as prescribed by law, shall punish crimes, protect innocent persons from criminal prosecution, resolve civil and administrative disputes, protect the lawful rights and interests of individuals and organizations, supervise the exercise of functions and powers by administrative organs according to law, safeguard national security and social order, uphold social fairness and justice, maintain the unity, dignity, and authority of the national legal system, and ensure the smooth progress of the construction of socialism with Chinese characteristics.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院依照宪法、法律和全国人民代表大会常务委员会的决定设置。

**Article 3** The People's Courts shall be established in accordance with the Constitution, laws, and decisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院依照法律规定独立行使审判权，不受行政机关、社会团体和个人的干涉。

**Article 4** The People's Courts shall, in accordance with the provisions of law, independently exercise their adjudicative power, free from interference by administrative organs, social organizations, or individuals.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院审判案件在适用法律上一律平等，不允许任何组织和个人有超越法律的特权，禁止任何形式的歧视。

**Article 5** In trying cases, the People's Courts shall apply the law equally to all, allowing no organization or individual to have privileges beyond the law, and prohibiting any form of discrimination.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院坚持司法公正，以事实为根据，以法律为准绳，遵守法定程序，依法保护个人和组织的诉讼权利和其他合法权益，尊重和保障人权。

**Article 6** The People's Courts shall uphold judicial fairness, take facts as the basis, the law as the criterion, observe statutory procedures, protect the litigation rights and other lawful rights and interests of individuals and organizations according to law, and respect and safeguard human rights.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民法院实行司法公开，法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 7** The People's Courts shall exercise judicial transparency, except as otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院实行司法责任制，建立健全权责统一的司法权力运行机制。

**Article 8** The People's Courts shall implement a judicial accountability system, establishing and improving a judicial power operation mechanism that unifies powers and responsibilities.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　最高人民法院对全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会负责并报告工作。地方各级人民法院对本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会负责并报告工作。  
各级人民代表大会及其常务委员会对本级人民法院的工作实施监督。&thinsp;<sup>[1]</sup>

**Article 9** The Supreme People's Court shall be responsible to and report its work to the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee. Local People's Courts at all levels shall be responsible to and report their work to the People's Congresses and their Standing Committees at the corresponding levels.  
People's Congresses and their Standing Committees at all levels shall exercise supervision over the work of the People's Courts at the corresponding levels.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　最高人民法院是最高审判机关。  
最高人民法院监督地方各级人民法院和专门人民法院的审判工作，上级人民法院监督下级人民法院的审判工作。

**Article 10** The Supreme People's Court is the highest adjudicatory organ.  
The Supreme People's Court shall supervise the adjudicatory work of local People's Courts at all levels and of specialized People's Courts; People's Courts at higher levels shall supervise the adjudicatory work of People's Courts at lower levels.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院应当接受人民群众监督，保障人民群众对人民法院工作依法享有知情权、参与权和监督权。

**Article 11** The People's Courts shall accept supervision by the people and ensure that the people enjoy the rights to be informed, to participate, and to supervise the work of the People's Courts according to law.

### 第二章　人民法院的设置和职权

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院分为：  
（一）最高人民法院；  
（二）地方各级人民法院；  
（三）专门人民法院。

**Article 12** The People's Courts are composed of:  
(1) the Supreme People's Court;  
(2) local People's Courts at all levels; and  
(3) specialized People's Courts.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　地方各级人民法院分为高级人民法院、中级人民法院和基层人民法院。

**Article 13** Local People's Courts at all levels consist of Higher People's Courts, Intermediate People's Courts, and Primary People's Courts.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　在新疆生产建设兵团设立的人民法院的组织、案件管辖范围和法官任免，依照全国人民代表大会常务委员会的有关规定。

**Article 14** The organization, scope of case jurisdiction, and appointment and removal of judges of People's Courts established in the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　专门人民法院包括军事法院和海事法院、知识产权法院、金融法院等。  
专门人民法院的设置、组织、职权和法官任免，由全国人民代表大会常务委员会规定。

**Article 15** Specialized People's Courts include military courts, maritime courts, intellectual property courts, financial courts, and so forth.  
The establishment, organization, functions and powers, and appointment and removal of judges of specialized People's Courts shall be prescribed by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　最高人民法院审理下列案件：  
（一）法律规定由其管辖的和其认为应当由自己管辖的第一审案件；  
（二）对高级人民法院判决和裁定的上诉、抗诉案件；  
（三）按照全国人民代表大会常务委员会的规定提起的上诉、抗诉案件；  
（四）按照审判监督程序提起的再审案件；  
（五）高级人民法院报请核准的死刑案件。

**Article 16** The Supreme People's Court shall try the following cases:  
(1) first instance cases that are prescribed by law as falling under its jurisdiction and those that it considers shall be under its own jurisdiction;  
(2) appeals and protests against judgments and rulings of Higher People's Courts;  
(3) appeals and protests filed in accordance with provisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress;  
(4) retrial cases initiated in accordance with the adjudicative supervision procedure; and  
(5) death penalty cases submitted by Higher People's Courts for approval.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　死刑除依法由最高人民法院判决的以外，应当报请最高人民法院核准。

**Article 17** Except for death sentences rendered by the Supreme People's Court according to law, all death sentences shall be submitted to the Supreme People's Court for approval.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　最高人民法院可以对属于审判工作中具体应用法律的问题进行解释。  
最高人民法院可以发布指导性案例。

**Article 18** The Supreme People's Court may provide interpretations on issues concerning the specific application of law in adjudicatory work.  
The Supreme People's Court may publish guiding cases.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　最高人民法院可以设巡回法庭，审理最高人民法院依法确定的案件。  
巡回法庭是最高人民法院的组成部分。巡回法庭的判决和裁定即最高人民法院的判决和裁定。

**Article 19** The Supreme People's Court may set up circuit courts to try cases determined by the Supreme People's Court according to law.  
A circuit court is an integral part of the Supreme People's Court. Judgments and rulings of a circuit court are those of the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　高级人民法院包括：  
（一）省高级人民法院；  
（二）自治区高级人民法院；  
（三）直辖市高级人民法院。

**Article 20** Higher People's Courts shall include:  
(1) Higher People's Courts of provinces;  
(2) Higher People's Courts of autonomous regions; and  
(3) Higher People's Courts of municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　高级人民法院审理下列案件：  
（一）法律规定由其管辖的第一审案件；  
（二）下级人民法院报请审理的第一审案件；  
（三）最高人民法院指定管辖的第一审案件；  
（四）对中级人民法院判决和裁定的上诉、抗诉案件；  
（五）按照审判监督程序提起的再审案件；  
（六）中级人民法院报请复核的死刑案件。

**Article 21** Higher People's Courts shall try the following cases:  
(1) first instance cases that are prescribed by law as falling under their jurisdiction;  
(2) first instance cases submitted by People's Courts at lower levels for trial;  
(3) first instance cases designated by the Supreme People's Court as under their jurisdiction;  
(4) appeals and protests against judgments and rulings of Intermediate People's Courts;  
(5) retrial cases initiated in accordance with the adjudicative supervision procedure; and  
(6) death penalty cases submitted by Intermediate People's Courts for review.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　中级人民法院包括：  
（一）省、自治区辖市的中级人民法院；  
（二）在直辖市内设立的中级人民法院；  
（三）自治州中级人民法院；  
（四）在省、自治区内按地区设立的中级人民法院。

**Article 22** Intermediate People's Courts shall include:  
(1) Intermediate People's Courts in cities under the jurisdiction of provinces or autonomous regions;  
(2) Intermediate People's Courts established in municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(3) Intermediate People's Courts of autonomous prefectures; and  
(4) Intermediate People's Courts established by regions within provinces or autonomous regions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　中级人民法院审理下列案件：  
（一）法律规定由其管辖的第一审案件；  
（二）基层人民法院报请审理的第一审案件；  
（三）上级人民法院指定管辖的第一审案件；  
（四）对基层人民法院判决和裁定的上诉、抗诉案件；  
（五）按照审判监督程序提起的再审案件。

**Article 23** Intermediate People's Courts shall try the following cases:  
(1) first instance cases that are prescribed by law as falling under their jurisdiction;  
(2) first instance cases submitted by Primary People's Courts for trial;  
(3) first instance cases designated by People's Courts at higher levels as under their jurisdiction;  
(4) appeals and protests against judgments and rulings of Primary People's Courts; and  
(5) retrial cases initiated in accordance with the adjudicative supervision procedure.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　基层人民法院包括：  
（一）县、自治县人民法院；  
（二）不设区的市人民法院；  
（三）市辖区人民法院。

**Article 24** Primary People's Courts shall include:  
(1) People's Courts of counties and autonomous counties;  
(2) People's Courts of cities not divided into districts; and  
(3) People's Courts of municipal districts.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　基层人民法院审理第一审案件，法律另有规定的除外。  
基层人民法院对人民调解委员会的调解工作进行业务指导。

**Article 25** Primary People's Courts shall try first instance cases, except as otherwise provided by law.  
Primary People's Courts shall provide professional guidance to the mediation work of the People's Mediation Committees.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　基层人民法院根据地区、人口和案件情况，可以设立若干人民法庭。  
人民法庭是基层人民法院的组成部分。人民法庭的判决和裁定即基层人民法院的判决和裁定。

**Article 26** Primary People's Courts may, based on the area, population, and case situation, establish several people's tribunals.  
A people's tribunal is an integral part of a Primary People's Court. Judgments and rulings of a people's tribunal are those of the Primary People's Court.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　人民法院根据审判工作需要，可以设必要的专业审判庭。法官员额较少的中级人民法院和基层人民法院，可以设综合审判庭或者不设审判庭。  
人民法院根据审判工作需要，可以设综合业务机构。法官员额较少的中级人民法院和基层人民法院，可以不设综合业务机构。

**Article 27** People's Courts may, based on the needs of adjudicatory work, establish necessary specialized adjudication divisions. Intermediate People's Courts and Primary People's Courts with a smaller number of judge posts may establish comprehensive adjudication divisions or may not establish adjudication divisions.  
People's Courts may, based on the needs of adjudicatory work, establish comprehensive operational organs. Intermediate People's Courts and Primary People's Courts with a smaller number of judge posts may not establish comprehensive operational organs.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　人民法院根据工作需要，可以设必要的审判辅助机构和行政管理机构。

**Article 28** People's Courts may, based on work needs, establish necessary adjudicative support organs and administrative management organs.

### 第三章　人民法院的审判组织

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　人民法院审理案件，由合议庭或者法官一人独任审理。  
合议庭和法官独任审理的案件范围由法律规定。

**Article 29** A People's Court shall try cases by a collegial panel or a single judge alone.  
The scope of cases to be tried by a collegial panel or a single judge shall be prescribed by law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　合议庭由法官组成，或者由法官和人民陪审员组成，成员为三人以上单数。  
合议庭由一名法官担任审判长。院长或者庭长参加审理案件时，由自己担任审判长。  
审判长主持庭审、组织评议案件，评议案件时与合议庭其他成员权利平等。

**Article 30** A collegial panel shall consist of judges, or judges and people's assessors, and shall have an odd number of members of at least three.  
A collegial panel shall have a presiding judge. When the president or the division chief participates in the trial, he or she shall serve as the presiding judge.  
The presiding judge shall preside over the court trial and organize the deliberation of the case; during deliberation, he or she shall have equal rights with other members of the collegial panel.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　合议庭评议案件应当按照多数人的意见作出决定，少数人的意见应当记入笔录。评议案件笔录由合议庭全体组成人员签名。

**Article 31** When deliberating a case, a collegial panel shall make decisions based on the opinions of the majority, and the opinions of the minority shall be recorded in the minutes. The minutes of the deliberation of a case shall be signed by all members of the collegial panel.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　合议庭或者法官独任审理案件形成的裁判文书，经合议庭组成人员或者独任法官签署，由人民法院发布。

**Article 32** The adjudicative documents formed in the trial of a case by a collegial panel or a single judge shall, after being signed by the members of the collegial panel or the single judge, be issued by the People's Court.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　合议庭审理案件，法官对案件的事实认定和法律适用负责；法官独任审理案件，独任法官对案件的事实认定和法律适用负责。  
人民法院应当加强内部监督，审判活动有违法情形的，应当及时调查核实，并根据违法情形依法处理。

**Article 33** In cases tried by a collegial panel, the judges shall be responsible for the determination of facts and the application of law; in cases tried by a single judge, the single judge shall be responsible for the determination of facts and the application of law.  
People's Courts shall strengthen internal supervision; if there are illegal circumstances in adjudicative activities, they shall promptly investigate and verify, and handle them according to law based on the illegal circumstances.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　人民陪审员依照法律规定参加合议庭审理案件。

**Article 34** People's assessors shall participate in the trial of cases by a collegial panel in accordance with the provisions of law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　中级以上人民法院设赔偿委员会，依法审理国家赔偿案件。  
赔偿委员会由三名以上法官组成，成员应当为单数，按照多数人的意见作出决定。

**Article 35** People's Courts at or above the intermediate level shall establish a Compensation Committee to try state compensation cases according to law.  
A Compensation Committee shall consist of at least three judges, with an odd number of members, and shall make decisions based on the opinions of the majority.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　各级人民法院设审判委员会。审判委员会由院长、副院长和若干资深法官组成，成员应当为单数。  
审判委员会会议分为全体会议和专业委员会会议。  
中级以上人民法院根据审判工作需要，可以按照审判委员会委员专业和工作分工，召开刑事审判、民事行政审判等专业委员会会议。

**Article 36** People's Courts at all levels shall establish an Adjudicative Committee. An Adjudicative Committee shall consist of the president, vice presidents, and a number of senior judges, and shall have an odd number of members.  
Meetings of the Adjudicative Committee shall be divided into plenary meetings and specialized committee meetings.  
People's Courts at or above the intermediate level may, based on the needs of adjudicatory work and in accordance with the specialties and work divisions of the members of the Adjudicative Committee, convene specialized committee meetings for criminal adjudication, civil and administrative adjudication, among others.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　审判委员会履行下列职能：  
（一）总结审判工作经验；  
（二）讨论决定重大、疑难、复杂案件的法律适用；  
（三）讨论决定本院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定、调解书是否应当再审；  
（四）讨论决定其他有关审判工作的重大问题。  
最高人民法院对属于审判工作中具体应用法律的问题进行解释，应当由审判委员会全体会议讨论通过；发布指导性案例，可以由审判委员会专业委员会会议讨论通过。&thinsp;<sup>[1]</sup>

**Article 37** The Adjudicative Committee shall perform the following functions:  
(1) summarizing experience in adjudicatory work;  
(2) discussing and deciding on the application of law in major, difficult, and complex cases;  
(3) discussing and deciding whether judgments, rulings, and mediation documents that have become legally effective of the court shall be retried; and  
(4) discussing and deciding on other major issues concerning adjudicatory work.  
Where the Supreme People's Court provides interpretations on issues concerning the specific application of law in adjudicatory work, such interpretations shall be discussed and adopted at a plenary meeting of the Adjudicative Committee; publication of guiding cases may be discussed and adopted at a specialized committee meeting of the Adjudicative Committee.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　审判委员会召开全体会议和专业委员会会议，应当有其组成人员的过半数出席。  
审判委员会会议由院长或者院长委托的副院长主持。审判委员会实行民主集中制。  
审判委员会举行会议时，同级人民检察院检察长或者检察长委托的副检察长可以列席。

**Article 38** When the Adjudicative Committee holds a plenary meeting or a specialized committee meeting, it shall have more than half of its members present.  
The meeting of the Adjudicative Committee shall be presided over by the president or a vice president authorized by the president. The Adjudicative Committee shall apply the principle of democratic centralism.  
When the Adjudicative Committee holds a meeting, the chief procurator of the People's Procuratorate at the same level, or a deputy chief procurator authorized by the chief procurator, may attend as a non-voting participant.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　合议庭认为案件需要提交审判委员会讨论决定的，由审判长提出申请，院长批准。  
审判委员会讨论案件，合议庭对其汇报的事实负责，审判委员会委员对本人发表的意见和表决负责。审判委员会的决定，合议庭应当执行。  
审判委员会讨论案件的决定及其理由应当在裁判文书中公开，法律规定不公开的除外。

**Article 39** If a collegial panel considers that a case needs to be submitted to the Adjudicative Committee for discussion and decision, the presiding judge shall apply, and the president shall approve.  
When the Adjudicative Committee discusses a case, the collegial panel shall be responsible for the facts reported, and the members of the Adjudicative Committee shall be responsible for the opinions expressed and the votes cast. The collegial panel shall execute the decisions of the Adjudicative Committee.  
The decisions of the Adjudicative Committee on a case and the reasons therefor shall be disclosed in the adjudicative documents, except as otherwise provided by law.

### 第四章　人民法院的人员组成

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　人民法院的审判人员由院长、副院长、审判委员会委员和审判员等人员组成。

**Article 40** The adjudicative personnel of a People's Court shall consist of the president, vice presidents, members of the Adjudicative Committee, and judges, among others.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　人民法院院长负责本院全面工作，监督本院审判工作，管理本院行政事务。人民法院副院长协助院长工作。

**Article 41** The president of a People's Court shall be responsible for the overall work of the court, supervise the adjudicative work of the court, and manage the administrative affairs of the court. The vice presidents of a People's Court shall assist the president in his or her work.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　最高人民法院院长由全国人民代表大会选举，副院长、审判委员会委员、庭长、副庭长和审判员由院长提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会任免。  
最高人民法院巡回法庭庭长、副庭长，由最高人民法院院长提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会任免。

**Article 42** The president of the Supreme People's Court shall be elected by the National People's Congress, and the vice presidents, members of the Adjudicative Committee, division chiefs, deputy division chiefs, and judges shall be appointed or removed by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress upon the recommendation of the president.  
The chief judges and deputy chief judges of circuit courts of the Supreme People's Court shall be appointed or removed by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress upon the recommendation of the president of the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　地方各级人民法院院长由本级人民代表大会选举，副院长、审判委员会委员、庭长、副庭长和审判员由院长提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会任免。  
在省、自治区内按地区设立的和在直辖市内设立的中级人民法院院长，由省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会常务委员会根据主任会议的提名决定任免，副院长、审判委员会委员、庭长、副庭长和审判员由高级人民法院院长提请省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会常务委员会任免。

**Article 43** The president of a local People's Court at any level shall be elected by the People's Congress at the corresponding level, and the vice presidents, members of the Adjudicative Committee, division chiefs, deputy division chiefs, and judges shall be appointed or removed by the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the corresponding level upon the recommendation of the president.  
The president of an Intermediate People's Court established by region within a province or autonomous region, and of an Intermediate People's Court established in a municipality directly under the Central Government, shall be appointed or removed upon the decision of the Standing Committee of the People's Congress of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government based on the nomination of the Council of Chairpersons; the vice presidents, members of the Adjudicative Committee, division chiefs, deputy division chiefs, and judges of such courts shall be appointed or removed by the Standing Committee of the People's Congress of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government upon the recommendation of the president of the Higher People's Court.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　人民法院院长任期与产生它的人民代表大会每届任期相同。  
各级人民代表大会有权罢免由其选出的人民法院院长。在地方人民代表大会闭会期间，本级人民代表大会常务委员会认为人民法院院长需要撤换的，应当报请上级人民代表大会常务委员会批准。

**Article 44** The term of office of a People's Court president shall be the same as that of the People's Congress that elects him or her.  
People's Congresses at all levels shall have the power to remove the presidents of People's Courts elected by them. When the local People's Congress is not in session, if the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the corresponding level considers that a People's Court president needs to be replaced, it shall report to the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the next higher level for approval.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　人民法院的法官、审判辅助人员和司法行政人员实行分类管理。

**Article 45** The judges, adjudicative support personnel, and judicial administrative personnel of a People's Court shall be managed by category.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　法官实行员额制。法官员额根据案件数量、经济社会发展情况、人口数量和人民法院审级等因素确定。  
最高人民法院法官员额由最高人民法院商有关部门确定。地方各级人民法院法官员额，在省、自治区、直辖市内实行总量控制、动态管理。

**Article 46** Judges shall be subject to a post system. The number of judge posts shall be determined based on factors such as the number of cases, economic and social development, population size, and the level of the People's Court.  
The number of judge posts of the Supreme People's Court shall be determined by the Supreme People's Court in consultation with relevant authorities. The number of judge posts of local People's Courts at all levels shall be subject to overall control and dynamic management within the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　法官从取得法律职业资格并且具备法律规定的其他条件的人员中选任。初任法官应当由法官遴选委员会进行专业能力审核。上级人民法院的法官一般从下级人民法院的法官中择优遴选。  
院长应当具有法学专业知识和法律职业经历。副院长、审判委员会委员应当从法官、检察官或者其他具备法官、检察官条件的人员中产生。  
法官的职责、管理和保障，依照《中华人民共和国法官法》的规定。

**Article 47** Judges shall be selected and appointed from among persons who have obtained legal professional qualifications and meet other conditions prescribed by law. The professional competence of candidate judges for initial appointment shall be reviewed by a judge selection committee. Judges of People's Courts at a higher level shall generally be selected and appointed from among outstanding judges of People's Courts at lower levels.  
A court president shall possess legal professional knowledge and legal professional experience. A vice president or a member of the Adjudicative Committee shall be selected from among judges, procurators, or other persons who meet the qualifications for judges or procurators.  
The duties, management, and safeguards of judges shall be governed by the provisions of the Judges Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　人民法院的法官助理在法官指导下负责审查案件材料、草拟法律文书等审判辅助事务。  
符合法官任职条件的法官助理，经遴选后可以按照法官任免程序任命为法官。

**Article 48** Judge assistants of a People's Court shall, under the guidance of judges, be responsible for adjudicative support matters such as reviewing case materials and drafting legal documents.  
A judge assistant who meets the conditions for serving as a judge may, after undergoing selection, be appointed as a judge in accordance with the procedures for the appointment and removal of judges.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　人民法院的书记员负责法庭审理记录等审判辅助事务。

**Article 49** Court clerks of a People's Court shall be responsible for court trial records and other adjudicative support matters.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　人民法院的司法警察负责法庭警戒、人员押解和看管等警务事项。  
司法警察依照《中华人民共和国人民警察法》管理。

**Article 50** Judicial police of a People's Court shall be responsible for police matters such as court security, escort of persons, and custody.  
Judicial police shall be administered in accordance with the People's Police Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　人民法院根据审判工作需要，可以设司法技术人员，负责与审判工作有关的事项。

**Article 51** A People's Court may, based on the needs of adjudication work, set up judicial technical personnel to be responsible for matters related to adjudication work.

### 第五章　人民法院行使职权的保障

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　任何单位或者个人不得要求法官从事超出法定职责范围的事务。  
对于领导干部等干预司法活动、插手具体案件处理，或者人民法院内部人员过问案件情况的，办案人员应当全面如实记录并报告；有违法违纪情形的，由有关机关根据情节轻重追究行为人的责任。

**Article 52** No entity or individual shall require a judge to engage in affairs beyond the scope of statutory duties.  
Where leading officials or other persons interfere with judicial activities, meddle in the handling of specific cases, or where internal personnel of a People's Court inquire about case circumstances, the case-handling personnel shall fully and truthfully record and report such matters; if violations of law or discipline occur, the relevant authority shall pursue the liability of the persons involved based on the seriousness of the circumstances.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　人民法院作出的判决、裁定等生效法律文书，义务人应当依法履行；拒不履行的，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 53** For effective legal documents such as judgments and rulings rendered by a People's Court, the obligor shall perform according to law; if they refuse to perform, legal liability shall be pursued according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　人民法院采取必要措施，维护法庭秩序和审判权威。对妨碍人民法院依法行使职权的违法犯罪行为，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 54** A People's Court shall take necessary measures to maintain courtroom order and adjudication authority. For illegal or criminal acts that obstruct a People's Court from exercising its powers according to law, legal liability shall be pursued according to law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　人民法院实行培训制度，法官、审判辅助人员和司法行政人员应当接受理论和业务培训。

**Article 55** A People's Court implements a training system. Judges, adjudication support personnel, and judicial administrative personnel shall receive theoretical and professional training.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　人民法院人员编制实行专项管理。

**Article 56** The personnel staffing of a People's Court shall be subject to special management.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　人民法院的经费按照事权划分的原则列入财政预算，保障审判工作需要。

**Article 57** The funds of a People's Court shall be included in the financial budget in accordance with the principle of division of administrative powers to guarantee the needs of adjudication work.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　人民法院应当加强信息化建设，运用现代信息技术，促进司法公开，提高工作效率。

**Article 58** A People's Court shall strengthen informatization construction, utilize modern information technology, promote judicial openness, and enhance work efficiency.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　本法自2019年1月1日起施行。

**Article 59** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2019.
