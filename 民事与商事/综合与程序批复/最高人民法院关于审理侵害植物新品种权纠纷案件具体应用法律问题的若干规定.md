# 最高人民法院关于审理侵害植物新品种权纠纷案件具体应用法律问题的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：2020-12-29  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> 第1411次会议通过，根据2020年12月23日最高人民法院 审判委员会第1823次会议通过的《最高人民法院关于修改 〈最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干 问题的解释（二）〉等十八件知识产权类司法解释的决定》修正）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　植物新品种权所有人（以下称品种权人）或者利害关系人认为植物新品种权受到侵害的，可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。  
前款所称利害关系人，包括植物新品种实施许可合同的被许可人、品种权财产权利的合法继承人等。  
独占实施许可合同的被许可人可以单独向人民法院提起诉讼；排他实施许可合同的被许可人可以和品种权人共同起诉，也可以在品种权人不起诉时，自行提起诉讼；普通实施许可合同的被许可人经品种权人明确授权，可以提起诉讼。

**Article 1** The owner of a new plant variety right (hereinafter referred to as the variety right holder) or an interested party who believes that the new plant variety right has been infringed may file a prosecution with the People's Court according to law.  
The interested party mentioned in the preceding paragraph includes the licensee under a contract for the exploitation of the new plant variety, the lawful successor of the property right of the variety right, etc.  
A licensee under an exclusive exploitation license contract may file a prosecution with the People's Court separately; a licensee under a sole exploitation license contract may file a prosecution jointly with the variety right holder, or may file a prosecution on its own when the variety right holder does not file a prosecution; a licensee under a non-exclusive exploitation license contract may file a prosecution with the express authorization of the variety right holder.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　未经品种权人许可，生产、繁殖或者销售授权品种的繁殖材料，或者为商业目的将授权品种的繁殖材料重复使用于生产另一品种的繁殖材料的，人民法院应当认定为侵害植物新品种权。  
被诉侵权物的特征、特性与授权品种的特征、特性相同，或者特征、特性的不同是因非遗传变异所致的，人民法院一般应当认定被诉侵权物属于生产、繁殖或者销售授权品种的繁殖材料。  
被诉侵权人重复以授权品种的繁殖材料为亲本与其他亲本另行繁殖的，人民法院一般应当认定属于为商业目的将授权品种的繁殖材料重复使用于生产另一品种的繁殖材料。

**Article 2** Without the permission of the variety right holder, anyone who produces, propagates or sells the propagating material of an authorized variety, or repeatedly uses the propagating material of the authorized variety for commercial purposes in the production of the propagating material of another variety, the People's Court shall determine that it constitutes an infringement of the new plant variety right.  
If the characteristics and properties of the alleged infringing object are identical to those of the authorized variety, or the differences in characteristics and properties are caused by non-genetic variation, the People's Court shall generally determine that the alleged infringing object is the propagating material of the authorized variety that has been produced, propagated or sold.  
If the alleged infringer repeatedly uses the propagating material of the authorized variety as a parent stock to propagate separately with other parent stocks, the People's Court shall generally determine that it constitutes the repeated use of the propagating material of the authorized variety for commercial purposes in the production of the propagating material of another variety.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　侵害植物新品种权纠纷案件涉及的专门性问题需要鉴定的，由双方当事人协商确定的有鉴定资格的鉴定机构、鉴定人鉴定；协商不成的，由人民法院指定的有鉴定资格的鉴定机构、鉴定人鉴定。  
没有前款规定的鉴定机构、鉴定人的，由具有相应品种检测技术水平的专业机构、专业人员鉴定。

**Article 3** If an expert evaluation is required for a specialized issue involved in a dispute case concerning infringement of a new plant variety right, the evaluation shall be conducted by an evaluation institution or evaluator with evaluation qualifications determined through consultation between the two parties; if consultation fails, the evaluation shall be conducted by an evaluation institution or evaluator with evaluation qualifications designated by the People's Court.  
If there is no evaluation institution or evaluator as specified in the preceding paragraph, the evaluation shall be conducted by a professional institution or professional personnel with corresponding technical level of variety testing.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　对于侵害植物新品种权纠纷案件涉及的专门性问题可以采取田间观察检测、基因指纹图谱检测等方法鉴定。  
对采取前款规定方法作出的鉴定意见，人民法院应当依法质证，认定其证明力。

**Article 4** For the specialized issues involved in a dispute case concerning infringement of a new plant variety right, methods such as field observation testing and genetic fingerprint testing may be adopted for evaluation.  
With respect to the evaluation opinions made by adopting the methods prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the People's Court shall conduct cross-examination according to law and determine their probative force.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　品种权人或者利害关系人向人民法院提起侵害植物新品种权诉讼前，可以提出行为保全或者证据保全请求，人民法院经审查作出裁定。  
人民法院采取证据保全措施时，可以根据案件具体情况，邀请有关专业技术人员按照相应的技术规程协助取证。

**Article 5** Before filing a lawsuit for infringement of a new plant variety right with the People's Court, the variety right holder or an interested party may file a request for preservation of conduct or preservation of evidence, and the People's Court shall make a ruling after examination.  
When the People's Court adopts evidence preservation measures, it may, based on the specific circumstances of the case, invite relevant professional technicians to assist in obtaining evidence in accordance with the corresponding technical procedures.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院审理侵害植物新品种权纠纷案件，应当依照[民法典第一百七十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-179)、第一千一百八十五条和[种子法第七十三条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国种子法.md#art-73)的规定，结合案件具体情况，判决侵权人承担停止侵害、赔偿损失等民事责任。  
人民法院可以根据权利人的请求，按照权利人因被侵权所受实际损失或者侵权人因侵权所得利益确定赔偿数额。权利人的损失或者侵权人获得的利益难以确定的，可以参照该植物新品种权许可使用费的倍数合理确定。权利人为制止侵权行为所支付的合理开支应当另行计算。  
依照前款规定难以确定赔偿数额的，人民法院可以综合考虑侵权的性质、期间、后果，植物新品种权许可使用费的数额，植物新品种实施许可的种类、时间、范围及权利人调查、制止侵权所支付的合理费用等因素，在300万元以下确定赔偿数额。  
故意侵害他人植物新品种权，情节严重的，可以按照第二款确定数额的一倍以上三倍以下确定赔偿数额。

**Article 6** When trying a dispute case concerning infringement of a new plant variety right, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 179 and 1185 of the Civil Code and Article 73 of the Seed Law, and in light of the specific circumstances of the case, order the infringer to bear civil liability such as cessation of infringement and compensation for losses.  
The People's Court may, upon the request of the right holder, determine the amount of compensation based on the actual losses suffered by the right holder due to the infringement or the benefits obtained by the infringer due to the infringement. If it is difficult to determine the losses of the right holder or the benefits obtained by the infringer, the amount may be reasonably determined by reference to a multiple of the license fee for the exploitation of the new plant variety right. Reasonable expenses paid by the right holder to stop the infringing act shall be calculated separately.  
If it is difficult to determine the amount of compensation in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the People's Court may, by comprehensively considering factors such as the nature, duration, and consequences of the infringement, the amount of the license fee for the exploitation of the new plant variety right, the type, time, and scope of the exploitation license for the new plant variety, and the reasonable expenses paid by the right holder for investigation and stopping the infringement, determine the amount of compensation at not more than RMB 3 million yuan.  
If a person intentionally infringes upon another person's new plant variety right and the circumstances are serious, the amount of compensation may be determined at not less than one time and not more than three times the amount determined in accordance with the second paragraph.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　权利人和侵权人均同意将侵权物折价抵扣权利人所受损失的，人民法院应当准许。权利人或者侵权人不同意折价抵扣的，人民法院依照当事人的请求，责令侵权人对侵权物作消灭活性等使其不能再被用作繁殖材料的处理。  
侵权物正处于生长期或者销毁侵权物将导致重大不利后果的，人民法院可以不采取责令销毁侵权物的方法，而判令其支付相应的合理费用。但法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 7** If both the right holder and the infringer agree to offset the losses suffered by the right holder by discounting the infringing articles, the People's Court shall permit it. If either the right holder or the infringer does not agree to the offset by discount, the People's Court shall, upon the request of the party, order the infringer to treat the infringing articles in a manner that eliminates their viability or otherwise renders them unusable as propagating materials.  
If the infringing articles are in the growth period or the destruction of the infringing articles would cause significant adverse consequences, the People's Court may not adopt the method of ordering the destruction of the infringing articles, but shall order the infringer to pay corresponding reasonable expenses, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　以农业或者林业种植为业的个人、农村承包经营户接受他人委托代为繁殖侵害品种权的繁殖材料，不知道代繁物是侵害品种权的繁殖材料并说明委托人的，不承担赔偿责任。

**Article 8** If an individual engaged in agricultural or forestry planting as their occupation, or a rural contractual management household, accepts another person's commission to propagate propagating materials that infringe a variety right, and does not know that the materials being propagated are propagating materials that infringe the variety right and identifies the principal, they shall not bear compensation liability.
