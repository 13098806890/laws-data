# 最高人民法院关于审理侵犯商业秘密民事案件适用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕7号  
**公布日期**：2020-09-12  
**生效日期**：2020-09-12

> 《最高人民法院关于审理侵犯商业秘密民事案件适用法律若干问题的规定》已于 2020年 8月 24日由最高人民法院审判委员会第 1810次会议通过，现予公布，自2020年9月12日起施行。 2020年9月10日 最高人民法院 关于审理侵犯商业秘密民事案件适用法律 若干问题的规定 法释〔2020〕7号 为正确审理侵犯商业秘密民事案件，根据《中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等有关法律规定，结合审判实际，制定本规定。 第一条　与技术有关的结构、原料、组分、配方、材料、样品、样式、植物新品种繁殖材料、工艺、方法或其步骤、算法、数据、计算机程序及其有关文档等信息，人民法院可以认定构成反不正当竞争法第九条第四款所称的技术信息。 与经营活动有关的创意、管理、销售、财务、计划、样本、招投标材料、客户信息、数据等信息，人民法院可以认定构成反不正当竞争法第九条第四款所称的经营信息。 前款所称的客户信息，包括客户的名称、地址、联系方式以及交易习惯、意向、内容等信息。 第二条　当事人仅以与特定客户保持长期稳定交易关系为由，主张该特定客户属于商业秘密的，人民法院不予支持。 客户基于对员工个人的信赖而与该员工所在单位进行交易，该员工离职后，能够证明客户自愿选择与该员工或者该员工所在的新单位进行交易的，人民法院应当认定该员工没有采用不正当手段获取权利人的商业秘密。 第三条　权利人请求保护的信息在被诉侵权行为发生时不为所属领域的相关人员普遍知悉和容易获得的，人民法院应当认定为反不正当竞争法第九条第四款所称的不为公众所知悉。 第四条　具有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以认定有关信息为公众所知悉： （一）该信息在所属领域属于一般常识或者行业惯例的；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　与技术有关的结构、原料、组分、配方、材料、样品、样式、植物新品种繁殖材料、工艺、方法或其步骤、算法、数据、计算机程序及其有关文档等信息，人民法院可以认定构成[反不正当竞争法第九条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-9)第四款所称的技术信息。  
与经营活动有关的创意、管理、销售、财务、计划、样本、招投标材料、客户信息、数据等信息，人民法院可以认定构成[反不正当竞争法第九条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-9)第四款所称的经营信息。  
前款所称的客户信息，包括客户的名称、地址、联系方式以及交易习惯、意向、内容等信息。

**Article 1** : The People's Court may determine that information related to structures, raw materials, components, formulas, recipes, materials, samples, styles, propagating materials of new plant varieties, processes, methods or steps thereof, algorithms, data, computer programs and related documentation, etc. constitutes technical information as referred to in Paragraph 4 of Article 9 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The People's Court may determine that information related to business activities, such as creativity, management, sales, finance, plans, samples, bidding materials, customer information, data, etc., constitutes business information as referred to in Paragraph 4 of Article 9 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The customer information referred to in the preceding paragraph includes the customer's name, address, contact information, as well as trading habits, intentions, content, and other information.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人仅以与特定客户保持长期稳定交易关系为由，主张该特定客户属于商业秘密的，人民法院不予支持。  
客户基于对员工个人的信赖而与该员工所在单位进行交易，该员工离职后，能够证明客户自愿选择与该员工或者该员工所在的新单位进行交易的，人民法院应当认定该员工没有采用不正当手段获取权利人的商业秘密。

**Article 2** : Where a party claims that a specific customer constitutes a trade secret merely on the grounds of maintaining a long-term and stable transaction relationship with that specific customer, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
Where a customer conducts transactions with the entity where an employee works based on personal trust in that employee, and after the employee resigns, it can be proven that the customer voluntarily chooses to transact with that employee or the new entity where that employee works, the People's Court shall determine that the employee has not used improper means to obtain the trade secrets of the right holder.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　权利人请求保护的信息在被诉侵权行为发生时不为所属领域的相关人员普遍知悉和容易获得的，人民法院应当认定为[反不正当竞争法第九条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-9)第四款所称的不为公众所知悉。

**Article 3** : Where the information for which the right holder seeks protection is not generally known to or readily accessible by relevant persons in the field at the time of the alleged infringing act, the People's Court shall determine that it is not known to the public as referred to in Paragraph 4 of Article 9 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　具有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以认定有关信息为公众所知悉：  
（一）该信息在所属领域属于一般常识或者行业惯例的；  
（二）该信息仅涉及产品的尺寸、结构、材料、部件的简单组合等内容，所属领域的相关人员通过观察上市产品即可直接获得的；  
（三）该信息已经在公开出版物或者其他媒体上公开披露的；  
（四）该信息已通过公开的报告会、展览等方式公开的；  
（五）所属领域的相关人员从其他公开渠道可以获得该信息的。  
将为公众所知悉的信息进行整理、改进、加工后形成的新信息，符合[本规定第三条](#art-3)规定的，应当认定该新信息不为公众所知悉。

**Article 4** : Under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may determine that the relevant information is known to the public:  
(1) The information constitutes common knowledge or industry practice in the field to which it belongs;  
(2) The information merely involves the simple combination of dimensions, structures, materials, components, etc. of a product, and can be directly obtained by relevant persons in the field through observing the product on the market;  
(3) The information has been publicly disclosed in publicly available publications or other media;  
(4) The information has been made public through public seminars, exhibitions, or other means;  
(5) The information can be obtained by relevant persons in the field from other public channels.  
Where new information formed by organizing, improving, or processing information known to the public meets the requirements of Article 3 of these Provisions, it shall be determined that such new information is not known to the public.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　权利人为防止商业秘密泄露，在被诉侵权行为发生以前所采取的合理保密措施，人民法院应当认定为[反不正当竞争法第九条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-9)第四款所称的相应保密措施。  
人民法院应当根据商业秘密及其载体的性质、商业秘密的商业价值、保密措施的可识别程度、保密措施与商业秘密的对应程度以及权利人的保密意愿等因素，认定权利人是否采取了相应保密措施。

**Article 5** : The reasonable confidentiality measures taken by the right holder to prevent the disclosure of trade secrets prior to the occurrence of the alleged infringing act shall be determined by the People's Court as the corresponding confidentiality measures referred to in Paragraph 4 of Article 9 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The People's Court shall determine whether the right holder has taken corresponding confidentiality measures based on factors such as the nature of the trade secret and its carrier, the commercial value of the trade secret, the identifiability of the confidentiality measures, the degree of correspondence between the confidentiality measures and the trade secret, and the right holder's intention to maintain confidentiality.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　具有下列情形之一，在正常情况下足以防止商业秘密泄露的，人民法院应当认定权利人采取了相应保密措施：  
（一）签订保密协议或者在合同中约定保密义务的；  
（二）通过章程、培训、规章制度、书面告知等方式，对能够接触、获取商业秘密的员工、前员工、供应商、客户、来访者等提出保密要求的；  
（三）对涉密的厂房、车间等生产经营场所限制来访者或者进行区分管理的;  
（四）以标记、分类、隔离、加密、封存、限制能够接触或者获取的人员范围等方式，对商业秘密及其载体进行区分和管理的；  
（五）对能够接触、获取商业秘密的计算机设备、电子设备、网络设备、存储设备、软件等，采取禁止或者限制使用、访问、存储、复制等措施的；  
（六）要求离职员工登记、返还、清除、销毁其接触或者获取的商业秘密及其载体，继续承担保密义务的；  
（七）采取其他合理保密措施的。

**Article 6** : Where any of the following circumstances exists and is sufficient to prevent the disclosure of trade secrets under normal conditions, the People's Court shall determine that the right holder has taken corresponding confidentiality measures:  
(1) Entering into a confidentiality agreement or stipulating confidentiality obligations in a contract;  
(2) Imposing confidentiality requirements on employees, former employees, suppliers, customers, visitors, etc. who have access to or can obtain trade secrets through articles of association, training, rules and regulations, written notifications, or other means;  
(3) Restricting visitors or implementing differentiated management in production and operation premises such as factories and workshops involving trade secrets;  
(4) Differentiating and managing trade secrets and their carriers by means of marking, classification, isolation, encryption, sealing, restricting the scope of persons who can access or obtain them, etc.;  
(5) Taking measures such as prohibiting or restricting the use, access, storage, copying, etc. of computer equipment, electronic equipment, network equipment, storage equipment, software, etc. that can access or obtain trade secrets;  
(6) Requiring departing employees to register, return, clear, or destroy the trade secrets and their carriers they have accessed or obtained, and continue to assume confidentiality obligations;  
(7) Taking other reasonable confidentiality measures.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　权利人请求保护的信息因不为公众所知悉而具有现实的或者潜在的商业价值的，人民法院经审查可以认定为[反不正当竞争法第九条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-9)第四款所称的具有商业价值。  
生产经营活动中形成的阶段性成果符合前款规定的，人民法院经审查可以认定该成果具有商业价值。

**Article 7** : Where the information for which the right holder seeks protection has actual or potential commercial value because it is not known to the public, the People's Court may, upon examination, determine that it has commercial value as referred to in Paragraph 4 of Article 9 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where a phased achievement formed in production and business activities meets the requirements of the preceding paragraph, the People's Court may, upon examination, determine that such achievement has commercial value.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　被诉侵权人以违反法律规定或者公认的商业道德的方式获取权利人的商业秘密的，人民法院应当认定属于[反不正当竞争法第九条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-9)第一款所称的以其他不正当手段获取权利人的商业秘密。

**Article 8** : Where the alleged infringer obtains the right holder's trade secrets by means that violate legal provisions or generally accepted commercial ethics, the People's Court shall determine that it constitutes obtaining the right holder's trade secrets by other improper means as referred to in Paragraph 1 of Article 9 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　被诉侵权人在生产经营活动中直接使用商业秘密，或者对商业秘密进行修改、改进后使用，或者根据商业秘密调整、优化、改进有关生产经营活动的，人民法院应当认定属于[反不正当竞争法第九条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-9)所称的使用商业秘密。

**Article 9** : Where the alleged infringer directly uses trade secrets in production and business activities, or uses them after modification or improvement, or adjusts, optimizes, or improves relevant production and business activities based on trade secrets, the People's Court shall determine that it constitutes using trade secrets as referred to in Article 9 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　当事人根据法律规定或者合同约定所承担的保密义务，人民法院应当认定属于[反不正当竞争法第九条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-9)第一款所称的保密义务。  
当事人未在合同中约定保密义务，但根据诚信原则以及合同的性质、目的、缔约过程、交易习惯等，被诉侵权人知道或者应当知道其获取的信息属于权利人的商业秘密的，人民法院应当认定被诉侵权人对其获取的商业秘密承担保密义务。

**Article 10** : The confidentiality obligations undertaken by the parties in accordance with legal provisions or contractual stipulations shall be determined by the People's Court as the confidentiality obligations referred to in Paragraph 1 of Article 9 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where the parties have not stipulated confidentiality obligations in the contract, but the alleged infringer knows or should have known that the information it obtained is a trade secret of the right holder based on the principle of good faith and the nature, purpose, contracting process, trading habits, etc. of the contract, the People's Court shall determine that the alleged infringer assumes confidentiality obligations for the trade secrets it obtained.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　法人、非法人组织的经营、管理人员以及具有劳动关系的其他人员，人民法院可以认定为[反不正当竞争法第九条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-9)第三款所称的员工、前员工。

**Article 11** : The People's Court may determine that the management and operational personnel of a legal person or unincorporated organization, as well as other persons with an employment relationship, constitute the employees or former employees referred to in Paragraph 3 of Article 9 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院认定员工、前员工是否有渠道或者机会获取权利人的商业秘密，可以考虑与其有关的下列因素：  
（一）职务、职责、权限；  
（二）承担的本职工作或者单位分配的任务；  
（三）参与和商业秘密有关的生产经营活动的具体情形；  
（四）是否保管、使用、存储、复制、控制或者以其他方式接触、获取商业秘密及其载体；  
（五）需要考虑的其他因素。

**Article 12** : When determining whether an employee or former employee has the channel or opportunity to obtain the right holder's trade secrets, the People's Court may consider the following factors relevant to that person:  
(1) Position, duties, and authority;  
(2) The primary work or tasks assigned by the unit;  
(3) The specific circumstances of participation in production and business activities related to trade secrets;  
(4) Whether the person stores, uses, stores, copies, controls, or otherwise accesses or obtains trade secrets and their carriers;  
(5) Other factors that need to be considered.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　被诉侵权信息与商业秘密不存在实质性区别的，人民法院可以认定被诉侵权信息与商业秘密构成[反不正当竞争法第三十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-32)第二款所称的实质上相同。  
人民法院认定是否构成前款所称的实质上相同，可以考虑下列因素：  
（一）被诉侵权信息与商业秘密的异同程度；  
（二）所属领域的相关人员在被诉侵权行为发生时是否容易想到被诉侵权信息与商业秘密的区别；  
（三）被诉侵权信息与商业秘密的用途、使用方式、目的、效果等是否具有实质性差异；  
（四）公有领域中与商业秘密相关信息的情况；  
（五）需要考虑的其他因素。

**Article 13** : Where there is no substantive difference between the alleged infringing information and the trade secret, the People's Court may determine that the alleged infringing information and the trade secret constitute being essentially the same as referred to in Paragraph 2 of Article 32 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China.  
When determining whether the circumstances referred to in the preceding paragraph constitute being essentially the same, the People's Court may consider the following factors:  
(1) The degree of similarity or difference between the alleged infringing information and the trade secret;  
(2) Whether relevant persons in the field would readily conceive of the differences between the alleged infringing information and the trade secret at the time of the alleged infringing act;  
(3) Whether there are substantive differences in the purpose, method of use, objective, effect, etc. between the alleged infringing information and the trade secret;  
(4) The status of information related to the trade secret in the public domain;  
(5) Other factors that need to be considered.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　通过自行开发研制或者反向工程获得被诉侵权信息的，人民法院应当认定不属于[反不正当竞争法第九条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-9)规定的侵犯商业秘密行为。  
前款所称的反向工程，是指通过技术手段对从公开渠道取得的产品进行拆卸、测绘、分析等而获得该产品的有关技术信息。  
被诉侵权人以不正当手段获取权利人的商业秘密后，又以反向工程为由主张未侵犯商业秘密的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 14** : Where the alleged infringing information is obtained through independent development or reverse engineering, the People's Court shall determine that it does not constitute an act of infringing trade secrets under Article 9 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Reverse engineering referred to in the preceding paragraph means obtaining technical information related to a product by disassembling, surveying, analyzing, etc. the product obtained from public channels through technical means.  
Where the alleged infringer obtains the right holder's trade secrets by improper means and later claims that it has not infringed trade secrets on the grounds of reverse engineering, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　被申请人试图或者已经以不正当手段获取、披露、使用或者允许他人使用权利人所主张的商业秘密，不采取行为保全措施会使判决难以执行或者造成当事人其他损害，或者将会使权利人的合法权益受到难以弥补的损害的，人民法院可以依法裁定采取行为保全措施。  
前款规定的情形属于[民事诉讼法第一百条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-100)、第一百零一条所称情况紧急的，人民法院应当在四十八小时内作出裁定。

**Article 15** : Where the respondent attempts or has obtained, disclosed, used, or allowed others to use the trade secrets claimed by the right holder by improper means, and failure to take behavioral preservation measures would make the judgment difficult to enforce or cause other harm to the parties, or would cause irreparable damage to the right holder's lawful rights and interests, the People's Court may, according to law, rule to take behavioral preservation measures.  
Where the circumstances described in the preceding paragraph fall under the urgent circumstances referred to in Article 100 and Article 101 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court shall make a ruling within forty-eight hours.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　经营者以外的其他自然人、法人和非法人组织侵犯商业秘密，权利人依据[反不正当竞争法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-17)的规定主张侵权人应当承担的民事责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 16** : Where a natural person, legal person, or unincorporated organization other than a business operator infringes trade secrets, and the right holder claims, in accordance with Article 17 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China, that the infringer shall bear civil liability, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人民法院对于侵犯商业秘密行为判决停止侵害的民事责任时，停止侵害的时间一般应当持续到该商业秘密已为公众所知悉时为止。  
依照前款规定判决停止侵害的时间明显不合理的，人民法院可以在依法保护权利人的商业秘密竞争优势的情况下，判决侵权人在一定期限或者范围内停止使用该商业秘密。

**Article 17** : When the People's Court imposes the civil liability of cessation of infringement for an act of trade secret infringement, the duration of cessation of infringement shall generally last until the trade secret becomes known to the public.  
If the period of cessation of infringement as determined in accordance with the preceding paragraph is clearly unreasonable, the People's Court may, while protecting the right holder's competitive advantage in trade secrets in accordance with law, order the infringer to cease using the trade secret within a certain period or scope.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　权利人请求判决侵权人返还或者销毁商业秘密载体，清除其控制的商业秘密信息的，人民法院一般应予支持。

**Article 18** : Where the right holder requests a judgment ordering the infringer to return or destroy the carrier of trade secrets and to eliminate the trade secret information under its control, the People's Court shall generally support such request.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　因侵权行为导致商业秘密为公众所知悉的，人民法院依法确定赔偿数额时，可以考虑商业秘密的商业价值。  
人民法院认定前款所称的商业价值，应当考虑研究开发成本、实施该项商业秘密的收益、可得利益、可保持竞争优势的时间等因素。

**Article 19** : Where a trade secret becomes known to the public due to an infringing act, the People's Court may, when determining the amount of damages according to law, consider the commercial value of the trade secret.  
When determining the commercial value referred to in the preceding paragraph, the People's Court shall consider factors such as research and development costs, the revenue from exploiting the trade secret, the obtainable benefits, and the period during which a competitive advantage can be maintained.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　权利人请求参照商业秘密许可使用费确定因被侵权所受到的实际损失的，人民法院可以根据许可的性质、内容、实际履行情况以及侵权行为的性质、情节、后果等因素确定。  
人民法院依照[反不正当竞争法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-17)第四款确定赔偿数额的，可以考虑商业秘密的性质、商业价值、研究开发成本、创新程度、能带来的竞争优势以及侵权人的主观过错、侵权行为的性质、情节、后果等因素。

**Article 20** : Where the right holder requests that the actual losses suffered from the infringement be determined by reference to the license fee for the trade secret, the People's Court may determine the amount based on factors such as the nature and content of the license, the actual performance thereof, and the nature, circumstances, and consequences of the infringing act.  
When determining the amount of damages in accordance with Paragraph 4 of Article 17 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court may consider factors such as the nature of the trade secret, its commercial value, research and development costs, degree of innovation, the competitive advantage it can bring, as well as the subjective fault of the infringer, and the nature, circumstances, and consequences of the infringing act.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对于涉及当事人或者案外人的商业秘密的证据、材料，当事人或者案外人书面申请人民法院采取保密措施的，人民法院应当在保全、证据交换、质证、委托鉴定、询问、庭审等诉讼活动中采取必要的保密措施。  
违反前款所称的保密措施的要求，擅自披露商业秘密或者在诉讼活动之外使用或者允许他人使用在诉讼中接触、获取的商业秘密的，应当依法承担民事责任。构成[民事诉讼法第一百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-111)规定情形的，人民法院可以依法采取强制措施。构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 21** : Where evidence or materials involve trade secrets of a party or a non-party, and the party or non-party applies in writing to the People's Court for confidentiality measures, the People's Court shall take necessary confidentiality measures in litigation activities such as preservation, evidence exchange, cross-examination, entrustment of expert evaluation, inquiry, and trial.  
Whoever violates the requirements of the confidentiality measures referred to in the preceding paragraph by disclosing trade secrets without authorization, or using or allowing others to use trade secrets accessed or obtained in litigation outside of litigation activities, shall bear civil liability according to law. If the circumstances constitute those specified in Article 111 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court may take compulsory measures according to law. If a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　人民法院审理侵犯商业秘密民事案件时，对在侵犯商业秘密犯罪刑事诉讼程序中形成的证据，应当按照法定程序，全面、客观地审查。  
由公安机关、检察机关或者人民法院保存的与被诉侵权行为具有关联性的证据，侵犯商业秘密民事案件的当事人及其诉讼代理人因客观原因不能自行收集，申请调查收集的，人民法院应当准许，但可能影响正在进行的刑事诉讼程序的除外。

**Article 22** : When hearing a civil case of trade secret infringement, the People's Court shall examine the evidence formed in the criminal procedure for the crime of trade secret infringement comprehensively and objectively in accordance with legal procedures.  
Where evidence kept by a public security organ, a procuratorial organ, or the People's Court that is related to the alleged infringing act cannot be collected by the party or its litigation agent due to objective reasons, and the party or its litigation agent applies for investigation and collection, the People's Court shall grant the application, unless it may affect an ongoing criminal procedure.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　当事人主张依据生效刑事裁判认定的实际损失或者违法所得确定涉及同一侵犯商业秘密行为的民事案件赔偿数额的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 23** : Where a party claims that the amount of damages in a civil case involving the same act of trade secret infringement shall be determined based on the actual losses or illegal gains found in an effective criminal judgment, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　权利人已经提供侵权人因侵权所获得的利益的初步证据，但与侵犯商业秘密行为相关的账簿、资料由侵权人掌握的，人民法院可以根据权利人的申请，责令侵权人提供该账簿、资料。侵权人无正当理由拒不提供或者不如实提供的，人民法院可以根据权利人的主张和提供的证据认定侵权人因侵权所获得的利益。

**Article 24** : Where the right holder has provided preliminary evidence of the profits gained by the infringer from the infringement, but the account books and materials related to the trade secret infringement are in the control of the infringer, the People's Court may, upon the application of the right holder, order the infringer to provide such account books and materials. If the infringer, without justifiable reasons, refuses to provide them or fails to provide them truthfully, the People's Court may determine the profits gained by the infringer from the infringement based on the right holder's claims and the evidence provided.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　当事人以涉及同一被诉侵犯商业秘密行为的刑事案件尚未审结为由，请求中止审理侵犯商业秘密民事案件，人民法院在听取当事人意见后认为必须以该刑事案件的审理结果为依据的，应予支持。

**Article 25** : Where a party requests the suspension of the trial of a civil case of trade secret infringement on the grounds that the criminal case involving the same alleged act of trade secret infringement has not yet been concluded, and the People's Court, after hearing the opinions of the parties, considers that the civil case must be based on the outcome of the criminal case, it shall support such request.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对于侵犯商业秘密行为，商业秘密独占使用许可合同的被许可人提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。  
排他使用许可合同的被许可人和权利人共同提起诉讼，或者在权利人不起诉的情况下自行提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。  
普通使用许可合同的被许可人和权利人共同提起诉讼，或者经权利人书面授权单独提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 26** : Where a licensee under an exclusive license contract for the use of trade secrets files a lawsuit regarding an act of trade secret infringement, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.  
Where a licensee under a sole license contract files a lawsuit jointly with the right holder, or files a lawsuit on its own where the right holder does not file a lawsuit, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.  
Where a licensee under a non-exclusive license contract files a lawsuit jointly with the right holder, or files a lawsuit individually with the written authorization of the right holder, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　权利人应当在一审法庭辩论结束前明确所主张的商业秘密具体内容。仅能明确部分的，人民法院对该明确的部分进行审理。  
权利人在第二审程序中另行主张其在一审中未明确的商业秘密具体内容的，第二审人民法院可以根据当事人自愿的原则就与该商业秘密具体内容有关的诉讼请求进行调解；调解不成的，告知当事人另行起诉。双方当事人均同意由第二审人民法院一并审理的，第二审人民法院可以一并裁判。

**Article 27** : The right holder shall specify the specific content of the trade secret claimed before the conclusion of the court debate in the first instance. If only part of it can be specified, the People's Court shall hear that specified part.  
Where the right holder separately claims in the second instance the specific content of the trade secret that was not specified in the first instance, the court of second instance may, based on the principle of voluntariness of the parties, mediate with respect to the claims related to the specific content of the trade secret; if mediation fails, the parties shall be informed to file a separate lawsuit. If both parties agree to have the court of second instance hear the matter together, the court of second instance may render a single judgment.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　人民法院审理侵犯商业秘密民事案件，适用被诉侵权行为发生时的法律。被诉侵权行为在法律修改之前已经发生且持续到法律修改之后的，适用修改后的法律。

**Article 28** : When hearing a civil case of trade secret infringement, the People's Court shall apply the law in effect at the time the alleged infringing act occurred. If the alleged infringing act occurred before the amendment of the law and continued after the amendment, the amended law shall apply.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本规定自2020年9月12日起施行。最高人民法院以前发布的相关司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。  
本规定施行后，人民法院正在审理的一审、二审案件适用本规定；施行前已经作出生效裁判的案件，不适用本规定再审。

**Article 29** : These Provisions shall come into force as of September 12, 2020. Where any relevant judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.  
After these Provisions come into force, they shall apply to cases of first instance and second instance being tried by the People's Court; cases in which an effective judgment has been rendered before these Provisions come into force shall not be subject to retrial under these Provisions.
