# 公平竞争审查条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2024-06-06  
**生效日期**：2024-08-01

> 第十条　起草单位起草的政策措施，没有法律、行政法规依据或者未经国务院批准，不得含有下列影响生产经营成本的内容： （一）给予特定经营者税收优惠； （二）给予特定经营者选择性、差异化的财政奖励或者补贴； （三）给予特定经营者要素获取、行政事业性收费、政府性基金、社会保险费等方面的优惠； （四）其他影响生产经营成本的内容。 第十一条　起草单位起草的政策措施，不得含有下列影响生产经营行为的内容： （一）强制或者变相强制经营者实施垄断行为，或者为经营者实施垄断行为提供便利条件； （二）超越法定权限制定政府指导价、政府定价，为特定经营者提供优惠价格； （三）违法干预实行市场调节价的商品、要素的价格水平； （四）其他影响生产经营行为的内容。 第十二条　起草单位起草的政策措施，具有或者可能具有排除、限制竞争效果，但符合下列情形之一，且没有对公平竞争影响更小的替代方案，并能够确定合理的实施期限或者终止条件的，可以出台： （一）为维护国家安全和发展利益的； （二）为促进科学技术进步、增强国家自主创新能力的； （三）为实现节约能源、保护环境、救灾救助等社会公共利益的； （四）法律、行政法规规定的其他情形。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　起草单位起草的政策措施，不得含有下列影响生产经营行为的内容：  
（一）强制或者变相强制经营者实施垄断行为，或者为经营者实施垄断行为提供便利条件；  
（二）超越法定权限制定政府指导价、政府定价，为特定经营者提供优惠价格；  
（三）违法干预实行市场调节价的商品、要素的价格水平；  
（四）其他影响生产经营行为的内容。

**Article 11** The policy measures drafted by the drafting entity shall not contain any of the following content affecting production and operation activities:  
(1) forcing or indirectly forcing business operators to implement monopolistic acts, or providing convenience for business operators to implement monopolistic acts;  
(2) exceeding statutory authority to set government-guided prices or government-fixed prices, or providing preferential prices for specific business operators;  
(3) illegally intervening in the price levels of commodities or factors with market-adjusted prices;  
(4) other content affecting production and operation activities.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　起草单位起草的政策措施，具有或者可能具有排除、限制竞争效果，但符合下列情形之一，且没有对公平竞争影响更小的替代方案，并能够确定合理的实施期限或者终止条件的，可以出台：  
（一）为维护国家安全和发展利益的；  
（二）为促进科学技术进步、增强国家自主创新能力的；  
（三）为实现节约能源、保护环境、救灾救助等社会公共利益的；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 12** Where a policy measure drafted by the drafting entity has or may have the effect of eliminating or restricting competition, but meets one of the following circumstances, and there is no alternative plan with a lesser impact on fair competition, and a reasonable implementation period or termination condition can be determined, it may be issued:  
(1) for safeguarding national security and development interests;  
(2) for promoting scientific and technological progress and enhancing national independent innovation capabilities;  
(3) for achieving social public interests such as energy conservation, environmental protection, disaster relief and assistance;  
(4) other circumstances provided for by laws and administrative regulations.

### 第三章　审查机制

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　拟由部门出台的政策措施，由起草单位在起草阶段开展公平竞争审查。  
拟由多个部门联合出台的政策措施，由牵头起草单位在起草阶段开展公平竞争审查。

**Article 13** For policy measures to be issued by a department, the drafting entity shall conduct a fair competition review during the drafting stage.  
For policy measures to be jointly issued by multiple departments, the lead drafting entity shall conduct a fair competition review during the drafting stage.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　拟由县级以上人民政府出台或者提请本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会审议的政策措施，由本级人民政府市场监督管理部门会同起草单位在起草阶段开展公平竞争审查。起草单位应当开展初审，并将政策措施草案和初审意见送市场监督管理部门审查。

**Article 14** For policy measures to be issued by a people's government at or above the county level or submitted to the people's congress at the same level and its standing committee for deliberation, the market supervision and administration department of the people's government at the same level shall, together with the drafting entity, conduct a fair competition review during the drafting stage. The drafting entity shall conduct a preliminary review and submit the draft policy measure and the preliminary review opinion to the market supervision and administration department for review.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家鼓励有条件的地区探索建立跨区域、跨部门的公平竞争审查工作机制。

**Article 15** The State encourages regions with the conditions to explore the establishment of a cross-regional and cross-departmental working mechanism for fair competition review.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　开展公平竞争审查，应当听取有关经营者、行业协会商会等利害关系人关于公平竞争影响的意见。涉及社会公众利益的，应当听取社会公众意见。

**Article 16** When conducting a fair competition review, opinions on the impact on fair competition shall be solicited from relevant stakeholders such as business operators, industry associations and chambers of commerce. Where social public interests are involved, opinions from the general public shall be solicited.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　开展公平竞争审查，应当按照本条例规定的审查标准，在评估对公平竞争影响后，作出审查结论。  
适用[本条例第十二条](#art-12)规定的，应当在审查结论中详细说明。

**Article 17** When conducting a fair competition review, a review conclusion shall be made after assessing the impact on fair competition in accordance with the review standards prescribed in these Regulations.  
Where Article 12 of these Regulations applies, a detailed explanation shall be provided in the review conclusion.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　政策措施未经公平竞争审查，或者经公平竞争审查认为违反本条例第八条至第十一条规定且不符合第十二条规定情形的，不得出台。

**Article 18** Where a policy measure has not undergone a fair competition review, or has been found through a fair competition review to violate the provisions of Articles 8 to 11 of these Regulations and does not comply with the circumstances under Article 12, it shall not be issued.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　有关部门和单位、个人对在公平竞争审查过程中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私，应当依法予以保密。

**Article 19** Relevant departments and entities and individuals shall keep confidential, according to law, any State secrets, trade secrets, and personal privacy learned during the fair competition review process.

### 第四章　监督保障

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院市场监督管理部门强化公平竞争审查工作监督保障，建立健全公平竞争审查抽查、举报处理、督查等机制。

**Article 20** The market supervision and administration department of the State Council shall strengthen the supervision and guarantee of fair competition review work, and establish and improve mechanisms such as spot checks, complaint handling, and supervision for fair competition review.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　市场监督管理部门建立健全公平竞争审查抽查机制，组织对有关政策措施开展抽查，经核查发现违反本条例规定的，应当督促起草单位进行整改。  
市场监督管理部门应当向本级人民政府报告抽查情况，抽查结果可以向社会公开。

**Article 21** The market supervision and administration department shall establish and improve a spot-check mechanism for fair competition review, organize spot checks on relevant policy measures, and upon verification finding any violation of these Regulations, shall urge the drafting entity to make rectification.  
The market supervision and administration department shall report the spot-check results to the people's government at the same level, and the spot-check results may be disclosed to the public.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　对违反本条例规定的政策措施，任何单位和个人可以向市场监督管理部门举报。市场监督管理部门接到举报后，应当及时处理或者转送有关部门处理。  
市场监督管理部门应当向社会公开受理举报的电话、信箱或者电子邮件地址。

**Article 22** Any entity or individual may report to the market supervision and administration department any policy measure that violates the provisions of these Regulations. Upon receiving a report, the market supervision and administration department shall handle it promptly or transfer it to the relevant department for handling.  
The market supervision and administration department shall publicly disclose the telephone number, mailbox, or email address for receiving reports.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院定期对县级以上地方人民政府公平竞争审查工作机制建设情况、公平竞争审查工作开展情况、举报处理情况等开展督查。国务院市场监督管理部门负责具体实施。

**Article 23** The State Council shall periodically conduct supervision on the construction of the fair competition review working mechanism, the implementation of fair competition review work, and the handling of reports of local people's governments at or above the county level. The market supervision and administration department of the State Council shall be responsible for the specific implementation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　起草单位未依照本条例规定开展公平竞争审查，经市场监督管理部门督促，逾期仍未整改的，上一级市场监督管理部门可以对其负责人进行约谈。

**Article 24** Where a drafting entity fails to conduct a fair competition review in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, and upon being urged by the market supervision and administration department, fails to make rectification within the time limit, the market supervision and administration department at the next higher level may interview its responsible person.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　未依照本条例规定开展公平竞争审查，造成严重不良影响的，对起草单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 25** Where a fair competition review is not conducted in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, causing serious adverse effects, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible personnel of the drafting entity shall be given sanctions according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国务院市场监督管理部门根据本条例制定公平竞争审查的具体实施办法。

**Article 26** The market supervision and administration department of the State Council shall formulate specific implementation measures for fair competition review in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本条例自2024年8月1日起施行。

**Article 27** These Regulations shall come into force as of August 1, 2024.
