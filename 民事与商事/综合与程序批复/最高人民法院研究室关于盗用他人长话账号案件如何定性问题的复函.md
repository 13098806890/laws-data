<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院研究室  
关于盗用他人长话账号案件如何定性  
问题的复函  
（1991年9月14日）  
公安部法制司：  
你司8月16日函询我们对盗用他人长话账号行为的定性意见。经研究，我们认为，这类案件一般来说符合盗窃罪的特征。但是，由于这类案件情况比较复杂，是否都追究刑事责任，还要具体案件具体分析。  
以上意见，供参考。  
附：  
公安部法制司  
关于盗用他人长话账号案件如何定性的函  
（1991年8月26日）  
最高人民法院研究室：  
最近安徽省公安厅就一起盗用他人长话账号案件如何定性问题请示我部。基本案情如下：被告人葛春生，男，26岁，劳改释放人员，现无业。葛春生从一朋友处知道被害人年经宝的长话账号。1991年1月至6月间，葛春生用此账号多次给香港的妻子打长途电话，用去被害人电话费6000余元。  
安徽省公安厅对此案有两种看法：一是认为构成盗窃罪；二是认为构成诈骗罪。经研究我们倾向于第一种意见。因为第一，所谓诈骗的行为是指被告人采用欺骗的手法使账号所有人产生错觉，而主动提供其使用，是受骗上当的结果。这是盗窃罪与诈骗罪的主要区别。而此案中的被告人并未向邮电局提供假账号，或者采用欺诈方法占有他人财物，而是采用盗用他人账号的方法实施犯罪，实际被侵害的对象是账号被盗用的人，因此不构成诈骗罪；第二，被告人的行为基本符合盗窃罪的特征，有占有他人财产的故意，客观上使被害人遭受了损失，并有秘密窃取的行为，此案与其他盗窃案不同的是行为方式上有一些不同，所以可以考虑按盗窃罪来处理。此案系实际执法中遇到的新情况，定性问题我们也拿不准，故送你院征求意见，望尽快答复。

**Article 1** Research Office of the Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Characterization of Cases Involving Unauthorized Use of Others' Long-Distance Telephone Accounts  
(September 14, 1991)  
Legal System Department of the Ministry of Public Security:  
Your Department inquired by letter on August 16 regarding our opinion on the characterization of the act of unauthorized use of others' long-distance telephone accounts. Upon study, we believe that such cases generally bear the characteristics of the crime of theft. However, given the complexity of such cases, whether criminal liability shall be investigated in all cases depends on a case-by-case analysis.  
The above opinions are for your reference.  
Attachment:  
Legal System Department of the Ministry of Public Security  
Letter on the Characterization of Cases Involving Unauthorized Use of Others' Long-Distance Telephone Accounts  
(August 26, 1991)  
Research Office of the Supreme People's Court:  
Recently, the Public Security Department of Anhui Province requested our Department's opinion on the characterization of a case involving unauthorized use of another person's long-distance telephone account. The basic facts are as follows: The defendant, Ge Chunsheng, male, 26 years old, a released re-education-through-labor offender, currently unemployed. Ge Chunsheng learned the victim Nian Jingbao's long-distance telephone account from a friend. From January to June 1991, Ge Chunsheng used this account to make multiple long-distance calls to his wife in Hong Kong, incurring telephone expenses of over 6,000 yuan for the victim.  
The Public Security Department of Anhui Province had two views on this case: first, that it constitutes the crime of theft; second, that it constitutes the crime of fraud. After study, we lean towards the first opinion. Because, first, the alleged fraudulent act refers to the defendant using deceptive means to cause the account owner to develop a mistaken belief and voluntarily provide the defendant with use, which is a result of being deceived. This is the main difference between the crime of theft and the crime of fraud. In this case, the defendant did not provide a false account to the post and telecommunications office, nor did he use fraudulent methods to take possession of another person's property. Instead, he committed the crime by unauthorized use of another person's account, and the actual victim was the person whose account was used without authorization. Therefore, the crime of fraud is not established. Second, the defendant's conduct generally conforms to the characteristics of the crime of theft: there was intent to possess another's property, objectively causing loss to the victim, and there was a secret act of taking. The difference between this case and other theft cases lies in some differences in the method of conduct, so it may be considered to be dealt with as the crime of theft. This case presents a new situation encountered in actual law enforcement, and we are also uncertain about the characterization. Therefore, we submit it to your Office for comments, and hope for a prompt reply.
