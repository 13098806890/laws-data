# 最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2003〕11号  
**公布日期**：2003-06-25  
**生效日期**：2003-07-09

> （2003年1月9日最高人民法院审判委员会第1263次会议通过　2003年6月25日最高人民法院公告公布　自2003年7月9日起施行） 为正确审理涉及工会经费和财产、工会工作人员权利的民事案件，维护工会和职工的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国工会法》、《中华人民共和国民法通则》和《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律的规定，现就有关法律的适用问题解释如下： 第一条　人民法院审理涉及工会组织的有关案件时，应当认定依照工会法建立的工会组织的社团法人资格。具有法人资格的工会组织依法独立享有民事权利，承担民事义务。建立工会的企业、事业单位、机关与所建工会以及工会投资兴办的企业，根据法律和司法解释的规定，应当分别承担各自的民事责任。 第二条　根据工会法第十八条规定，人民法院审理劳动争议案件，涉及确定基层工会专职主席、副主席或者委员延长的劳动合同期限的，应当自上述人员工会职务任职期限届满之日起计算，延长的期限等于其工会职务任职的期间。 工会法第十八条规定的“个人严重过失”，是指具有《中华人民共和国劳动法》第二十五条第（二）项、第（三）项或者第（四）项规定的情形。 第三条　基层工会或者上级工会依照工会法第四十三条规定向人民法院申请支付令的，由被申请人所在地的基层人民法院管辖。 第四条　人民法院根据工会法第四十三条的规定受理工会提出的拨缴工会经费的支付令申请后，应当先行征询被申请人的意见。被申请人仅对应拨缴经费数额有异议的，人民法院应当就无异议部分的工会经费数额发出支付令。 人民法院在审理涉及工会经费的案件中，需要按照工会法第四十二条第一款第（二）项规定的“全部职工”、“工资总额”确定拨缴数额的，“全部职工”、“工资总额”的计算，应当按照国家有关部门规定的标准执行。 第五条　根据工会法第四十三条和民事诉讼法的有关规定，上级工会向人民法院申请支付令或者提起诉讼，要求企业、事业单位拨缴工会经费的，人民法院应当受理。基层工会要求参加诉讼的，人民法院可以准许其作为共同申请人或者共同原告参加诉讼。 第六条　根据工会法第五十二条规定，人民法院审理涉及职工和工会工作人员因参加工会活动或者履行工会法规定的职责而被解除劳动合同的劳动争议案件，可以根据当事人的请求裁判用人单位恢复其工作，并补发被解除劳动合同期间应得的报酬；或者根据当事人的请求裁判用人单位给予本人年收入2倍的赔偿，并参照《违反和解除劳动合同的经济补偿办法》第八条规定给予解除劳动合同时的经济补偿金。 第七条　对于企业、事业单位无正当理由拖延或者拒不拨缴工会经费的，工会组织向人民法院请求保护其权利的诉讼时效期间，适用民法通则第一百三十五条的规定。 第八条　工会组织就工会经费的拨缴向人民法院申请支付令的，应当按照《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉若干问题的意见》第一百三十二条的规定交纳申请费；督促程序终结后，工会组织另行起诉的，按照《人民法院诉讼收费办法》规定的财产案件收费标准交纳诉讼费用。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审理涉及工会组织的有关案件时，应当认定依照工会法建立的工会组织的社团法人资格。具有法人资格的工会组织依法独立享有民事权利，承担民事义务。建立工会的企业、事业单位、机关与所建工会以及工会投资兴办的企业，根据法律和司法解释的规定，应当分别承担各自的民事责任。

**Article 1** When a People's Court tries a case involving a trade union organization, it shall recognize the status as a juristic person of a social organization of the trade union organization established in accordance with the Trade Union Law. A trade union organization with the status as a juristic person shall independently enjoy civil rights and assume civil obligations according to law. The enterprise, public institution, or government body that establishes a trade union, the trade union so established, and the enterprise invested and established by the trade union shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and judicial interpretations, respectively assume their own civil liabilities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　根据[工会法第十八条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国工会法.md#art-18)规定，人民法院审理劳动争议案件，涉及确定基层工会专职主席、副主席或者委员延长的劳动合同期限的，应当自上述人员工会职务任职期限届满之日起计算，延长的期限等于其工会职务任职的期间。  
[工会法第十八条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国工会法.md#art-18)规定的“个人严重过失”，是指具有[《中华人民共和国劳动法》第二十五条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动法.md#art-25)第（二）项、第（三）项或者第（四）项规定的情形。

**Article 2** According to the provisions of Article 18 of the Trade Union Law, when a People's Court tries a labor dispute case involving the determination of the extended period of a labor contract of a full-time chairman, vice-chairman, or member of a primary trade union, the extension shall be calculated from the date on which the term of office of the aforesaid person in the trade union expires, and the extended period shall be equal to the term of office in the trade union.  
"Personal gross negligence" as provided in Article 18 of the Trade Union Law refers to the circumstances specified in subparagraphs (2), (3), or (4) of Article 25 of the Labor Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　基层工会或者上级工会依照[工会法第四十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国工会法.md#art-43)规定向人民法院申请支付令的，由被申请人所在地的基层人民法院管辖。

**Article 3** Where a primary trade union or a higher-level trade union applies to a People's Court for an order of payment in accordance with Article 43 of the Trade Union Law, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the Primary People's Court of the place where the respondent is located.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院根据[工会法第四十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国工会法.md#art-43)的规定受理工会提出的拨缴工会经费的支付令申请后，应当先行征询被申请人的意见。被申请人仅对应拨缴经费数额有异议的，人民法院应当就无异议部分的工会经费数额发出支付令。  
人民法院在审理涉及工会经费的案件中，需要按照[工会法第四十二条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国工会法.md#art-42)第一款第（二）项规定的“全部职工”、“工资总额”确定拨缴数额的，“全部职工”、“工资总额”的计算，应当按照国家有关部门规定的标准执行。

**Article 4** After a People's Court accepts an application for an order of payment submitted by a trade union for the allocation of trade union funds in accordance with Article 43 of the Trade Union Law, it shall first consult the respondent. Where the respondent only disputes the amount of funds to be allocated, the People's Court shall issue an order of payment for the amount of trade union funds that is not in dispute.  
When a People's Court, in trying a case involving trade union funds, needs to determine the amount to be allocated in accordance with the terms "all employees" and "total wages" as specified in subparagraph (2) of paragraph 1 of Article 42 of the Trade Union Law, the calculation of "all employees" and "total wages" shall be carried out in accordance with the standards prescribed by the relevant state authorities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　根据[工会法第四十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国工会法.md#art-43)和民事诉讼法的有关规定，上级工会向人民法院申请支付令或者提起诉讼，要求企业、事业单位拨缴工会经费的，人民法院应当受理。基层工会要求参加诉讼的，人民法院可以准许其作为共同申请人或者共同原告参加诉讼。

**Article 5** According to Article 43 of the Trade Union Law and the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law, where a higher-level trade union applies to a People's Court for an order of payment or files a lawsuit to demand that an enterprise or public institution allocate trade union funds, the People's Court shall accept the case. Where a primary trade union requests to participate in the litigation, the People's Court may permit it to participate as a co-applicant or co-plaintiff.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　根据[工会法第五十二条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国工会法.md#art-52)规定，人民法院审理涉及职工和工会工作人员因参加工会活动或者履行工会法规定的职责而被解除劳动合同的劳动争议案件，可以根据当事人的请求裁判用人单位恢复其工作，并补发被解除劳动合同期间应得的报酬；或者根据当事人的请求裁判用人单位给予本人年收入2倍的赔偿，并参照《违反和解除劳动合同的经济补偿办法》第八条规定给予解除劳动合同时的经济补偿金。

**Article 6** According to Article 52 of the Trade Union Law, when a People's Court tries a labor dispute case involving an employee or a trade union staff member whose labor contract has been terminated for participating in trade union activities or performing duties as prescribed by the Trade Union Law, it may, upon the request of the party, adjudge the employer to reinstate the person's position and make up the remuneration that should have been received during the period of termination; or, upon the request of the party, adjudge the employer to pay compensation equal to two times the person's annual income, and grant economic compensation for the termination of the labor contract with reference to the provisions of Article 8 of the Measures for Economic Compensation for Violation of and Termination of Labor Contracts.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对于企业、事业单位无正当理由拖延或者拒不拨缴工会经费的，工会组织向人民法院请求保护其权利的诉讼时效期间，适用民法通则第一百三十五条的规定。

**Article 7** For an enterprise or public institution that delays or refuses to allocate trade union funds without justifiable reasons, the limitation of action period for the trade union organization to request the People's Court to protect its rights shall be subject to the provisions of Article 135 of the General Principles of the Civil Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　工会组织就工会经费的拨缴向人民法院申请支付令的，应当按照《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉若干问题的意见》第一百三十二条的规定交纳申请费；督促程序终结后，工会组织另行起诉的，按照《人民法院诉讼收费办法》规定的财产案件收费标准交纳诉讼费用。

**Article 8** Where a trade union organization applies to a People's Court for an order of payment regarding the allocation of trade union funds, it shall pay the application fee in accordance with the provisions of Article 132 of the Opinions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China; after the conclusion of the supervision procedure, if the trade union organization files a separate lawsuit, it shall pay the litigation costs in accordance with the charging standards for property cases as prescribed in the Measures for Litigation Fees of the People's Courts.
