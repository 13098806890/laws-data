# 最高人民法院关于军事法院管辖民事案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2025〕6号  
**公布日期**：2025-03-01  
**生效日期**：2025-05-01

> 《最高人民法院关于军事法院管辖民事案件若干问题的规定》已于2025年3月1日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1943次会议通过，现予公布，自2025年5月1日起施行。 2025年4月25日 最高人民法院 关于军事法院管辖民事案件若干问题的规定 法释〔2025〕6号 根据《中华人民共和国人民法院组织法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律规定，结合审判实践，就军事法院管辖民事案件有关问题，制定本规定。 第一条　下列民事案件，由军事法院管辖： （一）双方当事人均为军人或者军队单位的案件； （二）认定案件基本事实的主要证据涉及军事秘密的案件； （三）侵权行为发生在营区内的侵权责任纠纷，且当事人一方为军人或者军队单位的案件； （四）军队聘用制文职人员与军队单位发生解除或者终止聘用合同争议，不服劳动人事争议仲裁裁决，依法提起诉讼的案件； （五）申请宣告军人失踪或者死亡的案件； （六）申请认定军人无民事行为能力或者限制民事行为能力以及相应的指定监护人的案件； （七）军队设立选举委员会的选民资格案件； （八）认定营区内无主财产案件。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　下列民事案件，由军事法院管辖：  
（一）双方当事人均为军人或者军队单位的案件；  
（二）认定案件基本事实的主要证据涉及军事秘密的案件；  
（三）侵权行为发生在营区内的侵权责任纠纷，且当事人一方为军人或者军队单位的案件；  
（四）军队聘用制文职人员与军队单位发生解除或者终止聘用合同争议，不服劳动人事争议仲裁裁决，依法提起诉讼的案件；  
（五）申请宣告军人失踪或者死亡的案件；  
（六）申请认定军人无民事行为能力或者限制民事行为能力以及相应的指定监护人的案件；  
（七）军队设立选举委员会的选民资格案件；  
（八）认定营区内无主财产案件。  
军事法院依照本条第一款第一项受理民事案件后，根据当事人申请或者依职权追加地方当事人参加诉讼的，由军事法院继续审理。

**Article 1** The following civil actions shall be subject to the jurisdiction of military courts:  
(1) Cases where both parties are servicemen or military units;  
(2) Cases where the principal evidence determining the basic facts of the case involves military secrets;  
(3) Cases of tort liability disputes where the infringing act occurs within a military camp area, and one party is a serviceman or a military unit;  
(4) Cases where a military-employed civilian staff member disputes with a military unit over the rescission or termination of an employment contract, refuses to accept the arbitration award on labor and personnel disputes, and initiates a prosecution according to law;  
(5) Cases applying for declaration of a serviceman as missing or deceased;  
(6) Cases applying for determination of a serviceman as a person without or with limited capacity for civil conduct and for the corresponding designation of a guardian;  
(7) Cases concerning the voter qualification of an election committee established by the military;  
(8) Cases determining unowned property within a military camp area.  
Where, after accepting a civil action in accordance with subparagraph (1) of the first paragraph of this Article, a military court adds a local party to participate in the proceedings upon the party's application or ex officio, the military court shall continue to hear the case.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　下列民事案件，有关军事法院与地方人民法院都有权管辖，地方当事人向军事法院提起诉讼的，军事法院应当受理：  
（一）军人或者军队单位执行职务过程中造成他人损害的侵权责任纠纷案件；  
（二）当事人一方为军人的婚姻家庭纠纷案件；  
（三）[民事诉讼法第三十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-34)规定的不动产所在地、港口所在地、被继承人死亡时住所地或者主要遗产所在地在营区内，且当事人一方为军人或者军队单位的案件；  
（四）地方当事人与军队医疗机构之间的医疗损害责任纠纷案件。

**Article 2** For the following civil actions, both the relevant military court and the local People's Court shall have jurisdiction. Where a local party initiates a prosecution in a military court, the military court shall accept the case:  
(1) Cases of tort liability disputes where a serviceman or a military unit causes damage to another person in the performance of official duties;  
(2) Cases of marriage and family disputes where one party is a serviceman;  
(3) Cases where the location of the immovable property, the port location, the domicile of the decedent at the time of death, or the location of the principal estate as specified in Article 34 of the Civil Procedure Law is within a military camp area, and one party is a serviceman or a military unit;  
(4) Cases of medical damage liability disputes between a local party and a military medical institution.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　当事人一方是军人或者军队单位，且合同履行地或者标的物所在地在营区内的合同或者其他财产权益纠纷，当事人书面约定军事法院管辖，不违反法律关于级别管辖、专属管辖和专门管辖规定的，应当由军事法院管辖。

**Article 3** For a contract or other property rights dispute where one party is a serviceman or a military unit and the place of performance of the contract or the location of the subject matter is within a military camp area, if the parties agree in writing to the jurisdiction of a military court, and such agreement does not violate the provisions of the law on grade jurisdiction, exclusive jurisdiction, and special jurisdiction, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the military court.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　军事法院受理第一审民事案件，应当参照民事诉讼法关于级别管辖、地域管辖的规定确定。  
当事人住所地省级行政区划内没有可以受理案件的第一审军事法院，或者处于交通十分不便的边远地区，双方当事人同意由地方人民法院管辖的，地方人民法院可以管辖，但[本规定第一条](#art-1)第一款第二项规定的案件除外。

**Article 4** A military court accepting a civil case of first instance shall do so by reference to the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law on grade jurisdiction and territorial jurisdiction.  
If there is no military court of first instance capable of accepting the case within the provincial-level administrative division where the parties' domiciles are located, or if the parties are in a remote area with extremely inconvenient transportation, and both parties agree to submit the case to a local People's Court, the local People's Court may accept the case, except for the cases specified in subparagraph (2) of the first paragraph of Article 1 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　军事法院发现受理的民事案件属于地方人民法院管辖的，应当移送有管辖权的地方人民法院，受移送的地方人民法院应当受理。  
地方人民法院认为受移送的案件不属于本院管辖的，应当报请上级人民法院处理，不得再自行移送。  
地方人民法院发现受理的民事案件属于军事法院管辖的，参照第一款规定办理。军事法院认为受移送的案件不属于本院管辖的，参照第二款规定办理。

**Article 5** If a military court finds that a civil case it has accepted falls under the jurisdiction of a local People's Court, it shall transfer the case to the local People's Court with jurisdiction, and the local People's Court to which the case is transferred shall accept it.  
If a local People's Court considers that a transferred case does not fall under its own jurisdiction, it shall report to its superior People's Court for disposition and shall not make a further transfer on its own.  
If a local People's Court finds that a civil case it has accepted falls under the jurisdiction of a military court, it shall handle the matter by reference to the provisions of the first paragraph. If a military court considers that a transferred case does not fall under its own jurisdiction, it shall handle the matter by reference to the provisions of the second paragraph.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　军事法院与地方人民法院依照[本规定第五条](#art-5)移送管辖之前，可以先行协商。  
军事法院与地方人民法院之间因管辖权发生争议，由争议双方通过会商机制解决；协商不成的，报请各自的上级法院协商解决；仍然协商不成的，报请最高人民法院指定管辖。

**Article 6** Before transferring jurisdiction in accordance with Article 5 of these Provisions, the military court and the local People's Court may first hold consultations.  
Where a dispute over jurisdiction arises between a military court and a local People's Court, it shall be resolved by the disputing parties through a consultation mechanism; if consultation fails, the matter shall be reported to their respective superior courts for consultation and resolution; if consultation still fails, the matter shall be reported to the Supreme People's Court for designation of jurisdiction.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　军事法院受理案件后，当事人对管辖权有异议的，应当在提交答辩状期间提出。军事法院对当事人提出的异议，应当审查。异议成立的，裁定将案件移送有管辖权的军事法院或者地方人民法院；异议不成立的，裁定驳回。

**Article 7** After a military court accepts a case, if a party raises an objection to jurisdiction, it shall raise such objection within the period for submission of a statement of defense. The military court shall examine the objection raised by the party. If the objection is sustained, the court shall rule to transfer the case to a military court or a local People's Court with jurisdiction; if the objection is not sustained, the court shall rule to reject it.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　本规定所称军人是指中国人民解放军的现役军官、军士、义务兵及具有军籍的学员，中国人民武装警察部队的现役警官、警士、义务兵及具有军籍的学员。军队中的文职人员、由军队管理的离退休人员、具有军队编制的职工，参照军人确定管辖。  
军队单位是指中国人民解放军现役部队和预备役部队、中国人民武装警察部队及其编制内的企业事业单位。  
营区是指由军队管理使用的区域，包括军事禁区、军事管理区，以及军队设立的临时驻地等。

**Article 8** For the purposes of these Provisions, "serviceman" means active-duty officers, non-commissioned officers, conscripts, and cadets with military status of the Chinese People's Liberation Army, and active-duty officers, non-commissioned officers, conscripts, and cadets with military status of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force. For civilian staff within the military, retired personnel managed by the military, and employees with military establishment, jurisdiction shall be determined by reference to servicemen.  
"Military unit" means the active-duty forces and reserve forces of the Chinese People's Liberation Army, the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, and their enterprises and institutions within their establishment.  
"Military camp area" means the area managed and used by the military, including military restricted zones, military management zones, and temporary stations established by the military.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　本规定自2025年5月1日起施行。《最高人民法院关于军事法院管辖民事案件若干问题的规定》（2020年修正）同时废止；本规定施行前的司法解释以及司法解释性文件与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 9** These Provisions shall come into force as of May 1, 2025. The Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Jurisdiction of Military Courts over Civil Cases (as amended in 2020) shall be abolished simultaneously; where any judicial interpretation or document of a judicial interpretation nature issued before the coming into force of these Provisions is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
