# 最高人民法院关于审理垄断民事纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2024〕6号  
**公布日期**：2024-02-04  
**生效日期**：2024-07-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理垄断民事纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2024年2月4日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1915次会议通过，现予公布，自2024年7月1日起施行。 2024年6月24日 最高人民法院 关于审理垄断民事纠纷案件适用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2024〕6号 为维护市场公平竞争秩序，依法公正高效审理垄断民事纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》、《中华人民共和国反垄断法》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等有关法律规定，制定本解释。 一、程序规定 第一条　本解释所称垄断民事纠纷案件，是指自然人、法人或者非法人组织因垄断行为受到损失以及因合同内容或者经营者团体的章程、决议、决定等违反反垄断法而发生争议，依据反垄断法向人民法院提起民事诉讼的案件。 本解释所称经营者团体，包括行业协会等由两个以上经营者为了实现共同目的而组成的结合体或者联合体。 第二条　原告依据反垄断法直接向人民法院提起民事诉讼，或者在反垄断执法机构认定构成垄断行为的处理决定作出后向人民法院提起民事诉讼，且符合法律规定的受理条件的，人民法院应予受理。 原告起诉仅请求人民法院确认被告的特定行为构成垄断，而不请求被告承担民事责任的，人民法院不予受理。 第三条　一方当事人向人民法院提起垄断民事诉讼，另一方当事人以双方之间存在合同关系且有仲裁协议为由，主张人民法院不应受理的，人民法院不予支持。 第四条　第一审垄断民事纠纷案件，由知识产权法院和最高人民法院指定的中级人民法院管辖。 第五条　垄断民事纠纷案件的地域管辖，根据案件具体情况，依照民事诉讼法及相关司法解释有关侵权纠纷、合同纠纷等的管辖规定确定。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本解释所称垄断民事纠纷案件，是指自然人、法人或者非法人组织因垄断行为受到损失以及因合同内容或者经营者团体的章程、决议、决定等违反反垄断法而发生争议，依据反垄断法向人民法院提起民事诉讼的案件。  
本解释所称经营者团体，包括行业协会等由两个以上经营者为了实现共同目的而组成的结合体或者联合体。

**Article 1** For the purposes of this Interpretation, "monopoly civil dispute case" refers to a case where a natural person, legal person, or unincorporated organization, having suffered losses due to monopolistic conduct, or having a dispute arising from the content of a contract or the articles of association, resolutions, decisions, etc., of an association of undertakings that violate the Anti-Monopoly Law, institutes a civil action in a People's Court in accordance with the Anti-Monopoly Law.  
For the purposes of this Interpretation, "association of undertakings" includes industry associations and other combinations or unions formed by two or more undertakings for the purpose of achieving a common objective.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　原告依据反垄断法直接向人民法院提起民事诉讼，或者在反垄断执法机构认定构成垄断行为的处理决定作出后向人民法院提起民事诉讼，且符合法律规定的受理条件的，人民法院应予受理。  
原告起诉仅请求人民法院确认被告的特定行为构成垄断，而不请求被告承担民事责任的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 2** Where a plaintiff institutes a civil action directly in a People's Court in accordance with the Anti-Monopoly Law, or institutes a civil action in a People's Court after an anti-monopoly enforcement authority has rendered a decision determining that a monopolistic conduct exists, and the conditions for acceptance prescribed by law are met, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
Where a plaintiff files a lawsuit merely requesting the People's Court to confirm that a specific act of the defendant constitutes a monopoly, without requesting the defendant to bear civil liability, the People's Court shall not accept the case.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　一方当事人向人民法院提起垄断民事诉讼，另一方当事人以双方之间存在合同关系且有仲裁协议为由，主张人民法院不应受理的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 3** Where one party institutes a monopoly civil action in a People's Court, and the other party claims that the People's Court shall not accept the case on the grounds that a contractual relationship exists between the parties and there is an arbitration agreement, the People's Court shall not sustain such claim.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　第一审垄断民事纠纷案件，由知识产权法院和最高人民法院指定的中级人民法院管辖。

**Article 4** First-instance monopoly civil dispute cases shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intellectual Property Courts and the Intermediate People's Courts designated by the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　垄断民事纠纷案件的地域管辖，根据案件具体情况，依照民事诉讼法及相关司法解释有关侵权纠纷、合同纠纷等的管辖规定确定。

**Article 5** The territorial jurisdiction over monopoly civil dispute cases shall be determined in accordance with the provisions on jurisdiction over tort disputes, contract disputes, etc., under the Civil Procedure Law and relevant judicial interpretations, based on the specific circumstances of the case.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　原告依据反垄断法对在中华人民共和国境内没有住所的被告提起民事诉讼，主张被告在中华人民共和国境外的垄断行为对境内市场竞争产生排除、限制影响的，根据[民事诉讼法第二百七十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-276)的规定确定管辖法院。

**Article 6** Where a plaintiff institutes a civil action in accordance with the Anti-Monopoly Law against a defendant who has no domicile within the territory of the People's Republic of China, claiming that the monopolistic conduct of the defendant outside the territory of the People's Republic of China has the effect of eliminating or restricting competition in the domestic market, the competent court shall be determined in accordance with Article 276 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　案件立案时的案由并非垄断民事纠纷，人民法院受理后经审查发现属于垄断民事纠纷，但受诉人民法院并无垄断民事纠纷案件管辖权的，应当将案件移送有管辖权的人民法院。

**Article 7** Where the cause of action at the time of case filing is not a monopoly civil dispute, but upon examination after acceptance, the People's Court finds that the case is a monopoly civil dispute, yet the People's Court accepting the case has no jurisdiction over monopoly civil dispute cases, it shall transfer the case to a People's Court that has jurisdiction.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　两个以上原告因同一垄断行为向有管辖权的同一人民法院分别提起诉讼的，人民法院可以合并审理。  
两个以上原告因同一垄断行为向有管辖权的不同人民法院分别提起诉讼的，后立案的人民法院发现其他有管辖权的人民法院已先立案的，应当裁定将案件移送先立案的人民法院；受移送的人民法院可以合并审理。  
人民法院可以要求当事人提供与被诉垄断行为相关的行政执法、仲裁、诉讼等情况。当事人拒不如实提供的，可以作为认定其是否遵循诚信原则和构成滥用权利等的考量因素。

**Article 8** Where two or more plaintiffs separately institute actions against the same monopolistic conduct in the same People's Court that has jurisdiction, the People's Court may consolidate the trials.  
Where two or more plaintiffs separately institute actions against the same monopolistic conduct in different People's Courts that have jurisdiction, and the People's Court that later files the case discovers that another People's Court with jurisdiction has already filed the case earlier, it shall rule to transfer the case to the People's Court that first filed the case; the receiving People's Court may consolidate the trials.  
The People's Court may require the parties to provide information on administrative law enforcement, arbitration, litigation, etc., related to the alleged monopolistic conduct. Where a party refuses to provide such information truthfully, this may be considered as a factor in determining whether the party has acted in good faith and whether it constitutes an abuse of rights, among other things.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　原告无正当理由而根据影响地域、持续时间、实施场合、损害范围等因素对被告的同一垄断行为予以拆分，分别提起数个诉讼的，由最先受理诉讼的人民法院合并审理。

**Article 9** Where a plaintiff, without justifiable reasons, splits the same monopolistic conduct of the defendant into several parts based on factors such as the affected territory, duration, place of implementation, and scope of harm, and institutes several separate actions, the People's Court that first accepted the action shall consolidate the trials.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　反垄断执法机构认定构成垄断行为的处理决定在法定期限内未被提起行政诉讼或者已为人民法院生效裁判所确认，原告在相关垄断民事纠纷案件中据此主张该处理决定认定的基本事实为真实的，无需再行举证证明，但有相反证据足以推翻的除外。  
必要时，人民法院可以要求作出处理决定的反垄断执法机构对该处理决定的有关情况予以说明。反垄断执法机构提供的信息、材料等尚未公开的，人民法院应当依职权或者依申请采取合理保护措施。

**Article 10** Where an anti-monopoly enforcement authority's decision determining that a monopolistic conduct exists has not been challenged by an administrative action within the statutory time limit or has been confirmed by a legally effective judgment of a People's Court, and the plaintiff, in a relevant monopoly civil dispute case, relies on such decision to claim that the basic facts found by the decision are true, the plaintiff shall not be required to provide further evidence to prove such facts, unless there is contrary evidence sufficient to overturn them.  
Where necessary, the People's Court may request the anti-monopoly enforcement authority that made the decision to provide explanations regarding the relevant circumstances of the decision. Where the information, materials, etc., provided by the anti-monopoly enforcement authority are not yet public, the People's Court shall take reasonable protective measures ex officio or upon application.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　当事人可以向人民法院申请一至二名具有案件所涉领域、经济学等专门知识的人员出庭，就案件的专门性问题进行说明。  
当事人可以向人民法院申请委托专业机构或者专业人员就案件的专门性问题提出市场调查或者经济分析意见。该专业机构或者专业人员可以由双方当事人协商确定；协商不成的，由人民法院指定。人民法院可以参照民事诉讼法及相关司法解释有关鉴定意见的规定，对该专业机构或者专业人员提出的市场调查或者经济分析意见进行审查判断。  
一方当事人就案件的专门性问题自行委托有关专业机构或者专业人员提出市场调查或者经济分析意见，该意见缺乏可靠的事实、数据或者其他必要基础资料佐证，或者缺乏可靠的分析方法，或者另一方当事人提出证据或者理由足以反驳的，人民法院不予采信。

**Article 11** A party may apply to the People's Court for one or two persons with expertise in the field involved in the case, economics, or other specialized knowledge to appear in court to provide explanations on the specialized issues of the case.  
A party may apply to the People's Court to entrust a professional institution or professional to provide a market survey or economic analysis opinion on the specialized issues of the case. Such professional institution or professional may be determined by consultation between the parties; if consultation fails, the People's Court shall designate one. The People's Court may, by reference to the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law and relevant judicial interpretations concerning expert evaluation opinions, review and determine the market survey or economic analysis opinion submitted by such professional institution or professional.  
Where a party, on its own, entrusts a relevant professional institution or professional to provide a market survey or economic analysis opinion on the specialized issues of the case, and such opinion lacks reliable facts, data, or other necessary basic supporting materials, or lacks a reliable analysis method, or the other party provides evidence or reasons sufficient to rebut it, the People's Court shall not adopt it.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　经营者实施垄断行为损害社会公共利益，设区的市级以上人民检察院依法提起民事公益诉讼的，适用与公益诉讼有关的法律和司法解释的规定，但本解释另有规定的除外。

**Article 12** Where an undertaking commits monopolistic conduct that harms the public interest, and a People's Procuratorate at or above the level of a city with districts initiates a civil public interest litigation in accordance with the law, the provisions of laws and judicial interpretations concerning public interest litigation shall apply, unless otherwise provided in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　反垄断执法机构对被诉垄断行为已经立案调查的，人民法院可以根据案件具体情况，裁定中止诉讼。  
二、相关市场界定

**Article 13** Where an anti-monopoly enforcement authority has already initiated an investigation into the alleged monopolistic conduct, the People's Court may, based on the specific circumstances of the case, rule to suspend the proceedings.  
  
II. Definition of Relevant Market

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　原告主张被诉垄断行为违反反垄断法的，一般应当界定[反垄断法第十五条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-15)第二款所称的相关市场并提供证据或者充分说明理由。  
原告以被告在相关市场的市场份额为由主张其具有市场支配地位或者显著的市场力量的，应当界定相关市场并提供证据或者充分说明理由。  
原告提供证据足以直接证明下列情形之一的，可以不再对相关市场界定进一步承担举证责任：  
（一）被诉垄断协议的经营者具有显著的市场力量；  
（二）被诉滥用市场支配地位的经营者具有市场支配地位；  
（三）被诉垄断行为具有排除、限制竞争效果。  
原告主张被诉垄断行为属于[反垄断法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-17)第一项至第五项和第十八条第一款第一项、第二项规定情形的，可以不对相关市场界定提供证据。

**Article 14** Where a plaintiff claims that the alleged monopolistic conduct violates the Anti-Monopoly Law, the plaintiff shall generally define the relevant market as referred to in Article 15, paragraph 2 of the Anti-Monopoly Law and provide evidence or sufficiently explain the reasons therefor.  
Where a plaintiff claims that the defendant has a dominant market position or significant market power based on the defendant's market share in the relevant market, the plaintiff shall define the relevant market and provide evidence or sufficiently explain the reasons therefor.  
Where the plaintiff provides evidence sufficient to directly prove any of the following circumstances, the plaintiff may no longer bear the burden of adducing evidence further on the definition of the relevant market:  
(1) The undertakings involved in the alleged monopoly agreement have significant market power;  
(2) The undertaking alleged to have abused a dominant market position has a dominant market position;  
(3) The alleged monopolistic conduct has the effect of eliminating or restricting competition.  
Where the plaintiff claims that the alleged monopolistic conduct falls within the circumstances specified in Items 1 to 5 of Article 17 and Items 1 and 2 of paragraph 1 of Article 18 of the Anti-Monopoly Law, the plaintiff may not provide evidence on the definition of the relevant market.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民法院界定经营者在一定时期内就特定商品或者服务（以下统称商品）进行竞争的相关商品市场和相关地域市场，可以根据案件具体情况，以被诉垄断行为直接涉及的特定商品为基础，从需求者角度进行需求替代分析；供给替代对经营者行为产生的竞争约束类似于需求替代的，还可以从供给者角度进行供给替代分析。  
人民法院进行需求替代或者供给替代分析时，可以采用假定垄断者测试的分析方法，一般选择使用价格上涨的假定垄断者测试方法；经营者之间的竞争主要表现为质量、多样性、创新等非价格竞争的，可以选择质量下降、成本上升等假定垄断者测试方法。

**Article 15** When defining the relevant product market and the relevant geographic market in which undertakings compete for specific goods or services (hereinafter collectively referred to as "goods") within a certain period, the People's Court may, based on the specific circumstances of the case and taking the specific goods directly involved in the alleged monopolistic conduct as the basis, conduct a demand substitution analysis from the perspective of demanders; if the competitive constraints imposed on the conduct of undertakings by supply substitution are similar to those of demand substitution, the People's Court may also conduct a supply substitution analysis from the perspective of suppliers.  
When conducting a demand substitution or supply substitution analysis, the People's Court may adopt the hypothetical monopolist test method, and generally choose the hypothetical monopolist test method using a price increase; if the competition among undertakings is mainly in non-price aspects such as quality, diversity, or innovation, the People's Court may choose a hypothetical monopolist test method using a quality decline or cost increase.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院从需求替代的角度分析界定相关商品市场时，一般根据需求者对于商品特性、功能和用途的需求、质量的认可、价格的接受以及获取的难易程度等因素，确定由需求者认为具有较为紧密替代关系的一组或者一类商品所构成的市场为相关商品市场。从供给替代的角度分析界定相关商品市场时，可以综合考虑其他经营者进入市场的意图和能力、承担的成本与风险、克服的市场障碍、需要的时间等因素。  
分析界定互联网平台（以下称平台）所涉相关商品市场时，结合被诉垄断行为的特点、产生或者可能产生排除、限制竞争效果的具体情况、平台的类型等因素，一般可以根据该平台与被诉垄断行为最相关一边的商品界定相关商品市场，也可以根据被诉垄断行为所涉及的多边商品分别界定多个相关商品市场，必要时也可以根据特定平台整体界定相关商品市场。特定平台存在跨边网络效应，并给该平台经营者施加了足够的竞争约束的，可以根据该平台整体界定相关商品市场，也可以根据跨边网络效应所涉及的多边商品分别界定多个相关商品市场，并考虑各个相关商品市场之间的相互关系和影响。

**Article 16** When analyzing and defining the relevant product market from the perspective of demand substitution, the People's Court shall generally determine the market composed of a group or category of goods that demanders consider to have relatively close substitution relationships as the relevant product market, based on factors such as demanders' needs for the characteristics, functions, and purposes of the goods, acceptance of quality, price acceptance, and ease of access. When analyzing and defining the relevant product market from the perspective of supply substitution, the People's Court may comprehensively consider factors such as the intention and ability of other undertakings to enter the market, the costs and risks they bear, market barriers to be overcome, and the time required.  
When analyzing and defining the relevant product market involving an internet platform (hereinafter referred to as "platform"), taking into account the characteristics of the alleged monopolistic conduct, the specific circumstances of the actual or potential effect of eliminating or restricting competition, the type of platform, and other factors, the People's Court may generally define the relevant product market based on the goods on the side of the platform most relevant to the alleged monopolistic conduct, or may define multiple relevant product markets separately based on the multi-sided goods involved in the alleged monopolistic conduct, and where necessary, may also define the relevant product market based on the specific platform as a whole. If a specific platform has cross-side network effects and imposes sufficient competitive constraints on the platform operator, the relevant product market may be defined based on the platform as a whole, or multiple relevant product markets may be defined separately based on the multi-sided goods involved in the cross-side network effects, taking into account the mutual relationships and influences among the relevant product markets.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人民法院从需求替代的角度分析界定相关地域市场时，可以综合考虑需求者因商品价格或者其他竞争因素的变化而转向其他地域购买商品的情况、商品的运输成本和运输特征、多数需求者选择商品的实际区域和主要经营者的商品销售分布、地域间的市场障碍、特定区域需求者偏好等因素。从供给替代的角度分析界定相关地域市场时，可以综合考虑其他地域的经营者对商品价格等竞争因素的变化作出的反应、其他地域的经营者供应或者销售相关商品的及时性和可行性等因素。  
分析界定平台所涉相关地域市场，可以重点考虑多数需求者选择商品的实际区域、需求者的语言偏好和消费习惯、相关法律法规的要求、其他地域竞争者的现状及其进入相关地域市场的及时性等因素。  
三、垄断协议

**Article 17** When analyzing and defining the relevant geographic market from the perspective of demand substitution, the People's Court may comprehensively consider factors such as the circumstances under which demanders switch to purchasing goods in other geographic areas due to changes in commodity prices or other competitive factors, transportation costs and characteristics of the goods, the actual area where most demanders choose goods and the distribution of sales of major undertakings, market barriers between geographic areas, and preferences of demanders in specific areas. When analyzing and defining the relevant geographic market from the perspective of supply substitution, the People's Court may comprehensively consider factors such as the response of undertakings in other geographic areas to changes in commodity prices or other competitive factors, and the timeliness and feasibility of supply or sale of relevant goods by undertakings in other geographic areas.  
When analyzing and defining the relevant geographic market involving a platform, the People's Court may focus on factors such as the actual area where most demanders choose the goods, the language preferences and consumption habits of demanders, requirements of relevant laws and regulations, the current status of competitors in other geographic areas and the timeliness of their entry into the relevant geographic market.  
  
III. Monopoly Agreements

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法院认定[反垄断法第十六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-16)规定的其他协同行为，应当综合考虑下列因素：  
（一）经营者的市场行为是否具有一致性；  
（二）经营者之间是否进行过意思联络、信息交流或者传递；  
（三）相关市场的市场结构、竞争状况、市场变化等情况；  
（四）经营者能否对行为一致性作出合理解释。  
原告提供前款第一项和第二项的初步证据，或者第一项和第三项的初步证据，能够证明经营者存在协同行为的可能性较大的，被告应当提供证据或者进行充分说明，对其行为一致性作出合理解释；不能作出合理解释的，人民法院可以认定协同行为成立。  
本条所称合理解释，包括经营者系基于市场和竞争状况变化等而独立实施相关行为。

**Article 18** When determining other concerted practices as provided for in Article 16 of the Anti-Monopoly Law, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider the following factors:  
(1) Whether the market conduct of the undertakings is consistent;  
(2) Whether there has been any communication of intention, exchange or transmission of information among the undertakings;  
(3) The market structure, competition situation, market changes, etc., of the relevant market;  
(4) Whether an undertaking can provide a reasonable explanation for the consistency of conduct.  
Where the plaintiff provides preliminary evidence of items (1) and (2) of the preceding paragraph, or of items (1) and (3), which is sufficient to prove that it is highly probable that the undertakings engaged in concerted practices, the defendant shall provide evidence or sufficiently explain to provide a reasonable explanation for the consistency of its conduct; if it cannot provide a reasonable explanation, the People's Court may determine that the concerted practice is established.  
The "reasonable explanation" referred to in this Article includes the independent implementation of the relevant conduct by the undertaking based on changes in market and competitive conditions, among other things.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　[反垄断法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-17)规定的具有竞争关系的经营者，是指在商品生产经营过程中处于同一阶段、提供具有较为紧密替代关系的商品、独立经营决策并承担法律责任的两个以上的实际经营者或者可能进入同一相关市场进行竞争的潜在经营者。  
特定经营者取得对其他经营者的控制权或者能够对其他经营者施加决定性影响，或者两个以上经营者被同一第三方控制或者施加决定性影响，应当视为一个经济实体的，不构成前款所称具有竞争关系的经营者。

**Article 19** "Undertakings in a competitive relationship" as provided for in Article 17 of the Anti-Monopoly Law refers to two or more actual undertakings or potential undertakings that may enter the same relevant market to compete, which are at the same stage of production or operation of goods, provide goods with relatively close substitution relationships, and independently make business decisions and bear legal liability.  
Where a specific undertaking acquires control over other undertakings or is able to exercise decisive influence over other undertakings, or where two or more undertakings are controlled or subject to decisive influence by the same third party, and such undertakings shall be regarded as a single economic entity, they shall not constitute "undertakings in a competitive relationship" as referred to in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　原告有证据证明仿制药申请人与被仿制药专利权利人达成、实施的协议同时具备下列条件，主张该协议构成[反垄断法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-17)规定的垄断协议的，人民法院可予支持：  
（一）被仿制药专利权利人给予或者承诺给予仿制药申请人明显不合理的金钱或者其他形式的利益补偿；  
（二）仿制药申请人承诺不质疑被仿制药专利权的有效性或者延迟进入被仿制药相关市场。  
被告有证据证明前款所称的利益补偿仅系为弥补被仿制药专利相关纠纷解决成本或者具有其他正当理由，或者该协议符合[反垄断法第二十条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-20)规定，主张其不构成[反垄断法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-17)规定的垄断协议的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 20** Where a plaintiff has evidence proving that an agreement reached and implemented between a generic drug applicant and a patentee of the branded drug concurrently meets the following conditions, and the plaintiff claims that such agreement constitutes a monopoly agreement under Article 17 of the Anti-Monopoly Law, the People's Court may sustain the claim:  
(1) The patentee of the branded drug provides or promises to provide the generic drug applicant with obviously unreasonable monetary or other forms of compensation;  
(2) The generic drug applicant promises not to challenge the validity of the patent right of the branded drug or to delay entering the relevant market for the branded drug.  
Where the defendant has evidence proving that the compensation referred to in the preceding paragraph is solely to cover the costs of resolving the dispute related to the patent of the branded drug or has other justifiable reasons, or that the agreement complies with Article 20 of the Anti-Monopoly Law, and the defendant claims that it does not constitute a monopoly agreement under Article 17 of the Anti-Monopoly Law, the People's Court shall sustain the claim.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　被诉垄断行为属于[反垄断法第十八条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-18)第一款第一项、第二项规定的垄断协议的，应当由被告对该协议不具有排除、限制竞争效果承担举证责任。

**Article 21** Where the alleged monopolistic conduct constitutes a monopoly agreement under Items 1 and 2 of paragraph 1 of Article 18 of the Anti-Monopoly Law, the defendant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence to prove that the agreement does not have the effect of eliminating or restricting competition.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　人民法院依照[反垄断法第十八条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-18)第一款和第二款的规定审查认定被诉垄断协议是否具有排除、限制竞争效果时，可以综合考虑下列因素：  
（一）被告在相关市场的市场力量和协议对相关市场类似不利竞争效果的累积作用；  
（二）协议是否具有提高市场进入壁垒、阻碍更有效率的经营者或者经营模式、限制品牌间或者品牌内竞争等不利竞争效果；  
（三）协议是否具有防止搭便车、促进品牌间竞争、维护品牌形象、提升售前或者售后服务水平、促进创新等有利竞争效果，且为实现该效果所必需；  
（四）其他可以考虑的因素。  
在案证据足以证明的有利竞争效果明显超过不利竞争效果的，人民法院应当认定协议不具有排除、限制竞争效果。

**Article 22** When reviewing and determining whether an alleged monopoly agreement has the effect of eliminating or restricting competition in accordance with paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 of Article 18 of the Anti-Monopoly Law, the People's Court may comprehensively consider the following factors:  
(1) The market power of the defendant in the relevant market and the cumulative effect of the agreement on similar adverse competitive effects in the relevant market;  
(2) Whether the agreement has adverse competitive effects such as raising market entry barriers, hindering more efficient undertakings or business models, or restricting inter-brand or intra-brand competition;  
(3) Whether the agreement has pro-competitive effects such as preventing free-riding, promoting inter-brand competition, maintaining brand image, improving pre-sale or after-sale service levels, or promoting innovation, and whether it is necessary to achieve such effects;  
(4) Other factors that may be considered.  
Where the pro-competitive effects sufficiently proven by the evidence on record clearly outweigh the adverse competitive effects, the People's Court shall determine that the agreement does not have the effect of eliminating or restricting competition.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　被诉垄断协议具有下列情形之一，原告依据[反垄断法第十八条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-18)第一款的规定主张被告应当承担法律责任的，人民法院不予支持：  
（一）协议属于经营者与相对人之间的代理协议，且代理商不承担任何实质性商业或者经营风险；  
（二）被告在相关市场的市场份额低于国务院反垄断执法机构规定的标准并符合国务院反垄断执法机构规定的其他条件。

**Article 23** Where an alleged monopoly agreement falls under any of the following circumstances, and the plaintiff claims that the defendant shall bear legal liability pursuant to Article 18, paragraph 1 of the Anti-Monopoly Law, the People's Court shall not sustain the claim:  
(1) The agreement is an agency agreement between an undertaking and its counterparty, and the agent does not bear any substantial commercial or operational risk;  
(2) The defendant's market share in the relevant market is below the threshold prescribed by the Anti-Monopoly Law enforcement authority of the State Council and meets other conditions prescribed by the Anti-Monopoly Law enforcement authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　经营者利用数据、算法、技术等手段进行意思联络、信息交流或者传递，或者利用数据、算法、技术、平台规则等手段实现行为一致性，达成、实施被诉垄断协议的，人民法院可以依照[反垄断法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-17)的规定审查认定。  
经营者利用数据、算法、技术、平台规则等手段实现限定或者自动化设定转售商品价格等，达成、实施被诉垄断协议的，人民法院可以依照[反垄断法第十八条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-18)的规定审查认定。

**Article 24** Where an undertaking uses means such as data, algorithms, or technologies to communicate intentions, exchange or transmit information, or uses means such as data, algorithms, technologies, or platform rules to achieve consistency of conduct, thereby reaching or implementing an alleged monopoly agreement, the People's Court may review and determine the matter in accordance with Article 17 of the Anti-Monopoly Law.  
Where an undertaking uses means such as data, algorithms, technologies, or platform rules to achieve restrictions or automatic setting of resale prices for goods, thereby reaching or implementing an alleged monopoly agreement, the People's Court may review and determine the matter in accordance with Article 18 of the Anti-Monopoly Law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　平台经营者与平台内经营者的协议要求平台内经营者在该平台上提供与其他交易渠道相同或者更优惠交易条件的，根据原告的诉讼请求和具体案情，人民法院可以区别下列情形作出处理：  
（一）平台经营者与平台内经营者之间具有竞争关系的，依照[反垄断法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-17)的规定审查认定；  
（二）平台经营者与平台内经营者之间不具有竞争关系的，依照[反垄断法第十八条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-18)或者第十九条的规定审查认定；  
（三）原告主张平台经营者滥用市场支配地位的，依照[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)、[电子商务法第二十二条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国电子商务法.md#art-22)的规定审查认定；  
（四）原告主张平台经营者违反[电子商务法第三十五条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国电子商务法.md#art-35)的规定的，依照该条规定处理。

**Article 25** Where an agreement between a platform operator and a business operator on the platform requires the business operator on the platform to provide the same or more favorable transaction conditions on that platform as compared to other transaction channels, the People's Court may, based on the plaintiff's claims and the specific facts of the case, handle the matter by distinguishing the following circumstances:  
(1) If the platform operator and the business operator on the platform are in a competitive relationship, the matter shall be reviewed and determined in accordance with Article 17 of the Anti-Monopoly Law;  
(2) If the platform operator and the business operator on the platform are not in a competitive relationship, the matter shall be reviewed and determined in accordance with Article 18 or Article 19 of the Anti-Monopoly Law;  
(3) If the plaintiff claims that the platform operator has abused a dominant market position, the matter shall be reviewed and determined in accordance with Article 22 of the Anti-Monopoly Law and Article 22 of the E-Commerce Law;  
(4) If the plaintiff claims that the platform operator has violated Article 35 of the E-Commerce Law, the matter shall be handled in accordance with that Article.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　经营者、经营者团体等组织其他经营者达成、实施垄断协议，给原告造成损失，原告依据[民法典第一千一百六十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1168)的规定主张实施组织行为的经营者、经营者团体等与达成、实施垄断协议的其他经营者承担连带责任的，人民法院应当予以支持。  
经营者、经营者团体等为其他经营者达成、实施垄断协议提供实质性帮助，给原告造成损失，原告依据[民法典第一千一百六十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1169)第一款的规定主张提供帮助行为的经营者、经营者团体等与达成、实施垄断协议的其他经营者承担连带责任的，人民法院应当予以支持。但是，经营者、经营者团体等能够证明其不知道且不应当知道其他经营者达成、实施有关协议的除外。  
前款所称实质性帮助，是指对垄断协议达成或者实施具有直接、重要促进作用的引导产生违法意图、提供便利条件、充当信息渠道、帮助实施惩罚等行为。

**Article 26** Where an undertaking, an association of undertakings, or the like organizes other undertakings to reach or implement a monopoly agreement, causing losses to the plaintiff, and the plaintiff claims that the undertaking, association of undertakings, or the like that engaged in the organizing conduct shall bear joint and several liability with the other undertakings that reached or implemented the monopoly agreement in accordance with Article 1168 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court shall sustain the claim.  
Where an undertaking, an association of undertakings, or the like provides substantial assistance to other undertakings in reaching or implementing a monopoly agreement, causing losses to the plaintiff, and the plaintiff claims that the undertaking, association of undertakings, or the like that provided the assistance shall bear joint and several liability with the other undertakings that reached or implemented the monopoly agreement in accordance with Article 1169, paragraph 1 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court shall sustain the claim. However, this shall not apply if the undertaking, association of undertakings, or the like can prove that it neither knew nor should have known that the other undertakings were reaching or implementing the relevant agreement.  
"Substantial assistance" as referred to in the preceding paragraph means conduct that has a direct and important promotional effect on the reaching or implementation of a monopoly agreement, such as inducing the intent to violate the law, providing convenient conditions, acting as an information channel, or assisting in the implementation of sanctions.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　被告依据[反垄断法第二十条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-20)第一款第一项至第五项的规定提出抗辩的，应当提供证据证明如下事实：  
（一）被诉垄断协议能够实现相关目的或者效果；  
（二）被诉垄断协议为实现相关目的或者效果所必需；  
（三）被诉垄断协议不会严重限制相关市场的竞争；  
（四）消费者能够分享由此产生的利益。  
四、滥用市场支配地位

**Article 27** Where the defendant raises a defense in accordance with Items 1 to 5 of paragraph 1 of Article 20 of the Anti-Monopoly Law, the defendant shall provide evidence to prove the following facts:  
(1) The alleged monopoly agreement can achieve the relevant objectives or effects;  
(2) The alleged monopoly agreement is necessary to achieve the relevant objectives or effects;  
(3) The alleged monopoly agreement will not seriously restrict competition in the relevant market;  
(4) Consumers can share in the benefits generated thereby.  
  
IV. Abuse of Dominant Market Position

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　原告主张被诉垄断行为属于[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)第一款规定的滥用市场支配地位的，应当对被告在相关市场内具有支配地位和被告滥用市场支配地位承担举证责任。被告以其行为具有正当性为由抗辩的，应当承担举证责任。

**Article 28** Where the plaintiff claims that the alleged monopolistic conduct constitutes an abuse of a dominant market position under Article 22, paragraph 1 of the Anti-Monopoly Law, the plaintiff shall bear the burden of adducing evidence to prove that the defendant has a dominant position in the relevant market and that the defendant has abused its dominant market position. If the defendant defends on the ground that its conduct is justifiable, the defendant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　原告有证据证明经营者具有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以根据具体案件中相关市场的结构和实际竞争状况，结合相关市场经济规律等经济学知识，初步认定经营者在相关市场具有支配地位，但有相反证据足以反驳的除外：  
（一）经营者在较长时间内维持明显高于市场竞争水平的价格，或者在较长时间内商品质量明显下降却未见大量用户流失，且相关市场明显缺乏竞争、创新和新进入者；  
（二）经营者在较长时间内维持明显超过其他经营者的较高市场份额，且相关市场明显缺乏竞争、创新和新进入者。  
被告对外发布的信息可以作为原告证明被告具有市场支配地位的初步证据，但有相反证据足以反驳的除外。

**Article 29** Where the plaintiff has evidence proving that the undertaking falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may, based on the structure of the relevant market and the actual competitive conditions in the specific case, and in light of economic knowledge such as relevant market economic laws, preliminarily determine that the undertaking has a dominant position in the relevant market, unless there is contrary evidence sufficient to rebut it:  
(1) The undertaking maintains prices significantly higher than the competitive market level for a relatively long period, or the quality of goods significantly declines over a relatively long period without a significant loss of users, and the relevant market clearly lacks competition, innovation, and new entrants;  
(2) The undertaking maintains a relatively high market share significantly exceeding that of other undertakings for a relatively long period, and the relevant market clearly lacks competition, innovation, and new entrants.  
Information publicly released by the defendant may serve as preliminary evidence for the plaintiff to prove that the defendant has a dominant market position, unless there is contrary evidence sufficient to rebut it.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　[反垄断法第二十三条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-23)和第二十四条所称的“经营者在相关市场的市场份额”，可以根据被诉垄断行为发生时经营者一定时期内的相关商品交易金额、交易数量、生产能力或者其他指标在相关市场所占的比例确定。  
人民法院认定平台经营者在相关市场的市场份额时，可以采用能够反映相关市场实际竞争状况的交易金额、活跃用户数量、企业用户数量、用户使用时长、访问量、点击量、数据资产数量或者其他指标作为计算基准。

**Article 30** The "market share of an undertaking in the relevant market" as referred to in Articles 23 and 24 of the Anti-Monopoly Law may be determined based on the proportion of the undertaking's transaction amount, transaction volume, production capacity, or other indicators of relevant goods within a certain period at the time when the alleged monopolistic conduct occurred in the relevant market.  
When determining the market share of a platform operator in the relevant market, the People's Court may use as a calculation benchmark the transaction amount, number of active users, number of corporate users, user usage time, visits, clicks, amount of data assets, or other indicators that can reflect the actual competitive conditions in the relevant market.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　原告主张公用企业或者其他依法具有独占地位的经营者滥用市场支配地位的，人民法院可以根据市场结构和竞争状况的具体情况，认定被告在相关市场具有支配地位，但有相反证据足以反驳的除外。

**Article 31** Where a plaintiff claims that a public utility enterprise or other undertaking that has an exclusive position in accordance with the law has abused its dominant market position, the People's Court may, based on the specific circumstances of the market structure and competitive conditions, determine that the defendant has a dominant position in the relevant market, unless there is contrary evidence sufficient to rebut it.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　人民法院依照[反垄断法第二十三条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-23)的规定认定平台经营者的市场支配地位，可以重点考虑下列因素：  
（一）平台的商业模式及平台经营者实际受到的竞争约束；  
（二）平台经营者在相关市场的市场份额及该市场份额的持续时间；  
（三）平台经营是否存在显著的网络效应、规模效应、范围效应等；  
（四）平台经营者掌握的相关数据、算法、技术等情况；  
（五）平台经营者对相邻市场的影响；  
（六）用户或者平台内经营者对平台经营者的依赖程度及制衡能力、锁定效应、使用习惯、同时使用多个平台的情况、转向其他平台经营者的成本等；  
（七）其他经营者进入相关市场的意愿、能力及所面临的规模要求、技术要求、政策法律限制等市场进入障碍；  
（八）相关市场的创新和技术变化情况；  
（九）其他需要考虑的与平台经营相关的因素。

**Article 32** When a People's Court determines the market dominant position of a platform operator in accordance with Article 23 of the Anti-Monopoly Law, it may focus on the following factors:  
(1) The business model of the platform and the competitive constraints actually faced by the platform operator;  
(2) The market share of the platform operator in the relevant market and the duration of such market share;  
(3) Whether the platform operation has significant network effects, economies of scale, economies of scope, etc.;  
(4) The relevant data, algorithms, technologies, etc., controlled by the platform operator;  
(5) The impact of the platform operator on adjacent markets;  
(6) The degree of dependence of users or operators within the platform on the platform operator and their ability to counterbalance, lock-in effects, usage habits, multiple homing situations, costs of switching to other platform operators, etc.;  
(7) The willingness and ability of other operators to enter the relevant market and the market entry barriers they face, such as scale requirements, technical requirements, policy and legal restrictions;  
(8) The innovation and technological change in the relevant market;  
(9) Other factors related to platform operation that need to be considered.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　人民法院依照[反垄断法第二十三条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-23)的规定认定被诉滥用知识产权的经营者的市场支配地位，可以重点考虑下列因素：  
（一）相关市场内特定知识产权客体的可替代性、替代性客体的数量及转向替代性客体的成本；  
（二）利用该特定知识产权所提供的商品的可替代性及该商品的市场份额；  
（三）交易相对人对拥有该特定知识产权的经营者的制衡能力；  
（四）相关市场的创新和技术变化情况；  
（五）其他需要考虑的与知识产权行使相关的因素。  
经营者主张不能仅根据其拥有知识产权而推定具有市场支配地位的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 33** When a People's Court determines the market dominant position of an operator accused of abusing intellectual property rights in accordance with Article 23 of the Anti-Monopoly Law, it may focus on the following factors:  
(1) The substitutability of the specific intellectual property object in the relevant market, the number of substitute objects, and the cost of switching to substitute objects;  
(2) The substitutability of the commodities provided using such specific intellectual property and the market share of such commodities;  
(3) The ability of the counterparty to counterbalance the operator holding such specific intellectual property;  
(4) The innovation and technological change in the relevant market;  
(5) Other factors related to the exercise of intellectual property rights that need to be considered.  
If an operator claims that a market dominant position shall not be presumed solely on the basis of its ownership of intellectual property, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　依据[反垄断法第二十四条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-24)第一款第二项、第三项被推定共同具有市场支配地位的经营者，有证据证明具有下列情形之一，反驳上述推定的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）该两个以上经营者之间不具有行为一致性且存在实质性竞争；  
（二）该两个以上经营者作为整体在相关市场受到来自其他经营者的有效竞争约束。

**Article 34** If an operator presumed to jointly have a market dominant position pursuant to Article 24, paragraph 1, items (2) and (3) of the Anti-Monopoly Law has evidence proving the existence of any of the following circumstances to rebut the aforesaid presumption, the People's Court shall support such rebuttal:  
(1) There is no behavioral consistency among the two or more operators and there is substantial competition among them;  
(2) The two or more operators as a whole are subject to effective competitive constraints from other operators in the relevant market.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　经营者同时具备下列条件的，人民法院可以认定其构成[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)规定的滥用市场支配地位行为：  
（一）在相关市场具有支配地位；  
（二）实施了被诉垄断行为；  
（三）被诉垄断行为具有排除、限制竞争效果；  
（四）实施被诉垄断行为缺乏正当理由。

**Article 35** If an operator meets all of the following conditions, the People's Court may find that it has committed an abuse of market dominant position as provided in Article 22 of the Anti-Monopoly Law:  
(1) It has a dominant position in the relevant market;  
(2) It has implemented the allegedly monopolistic conduct;  
(3) The allegedly monopolistic conduct has the effect of eliminating or restricting competition;  
(4) There is no justifiable reason for implementing the allegedly monopolistic conduct.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　人民法院认定[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)第一款第一项规定的经营者“以不公平的高价销售商品或者以不公平的低价购买商品”，可以综合考虑下列因素：  
（一）该商品的收益率是否明显偏离竞争性市场中的合理收益率；  
（二）该商品的价格是否明显偏离其成本与竞争条件下的合理利润之和；  
（三）经营者向交易相对人销售或者购买商品的价格是否明显高于或者低于该经营者在上下游市场中销售或者购买相同商品或者可比商品的价格；  
（四）经营者向交易相对人销售或者购买商品的价格是否明显高于或者低于其他经营者在相同或者相似条件下销售或者购买相同商品或者可比商品的价格；  
（五）经营者向交易相对人销售或者购买商品的价格是否明显高于或者低于该经营者在相同或者相似条件下在其他地域市场销售或者购买相同商品或者可比商品的价格；  
（六）经营者向交易相对人销售商品的价格增长幅度是否明显高于该经营者成本增长幅度，或者购买商品的价格降低幅度明显高于交易相对人成本降低幅度；  
（七）该高价或者低价的持续时间；  
（八）其他可以考虑的因素。  
认定前款第四项、第五项所称相同或者相似条件，可以考虑经营模式、交易渠道、供求状况、监管环境、交易环节、成本结构、交易情况、平台类型等因素。

**Article 36** When a People's Court determines whether an operator, as provided in Article 22, paragraph 1, item (1) of the Anti-Monopoly Law, has "sold commodities at unfairly high prices or purchased commodities at unfairly low prices," it may comprehensively consider the following factors:  
(1) Whether the rate of return on the commodity significantly deviates from a reasonable rate of return in a competitive market;  
(2) Whether the price of the commodity significantly deviates from the sum of its cost and a reasonable profit under competitive conditions;  
(3) Whether the price at which the operator sells or purchases commodities to/from the counterparty is significantly higher or lower than the price at which the operator sells or purchases identical or comparable commodities in the upstream or downstream markets;  
(4) Whether the price at which the operator sells or purchases commodities to/from the counterparty is significantly higher or lower than the price at which other operators sell or purchase identical or comparable commodities under identical or similar conditions;  
(5) Whether the price at which the operator sells or purchases commodities to/from the counterparty is significantly higher or lower than the price at which the operator sells or purchases identical or comparable commodities in other geographic markets under identical or similar conditions;  
(6) Whether the rate of increase in the price at which the operator sells commodities to the counterparty is significantly higher than the rate of increase in the operator's costs, or whether the rate of decrease in the price at which it purchases commodities is significantly higher than the rate of decrease in the counterparty's costs;  
(7) The duration of such high or low price;  
(8) Other factors that may be considered.  
When determining the identical or similar conditions referred to in items (4) and (5) of the preceding paragraph, factors such as business model, trading channel, supply and demand situation, regulatory environment, transaction link, cost structure, transaction circumstances, and platform type may be considered.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　具有市场支配地位的经营者，具有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以初步认定其构成[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)第一款第二项规定的“以低于成本的价格销售商品”：  
（一）经营者在较长时间内持续以低于平均可变成本或者平均可避免成本的价格销售商品；  
（二）经营者在较长时间内持续以高于平均可变成本或者平均可避免成本，但低于平均总成本的价格销售商品，且有其他证据证明其具有排除、限制同等效率的其他经营者在相关市场开展有效竞争的明确意图。  
依照前款规定认定平台经营者以低于成本的价格销售商品，还应当考虑该平台涉及的多边市场中各相关市场之间的成本关联情况及其合理性。  
具有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以认定构成[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)第一款第二项规定的正当理由：  
（一）低价处理鲜活商品、季节性商品、淘汰商品、即将超过有效期限的商品或者积压商品等；  
（二）因清偿债务、转产、歇业等低价销售商品；  
（三）为推广新商品、发展新业务、吸引新用户在合理期限内低价促销；  
（四）能够证明行为具有正当性的其他理由。

**Article 37** If a market-dominant operator has any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may preliminarily find that it has committed "selling commodities at a price below cost" as provided in Article 22, paragraph 1, item (2) of the Anti-Monopoly Law:  
(1) The operator continuously sells commodities at a price below the average variable cost or average avoidable cost for a relatively long period of time;  
(2) The operator continuously sells commodities at a price above the average variable cost or average avoidable cost but below the average total cost for a relatively long period of time, and there is other evidence proving its clear intent to eliminate or restrict other operators of equivalent efficiency from effectively competing in the relevant market.  
When determining, in accordance with the preceding paragraph, that a platform operator sells commodities at a price below cost, the cost correlation among the relevant markets in the multi-sided market involved in the platform and its reasonableness shall also be considered.  
If any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court may find that it constitutes a justifiable reason as provided in Article 22, paragraph 1, item (2) of the Anti-Monopoly Law:  
(1) Selling at low prices perishable goods, seasonal goods, obsolete goods, goods about to expire, or overstocked goods, etc.;  
(2) Selling goods at low prices due to debt settlement, production conversion, business closure, etc.;  
(3) Conducting low-price promotions within a reasonable period for the purpose of promoting new products, developing new businesses, or attracting new users;  
(4) Other reasons capable of proving the justifiability of the conduct.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　具有市场支配地位的经营者，同时具备下列条件的，人民法院可以初步认定其构成[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)第一款第三项规定的“拒绝与交易相对人进行交易”：  
（一）经营者直接拒绝与交易相对人交易，提出交易相对人明显难以接受的交易条件，或者不合理地拖延交易，致使未能达成交易；  
（二）经营者与交易相对人进行交易在经济、技术、法律和安全上具有可行性；  
（三）拒绝交易行为排除、限制上游市场或者下游市场的竞争。  
具有市场支配地位的经营者没有正当理由，拒绝将其商品、平台或者软件系统等与其他经营者提供的特定商品、平台或者软件系统等相兼容，拒绝开放其技术、数据、平台接口，或者拒绝许可其知识产权的，人民法院依照[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)第一款第三项的规定予以认定时，可以综合考虑下列因素：  
（一）该经营者实施兼容、开放或者许可在经济、技术、法律和安全上的可行性；  
（二）该商品、平台或者软件系统、技术、数据、知识产权等的可替代性及重建成本；  
（三）其他经营者在上游市场或者下游市场开展有效竞争对该经营者商品、平台或者软件系统、技术、数据、知识产权等的依赖程度；  
（四）拒绝兼容、开放或者许可对创新以及推出新商品的影响；  
（五）实施兼容、开放或者许可对该经营者自身经营活动和合法权益的影响；  
（六）拒绝兼容、开放或者许可是否实质性地排除、限制相关市场的有效竞争；  
（七）其他可以考虑的因素。  
具有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以认定构成[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)第一款第三项规定的正当理由：  
（一）因不可抗力、情势变更等客观原因无法进行交易或者导致交易条件、结果明显不公平；  
（二）交易相对人具有经营状况严重恶化、转移财产或者抽逃资金以逃避债务等丧失或者可能丧失履行交易能力的情形，或者具有不良信用记录、丧失商业信誉、实施违法犯罪等情形，影响交易安全；  
（三）交易相对人拒绝接受适当的交易条件，或者不遵守经营者提出的合理要求；  
（四）与交易相对人交易将严重减损该经营者的正当利益；  
（五）能够证明行为具有正当性的其他理由。

**Article 38** If a market-dominant operator meets all of the following conditions, the People's Court may preliminarily find that it has committed "refusing to deal with a counterparty" as provided in Article 22, paragraph 1, item (3) of the Anti-Monopoly Law:  
(1) The operator directly refuses to deal with the counterparty, proposes trading conditions that are obviously unacceptable to the counterparty, or unreasonably delays the transaction, resulting in failure to reach a transaction;  
(2) Dealing with the counterparty is economically, technically, legally, and safely feasible for the operator;  
(3) The refusal to deal eliminates or restricts competition in the upstream or downstream market.  
If a market-dominant operator, without justifiable reason, refuses to make its commodities, platform, or software systems, etc., compatible with specific commodities, platforms, or software systems, etc., provided by other operators, refuses to open its technologies, data, or platform interfaces, or refuses to license its intellectual property, when the People's Court determines the matter in accordance with Article 22, paragraph 1, item (3) of the Anti-Monopoly Law, it may comprehensively consider the following factors:  
(1) The economic, technical, legal, and safety feasibility of the operator implementing compatibility, opening, or licensing;  
(2) The substitutability of such commodities, platforms, software systems, technologies, data, or intellectual property, etc., and the cost of rebuilding;  
(3) The degree of dependence of other operators in effectively competing in the upstream or downstream market on the operator's commodities, platforms, software systems, technologies, data, or intellectual property, etc.;  
(4) The impact of refusing compatibility, opening, or licensing on innovation and the launch of new products;  
(5) The impact of implementing compatibility, opening, or licensing on the operator's own business activities and lawful rights and interests;  
(6) Whether the refusal to be compatible, open, or license substantially eliminates or restricts effective competition in the relevant market;  
(7) Other factors that may be considered.  
If any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court may find that it constitutes a justifiable reason as provided in Article 22, paragraph 1, item (3) of the Anti-Monopoly Law:  
(1) It is impossible to conduct a transaction due to objective reasons such as force majeure or change of circumstances, or the transaction conditions or results become manifestly unfair;  
(2) The counterparty is in a situation where its business conditions have seriously deteriorated, it transfers property or withdraws funds to evade debts, or other circumstances where it loses or may lose its ability to perform the transaction, or it has a poor credit record, loses business reputation, commits illegal or criminal acts, etc., affecting transaction security;  
(3) The counterparty refuses to accept appropriate transaction conditions or fails to comply with reasonable requirements raised by the operator;  
(4) Dealing with the counterparty would seriously impair the operator's legitimate interests;  
(5) Other reasons capable of proving the justifiability of the conduct.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　具有市场支配地位的经营者，同时具备下列条件的，人民法院可以初步认定其构成[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)第一款第四项规定的“限定交易相对人只能与其进行交易或者只能与其指定的经营者进行交易”：  
（一）经营者直接限定或者以设定交易条件、提供交易指南等方式变相限定交易相对人只能与其进行交易或者只能与其指定的经营者进行交易，或者限定交易相对人不得与特定经营者进行交易；  
（二）限定交易行为排除、限制相关市场的竞争。  
认定限定交易行为是否具有排除、限制竞争效果，可以综合考虑下列因素：  
（一）限定交易的范围、程度及持续时间；  
（二）限定交易是否提高市场进入壁垒或者增加竞争对手的成本而产生市场封锁效应；  
（三）被告为平台经营者的，限定交易所针对的平台内经营者的可替代性和平台用户使用多个替代性平台的情况及其转向其他平台的成本；  
（四）限定交易是否实质剥夺交易相对人的自主选择权；  
（五）其他需要考虑的因素。  
具有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以认定构成[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)第一款第四项规定的正当理由：  
（一）为保护交易相对人和消费者利益所必需；  
（二）为满足商品安全要求所必需；  
（三）为保护知识产权或者数据安全所必需；  
（四）为保护针对交易进行的特定投入所必需；  
（五）为维护平台合理的商业模式所必需；  
（六）为防止对平台整体具有消极影响的不当行为所必需；  
（七）能够证明行为具有正当性的其他理由。

**Article 39** If a market-dominant operator meets all of the following conditions, the People's Court may preliminarily find that it has committed "restricting the counterparty to deal only with itself or only with operators designated by it" as provided in Article 22, paragraph 1, item (4) of the Anti-Monopoly Law:  
(1) The operator directly restricts, or indirectly restricts through means such as setting transaction conditions or providing transaction guidelines, the counterparty to deal only with itself or only with operators designated by it, or restricts the counterparty from dealing with specific operators;  
(2) The restrictive dealing conduct eliminates or restricts competition in the relevant market.  
When determining whether the restrictive dealing conduct has the effect of eliminating or restricting competition, the following factors may be comprehensively considered:  
(1) The scope, extent, and duration of the restrictive dealing;  
(2) Whether the restrictive dealing raises market entry barriers or increases competitors' costs, resulting in a market foreclosure effect;  
(3) If the defendant is a platform operator, the substitutability of the operators within the platform targeted by the restrictive dealing, the extent to which platform users use multiple alternative platforms, and the cost of switching to other platforms;  
(4) Whether the restrictive dealing substantially deprives the counterparty of its independent choice;  
(5) Other factors that need to be considered.  
If any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court may find that it constitutes a justifiable reason as provided in Article 22, paragraph 1, item (4) of the Anti-Monopoly Law:  
(1) It is necessary to protect the interests of the counterparty and consumers;  
(2) It is necessary to meet commodity safety requirements;  
(3) It is necessary to protect intellectual property or data security;  
(4) It is necessary to protect specific investments made for the transaction;  
(5) It is necessary to maintain the platform's reasonable business model;  
(6) It is necessary to prevent improper conduct that would have a negative impact on the platform as a whole;  
(7) Other reasons capable of proving the justifiability of the conduct.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　具有市场支配地位的经营者，同时具备下列条件的，人民法院可以初步认定其构成[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)第一款第五项规定的“搭售商品”：  
（一）经营者将可以单独销售的不同商品捆绑销售；  
（二）交易相对人违背意愿接受被搭售商品；  
（三）搭售行为排除、限制相关市场的竞争。  
[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)第一款第五项规定的“附加其他不合理的交易条件”，包括下列情形：  
（一）对交易达成、服务方式、付款方式、销售地域及对象、售后保障等附加不合理限制；  
（二）在交易对价之外索取缺乏合理依据的费用或者利益；  
（三）附加与所涉交易缺乏关联性的交易条件；  
（四）强制收集非必要的用户信息或者数据；  
（五）附加限制交易相对人改进技术、研究开发新产品等不竞争义务。  
具有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以认定构成[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)第一款第五项规定的正当理由：  
（一）符合正当的交易习惯、消费习惯或者商业惯例；  
（二）为保护交易相对人和消费者利益所必需；  
（三）为满足商品安全要求所必需；  
（四）为正常实施特定技术所必需；  
（五）为维护平台正常运行所必需；  
（六）能够证明行为具有正当性的其他理由。

**Article 40** If a market-dominant operator meets all of the following conditions, the People's Court may preliminarily find that it has committed "tying commodities" as provided in Article 22, paragraph 1, item (5) of the Anti-Monopoly Law:  
(1) The operator bundles different commodities that can be sold separately;  
(2) The counterparty accepts the tied commodities against its will;  
(3) The tying conduct eliminates or restricts competition in the relevant market.  
"Imposing other unreasonable trading conditions" as provided in Article 22, paragraph 1, item (5) of the Anti-Monopoly Law includes the following circumstances:  
(1) Imposing unreasonable restrictions on transaction conclusion, service method, payment method, sales territory and targets, after-sales guarantee, etc.;  
(2) Demanding fees or benefits without reasonable basis in addition to the transaction consideration;  
(3) Imposing transaction conditions that lack relevance to the transaction involved;  
(4) Forcibly collecting unnecessary user information or data;  
(5) Imposing non-compete obligations that restrict the counterparty from improving technology, researching and developing new products, etc.  
If any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court may find that it constitutes a justifiable reason as provided in Article 22, paragraph 1, item (5) of the Anti-Monopoly Law:  
(1) It conforms to legitimate trading practices, consumption habits, or commercial customs;  
(2) It is necessary to protect the interests of the counterparty and consumers;  
(3) It is necessary to meet commodity safety requirements;  
(4) It is necessary for the normal implementation of specific technologies;  
(5) It is necessary to maintain the normal operation of the platform;  
(6) Other reasons capable of proving the justifiability of the conduct.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　具有市场支配地位的经营者，同时具备下列条件的，人民法院可以初步认定其构成[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)第一款第六项规定的“对条件相同的交易相对人在交易价格等交易条件上实行差别待遇”：  
（一）经营者就相同商品对交易相对人在交易价格等交易条件上实行差别待遇；  
（二）与经营者的其他交易相对人相比，该交易相对人在交易安全、交易成本、规模和能力、信用状况、所处交易环节、交易持续时间等方面不存在影响交易的实质性差异；  
（三）差别待遇行为排除、限制相关市场的竞争。  
具有市场支配地位的经营者向交易相对人销售或者购买商品的价格高于或者低于该经营者在上下游市场中销售或者购买相同商品的价格，形成对交易相对人的利润挤压，足以排除、限制同等效率的交易相对人在相关市场开展有效竞争的，人民法院可以初步认定该经营者构成前款所称差别待遇。  
认定差别待遇是否具有排除、限制竞争效果，可以综合考虑下列因素：  
（一）是否排除、限制经营者与竞争对手之间的竞争；  
（二）是否致使交易相对人处于不利竞争地位，并排除、限制其所在相关市场的竞争；  
（三）是否损害消费者利益和社会公共利益；  
（四）其他可以考虑的因素。  
具有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以认定构成[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)第一款第六项规定的正当理由：  
（一）根据交易相对人的实际需求实行差别待遇且符合正当的交易习惯、消费习惯或者商业惯例；  
（二）针对新用户的首次交易在合理期限内开展优惠活动；  
（三）基于公平、合理、无歧视的平台规则实施的随机性交易；  
（四）能够证明行为具有正当性的其他理由。

**Article 41** If a market-dominant operator meets all of the following conditions, the People's Court may preliminarily find that it has committed "granting differential treatment to counterparties with identical conditions in terms of transaction price and other trading conditions" as provided in Article 22, paragraph 1, item (6) of the Anti-Monopoly Law:  
(1) The operator grants differential treatment to counterparties in terms of transaction price and other trading conditions with respect to identical commodities;  
(2) Compared with other counterparties of the operator, the counterparty has no substantive differences affecting the transaction in terms of transaction security, transaction cost, scale and capability, credit status, transaction link, transaction duration, etc.;  
(3) The differential treatment conduct eliminates or restricts competition in the relevant market.  
If a market-dominant operator sells or purchases commodities to/from a counterparty at a price higher or lower than the price at which it sells or purchases identical commodities in the upstream or downstream market, thereby creating a profit squeeze on the counterparty sufficient to eliminate or restrict counterparties of equivalent efficiency from effectively competing in the relevant market, the People's Court may preliminarily find that the operator has committed the differential treatment referred to in the preceding paragraph.  
When determining whether the differential treatment has the effect of eliminating or restricting competition, the following factors may be comprehensively considered:  
(1) Whether it eliminates or restricts competition between the operator and its competitors;  
(2) Whether it places the counterparty in a disadvantageous competitive position and eliminates or restricts competition in its relevant market;  
(3) Whether it harms consumer interests and social public interests;  
(4) Other factors that may be considered.  
If any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court may find that it constitutes a justifiable reason as provided in Article 22, paragraph 1, item (6) of the Anti-Monopoly Law:  
(1) Granting differential treatment based on the actual needs of the counterparty and conforming to legitimate trading practices, consumption habits, or commercial customs;  
(2) Conducting preferential activities for the first transaction of new users within a reasonable period;  
(3) Conducting random transactions based on fair, reasonable, and non-discriminatory platform rules;  
(4) Other reasons capable of proving the justifiability of the conduct.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　平台内经营者作为原告提起诉讼，主张平台经营者利用数据、算法、技术、平台规则等实施滥用市场支配地位或者其他违法行为，根据原告的诉讼请求和具体案情，人民法院可以区别下列情形作出处理：  
（一）平台经营者通过惩罚性或者激励性措施等限定平台内经营者交易、对平台内经营者附加不合理的交易条件、对条件相同的平台内经营者在交易价格等交易条件上实行差别待遇等，原告主张该平台经营者滥用市场支配地位的，依照[反垄断法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-22)、[电子商务法第二十二条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国电子商务法.md#art-22)的规定审查认定；  
（二）原告主张实施前项行为的平台经营者违反[电子商务法第三十五条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国电子商务法.md#art-35)的规定的，依照该条规定处理。  
五、民事责任

**Article 42** If an operator within a platform files a lawsuit as a plaintiff, claiming that the platform operator has abused its market dominant position or committed other illegal acts by utilizing data, algorithms, technologies, platform rules, etc., the People's Court may, based on the plaintiff's claims and the specific circumstances of the case, handle the matter by distinguishing the following situations:  
(1) If the platform operator, through punitive or incentive measures, restricts transactions by operators within the platform, imposes unreasonable trading conditions on operators within the platform, grants differential treatment to operators within the platform with identical conditions in terms of transaction price and other trading conditions, etc., and the plaintiff claims that the platform operator has abused its market dominant position, the matter shall be examined and determined in accordance with Article 22 of the Anti-Monopoly Law and Article 22 of the E-Commerce Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) If the plaintiff claims that the platform operator that committed the conduct in the preceding item has violated the provisions of Article 35 of the E-Commerce Law of the People's Republic of China, the matter shall be handled in accordance with that article.  
V. Civil Liability

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　被告实施垄断行为，给原告造成损失的，根据原告的诉讼请求和查明的事实，人民法院可以依法判令被告承担停止侵害、赔偿损失等民事责任。  
判令被告停止被诉垄断行为尚不足以消除排除、限制竞争效果的，根据原告的诉讼请求和具体案情，人民法院可以判令被告承担作出必要行为以恢复竞争的法律责任。

**Article 43** If the defendant commits a monopolistic act and causes losses to the plaintiff, the People's Court may, in accordance with the plaintiff's claims and the facts found, order the defendant to bear civil liability such as cessation of infringement and compensation for losses.  
If ordering the defendant to cease the allegedly monopolistic conduct is insufficient to eliminate the effect of eliminating or restricting competition, the People's Court may, based on the plaintiff's claims and the specific circumstances of the case, order the defendant to bear the legal liability of taking necessary actions to restore competition.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　原告因被诉垄断行为受到的损失包括直接损失和相对于该行为未发生条件下减少的可得利益。  
确定原告因被诉垄断行为受到的损失，可以考虑下列因素：  
（一）被诉垄断行为实施之前或者结束以后与实施期间相关市场的商品价格、经营成本、利润、市场份额等；  
（二）未受垄断行为影响的可比市场的商品价格、经营成本、利润等；  
（三）未受垄断行为影响的可比经营者的商品价格、经营成本、利润、市场份额等；  
（四）其他可以合理证明原告因被诉垄断行为所受损失的因素。  
原告有证据证明被诉垄断行为已经给其造成损失，但难以根据前款规定确定具体损失数额的，人民法院可以根据原告的主张和案件证据，考虑被诉垄断行为的性质、程度、持续时间、获得的利益等因素，酌情确定合理的赔偿数额。

**Article 44** The losses suffered by the plaintiff due to the allegedly monopolistic conduct include direct losses and the decreased obtainable benefits as compared to the conditions had the conduct not occurred.  
When determining the losses suffered by the plaintiff due to the allegedly monopolistic conduct, the following factors may be considered:  
(1) The commodity prices, operating costs, profits, market shares, etc., in the relevant market before the implementation or after the cessation of the allegedly monopolistic conduct, and during the period of its implementation;  
(2) The commodity prices, operating costs, profits, etc., in comparable markets not affected by the monopolistic conduct;  
(3) The commodity prices, operating costs, profits, market shares, etc., of comparable operators not affected by the monopolistic conduct;  
(4) Other factors that can reasonably prove the losses suffered by the plaintiff due to the allegedly monopolistic conduct.  
If the plaintiff has evidence proving that the allegedly monopolistic conduct has caused it losses, but it is difficult to determine the specific amount of losses in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the People's Court may, based on the plaintiff's claims and the case evidence, and taking into account factors such as the nature, extent, duration, and benefits obtained from the allegedly monopolistic conduct, determine a reasonable amount of compensation at its discretion.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　根据原告的诉讼请求和具体案情，人民法院可以将原告因调查、制止垄断行为所支付的合理开支，包括合理的市场调查费用、经济分析费用、律师费用等，计入损失赔偿范围。

**Article 45** Based on the plaintiff's claims and the specific circumstances of the case, the People's Court may include the reasonable expenses incurred by the plaintiff in investigating and stopping the monopolistic conduct, including reasonable market investigation costs, economic analysis costs, attorney fees, etc., in the scope of compensation for losses.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　多个被诉垄断行为相互关联，在同一相关市场或者多个相关市场给原告造成难以分割的整体性损失的，人民法院在确定损失时应当整体考虑。  
多个被诉垄断行为各自独立，在不同的相关市场给原告造成损失的，人民法院在确定损失时可以分别考虑。

**Article 46** If multiple allegedly monopolistic conducts are interrelated and cause indivisible overall losses to the plaintiff in the same relevant market or multiple relevant markets, the People's Court shall consider the losses as a whole when determining them.  
If multiple allegedly monopolistic conducts are independent of each other and cause losses to the plaintiff in different relevant markets, the People's Court may consider the losses separately when determining them.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　横向垄断协议的经营者以达成、实施该协议的其他经营者为被告，依据[反垄断法第六十条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-60)的规定请求赔偿其参与该协议期间的损失的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 47** If an operator that participated in a horizontal monopoly agreement files a claim for compensation for losses incurred during its participation in the agreement against other operators that entered into or implemented the agreement, in accordance with Article 60 of the Anti-Monopoly Law, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　当事人主张被诉垄断行为所涉合同或者经营者团体的章程、决议、决定等因违反反垄断法或者其他法律、行政法规的强制性规定而无效的，人民法院应当依照[民法典第一百五十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-153)的规定审查认定。  
被诉垄断行为所涉合同或者经营者团体的章程、决议、决定中的部分条款因违反反垄断法或者其他法律、行政法规的强制性规定而无效，当事人主张与该部分条款具有紧密关联、不具有独立存在意义或者便利被诉垄断行为实施的其他条款一并无效的，人民法院可予支持。

**Article 48** If a party claims that a contract involved in the allegedly monopolistic conduct, or the charter, resolution, decision, etc., of an association of operators, is void because it violates the mandatory provisions of the Anti-Monopoly Law or other laws or administrative regulations, the People's Court shall examine and determine the matter in accordance with Article 153 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China.  
If some clauses of the contract involved in the allegedly monopolistic conduct, or of the charter, resolution, decision, etc., of the association of operators, are void because they violate the mandatory provisions of the Anti-Monopoly Law or other laws or administrative regulations, and the party claims that other clauses that are closely related to such clauses, have no independent significance, or facilitate the implementation of the allegedly monopolistic conduct shall also be void, the People's Court may support such claim.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　因垄断行为产生的损害赔偿请求权诉讼时效期间，从原告知道或者应当知道权益受到损害以及义务人之日起计算。  
原告向反垄断执法机构举报被诉垄断行为的，诉讼时效从其举报之日起中断。反垄断执法机构决定不立案、撤销案件或者决定终止调查的，诉讼时效期间从原告知道或者应当知道该事由之日起重新计算。  
反垄断执法机构调查后认定构成垄断行为的，诉讼时效期间从原告知道或者应当知道反垄断执法机构认定构成垄断行为的处理决定确定发生法律效力之日起重新计算。  
六、附则

**Article 49** The limitation of action period for the right to claim damages arising from a monopolistic act shall be calculated from the date on which the plaintiff knows or should have known that its rights and interests have been harmed and who the obligor is.  
If the plaintiff reports the allegedly monopolistic conduct to the anti-monopoly enforcement authority, the limitation of action shall be interrupted from the date of such report. If the anti-monopoly enforcement authority decides not to file a case, closes the case, or decides to terminate the investigation, the limitation of action period shall be recalculated from the date on which the plaintiff knows or should have known of such matter.  
If the anti-monopoly enforcement authority, after investigation, determines that the conduct constitutes a monopolistic act, the limitation of action period shall be recalculated from the date on which the plaintiff knows or should have known that the decision of the anti-monopoly enforcement authority determining the conduct as a monopolistic act has become legally effective.  
VI. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　人民法院审理垄断民事纠纷案件，适用被诉垄断行为发生时施行的反垄断法。被诉垄断行为发生在修改后的反垄断法施行之前，行为持续至或者损害后果出现在修改后的反垄断法施行之后的，适用修改后的反垄断法。

**Article 50** When hearing civil monopoly dispute cases, the People's Court shall apply the Anti-Monopoly Law in force at the time of the allegedly monopolistic conduct. If the allegedly monopolistic conduct occurred before the revised Anti-Monopoly Law came into force, but the conduct continued or the harmful consequences appeared after the revised Anti-Monopoly Law came into force, the revised Anti-Monopoly Law shall apply.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　本解释自2024年7月1日起施行。《最高人民法院关于审理因垄断行为引发的民事纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的规定》（法释〔2012〕5号）同时废止。  
本解释施行后，人民法院正在审理的第一审、第二审案件适用本解释；本解释施行前已经作出生效裁判，当事人申请再审或者依照审判监督程序再审的案件，不适用本解释。

**Article 51** This Interpretation shall come into force on July 1, 2024. The Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Civil Disputes Arising from Monopolistic Conduct (Fa Shi [2012] No. 5) shall be abolished simultaneously.  
After this Interpretation comes into force, the cases of first instance and second instance being tried by the People's Court shall apply this Interpretation; the cases where an effective judgment has been made before this Interpretation comes into force, and the parties apply for retrial or retrial according to the trial supervision procedure, shall not apply this Interpretation.
