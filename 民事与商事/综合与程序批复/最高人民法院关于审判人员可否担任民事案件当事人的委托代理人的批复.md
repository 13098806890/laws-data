### 最高人民法院关于审判人员可否担任民事案件当事人的委托代理人的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于审判人员可否担任  
  
民事案件当事人的委托代理人的批复  
  
一九八四年一月十一日〔83〕民他字第37号  
  
河北省高级人民法院：  
  
你院一九八三年十二月二十六日关于审判人员可否担任民事案件当事人的委托代理人的请示收悉。经我们研究认为：人民法院的审判人员以不担任委托代理人为宜，如受委托的审判人员是委托人的近亲属或监护人，且不在受理该案的人民法院工作的，可作为特殊情况准许。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Whether Adjudicators May Serve as Authorized Agents of Parties in Civil Cases  
January 11, 1984 [83] Min Ta Zi No. 37  
Higher People's Court of Hebei Province:  
We have received your request dated December 26, 1983, regarding whether adjudicators may serve as authorized agents of parties in civil cases. Upon our study, it is considered that adjudicators of the People's Court shall not serve as authorized agents; however, if the adjudicator who is authorized is a close relative or guardian of the principal and does not work in the People's Court that is hearing the case, it may be permitted as a special circumstance.  
This is our reply.
