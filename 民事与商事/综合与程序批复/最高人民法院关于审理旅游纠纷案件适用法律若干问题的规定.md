# 最高人民法院关于审理旅游纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2010〕13号  
**公布日期**：2010-10-26  
**生效日期**：2010-11-01

> 最高人民法院《关于审理旅游纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的规定》已于2010年9月13日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1496次会议通过，现予公布，自2010年11月1日起施行。 2010年10月26日 最高人民法院 关于审理旅游纠纷案件适用法律 若干问题的规定 法释〔2010〕13号 （2010年9月13日最高人民法院审判委员会第1496次会议通过　2010年10月26日最高人民法院公告公布　自2010年11月1日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所称的旅游纠纷，是指旅游者与旅游经营者、旅游辅助服务者之间因旅游发生的合同纠纷或者侵权纠纷。  
“旅游经营者”是指以自己的名义经营旅游业务，向公众提供旅游服务的人。  
“旅游辅助服务者”是指与旅游经营者存在合同关系，协助旅游经营者履行旅游合同义务，实际提供交通、游览、住宿、餐饮、娱乐等旅游服务的人。  
旅游者在自行旅游过程中与旅游景点经营者因旅游发生的纠纷，参照适用本规定。

**Article 1** The term "tourism dispute" as used in these Provisions refers to a contractual dispute or tort dispute between a tourist and a tourism business operator or a tourism auxiliary service provider arising from tourism.  
"Tourism business operator" means a person who operates tourism business in its own name and provides tourism services to the public.  
"Tourism auxiliary service provider" means a person who has a contractual relationship with a tourism business operator, assists the tourism business operator in performing the obligations under the tourism contract, and actually provides tourism services such as transportation, sightseeing, accommodation, catering, and entertainment.  
Disputes arising between a tourist and a scenic spot operator during the tourist's independent travel shall be handled by reference to these Provisions.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　以单位、家庭等集体形式与旅游经营者订立旅游合同，在履行过程中发生纠纷，除集体以合同一方当事人名义起诉外，旅游者个人提起旅游合同纠纷诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 2** Where a group in the form of a work unit, family or the like concludes a tourism contract with a tourism business operator, and a dispute arises during the performance thereof, the People's Court shall accept a lawsuit initiated by an individual tourist concerning a tourism contract dispute, except where the group files a lawsuit in the name of a party to the contract.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　因旅游经营者方面的同一原因造成旅游者人身损害、财产损失，旅游者选择要求旅游经营者承担违约责任或者侵权责任的，人民法院应当根据当事人选择的案由进行审理。

**Article 3** Where personal injury or property damage is caused to a tourist by the same reason attributable to the tourism business operator, and the tourist elects to request the tourism business operator to bear liability for breach of contract or tort liability, the People's Court shall conduct the adjudication based on the cause of action chosen by the party.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　因旅游辅助服务者的原因导致旅游经营者违约，旅游者仅起诉旅游经营者的，人民法院可以将旅游辅助服务者追加为第三人。

**Article 4** Where a tourism business operator breaches the contract due to the reason of a tourism auxiliary service provider, and the tourist only sues the tourism business operator, the People's Court may add the tourism auxiliary service provider as a third party.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　旅游经营者已投保责任险，旅游者因保险责任事故仅起诉旅游经营者的，人民法院可以应当事人的请求将保险公司列为第三人。

**Article 5** Where a tourism business operator has insured liability insurance, and a tourist, due to an insurance liability accident, only sues the tourism business operator, the People's Court may, upon the request of a party, name the insurance company as a third party.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　旅游经营者以格式合同、通知、声明、告示等方式作出对旅游者不公平、不合理的规定，或者减轻、免除其损害旅游者合法权益的责任，旅游者请求依据[消费者权益保护法第二十四条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法.md#art-24)的规定认定该内容无效的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 6** Where a tourism business operator, by means of standard form contract, notice, declaration, announcement or the like, makes provisions that are unfair or unreasonable to tourists, or reduces or exempts its liability for damaging the lawful rights and interests of tourists, and the tourist requests that the content be determined invalid pursuant to Article 24 of the Law on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　旅游经营者、旅游辅助服务者未尽到安全保障义务，造成旅游者人身损害、财产损失，旅游者请求旅游经营者、旅游辅助服务者承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
因第三人的行为造成旅游者人身损害、财产损失，由第三人承担责任；旅游经营者、旅游辅助服务者未尽安全保障义务，旅游者请求其承担相应补充责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 7** Where a tourism business operator or a tourism auxiliary service provider fails to fulfill the safety guarantee obligation, causing personal injury or property damage to a tourist, and the tourist requests the tourism business operator or tourism auxiliary service provider to bear liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where personal injury or property damage is caused to a tourist by the act of a third party, the third party shall bear liability; if the tourism business operator or tourism auxiliary service provider fails to fulfill the safety guarantee obligation, and the tourist requests it to bear corresponding supplementary liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　旅游经营者、旅游辅助服务者对可能危及旅游者人身、财产安全的旅游项目未履行告知、警示义务，造成旅游者人身损害、财产损失，旅游者请求旅游经营者、旅游辅助服务者承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
旅游者未按旅游经营者、旅游辅助服务者的要求提供与旅游活动相关的个人健康信息并履行如实告知义务，或者不听从旅游经营者、旅游辅助服务者的告知、警示，参加不适合自身条件的旅游活动，导致旅游过程中出现人身损害、财产损失，旅游者请求旅游经营者、旅游辅助服务者承担责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 8** Where a tourism business operator or a tourism auxiliary service provider fails to perform the obligation of notification and warning with respect to tourism projects that may endanger the personal or property safety of tourists, causing personal injury or property damage to a tourist, and the tourist requests the tourism business operator or tourism auxiliary service provider to bear liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where a tourist fails to provide personal health information related to the tourism activity and fulfill the obligation of truthful notification as required by the tourism business operator or tourism auxiliary service provider, or fails to heed the notification and warning of the tourism business operator or tourism auxiliary service provider and participates in tourism activities unsuitable for his/her own conditions, resulting in personal injury or property damage during the tourism process, and the tourist requests the tourism business operator or tourism auxiliary service provider to bear liability, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　旅游经营者、旅游辅助服务者泄露旅游者个人信息或者未经旅游者同意公开其个人信息，旅游者请求其承担相应责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 9** Where a tourism business operator or a tourism auxiliary service provider discloses personal information of a tourist or makes public the tourist's personal information without the tourist's consent, and the tourist requests it to bear corresponding liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　旅游经营者将旅游业务转让给其他旅游经营者，旅游者不同意转让，请求解除旅游合同、追究旅游经营者违约责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
旅游经营者擅自将其旅游业务转让给其他旅游经营者，旅游者在旅游过程中遭受损害，请求与其签订旅游合同的旅游经营者和实际提供旅游服务的旅游经营者承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 10** Where a tourism business operator transfers the tourism business to another tourism business operator, and the tourist does not agree to the transfer and requests dissolution of the tourism contract and to pursue the liability for breach of contract of the tourism business operator, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where a tourism business operator arbitrarily transfers its tourism business to another tourism business operator, and a tourist suffers damage during the tourism process and requests the tourism business operator that concluded the tourism contract with the tourist and the tourism business operator that actually provides the tourism services to bear joint and several liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　除合同性质不宜转让或者合同另有约定之外，在旅游行程开始前的合理期间内，旅游者将其在旅游合同中的权利义务转让给第三人，请求确认转让合同效力的，人民法院应予支持。  
因前款所述原因，旅游经营者请求旅游者、第三人给付增加的费用或者旅游者请求旅游经营者退还减少的费用的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 11** Except where the nature of the contract is not suitable for transfer or the contract provides otherwise, within a reasonable period before the commencement of the tour itinerary, a tourist transfers his/her rights and obligations under the tourism contract to a third party and requests confirmation of the validity of the transfer contract, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
For the reason stated in the preceding paragraph, where the tourism business operator requests the tourist or the third party to pay the increased expenses, or the tourist requests the tourism business operator to refund the reduced expenses, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　旅游行程开始前或者进行中，因旅游者单方解除合同，旅游者请求旅游经营者退还尚未实际发生的费用，或者旅游经营者请求旅游者支付合理费用的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 12** Before or during the tour itinerary, if the tourist unilaterally dissolves the contract, and the tourist requests the tourism business operator to refund expenses that have not actually been incurred, or the tourism business operator requests the tourist to pay reasonable expenses, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　因不可抗力等不可归责于旅游经营者、旅游辅助服务者的客观原因导致旅游合同无法履行，旅游经营者、旅游者请求解除旅游合同的，人民法院应予支持。旅游经营者、旅游者请求对方承担违约责任的，人民法院不予支持。旅游者请求旅游经营者退还尚未实际发生的费用的，人民法院应予支持。  
因不可抗力等不可归责于旅游经营者、旅游辅助服务者的客观原因变更旅游行程，在征得旅游者同意后，旅游经营者请求旅游者分担因此增加的旅游费用或旅游者请求旅游经营者退还因此减少的旅游费用的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 13** Where a tourism contract cannot be performed due to objective reasons such as force majeure that are not attributable to the tourism business operator or the tourism auxiliary service provider, and the tourism business operator or the tourist requests dissolution of the tourism contract, the People's Court shall uphold such request. If the tourism business operator or the tourist requests the other party to bear liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall not uphold such request. If the tourist requests the tourism business operator to refund expenses that have not actually been incurred, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where the tour itinerary is changed due to objective reasons such as force majeure that are not attributable to the tourism business operator or the tourism auxiliary service provider, and after obtaining the consent of the tourist, the tourism business operator requests the tourist to share the increased tourism expenses, or the tourist requests the tourism business operator to refund the reduced tourism expenses, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　因旅游辅助服务者的原因造成旅游者人身损害、财产损失，旅游者选择请求旅游辅助服务者承担侵权责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
旅游经营者对旅游辅助服务者未尽谨慎选择义务，旅游者请求旅游经营者承担相应补充责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 14** Where personal injury or property damage is caused to a tourist due to the reason of a tourism auxiliary service provider, and the tourist elects to request the tourism auxiliary service provider to bear tort liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where a tourism business operator fails to fulfill the duty of careful selection with respect to a tourism auxiliary service provider, and the tourist requests the tourism business operator to bear corresponding supplementary liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　签订旅游合同的旅游经营者将其部分旅游业务委托旅游目的地的旅游经营者，因受托方未尽旅游合同义务，旅游者在旅游过程中受到损害，要求作出委托的旅游经营者承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
旅游经营者委托除前款规定以外的人从事旅游业务，发生旅游纠纷，旅游者起诉旅游经营者的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 15** Where a tourism business operator that concluded a tourism contract entrusts part of its tourism business to a tourism business operator at the tourism destination, and due to the entrusted party's failure to perform the obligations under the tourism contract, the tourist suffers damage during the tourism process and demands that the entrusting tourism business operator bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where a tourism business operator entrusts a person other than those specified in the preceding paragraph to engage in tourism business, and a tourism dispute arises, and the tourist sues the tourism business operator, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　旅游经营者准许他人挂靠其名下从事旅游业务，造成旅游者人身损害、财产损失，旅游者请求旅游经营者与挂靠人承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 16** Where a tourism business operator permits another person to operate tourism business under its name, causing personal injury or property damage to a tourist, and the tourist requests the tourism business operator and the name-lender to bear joint and several liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　旅游经营者违反合同约定，有擅自改变旅游行程、遗漏旅游景点、减少旅游服务项目、降低旅游服务标准等行为，旅游者请求旅游经营者赔偿未完成约定旅游服务项目等合理费用的，人民法院应予支持。  
旅游经营者提供服务时有欺诈行为，旅游者请求旅游经营者双倍赔偿其遭受的损失的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 17** Where a tourism business operator violates the contract by unilaterally changing the tour itinerary, omitting scenic spots, reducing tourism service items, lowering tourism service standards, or other similar acts, and the tourist requests the tourism business operator to compensate for reasonable expenses such as for the unfulfilled agreed tourism service items, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where a tourism business operator commits fraud when providing services, and the tourist requests the tourism business operator to pay double compensation for the losses suffered, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　因飞机、火车、班轮、城际客运班车等公共客运交通工具延误，导致合同不能按照约定履行，旅游者请求旅游经营者退还未实际发生的费用的，人民法院应予支持。合同另有约定的除外。

**Article 18** Where the contract cannot be performed as agreed due to delays in public passenger transport vehicles such as aircraft, trains, liners, or intercity passenger buses, and the tourist requests the tourism business operator to refund expenses that have not actually been incurred, the People's Court shall uphold such request, except where the contract provides otherwise.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　旅游者在自行安排活动期间遭受人身损害、财产损失，旅游经营者未尽到必要的提示义务、救助义务，旅游者请求旅游经营者承担相应责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
前款规定的自行安排活动期间，包括旅游经营者安排的在旅游行程中独立的自由活动期间、旅游者不参加旅游行程的活动期间以及旅游者经导游或者领队同意暂时离队的个人活动期间等。

**Article 19** Where a tourist suffers personal injury or property damage during the period of self-arranged activities, and the tourism business operator fails to fulfill the necessary duty of warning or rescue, and the tourist requests the tourism business operator to bear corresponding liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
The period of self-arranged activities as provided in the preceding paragraph includes the independent free activity period arranged by the tourism business operator during the tour itinerary, the activity period during which the tourist does not participate in the tour itinerary, and the personal activity period during which the tourist temporarily leaves the group with the consent of the tour guide or leader.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　旅游者在旅游行程中未经导游或者领队许可，故意脱离团队，遭受人身损害、财产损失，请求旅游经营者赔偿损失的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 20** Where a tourist, without the permission of the tour guide or leader, intentionally leaves the group during the tour itinerary and suffers personal injury or property damage, and requests the tourism business operator to compensate for the losses, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　旅游者提起违约之诉，主张精神损害赔偿的，人民法院应告知其变更为侵权之诉；旅游者仍坚持提起违约之诉的，对于其精神损害赔偿的主张，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 21** Where a tourist files a lawsuit for breach of contract and claims compensation for mental damages, the People's Court shall inform the tourist to change the lawsuit to a tort action; if the tourist still insists on filing a lawsuit for breach of contract, the People's Court shall not uphold the claim for compensation for mental damages.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　旅游经营者或者旅游辅助服务者为旅游者代管的行李物品损毁、灭失，旅游者请求赔偿损失的，人民法院应予支持，但下列情形除外：  
（一）损失是由于旅游者未听从旅游经营者或者旅游辅助服务者的事先声明或者提示，未将现金、有价证券、贵重物品由其随身携带而造成的；  
（二）损失是由于不可抗力、意外事件造成的；  
（三）损失是由于旅游者的过错造成的；  
（四）损失是由于物品的自然属性造成的。

**Article 22** Where luggage or articles kept by a tourism business operator or a tourism auxiliary service provider on behalf of a tourist are damaged or lost, and the tourist requests compensation for the losses, the People's Court shall uphold such request, except in the following circumstances:  
(1) The loss is caused by the tourist's failure to follow the prior statement or notice of the tourism business operator or tourism auxiliary service provider by not carrying cash, negotiable securities, or valuables on his/her person;  
(2) The loss is caused by force majeure or an accident;  
(3) The loss is caused by the fault of the tourist; or  
(4) The loss is caused by the natural properties of the articles.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　旅游者要求旅游经营者返还下列费用的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）因拒绝旅游经营者安排的购物活动或者另行付费的项目被增收的费用；  
（二）在同一旅游行程中，旅游经营者提供相同服务，因旅游者的年龄、职业等差异而增收的费用。

**Article 23** Where a tourist requests the tourism business operator to refund the following expenses, the People's Court shall uphold such request:  
(1) Expenses additionally charged due to the tourist's refusal to participate in shopping activities or separately paid items arranged by the tourism business operator; or  
(2) In the same tour itinerary, expenses additionally charged due to differences in the tourist's age, occupation, or the like, where the tourism business operator provides the same services.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　旅游经营者因过错致其代办的手续、证件存在瑕疵，或者未尽妥善保管义务而遗失、毁损，旅游者请求旅游经营者补办或者协助补办相关手续、证件并承担相应费用的，人民法院应予支持。  
因上述行为影响旅游行程，旅游者请求旅游经营者退还尚未发生的费用、赔偿损失的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 24** Where a tourism business operator, due to its fault, causes defects in the procedures or documents it handles on behalf of the tourist, or fails to fulfill the duty of proper custody, resulting in loss or damage, and the tourist requests the tourism business operator to re-handle or assist in re-handling the relevant procedures or documents and bear the corresponding expenses, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
If the aforesaid acts affect the tour itinerary, and the tourist requests the tourism business operator to refund expenses not yet incurred and compensate for losses, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　旅游经营者事先设计，并以确定的总价提供交通、住宿、游览等一项或者多项服务，不提供导游和领队服务，由旅游者自行安排游览行程的旅游过程中，旅游经营者提供的服务不符合合同约定，侵害旅游者合法权益，旅游者请求旅游经营者承担相应责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
旅游者在自行安排的旅游活动中合法权益受到侵害，请求旅游经营者、旅游辅助服务者承担责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 25** Where a tourism business operator pre-designs and provides one or more services such as transportation, accommodation, and sightseeing at a fixed total price, without providing tour guide and leader services, and the tourist arranges the tour itinerary on his/her own, if the services provided by the tourism business operator do not conform to the contract and infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of the tourist, and the tourist requests the tourism business operator to bear corresponding liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
If the lawful rights and interests of the tourist are infringed upon during the self-arranged tourism activities, and the tourist requests the tourism business operator or tourism auxiliary service provider to bear liability, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本规定施行前已经终审，本规定施行后当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本规定。

**Article 26** These Provisions shall not apply to cases that have been finally adjudicated before these Provisions come into force, and for which a party applies for a retrial or a case is decided to be retried pursuant to the trial supervision procedure after these Provisions come into force.
