# 最高人民法院关于审理因垄断行为引发的民事纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2012〕5号  
**公布日期**：2012-01-30  
**生效日期**：2012-06-01

> 最高人民法院《关于审理因垄断行为引发的民事纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的规定》已于2012年1月30日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1539次会议通过，现予公布，自2012年6月1日起施行。 2012年5月3日 最高人民法院 关于审理因垄断行为引发的民事纠纷案件 应用法律若干问题的规定 法释〔2012〕5号 为正确审理因垄断行为引发的民事纠纷案件，制止垄断行为，保护和促进市场公平竞争，维护消费者利益和社会公共利益，根据《中华人民共和国反垄断法》、《中华人民共和国侵权责任法》、《中华人民共和国合同法》和《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律的相关规定，制定本规定。 第一条  本规定所称因垄断行为引发的民事纠纷案件（以下简称垄断民事纠纷案件），是指因垄断行为受到损失以及因合同内容、行业协会的章程等违反反垄断法而发生争议的自然人、法人或者其他组织，向人民法院提起的民事诉讼案件。 第二条  原告直接向人民法院提起民事诉讼，或者在反垄断执法机构认定构成垄断行为的处理决定发生法律效力后向人民法院提起民事诉讼，并符合法律规定的其他受理条件的，人民法院应当受理。 第三条  第一审垄断民事纠纷案件，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府所在地的市、计划单列市中级人民法院以及最高人民法院指定的中级人民法院管辖。 经最高人民法院批准，基层人民法院可以管辖第一审垄断民事纠纷案件。 第四条  垄断民事纠纷案件的地域管辖，根据案件具体情况，依照民事诉讼法及相关司法解释有关侵权纠纷、合同纠纷等的管辖规定确定。 第五条  民事纠纷案件立案时的案由并非垄断纠纷，被告以原告实施了垄断行为为由提出抗辩或者反诉且有证据支持，或者案件需要依据反垄断法作出裁判，但受诉人民法院没有垄断民事纠纷案件管辖权的，应当将案件移送有管辖权的人民法院。 第六条  两个或者两个以上原告因同一垄断行为向有管辖权的同一法院分别提起诉讼的，人民法院可以合并审理。 两个或者两个以上原告因同一垄断行为向有管辖权的不同法院分别提起诉讼的，后立案的法院在得知有关法院先立案的情况后，应当在七日内裁定将案件移送先立案的法院；受移送的法院可以合并审理。被告应当在答辩阶段主动向受诉人民法院提供其因同一行为在其他法院涉诉的相关信息。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  本规定所称因垄断行为引发的民事纠纷案件（以下简称垄断民事纠纷案件），是指因垄断行为受到损失以及因合同内容、行业协会的章程等违反反垄断法而发生争议的自然人、法人或者其他组织，向人民法院提起的民事诉讼案件。

**Article 1** For the purposes of these Provisions, a civil dispute case arising from monopoly conduct (hereinafter referred to as a monopoly civil dispute case) refers to a civil action brought before the People's Court by a natural person, legal person, or any other organization that has suffered losses due to monopoly conduct or has a dispute arising from the content of a contract, the articles of association of an industry association, or other matters that violate the Anti-Monopoly Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  原告直接向人民法院提起民事诉讼，或者在反垄断执法机构认定构成垄断行为的处理决定发生法律效力后向人民法院提起民事诉讼，并符合法律规定的其他受理条件的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 2** Where a plaintiff directly brings a civil action before the People's Court, or brings a civil action before the People's Court after the decision of the anti-monopoly enforcement authority determining that the conduct constitutes a monopoly has taken legal effect, and meets other conditions for acceptance prescribed by law, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  第一审垄断民事纠纷案件，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府所在地的市、计划单列市中级人民法院以及最高人民法院指定的中级人民法院管辖。  
经最高人民法院批准，基层人民法院可以管辖第一审垄断民事纠纷案件。

**Article 3** A first instance monopoly civil dispute case shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court of the city where the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government is located, the Intermediate People's Court of a city with independent planning status, or an Intermediate People's Court designated by the Supreme People's Court.  
With the approval of the Supreme People's Court, a Primary People's Court may have jurisdiction over a first instance monopoly civil dispute case.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  垄断民事纠纷案件的地域管辖，根据案件具体情况，依照民事诉讼法及相关司法解释有关侵权纠纷、合同纠纷等的管辖规定确定。

**Article 4** The territorial jurisdiction over a monopoly civil dispute case shall be determined in light of the specific circumstances of the case and in accordance with the provisions on jurisdiction concerning tort disputes, contract disputes, etc. in the Civil Procedure Law and relevant judicial interpretations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  民事纠纷案件立案时的案由并非垄断纠纷，被告以原告实施了垄断行为为由提出抗辩或者反诉且有证据支持，或者案件需要依据反垄断法作出裁判，但受诉人民法院没有垄断民事纠纷案件管辖权的，应当将案件移送有管辖权的人民法院。

**Article 5** Where the cause of action at the time of filing a civil dispute case is not a monopoly dispute, but the defendant, supported by evidence, raises a defense or counterclaim on the ground that the plaintiff has committed a monopoly act, or the case requires a judgment to be rendered in accordance with the Anti-Monopoly Law, and the People's Court hearing the case does not have jurisdiction over monopoly civil dispute cases, the court shall transfer the case to a People's Court that has jurisdiction.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  两个或者两个以上原告因同一垄断行为向有管辖权的同一法院分别提起诉讼的，人民法院可以合并审理。  
两个或者两个以上原告因同一垄断行为向有管辖权的不同法院分别提起诉讼的，后立案的法院在得知有关法院先立案的情况后，应当在七日内裁定将案件移送先立案的法院；受移送的法院可以合并审理。被告应当在答辩阶段主动向受诉人民法院提供其因同一行为在其他法院涉诉的相关信息。

**Article 6** Where two or more plaintiffs separately bring actions before the same competent court for the same monopoly conduct, the People's Court may consolidate the trials.  
Where two or more plaintiffs separately bring actions before different competent courts for the same monopoly conduct, the court that later files the case shall, within seven days after learning that a court has earlier filed the case, rule to transfer the case to the court that earlier filed the case; the court to which the case is transferred may consolidate the trials. The defendant shall voluntarily provide the relevant information on the actions it has been involved in with other courts regarding the same conduct to the People's Court hearing the case during the defense stage.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  被诉垄断行为属于[反垄断法第十三条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-13)第一款第（一）项至第（五）项规定的垄断协议的，被告应对该协议不具有排除、限制竞争的效果承担举证责任。

**Article 7** Where the alleged monopoly conduct is a monopoly agreement as specified in Items (1) to (5) of Paragraph 1 of Article 13 of the Anti-Monopoly Law, the defendant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that the agreement does not have the effect of eliminating or restricting competition.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  被诉垄断行为属于[反垄断法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反垄断法.md#art-17)第一款规定的滥用市场支配地位的，原告应当对被告在相关市场内具有支配地位和其滥用市场支配地位承担举证责任。  
被告以其行为具有正当性为由进行抗辩的，应当承担举证责任。

**Article 8** Where the alleged monopoly conduct constitutes abuse of market dominance as specified in Paragraph 1 of Article 17 of the Anti-Monopoly Law, the plaintiff shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that the defendant has a dominant market position in the relevant market and has abused its dominant market position.  
Where the defendant raises a defense on the ground of justification of its conduct, the defendant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  被诉垄断行为属于公用企业或者其他依法具有独占地位的经营者滥用市场支配地位的，人民法院可以根据市场结构和竞争状况的具体情况，认定被告在相关市场内具有支配地位，但有相反证据足以推翻的除外。

**Article 9** Where the alleged monopoly conduct constitutes abuse of market dominance by a public utility enterprise or any other operator with a monopolistic status according to law, the People's Court may, in light of the specific market structure and competitive conditions, determine that the defendant has a dominant market position in the relevant market, unless there is sufficient evidence to the contrary.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  原告可以以被告对外发布的信息作为证明其具有市场支配地位的证据。被告对外发布的信息能够证明其在相关市场内具有支配地位的，人民法院可以据此作出认定，但有相反证据足以推翻的除外。

**Article 10** The plaintiff may use information published by the defendant as evidence to prove that the defendant has a dominant market position. Where the information published by the defendant can prove that the defendant has a dominant market position in the relevant market, the People's Court may make a determination accordingly, unless there is sufficient evidence to the contrary.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  证据涉及国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私或者其他依法应当保密的内容的，人民法院可以依职权或者当事人的申请采取不公开开庭、限制或者禁止复制、仅对代理律师展示、责令签署保密承诺书等保护措施。

**Article 11** Where evidence involves State secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, or other content that shall be kept confidential according to law, the People's Court may, ex officio or upon the application of a party, take protective measures such as holding a hearing in camera, restricting or prohibiting reproduction, showing the evidence only to the attorney, or ordering the signing of a confidentiality undertaking.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  当事人可以向人民法院申请一至二名具有相应专门知识的人员出庭，就案件的专门性问题进行说明。

**Article 12** A party may apply to the People's Court for one or two persons with relevant expertise to appear in court to provide explanations on specialized issues of the case.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  当事人可以向人民法院申请委托专业机构或者专业人员就案件的专门性问题作出市场调查或者经济分析报告。经人民法院同意，双方当事人可以协商确定专业机构或者专业人员；协商不成的，由人民法院指定。  
人民法院可以参照民事诉讼法及相关司法解释有关鉴定结论的规定，对前款规定的市场调查或者经济分析报告进行审查判断。

**Article 13** A party may apply to the People's Court for entrusting a specialized institution or professional to prepare a market survey or economic analysis report on specialized issues of the case. With the consent of the People's Court, the parties may negotiate to determine the specialized institution or professional; if no agreement is reached, the People's Court shall designate one.  
The People's Court may, by reference to the provisions on expert evaluation conclusions in the Civil Procedure Law and relevant judicial interpretations, examine and determine the market survey or economic analysis report as specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  被告实施垄断行为，给原告造成损失的，根据原告的诉讼请求和查明的事实，人民法院可以依法判令被告承担停止侵害、赔偿损失等民事责任。  
根据原告的请求，人民法院可以将原告因调查、制止垄断行为所支付的合理开支计入损失赔偿范围。

**Article 14** Where the defendant commits a monopoly act and causes losses to the plaintiff, the People's Court may, in accordance with the plaintiff's claims and the facts ascertained, order the defendant to bear civil liability such as cessation of infringement and compensation for losses.  
Upon the request of the plaintiff, the People's Court may include the reasonable expenses incurred by the plaintiff in investigating and stopping the monopoly act in the scope of compensation for losses.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  被诉合同内容、行业协会的章程等违反反垄断法或者其他法律、行政法规的强制性规定的，人民法院应当依法认定其无效。

**Article 15** Where the content of the alleged contract, the articles of association of an industry association, or the like violate the mandatory provisions of the Anti-Monopoly Law or other laws or administrative regulations, the People's Court shall determine them to be invalid according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  因垄断行为产生的损害赔偿请求权诉讼时效期间，从原告知道或者应当知道权益受侵害之日起计算。  
原告向反垄断执法机构举报被诉垄断行为的，诉讼时效从其举报之日起中断。反垄断执法机构决定不立案、撤销案件或者决定终止调查的，诉讼时效期间从原告知道或者应当知道不立案、撤销案件或者终止调查之日起重新计算。反垄断执法机构调查后认定构成垄断行为的，诉讼时效期间从原告知道或者应当知道反垄断执法机构认定构成垄断行为的处理决定发生法律效力之日起重新计算。  
原告起诉时被诉垄断行为已经持续超过二年，被告提出诉讼时效抗辩的，损害赔偿应当自原告向人民法院起诉之日起向前推算二年计算。

**Article 16** The limitation of action for the right to claim damages arising from monopoly conduct shall be counted from the date on which the plaintiff knows or should have known that its rights and interests have been infringed upon.  
Where the plaintiff reports the alleged monopoly conduct to the anti-monopoly enforcement authority, the limitation of action shall be interrupted from the date of the report. Where the anti-monopoly enforcement authority decides not to file a case, withdraws the case, or decides to terminate the investigation, the limitation of action shall be recalculated from the date on which the plaintiff knows or should have known of the decision not to file a case, the withdrawal of the case, or the termination of the investigation. Where the anti-monopoly enforcement authority determines after investigation that the conduct constitutes a monopoly, the limitation of action shall be recalculated from the date on which the plaintiff knows or should have known that the decision of the anti-monopoly enforcement authority determining the conduct as a monopoly has taken legal effect.  
Where the alleged monopoly conduct has continued for more than two years at the time the plaintiff brings the action, and the defendant raises a defense of limitation of action, the damages shall be calculated by counting back two years from the date on which the plaintiff filed the action with the People's Court.
