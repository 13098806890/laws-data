# 最高人民法院关于审理无正本提单交付货物案件适用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2009〕1号  
**公布日期**：2009-02-26  
**生效日期**：2009-03-05

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所称正本提单包括记名提单、指示提单和不记名提单。

**Article 1** For the purpose of these Provisions, an original bill of lading includes a straight bill of lading, an order bill of lading, and a bearer bill of lading.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　承运人违反法律规定，无正本提单交付货物，损害正本提单持有人提单权利的，正本提单持有人可以要求承运人承担由此造成损失的民事责任。

**Article 2** Where a carrier, in violation of legal provisions, delivers goods without an original bill of lading, thereby harming the bill of lading rights of the holder of the original bill of lading, the holder of the original bill of lading may request the carrier to bear civil liability for the losses caused thereby.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　承运人因无正本提单交付货物造成正本提单持有人损失的，正本提单持有人可以要求承运人承担违约责任，或者承担侵权责任。  
正本提单持有人要求承运人承担无正本提单交付货物民事责任的，适用海商法规定；海商法没有规定的，适用其他法律规定。

**Article 3** Where a carrier causes loss to the holder of an original bill of lading by delivering goods without an original bill of lading, the holder of the original bill of lading may request the carrier to bear liability for breach of contract, or to bear tort liability.  
Where the holder of an original bill of lading requests the carrier to bear civil liability for delivering goods without an original bill of lading, the provisions of the Maritime Law shall apply; where the Maritime Law has no provisions, other legal provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　承运人因无正本提单交付货物承担民事责任的，不适用[海商法第五十六条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-56)关于限制赔偿责任的规定。

**Article 4** Where a carrier bears civil liability for delivering goods without an original bill of lading, the provisions of Article 56 of the Maritime Law on limitation of liability shall not apply.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　提货人凭伪造的提单向承运人提取了货物，持有正本提单的收货人可以要求承运人承担无正本提单交付货物的民事责任。

**Article 5** Where the person taking delivery of the goods has taken delivery of the goods from the carrier on the basis of a forged bill of lading, the consignee holding the original bill of lading may request the carrier to bear civil liability for delivering goods without an original bill of lading.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　承运人因无正本提单交付货物造成正本提单持有人损失的赔偿额，按照货物装船时的价值加运费和保险费计算。

**Article 6** The amount of compensation for loss caused to the holder of an original bill of lading by the carrier's delivery of goods without an original bill of lading shall be calculated according to the value of the goods at the time of shipment plus freight and insurance premium.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　承运人依照提单载明的卸货港所在地法律规定，必须将承运到港的货物交付给当地海关或者港口当局的，不承担无正本提单交付货物的民事责任。

**Article 7** Where a carrier, in accordance with the legal provisions of the place of the discharge port as indicated in the bill of lading, must deliver the goods carried to the port to the local customs or port authorities, the carrier shall not bear civil liability for delivering goods without an original bill of lading.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　承运到港的货物超过法律规定期限无人向海关申报，被海关提取并依法变卖处理，或者法院依法裁定拍卖承运人留置的货物，承运人主张免除交付货物责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 8** Where goods carried to the port are not declared to customs within the statutory time limit, and are taken by customs and sold off according to law, or where the court rules according to law to auction the goods retained by the carrier, and the carrier claims exemption from liability for delivery of the goods, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　承运人按照记名提单托运人的要求中止运输、返还货物、变更到达地或者将货物交给其他收货人，持有记名提单的收货人要求承运人承担无正本提单交付货物民事责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 9** Where a carrier suspends carriage, returns goods, changes the place of destination, or delivers the goods to another consignee at the request of the shipper named in a straight bill of lading, and the consignee holding the straight bill of lading requests the carrier to bear civil liability for delivering goods without an original bill of lading, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　承运人签发一式数份正本提单，向最先提交正本提单的人交付货物后，其他持有相同正本提单的人要求承运人承担无正本提单交付货物民事责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 10** Where a carrier issues multiple original bills of lading in one set and delivers the goods to the person who first presents an original bill of lading, and other persons holding the same original bills of lading request the carrier to bear civil liability for delivering goods without an original bill of lading, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　正本提单持有人可以要求无正本提单交付货物的承运人与无正本提单提取货物的人承担连带赔偿责任。

**Article 11** A holder of an original bill of lading may request the carrier who delivered goods without an original bill of lading and the person who took delivery of goods without an original bill of lading to bear joint and several liability for compensation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　向承运人实际交付货物并持有指示提单的托运人，虽然在正本提单上没有载明其托运人身份，因承运人无正本提单交付货物，要求承运人依据海上货物运输合同承担无正本提单交付货物民事责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 12** Where a shipper who actually delivered the goods to the carrier and holds an order bill of lading, even if his identity as shipper is not stated on the original bill of lading, requests the carrier to bear civil liability for delivering goods without an original bill of lading under the contract of carriage of goods by sea due to the carrier's delivery of goods without an original bill of lading, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　在承运人未凭正本提单交付货物后，正本提单持有人与无正本提单提取货物的人就货款支付达成协议，在协议款项得不到赔付时，不影响正本提单持有人就其遭受的损失，要求承运人承担无正本提单交付货物的民事责任。

**Article 13** After the carrier delivers goods without an original bill of lading, if the holder of the original bill of lading reaches an agreement with the person who took delivery of goods without an original bill of lading on payment of the price of the goods, and the amount under the agreement is not paid, this shall not affect the holder of the original bill of lading's right to request the carrier to bear civil liability for delivering goods without an original bill of lading in respect of the losses sustained.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　正本提单持有人以承运人无正本提单交付货物为由提起的诉讼，适用[海商法第二百五十七条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-257)的规定，时效期间为一年，自承运人应当交付货物之日起计算。  
正本提单持有人以承运人与无正本提单提取货物的人共同实施无正本提单交付货物行为为由提起的侵权诉讼，诉讼时效适用本条前款规定。

**Article 14** Where a holder of an original bill of lading institutes an action on the ground that the carrier delivered goods without an original bill of lading, the provisions of Article 257 of the Maritime Law shall apply, and the limitation period shall be one year, commencing from the date on which the carrier should have delivered the goods.  
Where a holder of an original bill of lading institutes an action in tort on the ground that the carrier and the person who took delivery of goods without an original bill of lading jointly committed the act of delivering goods without an original bill of lading, the limitation of action shall be governed by the provisions of the preceding paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　正本提单持有人以承运人无正本提单交付货物为由提起的诉讼，时效中断适用[海商法第二百六十七条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-267)的规定。  
正本提单持有人以承运人与无正本提单提取货物的人共同实施无正本提单交付货物行为为由提起的侵权诉讼，时效中断适用本条前款规定。

**Article 15** Where a holder of an original bill of lading institutes an action on the ground that the carrier delivered goods without an original bill of lading, the interruption of the limitation period shall be governed by the provisions of Article 267 of the Maritime Law.  
Where a holder of an original bill of lading institutes an action in tort on the ground that the carrier and the person who took delivery of goods without an original bill of lading jointly committed the act of delivering goods without an original bill of lading, the interruption of the limitation period shall be governed by the provisions of the preceding paragraph of this Article.
