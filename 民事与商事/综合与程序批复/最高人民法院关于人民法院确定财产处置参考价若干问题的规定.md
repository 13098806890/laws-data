# 最高人民法院关于人民法院确定财产处置参考价若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2018〕15号  
**公布日期**：2018-06-04  
**生效日期**：2018-09-01

> 最高人民法院《关于人民法院确定财产处置参考价若干问题的规定》已于2018年6月4日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1741次会议通过，现予公布，自2018年9月1日起施行。 2018年8月28日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院确定财产处置参考价 若干问题的规定 法释〔2018〕15号 为公平、公正、高效确定财产处置参考价，维护当事人、利害关系人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律规定，结合人民法院工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条　人民法院查封、扣押、冻结财产后，对需要拍卖、变卖的财产，应当在三十日内启动确定财产处置参考价程序。 第二条　人民法院确定财产处置参考价，可以采取当事人议价、定向询价、网络询价、委托评估等方式。 第三条　人民法院确定参考价前，应当查明财产的权属、权利负担、占有使用、欠缴税费、质量瑕疵等事项。 人民法院查明前款规定事项需要当事人、有关单位或者个人提供相关资料的，可以通知其提交；拒不提交的，可以强制提取；对妨碍强制提取的，参照民事诉讼法第一百一十一条、第一百一十四条的规定处理。 查明本条第一款规定事项需要审计、鉴定的，人民法院可以先行审计、鉴定。 第四条　采取当事人议价方式确定参考价的，除一方当事人拒绝议价或者下落不明外，人民法院应当以适当的方式通知或者组织当事人进行协商，当事人应当在指定期限内提交议价结果。 双方当事人提交的议价结果一致，且不损害他人合法权益的，议价结果为参考价。 第五条　当事人议价不能或者不成，且财产有计税基准价、政府定价或者政府指导价的，人民法院应当向确定参考价时财产所在地的有关机构进行定向询价。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院查封、扣押、冻结财产后，对需要拍卖、变卖的财产，应当在三十日内启动确定财产处置参考价程序。

**Article 1** After a People's Court has sealed up, distrained, or frozen property, it shall commence the procedure for determining the reference price for disposal of the property within thirty days for property that needs to be auctioned or sold.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院确定财产处置参考价，可以采取当事人议价、定向询价、网络询价、委托评估等方式。

**Article 2** When a People's Court determines the reference price for disposal of property, it may adopt methods such as negotiation between the parties, targeted inquiry, online inquiry, or commissioned appraisal.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院确定参考价前，应当查明财产的权属、权利负担、占有使用、欠缴税费、质量瑕疵等事项。  
人民法院查明前款规定事项需要当事人、有关单位或者个人提供相关资料的，可以通知其提交；拒不提交的，可以强制提取；对妨碍强制提取的，参照[民事诉讼法第一百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-111)、第一百一十四条的规定处理。  
查明本条第一款规定事项需要审计、鉴定的，人民法院可以先行审计、鉴定。

**Article 3** Before determining the reference price, a People's Court shall ascertain matters such as the ownership, encumbrances, possession and use, overdue taxes and fees, and quality defects of the property.  
If a People's Court needs a party, relevant entity, or individual to provide relevant materials to ascertain the matters as specified in the preceding paragraph, it may notify them to submit such materials; if they refuse to submit, it may compulsorily extract them; for obstruction of compulsory extraction, it shall handle the matter by reference to the provisions of Articles 111 and 114 of the Civil Procedure Law.  
If auditing or expert evaluation is needed to ascertain the matters as specified in paragraph 1 of this Article, the People's Court may first conduct auditing or expert evaluation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　采取当事人议价方式确定参考价的，除一方当事人拒绝议价或者下落不明外，人民法院应当以适当的方式通知或者组织当事人进行协商，当事人应当在指定期限内提交议价结果。  
双方当事人提交的议价结果一致，且不损害他人合法权益的，议价结果为参考价。

**Article 4** Where the method of negotiation between the parties is adopted to determine the reference price, except where one party refuses to negotiate or its whereabouts are unknown, the People's Court shall notify or organize the parties to negotiate by appropriate means, and the parties shall submit the result of negotiation within the specified time limit.  
If the negotiation results submitted by both parties are consistent and do not harm the lawful rights and interests of others, the negotiation result shall be the reference price.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　当事人议价不能或者不成，且财产有计税基准价、政府定价或者政府指导价的，人民法院应当向确定参考价时财产所在地的有关机构进行定向询价。  
双方当事人一致要求直接进行定向询价，且财产有计税基准价、政府定价或者政府指导价的，人民法院应当准许。

**Article 5** If negotiation between the parties is impossible or unsuccessful, and the property has a tax base price, government-fixed price, or government-guided price, the People's Court shall conduct a targeted inquiry to the relevant institution at the location of the property at the time of determining the reference price.  
If both parties unanimously request direct targeted inquiry and the property has a tax base price, government-fixed price, or government-guided price, the People's Court shall permit it.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　采取定向询价方式确定参考价的，人民法院应当向有关机构出具询价函，询价函应当载明询价要求、完成期限等内容。  
接受定向询价的机构在指定期限内出具的询价结果为参考价。

**Article 6** Where the method of targeted inquiry is adopted to determine the reference price, the People's Court shall issue a letter of inquiry to the relevant institution, and the letter of inquiry shall state the inquiry requirements, completion deadline, and other contents.  
The result of the inquiry issued by the institution accepting the targeted inquiry within the specified time limit shall be the reference price.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　定向询价不能或者不成，财产无需由专业人员现场勘验或者鉴定，且具备网络询价条件的，人民法院应当通过司法网络询价平台进行网络询价。  
双方当事人一致要求或者同意直接进行网络询价，财产无需由专业人员现场勘验或者鉴定，且具备网络询价条件的，人民法院应当准许。

**Article 7** If targeted inquiry is impossible or unsuccessful, the property does not require on-site inspection or expert evaluation by professionals, and the conditions for online inquiry are met, the People's Court shall conduct online inquiry through a judicial online inquiry platform.  
If both parties unanimously request or agree to conduct online inquiry directly, and the property does not require on-site inspection or expert evaluation by professionals, and the conditions for online inquiry are met, the People's Court shall permit it.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　最高人民法院建立全国性司法网络询价平台名单库。  
司法网络询价平台应当同时符合下列条件：  
（一）具备能够依法开展互联网信息服务工作的资质；  
（二）能够合法获取并整合全国各地区同种类财产一定时期的既往成交价、政府定价、政府指导价或者市场公开交易价等不少于三类价格数据，并保证数据真实、准确；  
（三）能够根据数据化财产特征，运用一定的运算规则对市场既往交易价格、交易趋势予以分析；  
（四）程序运行规范、系统安全高效、服务质优价廉；  
（五）能够全程记载数据的分析过程，将形成的电子数据完整保存不少于十年，但法律、行政法规、司法解释另有规定的除外。

**Article 8** The Supreme People's Court shall establish a nationwide list database of judicial online inquiry platforms.  
A judicial online inquiry platform shall simultaneously meet the following conditions:  
(1) It possesses the qualifications to lawfully carry out Internet information services;  
(2) It can lawfully obtain and integrate at least three categories of price data, including historical transaction prices, government-fixed prices, government-guided prices, or public market transaction prices for the same type of property in various regions across the country over a certain period, and ensure the data are authentic and accurate;  
(3) It can analyze historical market transaction prices and trends using certain operational rules based on the digital characteristics of the property;  
(4) The procedural operation is standard, the system is safe and efficient, and the service is of high quality and low cost;  
(5) It can record the entire data analysis process and fully preserve the formed electronic data for no less than ten years, except as otherwise provided by laws, administrative regulations, or judicial interpretations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　最高人民法院组成专门的评审委员会，负责司法网络询价平台的选定、评审和除名。每年引入权威第三方对已纳入和新申请纳入名单库的司法网络询价平台予以评审并公布结果。  
司法网络询价平台具有下列情形之一的，应当将其从名单库中除名：  
（一）无正当理由拒绝进行网络询价；  
（二）无正当理由一年内累计五次未按期完成网络询价；  
（三）存在恶意串通、弄虚作假、泄露保密信息等行为；  
（四）经权威第三方评审认定不符合提供网络询价服务条件；  
（五）存在其他违反询价规则以及法律、行政法规、司法解释规定的情形。  
司法网络询价平台被除名后，五年内不得被纳入名单库。

**Article 9** The Supreme People's Court shall form a specialized evaluation committee responsible for the selection, evaluation, and removal of judicial online inquiry platforms. Each year, it shall introduce an authoritative third party to evaluate the judicial online inquiry platforms already included in and newly applying for inclusion in the list database, and publish the results.  
If a judicial online inquiry platform falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be removed from the list database:  
(1) It refuses to conduct online inquiry without justifiable reasons;  
(2) Without justifiable reasons, it fails to complete online inquiry on time for a cumulative total of five times within one year;  
(3) It engages in malicious collusion, fraud, disclosure of confidential information, or other similar acts;  
(4) It is determined by authoritative third-party evaluation to no longer meet the conditions for providing online inquiry services;  
(5) It has other circumstances that violate the inquiry rules and the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, or judicial interpretations.  
After a judicial online inquiry platform is removed, it shall not be included in the list database within five years.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　采取网络询价方式确定参考价的，人民法院应当同时向名单库中的全部司法网络询价平台发出网络询价委托书。网络询价委托书应当载明财产名称、物理特征、规格数量、目的要求、完成期限以及其他需要明确的内容等。

**Article 10** Where the method of online inquiry is adopted to determine the reference price, the People's Court shall simultaneously issue a letter of entrustment for online inquiry to all judicial online inquiry platforms in the list database. The letter of entrustment for online inquiry shall state the name, physical characteristics, specifications and quantity, purpose and requirements, completion deadline, and other contents that need to be clarified, etc.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　司法网络询价平台应当在收到人民法院网络询价委托书之日起三日内出具网络询价报告。网络询价报告应当载明财产的基本情况、参照样本、计算方法、询价结果及有效期等内容。  
司法网络询价平台不能在期限内完成询价的，应当在期限届满前申请延长期限。全部司法网络询价平台均未能在期限内出具询价结果的，人民法院应当根据各司法网络询价平台的延期申请延期三日；部分司法网络询价平台在期限内出具网络询价结果的，人民法院对其他司法网络询价平台的延期申请不予准许。  
全部司法网络询价平台均未在期限内出具或者补正网络询价报告，且未按照规定申请延长期限的，人民法院应当委托评估机构进行评估。  
人民法院未在网络询价结果有效期内发布一拍拍卖公告或者直接进入变卖程序的，应当通知司法网络询价平台在三日内重新出具网络询价报告。

**Article 11** A judicial online inquiry platform shall issue an online inquiry report within three days from the date of receiving the letter of entrustment for online inquiry from the People's Court. The online inquiry report shall state the basic information, reference samples, calculation method, inquiry result, and validity period of the property, etc.  
If a judicial online inquiry platform cannot complete the inquiry within the time limit, it shall apply for an extension before the expiration of the time limit. If all judicial online inquiry platforms fail to issue inquiry results within the time limit, the People's Court shall grant a three-day extension based on the extension applications of each judicial online inquiry platform; if some judicial online inquiry platforms issue online inquiry results within the time limit, the People's Court shall not grant the extension applications of the other judicial online inquiry platforms.  
If all judicial online inquiry platforms fail to issue or correct the online inquiry report within the time limit and fail to apply for an extension in accordance with the provisions, the People's Court shall entrust an appraisal institution to conduct an appraisal.  
If the People's Court does not publish the first auction announcement or directly enter the sale procedure within the validity period of the online inquiry result, it shall notify the judicial online inquiry platform to re-issue an online inquiry report within three days.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院应当对网络询价报告进行审查。网络询价报告均存在财产基本信息错误、超出财产范围或者遗漏财产等情形的，应当通知司法网络询价平台在三日内予以补正；部分网络询价报告不存在上述情形的，无需通知其他司法网络询价平台补正。

**Article 12** The People's Court shall review the online inquiry reports. If all online inquiry reports have errors in basic property information, exceed the scope of the property, or omit the property, etc., it shall notify the judicial online inquiry platforms to make corrections within three days; if some online inquiry reports do not have the above circumstances, there is no need to notify other judicial online inquiry platforms to make corrections.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　全部司法网络询价平台均在期限内出具询价结果或者补正结果的，人民法院应当以全部司法网络询价平台出具结果的平均值为参考价；部分司法网络询价平台在期限内出具询价结果或者补正结果的，人民法院应当以该部分司法网络询价平台出具结果的平均值为参考价。  
当事人、利害关系人依据[本规定第二十二条](#art-22)的规定对全部网络询价报告均提出异议，且所提异议被驳回或者司法网络询价平台已作出补正的，人民法院应当以异议被驳回或者已作出补正的各司法网络询价平台出具结果的平均值为参考价；对部分网络询价报告提出异议的，人民法院应当以网络询价报告未被提出异议的各司法网络询价平台出具结果的平均值为参考价。

**Article 13** If all judicial online inquiry platforms issue inquiry results or correction results within the time limit, the People's Court shall take the average of the results issued by all judicial online inquiry platforms as the reference price; if some judicial online inquiry platforms issue inquiry results or correction results within the time limit, the People's Court shall take the average of the results issued by those judicial online inquiry platforms as the reference price.  
If a party or interested person raises objections to all online inquiry reports pursuant to Article 22 of these Provisions, and the objections raised are rejected or the judicial online inquiry platform has made corrections, the People's Court shall take the average of the results issued by each judicial online inquiry platform whose objections were rejected or which has made corrections as the reference price; if objections are raised against some online inquiry reports, the People's Court shall take the average of the results issued by the judicial online inquiry platforms whose online inquiry reports are not subject to objections as the reference price.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　法律、行政法规规定必须委托评估、双方当事人要求委托评估或者网络询价不能或不成的，人民法院应当委托评估机构进行评估。

**Article 14** If laws or administrative regulations require that appraisal be commissioned, both parties request commissioned appraisal, or online inquiry is impossible or unsuccessful, the People's Court shall entrust an appraisal institution to conduct an appraisal.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　最高人民法院根据全国性评估行业协会推荐的评估机构名单建立人民法院司法评估机构名单库。按评估专业领域和评估机构的执业范围建立名单分库，在分库下根据行政区划设省、市两级名单子库。  
评估机构无正当理由拒绝进行司法评估或者存在弄虚作假等情形的，最高人民法院可以商全国性评估行业协会将其从名单库中除名；除名后五年内不得被纳入名单库。

**Article 15** The Supreme People's Court shall establish a list database of judicial appraisal institutions of the People's Courts based on the list of appraisal institutions recommended by the national appraisal industry association. It shall establish list sub-databases according to appraisal professional fields and the practice scope of appraisal institutions, and under the sub-databases, it shall set up provincial and municipal list sub-databases according to administrative divisions.  
If an appraisal institution refuses to conduct judicial appraisal without justifiable reasons or engages in fraud or other similar circumstances, the Supreme People's Court may, after consulting with the national appraisal industry association, remove it from the list database; after removal, it shall not be included in the list database within five years.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　采取委托评估方式确定参考价的，人民法院应当通知双方当事人在指定期限内从名单分库中协商确定三家评估机构以及顺序；双方当事人在指定期限内协商不成或者一方当事人下落不明的，采取摇号方式在名单分库或者财产所在地的名单子库中随机确定三家评估机构以及顺序。双方当事人一致要求在同一名单子库中随机确定的，人民法院应当准许。

**Article 16** Where the method of commissioned appraisal is adopted to determine the reference price, the People's Court shall notify both parties to negotiate and determine three appraisal institutions and their order from the list sub-database within the specified time limit; if the parties fail to reach an agreement within the specified time limit or one party’s whereabouts are unknown, the method of drawing lots shall be used to randomly determine three appraisal institutions and their order from the list sub-database or the list sub-database at the location of the property. If both parties unanimously request random determination in the same list sub-database, the People's Court shall permit it.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人民法院应当向顺序在先的评估机构出具评估委托书，评估委托书应当载明财产名称、物理特征、规格数量、目的要求、完成期限以及其他需要明确的内容等，同时应当将查明的财产情况及相关材料一并移交给评估机构。  
评估机构应当出具评估报告，评估报告应当载明评估财产的基本情况、评估方法、评估标准、评估结果及有效期等内容。

**Article 17** The People's Court shall issue a letter of entrustment for appraisal to the appraisal institution in the first order, and the letter of entrustment for appraisal shall state the name, physical characteristics, specifications and quantity, purpose and requirements, completion deadline, and other contents that need to be clarified, etc., and shall at the same time transfer the ascertained property information and related materials to the appraisal institution.  
The appraisal institution shall issue an appraisal report, and the appraisal report shall state the basic information, appraisal method, appraisal standards, appraisal result, and validity period of the appraised property, etc.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　评估需要进行现场勘验的，人民法院应当通知当事人到场；当事人不到场的，不影响勘验的进行，但应当有见证人见证。现场勘验需要当事人、协助义务人配合的，人民法院依法责令其配合；不予配合的，可以依法强制进行。

**Article 18** If an appraisal requires on-site inspection, the People's Court shall notify the parties to be present; if the parties are not present, it shall not affect the conduct of the inspection, but there shall be a witness to attest. If on-site inspection requires the cooperation of the parties or persons with duties to assist, the People's Court shall, according to law, order them to cooperate; if they fail to cooperate, it may compulsorily carry out the inspection according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　评估机构应当在三十日内出具评估报告。人民法院决定暂缓或者裁定中止执行的期间，应当从前述期限中扣除。  
评估机构不能在期限内出具评估报告的，应当在期限届满五日前书面向人民法院申请延长期限。人民法院决定延长期限的，延期次数不超过两次，每次不超过十五日。  
评估机构未在期限内出具评估报告、补正说明，且未按照规定申请延长期限的，人民法院应当通知该评估机构三日内将人民法院委托评估时移交的材料退回，另行委托下一顺序的评估机构重新进行评估。  
人民法院未在评估结果有效期内发布一拍拍卖公告或者直接进入变卖程序的，应当通知原评估机构在十五日内重新出具评估报告。

**Article 19** An appraisal institution shall issue an appraisal report within thirty days. The period during which the People's Court decides to suspend or rules to stay enforcement shall be deducted from the aforementioned time limit.  
If the appraisal institution cannot issue an appraisal report within the time limit, it shall apply in writing to the People's Court for an extension no later than five days before the expiration of the time limit. If the People's Court decides to grant an extension, the number of extensions shall not exceed two, and each extension shall not exceed fifteen days.  
If the appraisal institution fails to issue an appraisal report or a corrective explanation within the time limit and fails to apply for an extension in accordance with the provisions, the People's Court shall notify the appraisal institution to return the materials transferred by the People's Court at the time of commissioning the appraisal within three days, and shall separately entrust the next appraisal institution in order to conduct a new appraisal.  
If the People's Court does not publish the first auction announcement or directly enter the sale procedure within the validity period of the appraisal result, it shall notify the original appraisal institution to re-issue an appraisal report within fifteen days.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　人民法院应当对评估报告进行审查。具有下列情形之一的，应当责令评估机构在三日内予以书面说明或者补正：  
（一）财产基本信息错误；  
（二）超出财产范围或者遗漏财产；  
（三）选定的评估机构与评估报告上签章的评估机构不符；  
（四）评估人员执业资格证明与评估报告上署名的人员不符；  
（五）具有其他应当书面说明或者补正的情形。

**Article 20** The People's Court shall review the appraisal report. If it falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall order the appraisal institution to provide a written explanation or make corrections within three days:  
(1) The basic information of the property is erroneous;  
(2) The property is beyond the scope or omitted;  
(3) The selected appraisal institution does not match the appraisal institution that signed and sealed the appraisal report;  
(4) The practice qualification certificate of the appraiser does not match the person named in the appraisal report;  
(5) There are other circumstances that require a written explanation or correction.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民法院收到定向询价、网络询价、委托评估、说明补正等报告后，应当在三日内发送给当事人及利害关系人。  
当事人、利害关系人已提供有效送达地址的，人民法院应当将报告以直接送达、留置送达、委托送达、邮寄送达或者电子送达的方式送达；当事人、利害关系人下落不明或者无法获取其有效送达地址，人民法院无法按照前述规定送达的，应当在中国执行信息公开网上予以公示，公示满十五日即视为收到。

**Article 21** After receiving reports on targeted inquiry, online inquiry, commissioned appraisal, explanation and correction, etc., the People's Court shall send them to the parties and interested persons within three days.  
If a party or interested person has provided a valid address for service, the People's Court shall serve the report by direct service, service by leaving at the address, service by entrustment, service by mail, or electronic service; if the party or interested person is missing or cannot be reached to obtain a valid address for service, and the People's Court cannot serve in accordance with the preceding provisions, it shall publish the report on the China Enforcement Information Disclosure Website, and upon the expiration of 15 days of publication, the report shall be deemed to have been received.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　当事人、利害关系人认为网络询价报告或者评估报告具有下列情形之一的，可以在收到报告后五日内提出书面异议：  
（一）财产基本信息错误；  
（二）超出财产范围或者遗漏财产；  
（三）评估机构或者评估人员不具备相应评估资质；  
（四）评估程序严重违法。  
对当事人、利害关系人依据前款规定提出的书面异议，人民法院应当参照[民事诉讼法第二百二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-225)的规定处理。

**Article 22** If a party or interested person believes that an online inquiry report or appraisal report falls under any of the following circumstances, it may raise a written objection within five days after receiving the report:  
(1) The basic information of the property is erroneous;  
(2) The property is beyond the scope or omitted;  
(3) The appraisal institution or appraiser does not have the corresponding appraisal qualifications;  
(4) The appraisal procedure is seriously illegal.  
With respect to written objections raised by a party or interested person pursuant to the preceding paragraph, the People's Court shall handle the matter by reference to the provisions of Article 225 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　当事人、利害关系人收到评估报告后五日内对评估报告的参照标准、计算方法或者评估结果等提出书面异议的，人民法院应当在三日内交评估机构予以书面说明。评估机构在五日内未作说明或者当事人、利害关系人对作出的说明仍有异议的，人民法院应当交由相关行业协会在指定期限内组织专业技术评审，并根据专业技术评审出具的结论认定评估结果或者责令原评估机构予以补正。  
当事人、利害关系人提出前款异议，同时涉及[本规定第二十二条](#art-22)第一款第一、二项情形的，按照前款规定处理；同时涉及[本规定第二十二条](#art-22)第一款第三、四项情形的，按照[本规定第二十二条](#art-22)第二款先对第三、四项情形审查，异议成立的，应当通知评估机构三日内将人民法院委托评估时移交的材料退回，另行委托下一顺序的评估机构重新进行评估；异议不成立的，按照前款规定处理。

**Article 23** If a party or interested person raises a written objection to the reference standards, calculation methods, or appraisal results of the appraisal report within five days after receiving the appraisal report, the People's Court shall, within three days, refer the matter to the appraisal institution for a written explanation. If the appraisal institution fails to provide an explanation within five days or the party or interested person still has objections to the explanation provided, the People's Court shall refer the matter to the relevant industry association to organize a professional technical evaluation within the specified time limit, and shall determine the appraisal result or order the original appraisal institution to make corrections based on the conclusion issued by the professional technical evaluation.  
If a party or interested person raises the objection as provided in the preceding paragraph involving both the circumstances of items (1) and (2) of paragraph 1 of Article 22 of these Provisions, it shall be handled in accordance with the preceding paragraph; if it involves both the circumstances of items (3) and (4) of paragraph 1 of Article 22, the People's Court shall first review items (3) and (4) in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 22 of these Provisions; if the objection is sustained, it shall notify the appraisal institution to return the materials transferred by the People's Court at the time of commissioning the appraisal within three days, and shall separately entrust the next appraisal institution in order to conduct a new appraisal; if the objection is not sustained, it shall be handled in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　当事人、利害关系人未在[本规定第二十二条](#art-22)、第二十三条规定的期限内提出异议或者对网络询价平台、评估机构、行业协会按照[本规定第二十二条](#art-22)、第二十三条所作的补正说明、专业技术评审结论提出异议的，人民法院不予受理。  
当事人、利害关系人对议价或者定向询价提出异议的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 24** If a party or interested person fails to raise an objection within the time limit specified in Articles 22 and 23 of these Provisions, or raises an objection to the corrective explanations or professional technical evaluation conclusions made by an online inquiry platform, appraisal institution, or industry association in accordance with Articles 22 and 23 of these Provisions, the People's Court shall not accept it.  
If a party or interested person raises an objection to a negotiation or targeted inquiry, the People's Court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　当事人、利害关系人有证据证明具有下列情形之一，且在发布一拍拍卖公告或者直接进入变卖程序之前提出异议的，人民法院应当按照执行监督程序进行审查处理：  
（一）议价中存在欺诈、胁迫情形；  
（二）恶意串通损害第三人利益；  
（三）有关机构出具虚假定向询价结果；  
（四）依照[本规定第二十二条](#art-22)、第二十三条作出的处理结果确有错误。

**Article 25** If a party or interested person has evidence proving any of the following circumstances and raises an objection before the publication of the first auction announcement or directly entering the sale procedure, the People's Court shall conduct review and handling in accordance with the enforcement supervision procedure:  
(1) There is fraud or duress in the negotiation;  
(2) Malicious collusion harms the interests of a third party;  
(3) The relevant institution issues a false targeted inquiry result;  
(4) The handling result made in accordance with Articles 22 and 23 of these Provisions is indeed erroneous.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　当事人、利害关系人对评估报告未提出异议、所提异议被驳回或者评估机构已作出补正的，人民法院应当以评估结果或者补正结果为参考价；当事人、利害关系人对评估报告提出的异议成立的，人民法院应当以评估机构作出的补正结果或者重新作出的评估结果为参考价。专业技术评审对评估报告未作出否定结论的，人民法院应当以该评估结果为参考价。

**Article 26** If a party or interested person has not raised an objection to the appraisal report, the objection raised has been rejected, or the appraisal institution has made corrections, the People's Court shall take the appraisal result or the corrected result as the reference price; if the objection raised by the party or interested person against the appraisal report is sustained, the People's Court shall take the corrected result made by the appraisal institution or the re-issued appraisal result as the reference price. If the professional technical evaluation does not reach a negative conclusion on the appraisal report, the People's Court shall take the appraisal result as the reference price.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　司法网络询价平台、评估机构应当确定网络询价或者委托评估结果的有效期，有效期最长不得超过一年。  
当事人议价的，可以自行协商确定议价结果的有效期，但不得超过前款规定的期限；定向询价结果的有效期，参照前款规定确定。  
人民法院在议价、询价、评估结果有效期内发布一拍拍卖公告或者直接进入变卖程序，拍卖、变卖时未超过有效期六个月的，无需重新确定参考价，但法律、行政法规、司法解释另有规定的除外。

**Article 27** A judicial online inquiry platform or an appraisal institution shall determine the validity period of the online inquiry or commissioned appraisal result, and the validity period shall not exceed one year at most.  
If the parties negotiate, they may negotiate and determine the validity period of the negotiation result, but it shall not exceed the period specified in the preceding paragraph; the validity period of the targeted inquiry result shall be determined by reference to the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
If the People's Court publishes the first auction announcement or directly enters the sale procedure within the validity period of the negotiation, inquiry, or appraisal result, and the auction or sale is conducted within six months beyond the validity period, there is no need to re-determine the reference price, except as otherwise provided by laws, administrative regulations, or judicial interpretations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当决定暂缓网络询价或者委托评估：  
（一）案件暂缓执行或者中止执行；  
（二）评估材料与事实严重不符，可能影响评估结果，需要重新调查核实；  
（三）人民法院认为应当暂缓的其他情形。

**Article 28** If any of the following circumstances exist, the People's Court shall decide to suspend the online inquiry or commissioned appraisal:  
(1) The case is suspended or stayed in enforcement;  
(2) The appraisal materials are seriously inconsistent with the facts, which may affect the appraisal result, and reinvestigation and verification are needed;  
(3) Other circumstances that the People's Court considers should be suspended.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当撤回网络询价或者委托评估：  
（一）申请执行人撤回执行申请；  
（二）生效法律文书确定的义务已全部执行完毕；  
（三）据以执行的生效法律文书被撤销或者被裁定不予执行；  
（四）人民法院认为应当撤回的其他情形。  
人民法院决定网络询价或者委托评估后，双方当事人议价确定参考价或者协商不再对财产进行变价处理的，人民法院可以撤回网络询价或者委托评估。

**Article 29** If any of the following circumstances exist, the People's Court shall withdraw the online inquiry or commissioned appraisal:  
(1) The applicant for enforcement withdraws the enforcement application;  
(2) The obligations determined by the effective legal instrument have been fully enforced;  
(3) The effective legal instrument based on which enforcement is sought is revoked or ruled not to be enforced;  
(4) Other circumstances that the People's Court considers should be withdrawn.  
After the People's Court decides on online inquiry or commissioned appraisal, if both parties determine the reference price through negotiation or agree not to proceed with price conversion of the property, the People's Court may withdraw the online inquiry or commissioned appraisal.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　人民法院应当在参考价确定后十日内启动财产变价程序。拍卖的，参照参考价确定起拍价；直接变卖的，参照参考价确定变卖价。

**Article 30** The People's Court shall initiate the property price conversion procedure within ten days after the reference price is determined. If auction is conducted, the starting price shall be determined by reference to the reference price; if direct sale is conducted, the sale price shall be determined by reference to the reference price.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　人民法院委托司法网络询价平台进行网络询价的，网络询价费用应当按次计付给出具网络询价结果与财产处置成交价最接近的司法网络询价平台；多家司法网络询价平台出具的网络询价结果相同或者与财产处置成交价差距相同的，网络询价费用平均分配。  
人民法院依照[本规定第十一条](#art-11)第三款规定委托评估机构进行评估或者依照[本规定第二十九条](#art-29)规定撤回网络询价的，对司法网络询价平台不计付费用。

**Article 31** Where a People's Court entrusts a judicial online inquiry platform to conduct online inquiry, the online inquiry fee shall be paid on a per-occurrence basis to the judicial online inquiry platform whose online inquiry result is closest to the transaction price of the property disposal; if the online inquiry results issued by multiple judicial online inquiry platforms are the same or have the same difference from the transaction price of the property disposal, the online inquiry fee shall be distributed equally.  
If the People's Court entrusts an appraisal institution to conduct an appraisal in accordance with paragraph 3 of Article 11 of these Provisions or withdraws the online inquiry in accordance with Article 29 of these Provisions, no fee shall be paid to the judicial online inquiry platform.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　人民法院委托评估机构进行评估，财产处置未成交的，按照评估机构合理的实际支出计付费用；财产处置成交价高于评估价的，以评估价为基准计付费用；财产处置成交价低于评估价的，以财产处置成交价为基准计付费用。  
人民法院依照[本规定第二十九条](#art-29)规定撤回委托评估的，按照评估机构合理的实际支出计付费用；人民法院依照本规定通知原评估机构重新出具评估报告的，按照前款规定的百分之三十计付费用。  
人民法院依照本规定另行委托评估机构重新进行评估的，对原评估机构不计付费用。

**Article 32** Where a People's Court entrusts an appraisal institution to conduct an appraisal and the property is not sold in the disposal, the fee shall be paid based on the reasonable actual expenses of the appraisal institution; if the transaction price of the property disposal is higher than the appraised value, the fee shall be paid based on the appraised value; if the transaction price of the property disposal is lower than the appraised value, the fee shall be paid based on the transaction price of the property disposal.  
If the People's Court withdraws the commissioned appraisal in accordance with Article 29 of these Provisions, the fee shall be paid based on the reasonable actual expenses of the appraisal institution; if the People's Court notifies the original appraisal institution to re-issue an appraisal report in accordance with these Provisions, the fee shall be paid at 30% of the fee as provided in the preceding paragraph.  
If the People's Court separately entrusts another appraisal institution to conduct a new appraisal in accordance with these Provisions, no fee shall be paid to the original appraisal institution.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　网络询价费及委托评估费由申请执行人先行垫付，由被执行人负担。  
申请执行人通过签订保险合同的方式垫付网络询价费或者委托评估费的，保险人应当向人民法院出具担保书。担保书应当载明因申请执行人未垫付网络询价费或者委托评估费由保险人支付等内容，并附相关证据材料。

**Article 33** The online inquiry fee and the commissioned appraisal fee shall be advanced by the applicant for enforcement and borne by the person subject to enforcement.  
If the applicant for enforcement advances the online inquiry fee or the commissioned appraisal fee by entering into an insurance contract, the insurer shall issue a guarantee letter to the People's Court. The guarantee letter shall state that the insurer shall pay if the applicant for enforcement fails to advance the online inquiry fee or the commissioned appraisal fee, and shall attach relevant evidentiary materials.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　最高人民法院建设全国法院询价评估系统。询价评估系统与定向询价机构、司法网络询价平台、全国性评估行业协会的系统对接，实现数据共享。  
询价评估系统应当具有记载当事人议价、定向询价、网络询价、委托评估、摇号过程等功能，并形成固化数据，长期保存、随案备查。

**Article 34** The Supreme People's Court shall build a national court inquiry and appraisal system. The inquiry and appraisal system shall interface with the systems of targeted inquiry institutions, judicial online inquiry platforms, and the national appraisal industry association to achieve data sharing.  
The inquiry and appraisal system shall have functions such as recording the processes of party negotiation, targeted inquiry, online inquiry, commissioned appraisal, and drawing lots, and shall form solidified data, which shall be preserved for a long term and be available for inspection with the case.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本规定自2018年9月1日起施行。  
最高人民法院此前公布的司法解释及规范性文件与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 35** These Provisions shall come into force as of September 1, 2018.  
If any judicial interpretations or normative documents previously published by the Supreme People's Court are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
