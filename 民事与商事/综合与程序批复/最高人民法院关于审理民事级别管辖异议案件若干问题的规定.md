# 最高人民法院关于审理民事级别管辖异议案件若干问题的规定

**法律部门**：民法商法

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　被告在提交答辩状期间提出管辖权异议，认为受诉人民法院违反级别管辖规定，案件应当由上级人民法院或者下级人民法院管辖的，受诉人民法院应当审查，并在受理异议之日起十五日内作出裁定：  
（一）异议不成立的，裁定驳回；  
（二）异议成立的，裁定移送有管辖权的人民法院。

**Article 1** If, during the period for filing the statement of defense, the defendant raises an objection to jurisdiction on the ground that the People's Court hearing the case has violated the provisions on jurisdiction by level and that the case shall be under the jurisdiction of a superior or an inferior People's Court, the People's Court hearing the case shall examine the objection and shall, within fifteen days from the date of accepting the objection, render a ruling:  
(1) if the objection is unfounded, the ruling shall reject it;  
(2) if the objection is well-founded, the ruling shall transfer the case to the People's Court with jurisdiction.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在管辖权异议裁定作出前，原告申请撤回起诉，受诉人民法院作出准予撤回起诉裁定的，对管辖权异议不再审查，并在裁定书中一并写明。

**Article 2** If, before a ruling on the objection to jurisdiction is made, the plaintiff applies to withdraw the prosecution and the People's Court hearing the case renders a ruling granting the withdrawal of the prosecution, the objection to jurisdiction shall no longer be examined, and it shall be stated in the written ruling.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　提交答辩状期间届满后，原告增加诉讼请求金额致使案件标的额超过受诉人民法院级别管辖标准，被告提出管辖权异议，请求由上级人民法院管辖的，人民法院应当按照[本规定第一条](#art-1)审查并作出裁定。

**Article 3** If, after the expiration of the period for filing the statement of defense, the plaintiff increases the amount of claims in the action, causing the subject matter of the case to exceed the standard of jurisdiction by level of the People's Court hearing the case, and the defendant raises an objection to jurisdiction and requests that the case be under the jurisdiction of a superior People's Court, the People's Court shall examine the objection in accordance with Article 1 of these Provisions and render a ruling.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　上级人民法院根据[民事诉讼法第三十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-39)第一款的规定，将其管辖的第一审民事案件交由下级人民法院审理的，应当作出裁定。当事人对裁定不服提起上诉的，第二审人民法院应当依法审理并作出裁定。

**Article 4** If a superior People's Court, pursuant to Article 39, paragraph 1 of the Civil Procedure Law, assigns a first-instance civil case under its jurisdiction to an inferior People's Court for trial, the superior People's Court shall render a ruling. If a party refuses to accept the ruling and files an appeal, the second-instance People's Court shall hear the appeal according to law and render a ruling.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对于应由上级人民法院管辖的第一审民事案件，下级人民法院不得报请上级人民法院交其审理。

**Article 5** For a first-instance civil case that shall be under the jurisdiction of a superior People's Court, an inferior People's Court may not request the superior People's Court to assign the case to it for trial.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　被告以受诉人民法院同时违反级别管辖和地域管辖规定为由提出管辖权异议的，受诉人民法院应当一并作出裁定。

**Article 6** If the defendant raises an objection to jurisdiction on the ground that the People's Court hearing the case has violated both the provisions on jurisdiction by level and the provisions on territorial jurisdiction, the People's Court hearing the case shall render a single ruling on both grounds.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　当事人未依法提出管辖权异议，但受诉人民法院发现其没有级别管辖权的，应当将案件移送有管辖权的人民法院审理。

**Article 7** If a party does not raise an objection to jurisdiction according to law, but the People's Court hearing the case discovers that it does not have jurisdiction by level, it shall transfer the case to the People's Court with jurisdiction for trial.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对人民法院就级别管辖异议作出的裁定，当事人不服提起上诉的，第二审人民法院应当依法审理并作出裁定。

**Article 8** If a party refuses to accept a ruling made by a People's Court on an objection to jurisdiction by level and files an appeal, the second-instance People's Court shall hear the appeal according to law and render a ruling.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对于将案件移送上级人民法院管辖的裁定，当事人未提出上诉，但受移送的上级人民法院认为确有错误的，可以依职权裁定撤销。

**Article 9** If a party does not file an appeal against a ruling transferring a case to a superior People's Court for jurisdiction, but the superior People's Court to which the case is transferred considers that the ruling is definitely erroneous, it may, ex officio, render a ruling to revoke it.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　经最高人民法院批准的第一审民事案件级别管辖标准的规定，应当作为审理民事级别管辖异议案件的依据。

**Article 10** The provisions on the standards for jurisdiction by level in first-instance civil cases approved by the Supreme People's Court shall serve as the basis for hearing cases of objections to civil jurisdiction by level.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本规定施行前颁布的有关司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 11** If any relevant judicial interpretation promulgated before these Provisions come into force is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
