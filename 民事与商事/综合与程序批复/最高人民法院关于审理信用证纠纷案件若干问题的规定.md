# 最高人民法院关于审理信用证纠纷案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2005〕13号  
**公布日期**：2005-11-14  
**生效日期**：2006-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理信用证纠纷案件若干问题的规定》已于2005年10月24日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1368次会议通过，现予公布，自2006年1月1日起施行。 2005年11月14日 最高人民法院 关于审理信用证纠纷案件若干问题的规定 法释〔2005〕13号 （2005年10月24日最高人民法院审判委员会第1368次会议通过　2005年11月14日最高人民法院公告公布　自2006年1月1日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 本规定所指的信用证纠纷案件，是指在信用证开立、通知、修改、撤销、保兑、议付、偿付等环节产生的纠纷。

**Article 1** The disputes over letters of credit referred to in these Provisions mean disputes arising from the issuance, notification, amendment, revocation, confirmation, negotiation, reimbursement, and other links of letters of credit.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 人民法院审理信用证纠纷案件时，当事人约定适用相关国际惯例或者其他规定的，从其约定；当事人没有约定的，适用国际商会《跟单信用证统一惯例》或者其他相关国际惯例。

**Article 2** When a People's Court hears a dispute over a letter of credit, if the parties have agreed to apply relevant international customs or other provisions, such agreement shall prevail; if the parties have not so agreed, the Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits of the International Chamber of Commerce or other relevant international customs shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 开证申请人与开证行之间因申请开立信用证而产生的欠款纠纷、委托人和受托人之间因委托开立信用证产生的纠纷、担保人为申请开立信用证或者委托开立信用证提供担保而产生的纠纷以及信用证项下融资产生的纠纷，适用本规定。

**Article 3** These Provisions shall apply to: disputes over debts arising between the applicant and the issuing bank from the application for issuance of a letter of credit; disputes arising between the principal and the agent from the application for issuance of a letter of credit on an entrustment basis; disputes arising from the provision of guarantee by a guarantor for the application for issuance of a letter of credit or the application for issuance of a letter of credit on an entrustment basis; and disputes arising from financing under a letter of credit.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 因申请开立信用证而产生的欠款纠纷、委托开立信用证纠纷和因此产生的担保纠纷以及信用证项下融资产生的纠纷应当适用中华人民共和国相关法律。涉外合同当事人对法律适用另有约定的除外。

**Article 4** Disputes over debts arising from the application for issuance of a letter of credit, disputes over the application for issuance of a letter of credit on an entrustment basis and disputes over guarantees arising therefrom, as well as disputes arising from financing under a letter of credit shall be governed by the relevant laws of the People's Republic of China, unless the parties to a foreign-related contract have agreed otherwise on the application of law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 开证行在作出付款、承兑或者履行信用证项下其他义务的承诺后，只要单据与信用证条款、单据与单据之间在表面上相符，开证行应当履行在信用证规定的期限内付款的义务。当事人以开证申请人与受益人之间的基础交易提出抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。具有[本规定第八条](#art-8)的情形除外。

**Article 5** After the issuing bank has made a commitment to pay, accept or perform other obligations under the letter of credit, the issuing bank shall perform the obligation to pay within the time limit specified in the letter of credit, provided that the documents appear on their face to be in compliance with the terms and conditions of the letter of credit and with each other. If a party defends on grounds of the underlying transaction between the applicant and the beneficiary, the People's Court shall not support such defense, unless the circumstances specified in Article 8 of these Provisions exist.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 人民法院在审理信用证纠纷案件中涉及单证审查的，应当根据当事人约定适用的相关国际惯例或者其他规定进行；当事人没有约定的，应当按照国际商会《跟单信用证统一惯例》以及国际商会确定的相关标准，认定单据与信用证条款、单据与单据之间是否在表面上相符。  
信用证项下单据与信用证条款之间、单据与单据之间在表面上不完全一致，但并不导致相互之间产生歧义的，不应认定为不符点。

**Article 6** When a People's Court, in hearing a dispute over a letter of credit, involves the examination of documents, it shall conduct the examination in accordance with the relevant international customs or other provisions agreed to be applied by the parties; if the parties have not so agreed, the court shall determine whether the documents appear on their face to be in compliance with the terms and conditions of the letter of credit and with each other in accordance with the Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits of the International Chamber of Commerce and the relevant standards established by the International Chamber of Commerce.  
If the documents under a letter of credit are not completely consistent on their face with the terms and conditions of the letter of credit or with each other, but do not cause any ambiguity between them, such inconsistency shall not be deemed a discrepancy.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 开证行有独立审查单据的权利和义务，有权自行作出单据与信用证条款、单据与单据之间是否在表面上相符的决定，并自行决定接受或者拒绝接受单据与信用证条款、单据与单据之间的不符点。开证行发现信用证项下存在不符点后，可以自行决定是否联系开证申请人接受不符点。开证申请人决定是否接受不符点，并不影响开证行最终决定是否接受不符点。开证行和开证申请人另有约定的除外。  
开证行向受益人明确表示接受不符点的，应当承担付款责任。  
开证行拒绝接受不符点时，受益人以开证申请人已接受不符点为由要求开证行承担信用证项下付款责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 7** The issuing bank has the independent right and obligation to examine documents, has the right to decide on its own whether the documents appear on their face to be in compliance with the terms and conditions of the letter of credit and with each other, and has the right to decide on its own whether to accept or reject any discrepancies between the documents and the terms and conditions of the letter of credit or between documents themselves.  
After the issuing bank discovers a discrepancy under the letter of credit, it may decide on its own whether to contact the applicant to accept the discrepancy. The applicant's decision whether to accept the discrepancy shall not affect the issuing bank's final decision whether to accept the discrepancy, unless the issuing bank and the applicant have agreed otherwise.  
If the issuing bank expressly indicates to the beneficiary that it accepts the discrepancy, it shall assume the liability for payment.  
If the issuing bank rejects the discrepancy, and the beneficiary requests the issuing bank to assume the liability for payment under the letter of credit on the ground that the applicant has accepted the discrepancy, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 凡有下列情形之一的，应当认定存在信用证欺诈：  
（一）受益人伪造单据或者提交记载内容虚假的单据；  
（二）受益人恶意不交付货物或者交付的货物无价值；  
（三）受益人和开证申请人或者其他第三方串通提交假单据，而没有真实的基础交易；  
（四）其他进行信用证欺诈的情形。

**Article 8** A letter of credit fraud shall be found to exist in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The beneficiary forges documents or submits documents with false contents;  
(2) The beneficiary maliciously fails to deliver the goods or delivers goods of no value;  
(3) The beneficiary colludes with the applicant or any other third party to submit false documents without a genuine underlying transaction; or  
(4) Other circumstances constituting a letter of credit fraud.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 开证申请人、开证行或者其他利害关系人发现有[本规定第八条](#art-8)的情形，并认为将会给其造成难以弥补的损害时，可以向有管辖权的人民法院申请中止支付信用证项下的款项。

**Article 9** If the applicant, the issuing bank or any other interested party discovers the circumstances specified in Article 8 of these Provisions and believes that such circumstances will cause irreparable damage to it, it may apply to the People's Court with jurisdiction for an order to suspend payment of the proceeds under the letter of credit.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 人民法院认定存在信用证欺诈的，应当裁定中止支付或者判决终止支付信用证项下款项，但有下列情形之一的除外：  
（一）开证行的指定人、授权人已按照开证行的指令善意地进行了付款；  
（二）开证行或者其指定人、授权人已对信用证项下票据善意地作出了承兑；  
（三）保兑行善意地履行了付款义务；  
（四）议付行善意地进行了议付。

**Article 10** If a People's Court finds that a letter of credit fraud exists, it shall issue a ruling to suspend payment or a judgment to terminate payment of the proceeds under the letter of credit, except in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The nominee or authorized person of the issuing bank has made payment in good faith in accordance with the instructions of the issuing bank;  
(2) The issuing bank or its nominee or authorized person has accepted the draft under the letter of credit in good faith;  
(3) The confirming bank has performed its obligation of payment in good faith; or  
(4) The negotiating bank has negotiated in good faith.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 当事人在起诉前申请中止支付信用证项下款项符合下列条件的，人民法院应予受理：  
（一）受理申请的人民法院对该信用证纠纷案件享有管辖权；  
（二）申请人提供的证据材料证明存在[本规定第八条](#art-8)的情形；  
（三）如不采取中止支付信用证项下款项的措施，将会使申请人的合法权益受到难以弥补的损害；  
（四）申请人提供了可靠、充分的担保；  
（五）不存在[本规定第十条](#art-10)的情形。  
当事人在诉讼中申请中止支付信用证项下款项的，应当符合前款第（二）、（三）、（四）、（五）项规定的条件。

**Article 11** If a party applies for suspension of payment of the proceeds under a letter of credit before prosecution and meets the following conditions, the People's Court shall accept the application:  
(1) The People's Court receiving the application has jurisdiction over the dispute over the letter of credit;  
(2) The evidence submitted by the applicant proves the existence of the circumstances specified in Article 8 of these Provisions;  
(3) Failure to take the measure of suspending payment of the proceeds under the letter of credit will cause irreparable damage to the lawful rights and interests of the applicant;  
(4) The applicant has provided reliable and sufficient security; and  
(5) None of the circumstances specified in Article 10 of these Provisions exist.  
If a party applies for suspension of payment of the proceeds under a letter of credit in the course of litigation, it shall meet the conditions specified in items (2), (3), (4) and (5) of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 人民法院接受中止支付信用证项下款项申请后，必须在四十八小时内作出裁定；裁定中止支付的，应当立即开始执行。  
人民法院作出中止支付信用证项下款项的裁定，应当列明申请人、被申请人和第三人。

**Article 12** After a People's Court accepts an application for suspension of payment of the proceeds under a letter of credit, it must render a ruling within forty-eight hours; if it rules to suspend payment, the ruling shall be enforced immediately.  
When a People's Court makes a ruling to suspend payment of the proceeds under a letter of credit, it shall specify the applicant, the respondent and the third party.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 当事人对人民法院作出中止支付信用证项下款项的裁定有异议的，可以在裁定书送达之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议。上一级人民法院应当自收到复议申请之日起十日内作出裁定。  
复议期间，不停止原裁定的执行。

**Article 13** If a party objects to a ruling made by a People's Court to suspend payment of the proceeds under a letter of credit, it may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level within ten days from the date of service of the ruling. The People's Court at the next higher level shall render a ruling within ten days from the date of receipt of the application for reconsideration.  
During the period of reconsideration, enforcement of the original ruling shall not be suspended.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 人民法院在审理信用证欺诈案件过程中，必要时可以将信用证纠纷与基础交易纠纷一并审理。  
当事人以基础交易欺诈为由起诉的，可以将与案件有关的开证行、议付行或者其他信用证法律关系的利害关系人列为第三人；第三人可以申请参加诉讼，人民法院也可以通知第三人参加诉讼。

**Article 14** In the process of hearing a case of letter of credit fraud, a People's Court may, when necessary, hear the dispute over the letter of credit together with the dispute over the underlying transaction.  
If a party prosecutes on the ground of fraud in the underlying transaction, it may list the issuing bank, the negotiating bank or other interested parties in the legal relationship of the letter of credit as third parties; the third party may apply to participate in the proceedings, and the People's Court may also notify the third party to participate in the proceedings.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 人民法院通过实体审理，认定构成信用证欺诈并且不存在[本规定第十条](#art-10)的情形的，应当判决终止支付信用证项下的款项。

**Article 15** If a People's Court, through substantive trial, finds that a letter of credit fraud has been constituted and none of the circumstances specified in Article 10 of these Provisions exist, it shall render a judgment to terminate payment of the proceeds under the letter of credit.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 保证人以开证行或者开证申请人接受不符点未征得其同意为由请求免除保证责任的，人民法院不予支持。保证合同另有约定的除外。

**Article 16** If a guarantor requests to be exempted from the guarantee liability on the ground that the issuing bank or the applicant accepted the discrepancy without obtaining its consent, the People's Court shall not support such request, unless the guarantee contract provides otherwise.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 开证申请人与开证行对信用证进行修改未征得保证人同意的，保证人只在原保证合同约定的或者法律规定的期间和范围内承担保证责任。保证合同另有约定的除外。

**Article 17** If the applicant and the issuing bank amend the letter of credit without obtaining the consent of the guarantor, the guarantor shall only assume the guarantee liability within the period and scope agreed in the original guarantee contract or prescribed by law, unless the guarantee contract provides otherwise.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 本规定自2006年1月1日起施行。

**Article 18** These Provisions shall come into force as of January 1, 2006.

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所指的信用证纠纷案件，是指在信用证开立、通知、修改、撤销、保兑、议付、偿付等环节产生的纠纷。

**Article 1** The disputes over letters of credit referred to in these Provisions mean disputes arising from the issuance, notification, amendment, revocation, confirmation, negotiation, reimbursement, and other links of letters of credit.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院审理信用证纠纷案件时，当事人约定适用相关国际惯例或者其他规定的，从其约定；当事人没有约定的，适用国际商会《跟单信用证统一惯例》或者其他相关国际惯例。

**Article 2** When a People's Court hears a dispute over a letter of credit, if the parties have agreed to apply relevant international customs or other provisions, such agreement shall prevail; if the parties have not so agreed, the Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits of the International Chamber of Commerce or other relevant international customs shall apply.

**Article 3** These Provisions shall apply to: disputes over debts arising between the applicant and the issuing bank from the application for issuance of a letter of credit; disputes arising between the principal and the agent from the application for issuance of a letter of credit on an entrustment basis; disputes arising from the provision of guarantee by a guarantor for the application for issuance of a letter of credit or the application for issuance of a letter of credit on an entrustment basis; and disputes arising from financing under a letter of credit.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　因申请开立信用证而产生的欠款纠纷、委托开立信用证纠纷和因此产生的担保纠纷以及信用证项下融资产生的纠纷应当适用中华人民共和国相关法律。涉外合同当事人对法律适用另有约定的除外。

**Article 4** Disputes over debts arising from the application for issuance of a letter of credit, disputes over the application for issuance of a letter of credit on an entrustment basis and disputes over guarantees arising therefrom, as well as disputes arising from financing under a letter of credit shall be governed by the relevant laws of the People's Republic of China, unless the parties to a foreign-related contract have agreed otherwise on the application of law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　开证行在作出付款、承兑或者履行信用证项下其他义务的承诺后，只要单据与信用证条款、单据与单据之间在表面上相符，开证行应当履行在信用证规定的期限内付款的义务。当事人以开证申请人与受益人之间的基础交易提出抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。具有[本规定第八条](#art-8)的情形除外。

If a party defends on grounds of the underlying transaction between the applicant and the beneficiary, the People's Court shall not support such defense, unless the circumstances specified in Article 8 of these Provisions exist.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院在审理信用证纠纷案件中涉及单证审查的，应当根据当事人约定适用的相关国际惯例或者其他规定进行；当事人没有约定的，应当按照国际商会《跟单信用证统一惯例》以及国际商会确定的相关标准，认定单据与信用证条款、单据与单据之间是否在表面上相符。  
信用证项下单据与信用证条款之间、单据与单据之间在表面上不完全一致，但并不导致相互之间产生歧义的，不应认定为不符点。

**Article 6** When a People's Court, in hearing a dispute over a letter of credit, involves the examination of documents, it shall conduct the examination in accordance with the relevant international customs or other provisions agreed to be applied by the parties; if the parties have not so agreed, the court shall determine whether the documents appear on their face to be in compliance with the terms and conditions of the letter of credit and with each other in accordance with the Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits of the International Chamber of Commerce and the relevant standards established by the International Chamber of Commerce.  
If the documents under a letter of credit are not completely consistent on their face with the terms and conditions of the letter of credit or with each other, but do not cause any ambiguity between them, such inconsistency shall not be deemed a discrepancy.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　开证行有独立审查单据的权利和义务，有权自行作出单据与信用证条款、单据与单据之间是否在表面上相符的决定，并自行决定接受或者拒绝接受单据与信用证条款、单据与单据之间的不符点。  
开证行发现信用证项下存在不符点后，可以自行决定是否联系开证申请人接受不符点。开证申请人决定是否接受不符点，并不影响开证行最终决定是否接受不符点。开证行和开证申请人另有约定的除外。  
开证行向受益人明确表示接受不符点的，应当承担付款责任。  
开证行拒绝接受不符点时，受益人以开证申请人已接受不符点为由要求开证行承担信用证项下付款责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 7** The issuing bank has the independent right and obligation to examine documents, has the right to decide on its own whether the documents appear on their face to be in compliance with the terms and conditions of the letter of credit and with each other, and has the right to decide on its own whether to accept or reject any discrepancies between the documents and the terms and conditions of the letter of credit or between documents themselves.  
After the issuing bank discovers a discrepancy under the letter of credit, it may decide on its own whether to contact the applicant to accept the discrepancy. The applicant's decision whether to accept the discrepancy shall not affect the issuing bank's final decision whether to accept the discrepancy, unless the issuing bank and the applicant have agreed otherwise.  
If the issuing bank expressly indicates to the beneficiary that it accepts the discrepancy, it shall assume the liability for payment.  
If the issuing bank rejects the discrepancy, and the beneficiary requests the issuing bank to assume the liability for payment under the letter of credit on the ground that the applicant has accepted the discrepancy, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　凡有下列情形之一的，应当认定存在信用证欺诈：  
（一）受益人伪造单据或者提交记载内容虚假的单据；  
（二）受益人恶意不交付货物或者交付的货物无价值；  
（三）受益人和开证申请人或者其他第三方串通提交假单据，而没有真实的基础交易；  
（四）其他进行信用证欺诈的情形。

**Article 8** A letter of credit fraud shall be found to exist in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The beneficiary forges documents or submits documents with false contents;  
(2) The beneficiary maliciously fails to deliver the goods or delivers goods of no value;  
(3) The beneficiary colludes with the applicant or any other third party to submit false documents without a genuine underlying transaction; or  
(4) Other circumstances constituting a letter of credit fraud.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　开证申请人、开证行或者其他利害关系人发现有[本规定第八条](#art-8)的情形，并认为将会给其造成难以弥补的损害时，可以向有管辖权的人民法院申请中止支付信用证项下的款项。

**Article 9** If the applicant, the issuing bank or any other interested party discovers the circumstances specified in Article 8 of these Provisions and believes that such circumstances will cause irreparable damage to it, it may apply to the People's Court with jurisdiction for an order to suspend payment of the proceeds under the letter of credit.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院认定存在信用证欺诈的，应当裁定中止支付或者判决终止支付信用证项下款项，但有下列情形之一的除外：  
（一）开证行的指定人、授权人已按照开证行的指令善意地进行了付款；  
（二）开证行或者其指定人、授权人已对信用证项下票据善意地作出了承兑；  
（三）保兑行善意地履行了付款义务；  
（四）议付行善意地进行了议付。

**Article 10** If a People's Court finds that a letter of credit fraud exists, it shall issue a ruling to suspend payment or a judgment to terminate payment of the proceeds under the letter of credit, except in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The nominee or authorized person of the issuing bank has made payment in good faith in accordance with the instructions of the issuing bank;  
(2) The issuing bank or its nominee or authorized person has accepted the draft under the letter of credit in good faith;  
(3) The confirming bank has performed its obligation of payment in good faith; or  
(4) The negotiating bank has negotiated in good faith.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　当事人在起诉前申请中止支付信用证项下款项符合下列条件的，人民法院应予受理：  
（一）受理申请的人民法院对该信用证纠纷案件享有管辖权；  
（二）申请人提供的证据材料证明存在[本规定第八条](#art-8)的情形；  
（三）如不采取中止支付信用证项下款项的措施，将会使申请人的合法权益受到难以弥补的损害；  
（四）申请人提供了可靠、充分的担保；  
（五）不存在[本规定第十条](#art-10)的情形。  
当事人在诉讼中申请中止支付信用证项下款项的，应当符合前款第（二）、（三）、（四）、（五）项规定的条件。

**Article 11** If a party applies for suspension of payment of the proceeds under a letter of credit before prosecution and meets the following conditions, the People's Court shall accept the application:  
(1) The People's Court receiving the application has jurisdiction over the dispute over the letter of credit;  
(2) The evidence submitted by the applicant proves the existence of the circumstances specified in Article 8 of these Provisions;  
(3) Failure to take the measure of suspending payment of the proceeds under the letter of credit will cause irreparable damage to the lawful rights and interests of the applicant;  
(4) The applicant has provided reliable and sufficient security; and  
(5) None of the circumstances specified in Article 10 of these Provisions exist.  
If a party applies for suspension of payment of the proceeds under a letter of credit in the course of litigation, it shall meet the conditions specified in items (2), (3), (4) and (5) of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院接受中止支付信用证项下款项申请后，必须在四十八小时内作出裁定；裁定中止支付的，应当立即开始执行。  
人民法院作出中止支付信用证项下款项的裁定，应当列明申请人、被申请人和第三人。

**Article 12** After a People's Court accepts an application for suspension of payment of the proceeds under a letter of credit, it must render a ruling within forty-eight hours; if it rules to suspend payment, the ruling shall be enforced immediately.  
When a People's Court makes a ruling to suspend payment of the proceeds under a letter of credit, it shall specify the applicant, the respondent and the third party.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　当事人对人民法院作出中止支付信用证项下款项的裁定有异议的，可以在裁定书送达之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议。上一级人民法院应当自收到复议申请之日起十日内作出裁定。  
复议期间，不停止原裁定的执行。

**Article 13** If a party objects to a ruling made by a People's Court to suspend payment of the proceeds under a letter of credit, it may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level within ten days from the date of service of the ruling. The People's Court at the next higher level shall render a ruling within ten days from the date of receipt of the application for reconsideration.  
During the period of reconsideration, enforcement of the original ruling shall not be suspended.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院在审理信用证欺诈案件过程中，必要时可以将信用证纠纷与基础交易纠纷一并审理。  
当事人以基础交易欺诈为由起诉的，可以将与案件有关的开证行、议付行或者其他信用证法律关系的利害关系人列为第三人；第三人可以申请参加诉讼，人民法院也可以通知第三人参加诉讼。

**Article 14** In the process of hearing a case of letter of credit fraud, a People's Court may, when necessary, hear the dispute over the letter of credit together with the dispute over the underlying transaction.  
If a party prosecutes on the ground of fraud in the underlying transaction, it may list the issuing bank, the negotiating bank or other interested parties in the legal relationship of the letter of credit as third parties; the third party may apply to participate in the proceedings, and the People's Court may also notify the third party to participate in the proceedings.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民法院通过实体审理，认定构成信用证欺诈并且不存在[本规定第十条](#art-10)的情形的，应当判决终止支付信用证项下的款项。

**Article 15** If a People's Court, through substantive trial, finds that a letter of credit fraud has been constituted and none of the circumstances specified in Article 10 of these Provisions exist, it shall render a judgment to terminate payment of the proceeds under the letter of credit.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　保证人以开证行或者开证申请人接受不符点未征得其同意为由请求免除保证责任的，人民法院不予支持。保证合同另有约定的除外。

**Article 16** If a guarantor requests to be exempted from the guarantee liability on the ground that the issuing bank or the applicant accepted the discrepancy without obtaining its consent, the People's Court shall not support such request, unless the guarantee contract provides otherwise.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　开证申请人与开证行对信用证进行修改未征得保证人同意的，保证人只在原保证合同约定的或者法律规定的期间和范围内承担保证责任。保证合同另有约定的除外。

**Article 17** If the applicant and the issuing bank amend the letter of credit without obtaining the consent of the guarantor, the guarantor shall only assume the guarantee liability within the period and scope agreed in the original guarantee contract or prescribed by law, unless the guarantee contract provides otherwise.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本规定自2006年1月1日起施行。

**Article 18** These Provisions shall come into force as of January 1, 2006.

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所指的信用证纠纷案件，是指在信用证开立、通知、修改、撤销、保兑、议付、偿付等环节产生的纠纷。

**Article 1** The disputes over letters of credit referred to in these Provisions mean disputes arising from the issuance, notification, amendment, revocation, confirmation, negotiation, reimbursement, and other links of letters of credit.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院审理信用证纠纷案件时，当事人约定适用相关国际惯例或者其他规定的，从其约定；当事人没有约定的，适用国际商会《跟单信用证统一惯例》或者其他相关国际惯例。

**Article 2** When a People's Court hears a dispute over a letter of credit, if the parties have agreed to apply relevant international customs or other provisions, such agreement shall prevail; if the parties have not so agreed, the Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits of the International Chamber of Commerce or other relevant international customs shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　开证申请人与开证行之间因申请开立信用证而产生的欠款纠纷、委托人和受托人之间因委托开立信用证产生的纠纷、担保人为申请开立信用证或者委托开立信用证提供担保而产生的纠纷以及信用证项下融资产生的纠纷，适用本规定。

**Article 3** These Provisions shall apply to: disputes over debts arising between the applicant and the issuing bank from the application for issuance of a letter of credit; disputes arising between the principal and the agent from the application for issuance of a letter of credit on an entrustment basis; disputes arising from the provision of guarantee by a guarantor for the application for issuance of a letter of credit or the application for issuance of a letter of credit on an entrustment basis; and disputes arising from financing under a letter of credit.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　因申请开立信用证而产生的欠款纠纷、委托开立信用证纠纷和因此产生的担保纠纷以及信用证项下融资产生的纠纷应当适用中华人民共和国相关法律。涉外合同当事人对法律适用另有约定的除外。

**Article 4** Disputes over debts arising from the application for issuance of a letter of credit, disputes over the application for issuance of a letter of credit on an entrustment basis and disputes over guarantees arising therefrom, as well as disputes arising from financing under a letter of credit shall be governed by the relevant laws of the People's Republic of China, unless the parties to a foreign-related contract have agreed otherwise on the application of law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　开证行在作出付款、承兑或者履行信用证项下其他义务的承诺后，只要单据与信用证条款、单据与单据之间在表面上相符，开证行应当履行在信用证规定的期限内付款的义务。当事人以开证申请人与受益人之间的基础交易提出抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。具有[本规定第八条](#art-8)的情形除外。

**Article 5** After the issuing bank has made a commitment to pay, accept or perform other obligations under the letter of credit, the issuing bank shall perform the obligation to pay within the time limit specified in the letter of credit, provided that the documents appear on their face to be in compliance with the terms and conditions of the letter of credit and with each other. If a party defends on grounds of the underlying transaction between the applicant and the beneficiary, the People's Court shall not support such defense, unless the circumstances specified in Article 8 of these Provisions exist.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院在审理信用证纠纷案件中涉及单证审查的，应当根据当事人约定适用的相关国际惯例或者其他规定进行；当事人没有约定的，应当按照国际商会《跟单信用证统一惯例》以及国际商会确定的相关标准，认定单据与信用证条款、单据与单据之间是否在表面上相符。  
信用证项下单据与信用证条款之间、单据与单据之间在表面上不完全一致，但并不导致相互之间产生歧义的，不应认定为不符点。

**Article 6** When a People's Court, in hearing a dispute over a letter of credit, involves the examination of documents, it shall conduct the examination in accordance with the relevant international customs or other provisions agreed to be applied by the parties; if the parties have not so agreed, the court shall determine whether the documents appear on their face to be in compliance with the terms and conditions of the letter of credit and with each other in accordance with the Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits of the International Chamber of Commerce and the relevant standards established by the International Chamber of Commerce.  
If the documents under a letter of credit are not completely consistent on their face with the terms and conditions of the letter of credit or with each other, but do not cause any ambiguity between them, such inconsistency shall not be deemed a discrepancy.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　开证行有独立审查单据的权利和义务，有权自行作出单据与信用证条款、单据与单据之间是否在表面上相符的决定，并自行决定接受或者拒绝接受单据与信用证条款、单据与单据之间的不符点。  
开证行发现信用证项下存在不符点后，可以自行决定是否联系开证申请人接受不符点。开证申请人决定是否接受不符点，并不影响开证行最终决定是否接受不符点。开证行和开证申请人另有约定的除外。  
开证行向受益人明确表示接受不符点的，应当承担付款责任。  
开证行拒绝接受不符点时，受益人以开证申请人已接受不符点为由要求开证行承担信用证项下付款责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 7** The issuing bank has the independent right and obligation to examine documents, has the right to decide on its own whether the documents appear on their face to be in compliance with the terms and conditions of the letter of credit and with each other, and has the right to decide on its own whether to accept or reject any discrepancies between the documents and the terms and conditions of the letter of credit or between documents themselves.  
After the issuing bank discovers a discrepancy under the letter of credit, it may decide on its own whether to contact the applicant to accept the discrepancy. The applicant's decision whether to accept the discrepancy shall not affect the issuing bank's final decision whether to accept the discrepancy, unless the issuing bank and the applicant have agreed otherwise.  
If the issuing bank expressly indicates to the beneficiary that it accepts the discrepancy, it shall assume the liability for payment.  
If the issuing bank rejects the discrepancy, and the beneficiary requests the issuing bank to assume the liability for payment under the letter of credit on the ground that the applicant has accepted the discrepancy, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　凡有下列情形之一的，应当认定存在信用证欺诈：  
（一）受益人伪造单据或者提交记载内容虚假的单据；  
（二）受益人恶意不交付货物或者交付的货物无价值；  
（三）受益人和开证申请人或者其他第三方串通提交假单据，而没有真实的基础交易；  
（四）其他进行信用证欺诈的情形。

**Article 8** A letter of credit fraud shall be found to exist in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The beneficiary forges documents or submits documents with false contents;  
(2) The beneficiary maliciously fails to deliver the goods or delivers goods of no value;  
(3) The beneficiary colludes with the applicant or any other third party to submit false documents without a genuine underlying transaction; or  
(4) Other circumstances constituting a letter of credit fraud.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　开证申请人、开证行或者其他利害关系人发现有[本规定第八条](#art-8)的情形，并认为将会给其造成难以弥补的损害时，可以向有管辖权的人民法院申请中止支付信用证项下的款项。

**Article 9** If the applicant, the issuing bank or any other interested party discovers the circumstances specified in Article 8 of these Provisions and believes that such circumstances will cause irreparable damage to it, it may apply to the People's Court with jurisdiction for an order to suspend payment of the proceeds under the letter of credit.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院认定存在信用证欺诈的，应当裁定中止支付或者判决终止支付信用证项下款项，但有下列情形之一的除外：  
（一）开证行的指定人、授权人已按照开证行的指令善意地进行了付款；  
（二）开证行或者其指定人、授权人已对信用证项下票据善意地作出了承兑；  
（三）保兑行善意地履行了付款义务；  
（四）议付行善意地进行了议付。

**Article 10** If a People's Court finds that a letter of credit fraud exists, it shall issue a ruling to suspend payment or a judgment to terminate payment of the proceeds under the letter of credit, except in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The nominee or authorized person of the issuing bank has made payment in good faith in accordance with the instructions of the issuing bank;  
(2) The issuing bank or its nominee or authorized person has accepted the draft under the letter of credit in good faith;  
(3) The confirming bank has performed its obligation of payment in good faith; or  
(4) The negotiating bank has negotiated in good faith.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　当事人在起诉前申请中止支付信用证项下款项符合下列条件的，人民法院应予受理：  
（一）受理申请的人民法院对该信用证纠纷案件享有管辖权；  
（二）申请人提供的证据材料证明存在[本规定第八条](#art-8)的情形；  
（三）如不采取中止支付信用证项下款项的措施，将会使申请人的合法权益受到难以弥补的损害；  
（四）申请人提供了可靠、充分的担保；  
（五）不存在[本规定第十条](#art-10)的情形。  
当事人在诉讼中申请中止支付信用证项下款项的，应当符合前款第（二）、（三）、（四）、（五）项规定的条件。

**Article 11** If a party applies for suspension of payment of the proceeds under a letter of credit before prosecution and meets the following conditions, the People's Court shall accept the application:  
(1) The People's Court receiving the application has jurisdiction over the dispute over the letter of credit;  
(2) The evidence submitted by the applicant proves the existence of the circumstances specified in Article 8 of these Provisions;  
(3) Failure to take the measure of suspending payment of the proceeds under the letter of credit will cause irreparable damage to the lawful rights and interests of the applicant;  
(4) The applicant has provided reliable and sufficient security; and  
(5) None of the circumstances specified in Article 10 of these Provisions exist.  
If a party applies for suspension of payment of the proceeds under a letter of credit in the course of litigation, it shall meet the conditions specified in items (2), (3), (4) and (5) of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院接受中止支付信用证项下款项申请后，必须在四十八小时内作出裁定；裁定中止支付的，应当立即开始执行。  
人民法院作出中止支付信用证项下款项的裁定，应当列明申请人、被申请人和第三人。

**Article 12** After a People's Court accepts an application for suspension of payment of the proceeds under a letter of credit, it must render a ruling within forty-eight hours; if it rules to suspend payment, the ruling shall be enforced immediately.  
When a People's Court makes a ruling to suspend payment of the proceeds under a letter of credit, it shall specify the applicant, the respondent and the third party.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　当事人对人民法院作出中止支付信用证项下款项的裁定有异议的，可以在裁定书送达之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议。上一级人民法院应当自收到复议申请之日起十日内作出裁定。  
复议期间，不停止原裁定的执行。

**Article 13** If a party objects to a ruling made by a People's Court to suspend payment of the proceeds under a letter of credit, it may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level within ten days from the date of service of the ruling. The People's Court at the next higher level shall render a ruling within ten days from the date of receipt of the application for reconsideration.  
During the period of reconsideration, enforcement of the original ruling shall not be suspended.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院在审理信用证欺诈案件过程中，必要时可以将信用证纠纷与基础交易纠纷一并审理。  
当事人以基础交易欺诈为由起诉的，可以将与案件有关的开证行、议付行或者其他信用证法律关系的利害关系人列为第三人；第三人可以申请参加诉讼，人民法院也可以通知第三人参加诉讼。

**Article 14** In the process of hearing a case of letter of credit fraud, a People's Court may, when necessary, hear the dispute over the letter of credit together with the dispute over the underlying transaction.  
If a party prosecutes on the ground of fraud in the underlying transaction, it may list the issuing bank, the negotiating bank or other interested parties in the legal relationship of the letter of credit as third parties; the third party may apply to participate in the proceedings, and the People's Court may also notify the third party to participate in the proceedings.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民法院通过实体审理，认定构成信用证欺诈并且不存在[本规定第十条](#art-10)的情形的，应当判决终止支付信用证项下的款项。

**Article 15** If a People's Court, through substantive trial, finds that a letter of credit fraud has been constituted and none of the circumstances specified in Article 10 of these Provisions exist, it shall render a judgment to terminate payment of the proceeds under the letter of credit.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　保证人以开证行或者开证申请人接受不符点未征得其同意为由请求免除保证责任的，人民法院不予支持。保证合同另有约定的除外。

**Article 16** If a guarantor requests to be exempted from the guarantee liability on the ground that the issuing bank or the applicant accepted the discrepancy without obtaining its consent, the People's Court shall not support such request, unless the guarantee contract provides otherwise.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　开证申请人与开证行对信用证进行修改未征得保证人同意的，保证人只在原保证合同约定的或者法律规定的期间和范围内承担保证责任。保证合同另有约定的除外。

**Article 17** If the applicant and the issuing bank amend the letter of credit without obtaining the consent of the guarantor, the guarantor shall only assume the guarantee liability within the period and scope agreed in the original guarantee contract or prescribed by law, unless the guarantee contract provides otherwise.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本规定自2006年1月1日起施行。

**Article 18** These Provisions shall come into force as of January 1, 2006.
