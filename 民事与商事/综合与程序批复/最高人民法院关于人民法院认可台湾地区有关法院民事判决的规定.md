# 最高人民法院关于人民法院认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔1998〕11号  
**公布日期**：1998-01-15  
**生效日期**：1998-05-26

> （1998年1月15日最高人民法院审判委员会第 957次会议通过，自1998年5月26日起施行） 第一条 为保障我国台湾地区和其他省、自治区、直辖市的诉讼当事人的民事权益与诉讼权利，特制定本规定。 第二条 台湾地区有关法院的民事判决，当事人的住所地、经常居住地或者被执行财产所在地在其他省、自治区、直辖市的，当事人可以根据本规定向人民法院申请认可。 第三条 申请由申请人住所地、经常居住地或者被执行财产所在地中级人民法院受理。 第四条 申请人应提文申请书，并须附有不违反一个中国原则的台湾地区有关法院民事判决书正本或经证明无误的副本、证明文件。 第五条 申请书应记明以下事项： （一）申请人姓名、性别、年龄、职业、身份证件号码、申请时间和住址（申请人为法人或者其他组织的，应记明法人或者其他组织的名称、地址、法定代表人姓名、职务）； （二）当事人受传唤和应诉情况及证明文件； （三）请求和理由； （四）其他需要说明的情况。 第六条 人民法院收到申请书，经审查，符合本规定第四条和第五条的条件的，应当在七日内受理；不符合本规定第四条和第五条的条件的，不予受理，并在七日内通知申请人，同时说明不受理的理由。 第七条 人民法院审查认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的申请，由审判员组成合议庭进行。 第八条 人民法院受理申请后，对于台湾地区有关法院民事判决是否生效不能确定的，应告知申请人提交作出判决的法院出具的证明文件。 第九条 台湾地区有关法院的民事判决具有下列情形之一的，裁定不予认可： （一）申请认可的民事判决的效力未确定的；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 为保障我国台湾地区和其他省、自治区、直辖市的诉讼当事人的民事权益与诉讼权利，特制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of protecting the civil rights and interests and litigation rights of the litigation parties in the Taiwan region of China and other provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 台湾地区有关法院的民事判决，当事人的住所地、经常居住地或者被执行财产所在地在其他省、自治区、直辖市的，当事人可以根据本规定向人民法院申请认可。

**Article 2** Where the domicile, habitual residence of a party, or the location of property to be enforced in respect of a civil judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region is in another province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the party may apply to the People's Court for recognition in accordance with these Provisions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 申请由申请人住所地、经常居住地或者被执行财产所在地中级人民法院受理。

**Article 3** The application shall be accepted by the Intermediate People's Court at the place of the applicant's domicile, habitual residence, or the location of the property to be enforced.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 申请人应提文申请书，并须附有不违反一个中国原则的台湾地区有关法院民事判决书正本或经证明无误的副本、证明文件。

**Article 4** The applicant shall submit a written application and attach the original or a certified true copy of the civil judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region that does not violate the One-China principle, together with supporting documents.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 申请书应记明以下事项：  
（一）申请人姓名、性别、年龄、职业、身份证件号码、申请时间和住址（申请人为法人或者其他组织的，应记明法人或者其他组织的名称、地址、法定代表人姓名、职务）；  
（二）当事人受传唤和应诉情况及证明文件；  
（三）请求和理由；  
（四）其他需要说明的情况。

**Article 5** The application shall state the following matters:  
(1) The name, gender, age, occupation, identification document number, date of application, and address of the applicant (if the applicant is a legal person or other organization, the name, address, and name and position of the legal representative of the legal person or other organization shall be stated);  
(2) The circumstances of service of summons and appearance of the parties and supporting documents;  
(3) The claim and reasons;  
(4) Other matters that need to be explained.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 人民法院收到申请书，经审查，符合[本规定第四条](#art-4)和第五条的条件的，应当在七日内受理；不符合[本规定第四条](#art-4)和第五条的条件的，不予受理，并在七日内通知申请人，同时说明不受理的理由。

**Article 6** Upon receipt of the application, the People's Court shall, after examination, accept the application within seven days if it meets the requirements of Articles 4 and 5 of these Provisions; if it does not meet such requirements, the People's Court shall not accept the application and shall notify the applicant within seven days, stating the reasons for non-acceptance.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 人民法院审查认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的申请，由审判员组成合议庭进行。

**Article 7** The People's Court shall review the application for recognition of a civil judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region by forming a collegial panel composed of judges.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 人民法院受理申请后，对于台湾地区有关法院民事判决是否生效不能确定的，应告知申请人提交作出判决的法院出具的证明文件。

**Article 8** After accepting the application, if the People's Court cannot determine whether the civil judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region has taken effect, it shall inform the applicant to submit a supporting document issued by the court that rendered the judgment.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 台湾地区有关法院的民事判决具有下列情形之一的，裁定不予认可：  
（一）申请认可的民事判决的效力未确定的；  
（二）申请认可的民事判决，是在被告缺席又未经合法传唤或者在被告无诉讼行为能力又未得到适当代理的情况下作出的；  
（三）案件系人民法院专属管辖的；  
（四）案件的双方当事人订有仲裁协议的；  
（五）案件系人民法院已作出判决，或者外国、境外地区法院作出判决或境外仲裁机构作出仲裁裁决已为人民法院所承认的；  
（六）申请认可的民事判决具有违反国家法律的基本原则，或者损害社会公共利益情形的。

**Article 9** Where a civil judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall rule that it shall not be recognized:  
(1) The validity of the civil judgment for which recognition is sought has not been determined;  
(2) The civil judgment for which recognition is sought was rendered in the absence of the defendant without lawful summons, or where the defendant lacked legal capacity and was not properly represented;  
(3) The case is subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the People's Court;  
(4) The parties to the case have concluded an arbitration agreement;  
(5) The case has been adjudicated by the People's Court, or a judgment of a court of a foreign or extraterritorial region or an arbitral award of an extraterritorial arbitration institution has been recognized by the People's Court;  
(6) The civil judgment for which recognition is sought violates the fundamental principles of the national law or damages the public interest.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 人民法院审查申请后，对于台湾地区有关法院民事判决不具有[本规定第九条](#art-9)所列情形的，裁定认可其效力。

**Article 10** After examining the application, the People's Court shall rule to recognize the validity of the civil judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region if it does not fall under any of the circumstances listed in Article 9 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 申请人委托他人代理申请认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的，应当向人民法院提交由委托人签名或盖章并经当地公证机关公证的授权委托书。

**Article 11** Where an applicant entrusts another person to act as agent in applying for recognition of a civil judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region, the applicant shall submit to the People's Court a power of attorney signed or sealed by the principal and notarized by a local notarial office.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 人民法院受理认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的申请后，对当事人就同一案件事实起诉的，不予受理。

**Article 12** After accepting an application for recognition of a civil judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region, the People's Court shall not accept a prosecution initiated by a party on the same facts of the case.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 案件虽经台湾地区有关法院判决，但当事人未申请认可，而是就同一案件事实向人民法院提起诉讼的，应予受理。

**Article 13** Where a case has been adjudicated by a relevant court in the Taiwan region but the party has not applied for recognition and instead initiates a lawsuit in the People's Court on the same facts of the case, the People's Court shall accept the lawsuit.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 人民法院受理认可申请后，作出裁定前，申请人要求撤回申请的，应当允许。

**Article 14** After the People's Court accepts an application for recognition but before a ruling is made, if the applicant requests to withdraw the application, such withdrawal shall be permitted.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 对人民法院不予认可的民事判决，申请人不得再提出申请，但可以就同一案件事实向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 15** Where a civil judgment is not recognized by the People's Court, the applicant shall not submit another application, but may initiate a lawsuit in the People's Court on the same facts of the case.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 人民法院作出民事判决前，一方当事人申请认可台湾地区有关法院就同一案件事实作出的判决的，应当中止诉讼，对申请进行审查。经审查，对符合认可条件的申请，予以认可，并终结诉讼；对不符合认可条件的，则恢复诉讼。

**Article 16** Before the People's Court renders a civil judgment, if one party applies for recognition of a judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region on the same facts of the case, the People's Court shall suspend the proceedings and examine the application. Upon examination, if the application meets the conditions for recognition, the People's Court shall recognize the judgment and terminate the proceedings; if it does not meet such conditions, the People's Court shall resume the proceedings.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 申请认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的，应当在该判决发生效力后一年内提出。

**Article 17** An application for recognition of a civil judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region shall be filed within one year after the judgment takes effect.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 被认可的台湾地区有关法院民事判决需要执行的，依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》规定的程序办理。

**Article 18** Where a recognized civil judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region requires enforcement, it shall be handled in accordance with the procedures prescribed in the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 申请认可台湾地区有关法院民事裁定和台湾地区仲裁机构裁决的，适用本规定。

**Article 19** These Provisions shall apply to applications for recognition of civil rulings of relevant courts in the Taiwan region and awards of arbitration institutions in the Taiwan region.
