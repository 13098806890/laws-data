# 最高人民法院关于应当允许检察院派书记员随检察长或检察员出庭支持公诉的通知

**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：通〔1986〕3号  
**公布日期**：1986-11-07  
**生效日期**：1986-11-07

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
关于应当允许检察院派书记员随检察长或  
检察员出庭支持公诉的通知  
1986年11月7日      法（司）通〔1986〕3号  
湖北省高级人民法院：  
近接最高人民检察院转来湖北省人民检察院刑事检察处和远安县人民检察院两位同志的信，反映有的法院拒绝检察院派书记员随检察长或检察员出庭办理记录工作一事。经我们研究认为，虽然刑事诉讼法、人民法院组织法和人民检察院组织法对检察院可否派书记员随检察长或检察员出庭支持公诉问题未作明确规定，但从审判实践来看，检察院派书记员随检察长或检察员出庭有利于工作，应当允许。  
特此通知。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Notice on Allowing the People's Procuratorate to Send Clerks to Accompany the Chief Procurator or  
Procurator to Appear in Court to Support Public Prosecution  
November 7, 1986 Fa (Si) Tong [1986] No. 3  
Higher People's Court of Hubei Province:  
Recently, we received letters forwarded by the Supreme People's Procuratorate from two comrades of the Criminal Prosecution Department of the People's Procuratorate of Hubei Province and the People's Procuratorate of Yuan'an County, reporting that some courts refused to allow the People's Procuratorate to send clerks to accompany the Chief Procurator or Procurator to appear in court to perform recording work. Upon our study, it is held that although the Criminal Procedure Law, the Organic Law of the People's Courts, and the Organic Law of the People's Procuratorate do not expressly stipulate whether the People's Procuratorate may send clerks to accompany the Chief Procurator or Procurator to appear in court to support public prosecution, from the perspective of adjudication practice, it is beneficial for the work to have the People's Procuratorate send clerks to accompany the Chief Procurator or Procurator to appear in court, and thus such practice shall be allowed.  
This notice is hereby given.
