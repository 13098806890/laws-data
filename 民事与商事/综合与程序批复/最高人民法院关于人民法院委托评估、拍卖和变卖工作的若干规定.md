# 最高人民法院关于人民法院委托评估、拍卖和变卖工作的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2009〕16号  
**公布日期**：2009-08-24  
**生效日期**：2009-11-20

> 最高人民法院《关于人民法院委托评估、拍卖和变卖工作的若干规定》已于2009年8月24日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1472次会议通过，现予公布，自2009年11月20日起施行。 2009年11月12日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院委托评估、拍卖和 变卖工作的若干规定 法释〔2009〕16号 为规范人民法院委托评估、拍卖和变卖工作，保障当事人的合法权益，维护司法公正，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等有关法律的规定，结合人民法院委托评估、拍卖和变卖工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条  人民法院司法技术管理部门负责本院的委托评估、拍卖和流拍财产的变卖工作，依法对委托评估、拍卖机构的评估、拍卖活动进行监督。 第二条  根据工作需要，下级人民法院可将评估、拍卖和变卖工作报请上级人民法院办理。 第三条  人民法院需要对异地的财产进行评估或拍卖时，可以委托财产所在地人民法院办理。 第四条  人民法院按照公开、公平、择优的原则编制人民法院委托评估、拍卖机构名册。 人民法院编制委托评估、拍卖机构名册，应当先期公告，明确入册机构的条件和评审程序等事项。 第五条  人民法院在编制委托评估、拍卖机构名册时，由司法技术管理部门、审判部门、执行部门组成评审委员会，必要时可邀请评估、拍卖行业的专家参加评审。 第六条  评审委员会对申请加入人民法院委托评估、拍卖名册的机构，应当从资质等级、职业信誉、经营业绩、执业人员情况等方面进行审查、打分，按分数高低经过初审、公示、复审后确定进入名册的机构，并对名册进行动态管理。 第七条  人民法院选择评估、拍卖机构，应当在人民法院委托评估、拍卖机构名册内采取公开随机的方式选定。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  人民法院司法技术管理部门负责本院的委托评估、拍卖和流拍财产的变卖工作，依法对委托评估、拍卖机构的评估、拍卖活动进行监督。

**Article 1** The judicial technical management department of the People's Court shall be responsible for the entrusted appraisal, auction, and sale of property that fails to be auctioned within the court, and shall supervise the appraisal and auction activities of the entrusted appraisal and auction institutions according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  根据工作需要，下级人民法院可将评估、拍卖和变卖工作报请上级人民法院办理。

**Article 2** A lower People's Court may, based on work needs, submit the appraisal, auction, and sale work to a higher People's Court for handling.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  人民法院需要对异地的财产进行评估或拍卖时，可以委托财产所在地人民法院办理。

**Article 3** When a People's Court needs to appraise or auction property located in another place, it may entrust the People's Court at the property's location to handle the matter.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  人民法院按照公开、公平、择优的原则编制人民法院委托评估、拍卖机构名册。  
人民法院编制委托评估、拍卖机构名册，应当先期公告，明确入册机构的条件和评审程序等事项。

**Article 4** The People's Court shall compile a roster of institutions entrusted with appraisal and auction in accordance with the principles of openness, fairness, and merit-based selection.  
When compiling the roster of entrusted appraisal and auction institutions, the People's Court shall make a prior announcement, specifying the conditions for inclusion in the roster and the evaluation procedures, among other matters.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  人民法院在编制委托评估、拍卖机构名册时，由司法技术管理部门、审判部门、执行部门组成评审委员会，必要时可邀请评估、拍卖行业的专家参加评审。

**Article 5** When compiling the roster of entrusted appraisal and auction institutions, the People's Court shall form an evaluation committee composed of the judicial technical management department, adjudication department, and enforcement department. If necessary, experts from the appraisal and auction industry may be invited to participate in the evaluation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  评审委员会对申请加入人民法院委托评估、拍卖名册的机构，应当从资质等级、职业信誉、经营业绩、执业人员情况等方面进行审查、打分，按分数高低经过初审、公示、复审后确定进入名册的机构，并对名册进行动态管理。

**Article 6** The evaluation committee shall examine and score the institutions applying to be included in the roster of entrusted appraisal and auction institutions of the People's Court from aspects such as qualification grade, professional reputation, business performance, and practicing personnel. After preliminary review, public announcement, and re-review based on the scores, the institutions to be included in the roster shall be determined, and the roster shall be managed dynamically.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  人民法院选择评估、拍卖机构，应当在人民法院委托评估、拍卖机构名册内采取公开随机的方式选定。

**Article 7** When selecting an appraisal or auction institution, the People's Court shall adopt an open and random method within the roster of entrusted appraisal and auction institutions of the People's Court.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  人民法院选择评估、拍卖机构，应当通知审判、执行人员到场，视情况可邀请社会有关人员到场监督。

**Article 8** When selecting an appraisal or auction institution, the People's Court shall notify the adjudication and enforcement personnel to be present, and may, depending on the circumstances, invite relevant personnel from the public to be present for supervision.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  人民法院选择评估、拍卖机构，应当提前通知各方当事人到场；当事人不到场的，人民法院可将选择机构的情况，以书面形式送达当事人。

**Article 9** When selecting an appraisal or auction institution, the People's Court shall notify all parties in advance to be present; if a party fails to be present, the People's Court may deliver the information on the selection of the institution to the party in written form.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  评估、拍卖机构选定后，人民法院应当向选定的机构出具委托书，委托书中应当载明本次委托的要求和工作完成的期限等事项。

**Article 10** After the appraisal or auction institution is selected, the People's Court shall issue a letter of entrustment to the selected institution, which shall specify the requirements of this entrustment and the deadline for completing the work, among other matters.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  评估、拍卖机构接受人民法院的委托后，在规定期限内无正当理由不能完成委托事项的，人民法院应当解除委托，重新选择机构，并对其暂停备选资格或从委托评估、拍卖机构名册内除名。

**Article 11** If, after accepting the entrustment of the People's Court, an appraisal or auction institution fails to complete the entrusted matters within the prescribed time limit without justifiable reasons, the People's Court shall rescind the entrustment, re-select an institution, and suspend its candidate qualification or remove it from the roster of entrusted appraisal and auction institutions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  评估机构在工作中需要对现场进行勘验的，人民法院应当提前通知审判、执行人员和当事人到场。当事人不到场的，不影响勘验的进行，但应当有见证人见证。评估机构勘验现场，应当制作现场勘验笔录。  
勘验现场人员、当事人或见证人应当在勘验笔录上签字或盖章确认。

**Article 12** If an appraisal institution needs to conduct an inspection of the site during its work, the People's Court shall notify the adjudication and enforcement personnel and the parties in advance to be present. If a party fails to be present, it shall not affect the conduct of the inspection, but a witness shall witness the inspection. When an appraisal institution inspects the site, it shall make a written record of the on-site inspection.  
The personnel conducting the on-site inspection, the parties, or the witness shall sign or affix their seals on the inspection record for confirmation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  拍卖财产经过评估的，评估价即为第一次拍卖的保留价；未作评估的，保留价由人民法院参照市价确定，并应当征询有关当事人的意见。

**Article 13** If the auctioned property has been appraised, the appraised value shall be the reserve price for the first auction; if it has not been appraised, the reserve price shall be determined by the People's Court with reference to the market price, and the opinions of the relevant parties shall be consulted.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  审判、执行部门未经司法技术管理部门同意擅自委托评估、拍卖，或对流拍财产进行变卖的，按照有关纪律规定追究责任。

**Article 14** If the adjudication or enforcement department, without the consent of the judicial technical management department, entrusts appraisal or auction without authorization, or sells property that fails to be auctioned, liability shall be pursued in accordance with relevant disciplinary regulations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  人民法院司法技术管理部门，在组织评审委员会审查评估、拍卖入册机构，或选择评估、拍卖机构，或对流拍财产进行变卖时，应当通知本院纪检监察部门。纪检监察部门可视情况派员参加。

**Article 15** When the judicial technical management department of the People's Court organizes the evaluation committee to review institutions for inclusion in the appraisal and auction roster, or selects an appraisal or auction institution, or sells property that fails to be auctioned, it shall notify the discipline inspection and supervision department of the court. The discipline inspection and supervision department may, depending on the circumstances, send personnel to participate.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  施行前本院公布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 16** If any judicial interpretation previously published by this court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
