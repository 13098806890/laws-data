# 最高人民法院关于审理海上货运代理纠纷案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2012〕3号  
**公布日期**：2012-02-27  
**生效日期**：2012-05-01

> 最高人民法院《关于审理海上货运代理纠纷案件若干问题的规定》已于2012年1月9日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1538次会议通过，现予公布，自2012年5月1日起施行。 2012年2月27日 最高人民法院 关于审理海上货运代理纠纷案件 若干问题的规定 法释〔2012〕3号 （2012年1月9日最高人民法院审判委员会第1538次会议通过　2012年2月27日最高人民法院公告公布　自2012年5月1日起施行）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定适用于货运代理企业接受委托人委托处理与海上货物运输有关的货运代理事务时发生的下列纠纷：  
（一）因提供订舱、报关、报检、报验、保险服务所发生的纠纷；  
（二）因提供货物的包装、监装、监卸、集装箱装拆箱、分拨、中转服务所发生的纠纷；  
（三）因缮制、交付有关单证、费用结算所发生的纠纷；  
（四）因提供仓储、陆路运输服务所发生的纠纷；  
（五）因处理其他海上货运代理事务所发生的纠纷。

**Article 1** These Provisions shall apply to the following disputes arising from a freight forwarding enterprise accepting a principal's entrustment to handle freight forwarding affairs relating to the carriage of goods by sea:  
(1) disputes arising from the provision of services for booking space, customs declaration, inspection application, survey application, and insurance;  
(2) disputes arising from the provision of services for packaging, supervision of loading, supervision of unloading, container stuffing and stripping, distribution, and transshipment of goods;  
(3) disputes arising from the preparation and delivery of relevant documents and settlement of fees;  
(4) disputes arising from the provision of storage and road transport services;  
(5) disputes arising from handling other maritime freight forwarding affairs.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院审理海上货运代理纠纷案件，认定货运代理企业因处理海上货运代理事务与委托人之间形成代理、运输、仓储等不同法律关系的，应分别适用相关的法律规定。

**Article 2** When a People's Court hears a case of dispute over maritime freight forwarding and determines that the freight forwarding enterprise has formed different legal relationships with the principal, such as agency, carriage, or storage, due to handling maritime freight forwarding affairs, it shall apply the relevant legal provisions separately.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院应根据书面合同约定的权利义务的性质，并综合考虑货运代理企业取得报酬的名义和方式、开具发票的种类和收费项目、当事人之间的交易习惯以及合同实际履行的其他情况，认定海上货运代理合同关系是否成立。

**Article 3** A People's Court shall determine whether a maritime freight forwarding contract relationship is established based on the nature of the rights and obligations stipulated in the written contract, and by comprehensively considering the name and method by which the freight forwarding enterprise obtains remuneration, the types of invoices issued and the charging items, the trade practices between the parties, and other circumstances of the actual performance of the contract.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　货运代理企业在处理海上货运代理事务过程中以自己的名义签发提单、海运单或者其他运输单证，委托人据此主张货运代理企业承担承运人责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
货运代理企业以承运人代理人名义签发提单、海运单或者其他运输单证，但不能证明取得承运人授权，委托人据此主张货运代理企业承担承运人责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 4** Where a freight forwarding enterprise issues a bill of lading, sea waybill, or other transport document in its own name during the course of handling maritime freight forwarding affairs, and the principal claims on that basis that the freight forwarding enterprise shall bear the liability of a carrier, the People's Court shall support such a claim.  
Where a freight forwarding enterprise issues a bill of lading, sea waybill, or other transport document in the name of the carrier's agent but cannot prove that it has obtained authorization from the carrier, and the principal claims on that basis that the freight forwarding enterprise shall bear the liability of a carrier, the People's Court shall support such a claim.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　委托人与货运代理企业约定了转委托权限，当事人就权限范围内的海上货运代理事务主张委托人同意转委托的，人民法院应予支持。  
没有约定转委托权限，货运代理企业或第三人以委托人知道货运代理企业将海上货运代理事务转委托或部分转委托第三人处理而未表示反对为由，主张委托人同意转委托的，人民法院不予支持，但委托人的行为明确表明其接受转委托的除外。

**Article 5** Where the principal and the freight forwarding enterprise have agreed on the authority for sub-entrustment, and a party claims that the principal has consented to the sub-entrustment of maritime freight forwarding affairs within the scope of such authority, the People's Court shall support such a claim.  
Where no authority for sub-entrustment has been agreed upon, and the freight forwarding enterprise or a third party claims that the principal has consented to the sub-entrustment on the ground that the principal knew that the freight forwarding enterprise had sub-entrusted or partially sub-entrusted the maritime freight forwarding affairs to a third party but did not object, the People's Court shall not support such a claim, unless the principal's conduct clearly indicates that the principal accepted the sub-entrustment.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　一方当事人根据双方的交易习惯，有理由相信行为人有权代表对方当事人订立海上货运代理合同，该方当事人依据合同法第四十九条的规定主张合同成立的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 6** Where a party, based on the trade practices between the parties, has reason to believe that the actor is authorized to represent the other party in concluding a maritime freight forwarding contract, and that party claims that the contract is established pursuant to Article 49 of the Contract Law, the People's Court shall support such a claim.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　海上货运代理合同约定货运代理企业交付处理海上货运代理事务取得的单证以委托人支付相关费用为条件，货运代理企业以委托人未支付相关费用为由拒绝交付单证的，人民法院应予支持。  
合同未约定或约定不明确，货运代理企业以委托人未支付相关费用为由拒绝交付单证的，人民法院应予支持，但提单、海运单或者其他运输单证除外。

**Article 7** Where a maritime freight forwarding contract stipulates that the delivery by the freight forwarding enterprise of the documents obtained from handling maritime freight forwarding affairs is conditional upon the principal paying the relevant fees, and the freight forwarding enterprise refuses to deliver the documents on the ground that the principal has not paid the relevant fees, the People's Court shall support such a refusal.  
Where the contract does not stipulate or the stipulation is unclear, and the freight forwarding enterprise refuses to deliver the documents on the ground that the principal has not paid the relevant fees, the People's Court shall support such a refusal, except for bills of lading, sea waybills, or other transport documents.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　货运代理企业接受契约托运人的委托办理订舱事务，同时接受实际托运人的委托向承运人交付货物，实际托运人请求货运代理企业交付其取得的提单、海运单或者其他运输单证的，人民法院应予支持。  
契约托运人是指本人或者委托他人以本人名义或者委托他人为本人与承运人订立海上货物运输合同的人。  
实际托运人是指本人或者委托他人以本人名义或者委托他人为本人将货物交给与海上货物运输合同有关的承运人的人。

**Article 8** Where a freight forwarding enterprise accepts the entrustment from a contractual shipper to handle space booking affairs and simultaneously accepts the entrustment from an actual shipper to deliver the goods to the carrier, and the actual shipper requests the freight forwarding enterprise to deliver the bill of lading, sea waybill, or other transport document obtained by it, the People's Court shall support such a request.  
A contractual shipper means a person who, by himself or through another person acting in his name or on his behalf, concludes a contract of carriage of goods by sea with the carrier.  
An actual shipper means a person who, by himself or through another person acting in his name or on his behalf, delivers the goods to the carrier in connection with the contract of carriage of goods by sea.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　货运代理企业按照概括委托权限完成海上货运代理事务，请求委托人支付相关合理费用的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 9** Where a freight forwarding enterprise, having completed the maritime freight forwarding affairs within the scope of general entrustment authority, requests the principal to pay the relevant reasonable fees, the People's Court shall support such a request.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　委托人以货运代理企业处理海上货运代理事务给委托人造成损失为由，主张由货运代理企业承担相应赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持，但货运代理企业证明其没有过错的除外。

**Article 10** Where the principal claims that the freight forwarding enterprise shall bear corresponding compensation liability on the ground that the freight forwarding enterprise's handling of maritime freight forwarding affairs has caused losses to the principal, the People's Court shall support such a claim, unless the freight forwarding enterprise proves that it is not at fault.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　货运代理企业未尽谨慎义务，与未在我国交通主管部门办理提单登记的无船承运业务经营者订立海上货物运输合同，造成委托人损失的，应承担相应的赔偿责任。

**Article 11** Where a freight forwarding enterprise fails to exercise the duty of care and concludes a contract of carriage of goods by sea with a non-vessel-operating common carrier that has not registered its bill of lading with the competent transport authority of China, thereby causing losses to the principal, it shall bear the corresponding compensation liability.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　货运代理企业接受未在我国交通主管部门办理提单登记的无船承运业务经营者的委托签发提单，当事人主张由货运代理企业和无船承运业务经营者对提单项下的损失承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
货运代理企业承担赔偿责任后，有权向无船承运业务经营者追偿。

**Article 12** Where a freight forwarding enterprise issues a bill of lading upon the entrustment of a non-vessel-operating common carrier that has not registered its bill of lading with the competent transport authority of China, and a party claims that the freight forwarding enterprise and the non-vessel-operating common carrier shall bear joint and several liability for the losses under the bill of lading, the People's Court shall support such a claim.  
After bearing compensation liability, the freight forwarding enterprise shall have the right to seek recourse against the non-vessel-operating common carrier.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　因[本规定第一条](#art-1)所列纠纷提起的诉讼，由海事法院管辖。

**Article 13** An action brought for a dispute listed in Article 1 of these Provisions shall be under the jurisdiction of a maritime court.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院在案件审理过程中，发现不具有无船承运业务经营资格的货运代理企业违反《中华人民共和国国际海运条例》的规定，以自己的名义签发提单、海运单或者其他运输单证的，应当向有关交通主管部门发出司法建议，建议交通主管部门予以处罚。

**Article 14** Where, during the trial of a case, a People's Court discovers that a freight forwarding enterprise that does not have the qualification to engage in non-vessel-operating common carrier business has violated the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on International Maritime Transportation by issuing a bill of lading, sea waybill, or other transport document in its own name, it shall issue a judicial recommendation to the relevant transport authority, recommending that the transport authority impose a penalty.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　本规定不适用于与沿海、内河货物运输有关的货运代理纠纷案件。

**Article 15** These Provisions shall not apply to cases of disputes over freight forwarding related to the carriage of goods by coastal or inland waters.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本规定施行前本院作出的有关司法解释与本规定相抵触的，以本规定为准。  
本规定施行后，案件尚在一审或者二审阶段的，适用本规定；本规定施行前已经终审的案件，本规定施行后当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本规定。

**Article 16** Where any relevant judicial interpretation made by this Court before these Provisions come into force conflicts with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.  
After these Provisions come into force, cases that are still in the first instance or second instance stage shall be governed by these Provisions; cases that have been finally adjudicated before these Provisions come into force, and for which a party applies for a retrial after these Provisions come into force or which are decided to be retried under the trial supervision procedure, shall not be governed by these Provisions.
