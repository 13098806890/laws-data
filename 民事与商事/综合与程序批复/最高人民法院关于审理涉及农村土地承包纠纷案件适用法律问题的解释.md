# 最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村土地承包纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2005〕6号  
**公布日期**：2005-07-29  
**生效日期**：2005-09-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村土地承包纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释》已于2005年3月29日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1346次会议通过，现予公布，自2005年9月1日起施行。 2005年7月29日 最高人民法院 关于审理涉及农村土地承包纠纷案件 适用法律问题的解释 法释〔2005〕6号 （2005年3月29日最高人民法院审判委员会第1346次会议通过　2005年7月29日最高人民法院公告公布　自2005年9月1日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　下列涉及农村土地承包民事纠纷，人民法院应当依法受理：  
（一）承包合同纠纷；  
（二）承包经营权侵权纠纷；  
（三）承包经营权流转纠纷；  
（四）承包地征收补偿费用分配纠纷；  
（五）承包经营权继承纠纷。  
集体经济组织成员因未实际取得土地承包经营权提起民事诉讼的，人民法院应当告知其向有关行政主管部门申请解决。  
集体经济组织成员就用于分配的土地补偿费数额提起民事诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 1** With respect to the following civil disputes involving rural land contractual management, the People's Court shall accept them according to law:  
(1) disputes over contract for undertaking;  
(2) disputes over infringement of the right to contractual management;  
(3) disputes over transfer of the right to contractual management;  
(4) disputes over the distribution of compensation fees for expropriation of contracted land;  
(5) disputes over inheritance of the right to contractual management.  
Where a member of a collective economic organization initiates an civil action for failing to actually obtain the right to contractual management of land, the People's Court shall inform him to apply to the relevant administrative department for resolution.  
Where a member of a collective economic organization initiates an civil action with respect to the amount of land compensation fees to be distributed, the People's Court shall not accept the case.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人自愿达成书面仲裁协议的，受诉人民法院应当参照最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉若干问题的意见》第145条至第148条的规定处理。  
当事人未达成书面仲裁协议，一方当事人向农村土地承包仲裁机构申请仲裁，另一方当事人提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理，并书面通知仲裁机构。但另一方当事人接受仲裁管辖后又起诉的，人民法院不予受理。  
当事人对仲裁裁决不服并在收到裁决书之日起三十日内提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 2** Where the parties voluntarily reach a written arbitration agreement, the People's Court accepting the case shall handle the case by reference to the provisions of Articles 145 to 148 of the Opinions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where the parties have not reached a written arbitration agreement, and one party applies for arbitration to a rural land contractual arbitration institution while the other party initiates an action, the People's Court shall accept the case and shall notify the arbitration institution in writing. However, if the other party, after accepting the arbitration jurisdiction, subsequently initiates an action, the People's Court shall not accept the case.  
Where a party refuses to accept the arbitration award and initiates an action within thirty days from the date of receipt of the award, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　承包合同纠纷，以发包方和承包方为当事人。  
前款所称承包方是指以家庭承包方式承包本集体经济组织农村土地的农户，以及以其他方式承包农村土地的单位或者个人。

**Article 3** For a dispute over a contract for undertaking, the party who awards the contract and the party who undertakes the contract shall be the parties.  
The term "party who undertakes the contract" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph refers to a farming household that undertakes rural land of the collective economic organization in the form of family undertaking, and a unit or individual that undertakes rural land in other forms.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　农户成员为多人的，由其代表人进行诉讼。  
农户代表人按照下列情形确定：  
（一）土地承包经营权证等证书上记载的人；  
（二）未依法登记取得土地承包经营权证等证书的，为在承包合同上签字的人；  
（三）前两项规定的人死亡、丧失民事行为能力或者因其他原因无法进行诉讼的，为农户成员推选的人。  
二、家庭承包纠纷案件的处理

**Article 4** Where a farming household has multiple members, the representative thereof shall conduct the litigation.  
The representative of a farming household shall be determined in accordance with the following circumstances:  
(1) the person recorded on the certificate such as the certificate of right to contractual management of land;  
(2) where no such certificate as the certificate of right to contractual management of land has been obtained through registration according to law, the person who signed the contract for undertaking;  
(3) where the person specified in item (1) or (2) dies, loses civil capacity, or is unable to conduct the litigation for other reasons, the person elected by the members of the farming household.  
II. Handling of Cases Involving Disputes over Family Undertaking

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　承包合同中有关收回、调整承包地的约定违反[农村土地承包法第二十六条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国农村土地承包法.md#art-26)、第二十七条、第三十条、第三十五条规定的，应当认定该约定无效。

**Article 5** Where an agreement in a contract for undertaking concerning the taking back or adjustment of the contracted land violates the provisions of Articles 26, 27, 30 and 35 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Rural Land Contracting, such agreement shall be determined as null and void.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　因发包方违法收回、调整承包地，或者因发包方收回承包方弃耕、撂荒的承包地产生的纠纷，按照下列情形，分别处理：  
（一）发包方未将承包地另行发包，承包方请求返还承包地的，应予支持；  
（二）发包方已将承包地另行发包给第三人，承包方以发包方和第三人为共同被告，请求确认其所签订的承包合同无效、返还承包地并赔偿损失的，应予支持。但属于承包方弃耕、撂荒情形的，对其赔偿损失的诉讼请求，不予支持。  
前款第（二）项所称的第三人，请求受益方补偿其在承包地上的合理投入的，应予支持。

**Article 6** Disputes arising from the illegal taking back or adjustment of contracted land by the party who awards the contract, or from the taking back of contracted land abandoned or left uncultivated by the party who undertakes the contract shall be handled respectively in accordance with the following circumstances:  
(1) where the party who awards the contract has not separately awarded the contracted land to another party, the request of the party who undertakes the contract for the return of the contracted land shall be supported;  
(2) where the party who awards the contract has separately awarded the contracted land to a third party, the request of the party who undertakes the contract, taking the party who awards the contract and the third party as co-defendants, for confirmation that the contract for undertaking concluded between them is null and void, for return of the contracted land and for compensation for losses shall be supported. Provided that in the case of abandonment or leaving land uncultivated by the party who undertakes the contract, the claim for compensation for losses shall not be supported.  
Where a third party as mentioned in item (2) of the preceding paragraph requests the party benefiting therefrom to compensate for the reasonable investment made on the contracted land, such request shall be supported.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　承包合同约定或者土地承包经营权证等证书记载的承包期限短于农村土地承包法规定的期限，承包方请求延长的，应予支持。

**Article 7** Where the term of undertaking as agreed in the contract for undertaking or as recorded in the certificate such as the certificate of right to contractual management of land is shorter than the term prescribed by the Law of the People's Republic of China on Rural Land Contracting, the request of the party who undertakes the contract for extension shall be supported.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　承包方违反[农村土地承包法第十七条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国农村土地承包法.md#art-17)规定，将承包地用于非农建设或者对承包地造成永久性损害，发包方请求承包方停止侵害、恢复原状或者赔偿损失的，应予支持。

**Article 8** Where the party who undertakes the contract, in violation of the provisions of Article 17 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Rural Land Contracting, uses the contracted land for non-agricultural construction or causes permanent damage to the contracted land, the request of the party who awards the contract for the cessation of the infringement, restoration to the original condition, or compensation for losses shall be supported.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　发包方根据[农村土地承包法第二十六条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国农村土地承包法.md#art-26)规定收回承包地前，承包方已经以转包、出租等形式将其土地承包经营权流转给第三人，且流转期限尚未届满，因流转价款收取产生的纠纷，按照下列情形，分别处理：  
（一）承包方已经一次性收取了流转价款，发包方请求承包方返还剩余流转期限的流转价款的，应予支持；  
（二）流转价款为分期支付，发包方请求第三人按照流转合同的约定支付流转价款的，应予支持。

**Article 9** Before the party who awards the contract takes back the contracted land according to Article 26 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Rural Land Contracting, if the party who undertakes the contract has already transferred his right to contractual management of land to a third party through subcontract, lease or other forms, and the term of the transfer has not yet expired, disputes arising from the collection of the transfer price shall be handled respectively in accordance with the following circumstances:  
(1) where the party who undertakes the contract has collected the transfer price in a lump sum, the request of the party who awards the contract for the return of the transfer price corresponding to the remaining term of the transfer from the party who undertakes the contract shall be supported;  
(2) where the transfer price is paid in installments, the request of the party who awards the contract for the payment of the transfer price by the third party in accordance with the transfer contract shall be supported.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　承包方交回承包地不符合[农村土地承包法第二十九条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国农村土地承包法.md#art-29)规定程序的，不得认定其为自愿交回。

**Article 10** Where the act of returning the contracted land by the party who undertakes the contract does not comply with the procedures prescribed in Article 29 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Rural Land Contracting, such act shall not be determined as a voluntary return.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　土地承包经营权流转中，本集体经济组织成员在流转价款、流转期限等主要内容相同的条件下主张优先权的，应予支持。但下列情形除外：  
（一）在书面公示的合理期限内未提出优先权主张的；  
（二）未经书面公示，在本集体经济组织以外的人开始使用承包地两个月内未提出优先权主张的。

**Article 11** In the transfer of the right to contractual management of land, where a member of the collective economic organization asserts the right of priority under the condition that the main terms such as the transfer price and the term of transfer are the same, such assertion shall be supported, except under the following circumstances:  
(1) the right of priority is not asserted within a reasonable period after written public notice;  
(2) without written public notice, the right of priority is not asserted within two months from the date when a person outside the collective economic organization begins to use the contracted land.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　发包方强迫承包方将土地承包经营权流转给第三人，承包方请求确认其与第三人签订的流转合同无效的，应予支持。  
发包方阻碍承包方依法流转土地承包经营权，承包方请求排除妨碍、赔偿损失的，应予支持。

**Article 12** Where the party who awards the contract forces the party who undertakes the contract to transfer his right to contractual management of land to a third party, the request of the party who undertakes the contract for confirmation that the transfer contract concluded between him and the third party is null and void shall be supported.  
Where the party who awards the contract obstructs the party who undertakes the contract from transferring the right to contractual management of land according to law, the request of the party who undertakes the contract for removal of the obstruction and compensation for losses shall be supported.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　承包方未经发包方同意，采取转让方式流转其土地承包经营权的，转让合同无效。但发包方无法定理由不同意或者拖延表态的除外。

**Article 13** Where the party who undertakes the contract, without the consent of the party who awards the contract, transfers his right to contractual management of land by means of assignment, the assignment contract shall be null and void, except where the party who awards the contract has no legal reason to withhold consent or delays in expressing its position.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　承包方依法采取转包、出租、互换或者其他方式流转土地承包经营权，发包方仅以该土地承包经营权流转合同未报其备案为由，请求确认合同无效的，不予支持。

**Article 14** Where the party who undertakes the contract transfers the right to contractual management of land by means of subcontract, lease, exchange or other forms according to law, the request of the party who awards the contract for confirmation of the nullity of the contract solely on the ground that the land contractual management right transfer contract has not been filed with him shall not be supported.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　承包方以其土地承包经营权进行抵押或者抵偿债务的，应当认定无效。对因此造成的损失，当事人有过错的，应当承担相应的民事责任。

**Article 15** Where the party who undertakes the contract mortgages the right to contractual management of land or uses it to offset debts, such act shall be determined as null and void. For the losses caused thereby, if the party is at fault, it shall assume the corresponding civil liability.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　因承包方不收取流转价款或者向对方支付费用的约定产生纠纷，当事人协商变更无法达成一致，且继续履行又显失公平的，人民法院可以根据发生变更的客观情况，按照公平原则处理。

**Article 16** Where a dispute arises from an agreement that the party who undertakes the contract does not collect the transfer price or pays fees to the other party, and the parties fail to reach an agreement through consultation on the modification thereof, and continuous performance would be obviously unfair, the People's Court may, in light of the changed objective circumstances, handle the case in accordance with the principle of fairness.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　当事人对转包、出租地流转期限没有约定或者约定不明的，参照合同法第二百三十二条规定处理。除当事人另有约定或者属于林地承包经营外，承包地交回的时间应当在农作物收获期结束后或者下一耕种期开始前。  
对提高土地生产能力的投入，对方当事人请求承包方给予相应补偿的，应予支持。

**Article 17** Where the parties have not agreed upon the term of transfer by subcontract or lease, or the agreement is unclear, the matter shall be handled by reference to the provisions of Article 232 of the Contract Law of the People's Republic of China. Unless the parties have otherwise agreed or the case involves the undertaking of forest land, the time for returning the contracted land shall be after the end of the crop harvest period or before the start of the next cultivation period.  
Where the other party requests the party who undertakes the contract to make appropriate compensation for the investment made to improve the land productivity, such request shall be supported.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　发包方或者其他组织、个人擅自截留、扣缴承包收益或者土地承包经营权流转收益，承包方请求返还的，应予支持。  
发包方或者其他组织、个人主张抵消的，不予支持。  
三、其他方式承包纠纷的处理

**Article 18** Where the party who awards the contract or any other organization or individual intercepts or withholds without authorization the proceeds from undertaking or the proceeds from the transfer of the right to contractual management of land, the request of the party who undertakes the contract for the return thereof shall be supported.  
Where the party who awards the contract or any other organization or individual claims set-off, such claim shall not be supported.  
III. Handling of Disputes over Undertaking in Other Forms

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本集体经济组织成员在承包费、承包期限等主要内容相同的条件下主张优先承包权的，应予支持。但在发包方将农村土地发包给本集体经济组织以外的单位或者个人，已经法律规定的民主议定程序通过，并由乡（镇）人民政府批准后主张优先承包权的，不予支持。

**Article 19** Where a member of a collective economic organization asserts the right of priority to undertake under the condition that the main terms such as the contract fee and the term of undertaking are the same, such assertion shall be supported. However, if after the party who awards the contract has awarded the rural land to a unit or individual outside the collective economic organization, the matter has been approved through the democratic deliberation procedure prescribed by law and approved by the people's government of the township (town), the assertion of the right of priority to undertake thereafter shall not be supported.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　发包方就同一土地签订两个以上承包合同，承包方均主张取得土地承包经营权的，按照下列情形，分别处理：  
（一）已经依法登记的承包方，取得土地承包经营权；  
（二）均未依法登记的，生效在先合同的承包方取得土地承包经营权；  
（三）依前两项规定无法确定的，已经根据承包合同合法占有使用承包地的人取得土地承包经营权，但争议发生后一方强行先占承包地的行为和事实，不得作为确定土地承包经营权的依据。

**Article 20** Where the party who awards the contract concludes two or more contracts for undertaking with respect to the same land, and all the parties who undertake the contract claim to have obtained the right to contractual management of land, the matter shall be handled respectively in accordance with the following circumstances:  
(1) the party who has been registered according to law obtains the right to contractual management of land;  
(2) where none of the parties have been registered according to law, the party under the contract that first takes effect obtains the right to contractual management of land;  
(3) where it cannot be determined in accordance with the preceding two items, the person who has lawfully taken possession and used the contracted land under the contract for undertaking obtains the right to contractual management of land; provided that the act and fact of forcible prior possession of the contracted land by one party after the dispute arises shall not be used as the basis for determining the right to contractual management of land.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　承包方未依法登记取得土地承包经营权证等证书，即以转让、出租、入股、抵押等方式流转土地承包经营权，发包方请求确认该流转无效的，应予支持。但非因承包方原因未登记取得土地承包经营权证等证书的除外。  
承包方流转土地承包经营权，除法律或者本解释有特殊规定外，按照有关家庭承包土地承包经营权流转的规定处理。  
四、土地征收补偿费用分配及土地承包经营权继承纠纷的处理

**Article 21** Where the party who undertakes the contract, without having obtained the certificate such as the certificate of right to contractual management of land through registration according to law, transfers the right to contractual management of land by means of assignment, lease, shareholding, mortgage, etc., the request of the party who awards the contract for confirmation of the nullity of such transfer shall be supported, except where the failure to register and obtain the certificate such as the certificate of right to contractual management of land is not attributable to the party who undertakes the contract.  
Where the party who undertakes the contract transfers the right to contractual management of land, unless otherwise provided by law or this Interpretation, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions concerning the transfer of the right to contractual management of land in family undertaking.  
IV. Handling of Disputes over Distribution of Compensation Fees for Land Expropriation and Inheritance of the Right to Contractual Management of Land

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　承包地被依法征收，承包方请求发包方给付已经收到的地上附着物和青苗的补偿费的，应予支持。  
承包方已将土地承包经营权以转包、出租等方式流转给第三人的，除当事人另有约定外，青苗补偿费归实际投入人所有，地上附着物补偿费归附着物所有人所有。

**Article 22** Where the contracted land is lawfully expropriated, and the party who undertakes the contract requests the party who awards the contract to pay the compensation fees for the above-ground attachments and young crops that have been received, such request shall be supported.  
Where the party who undertakes the contract has transferred the right to contractual management of land to a third party through subcontract, lease or other forms, unless the parties have otherwise agreed, the compensation fees for young crops shall belong to the person who actually made the investment, and the compensation fees for above-ground attachments shall belong to the owner of the attachments.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　承包地被依法征收，放弃统一安置的家庭承包方，请求发包方给付已经收到的安置补助费的，应予支持。

**Article 23** Where the contracted land is lawfully expropriated, and the party who undertakes the contract in a family undertaking, who has given up unified resettlement, requests the party who awards the contract to pay the resettlement subsidies that have been received, such request shall be supported.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　农村集体经济组织或者村民委员会、村民小组，可以依照法律规定的民主议定程序，决定在本集体经济组织内部分配已经收到的土地补偿费。征地补偿安置方案确定时已经具有本集体经济组织成员资格的人，请求支付相应份额的，应予支持。但已报全国人大常委会、国务院备案的地方性法规、自治条例和单行条例、地方政府规章对土地补偿费在农村集体经济组织内部的分配办法另有规定的除外。

**Article 24** A rural collective economic organization, or a villagers' committee or villagers' group may decide on the distribution within the collective economic organization of the land compensation fees already received, in accordance with the democratic deliberation procedure prescribed by law. If a person who already has the membership qualification of the collective economic organization at the time when the land expropriation compensation and resettlement plan is finalized requests payment of the corresponding share, such request shall be supported, except where the local regulations, autonomous regulations and separate regulations, and local government rules that have been filed with the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress and the State Council for record have otherwise provided for the method of distribution of land compensation fees within the rural collective economic organization.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　林地家庭承包中，承包方的继承人请求在承包期内继续承包的，应予支持。  
其他方式承包中，承包方的继承人或者权利义务承受者请求在承包期内继续承包的，应予支持。  
五、其他规定

**Article 25** In the family undertaking of forest land, where the successor of the party who undertakes the contract requests to continue the undertaking within the term of the contract, such request shall be supported.  
In the undertaking in other forms, where the successor or the successor to the rights and obligations of the party who undertakes the contract requests to continue the undertaking within the term of the contract, such request shall be supported.  
V. Miscellaneous Provisions

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　人民法院在审理涉及[本解释第五条](#art-5)、第六条第一款第（二）项及第二款、第十六条的纠纷案件时，应当着重进行调解。必要时可以委托人民调解组织进行调解。

**Article 26** When hearing a dispute case involving Articles 5, item (2) of paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 of Article 6, and Article 16 of this Interpretation, the People's Court shall focus on mediation. When necessary, it may entrust a people's mediation organization to conduct mediation.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本解释自2005年9月1日起施行。施行后受理的第一审案件，适用本解释的规定。  
施行前已经生效的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 27** This Interpretation shall come into force as of September 1, 2005. The provisions of this Interpretation shall apply to cases of first instance accepted after its coming into force.  
Where any judicial interpretation that has taken effect before the coming into force of this Interpretation is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
