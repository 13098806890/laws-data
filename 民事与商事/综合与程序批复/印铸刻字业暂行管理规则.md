# 印铸刻字业暂行管理规则

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2024-12-06  
**生效日期**：2025-01-20

> （政务院政治法律委员会批准　1951年8月15日公安部发布　根据2024年12月6日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订） 第一条　为加强社会治安，保障印铸刻字业的合法经营，防范不法分子伪造假冒，特制定本规则。 第二条　凡经营铸造厂、制版社（制造钢印、铜版、胶版、石印版、珂罗版、火印、锌印、证明牌号等）、印刷局（以机械或化学材料印刷簿册、证券、商标等）、证章店、刻字店、刻字摊（雕刻戳记、印版、印章、胶皮印等）及所有上属性质之营业者，不论专营、兼营、公营、私营，（国家机关专用不以营业为目的者例外）或属何国籍，除法令另有规定者外，均依本规则管理之。 第三条　公章刻制经营者取得市场监管部门核发的营业执照后，应当在5日内将以下信息材料向所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案： （一）营业执照复印件； （二）法定代表人、经营负责人及从业人员有效身份证件复印件； （三）标注安全防范设施的经营场所内部结构平面图； （四）公章刻制和信息备案设备清单； （五）内部管理制度和安全制度。 公安机关能够通过部门间信息共享获得的备案信息，不要求当事人提供。 公章刻制经营者上述备案信息发生变化的，应当自有关变化发生之日起15日内向原备案公安机关更新备案信息。 公章刻制经营者终止公章刻制业务的，应当及时向公安机关办理备案注销。 第四条　凡在本规则颁布前，已开业之印铸刻字业，均须补办第三条规定之手续。 第五条　凡经营印铸刻字业者，均须遵守下列事项： （一）公章刻制经营者应当核验刻制公章的证明材料，采集用章单位、公章刻制申请人的基本信息，并应当在刻制公章后1日内，将用章单位、公章刻制申请人等基本信息及印模、刻制公章的证明材料报所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为加强社会治安，保障印铸刻字业的合法经营，防范不法分子伪造假冒，特制定本规则。

**Article 1** These Rules are formulated to strengthen public security, ensure the lawful operation of the printing, casting, and engraving industry, and prevent lawless elements from engaging in forgery and counterfeiting.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　凡经营铸造厂、制版社（制造钢印、铜版、胶版、石印版、珂罗版、火印、锌印、证明牌号等）、印刷局（以机械或化学材料印刷簿册、证券、商标等）、证章店、刻字店、刻字摊（雕刻戳记、印版、印章、胶皮印等）及所有上属性质之营业者，不论专营、兼营、公营、私营，（国家机关专用不以营业为目的者例外）或属何国籍，除法令另有规定者外，均依本规则管理之。

**Article 2** All persons engaged in the business of foundries, plate-making studios (manufacturing steel seals, copper plates, offset plates, lithographic plates, collotype plates, fire stamps, zinc stamps, certificate plates, etc.), printing houses (printing books, securities, trademarks, etc. by mechanical or chemical means), badge shops, seal-engraving shops, seal-engraving stalls (engraving stamps, printing plates, seals, rubber stamps, etc.), and all businesses of a similar nature, whether operating as specialized, concurrent, public, private enterprises (with the exception of state organs using such services exclusively for non-business purposes) or of any nationality, shall be governed by these Rules, unless otherwise provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　公章刻制经营者取得市场监管部门核发的营业执照后，应当在5日内将以下信息材料向所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案：  
（一）营业执照复印件；  
（二）法定代表人、经营负责人及从业人员有效身份证件复印件；  
（三）标注安全防范设施的经营场所内部结构平面图；  
（四）公章刻制和信息备案设备清单；  
（五）内部管理制度和安全制度。  
公安机关能够通过部门间信息共享获得的备案信息，不要求当事人提供。  
公章刻制经营者上述备案信息发生变化的，应当自有关变化发生之日起15日内向原备案公安机关更新备案信息。  
公章刻制经营者终止公章刻制业务的，应当及时向公安机关办理备案注销。

**Article 3** After obtaining a business license issued by the market supervision and administration department, an official seal engraving business operator shall, within 5 days, file the following information and materials with the public security organ of the people's government at the county level where it is located:  
(1) A copy of the business license;  
(2) Copies of the valid identity documents of the legal representative, person in charge of operations, and employees;  
(3) A floor plan of the internal structure of the business premises indicating security and prevention facilities;  
(4) A list of equipment for official seal engraving and information filing;  
(5) Internal management systems and security systems.  
Information that the public security organ may obtain through inter-departmental information sharing shall not be required to be provided by the party.  
If any of the filing information mentioned above of the official seal engraving business operator changes, it shall update the filing information with the original filing public security organ within 15 days from the date of such change.  
If an official seal engraving business operator terminates the official seal engraving business, it shall promptly go through the cancellation of the filing with the public security organ.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　凡在本规则颁布前，已开业之印铸刻字业，均须补办第三条规定之手续。

**Article 4** All printing, casting, and engraving businesses that have commenced operations prior to the promulgation of these Rules shall undergo the procedures prescribed in Article 3.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　凡经营印铸刻字业者，均须遵守下列事项：  
（一）公章刻制经营者应当核验刻制公章的证明材料，采集用章单位、公章刻制申请人的基本信息，并应当在刻制公章后1日内，将用章单位、公章刻制申请人等基本信息及印模、刻制公章的证明材料报所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案。  
（二）凡经营印铸刻字业者，均需备制营业登记簿，以备查验。属本条第一项规定之各印制品，承制者一律不准留样，不准仿制，或私自翻印。  
（三）遇有下列情形之一者，须迅速报告当地人民公安机关：  
1.伪造或仿造布告、护照、委任状、袖章、符号、胸章、证券及各机关之文件等。  
2.私自定制各机关、团体、学校、公营企业之钢印、火印、徽章、证明、号牌或仿制者。  
3.遇有定制非法之团体、机关戳记、印件、徽章或仿制者。  
4.印制反对人民民主、生产建设及宣传封建等各种反动印刷品者。  
（四）凡印刷铸刻本条第三项所规定之各项物品者，除没收其原料及成品外，得按照情节之轻重，予以惩处。  
（五）对人民公安机关之执行检查职务人员，应予协助进行。

**Article 5** All persons engaged in the printing, casting, and engraving industry shall comply with the following:  
(1) An official seal engraving business operator shall verify the supporting materials for engraving the official seal, collect the basic information of the seal-using entity and the applicant for official seal engraving, and shall, within 1 day after engraving the official seal, report the basic information of the seal-using entity and the applicant for official seal engraving, the seal impression, and the supporting materials for engraving the official seal to the public security organ of the people's government at the county level where it is located for filing.  
(2) All persons engaged in the printing, casting, and engraving industry shall prepare a business registration book for inspection. For the printed products listed in item (1) of this Article, the producer shall not keep samples, nor imitate or privately reprint them.  
(3) In any of the following circumstances, a report shall be made promptly to the local people's public security organ:  
1. Forging or imitating notices, passports, letters of appointment, armbands, symbols, badges, securities, and documents of various organs, etc.  
2. Privately ordering or imitating steel seals, fire stamps, emblems, certificates, license plates of various organs, organizations, schools, or public enterprises.  
3. Ordering or imitating stamps, printed matters, emblems of illegal groups or organs.  
4. Printing various reactionary printed matters that oppose people's democracy and production construction, or propagate feudalism.  
(4) Any person who prints, casts, or engraves any of the items stipulated in item (3) of this Article shall, in addition to confiscation of the raw materials and finished products, be penalized according to the severity of the circumstances.  
(5) Assistance shall be rendered to personnel of the people's public security organ in the performance of their inspection duties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　公安机关可以通过网络等方式，便利公章刻制经营者备案，并应当向备案的公章刻制经营者免费提供或者协助其安装公章刻制信息备案系统软件。

**Article 6** Public security organs may use the Internet or other means to facilitate the filing by official seal engraving business operators, and shall provide free of charge to, or assist in the installation for, the filing official seal engraving business operators of the official seal engraving information filing system software.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　违反[本规则第三条](#art-3)第一款、第三款规定的，由公安机关责令限期改正，予以警告；逾期不改正的，对公章刻制经营者处3000元以上3万元以下罚款。公章刻制经营者备案时提供虚假信息的，由公安机关责令限期改正，并处5000元以上1万元以下罚款；逾期不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下罚款。  
违反[本规则第五条](#art-5)第一项规定的，由公安机关责令限期改正，予以警告；逾期不改正的，责令停业整顿1个月至3个月，对公章刻制经营者并处5000元以上5万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处500元以上5000元以下罚款；情节较重的，由市场监管部门吊销营业执照。

**Article 7** Whoever violates the provisions of paragraph 1 or paragraph 3 of Article 3 of these Rules shall be ordered by the public security organ to make corrections within a specified period and given a warning; if no correction is made within the specified period, the official seal engraving business operator shall be fined not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan. Where an official seal engraving business operator provides false information when filing, the public security organ shall order it to make corrections within a specified period and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan; if no correction is made within the specified period, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Whoever violates the provisions of item (1) of Article 5 of these Rules shall be ordered by the public security organ to make corrections within a specified period and given a warning; if no correction is made within the specified period, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification for a period of 1 to 3 months, and the official seal engraving business operator shall be concurrently fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 500 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the business license shall be revoked by the market supervision and administration department.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　凡遵照本规则各条规定进行报告、检举，因而查获重大罪犯、破获重大案件者，由公安局酌情予以名誉或物质奖励。

**Article 8** Any person who makes reports or gives information in accordance with the provisions of these Rules, thereby leading to the apprehension of major offenders or the solving of major cases, shall be given honorary or material rewards by the public security bureau at its discretion.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　省级以上人民公安机关，得依据本规则之精神，制定补充办法；县（市）级人民公安机关如有制定补充办法之必要时，须经省级人民公安机关批准。

**Article 9** People's public security organs at or above the provincial level may formulate supplementary measures in accordance with the spirit of these Rules; if a people's public security organ at the county (city) level finds it necessary to formulate supplementary measures, it shall obtain approval from the people's public security organ at the provincial level.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　本规则经中央人民政府政务院政治法律委员会批准，由中央人民政府公安部发布施行。

**Article 10** These Rules are approved by the Political and Legal Affairs Committee of the Government Administration Council of the Central People's Government and are promulgated and come into force by the Ministry of Public Security of the Central People's Government.
