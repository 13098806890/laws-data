# 最高人民法院关于审理物业服务纠纷案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2009〕8号  
**公布日期**：2009-04-20  
**生效日期**：2009-10-01

> 最高人民法院《关于审理物业服务纠纷案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2009年4月20日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1466次会议通过，现予公布，自2009年10月1日起施行。 2009年5月15日 最高人民法院 关于审理物业服务纠纷案件具体应用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2009〕8号 为正确审理物业服务纠纷案件，依法保护当事人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》、《中华人民共和国物权法》、《中华人民共和国合同法》等法律规定，结合民事审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条  建设单位依法与物业服务企业签订的前期物业服务合同，以及业主委员会与业主大会依法选聘的物业服务企业签订的物业服务合同，对业主具有约束力。业主以其并非合同当事人为由提出抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。 第二条  符合下列情形之一，业主委员会或者业主请求确认合同或者合同相关条款无效的，人民法院应予支持： （一）物业服务企业将物业服务区域内的全部物业服务业务一并委托他人而签订的委托合同； （二）物业服务合同中免除物业服务企业责任、加重业主委员会或者业主责任、排除业主委员会或者业主主要权利的条款。 前款所称物业服务合同包括前期物业服务合同。 第三条  物业服务企业不履行或者不完全履行物业服务合同约定的或者法律、法规规定以及相关行业规范确定的维修、养护、管理和维护义务，业主请求物业服务企业承担继续履行、采取补救措施或者赔偿损失等违约责任的，人民法院应予支持。 物业服务企业公开作出的服务承诺及制定的服务细则，应当认定为物业服务合同的组成部分。 第四条  业主违反物业服务合同或者法律、法规、管理规约，实施妨害物业服务与管理的行为，物业服务企业请求业主承担恢复原状、停止侵害、排除妨害等相应民事责任的，人民法院应予支持。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  建设单位依法与物业服务企业签订的前期物业服务合同，以及业主委员会与业主大会依法选聘的物业服务企业签订的物业服务合同，对业主具有约束力。业主以其并非合同当事人为由提出抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 1** The preliminary property service contract concluded by the construction entity according to law with a property service enterprise, and the property service contract concluded by the业主委员会 with the property service enterprise legally selected by the owners' assembly, shall be binding on the owners. Where an owner raises a defense on the ground that he is not a party to the contract, the People's Court shall not support such a defense.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  符合下列情形之一，业主委员会或者业主请求确认合同或者合同相关条款无效的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）物业服务企业将物业服务区域内的全部物业服务业务一并委托他人而签订的委托合同；  
（二）物业服务合同中免除物业服务企业责任、加重业主委员会或者业主责任、排除业主委员会或者业主主要权利的条款。  
前款所称物业服务合同包括前期物业服务合同。

**Article 2** In any of the following circumstances, where the owners' committee or the owner requests confirmation that a contract or relevant contract clauses are void, the People's Court shall support such a request:  
(1) an entrustment contract concluded by a property service enterprise to entrust all property service business within the property service area to another person;  
(2) clauses in a property service contract that exempt the property service enterprise from liability, increase the liability of the owners' committee or the owner, or exclude the main rights of the owners' committee or the owner.  
The property service contract mentioned in the preceding paragraph includes a preliminary property service contract.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  物业服务企业不履行或者不完全履行物业服务合同约定的或者法律、法规规定以及相关行业规范确定的维修、养护、管理和维护义务，业主请求物业服务企业承担继续履行、采取补救措施或者赔偿损失等违约责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
物业服务企业公开作出的服务承诺及制定的服务细则，应当认定为物业服务合同的组成部分。

**Article 3** Where a property service enterprise fails to perform or completely perform the obligations of repair, maintenance, management, and upkeep as stipulated in the property service contract or as provided by laws, regulations, and relevant industry norms, and the owner requests the property service enterprise to bear liability for breach of contract such as continued performance, taking remedial measures, or compensating losses, the People's Court shall support such a request.  
The service commitments publicly made by the property service enterprise and the service details formulated by it shall be deemed as an integral part of the property service contract.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  业主违反物业服务合同或者法律、法规、管理规约，实施妨害物业服务与管理的行为，物业服务企业请求业主承担恢复原状、停止侵害、排除妨害等相应民事责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 4** Where an owner violates the property service contract, laws, regulations, or management rules, and commits acts that obstruct property services and management, and the property service enterprise requests the owner to bear corresponding civil liability such as restoring the original condition, ceasing the infringement, or removing the obstruction, the People's Court shall support such a request.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  物业服务企业违反物业服务合同约定或者法律、法规、部门规章规定，擅自扩大收费范围、提高收费标准或者重复收费，业主以违规收费为由提出抗辩的，人民法院应予支持。  
业主请求物业服务企业退还其已收取的违规费用的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 5** Where a property service enterprise violates the provisions of the property service contract, laws, regulations, or departmental rules, and arbitrarily expands the scope of charges, increases the charging standards, or duplicates charges, and the owner raises a defense on the ground of illegal charges, the People's Court shall support such a defense.  
Where the owner requests the property service enterprise to refund the illegal charges it has collected, the People's Court shall support such a request.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  经书面催交，业主无正当理由拒绝交纳或者在催告的合理期限内仍未交纳物业费，物业服务企业请求业主支付物业费的，人民法院应予支持。物业服务企业已经按照合同约定以及相关规定提供服务，业主仅以未享受或者无需接受相关物业服务为抗辩理由的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 6** Where, after written demand for payment, the owner refuses to pay without justifiable reasons or fails to pay within a reasonable period after the demand, and the property service enterprise requests the owner to pay the property fee, the People's Court shall support such a request. Where the property service enterprise has provided services in accordance with the contract and relevant provisions, and the owner merely uses as a defense that he has not enjoyed or does not need to receive the relevant property services, the People's Court shall not support such a defense.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  业主与物业的承租人、借用人或者其他物业使用人约定由物业使用人交纳物业费，物业服务企业请求业主承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 7** Where the owner and the lessee, borrower, or other user of the property agree that the property user shall pay the property fee, and the property service enterprise requests the owner to bear joint and several liability, the People's Court shall support such a request.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  业主大会按照物权法第七十六条规定的程序作出解聘物业服务企业的决定后，业主委员会请求解除物业服务合同的，人民法院应予支持。  
物业服务企业向业主委员会提出物业费主张的，人民法院应当告知其向拖欠物业费的业主另行主张权利。

**Article 8** After the owners' assembly makes a decision to dismiss the property service enterprise in accordance with the procedures stipulated in Article 76 of the Property Law, and the owners' committee requests the rescission of the property service contract, the People's Court shall support such a request.  
Where the property service enterprise lodges a claim for property fees against the owners' committee, the People's Court shall inform it to assert its rights separately against the owners who have defaulted on the property fees.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  物业服务合同的权利义务终止后，业主请求物业服务企业退还已经预收，但尚未提供物业服务期间的物业费的，人民法院应予支持。  
物业服务企业请求业主支付拖欠的物业费的，按照[本解释第六条](#art-6)规定处理。

**Article 9** After the termination of the rights and obligations under the property service contract, where the owner requests the property service enterprise to refund the property fees that have been collected in advance but relate to a period during which property services have not yet been provided, the People's Court shall support such a request.  
Where the property service enterprise requests the owner to pay the overdue property fees, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Article 6 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  物业服务合同的权利义务终止后，业主委员会请求物业服务企业退出物业服务区域、移交物业服务用房和相关设施，以及物业服务所必需的相关资料和由其代管的专项维修资金的，人民法院应予支持。  
物业服务企业拒绝退出、移交，并以存在事实上的物业服务关系为由，请求业主支付物业服务合同权利义务终止后的物业费的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 10** After the termination of the rights and obligations under the property service contract, where the owners' committee requests the property service enterprise to withdraw from the property service area, hand over the premises and relevant facilities for property services, and the relevant materials necessary for property services and the special maintenance funds managed by it on behalf of others, the People's Court shall support such a request.  
Where the property service enterprise refuses to withdraw and hand over, and on the ground that a de facto property service relationship exists, requests the owner to pay the property fees after the termination of the rights and obligations under the property service contract, the People's Court shall not support such a request.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  本解释涉及物业服务企业的规定，适用于物权法第七十六条、第八十一条、第八十二条所称其他管理人。

**Article 11** The provisions of this Interpretation concerning property service enterprises shall apply to other managers as mentioned in Articles 76, 81, and 82 of the Property Law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  因物业的承租人、借用人或者其他物业使用人实施违反物业服务合同，以及法律、法规或者管理规约的行为引起的物业服务纠纷，人民法院应当参照本解释关于业主的规定处理。

**Article 12** For a property service dispute arising from acts of the lessee, borrower, or other user of the property that violate the property service contract, laws, regulations, or management rules, the People's Court shall handle the matter by reference to the provisions of this Interpretation concerning owners.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  本解释自2009年10月1日起施行。  
本解释施行前已经终审，本解释施行后当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本解释。

**Article 13** This Interpretation shall come into force on October 1, 2009.  
For cases that have been finally adjudicated before the coming into force of this Interpretation, and for which a party applies for a retrial after the coming into force of this Interpretation, or for which a retrial is decided in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, this Interpretation shall not apply.
