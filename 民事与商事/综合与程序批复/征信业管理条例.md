# 征信业管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2013-01-21  
**生效日期**：2013-03-15

> (2012年12月26日国务院第228次常务会议通过　2013年1月21日中华人民共和国国务院令第631号公布　自2013年3月15日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范征信活动，保护当事人合法权益，引导、促进征信业健康发展，推进社会信用体系建设，制定本条例。 第二条　在中国境内从事征信业务及相关活动，适用本条例。 本条例所称征信业务，是指对企业、事业单位等组织(以下统称企业)的信用信息和个人的信用信息进行采集、整理、保存、加工，并向信息使用者提供的活动。 国家设立的金融信用信息基础数据库进行信息的采集、整理、保存、加工和提供，适用本条例第五章规定。 国家机关以及法律、法规授权的具有管理公共事务职能的组织依照法律、行政法规和国务院的规定，为履行职责进行的企业和个人信息的采集、整理、保存、加工和公布，不适用本条例。 第三条　从事征信业务及相关活动，应当遵守法律法规，诚实守信，不得危害国家秘密，不得侵犯商业秘密和个人隐私。 第四条　中国人民银行(以下称国务院征信业监督管理部门)及其派出机构依法对征信业进行监督管理。 县级以上地方人民政府和国务院有关部门依法推进本地区、本行业的社会信用体系建设，培育征信市场，推动征信业发展。 第二章　征信机构 第五条　本条例所称征信机构，是指依法设立，主要经营征信业务的机构。 第六条　设立经营个人征信业务的征信机构，应当符合《中华人民共和国公司法》规定的公司设立条件和下列条件，并经国务院征信业监督管理部门批准： (一)主要股东信誉良好，最近3年无重大违法违规记录； (二)注册资本不少于人民币5000万元；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范征信活动，保护当事人合法权益，引导、促进征信业健康发展，推进社会信用体系建设，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to regulate credit reporting activities, protect the legitimate rights and interests of the parties, guide and promote the healthy development of the credit reporting industry, and advance the construction of a social credit system.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中国境内从事征信业务及相关活动，适用本条例。  
本条例所称征信业务，是指对企业、事业单位等组织(以下统称企业)的信用信息和个人的信用信息进行采集、整理、保存、加工，并向信息使用者提供的活动。  
国家设立的金融信用信息基础数据库进行信息的采集、整理、保存、加工和提供，适用本条例第五章规定。  
国家机关以及法律、法规授权的具有管理公共事务职能的组织依照法律、行政法规和国务院的规定，为履行职责进行的企业和个人信息的采集、整理、保存、加工和公布，不适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the conducting of credit reporting businesses and related activities within the territory of China.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "credit reporting business" means the activities of collecting, sorting, preserving, processing the credit information of enterprises, public institutions and other organizations (hereinafter collectively referred to as "enterprises") and the personal information of individuals, and providing such information to information users.  
The provisions of Chapter V of these Regulations shall apply to the collection, sorting, preservation, processing and provision of information by the national financial credit information basic database established by the State.  
These Regulations shall not apply to the collection, sorting, preservation, processing and publication of enterprise and individual information by State organs and organizations authorized by laws and regulations to exercise the functions of public administration in accordance with laws, administrative regulations and the provisions of the State Council for the purpose of performing their duties.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　从事征信业务及相关活动，应当遵守法律法规，诚实守信，不得危害国家秘密，不得侵犯商业秘密和个人隐私。

**Article 3** In conducting credit reporting businesses and related activities, parties shall comply with laws and regulations, be honest and trustworthy, and shall not endanger State secrets, nor infringe upon trade secrets and personal privacy.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　中国人民银行(以下称国务院征信业监督管理部门)及其派出机构依法对征信业进行监督管理。  
县级以上地方人民政府和国务院有关部门依法推进本地区、本行业的社会信用体系建设，培育征信市场，推动征信业发展。

**Article 4** The People's Bank of China (hereinafter referred to as the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council) and its dispatched offices shall, according to law, supervise and administer the credit reporting industry.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and relevant departments of the State Council shall, according to law, advance the construction of a social credit system in their respective regions and industries, cultivate the credit reporting market, and promote the development of the credit reporting industry.

### 第二章　征信机构

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　本条例所称征信机构，是指依法设立，主要经营征信业务的机构。

**Article 5** For the purposes of these Regulations, "credit reporting agency" means an institution established according to law and mainly engaged in credit reporting businesses.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　设立经营个人征信业务的征信机构，应当符合《中华人民共和国公司法》规定的公司设立条件和下列条件，并经国务院征信业监督管理部门批准：  
(一)主要股东信誉良好，最近3年无重大违法违规记录；  
(二)注册资本不少于人民币5000万元；  
(三)有符合国务院征信业监督管理部门规定的保障信息安全的设施、设备和制度、措施；  
(四)拟任董事、监事和高级管理人员符合[本条例第八条](#art-8)规定的任职条件；  
(五)国务院征信业监督管理部门规定的其他审慎性条件。

**Article 6** To establish a credit reporting agency that engages in personal credit reporting business, it shall satisfy the conditions for the establishment of a company as prescribed in the Company Law of the People's Republic of China and the following conditions, and shall obtain approval from the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council:  
(1) Its major shareholders have a good reputation and have no record of major illegal or irregular acts within the most recent three years;  
(2) Its registered capital is not less than RMB 50 million yuan;  
(3) It has facilities, equipment, systems and measures to ensure information security as prescribed by the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council;  
(4) Its proposed directors, supervisors and senior officers meet the qualification requirements as prescribed in Article 8 of these Regulations;  
(5) Other prudential conditions as prescribed by the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请设立经营个人征信业务的征信机构，应当向国务院征信业监督管理部门提交申请书和证明其符合[本条例第六条](#art-6)规定条件的材料。  
国务院征信业监督管理部门应当依法进行审查，自受理申请之日起60日内作出批准或者不予批准的决定。决定批准的，颁发个人征信业务经营许可证；不予批准的，应当书面说明理由。  
经批准设立的经营个人征信业务的征信机构，凭个人征信业务经营许可证向公司登记机关办理登记。  
未经国务院征信业监督管理部门批准，任何单位和个人不得经营个人征信业务。

**Article 7** To apply for the establishment of a credit reporting agency that engages in personal credit reporting business, the applicant shall submit to the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council a written application and materials proving that it meets the conditions prescribed in Article 6 of these Regulations.  
The credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council shall, according to law, conduct examination and make a decision on approval or disapproval within 60 days from the date of accepting the application. If it decides to approve, it shall issue a personal credit reporting business license; if it decides to disapprove, it shall state the reasons in writing.  
A credit reporting agency that engages in personal credit reporting business upon approval shall, on the strength of the personal credit reporting business license, register with the company registration authority.  
No unit or individual may engage in personal credit reporting business without the approval of the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　经营个人征信业务的征信机构的董事、监事和高级管理人员，应当熟悉与征信业务相关的法律法规，具有履行职责所需的征信业从业经验和管理能力，最近3年无重大违法违规记录，并取得国务院征信业监督管理部门核准的任职资格。

**Article 8** The directors, supervisors and senior officers of a credit reporting agency that engages in personal credit reporting business shall be familiar with the laws and regulations related to credit reporting business, possess the professional experience and management capabilities in the credit reporting industry required for performing their duties, have no record of major illegal or irregular acts within the most recent three years, and obtain the qualification for the position approved by the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　经营个人征信业务的征信机构设立分支机构、合并或者分立、变更注册资本、变更出资额占公司资本总额5%以上或者持股占公司股份5%以上的股东的，应当经国务院征信业监督管理部门批准。  
经营个人征信业务的征信机构变更名称的，应当向国务院征信业监督管理部门办理备案。

**Article 9** Where a credit reporting agency that engages in personal credit reporting business establishes a branch, merges or splits, changes its registered capital, or changes shareholders whose capital contributions account for 5% or more of the total company capital or whose shareholdings account for 5% or more of the company's shares, it shall obtain approval from the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council.  
Where a credit reporting agency that engages in personal credit reporting business changes its name, it shall file a record with the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　设立经营企业征信业务的征信机构，应当符合《中华人民共和国公司法》规定的设立条件，并自公司登记机关准予登记之日起30日内向所在地的国务院征信业监督管理部门派出机构办理备案，并提供下列材料：  
(一)营业执照；  
(二)股权结构、组织机构说明；  
(三)业务范围、业务规则、业务系统的基本情况；  
(四)信息安全和风险防范措施。  
备案事项发生变更的，应当自变更之日起30日内向原备案机构办理变更备案。

**Article 10** To establish a credit reporting agency that engages in enterprise credit reporting business, it shall satisfy the conditions for establishment prescribed in the Company Law of the People's Republic of China, and shall, within 30 days from the date of approval of registration by the company registration authority, file a record with the dispatched office of the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council at the place where it is located, and provide the following materials:  
(1) The business license;  
(2) Description of the equity structure and organizational structure;  
(3) Basic information on the business scope, business rules, and business systems;  
(4) Information security and risk prevention measures.  
If any item recorded changes, it shall, within 30 days from the date of change, file a change of record with the original record-filing institution.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　征信机构应当按照国务院征信业监督管理部门的规定，报告上一年度开展征信业务的情况。  
国务院征信业监督管理部门应当向社会公告经营个人征信业务和企业征信业务的征信机构名单，并及时更新。

**Article 11** A credit reporting agency shall, in accordance with the provisions of the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council, report the circumstances of its credit reporting business conducted in the previous year.  
The credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council shall announce to the public the list of credit reporting agencies that engage in personal credit reporting business and enterprise credit reporting business, and shall update it in a timely manner.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　征信机构解散或者被依法宣告破产的，应当向国务院征信业监督管理部门报告，并按照下列方式处理信息数据库：  
(一)与其他征信机构约定并经国务院征信业监督管理部门同意，转让给其他征信机构；  
(二)不能依照前项规定转让的，移交给国务院征信业监督管理部门指定的征信机构；  
(三)不能依照前两项规定转让、移交的，在国务院征信业监督管理部门的监督下销毁。  
经营个人征信业务的征信机构解散或者被依法宣告破产的，还应当在国务院征信业监督管理部门指定的媒体上公告，并将个人征信业务经营许可证交国务院征信业监督管理部门注销。

**Article 12** Where a credit reporting agency is dissolved or is declared bankrupt according to law, it shall report to the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council, and shall handle the information database in the following ways:  
(1) Transfer it to another credit reporting agency upon agreement with such other agency and with the consent of the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council;  
(2) If it cannot be transferred in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, hand it over to a credit reporting agency designated by the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council;  
(3) If it cannot be transferred or handed over in accordance with the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs, destroy it under the supervision of the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council.  
Where a credit reporting agency that engages in personal credit reporting business is dissolved or is declared bankrupt according to law, it shall also make an announcement in the media designated by the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council, and shall hand over the personal credit reporting business license to the credit reporting industry regulatory department of the State Council for cancellation.

### 第三章　征信业务规则

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　采集个人信息应当经信息主体本人同意，未经本人同意不得采集。但是，依照法律、行政法规规定公开的信息除外。  
企业的董事、监事、高级管理人员与其履行职务相关的信息，不作为个人信息。

**Article 13** The collection of personal information shall be subject to the consent of the information subject; it shall not be collected without the consent of the information subject, except for information made public in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.  
Information relating to the performance of duties by directors, supervisors and senior officers of an enterprise shall not be regarded as personal information.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　禁止征信机构采集个人的宗教信仰、基因、指纹、血型、疾病和病史信息以及法律、行政法规规定禁止采集的其他个人信息。  
征信机构不得采集个人的收入、存款、有价证券、商业保险、不动产的信息和纳税数额信息。但是，征信机构明确告知信息主体提供该信息可能产生的不利后果，并取得其书面同意的除外。

**Article 14** A credit reporting agency is prohibited from collecting an individual's information on religious belief, genes, fingerprints, blood type, diseases and medical history, as well as other personal information the collection of which is prohibited by laws and administrative regulations.  
A credit reporting agency shall not collect an individual's information on income, deposits, securities, commercial insurance, real estate, and the amount of tax paid, unless the credit reporting agency clearly informs the information subject of the adverse consequences that may arise from providing such information and obtains the written consent of the information subject.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　信息提供者向征信机构提供个人不良信息，应当事先告知信息主体本人。但是，依照法律、行政法规规定公开的不良信息除外。

**Article 15** An information provider that provides adverse personal information to a credit reporting agency shall inform the information subject in advance, except for adverse information made public in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　征信机构对个人不良信息的保存期限，自不良行为或者事件终止之日起为5年；超过5年的，应当予以删除。  
在不良信息保存期限内，信息主体可以对不良信息作出说明，征信机构应当予以记载。

**Article 16** The preservation period of adverse personal information by a credit reporting agency shall be five years from the date of termination of the adverse act or event; if more than five years have elapsed, it shall be deleted.  
During the preservation period of adverse information, the information subject may provide an explanation regarding the adverse information, and the credit reporting agency shall record it.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　信息主体可以向征信机构查询自身信息。个人信息主体有权每年两次免费获取本人的信用报告。

**Article 17** An information subject may inquire about its own information from a credit reporting agency. An individual information subject has the right to obtain his or her own credit report free of charge twice per year.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　向征信机构查询个人信息的，应当取得信息主体本人的书面同意并约定用途。但是，法律规定可以不经同意查询的除外。  
征信机构不得违反前款规定提供个人信息。

**Article 18** Any party that inquires about personal information from a credit reporting agency shall obtain the written consent of the information subject and agree on the purpose of use, except as otherwise provided by law that inquiry may be made without consent.  
A credit reporting agency shall not provide personal information in violation of the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　征信机构或者信息提供者、信息使用者采用格式合同条款取得个人信息主体同意的，应当在合同中作出足以引起信息主体注意的提示，并按照信息主体的要求作出明确说明。

**Article 19** Where a credit reporting agency or an information provider or information user obtains the consent of an individual information subject by using standard contract terms, it shall make a prompt in the contract sufficient to draw the attention of the information subject, and shall make a clear explanation at the request of the information subject.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　信息使用者应当按照与个人信息主体约定的用途使用个人信息，不得用作约定以外的用途，不得未经个人信息主体同意向第三方提供。

**Article 20** An information user shall use personal information for the purposes agreed upon with the individual information subject, shall not use it for purposes other than those agreed upon, and shall not provide it to a third party without the consent of the individual information subject.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　征信机构可以通过信息主体、企业交易对方、行业协会提供信息，政府有关部门依法已公开的信息，人民法院依法公布的判决、裁定等渠道，采集企业信息。  
征信机构不得采集法律、行政法规禁止采集的企业信息。

**Article 21** A credit reference institution may collect enterprise information through channels such as information provided by data subjects, enterprise transaction counterparts, and industry associations, information already publicly disclosed by relevant government departments according to law, and judgments and rulings published by the People's Courts according to law.  
A credit reference institution shall not collect enterprise information the collection of which is prohibited by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　征信机构应当按照国务院征信业监督管理部门的规定，建立健全和严格执行保障信息安全的规章制度，并采取有效技术措施保障信息安全。  
经营个人征信业务的征信机构应当对其工作人员查询个人信息的权限和程序作出明确规定，对工作人员查询个人信息的情况进行登记，如实记载查询工作人员的姓名，查询的时间、内容及用途。工作人员不得违反规定的权限和程序查询信息，不得泄露工作中获取的信息。

**Article 22** A credit reference institution shall, in accordance with the provisions of the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council, establish, improve and strictly implement rules and regulations for ensuring information security, and adopt effective technical measures to ensure information security.  
A credit reference institution operating personal credit reference business shall clearly specify the authority and procedures for its staff to inquire personal information, keep records of inquiries of personal information by its staff, and truthfully record the name of the inquiring staff member, the time, content and purpose of the inquiry. The staff shall not inquire information in violation of the prescribed authority and procedures, and shall not disclose information obtained during their work.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　征信机构应当采取合理措施，保障其提供信息的准确性。  
征信机构提供的信息供信息使用者参考。

**Article 23** A credit reference institution shall take reasonable measures to ensure the accuracy of the information it provides.  
The information provided by a credit reference institution is for the reference of information users.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　征信机构在中国境内采集的信息的整理、保存和加工，应当在中国境内进行。  
征信机构向境外组织或者个人提供信息，应当遵守法律、行政法规和国务院征信业监督管理部门的有关规定。

**Article 24** The organization, storage and processing of information collected by a credit reference institution within the territory of China shall be carried out within the territory of China.  
Where a credit reference institution provides information to an organization or individual outside the territory of China, it shall comply with laws, administrative regulations and the relevant provisions of the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council.

### 第四章　异议和投诉

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　信息主体认为征信机构采集、保存、提供的信息存在错误、遗漏的，有权向征信机构或者信息提供者提出异议，要求更正。  
征信机构或者信息提供者收到异议，应当按照国务院征信业监督管理部门的规定对相关信息作出存在异议的标注，自收到异议之日起20日内进行核查和处理，并将结果书面答复异议人。  
经核查，确认相关信息确有错误、遗漏的，信息提供者、征信机构应当予以更正；确认不存在错误、遗漏的，应当取消异议标注；经核查仍不能确认的，对核查情况和异议内容应当予以记载。

**Article 25** Where a data subject believes that the information collected, stored or provided by a credit reference institution contains errors or omissions, it has the right to raise an objection to the credit reference institution or the information provider and request a correction.  
Upon receiving an objection, the credit reference institution or the information provider shall, in accordance with the provisions of the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council, mark the relevant information as being subject to objection, conduct verification and handle the matter within 20 days from the date of receiving the objection, and reply to the objector in writing with the result.  
Upon verification, if it is confirmed that the relevant information indeed contains errors or omissions, the information provider or the credit reference institution shall correct it; if it is confirmed that no error or omission exists, the objection mark shall be removed; if verification still fails to confirm, the circumstances of the verification and the content of the objection shall be recorded.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　信息主体认为征信机构或者信息提供者、信息使用者侵害其合法权益的，可以向所在地的国务院征信业监督管理部门派出机构投诉。  
受理投诉的机构应当及时进行核查和处理，自受理之日起30日内书面答复投诉人。  
信息主体认为征信机构或者信息提供者、信息使用者侵害其合法权益的，可以直接向人民法院起诉。

**Article 26** Where a data subject believes that a credit reference institution, an information provider or an information user infringes upon its lawful rights and interests, it may lodge a complaint with the local office of the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council.  
The institution that accepts the complaint shall conduct verification and handle the matter in a timely manner, and reply to the complainant in writing within 30 days from the date of acceptance.  
Where a data subject believes that a credit reference institution, an information provider or an information user infringes upon its lawful rights and interests, it may directly initiate a lawsuit in the People's Court.

### 第五章　金融信用信息基础数据库

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家设立金融信用信息基础数据库，为防范金融风险、促进金融业发展提供相关信息服务。  
金融信用信息基础数据库由专业运行机构建设、运行和维护。该运行机构不以营利为目的，由国务院征信业监督管理部门监督管理。

**Article 27** The State establishes a basic database of financial credit information to provide relevant information services for preventing financial risks and promoting the development of the financial industry.  
The basic database of financial credit information shall be built, operated and maintained by a specialized operating institution. Such operating institution shall be non-profit and shall be supervised and managed by the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　金融信用信息基础数据库接收从事信贷业务的机构按照规定提供的信贷信息。  
金融信用信息基础数据库为信息主体和取得信息主体本人书面同意的信息使用者提供查询服务。国家机关可以依法查询金融信用信息基础数据库的信息。

**Article 28** The basic database of financial credit information receives credit information provided by institutions engaged in credit business in accordance with the provisions.  
The basic database of financial credit information provides inquiry services for data subjects and information users who have obtained the written consent of the data subject himself. State organs may inquire information from the basic database of financial credit information according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　从事信贷业务的机构应当按照规定向金融信用信息基础数据库提供信贷信息。  
从事信贷业务的机构向金融信用信息基础数据库或者其他主体提供信贷信息，应当事先取得信息主体的书面同意，并适用本条例关于信息提供者的规定。

**Article 29** An institution engaged in credit business shall, in accordance with the provisions, provide credit information to the basic database of financial credit information.  
Where an institution engaged in credit business provides credit information to the basic database of financial credit information or other entities, it shall obtain the written consent of the data subject in advance, and the provisions of these Regulations concerning information providers shall apply.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　不从事信贷业务的金融机构向金融信用信息基础数据库提供、查询信用信息以及金融信用信息基础数据库接收其提供的信用信息的具体办法，由国务院征信业监督管理部门会同国务院有关金融监督管理机构依法制定。

**Article 30** The specific measures for financial institutions not engaged in credit business to provide or inquire credit information to the basic database of financial credit information, and for the basic database of financial credit information to receive the credit information provided by such institutions, shall be formulated by the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council jointly with the relevant financial regulatory authorities under the State Council according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　金融信用信息基础数据库运行机构可以按照补偿成本原则收取查询服务费用，收费标准由国务院价格主管部门规定。

**Article 31** The operating institution of the basic database of financial credit information may charge fees for inquiry services in accordance with the principle of cost recovery, and the fee standards shall be prescribed by the competent pricing department under the State Council.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　[本条例第十四条](#art-14)、第十六条、第十七条、第十八条、第二十二条、第二十三条、第二十四条、第二十五条、第二十六条适用于金融信用信息基础数据库运行机构。

**Article 32** Articles 14, 16, 17, 18, 22, 23, 24, 25 and 26 of these Regulations shall apply to the operating institution of the basic database of financial credit information.

### 第六章　监督管理

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院征信业监督管理部门及其派出机构依照法律、行政法规和国务院的规定，履行对征信业和金融信用信息基础数据库运行机构的监督管理职责，可以采取下列监督检查措施：  
(一)进入征信机构、金融信用信息基础数据库运行机构进行现场检查，对向金融信用信息基础数据库提供或者查询信息的机构遵守本条例有关规定的情况进行检查；  
(二)询问当事人和与被调查事件有关的单位和个人，要求其对与被调查事件有关的事项作出说明；  
(三)查阅、复制与被调查事件有关的文件、资料，对可能被转移、销毁、隐匿或者篡改的文件、资料予以封存；  
(四)检查相关信息系统。  
进行现场检查或者调查的人员不得少于2人，并应当出示合法证件和检查、调查通知书。  
被检查、调查的单位和个人应当配合，如实提供有关文件、资料，不得隐瞒、拒绝和阻碍。

**Article 33** The regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council and its local offices shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations and the provisions of the State Council, perform the supervision and management duties over the credit reference industry and the operating institution of the basic database of financial credit information, and may take the following inspection and supervision measures:  
(1) Entering a credit reference institution or the operating institution of the basic database of financial credit information to conduct on-site inspection, and inspecting the compliance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations by institutions that provide or inquire information to the basic database of financial credit information;  
(2) Inquiring the parties, and the entities and individuals concerned with the incident under investigation, and requesting them to make explanations on matters related to the incident under investigation;  
(3) Inspecting and copying documents and materials related to the incident under investigation, and sealing up documents and materials that may be transferred, destroyed, concealed or tampered with;  
(4) Inspecting the relevant information systems.  
The number of personnel conducting on-site inspection or investigation shall not be less than two, and they shall present their lawful credentials and the notice of inspection or investigation.  
The entities and individuals subject to inspection or investigation shall cooperate, truthfully provide relevant documents and materials, and shall not conceal, refuse or obstruct.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　经营个人征信业务的征信机构、金融信用信息基础数据库、向金融信用信息基础数据库提供或者查询信息的机构发生重大信息泄露等事件的，国务院征信业监督管理部门可以采取临时接管相关信息系统等必要措施，避免损害扩大。

**Article 34** Where a credit reference institution operating personal credit reference business, the basic database of financial credit information, or an institution that provides or inquires information to the basic database of financial credit information suffers a major information leakage incident, the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council may take necessary measures such as temporarily taking over the relevant information systems to avoid the escalation of damage.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国务院征信业监督管理部门及其派出机构的工作人员对在工作中知悉的国家秘密和信息主体的信息，应当依法保密。

**Article 35** The staff members of the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council and its local offices shall, according to law, keep confidential the State secrets and the information of data subjects that they become aware of during their work.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　未经国务院征信业监督管理部门批准，擅自设立经营个人征信业务的征信机构或者从事个人征信业务活动的，由国务院征信业监督管理部门予以取缔，没收违法所得，并处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 36** Where a credit reference institution operating personal credit reference business is established or personal credit reference business activities are engaged without the approval of the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council, the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council shall ban such institution or activities, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　经营个人征信业务的征信机构违反[本条例第九条](#art-9)规定的，由国务院征信业监督管理部门责令限期改正，对单位处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，处1万元以下的罚款。  
经营企业征信业务的征信机构未按照[本条例第十条](#art-10)规定办理备案的，由其所在地的国务院征信业监督管理部门派出机构责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 37** Where a credit reference institution operating personal credit reference business violates Article 9 of these Regulations, the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan on the entity; give a warning to the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons, and impose a fine of not more than RMB 10,000 yuan.  
Where a credit reference institution operating enterprise credit reference business fails to go through the recordation formalities in accordance with Article 10 of these Regulations, the local office of the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　征信机构、金融信用信息基础数据库运行机构违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由国务院征信业监督管理部门或者其派出机构责令限期改正，对单位处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。给信息主体造成损失的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)窃取或者以其他方式非法获取信息；  
(二)采集禁止采集的个人信息或者未经同意采集个人信息；  
(三)违法提供或者出售信息；  
(四)因过失泄露信息；  
(五)逾期不删除个人不良信息；  
(六)未按照规定对异议信息进行核查和处理；  
(七)拒绝、阻碍国务院征信业监督管理部门或者其派出机构检查、调查或者不如实提供有关文件、资料；  
(八)违反征信业务规则，侵害信息主体合法权益的其他行为。  
经营个人征信业务的征信机构有前款所列行为之一，情节严重或者造成严重后果的，由国务院征信业监督管理部门吊销其个人征信业务经营许可证。

**Article 38** Where a credit reference institution or the operating institution of the basic database of financial credit information violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council or its local office shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan on the entity; impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated. If losses are caused to the data subject, civil liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Stealing or otherwise illegally obtaining information;  
(2) Collecting personal information that is prohibited from collection or collecting personal information without consent;  
(3) Illegally providing or selling information;  
(4) Leaking information through negligence;  
(5) Failing to delete adverse personal information within the prescribed time limit;  
(6) Failing to verify and handle objection information in accordance with the provisions;  
(7) Refusing or obstructing inspection or investigation by the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council or its local office, or failing to truthfully provide relevant documents and materials;  
(8) Other acts that violate the rules of credit reference business and infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of data subjects.  
Where a credit reference institution operating personal credit reference business commits any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, and the circumstances are serious or cause serious consequences, the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council shall revoke its personal credit reference business operating permit.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　征信机构违反本条例规定，未按照规定报告其上一年度开展征信业务情况的，由国务院征信业监督管理部门或者其派出机构责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，对单位处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，处1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 39** Where a credit reference institution violates the provisions of these Regulations by failing to report its credit reference business activities of the previous year in accordance with the provisions, the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council or its local office shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed on the entity; a warning shall be given to the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons, and a fine of not more than RMB 10,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　向金融信用信息基础数据库提供或者查询信息的机构违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由国务院征信业监督管理部门或者其派出机构责令限期改正，对单位处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。给信息主体造成损失的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)违法提供或者出售信息；  
(二)因过失泄露信息；  
(三)未经同意查询个人信息或者企业的信贷信息；  
(四)未按照规定处理异议或者对确有错误、遗漏的信息不予更正；  
(五)拒绝、阻碍国务院征信业监督管理部门或者其派出机构检查、调查或者不如实提供有关文件、资料。

**Article 40** Where an institution that provides or inquires information to the basic database of financial credit information violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council or its local office shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan on the entity; impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated. If losses are caused to the data subject, civil liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Illegally providing or selling information;  
(2) Leaking information through negligence;  
(3) Inquiring personal information or the credit information of an enterprise without consent;  
(4) Failing to handle objections in accordance with the provisions or failing to correct information that is indeed erroneous or omitted;  
(5) Refusing or obstructing inspection or investigation by the regulatory department of the credit reference industry under the State Council or its local office, or failing to truthfully provide relevant documents and materials.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　信息提供者违反本条例规定，向征信机构、金融信用信息基础数据库提供非依法公开的个人不良信息，未事先告知信息主体本人，情节严重或者造成严重后果的，由国务院征信业监督管理部门或者其派出机构对单位处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；对个人处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 41** Any information provider that, in violation of these Regulations, provides non-public personal adverse information to a credit reference agency or the basic database of financial credit information without notifying the subject of the information in advance, if the circumstances are serious or the consequences are severe, shall be fined by the regulatory department of the credit reference industry of the State Council or its dispatched agencies not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan if such provider is an entity, or not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan if such provider is an individual.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　信息使用者违反本条例规定，未按照与个人信息主体约定的用途使用个人信息或者未经个人信息主体同意向第三方提供个人信息，情节严重或者造成严重后果的，由国务院征信业监督管理部门或者其派出机构对单位处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；对个人处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。给信息主体造成损失的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 42** Any information user that, in violation of these Regulations, fails to use personal information in accordance with the purpose agreed with the subject of the personal information, or provides personal information to a third party without the consent of the subject of the personal information, if the circumstances are serious or the consequences are severe, shall be fined by the regulatory department of the credit reference industry of the State Council or its dispatched agencies not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan if such user is an entity, or not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan if such user is an individual; if there are illegal gains, such gains shall be confiscated. If losses are caused to the subject of the information, civil liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国务院征信业监督管理部门及其派出机构的工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，不依法履行监督管理职责，或者泄露国家秘密、信息主体信息的，依法给予处分。给信息主体造成损失的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** Any staff member of the regulatory department of the credit reference industry of the State Council or its dispatched agencies who abuses power, neglects duty, engages in malpractice for personal gain, fails to perform supervision and administration duties according to law, or divulges state secrets or information of information subjects, shall be given sanctions according to law. If losses are caused to the subject of the information, civil liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
(一)信息提供者，是指向征信机构提供信息的单位和个人，以及向金融信用信息基础数据库提供信息的单位。  
(二)信息使用者，是指从征信机构和金融信用信息基础数据库获取信息的单位和个人。  
(三)不良信息，是指对信息主体信用状况构成负面影响的下列信息：信息主体在借贷、赊购、担保、租赁、保险、使用信用卡等活动中未按照合同履行义务的信息，对信息主体的行政处罚信息，人民法院判决或者裁定信息主体履行义务以及强制执行的信息，以及国务院征信业监督管理部门规定的其他不良信息。

**Article 44** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:  
(1) 'Information provider' refers to the entity or individual that provides information to a credit reference agency, as well as the entity that provides information to the basic database of financial credit information.  
(2) 'Information user' refers to the entity or individual that obtains information from a credit reference agency or the basic database of financial credit information.  
(3) 'Adverse information' refers to the following information that has a negative impact on the credit status of the subject of the information: information that the subject of the information fails to perform obligations under a contract in activities such as borrowing, purchasing on credit, guarantee, leasing, insurance, use of credit cards, etc.; administrative penalty information on the subject of the information; information that a People's Court judges or rules that the subject of the information shall perform obligations, and compulsory execution information; and other adverse information specified by the regulatory department of the credit reference industry of the State Council.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　外商投资征信机构的设立条件，由国务院征信业监督管理部门会同国务院有关部门制定，报国务院批准。  
境外征信机构在境内经营征信业务，应当经国务院征信业监督管理部门批准。

**Article 45** The conditions for the establishment of a credit reference agency with foreign investment shall be formulated by the regulatory department of the credit reference industry of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.  
A credit reference agency outside the territory that intends to conduct credit reference business within the territory shall be subject to the approval of the regulatory department of the credit reference industry of the State Council.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本条例施行前已经经营个人征信业务的机构，应当自本条例施行之日起6个月内，依照本条例的规定申请个人征信业务经营许可证。  
本条例施行前已经经营企业征信业务的机构，应当自本条例施行之日起3个月内，依照本条例的规定办理备案。

**Article 46** Any institution that already conducted personal credit reference business before these Regulations come into force shall, within six months from the date these Regulations come into force, apply for a personal credit reference business license in accordance with these Regulations.  
Any institution that already conducted enterprise credit reference business before these Regulations come into force shall, within three months from the date these Regulations come into force, complete the recordation formalities in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　本条例自2013年3月15日起施行。

**Article 47** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 15, 2013.
