# 最高人民法院关于审理侵犯植物新品种权纠纷案件具体应用法律问题的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2007〕1号  
**公布日期**：2006-12-25  
**生效日期**：2007-02-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理侵犯植物新品种权纠纷案件具体应用法律问题的若干规定》已于2006年12月25日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1411次会议通过，现予公布，自2007年2月1日起施行。 2007年1月12日 最高人民法院 关于审理侵犯植物新品种权纠纷案件 具体应用法律问题的若干规定 法释〔2007〕1号 为正确处理侵犯植物新品种权纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等有关规定，结合侵犯植物新品种权纠纷案件的审判经验和实际情况，就具体应用法律的若干问题规定如下： 第一条  植物新品种权所有人（以下称品种权人）或者利害关系人认为植物新品种权受到侵犯的，可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。 前款所称利害关系人，包括植物新品种实施许可合同的被许可人、品种权财产权利的合法继承人等。 独占实施许可合同的被许可人可以单独向人民法院提起诉讼；排他实施许可合同的被许可人可以和品种权人共同起诉，也可以在品种权人不起诉时，自行提起诉讼；普通实施许可合同的被许可人经品种权人明确授权，可以提起诉讼。 第二条  未经品种权人许可，为商业目的生产或销售授权品种的繁殖材料，或者为商业目的将授权品种的繁殖材料重复使用于生产另一品种的繁殖材料的，人民法院应当认定为侵犯植物新品种权。 被控侵权物的特征、特性与授权品种的特征、特性相同，或者特征、特性的不同是因非遗传变异所致的，人民法院一般应当认定被控侵权物属于商业目的生产或者销售授权品种的繁殖材料。 被控侵权人重复以授权品种的繁殖材料为亲本与其他亲本另行繁殖的，人民法院一般应当认定属于商业目的将授权品种的繁殖材料重复使用于生产另一品种的繁殖材料。 第三条  侵犯植物新品种权纠纷案件涉及的专门性问题需要鉴定的，由双方当事人协商确定的有鉴定资格的鉴定机构、鉴定人鉴定；协商不成的，由人民法院指定的有鉴定资格的鉴定机构、鉴定人鉴定。 没有前款规定的鉴定机构、鉴定人的，由具有相应品种检测技术水平的专业机构、专业人员鉴定。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  植物新品种权所有人（以下称品种权人）或者利害关系人认为植物新品种权受到侵犯的，可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。  
前款所称利害关系人，包括植物新品种实施许可合同的被许可人、品种权财产权利的合法继承人等。  
独占实施许可合同的被许可人可以单独向人民法院提起诉讼；排他实施许可合同的被许可人可以和品种权人共同起诉，也可以在品种权人不起诉时，自行提起诉讼；普通实施许可合同的被许可人经品种权人明确授权，可以提起诉讼。

**Article 1** A holder of a new plant variety right (hereinafter referred to as the variety rights holder) or an interested party who believes that the new plant variety right has been infringed may, according to law, initiate a prosecution with the People's Court.  
The interested party referred to in the preceding paragraph includes the licensee under a contract for the exploitation license of the new plant variety, the lawful successor to the property rights of the variety right, and others.  
The licensee under an exclusive exploitation license contract may separately initiate a prosecution with the People's Court; the licensee under a sole exploitation license contract may jointly initiate a prosecution with the variety rights holder, or may independently initiate a prosecution when the variety rights holder fails to do so; the licensee under a general exploitation license contract may, upon explicit authorization by the variety rights holder, initiate a prosecution.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  未经品种权人许可，为商业目的生产或销售授权品种的繁殖材料，或者为商业目的将授权品种的繁殖材料重复使用于生产另一品种的繁殖材料的，人民法院应当认定为侵犯植物新品种权。  
被控侵权物的特征、特性与授权品种的特征、特性相同，或者特征、特性的不同是因非遗传变异所致的，人民法院一般应当认定被控侵权物属于商业目的生产或者销售授权品种的繁殖材料。  
被控侵权人重复以授权品种的繁殖材料为亲本与其他亲本另行繁殖的，人民法院一般应当认定属于商业目的将授权品种的繁殖材料重复使用于生产另一品种的繁殖材料。

**Article 2** Where, without the permission of the variety rights holder, a person produces or sells the propagating material of an authorized variety for commercial purposes, or repeatedly uses the propagating material of an authorized variety for producing the propagating material of another variety for commercial purposes, the People's Court shall determine that such act infringes the new plant variety right.  
Where the characteristics and traits of the allegedly infringing product are identical to those of the authorized variety, or the differences in characteristics and traits are caused by non-genetic variation, the People's Court shall generally determine that the allegedly infringing product constitutes, for commercial purposes, the production or sale of the propagating material of the authorized variety.  
Where the alleged infringer repeatedly uses the propagating material of the authorized variety as a parent and propagates anew with other parents, the People's Court shall generally determine that such act constitutes, for commercial purposes, the repeated use of the propagating material of the authorized variety for producing the propagating material of another variety.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  侵犯植物新品种权纠纷案件涉及的专门性问题需要鉴定的，由双方当事人协商确定的有鉴定资格的鉴定机构、鉴定人鉴定；协商不成的，由人民法院指定的有鉴定资格的鉴定机构、鉴定人鉴定。  
没有前款规定的鉴定机构、鉴定人的，由具有相应品种检测技术水平的专业机构、专业人员鉴定。

**Article 3** Where the specialized issues involved in a dispute case over infringement of a new plant variety right require expert evaluation, the expert evaluation shall be conducted by an expert evaluation institution and expert evaluator with evaluation qualifications as determined by the parties through consultation; where consultation fails, the People's Court shall designate an expert evaluation institution and expert evaluator with evaluation qualifications.  
Where there is no expert evaluation institution or expert evaluator as provided in the preceding paragraph, the evaluation shall be conducted by a professional institution or professional person with a corresponding level of variety testing technology.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  对于侵犯植物新品种权纠纷案件涉及的专门性问题可以采取田间观察检测、基因指纹图谱检测等方法鉴定。  
对采取前款规定方法作出的鉴定结论，人民法院应当依法质证，认定其证明力。

**Article 4** For the specialized issues involved in a dispute case over infringement of a new plant variety right, methods such as field observation testing and genetic fingerprint testing may be adopted for expert evaluation.  
The expert evaluation conclusion made by adopting the methods provided in the preceding paragraph shall be subject to cross-examination according to law by the People's Court, which shall determine its probative force.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  品种权人或者利害关系人向人民法院提起侵犯植物新品种权诉讼时，同时提出先行停止侵犯植物新品种权行为或者保全证据请求的，人民法院经审查可以先行作出裁定。  
人民法院采取证据保全措施时，可以根据案件具体情况，邀请有关专业技术人员按照相应的技术规程协助取证。

**Article 5** Where, when initiating a lawsuit for infringement of a new plant variety right with the People's Court, the variety rights holder or interested party simultaneously files a request for preliminary cessation of the infringement of the new plant variety right or for preservation of evidence, the People's Court, after examination, may make a preliminary ruling.  
When the People's Court adopts evidence preservation measures, it may, according to the specific circumstances of the case, invite relevant professional and technical personnel to assist in obtaining evidence in accordance with the corresponding technical procedures.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  人民法院审理侵犯植物新品种权纠纷案件，应当依照民法通则第一百三十四条的规定，结合案件具体情况，判决侵权人承担停止侵害、赔偿损失等民事责任。  
人民法院可以根据被侵权人的请求，按照被侵权人因侵权所受损失或者侵权人因侵权所得利益确定赔偿数额。被侵权人请求按照植物新品种实施许可费确定赔偿数额的，人民法院可以根据植物新品种实施许可的种类、时间、范围等因素，参照该植物新品种实施许可费合理确定赔偿数额。  
依照前款规定难以确定赔偿数额的，人民法院可以综合考虑侵权的性质、期间、后果，植物新品种实施许可费的数额，植物新品种实施许可的种类、时间、范围及被侵权人调查、制止侵权所支付的合理费用等因素，在50万元以下确定赔偿数额。

**Article 6** When hearing a dispute case over infringement of a new plant variety right, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 134 of the General Principles of the Civil Law and in light of the specific circumstances of the case, adjudicate that the infringer shall bear civil liabilities such as cessation of infringement and compensation for losses.  
The People's Court may, at the request of the infringed party, determine the amount of compensation in accordance with the losses suffered by the infringed party due to the infringement or the benefits obtained by the infringer due to the infringement. Where the infringed party requests to determine the amount of compensation based on the exploitation license fee of the new plant variety, the People's Court may, in light of factors such as the type, duration, and scope of the exploitation license of the new plant variety, reasonably determine the amount of compensation with reference to the exploitation license fee of the new plant variety.  
Where it is difficult to determine the amount of compensation in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the People's Court may, after comprehensively considering factors such as the nature, duration, and consequences of the infringement, the amount of the exploitation license fee of the new plant variety, the type, duration, and scope of the exploitation license of the new plant variety, and the reasonable expenses paid by the infringed party for investigation and cessation of the infringement, determine the amount of compensation at no more than 500,000 yuan.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  被侵权人和侵权人均同意将侵权物折价抵扣被侵权人所受损失的，人民法院应当准许。被侵权人或者侵权人不同意折价抵扣的，人民法院依照当事人的请求，责令侵权人对侵权物作消灭活性等使其不能再被用作繁殖材料的处理。  
侵权物正处于生长期或者销毁侵权物将导致重大不利后果的，人民法院可以不采取责令销毁侵权物的方法，但法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 7** Where both the infringed party and the infringer agree to offset the infringed party's losses by converting the infringing objects into monetary value, the People's Court shall permit such offset. Where the infringed party or the infringer does not agree to such conversion and offset, the People's Court shall, at the request of the party, order the infringer to treat the infringing objects, such as by destroying their viability, so that they can no longer be used as propagating material.  
Where the infringing objects are in their growing period or where destroying the infringing objects would cause significant adverse consequences, the People's Court may refrain from ordering the destruction of the infringing objects, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  以农业或者林业种植为业的个人、农村承包经营户接受他人委托代为繁殖侵犯品种权的繁殖材料，不知道代繁物是侵犯品种权的繁殖材料并说明委托人的，不承担赔偿责任。

**Article 8** Where an individual engaged in agricultural or forestry planting, or a rural contractual household, accepts an entrustment from another person to propagate propagating material that infringes a variety right, and does not know that the material entrusted for propagation is propagating material that infringes the variety right and identifies the entrusting party, such individual or household shall not bear compensation liability.
