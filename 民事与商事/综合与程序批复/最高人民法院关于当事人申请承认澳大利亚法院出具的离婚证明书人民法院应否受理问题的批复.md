<a id="art-1"></a>2005年7月26日  
最高人民法院  
关于当事人申请承认澳大利亚法院出具的  
离婚证明书人民法院应否受理问题的批复  
法释〔2005〕8号  
（2005年7月11日最高人民法院审判委员会第1359次会议通过　2005年7月26日最高人民法院公告公布　自2005年8月1日起施行）  
广东省高级人民法院：  
你院报送的粤高法民一他字〔2004〕9号“关于当事人申请承认澳大利亚法院出具的离婚证明书有关问题”的请示收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
当事人持澳大利亚法院出具的离婚证明书向人民法院申请承认其效力的，人民法院应予受理，并依照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第二百六十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-267)和第二百六十八条以及最高人民法院《关于中国公民申请承认外国法院离婚判决程序问题的规定》的有关规定进行审查，依法作出承认或者不予承认的裁定。  
此复。

**Article 1** July 26, 2005  
Supreme People's Court  
Concerning a Party's Application for Recognition of a Certificate Issued by an Australian Court  
Divorce Certificate: Reply on Whether the People's Court Shall Accept the Matter  
Fa Shi [2005] No. 8  
(Adopted at the 1359th Session of the Judicial Committee of the Supreme People's Court on July 11, 2005; Promulgated by Announcement of the Supreme People's Court on July 26, 2005; Effective as of August 1, 2005)  
Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
We have received your Request (Yue Gao Fa Min Yi Ta Zi [2004] No. 9) concerning issues regarding a party's application for recognition of a divorce certificate issued by an Australian court. Upon study, we reply as follows:  
Where a party holds a divorce certificate issued by an Australian court and applies to a People's Court for recognition of its validity, the People's Court shall accept the case, and shall conduct examination in accordance with Articles 267 and 268 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and the relevant provisions of the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on the Procedures for Chinese Citizens' Application for Recognition of Foreign Courts' Divorce Judgments, and shall render a ruling on recognition or non-recognition according to law.  
This is a reply.
