# 最高人民法院关于审理医疗损害责任纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2017〕20号  
**公布日期**：2017-12-13  
**生效日期**：2017-12-14

> 《最高人民法院关于审理医疗损害责任纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2017年3月27日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1713次会议通过，现予公布，自2017年12月14日起施行。 2017年12月13日 最高人民法院 关于审理医疗损害责任纠纷案件适用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2017〕20号 （2017年3月27日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1713次会议通过，自2017年12月14日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　患者以在诊疗活动中受到人身或者财产损害为由请求医疗机构，医疗产品的生产者、销售者或者血液提供机构承担侵权责任的案件，适用本解释。  
患者以在美容医疗机构或者开设医疗美容科室的医疗机构实施的医疗美容活动中受到人身或者财产损害为由提起的侵权纠纷案件，适用本解释。  
当事人提起的医疗服务合同纠纷案件，不适用本解释。

**Article 1** This Interpretation shall apply to cases where a patient requests a medical institution, a producer or seller of medical products, or a blood supply institution to bear tort liability on the ground that the patient has suffered personal or property damage during medical diagnosis and treatment activities.  
This Interpretation shall apply to cases of tort disputes filed by a patient on the ground that the patient has suffered personal or property damage during medical aesthetic activities carried out by a medical aesthetic institution or a medical institution that has set up a medical aesthetic department.  
This Interpretation shall not apply to cases of disputes over medical service contracts filed by a party.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　患者因同一伤病在多个医疗机构接受诊疗受到损害，起诉部分或者全部就诊的医疗机构的，应予受理。  
患者起诉部分就诊的医疗机构后，当事人依法申请追加其他就诊的医疗机构为共同被告或者第三人的，应予准许。必要时，人民法院可以依法追加相关当事人参加诉讼。

**Article 2** Where a patient suffers damage from receiving medical treatment in multiple medical institutions for the same disease or injury and prosecutes some or all of the medical institutions where the patient visited, the case shall be accepted.  
After the patient prosecutes some of the medical institutions where the patient visited, if a party applies according to law for adding other medical institutions where the patient visited as co-defendants or third parties, such application shall be permitted. If necessary, the People's Court may add relevant parties to participate in the proceedings according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　患者因缺陷医疗产品受到损害，起诉部分或者全部医疗产品的生产者、销售者和医疗机构的，应予受理。  
患者仅起诉医疗产品的生产者、销售者、医疗机构中部分主体，当事人依法申请追加其他主体为共同被告或者第三人的，应予准许。必要时，人民法院可以依法追加相关当事人参加诉讼。  
患者因输入不合格的血液受到损害提起侵权诉讼的，参照适用前两款规定。

**Article 3** Where a patient suffers damage due to a defective medical product and prosecutes some or all of the producers, sellers of the medical product, and medical institutions, the case shall be accepted.  
If a patient prosecutes only some of the subjects among the producers, sellers of the medical product, and medical institutions, and a party applies according to law for adding other subjects as co-defendants or third parties, such application shall be permitted. If necessary, the People's Court may add relevant parties to participate in the proceedings according to law.  
Where a patient files a tort action for damage caused by the transfusion of non-conforming blood, the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　患者依据侵权责任法第五十四条规定主张医疗机构承担赔偿责任的，应当提交到该医疗机构就诊、受到损害的证据。  
患者无法提交医疗机构及其医务人员有过错、诊疗行为与损害之间具有因果关系的证据，依法提出医疗损害鉴定申请的，人民法院应予准许。  
医疗机构主张不承担责任的，应当就侵权责任法第六十条第一款规定情形等抗辩事由承担举证证明责任。

**Article 4** Where a patient claims that a medical institution shall bear compensation liability pursuant to Article 54 of the Tort Liability Law, the patient shall submit evidence of having visited the medical institution and having suffered damage.  
If the patient is unable to submit evidence that the medical institution and its medical personnel were at fault or that there is a causal relationship between the diagnosis and treatment act and the damage, and the patient applies for expert evaluation of medical damage according to law, the People's Court shall permit such application.  
Where a medical institution claims that it shall not bear liability, it shall bear the burden of proof regarding defenses such as the circumstances specified in Article 60, paragraph 1 of the Tort Liability Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　患者依据侵权责任法第五十五条规定主张医疗机构承担赔偿责任的，应当按照前条第一款规定提交证据。  
实施手术、特殊检查、特殊治疗的，医疗机构应当承担说明义务并取得患者或者患者近亲属书面同意，但属于侵权责任法第五十六条规定情形的除外。医疗机构提交患者或者患者近亲属书面同意证据的，人民法院可以认定医疗机构尽到说明义务，但患者有相反证据足以反驳的除外。

**Article 5** Where a patient claims that a medical institution shall bear compensation liability pursuant to Article 55 of the Tort Liability Law, the patient shall submit evidence in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of the preceding article.  
Where surgery, special examination, or special treatment is performed, the medical institution shall bear the duty of explanation and shall obtain written consent from the patient or the patient's near relative, except where the circumstances fall under Article 56 of the Tort Liability Law. If the medical institution submits evidence of written consent from the patient or the patient's near relative, the People's Court may find that the medical institution has fulfilled its duty of explanation, unless the patient has sufficient evidence to the contrary to rebut such finding.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　侵权责任法第五十八条规定的病历资料包括医疗机构保管的门诊病历、住院志、体温单、医嘱单、检验报告、医学影像检查资料、特殊检查（治疗）同意书、手术同意书、手术及麻醉记录、病理资料、护理记录、医疗费用、出院记录以及国务院卫生行政主管部门规定的其他病历资料。  
患者依法向人民法院申请医疗机构提交由其保管的与纠纷有关的病历资料等，医疗机构未在人民法院指定期限内提交的，人民法院可以依照侵权责任法第五十八条第二项规定推定医疗机构有过错，但是因不可抗力等客观原因无法提交的除外。

**Article 6** The medical records specified in Article 58 of the Tort Liability Law include the outpatient medical records, inpatient records, temperature charts, medical orders, test reports, medical imaging examination data, special examination (treatment) consent forms, surgical consent forms, surgical and anesthesia records, pathology data, nursing records, medical expenses, discharge records, and other medical records prescribed by the competent health administrative department of the State Council that are kept by the medical institution.  
Where a patient applies to the People's Court according to law for the medical institution to submit the medical records related to the dispute that are kept by the medical institution, and the medical institution fails to submit them within the time limit specified by the People's Court, the People's Court may presume that the medical institution is at fault in accordance with Article 58, item (2) of the Tort Liability Law, except where the medical institution is unable to submit due to force majeure or other objective reasons.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　患者依据侵权责任法第五十九条规定请求赔偿的，应当提交使用医疗产品或者输入血液、受到损害的证据。  
患者无法提交使用医疗产品或者输入血液与损害之间具有因果关系的证据，依法申请鉴定的，人民法院应予准许。  
医疗机构，医疗产品的生产者、销售者或者血液提供机构主张不承担责任的，应当对医疗产品不存在缺陷或者血液合格等抗辩事由承担举证证明责任。

**Article 7** Where a patient claims compensation pursuant to Article 59 of the Tort Liability Law, the patient shall submit evidence of using a medical product or receiving a blood transfusion and of having suffered damage.  
If the patient is unable to submit evidence of a causal relationship between the use of the medical product or the blood transfusion and the damage, and applies for expert evaluation according to law, the People's Court shall permit such application.  
Where a medical institution, a producer or seller of a medical product, or a blood supply institution claims that it shall not bear liability, it shall bear the burden of proof regarding defenses such as the absence of defects in the medical product or the conformity of the blood.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　当事人依法申请对医疗损害责任纠纷中的专门性问题进行鉴定的，人民法院应予准许。  
当事人未申请鉴定，人民法院对前款规定的专门性问题认为需要鉴定的，应当依职权委托鉴定。

**Article 8** Where a party applies according to law for an expert evaluation of a specialized issue in a dispute over liability for medical damage, the People's Court shall permit such application.  
If a party does not apply for an expert evaluation, and the People's Court considers that an expert evaluation of the specialized issue specified in the preceding paragraph is necessary, it shall, ex officio, commission an expert evaluation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　当事人申请医疗损害鉴定的，由双方当事人协商确定鉴定人。  
当事人就鉴定人无法达成一致意见，人民法院提出确定鉴定人的方法，当事人同意的，按照该方法确定；当事人不同意的，由人民法院指定。  
鉴定人应当从具备相应鉴定能力、符合鉴定要求的专家中确定。

**Article 9** Where a party applies for an expert evaluation of medical damage, the expert evaluator shall be determined through consultation between the parties.  
If the parties cannot reach a consensus on the expert evaluator, and the People's Court proposes a method for determining the expert evaluator, if the parties agree, the expert evaluator shall be determined in accordance with such method; if the parties do not agree, the People's Court shall designate the expert evaluator.  
The expert evaluator shall be determined from among experts who possess the corresponding evaluation capability and meet the evaluation requirements.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　委托医疗损害鉴定的，当事人应当按照要求提交真实、完整、充分的鉴定材料。提交的鉴定材料不符合要求的，人民法院应当通知当事人更换或者补充相应材料。  
在委托鉴定前，人民法院应当组织当事人对鉴定材料进行质证。

**Article 10** Where an expert evaluation of medical damage is commissioned, the parties shall submit truthful, complete, and sufficient evaluation materials as required. If the submitted evaluation materials do not meet the requirements, the People's Court shall notify the parties to replace or supplement the corresponding materials.  
Before commissioning the expert evaluation, the People's Court shall organize the parties to conduct cross-examination on the evaluation materials.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　委托鉴定书，应当有明确的鉴定事项和鉴定要求。鉴定人应当按照委托鉴定的事项和要求进行鉴定。  
下列专门性问题可以作为申请医疗损害鉴定的事项：  
（一）实施诊疗行为有无过错；  
（二）诊疗行为与损害后果之间是否存在因果关系以及原因力大小；  
（三）医疗机构是否尽到了说明义务、取得患者或者患者近亲属书面同意的义务；  
（四）医疗产品是否有缺陷、该缺陷与损害后果之间是否存在因果关系以及原因力的大小；  
（五）患者损伤残疾程度；  
（六）患者的护理期、休息期、营养期；  
（七）其他专门性问题。  
鉴定要求包括鉴定人的资质、鉴定人的组成、鉴定程序、鉴定意见、鉴定期限等。

**Article 11** A letter of commission for an expert evaluation shall specify clear evaluation items and evaluation requirements. The expert evaluator shall conduct the evaluation in accordance with the commissioned evaluation items and requirements.  
The following specialized issues may serve as items for an application for an expert evaluation of medical damage:  
(1) whether there was fault in the performance of the diagnosis and treatment act;  
(2) whether there is a causal relationship between the diagnosis and treatment act and the damage consequence, and the extent of the causative potency;  
(3) whether the medical institution fulfilled its duty of explanation and its duty to obtain written consent from the patient or the patient's near relative;  
(4) whether the medical product is defective, whether there is a causal relationship between the defect and the damage consequence, and the extent of the causative potency;  
(5) the degree of injury or disability of the patient;  
(6) the patient's nursing period, rest period, and nutritional period;  
(7) other specialized issues.  
Evaluation requirements include the qualifications of the expert evaluator, the composition of the expert evaluator, the evaluation procedure, the evaluation opinion, the evaluation time limit, and so on.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　鉴定意见可以按照导致患者损害的全部原因、主要原因、同等原因、次要原因、轻微原因或者与患者损害无因果关系，表述诊疗行为或者医疗产品等造成患者损害的原因力大小。

**Article 12** The expert evaluation opinion may express the extent of the causative potency of the diagnosis and treatment act or the medical product, etc., in causing damage to the patient according to the full cause, main cause, equal cause, secondary cause, minor cause, or no causal relationship with the patient's damage.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　鉴定意见应当经当事人质证。  
当事人申请鉴定人出庭作证，经人民法院审查同意，或者人民法院认为鉴定人有必要出庭的，应当通知鉴定人出庭作证。双方当事人同意鉴定人通过书面说明、视听传输技术或者视听资料等方式作证的，可以准许。  
鉴定人因健康原因、自然灾害等不可抗力或者其他正当理由不能按期出庭的，可以延期开庭；经人民法院许可，也可以通过书面说明、视听传输技术或者视听资料等方式作证。  
无前款规定理由，鉴定人拒绝出庭作证，当事人对鉴定意见又不认可的，对该鉴定意见不予采信。

**Article 13** An expert evaluation opinion shall be subject to cross-examination by the parties.  
Where a party applies for the expert evaluator to appear in court to testify, and upon examination and approval by the People's Court, or where the People's Court considers it necessary for the expert evaluator to appear in court, the People's Court shall notify the expert evaluator to appear in court to testify. If both parties agree that the expert evaluator may testify by means of a written explanation, audio-visual transmission technology, or audio-visual materials, permission may be granted.  
If the expert evaluator cannot appear in court on time due to health reasons, force majeure such as natural disasters, or other legitimate reasons, the hearing may be postponed; with the permission of the People's Court, the expert evaluator may also testify by means of a written explanation, audio-visual transmission technology, or audio-visual materials.  
If the expert evaluator refuses to appear in court to testify without the reasons specified in the preceding paragraph, and the parties do not recognize the expert evaluation opinion, such expert evaluation opinion shall not be admitted.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　当事人申请通知一至二名具有医学专门知识的人出庭，对鉴定意见或者案件的其他专门性事实问题提出意见，人民法院准许的，应当通知具有医学专门知识的人出庭。  
前款规定的具有医学专门知识的人提出的意见，视为当事人的陈述，经质证可以作为认定案件事实的根据。

**Article 14** Where a party applies for notification of one or two persons with specialized medical knowledge to appear in court to provide opinions on the expert evaluation opinion or other specialized factual issues of the case, and the People's Court permits such application, it shall notify the persons with specialized medical knowledge to appear in court.  
The opinions provided by the persons with specialized medical knowledge as specified in the preceding paragraph shall be regarded as statements of the party, and may serve as the basis for determining the facts of the case after cross-examination.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　当事人自行委托鉴定人作出的医疗损害鉴定意见，其他当事人认可的，可予采信。  
当事人共同委托鉴定人作出的医疗损害鉴定意见，一方当事人不认可的，应当提出明确的异议内容和理由。经审查，有证据足以证明异议成立的，对鉴定意见不予采信；异议不成立的，应予采信。

**Article 15** An expert evaluation opinion on medical damage made by an expert evaluator commissioned by a party on its own may be admitted if the other parties accept it.  
Where an expert evaluation opinion on medical damage is made by an expert evaluator jointly commissioned by the parties, and one party does not recognize it, such party shall present clear objections and reasons. Upon examination, if there is sufficient evidence to prove that the objections are valid, the expert evaluation opinion shall not be admitted; if the objections are not valid, it shall be admitted.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　对医疗机构及其医务人员的过错，应当依据法律、行政法规、规章以及其他有关诊疗规范进行认定，可以综合考虑患者病情的紧急程度、患者个体差异、当地的医疗水平、医疗机构与医务人员资质等因素。

**Article 16** The fault of a medical institution and its medical personnel shall be determined in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, rules, and other relevant diagnosis and treatment norms, and may comprehensively consider factors such as the urgency of the patient's condition, individual patient differences, the local medical level, and the qualifications of the medical institution and medical personnel.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　医务人员违反侵权责任法第五十五条第一款规定义务，但未造成患者人身损害，患者请求医疗机构承担损害赔偿责任的，不予支持。

**Article 17** Where medical personnel violate the duty specified in Article 55, paragraph 1 of the Tort Liability Law, but no personal damage is caused to the patient, and the patient requests the medical institution to bear liability for damages, such request shall not be supported.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　因抢救生命垂危的患者等紧急情况且不能取得患者意见时，下列情形可以认定为侵权责任法第五十六条规定的不能取得患者近亲属意见：  
（一）近亲属不明的；  
（二）不能及时联系到近亲属的；  
（三）近亲属拒绝发表意见的；  
（四）近亲属达不成一致意见的；  
（五）法律、法规规定的其他情形。  
前款情形，医务人员经医疗机构负责人或者授权的负责人批准立即实施相应医疗措施，患者因此请求医疗机构承担赔偿责任的，不予支持；医疗机构及其医务人员怠于实施相应医疗措施造成损害，患者请求医疗机构承担赔偿责任的，应予支持。

**Article 18** In emergency situations such as rescuing a patient whose life is in danger, and when the patient's opinion cannot be obtained, the following circumstances may be identified as the inability to obtain the opinion of the patient's near relative as specified in Article 56 of the Tort Liability Law:  
(1) the near relative is unknown;  
(2) the near relative cannot be contacted in a timely manner;  
(3) the near relative refuses to express an opinion;  
(4) the near relatives fail to reach a consensus;  
(5) other circumstances provided by laws or regulations.  
In the circumstances of the preceding paragraph, if the medical personnel immediately implement the corresponding medical measures with the approval of the person in charge of the medical institution or an authorized person in charge, and the patient thereby requests the medical institution to bear compensation liability, such request shall not be supported; if the medical institution or its medical personnel fail to implement the corresponding medical measures diligently, causing damage, and the patient requests the medical institution to bear compensation liability, such request shall be supported.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　两个以上医疗机构的诊疗行为造成患者同一损害，患者请求医疗机构承担赔偿责任的，应当区分不同情况，依照侵权责任法第八条、第十一条或者第十二条的规定，确定各医疗机构承担的赔偿责任。

**Article 19** Where the diagnosis and treatment acts of two or more medical institutions cause the same damage to a patient, and the patient requests the medical institutions to bear compensation liability, the compensation liability to be borne by each medical institution shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of Article 8, Article 11, or Article 12 of the Tort Liability Law, as the case may be.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　医疗机构邀请本单位以外的医务人员对患者进行诊疗，因受邀医务人员的过错造成患者损害的，由邀请医疗机构承担赔偿责任。

**Article 20** Where a medical institution invites medical personnel from outside its own unit to provide diagnosis and treatment to a patient, and damage is caused to the patient due to the fault of the invited medical personnel, the inviting medical institution shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　因医疗产品的缺陷或者输入不合格血液受到损害，患者请求医疗机构，缺陷医疗产品的生产者、销售者或者血液提供机构承担赔偿责任的，应予支持。  
医疗机构承担赔偿责任后，向缺陷医疗产品的生产者、销售者或者血液提供机构追偿的，应予支持。  
因医疗机构的过错使医疗产品存在缺陷或者血液不合格，医疗产品的生产者、销售者或者血液提供机构承担赔偿责任后，向医疗机构追偿的，应予支持。

**Article 21** Where a patient suffers damage due to a defect in a medical product or the transfusion of non-conforming blood, and the patient requests the medical institution, the producer or seller of the defective medical product, or the blood supply institution to bear compensation liability, such request shall be supported.  
After the medical institution bears compensation liability, if it seeks recourse against the producer or seller of the defective medical product or the blood supply institution, such claim shall be supported.  
Where a medical product is defective or blood is non-conforming due to the fault of the medical institution, and after the producer or seller of the medical product or the blood supply institution bears compensation liability, if it seeks recourse against the medical institution, such claim shall be supported.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　缺陷医疗产品与医疗机构的过错诊疗行为共同造成患者同一损害，患者请求医疗机构与医疗产品的生产者或者销售者承担连带责任的，应予支持。  
医疗机构或者医疗产品的生产者、销售者承担赔偿责任后，向其他责任主体追偿的，应当根据诊疗行为与缺陷医疗产品造成患者损害的原因力大小确定相应的数额。  
输入不合格血液与医疗机构的过错诊疗行为共同造成患者同一损害的，参照适用前两款规定。

**Article 22** Where a defective medical product and the faulty diagnosis and treatment act of a medical institution jointly cause the same damage to a patient, and the patient requests the medical institution and the producer or seller of the medical product to bear joint and several liability, such request shall be supported.  
After the medical institution or the producer or seller of the medical product bears compensation liability, if it seeks recourse against other liable parties, the corresponding amount shall be determined according to the extent of the causative potency of the diagnosis and treatment act and the defective medical product in causing the patient's damage.  
Where the transfusion of non-conforming blood and the faulty diagnosis and treatment act of a medical institution jointly cause the same damage to a patient, the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　医疗产品的生产者、销售者明知医疗产品存在缺陷仍然生产、销售，造成患者死亡或者健康严重损害，被侵权人请求生产者、销售者赔偿损失及二倍以下惩罚性赔偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 23** Where the producer or seller of a medical product, knowing that the medical product has a defect, nevertheless produces or sells it, causing the death of a patient or serious damage to health, and the infringed party requests the producer or seller to compensate for losses and to pay punitive damages of not more than twice the amount of losses, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　被侵权人同时起诉两个以上医疗机构承担赔偿责任，人民法院经审理，受诉法院所在地的医疗机构依法不承担赔偿责任，其他医疗机构承担赔偿责任的，残疾赔偿金、死亡赔偿金的计算，按下列情形分别处理：  
（一）一个医疗机构承担责任的，按照该医疗机构所在地的赔偿标准执行；  
（二）两个以上医疗机构均承担责任的，可以按照其中赔偿标准较高的医疗机构所在地标准执行。

**Article 24** Where the infringed party simultaneously prosecutes two or more medical institutions to bear compensation liability, and after trial, the People's Court finds that the medical institution located in the place of the court hearing the case shall not bear compensation liability according to law, while other medical institutions bear compensation liability, the calculation of disability compensation and death compensation shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) if one medical institution bears liability, the compensation standard of the place where that medical institution is located shall be applied;  
(2) if two or more medical institutions bear liability, the standard of the place of the medical institution with the higher compensation standard may be applied.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　患者死亡后，其近亲属请求医疗损害赔偿的，适用本解释；支付患者医疗费、丧葬费等合理费用的人请求赔偿该费用的，适用本解释。  
本解释所称的“医疗产品”包括药品、消毒药剂、医疗器械等。

**Article 25** After the patient dies, if the patient's near relative requests compensation for medical damage, this Interpretation shall apply; if a person who has paid reasonable expenses such as medical expenses and funeral expenses for the patient requests compensation for such expenses, this Interpretation shall apply.  
The term "medical products" as used in this Interpretation includes pharmaceuticals, disinfectants, medical devices, and the like.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本院以前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。  
本解释施行后尚未终审的案件，适用本解释；本解释施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本解释。

**Article 26** If any judicial interpretation previously issued by this Court is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.  
Cases that have not been finally adjudicated after this Interpretation comes into force shall be governed by this Interpretation; cases that have been finally adjudicated before this Interpretation comes into force and for which a party applies for retrial or which are decided to be retried in accordance with the trial supervision procedure shall not be governed by this Interpretation.
