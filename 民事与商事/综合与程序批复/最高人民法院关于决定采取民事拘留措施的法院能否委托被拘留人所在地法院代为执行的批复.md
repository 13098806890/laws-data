# 最高人民法院关于决定采取民事拘留措施的法院能否委托被拘留人所在地法院代为执行的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：复〔1987〕43号  
**公布日期**：1987-07-04

---

### 正文

一、在民事诉讼中，人民法院对故意妨害民事诉讼的诉讼参与人或者其他人决定采取民事拘留措施，必须十分慎重。尤其是到外省采取民事拘留措施的，作出决定的法院一般应向上一级人民法院报告后，再行委托实施。

**Article None** 1. In a civil action, a People's Court shall exercise extreme caution when deciding to impose civil detention on a participant in the action or any other person who intentionally obstructs the civil action. In particular, if the civil detention is to be enforced in another province, the court making the decision shall generally report to the People's Court at the next higher level before commissioning implementation.

二、对被拘留人不在作出民事拘留决定的法院所在地的，作出决定的法院应派员到被拘留人所在地法院，说明情况，面交委托书和《拘留决定书》，请该院代为执行。受委托的法院应及时派出司法警察，将被拘留人连同委托书和《拘留决定书》一并送交当地公安机关，由当地公安机关予以看管。

**Article None** 2. If the detained person is not at the location of the court that made the civil detention decision, the court making the decision shall send its personnel to the court at the detained person's location, explain the situation, personally deliver the letter of entrustment and the Detention Decision, and request that court to enforce the decision on its behalf. The entrusted court shall promptly dispatch judicial police to deliver the detained person together with the letter of entrustment and the Detention Decision to the local public security organ, which shall then take the detained person into custody.

三、受委托的法院对被拘留人申请复议，或者被拘留人在拘留期间承认并改正错误，需要提前解除拘留的，应向决定采取民事拘留措施的法院转达或者提出建议，由决定采取民事拘留措施的法院审查，作出决定。  
此复  
1987年10月15日

**Article None** 3. If the detained person applies for reconsideration to the entrusted court, or if during the detention period the detained person admits and corrects the error and thus early release from detention is necessary, the entrusted court shall convey or make a recommendation to the court that decided to impose the civil detention measure, and the court that decided to impose the civil detention measure shall review the matter and make a decision.  
This is in reply.
