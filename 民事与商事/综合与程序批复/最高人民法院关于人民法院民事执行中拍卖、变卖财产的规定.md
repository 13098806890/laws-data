# 最高人民法院关于人民法院民事执行中拍卖、变卖财产的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2004〕16号  
**公布日期**：2004-11-15  
**生效日期**：2005-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于人民法院民事执行中拍卖、变卖财产的规定》已于2004年10月26日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1330次会议通过，现予公布，自2005年1月1日起施行。 2004年11月15日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院民事执行中拍卖、 变卖财产的规定 法释〔2004〕16号 （2004年10月26日最高人民法院审判委员会第1330次会议通过　2004年11月15日最高人民法院公告公布　自2005年1月1日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　在执行程序中，被执行人的财产被查封、扣押、冻结后，人民法院应当及时进行拍卖、变卖或者采取其他执行措施。

**Article 1** In the enforcement procedure, after the property of the person subject to enforcement is sealed, seized, or frozen, the People's Court shall promptly conduct an auction, sell-off, or take other enforcement measures.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院对查封、扣押、冻结的财产进行变价处理时，应当首先采取拍卖的方式，但法律、司法解释另有规定的除外。

**Article 2** When the People's Court disposes of sealed, seized, or frozen property by conversion into money, it shall first adopt the method of auction, unless otherwise provided by laws or judicial interpretations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院拍卖被执行人财产，应当委托具有相应资质的拍卖机构进行，并对拍卖机构的拍卖进行监督，但法律、司法解释另有规定的除外。

**Article 3** When the People's Court auctions the property of the person subject to enforcement, it shall entrust an auction institution with corresponding qualifications to conduct the auction, and shall supervise the auction by the auction institution, unless otherwise provided by laws or judicial interpretations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　对拟拍卖的财产，人民法院应当委托具有相应资质的评估机构进行价格评估。对于财产价值较低或者价格依照通常方法容易确定的，可以不进行评估。  
当事人双方及其他执行债权人申请不进行评估的，人民法院应当准许。  
对被执行人的股权进行评估时，人民法院可以责令有关企业提供会计报表等资料；有关企业拒不提供的，可以强制提取。

**Article 4** For the property to be auctioned, the People's Court shall entrust an appraisal institution with corresponding qualifications to conduct a price appraisal. For property of low value or whose price is easily determined by ordinary methods, appraisal may be dispensed with.  
If both parties and other enforcement creditors apply for no appraisal, the People's Court shall permit it.  
When appraising the equity of the person subject to enforcement, the People's Court may order the relevant enterprise to provide financial statements and other materials; if the relevant enterprise refuses to provide them, the People's Court may forcibly extract them.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　评估机构由当事人协商一致后经人民法院审查确定；协商不成的，从负责执行的人民法院或者被执行人财产所在地的人民法院确定的评估机构名册中，采取随机的方式确定；当事人双方申请通过公开招标方式确定评估机构的，人民法院应当准许。

**Article 5** The appraisal institution shall be determined by the parties through consultation and then confirmed by the People's Court upon review; if consultation fails, it shall be determined by random selection from the list of appraisal institutions designated by the People's Court responsible for enforcement or the People's Court at the place where the property of the person subject to enforcement is located; if both parties apply to determine the appraisal institution through public bidding, the People's Court shall permit it.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院收到评估机构作出的评估报告后，应当在五日内将评估报告发送当事人及其他利害关系人。当事人或者其他利害关系人对评估报告有异议的，可以在收到评估报告后十日内以书面形式向人民法院提出。  
当事人或者其他利害关系人有证据证明评估机构、评估人员不具备相应的评估资质或者评估程序严重违法而申请重新评估的，人民法院应当准许。

**Article 6** After receiving the appraisal report made by the appraisal institution, the People's Court shall, within five days, send the appraisal report to the parties and other interested persons. If a party or other interested person objects to the appraisal report, it may submit a written objection to the People's Court within ten days after receiving the appraisal report.  
If a party or other interested person has evidence proving that the appraisal institution or appraiser does not have the corresponding appraisal qualifications or that the appraisal procedure is seriously illegal, and applies for a re-appraisal, the People's Court shall permit it.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　拍卖机构由当事人协商一致后经人民法院审查确定；协商不成的，从负责执行的人民法院或者被执行人财产所在地的人民法院确定的拍卖机构名册中，采取随机的方式确定；当事人双方申请通过公开招标方式确定拍卖机构的，人民法院应当准许。

**Article 7** The auction institution shall be determined by the parties through consultation and then confirmed by the People's Court upon review; if consultation fails, it shall be determined by random selection from the list of auction institutions designated by the People's Court responsible for enforcement or the People's Court at the place where the property of the person subject to enforcement is located; if both parties apply to determine the auction institution through public bidding, the People's Court shall permit it.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　拍卖应当确定保留价。  
拍卖保留价由人民法院参照评估价确定；未作评估的，参照市价确定，并应当征询有关当事人的意见。  
人民法院确定的保留价，第一次拍卖时，不得低于评估价或者市价的百分之八十；如果出现流拍，再行拍卖时，可以酌情降低保留价，但每次降低的数额不得超过前次保留价的百分之二十。

**Article 8** A reserve price shall be determined for the auction.  
The auction reserve price shall be determined by the People's Court with reference to the appraised price; if no appraisal has been made, it shall be determined with reference to the market price, and the opinions of the relevant parties shall be consulted.  
The reserve price determined by the People's Court, at the first auction, shall not be less than 80% of the appraised price or market price; if a failed auction occurs, the reserve price may be appropriately reduced for subsequent auctions, but the amount of each reduction shall not exceed 20% of the previous reserve price.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　保留价确定后，依据本次拍卖保留价计算，拍卖所得价款在清偿优先债权和强制执行费用后无剩余可能的，应当在实施拍卖前将有关情况通知申请执行人。申请执行人于收到通知后五日内申请继续拍卖的，人民法院应当准许，但应当重新确定保留价；重新确定的保留价应当大于该优先债权及强制执行费用的总额。  
依照前款规定流拍的，拍卖费用由申请执行人负担。

**Article 9** After the reserve price is determined, if it is calculated based on the reserve price for this auction that the proceeds from the auction have no possibility of any surplus after satisfying the priority claims and compulsory enforcement expenses, the relevant circumstances shall be notified to the applying enforcement party before the auction is conducted. If the applying enforcement party applies to continue the auction within five days after receiving the notice, the People's Court shall permit it, but a new reserve price shall be re-determined; the re-determined reserve price shall be greater than the total amount of such priority claims and compulsory enforcement expenses.  
If the auction fails in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the auction expenses shall be borne by the applying enforcement party.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　执行人员应当对拍卖财产的权属状况、占有使用情况等进行必要的调查，制作拍卖财产现状的调查笔录或者收集其他有关资料。

**Article 10** The enforcement personnel shall conduct necessary investigations into the ownership status, possession and use, etc. of the auction property, and make investigation records of the current status of the auction property or collect other relevant materials.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　拍卖应当先期公告。  
拍卖动产的，应当在拍卖七日前公告；拍卖不动产或者其他财产权的，应当在拍卖十五日前公告。

**Article 11** The auction shall be announced in advance.  
For auction of movable property, the announcement shall be made seven days before the auction; for auction of immovable property or other property rights, the announcement shall be made fifteen days before the auction.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　拍卖公告的范围及媒体由当事人双方协商确定；协商不成的，由人民法院确定。拍卖财产具有专业属性的，应当同时在专业性报纸上进行公告。  
当事人申请在其他新闻媒体上公告或者要求扩大公告范围的，应当准许，但该部分的公告费用由其自行承担。

**Article 12** The scope and media of the auction announcement shall be determined by both parties through consultation; if consultation fails, it shall be determined by the People's Court. If the auction property has a professional nature, the announcement shall also be made in professional newspapers.  
If a party applies for announcement in other news media or requests to expand the scope of the announcement, it shall be permitted, but the announcement costs for such part shall be borne by it.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　拍卖不动产、其他财产权或者价值较高的动产的，竞买人应当于拍卖前向人民法院预交保证金。申请执行人参加竞买的，可以不预交保证金。保证金的数额由人民法院确定，但不得低于评估价或者市价的百分之五。  
应当预交保证金而未交纳的，不得参加竞买。拍卖成交后，买受人预交的保证金充抵价款，其他竞买人预交的保证金应当在三日内退还；拍卖未成交的，保证金应当于三日内退还竞买人。

**Article 13** For auction of immovable property, other property rights, or movable property of relatively high value, bidders shall pay a deposit to the People's Court in advance before the auction. If the applying enforcement party participates in the bidding, it may be exempted from paying the deposit. The amount of the deposit shall be determined by the People's Court, but shall not be less than 5% of the appraised price or market price.  
If a deposit ought to be paid in advance but is not paid, the party shall not participate in the bidding. After the auction is concluded, the deposit paid in advance by the buyer shall be applied to the purchase price, and the deposits paid in advance by other bidders shall be returned within three days; if the auction is not concluded, the deposits shall be returned to the bidders within three days.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院应当在拍卖五日前以书面或者其他能够确认收悉的适当方式，通知当事人和已知的担保物权人、优先购买权人或者其他优先权人于拍卖日到场。  
优先购买权人经通知未到场的，视为放弃优先购买权。

**Article 14** The People's Court shall, five days before the auction, notify the parties and known security interest holders, preemptive rights holders, or other priority persons to appear on the auction date in writing or by other appropriate means that can confirm receipt.  
If a preemptive rights holder, after being notified, fails to appear, it shall be deemed to have waived its preemptive right.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　法律、行政法规对买受人的资格或者条件有特殊规定的，竞买人应当具备规定的资格或者条件。  
申请执行人、被执行人可以参加竞买。

**Article 15** If laws or administrative regulations have special provisions on the qualifications or conditions of a buyer, bidders shall meet the prescribed qualifications or conditions.  
The applying enforcement party and the person subject to enforcement may participate in the bidding.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　拍卖过程中，有最高应价时，优先购买权人可以表示以该最高价买受，如无更高应价，则拍归优先购买权人；如有更高应价，而优先购买权人不作表示的，则拍归该应价最高的竞买人。  
顺序相同的多个优先购买权人同时表示买受的，以抽签方式决定买受人。

**Article 16** During the auction, when there is a highest bid, the preemptive rights holder may indicate its intention to purchase at that highest price; if there is no higher bid, the property shall be knocked down to the preemptive rights holder; if there is a higher bid and the preemptive rights holder does not make any indication, the property shall be knocked down to the bidder with the highest bid.  
If multiple preemptive rights holders with the same order simultaneously indicate their intention to purchase, the buyer shall be determined by drawing lots.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　拍卖多项财产时，其中部分财产卖得的价款足以清偿债务和支付被执行人应当负担的费用的，对剩余的财产应当停止拍卖，但被执行人同意全部拍卖的除外。

**Article 17** When auctioning multiple items of property, if the proceeds from the sale of part of the property are sufficient to satisfy the debts and pay the expenses to be borne by the person subject to enforcement, the auction of the remaining property shall be stopped, unless the person subject to enforcement agrees to auction all of it.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　拍卖的多项财产在使用上不可分，或者分别拍卖可能严重减损其价值的，应当合并拍卖。

**Article 18** If multiple items of property to be auctioned are indivisible in use, or if separate auctions may seriously diminish their value, they shall be auctioned together.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　拍卖时无人竞买或者竞买人的最高应价低于保留价，到场的申请执行人或者其他执行债权人申请或者同意以该次拍卖所定的保留价接受拍卖财产的，应当将该财产交其抵债。  
有两个以上执行债权人申请以拍卖财产抵债的，由法定受偿顺位在先的债权人优先承受；受偿顺位相同的，以抽签方式决定承受人。承受人应受清偿的债权额低于抵债财产的价额的，人民法院应当责令其在指定的期间内补交差额。

**Article 19** If no one bids at the auction or the highest bid is lower than the reserve price, and the applying enforcement party or other enforcement creditor present applies for or agrees to accept the auction property at the reserve price set for that auction, the property shall be delivered to such party in satisfaction of the debt.  
If two or more enforcement creditors apply to accept the auction property in satisfaction of the debt, the creditor with a higher statutory priority of satisfaction shall receive it first; if the priorities are the same, the recipient shall be determined by drawing lots. If the amount of the debt to be satisfied of the recipient is lower than the value of the property used to satisfy the debt, the People's Court shall order it to pay the difference within a specified period.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　在拍卖开始前，有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当撤回拍卖委托：  
（一）据以执行的生效法律文书被撤销的；  
（二）申请执行人及其他执行债权人撤回执行申请的；  
（三）被执行人全部履行了法律文书确定的金钱债务的；  
（四）当事人达成了执行和解协议，不需要拍卖财产的；  
（五）案外人对拍卖财产提出确有理由的异议的；  
（六）拍卖机构与竞买人恶意串通的；  
（七）其他应当撤回拍卖委托的情形。

**Article 20** Before the auction begins, if any of the following circumstances exist, the People's Court shall withdraw the auction mandate:  
(1) The effective legal document on which enforcement is based is revoked;  
(2) The applying enforcement party and other enforcement creditors withdraw the enforcement application;  
(3) The person subject to enforcement has fully performed the monetary obligations determined by the legal document;  
(4) The parties have reached an enforcement settlement agreement and no auction of property is needed;  
(5) A person not involved in the case raises a well-grounded objection to the auction property;  
(6) The auction institution and the bidders collude maliciously;  
(7) Other circumstances in which the auction mandate should be withdrawn.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民法院委托拍卖后，遇有依法应当暂缓执行或者中止执行的情形的，应当决定暂缓执行或者裁定中止执行，并及时通知拍卖机构和当事人。拍卖机构收到通知后，应当立即停止拍卖，并通知竞买人。  
暂缓执行期限届满或者中止执行的事由消失后，需要继续拍卖的，人民法院应当在十五日内通知拍卖机构恢复拍卖。

**Article 21** After the People's Court has entrusted the auction, if circumstances arise where enforcement should be suspended or stayed according to law, it shall decide to suspend enforcement or rule to stay enforcement, and promptly notify the auction institution and the parties. Upon receiving the notice, the auction institution shall immediately stop the auction and notify the bidders.  
After the period of suspension expires or the cause for the stay disappears, if the auction needs to be continued, the People's Court shall, within fifteen days, notify the auction institution to resume the auction.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　被执行人在拍卖日之前向人民法院提交足额金钱清偿债务，要求停止拍卖的，人民法院应当准许，但被执行人应当负担因拍卖支出的必要费用。

**Article 22** If the person subject to enforcement submits sufficient money to the People's Court before the auction date to satisfy the debt and requests to stop the auction, the People's Court shall permit it, but the person subject to enforcement shall bear the necessary expenses incurred for the auction.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　拍卖成交或者以流拍的财产抵债的，人民法院应当作出裁定，并于价款或者需要补交的差价全额交付后十日内，送达买受人或者承受人。

**Article 23** If the auction is concluded or the failed auction property is used to satisfy the debt, the People's Court shall make a ruling, and serve it on the buyer or the recipient within ten days after the full payment of the purchase price or the difference to be made up.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　拍卖成交后，买受人应当在拍卖公告确定的期限或者人民法院指定的期限内将价款交付到人民法院或者汇入人民法院指定的账户。

**Article 24** After the auction is concluded, the buyer shall deliver the purchase price to the People's Court or deposit it into the account designated by the People's Court within the period determined in the auction announcement or within the period specified by the People's Court.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　拍卖成交或者以流拍的财产抵债后，买受人逾期未支付价款或者承受人逾期未补交差价而使拍卖、抵债的目的难以实现的，人民法院可以裁定重新拍卖。重新拍卖时，原买受人不得参加竞买。  
重新拍卖的价款低于原拍卖价款造成的差价、费用损失及原拍卖中的佣金，由原买受人承担。人民法院可以直接从其预交的保证金中扣除。扣除后保证金有剩余的，应当退还原买受人；保证金数额不足的，可以责令原买受人补交；拒不补交的，强制执行。

**Article 25** After the auction is concluded or the failed auction property is used to satisfy the debt, if the buyer fails to pay the purchase price within the prescribed period or the recipient fails to make up the difference within the prescribed period, making it difficult to achieve the purpose of the auction or satisfaction of debt, the People's Court may rule to re-auction. In the re-auction, the original buyer shall not participate in the bidding.  
If the price of the re-auction is lower than the price of the original auction, the resulting difference, loss of expenses, and the commission of the original auction shall be borne by the original buyer. The People's Court may directly deduct such amounts from the deposit paid in advance by the original buyer. If there is a surplus after deduction, it shall be returned to the original buyer; if the deposit is insufficient, the People's Court may order the original buyer to make up the shortfall; if the original buyer refuses to make up the shortfall, compulsory execution shall be carried out.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　拍卖时无人竞买或者竞买人的最高应价低于保留价，到场的申请执行人或者其他执行债权人不申请以该次拍卖所定的保留价抵债的，应当在六十日内再行拍卖。

**Article 26** If no one bids at the auction or the highest bid is lower than the reserve price, and the applying enforcement party or other enforcement creditor present does not apply to accept the property at the reserve price set for that auction in satisfaction of the debt, a re-auction shall be held within sixty days.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对于第二次拍卖仍流拍的动产，人民法院可以依照[本规定第十九条](#art-19)的规定将其作价交申请执行人或者其他执行债权人抵债。申请执行人或者其他执行债权人拒绝接受或者依法不能交付其抵债的，人民法院应当解除查封、扣押，并将该动产退还被执行人。

**Article 27** For movable property that still fails to sell at the second auction, the People's Court may, in accordance with the provisions of Article 19 of these Provisions, value it and deliver it to the applying enforcement party or other enforcement creditor in satisfaction of the debt. If the applying enforcement party or other enforcement creditor refuses to accept it or it cannot be delivered to them in satisfaction of the debt according to law, the People's Court shall release the seal and seizure and return the movable property to the person subject to enforcement.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对于第二次拍卖仍流拍的不动产或者其他财产权，人民法院可以依照[本规定第十九条](#art-19)的规定将其作价交申请执行人或者其他执行债权人抵债。申请执行人或者其他执行债权人拒绝接受或者依法不能交付其抵债的，应当在六十日内进行第三次拍卖。  
第三次拍卖流拍且申请执行人或者其他执行债权人拒绝接受或者依法不能接受该不动产或者其他财产权抵债的，人民法院应当于第三次拍卖终结之日起七日内发出变卖公告。自公告之日起六十日内没有买受人愿意以第三次拍卖的保留价买受该财产，且申请执行人、其他执行债权人仍不表示接受该财产抵债的，应当解除查封、冻结，将该财产退还被执行人，但对该财产可以采取其他执行措施的除外。

**Article 28** For immovable property or other property rights that still fail to sell at the second auction, the People's Court may, in accordance with the provisions of Article 19 of these Provisions, value it and deliver it to the applying enforcement party or other enforcement creditor in satisfaction of the debt. If the applying enforcement party or other enforcement creditor refuses to accept it or it cannot be delivered to them in satisfaction of the debt according to law, a third auction shall be held within sixty days.  
If the third auction fails to sell, and the applying enforcement party or other enforcement creditor refuses to accept or cannot accept the immovable property or other property rights in satisfaction of the debt according to law, the People's Court shall, within seven days from the date of conclusion of the third auction, issue a notice of sale. If within sixty days from the date of the notice, no buyer is willing to purchase the property at the reserve price of the third auction, and the applying enforcement party or other enforcement creditor still does not indicate acceptance of the property in satisfaction of the debt, the People's Court shall release the seal and freeze and return the property to the person subject to enforcement, unless other enforcement measures may be taken against the property.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　动产拍卖成交或者抵债后，其所有权自该动产交付时起转移给买受人或者承受人。  
不动产、有登记的特定动产或者其他财产权拍卖成交或者抵债后，该不动产、特定动产的所有权、其他财产权自拍卖成交或者抵债裁定送达买受人或者承受人时起转移。

**Article 29** After movable property is auctioned and concluded or used to satisfy a debt, its ownership is transferred to the buyer or the recipient from the time of delivery of the movable property.  
After immovable property, registered specific movable property, or other property rights are auctioned and concluded or used to satisfy a debt, the ownership of such immovable property or specific movable property, or the other property rights, are transferred from the time when the ruling on the auction conclusion or satisfaction of debt is served on the buyer or the recipient.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　人民法院裁定拍卖成交或者以流拍的财产抵债后，除有依法不能移交的情形外，应当于裁定送达后十五日内，将拍卖的财产移交买受人或者承受人。被执行人或者第三人占有拍卖财产应当移交而拒不移交的，强制执行。

**Article 30** After the People's Court rules that the auction is concluded or that the failed auction property is used to satisfy a debt, unless there are circumstances where delivery cannot be made according to law, the People's Court shall, within fifteen days after the ruling is served, deliver the auctioned property to the buyer or the recipient. If the person subject to enforcement or a third party possesses the auctioned property and should deliver it but refuses to do so, compulsory execution shall be carried out.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　拍卖财产上原有的担保物权及其他优先受偿权，因拍卖而消灭，拍卖所得价款，应当优先清偿担保物权人及其他优先受偿权人的债权，但当事人另有约定的除外。  
拍卖财产上原有的租赁权及其他用益物权，不因拍卖而消灭，但该权利继续存在于拍卖财产上，对在先的担保物权或者其他优先受偿权的实现有影响的，人民法院应当依法将其除去后进行拍卖。

**Article 31** The original security interests and other priority rights to satisfaction on the auctioned property are extinguished by the auction, and the proceeds from the auction shall be used first to satisfy the claims of the holders of security interests and other priority rights, unless otherwise agreed by the parties.  
The original leaseholds and other usufructs on the auctioned property are not extinguished by the auction; however, if such rights continue to exist on the auctioned property and affect the realization of prior security interests or other priority rights to satisfaction, the People's Court shall, according to law, remove them before auctioning the property.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　拍卖成交的，拍卖机构可以按照下列比例向买受人收取佣金：  
拍卖成交价200万元以下的，收取佣金的比例不得超过5%；超过200万元至1000万元的部分，不得超过3%；超过1000万元至5000万元的部分，不得超过2%；超过5000万元至1亿元的部分，不得超过1%；超过1亿元的部分，不得超过0.5%。  
采取公开招标方式确定拍卖机构的，按照中标方案确定的数额收取佣金。  
拍卖未成交或者非因拍卖机构的原因撤回拍卖委托的，拍卖机构为本次拍卖已经支出的合理费用，应当由被执行人负担。

**Article 32** If the auction is concluded, the auction institution may charge a commission from the buyer according to the following proportions:  
For an auction price of less than 2 million yuan, the proportion of commission charged shall not exceed 5%; for the portion exceeding 2 million yuan up to 10 million yuan, it shall not exceed 3%; for the portion exceeding 10 million yuan up to 50 million yuan, it shall not exceed 2%; for the portion exceeding 50 million yuan up to 100 million yuan, it shall not exceed 1%; for the portion exceeding 100 million yuan, it shall not exceed 0.5%.  
If the auction institution is determined through public bidding, the commission shall be charged according to the amount determined in the winning bid plan.  
If the auction is not concluded or the auction mandate is withdrawn for reasons not attributable to the auction institution, the reasonable expenses already incurred by the auction institution for this auction shall be borne by the person subject to enforcement.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　在执行程序中拍卖上市公司国有股和社会法人股的，适用最高人民法院《关于冻结、拍卖上市公司国有股和社会法人股若干问题的规定》。

**Article 33** When auctioning state-owned shares and social legal person shares of listed companies in the enforcement procedure, the Supreme People's Court's "Provisions on Several Issues concerning the Freezing and Auction of State-owned Shares and Social Legal Person Shares of Listed Companies" shall apply.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　对查封、扣押、冻结的财产，当事人双方及有关权利人同意变卖的，可以变卖。  
金银及其制品、当地市场有公开交易价格的动产、易腐烂变质的物品、季节性商品、保管困难或者保管费用过高的物品，人民法院可以决定变卖。

**Article 34** If both parties and the relevant right holders agree to a sale of the sealed, seized, or frozen property, it may be sold.  
For gold, silver and their products, movable property with a publicly traded price on the local market, perishable items, seasonal goods, items difficult to store or with high storage costs, the People's Court may decide to sell them.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　当事人双方及有关权利人对变卖财产的价格有约定的，按照其约定价格变卖；无约定价格但有市价的，变卖价格不得低于市价；无市价但价值较大、价格不易确定的，应当委托评估机构进行评估，并按照评估价格进行变卖。  
按照评估价格变卖不成的，可以降低价格变卖，但最低的变卖价不得低于评估价的二分之一。  
变卖的财产无人应买的，适用[本规定第十九条](#art-19)的规定将该财产交申请执行人或者其他执行债权人抵债；申请执行人或者其他执行债权人拒绝接受或者依法不能交付其抵债的，人民法院应当解除查封、扣押，并将该财产退还被执行人。

**Article 35** If both parties and the relevant right holders have an agreed price for the property to be sold, the sale shall be made at the agreed price; if there is no agreed price but there is a market price, the sale price shall not be lower than the market price; if there is no market price but the value is relatively large and the price is not easy to determine, an appraisal institution shall be entrusted to conduct an appraisal, and the sale shall be made at the appraised price.  
If the sale at the appraised price fails, the price may be reduced for sale, but the minimum sale price shall not be less than one-half of the appraised price.  
If no one is willing to purchase the property for sale, Article 19 of these Provisions shall apply to deliver the property to the applying enforcement party or other enforcement creditor in satisfaction of the debt; if the applying enforcement party or other enforcement creditor refuses to accept it or it cannot be delivered to them in satisfaction of the debt according to law, the People's Court shall release the seal and seizure and return the property to the person subject to enforcement.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本规定自2005年1月1日起施行。施行前本院公布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 36** These Provisions shall come into force as of January 1, 2005. If any judicial interpretations promulgated by this Court before the coming into force of these Provisions are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
