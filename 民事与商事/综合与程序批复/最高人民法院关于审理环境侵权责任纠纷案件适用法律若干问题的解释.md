# 最高人民法院关于审理环境侵权责任纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：2015-02-09

> 第1644次会议通过，根据2020年12月23日最高人民法院 审判委员会第1823次会议通过的《最高人民法院关于修改 〈最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国 工会法》若干问题的解释〉等二十七件民事类司法解释的决定》修正）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　因污染环境、破坏生态造成他人损害，不论侵权人有无过错，侵权人应当承担侵权责任。  
侵权人以排污符合国家或者地方污染物排放标准为由主张不承担责任的，人民法院不予支持。  
侵权人不承担责任或者减轻责任的情形，适用海洋环境保护法、水污染防治法、大气污染防治法等环境保护单行法的规定；相关环境保护单行法没有规定的，适用民法典的规定。

**Article 1** Where a tortfeasor causes damage to another person by polluting the environment or damaging the ecology, regardless of whether the tortfeasor is at fault, the tortfeasor shall bear tort liability.  
If the tortfeasor claims not to bear liability on the ground that the discharge of pollutants complies with national or local pollutant discharge standards, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
The circumstances under which the tortfeasor does not bear liability or bears reduced liability shall be governed by the provisions of the Marine Environmental Protection Law, the Law on the Prevention and Control of Water Pollution, the Law on the Prevention and Control of Atmospheric Pollution, and other separate environmental protection laws; if the relevant separate environmental protection laws do not provide for such circumstances, the provisions of the Civil Code shall apply.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　两个以上侵权人共同实施污染环境、破坏生态行为造成损害，被侵权人根据[民法典第一千一百六十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1168)规定请求侵权人承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 2** Where two or more tortfeasors jointly commit acts of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology causing damage, and the injured party requests the tortfeasors to bear joint and several liability in accordance with Article 1168 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　两个以上侵权人分别实施污染环境、破坏生态行为造成同一损害，每一个侵权人的污染环境、破坏生态行为都足以造成全部损害，被侵权人根据[民法典第一千一百七十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1171)规定请求侵权人承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
两个以上侵权人分别实施污染环境、破坏生态行为造成同一损害，每一个侵权人的污染环境、破坏生态行为都不足以造成全部损害，被侵权人根据[民法典第一千一百七十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1172)规定请求侵权人承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
两个以上侵权人分别实施污染环境、破坏生态行为造成同一损害，部分侵权人的污染环境、破坏生态行为足以造成全部损害，部分侵权人的污染环境、破坏生态行为只造成部分损害，被侵权人根据[民法典第一千一百七十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1171)规定请求足以造成全部损害的侵权人与其他侵权人就共同造成的损害部分承担连带责任，并对全部损害承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 3** Where two or more tortfeasors separately commit acts of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology causing the same damage, and each tortfeasor's act is sufficient to cause all the damage, the People's Court shall support the injured party's request that the tortfeasors bear joint and several liability in accordance with Article 1171 of the Civil Code.  
Where two or more tortfeasors separately commit acts of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology causing the same damage, and none of the tortfeasors' acts is sufficient to cause all the damage, the People's Court shall support the injured party's request that the tortfeasors bear liability in accordance with Article 1172 of the Civil Code.  
Where two or more tortfeasors separately commit acts of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology causing the same damage, and some tortfeasors' acts are sufficient to cause all the damage while others' acts only cause part of the damage, the People's Court shall support the injured party's request that the tortfeasors whose acts are sufficient to cause all the damage bear joint and several liability with the other tortfeasors for the part of damage jointly caused, and bear liability for all the damage, in accordance with Article 1171 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　两个以上侵权人污染环境、破坏生态，对侵权人承担责任的大小，人民法院应当根据污染物的种类、浓度、排放量、危害性，有无排污许可证、是否超过污染物排放标准、是否超过重点污染物排放总量控制指标，破坏生态的方式、范围、程度，以及行为对损害后果所起的作用等因素确定。

**Article 4** Where two or more tortfeasors pollute the environment or damage the ecology, the People's Court shall determine the extent of liability to be borne by each tortfeasor based on factors such as the type, concentration, discharge amount, and harmfulness of the pollutants, whether there is a pollutant discharge permit, whether the pollutant discharge standards are exceeded, whether the total discharge control indicators for key pollutants are exceeded, the manner, scope, and degree of ecological damage, and the role of each act in causing the damage.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　被侵权人根据[民法典第一千二百三十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1233)规定分别或者同时起诉侵权人、第三人的，人民法院应予受理。  
被侵权人请求第三人承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应当根据第三人的过错程度确定其相应赔偿责任。  
侵权人以第三人的过错污染环境、破坏生态造成损害为由主张不承担责任或者减轻责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 5** Where the injured party separately or simultaneously sues the tortfeasor and a third party in accordance with Article 1233 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
Where the injured party requests the third party to bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall determine the corresponding compensation liability of the third party based on the degree of fault of the third party.  
If the tortfeasor claims not to bear liability or to bear reduced liability on the ground that the damage was caused by the fault of a third party in polluting the environment or damaging the ecology, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　被侵权人根据民法典第七编第七章的规定请求赔偿的，应当提供证明以下事实的证据材料：  
（一）侵权人排放了污染物或者破坏了生态；  
（二）被侵权人的损害；  
（三）侵权人排放的污染物或者其次生污染物、破坏生态行为与损害之间具有关联性。

**Article 6** Where the injured party requests compensation in accordance with the provisions of Chapter VII of Part VII of the Civil Code, it shall provide evidentiary materials proving the following facts:  
(1) The tortfeasor discharged pollutants or damaged the ecology;  
(2) The damage suffered by the injured party;  
(3) There is a correlation between the pollutants discharged by the tortfeasor or their secondary pollutants, or the act of damaging the ecology, and the damage.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　侵权人举证证明下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定其污染环境、破坏生态行为与损害之间不存在因果关系：  
（一）排放污染物、破坏生态的行为没有造成该损害可能的；  
（二）排放的可造成该损害的污染物未到达该损害发生地的；  
（三）该损害于排放污染物、破坏生态行为实施之前已发生的；  
（四）其他可以认定污染环境、破坏生态行为与损害之间不存在因果关系的情形。

**Article 7** Where the tortfeasor provides evidence proving any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall find that there is no causal relationship between the tortfeasor's act of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology and the damage:  
(1) The act of discharging pollutants or damaging the ecology could not have caused the damage;  
(2) The pollutants discharged that could cause the damage did not reach the place where the damage occurred;  
(3) The damage had already occurred before the act of discharging pollutants or damaging the ecology was carried out;  
(4) Other circumstances under which it can be found that there is no causal relationship between the act of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology and the damage.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对查明环境污染、生态破坏案件事实的专门性问题，可以委托具备相关资格的司法鉴定机构出具鉴定意见或者由负有环境资源保护监督管理职责的部门推荐的机构出具检验报告、检测报告、评估报告或者监测数据。

**Article 8** For specialized issues in ascertaining the facts of a case of environmental pollution or ecological damage, an expert evaluation opinion may be entrusted to a judicial appraisal institution with relevant qualifications, or an inspection report, test report, assessment report, or monitoring data may be issued by an institution recommended by the department responsible for environmental and resource protection supervision and management.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　当事人申请通知一至两名具有专门知识的人出庭，就鉴定意见或者污染物认定、损害结果、因果关系、修复措施等专业问题提出意见的，人民法院可以准许。当事人未申请，人民法院认为有必要的，可以进行释明。  
具有专门知识的人在法庭上提出的意见，经当事人质证，可以作为认定案件事实的根据。

**Article 9** Where a party applies to notify one or two persons with specialized knowledge to appear in court to give opinions on professional issues such as expert evaluation opinions, identification of pollutants, damage results, causal relationship, and remediation measures, the People's Court may permit it. If the party does not apply and the People's Court deems it necessary, it may provide clarification.  
The opinions given by persons with specialized knowledge in court may, after being cross-examined by the parties, serve as the basis for finding the facts of the case.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　负有环境资源保护监督管理职责的部门或者其委托的机构出具的环境污染、生态破坏事件调查报告、检验报告、检测报告、评估报告或者监测数据等，经当事人质证，可以作为认定案件事实的根据。

**Article 10** Investigation reports, inspection reports, test reports, assessment reports, or monitoring data on environmental pollution or ecological damage incidents issued by the department responsible for environmental and resource protection supervision and management or an institution entrusted by it may, after being cross-examined by the parties, serve as the basis for finding the facts of the case.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对于突发性或者持续时间较短的环境污染、生态破坏行为，在证据可能灭失或者以后难以取得的情况下，当事人或者利害关系人根据[民事诉讼法第八十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-81)规定申请证据保全的，人民法院应当准许。

**Article 11** For sudden or short-duration acts of environmental pollution or ecological damage, where evidence may be destroyed or difficult to obtain later, and a party or an interested person applies for preservation of evidence in accordance with Article 81 of the Civil Procedure Law, the People's Court shall permit it.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　被申请人具有[环境保护法第六十三条](../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国环境保护法.md#art-63)规定情形之一，当事人或者利害关系人根据[民事诉讼法第一百条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-100)或者第一百零一条规定申请保全的，人民法院可以裁定责令被申请人立即停止侵害行为或者采取防治措施。

**Article 12** Where the respondent falls under one of the circumstances provided in Article 63 of the Environmental Protection Law, and a party or an interested person applies for preservation in accordance with Article 100 or Article 101 of the Civil Procedure Law, the People's Court may rule to order the respondent to immediately cease the infringing act or take prevention and control measures.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民法院应当根据被侵权人的诉讼请求以及具体案情，合理判定侵权人承担停止侵害、排除妨碍、消除危险、修复生态环境、赔礼道歉、赔偿损失等民事责任。

**Article 13** The People's Court shall, based on the injured party's claims and the specific circumstances of the case, reasonably determine that the tortfeasor bears civil liability such as cessation of infringement, removal of obstruction, elimination of danger, restoration of the ecological environment, apology, and compensation for losses.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　被侵权人请求修复生态环境的，人民法院可以依法裁判侵权人承担环境修复责任，并同时确定其不履行环境修复义务时应当承担的环境修复费用。  
侵权人在生效裁判确定的期限内未履行环境修复义务的，人民法院可以委托其他人进行环境修复，所需费用由侵权人承担。

**Article 14** Where the injured party requests restoration of the ecological environment, the People's Court may, according to law, adjudge the tortfeasor to bear the responsibility for environmental restoration and at the same time determine the environmental restoration costs to be borne by the tortfeasor if it fails to perform the environmental restoration obligation.  
If the tortfeasor fails to perform the environmental restoration obligation within the period specified in the effective judgment, the People's Court may entrust another person to carry out the environmental restoration, and the necessary costs shall be borne by the tortfeasor.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　被侵权人起诉请求侵权人赔偿因污染环境、破坏生态造成的财产损失、人身损害以及为防止损害发生和扩大、清除污染、修复生态环境而采取必要措施所支出的合理费用的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 15** Where the injured party sues requesting the tortfeasor to compensate for property losses, personal injury, and reasonable expenses incurred in taking necessary measures to prevent the occurrence and expansion of damage, to eliminate pollution, and to restore the ecological environment caused by the pollution of the environment or damage to the ecology, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　下列情形之一，应当认定为[环境保护法第六十五条](../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国环境保护法.md#art-65)规定的弄虚作假：  
（一）环境影响评价机构明知委托人提供的材料虚假而出具严重失实的评价文件的；  
（二）环境监测机构或者从事环境监测设备维护、运营的机构故意隐瞒委托人超过污染物排放标准或者超过重点污染物排放总量控制指标的事实的；  
（三）从事防治污染设施维护、运营的机构故意不运行或者不正常运行环境监测设备或者防治污染设施的；  
（四）有关机构在环境服务活动中其他弄虚作假的情形。

**Article 16** Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as falsification as provided in Article 65 of the Environmental Protection Law:  
(1) An environmental impact assessment institution knowingly provides a seriously false assessment document based on false materials provided by the client;  
(2) An environmental monitoring institution or an institution engaged in the maintenance or operation of environmental monitoring equipment intentionally conceals the fact that the client exceeds the pollutant discharge standards or exceeds the total discharge control indicators for key pollutants;  
(3) An institution engaged in the maintenance or operation of pollution prevention facilities intentionally fails to operate or improperly operates environmental monitoring equipment or pollution prevention facilities;  
(4) Other acts of falsification by relevant institutions in environmental service activities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本解释适用于审理因污染环境、破坏生态造成损害的民事案件，但法律和司法解释对环境民事公益诉讼案件另有规定的除外。  
相邻污染侵害纠纷、劳动者在职业活动中因受污染损害发生的纠纷，不适用本解释。

**Article 17** This Interpretation shall apply to the trial of civil cases concerning damage caused by pollution of the environment or damage to the ecology, except where laws and judicial interpretations otherwise provide for environmental civil public interest litigation cases.  
Disputes over infringement by adjacent pollution and disputes arising from damage suffered by workers due to pollution in the course of their occupational activities shall not be governed by this Interpretation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本解释施行后，人民法院尚未审结的一审、二审案件适用本解释规定。本解释施行前已经作出生效裁判的案件，本解释施行后依法再审的，不适用本解释。  
本解释施行后，最高人民法院以前颁布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，不再适用。

**Article 18** After this Interpretation comes into force, cases of first instance and second instance that have not yet been concluded by the People's Court shall apply the provisions of this Interpretation. For cases where an effective judgment has been rendered before this Interpretation comes into force, if a retrial is conducted according to law after this Interpretation comes into force, this Interpretation shall not apply.  
After this Interpretation comes into force, any previous judicial interpretations issued by the Supreme People's Court that are inconsistent with this Interpretation shall no longer apply.
