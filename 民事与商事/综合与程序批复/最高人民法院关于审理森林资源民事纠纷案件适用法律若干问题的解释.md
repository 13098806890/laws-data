# 最高人民法院关于审理森林资源民事纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕16号  
**公布日期**：2022-04-25  
**生效日期**：2022-06-15

> 《最高人民法院关于审理森林资源民事纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2022年4月25日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1869次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年6月15日起施行。 2022年6月13日 最高人民法院 关于审理森林资源民事纠纷案件适用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2022〕16号 为妥善审理森林资源民事纠纷案件，依法保护生态环境和当事人合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国环境保护法》《中华人民共和国森林法》《中华人民共和国农村土地承包法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条　人民法院审理涉及森林、林木、林地等森林资源的民事纠纷案件，应当贯彻民法典绿色原则，尊重自然、尊重历史、尊重习惯，依法推动森林资源保护和利用的生态效益、经济效益、社会效益相统一，促进人与自然和谐共生。 第二条　当事人因下列行为，对林地、林木的物权归属、内容产生争议，依据民法典第二百三十四条的规定提起民事诉讼，请求确认权利的，人民法院应当依法受理： （一）林地承包； （二）林地承包经营权互换、转让； （三）林地经营权流转； （四）林木流转； （五）林地、林木担保； （六）林地、林木继承；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审理涉及森林、林木、林地等森林资源的民事纠纷案件，应当贯彻民法典绿色原则，尊重自然、尊重历史、尊重习惯，依法推动森林资源保护和利用的生态效益、经济效益、社会效益相统一，促进人与自然和谐共生。

**Article 1** In hearing civil dispute cases involving forest resources such as forests, trees, and forest land, the People's Court shall implement the green principle of the Civil Code, respect nature, respect history, respect customs, and promote the unification of ecological, economic, and social benefits of forest resource protection and utilization according to law, so as to promote harmonious coexistence between humans and nature.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人因下列行为，对林地、林木的物权归属、内容产生争议，依据[民法典第二百三十四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-234)的规定提起民事诉讼，请求确认权利的，人民法院应当依法受理：  
（一）林地承包；  
（二）林地承包经营权互换、转让；  
（三）林地经营权流转；  
（四）林木流转；  
（五）林地、林木担保；  
（六）林地、林木继承；  
（七）其他引起林地、林木物权变动的行为。  
当事人因对行政机关作出的林地、林木确权、登记行为产生争议，提起民事诉讼的，人民法院告知其依法通过行政复议、行政诉讼程序解决。

**Article 2** Where a party has a dispute over the ownership or content of real rights to forest land or trees due to any of the following acts and institutes a civil action according to law pursuant to Article 234 of the Civil Code to request confirmation of rights, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law:  
(1) forest land contracting;  
(2) exchange or transfer of the right to operate contracted forest land;  
(3) transfer of the right to operate forest land;  
(4) transfer of trees;  
(5) security over forest land or trees;  
(6) succession of forest land or trees; and  
(7) other acts causing changes in real rights to forest land or trees.  
Where a party has a dispute over the confirmation or registration of forest land or trees by an administrative organ and institutes a civil action, the People's Court shall inform the party to resolve the dispute through administrative reconsideration or administrative action according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　当事人以未办理批准、登记、备案、审查、审核等手续为由，主张林地承包、林地承包经营权互换或者转让、林地经营权流转、林木流转、森林资源担保等合同无效的，人民法院不予支持。  
因前款原因，不能取得相关权利的当事人请求解除合同、由违约方承担违约责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 3** Where a party claims that a contract for forest land contracting, exchange or transfer of the right to operate contracted forest land, transfer of the right to operate forest land, transfer of trees, or security over forest resources is void on the ground that the formalities of approval, registration, filing, review, or examination have not been completed, the People's Court shall not support the claim.  
Where, due to the reason in the preceding paragraph, a party who is unable to obtain the relevant rights requests dissolution of the contract and that the breaching party bear liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support the claim according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　当事人一方未依法经林权证等权利证书载明的共有人同意，擅自处分林地、林木，另一方主张取得相关权利的，人民法院不予支持。但符合[民法典第三百一十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-311)关于善意取得规定的除外。

**Article 4** Where one party, without the consent of the co-owners indicated in the forest right certificate or other right certificate according to law, disposes of forest land or trees without authorization, and the other party claims to acquire the relevant rights, the People's Court shall not support the claim, unless the provisions of Article 311 of the Civil Code on bona fide acquisition are met.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　当事人以违反法律规定的民主议定程序为由，主张集体林地承包合同无效的，人民法院应予支持。但下列情形除外：  
（一）合同订立时，法律、行政法规没有关于民主议定程序的强制性规定的；  
（二）合同订立未经民主议定程序讨论决定，或者民主议定程序存在瑕疵，一审法庭辩论终结前已经依法补正的；  
（三）承包方对村民会议或者村民代表会议决议进行了合理审查，不知道且不应当知道决议系伪造、变造，并已经对林地大量投入的。

**Article 5** Where a party claims that a collective forest land contracting contract is void on the ground that it violates the democratic deliberation procedures prescribed by law, the People's Court shall support the claim, except in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) at the time of conclusion of the contract, there were no mandatory provisions of laws or administrative regulations on democratic deliberation procedures;  
(2) the conclusion of the contract was not decided through democratic deliberation procedures, or the democratic deliberation procedures had defects, but the defects were lawfully cured before the conclusion of the court debate at first instance; or  
(3) the contractor reasonably examined the resolutions of the villagers' meeting or villagers' representative meeting, did not know and should not have known that the resolutions were forged or altered, and has made substantial investments in the forest land.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　家庭承包林地的承包方转让林地承包经营权未经发包方同意，或者受让方不是本集体经济组织成员，受让方主张取得林地承包经营权的，人民法院不予支持。但发包方无法定理由不同意或者拖延表态的除外。

**Article 6** Where the contractor of a family-contracted forest land transfers the right to operate contracted forest land without the consent of the party that contracted out the land, or the transferee is not a member of the collective economic organization, and the transferee claims to acquire the right to operate contracted forest land, the People's Court shall not support the claim, unless the party that contracted out the land has no legal reason to withhold consent or delays in making a statement.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　当事人就同一集体林地订立多个经营权流转合同，在合同有效的情况下，受让方均主张取得林地经营权的，由具有下列情形的受让方取得：  
（一）林地经营权已经依法登记的；  
（二）林地经营权均未依法登记，争议发生前已经合法占有使用林地并大量投入的；  
（三）无前两项规定情形，合同生效在先的。  
未取得林地经营权的一方请求解除合同、由违约方承担违约责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 7** Where parties conclude multiple contracts for the transfer of the right to operate the same collective forest land, and the contracts are valid, and the transferees all claim to acquire the right to operate forest land, the transferee that meets any of the following conditions shall acquire the right:  
(1) the right to operate forest land has been registered according to law;  
(2) none of the rights to operate forest land have been registered according to law, but the transferee has lawfully taken possession and used the forest land and made substantial investments before the dispute arose; or  
(3) in the absence of the circumstances specified in the preceding two items, the contract that took effect earlier.  
Where a party who failed to acquire the right to operate forest land requests dissolution of the contract and that the breaching party bear liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support the claim according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　家庭承包林地的承包方以林地经营权人擅自再流转林地经营权为由，请求解除林地经营权流转合同、收回林地的，人民法院应予支持。但林地经营权人能够证明林地经营权再流转已经承包方书面同意的除外。

**Article 8** Where the contractor of a family-contracted forest land, on the ground that the holder of the right to operate forest land has, without authorization, retransferred the right to operate forest land, requests dissolution of the contract for the transfer of the right to operate forest land and recovery of the forest land, the People's Court shall support the request, unless the holder of the right to operate forest land can prove that the retransfer of the right to operate forest land was consented to in writing by the contractor.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　本集体经济组织成员以其在同等条件下享有的优先权受到侵害为由，主张家庭承包林地经营权流转合同无效的，人民法院不予支持；其请求赔偿损失的，依法予以支持。

**Article 9** Where a member of the collective economic organization claims that a contract for the transfer of the right to operate a family-contracted forest land is void on the ground that his or her preemptive right under the same conditions has been infringed, the People's Court shall not support the claim; where the member requests compensation for losses, the People's Court shall support the request according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　林地承包期内，因林地承包经营权互换、转让、继承等原因，承包方发生变动，林地经营权人请求新的承包方继续履行原林地经营权流转合同的，人民法院应予支持。但当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 10** During the period of forest land contracting, where the contractor changes due to exchange, transfer, or succession of the right to operate contracted forest land, and the holder of the right to operate forest land requests the new contractor to continue performing the original contract for the transfer of the right to operate forest land, the People's Court shall support the request, unless the parties have agreed otherwise.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　林地经营权流转合同约定的流转期限超过承包期的剩余期限，或者林地经营权再流转合同约定的流转期限超过原林地经营权流转合同的剩余期限，林地经营权流转、再流转合同当事人主张超过部分无效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 11** Where the term of transfer agreed in a contract for the transfer of the right to operate forest land exceeds the remaining term of the contracting period, or the term of retransfer agreed in a contract for the retransfer of the right to operate forest land exceeds the remaining term of the original contract for the transfer of the right to operate forest land, and the parties to the contract for the transfer or retransfer claim that the exceeding part is void, the People's Court shall not support the claim.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　林地经营权流转合同约定的流转期限超过承包期的剩余期限，发包方主张超过部分的约定对其不具有法律约束力的，人民法院应予支持。但发包方对此知道或者应当知道的除外。  
林地经营权再流转合同约定的流转期限超过原林地经营权流转合同的剩余期限，承包方主张超过部分的约定对其不具有法律约束力的，人民法院应予支持。但承包方对此知道或者应当知道的除外。  
因前两款原因，致使林地经营权流转合同、再流转合同不能履行，当事人请求解除合同、由违约方承担违约责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 12** Where the term of transfer agreed in a contract for the transfer of the right to operate forest land exceeds the remaining term of the contracting period, and the party that contracted out the land claims that the exceeding part of the agreement is not legally binding on it, the People's Court shall support the claim, unless the party that contracted out the land knew or should have known of such excess.  
Where the term of retransfer agreed in a contract for the retransfer of the right to operate forest land exceeds the remaining term of the original contract for the transfer of the right to operate forest land, and the contractor claims that the exceeding part of the agreement is not legally binding on it, the People's Court shall support the claim, unless the contractor knew or should have known of such excess.  
Where, due to the reasons in the preceding two paragraphs, the contract for the transfer or retransfer of the right to operate forest land cannot be performed, and a party requests dissolution of the contract and that the breaching party bear liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support the claim according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　林地经营权流转合同终止时，对于林地经营权人种植的地上林木，按照下列情形处理：  
（一）合同有约定的，按照约定处理，但该约定依据[民法典第一百五十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-153)的规定应当认定无效的除外；  
（二）合同没有约定或者约定不明，当事人协商一致延长合同期限至轮伐期或者其他合理期限届满，承包方请求由林地经营权人承担林地使用费的，对其合理部分予以支持；  
（三）合同没有约定或者约定不明，当事人未能就延长合同期限协商一致，林地经营权人请求对林木价值进行补偿的，对其合理部分予以支持。  
林地承包合同终止时，承包方种植的地上林木的处理，参照适用前款规定。

**Article 13** Where a contract for the transfer of the right to operate forest land is terminated, the above-ground trees planted by the holder of the right to operate forest land shall be handled according to the following circumstances:  
(1) if the contract has an agreement, it shall be handled according to the agreement, unless the agreement shall be found void pursuant to Article 153 of the Civil Code;  
(2) if the contract has no agreement or the agreement is unclear, and the parties reach a consensus to extend the contract term until the end of the rotation period or another reasonable period, and the contractor requests that the holder of the right to operate forest land bear the forest land use fee, the reasonable part of the request shall be supported; or  
(3) if the contract has no agreement or the agreement is unclear, and the parties fail to reach a consensus on extending the contract term, and the holder of the right to operate forest land requests compensation for the value of the trees, the reasonable part of the request shall be supported.  
Where a forest land contracting contract is terminated, the handling of the above-ground trees planted by the contractor shall be governed by mutatis mutandis the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院对于当事人为利用公益林林地资源和森林景观资源开展林下经济、森林旅游、森林康养等经营活动订立的合同，应当综合考虑公益林生态区位保护要求、公益林生态功能及是否经科学论证的合理利用等因素，依法认定合同效力。  
当事人仅以涉公益林为由主张经营合同无效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 14** In determining the validity of a contract concluded by a party for using public welfare forest land resources and forest landscape resources to carry out business activities such as under-forest economy, forest tourism, and forest health care, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider factors such as the ecological location protection requirements of the public welfare forest, the ecological functions of the public welfare forest, and whether the utilization is scientifically justified, and shall determine the validity of the contract according to law.  
Where a party merely claims that a business contract is void on the ground that it involves a public welfare forest, the People's Court shall not support the claim.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　以林地经营权、林木所有权等法律、行政法规未禁止抵押的森林资源资产设定抵押，债务人不履行到期债务或者发生当事人约定的实现抵押权的情形，抵押权人与抵押人协议以抵押的森林资源资产折价，并据此请求接管经营抵押财产的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
抵押权人与抵押人未就森林资源资产抵押权的实现方式达成协议，抵押权人依据[民事诉讼法第二百零三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-203)、第二百零四条的规定申请实现抵押权的，人民法院依法裁定拍卖、变卖抵押财产。

**Article 15** Where a mortgage is created over forest resources assets such as the right to operate forest land or ownership of trees, which are not prohibited by laws or administrative regulations from being mortgaged, and the debtor fails to perform the due obligation or the circumstances agreed by the parties for the enforcement of the mortgage occur, and the mortgagee and the mortgagor agree to discount the mortgaged forest resources assets and accordingly request to take over and manage the mortgaged property, the People's Court shall support the request according to law.  
Where the mortgagee and the mortgagor fail to reach an agreement on the method of enforcing the mortgage over forest resources assets, and the mortgagee applies for enforcement of the mortgage pursuant to Articles 203 and 204 of the Civil Procedure Law, the People's Court shall rule to auction or sell the mortgaged property.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　以森林生态效益补偿收益、林业碳汇等提供担保，债务人不履行到期债务或者发生当事人约定的实现担保物权的情形，担保物权人请求就担保财产优先受偿的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 16** Where a security is provided over forest ecological benefit compensation income, forestry carbon sinks, etc., and the debtor fails to perform the due obligation or the circumstances agreed by the parties for the enforcement of the security interest occur, and the security interest holder requests priority to be satisfied from the security property, the People's Court shall support the request according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　违反国家规定造成森林生态环境损害，生态环境能够修复的，国家规定的机关或者法律规定的组织依据[民法典第一千二百三十四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1234)的规定，请求侵权人在合理期限内以补种树木、恢复植被、恢复林地土壤性状、投放相应生物种群等方式承担修复责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
人民法院判决侵权人承担修复责任的，可以同时确定其在期限内不履行修复义务时应承担的森林生态环境修复费用。

**Article 17** Where damage to the forest ecological environment is caused in violation of national provisions, and the ecological environment is capable of restoration, and the authority designated by the State or the organization prescribed by law, pursuant to Article 1234 of the Civil Code, requests the infringer to bear the liability for restoration within a reasonable period by means such as replanting trees, restoring vegetation, restoring soil properties of forest land, or introducing corresponding biological populations, the People's Court shall support the request according to law.  
Where the People's Court adjudicates that the infringer shall bear the liability for ecological restoration, it may also determine the costs for forest ecological environment restoration to be borne by the infringer if the infringer fails to perform the restoration obligation within the specified period.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法院判决侵权人承担森林生态环境修复责任的，可以根据鉴定意见，或者参考林业主管部门、林业调查规划设计单位、相关科研机构和人员出具的专业意见，合理确定森林生态环境修复方案，明确侵权人履行修复义务的具体要求。

**Article 18** Where the People's Court adjudicates that the infringer shall bear the liability for forest ecological environment restoration, it may, based on expert evaluation opinions or with reference to professional opinions issued by forestry authorities, forestry survey and planning and design units, relevant scientific research institutions and personnel, reasonably determine the forest ecological environment restoration plan and clarify the specific requirements for the infringer to perform the restoration obligation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　人民法院依据[民法典第一千二百三十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1235)的规定确定侵权人承担的森林生态环境损害赔偿金额，应当综合考虑受损森林资源在调节气候、固碳增汇、保护生物多样性、涵养水源、保持水土、防风固沙等方面的生态环境服务功能，予以合理认定。

**Article 19** Where the People's Court, pursuant to Article 1235 of the Civil Code, determines the amount of compensation for forest ecological environmental damage to be borne by the infringer, it shall comprehensively consider the ecological environmental service functions of the damaged forest resources in terms of climate regulation, carbon sequestration and sink enhancement, biodiversity protection, water conservation, soil and water conservation, windbreak and sand fixation, etc., and shall make a reasonable determination.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　当事人请求以认购经核证的林业碳汇方式替代履行森林生态环境损害赔偿责任的，人民法院可以综合考虑各方当事人意见、不同责任方式的合理性等因素，依法予以准许。

**Article 20** Where a party requests to substitute the performance of the liability for forest ecological environment damage compensation by means of purchasing certified forestry carbon sinks, the People's Court may, after comprehensively considering the opinions of all parties, the reasonableness of different methods of bearing liability, and other factors, grant approval according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　当事人请求以森林管护、野生动植物保护、社区服务等劳务方式替代履行森林生态环境损害赔偿责任的，人民法院可以综合考虑侵权人的代偿意愿、经济能力、劳动能力、赔偿金额、当地相应工资标准等因素，决定是否予以准许，并合理确定劳务代偿方案。

**Article 21** Where a party requests to substitute the performance of the liability for forest ecological environment damage compensation by means of labor such as forest management and protection, wildlife conservation, or community service, the People's Court may, after comprehensively considering factors such as the infringer's willingness to substitute, economic capacity, labor capacity, amount of compensation, and local applicable wage standards, decide whether to grant approval, and shall reasonably determine a labor substitution plan.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　侵权人自愿交纳保证金作为履行森林生态环境修复义务担保的，在其不履行修复义务时，人民法院可以将保证金用于支付森林生态环境修复费用。

**Article 22** Where the infringer voluntarily pays a deposit as a guarantee for the performance of the forest ecological environment restoration obligation, and the infringer fails to perform the restoration obligation, the People's Court may use the deposit to pay the forest ecological environment restoration costs.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本解释自2022年6月15日起施行。施行前本院公布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 23** This Interpretation shall come into force as of June 15, 2022. Where any judicial interpretation previously promulgated by this Court is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
