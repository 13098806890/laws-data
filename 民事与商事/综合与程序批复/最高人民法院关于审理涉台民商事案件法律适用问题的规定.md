### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审理涉台民商事案件，应当适用法律和司法解释的有关规定。  
根据法律和司法解释中选择适用法律的规则，确定适用台湾地区民事法律的，人民法院予以适用。

**Article 1** The People's Court shall, when hearing civil and commercial cases involving Taiwan, apply the relevant provisions of laws and judicial interpretations.  
Where, in accordance with the rules on choice of applicable law in laws and judicial interpretations, it is determined that the civil law of the Taiwan region shall apply, the People's Court shall apply it.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　台湾地区当事人在人民法院参与民事诉讼，与大陆当事人有同等的诉讼权利和义务，其合法权益受法律平等保护。

**Article 2** A party from the Taiwan region participating in a civil action in the People's Court shall have equal litigation rights and obligations as a party from the mainland, and their lawful rights and interests shall be equally protected by law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　根据本规定确定适用有关法律违反国家法律的基本原则或者社会公共利益的，不予适用。

**Article 3** Where the application of a relevant law determined in accordance with these Provisions would violate the fundamental principles of national laws or the public interest of society, it shall not be applied.
