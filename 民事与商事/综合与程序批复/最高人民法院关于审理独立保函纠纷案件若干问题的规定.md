# 最高人民法院关于审理独立保函纠纷案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2016〕24号  
**公布日期**：2016-11-18  
**生效日期**：2016-12-01

> 最高人民法院《关于审理独立保函纠纷案件若干问题的规定》已于2016年7月11日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1688次会议通过，现予公布，自2016年12月1日起施行。 2016年11月18日 最高人民法院 关于审理独立保函纠纷案件若干问题的规定 法释〔2016〕24号 为正确审理独立保函纠纷案件，切实维护当事人的合法权益，服务和保障“一带一路”建设，促进对外开放，根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》《中华人民共和国合同法》《中华人民共和国担保法》《中华人民共和国涉外民事关系法律适用法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律，结合审判实际，制定本规定： 第一条　本规定所称的独立保函，是指银行或非银行金融机构作为开立人，以书面形式向受益人出具的，同意在受益人请求付款并提交符合保函要求的单据时，向其支付特定款项或在保函最高金额内付款的承诺。 前款所称的单据，是指独立保函载明的受益人应提交的付款请求书、违约声明、第三方签发的文件、法院判决、仲裁裁决、汇票、发票等表明发生付款到期事件的书面文件。 独立保函可以依保函申请人的申请而开立，也可以依另一金融机构的指示而开立。开立人依指示开立独立保函的，可以要求指示人向其开立用以保障追偿权的独立保函。 第二条　本规定所称的独立保函纠纷，是指在独立保函的开立、撤销、修改、转让、付款、追偿等环节产生的纠纷。 第三条　保函具有下列情形之一，当事人主张保函性质为独立保函的，人民法院应予支持，但保函未载明据以付款的单据和最高金额的除外： （一）保函载明见索即付； （二）保函载明适用国际商会《见索即付保函统一规则》等独立保函交易示范规则； （三）根据保函文本内容，开立人的付款义务独立于基础交易关系及保函申请法律关系，其仅承担相符交单的付款责任。 当事人以独立保函记载了对应的基础交易为由，主张该保函性质为一般保证或连带保证的，人民法院不予支持。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条    本规定所称的独立保函，是指银行或非银行金融机构作为开立人，以书面形式向受益人出具的，同意在受益人请求付款并提交符合保函要求的单据时，向其支付特定款项或在保函最高金额内付款的承诺。  
前款所称的单据，是指独立保函载明的受益人应提交的付款请求书、违约声明、第三方签发的文件、法院判决、仲裁裁决、汇票、发票等表明发生付款到期事件的书面文件。  
独立保函可以依保函申请人的申请而开立，也可以依另一金融机构的指示而开立。开立人依指示开立独立保函的，可以要求指示人向其开立用以保障追偿权的独立保函。

**Article 1** For the purposes of these Provisions, an independent guarantee refers to a written undertaking issued by a bank or non-bank financial institution as the issuer to the beneficiary, agreeing to pay a specified amount or an amount within the maximum amount of the guarantee to the beneficiary upon the beneficiary's request for payment and submission of documents that comply with the requirements of the guarantee.  
The documents referred to in the preceding paragraph mean the written documents specified in the independent guarantee that the beneficiary shall submit, such as a demand for payment, a statement of default, a document issued by a third party, a court judgment, an arbitral award, a bill of exchange, an invoice, etc., which indicate the occurrence of a payment due event.  
An independent guarantee may be issued upon the application of the applicant for the guarantee, or upon the instruction of another financial institution. Where the issuer issues an independent guarantee upon instruction, it may request the instructing party to issue to it an independent guarantee to secure its right of recourse.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条    本规定所称的独立保函纠纷，是指在独立保函的开立、撤销、修改、转让、付款、追偿等环节产生的纠纷。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Provisions, a dispute over an independent guarantee refers to a dispute arising from the issuance, revocation, amendment, transfer, payment, recourse, or other aspects of an independent guarantee.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条    保函具有下列情形之一，当事人主张保函性质为独立保函的，人民法院应予支持，但保函未载明据以付款的单据和最高金额的除外：  
（一）保函载明见索即付；  
（二）保函载明适用国际商会《见索即付保函统一规则》等独立保函交易示范规则；  
（三）根据保函文本内容，开立人的付款义务独立于基础交易关系及保函申请法律关系，其仅承担相符交单的付款责任。  
当事人以独立保函记载了对应的基础交易为由，主张该保函性质为一般保证或连带保证的，人民法院不予支持。  
当事人主张独立保函适用担保法关于一般保证或连带保证规定的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 3** Where a guarantee has any of the following circumstances, and the party claims that the nature of the guarantee is an independent guarantee, the People's Court shall support such claim, except where the guarantee does not specify the documents for payment and the maximum amount:  
(1) The guarantee specifies that it is payable upon first demand;  
(2) The guarantee specifies the application of the Uniform Rules for Demand Guarantees of the International Chamber of Commerce or other model rules for independent guarantee transactions;  
(3) According to the content of the guarantee text, the issuer's obligation to pay is independent of the underlying transaction relationship and the legal relationship of the guarantee application, and the issuer only bears the payment obligation upon presentation of conforming documents.  
Where a party, on the ground that the independent guarantee records the corresponding underlying transaction, claims that the nature of the guarantee is a general suretyship or joint and several suretyship, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
Where a party claims that the independent guarantee is subject to the provisions of the Security Law regarding general suretyship or joint and several suretyship, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条    独立保函的开立时间为开立人发出独立保函的时间。  
独立保函一经开立即生效，但独立保函载明生效日期或事件的除外。  
独立保函未载明可撤销，当事人主张独立保函开立后不可撤销的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 4** The time of issuance of an independent guarantee shall be the time when the issuer sends the independent guarantee.  
An independent guarantee shall take effect upon issuance, except where an effective date or event is specified in the independent guarantee.  
Where an independent guarantee does not specify that it is revocable, and a party claims that the independent guarantee is irrevocable after issuance, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条    独立保函载明适用《见索即付保函统一规则》等独立保函交易示范规则，或开立人和受益人在一审法庭辩论终结前一致援引的，人民法院应当认定交易示范规则的内容构成独立保函条款的组成部分。  
不具有前款情形，当事人主张独立保函适用相关交易示范规则的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 5** Where an independent guarantee specifies the application of the Uniform Rules for Demand Guarantees or other model rules for independent guarantee transactions, or the issuer and the beneficiary unanimously cite such rules before the conclusion of the debate at the first instance trial, the People's Court shall determine that the content of the model rules constitutes a part of the terms of the independent guarantee.  
Where the circumstances in the preceding paragraph do not exist, and a party claims that the independent guarantee is subject to the relevant model rules, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条    受益人提交的单据与独立保函条款之间、单据与单据之间表面相符，受益人请求开立人依据独立保函承担付款责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
开立人以基础交易关系或独立保函申请关系对付款义务提出抗辩的，人民法院不予支持，但有[本规定第十二条](#art-12)情形的除外。

**Article 6** Where the documents submitted by the beneficiary are on their face consistent with the terms of the independent guarantee and with each other, and the beneficiary requests the issuer to assume the payment obligation pursuant to the independent guarantee, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the issuer raises a defense against the payment obligation based on the underlying transaction relationship or the independent guarantee application relationship, the People's Court shall not support such defense, except where the circumstances in Article 12 of these Provisions exist.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条    人民法院在认定是否构成表面相符时，应当根据独立保函载明的审单标准进行审查；独立保函未载明的，可以参照适用国际商会确定的相关审单标准。  
单据与独立保函条款之间、单据与单据之间表面上不完全一致，但并不导致相互之间产生歧义的，人民法院应当认定构成表面相符。

**Article 7** When determining whether there is conformity on the face, the People's Court shall review the documents in accordance with the examination standards specified in the independent guarantee; where the independent guarantee does not specify such standards, it may, by reference, apply the relevant examination standards established by the International Chamber of Commerce.  
Where documents are not completely consistent on their face with the terms of the independent guarantee or with each other, but do not cause ambiguity between them, the People's Court shall determine that there is conformity on the face.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条    开立人有独立审查单据的权利与义务，有权自行决定单据与独立保函条款之间、单据与单据之间是否表面相符，并自行决定接受或拒绝接受不符点。  
开立人已向受益人明确表示接受不符点，受益人请求开立人承担付款责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
开立人拒绝接受不符点，受益人以保函申请人已接受不符点为由请求开立人承担付款责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 8** The issuer shall have the right and obligation to independently examine the documents, and shall have the right to decide whether the documents are on their face consistent with the terms of the independent guarantee and with each other, and to decide to accept or reject discrepancies.  
Where the issuer has expressly indicated to the beneficiary that it accepts the discrepancies, and the beneficiary requests the issuer to assume the payment obligation, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the issuer refuses to accept discrepancies, and the beneficiary, on the ground that the applicant for the guarantee has accepted the discrepancies, requests the issuer to assume the payment obligation, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条    开立人依据独立保函付款后向保函申请人追偿的，人民法院应予支持，但受益人提交的单据存在不符点的除外。

**Article 9** Where the issuer, after making payment pursuant to the independent guarantee, seeks recourse against the applicant for the guarantee, the People's Court shall support such claim, except where the documents submitted by the beneficiary contain discrepancies.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条    独立保函未同时载明可转让和据以确定新受益人的单据，开立人主张受益人付款请求权的转让对其不发生效力的，人民法院应予支持。独立保函对受益人付款请求权的转让有特别约定的，从其约定。

**Article 10** Where an independent guarantee does not simultaneously specify that it is transferable and the documents for determining the new beneficiary, and the issuer claims that the transfer of the beneficiary's right to demand payment is not effective against it, the People's Court shall support such claim. Where the independent guarantee has special provisions on the transfer of the beneficiary's right to demand payment, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条    独立保函具有下列情形之一，当事人主张独立保函权利义务终止的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）独立保函载明的到期日或到期事件届至，受益人未提交符合独立保函要求的单据；  
（二）独立保函项下的应付款项已经全部支付；  
（三）独立保函的金额已减额至零；  
（四）开立人收到受益人出具的免除独立保函项下付款义务的文件；  
（五）法律规定或者当事人约定终止的其他情形。  
独立保函具有前款权利义务终止的情形，受益人以其持有独立保函文本为由主张享有付款请求权的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 11** Where an independent guarantee has any of the following circumstances, and a party claims that the rights and obligations under the independent guarantee are terminated, the People's Court shall support such claim:  
(1) The expiry date or expiry event specified in the independent guarantee has occurred, and the beneficiary has not submitted documents that comply with the requirements of the independent guarantee;  
(2) The payable amount under the independent guarantee has been fully paid;  
(3) The amount of the independent guarantee has been reduced to zero;  
(4) The issuer has received a document issued by the beneficiary exempting the payment obligation under the independent guarantee;  
(5) Other circumstances under which termination is provided for by law or agreed upon by the parties.  
Where the independent guarantee has the circumstances of termination of rights and obligations in the preceding paragraph, and the beneficiary claims to have the right to demand payment on the ground that it holds the text of the independent guarantee, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条    具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定构成独立保函欺诈：  
（一）受益人与保函申请人或其他人串通，虚构基础交易的；  
（二）受益人提交的第三方单据系伪造或内容虚假的；  
（三）法院判决或仲裁裁决认定基础交易债务人没有付款或赔偿责任的；  
（四）受益人确认基础交易债务已得到完全履行或者确认独立保函载明的付款到期事件并未发生的；  
（五）受益人明知其没有付款请求权仍滥用该权利的其他情形。

**Article 12** Where any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall determine that fraud in the independent guarantee is constituted:  
(1) The beneficiary colludes with the applicant for the guarantee or others to fabricate the underlying transaction;  
(2) The documents submitted by the beneficiary from a third party are forged or their content is false;  
(3) A court judgment or arbitral award determines that the debtor of the underlying transaction has no payment or compensation obligation;  
(4) The beneficiary confirms that the debt of the underlying transaction has been fully performed or that the payment due event specified in the independent guarantee has not occurred;  
(5) Other circumstances where the beneficiary, knowing that it has no right to demand payment, nevertheless abuses such right.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条    独立保函的申请人、开立人或指示人发现有[本规定第十二条](#art-12)情形的，可以在提起诉讼或申请仲裁前，向开立人住所地或其他对独立保函欺诈纠纷案件具有管辖权的人民法院申请中止支付独立保函项下的款项，也可以在诉讼或仲裁过程中提出申请。

**Article 13** Where the applicant, issuer, or instructing party of an independent guarantee discovers the circumstances set forth in Article 12 of these Provisions, it may, before filing a lawsuit or applying for arbitration, apply to the People's Court in the place of the issuer's domicile or another People's Court that has jurisdiction over the dispute of fraud in the independent guarantee for a stay of payment of the amount under the independent guarantee, or may file such an application during the lawsuit or arbitration proceedings.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条    人民法院裁定中止支付独立保函项下的款项，必须同时具备下列条件：  
（一）止付申请人提交的证据材料证明[本规定第十二条](#art-12)情形的存在具有高度可能性；  
（二）情况紧急，不立即采取止付措施，将给止付申请人的合法权益造成难以弥补的损害；  
（三）止付申请人提供了足以弥补被申请人因止付可能遭受损失的担保。  
止付申请人以受益人在基础交易中违约为由请求止付的，人民法院不予支持。  
开立人在依指示开立的独立保函项下已经善意付款的，对保障该开立人追偿权的独立保函，人民法院不得裁定止付。

**Article 14** When the People's Court rules to stay payment of the amount under the independent guarantee, the following conditions must be satisfied simultaneously:  
(1) The evidence submitted by the applicant for the stay proves that the existence of the circumstances set forth in Article 12 of these Provisions is highly probable;  
(2) The situation is urgent, and failure to take immediate stay measures would cause irreparable damage to the lawful rights and interests of the applicant for the stay;  
(3) The applicant for the stay provides security sufficient to compensate for the losses that the respondent may suffer due to the stay.  
Where the applicant for the stay requests the stay on the ground that the beneficiary has breached the underlying transaction, the People's Court shall not support such request.  
Where the issuer has already made payment in good faith under an independent guarantee issued upon instruction, the People's Court shall not rule to stay payment on the independent guarantee that secures the issuer's right of recourse.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条    因止付申请错误造成损失，当事人请求止付申请人赔偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 15** Where losses are caused by an erroneous application for a stay, and a party requests compensation from the applicant for the stay, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条    人民法院受理止付申请后，应当在四十八小时内作出书面裁定。裁定应当列明申请人、被申请人和第三人，并包括初步查明的事实和是否准许止付申请的理由。  
裁定中止支付的，应当立即执行。  
止付申请人在止付裁定作出后三十日内未依法提起独立保函欺诈纠纷诉讼或申请仲裁的，人民法院应当解除止付裁定。

**Article 16** After accepting an application for a stay, the People's Court shall make a written ruling within forty-eight hours. The ruling shall list the applicant, the respondent, and the third party, and shall include the facts initially ascertained and the reasons for whether to grant the application for a stay.  
If the ruling orders a stay of payment, it shall be executed immediately.  
Where the applicant for the stay fails to file a lawsuit for fraud in the independent guarantee or apply for arbitration within thirty days after the ruling on the stay is made, the People's Court shall lift the stay ruling.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条    当事人对人民法院就止付申请作出的裁定有异议的，可以在裁定书送达之日起十日内向作出裁定的人民法院申请复议。复议期间不停止裁定的执行。  
人民法院应当在收到复议申请后十日内审查，并询问当事人。

**Article 17** Where a party has an objection to the ruling made by the People's Court on the application for a stay, it may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court that made the ruling within ten days from the date of service of the ruling. During the period of reconsideration, the execution of the ruling shall not be suspended.  
The People's Court shall review the application for reconsideration within ten days after receiving it, and shall inquire of the parties.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条    人民法院审理独立保函欺诈纠纷案件或处理止付申请，可以就当事人主张的[本规定第十二条](#art-12)的具体情形，审查认定基础交易的相关事实。

**Article 18** When hearing a case concerning a dispute over fraud in an independent guarantee or handling an application for a stay, the People's Court may examine and determine the relevant facts of the underlying transaction with respect to the specific circumstances under Article 12 of these Provisions claimed by the parties.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条    保函申请人在独立保函欺诈诉讼中仅起诉受益人的，独立保函的开立人、指示人可以作为第三人申请参加，或由人民法院通知其参加。

**Article 19** Where the applicant for the guarantee only sues the beneficiary in a lawsuit concerning fraud in an independent guarantee, the issuer and the instructing party of the independent guarantee may apply to participate as a third party, or the People's Court may notify them to participate.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条    人民法院经审理独立保函欺诈纠纷案件，能够排除合理怀疑地认定构成独立保函欺诈，并且不存在[本规定第十四条](#art-14)第三款情形的，应当判决开立人终止支付独立保函项下被请求的款项。

**Article 20** Where, after hearing a case concerning a dispute over fraud in an independent guarantee, the People's Court can determine beyond a reasonable doubt that fraud in the independent guarantee is constituted, and there is no circumstance under paragraph 3 of Article 14 of these Provisions, it shall render a judgment ordering the issuer to terminate payment of the demanded amount under the independent guarantee.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条    受益人和开立人之间因独立保函而产生的纠纷案件，由开立人住所地或被告住所地人民法院管辖，独立保函载明由其他法院管辖或提交仲裁的除外。当事人主张根据基础交易合同争议解决条款确定管辖法院或提交仲裁的，人民法院不予支持。  
独立保函欺诈纠纷案件由被请求止付的独立保函的开立人住所地或被告住所地人民法院管辖，当事人书面协议由其他法院管辖或提交仲裁的除外。当事人主张根据基础交易合同或独立保函的争议解决条款确定管辖法院或提交仲裁的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 21** Dispute cases between a beneficiary and an issuer arising from an independent guarantee shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the issuer is domiciled or the defendant is domiciled, unless the independent guarantee provides that another court has jurisdiction or that the dispute shall be submitted to arbitration. Where a party claims that the competent court or arbitration shall be determined in accordance with the dispute resolution clause of the underlying transaction contract, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
  
Dispute cases concerning fraud in an independent guarantee shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the issuer of the independent guarantee against which payment is requested to be stopped is domiciled or the defendant is domiciled, unless the parties have agreed in writing that another court has jurisdiction or that the dispute shall be submitted to arbitration. Where a party claims that the competent court or arbitration shall be determined in accordance with the dispute resolution clause of the underlying transaction contract or the independent guarantee, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条    涉外独立保函未载明适用法律，开立人和受益人在一审法庭辩论终结前亦未就适用法律达成一致的，开立人和受益人之间因涉外独立保函而产生的纠纷适用开立人经常居所地法律；独立保函由金融机构依法登记设立的分支机构开立的，适用分支机构登记地法律。  
涉外独立保函欺诈纠纷，当事人就适用法律不能达成一致的，适用被请求止付的独立保函的开立人经常居所地法律；独立保函由金融机构依法登记设立的分支机构开立的，适用分支机构登记地法律；当事人有共同经常居所地的，适用共同经常居所地法律。  
涉外独立保函止付保全程序，适用中华人民共和国法律。

**Article 22** Where a foreign-related independent guarantee does not specify the applicable law, and the issuer and the beneficiary fail to reach an agreement on the applicable law before the conclusion of the first-instance court debate, the law of the place of the issuer's habitual residence shall apply to disputes between the issuer and the beneficiary arising from the foreign-related independent guarantee; where the independent guarantee is issued by a branch legally registered and established by a financial institution, the law of the place where the branch is registered shall apply.  
  
Where the parties fail to reach an agreement on the applicable law for a dispute concerning fraud in a foreign-related independent guarantee, the law of the place of the issuer's habitual residence of the independent guarantee against which payment is requested to be stopped shall apply; where the independent guarantee is issued by a branch legally registered and established by a financial institution, the law of the place where the branch is registered shall apply; where the parties have a common habitual residence, the law of the common habitual residence shall apply.  
  
The procedures for the preservation of payment stoppage of a foreign-related independent guarantee shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条    当事人约定在国内交易中适用独立保函，一方当事人以独立保函不具有涉外因素为由，主张保函独立性的约定无效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 23** Where the parties agree to apply an independent guarantee in a domestic transaction, and one party claims that the agreement on the independence of the guarantee is invalid on the ground that the independent guarantee does not have a foreign-related element, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条    对于按照特户管理并移交开立人占有的独立保函开立保证金，人民法院可以采取冻结措施，但不得扣划。保证金账户内的款项丧失开立保证金的功能时，人民法院可以依法采取扣划措施。  
开立人已履行对外支付义务的，根据该开立人的申请，人民法院应当解除对开立保证金相应部分的冻结措施。

**Article 24** For the deposit for issuance of an independent guarantee that is managed under a special account and delivered to the possession of the issuer, the People's Court may adopt freezing measures, but shall not deduct or transfer the funds. When the funds in the deposit account cease to function as a deposit for issuance, the People's Court may adopt deduction or transfer measures according to law.  
  
Where the issuer has fulfilled its external payment obligations, upon application by the issuer, the People's Court shall lift the freezing measures on the corresponding part of the deposit for issuance.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条    本规定施行后尚未终审的案件，适用本规定；本规定施行前已经终审的案件，当事人申请再审或者人民法院按照审判监督程序再审的，不适用本规定。

**Article 25** Cases for which the final judgment has not been rendered after these Provisions come into force shall be governed by these Provisions; cases for which the final judgment has been rendered before these Provisions come into force, where a party applies for retrial or the People's Court conducts a retrial in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, these Provisions shall not apply.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条    本规定自2016年12月1日起施行。

**Article 26** These Provisions shall come into force as of December 1, 2016.

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所称的独立保函，是指银行或非银行金融机构作为开立人，以书面形式向受益人出具的，同意在受益人请求付款并提交符合保函要求的单据时，向其支付特定款项或在保函最高金额内付款的承诺。  
前款所称的单据，是指独立保函载明的受益人应提交的付款请求书、违约声明、第三方签发的文件、法院判决、仲裁裁决、汇票、发票等表明发生付款到期事件的书面文件。  
独立保函可以依保函申请人的申请而开立，也可以依另一金融机构的指示而开立。开立人依指示开立独立保函的，可以要求指示人向其开立用以保障追偿权的独立保函。

**Article 1** For the purposes of these Provisions, an independent guarantee refers to a written undertaking issued by a bank or non-bank financial institution as the issuer to the beneficiary, agreeing to pay a specified amount or an amount within the maximum amount of the guarantee to the beneficiary upon the beneficiary's request for payment and submission of documents that comply with the requirements of the guarantee.  
The documents referred to in the preceding paragraph mean the written documents specified in the independent guarantee that the beneficiary shall submit, such as a demand for payment, a statement of default, a document issued by a third party, a court judgment, an arbitral award, a bill of exchange, an invoice, etc., which indicate the occurrence of a payment due event.  
An independent guarantee may be issued upon the application of the applicant for the guarantee, or upon the instruction of another financial institution. Where the issuer issues an independent guarantee upon instruction, it may request the instructing party to issue to it an independent guarantee to secure its right of recourse.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定所称的独立保函纠纷，是指在独立保函的开立、撤销、修改、转让、付款、追偿等环节产生的纠纷。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Provisions, a dispute over an independent guarantee refers to a dispute arising from the issuance, revocation, amendment, transfer, payment, recourse, or other aspects of an independent guarantee.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　保函具有下列情形之一，当事人主张保函性质为独立保函的，人民法院应予支持，但保函未载明据以付款的单据和最高金额的除外：  
（一）保函载明见索即付；  
（二）保函载明适用国际商会《见索即付保函统一规则》等独立保函交易示范规则；  
（三）根据保函文本内容，开立人的付款义务独立于基础交易关系及保函申请法律关系，其仅承担相符交单的付款责任。  
当事人以独立保函记载了对应的基础交易为由，主张该保函性质为一般保证或连带保证的，人民法院不予支持。  
当事人主张独立保函适用民法典关于一般保证或连带保证规定的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 3** Where a guarantee has any of the following circumstances, and the party claims that the nature of the guarantee is an independent guarantee, the People's Court shall support such claim, except where the guarantee does not specify the documents for payment and the maximum amount:  
(1) The guarantee specifies that it is payable upon first demand;  
(2) The guarantee specifies the application of the Uniform Rules for Demand Guarantees of the International Chamber of Commerce or other model rules for independent guarantee transactions;  
(3) According to the content of the guarantee text, the issuer's obligation to pay is independent of the underlying transaction relationship and the legal relationship of the guarantee application, and the issuer only bears the payment obligation upon presentation of conforming documents.  
Where a party, on the ground that the independent guarantee records the corresponding underlying transaction, claims that the nature of the guarantee is a general suretyship or joint and several suretyship, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
Where a party claims that the independent guarantee is subject to the provisions of the Security Law regarding general suretyship or joint and several suretyship, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　独立保函的开立时间为开立人发出独立保函的时间。  
独立保函一经开立即生效，但独立保函载明生效日期或事件的除外。  
独立保函未载明可撤销，当事人主张独立保函开立后不可撤销的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 4** The time of issuance of an independent guarantee shall be the time when the issuer sends the independent guarantee.  
An independent guarantee shall take effect upon issuance, except where an effective date or event is specified in the independent guarantee.  
Where an independent guarantee does not specify that it is revocable, and a party claims that the independent guarantee is irrevocable after issuance, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　独立保函载明适用《见索即付保函统一规则》等独立保函交易示范规则，或开立人和受益人在一审法庭辩论终结前一致援引的，人民法院应当认定交易示范规则的内容构成独立保函条款的组成部分。  
不具有前款情形，当事人主张独立保函适用相关交易示范规则的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 5** Where an independent guarantee specifies the application of the Uniform Rules for Demand Guarantees or other model rules for independent guarantee transactions, or the issuer and the beneficiary unanimously cite such rules before the conclusion of the debate at the first instance trial, the People's Court shall determine that the content of the model rules constitutes a part of the terms of the independent guarantee.  
Where the circumstances in the preceding paragraph do not exist, and a party claims that the independent guarantee is subject to the relevant model rules, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　受益人提交的单据与独立保函条款之间、单据与单据之间表面相符，受益人请求开立人依据独立保函承担付款责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
开立人以基础交易关系或独立保函申请关系对付款义务提出抗辩的，人民法院不予支持，但有[本规定第十二条](#art-12)情形的除外。

**Article 6** Where the documents submitted by the beneficiary are on their face consistent with the terms of the independent guarantee and with each other, and the beneficiary requests the issuer to assume the payment obligation pursuant to the independent guarantee, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the issuer raises a defense against the payment obligation based on the underlying transaction relationship or the independent guarantee application relationship, the People's Court shall not support such defense, except where the circumstances in Article 12 of these Provisions exist.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民法院在认定是否构成表面相符时，应当根据独立保函载明的审单标准进行审查；独立保函未载明的，可以参照适用国际商会确定的相关审单标准。  
单据与独立保函条款之间、单据与单据之间表面上不完全一致，但并不导致相互之间产生歧义的，人民法院应当认定构成表面相符。

**Article 7** When determining whether there is conformity on the face, the People's Court shall review the documents in accordance with the examination standards specified in the independent guarantee; where the independent guarantee does not specify such standards, it may, by reference, apply the relevant examination standards established by the International Chamber of Commerce.  
Where documents are not completely consistent on their face with the terms of the independent guarantee or with each other, but do not cause ambiguity between them, the People's Court shall determine that there is conformity on the face.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　开立人有独立审查单据的权利与义务，有权自行决定单据与独立保函条款之间、单据与单据之间是否表面相符，并自行决定接受或拒绝接受不符点。  
开立人已向受益人明确表示接受不符点，受益人请求开立人承担付款责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
开立人拒绝接受不符点，受益人以保函申请人已接受不符点为由请求开立人承担付款责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 8** The issuer shall have the right and obligation to independently examine the documents, and shall have the right to decide whether the documents are on their face consistent with the terms of the independent guarantee and with each other, and to decide to accept or reject discrepancies.  
Where the issuer has expressly indicated to the beneficiary that it accepts the discrepancies, and the beneficiary requests the issuer to assume the payment obligation, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the issuer refuses to accept discrepancies, and the beneficiary, on the ground that the applicant for the guarantee has accepted the discrepancies, requests the issuer to assume the payment obligation, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　开立人依据独立保函付款后向保函申请人追偿的，人民法院应予支持，但受益人提交的单据存在不符点的除外。

**Article 9** Where the issuer, after making payment pursuant to the independent guarantee, seeks recourse against the applicant for the guarantee, the People's Court shall support such claim, except where the documents submitted by the beneficiary contain discrepancies.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　独立保函未同时载明可转让和据以确定新受益人的单据，开立人主张受益人付款请求权的转让对其不发生效力的，人民法院应予支持。独立保函对受益人付款请求权的转让有特别约定的，从其约定。

**Article 10** Where an independent guarantee does not simultaneously specify that it is transferable and the documents for determining the new beneficiary, and the issuer claims that the transfer of the beneficiary's right to demand payment is not effective against it, the People's Court shall support such claim. Where the independent guarantee has special provisions on the transfer of the beneficiary's right to demand payment, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　独立保函具有下列情形之一，当事人主张独立保函权利义务终止的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）独立保函载明的到期日或到期事件届至，受益人未提交符合独立保函要求的单据；  
（二）独立保函项下的应付款项已经全部支付；  
（三）独立保函的金额已减额至零；  
（四）开立人收到受益人出具的免除独立保函项下付款义务的文件；  
（五）法律规定或者当事人约定终止的其他情形。  
独立保函具有前款权利义务终止的情形，受益人以其持有独立保函文本为由主张享有付款请求权的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 11** Where an independent guarantee has any of the following circumstances, and a party claims that the rights and obligations under the independent guarantee are terminated, the People's Court shall support such claim:  
(1) The expiry date or expiry event specified in the independent guarantee has occurred, and the beneficiary has not submitted documents that comply with the requirements of the independent guarantee;  
(2) The payable amount under the independent guarantee has been fully paid;  
(3) The amount of the independent guarantee has been reduced to zero;  
(4) The issuer has received a document issued by the beneficiary exempting the payment obligation under the independent guarantee;  
(5) Other circumstances under which termination is provided for by law or agreed upon by the parties.  
Where the independent guarantee has the circumstances of termination of rights and obligations in the preceding paragraph, and the beneficiary claims to have the right to demand payment on the ground that it holds the text of the independent guarantee, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定构成独立保函欺诈：  
（一）受益人与保函申请人或其他人串通，虚构基础交易的；  
（二）受益人提交的第三方单据系伪造或内容虚假的；  
（三）法院判决或仲裁裁决认定基础交易债务人没有付款或赔偿责任的；  
（四）受益人确认基础交易债务已得到完全履行或者确认独立保函载明的付款到期事件并未发生的；  
（五）受益人明知其没有付款请求权仍滥用该权利的其他情形。

**Article 12** Where any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall determine that fraud in the independent guarantee is constituted:  
(1) The beneficiary colludes with the applicant for the guarantee or others to fabricate the underlying transaction;  
(2) The documents submitted by the beneficiary from a third party are forged or their content is false;  
(3) A court judgment or arbitral award determines that the debtor of the underlying transaction has no payment or compensation obligation;  
(4) The beneficiary confirms that the debt of the underlying transaction has been fully performed or that the payment due event specified in the independent guarantee has not occurred;  
(5) Other circumstances where the beneficiary, knowing that it has no right to demand payment, nevertheless abuses such right.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　独立保函的申请人、开立人或指示人发现有[本规定第十二条](#art-12)情形的，可以在提起诉讼或申请仲裁前，向开立人住所地或其他对独立保函欺诈纠纷案件具有管辖权的人民法院申请中止支付独立保函项下的款项，也可以在诉讼或仲裁过程中提出申请。

**Article 13** Where the applicant, issuer, or instructing party of an independent guarantee discovers the circumstances set forth in Article 12 of these Provisions, it may, before filing a lawsuit or applying for arbitration, apply to the People's Court in the place of the issuer's domicile or another People's Court that has jurisdiction over the dispute of fraud in the independent guarantee for a stay of payment of the amount under the independent guarantee, or may file such an application during the lawsuit or arbitration proceedings.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院裁定中止支付独立保函项下的款项，必须同时具备下列条件：  
（一）止付申请人提交的证据材料证明[本规定第十二条](#art-12)情形的存在具有高度可能性；  
（二）情况紧急，不立即采取止付措施，将给止付申请人的合法权益造成难以弥补的损害；  
（三）止付申请人提供了足以弥补被申请人因止付可能遭受损失的担保。  
止付申请人以受益人在基础交易中违约为由请求止付的，人民法院不予支持。  
开立人在依指示开立的独立保函项下已经善意付款的，对保障该开立人追偿权的独立保函，人民法院不得裁定止付。

**Article 14** When the People's Court rules to stay payment of the amount under the independent guarantee, the following conditions must be satisfied simultaneously:  
(1) The evidence submitted by the applicant for the stay proves that the existence of the circumstances set forth in Article 12 of these Provisions is highly probable;  
(2) The situation is urgent, and failure to take immediate stay measures would cause irreparable damage to the lawful rights and interests of the applicant for the stay;  
(3) The applicant for the stay provides security sufficient to compensate for the losses that the respondent may suffer due to the stay.  
Where the applicant for the stay requests the stay on the ground that the beneficiary has breached the underlying transaction, the People's Court shall not support such request.  
Where the issuer has already made payment in good faith under an independent guarantee issued upon instruction, the People's Court shall not rule to stay payment on the independent guarantee that secures the issuer's right of recourse.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　因止付申请错误造成损失，当事人请求止付申请人赔偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 15** Where losses are caused by an erroneous application for a stay, and a party requests compensation from the applicant for the stay, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院受理止付申请后，应当在四十八小时内作出书面裁定。裁定应当列明申请人、被申请人和第三人，并包括初步查明的事实和是否准许止付申请的理由。  
裁定中止支付的，应当立即执行。  
止付申请人在止付裁定作出后三十日内未依法提起独立保函欺诈纠纷诉讼或申请仲裁的，人民法院应当解除止付裁定。

**Article 16** After accepting an application for a stay, the People's Court shall make a written ruling within forty-eight hours. The ruling shall list the applicant, the respondent, and the third party, and shall include the facts initially ascertained and the reasons for whether to grant the application for a stay.  
If the ruling orders a stay of payment, it shall be executed immediately.  
Where the applicant for the stay fails to file a lawsuit for fraud in the independent guarantee or apply for arbitration within thirty days after the ruling on the stay is made, the People's Court shall lift the stay ruling.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　当事人对人民法院就止付申请作出的裁定有异议的，可以在裁定书送达之日起十日内向作出裁定的人民法院申请复议。复议期间不停止裁定的执行。  
人民法院应当在收到复议申请后十日内审查，并询问当事人。

**Article 17** Where a party has an objection to the ruling made by the People's Court on the application for a stay, it may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court that made the ruling within ten days from the date of service of the ruling. During the period of reconsideration, the execution of the ruling shall not be suspended.  
The People's Court shall review the application for reconsideration within ten days after receiving it, and shall inquire of the parties.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法院审理独立保函欺诈纠纷案件或处理止付申请，可以就当事人主张的[本规定第十二条](#art-12)的具体情形，审查认定基础交易的相关事实。

**Article 18** When hearing a case concerning a dispute over fraud in an independent guarantee or handling an application for a stay, the People's Court may examine and determine the relevant facts of the underlying transaction with respect to the specific circumstances under Article 12 of these Provisions claimed by the parties.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　保函申请人在独立保函欺诈诉讼中仅起诉受益人的，独立保函的开立人、指示人可以作为第三人申请参加，或由人民法院通知其参加。

**Article 19** Where the applicant for the guarantee only sues the beneficiary in a lawsuit concerning fraud in an independent guarantee, the issuer and the instructing party of the independent guarantee may apply to participate as a third party, or the People's Court may notify them to participate.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　人民法院经审理独立保函欺诈纠纷案件，能够排除合理怀疑地认定构成独立保函欺诈，并且不存在[本规定第十四条](#art-14)第三款情形的，应当判决开立人终止支付独立保函项下被请求的款项。

**Article 20** Where, after hearing a case concerning a dispute over fraud in an independent guarantee, the People's Court can determine beyond a reasonable doubt that fraud in the independent guarantee is constituted, and there is no circumstance under paragraph 3 of Article 14 of these Provisions, it shall render a judgment ordering the issuer to terminate payment of the demanded amount under the independent guarantee.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　受益人和开立人之间因独立保函而产生的纠纷案件，由开立人住所地或被告住所地人民法院管辖，独立保函载明由其他法院管辖或提交仲裁的除外。当事人主张根据基础交易合同争议解决条款确定管辖法院或提交仲裁的，人民法院不予支持。  
独立保函欺诈纠纷案件由被请求止付的独立保函的开立人住所地或被告住所地人民法院管辖，当事人书面协议由其他法院管辖或提交仲裁的除外。当事人主张根据基础交易合同或独立保函的争议解决条款确定管辖法院或提交仲裁的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 21** Dispute cases between a beneficiary and an issuer arising from an independent guarantee shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the issuer is domiciled or the defendant is domiciled, unless the independent guarantee provides that another court has jurisdiction or that the dispute shall be submitted to arbitration. Where a party claims that the competent court or arbitration shall be determined in accordance with the dispute resolution clause of the underlying transaction contract, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
  
Dispute cases concerning fraud in an independent guarantee shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the issuer of the independent guarantee against which payment is requested to be stopped is domiciled or the defendant is domiciled, unless the parties have agreed in writing that another court has jurisdiction or that the dispute shall be submitted to arbitration. Where a party claims that the competent court or arbitration shall be determined in accordance with the dispute resolution clause of the underlying transaction contract or the independent guarantee, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　涉外独立保函未载明适用法律，开立人和受益人在一审法庭辩论终结前亦未就适用法律达成一致的，开立人和受益人之间因涉外独立保函而产生的纠纷适用开立人经常居所地法律；独立保函由金融机构依法登记设立的分支机构开立的，适用分支机构登记地法律。  
涉外独立保函欺诈纠纷，当事人就适用法律不能达成一致的，适用被请求止付的独立保函的开立人经常居所地法律；独立保函由金融机构依法登记设立的分支机构开立的，适用分支机构登记地法律；当事人有共同经常居所地的，适用共同经常居所地法律。  
涉外独立保函止付保全程序，适用中华人民共和国法律。

**Article 22** Where a foreign-related independent guarantee does not specify the applicable law, and the issuer and the beneficiary fail to reach an agreement on the applicable law before the conclusion of the first-instance court debate, the law of the place of the issuer's habitual residence shall apply to disputes between the issuer and the beneficiary arising from the foreign-related independent guarantee; where the independent guarantee is issued by a branch legally registered and established by a financial institution, the law of the place where the branch is registered shall apply.  
  
Where the parties fail to reach an agreement on the applicable law for a dispute concerning fraud in a foreign-related independent guarantee, the law of the place of the issuer's habitual residence of the independent guarantee against which payment is requested to be stopped shall apply; where the independent guarantee is issued by a branch legally registered and established by a financial institution, the law of the place where the branch is registered shall apply; where the parties have a common habitual residence, the law of the common habitual residence shall apply.  
  
The procedures for the preservation of payment stoppage of a foreign-related independent guarantee shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　当事人约定在国内交易中适用独立保函，一方当事人以独立保函不具有涉外因素为由，主张保函独立性的约定无效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 23** Where the parties agree to apply an independent guarantee in a domestic transaction, and one party claims that the agreement on the independence of the guarantee is invalid on the ground that the independent guarantee does not have a foreign-related element, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对于按照特户管理并移交开立人占有的独立保函开立保证金，人民法院可以采取冻结措施，但不得扣划。保证金账户内的款项丧失开立保证金的功能时，人民法院可以依法采取扣划措施。  
开立人已履行对外支付义务的，根据该开立人的申请，人民法院应当解除对开立保证金相应部分的冻结措施。

**Article 24** For the deposit for issuance of an independent guarantee that is managed under a special account and delivered to the possession of the issuer, the People's Court may adopt freezing measures, but shall not deduct or transfer the funds. When the funds in the deposit account cease to function as a deposit for issuance, the People's Court may adopt deduction or transfer measures according to law.  
  
Where the issuer has fulfilled its external payment obligations, upon application by the issuer, the People's Court shall lift the freezing measures on the corresponding part of the deposit for issuance.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本规定施行后尚未终审的案件，适用本规定；本规定施行前已经终审的案件，当事人申请再审或者人民法院按照审判监督程序再审的，不适用本规定。

**Article 25** Cases for which the final judgment has not been rendered after these Provisions come into force shall be governed by these Provisions; cases for which the final judgment has been rendered before these Provisions come into force, where a party applies for retrial or the People's Court conducts a retrial in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, these Provisions shall not apply.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本规定自2016年12月1日起施行。

**Article 26** These Provisions shall come into force as of December 1, 2016.

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所称的独立保函，是指银行或非银行金融机构作为开立人，以书面形式向受益人出具的，同意在受益人请求付款并提交符合保函要求的单据时，向其支付特定款项或在保函最高金额内付款的承诺。  
前款所称的单据，是指独立保函载明的受益人应提交的付款请求书、违约声明、第三方签发的文件、法院判决、仲裁裁决、汇票、发票等表明发生付款到期事件的书面文件。  
独立保函可以依保函申请人的申请而开立，也可以依另一金融机构的指示而开立。开立人依指示开立独立保函的，可以要求指示人向其开立用以保障追偿权的独立保函。

**Article 1** For the purposes of these Provisions, an independent guarantee refers to a written undertaking issued by a bank or non-bank financial institution as the issuer to the beneficiary, agreeing to pay a specified amount or an amount within the maximum amount of the guarantee to the beneficiary upon the beneficiary's request for payment and submission of documents that comply with the requirements of the guarantee.  
The documents referred to in the preceding paragraph mean the written documents specified in the independent guarantee that the beneficiary shall submit, such as a demand for payment, a statement of default, a document issued by a third party, a court judgment, an arbitral award, a bill of exchange, an invoice, etc., which indicate the occurrence of a payment due event.  
An independent guarantee may be issued upon the application of the applicant for the guarantee, or upon the instruction of another financial institution. Where the issuer issues an independent guarantee upon instruction, it may request the instructing party to issue to it an independent guarantee to secure its right of recourse.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定所称的独立保函纠纷，是指在独立保函的开立、撤销、修改、转让、付款、追偿等环节产生的纠纷。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Provisions, a dispute over an independent guarantee refers to a dispute arising from the issuance, revocation, amendment, transfer, payment, recourse, or other aspects of an independent guarantee.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　保函具有下列情形之一，当事人主张保函性质为独立保函的，人民法院应予支持，但保函未载明据以付款的单据和最高金额的除外：  
（一）保函载明见索即付；  
（二）保函载明适用国际商会《见索即付保函统一规则》等独立保函交易示范规则；  
（三）根据保函文本内容，开立人的付款义务独立于基础交易关系及保函申请法律关系，其仅承担相符交单的付款责任。  
当事人以独立保函记载了对应的基础交易为由，主张该保函性质为一般保证或连带保证的，人民法院不予支持。  
当事人主张独立保函适用担保法关于一般保证或连带保证规定的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 3** Where a guarantee has any of the following circumstances, and the party claims that the nature of the guarantee is an independent guarantee, the People's Court shall support such claim, except where the guarantee does not specify the documents for payment and the maximum amount:  
(1) The guarantee specifies that it is payable upon first demand;  
(2) The guarantee specifies the application of the Uniform Rules for Demand Guarantees of the International Chamber of Commerce or other model rules for independent guarantee transactions;  
(3) According to the content of the guarantee text, the issuer's obligation to pay is independent of the underlying transaction relationship and the legal relationship of the guarantee application, and the issuer only bears the payment obligation upon presentation of conforming documents.  
Where a party, on the ground that the independent guarantee records the corresponding underlying transaction, claims that the nature of the guarantee is a general suretyship or joint and several suretyship, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
Where a party claims that the independent guarantee is subject to the provisions of the Security Law regarding general suretyship or joint and several suretyship, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　独立保函的开立时间为开立人发出独立保函的时间。  
独立保函一经开立即生效，但独立保函载明生效日期或事件的除外。  
独立保函未载明可撤销，当事人主张独立保函开立后不可撤销的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 4** The time of issuance of an independent guarantee shall be the time when the issuer sends the independent guarantee.  
An independent guarantee shall take effect upon issuance, except where an effective date or event is specified in the independent guarantee.  
Where an independent guarantee does not specify that it is revocable, and a party claims that the independent guarantee is irrevocable after issuance, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　独立保函载明适用《见索即付保函统一规则》等独立保函交易示范规则，或开立人和受益人在一审法庭辩论终结前一致援引的，人民法院应当认定交易示范规则的内容构成独立保函条款的组成部分。  
不具有前款情形，当事人主张独立保函适用相关交易示范规则的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 5** Where an independent guarantee specifies the application of the Uniform Rules for Demand Guarantees or other model rules for independent guarantee transactions, or the issuer and the beneficiary unanimously cite such rules before the conclusion of the debate at the first instance trial, the People's Court shall determine that the content of the model rules constitutes a part of the terms of the independent guarantee.  
Where the circumstances in the preceding paragraph do not exist, and a party claims that the independent guarantee is subject to the relevant model rules, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　受益人提交的单据与独立保函条款之间、单据与单据之间表面相符，受益人请求开立人依据独立保函承担付款责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
开立人以基础交易关系或独立保函申请关系对付款义务提出抗辩的，人民法院不予支持，但有[本规定第十二条](#art-12)情形的除外。

**Article 6** Where the documents submitted by the beneficiary are on their face consistent with the terms of the independent guarantee and with each other, and the beneficiary requests the issuer to assume the payment obligation pursuant to the independent guarantee, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the issuer raises a defense against the payment obligation based on the underlying transaction relationship or the independent guarantee application relationship, the People's Court shall not support such defense, except where the circumstances in Article 12 of these Provisions exist.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民法院在认定是否构成表面相符时，应当根据独立保函载明的审单标准进行审查；独立保函未载明的，可以参照适用国际商会确定的相关审单标准。  
单据与独立保函条款之间、单据与单据之间表面上不完全一致，但并不导致相互之间产生歧义的，人民法院应当认定构成表面相符。

**Article 7** When determining whether there is conformity on the face, the People's Court shall review the documents in accordance with the examination standards specified in the independent guarantee; where the independent guarantee does not specify such standards, it may, by reference, apply the relevant examination standards established by the International Chamber of Commerce.  
Where documents are not completely consistent on their face with the terms of the independent guarantee or with each other, but do not cause ambiguity between them, the People's Court shall determine that there is conformity on the face.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　开立人有独立审查单据的权利与义务，有权自行决定单据与独立保函条款之间、单据与单据之间是否表面相符，并自行决定接受或拒绝接受不符点。  
开立人已向受益人明确表示接受不符点，受益人请求开立人承担付款责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
开立人拒绝接受不符点，受益人以保函申请人已接受不符点为由请求开立人承担付款责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 8** The issuer shall have the right and obligation to independently examine the documents, and shall have the right to decide whether the documents are on their face consistent with the terms of the independent guarantee and with each other, and to decide to accept or reject discrepancies.  
Where the issuer has expressly indicated to the beneficiary that it accepts the discrepancies, and the beneficiary requests the issuer to assume the payment obligation, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the issuer refuses to accept discrepancies, and the beneficiary, on the ground that the applicant for the guarantee has accepted the discrepancies, requests the issuer to assume the payment obligation, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　开立人依据独立保函付款后向保函申请人追偿的，人民法院应予支持，但受益人提交的单据存在不符点的除外。

**Article 9** Where the issuer, after making payment pursuant to the independent guarantee, seeks recourse against the applicant for the guarantee, the People's Court shall support such claim, except where the documents submitted by the beneficiary contain discrepancies.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　独立保函未同时载明可转让和据以确定新受益人的单据，开立人主张受益人付款请求权的转让对其不发生效力的，人民法院应予支持。独立保函对受益人付款请求权的转让有特别约定的，从其约定。

**Article 10** Where an independent guarantee does not simultaneously specify that it is transferable and the documents for determining the new beneficiary, and the issuer claims that the transfer of the beneficiary's right to demand payment is not effective against it, the People's Court shall support such claim. Where the independent guarantee has special provisions on the transfer of the beneficiary's right to demand payment, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　独立保函具有下列情形之一，当事人主张独立保函权利义务终止的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）独立保函载明的到期日或到期事件届至，受益人未提交符合独立保函要求的单据；  
（二）独立保函项下的应付款项已经全部支付；  
（三）独立保函的金额已减额至零；  
（四）开立人收到受益人出具的免除独立保函项下付款义务的文件；  
（五）法律规定或者当事人约定终止的其他情形。  
独立保函具有前款权利义务终止的情形，受益人以其持有独立保函文本为由主张享有付款请求权的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 11** Where an independent guarantee has any of the following circumstances, and a party claims that the rights and obligations under the independent guarantee are terminated, the People's Court shall support such claim:  
(1) The expiry date or expiry event specified in the independent guarantee has occurred, and the beneficiary has not submitted documents that comply with the requirements of the independent guarantee;  
(2) The payable amount under the independent guarantee has been fully paid;  
(3) The amount of the independent guarantee has been reduced to zero;  
(4) The issuer has received a document issued by the beneficiary exempting the payment obligation under the independent guarantee;  
(5) Other circumstances under which termination is provided for by law or agreed upon by the parties.  
Where the independent guarantee has the circumstances of termination of rights and obligations in the preceding paragraph, and the beneficiary claims to have the right to demand payment on the ground that it holds the text of the independent guarantee, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定构成独立保函欺诈：  
（一）受益人与保函申请人或其他人串通，虚构基础交易的；  
（二）受益人提交的第三方单据系伪造或内容虚假的；  
（三）法院判决或仲裁裁决认定基础交易债务人没有付款或赔偿责任的；  
（四）受益人确认基础交易债务已得到完全履行或者确认独立保函载明的付款到期事件并未发生的；  
（五）受益人明知其没有付款请求权仍滥用该权利的其他情形。

**Article 12** Where any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall determine that fraud in the independent guarantee is constituted:  
(1) The beneficiary colludes with the applicant for the guarantee or others to fabricate the underlying transaction;  
(2) The documents submitted by the beneficiary from a third party are forged or their content is false;  
(3) A court judgment or arbitral award determines that the debtor of the underlying transaction has no payment or compensation obligation;  
(4) The beneficiary confirms that the debt of the underlying transaction has been fully performed or that the payment due event specified in the independent guarantee has not occurred;  
(5) Other circumstances where the beneficiary, knowing that it has no right to demand payment, nevertheless abuses such right.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　独立保函的申请人、开立人或指示人发现有[本规定第十二条](#art-12)情形的，可以在提起诉讼或申请仲裁前，向开立人住所地或其他对独立保函欺诈纠纷案件具有管辖权的人民法院申请中止支付独立保函项下的款项，也可以在诉讼或仲裁过程中提出申请。

**Article 13** Where the applicant, issuer, or instructing party of an independent guarantee discovers the circumstances set forth in Article 12 of these Provisions, it may, before filing a lawsuit or applying for arbitration, apply to the People's Court in the place of the issuer's domicile or another People's Court that has jurisdiction over the dispute of fraud in the independent guarantee for a stay of payment of the amount under the independent guarantee, or may file such an application during the lawsuit or arbitration proceedings.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院裁定中止支付独立保函项下的款项，必须同时具备下列条件：  
（一）止付申请人提交的证据材料证明[本规定第十二条](#art-12)情形的存在具有高度可能性；  
（二）情况紧急，不立即采取止付措施，将给止付申请人的合法权益造成难以弥补的损害；  
（三）止付申请人提供了足以弥补被申请人因止付可能遭受损失的担保。  
止付申请人以受益人在基础交易中违约为由请求止付的，人民法院不予支持。  
开立人在依指示开立的独立保函项下已经善意付款的，对保障该开立人追偿权的独立保函，人民法院不得裁定止付。

**Article 14** When the People's Court rules to stay payment of the amount under the independent guarantee, the following conditions must be satisfied simultaneously:  
(1) The evidence submitted by the applicant for the stay proves that the existence of the circumstances set forth in Article 12 of these Provisions is highly probable;  
(2) The situation is urgent, and failure to take immediate stay measures would cause irreparable damage to the lawful rights and interests of the applicant for the stay;  
(3) The applicant for the stay provides security sufficient to compensate for the losses that the respondent may suffer due to the stay.  
Where the applicant for the stay requests the stay on the ground that the beneficiary has breached the underlying transaction, the People's Court shall not support such request.  
Where the issuer has already made payment in good faith under an independent guarantee issued upon instruction, the People's Court shall not rule to stay payment on the independent guarantee that secures the issuer's right of recourse.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　因止付申请错误造成损失，当事人请求止付申请人赔偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 15** Where losses are caused by an erroneous application for a stay, and a party requests compensation from the applicant for the stay, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院受理止付申请后，应当在四十八小时内作出书面裁定。裁定应当列明申请人、被申请人和第三人，并包括初步查明的事实和是否准许止付申请的理由。  
裁定中止支付的，应当立即执行。  
止付申请人在止付裁定作出后三十日内未依法提起独立保函欺诈纠纷诉讼或申请仲裁的，人民法院应当解除止付裁定。

**Article 16** After accepting an application for a stay, the People's Court shall make a written ruling within forty-eight hours. The ruling shall list the applicant, the respondent, and the third party, and shall include the facts initially ascertained and the reasons for whether to grant the application for a stay.  
If the ruling orders a stay of payment, it shall be executed immediately.  
Where the applicant for the stay fails to file a lawsuit for fraud in the independent guarantee or apply for arbitration within thirty days after the ruling on the stay is made, the People's Court shall lift the stay ruling.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　当事人对人民法院就止付申请作出的裁定有异议的，可以在裁定书送达之日起十日内向作出裁定的人民法院申请复议。复议期间不停止裁定的执行。  
人民法院应当在收到复议申请后十日内审查，并询问当事人。

**Article 17** Where a party has an objection to the ruling made by the People's Court on the application for a stay, it may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court that made the ruling within ten days from the date of service of the ruling. During the period of reconsideration, the execution of the ruling shall not be suspended.  
The People's Court shall review the application for reconsideration within ten days after receiving it, and shall inquire of the parties.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法院审理独立保函欺诈纠纷案件或处理止付申请，可以就当事人主张的[本规定第十二条](#art-12)的具体情形，审查认定基础交易的相关事实。

**Article 18** When hearing a case concerning a dispute over fraud in an independent guarantee or handling an application for a stay, the People's Court may examine and determine the relevant facts of the underlying transaction with respect to the specific circumstances under Article 12 of these Provisions claimed by the parties.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　保函申请人在独立保函欺诈诉讼中仅起诉受益人的，独立保函的开立人、指示人可以作为第三人申请参加，或由人民法院通知其参加。

**Article 19** Where the applicant for the guarantee only sues the beneficiary in a lawsuit concerning fraud in an independent guarantee, the issuer and the instructing party of the independent guarantee may apply to participate as a third party, or the People's Court may notify them to participate.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　人民法院经审理独立保函欺诈纠纷案件，能够排除合理怀疑地认定构成独立保函欺诈，并且不存在[本规定第十四条](#art-14)第三款情形的，应当判决开立人终止支付独立保函项下被请求的款项。

**Article 20** Where, after hearing a case concerning a dispute over fraud in an independent guarantee, the People's Court can determine beyond a reasonable doubt that fraud in the independent guarantee is constituted, and there is no circumstance under paragraph 3 of Article 14 of these Provisions, it shall render a judgment ordering the issuer to terminate payment of the demanded amount under the independent guarantee.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　受益人和开立人之间因独立保函而产生的纠纷案件，由开立人住所地或被告住所地人民法院管辖，独立保函载明由其他法院管辖或提交仲裁的除外。当事人主张根据基础交易合同争议解决条款确定管辖法院或提交仲裁的，人民法院不予支持。  
独立保函欺诈纠纷案件由被请求止付的独立保函的开立人住所地或被告住所地人民法院管辖，当事人书面协议由其他法院管辖或提交仲裁的除外。当事人主张根据基础交易合同或独立保函的争议解决条款确定管辖法院或提交仲裁的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 21** Dispute cases between a beneficiary and an issuer arising from an independent guarantee shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the issuer is domiciled or the defendant is domiciled, unless the independent guarantee provides that another court has jurisdiction or that the dispute shall be submitted to arbitration. Where a party claims that the competent court or arbitration shall be determined in accordance with the dispute resolution clause of the underlying transaction contract, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
  
Dispute cases concerning fraud in an independent guarantee shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the issuer of the independent guarantee against which payment is requested to be stopped is domiciled or the defendant is domiciled, unless the parties have agreed in writing that another court has jurisdiction or that the dispute shall be submitted to arbitration. Where a party claims that the competent court or arbitration shall be determined in accordance with the dispute resolution clause of the underlying transaction contract or the independent guarantee, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　涉外独立保函未载明适用法律，开立人和受益人在一审法庭辩论终结前亦未就适用法律达成一致的，开立人和受益人之间因涉外独立保函而产生的纠纷适用开立人经常居所地法律；独立保函由金融机构依法登记设立的分支机构开立的，适用分支机构登记地法律。  
涉外独立保函欺诈纠纷，当事人就适用法律不能达成一致的，适用被请求止付的独立保函的开立人经常居所地法律；独立保函由金融机构依法登记设立的分支机构开立的，适用分支机构登记地法律；当事人有共同经常居所地的，适用共同经常居所地法律。  
涉外独立保函止付保全程序，适用中华人民共和国法律。

**Article 22** Where a foreign-related independent guarantee does not specify the applicable law, and the issuer and the beneficiary fail to reach an agreement on the applicable law before the conclusion of the first-instance court debate, the law of the place of the issuer's habitual residence shall apply to disputes between the issuer and the beneficiary arising from the foreign-related independent guarantee; where the independent guarantee is issued by a branch legally registered and established by a financial institution, the law of the place where the branch is registered shall apply.  
  
Where the parties fail to reach an agreement on the applicable law for a dispute concerning fraud in a foreign-related independent guarantee, the law of the place of the issuer's habitual residence of the independent guarantee against which payment is requested to be stopped shall apply; where the independent guarantee is issued by a branch legally registered and established by a financial institution, the law of the place where the branch is registered shall apply; where the parties have a common habitual residence, the law of the common habitual residence shall apply.  
  
The procedures for the preservation of payment stoppage of a foreign-related independent guarantee shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　当事人约定在国内交易中适用独立保函，一方当事人以独立保函不具有涉外因素为由，主张保函独立性的约定无效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 23** Where the parties agree to apply an independent guarantee in a domestic transaction, and one party claims that the agreement on the independence of the guarantee is invalid on the ground that the independent guarantee does not have a foreign-related element, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对于按照特户管理并移交开立人占有的独立保函开立保证金，人民法院可以采取冻结措施，但不得扣划。保证金账户内的款项丧失开立保证金的功能时，人民法院可以依法采取扣划措施。  
开立人已履行对外支付义务的，根据该开立人的申请，人民法院应当解除对开立保证金相应部分的冻结措施。

**Article 24** For the deposit for issuance of an independent guarantee that is managed under a special account and delivered to the possession of the issuer, the People's Court may adopt freezing measures, but shall not deduct or transfer the funds. When the funds in the deposit account cease to function as a deposit for issuance, the People's Court may adopt deduction or transfer measures according to law.  
  
Where the issuer has fulfilled its external payment obligations, upon application by the issuer, the People's Court shall lift the freezing measures on the corresponding part of the deposit for issuance.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本规定施行后尚未终审的案件，适用本规定；本规定施行前已经终审的案件，当事人申请再审或者人民法院按照审判监督程序再审的，不适用本规定。

**Article 25** Cases for which the final judgment has not been rendered after these Provisions come into force shall be governed by these Provisions; cases for which the final judgment has been rendered before these Provisions come into force, where a party applies for retrial or the People's Court conducts a retrial in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, these Provisions shall not apply.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本规定自2016年12月1日起施行。

**Article 26** These Provisions shall come into force as of December 1, 2016.
