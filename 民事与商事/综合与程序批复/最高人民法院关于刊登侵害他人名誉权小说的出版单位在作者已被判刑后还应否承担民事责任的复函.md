### 最高人民法院关于刊登侵害他人名誉权小说的出版单位在作者已被判刑后还应否承担民事责任的复函

<a id="art-1"></a>最 高 人 民 法 院  
  
关于刊登侵害他人名誉权小说的出版单位在  
  
作者已被判刑后还应否承担民事责任的复函  
  
（1992）民他字第1号  
  
江苏省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（ 1991）民请字第 10号《关于刊登侵害他人名誉权小说的出版单位在作者已被判刑后还应否承担民事责任的请示》收悉。经研究认为：出版单位刊登侮辱、诽谤他人的小说，原告多次向出版单位反映，要求其澄清事实、消除影响，出版单位未予置理。在作者为此被以诽谤罪追究刑事责任后，出版单位仍不采取措施，为原告消除影响，致使该小说继续流传于社会，扩大了不良影响，侵害了原告的名誉权。因此，出版单位应当承担民事责任。  
  
1992年8月14日

**Article 1** THE SUPREME PEOPLE'S COURT  
Reply on Whether a Publishing Unit That Published a Novel Infringing upon Another's Right to Reputation Shall Bear Civil Liability After the Author Has Been Sentenced to Criminal Punishment  
(1992) Min Ta Zi No. 1  
Jiangsu Higher People's Court:  
This Court has received your Request for Instructions No. (1991) Min Qing Zi No. 10, titled "Request for Instructions on Whether a Publishing Unit That Published a Novel Infringing upon Another's Right to Reputation Shall Bear Civil Liability After the Author Has Been Sentenced to Criminal Punishment." After study, it is held that: where a publishing unit publishes a novel that insults or defames another person, and the plaintiff repeatedly reflects this to the publishing unit, requesting it to clarify the facts and eliminate the adverse effects, but the publishing unit fails to take any action; and after the author is investigated for criminal liability for defamation, the publishing unit still fails to take measures to eliminate the adverse effects for the plaintiff, causing the novel to continue circulating in society and thus expanding the adverse effects and infringing upon the plaintiff's right to reputation, the publishing unit shall bear civil liability.  
August 14, 1992
