### 最高人民法院关于当事人申请财产保全错误造成案外人损失应否承担赔偿责任问题的解释

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于当事人申请财产保全错误造成案外人损失应否承担赔偿责任问题的解释  
  
法释［2005］11号  
  
近来，一些法院就当事人申请财产保全错误造成案外人损失引发的赔偿纠纷案件应如何适用法律问题请示我院。经研究，现解释如下：  
  
根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》第一百零六条、[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第九十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-96)等法律规定，当事人申请财产保全错误造成案外人损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。  
  
此复。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Interpretation concerning the Issue of Whether a Party Shall Bear Compensation Liability for Losses Caused to a Third Party by an Error in Applying for Property Preservation  
Fa Shi [2005] No. 11  
Recently, some courts have requested guidance from our court on the issue of how to apply the law in compensation dispute cases arising from losses caused to a third party by a party's error in applying for property preservation. Upon study, the interpretation is as follows:  
According to Article 106 of the General Principles of the Civil Law of the People's Republic of China, Article 96 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and other legal provisions, where a party causes losses to a third party by an error in applying for property preservation, the party shall bear compensation liability according to law.  
This is the reply.
