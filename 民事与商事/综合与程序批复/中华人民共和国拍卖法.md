### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范拍卖行为，维护拍卖秩序，保护拍卖活动各方当事人的合法权益，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of regulating auction activities, maintaining auction order, and protecting the lawful rights and interests of all parties involved in auction activities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法适用于中华人民共和国境内拍卖企业进行的拍卖活动。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to auction activities conducted by auction enterprises within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　拍卖是指以公开竞价的形式，将特定物品或者财产权利转让给最高应价者的买卖方式。

**Article 3** Auction refers to a method of sale whereby specific goods or property rights are transferred to the party offering the highest price through public bidding.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　拍卖活动应当遵守有关法律、行政法规，遵循公开、公平、公正、诚实信用的原则。

**Article 4** Auction activities shall comply with relevant laws and administrative regulations and shall follow the principles of openness, fairness, justice, honesty and credit.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院负责管理拍卖业的部门对全国拍卖业实施监督管理。  
省、自治区、直辖市的人民政府和设区的市的人民政府负责管理拍卖业的部门对本行政区域内的拍卖业实施监督管理。

**Article 5** The department of the State Council responsible for the administration of the auction industry shall exercise supervision and administration over the auction industry nationwide.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the people's governments of cities divided into districts, through their departments responsible for the administration of the auction industry, shall exercise supervision and administration over the auction industry within their respective administrative areas.

### 第二章　拍卖标的

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　拍卖标的应当是委托人所有或者依法可以处分的物品或者财产权利。

**Article 6** The subject matter of auction shall be goods or property rights owned by the principal or which the principal may dispose of according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　法律、行政法规禁止买卖的物品或者财产权利，不得作为拍卖标的。

**Article 7** Goods or property rights the sale of which is prohibited by laws or administrative regulations shall not be the subject matter of auction.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　依照法律或者按照国务院规定需经审批才能转让的物品或者财产权利，在拍卖前，应当依法办理审批手续。  
委托拍卖的文物，在拍卖前，应当经拍卖人住所地的文物行政管理部门依法鉴定、许可。

**Article 8** Goods or property rights that require approval for transfer in accordance with laws or regulations of the State Council shall undergo the formalities of approval according to law prior to auction.  
Cultural relics consigned for auction shall be subject to expert evaluation and permission by the administrative department for cultural relics at the place where the auctioneer is domiciled according to law prior to auction.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家行政机关依法没收的物品，充抵税款、罚款的物品和其他物品，按照国务院规定应当委托拍卖的，由财产所在地的省、自治区、直辖市的人民政府和设区的市的人民政府指定的拍卖人进行拍卖。  
拍卖由人民法院依法没收的物品，充抵罚金、罚款的物品以及无法返还的追回物品，适用前款规定。

**Article 9** Goods confiscated by state administrative organs according to law, goods used to offset taxes and fines, and other goods which, according to regulations of the State Council, shall be consigned for auction, shall be auctioned by an auctioneer designated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government or the city divided into districts where the goods are located.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply to the auction of goods confiscated by the People's Court according to law, goods used to offset fines and penalties, and recovered goods that cannot be returned.

### 第三章　拍卖当事人

#### 第一节　拍卖人

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　拍卖人是指依照本法和《中华人民共和国公司法》设立的从事拍卖活动的企业法人。

**Article 10** An auctioneer refers to an enterprise legal person established in accordance with this Law and the Company Law of the People's Republic of China to engage in auction activities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　企业取得从事拍卖业务的许可必须经所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理拍卖业的部门审核批准。拍卖企业可以在设区的市设立。

**Article 11** To obtain permission to engage in auction business, an enterprise must be examined and approved by the department responsible for the administration of the auction industry under the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located. An auction enterprise may be established in a city divided into districts.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　企业申请取得从事拍卖业务的许可，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有一百万元人民币以上的注册资本；  
（二）有自己的名称、组织机构、住所和章程；  
（三）有与从事拍卖业务相适应的拍卖师和其他工作人员；  
（四）有符合本法和其他有关法律规定的拍卖业务规则；  
（五）符合国务院有关拍卖业发展的规定；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 12** An enterprise applying for permission to engage in auction business shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) It has a registered capital of not less than RMB 1 million yuan;  
(2) It has its own name, organizational structure, domicile, and articles of association;  
(3) It has auctioneers and other staff suitable for the auction business;  
(4) It has auction business rules that comply with this Law and other relevant laws and regulations;  
(5) It meets the provisions of the State Council on the development of the auction industry; and  
(6) Other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　拍卖企业经营文物拍卖的，应当有一千万元人民币以上的注册资本，有具有文物拍卖专业知识的人员。

**Article 13** Where an auction enterprise operates the auction of cultural relics, it shall have a registered capital of not less than RMB 10 million yuan and have personnel with professional knowledge of cultural relics auction.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　拍卖活动应当由拍卖师主持。

**Article 14** Auction activities shall be presided over by an auctioneer.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　拍卖师应当具备下列条件：  
（一）具有高等院校专科以上学历和拍卖专业知识；  
（二）在拍卖企业工作两年以上；  
（三）品行良好。  
被开除公职或者吊销拍卖师资格证书未满五年的，或者因故意犯罪受过刑事处罚的，不得担任拍卖师。

**Article 15** An auctioneer shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) He has obtained a college degree or above and has professional knowledge of auction;  
(2) He has worked in an auction enterprise for at least two years; and  
(3) He has good conduct.  
A person who has been removed from public office or whose auctioneer qualification certificate has been revoked for less than five years, or who has received criminal punishment for intentional crime, may not serve as an auctioneer.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　拍卖师资格考核，由拍卖行业协会统一组织。经考核合格的，由拍卖行业协会发给拍卖师资格证书。

**Article 16** The auctioneer qualification assessment shall be organized uniformly by the auction industry association. Those who pass the assessment shall be issued an auctioneer qualification certificate by the auction industry association.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　拍卖行业协会是依法成立的社会团体法人，是拍卖业的自律性组织。拍卖行业协会依照本法并根据章程，对拍卖企业和拍卖师进行监督。

**Article 17** An auction industry association is a social organization legal person established according to law and a self-regulatory organization of the auction industry. The auction industry association shall, in accordance with this Law and its articles of association, supervise auction enterprises and auctioneers.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　拍卖人有权要求委托人说明拍卖标的的来源和瑕疵。  
拍卖人应当向竞买人说明拍卖标的的瑕疵。

**Article 18** The auctioneer has the right to require the client to explain the source and defects of the auction subject matter.  
The auctioneer shall explain the defects of the auction subject matter to the bidders.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　拍卖人对委托人交付拍卖的物品负有保管义务。

**Article 19** The auctioneer has the obligation to keep the items delivered by the client for auction.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　拍卖人接受委托后，未经委托人同意，不得委托其他拍卖人拍卖。

**Article 20** After accepting a mandate, the auctioneer may not entrust the auction to another auctioneer without the consent of the client.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　委托人、买受人要求对其身份保密的，拍卖人应当为其保密。

**Article 21** Where the client or buyer requests confidentiality of its identity, the auctioneer shall keep it confidential.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　拍卖人及其工作人员不得以竞买人的身份参与自己组织的拍卖活动，并不得委托他人代为竞买。

**Article 22** An auctioneer and its staff members may not participate as bidders in the auction activities they organize, and may not entrust others to bid on their behalf.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　拍卖人不得在自己组织的拍卖活动中拍卖自己的物品或者财产权利。

**Article 23** An auctioneer may not auction its own goods or property rights in the auction activities it organizes.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　拍卖成交后，拍卖人应当按照约定向委托人交付拍卖标的的价款，并按照约定将拍卖标的移交给买受人。

**Article 24** After the auction is concluded, the auctioneer shall deliver the price of the auction subject matter to the client according to the agreement, and shall transfer the auction subject matter to the buyer according to the agreement.

#### 第二节　委托人

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　委托人是指委托拍卖人拍卖物品或者财产权利的公民、法人或者其他组织。

**Article 25** The client refers to the citizen, legal person, or other organization that entrusts the auctioneer to auction goods or property rights.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　委托人可以自行办理委托拍卖手续，也可以由其代理人代为办理委托拍卖手续。

**Article 26** The client may handle the entrustment procedures for auction by itself, or may have its agent handle the entrustment procedures for auction on its behalf.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　委托人应当向拍卖人说明拍卖标的的来源和瑕疵。

**Article 27** The client shall explain the source and defects of the auction subject matter to the auctioneer.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　委托人有权确定拍卖标的的保留价并要求拍卖人保密。  
拍卖国有资产，依照法律或者按照国务院规定需要评估的，应当经依法设立的评估机构评估，并根据评估结果确定拍卖标的的保留价。

**Article 28** The client has the right to determine the reserve price of the auction subject matter and require the auctioneer to keep it confidential.  
Where state-owned assets are auctioned and appraisal is required in accordance with laws or regulations of the State Council, the assets shall be appraised by an appraisal institution established according to law, and the reserve price of the auction subject matter shall be determined based on the appraisal result.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　委托人在拍卖开始前可以撤回拍卖标的。委托人撤回拍卖标的的，应当向拍卖人支付约定的费用；未作约定的，应当向拍卖人支付为拍卖支出的合理费用。

**Article 29** The client may withdraw the auction subject matter before the auction begins. If the client withdraws the auction subject matter, it shall pay the agreed fees to the auctioneer; if no agreement has been made, it shall pay the reasonable expenses incurred by the auctioneer for the auction.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　委托人不得参与竞买，也不得委托他人代为竞买。

**Article 30** : The principal shall not participate in the bidding, nor shall it entrust another person to bid on its behalf.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　按照约定由委托人移交拍卖标的的，拍卖成交后，委托人应当将拍卖标的移交给买受人。

**Article 31** : Where the principal is to deliver the auction subject matter in accordance with the agreement, after the auction is concluded, the principal shall deliver the auction subject matter to the buyer.

#### 第三节　竞买人

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　竞买人是指参加竞购拍卖标的的公民、法人或者其他组织。

**Article 32** : A bidder means a citizen, legal person, or any other organization that participates in the bidding for an auction subject matter.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　法律、行政法规对拍卖标的的买卖条件有规定的，竞买人应当具备规定的条件。

**Article 33** : Where laws or administrative regulations have provisions on the conditions for sale of an auction subject matter, the bidder shall meet the prescribed conditions.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　竞买人可以自行参加竞买，也可以委托其代理人参加竞买。

**Article 34** : A bidder may participate in the bidding in person, or may entrust an agent to participate in the bidding.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　竞买人有权了解拍卖标的的瑕疵，有权查验拍卖标的和查阅有关拍卖资料。

**Article 35** : A bidder shall have the right to know the defects of the auction subject matter, and shall have the right to inspect the auction subject matter and consult the relevant auction materials.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　竞买人一经应价，不得撤回，当其他竞买人有更高应价时，其应价即丧失约束力。

**Article 36** : Once a bidder makes a bid, it shall not be withdrawn; when another bidder makes a higher bid, the original bid shall lose its binding force.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　竞买人之间、竞买人与拍卖人之间不得恶意串通，损害他人利益。

**Article 37** : Bidders and auctioneers shall not engage in malicious collusion to harm the interests of others.

#### 第四节　买受人

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　买受人是指以最高应价购得拍卖标的的竞买人。

**Article 38** : A buyer means the bidder who purchases the auction subject matter at the highest bid.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　买受人应当按照约定支付拍卖标的的价款，未按照约定支付价款的，应当承担违约责任，或者由拍卖人征得委托人的同意，将拍卖标的再行拍卖。  
拍卖标的再行拍卖的，原买受人应当支付第一次拍卖中本人及委托人应当支付的佣金。再行拍卖的价款低于原拍卖价款的，原买受人应当补足差额。

**Article 39** : The buyer shall pay the price for the auction subject matter in accordance with the agreement. Where the buyer fails to pay the price in accordance with the agreement, it shall bear liability for breach of contract, or the auctioneer may, with the consent of the principal, re-auction the auction subject matter.  
If the auction subject matter is re-auctioned, the original buyer shall pay the commission that should have been paid by itself and the principal in the first auction. If the price obtained in the re-auction is lower than the original auction price, the original buyer shall make up the difference.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　买受人未能按照约定取得拍卖标的的，有权要求拍卖人或者委托人承担违约责任。  
买受人未按照约定受领拍卖标的的，应当支付由此产生的保管费用。

**Article 40** : Where the buyer fails to obtain the auction subject matter in accordance with the agreement, it shall have the right to demand that the auctioneer or the principal bear liability for breach of contract.  
Where the buyer fails to take delivery of the auction subject matter in accordance with the agreement, it shall pay the storage costs incurred thereby.

### 第四章　拍卖程序

#### 第一节　拍卖委托

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　委托人委托拍卖物品或者财产权利，应当提供身份证明和拍卖人要求提供的拍卖标的的所有权证明或者依法可以处分拍卖标的的证明及其他资料。

**Article 41** : When a principal entrusts the auction of an article or a property right, it shall provide identification and, as required by the auctioneer, proof of ownership of the auction subject matter or proof that it may dispose of the auction subject matter according to law, as well as other materials.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　拍卖人应当对委托人提供的有关文件、资料进行核实。拍卖人接受委托的，应当与委托人签订书面委托拍卖合同。

**Article 42** : The auctioneer shall verify the relevant documents and materials provided by the principal. Where the auctioneer accepts the entrustment, it shall sign a written auction entrustment contract with the principal.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　拍卖人认为需要对拍卖标的进行鉴定的，可以进行鉴定。  
鉴定结论与委托拍卖合同载明的拍卖标的状况不相符的，拍卖人有权要求变更或者解除合同。

**Article 43** : Where the auctioneer deems it necessary to have an expert evaluation of the auction subject matter, it may have such evaluation conducted.  
If the conclusions of the expert evaluation are inconsistent with the status of the auction subject matter as stated in the auction entrustment contract, the auctioneer shall have the right to demand modification or dissolution of the contract.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　委托拍卖合同应当载明以下事项：  
（一）委托人、拍卖人的姓名或者名称、住所；  
（二）拍卖标的的名称、规格、数量、质量；  
（三）委托人提出的保留价；  
（四）拍卖的时间、地点；  
（五）拍卖标的交付或者转移的时间、方式；  
（六）佣金及其支付的方式、期限；  
（七）价款的支付方式、期限；  
（八）违约责任；  
（九）双方约定的其他事项。

**Article 44** : An auction entrustment contract shall specify the following matters:  
(1) the name or title and domicile of the principal and the auctioneer;  
(2) the name, specifications, quantity, and quality of the auction subject matter;  
(3) the reserve price proposed by the principal;  
(4) the time and place of the auction;  
(5) the time and method of delivery or transfer of the auction subject matter;  
(6) the commission and the method and time period for its payment;  
(7) the method and time period for payment of the price;  
(8) liability for breach of contract;  
(9) other matters agreed upon by the parties.

#### 第二节　拍卖公告与展示

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　拍卖人应当于拍卖日七日前发布拍卖公告。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-10" title="被《最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定》第10条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 45** : The auctioneer shall issue an auction notice seven days prior to the auction date.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　拍卖公告应当载明下列事项：  
（一）拍卖的时间、地点；  
（二）拍卖标的；  
（三）拍卖标的展示时间、地点；  
（四）参与竞买应当办理的手续；  
（五）需要公告的其他事项。

**Article 46** : An auction notice shall specify the following matters:  
(1) the time and place of the auction;  
(2) the auction subject matter;  
(3) the time and place for display of the auction subject matter;  
(4) the procedures to be completed for participation in the bidding;  
(5) other matters that need to be notified.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　拍卖公告应当通过报纸或者其他新闻媒介发布。

**Article 47** : An auction notice shall be published through newspapers or other news media.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　拍卖人应当在拍卖前展示拍卖标的，并提供查看拍卖标的的条件及有关资料。拍卖标的的展示时间不得少于两日。

**Article 48** : The auctioneer shall display the auction subject matter prior to the auction, and shall provide the conditions for inspecting the auction subject matter and relevant materials. The display period for the auction subject matter shall not be less than two days.

#### 第三节　拍卖的实施

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　拍卖师应当于拍卖前宣布拍卖规则和注意事项。

**Article 49** : The auctioneer shall announce the auction rules and matters of attention prior to the auction.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　拍卖标的无保留价的，拍卖师应当在拍卖前予以说明。  
拍卖标的有保留价的，竞买人的最高应价未达到保留价时，该应价不发生效力，拍卖师应当停止拍卖标的的拍卖。

**Article 50** Where the auction object has no reserve price, the auctioneer shall make an explanation before the auction.  
Where the auction object has a reserve price and the highest bid of the bidders fails to reach the reserve price, such bid shall not be effective, and the auctioneer shall stop the auction of the auction object.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　竞买人的最高应价经拍卖师落槌或者以其他公开表示买定的方式确认后，拍卖成交。

**Article 51** After the highest bid of the bidders is confirmed by the auctioneer's hammer or other publicly announced manner of acceptance, the auction shall be concluded.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　拍卖成交后，买受人和拍卖人应当签署成交确认书。

**Article 52** After the auction is concluded, the buyer and the auctioneer shall sign a confirmation of the transaction.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　拍卖人进行拍卖时，应当制作拍卖笔录。拍卖笔录应当由拍卖师、记录人签名；拍卖成交的，还应当由买受人签名。

**Article 53** When conducting an auction, the auctioneer shall prepare minutes of the auction. The minutes of the auction shall be signed by the auctioneer and the recorder; if the auction is concluded, they shall also be signed by the buyer.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　拍卖人应当妥善保管有关业务经营活动的完整账簿、拍卖笔录和其他有关资料。  
前款规定的账簿、拍卖笔录和其他有关资料的保管期限，自委托拍卖合同终止之日起计算，不得少于五年。

**Article 54** The auctioneer shall properly keep complete account books, auction minutes and other relevant materials concerning its business operation.  
The retention period for the account books, auction minutes and other relevant materials as prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be calculated from the date of termination of the entrustment auction contract, and shall not be less than five years.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　拍卖标的需要依法办理证照变更、产权过户手续的，委托人、买受人应当持拍卖人出具的成交证明和有关材料，向有关行政管理机关办理手续。

**Article 55** Where the auction object requires the procedures for change of certificates and licenses or transfer of property rights to be handled according to law, the entrustor and the buyer shall, by presenting the certificate of transaction and relevant materials issued by the auctioneer, go through the procedures with the relevant administrative authorities.

#### 第四节　佣金

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　委托人、买受人可以与拍卖人约定佣金的比例。  
委托人、买受人与拍卖人对佣金比例未作约定，拍卖成交的，拍卖人可以向委托人、买受人各收取不超过拍卖成交价百分之五的佣金。收取佣金的比例按照同拍卖成交价成反比的原则确定。  
拍卖未成交的，拍卖人可以向委托人收取约定的费用；未作约定的，可以向委托人收取为拍卖支出的合理费用。

**Article 56** The entrustor or the buyer may agree with the auctioneer on the percentage of the commission.  
Where the entrustor, the buyer and the auctioneer have not agreed on the percentage of the commission, and the auction is concluded, the auctioneer may charge each of the entrustor and the buyer a commission not exceeding five percent of the hammer price. The percentage of the commission charged shall be determined according to the principle that it is inversely proportional to the hammer price.  
Where the auction is not concluded, the auctioneer may charge the entrustor such expenses as agreed; if no agreement is reached, the auctioneer may charge the entrustor the reasonable expenses incurred for the auction.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　拍卖[本法第九条](#art-9)规定的物品成交的，拍卖人可以向买受人收取不超过拍卖成交价百分之五的佣金。收取佣金的比例按照同拍卖成交价成反比的原则确定。  
拍卖未成交的，适用[本法第五十六条](#art-56)第三款的规定。

**Article 57** Where the auction of the articles specified in Article 9 of this Law is concluded, the auctioneer may charge the buyer a commission not exceeding five percent of the hammer price. The percentage of the commission charged shall be determined according to the principle that it is inversely proportional to the hammer price.  
Where the auction is not concluded, the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 56 of this Law shall apply.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　委托人违反[本法第六条](#art-6)的规定，委托拍卖其没有所有权或者依法不得处分的物品或者财产权利的，应当依法承担责任。拍卖人明知委托人对拍卖的物品或者财产权利没有所有权或者依法不得处分的，应当承担连带责任。

**Article 58** Where a principal violates the provisions of Article 6 of this Law by consigning for auction goods or property rights he has no ownership or right of disposal according to law, he shall bear liability according to law. Where an auctioneer knows that the principal has no ownership or right of disposal according to law over the goods or property rights consigned for auction, the auctioneer shall bear joint and several liability.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国家机关违反[本法第九条](#art-9)的规定，将应当委托财产所在地的省、自治区、直辖市的人民政府或者设区的市的人民政府指定的拍卖人拍卖的物品擅自处理的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分，给国家造成损失的，还应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 59** Where a state organ, in violation of the provisions of Article 9 of this Law, disposes of goods without authorization that should have been consigned for auction to an auctioneer designated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government or the city divided into districts where the goods are located, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if losses are caused to the state, they shall also bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反[本法第十一条](#art-11)的规定，未经许可从事拍卖业务的，由工商行政管理部门予以取缔，没收违法所得，并可以处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下的罚款。

**Article 60** Where anyone engages in auction business without permission in violation of the provisions of Article 11 of this Law, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall ban it, confiscate its illegal gains, and may impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　拍卖人、委托人违反[本法第十八条](#art-18)第二款、第二十七条的规定，未说明拍卖标的的瑕疵，给买受人造成损害的，买受人有权向拍卖人要求赔偿；属于委托人责任的，拍卖人有权向委托人追偿。  
拍卖人、委托人在拍卖前声明不能保证拍卖标的的真伪或者品质的，不承担瑕疵担保责任。  
因拍卖标的存在瑕疵未声明的，请求赔偿的诉讼时效期间为一年，自当事人知道或者应当知道权利受到损害之日起计算。  
因拍卖标的存在缺陷造成人身、财产损害请求赔偿的诉讼时效期间，适用《中华人民共和国产品质量法》和其他法律的有关规定。

**Article 61** Where an auctioneer or principal violates the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 18 or Article 27 of this Law by failing to state the defects of the subject matter of auction, thereby causing damage to the buyer, the buyer shall have the right to demand compensation from the auctioneer; if it is the liability of the principal, the auctioneer shall have the right to recourse from the principal.  
Where the auctioneer or principal declares before the auction that he cannot guarantee the authenticity or quality of the subject matter of auction, he shall not bear the guaranty liability for defects.  
The limitation of action for claims for compensation due to defects of the subject matter of auction that are not stated shall be one year, counted from the date when the party knows or should have known that his rights are damaged.  
The limitation of action for claims for compensation due to personal injury or property damage caused by defects of the subject matter of auction shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the Product Quality Law of the People's Republic of China and other laws.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　拍卖人及其工作人员违反[本法第二十二条](#art-22)的规定，参与竞买或者委托他人代为竞买的，由工商行政管理部门对拍卖人给予警告，可以处拍卖佣金一倍以上五倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销营业执照。

**Article 62** Where an auctioneer or its staff member, in violation of the provisions of Article 22 of this Law, participates in bidding or commissions another to bid on its behalf, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall give a warning to the auctioneer and may impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the auction commission; if the circumstances are serious, the business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反[本法第二十三条](#art-23)的规定，拍卖人在自己组织的拍卖活动中拍卖自己的物品或者财产权利的，由工商行政管理部门没收拍卖所得。

**Article 63** Where an auctioneer, in violation of the provisions of Article 23 of this Law, auctions its own goods or property rights in an auction activity organized by itself, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall confiscate the auction proceeds.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反[本法第三十条](#art-30)的规定，委托人参与竞买或者委托他人代为竞买的，工商行政管理部门可以对委托人处拍卖成交价百分之三十以下的罚款。

**Article 64** Where a principal, in violation of the provisions of Article 30 of this Law, participates in bidding or commissions another to bid on its behalf, the administrative department for industry and commerce may impose a fine of not more than 30% of the hammer price on the principal.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反[本法第三十七条](#art-37)的规定，竞买人之间、竞买人与拍卖人之间恶意串通，给他人造成损害的，拍卖无效，应当依法承担赔偿责任。由工商行政管理部门对参与恶意串通的竞买人处最高应价百分之十以上百分之三十以下的罚款；对参与恶意串通的拍卖人处最高应价百分之十以上百分之五十以下的罚款。

**Article 65** Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 37 of this Law, bidders collude with each other or a bidder colludes with an auctioneer, thereby causing damage to others, the auction shall be invalid, and compensation liability shall be borne according to law. The administrative department for industry and commerce shall impose a fine of not less than 10% but not more than 30% of the highest bid on the bidders who participated in the malicious collusion; and a fine of not less than 10% but not more than 50% of the highest bid on the auctioneer who participated in the malicious collusion.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本法第四章第四节关于佣金比例的规定收取佣金的，拍卖人应当将超收部分返还委托人、买受人。物价管理部门可以对拍卖人处拍卖佣金一倍以上五倍以下的罚款。

**Article 66** Where an auctioneer charges commission in violation of the provisions on commission rates in Section 4 of Chapter IV of this Law, the auctioneer shall return the excess portion to the principal and the buyer. The price control department may impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the auction commission on the auctioneer.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　外国人、外国企业和组织在中华人民共和国境内委托拍卖或者参加竞买的，适用本法。

**Article 67** This Law shall apply to the consignment for auction or participation in bidding by foreigners, foreign enterprises and organizations within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　本法自1997年1月1日起施行。

**Article 68** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 1997.
