# 最高人民法院关于人民法院认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的补充规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2009〕4号  
**公布日期**：2009-03-30  
**生效日期**：2009-05-14

> 最高人民法院《关于人民法院认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的补充规定》已于2009年3月30日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1465次会议通过，现予公布，自2009年5月14日起施行。 2009年4月24日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院认可台湾地区 有关法院民事判决的补充规定 法释〔2009〕4号 为了更好地解决认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的相关问题，维护当事人的合法权益，现对最高人民法院《关于人民法院认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的规定》（以下简称《规定》）作出补充规定。 第一条  申请人同时提出认可和执行台湾地区有关法院民事判决申请的，人民法院应按规定对认可申请进行审查。 经人民法院裁定认可的台湾地区有关法院民事判决，与人民法院作出的生效判决具有同等效力。申请人依裁定向人民法院申请执行的，人民法院应予受理。 第二条  申请认可的台湾地区有关法院民事判决，包括对商事、知识产权、海事等民事纠纷案件作出的判决。 申请认可台湾地区有关法院民事裁定、调解书、支付令，以及台湾地区仲裁机构裁决的，适用《规定》和本补充规定。 第三条  申请人向两个以上有管辖权的中级人民法院申请认可的，由最先立案的中级人民法院管辖。 申请人向被执行财产所在地中级人民法院申请认可的，应当提供被执行财产存在的相关证据。 第四条  申请人申请认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决，应当提供相关证据，以证明该判决真实并且效力已确定。 第五条  申请人提出认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的申请时，或者在案件受理后、人民法院作出裁定前，可以提出财产保全申请。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  申请人同时提出认可和执行台湾地区有关法院民事判决申请的，人民法院应按规定对认可申请进行审查。  
经人民法院裁定认可的台湾地区有关法院民事判决，与人民法院作出的生效判决具有同等效力。申请人依裁定向人民法院申请执行的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 1** Where an applicant simultaneously files an application for recognition and enforcement of a civil judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region, the People's Court shall examine the application for recognition in accordance with the provisions.  
A civil judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region that has been recognized by the People's Court through a ruling shall have the same legal effect as a judgment that has taken effect rendered by the People's Court. If the applicant files an application for enforcement with the People's Court in accordance with the ruling, the People's Court shall accept the application.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  申请认可的台湾地区有关法院民事判决，包括对商事、知识产权、海事等民事纠纷案件作出的判决。  
申请认可台湾地区有关法院民事裁定、调解书、支付令，以及台湾地区仲裁机构裁决的，适用《规定》和本补充规定。

**Article 2** The civil judgments rendered by relevant courts in the Taiwan region for which recognition is applied for include judgments made in civil dispute cases concerning commercial, intellectual property, maritime and other matters.  
Where an application is filed for recognition of a civil ruling, mediation statement, payment order issued by a relevant court in the Taiwan region, or an award made by an arbitration institution in the Taiwan region, the Provisions and these Supplementary Provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  申请人向两个以上有管辖权的中级人民法院申请认可的，由最先立案的中级人民法院管辖。  
申请人向被执行财产所在地中级人民法院申请认可的，应当提供被执行财产存在的相关证据。

**Article 3** Where an applicant files applications for recognition with two or more Intermediate People's Courts that have jurisdiction, the Intermediate People's Court that first dockets the case shall have jurisdiction.  
Where an applicant files an application for recognition with the Intermediate People's Court at the location of the property subject to enforcement, it shall provide relevant evidence proving the existence of the property subject to enforcement.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  申请人申请认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决，应当提供相关证据，以证明该判决真实并且效力已确定。

**Article 4** An applicant applying for recognition of a civil judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region shall provide relevant evidence to prove that the judgment is authentic and its legal effect has been confirmed.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  申请人提出认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的申请时，或者在案件受理后、人民法院作出裁定前，可以提出财产保全申请。  
申请人申请财产保全的，应当向人民法院提供有效的担保。申请人不提供担保或者提供的担保不符合条件的，驳回其申请。

**Article 5** When an applicant files an application for recognition of a civil judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region, or after the case has been accepted but before the People's Court makes a ruling, the applicant may file an application for property preservation.  
If the applicant applies for property preservation, it shall provide effective security to the People's Court. If the applicant fails to provide security or the security provided does not meet the conditions, the application shall be rejected.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当及时解除财产保全：  
（一）人民法院作出准予财产保全的裁定后，被申请人提供有效担保的；  
（二）人民法院作出认可裁定后，申请人在申请执行期限内不申请执行的；  
（三）人民法院裁定不予认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的；  
（四）申请人撤回保全申请的。  
申请财产保全的其他程序，适用民事诉讼法及相关司法解释的规定。

**Article 6** The People's Court shall promptly dissolve the property preservation under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) After the People's Court makes a ruling granting property preservation, the respondent provides effective security;  
(2) After the People's Court makes a ruling of recognition, the applicant fails to apply for enforcement within the time limit for applying for enforcement;  
(3) The People's Court rules not to recognize the civil judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region;  
(4) The applicant withdraws the preservation application.  
For other procedures for applying for property preservation, the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law and relevant judicial interpretations shall apply.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  申请认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的案件，应根据案件的不同类型，由相关民事审判庭的审判人员组成合议庭进行审理。

**Article 7** For cases concerning applications for recognition of civil judgments rendered by relevant courts in the Taiwan region, a collegial panel shall be formed by adjudicators from the relevant civil adjudication divisions based on the different types of cases to conduct the trial.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  人民法院经审查能够确认该判决真实并且效力已确定，且不具有《规定》第九条所列情形的，裁定认可其效力；不能确认的，裁定驳回申请人的申请。

**Article 8** If, after examination, the People's Court can confirm that the judgment is authentic and its legal effect has been confirmed, and none of the circumstances listed in Article 9 of the Provisions exist, it shall rule to recognize its effect; if it cannot confirm, it shall rule to reject the applicant's application.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  申请认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的，应当在该判决效力确定后二年内提出。  
当事人因不可抗拒的事由或者其他正当理由耽误期限而不能提出认可申请的，在障碍消除后的十日内，可以申请顺延期限。

**Article 9** An application for recognition of a civil judgment rendered by a relevant court in the Taiwan region shall be filed within two years after the legal effect of the judgment is confirmed.  
If a party fails to file an application for recognition within the time limit due to force majeure or other legitimate reasons, it may apply for an extension of the time limit within ten days after the obstacle is removed.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  人民法院受理申请人申请后，应当在六个月内审结。

**Article 10** After accepting an applicant's application, the People's Court shall conclude the case within six months.
