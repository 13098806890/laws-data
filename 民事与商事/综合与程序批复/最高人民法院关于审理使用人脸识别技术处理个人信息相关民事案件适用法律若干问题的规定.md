# 最高人民法院关于审理使用人脸识别技术处理个人信息相关民事案件适用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2021〕15号  
**公布日期**：2021-06-08  
**生效日期**：2021-08-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理使用人脸识别技术处理个人信息相关民事案件适用法律若干问题的规定》已于2021年6月8日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1841次会议通过，现予公布，自2021年8月1日起施行。 2021年7月27日 最高人民法院 关于审理使用人脸识别技术处理个人信息 相关民事案件适用法律若干问题的规定 法释〔2021〕15号 为正确审理使用人脸识别技术处理个人信息相关民事案件，保护当事人合法权益，促进数字经济健康发展，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国网络安全法》《中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法》《中华人民共和国电子商务法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律的规定，结合审判实践，制定本规定。 第一条　因信息处理者违反法律、行政法规的规定或者双方的约定使用人脸识别技术处理人脸信息、处理基于人脸识别技术生成的人脸信息所引起的民事案件，适用本规定。 人脸信息的处理包括人脸信息的收集、存储、使用、加工、传输、提供、公开等。 本规定所称人脸信息属于民法典第一千零三十四条规定的“生物识别信息”。 第二条　信息处理者处理人脸信息有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定属于侵害自然人人格权益的行为： （一）在宾馆、商场、银行、车站、机场、体育场馆、娱乐场所等经营场所、公共场所违反法律、行政法规的规定使用人脸识别技术进行人脸验证、辨识或者分析； （二）未公开处理人脸信息的规则或者未明示处理的目的、方式、范围； （三）基于个人同意处理人脸信息的，未征得自然人或者其监护人的单独同意，或者未按照法律、行政法规的规定征得自然人或者其监护人的书面同意； （四）违反信息处理者明示或者双方约定的处理人脸信息的目的、方式、范围等；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　因信息处理者违反法律、行政法规的规定或者双方的约定使用人脸识别技术处理人脸信息、处理基于人脸识别技术生成的人脸信息所引起的民事案件，适用本规定。  
人脸信息的处理包括人脸信息的收集、存储、使用、加工、传输、提供、公开等。  
本规定所称人脸信息属于[民法典第一千零三十四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1034)规定的“生物识别信息”。

**Article 1** These Provisions shall apply to civil cases arising from an information processor's processing of facial information or processing of facial information generated based on facial recognition technology in violation of laws, administrative regulations, or the agreement of both parties.  
Processing of facial information includes the collection, storage, use, processing, transmission, provision, disclosure, etc. of facial information.  
Facial information as used in these Provisions falls within the "biometric information" as defined in Article 1,034 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　信息处理者处理人脸信息有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定属于侵害自然人人格权益的行为：  
（一）在宾馆、商场、银行、车站、机场、体育场馆、娱乐场所等经营场所、公共场所违反法律、行政法规的规定使用人脸识别技术进行人脸验证、辨识或者分析；  
（二）未公开处理人脸信息的规则或者未明示处理的目的、方式、范围；  
（三）基于个人同意处理人脸信息的，未征得自然人或者其监护人的单独同意，或者未按照法律、行政法规的规定征得自然人或者其监护人的书面同意；  
（四）违反信息处理者明示或者双方约定的处理人脸信息的目的、方式、范围等；  
（五）未采取应有的技术措施或者其他必要措施确保其收集、存储的人脸信息安全，致使人脸信息泄露、篡改、丢失；  
（六）违反法律、行政法规的规定或者双方的约定，向他人提供人脸信息；  
（七）违背公序良俗处理人脸信息；  
（八）违反合法、正当、必要原则处理人脸信息的其他情形。

**Article 2** Where an information processor processes facial information under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall find it to be an act infringing upon the personality rights of a natural person:  
(1) Using facial recognition technology for facial verification, identification, or analysis in business premises or public places such as hotels, shopping malls, banks, railway stations, airports, sports venues, entertainment venues, etc., in violation of laws or administrative regulations;  
(2) Failing to disclose the rules for processing facial information or failing to clearly state the purpose, method, and scope of processing;  
(3) When processing facial information based on individual consent, failing to obtain separate consent from the natural person or their guardian, or failing to obtain written consent from the natural person or their guardian in accordance with laws or administrative regulations;  
(4) Violating the purpose, method, scope, etc., of processing facial information as expressly stated by the information processor or agreed by both parties;  
(5) Failing to take appropriate technical measures or other necessary measures to ensure the security of the collected or stored facial information, resulting in the leakage, tampering, or loss of such information;  
(6) Providing facial information to others in violation of laws, administrative regulations, or the agreement of both parties;  
(7) Processing facial information in violation of public order and good customs;  
(8) Other circumstances of processing facial information in violation of the principles of legality, legitimacy, and necessity.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院认定信息处理者承担侵害自然人人格权益的民事责任，应当适用[民法典第九百九十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-998)的规定，并结合案件具体情况综合考量受害人是否为未成年人、告知同意情况以及信息处理的必要程度等因素。

**Article 3** When determining that an information processor shall bear civil liability for infringing upon the personality rights of a natural person, the People's Court shall apply the provisions of Article 998 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and, in light of the specific circumstances of the case, comprehensively consider factors such as whether the victim is a minor, the status of notification and consent, and the necessity of the information processing.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　有下列情形之一，信息处理者以已征得自然人或者其监护人同意为由抗辩的，人民法院不予支持：  
（一）信息处理者要求自然人同意处理其人脸信息才提供产品或者服务的，但是处理人脸信息属于提供产品或者服务所必需的除外；  
（二）信息处理者以与其他授权捆绑等方式要求自然人同意处理其人脸信息的；  
（三）强迫或者变相强迫自然人同意处理其人脸信息的其他情形。

**Article 4** Under any of the following circumstances, if an information processor defends on the ground that it has obtained the consent of the natural person or their guardian, the People's Court shall not support such defense:  
(1) The information processor requires the natural person to consent to the processing of their facial information before providing products or services, unless the processing of facial information is necessary for providing such products or services;  
(2) The information processor requires the natural person to consent to the processing of their facial information by bundling it with other authorizations, or by other means;  
(3) Other circumstances where the natural person is forced or constructively forced to consent to the processing of their facial information.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　有下列情形之一，信息处理者主张其不承担民事责任的，人民法院依法予以支持：  
（一）为应对突发公共卫生事件，或者紧急情况下为保护自然人的生命健康和财产安全所必需而处理人脸信息的；  
（二）为维护公共安全，依据国家有关规定在公共场所使用人脸识别技术的；  
（三）为公共利益实施新闻报道、舆论监督等行为在合理的范围内处理人脸信息的；  
（四）在自然人或者其监护人同意的范围内合理处理人脸信息的；  
（五）符合法律、行政法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 5** Under any of the following circumstances, if an information processor claims that it shall not bear civil liability, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law:  
(1) Processing of facial information is necessary for responding to a public health emergency, or for protecting the life, health, and property safety of a natural person in an emergency;  
(2) Use of facial recognition technology in public places is for maintaining public security in accordance with relevant national regulations;  
(3) Processing of facial information is within a reasonable scope for carrying out acts such as news reporting or public opinion supervision for the public interest;  
(4) Reasonable processing of facial information within the scope of consent given by the natural person or their guardian;  
(5) Other circumstances that comply with laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　当事人请求信息处理者承担民事责任的，人民法院应当依据[民事诉讼法第六十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-64)及《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》第九十条、第九十一条，《最高人民法院关于民事诉讼证据的若干规定》的相关规定确定双方当事人的举证责任。  
信息处理者主张其行为符合[民法典第一千零三十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1035)第一款规定情形的，应当就此所依据的事实承担举证责任。  
信息处理者主张其不承担民事责任的，应当就其行为符合[本规定第五条](#art-5)规定的情形承担举证责任。

**Article 6** Where a party requests that an information processor bear civil liability, the People's Court shall determine the burden of adducing evidence for both parties in accordance with Article 64 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, Articles 90 and 91 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and the relevant provisions of the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Evidence in Civil Proceedings.  
Where an information processor claims that its conduct complies with the circumstances set forth in the first paragraph of Article 1,035 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, it shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the facts on which such claim is based.  
Where an information processor claims that it shall not bear civil liability, it shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that its conduct falls within the circumstances provided in Article 5 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　多个信息处理者处理人脸信息侵害自然人人格权益，该自然人主张多个信息处理者按照过错程度和造成损害结果的大小承担侵权责任的，人民法院依法予以支持；符合[民法典第一千一百六十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1168)、第一千一百六十九条第一款、第一千一百七十条、第一千一百七十一条等规定的相应情形，该自然人主张多个信息处理者承担连带责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
信息处理者利用网络服务处理人脸信息侵害自然人人格权益的，适用[民法典第一千一百九十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1195)、第一千一百九十六条、第一千一百九十七条等规定。

**Article 7** Where multiple information processors process facial information and infringe upon the personality rights of a natural person, if the natural person claims that the multiple information processors shall bear tort liability according to the degree of fault and the extent of damage caused, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law; if the circumstances are consistent with the provisions of Articles 1,168, the first paragraph of Article 1,169, Article 1,170, Article 1,171, etc. of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, and the natural person claims that the multiple information processors shall bear joint and several liability, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.  
Where an information processor uses network services to process facial information and infringes upon the personality rights of a natural person, the provisions of Articles 1,195, 1,196, 1,197, etc. of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China shall apply.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　信息处理者处理人脸信息侵害自然人人格权益造成财产损失，该自然人依据[民法典第一千一百八十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1182)主张财产损害赔偿的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
自然人为制止侵权行为所支付的合理开支，可以认定为[民法典第一千一百八十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1182)规定的财产损失。合理开支包括该自然人或者委托代理人对侵权行为进行调查、取证的合理费用。人民法院根据当事人的请求和具体案情，可以将合理的律师费用计算在赔偿范围内。

**Article 8** Where an information processor processes facial information and infringes upon the personality rights of a natural person, causing property damage, if the natural person claims compensation for property damage pursuant to Article 1,182 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.  
The reasonable expenses paid by the natural person to stop the infringement may be determined as property damage under Article 1,182 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China. Reasonable expenses include the reasonable costs incurred by the natural person or their authorized agent in investigating and gathering evidence of the infringement. The People's Court may, based on the party's request and the specific circumstances of the case, include reasonable attorney's fees within the scope of compensation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　自然人有证据证明信息处理者使用人脸识别技术正在实施或者即将实施侵害其隐私权或者其他人格权益的行为，不及时制止将使其合法权益受到难以弥补的损害，向人民法院申请采取责令信息处理者停止有关行为的措施的，人民法院可以根据案件具体情况依法作出人格权侵害禁令。

**Article 9** Where a natural person has evidence that an information processor is using facial recognition technology to commit or is about to commit an act infringing upon the natural person's right to privacy or other personality rights, and failure to stop such act in a timely manner would cause irreparable damage to the natural person's lawful rights and interests, and the natural person applies to the People's Court for an order to stop the relevant act, the People's Court may, based on the specific circumstances of the case, issue an injunction against the infringement of personality rights according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　物业服务企业或者其他建筑物管理人以人脸识别作为业主或者物业使用人出入物业服务区域的唯一验证方式，不同意的业主或者物业使用人请求其提供其他合理验证方式的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
物业服务企业或者其他建筑物管理人存在[本规定第二条](#art-2)规定的情形，当事人请求物业服务企业或者其他建筑物管理人承担侵权责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 10** Where a property service enterprise or other building manager uses facial recognition as the sole method of verification for owners or users of the property to enter and exit the property service area, if an owner or user who does not consent requests that another reasonable verification method be provided, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.  
Where a property service enterprise or other building manager falls under the circumstances provided in Article 2 of these Provisions, and a party requests that the property service enterprise or other building manager bear tort liability, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　信息处理者采用格式条款与自然人订立合同，要求自然人授予其无期限限制、不可撤销、可任意转授权等处理人脸信息的权利，该自然人依据[民法典第四百九十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-497)请求确认格式条款无效的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 11** Where an information processor enters into a contract with a natural person using standard terms and requires the natural person to grant rights to process facial information without time limit, irrevocably, or with unrestricted sub-licensing, if the natural person requests that the standard terms be confirmed as void under Article 497 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　信息处理者违反约定处理自然人的人脸信息，该自然人请求其承担违约责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。该自然人请求信息处理者承担违约责任时，请求删除人脸信息的，人民法院依法予以支持；信息处理者以双方未对人脸信息的删除作出约定为由抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 12** Where an information processor processes a natural person's facial information in violation of the agreement, and the natural person requests that the information processor bear liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support such request according to law. Where the natural person requests the deletion of facial information when requesting the information processor to bear liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support such request according to law; if the information processor defends on the ground that the parties have not agreed on the deletion of facial information, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　基于同一信息处理者处理人脸信息侵害自然人人格权益发生的纠纷，多个受害人分别向同一人民法院起诉的，经当事人同意，人民法院可以合并审理。

**Article 13** Where disputes arise based on the same information processor's processing of facial information infringing upon the personality rights of natural persons, and multiple victims respectively file prosecutions with the same People's Court, with the consent of the parties, the People's Court may consolidate the trials.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　信息处理者处理人脸信息的行为符合[民事诉讼法第五十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-55)、[消费者权益保护法第四十七条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法.md#art-47)或者其他法律关于民事公益诉讼的相关规定，法律规定的机关和有关组织提起民事公益诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 14** Where an information processor's act of processing facial information meets the conditions set forth in Article 55 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, Article 47 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests, or other relevant provisions of laws on civil public interest litigation, if an organ or relevant organization prescribed by law initiates a civil public interest action, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　自然人死亡后，信息处理者违反法律、行政法规的规定或者双方的约定处理人脸信息，死者的近亲属依据[民法典第九百九十四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-994)请求信息处理者承担民事责任的，适用本规定。

**Article 15** After the death of a natural person, if an information processor processes facial information in violation of laws, administrative regulations, or the agreement of both parties, and the close relatives of the deceased request the information processor to bear civil liability under Article 994 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, these Provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本规定自2021年8月1日起施行。  
信息处理者使用人脸识别技术处理人脸信息、处理基于人脸识别技术生成的人脸信息的行为发生在本规定施行前的，不适用本规定。

**Article 16** These Provisions shall come into force on August 1, 2021.  
Where an information processor's act of using facial recognition technology to process facial information or processing facial information generated based on facial recognition technology occurred before the coming into force of these Provisions, these Provisions shall not apply.
