# 最高人民法院关于审理与企业改制相关的民事纠纷案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2003〕1号  
**公布日期**：2003-01-03  
**生效日期**：2003-02-01

> （2002年12月3日最高人民法院审判委员会第1259次会议通过　2003年1月3日最高人民法院公告公布　自2003年2月1日起施行） 为了正确审理与企业改制相关的民事纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》、《中华人民共和国公司法》、《中华人民共和国全民所有制工业企业法》、《中华人民共和国合同法》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律、法规的规定，结合审判实践，制定本规定。 一、案件受理 第一条　人民法院受理以下平等民事主体间在企业产权制度改造中发生的民事纠纷案件： （一）企业公司制改造中发生的民事纠纷； （二）企业股份合作制改造中发生的民事纠纷； （三）企业分立中发生的民事纠纷； （四）企业债权转股权纠纷； （五）企业出售合同纠纷； （六）企业兼并合同纠纷； （七）与企业改制相关的其他民事纠纷。 第二条　当事人起诉符合本规定第一条所列情形，并符合民事诉讼法第一百零八条规定的起诉条件的，人民法院应当予以受理。 第三条　政府主管部门在对企业国有资产进行行政性调整、划转过程中发生的纠纷，当事人向人民法院提起民事诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。 二、企业公司制改造 第四条　国有企业依公司法整体改造为国有独资有限责任公司的，原企业的债务，由改造后的有限责任公司承担。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院受理以下平等民事主体间在企业产权制度改造中发生的民事纠纷案件：  
（一）企业公司制改造中发生的民事纠纷；  
（二）企业股份合作制改造中发生的民事纠纷；  
（三）企业分立中发生的民事纠纷；  
（四）企业债权转股权纠纷；  
（五）企业出售合同纠纷；  
（六）企业兼并合同纠纷；  
（七）与企业改制相关的其他民事纠纷。

**Article 1** The People's Court shall accept the following civil dispute cases arising between equal civil subjects in the restructuring of enterprise property rights system:  
(1) civil disputes arising in the corporate restructuring of enterprises;  
(2) civil disputes arising in the joint-stock cooperative restructuring of enterprises;  
(3) civil disputes arising in the division of enterprises;  
(4) disputes over debt-to-equity conversion of enterprises;  
(5) disputes over contracts for the sale of enterprises;  
(6) disputes over contracts for the merger of enterprises;  
(7) other civil disputes relating to the restructuring of enterprises.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人起诉符合[本规定第一条](#art-1)所列情形，并符合[民事诉讼法第一百零八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-108)规定的起诉条件的，人民法院应当予以受理。

**Article 2** Where a party's prosecution meets the circumstances listed in Article 1 of these Provisions and satisfies the conditions for prosecution prescribed in Article 108 of the Civil Procedure Law, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　政府主管部门在对企业国有资产进行行政性调整、划转过程中发生的纠纷，当事人向人民法院提起民事诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。  
二、企业公司制改造

**Article 3** Where a dispute arises during the administrative adjustment or transfer of state-owned assets of enterprises by the competent government department, and the party files a civil action with the People's Court, the People's Court shall not accept the case.  
II. Corporate Restructuring of Enterprises

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国有企业依公司法整体改造为国有独资有限责任公司的，原企业的债务，由改造后的有限责任公司承担。

**Article 4** Where a state-owned enterprise is wholly restructured into a wholly state-owned limited liability company in accordance with the Company Law, the debts of the original enterprise shall be borne by the restructured limited liability company.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　企业通过增资扩股或者转让部分产权，实现他人对企业的参股，将企业整体改造为有限责任公司或者股份有限公司的，原企业债务由改造后的新设公司承担。

**Article 5** Where an enterprise, through capital increase and share expansion or transfer of part of its property rights, enables others to acquire equity in the enterprise, and wholly restructures the enterprise into a limited liability company or a company limited by shares, the debts of the original enterprise shall be borne by the newly established company after restructuring.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　企业以其部分财产和相应债务与他人组建新公司，对所转移的债务债权人认可的，由新组建的公司承担民事责任；对所转移的债务未通知债权人或者虽通知债权人，而债权人不予认可的，由原企业承担民事责任。原企业无力偿还债务，债权人就此向新设公司主张债权的，新设公司在所接收的财产范围内与原企业承担连带民事责任。

**Article 6** Where an enterprise uses part of its assets and corresponding debts to form a new company with others, if the creditors recognize the transferred debts, the newly formed company shall bear civil liability; if the transfer of debts is not notified to the creditors, or although notified, the creditors do not recognize it, the original enterprise shall bear civil liability. If the original enterprise is unable to repay the debts and the creditors claim rights against the new company, the new company shall bear joint and several civil liability with the original enterprise to the extent of the assets it has received.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　企业以其优质财产与他人组建新公司，而将债务留在原企业，债权人以新设公司和原企业作为共同被告提起诉讼主张债权的，新设公司应当在所接收的财产范围内与原企业共同承担连带责任。  
三、企业股份合作制改造

**Article 7** Where an enterprise uses its high-quality assets to form a new company with others but leaves the debts with the original enterprise, and the creditors file a lawsuit claiming debts by naming the new company and the original enterprise as co-defendants, the new company shall bear joint and several liability with the original enterprise to the extent of the assets it has received.  
III. Joint-Stock Cooperative Restructuring of Enterprises

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　由企业职工买断企业产权，将原企业改造为股份合作制的，原企业的债务，由改造后的股份合作制企业承担。

**Article 8** Where the employees of an enterprise buy out the property rights of the enterprise and restructure the original enterprise into a joint-stock cooperative enterprise, the debts of the original enterprise shall be borne by the restructured joint-stock cooperative enterprise.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　企业向其职工转让部分产权，由企业与职工共同组建股份合作制企业的，原企业的债务由改造后的股份合作制企业承担。

**Article 9** Where an enterprise transfers part of its property rights to its employees, and the enterprise and the employees jointly form a joint-stock cooperative enterprise, the debts of the original enterprise shall be borne by the restructured joint-stock cooperative enterprise.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　企业通过其职工投资增资扩股，将原企业改造为股份合作制企业的，原企业的债务由改造后的股份合作制企业承担。

**Article 10** Where an enterprise, through investment by its employees for capital increase and share expansion, restructures the original enterprise into a joint-stock cooperative enterprise, the debts of the original enterprise shall be borne by the restructured joint-stock cooperative enterprise.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　企业在进行股份合作制改造时，参照公司法的有关规定，公告通知了债权人。企业股份合作制改造后，债权人就原企业资产管理人（出资人）隐瞒或者遗漏的债务起诉股份合作制企业的，如债权人在公告期内申报过该债权，股份合作制企业在承担民事责任后，可再向原企业资产管理人（出资人）追偿。如债权人在公告期内未申报过该债权，则股份合作制企业不承担民事责任，人民法院可告知债权人另行起诉原企业资产管理人（出资人）。  
四、企业分立

**Article 11** Where an enterprise, when carrying out joint-stock cooperative restructuring, notifies creditors by public announcement in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Company Law. After the joint-stock cooperative restructuring, if a creditor files a lawsuit against the joint-stock cooperative enterprise regarding debts concealed or omitted by the original enterprise's asset manager (contributor), if the creditor has declared the claim within the announcement period, the joint-stock cooperative enterprise may, after bearing civil liability, seek recourse against the original enterprise's asset manager (contributor). If the creditor has not declared the claim within the announcement period, the joint-stock cooperative enterprise shall not bear civil liability, and the People's Court may inform the creditor to file a separate lawsuit against the original enterprise's asset manager (contributor).  
IV. Division of Enterprises

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　债权人向分立后的企业主张债权，企业分立时对原企业的债务承担有约定，并经债权人认可的，按照当事人的约定处理；企业分立时对原企业债务承担没有约定或者约定不明，或者虽然有约定但债权人不予认可的，分立后的企业应当承担连带责任。

**Article 12** Where creditors claim debts against the enterprises after division, if there is an agreement on the assumption of debts of the original enterprise at the time of division, and the creditors have recognized such agreement, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the agreement of the parties; if there is no agreement on the assumption of debts of the original enterprise at the time of division, or the agreement is unclear, or although there is an agreement, the creditors do not recognize it, the enterprises after division shall bear joint and several liability.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　分立的企业在承担连带责任后，各分立的企业间对原企业债务承担有约定的，按照约定处理；没有约定或者约定不明的，根据企业分立时的资产比例分担。  
五、企业债权转股权

**Article 13** After the divided enterprises bear joint and several liability, if there is an agreement among the divided enterprises on the assumption of the debts of the original enterprise, the matter shall be handled according to the agreement; if there is no agreement or the agreement is unclear, the debts shall be apportioned based on the proportion of assets at the time of division.  
V. Debt-to-Equity Conversion of Enterprises

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　债权人与债务人自愿达成债权转股权协议，且不违反法律和行政法规强制性规定的，人民法院在审理相关的民事纠纷案件中，应当确认债权转股权协议有效。  
政策性债权转股权，按照国务院有关部门的规定处理。

**Article 14** Where a creditor and a debtor voluntarily reach a debt-to-equity conversion agreement, and it does not violate the mandatory provisions of laws and administrative regulations, the People's Court, when trying relevant civil dispute cases, shall confirm the validity of the debt-to-equity conversion agreement.  
Policy-based debt-to-equity conversion shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　债务人以隐瞒企业资产或者虚列企业资产为手段，骗取债权人与其签订债权转股权协议，债权人在法定期间内行使撤销权的，人民法院应当予以支持。  
债权转股权协议被撤销后，债权人有权要求债务人清偿债务。

**Article 15** Where a debtor, by means of concealing enterprise assets or falsely listing enterprise assets, defrauds the creditor into signing a debt-to-equity conversion agreement with it, and the creditor exercises the right of rescission within the statutory period, the People's Court shall support it.  
After the debt-to-equity conversion agreement is rescinded, the creditor shall have the right to demand the debtor to discharge the debts.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　部分债权人进行债权转股权的行为，不影响其他债权人向债务人主张债权。  
六、国有小型企业出售

**Article 16** The act of some creditors carrying out debt-to-equity conversion shall not affect other creditors' claims against the debtor.  
VI. Sale of Small State-Owned Enterprises

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　以协议转让形式出售企业，企业出售合同未经有审批权的地方人民政府或其授权的职能部门审批的，人民法院在审理相关的民事纠纷案件时，应当确认该企业出售合同不生效。

**Article 17** Where an enterprise is sold by way of agreement transfer, and the enterprise sale contract has not been approved by the local people's government with approval authority or its authorized functional department, the People's Court, when trying relevant civil dispute cases, shall confirm that the enterprise sale contract does not take effect.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　企业出售中，当事人双方恶意串通，损害国家利益的，人民法院在审理相关的民事纠纷案件时，应当确认该企业出售行为无效。

**Article 18** Where, in the sale of an enterprise, the parties collude maliciously to harm the interests of the State, the People's Court, when trying relevant civil dispute cases, shall confirm that the enterprise sale act is void.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　企业出售中，出卖人实施的行为具有合同法第五十四条规定的情形，买受人在法定期限内行使撤销权的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 19** Where, in the sale of an enterprise, the seller's act falls under the circumstances prescribed in Article 54 of the Contract Law, and the buyer exercises the right of rescission within the statutory period, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　企业出售合同约定的履行期限届满，一方当事人拒不履行合同，或者未完全履行合同义务，致使合同目的不能实现，对方当事人要求解除合同并要求赔偿损失的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 20** Where the performance period stipulated in the enterprise sale contract expires, one party refuses to perform the contract, or fails to fully perform its contractual obligations, thereby frustrating the purpose of the contract, and the other party demands dissolution of the contract and compensation for losses, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　企业出售合同约定的履行期限届满，一方当事人未完全履行合同义务，对方当事人要求继续履行合同并要求赔偿损失的，人民法院应当予以支持。双方当事人均未完全履行合同义务的，应当根据当事人的过错，确定各自应当承担的民事责任。

**Article 21** Where the performance period stipulated in the enterprise sale contract expires, one party fails to fully perform its contractual obligations, and the other party demands continued performance of the contract and compensation for losses, the People's Court shall support it. Where both parties fail to fully perform their contractual obligations, the civil liability each shall bear shall be determined based on the fault of the parties.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　企业出售时，出卖人对所售企业的资产负债状况、损益状况等重大事项未履行如实告知义务，影响企业出售价格，买受人就此向人民法院起诉主张补偿的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 22** Where, at the time of selling the enterprise, the seller fails to fulfill the obligation of truthful disclosure regarding major matters such as the asset-liability status and profit-loss status of the enterprise being sold, thereby affecting the sale price, and the buyer files a lawsuit with the People's Court claiming compensation, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　企业出售合同被确认无效或者被撤销的，企业售出后买受人经营企业期间发生的经营盈亏，由买受人享有或者承担。

**Article 23** Where an enterprise sale contract is confirmed void or rescinded, the operating profits or losses incurred during the period when the buyer operated the enterprise after the sale shall be enjoyed or borne by the buyer.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　企业售出后，买受人将所购企业资产纳入本企业或者将所购企业变更为所属分支机构的，所购企业的债务，由买受人承担。但买卖双方另有约定，并经债权人认可的除外。

**Article 24** After the enterprise is sold, if the buyer incorporates the assets of the purchased enterprise into its own enterprise or changes the purchased enterprise into its subordinate branch, the debts of the purchased enterprise shall be borne by the buyer, unless otherwise agreed by the buyer and seller and recognized by the creditors.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　企业售出后，买受人将所购企业资产作价入股与他人重新组建新公司，所购企业法人予以注销的，对所购企业出售前的债务，买受人应当以其所有财产，包括在新组建公司中的股权承担民事责任。

**Article 25** After the enterprise is sold, if the buyer uses the assets of the purchased enterprise as capital contribution to form a new company with others, and the legal person of the purchased enterprise is cancelled, the buyer shall bear civil liability for the debts of the purchased enterprise before the sale with all its assets, including its equity interests in the newly formed company.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　企业售出后，买受人将所购企业重新注册为新的企业法人，所购企业法人被注销的，所购企业出售前的债务，应当由新注册的企业法人承担。但买卖双方另有约定，并经债权人认可的除外。

**Article 26** After the enterprise is sold, if the buyer re-registers the purchased enterprise as a new enterprise legal person, and the legal person of the purchased enterprise is cancelled, the debts of the purchased enterprise before the sale shall be borne by the newly registered enterprise legal person, unless otherwise agreed by the buyer and seller and recognized by the creditors.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　企业售出后，应当办理而未办理企业法人注销登记，债权人起诉该企业的，人民法院应当根据企业资产转让后的具体情况，告知债权人追加责任主体，并判令责任主体承担民事责任。

**Article 27** After the enterprise is sold, if the enterprise legal person cancellation registration should have been carried out but has not been carried out, and a creditor files a lawsuit against the enterprise, the People's Court shall, based on the specific circumstances after the transfer of enterprise assets, inform the creditor to add the liable entity and order the liable entity to bear civil liability.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　出售企业时，参照公司法的有关规定，出卖人公告通知了债权人。企业售出后，债权人就出卖人隐瞒或者遗漏的原企业债务起诉买受人的，如债权人在公告期内申报过该债权，买受人在承担民事责任后，可再行向出卖人追偿。如债权人在公告期内未申报过该债权，则买受人不承担民事责任。人民法院可告知债权人另行起诉出卖人。

**Article 28** Where, at the time of selling the enterprise, the seller notifies creditors by public announcement in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Company Law. After the enterprise is sold, if a creditor files a lawsuit against the buyer regarding the debts of the original enterprise concealed or omitted by the seller, if the creditor has declared the claim within the announcement period, the buyer may, after bearing civil liability, seek recourse against the seller. If the creditor has not declared the claim within the announcement period, the buyer shall not bear civil liability. The People's Court may inform the creditor to file a separate lawsuit against the seller.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　出售企业的行为具有合同法第七十四条规定的情形，债权人在法定期限内行使撤销权的，人民法院应当予以支持。  
七、企业兼并

**Article 29** Where the act of selling an enterprise falls under the circumstances prescribed in Article 74 of the Contract Law, and the creditor exercises the right of rescission within the statutory period, the People's Court shall support it.  
VII. Merger of Enterprises

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　企业兼并协议自当事人签字盖章之日起生效。需经政府主管部门批准的，兼并协议自批准之日起生效；未经批准的，企业兼并协议不生效。但当事人在一审法庭辩论终结前补办报批手续的，人民法院应当确认该兼并协议有效。

**Article 30** An enterprise merger agreement shall take effect from the date of signature and seal by the parties. Where approval by the competent government department is required, the merger agreement shall take effect from the date of approval; without approval, the enterprise merger agreement shall not take effect. However, if the parties complete the approval procedures before the conclusion of the first-instance trial debate, the People's Court shall confirm that the merger agreement is valid.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　企业吸收合并后，被兼并企业的债务应当由兼并方承担。

**Article 31** After an enterprise absorption merger, the debts of the merged enterprise shall be borne by the merging party.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　企业进行吸收合并时，参照公司法的有关规定，公告通知了债权人。企业吸收合并后，债权人就被兼并企业原资产管理人（出资人）隐瞒或者遗漏的企业债务起诉兼并方的，如债权人在公告期内申报过该笔债权，兼并方在承担民事责任后，可再行向被兼并企业原资产管理人（出资人）追偿。如债权人在公告期内未申报过该笔债权，则兼并方不承担民事责任。人民法院可告知债权人另行起诉被兼并企业原资产管理人（出资人）。

**Article 32** Where an enterprise, when carrying out an absorption merger, notifies creditors by public announcement in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Company Law. After the absorption merger, if a creditor files a lawsuit against the merging party regarding enterprise debts concealed or omitted by the original asset manager (contributor) of the merged enterprise, if the creditor has declared the claim within the announcement period, the merging party may, after bearing civil liability, seek recourse against the original asset manager (contributor) of the merged enterprise. If the creditor has not declared the claim within the announcement period, the merging party shall not bear civil liability. The People's Court may inform the creditor to file a separate lawsuit against the original asset manager (contributor) of the merged enterprise.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　企业新设合并后，被兼并企业的债务由新设合并后的企业法人承担。

**Article 33** After a new-entity merger of enterprises, the debts of the merged enterprise shall be borne by the enterprise legal person after the new-entity merger.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　企业吸收合并或新设合并后，被兼并企业应当办理而未办理工商注销登记，债权人起诉被兼并企业的，人民法院应当根据企业兼并后的具体情况，告知债权人追加责任主体，并判令责任主体承担民事责任。

**Article 34** After an absorption merger or a new-entity merger of enterprises, if the merged enterprise should have carried out industrial and commercial cancellation registration but has not done so, and a creditor files a lawsuit against the merged enterprise, the People's Court shall, based on the specific circumstances after the merger, inform the creditor to add the liable entity and order the liable entity to bear civil liability.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　以收购方式实现对企业控股的，被控股企业的债务，仍由其自行承担。但因控股企业抽逃资金、逃避债务，致被控股企业无力偿还债务的，被控股企业的债务则由控股企业承担。  
八、附　　则

**Article 35** Where control of an enterprise is achieved by way of acquisition, the debts of the controlled enterprise shall still be borne by itself. However, if the controlling enterprise withdraws capital or evades debts, causing the controlled enterprise to be unable to repay its debts, the debts of the controlled enterprise shall be borne by the controlling enterprise.  
VIII. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本规定自2003年2月1日起施行。在本规定施行前，本院制定的有关企业改制方面的司法解释与本规定相抵触的，不再适用。

**Article 36** These Provisions shall come into force as of February 1, 2003. Where any judicial interpretation concerning enterprise restructuring formulated by this Court before these Provisions come into force is inconsistent with these Provisions, it shall no longer be applied.
