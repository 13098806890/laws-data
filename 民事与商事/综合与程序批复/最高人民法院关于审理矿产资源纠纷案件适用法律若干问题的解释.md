# 最高人民法院关于审理矿产资源纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发文字号**：法释〔2026〕2号  
**公布日期**：2026-01-19  
**生效日期**：2026-02-01

> （2025年12月13日最高人民法院审判委员会第1961次会议通过，自2026年2月1日起施行） 为正确审理矿产资源纠纷案件，加强矿产资源和生态环境保护，依法保护当事人合法权益，维护市场秩序和交易安全，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》、《中华人民共和国矿产资源法》等法律的规定，结合审判实际，制定本解释。 第一条　矿业权出让部门作为出让人与受让人签订的矿业权出让合同，当事人请求确认自依法成立之日起生效的，人民法院应予支持，但是法律、行政法规另有规定或者当事人另有约定的除外。 第二条　出让人未按照矿业权出让合同的约定为受让人办理矿业权登记，经催告后在合理期限内仍未办理，或者因出让人原因致使受让人受让矿业权后不能依法取得矿业用地进行勘查、开采，受让人请求解除出让合同的，人民法院应予支持。 受让人未依约缴纳矿业权出让收益致使出让人不能实现合同目的，出让人请求解除出让合同的，人民法院应予支持。 第三条　当事人订立合同对未设置矿业权的矿产资源实施勘查、开采，违反矿产资源法第四条第二款规定的，人民法院应当认定合同无效。 以将来取得的矿业权进行合作勘查、开采或者转让的合同，当事人仅以合作人或者转让人在订立合同时未取得矿业权为由主张合同无效的，人民法院不予支持。 第四条　当事人约定在国家公园等自然保护地区域内勘查、开采矿产资源，违反国家公园法第二十七条、第二十八条等法律、行政法规的强制性规定的，人民法院应当认定合同无效。 第五条　当事人请求确认矿业权转让、出资、抵押或者合作勘查、开采等合同自依法成立之日起生效的，人民法院应予支持，但是国家另有规定、矿业权出让合同或者当事人另有约定的除外。 第六条　矿业权转让、出资合同生效后，矿业权人未依约履行转移矿业权的义务，合同相对人请求其继续履行的，人民法院应予支持。 矿业权人未依约履行转移矿业权的义务，经催告后在合理期限内仍未履行，合同相对人请求解除合同并由矿业权人承担违约责任的，人民法院应予支持。 第七条　矿业权转让合同生效后，转让人又将矿业权转让给第三人并办理了转移登记，受让人请求解除合同、返还已付转让款，并由转让人承担违约责任的，人民法院应予支持。 转让人与第三人恶意串通另行订立矿业权转让合同并办理了转移登记，导致受让人无法取得矿业权，受让人请求确认转让人与第三人订立的合同无效的，人民法院应予支持。 第八条　受让人请求确认自矿业权相关事项记载于矿业权登记簿时取得矿业权的，人民法院应予支持。 矿业权证书与矿业权登记簿记载不一致，当事人请求以矿业权登记簿为准的，除有证据证明矿业权登记簿确有错误外，人民法院应予支持。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　矿业权出让部门作为出让人与受让人签订的矿业权出让合同，当事人请求确认自依法成立之日起生效的，人民法院应予支持，但是法律、行政法规另有规定或者当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 1** Where a party requests confirmation that a mining rights granting contract concluded between the mining rights granting authority as the grantor and the grantee shall take effect from the date of its lawful formation, the People's Court shall uphold such request, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations or otherwise agreed by the parties.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　出让人未按照矿业权出让合同的约定为受让人办理矿业权登记，经催告后在合理期限内仍未办理，或者因出让人原因致使受让人受让矿业权后不能依法取得矿业用地进行勘查、开采，受让人请求解除出让合同的，人民法院应予支持。  
受让人未依约缴纳矿业权出让收益致使出让人不能实现合同目的，出让人请求解除出让合同的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 2** Where the grantor fails to handle the registration of mining rights for the grantee in accordance with the mining rights granting contract, and still fails to do so within a reasonable period after a demand for performance, or where, due to the grantor's reasons, the grantee is unable to obtain the mining land according to law for exploration and mining after acquiring the mining rights, and the grantee requests the dissolution of the granting contract, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where the grantee fails to pay the mining rights granting proceeds as agreed, thereby preventing the grantor from achieving the purpose of the contract, and the grantor requests the dissolution of the granting contract, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　当事人订立合同对未设置矿业权的矿产资源实施勘查、开采，违反[矿产资源法第四条](../../经济、税务与金融/农业、资源与能源/中华人民共和国矿产资源法.md#art-4)第二款规定的，人民法院应当认定合同无效。  
以将来取得的矿业权进行合作勘查、开采或者转让的合同，当事人仅以合作人或者转让人在订立合同时未取得矿业权为由主张合同无效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 3** Where the parties conclude a contract to explore or mine mineral resources on which no mining rights have been established, in violation of Article 4, paragraph 2 of the Mineral Resources Law, the People's Court shall determine the contract to be void.  
Where a contract for cooperative exploration, mining, or transfer is made based on mining rights to be acquired in the future, and a party merely claims that the contract is void on the ground that the cooperator or transferor had not obtained the mining rights at the time of concluding the contract, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　当事人约定在国家公园等自然保护地区域内勘查、开采矿产资源，违反[国家公园法第二十七条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国国家公园法.md#art-27)、第二十八条等法律、行政法规的强制性规定的，人民法院应当认定合同无效。

**Article 4** Where the parties agree to explore or mine mineral resources within the areas of national parks or other nature reserves, in violation of the mandatory provisions of laws or administrative regulations such as Articles 27 and 28 of the National Park Law, the People's Court shall determine the contract to be void.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　当事人请求确认矿业权转让、出资、抵押或者合作勘查、开采等合同自依法成立之日起生效的，人民法院应予支持，但是国家另有规定、矿业权出让合同或者当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 5** Where a party requests confirmation that a contract for transfer, contribution as capital, mortgage, or cooperative exploration or mining of mining rights shall take effect from the date of its lawful formation, the People's Court shall uphold such request, unless otherwise provided by the State, the mining rights granting contract, or otherwise agreed by the parties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　矿业权转让、出资合同生效后，矿业权人未依约履行转移矿业权的义务，合同相对人请求其继续履行的，人民法院应予支持。  
矿业权人未依约履行转移矿业权的义务，经催告后在合理期限内仍未履行，合同相对人请求解除合同并由矿业权人承担违约责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 6** After a contract for transfer or contribution as capital of mining rights takes effect, if the mining rights holder fails to perform its obligation to transfer the mining rights as agreed, and the counterparty requests its continued performance, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
If the mining rights holder fails to perform its obligation to transfer the mining rights as agreed, and still fails to do so within a reasonable period after a demand for performance, and the counterparty requests the dissolution of the contract and the assumption of liability for breach of contract by the mining rights holder, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　矿业权转让合同生效后，转让人又将矿业权转让给第三人并办理了转移登记，受让人请求解除合同、返还已付转让款，并由转让人承担违约责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
转让人与第三人恶意串通另行订立矿业权转让合同并办理了转移登记，导致受让人无法取得矿业权，受让人请求确认转让人与第三人订立的合同无效的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 7** After a mining rights transfer contract takes effect, if the transferor again transfers the mining rights to a third party and completes the transfer registration, and the transferee requests the dissolution of the contract, the return of the paid transfer price, and the assumption of liability for breach of contract by the transferor, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
If the transferor and the third party maliciously collude to conclude another mining rights transfer contract and complete the transfer registration, thereby causing the transferee to be unable to obtain the mining rights, and the transferee requests confirmation that the contract concluded between the transferor and the third party is void, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　受让人请求确认自矿业权相关事项记载于矿业权登记簿时取得矿业权的，人民法院应予支持。  
矿业权证书与矿业权登记簿记载不一致，当事人请求以矿业权登记簿为准的，除有证据证明矿业权登记簿确有错误外，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 8** Where a transferee requests confirmation that it has acquired the mining rights from the time the relevant matters of mining rights are recorded in the mining rights register, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where the mining rights certificate and the mining rights register are inconsistent, and a party requests to rely on the mining rights register, the People's Court shall uphold such request, unless there is evidence proving that the mining rights register is indeed erroneous.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　以矿业权设立抵押，当事人请求确认抵押权自抵押事项记载于矿业权登记簿时设立的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 9** Where a mortgage is established on mining rights, and a party requests confirmation that the mortgage right is established from the time the mortgage matters are recorded in the mining rights register, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　以矿业权设立抵押，债务人不履行到期债务或者发生当事人约定的实现抵押权的情形，抵押权人依照[民事诉讼法第二百零七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-207)、第二百零八条规定申请实现抵押权的，人民法院可以拍卖、变卖矿业权。

**Article 10** Where a mortgage is established on mining rights, and the debtor fails to perform its due obligations or a circumstance agreed by the parties for the realization of the mortgage right occurs, and the mortgagee applies for realization of the mortgage right in accordance with Articles 207 and 208 of the Civil Procedure Law, the People's Court may auction or sell off the mining rights.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　矿业权依法抵押后，因矿产资源被压覆、矿业权被收回等原因导致矿业权消灭，抵押权人请求按照原抵押权的顺位就抵押人获得的保险金、赔偿金或者补偿金等优先受偿或者将该款项予以提存的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 11** After mining rights are lawfully mortgaged, if the mining rights are extinguished due to reasons such as the covering of mineral resources or the withdrawal of mining rights, and the mortgagee requests to be preferentially satisfied in accordance with the original ranking of the mortgage right from the insurance proceeds, compensation, or indemnity obtained by the mortgagor, or to have such amounts deposited, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　越界勘查、开采矿产资源纠纷案件中，当事人因登记的勘查、开采区域重叠或者界限不清发生争议，依法应当由有关机关处理的，人民法院裁定不予受理，并告知当事人向有关机关申请解决；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 12** In disputes over cross-border exploration or mining of mineral resources, where parties dispute due to overlapping or unclear boundaries of the registered exploration or mining areas, and such matters shall be handled by relevant authorities according to law, the People's Court shall rule not to accept the case and inform the parties to apply to the relevant authorities for resolution; if the case has already been accepted, the People's Court shall rule to dismiss the prosecution.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　因越界勘查、开采矿产资源，矿业权人请求侵权人承担停止侵害、排除妨碍、返还财产、赔偿损失等民事责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 13** Where mineral resources are explored or mined across boundaries, and the mining rights holder requests the infringer to assume civil liability such as cessation of infringement, removal of obstruction, return of property, or compensation for losses, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　采矿权人请求侵权人赔偿因越界勘查、开采造成的下列损失的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）侵权人越界勘查、开采获得的矿产品价值；  
（二）侵权行为致使采矿权人按照批准的矿山初步设计、安全设施设计或者开采方案可以采出而无法采出的矿产品价值；  
（三）侵权行为致使采矿权人增加的开采成本、矿区生态修复费用。  
采矿权人请求按照损失发生时的市场价格计算前款规定的矿产品价值的，人民法院应予支持。  
探矿权人请求侵权人赔偿因越界勘查、开采增加的勘查成本、恢复费用以及依法可以获得的利益损失的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 14** Where a mining right holder requests the infringer to compensate for the following losses caused by cross-border exploration or mining, the People's Court shall uphold such request:  
(1) The value of mineral products obtained by the infringer from cross-border exploration or mining;  
(2) The value of mineral products that the mining right holder could have extracted but failed to extract in accordance with the approved preliminary mine design, safety facility design, or mining plan due to the infringing act;  
(3) The increased mining costs and ecological restoration costs of the mining area incurred by the mining right holder due to the infringing act.  
Where the mining right holder requests the calculation of the value of mineral products under the preceding paragraph based on the market price at the time of the loss, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where a prospecting right holder requests the infringer to compensate for the increased exploration costs, restoration costs, and losses of benefits lawfully obtainable due to cross-border exploration or mining, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　下列情形之一，人民法院可以认定为[矿产资源法第三十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/农业、资源与能源/中华人民共和国矿产资源法.md#art-32)规定的“压覆矿产资源”：  
（一）建设项目占地范围与依法登记的勘查、开采区域重叠，或者虽不重叠但是依据相关规定建设项目周边一定范围内禁止勘查、开采，直接影响矿业权行使的；  
（二）建设项目周边一定范围内限制勘查、开采，依据相关规定矿业权人勘查、开采前需取得建设项目权利人的同意或者相关行政主管部门的批准，但是在合理期限内未取得同意或者批准的。

**Article 15** Any of the following circumstances may be determined by the People's Court as "covering of mineral resources" as provided in Article 32 of the Mineral Resources Law:  
(1) The land occupation scope of a construction project overlaps with the lawfully registered exploration or mining area, or, although not overlapping, exploration or mining is prohibited within a certain range around the construction project according to relevant regulations, directly affecting the exercise of mining rights;  
(2) Exploration or mining is restricted within a certain range around the construction project, and according to relevant regulations, the mining rights holder is required to obtain the consent of the project owner or the approval of the relevant administrative authority before exploration or mining, but fails to obtain such consent or approval within a reasonable period.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　建设单位与矿业权人签订压覆矿产资源补偿协议后未履行约定义务，矿业权人请求建设单位继续履行并承担违约责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 16** Where a construction entity signs a compensation agreement for covering mineral resources with a mining rights holder but fails to perform its agreed obligations, and the mining rights holder requests the construction entity to continue performance and assume liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　建设单位未与矿业权人签订补偿协议即压覆矿产资源，矿业权人请求建设单位承担侵权责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
政府组织实施的能源、交通、水利等涉及公共利益的建设项目，需要压覆审批的已经自然资源主管部门批准，不需要压覆审批的已依法履行项目审批（核准）、规划许可等手续，建设单位未与矿业权人签订补偿协议即压覆矿产资源，矿业权人请求建设单位补偿被压覆矿产资源相应的已缴纳矿业权出让收益、勘查投资、已建开采设施投入及其利息，以及搬迁相应设施费用等损失的，人民法院应予支持。法律、行政法规对压覆矿产资源补偿范围另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 17** Where a construction entity covers mineral resources without signing a compensation agreement with the mining rights holder, and the mining rights holder requests the construction entity to assume tort liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
For construction projects involving public interests such as energy, transportation, and water conservancy implemented by the government, if cover approval is required and has been approved by the natural resources authority, or if cover approval is not required and the project approval (verification), planning permission, and other procedures have been lawfully completed, and the construction entity covers mineral resources without signing a compensation agreement with the mining rights holder, the mining rights holder requests the construction entity to compensate for losses such as the corresponding paid mining rights granting proceeds, exploration investment, investment in established mining facilities and their interest, and the costs of relocating corresponding facilities, the People's Court shall uphold such request. Where laws or administrative regulations have other provisions on the scope of compensation for covering mineral resources, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　当事人对被压覆矿产资源储量有争议的，人民法院应当根据自然资源主管部门压覆审批时的调查评估报告或者经自然资源主管部门评审备案的储量报告予以认定。  
矿业权人自行委托出具的储量报告，未经自然资源主管部门评审备案，建设单位不予认可的，人民法院不予采信。

**Article 18** Where the parties dispute the reserves of covered mineral resources, the People's Court shall determine them based on the investigation and assessment report at the time of the cover approval by the natural resources authority or the reserves report reviewed and filed by the natural resources authority.  
Where a reserves report issued upon the mining rights holder's own entrustment has not been reviewed and filed by the natural resources authority, and the construction entity does not recognize it, the People's Court shall not admit it as evidence.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　矿产资源被压覆时矿业权因期限届满已消灭，原矿业权人基于矿产资源被压覆请求建设单位赔偿或者补偿其损失的，人民法院不予支持，但是有证据证明因建设项目压覆导致矿业权未能续期的除外。

**Article 19** Where the mining rights have been extinguished due to the expiration of their term at the time the mineral resources are covered, and the former mining rights holder requests the construction entity to pay damages or compensation for its losses based on the covering of mineral resources, the People's Court shall not uphold such request, unless there is evidence proving that the mining rights could not be renewed due to the covering by the construction project.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　矿业权在期限届满前因公共利益的需要被依法收回，矿业权人请求作出收回决定的行政机关给予补偿的，人民法院应予支持。  
矿业权在期限届满前因不符合管控要求依法退出自然保护地，矿业权人请求作出退出决定的行政机关给予补偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 20** Where mining rights are lawfully withdrawn before the expiration of their term due to the needs of public interest, and the mining rights holder requests the administrative authority that made the withdrawal decision to grant compensation, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where mining rights exit a nature reserve according to law before the expiration of their term due to non-compliance with control requirements, and the mining rights holder requests the administrative authority that made the exit decision to grant compensation, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　探矿权人按照规定完成勘查区域的清理、恢复，或者采矿权人按照批准的矿区生态修复方案完成矿区生态修复，并已验收合格的，除有新的事实外，国家规定的机关或者法律规定的组织对同一勘查、开采行为造成的生态破坏提起民事公益诉讼，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 21** Where the prospecting right holder completes the cleaning and restoration of the exploration area in accordance with regulations, or the mining right holder completes the ecological restoration of the mining area in accordance with the approved mine ecological restoration plan, and has passed the acceptance inspection, unless there are new facts, the People's Court shall not accept a civil public interest lawsuit filed by an authority prescribed by the State or an organization prescribed by law against the ecological damage caused by the same exploration or mining activity.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　人民法院在审理矿产资源纠纷案件中，发现当事人无证勘查、开采，地质资料造假，或者勘查、开采未履行生态环境保护义务等行为涉嫌违法犯罪的，依法将有关违法犯罪线索、材料移送有关机关处理。

**Article 22** Where, in hearing disputes over mineral resources, the People's Court discovers that a party's conduct such as exploration or mining without a permit, falsification of geological data, or failure to perform ecological environment protection obligations in exploration or mining is suspected of violating the law or constituting a crime, it shall transfer the relevant clues and materials to the relevant authorities for handling according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　2025年7月1日《中华人民共和国矿产资源法》施行前的法律事实引起的矿产资源纠纷案件，适用当时的法律、司法解释的规定，但是法律另有规定的除外。  
本解释自2026年2月1日起施行。《最高人民法院关于审理矿业权纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2017〕12号）同时废止。

**Article 23** Disputes over mineral resources arising from legal facts occurring before July 1, 2025, when the Mineral Resources Law of the People's Republic of China comes into force, shall be governed by the laws and judicial interpretations in effect at that time, unless otherwise provided by law.  
This Interpretation shall take effect on February 1, 2026. The Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Disputes over Mining Rights (Fa Shi [2017] No. 12) shall be abolished simultaneously.
