# 物业管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2018-03-19  
**生效日期**：2018-03-19

> (2003年6月8日中华人民共和国国务院令第379号公布　根据2007年8月26日《国务院关于修改〈物业管理条例〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　根据2018年3月19日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范物业管理活动，维护业主和物业服务企业的合法权益，改善人民群众的生活和工作环境，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称物业管理，是指业主通过选聘物业服务企业，由业主和物业服务企业按照物业服务合同约定，对房屋及配套的设施设备和相关场地进行维修、养护、管理，维护物业管理区域内的环境卫生和相关秩序的活动。 第三条　国家提倡业主通过公开、公平、公正的市场竞争机制选择物业服务企业。 第四条　国家鼓励采用新技术、新方法，依靠科技进步提高物业管理和服务水平。 第五条　国务院建设行政主管部门负责全国物业管理活动的监督管理工作。 县级以上地方人民政府房地产行政主管部门负责本行政区域内物业管理活动的监督管理工作。 第二章　业主及业主大会 第六条　房屋的所有权人为业主。 业主在物业管理活动中，享有下列权利： (一)按照物业服务合同的约定，接受物业服务企业提供的服务； (二)提议召开业主大会会议，并就物业管理的有关事项提出建议； (三)提出制定和修改管理规约、业主大会议事规则的建议； (四)参加业主大会会议，行使投票权；

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范物业管理活动，维护业主和物业服务企业的合法权益，改善人民群众的生活和工作环境，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of regulating property management activities, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of owners and property service enterprises, and improving the living and working environment of the people.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称物业管理，是指业主通过选聘物业服务企业，由业主和物业服务企业按照物业服务合同约定，对房屋及配套的设施设备和相关场地进行维修、养护、管理，维护物业管理区域内的环境卫生和相关秩序的活动。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "property management" means the activities in which the owners, through selecting and engaging a property service enterprise, and the property service enterprise, in accordance with the terms of the property service contract, carry out repair, maintenance and management of the buildings and their supporting facilities, equipment and relevant sites, and maintain the environmental sanitation and relevant order within the property management area.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家提倡业主通过公开、公平、公正的市场竞争机制选择物业服务企业。

**Article 3** The State encourages owners to select property service enterprises through an open, fair and just market competition mechanism.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家鼓励采用新技术、新方法，依靠科技进步提高物业管理和服务水平。

**Article 4** The State encourages the adoption of new technologies and new methods, and relies on scientific and technological progress to improve the level of property management and services.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院建设行政主管部门负责全国物业管理活动的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府房地产行政主管部门负责本行政区域内物业管理活动的监督管理工作。

**Article 5** The competent administrative department of construction under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of property management activities nationwide.  
The competent administrative department of real estate of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of property management activities within its respective administrative region.

### 第二章　业主及业主大会

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　房屋的所有权人为业主。  
业主在物业管理活动中，享有下列权利：  
(一)按照物业服务合同的约定，接受物业服务企业提供的服务；  
(二)提议召开业主大会会议，并就物业管理的有关事项提出建议；  
(三)提出制定和修改管理规约、业主大会议事规则的建议；  
(四)参加业主大会会议，行使投票权；  
(五)选举业主委员会成员，并享有被选举权；  
(六)监督业主委员会的工作；  
(七)监督物业服务企业履行物业服务合同；  
(八)对物业共用部位、共用设施设备和相关场地使用情况享有知情权和监督权；  
(九)监督物业共用部位、共用设施设备专项维修资金(以下简称专项维修资金)的管理和使用；  
(十)法律、法规规定的其他权利。

**Article 6** The owner of a building shall be the owner.  
In property management activities, the owner shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) to accept the services provided by the property service enterprise in accordance with the terms of the property service contract;  
(2) to propose the convening of an owners' general meeting and to make suggestions on relevant matters of property management;  
(3) to propose suggestions on the formulation and amendment of the management rules and the rules of procedure for the owners' general meeting;  
(4) to attend the owners' general meeting and exercise the right to vote;  
(5) to elect members of the owners' committee and to enjoy the right to be elected;  
(6) to supervise the work of the owners' committee;  
(7) to supervise the property service enterprise in performing the property service contract;  
(8) to have the right to know and supervise the use of the common parts, common facilities and equipment and relevant sites of the property;  
(9) to supervise the management and use of the special maintenance fund for the common parts, common facilities and equipment of the property (hereinafter referred to as the special maintenance fund); and  
(10) other rights provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　业主在物业管理活动中，履行下列义务：  
(一)遵守管理规约、业主大会议事规则；  
(二)遵守物业管理区域内物业共用部位和共用设施设备的使用、公共秩序和环境卫生的维护等方面的规章制度；  
(三)执行业主大会的决定和业主大会授权业主委员会作出的决定；  
(四)按照国家有关规定交纳专项维修资金；  
(五)按时交纳物业服务费用；  
(六)法律、法规规定的其他义务。

**Article 7** In property management activities, an owner shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) to comply with the management rules and the rules of procedure for the owners' general meeting;  
(2) to comply with the rules and regulations regarding the use of the common parts and common facilities and equipment of the property within the property management area, and the maintenance of public order and environmental sanitation;  
(3) to implement the decisions of the owners' general meeting and the decisions made by the owners' committee as authorized by the owners' general meeting;  
(4) to pay the special maintenance fund in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(5) to pay the property service fees on time; and  
(6) other obligations provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　物业管理区域内全体业主组成业主大会。  
业主大会应当代表和维护物业管理区域内全体业主在物业管理活动中的合法权益。

**Article 8** All owners within a property management area shall form the owners' general meeting.  
The owners' general meeting shall represent and protect the legitimate rights and interests of all owners within the property management area in property management activities.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　一个物业管理区域成立一个业主大会。  
物业管理区域的划分应当考虑物业的共用设施设备、建筑物规模、社区建设等因素。具体办法由省、自治区、直辖市制定。

**Article 9** One owners' general meeting shall be established for one property management area.  
The division of property management areas shall take into account factors such as the common facilities and equipment of the property, the scale of the buildings, and community construction. Specific measures shall be formulated by the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　同一个物业管理区域内的业主，应当在物业所在地的区、县人民政府房地产行政主管部门或者街道办事处、乡镇人民政府的指导下成立业主大会，并选举产生业主委员会。但是，只有一个业主的，或者业主人数较少且经全体业主一致同意，决定不成立业主大会的，由业主共同履行业主大会、业主委员会职责。

**Article 10** Owners within the same property management area shall, under the guidance of the competent administrative department of real estate of the district or county people's government at the locality of the property or the sub-district office or the people's government of the town or township, establish the owners' general meeting and elect the owners' committee. However, where there is only one owner, or where the number of owners is small and all owners unanimously agree not to establish an owners' general meeting, the owners shall jointly perform the duties of the owners' general meeting and the owners' committee.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　下列事项由业主共同决定：  
(一)制定和修改业主大会议事规则；  
(二)制定和修改管理规约；  
(三)选举业主委员会或者更换业主委员会成员；  
(四)选聘和解聘物业服务企业；  
(五)筹集和使用专项维修资金；  
(六)改建、重建建筑物及其附属设施；  
(七)有关共有和共同管理权利的其他重大事项。

**Article 11** The following matters shall be jointly decided by the owners:  
(1) the formulation and amendment of the rules of procedure for the owners' general meeting;  
(2) the formulation and amendment of the management rules;  
(3) the election of the owners' committee or the replacement of members of the owners' committee;  
(4) the selection and engagement or dismissal of a property service enterprise;  
(5) the raising and use of the special maintenance fund;  
(6) the alteration or reconstruction of buildings and their ancillary facilities; and  
(7) other significant matters concerning the common rights and joint management rights.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　业主大会会议可以采用集体讨论的形式，也可以采用书面征求意见的形式；但是，应当有物业管理区域内专有部分占建筑物总面积过半数的业主且占总人数过半数的业主参加。  
业主可以委托代理人参加业主大会会议。  
业主大会决定[本条例第十一条](#art-11)第(五)项和第(六)项规定的事项，应当经专有部分占建筑物总面积2/3以上的业主且占总人数2/3以上的业主同意；决定[本条例第十一条](#art-11)规定的其他事项，应当经专有部分占建筑物总面积过半数的业主且占总人数过半数的业主同意。  
业主大会或者业主委员会的决定，对业主具有约束力。  
业主大会或者业主委员会作出的决定侵害业主合法权益的，受侵害的业主可以请求人民法院予以撤销。

**Article 12** The owners' general meeting may be held in the form of collective discussion or in the form of soliciting opinions in writing; however, it shall be attended by owners holding more than half of the total area of the exclusive parts of the buildings within the property management area and representing more than half of the total number of owners.  
An owner may entrust an authorized agent to attend the owners' general meeting.  
The decision of the owners' general meeting on the matters specified in Article 11, items (5) and (6) of these Regulations shall be approved by owners holding at least two-thirds of the total area of the exclusive parts of the buildings and representing at least two-thirds of the total number of owners; the decision on other matters specified in Article 11 of these Regulations shall be approved by owners holding more than half of the total area of the exclusive parts of the buildings and representing more than half of the total number of owners.  
Decisions of the owners' general meeting or the owners' committee shall be binding on the owners.  
Where a decision made by the owners' general meeting or the owners' committee infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of an owner, the aggrieved owner may request the People's Court to revoke it.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　业主大会会议分为定期会议和临时会议。  
业主大会定期会议应当按照业主大会议事规则的规定召开。经20%以上的业主提议，业主委员会应当组织召开业主大会临时会议。

**Article 13** The owners' general meeting shall be divided into regular meetings and extraordinary meetings.  
Regular meetings of the owners' general meeting shall be convened in accordance with the provisions of the rules of procedure for the owners' general meeting. Where more than 20% of the owners propose, the owners' committee shall convene an extraordinary meeting of the owners' general meeting.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　召开业主大会会议，应当于会议召开15日以前通知全体业主。  
住宅小区的业主大会会议，应当同时告知相关的居民委员会。  
业主委员会应当做好业主大会会议记录。

**Article 14** For convening an owners' general meeting, all owners shall be notified at least 15 days before the meeting is held.  
The owners' general meeting of a residential community shall also inform the relevant residents committee.  
The owners' committee shall keep records of the owners' general meeting.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　业主委员会执行业主大会的决定事项，履行下列职责：  
(一)召集业主大会会议，报告物业管理的实施情况；  
(二)代表业主与业主大会选聘的物业服务企业签订物业服务合同；  
(三)及时了解业主、物业使用人的意见和建议，监督和协助物业服务企业履行物业服务合同；  
(四)监督管理规约的实施；  
(五)业主大会赋予的其他职责。

**Article 15** The owners' committee shall implement the decisions of the owners' general meeting and perform the following duties:  
(1) to convene the owners' general meeting and report on the implementation of property management;  
(2) to sign the property service contract with the property service enterprise selected and engaged by the owners' general meeting on behalf of the owners;  
(3) to promptly obtain the opinions and suggestions of the owners and property users, and supervise and assist the property service enterprise in performing the property service contract;  
(4) to supervise and manage the implementation of the management rules; and  
(5) other duties assigned by the owners' general meeting.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　业主委员会应当自选举产生之日起30日内，向物业所在地的区、县人民政府房地产行政主管部门和街道办事处、乡镇人民政府备案。  
业主委员会委员应当由热心公益事业、责任心强、具有一定组织能力的业主担任。  
业主委员会主任、副主任在业主委员会成员中推选产生。

**Article 16** The owners' committee shall, within 30 days from the date of its election, file a record with the competent administrative department of real estate of the district or county people's government at the locality of the property and the sub-district office or the people's government of the town or township.  
Members of the owners' committee shall be owners who are enthusiastic about public welfare, have a strong sense of responsibility, and possess certain organizational skills.  
The chairman and vice-chairman of the owners' committee shall be elected from among the members of the owners' committee.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　管理规约应当对有关物业的使用、维护、管理，业主的共同利益，业主应当履行的义务，违反管理规约应当承担的责任等事项依法作出约定。  
管理规约应当尊重社会公德，不得违反法律、法规或者损害社会公共利益。  
管理规约对全体业主具有约束力。

**Article 17** The management rules shall, according to law, stipulate matters concerning the use, maintenance and management of the property, the common interests of the owners, the obligations to be performed by the owners, and the liability to be borne for violating the management rules.  
The management rules shall respect social moralities and shall not violate laws or regulations or harm the public interest.  
The management rules shall be binding on all owners.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　业主大会议事规则应当就业主大会的议事方式、表决程序、业主委员会的组成和成员任期等事项作出约定。

**Article 18** The rules of procedure for the owners' general meeting shall stipulate matters such as the deliberation methods of the owners' general meeting, the voting procedures, the composition of the owners' committee and the term of office of its members.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　业主大会、业主委员会应当依法履行职责，不得作出与物业管理无关的决定，不得从事与物业管理无关的活动。  
业主大会、业主委员会作出的决定违反法律、法规的，物业所在地的区、县人民政府房地产行政主管部门或者街道办事处、乡镇人民政府，应当责令限期改正或者撤销其决定，并通告全体业主。

**Article 19** The owners' general meeting and the owners' committee shall perform their duties according to law, and shall not make decisions unrelated to property management or engage in activities unrelated to property management.  
Where the decisions made by the owners' general meeting or the owners' committee violate laws or regulations, the competent administrative department of real estate of the district or county people's government at the locality of the property, or the sub-district office or the people's government of the town or township, shall order correction within a time limit or revoke the decision, and shall notify all owners thereof.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　业主大会、业主委员会应当配合公安机关，与居民委员会相互协作，共同做好维护物业管理区域内的社会治安等相关工作。  
在物业管理区域内，业主大会、业主委员会应当积极配合相关居民委员会依法履行自治管理职责，支持居民委员会开展工作，并接受其指导和监督。  
住宅小区的业主大会、业主委员会作出的决定，应当告知相关的居民委员会，并认真听取居民委员会的建议。

**Article 20** The owners' general meeting and the owners' committee shall cooperate with the public security organs and coordinate with the residents committees to jointly ensure the maintenance of public security and other related work within the property management area.  
Within the property management area, the owners' general meeting and the owners' committee shall actively cooperate with the relevant residents committee in performing its self-governance management duties according to law, support the work of the residents committee, and accept its guidance and supervision.  
Decisions made by the owners' general meeting and the owners' committee of a residential community shall be notified to the relevant residents committee, and their suggestions shall be seriously considered.

### 第三章　前期物业管理

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　在业主、业主大会选聘物业服务企业之前，建设单位选聘物业服务企业的，应当签订书面的前期物业服务合同。

**Article 21** Where a construction entity selects and engages a property service enterprise before the owners or the owners' general meeting select and engage a property service enterprise, it shall conclude a written preliminary property service contract.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　建设单位应当在销售物业之前，制定临时管理规约，对有关物业的使用、维护、管理，业主的共同利益，业主应当履行的义务，违反临时管理规约应当承担的责任等事项依法作出约定。  
建设单位制定的临时管理规约，不得侵害物业买受人的合法权益。

**Article 22** A construction entity shall, before selling the property, formulate provisional management rules and stipulate according to law such matters as the use, maintenance and management of the property, the common interests of the owners, the obligations to be performed by the owners, and the liability to be borne for violating the provisional management rules.  
The provisional management rules formulated by the construction entity shall not infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of the property purchasers.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　建设单位应当在物业销售前将临时管理规约向物业买受人明示，并予以说明。  
物业买受人在与建设单位签订物业买卖合同时，应当对遵守临时管理规约予以书面承诺。

**Article 23** A construction entity shall, before the sale of the property, present the provisional management rules to the property purchaser and explain them.  
The property purchaser shall, when signing the property sale contract with the construction entity, make a written commitment to comply with the provisional management rules.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家提倡建设单位按照房地产开发与物业管理相分离的原则，通过招投标的方式选聘物业服务企业。  
住宅物业的建设单位，应当通过招投标的方式选聘物业服务企业；投标人少于3个或者住宅规模较小的，经物业所在地的区、县人民政府房地产行政主管部门批准，可以采用协议方式选聘物业服务企业。

**Article 24** The State encourages construction entities to select and engage property service enterprises through tendering and bidding in accordance with the principle of separating real estate development from property management.  
A construction entity of a residential property shall select and engage a property service enterprise through tendering and bidding; where there are fewer than three bidders or the residential property is of a small scale, the construction entity may, with the approval of the competent administrative department of real estate of the district or county people's government at the locality of the property, select and engage a property service enterprise by agreement.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　建设单位与物业买受人签订的买卖合同应当包含前期物业服务合同约定的内容。

**Article 25** The sale contract signed between the construction entity and the property purchaser shall include the contents agreed upon in the preliminary property service contract.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　前期物业服务合同可以约定期限；但是，期限未满、业主委员会与物业服务企业签订的物业服务合同生效的，前期物业服务合同终止。

**Article 26** A preliminary property service contract may stipulate a term; however, where a property service contract signed by the owners' committee and the property service enterprise takes effect before the expiration of such term, the preliminary property service contract shall terminate.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　业主依法享有的物业共用部位、共用设施设备的所有权或者使用权，建设单位不得擅自处分。

**Article 27** A construction entity shall not, without authorization, dispose of the ownership or right to use of the common parts, common facilities and equipment of the property that the owners enjoy according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　物业服务企业承接物业时，应当对物业共用部位、共用设施设备进行查验。

**Article 28** When a property service enterprise takes over the property, it shall inspect the common parts, common facilities and equipment of the property.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　在办理物业承接验收手续时，建设单位应当向物业服务企业移交下列资料：  
(一)竣工总平面图，单体建筑、结构、设备竣工图，配套设施、地下管网工程竣工图等竣工验收资料；  
(二)设施设备的安装、使用和维护保养等技术资料；  
(三)物业质量保修文件和物业使用说明文件；  
(四)物业管理所必需的其他资料。  
物业服务企业应当在前期物业服务合同终止时将上述资料移交给业主委员会。

**Article 29** When handling the property takeover and acceptance procedures, the construction entity shall transfer the following materials to the property service enterprise:  
(1) the overall as-built plan, as-built drawings of individual buildings, structures, and equipment, as-built drawings of supporting facilities and underground pipeline works, and other completion acceptance materials;  
(2) technical materials such as those for the installation, use, maintenance and upkeep of facilities and equipment;  
(3) the property quality guarantee documents and property use instruction documents; and  
(4) other materials necessary for property management.  
The property service enterprise shall transfer the above materials to the owners' committee upon the termination of the preliminary property service contract.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　建设单位应当按照规定在物业管理区域内配置必要的物业管理用房。

**Article 30** A construction entity shall, in accordance with the provisions, allocate necessary premises for property management within the property management area.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　建设单位应当按照国家规定的保修期限和保修范围，承担物业的保修责任。

**Article 31** A construction entity shall bear the warranty liability for the property within the warranty period and warranty scope prescribed by the State.

### 第四章　物业管理服务

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　从事物业管理活动的企业应当具有独立的法人资格。  
国务院建设行政主管部门应当会同有关部门建立守信联合激励和失信联合惩戒机制，加强行业诚信管理。

**Article 32** An enterprise engaged in property management activities shall possess the status of an independent legal person.  
The competent administrative department of construction under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments, establish a mechanism for joint encouragement of trustworthiness and joint punishment for dishonesty, and strengthen the integrity management of the industry.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　一个物业管理区域由一个物业服务企业实施物业管理。

**Article 33** One property management area shall be managed by one property service enterprise.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　业主委员会应当与业主大会选聘的物业服务企业订立书面的物业服务合同。  
物业服务合同应当对物业管理事项、服务质量、服务费用、双方的权利义务、专项维修资金的管理与使用、物业管理用房、合同期限、违约责任等内容进行约定。

**Article 34** The owners' committee shall conclude a written property service contract with the property service enterprise selected and engaged by the owners' general meeting.  
The property service contract shall stipulate such contents as the items of property management, quality of services, service fees, the rights and obligations of both parties, the management and use of the special maintenance fund, premises for property management, the term of the contract, and liability for breach of contract.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　物业服务企业应当按照物业服务合同的约定，提供相应的服务。  
物业服务企业未能履行物业服务合同的约定，导致业主人身、财产安全受到损害的，应当依法承担相应的法律责任。

**Article 35** A property service enterprise shall provide corresponding services in accordance with the terms of the property service contract.  
Where a property service enterprise fails to perform the terms of the property service contract, causing damage to the personal or property safety of the owners, it shall bear corresponding legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　物业服务企业承接物业时，应当与业主委员会办理物业验收手续。  
业主委员会应当向物业服务企业移交[本条例第二十九条](#art-29)第一款规定的资料。

**Article 36** When a property service enterprise takes over the property, it shall handle the property acceptance procedures with the owners' committee.  
The owners' committee shall transfer to the property service enterprise the materials specified in the first paragraph of Article 29 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　物业管理用房的所有权依法属于业主。未经业主大会同意，物业服务企业不得改变物业管理用房的用途。

**Article 37** The ownership of the premises used for property management shall belong to the owners according to law. Without the consent of the owners' general meeting, the property service enterprise shall not change the use of the premises for property management.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　物业服务合同终止时，物业服务企业应当将物业管理用房和[本条例第二十九条](#art-29)第一款规定的资料交还给业主委员会。  
物业服务合同终止时，业主大会选聘了新的物业服务企业的，物业服务企业之间应当做好交接工作。

**Article 38** Upon termination of the property service contract, the property service enterprise shall return the premises for property management and the materials specified in the first paragraph of Article 29 of these Regulations to the owners' committee.  
Upon termination of the property service contract, where the owners' general meeting has selected and engaged a new property service enterprise, the property service enterprises shall properly handle the handover.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　物业服务企业可以将物业管理区域内的专项服务业务委托给专业性服务企业，但不得将该区域内的全部物业管理一并委托给他人。

**Article 39** A property service enterprise may entrust specialized service items within the property management area to specialized service enterprises, but shall not entrust the entire property management within the area to others.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　物业服务收费应当遵循合理、公开以及费用与服务水平相适应的原则，区别不同物业的性质和特点，由业主和物业服务企业按照国务院价格主管部门会同国务院建设行政主管部门制定的物业服务收费办法，在物业服务合同中约定。

**Article 40** Property service fees shall follow the principles of reasonableness, openness, and the fees being commensurate with the service level, and shall be stipulated in the property service contract by the owners and the property service enterprise in accordance with the measures for property service fees formulated by the competent pricing department under the State Council together with the competent administrative department of construction under the State Council, taking into account the different natures and characteristics of the property.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　业主应当根据物业服务合同的约定交纳物业服务费用。业主与物业使用人约定由物业使用人交纳物业服务费用的，从其约定，业主负连带交纳责任。  
已竣工但尚未出售或者尚未交给物业买受人的物业，物业服务费用由建设单位交纳。

**Article 41** An owner shall pay the property service fees in accordance with the terms of the property service contract. Where the owner and the property user have agreed that the property user shall pay the property service fees, such agreement shall prevail, and the owner shall bear the joint and several liability for payment.  
For property that has been completed but has not yet been sold or delivered to the property purchaser, the construction entity shall pay the property service fees.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　县级以上人民政府价格主管部门会同同级房地产行政主管部门，应当加强对物业服务收费的监督。

**Article 42** The competent pricing departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, together with the competent administrative departments of real estate at the same level, shall strengthen the supervision over the charges for property services.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　物业服务企业可以根据业主的委托提供物业服务合同约定以外的服务项目，服务报酬由双方约定。

**Article 43** A property service enterprise may, upon the entrustment of the owners, provide service items not specified in the property service contract, and the service remuneration shall be agreed upon by both parties.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　物业管理区域内，供水、供电、供气、供热、通信、有线电视等单位应当向最终用户收取有关费用。  
物业服务企业接受委托代收前款费用的，不得向业主收取手续费等额外费用。

**Article 44** Within the property management area, units such as water supply, power supply, gas supply, heating, telecommunications, and cable television shall collect relevant fees from the end users.  
Where a property service enterprise accepts the entrustment to collect the fees mentioned in the preceding paragraph, it shall not charge the owners additional fees such as service charges.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　对物业管理区域内违反有关治安、环保、物业装饰装修和使用等方面法律、法规规定的行为，物业服务企业应当制止，并及时向有关行政管理部门报告。  
有关行政管理部门在接到物业服务企业的报告后，应当依法对违法行为予以制止或者依法处理。

**Article 45** A property service enterprise shall stop any acts within the property management area that violate the provisions of laws and regulations concerning public security, environmental protection, property decoration and renovation, and use, and shall promptly report to the relevant administrative department.  
After receiving the report from the property service enterprise, the relevant administrative department shall stop the illegal act according to law or handle it according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　物业服务企业应当协助做好物业管理区域内的安全防范工作。发生安全事故时，物业服务企业在采取应急措施的同时，应当及时向有关行政管理部门报告，协助做好救助工作。  
物业服务企业雇请保安人员的，应当遵守国家有关规定。保安人员在维护物业管理区域内的公共秩序时，应当履行职责，不得侵害公民的合法权益。

**Article 46** A property service enterprise shall assist in the safety and security work within the property management area. When a safety accident occurs, the property service enterprise shall, while taking emergency measures, promptly report to the relevant administrative department and assist in the rescue work.  
Where a property service enterprise employs security personnel, it shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State. When maintaining public order within the property management area, security personnel shall perform their duties and shall not infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of citizens.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　物业使用人在物业管理活动中的权利义务由业主和物业使用人约定，但不得违反法律、法规和管理规约的有关规定。  
物业使用人违反本条例和管理规约的规定，有关业主应当承担连带责任。

**Article 47** The rights and obligations of a property user in property management activities shall be agreed upon by the owner and the property user, but shall not violate the relevant provisions of laws, regulations and the management rules.  
Where a property user violates the provisions of these Regulations and the management rules, the relevant owner shall bear joint and several liability.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上地方人民政府房地产行政主管部门应当及时处理业主、业主委员会、物业使用人和物业服务企业在物业管理活动中的投诉。

**Article 48** The competent administrative department of real estate of the local people's government at or above the county level shall promptly handle complaints from owners, the owners' committee, property users and property service enterprises in property management activities.

### 第五章　物业的使用与维护

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　物业管理区域内按照规划建设的公共建筑和共用设施，不得改变用途。  
业主依法确需改变公共建筑和共用设施用途的，应当在依法办理有关手续后告知物业服务企业；物业服务企业确需改变公共建筑和共用设施用途的，应当提请业主大会讨论决定同意后，由业主依法办理有关手续。

**Article 49** The use of public buildings and common facilities constructed in accordance with the plan within the property management area shall not be changed.  
Where an owner indeed needs to change the use of public buildings or common facilities according to law, he or she shall notify the property service enterprise after lawfully handling the relevant procedures; where a property service enterprise indeed needs to change the use of public buildings or common facilities, it shall submit the matter to the owners' general meeting for discussion and decision, and upon approval, the owners shall lawfully handle the relevant procedures.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　业主、物业服务企业不得擅自占用、挖掘物业管理区域内的道路、场地，损害业主的共同利益。  
因维修物业或者公共利益，业主确需临时占用、挖掘道路、场地的，应当征得业主委员会和物业服务企业的同意；物业服务企业确需临时占用、挖掘道路、场地的，应当征得业主委员会的同意。  
业主、物业服务企业应当将临时占用、挖掘的道路、场地，在约定期限内恢复原状。

**Article 50** Owners and property service enterprises shall not, without authorization, occupy or excavate roads or sites within the property management area, thereby harming the common interests of the owners.  
Where an owner indeed needs to temporarily occupy or excavate roads or sites for the repair of the property or the public interest, he or she shall obtain the consent of the owners' committee and the property service enterprise; where a property service enterprise indeed needs to temporarily occupy or excavate roads or sites, it shall obtain the consent of the owners' committee.  
Owners and property service enterprises shall restore the temporarily occupied or excavated roads or sites to their original condition within the agreed period.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　供水、供电、供气、供热、通信、有线电视等单位，应当依法承担物业管理区域内相关管线和设施设备维修、养护的责任。  
前款规定的单位因维修、养护等需要，临时占用、挖掘道路、场地的，应当及时恢复原状。

**Article 51** Units such as water supply, power supply, gas supply, heating, telecommunications, and cable television shall, according to law, bear the responsibility for the repair and maintenance of relevant pipelines, facilities and equipment within the property management area.  
Where the units specified in the preceding paragraph need to temporarily occupy or excavate roads or sites for repair, maintenance or other needs, they shall promptly restore them to their original condition.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　业主需要装饰装修房屋的，应当事先告知物业服务企业。  
物业服务企业应当将房屋装饰装修中的禁止行为和注意事项告知业主。

**Article 52** Where an owner needs to decorate or renovate a house, he or she shall inform the property service enterprise in advance.  
The property service enterprise shall inform the owner of the prohibited acts and matters needing attention in house decoration and renovation.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　住宅物业、住宅小区内的非住宅物业或者与单幢住宅楼结构相连的非住宅物业的业主，应当按照国家有关规定交纳专项维修资金。  
专项维修资金属于业主所有，专项用于物业保修期满后物业共用部位、共用设施设备的维修和更新、改造，不得挪作他用。  
专项维修资金收取、使用、管理的办法由国务院建设行政主管部门会同国务院财政部门制定。

**Article 53** Owners of residential properties, non-residential properties within a residential community, or non-residential properties structurally connected to a single residential building shall pay the special maintenance fund in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The special maintenance fund belongs to the owners and shall be used exclusively for the repair, renewal and renovation of the common parts, common facilities and equipment of the property after the expiration of the warranty period, and shall not be misappropriated for other purposes.  
Measures for the collection, use and management of the special maintenance fund shall be formulated by the competent administrative department of construction under the State Council together with the competent financial department under the State Council.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　利用物业共用部位、共用设施设备进行经营的，应当在征得相关业主、业主大会、物业服务企业的同意后，按照规定办理有关手续。业主所得收益应当主要用于补充专项维修资金，也可以按照业主大会的决定使用。

**Article 54** Where the common parts, common facilities and equipment of the property are used for business operations, the consent of the relevant owners, the owners' general meeting and the property service enterprise shall be obtained, and the relevant procedures shall be handled in accordance with the provisions. The income obtained by the owners shall be mainly used to supplement the special maintenance fund, or may be used in accordance with the decision of the owners' general meeting.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　物业存在安全隐患，危及公共利益及他人合法权益时，责任人应当及时维修养护，有关业主应当给予配合。  
责任人不履行维修养护义务的，经业主大会同意，可以由物业服务企业维修养护，费用由责任人承担。

**Article 55** Where a property poses a safety hazard that endangers the public interest or the legitimate rights and interests of others, the responsible person shall promptly carry out repair and maintenance, and the relevant owners shall provide cooperation.  
Where the responsible person fails to perform the obligation of repair and maintenance, with the consent of the owners' general meeting, the property service enterprise may carry out the repair and maintenance, and the expenses shall be borne by the responsible person.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本条例的规定，住宅物业的建设单位未通过招投标的方式选聘物业服务企业或者未经批准，擅自采用协议方式选聘物业服务企业的，由县级以上地方人民政府房地产行政主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告，可以并处10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 56** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a construction entity of a residential property fails to select and engage a property service enterprise through tendering and bidding or, without approval, adopts the method of agreement to select and engage a property service enterprise, the competent administrative department of real estate of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction within a time limit, give a warning, and may also impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本条例的规定，建设单位擅自处分属于业主的物业共用部位、共用设施设备的所有权或者使用权的，由县级以上地方人民政府房地产行政主管部门处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；给业主造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 57** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a construction entity disposes of the ownership or right to use of the common parts, common facilities and equipment of the property belonging to the owners without authorization, the competent administrative department of real estate of the local people's government at or above the county level shall impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if losses are caused to the owners, the construction entity shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本条例的规定，不移交有关资料的，由县级以上地方人民政府房地产行政主管部门责令限期改正；逾期仍不移交有关资料的，对建设单位、物业服务企业予以通报，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 58** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the relevant materials are not transferred, the competent administrative department of real estate of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction within a time limit; if the relevant materials are still not transferred upon expiration of the time limit, the construction entity or the property service enterprise shall be notified and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本条例的规定，物业服务企业将一个物业管理区域内的全部物业管理一并委托给他人的，由县级以上地方人民政府房地产行政主管部门责令限期改正，处委托合同价款30%以上50%以下的罚款。委托所得收益，用于物业管理区域内物业共用部位、共用设施设备的维修、养护，剩余部分按照业主大会的决定使用；给业主造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 59** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a property service enterprise entrusts the entire property management within a property management area to another person, the competent administrative department of real estate of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction within a time limit and impose a fine of not less than 30% but not more than 50% of the price of the entrustment contract. The income derived from the entrustment shall be used for the repair and maintenance of the common parts, common facilities and equipment of the property within the property management area, and the remaining part shall be used in accordance with the decision of the owners' general meeting; if losses are caused to the owners, the property service enterprise shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本条例的规定，挪用专项维修资金的，由县级以上地方人民政府房地产行政主管部门追回挪用的专项维修资金，给予警告，没收违法所得，可以并处挪用数额2倍以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员的刑事责任。

**Article 60** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the special maintenance fund is misappropriated, the competent administrative department of real estate of the local people's government at or above the county level shall recover the misappropriated special maintenance fund, give a warning, confiscate the illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not more than double the amount misappropriated; if a crime is constituted, the criminal liability of the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反本条例的规定，建设单位在物业管理区域内不按照规定配置必要的物业管理用房的，由县级以上地方人民政府房地产行政主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 61** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a construction entity fails to allocate necessary premises for property management in the property management area in accordance with the provisions, the competent administrative department of real estate of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction within a time limit, give a warning, confiscate the illegal gains, and also impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反本条例的规定，未经业主大会同意，物业服务企业擅自改变物业管理用房的用途的，由县级以上地方人民政府房地产行政主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；有收益的，所得收益用于物业管理区域内物业共用部位、共用设施设备的维修、养护，剩余部分按照业主大会的决定使用。

**Article 62** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a property service enterprise changes the use of the premises for property management without the consent of the owners' general meeting, the competent administrative department of real estate of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction within a time limit, give a warning, and also impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if there is income, such income shall be used for the repair and maintenance of the common parts, common facilities and equipment of the property within the property management area, and the remaining part shall be used in accordance with the decision of the owners' general meeting.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府房地产行政主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告，并按照本条第二款的规定处以罚款；所得收益，用于物业管理区域内物业共用部位、共用设施设备的维修、养护，剩余部分按照业主大会的决定使用：  
(一)擅自改变物业管理区域内按照规划建设的公共建筑和共用设施用途的；  
(二)擅自占用、挖掘物业管理区域内道路、场地，损害业主共同利益的；  
(三)擅自利用物业共用部位、共用设施设备进行经营的。  
个人有前款规定行为之一的，处1000元以上1万元以下的罚款；单位有前款规定行为之一的，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 63** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, any of the following acts is committed, the competent administrative department of real estate of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction within a time limit, give a warning, and impose a fine in accordance with the provisions of the second paragraph of this Article; the income derived shall be used for the repair and maintenance of the common parts, common facilities and equipment of the property within the property management area, and the remaining part shall be used in accordance with the decision of the owners' general meeting:  
(1) changing the use of public buildings and common facilities constructed in accordance with the plan within the property management area without authorization;  
(2) occupying or excavating roads or sites within the property management area without authorization, thereby harming the common interests of the owners; or  
(3) using the common parts, common facilities and equipment of the property for business operations without authorization.  
Where an individual commits any of the acts specified in the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed; where an entity commits any of the acts specified in the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反物业服务合同约定，业主逾期不交纳物业服务费用的，业主委员会应当督促其限期交纳；逾期仍不交纳的，物业服务企业可以向人民法院起诉。

**Article 64** Where an owner fails to pay the property service fees within the time limit in violation of the terms of the property service contract, the owners' committee shall urge him or her to pay within a time limit; if the owner still fails to pay upon expiration of the time limit, the property service enterprise may file a lawsuit with the People's Court.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　业主以业主大会或者业主委员会的名义，从事违反法律、法规的活动，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 65** Where an owner engages in activities in violation of laws or regulations in the name of the owners' general meeting or the owners' committee, if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if a crime is not constituted, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本条例的规定，国务院建设行政主管部门、县级以上地方人民政府房地产行政主管部门或者其他有关行政管理部门的工作人员利用职务上的便利，收受他人财物或者其他好处，不依法履行监督管理职责，或者发现违法行为不予查处，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 66** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a staff member of the competent administrative department of construction under the State Council, the competent administrative department of real estate of the local people's government at or above the county level, or any other relevant administrative department takes advantage of his or her position to accept property or other benefits from others, fails to perform the supervision and administration duties according to law, or fails to investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered, if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if a crime is not constituted, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　本条例自2003年9月1日起施行。

**Article 67** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2003.
