# 最高人民法院关于检察机关对先予执行的民事裁定提出抗诉人民法院应当如何审理问题的批复

**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：1996-08-08

### 最高人民法院关于检察机关对先予执行的民事裁定提出抗诉人民法院应当如何审理问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于检察机关对先予执行的民事裁定  
  
提出抗诉人民法院应当如何审理问题的批复  
  
法复〔1996〕13号  
  
江西省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于检察机关对先予执行的民事裁定提出抗诉的案件应当如何审理的请示》收悉。经研究认为，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的规定，人民检察院只能对人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定按照审判监督程序提出抗诉。人民法院对其抗诉亦应当按照审判监督程序进行再审。这种监督是案件终结后的“事后监督”。因此，对于人民法院在案件审理过程中作出的先予执行的裁定，因案件尚未审结，不涉及再审，人民检察院提出抗诉，于法无据。如其坚持抗诉，人民法院应以书面通知形式将抗诉书退回提出抗诉的人民检察院。  
  
1996年8月8日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Issues Concerning How the People's Court Shall Adjudicate a Protest Lodged by a Procuratorial Organ Against a Civil Ruling on Advance Execution  
Fa Fu [1996] No. 13  
Higher People's Court of Jiangxi Province:  
Your Court's Request for Instructions on How to Adjudicate a Case Where a Procuratorial Organ Lodges a Protest Against a Civil Ruling on Advance Execution has been received. Upon study, it is held that, pursuant to the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, a People's Procuratorate may only lodge a protest against a legally effective judgment or ruling of a People's Court in accordance with the trial supervision procedure. The People's Court shall also conduct a retrial of the protested case in accordance with the trial supervision procedure. Such supervision is a "post-judgment supervision" after the case is concluded. Therefore, where a People's Court makes a ruling on advance execution in the course of trial, since the case has not yet been finally adjudicated and does not involve a retrial, a protest lodged by a People's Procuratorate has no legal basis. If it insists on lodging a protest, the People's Court shall return the protest document to the People's Procuratorate that lodged it by means of a written notice.  
August 8, 1996
