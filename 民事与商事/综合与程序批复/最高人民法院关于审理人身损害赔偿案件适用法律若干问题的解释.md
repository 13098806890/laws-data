# 最高人民法院关于审理人身损害赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：2020-12-29  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> （2003年12月4日最高人民法院审判委员会第1299次会议通过，根据2020年12月23日最高人民法院审判委员会第1823次会议通过的《最高人民法院关于修改〈最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释〉等二十七件民事类司法解释的决定》修正） 为正确审理人身损害赔偿案件，依法保护当事人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等有关法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条　因生命、身体、健康遭受侵害，赔偿权利人起诉请求赔偿义务人赔偿物质损害和精神损害的，人民法院应予受理。 本条所称“赔偿权利人”，是指因侵权行为或者其他致害原因直接遭受人身损害的受害人以及死亡受害人的近亲属。 本条所称“赔偿义务人”，是指因自己或者他人的侵权行为以及其他致害原因依法应当承担民事责任的自然人、法人或者非法人组织。 第二条　赔偿权利人起诉部分共同侵权人的，人民法院应当追加其他共同侵权人作为共同被告。赔偿权利人在诉讼中放弃对部分共同侵权人的诉讼请求的，其他共同侵权人对被放弃诉讼请求的被告应当承担的赔偿份额不承担连带责任。责任范围难以确定的，推定各共同侵权人承担同等责任。 人民法院应当将放弃诉讼请求的法律后果告知赔偿权利人，并将放弃诉讼请求的情况在法律文书中叙明。 第三条　依法应当参加工伤保险统筹的用人单位的劳动者，因工伤事故遭受人身损害，劳动者或者其近亲属向人民法院起诉请求用人单位承担民事赔偿责任的，告知其按《工伤保险条例》的规定处理。 因用人单位以外的第三人侵权造成劳动者人身损害，赔偿权利人请求第三人承担民事赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。 第四条　无偿提供劳务的帮工人，在从事帮工活动中致人损害的，被帮工人应当承担赔偿责任。被帮工人承担赔偿责任后向有故意或者重大过失的帮工人追偿的，人民法院应予支持。被帮工人明确拒绝帮工的，不承担赔偿责任。 第五条　无偿提供劳务的帮工人因帮工活动遭受人身损害的，根据帮工人和被帮工人各自的过错承担相应的责任；被帮工人明确拒绝帮工的，被帮工人不承担赔偿责任，但可以在受益范围内予以适当补偿。 帮工人在帮工活动中因第三人的行为遭受人身损害的，有权请求第三人承担赔偿责任，也有权请求被帮工人予以适当补偿。被帮工人补偿后，可以向第三人追偿。 第六条　医疗费根据医疗机构出具的医药费、住院费等收款凭证，结合病历和诊断证明等相关证据确定。赔偿义务人对治疗的必要性和合理性有异议的，应当承担相应的举证责任。 医疗费的赔偿数额，按照一审法庭辩论终结前实际发生的数额确定。器官功能恢复训练所必要的康复费、适当的整容费以及其他后续治疗费，赔偿权利人可以待实际发生后另行起诉。但根据医疗证明或者鉴定结论确定必然发生的费用，可以与已经发生的医疗费一并予以赔偿。 第七条　误工费根据受害人的误工时间和收入状况确定。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　因生命、身体、健康遭受侵害，赔偿权利人起诉请求赔偿义务人赔偿物质损害和精神损害的，人民法院应予受理。  
本条所称“赔偿权利人”，是指因侵权行为或者其他致害原因直接遭受人身损害的受害人以及死亡受害人的近亲属。  
本条所称“赔偿义务人”，是指因自己或者他人的侵权行为以及其他致害原因依法应当承担民事责任的自然人、法人或者非法人组织。

**Article 1** Where a compensation obligee initiates a prosecution and requests a compensation obligor to compensate for material damage and mental damage due to infringement upon life, body, or health, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
The term 'compensation obligee' as used in this Article refers to the victim who directly suffers personal injury due to a tort or other injurious cause, and the close relatives of a deceased victim.  
The term 'compensation obligor' as used in this Article refers to a natural person, legal person, or unincorporated organization that shall bear civil liability according to law due to his/her own or another person's tort or other injurious cause.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　赔偿权利人起诉部分共同侵权人的，人民法院应当追加其他共同侵权人作为共同被告。赔偿权利人在诉讼中放弃对部分共同侵权人的诉讼请求的，其他共同侵权人对被放弃诉讼请求的被告应当承担的赔偿份额不承担连带责任。责任范围难以确定的，推定各共同侵权人承担同等责任。  
人民法院应当将放弃诉讼请求的法律后果告知赔偿权利人，并将放弃诉讼请求的情况在法律文书中叙明。

**Article 2** Where a compensation obligee initiates a prosecution against some of the joint tortfeasors, the People's Court shall add the other joint tortfeasors as joint defendants. Where the compensation obligee waives the claim against some of the joint tortfeasors during the litigation, the other joint tortfeasors shall not bear joint and several liability for the compensation share that the defendants against whom the claim is waived shall bear. If the scope of liability is difficult to determine, each joint tortfeasor shall be presumed to bear equal liability.  
The People's Court shall inform the compensation obligee of the legal consequences of waiving the claim, and shall state the circumstances of the waiver of the claim in the legal instrument.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　依法应当参加工伤保险统筹的用人单位的劳动者，因工伤事故遭受人身损害，劳动者或者其近亲属向人民法院起诉请求用人单位承担民事赔偿责任的，告知其按《工伤保险条例》的规定处理。  
因用人单位以外的第三人侵权造成劳动者人身损害，赔偿权利人请求第三人承担民事赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 3** Where a worker of an employing unit that shall participate in the overall planning of work-related injury insurance according to law suffers personal injury due to a work-related injury accident, and the worker or his/her close relatives initiates a prosecution to the People's Court requesting the employing unit to bear civil compensation liability, they shall be informed that the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Work-Related Injury Insurance.  
Where a worker suffers personal injury due to a tort by a third party other than the employing unit, and the compensation obligee requests the third party to bear civil compensation liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　无偿提供劳务的帮工人，在从事帮工活动中致人损害的，被帮工人应当承担赔偿责任。被帮工人承担赔偿责任后向有故意或者重大过失的帮工人追偿的，人民法院应予支持。被帮工人明确拒绝帮工的，不承担赔偿责任。

**Article 4** Where a helper who provides labor gratuitously causes damage to another person while engaging in the helping activity, the helped person shall bear the compensation liability. After the helped person bears the compensation liability, if he/she seeks recourse from the helper who acted with intent or gross negligence, the People's Court shall uphold such claim. If the helped person expressly refuses the help, he/she shall not bear the compensation liability.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　无偿提供劳务的帮工人因帮工活动遭受人身损害的，根据帮工人和被帮工人各自的过错承担相应的责任；被帮工人明确拒绝帮工的，被帮工人不承担赔偿责任，但可以在受益范围内予以适当补偿。  
帮工人在帮工活动中因第三人的行为遭受人身损害的，有权请求第三人承担赔偿责任，也有权请求被帮工人予以适当补偿。被帮工人补偿后，可以向第三人追偿。

**Article 5** Where a helper who provides labor gratuitously suffers personal injury due to the helping activity, the helper and the helped person shall bear corresponding liability according to their respective faults; if the helped person expressly refuses the help, the helped person shall not bear the compensation liability, but may provide appropriate compensation within the scope of benefits.  
Where a helper suffers personal injury due to the act of a third party during the helping activity, the helper shall have the right to request the third party to bear the compensation liability, and also have the right to request the helped person to provide appropriate compensation. After the helped person provides compensation, he/she may seek recourse from the third party.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　医疗费根据医疗机构出具的医药费、住院费等收款凭证，结合病历和诊断证明等相关证据确定。赔偿义务人对治疗的必要性和合理性有异议的，应当承担相应的举证责任。  
医疗费的赔偿数额，按照一审法庭辩论终结前实际发生的数额确定。器官功能恢复训练所必要的康复费、适当的整容费以及其他后续治疗费，赔偿权利人可以待实际发生后另行起诉。但根据医疗证明或者鉴定结论确定必然发生的费用，可以与已经发生的医疗费一并予以赔偿。

**Article 6** Medical expenses shall be determined based on the receipts for medical fees, hospitalization fees, etc. issued by the medical institution, together with relevant evidence such as medical records and diagnosis certificates. If the compensation obligor disputes the necessity and reasonableness of the treatment, he/she shall bear the corresponding burden of adducing evidence.  
The amount of compensation for medical expenses shall be determined based on the amount actually incurred before the conclusion of the court debate at the first instance. For rehabilitation expenses necessary for recovery training of organ functions, appropriate plastic surgery expenses, and other follow-up treatment expenses, the compensation obligee may initiate a separate prosecution after they actually occur. However, expenses that are determined to inevitably occur based on medical certificates or expert evaluation conclusions may be compensated together with the medical expenses already incurred.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　误工费根据受害人的误工时间和收入状况确定。  
误工时间根据受害人接受治疗的医疗机构出具的证明确定。受害人因伤致残持续误工的，误工时间可以计算至定残日前一天。  
受害人有固定收入的，误工费按照实际减少的收入计算。受害人无固定收入的，按照其最近三年的平均收入计算；受害人不能举证证明其最近三年的平均收入状况的，可以参照受诉法院所在地相同或者相近行业上一年度职工的平均工资计算。

**Article 7** Lost work expenses shall be determined based on the victim's lost work time and income status.  
Lost work time shall be determined based on the certificate issued by the medical institution where the victim receives treatment. If the victim suffers continuous lost work time due to disability caused by the injury, the lost work time may be calculated up to the day before the disability evaluation date.  
If the victim has a fixed income, the lost work expenses shall be calculated based on the actually reduced income. If the victim has no fixed income, the expenses shall be calculated based on the average income of the most recent three years; if the victim cannot provide evidence to prove the average income status of the most recent three years, the expenses may be calculated by reference to the average wage of the employees in the same or similar industry in the locality of the accepting court for the previous year.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　护理费根据护理人员的收入状况和护理人数、护理期限确定。  
护理人员有收入的，参照误工费的规定计算；护理人员没有收入或者雇佣护工的，参照当地护工从事同等级别护理的劳务报酬标准计算。护理人员原则上为一人，但医疗机构或者鉴定机构有明确意见的，可以参照确定护理人员人数。  
护理期限应计算至受害人恢复生活自理能力时止。受害人因残疾不能恢复生活自理能力的，可以根据其年龄、健康状况等因素确定合理的护理期限，但最长不超过二十年。  
受害人定残后的护理，应当根据其护理依赖程度并结合配制残疾辅助器具的情况确定护理级别。

**Article 8** Nursing expenses shall be determined based on the income status of the nursing personnel, the number of nursing personnel, and the nursing period.  
If the nursing personnel has income, the expenses shall be calculated by reference to the provisions on lost work expenses; if the nursing personnel has no income or a hired nurse is employed, the expenses shall be calculated by reference to the standard of labor remuneration for nurses engaged in the same level of nursing in the locality. In principle, there shall be one nursing personnel, but if the medical institution or expert evaluation institution has a clear opinion, the number of nursing personnel may be determined by reference thereto.  
The nursing period shall be calculated up to the time when the victim recovers the ability to take care of himself/herself in daily life. If the victim cannot recover the ability to take care of himself/herself due to disability, a reasonable nursing period may be determined based on factors such as the victim's age and health status, but shall not exceed twenty years.  
After the victim's disability evaluation, the level of nursing shall be determined based on the degree of nursing dependence and in combination with the circumstances of fitting disability assistive devices.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　交通费根据受害人及其必要的陪护人员因就医或者转院治疗实际发生的费用计算。交通费应当以正式票据为凭；有关凭据应当与就医地点、时间、人数、次数相符合。

**Article 9** Transportation expenses shall be calculated based on the expenses actually incurred by the victim and his/her necessary accompanying personnel for medical treatment or transfer to another hospital for treatment. Transportation expenses shall be supported by formal receipts; the relevant receipts shall correspond to the place, time, number of persons, and number of trips for medical treatment.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　住院伙食补助费可以参照当地国家机关一般工作人员的出差伙食补助标准予以确定。  
受害人确有必要到外地治疗，因客观原因不能住院，受害人本人及其陪护人员实际发生的住宿费和伙食费，其合理部分应予赔偿。

**Article 10** Hospitalization meal subsidies may be determined by reference to the standard of meal subsidies for ordinary staff of local state organs on business trips.  
If the victim genuinely needs to receive treatment in another locality and cannot be hospitalized due to objective reasons, the accommodation expenses and meal expenses actually incurred by the victim and his/her accompanying personnel shall be compensated for the reasonable portion.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　营养费根据受害人伤残情况参照医疗机构的意见确定。

**Article 11** Nutrition expenses shall be determined based on the victim's disability status and by reference to the opinion of the medical institution.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　残疾赔偿金根据受害人丧失劳动能力程度或者伤残等级，按照受诉法院所在地上一年度城镇居民人均可支配收入或者农村居民人均纯收入标准，自定残之日起按二十年计算。但六十周岁以上的，年龄每增加一岁减少一年；七十五周岁以上的，按五年计算。  
受害人因伤致残但实际收入没有减少，或者伤残等级较轻但造成职业妨害严重影响其劳动就业的，可以对残疾赔偿金作相应调整。

**Article 12** Disability compensation shall be calculated for twenty years from the date of disability evaluation based on the degree of loss of working capacity or the level of disability of the victim, and according to the standard of per capita disposable income of urban residents or per capita net income of rural residents in the locality of the accepting court for the previous year. However, if the victim is sixty years of age or older, one year shall be deducted for each additional year of age; if the victim is seventy-five years of age or older, the disability compensation shall be calculated for five years.  
If the victim's actual income has not decreased due to the disability, or the disability level is light but causes occupational hindrance that seriously affects his/her employment, the disability compensation may be adjusted accordingly.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　残疾辅助器具费按照普通适用器具的合理费用标准计算。伤情有特殊需要的，可以参照辅助器具配制机构的意见确定相应的合理费用标准。  
辅助器具的更换周期和赔偿期限参照配制机构的意见确定。

**Article 13** Expenses for disability assistive devices shall be calculated according to the reasonable cost standard for ordinary and suitable devices. If the injury condition has special needs, the corresponding reasonable cost standard may be determined by reference to the opinion of the assistive device fitting institution.  
The replacement cycle and compensation period of assistive devices shall be determined by reference to the opinion of the fitting institution.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　丧葬费按照受诉法院所在地上一年度职工月平均工资标准，以六个月总额计算。

**Article 14** Funeral expenses shall be calculated based on the standard of the average monthly wage of employees in the locality of the accepting court for the previous year, for a total of six months.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　死亡赔偿金按照受诉法院所在地上一年度城镇居民人均可支配收入或者农村居民人均纯收入标准，按二十年计算。但六十周岁以上的，年龄每增加一岁减少一年；七十五周岁以上的，按五年计算。

**Article 15** Death compensation shall be calculated for twenty years according to the standard of per capita disposable income of urban residents or per capita net income of rural residents in the locality of the accepting court for the previous year. However, if the victim is sixty years of age or older, one year shall be deducted for each additional year of age; if the victim is seventy-five years of age or older, the death compensation shall be calculated for five years.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　被扶养人生活费计入残疾赔偿金或者死亡赔偿金。

**Article 16** Living expenses for dependents shall be included in the disability compensation or death compensation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　被扶养人生活费根据扶养人丧失劳动能力程度，按照受诉法院所在地上一年度城镇居民人均消费性支出和农村居民人均年生活消费支出标准计算。被扶养人为未成年人的，计算至十八周岁；被扶养人无劳动能力又无其他生活来源的，计算二十年。但六十周岁以上的，年龄每增加一岁减少一年；七十五周岁以上的，按五年计算。  
被扶养人是指受害人依法应当承担扶养义务的未成年人或者丧失劳动能力又无其他生活来源的成年近亲属。被扶养人还有其他扶养人的，赔偿义务人只赔偿受害人依法应当负担的部分。被扶养人有数人的，年赔偿总额累计不超过上一年度城镇居民人均消费性支出额或者农村居民人均年生活消费支出额。

**Article 17** Living expenses for dependents shall be calculated based on the degree of loss of working capacity of the supporter, according to the standard of per capita consumption expenditure of urban residents and per capita annual living consumption expenditure of rural residents in the locality of the accepting court for the previous year. If the dependent is a minor, the calculation shall be up to the age of eighteen; if the dependent has no working capacity and no other source of living, the calculation shall be for twenty years. However, if the dependent is sixty years of age or older, one year shall be deducted for each additional year of age; if the dependent is seventy-five years of age or older, the calculation shall be for five years.  
Dependents refer to minors whom the victim shall support according to law, or adult close relatives who have lost their working capacity and have no other source of living. If the dependent has other supporters, the compensation obligor shall only compensate the part that the victim shall bear according to law. If there are several dependents, the total annual compensation amount shall not exceed the per capita consumption expenditure of urban residents or the per capita annual living consumption expenditure of rural residents for the previous year.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　赔偿权利人举证证明其住所地或者经常居住地城镇居民人均可支配收入或者农村居民人均纯收入高于受诉法院所在地标准的，残疾赔偿金或者死亡赔偿金可以按照其住所地或者经常居住地的相关标准计算。  
被扶养人生活费的相关计算标准，依照前款原则确定。

**Article 18** If the compensation obligee provides evidence proving that the per capita disposable income of urban residents or per capita net income of rural residents in his/her domicile or habitual residence is higher than the standard in the locality of the accepting court, the disability compensation or death compensation may be calculated according to the relevant standard of his/her domicile or habitual residence.  
The relevant calculation standard for living expenses for dependents shall be determined in accordance with the principle of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　超过确定的护理期限、辅助器具费给付年限或者残疾赔偿金给付年限，赔偿权利人向人民法院起诉请求继续给付护理费、辅助器具费或者残疾赔偿金的，人民法院应予受理。赔偿权利人确需继续护理、配制辅助器具，或者没有劳动能力和生活来源的，人民法院应当判令赔偿义务人继续给付相关费用五至十年。

**Article 19** Where the determined nursing period, period for payment of assistive device expenses, or period for payment of disability compensation has expired, and the compensation obligee initiates a prosecution to the People's Court requesting continued payment of nursing expenses, assistive device expenses, or disability compensation, the People's Court shall accept the case. If the compensation obligee genuinely needs continued nursing or fitting of assistive devices, or has no working capacity and source of living, the People's Court shall order the compensation obligor to continue paying the relevant expenses for five to ten years.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　赔偿义务人请求以定期金方式给付残疾赔偿金、辅助器具费的，应当提供相应的担保。人民法院可以根据赔偿义务人的给付能力和提供担保的情况，确定以定期金方式给付相关费用。但是，一审法庭辩论终结前已经发生的费用、死亡赔偿金以及精神损害抚慰金，应当一次性给付。

**Article 20** If the compensation obligor requests to pay disability compensation or assistive device expenses by way of periodic payments, he/she shall provide corresponding security. The People's Court may, based on the compensation obligor's payment ability and the provision of security, determine to pay the relevant expenses by way of periodic payments. However, expenses already incurred before the conclusion of the court debate at the first instance, death compensation, and consolation money for mental damage shall be paid in a lump sum.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民法院应当在法律文书中明确定期金的给付时间、方式以及每期给付标准。执行期间有关统计数据发生变化的，给付金额应当适时进行相应调整。  
定期金按照赔偿权利人的实际生存年限给付，不受本解释有关赔偿期限的限制。

**Article 21** The People's Court shall specify in the legal instrument the time, method, and standard of each periodic payment. If the relevant statistical data changes during the enforcement period, the payment amount shall be adjusted accordingly in a timely manner.  
Periodic payments shall be made according to the actual survival period of the compensation obligee, and shall not be subject to the limitation of the compensation period in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本解释所称“城镇居民人均可支配收入”“农村居民人均纯收入”“城镇居民人均消费性支出”“农村居民人均年生活消费支出”“职工平均工资”，按照政府统计部门公布的各省、自治区、直辖市以及经济特区和计划单列市上一年度相关统计数据确定。  
“上一年度”，是指一审法庭辩论终结时的上一统计年度。

**Article 22** The terms 'per capita disposable income of urban residents', 'per capita net income of rural residents', 'per capita consumption expenditure of urban residents', 'per capita annual living consumption expenditure of rural residents', and 'average wage of employees' as used in this Interpretation shall be determined according to the relevant statistical data published by the government statistics department for the previous year for each province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, as well as special economic zones and cities separately listed in the state plan.  
'Previous year' refers to the previous statistical year at the time of conclusion of the court debate at the first instance.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　精神损害抚慰金适用《最高人民法院关于确定民事侵权精神损害赔偿责任若干问题的解释》予以确定。

**Article 23** Consolation money for mental damage shall be determined by applying the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Determination of Compensation Liability for Mental Damage in Civil Torts.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本解释自2004年5月1日起施行。2004年5月1日后新受理的一审人身损害赔偿案件，适用本解释的规定。已经作出生效裁判的人身损害赔偿案件依法再审的，不适用本解释的规定。  
在本解释公布施行之前已经生效施行的司法解释，其内容与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 24** This Interpretation shall come into force as of May 1, 2004. Cases of compensation for personal injury newly accepted at the first instance after May 1, 2004 shall be governed by the provisions of this Interpretation. If a case of compensation for personal injury for which an effective judgment has already been made is subject to a retrial according to law, the provisions of this Interpretation shall not apply.  
If any judicial interpretation that has already taken effect and been implemented before the promulgation and implementation of this Interpretation contains provisions inconsistent with those of this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
