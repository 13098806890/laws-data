# 中华人民共和国民用航空器权利登记条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：1997-10-21  
**生效日期**：1997-10-21

> (1997年10月21日中华人民共和国国务院令第233号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国民用航空法》，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国办理民用航空器权利登记，应当遵守本条例。 第三条　国务院民用航空主管部门主管民用航空器权利登记工作，设立民用航空器权利登记簿，统一记载民用航空器权利登记事项。 同一民用航空器的权利登记事项应当记载于同一权利登记簿中。 第四条　办理民用航空器所有权、占有权或者抵押权登记的，民用航空器权利人应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定，分别填写民用航空器所有权、占有权或者抵押权登记申请书，并向国务院民用航空主管部门提交本条例第五条至第七条规定的相应文件。 办理民用航空器优先权登记的，民用航空器优先权的债权人应当自援救或者保管维护工作终了之日起3个月内，按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定，填写民用航空器优先权登记申请书，并向国务院民用航空主管部门提交足以证明其合法身份的文件和有关债权证明。 第五条　办理民用航空器所有权登记的，民用航空器的所有人应当提交下列文件或者经核对无误的复印件： (一)民用航空器国籍登记证书； (二)民用航空器所有权取得的证明文件； (三)国务院民用航空主管部门要求提交的其他必要的有关文件。 第六条　办理民用航空器占有权登记的，民用航空器的占有人应当提交下列文件或者经核对无误的复印件： (一)民用航空器国籍登记证书； (二)民用航空器所有权登记证书或者相应的所有权证明文件；民用航空器设定抵押的，还应当提供有关证明文件； (三)符合《中华人民共和国民用航空法》第十一条第(二)项或者第(三)项规定的民用航空器买卖合同或者租赁合同；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国民用航空法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Civil Aviation Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国办理民用航空器权利登记，应当遵守本条例。

**Article 2** The registration of rights in civil aircraft in the People's Republic of China shall comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院民用航空主管部门主管民用航空器权利登记工作，设立民用航空器权利登记簿，统一记载民用航空器权利登记事项。  
同一民用航空器的权利登记事项应当记载于同一权利登记簿中。

**Article 3** The competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall be responsible for the registration of rights in civil aircraft, shall establish a register of rights in civil aircraft, and shall uniformly record the particulars of registration of rights in civil aircraft.  
The particulars of registration of rights in the same civil aircraft shall be recorded in the same register of rights.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　办理民用航空器所有权、占有权或者抵押权登记的，民用航空器权利人应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定，分别填写民用航空器所有权、占有权或者抵押权登记申请书，并向国务院民用航空主管部门提交[本条例第五条](#art-5)至第七条规定的相应文件。  
办理民用航空器优先权登记的，民用航空器优先权的债权人应当自援救或者保管维护工作终了之日起3个月内，按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定，填写民用航空器优先权登记申请书，并向国务院民用航空主管部门提交足以证明其合法身份的文件和有关债权证明。

**Article 4** Where registration of ownership, right of possession or mortgage of a civil aircraft is to be effected, the right holder of the civil aircraft shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, fill in the application form for registration of ownership, right of possession or mortgage of the civil aircraft respectively, and submit to the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council the corresponding documents specified in Articles 5 through 7 of these Regulations.  
Where registration of a prior right in a civil aircraft is to be effected, the creditor entitled to the prior right in the civil aircraft shall, within three months from the date of completion of the salvage or preservation and maintenance work, fill in the application form for registration of the prior right in the civil aircraft in accordance with the provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, and submit to the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council documents sufficient to prove its legal identity and relevant evidence of the claim.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　办理民用航空器所有权登记的，民用航空器的所有人应当提交下列文件或者经核对无误的复印件：  
(一)民用航空器国籍登记证书；  
(二)民用航空器所有权取得的证明文件；  
(三)国务院民用航空主管部门要求提交的其他必要的有关文件。

**Article 5** Where registration of ownership of a civil aircraft is to be effected, the owner of the civil aircraft shall submit the following documents or verified true copies thereof:  
(1) the certificate of nationality registration of the civil aircraft;  
(2) the document evidencing the acquisition of ownership of the civil aircraft;  
(3) such other necessary relevant documents as required by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　办理民用航空器占有权登记的，民用航空器的占有人应当提交下列文件或者经核对无误的复印件：  
(一)民用航空器国籍登记证书；  
(二)民用航空器所有权登记证书或者相应的所有权证明文件；民用航空器设定抵押的，还应当提供有关证明文件；  
(三)符合[《中华人民共和国民用航空法》第十一条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国民用航空法.md#art-11)第(二)项或者第(三)项规定的民用航空器买卖合同或者租赁合同；  
(四)国务院民用航空主管部门要求提交的其他必要的有关文件。

**Article 6** Where registration of a right of possession of a civil aircraft is to be effected, the possessor of the civil aircraft shall submit the following documents or verified true copies thereof:  
(1) the certificate of nationality registration of the civil aircraft;  
(2) the certificate of ownership registration of the civil aircraft or the corresponding document evidencing ownership; if the civil aircraft is mortgaged, relevant certifying documents shall also be provided;  
(3) the contract for sale or lease of the civil aircraft that complies with the provisions of Article 11, item (2) or item (3) of the Civil Aviation Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(4) such other necessary relevant documents as required by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　办理民用航空器抵押权登记的，民用航空器的抵押权人和抵押人应当提交下列文件或者经核对无误的复印件：  
(一)民用航空器国籍登记证书；  
(二)民用航空器所有权登记证书或者相应的所有权证明文件；  
(三)民用航空器抵押合同；  
(四)国务院民用航空主管部门要求提交的其他必要的有关文件。

**Article 7** Where registration of a mortgage of a civil aircraft is to be effected, the mortgagee and the mortgagor of the civil aircraft shall submit the following documents or verified true copies thereof:  
(1) the certificate of nationality registration of the civil aircraft;  
(2) the certificate of ownership registration of the civil aircraft or the corresponding document evidencing ownership;  
(3) the mortgage contract for the civil aircraft;  
(4) such other necessary relevant documents as required by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　就两架以上民用航空器设定一项抵押权或者就同一民用航空器设定两项以上抵押权时，民用航空器的抵押权人和抵押人应当就每一架民用航空器或者每一项抵押权分别办理抵押权登记。

**Article 8** Where a single mortgage is created on two or more civil aircraft or two or more mortgages are created on the same civil aircraft, the mortgagee and the mortgagor of the civil aircraft shall separately effect mortgage registration for each civil aircraft or for each mortgage.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院民用航空主管部门应当自收到民用航空器权利登记申请之日起7个工作日内，对申请的权利登记事项进行审查。经审查符合本条例规定的，应当向民用航空器权利人颁发相应的民用航空器权利登记证书，并区别情况在民用航空器权利登记簿上载明[本条例第十条](#art-10)至第十三条规定的相应事项；经审查不符合本条例规定的，应当书面通知民用航空器权利人。

**Article 9** The competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall, within seven working days from the date of receipt of an application for registration of rights in a civil aircraft, examine the particulars for which registration is applied. If, upon examination, the application complies with the provisions of these Regulations, it shall issue the corresponding certificate of registration of rights in the civil aircraft to the right holder, and shall, as the case may be, record in the register of rights in civil aircraft the corresponding particulars specified in Articles 10 through 13 of these Regulations; if, upon examination, the application does not comply with the provisions of these Regulations, it shall notify the right holder of the civil aircraft in writing.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院民用航空主管部门向民用航空器所有人颁发民用航空器所有权登记证书时，应当在民用航空器权利登记簿上载明下列事项：  
(一)民用航空器国籍、国籍标志和登记标志；  
(二)民用航空器所有人的姓名或者名称、地址及其法定代表人的姓名；  
(三)民用航空器为数人共有的，载明民用航空器共有人的共有情况；  
(四)民用航空器所有权的取得方式和取得日期；  
(五)民用航空器制造人名称、制造日期和制造地点；  
(六)民用航空器价值、机体材料和主要技术数据；  
(七)民用航空器已设定抵押的，载明其抵押权的设定情况；  
(八)民用航空器所有权登记日期；  
(九)国务院民用航空主管部门规定的其他事项。

**Article 10** When issuing a certificate of ownership registration of a civil aircraft to the owner of the civil aircraft, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall record the following particulars in the register of rights in civil aircraft:  
(1) the nationality, nationality mark and registration mark of the civil aircraft;  
(2) the name or title, address of the owner of the civil aircraft and the name of its legal representative;  
(3) if the civil aircraft is co-owned by several persons, the particulars of co-ownership among the co-owners of the civil aircraft;  
(4) the manner and date of acquisition of ownership of the civil aircraft;  
(5) the name of the manufacturer of the civil aircraft, the date and place of manufacture;  
(6) the value, airframe material and main technical data of the civil aircraft;  
(7) if the civil aircraft has been mortgaged, the particulars of the creation of the mortgage;  
(8) the date of registration of ownership of the civil aircraft;  
(9) such other particulars as prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院民用航空主管部门向民用航空器占有人颁发民用航空器占有权登记证书时，应当在民用航空器权利登记簿上载明下列事项：  
(一)民用航空器的国籍、国籍标志和登记标志；  
(二)民用航空器占有人、所有人或者出租人的姓名或者名称、地址及其法定代表人的姓名；  
(三)民用航空器占有权的取得方式、取得日期和约定的占有条件；  
(四)民用航空器占有权登记日期；  
(五)国务院民用航空主管部门规定的其他事项。

**Article 11** When issuing a certificate of registration of a right of possession of a civil aircraft to the possessor of the civil aircraft, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall record the following particulars in the register of rights in civil aircraft:  
(1) the nationality, nationality mark and registration mark of the civil aircraft;  
(2) the name or title, address of the possessor, owner or lessor of the civil aircraft and the name of its legal representative;  
(3) the manner and date of acquisition of the right of possession of the civil aircraft and the agreed conditions of possession;  
(4) the date of registration of the right of possession of the civil aircraft;  
(5) such other particulars as prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院民用航空主管部门向民用航空器抵押权人颁发民用航空器抵押权登记证书时，应当在民用航空器权利登记簿上载明下列事项：  
(一)被抵押的民用航空器的国籍、国籍标志和登记标志；  
(二)抵押权人和抵押人的姓名或者名称、地址及其法定代表人的姓名；  
(三)民用航空器抵押所担保的债权数额、利息率、受偿期限；  
(四)民用航空器抵押权登记日期；  
(五)国务院民用航空主管部门规定的其他事项。

**Article 12** When issuing a certificate of registration of a mortgage of a civil aircraft to the mortgagee of the civil aircraft, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall record the following particulars in the register of rights in civil aircraft:  
(1) the nationality, nationality mark and registration mark of the mortgaged civil aircraft;  
(2) the name or title, address of the mortgagee and the mortgagor and the name of their legal representatives;  
(3) the amount of the claim secured by the mortgage of the civil aircraft, the interest rate, and the term for repayment;  
(4) the date of registration of the mortgage of the civil aircraft;  
(5) such other particulars as prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院民用航空主管部门向民用航空器优先权的债权人颁发民用航空器优先权登记证书时，应当在民用航空器权利登记簿上载明下列事项：  
(一)发生债权的民用航空器的国籍、国籍标志和登记标志；  
(二)民用航空器优先权的债权人的姓名或者名称、地址及其法定代表人的姓名；  
(三)发生债权的民用航空器的所有人、经营人或者承租人的姓名或者名称、地址及其法定代表人的姓名；  
(四)民用航空器优先权的债权人主张的债权数额和债权发生的时间、原因；  
(五)民用航空器优先权登记日期；  
(六)国务院民用航空主管部门规定的其他事项。

**Article 13** When issuing a certificate of registration of a prior right in a civil aircraft to the creditor entitled to the prior right in the civil aircraft, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall record the following particulars in the register of rights in civil aircraft:  
(1) the nationality, nationality mark and registration mark of the civil aircraft in respect of which the claim arises;  
(2) the name or title, address of the creditor entitled to the prior right in the civil aircraft and the name of its legal representative;  
(3) the name or title, address of the owner, operator or lessee of the civil aircraft in respect of which the claim arises and the name of its legal representative;  
(4) the amount of the claim asserted by the creditor entitled to the prior right in the civil aircraft and the time and cause of the claim;  
(5) the date of registration of the prior right in the civil aircraft;  
(6) such other particulars as prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　同一民用航空器设定两项以上抵押权的，国务院民用航空主管部门应当按照抵押权登记申请日期的先后顺序进行登记。

**Article 14** Where two or more mortgages are created on the same civil aircraft, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall effect registration in the chronological order of the dates of application for mortgage registration.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　民用航空器权利登记事项发生变更时，民用航空器权利人应当持有关的民用航空器权利登记证书和变更证明文件，向国务院民用航空主管部门办理变更登记。  
民用航空器抵押合同变更时，由抵押权人和抵押人共同向国务院民用航空主管部门办理变更登记。

**Article 15** When any registered particular of rights in a civil aircraft changes, the right holder of the civil aircraft shall, by presenting the relevant certificate of registration of rights in the civil aircraft and the document evidencing the change, effect an alteration registration with the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.  
When a mortgage contract for a civil aircraft is amended, the mortgagee and the mortgagor shall jointly effect an alteration registration with the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院民用航空主管部门应当自收到民用航空器权利变更登记申请之日起7个工作日内，对申请的权利变更登记事项进行审查。经审查符合本条例规定的，在有关权利登记证书和民用航空器权利登记簿上注明变更事项；经审查不符合本条例规定的，应当书面通知民用航空器权利人。

**Article 16** The competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall, within seven working days from the date of receipt of an application for alteration registration of rights in a civil aircraft, examine the particulars for which alteration registration is applied. If, upon examination, the application complies with the provisions of these Regulations, it shall record the alteration particulars on the relevant certificate of registration of rights and in the register of rights in civil aircraft; if, upon examination, the application does not comply with the provisions of these Regulations, it shall notify the right holder of the civil aircraft in writing.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　遇有下列情形之一时，民用航空器权利人应当持有关的民用航空器权利登记证书和证明文件，向国务院民用航空主管部门办理注销登记：  
(一)民用航空器所有权转移；  
(二)民用航空器灭失或者失踪；  
(三)民用航空器租赁关系终止或者民用航空器占有人停止占有；  
(四)民用航空器抵押权所担保的债权消灭；  
(五)民用航空器优先权消灭；  
(六)国务院民用航空主管部门规定的其他情形。

**Article 17** In any of the following circumstances, the right holder of the civil aircraft shall, by presenting the relevant certificate of registration of rights in the civil aircraft and supporting documents, effect a cancellation registration with the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council:  
(1) transfer of ownership of the civil aircraft;  
(2) loss or disappearance of the civil aircraft;  
(3) termination of the lease relationship of the civil aircraft or cessation of possession by the possessor of the civil aircraft;  
(4) extinction of the claim secured by the mortgage of the civil aircraft;  
(5) extinction of the prior right in the civil aircraft;  
(6) such other circumstances as prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院民用航空主管部门应当自收到民用航空器注销登记申请之日起7个工作日内，对申请的注销登记事项进行审查。经审查符合本条例规定的，收回有关的民用航空器权利登记证书，相应地注销民用航空器权利登记簿上的权利登记，并根据具体情况向民用航空器权利人出具民用航空器权利登记注销证明书；经审查不符合本条例规定的，应当书面通知民用航空器权利人。

**Article 18** The competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall, within seven working days from the date of receipt of an application for cancellation registration of a civil aircraft, examine the particulars for which cancellation registration is applied. If, upon examination, the application complies with the provisions of these Regulations, it shall retrieve the relevant certificate of registration of rights in the civil aircraft, cancel the registration of the rights in the register of rights in civil aircraft accordingly, and issue a certificate of cancellation of registration of rights in the civil aircraft to the right holder of the civil aircraft as the case may be; if, upon examination, the application does not comply with the provisions of these Regulations, it shall notify the right holder of the civil aircraft in writing.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　申请人办理民用航空器权利登记，应当缴纳登记费。登记费的收费标准由国务院民用航空主管部门会同国务院价格主管部门制定。

**Article 19** An applicant effecting registration of rights in a civil aircraft shall pay a registration fee. The fee standard for registration shall be formulated by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council jointly with the competent pricing authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 20** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
