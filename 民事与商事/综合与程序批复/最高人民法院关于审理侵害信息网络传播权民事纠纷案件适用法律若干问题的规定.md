# 最高人民法院关于审理侵害信息网络传播权民事纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：2020-12-29  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> 第1561次会议通过，根据2020年12月23日最高人民法院 审判委员会第1823次会议通过的《最高人民法院关于修改 〈最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干 问题的解释（二）〉等十八件知识产权类司法解释的决定》修正）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审理侵害信息网络传播权民事纠纷案件，在依法行使裁量权时，应当兼顾权利人、网络服务提供者和社会公众的利益。

**Article 1** When hearing civil disputes over infringement of the right of communication through information networks, the People's Court shall, in exercising its discretion according to law, balance the interests of the right holder, the network service provider, and the public.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定所称信息网络，包括以计算机、电视机、固定电话机、移动电话机等电子设备为终端的计算机互联网、广播电视网、固定通信网、移动通信网等信息网络，以及向公众开放的局域网络。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Provisions, information networks shall include computer networks, broadcast television networks, fixed communication networks, mobile communication networks, and other information networks that use electronic devices such as computers, televisions, fixed telephones, and mobile telephones as terminals, as well as local area networks open to the public.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　网络用户、网络服务提供者未经许可，通过信息网络提供权利人享有信息网络传播权的作品、表演、录音录像制品，除法律、行政法规另有规定外，人民法院应当认定其构成侵害信息网络传播权行为。  
通过上传到网络服务器、设置共享文件或者利用文件分享软件等方式，将作品、表演、录音录像制品置于信息网络中，使公众能够在个人选定的时间和地点以下载、浏览或者其他方式获得的，人民法院应当认定其实施了前款规定的提供行为。

**Article 3** Where a network user or network service provider, without permission, provides through an information network a work, performance, or audio-visual recording product to which the right holder is entitled to the right of communication through information networks, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations, the People's Court shall determine that such act constitutes an infringement of the right of communication through information networks.  
Where a work, performance, or audio-visual recording product is placed in an information network by means such as uploading to a network server, setting up shared files, or using file-sharing software, thereby enabling the public to obtain it by downloading, browsing, or other means at a time and place individually chosen, the People's Court shall determine that the providing act as prescribed in the preceding paragraph has been carried out.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　有证据证明网络服务提供者与他人以分工合作等方式共同提供作品、表演、录音录像制品，构成共同侵权行为的，人民法院应当判令其承担连带责任。网络服务提供者能够证明其仅提供自动接入、自动传输、信息存储空间、搜索、链接、文件分享技术等网络服务，主张其不构成共同侵权行为的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 4** Where there is evidence that a network service provider and another person jointly provide a work, performance, or audio-visual recording product through division of labor or other means, constituting a joint infringement, the People's Court shall order them to bear joint and several liability. Where a network service provider can prove that it only provides network services such as automatic access, automatic transmission, information storage space, search, linking, or file-sharing technology, and claims that it does not constitute a joint infringement, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　网络服务提供者以提供网页快照、缩略图等方式实质替代其他网络服务提供者向公众提供相关作品的，人民法院应当认定其构成提供行为。  
前款规定的提供行为不影响相关作品的正常使用，且未不合理损害权利人对该作品的合法权益，网络服务提供者主张其未侵害信息网络传播权的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 5** Where a network service provider, by providing web page snapshots, thumbnails, or other means, substantially replaces other network service providers in providing relevant works to the public, the People's Court shall determine that it has committed a providing act.  
Where the providing act as prescribed in the preceding paragraph does not affect the normal use of the relevant work and does not unreasonably damage the right holder's legitimate rights and interests in the work, and the network service provider claims that it has not infringed the right of communication through information networks, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　原告有初步证据证明网络服务提供者提供了相关作品、表演、录音录像制品，但网络服务提供者能够证明其仅提供网络服务，且无过错的，人民法院不应认定为构成侵权。

**Article 6** Where the plaintiff has prima facie evidence that the network service provider has provided the relevant work, performance, or audio-visual recording product, but the network service provider can prove that it only provided network services and is not at fault, the People's Court shall not determine that it constitutes infringement.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　网络服务提供者在提供网络服务时教唆或者帮助网络用户实施侵害信息网络传播权行为的，人民法院应当判令其承担侵权责任。  
网络服务提供者以言语、推介技术支持、奖励积分等方式诱导、鼓励网络用户实施侵害信息网络传播权行为的，人民法院应当认定其构成教唆侵权行为。  
网络服务提供者明知或者应知网络用户利用网络服务侵害信息网络传播权，未采取删除、屏蔽、断开链接等必要措施，或者提供技术支持等帮助行为的，人民法院应当认定其构成帮助侵权行为。

**Article 7** Where a network service provider, in providing network services, induces or assists a network user in committing an act of infringing the right of communication through information networks, the People's Court shall order it to bear tort liability.  
Where a network service provider induces or encourages a network user to commit an act of infringing the right of communication through information networks by means of words, recommending technical support, awarding points, or other means, the People's Court shall determine that it constitutes an inducing infringement.  
Where a network service provider, knowing or should have known that a network user is using its network services to infringe the right of communication through information networks, fails to take necessary measures such as deletion, blocking, or disconnection of links, or provides assistance such as technical support, the People's Court shall determine that it constitutes a contributory infringement.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院应当根据网络服务提供者的过错，确定其是否承担教唆、帮助侵权责任。网络服务提供者的过错包括对于网络用户侵害信息网络传播权行为的明知或者应知。  
网络服务提供者未对网络用户侵害信息网络传播权的行为主动进行审查的，人民法院不应据此认定其具有过错。  
网络服务提供者能够证明已采取合理、有效的技术措施，仍难以发现网络用户侵害信息网络传播权行为的，人民法院应当认定其不具有过错。

**Article 8** The People's Court shall determine whether the network service provider shall bear liability for inducing or contributory infringement based on its fault. The fault of a network service provider includes actual knowledge or constructive knowledge of a network user's act of infringing the right of communication through information networks.  
Where a network service provider does not proactively examine a network user's act of infringing the right of communication through information networks, the People's Court shall not determine it to be at fault solely on that ground.  
Where a network service provider can prove that it has taken reasonable and effective technical measures but still finds it difficult to discover a network user's act of infringing the right of communication through information networks, the People's Court shall determine that it is not at fault.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院应当根据网络用户侵害信息网络传播权的具体事实是否明显，综合考虑以下因素，认定网络服务提供者是否构成应知：  
（一）基于网络服务提供者提供服务的性质、方式及其引发侵权的可能性大小，应当具备的管理信息的能力；  
（二）传播的作品、表演、录音录像制品的类型、知名度及侵权信息的明显程度；  
（三）网络服务提供者是否主动对作品、表演、录音录像制品进行了选择、编辑、修改、推荐等；  
（四）网络服务提供者是否积极采取了预防侵权的合理措施；  
（五）网络服务提供者是否设置便捷程序接收侵权通知并及时对侵权通知作出合理的反应；  
（六）网络服务提供者是否针对同一网络用户的重复侵权行为采取了相应的合理措施；  
（七）其他相关因素。

**Article 9** The People's Court shall determine whether a network service provider constitutes constructive knowledge by comprehensively considering the following factors based on whether the specific facts of the network user's infringement of the right of communication through information networks are obvious:  
(1) the ability to manage information that the network service provider should possess based on the nature and manner of the services it provides and the likelihood of infringement arising therefrom;  
(2) the type, popularity, and degree of obviousness of the infringing information of the work, performance, or audio-visual recording product disseminated;  
(3) whether the network service provider has proactively selected, edited, modified, or recommended the work, performance, or audio-visual recording product;  
(4) whether the network service provider has actively taken reasonable measures to prevent infringement;  
(5) whether the network service provider has established a convenient procedure to receive infringement notices and has promptly responded to infringement notices in a reasonable manner;  
(6) whether the network service provider has taken corresponding reasonable measures against repeated infringements by the same network user;  
(7) other relevant factors.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　网络服务提供者在提供网络服务时，对热播影视作品等以设置榜单、目录、索引、描述性段落、内容简介等方式进行推荐，且公众可以在其网页上直接以下载、浏览或者其他方式获得的，人民法院可以认定其应知网络用户侵害信息网络传播权。

**Article 10** Where a network service provider, in providing network services, recommends popular film and television works or the like by setting up rankings, directories, indexes, descriptive paragraphs, content summaries, or other means, and the public can directly obtain them on its webpage by downloading, browsing, or other means, the People's Court may determine that it should have known that the network user is infringing the right of communication through information networks.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　网络服务提供者从网络用户提供的作品、表演、录音录像制品中直接获得经济利益的，人民法院应当认定其对该网络用户侵害信息网络传播权的行为负有较高的注意义务。  
网络服务提供者针对特定作品、表演、录音录像制品投放广告获取收益，或者获取与其传播的作品、表演、录音录像制品存在其他特定联系的经济利益，应当认定为前款规定的直接获得经济利益。网络服务提供者因提供网络服务而收取一般性广告费、服务费等，不属于本款规定的情形。

**Article 11** Where a network service provider directly obtains economic benefits from a work, performance, or audio-visual recording product provided by a network user, the People's Court shall determine that it bears a higher duty of care with respect to the network user's act of infringing the right of communication through information networks.  
Where a network service provider places advertisements for specific works, performances, or audio-visual recording products to obtain revenue, or obtains economic benefits that have other specific connections with the works, performances, or audio-visual recording products it disseminates, it shall be deemed to have directly obtained economic benefits as prescribed in the preceding paragraph. The collection of general advertising fees, service fees, or the like by a network service provider for providing network services shall not fall within the circumstances specified in this paragraph.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以根据案件具体情况，认定提供信息存储空间服务的网络服务提供者应知网络用户侵害信息网络传播权：  
（一）将热播影视作品等置于首页或者其他主要页面等能够为网络服务提供者明显感知的位置的；  
（二）对热播影视作品等的主题、内容主动进行选择、编辑、整理、推荐，或者为其设立专门的排行榜的；  
（三）其他可以明显感知相关作品、表演、录音录像制品为未经许可提供，仍未采取合理措施的情形。

**Article 12** Where any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court may, based on the specific circumstances of the case, determine that a network service provider providing information storage space services should have known that a network user is infringing the right of communication through information networks:  
(1) placing popular film and television works or the like on the homepage or other main pages, or in positions that can be clearly perceived by the network service provider;  
(2) proactively selecting, editing, organizing, or recommending the themes or contents of popular film and television works or the like, or setting up dedicated rankings for them;  
(3) other circumstances in which it can be clearly perceived that relevant works, performances, or audio-visual recording products are provided without permission, yet reasonable measures are still not taken.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　网络服务提供者接到权利人以书信、传真、电子邮件等方式提交的通知及构成侵权的初步证据，未及时根据初步证据和服务类型采取必要措施的，人民法院应当认定其明知相关侵害信息网络传播权行为。

**Article 13** Where a network service provider receives a notice submitted by the right holder by letter, fax, email, or other means, together with prima facie evidence of infringement, and fails to take necessary measures in a timely manner based on the prima facie evidence and the type of service, the People's Court shall determine that it has actual knowledge of the relevant act of infringing the right of communication through information networks.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院认定网络服务提供者转送通知、采取必要措施是否及时，应当根据权利人提交通知的形式，通知的准确程度，采取措施的难易程度，网络服务的性质，所涉作品、表演、录音录像制品的类型、知名度、数量等因素综合判断。

**Article 14** When determining whether a network service provider has forwarded the notice or taken necessary measures in a timely manner, the People's Court shall make a comprehensive judgment based on factors such as the form of the notice submitted by the right holder, the accuracy of the notice, the difficulty of taking measures, the nature of the network service, and the type, popularity, and quantity of the works, performances, or audio-visual recording products involved.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　侵害信息网络传播权民事纠纷案件由侵权行为地或者被告住所地人民法院管辖。侵权行为地包括实施被诉侵权行为的网络服务器、计算机终端等设备所在地。侵权行为地和被告住所地均难以确定或者在境外的，原告发现侵权内容的计算机终端等设备所在地可以视为侵权行为地。

**Article 15** Civil disputes over infringement of the right of communication through information networks shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court at the place where the infringing act is committed or where the defendant has its domicile. The place where the infringing act is committed includes the place where the network server, computer terminal, or other equipment used to commit the alleged infringing act is located. Where both the place of infringement and the defendant's domicile are difficult to determine or are located outside the territory, the place where the computer terminal or other equipment on which the plaintiff discovers the infringing content is located may be deemed as the place of infringement.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本规定施行之日起，《最高人民法院关于审理涉及计算机网络著作权纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2006〕11号）同时废止。  
本规定施行之后尚未终审的侵害信息网络传播权民事纠纷案件，适用本规定。本规定施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的，不适用本规定。

**Article 16** Upon the date these Provisions come into force, the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in Hearing Copyright Disputes Involving Computer Networks (Fa Shi [2006] No. 11) shall be abolished simultaneously.  
For civil disputes over infringement of the right of communication through information networks that have not been finally adjudicated after these Provisions come into force, these Provisions shall apply. For cases that have been finally adjudicated before these Provisions come into force, where a party applies for retrial or a decision is made to conduct a retrial in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, these Provisions shall not apply.
