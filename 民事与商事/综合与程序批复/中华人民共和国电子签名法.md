### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范电子签名行为，确立电子签名的法律效力，维护有关各方的合法权益，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of regulating electronic signature conduct, establishing the legal effect of electronic signatures, and safeguarding the lawful rights and interests of relevant parties.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称电子签名，是指数据电文中以电子形式所含、所附用于识别签名人身份并表明签名人认可其中内容的数据。  
本法所称数据电文，是指以电子、光学、磁或者类似手段生成、发送、接收或者储存的信息。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, an electronic signature refers to data in electronic form contained in or attached to a data message for the purpose of identifying the signer and indicating the signer's approval of the content therein.  
For the purposes of this Law, a data message refers to information generated, sent, received or stored by electronic, optical, magnetic or similar means.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　民事活动中的合同或者其他文件、单证等文书，当事人可以约定使用或者不使用电子签名、数据电文。  
当事人约定使用电子签名、数据电文的文书，不得仅因为其采用电子签名、数据电文的形式而否定其法律效力。  
前款规定不适用下列文书：  
（一）涉及婚姻、收养、继承等人身关系的；  
（二）涉及停止供水、供热、供气等公用事业服务的；  
（三）法律、行政法规规定的不适用电子文书的其他情形。

**Article 3** In a contract or other document, instrument, certificate, etc. in civil activities, the parties may agree on the use or non-use of electronic signatures or data messages.  
The legal effect of a document in which the parties have agreed to use electronic signatures or data messages shall not be denied solely on the ground that it is in the form of electronic signatures or data messages.  
The preceding paragraph shall not apply to the following documents:  
(1) those involving personal relationships such as marriage, adoption or succession;  
(2) those involving the cessation of public utility services such as water supply, heat supply or gas supply; and  
(3) other circumstances as prescribed by laws or administrative regulations where electronic documents are not applicable.

### 第二章　数据电文

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　能够有形地表现所载内容，并可以随时调取查用的数据电文，视为符合法律、法规要求的书面形式。

**Article 4** A data message that can tangibly represent the content contained therein and can be accessed and used at any time shall be deemed to meet the requirements for written form prescribed by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　符合下列条件的数据电文，视为满足法律、法规规定的原件形式要求：  
（一）能够有效地表现所载内容并可供随时调取查用；  
（二）能够可靠地保证自最终形成时起，内容保持完整、未被更改。但是，在数据电文上增加背书以及数据交换、储存和显示过程中发生的形式变化不影响数据电文的完整性。

**Article 5** A data message that meets the following conditions shall be deemed to satisfy the requirements for original form prescribed by laws and regulations:  
(1) it can effectively represent the content contained therein and can be accessed and used at any time; and  
(2) it can reliably ensure that the content remains complete and unaltered from the time it is finally formed. However, the addition of endorsements to a data message, and any formal changes occurring in the course of data exchange, storage or display, shall not affect the integrity of the data message.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　符合下列条件的数据电文，视为满足法律、法规规定的文件保存要求：  
（一）能够有效地表现所载内容并可供随时调取查用；  
（二）数据电文的格式与其生成、发送或者接收时的格式相同，或者格式不相同但是能够准确表现原来生成、发送或者接收的内容；  
（三）能够识别数据电文的发件人、收件人以及发送、接收的时间。

**Article 6** A data message that meets the following conditions shall be deemed to satisfy the requirements for record retention prescribed by laws and regulations:  
(1) it can effectively represent the content contained therein and can be accessed and used at any time;  
(2) the format of the data message is the same as the format when it was generated, sent or received, or if the format is different, it can accurately represent the content originally generated, sent or received; and  
(3) the sender, recipient and the time of sending and receiving of the data message can be identified.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　数据电文不得仅因为其是以电子、光学、磁或者类似手段生成、发送、接收或者储存的而被拒绝作为证据使用。

**Article 7** A data message shall not be rejected as evidence solely on the ground that it is generated, sent, received or stored by electronic, optical, magnetic or similar means.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　审查数据电文作为证据的真实性，应当考虑以下因素：  
（一）生成、储存或者传递数据电文方法的可靠性；  
（二）保持内容完整性方法的可靠性；  
（三）用以鉴别发件人方法的可靠性；  
（四）其他相关因素。

**Article 8** In examining the authenticity of a data message as evidence, the following factors shall be considered:  
(1) the reliability of the method by which the data message was generated, stored or transmitted;  
(2) the reliability of the method for maintaining the integrity of the content;  
(3) the reliability of the method used to identify the sender; and  
(4) other relevant factors.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　数据电文有下列情形之一的，视为发件人发送：  
（一）经发件人授权发送的；  
（二）发件人的信息系统自动发送的；  
（三）收件人按照发件人认可的方法对数据电文进行验证后结果相符的。  
当事人对前款规定的事项另有约定的，从其约定。

**Article 9** A data message shall be deemed to have been sent by the sender in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) it is sent by an authorized person of the sender;  
(2) it is automatically sent by the sender's information system; or  
(3) after verification by the recipient in a manner agreed by the sender, the result is consistent.  
If the parties have otherwise agreed on the matters specified in the preceding paragraph, such agreement shall prevail.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　法律、行政法规规定或者当事人约定数据电文需要确认收讫的，应当确认收讫。发件人收到收件人的收讫确认时，数据电文视为已经收到。

**Article 10** If laws or administrative regulations provide, or the parties agree, that a data message requires an acknowledgement of receipt, the receipt shall be acknowledged. When the sender receives the acknowledgement of receipt from the recipient, the data message shall be deemed to have been received.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　数据电文进入发件人控制之外的某个信息系统的时间，视为该数据电文的发送时间。  
收件人指定特定系统接收数据电文的，数据电文进入该特定系统的时间，视为该数据电文的接收时间；未指定特定系统的，数据电文进入收件人的任何系统的首次时间，视为该数据电文的接收时间。  
当事人对数据电文的发送时间、接收时间另有约定的，从其约定。

**Article 11** The time when a data message enters an information system outside the sender's control shall be deemed the time of sending of such data message.  
If the recipient designates a specific system for receiving data messages, the time when the data message enters that specific system shall be deemed the time of receipt of such data message; if no specific system is designated, the time when the data message first enters any system of the recipient shall be deemed the time of receipt.  
If the parties have otherwise agreed on the time of sending or time of receipt of a data message, such agreement shall prevail.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　发件人的主营业地为数据电文的发送地点，收件人的主营业地为数据电文的接收地点。没有主营业地的，其经常居住地为发送或者接收地点。  
当事人对数据电文的发送地点、接收地点另有约定的，从其约定。

**Article 12** The place of business of the sender shall be deemed the place of sending of the data message, and the place of business of the recipient shall be deemed the place of receipt. If there is no place of business, the habitual residence shall be deemed the place of sending or receipt.  
If the parties have otherwise agreed on the place of sending or place of receipt of a data message, such agreement shall prevail.

### 第三章　电子签名与认证

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　电子签名同时符合下列条件的，视为可靠的电子签名：  
（一）电子签名制作数据用于电子签名时，属于电子签名人专有；  
（二）签署时电子签名制作数据仅由电子签名人控制；  
（三）签署后对电子签名的任何改动能够被发现；  
（四）签署后对数据电文内容和形式的任何改动能够被发现。  
当事人也可以选择使用符合其约定的可靠条件的电子签名。

**Article 13** An electronic signature shall be deemed a reliable electronic signature if it meets all of the following conditions:  
(1) when the electronic signature creation data are used for the electronic signature, they are unique to the electronic signer;  
(2) at the time of signing, the electronic signature creation data are under the sole control of the electronic signer;  
(3) any alteration to the electronic signature after signing can be detected; and  
(4) any alteration to the content and form of the data message after signing can be detected.  
The parties may also choose to use an electronic signature that meets the reliability conditions agreed upon by them.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　可靠的电子签名与手写签名或者盖章具有同等的法律效力。

**Article 14** A reliable electronic signature shall have the same legal effect as a handwritten signature or a seal.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　电子签名人应当妥善保管电子签名制作数据。电子签名人知悉电子签名制作数据已经失密或者可能已经失密时，应当及时告知有关各方，并终止使用该电子签名制作数据。

**Article 15** An electronic signer shall properly keep the electronic signature creation data. When the electronic signer becomes aware that the electronic signature creation data have been or may have been compromised, the electronic signer shall promptly inform all relevant parties and cease using such electronic signature creation data.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　电子签名需要第三方认证的，由依法设立的电子认证服务提供者提供认证服务。

**Article 16** Where an electronic signature requires certification by a third party, the certification service shall be provided by an electronic certification service provider established according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　提供电子认证服务，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）取得企业法人资格；  
（二）具有与提供电子认证服务相适应的专业技术人员和管理人员；  
（三）具有与提供电子认证服务相适应的资金和经营场所；  
（四）具有符合国家安全标准的技术和设备；  
（五）具有国家密码管理机构同意使用密码的证明文件；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 17** To provide electronic certification services, the following conditions shall be satisfied:  
(1) obtaining the status of an enterprise legal person;  
(2) having professional technical and managerial personnel appropriate to providing electronic certification services;  
(3) having funds and business premises appropriate to providing electronic certification services;  
(4) having technology and equipment that meet national security standards;  
(5) having certification documents from the state cryptography administration authority approving the use of cryptography; and  
(6) other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　从事电子认证服务，应当向国务院信息产业主管部门提出申请，并提交符合[本法第十七条](#art-17)规定条件的相关材料。国务院信息产业主管部门接到申请后经依法审查，征求国务院商务主管部门等有关部门的意见后，自接到申请之日起四十五日内作出许可或者不予许可的决定。予以许可的，颁发电子认证许可证书；不予许可的，应当书面通知申请人并告知理由。  
取得认证资格的电子认证服务提供者，应当按照国务院信息产业主管部门的规定在互联网上公布其名称、许可证号等信息。

**Article 18** To engage in electronic certification services, an application shall be submitted to the competent department of information industry of the State Council, together with the relevant materials that meet the conditions provided in Article 17 of this Law. After receiving the application, the competent department of information industry of the State Council shall, after examining it according to law and consulting the opinions of the competent department of commerce of the State Council and other relevant departments, make a decision on whether to grant permission within forty-five days from the date of receipt of the application. If permission is granted, an electronic certification license certificate shall be issued; if permission is not granted, the applicant shall be notified in writing and the reasons shall be stated.  
An electronic certification service provider that has obtained certification qualification shall publish its name, license number and other information on the Internet in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of information industry of the State Council.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　电子认证服务提供者应当制定、公布符合国家有关规定的电子认证业务规则，并向国务院信息产业主管部门备案。  
电子认证业务规则应当包括责任范围、作业操作规范、信息安全保障措施等事项。

**Article 19** An electronic certification service provider shall formulate and publish electronic certification business rules that comply with the relevant provisions of the State, and file them with the competent department of information industry of the State Council.  
The electronic certification business rules shall include matters such as the scope of liability, operational specifications, and information security safeguards.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　电子签名人向电子认证服务提供者申请电子签名认证证书，应当提供真实、完整和准确的信息。  
电子认证服务提供者收到电子签名认证证书申请后，应当对申请人的身份进行查验，并对有关材料进行审查。

**Article 20** Where an electronic signer applies to an electronic certification service provider for an electronic signature certification certificate, the electronic signer shall provide true, complete and accurate information.  
After receiving the application for an electronic signature certification certificate, the electronic certification service provider shall verify the identity of the applicant and examine the relevant materials.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　电子认证服务提供者签发的电子签名认证证书应当准确无误，并应当载明下列内容：  
（一）电子认证服务提供者名称；  
（二）证书持有人名称；  
（三）证书序列号；  
（四）证书有效期；  
（五）证书持有人的电子签名验证数据；  
（六）电子认证服务提供者的电子签名；  
（七）国务院信息产业主管部门规定的其他内容。

**Article 21** The electronic signature certification certificate issued by an electronic certification service provider shall be accurate and error-free, and shall contain the following:  
(1) the name of the electronic certification service provider;  
(2) the name of the certificate holder;  
(3) the serial number of the certificate;  
(4) the validity period of the certificate;  
(5) the electronic signature verification data of the certificate holder;  
(6) the electronic signature of the electronic certification service provider; and  
(7) other matters prescribed by the competent department of information industry of the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　电子认证服务提供者应当保证电子签名认证证书内容在有效期内完整、准确，并保证电子签名依赖方能够证实或者了解电子签名认证证书所载内容及其他有关事项。

**Article 22** An electronic certification service provider shall ensure that the content of the electronic signature certification certificate is complete and accurate during its validity period, and shall ensure that an electronic signature relying party can verify or become aware of the content of the electronic signature certification certificate and other relevant matters.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　电子认证服务提供者拟暂停或者终止电子认证服务的，应当在暂停或者终止服务九十日前，就业务承接及其他有关事项通知有关各方。  
电子认证服务提供者拟暂停或者终止电子认证服务的，应当在暂停或者终止服务六十日前向国务院信息产业主管部门报告，并与其他电子认证服务提供者就业务承接进行协商，作出妥善安排。  
电子认证服务提供者未能就业务承接事项与其他电子认证服务提供者达成协议的，应当申请国务院信息产业主管部门安排其他电子认证服务提供者承接其业务。  
电子认证服务提供者被依法吊销电子认证许可证书的，其业务承接事项的处理按照国务院信息产业主管部门的规定执行。

**Article 23** Where an electronic certification service provider intends to suspend or terminate its electronic certification services, it shall notify all relevant parties ninety days prior to the suspension or termination regarding business succession and other relevant matters.  
Where an electronic certification service provider intends to suspend or terminate its electronic certification services, it shall report to the competent department of information industry of the State Council sixty days prior to the suspension or termination, and shall negotiate with other electronic certification service providers concerning business succession and make proper arrangements.  
If an electronic certification service provider fails to reach an agreement with other electronic certification service providers on business succession, it shall apply to the competent department of information industry of the State Council to arrange for another electronic certification service provider to succeed its business.  
Where an electronic certification service provider's electronic certification license certificate is revoked according to law, the handling of its business succession matters shall be governed by the provisions of the competent department of information industry of the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　电子认证服务提供者应当妥善保存与认证相关的信息，信息保存期限至少为电子签名认证证书失效后五年。

**Article 24** An electronic certification service provider shall properly keep information related to certification, and the retention period for such information shall be at least five years after the electronic signature certification certificate ceases to have effect.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国务院信息产业主管部门依照本法制定电子认证服务业的具体管理办法，对电子认证服务提供者依法实施监督管理。

**Article 25** The competent department of information industry of the State Council shall formulate specific administrative measures for the electronic certification service industry in accordance with this Law, and shall exercise supervision and administration over electronic certification service providers according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　经国务院信息产业主管部门根据有关协议或者对等原则核准后，中华人民共和国境外的电子认证服务提供者在境外签发的电子签名认证证书与依照本法设立的电子认证服务提供者签发的电子签名认证证书具有同等的法律效力。

**Article 26** Upon approval by the competent department of information industry of the State Council under relevant agreements or the principle of reciprocity, electronic signature certification certificates issued outside the People's Republic of China by electronic certification service providers outside the territory shall have the same legal effect as electronic signature certification certificates issued by electronic certification service providers established in accordance with this Law.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　电子签名人知悉电子签名制作数据已经失密或者可能已经失密未及时告知有关各方、并终止使用电子签名制作数据，未向电子认证服务提供者提供真实、完整和准确的信息，或者有其他过错，给电子签名依赖方、电子认证服务提供者造成损失的，承担赔偿责任。

**Article 27** Where an electronic signer, having become aware that the electronic signature creation data have been or may have been compromised, fails to promptly inform all relevant parties and cease using such data, fails to provide true, complete and accurate information to the electronic certification service provider, or has other faults, thereby causing losses to the electronic signature relying party or the electronic certification service provider, the electronic signer shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　电子签名人或者电子签名依赖方因依据电子认证服务提供者提供的电子签名认证服务从事民事活动遭受损失，电子认证服务提供者不能证明自己无过错的，承担赔偿责任。

**Article 28** Where an electronic signer or an electronic signature relying party suffers losses in engaging in civil activities based on the electronic signature certification services provided by an electronic certification service provider, the electronic certification service provider shall bear compensation liability if it cannot prove that it is not at fault.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　未经许可提供电子认证服务的，由国务院信息产业主管部门责令停止违法行为；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；违法所得三十万元以上的，处违法所得一倍以上三倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足三十万元的，处十万元以上三十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 29** Where any person provides electronic certification services without permission, the competent department of information industry of the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal act; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are 300,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 300,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　电子认证服务提供者暂停或者终止电子认证服务，未在暂停或者终止服务六十日前向国务院信息产业主管部门报告的，由国务院信息产业主管部门对其直接负责的主管人员处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 30** Where an electronic certification service provider suspends or terminates electronic certification services without reporting to the competent department of information industry of the State Council sixty days prior to the suspension or termination, the competent department of information industry of the State Council shall impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the person directly in charge.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　电子认证服务提供者不遵守认证业务规则、未妥善保存与认证相关的信息，或者有其他违法行为的，由国务院信息产业主管部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，吊销电子认证许可证书，其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员十年内不得从事电子认证服务。吊销电子认证许可证书的，应当予以公告并通知工商行政管理部门。

**Article 31** Where an electronic certification service provider fails to comply with the certification business rules, fails to properly keep information related to certification, or commits other illegal acts, the competent department of information industry of the State Council shall order rectification within a prescribed time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, the electronic certification license certificate shall be revoked, and the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall not engage in electronic certification services for ten years. Where an electronic certification license certificate is revoked, a public announcement shall be made and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall be notified.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　伪造、冒用、盗用他人的电子签名，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；给他人造成损失的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 32** Where any person forges, fraudulently uses or steals another person's electronic signature, if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if losses are caused to others, civil liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　依照本法负责电子认证服务业监督管理工作的部门的工作人员，不依法履行行政许可、监督管理职责的，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 33** Where any staff member of the department responsible for the supervision and administration of the electronic certification service industry in accordance with this Law fails to perform the duties of administrative licensing and supervision according to law, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本法中下列用语的含义：  
（一）电子签名人，是指持有电子签名制作数据并以本人身份或者以其所代表的人的名义实施电子签名的人；  
（二）电子签名依赖方，是指基于对电子签名认证证书或者电子签名的信赖从事有关活动的人；  
（三）电子签名认证证书，是指可证实电子签名人与电子签名制作数据有联系的数据电文或者其他电子记录；  
（四）电子签名制作数据，是指在电子签名过程中使用的，将电子签名与电子签名人可靠地联系起来的字符、编码等数据；  
（五）电子签名验证数据，是指用于验证电子签名的数据，包括代码、口令、算法或者公钥等。

**Article 34** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) “electronic signer” means a person who holds the electronic signature creation data and executes an electronic signature in his or her own name or in the name of the person represented;  
(2) “electronic signature relying party” means a person who engages in relevant activities based on reliance on an electronic signature certification certificate or an electronic signature;  
(3) “electronic signature certification certificate” means a data message or other electronic record that can confirm the connection between the electronic signer and the electronic signature creation data;  
(4) “electronic signature creation data” means data, such as characters or codes, used in the process of creating an electronic signature to reliably associate the electronic signature with the electronic signer; and  
(5) “electronic signature verification data” means data used to verify an electronic signature, including codes, passwords, algorithms or public keys.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国务院或者国务院规定的部门可以依据本法制定政务活动和其他社会活动中使用电子签名、数据电文的具体办法。

**Article 35** The State Council or a department prescribed by the State Council may formulate specific measures for the use of electronic signatures and data messages in government affairs and other social activities in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本法自2005年4月1日起施行。

**Article 36** This Law shall come into force as of April 1, 2005.
