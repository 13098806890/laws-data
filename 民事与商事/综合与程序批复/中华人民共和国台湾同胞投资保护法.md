### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护和鼓励台湾同胞投资，促进海峡两岸的经济发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of protecting and encouraging the investment of compatriots from Taiwan, and promoting the economic development across the Taiwan Strait.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　台湾同胞投资适用本法；本法未规定的，国家其他有关法律、行政法规对台湾同胞投资有规定的，依照该规定执行。  
本法所称台湾同胞投资是指台湾地区的公司、企业、其他经济组织或者个人作为投资者在其他省、自治区和直辖市投资。

**Article 2** The investment of compatriots from Taiwan shall be governed by this Law. Where matters are not provided for in this Law, and other relevant laws and administrative regulations of the State contain provisions on the investment of compatriots from Taiwan, such provisions shall apply.  
For the purposes of this Law, "investment of compatriots from Taiwan" means investment made by companies, enterprises, other economic organizations or individuals from the Taiwan region as investors in other provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家依法保护台湾同胞投资者的投资、投资收益和其他合法权益。  
台湾同胞投资必须遵守国家的法律、法规。

**Article 3** The State shall, according to law, protect the investment, investment returns and other lawful rights and interests of investors who are compatriots from Taiwan.  
The investment of compatriots from Taiwan must comply with the laws and regulations of the State.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对台湾同胞投资者的投资不实行国有化和征收；在特殊情况下，根据社会公共利益的需要，对台湾同胞投资者的投资可以依照法律程序实行征收，并给予相应的补偿。

**Article 4** The State shall not nationalize or expropriate the investment of investors who are compatriots from Taiwan. Under special circumstances, where required by the public interest of society, the investment of investors who are compatriots from Taiwan may be expropriated in accordance with legal procedures, and appropriate compensation shall be given.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　台湾同胞投资者投资的财产、工业产权、投资收益和其他合法权益，可以依法转让和继承。

**Article 5** The property, industrial property rights, investment returns and other lawful rights and interests invested by investors who are compatriots from Taiwan may be transferred and inherited according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　台湾同胞投资者可以用可自由兑换货币、机器设备或者其他实物、工业产权、非专利技术等作为投资。  
台湾同胞投资者可以用投资获得的收益进行再投资。

**Article 6** Investors who are compatriots from Taiwan may make investment in the form of freely convertible currencies, machinery and equipment or other tangible assets, industrial property rights, non-patented technology, etc.  
Investors who are compatriots from Taiwan may use the returns obtained from their investment for reinvestment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　台湾同胞投资，可以举办全部或者部分由台湾同胞投资者投资的企业（以下简称台湾同胞投资企业），也可以采用法律、行政法规或者国务院规定的其他投资形式。  
举办台湾同胞投资企业，应当符合国家的产业政策，有利于国民经济的发展。

**Article 7** Investment by compatriots from Taiwan may be made by establishing enterprises wholly or partly invested by such investors (hereinafter referred to as Taiwan compatriot-invested enterprises), or by adopting other forms of investment prescribed by laws, administrative regulations or the State Council.  
The establishment of Taiwan compatriot-invested enterprises shall conform to the industrial policies of the State and be conducive to the development of the national economy.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　台湾同胞投资企业依法进行经营管理活动，其经营管理的自主权不受干涉。

**Article 8** Taiwan compatriot-invested enterprises shall conduct business operation and management activities according to law, and their autonomy in operation and management shall not be interfered with.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　在台湾同胞投资企业集中的地区，可以依法成立台湾同胞投资企业协会，其合法权益受法律保护。

**Article 9** In areas where Taiwan compatriot-invested enterprises are concentrated, associations of such enterprises may be established according to law, and their lawful rights and interests shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　台湾同胞投资者依法获得的投资收益、其他合法收入和清算后的资金，可以依法汇回台湾或者汇往境外。

**Article 10** The investment returns, other lawful incomes and funds after liquidation lawfully obtained by investors who are compatriots from Taiwan may be remitted back to Taiwan or abroad according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　台湾同胞投资者可以委托亲友作为其投资的代理人。

**Article 11** Investors who are compatriots from Taiwan may entrust their relatives or friends as agents for their investment.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　台湾同胞投资企业依照国务院关于鼓励台湾同胞投资的有关规定，享受优惠待遇。

**Article 12** Taiwan compatriot-invested enterprises shall enjoy preferential treatment in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council on encouraging investment by compatriots from Taiwan.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　台湾同胞投资者与其他省、自治区和直辖市的公司、企业、其他经济组织或者个人之间发生的与投资有关的争议，当事人可以通过协商或者调解解决。  
当事人不愿协商、调解的，或者经协商、调解不成的，可以依据合同中的仲裁条款或者事后达成的书面仲裁协议，提交仲裁机构仲裁。  
当事人未在合同中订立仲裁条款，事后又未达成书面仲裁协议的，可以向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 13** Disputes relating to investment arising between investors who are compatriots from Taiwan and companies, enterprises, other economic organizations or individuals of other provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government may be resolved by the parties through consultation or mediation.  
Where the parties are unwilling to resolve the dispute through consultation or mediation, or where consultation or mediation fails, the dispute may be submitted to an arbitration institution for arbitration in accordance with the arbitration clause stipulated in the contract or a written arbitration agreement reached thereafter.  
Where the parties have not included an arbitration clause in the contract and have not reached a written arbitration agreement thereafter, they may bring a lawsuit in a People's Court.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本法自公布之日起施行。

**Article 14** This Law shall come into force as of the date of its promulgation.
