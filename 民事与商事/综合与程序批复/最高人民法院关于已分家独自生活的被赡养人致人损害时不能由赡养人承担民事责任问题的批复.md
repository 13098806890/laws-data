**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：1990-02-10

### 最高人民法院关于已分家独自生活的被赡养人致人损害时不能由赡养人承担民事责任问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于已分家独自生活的被赡养人致人损害时  
  
不能由赡养人承担民事责任问题的批复  
  
（89）法民字第32号  
  
四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院川法研（89）第43号关于被赡养人因损害行为引起他人经济损失，本人无经济收入的能否按照《关于贯彻执行＜中华人民共和国民法通则＞若干问题的意见（试行）》第一百六十一条第二款（以下简称 “意见”）的规定，由赡养人垫付的请示收悉。经研究，我们认为，已分家独自生活的被赡养人致人损害时，应由本人承担民事责任，赡养人既不是共同被告，也不是第三人，不应列为诉讼当事人。你院所请示的问题，不适用 “意见”第一百六十一条第二款的规定。至于赡养人自愿为被赡养人支付赔偿费用，可由他们自行协商解决，人民法院不宜作出具有法律效力的调解或者判决。  
  
1990年2月10日

**Article 1** Official Reply concerning the Issue that a Supported Person Who Has Divided Family Property and Lives Independently Causes Damage to Others and the Supporter Shall Not Bear Civil Liability  
Fa-Min Zi No. 32 (1989)  
Sichuan Higher People's Court:  
Your Request for Instructions, Chuan Fa Yan (89) No. 43, regarding whether, when a supported person causes economic losses to others through a tortious act and has no personal income, the supporter may advance payment in accordance with the provisions of Article 161, Paragraph 2 (hereinafter referred to as the "Opinions") of the Opinions on Several Issues Concerning the Implementation of the General Principles of the Civil Law of the People's Republic of China (for Trial Implementation), has been received and reviewed. After study, we are of the opinion that when a supported person who has divided family property and lives independently causes damage to others, he/she shall bear civil liability by himself/herself. The supporter shall not be a joint defendant or a third party and shall not be listed as a party to the proceedings. The issue raised in your Request for Instructions does not apply to the provisions of Article 161, Paragraph 2 of the "Opinions". As for the supporter voluntarily paying compensation expenses for the supported person, this may be resolved through consultation between them, and the People's Court shall not render a mediation or judgment with legal effect.  
February 10, 1990
