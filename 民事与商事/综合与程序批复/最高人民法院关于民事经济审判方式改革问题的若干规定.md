# 最高人民法院关于民事经济审判方式改革问题的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔1998〕14号  
**公布日期**：1998-06-19  
**生效日期**：1998-07-11

> (1998年6月19日最高人民法院审判委员会第995次会议通过，自1998年7月11日起施行） 为了正确适用《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》（以下简称民事诉讼法），建立与社会主义市场经济体制相适应的民事经济审判机制，保证依法、正确、及时地审理案件，在总结各地实践经验的基础上，对民事、经济审判方式改革中的有关问题作出如下规定。 关于当事人举证和法院调查收集证据问题 一、人民法院可以制定各类案件举证须知，明确举证内容及其范围和要求。 二、人民法院在送达受理案件通知书和应诉通知书时，应当告知当事人围绕自已的主张提供证据。 三、下列证据由人民法院调查收集： 1、当事人及其诉讼代理人因客观原因不能自行收集并已提出调取证据的申请和该证据线索的； 2、应当由人民法院勘验或者委托鉴定的； 3、当事人双方提出的影响查明案件主要事实的证据材料相互矛盾，经过庭审质证无法认定其效力的； 4、人民法院认为需要自行调查收集的其他证据。上述证据经人民法院调查，未能收集到的，仍由负有举证责任的当事人承担举证不能的后果。 四、审判人员收到当事人或者其诉讼代理人递交的证据材料应当出具收据。 关于做好庭前必要准备及时开庭审理问题 五、开庭前应当做好下列准备工作： 1、在法定期限内，分别向当事人送达受理案件通知书、应诉通知书和起诉状、答辩状副本； 2、通知必须共同进行诉讼的当事人参加诉讼；

---

### 正文

一、人民法院可以制定各类案件举证须知，明确举证内容及其范围和要求。

**Article None** 1. The People's Court may formulate guidelines on adducing evidence for various types of cases to clarify the content, scope and requirements of adducing evidence.

二、人民法院在送达受理案件通知书和应诉通知书时，应当告知当事人围绕自已的主张提供证据。

**Article None** 2. When serving the notice of acceptance of a case and the notice of response, the People's Court shall inform the parties to adduce evidence around their own claims.

三、下列证据由人民法院调查收集：  
1、当事人及其诉讼代理人因客观原因不能自行收集并已提出调取证据的申请和该证据线索的；  
2、应当由人民法院勘验或者委托鉴定的；  
3、当事人双方提出的影响查明案件主要事实的证据材料相互矛盾，经过庭审质证无法认定其效力的；  
4、人民法院认为需要自行调查收集的其他证据。上述证据经人民法院调查，未能收集到的，仍由负有举证责任的当事人承担举证不能的后果。

**Article None** 3. The following evidence shall be investigated and collected by the People's Court:  
1. Evidence that a party or its litigation agent is unable to collect by themselves due to objective reasons and has submitted an application for obtaining evidence and the leads for such evidence;  
2. Evidence that shall be inspected by the People's Court or entrusted for expert evaluation;  
3. Where the evidentiary materials submitted by both parties that affect the ascertainment of the main facts of the case are contradictory, and their validity cannot be determined after courtroom cross-examination;  
4. Other evidence that the People's Court deems necessary to investigate and collect on its own. If the aforesaid evidence is investigated by the People's Court but fails to be collected, the party bearing the burden of adducing evidence shall still bear the consequences of inability to adduce evidence.

四、审判人员收到当事人或者其诉讼代理人递交的证据材料应当出具收据。  
关于做好庭前必要准备及时开庭审理问题

**Article None** 4. Upon receiving evidentiary materials submitted by a party or its litigation agent, the adjudicators shall issue a receipt.  
On the issue of making necessary pre-trial preparations and conducting trial in a timely manner

五、开庭前应当做好下列准备工作：  
1、在法定期限内，分别向当事人送达受理案件通知书、应诉通知书和起诉状、答辩状副本；  
2、通知必须共同进行诉讼的当事人参加诉讼；  
3、告知当事人有关的诉讼权利和义务、合议庭组成人员；  
4、审查有关的诉讼材料，了解双方当事人争议的焦点和应当适用的有关法律以及有关专业知识；  
5、调查收集应当由人民法院调查收集的证据；  
6、需要由人民法院勘验或者委托鉴定的，进行勘验或者委托有关部门鉴定；  
7、案情比较复杂、证据材料较多的案件，可以组织当事人交换证据；  
8、其他必要的准备工作。

**Article None** 5. Before trial, the following preparatory work shall be carried out:  
1. Within the statutory time limit, serve the notice of acceptance, the notice of response, and copies of the statement of complaint and statement of defense on the parties respectively;  
2. Notify the parties who must be joined in the action to participate in the proceedings;  
3. Inform the parties of the relevant litigation rights and obligations and the composition of the collegiate bench;  
4. Review the relevant litigation materials, understand the disputed issues between the parties and the relevant laws that shall apply as well as relevant professional knowledge;  
5. Investigate and collect evidence that shall be investigated and collected by the People's Court;  
6. If inspection by the People's Court or entrusted expert evaluation is necessary, carry out the inspection or entrust a relevant department for expert evaluation;  
7. For cases with relatively complex facts and large amounts of evidentiary materials, may organize the parties to exchange evidence;  
8. Other necessary preparatory work.

六、合议庭成员和独任审判员开庭前不得单独接触一方当事人及其诉讼代理人。

**Article None** 6. Members of the collegiate bench and the sole adjudicator shall not have separate contact with one party or its litigation agent before trial.

七、按普通程序审理的案件，开庭审理应当在答辩期届满并做好必要的准备工作后进行。当事人明确表示不提交答辩状，或者在答辩期届满前已经答辩，或者同意在答辩期间开庭的，也可以在答辩期限届满前开庭审理。  
关于改进庭审方式问题

**Article None** 7. For cases tried under ordinary procedure, trial shall be conducted after the expiration of the defense period and upon completion of necessary preparatory work. If the party explicitly states that it will not submit a statement of defense, or has already submitted a defense before the expiration of the defense period, or agrees to a trial during the defense period, trial may also be conducted prior to the expiration of the defense period.  
On the issue of improving trial methods

八、法庭调查按下列顺序进行：  
1、由原告口头陈述事实或者宣读起诉状，讲明具体诉讼请求和理由。  
2、由被告口头陈述事实或者宣读答辩状，对原告诉讼请求提出异议或者反诉的，讲明具体请求和理由。  
3、第三人陈述或者答辩，有独立请求权的第三人陈述诉讼请求和理由；无独立请求权的第三人针对原、被告的陈述提出承认或者否认的答辩意见。  
4、原告或者被告对第三人的陈述进行答辩。  
5、审判长或者独任审判员归纳本案争议焦点或者法庭调查重点，并征求当事人的意见。  
6、原告出示证据，被告进行质证；被告出示证据，原告进行质证。  
7、原、被告对第三人出示的证据进行质证；第三人对原告或者被告出示的证据进行质证。  
8、审判人员出示人民法院调查收集的证据，原告、被告和第三人进行质证。  
经审判长许可，当事人可以向证人发问，当事人可以互相发问。  
审判人员可以询问当事人。

**Article None** 8. Court investigation shall be conducted in the following order:  
1. The plaintiff shall orally state the facts or read out the statement of complaint, specifying the specific claims and reasons.  
2. The defendant shall orally state the facts or read out the statement of defense; if raising objections to the plaintiff's claims or filing a counterclaim, specify the specific claims and reasons.  
3. The third party shall make statements or defense; a third party with independent claims shall state its claims and reasons; a third party without independent claims shall present responses admitting or denying the statements of the plaintiff and defendant.  
4. The plaintiff or defendant shall respond to the third party's statement.  
5. The presiding judge or the sole adjudicator shall summarize the disputed issues of the case or the focus of the court investigation, and solicit the opinions of the parties.  
6. The plaintiff shall present evidence, and the defendant shall conduct cross-examination; the defendant shall present evidence, and the plaintiff shall conduct cross-examination.  
7. The plaintiff and defendant shall conduct cross-examination on evidence presented by the third party; the third party shall conduct cross-examination on evidence presented by the plaintiff or defendant.  
8. The adjudicators shall present evidence investigated and collected by the People's Court, and the plaintiff, defendant and third party shall conduct cross-examination.  
With the permission of the presiding judge, the parties may question the witnesses, and the parties may question each other.  
The adjudicators may question the parties.

九、案件有两个以上独立存在的事实或者诉讼请求的，可以要求当事人逐项陈述事实和理由，逐个出示证据并分别进行调查和质证。  
对当事人无争议的事实，无需举证、质证。

**Article None** 9. Where a case has two or more independent facts or claims, the parties may be required to state the facts and reasons item by item, present evidence one by one, and conduct investigation and cross-examination separately.  
Facts not disputed by the parties need not be adduced or cross-examined.

十、当事人向法庭提出的证据，应当由当事人或者其诉讼代理人宣读。当事人及其诉讼代理人因客观原因不能宣读的证据，可以由审判人员代为宣读。人民法院依职权调查收集的证据由审判人员宣读。

**Article None** 10. Evidence submitted to the court by a party shall be read out by the party or its litigation agent. Evidence that the party or its litigation agent is unable to read out due to objective reasons may be read out by the adjudicators on their behalf. Evidence investigated and collected by the People's Court ex officio shall be read out by the adjudicators.

十一、案件的同一事实，除举证责任倒置外，由提出主张的一方当事人首先举证，然后由另一方当事人举证。另一方当事人不能提出足以推翻前一事实的证据的，对这一事实可以认定；提出足以推翻前一事实的证据的，再转由提出主张的当事人继续举证。

**Article None** 11. For the same fact of a case, except in cases of inversion of the burden of adducing evidence, the party asserting the claim shall first adduce evidence, and then the other party shall adduce evidence. If the other party is unable to adduce evidence sufficient to overturn the previous fact, this fact may be affirmed; if it adduces evidence sufficient to overturn the previous fact, the burden shall then shift back to the party asserting the claim to continue adducing evidence.

十二、经过庭审质证的证据，能够当即认定的，应当当即认定；当即不能认定的，可以休庭合议后再予以认定；合议之后认为需要继续举证或者进行鉴定、勘验等工作的，可以在下次开庭质证后认定。未经庭审质证的证据，不能作为定案的根据。

**Article None** 12. Evidence that has been cross-examined in court and can be determined immediately shall be determined immediately; if it cannot be determined immediately, it may be determined after adjournment and deliberation; if after deliberation it is deemed necessary to continue adducing evidence or to conduct expert evaluation, inspection, etc., it may be determined after cross-examination at the next court session. Evidence that has not been cross-examined in court shall not be used as the basis for deciding the case.

十三、一方当事人要求补充证据或者申请重新鉴定、勘验，人民法院认为有必要的可以准许。补充的证据或者重新进行鉴定、勘验的结论，必须再次开庭质证。

**Article None** 13. If one party requests to supplement evidence or applies for re-evaluation or re-inspection, the People's Court may grant it if it deems necessary. The supplementary evidence or the conclusions of re-evaluation or re-inspection must be cross-examined in court again.

十四、法庭决定再次开庭的，审判长或者独任审判员对本次开庭情况应当进行小结，指出庭审已经确认的证据，并指明下次开庭调查的重点。

**Article None** 14. If the court decides to hold another court session, the presiding judge or the sole adjudicator shall summarize the proceedings of the current court session, point out the evidence that has been confirmed during the trial, and indicate the focus of investigation for the next court session.

十五、第二次开庭审理时，只就未经调查的事项进行调查和审理，对已经调查、质证并已认定的证据不再重复审理。

**Article None** 15. At the second court session, only matters that have not been investigated shall be investigated and tried; evidence that has been investigated, cross-examined and affirmed shall not be retried.

十六、法庭调查结束前，审判长或者独任审判员应当就法庭调查认定的事实和当事人争议的问题进行归纳总结。

**Article None** 16. Before the conclusion of court investigation, the presiding judge or the sole adjudicator shall summarize the facts affirmed through court investigation and the issues disputed by the parties.

十七、审判人员应当引导当事人围绕争议焦点进行辩论。当事人及其诉讼代理人的发言与本案无关或者重复未被法庭认定的事实，审判人员应当予以制止。

**Article None** 17. The adjudicators shall guide the parties to debate around the disputed issues. If a party or its litigation agent makes remarks irrelevant to the case or repeats facts not affirmed by the court, the adjudicators shall stop them.

十八、法庭辩论由各方当事人依次发言。一轮辩论结束后当事人要求继续辩论的，可以进行下一轮辩论。下一轮辩论不得重复第一轮辩论的内容。

**Article None** 18. The court debate shall be conducted with each party speaking in turn. If a party requests to continue the debate after one round, a next round of debate may be conducted. The next round of debate shall not repeat the content of the first round.

十九、法庭辩论时，审判人员不得对案件性质、是非责任发表意见，不得与当事人辩论。  
法庭辩论终结，审判长或者独任审判员征得各方当事人同意后，可以依法进行调解，调解不成的，应当及时判决。

**Article None** 19. During the court debate, the adjudicators shall not express opinions on the nature of the case, the rights and wrongs or the responsibility, and shall not debate with the parties.  
After the conclusion of the court debate, the presiding judge or the sole adjudicator, upon obtaining the consent of all parties, may conduct mediation according to law; if mediation fails, a judgment shall be rendered in a timely manner.

二十、适用简易程序审理的案件，当事人同时到庭的，可以迳行开庭进行调解。调解前告知当事人诉讼权利义务和主持调解的审判人员，在询问当事人是否申请审判人员回避后，当事人不申请回避的，可以直接进行调解。调解不成的或者达成协议后当事人反悔又未提出新的事实和证据，可以不再重新开庭，直接作出判决。  
关于对证据的审核和认定问题

**Article None** 20. For cases tried under summary procedure, if the parties appear in court simultaneously, the court may proceed directly to court mediation. Before mediation, the parties shall be informed of the litigation rights and obligations and the adjudicator presiding over the mediation; after inquiring whether the parties apply for the withdrawal of the adjudicator, if the parties do not apply for withdrawal, mediation may proceed directly. If mediation fails or if a party repudiates after reaching an agreement and does not present new facts or evidence, the court may directly render a judgment without holding another court session.  
On the issue of examination and affirmation of evidence

二十一、当事人对自己的主张，只有本人陈述而不能提出其他相关证据的，除对方当事人认可外，其主张不予支持。

**Article None** 21. If a party only has its own statement for its claim and is unable to produce other relevant evidence, its claim shall not be upheld, unless the opposing party acknowledges it.

二十二、一方当事人提出的证据，对方当事人认可或者不予反驳的，可以确认其证明力。

**Article None** 22. Evidence adduced by one party, if acknowledged or not rebutted by the opposing party, may have its probative force confirmed.

二十三、一方当事人提出的证据，对方当事人举不出相应证据反驳的，可以综合全案情况对该证据予以认定。

**Article None** 23. If the opposing party is unable to adduce corresponding evidence to rebut evidence adduced by one party, the evidence may be affirmed based on a comprehensive assessment of the whole case.

二十四、双方当事人对同一事实分别举出相反的证据，但都没有足够理由否定对方证据的，应当分别对当事人提出的证据进行审查，并结合其他证据综合认定。

**Article None** 24. If both parties adduce opposite evidence on the same fact but neither has sufficient reasons to deny the other's evidence, the evidence adduced by each party shall be examined separately and comprehensively affirmed in conjunction with other evidence.

二十五、当事人在庭审质证时对证据表示认可，庭审后又反悔，但提不出相应证据的，不能推翻已认定的证据。

**Article None** 25. If a party acknowledges evidence during court cross-examination but later retracts after the court session without adducing corresponding evidence, the evidence that has been affirmed shall not be overturned.

二十六、对单一证据，应当注意从以下几个方面进行审查。  
1、证据取得的方式；  
2、证据形成的原因；  
3、证据的形式；  
4、证据提供者的情况及其与本案的关系；  
5、书证是否系原件，物证是否系原物；复印件或者复制品是否与原件、原物的内容、形式及其他特征相符合。

**Article None** 26. For a single piece of evidence, attention shall be paid to examining it from the following aspects.  
1. The method of obtaining the evidence;  
2. The cause of formation of the evidence;  
3. The form of the evidence;  
4. The circumstances of the provider of the evidence and its relationship with the case;  
5. Whether documentary evidence is an original, whether physical evidence is the original object; whether copies or reproductions conform to the content, form and other characteristics of the original document or original object.

二十七、判断数个证据的效力应当注意以下几种情况：  
1、物证、历史档案、鉴定结论、勘验笔录或者经过公证、登记的书证，其证明力一般高于其他书证、视听资料和证人证言。  
2、证人提供的对与其有亲属关系或者其他密切关系的一方当事人有利的证言，其证明力低于其他证人证言。  
3、原始证据的证明力大于传来证据。  
4、对证人的智力状况、品德、知识、经验、法律意识和专业技能等进行综合分析。

**Article None** 27. In assessing the validity of multiple pieces of evidence, attention shall be paid to the following circumstances:  
1. Physical evidence, historical archives, expert conclusions, inspection records, or documentary evidence that has been notarized or registered have probative force generally higher than that of other documentary evidence, audio-visual materials, and witness testimony.  
2. Testimony given by a witness favorable to a party who has a familial relationship or other close relationship with the witness has probative force lower than that of other witness testimony.  
3. The probative force of original evidence is greater than that of derivative evidence.  
4. Conduct a comprehensive analysis of the witness's intellectual capacity, moral character, knowledge, experience, legal awareness, and professional skills.

二十八、下列证据，不能单独作为认定案件事实的依据：  
1、未成年人所作的与其年龄和智力状况不相当的证言；  
2、与一方当事人有亲属关系的证人出具的对该当事人有利的证言；  
3、没有其他证据印证并有疑点的视听资料；  
4、无法与原件、原物核对的复印件、复制品。

**Article None** 28. The following evidence shall not be used as the sole basis for determining the facts of a case:  
1. Testimony given by a minor that is inconsistent with his/her age and intellectual capacity;  
2. Testimony issued by a witness who has a familial relationship with one party and is favorable to that party;  
3. Audio-visual materials that are not corroborated by other evidence and are questionable;  
4. Copies or reproductions that cannot be verified against the original document or original object.

二十九、当事人提供的证人在人民法院通知的开庭日期，没有正当理由拒不出庭的，由提供该证人的当事人承担举证不能的责任。

**Article None** 29. If a witness provided by a party refuses to appear in court without justifiable reason on the trial date notified by the People's Court, the party who provided the witness shall bear the liability for inability to adduce evidence.

三十、有证据证明持有证据的一方当事人无正当理由拒不提供，如果对方当事人主张该证据的内容不利于证据持有人，可以推定该主张成立。  
关于加强合议庭和独任审判员职责问题

**Article None** 30. If there is evidence proving that the party in possession of evidence refuses to provide it without justifiable reason, and if the opposing party claims that the content of the evidence is unfavorable to the party in possession, it may be presumed that the claim is established.  
On the issue of strengthening the duties of the collegiate bench and sole adjudicator

三十一、合议庭组成人员必须共同参加对案件的审理，对案件的事实、证据、性质、责任、适用法律以及处理结果等共同负责。

**Article None** 31. Members of the collegiate bench must jointly participate in the trial of the case and shall be jointly responsible for the facts, evidence, nature, liability, application of law and the outcome of the case.

三十二、经过开庭审理当庭达成调解协议的，由审判长或者独任审判员签发调解书。

**Article None** 32. If a mediation agreement is reached in court during trial, the mediation statement shall be signed and issued by the presiding judge or the sole adjudicator.

三十三、事实清楚、法律关系明确、是非责任分明、合议庭意见一致的裁判，可以由审判长或者独任审判员签发法律文书。但应当由院长签发的除外。

**Article None** 33. For a judgment where the facts are clear, the legal relationship is clear, the rights and wrongs and liability are distinct, and the collegiate bench has unanimous opinion, the legal document may be signed and issued by the presiding judge or the sole adjudicator, except where it shall be signed and issued by the president of the court.

三十四、合议庭、独任审判员审理决定的案件或者经院长提交审判委员会决定的案件，发现认定事实或者适用法律有重大错误并造成严重后果的，按照有关规定由有关人员承担相应责任。  
关于第二审程序中的有关问题

**Article None** 34. If it is found that there is a major error in the determination of facts or application of law causing serious consequences in cases decided by the collegiate bench or the sole adjudicator after trial, or cases submitted by the president to the judicial committee for decision, the relevant persons shall bear corresponding liability in accordance with relevant provisions.  
On issues relating to the procedure of second instance

三十五、第二审案件的审理应当围绕当事人上诉请求的范围进行，当事人没有提出请求的，不予审查。但判决违反法律禁止性规定、侵害社会公共利益或者他人利益的除外。

**Article None** 35. The trial of a case on second instance shall be conducted within the scope of the appellant's claims on appeal; matters not raised by the parties shall not be examined, except where the judgment violates prohibitive provisions of law, violates public interest or the interests of others.

三十六、被上诉人在答辩中要求变更或者补充第一审判决内容的，第二审人民法院可以不予审查。

**Article None** 36. If the respondent requests in the response to change or supplement the content of the first instance judgment, the People's Court of second instance may not examine it.

三十七、第二审人民法院在审理上诉案件时，需要对原证据重新审查或者当事人提出新证据的，应当开庭审理。对事实清楚、适用法律正确和事实清楚，只是定性错误或者适用法律错误的案件，可以在询问当事人后迳行裁判。

**Article None** 37. When the People's Court of second instance hears an appeal case, if it is necessary to re-examine the original evidence or a party presents new evidence, a court session shall be held. For cases where the facts are clear and the application of law is correct, and for cases where the facts are clear but only the characterization or application of law is incorrect, the judgment may be rendered directly after questioning the parties.

三十八、第二审人民法院根据当事人提出的新证据对案件改判或者发回重审的，应当在判决书或者裁定书中写明对新证据的确认，不应当认为是第一审裁判错误。

**Article None** 38. If the People's Court of second instance reverses the judgment or remands the case for retrial based on new evidence presented by a party, it shall state in the judgment or ruling the confirmation of the new evidence, and such action shall not be deemed as an error in the first instance adjudication.

三十九、在第二审中，一方当事人提出新证据致使案件被发回重审的，对方当事人有权要求其补偿误工费、差旅费等费用。

**Article None** 39. In the second instance, if one party presents new evidence, thereby causing the case to be remanded for retrial, the opposing party has the right to require the said party to compensate for lost wages, travel expenses and other costs.
