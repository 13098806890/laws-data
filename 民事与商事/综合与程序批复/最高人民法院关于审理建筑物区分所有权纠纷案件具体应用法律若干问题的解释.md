# 最高人民法院关于审理建筑物区分所有权纠纷案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2009〕7号  
**公布日期**：2009-03-23  
**生效日期**：2009-10-01

> 最高人民法院《关于审理建筑物区分所有权纠纷案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2009年3月23日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1464次会议通过，现予公布，自2009年10月1日起施行。 2009年5月14日 最高人民法院 关于审理建筑物区分所有权纠纷案件 具体应用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2009〕7号 为正确审理建筑物区分所有权纠纷案件，依法保护当事人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国物权法》等法律的规定，结合民事审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条  依法登记取得或者根据物权法第二章第三节规定取得建筑物专有部分所有权的人，应当认定为物权法第六章所称的业主。 基于与建设单位之间的商品房买卖民事法律行为，已经合法占有建筑物专有部分，但尚未依法办理所有权登记的人，可以认定为物权法第六章所称的业主。 第二条  建筑区划内符合下列条件的房屋，以及车位、摊位等特定空间，应当认定为物权法第六章所称的专有部分： （一）具有构造上的独立性，能够明确区分； （二）具有利用上的独立性，可以排他使用； （三）能够登记成为特定业主所有权的客体。 规划上专属于特定房屋，且建设单位销售时已经根据规划列入该特定房屋买卖合同中的露台等，应当认定为物权法第六章所称专有部分的组成部分。 本条第一款所称房屋，包括整栋建筑物。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  依法登记取得或者根据物权法第二章第三节规定取得建筑物专有部分所有权的人，应当认定为物权法第六章所称的业主。  
基于与建设单位之间的商品房买卖民事法律行为，已经合法占有建筑物专有部分，但尚未依法办理所有权登记的人，可以认定为物权法第六章所称的业主。

**Article 1** A person who has obtained the ownership of a specific part of a building through registration according to law or pursuant to the provisions of Section 3, Chapter II of the Property Law shall be recognized as the owner referred to in Chapter VI of the Property Law.  
A person who, based on a civil legal act for the sale of commercial housing between an owner and a construction unit, has lawfully taken possession of a specific part of a building but has not yet completed the registration of ownership according to law may be recognized as the owner referred to in Chapter VI of the Property Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  建筑区划内符合下列条件的房屋，以及车位、摊位等特定空间，应当认定为物权法第六章所称的专有部分：  
（一）具有构造上的独立性，能够明确区分；  
（二）具有利用上的独立性，可以排他使用；  
（三）能够登记成为特定业主所有权的客体。  
规划上专属于特定房屋，且建设单位销售时已经根据规划列入该特定房屋买卖合同中的露台等，应当认定为物权法第六章所称专有部分的组成部分。  
本条第一款所称房屋，包括整栋建筑物。

**Article 2** Houses within the building subdivision that meet the following conditions, as well as specific spaces such as parking spaces and stalls, shall be recognized as the specific parts referred to in Chapter VI of the Property Law:  
(1) having structural independence and being capable of clear distinction;  
(2) having functional independence and being capable of exclusive use;  
(3) being capable of being registered as an object of a specific owner's ownership.  
Terraces that are exclusively designated for a specific house in the planning and that have been included in the sales contract for that specific house according to the planning at the time of sale by the construction unit, etc., shall be recognized as component parts of the specific part referred to in Chapter VI of the Property Law.  
The houses mentioned in paragraph 1 of this Article include entire buildings.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  除法律、行政法规规定的共有部分外，建筑区划内的以下部分，也应当认定为物权法第六章所称的共有部分：  
（一）建筑物的基础、承重结构、外墙、屋顶等基本结构部分，通道、楼梯、大堂等公共通行部分，消防、公共照明等附属设施、设备，避难层、设备层或者设备间等结构部分；  
（二）其他不属于业主专有部分，也不属于市政公用部分或者其他权利人所有的场所及设施等。  
建筑区划内的土地，依法由业主共同享有建设用地使用权，但属于业主专有的整栋建筑物的规划占地或者城镇公共道路、绿地占地除外。

**Article 3** In addition to the common parts prescribed by laws and administrative regulations, the following parts within the building subdivision shall also be recognized as the common parts referred to in Chapter VI of the Property Law:  
(1) basic structural parts such as the foundation, load-bearing structure, exterior walls, and roof of the building; public access parts such as passages, staircases, and lobbies; auxiliary facilities and equipment such as fire protection and public lighting; structural parts such as refuge floors, equipment floors, or equipment rooms;  
(2) other premises and facilities that are neither specific parts owned by an owner nor municipal public parts or owned by other right holders.  
The land within the building subdivision shall be subject to the common enjoyment of the right to use construction land by the owners according to law, except for the planned land occupation of the entire building that is an owner's specific part or the land occupied by urban public roads and green spaces.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  业主基于对住宅、经营性用房等专有部分特定使用功能的合理需要，无偿利用屋顶以及与其专有部分相对应的外墙面等共有部分的，不应认定为侵权。但违反法律、法规、管理规约，损害他人合法权益的除外。

**Article 4** Where an owner, based on the reasonable necessity for the specific use function of a specific part such as a residence or commercial premises, uses without compensation common parts such as the roof and the exterior wall corresponding to his specific part, such use shall not be recognized as an infringement, except where it violates laws, regulations, or management rules and damages the lawful rights and interests of others.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  建设单位按照配置比例将车位、车库，以出售、附赠或者出租等方式处分给业主的，应当认定其行为符合物权法第七十四条第一款有关“应当首先满足业主的需要”的规定。  
前款所称配置比例是指规划确定的建筑区划内规划用于停放汽车的车位、车库与房屋套数的比例。

**Article 5** Where a construction unit disposes of parking spaces and garages to owners by means of sale, gift, or lease in accordance with the allocation ratio, such act shall be recognized as complying with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 74 of the Property Law that "the needs of owners shall be satisfied first."  
The allocation ratio mentioned in the preceding paragraph refers to the ratio of the number of parking spaces and garages planned for parking motor vehicles within the building subdivision as determined by the planning to the number of housing units.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  建筑区划内在规划用于停放汽车的车位之外，占用业主共有道路或者其他场地增设的车位，应当认定为物权法第七十四条第三款所称的车位。

**Article 6** Parking spaces added by occupying common roads of the owners or other sites within the building subdivision, beyond the parking spaces planned for parking motor vehicles, shall be recognized as the parking spaces referred to in paragraph 3 of Article 74 of the Property Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  改变共有部分的用途、利用共有部分从事经营性活动、处分共有部分，以及业主大会依法决定或者管理规约依法确定应由业主共同决定的事项，应当认定为物权法第七十六条第一款第（七）项规定的有关共有和共同管理权利的“其他重大事项”。

**Article 7** Changing the use of common parts, using common parts for business activities, disposing of common parts, and matters that the owners' assembly decides according to law or that the management rules determine according to law shall be decided jointly by the owners shall be recognized as "other major matters" concerning common ownership and common management rights as specified in Item (7) of paragraph 1 of Article 76 of the Property Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  物权法第七十六条第二款和第八十条规定的专有部分面积和建筑物总面积，可以按照下列方法认定：  
（一）专有部分面积，按照不动产登记簿记载的面积计算；尚未进行物权登记的，暂按测绘机构的实测面积计算；尚未进行实测的，暂按房屋买卖合同记载的面积计算；  
（二）建筑物总面积，按照前项的统计总和计算。

**Article 8** The area of specific parts and the total building area as specified in paragraph 2 of Article 76 and Article 80 of the Property Law may be determined by the following methods:  
(1) the area of a specific part shall be calculated according to the area recorded in the real estate register; where no real right registration has been made, it shall be provisionally calculated according to the actual measured area by the surveying institution; where no actual measurement has been made, it shall be provisionally calculated according to the area recorded in the housing sales contract;  
(2) the total building area shall be calculated as the sum of the statistics under the preceding item.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  物权法第七十六条第二款规定的业主人数和总人数，可以按照下列方法认定：  
（一）业主人数，按照专有部分的数量计算，一个专有部分按一人计算。但建设单位尚未出售和虽已出售但尚未交付的部分，以及同一买受人拥有一个以上专有部分的，按一人计算；  
（二）总人数，按照前项的统计总和计算。

**Article 9** The number of owners and the total number of persons as specified in paragraph 2 of Article 76 of the Property Law may be determined by the following methods:  
(1) the number of owners shall be calculated according to the number of specific parts, with one specific part counted as one person. However, parts that the construction unit has not yet sold, or that have been sold but not yet delivered, and cases where the same buyer owns more than one specific part, shall be counted as one person;  
(2) the total number of persons shall be calculated as the sum of the statistics under the preceding item.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  业主将住宅改变为经营性用房，未按照物权法第七十七条的规定经有利害关系的业主同意，有利害关系的业主请求排除妨害、消除危险、恢复原状或者赔偿损失的，人民法院应予支持。  
将住宅改变为经营性用房的业主以多数有利害关系的业主同意其行为进行抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 10** Where an owner changes a residence into commercial premises without obtaining the consent of the interested owners as provided in Article 77 of the Property Law, and an interested owner requests the removal of nuisance, elimination of danger, restoration to the original condition, or compensation for losses, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where an owner who changes a residence into commercial premises defends on the ground that a majority of the interested owners consent to his act, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  业主将住宅改变为经营性用房，本栋建筑物内的其他业主，应当认定为物权法第七十七条所称“有利害关系的业主”。建筑区划内，本栋建筑物之外的业主，主张与自己有利害关系的，应证明其房屋价值、生活质量受到或者可能受到不利影响。

**Article 11** Where an owner changes a residence into commercial premises, other owners within the same building shall be recognized as the "interested owners" referred to in Article 77 of the Property Law. An owner outside the same building within the building subdivision who claims to have an interest in the matter shall prove that his housing value or quality of life has been or may be adversely affected.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  业主以业主大会或者业主委员会作出的决定侵害其合法权益或者违反了法律规定的程序为由，依据物权法第七十八条第二款的规定请求人民法院撤销该决定的，应当在知道或者应当知道业主大会或者业主委员会作出决定之日起一年内行使。

**Article 12** Where an owner, on the ground that a decision made by the owners' assembly or the owners' committee infringes upon his lawful rights and interests or violates the procedures prescribed by law, requests the People's Court to revoke such decision pursuant to paragraph 2 of Article 78 of the Property Law, he shall exercise the right within one year from the date he knows or should have known that the decision was made by the owners' assembly or the owners' committee.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  业主请求公布、查阅下列应当向业主公开的情况和资料的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）建筑物及其附属设施的维修资金的筹集、使用情况；  
（二）管理规约、业主大会议事规则，以及业主大会或者业主委员会的决定及会议记录；  
（三）物业服务合同、共有部分的使用和收益情况；  
（四）建筑区划内规划用于停放汽车的车位、车库的处分情况；  
（五）其他应当向业主公开的情况和资料。

**Article 13** Where an owner requests the publication or inspection of the following information and materials that shall be disclosed to the owners, the People's Court shall support such request:  
(1) the collection and use of maintenance funds for the building and its ancillary facilities;  
(2) the management rules, the rules of procedure of the owners' assembly, and the decisions and meeting records of the owners' assembly or the owners' committee;  
(3) the property service contract, and the use and income from common parts;  
(4) the disposal of parking spaces and garages planned for parking motor vehicles within the building subdivision;  
(5) other information and materials that shall be disclosed to the owners.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  建设单位或者其他行为人擅自占用、处分业主共有部分、改变其使用功能或者进行经营性活动，权利人请求排除妨害、恢复原状、确认处分行为无效或者赔偿损失的，人民法院应予支持。  
属于前款所称擅自进行经营性活动的情形，权利人请求行为人将扣除合理成本之后的收益用于补充专项维修资金或者业主共同决定的其他用途的，人民法院应予支持。行为人对成本的支出及其合理性承担举证责任。

**Article 14** Where a construction unit or another actor, without authorization, occupies or disposes of a common part of the owners, changes its use function, or engages in business activities, and the right holder requests the removal of nuisance, restoration to the original condition, confirmation of the invalidity of the disposal act, or compensation for losses, the People's Court shall support such request.  
In the case of engaging in business activities without authorization as mentioned in the preceding paragraph, where the right holder requests the actor to use the income after deducting reasonable costs for replenishing the special maintenance fund or for other purposes jointly decided by the owners, the People's Court shall support such request. The actor shall bear the burden of adducing evidence regarding the expenditure of costs and its reasonableness.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  业主或者其他行为人违反法律、法规、国家相关强制性标准、管理规约，或者违反业主大会、业主委员会依法作出的决定，实施下列行为的，可以认定为物权法第八十三条第二款所称的其他“损害他人合法权益的行为”：  
（一）损害房屋承重结构，损害或者违章使用电力、燃气、消防设施，在建筑物内放置危险、放射性物品等危及建筑物安全或者妨碍建筑物正常使用；  
（二）违反规定破坏、改变建筑物外墙面的形状、颜色等损害建筑物外观；  
（三）违反规定进行房屋装饰装修；  
（四）违章加建、改建，侵占、挖掘公共通道、道路、场地或者其他共有部分。

**Article 15** Where an owner or another actor violates laws, regulations, relevant mandatory national standards, management rules, or decisions lawfully made by the owners' assembly or the owners' committee, and commits any of the following acts, it may be recognized as other "acts harming the lawful rights and interests of others" referred to in paragraph 2 of Article 83 of the Property Law:  
(1) damaging the load-bearing structure of the building, damaging or illegally using electric power, gas, or fire protection facilities, placing dangerous or radioactive materials in the building, thereby endangering the safety of the building or hindering its normal use;  
(2) destroying or altering the shape, color, etc. of the exterior wall of the building in violation of regulations, thereby damaging the appearance of the building;  
(3) decorating or renovating the house in violation of regulations;  
(4) illegally constructing or altering, encroaching upon or excavating public passages, roads, sites, or other common parts.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  建筑物区分所有权纠纷涉及专有部分的承租人、借用人等物业使用人的，参照本解释处理。  
专有部分的承租人、借用人等物业使用人，根据法律、法规、管理规约、业主大会或者业主委员会依法作出的决定，以及其与业主的约定，享有相应权利，承担相应义务。

**Article 16** Where a dispute over the divided ownership of a building involves a lessee, borrower, or other property user of a specific part, this Interpretation shall apply mutatis mutandis.  
A lessee, borrower, or other property user of a specific part shall enjoy corresponding rights and assume corresponding obligations according to laws, regulations, management rules, decisions lawfully made by the owners' assembly or the owners' committee, and the agreement between such person and the owner.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  本解释所称建设单位，包括包销期满，按照包销合同约定的包销价格购买尚未销售的物业后，以自己名义对外销售的包销人。

**Article 17** The term "construction unit" in this Interpretation includes a exclusive sales agent who, after the expiration of the exclusive sales period, purchases the unsold property at the exclusive price agreed in the exclusive sales contract and then sells it externally in its own name.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  人民法院审理建筑物区分所有权案件中，涉及有关物权归属争议的，应当以法律、行政法规为依据。

**Article 18** Where a dispute over the attribution of real rights is involved in a case concerning the divided ownership of a building tried by the People's Court, the adjudication shall be based on laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条  本解释自2009年10月1日起施行。  
因物权法施行后实施的行为引起的建筑物区分所有权纠纷案件，适用本解释。  
本解释施行前已经终审，本解释施行后当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本解释。

**Article 19** This Interpretation shall come into force as of October 1, 2009.  
Dispute cases over divided ownership of buildings arising from acts committed after the Property Law came into force shall be governed by this Interpretation.  
Cases for which a final adjudication has been made before the coming into force of this Interpretation, and which are subject to an application for retrial by a party after the coming into force of this Interpretation or a decision to retry the case under the trial supervision procedure, shall not be governed by this Interpretation.
