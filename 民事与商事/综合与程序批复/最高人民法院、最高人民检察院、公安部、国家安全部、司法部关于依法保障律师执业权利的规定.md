# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部关于依法保障律师执业权利的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2015-09-16

> 第十五条　辩护律师提交与案件有关材料的，办案机关应当在工作时间和办公场所予以接待，当面了解辩护律师提交材料的目的、材料的来源和主要内容等有关情况并记录在案，与相关材料一并附卷，并出具回执。辩护律师应当提交原件，提交原件确有困难的，经办案机关准许，也可以提交复印件，经与原件核对无误后由辩护律师签名确认。辩护律师通过服务平台网上提交相关材料的，办案机关应当在网上出具回执。辩护律师应当及时向办案机关提供原件核对，并签名确认。 第十六条　在刑事诉讼审查起诉、审理期间，辩护律师书面申请调取公安机关、人民检察院在侦查、审查起诉期间收集但未提交的证明犯罪嫌疑人、被告人无罪或者罪轻的证据材料的，人民检察院、人民法院应当依法及时审查。经审查，认为辩护律师申请调取的证据材料已收集并且与案件事实有联系的，应当及时调取。相关证据材料提交后，人民检察院、人民法院应当及时通知辩护律师查阅、摘抄、复制。经审查决定不予调取的，应当书面说明理由。 第十七条　辩护律师申请向被害人或者其近亲属、被害人提供的证人收集与本案有关的材料的，人民检察院、人民法院应当在七日以内作出是否许可的决定，并通知辩护律师。辩护律师书面提出有关申请时，办案机关不许可的，应当书面说明理由；辩护律师口头提出申请的，办案机关可以口头答复。 第十八条　辩护律师申请人民检察院、人民法院收集、调取证据的，人民检察院、人民法院应当在三日以内作出是否同意的决定，并通知辩护律师。辩护律师书面提出有关申请时，办案机关不同意的，应当书面说明理由；辩护律师口头提出申请的，办案机关可以口头答复。 第十九条　辩护律师申请向正在服刑的罪犯收集与案件有关的材料的，监狱和其他监管机关在查验律师执业证书、律师事务所证明和犯罪嫌疑人、被告人委托书或法律援助公函后，应当及时安排并提供合适的场所和便利。 正在服刑的罪犯属于辩护律师所承办案件的被害人或者其近亲属、被害人提供的证人的，应当经人民检察院或者人民法院许可。 第二十条　在民事诉讼、行政诉讼过程中，律师因客观原因无法自行收集证据的，可以依法向人民法院申请调取。经审查符合规定的，人民法院应当予以调取。 第二十一条　侦查机关在案件侦查终结前，人民检察院、人民法院在审查批准、决定逮捕期间，最高人民法院在复核死刑案件期间，辩护律师提出要求的，办案机关应当听取辩护律师的意见。人民检察院审查起诉、第二审人民法院决定不开庭审理的，应当充分听取辩护律师的意见。 辩护律师要求当面反映意见或者提交证据材料的，办案机关应当依法办理，并制作笔录附卷。辩护律师提出的书面意见和证据材料，应当附卷。 第二十二条　辩护律师书面申请变更或者解除强制措施的，办案机关应当在三日以内作出处理决定。辩护律师的申请符合法律规定的，办案机关应当及时变更或者解除强制措施；经审查认为不应当变更或者解除强制措施的，应当告知辩护律师，并书面说明理由。 第二十三条　辩护律师在侦查、审查起诉、审判期间发现案件有关证据存在刑事诉讼法第五十四条规定的情形的，可以向办案机关申请排除非法证据。 辩护律师在开庭以前申请排除非法证据，人民法院对证据收集合法性有疑问的，应当依照刑事诉讼法第一百八十二条第二款的规定召开庭前会议，就非法证据排除问题了解情况，听取意见。 辩护律师申请排除非法证据的，办案机关应当听取辩护律师的意见，按照法定程序审查核实相关证据，并依法决定是否予以排除。 第二十四条　辩护律师在开庭以前提出召开庭前会议、回避、补充鉴定或者重新鉴定以及证人、鉴定人出庭等申请的，人民法院应当及时审查作出处理决定，并告知辩护律师。 第二十五条　人民法院确定案件开庭日期时，应当为律师出庭预留必要的准备时间并书面通知律师。律师因开庭日期冲突等正当理由申请变更开庭日期的，人民法院应当在不影响案件审理期限的情况下，予以考虑并调整日期，决定调整日期的，应当及时通知律师。

### 正文

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　在刑事诉讼审查起诉、审理期间，辩护律师书面申请调取公安机关、人民检察院在侦查、审查起诉期间收集但未提交的证明犯罪嫌疑人、被告人无罪或者罪轻的证据材料的，人民检察院、人民法院应当依法及时审查。经审查，认为辩护律师申请调取的证据材料已收集并且与案件事实有联系的，应当及时调取。相关证据材料提交后，人民检察院、人民法院应当及时通知辩护律师查阅、摘抄、复制。经审查决定不予调取的，应当书面说明理由。

**Article 16** During the period of examination and prosecution and adjudication in a criminal action, if a defense lawyer submits a written application to obtain evidence materials collected but not submitted by a public security organ or a People's Procuratorate during investigation or examination and prosecution that prove the criminal suspect or defendant is innocent or has a lesser crime, the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court shall examine it in a timely manner according to law. Upon examination, if they find that the evidence materials requested by the defense lawyer have been collected and are related to the facts of the case, they shall obtain them in a timely manner. After the relevant evidence materials are submitted, the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court shall promptly notify the defense lawyer to consult, extract, and copy them. If a decision is made not to obtain them, the reasons shall be stated in writing.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　辩护律师申请向被害人或者其近亲属、被害人提供的证人收集与本案有关的材料的，人民检察院、人民法院应当在七日以内作出是否许可的决定，并通知辩护律师。辩护律师书面提出有关申请时，办案机关不许可的，应当书面说明理由；辩护律师口头提出申请的，办案机关可以口头答复。

**Article 17** If a defense lawyer applies to collect materials related to the case from a victim or his close relatives, or witnesses provided by the victim, the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court shall, within seven days, make a decision on whether to permit it and notify the defense lawyer. When the defense lawyer submits such an application in writing and the case-handling authority does not permit it, it shall state the reasons in writing; if the defense lawyer applies orally, the case-handling authority may reply orally.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　辩护律师申请人民检察院、人民法院收集、调取证据的，人民检察院、人民法院应当在三日以内作出是否同意的决定，并通知辩护律师。辩护律师书面提出有关申请时，办案机关不同意的，应当书面说明理由；辩护律师口头提出申请的，办案机关可以口头答复。

**Article 18** If a defense lawyer applies to the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court to collect or obtain evidence, the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court shall, within three days, make a decision on whether to approve it and notify the defense lawyer. When the defense lawyer submits such an application in writing and the case-handling authority does not approve it, it shall state the reasons in writing; if the defense lawyer applies orally, the case-handling authority may reply orally.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　辩护律师申请向正在服刑的罪犯收集与案件有关的材料的，监狱和其他监管机关在查验律师执业证书、律师事务所证明和犯罪嫌疑人、被告人委托书或法律援助公函后，应当及时安排并提供合适的场所和便利。  
正在服刑的罪犯属于辩护律师所承办案件的被害人或者其近亲属、被害人提供的证人的，应当经人民检察院或者人民法院许可。

**Article 19** If a defense lawyer applies to collect materials related to the case from a criminal serving a sentence, the prison or other supervisory authority shall, after verifying the lawyer's practice certificate, the law firm's certification, and the power of attorney from the criminal suspect or defendant or the legal aid letter, promptly arrange and provide a suitable venue and convenience.  
If the criminal serving a sentence is a victim of the case handled by the defense lawyer, or a close relative of the victim, or a witness provided by the victim, the consent of the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court shall be obtained.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　在民事诉讼、行政诉讼过程中，律师因客观原因无法自行收集证据的，可以依法向人民法院申请调取。经审查符合规定的，人民法院应当予以调取。

**Article 20** In the course of a civil action or administrative action, if a lawyer is unable to collect evidence on his own due to objective reasons, he may apply to the People's Court according to law for obtaining it. After examination, if it complies with the provisions, the People's Court shall obtain it.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　侦查机关在案件侦查终结前，人民检察院、人民法院在审查批准、决定逮捕期间，最高人民法院在复核死刑案件期间，辩护律师提出要求的，办案机关应当听取辩护律师的意见。人民检察院审查起诉、第二审人民法院决定不开庭审理的，应当充分听取辩护律师的意见。  
辩护律师要求当面反映意见或者提交证据材料的，办案机关应当依法办理，并制作笔录附卷。辩护律师提出的书面意见和证据材料，应当附卷。

**Article 21** Before the conclusion of investigation by an investigation authority, during the period of examination and approval or decision of arrest by the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court, and during the period of review of death penalty cases by the Supreme People's Court, if the defense lawyer makes a request, the case-handling authority shall hear the opinions of the defense lawyer. When the People's Procuratorate examines and prosecutes, or the second-instance People's Court decides not to hold a court session, it shall fully hear the opinions of the defense lawyer.  
If the defense lawyer requests to express opinions in person or submit evidence materials, the case-handling authority shall handle it according to law and make a record attached to the file. The written opinions and evidence materials submitted by the defense lawyer shall be attached to the file.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　辩护律师书面申请变更或者解除强制措施的，办案机关应当在三日以内作出处理决定。辩护律师的申请符合法律规定的，办案机关应当及时变更或者解除强制措施；经审查认为不应当变更或者解除强制措施的，应当告知辩护律师，并书面说明理由。

**Article 22** If a defense lawyer submits a written application to modify or remove compulsory measures, the case-handling authority shall, within three days, make a decision on handling. If the defense lawyer's application complies with the provisions of the law, the case-handling authority shall promptly modify or remove the compulsory measures; if after examination it finds that the compulsory measures should not be modified or removed, it shall inform the defense lawyer and state the reasons in writing.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　辩护律师在侦查、审查起诉、审判期间发现案件有关证据存在[刑事诉讼法第五十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-54)规定的情形的，可以向办案机关申请排除非法证据。  
辩护律师在开庭以前申请排除非法证据，人民法院对证据收集合法性有疑问的，应当依照[刑事诉讼法第一百八十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-182)第二款的规定召开庭前会议，就非法证据排除问题了解情况，听取意见。  
辩护律师申请排除非法证据的，办案机关应当听取辩护律师的意见，按照法定程序审查核实相关证据，并依法决定是否予以排除。

**Article 23** If during investigation, examination and prosecution, or adjudication, a defense lawyer discovers that the evidence related to the case falls under the circumstances specified in Article 54 of the Criminal Procedure Law, he may apply to the case-handling authority to exclude illegal evidence.  
If the defense lawyer applies to exclude illegal evidence before trial and the People's Court has doubts about the legality of evidence collection, it shall convene a pre-trial meeting in accordance with the provisions of Article 182, paragraph 2 of the Criminal Procedure Law to understand the situation and hear opinions on the issue of excluding illegal evidence.  
When the defense lawyer applies to exclude illegal evidence, the case-handling authority shall hear the opinions of the defense lawyer, examine and verify the relevant evidence in accordance with statutory procedures, and decide whether to exclude it according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　辩护律师在开庭以前提出召开庭前会议、回避、补充鉴定或者重新鉴定以及证人、鉴定人出庭等申请的，人民法院应当及时审查作出处理决定，并告知辩护律师。

**Article 24** If a defense lawyer, before trial, submits applications such as for convening a pre-trial meeting, withdrawal, supplementary expert evaluation or re-evaluation, and appearance of witnesses or expert evaluators, the People's Court shall promptly examine and make a handling decision, and inform the defense lawyer.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　人民法院确定案件开庭日期时，应当为律师出庭预留必要的准备时间并书面通知律师。律师因开庭日期冲突等正当理由申请变更开庭日期的，人民法院应当在不影响案件审理期限的情况下，予以考虑并调整日期，决定调整日期的，应当及时通知律师。  
律师可以根据需要，向人民法院申请带律师助理参加庭审。律师助理参加庭审仅能从事相关辅助工作，不得发表辩护、代理意见。

**Article 25** When the People's Court determines the trial date of a case, it shall reserve necessary preparation time for the lawyer to appear in court and notify the lawyer in writing. If a lawyer applies to change the trial date due to legitimate reasons such as a conflict in trial dates, the People's Court shall, without affecting the case adjudication deadline, consider and adjust the date; if a decision to adjust the date is made, it shall promptly notify the lawyer.  
A lawyer may, as needed, apply to the People's Court to bring a paralegal to attend the trial. A paralegal attending the trial may only engage in related auxiliary work and shall not express defense or representation opinions.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　有条件的人民法院应当建立律师参与诉讼专门通道，律师进入人民法院参与诉讼确需安全检查的，应当与出庭履行职务的检察人员同等对待。有条件的人民法院应当设置专门的律师更衣室、休息室或者休息区域，并配备必要的桌椅、饮水及上网设施等，为律师参与诉讼提供便利。

**Article 26** People's Courts with the conditions shall establish a dedicated channel for lawyers to participate in litigation. If lawyers entering the People's Court to participate in litigation require security checks, they shall be treated equally as prosecutorial personnel appearing in court to perform their duties. People's Courts with the conditions shall set up dedicated lawyer changing rooms, rest rooms or rest areas, and equip them with necessary tables, chairs, drinking water, and internet access facilities, to provide convenience for lawyers to participate in litigation.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　法庭审理过程中，律师对审判人员、检察人员提出回避申请的，人民法院、人民检察院应当依法作出处理。

**Article 27** During court trial, if a lawyer applies for the withdrawal of an adjudicatory personnel or prosecutorial personnel, the People's Court or the People's Procuratorate shall handle it according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　法庭审理过程中，经审判长准许，律师可以向当事人、证人、鉴定人和有专门知识的人发问。

**Article 28** During court trial, with the permission of the presiding judge, a lawyer may pose questions to the parties, witnesses, expert evaluators, and persons with specialized knowledge.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　法庭审理过程中，律师可以就证据的真实性、合法性、关联性，从证明目的、证明效果、证明标准、证明过程等方面，进行法庭质证和相关辩论。

**Article 29** During court trial, a lawyer may conduct court cross-examination and relevant debate on the authenticity, legality, and relevance of evidence from aspects such as the purpose of proof, effect of proof, standard of proof, and process of proof.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　法庭审理过程中，律师可以就案件事实、证据和适用法律等问题，进行法庭辩论。

**Article 30** During court trial, a lawyer may conduct court debate on issues such as the facts of the case, evidence, and application of law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　法庭审理过程中，法官应当注重诉讼权利平等和控辩平衡。对于律师发问、质证、辩论的内容、方式、时间等，法庭应当依法公正保障，以便律师充分发表意见，查清案件事实。  
法庭审理过程中，法官可以对律师的发问、辩论进行引导，除发言过于重复、相关问题已在庭前会议达成一致、与案件无关或者侮辱、诽谤、威胁他人，故意扰乱法庭秩序的情况外，法官不得随意打断或者制止律师按程序进行的发言。

**Article 31** During court trial, the judge shall pay attention to the equality of litigation rights and the balance between prosecution and defense. With respect to the content, manner, and time of the lawyer's questioning, cross-examination, and debate, the court shall fairly guarantee them according to law, so that the lawyer may fully express opinions and ascertain the facts of the case.  
During court trial, the judge may guide the lawyer's questioning and debate. Except in cases where the remarks are too repetitive, the relevant issues have already been agreed upon at the pre-trial meeting, they are unrelated to the case, or they involve insults, defamation, threats to others, or intentional disruption of court order, the judge shall not arbitrarily interrupt or stop the lawyer's remarks made in accordance with procedures.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条 法庭审理过程中，律师可以提出证据材料，申请通知新的证人、有专门知识的人出庭，申请调取新的证据，申请重新鉴定或者勘验、检查。在民事诉讼中，申请有专门知识的人出庭，应当在举证期限届满前向人民法院申请，经法庭许可后才可以出庭。

**Article 32** During court trial, a lawyer may submit evidence materials, apply to summon new witnesses or persons with specialized knowledge to appear in court, apply to obtain new evidence, and apply for re-evaluation or inspection or examination. In a civil action, an application for a person with specialized knowledge to appear in court shall be submitted to the People's Court before the expiration of the time limit for submission of evidence, and such person may appear in court only with the permission of the court.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　法庭审理过程中，遇有被告人供述发生重大变化、拒绝辩护等重大情形，经审判长许可，辩护律师可以与被告人进行交流。

**Article 33** During court trial, in the event of significant circumstances such as a major change in the defendant's statement or refusal of defense, with the permission of the presiding judge, the defense lawyer may communicate with the defendant.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　法庭审理过程中，有下列情形之一的，律师可以向法庭申请休庭：  
（一）辩护律师因法定情形拒绝为被告人辩护的；  
（二）被告人拒绝辩护律师为其辩护的；  
（三）需要对新的证据作辩护准备的；  
（四）其他严重影响庭审正常进行的情形。

**Article 34** During court trial, if any of the following circumstances exists, a lawyer may apply to the court for an adjournment:  
(1) The defense lawyer refuses to defend the defendant due to statutory circumstances;  
(2) The defendant refuses the defense lawyer to defend him;  
(3) It is necessary to prepare for the defense of new evidence;  
(4) Other circumstances that seriously affect the normal conduct of the trial.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　辩护律师作无罪辩护的，可以当庭就量刑问题发表辩护意见，也可以庭后提交量刑辩护意见。

**Article 35** If a defense lawyer makes a not guilty plea, he may express defense opinions on the issue of sentencing in court, or may submit sentencing defense opinions after court.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　人民法院适用普通程序审理案件，应当在裁判文书中写明律师依法提出的辩护、代理意见，以及是否采纳的情况，并说明理由。

**Article 36** When a People's Court adjudicates a case under ordinary procedures, it shall state in the judgment document the defense and representation opinions proposed by the lawyer according to law, and whether they have been adopted, and state the reasons.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　对于诉讼中的重大程序信息和送达当事人的诉讼文书，办案机关应当通知辩护、代理律师。

**Article 37** For major procedural information in litigation and litigation documents served on parties, the case-handling authority shall notify the defense and representation lawyers.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　法庭审理过程中，律师就回避，案件管辖，非法证据排除，申请通知证人、鉴定人、有专门知识的人出庭，申请通知新的证人到庭，调取新的证据，申请重新鉴定、勘验等问题当庭提出申请，或者对法庭审理程序提出异议的，法庭原则上应当休庭进行审查，依照法定程序作出决定。其他律师有相同异议的，应一并提出，法庭一并休庭审查。法庭决定驳回申请或者异议的，律师可当庭提出复议。经复议后，律师应当尊重法庭的决定，服从法庭的安排。  
律师不服法庭决定保留意见的内容应当详细记入法庭笔录，可以作为上诉理由，或者向同级或者上一级人民检察院申诉、控告。

**Article 38** During court trial, if a lawyer makes an application in court on issues such as withdrawal, jurisdiction of the case, exclusion of illegal evidence, application to summon witnesses, expert evaluators, or persons with specialized knowledge to appear in court, application to summon new witnesses to appear in court, obtaining new evidence, application for re-evaluation or inspection, or raises an objection to the court trial procedure, the court shall, in principle, adjourn for examination and make a decision in accordance with statutory procedures. If other lawyers have the same objection, they shall raise it together, and the court shall adjourn for examination together. If the court decides to reject the application or objection, the lawyer may request reconsideration in court. After reconsideration, the lawyer shall respect the court's decision and comply with the court's arrangement.  
The content of the lawyer's reservation of opinion regarding the court's decision shall be recorded in detail in the court record, and may serve as a ground for appeal, or may be used to lodge a complaint or petition with the People's Procuratorate at the same level or the next higher level.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　律师申请查阅人民法院录制的庭审过程的录音、录像的，人民法院应当准许。

**Article 39** If a lawyer applies to review the audio or video recordings of the court trial process made by the People's Court, the People's Court shall permit it.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　侦查机关依法对在诉讼活动中涉嫌犯罪的律师采取强制措施后，应当在四十八小时以内通知其所在的律师事务所或者所属的律师协会。

**Article 40** After an investigation authority takes compulsory measures according to law against a lawyer suspected of committing a crime in litigation activities, it shall, within forty-eight hours, notify the law firm or the lawyers association to which he belongs.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　律师认为办案机关及其工作人员明显违反法律规定，阻碍律师依法履行辩护、代理职责，侵犯律师执业权利的，可以向该办案机关或者其上一级机关投诉。  
办案机关应当畅通律师反映问题和投诉的渠道，明确专门部门负责处理律师投诉，并公开联系方式。  
办案机关应当对律师的投诉及时调查，律师要求当面反映情况的，应当当面听取律师的意见。经调查情况属实的，应当依法立即纠正，及时答复律师，做好说明解释工作，并将处理情况通报其所在地司法行政机关或者所属的律师协会。

**Article 41** If a lawyer believes that a case-handling authority or its staff has obviously violated the provisions of the law, impeded the lawyer from performing defense or representation duties according to law, or infringed upon the lawyer's right to practice, he may file a complaint with that case-handling authority or its higher authority.  
The case-handling authority shall smooth the channels for lawyers to reflect issues and file complaints, designate a specific department to handle lawyer complaints, and make public its contact information.  
The case-handling authority shall investigate the lawyer's complaint in a timely manner. If the lawyer requests to report the situation in person, the authority shall hear the lawyer's opinions in person. If upon investigation the situation is confirmed, the authority shall promptly correct it according to law, reply to the lawyer in a timely manner, provide explanations, and notify the judicial administrative authority or the lawyers association in the jurisdiction where the lawyer practices of the handling results.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　在刑事诉讼中，律师认为办案机关及其工作人员的下列行为阻碍律师依法行使诉讼权利的，可以向同级或者上一级人民检察院申诉、控告：  
（一）未依法向律师履行告知、转达、通知和送达义务的；  
（二）办案机关认定律师不得担任辩护人、代理人的情形有误的；  
（三）对律师依法提出的申请，不接收、不答复的；  
（四）依法应当许可律师提出的申请未许可的；  
（五）依法应当听取律师的意见未听取的；  
（六）其他阻碍律师依法行使诉讼权利的行为。  
律师依照前款规定提出申诉、控告的，人民检察院应当在受理后十日以内进行审查，并将处理情况书面答复律师。情况属实的，通知有关机关予以纠正。情况不属实的，做好说明解释工作。  
人民检察院应当依法严格履行保障律师依法执业的法律监督职责，处理律师申诉控告。在办案过程中发现有阻碍律师依法行使诉讼权利行为的，应当依法、及时提出纠正意见。

**Article 42** In a criminal action, if a lawyer believes that any of the following acts by the case-handling authority or its staff impedes the lawyer from exercising litigation rights according to law, he may lodge a complaint or petition with the People's Procuratorate at the same level or the next higher level:  
(1) Failing to perform the obligation of informing, conveying, notifying, or serving documents to the lawyer according to law;  
(2) The case-handling authority erroneously determines that the lawyer is not qualified to act as defender or representative;  
(3) Not accepting or responding to an application lawfully submitted by the lawyer;  
(4) Not granting an application that should have been granted according to law;  
(5) Not hearing the lawyer's opinions when required by law;  
(6) Other acts that impede the lawyer from exercising litigation rights according to law.  
If the lawyer lodges a complaint or petition in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the People's Procuratorate shall, within ten days after acceptance, conduct an examination and reply to the lawyer in writing regarding the handling situation. If the situation is confirmed, it shall notify the relevant authority to correct it. If the situation is not confirmed, it shall provide explanations.  
The People's Procuratorate shall strictly fulfill its legal supervision duty of protecting lawyers' lawful practice according to law and handle lawyers' complaints and petitions. If it discovers acts that impede lawyers from exercising litigation rights according to law during case handling, it shall promptly propose corrective opinions according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　办案机关或者其上一级机关、人民检察院对律师提出的投诉、申诉、控告，经调查核实后要求有关机关予以纠正，有关机关拒不纠正或者累纠累犯的，应当由相关机关的纪检监察部门依照有关规定调查处理，相关责任人构成违纪的，给予纪律处分。

**Article 43** If, after investigation and verification, the case-handling authority or its higher authority or the People's Procuratorate requires the relevant authority to correct based on a lawyer's complaint, petition, or accusation, and the relevant authority refuses to correct or repeatedly commits the same violation, the disciplinary inspection and supervision department of the relevant authority shall investigate and handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions. If the responsible person constitutes a disciplinary violation, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　律师认为办案机关及其工作人员阻碍其依法行使执业权利的，可以向其所执业律师事务所所在地的市级司法行政机关、所属的律师协会申请维护执业权利。情况紧急的，可以向事发地的司法行政机关、律师协会申请维护执业权利。事发地的司法行政机关、律师协会应当给予协助。  
司法行政机关、律师协会应当建立维护律师执业权利快速处置机制和联动机制，及时安排专人负责协调处理。律师的维权申请合法有据的，司法行政机关、律师协会应当建议有关办案机关依法处理，有关办案机关应当将处理情况及时反馈司法行政机关、律师协会。  
司法行政机关、律师协会持有关证明调查核实律师权益保障或者违纪有关情况的，办案机关应当予以配合、协助，提供相关材料。

**Article 44** If a lawyer believes that a case-handling authority or its staff impedes him from exercising his right to practice according to law, he may apply to the municipal judicial administrative authority of the place where his practicing law firm is located or the lawyers association to which he belongs for protection of his right to practice. If the situation is urgent, he may apply to the judicial administrative authority or lawyers association at the place of occurrence for protection. The judicial administrative authority or lawyers association at the place of occurrence shall provide assistance.  
The judicial administrative authorities and lawyers associations shall establish a rapid response mechanism and a joint mechanism for protecting lawyers' right to practice, and promptly assign dedicated personnel to coordinate and handle the matter. If the lawyer's application for rights protection is lawful and justified, the judicial administrative authority or lawyers association shall suggest that the relevant case-handling authority handle it according to law, and the case-handling authority shall promptly inform the judicial administrative authority or lawyers association of the handling results.  
When the judicial administrative authority or lawyers association holds relevant certifications to investigate and verify matters related to protection of lawyers' rights and interests or disciplinary violations, the case-handling authority shall cooperate and assist, and provide relevant materials.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关、国家安全机关、司法行政机关和律师协会应当建立联席会议制度，定期沟通保障律师执业权利工作情况，及时调查处理侵犯律师执业权利的突发事件。

**Article 45** The People's Courts, People's Procuratorates, public security organs, state security organs, judicial administrative authorities, and lawyers associations shall establish a joint meeting system, regularly communicate on the work of protecting lawyers' right to practice, and promptly investigate and handle emergencies involving infringement of lawyers' right to practice.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　依法规范法律服务秩序，严肃查处假冒律师执业和非法从事法律服务的行为。对未取得律师执业证书或者已经被注销、吊销执业证书的人员以律师名义提供法律服务或者从事相关活动的，或者利用相关法律关于公民代理的规定从事诉讼代理或者辩护业务非法牟利的，依法追究责任，造成严重后果的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 46** The order of legal services shall be regulated according to law, and acts of falsely practicing as a lawyer or illegally engaging in legal services shall be seriously investigated and punished. For persons who have not obtained a lawyer's practice certificate or whose practice certificate has been cancelled or revoked and who provide legal services or engage in related activities in the name of a lawyer, or who use the provisions on citizen representation in relevant laws to engage in litigation representation or defense business for illegal profit, responsibility shall be investigated according to law; if serious consequences are caused, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　本规定所称“办案机关”，是指负责侦查、审查逮捕、审查起诉和审判工作的公安机关、国家安全机关、人民检察院和人民法院。

**Article 47** For the purposes of these Provisions, "case-handling authority" refers to the public security organs, state security organs, People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts responsible for investigation, examination and approval of arrest, examination and prosecution, and adjudication.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本规定所称“律师助理”，是指辩护、代理律师所在律师事务所的其他律师和申请律师执业实习人员。

**Article 48** For the purposes of these Provisions, "paralegal" refers to other lawyers in the law firm where the defense or representation lawyer practices and trainee lawyers applying for lawyer practice.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 49** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
