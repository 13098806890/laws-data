# 中华人民共和国信托法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2001-04-28  
**生效日期**：2001-10-01

> （2001年4月28日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十一次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　信托的设立 第三章　信托财产 第四章　信托当事人 第一节　委托人 第二节　受托人 第三节　受益人 第五章　信托的变更与终止 第六章　公益信托 第七章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了调整信托关系，规范信托行为，保护信托当事人的合法权益，促进信托事业的健康发展，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称信托，是指委托人基于对受托人的信任，将其财产权委托给受托人，由受托人按委托人的意愿以自己的名义，为受益人的利益或者特定目的，进行管理或者处分的行为。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了调整信托关系，规范信托行为，保护信托当事人的合法权益，促进信托事业的健康发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of regulating trust relationships, standardizing trust activities, protecting the lawful rights and interests of the parties to a trust, and promoting the healthy development of the trust undertaking.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称信托，是指委托人基于对受托人的信任，将其财产权委托给受托人，由受托人按委托人的意愿以自己的名义，为受益人的利益或者特定目的，进行管理或者处分的行为。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, trust refers to the act whereby the settlor, based on his trust in the trustee, entrusts his property rights to the trustee, and the trustee, in his own name but according to the settlor's wishes, manages or disposes of such property for the benefit of the beneficiary or for a specific purpose.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　委托人、受托人、受益人（以下统称信托当事人）在中华人民共和国境内进行民事、营业、公益信托活动，适用本法。

**Article 3** This Law shall apply to civil, commercial and charitable trust activities conducted within the territory of the People's Republic of China by settlers, trustees and beneficiaries (hereinafter collectively referred to as the parties to a trust).

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　受托人采取信托机构形式从事信托活动，其组织和管理由国务院制定具体办法。

**Article 4** Where trustees conduct trust activities in the form of trust institutions, the State Council shall formulate specific measures for their organization and administration.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　信托当事人进行信托活动，必须遵守法律、行政法规，遵循自愿、公平和诚实信用原则，不得损害国家利益和社会公共利益。

**Article 5** The parties to a trust shall, when conducting trust activities, comply with laws and administrative regulations, adhere to the principles of voluntariness, fairness, honesty and credit, and shall not harm the interests of the State or the public interests.

### 第二章　信托的设立

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　设立信托，必须有合法的信托目的。

**Article 6** For the creation of a trust, there shall be a lawful trust purpose.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　设立信托，必须有确定的信托财产，并且该信托财产必须是委托人合法所有的财产。  
本法所称财产包括合法的财产权利。

**Article 7** For the creation of a trust, there shall be ascertainable trust property, and such trust property shall be property lawfully owned by the settlor.  
For the purposes of this Law, property includes lawful property rights.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　设立信托，应当采取书面形式。  
书面形式包括信托合同、遗嘱或者法律、行政法规规定的其他书面文件等。  
采取信托合同形式设立信托的，信托合同签订时，信托成立。采取其他书面形式设立信托的，受托人承诺信托时，信托成立。

**Article 8** A trust shall be created in writing.  
The written form includes a trust contract, a will, or other written documents as prescribed by laws or administrative regulations.  
Where a trust is created by means of a trust contract, the trust shall be established upon the execution of the trust contract. Where a trust is created by other written forms, the trust shall be established upon the trustee's acceptance of the trust.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　设立信托，其书面文件应当载明下列事项：  
（一）信托目的；  
（二）委托人、受托人的姓名或者名称、住所；  
（三）受益人或者受益人范围；  
（四）信托财产的范围、种类及状况；  
（五）受益人取得信托利益的形式、方法。  
除前款所列事项外，可以载明信托期限、信托财产的管理方法、受托人的报酬、新受托人的选任方式、信托终止事由等事项。

**Article 9** For the creation of a trust, the written document shall specify the following matters:  
(1) the trust purpose;  
(2) the name or title and domicile of the settlor and the trustee;  
(3) the beneficiary or the scope of beneficiaries;  
(4) the scope, type and status of the trust property;  
(5) the form and method by which the beneficiary receives trust benefits.  
In addition to the matters set forth in the preceding paragraph, matters such as the trust term, the method of administration of the trust property, the remuneration of the trustee, the method of appointing a new trustee, and the causes for termination of the trust may be specified.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　设立信托，对于信托财产，有关法律、行政法规规定应当办理登记手续的，应当依法办理信托登记。  
未依照前款规定办理信托登记的，应当补办登记手续；不补办的，该信托不产生效力。

**Article 10** For the creation of a trust, where the relevant laws or administrative regulations provide that registration procedures shall be carried out for the trust property, the trust registration shall be carried out according to law.  
Where the trust registration is not carried out in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the registration procedures shall be completed; if not completed, the trust shall have no effect.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　有下列情形之一的，信托无效：  
（一）信托目的违反法律、行政法规或者损害社会公共利益；  
（二）信托财产不能确定；  
（三）委托人以非法财产或者本法规定不得设立信托的财产设立信托；  
（四）专以诉讼或者讨债为目的设立信托；  
（五）受益人或者受益人范围不能确定；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 11** A trust shall be void under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the trust purpose violates a law or administrative regulation or harms the public interests;  
(2) the trust property cannot be ascertained;  
(3) the settlor creates a trust with illegal property or property that may not be used to create a trust under this Law;  
(4) the trust is created exclusively for the purpose of litigation or debt collection;  
(5) the beneficiary or the scope of beneficiaries cannot be ascertained;  
(6) other circumstances as prescribed by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　委托人设立信托损害其债权人利益的，债权人有权申请人民法院撤销该信托。  
人民法院依照前款规定撤销信托的，不影响善意受益人已经取得的信托利益。  
本条第一款规定的申请权，自债权人知道或者应当知道撤销原因之日起一年内不行使的，归于消灭。

**Article 12** Where the creation of a trust by the settlor harms the interests of his creditors, the creditors shall have the right to apply to the People's Court for revocation of the trust.  
Where the People's Court revokes the trust in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the trust benefits already obtained by a bona fide beneficiary shall not be affected.  
The right to apply as provided in the first paragraph of this Article shall lapse if it is not exercised within one year from the date on which the creditor knows or should have known the cause for revocation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　设立遗嘱信托，应当遵守继承法关于遗嘱的规定。  
遗嘱指定的人拒绝或者无能力担任受托人的，由受益人另行选任受托人；受益人为无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人的，依法由其监护人代行选任。遗嘱对选任受托人另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 13** The creation of a testamentary trust shall comply with the provisions of the succession law regarding wills.  
Where the person designated by the will refuses or is unable to act as trustee, the beneficiary shall appoint another trustee; where the beneficiary is a person without capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct, his guardian shall make the appointment on his behalf according to law. If the will has other provisions regarding the appointment of a trustee, such provisions shall prevail.

### 第三章　信托财产

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　受托人因承诺信托而取得的财产是信托财产。  
受托人因信托财产的管理运用、处分或者其他情形而取得的财产，也归入信托财产。  
法律、行政法规禁止流通的财产，不得作为信托财产。  
法律、行政法规限制流通的财产，依法经有关主管部门批准后，可以作为信托财产。

**Article 14** Property acquired by the trustee upon acceptance of the trust shall be trust property.  
Property acquired by the trustee through the administration, use, disposition or other circumstances of the trust property shall also be included in the trust property.  
Property the circulation of which is prohibited by laws or administrative regulations shall not be used as trust property.  
Property the circulation of which is restricted by laws or administrative regulations may, after approval by the relevant competent authority according to law, be used as trust property.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　信托财产与委托人未设立信托的其他财产相区别。设立信托后，委托人死亡或者依法解散、被依法撤销、被宣告破产时，委托人是唯一受益人的，信托终止，信托财产作为其遗产或者清算财产；委托人不是唯一受益人的，信托存续，信托财产不作为其遗产或者清算财产；但作为共同受益人的委托人死亡或者依法解散、被依法撤销、被宣告破产时，其信托受益权作为其遗产或者清算财产。

**Article 15** Trust property shall be separate from the other property of the settlor which is not placed in trust. After the creation of a trust, where the settlor dies or is dissolved, revoked or declared bankrupt according to law, if the settlor is the sole beneficiary, the trust shall terminate and the trust property shall be treated as his estate or liquidation property; if the settlor is not the sole beneficiary, the trust shall continue and the trust property shall not be treated as his estate or liquidation property; provided that where the settlor who is a co-beneficiary dies or is dissolved, revoked or declared bankrupt according to law, his trust beneficial right shall be treated as his estate or liquidation property.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　信托财产与属于受托人所有的财产（以下简称固有财产）相区别，不得归入受托人的固有财产或者成为固有财产的一部分。  
受托人死亡或者依法解散、被依法撤销、被宣告破产而终止，信托财产不属于其遗产或者清算财产。

**Article 16** Trust property shall be separate from property owned by the trustee (hereinafter referred to as the trustee's own property) and shall not be included in or become part of the trustee's own property.  
Where the trustee dies or is terminated due to dissolution, revocation or declaration of bankruptcy according to law, the trust property shall not form part of his estate or liquidation property.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　除因下列情形之一外，对信托财产不得强制执行：  
（一）设立信托前债权人已对该信托财产享有优先受偿的权利，并依法行使该权利的；  
（二）受托人处理信托事务所产生债务，债权人要求清偿该债务的；  
（三）信托财产本身应担负的税款；  
（四）法律规定的其他情形。  
对于违反前款规定而强制执行信托财产，委托人、受托人或者受益人有权向人民法院提出异议。

**Article 17** No compulsory execution shall be imposed on trust property except under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) prior to the creation of the trust, the creditors already had a right to be satisfied out of the trust property with priority, and they exercise such right according to law;  
(2) debts arise from the trustee's handling of trust affairs, and the creditors demand satisfaction of such debts;  
(3) taxes that shall be borne by the trust property itself;  
(4) other circumstances as prescribed by law.  
Where compulsory execution is imposed on trust property in violation of the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the settlor, trustee or beneficiary shall have the right to raise an objection to the People's Court.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　受托人管理运用、处分信托财产所产生的债权，不得与其固有财产产生的债务相抵销。  
受托人管理运用、处分不同委托人的信托财产所产生的债权债务，不得相互抵销。

**Article 18** Claims arising from the trustee's administration, use or disposition of trust property shall not be set off against debts arising from his own property.  
Claims and debts arising from the trustee's administration, use or disposition of trust property of different settlers shall not be set off against each other.

### 第四章　信托当事人

#### 第一节　委托人

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　委托人应当是具有完全民事行为能力的自然人、法人或者依法成立的其他组织。

**Article 19** The settlor shall be a natural person with full capacity for civil conduct, a legal person, or another organization established according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　委托人有权了解其信托财产的管理运用、处分及收支情况，并有权要求受托人作出说明。  
委托人有权查阅、抄录或者复制与其信托财产有关的信托帐目以及处理信托事务的其他文件。

**Article 20** The settlor shall have the right to be informed of the administration, use, disposition, receipts and payments of his trust property, and to request the trustee to provide explanations.  
The settlor shall have the right to inspect, transcribe or copy the trust accounts relating to his trust property and other documents relating to the handling of trust affairs.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　因设立信托时未能预见的特别事由，致使信托财产的管理方法不利于实现信托目的或者不符合受益人的利益时，委托人有权要求受托人调整该信托财产的管理方法。

**Article 21** Where due to special circumstances unforeseen at the time of the creation of the trust, the method of administration of the trust property is not conducive to achieving the trust purpose or does not serve the interests of the beneficiary, the settlor shall have the right to request the trustee to adjust the method of administration of the trust property.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　受托人违反信托目的处分信托财产或者因违背管理职责、处理信托事务不当致使信托财产受到损失的，委托人有权申请人民法院撤销该处分行为，并有权要求受托人恢复信托财产的原状或者予以赔偿；该信托财产的受让人明知是违反信托目的而接受该财产的，应当予以返还或者予以赔偿。  
前款规定的申请权，自委托人知道或者应当知道撤销原因之日起一年内不行使的，归于消灭。

**Article 22** Where the trustee disposes of trust property in violation of the trust purpose, or causes loss to the trust property due to breach of his management duties or improper handling of trust affairs, the settlor shall have the right to apply to the People's Court for revocation of such disposition, and to request the trustee to restore the trust property to its original state or to make compensation; if the transferee of the trust property knew that the property was accepted in violation of the trust purpose, he shall return the property or make compensation.  
The right to apply as provided in the preceding paragraph shall lapse if it is not exercised within one year from the date on which the settlor knows or should have known the cause for revocation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　受托人违反信托目的处分信托财产或者管理运用、处分信托财产有重大过失的，委托人有权依照信托文件的规定解任受托人，或者申请人民法院解任受托人。

**Article 23** Where the trustee disposes of trust property in violation of the trust purpose, or is grossly negligent in the administration, use or disposition of trust property, the settlor shall have the right to remove the trustee in accordance with the provisions of the trust instrument, or to apply to the People's Court for removal of the trustee.

#### 第二节　受托人

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　受托人应当是具有完全民事行为能力的自然人、法人。  
法律、行政法规对受托人的条件另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 24** The trustee shall be a natural person with full capacity for civil conduct or a legal person.  
Where laws or administrative regulations have other provisions regarding the qualifications of a trustee, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　受托人应当遵守信托文件的规定，为受益人的最大利益处理信托事务。  
受托人管理信托财产，必须恪尽职守，履行诚实、信用、谨慎、有效管理的义务。

**Article 25** The trustee shall comply with the provisions of the trust instrument and handle trust affairs for the best interests of the beneficiary.  
In administering trust property, the trustee shall discharge his duties with prudence, and fulfill the obligations of honesty, credit, care and effective management.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　受托人除依照本法规定取得报酬外，不得利用信托财产为自己谋取利益。  
受托人违反前款规定，利用信托财产为自己谋取利益的，所得利益归入信托财产。

**Article 26** The trustee shall not use the trust property to seek benefits for himself, except for obtaining remuneration in accordance with the provisions of this Law.  
Where the trustee, in violation of the provisions of the preceding paragraph, uses the trust property to seek benefits for himself, the benefits so obtained shall be included in the trust property.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　受托人不得将信托财产转为其固有财产。受托人将信托财产转为其固有财产的，必须恢复该信托财产的原状；造成信托财产损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 27** The trustee shall not convert trust property into his own property. Where the trustee converts trust property into his own property, he shall restore the trust property to its original state; if loss is caused to the trust property, he shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　受托人不得将其固有财产与信托财产进行交易或者将不同委托人的信托财产进行相互交易，但信托文件另有规定或者经委托人或者受益人同意，并以公平的市场价格进行交易的除外。  
受托人违反前款规定，造成信托财产损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 28** The trustee shall not conduct transactions between his own property and trust property or between trust properties of different settlers, except where otherwise provided in the trust instrument or with the consent of the settlor or beneficiary and at fair market prices.  
Where the trustee violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph and causes loss to the trust property, he shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　受托人必须将信托财产与其固有财产分别管理、分别记帐，并将不同委托人的信托财产分别管理、分别记帐。

**Article 29** The trustee shall administer and maintain separate accounts for trust property and his own property, and shall also administer and maintain separate accounts for trust properties of different settlers.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　受托人应当自己处理信托事务，但信托文件另有规定或者有不得已事由的，可以委托他人代为处理。  
受托人依法将信托事务委托他人代理的，应当对他人处理信托事务的行为承担责任。

**Article 30** The trustee shall handle trust affairs by himself, except where otherwise provided in the trust instrument or where there is a compelling reason, in which case he may entrust another person to handle them on his behalf.  
Where the trustee, according to law, entrusts another person to handle trust affairs on his behalf, he shall be liable for the acts of such person in handling the trust affairs.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　同一信托的受托人有两个以上的，为共同受托人。  
共同受托人应当共同处理信托事务，但信托文件规定对某些具体事务由受托人分别处理的，从其规定。  
共同受托人共同处理信托事务，意见不一致时，按信托文件规定处理；信托文件未规定的，由委托人、受益人或者其利害关系人决定。

**Article 31** Where there are two or more trustees for the same trust, they are co-trustees.  
Co-trustees shall handle trust affairs jointly, except where the trust instrument provides that certain specific affairs shall be handled separately by the trustees, in which case such provisions shall prevail.  
Where co-trustees handle trust affairs jointly and fail to reach a unanimous opinion, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the trust instrument; if the trust instrument does not provide for such matter, it shall be decided by the settlor, the beneficiary or their interested parties.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　共同受托人处理信托事务对第三人所负债务，应当承担连带清偿责任。第三人对共同受托人之一所作的意思表示，对其他受托人同样有效。  
共同受托人之一违反信托目的处分信托财产或者因违背管理职责、处理信托事务不当致使信托财产受到损失的，其他受托人应当承担连带赔偿责任。

**Article 32** Co-trustees shall bear joint and several liability for debts owed to third parties arising from the handling of trust affairs. The manifestation of intention made by a third party to any one of the co-trustees shall be equally effective against the other trustees.  
Where one of the co-trustees disposes of trust property in violation of the trust purpose, or causes loss to the trust property due to breach of his management duties or improper handling of trust affairs, the other trustees shall bear joint and several compensation liability.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　受托人必须保存处理信托事务的完整记录。  
受托人应当每年定期将信托财产的管理运用、处分及收支情况，报告委托人和受益人。  
受托人对委托人、受益人以及处理信托事务的情况和资料负有依法保密的义务。

**Article 33** The trustee shall keep complete records of the handling of trust affairs.  
The trustee shall, annually and periodically, report to the settlor and the beneficiary on the administration, use, disposition, receipts and payments of the trust property.  
The trustee shall have the obligation to keep confidential, according to law, the information and materials concerning the settlor, the beneficiary, and the handling of trust affairs.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　受托人以信托财产为限向受益人承担支付信托利益的义务。

**Article 34** The trustee shall be obligated to pay trust benefits to the beneficiary to the extent of the trust property.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　受托人有权依照信托文件的约定取得报酬。信托文件未作事先约定的，经信托当事人协商同意，可以作出补充约定；未作事先约定和补充约定的，不得收取报酬。  
约定的报酬经信托当事人协商同意，可以增减其数额。

**Article 35** The trustee shall have the right to obtain remuneration in accordance with the trust instrument. Where no prior provision is made in the trust instrument, supplementary provisions may be made through consultation and agreement among the parties to the trust; if there is no prior provision or supplementary provision, no remuneration shall be charged.  
The amount of the agreed remuneration may be increased or decreased through consultation and agreement among the parties to the trust.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　受托人违反信托目的处分信托财产或者因违背管理职责、处理信托事务不当致使信托财产受到损失的，在未恢复信托财产的原状或者未予赔偿前，不得请求给付报酬。

**Article 36** Where the trustee disposes of trust property in violation of the trust purpose, or causes loss to the trust property due to breach of his management duties or improper handling of trust affairs, he shall not demand payment of remuneration before restoring the trust property to its original state or making compensation.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　受托人因处理信托事务所支出的费用、对第三人所负债务，以信托财产承担。受托人以其固有财产先行支付的，对信托财产享有优先受偿的权利。  
受托人违背管理职责或者处理信托事务不当对第三人所负债务或者自己所受到的损失，以其固有财产承担。

**Article 37** Expenses incurred by the trustee in handling trust affairs and debts owed to third parties shall be borne by the trust property. Where the trustee makes advance payment out of his own property, he shall have the right to be reimbursed out of the trust property with priority.  
Where the trustee incurs debts to third parties or suffers losses due to breach of his management duties or improper handling of trust affairs, such debts or losses shall be borne by his own property.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　设立信托后，经委托人和受益人同意，受托人可以辞任。本法对公益信托的受托人辞任另有规定的，从其规定。  
受托人辞任的，在新受托人选出前仍应履行管理信托事务的职责。

**Article 38** After the creation of a trust, the trustee may resign with the consent of the settlor and the beneficiary. Where this Law has other provisions concerning the resignation of a trustee of a charitable trust, such provisions shall prevail.  
Where the trustee resigns, he shall continue to perform his duties in administering trust affairs until a new trustee is appointed.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　受托人有下列情形之一的，其职责终止：  
（一）死亡或者被依法宣告死亡；  
（二）被依法宣告为无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人；  
（三）被依法撤销或者被宣告破产；  
（四）依法解散或者法定资格丧失；  
（五）辞任或者被解任；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定的其他情形。  
受托人职责终止时，其继承人或者遗产管理人、监护人、清算人应当妥善保管信托财产，协助新受托人接管信托事务。

**Article 39** The duties of a trustee shall terminate under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) death or being declared dead according to law;  
(2) being declared as a person without capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct according to law;  
(3) being revoked or declared bankrupt according to law;  
(4) dissolution according to law or loss of legal qualifications;  
(5) resignation or removal;  
(6) other circumstances as prescribed by laws or administrative regulations.  
Upon the termination of the trustee's duties, his heirs or estate administrators, guardians, or liquidators shall take good care of the trust property and assist the new trustee in taking over the trust affairs.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　受托人职责终止的，依照信托文件规定选任新受托人；信托文件未规定的，由委托人选任；委托人不指定或者无能力指定的，由受益人选任；受益人为无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人的，依法由其监护人代行选任。  
原受托人处理信托事务的权利和义务，由新受托人承继。

**Article 40** Where the trustee's duties terminate, a new trustee shall be appointed in accordance with the trust instrument; if the trust instrument does not provide for such appointment, the settlor shall appoint the new trustee; if the settlor fails to appoint or is unable to appoint, the beneficiary shall appoint; where the beneficiary is a person without capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct, his guardian shall make the appointment on his behalf according to law.  
The rights and obligations of the original trustee in handling trust affairs shall be succeeded by the new trustee.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　受托人有[本法第三十九条](#art-39)第一款第（三）项至第（六）项所列情形之一，职责终止的，应当作出处理信托事务的报告，并向新受托人办理信托财产和信托事务的移交手续。  
前款报告经委托人或者受益人认可，原受托人就报告中所列事项解除责任。但原受托人有不正当行为的除外。

**Article 41** Where the trustee's duties terminate under any of the circumstances set forth in subparagraphs (3) through (6) of the first paragraph of Article 39 of this Law, he shall make a report on the handling of trust affairs and complete the procedures for transferring the trust property and trust affairs to the new trustee.  
Where the report referred to in the preceding paragraph is confirmed by the settlor or the beneficiary, the original trustee shall be released from liability with respect to the matters stated in the report, unless the original trustee has engaged in improper conduct.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　共同受托人之一职责终止的，信托财产由其他受托人管理和处分。

**Article 42** Where the duties of one of the co-trustees terminate, the trust property shall be administered and disposed of by the other trustees.

#### 第三节　受益人

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　受益人是在信托中享有信托受益权的人。受益人可以是自然人、法人或者依法成立的其他组织。  
委托人可以是受益人，也可以是同一信托的唯一受益人。  
受托人可以是受益人，但不得是同一信托的唯一受益人。

**Article 43** A beneficiary is a person who enjoys the trust beneficial right in a trust. A beneficiary may be a natural person, a legal person, or another organization established according to law.  
The settlor may be a beneficiary, and may also be the sole beneficiary of the same trust.  
The trustee may be a beneficiary, but shall not be the sole beneficiary of the same trust.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　受益人自信托生效之日起享有信托受益权。信托文件另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 44** A beneficiary shall enjoy the trust beneficial right from the date on which the trust takes effect. If the trust instrument provides otherwise, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　共同受益人按照信托文件的规定享受信托利益。信托文件对信托利益的分配比例或者分配方法未作规定的，各受益人按照均等的比例享受信托利益。

**Article 45** Co-beneficiaries shall enjoy trust benefits in accordance with the provisions of the trust instrument. Where the trust instrument does not provide for the proportion or method of distribution of trust benefits, each beneficiary shall enjoy trust benefits in equal proportion.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　受益人可以放弃信托受益权。  
全体受益人放弃信托受益权的，信托终止。  
部分受益人放弃信托受益权的，被放弃的信托受益权按下列顺序确定归属：  
（一）信托文件规定的人；  
（二）其他受益人；  
（三）委托人或者其继承人。

**Article 46** A beneficiary may renounce the trust beneficial right.  
Where all beneficiaries renounce the trust beneficial right, the trust shall terminate.  
Where some beneficiaries renounce the trust beneficial right, the renounced trust beneficial right shall be attributed in the following order:  
(1) the person designated in the trust instrument;  
(2) other beneficiaries;  
(3) the settlor or his heirs.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　受益人不能清偿到期债务的，其信托受益权可以用于清偿债务，但法律、行政法规以及信托文件有限制性规定的除外。

**Article 47** Where a beneficiary is unable to pay his due debts, his trust beneficial right may be used to satisfy such debts, except where there are restrictive provisions in laws, administrative regulations or the trust instrument.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　受益人的信托受益权可以依法转让和继承，但信托文件有限制性规定的除外。

**Article 48** The trust beneficial right of a beneficiary may be transferred and inherited according to law, except where there are restrictive provisions in the trust instrument.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　受益人可以行使[本法第二十条](#art-20)至第二十三条规定的委托人享有的权利。受益人行使上述权利，与委托人意见不一致时，可以申请人民法院作出裁定。  
受托人有[本法第二十二条](#art-22)第一款所列行为，共同受益人之一申请人民法院撤销该处分行为的，人民法院所作出的撤销裁定，对全体共同受益人有效。

**Article 49** A beneficiary may exercise the rights enjoyed by the settlor as provided in Articles 20 through 23 of this Law. Where the beneficiary's exercise of the aforesaid rights is inconsistent with the settlor's opinion, the beneficiary may apply to the People's Court for a ruling.  
Where the trustee commits any of the acts listed in the first paragraph of Article 22 of this Law, and one of the co-beneficiaries applies to the People's Court for revocation of such disposition, the ruling of revocation made by the People's Court shall be binding on all co-beneficiaries.

### 第五章　信托的变更与终止

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　委托人是唯一受益人的，委托人或者其继承人可以解除信托。信托文件另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 50** Where the settlor is the sole beneficiary, the settlor or his heir may dissolve the trust. If the trust instrument provides otherwise, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　设立信托后，有下列情形之一的，委托人可以变更受益人或者处分受益人的信托受益权：  
（一）受益人对委托人有重大侵权行为；  
（二）受益人对其他共同受益人有重大侵权行为；  
（三）经受益人同意；  
（四）信托文件规定的其他情形。  
有前款第（一）项、第（三）项、第（四）项所列情形之一的，委托人可以解除信托。

**Article 51** After the creation of a trust, under any of the following circumstances, the settlor may change the beneficiary or dispose of the beneficiary's trust beneficial right:  
(1) the beneficiary commits a major tort against the settlor;  
(2) the beneficiary commits a major tort against other co-beneficiaries;  
(3) with the consent of the beneficiary;  
(4) other circumstances as provided in the trust instrument.  
Under any of the circumstances set forth in subparagraphs (1), (3) or (4) of the preceding paragraph, the settlor may dissolve the trust.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　信托不因委托人或者受托人的死亡、丧失民事行为能力、依法解散、被依法撤销或者被宣告破产而终止，也不因受托人的辞任而终止。但本法或者信托文件另有规定的除外。

**Article 52** A trust shall not terminate due to the death, loss of capacity for civil conduct, dissolution, revocation or declaration of bankruptcy according to law of the settlor or the trustee, nor shall it terminate due to the resignation of the trustee, except where otherwise provided in this Law or the trust instrument.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　有下列情形之一的，信托终止：  
（一）信托文件规定的终止事由发生；  
（二）信托的存续违反信托目的；  
（三）信托目的已经实现或者不能实现；  
（四）信托当事人协商同意；  
（五）信托被撤销；  
（六）信托被解除。

**Article 53** A trust shall terminate under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the cause for termination provided in the trust instrument occurs;  
(2) the continuation of the trust violates the trust purpose;  
(3) the trust purpose has been achieved or cannot be achieved;  
(4) the parties to the trust agree to terminate;  
(5) the trust is revoked;  
(6) the trust is dissolved.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　信托终止的，信托财产归属于信托文件规定的人；信托文件未规定的，按下列顺序确定归属：  
（一）受益人或者其继承人；  
（二）委托人或者其继承人。

**Article 54** Upon termination of a trust, the trust property shall belong to the person designated in the trust instrument; if the trust instrument does not so designate, the attribution shall be determined in the following order:  
(1) the beneficiary or his heir;  
(2) the settlor or his heir.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　依照前条规定，信托财产的归属确定后，在该信托财产转移给权利归属人的过程中，信托视为存续，权利归属人视为受益人。

**Article 55** After the attribution of the trust property is determined in accordance with the preceding Article, during the process of transferring the trust property to the person entitled to it, the trust shall be deemed to continue, and the person entitled to it shall be deemed to be the beneficiary.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　信托终止后，人民法院依据[本法第十七条](#art-17)的规定对原信托财产进行强制执行的，以权利归属人为被执行人。

**Article 56** After the termination of a trust, if the People's Court imposes compulsory execution on the former trust property in accordance with Article 17 of this Law, the person entitled to the trust property shall be the person subject to execution.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　信托终止后，受托人依照本法规定行使请求给付报酬、从信托财产中获得补偿的权利时，可以留置信托财产或者对信托财产的权利归属人提出请求。

**Article 57** After the termination of a trust, when exercising the right to demand payment of remuneration or to obtain compensation from the trust property in accordance with this Law, the trustee may retain the trust property or make a claim against the person entitled to the trust property.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　信托终止的，受托人应当作出处理信托事务的清算报告。受益人或者信托财产的权利归属人对清算报告无异议的，受托人就清算报告所列事项解除责任。但受托人有不正当行为的除外。

**Article 58** Upon termination of a trust, the trustee shall make a liquidation report on the handling of trust affairs. If the beneficiary or the person entitled to the trust property raises no objection to the liquidation report, the trustee shall be released from liability with respect to the matters stated in the liquidation report, unless the trustee has engaged in improper conduct.

### 第六章　公益信托

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　公益信托适用本章规定。本章未规定的，适用本法及其他相关法律的规定。

**Article 59** Charitable trusts shall be governed by the provisions of this Chapter. Matters not provided for in this Chapter shall be governed by the provisions of this Law and other relevant laws.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　为了下列公共利益目的之一而设立的信托，属于公益信托：  
（一）救济贫困；  
（二）救助灾民；  
（三）扶助残疾人；  
（四）发展教育、科技、文化、艺术、体育事业：  
（五）发展医疗卫生事业；  
（六）发展环境保护事业，维护生态环境；  
（七）发展其他社会公益事业。

**Article 60** A trust established for any of the following public interest purposes shall be a charitable trust:  
(1) relief of poverty;  
(2) relief of disaster victims;  
(3) assistance to the disabled;  
(4) development of education, science and technology, culture, the arts, and sports;  
(5) development of medical and health care;  
(6) development of environmental protection and preservation of the ecological environment;  
(7) development of other social public welfare undertakings.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家鼓励发展公益信托。

**Article 61** The State encourages the development of charitable trusts.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　公益信托的设立和确定其受托人，应当经有关公益事业的管理机构（以下简称公益事业管理机构）批准。  
未经公益事业管理机构的批准，不得以公益信托的名义进行活动。  
公益事业管理机构对于公益信托活动应当给予支持。

**Article 62** The creation of a charitable trust and the appointment of its trustee shall be subject to the approval of the administrative authority for the relevant public welfare undertaking (hereinafter referred to as the public welfare undertaking administrative authority).  
No activities shall be conducted in the name of a charitable trust without the approval of the public welfare undertaking administrative authority.  
The public welfare undertaking administrative authority shall provide support for charitable trust activities.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　公益信托的信托财产及其收益，不得用于非公益目的。

**Article 63** The trust property and its income of a charitable trust shall not be used for non-charitable purposes.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　公益信托应当设置信托监察人。  
信托监察人由信托文件规定。信托文件未规定的，由公益事业管理机构指定。

**Article 64** A charitable trust shall have a trust supervisor.  
The trust supervisor shall be designated in the trust instrument. If the trust instrument does not so designate, the public welfare undertaking administrative authority shall appoint one.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　信托监察人有权以自己的名义，为维护受益人的利益，提起诉讼或者实施其他法律行为。

**Article 65** The trust supervisor shall have the right to institute legal proceedings or perform other legal acts in its own name to protect the interests of the beneficiaries.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　公益信托的受托人未经公益事业管理机构批准，不得辞任。

**Article 66** The trustee of a charitable trust shall not resign without the approval of the public welfare undertaking administrative authority.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　公益事业管理机构应当检查受托人处理公益信托事务的情况及财产状况。  
受托人应当至少每年一次作出信托事务处理情况及财产状况报告，经信托监察人认可后，报公益事业管理机构核准，并由受托人予以公告。

**Article 67** The public welfare undertaking administrative authority shall inspect the trustee's handling of charitable trust affairs and the status of the property.  
The trustee shall, at least once a year, make a report on the handling of trust affairs and the status of the property, which, after being confirmed by the trust supervisor, shall be submitted to the public welfare undertaking administrative authority for approval and shall be published by the trustee.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　公益信托的受托人违反信托义务或者无能力履行其职责的，由公益事业管理机构变更受托人。

**Article 68** Where the trustee of a charitable trust violates the trust obligations or is unable to perform its duties, the public welfare undertaking administrative authority shall replace the trustee.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　公益信托成立后，发生设立信托时不能预见的情形，公益事业管理机构可以根据信托目的，变更信托文件中的有关条款。

**Article 69** After a charitable trust is established, if circumstances occur that could not be foreseen at the time of its creation, the public welfare undertaking administrative authority may, in accordance with the trust purpose, modify the relevant terms of the trust instrument.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　公益信托终止的，受托人应当于终止事由发生之日起十五日内，将终止事由和终止日期报告公益事业管理机构。

**Article 70** Upon termination of a charitable trust, the trustee shall, within fifteen days from the date on which the cause for termination occurs, report the cause and date of termination to the public welfare undertaking administrative authority.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　公益信托终止的，受托人作出的处理信托事务的清算报告，应当经信托监察人认可后，报公益事业管理机构核准，并由受托人予以公告。

**Article 71** Upon termination of a charitable trust, the liquidation report on the handling of trust affairs made by the trustee shall, after being confirmed by the trust supervisor, be submitted to the public welfare undertaking administrative authority for approval and shall be published by the trustee.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　公益信托终止，没有信托财产权利归属人或者信托财产权利归属人是不特定的社会公众的，经公益事业管理机构批准，受托人应当将信托财产用于与原公益目的相近似的目的，或者将信托财产转移给具有近似目的的公益组织或者其他公益信托。

**Article 72** Upon termination of a charitable trust, if there is no person entitled to the trust property, or if the person entitled to the trust property is an unspecified public, the trustee shall, with the approval of the public welfare undertaking administrative authority, apply the trust property to purposes similar to the original charitable purpose, or transfer the trust property to a charitable organization or another charitable trust with a similar purpose.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　公益事业管理机构违反本法规定的，委托人、受托人或者受益人有权向人民法院起诉。

**Article 73** Where the public welfare undertaking administrative authority violates the provisions of this Law, the settlor, trustee or beneficiary shall have the right to institute legal proceedings in the People's Court.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　本法自2001年10月1日起施行。

**Article 74** This Law shall come into force as of October 1, 2001.
