# 最高人民法院关于确定民事侵权精神损害赔偿责任若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2001〕7号  
**公布日期**：2001-03-08  
**生效日期**：2001-03-10

> （2001年2月26日最高人民法院审判委员会第1161次会议通过　2001年3月8日最高人民法院公告公布　自2001年3月10日起施行） 为在审理民事侵权案件中正确确定精神损害赔偿责任，根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》等有关法律规定，结合审判实践经验，对有关问题作如下解释： 第一条　自然人因下列人格权利遭受非法侵害，向人民法院起诉请求赔偿精神损害的，人民法院应当依法予以受理： （一）生命权、健康权、身体权； （二）姓名权、肖像权、名誉权、荣誉权； （三）人格尊严权、人身自由权。 违反社会公共利益、社会公德侵害他人隐私或者其他人格利益，受害人以侵权为由向人民法院起诉请求赔偿精神损害的，人民法院应当依法予以受理。 第二条　非法使被监护人脱离监护，导致亲子关系或者近亲属间的亲属关系遭受严重损害，监护人向人民法院起诉请求赔偿精神损害的，人民法院应当依法予以受理。 第三条　自然人死亡后，其近亲属因下列侵权行为遭受精神痛苦，向人民法院起诉请求赔偿精神损害的，人民法院应当依法予以受理： （一）以侮辱、诽谤、贬损、丑化或者违反社会公共利益、社会公德的其他方式，侵害死者姓名、肖像、名誉、荣誉； （二）非法披露、利用死者隐私，或者以违反社会公共利益、社会公德的其他方式侵害死者隐私； （三）非法利用、损害遗体、遗骨，或者以违反社会公共利益、社会公德的其他方式侵害遗体、遗骨。 第四条　具有人格象征意义的特定纪念物品，因侵权行为而永久性灭失或者毁损，物品所有人以侵权为由，向人民法院起诉请求赔偿精神损害的，人民法院应当依法予以受理。 第五条　法人或者其他组织以人格权利遭受侵害为由，向人民法院起诉请求赔偿精神损害的，人民法院不予受理。 第六条　当事人在侵权诉讼中没有提出赔偿精神损害的诉讼请求，诉讼终结后又基于同一侵权事实另行起诉请求赔偿精神损害的，人民法院不予受理。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　自然人因下列人格权利遭受非法侵害，向人民法院起诉请求赔偿精神损害的，人民法院应当依法予以受理：  
（一）生命权、健康权、身体权；  
（二）姓名权、肖像权、名誉权、荣誉权；  
（三）人格尊严权、人身自由权。  
违反社会公共利益、社会公德侵害他人隐私或者其他人格利益，受害人以侵权为由向人民法院起诉请求赔偿精神损害的，人民法院应当依法予以受理。

**Article 1** Where a natural person, due to illegal infringement of the following personality rights, prosecutes to the People's Court claiming compensation for mental damages, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law:  
(1) the right to life, the right to health, and the right to bodily integrity;  
(2) the right to name, the right to portrait, the right to reputation, and the right to honor;  
(3) the right to human dignity and the right to personal freedom.  
Where a person violates public interests or social ethics to infringe upon another's privacy or other personality interests, and the victim, on the ground of tort, prosecutes to the People's Court claiming compensation for mental damages, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　非法使被监护人脱离监护，导致亲子关系或者近亲属间的亲属关系遭受严重损害，监护人向人民法院起诉请求赔偿精神损害的，人民法院应当依法予以受理。

**Article 2** Where a person illegally removes a ward from guardianship, causing serious damage to the parent-child relationship or the kinship between close relatives, and the guardian prosecutes to the People's Court claiming compensation for mental damages, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　自然人死亡后，其近亲属因下列侵权行为遭受精神痛苦，向人民法院起诉请求赔偿精神损害的，人民法院应当依法予以受理：  
（一）以侮辱、诽谤、贬损、丑化或者违反社会公共利益、社会公德的其他方式，侵害死者姓名、肖像、名誉、荣誉；  
（二）非法披露、利用死者隐私，或者以违反社会公共利益、社会公德的其他方式侵害死者隐私；  
（三）非法利用、损害遗体、遗骨，或者以违反社会公共利益、社会公德的其他方式侵害遗体、遗骨。

**Article 3** After the death of a natural person, where his close relatives suffer mental distress due to the following tortious acts and prosecute to the People's Court claiming compensation for mental damages, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law:  
(1) infringing upon the deceased's name, portrait, reputation, or honor by means of insult, defamation, disparagement, vilification, or other methods violating public interests or social ethics;  
(2) illegally disclosing or exploiting the deceased's privacy, or infringing upon the deceased's privacy by other methods violating public interests or social ethics;  
(3) illegally using or damaging the deceased's remains or ashes, or infringing upon the deceased's remains or ashes by other methods violating public interests or social ethics.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　具有人格象征意义的特定纪念物品，因侵权行为而永久性灭失或者毁损，物品所有人以侵权为由，向人民法院起诉请求赔偿精神损害的，人民法院应当依法予以受理。

**Article 4** Where a specific commemorative article with symbolic personal significance is permanently lost or damaged due to a tortious act, and the owner of the article, on the ground of tort, prosecutes to the People's Court claiming compensation for mental damages, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　法人或者其他组织以人格权利遭受侵害为由，向人民法院起诉请求赔偿精神损害的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 5** Where a legal person or any other organization, on the ground that its personality rights have been infringed, prosecutes to the People's Court claiming compensation for mental damages, the People's Court shall not accept the case.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　当事人在侵权诉讼中没有提出赔偿精神损害的诉讼请求，诉讼终结后又基于同一侵权事实另行起诉请求赔偿精神损害的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 6** Where a party has not raised a claim for compensation for mental damages in the tort action, and after the conclusion of the action, files another prosecution based on the same tortious facts claiming compensation for mental damages, the People's Court shall not accept the case.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　自然人因侵权行为致死，或者自然人死亡后其人格或者遗体遭受侵害，死者的配偶、父母和子女向人民法院起诉请求赔偿精神损害的，列其配偶、父母和子女为原告；没有配偶、父母和子女的，可以由其他近亲属提起诉讼，列其他近亲属为原告。

**Article 7** Where a natural person dies due to a tortious act, or after death, his personality or remains are infringed, and the deceased's spouse, parents, and children prosecute to the People's Court claiming compensation for mental damages, the spouse, parents, and children shall be listed as plaintiffs; if there is no spouse, parents, or children, other close relatives may bring the prosecution, and such other close relatives shall be listed as plaintiffs.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　因侵权致人精神损害，但未造成严重后果，受害人请求赔偿精神损害的，一般不予支持，人民法院可以根据情形判令侵权人停止侵害、恢复名誉、消除影响、赔礼道歉。  
因侵权致人精神损害，造成严重后果的，人民法院除判令侵权人承担停止侵害、恢复名誉、消除影响、赔礼道歉等民事责任外，可以根据受害人一方的请求判令其赔偿相应的精神损害抚慰金。

**Article 8** Where a tort causes mental damage to a person but does not result in serious consequences, and the victim claims compensation for mental damages, generally it shall not be supported; the People's Court may, according to the circumstances, order the tortfeasor to cease the infringement, restore the victim's reputation, eliminate the effects, and make an apology.  
Where a tort causes mental damage to a person and results in serious consequences, the People's Court, in addition to ordering the tortfeasor to assume civil liabilities such as ceasing the infringement, restoring the victim's reputation, eliminating the effects, and making an apology, may, upon the request of the victim, order the tortfeasor to pay corresponding mental damages solatium.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　精神损害抚慰金包括以下方式：  
（一）致人残疾的，为残疾赔偿金；  
（二）致人死亡的，为死亡赔偿金；  
（三）其他损害情形的精神抚慰金。

**Article 9** Mental damages solatium includes the following forms:  
(1) where the person is disabled, it shall be disability compensation;  
(2) where the person dies, it shall be death compensation;  
(3) mental solatium for other damage situations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　精神损害的赔偿数额根据以下因素确定：  
（一）侵权人的过错程度，法律另有规定的除外；  
（二）侵害的手段、场合、行为方式等具体情节；  
（三）侵权行为所造成的后果；  
（四）侵权人的获利情况；  
（五）侵权人承担责任的经济能力；  
（六）受诉法院所在地平均生活水平。  
法律、行政法规对残疾赔偿金、死亡赔偿金等有明确规定的，适用法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 10** The amount of compensation for mental damages shall be determined based on the following factors:  
(1) the degree of fault of the tortfeasor, except where the law provides otherwise;  
(2) the specific circumstances of the infringement, such as means, occasion, and manner of conduct;  
(3) the consequences caused by the tortious act;  
(4) the profits gained by the tortfeasor;  
(5) the economic capacity of the tortfeasor to assume liability;  
(6) the average living standard of the place where the accepting court is located.  
Where laws or administrative regulations have explicit provisions on disability compensation and death compensation, the provisions of laws and administrative regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　受害人对损害事实和损害后果的发生有过错的，可以根据其过错程度减轻或者免除侵权人的精神损害赔偿责任。

**Article 11** Where the victim is at fault for the occurrence of the damage fact and the damage consequences, the tortfeasor's liability for compensation for mental damages may be reduced or exempted according to the degree of fault.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在本解释公布施行之前已经生效施行的司法解释，其内容有与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 12** Where the content of any judicial interpretation that has already come into force and taken effect prior to the promulgation and coming into force of this Interpretation is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
