# 最高人民法院关于人民法院办理财产保全案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2016〕22号  
**公布日期**：2016-11-07  
**生效日期**：2016-12-01

> 最高人民法院《关于人民法院办理财产保全案件若干问题的规定》已于2016年10月17日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1696次会议通过，现予公布，自2016年12月1日起施行。 2016年11月7日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院办理财产保全案件 若干问题的规定 法释〔2016〕22号 为依法保护当事人、利害关系人的合法权益，规范人民法院办理财产保全案件，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律规定，结合审判、执行实践，制定本规定。 第一条　当事人、利害关系人申请财产保全，应当向人民法院提交申请书，并提供相关证据材料。 申请书应当载明下列事项： （一）申请保全人与被保全人的身份、送达地址、联系方式； （二）请求事项和所根据的事实与理由； （三）请求保全数额或者争议标的； （四）明确的被保全财产信息或者具体的被保全财产线索； （五）为财产保全提供担保的财产信息或资信证明，或者不需要提供担保的理由； （六）其他需要载明的事项。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　当事人、利害关系人申请财产保全，应当向人民法院提交申请书，并提供相关证据材料。  
申请书应当载明下列事项：  
（一）申请保全人与被保全人的身份、送达地址、联系方式；  
（二）请求事项和所根据的事实与理由；  
（三）请求保全数额或者争议标的；  
（四）明确的被保全财产信息或者具体的被保全财产线索；  
（五）为财产保全提供担保的财产信息或资信证明，或者不需要提供担保的理由；  
（六）其他需要载明的事项。  
法律文书生效后，进入执行程序前，债权人申请财产保全的，应当写明生效法律文书的制作机关、文号和主要内容，并附生效法律文书副本。

**Article 1** A party or an interested person applying for property preservation shall submit a written application to the People's Court and provide relevant evidence materials.  
The written application shall specify the following:  
(1) The identities, service addresses, and contact information of the applicant for preservation and the person against whom preservation is sought;  
(2) The matters requested and the facts and reasons on which they are based;  
(3) The amount sought to be preserved or the subject matter in dispute;  
(4) Clear information on the property to be preserved or specific leads to the property to be preserved;  
(5) Property information or credit certification for providing security for property preservation, or the reasons for not providing security;  
(6) Other matters that need to be specified.  
After a legal instrument takes effect but before the enforcement procedure is initiated, if a creditor applies for property preservation, the creditor shall specify the issuing authority, document number, and main content of the effective legal instrument, and attach a copy of the effective legal instrument.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院进行财产保全，由立案、审判机构作出裁定，一般应当移送执行机构实施。

**Article 2** When a People's Court conducts property preservation, the case-filing or adjudication division shall render a ruling, and generally, the ruling shall be transferred to the enforcement division for execution.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　仲裁过程中，当事人申请财产保全的，应当通过仲裁机构向人民法院提交申请书及仲裁案件受理通知书等相关材料。人民法院裁定采取保全措施或者裁定驳回申请的，应当将裁定书送达当事人，并通知仲裁机构。

**Article 3** In the course of arbitration, if a party applies for property preservation, the party shall submit a written application and relevant materials such as the notice of acceptance of the arbitration case to the People's Court through the arbitration institution. If the People's Court rules to adopt preservation measures or to reject the application, it shall serve the ruling on the parties and notify the arbitration institution.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院接受财产保全申请后，应当在五日内作出裁定；需要提供担保的，应当在提供担保后五日内作出裁定；裁定采取保全措施的，应当在五日内开始执行。对情况紧急的，必须在四十八小时内作出裁定；裁定采取保全措施的，应当立即开始执行。

**Article 4** After accepting an application for property preservation, the People's Court shall render a ruling within five days; if security needs to be provided, it shall render a ruling within five days after the security is provided; if it rules to adopt preservation measures, it shall begin enforcement within five days. In urgent circumstances, it must render a ruling within forty-eight hours; if it rules to adopt preservation measures, it shall immediately begin enforcement.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院依照[民事诉讼法第一百条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-100)规定责令申请保全人提供财产保全担保的，担保数额不超过请求保全数额的百分之三十；申请保全的财产系争议标的的，担保数额不超过争议标的价值的百分之三十。  
利害关系人申请诉前财产保全的，应当提供相当于请求保全数额的担保；情况特殊的，人民法院可以酌情处理。  
财产保全期间，申请保全人提供的担保不足以赔偿可能给被保全人造成的损失的，人民法院可以责令其追加相应的担保；拒不追加的，可以裁定解除或者部分解除保全。

**Article 5** If the People's Court, in accordance with Article 100 of the Civil Procedure Law, orders the applicant for preservation to provide security for property preservation, the amount of security shall not exceed thirty percent of the amount sought to be preserved; if the property subject to the preservation application is the subject matter in dispute, the amount of security shall not exceed thirty percent of the value of the subject matter in dispute.  
If an interested person applies for pre-litigation property preservation, the person shall provide security equivalent to the amount sought to be preserved; under special circumstances, the People's Court may handle the matter at its discretion.  
During the period of property preservation, if the security provided by the applicant for preservation is insufficient to compensate for the losses that may be caused to the person against whom preservation is sought, the People's Court may order the applicant to provide additional security accordingly; if the applicant refuses to do so, it may rule to dissolve or partially dissolve the preservation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　申请保全人或第三人为财产保全提供财产担保的，应当向人民法院出具担保书。担保书应当载明担保人、担保方式、担保范围、担保财产及其价值、担保责任承担等内容，并附相关证据材料。  
第三人为财产保全提供保证担保的，应当向人民法院提交保证书。保证书应当载明保证人、保证方式、保证范围、保证责任承担等内容，并附相关证据材料。  
对财产保全担保，人民法院经审查，认为违反物权法、担保法、公司法等有关法律禁止性规定的，应当责令申请保全人在指定期限内提供其他担保；逾期未提供的，裁定驳回申请。

**Article 6** If an applicant for preservation or a third party provides property security for property preservation, the applicant or third party shall submit a guarantee letter to the People's Court. The guarantee letter shall specify the guarantor, the method of guarantee, the scope of guarantee, the property provided as security and its value, the assumption of guarantee liability, and other contents, and shall attach relevant evidence materials.  
If a third party provides a guarantee in the form of a suretyship for property preservation, the third party shall submit a letter of suretyship to the People's Court. The letter of suretyship shall specify the surety, the method of suretyship, the scope of suretyship, the assumption of suretyship liability, and other contents, and shall attach relevant evidence materials.  
With respect to the security for property preservation, if the People's Court, upon examination, finds that it violates prohibitive provisions of laws such as the Property Law, the Security Law, or the Company Law, it shall order the applicant for preservation to provide other security within a specified period; if the applicant fails to do so within the time limit, the People's Court shall rule to reject the application.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　保险人以其与申请保全人签订财产保全责任险合同的方式为财产保全提供担保的，应当向人民法院出具担保书。  
担保书应当载明，因申请财产保全错误，由保险人赔偿被保全人因保全所遭受的损失等内容，并附相关证据材料。

**Article 7** If an insurer provides security for property preservation by entering into a contract of property preservation liability insurance with the applicant for preservation, the insurer shall submit a guarantee letter to the People's Court.  
The guarantee letter shall specify that, if the application for property preservation is erroneous, the insurer shall compensate the person against whom preservation is sought for any losses suffered as a result of the preservation, and shall attach relevant evidence materials.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　金融监管部门批准设立的金融机构以独立保函形式为财产保全提供担保的，人民法院应当依法准许。

**Article 8** If a financial institution established with the approval of a financial regulatory authority provides security for property preservation in the form of an independent guarantee, the People's Court shall permit it in accordance with law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　当事人在诉讼中申请财产保全，有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以不要求提供担保：  
（一）追索赡养费、扶养费、抚育费、抚恤金、医疗费用、劳动报酬、工伤赔偿、交通事故人身损害赔偿的；  
（二）婚姻家庭纠纷案件中遭遇家庭暴力且经济困难的；  
（三）人民检察院提起的公益诉讼涉及损害赔偿的；  
（四）因见义勇为遭受侵害请求损害赔偿的；  
（五）案件事实清楚、权利义务关系明确，发生保全错误可能性较小的；  
（六）申请保全人为商业银行、保险公司等由金融监管部门批准设立的具有独立偿付债务能力的金融机构及其分支机构的。  
法律文书生效后，进入执行程序前，债权人申请财产保全的，人民法院可以不要求提供担保。

**Article 9** If a party applies for property preservation during litigation, the People's Court may not require the party to provide security in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The claim involves recourse for alimony, support payments, child-rearing expenses, pensions, medical expenses, labor remuneration, work-related injury compensation, or compensation for personal injury in a traffic accident;  
(2) In a marriage or family dispute case, if the party is subjected to domestic violence and is in financial difficulty;  
(3) A public interest litigation initiated by a People's Procuratorate involves damages;  
(4) A claim for damages arising from injury suffered in performing a courageous act;  
(5) The facts of the case are clear, the rights and obligations are definite, and the likelihood of an erroneous preservation is low;  
(6) The applicant for preservation is a commercial bank, insurance company, or other financial institution established with the approval of a financial regulatory authority with independent debt-paying capacity, or a branch thereof.  
After a legal instrument takes effect but before the enforcement procedure is initiated, if a creditor applies for property preservation, the People's Court may not require the creditor to provide security.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　当事人、利害关系人申请财产保全，应当向人民法院提供明确的被保全财产信息。  
当事人在诉讼中申请财产保全，确因客观原因不能提供明确的被保全财产信息，但提供了具体财产线索的，人民法院可以依法裁定采取财产保全措施。

**Article 10** A party or an interested person applying for property preservation shall provide clear information on the property to be preserved to the People's Court.  
If a party applies for property preservation during litigation and is genuinely unable to provide clear information on the property to be preserved due to objective reasons, but has provided specific leads to the property, the People's Court may rule to adopt property preservation measures in accordance with law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院依照[本规定第十条](#art-10)第二款规定作出保全裁定的，在该裁定执行过程中，申请保全人可以向已经建立网络执行查控系统的执行法院，书面申请通过该系统查询被保全人的财产。  
申请保全人提出查询申请的，执行法院可以利用网络执行查控系统，对裁定保全的财产或者保全数额范围内的财产进行查询，并采取相应的查封、扣押、冻结措施。  
人民法院利用网络执行查控系统未查询到可供保全财产的，应当书面告知申请保全人。

**Article 11** If the People's Court makes a preservation ruling in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 10 of these Provisions, during the enforcement of that ruling, the applicant for preservation may submit a written application to the enforcing court that has established a network execution inquiry and control system, requesting that the court use such system to inquire about the property of the person against whom preservation is sought.  
If the applicant for preservation submits an inquiry application, the enforcing court may use the network execution inquiry and control system to inquire about the property that is the subject of the preservation ruling or property within the amount of preservation, and shall take corresponding measures of seizure, impoundment, and freezing.  
If the People's Court, using the network execution inquiry and control system, fails to locate any property that may be preserved, it shall notify the applicant for preservation in writing.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院对查询到的被保全人财产信息，应当依法保密。除依法保全的财产外，不得泄露被保全人其他财产信息，也不得在财产保全、强制执行以外使用相关信息。

**Article 12** The People's Court shall keep confidential the information about the property of the person against whom preservation is sought that it has discovered through inquiry. Except for the property preserved in accordance with law, it shall not disclose any other property information of the person against whom preservation is sought, nor shall it use such information for purposes other than property preservation or compulsory enforcement.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　被保全人有多项财产可供保全的，在能够实现保全目的的情况下，人民法院应当选择对其生产经营活动影响较小的财产进行保全。  
人民法院对厂房、机器设备等生产经营性财产进行保全时，指定被保全人保管的，应当允许其继续使用。

**Article 13** If the person against whom preservation is sought has multiple items of property that may be preserved, the People's Court shall, to the extent that the purpose of preservation can be achieved, select property that has a relatively minor impact on the business activities of the person for preservation.  
When the People's Court preserves production and operation property such as factory buildings and machinery and equipment, and designates the person against whom preservation is sought to retain custody of such property, it shall permit the person to continue to use the property.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　被保全财产系机动车、航空器等特殊动产的，除被保全人下落不明的以外，人民法院应当责令被保全人书面报告该动产的权属和占有、使用等情况，并予以核实。

**Article 14** If the preserved property is a motor vehicle, aircraft, or other special movable property, the People's Court shall, unless the person against whom preservation is sought cannot be located, order the person to provide a written report on the ownership, possession, and use of such movable property, and shall verify the report.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民法院应当依据财产保全裁定采取相应的查封、扣押、冻结措施。  
可供保全的土地、房屋等不动产的整体价值明显高于保全裁定载明金额的，人民法院应当对该不动产的相应价值部分采取查封、扣押、冻结措施，但该不动产在使用上不可分或者分割会严重减损其价值的除外。  
对银行账户内资金采取冻结措施的，人民法院应当明确具体的冻结数额。

**Article 15** The People's Court shall take corresponding measures of seizure, impoundment, and freezing pursuant to the property preservation ruling.  
If the overall value of immovable property such as land or houses that may be preserved is clearly higher than the amount specified in the preservation ruling, the People's Court shall take measures of seizure, impoundment, and freezing with respect to the corresponding portion of the value of such immovable property, unless such immovable property is indivisible in use or its division would significantly reduce its value.  
When taking freezing measures with respect to funds in a bank account, the People's Court shall specify the exact amount to be frozen.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院在财产保全中采取查封、扣押、冻结措施，需要有关单位协助办理登记手续的，有关单位应当在裁定书和协助执行通知书送达后立即办理。针对同一财产有多个裁定书和协助执行通知书的，应当按照送达的时间先后办理登记手续。

**Article 16** If, in the course of property preservation, the People's Court takes measures of seizure, impoundment, or freezing and needs a relevant unit to assist in handling registration procedures, the relevant unit shall do so immediately upon service of the ruling and the notice of assistance in enforcement. If there are multiple rulings and notices of assistance in enforcement with respect to the same property, the registration procedures shall be handled in the order in which they are served.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　利害关系人申请诉前财产保全，在人民法院采取保全措施后三十日内依法提起诉讼或者申请仲裁的，诉前财产保全措施自动转为诉讼或仲裁中的保全措施；进入执行程序后，保全措施自动转为执行中的查封、扣押、冻结措施。  
依前款规定，自动转为诉讼、仲裁中的保全措施或者执行中的查封、扣押、冻结措施的，期限连续计算，人民法院无需重新制作裁定书。

**Article 17** If an interested person applies for pre-litigation property preservation and, within thirty days after the People's Court takes preservation measures, initiates a lawsuit or applies for arbitration in accordance with law, the pre-litigation property preservation measures shall automatically convert into preservation measures in litigation or arbitration; after the enforcement procedure is initiated, the preservation measures shall automatically convert into measures of seizure, impoundment, or freezing in enforcement.  
If, in accordance with the preceding paragraph, preservation measures automatically convert into preservation measures in litigation or arbitration or into measures of seizure, impoundment, or freezing in enforcement, the periods shall be calculated consecutively, and the People's Court need not make a new ruling.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　申请保全人申请续行财产保全的，应当在保全期限届满七日前向人民法院提出；逾期申请或者不申请的，自行承担不能续行保全的法律后果。  
人民法院进行财产保全时，应当书面告知申请保全人明确的保全期限届满日以及前款有关申请续行保全的事项。

**Article 18** If an applicant for preservation applies for the continuation of property preservation, the applicant shall submit the application to the People's Court at least seven days before the expiration of the preservation period; if the applicant fails to apply in a timely manner or does not apply at all, the applicant shall bear the legal consequences of the failure to continue the preservation.  
When conducting property preservation, the People's Court shall inform the applicant for preservation in writing of the specific date of expiration of the preservation period and the matters concerning the application for continuation of preservation as provided in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　再审审查期间，债务人申请保全生效法律文书确定给付的财产的，人民法院不予受理。  
再审审理期间，原生效法律文书中止执行，当事人申请财产保全的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 19** During the review of a retrial application, if the debtor applies for preservation of property concerning the payment determined by an effective legal instrument, the People's Court shall not accept the application.  
During the retrial hearing, if the enforcement of the original effective legal instrument is suspended and a party applies for property preservation, the People's Court shall accept the application.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　财产保全期间，被保全人请求对被保全财产自行处分，人民法院经审查，认为不损害申请保全人和其他执行债权人合法权益的，可以准许，但应当监督被保全人按照合理价格在指定期限内处分，并控制相应价款。  
被保全人请求对作为争议标的的被保全财产自行处分的，须经申请保全人同意。  
人民法院准许被保全人自行处分被保全财产的，应当通知申请保全人；申请保全人不同意的，可以依照[民事诉讼法第二百二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-225)规定提出异议。

**Article 20** During the period of property preservation, if the person against whom preservation is sought requests to dispose of the preserved property on their own, the People's Court may, upon examination and if it finds that doing so would not harm the lawful rights and interests of the applicant for preservation or other enforcement creditors, permit the disposal, provided that it supervises the person to dispose of the property at a reasonable price within a specified period and controls the corresponding proceeds.  
If the person against whom preservation is sought requests to dispose of the preserved property that is the subject matter in dispute on their own, the consent of the applicant for preservation is required.  
If the People's Court permits the person against whom preservation is sought to dispose of the preserved property on their own, it shall notify the applicant for preservation; if the applicant for preservation disagrees, the applicant may raise an objection in accordance with Article 225 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　保全法院在首先采取查封、扣押、冻结措施后超过一年未对被保全财产进行处分的，除被保全财产系争议标的外，在先轮候查封、扣押、冻结的执行法院可以商请保全法院将被保全财产移送执行。但司法解释另有特别规定的，适用其规定。  
保全法院与在先轮候查封、扣押、冻结的执行法院就移送被保全财产发生争议的，可以逐级报请共同的上级法院指定该财产的执行法院。  
共同的上级法院应当根据被保全财产的种类及所在地、各债权数额与被保全财产价值之间的关系等案件具体情况指定执行法院，并督促其在指定期限内处分被保全财产。

**Article 21** If the preserving court, after being the first to take measures of seizure, impoundment, or freezing, fails to dispose of the preserved property for more than one year, unless the preserved property is the subject matter in dispute, the enforcing court that has the prior sequential seizure, impoundment, or freezing may consult with the preserving court to transfer the preserved property for enforcement. However, if a judicial interpretation provides otherwise, that provision shall apply.  
If a dispute arises between the preserving court and the prior sequential enforcing court regarding the transfer of the preserved property, they may refer the matter level by level to their common higher People's Court for designation of the enforcing court for such property.  
The common higher People's Court shall designate the enforcing court based on the type and location of the preserved property, the relationship between the amounts of each claim and the value of the preserved property, and other specific circumstances of the case, and shall supervise the designated court to dispose of the preserved property within a specified period.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　财产纠纷案件，被保全人或第三人提供充分有效担保请求解除保全，人民法院应当裁定准许。被保全人请求对作为争议标的的财产解除保全的，须经申请保全人同意。

**Article 22** In a property dispute case, if the person against whom preservation is sought or a third party provides sufficient and effective security and requests the dissolution of preservation, the People's Court shall rule to permit the dissolution. If the person against whom preservation is sought requests the dissolution of preservation with respect to property that is the subject matter in dispute, the consent of the applicant for preservation is required.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　人民法院采取财产保全措施后，有下列情形之一的，申请保全人应当及时申请解除保全：  
（一）采取诉前财产保全措施后三十日内不依法提起诉讼或者申请仲裁的；  
（二）仲裁机构不予受理仲裁申请、准许撤回仲裁申请或者按撤回仲裁申请处理的；  
（三）仲裁申请或者请求被仲裁裁决驳回的；  
（四）其他人民法院对起诉不予受理、准许撤诉或者按撤诉处理的；  
（五）起诉或者诉讼请求被其他人民法院生效裁判驳回的；  
（六）申请保全人应当申请解除保全的其他情形。  
人民法院收到解除保全申请后，应当在五日内裁定解除保全；对情况紧急的，必须在四十八小时内裁定解除保全。  
申请保全人未及时申请人民法院解除保全，应当赔偿被保全人因财产保全所遭受的损失。  
被保全人申请解除保全，人民法院经审查认为符合法律规定的，应当在本条第二款规定的期间内裁定解除保全。

**Article 23** After the People's Court takes property preservation measures, in any of the following circumstances, the applicant for preservation shall promptly apply for the dissolution of preservation:  
(1) Within thirty days after taking pre-litigation property preservation measures, the applicant fails to initiate a lawsuit or apply for arbitration in accordance with law;  
(2) The arbitration institution does not accept the arbitration application, permits withdrawal of the arbitration application, or treats the application as withdrawn;  
(3) The arbitration application or claim is rejected by the arbitration award;  
(4) Another People's Court does not accept the lawsuit, permits withdrawal of the lawsuit, or treats the lawsuit as withdrawn;  
(5) The lawsuit or litigation claim is rejected by a final judgment of another People's Court;  
(6) Other circumstances in which the applicant for preservation shall apply for dissolution of preservation.  
After receiving an application for dissolution of preservation, the People's Court shall rule to dissolve the preservation within five days; in urgent circumstances, it must rule to dissolve the preservation within forty-eight hours.  
If the applicant for preservation fails to promptly apply to the People's Court for dissolution of preservation, the applicant shall compensate the person against whom preservation is sought for any losses suffered as a result of the property preservation.  
If the person against whom preservation is sought applies for dissolution of preservation, and the People's Court, upon examination, finds that the application complies with legal provisions, it shall rule to dissolve the preservation within the period specified in the second paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　财产保全裁定执行中，人民法院发现保全裁定的内容与被保全财产的实际情况不符的，应当予以撤销、变更或补正。

**Article 24** During the enforcement of a property preservation ruling, if the People's Court discovers that the content of the preservation ruling does not correspond with the actual circumstances of the preserved property, it shall revoke, modify, or correct the ruling.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　申请保全人、被保全人对保全裁定或者驳回申请裁定不服的，可以自裁定书送达之日起五日内向作出裁定的人民法院申请复议一次。人民法院应当自收到复议申请后十日内审查。  
对保全裁定不服申请复议的，人民法院经审查，理由成立的，裁定撤销或变更；理由不成立的，裁定驳回。  
对驳回申请裁定不服申请复议的，人民法院经审查，理由成立的，裁定撤销，并采取保全措施；理由不成立的，裁定驳回。

**Article 25** If an applicant for preservation or a person against whom preservation is sought is dissatisfied with a preservation ruling or a ruling rejecting an application, the party may apply for reconsideration once within five days from the date of service of the ruling to the People's Court that made the ruling. The People's Court shall review the application within ten days after receiving the application for reconsideration.  
If a party applies for reconsideration of a preservation ruling, and upon review by the People's Court, the grounds are found to be valid, the People's Court shall rule to revoke or modify the ruling; if the grounds are found to be invalid, it shall rule to reject the application.  
If a party applies for reconsideration of a ruling rejecting an application, and upon review by the People's Court, the grounds are found to be valid, the People's Court shall rule to revoke the ruling and take preservation measures; if the grounds are found to be invalid, it shall rule to reject the application.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　申请保全人、被保全人、利害关系人认为保全裁定实施过程中的执行行为违反法律规定提出书面异议的，人民法院应当依照[民事诉讼法第二百二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-225)规定审查处理。

**Article 26** If an applicant for preservation, a person against whom preservation is sought, or an interested person believes that an enforcement act in the process of implementing a preservation ruling violates legal provisions and raises a written objection, the People's Court shall review and handle the matter in accordance with Article 225 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　人民法院对诉讼争议标的以外的财产进行保全，案外人对保全裁定或者保全裁定实施过程中的执行行为不服，基于实体权利对被保全财产提出书面异议的，人民法院应当依照[民事诉讼法第二百二十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-227)规定审查处理并作出裁定。案外人、申请保全人对该裁定不服的，可以自裁定送达之日起十五日内向人民法院提起执行异议之诉。  
人民法院裁定案外人异议成立后，申请保全人在法律规定的期间内未提起执行异议之诉的，人民法院应当自起诉期限届满之日起七日内对该被保全财产解除保全。

**Article 27** If the People's Court preserves property other than the subject matter in dispute, and an outsider is dissatisfied with the preservation ruling or an enforcement act in the process of implementing the preservation ruling, and raises a written objection to the preserved property based on substantive rights, the People's Court shall review and handle the matter in accordance with Article 227 of the Civil Procedure Law and make a ruling. If the outsider or the applicant for preservation is dissatisfied with that ruling, the party may initiate an enforcement objection lawsuit with the People's Court within fifteen days from the date of service of the ruling.  
After the People's Court rules that the outsider's objection is established, if the applicant for preservation does not initiate an enforcement objection lawsuit within the time limit prescribed by law, the People's Court shall dissolve the preservation of the preserved property within seven days from the date of expiration of the period for filing such lawsuit.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　海事诉讼中，海事请求人申请海事请求保全，适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》及相关司法解释。

**Article 28** In maritime litigation, if a maritime claimant applies for maritime claim preservation, the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and relevant judicial interpretations shall apply.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本规定自2016年12月1日起施行。  
本规定施行前公布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 29** These Provisions shall come into force as of December 1, 2016.  
If any judicial interpretation published before the coming into force of these Provisions is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
