# 最高人民法院关于审理涉及人民调解协议的民事案件的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2002〕29号  
**公布日期**：2002-09-05  
**生效日期**：2002-11-01

> （2002年9月5日最高人民法院审判委员会第1240次会议通过，自2002年11月1日起施行。） 法释〔2002〕29号 为了公正审理涉及人民调解协议的民事案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》、《中华人民共和国合同法》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》，参照《人民调解委员会组织条例》，结合民事审判经验和实际情况，对审理涉及人民调解协议的民事案件的有关问题作如下规定： 第一条 经人民调解委员会调解达成的、有民事权利义务内容，并由双方当事人签字或者盖章的调解协议，具有民事合同性质。当事人应当按照约定履行自己的义务，不得擅自变更或者解除调解协议。 第二条 当事人一方向人民法院起诉，请求对方当事人履行调解协议的，人民法院应当受理。 当事人一方向人民法院起诉，请求变更或者撤销调解协议，或者请求确认调解协议无效的，人民法院应当受理。 第三条 当事人一方起诉请求履行调解协议，对方当事人反驳的，有责任对反驳诉讼请求所依据的事实提供证据予以证明。 当事人一方起诉请求变更或者撤销调解协议，或者请求确认调解协议无效的，有责任对自己的诉讼请求所依据的事实提供证据予以证明。 当事人一方以原纠纷向人民法院起诉，对方当事人以调解协议抗辩的，应当提供调解协议书。 第四条 具备下列条件的，调解协议有效： （一）当事人具有完全民事行为能力； （二）意思表示真实； （三）不违反法律、行政法规的强制性规定或者社会公共利益。 第五条 有下列情形之一的，调解协议无效： （一）损害国家、集体或者第三人利益；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 经人民调解委员会调解达成的、有民事权利义务内容，并由双方当事人签字或者盖章的调解协议，具有民事合同性质。当事人应当按照约定履行自己的义务，不得擅自变更或者解除调解协议。

**Article 1** A mediation agreement reached through mediation by the People's Mediation Committee, which contains civil rights and obligations and is signed or sealed by both parties, shall have the nature of a civil contract. The parties shall perform their obligations in accordance with the agreement and shall not modify or rescind the mediation agreement without authorization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 当事人一方向人民法院起诉，请求对方当事人履行调解协议的，人民法院应当受理。  
当事人一方向人民法院起诉，请求变更或者撤销调解协议，或者请求确认调解协议无效的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 2** Where one party brings a prosecution to the People's Court requesting the other party to perform the mediation agreement, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
Where one party brings a prosecution to the People's Court requesting modification or revocation of the mediation agreement, or requesting confirmation that the mediation agreement is invalid, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 当事人一方起诉请求履行调解协议，对方当事人反驳的，有责任对反驳诉讼请求所依据的事实提供证据予以证明。  
当事人一方起诉请求变更或者撤销调解协议，或者请求确认调解协议无效的，有责任对自己的诉讼请求所依据的事实提供证据予以证明。  
当事人一方以原纠纷向人民法院起诉，对方当事人以调解协议抗辩的，应当提供调解协议书。

**Article 3** Where one party brings a prosecution requesting performance of the mediation agreement, and the other party refutes, the latter shall have the burden to provide evidence to prove the facts on which its refutation is based.  
Where one party brings a prosecution requesting modification or revocation of the mediation agreement, or requesting confirmation that the mediation agreement is invalid, such party shall have the burden to provide evidence to prove the facts on which its own claim is based.  
Where one party brings a prosecution to the People's Court regarding the original dispute, and the other party raises a defense based on the mediation agreement, the latter shall provide the mediation agreement.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 具备下列条件的，调解协议有效：  
（一）当事人具有完全民事行为能力；  
（二）意思表示真实；  
（三）不违反法律、行政法规的强制性规定或者社会公共利益。

**Article 4** A mediation agreement shall be valid if the following conditions are met:  
(1) The parties have full capacity for civil conduct;  
(2) The expression of intention is genuine;  
(3) It does not violate the mandatory provisions of laws or administrative regulations or the public interest.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 有下列情形之一的，调解协议无效：  
（一）损害国家、集体或者第三人利益；  
（二）以合法形式掩盖非法目的；  
（三）损害社会公共利益；  
（四）违反法律、行政法规的强制性规定。  
人民调解委员会强迫调解的，调解协议无效。

**Article 5** A mediation agreement shall be invalid under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) It harms the interests of the State, a collective, or a third party;  
(2) It conceals an illegal purpose by lawful means;  
(3) It harms the public interest;  
(4) It violates the mandatory provisions of laws or administrative regulations.  
Where the People's Mediation Committee compels mediation, the mediation agreement shall be invalid.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 下列调解协议，当事人一方有权请求人民法院变更或者撤销：  
（一）因重大误解订立的；  
（二）在订立调解协议时显失公平的；  
一方以欺诈、胁迫的手段或者乘人之危，使对方在违背真实意思的情况下订立的调解协议，受损害方有权请求人民法院变更或者撤销。  
当事人请求变更的，人民法院不得撤销。

**Article 6** For any of the following mediation agreements, one party shall have the right to request the People's Court to modify or revoke it:  
(1) It is concluded due to a material mistake;  
(2) It is obviously unfair at the time of conclusion;  
Where one party, by means of fraud, coercion, or taking advantage of the other party's distress, causes the other party to conclude a mediation agreement contrary to its true intention, the injured party shall have the right to request the People's Court to modify or revoke the agreement.  
If a party requests modification, the People's Court shall not revoke it.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 有下列情形之一的，撤销权消灭：  
（一）具有撤销权的当事人自知道或者应当知道撤销事由之日起一年内没有行使撤销权；  
（二）具有撤销权的当事人知道撤销事由后明确表示或者以自己的行为放弃撤销权。

**Article 7** The right of revocation shall be extinguished under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The party entitled to the right of revocation fails to exercise it within one year from the date when it knows or should have known the cause of revocation;  
(2) The party entitled to the right of revocation, after knowing the cause of revocation, expressly waives or by its own conduct abandons the right of revocation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 无效的调解协议或者被撤销的调解协议自始没有法律约束力。调解协议部分无效，不影响其他部分效力的，其他部分仍然有效。

**Article 8** An invalid mediation agreement or a mediation agreement that has been revoked shall have no legal binding effect ab initio. If the invalidity of a part of a mediation agreement does not affect the validity of the other parts, the other parts shall remain valid.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 调解协议的诉讼时效，适用民法通则第一百三十五条的规定。  
原纠纷的诉讼时效因人民调解委员会调解而中断。  
调解协议被撤销或者被认定无效后，当事人以原纠纷起诉的，诉讼时效自调解协议被撤销或者被认定无效的判决生效之日起重新计算。

**Article 9** The limitation of action for a mediation agreement shall be governed by the provisions of Article 135 of the General Principles of the Civil Law.  
The limitation of action for the original dispute shall be interrupted by the mediation of the People's Mediation Committee.  
After a mediation agreement is revoked or confirmed as invalid, if a party brings a prosecution regarding the original dispute, the limitation of action shall be recalculated from the date on which the judgment revoking or confirming the invalidity of the mediation agreement takes effect.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 具有债权内容的调解协议，公证机关依法赋予强制执行效力的，债权人可以向被执行人住所地或者被执行人的财产所在地人民法院申请执行。

**Article 10** Where a mediation agreement with a content of creditor's rights is granted compulsory enforcement effect by a notarial authority according to law, the creditor may apply to the People's Court at the domicile of the person subject to enforcement or at the location of the property of the person subject to enforcement for enforcement.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 基层人民法院及其派出的人民法庭审理涉及人民调解协议的民事案件，一般应当适用简易程序。

**Article 11** When a Primary People's Court or its dispatched people's tribunal tries a civil case involving a mediation agreement of the People's Mediation Committee, it shall generally apply the summary procedure.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 人民法院审理涉及人民调解协议的民事案件，调解协议被人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决变更、撤销，或者被确认无效的，可以适当的方式告知当地的司法行政机关或者人民调解委员会。

**Article 12** Where, in a civil case involving a mediation agreement tried by the People's Court, the mediation agreement is modified, revoked, or confirmed as invalid by a legally effective judgment of the People's Court, the People's Court may, by appropriate means, inform the local judicial administrative organ or the People's Mediation Committee.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 本规定自2002年11月1日起施行。  
人民法院审理民事案件涉及2002年11月1日以后达成的人民调解协议的，适用本规定。  
2002年9月16日

**Article 13** These Provisions shall come into force as of November 1, 2002.  
Where the People's Court tries a civil case involving a mediation agreement of the People's Mediation Committee reached after November 1, 2002, these Provisions shall apply.  
September 16, 2002
