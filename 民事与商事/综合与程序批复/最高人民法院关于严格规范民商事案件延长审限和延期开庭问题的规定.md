# 最高人民法院关于严格规范民商事案件延长审限和延期开庭问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2018〕9号  
**公布日期**：2018-04-25  
**生效日期**：2018-04-26

> 《最高人民法院关于严格规范民商事案件延长审限和延期开庭问题的规定》已于2018年4月23日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1737次会议通过，现予公布，自2018年4月26日起施行。 2018年4月25日 最高人民法院 关于严格规范民商事案件延长审限和 延期开庭问题的规定 法释〔2018〕9号 （2018年4月23日最高人民法院审判委员会第1737次会议通过，自2018年4月26日起施行）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审理民商事案件时，应当严格遵守法律及司法解释有关审限的规定。适用普通程序审理的第一审案件，审限为六个月；适用简易程序审理的第一审案件，审限为三个月。审理对判决的上诉案件，审限为三个月；审理对裁定的上诉案件，审限为三十日。  
法律规定有特殊情况需要延长审限的，独任审判员或合议庭应当在期限届满十五日前向本院院长提出申请，并说明详细情况和理由。院长应当在期限届满五日前作出决定。  
经本院院长批准延长审限后尚不能结案，需要再次延长的，应当在期限届满十五日前报请上级人民法院批准。上级人民法院应当在审限届满五日前作出决定。

**Article 1** When a People's Court tries civil or commercial cases, it shall strictly comply with the provisions of laws and judicial interpretations on trial limits. For first-instance cases tried under ordinary procedure, the trial limit shall be six months; for first-instance cases tried under summary procedure, the trial limit shall be three months. For cases on appeal against a judgment, the trial limit shall be three months; for cases on appeal against a ruling, the trial limit shall be thirty days.  
Where the law provides that the trial limit may be extended due to special circumstances, the sole adjudicator or the collegial panel shall submit an application to the president of the court within fifteen days before the expiry of the time limit, and shall explain the detailed circumstances and reasons. The president shall make a decision within five days before the expiry of the time limit.  
Where a case still cannot be concluded after the trial limit has been extended with the approval of the president of the court, and a further extension is required, the matter shall be submitted to the higher people's court for approval within fifteen days before the expiry of the time limit. The higher people's court shall make a decision within five days before the expiry of the trial limit.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院开庭审理民商事案件后，认为需要再次开庭的，应当依法告知当事人下次开庭的时间。两次开庭间隔时间不得超过一个月，但因不可抗力或当事人同意的除外。

**Article 2** After a People's Court has opened a court session to try a civil or commercial case, if it considers that another court session is necessary, it shall, according to law, inform the parties of the time for the next court session. The interval between two court sessions shall not exceed one month, unless due to force majeure or with the consent of the parties.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　独任审判员或者合议庭适用[民事诉讼法第一百四十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-146)第四项规定决定延期开庭的，应当报本院院长批准。

**Article 3** Where a sole adjudicator or a collegial panel decides to adjourn a court session by applying Article 146, Paragraph 4 of the Civil Procedure Law, it shall report to the president of the court for approval.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院应当将案件的立案时间、审理期限，扣除、延长、重新计算审限，延期开庭审理的情况及事由，按照《最高人民法院关于人民法院通过互联网公开审判流程信息的规定》及时向当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人公开。当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人有异议的，可以依法向受理案件的法院申请监督。

**Article 4** A People's Court shall disclose to the parties and their statutory agents and litigation agents, in a timely manner and in accordance with the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on the Disclosure of Trial Process Information by People's Courts through the Internet, the following: the date of case filing, the trial limit, the deduction, extension, or recalculation of the trial limit, and the circumstances and reasons for the adjournment of a court session. Where a party or its statutory agent or litigation agent raises an objection, it may apply for supervision to the court that accepted the case according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　故意违反法律、审判纪律、审判管理规定拖延办案，或者因过失延误办案，造成严重后果的，依照《人民法院工作人员处分条例》第四十七条的规定予以处分。

**Article 5** A person who intentionally violates laws, trial discipline, or trial management provisions to delay case handling, or negligently delays case handling, causing serious consequences, shall be sanctioned in accordance with Article 47 of the Regulations on Sanctions for Personnel of People's Courts.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　本规定自2018年4月26日起施行；最高人民法院此前发布的司法解释及规范性文件与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 6** These Provisions shall come into force on April 26, 2018; where any judicial interpretation or normative document previously issued by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
