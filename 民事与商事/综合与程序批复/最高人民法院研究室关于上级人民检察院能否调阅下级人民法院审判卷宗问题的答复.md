# 最高人民法院研究室关于上级人民检察院能否调阅下级人民法院审判卷宗问题的答复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发文字号**：法明传〔1995〕16号  
**公布日期**：1995-01-17  
**生效日期**：1995-01-17

> 根据刑事诉讼法有关规定的精神，上级人民检察院或者同级人民检察院在办理刑事案件过程中，可以查阅同级人民法院的有关案卷材料或者通过同级人民法院查阅下级人民法院的有关案卷材料，但是不应直接调阅下级人民法院的有关案卷材料。可以查阅的案卷不包括合议庭评议记录、审判委员会讨论记录等法院内部对案件研究处理的意见材料。 附： 四川省高级人民法院 关于上级检察机关能否调阅下级 人民法院审判卷宗问题的请示 1994年11月9日      川高法〔1994〕167号 最高人民法院： 近年来，我省一些地方人民法院与检察院在检察机关能否调阅人民法院审判卷宗问题上认识不一。讨论中有三种意见：一种意见认为，只有上级法院才有权调阅下级法院的审判卷宗，上级检察机关或同级检察机关因办案需要，可以向下级法院或同级法院借卷；第二种意见认为，根据刑事诉讼法规定精神，上级检察机关或同级检察机关根据办案需要，可以调阅同级法院或通过同级法院调阅下级法院的审判卷宗。但不能调阅法院内部的“审委会笔录”、“请示”、“答复”、“研究记录”、“审理报告”、“合议庭记录”等材料；第三种意见认为，继续执行最高人民检察院1985年4月27日高检研发字〔1985〕第14号对云南省人民检察院的请示批复，上级人民检察院有权调阅下级人民法院的审判卷宗，并不受调阅方法和范围的限制。 我们倾向于第二种意见。 当否，请批复。

我们倾向于第二种意见。  
当否，请批复。

**Article 1** Appendix:  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province  
Request for Instructions on Whether a Higher-Level People's Procuratorate May Access the Trial Archives of a Lower-Level People's Court  
November 9, 1994 Chuan Gao Fa [1994] No. 167  
Supreme People's Court:  
In recent years, some local people's courts and people's procuratorates in our province have held differing views on whether a people's procuratorate may access the trial archives of a people's court. During the discussion, three opinions emerged: one opinion holds that only the higher-level people's court shall have the right to access the trial archives of a lower-level people's court, while a higher-level or equivalent-level people's procuratorate may, for the needs of case handling, borrow case files from a lower-level or equivalent-level court; the second opinion holds that, in accordance with the spirit of the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law, a higher-level or equivalent-level people's procuratorate may, for the needs of case handling, access the trial archives of an equivalent-level court or, through the equivalent-level court, access the trial archives of a lower-level court. However, they may not access materials such as the “Adjudication Committee Minutes,” “Requests for Instructions,” “Replies,” “Research Records,” “Trial Reports,” or “Collegial Panel Records” within the court; the third opinion holds that the Supreme People's Procuratorate's Reply to the Yunnan Provincial People's Procuratorate (No. 14 of Gao Jian Yan Fa Zi [1985]), issued on April 27, 1985, shall continue to be implemented, whereby a higher-level people's procuratorate has the right to access the trial archives of a lower-level people's court, and such access shall not be limited by method or scope.  
We are inclined toward the second opinion.  
We request your instructions on whether this is appropriate.
