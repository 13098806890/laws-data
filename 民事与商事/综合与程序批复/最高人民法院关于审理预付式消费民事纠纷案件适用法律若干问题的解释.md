# 最高人民法院关于审理预付式消费民事纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2025〕4号  
**公布日期**：2024-11-18  
**生效日期**：2025-05-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理预付式消费民事纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2024年11月18日经最高人民法院审判委员会第1932次会议通过，现予公布，自2025年5月1日起施行。 2025年3月13日 最高人民法院 关于审理预付式消费民事纠纷案件 适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2025〕4号 为正确审理预付式消费民事纠纷案件，保护消费者和经营者权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条　在零售、住宿、餐饮、健身、出行、理发、美容、培训、养老、旅游等生活消费领域，经营者收取预付款后多次或者持续向消费者兑付商品或者提供服务产生的纠纷适用本解释。 第二条　不记名预付卡的持卡人起诉请求经营者承担民事责任的，人民法院应当依法受理。记名预付卡的实际持卡人与预付卡记载的持卡人不一致，但提供其系合法持卡人的初步证据，起诉请求经营者承担民事责任的，人民法院应当依法受理。 消费者提供其与经营者存在预付式消费合同关系的其他初步证据，起诉请求经营者承担民事责任的，人民法院应当依法受理。 第三条　监护人与经营者订立预付式消费合同，约定由经营者向被监护人兑付商品或者提供服务，监护人因预付式消费合同纠纷以被监护人名义起诉，请求经营者承担民事责任的，人民法院应当向监护人释明应以其本人名义起诉。 被监护人因接受商品或者服务权益受到损害，起诉请求经营者承担责任的，人民法院应当依法受理。 第四条　经营者允许他人使用其营业执照或者以其他方式允许他人使用其名义与消费者订立预付式消费合同，消费者请求经营者承担民事责任，经营者以其并非实际经营者为由提出抗辩的，人民法院对其抗辩不予支持。 第五条　同一品牌商业特许经营体系内企业标志或者注册商标使用权的特许人与消费者订立预付式消费合同，消费者因权益受到损害请求被特许人承担民事责任，存在下列情形之一的，人民法院应予支持： （一）被特许人事先同意承担预付式消费合同义务；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　在零售、住宿、餐饮、健身、出行、理发、美容、培训、养老、旅游等生活消费领域，经营者收取预付款后多次或者持续向消费者兑付商品或者提供服务产生的纠纷适用本解释。

**Article 1** This Interpretation applies to disputes arising from operators collecting prepayments and subsequently delivering goods or providing services to consumers multiple times or continuously in the fields of retail, accommodation, catering, fitness, transportation, haircutting, beauty, training, elderly care, tourism, and other living consumption areas.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　不记名预付卡的持卡人起诉请求经营者承担民事责任的，人民法院应当依法受理。记名预付卡的实际持卡人与预付卡记载的持卡人不一致，但提供其系合法持卡人的初步证据，起诉请求经营者承担民事责任的，人民法院应当依法受理。  
消费者提供其与经营者存在预付式消费合同关系的其他初步证据，起诉请求经营者承担民事责任的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 2** Where a holder of an unnamed prepaid card initiates a prosecution requesting the operator to bear civil liability, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law. Where the actual holder of a named prepaid card is inconsistent with the holder recorded on the prepaid card, but provides preliminary evidence that he or she is the lawful holder, and initiates a prosecution requesting the operator to bear civil liability, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.  
Where a consumer provides other preliminary evidence that a prepaid consumption contractual relationship exists between the consumer and the operator, and initiates a prosecution requesting the operator to bear civil liability, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　监护人与经营者订立预付式消费合同，约定由经营者向被监护人兑付商品或者提供服务，监护人因预付式消费合同纠纷以被监护人名义起诉，请求经营者承担民事责任的，人民法院应当向监护人释明应以其本人名义起诉。  
被监护人因接受商品或者服务权益受到损害，起诉请求经营者承担责任的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 3** Where a guardian concludes a prepaid consumption contract with an operator, agreeing that the operator shall deliver goods or provide services to the ward, and the guardian initiates a prosecution in the ward's name due to a dispute over the prepaid consumption contract, requesting the operator to bear civil liability, the People's Court shall explain to the guardian that the guardian shall initiate the prosecution in his or her own name.  
Where the ward's rights and interests are damaged due to receiving goods or services, and the ward initiates a prosecution requesting the operator to bear liability, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　经营者允许他人使用其营业执照或者以其他方式允许他人使用其名义与消费者订立预付式消费合同，消费者请求经营者承担民事责任，经营者以其并非实际经营者为由提出抗辩的，人民法院对其抗辩不予支持。

**Article 4** Where an operator permits another person to use its business license or otherwise permits another person to use its name to conclude a prepaid consumption contract with a consumer, and the consumer requests the operator to bear civil liability, but the operator raises a defense on the ground that it is not the actual operator, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　同一品牌商业特许经营体系内企业标志或者注册商标使用权的特许人与消费者订立预付式消费合同，消费者因权益受到损害请求被特许人承担民事责任，存在下列情形之一的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）被特许人事先同意承担预付式消费合同义务；  
（二）被特许人事后追认预付式消费合同；  
（三）特许经营合同约定消费者可以直接请求被特许人向其履行债务；  
（四）被特许人的行为使消费者有理由相信其受预付式消费合同约束。  
消费者与被特许人订立预付式消费合同后，因权益受到损害请求特许人承担民事责任的，参照适用前款规定。  
不存在前两款规定情形，但特许人对消费者损失产生或者扩大有过错，消费者请求特许人根据其过错承担民事责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 5** Where a franchisor of an enterprise logo or registered trademark use right within the same brand commercial franchise system concludes a prepaid consumption contract with a consumer, and the consumer, due to damage to his or her rights and interests, requests the franchisee to bear civil liability, and any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall support the request:  
(1) The franchisee has previously agreed to assume the obligations under the prepaid consumption contract;  
(2) The franchisee has subsequently ratified the prepaid consumption contract;  
(3) The franchise contract stipulates that the consumer may directly request the franchisee to perform the obligations toward the consumer;  
(4) The franchisee's conduct has given the consumer reason to believe that the franchisee is bound by the prepaid consumption contract.  
Where a consumer concludes a prepaid consumption contract with a franchisee and subsequently, due to damage to his or her rights and interests, requests the franchisor to bear civil liability, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply mutatis mutandis.  
Where the circumstances specified in the preceding two paragraphs do not exist, but the franchisor is at fault for the occurrence or expansion of the consumer's loss, and the consumer requests the franchisor to bear civil liability according to its fault, the People's Court shall support the request.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　商场场地出租者未要求租赁商场场地的经营者提供经营资质证明、营业执照，致使不具有资质的经营者租赁其场地收取消费者预付款并造成消费者损失，消费者请求场地出租者根据其过错承担民事责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
场地出租者承担赔偿责任后，向租赁商场场地的经营者追偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 6** Where a lessor of a market premises fails to require the lessee operating on the market premises to provide business qualification certificates or a business license, thereby causing a lessee without qualifications to lease the premises, collect prepayments from consumers, and cause losses to consumers, and the consumer requests the premises lessor to bear civil liability according to its fault, the People's Court shall support the request.  
After the premises lessor bears compensation liability, if it seeks recourse against the lessee operating on the market premises, the People's Court shall support the request.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　经营者收取预付款后因经营困难不能按照合同约定兑付商品或者提供服务的，应当及时依法清算。经营者依法应当清算但未及时进行清算，造成消费者损失，消费者请求经营者的清算义务人依法承担民事责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 7** Where an operator, after collecting prepayments, is unable to deliver goods or provide services in accordance with the contract due to operational difficulties, it shall promptly conduct liquidation according to law. Where the operator shall conduct liquidation according to law but fails to do so in a timely manner, causing losses to consumers, and the consumer requests the operator's liquidation obligor to bear civil liability according to law, the People's Court shall support the request.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　经营者未与消费者就商品或者服务的质量、价款、履行期限、履行地点和履行方式等内容订立书面合同或者虽订立书面合同但对合同内容约定不明，依照[民法典第五百一十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-510)、第五百一十一条等规定对合同内容可以作出两种以上解释，消费者主张就合同内容作出对其有利的解释的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 8** Where an operator fails to conclude a written contract with a consumer regarding the quality, price, time limit for performance, place of performance, method of performance, and other contents of goods or services, or although a written contract is concluded, the contract contents are unclear, and pursuant to the provisions of Articles 510 and 511 of the Civil Code and other provisions, the contract contents may be interpreted in two or more ways, and the consumer claims that the contract contents shall be interpreted in a manner favorable to the consumer, the People's Court shall support the claim.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　消费者依照[消费者权益保护法第二十六条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法.md#art-26)、[民法典第四百九十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-497)等法律规定，主张经营者提供的下列格式条款无效的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）排除消费者依法解除合同或者请求返还预付款的权利；  
（二）不合理地限制消费者转让预付式消费合同债权；  
（三）约定消费者遗失记名预付卡后不补办；  
（四）约定经营者有权单方变更兑付商品或者提供服务的价款、种类、质量、数量等合同实质性内容；  
（五）免除经营者对所兑付商品或者提供服务的瑕疵担保责任或者造成消费者损失的赔偿责任；  
（六）约定的解决争议方法不合理增加消费者维权成本；  
（七）存在其他排除或者限制消费者权利、减轻或者免除经营者责任、加重消费者责任等对消费者不公平、不合理情形。

**Article 9** Where a consumer, pursuant to Article 26 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests, Article 497 of the Civil Code, and other legal provisions, claims that the following standard terms provided by the operator are void, the People's Court shall support the claim:  
(1) excluding the consumer's right to dissolve the contract or request the return of prepayments according to law;  
(2) unreasonably restricting the consumer's transfer of prepaid consumption contract rights;  
(3) stipulating that a lost named prepaid card shall not be reissued;  
(4) stipulating that the operator has the right to unilaterally change the price, type, quality, quantity, and other substantive contents of the goods or services to be delivered;  
(5) exempting the operator from the warranty liability for defects in the goods or services delivered or the liability for compensation for losses caused to the consumer;  
(6) the agreed dispute resolution method unreasonably increases the consumer's cost of rights protection;  
(7) other circumstances that exclude or restrict consumers' rights, reduce or exempt the operator's liability, increase consumers' liability, or are otherwise unfair or unreasonable to consumers.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　无民事行为能力人与经营者订立预付式消费合同，向经营者支付预付款，法定代理人请求确认合同无效、经营者返还预付款的，人民法院应予支持。  
限制民事行为能力人与经营者订立预付式消费合同，向经营者支付预付款，法定代理人请求确认合同无效、经营者返还预付款的，人民法院应予支持，但该合同经法定代理人同意、追认或者预付款金额等合同内容与限制民事行为能力人的年龄、智力相适应的除外。  
经营者主张从预付款中抵扣已经兑付商品或者提供服务价款的，人民法院依法予以支持，但经营者违反法律规定向未成年人提供网络付费游戏等服务的除外。

**Article 10** Where a person without capacity for civil conduct concludes a prepaid consumption contract with an operator and pays prepayments to the operator, and the statutory agent requests confirmation that the contract is void and that the operator shall return the prepayments, the People's Court shall support the request.  
Where a person with limited capacity for civil conduct concludes a prepaid consumption contract with an operator and pays prepayments to the operator, and the statutory agent requests confirmation that the contract is void and that the operator shall return the prepayments, the People's Court shall support the request, unless the contract has been consented to or ratified by the statutory agent, or the contract contents such as the amount of prepayments are adapted to the age and intelligence of the person with limited capacity for civil conduct.  
Where the operator claims to deduct the price of goods delivered or services provided from the prepayments, the People's Court shall support the claim according to law, unless the operator has violated legal provisions by providing services such as online paid games to minors.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　消费者转让预付式消费合同债权的，自债权转让通知到达经营者时对经营者发生法律效力。债权转让对经营者发生效力后，受让人请求经营者依据预付式消费合同约定兑付商品或者提供服务的，人民法院依法予以支持。受让人请求经营者提供预付卡更名、修改密码等服务的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
预付式消费合同约定经营者在履行期限内向消费者提供不限次数服务，消费者违反诚实信用原则，以债权转让的名义让多名消费者行使本应由一名消费者行使的权利、损害经营者利益，经营者主张债权转让行为对其不发生效力的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 11** Where a consumer transfers prepaid consumption contract rights, the transfer takes legal effect vis-à-vis the operator from the time the notice of the transfer of rights reaches the operator. After the transfer of rights takes effect vis-à-vis the operator, if the transferee requests the operator to deliver goods or provide services in accordance with the prepaid consumption contract, the People's Court shall support the request according to law. If the transferee requests the operator to provide services such as renaming or changing the password of the prepaid card, the People's Court shall support the request according to law.  
Where the prepaid consumption contract stipulates that the operator shall provide unlimited services to the consumer within the performance period, but the consumer, in violation of the principle of good faith, causes multiple consumers to exercise rights that should be exercised by one consumer by means of a transfer of rights, thereby harming the operator's interests, and the operator claims that the transfer of rights is not effective vis-à-vis the operator, the People's Court shall support the claim.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　经营者与消费者订立预付式消费合同后，未经消费者同意，单方提高商品或者服务的价格、降低商品或者服务的质量，消费者请求经营者按合同约定履行义务并承担相应违约责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 12** Where, after concluding a prepaid consumption contract with a consumer, the operator unilaterally increases the price of goods or services or reduces the quality of goods or services without the consumer's consent, and the consumer requests the operator to perform its obligations in accordance with the contract and bear corresponding liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support the request.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　消费者请求解除预付式消费合同，经营者存在下列情形之一的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）变更经营场所给消费者接受商品或者服务造成明显不便；  
（二）未经消费者同意将预付式消费合同义务转移给第三人；  
（三）承诺在合同约定期限内提供不限次数服务却不能正常提供；  
（四）法律规定或者合同约定消费者享有解除合同权利的其他情形。  
预付式消费合同成立后，消费者身体健康等合同的基础条件发生了当事人在订立合同时无法预见、不属于商业风险的重大变化，继续履行合同对于消费者明显不公平的，消费者可以与经营者重新协商；在合理期限内协商不成，消费者请求人民法院变更或者解除预付式消费合同的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 13** Where a consumer requests dissolution of a prepaid consumption contract, and the operator is in any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall support the request:  
(1) Changing the business premises causes significant inconvenience to the consumer in receiving goods or services;  
(2) Transferring the obligations under the prepaid consumption contract to a third party without the consumer's consent;  
(3) Promising to provide unlimited services within the contractually agreed period but failing to provide them normally;  
(4) Other circumstances provided by law or the contract under which the consumer has the right to dissolve the contract.  
After the prepaid consumption contract is formed, if fundamental conditions of the contract, such as the consumer's health, undergo a significant change that the parties could not foresee at the time of concluding the contract and that does not constitute a commercial risk, and continued performance of the contract is obviously unfair to the consumer, the consumer may renegotiate with the operator; if negotiation fails within a reasonable period, and the consumer requests the People's Court to modify or dissolve the prepaid consumption contract, the People's Court shall support the request.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　消费者自付款之日起七日内请求经营者返还预付款本金的，人民法院应予支持，但存在下列情形之一的除外：  
（一）消费者订立预付式消费合同时已经从经营者处获得过相同商品或者服务；  
（二）消费者订立预付式消费合同时已经从其他经营者处获得过相同商品或者服务。  
当事人就消费者无理由退款作出对消费者更有利的约定的，按照约定处理。

**Article 14** Where a consumer requests the operator to return the principal amount of the prepayment within seven days from the date of payment, the People's Court shall support the request, except in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The consumer has already received the same goods or services from the operator at the time of concluding the prepaid consumption contract;  
(2) The consumer has already received the same goods or services from another operator at the time of concluding the prepaid consumption contract.  
If the parties have agreed on terms more favorable to the consumer regarding the consumer's unconditional refund, such agreement shall prevail.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　预付式消费合同解除、无效、被撤销或者确定不发生效力，消费者请求经营者返还剩余预付款并支付利息的，人民法院应予支持。返还预付款本金应为预付款扣减已兑付商品或者提供服务的价款后的余额。  
预付式消费合同解除、无效、被撤销或者确定不发生效力，当事人依照[民法典第一百五十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-157)、第五百六十六条等规定请求赔偿其支付的合理费用等损失的，人民法院应予支持，但当事人因不可抗力或者情势变更解除合同的除外。  
经营者支付给员工等人员的预付款提成不属于前款规定的合理费用。

**Article 15** Where a prepaid consumption contract is dissolved, void, revoked, or determined not to take effect, and the consumer requests the operator to return the remaining prepayment and pay interest, the People's Court shall support the request. The principal amount of the prepayment to be returned shall be the balance after deducting the price of goods delivered or services provided from the prepayment.  
Where a prepaid consumption contract is dissolved, void, revoked, or determined not to take effect, and the party requests compensation for losses such as reasonable expenses incurred, pursuant to Articles 157 and 566 of the Civil Code and other provisions, the People's Court shall support the request, except where the contract is dissolved due to force majeure or change of circumstances.  
The prepayment commission paid by the operator to employees or other personnel shall not constitute the reasonable expenses referred to in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　当事人对返还预付款利息计付标准有约定的，按照约定处理。没有约定或者约定不明，因经营者原因返还预付款的，按照预付式消费合同成立时一年期贷款市场报价利率计算利息；因消费者原因返还预付款的，按照预付式消费合同成立时中国人民银行公布的一年定期存款基准利率计算利息。  
经营者依照行政主管部门要求已将预付款转入监管账户，消费者请求按被监管资金的实际利率计算应返还的被监管部分预付款利息的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 16** Where the parties have agreed on the standard for calculating interest on the returned prepayment, such agreement shall prevail. Where there is no agreement or the agreement is unclear, if the prepayment is returned due to the operator's reasons, interest shall be calculated based on the one-year loan prime rate at the time the prepaid consumption contract was formed; if the prepayment is returned due to the consumer's reasons, interest shall be calculated based on the one-year time deposit benchmark interest rate published by the People's Bank of China at the time the prepaid consumption contract was formed.  
Where the operator has transferred the prepayment into a supervised account as required by the competent administrative department, and the consumer requests that interest on the supervised portion of the prepayment to be returned be calculated based on the actual interest rate on the supervised funds, the People's Court shall support the request.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　预付式消费合同解除、无效、被撤销或者确定不发生效力，消费者请求返还预付款的，自合同解除、被确认无效、被撤销或者确定不发生效力时起计算利息。  
当事人就返还预付款利息起算时间作出对消费者更有利的约定或者法律另有规定的，按照当事人约定或者法律规定处理。

**Article 17** Where a prepaid consumption contract is dissolved, void, revoked, or determined not to take effect, and the consumer requests the return of the prepayment, interest shall be calculated from the time the contract is dissolved, confirmed void, revoked, or determined not to take effect.  
Where the parties have agreed on a more favorable calculation time for interest on the returned prepayment to the consumer, or the law provides otherwise, the parties' agreement or the law shall prevail.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　非因消费者原因返还预付款的，人民法院按下列方式计算已兑付商品或者提供服务的价款：  
（一）经营者向消费者提供折扣商品或者服务的，按折扣价计算已兑付商品或者提供服务的价款；  
（二）经营者向消费者赠送消费金额的，根据消费者实付金额与实付金额加赠送金额之比计算优惠比例，按优惠比例计算已兑付商品或者提供服务的价款。  
当事人就已兑付商品或者提供服务折价作出对消费者更有利的约定的，按照约定处理。

**Article 18** Where the prepayment is returned not due to the consumer's reasons, the People's Court shall calculate the price of goods delivered or services provided in the following manner:  
(1) If the operator provides discounted goods or services to the consumer, the price of goods delivered or services provided shall be calculated based on the discounted price;  
(2) If the operator provides the consumer with a bonus consumption amount, the discount ratio shall be calculated based on the ratio of the consumer's actual payment to the sum of the actual payment and the bonus amount, and the price of goods delivered or services provided shall be calculated based on the discount ratio.  
If the parties have agreed on a more favorable method for valuing the goods delivered or services provided to the consumer, such agreement shall prevail.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　因消费者原因返还预付款，经营者向消费者提供折扣商品、服务或者向消费者赠送消费金额的，人民法院应当按商品或者服务打折前的价格计算已兑付商品或者提供服务的价款。  
消费者主张打折前的价格明显不合理，经营者不能提供打折前价格交易记录的，人民法院可以按照订立合同时履行地同类商品或者服务的市场价格计算已兑付商品或者提供服务的价款。  
当事人就已兑付商品或者提供服务折价作出对消费者更有利的约定的，按照约定处理。

**Article 19** Where the prepayment is returned due to the consumer's reasons, and the operator has provided discounted goods or services or a bonus consumption amount to the consumer, the People's Court shall calculate the price of goods delivered or services provided based on the price before the discount.  
If the consumer claims that the price before the discount is obviously unreasonable, and the operator cannot provide transaction records of the price before the discount, the People's Court may calculate the price of goods delivered or services provided based on the market price of similar goods or services at the place of performance at the time the contract was formed.  
If the parties have agreed on a more favorable method for valuing the goods delivered or services provided to the consumer, such agreement shall prevail.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　按折扣价或者优惠比例计算已兑付商品或者提供服务的价款未超出消费者预付款，但按打折前的价格计算已兑付商品或者提供服务的价款超出消费者预付款，经营者请求消费者支付按打折前的价格计算超出预付款部分价款的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 20** Where the price of goods delivered or services provided calculated based on the discounted price or discount ratio does not exceed the consumer's prepayment, but the price calculated based on the price before the discount exceeds the consumer's prepayment, and the operator requests the consumer to pay the portion exceeding the prepayment calculated based on the price before the discount, the People's Court shall not support the request.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　经营者向消费者赠送商品或者服务，消费者在预付式消费合同解除、无效、被撤销、确定不发生效力后请求返还剩余预付款，经营者主张消费者返还或者折价补偿已经赠送的商品或者服务的，人民法院应当综合考虑已经赠送的商品或者服务的价值、预付式消费合同标的金额、合同履行情况、退款原因等因素，依照诚实信用原则对是否支持经营者主张作出认定。

**Article 21** Where the operator provides the consumer with bonus goods or services, and the consumer requests the return of the remaining prepayment after the prepaid consumption contract is dissolved, void, revoked, or determined not to take effect, and the operator claims that the consumer shall return or compensate for the value of the bonus goods or services already provided, the People's Court shall, taking into account factors such as the value of the bonus goods or services already provided, the subject amount of the prepaid consumption contract, the performance status of the contract, and the reason for the refund, and in accordance with the principle of good faith, determine whether to support the operator's claim.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　预付式消费合同约定经营者在履行期限内向消费者提供不限次数服务，消费者请求按合同解除后的剩余履行期限与全部履行期限的比例计算应予返还的预付款的，人民法院应予支持。  
经营者在预付式消费合同解除前已经停止提供商品或者服务，消费者请求按经营者停止提供商品或者服务后的剩余履行期限与全部履行期限的比例计算应予返还的预付款的，人民法院应予支持。  
消费者因自身原因未在合同约定履行期限内要求经营者提供服务，请求返还预付款的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 22** Where the prepaid consumption contract stipulates that the operator shall provide unlimited services to the consumer within the performance period, and the consumer requests that the prepayment to be returned be calculated based on the ratio of the remaining performance period after dissolution of the contract to the total performance period, the People's Court shall support the request.  
Where the operator has stopped providing goods or services before the dissolution of the prepaid consumption contract, and the consumer requests that the prepayment to be returned be calculated based on the ratio of the remaining performance period after the operator stopped providing goods or services to the total performance period, the People's Court shall support the request.  
Where the consumer, for reasons attributable to himself or herself, fails to request the operator to provide services within the contractually agreed performance period and requests the return of the prepayment, the People's Court shall not support the request.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　经营者收取预付款后终止营业，既不按照约定兑付商品或者提供服务又恶意逃避消费者申请退款，消费者请求经营者承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
经营者行为涉嫌刑事犯罪的，人民法院应当将犯罪线索移送公安机关。

**Article 23** Where the operator terminates its business after collecting prepayments, neither delivers goods or provides services as agreed nor maliciously evades consumers' applications for refunds, and the consumer requests the operator to bear punitive damages, the People's Court shall support the request according to law.  
Where the operator's conduct is suspected of constituting a criminal offense, the People's Court shall transfer the criminal clues to the public security organ.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　消费者请求经营者对尚有资金余额的预付卡提供激活、换卡等服务的，人民法院应予支持。  
消费者请求经营者对尚有资金余额的记名预付卡提供挂失和补办服务的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 24** Where a consumer requests the operator to provide services such as activation or card replacement for a prepaid card with a remaining balance, the People's Court shall support the request.  
Where a consumer requests the operator to provide reporting of loss and reissuance services for a named prepaid card with a remaining balance, the People's Court shall support the request.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　经营者控制合同文本或者记录消费内容、消费次数、消费金额、预付款余额等信息的证据，无正当理由拒不提交，消费者主张该证据的内容不利于经营者的，人民法院可以根据消费者的主张认定争议事实。

**Article 25** Where the operator controls evidence such as the contract text or records of consumption content, consumption times, consumption amounts, and prepayment balances, and refuses to submit it without justifiable reasons, and the consumer claims that the content of such evidence is unfavorable to the operator, the People's Court may determine the disputed facts based on the consumer's claim.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本解释所称预付卡为单用途商业预付卡，包括以磁条卡、芯片卡、纸券等为载体的实体卡和以密码、串码、图形、生物特征信息等为载体的虚拟卡。  
因多用途预付卡产生的纠纷不适用本解释。

**Article 26** The term "prepaid card" as used in this Interpretation refers to single-purpose commercial prepaid cards, including physical cards using magnetic stripe cards, chip cards, paper vouchers, etc., as carriers, and virtual cards using passwords, serial codes, graphics, biometric information, etc., as carriers.  
This Interpretation does not apply to disputes arising from multi-purpose prepaid cards.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本解释自2025年5月1日起施行。

**Article 27** This Interpretation shall come into force on May 1, 2025.
