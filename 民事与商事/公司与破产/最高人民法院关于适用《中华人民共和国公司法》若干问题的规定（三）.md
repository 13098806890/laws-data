# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》若干问题的规定（三）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：2014-02-20  
**生效日期**：2014-03-01

> （2010年12月6日最高人民法院审判委员会第1504次会议通过　根据2014年2月17日最高人民法院审判委员会第1607次会议《关于修改关于适用〈中华人民共和国公司法〉若干问题的规定的决定》修正） 为正确适用《中华人民共和国公司法》，结合审判实践，就人民法院审理公司设立、出资、股权确认等纠纷案件适用法律问题作出如下规定。 第一条　为设立公司而签署公司章程、向公司认购出资或者股份并履行公司设立职责的人，应当认定为公司的发起人，包括有限责任公司设立时的股东。 第二条　发起人为设立公司以自己名义对外签订合同，合同相对人请求该发起人承担合同责任的，人民法院应予支持。 公司成立后对前款规定的合同予以确认，或者已经实际享有合同权利或者履行合同义务，合同相对人请求公司承担合同责任的，人民法院应予支持。 第三条　发起人以设立中公司名义对外签订合同，公司成立后合同相对人请求公司承担合同责任的，人民法院应予支持。 公司成立后有证据证明发起人利用设立中公司的名义为自己的利益与相对人签订合同，公司以此为由主张不承担合同责任的，人民法院应予支持，但相对人为善意的除外。 第四条　公司因故未成立，债权人请求全体或者部分发起人对设立公司行为所产生的费用和债务承担连带清偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。 部分发起人依照前款规定承担责任后，请求其他发起人分担的，人民法院应当判令其他发起人按照约定的责任承担比例分担责任；没有约定责任承担比例的，按照约定的出资比例分担责任；没有约定出资比例的，按照均等份额分担责任。 因部分发起人的过错导致公司未成立，其他发起人主张其承担设立行为所产生的费用和债务的，人民法院应当根据过错情况，确定过错一方的责任范围。 第五条　发起人因履行公司设立职责造成他人损害，公司成立后受害人请求公司承担侵权赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持；公司未成立，受害人请求全体发起人承担连带赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。 公司或者无过错的发起人承担赔偿责任后，可以向有过错的发起人追偿。 第六条　股份有限公司的认股人未按期缴纳所认股份的股款，经公司发起人催缴后在合理期间内仍未缴纳，公司发起人对该股份另行募集的，人民法院应当认定该募集行为有效。认股人延期缴纳股款给公司造成损失，公司请求该认股人承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。 第七条　出资人以不享有处分权的财产出资，当事人之间对于出资行为效力产生争议的，人民法院可以参照物权法第一百零六条的规定予以认定。 以贪污、受贿、侵占、挪用等违法犯罪所得的货币出资后取得股权的，对违法犯罪行为予以追究、处罚时，应当采取拍卖或者变卖的方式处置其股权。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为设立公司而签署公司章程、向公司认购出资或者股份并履行公司设立职责的人，应当认定为公司的发起人，包括有限责任公司设立时的股东。

**Article 1** A person who signs the articles of association, subscribes to capital contributions or shares, and performs the duties of company establishment for the purpose of incorporating a company shall be deemed as a promoter of the company, including shareholders at the time of incorporation of a limited liability company.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　发起人为设立公司以自己名义对外签订合同，合同相对人请求该发起人承担合同责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
公司成立后对前款规定的合同予以确认，或者已经实际享有合同权利或者履行合同义务，合同相对人请求公司承担合同责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 2** Where a promoter concludes a contract with a third party in its own name for the purpose of incorporating a company, and the counterparty to the contract requests the promoter to bear contractual liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where after the incorporation of the company, the company confirms the contract specified in the preceding paragraph or has actually enjoyed the contractual rights or performed the contractual obligations, and the counterparty to the contract requests the company to bear contractual liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　发起人以设立中公司名义对外签订合同，公司成立后合同相对人请求公司承担合同责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
公司成立后有证据证明发起人利用设立中公司的名义为自己的利益与相对人签订合同，公司以此为由主张不承担合同责任的，人民法院应予支持，但相对人为善意的除外。

**Article 3** Where a promoter concludes a contract with a third party in the name of a company under incorporation, and after the incorporation of the company, the counterparty to the contract requests the company to bear contractual liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where after the incorporation of the company, there is evidence proving that the promoter used the name of the company under incorporation to conclude a contract with the counterparty for its own benefit, and the company claims not to bear contractual liability on such ground, the People's Court shall uphold such claim, unless the counterparty is in good faith.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　公司因故未成立，债权人请求全体或者部分发起人对设立公司行为所产生的费用和债务承担连带清偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
部分发起人依照前款规定承担责任后，请求其他发起人分担的，人民法院应当判令其他发起人按照约定的责任承担比例分担责任；没有约定责任承担比例的，按照约定的出资比例分担责任；没有约定出资比例的，按照均等份额分担责任。  
因部分发起人的过错导致公司未成立，其他发起人主张其承担设立行为所产生的费用和债务的，人民法院应当根据过错情况，确定过错一方的责任范围。

**Article 4** Where a company is not incorporated for any reason, and a creditor requests all or some of the promoters to bear joint and several liability for the discharge of the expenses and debts incurred from the act of incorporation of the company, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where some of the promoters assume liability in accordance with the preceding paragraph and then request other promoters to share such liability, the People's Court shall order the other promoters to share the liability in proportion to the agreed liability-sharing ratio; if there is no agreed liability-sharing ratio, in proportion to the agreed capital contribution ratio; if there is no agreed capital contribution ratio, in equal shares.  
Where the non-incorporation of the company is caused by the fault of some promoters, and other promoters claim that the former shall bear the expenses and debts incurred from the act of incorporation, the People's Court shall determine the scope of liability of the faulty party based on the fault circumstances.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　发起人因履行公司设立职责造成他人损害，公司成立后受害人请求公司承担侵权赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持；公司未成立，受害人请求全体发起人承担连带赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
公司或者无过错的发起人承担赔偿责任后，可以向有过错的发起人追偿。

**Article 5** Where a promoter causes damage to another person in the performance of the duties of company incorporation, and after the incorporation of the company, the victim requests the company to bear tort compensation liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request; if the company is not incorporated, and the victim requests all promoters to bear joint and several compensation liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
After the company or a promoter without fault assumes compensation liability, it may seek recourse from the promoter at fault.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　股份有限公司的认股人未按期缴纳所认股份的股款，经公司发起人催缴后在合理期间内仍未缴纳，公司发起人对该股份另行募集的，人民法院应当认定该募集行为有效。认股人延期缴纳股款给公司造成损失，公司请求该认股人承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 6** Where a subscriber of shares in a joint stock limited company fails to pay the subscription price for the subscribed shares within the prescribed period, and after a demand for payment by the company's promoters, still fails to pay within a reasonable period, and the promoters raise the shares separately, the People's Court shall deem such raising valid. Where the subscriber's delayed payment of the subscription price causes losses to the company, and the company requests the subscriber to bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　出资人以不享有处分权的财产出资，当事人之间对于出资行为效力产生争议的，人民法院可以参照物权法第一百零六条的规定予以认定。  
以贪污、受贿、侵占、挪用等违法犯罪所得的货币出资后取得股权的，对违法犯罪行为予以追究、处罚时，应当采取拍卖或者变卖的方式处置其股权。

**Article 7** Where a capital contributor makes a capital contribution with property over which it has no power of disposition, and the parties dispute the validity of the capital contribution, the People's Court may make a determination by reference to Article 106 of the Property Law.  
Where equity is obtained by making a capital contribution in monetary form from illegal or criminal proceeds such as embezzlement, bribery, misappropriation, or illegal use of funds, when the illegal or criminal act is investigated and punished, the equity shall be disposed of by auction or sale.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　出资人以划拨土地使用权出资，或者以设定权利负担的土地使用权出资，公司、其他股东或者公司债权人主张认定出资人未履行出资义务的，人民法院应当责令当事人在指定的合理期间内办理土地变更手续或者解除权利负担；逾期未办理或者未解除的，人民法院应当认定出资人未依法全面履行出资义务。

**Article 8** Where a capital contributor makes a capital contribution with a land use right obtained through allocation or with a land use right subject to a security interest or other encumbrance, and the company, other shareholders, or corporate creditors claim that the capital contributor has not fulfilled its capital contribution obligations, the People's Court shall order the party to undergo the procedures for the change of land use right or remove the encumbrance within a specified reasonable period; if the party fails to do so within the prescribed period, the People's Court shall deem that the capital contributor has not fully performed its capital contribution obligations in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　出资人以非货币财产出资，未依法评估作价，公司、其他股东或者公司债权人请求认定出资人未履行出资义务的，人民法院应当委托具有合法资格的评估机构对该财产评估作价。评估确定的价额显著低于公司章程所定价额的，人民法院应当认定出资人未依法全面履行出资义务。

**Article 9** Where a capital contributor makes a capital contribution with non-monetary property without having it lawfully evaluated and priced, and the company, other shareholders, or corporate creditors request a determination that the capital contributor has not fulfilled its capital contribution obligations, the People's Court shall entrust a legally qualified appraisal institution to evaluate and price the property. If the appraised value is significantly lower than the value stipulated in the company's articles of association, the People's Court shall deem that the capital contributor has not fully performed its capital contribution obligations in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　出资人以房屋、土地使用权或者需要办理权属登记的知识产权等财产出资，已经交付公司使用但未办理权属变更手续，公司、其他股东或者公司债权人主张认定出资人未履行出资义务的，人民法院应当责令当事人在指定的合理期间内办理权属变更手续；在前述期间内办理了权属变更手续的，人民法院应当认定其已经履行了出资义务；出资人主张自其实际交付财产给公司使用时享有相应股东权利的，人民法院应予支持。  
出资人以前款规定的财产出资，已经办理权属变更手续但未交付给公司使用，公司或者其他股东主张其向公司交付、并在实际交付之前不享有相应股东权利的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 10** Where a capital contributor makes a capital contribution with property such as a building, land use right, or intellectual property rights that require registration of title, and has delivered the property to the company for use but has not undergone the formalities for change of title, and the company, other shareholders, or corporate creditors claim that the capital contributor has not fulfilled its capital contribution obligations, the People's Court shall order the party to complete the change of title formalities within a specified reasonable period; if the change of title formalities are completed within the aforementioned period, the People's Court shall deem that the capital contributor has performed its capital contribution obligations; if the capital contributor claims that it shall enjoy the corresponding shareholder rights from the date it actually delivered the property to the company for use, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.  
Where a capital contributor makes a capital contribution with the property specified in the preceding paragraph, has completed the change of title formalities but has not delivered the property to the company for use, and the company or other shareholders claim that it shall deliver the property to the company and shall not enjoy the corresponding shareholder rights before actual delivery, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　出资人以其他公司股权出资，符合下列条件的，人民法院应当认定出资人已履行出资义务：  
（一）出资的股权由出资人合法持有并依法可以转让；  
（二）出资的股权无权利瑕疵或者权利负担；  
（三）出资人已履行关于股权转让的法定手续；  
（四）出资的股权已依法进行了价值评估。  
股权出资不符合前款第（一）、（二）、（三）项的规定，公司、其他股东或者公司债权人请求认定出资人未履行出资义务的，人民法院应当责令该出资人在指定的合理期间内采取补正措施，以符合上述条件；逾期未补正的，人民法院应当认定其未依法全面履行出资义务。  
股权出资不符合本条第一款第（四）项的规定，公司、其他股东或者公司债权人请求认定出资人未履行出资义务的，人民法院应当按照[本规定第九条](#art-9)的规定处理。

**Article 11** Where a capital contributor makes a capital contribution with equity in another company, and the following conditions are satisfied, the People's Court shall determine that the capital contributor has performed its capital contribution obligations:  
(1) The equity contributed is lawfully held by the capital contributor and may be transferred in accordance with the law;  
(2) The equity contributed is free from any defects or encumbrances;  
(3) The capital contributor has completed the statutory formalities for the transfer of equity;  
(4) The equity contributed has been lawfully valued.  
Where the equity contribution does not comply with the provisions of items (1), (2), or (3) of the preceding paragraph, and the company, other shareholders, or corporate creditors request a determination that the capital contributor has not fulfilled its capital contribution obligations, the People's Court shall order the capital contributor to take remedial measures to satisfy the above conditions within a specified reasonable period; if the capital contributor fails to do so within the prescribed period, the People's Court shall deem that the capital contributor has not fully performed its capital contribution obligations in accordance with the law.  
Where the equity contribution does not comply with the provisions of item (4) of the first paragraph of this Article, and the company, other shareholders, or corporate creditors request a determination that the capital contributor has not fulfilled its capital contribution obligations, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of Article 9 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　公司成立后，公司、股东或者公司债权人以相关股东的行为符合下列情形之一且损害公司权益为由，请求认定该股东抽逃出资的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）制作虚假财务会计报表虚增利润进行分配；  
（二）通过虚构债权债务关系将其出资转出；  
（三）利用关联交易将出资转出；  
（四）其他未经法定程序将出资抽回的行为。

**Article 12** After the incorporation of the company, where the company, shareholders, or corporate creditors request a determination that the relevant shareholder has withdrawn its capital contribution on the ground that the shareholder's conduct meets any of the following circumstances and damages the company's interests, the People's Court shall uphold such request:  
(1) fabricating false financial accounting statements to inflate profits for distribution;  
(2) transferring out its capital contribution through fictitious creditor-debtor relationships;  
(3) transferring out its capital contribution through related-party transactions;  
(4) other acts of withdrawing capital contribution without going through legal procedures.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　股东未履行或者未全面履行出资义务，公司或者其他股东请求其向公司依法全面履行出资义务的，人民法院应予支持。  
公司债权人请求未履行或者未全面履行出资义务的股东在未出资本息范围内对公司债务不能清偿的部分承担补充赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持；未履行或者未全面履行出资义务的股东已经承担上述责任，其他债权人提出相同请求的，人民法院不予支持。  
股东在公司设立时未履行或者未全面履行出资义务，依照本条第一款或者第二款提起诉讼的原告，请求公司的发起人与被告股东承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持；公司的发起人承担责任后，可以向被告股东追偿。  
股东在公司增资时未履行或者未全面履行出资义务，依照本条第一款或者第二款提起诉讼的原告，请求未尽[公司法第一百四十七条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-147)第一款规定的义务而使出资未缴足的董事、高级管理人员承担相应责任的，人民法院应予支持；董事、高级管理人员承担责任后，可以向被告股东追偿。

**Article 13** Where a shareholder fails to perform or fully perform its capital contribution obligations, and the company or other shareholders request it to fully perform its capital contribution obligations in accordance with the law, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where a corporate creditor requests a shareholder that has failed to perform or fully perform its capital contribution obligations to assume supplementary compensation liability for the part of the company's debts that cannot be satisfied, within the scope of the unpaid capital contribution and the interest thereon, the People's Court shall uphold such request; if the shareholder that has failed to perform or fully perform its capital contribution obligations has already assumed the above liability, and other creditors make the same request, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.  
Where a shareholder fails to perform or fully perform its capital contribution obligations at the time of incorporation of the company, and the plaintiff who initiates an action in accordance with the first or second paragraph of this Article requests that the promoters of the company bear joint and several liability with the defendant shareholder, the People's Court shall uphold such request; after the promoters of the company assume liability, they may seek recourse from the defendant shareholder.  
Where a shareholder fails to perform or fully perform its capital contribution obligations at the time of capital increase of the company, and the plaintiff who initiates an action in accordance with the first or second paragraph of this Article requests that the directors or senior officers who have failed to fulfill the obligations under the first paragraph of Article 147 of the Company Law and thereby caused the capital contribution to be unpaid assume corresponding liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request; after the directors or senior officers assume liability, they may seek recourse from the defendant shareholder.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　股东抽逃出资，公司或者其他股东请求其向公司返还出资本息、协助抽逃出资的其他股东、董事、高级管理人员或者实际控制人对此承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
公司债权人请求抽逃出资的股东在抽逃出资本息范围内对公司债务不能清偿的部分承担补充赔偿责任、协助抽逃出资的其他股东、董事、高级管理人员或者实际控制人对此承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持；抽逃出资的股东已经承担上述责任，其他债权人提出相同请求的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 14** Where a shareholder withdraws its capital contribution, and the company or other shareholders request it to return the capital contribution and interest to the company and request that other shareholders, directors, senior officers, or actual controllers who assisted in the withdrawal bear joint and several liability therefor, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where a corporate creditor requests the shareholder that withdrew its capital contribution to assume supplementary compensation liability for the part of the company's debts that cannot be satisfied, within the scope of the withdrawn capital contribution and the interest thereon, and requests that other shareholders, directors, senior officers, or actual controllers who assisted in the withdrawal bear joint and several liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request; if the shareholder that withdrew its capital contribution has already assumed the above liability, and other creditors make the same request, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　出资人以符合法定条件的非货币财产出资后，因市场变化或者其他客观因素导致出资财产贬值，公司、其他股东或者公司债权人请求该出资人承担补足出资责任的，人民法院不予支持。但是，当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 15** Where a capital contributor makes a capital contribution with non-monetary property that meets the statutory conditions, and thereafter the contributed property depreciates due to market changes or other objective factors, and the company, other shareholders, or corporate creditors request the capital contributor to bear the liability to make up the shortfall in the capital contribution, the People's Court shall not uphold such request, unless the parties have otherwise agreed.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　股东未履行或者未全面履行出资义务或者抽逃出资，公司根据公司章程或者股东会决议对其利润分配请求权、新股优先认购权、剩余财产分配请求权等股东权利作出相应的合理限制，该股东请求认定该限制无效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 16** Where a shareholder has failed to perform or fully perform its capital contribution obligations or has withdrawn its capital contribution, and the company imposes corresponding reasonable restrictions on the shareholder's rights such as the right to claim profit distribution, preemptive right to subscribe for new shares, and right to claim distribution of residual property in accordance with the company's articles of association or a resolution of the shareholders' meeting, and the shareholder requests a determination that such restrictions are invalid, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　有限责任公司的股东未履行出资义务或者抽逃全部出资，经公司催告缴纳或者返还，其在合理期间内仍未缴纳或者返还出资，公司以股东会决议解除该股东的股东资格，该股东请求确认该解除行为无效的，人民法院不予支持。  
在前款规定的情形下，人民法院在判决时应当释明，公司应当及时办理法定减资程序或者由其他股东或者第三人缴纳相应的出资。在办理法定减资程序或者其他股东或者第三人缴纳相应的出资之前，公司债权人依照[本规定第十三条](#art-13)或者第十四条请求相关当事人承担相应责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 17** Where a shareholder of a limited liability company fails to perform its capital contribution obligations or withdraws all of its capital contribution, and after the company demands payment or return within a reasonable period, the shareholder still fails to pay or return the capital contribution, and the company resolves by a resolution of the shareholders' meeting to remove the shareholder's status, and the shareholder requests confirmation that such removal is invalid, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.  
In the circumstances set forth in the preceding paragraph, the People's Court shall, in its judgment, clarify that the company shall promptly undergo statutory capital reduction procedures or have the capital contribution made by other shareholders or a third party. Before the statutory capital reduction procedures are completed or the capital contribution is made by other shareholders or a third party, if corporate creditors request the relevant parties to assume corresponding liability in accordance with Article 13 or Article 14 of these Provisions, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　有限责任公司的股东未履行或者未全面履行出资义务即转让股权，受让人对此知道或者应当知道，公司请求该股东履行出资义务、受让人对此承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持；公司债权人依照[本规定第十三条](#art-13)第二款向该股东提起诉讼，同时请求前述受让人对此承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
受让人根据前款规定承担责任后，向该未履行或者未全面履行出资义务的股东追偿的，人民法院应予支持。但是，当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 18** Where a shareholder of a limited liability company transfers its equity without having performed or fully performed its capital contribution obligations, and the transferee knew or should have known of such fact, and the company requests the shareholder to perform the capital contribution obligations and the transferee to bear joint and several liability therefor, the People's Court shall uphold such request; where a corporate creditor initiates an action against the shareholder in accordance with the second paragraph of Article 13 of these Provisions and simultaneously requests the aforementioned transferee to bear joint and several liability therefor, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where the transferee assumes liability in accordance with the preceding paragraph and then seeks recourse from the shareholder that failed to perform or fully perform its capital contribution obligations, the People's Court shall uphold such request, unless the parties have otherwise agreed.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　公司股东未履行或者未全面履行出资义务或者抽逃出资，公司或者其他股东请求其向公司全面履行出资义务或者返还出资，被告股东以诉讼时效为由进行抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。  
公司债权人的债权未过诉讼时效期间，其依照[本规定第十三条](#art-13)第二款、第十四条第二款的规定请求未履行或者未全面履行出资义务或者抽逃出资的股东承担赔偿责任，被告股东以出资义务或者返还出资义务超过诉讼时效期间为由进行抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 19** Where a shareholder of a company fails to perform or fully perform its capital contribution obligations or withdraws its capital contribution, and the company or other shareholders request it to fully perform its capital contribution obligations or return the capital contribution, if the defendant shareholder defends on the ground of limitation of action, the People's Court shall not uphold such defense.  
Where the claims of a corporate creditor have not exceeded the limitation of action period, and the creditor requests, in accordance with the second paragraph of Article 13 and the second paragraph of Article 14 of these Provisions, that the shareholder that failed to perform or fully perform its capital contribution obligations or withdrew its capital contribution bear compensation liability, and the defendant shareholder defends on the ground that the capital contribution obligation or the obligation to return the capital contribution has exceeded the limitation of action period, the People's Court shall not uphold such defense.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　当事人之间对是否已履行出资义务发生争议，原告提供对股东履行出资义务产生合理怀疑证据的，被告股东应当就其已履行出资义务承担举证责任。

**Article 20** Where the parties dispute whether the capital contribution obligations have been performed, and the plaintiff adduces evidence that gives rise to a reasonable doubt regarding the shareholder's performance of capital contribution obligations, the defendant shareholder shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that it has performed its capital contribution obligations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　当事人向人民法院起诉请求确认其股东资格的，应当以公司为被告，与案件争议股权有利害关系的人作为第三人参加诉讼。

**Article 21** Where a party initiates a prosecution before the People's Court requesting confirmation of its shareholder status, the company shall be named as the defendant, and persons who have an interest in the disputed equity shall participate in the proceedings as third parties.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　当事人之间对股权归属发生争议，一方请求人民法院确认其享有股权的，应当证明以下事实之一：  
（一）已经依法向公司出资或者认缴出资，且不违反法律法规强制性规定；  
（二）已经受让或者以其他形式继受公司股权，且不违反法律法规强制性规定。

**Article 22** Where the parties dispute the ownership of equity, and a party requests the People's Court to confirm that it holds the equity, it shall prove one of the following facts:  
(1) It has lawfully made a capital contribution or subscribed to a capital contribution to the company, and such act does not violate any mandatory provisions of laws or regulations;  
(2) It has lawfully acquired or otherwise succeeded to the equity of the company, and such act does not violate any mandatory provisions of laws or regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　当事人依法履行出资义务或者依法继受取得股权后，公司未根据[公司法第三十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-31)、第三十二条的规定签发出资证明书、记载于股东名册并办理公司登记机关登记，当事人请求公司履行上述义务的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 23** Where, after a party lawfully performs its capital contribution obligations or lawfully succeeds to and acquires equity, the company fails to issue a capital contribution certificate, record the shareholder in the register of shareholders, and register with the company registration authority in accordance with Articles 31 and 32 of the Company Law, and the party requests the company to perform the above obligations, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　有限责任公司的实际出资人与名义出资人订立合同，约定由实际出资人出资并享有投资权益，以名义出资人为名义股东，实际出资人与名义股东对该合同效力发生争议的，如无合同法第五十二条规定的情形，人民法院应当认定该合同有效。  
前款规定的实际出资人与名义股东因投资权益的归属发生争议，实际出资人以其实际履行了出资义务为由向名义股东主张权利的，人民法院应予支持。名义股东以公司股东名册记载、公司登记机关登记为由否认实际出资人权利的，人民法院不予支持。  
实际出资人未经公司其他股东半数以上同意，请求公司变更股东、签发出资证明书、记载于股东名册、记载于公司章程并办理公司登记机关登记的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 24** Where a beneficial investor of a limited liability company and a nominal investor enter into a contract stipulating that the beneficial investor shall make the capital contribution and enjoy the investment rights and interests, and the nominal investor shall be the nominal shareholder, and a dispute arises between the beneficial investor and the nominal shareholder over the validity of the contract, if there is no circumstance described in Article 52 of the Contract Law, the People's Court shall determine that the contract is valid.  
Where a dispute arises between the beneficial investor and the nominal shareholder specified in the preceding paragraph over the ownership of investment rights and interests, and the beneficial investor asserts rights against the nominal shareholder on the ground that it has actually performed the capital contribution obligations, the People's Court shall uphold such claim. If the nominal shareholder denies the rights of the beneficial investor on the ground of the records in the company's register of shareholders or the registration with the company registration authority, the People's Court shall not uphold such defense.  
Where the beneficial investor, without the consent of more than half of the other shareholders of the company, requests the company to change the shareholder, issue a capital contribution certificate, record the shareholder in the register of shareholders and the articles of association, and undergo registration with the company registration authority, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　名义股东将登记于其名下的股权转让、质押或者以其他方式处分，实际出资人以其对于股权享有实际权利为由，请求认定处分股权行为无效的，人民法院可以参照物权法第一百零六条的规定处理。  
名义股东处分股权造成实际出资人损失，实际出资人请求名义股东承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 25** Where the nominal shareholder transfers, pledges, or otherwise disposes of the equity registered in its name, and the beneficial investor requests a determination that the disposal of the equity is invalid on the ground that it enjoys the actual right to the equity, the People's Court may handle the matter by reference to Article 106 of the Property Law.  
Where the nominal shareholder's disposal of the equity causes losses to the beneficial investor, and the beneficial investor requests the nominal shareholder to bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　公司债权人以登记于公司登记机关的股东未履行出资义务为由，请求其对公司债务不能清偿的部分在未出资本息范围内承担补充赔偿责任，股东以其仅为名义股东而非实际出资人为由进行抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。  
名义股东根据前款规定承担赔偿责任后，向实际出资人追偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 26** Where a corporate creditor requests a shareholder registered with the company registration authority to assume supplementary compensation liability for the part of the company's debts that cannot be satisfied, within the scope of the unpaid capital contribution and the interest thereon, on the ground that the shareholder has not performed its capital contribution obligations, and the shareholder defends on the ground that it is merely a nominal shareholder and not a beneficial investor, the People's Court shall not uphold such defense.  
Where a nominal shareholder, after assuming compensation liability in accordance with the preceding paragraph, seeks recourse from the beneficial investor, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　股权转让后尚未向公司登记机关办理变更登记，原股东将仍登记于其名下的股权转让、质押或者以其他方式处分，受让股东以其对于股权享有实际权利为由，请求认定处分股权行为无效的，人民法院可以参照物权法第一百零六条的规定处理。  
原股东处分股权造成受让股东损失，受让股东请求原股东承担赔偿责任、对于未及时办理变更登记有过错的董事、高级管理人员或者实际控制人承担相应责任的，人民法院应予支持；受让股东对于未及时办理变更登记也有过错的，可以适当减轻上述董事、高级管理人员或者实际控制人的责任。

**Article 27** Where, after the transfer of equity but before the completion of change registration with the company registration authority, the former shareholder transfers, pledges, or otherwise disposes of the equity still registered in its name, and the transferee shareholder requests a determination that the disposal of the equity is invalid on the ground that it enjoys the actual right to the equity, the People's Court may handle the matter by reference to Article 106 of the Property Law.  
Where the former shareholder's disposal of the equity causes losses to the transferee shareholder, and the transferee shareholder requests the former shareholder to bear compensation liability and requests that directors, senior officers, or actual controllers who were at fault for failing to timely undergo the change registration bear corresponding liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request; if the transferee shareholder was also at fault for failing to timely undergo the change registration, the liability of the aforementioned directors, senior officers, or actual controllers may be appropriately mitigated.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　冒用他人名义出资并将该他人作为股东在公司登记机关登记的，冒名登记行为人应当承担相应责任；公司、其他股东或者公司债权人以未履行出资义务为由，请求被冒名登记为股东的承担补足出资责任或者对公司债务不能清偿部分的赔偿责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 28** Where a person makes a capital contribution using the name of another person without authorization and registers such other person as a shareholder with the company registration authority, the person who made the fraudulent registration shall bear the corresponding liability; if the company, other shareholders, or corporate creditors request, on the ground of failure to perform capital contribution obligations, that the person fraudulently registered as a shareholder bear the liability to make up the shortfall in capital contribution or bear compensation liability for the part of the company's debts that cannot be satisfied, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.
