# 最高人民法院关于审理企业破产案件确定管理人报酬的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2007〕9号  
**公布日期**：2007-04-04  
**生效日期**：2007-06-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理企业破产案件确定管理人报酬的规定》已于2007年4月4日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1422次会议通过，现予公布，自2007年6月1日起施行。 2007年4月12日 最高人民法院 关于审理企业破产案件确定 管理人报酬的规定 法释〔2007〕9号 为公正、高效审理企业破产案件，规范人民法院确定管理人报酬工作，根据《中华人民共和国企业破产法》的规定，制定本规定。 第一条  管理人履行企业破产法第二十五条规定的职责，有权获得相应报酬。 管理人报酬由审理企业破产案件的人民法院依据本规定确定。 第二条  人民法院应根据债务人最终清偿的财产价值总额，在以下比例限制范围内分段确定管理人报酬： （一）不超过一百万元（含本数，下同）的，在12%以下确定； （二）超过一百万元至五百万元的部分，在10%以下确定； （三）超过五百万元至一千万元的部分，在8%以下确定； （四）超过一千万元至五千万元的部分，在6%以下确定; （五）超过五千万元至一亿元的部分，在3%以下确定；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  管理人履行[企业破产法第二十五条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-25)规定的职责，有权获得相应报酬。  
管理人报酬由审理企业破产案件的人民法院依据本规定确定。

**Article 1** . The administrator, when performing the duties specified in Article 25 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, shall have the right to obtain corresponding remuneration.  
The administrator's remuneration shall be determined by the People's Court that hears the enterprise bankruptcy case pursuant to these Provisions.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  人民法院应根据债务人最终清偿的财产价值总额，在以下比例限制范围内分段确定管理人报酬：  
（一）不超过一百万元（含本数，下同）的，在12%以下确定；  
（二）超过一百万元至五百万元的部分，在10%以下确定；  
（三）超过五百万元至一千万元的部分，在8%以下确定；  
（四）超过一千万元至五千万元的部分，在6%以下确定;  
（五）超过五千万元至一亿元的部分，在3%以下确定；  
（六）超过一亿元至五亿元的部分，在1%以下确定；  
（七）超过五亿元的部分，在0.5%以下确定。  
担保权人优先受偿的担保物价值，不计入前款规定的财产价值总额。  
高级人民法院认为有必要的，可以参照上述比例在30%的浮动范围内制定符合当地实际情况的管理人报酬比例限制范围，并通过当地有影响的媒体公告，同时报最高人民法院备案。

**Article 2** . The People's Court shall determine the administrator's remuneration in segments based on the total value of the debtor's property ultimately distributed for payment of claims, within the following percentage limits:  
(1) For amounts not exceeding RMB 1,000,000 (including such amount, the same below), the remuneration shall be determined at not more than 12%;  
(2) For the portion exceeding RMB 1,000,000 up to RMB 5,000,000, at not more than 10%;  
(3) For the portion exceeding RMB 5,000,000 up to RMB 10,000,000, at not more than 8%;  
(4) For the portion exceeding RMB 10,000,000 up to RMB 50,000,000, at not more than 6%;  
(5) For the portion exceeding RMB 50,000,000 up to RMB 100,000,000, at not more than 3%;  
(6) For the portion exceeding RMB 100,000,000 up to RMB 500,000,000, at not more than 1%;  
(7) For the portion exceeding RMB 500,000,000, at not more than 0.5%.  
The value of the secured property subject to the priority right of a secured creditor shall not be included in the total value of property as specified in the preceding paragraph.  
If the Higher People's Court deems it necessary, it may, with reference to the above percentages and within a 30% fluctuation range, formulate a limit range for the administrator's remuneration percentage that corresponds to local conditions, and shall announce it through local influential media, and simultaneously report it to the Supreme People's Court for recordation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  人民法院可以根据破产案件的实际情况，确定管理人分期或者最后一次性收取报酬。

**Article 3** . The People's Court may, based on the actual circumstances of the bankruptcy case, determine that the administrator shall collect the remuneration in installments or in a lump sum at the conclusion.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  人民法院受理企业破产申请后，应当对债务人可供清偿的财产价值和管理人的工作量作出预测，初步确定管理人报酬方案。管理人报酬方案应当包括管理人报酬比例和收取时间。

**Article 4** . After accepting an application for enterprise bankruptcy, the People's Court shall make a forecast of the value of the debtor's property available for payment of claims and the workload of the administrator, and shall preliminarily determine the plan for the administrator's remuneration. The plan for the administrator's remuneration shall include the percentage of the administrator's remuneration and the time of collection.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  人民法院采取公开竞争方式指定管理人的，可以根据社会中介机构提出的报价确定管理人报酬方案，但报酬比例不得超出[本规定第二条](#art-2)规定的限制范围。  
上述报酬方案一般不予调整，但债权人会议异议成立的除外。

**Article 5** . Where the People's Court designates the administrator through open competition, it may determine the plan for the administrator's remuneration based on the quotation submitted by the social intermediary institution, provided that the percentage of remuneration shall not exceed the limits specified in Article 2 of these Provisions.  
The remuneration plan mentioned above shall generally not be adjusted, except where the objection raised by the creditors' meeting is sustained.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  人民法院应当自确定管理人报酬方案之日起三日内，书面通知管理人。  
管理人应当在第一次债权人会议上报告管理人报酬方案内容。

**Article 6** . The People's Court shall, within three days from the date of determining the plan for the administrator's remuneration, notify the administrator in writing.  
The administrator shall report the content of the plan for the administrator's remuneration at the first creditors' meeting.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  管理人、债权人会议对管理人报酬方案有意见的，可以进行协商。双方就调整管理人报酬方案内容协商一致的，管理人应向人民法院书面提出具体的请求和理由，并附相应的债权人会议决议。  
人民法院经审查认为上述请求和理由不违反法律和行政法规强制性规定，且不损害他人合法权益的，应当按照双方协商的结果调整管理人报酬方案。

**Article 7** . If the administrator or the creditors' meeting has opinions on the plan for the administrator's remuneration, they may conduct consultations. If the parties reach a consensus on the adjustment of the content of the plan for the administrator's remuneration, the administrator shall submit a written request with specific grounds and reasons to the People's Court, together with the corresponding resolution of the creditors' meeting.  
If the People's Court, upon review, finds that the said request and reasons do not violate mandatory provisions of laws and administrative regulations and do not harm the lawful rights and interests of other persons, it shall adjust the plan for the administrator's remuneration in accordance with the result of the consultation between the parties.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  人民法院确定管理人报酬方案后，可以根据破产案件和管理人履行职责的实际情况进行调整。  
人民法院应当自调整管理人报酬方案之日起三日内，书面通知管理人。管理人应当自收到上述通知之日起三日内，向债权人委员会或者债权人会议主席报告管理人报酬方案调整内容。

**Article 8** . After determining the plan for the administrator's remuneration, the People's Court may adjust it based on the actual circumstances of the bankruptcy case and the performance of the administrator's duties.  
The People's Court shall, within three days from the date of adjusting the plan for the administrator's remuneration, notify the administrator in writing. The administrator shall, within three days from the date of receiving the said notice, report the adjusted content of the plan for the administrator's remuneration to the creditors' committee or the chairperson of the creditors' meeting.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  人民法院确定或者调整管理人报酬方案时，应当考虑以下因素：  
（一）破产案件的复杂性；  
（二）管理人的勤勉程度；  
（三）管理人为重整、和解工作做出的实际贡献；  
（四）管理人承担的风险和责任；  
（五）债务人住所地居民可支配收入及物价水平；  
（六）其他影响管理人报酬的情况。

**Article 9** . When determining or adjusting the plan for the administrator's remuneration, the People's Court shall consider the following factors:  
(1) The complexity of the bankruptcy case;  
(2) The diligence of the administrator;  
(3) The actual contributions made by the administrator to the reorganization and composition work;  
(4) The risks and liabilities undertaken by the administrator;  
(5) The disposable income of residents and the price level in the place of the debtor's domicile;  
(6) Other circumstances affecting the administrator's remuneration.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  最终确定的管理人报酬及收取情况，应列入破产财产分配方案。在和解、重整程序中，管理人报酬方案内容应列入和解协议草案或重整计划草案。

**Article 10** . The finally determined administrator's remuneration and the circumstances of its collection shall be included in the plan for distribution of bankruptcy property. In composition or reorganization proceedings, the content of the plan for the administrator's remuneration shall be included in the draft composition agreement or the draft reorganization plan.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  管理人收取报酬，应当向人民法院提出书面申请。申请书应当包括以下内容：  
（一）可供支付报酬的债务人财产情况；  
（二）申请收取报酬的时间和数额；  
（三）管理人履行职责的情况。  
人民法院应当自收到上述申请书之日起十日内，确定支付管理人的报酬数额。

**Article 11** . To collect the remuneration, the administrator shall submit a written application to the People's Court. The application shall include the following:  
(1) The status of the debtor's property available for payment of the remuneration;  
(2) The time and amount of the remuneration applied for;  
(3) The circumstances of the administrator's performance of duties.  
The People's Court shall, within ten days from the date of receiving the said application, determine the amount of remuneration to be paid to the administrator.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  管理人报酬从债务人财产中优先支付。  
债务人财产不足以支付管理人报酬和管理人执行职务费用的，管理人应当提请人民法院终结破产程序。但债权人、管理人、债务人的出资人或者其他利害关系人愿意垫付上述报酬和费用的，破产程序可以继续进行。  
上述垫付款项作为破产费用从债务人财产中向垫付人随时清偿。

**Article 12** . The administrator's remuneration shall be paid in priority from the debtor's property.  
If the debtor's property is insufficient to pay the administrator's remuneration and the expenses incurred by the administrator in performing its duties, the administrator shall request the People's Court to terminate the bankruptcy proceedings. However, if creditors, the administrator, the debtor's contributors, or other interested parties are willing to advance the said remuneration and expenses, the bankruptcy proceedings may continue.  
The advanced amount mentioned above shall, as a bankruptcy expense, be repaid to the person who advanced it at any time from the debtor's property.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  管理人对担保物的维护、变现、交付等管理工作付出合理劳动的，有权向担保权人收取适当的报酬。管理人与担保权人就上述报酬数额不能协商一致的，人民法院应当参照[本规定第二条](#art-2)规定的方法确定，但报酬比例不得超出该条规定限制范围的10%。

**Article 13** . If the administrator has performed reasonable work in the administration of the security property, including its maintenance, realization, and delivery, it shall have the right to collect appropriate remuneration from the secured creditor. If the administrator and the secured creditor fail to reach a consensus on the amount of the said remuneration, the People's Court shall determine it by reference to the method specified in Article 2 of these Provisions, provided that the percentage of remuneration shall not exceed 10% of the limit range specified in that Article.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  律师事务所、会计师事务所通过聘请本专业的其他社会中介机构或者人员协助履行管理人职责的，所需费用从其报酬中支付。  
破产清算事务所通过聘请其他社会中介机构或者人员协助履行管理人职责的，所需费用从其报酬中支付。

**Article 14** . Where a law firm or an accounting firm engages other social intermediary institutions or personnel in its own profession to assist in performing the duties of the administrator, the expenses required shall be paid from its remuneration.  
Where a bankruptcy liquidation firm engages other social intermediary institutions or personnel to assist in performing the duties of the administrator, the expenses required shall be paid from its remuneration.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  清算组中有关政府部门派出的工作人员参与工作的不收取报酬。其他机构或人员的报酬根据其履行职责的情况确定。

**Article 15** . Where staff members dispatched by relevant government departments in the liquidation team participate in the work, they shall not collect any remuneration. The remuneration of other institutions or personnel shall be determined based on the circumstances of their performance of duties.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  管理人发生更换的，人民法院应当分别确定更换前后的管理人报酬。其报酬比例总和不得超出[本规定第二条](#art-2)规定的限制范围。

**Article 16** . Where the administrator is replaced, the People's Court shall determine the remuneration of the administrator before and after the replacement separately. The sum of the percentages of their remuneration shall not exceed the limits specified in Article 2 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  债权人会议对管理人报酬有异议的，应当向人民法院书面提出具体的请求和理由。异议书应当附有相应的债权人会议决议。

**Article 17** . If the creditors' meeting has an objection to the administrator's remuneration, it shall submit a written request with specific grounds and reasons to the People's Court. The written objection shall be accompanied by the corresponding resolution of the creditors' meeting.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  人民法院应当自收到债权人会议异议书之日起三日内通知管理人。管理人应当自收到通知之日起三日内作出书面说明。  
人民法院认为有必要的，可以举行听证会，听取当事人意见。  
人民法院应当自收到债权人会议异议书之日起十日内，就是否调整管理人报酬问题书面通知管理人、债权人委员会或者债权人会议主席。

**Article 18** . The People's Court shall, within three days from the date of receiving the written objection from the creditors' meeting, notify the administrator. The administrator shall, within three days from the date of receiving the notice, make a written explanation.  
If the People's Court deems it necessary, it may hold a hearing to hear the opinions of the parties.  
The People's Court shall, within ten days from the date of receiving the written objection from the creditors' meeting, notify in writing the administrator, the creditors' committee, or the chairperson of the creditors' meeting regarding whether to adjust the administrator's remuneration.
