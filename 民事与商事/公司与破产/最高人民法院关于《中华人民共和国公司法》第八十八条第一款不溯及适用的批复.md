关于[《中华人民共和国公司法》第八十八条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-88)第一款  
  
不溯及适用的批复  
  
法释〔2024〕15号  
  
河南省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于公司法第八十八条第一款是否溯及适用的请示》收悉。经研究，批复如下：  
  
2024年7月1日起施行的[《中华人民共和国公司法》第八十八条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-88)第一款仅适用于2024年7月1日之后发生的未届出资期限的股权转让行为。对于2024年7月1日之前股东未届出资期限转让股权引发的出资责任纠纷，人民法院应当根据原公司法等有关法律的规定精神公平公正处理。  
  
本批复公布施行后，最高人民法院以前发布的司法解释与本批复规定不一致的，不再适用。

**Article 1** December 24, 2024  
Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Non-retroactive Application of Paragraph 1 of Article 88 of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China  
Fa Shi [2024] No. 15  
Higher People's Court of Henan Province:  
We have received your Request on Whether Paragraph 1 of Article 88 of the Company Law shall Apply Retroactively. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Paragraph 1 of Article 88 of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China, which came into force on July 1, 2024, shall only apply to transfers of equity interests where the capital contribution period has not expired that occurred after July 1, 2024. For disputes over capital contribution liability arising from equity transfers before July 1, 2024 where the shareholder's capital contribution period has not expired, the People's Court shall handle them fairly and justly in accordance with the spirit of the provisions of the original Company Law and other relevant laws.  
After this Reply is promulgated and comes into force, if any judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with the provisions of this Reply, it shall no longer apply.
