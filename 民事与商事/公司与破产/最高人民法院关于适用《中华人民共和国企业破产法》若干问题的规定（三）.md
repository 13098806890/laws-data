# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国企业破产法》若干问题的规定（三）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2019〕3号  
**公布日期**：2019-03-27  
**生效日期**：2019-03-28

> 《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国企业破产法〉若干问题的规定（三）》已于2019年2月25日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1762次会议通过，现予公布，自2019年3月28日起施行。 2019年3月27日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国企业破产法》 若干问题的规定（三）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  人民法院裁定受理破产申请的，此前债务人尚未支付的公司强制清算费用、未终结的执行程序中产生的评估费、公告费、保管费等执行费用，可以参照企业破产法关于破产费用的规定，由债务人财产随时清偿。  
此前债务人尚未支付的案件受理费、执行申请费，可以作为破产债权清偿。

**Article 1** Where the People's Court rules to accept a bankruptcy application, the company compulsory liquidation expenses that the debtor has not yet paid, and enforcement expenses such as appraisal fees, announcement fees, and custody fees incurred in the uncompleted enforcement proceedings, may be paid at any time from the debtor's property by reference to the provisions of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law on bankruptcy expenses.  
The case acceptance fees and enforcement application fees that the debtor has not yet paid may be satisfied as bankruptcy claims.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  破产申请受理后，经债权人会议决议通过，或者第一次债权人会议召开前经人民法院许可，管理人或者自行管理的债务人可以为债务人继续营业而借款。提供借款的债权人主张参照[企业破产法第四十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-42)第四项的规定优先于普通破产债权清偿的，人民法院应予支持，但其主张优先于此前已就债务人特定财产享有担保的债权清偿的，人民法院不予支持。  
管理人或者自行管理的债务人可以为前述借款设定抵押担保，抵押物在破产申请受理前已为其他债权人设定抵押的，债权人主张按照物权法第一百九十九条规定的顺序清偿，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 2** After a bankruptcy application is accepted, with a resolution of the creditors' meeting or, before the first creditors' meeting is convened, with the permission of the People's Court, the administrator or the debtor under self-administration may borrow funds for the debtor's continued business operations. Where a creditor that provides the loan claims priority over common bankruptcy claims in satisfaction by reference to Article 42(4) of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the People's Court shall support such claim; however, where such creditor claims priority over claims that have already been secured by the debtor's specific property, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
The administrator or the debtor under self-administration may create a mortgage or pledge as security for the aforementioned loan. If the collateral has already been mortgaged to another creditor before the bankruptcy application is accepted, and a creditor claims satisfaction in the order specified in Article 199 of the Property Law, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  破产申请受理后，债务人欠缴款项产生的滞纳金，包括债务人未履行生效法律文书应当加倍支付的迟延利息和劳动保险金的滞纳金，债权人作为破产债权申报的，人民法院不予确认。

**Article 3** After a bankruptcy application is accepted, with respect to overdue fines arising from the debtor's overdue payments, including the double interest for delayed performance payable due to the debtor's failure to perform an effective legal instrument and overdue fines for labor insurance premiums, if a creditor reports such as bankruptcy claims, the People's Court shall not confirm them.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  保证人被裁定进入破产程序的，债权人有权申报其对保证人的保证债权。  
主债务未到期的，保证债权在保证人破产申请受理时视为到期。一般保证的保证人主张行使先诉抗辩权的，人民法院不予支持，但债权人在一般保证人破产程序中的分配额应予提存，待一般保证人应承担的保证责任确定后再按照破产清偿比例予以分配。  
保证人被确定应当承担保证责任的，保证人的管理人可以就保证人实际承担的清偿额向主债务人或其他债务人行使求偿权。

**Article 4** Where a surety is ruled to enter bankruptcy proceedings, a creditor has the right to report its guarantee claim against the surety.  
If the principal debt is not yet due, the guarantee claim shall be deemed due at the time of acceptance of the surety's bankruptcy application. Where a surety of general suretyship claims to exercise the right of preliminary objection, the People's Court shall not support such claim; however, the creditor's distributive share in the bankruptcy proceedings of the general surety shall be escrowed and shall be distributed according to the bankruptcy satisfaction ratio after the surety liability that the general surety shall bear is determined.  
Where a surety is determined to bear surety liability, the administrator of the surety may exercise the right of recourse against the principal debtor or other debtors for the amount actually satisfied by the surety.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  债务人、保证人均被裁定进入破产程序的，债权人有权向债务人、保证人分别申报债权。  
债权人向债务人、保证人均申报全部债权的，从一方破产程序中获得清偿后，其对另一方的债权额不作调整，但债权人的受偿额不得超出其债权总额。保证人履行保证责任后不再享有求偿权。

**Article 5** Where both the debtor and the surety are ruled to enter bankruptcy proceedings, a creditor has the right to report claims separately against the debtor and the surety.  
If a creditor reports the full amount of claims against both the debtor and the surety, after obtaining satisfaction from one party's bankruptcy proceedings, the amount of its claim against the other party shall not be adjusted; however, the creditor's total satisfaction shall not exceed the total amount of its claim. After the surety performs its suretyship liability, it shall no longer enjoy the right of recourse.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  管理人应当依照[企业破产法第五十七条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-57)的规定对所申报的债权进行登记造册，详尽记载申报人的姓名、单位、代理人、申报债权额、担保情况、证据、联系方式等事项，形成债权申报登记册。  
管理人应当依照[企业破产法第五十七条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-57)的规定对债权的性质、数额、担保财产、是否超过诉讼时效期间、是否超过强制执行期间等情况进行审查、编制债权表并提交债权人会议核查。  
债权表、债权申报登记册及债权申报材料在破产期间由管理人保管，债权人、债务人、债务人职工及其他利害关系人有权查阅。

**Article 6** The administrator shall, in accordance with Article 57 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, register and record the reported claims in detail, including the claimant's name, unit, agent, reported claim amount, security status, evidence, contact information, and other matters, to form a register of reported claims.  
The administrator shall, in accordance with Article 57 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, examine the nature, amount, secured property, whether the limitation of action has expired, whether the period for compulsory enforcement has expired, and other circumstances of the claims, prepare a schedule of claims, and submit it to the creditors' meeting for verification.  
The schedule of claims, the register of reported claims, and the materials for reporting claims shall be kept by the administrator during the bankruptcy period. Creditors, the debtor, employees of the debtor, and other interested parties have the right to inspect them.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  已经生效法律文书确定的债权，管理人应当予以确认。  
管理人认为债权人据以申报债权的生效法律文书确定的债权错误，或者有证据证明债权人与债务人恶意通过诉讼、仲裁或者公证机关赋予强制执行力公证文书的形式虚构债权债务的，应当依法通过审判监督程序向作出该判决、裁定、调解书的人民法院或者上一级人民法院申请撤销生效法律文书，或者向受理破产申请的人民法院申请撤销或者不予执行仲裁裁决、不予执行公证债权文书后，重新确定债权。

**Article 7** Where a claim has been determined by an effective legal instrument, the administrator shall confirm it.  
If the administrator believes that the claim determined by the effective legal instrument on the basis of which the creditor reports the claim is erroneous, or there is evidence that the creditor and the debtor have fraudulently created a claim and debt through litigation, arbitration, or a notarized document endowed with compulsory enforcement effect by a notarial office, the administrator shall, according to law, apply through the trial supervision procedure to the People's Court that rendered the judgment, ruling, or mediation statement or to the People's Court at the next higher level for revocation of the effective legal instrument, or apply to the People's Court that accepted the bankruptcy application for revocation or non-enforcement of the arbitration award or non-enforcement of the notarized claim document, and then redetermine the claim.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  债务人、债权人对债权表记载的债权有异议的，应当说明理由和法律依据。经管理人解释或调整后，异议人仍然不服的，或者管理人不予解释或调整的，异议人应当在债权人会议核查结束后十五日内向人民法院提起债权确认的诉讼。当事人之间在破产申请受理前订立有仲裁条款或仲裁协议的，应当向选定的仲裁机构申请确认债权债务关系。

**Article 8** Where the debtor or a creditor has an objection to a claim recorded in the schedule of claims, it shall state the reasons and legal basis. If after the administrator's explanation or adjustment the objector still disagrees, or if the administrator does not provide an explanation or adjustment, the objector shall, within fifteen days after the conclusion of the verification by the creditors' meeting, file a lawsuit with the People's Court for confirmation of the claim. If an arbitration clause or arbitration agreement was concluded between the parties before the bankruptcy application was accepted, the objector shall apply to the selected arbitration institution for confirmation of the claim and debt relationship.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  债务人对债权表记载的债权有异议向人民法院提起诉讼的，应将被异议债权人列为被告。债权人对债权表记载的他人债权有异议的，应将被异议债权人列为被告；债权人对债权表记载的本人债权有异议的，应将债务人列为被告。  
对同一笔债权存在多个异议人，其他异议人申请参加诉讼的，应当列为共同原告。

**Article 9** If the debtor files a lawsuit with the People's Court against a claim recorded in the schedule of claims, it shall name the objected creditor as the defendant. If a creditor objects to another person's claim recorded in the schedule of claims, it shall name the objected creditor as the defendant; if a creditor objects to its own claim recorded in the schedule of claims, it shall name the debtor as the defendant.  
Where there are multiple objectors to the same claim, and other objectors apply to participate in the lawsuit, they shall be listed as co-plaintiffs.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  单个债权人有权查阅债务人财产状况报告、债权人会议决议、债权人委员会决议、管理人监督报告等参与破产程序所必需的债务人财务和经营信息资料。管理人无正当理由不予提供的，债权人可以请求人民法院作出决定；人民法院应当在五日内作出决定。  
上述信息资料涉及商业秘密的，债权人应当依法承担保密义务或者签署保密协议；涉及国家秘密的应当依照相关法律规定处理。

**Article 10** A single creditor has the right to inspect the debtor's property status report, resolutions of the creditors' meeting, resolutions of the creditors' committee, the administrator's supervision report, and other financial and operational information of the debtor necessary for participating in the bankruptcy proceedings. If the administrator without justifiable reasons refuses to provide such information, the creditor may request the People's Court to make a decision; the People's Court shall make a decision within five days.  
If the above information involves trade secrets, the creditor shall bear confidentiality obligations according to law or sign a confidentiality agreement; if it involves state secrets, it shall be handled in accordance with relevant legal provisions.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  债权人会议的决议除现场表决外，可以由管理人事先将相关决议事项告知债权人，采取通信、网络投票等非现场方式进行表决。采取非现场方式进行表决的，管理人应当在债权人会议召开后的三日内，以信函、电子邮件、公告等方式将表决结果告知参与表决的债权人。  
根据[企业破产法第八十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-82)规定，对重整计划草案进行分组表决时，权益因重整计划草案受到调整或者影响的债权人或者股东，有权参加表决；权益未受到调整或者影响的债权人或者股东，参照[企业破产法第八十三条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-83)的规定，不参加重整计划草案的表决。

**Article 11** Resolutions of the creditors' meeting may, in addition to on-site voting, be adopted by non-on-site methods such as correspondence or online voting after the administrator informs the creditors of the relevant resolution matters in advance. Where non-on-site voting is adopted, the administrator shall, within three days after the creditors' meeting is convened, inform the creditors who participated in the voting of the voting results by letter, email, announcement, or other means.  
In accordance with Article 82 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, when voting on a draft reorganization plan by groups, creditors or shareholders whose rights and interests are adjusted or affected by the draft reorganization plan have the right to participate in the voting; creditors or shareholders whose rights and interests are not adjusted or affected, by reference to Article 83 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, shall not participate in the voting on the draft reorganization plan.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  债权人会议的决议具有以下情形之一，损害债权人利益，债权人申请撤销的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）债权人会议的召开违反法定程序；  
（二）债权人会议的表决违反法定程序；  
（三）债权人会议的决议内容违法；  
（四）债权人会议的决议超出债权人会议的职权范围。  
人民法院可以裁定撤销全部或者部分事项决议，责令债权人会议依法重新作出决议。  
债权人申请撤销债权人会议决议的，应当提出书面申请。债权人会议采取通信、网络投票等非现场方式进行表决的，债权人申请撤销的期限自债权人收到通知之日起算。

**Article 12** If a resolution of the creditors' meeting falls under any of the following circumstances and harms the interests of creditors, and a creditor applies for revocation, the People's Court shall support such application:  
(1) The convening of the creditors' meeting violates statutory procedures;  
(2) The voting at the creditors' meeting violates statutory procedures;  
(3) The content of the resolution of the creditors' meeting is illegal;  
(4) The resolution of the creditors' meeting exceeds the scope of authority of the creditors' meeting.  
The People's Court may rule to revoke all or part of the resolution and order the creditors' meeting to make a new resolution according to law.  
A creditor applying for revocation of a resolution of the creditors' meeting shall submit a written application. Where the creditors' meeting adopts non-on-site voting methods such as correspondence or online voting, the time limit for the creditor to apply for revocation shall be calculated from the date the creditor receives the notice.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  债权人会议可以依照[企业破产法第六十八条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-68)第一款第四项的规定，委托债权人委员会行使[企业破产法第六十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-61)第一款第二、三、五项规定的债权人会议职权。债权人会议不得作出概括性授权，委托其行使债权人会议所有职权。

**Article 13** The creditors' meeting may, in accordance with Article 68(1)(4) of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, entrust the creditors' committee to exercise the functions and powers of the creditors' meeting as specified in Article 61(1)(2), (3), and (5) of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law. The creditors' meeting shall not grant a blanket authorization entrusting the creditors' committee to exercise all functions and powers of the creditors' meeting.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  债权人委员会决定所议事项应获得全体成员过半数通过，并作成议事记录。债权人委员会成员对所议事项的决议有不同意见的，应当在记录中载明。  
债权人委员会行使职权应当接受债权人会议的监督，以适当的方式向债权人会议及时汇报工作，并接受人民法院的指导。

**Article 14** A decision of the creditors' committee on a matter under discussion shall be passed by more than half of all its members and shall be recorded in the minutes of the meeting. If a member of the creditors' committee has a dissenting opinion on the resolution of the matter under discussion, such opinion shall be recorded in the minutes.  
When exercising its functions and powers, the creditors' committee shall accept the supervision of the creditors' meeting, report its work to the creditors' meeting in a timely manner by appropriate means, and accept the guidance of the People's Court.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  管理人处分[企业破产法第六十九条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-69)规定的债务人重大财产的，应当事先制作财产管理或者变价方案并提交债权人会议进行表决，债权人会议表决未通过的，管理人不得处分。  
管理人实施处分前，应当根据[企业破产法第六十九条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-69)的规定，提前十日书面报告债权人委员会或者人民法院。债权人委员会可以依照[企业破产法第六十八条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-68)第二款的规定，要求管理人对处分行为作出相应说明或者提供有关文件依据。  
债权人委员会认为管理人实施的处分行为不符合债权人会议通过的财产管理或变价方案的，有权要求管理人纠正。管理人拒绝纠正的，债权人委员会可以请求人民法院作出决定。  
人民法院认为管理人实施的处分行为不符合债权人会议通过的财产管理或变价方案的，应当责令管理人停止处分行为。管理人应当予以纠正，或者提交债权人会议重新表决通过后实施。

**Article 15** Where the administrator disposes of the debtor's significant property as specified in Article 69 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, it shall first formulate a property management or realization plan and submit it to the creditors' meeting for voting. If the creditors' meeting does not pass the plan, the administrator shall not make the disposition.  
Before implementing the disposition, the administrator shall, in accordance with Article 69 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, report in writing to the creditors' committee or the People's Court ten days in advance. The creditors' committee may, in accordance with Article 68(2) of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, require the administrator to provide corresponding explanations or relevant documents as the basis for the disposition act.  
If the creditors' committee considers that the disposition act implemented by the administrator does not conform to the property management or realization plan approved by the creditors' meeting, it has the right to require the administrator to rectify. If the administrator refuses to rectify, the creditors' committee may request the People's Court to make a decision.  
If the People's Court considers that the disposition act implemented by the administrator does not conform to the property management or realization plan approved by the creditors' meeting, it shall order the administrator to cease the disposition act. The administrator shall make corrections or submit the matter to the creditors' meeting for a new vote and implement it thereafter.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  本规定自2019年3月28日起实施。  
实施前本院发布的有关企业破产的司法解释，与本规定相抵触的，自本规定实施之日起不再适用。

**Article 16** These Provisions shall take effect as of March 28, 2019.  
Any judicial interpretation on enterprise bankruptcy issued by this Court before the implementation of these Provisions that is inconsistent with these Provisions shall cease to apply as of the date of implementation of these Provisions.
