# 中华人民共和国合伙企业法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2006-08-27  
**生效日期**：2007-06-01

> （1997年2月23日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十四次会议通过　2006年8月27日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十三次会议修订） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　普通合伙企业 第一节　合伙企业设立 第二节　合伙企业财产 第三节　合伙事务执行 第四节　合伙企业与第三人关系 第五节　入伙、退伙 第六节　特殊的普通合伙企业 第三章　有限合伙企业 第四章　合伙企业解散、清算 第五章　法律责任 第六章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范合伙企业的行为，保护合伙企业及其合伙人、债权人的合法权益，维护社会经济秩序，促进社会主义市场经济的发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purposes of regulating the acts of partnership enterprises, protecting the lawful rights and interests of partnership enterprises and their partners and creditors, maintaining the social and economic order, and promoting the development of the socialist market economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称合伙企业，是指自然人、法人和其他组织依照本法在中国境内设立的普通合伙企业和有限合伙企业。  
普通合伙企业由普通合伙人组成，合伙人对合伙企业债务承担无限连带责任。本法对普通合伙人承担责任的形式有特别规定的，从其规定。  
有限合伙企业由普通合伙人和有限合伙人组成，普通合伙人对合伙企业债务承担无限连带责任，有限合伙人以其认缴的出资额为限对合伙企业债务承担责任。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "partnership enterprise" means a general partnership enterprise and a limited partnership enterprise established within the territory of China by natural persons, legal persons and other organizations in accordance with this Law.  
A general partnership enterprise shall be composed of general partners, and the partners shall bear unlimited joint and several liability for the debts of the partnership enterprise. Where this Law has special provisions on the form of liability undertaken by general partners, such provisions shall prevail.  
A limited partnership enterprise shall be composed of general partners and limited partners, where the general partners shall bear unlimited joint and several liability for the debts of the partnership enterprise, and the limited partners shall bear liability for the debts of the partnership enterprise to the extent of their subscribed capital contributions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国有独资公司、国有企业、上市公司以及公益性的事业单位、社会团体不得成为普通合伙人。

**Article 3** Wholly state-owned companies, state-owned enterprises, listed companies, and public welfare institutions and social organizations shall not become general partners.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　合伙协议依法由全体合伙人协商一致、以书面形式订立。

**Article 4** A partnership agreement shall be concluded in writing upon unanimous agreement through consultation among all partners according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　订立合伙协议、设立合伙企业，应当遵循自愿、平等、公平、诚实信用原则。

**Article 5** When concluding a partnership agreement and establishing a partnership enterprise, the principles of voluntariness, equality, fairness, and good faith shall be followed.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　合伙企业的生产经营所得和其他所得，按照国家有关税收规定，由合伙人分别缴纳所得税。

**Article 6** The income from production and operation and other income of a partnership enterprise shall be subject to income tax paid separately by the partners in accordance with the relevant taxation provisions of the State.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　合伙企业及其合伙人必须遵守法律、行政法规，遵守社会公德、商业道德，承担社会责任。

**Article 7** A partnership enterprise and its partners must comply with laws and administrative regulations, observe social ethics and business ethics, and bear social responsibility.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　合伙企业及其合伙人的合法财产及其权益受法律保护。

**Article 8** The lawful property and rights and interests of a partnership enterprise and its partners shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请设立合伙企业，应当向企业登记机关提交登记申请书、合伙协议书、合伙人身份证明等文件。  
合伙企业的经营范围中有属于法律、行政法规规定在登记前须经批准的项目的，该项经营业务应当依法经过批准，并在登记时提交批准文件。

**Article 9** To apply for the establishment of a partnership enterprise, an application for registration, a partnership agreement, identity documents of the partners, and other documents shall be submitted to the enterprise registration authority.  
Where the business scope of the partnership enterprise covers items that require approval before registration as provided for by laws or administrative regulations, such business operations shall be approved according to law, and the approval documents shall be submitted at the time of registration.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　申请人提交的登记申请材料齐全、符合法定形式，企业登记机关能够当场登记的，应予当场登记，发给营业执照。  
除前款规定情形外，企业登记机关应当自受理申请之日起二十日内，作出是否登记的决定。予以登记的，发给营业执照；不予登记的，应当给予书面答复，并说明理由。

**Article 10** If the registration application materials submitted by the applicant are complete and conform to the statutory form, and the enterprise registration authority can register on the spot, it shall register on the spot and issue a business license.  
Except for the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, the enterprise registration authority shall, within twenty days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision on whether to register. If it decides to register, it shall issue a business license; if it decides not to register, it shall give a written reply and state the reasons.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　合伙企业的营业执照签发日期，为合伙企业成立日期。  
合伙企业领取营业执照前，合伙人不得以合伙企业名义从事合伙业务。

**Article 11** The date of issuance of the business license of a partnership enterprise shall be the date of establishment of the partnership enterprise.  
Before the partnership enterprise obtains the business license, the partners shall not engage in partnership business in the name of the partnership enterprise.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　合伙企业设立分支机构，应当向分支机构所在地的企业登记机关申请登记，领取营业执照。

**Article 12** Where a partnership enterprise establishes a branch, it shall apply for registration with the enterprise registration authority at the location of the branch and obtain a business license.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　合伙企业登记事项发生变更的，执行合伙事务的合伙人应当自作出变更决定或者发生变更事由之日起十五日内，向企业登记机关申请办理变更登记。

**Article 13** Where the registered items of a partnership enterprise change, the partner(s) executing partnership affairs shall, within fifteen days from the date of making the decision to change or the occurrence of the change event, apply to the enterprise registration authority for registration of the change.

### 第二章　普通合伙企业

#### 第一节　合伙企业设立

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　设立合伙企业，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有二个以上合伙人。合伙人为自然人的，应当具有完全民事行为能力；  
（二）有书面合伙协议；  
（三）有合伙人认缴或者实际缴付的出资；  
（四）有合伙企业的名称和生产经营场所；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 14** To establish a partnership enterprise, the following conditions shall be fulfilled:  
(1) There shall be two or more partners. If a partner is a natural person, he or she shall have full civil capacity;  
(2) There shall be a written partnership agreement;  
(3) There shall be capital contributions subscribed or actually paid by the partners;  
(4) There shall be a name and a place of business for the partnership enterprise; and  
(5) Other conditions as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　合伙企业名称中应当标明“普通合伙”字样。

**Article 15** The name of a partnership enterprise shall indicate the words "general partnership".

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　合伙人可以用货币、实物、知识产权、土地使用权或者其他财产权利出资，也可以用劳务出资。  
合伙人以实物、知识产权、土地使用权或者其他财产权利出资，需要评估作价的，可以由全体合伙人协商确定，也可以由全体合伙人委托法定评估机构评估。  
合伙人以劳务出资的，其评估办法由全体合伙人协商确定，并在合伙协议中载明。

**Article 16** A partner may make capital contributions in currency, in kind, intellectual property, land use rights, or other property rights, and may also contribute with labor services.  
If a partner contributes in kind, intellectual property, land use rights, or other property rights and such contribution requires valuation, the valuation may be determined through negotiation by all the partners or may be appraised by a statutory appraisal institution entrusted by all the partners.  
If a partner contributes with labor services, the valuation method thereof shall be determined through negotiation by all the partners and shall be specified in the partnership agreement.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　合伙人应当按照合伙协议约定的出资方式、数额和缴付期限，履行出资义务。  
以非货币财产出资的，依照法律、行政法规的规定，需要办理财产权转移手续的，应当依法办理。

**Article 17** A partner shall fulfill the obligation of capital contribution in accordance with the method, amount, and time limit for payment as agreed in the partnership agreement.  
If the capital contribution is made with non-monetary property and, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, the formalities for the transfer of property rights shall be completed, such formalities shall be completed according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　合伙协议应当载明下列事项：  
（一）合伙企业的名称和主要经营场所的地点；  
（二）合伙目的和合伙经营范围；  
（三）合伙人的姓名或者名称、住所；  
（四）合伙人的出资方式、数额和缴付期限；  
（五）利润分配、亏损分担方式；  
（六）合伙事务的执行；  
（七）入伙与退伙；  
（八）争议解决办法；  
（九）合伙企业的解散与清算；  
（十）违约责任。

**Article 18** A partnership agreement shall specify the following matters:  
(1) The name of the partnership enterprise and the location of its principal place of business;  
(2) The purpose of the partnership and the scope of business;  
(3) The name or title and domicile of each partner;  
(4) The method, amount, and time limit for payment of capital contributions by the partners;  
(5) The method for profit distribution and loss sharing;  
(6) The execution of partnership affairs;  
(7) Admission and withdrawal of partners;  
(8) The method for dispute resolution;  
(9) The dissolution and liquidation of the partnership enterprise; and  
(10) Liability for breach of contract.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　合伙协议经全体合伙人签名、盖章后生效。合伙人按照合伙协议享有权利，履行义务。  
修改或者补充合伙协议，应当经全体合伙人一致同意；但是，合伙协议另有约定的除外。  
合伙协议未约定或者约定不明确的事项，由合伙人协商决定；协商不成的，依照本法和其他有关法律、行政法规的规定处理。

**Article 19** A partnership agreement shall take effect after being signed and sealed by all the partners. The partners shall enjoy the rights and perform the obligations under the partnership agreement.  
Any amendment to or supplementation of the partnership agreement shall be subject to the unanimous consent of all the partners, unless otherwise agreed in the partnership agreement.  
Matters not agreed upon or not clearly agreed upon in the partnership agreement shall be decided through negotiation among the partners; if negotiation fails, the matter shall be handled in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws and administrative regulations.

#### 第二节　合伙企业财产

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　合伙人的出资、以合伙企业名义取得的收益和依法取得的其他财产，均为合伙企业的财产。

**Article 20** A partner's capital contributions, the income acquired in the name of the partnership enterprise, and other property obtained according to law shall all be the property of the partnership enterprise.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　合伙人在合伙企业清算前，不得请求分割合伙企业的财产；但是，本法另有规定的除外。  
合伙人在合伙企业清算前私自转移或者处分合伙企业财产的，合伙企业不得以此对抗善意第三人。

**Article 21** Before the liquidation of a partnership enterprise, a partner shall not request the division of the property of the partnership enterprise; however, this shall not apply if this Law provides otherwise.  
If a partner privately transfers or disposes of the property of the partnership enterprise before the liquidation thereof, the partnership enterprise may not use such act to defend against a bona fide third party.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　除合伙协议另有约定外，合伙人向合伙人以外的人转让其在合伙企业中的全部或者部分财产份额时，须经其他合伙人一致同意。  
合伙人之间转让在合伙企业中的全部或者部分财产份额时，应当通知其他合伙人。

**Article 22** Unless otherwise agreed in the partnership agreement, when a partner transfers all or part of his or her property share in the partnership enterprise to a person other than a partner, the consent of all other partners shall be obtained.  
When a partner transfers all or part of his or her property share to another partner, he or she shall notify the other partners.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　合伙人向合伙人以外的人转让其在合伙企业中的财产份额的，在同等条件下，其他合伙人有优先购买权；但是，合伙协议另有约定的除外。

**Article 23** Where a partner transfers his or her property share in the partnership enterprise to a person other than a partner, the other partners shall have the preemptive right to purchase under the same conditions; however, this shall not apply if the partnership agreement provides otherwise.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　合伙人以外的人依法受让合伙人在合伙企业中的财产份额的，经修改合伙协议即成为合伙企业的合伙人，依照本法和修改后的合伙协议享有权利，履行义务。

**Article 24** Where a person other than a partner lawfully acquires a partner's property share in the partnership enterprise, such person shall, upon amendment of the partnership agreement, become a partner of the partnership enterprise and shall enjoy the rights and perform the obligations in accordance with this Law and the amended partnership agreement.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　合伙人以其在合伙企业中的财产份额出质的，须经其他合伙人一致同意；未经其他合伙人一致同意，其行为无效，由此给善意第三人造成损失的，由行为人依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 25** Where a partner pledges his or her property share in the partnership enterprise, the consent of all other partners shall be obtained; if such consent is not obtained, the act shall be null and void, and if losses are caused to a bona fide third party as a result, the actor shall bear compensation liability according to law.

#### 第三节　合伙事务执行

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　合伙人对执行合伙事务享有同等的权利。  
按照合伙协议的约定或者经全体合伙人决定，可以委托一个或者数个合伙人对外代表合伙企业，执行合伙事务。  
作为合伙人的法人、其他组织执行合伙事务的，由其委派的代表执行。

**Article 26** Partners shall have equal rights to execute partnership affairs.  
In accordance with the partnership agreement or by a decision of all the partners, one or more partners may be entrusted to represent the partnership enterprise externally and execute partnership affairs.  
If a legal person or other organization acting as a partner executes partnership affairs, such affairs shall be executed by the representative appointed by it.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　依照[本法第二十六条](#art-26)第二款规定委托一个或者数个合伙人执行合伙事务的，其他合伙人不再执行合伙事务。  
不执行合伙事务的合伙人有权监督执行事务合伙人执行合伙事务的情况。

**Article 27** If one or more partners are entrusted to execute partnership affairs pursuant to the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 26 of this Law, the other partners shall no longer execute partnership affairs.  
A partner who does not execute partnership affairs shall have the right to supervise the execution of partnership affairs by the executing partner(s).

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　由一个或者数个合伙人执行合伙事务的，执行事务合伙人应当定期向其他合伙人报告事务执行情况以及合伙企业的经营和财务状况，其执行合伙事务所产生的收益归合伙企业，所产生的费用和亏损由合伙企业承担。  
合伙人为了解合伙企业的经营状况和财务状况，有权查阅合伙企业会计账簿等财务资料。

**Article 28** Where one or more partners execute partnership affairs, the executing partner(s) shall periodically report to the other partners on the execution of affairs and on the operation and financial status of the partnership enterprise. The proceeds arising from the execution of partnership affairs shall belong to the partnership enterprise, and the expenses and losses incurred shall be borne by the partnership enterprise.  
For the purpose of understanding the operation and financial status of the partnership enterprise, a partner shall have the right to review the accounting books and other financial materials of the partnership enterprise.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　合伙人分别执行合伙事务的，执行事务合伙人可以对其他合伙人执行的事务提出异议。提出异议时，应当暂停该项事务的执行。如果发生争议，依照[本法第三十条](#art-30)规定作出决定。  
受委托执行合伙事务的合伙人不按照合伙协议或者全体合伙人的决定执行事务的，其他合伙人可以决定撤销该委托。

**Article 29** If partners execute partnership affairs separately, an executing partner may raise an objection to the affairs executed by another partner. When an objection is raised, the execution of the relevant affairs shall be suspended. If a dispute arises, a decision shall be made in accordance with the provisions of Article 30 of this Law.  
If a partner entrusted to execute partnership affairs fails to execute the affairs in accordance with the partnership agreement or the decision of all partners, the other partners may decide to revoke the entrustment.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　合伙人对合伙企业有关事项作出决议，按照合伙协议约定的表决办法办理。合伙协议未约定或者约定不明确的，实行合伙人一人一票并经全体合伙人过半数通过的表决办法。  
本法对合伙企业的表决办法另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 30** Where partners make a resolution on matters concerning the partnership enterprise, the voting method agreed in the partnership agreement shall be followed. If the partnership agreement does not specify a voting method or the specification is unclear, the voting method of one vote per partner with approval by more than half of all the partners shall be adopted.  
If this Law provides otherwise on the voting method for a partnership enterprise, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　除合伙协议另有约定外，合伙企业的下列事项应当经全体合伙人一致同意：  
（一）改变合伙企业的名称；  
（二）改变合伙企业的经营范围、主要经营场所的地点；  
（三）处分合伙企业的不动产；  
（四）转让或者处分合伙企业的知识产权和其他财产权利；  
（五）以合伙企业名义为他人提供担保；  
（六）聘任合伙人以外的人担任合伙企业的经营管理人员。

**Article 31** Unless otherwise agreed in the partnership agreement, the following matters of a partnership enterprise shall be subject to the unanimous consent of all the partners:  
(1) Changing the name of the partnership enterprise;  
(2) Changing the scope of business or the location of the principal place of business of the partnership enterprise;  
(3) Disposing of the immovable property of the partnership enterprise;  
(4) Transferring or disposing of the intellectual property and other property rights of the partnership enterprise;  
(5) Providing a guarantee for another person in the name of the partnership enterprise; and  
(6) Appointing a person other than a partner as the manager of the partnership enterprise.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　合伙人不得自营或者同他人合作经营与本合伙企业相竞争的业务。  
除合伙协议另有约定或者经全体合伙人一致同意外，合伙人不得同本合伙企业进行交易。  
合伙人不得从事损害本合伙企业利益的活动。

**Article 32** A partner shall not operate business competing with the partnership enterprise on his or her own or in cooperation with others.  
Unless otherwise agreed in the partnership agreement or unanimously consented to by all partners, a partner shall not enter into transactions with the partnership enterprise.  
A partner shall not engage in any activities that harm the interests of the partnership enterprise.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　合伙企业的利润分配、亏损分担，按照合伙协议的约定办理；合伙协议未约定或者约定不明确的，由合伙人协商决定；协商不成的，由合伙人按照实缴出资比例分配、分担；无法确定出资比例的，由合伙人平均分配、分担。  
合伙协议不得约定将全部利润分配给部分合伙人或者由部分合伙人承担全部亏损。

**Article 33** The distribution of profits and sharing of losses of a partnership enterprise shall be handled in accordance with the partnership agreement; if the partnership agreement does not provide therefor or the provisions are unclear, the matter shall be decided through negotiation among the partners; if negotiation fails, the profits shall be distributed and losses shall be shared among the partners in proportion to their actual capital contributions; if the proportion of capital contributions cannot be determined, the profits shall be distributed and losses shall be shared equally among the partners.  
The partnership agreement shall not stipulate that all profits shall be distributed to some partners or that all losses shall be borne by some partners.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　合伙人按照合伙协议的约定或者经全体合伙人决定，可以增加或者减少对合伙企业的出资。

**Article 34** A partner may increase or reduce its capital contribution to the partnership enterprise in accordance with the provisions of the partnership agreement or as decided by all partners.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　被聘任的合伙企业的经营管理人员应当在合伙企业授权范围内履行职务。  
被聘任的合伙企业的经营管理人员，超越合伙企业授权范围履行职务，或者在履行职务过程中因故意或者重大过失给合伙企业造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 35** The hired management personnel of a partnership enterprise shall perform their duties within the scope of authorization of the partnership enterprise.  
Where the hired management personnel of a partnership enterprise perform their duties beyond the scope of authorization of the partnership enterprise, or cause losses to the partnership enterprise due to intent or gross negligence in the course of performing duties, they shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　合伙企业应当依照法律、行政法规的规定建立企业财务、会计制度。

**Article 36** A partnership enterprise shall establish its financial and accounting systems in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.

#### 第四节　合伙企业与第三人关系

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　合伙企业对合伙人执行合伙事务以及对外代表合伙企业权利的限制，不得对抗善意第三人。

**Article 37** The restrictions imposed by a partnership enterprise on a partner's execution of partnership affairs and the partner's authority to represent the partnership enterprise externally shall not be asserted against a bona fide third party.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　合伙企业对其债务，应先以其全部财产进行清偿。

**Article 38** A partnership enterprise shall first satisfy its debts with all of its property.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　合伙企业不能清偿到期债务的，合伙人承担无限连带责任。

**Article 39** Where a partnership enterprise is unable to discharge its due debts, the partners shall bear unlimited joint and several liability.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　合伙人由于承担无限连带责任，清偿数额超过[本法第三十三条](#art-33)第一款规定的其亏损分担比例的，有权向其他合伙人追偿。

**Article 40** Where a partner, due to bearing unlimited joint and several liability, pays an amount exceeding its share of losses as specified in Article 33, paragraph 1 of this Law, it shall have the right to recover the excess from the other partners.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　合伙人发生与合伙企业无关的债务，相关债权人不得以其债权抵销其对合伙企业的债务；也不得代位行使合伙人在合伙企业中的权利。

**Article 41** Where a partner incurs a debt unrelated to the partnership enterprise, the relevant creditor shall not set off its claim against the partner's debt owed to the partnership enterprise, nor shall it exercise by subrogation the partner's rights in the partnership enterprise.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　合伙人的自有财产不足清偿其与合伙企业无关的债务的，该合伙人可以以其从合伙企业中分取的收益用于清偿；债权人也可以依法请求人民法院强制执行该合伙人在合伙企业中的财产份额用于清偿。  
人民法院强制执行合伙人的财产份额时，应当通知全体合伙人，其他合伙人有优先购买权；其他合伙人未购买，又不同意将该财产份额转让给他人的，依照[本法第五十一条](#art-51)的规定为该合伙人办理退伙结算，或者办理削减该合伙人相应财产份额的结算。

**Article 42** Where a partner's own property is insufficient to satisfy its debt unrelated to the partnership enterprise, the partner may use the distributions it receives from the partnership enterprise to satisfy the debt; the creditor may also request the People's Court to carry out compulsory execution against the partner's property share in the partnership enterprise to satisfy the debt according to law.  
When the People's Court enforces compulsory execution against a partner's property share, it shall notify all partners, and the other partners shall have a preemptive right to purchase; if the other partners do not purchase and do not consent to the transfer of the property share to another person, the partnership enterprise shall, in accordance with Article 51 of this Law, effect a settlement for the withdrawal of the partner or a settlement for reduction of the corresponding property share of the partner.

#### 第五节　入伙、退伙

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　新合伙人入伙，除合伙协议另有约定外，应当经全体合伙人一致同意，并依法订立书面入伙协议。  
订立入伙协议时，原合伙人应当向新合伙人如实告知原合伙企业的经营状况和财务状况。

**Article 43** The admission of a new partner shall, unless otherwise provided in the partnership agreement, be subject to the unanimous consent of all partners, and a written admission agreement shall be concluded according to law.  
When concluding the admission agreement, the existing partners shall truthfully inform the new partner of the operational and financial conditions of the original partnership enterprise.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　入伙的新合伙人与原合伙人享有同等权利，承担同等责任。入伙协议另有约定的，从其约定。  
新合伙人对入伙前合伙企业的债务承担无限连带责任。

**Article 44** A new partner who is admitted shall enjoy the same rights and assume the same liabilities as the existing partners. If the admission agreement provides otherwise, such provisions shall prevail.  
A new partner shall bear unlimited joint and several liability for the debts of the partnership enterprise incurred before its admission.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　合伙协议约定合伙期限的，在合伙企业存续期间，有下列情形之一的，合伙人可以退伙：  
（一）合伙协议约定的退伙事由出现；  
（二）经全体合伙人一致同意；  
（三）发生合伙人难以继续参加合伙的事由；  
（四）其他合伙人严重违反合伙协议约定的义务。

**Article 45** Where the partnership agreement specifies a term of partnership, a partner may withdraw from the partnership during the existence of the partnership enterprise under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the occurrence of the cause for withdrawal as specified in the partnership agreement;  
(2) the unanimous consent of all partners;  
(3) the occurrence of a cause that makes it difficult for the partner to continue to participate in the partnership; or  
(4) other partners' material breach of the obligations specified in the partnership agreement.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　合伙协议未约定合伙期限的，合伙人在不给合伙企业事务执行造成不利影响的情况下，可以退伙，但应当提前三十日通知其他合伙人。

**Article 46** Where the partnership agreement does not specify a term of partnership, a partner may withdraw from the partnership provided that it does not adversely affect the execution of the partnership enterprise's affairs, but it shall notify the other partners thirty days in advance.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　合伙人违反[本法第四十五条](#art-45)、第四十六条的规定退伙的，应当赔偿由此给合伙企业造成的损失。

**Article 47** Where a partner withdraws in violation of the provisions of Articles 45 and 46 of this Law, it shall compensate the losses thereby caused to the partnership enterprise.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　合伙人有下列情形之一的，当然退伙：  
（一）作为合伙人的自然人死亡或者被依法宣告死亡；  
（二）个人丧失偿债能力；  
（三）作为合伙人的法人或者其他组织依法被吊销营业执照、责令关闭、撤销，或者被宣告破产；  
（四）法律规定或者合伙协议约定合伙人必须具有相关资格而丧失该资格；  
（五）合伙人在合伙企业中的全部财产份额被人民法院强制执行。  
合伙人被依法认定为无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人的，经其他合伙人一致同意，可以依法转为有限合伙人，普通合伙企业依法转为有限合伙企业。其他合伙人未能一致同意的，该无民事行为能力或者限制民事行为能力的合伙人退伙。  
退伙事由实际发生之日为退伙生效日。

**Article 48** A partner shall be automatically withdrawn from the partnership under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the partner, being a natural person, dies or is legally declared dead;  
(2) the partner loses the ability to pay its debts;  
(3) the partner, being a legal person or other organization, has its business license lawfully revoked, is ordered to close down, is dissolved, or is declared bankrupt;  
(4) a qualification that the partner must possess as required by law or the partnership agreement is lost; or  
(5) the partner's entire property share in the partnership enterprise is enforced by the People's Court for compulsory execution.  
Where a partner is legally determined to be a person with no capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct, with the unanimous consent of the other partners, it may become a limited partner according to law, and the general partnership enterprise shall become a limited partnership enterprise according to law. If the other partners fail to give unanimous consent, the partner with no capacity for civil conduct or limited capacity for civil conduct shall withdraw from the partnership.  
The date on which the cause for withdrawal actually occurs shall be the effective date of withdrawal.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　合伙人有下列情形之一的，经其他合伙人一致同意，可以决议将其除名：  
（一）未履行出资义务；  
（二）因故意或者重大过失给合伙企业造成损失；  
（三）执行合伙事务时有不正当行为；  
（四）发生合伙协议约定的事由。  
对合伙人的除名决议应当书面通知被除名人。被除名人接到除名通知之日，除名生效，被除名人退伙。  
被除名人对除名决议有异议的，可以自接到除名通知之日起三十日内，向人民法院起诉。

**Article 49** A partner may be expelled by resolution upon the unanimous consent of the other partners under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) it fails to perform its capital contribution obligation;  
(2) it causes losses to the partnership enterprise due to intent or gross negligence;  
(3) it engages in improper conduct when executing partnership affairs; or  
(4) the occurrence of a cause specified in the partnership agreement.  
The resolution to expel a partner shall be notified in writing to the expelled partner. The expulsion shall take effect on the date when the expelled partner receives the expulsion notice, and the expelled partner shall withdraw from the partnership.  
If the expelled partner objects to the expulsion resolution, it may file a prosecution with the People's Court within thirty days from the date of receiving the expulsion notice.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　合伙人死亡或者被依法宣告死亡的，对该合伙人在合伙企业中的财产份额享有合法继承权的继承人，按照合伙协议的约定或者经全体合伙人一致同意，从继承开始之日起，取得该合伙企业的合伙人资格。  
有下列情形之一的，合伙企业应当向合伙人的继承人退还被继承合伙人的财产份额：  
（一）继承人不愿意成为合伙人；  
（二）法律规定或者合伙协议约定合伙人必须具有相关资格，而该继承人未取得该资格；  
（三）合伙协议约定不能成为合伙人的其他情形。  
合伙人的继承人为无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人的，经全体合伙人一致同意，可以依法成为有限合伙人，普通合伙企业依法转为有限合伙企业。全体合伙人未能一致同意的，合伙企业应当将被继承合伙人的财产份额退还该继承人。

**Article 50** Where a partner dies or is legally declared dead, the heir who is lawfully entitled to inherit the partner's property share in the partnership enterprise may, in accordance with the provisions of the partnership agreement or with the unanimous consent of all partners, acquire the status of a partner of the partnership enterprise from the date succession commences.  
Under any of the following circumstances, the partnership enterprise shall return the property share of the deceased partner to the heir:  
(1) the heir is unwilling to become a partner;  
(2) the law or the partnership agreement provides that a partner must possess a relevant qualification, and the heir fails to obtain such qualification; or  
(3) other circumstances specified in the partnership agreement under which a person cannot become a partner.  
Where the heir of a partner is a person with no capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct, with the unanimous consent of all partners, such heir may become a limited partner according to law, and the general partnership enterprise shall become a limited partnership enterprise according to law. If all partners fail to give unanimous consent, the partnership enterprise shall return the property share of the deceased partner to the heir.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　合伙人退伙，其他合伙人应当与该退伙人按照退伙时的合伙企业财产状况进行结算，退还退伙人的财产份额。退伙人对给合伙企业造成的损失负有赔偿责任的，相应扣减其应当赔偿的数额。  
退伙时有未了结的合伙企业事务的，待该事务了结后进行结算。

**Article 51** Where a partner withdraws, the other partners shall settle accounts with the withdrawing partner based on the property status of the partnership enterprise at the time of withdrawal, and return the withdrawing partner's property share. If the withdrawing partner bears compensation liability for losses caused to the partnership enterprise, the amount of compensation payable shall be deducted accordingly.  
Where there are unfinished affairs of the partnership enterprise at the time of withdrawal, settlement shall be made after such affairs are concluded.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　退伙人在合伙企业中财产份额的退还办法，由合伙协议约定或者由全体合伙人决定，可以退还货币，也可以退还实物。

**Article 52** The method for returning the withdrawing partner's property share in the partnership enterprise shall be specified in the partnership agreement or decided by all partners; it may be returned in currency or in kind.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　退伙人对基于其退伙前的原因发生的合伙企业债务，承担无限连带责任。

**Article 53** A withdrawing partner shall bear unlimited joint and several liability for the debts of the partnership enterprise arising from causes that occurred prior to its withdrawal.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　合伙人退伙时，合伙企业财产少于合伙企业债务的，退伙人应当依照[本法第三十三条](#art-33)第一款的规定分担亏损。

**Article 54** When a partner withdraws from the partnership, if the partnership property is less than the partnership debts, the withdrawing partner shall share the losses in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 33 of this Law.

#### 第六节　特殊的普通合伙企业

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　以专业知识和专门技能为客户提供有偿服务的专业服务机构，可以设立为特殊的普通合伙企业。  
特殊的普通合伙企业是指合伙人依照[本法第五十七条](#art-57)的规定承担责任的普通合伙企业。  
特殊的普通合伙企业适用本节规定；本节未作规定的，适用本章第一节至第五节的规定。

**Article 55** A professional service institution that provides paid services to clients with professional knowledge and specialized skills may be established as a special general partnership.  
A special general partnership refers to a general partnership in which the partners assume liability in accordance with Article 57 of this Law.  
A special general partnership shall be governed by the provisions of this Section; matters not covered in this Section shall be governed by the provisions of Sections 1 to 5 of this Chapter.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　特殊的普通合伙企业名称中应当标明“特殊普通合伙”字样。

**Article 56** The name of a special general partnership shall indicate the words "special general partnership".

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　一个合伙人或者数个合伙人在执业活动中因故意或者重大过失造成合伙企业债务的，应当承担无限责任或者无限连带责任，其他合伙人以其在合伙企业中的财产份额为限承担责任。  
合伙人在执业活动中非因故意或者重大过失造成的合伙企业债务以及合伙企业的其他债务，由全体合伙人承担无限连带责任。

**Article 57** Where one or several partners cause partnership debts due to intentional act or gross negligence in the course of practicing, such partner(s) shall bear unlimited liability or unlimited joint and several liability, and the other partners shall bear liability within the limit of their respective shares of property in the partnership.  
Partnership debts caused by a partner in the course of practicing not due to intentional act or gross negligence, as well as other debts of the partnership, shall be borne by all partners with unlimited joint and several liability.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　合伙人执业活动中因故意或者重大过失造成的合伙企业债务，以合伙企业财产对外承担责任后，该合伙人应当按照合伙协议的约定对给合伙企业造成的损失承担赔偿责任。

**Article 58** After the partnership property has been used to assume liability to outsiders for the partnership debts caused by a partner due to intentional act or gross negligence in the course of practicing, such partner shall bear compensation liability for the losses caused to the partnership in accordance with the partnership agreement.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　特殊的普通合伙企业应当建立执业风险基金、办理职业保险。  
执业风险基金用于偿付合伙人执业活动造成的债务。执业风险基金应当单独立户管理。具体管理办法由国务院规定。

**Article 59** A special general partnership shall establish a practice risk fund and obtain professional insurance.  
The practice risk fund shall be used to pay debts caused by partners' practice activities. The practice risk fund shall be managed in a separate account. The specific management measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.

### 第三章　有限合伙企业

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　有限合伙企业及其合伙人适用本章规定；本章未作规定的，适用本法第二章第一节至第五节关于普通合伙企业及其合伙人的规定。

**Article 60** The provisions of this Chapter shall apply to limited partnership enterprises and their partners; where this Chapter does not provide, the provisions of Sections 1 to 5 of Chapter II of this Law on general partnership enterprises and their partners shall apply.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　有限合伙企业由二个以上五十个以下合伙人设立；但是，法律另有规定的除外。  
有限合伙企业至少应当有一个普通合伙人。

**Article 61** A limited partnership enterprise shall be established by not less than two and not more than fifty partners, unless otherwise provided by law.  
A limited partnership enterprise shall have at least one general partner.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　有限合伙企业名称中应当标明“有限合伙”字样。

**Article 62** The name of a limited partnership enterprise shall contain the words "limited partnership".

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　合伙协议除符合[本法第十八条](#art-18)的规定外，还应当载明下列事项：  
（一）普通合伙人和有限合伙人的姓名或者名称、住所；  
（二）执行事务合伙人应具备的条件和选择程序；  
（三）执行事务合伙人权限与违约处理办法；  
（四）执行事务合伙人的除名条件和更换程序；  
（五）有限合伙人入伙、退伙的条件、程序以及相关责任；  
（六）有限合伙人和普通合伙人相互转变程序。

**Article 63** In addition to conforming to the provisions of Article 18 of this Law, the partnership agreement shall also specify the following:  
(1) the names or titles and domiciles of the general partners and limited partners;  
(2) the conditions and selection procedures for the partner(s) executing partnership affairs;  
(3) the scope of authority of the partner(s) executing partnership affairs and the methods for handling breach of contract;  
(4) the conditions for removal and replacement procedures of the partner(s) executing partnership affairs;  
(5) the conditions, procedures, and relevant liabilities for the admission and withdrawal of limited partners;  
(6) the procedures for mutual conversion between limited partners and general partners.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　有限合伙人可以用货币、实物、知识产权、土地使用权或者其他财产权利作价出资。  
有限合伙人不得以劳务出资。

**Article 64** A limited partner may make capital contributions in currency, tangible assets, intellectual property, land use rights, or other property rights evaluated in monetary terms.  
A limited partner shall not make capital contributions in the form of labor services.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　有限合伙人应当按照合伙协议的约定按期足额缴纳出资；未按期足额缴纳的，应当承担补缴义务，并对其他合伙人承担违约责任。

**Article 65** A limited partner shall make its capital contributions in full and on time in accordance with the partnership agreement; if it fails to make contributions in full and on time, it shall bear the obligation to make up the contribution and shall bear liability for breach of contract to the other partners.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　有限合伙企业登记事项中应当载明有限合伙人的姓名或者名称及认缴的出资数额。

**Article 66** The registered items of a limited partnership enterprise shall include the names or titles of the limited partners and the amount of their subscribed capital contributions.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　有限合伙企业由普通合伙人执行合伙事务。执行事务合伙人可以要求在合伙协议中确定执行事务的报酬及报酬提取方式。

**Article 67** In a limited partnership, the general partners shall execute the partnership affairs. The partner executing affairs may require that the remuneration for executing affairs and the method of drawing such remuneration be determined in the partnership agreement.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　有限合伙人不执行合伙事务，不得对外代表有限合伙企业。  
有限合伙人的下列行为，不视为执行合伙事务：  
（一）参与决定普通合伙人入伙、退伙；  
（二）对企业的经营管理提出建议；  
（三）参与选择承办有限合伙企业审计业务的会计师事务所；  
（四）获取经审计的有限合伙企业财务会计报告；  
（五）对涉及自身利益的情况，查阅有限合伙企业财务会计账簿等财务资料；  
（六）在有限合伙企业中的利益受到侵害时，向有责任的合伙人主张权利或者提起诉讼；  
（七）执行事务合伙人怠于行使权利时，督促其行使权利或者为了本企业的利益以自己的名义提起诉讼；  
（八）依法为本企业提供担保。

**Article 68** A limited partner shall not execute partnership affairs and shall not represent the limited partnership externally.  
  
The following acts of a limited partner shall not be deemed as executing partnership affairs:  
  
(1) participating in deciding on the admission and withdrawal of general partners;  
(2) making proposals on the operation and management of the enterprise;  
(3) participating in the selection of an accounting firm that undertakes the audit business of the limited partnership;  
(4) obtaining the audited financial accounting reports of the limited partnership;  
(5) inspecting the financial accounting books and other financial materials of the limited partnership where the partner's own interests are involved;  
(6) claiming rights or bringing a lawsuit against the responsible partner when the partner's interests in the limited partnership are infringed upon;  
(7) urging the partner executing partnership affairs to exercise his rights when he neglects to do so, or bringing a lawsuit in his own name for the benefit of the partnership;  
(8) providing security for the partnership in accordance with law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　有限合伙企业不得将全部利润分配给部分合伙人；但是，合伙协议另有约定的除外。

**Article 69** A limited partnership shall not distribute all profits to some of the partners, unless otherwise agreed in the partnership agreement.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　有限合伙人可以同本有限合伙企业进行交易；但是，合伙协议另有约定的除外。

**Article 70** A limited partner may conduct transactions with the limited partnership, unless otherwise agreed in the partnership agreement.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　有限合伙人可以自营或者同他人合作经营与本有限合伙企业相竞争的业务；但是，合伙协议另有约定的除外。

**Article 71** A limited partner may operate business competing with the limited partnership on his own or in cooperation with others, unless otherwise agreed in the partnership agreement.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　有限合伙人可以将其在有限合伙企业中的财产份额出质；但是，合伙协议另有约定的除外。

**Article 72** A limited partner may pledge his property share in the limited partnership, unless otherwise agreed in the partnership agreement.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　有限合伙人可以按照合伙协议的约定向合伙人以外的人转让其在有限合伙企业中的财产份额，但应当提前三十日通知其他合伙人。

**Article 73** A limited partner may transfer his property share in the limited partnership to a person other than the partners in accordance with the partnership agreement, but he shall notify the other partners thirty days in advance.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　有限合伙人的自有财产不足清偿其与合伙企业无关的债务的，该合伙人可以以其从有限合伙企业中分取的收益用于清偿；债权人也可以依法请求人民法院强制执行该合伙人在有限合伙企业中的财产份额用于清偿。  
人民法院强制执行有限合伙人的财产份额时，应当通知全体合伙人。在同等条件下，其他合伙人有优先购买权。

**Article 74** If a limited partner's own property is insufficient to pay off his debts unrelated to the partnership, the partner may use the income he receives from the limited partnership for payment; the creditor may also, according to law, request the People's Court to enforce compulsory execution against the partner's property share in the limited partnership for payment.  
  
When the People's Court enforces compulsory execution against a limited partner's property share, it shall notify all partners. Under the same conditions, other partners have a preemptive right to purchase.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　有限合伙企业仅剩有限合伙人的，应当解散；有限合伙企业仅剩普通合伙人的，转为普通合伙企业。

**Article 75** If a limited partnership has only limited partners remaining, it shall be dissolved; if a limited partnership has only general partners remaining, it shall be converted into a general partnership.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　第三人有理由相信有限合伙人为普通合伙人并与其交易的，该有限合伙人对该笔交易承担与普通合伙人同样的责任。  
有限合伙人未经授权以有限合伙企业名义与他人进行交易，给有限合伙企业或者其他合伙人造成损失的，该有限合伙人应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 76** If a third party has reason to believe that a limited partner is a general partner and enters into a transaction with him, the limited partner shall bear the same liability for the transaction as a general partner.  
  
If a limited partner, without authorization, conducts transactions with others in the name of the limited partnership, causing losses to the limited partnership or other partners, the limited partner shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　新入伙的有限合伙人对入伙前有限合伙企业的债务，以其认缴的出资额为限承担责任。

**Article 77** A newly admitted limited partner shall bear liability for the debts of the limited partnership incurred before his admission to the extent of his subscribed capital contribution.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　有限合伙人有[本法第四十八条](#art-48)第一款第一项、第三项至第五项所列情形之一的，当然退伙。

**Article 78** If a limited partner falls under any of the circumstances specified in Items (1) and (3) to (5) of Paragraph 1 of Article 48 of this Law, he shall be automatically withdrawn from the partnership.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　作为有限合伙人的自然人在有限合伙企业存续期间丧失民事行为能力的，其他合伙人不得因此要求其退伙。

**Article 79** If a natural person who is a limited partner loses his capacity for civil conduct during the existence of the limited partnership, the other partners shall not demand his withdrawal on such ground.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　作为有限合伙人的自然人死亡、被依法宣告死亡或者作为有限合伙人的法人及其他组织终止时，其继承人或者权利承受人可以依法取得该有限合伙人在有限合伙企业中的资格。

**Article 80** When a natural person who is a limited partner dies or is declared dead according to law, or when a legal person or other organization that is a limited partner is terminated, his successor or rights successor may, according to law, acquire the status of the limited partner in the limited partnership.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　有限合伙人退伙后，对基于其退伙前的原因发生的有限合伙企业债务，以其退伙时从有限合伙企业中取回的财产承担责任。

**Article 81** After a limited partner withdraws from the partnership, he shall be liable for the debts of the limited partnership arising from causes prior to his withdrawal to the extent of the property he took back from the limited partnership at the time of withdrawal.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　除合伙协议另有约定外，普通合伙人转变为有限合伙人，或者有限合伙人转变为普通合伙人，应当经全体合伙人一致同意。

**Article 82** Unless otherwise agreed in the partnership agreement, a general partner's conversion into a limited partner, or a limited partner's conversion into a general partner, shall be subject to the unanimous consent of all partners.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　有限合伙人转变为普通合伙人的，对其作为有限合伙人期间有限合伙企业发生的债务承担无限连带责任。

**Article 83** If a limited partner is converted into a general partner, he shall bear unlimited joint and several liability for the debts of the limited partnership incurred during the period when he served as a limited partner.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　普通合伙人转变为有限合伙人的，对其作为普通合伙人期间合伙企业发生的债务承担无限连带责任。

**Article 84** If a general partner is converted into a limited partner, he shall bear unlimited joint and several liability for the debts of the partnership incurred during the period when he served as a general partner.

### 第四章　合伙企业解散、清算

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　合伙企业有下列情形之一的，应当解散：  
（一）合伙期限届满，合伙人决定不再经营；  
（二）合伙协议约定的解散事由出现；  
（三）全体合伙人决定解散；  
（四）合伙人已不具备法定人数满三十天；  
（五）合伙协议约定的合伙目的已经实现或者无法实现；  
（六）依法被吊销营业执照、责令关闭或者被撤销；  
（七）法律、行政法规规定的其他原因。

**Article 85** A partnership shall be dissolved under any of the following circumstances:  
  
(1) The term of the partnership expires and the partners decide not to continue operation;  
(2) The causes for dissolution as stipulated in the partnership agreement occur;  
(3) All partners decide to dissolve the partnership;  
(4) The number of partners has not met the statutory number for thirty days;  
(5) The purpose of the partnership as agreed in the partnership agreement has been achieved or cannot be achieved;  
(6) The business license is revoked, the partnership is ordered to close, or it is cancelled, according to law;  
(7) Other reasons provided by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　合伙企业解散，应当由清算人进行清算。  
清算人由全体合伙人担任；经全体合伙人过半数同意，可以自合伙企业解散事由出现后十五日内指定一个或者数个合伙人，或者委托第三人，担任清算人。  
自合伙企业解散事由出现之日起十五日内未确定清算人的，合伙人或者其他利害关系人可以申请人民法院指定清算人。

**Article 86** When a partnership is dissolved, it shall be liquidated by a liquidator.  
  
The liquidator shall be all partners; with the consent of more than half of all partners, one or more partners may be designated within fifteen days after the occurrence of the cause for dissolution, or a third party may be entrusted, to serve as the liquidator.  
  
If no liquidator is determined within fifteen days from the date of occurrence of the cause for dissolution, the partners or other interested persons may apply to the People's Court to designate a liquidator.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　清算人在清算期间执行下列事务：  
（一）清理合伙企业财产，分别编制资产负债表和财产清单；  
（二）处理与清算有关的合伙企业未了结事务；  
（三）清缴所欠税款；  
（四）清理债权、债务；  
（五）处理合伙企业清偿债务后的剩余财产；  
（六）代表合伙企业参加诉讼或者仲裁活动。

**Article 87** The liquidator shall execute the following affairs during the liquidation period:  
(1) to liquidate the partnership's property and respectively prepare a balance sheet and an inventory of property;  
(2) to handle the uncompleted affairs of the partnership related to liquidation;  
(3) to pay off the taxes owed;  
(4) to clear claims and debts;  
(5) to dispose of the remaining property after the partnership has paid off its debts;  
(6) to represent the partnership in litigation or arbitration activities.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　清算人自被确定之日起十日内将合伙企业解散事项通知债权人，并于六十日内在报纸上公告。债权人应当自接到通知书之日起三十日内，未接到通知书的自公告之日起四十五日内，向清算人申报债权。  
债权人申报债权，应当说明债权的有关事项，并提供证明材料。清算人应当对债权进行登记。  
清算期间，合伙企业存续，但不得开展与清算无关的经营活动。

**Article 88** The liquidator shall, within ten days from the date of being confirmed, notify the creditors of the dissolution of the partnership, and shall make an announcement in a newspaper within sixty days. The creditors shall, within thirty days from the date of receipt of the notice, or within forty-five days from the date of the announcement if they do not receive the notice, declare their claims to the liquidator.  
When declaring claims, the creditors shall specify the relevant matters of the claims and provide evidentiary materials. The liquidator shall register the claims.  
During the liquidation period, the partnership shall remain in existence, but shall not engage in any business activities unrelated to liquidation.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　合伙企业财产在支付清算费用和职工工资、社会保险费用、法定补偿金以及缴纳所欠税款、清偿债务后的剩余财产，依照[本法第三十三条](#art-33)第一款的规定进行分配。

**Article 89** The remaining property of the partnership after payment of liquidation expenses, employees' wages, social insurance premiums, statutory compensations, payment of taxes owed, and satisfaction of debts shall be distributed in accordance with the provisions of Article 33, Paragraph 1 of this Law.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　清算结束，清算人应当编制清算报告，经全体合伙人签名、盖章后，在十五日内向企业登记机关报送清算报告，申请办理合伙企业注销登记。

**Article 90** After the liquidation is completed, the liquidator shall prepare a liquidation report, which shall be signed and sealed by all partners, and shall submit the liquidation report to the enterprise registration authority within fifteen days to apply for the deregistration of the partnership.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　合伙企业注销后，原普通合伙人对合伙企业存续期间的债务仍应承担无限连带责任。

**Article 91** After the partnership is deregistered, the former general partners shall still bear unlimited joint and several liability for the debts incurred during the existence of the partnership.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　合伙企业不能清偿到期债务的，债权人可以依法向人民法院提出破产清算申请，也可以要求普通合伙人清偿。  
合伙企业依法被宣告破产的，普通合伙人对合伙企业债务仍应承担无限连带责任。

**Article 92** Where a partnership is unable to pay its due debts, the creditors may, according to law, file a bankruptcy liquidation application with the People's Court, or may demand the general partners to pay off the debts.  
Where the partnership is declared bankrupt according to law, the general partners shall still bear unlimited joint and several liability for the debts of the partnership.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　违反本法规定，提交虚假文件或者采取其他欺骗手段，取得合伙企业登记的，由企业登记机关责令改正，处以五千元以上五万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，撤销企业登记，并处以五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 93** Where a partnership registration is obtained by submitting false documents or by other deceptive means in violation of the provisions of this Law, the enterprise registration authority shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the enterprise registration shall be revoked and a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　违反本法规定，合伙企业未在其名称中标明“普通合伙”、“特殊普通合伙”或者“有限合伙”字样的，由企业登记机关责令限期改正，处以二千元以上一万元以下的罚款。

**Article 94** Where a partnership violates the provisions of this Law by failing to indicate the words "general partnership", "special general partnership" or "limited partnership" in its name, the enterprise registration authority shall order it to make rectification within a specified time limit and impose a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　违反本法规定，未领取营业执照，而以合伙企业或者合伙企业分支机构名义从事合伙业务的，由企业登记机关责令停止，处以五千元以上五万元以下的罚款。  
合伙企业登记事项发生变更时，未依照本法规定办理变更登记的，由企业登记机关责令限期登记；逾期不登记的，处以二千元以上二万元以下的罚款。  
合伙企业登记事项发生变更，执行合伙事务的合伙人未按期申请办理变更登记的，应当赔偿由此给合伙企业、其他合伙人或者善意第三人造成的损失。

**Article 95** Where a person engages in partnership business in the name of a partnership or a branch of a partnership without obtaining a business license in violation of the provisions of this Law, the enterprise registration authority shall order the cessation of such activities and impose a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan.  
Where a partnership fails to undergo change registration in accordance with the provisions of this Law when its registered items change, the enterprise registration authority shall order it to register within a specified time limit; if it fails to register within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where a partner managing partnership affairs fails to apply for change registration within the time limit when the registered items of the partnership change, the partner shall compensate the partnership, other partners, or bona fide third parties for the losses caused thereby.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　合伙人执行合伙事务，或者合伙企业从业人员利用职务上的便利，将应当归合伙企业的利益据为己有的，或者采取其他手段侵占合伙企业财产的，应当将该利益和财产退还合伙企业；给合伙企业或者其他合伙人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 96** Where a partner, in executing partnership affairs, or a person employed by the partnership, by taking advantage of his position, appropriates to himself the interests that should belong to the partnership, or takes other means to embezzle the partnership property, he shall return such interests and property to the partnership; if losses are caused to the partnership or other partners, he shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　合伙人对本法规定或者合伙协议约定必须经全体合伙人一致同意始得执行的事务擅自处理，给合伙企业或者其他合伙人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 97** Where a partner, without authorization, handles any matter that, under the provisions of this Law or the partnership agreement, shall be executed only with the unanimous consent of all partners, thereby causing losses to the partnership or other partners, he shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　不具有事务执行权的合伙人擅自执行合伙事务，给合伙企业或者其他合伙人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 98** Where a partner who does not have the authority to execute partnership affairs executes partnership affairs without authorization, thereby causing losses to the partnership or other partners, he shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　合伙人违反本法规定或者合伙协议的约定，从事与本合伙企业相竞争的业务或者与本合伙企业进行交易的，该收益归合伙企业所有；给合伙企业或者其他合伙人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 99** Where a partner, in violation of the provisions of this Law or the partnership agreement, engages in a business that competes with the partnership or enters into a transaction with the partnership, the gains derived therefrom shall belong to the partnership; if losses are caused to the partnership or other partners, he shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　清算人未依照本法规定向企业登记机关报送清算报告，或者报送清算报告隐瞒重要事实，或者有重大遗漏的，由企业登记机关责令改正。由此产生的费用和损失，由清算人承担和赔偿。

**Article 100** Where a liquidator fails to submit a liquidation report to the enterprise registration authority in accordance with the provisions of this Law, or submits a liquidation report that conceals important facts or contains major omissions, the enterprise registration authority shall order rectification. The expenses and losses arising therefrom shall be borne and compensated by the liquidator.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　清算人执行清算事务，牟取非法收入或者侵占合伙企业财产的，应当将该收入和侵占的财产退还合伙企业；给合伙企业或者其他合伙人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 101** Where a liquidator, in executing liquidation affairs, seeks illegal gains or embezzles partnership property, he shall return such gains and embezzled property to the partnership; if losses are caused to the partnership or other partners, he shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　清算人违反本法规定，隐匿、转移合伙企业财产，对资产负债表或者财产清单作虚假记载，或者在未清偿债务前分配财产，损害债权人利益的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 102** Where a liquidator, in violation of the provisions of this Law, conceals or transfers partnership property, makes false records on the balance sheet or inventory of property, or distributes property before debts are paid off, thereby harming the interests of creditors, he shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　合伙人违反合伙协议的，应当依法承担违约责任。  
合伙人履行合伙协议发生争议的，合伙人可以通过协商或者调解解决。不愿通过协商、调解解决或者协商、调解不成的，可以按照合伙协议约定的仲裁条款或者事后达成的书面仲裁协议，向仲裁机构申请仲裁。合伙协议中未订立仲裁条款，事后又没有达成书面仲裁协议的，可以向人民法院起诉。

**Article 103** Where a partner violates the partnership agreement, he shall bear liability for breach of contract according to law.  
Where a dispute arises between partners over the performance of the partnership agreement, the partners may resolve it through negotiation or mediation. If they are unwilling to resolve it through negotiation or mediation, or if negotiation or mediation fails, they may submit the dispute to an arbitration institution for arbitration in accordance with the arbitration clause stipulated in the partnership agreement or a written arbitration agreement reached afterwards. If no arbitration clause is stipulated in the partnership agreement and no written arbitration agreement is reached afterwards, they may initiate a prosecution in the People's Court.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　有关行政管理机关的工作人员违反本法规定，滥用职权、徇私舞弊、收受贿赂、侵害合伙企业合法权益的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 104** Where a staff member of the relevant administrative authority, in violation of the provisions of this Law, abuses his power, engages in malpractices for personal gain, accepts bribes, or infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of a partnership, he shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 105** Where a violation of the provisions of this Law constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　违反本法规定，应当承担民事赔偿责任和缴纳罚款、罚金，其财产不足以同时支付的，先承担民事赔偿责任。

**Article 106** Where a person violates the provisions of this Law and shall bear civil compensation liability and pay fines or pecuniary penalties, and his property is insufficient to pay all of them at the same time, he shall first bear the civil compensation liability.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　非企业专业服务机构依据有关法律采取合伙制的，其合伙人承担责任的形式可以适用本法关于特殊的普通合伙企业合伙人承担责任的规定。

**Article 107** If a non-enterprise professional service organization adopts a partnership form pursuant to relevant laws, the form of liability undertaken by its partners may be subject to the provisions of this Law regarding the liability of partners in a special general partnership.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　外国企业或者个人在中国境内设立合伙企业的管理办法由国务院规定。

**Article 108** The administrative measures for the establishment of partnership enterprises within the territory of China by foreign enterprises or individuals shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　本法自2007年6月1日起施行。

**Article 109** This Law shall come into force on June 1, 2007.
