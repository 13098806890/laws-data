**法律部门**：民法商法

**生效日期**：2025-02-15

### 正文

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　地方各级人民政府不得以股票公开发行上市结果为条件，给予发行人或者中介机构奖励。

**Article 10** Local people's governments at all levels shall not grant rewards to issuers or intermediary institutions on the condition of the results of the public issuance and listing of stocks.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　发行人申请公开发行股票时，应当在所报送的招股说明书或者其他相关信息披露文件中详细列明各类中介服务收费标准、金额以及发行人付费安排等信息。

**Article 11** When an issuer applies for public issuance of stocks, it shall, in the submitted prospectus or other relevant information disclosure documents, set forth in detail the fee standards and amounts of various types of intermediary services, and the issuer's payment arrangements, among other information.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　证券监督管理、财政、司法行政等部门应当加强信息沟通和协调配合，按照职责分工，依法对中介机构执业行为加强监管；必要时可以采取联合现场检查等措施，依法查处违法违规行为。

**Article 12** The securities regulatory, finance, judicial administration and other departments shall strengthen information communication and coordination, and according to their respective functions and duties, strengthen supervision over the practice of intermediary institutions according to law; when necessary, they may take measures such as joint on-site inspections to investigate and deal with illegal and non-compliant acts according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　中介机构违反本规定的，由证券监督管理、财政、司法行政、市场监督管理等部门按照职责分工责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，可以并处违法所得1倍以上10倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足10万元的，可以并处10万元以上100万元以下罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，可以并处10万元以上100万元以下罚款。法律另有规定的，从其规定。  
中介机构从业人员违反本规定第九条规定的，由证券监督管理、财政、司法行政等部门按照职责分工责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，可以并处违法所得1倍以上10倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足10万元的，可以并处10万元以上100万元以下罚款；情节严重的，可以并处暂停从事相关业务1个月至1年。法律另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 13** Where an intermediary institution violates these Provisions, the securities regulatory, finance, judicial administration, market regulation and other departments shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order it to make corrections, issue a warning, confiscate the illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1 million yuan may be imposed concurrently; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and may also be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1 million yuan. Where there are other provisions in laws, those provisions shall prevail.  
Where an employee of an intermediary institution violates the provisions of Article 9 of these Provisions, the securities regulatory, finance, judicial administration and other departments shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order them to make corrections, issue a warning, confiscate the illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1 million yuan may be imposed concurrently; if the circumstances are serious, the employee may also be suspended from engaging in the relevant business for a period of not less than one month but not more than one year. Where there are other provisions in laws, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　发行人违反[本规定第十一条](#art-11)规定的，由证券监督管理机构责令改正，给予警告，可以并处10万元以上100万元以下罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，可以并处10万元以上100万元以下罚款。发行人的控股股东、实际控制人组织、指使从事上述违法行为，或者隐瞒相关事项导致发生上述情形的，处10万元以上100万元以下罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处10万元以上100万元以下罚款。

**Article 14** Where an issuer violates the provisions of Article 11 of these Provisions, the securities regulatory agency shall order it to make corrections, issue a warning, and may also impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1 million yuan; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and may also be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1 million yuan. Where the controlling shareholder or actual controller of the issuer organizes or instigates the commission of the aforesaid illegal acts, or conceals relevant matters resulting in the occurrence of the aforesaid circumstances, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1 million yuan shall be imposed; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and shall also be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1 million yuan.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院证券监督管理机构依法将中介机构遵守本规定的情况纳入证券市场诚信档案。

**Article 15** The securities regulatory agency of the State Council shall, according to law, include the intermediary institution's compliance with these Provisions into the credit records of the securities market.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　自本规定施行之日起，地方各级人民政府违反[本规定第十条](#art-10)规定，给予发行人或者中介机构奖励的，应当追回，并由有关机关对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 16** As of the date these Provisions come into force, where a local people's government at any level, in violation of Article 10 of these Provisions, grants a reward to an issuer or an intermediary institution, the reward shall be recovered, and the relevant authorities shall, according to law, impose sanctions on the responsible leaders and directly liable persons.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　中介机构为公司公开发行存托凭证、可转换为股票的公司债券提供服务的，参照适用本规定。

**Article 17** Where an intermediary institution provides services for a company's public issuance of depositary receipts or corporate bonds convertible into stocks, these Provisions shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院证券监督管理、财政、司法行政等部门可以根据本规定制定实施办法。

**Article 18** The securities regulatory agency of the State Council, the finance department, the judicial administration department and other departments may formulate implementing rules in accordance with these Provisions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本规定自2025年2月15日起施行。

**Article 19** These Provisions shall come into force as of February 15, 2025.
