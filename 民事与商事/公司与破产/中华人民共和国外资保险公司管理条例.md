### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了适应对外开放和经济发展的需要，加强和完善对外资保险公司的监督管理，促进保险业的健康发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of adapting to the needs of opening to the outside world and economic development, strengthening and improving the supervision and administration of foreign-funded insurance companies, and promoting the healthy development of the insurance industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称外资保险公司，是指依照中华人民共和国有关法律、行政法规的规定，经批准在中国境内设立和营业的下列保险公司：  
(一)外国保险公司同中国的公司、企业在中国境内合资经营的保险公司(以下简称合资保险公司)；  
(二)外国保险公司在中国境内投资经营的外国资本保险公司(以下简称独资保险公司)；  
(三)外国保险公司在中国境内的分公司(以下简称外国保险公司分公司)。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, the term "foreign-funded insurance company" means the following categories of insurance companies that are established and operated within the territory of China with approval according to the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) insurance companies jointly operated by foreign insurance companies and Chinese companies or enterprises within the territory of China (hereinafter referred to as equity joint venture insurance companies);  
(2) wholly foreign-owned insurance companies invested and operated by foreign insurance companies within the territory of China (hereinafter referred to as wholly foreign-owned insurance companies);  
(3) branches of foreign insurance companies within the territory of China (hereinafter referred to as branches of foreign insurance companies).

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　外资保险公司必须遵守中国法律、法规，不得损害中国的社会公共利益。  
外资保险公司的正当业务活动和合法权益受中国法律保护。

**Article 3** Foreign-funded insurance companies must abide by the laws and regulations of China and shall not harm the public interests of China.  
The legitimate business activities and lawful rights and interests of foreign-funded insurance companies shall be protected by the laws of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院保险监督管理机构负责对外资保险公司实施监督管理。国务院保险监督管理机构的派出机构根据国务院保险监督管理机构的授权，对本辖区的外资保险公司进行日常监督管理。

**Article 4** The insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of foreign-funded insurance companies. The local offices of the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall, according to the authorization of the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, carry out routine supervision and administration over foreign-funded insurance companies within their respective jurisdictions.

### 第二章　设立与登记

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　设立外资保险公司，应当经国务院保险监督管理机构批准。  
设立外资保险公司的地区，由国务院保险监督管理机构按照有关规定确定。

**Article 5** The establishment of a foreign-funded insurance company shall be subject to the approval of the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The regions where foreign-funded insurance companies may be established shall be determined by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　设立经营人身保险业务的外资保险公司和经营财产保险业务的外资保险公司，其设立形式、外资比例由国务院保险监督管理机构按照有关规定确定。

**Article 6** For the establishment of a foreign-funded insurance company engaging in life insurance business and a foreign-funded insurance company engaging in property insurance business, the establishment form and the proportion of foreign capital shall be determined by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　合资保险公司、独资保险公司的注册资本最低限额为2亿元人民币或者等值的自由兑换货币；其注册资本最低限额必须为实缴货币资本。  
外国保险公司分公司应当由其总公司无偿拨给不少于2亿元人民币或者等值的自由兑换货币的营运资金。  
国务院保险监督管理机构根据外资保险公司业务范围、经营规模，可以提高前两款规定的外资保险公司注册资本或者营运资金的最低限额。

**Article 7** The minimum registered capital of an equity joint venture insurance company or a wholly foreign-owned insurance company shall be RMB 200 million yuan or an equivalent amount in a freely convertible currency; the minimum registered capital shall be paid-in monetary capital.  
A branch of a foreign insurance company shall be provided by its head office with operating funds of not less than RMB 200 million yuan or an equivalent amount in a freely convertible currency free of charge.  
The insurance regulatory authority of the State Council may, according to the business scope and operation scale of a foreign-funded insurance company, raise the minimum amount of registered capital or operating funds of the foreign-funded insurance company as provided for in the preceding two paragraphs.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请设立外资保险公司的外国保险公司，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)提出设立申请前1年年末总资产不少于50亿美元；  
(二)所在国家或者地区有完善的保险监管制度，并且该外国保险公司已经受到所在国家或者地区有关主管当局的有效监管；  
(三)符合所在国家或者地区偿付能力标准；  
(四)所在国家或者地区有关主管当局同意其申请；  
(五)国务院保险监督管理机构规定的其他审慎性条件。

**Article 8** A foreign insurance company applying for the establishment of a foreign-funded insurance company shall satisfy the following conditions:  
(1) its total assets at the end of the year preceding the year in which the application is filed are not less than US$5 billion;  
(2) the country or region where it is domiciled has a sound insurance regulatory system, and the foreign insurance company has been under the effective supervision of the competent authority of that country or region;  
(3) it meets the solvency standards of the country or region where it is domiciled;  
(4) the competent authority of the country or region where it is domiciled has consented to its application; and  
(5) other prudential conditions prescribed by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　设立外资保险公司，申请人应当向国务院保险监督管理机构提出书面申请，并提交下列资料：  
(一)申请人法定代表人签署的申请书，其中设立合资保险公司的，申请书由合资各方法定代表人共同签署；  
(二)外国申请人所在国家或者地区有关主管当局核发的营业执照(副本)、对其符合偿付能力标准的证明及对其申请的意见书；  
(三)外国申请人的公司章程、最近3年的年报；  
(四)设立合资保险公司的，中国申请人的有关资料；  
(五)拟设公司的可行性研究报告及筹建方案；  
(六)拟设公司的筹建负责人员名单、简历和任职资格证明；  
(七)国务院保险监督管理机构规定提供的其他资料。

**Article 9** To establish a foreign-funded insurance company, the applicant shall submit a written application to the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council together with the following documents:  
(1) an application signed by the legal representative of the applicant, and if an equity joint venture insurance company is to be established, the application shall be jointly signed by the legal representatives of all parties to the joint venture;  
(2) a duplicate of the business license issued by the competent authority of the country or region where the foreign applicant is domiciled, a certificate proving that it meets the solvency standards, and a written opinion on its application;  
(3) the articles of association of the foreign applicant and its annual reports for the most recent three years;  
(4) the relevant documents of the Chinese applicant, if an equity joint venture insurance company is to be established;  
(5) a feasibility study report and a preparatory plan for the proposed company;  
(6) a list of persons responsible for the preparation of the proposed company, their curricula vitae and certificates of qualification for the position;  
(7) other documents required to be submitted by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院保险监督管理机构应当对设立外资保险公司的申请进行初步审查，自收到完整的申请文件之日起6个月内作出受理或者不受理的决定。决定受理的，发给正式申请表；决定不受理的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 10** The insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall conduct a preliminary examination of the application for establishing a foreign-funded insurance company, and shall, within six months from the date of receipt of the complete application documents, make a decision to accept or not to accept the application. If it decides to accept the application, it shall issue a formal application form; if it decides not to accept the application, it shall notify the applicant in writing and give reasons therefor.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请人应当自接到正式申请表之日起1年内完成筹建工作；在规定的期限内未完成筹建工作，有正当理由的，经国务院保险监督管理机构批准，可以延长3个月。在延长期内仍未完成筹建工作的，国务院保险监督管理机构作出的受理决定自动失效。筹建工作完成后，申请人应当将填写好的申请表连同下列文件报国务院保险监督管理机构审批：  
(一)筹建报告；  
(二)拟设公司的章程；  
(三)拟设公司的出资人及其出资额；  
(四)法定验资机构出具的验资证明；  
(五)对拟任该公司主要负责人的授权书；  
(六)拟设公司的高级管理人员名单、简历和任职资格证明；  
(七)拟设公司未来3年的经营规划和分保方案；  
(八)拟在中国境内开办保险险种的保险条款、保险费率及责任准备金的计算说明书；  
(九)拟设公司的营业场所和与业务有关的其他设施的资料；  
(十)设立外国保险公司分公司的，其总公司对该分公司承担税务、债务的责任担保书；  
(十一)设立合资保险公司的，其合资经营合同；  
(十二)国务院保险监督管理机构规定提供的其他文件。

**Article 11** The applicant shall complete the preparatory work within one year from the date of receipt of the formal application form; if the preparatory work is not completed within the prescribed time limit for a justified reason, an extension of three months may be granted with the approval of the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council. If the preparatory work is still not completed within the extended period, the decision of acceptance made by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall automatically cease to have effect. After the completion of the preparatory work, the applicant shall submit the completed application form together with the following documents to the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council for examination and approval:  
(1) a preparatory report;  
(2) the articles of association of the proposed company;  
(3) the contributors to the proposed company and the amount of their contributions;  
(4) a capital verification certificate issued by a statutory capital verification institution;  
(5) the power of attorney for the proposed principal person in charge of the company;  
(6) a list of senior officers of the proposed company, their curricula vitae and certificates of qualification for the position;  
(7) the business plan and reinsurance plan of the proposed company for the next three years;  
(8) the insurance clauses, premium rates and explanatory notes on the calculation of liability reserves for the types of insurance to be offered within the territory of China;  
(9) information on the business premises and other facilities related to the business of the proposed company;  
(10) if a branch of a foreign insurance company is to be established, the letter of guarantee from its head office for assuming tax and debt liabilities of the branch;  
(11) if an equity joint venture insurance company is to be established, its joint venture contract; and  
(12) other documents required to be submitted by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院保险监督管理机构应当自收到设立外资保险公司完整的正式申请文件之日起60日内，作出批准或者不批准的决定。决定批准的，颁发经营保险业务许可证；决定不批准的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
经批准设立外资保险公司的，申请人凭经营保险业务许可证向市场监督管理部门办理登记，领取营业执照。

**Article 12** The insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall, within 60 days from the date of receipt of the complete formal application documents for establishing a foreign-funded insurance company, make a decision to approve or disapprove the application. If it decides to approve, it shall issue a license for the operation of insurance business; if it decides to disapprove, it shall notify the applicant in writing and give reasons therefor.  
Where the establishment of a foreign-funded insurance company is approved, the applicant shall, by presenting the license for the operation of insurance business, undergo registration with the market supervision and administration department and obtain a business license.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　外资保险公司成立后，应当按照其注册资本或者营运资金总额的20%提取保证金，存入国务院保险监督管理机构指定的银行；保证金除外资保险公司清算时用于清偿债务外，不得动用。

**Article 13** After a foreign-funded insurance company is established, it shall set aside a guarantee fund equal to 20% of its total registered capital or total operating funds and deposit it in a bank designated by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council; the guarantee fund shall not be used except for the settlement of debts upon the liquidation of the foreign-funded insurance company.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　外资保险公司在中国境内设立分支机构，由国务院保险监督管理机构按照有关规定审核批准。

**Article 14** The establishment of branches by a foreign-funded insurance company within the territory of China shall be subject to examination and approval by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with the relevant provisions.

### 第三章　业务范围

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　外资保险公司按照国务院保险监督管理机构核定的业务范围，可以全部或者部分依法经营下列种类的保险业务：  
(一)财产保险业务，包括财产损失保险、责任保险、信用保险等保险业务；  
(二)人身保险业务，包括人寿保险、健康保险、意外伤害保险等保险业务。  
外资保险公司经国务院保险监督管理机构按照有关规定核定，可以在核定的范围内经营大型商业风险保险业务、统括保单保险业务。

**Article 15** A foreign-funded insurance company may, according to the scope of business verified by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, engage in all or part of the following types of insurance business according to law:  
(1) property insurance business, including property loss insurance, liability insurance, credit insurance and other insurance businesses;  
(2) life insurance business, including life insurance, health insurance, accident insurance and other insurance businesses.  
A foreign-funded insurance company may, upon verification by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with the relevant provisions, engage in large commercial risk insurance business and master policy insurance business within the verified scope.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　同一外资保险公司不得同时兼营财产保险业务和人身保险业务。

**Article 16** No same foreign-funded insurance company may concurrently engage in both property insurance business and life insurance business.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　外资保险公司可以依法经营[本条例第十五条](#art-15)规定的保险业务的下列再保险业务：  
(一)分出保险；  
(二)分入保险。

**Article 17** A foreign-funded insurance company may, according to law, engage in the following reinsurance businesses for the insurance business specified in Article 15 of these Regulations:  
(1) outward reinsurance;  
(2) inward reinsurance.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　外资保险公司的具体业务范围、业务地域范围和服务对象范围，由国务院保险监督管理机构按照有关规定核定。外资保险公司只能在核定的范围内从事保险业务活动。

**Article 18** The specific business scope, geographical scope of business and scope of clients of a foreign-funded insurance company shall be verified by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with the relevant provisions. A foreign-funded insurance company may only engage in insurance business activities within the verified scope.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国务院保险监督管理机构有权检查外资保险公司的业务状况、财务状况及资金运用状况，有权要求外资保险公司在规定的期限内提供有关文件、资料和书面报告，有权对违法违规行为依法进行处罚、处理。  
外资保险公司应当接受国务院保险监督管理机构依法进行的监督检查，如实提供有关文件、资料和书面报告，不得拒绝、阻碍、隐瞒。

**Article 19** The insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall have the power to inspect the business conditions, financial conditions and capital utilization conditions of foreign-funded insurance companies, and shall have the power to require foreign-funded insurance companies to provide relevant documents, materials and written reports within a prescribed time limit, and shall have the power to impose penalties and take measures against illegal or irregular acts according to law.  
Foreign-funded insurance companies shall accept the supervision and inspection conducted according to law by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, and shall truthfully provide the relevant documents, materials and written reports, and shall not refuse, obstruct or conceal.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　除经国务院保险监督管理机构批准外，外资保险公司不得与其关联企业进行资产买卖或者其他交易。  
前款所称关联企业，是指与外资保险公司有下列关系之一的企业：  
(一)在股份、出资方面存在控制关系；  
(二)在股份、出资方面同为第三人所控制；  
(三)在利益上具有其他相关联的关系。

**Article 20** Unless approved by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, a foreign-funded insurance company may not conduct asset purchases or sales or other transactions with its affiliated enterprises.  
For the purposes of the preceding paragraph, the term "affiliated enterprises" means enterprises that have any of the following relationships with the foreign-funded insurance company:  
(1) a controlling relationship in terms of shares or capital contributions;  
(2) both are controlled by a third party in terms of shares or capital contributions; or  
(3) any other relationship of association in terms of interests.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　外国保险公司分公司应当于每一会计年度终了后3个月内，将该分公司及其总公司上一年度的财务会计报告报送国务院保险监督管理机构，并予公布。

**Article 21** A branch of a foreign insurance company shall, within three months after the end of each fiscal year, submit the financial and accounting reports of the branch and its head office for the preceding year to the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, and shall publish them.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　外国保险公司分公司的总公司有下列情形之一的，该分公司应当自各该情形发生之日起10日内，将有关情况向国务院保险监督管理机构提交书面报告：  
(一)变更名称、主要负责人或者注册地；  
(二)变更资本金；  
(三)变更持有资本总额或者股份总额10%以上的股东；  
(四)调整业务范围；  
(五)受到所在国家或者地区有关主管当局处罚；  
(六)发生重大亏损；  
(七)分立、合并、解散、依法被撤销或者被宣告破产；  
(八)国务院保险监督管理机构规定的其他情形。

**Article 22** Where any of the following circumstances occurs to the head office of a branch of a foreign insurance company, the branch shall, within 10 days from the date of occurrence of the circumstance, submit a written report to the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council concerning the relevant information:  
(1) change of name, principal person in charge or place of registration;  
(2) change of capital;  
(3) change of shareholders holding 10% or more of the total capital or total shares;  
(4) adjustment of business scope;  
(5) being subject to a penalty imposed by the competent authority of the country or region where it is domiciled;  
(6) incurring significant losses;  
(7) division, merger, dissolution, lawful revocation or declaration of bankruptcy; or  
(8) other circumstances prescribed by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　外国保险公司分公司的总公司解散、依法被撤销或者被宣告破产的，国务院保险监督管理机构应当停止该分公司开展新业务。

**Article 23** Where the head office of a branch of a foreign insurance company is dissolved, lawfully revoked or declared bankrupt, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall cause the branch to cease the conduct of new business.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　外资保险公司经营外汇保险业务的，应当遵守国家有关外汇管理的规定。  
除经国家外汇管理机关批准外，外资保险公司在中国境内经营保险业务的，应当以人民币计价结算。

**Article 24** Where a foreign-funded insurance company operates foreign exchange insurance business, it shall comply with the provisions of the State on foreign exchange control.  
Unless approved by the State foreign exchange control authority, a foreign-funded insurance company that operates insurance business within the territory of China shall price and settle in Renminbi.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本条例规定向国务院保险监督管理机构提交、报送文件、资料和书面报告的，应当提供中文本。

**Article 25** Where these Regulations require that documents, materials and written reports be submitted or reported to the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, the Chinese version shall be provided.

### 第五章　终止与清算

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　外资保险公司因分立、合并或者公司章程规定的解散事由出现，经国务院保险监督管理机构批准后解散。外资保险公司解散的，应当依法成立清算组，进行清算。  
经营人寿保险业务的外资保险公司，除分立、合并外，不得解散。

**Article 26** A foreign-funded insurance company shall be dissolved with the approval of the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council due to division, merger or the occurrence of a ground for dissolution as prescribed in its articles of association. Where a foreign-funded insurance company is dissolved, a liquidation team shall be established according to law to carry out liquidation.  
A foreign-funded insurance company engaging in life insurance business may not be dissolved except for division or merger.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　外资保险公司违反法律、行政法规，被国务院保险监督管理机构吊销经营保险业务许可证的，依法撤销，由国务院保险监督管理机构依法及时组织成立清算组进行清算。

**Article 27** Where a foreign-funded insurance company violates laws or administrative regulations and its license for the operation of insurance business is revoked by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, it shall be revoked according to law, and the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall, according to law, promptly organize the establishment of a liquidation team to carry out liquidation.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　外资保险公司因解散、依法被撤销而清算的，应当自清算组成立之日起60日内在报纸上至少公告3次。公告内容应当经国务院保险监督管理机构核准。

**Article 28** Where a foreign-funded insurance company is liquidated due to dissolution or lawful revocation, it shall make at least three announcements in a newspaper within 60 days from the date of establishment of the liquidation team. The contents of the announcements shall be verified by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　外资保险公司不能支付到期债务，经国务院保险监督管理机构同意，由人民法院依法宣告破产。外资保险公司被宣告破产的，由人民法院组织国务院保险监督管理机构等有关部门和有关人员成立清算组，进行清算。

**Article 29** Where a foreign-funded insurance company is unable to pay its debts as they become due, subject to the consent of the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, it shall be declared bankrupt by a People's Court according to law. Where a foreign-funded insurance company is declared bankrupt, the People's Court shall organize the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council and other relevant departments and persons to establish a liquidation team to carry out liquidation.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　外资保险公司解散、依法被撤销或者被宣告破产的，未清偿债务前，不得将其财产转移至中国境外。

**Article 30** Where a foreign-funded insurance company is dissolved, lawfully revoked or declared bankrupt, it may not transfer its property out of the territory of China before its debts are fully settled.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　违反本条例规定，擅自设立外资保险公司或者非法从事保险业务活动的，由国务院保险监督管理机构予以取缔；依照刑法关于擅自设立金融机构罪、非法经营罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由国务院保险监督管理机构没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足20万元的，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款。

**Article 31** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, establishes a foreign-funded insurance company without authorization or illegally engages in insurance business activities, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall ban such establishment or activities; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of unauthorized establishment of a financial institution, the crime of illegal business operation or other crimes; if the act does not constitute a crime, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains, or if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 200,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 yuan but not more than RMB 1 million yuan.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　外资保险公司违反本条例规定，超出核定的业务范围、业务地域范围或者服务对象范围从事保险业务活动的，依照刑法关于非法经营罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由国务院保险监督管理机构责令改正，责令退还收取的保险费，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足10万元的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正或者造成严重后果的，责令限期停业或者吊销经营保险业务许可证。

**Article 32** Where a foreign-funded insurance company, in violation of these Regulations, engages in insurance business activities beyond the verified business scope, geographical scope of business or scope of clients, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operation or other crimes; if the act does not constitute a crime, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections, order it to refund the insurance premiums collected, confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains, or if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit or if serious consequences are caused, it shall be ordered to suspend business for a specified period of time or its license for the operation of insurance business shall be revoked.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　外资保险公司违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由国务院保险监督管理机构责令改正，处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，可以责令停止接受新业务或者吊销经营保险业务许可证：  
(一)未按照规定提存保证金或者违反规定动用保证金的；  
(二)违反规定与其关联企业从事交易活动的；  
(三)未按照规定补足注册资本或者营运资金的。

**Article 33** Where a foreign-funded insurance company, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it may be ordered to cease accepting new business or its license for the operation of insurance business may be revoked:  
(1) failing to set aside a guarantee fund in accordance with the provisions or using the guarantee fund in violation of the provisions;  
(2) engaging in transactions with its affiliated enterprises in violation of the provisions; or  
(3) failing to replenish the registered capital or operating funds in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　外资保险公司违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由国务院保险监督管理机构责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款：  
(一)未按照规定提交、报送有关文件、资料和书面报告的；  
(二)未按照规定公告的。

**Article 34** Where a foreign-funded insurance company, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) failing to submit or report the relevant documents, materials or written reports in accordance with the provisions; or  
(2) failing to make announcements in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　外资保险公司违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由国务院保险监督管理机构处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款：  
(一)提供虚假的文件、资料和书面报告的；  
(二)拒绝或者阻碍依法监督检查的。

**Article 35** Where a foreign-funded insurance company, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan:  
(1) providing false documents, materials or written reports; or  
(2) refusing or obstructing supervision and inspection conducted according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　外资保险公司违反本条例规定，将其财产转移至中国境外的，由国务院保险监督管理机构责令转回转移的财产，处转移财产金额20%以上等值以下的罚款。

**Article 36** Where a foreign-funded insurance company, in violation of these Regulations, transfers its property out of the territory of China, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to transfer back the property transferred and impose a fine of not less than 20% but not more than the equivalent amount of the property transferred.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　外资保险公司违反中国有关法律、行政法规和本条例规定的，国务院保险监督管理机构可以取消该外资保险公司高级管理人员一定期限直至终身在中国的任职资格。

**Article 37** Where a foreign-funded insurance company violates the relevant laws, administrative regulations of China and the provisions of these Regulations, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council may disqualify the senior officers of the foreign-funded insurance company from holding office in China for a specified period or for life.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　对外资保险公司的管理，本条例未作规定的，适用《中华人民共和国保险法》和其他有关法律、行政法规和国家其他有关规定。

**Article 38** The administration of foreign-funded insurance companies not covered by these Regulations shall be governed by the Insurance Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws, administrative regulations, and other relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区的保险公司在内地(大陆)设立和营业的保险公司，比照适用本条例。

**Article 39** Insurance companies from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region and the Taiwan region that are established and operate in the mainland (the mainland of China) shall apply these Regulations mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　外国保险集团公司可以在中国境内设立外资保险公司，具体管理办法由国务院保险监督管理机构依照本条例的原则制定。

**Article 40** Foreign insurance group companies may establish foreign-funded insurance companies within the territory of China. The specific measures for administration shall be formulated by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with the principles of these Regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　境外金融机构可以入股外资保险公司，具体管理办法由国务院保险监督管理机构制定。

**Article 41** Overseas financial institutions may hold equity in foreign-funded insurance companies. The specific measures for administration shall be formulated by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　本条例自2002年2月1日起施行。

**Article 42** These Regulations shall come into force as of February 1, 2002.
