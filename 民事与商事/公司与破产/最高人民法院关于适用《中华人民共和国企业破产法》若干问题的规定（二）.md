# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国企业破产法》若干问题的规定（二）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2013〕22号  
**公布日期**：2013-09-05  
**生效日期**：2013-09-16

> 最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国企业破产法〉若干问题的规定（二）》已于2013年7月29日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1586次会议通过，现予公布，自2013年9月16日起施行。 2013年9月5日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国企业破产法》 若干问题的规定（二）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　除债务人所有的货币、实物外，债务人依法享有的可以用货币估价并可以依法转让的债权、股权、知识产权、用益物权等财产和财产权益，人民法院均应认定为债务人财产。

**Article 1** In addition to currency and tangible assets owned by the debtor, the People's Court shall recognize as debtor's property the claims, equity interests, intellectual property, usufruct, and other properties and property rights that are legally enjoyed by the debtor and can be appraised in currency and transferred according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　下列财产不应认定为债务人财产：  
（一）债务人基于仓储、保管、承揽、代销、借用、寄存、租赁等合同或者其他法律关系占有、使用的他人财产；  
（二）债务人在所有权保留买卖中尚未取得所有权的财产；  
（三）所有权专属于国家且不得转让的财产；  
（四）其他依照法律、行政法规不属于债务人的财产。

**Article 2** The following property shall not be recognized as debtor's property:  
(a) Property owned or used by the debtor under contracts of warehousing, custodianship, contract work, consignment sales, borrowing, deposit, lease, or other legal relationships;  
(b) Property for which the debtor has not yet acquired ownership in a sale with retention of title;  
(c) Property exclusively owned by the State and not transferable; and  
(d) Other property that is not property of the debtor in accordance with laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　债务人已依法设定担保物权的特定财产，人民法院应当认定为债务人财产。  
对债务人的特定财产在担保物权消灭或者实现担保物权后的剩余部分，在破产程序中可用以清偿破产费用、共益债务和其他破产债权。

**Article 3** The People's Court shall recognize as debtor's property the specific property on which the debtor has legally created a security interest.  
The remaining part of the debtor's specific property after the security interest is extinguished or realized may be used in the bankruptcy proceedings to satisfy the bankruptcy expenses, debts of common interest, and other bankruptcy claims.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　债务人对按份享有所有权的共有财产的相关份额，或者共同享有所有权的共有财产的相应财产权利，以及依法分割共有财产所得部分，人民法院均应认定为债务人财产。  
人民法院宣告债务人破产清算，属于共有财产分割的法定事由。人民法院裁定债务人重整或者和解的，共有财产的分割应当依据物权法第九十九条的规定进行；基于重整或者和解的需要必须分割共有财产，管理人请求分割的，人民法院应予准许。  
因分割共有财产导致其他共有人损害产生的债务，其他共有人请求作为共益债务清偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 4** The People's Court shall recognize as debtor's property the relevant share of the jointly owned property held as tenants in common by the debtor, the corresponding property right of the jointly owned property held as joint tenants, and the portion obtained from the lawful partition of jointly owned property.  
The People's Court's declaration of bankruptcy liquidation of the debtor constitutes a statutory cause for partition of jointly owned property. Where the People's Court orders reorganization or conciliation of the debtor, the partition of jointly owned property shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of Article 99 of the Property Law; where partition of jointly owned property is necessary for the purposes of reorganization or conciliation, and the administrator requests the partition, the People's Court shall grant permission.  
For debts arising from damage caused to other co-owners by the partition of jointly owned property, if the other co-owners request that such debts be satisfied as debts of common interest, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　破产申请受理后，有关债务人财产的执行程序未依照[企业破产法第十九条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-19)的规定中止的，采取执行措施的相关单位应当依法予以纠正。依法执行回转的财产，人民法院应当认定为债务人财产。

**Article 5** After the acceptance of a bankruptcy application, if the enforcement proceedings relating to the debtor's property have not been suspended in accordance with Article 19 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the relevant unit that took enforcement measures shall correct the matter according to law. The People's Court shall recognize as debtor's property the property that is properly returned upon reversal of enforcement.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　破产申请受理后，对于可能因有关利益相关人的行为或者其他原因，影响破产程序依法进行的，受理破产申请的人民法院可以根据管理人的申请或者依职权，对债务人的全部或者部分财产采取保全措施。

**Article 6** After the acceptance of a bankruptcy application, if the conduct of relevant interested persons or other reasons may affect the lawful process of the bankruptcy proceedings, the People's Court that accepted the bankruptcy application may, upon the application of the administrator or on its own authority, take preservation measures against all or part of the debtor's property.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对债务人财产已采取保全措施的相关单位，在知悉人民法院已裁定受理有关债务人的破产申请后，应当依照[企业破产法第十九条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-19)的规定及时解除对债务人财产的保全措施。

**Article 7** If a relevant unit that has taken preservation measures against the debtor's property becomes aware that the People's Court has ruled to accept the bankruptcy application concerning the debtor, it shall promptly lift the preservation measures against the debtor's property in accordance with Article 19 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院受理破产申请后至破产宣告前裁定驳回破产申请，或者依据[企业破产法第一百零八条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-108)的规定裁定终结破产程序的，应当及时通知原已采取保全措施并已依法解除保全措施的单位按照原保全顺位恢复相关保全措施。  
在已依法解除保全的单位恢复保全措施或者表示不再恢复之前，受理破产申请的人民法院不得解除对债务人财产的保全措施。

**Article 8** Where the People's Court, after accepting a bankruptcy application but before declaring bankruptcy, rules to dismiss the bankruptcy application, or orders the termination of bankruptcy proceedings in accordance with Article 108 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, it shall promptly notify the unit that previously took preservation measures and has lawfully lifted them to restore the relevant preservation measures in accordance with the original priority order.  
Before the unit that has lawfully lifted the preservation measures restores them or indicates that it will not restore them, the People's Court that accepted the bankruptcy application shall not lift the preservation measures against the debtor's property.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　管理人依据[企业破产法第三十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-31)和第三十二条的规定提起诉讼，请求撤销涉及债务人财产的相关行为并由相对人返还债务人财产的，人民法院应予支持。  
管理人因过错未依法行使撤销权导致债务人财产不当减损，债权人提起诉讼主张管理人对其损失承担相应赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 9** If the administrator, in accordance with Articles 31 and 32 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, institutes an action requesting the revocation of relevant actions involving the debtor's property and the return of such property to the debtor by the counterparty, the People's Court shall uphold the request.  
If the administrator, due to fault, fails to exercise the right of revocation according to law, resulting in an improper decrease of the debtor's property, and a creditor institutes an action claiming that the administrator shall bear corresponding compensation liability for its losses, the People's Court shall uphold such a claim.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　债务人经过行政清理程序转入破产程序的，[企业破产法第三十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-31)和第三十二条规定的可撤销行为的起算点，为行政监管机构作出撤销决定之日。  
债务人经过强制清算程序转入破产程序的，[企业破产法第三十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-31)和第三十二条规定的可撤销行为的起算点，为人民法院裁定受理强制清算申请之日。

**Article 10** Where a debtor enters bankruptcy proceedings after undergoing administrative clearance procedures, the starting point for the calculation of the revocable acts under Articles 31 and 32 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law shall be the date on which the administrative regulatory authority makes the decision on revocation.  
Where a debtor enters bankruptcy proceedings after undergoing compulsory liquidation procedures, the starting point for the calculation of the revocable acts under Articles 31 and 32 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law shall be the date on which the People's Court rules to accept the compulsory liquidation application.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院根据管理人的请求撤销涉及债务人财产的以明显不合理价格进行的交易的，买卖双方应当依法返还从对方获取的财产或者价款。  
因撤销该交易，对于债务人应返还受让人已支付价款所产生的债务，受让人请求作为共益债务清偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 11** Where the People's Court, upon the request of the administrator, revokes a transaction involving the debtor's property at a manifestly unreasonable price, the buyer and seller shall return the property or price obtained from the other party according to law.  
For debts arising from the debtor's obligation to return the price that has been paid to the transferee due to the revocation of such transaction, if the transferee requests that such debts be satisfied as debts of common interest, the People's Court shall uphold such a request.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　破产申请受理前一年内债务人提前清偿的未到期债务，在破产申请受理前已经到期，管理人请求撤销该清偿行为的，人民法院不予支持。但是，该清偿行为发生在破产申请受理前六个月内且债务人有[企业破产法第二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-2)第一款规定情形的除外。

**Article 12** Where the debtor, within one year before the acceptance of a bankruptcy application, makes an advance repayment of a debt not yet due, and such debt becomes due before the acceptance of the bankruptcy application, the People's Court shall not uphold the administrator's request to revoke such repayment. However, this shall not apply if the repayment occurs within the six months before the acceptance of the bankruptcy application and the debtor is in the circumstances specified in the first paragraph of Article 2 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　破产申请受理后，管理人未依据[企业破产法第三十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-31)的规定请求撤销债务人无偿转让财产、以明显不合理价格交易、放弃债权行为的，债权人依据合同法第七十四条等规定提起诉讼，请求撤销债务人上述行为并将因此追回的财产归入债务人财产的，人民法院应予受理。  
相对人以债权人行使撤销权的范围超出债权人的债权抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 13** After the acceptance of a bankruptcy application, if the administrator fails to request, in accordance with Article 31 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the revocation of the debtor's acts of gratuitously transferring property, trading at a manifestly unreasonable price, or waiving claims, and a creditor institutes an action in accordance with Article 74 of the Contract Law requesting the revocation of the aforesaid acts of the debtor and the inclusion of the property so recovered in the debtor's property, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
If the counterparty defends on the ground that the scope of the creditor's right of revocation exceeds the creditor's claims, the People's Court shall not uphold such defense.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　债务人对以自有财产设定担保物权的债权进行的个别清偿，管理人依据[企业破产法第三十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-32)的规定请求撤销的，人民法院不予支持。但是，债务清偿时担保财产的价值低于债权额的除外。

**Article 14** If the debtor makes a specific repayment to a creditor whose claim is secured by a security interest created on the debtor's own property, and the administrator requests the revocation of such repayment in accordance with Article 32 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the People's Court shall not uphold the request. However, this shall not apply if the value of the secured property at the time of repayment is less than the amount of the claim.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　债务人经诉讼、仲裁、执行程序对债权人进行的个别清偿，管理人依据[企业破产法第三十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-32)的规定请求撤销的，人民法院不予支持。但是，债务人与债权人恶意串通损害其他债权人利益的除外。

**Article 15** If the debtor makes a specific repayment to a creditor through proceedings of litigation, arbitration, or enforcement, and the administrator requests the revocation of such repayment in accordance with Article 32 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the People's Court shall not uphold the request. However, this shall not apply where the debtor and the creditor have maliciously colluded to harm the interests of other creditors.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　债务人对债权人进行的以下个别清偿，管理人依据[企业破产法第三十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-32)的规定请求撤销的，人民法院不予支持：  
（一）债务人为维系基本生产需要而支付水费、电费等的；  
（二）债务人支付劳动报酬、人身损害赔偿金的；  
（三）使债务人财产受益的其他个别清偿。

**Article 16** The People's Court shall not uphold the administrator's request, made in accordance with Article 32 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, to revoke the following specific repayments made by the debtor to a creditor:  
(a) Payments of water, electricity, and similar charges made by the debtor to maintain basic production needs;  
(b) Payments of labor remuneration and compensation for personal injury; and  
(c) Other specific repayments that benefit the debtor's property.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　管理人依据[企业破产法第三十三条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-33)的规定提起诉讼，主张被隐匿、转移财产的实际占有人返还债务人财产，或者主张债务人虚构债务或者承认不真实债务的行为无效并返还债务人财产的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 17** If the administrator, in accordance with Article 33 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, institutes an action claiming the return of the concealed or transferred property by the actual possessor to the debtor, or claiming that the debtor's act of fabricating debts or acknowledging false debts is void and claiming the return of property to the debtor, the People's Court shall uphold the claim.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　管理人代表债务人依据[企业破产法第一百二十八条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-128)的规定，以债务人的法定代表人和其他直接责任人员对所涉债务人财产的相关行为存在故意或者重大过失，造成债务人财产损失为由提起诉讼，主张上述责任人员承担相应赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 18** If the administrator, representing the debtor and in accordance with Article 128 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, institutes an action on the ground that the legal representative and other directly responsible persons of the debtor have intentionally or grossly negligently committed relevant acts involving the debtor's property, thereby causing losses to the debtor's property, and claims that the aforesaid responsible persons shall bear corresponding compensation liability, the People's Court shall uphold the claim.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　债务人对外享有债权的诉讼时效，自人民法院受理破产申请之日起中断。  
债务人无正当理由未对其到期债权及时行使权利，导致其对外债权在破产申请受理前一年内超过诉讼时效期间的，人民法院受理破产申请之日起重新计算上述债权的诉讼时效期间。

**Article 19** The limitation of action for a claim owed to the debtor shall be interrupted from the date on which the People's Court accepts the bankruptcy application.  
If the debtor, without justifiable grounds, fails to timely exercise its right over its due claims, resulting in such claims exceeding the limitation period within one year before the acceptance of the bankruptcy application, the limitation period for such claims shall be recalculated from the date of acceptance of the bankruptcy application.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　管理人代表债务人提起诉讼，主张出资人向债务人依法缴付未履行的出资或者返还抽逃的出资本息，出资人以认缴出资尚未届至公司章程规定的缴纳期限或者违反出资义务已经超过诉讼时效为由抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。  
管理人依据公司法的相关规定代表债务人提起诉讼，主张公司的发起人和负有监督股东履行出资义务的董事、高级管理人员，或者协助抽逃出资的其他股东、董事、高级管理人员、实际控制人等，对股东违反出资义务或者抽逃出资承担相应责任，并将财产归入债务人财产的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 20** If the administrator, representing the debtor, institutes an action claiming that a capital contributor shall lawfully pay its unfulfilled capital contribution to the debtor or return the principal and interest of the withdrawn capital contribution, and the capital contributor defends on the ground that the contribution period has not yet expired as stipulated in the company's articles of association or that the limitation period for breaching the capital contribution obligation has already expired, the People's Court shall not uphold such defense.  
If the administrator, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Company Law, institutes an action representing the debtor, claiming that the promoters of the company and the directors and senior officers who have the duty to supervise the capital contribution by shareholders, or other shareholders, directors, senior officers, actual controllers, etc., who have assisted in the withdrawal of capital contributions, shall bear corresponding liability for the shareholders' breach of capital contribution obligations or withdrawal of capital contributions, and the property shall be included in the debtor's property, the People's Court shall uphold the claim.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　破产申请受理前，债权人就债务人财产提起下列诉讼，破产申请受理时案件尚未审结的，人民法院应当中止审理：  
（一）主张次债务人代替债务人直接向其偿还债务的；  
（二）主张债务人的出资人、发起人和负有监督股东履行出资义务的董事、高级管理人员，或者协助抽逃出资的其他股东、董事、高级管理人员、实际控制人等直接向其承担出资不实或者抽逃出资责任的；  
（三）以债务人的股东与债务人法人人格严重混同为由，主张债务人的股东直接向其偿还债务人对其所负债务的；  
（四）其他就债务人财产提起的个别清偿诉讼。  
债务人破产宣告后，人民法院应当依照[企业破产法第四十四条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-44)的规定判决驳回债权人的诉讼请求。但是，债权人一审中变更其诉讼请求为追收的相关财产归入债务人财产的除外。  
债务人破产宣告前，人民法院依据[企业破产法第十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-12)或者第一百零八条的规定裁定驳回破产申请或者终结破产程序的，上述中止审理的案件应当依法恢复审理。

**Article 21** Before the acceptance of a bankruptcy application, if a creditor has instituted any of the following actions against the debtor's property and the case has not yet been concluded at the time of acceptance of the bankruptcy application, the People's Court shall suspend the trial:  
(a) Claiming that a secondary obligor shall directly repay the debt to the creditor on behalf of the debtor;  
(b) Claiming that the debtor's capital contributors, promoters, directors and senior officers having the duty to supervise the capital contribution by shareholders, or other shareholders, directors, senior officers, actual controllers, etc., who have assisted in the withdrawal of capital contributions, shall directly bear liability for false capital contribution or withdrawal of capital contributions to the creditor;  
(c) Claiming that, on the ground that the debtor's shareholders and the debtor's legal personality are severely confused, the debtor's shareholders shall directly repay the debts owed by the debtor to the creditor; or  
(d) Other individual repayment actions brought against the debtor's property.  
After the debtor is declared bankrupt, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 44 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, rule to dismiss the creditor's claim. However, this shall not apply if the creditor changes its claim in the first instance to request that the recovered related property be included in the debtor's property.  
Before the debtor is declared bankrupt, if the People's Court rules to dismiss the bankruptcy application or terminate the bankruptcy proceedings in accordance with Article 12 or Article 108 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the above-suspended cases shall be resumed according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　破产申请受理前，债权人就债务人财产向人民法院提起[本规定第二十一条](#art-21)第一款所列诉讼，人民法院已经作出生效民事判决书或者调解书但尚未执行完毕的，破产申请受理后，相关执行行为应当依据[企业破产法第十九条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-19)的规定中止，债权人应当依法向管理人申报相关债权。

**Article 22** Before the acceptance of a bankruptcy application, if a creditor institutes against the debtor's property any of the actions listed in the first paragraph of Article 21 of these Provisions before the People's Court, and the People's Court has already rendered an effective civil judgment or mediation statement but enforcement has not yet been completed, after the acceptance of the bankruptcy application, the relevant enforcement action shall be suspended in accordance with Article 19 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, and the creditor shall declare the relevant claim to the administrator according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　破产申请受理后，债权人就债务人财产向人民法院提起[本规定第二十一条](#art-21)第一款所列诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。  
债权人通过债权人会议或者债权人委员会，要求管理人依法向次债务人、债务人的出资人等追收债务人财产，管理人无正当理由拒绝追收，债权人会议依据[企业破产法第二十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-22)的规定，申请人民法院更换管理人的，人民法院应予支持。  
管理人不予追收，个别债权人代表全体债权人提起相关诉讼，主张次债务人或者债务人的出资人等向债务人清偿或者返还债务人财产，或者依法申请合并破产的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 23** After the acceptance of a bankruptcy application, if a creditor institutes against the debtor's property any of the actions listed in the first paragraph of Article 21 of these Provisions before the People's Court, the People's Court shall not accept the case.  
If a creditor, through the creditors' meeting or the creditors' committee, requests the administrator to recover the debtor's property from a secondary obligor, the debtor's capital contributor, etc., according to law, and the administrator refuses to do so without justifiable grounds, and the creditors' meeting applies to the People's Court to replace the administrator in accordance with Article 22 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the People's Court shall uphold the application.  
If the administrator fails to recover, and an individual creditor institutes a relevant action on behalf of all creditors, claiming that the secondary obligor or the debtor's capital contributor, etc., shall repay or return the debtor's property to the debtor, or applying for consolidated bankruptcy according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　债务人有[企业破产法第二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-2)第一款规定的情形时，债务人的董事、监事和高级管理人员利用职权获取的以下收入，人民法院应当认定为[企业破产法第三十六条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-36)规定的非正常收入：  
（一）绩效奖金；  
（二）普遍拖欠职工工资情况下获取的工资性收入；  
（三）其他非正常收入。  
债务人的董事、监事和高级管理人员拒不向管理人返还上述债务人财产，管理人主张上述人员予以返还的，人民法院应予支持。  
债务人的董事、监事和高级管理人员因返还第一款第（一）项、第（三）项非正常收入形成的债权，可以作为普通破产债权清偿。因返还第一款第（二）项非正常收入形成的债权，依据[企业破产法第一百一十三条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-113)第三款的规定，按照该企业职工平均工资计算的部分作为拖欠职工工资清偿；高出该企业职工平均工资计算的部分，可以作为普通破产债权清偿。

**Article 24** When the debtor is in the circumstances specified in the first paragraph of Article 2 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the following income obtained by the directors, supervisors, and senior officers of the debtor by taking advantage of their positions shall be recognized by the People's Court as abnormal income as stipulated in Article 36 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law:  
(a) Performance bonuses;  
(b) Salary income obtained under circumstances where wage arrears are widespread among employees; and  
(c) Other abnormal income.  
If the directors, supervisors, and senior officers of the debtor refuse to return the above debtor's property to the administrator, and the administrator claims that the above persons shall return it, the People's Court shall uphold the claim.  
The claims arising from the return of abnormal income as specified in items (a) and (c) of the first paragraph by the debtor's directors, supervisors, and senior officers may be satisfied as ordinary bankruptcy claims. The claims arising from the return of abnormal income as specified in item (b) of the first paragraph shall, in accordance with the third paragraph of Article 113 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, be satisfied as overdue wages for the part calculated based on the average wage of the enterprise's employees; the part exceeding the calculation based on the average wage of the enterprise's employees may be satisfied as ordinary bankruptcy claims.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　管理人拟通过清偿债务或者提供担保取回质物、留置物，或者与质权人、留置权人协议以质物、留置物折价清偿债务等方式，进行对债权人利益有重大影响的财产处分行为的，应当及时报告债权人委员会。未设立债权人委员会的，管理人应当及时报告人民法院。

**Article 25** Where an administrator intends to conduct an act of disposition of property that has a material impact on the interests of creditors by, among others, discharging debts or providing security to retrieve pledged property or retained property, or entering into an agreement with the pledgee or lien holder to offset the debts with the pledged property or retained property at a discounted price, the administrator shall promptly report to the creditors committee. Where no creditors committee is established, the administrator shall promptly report to the People's Court.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　权利人依据[企业破产法第三十八条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-38)的规定行使取回权，应当在破产财产变价方案或者和解协议、重整计划草案提交债权人会议表决前向管理人提出。权利人在上述期限后主张取回相关财产的，应当承担延迟行使取回权增加的相关费用。

**Article 26** A person entitled to exercise the right of taking back property in accordance with Article 38 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law shall assert such right to the administrator before the plan for the realization of the bankruptcy property, the conciliation agreement, or the draft reorganization plan is submitted to the creditors' meeting for voting. If the entitled person asserts the right of taking back the relevant property after the above-mentioned period, it shall bear the additional costs incurred due to the delay in exercising such right.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　权利人依据[企业破产法第三十八条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-38)的规定向管理人主张取回相关财产，管理人不予认可，权利人以债务人为被告向人民法院提起诉讼请求行使取回权的，人民法院应予受理。  
权利人依据人民法院或者仲裁机关的相关生效法律文书向管理人主张取回所涉争议财产，管理人以生效法律文书错误为由拒绝其行使取回权的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 27** If a person entitled to property asserts the right of taking back the relevant property from the administrator in accordance with Article 38 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, and the administrator does not recognize such right, and the entitled person institutes an action with the debtor as the defendant before the People's Court requesting the exercise of the right of taking back, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
If the entitled person, in accordance with relevant effective legal documents of the People's Court or arbitration institution, asserts the right of taking back the disputed property in question from the administrator, and the administrator refuses to allow the exercise of such right on the ground that the effective legal document is erroneous, the People's Court shall not uphold such refusal.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　权利人行使取回权时未依法向管理人支付相关的加工费、保管费、托运费、委托费、代销费等费用，管理人拒绝其取回相关财产的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 28** If an entitled person, when exercising the right of taking back, fails to pay the relevant processing fees, custody fees, freight charges, commission fees, consignment sale fees, and other expenses to the administrator according to law, and the administrator refuses to allow the taking back of the relevant property, the People's Court shall uphold the administrator's refusal.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　对债务人占有的权属不清的鲜活易腐等不易保管的财产或者不及时变现价值将严重贬损的财产，管理人及时变价并提存变价款后，有关权利人就该变价款行使取回权的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 29** With respect to perishable or difficult-to-preserve property in the possession of the debtor, such as fresh or live goods with unclear ownership, or property whose value would seriously depreciate if not promptly realized, if the administrator promptly disposes of such property and deposits the proceeds, and the relevant entitled person exercises the right of taking back against such proceeds, the People's Court shall uphold such exercise.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　债务人占有的他人财产被违法转让给第三人，依据物权法第一百零六条的规定第三人已善意取得财产所有权，原权利人无法取回该财产的，人民法院应当按照以下规定处理：  
（一）转让行为发生在破产申请受理前的，原权利人因财产损失形成的债权，作为普通破产债权清偿；  
（二）转让行为发生在破产申请受理后的，因管理人或者相关人员执行职务导致原权利人损害产生的债务，作为共益债务清偿。

**Article 30** If another person's property in the possession of the debtor is illegally transferred to a third party, and the third party has acquired ownership in good faith in accordance with Article 106 of the Property Law, so that the original owner cannot recover the property, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with the following provisions:  
(a) If the transfer occurs before the acceptance of the bankruptcy application, the claim arising from the original owner's property loss shall be satisfied as an ordinary bankruptcy claim;  
(b) If the transfer occurs after the acceptance of the bankruptcy application, the debt arising from damage to the original owner caused by the administrator or relevant persons in performing their duties shall be satisfied as a debt of common interest.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　债务人占有的他人财产被违法转让给第三人，第三人已向债务人支付了转让价款，但依据物权法第一百零六条的规定未取得财产所有权，原权利人依法追回转让财产的，对因第三人已支付对价而产生的债务，人民法院应当按照以下规定处理：  
（一）转让行为发生在破产申请受理前的，作为普通破产债权清偿；  
（二）转让行为发生在破产申请受理后的，作为共益债务清偿。

**Article 31** If another person's property in the possession of the debtor is illegally transferred to a third party, and the third party has paid the transfer price to the debtor but has not acquired ownership in accordance with Article 106 of the Property Law, and the original owner lawfully recovers the transferred property, the People's Court shall handle the debt arising from the third party's payment of consideration in accordance with the following provisions:  
(a) If the transfer occurs before the acceptance of the bankruptcy application, it shall be satisfied as an ordinary bankruptcy claim;  
(b) If the transfer occurs after the acceptance of the bankruptcy application, it shall be satisfied as a debt of common interest.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　债务人占有的他人财产毁损、灭失，因此获得的保险金、赔偿金、代偿物尚未交付给债务人，或者代偿物虽已交付给债务人但能与债务人财产予以区分的，权利人主张取回就此获得的保险金、赔偿金、代偿物的，人民法院应予支持。  
保险金、赔偿金已经交付给债务人，或者代偿物已经交付给债务人且不能与债务人财产予以区分的，人民法院应当按照以下规定处理：  
（一）财产毁损、灭失发生在破产申请受理前的，权利人因财产损失形成的债权，作为普通破产债权清偿；  
（二）财产毁损、灭失发生在破产申请受理后的，因管理人或者相关人员执行职务导致权利人损害产生的债务，作为共益债务清偿。  
债务人占有的他人财产毁损、灭失，没有获得相应的保险金、赔偿金、代偿物，或者保险金、赔偿物、代偿物不足以弥补其损失的部分，人民法院应当按照本条第二款的规定处理。

**Article 32** If another person's property in the possession of the debtor is damaged or lost, and the insurance proceeds, compensation, or substitute property obtained therefrom have not yet been delivered to the debtor, or although the substitute property has been delivered to the debtor, it can be distinguished from the debtor's property, and the entitled person claims the right of taking back against the insurance proceeds, compensation, or substitute property so obtained, the People's Court shall uphold the claim.  
If the insurance proceeds or compensation have been delivered to the debtor, or the substitute property has been delivered to the debtor and cannot be distinguished from the debtor's property, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with the following provisions:  
(a) If the damage or loss occurs before the acceptance of the bankruptcy application, the claim arising from the entitled person's property loss shall be satisfied as an ordinary bankruptcy claim;  
(b) If the damage or loss occurs after the acceptance of the bankruptcy application, the debt arising from damage to the entitled person caused by the administrator or relevant persons in performing their duties shall be satisfied as a debt of common interest.  
If another person's property in the possession of the debtor is damaged or lost, and no corresponding insurance proceeds, compensation, or substitute property have been obtained, or the insurance proceeds, compensation, or substitute property are insufficient to cover the loss, the People's Court shall handle the insufficient portion in accordance with the provisions of the second paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　管理人或者相关人员在执行职务过程中，因故意或者重大过失不当转让他人财产或者造成他人财产毁损、灭失，导致他人损害产生的债务作为共益债务，由债务人财产随时清偿不足弥补损失，权利人向管理人或者相关人员主张承担补充赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
上述债务作为共益债务由债务人财产随时清偿后，债权人以管理人或者相关人员执行职务不当导致债务人财产减少给其造成损失为由提起诉讼，主张管理人或者相关人员承担相应赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 33** If, in the course of performing their duties, the administrator or relevant persons improperly transfer another person's property or cause damage or loss to another person's property due to intent or gross negligence, resulting in damage to another person, the debt arising therefrom shall be satisfied as a debt of common interest from the debtor's property. If the satisfaction from the debtor's property is insufficient to cover the loss, and the entitled person claims that the administrator or relevant persons shall bear supplementary compensation liability, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.  
After the above debt is satisfied as a debt of common interest from the debtor's property from time to time, if a creditor institutes an action on the ground that the improper performance of duties by the administrator or relevant persons has caused the debtor's property to decrease, thereby causing losses to the creditor, and claims that the administrator or relevant persons shall bear corresponding compensation liability, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　买卖合同双方当事人在合同中约定标的物所有权保留，在标的物所有权未依法转移给买受人前，一方当事人破产的，该买卖合同属于双方均未履行完毕的合同，管理人有权依据[企业破产法第十八条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-18)的规定决定解除或者继续履行合同。

**Article 34** If the parties to a sales contract stipulate retention of title in the contract, and before the ownership of the subject matter is lawfully transferred to the buyer, one party becomes bankrupt, the sales contract shall be deemed a contract that neither party has fully performed. The administrator shall have the right to decide to rescind or continue to perform the contract in accordance with Article 18 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　出卖人破产，其管理人决定继续履行所有权保留买卖合同的，买受人应当按照原买卖合同的约定支付价款或者履行其他义务。  
买受人未依约支付价款或者履行完毕其他义务，或者将标的物出卖、出质或者作出其他不当处分，给出卖人造成损害，出卖人管理人依法主张取回标的物的，人民法院应予支持。但是，买受人已经支付标的物总价款百分之七十五以上或者第三人善意取得标的物所有权或者其他物权的除外。  
因本条第二款规定未能取回标的物，出卖人管理人依法主张买受人继续支付价款、履行完毕其他义务，以及承担相应赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 35** If the seller becomes bankrupt and its administrator decides to continue performing the sales contract with retention of title, the buyer shall pay the price or perform other obligations in accordance with the terms of the original sales contract.  
If the buyer fails to pay the price or perform other obligations in accordance with the contract, or sells, pledges, or otherwise improperly disposes of the subject matter, causing damage to the seller, and the seller's administrator claims to take back the subject matter according to law, the People's Court shall uphold the claim. However, this shall not apply if the buyer has already paid seventy-five percent or more of the total price of the subject matter, or a third party has acquired ownership or other real rights in the subject matter in good faith.  
If the subject matter cannot be taken back due to the circumstances specified in the second paragraph, and the seller's administrator claims that the buyer shall continue to pay the price, perform the remaining other obligations, and bear corresponding compensation liability according to law, the People's Court shall uphold the claim.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　出卖人破产，其管理人决定解除所有权保留买卖合同，并依据[企业破产法第十七条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-17)的规定要求买受人向其交付买卖标的物的，人民法院应予支持。  
买受人以其不存在未依约支付价款或者履行完毕其他义务，或者将标的物出卖、出质或者作出其他不当处分情形抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。  
买受人依法履行合同义务并依据本条第一款将买卖标的物交付出卖人管理人后，买受人已支付价款损失形成的债权作为共益债务清偿。但是，买受人违反合同约定，出卖人管理人主张上述债权作为普通破产债权清偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 36** If the seller becomes bankrupt and its administrator decides to rescind the sales contract with retention of title, and requests the buyer to deliver the subject matter of the sale to it in accordance with Article 17 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the People's Court shall uphold the request.  
If the buyer defends on the ground that it does not have the circumstances of failing to pay the price or perform other obligations in accordance with the contract, or selling, pledging, or otherwise improperly disposing of the subject matter, the People's Court shall not uphold such defense.  
After the buyer has lawfully performed its contractual obligations and delivered the subject matter of the sale to the seller's administrator in accordance with the first paragraph, the claim arising from the buyer's loss of the paid price shall be satisfied as a debt of common interest. However, if the buyer violates the contract and the seller's administrator claims that the above claim shall be satisfied as an ordinary bankruptcy claim, the People's Court shall uphold the claim.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　买受人破产，其管理人决定继续履行所有权保留买卖合同的，原买卖合同中约定的买受人支付价款或者履行其他义务的期限在破产申请受理时视为到期，买受人管理人应当及时向出卖人支付价款或者履行其他义务。  
买受人管理人无正当理由未及时支付价款或者履行完毕其他义务，或者将标的物出卖、出质或者作出其他不当处分，给出卖人造成损害，出卖人依据合同法第一百三十四条等规定主张取回标的物的，人民法院应予支持。但是，买受人已支付标的物总价款百分之七十五以上或者第三人善意取得标的物所有权或者其他物权的除外。  
因本条第二款规定未能取回标的物，出卖人依法主张买受人继续支付价款、履行完毕其他义务，以及承担相应赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。对因买受人未支付价款或者未履行完毕其他义务，以及买受人管理人将标的物出卖、出质或者作出其他不当处分导致出卖人损害产生的债务，出卖人主张作为共益债务清偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 37** If the buyer becomes bankrupt and its administrator decides to continue performing the sales contract with retention of title, the time limit for the buyer to pay the price or perform other obligations stipulated in the original sales contract shall be deemed to have expired upon acceptance of the bankruptcy application, and the buyer's administrator shall promptly pay the price or perform other obligations to the seller.  
If the buyer's administrator fails to pay the price or perform other obligations without justifiable grounds, or sells, pledges, or otherwise improperly disposes of the subject matter, causing damage to the seller, and the seller claims to take back the subject matter in accordance with Article 134 of the Contract Law and other provisions, the People's Court shall uphold the claim. However, this shall not apply if the buyer has paid seventy-five percent or more of the total price of the subject matter, or a third party has acquired ownership or other real rights in the subject matter in good faith.  
If the subject matter cannot be taken back due to the circumstances specified in the second paragraph, and the seller claims that the buyer shall continue to pay the price, perform the remaining other obligations, and bear corresponding compensation liability according to law, the People's Court shall uphold the claim. With respect to the debt arising from the buyer's failure to pay the price or failure to perform other obligations, or from the buyer's administrator's sale, pledge, or other improper disposal of the subject matter causing damage to the seller, if the seller claims that such debt be satisfied as a debt of common interest, the People's Court shall uphold the claim.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　买受人破产，其管理人决定解除所有权保留买卖合同，出卖人依据[企业破产法第三十八条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-38)的规定主张取回买卖标的物的，人民法院应予支持。  
出卖人取回买卖标的物，买受人管理人主张出卖人返还已支付价款的，人民法院应予支持。取回的标的物价值明显减少给出卖人造成损失的，出卖人可从买受人已支付价款中优先予以抵扣后，将剩余部分返还给买受人；对买受人已支付价款不足以弥补出卖人标的物价值减损损失形成的债权，出卖人主张作为共益债务清偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 38** If the buyer becomes bankrupt and its administrator decides to rescind the sales contract with retention of title, and the seller claims to take back the subject matter of the sale in accordance with Article 38 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the People's Court shall uphold the claim.  
If the seller takes back the subject matter of the sale, and the buyer's administrator claims that the seller shall return the price already paid, the People's Court shall uphold the claim. If the value of the taken-back subject matter is significantly reduced, causing loss to the seller, the seller may deduct such loss from the price already paid by the buyer and return the remaining amount to the buyer; if the price already paid by the buyer is insufficient to cover the loss from the reduction in value of the subject matter, and the seller claims that the claim arising therefrom be satisfied as a debt of common interest, the People's Court shall uphold the claim.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　出卖人依据[企业破产法第三十九条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-39)的规定，通过通知承运人或者实际占有人中止运输、返还货物、变更到达地，或者将货物交给其他收货人等方式，对在运途中标的物主张了取回权但未能实现，或者在货物未达管理人前已向管理人主张取回在运途中标的物，在买卖标的物到达管理人后，出卖人向管理人主张取回的，管理人应予准许。  
出卖人对在运途中标的物未及时行使取回权，在买卖标的物到达管理人后向管理人行使在运途中标的物取回权的，管理人不应准许。

**Article 39** If the seller, in accordance with Article 39 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, asserts the right of taking back the subject matter in transit but fails to realize it through means such as notifying the carrier or actual possessor to suspend transportation, return the goods, change the place of destination, or deliver the goods to another consignee, or has asserted the right of taking back the subject matter in transit to the administrator before the goods arrive, and after the subject matter of the sale arrives at the administrator, the seller asserts the right of taking back to the administrator, the administrator shall allow such taking back.  
If the seller fails to timely exercise the right of taking back the subject matter in transit and, after the subject matter of the sale arrives at the administrator, asserts the right of taking back the subject matter in transit to the administrator, the administrator shall not allow such taking back.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　债务人重整期间，权利人要求取回债务人合法占有的权利人的财产，不符合双方事先约定条件的，人民法院不予支持。但是，因管理人或者自行管理的债务人违反约定，可能导致取回物被转让、毁损、灭失或者价值明显减少的除外。

**Article 40** During the reorganization period of the debtor, if an entitled person requests the taking back of property lawfully possessed by the debtor, but the request does not meet the conditions previously agreed by the parties, the People's Court shall not uphold the request. However, this shall not apply if the administrator or the debtor under self-administration violates the agreement, which may lead to the transfer, damage, or loss of the property subject to taking back, or a significant reduction in its value.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　债权人依据[企业破产法第四十条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-40)的规定行使抵销权，应当向管理人提出抵销主张。  
管理人不得主动抵销债务人与债权人的互负债务，但抵销使债务人财产受益的除外。

**Article 41** A creditor who exercises the right of set-off in accordance with Article 40 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law shall assert the set-off claim to the administrator.  
The administrator shall not proactively set off the debts owed mutually between the debtor and the creditor, unless the set-off benefits the debtor's property.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　管理人收到债权人提出的主张债务抵销的通知后，经审查无异议的，抵销自管理人收到通知之日起生效。  
管理人对抵销主张有异议的，应当在约定的异议期限内或者自收到主张债务抵销的通知之日起三个月内向人民法院提起诉讼。无正当理由逾期提起的，人民法院不予支持。  
人民法院判决驳回管理人提起的抵销无效诉讼请求的，该抵销自管理人收到主张债务抵销的通知之日起生效。

**Article 42** After receiving a notice from a creditor asserting the set-off of debts, if the administrator finds no objection upon examination, the set-off shall take effect from the date the administrator receives the notice.  
If the administrator has an objection to the set-off claim, it shall institute an action in the People's Court within the agreed objection period or within three months from the date of receiving the notice asserting the set-off of debts. If the administrator fails to do so without justifiable grounds, the People's Court shall not uphold such lawsuit.  
If the People's Court rules to dismiss the administrator's action for invalidity of set-off, the set-off shall take effect from the date the administrator receives the notice asserting the set-off of debts.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　债权人主张抵销，管理人以下列理由提出异议的，人民法院不予支持：  
（一）破产申请受理时，债务人对债权人负有的债务尚未到期；  
（二）破产申请受理时，债权人对债务人负有的债务尚未到期；  
（三）双方互负债务标的物种类、品质不同。

**Article 43** If a creditor asserts set-off, and the administrator raises an objection on any of the following grounds, the People's Court shall not uphold the objection:  
(a) At the time of acceptance of the bankruptcy application, the debt owed by the debtor to the creditor was not yet due;  
(b) At the time of acceptance of the bankruptcy application, the debt owed by the creditor to the debtor was not yet due;  
(c) The types or quality of the subject matters of the mutual debts between the parties differ.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　破产申请受理前六个月内，债务人有[企业破产法第二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-2)第一款规定的情形，债务人与个别债权人以抵销方式对个别债权人清偿，其抵销的债权债务属于[企业破产法第四十条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-40)第（二）、（三）项规定的情形之一，管理人在破产申请受理之日起三个月内向人民法院提起诉讼，主张该抵销无效的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 44** If, within the six months before the acceptance of the bankruptcy application, the debtor was in the circumstances specified in the first paragraph of Article 2 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, and the debtor made a repayment to an individual creditor by way of set-off, and the debts and claims subject to set-off fall under any of the circumstances specified in subparagraphs (2) and (3) of Article 40 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, and the administrator institutes an action in the People's Court within three months from the date of acceptance of the bankruptcy application claiming that the set-off is invalid, the People's Court shall uphold the claim.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　[企业破产法第四十条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-40)所列不得抵销情形的债权人，主张以其对债务人特定财产享有优先受偿权的债权，与债务人对其不享有优先受偿权的债权抵销，债务人管理人以抵销存在[企业破产法第四十条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-40)规定的情形提出异议的，人民法院不予支持。但是，用以抵销的债权大于债权人享有优先受偿权财产价值的除外。

**Article 45** If a creditor who falls under the circumstances where set-off is not allowed as listed in Article 40 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law asserts the set-off of its claim with priority right to be satisfied against the debtor's specific property against the debtor's claim that does not have priority right, and the debtor's administrator raises an objection on the ground that the set-off falls under the circumstances specified in Article 40 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the People's Court shall not uphold the objection. However, this shall not apply if the set-off claim exceeds the value of the property over which the creditor enjoys the priority right.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　债务人的股东主张以下列债务与债务人对其负有的债务抵销，债务人管理人提出异议的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）债务人股东因欠缴债务人的出资或者抽逃出资对债务人所负的债务；  
（二）债务人股东滥用股东权利或者关联关系损害公司利益对债务人所负的债务。

**Article 46** If a shareholder of the debtor asserts the set-off of the following debts against the debts owed by the debtor to it, and the debtor's administrator raises an objection, the People's Court shall uphold the objection:  
(a) Debts owed by the debtor's shareholder to the debtor due to unpaid capital contributions or withdrawal of capital contributions;  
(b) Debts owed by the debtor's shareholder to the debtor due to abuse of shareholder rights or affiliated relationships causing damage to the company's interests.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　人民法院受理破产申请后，当事人提起的有关债务人的民事诉讼案件，应当依据[企业破产法第二十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-21)的规定，由受理破产申请的人民法院管辖。  
受理破产申请的人民法院管辖的有关债务人的第一审民事案件，可以依据[民事诉讼法第三十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-38)的规定，由上级人民法院提审，或者报请上级人民法院批准后交下级人民法院审理。  
受理破产申请的人民法院，如对有关债务人的海事纠纷、专利纠纷、证券市场因虚假陈述引发的民事赔偿纠纷等案件不能行使管辖权的，可以依据[民事诉讼法第三十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-37)的规定，由上级人民法院指定管辖。

**Article 47** After the People's Court accepts a bankruptcy application, any civil action brought by a party concerning the debtor shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court that accepted the bankruptcy application, in accordance with Article 21 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law.  
For a first-instance civil case concerning the debtor under the jurisdiction of the People's Court that accepted the bankruptcy application, such court may, in accordance with Article 38 of the Civil Procedure Law, refer the case to a higher People's Court for trial, or submit it to the higher People's Court for approval to transfer it to a lower People's Court for trial.  
If the People's Court that accepted the bankruptcy application cannot exercise jurisdiction over a case concerning the debtor, such as a maritime dispute, patent dispute, or civil compensation dispute arising from false statements in the securities market, it may, in accordance with Article 37 of the Civil Procedure Law, request a higher People's Court to designate jurisdiction.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本规定施行前本院发布的有关企业破产的司法解释，与本规定相抵触的，自本规定施行之日起不再适用。

**Article 48** Where any judicial interpretation on enterprise bankruptcy previously issued by this Court before the coming into force of these Provisions conflicts with these Provisions, such interpretation shall no longer apply from the date these Provisions come into force.
