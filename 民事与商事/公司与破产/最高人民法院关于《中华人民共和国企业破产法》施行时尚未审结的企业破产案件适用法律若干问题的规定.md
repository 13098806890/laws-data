# 最高人民法院关于《中华人民共和国企业破产法》施行时尚未审结的企业破产案件适用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2007〕10号  
**公布日期**：2007-04-23  
**生效日期**：2007-06-01

> 《最高人民法院关于〈中华人民共和国企业破产法〉施行时尚未审结的企业破产案件适用法律若干问题的规定》已于2007年4月23日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1425次会议通过，现予公布，自2007年6月1日起施行。 2007年4月25日 最高人民法院 关于《中华人民共和国企业破产法》 施行时尚未审结的企业破产案件 适用法律若干问题的规定 法释〔2007〕10号 为正确适用《中华人民共和国企业破产法》，对人民法院审理企业破产法施行前受理的、施行时尚未审结的企业破产案件具体适用法律问题，规定如下： 第一条  债权人、债务人或者出资人向人民法院提出重整或者和解申请，符合下列条件之一的，人民法院应予受理： （一）债权人申请破产清算的案件，债务人或者出资人于债务人被宣告破产前提出重整申请，且符合企业破产法第七十条第二款的规定； （二）债权人申请破产清算的案件，债权人于债务人被宣告破产前提出重整申请，且符合企业破产法关于债权人直接向人民法院申请重整的规定； （三）债务人申请破产清算的案件，债务人于被宣告破产前提出重整申请，且符合企业破产法关于债务人直接向人民法院申请重整的规定； （四）债务人依据企业破产法第九十五条的规定申请和解。 第二条  清算组在企业破产法施行前未通知或者答复未履行完毕合同的对方当事人解除或者继续履行合同的，从企业破产法施行之日起计算，在该法第十八条第一款规定的期限内未通知或者答复的，视为解除合同。 第三条  已经成立清算组的，企业破产法施行后，人民法院可以指定该清算组为管理人。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  债权人、债务人或者出资人向人民法院提出重整或者和解申请，符合下列条件之一的，人民法院应予受理：  
（一）债权人申请破产清算的案件，债务人或者出资人于债务人被宣告破产前提出重整申请，且符合[企业破产法第七十条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-70)第二款的规定；  
（二）债权人申请破产清算的案件，债权人于债务人被宣告破产前提出重整申请，且符合企业破产法关于债权人直接向人民法院申请重整的规定；  
（三）债务人申请破产清算的案件，债务人于被宣告破产前提出重整申请，且符合企业破产法关于债务人直接向人民法院申请重整的规定；  
（四）债务人依据[企业破产法第九十五条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-95)的规定申请和解。

**Article 1** Where a creditor, debtor, or capital contributor files an application with the People's Court for reorganization or composition, and meets any of the following conditions, the People's Court shall accept the application:  
(1) In a case where a creditor applies for bankruptcy liquidation, the debtor or capital contributor files an application for reorganization before the debtor is declared bankrupt, and meets the provisions of Article 70(2) of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law;  
(2) In a case where a creditor applies for bankruptcy liquidation, the creditor files an application for reorganization before the debtor is declared bankrupt, and meets the provisions of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law on a creditor filing an application for reorganization directly with the People's Court;  
(3) In a case where the debtor applies for bankruptcy liquidation, the debtor files an application for reorganization before being declared bankrupt, and meets the provisions of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law on the debtor filing an application for reorganization directly with the People's Court;  
(4) The debtor files an application for composition in accordance with Article 95 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  清算组在企业破产法施行前未通知或者答复未履行完毕合同的对方当事人解除或者继续履行合同的，从企业破产法施行之日起计算，在该法第十八条第一款规定的期限内未通知或者答复的，视为解除合同。

**Article 2** Where the liquidation group fails to notify or reply to the other party to an unperformed contract regarding the dissolution or continued performance of the contract before the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law comes into force, and, counted from the date when the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law comes into force, fails to notify or reply within the time limit prescribed in Article 18(1) of that Law, the contract shall be deemed dissolved.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  已经成立清算组的，企业破产法施行后，人民法院可以指定该清算组为管理人。  
尚未成立清算组的，人民法院应当依照企业破产法和《最高人民法院关于审理企业破产案件指定管理人的规定》及时指定管理人。

**Article 3** Where a liquidation group has already been established, after the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law comes into force, the People's Court may designate such liquidation group as the administrator.  
Where no liquidation group has been established, the People's Court shall promptly designate an administrator in accordance with the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law and the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on the Designation of Administrators in Enterprise Bankruptcy Cases.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  债权人主张对债权债务抵销的，应当符合[企业破产法第四十条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-40)规定的情形；但企业破产法施行前，已经依据有关法律规定抵销的除外。

**Article 4** Where a creditor claims offset of mutual debts and claims, the circumstances set forth in Article 40 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law shall be satisfied; except that such offset has already been made pursuant to relevant legal provisions before the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law comes into force.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  对于尚未清偿的破产费用，应当按[企业破产法第四十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-41)和第四十二条的规定区分破产费用和共益债务，并依据[企业破产法第四十三条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-43)的规定清偿。

**Article 5** For bankruptcy expenses that have not yet been paid, the distinction between bankruptcy expenses and community debts shall be made in accordance with Articles 41 and 42 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, and payment shall be made in accordance with Article 43 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  人民法院尚未宣告债务人破产的，应当适用[企业破产法第四十六条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-46)的规定确认债权利息；已经宣告破产的，依据企业破产法施行前的法律规定确认债权利息。

**Article 6** Where the People's Court has not yet declared the debtor bankrupt, the provisions of Article 46 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law shall apply to confirm the interests on debts; where bankruptcy has already been declared, the interests on debts shall be confirmed in accordance with the legal provisions applicable before the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law comes into force.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  债权人已经向人民法院申报债权的，由人民法院将相关申报材料移交给管理人；尚未申报的，债权人应当直接向管理人申报。

**Article 7** Where a creditor has already declared its claim to the People's Court, the People's Court shall transfer the relevant declaration materials to the administrator; where no declaration has been made, the creditor shall declare directly to the administrator.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  债权人未在人民法院确定的债权申报期内向人民法院申报债权的，可以依据[企业破产法第五十六条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-56)的规定补充申报。

**Article 8** Where a creditor fails to declare its claim to the People's Court within the claim declaration period determined by the People's Court, it may supplement its declaration in accordance with Article 56 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  债权人对债权表记载债权有异议，向受理破产申请的人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依据[企业破产法第二十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-21)和第五十八条的规定予以受理。但人民法院对异议债权已经作出裁决的除外。  
债权人就争议债权起诉债务人，要求其承担偿还责任的，人民法院应当告知该债权人变更其诉讼请求为确认债权。

**Article 9** Where a creditor who disputes a claim recorded in the schedule of claims files a lawsuit with the People's Court accepting the bankruptcy application, the People's Court shall accept the case in accordance with Articles 21 and 58 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, except where the People's Court has already rendered a ruling on the disputed claim.  
Where a creditor files a lawsuit against the debtor regarding a disputed claim, requesting the debtor to assume the obligation of repayment, the People's Court shall inform such creditor to change its claim to one for confirmation of the claim.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  债务人的职工就清单记载有异议，向受理破产申请的人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依据[企业破产法第二十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-21)和第四十八条的规定予以受理。但人民法院对异议债权已经作出裁决的除外。

**Article 10** Where an employee of the debtor who disputes a claim recorded in the list files a lawsuit with the People's Court accepting the bankruptcy application, the People's Court shall accept the case in accordance with Articles 21 and 48 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, except where the People's Court has already rendered a ruling on the disputed claim.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  有财产担保的债权人未放弃优先受偿权利的，对于[企业破产法第六十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-61)第一款第七项、第十项规定以外的事项享有表决权。但该债权人对于企业破产法施行前已经表决的事项主张行使表决权，或者以其未行使表决权为由请求撤销债权人会议决议的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 11** A creditor with property security who has not waived the right to priority of repayment shall have the right to vote on matters other than those specified in Article 61(1)(7) and (10) of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law. However, if such creditor claims the right to vote on matters already voted on before the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law comes into force, or requests the revocation of a resolution of the creditors' meeting on the ground that it did not exercise the right to vote, the People's Court shall not support such claims.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  债权人认为债权人会议的决议违反法律规定，损害其利益，向人民法院请求撤销该决议，裁定尚未作出的，人民法院应当依据[企业破产法第六十四条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-64)的规定作出裁定。

**Article 12** Where a creditor considers that a resolution of the creditors' meeting violates legal provisions and harms its interests, and requests the People's Court to revoke the resolution, and no ruling has yet been made, the People's Court shall render a ruling in accordance with Article 64 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  债权人对于财产分配方案的裁定不服，已经申诉的，由上一级人民法院依据申诉程序继续审理；企业破产法施行后提起申诉的，人民法院应当告知其依据[企业破产法第六十六条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-66)的规定申请复议。  
债权人对于人民法院作出的债务人财产管理方案的裁定或者破产财产变价方案的裁定不服，向受理破产申请的人民法院申请复议的，人民法院应当依据[企业破产法第六十六条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-66)的规定予以受理。  
债权人或者债务人对破产宣告裁定有异议，已经申诉的，由上一级人民法院依据申诉程序继续审理；企业破产法施行后提起申诉的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 13** Where a creditor who is dissatisfied with the ruling on the property distribution plan has already filed a petition, the court at the next higher level shall continue the trial in accordance with the petition procedure; where a petition is filed after the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law comes into force, the People's Court shall inform the creditor to apply for reconsideration in accordance with Article 66 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law.  
Where a creditor who is dissatisfied with the People's Court's ruling on the debtor's property management plan or the bankruptcy property realization plan applies to the People's Court accepting the bankruptcy application for reconsideration, the People's Court shall accept such application in accordance with Article 66 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law.  
Where a creditor or the debtor who disagrees with the ruling declaring bankruptcy has already filed a petition, the court at the next higher level shall continue the trial in accordance with the petition procedure; where a petition is filed after the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law comes into force, the People's Court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  企业破产法施行后，破产人的职工依据[企业破产法第一百三十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-132)的规定主张权利的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 14** After the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law comes into force, where an employee of the bankrupt person claims rights in accordance with Article 132 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  破产人所欠董事、监事和高级管理人员的工资，应当依据[企业破产法第一百一十三条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-113)第三款的规定予以调整。

**Article 15** The wages owed by the bankrupt person to its directors, supervisors, and senior officers shall be adjusted in accordance with Article 113(3) of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  本规定施行前本院作出的有关司法解释与本规定相抵触的，人民法院审理尚未审结的企业破产案件不再适用。

**Article 16** Where any relevant judicial interpretation made by the Supreme People's Court before these Provisions come into force conflicts with these Provisions, the People's Court shall no longer apply such interpretation when hearing enterprise bankruptcy cases that have not yet been concluded.
