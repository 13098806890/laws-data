# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》时间效力的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2024〕7号  
**公布日期**：2024-06-27  
**生效日期**：2024-07-01

> 《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国公司法〉时间效力的若干规定》已于2024年6月27日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1922次会议通过，现予公布，自2024年7月1日起施行。 2024年6月29日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》 时间效力的若干规定 法释〔2024〕7号 为正确适用2023年12月29日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第七次会议第二次修订的《中华人民共和国公司法》，根据《中华人民共和国立法法》《中华人民共和国民法典》等法律规定，就人民法院在审理与公司有关的民事纠纷案件中，涉及公司法时间效力的有关问题作出如下规定。 第一条　公司法施行后的法律事实引起的民事纠纷案件，适用公司法的规定。 公司法施行前的法律事实引起的民事纠纷案件，当时的法律、司法解释有规定的，适用当时的法律、司法解释的规定，但是适用公司法更有利于实现其立法目的，适用公司法的规定： （一）公司法施行前，公司的股东会召集程序不当，未被通知参加会议的股东自决议作出之日起一年内请求人民法院撤销的，适用公司法第二十六条第二款的规定； （二）公司法施行前的股东会决议、董事会决议被人民法院依法确认不成立，对公司根据该决议与善意相对人形成的法律关系效力发生争议的，适用公司法第二十八条第二款的规定； （三）公司法施行前，股东以债权出资，因出资方式发生争议的，适用公司法第四十八条第一款的规定； （四）公司法施行前，有限责任公司股东向股东以外的人转让股权，因股权转让发生争议的，适用公司法第八十四条第二款的规定； （五）公司法施行前，公司违反法律规定向股东分配利润、减少注册资本造成公司损失，因损害赔偿责任发生争议的，分别适用公司法第二百一十一条、第二百二十六条的规定； （六）公司法施行前作出利润分配决议，因利润分配时限发生争议的，适用公司法第二百一十二条的规定；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　公司法施行后的法律事实引起的民事纠纷案件，适用公司法的规定。  
公司法施行前的法律事实引起的民事纠纷案件，当时的法律、司法解释有规定的，适用当时的法律、司法解释的规定，但是适用公司法更有利于实现其立法目的，适用公司法的规定：  
（一）公司法施行前，公司的股东会召集程序不当，未被通知参加会议的股东自决议作出之日起一年内请求人民法院撤销的，适用[公司法第二十六条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-26)第二款的规定；  
（二）公司法施行前的股东会决议、董事会决议被人民法院依法确认不成立，对公司根据该决议与善意相对人形成的法律关系效力发生争议的，适用[公司法第二十八条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-28)第二款的规定；  
（三）公司法施行前，股东以债权出资，因出资方式发生争议的，适用[公司法第四十八条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-48)第一款的规定；  
（四）公司法施行前，有限责任公司股东向股东以外的人转让股权，因股权转让发生争议的，适用[公司法第八十四条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-84)第二款的规定；  
（五）公司法施行前，公司违反法律规定向股东分配利润、减少注册资本造成公司损失，因损害赔偿责任发生争议的，分别适用[公司法第二百一十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-211)、第二百二十六条的规定；  
（六）公司法施行前作出利润分配决议，因利润分配时限发生争议的，适用[公司法第二百一十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-212)的规定；  
（七）公司法施行前，公司减少注册资本，股东对相应减少出资额或者股份数量发生争议的，适用[公司法第二百二十四条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-224)第三款的规定。

**Article 1** For civil dispute cases arising from legal facts that occur after the Company Law comes into force, the provisions of the Company Law shall apply.  
For civil dispute cases arising from legal facts that occur before the Company Law comes into force, if the laws and judicial interpretations in force at that time have provisions, such laws and judicial interpretations shall apply; however, if the application of the Company Law is more conducive to achieving its legislative purpose, the provisions of the Company Law shall apply:  
(1) For a shareholders' meeting convened through improper procedures before the Company Law comes into force, where a shareholder who was not notified of the meeting requests the People's Court to rescind the resolution within one year from the date the resolution was made, the provisions of Article 26, paragraph 2 of the Company Law shall apply;  
(2) Where a shareholders' meeting resolution or board of directors resolution made before the Company Law comes into force is confirmed by the People's Court as not established according to law, and a dispute arises over the validity of the legal relationship formed between the company and a bona fide counterparty based on such resolution, the provisions of Article 28, paragraph 2 of the Company Law shall apply;  
(3) Where a shareholder contributes capital by way of creditor's rights before the Company Law comes into force, and a dispute arises over the method of capital contribution, the provisions of Article 48, paragraph 1 of the Company Law shall apply;  
(4) Where a shareholder of a limited liability company transfers equity to a person other than a shareholder before the Company Law comes into force, and a dispute arises over the equity transfer, the provisions of Article 84, paragraph 2 of the Company Law shall apply;  
(5) Where a company, in violation of legal provisions, distributes profits to shareholders or reduces registered capital before the Company Law comes into force, causing losses to the company, and a dispute arises over liability for damages, the provisions of Articles 211 and 226 of the Company Law shall apply respectively;  
(6) Where a resolution on profit distribution is made before the Company Law comes into force, and a dispute arises over the time limit for profit distribution, the provisions of Article 212 of the Company Law shall apply;  
(7) Where a company reduces its registered capital before the Company Law comes into force, and a dispute arises between shareholders over the corresponding reduction in capital contribution amount or number of shares, the provisions of Article 224, paragraph 3 of the Company Law shall apply.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　公司法施行前与公司有关的民事法律行为，依据当时的法律、司法解释认定无效而依据公司法认定有效，因民事法律行为效力发生争议的下列情形，适用公司法的规定：  
（一）约定公司对所投资企业债务承担连带责任，对该约定效力发生争议的，适用[公司法第十四条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-14)第二款的规定；  
（二）公司作出使用资本公积金弥补亏损的公司决议，对该决议效力发生争议的，适用[公司法第二百一十四条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-214)的规定；  
（三）公司与其持股百分之九十以上的公司合并，对合并决议效力发生争议的，适用[公司法第二百一十九条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-219)的规定。

**Article 2** For civil juristic acts related to a company that occur before the Company Law comes into force, where such acts are determined to be invalid under the laws and judicial interpretations in force at that time but are determined to be valid under the Company Law, the following circumstances where a dispute arises over the validity of the civil juristic act shall be governed by the provisions of the Company Law:  
(1) Where an agreement stipulates that the company shall bear joint and several liability for the debts of the enterprise in which it invests, and a dispute arises over the validity of such agreement, the provisions of Article 14, paragraph 2 of the Company Law shall apply;  
(2) Where a company makes a resolution to use the capital reserve to cover losses, and a dispute arises over the validity of such resolution, the provisions of Article 214 of the Company Law shall apply;  
(3) Where a company merges with a company in which it holds ninety percent or more of the shares, and a dispute arises over the validity of the merger resolution, the provisions of Article 219 of the Company Law shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　公司法施行前订立的与公司有关的合同，合同的履行持续至公司法施行后，因公司法施行前的履行行为发生争议的，适用当时的法律、司法解释的规定；因公司法施行后的履行行为发生争议的下列情形，适用公司法的规定：  
（一）代持上市公司股票合同，适用[公司法第一百四十条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-140)第二款的规定；  
（二）上市公司控股子公司取得该上市公司股份合同，适用[公司法第一百四十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-141)的规定；  
（三）股份有限公司为他人取得本公司或者母公司的股份提供赠与、借款、担保以及其他财务资助合同，适用[公司法第一百六十三条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-163)的规定。

**Article 3** For contracts related to a company that are concluded before the Company Law comes into force, where the performance of the contract continues after the Company Law comes into force, if a dispute arises over the performance before the Company Law comes into force, the provisions of the laws and judicial interpretations in force at that time shall apply; if a dispute arises over the performance after the Company Law comes into force in the following circumstances, the provisions of the Company Law shall apply:  
(1) For a contract on the nominee holding of shares of a listed company, the provisions of Article 140, paragraph 2 of the Company Law shall apply;  
(2) For a contract under which a controlled subsidiary of a listed company acquires shares of such listed company, the provisions of Article 141 of the Company Law shall apply;  
(3) For a contract under which a joint stock limited company provides gifts, loans, guarantees, or other financial assistance to another person for the acquisition of shares of the company or its parent company, the provisions of Article 163 of the Company Law shall apply.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　公司法施行前的法律事实引起的民事纠纷案件，当时的法律、司法解释没有规定而公司法作出规定的下列情形，适用公司法的规定：  
（一）股东转让未届出资期限的股权，受让人未按期足额缴纳出资的，关于转让人、受让人出资责任的认定，适用[公司法第八十八条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-88)第一款的规定；  
（二）有限责任公司的控股股东滥用股东权利，严重损害公司或者其他股东利益，其他股东请求公司按照合理价格收购其股权的，适用[公司法第八十九条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-89)第三款、第四款的规定；  
（三）对股份有限公司股东会决议投反对票的股东请求公司按照合理价格收购其股份的，适用[公司法第一百六十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-161)的规定；  
（四）不担任公司董事的控股股东、实际控制人执行公司事务的民事责任认定，适用[公司法第一百八十条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-180)的规定；  
（五）公司的控股股东、实际控制人指示董事、高级管理人员从事活动损害公司或者股东利益的民事责任认定，适用[公司法第一百九十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-192)的规定；  
（六）不明显背离相关当事人合理预期的其他情形。

**Article 4** For civil dispute cases arising from legal facts that occur before the Company Law comes into force, where the laws and judicial interpretations in force at that time have no provisions but the Company Law makes provisions in the following circumstances, the provisions of the Company Law shall apply:  
(1) Where a shareholder transfers equity with an unpaid capital contribution period not yet due, and the transferee fails to fully pay the capital contribution on time, the determination of the capital contribution liability of the transferor and the transferee shall be governed by Article 88, paragraph 1 of the Company Law;  
(2) Where a controlling shareholder of a limited liability company abuses its shareholder rights, causing serious damage to the company or other shareholders, and other shareholders request the company to purchase their equity at a reasonable price, the provisions of Article 89, paragraphs 3 and 4 of the Company Law shall apply;  
(3) Where a shareholder who voted against a resolution of a shareholders' meeting of a joint stock limited company requests the company to purchase its shares at a reasonable price, the provisions of Article 161 of the Company Law shall apply;  
(4) For the determination of civil liability of a controlling shareholder or actual controller who does not serve as a director of the company but executes company affairs, the provisions of Article 180 of the Company Law shall apply;  
(5) For the determination of civil liability of a controlling shareholder or actual controller of a company who instructs directors or senior officers to engage in activities that harm the interests of the company or shareholders, the provisions of Article 192 of the Company Law shall apply;  
(6) Other circumstances that do not obviously deviate from the reasonable expectations of the relevant parties.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　公司法施行前的法律事实引起的民事纠纷案件，当时的法律、司法解释已有原则性规定，公司法作出具体规定的下列情形，适用公司法的规定：  
（一）股份有限公司章程对股份转让作了限制规定，因该规定发生争议的，适用[公司法第一百五十七条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-157)的规定；  
（二）对公司监事实施挪用公司资金等禁止性行为、违法关联交易、不当谋取公司商业机会、经营限制的同类业务的赔偿责任认定，分别适用[公司法第一百八十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-181)、第一百八十二条第一款、第一百八十三条、第一百八十四条的规定；  
（三）对公司董事、高级管理人员不当谋取公司商业机会、经营限制的同类业务的赔偿责任认定，分别适用[公司法第一百八十三条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-183)、第一百八十四条的规定；  
（四）对关联关系主体范围以及关联交易性质的认定，适用[公司法第一百八十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-182)、第二百六十五条第四项的规定。

**Article 5** For civil dispute cases arising from legal facts that occur before the Company Law comes into force, where the laws and judicial interpretations in force at that time have already provided general principles and the Company Law makes specific provisions in the following circumstances, the provisions of the Company Law shall apply:  
(1) Where the articles of association of a joint stock limited company impose restrictions on share transfer, and a dispute arises over such restrictions, the provisions of Article 157 of the Company Law shall apply;  
(2) For the determination of liability for damages of a company's supervisor who commits prohibited acts such as misappropriation of company funds, illegal related-party transactions, improper acquisition of company business opportunities, or operation of restricted similar businesses, the provisions of Articles 181, 182(1), 183, and 184 of the Company Law shall apply respectively;  
(3) For the determination of liability for damages of a company's director or senior officer who improperly acquires company business opportunities or operates restricted similar businesses, the provisions of Articles 183 and 184 of the Company Law shall apply respectively;  
(4) For the determination of the scope of related parties and the nature of related-party transactions, the provisions of Article 182 and Article 265(4) of the Company Law shall apply.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　应当进行清算的法律事实发生在公司法施行前，因清算责任发生争议的，适用当时的法律、司法解释的规定。  
应当清算的法律事实发生在公司法施行前，但至公司法施行日未满十五日的，适用[公司法第二百三十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-232)的规定，清算义务人履行清算义务的期限自公司法施行日重新起算。

**Article 6** Where the legal facts that should trigger liquidation occur before the Company Law comes into force, and a dispute arises over liquidation liability, the provisions of the laws and judicial interpretations in force at that time shall apply.  
Where the legal facts that should trigger liquidation occur before the Company Law comes into force, but less than fifteen days have passed before the effective date of the Company Law, the provisions of Article 232 of the Company Law shall apply, and the period for the liquidation obligor to perform the liquidation obligation shall be recalculated from the effective date of the Company Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　公司法施行前已经终审的民事纠纷案件，当事人申请再审或者人民法院按照审判监督程序决定再审的，适用当时的法律、司法解释的规定。

**Article 7** For civil dispute cases that have been finally adjudicated before the Company Law comes into force, where a party applies for retrial or the People's Court decides to retry the case in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, the provisions of the laws and judicial interpretations in force at that time shall apply.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　本规定自2024年7月1日起施行。

**Article 8** These Provisions shall come into force on July 1, 2024.
