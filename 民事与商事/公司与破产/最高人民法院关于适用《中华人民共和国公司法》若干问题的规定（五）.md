# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》若干问题的规定（五）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2019〕7号  
**公布日期**：2019-04-28  
**生效日期**：2019-04-29

> 《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国公司法〉若干问题的规定（五）》已于2019年4月22日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1766次会议通过，现予公布，自2019年4月29日起施行。 2019年4月28日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》 若干问题的规定（五）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  关联交易损害公司利益，原告公司依据[公司法第二十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-21)规定请求控股股东、实际控制人、董事、监事、高级管理人员赔偿所造成的损失，被告仅以该交易已经履行了信息披露、经股东会或者股东大会同意等法律、行政法规或者公司章程规定的程序为由抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。  
公司没有提起诉讼的，符合[公司法第一百五十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-151)第一款规定条件的股东，可以依据[公司法第一百五十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-151)第二款、第三款规定向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 1** Where a connected transaction damages the company's interests, and the plaintiff company requests that the controlling shareholder, actual controller, director, supervisor, or senior officer compensate for the losses caused pursuant to Article 21 of the Company Law, if the defendant only defends on the ground that the transaction has fulfilled the procedures required by laws, administrative regulations, or the company's articles of association, such as information disclosure, approval by the shareholders' meeting or the general meeting of shareholders, the People's Court shall not support such defense.  
Where the company does not initiate a lawsuit, the shareholders who meet the conditions set forth in Article 151(1) of the Company Law may initiate a lawsuit in the People's Court pursuant to Article 151(2) and (3) of the Company Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  关联交易合同存在无效或者可撤销情形，公司没有起诉合同相对方的，符合[公司法第一百五十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-151)第一款规定条件的股东，可以依据[公司法第一百五十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-151)第二款、第三款规定向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 2** Where a connected transaction contract is void or voidable, and the company does not initiate a lawsuit against the counterparty to the contract, the shareholders who meet the conditions set forth in Article 151(1) of the Company Law may initiate a lawsuit in the People's Court pursuant to Article 151(2) and (3) of the Company Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  董事任期届满前被股东会或者股东大会有效决议解除职务，其主张解除不发生法律效力的，人民法院不予支持。  
董事职务被解除后，因补偿与公司发生纠纷提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依据法律、行政法规、公司章程的规定或者合同的约定，综合考虑解除的原因、剩余任期、董事薪酬等因素，确定是否补偿以及补偿的合理数额。

**Article 3** Where a director's term of office is terminated before its expiration by a valid resolution of the shareholders' meeting or the general meeting of shareholders, and the director claims that the termination is not legally effective, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
After the director's position is terminated, if a dispute arises over compensation with the company and a lawsuit is initiated, the People's Court shall, pursuant to the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, the company's articles of association, or the contract, comprehensively consider factors such as the reason for termination, remaining term of office, and director's remuneration, to determine whether compensation shall be made and the reasonable amount of compensation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  分配利润的股东会或者股东大会决议作出后，公司应当在决议载明的时间内完成利润分配。决议没有载明时间的，以公司章程规定的为准。决议、章程中均未规定时间或者时间超过一年的，公司应当自决议作出之日起一年内完成利润分配。  
决议中载明的利润分配完成时间超过公司章程规定时间的，股东可以依据[公司法第二十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-22)第二款规定请求人民法院撤销决议中关于该时间的规定。

**Article 4** After a resolution of the shareholders' meeting or the general meeting of shareholders on profit distribution is made, the company shall complete the profit distribution within the time specified in the resolution. If the resolution does not specify a time, the time stipulated in the company's articles of association shall prevail. If neither the resolution nor the articles of association specifies a time, or the time exceeds one year, the company shall complete the profit distribution within one year from the date the resolution is made.  
If the time for completing profit distribution specified in the resolution exceeds the time stipulated in the company's articles of association, the shareholders may, pursuant to Article 22(2) of the Company Law, request the People's Court to revoke the provision concerning such time in the resolution.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  人民法院审理涉及有限责任公司股东重大分歧案件时，应当注重调解。当事人协商一致以下列方式解决分歧，且不违反法律、行政法规的强制性规定的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）公司回购部分股东股份；  
（二）其他股东受让部分股东股份；  
（三）他人受让部分股东股份；  
（四）公司减资；  
（五）公司分立；  
（六）其他能够解决分歧，恢复公司正常经营，避免公司解散的方式。

**Article 5** When the People's Court adjudicates a case involving major disagreements among shareholders of a limited liability company, it shall emphasize mediation. Where the parties agree through consultation to resolve the disagreements by the following methods, and such methods do not violate the mandatory provisions of laws and administrative regulations, the People's Court shall support them:  
(1) the company repurchases part of the shareholders' shares;  
(2) other shareholders acquire part of the shareholders' shares;  
(3) a third party acquires part of the shareholders' shares;  
(4) the company reduces its capital;  
(5) the company splits;  
(6) other methods that can resolve the disagreements, restore the normal operation of the company, and avoid the dissolution of the company.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  本规定自2019年4月29日起施行。  
本规定施行后尚未终审的案件，适用本规定；本规定施行前已经终审的案件，或者适用审判监督程序再审的案件，不适用本规定。  
本院以前发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 6** These Provisions shall come into force on April 29, 2019.  
For cases that have not yet been finally adjudicated after these Provisions come into force, these Provisions shall apply; for cases that have been finally adjudicated before these Provisions come into force, or cases retried under the trial supervision procedure, these Provisions shall not apply.  
Where any judicial interpretation previously issued by this Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
