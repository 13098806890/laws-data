# 最高人民法院关于冻结、拍卖上市公司国有股和社会法人股若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2001〕28号  
**公布日期**：2001-08-28  
**生效日期**：2001-09-30

> （2001年8月28日最高人民法院审判委员会第1188次会议通过，自2001年9月30日起施行。） 法释〔2001〕28号 为了保护债权人以及其他当事人的合法权益，维护证券市场的正常交易秩序，根据《中华人民共和国证券法》、《中华人民共和国公司法》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》，参照《中华人民共和国拍卖法》等法律的有关规定，对人民法院在财产保全和执行过程中，冻结、拍卖上市公司国有股和社会法人股（以下均简称股权）等有关问题，作如下规定： 第一条 人民法院在审理民事纠纷案件过程中，对股权采取冻结、评估、拍卖和办理股权过户等财产保全和执行措施，适用本规定。 第二条 本规定所指上市公司国有股，包括国家股和国有法人股。国家股指有权代表国家投资的机构或部门向股份有限公司出资或依据法定程序取得的股份；国有法人股指国有法人单位，包括国有资产比例超过50%的国有控股企业，以其依法占有的法人资产向股份有限公司出资形成或者依据法定程序取得的股份。 本规定所指社会法人股是指非国有法人资产投资于上市公司形成的股份。 第三条 人民法院对股权采取冻结、拍卖措施时，被保全人和被执行人应当是股权的持有人或者所有权人。被冻结、拍卖股权的上市公司非依据法定程序确定为案件当事人或者被执行人，人民法院不得对其采取保全或执行措施。 第四条 人民法院在审理案件过程中，股权持有人或者所有权人作为债务人，如有偿还能力的，人民法院一般不应对其股权采取冻结保全措施。 人民法院已对股权采取冻结保全措施的，股权持有人、所有权人或者第三人提供了有效担保，人民法院经审查符合法律规定的，可以解除对股权的冻结。 第五条 人民法院裁定冻结或者解除冻结股权，除应当将法律文书送达负有协助执行义务的单位以外，还应当在作出冻结或者解除冻结裁定后7日内，将法律文书送达股权持有人或者所有权人并书面通知上市公司。 人民法院裁定拍卖上市公司股权，应当于委托拍卖之前将法律文书送达股权持有人或者所有权人并书面通知上市公司。 被冻结或者拍卖股权的当事人是国有股份持有人的，人民法院在向该国有股份持有人送达冻结或者拍卖裁定时，应当告其于5日内报主管财政部门备案。 第六条 冻结股权的期限不超过一年。如申请人需要延长期限的，人民法院应当根据申请，在冻结期限届满前办理续冻手续，每次续冻期限不超过6个月。逾期不办理续冻手续的，视为自动撤销冻结。 第七条 人民法院采取保全措施，所冻结的股权价值不得超过股权持有人或者所有权人的债务总额。股权价值应当按照上市公司最近期报表每股资产净值计算。 股权冻结的效力及于股权产生的股息以及红利、红股等孳息，但股权持有人或者所有权人仍可享有因上市公司增发、配售新股而产生的权利。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 人民法院在审理民事纠纷案件过程中，对股权采取冻结、评估、拍卖和办理股权过户等财产保全和执行措施，适用本规定。

**Article 1** When a People's Court adopts property preservation and enforcement measures such as freezing, appraisal, auction, and handling equity transfer with respect to equity interests in the course of hearing civil dispute cases, these Provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 本规定所指上市公司国有股，包括国家股和国有法人股。国家股指有权代表国家投资的机构或部门向股份有限公司出资或依据法定程序取得的股份；国有法人股指国有法人单位，包括国有资产比例超过50%的国有控股企业，以其依法占有的法人资产向股份有限公司出资形成或者依据法定程序取得的股份。  
本规定所指社会法人股是指非国有法人资产投资于上市公司形成的股份。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Provisions, state-owned shares of a listed company shall include state shares and state-owned legal person shares. State shares refer to the shares contributed by an institution or department authorized to invest on behalf of the state to a joint stock limited company or obtained pursuant to statutory procedures; state-owned legal person shares refer to the shares contributed by a state-owned legal person entity, including a state-controlled enterprise with a state-owned asset ratio exceeding 50%, by using its legal person assets lawfully occupied to a joint stock limited company, or obtained pursuant to statutory procedures.  
For the purposes of these Provisions, social legal person shares refer to the shares formed by investment of non-state-owned legal person assets in a listed company.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 人民法院对股权采取冻结、拍卖措施时，被保全人和被执行人应当是股权的持有人或者所有权人。被冻结、拍卖股权的上市公司非依据法定程序确定为案件当事人或者被执行人，人民法院不得对其采取保全或执行措施。

**Article 3** When a People's Court adopts measures to freeze or auction equity interests, the preserved person and the person subject to enforcement shall be the holder or owner of the equity interests. If the listed company whose equity interests are frozen or auctioned is not determined as a party or a person subject to enforcement in the case according to statutory procedures, the People's Court shall not adopt preservation or enforcement measures against it.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 人民法院在审理案件过程中，股权持有人或者所有权人作为债务人，如有偿还能力的，人民法院一般不应对其股权采取冻结保全措施。  
人民法院已对股权采取冻结保全措施的，股权持有人、所有权人或者第三人提供了有效担保，人民法院经审查符合法律规定的，可以解除对股权的冻结。

**Article 4** In the course of hearing a case, if the holder or owner of the equity interests, as a debtor, has the ability to repay, the People's Court shall generally not adopt freezing preservation measures against its equity interests.  
Where the People's Court has already adopted freezing preservation measures against the equity interests, and the holder, owner, or a third party provides valid security, the People's Court may, upon examination and if it conforms to the provisions of law, lift the freeze on the equity interests.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 人民法院裁定冻结或者解除冻结股权，除应当将法律文书送达负有协助执行义务的单位以外，还应当在作出冻结或者解除冻结裁定后7日内，将法律文书送达股权持有人或者所有权人并书面通知上市公司。  
人民法院裁定拍卖上市公司股权，应当于委托拍卖之前将法律文书送达股权持有人或者所有权人并书面通知上市公司。  
被冻结或者拍卖股权的当事人是国有股份持有人的，人民法院在向该国有股份持有人送达冻结或者拍卖裁定时，应当告其于5日内报主管财政部门备案。

**Article 5** Where a People's Court rules to freeze or lift the freeze on equity interests, in addition to serving the legal instruments on the unit obligated to assist in enforcement, it shall also, within seven days after making the freeze or lift-freeze ruling, serve the legal instruments on the holder or owner of the equity interests and notify the listed company in writing.  
Where a People's Court rules to auction the equity interests of a listed company, it shall, before entrusting the auction, serve the legal instruments on the holder or owner of the equity interests and notify the listed company in writing.  
If the party whose equity interests are frozen or auctioned is a holder of state-owned shares, when serving the freeze or auction ruling on such holder of state-owned shares, the People's Court shall inform it to report to the competent financial department for recordation within five days.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 冻结股权的期限不超过一年。如申请人需要延长期限的，人民法院应当根据申请，在冻结期限届满前办理续冻手续，每次续冻期限不超过6个月。逾期不办理续冻手续的，视为自动撤销冻结。

**Article 6** The term of freezing equity interests shall not exceed one year. If the applicant needs to extend the term, the People's Court shall, upon application, handle the renewal procedures before the expiration of the freezing term, and each renewal term shall not exceed six months. If the renewal procedures are not handled within the time limit, the freeze shall be deemed automatically revoked.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 人民法院采取保全措施，所冻结的股权价值不得超过股权持有人或者所有权人的债务总额。股权价值应当按照上市公司最近期报表每股资产净值计算。  
股权冻结的效力及于股权产生的股息以及红利、红股等孳息，但股权持有人或者所有权人仍可享有因上市公司增发、配售新股而产生的权利。

**Article 7** When a People's Court adopts preservation measures, the value of the frozen equity interests shall not exceed the total amount of debts of the holder or owner of the equity interests. The value of the equity interests shall be calculated based on the net asset value per share in the latest financial statements of the listed company.  
The effect of the freezing of equity interests shall extend to the dividends, bonuses, bonus shares and other fruits generated by the equity interests, but the holder or owner of the equity interests may still enjoy the rights arising from the listed company's issuance of additional shares or rights offering of new shares.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 人民法院采取强制执行措施时，如果股权持有人或者所有权人在限期内提供了方便执行的其他财产，应当首先执行其他财产。其他财产不足以清偿债务的，方可执行股权。  
本规定所称可供方便执行的其他财产，是指存款、现金、成品和半成品、原材料、交通工具等。  
人民法院执行股权，必须进行拍卖。  
股权的持有人或者所有权人以股权向债权人质押的，人民法院执行时也应当通过拍卖方式进行，不得直接将股权执行给债权人。

**Article 8** When a People's Court adopts compulsory enforcement measures, if the holder or owner of the equity interests provides other property convenient for enforcement within the time limit, such other property shall be enforced first. Only when such other property is insufficient to satisfy the debt may the equity interests be enforced.  
For the purposes of these Provisions, "other property convenient for enforcement" refers to deposits, cash, finished products and semi-finished products, raw materials, means of transport, etc.  
When a People's Court enforces equity interests, it must conduct an auction.  
If the holder or owner of the equity interests has pledged the equity interests to a creditor, the People's Court shall also enforce by way of auction and shall not directly assign the equity interests to the creditor.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 拍卖股权之前，人民法院应当委托具有证券从业资格的资产评估机构对股权价值进行评估。资产评估机构由债权人和债务人协商选定。不能达成一致意见的，由人民法院召集债权人和债务人提出候选评估机构，以抽签方式决定。

**Article 9** Before auctioning equity interests, the People's Court shall entrust an asset appraisal institution with securities practice qualifications to appraise the value of the equity interests. The asset appraisal institution shall be selected by the creditor and the debtor through consultation. If they fail to reach a consensus, the People's Court shall convene the creditor and the debtor to propose candidate appraisal institutions, and the decision shall be made by drawing lots.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 人民法院委托资产评估机构评估时，应当要求资产评估机构严格依照国家规定的标准、程序和方法对股权价值进行评估，并说明其应当对所作出的评估报告依法承担相应责任。  
人民法院还应当要求上市公司向接受人民法院委托的资产评估机构如实提供有关情况和资料；要求资产评估机构对上市公司提供的情况和资料保守秘密。

**Article 10** When entrusting an asset appraisal institution with appraisal, the People's Court shall require the asset appraisal institution to strictly appraise the value of the equity interests in accordance with the standards, procedures and methods prescribed by the state, and shall state that it shall bear corresponding responsibility according to law for the appraisal report it produces.  
The People's Court shall also require the listed company to truthfully provide relevant information and materials to the asset appraisal institution entrusted by the People's Court; and require the asset appraisal institution to keep confidential the information and materials provided by the listed company.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 人民法院收到资产评估机构作出的评估报告后，须将评估报告分别送达债权人和债务人以及上市公司。债权人和债务人以及上市公司对评估报告有异议的，应当在收到评估报告后7日内书面提出。人民法院应当将异议书交资产评估机构，要求该机构在10日之内作出说明或者补正。

**Article 11** After receiving the appraisal report produced by the asset appraisal institution, the People's Court shall serve the appraisal report on the creditor, the debtor and the listed company respectively. If the creditor, the debtor and the listed company have objections to the appraisal report, they shall raise them in writing within seven days after receiving the appraisal report. The People's Court shall submit the written objection to the asset appraisal institution and require it to make an explanation or correction within ten days.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 对股权拍卖，人民法院应当委托依法成立的拍卖机构进行。拍卖机构的选定，参照[本规定第九条](#art-9)规定的方法进行。

**Article 12** For the auction of equity interests, the People's Court shall entrust an auction institution established according to law. The selection of the auction institution shall be made by reference to the method prescribed in Article 9 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 股权拍卖保留价，应当按照评估值确定。  
第一次拍卖最高应价未达到保留价时，应当继续进行拍卖，每次拍卖的保留价应当不低于前次保留价的90%。经三次拍卖仍不能成交时，人民法院应当将所拍卖的股权按第三次拍卖的保留价折价抵偿给债权人。  
人民法院可以在每次拍卖未成交后主持调解，将所拍卖的股权参照该次拍卖保留价折价抵偿给债权人。

**Article 13** The reserve price for the auction of equity interests shall be determined based on the appraised value.  
If the highest bid in the first auction fails to reach the reserve price, the auction shall continue, and the reserve price for each subsequent auction shall not be less than 90% of the previous reserve price. If no transaction is concluded after three auctions, the People's Court shall convert the auctioned equity interests into a compensation to the creditor at the reserve price of the third auction.  
The People's Court may, after each unsuccessful auction, preside over mediation to convert the auctioned equity interests into a compensation to the creditor by reference to the reserve price of that auction.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 拍卖股权，人民法院应当委托拍卖机构于拍卖日前10天，在《中国证券报》、《证券时报》或者《上海证券报》上进行公告。

**Article 14** When auctioning equity interests, the People's Court shall entrust the auction institution to publish a notice in the China Securities Journal, the Securities Times or the Shanghai Securities News ten days before the auction date.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 国有股权竞买人应当具备依法受让国有股权的条件。

**Article 15** A bidder for state-owned equity interests shall meet the conditions for acquiring state-owned equity interests according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 股权拍卖过程中，竞买人已经持有的该上市公司股份数额和其竞买的股份数额累计不得超过该上市公司已经发行股份数额的30%。如竞买人累计持有该上市公司股份数额已达到30%仍参与竞买的，须依照《中华人民共和国证券法》的相关规定办理，在此期间应当中止拍卖程序。

**Article 16** In the course of auctioning equity interests, the total number of shares of the listed company already held by a bidder and the number of shares it bids for shall not exceed 30% of the total issued shares of the listed company. If a bidder whose cumulative shareholding in the listed company has reached 30% still participates in the bidding, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Securities Law of the People's Republic of China, and the auction procedure shall be suspended during this period.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 拍卖成交后，人民法院应当向证券交易市场和证券登记结算公司出具协助执行通知书，由买受人持拍卖机构出具的成交证明和财政主管部门对股权性质的界定等有关文件，向证券交易市场和证券登记结算公司办理股权变更登记。

**Article 17** After the auction is concluded, the People's Court shall issue a notice of assistance in enforcement to the securities trading market and the securities registration and settlement company. The buyer shall, holding the transaction certificate issued by the auction institution and the relevant documents such as the definition of the nature of the equity interests by the competent financial department, handle the registration of change of equity interests with the securities trading market and the securities registration and settlement company.
