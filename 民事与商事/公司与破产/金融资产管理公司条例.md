### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范金融资产管理公司的活动，依法处理国有银行不良贷款，促进国有银行和国有企业的改革和发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to regulate the activities of financial asset management companies, deal with non-performing loans of state-owned banks according to law, and promote the reform and development of state-owned banks and state-owned enterprises.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　金融资产管理公司，是指经国务院决定设立的收购国有银行不良贷款，管理和处置因收购国有银行不良贷款形成的资产的国有独资非银行金融机构。

**Article 2** Financial asset management companies refer to wholly state-owned non-banking financial institutions established upon decision of the State Council to acquire non-performing loans of state-owned banks, and to manage and dispose of assets formed from the acquisition of non-performing loans of state-owned banks.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　金融资产管理公司以最大限度保全资产、减少损失为主要经营目标，依法独立承担民事责任。

**Article 3** Financial asset management companies shall, with maximizing asset preservation and minimizing losses as their main operational objectives, independently bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　中国人民银行、财政部和中国证券监督管理委员会依据各自的法定职责对金融资产管理公司实施监督管理。

**Article 4** The People's Bank of China, the Ministry of Finance and the China Securities Regulatory Commission shall, in accordance with their respective statutory duties, exercise supervision and administration over financial asset management companies.

### 第二章　公司的设立和业务范围

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　金融资产管理公司的注册资本为人民币100亿元，由财政部核拨。

**Article 5** The registered capital of a financial asset management company shall be RMB 10 billion, allocated by the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　金融资产管理公司由中国人民银行颁发《金融机构法人许可证》，并向工商行政管理部门依法办理登记。

**Article 6** A financial asset management company shall be issued a "Financial Institution Legal Person Permit" by the People's Bank of China, and shall undergo registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　金融资产管理公司设立分支机构，须经财政部同意，并报中国人民银行批准，由中国人民银行颁发《金融机构营业许可证》，并向工商行政管理部门依法办理登记。

**Article 7** For the establishment of branches, a financial asset management company shall obtain the consent of the Ministry of Finance and submit to the People's Bank of China for approval, shall be issued a "Financial Institution Business Permit" by the People's Bank of China, and shall undergo registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　金融资产管理公司设总裁1人、副总裁若干人。总裁、副总裁由国务院任命。总裁对外代表金融资产管理公司行使职权，负责金融资产管理公司的经营管理。  
金融资产管理公司的高级管理人员须经中国人民银行审查任职资格。

**Article 8** A financial asset management company shall have one president and several vice-presidents. The president and vice-presidents shall be appointed by the State Council. The president shall exercise powers on behalf of the financial asset management company externally, and shall be responsible for the operation and management of the financial asset management company.  
The senior officers of a financial asset management company shall have their qualifications examined by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　金融资产管理公司监事会的组成、职责和工作程序，依照《国有重点金融机构监事会暂行条例》执行。

**Article 9** The composition, duties and working procedures of the board of supervisors of a financial asset management company shall be implemented in accordance with the Interim Regulations on the Board of Supervisors of Key State-owned Financial Institutions.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　金融资产管理公司在其收购的国有银行不良贷款范围内，管理和处置因收购国有银行不良贷款形成的资产时，可以从事下列业务活动：  
(一)追偿债务；  
(二)对所收购的不良贷款形成的资产进行租赁或者以其他形式转让、重组；  
(三)债权转股权，并对企业阶段性持股；  
(四)资产管理范围内公司的上市推荐及债券、股票承销；  
(五)发行金融债券，向金融机构借款；  
(六)财务及法律咨询，资产及项目评估；  
(七)中国人民银行、中国证券监督管理委员会批准的其他业务活动。  
金融资产管理公司可以向中国人民银行申请再贷款。

**Article 10** When managing and disposing of assets formed from the acquisition of non-performing loans within the scope of the acquired non-performing loans of state-owned banks, a financial asset management company may engage in the following business activities:  
(1) recovering debts;  
(2) leasing or transferring and restructuring in other forms the assets formed from the acquired non-performing loans;  
(3) converting debts into equity, and holding shares in enterprises on a temporary basis;  
(4) recommending companies within the scope of asset management for listing and underwriting bonds and stocks;  
(5) issuing financial bonds and borrowing from financial institutions;  
(6) providing financial and legal consulting, asset and project evaluation;  
(7) other business activities approved by the People's Bank of China and the China Securities Regulatory Commission.  
A financial asset management company may apply for reloans from the People's Bank of China.

### 第三章　收购不良贷款的范围、额度及资金来源

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　金融资产管理公司按照国务院确定的范围和额度收购国有银行不良贷款；超出确定的范围或者额度收购的，须经国务院专项审批。

**Article 11** A financial asset management company shall acquire non-performing loans of state-owned banks within the scope and amount determined by the State Council; if the acquisition exceeds the determined scope or amount, special approval from the State Council shall be obtained.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在国务院确定的额度内，金融资产管理公司按照账面价值收购有关贷款本金和相对应的计入损益的应收未收利息；对未计入损益的应收未收利息，实行无偿划转。

**Article 12** Within the amount determined by the State Council, a financial asset management company shall acquire, at book value, the principal of relevant loans and the corresponding accrued interest receivable that has been recognized in profit or loss; for accrued interest receivable not recognized in profit or loss, a free transfer shall be effected.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　金融资产管理公司收购不良贷款后，即取得原债权人对债务人的各项权利。原借款合同的债务人、担保人及有关当事人应当继续履行合同规定的义务。

**Article 13** After acquiring non-performing loans, a financial asset management company shall immediately obtain all rights of the original creditors against the debtors. The debtors, guarantors and relevant parties under the original loan contracts shall continue to perform the obligations stipulated in the contracts.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　金融资产管理公司收购不良贷款的资金来源包括：  
(一)划转中国人民银行发放给国有独资商业银行的部分再贷款；  
(二)发行金融债券。  
中国人民银行发放给国有独资商业银行的再贷款划转给金融资产管理公司，实行固定利率，年利率为2.25%。

**Article 14** The sources of funds for a financial asset management company to acquire non-performing loans include:  
(1) the transfer of part of the reloans extended by the People's Bank of China to wholly state-owned commercial banks;  
(2) the issuance of financial bonds.  
The reloans extended by the People's Bank of China to wholly state-owned commercial banks which are transferred to the financial asset management company shall be subject to a fixed interest rate at an annual rate of 2.25%.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　金融资产管理公司发行金融债券，由中国人民银行会同财政部审批。

**Article 15** The issuance of financial bonds by a financial asset management company shall be subject to the approval of the People's Bank of China in conjunction with the Ministry of Finance.

### 第四章　债权转股权

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　金融资产管理公司可以将收购国有银行不良贷款取得的债权转为对借款企业的股权。  
金融资产管理公司持有的股权，不受本公司净资产额或者注册资本的比例限制。

**Article 16** A financial asset management company may convert the creditor's rights obtained from acquiring non-performing loans of state-owned banks into equity in the borrowing enterprises.  
The equity held by a financial asset management company shall not be subject to the proportion restrictions of its net asset value or registered capital.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　实施债权转股权，应当贯彻国家产业政策，有利于优化经济结构，促进有关企业的技术进步和产品升级。

**Article 17** In implementing debt-to-equity conversion, the national industrial policy shall be implemented, which shall be conducive to optimizing the economic structure and promoting technological progress and product upgrading of relevant enterprises.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　实施债权转股权的企业，由国家经济贸易委员会向金融资产管理公司推荐。金融资产管理公司对被推荐的企业进行独立评审，制定企业债权转股权的方案并与企业签订债权转股权协议。债权转股权的方案和协议由国家经济贸易委员会会同财政部、中国人民银行审核，报国务院批准后实施。

**Article 18** Enterprises implementing debt-to-equity conversion shall be recommended by the State Economic and Trade Commission to the financial asset management company. The financial asset management company shall conduct an independent evaluation of the recommended enterprises, formulate a debt-to-equity conversion plan for the enterprises, and sign a debt-to-equity conversion agreement with the enterprises. The debt-to-equity conversion plan and agreement shall be reviewed by the State Economic and Trade Commission in conjunction with the Ministry of Finance and the People's Bank of China, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval before implementation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　实施债权转股权的企业，应当按照现代企业制度的要求，转换经营机制，建立规范的公司法人治理结构，加强企业管理。有关地方人民政府应当帮助企业减员增效、下岗分流，分离企业办社会的职能。

**Article 19** Enterprises implementing debt-to-equity conversion shall, in accordance with the requirements of the modern enterprise system, transform their operational mechanisms, establish a standardized corporate governance structure, and strengthen enterprise management. The relevant local people's governments shall assist enterprises in downsizing staff, increasing efficiency, diverting laid-off workers, and separating social functions from enterprises.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　金融资产管理公司的债权转股权后，作为企业的股东，可以派员参加企业董事会、监事会，依法行使股东权利。

**Article 20** After the debt-to-equity conversion by a financial asset management company, as a shareholder of the enterprise, it may send representatives to participate in the board of directors and the board of supervisors of the enterprise, and exercise shareholder rights according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　金融资产管理公司持有的企业股权，可以按照国家有关规定向境内外投资者转让，也可以由债权转股权企业依法回购。

**Article 21** The equity held by a financial asset management company in an enterprise may be transferred to domestic and foreign investors in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, or may be repurchased by the debt-to-equity conversion enterprise according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　企业实施债权转股权后，应当按照国家有关规定办理企业产权变更等有关登记。

**Article 22** After implementing debt-to-equity conversion, an enterprise shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, undergo registration for changes in enterprise property rights and other relevant matters.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家经济贸易委员会负责组织、指导、协调企业债权转股权工作。

**Article 23** The State Economic and Trade Commission shall be responsible for organizing, guiding and coordinating the debt-to-equity conversion work of enterprises.

### 第五章　公司的经营和管理

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　金融资产管理公司实行经营目标责任制。  
财政部根据不良贷款质量的情况，确定金融资产管理公司处置不良贷款的经营目标，并进行考核和监督。

**Article 24** A financial asset management company shall implement an operational target responsibility system.  
The Ministry of Finance shall, based on the quality of non-performing loans, determine the operational targets of the financial asset management company for disposing of non-performing loans, and shall conduct assessment and supervision.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　金融资产管理公司应当根据不良贷款的特点，制定经营方针和有关措施，完善内部治理结构，建立内部约束机制和激励机制。

**Article 25** A financial asset management company shall, based on the characteristics of non-performing loans, formulate operational policies and relevant measures, improve its internal governance structure, and establish internal restraint and incentive mechanisms.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　金融资产管理公司管理、处置因收购国有银行不良贷款形成的资产，应当按照公开、竞争、择优的原则运作。  
金融资产管理公司转让资产，主要采取招标、拍卖等方式。  
金融资产管理公司的债权因债务人破产等原因得不到清偿的，按照国务院的规定处理。  
金融资产管理公司资产处置管理办法由财政部制定。

**Article 26** A financial asset management company shall, in managing and disposing of assets formed from the acquisition of non-performing loans of state-owned banks, operate in accordance with the principles of openness, competition and selection of the best.  
A financial asset management company shall mainly adopt methods such as tendering and auctioning when transferring assets.  
If the creditor's rights of a financial asset management company cannot be satisfied due to bankruptcy of the debtor or other reasons, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.  
The administrative measures for asset disposal of financial asset management companies shall be formulated by the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　金融资产管理公司根据业务需要，可以聘请具有会计、资产评估和法律服务等资格的中介机构协助开展业务。

**Article 27** A financial asset management company may, based on business needs, engage intermediary agencies with qualifications in accounting, asset appraisal, legal services, etc. to assist in conducting business.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　金融资产管理公司免交在收购国有银行不良贷款和承接、处置因收购国有银行不良贷款形成的资产的业务活动中的税收。具体办法由财政部会同国家税务总局制定。  
金融资产管理公司免交工商登记注册费等行政性收费。

**Article 28** A financial asset management company shall be exempt from taxes in the business activities of acquiring non-performing loans of state-owned banks and undertaking and disposing of assets formed from the acquisition of non-performing loans of state-owned banks. Specific measures shall be formulated by the Ministry of Finance in conjunction with the State Administration of Taxation.  
A financial asset management company shall be exempt from administrative charges such as industrial and commercial registration fees.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　金融资产管理公司应当按照中国人民银行、财政部和中国证券监督管理委员会等有关部门的要求，报送财务、统计报表和其他有关材料。

**Article 29** A financial asset management company shall, in accordance with the requirements of the People's Bank of China, the Ministry of Finance, the China Securities Regulatory Commission and other relevant departments, submit financial and statistical reports and other relevant materials.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　金融资产管理公司应当依法接受审计机关的审计监督。  
金融资产管理公司应当聘请财政部认可的注册会计师对其财务状况进行年度审计，并将审计报告及时报送各有关监督管理部门。

**Article 30** A financial asset management company shall accept audit supervision by the audit authorities according to law.  
A financial asset management company shall engage certified public accountants recognized by the Ministry of Finance to conduct an annual audit of its financial status, and shall promptly submit the audit report to all relevant supervisory and administrative departments.

### 第六章　公司的终止和清算

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　金融资产管理公司终止时，由财政部组织清算组，进行清算。

**Article 31** When a financial asset management company is terminated, the Ministry of Finance shall organize a liquidation group to carry out liquidation.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　金融资产管理公司处置不良贷款形成的最终损失，由财政部提出解决方案，报国务院批准执行。

**Article 32** The final losses arising from the disposal of non-performing loans by a financial asset management company shall have a solution proposed by the Ministry of Finance, which shall be submitted to the State Council for approval and implementation.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　金融资产管理公司违反金融法律、行政法规的，由中国人民银行依照有关法律和《金融违法行为处罚办法》给予处罚；违反其他有关法律、行政法规的，由有关部门依法给予处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 33** If a financial asset management company violates financial laws and administrative regulations, it shall be punished by the People's Bank of China in accordance with the relevant laws and the Measures on Punishment for Financial Violations; if it violates other relevant laws and administrative regulations, it shall be punished by the relevant departments according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 34** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
