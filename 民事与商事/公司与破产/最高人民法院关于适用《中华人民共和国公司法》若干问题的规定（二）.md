# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》若干问题的规定（二）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：2014-02-20  
**生效日期**：2014-03-01

> （2008年5月5日最高人民法院审判委员会第1447次会议通过　根据2014年2月17日最高人民法院审判委员会第1607次会议《关于修改关于适用〈中华人民共和国公司法〉若干问题的规定的决定》修正） 为正确适用《中华人民共和国公司法》，结合审判实践，就人民法院审理公司解散和清算案件适用法律问题作出如下规定。 第一条　单独或者合计持有公司全部股东表决权百分之十以上的股东，以下列事由之一提起解散公司诉讼，并符合公司法第一百八十二条规定的，人民法院应予受理： （一）公司持续两年以上无法召开股东会或者股东大会，公司经营管理发生严重困难的； （二）股东表决时无法达到法定或者公司章程规定的比例，持续两年以上不能做出有效的股东会或者股东大会决议，公司经营管理发生严重困难的； （三）公司董事长期冲突，且无法通过股东会或者股东大会解决，公司经营管理发生严重困难的； （四）经营管理发生其他严重困难，公司继续存续会使股东利益受到重大损失的情形。 股东以知情权、利润分配请求权等权益受到损害，或者公司亏损、财产不足以偿还全部债务，以及公司被吊销企业法人营业执照未进行清算等为由，提起解散公司诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。 第二条　股东提起解散公司诉讼，同时又申请人民法院对公司进行清算的，人民法院对其提出的清算申请不予受理。人民法院可以告知原告，在人民法院判决解散公司后，依据公司法第一百八十三条和本规定第七条的规定，自行组织清算或者另行申请人民法院对公司进行清算。 第三条　股东提起解散公司诉讼时，向人民法院申请财产保全或者证据保全的，在股东提供担保且不影响公司正常经营的情形下，人民法院可予以保全。 第四条　股东提起解散公司诉讼应当以公司为被告。 原告以其他股东为被告一并提起诉讼的，人民法院应当告知原告将其他股东变更为第三人；原告坚持不予变更的，人民法院应当驳回原告对其他股东的起诉。 原告提起解散公司诉讼应当告知其他股东，或者由人民法院通知其参加诉讼。其他股东或者有关利害关系人申请以共同原告或者第三人身份参加诉讼的，人民法院应予准许。 第五条　人民法院审理解散公司诉讼案件，应当注重调解。当事人协商同意由公司或者股东收购股份，或者以减资等方式使公司存续，且不违反法律、行政法规强制性规定的，人民法院应予支持。当事人不能协商一致使公司存续的，人民法院应当及时判决。 经人民法院调解公司收购原告股份的，公司应当自调解书生效之日起六个月内将股份转让或者注销。股份转让或者注销之前，原告不得以公司收购其股份为由对抗公司债权人。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　单独或者合计持有公司全部股东表决权百分之十以上的股东，以下列事由之一提起解散公司诉讼，并符合[公司法第一百八十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-182)规定的，人民法院应予受理：  
（一）公司持续两年以上无法召开股东会或者股东大会，公司经营管理发生严重困难的；  
（二）股东表决时无法达到法定或者公司章程规定的比例，持续两年以上不能做出有效的股东会或者股东大会决议，公司经营管理发生严重困难的；  
（三）公司董事长期冲突，且无法通过股东会或者股东大会解决，公司经营管理发生严重困难的；  
（四）经营管理发生其他严重困难，公司继续存续会使股东利益受到重大损失的情形。  
股东以知情权、利润分配请求权等权益受到损害，或者公司亏损、财产不足以偿还全部债务，以及公司被吊销企业法人营业执照未进行清算等为由，提起解散公司诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 1** Where a shareholder individually or jointly holding 10% or more of the total voting rights of all shareholders of a company initiates a lawsuit for dissolution of the company on any of the following grounds, and meets the requirements of Article 182 of the Company Law, the People's Court shall accept the case:  
(1) The company has been unable to convene a shareholders' meeting or a general meeting of shareholders for two consecutive years or more, and the company's operation and management have suffered severe difficulties;  
(2) The shareholders cannot reach the statutory or the proportion stipulated in the company's Articles of Association when voting, and have been unable to make valid shareholders' meeting or general meeting of shareholders' resolutions for two consecutive years or more, and the company's operation and management have suffered severe difficulties;  
(3) There has been a long-term conflict among the directors of the company, and it cannot be resolved through the shareholders' meeting or the general meeting of shareholders, and the company's operation and management have suffered severe difficulties;  
(4) Other severe difficulties in operation and management occur, where the continued existence of the company would cause major losses to the interests of shareholders.  
If a shareholder initiates a lawsuit for dissolution of the company on the grounds that his rights to know, right to claim profit distribution, etc. are damaged, or that the company is at a loss, its property is insufficient to repay all debts, or that the company's business license of enterprise legal person has been revoked and no liquidation has been carried out, the People's Court shall not accept the case.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　股东提起解散公司诉讼，同时又申请人民法院对公司进行清算的，人民法院对其提出的清算申请不予受理。人民法院可以告知原告，在人民法院判决解散公司后，依据[公司法第一百八十三条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-183)和[本规定第七条](#art-7)的规定，自行组织清算或者另行申请人民法院对公司进行清算。

**Article 2** Where a shareholder initiates a lawsuit for dissolution of the company and simultaneously applies to the People's Court for liquidation of the company, the People's Court shall not accept the liquidation application. The People's Court may inform the plaintiff that, after the People's Court decides to dissolve the company, the plaintiff may organize liquidation on its own or separately apply to the People's Court for liquidation of the company in accordance with Article 183 of the Company Law and Article 7 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　股东提起解散公司诉讼时，向人民法院申请财产保全或者证据保全的，在股东提供担保且不影响公司正常经营的情形下，人民法院可予以保全。

**Article 3** Where a shareholder, when initiating a lawsuit for dissolution of the company, applies to the People's Court for property preservation or evidence preservation, the People's Court may grant the preservation provided that the shareholder provides a guarantee and the normal operation of the company is not affected.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　股东提起解散公司诉讼应当以公司为被告。  
原告以其他股东为被告一并提起诉讼的，人民法院应当告知原告将其他股东变更为第三人；原告坚持不予变更的，人民法院应当驳回原告对其他股东的起诉。  
原告提起解散公司诉讼应当告知其他股东，或者由人民法院通知其参加诉讼。其他股东或者有关利害关系人申请以共同原告或者第三人身份参加诉讼的，人民法院应予准许。

**Article 4** A shareholder initiating a lawsuit for dissolution of the company shall name the company as the defendant.  
If the plaintiff files a lawsuit naming other shareholders as defendants as well, the People's Court shall inform the plaintiff to change those other shareholders to third parties; if the plaintiff insists on not making the change, the People's Court shall dismiss the plaintiff's claim against those other shareholders.  
The plaintiff initiating a lawsuit for dissolution of the company shall inform the other shareholders, or the People's Court shall notify them to participate in the lawsuit. If any other shareholders or interested persons apply to participate in the lawsuit as co-plaintiffs or third parties, the People's Court shall permit such application.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院审理解散公司诉讼案件，应当注重调解。当事人协商同意由公司或者股东收购股份，或者以减资等方式使公司存续，且不违反法律、行政法规强制性规定的，人民法院应予支持。当事人不能协商一致使公司存续的，人民法院应当及时判决。  
经人民法院调解公司收购原告股份的，公司应当自调解书生效之日起六个月内将股份转让或者注销。股份转让或者注销之前，原告不得以公司收购其股份为由对抗公司债权人。

**Article 5** When hearing a case concerning a lawsuit for dissolution of the company, the People's Court shall focus on mediation. If the parties negotiate and agree that the company or shareholders shall purchase the shares, or that the company shall continue its existence through capital reduction or other means, and such agreement does not violate the mandatory provisions of laws or administrative regulations, the People's Court shall support it. If the parties cannot reach an agreement for the company to continue its existence, the People's Court shall render a judgment in a timely manner.  
If, through mediation by the People's Court, the company purchases the plaintiff's shares, the company shall transfer or cancel the shares within six months from the date the mediation document takes effect. Before the shares are transferred or canceled, the plaintiff shall not oppose the company's creditors on the ground that the company has purchased his shares.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院关于解散公司诉讼作出的判决，对公司全体股东具有法律约束力。  
人民法院判决驳回解散公司诉讼请求后，提起该诉讼的股东或者其他股东又以同一事实和理由提起解散公司诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 6** A judgment rendered by the People's Court concerning a lawsuit for dissolution of the company shall be legally binding on all shareholders of the company.  
After the People's Court has rendered a judgment dismissing a request for dissolution of the company, if the shareholder who initiated the lawsuit or any other shareholder files another lawsuit for dissolution of the company based on the same facts and grounds, the People's Court shall not accept the case.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　公司应当依照[公司法第一百八十三条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-183)的规定，在解散事由出现之日起十五日内成立清算组，开始自行清算。  
有下列情形之一，债权人申请人民法院指定清算组进行清算的，人民法院应予受理：  
（一）公司解散逾期不成立清算组进行清算的；  
（二）虽然成立清算组但故意拖延清算的；  
（三）违法清算可能严重损害债权人或者股东利益的。  
具有本条第二款所列情形，而债权人未提起清算申请，公司股东申请人民法院指定清算组对公司进行清算的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 7** The company shall, in accordance with Article 183 of the Company Law, establish a liquidation group within 15 days from the date on which the ground for dissolution arises, and commence self-liquidation.  
If any of the following circumstances exist, and a creditor applies to the People's Court for designation of a liquidation group to carry out liquidation, the People's Court shall accept the application:  
(1) The company fails to establish a liquidation group to carry out liquidation within the time limit after dissolution;  
(2) Although a liquidation group has been established, it deliberately delays the liquidation;  
(3) The illegal liquidation may seriously damage the interests of creditors or shareholders.  
If the circumstances listed in the preceding paragraph exist, and the creditor has not filed a liquidation application, but the company's shareholders apply to the People's Court for designation of a liquidation group to liquidate the company, the People's Court shall accept the application.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院受理公司清算案件，应当及时指定有关人员组成清算组。  
清算组成员可以从下列人员或者机构中产生：  
（一）公司股东、董事、监事、高级管理人员；  
（二）依法设立的律师事务所、会计师事务所、破产清算事务所等社会中介机构；  
（三）依法设立的律师事务所、会计师事务所、破产清算事务所等社会中介机构中具备相关专业知识并取得执业资格的人员。

**Article 8** After accepting a case of company liquidation, the People's Court shall promptly designate relevant personnel to form a liquidation group.  
Members of the liquidation group may be selected from the following persons or institutions:  
(1) Shareholders, directors, supervisors, and senior officers of the company;  
(2) Social intermediary institutions such as law firms, accounting firms, and bankruptcy liquidation firms established according to law;  
(3) Personnel who have relevant professional knowledge and have obtained practicing qualifications from among the social intermediary institutions such as law firms, accounting firms, and bankruptcy liquidation firms established according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院指定的清算组成员有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以根据债权人、股东的申请，或者依职权更换清算组成员：  
（一）有违反法律或者行政法规的行为；  
（二）丧失执业能力或者民事行为能力；  
（三）有严重损害公司或者债权人利益的行为。

**Article 9** Where a member of the liquidation group designated by the People's Court falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may replace the member upon the application of a creditor or a shareholder, or on its own initiative:  
(1) Having engaged in acts that violate laws or administrative regulations;  
(2) Having lost his practicing ability or civil capacity;  
(3) Having engaged in acts that seriously damage the interests of the company or creditors.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　公司依法清算结束并办理注销登记前，有关公司的民事诉讼，应当以公司的名义进行。  
公司成立清算组的，由清算组负责人代表公司参加诉讼；尚未成立清算组的，由原法定代表人代表公司参加诉讼。

**Article 10** Before a company's liquidation according to law is completed and its cancellation registration is handled, any civil action related to the company shall be conducted in the name of the company.  
If the company has established a liquidation group, the person in charge of the liquidation group shall represent the company in the action; if no liquidation group has been established, the original legal representative shall represent the company in the action.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　公司清算时，清算组应当按照[公司法第一百八十五条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-185)的规定，将公司解散清算事宜书面通知全体已知债权人，并根据公司规模和营业地域范围在全国或者公司注册登记地省级有影响的报纸上进行公告。  
清算组未按照前款规定履行通知和公告义务，导致债权人未及时申报债权而未获清偿，债权人主张清算组成员对因此造成的损失承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应依法予以支持。

**Article 11** When a company is under liquidation, the liquidation group shall, in accordance with Article 185 of the Company Law, notify in writing all known creditors of the matters concerning the company's dissolution and liquidation, and shall publish a notice on a nationally influential newspaper or a newspaper influential at the provincial level of the place where the company is registered, depending on the company's scale and business scope.  
If the liquidation group fails to perform its obligation of notification and publication as provided in the preceding paragraph, resulting in a creditor's failure to timely declare its claim and thus not being satisfied, and the creditor claims that the members of the liquidation group shall bear compensation liability for the losses caused thereby, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　公司清算时，债权人对清算组核定的债权有异议的，可以要求清算组重新核定。清算组不予重新核定，或者债权人对重新核定的债权仍有异议，债权人以公司为被告向人民法院提起诉讼请求确认的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 12** When a company is under liquidation, if a creditor has an objection to the claim verified by the liquidation group, the creditor may request the liquidation group to re-verify. If the liquidation group refuses to re-verify, or the creditor still has an objection to the re-verified claim, and the creditor files a lawsuit against the company as the defendant with the People's Court for confirmation, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　债权人在规定的期限内未申报债权，在公司清算程序终结前补充申报的，清算组应予登记。  
公司清算程序终结，是指清算报告经股东会、股东大会或者人民法院确认完毕。

**Article 13** If a creditor fails to declare a claim within the prescribed period and makes a supplementary declaration before the conclusion of the company's liquidation procedure, the liquidation group shall register such claim.  
The conclusion of the company's liquidation procedure refers to the completion of confirmation of the liquidation report by the shareholders' meeting, the general meeting of shareholders, or the People's Court.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　债权人补充申报的债权，可以在公司尚未分配财产中依法清偿。公司尚未分配财产不能全额清偿，债权人主张股东以其在剩余财产分配中已经取得的财产予以清偿的，人民法院应予支持；但债权人因重大过错未在规定期限内申报债权的除外。  
债权人或者清算组，以公司尚未分配财产和股东在剩余财产分配中已经取得的财产，不能全额清偿补充申报的债权为由，向人民法院提出破产清算申请的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 14** A claim supplemented and declared by a creditor may be satisfied out of the undistributed property of the company according to law. If the undistributed property of the company is insufficient for full satisfaction, and the creditor claims that the shareholder shall be satisfied out of the property already obtained by the shareholder in the distribution of residual property, the People's Court shall support such claim, except where the creditor fails to declare the claim within the prescribed period due to gross negligence.  
If the creditor or the liquidation group files an application for bankruptcy liquidation with the People's Court on the ground that the undistributed property of the company and the property already obtained by the shareholder in the distribution of residual property are insufficient for full satisfaction of the supplemented and declared claim, the People's Court shall not accept such application.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　公司自行清算的，清算方案应当报股东会或者股东大会决议确认；人民法院组织清算的，清算方案应当报人民法院确认。未经确认的清算方案，清算组不得执行。  
执行未经确认的清算方案给公司或者债权人造成损失，公司、股东或者债权人主张清算组成员承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应依法予以支持。

**Article 15** If the company carries out self-liquidation, the liquidation plan shall be submitted to the shareholders' meeting or the general meeting of shareholders for resolution and confirmation; if the People's Court organizes the liquidation, the liquidation plan shall be submitted to the People's Court for confirmation. A liquidation plan that has not been confirmed shall not be executed by the liquidation group.  
If the execution of a liquidation plan that has not been confirmed causes losses to the company or creditors, and the company, shareholders, or creditors claim that the members of the liquidation group shall bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院组织清算的，清算组应当自成立之日起六个月内清算完毕。  
因特殊情况无法在六个月内完成清算的，清算组应当向人民法院申请延长。

**Article 16** If the People's Court organizes the liquidation, the liquidation group shall complete the liquidation within six months from the date of its establishment.  
If the liquidation cannot be completed within six months due to special circumstances, the liquidation group shall apply to the People's Court for an extension.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人民法院指定的清算组在清理公司财产、编制资产负债表和财产清单时，发现公司财产不足清偿债务的，可以与债权人协商制作有关债务清偿方案。  
债务清偿方案经全体债权人确认且不损害其他利害关系人利益的，人民法院可依清算组的申请裁定予以认可。清算组依据该清偿方案清偿债务后，应当向人民法院申请裁定终结清算程序。  
债权人对债务清偿方案不予确认或者人民法院不予认可的，清算组应当依法向人民法院申请宣告破产。

**Article 17** When a liquidation group designated by the People's Court, while liquidating the company's property and preparing the balance sheet and inventory of property, discovers that the company's property is insufficient to pay off its debts, it may negotiate with the creditors to formulate a relevant debt settlement plan.  
If the debt settlement plan is confirmed by all creditors and does not harm the interests of other interested persons, the People's Court may, upon the application of the liquidation group, issue a ruling to approve it. After the liquidation group has paid off the debts in accordance with the settlement plan, it shall apply to the People's Court for a ruling to terminate the liquidation procedure.  
If the creditors do not confirm the debt settlement plan or the People's Court does not approve it, the liquidation group shall apply to the People's Court for a declaration of bankruptcy according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　有限责任公司的股东、股份有限公司的董事和控股股东未在法定期限内成立清算组开始清算，导致公司财产贬值、流失、毁损或者灭失，债权人主张其在造成损失范围内对公司债务承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应依法予以支持。  
有限责任公司的股东、股份有限公司的董事和控股股东因怠于履行义务，导致公司主要财产、账册、重要文件等灭失，无法进行清算，债权人主张其对公司债务承担连带清偿责任的，人民法院应依法予以支持。  
上述情形系实际控制人原因造成，债权人主张实际控制人对公司债务承担相应民事责任的，人民法院应依法予以支持。

**Article 18** Where the shareholders of a limited liability company, or the directors and controlling shareholders of a joint stock limited company, fail to establish a liquidation group and commence liquidation within the statutory time limit, causing depreciation, loss, damage, or destruction of the company's property, and the creditor claims that they shall bear compensation liability for the company's debts within the scope of the losses caused, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.  
Where the shareholders of a limited liability company, or the directors and controlling shareholders of a joint stock limited company, neglect to perform their obligations, causing the loss or destruction of the company's main property, account books, important documents, etc., making it impossible to carry out liquidation, and the creditor claims that they shall bear joint and several liability for the company's debts, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.  
If the above circumstances are caused by the actual controller, and the creditor claims that the actual controller shall bear corresponding civil liability for the company's debts, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　有限责任公司的股东、股份有限公司的董事和控股股东，以及公司的实际控制人在公司解散后，恶意处置公司财产给债权人造成损失，或者未经依法清算，以虚假的清算报告骗取公司登记机关办理法人注销登记，债权人主张其对公司债务承担相应赔偿责任的，人民法院应依法予以支持。

**Article 19** Where, after the dissolution of the company, the shareholders of a limited liability company, the directors and controlling shareholders of a joint stock limited company, or the actual controller of the company maliciously dispose of the company's property, causing losses to creditors, or, without liquidation according to law, obtain the registration of legal person cancellation from the company registration authority by using a false liquidation report, and the creditor claims that they shall bear corresponding compensation liability for the company's debts, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　公司解散应当在依法清算完毕后，申请办理注销登记。公司未经清算即办理注销登记，导致公司无法进行清算，债权人主张有限责任公司的股东、股份有限公司的董事和控股股东，以及公司的实际控制人对公司债务承担清偿责任的，人民法院应依法予以支持。  
公司未经依法清算即办理注销登记，股东或者第三人在公司登记机关办理注销登记时承诺对公司债务承担责任，债权人主张其对公司债务承担相应民事责任的，人民法院应依法予以支持。

**Article 20** A company shall apply for cancellation registration after the completion of liquidation according to law. If the company undergoes cancellation registration without liquidation, making it impossible to carry out liquidation, and the creditor claims that the shareholders of a limited liability company, the directors and controlling shareholders of a joint stock limited company, and the actual controller of the company shall bear liability for satisfying the company's debts, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.  
If the company undergoes cancellation registration without liquidation according to law, and a shareholder or a third party has promised to bear responsibility for the company's debts at the time of registration, and the creditor claims that such shareholder or third party shall bear corresponding civil liability for the company's debts, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　有限责任公司的股东、股份有限公司的董事和控股股东，以及公司的实际控制人为二人以上的，其中一人或者数人按照[本规定第十八条](#art-18)和第二十条第一款的规定承担民事责任后，主张其他人员按照过错大小分担责任的，人民法院应依法予以支持。

**Article 21** Where there are two or more shareholders of a limited liability company, directors and controlling shareholders of a joint stock limited company, and actual controllers of the company, after one or several of them have borne civil liability in accordance with Article 18 and paragraph 1 of Article 20 of these Provisions, if they claim that the other persons shall share the liability in proportion to their fault, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　公司解散时，股东尚未缴纳的出资均应作为清算财产。股东尚未缴纳的出资，包括到期应缴未缴的出资，以及依照[公司法第二十六条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-26)和第八十条的规定分期缴纳尚未届满缴纳期限的出资。  
公司财产不足以清偿债务时，债权人主张未缴出资股东，以及公司设立时的其他股东或者发起人在未缴出资范围内对公司债务承担连带清偿责任的，人民法院应依法予以支持。

**Article 22** When a company is dissolved, the capital contributions that have not yet been paid by the shareholders shall all be deemed as liquidation property. The capital contributions not yet paid by shareholders include those that are due but not paid, as well as those to be paid in installments in accordance with Articles 26 and 80 of the Company Law, for which the time limit for payment has not yet expired.  
If the company's property is insufficient to pay off its debts, and the creditor claims that the shareholders who have not paid their capital contributions, as well as other shareholders or promoters at the time of the company's establishment, shall bear joint and several liability for the company's debts to the extent of the unpaid capital contributions, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　清算组成员从事清算事务时，违反法律、行政法规或者公司章程给公司或者债权人造成损失，公司或者债权人主张其承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应依法予以支持。  
有限责任公司的股东、股份有限公司连续一百八十日以上单独或者合计持有公司百分之一以上股份的股东，依据[公司法第一百五十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-151)第三款的规定，以清算组成员有前款所述行为为由向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。  
公司已经清算完毕注销，上述股东参照[公司法第一百五十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-151)第三款的规定，直接以清算组成员为被告、其他股东为第三人向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 23** Where a member of a liquidation group, when handling liquidation affairs, violates laws, administrative regulations, or the company's articles of association, causing losses to the company or creditors, and the company or creditors claim that the member shall bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.  
Where a shareholder of a limited liability company, or a shareholder of a joint stock limited company who individually or jointly holds 1% or more of the company's shares for 180 consecutive days or more, files a lawsuit with the People's Court on the ground that a member of the liquidation group has committed the acts described in the preceding paragraph, in accordance with paragraph 3 of Article 151 of the Company Law, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
If the company has completed liquidation and cancellation, and the aforementioned shareholder directly files a lawsuit with the People's Court naming the member of the liquidation group as the defendant and other shareholders as third parties, by reference to paragraph 3 of Article 151 of the Company Law, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　解散公司诉讼案件和公司清算案件由公司住所地人民法院管辖。公司住所地是指公司主要办事机构所在地。公司办事机构所在地不明确的，由其注册地人民法院管辖。  
基层人民法院管辖县、县级市或者区的公司登记机关核准登记公司的解散诉讼案件和公司清算案件；中级人民法院管辖地区、地级市以上的公司登记机关核准登记公司的解散诉讼案件和公司清算案件。

**Article 24** Cases concerning lawsuits for dissolution of a company and cases concerning company liquidation shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the company is domiciled. The domicile of a company refers to the location of its principal place of business. If the location of the company's place of business is unclear, the People's Court of the place of its registration shall have jurisdiction.  
The Primary People's Court shall have jurisdiction over cases concerning lawsuits for dissolution and company liquidation for companies registered with the company registration authority of a county, county-level city, or district; the Intermediate People's Court shall have jurisdiction over cases concerning lawsuits for dissolution and company liquidation for companies registered with the company registration authority at the prefecture level, prefecture-level city level, or above.
