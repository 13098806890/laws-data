### 最高人民法院关于金融机构为行政机关批准开办的公司提供注册资金验资报告不实应当承担责任问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于金融机构为行政机关批准开办的公司  
  
提供注册资金验资报告不实应当承担责任问题的批复  
  
法复〔1996〕3号  
  
四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院川高法〔1995〕194号《关于金融机构为行政机关批准开办的公司提供注册资金验资报告不实，应否承担公司资不抵债的还款责任问题的请示》已收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
金融机构根据行政机关出具的注册资金证明，为该行政机关批准开办的公司出具不实的验资报告，公司因资不抵债无力偿还债务，给债权人造成损失的，金融机构除应退出收取的验资手续费外，还应当在该注册资金范围内承担与其过错相应的民事责任；金融机构按照验资程序进行审查核实，公司注册登记后又抽逃资金的，金融机构不承担退出验资手续费和赔偿损失的责任。  
  
1996年3月27日

**Article 1** Reply on Issues Concerning Liability for Providing False Capital Verification Reports for Registered Capital  
Fa Fu [1996] No. 3  
Sichuan Higher People's Court:  
Your Court's Chuan Gao Fa [1995] No. 194 "Request for Instructions on Whether Financial Institutions Should Bear the Responsibility for Repayment of Debts When the Company Is Insolvent Due to False Capital Verification Reports Provided for Companies Approved by Administrative Organs" has been received. Upon deliberation, the reply is as follows:  
Where a financial institution, based on the certificate of registered capital issued by an administrative organ, issues a false capital verification report for a company approved to be established by such administrative organ, and the company is unable to repay its debts due to insolvency, thereby causing losses to creditors, the financial institution shall, in addition to refunding the collected capital verification service fees, also assume civil liability within the scope of the registered capital in proportion to its fault; where the financial institution has examined and verified in accordance with the capital verification procedures, and the company withdraws its capital after registration, the financial institution shall not bear the liability for refunding the capital verification service fees or compensating for losses.  
March 27, 1996
