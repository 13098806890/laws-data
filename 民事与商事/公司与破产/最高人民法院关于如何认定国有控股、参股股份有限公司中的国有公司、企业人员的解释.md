### 最高人民法院关于如何认定国有控股、参股股份有限公司中的国有公司、企业人员的解释

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于如何认定国有控股、参股股份有限公司中的国有公司、企业人员的解释  
  
法释［ 2005］10号  
  
为准确认定刑法分则第三章第三节中的国有公司、企业人员，现对国有控股、参股的股份有限公司中的国有公司、企业人员解释如下：  
  
国有公司、企业委派到国有控股、参股公司从事公务的人员，以国有公司、企业人员论。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Interpretation on How to Determine Persons of State-owned Companies and Enterprises in State-controlled and State-invested Joint-stock Limited Companies  
Fa Shi [2005] No. 10  
For the accurate determination of persons of state-owned companies and enterprises in Section 3 of Chapter 3 of the Specific Provisions of the Criminal Law, the following interpretation is given regarding persons of state-owned companies and enterprises in state-controlled and state-invested joint-stock limited companies:  
Persons dispatched by state-owned companies or enterprises to state-controlled or state-invested companies to perform public duties shall be deemed as persons of state-owned companies and enterprises.
