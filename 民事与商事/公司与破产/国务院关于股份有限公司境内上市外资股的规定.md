# 国务院关于股份有限公司境内上市外资股的规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：1995-12-25  
**生效日期**：1995-12-25

> (1995年11月2日国务院第37次常务会议通过　1995年12月25日中华人民共和国国务院令第189号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一条　为了规范股份有限公司境内上市外资股的发行及交易，保护投资人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国公司法》(以下简称《公司法》)的有关规定，制定本规定。 第二条　经国务院证券委员会批准，股份有限公司(以下简称公司)可以发行境内上市外资股；但是，拟发行境内上市外资股的面值总额超过3000万美元的，国务院证券委员会应当报国务院批准。 前款所称公司发行境内上市外资股，包括以募集方式设立公司发行境内上市外资股和公司增加资本发行境内上市外资股。 国务院证券委员会批准发行境内上市外资股的总额应当控制在国家确定的总规模之内。 第三条　公司发行的境内上市外资股，采取记名股票形式，以人民币标明面值，以外币认购、买卖，在境内证券交易所上市交易。 发行境内上市外资股的公司向境内投资人发行的股份(以下简称内资股)，采取记名股票形式。 第四条　境内上市外资股投资人限于： (一)外国的自然人、法人和其他组织； (二)中国香港、澳门、台湾地区的自然人、法人和其他组织； (三)定居在国外的中国公民； (四)国务院证券委员会规定的境内上市外资股其他投资人。 境内上市外资股投资人认购、买卖境内上市外资股，应当提供证明其投资人身份和资格的有效文件。 第五条　持有同一种类股份的境内上市外资股股东与内资股股东，依照《公司法》享有同等权利和履行同等义务。 公司可以在其公司章程中对股东行使权利和履行义务的特殊事宜，作出具体规定。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范股份有限公司境内上市外资股的发行及交易，保护投资人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国公司法》(以下简称《公司法》)的有关规定，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the "Company Law") for the purposes of regulating the issuance and trading of foreign capital shares listed domestically by companies limited by shares and protecting the legitimate rights and interests of investors.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　经国务院证券委员会批准，股份有限公司(以下简称公司)可以发行境内上市外资股；但是，拟发行境内上市外资股的面值总额超过3000万美元的，国务院证券委员会应当报国务院批准。  
前款所称公司发行境内上市外资股，包括以募集方式设立公司发行境内上市外资股和公司增加资本发行境内上市外资股。  
国务院证券委员会批准发行境内上市外资股的总额应当控制在国家确定的总规模之内。

**Article 2** A company limited by shares (hereinafter referred to as the "Company") may issue foreign capital shares listed domestically upon approval by the Securities Committee of the State Council; however, if the total par value of the proposed issuance of foreign capital shares listed domestically exceeds USD 30 million, the Securities Committee of the State Council shall report to the State Council for approval.  
The issuance of foreign capital shares listed domestically by a Company as mentioned in the preceding paragraph includes the issuance of foreign capital shares listed domestically upon the establishment of a Company by means of offer and the issuance of foreign capital shares listed domestically for increasing capital.  
The total amount of foreign capital shares listed domestically approved by the Securities Committee of the State Council shall be controlled within the total scale determined by the State.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　公司发行的境内上市外资股，采取记名股票形式，以人民币标明面值，以外币认购、买卖，在境内证券交易所上市交易。  
发行境内上市外资股的公司向境内投资人发行的股份(以下简称内资股)，采取记名股票形式。

**Article 3** The foreign capital shares listed domestically issued by a Company shall be in the form of registered shares, with the par value denominated in Renminbi, subscribed for and traded in foreign currency, and listed and traded on a domestic stock exchange.  
The shares issued by a Company issuing foreign capital shares listed domestically to domestic investors (hereinafter referred to as "Domestic Shares") shall be in the form of registered shares.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　境内上市外资股投资人限于：  
(一)外国的自然人、法人和其他组织；  
(二)中国香港、澳门、台湾地区的自然人、法人和其他组织；  
(三)定居在国外的中国公民；  
(四)国务院证券委员会规定的境内上市外资股其他投资人。  
境内上市外资股投资人认购、买卖境内上市外资股，应当提供证明其投资人身份和资格的有效文件。

**Article 4** Investors in foreign capital shares listed domestically shall be limited to:  
(1) foreign natural persons, legal persons, and other organizations;  
(2) natural persons, legal persons, and other organizations from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Macao Special Administrative Region, and Taiwan region of China;  
(3) Chinese citizens residing abroad; and  
(4) other investors in foreign capital shares listed domestically as prescribed by the Securities Committee of the State Council.  
Investors in foreign capital shares listed domestically, when subscribing for or trading in foreign capital shares listed domestically, shall provide valid documents certifying their identities and qualifications as investors.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　持有同一种类股份的境内上市外资股股东与内资股股东，依照《公司法》享有同等权利和履行同等义务。  
公司可以在其公司章程中对股东行使权利和履行义务的特殊事宜，作出具体规定。

**Article 5** Shareholders holding the same class of foreign capital shares listed domestically and shareholders holding Domestic Shares shall, in accordance with the Company Law, enjoy the same rights and perform the same obligations.  
A Company may, in its articles of association, make specific provisions on special matters concerning the exercise of rights and performance of obligations by shareholders.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　公司章程对公司及其股东、董事、监事、经理和其他高级管理人员具有约束力。  
公司的董事、监事、经理和其他高级管理人员对公司负有诚信和勤勉的义务。  
本条第一款、第二款所称其他高级管理人员包括公司财务负责人、董事会秘书和公司章程规定的其他人员。

**Article 6** The articles of association of a Company shall be binding on the Company, its shareholders, directors, supervisors, managers, and other senior officers.  
The directors, supervisors, managers, and other senior officers of a Company shall owe duties of good faith and diligence to the Company.  
"Other senior officers" as mentioned in the first and second paragraphs of this Article shall include the person in charge of financial affairs of the Company, the secretary of the board of directors, and other persons as specified in the articles of association of the Company.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院证券委员会及其监督管理执行机构中国证券监督管理委员会(以下简称中国证监会)，依照法律、行政法规的规定，对境内上市外资股的发行、交易及相关活动实施管理和监督。

**Article 7** The Securities Committee of the State Council and its executive body for supervision and administration, the China Securities Regulatory Commission (hereinafter referred to as the "CSRC"), shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, exercise administration and supervision over the issuance, trading, and related activities of foreign capital shares listed domestically.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　以募集方式设立公司，申请发行境内上市外资股的，应当符合下列条件：  
(一)所筹资金用途符合国家产业政策；  
(二)符合国家有关固定资产投资立项的规定；  
(三)符合国家有关利用外资的规定；  
(四)发起人认购的股本总额不少于公司拟发行股本总额的35%；  
(五)发起人出资总额不少于1.5亿元人民币；  
(六)拟向社会发行的股份达公司股份总数的25%以上；拟发行的股本总额超过4亿元人民币的，其拟向社会发行股份的比例达15%以上；  
(七)改组设立公司的原有企业或者作为公司主要发起人的国有企业，在最近3年内没有重大违法行为；  
(八)改组设立公司的原有企业或者作为公司主要发起人的国有企业，最近3年连续盈利；  
(九)国务院证券委员会规定的其他条件。

**Article 8** Where a Company is to be established by means of offer and applies for the issuance of foreign capital shares listed domestically, it shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) the use of the funds raised is in compliance with the state industrial policies;  
(2) it complies with the state provisions on the establishment of fixed asset investment projects;  
(3) it complies with the state provisions on the utilization of foreign capital;  
(4) the total share capital subscribed for by the promoters is not less than 35% of the total share capital proposed to be issued by the Company;  
(5) the total amount of capital contributed by the promoters is not less than RMB 150 million;  
(6) the shares proposed to be issued to the public account for not less than 25% of the total shares of the Company; where the total share capital proposed to be issued exceeds RMB 400 million, the proportion of the shares proposed to be issued to the public shall be not less than 15%;  
(7) the original enterprise that is restructured to establish the Company or the state-owned enterprise that serves as the main promoter of the Company has not committed any major illegal acts in the most recent three years;  
(8) the original enterprise that is restructured to establish the Company or the state-owned enterprise that serves as the main promoter of the Company has been profitable for the most recent three consecutive years; and  
(9) other conditions as prescribed by the Securities Committee of the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　公司增加资本，申请发行境内上市外资股的，除应当符合[本规定第八条](#art-8)第(一)、(二)、(三)项的规定外，还应当符合下列条件：  
(一)公司前一次发行的股份已经募足，所得资金的用途与募股时确定的用途相符，并且资金使用效益良好；  
(二)公司净资产总值不低于1.5亿元人民币；  
(三)公司从前一次发行股票到本次申请期间没有重大违法行为；  
(四)公司最近3年连续盈利；原有企业改组或者国有企业作为主要发起人设立的公司，可以连续计算；  
(五)国务院证券委员会规定的其他条件。  
以发起方式设立的公司首次增加资本，申请发行境内上市外资股的，还应当符合[本规定第八条](#art-8)第(六)项的规定。

**Article 9** Where a Company applies for the issuance of foreign capital shares listed domestically to increase its capital, in addition to meeting the conditions specified in subparagraphs (1), (2), and (3) of Article 8 of these Provisions, it shall also meet the following conditions:  
(1) the shares issued in the previous issuance have been fully subscribed, and the use of the funds raised is consistent with the purpose determined at the time of the share offer, and the funds have been used in a manner that yields good benefits;  
(2) the total net assets of the Company are not less than RMB 150 million;  
(3) the Company has not committed any major illegal acts during the period from the previous share issuance to the current application;  
(4) the Company has been profitable for the most recent three consecutive years; for a Company established by restructuring an original enterprise or with a state-owned enterprise as the main promoter, the profit period may be calculated consecutively; and  
(5) other conditions as prescribed by the Securities Committee of the State Council.  
Where a Company established by means of promotion applies for the issuance of foreign capital shares listed domestically for its first increase of capital, it shall also meet the condition specified in subparagraph (6) of Article 8 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　申请发行境内上市外资股，按照下列程序办理：  
(一)发起人或者公司向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者国务院有关企业主管部门提出申请，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者国务院有关企业主管部门向国务院证券委员会推荐；  
(二)国务院证券委员会会商国务院有关部门选定可以发行境内上市外资股的公司；  
(三)被选定的公司将[本规定第十一条](#art-11)、第十二条所列文件提交中国证监会审核；  
(四)经中国证监会审核符合条件的，报经国务院证券委员会批准或者依照[本规定第二条](#art-2)第一款的规定经国务院批准后，公司方可发行境内上市外资股。

**Article 10** The application for the issuance of foreign capital shares listed domestically shall be handled in accordance with the following procedures:  
(1) the promoter or the Company shall submit an application to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or the relevant competent department of the State Council, and the said people's government or department shall recommend the application to the Securities Committee of the State Council;  
(2) the Securities Committee of the State Council shall, after consultation with the relevant departments of the State Council, select the Company that may issue foreign capital shares listed domestically;  
(3) the selected Company shall submit the documents listed in Articles 11 and 12 of these Provisions to the CSRC for review; and  
(4) after the CSRC verifies that the conditions are met, the Company may issue foreign capital shares listed domestically only after the approval of the Securities Committee of the State Council or, in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 2 of these Provisions, the approval of the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　以募集方式设立公司，申请发行境内上市外资股的，应当向中国证监会报送下列文件：  
(一)申请报告；  
(二)发起人姓名或者名称，发起人认购的股份数、出资种类及验资证明；  
(三)发起人会议同意公开发行境内上市外资股的决议；  
(四)国务院授权的部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准设立公司的文件；  
(五)省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者国务院有关企业主管部门的推荐文件；  
(六)公司登记机关颁发的《企业名称预先核准通知书》；  
(七)公司章程草案；  
(八)招股说明书；  
(九)资金运用的可行性报告；所筹资金用于固定资产投资项目需要立项审批的，还应当提供有关部门同意固定资产投资立项的批准文件；  
(十)经注册会计师及其所在事务所审计的原有企业或者作为公司主要发起人的国有企业最近3年的财务报告和有2名以上注册会计师及其所在事务所签字、盖章的审计报告；  
(十一)经2名以上专业评估人员及其所在机构签字、盖章的资产评估报告；涉及国有资产的，还应当提供国有资产管理部门出具的确认文件及国有股权的批准文件；  
(十二)经2名以上律师及其所在事务所就有关事项签字、盖章的法律意见书；  
(十三)股票发行承销方案和承销协议；  
(十四)中国证监会要求提供的其他文件。

**Article 11** Where a Company is to be established by means of offer and applies for the issuance of foreign capital shares listed domestically, it shall submit the following documents to the CSRC:  
(1) an application report;  
(2) the names or titles of the promoters, the number of shares subscribed for by the promoters, the types of capital contributions, and the capital verification certificate;  
(3) the resolution of the promoters' meeting approving the public issuance of foreign capital shares listed domestically;  
(4) the document approving the establishment of the Company issued by the department authorized by the State Council or the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government;  
(5) the recommendation document of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or the relevant competent department of the State Council;  
(6) the "Notice of Pre-approval of Enterprise Name" issued by the company registration authority;  
(7) the draft articles of association of the Company;  
(8) the prospectus;  
(9) the feasibility report on the use of funds; where the funds raised are to be used for fixed asset investment projects requiring project approval, the approval document of the relevant department approving the establishment of the fixed asset investment project shall also be provided;  
(10) the financial reports of the original enterprise that is restructured to establish the Company or the state-owned enterprise that serves as the main promoter for the most recent three years, audited by certified public accountants and their firms, and the audit report signed and sealed by no fewer than two certified public accountants and their firms;  
(11) the asset appraisal report signed and sealed by no fewer than two professional appraisers and their organizations; where state-owned assets are involved, the confirmation document issued by the state-owned assets administration department and the approval document for the state-owned equity shall also be provided;  
(12) the legal opinion signed and sealed by no fewer than two lawyers and their firms on relevant matters;  
(13) the share issuance underwriting plan and the underwriting agreement; and  
(14) other documents as required by the CSRC.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　公司增加资本，申请发行境内上市外资股的，应当向中国证监会报送下列文件：  
(一)申请报告；  
(二)股东大会同意公开发行境内上市外资股的决议；  
(三)国务院授权的部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府同意增资发行新股的文件；  
(四)省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者国务院有关企业主管部门的推荐文件；  
(五)公司登记机关颁发的公司营业执照；  
(六)公司章程；  
(七)招股说明书；  
(八)资金运用的可行性报告；所筹资金用于固定资产投资项目需要立项审批的，还应当提供有关部门同意固定资产投资立项的批准文件；  
(九)经注册会计师及其所在事务所审计的公司最近3年的财务报告和有2名以上注册会计师及其所在事务所签字、盖章的审计报告；  
(十)经2名以上律师及其所在事务所就有关事项签字、盖章的法律意见书；  
(十一)股票发行承销方案和承销协议；  
(十二)中国证监会要求提供的其他文件。

**Article 12** Where a Company applies for the issuance of foreign capital shares listed domestically to increase its capital, it shall submit the following documents to the CSRC:  
(1) an application report;  
(2) the resolution of the shareholders' general meeting approving the public issuance of foreign capital shares listed domestically;  
(3) the document approving the increase of capital and issuance of new shares issued by the department authorized by the State Council or the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government;  
(4) the recommendation document of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or the relevant competent department of the State Council;  
(5) the business license of the Company issued by the company registration authority;  
(6) the articles of association of the Company;  
(7) the prospectus;  
(8) the feasibility report on the use of funds; where the funds raised are to be used for fixed asset investment projects requiring project approval, the approval document of the relevant department approving the establishment of the fixed asset investment project shall also be provided;  
(9) the financial reports of the Company for the most recent three years, audited by certified public accountants and their firms, and the audit report signed and sealed by no fewer than two certified public accountants and their firms;  
(10) the legal opinion signed and sealed by no fewer than two lawyers and their firms on relevant matters;  
(11) the share issuance underwriting plan and the underwriting agreement; and  
(12) other documents as required by the CSRC.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　公司发行境内上市外资股与发行内资股的间隔时间可以少于12个月。

**Article 13** The interval between a Company's issuance of foreign capital shares listed domestically and its issuance of Domestic Shares may be less than twelve months.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　公司应当聘用符合国家规定的注册会计师及其所在事务所，对其财务报告进行审计或者复核。

**Article 14** A Company shall engage certified public accountants and their firms that meet the state requirements to audit or review its financial reports.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　公司应当按照国家有关规定进行会计核算和编制财务报告。  
公司向境内上市外资股投资人披露的财务报告，按照其他国家或者地区的会计准则进行相应调整的，应当对有关差异作出说明。

**Article 15** A Company shall conduct accounting and prepare financial reports in accordance with the relevant state provisions.  
Where the financial reports disclosed by a Company to investors in foreign capital shares listed domestically are adjusted in accordance with the accounting standards of another country or region, explanations shall be given for the relevant differences.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　发行境内上市外资股的公司应当依法向社会公众披露信息，并在其公司章程中对信息披露的地点、方式等事宜作出具体规定。

**Article 16** A Company that issues foreign capital shares listed domestically shall disclose information to the public according to law and shall, in its articles of association, make specific provisions on matters such as the place and method of information disclosure.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　发行境内上市外资股的公司的信息披露文件，以中文制作；需要提供外文译本的，应当提供一种通用的外国语言文本。中文文本、外文文本发生歧义时，以中文文本为准。

**Article 17** The information disclosure documents of a Company that issues foreign capital shares listed domestically shall be prepared in Chinese; where a foreign language translation is required, a commonly used foreign language text shall be provided. In the event of any discrepancy between the Chinese text and the foreign language text, the Chinese text shall prevail.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　公司发行境内上市外资股，应当委托中国人民银行依法批准设立并经中国证监会认可的境内证券经营机构作为主承销商或者主承销商之一。

**Article 18** A Company issuing foreign capital shares listed domestically shall entrust a domestic securities institution that has been approved to be established according to law by the People's Bank of China and recognized by the CSRC to serve as the lead underwriter or one of the lead underwriters.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　发行境内上市外资股的公司，应当在具有经营外汇业务资格的境内银行开立外汇帐户。公司开立外汇帐户应当按照国家有关外汇管理的规定办理。  
承销境内上市外资股的主承销商应当在承销协议约定的期限内，将所筹款项划入发行境内上市外资股的公司的外汇帐户。

**Article 19** A Company that issues foreign capital shares listed domestically shall open a foreign exchange account with a domestic bank that is qualified to engage in foreign exchange business. The opening of a foreign exchange account by the Company shall be handled in accordance with the state provisions on foreign exchange control.  
The lead underwriter underwriting foreign capital shares listed domestically shall, within the period agreed upon in the underwriting agreement, transfer the funds raised to the foreign exchange account of the Company that issued the foreign capital shares listed domestically.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　境内上市外资股股票的代理买卖业务，应当由中国人民银行依法批准设立并经中国证监会认可的证券经营机构办理。

**Article 20** The business of acting as agent for the purchase and sale of foreign capital shares listed domestically shall be handled by a securities institution that has been approved to be established according to law by the People's Bank of China and recognized by the CSRC.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　境内上市外资股股东可以委托代理人代为行使其股东权利；代理人代行股东权利时，应当提供证明其代理资格的有效文件。

**Article 21** A shareholder of foreign capital shares listed domestically may appoint an authorized agent to exercise its shareholders' rights on its behalf; when the authorized agent exercises the shareholders' rights, it shall provide valid documents certifying its agency qualification.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　境内上市外资股的权益拥有人，可以将其股份登记在名义持有人名下。  
境内上市外资股的权益拥有人应当依法披露其持股变动信息。

**Article 22** A beneficial owner of foreign capital shares listed domestically may register its shares in the name of a nominee holder.  
A beneficial owner of foreign capital shares listed domestically shall, according to law, disclose information on changes in its shareholding.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　境内上市外资股的交易、保管、清算交割、过户和登记，应当遵守法律、行政法规以及国务院证券委员会的有关规定。

**Article 23** The trading, custody, clearing and settlement, transfer, and registration of foreign capital shares listed domestically shall comply with the relevant provisions of laws, administrative regulations, and the Securities Committee of the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　经国务院证券委员会批准，境内上市外资股或者其派生形式可以在境外流通转让。  
前款所称派生形式，是指股票的认股权凭证和境外存股凭证。

**Article 24** Upon approval by the Securities Committee of the State Council, foreign capital shares listed domestically or their derivatives may be circulated and transferred outside China.  
"Derivatives" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph refer to share warrants and overseas depositary receipts.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　公司向境内上市外资股股东支付股利及其他款项，以人民币计价和宣布，以外币支付。公司所筹集的外币资本金的管理和公司支付股利及其他款项所需的外币，按照国家有关外汇管理的规定办理。  
公司章程规定由其他机构代为兑换外币并付给股东的，可以按照公司章程的规定办理。

**Article 25** A Company shall, when paying dividends and other amounts to shareholders of foreign capital shares listed domestically, price and declare them in Renminbi and pay them in foreign currency. The management of the foreign currency capital raised by the Company and the foreign currency required for the Company to pay dividends and other amounts shall be handled in accordance with the state provisions on foreign exchange control.  
Where the articles of association of the Company provide that another institution shall exchange the foreign currency and pay it to the shareholders, this may be handled in accordance with the provisions of the articles of association of the Company.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　境内上市外资股的股利和其他收益依法纳税后，可以汇出境外。

**Article 26** Dividends and other income from foreign capital shares listed domestically may, after taxes are paid according to law, be remitted outside China.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国务院证券委员会可以根据本规定制定实施细则。

**Article 27** The Securities Committee of the State Council may formulate detailed implementing rules in accordance with these Provisions.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本规定自发布之日起施行。中国人民银行、上海市人民政府1991年11月22日发布的《上海市人民币特种股票管理办法》，中国人民银行、深圳市人民政府1991年12月5日发布的《深圳市人民币特种股票管理暂行办法》同时废止。

**Article 28** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The "Shanghai Renminbi Special Shares Management Measures" promulgated by the People's Bank of China and the Shanghai Municipal People's Government on November 22, 1991, and the "Shenzhen Renminbi Special Shares Interim Management Measures" promulgated by the People's Bank of China and the Shenzhen Municipal People's Government on December 5, 1991, shall be abolished simultaneously.
