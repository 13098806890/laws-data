# 证券公司风险处置条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2023-07-20  
**生效日期**：2023-07-20

> （2008年4月23日中华人民共和国国务院令第523号公布　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2023年7月20日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了控制和化解证券公司风险，保护投资者合法权益和社会公共利益，保障证券业健康发展，根据《中华人民共和国证券法》（以下简称《证券法》）、《中华人民共和国企业破产法》（以下简称《企业破产法》），制定本条例。 第二条　国务院证券监督管理机构依法对处置证券公司风险工作进行组织、协调和监督。 第三条　国务院证券监督管理机构应当会同中国人民银行、国务院财政部门、国务院公安部门、国务院其他金融监督管理机构以及省级人民政府建立处置证券公司风险的协调配合与快速反应机制。 第四条　处置证券公司风险过程中，有关地方人民政府应当采取有效措施维护社会稳定。 第五条　处置证券公司风险过程中，应当保障证券经纪业务正常进行。 第二章　停业整顿、托管、接管、行政重组 第六条　国务院证券监督管理机构发现证券公司存在重大风险隐患，可以派出风险监控现场工作组对证券公司进行专项检查，对证券公司划拨资金、处置资产、调配人员、使用印章、订立以及履行合同等经营、管理活动进行监控，并及时向有关地方人民政府通报情况。 第七条　证券公司风险控制指标不符合有关规定，在规定期限内未能完成整改的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以责令证券公司停止部分或者全部业务进行整顿。停业整顿的期限不超过3个月。 证券经纪业务被责令停业整顿的，证券公司在规定的期限内可以将其证券经纪业务委托给国务院证券监督管理机构认可的证券公司管理，或者将客户转移到其他证券公司。证券公司逾期未按照要求委托证券经纪业务或者未转移客户的，国务院证券监督管理机构应当将客户转移到其他证券公司。 第八条　证券公司有下列情形之一的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以对其证券经纪等涉及客户的业务进行托管；情节严重的，可以对该证券公司进行接管： （一）治理混乱，管理失控； （二）挪用客户资产并且不能自行弥补； （三）在证券交易结算中多次发生交收违约或者交收违约数额较大；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了控制和化解证券公司风险，保护投资者合法权益和社会公共利益，保障证券业健康发展，根据《中华人民共和国证券法》（以下简称《证券法》）、《中华人民共和国企业破产法》（以下简称《企业破产法》），制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Securities Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the "Securities Law") and the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the "Enterprise Bankruptcy Law") for the purposes of controlling and mitigating risks of securities companies, protecting the lawful rights and interests of investors and the public interest, and ensuring the healthy development of the securities industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国务院证券监督管理机构依法对处置证券公司风险工作进行组织、协调和监督。

**Article 2** The securities regulatory authority under the State Council shall, according to law, organize, coordinate, and supervise the work of disposing of risks of securities companies.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院证券监督管理机构应当会同中国人民银行、国务院财政部门、国务院公安部门、国务院其他金融监督管理机构以及省级人民政府建立处置证券公司风险的协调配合与快速反应机制。

**Article 3** The securities regulatory authority under the State Council shall, together with the People's Bank of China, the finance department of the State Council, the public security department of the State Council, other financial regulatory authorities under the State Council, and the people's governments of provinces, establish a coordination and rapid response mechanism for disposing of risks of securities companies.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　处置证券公司风险过程中，有关地方人民政府应当采取有效措施维护社会稳定。

**Article 4** In the process of disposing of risks of securities companies, the relevant local people's governments shall take effective measures to maintain social stability.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　处置证券公司风险过程中，应当保障证券经纪业务正常进行。

**Article 5** In the process of disposing of risks of securities companies, the normal operation of securities brokerage business shall be ensured.

### 第二章　停业整顿、托管、接管、行政重组

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院证券监督管理机构发现证券公司存在重大风险隐患，可以派出风险监控现场工作组对证券公司进行专项检查，对证券公司划拨资金、处置资产、调配人员、使用印章、订立以及履行合同等经营、管理活动进行监控，并及时向有关地方人民政府通报情况。

**Article 6** Where the securities regulatory authority under the State Council discovers that a securities company has major potential risks, it may dispatch an on-site risk monitoring working group to conduct a special inspection of the securities company, monitor the securities company's operational and management activities such as transferring funds, disposing of assets, deploying personnel, using seals, and concluding and performing contracts, and promptly report the situation to the relevant local people's government.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　证券公司风险控制指标不符合有关规定，在规定期限内未能完成整改的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以责令证券公司停止部分或者全部业务进行整顿。停业整顿的期限不超过3个月。  
证券经纪业务被责令停业整顿的，证券公司在规定的期限内可以将其证券经纪业务委托给国务院证券监督管理机构认可的证券公司管理，或者将客户转移到其他证券公司。证券公司逾期未按照要求委托证券经纪业务或者未转移客户的，国务院证券监督管理机构应当将客户转移到其他证券公司。

**Article 7** Where the risk control indicators of a securities company do not comply with the relevant provisions and it fails to complete rectification within the prescribed time limit, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council may order the securities company to suspend part or all of its business for rectification. The period for suspension of business for rectification shall not exceed three months.  
Where the securities brokerage business is ordered to suspend for rectification, the securities company may, within the prescribed time limit, entrust its securities brokerage business to another securities company recognized by the securities regulatory authority under the State Council for management, or transfer its clients to another securities company. Where the securities company fails to entrust the securities brokerage business or transfer its clients as required within the time limit, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council shall transfer the clients to another securities company.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　证券公司有下列情形之一的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以对其证券经纪等涉及客户的业务进行托管；情节严重的，可以对该证券公司进行接管：  
（一）治理混乱，管理失控；  
（二）挪用客户资产并且不能自行弥补；  
（三）在证券交易结算中多次发生交收违约或者交收违约数额较大；  
（四）风险控制指标不符合规定，发生重大财务危机；  
（五）其他可能影响证券公司持续经营的情形。

**Article 8** Where a securities company falls under any of the following circumstances, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council may place its securities brokerage and other client-related businesses under custodianship; if the circumstances are serious, it may take over the securities company:  
(1) governance is chaotic and management is out of control;  
(2) it misappropriates client assets and cannot make up for them on its own;  
(3) it repeatedly defaults on settlement in securities trading settlement or the amount of default is relatively large;  
(4) risk control indicators do not comply with provisions and a major financial crisis occurs;  
(5) other circumstances that may affect the sustainable operation of the securities company.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院证券监督管理机构决定对证券公司证券经纪等涉及客户的业务进行托管的，应当按照规定程序选择证券公司等专业机构成立托管组，行使被托管证券公司的证券经纪等涉及客户的业务的经营管理权。  
托管组自托管之日起履行下列职责：  
（一）保障证券公司证券经纪业务正常合规运行，必要时依照规定垫付营运资金和客户的交易结算资金；  
（二）采取有效措施维护托管期间客户资产的安全；  
（三）核查证券公司存在的风险，及时向国务院证券监督管理机构报告业务运行中出现的紧急情况，并提出解决方案；  
（四）国务院证券监督管理机构要求履行的其他职责。  
托管期限一般不超过12个月。满12个月，确需继续托管的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以决定延长托管期限，但延长托管期限最长不得超过12个月。

**Article 9** Where the securities regulatory authority under the State Council decides to place the securities brokerage and other client-related businesses of a securities company under custodianship, it shall, in accordance with the prescribed procedures, select professional institutions such as securities companies to establish a custodianship group, which shall exercise the operational and management rights over the securities brokerage and other client-related businesses of the securities company under custodianship.  
From the date of custodianship, the custodianship group shall perform the following duties:  
(1) ensure the normal and compliant operation of the securities company's securities brokerage business, and when necessary, advance operating funds and clients' transaction settlement funds in accordance with provisions;  
(2) take effective measures to maintain the safety of clients' assets during the custodianship period;  
(3) examine the risks existing in the securities company, promptly report emergency situations arising in business operations to the securities regulatory authority under the State Council, and propose solutions;  
(4) other duties required by the securities regulatory authority under the State Council to be performed.  
The custodianship period shall generally not exceed 12 months. Upon expiration of 12 months, if it is truly necessary to continue the custodianship, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council may decide to extend the custodianship period, but the extension shall not exceed 12 months at most.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　被托管证券公司应当承担托管费用和托管期间的营运费用。国务院证券监督管理机构应当对托管费用和托管期间的营运费用进行审核。  
托管组不承担被托管证券公司的亏损。

**Article 10** The securities company under custodianship shall bear the custodianship expenses and the operating expenses during the custodianship period. The securities regulatory authority under the State Council shall review the custodianship expenses and the operating expenses during the custodianship period.  
The custodianship group shall not bear the losses of the securities company under custodianship.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院证券监督管理机构决定对证券公司进行接管的，应当按照规定程序组织专业人员成立接管组，行使被接管证券公司的经营管理权，接管组负责人行使被接管证券公司法定代表人职权，被接管证券公司的股东会或者股东大会、董事会、监事会以及经理、副经理停止履行职责。  
接管组自接管之日起履行下列职责：  
（一）接管证券公司的财产、印章和账簿、文书等资料；  
（二）决定证券公司的管理事务；  
（三）保障证券公司证券经纪业务正常合规运行，完善内控制度；  
（四）清查证券公司财产，依法保全、追收资产；  
（五）控制证券公司风险，提出风险化解方案；  
（六）核查证券公司有关人员的违法行为；  
（七）国务院证券监督管理机构要求履行的其他职责。  
接管期限一般不超过12个月。满12个月，确需继续接管的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以决定延长接管期限，但延长接管期限最长不得超过12个月。

**Article 11** Where the securities regulatory authority under the State Council decides to take over a securities company, it shall, in accordance with the prescribed procedures, organize professionals to establish a takeover group, which shall exercise the operational and management rights of the securities company under takeover, and the person in charge of the takeover group shall exercise the functions and powers of the legal representative of the securities company under takeover. The shareholders' meeting or general meeting of shareholders, board of directors, board of supervisors, and the manager and deputy manager of the securities company under takeover shall cease to perform their duties.  
From the date of takeover, the takeover group shall perform the following duties:  
(1) take over the property, seals, account books, documents and other materials of the securities company;  
(2) decide on the management affairs of the securities company;  
(3) ensure the normal and compliant operation of the securities company's securities brokerage business and improve the internal control system;  
(4) conduct a thorough inventory of the securities company's property, and preserve and recover assets according to law;  
(5) control the risks of the securities company and propose risk resolution plans;  
(6) investigate the illegal acts of the relevant personnel of the securities company;  
(7) other duties required by the securities regulatory authority under the State Council to be performed.  
The takeover period shall generally not exceed 12 months. Upon expiration of 12 months, if it is truly necessary to continue the takeover, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council may decide to extend the takeover period, but the extension shall not exceed 12 months at most.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　证券公司出现重大风险，但具备下列条件的，可以由国务院证券监督管理机构对其进行行政重组：  
（一）财务信息真实、完整；  
（二）省级人民政府或者有关方面予以支持；  
（三）整改措施具体，有可行的重组计划。  
被停业整顿、托管、接管的证券公司，具备前款规定条件的，也可以由国务院证券监督管理机构对其进行行政重组。

**Article 12** Where a securities company has significant risks, but meets the following conditions, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council may conduct an administrative reorganization on it:  
(1) the financial information is true and complete;  
(2) the provincial people's government or relevant parties provide support;  
(3) the rectification measures are specific and there is a feasible reorganization plan.  
A securities company that is subject to suspension of business for rectification, custodianship, or takeover may also be subject to administrative reorganization by the securities regulatory authority under the State Council if it meets the conditions set forth in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　证券公司进行行政重组，可以采取注资、股权重组、债务重组、资产重组、合并或者其他方式。  
行政重组期限一般不超过12个月。满12个月，行政重组未完成的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以决定延长行政重组期限，但延长行政重组期限最长不得超过6个月。  
国务院证券监督管理机构对证券公司的行政重组进行协调和指导。

**Article 13** For administrative reorganization of a securities company, methods such as capital injection, equity reorganization, debt restructuring, asset reorganization, merger, or other methods may be adopted.  
The period for administrative reorganization shall generally not exceed 12 months. Upon expiration of 12 months, if the administrative reorganization is not completed, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council may decide to extend the administrative reorganization period, but the extension shall not exceed 6 months at most.  
The securities regulatory authority under the State Council shall coordinate and guide the administrative reorganization of the securities company.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院证券监督管理机构对证券公司做出责令停业整顿、托管、接管、行政重组的处置决定，应当予以公告，并将公告张贴于被处置证券公司的营业场所。  
处置决定包括被处置证券公司的名称、处置措施、事由以及范围等有关事项。  
处置决定的公告日期为处置日，处置决定自公告之时生效。

**Article 14** Where the securities regulatory authority under the State Council makes a disposal decision ordering a securities company to suspend business for rectification, or placing it under custodianship, takeover, or administrative reorganization, it shall make a public announcement and post the announcement at the business premises of the securities company under disposal.  
The disposal decision shall include the name of the securities company under disposal, the disposal measures, the reasons, the scope, and other relevant matters.  
The date of the announcement of the disposal decision shall be the disposal date, and the disposal decision shall take effect from the time of the announcement.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　证券公司被责令停业整顿、托管、接管、行政重组的，其债权债务关系不因处置决定而变化。

**Article 15** Where a securities company is ordered to suspend business for rectification, or is placed under custodianship, takeover, or administrative reorganization, its creditor-debtor relationships shall not be changed by the disposal decision.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　证券公司经停业整顿、托管、接管或者行政重组在规定期限内达到正常经营条件的，经国务院证券监督管理机构批准，可以恢复正常经营。

**Article 16** Where a securities company, after undergoing suspension of business for rectification, custodianship, takeover, or administrative reorganization, meets the conditions for normal operation within the prescribed time limit, it may resume normal operation upon approval by the securities regulatory authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　证券公司经停业整顿、托管、接管或者行政重组在规定期限内仍达不到正常经营条件，但能够清偿到期债务的，国务院证券监督管理机构依法撤销其证券业务许可。

**Article 17** Where a securities company, after undergoing suspension of business for rectification, custodianship, takeover, or administrative reorganization, still fails to meet the conditions for normal operation within the prescribed time limit, but is able to repay its due debts, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council shall, according to law, revoke its securities business license.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　被撤销证券业务许可的证券公司应当停止经营证券业务，按照客户自愿的原则将客户安置到其他证券公司，安置过程中相关各方应当采取必要措施保证客户证券交易的正常进行。  
被撤销证券业务许可的证券公司有未安置客户等情形的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以比照本条例第三章的规定，成立行政清理组，清理账户、安置客户、转让证券类资产。

**Article 18** A securities company whose securities business license has been revoked shall cease to operate securities business, and shall place its clients with other securities companies in accordance with the principle of client voluntariness. During the placement process, all relevant parties shall take necessary measures to ensure the normal conduct of clients' securities transactions.  
Where a securities company whose securities business license has been revoked has clients that have not been placed or other such circumstances, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council may, by analogy with the provisions of Chapter III of these Regulations, establish an administrative clearing group to clear accounts, place clients, and transfer securities-type assets.

### 第三章　撤销

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　证券公司同时有下列情形的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以直接撤销该证券公司：  
（一）违法经营情节特别严重、存在巨大经营风险；  
（二）不能清偿到期债务，并且资产不足以清偿全部债务或者明显缺乏清偿能力；  
（三）需要动用证券投资者保护基金。

**Article 19** Where a securities company concurrently has the following circumstances, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council may directly revoke the securities company:  
(1) the circumstances of illegal operation are particularly serious and there are huge operational risks;  
(2) it is unable to repay its due debts, and its assets are insufficient to repay all debts or it is clearly lacking in solvency;  
(3) it is necessary to use the securities investor protection fund.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　证券公司经停业整顿、托管、接管或者行政重组在规定期限内仍达不到正常经营条件，并且有[本条例第十九条](#art-19)第（二）项或者第（三）项规定情形的，国务院证券监督管理机构应当撤销该证券公司。

**Article 20** Where a securities company, after undergoing suspension of business for rectification, custodianship, takeover, or administrative reorganization, still fails to meet the conditions for normal operation within the prescribed time limit, and has the circumstances specified in subparagraph (2) or (3) of Article 19 of these Regulations, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council shall revoke the securities company.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国务院证券监督管理机构撤销证券公司，应当做出撤销决定，并按照规定程序选择律师事务所、会计师事务所等专业机构成立行政清理组，对该证券公司进行行政清理。  
撤销决定应当予以公告，撤销决定的公告日期为处置日，撤销决定自公告之时生效。  
本条例施行前，国务院证券监督管理机构已经对证券公司进行行政清理的，行政清理的公告日期为处置日。

**Article 21** Where the securities regulatory authority under the State Council revokes a securities company, it shall make a revocation decision and, in accordance with the prescribed procedures, select professional institutions such as law firms and accounting firms to establish an administrative clearing group to conduct administrative clearing of the securities company.  
The revocation decision shall be announced, and the date of the announcement of the revocation decision shall be the disposal date, and the revocation decision shall take effect from the time of the announcement.  
Where, prior to the coming into force of these Regulations, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council has already conducted administrative clearing of a securities company, the date of the announcement of the administrative clearing shall be the disposal date.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　行政清理期间，行政清理组负责人行使被撤销证券公司法定代表人职权。  
行政清理组履行下列职责：  
（一）管理证券公司的财产、印章和账簿、文书等资料；  
（二）清理账户，核实资产负债有关情况，对符合国家规定的债权进行登记；  
（三）协助甄别确认、收购符合国家规定的债权；  
（四）协助证券投资者保护基金管理机构弥补客户的交易结算资金；  
（五）按照客户自愿的原则安置客户；  
（六）转让证券类资产；  
（七）国务院证券监督管理机构要求履行的其他职责。  
前款所称证券类资产，是指证券公司为维持证券经纪业务正常进行所必需的计算机信息管理系统、交易系统、通信网络系统、交易席位等资产。

**Article 22** During the period of administrative clearing, the person in charge of the administrative clearing group shall exercise the functions and powers of the legal representative of the revoked securities company.  
The administrative clearing group shall perform the following duties:  
(1) manage the property, seals, account books, documents and other materials of the securities company;  
(2) clear accounts, verify the relevant conditions of assets and liabilities, and register claims that comply with state provisions;  
(3) assist in identifying, confirming, and purchasing claims that comply with state provisions;  
(4) assist the securities investor protection fund management institution in making up clients' transaction settlement funds;  
(5) place clients in accordance with the principle of client voluntariness;  
(6) transfer securities-type assets;  
(7) other duties required by the securities regulatory authority under the State Council to be performed.  
The term "securities-type assets" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph refers to assets such as computer information management systems, trading systems, communication network systems, and trading seats that are necessary for the securities company to maintain the normal operation of its securities brokerage business.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　被撤销证券公司的股东会或者股东大会、董事会、监事会以及经理、副经理停止履行职责。  
行政清理期间，被撤销证券公司的股东不得自行组织清算，不得参与行政清理工作。

**Article 23** The shareholders' meeting or general meeting of shareholders, board of directors, board of supervisors, and the manager and deputy manager of the revoked securities company shall cease to perform their duties.  
During the period of administrative clearing, the shareholders of the revoked securities company shall not organize liquidation on their own, nor participate in the administrative clearing work.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　行政清理期间，被撤销证券公司的证券经纪等涉及客户的业务，由国务院证券监督管理机构按照规定程序选择证券公司等专业机构进行托管。

**Article 24** During the period of administrative clearing, the securities brokerage and other client-related businesses of the revoked securities company shall be placed under custodianship by professional institutions such as securities companies selected by the securities regulatory authority under the State Council in accordance with the prescribed procedures.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　证券公司设立或者实际控制的关联公司，其资产、人员、财务或者业务与被撤销证券公司混合的，经国务院证券监督管理机构审查批准，纳入行政清理范围。

**Article 25** Where an affiliate company established or actually controlled by a securities company has its assets, personnel, finances, or business mixed with those of the revoked securities company, it shall, upon examination and approval by the securities regulatory authority under the State Council, be included in the scope of administrative clearing.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　证券公司的债权债务关系不因其被撤销而变化。  
自证券公司被撤销之日起，证券公司的债务停止计算利息。

**Article 26** The creditor-debtor relationships of a securities company shall not be changed by its revocation.  
From the date of revocation of the securities company, interest on the debts of the securities company shall cease to accrue.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　行政清理组清理被撤销证券公司账户的结果，应当经具有证券、期货相关业务资格的会计师事务所审计，并报国务院证券监督管理机构认定。  
行政清理组根据经国务院证券监督管理机构认定的账户清理结果，向证券投资者保护基金管理机构申请弥补客户的交易结算资金的资金。

**Article 27** The results of the administrative clearing group's clearing of the accounts of the revoked securities company shall be audited by an accounting firm qualified for securities and futures related businesses, and submitted to the securities regulatory authority under the State Council for confirmation.  
The administrative clearing group shall, based on the account clearing results confirmed by the securities regulatory authority under the State Council, apply to the securities investor protection fund management institution for funds to make up clients' transaction settlement funds.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　行政清理组应当自成立之日起10日内，将债权人需要登记的相关事项予以公告。  
符合国家有关规定的债权人应当自公告之日起90日内，持相关证明材料向行政清理组申报债权，行政清理组按照规定登记。无正当理由逾期申报的，不予登记。  
已登记债权经甄别确认符合国家收购规定的，行政清理组应当及时按照国家有关规定申请收购资金并协助收购；经甄别确认不符合国家收购规定的，行政清理组应当告知申报的债权人。

**Article 28** The administrative clearing group shall, within 10 days from the date of its establishment, announce the relevant matters that creditors need to register.  
Creditors that comply with the relevant state provisions shall, within 90 days from the date of the announcement, submit their claims to the administrative clearing group with relevant supporting materials, and the administrative clearing group shall register them in accordance with the provisions. Claims submitted after the time limit without justifiable reasons shall not be registered.  
Where registered claims, after identification and confirmation, comply with the state acquisition provisions, the administrative clearing group shall promptly apply for acquisition funds and assist in the acquisition in accordance with the relevant state provisions; where they are identified and confirmed not to comply with the state acquisition provisions, the administrative clearing group shall inform the creditors that submitted the claims.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　行政清理组应当在具备证券业务经营资格的机构中，采用招标、公开询价等公开方式转让证券类资产。证券类资产转让方案应当报国务院证券监督管理机构批准。

**Article 29** The administrative clearing group shall, among institutions qualified to engage in securities business, transfer securities-type assets by public methods such as bidding and public inquiry. The plan for transferring securities-type assets shall be submitted to the securities regulatory authority under the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　行政清理组不得转让证券类资产以外的资产，但经国务院证券监督管理机构批准，易贬损并可能遭受损失的资产或者确为保护客户和债权人利益的其他情形除外。

**Article 30** The administrative clearing group shall not transfer assets other than securities-type assets, except for assets that are prone to depreciation and may suffer losses, or other circumstances that are truly for protecting the interests of clients and creditors, subject to approval by the securities regulatory authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　行政清理组不得对债务进行个别清偿，但为保护客户和债权人利益的下列情形除外：  
（一）因行政清理组请求对方当事人履行双方均未履行完毕的合同所产生的债务；  
（二）为维持业务正常进行而应当支付的职工劳动报酬和社会保险费用等正常支出；  
（三）行政清理组履行职责所产生的其他费用。

**Article 31** The administrative clearing group shall not make individual repayments of debts, except in the following circumstances for protecting the interests of clients and creditors:  
(1) debts arising from the administrative clearing group's request that the other party perform a contract that both parties have not yet fully performed;  
(2) normal expenditures such as labor remuneration of employees and social insurance premiums that shall be paid to maintain normal business operations;  
(3) other expenses incurred by the administrative clearing group in performing its duties.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　为保护债权人利益，经国务院证券监督管理机构批准，行政清理组可以向人民法院申请对处置前被采取查封、扣押、冻结等强制措施的证券类资产以及其他资产进行变现处置，变现后的资金应当予以冻结。

**Article 32** For the purpose of protecting the interests of creditors, with the approval of the securities regulatory authority under the State Council, the administrative clearing group may apply to the People's Court for the realization and disposal of securities-type assets and other assets that were subject to compulsory measures such as sealing up, distraining, or freezing prior to the disposal. The funds realized shall be frozen.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　行政清理费用经国务院证券监督管理机构审核后，从被处置证券公司财产中随时清偿。  
前款所称行政清理费用，是指行政清理组管理、转让证券公司财产所需的费用，行政清理组履行职务和聘用专业机构的费用等。

**Article 33** The administrative clearing expenses, after being reviewed by the securities regulatory authority under the State Council, shall be paid from the property of the securities company under disposal at any time.  
The term "administrative clearing expenses" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph refers to the expenses necessary for the administrative clearing group to manage and transfer the property of the securities company, the expenses of the administrative clearing group in performing its duties and hiring professional institutions, etc.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　行政清理期限一般不超过12个月。满12个月，行政清理未完成的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以决定延长行政清理期限，但延长行政清理期限最长不得超过12个月。

**Article 34** The period for administrative clearing shall generally not exceed 12 months. Upon expiration of 12 months, if the administrative clearing is not completed, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council may decide to extend the administrative clearing period, but the extension shall not exceed 12 months at most.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　行政清理期间，被处置证券公司免缴行政性收费和增值税、营业税等行政法规规定的税收。

**Article 35** During the period of administrative clearing, the securities company under disposal shall be exempt from administrative fees and taxes such as value-added tax, business tax, and other taxes prescribed by administrative regulations.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　证券公司被国务院证券监督管理机构依法责令关闭，需要进行行政清理的，比照本章的有关规定执行。

**Article 36** Where a securities company is ordered to be closed by the securities regulatory authority under the State Council according to law and administrative clearing is required, the relevant provisions of this Chapter shall be applied by analogy.

### 第四章　破产清算和重整

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　证券公司被依法撤销、关闭时，有[《企业破产法》第二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-2)规定情形的，行政清理工作完成后，国务院证券监督管理机构或者其委托的行政清理组依照《企业破产法》的有关规定，可以向人民法院申请对被撤销、关闭证券公司进行破产清算。

**Article 37** Where a securities company is revoked or closed according to law and falls under the circumstances specified in Article 2 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, after the administrative clearing work is completed, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council or the administrative clearing group entrusted by it may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, apply to the People's Court for bankruptcy liquidation of the revoked or closed securities company.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　证券公司有[《企业破产法》第二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-2)规定情形的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以直接向人民法院申请对该证券公司进行重整。  
证券公司或者其债权人依照《企业破产法》的有关规定，可以向人民法院提出对证券公司进行破产清算或者重整的申请，但应当依照[《证券法》第一百二十二条](../../民事与商事/证券与期货/中华人民共和国证券法.md#art-122)的规定报经国务院证券监督管理机构批准。

**Article 38** Where a securities company falls under the circumstances specified in Article 2 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council may directly apply to the People's Court for reorganization of the securities company.  
A securities company or its creditor may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, file an application with the People's Court for bankruptcy liquidation or reorganization of the securities company, but shall submit it to the securities regulatory authority under the State Council for approval in accordance with Article 122 of the Securities Law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　对不需要动用证券投资者保护基金的证券公司，国务院证券监督管理机构应当在批准破产清算前撤销其证券业务许可。证券公司应当依照[本条例第十八条](#art-18)的规定停止经营证券业务，安置客户。  
对需要动用证券投资者保护基金的证券公司，国务院证券监督管理机构对该证券公司或者其债权人的破产清算申请不予批准，并依照本条例第三章的规定撤销该证券公司，进行行政清理。

**Article 39** For a securities company that does not need to use the securities investor protection fund, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council shall revoke its securities business license before approving bankruptcy liquidation. The securities company shall cease to operate securities business and place its clients in accordance with Article 18 of these Regulations.  
For a securities company that needs to use the securities investor protection fund, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council shall not approve the application for bankruptcy liquidation filed by the securities company or its creditor, and shall revoke the securities company in accordance with the provisions of Chapter III of these Regulations and carry out administrative clearing.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　人民法院裁定受理证券公司重整或者破产清算申请的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以向人民法院推荐管理人人选。

**Article 40** Where the People's Court rules to accept an application for reorganization or bankruptcy liquidation of a securities company, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council may recommend a candidate for the administrator to the People's Court.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　证券公司进行破产清算的，行政清理时已登记的不符合国家收购规定的债权，管理人可以直接予以登记。

**Article 41** Where a securities company undergoes bankruptcy liquidation, the administrator may directly register claims that were registered during administrative clearing but did not comply with the state acquisition provisions.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　人民法院裁定证券公司重整的，证券公司或者管理人应当同时向债权人会议、国务院证券监督管理机构和人民法院提交重整计划草案。

**Article 42** Where the People's Court rules for reorganization of a securities company, the securities company or the administrator shall submit the draft reorganization plan to the creditors' meeting, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council, and the People's Court at the same time.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　自债权人会议各表决组通过重整计划草案之日起10日内，证券公司或者管理人应当向人民法院提出批准重整计划的申请。重整计划涉及[《证券法》第一百二十二条](../../民事与商事/证券与期货/中华人民共和国证券法.md#art-122)规定相关事项的，证券公司或者管理人应当同时向国务院证券监督管理机构提出批准相关事项的申请，国务院证券监督管理机构应当自收到申请之日起15日内做出批准或者不予批准的决定。

**Article 43** Within 10 days from the date on which each voting group of the creditors' meeting adopts the draft reorganization plan, the securities company or the administrator shall file an application with the People's Court for approval of the reorganization plan. Where the reorganization plan involves matters specified in Article 122 of the Securities Law, the securities company or the administrator shall simultaneously file an application with the securities regulatory authority under the State Council for approval of the relevant matters, and the securities regulatory authority under the State Council shall make a decision on approval or disapproval within 15 days from the date of receiving the application.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　债权人会议部分表决组未通过重整计划草案，但重整计划草案符合[《企业破产法》第八十七条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-87)第二款规定条件的，证券公司或者管理人可以申请人民法院批准重整计划草案。重整计划草案涉及[《证券法》第一百二十二条](../../民事与商事/证券与期货/中华人民共和国证券法.md#art-122)规定相关事项的，证券公司或者管理人应当同时向国务院证券监督管理机构提出批准相关事项的申请，国务院证券监督管理机构应当自收到申请之日起15日内做出批准或者不予批准的决定。

**Article 44** Where some voting groups of the creditors' meeting do not adopt the draft reorganization plan, but the draft reorganization plan meets the conditions specified in Article 87, paragraph 2 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the securities company or the administrator may apply to the People's Court for approval of the draft reorganization plan. Where the draft reorganization plan involves matters specified in Article 122 of the Securities Law, the securities company or the administrator shall simultaneously file an application with the securities regulatory authority under the State Council for approval of the relevant matters, and the securities regulatory authority under the State Council shall make a decision on approval or disapproval within 15 days from the date of receiving the application.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　经批准的重整计划由证券公司执行，管理人负责监督。监督期届满，管理人应当向人民法院和国务院证券监督管理机构提交监督报告。

**Article 45** The approved reorganization plan shall be executed by the securities company, and the administrator shall be responsible for supervision. Upon expiration of the supervision period, the administrator shall submit a supervision report to the People's Court and the securities regulatory authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　重整计划的相关事项未获国务院证券监督管理机构批准，或者重整计划未获人民法院批准的，人民法院裁定终止重整程序，并宣告证券公司破产。

**Article 46** Where the relevant matters of the reorganization plan are not approved by the securities regulatory authority under the State Council, or the reorganization plan is not approved by the People's Court, the People's Court shall rule to terminate the reorganization proceedings and declare the securities company bankrupt.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　重整程序终止，人民法院宣告证券公司破产的，国务院证券监督管理机构应当对证券公司做出撤销决定，人民法院依照《企业破产法》的规定组织破产清算。涉及税收事项，依照《企业破产法》和《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》的规定执行。  
人民法院认为应当对证券公司进行行政清理的，国务院证券监督管理机构比照本条例第三章的规定成立行政清理组，负责清理账户，协助甄别确认、收购符合国家规定的债权，协助证券投资者保护基金管理机构弥补客户的交易结算资金，转让证券类资产等。

**Article 47** Where the reorganization proceedings are terminated and the People's Court declares the securities company bankrupt, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council shall make a revocation decision on the securities company, and the People's Court shall organize bankruptcy liquidation in accordance with the provisions of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law. Matters involving taxation shall be handled in accordance with the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection.  
Where the People's Court considers that administrative clearing should be conducted on the securities company, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council shall, by analogy with the provisions of Chapter III of these Regulations, establish an administrative clearing group to be responsible for clearing accounts, assisting in identifying, confirming, and purchasing claims that comply with state provisions, assisting the securities investor protection fund management institution in making up clients' transaction settlement funds, and transferring securities-type assets, etc.

### 第五章　监督协调

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国务院证券监督管理机构在处置证券公司风险工作中，履行下列职责：  
（一）制订证券公司风险处置方案并组织实施；  
（二）派驻风险处置现场工作组，对被处置证券公司、托管组、接管组、行政清理组、管理人以及参与风险处置的其他机构和人员进行监督和指导；  
（三）协调证券交易所、证券登记结算机构、证券投资者保护基金管理机构，保障被处置证券公司证券经纪业务正常进行；  
（四）对证券公司的违法行为立案稽查并予以处罚；  
（五）及时向公安机关等通报涉嫌刑事犯罪的情况，按照有关规定移送涉嫌犯罪的案件；  
（六）向有关地方人民政府通报证券公司风险状况以及影响社会稳定的情况；  
（七）法律、行政法规要求履行的其他职责。

**Article 48** In the work of disposing of risks of securities companies, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council shall perform the following duties:  
(1) formulate risk disposal plans for securities companies and organize their implementation;  
(2) dispatch on-site risk disposal working groups to supervise and guide the securities company under disposal, the custodianship group, the takeover group, the administrative clearing group, the administrator, and other institutions and personnel involved in the risk disposal;  
(3) coordinate with the stock exchange, the securities registration and settlement institution, and the securities investor protection fund management institution to ensure the normal operation of the securities brokerage business of the securities company under disposal;  
(4) initiate investigation and impose penalties on illegal acts of securities companies;  
(5) promptly report suspected criminal offenses to public security organs, etc., and transfer cases involving suspected crimes in accordance with relevant provisions;  
(6) report the risk situation of the securities company and the situation affecting social stability to the relevant local people's governments;  
(7) other duties required by laws and administrative regulations to be performed.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　处置证券公司风险过程中，发现涉嫌犯罪的案件，属公安机关管辖的，应当由国务院公安部门统一组织依法查处。有关地方人民政府应当予以支持和配合。  
风险处置现场工作组、行政清理组和管理人需要从公安机关扣押资料中查询、复制与其工作有关资料的，公安机关应当支持和配合。证券公司进入破产程序的，公安机关应当依法将冻结的涉案资产移送给受理破产案件的人民法院，并留存必需的相关证据材料。

**Article 49** Where during the process of disposing of risks of securities companies, cases involving suspected crimes are discovered and fall under the jurisdiction of the public security organs, the public security department of the State Council shall organize the investigation and handling according to law. The relevant local people's governments shall provide support and cooperation.  
Where the on-site risk disposal working group, the administrative clearing group, and the administrator need to access or copy materials related to their work from the materials seized by the public security organs, the public security organs shall provide support and cooperation. Where a securities company enters bankruptcy proceedings, the public security organs shall, according to law, transfer the frozen assets involved in the case to the People's Court accepting the bankruptcy case, and retain necessary relevant evidence materials.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国务院证券监督管理机构依照本条例第二章、第三章对证券公司进行处置的，可以向人民法院提出申请中止以该证券公司以及其分支机构为被告、第三人或者被执行人的民事诉讼程序或者执行程序。  
证券公司设立或者实际控制的关联公司，其资产、人员、财务或者业务与被处置证券公司混合的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以向人民法院提出申请中止以该关联公司为被告、第三人或者被执行人的民事诉讼程序或者执行程序。  
采取前两款规定措施期间，除[本条例第三十一条](#art-31)规定的情形外，不得对被处置证券公司债务进行个别清偿。

**Article 50** Where the securities regulatory authority under the State Council disposes of a securities company in accordance with Chapter II and Chapter III of these Regulations, it may apply to the People's Court for suspension of civil proceedings or enforcement proceedings in which the securities company or its branches are the defendant, a third party, or the person subject to enforcement.  
Where an affiliate company established or actually controlled by a securities company has its assets, personnel, finances, or business mixed with those of the securities company under disposal, the securities regulatory authority under the State Council may apply to the People's Court for suspension of civil proceedings or enforcement proceedings in which the affiliate company is the defendant, a third party, or the person subject to enforcement.  
During the period when the measures prescribed in the preceding two paragraphs are taken, no individual repayment of debts of the securities company under disposal shall be made, except in the circumstances specified in Article 31 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　被处置证券公司或者其关联客户可能转移、隐匿违法资金、证券，或者证券公司违反本条例规定可能对债务进行个别清偿的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以禁止相关资金账户、证券账户的资金和证券转出。

**Article 51** Where a securities company under disposal or its related clients may transfer or conceal illegal funds or securities, or the securities company may individually settle its debts in violation of these Regulations, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council may prohibit the transfer of funds and securities out of the relevant fund accounts and securities accounts.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　被处置证券公司以及其分支机构所在地人民政府，应当按照国家有关规定配合证券公司风险处置工作，制订维护社会稳定的预案，排查、预防和化解不稳定因素，维护被处置证券公司正常的营业秩序。  
被处置证券公司以及其分支机构所在地人民政府，应当组织相关单位的人员成立个人债权甄别确认小组，按照国家规定对已登记的个人债权进行甄别确认。

**Article 52** The people's governments of the places where the securities company under disposal and its branches are located shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, cooperate in the risk disposal of the securities company, formulate plans for maintaining social stability, investigate, prevent and resolve unstable factors, and maintain the normal business order of the securities company under disposal.  
The people's governments of the places where the securities company under disposal and its branches are located shall organize personnel from relevant units to form a team for the identification and confirmation of individual claims, and conduct identification and confirmation of registered individual claims in accordance with the provisions of the State.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　证券投资者保护基金管理机构应当按照国家规定，收购债权、弥补客户的交易结算资金。  
证券投资者保护基金管理机构可以对证券投资者保护基金的使用情况进行检查。

**Article 53** The securities investor protection fund management institution shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State, acquire claims and make up for clients' transaction settlement funds.  
The securities investor protection fund management institution may inspect the use of the securities investor protection fund.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　被处置证券公司的股东、实际控制人、债权人以及与被处置证券公司有关的机构和人员，应当配合证券公司风险处置工作。

**Article 54** The shareholders, actual controllers, creditors of the securities company under disposal, as well as the institutions and persons related to the securities company under disposal, shall cooperate in the risk disposal work of the securities company.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　被处置证券公司的董事、监事、高级管理人员以及其他有关人员应当妥善保管其使用和管理的证券公司财产、印章和账簿、文书等资料以及其他物品，按照要求向托管组、接管组、行政清理组或者管理人移交，并配合风险处置现场工作组、托管组、接管组、行政清理组的调查工作。

**Article 55** The directors, supervisors, senior officers and other relevant persons of the securities company under disposal shall properly keep the securities company's property, seals, account books, documents and other materials and articles under their use and management, hand them over to the trusteeship group, takeover group, administrative clearing group or administrator as required, and cooperate with the investigation work of the on-site risk disposal working group, trusteeship group, takeover group and administrative clearing group.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　托管组、接管组、行政清理组以及被责令停业整顿、托管和行政重组的证券公司，应当按照规定向国务院证券监督管理机构报告工作情况。

**Article 56** The trusteeship group, takeover group, administrative clearing group, and the securities company that is ordered to suspend business for rectification, placed under trusteeship or subject to administrative reorganization shall report their work to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　托管组、接管组、行政清理组以及其工作人员应当勤勉尽责，忠实履行职责。  
被处置证券公司的股东以及债权人有证据证明托管组、接管组、行政清理组以及其工作人员未依法履行职责的，可以向国务院证券监督管理机构投诉。经调查核实，由国务院证券监督管理机构责令托管组、接管组、行政清理组以及其工作人员改正或者对其予以更换。

**Article 57** The trusteeship group, takeover group, administrative clearing group and their staff members shall be diligent and faithful in performing their duties.  
If the shareholders and creditors of the securities company under disposal have evidence proving that the trusteeship group, takeover group, administrative clearing group or their staff members fail to perform their duties according to law, they may lodge a complaint with the securities regulatory authority of the State Council. After investigation and verification, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall order the trusteeship group, takeover group, administrative clearing group or their staff members to correct the situation or replace them.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　有下列情形之一的机构或者人员，禁止参与处置证券公司风险工作：  
（一）曾受过刑事处罚或者涉嫌犯罪正在被立案侦查、起诉；  
（二）涉嫌严重违法正在被行政管理部门立案稽查或者曾因严重违法行为受到行政处罚未逾3年；  
（三）仍处于证券市场禁入期；  
（四）内部控制薄弱、存在重大风险隐患；  
（五）与被处置证券公司处置事项有利害关系；  
（六）国务院证券监督管理机构认定不宜参与处置证券公司风险工作的其他情形。

**Article 58** An institution or person under any of the following circumstances shall be prohibited from participating in the risk disposal work of a securities company:  
(1) Having been subjected to criminal punishment or being under investigation and prosecution for suspected crimes;  
(2) Being under investigation by an administrative department for suspected serious illegal acts or having been subjected to administrative penalties for serious illegal acts within less than three years;  
(3) Still being in the period of market prohibition;  
(4) Having weak internal control and major risk hazards;  
(5) Having an interest in the disposal matters of the securities company under disposal;  
(6) Other circumstances determined by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council as unsuitable for participating in the risk disposal work of a securities company.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　证券公司的董事、监事、高级管理人员等对该证券公司被处置负有主要责任，情节严重的，可以按照规定对其采取证券市场禁入的措施。

**Article 59** Where directors, supervisors, senior officers and others of a securities company bear primary responsibility for the disposal of the securities company and the circumstances are serious, measures of market prohibition may be imposed on them in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　被处置证券公司的董事、监事、高级管理人员等有关人员有下列情形之一的，处以其年收入1倍以上2倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，处以其年收入2倍以上5倍以下的罚款，并可以按照规定对其采取证券市场禁入的措施：  
（一）拒绝配合现场工作组、托管组、接管组、行政清理组依法履行职责；  
（二）拒绝向托管组、接管组、行政清理组移交财产、印章或者账簿、文书等资料；  
（三）隐匿、销毁、伪造有关资料，或者故意提供虚假情况；  
（四）隐匿财产，擅自转移、转让财产；  
（五）妨碍证券公司正常经营管理秩序和业务运行，诱发不稳定因素；  
（六）妨碍处置证券公司风险工作正常进行的其他情形。  
证券公司控股股东或者实际控制人指使董事、监事、高级管理人员有前款规定的违法行为的，对控股股东、实际控制人依照前款规定从重处罚。

**Article 60** Where directors, supervisors, senior officers and other relevant persons of a securities company under disposal are under any of the following circumstances, a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times his annual income shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times his annual income shall be imposed, and measures of market prohibition may be imposed on him in accordance with the provisions:  
(1) Refusing to cooperate with the on-site working group, trusteeship group, takeover group or administrative clearing group in performing their duties according to law;  
(2) Refusing to hand over property, seals, account books, documents and other materials to the trusteeship group, takeover group or administrative clearing group;  
(3) Concealing, destroying or forging relevant materials, or intentionally providing false information;  
(4) Concealing property, or transferring or assigning property without authorization;  
(5) Obstructing the normal management order and business operation of the securities company, inducing unstable factors;  
(6) Other circumstances that obstruct the normal progress of risk disposal work of the securities company.  
If the controlling shareholder or actual controller of a securities company instigates the directors, supervisors or senior officers to commit any of the illegal acts as specified in the preceding paragraph, the controlling shareholder or actual controller shall be given a severe punishment in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　证券公司因分立、合并或者出现公司章程规定的解散事由需要解散的，应当向国务院证券监督管理机构提出解散申请，并附解散理由和转让证券类资产、了结证券业务、安置客户等方案，经国务院证券监督管理机构批准后依法解散并清算，清算过程接受国务院证券监督管理机构的监督。

**Article 61** Where a securities company needs to be dissolved due to split, merger or occurrence of a dissolution event specified in the company's articles of association, it shall file an application for dissolution with the securities regulatory authority of the State Council, and attach the reasons for dissolution and plans for transferring securities-type assets, settling securities business, and arranging clients, etc. After approval by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council, the securities company shall be dissolved and liquidated according to law, and the liquidation process shall be subject to the supervision of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　期货公司风险处置参照本条例的规定执行。

**Article 62** The risk disposal of futures companies shall be carried out with reference to the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 63** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
