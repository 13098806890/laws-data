# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》若干问题的规定（四）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2017〕16号  
**公布日期**：2017-08-25  
**生效日期**：2017-09-01

> 最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国公司法〉若干问题的规定（四）》已于2016年12月5日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1702次会议通过，现予公布，自2017年9月1日起施行。 2017年8月25日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》 若干问题的规定（四）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　公司股东、董事、监事等请求确认股东会或者股东大会、董事会决议无效或者不成立的，人民法院应当依法予以受理。

**Article 1** Where a shareholder, director, supervisor or other person of a company requests to confirm that a resolution of the shareholders' meeting, general meeting of shareholders or board of directors is void or does not exist, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　依据[公司法第二十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-22)第二款请求撤销股东会或者股东大会、董事会决议的原告，应当在起诉时具有公司股东资格。

**Article 2** A plaintiff who requests to rescind a resolution of the shareholders' meeting, general meeting of shareholders or board of directors pursuant to Article 22, paragraph 2 of the Company Law shall have the status of a company shareholder at the time of prosecution.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　原告请求确认股东会或者股东大会、董事会决议不成立、无效或者撤销决议的案件，应当列公司为被告。对决议涉及的其他利害关系人，可以依法列为第三人。  
一审法庭辩论终结前，其他有原告资格的人以相同的诉讼请求申请参加前款规定诉讼的，可以列为共同原告。

**Article 3** In a case where the plaintiff requests to confirm that a resolution of the shareholders' meeting, general meeting of shareholders or board of directors does not exist, is void or to rescind the resolution, the company shall be listed as the defendant. Other interested persons involved in the resolution may be listed as third parties according to law.  
Before the conclusion of the court debate in the first instance, if other persons qualified as plaintiffs apply to participate in the litigation prescribed in the preceding paragraph with the same claim, they may be listed as co-plaintiffs.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　股东请求撤销股东会或者股东大会、董事会决议，符合[公司法第二十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-22)第二款规定的，人民法院应当予以支持，但会议召集程序或者表决方式仅有轻微瑕疵，且对决议未产生实质影响的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 4** Where a shareholder requests to rescind a resolution of the shareholders' meeting, general meeting of shareholders or board of directors, and such request meets the provisions of Article 22, paragraph 2 of the Company Law, the People's Court shall support it; provided that if the meeting convening procedure or voting method has only minor defects and does not have a substantive impact on the resolution, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　股东会或者股东大会、董事会决议存在下列情形之一，当事人主张决议不成立的，人民法院应当予以支持：  
（一）公司未召开会议的，但依据[公司法第三十七条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-37)第二款或者公司章程规定可以不召开股东会或者股东大会而直接作出决定，并由全体股东在决定文件上签名、盖章的除外；  
（二）会议未对决议事项进行表决的；  
（三）出席会议的人数或者股东所持表决权不符合公司法或者公司章程规定的；  
（四）会议的表决结果未达到公司法或者公司章程规定的通过比例的；  
（五）导致决议不成立的其他情形。

**Article 5** Where a resolution of the shareholders' meeting, general meeting of shareholders or board of directors falls under any of the following circumstances, and a party claims that the resolution does not exist, the People's Court shall support it:  
(1) The company did not convene a meeting, except where a decision may be made directly without convening a shareholders' meeting or general meeting of shareholders pursuant to Article 37, paragraph 2 of the Company Law or the provisions of the company's articles of association, and all shareholders have signed or affixed their seals on the decision document;  
(2) The meeting did not vote on the matter of the resolution;  
(3) The number of persons attending the meeting or the voting rights held by shareholders do not conform to the provisions of the Company Law or the company's articles of association;  
(4) The voting result of the meeting does not reach the passage ratio required by the Company Law or the company's articles of association;  
(5) Other circumstances that cause the resolution not to exist.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　股东会或者股东大会、董事会决议被人民法院判决确认无效或者撤销的，公司依据该决议与善意相对人形成的民事法律关系不受影响。

**Article 6** Where a resolution of the shareholders' meeting, general meeting of shareholders or board of directors is confirmed void or rescinded by a judgment of the People's Court, the civil legal relationship formed between the company and a bona fide counterparty based on such resolution shall not be affected.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　股东依据[公司法第三十三条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-33)、第九十七条或者公司章程的规定，起诉请求查阅或者复制公司特定文件材料的，人民法院应当依法予以受理。  
公司有证据证明前款规定的原告在起诉时不具有公司股东资格的，人民法院应当驳回起诉，但原告有初步证据证明在持股期间其合法权益受到损害，请求依法查阅或者复制其持股期间的公司特定文件材料的除外。

**Article 7** Where a shareholder, pursuant to Article 33 or Article 97 of the Company Law or the provisions of the company's articles of association, prosecutes to request inspection or reproduction of specific documents and materials of the company, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.  
If the company has evidence to prove that the plaintiff prescribed in the preceding paragraph does not have the status of a company shareholder at the time of prosecution, the People's Court shall dismiss the prosecution, unless the plaintiff has preliminary evidence to prove that its legitimate rights and interests were harmed during the period of holding shares and requests to inspect or reproduce the specific documents and materials of the company during such period according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　有限责任公司有证据证明股东存在下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定股东有[公司法第三十三条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-33)第二款规定的“不正当目的”：  
（一）股东自营或者为他人经营与公司主营业务有实质性竞争关系业务的，但公司章程另有规定或者全体股东另有约定的除外；  
（二）股东为了向他人通报有关信息查阅公司会计账簿，可能损害公司合法利益的；  
（三）股东在向公司提出查阅请求之日前的三年内，曾通过查阅公司会计账簿，向他人通报有关信息损害公司合法利益的；  
（四）股东有不正当目的的其他情形。

**Article 8** Where a limited liability company has evidence to prove that a shareholder falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall determine that the shareholder has an 'improper purpose' as prescribed in Article 33, paragraph 2 of the Company Law:  
(1) The shareholder operates a business that has a substantive competitive relationship with the company's main business, either independently or for others, unless otherwise provided in the company's articles of association or otherwise agreed by all shareholders;  
(2) The shareholder inspects the company's accounting books for the purpose of disclosing relevant information to others, which may harm the company's legitimate interests;  
(3) Within three years prior to the date of the shareholder's request for inspection to the company, the shareholder, through inspecting the company's accounting books, disclosed relevant information to others, harming the company's legitimate interests;  
(4) Other circumstances where the shareholder has an improper purpose.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　公司章程、股东之间的协议等实质性剥夺股东依据[公司法第三十三条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-33)、第九十七条规定查阅或者复制公司文件材料的权利，公司以此为由拒绝股东查阅或者复制的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 9** Where the company's articles of association, an agreement among shareholders, etc., substantially deprive a shareholder of the right to inspect or reproduce the company's documents and materials as prescribed in Article 33 or Article 97 of the Company Law, and the company refuses such inspection or reproduction on that ground, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院审理股东请求查阅或者复制公司特定文件材料的案件，对原告诉讼请求予以支持的，应当在判决中明确查阅或者复制公司特定文件材料的时间、地点和特定文件材料的名录。  
股东依据人民法院生效判决查阅公司文件材料的，在该股东在场的情况下，可以由会计师、律师等依法或者依据执业行为规范负有保密义务的中介机构执业人员辅助进行。

**Article 10** Where the People's Court hears a case in which a shareholder requests inspection or reproduction of specific documents and materials of the company, and sustains the plaintiff's claim, it shall specify in the judgment the time, place and list of specific documents and materials for inspection or reproduction.  
Where a shareholder inspects the company's documents and materials pursuant to an effective judgment of the People's Court, such inspection may be assisted by accountants, lawyers or other professionals of intermediary institutions who are obliged to maintain confidentiality according to law or professional conduct rules, provided that the shareholder is present.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　股东行使知情权后泄露公司商业秘密导致公司合法利益受到损害，公司请求该股东赔偿相关损失的，人民法院应当予以支持。  
根据[本规定第十条](#art-10)辅助股东查阅公司文件材料的会计师、律师等泄露公司商业秘密导致公司合法利益受到损害，公司请求其赔偿相关损失的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 11** Where a shareholder, after exercising the right to know, discloses the company's trade secrets, causing harm to the company's legitimate interests, and the company requests the shareholder to compensate for the relevant losses, the People's Court shall support it.  
Where an accountant, lawyer or other person who assists a shareholder in inspecting the company's documents and materials according to Article 10 of these Provisions discloses the company's trade secrets, causing harm to the company's legitimate interests, and the company requests such person to compensate for the relevant losses, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　公司董事、高级管理人员等未依法履行职责，导致公司未依法制作或者保存[公司法第三十三条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-33)、第九十七条规定的公司文件材料，给股东造成损失，股东依法请求负有相应责任的公司董事、高级管理人员承担民事赔偿责任的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 12** Where directors, senior officers or other persons of a company fail to perform their duties according to law, resulting in the company's failure to produce or preserve the company's documents and materials as prescribed in Article 33 or Article 97 of the Company Law according to law, causing losses to shareholders, and the shareholders request according to law that the directors or senior officers who bear corresponding liability bear civil liability for compensation, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　股东请求公司分配利润案件，应当列公司为被告。  
一审法庭辩论终结前，其他股东基于同一分配方案请求分配利润并申请参加诉讼的，应当列为共同原告。

**Article 13** In a case where a shareholder requests the company to distribute profits, the company shall be listed as the defendant.  
Before the conclusion of the court debate in the first instance, if other shareholders request distribution of profits based on the same distribution plan and apply to participate in the litigation, they shall be listed as co-plaintiffs.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　股东提交载明具体分配方案的股东会或者股东大会的有效决议，请求公司分配利润，公司拒绝分配利润且其关于无法执行决议的抗辩理由不成立的，人民法院应当判决公司按照决议载明的具体分配方案向股东分配利润。

**Article 14** Where a shareholder submits a valid resolution of the shareholders' meeting or general meeting of shareholders specifying a specific distribution plan, and requests the company to distribute profits, and the company refuses to distribute profits and its defense reason that the resolution cannot be executed is not established, the People's Court shall render a judgment ordering the company to distribute profits to the shareholder in accordance with the specific distribution plan specified in the resolution.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　股东未提交载明具体分配方案的股东会或者股东大会决议，请求公司分配利润的，人民法院应当驳回其诉讼请求，但违反法律规定滥用股东权利导致公司不分配利润，给其他股东造成损失的除外。

**Article 15** Where a shareholder requests the company to distribute profits without submitting a valid resolution of the shareholders' meeting or general meeting of shareholders specifying a specific distribution plan, the People's Court shall dismiss its claim, unless the failure to distribute profits is caused by the abuse of shareholders' rights in violation of legal provisions, thereby causing losses to other shareholders.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　有限责任公司的自然人股东因继承发生变化时，其他股东主张依据[公司法第七十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-71)第三款规定行使优先购买权的，人民法院不予支持，但公司章程另有规定或者全体股东另有约定的除外。

**Article 16** Where a natural person shareholder of a limited liability company undergoes a change due to succession, if other shareholders claim to exercise the preemptive right to purchase according to Article 71, paragraph 3 of the Company Law, the People's Court shall not support it, unless otherwise provided in the company's articles of association or otherwise agreed by all shareholders.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　有限责任公司的股东向股东以外的人转让股权，应就其股权转让事项以书面或者其他能够确认收悉的合理方式通知其他股东征求同意。其他股东半数以上不同意转让，不同意的股东不购买的，人民法院应当认定视为同意转让。  
经股东同意转让的股权，其他股东主张转让股东应当向其以书面或者其他能够确认收悉的合理方式通知转让股权的同等条件的，人民法院应当予以支持。  
经股东同意转让的股权，在同等条件下，转让股东以外的其他股东主张优先购买的，人民法院应当予以支持，但转让股东依据[本规定第二十条](#art-20)放弃转让的除外。

**Article 17** Where a shareholder of a limited liability company transfers equity to a person other than a shareholder, it shall notify other shareholders of the equity transfer matter in writing or by other reasonable means that can confirm receipt, seeking their consent. If more than half of the other shareholders do not consent to the transfer, and the dissenting shareholders do not purchase the equity, the People's Court shall determine that they are deemed to have consented to the transfer.  
Where equity is transferred with the consent of the shareholders, if other shareholders claim that the transferring shareholder shall notify them of the same conditions for the equity transfer in writing or by other reasonable means that can confirm receipt, the People's Court shall support it.  
Where equity is transferred with the consent of the shareholders, if other shareholders other than the transferring shareholder claim the preemptive right to purchase under the same conditions, the People's Court shall support it, unless the transferring shareholder abandons the transfer pursuant to Article 20 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法院在判断是否符合[公司法第七十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-71)第三款及本规定所称的“同等条件”时，应当考虑转让股权的数量、价格、支付方式及期限等因素。

**Article 18** When determining whether the 'same conditions' as referred to in Article 71, paragraph 3 of the Company Law and these Provisions are met, the People's Court shall consider factors such as the quantity, price, payment method and time limit of the equity transfer.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　有限责任公司的股东主张优先购买转让股权的，应当在收到通知后，在公司章程规定的行使期间内提出购买请求。公司章程没有规定行使期间或者规定不明确的，以通知确定的期间为准，通知确定的期间短于三十日或者未明确行使期间的，行使期间为三十日。

**Article 19** Where a shareholder of a limited liability company claims the preemptive right to purchase the transferred equity, it shall submit a purchase request within the exercise period prescribed in the company's articles of association after receiving the notice. If the company's articles of association do not prescribe an exercise period or the prescription is unclear, the period determined in the notice shall prevail. If the period determined in the notice is shorter than 30 days or if the exercise period is not specified in the notice, the exercise period shall be 30 days.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　有限责任公司的转让股东，在其他股东主张优先购买后又不同意转让股权的，对其他股东优先购买的主张，人民法院不予支持，但公司章程另有规定或者全体股东另有约定的除外。其他股东主张转让股东赔偿其损失合理的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 20** Where the transferring shareholder of a limited liability company, after other shareholders claim the preemptive right to purchase, subsequently does not consent to the equity transfer, the People's Court shall not support the claim for preemptive purchase by other shareholders, unless otherwise provided in the company's articles of association or otherwise agreed by all shareholders. If other shareholders claim that the transferring shareholder shall compensate them for reasonable losses, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　有限责任公司的股东向股东以外的人转让股权，未就其股权转让事项征求其他股东意见，或者以欺诈、恶意串通等手段，损害其他股东优先购买权，其他股东主张按照同等条件购买该转让股权的，人民法院应当予以支持，但其他股东自知道或者应当知道行使优先购买权的同等条件之日起三十日内没有主张，或者自股权变更登记之日起超过一年的除外。  
前款规定的其他股东仅提出确认股权转让合同及股权变动效力等请求，未同时主张按照同等条件购买转让股权的，人民法院不予支持，但其他股东非因自身原因导致无法行使优先购买权，请求损害赔偿的除外。  
股东以外的股权受让人，因股东行使优先购买权而不能实现合同目的的，可以依法请求转让股东承担相应民事责任。

**Article 21** Where a shareholder of a limited liability company transfers equity to a person other than a shareholder without seeking opinions from other shareholders on the equity transfer matter, or harms other shareholders' preemptive right by means such as fraud or malicious collusion, and other shareholders claim to purchase the transferred equity under the same conditions, the People's Court shall support it, unless such other shareholders have not asserted the claim within 30 days from the date when they knew or should have known the same conditions for exercising the preemptive right, or more than one year has elapsed since the equity change registration.  
If other shareholders prescribed in the preceding paragraph only raise requests such as confirming the validity of the equity transfer contract and the equity change, without simultaneously claiming to purchase the transferred equity under the same conditions, the People's Court shall not support it, unless such other shareholders are unable to exercise the preemptive right due to reasons not attributable to themselves and claim damages.  
Where the transferee of equity other than a shareholder cannot achieve the purpose of the contract due to the shareholder's exercise of the preemptive right, it may request the transferring shareholder to bear corresponding civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　通过拍卖向股东以外的人转让有限责任公司股权的，适用[公司法第七十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-71)第二款、第三款或者第七十二条规定的“书面通知”“通知”“同等条件”时，根据相关法律、司法解释确定。  
在依法设立的产权交易场所转让有限责任公司国有股权的，适用[公司法第七十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-71)第二款、第三款或者第七十二条规定的“书面通知”“通知”“同等条件”时，可以参照产权交易场所的交易规则。

**Article 22** Where the equity of a limited liability company is transferred to a person other than a shareholder by auction, the application of the terms 'written notice', 'notice' and 'same conditions' as prescribed in Article 71, paragraphs 2 and 3, or Article 72 of the Company Law shall be determined in accordance with relevant laws and judicial interpretations.  
Where the state-owned equity of a limited liability company is transferred at a lawfully established property rights exchange, the application of the terms 'written notice', 'notice' and 'same conditions' as prescribed in Article 71, paragraphs 2 and 3, or Article 72 of the Company Law may refer to the trading rules of the property rights exchange.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　监事会或者不设监事会的有限责任公司的监事依据[公司法第一百五十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-151)第一款规定对董事、高级管理人员提起诉讼的，应当列公司为原告，依法由监事会主席或者不设监事会的有限责任公司的监事代表公司进行诉讼。  
董事会或者不设董事会的有限责任公司的执行董事依据[公司法第一百五十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-151)第一款规定对监事提起诉讼的，或者依据[公司法第一百五十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-151)第三款规定对他人提起诉讼的，应当列公司为原告，依法由董事长或者执行董事代表公司进行诉讼。

**Article 23** Where the board of supervisors, or the supervisor of a limited liability company without a board of supervisors, brings a lawsuit against a director or senior officer pursuant to Article 151, paragraph 1 of the Company Law, the company shall be listed as the plaintiff, and the chairman of the board of supervisors, or the supervisor of a limited liability company without a board of supervisors, shall represent the company in the litigation according to law.  
Where the board of directors, or the executive director of a limited liability company without a board of directors, brings a lawsuit against a supervisor pursuant to Article 151, paragraph 1 of the Company Law, or brings a lawsuit against another person pursuant to Article 151, paragraph 3 of the Company Law, the company shall be listed as the plaintiff, and the chairman of the board of directors or the executive director shall represent the company in the litigation according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　符合[公司法第一百五十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-151)第一款规定条件的股东，依据[公司法第一百五十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-151)第二款、第三款规定，直接对董事、监事、高级管理人员或者他人提起诉讼的，应当列公司为第三人参加诉讼。  
一审法庭辩论终结前，符合[公司法第一百五十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-151)第一款规定条件的其他股东，以相同的诉讼请求申请参加诉讼的，应当列为共同原告。

**Article 24** Where a shareholder who meets the conditions prescribed in Article 151, paragraph 1 of the Company Law, directly brings a lawsuit against a director, supervisor, senior officer or another person pursuant to Article 151, paragraphs 2 and 3 of the Company Law, the company shall be listed as a third party to participate in the litigation.  
Before the conclusion of the court debate in the first instance, if other shareholders who meet the conditions prescribed in Article 151, paragraph 1 of the Company Law apply to participate in the litigation with the same claim, they shall be listed as co-plaintiffs.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　股东依据[公司法第一百五十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-151)第二款、第三款规定直接提起诉讼的案件，胜诉利益归属于公司。股东请求被告直接向其承担民事责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 25** In a case where a shareholder directly brings a lawsuit pursuant to Article 151, paragraphs 2 and 3 of the Company Law, the benefits of winning the lawsuit shall belong to the company. If the shareholder requests the defendant to directly bear civil liability towards it, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　股东依据[公司法第一百五十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-151)第二款、第三款规定直接提起诉讼的案件，其诉讼请求部分或者全部得到人民法院支持的，公司应当承担股东因参加诉讼支付的合理费用。

**Article 26** In a case where a shareholder directly brings a lawsuit pursuant to Article 151, paragraphs 2 and 3 of the Company Law, if its claims are partially or fully sustained by the People's Court, the company shall bear the reasonable expenses paid by the shareholder for participating in the litigation.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本规定自2017年9月1日起施行。  
本规定施行后尚未终审的案件，适用本规定；本规定施行前已经终审的案件，或者适用审判监督程序再审的案件，不适用本规定。

**Article 27** These Provisions shall come into force as of September 1, 2017.  
Cases that have not yet been finally adjudicated after these Provisions come into force shall be governed by these Provisions; cases that have been finally adjudicated before these Provisions come into force, or cases subject to retrial under the trial supervision procedure, shall not be governed by these Provisions.
