# 最高人民法院关于审理企业破产案件指定管理人的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2007〕8号  
**公布日期**：2007-04-04  
**生效日期**：2007-06-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理企业破产案件指定管理人的规定》已于2007年4月4日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1422次会议通过，现予公布，自2007年6月1日起施行。 2007年4月12日 最高人民法院 关于审理企业破产案件指定管理人的规定 法释〔2007〕8号 为公平、公正审理企业破产案件，保证破产审判工作依法顺利进行，促进管理人制度的完善和发展，根据《中华人民共和国企业破产法》的规定，制定本规定。 一、管理人名册的编制 第一条  人民法院审理企业破产案件应当指定管理人。除企业破产法和本规定另有规定外，管理人应当从管理人名册中指定。 第二条  高级人民法院应当根据本辖区律师事务所、会计师事务所、破产清算事务所等社会中介机构及专职从业人员数量和企业破产案件数量，确定由本院或者所辖中级人民法院编制管理人名册。 人民法院应当分别编制社会中介机构管理人名册和个人管理人名册。由直辖市以外的高级人民法院编制的管理人名册中，应当注明社会中介机构和个人所属中级人民法院辖区。 第三条  符合企业破产法规定条件的社会中介机构及其具备相关专业知识并取得执业资格的人员，均可申请编入管理人名册。已被编入机构管理人名册的社会中介机构中，具备相关专业知识并取得执业资格的人员，可以申请编入个人管理人名册。 第四条  社会中介机构及个人申请编入管理人名册的，应当向所在地区编制管理人名册的人民法院提出，由该人民法院予以审定。 人民法院不受理异地申请，但异地社会中介机构在本辖区内设立的分支机构提出申请的除外。 第五条  人民法院应当通过本辖区有影响的媒体就编制管理人名册的有关事项进行公告。公告应当包括以下内容： （一）管理人申报条件；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  人民法院审理企业破产案件应当指定管理人。除企业破产法和本规定另有规定外，管理人应当从管理人名册中指定。

**Article 1** When hearing an enterprise bankruptcy case, the People's Court shall appoint an administrator. Unless otherwise provided by the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law and these Provisions, the administrator shall be appointed from the administrator list.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  高级人民法院应当根据本辖区律师事务所、会计师事务所、破产清算事务所等社会中介机构及专职从业人员数量和企业破产案件数量，确定由本院或者所辖中级人民法院编制管理人名册。  
人民法院应当分别编制社会中介机构管理人名册和个人管理人名册。由直辖市以外的高级人民法院编制的管理人名册中，应当注明社会中介机构和个人所属中级人民法院辖区。

**Article 2** The Higher People's Court shall, based on the number of social intermediary institutions such as law firms, accounting firms, and bankruptcy liquidation firms, as well as the number of full-time practitioners and enterprise bankruptcy cases within its jurisdiction, determine whether to compile the administrator list by itself or by the Intermediate People's Court under its jurisdiction.  
The People's Court shall separately compile the administrator list for social intermediary institutions and the administrator list for individuals. In the administrator list compiled by the Higher People's Court other than that of a municipality directly under the Central Government, the intermediate people's court jurisdiction to which the social intermediary institution or individual belongs shall be indicated.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  符合企业破产法规定条件的社会中介机构及其具备相关专业知识并取得执业资格的人员，均可申请编入管理人名册。已被编入机构管理人名册的社会中介机构中，具备相关专业知识并取得执业资格的人员，可以申请编入个人管理人名册。

**Article 3** Social intermediary institutions that meet the conditions prescribed by the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, and the personnel thereof who possess relevant professional knowledge and have obtained practice qualifications, may all apply for inclusion in the administrator list. Personnel who possess relevant professional knowledge and have obtained practice qualifications within a social intermediary institution that has already been included in the institutional administrator list may apply for inclusion in the individual administrator list.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  社会中介机构及个人申请编入管理人名册的，应当向所在地区编制管理人名册的人民法院提出，由该人民法院予以审定。  
人民法院不受理异地申请，但异地社会中介机构在本辖区内设立的分支机构提出申请的除外。

**Article 4** Where a social intermediary institution or an individual applies for inclusion in the administrator list, the application shall be submitted to the People's Court in the region that compiles the administrator list, and such People's Court shall examine and determine the application.  
The People's Court shall not accept an application from another locality, except where an application is filed by a branch office established within its jurisdiction by a social intermediary institution from another locality.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  人民法院应当通过本辖区有影响的媒体就编制管理人名册的有关事项进行公告。公告应当包括以下内容：  
（一）管理人申报条件；  
（二）应当提交的材料；  
（三）评定标准、程序；  
（四）管理人的职责以及相应的法律责任；  
（五）提交申报材料的截止时间；  
（六）人民法院认为应当公告的其他事项。

**Article 5** The People's Court shall make a public announcement through influential media within its jurisdiction regarding the matters related to the compilation of the administrator list. The announcement shall include the following:  
(1) Conditions for applying as an administrator;  
(2) Materials to be submitted;  
(3) Evaluation criteria and procedures;  
(4) Duties of the administrator and the corresponding legal liability;  
(5) Deadline for submitting application materials;  
(6) Other matters that the People's Court considers shall be announced.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  律师事务所、会计师事务所申请编入管理人名册的，应当提供下列材料：  
（一）执业证书、依法批准设立文件或者营业执照；  
（二）章程；  
（三）本单位专职从业人员名单及其执业资格证书复印件；  
（四）业务和业绩材料；  
（五）行业自律组织对所提供材料真实性以及有无被行政处罚或者纪律处分情况的证明；  
（六）人民法院要求的其他材料。

**Article 6** Where a law firm or an accounting firm applies for inclusion in the administrator list, it shall provide the following materials:  
(1) Practice certificate, document of approval for establishment according to law, or business license;  
(2) Articles of association;  
(3) List of full-time practitioners of the institution and copies of their practice qualification certificates;  
(4) Business and performance materials;  
(5) Certificate issued by a self-regulatory organization of the industry on the authenticity of the materials provided and whether any administrative penalty or disciplinary sanction has been imposed;  
(6) Other materials required by the People's Court.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  破产清算事务所申请编入管理人名册的，应当提供以下材料：  
（一）营业执照或者依法批准设立的文件；  
（二）本单位专职从业人员的法律或者注册会计师资格证书，或者经营管理经历的证明材料；  
（三）业务和业绩材料；  
（四）能够独立承担民事责任的证明材料；  
（五）行业自律组织对所提供材料真实性以及有无被行政处罚或者纪律处分情况的证明，或者申请人就上述情况所作的真实性声明；  
（六）人民法院要求的其他材料。

**Article 7** Where a bankruptcy liquidation firm applies for inclusion in the administrator list, it shall provide the following materials:  
(1) Business license or document of approval for establishment according to law;  
(2) Legal or certified public accountant qualification certificates of its full-time practitioners, or evidentiary materials of their management experience;  
(3) Business and performance materials;  
(4) Evidentiary materials demonstrating the ability to independently bear civil liability;  
(5) Certificate issued by a self-regulatory organization of the industry on the authenticity of the materials provided and whether any administrative penalty or disciplinary sanction has been imposed, or a declaration of authenticity made by the applicant in respect of the foregoing;  
(6) Other materials required by the People's Court.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  个人申请编入管理人名册的，应当提供下列材料：  
（一）律师或者注册会计师执业证书复印件以及执业年限证明；  
（二）所在社会中介机构同意其担任管理人的函件；  
（三）业务专长及相关业绩材料；  
（四）执业责任保险证明；  
（五）行业自律组织对所提供材料真实性以及有无被行政处罚或者纪律处分情况的证明；  
（六）人民法院要求的其他材料。

**Article 8** Where an individual applies for inclusion in the administrator list, he or she shall provide the following materials:  
(1) Copy of the lawyer's or certified public accountant's practice certificate and proof of years of practice;  
(2) Letter from the social intermediary institution to which he or she belongs consenting to his or her appointment as an administrator;  
(3) Materials on professional expertise and relevant performance;  
(4) Proof of professional liability insurance;  
(5) Certificate issued by a self-regulatory organization of the industry on the authenticity of the materials provided and whether any administrative penalty or disciplinary sanction has been imposed;  
(6) Other materials required by the People's Court.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  社会中介机构及个人具有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以适用[企业破产法第二十四条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-24)第三款第四项的规定：  
（一）因执业、经营中故意或者重大过失行为，受到行政机关、监管机构或者行业自律组织行政处罚或者纪律处分之日起未逾三年；  
（二）因涉嫌违法行为正被相关部门调查；  
（三）因不适当履行职务或者拒绝接受人民法院指定等原因，被人民法院从管理人名册除名之日起未逾三年；  
（四）缺乏担任管理人所应具备的专业能力；  
（五）缺乏承担民事责任的能力；  
（六）人民法院认为可能影响履行管理人职责的其他情形。

**Article 9** Where a social intermediary institution or an individual falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may apply the provisions of Article 24, Paragraph 3, Item (4) of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law:  
(1) Less than three years have elapsed since the date of imposition of an administrative penalty or disciplinary sanction by an administrative organ, regulatory authority, or self-regulatory organization of the industry due to intentional or grossly negligent acts in the course of practice or business operations;  
(2) Being under investigation by relevant departments for suspected illegal acts;  
(3) Less than three years have elapsed since the date of removal from the administrator list by the People's Court due to improper performance of duties or refusal to accept appointment by the People's Court, among other reasons;  
(4) Lacking the professional competence required to serve as an administrator;  
(5) Lacking the ability to bear civil liability;  
(6) Other circumstances that the People's Court considers may affect the discharge of the administrator's duties.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  编制管理人名册的人民法院应当组成专门的评审委员会，决定编入管理人名册的社会中介机构和个人名单。评审委员会成员应不少于七人。  
人民法院应当根据本辖区社会中介机构以及社会中介机构中个人的实际情况，结合其执业业绩、能力、专业水准、社会中介机构的规模、办理企业破产案件的经验等因素制定管理人评定标准，由评审委员会根据申报人的具体情况评定其综合分数。  
人民法院根据评审委员会评审结果，确定管理人初审名册。

**Article 10** The People's Court compiling the administrator list shall form a special evaluation committee to decide on the list of social intermediary institutions and individuals to be included in the administrator list. The members of the evaluation committee shall be no fewer than seven persons.  
The People's Court shall formulate evaluation criteria for administrators based on the actual circumstances of social intermediary institutions and individuals within its jurisdiction, taking into account factors such as their practice performance, capability, professional standards, the size of the social intermediary institution, and experience in handling enterprise bankruptcy cases. The evaluation committee shall assess the comprehensive scores of the applicants based on their specific circumstances.  
The People's Court shall, based on the evaluation results of the evaluation committee, determine the preliminary administrator list.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  人民法院应当将管理人初审名册通过本辖区有影响的媒体进行公示，公示期为十日。  
对于针对编入初审名册的社会中介机构和个人提出的异议，人民法院应当进行审查。异议成立、申请人确不宜担任管理人的，人民法院应将该社会中介机构或者个人从管理人初审名册中删除。

**Article 11** The People's Court shall publicize the preliminary administrator list through influential media within its jurisdiction, and the publicity period shall be ten days.  
The People's Court shall examine any objections raised against the social intermediary institutions or individuals included in the preliminary list. If the objection is established and the applicant is indeed unsuitable to serve as an administrator, the People's Court shall remove such social intermediary institution or individual from the preliminary administrator list.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  公示期满后，人民法院应审定管理人名册，并通过全国有影响的媒体公布，同时逐级报最高人民法院备案。

**Article 12** After the publicity period expires, the People's Court shall finalize the administrator list and publish it through nationally influential media, and at the same time report it to the Supreme People's Court for archival filing level by level.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  人民法院可以根据本辖区的实际情况，分批确定编入管理人名册的社会中介机构及个人。  
编制管理人名册的全部资料应当建立档案备查。

**Article 13** The People's Court may, based on the actual circumstances of its jurisdiction, determine the social intermediary institutions and individuals to be included in the administrator list in batches.  
All materials for compiling the administrator list shall be archived for future reference.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  人民法院可以根据企业破产案件受理情况、管理人履行职务以及管理人资格变化等因素，对管理人名册适时进行调整。新编入管理人名册的社会中介机构和个人应当按照本规定的程序办理。  
人民法院发现社会中介机构或者个人有[企业破产法第二十四条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-24)第三款规定情形的，应当将其从管理人名册中除名。  
二、管理人的指定

**Article 14** The People's Court may, based on factors such as the acceptance of enterprise bankruptcy cases, the performance of duties by administrators, and changes in the qualifications of administrators, make timely adjustments to the administrator list. Social intermediary institutions and individuals newly included in the administrator list shall follow the procedures set forth in these Provisions.  
Where the People's Court discovers that a social intermediary institution or an individual falls under the circumstances specified in Article 24, Paragraph 3 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, it shall remove such person or institution from the administrator list.  
II. Appointment of Administrators

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  受理企业破产案件的人民法院指定管理人，一般应从本地管理人名册中指定。  
对于商业银行、证券公司、保险公司等金融机构以及在全国范围内有重大影响、法律关系复杂、债务人财产分散的企业破产案件，人民法院可以从所在地区高级人民法院编制的管理人名册列明的其他地区管理人或者异地人民法院编制的管理人名册中指定管理人。

**Article 15** The People's Court accepting an enterprise bankruptcy case shall, in general, appoint an administrator from the local administrator list.  
For enterprise bankruptcy cases involving financial institutions such as commercial banks, securities companies, and insurance companies, as well as cases that have a significant impact nationwide, involve complex legal relationships, or have dispersed debtor assets, the People's Court may appoint an administrator from among administrators in other regions listed in the administrator list compiled by the Higher People's Court of the region where the court is located, or from the administrator list compiled by a People's Court of another locality.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  受理企业破产案件的人民法院，一般应指定管理人名册中的社会中介机构担任管理人。

**Article 16** The People's Court accepting an enterprise bankruptcy case shall, in general, appoint a social intermediary institution from the administrator list as the administrator.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  对于事实清楚、债权债务关系简单、债务人财产相对集中的企业破产案件，人民法院可以指定管理人名册中的个人为管理人。

**Article 17** For enterprise bankruptcy cases with clear facts, simple creditor-debtor relationships, and relatively concentrated debtor assets, the People's Court may appoint an individual from the administrator list as the administrator.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  企业破产案件有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以指定清算组为管理人：  
（一）破产申请受理前，根据有关规定已经成立清算组，人民法院认为符合[本规定第十九条](#art-19)的规定；  
（二）审理[企业破产法第一百三十三条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-133)规定的案件；  
（三）有关法律规定企业破产时成立清算组；  
（四）人民法院认为可以指定清算组为管理人的其他情形。

**Article 18** Where an enterprise bankruptcy case falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may appoint a liquidation committee as the administrator:  
(1) A liquidation committee has already been established before the acceptance of the bankruptcy petition in accordance with relevant provisions, and the People's Court deems it meets the requirements of Article 19 of these Provisions;  
(2) Adjudicating a case as prescribed by Article 133 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law;  
(3) Relevant laws provide for the establishment of a liquidation committee upon enterprise bankruptcy;  
(4) Other circumstances under which the People's Court considers it may appoint a liquidation committee as the administrator.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条  清算组为管理人的，人民法院可以从政府有关部门、编入管理人名册的社会中介机构、金融资产管理公司中指定清算组成员，人民银行及金融监督管理机构可以按照有关法律和行政法规的规定派人参加清算组。

**Article 19** Where a liquidation committee acts as the administrator, the People's Court may appoint members of the liquidation committee from relevant government departments, social intermediary institutions included in the administrator list, and financial asset management companies. The People's Bank of China and financial regulatory authorities may send personnel to participate in the liquidation committee in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条  人民法院一般应当按照管理人名册所列名单采取轮候、抽签、摇号等随机方式公开指定管理人。

**Article 20** The People's Court shall, in general, publicly appoint an administrator by random methods such as rotation, drawing lots, or shaking numbers in accordance with the list set out in the administrator list.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条  对于商业银行、证券公司、保险公司等金融机构或者在全国范围有重大影响、法律关系复杂、债务人财产分散的企业破产案件，人民法院可以采取公告的方式，邀请编入各地人民法院管理人名册中的社会中介机构参与竞争，从参与竞争的社会中介机构中指定管理人。参与竞争的社会中介机构不得少于三家。  
采取竞争方式指定管理人的，人民法院应当组成专门的评审委员会。  
评审委员会应当结合案件的特点，综合考量社会中介机构的专业水准、经验、机构规模、初步报价等因素，从参与竞争的社会中介机构中择优指定管理人。被指定为管理人的社会中介机构应经评审委员会成员二分之一以上通过。  
采取竞争方式指定管理人的，人民法院应当确定一至两名备选社会中介机构，作为需要更换管理人时的接替人选。

**Article 21** For enterprise bankruptcy cases involving financial institutions such as commercial banks, securities companies, and insurance companies, or cases that have a significant impact nationwide, involve complex legal relationships, or have dispersed debtor assets, the People's Court may, by way of public announcement, invite social intermediary institutions included in the administrator lists of various people's courts to participate in competition, and appoint an administrator from among the social intermediary institutions that participate in the competition. The number of social intermediary institutions participating in the competition shall be no fewer than three.  
Where an administrator is appointed through competition, the People's Court shall form a special evaluation committee.  
The evaluation committee shall, taking into account the characteristics of the case, comprehensively evaluate factors such as the professional standards, experience, institutional scale, and preliminary quotation of the social intermediary institutions, and select and appoint an administrator from among the social intermediary institutions participating in the competition. The social intermediary institution appointed as the administrator shall be approved by more than one-half of the members of the evaluation committee.  
Where an administrator is appointed through competition, the People's Court shall determine one or two alternative social intermediary institutions to serve as replacements when a change of administrator is required.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条  对于经过行政清理、清算的商业银行、证券公司、保险公司等金融机构的破产案件，人民法院除可以按照[本规定第十八条](#art-18)第一项的规定指定管理人外，也可以在金融监督管理机构推荐的已编入管理人名册的社会中介机构中指定管理人。

**Article 22** For bankruptcy cases involving financial institutions such as commercial banks, securities companies, and insurance companies that have undergone administrative clean-up or liquidation, in addition to appointing an administrator in accordance with Article 18(1) of these Provisions, the People's Court may also appoint an administrator from among the social intermediary institutions included in the administrator list that are recommended by financial regulatory authorities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条  社会中介机构、清算组成员有下列情形之一，可能影响其忠实履行管理人职责的，人民法院可以认定为[企业破产法第二十四条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-24)第三款第三项规定的利害关系：  
（一）与债务人、债权人有未了结的债权债务关系；  
（二）在人民法院受理破产申请前三年内，曾为债务人提供相对固定的中介服务；  
（三）现在是或者在人民法院受理破产申请前三年内曾经是债务人、债权人的控股股东或者实际控制人；  
（四）现在担任或者在人民法院受理破产申请前三年内曾经担任债务人、债权人的财务顾问、法律顾问；  
（五）人民法院认为可能影响其忠实履行管理人职责的其他情形。

**Article 23** Where a social intermediary institution or a member of a liquidation committee falls under any of the following circumstances, which may affect its or his faithful discharge of the administrator's duties, the People's Court may determine that it constitutes an interest as prescribed in Article 24, Paragraph 3, Item (3) of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law:  
(1) Having an unsettled creditor-debtor relationship with the debtor or a creditor;  
(2) Having provided relatively fixed intermediary services to the debtor within three years prior to the acceptance of the bankruptcy petition by the People's Court;  
(3) Currently being, or having been within three years prior to the acceptance of the bankruptcy petition by the People's Court, a controlling shareholder or de facto controller of the debtor or a creditor;  
(4) Currently serving, or having served within three years prior to the acceptance of the bankruptcy petition by the People's Court, as a financial advisor or legal counsel of the debtor or a creditor;  
(5) Other circumstances that the People's Court considers may affect its or his faithful discharge of the administrator's duties.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条  清算组成员的派出人员、社会中介机构的派出人员、个人管理人有下列情形之一，可能影响其忠实履行管理人职责的，可以认定为[企业破产法第二十四条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-24)第三款第三项规定的利害关系：  
（一）具有[本规定第二十三条](#art-23)规定情形；  
（二）现在担任或者在人民法院受理破产申请前三年内曾经担任债务人、债权人的董事、监事、高级管理人员；  
（三）与债权人或者债务人的控股股东、董事、监事、高级管理人员存在夫妻、直系血亲、三代以内旁系血亲或者近姻亲关系；  
（四）人民法院认为可能影响其公正履行管理人职责的其他情形。

**Article 24** Where a delegated person of a member of a liquidation committee, a delegated person of a social intermediary institution, or an individual administrator falls under any of the following circumstances, which may affect his faithful discharge of the administrator's duties, it may be determined as an interest as prescribed in Article 24, Paragraph 3, Item (3) of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law:  
(1) Having the circumstances specified in Article 23 of these Provisions;  
(2) Currently serving, or having served within three years prior to the acceptance of the bankruptcy petition by the People's Court, as a director, supervisor, or senior officer of the debtor or a creditor;  
(3) Having a spousal relationship, lineal blood relationship, collateral blood relationship within three generations, or close in-law relationship with the controlling shareholder, director, supervisor, or senior officer of a creditor or the debtor;  
(4) Other circumstances that the People's Court considers may affect his impartial discharge of the administrator's duties.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条  在进入指定管理人程序后，社会中介机构或者个人发现与本案有利害关系的，应主动申请回避并向人民法院书面说明情况。人民法院认为社会中介机构或者个人与本案有利害关系的，不应指定该社会中介机构或者个人为本案管理人。

**Article 25** After entering the procedure for appointing an administrator, where a social intermediary institution or an individual discovers that it or he has an interest in the case, it or he shall voluntarily apply for withdrawal and explain the circumstances in writing to the People's Court. If the People's Court considers that the social intermediary institution or individual has an interest in the case, it shall not appoint such social intermediary institution or individual as the administrator in the case.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条  社会中介机构或者个人有重大债务纠纷或者因涉嫌违法行为正被相关部门调查的，人民法院不应指定该社会中介机构或者个人为本案管理人。

**Article 26** Where a social intermediary institution or an individual has a major debt dispute or is under investigation by relevant departments for suspected illegal acts, the People's Court shall not appoint such social intermediary institution or individual as the administrator in the case.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条  人民法院指定管理人应当制作决定书，并向被指定为管理人的社会中介机构或者个人、破产申请人、债务人、债务人的企业登记机关送达。决定书应与受理破产申请的民事裁定书一并公告。

**Article 27** Where the People's Court appoints an administrator, it shall issue a written decision and shall serve it on the social intermediary institution or individual appointed as the administrator, the bankruptcy petitioner, the debtor, and the enterprise registration authority of the debtor. The written decision shall be published together with the civil ruling accepting the bankruptcy petition.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条  管理人无正当理由，不得拒绝人民法院的指定。  
管理人一经指定，不得以任何形式将管理人应当履行的职责全部或者部分转给其他社会中介机构或者个人。

**Article 28** An administrator shall not refuse the appointment by the People's Court without justifiable reasons.  
Once appointed, the administrator shall not, in any form, transfer all or part of the duties that the administrator shall perform to any other social intermediary institution or individual.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条  管理人凭指定管理人决定书按照国家有关规定刻制管理人印章，并交人民法院封样备案后启用。  
管理人印章只能用于所涉破产事务。管理人根据[企业破产法第一百二十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-122)规定终止执行职务后，应当将管理人印章交公安机关销毁，并将销毁的证明送交人民法院。

**Article 29** The administrator shall, based on the written decision appointing the administrator, have the administrator's seal engraved in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and shall submit it to the People's Court for sealing and filing before use.  
The administrator's seal may only be used in the bankruptcy matters concerned. After the administrator terminates the performance of duties in accordance with Article 122 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the administrator shall deliver the administrator's seal to the public security organ for destruction and shall submit the certificate of destruction to the People's Court.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条  受理企业破产案件的人民法院应当将指定管理人过程中形成的材料存入企业破产案件卷宗，债权人会议或者债权人委员会有权查阅。  
三、管理人的更换

**Article 30** The People's Court accepting an enterprise bankruptcy case shall place the materials formed during the process of appointing the administrator into the case file of the enterprise bankruptcy case, and the creditors' meeting or the creditors' committee shall have the right to inspect them.  
III. Replacement of Administrators

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条  债权人会议根据[企业破产法第二十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-22)第二款的规定申请更换管理人的，应由债权人会议作出决议并向人民法院提出书面申请。  
人民法院在收到债权人会议的申请后，应当通知管理人在两日内作出书面说明。

**Article 31** Where the creditors' meeting applies for replacement of the administrator in accordance with Article 22, Paragraph 2 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the creditors' meeting shall adopt a resolution and submit a written application to the People's Court.  
After receiving the application from the creditors' meeting, the People's Court shall notify the administrator to make a written explanation within two days.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条  人民法院认为申请理由不成立的，应当自收到管理人书面说明之日起十日内作出驳回申请的决定。  
人民法院认为申请更换管理人的理由成立的，应当自收到管理人书面说明之日起十日内作出更换管理人的决定。

**Article 32** If the People's Court considers that the reasons for the application are not established, it shall make a decision to reject the application within ten days from the date of receipt of the written explanation from the administrator.  
If the People's Court considers that the reasons for applying for replacement of the administrator are established, it shall make a decision to replace the administrator within ten days from the date of receipt of the written explanation from the administrator.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条  社会中介机构管理人有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以根据债权人会议的申请或者依职权迳行决定更换管理人：  
（一）执业许可证或者营业执照被吊销或者注销；  
（二）出现解散、破产事由或者丧失承担执业责任风险的能力；  
（三）与本案有利害关系；  
（四）履行职务时，因故意或者重大过失导致债权人利益受到损害；  
（五）有[本规定第二十六条](#art-26)规定的情形。  
清算组成员参照适用前款规定。

**Article 33** Where a social intermediary institution acting as an administrator falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may, upon application by the creditors' meeting or ex officio, directly decide to replace the administrator:  
(1) The practice license or business license is revoked or cancelled;  
(2) The occurrence of dissolution or bankruptcy grounds, or loss of the ability to bear the risk of professional liability;  
(3) Having an interest in the case;  
(4) Causing damage to the interests of creditors due to intentional or grossly negligent acts during the performance of duties;  
(5) Having the circumstances specified in Article 26 of these Provisions.  
Members of a liquidation committee shall apply mutatis mutandis the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条  个人管理人有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以根据债权人会议的申请或者依职权迳行决定更换管理人：  
（一）执业资格被取消、吊销；  
（二）与本案有利害关系；  
（三）履行职务时，因故意或者重大过失导致债权人利益受到损害；  
（四）失踪、死亡或者丧失民事行为能力；  
（五）因健康原因无法履行职务；  
（六）执业责任保险失效；  
（七）有[本规定第二十六条](#art-26)规定的情形。  
清算组成员的派出人员、社会中介机构的派出人员参照适用前款规定。

**Article 34** Where an individual administrator falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may, upon application by the creditors' meeting or ex officio, directly decide to replace the administrator:  
(1) The practice qualification is cancelled or revoked;  
(2) Having an interest in the case;  
(3) Causing damage to the interests of creditors due to intentional or grossly negligent acts during the performance of duties;  
(4) Missing, deceased, or losing capacity for civil conduct;  
(5) Unable to perform duties due to health reasons;  
(6) The professional liability insurance has ceased to have effect;  
(7) Having the circumstances specified in Article 26 of these Provisions.  
Delegated persons of members of a liquidation committee and delegated persons of social intermediary institutions shall apply mutatis mutandis the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条  管理人无正当理由申请辞去职务的，人民法院不予许可。正当理由的认定，可参照适用[本规定第三十三条](#art-33)、第三十四条规定的情形。

**Article 35** Where an administrator applies to resign without justifiable reasons, the People's Court shall not grant permission. The determination of justifiable reasons may be made by referring to the circumstances specified in Articles 33 and 34 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条  人民法院对管理人申请辞去职务未予许可，管理人仍坚持辞去职务并不再履行管理人职责的，人民法院应当决定更换管理人。

**Article 36** Where the People's Court does not grant permission for an administrator's application to resign, and the administrator still insists on resigning and ceases to perform the administrator's duties, the People's Court shall decide to replace the administrator.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条  人民法院决定更换管理人的，原管理人应当自收到决定书之次日起，在人民法院监督下向新任管理人移交全部资料、财产、营业事务及管理人印章，并及时向新任管理人书面说明工作进展情况。原管理人不能履行上述职责的，新任管理人可以直接接管相关事务。  
在破产程序终结前，原管理人应当随时接受新任管理人、债权人会议、人民法院关于其履行管理人职责情况的询问。

**Article 37** Where the People's Court decides to replace the administrator, the former administrator shall, from the day following the receipt of the written decision, hand over all materials, property, business operations, and the administrator's seal to the new administrator under the supervision of the People's Court, and shall promptly explain the progress of the work in writing to the new administrator. If the former administrator fails to perform the above duties, the new administrator may directly take over the relevant affairs.  
Before the conclusion of the bankruptcy proceedings, the former administrator shall at all times accept inquiries from the new administrator, the creditors' meeting, and the People's Court regarding its or his performance of the administrator's duties.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条  人民法院决定更换管理人的，应将决定书送达原管理人、新任管理人、破产申请人、债务人以及债务人的企业登记机关，并予公告。

**Article 38** Where the People's Court decides to replace the administrator, it shall serve the written decision on the former administrator, the new administrator, the bankruptcy petitioner, the debtor, and the enterprise registration authority of the debtor, and shall make a public announcement.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条  管理人申请辞去职务未获人民法院许可，但仍坚持辞职并不再履行管理人职责，或者人民法院决定更换管理人后，原管理人拒不向新任管理人移交相关事务，人民法院可以根据[企业破产法第一百三十条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-130)的规定和具体情况，决定对管理人罚款。对社会中介机构为管理人的罚款5万元至20万元人民币，对个人为管理人的罚款1万元至5万元人民币。  
管理人有前款规定行为或者无正当理由拒绝人民法院指定的，编制管理人名册的人民法院可以决定停止其担任管理人一年至三年，或者将其从管理人名册中除名。

**Article 39** Where an administrator's application to resign is not approved by the People's Court but the administrator still insists on resigning and ceases to perform the administrator's duties, or where after the People's Court decides to replace the administrator, the former administrator refuses to hand over the relevant affairs to the new administrator, the People's Court may, in accordance with Article 130 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law and the specific circumstances, decide to impose a fine on the administrator. The fine for a social intermediary institution serving as an administrator shall be between RMB 50,000 and RMB 200,000, and the fine for an individual serving as an administrator shall be between RMB 10,000 and RMB 50,000.  
If an administrator commits the acts specified in the preceding paragraph or refuses the appointment by the People's Court without justifiable reasons, the People's Court that compiled the administrator list may decide to suspend his or its qualification to serve as an administrator for one to three years, or remove him or it from the administrator list.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条  管理人不服罚款决定的，可以向上一级人民法院申请复议，上级人民法院应在收到复议申请后五日内作出决定，并将复议结果通知下级人民法院和当事人。

**Article 40** If an administrator does not accept the decision on a fine, it or he may apply for reconsideration to the people's court at the next higher level. The higher people's court shall make a decision within five days after receiving the application for reconsideration and shall notify the lower people's court and the parties of the reconsideration result.
