# 不动产登记暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2024-03-10  
**生效日期**：2024-05-01

> （2014年11月24日中华人民共和国国务院令第656号公布　根据2019年3月24日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2024年3月10日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为整合不动产登记职责，规范登记行为，方便群众申请登记，保护权利人合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》等法律，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称不动产登记，是指不动产登记机构依法将不动产权利归属和其他法定事项记载于不动产登记簿的行为。 本条例所称不动产，是指土地、海域以及房屋、林木等定着物。 第三条　不动产首次登记、变更登记、转移登记、注销登记、更正登记、异议登记、预告登记、查封登记等，适用本条例。 第四条　国家实行不动产统一登记制度。 不动产登记遵循严格管理、稳定连续、方便群众的原则。 不动产权利人已经依法享有的不动产权利，不因登记机构和登记程序的改变而受到影响。 第五条　下列不动产权利，依照本条例的规定办理登记： （一）集体土地所有权； （二）房屋等建筑物、构筑物所有权； （三）森林、林木所有权； （四）耕地、林地、草地等土地承包经营权； （五）建设用地使用权；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为整合不动产登记职责，规范登记行为，方便群众申请登记，保护权利人合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》等法律，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of integrating the duties of immovable property registration, regulating registration activities, facilitating the public's application for registration, protecting the lawful rights and interests of right holders, and in accordance with laws such as the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称不动产登记，是指不动产登记机构依法将不动产权利归属和其他法定事项记载于不动产登记簿的行为。  
本条例所称不动产，是指土地、海域以及房屋、林木等定着物。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "immovable property registration" means the act of an immovable property registration authority recording the ownership of immovable property and other statutory matters in the immovable property register according to law.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "immovable property" means land, sea areas, and fixtures such as houses, trees, etc.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　不动产首次登记、变更登记、转移登记、注销登记、更正登记、异议登记、预告登记、查封登记等，适用本条例。

**Article 3** These Regulations shall apply to initial registration, change registration, transfer registration, cancellation registration, correction registration, objection registration, advance notice registration, seizure registration, etc., of immovable property.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家实行不动产统一登记制度。  
不动产登记遵循严格管理、稳定连续、方便群众的原则。  
不动产权利人已经依法享有的不动产权利，不因登记机构和登记程序的改变而受到影响。

**Article 4** The State shall implement a unified registration system for immovable property.  
Immovable property registration shall follow the principles of strict management, stability and continuity, and convenience for the public.  
The immovable property rights lawfully enjoyed by the right holder of immovable property shall not be affected by changes in the registration authority or registration procedures.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　下列不动产权利，依照本条例的规定办理登记：  
（一）集体土地所有权；  
（二）房屋等建筑物、构筑物所有权；  
（三）森林、林木所有权；  
（四）耕地、林地、草地等土地承包经营权；  
（五）建设用地使用权；  
（六）宅基地使用权；  
（七）海域使用权；  
（八）地役权；  
（九）抵押权；  
（十）法律规定需要登记的其他不动产权利。

**Article 5** The following immovable property rights shall be registered in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations:  
(1) ownership of collectively-owned land;  
(2) ownership of buildings such as houses and structures;  
(3) ownership of forests and trees;  
(4) contractual management rights of arable land, forest land, grassland, etc.;  
(5) right to use construction land;  
(6) right to use residential land;  
(7) right to use sea areas;  
(8) easement rights;  
(9) mortgage rights;  
(10) other immovable property rights that require registration as provided by law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院自然资源主管部门负责指导、监督全国不动产登记工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当确定一个部门为本行政区域的不动产登记机构，负责不动产登记工作，并接受上级人民政府不动产登记主管部门的指导、监督。

**Article 6** The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall be responsible for guiding and supervising the nationwide immovable property registration work.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall designate a department as the immovable property registration authority for their respective administrative regions, which shall be responsible for immovable property registration work and shall accept the guidance and supervision of the immovable property registration competent department of the people's government at the higher level.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　不动产登记由不动产所在地的县级人民政府不动产登记机构办理；直辖市、设区的市人民政府可以确定本级不动产登记机构统一办理所属各区的不动产登记。  
跨县级行政区域的不动产登记，由所跨县级行政区域的不动产登记机构分别办理。不能分别办理的，由所跨县级行政区域的不动产登记机构协商办理；协商不成的，由共同的上一级人民政府不动产登记主管部门指定办理。  
国务院确定的重点国有林区的森林、林木和林地，国务院批准项目用海、用岛，中央国家机关使用的国有土地等不动产登记，由国务院自然资源主管部门会同有关部门规定。

**Article 7** Immovable property registration shall be handled by the immovable property registration authority of the people's government at the county level where the immovable property is located; the people's government of a municipality directly under the Central Government or a city divided into districts may designate the immovable property registration authority at its own level to uniformly handle the registration in all districts under its jurisdiction.  
For immovable property registration that spans multiple county-level administrative regions, the immovable property registration authorities of the spanned county-level administrative regions shall handle the registration separately. If separate handling is not possible, the immovable property registration authorities of the spanned county-level administrative regions shall negotiate the handling; if negotiation fails, the common immovable property registration competent department of the people's government at the next higher level shall designate the entity to handle it.  
The registration of immovable property such as forests, trees and forest land in key state-owned forest areas determined by the State Council, the use of sea areas and islands approved by the State Council, and state-owned land used by central state organs shall be prescribed by the competent department of natural resources of the State Council in conjunction with relevant departments.

### 第二章　不动产登记簿

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　不动产以不动产单元为基本单位进行登记。不动产单元具有唯一编码。  
不动产登记机构应当按照国务院自然资源主管部门的规定设立统一的不动产登记簿。  
不动产登记簿应当记载以下事项：  
（一）不动产的坐落、界址、空间界限、面积、用途等自然状况；  
（二）不动产权利的主体、类型、内容、来源、期限、权利变化等权属状况；  
（三）涉及不动产权利限制、提示的事项；  
（四）其他相关事项。

**Article 8** Immovable property shall be registered with the immovable property unit as the basic unit. Each immovable property unit shall have a unique code.  
The immovable property registration authority shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of natural resources of the State Council, establish a unified immovable property register.  
The immovable property register shall record the following matters:  
(1) natural conditions such as the location, boundaries, spatial limits, area, and use of the immovable property;  
(2) ownership conditions such as the subject, type, content, source, term, and changes of rights in the immovable property;  
(3) matters concerning restrictions on and prompts regarding immovable property rights;  
(4) other relevant matters.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　不动产登记簿应当采用电子介质，暂不具备条件的，可以采用纸质介质。不动产登记机构应当明确不动产登记簿唯一、合法的介质形式。  
不动产登记簿采用电子介质的，应当定期进行异地备份，并具有唯一、确定的纸质转化形式。

**Article 9** The immovable property register shall be in electronic medium; where conditions are temporarily not available, it may be in paper medium. The immovable property registration authority shall specify the sole and legal medium form of the immovable property register.  
If the immovable property register is in electronic medium, remote backup shall be made periodically, and there shall be a unique and definite form for conversion to paper.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　不动产登记机构应当依法将各类登记事项准确、完整、清晰地记载于不动产登记簿。任何人不得损毁不动产登记簿，除依法予以更正外不得修改登记事项。

**Article 10** The immovable property registration authority shall, according to law, accurately, completely, and clearly record all types of registration matters in the immovable property register. No person shall damage the immovable property register, and no registration matter shall be modified except for corrections made according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　不动产登记工作人员应当具备与不动产登记工作相适应的专业知识和业务能力。  
不动产登记机构应当加强对不动产登记工作人员的管理和专业技术培训。

**Article 11** Personnel of the real estate registration authority shall have the professional knowledge and business capabilities commensurate with the real estate registration work.  
The real estate registration authority shall strengthen the management and professional technical training of its personnel.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　不动产登记机构应当指定专人负责不动产登记簿的保管，并建立健全相应的安全责任制度。  
采用纸质介质不动产登记簿的，应当配备必要的防盗、防火、防渍、防有害生物等安全保护设施。  
采用电子介质不动产登记簿的，应当配备专门的存储设施，并采取信息网络安全防护措施。

**Article 12** The real estate registration authority shall designate dedicated personnel responsible for the custody of the real estate register and establish a sound safety responsibility system.  
Where a paper-based real estate register is used, necessary safety protection facilities against theft, fire, moisture, harmful organisms, etc., shall be provided.  
Where an electronic real estate register is used, dedicated storage facilities shall be provided and information network security protection measures shall be taken.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　不动产登记簿由不动产登记机构永久保存。不动产登记簿损毁、灭失的，不动产登记机构应当依据原有登记资料予以重建。  
行政区域变更或者不动产登记机构职能调整的，应当及时将不动产登记簿移交相应的不动产登记机构。

**Article 13** The real estate register shall be permanently preserved by the real estate registration authority. If the real estate register is damaged or lost, the real estate registration authority shall reconstruct it based on the original registration records.  
Where administrative divisions change or the functions of the real estate registration authority are adjusted, the real estate register shall be timely transferred to the corresponding real estate registration authority.

### 第三章　登记程序

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　因买卖、设定抵押权等申请不动产登记的，应当由当事人双方共同申请。  
属于下列情形之一的，可以由当事人单方申请：  
（一）尚未登记的不动产首次申请登记的；  
（二）继承、接受遗赠取得不动产权利的；  
（三）人民法院、仲裁委员会生效的法律文书或者人民政府生效的决定等设立、变更、转让、消灭不动产权利的；  
（四）权利人姓名、名称或者自然状况发生变化，申请变更登记的；  
（五）不动产灭失或者权利人放弃不动产权利，申请注销登记的；  
（六）申请更正登记或者异议登记的；  
（七）法律、行政法规规定可以由当事人单方申请的其他情形。

**Article 14** Where an application for real estate registration is made due to a sale, creation of a mortgage, etc., it shall be jointly filed by both parties.  
In any of the following circumstances, it may be filed by a party unilaterally:  
(1) initial registration of real estate that has not yet been registered;  
(2) acquisition of real estate rights by succession or acceptance of a bequest;  
(3) establishment, alteration, transfer, or extinguishment of real estate rights based on effective legal documents of the People's Court or arbitration commission, or effective decisions of the people's government;  
(4) change of the right holder's name, title, or natural status, applying for registration of change;  
(5) real estate has been destroyed or the right holder has abandoned the real estate rights, applying for cancellation of registration;  
(6) applying for correction registration or caveat registration;  
(7) other circumstances that may be filed unilaterally by a party as provided by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　当事人或者其代理人应当向不动产登记机构申请不动产登记。  
不动产登记机构将申请登记事项记载于不动产登记簿前，申请人可以撤回登记申请。

**Article 15** A party or its agent shall apply to the real estate registration authority for real estate registration.  
Before the real estate registration authority records the matters applied for registration in the real estate register, the applicant may withdraw the registration application.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　申请人应当提交下列材料，并对申请材料的真实性负责：  
（一）登记申请书；  
（二）申请人、代理人身份证明材料、授权委托书；  
（三）相关的不动产权属来源证明材料、登记原因证明文件、不动产权属证书；  
（四）不动产界址、空间界限、面积等材料；  
（五）与他人利害关系的说明材料；  
（六）法律、行政法规以及本条例实施细则规定的其他材料。  
不动产登记机构应当在办公场所和门户网站公开申请登记所需材料目录和示范文本等信息。

**Article 16** An applicant shall submit the following materials and be responsible for the authenticity of the application materials:  
(1) a registration application form;  
(2) identity certification materials of the applicant and agent, and a power of attorney;  
(3) relevant certification materials of the source of real estate title, documents proving the cause of registration, and the certificate of real estate title;  
(4) materials on the boundaries, spatial limits, area, etc. of the real estate;  
(5) materials explaining the interest relationship with others;  
(6) other materials as provided by laws, administrative regulations, and the Implementation Rules of these Regulations.  
The real estate registration authority shall make public the catalog of materials required for registration and model documents at its office premises and on its portal website.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　不动产登记机构收到不动产登记申请材料，应当分别按照下列情况办理：  
（一）属于登记职责范围，申请材料齐全、符合法定形式，或者申请人按照要求提交全部补正申请材料的，应当受理并书面告知申请人；  
（二）申请材料存在可以当场更正的错误的，应当告知申请人当场更正，申请人当场更正后，应当受理并书面告知申请人；  
（三）申请材料不齐全或者不符合法定形式的，应当当场书面告知申请人不予受理并一次性告知需要补正的全部内容；  
（四）申请登记的不动产不属于本机构登记范围的，应当当场书面告知申请人不予受理并告知申请人向有登记权的机构申请。  
不动产登记机构未当场书面告知申请人不予受理的，视为受理。

**Article 17** Upon receiving the application materials for real estate registration, the real estate registration authority shall handle them according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the matter falls within the scope of registration duties, the application materials are complete and conform to the statutory form, or the applicant has submitted all supplementary materials as required, the authority shall accept the application and notify the applicant in writing;  
(2) If there are errors in the application materials that can be corrected on-site, the authority shall notify the applicant to make corrections on-site. After the applicant makes corrections on-site, the authority shall accept the application and notify the applicant in writing;  
(3) If the application materials are incomplete or do not conform to the statutory form, the authority shall notify the applicant in writing on the spot of non-acceptance and inform the applicant of all the contents to be supplemented in one go;  
(4) If the real estate applied for registration does not fall within the registration scope of this authority, the authority shall notify the applicant in writing on the spot of non-acceptance and inform the applicant to apply to the authority with registration authority.  
If the real estate registration authority fails to notify the applicant in writing on the spot of non-acceptance, it shall be deemed as acceptance.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　不动产登记机构受理不动产登记申请的，应当按照下列要求进行查验：  
（一）不动产界址、空间界限、面积等材料与申请登记的不动产状况是否一致；  
（二）有关证明材料、文件与申请登记的内容是否一致；  
（三）登记申请是否违反法律、行政法规规定。

**Article 18** After accepting an application for real estate registration, the real estate registration authority shall examine the following requirements:  
(1) Whether the materials on the boundaries, spatial limits, area, etc. of the real estate are consistent with the status of the real estate applied for registration;  
(2) Whether the relevant certification materials and documents are consistent with the content applied for registration;  
(3) Whether the registration application violates the provisions of laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　属于下列情形之一的，不动产登记机构可以对申请登记的不动产进行实地查看：  
（一）房屋等建筑物、构筑物所有权首次登记；  
（二）在建建筑物抵押权登记；  
（三）因不动产灭失导致的注销登记；  
（四）不动产登记机构认为需要实地查看的其他情形。  
对可能存在权属争议，或者可能涉及他人利害关系的登记申请，不动产登记机构可以向申请人、利害关系人或者有关单位进行调查。  
不动产登记机构进行实地查看或者调查时，申请人、被调查人应当予以配合。

**Article 19** In any of the following circumstances, the real estate registration authority may conduct an on-site inspection of the real estate applied for registration:  
(1) initial registration of ownership of buildings such as houses and structures;  
(2) registration of mortgage on buildings under construction;  
(3) cancellation of registration due to destruction of real estate;  
(4) other circumstances where the real estate registration authority deems it necessary to conduct an on-site inspection.  
For a registration application that may involve a dispute over ownership or may involve the interests of others, the real estate registration authority may investigate the applicant, interested parties, or relevant units.  
When the real estate registration authority conducts an on-site inspection or investigation, the applicant and the person being investigated shall cooperate.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　不动产登记机构应当自受理登记申请之日起30个工作日内办结不动产登记手续，法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 20** The real estate registration authority shall complete the real estate registration formalities within 30 working days from the date of acceptance of the registration application, unless otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　登记事项自记载于不动产登记簿时完成登记。  
不动产登记机构完成登记，应当依法向申请人核发不动产权属证书或者登记证明。

**Article 21** The registration of a registration item shall be completed when it is recorded in the real estate register.  
After completing registration, the real estate registration authority shall issue a certificate of title or registration certificate to the applicant according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　登记申请有下列情形之一的，不动产登记机构应当不予登记，并书面告知申请人：  
（一）违反法律、行政法规规定的；  
（二）存在尚未解决的权属争议的；  
（三）申请登记的不动产权利超过规定期限的；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定不予登记的其他情形。

**Article 22** Where a registration application falls under any of the following circumstances, the real estate registration authority shall not register and shall notify the applicant in writing:  
(1) it violates laws or administrative regulations;  
(2) there is an unresolved dispute over ownership;  
(3) the real estate right applied for registration exceeds the prescribed term;  
(4) other circumstances under which registration shall not be made as provided by laws or administrative regulations.

### 第四章　登记信息共享与保护

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院自然资源主管部门应当会同有关部门建立统一的不动产登记信息管理基础平台。  
各级不动产登记机构登记的信息应当纳入统一的不动产登记信息管理基础平台，确保国家、省、市、县四级登记信息的实时共享。

**Article 23** The natural resources department of the State Council shall, together with relevant departments, establish a unified basic platform for real estate registration information management.  
Information registered by real estate registration authorities at all levels shall be incorporated into the unified basic platform for real estate registration information management to ensure real-time sharing of registration information at the national, provincial, municipal, and county levels.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　不动产登记有关信息与住房城乡建设、农业农村、林业草原等部门审批信息、交易信息等应当实时互通共享。  
不动产登记机构能够通过实时互通共享取得的信息，不得要求不动产登记申请人重复提交。

**Article 24** Information related to real estate registration and the approval information and transaction information of the departments of housing and urban-rural construction, agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland, etc., shall be mutually shared in real time.  
For information that the real estate registration authority can obtain through real-time mutual sharing, it shall not require the real estate registration applicant to submit it repeatedly.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　自然资源、公安、民政、财政、税务、市场监管、金融、审计、统计等部门应当加强不动产登记有关信息互通共享。

**Article 25** The departments of natural resources, public security, civil affairs, finance, taxation, market regulation, banking, audit, and statistics shall strengthen the mutual sharing of information related to real estate registration.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　不动产登记机构、不动产登记信息共享单位及其工作人员应当对不动产登记信息保密；涉及国家秘密的不动产登记信息，应当依法采取必要的安全保密措施。

**Article 26** The real estate registration authority, the units sharing real estate registration information, and their staff members shall keep real estate registration information confidential; with respect to real estate registration information involving state secrets, necessary security and confidentiality measures shall be taken according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　权利人、利害关系人可以依法查询、复制不动产登记资料，不动产登记机构应当提供。  
有关国家机关可以依照法律、行政法规的规定查询、复制与调查处理事项有关的不动产登记资料。

**Article 27** The right holder and interested party may, according to law, consult and copy real estate registration materials, and the real estate registration authority shall provide them.  
Relevant state organs may, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, consult and copy real estate registration materials related to the matters under investigation and handling.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　查询不动产登记资料的单位、个人应当向不动产登记机构说明查询目的，不得将查询获得的不动产登记资料用于其他目的；未经权利人同意，不得泄露查询获得的不动产登记资料。

**Article 28** The units and individuals consulting real estate registration materials shall explain the purpose of consultation to the real estate registration authority, and shall not use the real estate registration materials obtained through consultation for other purposes; without the consent of the right holder, they shall not disclose the real estate registration materials obtained through consultation.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　不动产登记机构登记错误给他人造成损害，或者当事人提供虚假材料申请登记给他人造成损害的，依照《中华人民共和国民法典》的规定承担赔偿责任。

**Article 29** Where the real estate registration authority causes damage to others due to registration errors, or a party causes damage to others by applying for registration with false materials, the compensation liability shall be borne in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　不动产登记机构工作人员进行虚假登记，损毁、伪造不动产登记簿，擅自修改登记事项，或者有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守行为的，依法给予处分；给他人造成损害的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 30** Where a staff member of the real estate registration authority makes false registration, damages or forges the real estate register, modifies registration items without authorization, or commits other acts of abuse of power or dereliction of duty, the staff member shall be given sanctions according to law; if causing damage to others, shall bear compensation liability according to law; if constituting a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　伪造、变造不动产权属证书、不动产登记证明，或者买卖、使用伪造、变造的不动产权属证书、不动产登记证明的，由不动产登记机构或者公安机关依法予以收缴；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；给他人造成损害的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 31** Whoever forges or alters a real estate ownership certificate or a real estate registration certificate, or buys, sells, or uses a forged or altered real estate ownership certificate or real estate registration certificate, such certificate shall be confiscated according to law by the real estate registration authority or the public security organ; if there are illegal gains, such gains shall be confiscated; if damage is caused to another person, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　不动产登记机构、不动产登记信息共享单位及其工作人员，查询不动产登记资料的单位或者个人违反国家规定，泄露不动产登记资料、登记信息，或者利用不动产登记资料、登记信息进行不正当活动，给他人造成损害的，依法承担赔偿责任；对有关责任人员依法给予处分；有关责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 32** Where a real estate registration authority, a unit sharing real estate registration information, or any of their staff members, or a unit or individual that queries real estate registration materials, violates the provisions of the state by divulging real estate registration materials or registration information, or by using such materials or information to engage in improper activities, thereby causing damage to another person, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; the relevant responsible person shall be given a sanction according to law; if the responsible person constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　本条例施行前依法颁发的各类不动产权属证书和制作的不动产登记簿继续有效。  
不动产统一登记过渡期内，农村土地承包经营权的登记按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 33** All types of real estate ownership certificates issued according to law and real estate registers made before the coming into force of these Regulations shall continue to be valid.  
During the transitional period of unified real estate registration, the registration of the right to the contracted management of rural land shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本条例实施细则由国务院自然资源主管部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 34** The detailed rules for the implementation of these Regulations shall be formulated by the competent department of natural resources of the State Council in conjunction with the relevant departments.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本条例自2015年3月1日起施行。本条例施行前公布的行政法规有关不动产登记的规定与本条例规定不一致的，以本条例规定为准。

**Article 35** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 2015. Where any provision of the administrative regulations promulgated before the coming into force of these Regulations concerning real estate registration is inconsistent with these Regulations, the provisions of these Regulations shall prevail.
