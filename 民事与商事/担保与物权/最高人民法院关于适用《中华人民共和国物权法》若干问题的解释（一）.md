# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国物权法》若干问题的解释（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2016〕5号  
**公布日期**：2015-12-10  
**生效日期**：2016-03-01

> 最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国物权法〉若干问题的解释（一）》已于2015年12月10日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1670次会议通过，现予公布，自2016年3月1日起施行。 2016年2月22日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国物权法》若干问题的解释（一）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   因不动产物权的归属，以及作为不动产物权登记基础的买卖、赠与、抵押等产生争议，当事人提起民事诉讼的，应当依法受理。当事人已经在行政诉讼中申请一并解决上述民事争议，且人民法院一并审理的除外。

**Article 1** Disputes arising from the ownership of real rights in immovable property, and from the basis for registration of real rights in immovable property such as sale, gift, mortgage, etc., where a party initiates a civil action, shall be accepted according to law, except where the party has already applied in an administrative action for simultaneous resolution of the aforesaid civil dispute and the People's Court has tried them together.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   当事人有证据证明不动产登记簿的记载与真实权利状态不符、其为该不动产物权的真实权利人，请求确认其享有物权的，应予支持。

**Article 2** Where a party has evidence to prove that the records in the Register of Immovable Property do not match the actual state of rights and that it is the true owner of the real right in the immovable property, and requests confirmation of its real right, the request shall be supported.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   异议登记因物权法第十九条第二款规定的事由失效后，当事人提起民事诉讼，请求确认物权归属的，应当依法受理。异议登记失效不影响人民法院对案件的实体审理。

**Article 3** After an objection registration ceases to have effect due to the circumstances specified in Article 19, paragraph 2 of the Property Law, where a party initiates a civil action requesting confirmation of the ownership of the real right, the action shall be accepted according to law. The cessation of effect of the objection registration shall not affect the People's Court's substantive adjudication of the case.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条    未经预告登记的权利人同意，转移不动产所有权，或者设定建设用地使用权、地役权、抵押权等其他物权的，应当依照物权法第二十条第一款的规定，认定其不发生物权效力。

**Article 4** Without the consent of the holder of a cautionary registration, any transfer of ownership of immovable property or creation of other real rights such as construction land use rights, easements, mortgages, etc., shall be determined not to have the effect of a real right in accordance with Article 20, paragraph 1 of the Property Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   买卖不动产物权的协议被认定无效、被撤销、被解除，或者预告登记的权利人放弃债权的，应当认定为物权法第二十条第二款所称的“债权消灭”。

**Article 5** Where an agreement for the sale of real rights in immovable property is determined to be void, rescinded, or discharged, or where the holder of a cautionary registration abandons a creditor's right, it shall be determined as 'extinction of creditor's right' as referred to in Article 20, paragraph 2 of the Property Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   转让人转移船舶、航空器和机动车等所有权，受让人已经支付对价并取得占有，虽未经登记，但转让人的债权人主张其为物权法第二十四条所称的“善意第三人”的，不予支持，法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 6** Where a transferor transfers ownership of ships, aircraft, motor vehicles, etc., and the transferee has paid the consideration and taken possession, although not registered, if the transferor's creditor claims that it is a 'bona fide third party' as referred to in Article 24 of the Property Law, the claim shall not be supported, unless the law provides otherwise.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   人民法院、仲裁委员会在分割共有不动产或者动产等案件中作出并依法生效的改变原有物权关系的判决书、裁决书、调解书，以及人民法院在执行程序中作出的拍卖成交裁定书、以物抵债裁定书，应当认定为物权法第二十八条所称导致物权设立、变更、转让或者消灭的人民法院、仲裁委员会的法律文书。

**Article 7** Judgments, awards, and mediation statements that have taken effect according to law and change the original real right relations, made by the People's Court or arbitration commission in cases such as partition of co-owned immovable or movable property, as well as auction sale rulings and rulings on satisfaction of debt with property made by the People's Court during enforcement proceedings, shall be determined as legal documents of the People's Court or arbitration commission that cause the creation, alteration, transfer, or extinguishment of real rights as referred to in Article 28 of the Property Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条   依照物权法第二十八条至第三十条规定享有物权，但尚未完成动产交付或者不动产登记的物权人，根据物权法第三十四条至第三十七条的规定，请求保护其物权的，应予支持。

**Article 8** Where a person entitled to a real right in accordance with Articles 28 to 30 of the Property Law but has not yet completed delivery of the movable property or registration of the immovable property requests protection of its real right under Articles 34 to 37 of the Property Law, the request shall be supported.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条   共有份额的权利主体因继承、遗赠等原因发生变化时，其他按份共有人主张优先购买的，不予支持，但按份共有人之间另有约定的除外。

**Article 9** Where the holder of a share in co-ownership changes due to succession, testamentary gift, or other reasons, if other co-tenants in common claim a right of first refusal, the claim shall not be supported, unless otherwise agreed among the co-tenants in common.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条   物权法第一百零一条所称的“同等条件”，应当综合共有份额的转让价格、价款履行方式及期限等因素确定。

**Article 10** The 'equivalent conditions' referred to in Article 101 of the Property Law shall be determined by comprehensively considering factors such as the transfer price of the share in co-ownership, the method and time limit for performance of the price, etc.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条   优先购买权的行使期间，按份共有人之间有约定的，按照约定处理；没有约定或者约定不明的，按照下列情形确定：  
（一）转让人向其他按份共有人发出的包含同等条件内容的通知中载明行使期间的，以该期间为准；  
（二）通知中未载明行使期间，或者载明的期间短于通知送达之日起十五日的，为十五日；  
（三）转让人未通知的，为其他按份共有人知道或者应当知道最终确定的同等条件之日起十五日；  
（四）转让人未通知，且无法确定其他按份共有人知道或者应当知道最终确定的同等条件的，为共有份额权属转移之日起六个月。

**Article 11** The exercise period for the right of first refusal shall be handled according to the agreement if there is an agreement among the co-tenants in common; if there is no agreement or the agreement is unclear, it shall be determined according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the notice sent by the transferor to other co-tenants in common containing the equivalent conditions specifies an exercise period, that period shall prevail;  
(2) If the notice does not specify an exercise period, or the specified period is shorter than 15 days from the date of service of the notice, the period shall be 15 days;  
(3) If the transferor has not given notice, the period shall be 15 days from the date when the other co-tenants in common know or should know the finally determined equivalent conditions;  
(4) If the transferor has not given notice and it is impossible to determine the date when the other co-tenants in common know or should know the finally determined equivalent conditions, the period shall be six months from the date of transfer of the ownership of the co-owned share.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条   按份共有人向共有人之外的人转让其份额，其他按份共有人根据法律、司法解释规定，请求按照同等条件购买该共有份额的，应予支持。  
其他按份共有人的请求具有下列情形之一的，不予支持：  
（一）未在[本解释第十一条](#art-11)规定的期间内主张优先购买，或者虽主张优先购买，但提出减少转让价款、增加转让人负担等实质性变更要求；  
（二）以其优先购买权受到侵害为由，仅请求撤销共有份额转让合同或者认定该合同无效。

**Article 12** Where a co-tenant in common transfers its share to a person other than the co-owners, and other co-tenants in common request to purchase the co-owned share on equivalent conditions in accordance with laws and judicial interpretations, the request shall be supported.  
Where a request of other co-tenants in common falls under any of the following circumstances, the request shall not be supported:  
(1) The right of first refusal is not claimed within the period specified in Article 11 of this Interpretation, or although the right of first refusal is claimed, substantive changes such as reducing the transfer price or increasing the transferor's burden are proposed;  
(2) Only a request is made to rescind the contract for the transfer of the co-owned share or to determine such contract void on the ground that the right of first refusal has been infringed.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条   按份共有人之间转让共有份额，其他按份共有人主张根据物权法第一百零一条规定优先购买的，不予支持，但按份共有人之间另有约定的除外。

**Article 13** Where co-tenants in common transfer shares among themselves, if other co-tenants in common claim the right of first refusal in accordance with Article 101 of the Property Law, the claim shall not be supported, unless otherwise agreed among the co-tenants in common.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条   两个以上按份共有人主张优先购买且协商不成时，请求按照转让时各自份额比例行使优先购买权的，应予支持。

**Article 14** Where two or more co-tenants in common claim the right of first refusal and fail to reach an agreement, a request to exercise the right of first refusal in proportion to their respective shares at the time of transfer shall be supported.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条   受让人受让不动产或者动产时，不知道转让人无处分权，且无重大过失的，应当认定受让人为善意。  
真实权利人主张受让人不构成善意的，应当承担举证证明责任。

**Article 15** Where a transferee, at the time of acquiring immovable or movable property, does not know that the transferor has no power of disposition and is not grossly negligent, the transferee shall be determined to be a bona fide party.  
Where a true right holder claims that the transferee is not bona fide, it shall bear the burden of proof.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条   具有下列情形之一的，应当认定不动产受让人知道转让人无处分权：  
（一）登记簿上存在有效的异议登记；  
（二）预告登记有效期内，未经预告登记的权利人同意；  
（三）登记簿上已经记载司法机关或者行政机关依法裁定、决定查封或者以其他形式限制不动产权利的有关事项；  
（四）受让人知道登记簿上记载的权利主体错误；  
（五）受让人知道他人已经依法享有不动产物权。  
真实权利人有证据证明不动产受让人应当知道转让人无处分权的，应当认定受让人具有重大过失。

**Article 16** Where any of the following circumstances exists, the transferee of immovable property shall be determined to know that the transferor has no power of disposition:  
(1) There is a valid objection registration in the register;  
(2) During the validity period of a cautionary registration, without the consent of the holder of the cautionary registration;  
(3) Matters concerning the restriction of rights in immovable property by judicial or administrative organs according to law by ruling, decision to seal up, or other forms have been recorded in the register;  
(4) The transferee knows that the holder of rights recorded in the register is incorrect;  
(5) The transferee knows that another person has lawfully acquired a real right in the immovable property.  
Where the true right holder has evidence to prove that the transferee of immovable property should have known that the transferor has no power of disposition, the transferee shall be determined to be grossly negligent.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条   受让人受让动产时，交易的对象、场所或者时机等不符合交易习惯的，应当认定受让人具有重大过失。

**Article 17** Where, at the time a transferee acquires movable property, the subject, place, or timing of the transaction does not conform to trade practices, the transferee shall be determined to be grossly negligent.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条   物权法第一百零六条第一款第一项所称的“受让人受让该不动产或者动产时”，是指依法完成不动产物权转移登记或者动产交付之时。  
当事人以物权法第二十五条规定的方式交付动产的，转让动产法律行为生效时为动产交付之时；当事人以物权法第二十六条规定的方式交付动产的，转让人与受让人之间有关转让返还原物请求权的协议生效时为动产交付之时。  
法律对不动产、动产物权的设立另有规定的，应当按照法律规定的时间认定权利人是否为善意。

**Article 18** The term 'at the time the transferee acquires the immovable or movable property' as referred to in Article 106, paragraph 1, item 1 of the Property Law means the time when the transfer registration of the real right in immovable property is completed or the time when movable property is delivered according to law.  
Where a party delivers movable property in the manner prescribed in Article 25 of the Property Law, the time when the legal act of transferring the movable property takes effect shall be the time of delivery of the movable property; where a party delivers movable property in the manner prescribed in Article 26 of the Property Law, the time when the agreement between the transferor and the transferee concerning the transfer of the right to claim the return of the original property takes effect shall be the time of delivery of the movable property.  
Where the law provides otherwise for the creation of real rights in immovable or movable property, whether the right holder is bona fide shall be determined according to the time prescribed by law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条   物权法第一百零六条第一款第二项所称“合理的价格”，应当根据转让标的物的性质、数量以及付款方式等具体情况，参考转让时交易地市场价格以及交易习惯等因素综合认定。

**Article 19** The 'reasonable price' referred to in Article 106, paragraph 1, item 2 of the Property Law shall be determined comprehensively based on the specific circumstances such as the nature and quantity of the subject matter transferred and the method of payment, with reference to factors such as the market price at the place of transaction at the time of transfer and trade practices.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条   转让人将物权法第二十四条规定的船舶、航空器和机动车等交付给受让人的，应当认定符合物权法第一百零六条第一款第三项规定的善意取得的条件。

**Article 20** Where a transferor delivers ships, aircraft, motor vehicles, etc., as prescribed in Article 24 of the Property Law to the transferee, it shall be determined that the conditions for acquisition in good faith as prescribed in Article 106, paragraph 1, item 3 of the Property Law are satisfied.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条   具有下列情形之一，受让人主张根据物权法第一百零六条规定取得所有权的，不予支持：  
（一）转让合同因违反合同法第五十二条规定被认定无效；  
（二）转让合同因受让人存在欺诈、胁迫或者乘人之危等法定事由被撤销。

**Article 21** Where any of the following circumstances exists, if the transferee claims to acquire ownership in accordance with Article 106 of the Property Law, the claim shall not be supported:  
(1) The transfer contract has been determined to be void due to violation of Article 52 of the Contract Law;  
(2) The transfer contract has been rescinded due to legal causes such as fraud, duress, or taking advantage of the other party's distress.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条   本解释自2016年3月1日起施行。  
本解释施行后人民法院新受理的一审案件，适用本解释。  
本解释施行前人民法院已经受理、施行后尚未审结的一审、二审案件，以及本解释施行前已经终审、施行后当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本解释。

**Article 22** This Interpretation shall come into force on March 1, 2016.  
Cases of first instance newly accepted by the People's Court after this Interpretation comes into force shall be governed by this Interpretation.  
Cases of first instance or second instance that have been accepted by the People's Court before the coming into force of this Interpretation but have not been finally adjudicated after its coming into force, as well as cases that have been finally adjudicated before the coming into force of this Interpretation and for which a party applies for retrial or which are decided to be retried under the trial supervision procedure after its coming into force, shall not be governed by this Interpretation.
