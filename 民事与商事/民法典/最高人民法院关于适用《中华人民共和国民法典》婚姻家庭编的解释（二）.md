# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》婚姻家庭编的解释（二）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法典  
**发文字号**：法释〔2025〕1号  
**公布日期**：2025-01-15  
**生效日期**：2025-02-01

> （2024年11月25日最高人民法院审判委员会第1933次会议通过，自2025年2月1日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　当事人依据民法典第一千零五十一条第一项规定请求确认重婚的婚姻无效，提起诉讼时合法婚姻当事人已经离婚或者配偶已经死亡，被告以此为由抗辩后一婚姻自以上情形发生时转为有效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 1** . Where a party requests confirmation of the invalidity of a marriage due to bigamy pursuant to Item 1 of Article 1051 of the Civil Code, and the party to the legal marriage has already divorced or the spouse has died by the time the action is filed, and the respondent defends on the ground that the subsequent marriage has become valid from the time of the occurrence of the above circumstances, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　夫妻登记离婚后，一方以双方意思表示虚假为由请求确认离婚无效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 2** . After a husband and wife have registered the divorce, where one party requests confirmation of the invalidity of the divorce on the ground of false expression of intention by both parties, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　夫妻一方的债权人有证据证明离婚协议中财产分割条款影响其债权实现，请求参照适用民法典第五百三十八条或者第五百三十九条规定撤销相关条款的，人民法院应当综合考虑夫妻共同财产整体分割及履行情况、子女抚养费负担、离婚过错等因素，依法予以支持。

**Article 3** . Where a creditor of one spouse has evidence that the property division clause in the divorce agreement affects the realization of the creditor's claim, and requests, by reference to Article 538 or Article 539 of the Civil Code, the revocation of the relevant clause, the People's Court shall, taking into comprehensive consideration such factors as the overall division and performance of the community property of the spouses, the burden of child support, and the fault in the divorce, support the request in accordance with law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　双方均无配偶的同居关系析产纠纷案件中，对同居期间所得的财产，有约定的，按照约定处理；没有约定且协商不成的，人民法院按照以下情形分别处理：  
（一）各自所得的工资、奖金、劳务报酬、知识产权收益，各自继承或者受赠的财产以及单独生产、经营、投资的收益等，归各自所有；  
（二）共同出资购置的财产或者共同生产、经营、投资的收益以及其他无法区分的财产，以各自出资比例为基础，综合考虑共同生活情况、有无共同子女、对财产的贡献大小等因素进行分割。

**Article 4** . In a case involving the division of property from a cohabitation relationship where neither party has a spouse, the property acquired during the cohabitation period shall, if there is an agreement, be dealt with according to the agreement; in the absence of an agreement and where negotiation fails, the People's Court shall handle the matter according to the following circumstances:  
(1) The wages, bonuses, labor remuneration, income from intellectual property earned by each party individually, property inherited or donated to each party individually, and income from independent production, business, or investment shall belong to each party respectively;  
(2) Property jointly purchased or the proceeds from joint production, business, or investment, and other property that cannot be distinguished, shall be divided on the basis of each party's respective contribution ratio, taking into comprehensive consideration factors such as the circumstances of cohabitation, whether there are children of the relationship, and each party's contribution to the property.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　婚前或者婚姻关系存续期间，当事人约定将一方所有的房屋转移登记至另一方或者双方名下，离婚诉讼时房屋所有权尚未转移登记，双方对房屋归属或者分割有争议且协商不成的，人民法院可以根据当事人诉讼请求，结合给予目的，综合考虑婚姻关系存续时间、共同生活及孕育共同子女情况、离婚过错、对家庭的贡献大小以及离婚时房屋市场价格等因素，判决房屋归其中一方所有，并确定是否由获得房屋一方对另一方予以补偿以及补偿的具体数额。  
婚前或者婚姻关系存续期间，一方将其所有的房屋转移登记至另一方或者双方名下，离婚诉讼中，双方对房屋归属或者分割有争议且协商不成的，如果婚姻关系存续时间较短且给予方无重大过错，人民法院可以根据当事人诉讼请求，判决该房屋归给予方所有，并结合给予目的，综合考虑共同生活及孕育共同子女情况、离婚过错、对家庭的贡献大小以及离婚时房屋市场价格等因素，确定是否由获得房屋一方对另一方予以补偿以及补偿的具体数额。  
给予方有证据证明另一方存在欺诈、胁迫、严重侵害给予方或者其近亲属合法权益、对给予方有扶养义务而不履行等情形，请求撤销前两款规定的民事法律行为的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 5** . Before marriage or during the existence of a marital relationship, where the parties agree to transfer the registration of ownership of a house owned by one party to the other party or to both parties, and the ownership of the house has not yet been transferred at the time of the divorce action, and the parties dispute the ownership or division of the house and fail to reach an agreement through negotiation, the People's Court may, based on the party's claim and considering the purpose of the gift, the duration of the marital relationship, the circumstances of cohabitation and having children, the fault in the divorce, the contribution to the family, and the market price of the house at the time of divorce, render a judgment that the house belongs to one of the parties and determine whether the party who obtains the house shall compensate the other party and the specific amount of compensation.  
Before marriage or during the existence of a marital relationship, where one party transfers the registration of ownership of a house owned by that party to the other party or to both parties, and in the divorce action, the parties dispute the ownership or division of the house and fail to reach an agreement through negotiation, if the duration of the marital relationship is relatively short and the gifting party has no serious fault, the People's Court may, based on the party's claim, render a judgment that the house belongs to the gifting party, and, considering the purpose of the gift, the circumstances of cohabitation and having children, the fault in the divorce, the contribution to the family, and the market price of the house at the time of divorce, determine whether the party who obtains the house shall compensate the other party and the specific amount of compensation.  
If the gifting party has evidence that the other party fraudulently coerced, seriously infringed upon the lawful rights and interests of the gifting party or its close relatives, or failed to perform a maintenance obligation towards the gifting party, and requests the revocation of the civil juristic acts provided in the preceding two paragraphs, the People's Court shall support such request in accordance with law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　夫妻一方未经另一方同意，在网络直播平台用夫妻共同财产打赏，数额明显超出其家庭一般消费水平，严重损害夫妻共同财产利益的，可以认定为民法典第一千零六十六条和第一千零九十二条规定的“挥霍”。另一方请求在婚姻关系存续期间分割夫妻共同财产，或者在离婚分割夫妻共同财产时请求对打赏一方少分或者不分的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 6** . Where one spouse, without the consent of the other spouse, uses community property to make tips on a live-streaming platform, and the amount clearly exceeds the general household consumption level, thereby seriously damaging the interests of the community property, this may be determined as "wasteful spending" within the meaning of Articles 1066 and 1092 of the Civil Code. If the other spouse requests the division of the community property during the existence of the marital relationship, or requests that the tipping party receive a smaller share or no share when dividing the community property upon divorce, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　夫妻一方为重婚、与他人同居以及其他违反夫妻忠实义务等目的，将夫妻共同财产赠与他人或者以明显不合理的价格处分夫妻共同财产，另一方主张该民事法律行为违背公序良俗无效的，人民法院应予支持并依照民法典第一百五十七条规定处理。  
夫妻一方存在前款规定情形，另一方以该方存在转移、变卖夫妻共同财产行为，严重损害夫妻共同财产利益为由，依据民法典第一千零六十六条规定请求在婚姻关系存续期间分割夫妻共同财产，或者依据民法典第一千零九十二条规定请求在离婚分割夫妻共同财产时对该方少分或者不分的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 7** . Where one spouse, for the purposes of bigamy, cohabitation with another person, or other violations of the duty of fidelity between spouses, gifts community property to another person or disposes of community property at a clearly unreasonable price, and the other spouse claims that such civil juristic act is void for violating public order and good customs, the People's Court shall support such claim and handle the matter in accordance with Article 157 of the Civil Code.  
If one spouse commits an act as described in the preceding paragraph, and the other spouse, on the ground that such act of transferring or selling community property seriously damages the interests of the community property, requests the division of the community property during the existence of the marital relationship in accordance with Article 1066 of the Civil Code, or requests that the party receive a smaller share or no share when dividing the community property upon divorce in accordance with Article 1092 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　婚姻关系存续期间，夫妻购置房屋由一方父母全额出资，如果赠与合同明确约定只赠与自己子女一方的，按照约定处理；没有约定或者约定不明确的，离婚分割夫妻共同财产时，人民法院可以判决该房屋归出资人子女一方所有，并综合考虑共同生活及孕育共同子女情况、离婚过错、对家庭的贡献大小以及离婚时房屋市场价格等因素，确定是否由获得房屋一方对另一方予以补偿以及补偿的具体数额。  
婚姻关系存续期间，夫妻购置房屋由一方父母部分出资或者双方父母出资，如果赠与合同明确约定相应出资只赠与自己子女一方的，按照约定处理；没有约定或者约定不明确的，离婚分割夫妻共同财产时，人民法院可以根据当事人诉讼请求，以出资来源及比例为基础，综合考虑共同生活及孕育共同子女情况、离婚过错、对家庭的贡献大小以及离婚时房屋市场价格等因素，判决房屋归其中一方所有，并由获得房屋一方对另一方予以合理补偿。

**Article 8** . During the existence of a marital relationship, if a house is purchased by the husband and wife and fully funded by one parent, and the gift contract clearly stipulates that the gift is only to the parent's own child, it shall be dealt with according to the agreement; if there is no agreement or the agreement is unclear, when dividing the community property upon divorce, the People's Court may render a judgment that the house belongs to the child of the contributing parent, and, taking into comprehensive consideration such factors as the circumstances of cohabitation and having children, the fault in the divorce, the contribution to the family, and the market price of the house at the time of divorce, determine whether the party who obtains the house shall compensate the other party and the specific amount of compensation.  
During the existence of a marital relationship, if a house is purchased by the husband and wife and partially funded by one parent or funded by both parents, and the gift contract clearly stipulates that the corresponding contribution is a gift only to the parents' own child, it shall be dealt with according to the agreement; if there is no agreement or the agreement is unclear, when dividing the community property upon divorce, the People's Court may, based on the parties' claims, using the source and proportion of the contributions as a basis, and taking into comprehensive consideration such factors as the circumstances of cohabitation and having children, the fault in the divorce, the contribution to the family, and the market price of the house at the time of divorce, render a judgment that the house belongs to one of the parties, and the party who obtains the house shall make reasonable compensation to the other party.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　夫妻一方转让用夫妻共同财产出资但登记在自己名下的有限责任公司股权，另一方以未经其同意侵害夫妻共同财产利益为由请求确认股权转让合同无效的，人民法院不予支持，但有证据证明转让人与受让人恶意串通损害另一方合法权益的除外。

**Article 9** . Where one spouse transfers equity in a limited liability company that was funded with community property but registered in that spouse's own name, and the other spouse requests confirmation of the invalidity of the equity transfer contract on the ground that the transfer damaged the interests of the community property without consent, the People's Court shall not support such request, except where there is evidence that the transferor and the transferee maliciously colluded to damage the lawful rights and interests of the other spouse.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　夫妻以共同财产投资有限责任公司，并均登记为股东，双方对相应股权的归属没有约定或者约定不明确，离婚时，一方请求按照股东名册或者公司章程记载的各自出资额确定股权分割比例的，人民法院不予支持；对当事人分割夫妻共同财产的请求，人民法院依照民法典第一千零八十七条规定处理。

**Article 10** . Where the husband and wife invest community property in a limited liability company and both are registered as shareholders, and there is no agreement or the agreement is unclear on the ownership of the corresponding equity, upon divorce, if one party requests that the proportion of equity division be determined in accordance with the respective capital contributions recorded in the shareholder register or the company's articles of association, the People's Court shall not support such request; the People's Court shall handle the party's claim for division of community property in accordance with Article 1087 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　夫妻一方以另一方可继承的财产为夫妻共同财产、放弃继承侵害夫妻共同财产利益为由主张另一方放弃继承无效的，人民法院不予支持，但有证据证明放弃继承导致放弃一方不能履行法定扶养义务的除外。

**Article 11** . Where one spouse claims that the waiver of inheritance by the other spouse is invalid on the ground that the inheritable property of the other spouse is community property and the waiver damages the interests of the community property, the People's Court shall not support such claim, except where there is evidence that the waiver of inheritance makes the waiving party unable to perform a statutory maintenance obligation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　父母一方或者其近亲属等抢夺、藏匿未成年子女，另一方向人民法院申请人身安全保护令或者参照适用民法典第九百九十七条规定申请人格权侵害禁令的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
抢夺、藏匿未成年子女一方以另一方存在赌博、吸毒、家庭暴力等严重侵害未成年子女合法权益情形，主张其抢夺、藏匿行为有合理事由的，人民法院应当告知其依法通过撤销监护人资格、中止探望或者变更抚养关系等途径解决。当事人对其上述主张未提供证据证明且未在合理期限内提出相关请求的，人民法院依照前款规定处理。

**Article 12** . Where a parent or a close relative, among others, forcibly takes and conceals a minor child, and the other spouse applies to the People's Court for a personal safety protection order or, by reference to Article 997 of the Civil Code, for an injunction against the infringement of personality rights, the People's Court shall support such application in accordance with law. If the parent who forcibly took and concealed the minor child claims that such act had a reasonable cause based on the existence of circumstances where the other spouse, through gambling, drug abuse, domestic violence, or other conduct, seriously infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of the minor child, the People's Court shall instruct that party to resolve the matter through legal channels such as revocation of guardianship, suspension of visitation rights, or modification of the custody arrangement. If the party fails to provide evidence to support the above claim and does not file a relevant request within a reasonable time, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　夫妻分居期间，一方或者其近亲属等抢夺、藏匿未成年子女，致使另一方无法履行监护职责，另一方请求行为人承担民事责任的，人民法院可以参照适用民法典第一千零八十四条关于离婚后子女抚养的有关规定，暂时确定未成年子女的抚养事宜，并明确暂时直接抚养未成年子女一方有协助另一方履行监护职责的义务。

**Article 13** . During a period of separation between spouses, where one party or a close relative of such party forcibly takes and conceals a minor child, thereby preventing the other spouse from exercising the duty of guardianship, and the other spouse requests that the actor bear civil liability, the People's Court may, by reference to the provisions of Article 1084 of the Civil Code on child custody after divorce, temporarily determine the custody arrangements for the minor child and specify that the party with temporary direct custody has the obligation to assist the other party in exercising the duty of guardianship.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　离婚诉讼中，父母均要求直接抚养已满两周岁的未成年子女，一方有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当按照最有利于未成年子女的原则，优先考虑由另一方直接抚养：  
（一）实施家庭暴力或者虐待、遗弃家庭成员；  
（二）有赌博、吸毒等恶习；  
（三）重婚、与他人同居或者其他严重违反夫妻忠实义务情形；  
（四）抢夺、藏匿未成年子女且另一方不存在本条第一项或者第二项等严重侵害未成年子女合法权益情形；  
（五）其他不利于未成年子女身心健康的情形。

**Article 14** . In a divorce action, where both parents request direct custody of a minor child who has attained the age of two, and one parent is in any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the principle of best interests of the minor child, prioritize consideration for the other parent to have direct custody:  
(1) Committing domestic violence or abusing or abandoning family members;  
(2) Having bad habits such as gambling or drug abuse;  
(3) Bigamy, cohabitation with another person, or other serious violations of the duty of fidelity between spouses;  
(4) Forcibly taking and concealing the minor child, while the other party does not have circumstances under Paragraphs (1) or (2) that seriously infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of the minor child  
(5) Other circumstances that are detrimental to the physical or mental health of the minor child.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　父母双方以法定代理人身份处分用夫妻共同财产购买并登记在未成年子女名下的房屋后，又以违反民法典第三十五条规定损害未成年子女利益为由向相对人主张该民事法律行为无效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 15** . After both parents, acting as legal representatives, dispose of a house purchased with community property and registered in the name of a minor child, if they subsequently claim the invalidity of the civil juristic act against the counterparty on the ground that it damages the interests of the minor child in violation of Article 35 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　离婚协议中关于一方直接抚养未成年子女或者不能独立生活的成年子女、另一方不负担抚养费的约定，对双方具有法律约束力。但是，离婚后，直接抚养子女一方经济状况发生变化导致原生活水平显著降低或者子女生活、教育、医疗等必要合理费用确有显著增加，未成年子女或者不能独立生活的成年子女请求另一方支付抚养费的，人民法院依法予以支持，并综合考虑离婚协议整体约定、子女实际需要、另一方的负担能力、当地生活水平等因素，确定抚养费的数额。  
前款但书规定情形下，另一方以直接抚养子女一方无抚养能力为由请求变更抚养关系的，人民法院依照民法典第一千零八十四条规定处理。

**Article 16** . An agreement in a divorce settlement that one party shall directly support a minor child or an adult child who is unable to live independently, and the other party shall not bear child support, is legally binding on both parties. However, after the divorce, if the financial circumstances of the party with direct custody significantly deteriorate, leading to a marked reduction in the original standard of living, or if the necessary and reasonable expenses for the child's life, education, medical care, etc., have indeed increased significantly, and the minor child or adult child who is unable to live independently requests the other party to pay child support, the People's Court shall support such request in accordance with law, and shall determine the amount of child support by taking into comprehensive consideration such factors as the overall agreement of the divorce settlement, the actual needs of the child, the other party's ability to pay, and the local standard of living. In the circumstances specified in the proviso of the preceding paragraph, if the other party requests a change in custody on the ground that the party with direct custody lacks the ability to support the child, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with Article 1084 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　离婚后，不直接抚养子女一方未按照离婚协议约定或者以其他方式作出的承诺给付抚养费，未成年子女或者不能独立生活的成年子女请求其支付欠付的抚养费的，人民法院应予支持。  
前款规定情形下，如果子女已经成年并能够独立生活，直接抚养子女一方请求另一方支付欠付的费用的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 17** . After divorce, where the parent who does not have direct custody fails to pay child support as agreed in the divorce settlement or promised in other ways, and the minor child or adult child who is unable to live independently requests the payment of overdue child support, the People's Court shall support such request.  
In the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, if the child has already reached adulthood and is able to live independently, and the parent with direct custody requests the other parent to pay the overdue amount, the People's Court shall support such request in accordance with law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对民法典第一千零七十二条中继子女受继父或者继母抚养教育的事实，人民法院应当以共同生活时间长短为基础，综合考虑共同生活期间继父母是否实际进行生活照料、是否履行家庭教育职责、是否承担抚养费等因素予以认定。

**Article 18** . For the fact that a stepchild receives maintenance and education from a stepfather or stepmother as provided in Article 1072 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall determine it based on the length of cohabitation, taking into comprehensive consideration whether the stepparent actually provided daily care, performed the duties of family education, and bore child support expenses during the period of cohabitation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　生父与继母或者生母与继父离婚后，当事人主张继父或者继母和曾受其抚养教育的继子女之间的权利义务关系不再适用民法典关于父母子女关系规定的，人民法院应予支持，但继父或者继母与继子女存在依法成立的收养关系或者继子女仍与继父或者继母共同生活的除外。  
继父母子女关系解除后，缺乏劳动能力又缺乏生活来源的继父或者继母请求曾受其抚养教育的成年继子女给付生活费的，人民法院可以综合考虑抚养教育情况、成年继子女负担能力等因素，依法予以支持，但是继父或者继母曾存在虐待、遗弃继子女等情况的除外。

**Article 19** . After the divorce of the biological father and stepmother, or the biological mother and stepfather, where a party claims that the rights and obligations between the stepfather or stepmother and the stepchild who has been maintained and educated by the stepparent shall no longer apply the provisions of the Civil Code on the relationship between parents and children, the People's Court shall support such claim, except where there is a legally established adoptive relationship between the stepfather or stepmother and the stepchild, or where the stepchild still lives with the stepfather or stepmother.  
After the dissolution of the stepparent-stepchild relationship, if the stepfather or stepmother, lacking both labor capacity and sources of livelihood, requests the adult stepchild who was previously maintained and educated by the stepparent to provide living expenses, the People's Court may, by taking into comprehensive consideration factors such as the circumstances of maintenance and education and the adult stepchild's ability to pay, support such request in accordance with law, except where the stepfather or stepmother has previously abused or abandoned the stepchild.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　离婚协议约定将部分或者全部夫妻共同财产给予子女，离婚后，一方在财产权利转移之前请求撤销该约定的，人民法院不予支持，但另一方同意的除外。  
一方不履行前款离婚协议约定的义务，另一方请求其承担继续履行或者因无法履行而赔偿损失等民事责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
双方在离婚协议中明确约定子女可以就本条第一款中的相关财产直接主张权利，一方不履行离婚协议约定的义务，子女请求参照适用民法典第五百二十二条第二款规定，由该方承担继续履行或者因无法履行而赔偿损失等民事责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
离婚协议约定将部分或者全部夫妻共同财产给予子女，离婚后，一方有证据证明签订离婚协议时存在欺诈、胁迫等情形，请求撤销该约定的，人民法院依法予以支持；当事人同时请求分割该部分夫妻共同财产的，人民法院依照民法典第一千零八十七条规定处理。

**Article 20** . Where a divorce settlement stipulates that part or all of the community property shall be given to the child, and after the divorce, one party requests the revocation of such stipulation before the transfer of the property right, the People's Court shall not support such request, except with the consent of the other party.  
If one party fails to perform the obligation stipulated in the divorce settlement in the preceding paragraph, and the other party requests that such party bear civil liability such as continued performance or compensation for losses due to impossibility of performance, the People's Court shall support such request in accordance with law.  
If the parties expressly stipulate in the divorce settlement that the child may directly claim rights with respect to the relevant property under Paragraph 1 of this Article, and one party fails to perform the obligation stipulated in the divorce settlement, and the child requests that, by reference to Paragraph 2 of Article 522 of the Civil Code, such party bear civil liability such as continued performance or compensation for losses due to impossibility of performance, the People's Court shall support such request in accordance with law.  
Where a divorce settlement stipulates that part or all of the community property shall be given to the child, and after the divorce, one party has evidence that there were circumstances such as fraud or coercion when signing the divorce settlement, and requests the revocation of such stipulation, the People's Court shall support such request in accordance with law; if the party simultaneously requests the division of that part of the community property, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with Article 1087 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　离婚诉讼中，夫妻一方有证据证明在婚姻关系存续期间因抚育子女、照料老年人、协助另一方工作等负担较多义务，依据民法典第一千零八十八条规定请求另一方给予补偿的，人民法院可以综合考虑负担相应义务投入的时间、精力和对双方的影响以及给付方负担能力、当地居民人均可支配收入等因素，确定补偿数额。

**Article 21** In divorce proceedings, where one spouse has evidence proving that they have borne more obligations during the marital relationship by raising children, caring for the elderly, or assisting the other spouse's work, and requests compensation from the other spouse in accordance with Article 1088 of the Civil Code, the People's Court may comprehensively consider the time and energy invested in fulfilling such obligations, the impact on both parties, the paying party's ability to bear, and the per capita disposable income of local residents, among other factors, to determine the amount of compensation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　离婚诉讼中，一方存在年老、残疾、重病等生活困难情形，依据民法典第一千零九十条规定请求有负担能力的另一方给予适当帮助的，人民法院可以根据当事人请求，结合另一方财产状况，依法予以支持。

**Article 22** In divorce proceedings, where one party is in financial difficulty due to old age, disability, serious illness, or other circumstances, and requests appropriate assistance from the other party with the ability to bear in accordance with Article 1090 of the Civil Code, the People's Court may, upon the party's request and considering the other party's property status, support the request according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本解释自2025年2月1日起施行。

**Article 23** This Interpretation shall come into force on February 1, 2025.
