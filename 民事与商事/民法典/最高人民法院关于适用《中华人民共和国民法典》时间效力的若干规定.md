# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》时间效力的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法典  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕15号  
**公布日期**：2020-12-29  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> 第1821次会议通过，自2021年1月1日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　民法典施行后的法律事实引起的民事纠纷案件，适用民法典的规定。  

**Article 1** : Civil dispute cases arising from legal facts that occurred after the Civil Code came into force shall be governed by the provisions of the Civil Code.
Civil dispute cases arising from legal facts that occurred before the Civil Code came into force shall be governed by the provisions of the laws and judicial interpretations in effect at that time, except as otherwise provided by laws and judicial interpretations.
Where legal facts that occurred before the Civil Code came into force continue until after the Civil Code came into force, the civil dispute cases arising from such legal facts shall be governed by the provisions of the Civil Code, except as otherwise provided by laws and judicial interpretations.
民法典施行前的法律事实引起的民事纠纷案件，适用当时的法律、司法解释的规定，但是法律、司法解释另有规定的除外。  
民法典施行前的法律事实持续至民法典施行后，该法律事实引起的民事纠纷案件，适用民法典的规定，但是法律、司法解释另有规定的除外。

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　民法典施行前的法律事实引起的民事纠纷案件，当时的法律、司法解释有规定，适用当时的法律、司法解释的规定，但是适用民法典的规定更有利于保护民事主体合法权益，更有利于维护社会和经济秩序，更有利于弘扬社会主义核心价值观的除外。

**Article 2** : For civil dispute cases arising from legal facts that occurred before the Civil Code came into force, if the laws and judicial interpretations in effect at that time contain provisions, those provisions shall apply, unless the application of the provisions of the Civil Code is more conducive to protecting the legitimate rights and interests of civil subjects, maintaining social and economic order, and promoting the core socialist values.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　民法典施行前的法律事实引起的民事纠纷案件，当时的法律、司法解释没有规定而民法典有规定的，可以适用民法典的规定，但是明显减损当事人合法权益、增加当事人法定义务或者背离当事人合理预期的除外。

**Article 3** : For civil dispute cases arising from legal facts that occurred before the Civil Code came into force, if the laws and judicial interpretations in effect at that time have no provisions but the Civil Code does, the provisions of the Civil Code may apply, unless doing so would obviously harm the legitimate rights and interests of the parties, increase the legal obligations of the parties, or deviate from the reasonable expectations of the parties.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　民法典施行前的法律事实引起的民事纠纷案件，当时的法律、司法解释仅有原则性规定而民法典有具体规定的，适用当时的法律、司法解释的规定，但是可以依据民法典具体规定进行裁判说理。

**Article 4** : For civil dispute cases arising from legal facts that occurred before the Civil Code came into force, if the laws and judicial interpretations in effect at that time only contain general provisions but the Civil Code has specific provisions, the provisions of the laws and judicial interpretations in effect at that time shall apply, but the specific provisions of the Civil Code may be used for reasoning in the adjudication.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　民法典施行前已经终审的案件，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的，不适用民法典的规定。  

**Article 5** : For cases that have already been finally adjudicated before the Civil Code came into force, if the parties apply for a retrial or if a retrial is decided pursuant to the trial supervision procedure, the provisions of the Civil Code shall not apply.
II. Specific Provisions on Retroactive Application
二、溯及适用的具体规定

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　《中华人民共和国民法总则》施行前，侵害英雄烈士等的姓名、肖像、名誉、荣誉，损害社会公共利益引起的民事纠纷案件，适用[民法典第一百八十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-185)的规定。

**Article 6** : Civil dispute cases arising from acts that occurred before the General Principles of the Civil Law of the People's Republic of China came into force, involving infringement of the name, portrait, reputation, or honor of heroes and martyrs, etc., and harming the public interest, shall be governed by Article 185 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　民法典施行前，当事人在债务履行期限届满前约定债务人不履行到期债务时抵押财产或者质押财产归债权人所有的，适用[民法典第四百零一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-401)和第四百二十八条的规定。

**Article 7** : Where, before the Civil Code came into force, the parties agreed before the expiration of the debt performance period that the mortgaged property or pledged property would belong to the creditor if the debtor failed to perform the due debt, Articles 401 and 428 of the Civil Code shall apply.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　民法典施行前成立的合同，适用当时的法律、司法解释的规定合同无效而适用民法典的规定合同有效的，适用民法典的相关规定。

**Article 8** : For contracts concluded before the Civil Code came into force, if the contract is invalid under the provisions of the laws and judicial interpretations in effect at that time but is valid under the provisions of the Civil Code, the relevant provisions of the Civil Code shall apply.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　民法典施行前订立的合同，提供格式条款一方未履行提示或者说明义务，涉及格式条款效力认定的，适用[民法典第四百九十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-496)的规定。

**Article 9** : For contracts concluded before the Civil Code came into force, if the party providing the standard terms failed to fulfill the obligation to draw attention or provide explanations, and the validity of the standard terms is at issue, Article 496 of the Civil Code shall apply.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　民法典施行前，当事人一方未通知对方而直接以提起诉讼方式依法主张解除合同的，适用[民法典第五百六十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-565)第二款的规定。

**Article 10** : Where, before the Civil Code came into force, one party sought to dissolve the contract by directly filing a lawsuit without notifying the other party in accordance with the law, Article 565(2) of the Civil Code shall apply.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　民法典施行前成立的合同，当事人一方不履行非金钱债务或者履行非金钱债务不符合约定，对方可以请求履行，但是有[民法典第五百八十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-580)第一款第一项、第二项、第三项除外情形之一，致使不能实现合同目的，当事人请求终止合同权利义务关系的，适用[民法典第五百八十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-580)第二款的规定。

**Article 11** : For contracts concluded before the Civil Code came into force, if one party fails to perform a non-monetary obligation or performs a non-monetary obligation in a manner inconsistent with the agreement, and the other party may request performance, but one of the exceptions under Article 580(1)(i), (ii), or (iii) of the Civil Code exists, making it impossible to achieve the purpose of the contract, and the party requests termination of the contractual rights and obligations, Article 580(2) of the Civil Code shall apply.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　民法典施行前订立的保理合同发生争议的，适用民法典第三编第十六章的规定。

**Article 12** : Disputes arising from factoring contracts concluded before the Civil Code came into force shall be governed by the provisions of Chapter 16, Part III of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　民法典施行前，继承人有[民法典第一千一百二十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1125)第一款第四项和第五项规定行为之一，对该继承人是否丧失继承权发生争议的，适用[民法典第一千一百二十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1125)第一款和第二款的规定。  

**Article 13** : Where, before the Civil Code came into force, a heir committed one of the acts specified in Article 1125(1)(iv) and (v) of the Civil Code, and there is a dispute over whether the heir has lost the right of inheritance, Articles 1125(1) and (2) of the Civil Code shall apply.
Where, before the Civil Code came into force, a legatee committed one of the acts specified in Article 1125(1) of the Civil Code, and there is a dispute over whether the legatee has lost the right to receive the legacy, Articles 1125(1) and (3) of the Civil Code shall apply.
民法典施行前，受遗赠人有[民法典第一千一百二十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1125)第一款规定行为之一，对受遗赠人是否丧失受遗赠权发生争议的，适用[民法典第一千一百二十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1125)第一款和第三款的规定。

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　被继承人在民法典施行前死亡，遗产无人继承又无人受遗赠，其兄弟姐妹的子女请求代位继承的，适用[民法典第一千一百二十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1128)第二款和第三款的规定，但是遗产已经在民法典施行前处理完毕的除外。

**Article 14** : Where the decedent died before the Civil Code came into force, leaving no heir or legatee, and the children of the decedent's siblings request subrogation inheritance, Articles 1128(2) and (3) of the Civil Code shall apply, unless the estate had already been disposed of before the Civil Code came into force.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　民法典施行前，遗嘱人以打印方式立的遗嘱，当事人对该遗嘱效力发生争议的，适用[民法典第一千一百三十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1136)的规定，但是遗产已经在民法典施行前处理完毕的除外。

**Article 15** : Where, before the Civil Code came into force, a testator made a will by printing, and the parties dispute the validity of the will, Article 1136 of the Civil Code shall apply, unless the estate had already been disposed of before the Civil Code came into force.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　民法典施行前，受害人自愿参加具有一定风险的文体活动受到损害引起的民事纠纷案件，适用[民法典第一千一百七十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1176)的规定。

**Article 16** : Civil dispute cases arising from injuries suffered by victims who voluntarily participated in recreational activities with certain risks before the Civil Code came into force shall be governed by Article 1176 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　民法典施行前，受害人为保护自己合法权益采取扣留侵权人的财物等措施引起的民事纠纷案件，适用[民法典第一千一百七十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1177)的规定。

**Article 17** : Civil dispute cases arising from victims taking measures to detain the infringer's property, etc. to protect their own legitimate rights and interests before the Civil Code came into force shall be governed by Article 1177 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　民法典施行前，因非营运机动车发生交通事故造成无偿搭乘人损害引起的民事纠纷案件，适用[民法典第一千二百一十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1217)的规定。

**Article 18** : Civil dispute cases arising from injuries to unpaid passengers caused by traffic accidents involving non-operating motor vehicles before the Civil Code came into force shall be governed by Article 1217 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　民法典施行前，从建筑物中抛掷物品或者从建筑物上坠落的物品造成他人损害引起的民事纠纷案件，适用[民法典第一千二百五十四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1254)的规定。  

**Article 19** : Civil dispute cases arising from injuries to others caused by objects thrown from a building or objects falling from a building before the Civil Code came into force shall be governed by Article 1254 of the Civil Code.
III. Specific Provisions on Transitional Application
三、衔接适用的具体规定

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　民法典施行前成立的合同，依照法律规定或者当事人约定该合同的履行持续至民法典施行后，因民法典施行前履行合同发生争议的，适用当时的法律、司法解释的规定；因民法典施行后履行合同发生争议的，适用民法典第三编第四章和第五章的相关规定。

**Article 20** : For contracts concluded before the Civil Code came into force, if the performance of the contract continues after the Civil Code came into force in accordance with the law or the agreement of the parties, disputes arising from the performance of the contract before the Civil Code came into force shall be governed by the provisions of the laws and judicial interpretations in effect at that time; disputes arising from the performance of the contract after the Civil Code came into force shall be governed by the relevant provisions of Chapters 4 and 5, Part III of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　民法典施行前租赁期限届满，当事人主张适用[民法典第七百三十四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-734)第二款规定的，人民法院不予支持；租赁期限在民法典施行后届满，当事人主张适用[民法典第七百三十四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-734)第二款规定的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 21** Where a lease term expires before the entry into force of the Civil Code, and a party claims the application of paragraph 2 of Article 734 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall not support such claim; where a lease term expires after the entry into force of the Civil Code, and a party claims the application of paragraph 2 of Article 734 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall support such claim in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　民法典施行前，经人民法院判决不准离婚后，双方又分居满一年，一方再次提起离婚诉讼的，适用[民法典第一千零七十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1079)第五款的规定。

**Article 22** Where, before the entry into force of the Civil Code, a people's court has rendered a judgment dismissing a divorce claim, and thereafter the parties have been living apart for a full year, and one party files another divorce action, the provisions of paragraph 5 of Article 1079 of the Civil Code shall apply.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　被继承人在民法典施行前立有公证遗嘱，民法典施行后又立有新遗嘱，其死亡后，因该数份遗嘱内容相抵触发生争议的，适用[民法典第一千一百四十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1142)第三款的规定。

**Article 23** Where a decedent made a notarized will before the entry into force of the Civil Code and made a new will after the entry into force of the Civil Code, and a dispute arises after his or her death due to conflicting contents among these multiple wills, the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 1142 of the Civil Code shall apply.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　侵权行为发生在民法典施行前，但是损害后果出现在民法典施行后的民事纠纷案件，适用民法典的规定。

**Article 24** For a civil dispute case where the tortious act occurred before the entry into force of the Civil Code, but the harmful consequence arises after the entry into force of the Civil Code, the provisions of the Civil Code shall apply.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　民法典施行前成立的合同，当时的法律、司法解释没有规定且当事人没有约定解除权行使期限，对方当事人也未催告的，解除权人在民法典施行前知道或者应当知道解除事由，自民法典施行之日起一年内不行使的，人民法院应当依法认定该解除权消灭；解除权人在民法典施行后知道或者应当知道解除事由的，适用[民法典第五百六十四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-564)第二款关于解除权行使期限的规定。

**Article 25** For a contract concluded before the entry into force of the Civil Code, if the laws and judicial interpretations at that time had no provisions, and the parties did not agree on the period for exercising the right to rescind, and the other party did not serve a reminder, and the person holding the right to rescind knew or should have known the grounds for rescission before the entry into force of the Civil Code but failed to exercise the right within one year from the date of the Civil Code's entry into force, the People's Court shall determine in accordance with the law that such right to rescind is extinguished; if the person holding the right to rescind knew or should have known the grounds for rescission after the entry into force of the Civil Code, the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 564 of the Civil Code concerning the period for exercising the right to rescind shall apply.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　当事人以民法典施行前受胁迫结婚为由请求人民法院撤销婚姻的，撤销权的行使期限适用[民法典第一千零五十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1052)第二款的规定。

**Article 26** Where a party requests the People's Court to annul a marriage on the ground that the marriage was entered into under duress before the entry into force of the Civil Code, the period for exercising the right to annulment shall be governed by the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 1052 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　 民法典施行前成立的保证合同，当事人对保证期间约定不明确，主债务履行期限届满至民法典施行之日不满二年，当事人主张保证期间为主债务履行期限届满之日起二年的，人民法院依法予以支持；当事人对保证期间没有约定，主债务履行期限届满至民法典施行之日不满六个月，当事人主张保证期间为主债务履行期限届满之日起六个月的，人民法院依法予以支持。  

**Article 27** For a suretyship contract concluded before the entry into force of the Civil Code, if the parties' agreement on the suretyship period is unclear, and the period from the expiration of the performance term of the principal obligation to the date of entry into force of the Civil Code is less than two years, and a party claims that the suretyship period shall be two years from the date of expiration of the performance term of the principal obligation, the People's Court shall support such claim in accordance with the law; if the parties have not agreed on the suretyship period, and the period from the expiration of the performance term of the principal obligation to the date of entry into force of the Civil Code is less than six months, and a party claims that the suretyship period shall be six months from the date of expiration of the performance term of the principal obligation, the People's Court shall support such claim in accordance with the law.
IV. Supplementary Provisions
四、附则

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本规定自2021年1月1日起施行。  

**Article 28** These Provisions shall come into force as of January 1, 2021.
After the entry into force of these Provisions, cases of first instance and second instance that have not yet been finally adjudicated by the People's Court shall be governed by these Provisions.
本规定施行后，人民法院尚未审结的一审、二审案件适用本规定。
