# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》有关担保制度的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法典  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕28号  
**公布日期**：2020-12-31  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> 第1824次会议通过，自2021年1月1日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　因抵押、质押、留置、保证等担保发生的纠纷，适用本解释。所有权保留买卖、融资租赁、保理等涉及担保功能发生的纠纷，适用本解释的有关规定。



**Article 1** Disputes arising from guarantees such as mortgage, pledge, lien, and suretyship shall be governed by this Interpretation. Disputes involving guarantee functions, such as conditional sale, financial leasing, and factoring, shall be governed by the relevant provisions of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人在担保合同中约定担保合同的效力独立于主合同，或者约定担保人对主合同无效的法律后果承担担保责任，该有关担保独立性的约定无效。主合同有效的，有关担保独立性的约定无效不影响担保合同的效力；主合同无效的，人民法院应当认定担保合同无效，但是法律另有规定的除外。  



**Article 2** Where the parties stipulate in the guarantee contract that the validity of the guarantee contract is independent from the principal contract, or that the guarantor shall bear the guarantee liability for the legal consequences of the invalidity of the principal contract, such stipulation on the independence of the guarantee shall be void. If the principal contract is valid, the invalidity of the stipulation on the independence of the guarantee shall not affect the validity of the guarantee contract; if the principal contract is void, the People's Court shall determine that the guarantee contract is void, unless otherwise provided by law. Disputes arising from independent letters of guarantee issued by financial institutions shall be governed by the "Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Trial of Disputes Involving Independent Letters of Guarantee."

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　当事人对担保责任的承担约定专门的违约责任，或者约定的担保责任范围超出债务人应当承担的责任范围，担保人主张仅在债务人应当承担的责任范围内承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 3** Where the parties stipulate a specific liability for breach of contract for assuming the guarantee liability, or the agreed scope of guarantee liability exceeds the scope of liability that the debtor shall bear, and the guarantor claims to bear liability only within the scope of liability that the debtor shall bear, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
If the liability borne by the guarantor exceeds the scope of liability that the debtor shall bear, and the guarantor seeks recourse against the debtor, and the debtor claims to bear liability only within the scope of liability it shall bear, the People's Court shall support such claim; if the guarantor requests the creditor to return the excess amount, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　有下列情形之一，当事人将担保物权登记在他人名下，债务人不履行到期债务或者发生当事人约定的实现担保物权的情形，债权人或者其受托人主张就该财产优先受偿的，人民法院依法予以支持：  



**Article 4** Under any of the following circumstances, where a party registers a security interest in the name of another person, and the debtor fails to perform its due debt or the circumstances for realizing the security interest agreed upon by the parties arise, the creditor or its trustee claims to be repaid from the property on a priority basis, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law: (1) The security interest provided for bondholders is registered in the name of the bond trustee. (2) The security interest provided for a entrusted lender is registered in the name of the trustee. (3) Other circumstances where the guarantor knows that there is a entrustment relationship between the creditor and another person.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　机关法人提供担保的，人民法院应当认定担保合同无效，但是经国务院批准为使用外国政府或者国际经济组织贷款进行转贷的除外。  



**Article 5** Where an institutional legal person provides a guarantee, the People's Court shall determine that the guarantee contract is void, unless the guarantee is provided for on-lending of loans from foreign governments or international economic organizations with the approval of the State Council.  
Where a residents' committee or a villagers' committee provides a guarantee, the People's Court shall determine that the guarantee contract is void, unless a villagers' committee that performs the functions of a village collective economic organization according to law provides a guarantee externally in accordance with the discussion and decision procedures stipulated in the Organic Law of the Villagers' Committees.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　以公益为目的的非营利性学校、幼儿园、医疗机构、养老机构等提供担保的，人民法院应当认定担保合同无效，但是有下列情形之一的除外：  



**Article 6** Where a non-profit school, kindergarten, medical institution, elderly care institution, or similar entity, which is established for public welfare purposes, provides a guarantee, the People's Court shall determine that the guarantee contract is void, except under any of the following circumstances: (1) When purchasing or leasing (through financial leasing) educational facilities, medical and health facilities, elderly care service facilities, or other public welfare facilities, the seller or lessor retains ownership of the public welfare facility to secure the payment of the price or rent; (2) Creating a security interest on immovable property, movable property, or property rights other than educational facilities, medical and health facilities, elderly care service facilities, or other public welfare facilities. Where a school, kindergarten, medical institution, elderly care institution, or similar entity registered as a for-profit legal person provides a guarantee, and a party claims that the guarantee contract is void on the grounds that the entity lacks guarantee capacity, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　公司的法定代表人违反公司法关于公司对外担保决议程序的规定，超越权限代表公司与相对人订立担保合同，人民法院应当依照[民法典第六十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-61)和第五百零四条等规定处理：  



**Article 7** Where the legal representative of a company violates the provisions of the Company Law regarding the resolution procedures for the company's external guarantees and, exceeding authority, enters into a guarantee contract with a counterparty on behalf of the company, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with Articles 61 and 504 of the Civil Code and other relevant provisions: (1) If the counterparty is in good faith, the guarantee contract shall be binding on the company; if the counterparty requests the company to bear the guarantee liability, the People's Court shall support such request. (2) If the counterparty is not in good faith, the guarantee contract shall not be binding on the company; if the counterparty requests the company to bear compensation liability, the relevant provisions of Article 17 of this Interpretation shall apply mutatis mutandis. Where the legal representative, exceeding authority, provides a guarantee and causes losses to the company, and the company requests the legal representative to bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall support such request. "Good faith" as referred to in the first paragraph means that the counterparty did not know and should not have known that the legal representative was exceeding authority at the time of concluding the guarantee contract. If the counterparty has evidence to prove that it has reasonably reviewed the company's resolution, the People's Court shall determine that it constitutes good faith, unless the company has evidence to prove that the counterparty knew or should have known that the resolution was forged or altered.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　有下列情形之一，公司以其未依照公司法关于公司对外担保的规定作出决议为由主张不承担担保责任的，人民法院不予支持：  



**Article 8** Under any of the following circumstances, where a company claims that it shall not bear guarantee liability on the grounds that it has not adopted a resolution in accordance with the provisions of the Company Law on external guarantees by companies, the People's Court shall not support such claim: (1) A financial institution issues a letter of guarantee or a guarantee company provides a guarantee; (2) A company provides a guarantee for its wholly-owned subsidiary in the course of its business operations; (3) The guarantee contract is signed with the consent of shareholders who individually or jointly hold two-thirds or more of the voting rights on the guarantee matter. The provisions of items (2) and (3) of the preceding paragraph shall not apply where a listed company provides an external guarantee.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　相对人根据上市公司公开披露的关于担保事项已经董事会或者股东大会决议通过的信息，与上市公司订立担保合同，相对人主张担保合同对上市公司发生效力，并由上市公司承担担保责任的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 9** Where a counterparty, based on the information publicly disclosed by a listed company that a resolution on the guarantee matter has been passed by the board of directors or the shareholders' meeting, enters into a guarantee contract with the listed company, and the counterparty claims that the guarantee contract is binding on the listed company and that the listed company shall bear the guarantee liability, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
Where a counterparty enters into a guarantee contract with a listed company without relying on the information publicly disclosed by the listed company that a resolution on the guarantee matter has been passed by the board of directors or the shareholders' meeting, and the listed company claims that the guarantee contract is not binding on it and that it shall not bear guarantee or compensation liability, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
The provisions of the preceding two paragraphs shall apply to guarantee contracts entered into between a counterparty and a publicly disclosed controlling subsidiary of a listed company, or between a counterparty and a company whose shares are traded on other national securities trading venues approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　一人有限责任公司为其股东提供担保，公司以违反公司法关于公司对外担保决议程序的规定为由主张不承担担保责任的，人民法院不予支持。公司因承担担保责任导致无法清偿其他债务，提供担保时的股东不能证明公司财产独立于自己的财产，其他债权人请求该股东承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。



**Article 10** Where a one-person limited liability company provides a guarantee for its shareholder, and the company claims that it shall not bear the guarantee liability on the grounds that the company violated the provisions of the Company Law on the resolution procedures for external guarantees by companies, the People's Court shall not support such claim. If the company is unable to discharge its other debts due to bearing the guarantee liability, and the shareholder at the time of providing the guarantee cannot prove that the company's property is independent from its own property, and other creditors request that the shareholder bear joint and several liability, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　公司的分支机构未经公司股东（大）会或者董事会决议以自己的名义对外提供担保，相对人请求公司或者其分支机构承担担保责任的，人民法院不予支持，但是相对人不知道且不应当知道分支机构对外提供担保未经公司决议程序的除外。  



**Article 11** Where a branch of a company provides a guarantee to an external party in its own name without a resolution of the company's shareholders' meeting (or general meeting) or board of directors, and the counterparty requests the company or its branch to assume guarantee liability, the People's Court shall not support such a request, unless the counterparty neither knew nor should have known that the branch provided the external guarantee without company resolution procedures. Where a branch of a financial institution issues a letter of guarantee within the business scope recorded in its business license, or issues a letter of guarantee upon authorization by a superior entity authorized to engage in guarantee business, and the financial institution or its branch asserts that it shall not assume guarantee liability on the ground of violating the provisions of the Company Law on the resolution procedures for external guarantees by the company, the People's Court shall not support such an assertion. Where a branch of a financial institution provides a guarantee other than a letter of guarantee without authorization from the financial institution, and the financial institution or its branch asserts that it shall not assume guarantee liability, the People's Court shall support such an assertion, unless the counterparty neither knew nor should have known that the branch provided the external guarantee without authorization from the financial institution. Where a branch of a guarantee company provides an external guarantee without authorization from the guarantee company, and the guarantee company or its branch asserts that it shall not assume guarantee liability, the People's Court shall support such an assertion, unless the counterparty neither knew nor should have known that the branch provided the external guarantee without authorization from the guarantee company. Where a branch of a company provides an external guarantee and the counterparty is not in good faith, and requests the company to assume compensation liability, the matter shall be handled by reference to the relevant provisions of Article 17 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　法定代表人依照[民法典第五百五十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-552)的规定以公司名义加入债务的，人民法院在认定该行为的效力时，可以参照本解释关于公司为他人提供担保的有关规则处理。



**Article 12** Where the legal representative, in accordance with the provisions of Article 552 of the Civil Code, joins the debt in the name of the company, the People's Court may, when determining the validity of such act, handle the matter by reference to the relevant rules in this Interpretation regarding guarantees provided by a company for others.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　同一债务有两个以上第三人提供担保，担保人之间约定相互追偿及分担份额，承担了担保责任的担保人请求其他担保人按照约定分担份额的，人民法院应予支持；担保人之间约定承担连带共同担保，或者约定相互追偿但是未约定分担份额的，各担保人按照比例分担向债务人不能追偿的部分。  



**Article 13** Where two or more third parties provide guarantees for the same debt, and the guarantors have agreed on mutual recourse and the allocation of shares, and a guarantor who has assumed guarantee liability requests other guarantors to share the allocated amounts as agreed, the People's Court shall support such a request. Where the guarantors have agreed to assume joint and several co-guarantee, or have agreed on mutual recourse but have not agreed on the allocation of shares, each guarantor shall share, in proportion, the portion that cannot be recovered from the debtor. Where two or more third parties provide guarantees for the same debt, and the guarantors have not made an agreement on mutual recourse and have not agreed to assume joint and several co-guarantee, but each guarantor has signed, sealed, or affixed a fingerprint on the same contract document, and a guarantor who has assumed guarantee liability requests other guarantors to share, in proportion, the portion that cannot be recovered from the debtor, the People's Court shall support such a request. Except for the circumstances provided in the preceding two paragraphs, where a guarantor who has assumed guarantee liability requests other guarantors to share the portion that cannot be recovered from the debtor, the People's Court shall not support such a request.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　同一债务有两个以上第三人提供担保，担保人受让债权的，人民法院应当认定该行为系承担担保责任。受让债权的担保人作为债权人请求其他担保人承担担保责任的，人民法院不予支持；该担保人请求其他担保人分担相应份额的，依照[本解释第十三条](#art-13)的规定处理。



**Article 14** Where two or more third parties provide guarantees for the same debt, and a guarantor acquires the creditor's rights, the People's Court shall determine that such act constitutes the assumption of guarantee liability. Where the guarantor who has acquired the creditor's rights, acting as a creditor, requests other guarantors to assume guarantee liability, the People's Court shall not support such a request. Where such guarantor requests other guarantors to share the corresponding shares, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 13 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　最高额担保中的最高债权额，是指包括主债权及其利息、违约金、损害赔偿金、保管担保财产的费用、实现债权或者实现担保物权的费用等在内的全部债权，但是当事人另有约定的除外。  



**Article 15** The maximum amount of claim in a maximum amount guarantee refers to all claims including the principal claim and its interest, liquidated damages, compensation for damages, expenses for keeping the secured property, and expenses for realizing the claim or realizing the security interest, unless the parties have agreed otherwise.  
Where the registered maximum amount of claim is inconsistent with the maximum amount of claim agreed upon by the parties, the People's Court shall determine the scope of the creditor's priority right to be satisfied based on the registered maximum amount of claim.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　主合同当事人协议以新贷偿还旧贷，债权人请求旧贷的担保人承担担保责任的，人民法院不予支持；债权人请求新贷的担保人承担担保责任的，按照下列情形处理：  



**Article 16** Where the parties to the main contract agree to repay an old loan with a new loan, and the creditor requests the guarantor of the old loan to assume guarantee liability, the People's Court shall not support such a request. Where the creditor requests the guarantor of the new loan to assume guarantee liability, the matter shall be handled according to the following circumstances: (1) If the guarantor of the new loan is the same as that of the old loan, the People's Court shall support the request; (2) If the guarantor of the new loan is different from that of the old loan, or if the old loan has no guarantee but the new loan has a guarantee, the People's Court shall not support the request, unless the creditor has evidence proving that the guarantor of the new loan knew or should have known the fact of repaying the old loan with the new loan at the time of providing the guarantee. Where the parties to the main contract agree to repay an old loan with a new loan, and the security provider of the old loan agrees to continue providing guarantee for the new loan while the registration has not yet been cancelled, and before concluding the new loan contract, creates a security interest in the secured property for another creditor, and the other creditor claims that its security interest has priority over the creditor of the new loan, the People's Court shall not support such a claim.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　主合同有效而第三人提供的担保合同无效，人民法院应当区分不同情形确定担保人的赔偿责任：  



**Article 17** Where the main contract is valid but the guarantee contract provided by a third party is invalid, the People's Court shall determine the guarantor's compensation liability based on different circumstances: (1) If both the creditor and the guarantor are at fault, the compensation liability borne by the guarantor shall not exceed one-half of the portion that the debtor cannot pay; (2) If the guarantor is at fault but the creditor is not, the guarantor shall bear compensation liability for the portion that the debtor cannot pay; (3) If the creditor is at fault but the guarantor is not, the guarantor shall not bear compensation liability. Where the invalidity of the main contract causes the guarantee contract provided by a third party to be invalid, and the guarantor is not at fault, the guarantor shall not bear compensation liability. If the guarantor is at fault, the compensation liability borne by the guarantor shall not exceed one-third of the portion that the debtor cannot pay.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　承担了担保责任或者赔偿责任的担保人，在其承担责任的范围内向债务人追偿的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 18** Where a guarantor who has assumed guarantee liability or compensation liability seeks recourse against the debtor within the scope of the liability it has borne, the People's Court shall support such a request.  
Where the same creditor's rights are secured by both a security interest provided by the debtor itself and a guarantee provided by a third party, and the third party who has assumed guarantee liability or compensation liability claims to exercise the creditor's security interest against the debtor, the People's Court shall support such a claim.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　担保合同无效，承担了赔偿责任的担保人按照反担保合同的约定，在其承担赔偿责任的范围内请求反担保人承担担保责任的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 19** Where the guarantee contract is invalid, and a guarantor who has assumed compensation liability requests the counter-guarantor to assume guarantee liability within the scope of the compensation liability it has borne, in accordance with the provisions of the counter-guarantee contract, the People's Court shall support such a request. If the counter-guarantee contract is invalid, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of Article 17 of this Interpretation. Where a party asserts the invalidity of the counter-guarantee contract solely on the ground that the guarantee contract is invalid, the People's Court shall not support such an assertion.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　人民法院在审理第三人提供的物的担保纠纷案件时，可以适用[民法典第六百九十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-695)第一款、第六百九十六条第一款、第六百九十七条第二款、第六百九十九条、第七百条、第七百零一条、第七百零二条等关于保证合同的规定。



**Article 20** When adjudicating a case involving a dispute over a security interest provided by a third party, the People's Court may apply the provisions on guarantee contracts, such as Article 695, Paragraph 1; Article 696, Paragraph 1; Article 697, Paragraph 2; Article 699; Article 700; Article 701; and Article 702 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　主合同或者担保合同约定了仲裁条款的，人民法院对约定仲裁条款的合同当事人之间的纠纷无管辖权。  



**Article 21** Where a master contract or a guarantee contract stipulates an arbitration clause, the People's Court shall have no jurisdiction over disputes among the parties to the contract that stipulates the arbitration clause.  
Where a creditor jointly sues the debtor and the guarantor, the court with jurisdiction shall be determined based on the master contract.  
Where a creditor may lawfully sue the guarantor alone and does so, the court with jurisdiction shall be determined based on the guarantee contract.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　人民法院受理债务人破产案件后，债权人请求担保人承担担保责任，担保人主张担保债务自人民法院受理破产申请之日起停止计息的，人民法院对担保人的主张应予支持。



**Article 22** After a People's Court accepts a debtor's bankruptcy case, if a creditor requests the guarantor to assume the guarantee liability, and the guarantor claims that the interest on the guaranteed debt shall cease to accrue from the date on which the People's Court accepts the bankruptcy application, the People's Court shall uphold the guarantor's claim.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　人民法院受理债务人破产案件，债权人在破产程序中申报债权后又向人民法院提起诉讼，请求担保人承担担保责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。  



**Article 23** Where a People's Court accepts a debtor's bankruptcy case, and a creditor, having declared its claims in the bankruptcy proceedings, subsequently files a lawsuit with the People's Court requesting the guarantor to assume the guarantee liability, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.  
After the guarantor has fully repaid all of the creditor's claims, it may be subrogated to the creditor's position in the bankruptcy proceedings to receive distribution; before the creditor's claims are fully satisfied, the guarantor shall not be subrogated to the creditor's position in the bankruptcy proceedings, but shall have the right, to the extent of the guarantee liability assumed, to request the creditor to return any portion of the total amount received by the creditor through bankruptcy distribution and realization of the guaranteed claims that exceeds the claims.  
Where a creditor has not been fully satisfied in the debtor's bankruptcy proceedings and requests the guarantor to continue assuming the guarantee liability, the People's Court shall support such request; where the guarantor, after assuming the guarantee liability, seeks recourse against the debtor after the completion of the implementation of a composition agreement or a reorganization plan, the People's Court shall not support such recourse.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　债权人知道或者应当知道债务人破产，既未申报债权也未通知担保人，致使担保人不能预先行使追偿权的，担保人就该债权在破产程序中可能受偿的范围内免除担保责任，但是担保人因自身过错未行使追偿权的除外。  



**Article 24** Where a creditor knows or should have known that the debtor is in bankruptcy, but neither declares its claims nor notifies the guarantor, thereby causing the guarantor to be unable to exercise its right of recourse in advance, the guarantor shall be exonerated from the guarantee liability to the extent of the amount that such claim could possibly be satisfied in the bankruptcy proceedings, except where the guarantor fails to exercise its right of recourse due to its own fault.  
II. Guaranty Contracts

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　当事人在保证合同中约定了保证人在债务人不能履行债务或者无力偿还债务时才承担保证责任等类似内容，具有债务人应当先承担责任的意思表示的，人民法院应当将其认定为一般保证。  



**Article 25** Where the parties stipulate in the guaranty contract that the guarantor shall assume the guarantee liability only when the debtor is unable to perform the debt or lacks the ability to repay the debt, or similar content, which manifests an intention that the debtor shall bear liability first, the People's Court shall recognize it as a general suretyship.  
Where the parties stipulate in the guaranty contract that the guarantor shall assume the guarantee liability immediately upon the debtor's failure to perform the debt or repay the debt, or shall assume the guarantee liability unconditionally, or similar content, which does not manifest an intention that the debtor shall bear liability first, the People's Court shall recognize it as a joint and several suretyship.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　一般保证中，债权人以债务人为被告提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。债权人未就主合同纠纷提起诉讼或者申请仲裁，仅起诉一般保证人的，人民法院应当驳回起诉。  



**Article 26** In a general suretyship, where a creditor files a lawsuit naming the debtor as the defendant, the People's Court shall accept the case. Where the creditor, without filing a lawsuit or applying for arbitration regarding the dispute over the master contract, only sues the general surety, the People's Court shall dismiss the lawsuit. In a general suretyship, where a creditor jointly sues the debtor and the surety, the People's Court may accept the case; however, when rendering the judgment, except as provided for in the proviso of Article 687, paragraph 2 of the Civil Code, the judgment shall expressly state in the main text that the surety shall only be liable for the portion of the debt that remains unfulfilled after compulsory enforcement against the debtor's property is carried out according to law. Where the creditor has not applied for preservation of the debtor's property, or the preserved property of the debtor is sufficient to satisfy the debt, and the creditor applies for preservation of the general surety's property, the People's Court shall not grant the application.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　一般保证的债权人取得对债务人赋予强制执行效力的公证债权文书后，在保证期间内向人民法院申请强制执行，保证人以债权人未在保证期间内对债务人提起诉讼或者申请仲裁为由主张不承担保证责任的，人民法院不予支持。



**Article 27** Where a creditor in a general suretyship obtains a notarized document of obligatory rights that gives it enforceable effect against the debtor, and applies to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement within the suretyship period, if the surety claims that it shall not bear the suretyship liability on the ground that the creditor did not file a lawsuit or apply for arbitration against the debtor within the suretyship period, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　一般保证中，债权人依据生效法律文书对债务人的财产依法申请强制执行，保证债务诉讼时效的起算时间按照下列规则确定：  



**Article 28** In a general suretyship, where the creditor applies for compulsory enforcement against the debtor's property according to law based on an effective legal instrument, the limitation period for the surety debt shall commence in accordance with the following rules: (1) Where the People's Court renders a ruling to conclude the current enforcement proceedings, or renders a ruling to terminate enforcement pursuant to subparagraphs (3) and (5) of Article 257 of the Civil Procedure Law, the period shall commence from the date on which the ruling is served on the creditor; (2) Where the People's Court fails to render the ruling referred to in the preceding subparagraph within one year from the date of receiving the application for enforcement, the period shall commence from the date on which one year has elapsed from the date of the People's Court's receipt of the application for enforcement, except where the surety has evidence proving that the debtor still has property available for enforcement. Where the creditor in a general suretyship files a lawsuit or applies for arbitration against the debtor before the expiration of the suretyship period, and the creditor adduces evidence proving the existence of the circumstances specified in the proviso of Article 687, paragraph 2 of the Civil Code, the limitation period for the surety debt shall commence from the date on which the creditor knows or should have known of such circumstances.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　同一债务有两个以上保证人，债权人以其已经在保证期间内依法向部分保证人行使权利为由，主张已经在保证期间内向其他保证人行使权利的，人民法院不予支持。  



**Article 29** Where there are two or more sureties for the same debt, and a creditor claims to have exercised its right against the other sureties within the suretyship period on the ground that it has lawfully exercised its right against some of the sureties within the suretyship period, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
Where there are two or more sureties for the same debt, and the sureties have a mutual right of recourse, if the creditor fails to lawfully exercise its right against some of the sureties within the suretyship period, thereby causing other sureties to lose their right of recourse after assuming the suretyship liability, and those other sureties claim to be exonerated from the suretyship liability to the extent of the amount they cannot recover, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　最高额保证合同对保证期间的计算方式、起算时间等有约定的，按照其约定。  



**Article 30** Where a contract of maximum amount suretyship has an agreement on the method for calculating the suretyship period, the starting time, or other matters, such agreement shall prevail.  
Where a contract of maximum amount suretyship has no agreement or the agreement is unclear on the method for calculating the suretyship period, the starting time, or other matters, and the performance periods of all guaranteed claims have expired, the suretyship period shall commence from the date on which the claims are determined; where the performance periods of the guaranteed claims have not yet expired, the suretyship period shall commence from the date on which the performance period of the last due claim expires.  
The date on which the claims are determined as mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be identified in accordance with Article 423 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　 一般保证的债权人在保证期间内对债务人提起诉讼或者申请仲裁后，又撤回起诉或者仲裁申请，债权人在保证期间届满前未再行提起诉讼或者申请仲裁，保证人主张不再承担保证责任的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 31** If a creditor under a general suretyship brings an action or applies for arbitration against the debtor within the suretyship period, and then withdraws the action or arbitration application, and the creditor fails to bring another action or apply for arbitration again before the expiry of the suretyship period, and the surety claims that it shall no longer bear the suretyship liability, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.  
If a creditor under a joint and several suretyship brings an action or applies for arbitration against the surety within the suretyship period, and then withdraws the action or arbitration application, and the copy of the complaint or the copy of the arbitration application has been served on the surety, the People's Court shall find that the creditor has exercised its right against the surety within the suretyship period.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　保证合同约定保证人承担保证责任直至主债务本息还清时为止等类似内容的，视为约定不明，保证期间为主债务履行期限届满之日起六个月。



**Article 32** If a suretyship contract stipulates that the surety shall bear suretyship liability until the principal debt and its interest are fully repaid, or contains similar content, such stipulation shall be deemed unclear, and the suretyship period shall be six months commencing from the date on which the performance period of the principal debt expires.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　保证合同无效，债权人未在约定或者法定的保证期间内依法行使权利，保证人主张不承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。



**Article 33** If the suretyship contract is void, and the creditor fails to exercise its rights according to law within the agreed or statutory suretyship period, and the surety claims that it shall not bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　人民法院在审理保证合同纠纷案件时，应当将保证期间是否届满、债权人是否在保证期间内依法行使权利等事实作为案件基本事实予以查明。  



**Article 34** When adjudicating a dispute over a suretyship contract, the People's Court shall ascertain as basic facts of the case such matters as whether the suretyship period has expired and whether the creditor has exercised its rights according to law within the suretyship period. If the creditor fails to exercise its rights according to law within the suretyship period, the suretyship liability shall be extinguished. After the suretyship liability has been extinguished, the creditor gives the surety written notice demanding that the surety bear the suretyship liability, and the surety signs, affixes its seal, or affixes its fingerprint on the notice, and the creditor requests the surety to continue to bear the suretyship liability, the People's Court shall not uphold such request, unless the creditor has evidence proving that a new suretyship contract has been formed.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　保证人知道或者应当知道主债权诉讼时效期间届满仍然提供保证或者承担保证责任，又以诉讼时效期间届满为由拒绝承担保证责任或者请求返还财产的，人民法院不予支持；保证人承担保证责任后向债务人追偿的，人民法院不予支持，但是债务人放弃诉讼时效抗辩的除外。



**Article 35** If a surety, knowing or ought to have known that the limitation period for the principal claim has expired, still provides a suretyship or bears suretyship liability, and subsequently refuses to bear suretyship liability or requests the return of property on the ground that the limitation period has expired, the People's Court shall not uphold such refusal or request. If, after bearing the suretyship liability, the surety seeks recourse against the debtor, the People's Court shall not uphold such recourse, unless the debtor has waived the limitation defense.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　第三人向债权人提供差额补足、流动性支持等类似承诺文件作为增信措施，具有提供担保的意思表示，债权人请求第三人承担保证责任的，人民法院应当依照保证的有关规定处理。  



**Article 36** If a third party provides commitment documents such as shortfall guarantees, liquidity support, or similar instruments to the creditor as a credit enhancement measure, and such documents express the intent to provide a security, and the creditor requests the third party to bear suretyship liability, the People's Court shall deal with the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions on suretyship.  
If a commitment document provided by a third party to the creditor expresses the intent to join in the debt or to bear the debt jointly with the debtor, the People's Court shall find it to be a debt joinder as provided in Article 552 of the Civil Code.  
If it is difficult to determine whether the commitment document provided by the third party under the preceding two paragraphs constitutes a suretyship or a debt joinder, the People's Court shall find it to be a suretyship.  
If a commitment document provided by a third party to the creditor does not meet the circumstances set out in the preceding three paragraphs, and the creditor requests the third party to bear suretyship liability or joint and several liability, the People's Court shall not uphold such request, provided that this shall not affect the creditor's right to request the third party to perform agreed obligations or bear corresponding civil liability based on the commitment document.  
III.  
Security Interest (a) Validity of Security Contracts and Security Interest

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　当事人以所有权、使用权不明或者有争议的财产抵押，经审查构成无权处分的，人民法院应当依照[民法典第三百一十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-311)的规定处理。  



**Article 37** If a party mortgages property whose ownership or right of use is unclear or disputed, and upon examination it is found to constitute a disposal without authority, the People's Court shall deal with the matter in accordance with Article 311 of the Civil Code. If a party mortgages property that has been sealed up or distrained according to law, and the mortgagee requests to exercise the mortgage right, and upon examination it is found that the measure of sealing up or distraining has been lifted, the People's Court shall uphold such request. If the mortgagor claims that the mortgage contract is void on the ground that the property was sealed up or distrained at the time the mortgage right was created, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim. If property under supervision according to law is mortgaged, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　主债权未受全部清偿，担保物权人主张就担保财产的全部行使担保物权的，人民法院应予支持,但是留置权人行使留置权的，应当依照[民法典第四百五十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-450)的规定处理。  



**Article 38** If the principal claim has not been fully satisfied, and the security interest holder claims to exercise the security interest over the entire secured property, the People's Court shall uphold such claim; provided that if a lien holder exercises the lien right, this shall be dealt with in accordance with Article 450 of the Civil Code.  
If the secured property has been divided or partially transferred, and the security interest holder claims to exercise the security interest over the secured property after the division or transfer, the People's Court shall uphold such claim, unless otherwise provided by law or judicial interpretation.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　主债权被分割或者部分转让，各债权人主张就其享有的债权份额行使担保物权的，人民法院应予支持，但是法律另有规定或者当事人另有约定的除外。  



**Article 39** If the principal claim is divided or partially transferred, and each creditor claims to exercise the security interest on the share of the claim it holds, the People's Court shall uphold such claim, unless otherwise provided by law or otherwise agreed by the parties. If the principal debt is divided or partially transferred, and the debtor itself provides a property guarantee, and the creditor requests that the guaranteed property secure the performance of all debts, the People's Court shall uphold such request. If a third party provides a property guarantee and claims that it shall no longer bear the guarantee liability for the debt transferred without its written consent, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　从物产生于抵押权依法设立前，抵押权人主张抵押权的效力及于从物的，人民法院应予支持，但是当事人另有约定的除外。  



**Article 40** If an accessory thing arises before a mortgage right is created according to law, and the mortgagee claims that the effect of the mortgage right extends to the accessory thing, the People's Court shall uphold such claim, unless otherwise agreed by the parties.  
If an accessory thing arises after a mortgage right is created according to law, and the mortgagee claims that the effect of the mortgage right extends to the accessory thing, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim; provided that the accessory thing may be disposed of together when the mortgage right is realized.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　抵押权依法设立后，抵押财产被添附，添附物归第三人所有，抵押权人主张抵押权效力及于补偿金的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 41** After a mortgage right is established according to law, if the mortgaged property is accreted and the accreted thing belongs to a third party, and the mortgagee claims that the effect of the mortgage right extends to the compensation, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
After a mortgage right is established according to law, if the mortgaged property is accreted and the mortgagor has ownership of the accreted thing, and the mortgagee claims that the effect of the mortgage right extends to the accreted thing, the People's Court shall support such claim; provided that if the accretion increases the value of the mortgaged property, the effect of the mortgage right shall not extend to the increased portion of value.  
After a mortgage right is established according to law, if the mortgagor and a third party become co-owners of the accreted thing due to accretion, and the mortgagee claims that the effect of the mortgage right extends to the share of the mortgagor in the co-owned property, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
For the purposes of this Article, accretion includes attachment, mixture, and processing.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　抵押权依法设立后，抵押财产毁损、灭失或者被征收等，抵押权人请求按照原抵押权的顺位就保险金、赔偿金或者补偿金等优先受偿的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 42** After a mortgage right is established according to law, if the mortgaged property is damaged, destroyed, or requisitioned, and the mortgagee requests to receive priority payment from the insurance proceeds, compensation, or indemnity in accordance with the original ranking of the mortgage right, the People's Court shall support such request.  
If the obligor of payment has already paid the insurance proceeds, compensation, or indemnity to the mortgagor, and the mortgagee requests the obligor of payment to pay such proceeds, compensation, or indemnity to the mortgagee, the People's Court shall not support such request; provided that this shall not apply where the obligor of payment, after receiving notice from the mortgagee demanding payment to the mortgagee, still makes payment to the mortgagor.  
Where the mortgagee requests the obligor of payment to pay the insurance proceeds, compensation, or indemnity to the mortgagee, the People's Court may notify the mortgagor to participate in the action as a third party.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　当事人约定禁止或者限制转让抵押财产但是未将约定登记，抵押人违反约定转让抵押财产，抵押权人请求确认转让合同无效的，人民法院不予支持；抵押财产已经交付或者登记，抵押权人请求确认转让不发生物权效力的，人民法院不予支持，但是抵押权人有证据证明受让人知道的除外；抵押权人请求抵押人承担违约责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。  



**Article 43** Where the parties have agreed to prohibit or restrict the transfer of the mortgaged property but have not registered such agreement, and the mortgagor transfers the mortgaged property in violation of the agreement, if the mortgagee requests confirmation that the transfer contract is void, the People's Court shall not support such request; if the mortgaged property has been delivered or registered, and the mortgagee requests confirmation that the transfer has no effect in real right, the People's Court shall not support such request, unless the mortgagee has evidence proving that the transferee had knowledge of the agreement; if the mortgagee requests the mortgagor to bear liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.  
Where the parties have agreed to prohibit or restrict the transfer of the mortgaged property and have registered such agreement, and the mortgagor transfers the mortgaged property in violation of the agreement, if the mortgagee requests confirmation that the transfer contract is void, the People's Court shall not support such request; if the mortgaged property has been delivered or registered, and the mortgagee claims that the transfer has no effect in real right, the People's Court shall support such claim, unless the mortgage right is extinguished because the transferee has discharged the debt on behalf of the debtor.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　主债权诉讼时效期间届满后，抵押权人主张行使抵押权的，人民法院不予支持；抵押人以主债权诉讼时效期间届满为由，主张不承担担保责任的，人民法院应予支持。主债权诉讼时效期间届满前，债权人仅对债务人提起诉讼，经人民法院判决或者调解后未在民事诉讼法规定的申请执行时效期间内对债务人申请强制执行，其向抵押人主张行使抵押权的，人民法院不予支持。  



**Article 44** After the limitation period for the principal claim has expired, if the mortgagee claims to exercise the mortgage right, the People's Court shall not support such claim; if the mortgagor claims that it shall not bear security liability on the ground that the limitation period for the principal claim has expired, the People's Court shall support such claim. If before the expiration of the limitation period for the principal claim, the creditor only initiates an action against the debtor, and after a judgment or mediation by the People's Court, does not apply for compulsory enforcement against the debtor within the time limit for applying for enforcement provided in the Civil Procedure Law, and then claims to exercise the mortgage right against the mortgagor, the People's Court shall not support such claim. After the limitation period for the principal claim has expired, if the debtor whose property has been subject to a lien or a third party who has ownership of the property subject to the lien requests the creditor to return the property subject to the lien, the People's Court shall not support such request; if the debtor or third party requests an auction or sale of the property subject to the lien and payment of the debt from the proceeds obtained, the People's Court shall support such request. Regarding the legal consequences of the expiration of the limitation period for the principal claim, a pledge of rights for which registration is the method of publicity shall apply mutatis mutandis the provisions of paragraph 1; a pledge of movable property and a pledge of rights for which delivery of a title document is the method of publicity shall apply mutatis mutandis the provisions of paragraph 2.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　当事人约定当债务人不履行到期债务或者发生当事人约定的实现担保物权的情形,担保物权人有权将担保财产自行拍卖、变卖并就所得的价款优先受偿的，该约定有效。因担保人的原因导致担保物权人无法自行对担保财产进行拍卖、变卖，担保物权人请求担保人承担因此增加的费用的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 45** Where the parties agree that, when the debtor fails to perform an obligation upon maturity or the circumstances agreed upon by the parties for enforcing the security interest occur, the secured party has the right to self-auction or self-sell the secured property and receive priority payment from the proceeds obtained, such agreement shall be valid. If due to reasons attributable to the grantor of the security, the secured party is unable to self-auction or self-sell the secured property, and the secured party requests the grantor of the security to bear the costs incurred as a result, the People's Court shall support such request. Where a party applies to the People's Court for an auction or sale of the secured property in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law concerning 'cases of enforcing security interests,' and the respondent requests the dismissal of the application on the ground that the security contract contains an arbitration clause, the People's Court, after review, shall handle the matter according to the following circumstances: (1) If the parties have no substantive dispute over the security interest and the conditions for enforcing the security interest have been fulfilled, the People's Court shall rule to permit the auction or sale of the secured property; (2) If the parties have a partial substantive dispute over the enforcement of the security interest, the People's Court may rule to permit the auction or sale of the secured property with respect to the undisputed part and inform the parties that they may apply for arbitration with respect to the disputed part; (3) If the parties have a substantive dispute over the enforcement of the security interest, the People's Court shall rule to dismiss the application and inform the parties that they may apply for arbitration with an arbitration institution. Where a creditor exercises a security interest by way of litigation, the debtor and the grantor of the security shall be named as co-defendants. (2) Mortgage of Immovable Property

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　不动产抵押合同生效后未办理抵押登记手续，债权人请求抵押人办理抵押登记手续的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 46** After an immovable property mortgage contract takes effect, if the mortgage registration procedures have not been completed, and the creditor requests the mortgagor to complete the mortgage registration procedures, the People's Court shall support such request.  
If the mortgaged property is destroyed or requisitioned for reasons not attributable to the mortgagor itself, making it impossible to complete the mortgage registration, and the creditor requests the mortgagor to bear liability within the agreed scope of security, the People's Court shall not support such request; provided that if the mortgagor has obtained insurance proceeds, compensation, or indemnity, and the creditor requests the mortgagor to bear compensation liability within the amount obtained, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.  
If it is impossible to complete the mortgage registration due to the mortgagor's transfer of the mortgaged property or other reasons attributable to the mortgagor itself, and the creditor requests the mortgagor to bear liability within the agreed scope of security, the People's Court shall support such request according to law, provided that such liability shall not exceed the scope of liability that the mortgagor should bear had the mortgage right been able to be established.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　不动产登记簿就抵押财产、被担保的债权范围等所作的记载与抵押合同约定不一致的，人民法院应当根据登记簿的记载确定抵押财产、被担保的债权范围等事项。



**Article 47** If the entries in the immovable property register regarding the mortgaged property, the scope of the secured claim, etc., are inconsistent with the provisions of the mortgage contract, the People's Court shall determine the matters of the mortgaged property, the scope of the secured claim, etc., based on the entries in the register.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　当事人申请办理抵押登记手续时，因登记机构的过错致使其不能办理抵押登记，当事人请求登记机构承担赔偿责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。



**Article 48** Where, at the time a party applies for completing mortgage registration procedures, the party is unable to complete the mortgage registration due to the fault of the registration authority, and the party requests the registration authority to bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　以违法的建筑物抵押的，抵押合同无效，但是一审法庭辩论终结前已经办理合法手续的除外。抵押合同无效的法律后果，依照[本解释第十七条](#art-17)的有关规定处理。  



**Article 49** Where a mortgage is created on an illegal building, the mortgage contract shall be void, unless the legal procedures have been completed before the conclusion of the court debate at first instance. The legal consequences of the void mortgage contract shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of Article 17 of this Interpretation. If a party creates a mortgage on the right to the use of construction land according to law, and the mortgagor claims the mortgage contract is void on the ground that an illegal building exists on the land, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　抵押人以划拨建设用地上的建筑物抵押，当事人以该建设用地使用权不能抵押或者未办理批准手续为由主张抵押合同无效或者不生效的，人民法院不予支持。抵押权依法实现时，拍卖、变卖建筑物所得的价款，应当优先用于补缴建设用地使用权出让金。  



**Article 50** Where a mortgagor creates a mortgage on a building on land allocated for construction use, and a party claims the mortgage contract is void or does not take effect on the ground that the right to the use of allocated construction land cannot be mortgaged or the approval procedures have not been completed, the People's Court shall not support such claim. When the mortgage right is enforced according to law, the proceeds from the auction or sale of the building shall be used first to make up the grant fee for the right to the use of construction land. Where a party creates a mortgage on a right to the use of construction land obtained through allocation, and the mortgagor claims the mortgage contract is void or does not take effect on the ground that the approval procedures have not been completed, the People's Court shall not support such claim. If the mortgage registration has been completed according to law, and the mortgagee claims to exercise the mortgage right, the People's Court shall support such claim. The proceeds obtained when the mortgage right is enforced according to law shall be handled by reference to the relevant provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　当事人仅以建设用地使用权抵押，债权人主张抵押权的效力及于土地上已有的建筑物以及正在建造的建筑物已完成部分的，人民法院应予支持。债权人主张抵押权的效力及于正在建造的建筑物的续建部分以及新增建筑物的，人民法院不予支持。  



**Article 51** Where a party only mortgages the right to use construction land, and a creditor claims that the effect of the mortgage extends to the existing buildings on the land as well as the completed parts of the buildings under construction, the People's Court shall support such claim. Where a creditor claims that the effect of the mortgage extends to the parts of buildings under construction that are to be continued and newly built buildings, the People's Court shall not support such claim. Where a party mortgages a building under construction, the scope of effect of the mortgage shall be limited to the part for which the mortgage has been registered. Where, in accordance with the provisions of the security contract, a party claims that the effect of the mortgage extends to the parts to be continued, newly built buildings, and buildings planned but not yet constructed, the People's Court shall not support such claim. Where a mortgagor mortgages the right to use construction land, buildings on the land, or buildings under construction to different creditors separately, the People's Court shall determine the order of satisfaction according to the chronological order of mortgage registration.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　当事人办理抵押预告登记后，预告登记权利人请求就抵押财产优先受偿，经审查存在尚未办理建筑物所有权首次登记、预告登记的财产与办理建筑物所有权首次登记时的财产不一致、抵押预告登记已经失效等情形，导致不具备办理抵押登记条件的，人民法院不予支持；经审查已经办理建筑物所有权首次登记，且不存在预告登记失效等情形的，人民法院应予支持，并应当认定抵押权自预告登记之日起设立。  



**Article 52** After a party has registered a mortgage advance notice, the holder of the advance notice registration requests priority satisfaction from the mortgaged property. If upon examination, it is found that there are circumstances such as the failure to undergo the first registration of ownership of the building, the property subject to the advance notice registration is inconsistent with the property at the time of the first registration of ownership of the building, or the mortgage advance notice registration has become invalid, resulting in the conditions for mortgage registration not being met, the People's Court shall not support the claim. If upon examination, the first registration of ownership of the building has been completed, and there is no circumstance such as invalidity of the advance notice registration, the People's Court shall support the claim, and shall determine that the mortgage right is established from the date of the mortgage advance notice registration. Where a party has registered a mortgage advance notice, and the mortgagor becomes bankrupt, and upon examination the mortgaged property is found to be part of the bankruptcy estate, and the holder of the advance notice registration claims priority satisfaction from the mortgaged property, the People's Court shall support such claim within the value of the mortgaged property at the time of accepting the bankruptcy application, except where, within one year before the acceptance of the bankruptcy application by the People's Court, the debtor has established a mortgage advance notice for a debt that was previously without property security.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　当事人在动产和权利担保合同中对担保财产进行概括描述，该描述能够合理识别担保财产的，人民法院应当认定担保成立。



**Article 53** Where a party provides a general description of the secured property in a security contract for movables and rights, and such description enables the reasonable identification of the secured property, the People's Court shall determine that the security is established.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　动产抵押合同订立后未办理抵押登记，动产抵押权的效力按照下列情形分别处理：  



**Article 54** Where a mortgage contract for movables is concluded but the mortgage is not registered, the effect of the mortgage on movables shall be dealt with according to the following circumstances: (1) Where the mortgagor transfers the mortgaged property, and the transferee takes possession of the mortgaged property, if the mortgagee requests the transferee to exercise the mortgage right, the People's Court shall not support such request, except where the mortgagee can adduce evidence proving that the transferee knew or should have known that a mortgage contract had been concluded; (2) Where the mortgagor leases the mortgaged property to another person and transfers possession, and the mortgagee exercises the mortgage right, the lease relationship shall not be affected, except where the mortgagee can adduce evidence proving that the lessee knew or should have known that a mortgage contract had been concluded; (3) Where another creditor of the mortgagor applies to the People's Court for preservation or execution of the mortgaged property, and the People's Court has made a property preservation ruling or taken enforcement measures, if the mortgagee claims priority satisfaction from the mortgaged property, the People's Court shall not support such claim; (4) Where the mortgagor becomes bankrupt, and the mortgagee claims priority satisfaction from the mortgaged property, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　债权人、出质人与监管人订立三方协议，出质人以通过一定数量、品种等概括描述能够确定范围的货物为债务的履行提供担保，当事人有证据证明监管人系受债权人的委托监管并实际控制该货物的，人民法院应当认定质权于监管人实际控制货物之日起设立。监管人违反约定向出质人或者其他人放货、因保管不善导致货物毁损灭失，债权人请求监管人承担违约责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。  



**Article 55** Where a creditor, a pledgor, and a custodian conclude a tripartite agreement, the pledgor provides a guarantee for the performance of the debt with goods that can be identified in scope through a general description such as a certain quantity or type, and a party has evidence proving that the custodian is entrusted by the creditor to supervise and actually controls the goods, the People's Court shall determine that the pledge is established from the date the custodian actually controls the goods. Where the custodian releases the goods to the pledgor or others in violation of the agreement, or causes damage or loss to the goods due to improper custody, if the creditor requests the custodian to bear liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support such request according to law. In the circumstances set forth in the preceding paragraph, if a party has evidence proving that the custodian is entrusted by the pledgor to supervise the goods, or although entrusted by the creditor, does not actually perform the supervision duties, resulting in the goods still being actually controlled by the pledgor, the People's Court shall determine that the pledge is not established. The creditor may request the pledgor to bear liability for breach of contract in accordance with the provisions of the pledge contract, but such liability shall not exceed the scope of liability that the pledgor should bear if the pledge had been validly established. Where the custodian fails to perform the supervision duties, and the creditor requests the custodian to bear liability, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　买受人在出卖人正常经营活动中通过支付合理对价取得已被设立担保物权的动产，担保物权人请求就该动产优先受偿的，人民法院不予支持，但是有下列情形之一的除外：  



**Article 56** Where a buyer acquires, in the ordinary course of business of the seller, by paying a reasonable price, a movable on which a security interest has been established, and the holder of the security interest requests priority satisfaction from such movable, the People's Court shall not support such request, except in any of the following circumstances: (1) The quantity of goods purchased significantly exceeds that of an ordinary buyer; (2) The purchase is of the seller's production equipment; (3) The purpose of concluding the sales contract is to guarantee the performance of a debt by the seller or a third party; (4) A direct or indirect control relationship exists between the buyer and the seller; (5) Other circumstances where the buyer should have searched the mortgage registration but failed to do so. The term 'ordinary course of business of the seller' as set forth in the preceding paragraph means that the seller's business activities fall within the business scope explicitly stated in its business license, and the seller continuously sells the same type of goods. The term 'holder of the security interest' as set forth in the preceding paragraph refers to a mortgagee who has undergone registration, a seller in a sale with retention of title, or a lessor in a financial lease contract.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　担保人在设立动产浮动抵押并办理抵押登记后又购入或者以融资租赁方式承租新的动产，下列权利人为担保价款债权或者租金的实现而订立担保合同，并在该动产交付后十日内办理登记，主张其权利优先于在先设立的浮动抵押权的，人民法院应予支持：  



**Article 57** After a security provider has established a floating mortgage on movables and registered such mortgage, if the security provider subsequently acquires or leases under a financial lease arrangement new movables, and the following right holders, for the purpose of securing the realization of the purchase price claim or the rent, conclude a security contract and undergo registration within ten days after the delivery of such movables, claiming that their rights are prioritized over the previously established floating mortgage, the People's Court shall support such claim: (1) The seller who establishes a mortgage on or retains title to such movables; (2) A creditor who, to provide financing for the payment of the purchase price, establishes a mortgage on such movables; (3) The lessor who leases such movables under a financial lease arrangement. Where a buyer acquires movables but does not pay the full purchase price, or a lessee takes possession of the leased property under a financial lease arrangement but does not pay the full rent, and subsequently establishes a security interest on the subject matter in favor of another person, if the right holders listed in the preceding paragraph, for the purpose of securing the realization of the purchase price claim or the rent, conclude a security contract and undergo registration within ten days after the delivery of such movables, claiming that their rights are prioritized over the security interest established by the buyer in favor of another person, the People's Court shall support such claim. Where multiple purchase money security interests exist on the same movable, the People's Court shall determine the order of satisfaction according to the chronological order of registration.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　以汇票出质，当事人以背书记载“质押”字样并在汇票上签章，汇票已经交付质权人的，人民法院应当认定质权自汇票交付质权人时设立。



**Article 58** Where a bill of exchange is pledged, and a party endorses the bill of exchange with the notation 'pledge' and signs on the bill of exchange, and the bill of exchange has been delivered to the pledgee, the People's Court shall determine that the pledge is established from the date the bill of exchange is delivered to the pledgee.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　存货人或者仓单持有人在仓单上以背书记载“质押”字样，并经保管人签章，仓单已经交付质权人的，人民法院应当认定质权自仓单交付质权人时设立。没有权利凭证的仓单，依法可以办理出质登记的，仓单质权自办理出质登记时设立。  



**Article 59** Where the depositor or the holder of a warehouse receipt endorses the warehouse receipt with the notation 'pledge' and the keeper signs it, and the warehouse receipt has been delivered to the pledgee, the People's Court shall determine that the pledge is established from the date the warehouse receipt is delivered to the pledgee. For a warehouse receipt without a title document, if the pledge registration may be conducted according to law, the pledge on the warehouse receipt shall be established from the date of the pledge registration. Where the pledgor pledges both the warehouse receipt and establishes a security on the stored goods, the order of satisfaction shall be determined according to the chronological order of publicity; if the order is difficult to determine, satisfaction shall be made in proportion to the claims. Where the keeper issues multiple warehouse receipts for the same goods, and the pledgor establishes multiple pledges on multiple warehouse receipts, the order of satisfaction shall be determined according to the chronological order of publicity; if the order is difficult to determine, satisfaction shall be made in proportion to the claims. In the circumstances set forth in the second and third paragraphs, if the creditor adduces evidence to prove that its losses are caused by the joint conduct of the pledgor and the keeper, and requests the pledgor and the keeper to bear joint and several compensation liability, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　在跟单信用证交易中，开证行与开证申请人之间约定以提单作为担保的，人民法院应当依照民法典关于质权的有关规定处理。  



**Article 60** In a documentary credit transaction, where an agreement is made between the issuing bank and the applicant for the credit that a bill of lading shall serve as security, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Code regarding pledges. In a documentary credit transaction, where the issuing bank holds a bill of lading in accordance with its agreement with the applicant for the credit or the practice of documentary credits, and the applicant for the credit fails to make payment and obtain the bill of lading as agreed, if the issuing bank claims priority satisfaction from the goods under the bill of lading, the People's Court shall support such claim. Where the issuing bank claims ownership of the goods under the bill of lading, the People's Court shall not support such claim. In a documentary credit transaction, where the issuing bank obtains payment by transferring the bill of lading or the goods under the bill of lading in accordance with its agreement with the applicant for the credit or the practice of documentary credits, if the applicant for the credit requests the return of the amount exceeding the claim, the People's Court shall support such request. The provisions of the preceding three paragraphs shall not affect the right of the issuing bank that lawfully holds a bill of lading to assert its rights under the contract of carriage in its capacity as the holder of the bill of lading.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　以现有的应收账款出质，应收账款债务人向质权人确认应收账款的真实性后，又以应收账款不存在或者已经消灭为由主张不承担责任的，人民法院不予支持。  



**Article 61** Where an existing account receivable is pledged, and after the debtor of the account receivable confirms the authenticity of the account receivable to the pledgee, the debtor of the account receivable claims that it shall not bear liability on the ground that the account receivable does not exist or has been extinguished, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
Where an existing account receivable is pledged, and the debtor of the account receivable has not confirmed the authenticity of the account receivable, if the pledgee, taking the debtor of the account receivable as the defendant, requests priority in satisfaction from the account receivable and can adduce evidence to prove that the account receivable existed at the time of the pledge registration, the People's Court shall support such request; if the pledgee cannot adduce evidence to prove that the account receivable existed at the time of the pledge registration and merely requests priority in satisfaction from the account receivable on the ground that the pledge registration has been completed, the People's Court shall not support such request.  
Where an existing account receivable is pledged, and the debtor of the account receivable has already performed the debt to the creditor of the account receivable, if the pledgee requests the debtor of the account receivable to perform the debt, the People's Court shall not support such request, except where the debtor of the account receivable, after receiving a notice from the pledgee demanding performance to it, nonetheless performs to the creditor of the account receivable.  
Where the right to income from infrastructure and public utility projects, a claim arising from the provision of services or labor, or other future accounts receivable is pledged, and the parties establish a specific account for the account receivable, when a statutory or agreed event for the realization of the pledge right occurs, if the pledgee requests priority in satisfaction from the funds in such specific account, the People's Court shall support such request; where the funds in the specific account are insufficient to satisfy the debt or no specific account has been established, if the pledgee requests a valuation or auction or sale of the right to project income or other future accounts receivable and priority in satisfaction from the proceeds obtained, the People's Court shall support such request in accordance with law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　债务人不履行到期债务，债权人因同一法律关系留置合法占有的第三人的动产，并主张就该留置财产优先受偿的，人民法院应予支持。第三人以该留置财产并非债务人的财产为由请求返还的，人民法院不予支持。  



**Article 62** Where the debtor fails to perform an obligation as it falls due, and the creditor, under the same legal relationship, retains a chattel of a third party which it legally possesses and claims priority in satisfaction from such retained property, the People's Court shall support such claim. If the third party requests the return of the retained property on the ground that such property is not the debtor's property, the People's Court shall not support such request. Where the chattel retained between enterprises is not under the same legal relationship as the claim, if the debtor requests the creditor to return the retained property on the ground that the claim is not a claim arising in the normal course of the enterprise's business, the People's Court shall support such request. Where the chattel retained between enterprises is not under the same legal relationship as the claim, and the creditor retains the property of a third party, if the third party requests the creditor to return the retained property, the People's Court shall support such request. IV. Atypical Security

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　债权人与担保人订立担保合同，约定以法律、行政法规尚未规定可以担保的财产权利设立担保，当事人主张合同无效的，人民法院不予支持。当事人未在法定的登记机构依法进行登记，主张该担保具有物权效力的，人民法院不予支持。



**Article 63** Where a creditor and a security provider enter into a security contract stipulating the creation of security with property rights for which security is not yet provided by any law or administrative regulation, if a party claims that the contract is void, the People's Court shall not support such claim. If a party, without having registered with the statutory registration authority according to law, claims that such security has the effect of a real right, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　在所有权保留买卖中，出卖人依法有权取回标的物，但是与买受人协商不成，当事人请求参照民事诉讼法“实现担保物权案件”的有关规定，拍卖、变卖标的物的，人民法院应予准许。  



**Article 64** In a sale with retention of title, where the seller is entitled by law to retake the subject matter but fails to reach an agreement with the buyer through negotiation, if a party requests a reference to the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law on "cases for the realization of security interests" for the auction or sale of the subject matter, the People's Court shall permit such request.  
If the seller requests to retake the subject matter and such request satisfies the provisions of Article 642 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall support such request; if the buyer, by way of defense or counterclaim, advocates the auction or sale of the subject matter and the return of the remaining amount after deducting the unpaid price and necessary expenses, the People's Court shall handle such matters together.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　在融资租赁合同中，承租人未按照约定支付租金，经催告后在合理期限内仍不支付，出租人请求承租人支付全部剩余租金，并以拍卖、变卖租赁物所得的价款受偿的，人民法院应予支持；当事人请求参照民事诉讼法“实现担保物权案件”的有关规定，以拍卖、变卖租赁物所得价款支付租金的，人民法院应予准许。  



**Article 65** In a financial leasing contract, where the lessee fails to pay the rent as agreed and, after a demand for payment, still fails to pay within a reasonable period, if the lessor requests the lessee to pay all remaining rent and to be satisfied from the proceeds of an auction or sale of the leased property, the People's Court shall support such request; if a party requests a reference to the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law on "cases for the realization of security interests" for the payment of rent from the proceeds of an auction or sale of the leased property, the People's Court shall permit such request. If the lessor requests the dissolution of the financial leasing contract and the repossession of the leased property, and the lessee, by way of defense or counterclaim, advocates the return of the portion of the value of the leased property that exceeds the arrears of rent and other expenses, the People's Court shall handle such matters together. Where the parties dispute the value of the leased property, the value thereof shall be determined in accordance with the following rules: (1) if the financial leasing contract provides therefor, in accordance with such provision; (2) if the financial leasing contract does not provide therefor or the provision is unclear, in accordance with the agreed depreciation of the leased property and the residual value of the leased property after the expiration of the contract; (3) if the value is still difficult to determine according to the methods provided in the preceding two subparagraphs, or if a party believes that the value determined according to the methods provided in the preceding two subparagraphs seriously deviates from the actual value of the leased property, a qualified institution shall be entrusted for appraisal upon a party's application.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　同一应收账款同时存在保理、应收账款质押和债权转让，当事人主张参照[民法典第七百六十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-768)的规定确定优先顺序的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 66** Where factoring, pledge of accounts receivable, and assignment of claims exist simultaneously in respect of the same account receivable, if a party advocates determining the priority order by reference to the provisions of Article 768 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall support such advocacy.  
In factoring with recourse, if the factor initiates a lawsuit against the creditor of the account receivable or the debtor of the account receivable as the defendant, the People's Court shall accept the case; if the factor initiates a lawsuit against both the creditor of the account receivable and the debtor of the account receivable together, the People's Court may accept the case.  
After the creditor of the account receivable has returned the principal and interest of the factoring financing or repurchased the account receivable claim to the factor, if the creditor requests the debtor of the account receivable to perform the account receivable obligation to it, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　在所有权保留买卖、融资租赁等合同中，出卖人、出租人的所有权未经登记不得对抗的“善意第三人”的范围及其效力，参照[本解释第五十四条](#art-54)的规定处理。



**Article 67** In contracts such as sales with retention of title and financial leasing, the scope and effect of a "bona fide third party" against whom the ownership of the seller or lessor may not be asserted if not registered shall be dealt with by reference to the provisions of Article 54 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　债务人或者第三人与债权人约定将财产形式上转移至债权人名下，债务人不履行到期债务，债权人有权对财产折价或者以拍卖、变卖该财产所得价款偿还债务的，人民法院应当认定该约定有效。当事人已经完成财产权利变动的公示，债务人不履行到期债务，债权人请求参照民法典关于担保物权的有关规定就该财产优先受偿的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 68** Where a debtor or a third party agrees with a creditor to formally transfer property into the creditor's name, and if the debtor fails to perform the obligation as it falls due, the creditor is entitled to have the property valued or to have the debt satisfied from the proceeds of an auction or sale of such property, the People's Court shall find such agreement valid. If the parties have completed the publicity of the change in the property right, and the debtor fails to perform the obligation as it falls due, and the creditor requests, by reference to the relevant provisions of the Civil Code on security interests, priority in satisfaction from such property, the People's Court shall support such request. Where a debtor or a third party agrees with a creditor to formally transfer property into the creditor's name, and if the debtor fails to perform the obligation as it falls due, the property shall belong to the creditor, the People's Court shall find such agreement void, provided that the validity of the parties' manifestation of intent to provide security shall not be affected. If the parties have completed the publicity of the change in the property right, and the debtor fails to perform the obligation as it falls due, and the creditor requests ownership of such property, the People's Court shall not support such request; if the creditor requests, by reference to the provisions of the Civil Code on security interests, to have the property valued or to obtain priority in satisfaction from the proceeds of an auction or sale of such property, the People's Court shall support such request; if, after the debtor performs the obligation, it requests the return of the property, or requests that the debt be satisfied from the proceeds of a valuation or auction or sale of the property, the People's Court shall support such request. Where a debtor and a creditor agree to transfer property into the creditor's name, with a provision that after a certain period, the debtor or a designated third party shall repurchase it at the transaction principal plus a premium, and if the debtor fails to perform the repurchase obligation when due, the property shall belong to the creditor, the People's Court shall deal with the matter by reference to the provisions of the second paragraph. If the object of repurchase never exists from the outset, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 146 of the Civil Code, deal with the matter according to the legal relationship actually constituted thereby.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　股东以将其股权转移至债权人名下的方式为债务履行提供担保，公司或者公司的债权人以股东未履行或者未全面履行出资义务、抽逃出资等为由，请求作为名义股东的债权人与股东承担连带责任的，人民法院不予支持。



**Article 69** Where a shareholder provides security for the performance of an obligation by transferring its equity interests to the name of a creditor, if the company or a creditor of the company requests the creditor, as the nominal shareholder, to bear joint and several liability with the shareholder on the ground that the shareholder has failed to perform or has not fully performed its capital contribution obligation, or has withdrawn its capital contribution, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　债务人或者第三人为担保债务的履行，设立专门的保证金账户并由债权人实际控制，或者将其资金存入债权人设立的保证金账户，债权人主张就账户内的款项优先受偿的，人民法院应予支持。当事人以保证金账户内的款项浮动为由，主张实际控制该账户的债权人对账户内的款项不享有优先受偿权的，人民法院不予支持。  



**Article 70** Where a debtor or a third party, for the purpose of securing the performance of an obligation, establishes a dedicated margin account and has it actually controlled by the creditor, or deposits its funds into a margin account established by the creditor, if the creditor claims priority in satisfaction from the funds in the account, the People's Court shall support such claim. If a party claims that the creditor who actually controls the account does not have the right to priority in satisfaction from the funds in the account on the ground that the funds in the margin account fluctuate, the People's Court shall not support such claim. A margin sub-account established under a bank account shall be dealt with by reference to the provisions of the preceding paragraph. Where the margin agreed upon by the parties is not established for the purpose of securing the performance of an obligation, or the circumstances do not conform to those provided in the preceding two paragraphs, if the creditor claims priority in satisfaction from the margin, the People's Court shall not support such claim, provided that this shall not affect the party's right to assert its rights in accordance with the provisions of law or the agreement of the parties. V. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　本解释自2021年1月1日起施行。
