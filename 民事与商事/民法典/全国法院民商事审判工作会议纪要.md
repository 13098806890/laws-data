# 全国法院民商事审判工作会议纪要

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法典  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法〔2019〕254号  
**公布日期**：2019-11-14

> 《全国法院民商事审判工作会议纪要》（以下简称《会议纪要》）已于2019年9月11日经最高人民法院审判委员会民事行政专业委员会第319次会议原则通过。为便于进一步学习领会和正确适用《会议纪要》，特作如下通知： 一、充分认识《会议纪要》出台的意义 《会议纪要》针对民商事审判中的前沿疑难争议问题，在广泛征求各方面意见的基础上，经最高人民法院审判委员会民事行政专业委员会讨论决定。《会议纪要》的出台，对统一裁判思路，规范法官自由裁量权，增强民商事审判的公开性、透明度以及可预期性，提高司法公信力具有重要意义。各级人民法院要正确把握和理解适用《会议纪要》的精神实质和基本内容。 二、及时组织学习培训 为使各级人民法院尽快准确理解掌握《会议纪要》的内涵，在案件审理中正确理解适用，各级人民法院要在妥善处理好工学关系的前提下，通过多种形式组织学习培训，做好宣传工作。 三、准确把握《会议纪要》的应用范围 纪要不是司法解释，不能作为裁判依据进行援引。《会议纪要》发布后，人民法院尚未审结的一审、二审案件，在裁判文书“本院认为”部分具体分析法律适用的理由时，可以根据《会议纪要》的相关规定进行说理。 对于适用中存在的问题,请层报最高人民法院。 最高人民法院 2019年11月8日 全国法院民商事审判工作会议纪要 目录 引言 一、关于民法总则适用的法律衔接 二、关于公司纠纷案件的审理

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　【民法总则与民法通则的关系及其适用】民法通则既规定了民法的一些基本制度和一般性规则，也规定了合同、所有权及其他财产权、知识产权、民事责任、涉外民事法律关系适用等具体内容。民法总则基本吸收了民法通则规定的基本制度和一般性规则，同时作了补充、完善和发展。民法通则规定的合同、所有权及其他财产权、民事责任等具体内容还需要在编撰民法典各分编时作进一步统筹，系统整合。因民法总则施行后暂不废止民法通则，在此之前，民法总则与民法通则规定不一致的，根据新的规定优于旧的规定的法律适用规则，适用民法总则的规定。最高人民法院已依据民法总则制定了关于诉讼时效问题的司法解释，而原依据民法通则制定的关于诉讼时效的司法解释，只要与民法总则不冲突，仍可适用。

**Article 1** [Relationship between the General Part of Civil Law and the General Principles of Civil Law and Their Application] The General Principles of Civil Law provides both some basic systems and general rules of civil law, and specific contents such as contracts, ownership and other property rights, intellectual property, civil liability, and the application of laws in foreign-related civil legal relationships. The General Part of Civil Law has essentially absorbed the basic systems and general rules provided in the General Principles of Civil Law, while making supplements, improvements, and developments. The specific contents provided in the General Principles of Civil Law, such as contracts, ownership and other property rights, and civil liability, still need to be further coordinated and systematically integrated when compiling the individual parts of the Civil Code. Since the General Principles of Civil Law will not be abolished temporarily after the General Part of Civil Law comes into force, where the provisions of the General Part of Civil Law are inconsistent with those of the General Principles of Civil Law before that time, the provisions of the General Part of Civil Law shall apply according to the rule of application of law that new provisions prevail over old ones. The Supreme People's Court has formulated a judicial interpretation on the issue of the limitation of action based on the General Part of Civil Law, while the original judicial interpretation on the limitation of action formulated based on the General Principles of Civil Law may still be applied as long as it does not conflict with the General Part of Civil Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　【民法总则与合同法的关系及其适用】根据民法典编撰工作“两步走”的安排，民法总则施行后，目前正在进行民法典的合同编、物权编等各分编的编撰工作。民法典施行后，合同法不再保留。在这之前，因民法总则施行前成立的合同发生的纠纷，原则上适用合同法的有关规定处理。因民法总则施行后成立的合同发生的纠纷，如果合同法“总则”对此的规定与民法总则的规定不一致的，根据新的规定优于旧的规定的法律适用规则，适用民法总则的规定。例如，关于欺诈、胁迫问题，根据合同法的规定，只有合同当事人之间存在欺诈、胁迫行为的，被欺诈、胁迫一方才享有撤销合同的权利。而依民法总则的规定，第三人实施的欺诈、胁迫行为，被欺诈、胁迫一方也有撤销合同的权利。另外，合同法视欺诈、胁迫行为所损害利益的不同，对合同效力作出了不同规定：损害合同当事人利益的，属于可撤销或者可变更合同；损害国家利益的，则属于无效合同。民法总则则未加区别，规定一律按可撤销合同对待。再如，关于显失公平问题，合同法将显失公平与乘人之危作为两类不同的可撤销或者可变更合同事由，而民法总则则将二者合并为一类可撤销合同事由。  
民法总则施行后发生的纠纷，在民法典施行前，如果合同法“分则”对此的规定与民法总则不一致的，根据特别规定优于一般规定的法律适用规则，适用合同法“分则”的规定。例如，民法总则仅规定了显名代理，没有规定《合同法》第402条的隐名代理和第403条的间接代理。在民法典施行前，这两条规定应当继续适用。

**Article 2** [Relationship between the General Part of Civil Law and the Contract Law and Their Application] According to the “two-step” arrangement of the codification work of the Civil Code, after the General Part of Civil Law comes into force, the compilation of the individual parts of the Civil Code, such as the Contract Book and the Real Rights Book, is currently underway. After the Civil Code comes into force, the Contract Law shall no longer be retained. Prior to that, disputes arising from contracts concluded before the General Part of Civil Law came into force shall, in principle, be handled by applying the relevant provisions of the Contract Law. For disputes arising from contracts concluded after the General Part of Civil Law comes into force, if the provisions of the “General Part” of the Contract Law are inconsistent with those of the General Part of Civil Law on the matter, the provisions of the General Part of Civil Law shall apply according to the rule of application of law that new provisions prevail over old ones. For example, regarding the issues of fraud and duress, according to the Contract Law, only where there is fraud or duress between the parties to a contract does the defrauded or coerced party have the right to revoke the contract. However, according to the General Part of Civil Law, where fraud or duress is committed by a third party, the defrauded or coerced party also has the right to revoke the contract. In addition, the Contract Law makes different provisions on the validity of the contract depending on the interests damaged by the fraud or duress: if the interests of the parties to the contract are damaged, the contract is voidable or modifiable; if the interests of the state are damaged, the contract is void. The General Part of Civil Law does not make such a distinction and provides that all such contracts shall be treated as voidable contracts. Another example, regarding the issue of obvious unfairness, the Contract Law treats obvious unfairness and exploitation of another's distress as two different types of grounds for voidable or modifiable contracts, whereas the General Part of Civil Law merges the two into one type of ground for voidable contracts.  
For disputes arising after the General Part of Civil Law comes into force but before the Civil Code comes into force, if the provisions of the “Special Part” of the Contract Law are inconsistent with those of the General Part of Civil Law on the matter, the provisions of the “Special Part” of the Contract Law shall apply according to the rule of application of law that special provisions prevail over general provisions. For example, the General Part of Civil Law only provides for disclosed agency and does not provide for undisclosed agency under Article 402 of the Contract Law or indirect agency under Article 403 of the Contract Law. Before the Civil Code comes into force, these two provisions shall continue to apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　【民法总则与公司法的关系及其适用】民法总则与公司法的关系，是一般法与商事特别法的关系。民法总则第三章“法人”第一节“一般规定”和第二节“营利法人”基本上是根据公司法的有关规定提炼的，二者的精神大体一致。因此，涉及民法总则这一部分的内容，规定一致的，适用民法总则或者公司法皆可；规定不一致的，根据《民法总则》第11条有关“其他法律对民事关系有特别规定的，依照其规定”的规定，原则上应当适用公司法的规定。但应当注意也有例外情况，主要表现在两个方面：一是就同一事项，民法总则制定时有意修正公司法有关条款的，应当适用民法总则的规定。例如，《公司法》第32条第3款规定：“公司应当将股东的姓名或者名称及其出资额向公司登记机关登记；登记事项发生变更的，应当办理变更登记。未经登记或者变更登记的，不得对抗第三人。”而《民法总则》第65条的规定则把“不得对抗第三人”修正为“不得对抗善意相对人”。经查询有关立法理由，可以认为，此种情况应当适用民法总则的规定。二是民法总则在公司法规定基础上增加了新内容的，如《公司法》第22条第2款就公司决议的撤销问题进行了规定，《民法总则》第85条在该条基础上增加规定：“但是营利法人依据该决议与善意相对人形成的民事法律关系不受影响。”此时，也应当适用民法总则的规定。

**Article 3** [Relationship between the General Part of Civil Law and the Company Law and Their Application] The relationship between the General Part of Civil Law and the Company Law is that of a general law and a special commercial law. The first section “General Provisions” and the second section “For-Profit Legal Persons” of Chapter III “Legal Persons” of the General Part of Civil Law are essentially distilled from the relevant provisions of the Company Law, and the spirit of the two is generally consistent. Therefore, for matters covered by this part of the General Part of Civil Law, where the provisions are consistent, either the General Part of Civil Law or the Company Law may apply; where the provisions are inconsistent, in principle, the provisions of the Company Law shall apply in accordance with Article 11 of the General Part of Civil Law, which provides that “where other laws have special provisions on civil relations, such provisions shall prevail.” However, attention shall be paid to exceptions, mainly in two aspects: First, where, on the same matter, the General Part of Civil Law was intentionally formulated to amend the relevant provisions of the Company Law, the provisions of the General Part of Civil Law shall apply. For example, Article 32, paragraph 3 of the Company Law provides: “A company shall register the names or titles of its shareholders and their capital contributions with the company registration authority; where the registered items change, it shall undergo change registration. Without registration or change registration, it shall not be used to oppose a third party.” However, Article 65 of the General Part of Civil Law amends “shall not be used to oppose a third party” to “shall not be used to oppose a bona fide counterparty.” After consulting the relevant legislative reasons, it may be considered that the provisions of the General Part of Civil Law shall apply in such circumstances. Second, where the General Part of Civil Law adds new content on the basis of the Company Law, such as Article 22, paragraph 2 of the Company Law providing for the revocation of corporate resolutions, Article 85 of the General Part of Civil Law adds a provision on that basis: “However, the civil legal relationship formed between the for-profit legal person and a bona fide counterparty based on such resolution shall not be affected.” In such case, the provisions of the General Part of Civil Law shall also apply.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　【民法总则的时间效力】根据“法不溯及既往”的原则，民法总则原则上没有溯及力，故只能适用于施行后发生的法律事实；民法总则施行前发生的法律事实，适用当时的法律；某一法律事实发生在民法总则施行前，其行为延续至民法总则施行后的，适用民法总则的规定。但要注意有例外情形，如虽然法律事实发生在民法总则施行前，但当时的法律对此没有规定而民法总则有规定的，例如，对于虚伪意思表示、第三人实施欺诈行为，合同法均无规定，发生纠纷后，基于“法官不得拒绝裁判”规则，可以将民法总则的相关规定作为裁判依据。又如，民法总则施行前成立的合同，根据当时的法律应当认定无效，而根据民法总则应当认定有效或者可撤销的，应当适用民法总则的规定。  
在民法总则无溯及力的场合，人民法院应当依据法律事实发生时的法律进行裁判，但如果法律事实发生时的法律虽有规定，但内容不具体、不明确的，如关于无权代理在被代理人不予追认时的法律后果，民法通则和合同法均规定由行为人承担民事责任，但对民事责任的性质和方式没有规定，而民法总则对此有明确且详细的规定，人民法院在审理案件时，就可以在裁判文书的说理部分将民法总则规定的内容作为解释法律事实发生时法律规定的参考。

**Article 4** [Temporal Effect of the General Part of Civil Law] According to the principle that “laws shall not be retroactive,” the General Part of Civil Law shall, in principle, have no retroactive effect, and therefore may only apply to legal facts that occur after its coming into force; legal facts that occurred before the General Part of Civil Law came into force shall be governed by the law in force at that time; where a legal fact occurs before the General Part of Civil Law comes into force but its conduct continues after the General Part of Civil Law comes into force, the provisions of the General Part of Civil Law shall apply. However, attention shall be paid to exceptional circumstances. For example, although a legal fact occurs before the General Part of Civil Law comes into force, if the law in force at that time has no provisions on the matter while the General Part of Civil Law has provisions, such as where the Contract Law has no provisions on false declaration of intention or fraud committed by a third party, after a dispute arises, based on the rule that “a judge shall not refuse to adjudicate,” the relevant provisions of the General Part of Civil Law may be used as the basis for adjudication. Another example, where a contract concluded before the General Part of Civil Law comes into force should be found void under the law in force at that time, but should be found valid or voidable under the General Part of Civil Law, the provisions of the General Part of Civil Law shall apply.  
In cases where the General Part of Civil Law has no retroactive effect, the People's Court shall render a judgment in accordance with the law in force at the time of the legal fact. However, if the law in force at the time of the legal fact has provisions, but the contents are not specific or clear, such as regarding the legal consequences of unauthorized agency when the principal does not ratify, the General Principles of Civil Law and the Contract Law both provide that the actor shall bear civil liability, but do not specify the nature and method of such civil liability, while the General Part of Civil Law has clear and detailed provisions on this matter, then when a People's Court tries a case, it may, in the reasoning part of its judgment, use the provisions of the General Part of Civil Law as a reference for interpreting the law in force at the time of the legal fact.

### 二、关于公司纠纷案件的审理

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　【与目标公司“对赌”】投资方与目标公司订立的“对赌协议”在不存在法定无效事由的情况下，目标公司仅以存在股权回购或者金钱补偿约定为由，主张“对赌协议”无效的，人民法院不予支持，但投资方主张实际履行的，人民法院应当审查是否符合公司法关于“股东不得抽逃出资”及股份回购的强制性规定，判决是否支持其诉讼请求。  
投资方请求目标公司回购股权的，人民法院应当依据《公司法》第35条关于“股东不得抽逃出资”或者第142条关于股份回购的强制性规定进行审查。经审查，目标公司未完成减资程序的，人民法院应当驳回其诉讼请求。  
投资方请求目标公司承担金钱补偿义务的，人民法院应当依据《公司法》第35条关于“股东不得抽逃出资”和第166条关于利润分配的强制性规定进行审查。经审查，目标公司没有利润或者虽有利润但不足以补偿投资方的，人民法院应当驳回或者部分支持其诉讼请求。今后目标公司有利润时，投资方还可以依据该事实另行提起诉讼。  
（二）关于股东出资加速到期及表决权

**Article 5** [“Valuation Adjustment Mechanism” with Target Company] Where a “valuation adjustment mechanism agreement” concluded between an investor and a target company does not have any statutory ground for invalidity, and the target company claims that the “valuation adjustment mechanism agreement” is void solely on the ground that it contains an equity repurchase or monetary compensation clause, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim. However, if the investor requests actual performance, the People's Court shall examine whether it conforms to the mandatory provisions of the Company Law regarding “shareholders shall not withdraw capital contributions” and share repurchase, and decide whether to uphold the investor's claim.  
Where the investor requests the target company to repurchase equity, the People's Court shall examine it in accordance with Article 35 of the Company Law regarding “shareholders shall not withdraw capital contributions” or Article 142 regarding the mandatory provisions on share repurchase. After examination, if the target company has not completed the capital reduction procedure, the People's Court shall dismiss the investor's claim.  
Where the investor requests the target company to bear a monetary compensation obligation, the People's Court shall examine it in accordance with Article 35 of the Company Law regarding “shareholders shall not withdraw capital contributions” and Article 166 regarding the mandatory provisions on profit distribution. After examination, if the target company has no profit or has profit but insufficient to compensate the investor, the People's Court shall dismiss or partially uphold the investor's claim. Where the target company has profit in the future, the investor may file another lawsuit based on that fact.  
(II) Concerning Acceleration of Shareholders' Capital Contribution and Voting Rights

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　【股东出资应否加速到期】在注册资本认缴制下，股东依法享有期限利益。债权人以公司不能清偿到期债务为由，请求未届出资期限的股东在未出资范围内对公司不能清偿的债务承担补充赔偿责任的，人民法院不予支持。但是，下列情形除外：  
（1）公司作为被执行人的案件，人民法院穷尽执行措施无财产可供执行，已具备破产原因，但不申请破产的；  
（2）在公司债务产生后，公司股东（大）会决议或以其他方式延长股东出资期限的。

**Article 6** [Whether Shareholders' Capital Contribution Should Be Accelerated] Under the system of subscribed capital contribution, shareholders shall enjoy the benefit of term according to law. Where a creditor, on the ground that the company is unable to pay its due debts, requests a shareholder whose capital contribution term has not yet expired to assume supplementary compensation liability for the company's debts that cannot be paid, within the scope of the unpaid contribution, the People's Court shall not uphold such request, except in the following circumstances:  
(1) In a case where the company is the person subject to enforcement, the People's Court has exhausted enforcement measures and has no property available for enforcement, the company already has grounds for bankruptcy but does not apply for bankruptcy;  
(2) After the company's debts arise, the shareholders' meeting (or general meeting) of the company resolves or extends the shareholders' capital contribution term by other means.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　【表决权能否受限】股东认缴的出资未届履行期限，对未缴纳部分的出资是否享有以及如何行使表决权等问题，应当根据公司章程来确定。公司章程没有规定的，应当按照认缴出资的比例确定。如果股东（大）会作出不按认缴出资比例而按实际出资比例或者其他标准确定表决权的决议，股东请求确认决议无效的，人民法院应当审查该决议是否符合修改公司章程所要求的表决程序，即必须经代表三分之二以上表决权的股东通过。符合的，人民法院不予支持；反之，则依法予以支持。  
（三）关于股权转让

**Article 7** [Whether Voting Rights May Be Restricted] Whether a shareholder whose subscribed capital contribution has not yet fallen due is entitled to voting rights on the unpaid portion of the contribution, and how to exercise such voting rights, shall be determined by the company's articles of association. If the articles of association do not provide for it, it shall be determined according to the proportion of subscribed capital contribution. If the shareholders' meeting (or general meeting) adopts a resolution that determines voting rights not according to the proportion of subscribed capital contribution but according to the proportion of actual capital contribution or other standards, and a shareholder requests that the resolution be declared void, the People's Court shall examine whether the resolution conforms to the voting procedure required for amending the company's articles of association, i.e., it must be passed by shareholders representing more than two-thirds of the voting rights. If it does, the People's Court shall not uphold the request; otherwise, it shall uphold it according to law.  
(III) Concerning Transfer of Equity

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　【有限责任公司的股权变动】当事人之间转让有限责任公司股权，受让人以其姓名或者名称已记载于股东名册为由主张其已经取得股权的，人民法院依法予以支持，但法律、行政法规规定应当办理批准手续生效的股权转让除外。未向公司登记机关办理股权变更登记的，不得对抗善意相对人。

**Article 8** [Equity Changes in a Limited Liability Company] Where equity in a limited liability company is transferred between the parties, and the transferee claims that it has obtained the equity on the ground that its name or title has been recorded in the register of shareholders, the People's Court shall uphold such claim according to law, except where the laws or administrative regulations provide that the equity transfer shall take effect upon approval procedures. Where the equity change has not been registered with the company registration authority, it shall not be used to oppose a bona fide counterparty.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　【侵犯优先购买权的股权转让合同的效力】审判实践中，部分人民法院对公司法司法解释（四）第21条规定的理解存在偏差，往往以保护其他股东的优先购买权为由认定股权转让合同无效。准确理解该条规定，既要注意保护其他股东的优先购买权，也要注意保护股东以外的股权受让人的合法权益，正确认定有限责任公司的股东与股东以外的股权受让人订立的股权转让合同的效力。一方面，其他股东依法享有优先购买权，在其主张按照股权转让合同约定的同等条件购买股权的情况下，应当支持其诉讼请求，除非出现该条第1款规定的情形。另一方面，为保护股东以外的股权受让人的合法权益，股权转让合同如无其他影响合同效力的事由，应当认定有效。其他股东行使优先购买权的，虽然股东以外的股权受让人关于继续履行股权转让合同的请求不能得到支持，但不影响其依约请求转让股东承担相应的违约责任。  
（四）关于公司人格否认  
公司人格独立和股东有限责任是公司法的基本原则。否认公司独立人格，由滥用公司法人独立地位和股东有限责任的股东对公司债务承担连带责任，是股东有限责任的例外情形，旨在矫正有限责任制度在特定法律事实发生时对债权人保护的失衡现象。在审判实践中，要准确把握《公司法》第20条第3款规定的精神。一是只有在股东实施了滥用公司法人独立地位及股东有限责任的行为，且该行为严重损害了公司债权人利益的情况下，才能适用。损害债权人利益，主要是指股东滥用权利使公司财产不足以清偿公司债权人的债权。二是只有实施了滥用法人独立地位和股东有限责任行为的股东才对公司债务承担连带清偿责任，而其他股东不应承担此责任。三是公司人格否认不是全面、彻底、永久地否定公司的法人资格，而只是在具体案件中依据特定的法律事实、法律关系,突破股东对公司债务不承担责任的一般规则，例外地判令其承担连带责任。人民法院在个案中否认公司人格的判决的既判力仅仅约束该诉讼的各方当事人，不当然适用于涉及该公司的其他诉讼，不影响公司独立法人资格的存续。如果其他债权人提起公司人格否认诉讼，已生效判决认定的事实可以作为证据使用。四是《公司法》第20条第3款规定的滥用行为，实践中常见的情形有人格混同、过度支配与控制、资本显著不足等。在审理案件时，需要根据查明的案件事实进行综合判断，既审慎适用，又当用则用。实践中存在标准把握不严而滥用这一例外制度的现象，同时也存在因法律规定较为原则、抽象，适用难度大，而不善于适用、不敢于适用的现象，均应当引起高度重视。

**Article 9** [Effect of Equity Transfer Contract Infringing Preemptive Right] In trial practice, some People's Courts have a biased understanding of Article 21 of the Judicial Interpretation (IV) of the Company Law, often holding an equity transfer contract void on the ground of protecting other shareholders' preemptive right. To accurately understand this provision, attention shall be paid to both protecting other shareholders' preemptive right and protecting the lawful rights and interests of the transferee who is not a shareholder, and correctly determine the validity of the equity transfer contract concluded between a shareholder of a limited liability company and a transferee who is not a shareholder. On the one hand, other shareholders enjoy a preemptive right according to law; where they claim to purchase the equity under the same conditions as stipulated in the equity transfer contract, their claim shall be upheld, unless the circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of that Article occur. On the other hand, to protect the lawful rights and interests of the transferee who is not a shareholder, the equity transfer contract shall be found valid if there are no other grounds affecting its validity. Where other shareholders exercise their preemptive right, although the request of the transferee who is not a shareholder for continued performance of the equity transfer contract cannot be upheld, this does not affect its right to request the transferor shareholder to bear corresponding liability for breach of contract in accordance with the contract.  
(IV) Concerning Piercing the Corporate Veil  
Corporate independence and limited liability of shareholders are the basic principles of the Company Law. Piercing the corporate veil, whereby shareholders who abuse the independent legal person status of the company and limited liability assume joint and several liability for the company's debts, is an exception to limited liability, aiming to correct the imbalance in the protection of creditors under the limited liability system when specific legal facts occur. In trial practice, the spirit of Article 20, paragraph 3 of the Company Law shall be accurately grasped. First, it may only be applied where a shareholder has engaged in acts of abusing the independent legal person status of the company and limited liability, and such acts have seriously damaged the interests of the company's creditors. Damage to creditors' interests mainly refers to the shareholder's abuse of rights rendering the company's property insufficient to satisfy the claims of the company's creditors. Second, only the shareholder who has engaged in acts of abusing the independent legal person status and limited liability shall bear joint and several liability for the company's debts, while other shareholders shall not bear such liability. Third, piercing the corporate veil does not wholly, thoroughly, or permanently deny the company's legal personality, but only in a specific case, based on specific legal facts and legal relationships, breaks through the general rule that shareholders do not bear liability for the company's debts, and exceptionally orders them to assume joint and several liability. The res judicata of a People's Court's judgment piercing the corporate veil in a specific case only binds the parties to that action and does not automatically apply to other lawsuits involving the same company, nor does it affect the continued existence of the company's independent legal personality. If other creditors file a lawsuit to pierce the corporate veil, the facts affirmed in an effective judgment may be used as evidence. Fourth, the abusive acts specified in Article 20, paragraph 3 of the Company Law commonly include in practice personality confusion, excessive domination and control, significant capital inadequacy, etc. When trying a case, a comprehensive judgment shall be made based on the ascertained facts of the case, both applying it prudently and using it when appropriate. In practice, there are phenomena of abusing this exceptional system due to lenient standards, as well as phenomena of being unskilled or hesitant to apply it due to the relatively principled and abstract nature of the legal provisions, which are difficult to apply; all these shall be given high attention.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　【人格混同】认定公司人格与股东人格是否存在混同，最根本的判断标准是公司是否具有独立意思和独立财产，最主要的表现是公司的财产与股东的财产是否混同且无法区分。在认定是否构成人格混同时，应当综合考虑以下因素：  
（1）股东无偿使用公司资金或者财产，不作财务记载的；  
（2）股东用公司的资金偿还股东的债务，或者将公司的资金供关联公司无偿使用，不作财务记载的；  
（3）公司账簿与股东账簿不分，致使公司财产与股东财产无法区分的；  
（4）股东自身收益与公司盈利不加区分，致使双方利益不清的；  
（5）公司的财产记载于股东名下，由股东占有、使用的；  
（6）人格混同的其他情形。  
在出现人格混同的情况下，往往同时出现以下混同：公司业务和股东业务混同；公司员工与股东员工混同，特别是财务人员混同；公司住所与股东住所混同。人民法院在审理案件时，关键要审查是否构成人格混同，而不要求同时具备其他方面的混同，其他方面的混同往往只是人格混同的补强。

**Article 10** [Personality Confusion] To determine whether there is confusion between the personality of the company and the personality of its shareholders, the most fundamental criterion is whether the company has independent will and independent property, and the main manifestation is whether the company's property and the shareholders' property are confused and indistinguishable. When determining whether personality confusion is constituted, the following factors shall be comprehensively considered:  
(1) The shareholder uses the company's funds or property without compensation and fails to make financial records;  
(2) The shareholder uses the company's funds to repay the shareholder's debts, or supplies the company's funds to an affiliated company for use without compensation and fails to make financial records;  
(3) The company's books are not separated from the shareholder's books, making it impossible to distinguish between the company's property and the shareholder's property;  
(4) The shareholder's own income is not distinguished from the company's earnings, making the interests of both parties unclear;  
(5) The company's property is recorded in the shareholder's name, and is possessed and used by the shareholder;  
(6) Other circumstances of personality confusion.  
Where personality confusion occurs, the following confusion often also occurs simultaneously: confusion between the company's business and the shareholder's business; confusion between the company's employees and the shareholder's employees, especially financial personnel; confusion between the company's domicile and the shareholder's domicile. When trying a case, the People's Court shall focus on examining whether personality confusion is constituted, without requiring the simultaneous existence of other types of confusion; other types of confusion are often merely supplementary evidence of personality confusion.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　【过度支配与控制】公司控制股东对公司过度支配与控制，操纵公司的决策过程，使公司完全丧失独立性，沦为控制股东的工具或躯壳，严重损害公司债权人利益，应当否认公司人格，由滥用控制权的股东对公司债务承担连带责任。实践中常见的情形包括：  
（1）母子公司之间或者子公司之间进行利益输送的；  
（2）母子公司或者子公司之间进行交易，收益归一方，损失却由另一方承担的；  
（3）先从原公司抽走资金，然后再成立经营目的相同或者类似的公司，逃避原公司债务的；  
（4）先解散公司，再以原公司场所、设备、人员及相同或者相似的经营目的另设公司，逃避原公司债务的；  
（5）过度支配与控制的其他情形。  
控制股东或实际控制人控制多个子公司或者关联公司，滥用控制权使多个子公司或者关联公司财产边界不清、财务混同，利益相互输送，丧失人格独立性，沦为控制股东逃避债务、非法经营，甚至违法犯罪工具的，可以综合案件事实，否认子公司或者关联公司法人人格，判令承担连带责任。

**Article 11** [Excessive Domination and Control] Where the controlling shareholder exercises excessive domination and control over the company, manipulates the company's decision-making process, causing the company to completely lose its independence and become a tool or shell of the controlling shareholder, and seriously damages the interests of the company's creditors, the corporate veil shall be pierced, and the shareholder who abuses its control rights shall bear joint and several liability for the company's debts. Common circumstances in practice include:  
(1) Transferring benefits between parent company and subsidiary or between subsidiaries;  
(2) Conducting transactions between parent company and subsidiary or between subsidiaries, where the benefits belong to one party but the losses are borne by the other party;  
(3) First withdrawing funds from the original company, then establishing a company with the same or similar business purpose to evade the original company's debts;  
(4) First dissolving the company, then establishing another company using the original company's premises, equipment, personnel, and the same or similar business purpose to evade the original company's debts;  
(5) Other circumstances of excessive domination and control.  
Where a controlling shareholder or actual controller controls multiple subsidiaries or affiliated companies, abuses its control rights to make the property boundaries of multiple subsidiaries or affiliated companies unclear, financial affairs confused, interests transferred among them, and they lose personality independence, becoming tools for the controlling shareholder to evade debts, conduct illegal operations, or even commit illegal or criminal acts, based on the comprehensive facts of the case, the corporate personality of the subsidiaries or affiliated companies may be pierced and they may be ordered to assume joint and several liability.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　【资本显著不足】资本显著不足指的是，公司设立后在经营过程中，股东实际投入公司的资本数额与公司经营所隐含的风险相比明显不匹配。股东利用较少资本从事力所不及的经营，表明其没有从事公司经营的诚意，实质是恶意利用公司独立人格和股东有限责任把投资风险转嫁给债权人。由于资本显著不足的判断标准有很大的模糊性，特别是要与公司采取“以小博大”的正常经营方式相区分，因此在适用时要十分谨慎，应当与其他因素结合起来综合判断。

**Article 12** [Significant Capital Inadequacy] Significant capital inadequacy refers to a situation where, after the establishment of the company and during its operation, the amount of capital actually invested by shareholders is clearly disproportionate to the risks implied by the company's business. Shareholders using relatively small capital to engage in business beyond their capacity indicates that they lack sincerity in operating the company, and is essentially a malicious use of the company's independent personality and shareholders' limited liability to shift investment risks to creditors. Since the criterion for determining significant capital inadequacy is highly ambiguous, especially as it must be distinguished from the normal business practice of “using small capital to leverage large gains,” it shall be applied with great caution and shall be comprehensively judged in combination with other factors.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　【诉讼地位】人民法院在审理公司人格否认纠纷案件时，应当根据不同情形确定当事人的诉讼地位：  
（1）债权人对债务人公司享有的债权已经由生效裁判确认，其另行提起公司人格否认诉讼，请求股东对公司债务承担连带责任的，列股东为被告，公司为第三人；  
（2）债权人对债务人公司享有的债权提起诉讼的同时，一并提起公司人格否认诉讼，请求股东对公司债务承担连带责任的，列公司和股东为共同被告；  
（3）债权人对债务人公司享有的债权尚未经生效裁判确认，直接提起公司人格否认诉讼，请求公司股东对公司债务承担连带责任的，人民法院应当向债权人释明，告知其追加公司为共同被告。债权人拒绝追加的，人民法院应当裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 13** [Litigation Status] When trying a case involving a dispute over piercing the corporate veil, the People's Court shall determine the litigation status of the parties according to different circumstances:  
(1) Where a creditor's claim against the debtor company has been confirmed by an effective judgment, and the creditor separately files a lawsuit to pierce the corporate veil requesting shareholders to assume joint and several liability for the company's debts, the shareholder(s) shall be listed as the defendant(s) and the company as the third party;  
(2) Where a creditor, while filing a lawsuit on its claim against the debtor company, also files a lawsuit to pierce the corporate veil requesting shareholders to assume joint and several liability for the company's debts, the company and the shareholder(s) shall be listed as co-defendants;  
(3) Where a creditor, whose claim against the debtor company has not yet been confirmed by an effective judgment, directly files a lawsuit to pierce the corporate veil requesting company shareholders to assume joint and several liability for the company's debts, the People's Court shall explain to the creditor and inform it to add the company as a co-defendant. If the creditor refuses to add the company, the People's Court shall rule to dismiss the lawsuit.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　【怠于履行清算义务的认定】公司法司法解释（二）第18条第2款规定的“怠于履行义务”，是指有限责任公司的股东在法定清算事由出现后，在能够履行清算义务的情况下，故意拖延、拒绝履行清算义务，或者因过失导致无法进行清算的消极行为。股东举证证明其已经为履行清算义务采取了积极措施，或者小股东举证证明其既不是公司董事会或者监事会成员，也没有选派人员担任该机关成员，且从未参与公司经营管理，以不构成“怠于履行义务”为由，主张其不应当对公司债务承担连带清偿责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 14** [Determination of Failure to Perform Obligations with Wilful Neglect] The “failure to perform obligations with wilful neglect” as stipulated in Article 18, paragraph 2 of the Judicial Interpretation (II) of the Company Law refers to the negative conduct of a shareholder of a limited liability company who, after the occurrence of statutory grounds for liquidation and when able to perform the liquidation obligation, intentionally delays or refuses to perform the liquidation obligation, or causes the inability to liquidate through negligence. Where a shareholder adduces evidence proving that it has taken active measures to perform the liquidation obligation, or a small shareholder adduces evidence proving that it is neither a member of the board of directors nor the board of supervisors of the company, nor has it appointed any person to serve as a member of such organs, and has never participated in the company's operation and management, and claims on the ground that it does not constitute “failure to perform obligations with wilful neglect” that it shall not bear joint and several liability for the company's debts, the People's Court shall uphold such claim according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　【因果关系抗辩】有限责任公司的股东举证证明其“怠于履行义务”的消极不作为与“公司主要财产、账册、重要文件等灭失，无法进行清算”的结果之间没有因果关系，主张其不应对公司债务承担连带清偿责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 15** [Causation Defense] Where a shareholder of a limited liability company adduces evidence proving that there is no causal relationship between its negative omission of “failure to perform obligations with wilful neglect” and the result that “the company's main property, accounts, important documents, etc. are lost, making liquidation impossible,” and claims that it shall not bear joint and several liability for the company's debts, the People's Court shall uphold such claim according to law.

Regarding the validity of a contract where a company provides a guarantee for others, the inconsistency in adjudication standards in trial practice has seriously affected judicial credibility, making it necessary to regulate this matter. In this regard, the following points shall be grasped:

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　【违反《公司法》第16条构成越权代表】为防止法定代表人随意代表公司为他人提供担保给公司造成损失，损害中小股东利益，《公司法》第16条对法定代表人的代表权进行了限制。根据该条规定，担保行为不是法定代表人所能单独决定的事项，而必须以公司股东（大）会、董事会等公司机关的决议作为授权的基础和来源。法定代表人未经授权擅自为他人提供担保的，构成越权代表，人民法院应当根据《合同法》第50条关于法定代表人越权代表的规定，区分订立合同时债权人是否善意分别认定合同效力：债权人善意的，合同有效；反之，合同无效。

**Article 17** [Violation of Article 16 of the Company Law Constituting Ultra Vires Representation] To prevent the legal representative from arbitrarily representing the company to provide guarantees for others, causing losses to the company and damaging the interests of minority shareholders, Article 16 of the Company Law restricts the power of representation of the legal representative. According to that article, a guarantee act is not a matter that the legal representative may decide alone; rather, it must be based on and sourced from the resolution of the company's shareholder (general) meeting, board of directors, or other company organs. Where a legal representative, without authorization, provides a guarantee for others on behalf of the company, it constitutes ultra vires representation. The People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 50 of the Contract Law regarding ultra vires representation by a legal representative, distinguish whether the creditor is bona fide at the time of concluding the contract and determine the validity of the contract accordingly: if the creditor is bona fide, the contract is valid; otherwise, the contract is void.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　【善意的认定】前条所称的善意，是指债权人不知道或者不应当知道法定代表人超越权限订立担保合同。《公司法》第16条对关联担保和非关联担保的决议机关作出了区别规定，相应地，在善意的判断标准上也应当有所区别。一种情形是，为公司股东或者实际控制人提供关联担保，《公司法》第16条明确规定必须由股东（大）会决议，未经股东（大）会决议，构成越权代表。在此情况下，债权人主张担保合同有效，应当提供证据证明其在订立合同时对股东（大）会决议进行了审查，决议的表决程序符合《公司法》第16条的规定，即在排除被担保股东表决权的情况下，该项表决由出席会议的其他股东所持表决权的过半数通过，签字人员也符合公司章程的规定。另一种情形是，公司为公司股东或者实际控制人以外的人提供非关联担保，根据《公司法》第16条的规定，此时由公司章程规定是由董事会决议还是股东（大）会决议。无论章程是否对决议机关作出规定，也无论章程规定决议机关为董事会还是股东（大）会，根据《民法总则》第61条第3款关于“法人章程或者法人权力机构对法定代表人代表权的限制，不得对抗善意相对人”的规定，只要债权人能够证明其在订立担保合同时对董事会决议或者股东（大）会决议进行了审查，同意决议的人数及签字人员符合公司章程的规定，就应当认定其构成善意，但公司能够证明债权人明知公司章程对决议机关有明确规定的除外。  
债权人对公司机关决议内容的审查一般限于形式审查，只要求尽到必要的注意义务即可，标准不宜太过严苛。公司以机关决议系法定代表人伪造或者变造、决议程序违法、签章（名）不实、担保金额超过法定限额等事由抗辩债权人非善意的，人民法院一般不予支持。但是，公司有证据证明债权人明知决议系伪造或者变造的除外。

**Article 18** [Determination of Bona Fide] The term “bona fide” in the preceding article means that the creditor did not know and should not have known that the legal representative had exceeded his authority in concluding the guarantee contract. Article 16 of the Company Law makes different provisions on the resolution organ for connected guarantees and non-connected guarantees. Correspondingly, there shall also be differences in the criteria for determining bona fides. One situation is where a connected guarantee is provided for a shareholder or actual controller of the company. Article 16 of the Company Law clearly stipulates that this must be resolved by the shareholders' meeting (or general meeting); without a resolution of the shareholders' meeting (or general meeting), it constitutes ultra vires representation. In such case, if the creditor claims that the guarantee contract is valid, it shall provide evidence proving that it examined the resolution of the shareholders' meeting (or general meeting) at the time of concluding the contract, and that the voting procedure of the resolution complies with Article 16 of the Company Law, i.e., after excluding the voting rights of the guaranteed shareholder, the resolution was passed by more than half of the voting rights held by other shareholders present at the meeting, and the signatories also comply with the provisions of the company's articles of association. Another situation is where the company provides a non-connected guarantee for a person other than a shareholder or actual controller. According to Article 16 of the Company Law, at this time, it shall be determined by the company's articles of association whether the board of directors or the shareholders' meeting (or general meeting) shall make the resolution. Regardless of whether the articles of association provide for the resolution organ, and regardless of whether the articles of association designate the board of directors or the shareholders' meeting (or general meeting) as the resolution organ, according to Article 61, paragraph 3 of the General Part of Civil Law, which provides that “the restrictions imposed by the legal person's articles of association or its authority organ on the power of representation of the legal representative shall not be used to oppose a bona fide counterparty,” as long as the creditor can prove that it examined the resolution of the board of directors or the resolution of the shareholders' meeting (or general meeting) at the time of concluding the guarantee contract, and that the number of persons consenting to the resolution and the signatories comply with the provisions of the company's articles of association, it shall be determined that the creditor is bona fide, except where the company can prove that the creditor clearly knew that the articles of association had specific provisions on the resolution organ.  
The creditor's examination of the content of the company organ's resolution is generally limited to formal examination; only a duty of necessary care is required, and the standard shall not be too strict. Where the company defends that the creditor is not bona fide on grounds such as that the resolution of the organ was forged or altered by the legal representative, the resolution procedure was illegal, the signature (or seal) was false, or the guarantee amount exceeded the statutory limit, the People's Court shall generally not uphold such defense. However, this is excepted where the company has evidence proving that the creditor clearly knew that the resolution was forged or altered.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　【无须机关决议的例外情况】存在下列情形的，即便债权人知道或者应当知道没有公司机关决议，也应当认定担保合同符合公司的真实意思表示，合同有效：  
（1）公司是以为他人提供担保为主营业务的担保公司，或者是开展保函业务的银行或者非银行金融机构；  
（2）公司为其直接或者间接控制的公司开展经营活动向债权人提供担保；  
（3）公司与主债务人之间存在相互担保等商业合作关系；  
（4）担保合同系由单独或者共同持有公司三分之二以上有表决权的股东签字同意。

**Article 19** [Exceptions Where No Organ Resolution Is Required] Where any of the following circumstances exists, even if the creditor knows or should have known that there is no resolution of a company organ, the guarantee contract shall be found to conform to the company's true expression of intention and shall be valid:  
(1) The company is a guarantee company whose main business is providing guarantees for others, or is a bank or non-bank financial institution that conducts guarantee letter business;  
(2) The company provides a guarantee to a creditor for the purpose of conducting business activities by a company directly or indirectly controlled by it;  
(3) There is a commercial cooperative relationship such as mutual guarantee between the company and the principal debtor;  
(4) The guarantee contract is signed and consented to by shareholders who individually or jointly hold more than two-thirds of the voting rights of the company.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　【越权担保的民事责任】依据前述3条规定，担保合同有效，债权人请求公司承担担保责任的，人民法院依法予以支持；担保合同无效，债权人请求公司承担担保责任的，人民法院不予支持，但可以按照担保法及有关司法解释关于担保无效的规定处理。公司举证证明债权人明知法定代表人超越权限或者机关决议系伪造或者变造，债权人请求公司承担合同无效后的民事责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 20** [Civil Liability for Ultra Vires Guarantee] In accordance with the provisions of the preceding 3 articles, if the guarantee contract is valid, and the creditor requests the company to bear guarantee liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request according to law; if the guarantee contract is void, and the creditor requests the company to bear guarantee liability, the People's Court shall not uphold such request, but may handle it in accordance with the Guarantee Law and relevant judicial interpretations on void guarantees. Where the company adduces evidence proving that the creditor clearly knew that the legal representative exceeded his authority or that the organ's resolution was forged or altered, and the creditor requests the company to bear civil liability after the contract is void, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　【权利救济】法定代表人的越权担保行为给公司造成损失，公司请求法定代表人承担赔偿责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。公司没有提起诉讼，股东依据《公司法》第151条的规定请求法定代表人承担赔偿责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 21** [Remedy for Rights] Where the legal representative's act of providing guarantee beyond authority causes losses to the company, and the company requests the legal representative to bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall support such request according to law. Where the company fails to file a lawsuit, and a shareholder requests the legal representative to bear compensation liability in accordance with Article 151 of the Company Law, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　【上市公司为他人提供担保】债权人根据上市公司公开披露的关于担保事项已经董事会或者股东大会决议通过的信息订立的担保合同，人民法院应当认定有效。

**Article 22** [Listed Company Providing Guarantee for Others] Where a creditor concludes a guarantee contract based on information publicly disclosed by a listed company that the guarantee matter has been approved by a resolution of the board of directors or the shareholders' general meeting, the People's Court shall determine the contract to be valid.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　【债务加入准用担保规则】法定代表人以公司名义与债务人约定加入债务并通知债权人或者向债权人表示愿意加入债务，该约定的效力问题，参照本纪要关于公司为他人提供担保的有关规则处理。  
（七）关于股东代表诉讼

**Article 23** [Rules on Guarantee Applied Mutatis Mutandis to Debt Assumption] Where the legal representative, in the name of the company, agrees with the debtor to assume the debt and notifies the creditor or indicates to the creditor its willingness to assume the debt, the validity of such agreement shall be dealt with by reference to the relevant rules of this Summary on a company providing guarantee for others.  
(VII) Concerning Shareholder Representative Action

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　【何时成为股东不影响起诉】股东提起股东代表诉讼，被告以行为发生时原告尚未成为公司股东为由抗辩该股东不是适格原告的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 24** [Time of Becoming Shareholder Does Not Affect Action] Where a shareholder initiates a shareholder representative action, and the defendant argues that the plaintiff was not a shareholder of the company at the time of the act and thus is not a proper plaintiff, the People's Court shall not support such argument.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　【正确适用前置程序】根据《公司法》第151条的规定，股东提起代表诉讼的前置程序之一是，股东必须先书面请求公司有关机关向人民法院提起诉讼。一般情况下，股东没有履行该前置程序的，应当驳回起诉。但是，该项前置程序针对的是公司治理的一般情况，即在股东向公司有关机关提出书面申请之时，存在公司有关机关提起诉讼的可能性。如果查明的相关事实表明，根本不存在该种可能性的，人民法院不应当以原告未履行前置程序为由驳回起诉。

**Article 25** [Correct Application of Pre-procedure] According to Article 151 of the Company Law, one of the pre-procedures for a shareholder to initiate a representative action is that the shareholder shall first request in writing the relevant organ of the company to file a lawsuit with the People's Court. In general, if the shareholder fails to complete this pre-procedure, the action shall be dismissed. However, this pre-procedure is designed for the general situation of corporate governance, i.e., at the time the shareholder makes a written request to the relevant organ of the company, there exists the possibility that the relevant organ of the company will file a lawsuit. If the relevant facts ascertained indicate that such possibility is entirely absent, the People's Court shall not dismiss the action on the ground that the plaintiff has not fulfilled the pre-procedure.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　【股东代表诉讼的反诉】股东依据《公司法》第151条第3款的规定提起股东代表诉讼后，被告以原告股东恶意起诉侵犯其合法权益为由提起反诉的，人民法院应予受理。被告以公司在案涉纠纷中应当承担侵权或者违约等责任为由对公司提出的反诉，因不符合反诉的要件，人民法院应当裁定不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 26** [Counterclaim in Shareholder Representative Action] After a shareholder initiates a shareholder representative action in accordance with Article 151(3) of the Company Law, if the defendant files a counterclaim on the ground that the plaintiff shareholder maliciously initiated the action and infringed upon its lawful rights and interests, the People's Court shall accept the counterclaim. If the defendant files a counterclaim against the company on the ground that the company shall bear tortious or contractual liability in the dispute involved in the case, which does not satisfy the requirements for a counterclaim, the People's Court shall rule to not accept the counterclaim; if it has already been accepted, the People's Court shall rule to dismiss the counterclaim.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　【股东代表诉讼的调解】公司是股东代表诉讼的最终受益人，为避免因原告股东与被告通过调解损害公司利益，人民法院应当审查调解协议是否为公司的意思。只有在调解协议经公司股东（大）会、董事会决议通过后，人民法院才能出具调解书予以确认。至于具体决议机关，取决于公司章程的规定。公司章程没有规定的，人民法院应当认定公司股东（大）会为决议机关。  
（八）其他问题

**Article 27** [Mediation in Shareholder Representative Action] The company is the ultimate beneficiary of a shareholder representative action. To prevent the plaintiff shareholder and the defendant from harming the company's interests through mediation, the People's Court shall review whether the mediation agreement reflects the company's intent. Only after the mediation agreement has been approved by a resolution of the company's shareholders' (general) meeting or board of directors may the People's Court issue a mediation document to confirm it. The specific resolution-making organ shall be determined by the company's articles of association. If the articles of association do not stipulate it, the People's Court shall determine that the company's shareholders' (general) meeting is the resolution-making organ.  
(VIII) Other Issues

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　【实际出资人显名的条件】实际出资人能够提供证据证明有限责任公司过半数的其他股东知道其实际出资的事实，且对其实际行使股东权利未曾提出异议的，对实际出资人提出的登记为公司股东的请求，人民法院依法予以支持。公司以实际出资人的请求不符合公司法司法解释（三）第24条的规定为由抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 28** [Conditions for Actual Contributor to Appear as Shareholder] Where the actual contributor can provide evidence to prove that more than half of the other shareholders of a limited liability company are aware of the fact of its actual contribution and have not raised objections to its actual exercise of shareholder rights, the People's Court shall support, according to law, the request of the actual contributor to be registered as a shareholder of the company. If the company argues that the actual contributor's request does not conform to Article 24 of the Judicial Interpretation (III) of the Company Law, the People's Court shall not support such argument.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　【请求召开股东（大）会不可诉】公司召开股东（大）会本质上属于公司内部治理范围。股东请求判令公司召开股东（大）会的，人民法院应当告知其按照《公司法》第40条或者第101条规定的程序自行召开。股东坚持起诉的，人民法院应当裁定不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 29** [Request to Convene Shareholders' Meeting Not Actionable] The convening of a shareholders' (general) meeting of a company is essentially within the scope of the company's internal governance. Where a shareholder requests a judgment ordering the company to convene a shareholders' (general) meeting, the People's Court shall inform the shareholder to convene it on its own in accordance with the procedures prescribed in Article 40 or Article 101 of the Company Law. If the shareholder insists on filing the lawsuit, the People's Court shall rule to not accept the case; if it has already been accepted, the People's Court shall rule to dismiss the action.

### 三、关于合同纠纷案件的审理

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　【强制性规定的识别】合同法施行后，针对一些人民法院动辄以违反法律、行政法规的强制性规定为由认定合同无效，不当扩大无效合同范围的情形，合同法司法解释（二）第14条将《合同法》第52条第5项规定的“强制性规定”明确限于“效力性强制性规定”。此后，《最高人民法院关于当前形势下审理民商事合同纠纷案件若干问题的指导意见》进一步提出了“管理性强制性规定”的概念，指出违反管理性强制性规定的，人民法院应当根据具体情形认定合同效力。随着这一概念的提出，审判实践中又出现了另一种倾向，有的人民法院认为凡是行政管理性质的强制性规定都属于“管理性强制性规定”，不影响合同效力。这种望文生义的认定方法，应予纠正。  
人民法院在审理合同纠纷案件时，要依据《民法总则》第153条第1款和合同法司法解释（二）第14条的规定慎重判断“强制性规定”的性质，特别是要在考量强制性规定所保护的法益类型、违法行为的法律后果以及交易安全保护等因素的基础上认定其性质，并在裁判文书中充分说明理由。下列强制性规定，应当认定为“效力性强制性规定”：强制性规定涉及金融安全、市场秩序、国家宏观政策等公序良俗的；交易标的禁止买卖的，如禁止人体器官、毒品、枪支等买卖；违反特许经营规定的，如场外配资合同；交易方式严重违法的，如违反招投标等竞争性缔约方式订立的合同；交易场所违法的，如在批准的交易场所之外进行期货交易。关于经营范围、交易时间、交易数量等行政管理性质的强制性规定，一般应当认定为“管理性强制性规定”。

**Article 30** [Identification of Mandatory Provisions] After the Contract Law came into force, some People's Courts tended to determine contracts void on the ground of violation of mandatory provisions of laws and administrative regulations, improperly expanding the scope of void contracts. In response, Article 14 of the Judicial Interpretation (II) of the Contract Law expressly limited the “mandatory provisions” under Article 52(5) of the Contract Law to “mandatory provisions on validity”. Subsequently, the Guiding Opinions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Trial of Civil and Commercial Contract Disputes under the Current Circumstances further introduced the concept of “mandatory provisions on administration”, pointing out that where a mandatory provision on administration is violated, the People's Court shall determine the validity of the contract based on the specific circumstances. With the introduction of this concept, another tendency emerged in judicial practice: some People's Courts considered that all mandatory provisions of an administrative nature belong to “mandatory provisions on administration” and do not affect the validity of the contract. This literal interpretation method shall be corrected.  
When adjudicating contract dispute cases, the People's Court shall carefully determine the nature of a “mandatory provision” in accordance with Article 153(1) of the General Principles of the Civil Law and Article 14 of the Judicial Interpretation (II) of the Contract Law, and in particular, shall determine its nature based on consideration of factors such as the type of legal interests protected by the mandatory provision, the legal consequences of the illegal act, and the protection of transaction security, and shall fully state the reasons in the adjudicatory document. The following mandatory provisions shall be determined as “mandatory provisions on validity”: mandatory provisions concerning public order and good customs such as financial security, market order, national macro-policy, etc.; those prohibiting the trading of subject matter, such as the prohibition of trading human organs, drugs, firearms, etc.; those violating franchise regulations, such as off-site margin financing contracts; those where the transaction method is seriously illegal, such as contracts concluded in violation of competitive contracting methods like bidding; those where the transaction venue is illegal, such as conducting futures trading outside approved trading venues. Mandatory provisions of an administrative nature concerning business scope, transaction time, transaction quantity, etc., shall generally be determined as “mandatory provisions on administration”.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　【违反规章的合同效力】违反规章一般情况下不影响合同效力，但该规章的内容涉及金融安全、市场秩序、国家宏观政策等公序良俗的，应当认定合同无效。人民法院在认定规章是否涉及公序良俗时，要在考察规范对象基础上，兼顾监管强度、交易安全保护以及社会影响等方面进行慎重考量，并在裁判文书中进行充分说理。

**Article 31** [Validity of Contracts Violating Rules] A violation of regulatory rules generally does not affect the validity of a contract. However, if the content of such rules involves public order and good customs such as financial security, market order, national macro-policy, etc., the contract shall be determined void. When determining whether a regulatory rule involves public order and good customs, the People's Court shall, on the basis of examining the object of the regulation, give careful consideration to factors such as the intensity of regulation, protection of transaction security, and social impact, and shall provide full reasoning in the adjudicatory document.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　【合同不成立、无效或者被撤销的法律后果】《合同法》第58条就合同无效或者被撤销时的财产返还责任和损害赔偿责任作了规定，但未规定合同不成立的法律后果。考虑到合同不成立时也可能发生财产返还和损害赔偿责任问题，故应当参照适用该条的规定。  
在确定合同不成立、无效或者被撤销后财产返还或者折价补偿范围时，要根据诚实信用原则的要求，在当事人之间合理分配，不能使不诚信的当事人因合同不成立、无效或者被撤销而获益。合同不成立、无效或者被撤销情况下，当事人所承担的缔约过失责任不应超过合同履行利益。比如，依据《最高人民法院关于审理建设工程施工合同纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释》第2条规定，建设工程施工合同无效，在建设工程经竣工验收合格情况下，可以参照合同约定支付工程款，但除非增加了合同约定之外新的工程项目，一般不应超出合同约定支付工程款。

**Article 32** [Legal Consequences of Contract Not Concluded, Void, or Revoked] Article 58 of the Contract Law provides for the liability for return of property and damages when a contract is void or revoked, but does not stipulate the legal consequences of a contract not concluded. Considering that issues of property return and damages may also arise when a contract is not concluded, the provisions of that Article shall apply mutatis mutandis.  
When determining the scope of property return or compensation at a discount after a contract is not concluded, void, or revoked, the allocation between the parties shall be reasonable in accordance with the principle of good faith, and no party acting in bad faith may profit from the contract not being concluded, being void, or being revoked. Where a contract is not concluded, is void, or is revoked, the pre-contractual liability borne by the party shall not exceed the benefit of contract performance. For example, according to Article 2 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Issues Concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Disputes over Construction Project Construction Contracts, where a construction project construction contract is void but the construction project has passed acceptance after completion, payment for the project may be made by reference to the contract, unless new project items beyond those agreed in the contract have been added, in which case payment shall generally not exceed the amount agreed in the contract.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　【财产返还与折价补偿】合同不成立、无效或者被撤销后，在确定财产返还时，要充分考虑财产增值或者贬值的因素。双务合同不成立、无效或者被撤销后，双方因该合同取得财产的，应当相互返还。应予返还的股权、房屋等财产相对于合同约定价款出现增值或者贬值的，人民法院要综合考虑市场因素、受让人的经营或者添附等行为与财产增值或者贬值之间的关联性，在当事人之间合理分配或者分担，避免一方因合同不成立、无效或者被撤销而获益。在标的物已经灭失、转售他人或者其他无法返还的情况下，当事人主张返还原物的，人民法院不予支持，但其主张折价补偿的，人民法院依法予以支持。折价时，应当以当事人交易时约定的价款为基础，同时考虑当事人在标的物灭失或者转售时的获益情况综合确定补偿标准。标的物灭失时当事人获得的保险金或者其他赔偿金，转售时取得的对价，均属于当事人因标的物而获得的利益。对获益高于或者低于价款的部分，也应当在当事人之间合理分配或者分担。

**Article 33** [Return of Property and Compensation at a Discount] After a contract is not concluded, void, or revoked, when determining the return of property, factors such as appreciation or depreciation of the property shall be fully considered. After a bilateral contract is not concluded, void, or revoked, if both parties have obtained property under the contract, they shall return it to each other. Where property such as equity or real estate to be returned has appreciated or depreciated relative to the price agreed in the contract, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider market factors and the correlation between the transferee's operations, additions, or other acts and the appreciation or depreciation of the property, and reasonably allocate or apportion it between the parties to prevent one party from profiting from the contract not being concluded, being void, or being revoked. In the case where the subject matter has been destroyed, resold to others, or otherwise cannot be returned, if a party claims the return of the original property, the People's Court shall not support it; but if the party claims compensation at a discount, the People's Court shall support it according to law. When determining the discount, the price agreed upon by the parties at the time of transaction shall serve as the basis, and the compensation standard shall be determined comprehensively by also considering the gains obtained by the party upon the destruction or resale of the subject matter. Insurance proceeds or other compensation received by the party upon the destruction of the subject matter, and the consideration obtained upon resale, are all benefits derived by the party from the subject matter. The portion of gains exceeding or falling short of the price shall also be reasonably allocated or apportioned between the parties.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　【价款返还】双务合同不成立、无效或者被撤销时，标的物返还与价款返还互为对待给付，双方应当同时返还。关于应否支付利息问题，只要一方对标的物有使用情形的，一般应当支付使用费，该费用可与占有价款一方应当支付的资金占用费相互抵销，故在一方返还原物前，另一方仅须支付本金，而无须支付利息。

**Article 34** [Return of Price] Where a bilateral contract is not concluded, void, or revoked, the return of the subject matter and the return of the price are mutual consideration, and both parties shall return them simultaneously. As to whether interest shall be paid, as long as one party has used the subject matter, it shall generally pay a usage fee, which may be offset against the capital occupation fee that the party in possession of the price shall pay. Therefore, before one party returns the original property, the other party need only pay the principal and need not pay interest.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　【损害赔偿】合同不成立、无效或者被撤销时，仅返还财产或者折价补偿不足以弥补损失，一方还可以向有过错的另一方请求损害赔偿。在确定损害赔偿范围时，既要根据当事人的过错程度合理确定责任，又要考虑在确定财产返还范围时已经考虑过的财产增值或者贬值因素，避免双重获利或者双重受损的现象发生。

**Article 35** [Damages] Where a contract is not concluded, void, or revoked, if merely returning the property or compensating at a discount is insufficient to cover the losses, a party may also claim damages from the other party at fault. When determining the scope of damages, the liability shall be reasonably determined based on the degree of fault of the parties, and the factors of property appreciation or depreciation already considered when determining the scope of property return shall also be taken into account to avoid double compensation or double loss.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　【合同无效时的释明问题】在双务合同中，原告起诉请求确认合同有效并请求继续履行合同，被告主张合同无效的，或者原告起诉请求确认合同无效并返还财产，而被告主张合同有效的，都要防止机械适用“不告不理”原则，仅就当事人的诉讼请求进行审理，而应向原告释明变更或者增加诉讼请求，或者向被告释明提出同时履行抗辩，尽可能一次性解决纠纷。例如，基于合同有给付行为的原告请求确认合同无效，但并未提出返还原物或者折价补偿、赔偿损失等请求的，人民法院应当向其释明，告知其一并提出相应诉讼请求；原告请求确认合同无效并要求被告返还原物或者赔偿损失，被告基于合同也有给付行为的，人民法院同样应当向被告释明，告知其也可以提出返还请求；人民法院经审理认定合同无效的，除了要在判决书“本院认为”部分对同时返还作出认定外，还应当在判项中作出明确表述，避免因判令单方返还而出现不公平的结果。  
第一审人民法院未予释明，第二审人民法院认为应当对合同不成立、无效或者被撤销的法律后果作出判决的，可以直接释明并改判。当然，如果返还财产或者赔偿损失的范围确实难以确定或者双方争议较大的，也可以告知当事人通过另行起诉等方式解决，并在裁判文书中予以明确。  
当事人按照释明变更诉讼请求或者提出抗辩的，人民法院应当将其归纳为案件争议焦点，组织当事人充分举证、质证、辩论。

**Article 36** [Clarification Issues When Contract Is Void] In a bilateral contract, where the plaintiff sues for confirmation of the contract's validity and continued performance, and the defendant argues that the contract is void; or where the plaintiff sues for confirmation of the contract's invalidity and return of property, and the defendant argues that the contract is valid, the principle of "no trial without complaint" shall not be mechanically applied to only adjudicate on the parties' claims. Instead, the court shall explain to the plaintiff to modify or add claims, or explain to the defendant to raise a defense of simultaneous performance, so as to resolve the dispute in a single proceeding as far as possible. For example, if the plaintiff, who has made a performance under the contract, requests confirmation that the contract is void but does not request return of the original property or compensation at a discount, or damages, the People's Court shall explain to it and inform it to propose corresponding claims together. If the plaintiff requests confirmation that the contract is void and requests the defendant to return the original property or pay damages, and the defendant has also made a performance under the contract, the People's Court shall likewise explain to the defendant and inform it that it may also request return. If, after trial, the People's Court determines that the contract is void, it shall not only make a finding on simultaneous return in the "opinion of the court" section of the judgment, but also expressly state it in the operative part to avoid an unfair result from ordering unilateral return.  
If the court of first instance fails to give such clarification and the court of second instance considers that a judgment on the legal consequences of the contract not being concluded, being void, or being revoked should be made, it may directly give clarification and amend the judgment. Of course, if the scope of return of property or damages is truly difficult to determine or the dispute between the parties is substantial, the parties may be informed to resolve it through a separate lawsuit or other means, and this shall be clarified in the adjudicatory document.  
If a party modifies its claims or raises a defense in accordance with the clarification, the People's Court shall summarize them as the focus of the dispute in the case and organize the parties to fully adduce evidence, cross-examine, and debate.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　【未经批准合同的效力】法律、行政法规规定某类合同应当办理批准手续生效的，如商业银行法、证券法、保险法等法律规定购买商业银行、证券公司、保险公司5%以上股权须经相关主管部门批准，依据《合同法》第44条第2款的规定，批准是合同的法定生效条件，未经批准的合同因欠缺法律规定的特别生效条件而未生效。实践中的一个突出问题是，把未生效合同认定为无效合同，或者虽认定为未生效，却按无效合同处理。无效合同从本质上来说是欠缺合同的有效要件，或者具有合同无效的法定事由，自始不发生法律效力。而未生效合同已具备合同的有效要件，对双方具有一定的拘束力，任何一方不得擅自撤回、解除、变更，但因欠缺法律、行政法规规定或当事人约定的特别生效条件，在该生效条件成就前，不能产生请求对方履行合同主要权利义务的法律效力。

**Article 37** [Validity of Contracts Not Approved] Where a law or administrative regulation provides that a certain type of contract shall undergo an approval procedure to take effect, such as the provisions in the Commercial Banking Law, Securities Law, Insurance Law, etc., that the acquisition of 5% or more of the equity of a commercial bank, securities company, or insurance company requires approval from the relevant competent authority, approval is a statutory condition for the contract to take effect in accordance with Article 44(2) of the Contract Law. A contract that has not been approved is not effective because it lacks the special condition for validity prescribed by law. A prominent problem in practice is that a contract that is not yet effective is determined to be a void contract, or although it is determined to be not yet effective, it is treated as a void contract. A void contract is essentially one that lacks the valid elements of a contract or has statutory grounds for invalidity, and it has no legal effect from the outset. In contrast, a contract that is not yet effective already possesses the valid elements of a contract and has a certain binding force on both parties: neither party may arbitrarily withdraw, rescind, or modify it. However, because it lacks the special condition for taking effect prescribed by law or administrative regulation or agreed by the parties, before that condition is fulfilled, it cannot produce the legal effect of requiring the other party to perform the principal rights and obligations under the contract.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　【报批义务及相关违约条款独立生效】须经行政机关批准生效的合同，对报批义务及未履行报批义务的违约责任等相关内容作出专门约定的，该约定独立生效。一方因另一方不履行报批义务，请求解除合同并请求其承担合同约定的相应违约责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 38** [Independent Effectiveness of Reporting Obligation and Related Default Clauses] Where a contract that requires approval from an administrative authority to take effect contains a specific agreement on the obligation to apply for approval and the liability for breach of contract for failure to perform such obligation, such agreement shall be independently effective. If one party requests to rescind the contract and requests the other party to bear the corresponding liability for breach of contract as agreed in the contract due to the other party's failure to perform the obligation to apply for approval, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　【报批义务的释明】须经行政机关批准生效的合同，一方请求另一方履行合同主要权利义务的，人民法院应当向其释明，将诉讼请求变更为请求履行报批义务。一方变更诉讼请求的，人民法院依法予以支持；经释明后当事人拒绝变更的，应当驳回其诉讼请求，但不影响其另行提起诉讼。

**Article 39** [Clarification Regarding Obligation to Apply for Approval] For a contract that requires approval from an administrative authority to take effect, if one party requests the other party to perform the principal rights and obligations under the contract, the People's Court shall explain to it that the claim shall be changed to a request for performance of the obligation to apply for approval. If a party changes its claim, the People's Court shall support it according to law. If, after clarification, the party refuses to change its claim, the claim shall be dismissed, but this does not affect its right to file a separate lawsuit.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　【判决履行报批义务后的处理】人民法院判决一方履行报批义务后，该当事人拒绝履行，经人民法院强制执行仍未履行，对方请求其承担合同违约责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。一方依据判决履行报批义务，行政机关予以批准，合同发生完全的法律效力，其请求对方履行合同的，人民法院依法予以支持；行政机关没有批准，合同不具有法律上的可履行性，一方请求解除合同的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 40** [Handling After Judgment to Perform Obligation to Apply for Approval] After the People's Court has adjudicated that a party shall perform the obligation to apply for approval, if that party refuses to perform and, after compulsory execution by the People's Court, still fails to perform, and the other party requests it to bear liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support such request according to law. If a party performs the obligation to apply for approval in accordance with the judgment, and the administrative authority grants approval, the contract acquires full legal effect, and if it requests the other party to perform the contract, the People's Court shall support it according to law. If the administrative authority does not grant approval, the contract does not have legal enforceability, and if one party requests to rescind the contract, the People's Court shall support it according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　【盖章行为的法律效力】司法实践中，有些公司有意刻制两套甚至多套公章，有的法定代表人或者代理人甚至私刻公章，订立合同时恶意加盖非备案的公章或者假公章，发生纠纷后法人以加盖的是假公章为由否定合同效力的情形并不鲜见。人民法院在审理案件时，应当主要审查签约人于盖章之时有无代表权或者代理权，从而根据代表或者代理的相关规则来确定合同的效力。  
法定代表人或者其授权之人在合同上加盖法人公章的行为，表明其是以法人名义签订合同，除《公司法》第16条等法律对其职权有特别规定的情形外，应当由法人承担相应的法律后果。法人以法定代表人事后已无代表权、加盖的是假章、所盖之章与备案公章不一致等为由否定合同效力的，人民法院不予支持。  
代理人以被代理人名义签订合同，要取得合法授权。代理人取得合法授权后，以被代理人名义签订的合同，应当由被代理人承担责任。被代理人以代理人事后已无代理权、加盖的是假章、所盖之章与备案公章不一致等为由否定合同效力的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 41** [Legal Effect of Affixing Seal] In judicial practice, some companies intentionally engrave two or more sets of company seals, and some legal representatives or agents even privately engrave seals, maliciously affixing non-filed seals or fake seals when concluding contracts. After a dispute arises, the legal person denies the validity of the contract on the ground that a fake seal was affixed; such situations are not uncommon. When adjudicating a case, a People's Court shall primarily examine whether the signatory had the power of representation or the power of agency at the time of affixing the seal, and shall then determine the validity of the contract in accordance with the relevant rules on representation or agency.  
The act of a legal representative or a person authorized by him/her affixing the legal person's seal on a contract indicates that the contract is concluded in the name of the legal person. Except where the law, such as Article 16 of the Company Law, has special provisions on its functions and powers, the legal person shall bear the corresponding legal consequences. Where the legal person denies the validity of the contract on the grounds that, after the fact, the legal representative no longer has the power of representation, a fake seal was affixed, or the affixed seal is inconsistent with the filed seal, the People's Court shall not support such denial.  
An agent who concludes a contract in the name of the principal must obtain legal authorization. After the agent obtains legal authorization, the contract concluded in the name of the principal shall be performed by the principal, who shall bear the liability. Where the principal denies the validity of the contract on the grounds that, after the fact, the agent no longer has the power of agency, a fake seal was affixed, or the affixed seal is inconsistent with the filed seal, the People's Court shall not support such denial.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　【撤销权的行使】撤销权应当由当事人行使。当事人未请求撤销的，人民法院不应当依职权撤销合同。一方请求另一方履行合同，另一方以合同具有可撤销事由提出抗辩的，人民法院应当在审查合同是否具有可撤销事由以及是否超过法定期间等事实的基础上，对合同是否可撤销作出判断，不能仅以当事人未提起诉讼或者反诉为由不予审查或者不予支持。一方主张合同无效，依据的却是可撤销事由，此时人民法院应当全面审查合同是否具有无效事由以及当事人主张的可撤销事由。当事人关于合同无效的事由成立的，人民法院应当认定合同无效。当事人主张合同无效的理由不成立，而可撤销的事由成立的，因合同无效和可撤销的后果相同，人民法院也可以结合当事人的诉讼请求，直接判决撤销合同。  
（二）关于合同履行与救济  
在认定以物抵债协议的性质和效力时，要根据订立协议时履行期限是否已经届满予以区别对待。合同解除、违约责任都是非违约方寻求救济的主要方式，人民法院在认定合同应否解除时，要根据当事人有无解除权、是约定解除还是法定解除等不同情形，分别予以处理。在确定违约责任时，尤其要注意依法适用违约金调整的相关规则，避免简单地以民间借贷利率的司法保护上限作为调整依据。

**Article 42** [Exercise of the Right to Revoke] The right to revoke shall be exercised by the party concerned. If the party does not request revocation, the People's Court shall not revoke the contract ex officio. Where one party requests the other party to perform the contract, and the other party raises a defense on the ground that the contract has a cause for revocability, the People's Court shall, on the basis of examining whether the contract has a cause for revocability and whether the statutory period has expired, make a judgment on whether the contract is revocable. The People's Court shall not refuse to examine or refuse to support the claim solely on the ground that the party has not filed a lawsuit or counterclaim. Where one party asserts that the contract is void, but the ground relied upon is a cause for revocability, the People's Court shall comprehensively examine whether the contract has a cause for voidness and the cause for revocability asserted by the party. If the cause for voidness of the contract asserted by the party is established, the People's Court shall find the contract void. If the reason asserted by the party for the voidness of the contract is not established, but the cause for revocability is established, since the consequences of voidness and revocability are the same, the People's Court may, in light of the party's claim, directly render a judgment revoking the contract.  
(II) Regarding Contract Performance and Remedies  
When determining the nature and validity of an agreement to use property to satisfy a debt, a distinction shall be made based on whether the performance period has expired at the time of concluding the agreement. Dissolution of contract and liability for breach of contract are the main ways for the non-breaching party to seek remedies. When determining whether a contract shall be dissolved, the People's Court shall, in light of different circumstances such as whether the party has the right to dissolve, whether the dissolution is agreed or statutory, handle the matter separately. When determining liability for breach of contract, special attention shall be paid to applying the relevant rules on adjustment of liquidated damages in accordance with the law, and the upper limit of the judicial protection of interest rates for private lending shall not be simply used as the basis for adjustment.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　【抵销】抵销权既可以通知的方式行使，也可以提出抗辩或者提起反诉的方式行使。抵销的意思表示自到达对方时生效，抵销一经生效，其效力溯及自抵销条件成就之时，双方互负的债务在同等数额内消灭。双方互负的债务数额，是截至抵销条件成就之时各自负有的包括主债务、利息、违约金、赔偿金等在内的全部债务数额。行使抵销权一方享有的债权不足以抵销全部债务数额，当事人对抵销顺序又没有特别约定的，应当根据实现债权的费用、利息、主债务的顺序进行抵销。

**Article 43** [Set-off] The right of set-off may be exercised by means of notice, or by means of raising a defense or filing a counterclaim. The declaration of intention of set-off shall take effect upon reaching the other party. Once the set-off takes effect, its validity shall retroact to the time when the conditions for set-off are satisfied, and the mutual debts owed by the parties shall be extinguished to the same amount. The amount of mutual debts owed by the parties refers to the total amount of debts owed by each party, including the principal debt, interest, liquidated damages, compensation, etc., as of the time when the conditions for set-off are satisfied. Where the claim held by the party exercising the right of set-off is insufficient to set off the total amount of debts, and the parties have not specifically agreed on the order of set-off, the set-off shall be carried out in the order of the expenses for realizing the claim, interest, and principal debt.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　【履行期届满后达成的以物抵债协议】当事人在债务履行期限届满后达成以物抵债协议，抵债物尚未交付债权人，债权人请求债务人交付的，人民法院要着重审查以物抵债协议是否存在恶意损害第三人合法权益等情形，避免虚假诉讼的发生。经审查，不存在以上情况，且无其他无效事由的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
当事人在一审程序中因达成以物抵债协议申请撤回起诉的，人民法院可予准许。当事人在二审程序中申请撤回上诉的，人民法院应当告知其申请撤回起诉。当事人申请撤回起诉，经审查不损害国家利益、社会公共利益、他人合法权益的，人民法院可予准许。当事人不申请撤回起诉，请求人民法院出具调解书对以物抵债协议予以确认的，因债务人完全可以立即履行该协议，没有必要由人民法院出具调解书，故人民法院不应准许，同时应当继续对原债权债务关系进行审理。

**Article 44** [Agreement to Use Property to Satisfy Debt Reached After Maturity of the Performance Period] Where the parties reach an agreement to use property to satisfy a debt after the expiration of the performance period for the debt, and the property has not yet been delivered to the creditor, and the creditor requests the debtor to deliver the property, the People's Court shall focus on examining whether the agreement to use property to satisfy a debt involves maliciously harming the lawful rights and interests of third parties or other circumstances, so as to avoid the occurrence of false litigation. Upon examination, if none of the above circumstances exist and there are no other causes for invalidity, the People's Court shall support the claim in accordance with the law.  
Where a party applies for withdrawal of the complaint during the first-instance proceedings due to reaching an agreement to use property to satisfy a debt, the People's Court may permit it. Where a party applies for withdrawal of the appeal during the second-instance proceedings, the People's Court shall inform the party to apply for withdrawal of the complaint. Where a party applies for withdrawal of the complaint, and upon examination, it does not harm the national interest, the social public interest, or the lawful rights and interests of others, the People's Court may permit it. Where a party does not apply for withdrawal of the complaint, but requests the People's Court to issue a mediation document to confirm the agreement to use property to satisfy a debt, since the debtor can fully perform the agreement immediately, there is no need for the People's Court to issue a mediation document, so the People's Court shall not permit it, and shall continue to adjudicate the original creditor-debt relationship.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　【履行期届满前达成的以物抵债协议】当事人在债务履行期届满前达成以物抵债协议，抵债物尚未交付债权人，债权人请求债务人交付的，因此种情况不同于本纪要第71条规定的让与担保，人民法院应当向其释明，其应当根据原债权债务关系提起诉讼。经释明后当事人仍拒绝变更诉讼请求的，应当驳回其诉讼请求，但不影响其根据原债权债务关系另行提起诉讼。

**Article 45** [Agreement to Use Property to Satisfy Debt Reached Before Maturity of the Performance Period] Where the parties reach an agreement to use property to satisfy a debt before the expiration of the performance period for the debt, and the property has not yet been delivered to the creditor, and the creditor requests the debtor to deliver the property, since this situation differs from the assignment guarantee as stipulated in Article 71 of this Summary, the People's Court shall enlighten the creditor that it shall file a lawsuit based on the original creditor-debt relationship. If the party still refuses to change the claim after being enlightened, the lawsuit claim shall be dismissed, but this shall not affect the party's right to file another lawsuit based on the original creditor-debt relationship.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　【通知解除的条件】审判实践中，部分人民法院对合同法司法解释（二）第24条的理解存在偏差，认为不论发出解除通知的一方有无解除权，只要另一方未在异议期限内以起诉方式提出异议，就判令解除合同，这不符合合同法关于合同解除权行使的有关规定。对该条的准确理解是，只有享有法定或者约定解除权的当事人才能以通知方式解除合同。不享有解除权的一方向另一方发出解除通知，另一方即便未在异议期限内提起诉讼，也不发生合同解除的效果。人民法院在审理案件时，应当审查发出解除通知的一方是否享有约定或者法定的解除权来决定合同应否解除，不能仅以受通知一方在约定或者法定的异议期限届满内未起诉这一事实就认定合同已经解除。

**Article 46** [Conditions for Notice of Dissolution] In adjudication practice, some People's Courts have a biased understanding of Article 24 of the Judicial Interpretation (II) of the Contract Law, believing that regardless of whether the party issuing the notice of dissolution has the right to dissolve, as long as the other party fails to raise an objection by filing a lawsuit within the objection period, the contract shall be ordered to be dissolved. This does not comply with the relevant provisions of the Contract Law on the exercise of the right to dissolve a contract. The accurate understanding of this article is that only a party who has a statutory or agreed right to dissolve may dissolve the contract by means of notice. If a party who does not have the right to dissolve issues a notice of dissolution to the other party, even if the other party does not file a lawsuit within the objection period, the effect of dissolution of the contract does not occur. When adjudicating a case, the People's Court shall examine whether the party issuing the notice of dissolution has the agreed or statutory right to dissolve, in order to decide whether the contract shall be dissolved. The court shall not determine that the contract has been dissolved solely based on the fact that the notified party did not file a lawsuit within the agreed or statutory objection period.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　【约定解除条件】合同约定的解除条件成就时，守约方以此为由请求解除合同的，人民法院应当审查违约方的违约程度是否显著轻微，是否影响守约方合同目的实现，根据诚实信用原则，确定合同应否解除。违约方的违约程度显著轻微，不影响守约方合同目的实现，守约方请求解除合同的，人民法院不予支持；反之，则依法予以支持。

**Article 47** [Conditions for Agreed Dissolution] When the conditions for dissolution agreed in the contract are satisfied, and the non-breaching party requests dissolution of the contract on this ground, the People's Court shall examine whether the degree of breach by the breaching party is de minimis and whether it affects the realization of the purpose of the contract by the non-breaching party, and shall determine whether the contract shall be dissolved in accordance with the principle of good faith. If the degree of breach by the breaching party is de minimis and does not affect the realization of the purpose of the contract by the non-breaching party, the People's Court shall not support the non-breaching party's request for dissolution of the contract; otherwise, it shall support the request in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　【违约方起诉解除】违约方不享有单方解除合同的权利。但是，在一些长期性合同如房屋租赁合同履行过程中，双方形成合同僵局，一概不允许违约方通过起诉的方式解除合同，有时对双方都不利。在此前提下，符合下列条件，违约方起诉请求解除合同的，人民法院依法予以支持：  
（1）违约方不存在恶意违约的情形；  
（2）违约方继续履行合同，对其显失公平；  
（3）守约方拒绝解除合同，违反诚实信用原则。  
人民法院判决解除合同的，违约方本应当承担的违约责任不能因解除合同而减少或者免除。

**Article 48** [Dissolution of Contract by the Breaching Party through Lawsuit] The breaching party does not have the right to unilaterally dissolve the contract. However, in the course of performance of some long-term contracts, such as house lease contracts, a contractual deadlock may arise between the parties. If the breaching party is absolutely not allowed to dissolve the contract through a lawsuit, it may sometimes be disadvantageous to both parties. Under this premise, if the following conditions are met, the People's Court shall support the breaching party's request to dissolve the contract through a lawsuit in accordance with the law:  
(1) There is no malicious breach by the breaching party;  
(2) Continued performance of the contract by the breaching party would be manifestly unfair to it;  
(3) The non-breaching party's refusal to dissolve the contract violates the principle of good faith.  
Where the People's Court renders a judgment dissolving the contract, the liability for breach of contract that the breaching party should bear shall not be reduced or exempted due to the dissolution of the contract.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　【合同解除的法律后果】合同解除时，一方依据合同中有关违约金、约定损害赔偿的计算方法、定金责任等违约责任条款的约定，请求另一方承担违约责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
双务合同解除时人民法院的释明问题，参照本纪要第36条的相关规定处理。

**Article 49** [Legal Consequences of Dissolution of Contract] When a contract is dissolved, if one party requests the other party to bear liability for breach of contract in accordance with the provisions of the contract on liquidated damages, the calculation method for agreed damages, deposit liability, or other liability for breach of contract clauses, the People's Court shall support the claim in accordance with the law.  
The issue of elucidation by the People's Court upon dissolution of a bilateral contract shall be dealt with by reference to the relevant provisions of Article 36 of this Summary.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　【违约金过高标准及举证责任】认定约定违约金是否过高，一般应当以《合同法》第113条规定的损失为基础进行判断，这里的损失包括合同履行后可以获得的利益。除借款合同外的双务合同，作为对价的价款或者报酬给付之债，并非借款合同项下的还款义务，不能以受法律保护的民间借贷利率上限作为判断违约金是否过高的标准，而应当兼顾合同履行情况、当事人过错程度以及预期利益等因素综合确定。主张违约金过高的违约方应当对违约金是否过高承担举证责任。  
（三）关于借款合同  
人民法院在审理借款合同纠纷案件过程中，要根据防范化解重大金融风险、金融服务实体经济、降低融资成本的精神，区别对待金融借贷与民间借贷，并适用不同规则与利率标准。要依法否定高利转贷行为、职业放贷行为的效力，充分发挥司法的示范、引导作用，促进金融服务实体经济。要注意到，为深化利率市场化改革，推动降低实体利率水平，自2019年8月20日起，中国人民银行已经授权全国银行间同业拆借中心于每月20日（遇节假日顺延）9时30分公布贷款市场报价利率（LPR）,中国人民银行贷款基准利率这一标准已经取消。因此，自此之后人民法院裁判贷款利息的基本标准应改为全国银行间同业拆借中心公布的贷款市场报价利率。应予注意的是，贷款利率标准尽管发生了变化，但存款基准利率并未发生相应变化，相关标准仍可适用。

**Article 50** [Standard for Excessive Liquidated Damages and Burden of Adducing Evidence] In determining whether the agreed liquidated damages are excessively high, the determination shall generally be made on the basis of the losses as provided for in Article 113 of the Contract Law, which include the benefits obtainable after performance of the contract. For bilateral contracts other than loan contracts, the obligation to pay the price or remuneration as consideration is not a repayment obligation under a loan contract. Therefore, the upper limit of the interest rate for private lending protected by law shall not be used as the standard for judging whether liquidated damages are excessively high. Instead, factors such as the performance of the contract, the degree of fault of the parties, and the expected benefits shall be taken into comprehensive consideration for determination. The breaching party that claims that the liquidated damages are excessively high shall bear the burden of adducing evidence to prove that the liquidated damages are excessively high.  
(III) Regarding Loan Contracts  
In the process of adjudicating cases involving disputes over loan contracts, People's Courts shall, in accordance with the spirit of preventing and mitigating major financial risks, ensuring that financial services serve the real economy, and reducing financing costs, treat financial lending and private lending differently and apply different rules and interest rate standards. The validity of usury relending and professional moneylending activities shall be denied in accordance with the law, and the exemplary and guiding role of the judiciary shall be fully utilized to promote financial services for the real economy. It shall be noted that, in order to deepen the market-oriented reform of interest rates and promote the reduction of real interest rates, since August 20, 2019, the People's Bank of China has authorized the National Interbank Funding Center to publish the Loan Prime Rate (LPR) at 9:30 a.m. on the 20th of each month (postponed in case of holidays). The standard of the benchmark loan interest rate of the People's Bank of China has been abolished. Therefore, from that date onward, the basic standard for adjudicating loan interest by People's Courts shall be changed to the Loan Prime Rate published by the National Interbank Funding Center. It shall be noted that, although the standard for loan interest rates has changed, the benchmark deposit interest rate has not changed correspondingly, and the relevant standards may still be applicable.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　【变相利息的认定】 金融借款合同纠纷中，借款人认为金融机构以服务费、咨询费、顾问费、管理费等为名变相收取利息，金融机构或者由其指定的人收取的相关费用不合理的，人民法院可以根据提供服务的实际情况确定借款人应否支付或者酌减相关费用。

**Article 51** [Determination of Disguised Interest] In disputes over financial loan contracts, if the borrower considers that the financial institution has disguised interest collection in the name of service fees, consultation fees, advisory fees, management fees, etc., and the relevant fees charged by the financial institution or a person designated by it are unreasonable, the People's Court may, based on the actual circumstances of the services provided, determine whether the borrower shall pay or appropriately reduce the relevant fees.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　【高利转贷】民间借贷中，出借人的资金必须是自有资金。出借人套取金融机构信贷资金又高利转贷给借款人的民间借贷行为，既增加了融资成本，又扰乱了信贷秩序，根据民间借贷司法解释第14条第1项的规定，应当认定此类民间借贷行为无效。人民法院在适用该条规定时，应当注意把握以下几点：一是要审查出借人的资金来源。借款人能够举证证明在签订借款合同时出借人尚欠银行贷款未还的，一般可以推定为出借人套取信贷资金，但出借人能够举反证予以推翻的除外；二是从宽认定“高利”转贷行为的标准，只要出借人通过转贷行为牟利的，就可以认定为是“高利”转贷行为；三是对该条规定的“借款人事先知道或者应当知道的”要件，不宜把握过苛。实践中，只要出借人在签订借款合同时存在尚欠银行贷款未还事实的，一般可以认为满足了该条规定的“借款人事先知道或者应当知道”这一要件。

**Article 52** [Usury Relending] In private lending, the lender's funds must be its own funds. Where a lender obtains credit funds from a financial institution and then relends them at a high interest rate to a borrower, such private lending activity not only increases financing costs but also disrupts the credit order. Pursuant to Article 14, Item 1 of the Judicial Interpretation on Private Lending, such private lending activity shall be determined to be void. When applying this article, the People's Court shall pay attention to the following points: First, the source of the lender's funds shall be examined. If the borrower can adduce evidence proving that, at the time of concluding the loan contract, the lender still had outstanding bank loans that were not repaid, it may generally be presumed that the lender obtained credit funds from the financial institution, unless the lender can adduce rebuttal evidence to the contrary. Second, the standard for determining "high interest" relending shall be lenient. As long as the lender seeks profit through the relending activity, it may be determined to be a "high interest" relending activity. Third, the requirement that "the borrower knew or should have known in advance" as stipulated in this article shall not be applied too strictly. In practice, as long as the lender had outstanding bank loans not repaid at the time of concluding the loan contract, it may generally be considered that the requirement of "the borrower knew or should have known" in this article is satisfied.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　【职业放贷人】未依法取得放贷资格的以民间借贷为业的法人，以及以民间借贷为业的非法人组织或者自然人从事的民间借贷行为，应当依法认定无效。同一出借人在一定期间内多次反复从事有偿民间借贷行为的，一般可以认定为是职业放贷人。民间借贷比较活跃的地方的高级人民法院或者经其授权的中级人民法院，可以根据本地区的实际情况制定具体的认定标准。

**Article 53** [Professional Moneylenders] The private lending activities of a legal person engaged in private lending as a business without obtaining lending qualifications in accordance with the law, as well as those of an unincorporated organization or a natural person engaged in private lending as a business, shall be determined to be void in accordance with the law. Where the same lender repeatedly engages in compensated private lending activities multiple times within a certain period, it may generally be determined as a professional moneylender. The Higher People's Court of a place where private lending is relatively active, or an Intermediate People's Court authorized by it, may formulate specific determination standards based on the actual circumstances of the region.

### 四、关于担保纠纷案件的审理

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　【独立担保】从属性是担保的基本属性，但由银行或者非银行金融机构开立的独立保函除外。独立保函纠纷案件依据《最高人民法院关于审理独立保函纠纷案件若干问题的规定》处理。需要进一步明确的是：凡是由银行或者非银行金融机构开立的符合该司法解释第1条、第3条规定情形的保函，无论是用于国际商事交易还是用于国内商事交易，均不影响保函的效力。银行或者非银行金融机构之外的当事人开立的独立保函，以及当事人有关排除担保从属性的约定，应当认定无效。但是，根据“无效法律行为的转换”原理，在否定其独立担保效力的同时，应当将其认定为从属性担保。此时，如果主合同有效，则担保合同有效，担保人与主债务人承担连带保证责任。主合同无效，则该所谓的独立担保也随之无效，担保人无过错的，不承担责任；担保人有过错的，其承担民事责任的部分，不应超过债务人不能清偿部分的三分之一。

**Article 54** [Independent Guarantee] Subsidiary nature is the basic attribute of a guarantee, except for independent guarantee letters issued by banks or non-bank financial institutions. Cases involving disputes over independent guarantee letters shall be handled in accordance with the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Adjudication of Disputes over Independent Guarantee Letters. What needs to be further clarified is that, for any guarantee letter issued by a bank or non-bank financial institution that meets the circumstances set out in Articles 1 and 3 of such Judicial Interpretation, whether used for international commercial transactions or domestic commercial transactions, the validity of the guarantee letter shall not be affected. An independent guarantee letter issued by a party other than a bank or non-bank financial institution, as well as an agreement between the parties to exclude the subsidiary nature of a guarantee, shall be determined to be void. However, in accordance with the principle of "conversion of void legal acts", while denying the validity of the independent guarantee, it shall be determined as a subsidiary guarantee. In such case, if the principal contract is valid, then the guarantee contract is valid, and the guarantor and the principal debtor shall bear joint and several suretyship liability. If the principal contract is void, the so-called independent guarantee also becomes void. If the guarantor is without fault, it shall not bear liability; if the guarantor is at fault, the portion of civil liability it bears shall not exceed one-third of the portion that the debtor is unable to repay.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　【担保责任的范围】担保人承担的担保责任范围不应当大于主债务，是担保从属性的必然要求。当事人约定的担保责任的范围大于主债务的，如针对担保责任约定专门的违约责任、担保责任的数额高于主债务、担保责任约定的利息高于主债务利息、担保责任的履行期先于主债务履行期届满，等等，均应当认定大于主债务部分的约定无效，从而使担保责任缩减至主债务的范围。

**Article 55** [Scope of Guarantee Liability] The scope of guarantee liability borne by the guarantor shall not be greater than the principal debt; this is an inevitable requirement of the subsidiary nature of a guarantee. If the scope of guarantee liability agreed by the parties is greater than the principal debt, such as stipulating a separate liability for breach of contract for the guarantee liability, the amount of the guarantee liability being higher than the principal debt, the interest agreed for the guarantee liability being higher than the interest on the principal debt, the performance period of the guarantee liability expiring before the performance period of the principal debt, etc., the part of the agreement that is greater than the principal debt shall be determined to be void, thereby reducing the guarantee liability to the scope of the principal debt.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　【混合担保中担保人之间的追偿问题】被担保的债权既有保证又有第三人提供的物的担保的，担保法司法解释第38条明确规定，承担了担保责任的担保人可以要求其他担保人清偿其应当分担的份额。但《物权法》第176条并未作出类似规定，根据《物权法》第178条关于“担保法与本法的规定不一致的，适用本法”的规定，承担了担保责任的担保人向其他担保人追偿的，人民法院不予支持，但担保人在担保合同中约定可以相互追偿的除外。

**Article 56** [Right of Recourse Among Guarantors in Mixed Guarantee] Where the secured claim has both a suretyship and a security interest in property provided by a third party, Article 38 of the Judicial Interpretation of the Guarantee Law explicitly provides that a guarantor who has borne the guarantee liability may demand that other guarantors discharge the portion they should bear. However, Article 176 of the Property Law does not have a similar provision. Pursuant to Article 178 of the Property Law, which states that "where the Guarantee Law is inconsistent with this Law, this Law shall apply," if a guarantor who has borne the guarantee liability seeks recourse against other guarantors, the People's Court shall not support it, unless the guarantors have agreed in the guarantee contract that they may have recourse against each other.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　【借新还旧的担保物权】贷款到期后，借款人与贷款人订立新的借款合同，将新贷用于归还旧贷，旧贷因清偿而消灭，为旧贷设立的担保物权也随之消灭。贷款人以旧贷上的担保物权尚未进行涂销登记为由，主张对新贷行使担保物权的，人民法院不予支持，但当事人约定继续为新贷提供担保的除外。

**Article 57** [Security Interest for Refinancing (New Loan to Repay Old Loan)] After a loan becomes due, the borrower and the lender conclude a new loan contract, and the new loan is used to repay the old loan. The old loan is extinguished due to repayment, and the security interest established for the old loan is also extinguished accordingly. If the lender claims to exercise the security interest over the new loan on the ground that the security interest on the old loan has not yet been cancelled from the register, the People's Court shall not support it, unless the parties have agreed that the security interest shall continue to secure the new loan.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　【担保债权的范围】以登记作为公示方式的不动产担保物权的担保范围，一般应当以登记的范围为准。但是，我国目前不动产担保物权登记，不同地区的系统设置及登记规则并不一致，人民法院在审理案件时应当充分注意制度设计上的差别，作出符合实际的判断：一是多数省区市的登记系统未设置“担保范围”栏目，仅有“被担保主债权数额（最高债权数额）”的表述，且只能填写固定数字。而当事人在合同中又往往约定担保物权的担保范围包括主债权及其利息、违约金等附属债权，致使合同约定的担保范围与登记不一致。显然，这种不一致是由于该地区登记系统设置及登记规则造成的该地区的普遍现象。人民法院以合同约定认定担保物权的担保范围，是符合实际的妥当选择。二是一些省区市不动产登记系统设置与登记规则比较规范，担保物权登记范围与合同约定一致在该地区是常态或者普遍现象，人民法院在审理案件时，应当以登记的担保范围为准。

**Article 58** [Scope of Secured Claims] The scope of security for a real property security interest that uses registration as the method of public notice shall generally be based on the scope registered. However, currently, in the registration of real property security interests in China, the system settings and registration rules vary in different regions. When adjudicating cases, People's Courts shall pay full attention to the differences in institutional design and make judgments that are consistent with reality: First, in most provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, the registration system does not have a column for "scope of security," only an expression of "amount of principal secured claim (maximum claim amount)," and only a fixed number may be entered. However, the parties often stipulate in the contract that the scope of security of the security interest includes the principal claim and its accrued interest, liquidated damages, and other ancillary claims, resulting in inconsistency between the contractual scope of security and the registered scope. Obviously, this inconsistency is a widespread phenomenon in that region caused by the registration system settings and registration rules of that region. It is a practical and appropriate choice for the People's Court to determine the scope of security of the security interest based on the contract. Second, in some provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, the real property registration system settings and registration rules are relatively standardized. It is normal or common in that region for the registered scope of security for the security interest to be consistent with the contractual agreement. In such cases, when adjudicating cases, the People's Court shall base its determination on the registered scope of security.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　【主债权诉讼时效届满的法律后果】抵押权人应当在主债权的诉讼时效期间内行使抵押权。抵押权人在主债权诉讼时效届满前未行使抵押权，抵押人在主债权诉讼时效届满后请求涂销抵押权登记的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
以登记作为公示方法的权利质权，参照适用前款规定。  
（二）关于不动产担保物权

**Article 59** [Legal Consequences of Expiration of Limitation of Action on Principal Claim] The mortgagee shall exercise the mortgage within the limitation of action for the principal claim. If the mortgagee fails to exercise the mortgage before the expiration of the limitation of action for the principal claim, and the mortgagor requests cancellation of the mortgage registration after the expiration of the limitation of action for the principal claim, the People's Court shall support the request in accordance with the law.  
A pledge of rights that uses registration as the method of public notice shall be governed by reference to the preceding paragraph.  
(II) Regarding Real Property Security Interests

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　【未办理登记的不动产抵押合同的效力】不动产抵押合同依法成立，但未办理抵押登记手续，债权人请求抵押人办理抵押登记手续的，人民法院依法予以支持。因抵押物灭失以及抵押物转让他人等原因不能办理抵押登记，债权人请求抵押人以抵押物的价值为限承担责任的，人民法院依法予以支持，但其范围不得超过抵押权有效设立时抵押人所应当承担的责任。

**Article 60** [Effect of Unregistered Real Property Mortgage Contract] Where a real property mortgage contract is established in accordance with the law but the mortgage registration formalities have not been completed, and the creditor requests the mortgagor to complete the mortgage registration formalities, the People's Court shall support the request in accordance with the law. If the mortgage registration cannot be completed due to reasons such as the loss of the mortgaged property or the transfer of the mortgaged property to another person, and the creditor requests the mortgagor to bear liability to the extent of the value of the mortgaged property, the People's Court shall support the request in accordance with the law, provided that the scope shall not exceed the liability that the mortgagor should have borne if the mortgage had been effectively established.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　【房地分别抵押】根据《物权法》第182条之规定，仅以建筑物设定抵押的，抵押权的效力及于占用范围内的土地；仅以建设用地使用权抵押的，抵押权的效力亦及于其上的建筑物。在房地分别抵押，即建设用地使用权抵押给一个债权人，而其上的建筑物又抵押给另一个人的情况下，可能产生两个抵押权的冲突问题。基于“房地一体”规则，此时应当将建筑物和建设用地使用权视为同一财产，从而依照《物权法》第199条的规定确定清偿顺序：登记在先的先清偿；同时登记的，按照债权比例清偿。同一天登记的，视为同时登记。应予注意的是，根据《物权法》第200条的规定，建设用地使用权抵押后，该土地上新增的建筑物不属于抵押财产。

**Article 61** [Separate Mortgage of Building and Land] Pursuant to Article 182 of the Property Law, where a mortgage is created only on a building, the effect of the mortgage shall extend to the land within the area occupied by the building; where a mortgage is created only on the right to use construction land, the effect of the mortgage shall also extend to the building thereon. Where the building and the land are mortgaged separately, that is, the right to use construction land is mortgaged to one creditor and the building thereon is mortgaged to another person, a conflict between the two mortgages may arise. Based on the rule of "unity of building and land," the building and the right to use construction land shall be treated as the same property, and the order of satisfaction shall be determined in accordance with Article 199 of the Property Law: the one registered earlier shall be satisfied first; those registered on the same day shall be deemed to be registered simultaneously. It shall be noted that, pursuant to Article 200 of the Property Law, after the mortgage of the right to use construction land is created, the buildings newly added on such land shall not form part of the mortgaged property.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　【抵押权随主债权转让】抵押权是从属于主合同的从权利，根据“从随主”规则，债权转让的，除法律另有规定或者当事人另有约定外，担保该债权的抵押权一并转让。受让人向抵押人主张行使抵押权，抵押人以受让人不是抵押合同的当事人、未办理变更登记等为由提出抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。  
（三）关于动产担保物权

**Article 62** [Transfer of Mortgage with the Assignment of Principal Claim] A mortgage is an accessory right subordinate to the principal contract. According to the rule that "the accessory follows the principal," when a claim is assigned, unless otherwise provided by law or otherwise agreed by the parties, the mortgage securing such claim shall be transferred together. Where the assignee claims to exercise the mortgage against the mortgagor, and the mortgagor raises a defense on the ground that the assignee is not a party to the mortgage contract or has not undergone alteration registration, the People's Court shall not support such defense.  
(III) Security Interests in Movables

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　【流动质押的设立与监管人的责任】在流动质押中，经常由债权人、出质人与监管人订立三方监管协议，此时应当查明监管人究竟是受债权人的委托还是受出质人的委托监管质物，确定质物是否已经交付债权人，从而判断质权是否有效设立。如果监管人系受债权人的委托监管质物，则其是债权人的直接占有人，应当认定完成了质物交付，质权有效设立。监管人违反监管协议约定，违规向出质人放货、因保管不善导致质物毁损灭失，债权人请求监管人承担违约责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
如果监管人系受出质人委托监管质物，表明质物并未交付债权人，应当认定质权未有效设立。尽管监管协议约定监管人系受债权人的委托监管质物，但有证据证明其并未履行监管职责，质物实际上仍由出质人管领控制的，也应当认定质物并未实际交付，质权未有效设立。此时，债权人可以基于质押合同的约定请求质押人承担违约责任，但其范围不得超过质权有效设立时质押人所应当承担的责任。监管人未履行监管职责的，债权人也可以请求监管人承担违约责任。

**Article 63** [Establishment of Floating Pledge and Liability of Custodian] In a floating pledge, the creditor, the pledgor and the custodian often enter into a tripartite custody agreement. In such case, it shall be ascertained whether the custodian supervises the pledged property on behalf of the creditor or on behalf of the pledgor, and whether the pledged property has been delivered to the creditor, so as to determine whether the pledge right has been validly established. If the custodian supervises the pledged property on behalf of the creditor, the custodian shall be deemed the direct possessor of the creditor, and it shall be determined that the delivery of the pledged property has been completed and the pledge right has been validly established. Where the custodian violates the provisions of the custody agreement, improperly releases the goods to the pledgor, or causes damage or loss to the pledged property due to failure in proper custody, and the creditor requests the custodian to bear liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.  
If the custodian supervises the pledged property on behalf of the pledgor, it indicates that the pledged property has not been delivered to the creditor, and it shall be determined that the pledge right has not been validly established. Even if the custody agreement stipulates that the custodian supervises the pledged property on behalf of the creditor, if there is evidence proving that the custodian has not performed the supervision duties and the pledged property is actually still under the control of the pledgor, it shall also be determined that the pledged property has not been actually delivered and the pledge right has not been validly established. In such case, the creditor may request the pledgor to bear liability for breach of contract based on the provisions of the pledge contract, but the scope thereof shall not exceed the liability that the pledgor would have assumed if the pledge right had been validly established. If the custodian fails to perform the supervision duties, the creditor may also request the custodian to bear liability for breach of contract.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　【浮动抵押的效力】企业将其现有的以及将有的生产设备、原材料、半成品及产品等财产设定浮动抵押后，又将其中的生产设备等部分财产设定了动产抵押，并都办理了抵押登记的，根据《物权法》第199条的规定，登记在先的浮动抵押优先于登记在后的动产抵押。

**Article 64** [Effect of Floating Mortgage] Where an enterprise creates a floating mortgage over its existing and after-acquired production equipment, raw materials, semi-finished products and finished products, and subsequently creates a chattel mortgage over part of such property, such as the production equipment, and both mortgages have been registered, then, pursuant to Article 199 of the Property Law, the floating mortgage registered earlier shall have priority over the chattel mortgage registered later.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　【动产抵押权与质权竞存】 同一动产上同时设立质权和抵押权的，应当参照适用《物权法》第199条的规定，根据是否完成公示以及公示先后情况来确定清偿顺序：质权有效设立、抵押权办理了抵押登记的，按照公示先后确定清偿顺序；顺序相同的，按照债权比例清偿；质权有效设立，抵押权未办理抵押登记的，质权优先于抵押权；质权未有效设立，抵押权未办理抵押登记的，因此时抵押权已经有效设立，故抵押权优先受偿。  
根据《物权法》第178条规定的精神，担保法司法解释第79条第1款不再适用。  
（四）关于非典型担保

**Article 65** [Coexistence of Chattel Mortgage and Pledge] Where a pledge and a mortgage are simultaneously created on the same chattel, Article 199 of the Property Law shall be applied by reference to determine the order of satisfaction based on whether the publicity has been completed and the sequence of such publicity: if the pledge right is validly established and the mortgage has been registered, the order of satisfaction shall be determined by the sequence of publicity; if the sequence is the same, satisfaction shall be made in proportion to the claims; if the pledge right is validly established but the mortgage has not been registered, the pledge right shall have priority over the mortgage; if the pledge right has not been validly established and the mortgage has not been registered, the mortgage shall have priority in satisfaction because the mortgage right has been validly established at that time.  
In accordance with the spirit of Article 178 of the Property Law, paragraph 1 of Article 79 of the Judicial Interpretation of the Security Law shall no longer apply.  
(IV) Atypical Security

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　【担保关系的认定】当事人订立的具有担保功能的合同，不存在法定无效情形的，应当认定有效。虽然合同约定的权利义务关系不属于物权法规定的典型担保类型，但是其担保功能应予肯定。

**Article 66** [Determination of Security Relationship] Where a contract entered into by the parties has a security function and does not fall under any statutory circumstance of invalidity, it shall be determined to be valid. Although the rights and obligations stipulated in the contract do not belong to the typical types of security provided for in the Property Law, the security function thereof shall be affirmed.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　【约定担保物权的效力】债权人与担保人订立担保合同，约定以法律、行政法规未禁止抵押或者质押的财产设定以登记作为公示方法的担保，因无法定的登记机构而未能进行登记的，不具有物权效力。当事人请求按照担保合同的约定就该财产折价、变卖或者拍卖所得价款等方式清偿债务的，人民法院依法予以支持，但对其他权利人不具有对抗效力和优先性。

**Article 67** [Effect of Contractual Security Interest] Where a creditor and a security provider enter into a security contract, agreeing to create a security over property that is not prohibited from being mortgaged or pledged by laws or administrative regulations, using registration as the method of publicity, but fails to register due to the absence of a statutory registration authority, such security shall not have real right effect. Where a party requests to satisfy the debt by means of converting the property into money, selling, or auctioning the property in accordance with the security contract, the People's Court shall support such request according to law, but such security shall not have opposability or priority against other right holders.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　【保兑仓交易】保兑仓交易作为一种新类型融资担保方式，其基本交易模式是，以银行信用为载体、以银行承兑汇票为结算工具、由银行控制货权、卖方（或者仓储方）受托保管货物并以承兑汇票与保证金之间的差额作为担保。其基本的交易流程是：卖方、买方和银行订立三方合作协议，其中买方向银行缴存一定比例的承兑保证金，银行向买方签发以卖方为收款人的银行承兑汇票，买方将银行承兑汇票交付卖方作为货款，银行根据买方缴纳的保证金的一定比例向卖方签发提货单，卖方根据提货单向买方交付对应金额的货物，买方销售货物后，将货款再缴存为保证金。  
在三方协议中，一般来说，银行的主要义务是及时签发承兑汇票并按约定方式将其交给卖方，卖方的主要义务是根据银行签发的提货单发货，并在买方未及时销售或者回赎货物时，就保证金与承兑汇票之间的差额部分承担责任。银行为保障自身利益，往往还会约定卖方要将货物交给由其指定的当事人监管，并设定质押，从而涉及监管协议以及流动质押等问题。实践中，当事人还可能在前述基本交易模式基础上另行作出其他约定，只要不违反法律、行政法规的效力性强制性规定，这些约定应当认定有效。  
一方当事人因保兑仓交易纠纷提起诉讼的，人民法院应当以保兑仓交易合同作为审理案件的基本依据，但买卖双方没有真实买卖关系的除外。

**Article 68** [Confirmed Warehouse Transaction] As a new type of financing security method, the basic transaction model of a confirmed warehouse transaction is: using bank credit as the carrier, bank acceptance bills as the settlement instrument, the bank controlling the title to the goods, the seller (or warehouse) being entrusted to keep the goods, and the difference between the acceptance bill and the margin serving as security. The basic transaction process is: the seller, the buyer and the bank enter into a tripartite cooperation agreement, whereby the buyer deposits a certain proportion of acceptance margin with the bank, the bank issues to the buyer a bank acceptance bill with the seller as the payee, the buyer delivers the bank acceptance bill to the seller as payment for the goods, the bank issues a delivery order to the seller based on a certain proportion of the margin paid by the buyer, the seller delivers goods of corresponding value to the buyer according to the delivery order, and after the buyer sells the goods, the proceeds are deposited again as margin.  
In the tripartite agreement, generally speaking, the main obligation of the bank is to issue the acceptance bill in a timely manner and deliver it to the seller in the agreed manner; the main obligation of the seller is to deliver goods according to the delivery order issued by the bank and to bear liability for the difference between the margin and the acceptance bill if the buyer fails to sell or redeem the goods in a timely manner. To protect its own interests, the bank often also stipulates that the seller shall deliver the goods to a party designated by the bank for custody and create a pledge, thus involving custody agreements and floating pledge issues. In practice, the parties may also make other agreements based on the foregoing basic transaction model; as long as they do not violate the mandatory provisions of laws and administrative regulations, such agreements shall be determined to be valid.  
Where a party initiates an action due to a dispute over a confirmed warehouse transaction, the People's Court shall take the confirmed warehouse transaction contract as the basic basis for adjudicating the case, unless there is no genuine sales relationship between the buyer and the seller.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　【无真实贸易背景的保兑仓交易】保兑仓交易以买卖双方有真实买卖关系为前提。双方无真实买卖关系的，该交易属于名为保兑仓交易实为借款合同，保兑仓交易因构成虚伪意思表示而无效，被隐藏的借款合同是当事人的真实意思表示，如不存在其他合同无效情形，应当认定有效。保兑仓交易认定为借款合同关系的，不影响卖方和银行之间担保关系的效力，卖方仍应当承担担保责任。

**Article 69** [Confirmed Warehouse Transaction without Genuine Trade Background] A confirmed warehouse transaction is premised on a genuine sales relationship between the buyer and the seller. If there is no genuine sales relationship between the parties, the transaction is in substance a loan contract under the guise of a confirmed warehouse transaction; the confirmed warehouse transaction shall be void because it constitutes a fictitious declaration of intent, and the hidden loan contract is the true declaration of intent of the parties, and shall be determined to be valid if there are no other circumstances of invalidity. Where a confirmed warehouse transaction is determined to be a loan contract relationship, the validity of the security relationship between the seller and the bank shall not be affected, and the seller shall still bear the security liability.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　【保兑仓交易的合并审理】当事人就保兑仓交易中的不同法律关系的相对方分别或者同时向同一人民法院起诉的，人民法院可以根据民事诉讼法司法解释第221条的规定，合并审理。当事人未起诉某一方当事人的，人民法院可以依职权追加未参加诉讼的当事人为第三人，以便查明相关事实，正确认定责任。

**Article 70** [Consolidated Adjudication of Confirmed Warehouse Transaction] Where a party initiates separate or simultaneous lawsuits against the counterparties of different legal relationships in a confirmed warehouse transaction before the same People's Court, the People's Court may consolidate the adjudication in accordance with Article 221 of the Judicial Interpretation of the Civil Procedure Law. If a party fails to sue a certain party, the People's Court may, ex officio, add the party not participating in the proceedings as a third party so as to ascertain the relevant facts and correctly determine the liability.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　【让与担保】债务人或者第三人与债权人订立合同，约定将财产形式上转让至债权人名下，债务人到期清偿债务，债权人将该财产返还给债务人或第三人，债务人到期没有清偿债务，债权人可以对财产拍卖、变卖、折价偿还债权的，人民法院应当认定合同有效。合同如果约定债务人到期没有清偿债务，财产归债权人所有的，人民法院应当认定该部分约定无效，但不影响合同其他部分的效力。  
当事人根据上述合同约定，已经完成财产权利变动的公示方式转让至债权人名下，债务人到期没有清偿债务，债权人请求确认财产归其所有的，人民法院不予支持，但债权人请求参照法律关于担保物权的规定对财产拍卖、变卖、折价优先偿还其债权的，人民法院依法予以支持。债务人因到期没有清偿债务，请求对该财产拍卖、变卖、折价偿还所欠债权人合同项下债务的，人民法院亦应依法予以支持。

**Article 71** [Security by Transfer] Where a debtor or a third party enters into a contract with a creditor, agreeing to transfer the property formally into the name of the creditor, and upon the debtor's satisfaction of the debt at maturity, the creditor shall return the property to the debtor or the third party; if the debtor fails to satisfy the debt at maturity, the creditor may auction, sell, or convert the property into money to satisfy the claim, the People's Court shall determine the contract to be valid. If the contract stipulates that upon the debtor's failure to satisfy the debt at maturity, the property shall vest in the creditor, the People's Court shall determine such provision to be void, but it shall not affect the validity of the other parts of the contract.  
Where, in accordance with the said contract, the parties have completed the transfer of the property right to the creditor by means of publicity, and the debtor fails to satisfy the debt at maturity, and the creditor requests to confirm that the property belongs to it, the People's Court shall not support such request; however, if the creditor requests, by reference to the provisions of law on security interests, to auction, sell, or convert the property into money for priority satisfaction of its claim, the People's Court shall support such request according to law. Where the debtor, due to failure to satisfy the debt at maturity, requests to auction, sell, or convert the property into money to satisfy the debt owed to the creditor under the contract, the People's Court shall also support such request according to law.

### 五、关于金融消费者权益保护纠纷案件的审理

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　【适当性义务】 适当性义务是指卖方机构在向金融消费者推介、销售银行理财产品、保险投资产品、信托理财产品、券商集合理财计划、杠杆基金份额、期权及其他场外衍生品等高风险等级金融产品，以及为金融消费者参与融资融券、新三板、创业板、科创板、期货等高风险等级投资活动提供服务的过程中，必须履行的了解客户、了解产品、将适当的产品（或者服务）销售（或者提供）给适合的金融消费者等义务。卖方机构承担适当性义务的目的是为了确保金融消费者能够在充分了解相关金融产品、投资活动的性质及风险的基础上作出自主决定，并承受由此产生的收益和风险。在推介、销售高风险等级金融产品和提供高风险等级金融服务领域，适当性义务的履行是“卖者尽责”的主要内容，也是“买者自负”的前提和基础。

**Article 72** [Suitability Obligation] The suitability obligation refers to the obligations that the seller institution must perform in the process of recommending and selling high-risk financial products such as bank wealth management products, insurance investment products, trust wealth management products, securities company collective asset management plans, leveraged fund shares, options and other over-the-counter derivatives to financial consumers, and providing services for financial consumers to participate in high-risk investment activities such as margin trading, the New Third Board, the ChiNext Board, the STAR Market, and futures, including the obligations to know the customer, know the product, and sell (or provide) the appropriate product (or service) to the suitable financial consumer. The purpose of the seller institution assuming the suitability obligation is to ensure that the financial consumer can make autonomous decisions based on a full understanding of the nature and risks of the relevant financial products and investment activities, and bear the resulting gains and risks. In the field of recommending and selling high-risk financial products and providing high-risk financial services, the performance of the suitability obligation is the main content of "due diligence by the seller" and also the prerequisite and basis for "buyer beware."

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　【法律适用规则】在确定卖方机构适当性义务的内容时，应当以合同法、证券法、证券投资基金法、信托法等法律规定的基本原则和国务院发布的规范性文件作为主要依据。相关部门在部门规章、规范性文件中对高风险等级金融产品的推介、销售，以及为金融消费者参与高风险等级投资活动提供服务作出的监管规定，与法律和国务院发布的规范性文件的规定不相抵触的，可以参照适用。

**Article 73** [Rules on Application of Law] In determining the content of the suitability obligation of the seller institution, the basic principles of laws such as the Contract Law, the Securities Law, the Securities Investment Fund Law, and the Trust Law, as well as the normative documents issued by the State Council, shall serve as the main basis. Where the regulatory provisions made by relevant departments in departmental rules and normative documents regarding the recommendation and sale of high-risk financial products and the provision of services for financial consumers to participate in high-risk investment activities do not conflict with the provisions of laws and the normative documents issued by the State Council, such provisions may be applied by reference.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　【责任主体】金融产品发行人、销售者未尽适当性义务，导致金融消费者在购买金融产品过程中遭受损失的，金融消费者既可以请求金融产品的发行人承担赔偿责任，也可以请求金融产品的销售者承担赔偿责任，还可以根据《民法总则》第167条的规定，请求金融产品的发行人、销售者共同承担连带赔偿责任。发行人、销售者请求人民法院明确各自的责任份额的，人民法院可以在判决发行人、销售者对金融消费者承担连带赔偿责任的同时，明确发行人、销售者在实际承担了赔偿责任后，有权向责任方追偿其应当承担的赔偿份额。  
金融服务提供者未尽适当性义务，导致金融消费者在接受金融服务后参与高风险等级投资活动遭受损失的，金融消费者可以请求金融服务提供者承担赔偿责任。

**Article 74** [Liability Subjects] Where the issuer or seller of a financial product fails to perform the suitability obligation, causing losses to the financial consumer in the course of purchasing the financial product, the financial consumer may request either the issuer or the seller of the financial product to bear compensation liability, and may also request the issuer and the seller of the financial product to bear joint and several compensation liability in accordance with Article 167 of the General Principles of the Civil Law. Where the issuer or the seller requests the People's Court to clarify their respective shares of liability, the People's Court may, while adjudging the issuer and the seller to bear joint and several compensation liability to the financial consumer, also clarify that after actually bearing the compensation liability, the issuer or seller has the right to recourse against the liable party for the compensation share that such party should bear.  
Where a financial service provider fails to perform the suitability obligation, causing losses to the financial consumer after receiving financial services and participating in high-risk investment activities, the financial consumer may request the financial service provider to bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　【举证责任分配】在案件审理过程中，金融消费者应当对购买产品（或者接受服务）、遭受的损失等事实承担举证责任。卖方机构对其是否履行了适当性义务承担举证责任。卖方机构不能提供其已经建立了金融产品（或者服务）的风险评估及相应管理制度、对金融消费者的风险认知、风险偏好和风险承受能力进行了测试、向金融消费者告知产品（或者服务）的收益和主要风险因素等相关证据的，应当承担举证不能的法律后果。

**Article 75** [Allocation of Burden of Proof] During the trial of a case, the financial consumer shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the facts such as the purchase of the product (or acceptance of the service) and the losses suffered. The seller institution shall bear the burden of adducing evidence as to whether it has performed the suitability obligation. If the seller institution fails to provide evidence that it has established a risk assessment and corresponding management system for the financial product (or service), conducted tests on the financial consumer's risk awareness, risk preference and risk tolerance, and informed the financial consumer of the income and main risk factors of the product (or service), it shall bear the legal consequences of failure to adduce evidence.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　【告知说明义务】告知说明义务的履行是金融消费者能够真正了解各类高风险等级金融产品或者高风险等级投资活动的投资风险和收益的关键，人民法院应当根据产品、投资活动的风险和金融消费者的实际情况，综合理性人能够理解的客观标准和金融消费者能够理解的主观标准来确定卖方机构是否已经履行了告知说明义务。卖方机构简单地以金融消费者手写了诸如“本人明确知悉可能存在本金损失风险”等内容主张其已经履行了告知说明义务，不能提供其他相关证据的，人民法院对其抗辩理由不予支持。

**Article 76** [Obligation of Disclosure and Explanation] The performance of the obligation of disclosure and explanation is the key for financial consumers to truly understand the investment risks and returns of various high-risk financial products or high-risk investment activities. The People's Court shall, based on the risks of the product and the investment activity and the actual circumstances of the financial consumer, comprehensively consider the objective standard that a reasonable person can understand and the subjective standard that the financial consumer can understand to determine whether the seller institution has performed the obligation of disclosure and explanation. If the seller institution simply claims that it has performed the obligation of disclosure and explanation by relying on the financial consumer's handwritten statement such as "I expressly acknowledge that there may be a risk of loss of principal" and cannot provide other relevant evidence, the People's Court shall not support its defense.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　【损失赔偿数额】卖方机构未尽适当性义务导致金融消费者损失的，应当赔偿金融消费者所受的实际损失。实际损失为损失的本金和利息，利息按照中国人民银行发布的同期同类存款基准利率计算。  
金融消费者因购买高风险等级金融产品或者为参与高风险投资活动接受服务，以卖方机构存在欺诈行为为由，主张卖方机构应当根据《消费者权益保护法》第55条的规定承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，人民法院不予支持。卖方机构的行为构成欺诈的，对金融消费者提出赔偿其支付金钱总额的利息损失请求，应当注意区分不同情况进行处理：  
（1）金融产品的合同文本中载明了预期收益率、业绩比较基准或者类似约定的，可以将其作为计算利息损失的标准；  
（2）合同文本以浮动区间的方式对预期收益率或者业绩比较基准等进行约定，金融消费者请求按照约定的上限作为利息损失计算标准的，人民法院依法予以支持；  
（3）合同文本虽然没有关于预期收益率、业绩比较基准或者类似约定，但金融消费者能够提供证据证明产品发行的广告宣传资料中载明了预期收益率、业绩比较基准或者类似表述的，应当将宣传资料作为合同文本的组成部分;  
（4）合同文本及广告宣传资料中未载明预期收益率、业绩比较基准或者类似表述的，按照全国银行间同业拆借中心公布的贷款市场报价利率计算。  
78．【免责事由】因金融消费者故意提供虚假信息、拒绝听取卖方机构的建议等自身原因导致其购买产品或者接受服务不适当，卖方机构请求免除相应责任的，人民法院依法予以支持，但金融消费者能够证明该虚假信息的出具系卖方机构误导的除外。卖方机构能够举证证明根据金融消费者的既往投资经验、受教育程度等事实，适当性义务的违反并未影响金融消费者作出自主决定的，对其关于应当由金融消费者自负投资风险的抗辩理由，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 77** [Amount of Compensation for Loss] Where the seller institution fails to perform the suitability obligation, causing losses to the financial consumer, it shall compensate the actual losses suffered by the financial consumer. The actual losses shall be the lost principal and interest, and the interest shall be calculated based on the benchmark interest rate for deposits of the same type and same term published by the People's Bank of China.  
Where a financial consumer purchases a high-risk financial product or accepts services for participating in high-risk investment activities and claims that the seller institution shall bear punitive damages in accordance with Article 55 of the Consumer Rights Protection Law on the ground that the seller institution has committed fraud, the People's Court shall not support such claim. If the conduct of the seller institution constitutes fraud, the request of the financial consumer for compensation for interest losses on the total amount of money paid shall be dealt with by distinguishing different situations:  
(1) If the contract document of the financial product contains the expected rate of return, performance benchmark or similar provisions, such provisions may be used as the standard for calculating interest losses;  
(2) If the contract document stipulates the expected rate of return or performance benchmark in a floating range, and the financial consumer requests to use the upper limit of such range as the standard for calculating interest losses, the People's Court shall support such request according to law;  
(3) If the contract document does not contain the expected rate of return, performance benchmark or similar provisions, but the financial consumer can provide evidence proving that the advertising and promotional materials for the product contain the expected rate of return, performance benchmark or similar expressions, such promotional materials shall be deemed as part of the contract document;  
(4) If neither the contract document nor the advertising and promotional materials contain the expected rate of return, performance benchmark or similar expressions, the loan prime rate published by the National Interbank Funding Center shall be applied.  
78. [Exemption from Liability] Where, due to the financial consumer's own reasons such as intentionally providing false information or refusing to follow the seller institution's advice, the purchase of the product or acceptance of the service is inappropriate, and the seller institution requests to be exempted from corresponding liability, the People's Court shall support such request according to law, unless the financial consumer can prove that the false information was provided due to the seller institution's misrepresentation. If the seller institution can adduce evidence proving that, based on facts such as the financial consumer's past investment experience and education level, the breach of the suitability obligation did not affect the financial consumer's autonomous decision, the People's Court shall support the seller institution's defense that the financial consumer shall bear the investment risk on its own.

<a id="art-78"></a>【免责事由】因金融消费者故意提供虚假信息、拒绝听取卖方机构的建议等自身原因导致其购买产品或者接受服务不适当，卖方机构请求免除相应责任的，人民法院依法予以支持，但金融消费者能够证明该虚假信息的出具系卖方机构误导的除外。卖方机构能够举证证明根据金融消费者的既往投资经验、受教育程度等事实，适当性义务的违反并未影响金融消费者作出自主决定的，对其关于应当由金融消费者自负投资风险的抗辩理由，人民法院依法予以支持。  
六、关于证券纠纷案件的审理  
（一）关于证券虚假陈述  
会议认为，《最高人民法院关于审理证券市场因虚假陈述引发的民事赔偿案件的若干规定》施行以来，证券市场的发展出现了新的情况，证券虚假陈述纠纷案件的审理对司法能力提出了更高的要求。在案件审理过程中，对于需要借助其他学科领域的专业知识进行职业判断的问题，要充分发挥专家证人的作用，使得案件的事实认定符合证券市场的基本常识和普遍认知或者认可的经验法则，责任承担与侵权行为及其主观过错程度相匹配，在切实维护投资者合法权益的同时，通过民事责任追究实现震慑违法的功能，维护公开、公平、公正的资本市场秩序。

**Article 78** [Exemption from Liability] Where, due to the financial consumer's own reasons such as intentionally providing false information or refusing to follow the seller institution's advice, the purchase of the product or acceptance of the service is inappropriate, and the seller institution requests to be exempted from corresponding liability, the People's Court shall support such request according to law, unless the financial consumer can prove that the false information was provided due to the seller institution's misrepresentation. If the seller institution can adduce evidence proving that, based on facts such as the financial consumer's past investment experience and education level, the breach of the suitability obligation did not affect the financial consumer's autonomous decision, the People's Court shall support the seller institution's defense that the financial consumer shall bear the investment risk on its own.  
VI. Trial of Securities Dispute Cases  
(I) Securities Misrepresentation  
It is considered at the meeting that since the implementation of the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Trial of Civil Compensation Cases Arising from Misrepresentation in the Securities Market, new circumstances have emerged in the development of the securities market, and the trial of securities misrepresentation dispute cases has raised higher requirements for judicial capacity. In the course of trial, for issues requiring professional judgment with the aid of expertise from other disciplines, the role of expert witnesses shall be fully utilized, so that the determination of facts in the case conforms to the basic common sense and generally recognized or accepted rules of experience in the securities market, and the assumption of liability matches the tortious act and the degree of subjective fault, thereby effectively protecting the legitimate rights and interests of investors while achieving the function of deterring illegal acts through civil liability, and maintaining an open, fair and just capital market order.

### 六、关于证券纠纷案件的审理

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　【共同管辖的案件移送】原告以发行人、上市公司以外的虚假陈述行为人为被告提起诉讼，被告申请追加发行人或者上市公司为共同被告的，人民法院应予准许。人民法院在追加后发现其他有管辖权的人民法院已先行受理因同一虚假陈述引发的民事赔偿案件的，应当按照民事诉讼法司法解释第36条的规定，将案件移送给先立案的人民法院。

**Article 79** [Transfer of Cases with Concurrent Jurisdiction] Where the plaintiff initiates an action against a person other than the issuer or the listed company as the defendant for misrepresentation, and the defendant applies to add the issuer or the listed company as a co-defendant, the People's Court shall permit it. If, after adding the party, the People's Court discovers that another People's Court having jurisdiction has already accepted a civil compensation case arising from the same misrepresentation, the People's Court shall transfer the case to the People's Court that first docketed the case in accordance with Article 36 of the Judicial Interpretation of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　【案件审理方式】案件审理方式方面，在传统的“一案一立、分别审理”的方式之外，一些人民法院已经进行了将部分案件合并审理、在示范判决基础上委托调解等改革，初步实现了案件审理的集约化和诉讼经济。在认真总结审判实践经验的基础上，有条件的地方人民法院可以选择个案以《民事诉讼法》第54条规定的代表人诉讼方式进行审理，逐步展开试点工作。就案件审理中涉及的适格原告范围认定、公告通知方式、投资者权利登记、代表人推选、执行款项的发放等具体工作，积极协调相关部门和有关方面，推动信息技术审判辅助平台和常态化、可持续的工作机制建设，保障投资者能够便捷、高效、透明和低成本地维护自身合法权益，为构建符合中国国情的证券民事诉讼制度积累审判经验，培养审判队伍。

**Article 80** [Case Trial Methods] In terms of case trial methods, in addition to the traditional approach of "one case, one filing, separate trials," some People's Courts have carried out reforms such as consolidating some cases for trial and entrusting mediation on the basis of model judgments, initially achieving intensive case handling and litigation economy. On the basis of carefully summarizing trial practice experience, qualified local People's Courts may choose individual cases to be tried by representative action in accordance with Article 54 of the Civil Procedure Law, and gradually carry out pilot work. With regard to specific tasks involved in case trial, such as determining the scope of qualified plaintiffs, the method of public notice, registration of investors' rights, selection of representatives, and distribution of enforcement proceeds, the People's Courts shall actively coordinate with relevant departments and parties, promote the construction of information technology-assisted trial platforms and normalized, sustainable working mechanisms, ensure that investors can safeguard their legitimate rights and interests in a convenient, efficient, transparent and low-cost manner, accumulate trial experience for building a securities civil litigation system suitable for China's national conditions, and foster trial teams.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　【立案登记】多个投资者就同一虚假陈述向人民法院提起诉讼，可以采用代表人诉讼方式对案件进行审理的，人民法院在登记立案时可以根据原告起诉状中所描述的虚假陈述的数量、性质及其实施日、揭露日或者更正日等时间节点，将投资者作为共同原告统一立案登记。原告主张被告实施了多个虚假陈述的，可以分别立案登记。

**Article 81** [Case Filing and Registration] Where multiple investors initiate lawsuits in the People's Court concerning the same misrepresentation, and the case may be heard by way of representative action, the People's Court may, when registering the case, treat the investors as co-plaintiffs for unified case registration based on the number and nature of the misrepresentations described in the plaintiffs' complaints as well as the time points such as the implementation date, disclosure date, or correction date. Where the plaintiff claims that the defendant has committed multiple misrepresentations, separate case registration may be made.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　【案件甄别及程序决定】人民法院决定采用《民事诉讼法》第54条规定的方式审理案件的，在发出公告前，应当先行就被告的行为是否构成虚假陈述，投资者的交易方向与诱多、诱空的虚假陈述是否一致，以及虚假陈述的实施日、揭露日或者更正日等案件基本事实进行审查。

**Article 82** [Case Screening and Procedural Decision] Where the People's Court decides to hear a case by the method provided in Article 54 of the Civil Procedure Law, it shall, before issuing the announcement, first examine whether the defendant's conduct constitutes misrepresentation, whether the trading direction of investors is consistent with the misrepresentation that induces buying or selling, and the basic facts of the case such as the implementation date, disclosure date, or correction date of the misrepresentation.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　【选定代表人】权利登记的期间届满后，人民法院应当通知当事人在指定期间内完成代表人的推选工作。推选不出代表人的，人民法院可以与当事人商定代表人。人民法院在提出人选时，应当将当事人诉讼请求的典型性和利益诉求的份额等作为考量因素，确保代表行为能够充分、公正地表达投资者的诉讼主张。国家设立的投资者保护机构以自己的名义提起诉讼，或者接受投资者的委托指派工作人员或者委托诉讼代理人参与案件审理活动的，人民法院可以商定该机构或者其代理的当事人作为代表人。

**Article 83** [Selection of Representative] After the period for registration of rights expires, the People's Court shall notify the parties to complete the selection of representatives within a specified period. If the representatives cannot be elected, the People's Court may consult with the parties to appoint representatives. In proposing candidates, the People's Court shall consider factors such as the representativeness of the parties' claims and the proportion of their interests, ensuring that the representative action can fully and fairly express the investors' claims. Where an investor protection institution established by the state initiates an action in its own name, or accepts an entrustment from investors to designate its staff or appoint a litigation agent to participate in the case, the People's Court may consult with the institution or the parties it represents to act as representatives.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　【揭露日和更正日的认定】虚假陈述的揭露和更正，是指虚假陈述被市场所知悉、了解，其精确程度并不以“镜像规则”为必要，不要求达到全面、完整、准确的程度。原则上，只要交易市场对监管部门立案调查、权威媒体刊载的揭露文章等信息存在着明显的反应，对一方主张市场已经知悉虚假陈述的抗辩，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 84** [Determination of Disclosure Date and Correction Date] The disclosure and correction of a misrepresentation refer to the situation where the misrepresentation becomes known and understood by the market. The degree of precision does not require the “mirror rule”; it does not need to reach a comprehensive, complete, or accurate level. In principle, as long as the trading market has an obvious reaction to information such as the regulatory authority's case filing and investigation or the disclosure article published by an authoritative media outlet, the People's Court shall support, according to law, the defense that the market has already known the misrepresentation.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　【重大性要件的认定】审判实践中，部分人民法院对重大性要件和信赖要件存在着混淆认识，以行政处罚认定的信息披露违法行为对投资者的交易决定没有影响为由否定违法行为的重大性，应当引起注意。重大性是指可能对投资者进行投资决策具有重要影响的信息，虚假陈述已经被监管部门行政处罚的，应当认为是具有重大性的违法行为。在案件审理过程中，对于一方提出的监管部门作出处罚决定的行为不具有重大性的抗辩，人民法院不予支持，同时应当向其释明，该抗辩并非民商事案件的审理范围，应当通过行政复议、行政诉讼加以解决。  
（二）关于场外配资  
会议认为，将证券市场的信用交易纳入国家统一监管的范围，是维护金融市场透明度和金融稳定的重要内容。不受监管的场外配资业务，不仅盲目扩张了资本市场信用交易的规模，也容易冲击资本市场的交易秩序。融资融券作为证券市场的主要信用交易方式和证券经营机构的核心业务之一，依法属于国家特许经营的金融业务，未经依法批准，任何单位和个人不得非法从事配资业务。

**Article 85** [Determination of Materiality Requirement] In trial practice, some People's Courts have confusing understandings of the materiality requirement and the reliance requirement, denying materiality of an illegal act on the ground that the information disclosure violation determined by an administrative penalty has no impact on investors' trading decisions. This requires attention. Materiality refers to information that may have a significant influence on investors' investment decisions. Where a misrepresentation has already been subject to an administrative penalty by the regulatory authority, it shall be deemed an illegal act with materiality. In the course of case adjudication, where a party raises a defense that the act for which the regulatory authority has imposed a penalty is not material, the People's Court shall not support such defense and shall at the same time explain that such defense is not within the scope of trial of civil and commercial cases and shall be resolved through administrative reconsideration or administrative litigation.  
(II) Concerning Off-Exchange Margin Financing  
At the meeting, it was considered that bringing credit transactions in the securities market under the unified supervision of the state is an important element of maintaining the transparency and stability of the financial market. Unregulated off-exchange margin financing not only blindly expands the scale of credit transactions in the capital market but also easily impacts the trading order of the capital market. Margin trading and short selling, as the main credit transaction method in the securities market and one of the core businesses of securities operating institutions, are financial businesses subject to state franchise according to law. Without approval according to law, no entity or individual may illegally engage in margin financing.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　【场外配资合同的效力】从审判实践看，场外配资业务主要是指一些P2P公司或者私募类配资公司利用互联网信息技术，搭建起游离于监管体系之外的融资业务平台，将资金融出方、资金融入方即用资人和券商营业部三方连接起来，配资公司利用计算机软件系统的二级分仓功能将其自有资金或者以较低成本融入的资金出借给用资人，赚取利息收入的行为。这些场外配资公司所开展的经营活动，本质上属于只有证券公司才能依法开展的融资活动，不仅规避了监管部门对融资融券业务中资金来源、投资标的、杠杆比例等诸多方面的限制，也加剧了市场的非理性波动。在案件审理过程中，除依法取得融资融券资格的证券公司与客户开展的融资融券业务外，对其他任何单位或者个人与用资人的场外配资合同，人民法院应当根据《证券法》第142条、合同法司法解释（一）第10条的规定，认定为无效。

**Article 86** [Validity of Off-Exchange Margin Financing Contracts] From a trial practice perspective, off-exchange margin financing mainly refers to the activities where some P2P companies or private equity-type margin financing companies use internet information technology to build a financing business platform outside the regulatory system, connecting the fund provider, the fund user (i.e., the capital user), and the securities broker's business department. The margin financing company uses the secondary sub-account function of the computer software system to lend its own funds or funds raised at a lower cost to the capital user, thereby earning interest income. The business activities carried out by these off-exchange margin financing companies are essentially financing activities that only securities companies may conduct according to law. They not only circumvent the regulatory restrictions on sources of funds, investment targets, leverage ratios, and many other aspects of margin trading and short selling but also exacerbate irrational market fluctuations. In the course of case adjudication, except for the margin trading and short selling business conducted between a securities company that has legally obtained the qualification for margin trading and short selling and its clients, the People's Court shall, with respect to any off-exchange margin financing contract between any other entity or individual and a capital user, declare it void in accordance with Article 142 of the Securities Law and Article 10 of the Interpretation (I) of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Application of the Contract Law.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　【合同无效的责任承担】场外配资合同被确认无效后，配资方依场外配资合同的约定，请求用资人向其支付约定的利息和费用的，人民法院不予支持。  
配资方依场外配资合同的约定，请求分享用资人因使用配资所产生的收益的，人民法院不予支持。  
用资人以其因使用配资导致投资损失为由请求配资方予以赔偿的，人民法院不予支持。用资人能够证明因配资方采取更改密码等方式控制账户使得用资人无法及时平仓止损，并据此请求配资方赔偿其因此遭受的损失的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
用资人能够证明配资合同是因配资方招揽、劝诱而订立，请求配资方赔偿其全部或者部分损失的，人民法院应当综合考虑配资方招揽、劝诱行为的方式、对用资人的实际影响、用资人自身的投资经历、风险判断和承受能力等因素，判决配资方承担与其过错相适应的赔偿责任。

**Article 87** [Liability After Contract Void] After an off-exchange margin financing contract is confirmed void, if the margin financing party requests the capital user to pay agreed interest and fees in accordance with the off-exchange margin financing contract, the People's Court shall not support such a request.  
If the margin financing party requests to share the profits generated by the capital user's use of the margin financing funds in accordance with the off-exchange margin financing contract, the People's Court shall not support such a request.  
If the capital user requests compensation for investment losses incurred due to the use of margin financing, the People's Court shall not support such a request. However, if the capital user can prove that the margin financing party controlled the account by changing passwords or other means, preventing the capital user from timely closing positions to stop losses, and accordingly requests the margin financing party to compensate the losses suffered thereby, the People's Court shall support such a request according to law.  
If the capital user can prove that the margin financing contract was concluded due to solicitation or inducement by the margin financing party, and requests the margin financing party to compensate all or part of its losses, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider factors such as the manner of the margin financing party's solicitation or inducement, its actual impact on the capital user, and the capital user's own investment experience, risk judgment, and risk tolerance, and shall render a judgment ordering the margin financing party to bear compensation liability corresponding to its fault.

### 七、关于营业信托纠纷案件的审理

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　【营业信托纠纷的认定】信托公司根据法律法规以及金融监督管理部门的监管规定，以取得信托报酬为目的接受委托人的委托，以受托人身份处理信托事务的经营行为，属于营业信托。由此产生的信托当事人之间的纠纷，为营业信托纠纷。  
根据《关于规范金融机构资产管理业务的指导意见》的规定，其他金融机构开展的资产管理业务构成信托关系的，当事人之间的纠纷适用信托法及其他有关规定处理。

**Article 88** [Determination of Business Trust Disputes] The operational conduct of a trust company that, in accordance with laws, regulations, and the regulatory provisions of financial regulatory authorities, accepts a trustor's entrustment for the purpose of obtaining trust remuneration and handles trust affairs as a trustee, constitutes a business trust. Disputes arising between the parties to such a trust are business trust disputes.  
Pursuant to the provisions of the Guiding Opinions on Regulating the Asset Management Business of Financial Institutions, where the asset management business carried out by other financial institutions constitutes a trust relationship, disputes between the parties shall be handled in accordance with the Trust Law and other relevant provisions.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　【资产或者资产收益权转让及回购】信托公司在资金信托成立后，以募集的信托资金受让特定资产或者特定资产收益权，属于信托公司在资金依法募集后的资金运用行为，由此引发的纠纷不应当认定为营业信托纠纷。如果合同中约定由转让方或者其指定的第三方在一定期间后以交易本金加上溢价款等固定价款无条件回购的，无论转让方所转让的标的物是否真实存在、是否实际交付或者过户，只要合同不存在法定无效事由，对信托公司提出的由转让方或者其指定的第三方按约定承担责任的诉讼请求，人民法院依法予以支持。  
当事人在相关合同中同时约定采用信托公司受让目标公司股权、向目标公司增资方式并以相应股权担保债权实现的，应当认定在当事人之间成立让与担保法律关系。当事人之间的具体权利义务，根据本纪要第71 条的规定加以确定。

**Article 89** [Transfer and Repurchase of Assets or Asset Income Rights] After the establishment of a fund trust, the trust company's use of the raised trust funds to acquire specific assets or specific asset income rights constitutes a capital utilization act following the lawful raising of funds, and disputes arising therefrom shall not be deemed as business trust disputes. If the contract provides that the transferor or a designated third party shall unconditionally repurchase the assets after a certain period at a fixed price consisting of the transaction principal plus a premium, regardless of whether the subject matter transferred by the transferor actually exists, is actually delivered, or undergoes registration of transfer, as long as the contract has no statutory grounds for invalidity, the People's Court shall support the trust company's claim that the transferor or the designated third party shall bear liability in accordance with the agreement.  
Where the parties in the related contract simultaneously agree that the trust company shall acquire the equity of the target company or increase capital in the target company, and shall secure the claim with the corresponding equity, it shall be determined that a relationship of transfer for security exists between the parties. The specific rights and obligations between the parties shall be determined in accordance with Article 71 of this Summary.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　【劣后级受益人的责任承担】信托文件及相关合同将受益人区分为优先级受益人和劣后级受益人等不同类别，约定优先级受益人以其财产认购信托计划份额，在信托到期后，劣后级受益人负有对优先级受益人从信托财产获得利益与其投资本金及约定收益之间的差额承担补足义务，优先级受益人请求劣后级受益人按照约定承担责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
信托文件中关于不同类型受益人权利义务关系的约定，不影响受益人与受托人之间信托法律关系的认定。

**Article 90** [Liability of Subordinated Beneficiaries] Where the trust document and related contracts classify beneficiaries into different categories such as senior beneficiaries and subordinated beneficiaries, and provide that senior beneficiaries subscribe to trust plan units with their property, and upon the maturity of the trust, the subordinated beneficiaries have the obligation to make up the difference between the benefits received by the senior beneficiaries from the trust property and their investment principal plus agreed returns, the People's Court shall support the senior beneficiary's request that the subordinated beneficiary bear liability in accordance with the agreement.  
The provisions in the trust document concerning the rights and obligations of different types of beneficiaries shall not affect the determination of the trust legal relationship between the beneficiaries and the trustee.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　【增信文件的性质】信托合同之外的当事人提供第三方差额补足、代为履行到期回购义务、流动性支持等类似承诺文件作为增信措施，其内容符合法律关于保证的规定的，人民法院应当认定当事人之间成立保证合同关系。其内容不符合法律关于保证的规定的，依据承诺文件的具体内容确定相应的权利义务关系，并根据案件事实情况确定相应的民事责任。

**Article 91** [Nature of Credit Enhancement Documents] Where a party other than the trust contract provides a third-party shortfall undertaking, assumption of the obligation to repurchase at maturity, liquidity support, or similar commitment documents as credit enhancement measures, and the content thereof complies with the provisions of law on suretyship, the People's Court shall determine that a suretyship contract relationship exists between the parties. If the content does not comply with the provisions of law on suretyship, the corresponding rights and obligations shall be determined according to the specific content of the commitment documents, and the corresponding civil liability shall be determined based on the facts of the case.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　【保底或者刚兑条款无效】信托公司、商业银行等金融机构作为资产管理产品的受托人与受益人订立的含有保证本息固定回报、保证本金不受损失等保底或者刚兑条款的合同，人民法院应当认定该条款无效。受益人请求受托人对其损失承担与其过错相适应的赔偿责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
实践中，保底或者刚兑条款通常不在资产管理产品合同中明确约定，而是以“抽屉协议”或者其他方式约定，不管形式如何，均应认定无效。

**Article 92** [Invalidity of Guaranteed Return or Rigid Redemption Clauses] Where a trust company, commercial bank, or other financial institution, acting as the trustee of an asset management product, enters into a contract with a beneficiary that contains clauses guaranteeing a fixed return of principal and interest, guaranteeing the principal against loss, or other guaranteed return or rigid redemption clauses, the People's Court shall find such clauses void. Where the beneficiary requests the trustee to bear compensation liability commensurate with its fault for the beneficiary's losses, the People's Court shall support such a request according to law.  
In practice, guaranteed return or rigid redemption clauses are often not expressly stipulated in asset management product contracts but are agreed upon in “drawer agreements” or other forms. Regardless of the form, they shall all be found void.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　【通道业务的效力】当事人在信托文件中约定，委托人自主决定信托设立、信托财产运用对象、信托财产管理运用处分方式等事宜，自行承担信托资产的风险管理责任和相应风险损失，受托人仅提供必要的事务协助或者服务，不承担主动管理职责的，应当认定为通道业务。《中国人民银行、中国银行保险监督管理委员会、中国证券监督管理委员会、国家外汇管理局关于规范金融机构资产管理业务的指导意见》第22条在规定“金融机构不得为其他金融机构的资产管理产品提供规避投资范围、杠杆约束等监管要求的通道服务”的同时，也在第29条明确按照“新老划断”原则，将过渡期设置为截止2020年底，确保平稳过渡。在过渡期内，对通道业务中存在的利用信托通道掩盖风险，规避资金投向、资产分类、拨备计提和资本占用等监管规定，或者通过信托通道将表内资产虚假出表等信托业务，如果不存在其他无效事由，一方以信托目的违法违规为由请求确认无效的，人民法院不予支持。至于委托人和受托人之间的权利义务关系，应当依据信托文件的约定加以确定。

**Article 93** [Validity of Channel Business] Where the parties stipulate in the trust document that the trustor independently decides on the establishment of the trust, the objects of the trust property application, the methods of management, application and disposal of the trust property, and other matters, and assumes the risk management responsibility and corresponding risk losses of the trust assets, while the trustee only provides necessary administrative assistance or services and does not undertake active management duties, such business shall be recognized as channel business. Article 22 of the Guiding Opinions on Regulating the Asset Management Business of Financial Institutions by the People's Bank of China, the China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission, the China Securities Regulatory Commission, and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange provides that “financial institutions shall not provide channel services for asset management products of other financial institutions that circumvent regulatory requirements such as investment scope and leverage constraints,” while Article 29 of the same Guiding Opinions explicitly adopts the “new and old demarcation” principle, setting a transition period ending by the end of 2020 to ensure a smooth transition. Within the transition period, with respect to channel business that uses the trust channel to conceal risks, circumvent regulatory provisions on investment direction, asset classification, provisioning, and capital occupation, or to falsely remove on-balance-sheet assets through the trust channel, and other trust business, if there are no other grounds for invalidity, and a party requests confirmation of invalidity on the ground that the trust purpose violates laws or regulations, the People's Court shall not support such request. As to the rights and obligations between the trustor and the trustee, they shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of the trust document.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　【受托人的举证责任】资产管理产品的委托人以受托人未履行勤勉尽责、公平对待客户等义务损害其合法权益为由，请求受托人承担损害赔偿责任的，应当由受托人举证证明其已经履行了义务。受托人不能举证证明，委托人请求其承担相应赔偿责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 94** [Burden of Proof of the Trustee] Where the trustor of an asset management product requests the trustee to bear liability for damages on the ground that the trustee has failed to perform its duties such as diligence and fair treatment of clients, thereby harming the trustor's lawful rights and interests, the trustee shall bear the burden of proof to demonstrate that it has performed its obligations. If the trustee cannot prove this, and the trustor requests the trustee to bear corresponding compensation liability, the People's Court shall support such a request according to law.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　【信托财产的诉讼保全】信托财产在信托存续期间独立于委托人、受托人、受益人各自的固有财产。委托人将其财产委托给受托人进行管理，在信托依法设立后，该信托财产即独立于委托人未设立信托的其他固有财产。受托人因承诺信托而取得的信托财产，以及通过对信托财产的管理、运用、处分等方式取得的财产，均独立于受托人的固有财产。受益人对信托财产享有的权利表现为信托受益权，信托财产并非受益人的责任财产。因此，当事人因其与委托人、受托人或者受益人之间的纠纷申请对存管银行或者信托公司专门账户中的信托资金采取保全措施的，除符合《信托法》第17条规定的情形外，人民法院不应当准许。已经采取保全措施的，存管银行或者信托公司能够提供证据证明该账户为信托账户的，应当立即解除保全措施。对信托公司管理的其他信托财产的保全，也应当根据前述规则办理。  
当事人申请对受益人的受益权采取保全措施的，人民法院应当根据《信托法》第47条的规定进行审查，决定是否采取保全措施。决定采取保全措施的，应当将保全裁定送达受托人和受益人。

**Article 95** [Litigation Preservation of Trust Property] Trust property is independent, during the existence of the trust, from the respective own property of the trustor, the trustee, and the beneficiary. After the trustor entrusts its property to the trustee for management, upon the lawful establishment of the trust, such trust property becomes independent from the trustor's other own property that is not under trust. The trust property acquired by the trustee upon acceptance of the trust, and the property acquired through the management, application, or disposal of the trust property, are all independent from the trustee's own property. The rights enjoyed by the beneficiary over the trust property are manifested as trust beneficial rights; the trust property is not the beneficiary's property subject to liability. Therefore, where a party applies for preservation measures against the trust funds in a special account of a depository bank or a trust company due to a dispute with the trustor, trustee, or beneficiary, the People's Court shall not grant such application except in circumstances specified in Article 17 of the Trust Law. If preservation measures have already been taken, and the depository bank or the trust company can provide evidence proving that the account is a trust account, the preservation measures shall be immediately lifted. The preservation of other trust property managed by the trust company shall also be handled in accordance with the foregoing rules.  
Where a party applies for preservation measures against the beneficiary's beneficial rights, the People's Court shall examine the matter in accordance with Article 47 of the Trust Law and decide whether to take preservation measures. If preservation measures are decided, the preservation order shall be served on the trustee and the beneficiary.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　【信托公司固有财产的诉讼保全】除信托公司作为被告外，原告申请对信托公司固有资金账户的资金采取保全措施的，人民法院不应准许。信托公司作为被告，确有必要对其固有财产采取诉讼保全措施的，必须强化善意执行理念，防范发生金融风险。要严格遵守相应的适用条件与法定程序，坚决杜绝超标的执行。在采取具体保全措施时，要尽量寻求依法平等保护各方利益的平衡点，优先采取方便执行且对信托公司正常经营影响最小的执行措施，能采取“活封”“活扣”措施的，尽量不进行“死封”“死扣”。在条件允许的情况下，可以为信托公司预留必要的流动资金和往来账户，最大限度降低对信托公司正常经营活动的不利影响。信托公司申请解除财产保全符合法律、司法解释规定情形的，应当在法定期限内及时解除保全措施。

**Article 96** [Litigation Preservation of Trust Company's Own Property] Except where the trust company is the defendant, if the plaintiff applies for preservation measures against funds in the trust company's own capital account, the People's Court shall not grant such application. Where the trust company is the defendant and it is truly necessary to take litigation preservation measures against its own property, the concept of good-faith enforcement must be strengthened to prevent financial risks. The corresponding application conditions and statutory procedures shall be strictly observed, and over-enforcement exceeding the claim amount shall be resolutely prohibited. When taking specific preservation measures, a balance point shall be sought to protect the interests of all parties equally according to law. Priority shall be given to enforcement measures that are convenient for execution and have the least impact on the normal operation of the trust company. Where “flexible sealing” or “flexible seizure” measures can be taken, “rigid sealing” or “rigid seizure” measures shall be avoided as much as possible. Where conditions permit, necessary working capital and current accounts may be reserved for the trust company to minimize adverse effects on its normal business activities. Where the trust company's application for lifting the property preservation complies with the provisions of laws and judicial interpretations, the preservation measures shall be lifted promptly within the statutory time limit.

### 八、关于财产保险合同纠纷案件的审理

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　【未依约支付保险费的合同效力】当事人在财产保险合同中约定以投保人支付保险费作为合同生效条件，但对该生效条件是否为全额支付保险费约定不明，已经支付了部分保险费的投保人主张保险合同已经生效的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 97** [Validity of Contract Where Insurance Premium Not Paid as Agreed] Where the parties stipulate in a property insurance contract that the contract takes effect upon the payment of the insurance premium by the policyholder, but the condition for effectiveness is unclear as to whether it requires full payment of the insurance premium, and the policyholder who has paid part of the insurance premium claims that the insurance contract has already taken effect, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　【仲裁协议对保险人的效力】被保险人和第三者在保险事故发生前达成的仲裁协议，对行使保险代位求偿权的保险人是否具有约束力，实务中存在争议。保险代位求偿权是一种法定债权转让，保险人在向被保险人赔偿保险金后，有权行使被保险人对第三者请求赔偿的权利。被保险人和第三者在保险事故发生前达成的仲裁协议，对保险人具有约束力。考虑到涉外民商事案件的处理常常涉及国际条约、国际惯例的适用，相关问题具有特殊性，故具有涉外因素的民商事纠纷案件中该问题的处理，不纳入本条规范的范围。

**Article 98** [Effect of Arbitration Agreement on the Insurer] Whether an arbitration agreement concluded between the insured and a third party before the occurrence of an insured event is binding on the insurer exercising the right of subrogation is a matter of controversy in practice. The right of subrogation is a statutory transfer of creditor's rights; after the insurer has indemnified the insured, it has the right to exercise the insured's right to claim against the third party. An arbitration agreement concluded between the insured and the third party before the occurrence of the insured event is binding on the insurer. Considering that the handling of foreign-related civil and commercial cases often involves the application of international treaties and international practices, and the relevant issues are of a special nature, the handling of this issue in civil and commercial dispute cases with foreign-related elements is not included in the scope of regulation of this Article.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　【直接索赔的诉讼时效】商业责任保险的被保险人给第三者造成损害，被保险人对第三者应当承担的赔偿责任确定后，保险人应当根据被保险人的请求，直接向第三者赔偿保险金。被保险人怠于提出请求的，第三者有权依据《保险法》第65条第2款的规定，就其应获赔偿部分直接向保险人请求赔偿保险金。保险人拒绝赔偿的，第三者请求保险人直接赔偿保险金的诉讼时效期间的起算时间如何认定，实务中存在争议。根据诉讼时效制度的基本原理，第三者请求保险人直接赔偿保险金的诉讼时效期间，自其知道或者应当知道向保险人的保险金赔偿请求权行使条件成就之日起计算。

**Article 99** [Limitation of Action for Direct Claim] Where the insured under a commercial liability insurance causes damage to a third party, and the compensation liability of the insured to the third party is determined, the insurer shall, upon the request of the insured, directly indemnify the third party with insurance proceeds. If the insured is dilatory in making the request, the third party has the right to directly request the insurer to indemnify the part of the loss that should be compensated, in accordance with Article 65, paragraph 2 of the Insurance Law. If the insurer refuses to indemnify, there is a controversy in practice as to how to determine the starting point of the limitation period for the third party's action requesting the insurer to directly indemnify. According to the basic principles of the limitation of action system, the limitation period for the third party's action requesting the insurer to directly indemnify shall commence from the date when the third party knows or should know that the conditions for exercising the right to claim insurance proceeds against the insurer are satisfied.

### 九、关于票据纠纷案件的审理

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　【合谋伪造贴现申请材料的后果】贴现行的负责人或者有权从事该业务的工作人员与贴现申请人合谋，伪造贴现申请人与其前手之间具有真实的商品交易关系的合同、增值税专用发票等材料申请贴现，贴现行主张其享有票据权利的，人民法院不予支持。对贴现行因支付资金而产生的损失，按照基础关系处理。

**Article 100** [Consequences of Colluding to Forge Discount Application Materials] Where the person in charge of a discounting bank or a staff member authorized to handle such business colludes with the discount applicant to forge documents such as contracts and special VAT invoices evidencing a genuine commodity transaction relationship between the discount applicant and its prior holder for the purpose of applying for discount, and the discounting bank claims that it enjoys the rights on the instrument, the People's Court shall not support such claim. Losses incurred by the discounting bank due to the payment of funds shall be handled in accordance with the underlying relationship.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百一条　【民间贴现行为的效力】票据贴现属于国家特许经营业务，合法持票人向不具有法定贴现资质的当事人进行“贴现”的，该行为应当认定无效，贴现款和票据应当相互返还。当事人不能返还票据的，原合法持票人可以拒绝返还贴现款。人民法院在民商事案件审理过程中，发现不具有法定资质的当事人以“贴现”为业的，因该行为涉嫌犯罪，应当将有关材料移送公安机关。民商事案件的审理必须以相关刑事案件的审理结果为依据的，应当中止诉讼，待刑事案件审结后，再恢复案件的审理。案件的基本事实无须以相关刑事案件的审理结果为依据的，人民法院应当继续审理。  
根据票据行为无因性原理，在合法持票人向不具有贴现资质的主体进行“贴现”，该“贴现”人给付贴现款后直接将票据交付其后手，其后手支付对价并记载自己为被背书人后，又基于真实的交易关系和债权债务关系将票据进行背书转让的情形下，应当认定最后持票人为合法持票人。

**Article 101** [Effect of Private Discounting] Discounting of negotiable instruments is a business subject to state特许经营 (special license). Where a lawful holder conducts "discounting" with a party that does not possess the statutory qualification for discounting, such act shall be determined invalid, and the discount proceeds and the instrument shall be returned to each other. If the party cannot return the instrument, the original lawful holder may refuse to return the discount proceeds. Where, in the course of trying a civil or commercial case, a People's Court discovers that a party without statutory qualification engages in "discounting" as a business, as such act is suspected of constituting a crime, the People's Court shall transfer the relevant materials to the public security organ. Where the trial of the civil or commercial case must be based on the trial results of the relevant criminal case, the proceedings shall be suspended, and shall be resumed after the criminal case is concluded. Where the basic facts of the case do not need to be based on the trial results of the relevant criminal case, the People's Court shall continue the trial.  
According to the principle of the abstract nature of negotiable instrument acts, where a lawful holder conducts "discounting" with a subject who does not have discounting qualification, and after the "discounter" pays the discount proceeds and directly delivers the instrument to its subsequent party, and the subsequent party pays consideration and records itself as the endorse, and then endorses and transfers the instrument based on a genuine transaction relationship or creditor-debt relationship, it shall be determined that the last holder is a lawful holder.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百二条　【转贴现协议】转贴现是通过票据贴现持有票据的商业银行为了融通资金，在票据到期日之前将票据权利转让给其他商业银行，由转贴现行在收取一定的利息后，将转贴现款支付给持票人的票据转让行为。转贴现行提示付款被拒付后，依据转贴现协议的约定，请求未在票据上背书的转贴现申请人按照合同法律关系返还转贴现款并赔偿损失的，案由应当确定为合同纠纷。转贴现合同法律关系有效成立的，对于原告的诉讼请求，人民法院依法予以支持。当事人虚构转贴现事实，或者当事人之间不存在真实的转贴现合同法律关系的，人民法院应当向当事人释明按照真实交易关系提出诉讼请求，并按照真实交易关系和当事人约定本意依法确定当事人的责任。

**Article 102** [Rediscount Agreement] Rediscount is the act of transferring the rights under a bill by a commercial bank that holds the bill through discounting, for the purpose of financing, to another commercial bank before the maturity date of the bill, where the rediscounting bank pays the rediscount proceeds to the holder after deducting a certain amount of interest. After the rediscounting bank presents the bill for payment and is refused, if it requests, in accordance with the provisions of the rediscount agreement, the rediscount applicant who has not endorsed the bill to return the rediscount proceeds and compensate for losses according to contractual legal relationship, the cause of action shall be determined as contract dispute. Where the rediscount contractual legal relationship is validly established, the People's Court shall support the plaintiff's claims according to law. Where the parties fabricate the rediscount facts, or where there is no genuine rediscount contractual legal relationship between the parties, the People's Court shall explain to the parties that they shall bring claims according to the genuine transaction relationship, and determine the parties' liability according to law based on the genuine transaction relationship and the true intention of the parties' agreement.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百三条　【票据清单交易、封包交易案件中的票据权利】审判实践中，以票据贴现为手段的多链条融资模式引发的案件应当引起重视。这种交易俗称票据清单交易、封包交易，是指商业银行之间就案涉票据订立转贴现或者回购协议，附以票据清单，或者将票据封包作为质押，双方约定按照票据清单中列明的基本信息进行票据转贴现或者回购，但往往并不进行票据交付和背书。实务中，双方还往往再订立一份代保管协议，约定由原票据持有人代对方继续持有票据，从而实现合法、合规的形式要求。  
出资银行仅以参与交易的单个或者部分银行为被告提起诉讼行使票据追索权，被告能够举证证明票据交易存在诸如不符合正常转贴现交易顺序的倒打款、未进行背书转让、票据未实际交付等相关证据，并据此主张相关金融机构之间并无转贴现的真实意思表示，抗辩出资银行不享有票据权利的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
出资银行在取得商业承兑汇票后又将票据转贴现给其他商业银行，持票人向其前手主张票据权利的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 103** [Bill Rights in Bill List Transactions and Sealed Package Transactions] In adjudication practice, cases arising from multi-chain financing models using bill discounting as a means shall be given attention. Such transactions are commonly known as bill list transactions or sealed package transactions, which refer to situations where commercial banks enter into a rediscount or repurchase agreement regarding the bill(s) in question, accompanied by a bill list, or seal the bill(s) in a package as a pledge, and the parties agree to conduct bill rediscount or repurchase according to the basic information listed in the bill list, but often do not deliver or endorse the bill. In practice, the parties also often enter into a separate escrow agreement, stipulating that the original bill holder shall continue to hold the bill on behalf of the other party, thereby satisfying the formal requirements of legality and compliance.  
Where the funding bank initiates a lawsuit exercising the right of recourse against only a single or some of the banks participating in the transaction, and the defendant can adduce evidence proving that the bill transaction involves circumstances such as reverse payment that does not conform to the normal rediscount transaction sequence, absence of endorsement and transfer, or no actual delivery of the bill, and on this basis claims that there was no genuine intention of rediscount among the relevant financial institutions and defends that the funding bank does not enjoy bill rights, the People's Court shall support such defense according to law.  
Where the funding bank, after acquiring a commercial acceptance bill, rediscounts the bill to another commercial bank, and the holder claims bill rights against its prior party, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百四条　【票据清单交易、封包交易案件的处理原则】在村镇银行、农信社等作为直贴行，农信社、农商行、城商行、股份制银行等多家金融机构共同开展以商业承兑汇票为基础的票据清单交易、封包交易引发的纠纷案件中，在商业承兑汇票的出票人等实际用资人不能归还票款的情况下，为实现纠纷的一次性解决，出资银行以实际用资人和参与交易的其他金融机构为共同被告，请求实际用资人归还本息、参与交易的其他金融机构承担与其过错相适应的赔偿责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
出资银行仅以整个交易链条的部分当事人为被告提起诉讼的，人民法院应当向其释明，其应当申请追加参与交易的其他当事人作为共同被告。出资银行拒绝追加实际用资人为被告的，人民法院应当驳回其诉讼请求；出资银行拒绝追加参与交易的其他金融机构为被告的，人民法院在确定其他金融机构的过错责任范围时，应当将未参加诉讼的当事人应当承担的相应份额作为考量因素，相应减轻本案当事人的责任。在确定参与交易的其他金融机构的过错责任范围时，可以参照其收取的“通道费”“过桥费”等费用的比例以及案件的其他情况综合加以确定。

**Article 104** [Principles for Handling Bill List Transaction and Sealed Package Transaction Cases] In dispute cases arising from bill list transactions or sealed package transactions based on commercial acceptance bills, where multiple financial institutions such as village banks, rural credit cooperatives as direct discounting banks, and rural credit cooperatives, rural commercial banks, city commercial banks, joint-stock banks, etc., jointly participate, and the actual fund users such as the drawer of the commercial acceptance bill are unable to repay the bill amount, for the purpose of resolving the dispute in one proceeding, if the funding bank names both the actual fund user and other financial institutions participating in the transaction as co-defendants and requests the actual fund user to repay the principal and interest, and the other financial institutions participating in the transaction to bear compensation liability commensurate with their fault, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.  
Where the funding bank initiates a lawsuit against only some of the parties in the entire transaction chain, the People's Court shall explain to it that it shall apply to add the other parties participating in the transaction as co-defendants. If the funding bank refuses to add the actual fund user as a defendant, the People's Court shall dismiss its claim; if the funding bank refuses to add other financial institutions participating in the transaction as defendants, when determining the scope of fault liability of the other financial institutions, the People's Court shall take into account the corresponding share that the parties not participating in the lawsuit should bear, and accordingly reduce the liability of the parties in the present case. When determining the scope of fault liability of other financial institutions participating in the transaction, reference may be made to the proportion of fees such as "channel fees" and "bridge fees" collected by them, as well as other circumstances of the case, for a comprehensive determination.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百五条　【票据清单交易、封包交易案件中的民刑交叉问题】人民法院在案件审理过程中，如果发现公安机关已经就实际用资人、直贴行、出资银行的工作人员涉嫌骗取票据承兑罪、伪造印章罪等立案侦查，一方当事人根据《最高人民法院关于在审理经济纠纷案件中涉及经济犯罪嫌疑若干问题的规定》第11条的规定申请将案件移送公安机关的，因该节事实对于查明出资银行是否为正当持票人，以及参与交易的其他金融机构的抗辩理由能否成立存在重要关联，人民法院应当将有关材料移送公安机关。民商事案件的审理必须以相关刑事案件的审理结果为依据的，应当中止诉讼，待刑事案件审结后，再恢复案件的审理。案件的基本事实无须以相关刑事案件的审理结果为依据的，人民法院应当继续案件的审理。  
参与交易的其他商业银行以公安机关已经对其工作人员涉嫌受贿、伪造印章等犯罪立案侦查为由请求将案件移送公安机关的，因该节事实并不影响相关当事人民事责任的承担，人民法院应当根据《最高人民法院关于在审理经济纠纷案件中涉及经济犯罪嫌疑若干问题的规定》第10条的规定继续审理。

**Article 105** [Intersection of Civil and Criminal Issues in Bill List Transaction and Sealed Package Transaction Cases] During the adjudication of a case, if a People's Court discovers that a public security organ has already opened a case for investigation into the suspected crimes of fraudulently obtaining bill acceptance or forging seals by the staff of the actual fund user, direct discounting bank, or funding bank, and one party applies, pursuant to Article 11 of the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Handling of Economic Crime Suspicions in the Adjudication of Economic Dispute Cases, to transfer the case to the public security organ, because such facts are materially relevant to determining whether the funding bank is a bona fide holder and whether the defenses of other financial institutions participating in the transaction can be established, the People's Court shall transfer the relevant materials to the public security organ. Where the adjudication of a civil or commercial case must be based on the adjudication result of a related criminal case, the proceedings shall be suspended and resumed after the criminal case is concluded. Where the basic facts of the case do not need to be based on the adjudication result of a related criminal case, the People's Court shall continue the adjudication.  
Where other commercial banks participating in the transaction request to transfer the case to the public security organ on the ground that the public security organ has already opened a case for investigating the suspected crimes of bribery, forging seals, etc., committed by their staff, because such facts do not affect the bearing of civil liability by the relevant parties, the People's Court shall, pursuant to Article 10 of the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Handling of Economic Crime Suspicions in the Adjudication of Economic Dispute Cases, continue the adjudication.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百六条　【恶意申请公示催告的救济】公示催告程序本为对合法持票人进行失票救济所设，但实践中却沦为部分票据出卖方在未获得票款情形下，通过伪报票据丧失事实申请公示催告、阻止合法持票人行使票据权利的工具。对此，民事诉讼法司法解释已经作出了相应规定。适用时，应当区别付款人是否已经付款等情形，作出不同认定：  
（1）在除权判决作出后，付款人尚未付款的情况下，最后合法持票人可以根据《民事诉讼法》第223条的规定，在法定期限内请求撤销除权判决，待票据恢复效力后再依法行使票据权利。最后合法持票人也可以基于基础法律关系向其直接前手退票并请求其直接前手另行给付基础法律关系项下的对价。  
（2）除权判决作出后，付款人已经付款的，因恶意申请公示催告并持除权判决获得票款的行为损害了最后合法持票人的权利，最后合法持票人请求申请人承担侵权损害赔偿责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 106** [Relief Against Malicious Application for Public Notice of Exigency] The procedure of public notice of exigency is originally designed to provide relief for lawful holders who have lost their bills. However, in practice, it has become a tool for some bill sellers who, having not obtained the bill amount, maliciously apply for public notice of exigency by falsely reporting the loss of the bill, thereby preventing the lawful holder from exercising bill rights. The Judicial Interpretation of the Civil Procedure Law has already made corresponding provisions on this. When applying them, different findings shall be made depending on whether the drawee has paid, etc.:  
(1) Where the judgment of nullification has been rendered but the drawee has not yet paid, the final lawful holder may, pursuant to Article 223 of the Civil Procedure Law, request the revocation of the judgment of nullification within the statutory period, and after the bill's validity is restored, exercise the bill rights according to law. The final lawful holder may also, based on the underlying legal relationship, return the bill to its immediate prior party and request that immediate prior party to separately provide consideration under the underlying legal relationship.  
(2) Where the judgment of nullification has been rendered and the drawee has already paid, because the act of maliciously applying for public notice of exigency and obtaining the bill amount by virtue of the judgment of nullification infringes upon the rights of the final lawful holder, if the final lawful holder requests the applicant to bear tort compensation liability, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.

### 十、关于破产纠纷案件的审理

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百七条　【继续推动破产案件的及时受理】充分发挥破产重整案件信息网的线上预约登记功能，提高破产案件的受理效率。当事人提出破产申请的，人民法院不得以非法定理由拒绝接收破产申请材料。如果可能影响社会稳定的，要加强府院协调，制定相应预案，但不应当以“影响社会稳定”之名，行消极不作为之实。破产申请材料不完备的，立案部门应当告知当事人在指定期限内补充材料，待材料齐备后以“破申”作为案件类型代字编制案号登记立案，并及时将案件移送破产审判部门进行破产审查。  
注重发挥破产和解制度简便快速清理债权债务关系的功能，债务人根据《企业破产法》第95条的规定，直接提出和解申请，或者在破产申请受理后宣告破产前申请和解的，人民法院应当依法受理并及时作出是否批准的裁定。

**Article 107** [Continuously Promoting Timely Acceptance of Bankruptcy Cases] The online reservation and registration function of the bankruptcy reorganization case information network shall be fully utilized to improve the efficiency of accepting bankruptcy cases. Where a party files a bankruptcy application, a People's Court shall not refuse to accept the bankruptcy application materials on non-statutory grounds. If it may affect social stability, coordination between the government and the court shall be strengthened, and corresponding contingency plans shall be formulated; however, passive inaction shall not be taken under the pretext of "affecting social stability". Where the bankruptcy application materials are incomplete, the case-filing department shall notify the party to supplement the materials within a specified period; after the materials are complete, a case number shall be assigned with the type code "Po Shen" (bankruptcy application) for registration and case-filing, and the case shall be promptly transferred to the bankruptcy adjudication department for bankruptcy review.  
Attention shall be paid to leveraging the function of the bankruptcy composition system for simple and rapid clearance of creditor-debtor relationships. Where a debtor directly files a composition application pursuant to Article 95 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, or applies for composition after the bankruptcy application is accepted but before bankruptcy is declared, the People's Court shall accept it according to law and promptly render a ruling on whether to approve it.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百八条　【破产申请的不予受理和撤回】人民法院裁定受理破产申请前，提出破产申请的债权人的债权因清偿或者其他原因消灭的，因申请人不再具备申请资格，人民法院应当裁定不予受理。但该裁定不影响其他符合条件的主体再次提出破产申请。破产申请受理后，管理人以上述清偿符合《企业破产法》第31条、第32条为由请求撤销的，人民法院查实后应当予以支持。  
人民法院裁定受理破产申请系对债务人具有破产原因的初步认可，破产申请受理后，申请人请求撤回破产申请的，人民法院不予准许。除非存在《企业破产法》第12条第2款规定的情形，人民法院不得裁定驳回破产申请。

**Article 108** [Non-Acceptance and Withdrawal of Bankruptcy Application] Before a People's Court renders a ruling accepting a bankruptcy application, if the creditor who filed the bankruptcy application has its claim extinguished due to performance or other reasons, because the applicant no longer has the qualification to apply, the People's Court shall rule not to accept the application. However, such ruling shall not affect other qualified entities from filing a bankruptcy application again. After the bankruptcy application is accepted, if the administrator requests the revocation of such performance on the ground that it falls under Article 31 or Article 32 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the People's Court, upon verification, shall support the request.  
A People's Court's ruling to accept a bankruptcy application is a preliminary recognition that the debtor has grounds for bankruptcy. After the bankruptcy application is accepted, if the applicant requests to withdraw the bankruptcy application, the People's Court shall not permit it. Unless there are circumstances provided in Article 12, paragraph 2 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the People's Court shall not rule to dismiss the bankruptcy application.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百九条　【受理后债务人财产保全措施的处理】要切实落实破产案件受理后相关保全措施应予解除、相关执行措施应当中止、债务人财产应当及时交付管理人等规定，充分运用信息化技术手段，通过信息共享与整合，维护债务人财产的完整性。相关人民法院拒不解除保全措施或者拒不中止执行的，破产受理人民法院可以请求该法院的上级人民法院依法予以纠正。对债务人财产采取保全措施或者执行措施的人民法院未依法及时解除保全措施、移交处置权，或者中止执行程序并移交有关财产的，上级人民法院应当依法予以纠正。相关人员违反上述规定造成严重后果的，破产受理人民法院可以向人民法院纪检监察部门移送其违法审判责任线索。  
人民法院审理企业破产案件时，有关债务人财产被其他具有强制执行权力的国家行政机关，包括税务机关、公安机关、海关等采取保全措施或者执行程序的, 人民法院应当积极与上述机关进行协调和沟通，取得有关机关的配合，参照上述具体操作规程，解除有关保全措施，中止有关执行程序，以便保障破产程序顺利进行。

**Article 109** [Handling of Preservation Measures on Debtor's Property After Acceptance] The provisions that after acceptance of a bankruptcy case, relevant preservation measures shall be discharged, relevant enforcement measures shall be suspended, and the debtor's property shall be promptly delivered to the administrator shall be effectively implemented. Information technology means shall be fully utilized, and through information sharing and integration, the integrity of the debtor's property shall be maintained. If the relevant People's Court refuses to discharge the preservation measures or refuses to suspend enforcement, the People's Court that accepted the bankruptcy case may request the higher People's Court of that court to correct it according to law. If a People's Court that has taken preservation measures or enforcement measures against the debtor's property fails to timely discharge the preservation measures, transfer the disposal right, or suspend the enforcement procedure and hand over the relevant property according to law, the higher People's Court shall correct it according to law. If the relevant personnel violate the above provisions and cause serious consequences, the People's Court that accepted the bankruptcy case may transfer the clues of their illegal adjudication responsibility to the discipline inspection and supervision department of the People's Court.  
When a People's Court adjudicates an enterprise bankruptcy case, if the debtor's property is subject to preservation measures or enforcement procedures by other state administrative organs with compulsory enforcement powers, including tax authorities, public security organs, customs, etc., the People's Court shall actively coordinate and communicate with the above organs to obtain their cooperation, and by reference to the above specific operating procedures, discharge the relevant preservation measures and suspend the relevant enforcement procedures, so as to ensure the smooth progress of the bankruptcy proceedings.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百十条　【受理后有关债务人诉讼的处理】人民法院受理破产申请后，已经开始而尚未终结的有关债务人的民事诉讼，在管理人接管债务人财产和诉讼事务后继续进行。债权人已经对债务人提起的给付之诉，破产申请受理后，人民法院应当继续审理，但是在判定相关当事人实体权利义务时，应当注意与企业破产法及其司法解释的规定相协调。  
上述裁判作出并生效前，债权人可以同时向管理人申报债权，但其作为债权尚未确定的债权人,原则上不得行使表决权，除非人民法院临时确定其债权额。上述裁判生效后，债权人应当根据裁判认定的债权数额在破产程序中依法统一受偿，其对债务人享有的债权利息应当按照《企业破产法》第46条第2款的规定停止计算。  
人民法院受理破产申请后，债权人新提起的要求债务人清偿的民事诉讼，人民法院不予受理，同时告知债权人应当向管理人申报债权。债权人申报债权后，对管理人编制的债权表记载有异议的，可以根据《企业破产法》第58条的规定提起债权确认之诉。

**Article 110** [Handling of Lawsuits Concerning the Debtor After Acceptance] After a People's Court accepts a bankruptcy application, any civil lawsuit concerning the debtor that has already been initiated but has not yet concluded shall continue after the administrator takes over the debtor's property and litigation affairs. Where a creditor has already initiated a claim for performance against the debtor, after the bankruptcy application is accepted, the People's Court shall continue the adjudication; however, when determining the substantive rights and obligations of the relevant parties, attention shall be paid to coordination with the provisions of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law and its judicial interpretations.  
Before the above judgment or ruling is rendered and becomes effective, the creditor may simultaneously declare its claim to the administrator; however, as a creditor whose claim has not yet been ascertained, it shall generally not be entitled to exercise the voting right, unless the People's Court temporarily determines the amount of its claim. After the above judgment or ruling becomes effective, the creditor shall, based on the amount of claim determined by the judgment or ruling, receive satisfaction uniformly in the bankruptcy proceedings according to law, and the interest on the claim it holds against the debtor shall cease to accrue in accordance with Article 46, paragraph 2 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law.  
After a People's Court accepts a bankruptcy application, if a creditor initiates a new civil lawsuit demanding satisfaction from the debtor, the People's Court shall not accept it, and shall at the same time inform the creditor that it shall declare its claim to the administrator. After declaring its claim, if the creditor disputes the record of claims prepared by the administrator, it may, pursuant to Article 58 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, file a lawsuit for confirmation of the claim.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百十一条　【债务人自行管理的条件】重整期间，债务人同时符合下列条件的，经申请，人民法院可以批准债务人在管理人的监督下自行管理财产和营业事务：  
（1）债务人的内部治理机制仍正常运转；  
（2）债务人自行管理有利于债务人继续经营；  
（3）债务人不存在隐匿、转移财产的行为；  
（4）债务人不存在其他严重损害债权人利益的行为。  
债务人提出重整申请时可以一并提出自行管理的申请。经人民法院批准由债务人自行管理财产和营业事务的，企业破产法规定的管理人职权中有关财产管理和营业经营的职权应当由债务人行使。  
管理人应当对债务人的自行管理行为进行监督。管理人发现债务人存在严重损害债权人利益的行为或者有其他不适宜自行管理情形的，可以申请人民法院作出终止债务人自行管理的决定。人民法院决定终止的，应当通知管理人接管债务人财产和营业事务。债务人有上述行为而管理人未申请人民法院作出终止决定的，债权人等利害关系人可以向人民法院提出申请。

**Article 111** [Conditions for Self-Administration by the Debtor] During the reorganization period, if the debtor concurrently meets the following conditions, upon application, the People's Court may approve the debtor to self-administer its property and business operations under the supervision of the administrator:  
(1) The debtor's internal governance mechanism is still functioning normally;  
(2) Self-administration by the debtor is conducive to the debtor's continued operation;  
(3) The debtor has not concealed or transferred property;  
(4) The debtor has no other acts that seriously harm the interests of creditors.  
When filing a reorganization application, the debtor may simultaneously file an application for self-administration. Where the People's Court approves the debtor to self-administer its property and business operations, the powers of the administrator under the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law concerning property management and business operations shall be exercised by the debtor.  
The administrator shall supervise the debtor's self-administration. If the administrator finds that the debtor has committed acts that seriously harm the interests of creditors or there are other situations where self-administration is inappropriate, it may apply to the People's Court for a decision to terminate the debtor's self-administration. If the People's Court decides to terminate, it shall notify the administrator to take over the debtor's property and business operations. If the debtor has committed the above acts but the administrator does not apply to the People's Court for a termination decision, creditors or other interested parties may file an application with the People's Court.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百十二条　【重整中担保物权的恢复行使】重整程序中，要依法平衡保护担保物权人的合法权益和企业重整价值。重整申请受理后，管理人或者自行管理的债务人应当及时确定设定有担保物权的债务人财产是否为重整所必需。如果认为担保物不是重整所必需，管理人或者自行管理的债务人应当及时对担保物进行拍卖或者变卖，拍卖或者变卖担保物所得价款在支付拍卖、变卖费用后优先清偿担保物权人的债权。  
在担保物权暂停行使期间，担保物权人根据《企业破产法》第75条的规定向人民法院请求恢复行使担保物权的，人民法院应当自收到恢复行使担保物权申请之日起三十日内作出裁定。经审查，担保物权人的申请不符合第75条的规定，或者虽然符合该条规定但管理人或者自行管理的债务人有证据证明担保物是重整所必需，并且提供与减少价值相应担保或者补偿的，人民法院应当裁定不予批准恢复行使担保物权。担保物权人不服该裁定的，可以自收到裁定书之日起十日内，向作出裁定的人民法院申请复议。人民法院裁定批准行使担保物权的，管理人或者自行管理的债务人应当自收到裁定书之日起十五日内启动对担保物的拍卖或者变卖，拍卖或者变卖担保物所得价款在支付拍卖、变卖费用后优先清偿担保物权人的债权。

**Article 112** [Resumption of Exercise of Security Interests in Reorganization] In the reorganization proceedings, the legitimate rights and interests of the holder of a security interest and the value of the enterprise in reorganization shall be balanced and protected according to law. After the reorganization application is accepted, the administrator or the debtor in self-administration shall promptly determine whether the debtor's property subject to a security interest is necessary for the reorganization. If it is determined that the collateral is not necessary for the reorganization, the administrator or the debtor in self-administration shall promptly auction or sell the collateral, and the proceeds from the auction or sale of the collateral shall, after payment of the auction or sale expenses, be used to satisfy the claim of the holder of the security interest in priority.  
During the period when the exercise of the security interest is suspended, if the holder of the security interest requests the People's Court to resume the exercise of the security interest pursuant to Article 75 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the People's Court shall render a ruling within thirty days from the date of receiving the application for resumption of exercise of the security interest. Upon review, if the application of the holder of the security interest does not comply with the provisions of Article 75, or although it complies with such provisions but the administrator or the debtor in self-administration has evidence proving that the collateral is necessary for the reorganization and provides security or compensation corresponding to the diminution in value, the People's Court shall rule not to approve the resumption of exercise of the security interest. If the holder of the security interest is dissatisfied with the ruling, it may, within ten days from the date of receipt of the written ruling, apply for reconsideration to the People's Court that made the ruling. If the People's Court rules to approve the exercise of the security interest, the administrator or the debtor in self-administration shall, within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the written ruling, initiate the auction or sale of the collateral, and the proceeds from the auction or sale of the collateral shall, after payment of the auction or sale expenses, be used to satisfy the claim of the holder of the security interest in priority.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百十三条　【重整计划监督期间的管理人报酬及诉讼管辖】要依法确保重整计划的执行和有效监督。重整计划的执行期间和监督期间原则上应当一致。二者不一致的，人民法院在确定和调整重整程序中的管理人报酬方案时，应当根据重整期间和重整计划监督期间管理人工作量的不同予以区别对待。其中，重整期间的管理人报酬应当根据管理人对重整发挥的实际作用等因素予以确定和支付；重整计划监督期间管理人报酬的支付比例和支付时间，应当根据管理人监督职责的履行情况，与债权人按照重整计划实际受偿比例和受偿时间相匹配。  
重整计划执行期间，因重整程序终止后新发生的事实或者事件引发的有关债务人的民事诉讼，不适用《企业破产法》第21条有关集中管辖的规定。除重整计划有明确约定外，上述纠纷引发的诉讼，不再由管理人代表债务人进行。

**Article 113** [Administrator's Remuneration and Jurisdiction During the Supervision Period of the Reorganization Plan] The execution and effective supervision of the reorganization plan shall be ensured according to law. The execution period and the supervision period of the reorganization plan shall in principle be consistent. Where they are inconsistent, when determining and adjusting the administrator's remuneration plan in the reorganization proceedings, the People's Court shall treat them differently based on the difference in the administrator's workload during the reorganization period and the supervision period of the reorganization plan. Among them, the administrator's remuneration during the reorganization period shall be determined and paid based on factors such as the actual role the administrator played in the reorganization; the payment proportion and timing of the administrator's remuneration during the supervision period of the reorganization plan shall, based on the performance of the administrator's supervisory duties, match the actual proportion and timing of creditor satisfaction under the reorganization plan.  
During the execution period of the reorganization plan, civil lawsuits concerning the debtor arising from facts or events that occur after the termination of the reorganization proceedings shall not be subject to the provisions on centralized jurisdiction in Article 21 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law. Unless otherwise expressly provided in the reorganization plan, lawsuits arising from the above disputes shall no longer be conducted by the administrator on behalf of the debtor.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百十四条　【重整程序与破产清算程序的衔接】重整期间或者重整计划执行期间，债务人因法定事由被宣告破产的，人民法院不再另立新的案号，原重整程序的管理人原则上应当继续履行破产清算程序中的职责。原重整程序的管理人不能继续履行职责或者不适宜继续担任管理人的，人民法院应当依法重新指定管理人。  
重整程序转破产清算案件中的管理人报酬，应当综合管理人为重整工作和清算工作分别发挥的实际作用等因素合理确定。重整期间因法定事由转入破产清算程序的，应当按照破产清算案件确定管理人报酬。重整计划执行期间因法定事由转入破产清算程序的，后续破产清算阶段的管理人报酬应当根据管理人实际工作量予以确定，不能简单根据债务人最终清偿的财产价值总额计算。  
重整程序因人民法院裁定批准重整计划草案而终止的，重整案件可作结案处理。重整计划执行完毕后，人民法院可以根据管理人等利害关系人申请，作出重整程序终结的裁定。

**Article 114** [Connection Between Reorganization Proceedings and Bankruptcy Liquidation Proceedings] During the reorganization period or the execution period of the reorganization plan, if the debtor is declared bankrupt due to statutory reasons, the People's Court shall not create a new case number, and the administrator of the original reorganization proceedings shall in principle continue to perform the duties in the bankruptcy liquidation proceedings. If the administrator of the original reorganization proceedings is unable to continue performing its duties or is unsuitable to continue serving as administrator, the People's Court shall appoint a new administrator according to law.  
The administrator's remuneration in a case that transfers from reorganization proceedings to bankruptcy liquidation proceedings shall be reasonably determined by comprehensively considering factors such as the actual roles played by the administrator in the reorganization work and the liquidation work respectively. Where the case is transferred to bankruptcy liquidation proceedings during the reorganization period due to statutory reasons, the administrator's remuneration shall be determined as in a bankruptcy liquidation case. Where the case is transferred to bankruptcy liquidation proceedings during the execution period of the reorganization plan due to statutory reasons, the administrator's remuneration in the subsequent bankruptcy liquidation stage shall be determined based on the administrator's actual workload, and shall not be simply calculated based on the total value of the debtor's property finally distributed.  
Where the reorganization proceedings are terminated due to the People's Court's ruling approving the draft reorganization plan, the reorganization case may be closed. After the execution of the reorganization plan is completed, the People's Court may, upon application by the administrator or other interested parties, make a ruling terminating the reorganization proceedings.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百十五条　【庭外重组协议效力在重整程序中的延伸】继续完善庭外重组与庭内重整的衔接机制，降低制度性成本，提高破产制度效率。人民法院受理重整申请前，债务人和部分债权人已经达成的有关协议与重整程序中制作的重整计划草案内容一致的，有关债权人对该协议的同意视为对该重整计划草案表决的同意。但重整计划草案对协议内容进行了修改并对有关债权人有不利影响，或者与有关债权人重大利益相关的，受到影响的债权人有权按照企业破产法的规定对重整计划草案重新进行表决。

**Article 115** [Extension of Effect of Out-of-Court Restructuring Agreements in Reorganization Proceedings] The connection mechanism between out-of-court restructuring and in-court reorganization shall be continuously improved, institutional costs shall be reduced, and the efficiency of the bankruptcy system shall be enhanced. Before a People's Court accepts a reorganization application, if an agreement reached between the debtor and some creditors is consistent in content with the draft reorganization plan prepared in the reorganization proceedings, the consent of the relevant creditor to that agreement shall be deemed as consent in the vote on the draft reorganization plan. However, if the draft reorganization plan modifies the content of the agreement and has an adverse effect on the relevant creditor, or involves material interests of the relevant creditor, the affected creditor shall have the right to re-vote on the draft reorganization plan in accordance with the provisions of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百十六条　【审计、评估等中介机构的确定及责任】要合理区分人民法院和管理人在委托审计、评估等财产管理工作中的职责。破产程序中确实需要聘请中介机构对债务人财产进行审计、评估的，根据《企业破产法》第28条的规定，经人民法院许可后，管理人可以自行公开聘请，但是应当对其聘请的中介机构的相关行为进行监督。上述中介机构因不当履行职责给债务人、债权人或者第三人造成损害的，应当承担赔偿责任。管理人在聘用过程中存在过错的，应当在其过错范围内承担相应的补充赔偿责任。

**Article 116** [Appointment and Liability of Intermediary Institutions such as Auditors and Appraisers] The duties of the People's Court and the administrator in entrusting audit, appraisal, and other property management work shall be reasonably distinguished. Where it is truly necessary to engage an intermediary institution to audit or appraise the debtor's property in the bankruptcy proceedings, the administrator may, after obtaining permission from the People's Court pursuant to Article 28 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, publicly engage the intermediary institution on its own; however, it shall supervise the relevant conduct of the intermediary institution it engages. If the above intermediary institution causes damage to the debtor, creditors, or a third party due to improper performance of its duties, it shall bear compensation liability. If the administrator is at fault in the engagement process, it shall bear corresponding supplementary compensation liability within the scope of its fault.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百十七条　【公司解散清算与破产清算的衔接】要依法区分公司解散清算与破产清算的不同功能和不同适用条件。债务人同时符合破产清算条件和强制清算条件的，应当及时适用破产清算程序实现对债权人利益的公平保护。债权人对符合破产清算条件的债务人提起公司强制清算申请，经人民法院释明，债权人仍然坚持申请对债务人强制清算的，人民法院应当裁定不予受理。

**Article 117** [Connection Between Company Dissolution Liquidation and Bankruptcy Liquidation] The different functions and different applicable conditions of company dissolution liquidation and bankruptcy liquidation shall be distinguished according to law. Where a debtor concurrently meets the conditions for bankruptcy liquidation and the conditions for compulsory liquidation, the bankruptcy liquidation proceedings shall be promptly applied to achieve fair protection of creditors' interests. If a creditor files an application for compulsory liquidation of a company against a debtor that meets the conditions for bankruptcy liquidation, and after the People's Court explains the situation, the creditor still insists on applying for compulsory liquidation of the debtor, the People's Court shall rule not to accept the application.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百十八条　【无法清算案件的审理与责任承担】人民法院在审理债务人相关人员下落不明或者财产状况不清的破产案件时，应当充分贯彻债权人利益保护原则，避免债务人通过破产程序不当损害债权人利益，同时也要避免不当突破股东有限责任原则。  
人民法院在适用《最高人民法院关于债权人对人员下落不明或者财产状况不清的债务人申请破产清算案件如何处理的批复》第3款的规定，判定债务人相关人员承担责任时，应当依照企业破产法的相关规定来确定相关主体的义务内容和责任范围，不得根据公司法司法解释（二）第18条第2款的规定来判定相关主体的责任。  
上述批复第3款规定的“债务人的有关人员不履行法定义务，人民法院可依据有关法律规定追究其相应法律责任”，系指债务人的法定代表人、财务管理人员和其他经营管理人员不履行《企业破产法》第15条规定的配合清算义务，人民法院可以根据《企业破产法》第126条、第127条追究其相应法律责任，或者参照《民事诉讼法》第111条的规定，依法拘留，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；债务人的法定代表人或者实际控制人不配合清算的，人民法院可以依据《出境入境管理法》第12条的规定，对其作出不准出境的决定，以确保破产程序顺利进行。  
上述批复第3款规定的“其行为导致无法清算或者造成损失”，系指债务人的有关人员不配合清算的行为导致债务人财产状况不明，或者依法负有清算责任的人未依照《企业破产法》第7条第3款的规定及时履行破产申请义务，导致债务人主要财产、账册、重要文件等灭失，致使管理人无法执行清算职务，给债权人利益造成损害。“有关权利人起诉请求其承担相应民事责任”，系指管理人请求上述主体承担相应损害赔偿责任并将因此获得的赔偿归入债务人财产。管理人未主张上述赔偿，个别债权人可以代表全体债权人提起上述诉讼。  
上述破产清算案件被裁定终结后，相关主体以债务人主要财产、账册、重要文件等重新出现为由，申请对破产清算程序启动审判监督的，人民法院不予受理，但符合《企业破产法》第123条规定的，债权人可以请求人民法院追加分配。

**Article 118** [Adjudication of Cases Where Liquidation Cannot Be Conducted and Assumption of Liability] When adjudicating a bankruptcy case where the relevant persons of the debtor are whereabouts unknown or the property status is unclear, a People's Court shall fully implement the principle of protecting creditors' interests, avoid the debtor improperly harming creditors' interests through bankruptcy proceedings, and also avoid improperly breaking through the principle of limited liability of shareholders.  
When a People's Court applies the provisions of paragraph 3 of the Reply of the Supreme People's Court on How to Handle Cases Where a Creditor Applies for Bankruptcy Liquidation of a Debtor Whose Personnel Are Whereabouts Unknown or Whose Property Status Is Unclear to determine the liability of the relevant persons of the debtor, it shall determine the content of obligations and the scope of liability of the relevant entities in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, and shall not determine the liability of the relevant entities according to the provisions of Article 18, paragraph 2 of the Judicial Interpretation (II) of the Company Law.  
"The relevant persons of the debtor fail to perform statutory obligations, and the People's Court may pursue their corresponding legal liability in accordance with relevant legal provisions" as stated in paragraph 3 of the above Reply refers to the situation where the legal representative, financial management personnel, and other management personnel of the debtor fail to perform the obligation to cooperate in liquidation as stipulated in Article 15 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law; the People's Court may pursue their corresponding legal liability pursuant to Articles 126 and 127 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, or, by reference to Article 111 of the Civil Procedure Law, detain them according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law. If the legal representative or actual controller of the debtor fails to cooperate in the liquidation, the People's Court may, pursuant to Article 12 of the Exit and Entry Administration Law, make a decision not to allow them to leave the country, so as to ensure the smooth progress of the bankruptcy proceedings.  
"Their acts cause the liquidation to be impossible or cause losses" as stated in paragraph 3 of the above Reply refers to the situation where the failure of the relevant persons of the debtor to cooperate in the liquidation causes the debtor's property status to be unclear, or where the person legally responsible for liquidation fails to timely perform the obligation to file for bankruptcy in accordance with Article 7, paragraph 3 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, resulting in the loss of the debtor's main property, account books, important documents, etc., thereby rendering the administrator unable to perform its liquidation duties and causing damage to the interests of creditors. "The relevant right holder sues requesting them to bear corresponding civil liability" refers to the administrator requesting the above entities to bear corresponding compensation liability for damages and to include the compensation obtained thereby into the debtor's property. If the administrator does not claim the above compensation, an individual creditor may file the above lawsuit on behalf of all creditors.  
After the above bankruptcy liquidation case is ruled to be closed, if a relevant entity applies for the initiation of trial supervision over the bankruptcy liquidation proceedings on the ground that the debtor's main property, account books, important documents, etc., have reappeared, the People's Court shall not accept the application; however, if the conditions under Article 123 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law are met, a creditor may request the People's Court for supplementary distribution.

### 十一、关于案外人救济案件的审理

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百十九条　【案外人执行异议之诉的审理】案外人执行异议之诉以排除对特定标的物的执行为目的，从程序上而言，案外人依据《民事诉讼法》第227条提出执行异议被驳回的，即可向执行人民法院提起执行异议之诉。人民法院对执行异议之诉的审理，一般应当就案外人对执行标的物是否享有权利、享有什么样的权利、权利是否足以排除强制执行进行判断。至于是否作出具体的确权判项，视案外人的诉讼请求而定。案外人未提出确权或者给付诉讼请求的，不作出确权判项，仅在裁判理由中进行分析判断并作出是否排除执行的判项即可。但案外人既提出确权、给付请求，又提出排除执行请求的，人民法院对该请求是否支持、是否排除执行，均应当在具体判项中予以明确。执行异议之诉不以否定作为执行依据的生效裁判为目的，案外人如认为裁判确有错误的，只能通过申请再审或者提起第三人撤销之诉的方式进行救济。

**Article 119** [Adjudication of Third-Party Enforcement Objection Lawsuit] A third-party enforcement objection lawsuit aims to exclude enforcement against a specific subject matter. Procedurally, if a third party's enforcement objection raised pursuant to Article 227 of the Civil Procedure Law is dismissed, it may file an enforcement objection lawsuit with the enforcement People's Court. In the adjudication of an enforcement objection lawsuit, a People's Court shall generally determine whether the third party has a right in the subject matter of enforcement, what kind of right it has, and whether the right is sufficient to exclude compulsory enforcement. Whether a specific judgment item confirming the right is made shall depend on the third party's claims. If the third party does not raise a claim for confirmation of rights or for performance, no judgment item confirming the right shall be made; instead, analysis and determination may be made in the reasoning part of the judgment, and only a judgment item on whether to exclude enforcement shall be made. However, if the third party raises both a claim for confirmation of rights or performance and a claim for exclusion of enforcement, the People's Court shall specify in the specific judgment items whether the claim is supported and whether enforcement is excluded. The enforcement objection lawsuit is not intended to deny the validity of the effective judgment that serves as the basis for enforcement. If the third party believes that the judgment is indeed erroneous, the only means of relief are to apply for retrial or to file a third-party revocation action.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　【债权人能否提起第三人撤销之诉】第三人撤销之诉中的第三人仅局限于《民事诉讼法》第56条规定的有独立请求权及无独立请求权的第三人，而且一般不包括债权人。但是，设立第三人撤销之诉的目的在于，救济第三人享有的因不能归责于本人的事由未参加诉讼但因生效裁判文书内容错误受到损害的民事权益，因此，债权人在下列情况下可以提起第三人撤销之诉：  
（1）该债权是法律明确给予特殊保护的债权，如《合同法》第286条规定的建设工程价款优先受偿权，《海商法》第22条规定的船舶优先权；  
（2）因债务人与他人的权利义务被生效裁判文书确定，导致债权人本来可以对《合同法》第74条和《企业破产法》第31条规定的债务人的行为享有撤销权而不能行使的；  
（3）债权人有证据证明，裁判文书主文确定的债权内容部分或者全部虚假的。  
债权人提起第三人撤销之诉还要符合法律和司法解释规定的其他条件。对于除此之外的其他债权，债权人原则上不得提起第三人撤销之诉。

**Article 120** [Whether a Creditor May File a Third-Party Revocation Action] The third party in a third-party revocation action is limited to the third party with an independent claim or the third party without an independent claim as provided in Article 56 of the Civil Procedure Law, and generally does not include creditors. However, the purpose of establishing the third-party revocation action is to remedy the civil rights and interests of a third party that, due to reasons not attributable to itself, failed to participate in the proceedings but suffered damage due to errors in the content of an effective judgment document. Therefore, a creditor may file a third-party revocation action in the following circumstances:  
(1) The creditor's right is one that the law specifically gives special protection, such as the priority right to be paid for construction project price under Article 286 of the Contract Law, or the maritime lien under Article 22 of the Maritime Law;  
(2) Due to the determination of the rights and obligations between the debtor and another person by an effective judgment document, the creditor cannot exercise the right of revocation that it would otherwise have against the debtor's acts under Article 74 of the Contract Law and Article 31 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law;  
(3) The creditor has evidence proving that the content of the creditor's right determined in the main text of the judgment document is wholly or partially false.  
A creditor filing a third-party revocation action shall also meet other conditions provided by law and judicial interpretations. For creditor's rights other than those mentioned above, a creditor shall, in principle, not file a third-party revocation action.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　【必要共同诉讼漏列的当事人申请再审】民事诉讼法司法解释对必要共同诉讼漏列的当事人申请再审规定了两种不同的程序，二者在管辖法院及申请再审期限的起算点上存在明显差别，人民法院在审理相关案件时应予注意：  
（1）该当事人在执行程序中以案外人身份提出异议，异议被驳回的，根据民事诉讼法司法解释第423条的规定，其可以在驳回异议裁定送达之日起6个月内向原审人民法院申请再审；  
（2）该当事人未在执行程序中以案外人身份提出异议的，根据民事诉讼法司法解释第422条的规定，其可以根据《民事诉讼法》第200条第8项的规定，自知道或者应当知道生效裁判之日起6个月内向上一级人民法院申请再审。当事人一方人数众多或者当事人双方为公民的案件，也可以向原审人民法院申请再审。

**Article 121** : [Application for Retrial by a Missing Party in Necessary Joint Action] The Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law provides two different procedures for a missing party in a necessary joint action to apply for a retrial. There are significant differences between the two in terms of the competent court and the starting point of the time limit for applying for retrial. The People's Court shall pay attention to this when adjudicating relevant cases:  
(1) If such party raises an objection in the enforcement proceedings as a person not involved in the case and the objection is rejected, according to Article 423 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law, it may apply for a retrial to the People's Court of first instance within six months from the date of service of the ruling rejecting the objection;  
(2) If such party does not raise an objection in the enforcement proceedings as a person not involved in the case, according to Article 422 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law, it may, in accordance with Article 200(8) of the Civil Procedure Law, apply for a retrial to the People's Court at the next higher level within six months from the date it knows or should have known of the effective judgment. For a case where one party consists of a large number of persons or both parties are citizens, a retrial may also be applied for to the People's Court of first instance.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　【程序启动后案外人不享有程序选择权】案外人申请再审与第三人撤销之诉功能上近似，如果案外人既有申请再审的权利，又符合第三人撤销之诉的条件，对于案外人是否可以行使选择权，民事诉讼法司法解释采取了限制的司法态度，即依据民事诉讼法司法解释第303条的规定，按照启动程序的先后，案外人只能选择相应的救济程序：案外人先启动执行异议程序的，对执行异议裁定不服，认为原裁判内容错误损害其合法权益的，只能向作出原裁判的人民法院申请再审，而不能提起第三人撤销之诉；案外人先启动了第三人撤销之诉，即便在执行程序中又提出执行异议，也只能继续进行第三人撤销之诉，而不能依《民事诉讼法》第227条申请再审。

**Article 122** : [No Right of Choice for Persons Not Involved in the Case After Commencement of Procedure] The application for retrial by a person not involved in the case and the third-party revocation action are functionally similar. If a person not involved in the case has both the right to apply for retrial and meets the conditions for a third-party revocation action, the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law adopts a restrictive judicial attitude as to whether such person may exercise the right of choice. That is, according to Article 303 of the said Interpretation, based on the order of commencement of the procedures, the person not involved in the case may only choose the corresponding remedy procedure: if such person first commences the objection procedure in enforcement and is dissatisfied with the ruling on the objection, believing that the content of the original judgment is erroneous and damages its lawful rights and interests, it may only apply for retrial to the People's Court that rendered the original judgment and may not file a third-party revocation action; if such person first commences a third-party revocation action, even if it subsequently raises an objection in enforcement proceedings, it may only continue with the third-party revocation action and may not apply for retrial under Article 227 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　【案外人依据另案生效裁判对非金钱债权的执行提起执行异议之诉】审判实践中，案外人有时依据另案生效裁判所认定的与执行标的物有关的权利提起执行异议之诉，请求排除对标的物的执行。此时，鉴于作为执行依据的生效裁判与作为案外人提出执行异议依据的生效裁判，均涉及对同一标的物权属或给付的认定，性质上属于两个生效裁判所认定的权利之间可能产生的冲突，人民法院在审理执行异议之诉时，需区别不同情况作出判断：如果作为执行依据的生效裁判是确权裁判，不论作为执行异议依据的裁判是确权裁判还是给付裁判，一般不应据此排除执行，但人民法院应当告知案外人对作为执行依据的确权裁判申请再审；如果作为执行依据的生效裁判是给付标的物的裁判，而作为提出异议之诉依据的裁判是确权裁判，一般应据此排除执行，此时人民法院应告知其对该确权裁判申请再审；如果两个裁判均属给付标的物的裁判，人民法院需依法判断哪个裁判所认定的给付权利具有优先性，进而判断是否可以排除执行。

**Article 123** : [Action for Objection to Enforcement of Non-Monetary Claims by a Person Not Involved in the Case Based on Another Effective Judgment] In adjudication practice, a person not involved in the case sometimes initiates an action for objection to enforcement based on rights relating to the subject matter of enforcement as determined by another effective judgment, requesting exclusion of enforcement against the subject matter. In such case, given that both the effective judgment serving as the basis for enforcement and the effective judgment serving as the basis for the person not involved in the case to raise the objection involve the determination of ownership or performance regarding the same subject matter, and the nature of the matter is a potential conflict between the rights determined by the two effective judgments, the People's Court, when adjudicating the action for objection to enforcement, shall make a determination by distinguishing different circumstances: if the effective judgment serving as the basis for enforcement is a judgment confirming rights, regardless of whether the judgment serving as the basis for the objection is a judgment confirming rights or a judgment ordering performance, enforcement shall generally not be excluded on that basis; however, the People's Court shall inform the person not involved in the case that it may apply for retrial of the judgment confirming rights that serves as the basis for enforcement; if the effective judgment serving as the basis for enforcement is a judgment ordering performance of the subject matter, and the judgment serving as the basis for the objection action is a judgment confirming rights, enforcement shall generally be excluded on that basis; in such case, the People's Court shall inform the person that it may apply for retrial of the judgment confirming rights; if both judgments are judgments ordering performance of the subject matter, the People's Court shall, according to law, determine which judgment's determined performance right has priority, and then determine whether enforcement may be excluded.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　【案外人依据另案生效裁判对金钱债权的执行提起执行异议之诉】作为执行依据的生效裁判并未涉及执行标的物，只是执行中为实现金钱债权对特定标的物采取了执行措施。对此种情形，《最高人民法院关于人民法院办理执行异议和复议案件若干问题的规定》第26条规定了解决案外人执行异议的规则，在审理执行异议之诉时可以参考适用。依据该条规定，作为案外人提起执行异议之诉依据的裁判将执行标的物确权给案外人，可以排除执行；作为案外人提起执行异议之诉依据的裁判，未将执行标的物确权给案外人，而是基于不以转移所有权为目的的有效合同（如租赁、借用、保管合同），判令向案外人返还执行标的物的，其性质属于物权请求权，亦可以排除执行；基于以转移所有权为目的有效合同（如买卖合同），判令向案外人交付标的物的，其性质属于债权请求权，不能排除执行。  
应予注意的是，在金钱债权执行中，如果案外人提出执行异议之诉依据的生效裁判认定以转移所有权为目的的合同（如买卖合同）无效或应当解除，进而判令向案外人返还执行标的物的，此时案外人享有的是物权性质的返还请求权，本可排除金钱债权的执行，但在双务合同无效的情况下，双方互负返还义务，在案外人未返还价款的情况下，如果允许其排除金钱债权的执行，将会使申请执行人既执行不到被执行人名下的财产，又执行不到本应返还给被执行人的价款，显然有失公允。为平衡各方当事人的利益，只有在案外人已经返还价款的情况下，才能排除普通债权人的执行。反之，案外人未返还价款的，不能排除执行。

**Article 124** : [Action for Objection to Enforcement of Monetary Claims by a Person Not Involved in the Case Based on Another Effective Judgment] Where the effective judgment serving as the basis for enforcement does not involve the subject matter of enforcement, and enforcement measures are taken against a specific subject matter only for the purpose of realizing a monetary claim in the course of enforcement. For such circumstance, Article 26 of the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Handling of Objections to Enforcement and Review Cases by People's Courts stipulates rules for resolving objections to enforcement raised by persons not involved in the case, which may be referred to and applied when adjudicating actions for objection to enforcement. According to that Article, if the judgment serving as the basis for the action for objection to enforcement initiated by the person not involved in the case confirms the ownership of the subject matter of enforcement in favor of such person, enforcement may be excluded; if such judgment does not confirm ownership of the subject matter of enforcement in favor of the person not involved in the case, but orders the return of the subject matter of enforcement to such person based on a valid contract not aimed at transferring ownership (such as a lease, loan-for-use, or deposit contract), the nature of the claim is a real right claim, and enforcement may also be excluded; if the judgment orders delivery of the subject matter to the person not involved in the case based on a valid contract aimed at transferring ownership (such as a sales contract), the nature of the claim is a creditor's right claim, and enforcement may not be excluded.  
It shall be noted that, in the enforcement of monetary claims, if the effective judgment serving as the basis for the action for objection to enforcement initiated by the person not involved in the case determines that a contract aimed at transferring ownership (such as a sales contract) is void or shall be dissolved, and consequently orders the return of the subject matter of enforcement to such person, then the person not involved in the case enjoys a restitution claim of a real right nature, which could otherwise exclude enforcement of the monetary claim. However, in the case of a void bilateral contract where both parties have reciprocal restitution obligations, if the person not involved in the case has not returned the purchase price, allowing it to exclude enforcement of the monetary claim would mean that the enforcement applicant can neither enforce against the property under the name of the person subject to enforcement nor obtain the purchase price that should have been returned to the person subject to enforcement, which is obviously unfair. To balance the interests of all parties, only when the person not involved in the case has already returned the purchase price may enforcement by an ordinary creditor be excluded. Conversely, if the person not involved in the case has not returned the purchase price, enforcement may not be excluded.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　【案外人系商品房消费者】实践中，商品房消费者向房地产开发企业购买商品房，往往没有及时办理房地产过户手续。房地产开发企业因欠债而被强制执行，人民法院在对尚登记在房地产开发企业名下但已出卖给消费者的商品房采取执行措施时，商品房消费者往往会提出执行异议，以排除强制执行。对此，《最高人民法院关于人民法院办理执行异议和复议案件若干问题的规定》第29条规定，符合下列情形的，应当支持商品房消费者的诉讼请求：一是在人民法院查封之前已签订合法有效的书面买卖合同；二是所购商品房系用于居住且买受人名下无其他用于居住的房屋；三是已支付的价款超过合同约定总价款的百分之五十。人民法院在审理执行异议之诉案件时，可参照适用此条款。  
问题是，对于其中 “所购商品房系用于居住且买受人名下无其他用于居住的房屋”如何理解，审判实践中掌握的标准不一。“买受人名下无其他用于居住的房屋”，可以理解为在案涉房屋同一设区的市或者县级市范围内商品房消费者名下没有用于居住的房屋。商品房消费者名下虽然已有1套房屋，但购买的房屋在面积上仍然属于满足基本居住需要的，可以理解为符合该规定的精神。  
对于其中 “已支付的价款超过合同约定总价款的百分之五十” 如何理解，审判实践中掌握的标准也不一致。如果商品房消费者支付的价款接近于百分之五十，且已按照合同约定将剩余价款支付给申请执行人或者按照人民法院的要求交付执行的，可以理解为符合该规定的精神。

**Article 125** : [Person Not Involved in the Case Being a Commercial Housing Consumer] In practice, commercial housing consumers who purchase commercial housing from real estate development enterprises often fail to promptly complete the real estate transfer procedures. When a real estate development enterprise is subject to compulsory enforcement due to debt, and the People's Court takes enforcement measures against the commercial housing that is still registered in the name of the real estate development enterprise but has been sold to a consumer, the commercial housing consumer often raises an objection to enforcement to exclude compulsory enforcement. In this regard, Article 29 of the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Handling of Objections to Enforcement and Review Cases by People's Courts stipulates that the following circumstances shall be met for the court to support the claim of the commercial housing consumer: (1) a legally valid written sales contract had been signed before the seizure by the People's Court; (2) the purchased commercial housing is for residential use and the buyer has no other housing for residential use under its name; (3) the price already paid exceeds 50% of the total price agreed in the contract. When adjudicating cases of action for objection to enforcement, the People's Court may refer to and apply this Article.  
The issue is how to understand "the purchased commercial housing is for residential use and the buyer has no other housing for residential use under its name." The standards adopted in adjudication practice vary. "The buyer has no other housing for residential use under its name" may be understood to mean that within the same city divided into districts or the same county-level city where the housing in question is located, the commercial housing consumer has no housing for residential use under its name. Even if the commercial housing consumer already has one housing unit under its name, if the purchased housing still satisfies the basic residential needs in terms of area, it may be understood to meet the spirit of this provision.  
As for how to understand "the price already paid exceeds 50% of the total price agreed in the contract," the standards adopted in adjudication practice also vary. If the commercial housing consumer has paid a price close to 50% and has paid the remaining price to the enforcement applicant in accordance with the contract or has delivered it for enforcement as required by the People's Court, it may be understood to meet the spirit of this provision.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条　【商品房消费者的权利与抵押权的关系】根据《最高人民法院关于建设工程价款优先受偿权问题的批复》第1条、第2条的规定，交付全部或者大部分款项的商品房消费者的权利优先于抵押权人的抵押权，故抵押权人申请执行登记在房地产开发企业名下但已销售给消费者的商品房，消费者提出执行异议的，人民法院依法予以支持。但应当特别注意的是，此情况是针对实践中存在的商品房预售不规范现象为保护消费者生存权而作出的例外规定，必须严格把握条件，避免扩大范围，以免动摇抵押权具有优先性的基本原则。因此，这里的商品房消费者应当仅限于符合本纪要第125条规定的商品房消费者。买受人不是本纪要第125条规定的商品房消费者，而是一般的房屋买卖合同的买受人，不适用上述处理规则。

**Article 126** : [Relationship Between the Rights of Commercial Housing Consumers and Mortgage Rights] According to Paragraphs 1 and 2 of the Reply of the Supreme People's Court on the Issue of Priority of Compensation for Construction Project Price, the rights of commercial housing consumers who have paid all or a substantial portion of the price take precedence over the mortgage rights of the mortgagee. Therefore, if a mortgagee applies for enforcement of commercial housing registered in the name of a real estate development enterprise but already sold to a consumer, and the consumer raises an objection to enforcement, the People's Court shall support it according to law. However, it shall be particularly noted that this circumstance is an exceptional provision made to protect the right of survival of consumers in response to the irregular practice of commercial housing pre-sale in practice. The conditions must be strictly controlled to avoid expanding the scope, so as not to undermine the basic principle of the priority of mortgage rights. Therefore, the commercial housing consumer referred to herein shall be limited to those who meet the requirements of Article 125 of this Summary. If the buyer is not a commercial housing consumer as provided in Article 125 of this Summary but rather a buyer under a general housing sales contract, the above handling rules shall not apply.

<a id="art-127"></a>第一百二十七条　【案外人系商品房消费者之外的一般买受人】金钱债权执行中，商品房消费者之外的一般买受人对登记在被执行人名下的不动产提出异议，请求排除执行的，《最高人民法院关于人民法院办理执行异议和复议案件若干问题的规定》第28条规定，符合下列情形的依法予以支持：一是在人民法院查封之前已签订合法有效的书面买卖合同；二是在人民法院查封之前已合法占有该不动产；三是已支付全部价款，或者已按照合同约定支付部分价款且将剩余价款按照人民法院的要求交付执行；四是非因买受人自身原因未办理过户登记。人民法院在审理执行异议之诉案件时，可参照适用此条款。  
实践中，对于该规定的前3个条件，理解并无分歧。对于其中的第4个条件，理解不一致。一般而言，买受人只要有向房屋登记机构递交过户登记材料，或向出卖人提出了办理过户登记的请求等积极行为的，可以认为符合该条件。买受人无上述积极行为，其未办理过户登记有合理的客观理由的，亦可认定符合该条件。

**Article 127** : [Person Not Involved in the Case Being a General Buyer Other Than a Commercial Housing Consumer] In the enforcement of monetary claims, if a general buyer other than a commercial housing consumer raises an objection to the immovable property registered under the name of the person subject to enforcement, requesting exclusion of enforcement, Article 28 of the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Handling of Objections to Enforcement and Review Cases by People's Courts provides that the following circumstances shall be supported according to law: (1) a legally valid written sales contract had been signed before the seizure by the People's Court; (2) the buyer had lawfully taken possession of the immovable property before the seizure by the People's Court; (3) the full price has been paid, or part of the price has been paid in accordance with the contract and the remaining price is delivered for enforcement as required by the People's Court; (4) the failure to complete the transfer registration is not due to the buyer's own reasons. When adjudicating cases of action for objection to enforcement, the People's Court may refer to and apply this Article.  
In practice, there is no divergence in understanding the first three conditions of this provision. As for the fourth condition, understanding varies. Generally, if the buyer has taken affirmative actions such as submitting registration documents to the housing registration authority or making a request to the seller to complete the transfer registration, it may be deemed that the condition is met. If the buyer has not taken such affirmative actions but has a reasonable objective reason for failing to complete the transfer registration, it may also be deemed to meet the condition.

### 十二、关于民刑交叉案件的程序处理

<a id="art-128"></a>第一百二十八条　【分别审理】同一当事人因不同事实分别发生民商事纠纷和涉嫌刑事犯罪，民商事案件与刑事案件应当分别审理，主要有下列情形：  
（1）主合同的债务人涉嫌刑事犯罪或者刑事裁判认定其构成犯罪，债权人请求担保人承担民事责任的；  
（2）行为人以法人、非法人组织或者他人名义订立合同的行为涉嫌刑事犯罪或者刑事裁判认定其构成犯罪，合同相对人请求该法人、非法人组织或者他人承担民事责任的；  
（3）法人或者非法人组织的法定代表人、负责人或者其他工作人员的职务行为涉嫌刑事犯罪或者刑事裁判认定其构成犯罪，受害人请求该法人或者非法人组织承担民事责任的；  
（4）侵权行为人涉嫌刑事犯罪或者刑事裁判认定其构成犯罪，被保险人、受益人或者其他赔偿权利人请求保险人支付保险金的；  
（5）受害人请求涉嫌刑事犯罪的行为人之外的其他主体承担民事责任的。  
审判实践中出现的问题是，在上述情形下，有的人民法院仍然以民商事案件涉嫌刑事犯罪为由不予受理，已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。对此，应予纠正。

**Article 128** : [Separate Adjudication] Where the same party is involved in civil or commercial disputes and suspected criminal offenses based on different facts, the civil or commercial case and the criminal case shall be adjudicated separately. The main circumstances are as follows:  
(1) the debtor under the main contract is suspected of a criminal offense or is determined by a criminal judgment to have committed a crime, and the creditor requests the guarantor to bear civil liability;  
(2) a person's act of entering into a contract in the name of a legal person, unincorporated organization, or another person is suspected of a criminal offense or is determined by a criminal judgment to have committed a crime, and the counterparty to the contract requests such legal person, unincorporated organization, or other person to bear civil liability;  
(3) the act of a legal representative, responsible person, or other staff member of a legal person or unincorporated organization in the course of their duties is suspected of a criminal offense or is determined by a criminal judgment to have committed a crime, and the victim requests such legal person or unincorporated organization to bear civil liability;  
(4) the tortfeasor is suspected of a criminal offense or is determined by a criminal judgment to have committed a crime, and the insured, beneficiary, or other compensation claimant requests the insurer to pay insurance proceeds;  
(5) the victim requests a party other than the person suspected of the criminal offense to bear civil liability.  
The issue arising in adjudication practice is that, in the above circumstances, some People's Courts still refuse to accept the civil or commercial case on the ground that it is suspected of a criminal offense, or if already accepted, they rule to dismiss the action. This shall be corrected.

<a id="art-129"></a>第一百二十九条　【涉众型经济犯罪与民商事案件的程序处理】2014年颁布实施的《最高人民法院最高人民检察院公安部关于办理非法集资刑事案件适用法律若干问题的意见》和2019年1月颁布实施的《最高人民法院最高人民检察院公安部关于办理非法集资刑事案件若干问题的意见》规定的涉嫌集资诈骗、非法吸收公众存款等涉众型经济犯罪，所涉人数众多、当事人分布地域广、标的额特别巨大、影响范围广，严重影响社会稳定，对于受害人就同一事实提起的以犯罪嫌疑人或者刑事被告人为被告的民事诉讼，人民法院应当裁定不予受理，并将有关材料移送侦查机关、检察机关或者正在审理该刑事案件的人民法院。受害人的民事权利保护应当通过刑事追赃、退赔的方式解决。正在审理民商事案件的人民法院发现有上述涉众型经济犯罪线索的，应当及时将犯罪线索和有关材料移送侦查机关。侦查机关作出立案决定前，人民法院应当中止审理；作出立案决定后，应当裁定驳回起诉；侦查机关未及时立案的，人民法院必要时可以将案件报请党委政法委协调处理。除上述情形人民法院不予受理外，要防止通过刑事手段干预民商事审判，搞地方保护，影响营商环境。  
当事人因租赁、买卖、金融借款等与上述涉众型经济犯罪无关的民事纠纷，请求上述主体承担民事责任的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 129** : [Procedural Handling of Mass Economic Crimes and Civil/Commercial Cases] For the mass economic crimes such as fund-raising fraud and illegal absorption of public deposits as stipulated in the Opinions of the Supreme People's Court, the Supreme People's Procuratorate, and the Ministry of Public Security on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in Handling Criminal Cases of Illegal Fund-raising (promulgated and implemented in 2014) and the Opinions of the Supreme People's Court, the Supreme People's Procuratorate, and the Ministry of Public Security on Several Issues concerning the Handling of Criminal Cases of Illegal Fund-raising (promulgated and implemented in January 2019), which involve a large number of persons, parties distributed over a wide geographical area, extremely large subject matter amounts, broad impact, and serious impact on social stability, the People's Court shall rule not to accept a civil action filed by the victim against the criminal suspect or criminal defendant based on the same facts, and shall transfer the relevant materials to the investigative organ, the procuratorial organ, or the People's Court that is trying the criminal case. The protection of the victim's civil rights shall be resolved through the recovery of stolen property and compensation in criminal proceedings. If a People's Court that is trying a civil or commercial case discovers clues of the above-mentioned mass economic crimes, it shall promptly transfer the criminal clues and relevant materials to the investigative organ. Before the investigative organ makes a decision to file a case, the People's Court shall suspend the adjudication; after the decision to file a case is made, the People's Court shall rule to dismiss the action; if the investigative organ fails to file a case in a timely manner, the People's Court may, when necessary, report the case to the Party's Political and Legal Committee for coordination and handling. Except for the circumstances where the People's Court does not accept the case as provided above, it shall be prevented from interfering with civil and commercial adjudication through criminal means, engaging in local protection, or affecting the business environment.  
If a party, due to civil disputes unrelated to the above-mentioned mass economic crimes, such as lease, sale, or financial loan, requests the above-mentioned entity to bear civil liability, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-130"></a>第一百三十条　【民刑交叉案件中民商事案件中止审理的条件】人民法院在审理民商事案件时，如果民商事案件必须以相关刑事案件的审理结果为依据，而刑事案件尚未审结的，应当根据《民事诉讼法》第150条第5项的规定裁定中止诉讼。待刑事案件审结后，再恢复民商事案件的审理。如果民商事案件不是必须以相关的刑事案件的审理结果为依据，则民商事案件应当继续审理。

**Article 130** : [Conditions for Suspension of Civil/Commercial Cases in Overlapping Civil and Criminal Cases] When a People's Court is adjudicating a civil or commercial case, if the civil or commercial case must be based on the outcome of the adjudication of a related criminal case, and the criminal case has not yet been concluded, the court shall, in accordance with Article 150(5) of the Civil Procedure Law, rule to suspend the action. After the criminal case is concluded, the adjudication of the civil or commercial case shall be resumed.
