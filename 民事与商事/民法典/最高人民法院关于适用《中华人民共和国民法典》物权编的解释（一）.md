# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》物权编的解释（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法典  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕24号  
**公布日期**：2020-12-29  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> 第1825次会议通过，自2021年1月1日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　因不动产物权的归属，以及作为不动产物权登记基础的买卖、赠与、抵押等产生争议，当事人提起民事诉讼的，应当依法受理。当事人已经在行政诉讼中申请一并解决上述民事争议，且人民法院一并审理的除外。

**Article 1** Where a dispute arises over the ownership of a real property right, or over the sale, gift, mortgage, etc. that serves as the basis for the registration of a real property right, and a party initiates a civil action, the action shall be accepted according to law, except where the party has already applied in an administrative action for a joint resolution of the aforesaid civil dispute and the People's Court hears it jointly.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人有证据证明不动产登记簿的记载与真实权利状态不符、其为该不动产物权的真实权利人，请求确认其享有物权的，应予支持。

**Article 2** Where a party has evidence proving that the entry in the real estate register is inconsistent with the true state of rights and that such party is the true holder of the real property right, and petitions for the confirmation of its real right, the petition shall be upheld.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　异议登记因[民法典第二百二十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-220)第二款规定的事由失效后，当事人提起民事诉讼，请求确认物权归属的，应当依法受理。异议登记失效不影响人民法院对案件的实体审理。

**Article 3** After a caveat registration becomes invalid due to the circumstances specified in the second paragraph of Article 220 of the Civil Code, if a party initiates a civil action petitioning for the confirmation of the ownership of a real right, the action shall be accepted according to law. The invalidity of the caveat registration shall not affect the substantive trial of the case by the People's Court.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　未经预告登记的权利人同意，转让不动产所有权等物权，或者设立建设用地使用权、居住权、地役权、抵押权等其他物权的，应当依照[民法典第二百二十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-221)第一款的规定，认定其不发生物权效力。

**Article 4** Where, without the consent of the holder of a priority notice registration, the ownership of a real property or other real rights are transferred, or other real rights such as construction land use right, habitation right, easement, or mortgage right are created, the act shall be determined to have no real right effect in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 221 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　预告登记的买卖不动产物权的协议被认定无效、被撤销，或者预告登记的权利人放弃债权的，应当认定为[民法典第二百二十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-221)第二款所称的“债权消灭”。

**Article 5** Where an agreement for the sale of a real property right that has been registered under a priority notice is determined to be void, or is revoked, or the holder of the priority notice registration relinquishes its claim, it shall be determined that the “obligation or claim is extinguished” as referred to in the second paragraph of Article 221 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　转让人转让船舶、航空器和机动车等所有权，受让人已经支付合理价款并取得占有，虽未经登记，但转让人的债权人主张其为[民法典第二百二十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-225)所称的“善意第三人”的，不予支持，法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 6** Where a transferor transfers the ownership of a vessel, aircraft, motor vehicle, or the like, and the transferee has paid a reasonable price and taken possession thereof, if, despite the lack of registration, a creditor of the transferor claims that it is a “bona fide third party” as referred to in Article 225 of the Civil Code, such claim shall not be upheld, unless otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民法院、仲裁机构在分割共有不动产或者动产等案件中作出并依法生效的改变原有物权关系的判决书、裁决书、调解书，以及人民法院在执行程序中作出的拍卖成交裁定书、变卖成交裁定书、以物抵债裁定书，应当认定为[民法典第二百二十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-229)所称导致物权设立、变更、转让或者消灭的人民法院、仲裁机构的法律文书。

**Article 7** A judgment, award, or consent judgment rendered and taking legal effect according to law in a case involving the partition of co-owned immovable or movable property, or the like, by a People's Court or an arbitration institution, which alters the original real right relationship, as well as a ruling for a successful auction, a ruling for a successful sale, or a ruling for transfer of property in satisfaction of a debt made by a People's Court during enforcement proceedings, shall be determined as the legal instrument of the People's Court or the arbitration institution that causes the creation, alteration, transfer, or extinguishment of a real right as referred to in Article 229 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　依据[民法典第二百二十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-229)至第二百三十一条规定享有物权，但尚未完成动产交付或者不动产登记的权利人，依据[民法典第二百三十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-235)至第二百三十八条的规定，请求保护其物权的，应予支持。

**Article 8** Where a holder of a real right, who enjoys the real right pursuant to Articles 229 to 231 of the Civil Code but has not yet completed the delivery of the movable property or the registration of the immovable property, petitions for the protection of its real right in accordance with Articles 235 to 238 of the Civil Code, the petition shall be upheld.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　共有份额的权利主体因继承、遗赠等原因发生变化时，其他按份共有人主张优先购买的，不予支持，但按份共有人之间另有约定的除外。

**Article 9** When the holder of a right to a co-owned share changes due to inheritance, legacy, or other reasons, if other co-owners by shares claim a right of first refusal, such claim shall not be upheld, unless otherwise agreed among the co-owners by shares.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　[民法典第三百零五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-305)所称的“同等条件”，应当综合共有份额的转让价格、价款履行方式及期限等因素确定。

**Article 10** The “equivalent conditions” as referred to in Article 305 of the Civil Code shall be determined by comprehensively considering factors such as the transfer price of the co-owned share, the method and term for payment of the price, and other relevant factors.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　优先购买权的行使期间，按份共有人之间有约定的，按照约定处理；没有约定或者约定不明的，按照下列情形确定：  
（一）转让人向其他按份共有人发出的包含同等条件内容的通知中载明行使期间的，以该期间为准；  
（二）通知中未载明行使期间，或者载明的期间短于通知送达之日起十五日的，为十五日；  
（三）转让人未通知的，为其他按份共有人知道或者应当知道最终确定的同等条件之日起十五日；  
（四）转让人未通知，且无法确定其他按份共有人知道或者应当知道最终确定的同等条件的，为共有份额权属转移之日起六个月。

**Article 11** The period for exercising the right of first refusal shall be governed by the agreement among the co-owners by shares if there is one; in the absence of an agreement or if the agreement is unclear, it shall be determined in accordance with the following circumstances:  
(1) If the period for exercise is specified in the notice containing the contents of the equivalent conditions sent by the transferor to the other co-owners by shares, such period shall prevail;  
(2) If the notice does not specify the period for exercise, or if the specified period is shorter than fifteen days from the date of service of the notice, the period shall be fifteen days;  
(3) If the transferor fails to give notice, the period shall be fifteen days from the date on which the other co-owners by shares know or should have known the final equivalent conditions;  
(4) If the transferor fails to give notice and it is impossible to determine when the other co-owners by shares knew or should have known the final equivalent conditions, the period shall be six months from the date of transfer of the right to the co-owned share.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　按份共有人向共有人之外的人转让其份额，其他按份共有人根据法律、司法解释规定，请求按照同等条件优先购买该共有份额的，应予支持。其他按份共有人的请求具有下列情形之一的，不予支持：  
（一）未在[本解释第十一条](#art-11)规定的期间内主张优先购买，或者虽主张优先购买，但提出减少转让价款、增加转让人负担等实质性变更要求；  
（二）以其优先购买权受到侵害为由，仅请求撤销共有份额转让合同或者认定该合同无效。

**Article 12** Where a co-owner by shares transfers its share to a person other than the co-owners, and another co-owner by shares petitions to purchase the co-owned share by right of first refusal under equivalent conditions in accordance with the provisions of laws and judicial interpretations, the petition shall be upheld. The petition of another co-owner by shares shall not be upheld in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The right of first refusal is not claimed within the period specified in Article 11 of this Interpretation, or although the right of first refusal is claimed, a substantive modification such as a reduction in the transfer price or an increase in the transferor's burden is proposed;  
(2) Only the revocation of the contract for the transfer of the co-owned share or the invalidity of such contract is claimed on the ground that the right of first refusal has been infringed.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　按份共有人之间转让共有份额，其他按份共有人主张依据[民法典第三百零五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-305)规定优先购买的，不予支持，但按份共有人之间另有约定的除外。

**Article 13** Where a co-owner by shares transfers a co-owned share to another co-owner by shares, and other co-owners by shares claim the right of first refusal in accordance with Article 305 of the Civil Code, such claim shall not be upheld, unless otherwise agreed among the co-owners by shares.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　受让人受让不动产或者动产时，不知道转让人无处分权，且无重大过失的，应当认定受让人为善意。  
真实权利人主张受让人不构成善意的，应当承担举证证明责任。

**Article 14** Where, at the time of acquiring immovable or movable property, the transferee did not know that the transferor had no right of disposition and was not grossly negligent, the transferee shall be determined to have acted in good faith.  
If the true holder of the right claims that the transferee did not act in good faith, the true holder shall bear the burden of adducing evidence and the burden of proof.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　具有下列情形之一的，应当认定不动产受让人知道转让人无处分权：  
（一）登记簿上存在有效的异议登记；  
（二）预告登记有效期内，未经预告登记的权利人同意；  
（三）登记簿上已经记载司法机关或者行政机关依法裁定、决定查封或者以其他形式限制不动产权利的有关事项；  
（四）受让人知道登记簿上记载的权利主体错误；  
（五）受让人知道他人已经依法享有不动产物权。  
真实权利人有证据证明不动产受让人应当知道转让人无处分权的，应当认定受让人具有重大过失。

**Article 15** In any of the following circumstances, the transferee of the immovable property shall be determined to have known that the transferor had no right of disposition:  
(1) There is a valid caveat registration on the register;  
(2) During the effective period of a priority notice registration, the transaction is conducted without the consent of the holder of the priority notice registration;  
(3) Matters concerning the sealing up, freezing, or other forms of restriction on the right to the immovable property by a judicial or administrative organ according to law are already recorded on the register;  
(4) The transferee knows that the holder of the right recorded on the register is incorrect;  
(5) The transferee knows that another person already enjoys the real right in the immovable property according to law. If the true holder has evidence proving that the transferee of the immovable property should have known that the transferor had no right of disposition, the transferee shall be determined to have been grossly negligent.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　受让人受让动产时，交易的对象、场所或者时机等不符合交易习惯的，应当认定受让人具有重大过失。

**Article 16** Where, at the time the transferee acquires the movable property, the subject, place, or timing of the transaction does not conform to trading practices, the transferee shall be determined to have been grossly negligent.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　[民法典第三百一十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-311)第一款第一项所称的“受让人受让该不动产或者动产时”，是指依法完成不动产物权转移登记或者动产交付之时。  
当事人以[民法典第二百二十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-226)规定的方式交付动产的，转让动产民事法律行为生效时为动产交付之时；当事人以[民法典第二百二十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-227)规定的方式交付动产的，转让人与受让人之间有关转让返还原物请求权的协议生效时为动产交付之时。  
法律对不动产、动产物权的设立另有规定的，应当按照法律规定的时间认定权利人是否为善意。

**Article 17** The phrase “at the time the transferee acquires the immovable or movable property” as referred to in subparagraph (1) of the first paragraph of Article 311 of the Civil Code means the time when the transfer of the real property right is registered or when the movable property is delivered according to law.  
If the parties deliver the movable property in the manner prescribed in Article 226 of the Civil Code, the time of delivery of the movable property shall be the time when the civil legal act for the transfer of the movable property takes effect; if the parties deliver the movable property in the manner prescribed in Article 227 of the Civil Code, the time of delivery of the movable property shall be the time when the agreement between the transferor and the transferee on the transfer of the right to claim the return of the original property takes effect.  
If there are other provisions of law on the creation of real rights in immovable or movable property, the time for determining whether the holder is in good faith shall be in accordance with the time prescribed by law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　[民法典第三百一十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-311)第一款第二项所称“合理的价格”，应当根据转让标的物的性质、数量以及付款方式等具体情况，参考转让时交易地市场价格以及交易习惯等因素综合认定。

**Article 18** The “reasonable price” as referred to in subparagraph (2) of the first paragraph of Article 311 of the Civil Code shall be comprehensively determined in accordance with the specific circumstances such as the nature and quantity of the subject matter of the transfer and the method of payment, with reference to factors such as the market price at the place of transaction at the time of transfer and trading practices.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　转让人将[民法典第二百二十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-225)规定的船舶、航空器和机动车等交付给受让人的，应当认定符合[民法典第三百一十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-311)第一款第三项规定的善意取得的条件。

**Article 19** Where the transferor delivers vessels, aircraft, motor vehicles, or the like as specified in Article 225 of the Civil Code to the transferee, it shall be determined that the conditions for bona fide acquisition as stipulated in subparagraph (3) of the first paragraph of Article 311 of the Civil Code are satisfied.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　具有下列情形之一，受让人主张依据[民法典第三百一十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-311)规定取得所有权的，不予支持：  
（一）转让合同被认定无效；  
（二）转让合同被撤销。

**Article 20** In any of the following circumstances, if the transferee claims to have acquired ownership in accordance with Article 311 of the Civil Code, such claim shall not be upheld:  
(1) The contract of transfer is determined to be void;  
(2) The contract of transfer is revoked.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本解释自2021年1月1日起施行。

**Article 21** This Interpretation shall come into force as of January 1, 2021.
