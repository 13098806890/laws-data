# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》物权编的解释（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法典  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕24号  
**公布日期**：2020-12-29  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> 第1825次会议通过，自2021年1月1日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　因不动产物权的归属，以及作为不动产物权登记基础的买卖、赠与、抵押等产生争议，当事人提起民事诉讼的，应当依法受理。当事人已经在行政诉讼中申请一并解决上述民事争议，且人民法院一并审理的除外。

**Article 1** Where a dispute arises over the ownership of a real right in immovable property or over the basis for registering the real right in immovable property, such as a sale, gift, or mortgage, and a party initiates a civil action, the action shall be accepted according to law, except where the party has already applied for the simultaneous resolution of the aforementioned civil dispute in an administrative action and the People's Court has heard the cases together.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人有证据证明不动产登记簿的记载与真实权利状态不符、其为该不动产物权的真实权利人，请求确认其享有物权的，应予支持。

**Article 2** Where a party has evidence proving that the entry in the real estate register is inconsistent with the true state of rights and that such party is the true right holder of the real right in immovable property, a request to confirm that such party enjoys the real right shall be sustained.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　异议登记因[民法典第二百二十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-220)第二款规定的事由失效后，当事人提起民事诉讼，请求确认物权归属的，应当依法受理。异议登记失效不影响人民法院对案件的实体审理。

**Article 3** After an objection registration ceases to have effect due to the circumstances specified in paragraph 2 of Article 220 of the Civil Code, if a party initiates a civil action to request confirmation of the ownership of a real right, the action shall be accepted according to law. The invalidity of the objection registration shall not affect the People's Court's substantive hearing of the case.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　未经预告登记的权利人同意，转让不动产所有权等物权，或者设立建设用地使用权、居住权、地役权、抵押权等其他物权的，应当依照[民法典第二百二十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-221)第一款的规定，认定其不发生物权效力。

**Article 4** Where, without the consent of the right holder of a advance notice registration, the ownership of immovable property or other real rights are transferred, or other real rights such as the right to use construction land, the right of habitation, the easement, or the mortgage are created, it shall be determined, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 221 of the Civil Code, that such transfer or creation does not have the effect of a real right.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　预告登记的买卖不动产物权的协议被认定无效、被撤销，或者预告登记的权利人放弃债权的，应当认定为[民法典第二百二十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-221)第二款所称的“债权消灭”。

**Article 5** Where an agreement for the sale and purchase of a real right in immovable property subject to advance notice registration is determined to be void or is revoked, or where the right holder of the advance notice registration waives the claim, it shall be determined to constitute an 'extinguishment of the claim' as referred to in paragraph 2 of Article 221 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　转让人转让船舶、航空器和机动车等所有权，受让人已经支付合理价款并取得占有，虽未经登记，但转让人的债权人主张其为[民法典第二百二十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-225)所称的“善意第三人”的，不予支持，法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 6** Where a transferor transfers the ownership of a vessel, aircraft, motor vehicle, or the like, and the transferee has paid a reasonable price and obtained possession, although without registration, if a creditor of the transferor claims that such creditor is a 'bona fide third party' as referred to in Article 225 of the Civil Code, such claim shall not be sustained, except as otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民法院、仲裁机构在分割共有不动产或者动产等案件中作出并依法生效的改变原有物权关系的判决书、裁决书、调解书，以及人民法院在执行程序中作出的拍卖成交裁定书、变卖成交裁定书、以物抵债裁定书，应当认定为[民法典第二百二十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-229)所称导致物权设立、变更、转让或者消灭的人民法院、仲裁机构的法律文书。

**Article 7** Judgments, awards, and mediation documents that change the original real right relationship, made by a People's Court or an arbitration institution in cases such as the partition of co-owned immovable or movable property and that have taken legal effect, as well as rulings on the conclusion of an auction sale, rulings on the conclusion of a sale by private treaty, and rulings on the transfer of property in satisfaction of a debt, made by a People's Court during enforcement proceedings, shall be determined to constitute the legal documents of a People's Court or an arbitration institution that lead to the creation, modification, transfer, or extinguishment of a real right as referred to in Article 229 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　依据[民法典第二百二十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-229)至第二百三十一条规定享有物权，但尚未完成动产交付或者不动产登记的权利人，依据[民法典第二百三十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-235)至第二百三十八条的规定，请求保护其物权的，应予支持。

**Article 8** Where a right holder enjoys a real right pursuant to Articles 229 through 231 of the Civil Code but has not yet completed the delivery of movable property or the registration of immovable property, a request by such right holder for protection of the real right pursuant to Articles 235 through 238 of the Civil Code shall be sustained.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　共有份额的权利主体因继承、遗赠等原因发生变化时，其他按份共有人主张优先购买的，不予支持，但按份共有人之间另有约定的除外。

**Article 9** Where the right holder of a share in co-owned property changes due to inheritance, bequest, or other reasons, if other co-owners by shares claim a preemptive right to purchase, such claim shall not be sustained, except where otherwise agreed among the co-owners by shares.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　[民法典第三百零五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-305)所称的“同等条件”，应当综合共有份额的转让价格、价款履行方式及期限等因素确定。

**Article 10** The 'same conditions' as referred to in Article 305 of the Civil Code shall be determined by comprehensively considering factors such as the transfer price of the co-owned share, the method and period for performance of the price, and other relevant factors.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　优先购买权的行使期间，按份共有人之间有约定的，按照约定处理；没有约定或者约定不明的，按照下列情形确定：  

**Article 11** The period for exercising the preemptive right to purchase shall be handled in accordance with any agreement reached among the co-owners by shares; in the absence of such an agreement or if the agreement is unclear, it shall be determined according to the following circumstances:
(1) If the notice sent by the transferor to other co-owners by shares containing the content of the same conditions specifies the exercise period, such period shall prevail;
(2) If the notice does not specify the exercise period, or the specified period is shorter than fifteen days from the date of service of the notice, the period shall be fifteen days;
(3) If the transferor fails to give notice, the period shall be fifteen days from the date on which the other co-owners by shares know or should have known the finally determined same conditions;
(4) If the transferor fails to give notice and it is impossible to determine the date on which the other co-owners by shares know or should have known the finally determined same conditions, the period shall be six months from the date of transfer of the ownership of the co-owned share.
（一）转让人向其他按份共有人发出的包含同等条件内容的通知中载明行使期间的，以该期间为准；  
（二）通知中未载明行使期间，或者载明的期间短于通知送达之日起十五日的，为十五日；  
（三）转让人未通知的，为其他按份共有人知道或者应当知道最终确定的同等条件之日起十五日；  
（四）转让人未通知，且无法确定其他按份共有人知道或者应当知道最终确定的同等条件的，为共有份额权属转移之日起六个月。

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　按份共有人向共有人之外的人转让其份额，其他按份共有人根据法律、司法解释规定，请求按照同等条件优先购买该共有份额的，应予支持。其他按份共有人的请求具有下列情形之一的，不予支持：  

**Article 12** Where a co-owner by shares transfers a share to a person other than the co-owners, and other co-owners by shares request, in accordance with the provisions of laws or judicial interpretations, to preemptively purchase the co-owned share on the same conditions, such request shall be sustained. The request of other co-owners by shares shall not be sustained in any of the following circumstances:
(1) They fail to claim the preemptive right within the period specified in Article 11 of this Interpretation, or although they claim the preemptive right, they propose substantive changes such as reducing the transfer price or increasing the transferor's burdens;
(2) They only request the revocation of the contract for the transfer of the co-owned share or a determination that the contract is void on the ground that their preemptive right has been infringed.
（一）未在[本解释第十一条](#art-11)规定的期间内主张优先购买，或者虽主张优先购买，但提出减少转让价款、增加转让人负担等实质性变更要求；  
（二）以其优先购买权受到侵害为由，仅请求撤销共有份额转让合同或者认定该合同无效。

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　按份共有人之间转让共有份额，其他按份共有人主张依据[民法典第三百零五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-305)规定优先购买的，不予支持，但按份共有人之间另有约定的除外。

**Article 13** Where a co-owner by shares transfers a co-owned share to another co-owner by shares, if other co-owners by shares claim a preemptive right to purchase pursuant to Article 305 of the Civil Code, such claim shall not be sustained, except where otherwise agreed among the co-owners by shares.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　受让人受让不动产或者动产时，不知道转让人无处分权，且无重大过失的，应当认定受让人为善意。  

**Article 14** Where, at the time of acquiring the immovable or movable property, the transferee neither knew that the transferor had no right to dispose of the property nor was grossly negligent, the transferee shall be determined to be bona fide.
The true right holder who claims that the transferee is not bona fide shall bear the burden of adducing evidence and proving such claim.
真实权利人主张受让人不构成善意的，应当承担举证证明责任。

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　具有下列情形之一的，应当认定不动产受让人知道转让人无处分权：  

**Article 15** In any of the following circumstances, the transferee of immovable property shall be determined to know that the transferor had no right to dispose of the property:
(1) A valid objection registration exists in the register;
(2) During the validity period of the advance notice registration, the consent of the right holder of the advance notice registration has not been obtained;
(3) Matters concerning the sealing up or other forms of restriction on the rights to immovable property that have been ruled or decided by a judicial organ or administrative organ according to law are already recorded in the register;
(4) The transferee knows that the right holder recorded in the register is erroneous;
(5) The transferee knows that another person already enjoys a real right in the immovable property according to law.
If the true right holder has evidence proving that the transferee of immovable property should have known that the transferor had no right to dispose of the property, the transferee shall be determined to have gross negligence.
（一）登记簿上存在有效的异议登记；  
（二）预告登记有效期内，未经预告登记的权利人同意；  
（三）登记簿上已经记载司法机关或者行政机关依法裁定、决定查封或者以其他形式限制不动产权利的有关事项；  
（四）受让人知道登记簿上记载的权利主体错误；  
（五）受让人知道他人已经依法享有不动产物权。  
真实权利人有证据证明不动产受让人应当知道转让人无处分权的，应当认定受让人具有重大过失。

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　受让人受让动产时，交易的对象、场所或者时机等不符合交易习惯的，应当认定受让人具有重大过失。

**Article 16** Where, at the time of acquiring the movable property, the subject matter, place, or timing of the transaction does not conform to trade practices, the transferee shall be determined to have gross negligence.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　[民法典第三百一十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-311)第一款第一项所称的“受让人受让该不动产或者动产时”，是指依法完成不动产物权转移登记或者动产交付之时。  

**Article 17** The phrase 'at the time when the transferee acquires the immovable or movable property' as referred to in subparagraph (1) of paragraph 1 of Article 311 of the Civil Code means the time when the registration of transfer of the real right in immovable property is completed according to law, or the time when the delivery of movable property is completed.
Where the parties deliver movable property in the manner prescribed in Article 226 of the Civil Code, the time when the civil juristic act for the transfer of the movable property takes effect shall be the time of delivery of the movable property; where the parties deliver movable property in the manner prescribed in Article 227 of the Civil Code, the time when the agreement between the transferor and the transferee on the transfer of the right to claim the return of the original property takes effect shall be the time of delivery of the movable property.
If the law provides otherwise for the creation of a real right in immovable or movable property, whether the right holder is bona fide shall be determined at the time specified by the legal provisions.
当事人以[民法典第二百二十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-226)规定的方式交付动产的，转让动产民事法律行为生效时为动产交付之时；当事人以[民法典第二百二十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-227)规定的方式交付动产的，转让人与受让人之间有关转让返还原物请求权的协议生效时为动产交付之时。  
法律对不动产、动产物权的设立另有规定的，应当按照法律规定的时间认定权利人是否为善意。

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　[民法典第三百一十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-311)第一款第二项所称“合理的价格”，应当根据转让标的物的性质、数量以及付款方式等具体情况，参考转让时交易地市场价格以及交易习惯等因素综合认定。

**Article 18** The 'reasonable price' as referred to in subparagraph (2) of paragraph 1 of Article 311 of the Civil Code shall be determined comprehensively based on the specific circumstances such as the nature, quantity, and payment method of the subject matter of the transfer, with reference to the market price at the place of transaction at the time of the transfer and trade practices, among other factors.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　转让人将[民法典第二百二十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-225)规定的船舶、航空器和机动车等交付给受让人的，应当认定符合[民法典第三百一十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-311)第一款第三项规定的善意取得的条件。

**Article 19** Where the transferor delivers a vessel, aircraft, motor vehicle, or the like as specified in Article 225 of the Civil Code to the transferee, it shall be determined that the conditions for bona fide acquisition as specified in subparagraph (3) of paragraph 1 of Article 311 of the Civil Code are satisfied.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　具有下列情形之一，受让人主张依据[民法典第三百一十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-311)规定取得所有权的，不予支持：  

**Article 20** In any of the following circumstances, a claim by the transferee to acquire ownership pursuant to Article 311 of the Civil Code shall not be sustained:
(1) The transfer contract is determined to be void;
(2) The transfer contract is revoked.
（一）转让合同被认定无效；  
（二）转让合同被撤销。

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本解释自2021年1月1日起施行。

**Article 21** This Interpretation shall come into force as of January 1, 2021.
