# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》侵权责任编的解释（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法典  
**发文字号**：法释〔2024〕12号  
**公布日期**：2024-09-25  
**生效日期**：2024-09-27

> （2023年12月18日最高人民法院审判委员会第1909次会议通过，自2024年9月27日起施行） 为正确审理侵权责任纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条　非法使被监护人脱离监护，监护人请求赔偿为恢复监护状态而支出的合理费用等财产损失的，人民法院应予支持。 第二条　非法使被监护人脱离监护，导致父母子女关系或者其他近亲属关系受到严重损害的，应当认定为民法典第一千一百八十三条第一款规定的严重精神损害。 第三条　非法使被监护人脱离监护，被监护人在脱离监护期间死亡，作为近亲属的监护人既请求赔偿人身损害，又请求赔偿监护关系受侵害产生的损失的，人民法院依法予以支持。 第四条　无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人造成他人损害，被侵权人请求监护人承担侵权责任，或者合并请求监护人和受托履行监护职责的人承担侵权责任的，人民法院应当将无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人列为共同被告。 第五条　无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人造成他人损害，被侵权人请求监护人承担侵权人应承担的全部责任的，人民法院应予支持，并在判决中明确，赔偿费用可以先从被监护人财产中支付，不足部分由监护人支付。 监护人抗辩主张承担补充责任，或者被侵权人、监护人主张人民法院判令有财产的无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人承担赔偿责任的，人民法院不予支持。 从被监护人财产中支付赔偿费用的，应当保留被监护人所必需的生活费和完成义务教育所必需的费用。 第六条　行为人在侵权行为发生时不满十八周岁，被诉时已满十八周岁的，被侵权人请求原监护人承担侵权人应承担的全部责任的，人民法院应予支持，并在判决中明确，赔偿费用可以先从被监护人财产中支付，不足部分由监护人支付。 前款规定情形，被侵权人仅起诉行为人的，人民法院应当向原告释明申请追加原监护人为共同被告。 第七条　未成年子女造成他人损害，被侵权人请求父母共同承担侵权责任的，人民法院依照民法典第二十七条第一款、第一千零六十八条以及第一千一百八十八条的规定予以支持。 第八条　夫妻离婚后，未成年子女造成他人损害，被侵权人请求离异夫妻共同承担侵权责任的，人民法院依照民法典第一千零六十八条、第一千零八十四条以及第一千一百八十八条的规定予以支持。一方以未与该子女共同生活为由主张不承担或者少承担责任的，人民法院不予支持。 离异夫妻之间的责任份额，可以由双方协议确定；协议不成的，人民法院可以根据双方履行监护职责的约定和实际履行情况等确定。实际承担责任超过自己责任份额的一方向另一方追偿的，人民法院应予支持。 第九条　未成年子女造成他人损害的，依照民法典第一千零七十二条第二款的规定，未与该子女形成抚养教育关系的继父或者继母不承担监护人的侵权责任，由该子女的生父母依照本解释第八条的规定承担侵权责任。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　非法使被监护人脱离监护，监护人请求赔偿为恢复监护状态而支出的合理费用等财产损失的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 1** Where a person illegally causes a ward to be removed from guardianship, and the guardian claims compensation for property losses such as reasonable expenses incurred to restore the guardianship status, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　非法使被监护人脱离监护，导致父母子女关系或者其他近亲属关系受到严重损害的，应当认定为[民法典第一千一百八十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1183)第一款规定的严重精神损害。

**Article 2** Where a person illegally causes a ward to be removed from guardianship, resulting in serious damage to the parent-child relationship or other close relative relationships, it shall be determined as serious mental damage as provided in Paragraph 1 of Article 1183 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　非法使被监护人脱离监护，被监护人在脱离监护期间死亡，作为近亲属的监护人既请求赔偿人身损害，又请求赔偿监护关系受侵害产生的损失的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 3** Where a person illegally causes a ward to be removed from guardianship, and the ward dies during such removal, if the guardian, as a close relative, claims compensation for both personal injury and losses arising from infringement of the guardianship relationship, the People's Court shall uphold such claims according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人造成他人损害，被侵权人请求监护人承担侵权责任，或者合并请求监护人和受托履行监护职责的人承担侵权责任的，人民法院应当将无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人列为共同被告。

**Article 4** Where a person with no capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct causes damage to another person, and the infringed party requests the guardian to bear tort liability, or jointly requests the guardian and the person entrusted with performing guardianship duties to bear tort liability, the People's Court shall list the person with no capacity for civil conduct or the person with limited capacity for civil conduct as a co-defendant.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人造成他人损害，被侵权人请求监护人承担侵权人应承担的全部责任的，人民法院应予支持，并在判决中明确，赔偿费用可以先从被监护人财产中支付，不足部分由监护人支付。  
监护人抗辩主张承担补充责任，或者被侵权人、监护人主张人民法院判令有财产的无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人承担赔偿责任的，人民法院不予支持。  
从被监护人财产中支付赔偿费用的，应当保留被监护人所必需的生活费和完成义务教育所必需的费用。

**Article 5** Where a person with no capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct causes damage to another person, and the infringed party requests the guardian to bear all the liabilities that the tortfeasor shall bear, the People's Court shall uphold such claim and specify in the judgment that the compensation expenses may be paid first from the ward's property, with the shortfall to be paid by the guardian.  
Where the guardian defends on the ground that it shall bear supplementary liability, or the infringed party or the guardian argues that the People's Court shall order the person with no capacity for civil conduct or the person with limited capacity for civil conduct who has property to bear the compensation liability, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim.  
Where compensation expenses are paid from the ward's property, the necessary living expenses and the necessary expenses for completing compulsory education shall be reserved.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　行为人在侵权行为发生时不满十八周岁，被诉时已满十八周岁的，被侵权人请求原监护人承担侵权人应承担的全部责任的，人民法院应予支持，并在判决中明确，赔偿费用可以先从被监护人财产中支付，不足部分由监护人支付。  
前款规定情形，被侵权人仅起诉行为人的，人民法院应当向原告释明申请追加原监护人为共同被告。

**Article 6** Where the actor was under the age of 18 at the time of the tortious act but has reached the age of 18 at the time of being sued, and the infringed party requests the former guardian to bear all the liabilities that the tortfeasor shall bear, the People's Court shall uphold such claim and specify in the judgment that the compensation expenses may be paid first from the ward's property, with the shortfall to be paid by the guardian.  
In the circumstance specified in the preceding paragraph, if the infringed party only sues the actor, the People's Court shall instruct the plaintiff to apply for adding the former guardian as a co-defendant.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　未成年子女造成他人损害，被侵权人请求父母共同承担侵权责任的，人民法院依照[民法典第二十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-27)第一款、第一千零六十八条以及第一千一百八十八条的规定予以支持。

**Article 7** Where a minor child causes damage to another person, and the infringed party requests the parents to jointly bear tort liability, the People's Court shall uphold such claim in accordance with Article 27(1), Article 1068, and Article 1188 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　夫妻离婚后，未成年子女造成他人损害，被侵权人请求离异夫妻共同承担侵权责任的，人民法院依照[民法典第一千零六十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1068)、第一千零八十四条以及第一千一百八十八条的规定予以支持。一方以未与该子女共同生活为由主张不承担或者少承担责任的，人民法院不予支持。  
离异夫妻之间的责任份额，可以由双方协议确定；协议不成的，人民法院可以根据双方履行监护职责的约定和实际履行情况等确定。实际承担责任超过自己责任份额的一方向另一方追偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 8** After a couple divorces, if the minor child causes damage to another person, and the infringed party requests the divorced parents to jointly bear tort liability, the People's Court shall uphold such claim in accordance with Article 1068, Article 1084, and Article 1188 of the Civil Code. Where one party argues that it shall not bear liability or shall bear reduced liability on the ground that it does not live with the child, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim.  
The share of liability between the divorced parents may be determined by agreement between the parties; if no agreement is reached, the People's Court may determine it based on the agreement on the performance of guardianship duties and the actual performance of such duties, among other factors. Where one party actually bears liability exceeding its own share and seeks recourse from the other party, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　未成年子女造成他人损害的，依照[民法典第一千零七十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1072)第二款的规定，未与该子女形成抚养教育关系的继父或者继母不承担监护人的侵权责任，由该子女的生父母依照[本解释第八条](#art-8)的规定承担侵权责任。

**Article 9** Where a minor child causes damage to another person, in accordance with Paragraph 2 of Article 1072 of the Civil Code, a stepfather or stepmother who has not formed a fosterage or education relationship with the child shall not bear the guardian's tort liability; the biological parents of the child shall bear tort liability in accordance with Article 8 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人造成他人损害，被侵权人合并请求监护人和受托履行监护职责的人承担侵权责任的，依照[民法典第一千一百八十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1189)的规定，监护人承担侵权人应承担的全部责任；受托人在过错范围内与监护人共同承担责任，但责任主体实际支付的赔偿费用总和不应超出被侵权人应受偿的损失数额。  
监护人承担责任后向受托人追偿的，人民法院可以参照[民法典第九百二十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-929)的规定处理。  
仅有一般过失的无偿受托人承担责任后向监护人追偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 10** Where a person with no capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct causes damage to another person, and the infringed party jointly requests the guardian and the person entrusted with performing guardianship duties to bear tort liability, in accordance with Article 1189 of the Civil Code, the guardian shall bear all the liabilities that the tortfeasor shall bear; the entrusted person shall jointly bear liability with the guardian within the scope of fault, provided that the total compensation expenses actually paid by the liable persons shall not exceed the amount of loss that the infringed party is entitled to receive.  
Where the guardian, after bearing liability, seeks recourse against the entrusted person, the People's Court may handle the matter by reference to Article 929 of the Civil Code.  
Where a gratuitous entrusted person who has only ordinary negligence seeks recourse against the guardian after bearing liability, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　教唆、帮助无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人实施侵权行为，教唆人、帮助人以其不知道且不应当知道行为人为无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人为由，主张不承担侵权责任或者与行为人的监护人承担连带责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 11** Where a person instigates or assists a person with no capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct to commit a tortious act, and the instigator or assistant argues that it does not bear tort liability or shall bear joint and several liability with the guardian of the actor on the ground that it did not know and should not have known that the actor was a person with no capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　教唆、帮助无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人实施侵权行为，被侵权人合并请求教唆人、帮助人以及监护人承担侵权责任的，依照[民法典第一千一百六十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1169)第二款的规定，教唆人、帮助人承担侵权人应承担的全部责任；监护人在未尽到监护职责的范围内与教唆人、帮助人共同承担责任，但责任主体实际支付的赔偿费用总和不应超出被侵权人应受偿的损失数额。  
监护人先行支付赔偿费用后，就超过自己相应责任的部分向教唆人、帮助人追偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 12** Where a person instigates or assists a person with no capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct to commit a tortious act, and the infringed party jointly requests the instigator, assistant, and guardian to bear tort liability, in accordance with Paragraph 2 of Article 1169 of the Civil Code, the instigator and assistant shall bear all the liabilities that the tortfeasor shall bear; the guardian shall jointly bear liability with the instigator and assistant to the extent that the guardian fails to perform guardianship duties, provided that the total compensation expenses actually paid by the liable persons shall not exceed the amount of loss that the infringed party is entitled to receive.  
Where the guardian pays compensation expenses in advance, and seeks recourse against the instigator or assistant for the portion exceeding its own corresponding liability, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　教唆、帮助无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人实施侵权行为，被侵权人合并请求教唆人、帮助人与监护人以及受托履行监护职责的人承担侵权责任的，依照[本解释第十条](#art-10)、第十二条的规定认定民事责任。

**Article 13** Where a person instigates or assists a person with no capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct to commit a tortious act, and the infringed party jointly requests the instigator, assistant, guardian, and the person entrusted with performing guardianship duties to bear tort liability, the civil liability shall be determined in accordance with Articles 10 and 12 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人在幼儿园、学校或者其他教育机构学习、生活期间，受到教育机构以外的第三人人身损害，第三人、教育机构作为共同被告且依法应承担侵权责任的，人民法院应当在判决中明确，教育机构在人民法院就第三人的财产依法强制执行后仍不能履行的范围内，承担与其过错相应的补充责任。  
被侵权人仅起诉教育机构的，人民法院应当向原告释明申请追加实施侵权行为的第三人为共同被告。  
第三人不确定的，未尽到管理职责的教育机构先行承担与其过错相应的责任；教育机构承担责任后向已经确定的第三人追偿的，人民法院依照[民法典第一千二百零一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1201)的规定予以支持。

**Article 14** Where a person with no capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct suffers personal injury from a third party outside the educational institution during the period of study or life in a kindergarten, school, or other educational institution, and the third party and the educational institution are co-defendants and shall bear tort liability according to law, the People's Court shall specify in the judgment that the educational institution shall bear supplementary liability corresponding to its fault within the scope that the third party's property still cannot satisfy the performance after compulsory enforcement by the People's Court according to law.  
Where the infringed party only sues the educational institution, the People's Court shall instruct the plaintiff to apply for adding the third party who committed the tort as a co-defendant.  
Where the third party is uncertain, the educational institution that fails to fulfill its management duties shall bear liability corresponding to its fault in advance; after bearing such liability, if the educational institution seeks recourse against the third party who has been determined, the People's Court shall uphold such claim in accordance with Article 1201 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　与用人单位形成劳动关系的工作人员、执行用人单位工作任务的其他人员，因执行工作任务造成他人损害，被侵权人依照[民法典第一千一百九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1191)第一款的规定，请求用人单位承担侵权责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
个体工商户的从业人员因执行工作任务造成他人损害的，适用[民法典第一千一百九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1191)第一款的规定认定民事责任。

**Article 15** Where a worker who has formed a labor relationship with an employer, or another person who performs work tasks of the employer, causes damage to another person while performing work tasks, and the infringed party requests the employer to bear tort liability in accordance with Paragraph 1 of Article 1191 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.  
Where an employee of an individually-owned business causes damage to another person while performing work tasks, the civil liability shall be determined by applying Paragraph 1 of Article 1191 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　劳务派遣期间，被派遣的工作人员因执行工作任务造成他人损害，被侵权人合并请求劳务派遣单位与接受劳务派遣的用工单位承担侵权责任的，依照[民法典第一千一百九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1191)第二款的规定，接受劳务派遣的用工单位承担侵权人应承担的全部责任；劳务派遣单位在不当选派工作人员、未依法履行培训义务等过错范围内，与接受劳务派遣的用工单位共同承担责任，但责任主体实际支付的赔偿费用总和不应超出被侵权人应受偿的损失数额。  
劳务派遣单位先行支付赔偿费用后，就超过自己相应责任的部分向接受劳务派遣的用工单位追偿的，人民法院应予支持，但双方另有约定的除外。

**Article 16** During the period of labor dispatch, where a dispatched worker causes damage to another person while performing work tasks, and the infringed party jointly requests the labor dispatch unit and the accepting entity to bear tort liability, in accordance with Paragraph 2 of Article 1191 of the Civil Code, the accepting entity shall bear all the liabilities that the tortfeasor shall bear; the labor dispatch unit shall jointly bear liability with the accepting entity within the scope of fault such as improper selection of workers or failure to perform training obligations according to law, provided that the total compensation expenses actually paid by the liable persons shall not exceed the amount of loss that the infringed party is entitled to receive.  
Where the labor dispatch unit pays compensation expenses in advance, and seeks recourse against the accepting entity for the portion exceeding its own corresponding liability, the People's Court shall uphold such claim, unless otherwise agreed by the parties.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　工作人员在执行工作任务中实施的违法行为造成他人损害，构成自然人犯罪的，工作人员承担刑事责任不影响用人单位依法承担民事责任。依照[民法典第一千一百九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1191)规定用人单位应当承担侵权责任的，在刑事案件中已完成的追缴、退赔可以在民事判决书中明确并扣减，也可以在执行程序中予以扣减。

**Article 17** Where the illegal act committed by a worker during the performance of work tasks causes damage to another person and constitutes a crime by a natural person, the criminal liability borne by the worker shall not affect the employer's bearing of civil liability according to law. Where the employer shall bear tort liability in accordance with Article 1191 of the Civil Code, the recovery and restitution already completed in the criminal case may be specified and deducted in the civil judgment, or may be deducted in the enforcement proceedings.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　承揽人在完成工作过程中造成第三人损害的，人民法院依照[民法典第一千一百六十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1165)的规定认定承揽人的民事责任。  
被侵权人合并请求定作人和承揽人承担侵权责任的，依照[民法典第一千一百六十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1165)、第一千一百九十三条的规定，造成损害的承揽人承担侵权人应承担的全部责任；定作人在定作、指示或者选任过错范围内与承揽人共同承担责任，但责任主体实际支付的赔偿费用总和不应超出被侵权人应受偿的损失数额。  
定作人先行支付赔偿费用后，就超过自己相应责任的部分向承揽人追偿的，人民法院应予支持，但双方另有约定的除外。

**Article 18** Where a contractor causes damage to a third party during the process of completing the work, the People's Court shall determine the civil liability of the contractor in accordance with Article 1165 of the Civil Code.  
Where the infringed party jointly requests the hiring party and the contractor to bear tort liability, in accordance with Articles 1165 and 1193 of the Civil Code, the contractor who caused the damage shall bear all the liabilities that the tortfeasor shall bear; the hiring party shall jointly bear liability with the contractor within the scope of fault in ordering, instructing, or selecting the contractor, provided that the total compensation expenses actually paid by the liable persons shall not exceed the amount of loss that the infringed party is entitled to receive.  
Where the hiring party pays compensation expenses in advance, and seeks recourse against the contractor for the portion exceeding its own corresponding liability, the People's Court shall uphold such claim, unless otherwise agreed by the parties.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　因产品存在缺陷造成买受人财产损害，买受人请求产品的生产者或者销售者赔偿缺陷产品本身损害以及其他财产损害的，人民法院依照[民法典第一千二百零二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1202)、第一千二百零三条的规定予以支持。

**Article 19** Where a defect in a product causes property damage to the buyer, and the buyer requests the producer or seller of the product to compensate for the damage to the defective product itself and other property damage, the People's Court shall uphold such claim in accordance with Articles 1202 and 1203 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　以买卖或者其他方式转让拼装或者已经达到报废标准的机动车，发生交通事故造成损害，转让人、受让人以其不知道且不应当知道该机动车系拼装或者已经达到报废标准为由，主张不承担侵权责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 20** Where a motor vehicle that is assembled or has reached the scrapping standard is transferred by sale or other means, and a traffic accident occurs causing damage, if the transferor or transferee argues that it shall not bear tort liability on the ground that it did not know and should not have known that the motor vehicle was assembled or had reached the scrapping standard, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　未依法投保强制保险的机动车发生交通事故造成损害，投保义务人和交通事故责任人不是同一人，被侵权人合并请求投保义务人和交通事故责任人承担侵权责任的，交通事故责任人承担侵权人应承担的全部责任；投保义务人在机动车强制保险责任限额范围内与交通事故责任人共同承担责任，但责任主体实际支付的赔偿费用总和不应超出被侵权人应受偿的损失数额。  
投保义务人先行支付赔偿费用后，就超出机动车强制保险责任限额范围部分向交通事故责任人追偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 21** Where a motor vehicle that has not purchased compulsory insurance according to law causes damage in a traffic accident, and the person obligated to insure is not the same person as the person responsible for the traffic accident, and the infringed party jointly requests the person obligated to insure and the person responsible for the traffic accident to bear tort liability, the person responsible for the traffic accident shall bear all the liability that the tortfeasor shall bear; the person obligated to insure shall jointly bear liability with the person responsible for the traffic accident within the limits of the liability coverage of the motor vehicle compulsory insurance, provided that the total amount of compensation actually paid by the liable parties shall not exceed the amount of loss that the infringed party is entitled to recover.  
After the person obligated to insure pays the compensation in advance and seeks recourse against the person responsible for the traffic accident for the portion exceeding the limits of the liability coverage of the motor vehicle compulsory insurance, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　机动车驾驶人离开本车后，因未采取制动措施等自身过错受到本车碰撞、碾压造成损害，机动车驾驶人请求承保本车机动车强制保险的保险人在强制保险责任限额范围内，以及承保本车机动车商业第三者责任保险的保险人按照保险合同的约定赔偿的，人民法院不予支持，但可以依据机动车车上人员责任保险的有关约定支持相应的赔偿请求。

**Article 22** Where a motor vehicle driver leaves the vehicle and, due to his own fault such as failure to apply the brakes, is hit or run over by the vehicle and sustains damage, and the motor vehicle driver requests the insurer underwriting the compulsory motor vehicle insurance for the vehicle to compensate within the limits of the liability coverage of the compulsory insurance, and the insurer underwriting the commercial third-party liability insurance for the vehicle to compensate in accordance with the insurance contract, the People's Court shall not support such request; however, it may support the corresponding claim for compensation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the motor vehicle occupant liability insurance.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　禁止饲养的烈性犬等危险动物造成他人损害，动物饲养人或者管理人主张不承担责任或者减轻责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 23** Where a ferocious dog or other dangerous animal the keeping of which is prohibited causes damage to another person, and the animal keeper or manager claims that it shall bear no liability or that the liability shall be mitigated, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　物业服务企业等建筑物管理人未采取必要的安全保障措施防止从建筑物中抛掷物品或者从建筑物上坠落的物品造成他人损害，具体侵权人、物业服务企业等建筑物管理人作为共同被告的，人民法院应当依照[民法典第一千一百九十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1198)第二款、第一千二百五十四条的规定，在判决中明确，未采取必要安全保障措施的物业服务企业等建筑物管理人在人民法院就具体侵权人的财产依法强制执行后仍不能履行的范围内，承担与其过错相应的补充责任。

**Article 24** Where a property service enterprise or other building manager fails to take necessary safety and security measures to prevent objects thrown from a building or objects falling from a building from causing damage to another person, and the specific tortfeasor and the property service enterprise or other building manager are named as co-defendants, the People's Court shall, in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 1198 and Article 1254 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, specify in the judgment that the property service enterprise or other building manager that fails to take necessary safety and security measures shall, to the extent that the property of the specific tortfeasor is still insufficient after compulsory execution by the People's Court according to law, bear supplementary liability corresponding to its fault.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　物业服务企业等建筑物管理人未采取必要的安全保障措施防止从建筑物中抛掷物品或者从建筑物上坠落的物品造成他人损害，经公安等机关调查，在民事案件一审法庭辩论终结前仍难以确定具体侵权人的，未采取必要安全保障措施的物业服务企业等建筑物管理人承担与其过错相应的责任。被侵权人其余部分的损害，由可能加害的建筑物使用人给予适当补偿。  
具体侵权人确定后，已经承担责任的物业服务企业等建筑物管理人、可能加害的建筑物使用人向具体侵权人追偿的，人民法院依照[民法典第一千一百九十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1198)第二款、第一千二百五十四条第一款的规定予以支持。

**Article 25** Where a property service enterprise or other building manager fails to take necessary safety and security measures to prevent objects thrown from a building or objects falling from a building from causing damage to another person, and after investigation by the public security and other authorities, it is still difficult to identify the specific tortfeasor before the conclusion of the court debate in the first instance of the civil case, the property service enterprise or other building manager that fails to take necessary safety and security measures shall bear liability corresponding to its fault. The remaining damage suffered by the infringed party shall be appropriately compensated by the building users who might have caused the damage.  
After the specific tortfeasor is identified, where the property service enterprise or other building manager that has already borne liability or the building users who might have caused the damage seek recourse against the specific tortfeasor, the People's Court shall support such claim in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 1198 and paragraph 1 of Article 1254 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本解释自2024年9月27日起施行。  
本解释施行后，人民法院尚未审结的一审、二审案件适用本解释。本解释施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的，适用当时的法律、司法解释规定。

**Article 26** This Interpretation shall come into force as of September 27, 2024.  
After this Interpretation comes into force, cases of first instance and second instance that have not yet been concluded by the People's Court shall be governed by this Interpretation. For cases that have been finally adjudicated before this Interpretation comes into force, if a party applies for a retrial or a retrial is decided in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, the provisions of the laws and judicial interpretations in force at that time shall apply.
