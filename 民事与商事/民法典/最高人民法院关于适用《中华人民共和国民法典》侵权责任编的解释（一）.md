# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》侵权责任编的解释（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法典  
**发文字号**：法释〔2024〕12号  
**公布日期**：2024-09-25  
**生效日期**：2024-09-27

> （2023年12月18日最高人民法院审判委员会第1909次会议通过，自2024年9月27日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　非法使被监护人脱离监护，监护人请求赔偿为恢复监护状态而支出的合理费用等财产损失的，人民法院应予支持。



**Article 1** Where a person unlawfully causes a ward to be separated from guardianship, and the guardian requests compensation for property losses such as reasonable expenses incurred to restore the guardianship status, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　非法使被监护人脱离监护，导致父母子女关系或者其他近亲属关系受到严重损害的，应当认定为[民法典第一千一百八十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1183)第一款规定的严重精神损害。



**Article 2** Where a person unlawfully causes a ward to be separated from guardianship, resulting in serious damage to the parent-child relationship or other close kinship relationship, it shall be determined as the serious mental damage as stipulated in Article 1183, paragraph 1 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　非法使被监护人脱离监护，被监护人在脱离监护期间死亡，作为近亲属的监护人既请求赔偿人身损害，又请求赔偿监护关系受侵害产生的损失的，人民法院依法予以支持。



**Article 3** Where a person unlawfully causes a ward to be separated from guardianship, and the ward dies during the period of separation, if the guardian, as a close relative, requests compensation for both personal injury and losses arising from the infringement of the guardianship relationship, the People's Court shall support such requests in accordance with law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人造成他人损害，被侵权人请求监护人承担侵权责任，或者合并请求监护人和受托履行监护职责的人承担侵权责任的，人民法院应当将无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人列为共同被告。



**Article 4** Where a person without capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct causes damage to another person, and the infringed party requests the guardian to assume tort liability, or jointly requests the guardian and the person entrusted with the performance of guardianship duties to assume tort liability, the People's Court shall list the person without capacity for civil conduct or the person with limited capacity for civil conduct as a co-defendant.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人造成他人损害，被侵权人请求监护人承担侵权人应承担的全部责任的，人民法院应予支持，并在判决中明确，赔偿费用可以先从被监护人财产中支付，不足部分由监护人支付。  



**Article 5** Where a person without capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct causes damage to another person, and the infringed party requests the guardian to assume all the liability that the tortfeasor shall bear, the People's Court shall support such request, and shall specify in the judgment that the compensation expenses may be paid first from the property of the ward, and the shortfall shall be paid by the guardian.  
Where the guardian defends by arguing for the assumption of supplementary liability, or the infringed party or the guardian requests the People's Court to order a person without capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct who has property to bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall not support such requests.  
Where compensation expenses are paid from the property of the ward, the necessary living expenses and expenses necessary for completing compulsory education of the ward shall be reserved.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　行为人在侵权行为发生时不满十八周岁，被诉时已满十八周岁的，被侵权人请求原监护人承担侵权人应承担的全部责任的，人民法院应予支持，并在判决中明确，赔偿费用可以先从被监护人财产中支付，不足部分由监护人支付。  



**Article 6** Where a person was under the age of 18 at the time of the commission of the tort but has reached the age of 18 at the time of being sued, and the infringed party requests the former guardian to assume all the liability that the tortfeasor shall bear, the People's Court shall support such request, and shall specify in the judgment that the compensation expenses may be paid first from the property of the ward, and the shortfall shall be paid by the guardian.  
In the circumstances set forth in the preceding paragraph, if the infringed party only sues the actor, the People's Court shall explain to the plaintiff the requirement to apply for adding the former guardian as a co-defendant.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　未成年子女造成他人损害，被侵权人请求父母共同承担侵权责任的，人民法院依照[民法典第二十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-27)第一款、第一千零六十八条以及第一千一百八十八条的规定予以支持。



**Article 7** Where a minor child causes damage to another person, and the infringed party requests both parents to jointly assume tort liability, the People's Court shall support such request in accordance with Article 27, paragraph 1, Article 1068, and Article 1188 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　夫妻离婚后，未成年子女造成他人损害，被侵权人请求离异夫妻共同承担侵权责任的，人民法院依照[民法典第一千零六十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1068)、第一千零八十四条以及第一千一百八十八条的规定予以支持。一方以未与该子女共同生活为由主张不承担或者少承担责任的，人民法院不予支持。  



**Article 8** Where, after the divorce of a husband and wife, a minor child causes damage to another person, and the infringed party requests the divorced couple to jointly assume tort liability, the People's Court shall support such request in accordance with Article 1068, Article 1084, and Article 1188 of the Civil Code. Where one party claims not to assume or to assume less liability on the ground that it does not live with the child, the People's Court shall not support such claim. The share of liability between the divorced couple may be determined by agreement between the parties; where no agreement is reached, the People's Court may determine the share based on the agreement between the parties on the performance of guardianship duties and the actual circumstances of performance, among others. Where a party that has actually assumed liability exceeding its own share of liability seeks recourse against the other party, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　未成年子女造成他人损害的，依照[民法典第一千零七十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1072)第二款的规定，未与该子女形成抚养教育关系的继父或者继母不承担监护人的侵权责任，由该子女的生父母依照[本解释第八条](#art-8)的规定承担侵权责任。



**Article 9** Where a minor child causes damage to another person, in accordance with Article 1072, paragraph 2 of the Civil Code, a stepfather or stepmother who has not formed a foster and educational relationship with the child shall not assume the guardian's tort liability, and the child's biological parents shall assume tort liability in accordance with Article 8 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人造成他人损害，被侵权人合并请求监护人和受托履行监护职责的人承担侵权责任的，依照[民法典第一千一百八十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1189)的规定，监护人承担侵权人应承担的全部责任；受托人在过错范围内与监护人共同承担责任，但责任主体实际支付的赔偿费用总和不应超出被侵权人应受偿的损失数额。  



**Article 10** Where a person without capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct causes damage to another person, and the infringed party jointly requests the guardian and the person entrusted with the performance of guardianship duties to assume tort liability, in accordance with Article 1189 of the Civil Code, the guardian shall assume all the liability that the tortfeasor shall bear; the trustee shall jointly bear liability with the guardian within the scope of its fault, provided that the total sum of compensation expenses actually paid by the liable persons shall not exceed the amount of loss recoverable by the infringed party.  
Where the guardian, after assuming liability, seeks recourse against the trustee, the People's Court may handle it by reference to Article 929 of the Civil Code.  
Where a gratuitous trustee who has only ordinary negligence, after assuming liability, seeks recourse against the guardian, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　教唆、帮助无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人实施侵权行为，教唆人、帮助人以其不知道且不应当知道行为人为无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人为由，主张不承担侵权责任或者与行为人的监护人承担连带责任的，人民法院不予支持。



**Article 11** Where a person who abets or aids a person without capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct in committing a tortious act, and such abettor or aider claims, on the grounds that they did not know and should not have known that the actor was a person without capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct, that they shall not bear tort liability or shall bear joint and several liability with the actor's guardian, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　教唆、帮助无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人实施侵权行为，被侵权人合并请求教唆人、帮助人以及监护人承担侵权责任的，依照[民法典第一千一百六十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1169)第二款的规定，教唆人、帮助人承担侵权人应承担的全部责任；监护人在未尽到监护职责的范围内与教唆人、帮助人共同承担责任，但责任主体实际支付的赔偿费用总和不应超出被侵权人应受偿的损失数额。  



**Article 12** Where a person who abets or aids a person without capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct in committing a tortious act, and the infringed party jointly requests the abettor, aider, and guardian to bear tort liability, in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 1169 of the Civil Code, the abettor and aider shall bear all liability that the tortfeasor shall bear; the guardian shall bear liability jointly with the abettor and aider to the extent that the guardian failed to fulfill their guardianship duties, provided that the total compensation actually paid by the liable parties shall not exceed the amount of loss the infringed party is entitled to recover.  
Where the guardian pays compensation in advance and then seeks recourse from the abettor or aider for the portion exceeding the guardian's own corresponding liability, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　教唆、帮助无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人实施侵权行为，被侵权人合并请求教唆人、帮助人与监护人以及受托履行监护职责的人承担侵权责任的，依照[本解释第十条](#art-10)、第十二条的规定认定民事责任。



**Article 13** Where a person who abets or aids a person without capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct in committing a tortious act, and the infringed party jointly requests the abettor, aider, guardian, and the person entrusted to perform guardianship duties to bear tort liability, the civil liability shall be determined in accordance with Articles 10 and 12 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人在幼儿园、学校或者其他教育机构学习、生活期间，受到教育机构以外的第三人人身损害，第三人、教育机构作为共同被告且依法应承担侵权责任的，人民法院应当在判决中明确，教育机构在人民法院就第三人的财产依法强制执行后仍不能履行的范围内，承担与其过错相应的补充责任。  



**Article 14** Where a person without capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct suffers personal injury from a third party other than the educational institution during the period of study or living in a kindergarten, school, or other educational institution, and the third party and the educational institution are named as co-defendants and shall bear tort liability according to law, the People's Court shall specify in the judgment that the educational institution shall bear supplementary liability corresponding to its fault, to the extent that enforcement against the third party's property according to law fails to fully satisfy the liability.  
Where the infringed party only sues the educational institution, the People's Court shall explain to the plaintiff and request them to apply to add the third party who committed the tortious act as a co-defendant.  
Where the third party is unidentified, the educational institution that failed to fulfill its management duties shall first bear liability corresponding to its fault; where the educational institution, after bearing liability, seeks recourse against the now-identified third party, the People's Court shall uphold such claim in accordance with Article 1201 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　与用人单位形成劳动关系的工作人员、执行用人单位工作任务的其他人员，因执行工作任务造成他人损害，被侵权人依照[民法典第一千一百九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1191)第一款的规定，请求用人单位承担侵权责任的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 15** Where a staff member who has formed an employment relationship with an employer, or any other person who performs work tasks for an employer, causes harm to another person while performing work tasks, and the infringed party requests the employer to bear tort liability in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 1191 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.  
Where an employee of an individual industrial or commercial household causes harm to another person while performing work tasks, the civil liability shall be determined by applying paragraph 1 of Article 1191 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　劳务派遣期间，被派遣的工作人员因执行工作任务造成他人损害，被侵权人合并请求劳务派遣单位与接受劳务派遣的用工单位承担侵权责任的，依照[民法典第一千一百九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1191)第二款的规定，接受劳务派遣的用工单位承担侵权人应承担的全部责任；劳务派遣单位在不当选派工作人员、未依法履行培训义务等过错范围内，与接受劳务派遣的用工单位共同承担责任，但责任主体实际支付的赔偿费用总和不应超出被侵权人应受偿的损失数额。  



**Article 16** Where, during a labor dispatch period, a dispatched worker causes harm to another person while performing work tasks, and the infringed party jointly requests the labor dispatch unit and the accepting entity that receives the dispatched worker to bear tort liability, in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 1191 of the Civil Code, the accepting entity that receives the dispatched worker shall bear all liability that the tortfeasor shall bear; the labor dispatch unit shall bear liability jointly with the accepting entity that receives the dispatched worker, to the extent of its fault such as improperly selecting the worker or failing to fulfill training obligations according to law, provided that the total compensation actually paid by the liable parties shall not exceed the amount of loss the infringed party is entitled to recover.  
Where the labor dispatch unit pays compensation in advance and then seeks recourse from the accepting entity that receives the dispatched worker for the portion exceeding its own corresponding liability, the People's Court shall uphold such claim, unless otherwise agreed by the parties.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　工作人员在执行工作任务中实施的违法行为造成他人损害，构成自然人犯罪的，工作人员承担刑事责任不影响用人单位依法承担民事责任。依照[民法典第一千一百九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1191)规定用人单位应当承担侵权责任的，在刑事案件中已完成的追缴、退赔可以在民事判决书中明确并扣减，也可以在执行程序中予以扣减。



**Article 17** Where an illegal act committed by a staff member during the performance of work tasks causes harm to another person and constitutes a crime committed by a natural person, the staff member's assumption of criminal liability shall not affect the employer's assumption of civil liability according to law. Where the employer shall bear tort liability in accordance with Article 1191 of the Civil Code, the recovery and restitution already completed in the criminal case may be specified and deducted in the civil judgment, or may be deducted during the enforcement proceedings.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　承揽人在完成工作过程中造成第三人损害的，人民法院依照[民法典第一千一百六十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1165)的规定认定承揽人的民事责任。  



**Article 18** Where a contractor causes harm to a third party during the process of completing work, the People's Court shall determine the contractor's civil liability in accordance with Article 1165 of the Civil Code.  
Where the infringed party jointly requests the hirer and the contractor to bear tort liability, in accordance with Articles 1165 and 1193 of the Civil Code, the contractor who caused the harm shall bear all liability that the tortfeasor shall bear; the hirer shall bear liability jointly with the contractor to the extent of its fault in placing the order, giving instructions, or selecting the contractor, provided that the total compensation actually paid by the liable parties shall not exceed the amount of loss the infringed party is entitled to recover.  
Where the hirer pays compensation in advance and then seeks recourse from the contractor for the portion exceeding its own corresponding liability, the People's Court shall uphold such claim, unless otherwise agreed by the parties.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　因产品存在缺陷造成买受人财产损害，买受人请求产品的生产者或者销售者赔偿缺陷产品本身损害以及其他财产损害的，人民法院依照[民法典第一千二百零二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1202)、第一千二百零三条的规定予以支持。



**Article 19** Where damage to the buyer's property is caused by a defect in a product, and the buyer requests the manufacturer or seller of the product to compensate for damages to the defective product itself and other property damage, the People's Court shall uphold such claim in accordance with Articles 1202 and 1203 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　以买卖或者其他方式转让拼装或者已经达到报废标准的机动车，发生交通事故造成损害，转让人、受让人以其不知道且不应当知道该机动车系拼装或者已经达到报废标准为由，主张不承担侵权责任的，人民法院不予支持。



**Article 20** Where a motor vehicle that is assembled or has reached the scrap standard is transferred by sale or other means, and a traffic accident occurs causing damage, the transferor and the transferee shall not be upheld by the People's Court if they claim, on the grounds that they did not know and should not have known that the motor vehicle was assembled or had reached the scrap standard, that they shall not bear tort liability.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　未依法投保强制保险的机动车发生交通事故造成损害，投保义务人和交通事故责任人不是同一人，被侵权人合并请求投保义务人和交通事故责任人承担侵权责任的，交通事故责任人承担侵权人应承担的全部责任；投保义务人在机动车强制保险责任限额范围内与交通事故责任人共同承担责任，但责任主体实际支付的赔偿费用总和不应超出被侵权人应受偿的损失数额。  



**Article 21** Where a motor vehicle not insured with compulsory insurance in accordance with law causes damage in a traffic accident, and the party obligated to insure and the person liable for the traffic accident are not the same person, if the infringed party jointly requests the party obligated to insure and the person liable for the traffic accident to bear tort liability, the person liable for the traffic accident shall bear all the liability that the tortfeasor shall bear; the party obligated to insure shall bear joint liability with the person liable for the traffic accident within the limit of liability of the motor vehicle compulsory insurance, provided that the total compensation actually paid by the liable persons shall not exceed the amount of losses that the infringed party is entitled to recover.  
Where the party obligated to insure pays the compensation in advance and then seeks recourse against the person liable for the traffic accident for the portion exceeding the limit of liability of the motor vehicle compulsory insurance, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　机动车驾驶人离开本车后，因未采取制动措施等自身过错受到本车碰撞、碾压造成损害，机动车驾驶人请求承保本车机动车强制保险的保险人在强制保险责任限额范围内，以及承保本车机动车商业第三者责任保险的保险人按照保险合同的约定赔偿的，人民法院不予支持，但可以依据机动车车上人员责任保险的有关约定支持相应的赔偿请求。



**Article 22** Where a motor vehicle driver, after leaving the vehicle, suffers damage from being struck or run over by the vehicle due to his or her own fault such as failing to take braking measures, and the driver requests compensation from the insurer underwriting the motor vehicle compulsory insurance within the limit of liability of the compulsory insurance, and from the insurer underwriting the commercial motor vehicle third-party liability insurance in accordance with the provisions of the insurance contract, the People's Court shall not support such request; however, it may support a corresponding claim for compensation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the motor vehicle occupant liability insurance.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　禁止饲养的烈性犬等危险动物造成他人损害，动物饲养人或者管理人主张不承担责任或者减轻责任的，人民法院不予支持。



**Article 23** Where a dangerous animal, such as a ferocious dog whose keeping is prohibited, causes damage to another person, and the animal keeper or manager claims exemption from liability or reduction of liability, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　物业服务企业等建筑物管理人未采取必要的安全保障措施防止从建筑物中抛掷物品或者从建筑物上坠落的物品造成他人损害，具体侵权人、物业服务企业等建筑物管理人作为共同被告的，人民法院应当依照[民法典第一千一百九十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1198)第二款、第一千二百五十四条的规定，在判决中明确，未采取必要安全保障措施的物业服务企业等建筑物管理人在人民法院就具体侵权人的财产依法强制执行后仍不能履行的范围内，承担与其过错相应的补充责任。



**Article 24** Where a property service enterprise or other building manager fails to take necessary security measures to prevent damage to another person caused by objects thrown from or falling from a building, and the specific tortfeasor and the property service enterprise or other building manager are named as co-defendants, the People's Court shall, in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 1198 and Article 1254 of the Civil Code, specify in the judgment that the property service enterprise or other building manager that fails to take necessary security measures shall bear supplementary liability corresponding to its fault, to the extent that the specific tortfeasor's property is still insufficient to satisfy the liability after compulsory enforcement by the People's Court in accordance with law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　物业服务企业等建筑物管理人未采取必要的安全保障措施防止从建筑物中抛掷物品或者从建筑物上坠落的物品造成他人损害，经公安等机关调查，在民事案件一审法庭辩论终结前仍难以确定具体侵权人的，未采取必要安全保障措施的物业服务企业等建筑物管理人承担与其过错相应的责任。被侵权人其余部分的损害，由可能加害的建筑物使用人给予适当补偿。  



**Article 25** Where a property service enterprise or other building manager fails to take necessary security measures to prevent damage to another person caused by objects thrown from or falling from a building, and after investigation by the public security or other authorities, the specific tortfeasor remains difficult to identify before the conclusion of the debate at the first trial of the civil case, the property service enterprise or other building manager that fails to take necessary security measures shall bear liability corresponding to its fault. For the remaining part of the damage to the infringed party, the possible injuring users of the building shall provide appropriate compensation. Where, after the specific tortfeasor is identified, the property service enterprise or other building manager or the possible injuring users of the building that have already borne liability seek recourse against the specific tortfeasor, the People's Court shall support such claim in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 1198 and paragraph 1 of Article 1254 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本解释自2024年9月27日起施行。  



**Article 26** This Interpretation shall come into force as of September 27, 2024. After this Interpretation comes into force, cases of first instance and second instance that have not yet been concluded by the People's Court shall be governed by this Interpretation. Where a case has been finally adjudicated before this Interpretation comes into force and a party applies for retrial or a retrial is decided in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, the laws and judicial interpretations in force at that time shall apply.
