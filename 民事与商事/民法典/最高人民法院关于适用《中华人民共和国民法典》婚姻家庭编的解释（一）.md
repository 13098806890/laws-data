# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》婚姻家庭编的解释（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法典  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕22号  
**公布日期**：2020-12-29  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> 第1825次会议通过，自2021年1月1日起施行） 为正确审理婚姻家庭纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等相关法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 一、一般规定 第一条　持续性、经常性的家庭暴力，可以认定为民法典第一千零四十二条、第一千零七十九条、第一千零九十一条所称的“虐待”。 第二条　民法典第一千零四十二条、第一千零七十九条、第一千零九十一条规定的“与他人同居”的情形，是指有配偶者与婚外异性，不以夫妻名义，持续、稳定地共同居住。 第三条　当事人提起诉讼仅请求解除同居关系的，人民法院不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。 当事人因同居期间财产分割或者子女抚养纠纷提起诉讼的，人民法院应当受理。 第四条　当事人仅以民法典第一千零四十三条为依据提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。 第五条　当事人请求返还按照习俗给付的彩礼的，如果查明属于以下情形，人民法院应当予以支持： （一）双方未办理结婚登记手续； （二）双方办理结婚登记手续但确未共同生活； （三）婚前给付并导致给付人生活困难。 适用前款第二项、第三项的规定，应当以双方离婚为条件。 二、结婚 第六条　男女双方依据民法典第一千零四十九条规定补办结婚登记的，婚姻关系的效力从双方均符合民法典所规定的结婚的实质要件时起算。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　持续性、经常性的家庭暴力，可以认定为[民法典第一千零四十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1042)、第一千零七十九条、第一千零九十一条所称的“虐待”。

**Article 1** Persistent and frequent domestic violence may be determined as 'maltreatment' as referred to in Articles 1042, 1079, and 1091 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　[民法典第一千零四十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1042)、第一千零七十九条、第一千零九十一条规定的“与他人同居”的情形，是指有配偶者与婚外异性，不以夫妻名义，持续、稳定地共同居住。

**Article 2** The circumstance of 'cohabitation with another person' as prescribed in Articles 1042, 1079, and 1091 of the Civil Code means a person having a spouse cohabits continuously and stably with a person of the opposite sex outside marriage, without holding themselves out as husband and wife.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　当事人提起诉讼仅请求解除同居关系的，人民法院不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。  
当事人因同居期间财产分割或者子女抚养纠纷提起诉讼的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 3** Where a party institutes an action merely requesting dissolution of a cohabitation relationship, the People's Court shall not accept the case; if it has already accepted the case, it shall rule to dismiss the action.  
Where a party institutes an action due to a dispute over property division or child support during the period of cohabitation, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　当事人仅以[民法典第一千零四十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1043)为依据提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 4** Where a party institutes an action solely on the basis of Article 1043 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall not accept the case; if it has already accepted the case, it shall rule to dismiss the action.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　当事人请求返还按照习俗给付的彩礼的，如果查明属于以下情形，人民法院应当予以支持：  
（一）双方未办理结婚登记手续；  
（二）双方办理结婚登记手续但确未共同生活；  
（三）婚前给付并导致给付人生活困难。  
适用前款第二项、第三项的规定，应当以双方离婚为条件。  
二、结婚

**Article 5** Where a party requests the return of betrothal gifts given in accordance with custom, if it is ascertained that any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall support the request:  
(1) the parties have not gone through the marriage registration procedures;  
(2) the parties have gone through the marriage registration procedures but have not actually lived together  
(3) the gifts were given before marriage and caused the giver to live in difficulty. The application of the provisions in subparagraphs (2) and (3) of the preceding paragraph shall be conditional upon the divorce of the parties. II. Marriage

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　男女双方依据[民法典第一千零四十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1049)规定补办结婚登记的，婚姻关系的效力从双方均符合民法典所规定的结婚的实质要件时起算。

**Article 6** Where a man and a woman retroactively go through the marriage registration in accordance with the provisions of Article 1049 of the Civil Code, the validity of the marital relationship shall be calculated from the time when both parties meet the substantive requirements for marriage as prescribed in the Civil Code.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　未依据[民法典第一千零四十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1049)规定办理结婚登记而以夫妻名义共同生活的男女，提起诉讼要求离婚的，应当区别对待：  
（一）1994年2月1日民政部《婚姻登记管理条例》公布实施以前，男女双方已经符合结婚实质要件的，按事实婚姻处理。  
（二）1994年2月1日民政部《婚姻登记管理条例》公布实施以后，男女双方符合结婚实质要件的，人民法院应当告知其补办结婚登记。未补办结婚登记的，依据[本解释第三条](#art-3)规定处理。

**Article 7** Where a man and a woman who have lived together as husband and wife without going through the marriage registration in accordance with the provisions of Article 1049 of the Civil Code institute an action requesting divorce, the matter shall be dealt with differently:  
(1) If, before the promulgation and implementation of the Regulations on Marriage Registration Administration of the Ministry of Civil Affairs on February 1, 1994, the man and woman have already met the substantive requirements for marriage, the case shall be handled as a de facto marriage.  
(2) If, after the promulgation and implementation of the Regulations on Marriage Registration Administration of the Ministry of Civil Affairs on February 1, 1994, the man and woman meet the substantive requirements for marriage, the People's Court shall inform them to retroactively go through the marriage registration. If they fail to do so, the case shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 3 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　未依据[民法典第一千零四十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1049)规定办理结婚登记而以夫妻名义共同生活的男女，一方死亡，另一方以配偶身份主张享有继承权的，依据[本解释第七条](#art-7)的原则处理。

**Article 8** Where a man and a woman who have lived together as husband and wife without going through the marriage registration in accordance with the provisions of Article 1049 of the Civil Code, and one party dies, the other party claims the right to inheritance as a spouse, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the principles of Article 7 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　有权依据[民法典第一千零五十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1051)规定向人民法院就已办理结婚登记的婚姻请求确认婚姻无效的主体，包括婚姻当事人及利害关系人。其中，利害关系人包括：  
（一）以重婚为由的，为当事人的近亲属及基层组织；  
（二）以未到法定婚龄为由的，为未到法定婚龄者的近亲属；  
（三）以有禁止结婚的亲属关系为由的，为当事人的近亲属。

**Article 9** The persons entitled to request the People's Court to confirm the invalidity of a marriage that has already been registered, in accordance with the provisions of Article 1051 of the Civil Code, include the parties to the marriage and interested persons. Among them, interested persons include:  
(1) in the case of bigamy, the close relatives of the parties and the grassroots organizations;  
(2) in the case of failure to reach the legal age for marriage, the close relatives of the person who has not reached the legal age for marriage; and (3) in the case of existence of a prohibited kinship relationship for marriage, the close relatives of the parties.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　当事人依据[民法典第一千零五十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1051)规定向人民法院请求确认婚姻无效，法定的无效婚姻情形在提起诉讼时已经消失的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 10** Where a party requests the People's Court to confirm the invalidity of a marriage in accordance with the provisions of Article 1051 of the Civil Code, and the statutory circumstance for the invalid marriage has ceased to exist at the time of instituting the action, the People's Court shall not support the request.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院受理请求确认婚姻无效案件后，原告申请撤诉的，不予准许。  
对婚姻效力的审理不适用调解，应当依法作出判决。  
涉及财产分割和子女抚养的，可以调解。调解达成协议的，另行制作调解书；未达成调解协议的，应当一并作出判决。

**Article 11** After the People's Court accepts a case requesting confirmation of invalidity of a marriage, if the plaintiff applies for withdrawal of the action, the court shall not permit it. Mediation shall not apply to the adjudication of the validity of the marriage; a judgment shall be made in accordance with law. Where property division and child support are involved, mediation may be conducted. If an agreement is reached through mediation, a separate mediation document shall be prepared; if no mediation agreement is reached, a judgment shall be made together with the decision on the validity of the marriage.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院受理离婚案件后，经审理确属无效婚姻的，应当将婚姻无效的情形告知当事人，并依法作出确认婚姻无效的判决。

**Article 12** After the People's Court accepts a divorce case, if upon trial it is confirmed to be an invalid marriage, the court shall inform the parties of the circumstance of the invalidity of the marriage and shall make a judgment confirming the invalidity of the marriage in accordance with law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民法院就同一婚姻关系分别受理了离婚和请求确认婚姻无效案件的，对于离婚案件的审理，应当待请求确认婚姻无效案件作出判决后进行。

**Article 13** Where the People's Court has separately accepted cases of divorce and a request for confirmation of invalidity of a marriage concerning the same marital relationship, the trial of the divorce case shall be conducted after a judgment is made in the case requesting confirmation of the invalidity of the marriage.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　夫妻一方或者双方死亡后，生存一方或者利害关系人依据[民法典第一千零五十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1051)的规定请求确认婚姻无效的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 14** After one or both spouses die, if the surviving spouse or an interested person requests confirmation of invalidity of the marriage in accordance with the provisions of Article 1051 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　利害关系人依据[民法典第一千零五十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1051)的规定，请求人民法院确认婚姻无效的，利害关系人为原告，婚姻关系当事人双方为被告。  
夫妻一方死亡的，生存一方为被告。

**Article 15** Where an interested person requests the People's Court to confirm the invalidity of a marriage in accordance with the provisions of Article 1051 of the Civil Code, the interested person shall be the plaintiff, and both parties to the marital relationship shall be the defendants.  
If one spouse has died, the surviving spouse shall be the defendant.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院审理重婚导致的无效婚姻案件时，涉及财产处理的，应当准许合法婚姻当事人作为有独立请求权的第三人参加诉讼。

**Article 16** When the People's Court tries a case of invalid marriage caused by bigamy involving property disposition, it shall permit the party to the legal marriage to participate in the proceedings as a third party with independent claims.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　当事人以[民法典第一千零五十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1051)规定的三种无效婚姻以外的情形请求确认婚姻无效的，人民法院应当判决驳回当事人的诉讼请求。  
当事人以结婚登记程序存在瑕疵为由提起民事诉讼，主张撤销结婚登记的，告知其可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 17** Where a party requests confirmation of invalidity of a marriage on grounds other than the three types of invalid marriage as prescribed in Article 1051 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall enter judgment to dismiss the party's claims.  
Where a party initiates a civil action on the ground of defects in the marriage registration procedures, claiming revocation of the marriage registration, the court shall inform the party that it may apply for administrative reconsideration or institute an administrative action in accordance with law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　行为人以给另一方当事人或者其近亲属的生命、身体、健康、名誉、财产等方面造成损害为要挟，迫使另一方当事人违背真实意愿结婚的，可以认定为[民法典第一千零五十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1052)所称的“胁迫”。  
因受胁迫而请求撤销婚姻的，只能是受胁迫一方的婚姻关系当事人本人。

**Article 18** Where an actor threatens to cause harm to the life, body, health, reputation, property, or other aspects of the other party or his/her close relatives, thereby compelling the other party to marry against his/her true will, this may be determined as 'duress' as referred to in Article 1052 of the Civil Code.  
Where a request for revocation of a marriage is made on the ground of duress, only the party to the marital relationship who is subjected to duress may make such a request.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　[民法典第一千零五十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1052)规定的“一年”，不适用诉讼时效中止、中断或者延长的规定。  
受胁迫或者被非法限制人身自由的当事人请求撤销婚姻的，不适用[民法典第一百五十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-152)第二款的规定。

**Article 19** The 'one year' prescribed in Article 1052 of the Civil Code shall not be subject to the provisions on suspension, interruption, or extension of the limitation of action.  
Where a party under duress or whose personal freedom is illegally restricted requests revocation of the marriage, the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 152 of the Civil Code shall not apply.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　[民法典第一千零五十四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1054)所规定的“自始没有法律约束力”，是指无效婚姻或者可撤销婚姻在依法被确认无效或者被撤销时，才确定该婚姻自始不受法律保护。

**Article 20** The provision in Article 1054 of the Civil Code that '[it] shall have no legally binding force from the very beginning' means that it is only when an invalid marriage or a voidable marriage is confirmed invalid or dissolved in accordance with law that it is determined that the marriage is not protected by law from the very beginning.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民法院根据当事人的请求，依法确认婚姻无效或者撤销婚姻的，应当收缴双方的结婚证书并将生效的判决书寄送当地婚姻登记管理机关。

**Article 21** Where a People's Court confirms the invalidity or revokes a marriage upon the request of a party according to law, it shall confiscate the marriage certificates of both parties and send the effective judgment to the local marriage registration authority.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　被确认无效或者被撤销的婚姻，当事人同居期间所得的财产，除有证据证明为当事人一方所有的以外，按共同共有处理。  
三、夫妻关系

**Article 22** For a marriage confirmed as invalid or revoked, the property acquired by the parties during their cohabitation shall be treated as property held in joint ownership, unless there is evidence proving that it is owned by one party alone.  
III. Spousal Relationship

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　夫以妻擅自中止妊娠侵犯其生育权为由请求损害赔偿的，人民法院不予支持；夫妻双方因是否生育发生纠纷，致使感情确已破裂，一方请求离婚的，人民法院经调解无效，应依照[民法典第一千零七十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1079)第三款第五项的规定处理。

**Article 23** Where a husband claims damages on the ground that the wife's unilateral termination of pregnancy infringes upon his reproductive right, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim; where a dispute between spouses over whether to have children leads to the complete breakdown of their mutual affection, and one party requests a divorce, the People's Court shall, if mediation fails, handle the matter in accordance with Item (5) of Paragraph 3 of Article 1079 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　[民法典第一千零六十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1062)第一款第三项规定的“知识产权的收益”，是指婚姻关系存续期间，实际取得或者已经明确可以取得的财产性收益。

**Article 24** "Income from intellectual property rights" as specified in Item (3) of Paragraph 1 of Article 1062 of the Civil Code refers to the property income actually obtained or clearly obtainable during the existence of the marital relationship.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　婚姻关系存续期间，下列财产属于[民法典第一千零六十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1062)规定的“其他应当归共同所有的财产”：  
（一）一方以个人财产投资取得的收益；  
（二）男女双方实际取得或者应当取得的住房补贴、住房公积金；  
（三）男女双方实际取得或者应当取得的基本养老金、破产安置补偿费。

**Article 25** During the existence of the marital relationship, the following property shall be deemed as "other property that shall be jointly owned" as specified in Article 1062 of the Civil Code:  
(1) income derived from investment of one party's personal property;  
(2) housing subsidies and housing provident funds actually obtained or should be obtained by either spouse;  
(3) basic pensions and bankruptcy resettlement compensation fees actually obtained or should be obtained by either spouse.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　夫妻一方个人财产在婚后产生的收益，除孳息和自然增值外，应认定为夫妻共同财产。

**Article 26** The income generated from one spouse's personal property after marriage shall be recognized as marital property, except for fruits and natural appreciation.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　由一方婚前承租、婚后用共同财产购买的房屋，登记在一方名下的，应当认定为夫妻共同财产。

**Article 27** A house that is rented by one party before marriage and purchased with marital property after marriage, with the title registered in the name of one party, shall be recognized as marital property.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　一方未经另一方同意出售夫妻共同所有的房屋，第三人善意购买、支付合理对价并已办理不动产登记，另一方主张追回该房屋的，人民法院不予支持。  
夫妻一方擅自处分共同所有的房屋造成另一方损失，离婚时另一方请求赔偿损失的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 28** Where one spouse sells a house jointly owned by the spouses without the consent of the other spouse, and a third party purchases it in good faith, pays a reasonable price, and has completed the real estate registration, the People's Court shall not uphold the other spouse's claim to recover the house.  
Where one spouse, without authorization, disposes of a jointly owned house, causing losses to the other spouse, and the other spouse claims compensation for the losses at the time of divorce, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　当事人结婚前，父母为双方购置房屋出资的，该出资应当认定为对自己子女个人的赠与，但父母明确表示赠与双方的除外。  
当事人结婚后，父母为双方购置房屋出资的，依照约定处理；没有约定或者约定不明确的，按照[民法典第一千零六十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1062)第一款第四项规定的原则处理。

**Article 29** Where, before the parties' marriage, the parents contribute capital for the purchase of a house for both parties, such contribution shall be recognized as a gift to their own child individually, unless the parents explicitly indicate that it is a gift to both parties.  
Where, after the parties' marriage, the parents contribute capital for the purchase of a house for both parties, the matter shall be handled according to the agreement; where there is no agreement or the agreement is unclear, it shall be handled in accordance with the principle specified in Item (4) of Paragraph 1 of Article 1062 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　军人的伤亡保险金、伤残补助金、医药生活补助费属于个人财产。

**Article 30** The death and injury insurance benefits, disability subsidies, and medical and living subsidies of a military serviceman shall be personal property.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　[民法典第一千零六十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1063)规定为夫妻一方的个人财产，不因婚姻关系的延续而转化为夫妻共同财产。但当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 31** Personal property of one spouse as specified in Article 1063 of the Civil Code shall not be converted into marital property due to the continuation of the marital relationship, unless the parties have agreed otherwise.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　婚前或者婚姻关系存续期间，当事人约定将一方所有的房产赠与另一方或者共有，赠与方在赠与房产变更登记之前撤销赠与，另一方请求判令继续履行的，人民法院可以按照[民法典第六百五十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-658)的规定处理。

**Article 32** Where, before marriage or during the existence of the marital relationship, the parties agree to donate a house owned by one party to the other party or to jointly own it, and the donor revokes the donation before the registration of the change of ownership of the donated house, and the other party requests a court order for continued performance, the People's Court may handle the matter in accordance with Article 658 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　债权人就一方婚前所负个人债务向债务人的配偶主张权利的，人民法院不予支持。但债权人能够证明所负债务用于婚后家庭共同生活的除外。

**Article 33** Where a creditor asserts a claim against the spouse of a debtor for a personal debt incurred by one party before marriage, the People's Court shall not uphold the claim, unless the creditor can prove that the debt was used for the common family life after marriage.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　夫妻一方与第三人串通，虚构债务，第三人主张该债务为夫妻共同债务的，人民法院不予支持。  
夫妻一方在从事赌博、吸毒等违法犯罪活动中所负债务，第三人主张该债务为夫妻共同债务的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 34** Where one spouse colludes with a third party to fabricate a debt, and the third party claims that the debt is a marital debt jointly owed by the spouses, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim.  
Where a debt is incurred by one spouse in engaging in illegal or criminal activities such as gambling or drug abuse, and the third party claims that the debt is a marital debt jointly owed by the spouses, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　当事人的离婚协议或者人民法院生效判决、裁定、调解书已经对夫妻财产分割问题作出处理的，债权人仍有权就夫妻共同债务向男女双方主张权利。  
一方就夫妻共同债务承担清偿责任后，主张由另一方按照离婚协议或者人民法院的法律文书承担相应债务的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 35** Where the parties' divorce agreement or the effective judgment, ruling, or mediation statement of the People's Court has already addressed the division of marital property, creditors shall still have the right to assert claims against either spouse regarding the marital debts jointly owed.  
Where one party, after bearing the liability for discharging marital debts, claims that the other party shall assume the corresponding debts in accordance with the divorce agreement or the People's Court's legal document, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　夫或者妻一方死亡的，生存一方应当对婚姻关系存续期间的夫妻共同债务承担清偿责任。

**Article 36** Where one spouse dies, the surviving spouse shall bear the liability for discharging the marital debts jointly incurred during the existence of the marital relationship.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　[民法典第一千零六十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1065)第三款所称“相对人知道该约定的”，夫妻一方对此负有举证责任。

**Article 37** With respect to the phrase "the counterparty knows of such agreement" as referred to in Paragraph 3 of Article 1065 of the Civil Code, one spouse shall bear the burden of adducing evidence thereof.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　婚姻关系存续期间，除[民法典第一千零六十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1066)规定情形以外，夫妻一方请求分割共同财产的，人民法院不予支持。  
四、父母子女关系

**Article 38** During the existence of the marital relationship, except for the circumstances specified in Article 1066 of the Civil Code, where one spouse requests the division of marital property, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.  
IV. Parent-Child Relationship

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　父或者母向人民法院起诉请求否认亲子关系，并已提供必要证据予以证明，另一方没有相反证据又拒绝做亲子鉴定的，人民法院可以认定否认亲子关系一方的主张成立。  
父或者母以及成年子女起诉请求确认亲子关系，并提供必要证据予以证明，另一方没有相反证据又拒绝做亲子鉴定的，人民法院可以认定确认亲子关系一方的主张成立。

**Article 39** Where a father or mother files a lawsuit with the People's Court to deny the parent-child relationship and has provided necessary evidence to prove it, and the other party has no contrary evidence and refuses to undergo a parentage test, the People's Court may uphold the claim of the party denying the parent-child relationship.  
Where a father, mother, or adult child files a lawsuit to confirm the parent-child relationship and provides necessary evidence to prove it, and the other party has no contrary evidence and refuses to undergo a parentage test, the People's Court may uphold the claim of the party seeking to confirm the parent-child relationship.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　婚姻关系存续期间，夫妻双方一致同意进行人工授精，所生子女应视为婚生子女，父母子女间的权利义务关系适用民法典的有关规定。

**Article 40** Where, during the existence of the marital relationship, both spouses jointly consent to artificial insemination, the child born shall be deemed a legitimate child, and the rights and obligations between the parents and the child shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　尚在校接受高中及其以下学历教育，或者丧失、部分丧失劳动能力等非因主观原因而无法维持正常生活的成年子女，可以认定为[民法典第一千零六十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1067)规定的“不能独立生活的成年子女”。

**Article 41** Adult children who are still receiving education at high school level or below, or who have lost or partially lost their ability to work, or who are unable to maintain a normal life due to other reasons beyond their subjective control, may be determined as 'adult children who are unable to live independently' as stipulated in Article 1067 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　[民法典第一千零六十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1067)所称“抚养费”，包括子女生活费、教育费、医疗费等费用。

**Article 42** The term 'child support' as used in Article 1067 of the Civil Code shall include expenses for the child's living expenses, education expenses, medical expenses, and other similar costs.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　婚姻关系存续期间，父母双方或者一方拒不履行抚养子女义务，未成年子女或者不能独立生活的成年子女请求支付抚养费的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 43** Where, during the duration of the marital relationship, both parents or one parent refuses to perform the obligation of child support, and a minor child or an adult child who is unable to live independently requests the payment of child support, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　离婚案件涉及未成年子女抚养的，对不满两周岁的子女，按照[民法典第一千零八十四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1084)第三款规定的原则处理。母亲有下列情形之一，父亲请求直接抚养的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）患有久治不愈的传染性疾病或者其他严重疾病，子女不宜与其共同生活；  
（二）有抚养条件不尽抚养义务，而父亲要求子女随其生活；  
（三）因其他原因，子女确不宜随母亲生活。

**Article 44** Where a divorce case involves the custody of a minor child, the handling of a child under the age of two shall follow the principles stipulated in paragraph 3 of Article 1084 of the Civil Code. If the mother falls under any of the following circumstances, and the father requests direct custody, the People's Court shall support such request:  
(1) The mother suffers from a chronic infectious disease or other serious illness that is difficult to cure, rendering it unsuitable for the child to live with her;  
(2) The mother has the conditions for child support but fails to fulfill the support obligation, and the father requests that the child live with him;  
(3) For other reasons, the child is indeed unsuitable to live with the mother.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　父母双方协议不满两周岁子女由父亲直接抚养，并对子女健康成长无不利影响的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 45** Where both parents agree that the father shall have direct custody of a child under the age of two, and such arrangement has no adverse effect on the healthy growth of the child, the People's Court shall support such agreement.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　对已满两周岁的未成年子女，父母均要求直接抚养，一方有下列情形之一的，可予优先考虑：  
（一）已做绝育手术或者因其他原因丧失生育能力；  
（二）子女随其生活时间较长，改变生活环境对子女健康成长明显不利；  
（三）无其他子女，而另一方有其他子女；  
（四）子女随其生活，对子女成长有利，而另一方患有久治不愈的传染性疾病或者其他严重疾病，或者有其他不利于子女身心健康的情形，不宜与子女共同生活。

**Article 46** Where both parents request direct custody of a minor child who has attained the age of two, priority may be given to one parent under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The parent has undergone sterilization or has lost the ability to procreate due to other reasons;  
(2) The child has lived with the parent for a relatively long period, and a change in the living environment would be clearly detrimental to the child's healthy growth;  
(3) The parent has no other children, while the other parent has other children;  
(4) Living with the parent is beneficial to the child's growth, but the other parent suffers from a chronic infectious disease or other serious illness that is difficult to cure, or has other conditions detrimental to the child's physical and mental health, rendering it unsuitable for the child to live with that other parent.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　父母抚养子女的条件基本相同，双方均要求直接抚养子女，但子女单独随祖父母或者外祖父母共同生活多年，且祖父母或者外祖父母要求并且有能力帮助子女照顾孙子女或者外孙子女的，可以作为父或者母直接抚养子女的优先条件予以考虑。

**Article 47** Where the conditions for child support of both parents are essentially the same, and both parents request direct custody, but the child has lived alone with the paternal or maternal grandparents for many years, and the grandparents request and have the ability to assist the parents in caring for the grandchild, this may be considered as a priority condition for the father or mother to be granted direct custody.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　在有利于保护子女利益的前提下，父母双方协议轮流直接抚养子女的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 48** Where, under the premise of being conducive to protecting the child's interests, both parents agree on a rotating direct custody arrangement, the People's Court shall support such agreement.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　抚养费的数额，可以根据子女的实际需要、父母双方的负担能力和当地的实际生活水平确定。  
有固定收入的，抚养费一般可以按其月总收入的百分之二十至三十的比例给付。负担两个以上子女抚养费的，比例可以适当提高，但一般不得超过月总收入的百分之五十。  
无固定收入的，抚养费的数额可以依据当年总收入或者同行业平均收入，参照上述比例确定。  
有特殊情况的，可以适当提高或者降低上述比例。

**Article 49** The amount of child support may be determined based on the actual needs of the child, the financial capacity of both parents, and the local actual living standard. For parents with a stable income, child support may generally be paid at a rate of 20% to 30% of their total monthly income. If the parent is responsible for supporting two or more children, the proportion may be appropriately increased, but shall generally not exceed 50% of the total monthly income. For parents without a stable income, the amount of child support may be determined by reference to the above proportions based on the total annual income or the average income of the same industry. In special circumstances, the above proportions may be appropriately increased or decreased.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　抚养费应当定期给付，有条件的可以一次性给付。

**Article 50** Child support shall be paid periodically. Where conditions permit, it may be paid in a lump sum.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　父母一方无经济收入或者下落不明的，可以用其财物折抵抚养费。

**Article 51** Where one parent has no economic income or his or her whereabouts are unknown, the parent's property may be used to offset child support obligations.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　父母双方可以协议由一方直接抚养子女并由直接抚养方负担子女全部抚养费。但是，直接抚养方的抚养能力明显不能保障子女所需费用，影响子女健康成长的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 52** Both parents may agree that one parent shall have direct custody and bear all child support expenses. However, if the custodial parent's financial capacity is clearly insufficient to cover the child's necessary expenses, thus affecting the child's healthy growth, the People's Court shall not support such agreement.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　抚养费的给付期限，一般至子女十八周岁为止。  
十六周岁以上不满十八周岁，以其劳动收入为主要生活来源，并能维持当地一般生活水平的，父母可以停止给付抚养费。

**Article 53** The period for payment of child support shall generally end when the child reaches the age of 18.  
For a child aged 16 or above but under 18, if the child's labor income is the main source of living and can maintain the local general living standard, the parents may cease payment of child support.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　生父与继母离婚或者生母与继父离婚时，对曾受其抚养教育的继子女，继父或者继母不同意继续抚养的，仍应由生父或者生母抚养。

**Article 54** Where a biological father and stepmother divorce, or a biological mother and stepfather divorce, if the stepfather or stepmother does not agree to continue supporting a stepchild whom they had previously supported and educated, the stepchild shall still be supported by the biological father or biological mother.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　离婚后，父母一方要求变更子女抚养关系的，或者子女要求增加抚养费的，应当另行提起诉讼。

**Article 55** After divorce, if one parent requests a change in the child custody arrangement, or if the child requests an increase in child support, a separate lawsuit shall be filed.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　具有下列情形之一，父母一方要求变更子女抚养关系的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）与子女共同生活的一方因患严重疾病或者因伤残无力继续抚养子女；  
（二）与子女共同生活的一方不尽抚养义务或有虐待子女行为，或者其与子女共同生活对子女身心健康确有不利影响；  
（三）已满八周岁的子女，愿随另一方生活，该方又有抚养能力；  
（四）有其他正当理由需要变更。

**Article 56** Where any of the following circumstances exists, and one parent requests a change in the child custody arrangement, the People's Court shall support such request:  
(1) The parent with whom the child lives is unable to continue supporting the child due to a serious illness or disability;  
(2) The parent with whom the child lives fails to fulfill the support obligation or abuses the child, or living with that parent has a definite adverse effect on the child's physical and mental health;  
(3) A child who has attained the age of eight wishes to live with the other parent, and that other parent has the ability to provide support;  
(4) There are other legitimate reasons requiring a change.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　父母双方协议变更子女抚养关系的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 57** Where both parents agree to change the child custody arrangement, the People's Court shall support such agreement.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　具有下列情形之一，子女要求有负担能力的父或者母增加抚养费的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）原定抚养费数额不足以维持当地实际生活水平；  
（二）因子女患病、上学，实际需要已超过原定数额；  
（三）有其他正当理由应当增加。

**Article 58** Where any of the following circumstances exists, and the child requests an increase in child support from the father or mother who has the financial capacity, the People's Court shall support such request:  
(1) The originally determined amount of child support is insufficient to maintain the local actual living standard;  
(2) Due to the child's illness or schooling, the actual needs have exceeded the originally determined amount;  
(3) There are other legitimate reasons for an increase.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　父母不得因子女变更姓氏而拒付子女抚养费。父或者母擅自将子女姓氏改为继母或继父姓氏而引起纠纷的，应当责令恢复原姓氏。

**Article 59** Parents shall not refuse to pay child support because the child has changed his or her surname. If a dispute arises because the father or mother has unilaterally changed the child's surname to that of the stepmother or stepfather, the original surname shall be ordered to be restored.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　在离婚诉讼期间，双方均拒绝抚养子女的，可以先行裁定暂由一方抚养。

**Article 60** During divorce proceedings, if both parents refuse to support the child, a ruling may be made in the first instance that one parent shall temporarily support the child.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　对拒不履行或者妨害他人履行生效判决、裁定、调解书中有关子女抚养义务的当事人或者其他人，人民法院可依照[民事诉讼法第一百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-111)的规定采取强制措施。  
五、离婚

**Article 61** Where a party or any other person refuses to perform, or obstructs others from performing, the obligations concerning child support specified in an effective judgment, ruling, or mediation statement, the People's Court may adopt compulsory measures in accordance with Article 111 of the Civil Procedure Law.  
V. Divorce

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　无民事行为能力人的配偶有[民法典第三十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-36)第一款规定行为,其他有监护资格的人可以要求撤销其监护资格，并依法指定新的监护人；变更后的监护人代理无民事行为能力一方提起离婚诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 62** Where the spouse of a person with no civil capacity for conduct commits any act prescribed in Paragraph 1 of Article 36 of the Civil Code, any other person with guardianship qualification may request a revocation of the guardianship qualification and, according to law, designate a new guardian; if the guardian after the change initiates a divorce action on behalf of the party with no civil capacity for conduct, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　人民法院审理离婚案件，符合[民法典第一千零七十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1079)第三款规定“应当准予离婚”情形的，不应当因当事人有过错而判决不准离婚。

**Article 63** When a People's Court hears a divorce case and the circumstances specified in Paragraph 3 of Article 1079 of the Civil Code as “shall grant the divorce” exist, the court shall not render a judgment dismissing the divorce on the grounds that a party is at fault.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　[民法典第一千零八十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1081)所称的“军人一方有重大过错”，可以依据[民法典第一千零七十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1079)第三款前三项规定及军人有其他重大过错导致夫妻感情破裂的情形予以判断。

**Article 64** The phrase “the military personnel party has committed a serious fault” as used in Article 1081 of the Civil Code may be determined by reference to the circumstances set forth in the first three items of Paragraph 3 of Article 1079 of the Civil Code and any other serious fault of the military personnel party that causes a breakdown of the marital relationship.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　人民法院作出的生效的离婚判决中未涉及探望权，当事人就探望权问题单独提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 65** If an effective divorce judgment rendered by the People's Court does not address the right of visitation, and a party separately files a lawsuit on the issue of visitation, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　当事人在履行生效判决、裁定或者调解书的过程中，一方请求中止探望的，人民法院在征询双方当事人意见后，认为需要中止探望的，依法作出裁定；中止探望的情形消失后，人民法院应当根据当事人的请求书面通知其恢复探望。

**Article 66** Where, in the course of performing an effective judgment, ruling, or mediation statement, a party requests the suspension of visitation, the People's Court, after consulting the opinions of both parties, shall render a ruling if it deems that such suspension is necessary; once the circumstances for suspension of visitation have ceased to exist, the People's Court shall, upon the request of a party, notify the party in writing to resume visitation.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　未成年子女、直接抚养子女的父或者母以及其他对未成年子女负担抚养、教育、保护义务的法定监护人，有权向人民法院提出中止探望的请求。

**Article 67** The minor child, the parent who directly supports the child, and any other legal guardian who bears the obligations of support, education, and protection of the minor child shall have the right to submit a request to the People's Court for the suspension of visitation.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　对于拒不协助另一方行使探望权的有关个人或者组织，可以由人民法院依法采取拘留、罚款等强制措施，但是不能对子女的人身、探望行为进行强制执行。

**Article 68** For any individual or organization that refuses to assist the other party in exercising the right of visitation, the People's Court may, according to law, adopt such compulsory measures as detention or a fine; however, no compulsory enforcement shall be carried out against the person of the child or the act of visitation per se.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　当事人达成的以协议离婚或者到人民法院调解离婚为条件的财产以及债务处理协议，如果双方离婚未成，一方在离婚诉讼中反悔的，人民法院应当认定该财产以及债务处理协议没有生效，并根据实际情况依照[民法典第一千零八十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1087)和第一千零八十九条的规定判决。  
当事人依照[民法典第一千零七十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1076)签订的离婚协议中关于财产以及债务处理的条款，对男女双方具有法律约束力。登记离婚后当事人因履行上述协议发生纠纷提起诉讼的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 69** Where the parties reach an agreement on the disposition of property and debts contingent upon a consensual divorce or a divorce mediated in the People's Court, if the divorce is not finalized and a party subsequently repudiates the agreement in the divorce action, the People's Court shall determine that the agreement on the disposition of property and debts has not taken effect, and shall render a judgment in accordance with Articles 1087 and 1089 of the Civil Code based on the actual circumstances. The provisions concerning the disposition of property and debts in the divorce agreement executed by the parties in accordance with Article 1076 of the Civil Code shall have legal binding force on both parties. Where, after the divorce is registered, a dispute arises between the parties over the performance of the aforesaid agreement and an action is initiated, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　夫妻双方协议离婚后就财产分割问题反悔，请求撤销财产分割协议的，人民法院应当受理。  
人民法院审理后，未发现订立财产分割协议时存在欺诈、胁迫等情形的，应当依法驳回当事人的诉讼请求。

**Article 70** Where, after a consensual divorce, both spouses repudiate the agreement on property division and request the revocation of the property division agreement, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
After trial, if the People's Court finds no circumstances such as fraud or duress at the time of concluding the property division agreement, it shall dismiss the party's claim according to law.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　人民法院审理离婚案件，涉及分割发放到军人名下的复员费、自主择业费等一次性费用的，以夫妻婚姻关系存续年限乘以年平均值，所得数额为夫妻共同财产。  
前款所称年平均值，是指将发放到军人名下的上述费用总额按具体年限均分得出的数额。其具体年限为人均寿命七十岁与军人入伍时实际年龄的差额。

**Article 71** Where a People's Court, in adjudicating a divorce case, involves the division of one-time payments such as demobilization fees and self-employment placement fees disbursed to a serviceman, the amount obtained by multiplying the number of years the marital relationship subsisted by the annual average shall be the community property of the husband and wife. The annual average mentioned in the preceding paragraph means the amount obtained by equally dividing the total amount of the aforesaid fees disbursed to the serviceman by the specific number of years. The specific number of years shall be the difference between the average life expectancy of seventy years old and the actual age of the serviceman at the time of enlistment.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　夫妻双方分割共同财产中的股票、债券、投资基金份额等有价证券以及未上市股份有限公司股份时，协商不成或者按市价分配有困难的，人民法院可以根据数量按比例分配。

**Article 72** Where the husband and wife, in dividing stocks, bonds, investment fund units, and other negotiable securities, as well as shares of an unlisted joint stock limited company, which are part of the community property, fail to reach an agreement through negotiation or encounter difficulties in distributing them according to the market price, the People's Court may distribute them on a pro rata basis according to quantity.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　人民法院审理离婚案件，涉及分割夫妻共同财产中以一方名义在有限责任公司的出资额，另一方不是该公司股东的，按以下情形分别处理：  
（一）夫妻双方协商一致将出资额部分或者全部转让给该股东的配偶，其他股东过半数同意，并且其他股东均明确表示放弃优先购买权的，该股东的配偶可以成为该公司股东；  
（二）夫妻双方就出资额转让份额和转让价格等事项协商一致后，其他股东半数以上不同意转让，但愿意以同等条件购买该出资额的，人民法院可以对转让出资所得财产进行分割。其他股东半数以上不同意转让，也不愿意以同等条件购买该出资额的，视为其同意转让，该股东的配偶可以成为该公司股东。  
用于证明前款规定的股东同意的证据，可以是股东会议材料，也可以是当事人通过其他合法途径取得的股东的书面声明材料。

**Article 73** Where a People's Court, in adjudicating a divorce case, involves the division of a capital contribution in a limited liability company registered in the name of one of the spouses that is part of the community property of the husband and wife, and the other spouse is not a shareholder of that company, the matter shall be handled according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the husband and wife reach an agreement to transfer part or all of the capital contribution to the spouse of the shareholder, with the consent of a majority of the other shareholders, and all other shareholders expressly waive their right of first refusal, the spouse of the shareholder may become a shareholder of the company;  
(2) If the husband and wife reach an agreement on matters such as the portion and price of the capital contribution to be transferred, but a majority of the other shareholders do not consent to the transfer but are willing to purchase the capital contribution at the same price, the People's Court may divide the proceeds from the transfer of the capital contribution. If a majority of the other shareholders do not consent to the transfer and are not willing to purchase it at the same price, they shall be deemed to have consented to the transfer, and the spouse of the shareholder may become a shareholder of the company. The evidence used to prove the consent of the shareholders as provided for in the preceding paragraph may be minutes of the shareholders' meeting or written statements of the shareholders obtained by the parties through other lawful means.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　人民法院审理离婚案件，涉及分割夫妻共同财产中以一方名义在合伙企业中的出资,另一方不是该企业合伙人的，当夫妻双方协商一致，将其合伙企业中的财产份额全部或者部分转让给对方时，按以下情形分别处理：  
（一）其他合伙人一致同意的，该配偶依法取得合伙人地位；  
（二）其他合伙人不同意转让，在同等条件下行使优先购买权的，可以对转让所得的财产进行分割；  
（三）其他合伙人不同意转让，也不行使优先购买权，但同意该合伙人退伙或者削减部分财产份额的，可以对结算后的财产进行分割；  
（四）其他合伙人既不同意转让，也不行使优先购买权，又不同意该合伙人退伙或者削减部分财产份额的，视为全体合伙人同意转让，该配偶依法取得合伙人地位。

**Article 74** Where a People's Court, in adjudicating a divorce case, involves the division of a capital contribution in a partnership enterprise registered in the name of one of the spouses that is part of the community property of the husband and wife, and the other spouse is not a partner of that enterprise, when the husband and wife reach an agreement to transfer all or part of the property interest in the partnership enterprise to the other, the matter shall be handled according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If all other partners consent, the said spouse shall acquire the status of a partner according to law;  
(2) If the other partners do not consent to the transfer but exercise their right of first refusal under the same conditions, the proceeds from the transfer may be divided;  
(3) If the other partners do not consent to the transfer and do not exercise their right of first refusal, but consent to the withdrawal of the partner or reduction of part of the property interest, the property after settlement may be divided;  
(4) If the other partners neither consent to the transfer nor exercise their right of first refusal, nor consent to the withdrawal of the partner or reduction of part of the property interest, they shall be deemed to have consented to the transfer, and the said spouse shall acquire the status of a partner according to law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　夫妻以一方名义投资设立个人独资企业的，人民法院分割夫妻在该个人独资企业中的共同财产时，应当按照以下情形分别处理：  
（一）一方主张经营该企业的，对企业资产进行评估后，由取得企业资产所有权一方给予另一方相应的补偿；  
（二）双方均主张经营该企业的，在双方竞价基础上，由取得企业资产所有权的一方给予另一方相应的补偿；  
（三）双方均不愿意经营该企业的，按照《中华人民共和国个人独资企业法》等有关规定办理。

**Article 75** Where the husband and wife invest in and establish a sole proprietorship enterprise in the name of one of them, when the People's Court divides the community property of the husband and wife in the said sole proprietorship enterprise, the matter shall be handled according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If one of the spouses claims to operate the enterprise, after an evaluation of the enterprise's assets, the spouse who acquires ownership of the enterprise's assets shall provide corresponding compensation to the other spouse;  
(2) If both of the spouses claim to operate the enterprise, on the basis of bidding between the two spouses, the spouse who acquires ownership of the enterprise's assets shall provide corresponding compensation to the other spouse;  
(3) If neither spouse is willing to operate the enterprise, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Sole Proprietorship Enterprise Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant regulations.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　双方对夫妻共同财产中的房屋价值及归属无法达成协议时，人民法院按以下情形分别处理：  
（一）双方均主张房屋所有权并且同意竞价取得的，应当准许；  
（二）一方主张房屋所有权的，由评估机构按市场价格对房屋作出评估，取得房屋所有权的一方应当给予另一方相应的补偿；  
（三）双方均不主张房屋所有权的，根据当事人的申请拍卖、变卖房屋，就所得价款进行分割。

**Article 76** Where both parties fail to reach an agreement on the value and attribution of a house that is part of the community property of the husband and wife, the People's Court shall handle the matter according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If both parties claim ownership of the house and agree to acquire it through bidding, this shall be permitted;  
(2) If one party claims ownership of the house, an appraisal institution shall evaluate the house at the market price, and the party acquiring ownership of the house shall provide corresponding compensation to the other party;  
(3) If neither party claims ownership of the house, the house shall be auctioned or sold off upon application by the parties, and the proceeds obtained shall be divided.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　离婚时双方对尚未取得所有权或者尚未取得完全所有权的房屋有争议且协商不成的，人民法院不宜判决房屋所有权的归属，应当根据实际情况判决由当事人使用。  
当事人就前款规定的房屋取得完全所有权后，有争议的，可以另行向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 77** Where at the time of divorce the parties have a dispute over a house for which ownership or full ownership has not yet been acquired and fail to reach an agreement through negotiation, the People's Court shall not rule on the attribution of ownership of the house but shall, based on the actual circumstances, rule on the use of the house by the parties.  
After the parties acquire full ownership of the house as described in the preceding paragraph, if a dispute arises, they may file a separate legal action with the People's Court.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　夫妻一方婚前签订不动产买卖合同，以个人财产支付首付款并在银行贷款，婚后用夫妻共同财产还贷，不动产登记于首付款支付方名下的，离婚时该不动产由双方协议处理。  
依前款规定不能达成协议的，人民法院可以判决该不动产归登记一方，尚未归还的贷款为不动产登记一方的个人债务。双方婚后共同还贷支付的款项及其相对应财产增值部分，离婚时应根据[民法典第一千零八十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1087)第一款规定的原则，由不动产登记一方对另一方进行补偿。

**Article 78** Where one of the spouses enters into a contract for the purchase of real property before marriage, pays the down payment with his or her own property and obtains a loan from a bank, the loan is repaid after marriage with the community property of the husband and wife, and the real property is registered in the name of the party who paid the down payment, at the time of divorce the said real property shall be dealt with by the two parties through agreement. If an agreement cannot be reached in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the People's Court may rule that the real property belongs to the party in whose name it is registered, and the outstanding loan shall be the personal debt of the party in whose name the real property is registered. The portion of the joint loan repayment made by the spouses after marriage and the corresponding increase in value of the property shall, at the time of divorce, be compensated by the party in whose name the real property is registered to the other party in accordance with the principle set out in Article 1087, paragraph 1 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　婚姻关系存续期间，双方用夫妻共同财产出资购买以一方父母名义参加房改的房屋，登记在一方父母名下，离婚时另一方主张按照夫妻共同财产对该房屋进行分割的，人民法院不予支持。购买该房屋时的出资，可以作为债权处理。

**Article 79** During the subsistence of the marital relationship, if both parties use the community property of the husband and wife to purchase a house that is part of a housing reform scheme in the name of one party's parents, and the house is registered in the name of that party's parents, and at the time of divorce the other party claims to have the house divided as community property of the husband and wife, the People's Court shall not support such a claim. The capital contribution made at the time of purchasing the house may be treated as a creditor's right.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　离婚时夫妻一方尚未退休、不符合领取基本养老金条件，另一方请求按照夫妻共同财产分割基本养老金的，人民法院不予支持；婚后以夫妻共同财产缴纳基本养老保险费，离婚时一方主张将养老金账户中婚姻关系存续期间个人实际缴纳部分及利息作为夫妻共同财产分割的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 80** Where at the time of divorce one of the spouses has not yet retired and does not meet the conditions for receiving basic pension, and the other spouse requests to have the basic pension divided as community property of the husband and wife, the People's Court shall not support such a request; if after marriage the basic pension insurance contributions are made with the community property of the husband and wife, and at the time of divorce one spouse claims to have the portion actually paid by the individual during the subsistence of the marital relationship and the interest thereon in the pension account divided as community property of the husband and wife, the People's Court shall support such a claim.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　婚姻关系存续期间，夫妻一方作为继承人依法可以继承的遗产，在继承人之间尚未实际分割，起诉离婚时另一方请求分割的，人民法院应当告知当事人在继承人之间实际分割遗产后另行起诉。

**Article 81** Where, during the existence of the marital relationship, one spouse, as an heir, is legally entitled to inherit an estate that has not yet been actually distributed among the heirs, and the other spouse requests partition of such estate when filing for divorce, the People's Court shall inform the parties to file a separate action after the estate has been actually distributed among the heirs.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　夫妻之间订立借款协议，以夫妻共同财产出借给一方从事个人经营活动或者用于其他个人事务的，应视为双方约定处分夫妻共同财产的行为，离婚时可以按照借款协议的约定处理。

**Article 82** Where a couple enters into a loan agreement to lend community property to one spouse for personal business operations or other personal affairs, such act shall be deemed as an agreement between the parties on the disposition of community property, and upon divorce, the matter may be handled in accordance with the terms of the loan agreement.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　离婚后，一方以尚有夫妻共同财产未处理为由向人民法院起诉请求分割的，经审查该财产确属离婚时未涉及的夫妻共同财产，人民法院应当依法予以分割。

**Article 83** After divorce, if one spouse files a lawsuit with the People's Court requesting partition on the grounds that there remains community property not yet disposed of, and upon examination, the property is confirmed to be community property not involved at the time of divorce, the People's Court shall partition it according to law.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　当事人依据[民法典第一千零九十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1092)的规定向人民法院提起诉讼，请求再次分割夫妻共同财产的诉讼时效期间为三年，从当事人发现之日起计算。

**Article 84** The statute of limitation for a party to file a lawsuit with the People's Court pursuant to Article 1092 of the Civil Code, requesting a further partition of community property, shall be three years, calculated from the date on which the party discovers such property.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　夫妻一方申请对配偶的个人财产或者夫妻共同财产采取保全措施的，人民法院可以在采取保全措施可能造成损失的范围内，根据实际情况，确定合理的财产担保数额。

**Article 85** Where one spouse applies for preservation measures against the personal property of the other spouse or community property, the People's Court may, within the scope of potential losses that may be caused by the preservation measures, determine a reasonable amount of property security based on the actual circumstances.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　[民法典第一千零九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1091)规定的“损害赔偿”，包括物质损害赔偿和精神损害赔偿。涉及精神损害赔偿的，适用《最高人民法院关于确定民事侵权精神损害赔偿责任若干问题的解释》的有关规定。

**Article 86** The term 'compensation for damages' as stipulated in Article 1091 of the Civil Code shall include compensation for material damages and compensation for moral damages. Where compensation for moral damages is involved, the relevant provisions of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Determination of Liability for Moral Damages in Civil Torts shall apply.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　承担[民法典第一千零九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1091)规定的损害赔偿责任的主体，为离婚诉讼当事人中无过错方的配偶。  
人民法院判决不准离婚的案件，对于当事人基于[民法典第一千零九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1091)提出的损害赔偿请求，不予支持。  
在婚姻关系存续期间，当事人不起诉离婚而单独依据[民法典第一千零九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1091)提起损害赔偿请求的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 87** The subject liable for compensation for damages as stipulated in Article 1091 of the Civil Code shall be the spouse of the non-fault party in the divorce action.  
In a case where the People's Court renders a judgment not granting divorce, any claim for compensation for damages raised by a party based on Article 1091 of the Civil Code shall not be upheld.  
Where, during the existence of the marital relationship, a party does not file for divorce but separately files a claim for compensation for damages under Article 1091 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　人民法院受理离婚案件时，应当将[民法典第一千零九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1091)等规定中当事人的有关权利义务，书面告知当事人。在适用[民法典第一千零九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1091)时，应当区分以下不同情况：  
（一）符合[民法典第一千零九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1091)规定的无过错方作为原告基于该条规定向人民法院提起损害赔偿请求的，必须在离婚诉讼的同时提出。  
（二）符合[民法典第一千零九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1091)规定的无过错方作为被告的离婚诉讼案件，如果被告不同意离婚也不基于该条规定提起损害赔偿请求的，可以就此单独提起诉讼。  
（三）无过错方作为被告的离婚诉讼案件，一审时被告未基于[民法典第一千零九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1091)规定提出损害赔偿请求，二审期间提出的，人民法院应当进行调解；调解不成的，告知当事人另行起诉。双方当事人同意由第二审人民法院一并审理的，第二审人民法院可以一并裁判。

**Article 88** When accepting a divorce case, the People's Court shall inform the parties in writing of their relevant rights and obligations under Article 1091 of the Civil Code and other provisions. When applying Article 1091 of the Civil Code, the following different circumstances shall be distinguished:  
(1) If the non-fault party meeting the conditions stipulated in Article 1091 of the Civil Code, as the plaintiff, files a claim for compensation for damages with the People's Court based on that Article, this must be raised simultaneously with the divorce action.  
(2) In a divorce action where the non-fault party meeting the conditions stipulated in Article 1091 of the Civil Code is the defendant, if the defendant neither agrees to the divorce nor files a claim for compensation for damages based on that Article, the defendant may file a separate action in this regard.  
(3) In a divorce action where the non-fault party is the defendant, if the defendant fails to file a claim for compensation for damages based on Article 1091 of the Civil Code during the first instance but raises it during the second instance, the People's Court shall conduct mediation; if mediation fails, the court shall inform the parties to file a separate action. If both parties agree to have the case heard together by the second instance People's Court, the second instance People's Court may adjudicate it jointly.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　当事人在婚姻登记机关办理离婚登记手续后，以[民法典第一千零九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1091)规定为由向人民法院提出损害赔偿请求的，人民法院应当受理。但当事人在协议离婚时已经明确表示放弃该项请求的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 89** After the parties have completed divorce registration at the marriage registration authority, if a party files a claim for compensation for damages with the People's Court based on Article 1091 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall accept it. However, if the party has expressly waived such claim at the time of the agreed divorce, the People's Court shall not uphold it.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　夫妻双方均有[民法典第一千零九十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1091)规定的过错情形，一方或者双方向对方提出离婚损害赔偿请求的，人民法院不予支持。  
六、附则

**Article 90** Where both spouses have the fault circumstances stipulated in Article 1091 of the Civil Code, and one or both parties file a claim for divorce compensation for damages against the other, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim.  
VI. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　本解释自2021年1月1日起施行。

**Article 91** This Interpretation shall come into force as of January 1, 2021.
