# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》继承编的解释（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法典  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕23号  
**公布日期**：2020-12-29  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> 第1825次会议通过，自2021年1月1日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　继承从被继承人生理死亡或者被宣告死亡时开始。  

**Article 1** Succession shall commence upon the physiological death of the deceased or upon the declaration of death of the deceased.
Where death is declared, the date of death determined in accordance with Article 48 of the Civil Code shall be the time when succession commences.
宣告死亡的，根据[民法典第四十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-48)规定确定的死亡日期，为继承开始的时间。

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　承包人死亡时尚未取得承包收益的，可以将死者生前对承包所投入的资金和所付出的劳动及其增值和孳息，由发包单位或者接续承包合同的人合理折价、补偿。其价额作为遗产。

**Article 2** Where a contractor dies before obtaining any income from the contracted project, the funds invested in the contracted project and the labor provided by the deceased during his or her lifetime, as well as the appreciation and fruits thereof, may be reasonably valued and compensated by the entity that issued the contract or the person who succeeds to the contract. The value thereof shall be treated as part of the estate.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　被继承人生前与他人订有遗赠扶养协议，同时又立有遗嘱的，继承开始后，如果遗赠扶养协议与遗嘱没有抵触，遗产分别按协议和遗嘱处理；如果有抵触，按协议处理，与协议抵触的遗嘱全部或者部分无效。

**Article 3** Where the deceased entered into a legacy-support agreement with another person during his or her lifetime and also made a will, after succession commences, if there is no conflict between the legacy-support agreement and the will, the estate shall be dealt with respectively under the agreement and the will; if there is a conflict, the matter shall be dealt with under the agreement, and that part of the will which conflicts with the agreement shall be wholly or partially void.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　遗嘱继承人依遗嘱取得遗产后，仍有权依照[民法典第一千一百三十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1130)的规定取得遗嘱未处分的遗产。

**Article 4** After a testamentary successor has acquired the estate under the will, he or she shall still have the right to acquire the estate not disposed of by the will, in accordance with Article 1,130 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在遗产继承中，继承人之间因是否丧失继承权发生纠纷，向人民法院提起诉讼的，由人民法院依据[民法典第一千一百二十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1125)的规定，判决确认其是否丧失继承权。

**Article 5** In an inheritance, where a dispute arises among successors as to whether a successor has lost the right of inheritance and an action is brought in a People's Court, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 1,125 of the Civil Code, determine by judgment whether the successor has lost the right of inheritance.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　继承人是否符合[民法典第一千一百二十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1125)第一款第三项规定的“虐待被继承人情节严重”，可以从实施虐待行为的时间、手段、后果和社会影响等方面认定。  

**Article 6** Whether a successor falls under the circumstance of "ill-treatment of the deceased under aggravated circumstances" as provided in Article 1,125, paragraph 1, subparagraph (3) of the Civil Code may be determined based on factors such as the duration, means, consequences, and social impact of the ill-treatment.
Where a successor has ill-treated the deceased under aggravated circumstances, regardless of whether criminal liability is pursued, the successor may be confirmed to have lost the right of inheritance.
虐待被继承人情节严重的，不论是否追究刑事责任，均可确认其丧失继承权。

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　继承人故意杀害被继承人的，不论是既遂还是未遂，均应当确认其丧失继承权。

**Article 7** Where a successor intentionally kills the deceased, whether the killing is accomplished or attempted, the successor shall be confirmed to have lost the right of inheritance.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　继承人有[民法典第一千一百二十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1125)第一款第一项或者第二项所列之行为，而被继承人以遗嘱将遗产指定由该继承人继承的，可以确认遗嘱无效，并确认该继承人丧失继承权。

**Article 8** Where a successor commits an act listed in Article 1,125, paragraph 1, subparagraph (1) or subparagraph (2) of the Civil Code, and the deceased designates in a will that the estate shall be inherited by that successor, the will may be confirmed void, and the successor may be confirmed to have lost the right of inheritance.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　继承人伪造、篡改、隐匿或者销毁遗嘱，侵害了缺乏劳动能力又无生活来源的继承人的利益，并造成其生活困难的，应当认定为[民法典第一千一百二十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1125)第一款第四项规定的“情节严重”。  

**Article 9** Where a successor forges, alters, conceals, or destroys a will, thereby infringing upon the interests of a successor who lacks the ability to work and has no source of income, and causing hardship in the life of that successor, this shall be deemed as the "aggravated circumstances" provided in Article 1,125, paragraph 1, subparagraph (4) of the Civil Code.
II. Statutory Succession
二、法定继承

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　被收养人对养父母尽了赡养义务，同时又对生父母扶养较多的，除可以依照[民法典第一千一百二十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1127)的规定继承养父母的遗产外，还可以依照[民法典第一千一百三十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1131)的规定分得生父母适当的遗产。

**Article 10** Where an adopted child has fulfilled the obligation of support for the adoptive parents and has also provided considerable support for the biological parents, in addition to inheriting the estate of the adoptive parents in accordance with Article 1,127 of the Civil Code, the adopted child may also receive an appropriate share of the estate of the biological parents in accordance with Article 1,131 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　继子女继承了继父母遗产的，不影响其继承生父母的遗产。  

**Article 11** Where a stepchild inherits the estate of stepparents, this shall not affect the stepchild's inheritance of the estate of his or her biological parents.
Where a stepparent inherits the estate of a stepchild, this shall not affect the stepparent's inheritance of the estate of his or her biological children.
继父母继承了继子女遗产的，不影响其继承生子女的遗产。

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　养子女与生子女之间、养子女与养子女之间，系养兄弟姐妹，可以互为第二顺序继承人。  

**Article 12** Between adopted children and biological children, and between adopted children, who are adoptive siblings, they may be heirs of the second order to each other.
The rights and obligations between an adopted child and his or her biological siblings shall be extinguished upon the establishment of the adoptive relationship, and they shall not be heirs of the second order to each other.
被收养人与其亲兄弟姐妹之间的权利义务关系，因收养关系的成立而消除，不能互为第二顺序继承人。

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　继兄弟姐妹之间的继承权，因继兄弟姐妹之间的扶养关系而发生。没有扶养关系的，不能互为第二顺序继承人。  

**Article 13** The right of inheritance between stepbrothers and stepsisters arises from the maintenance relationship between them. Where there is no maintenance relationship, they cannot be heirs of the second order to each other.
Where stepbrothers and stepsisters have mutually inherited each other's estate, this shall not affect their inheritance of the estate of their biological siblings.
继兄弟姐妹之间相互继承了遗产的，不影响其继承亲兄弟姐妹的遗产。

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　被继承人的孙子女、外孙子女、曾孙子女、外曾孙子女都可以代位继承，代位继承人不受辈数的限制。

**Article 14** Grandchildren, maternal grandchildren, great-grandchildren, and maternal great-grandchildren of the deceased may all inherit by subrogation. Subrogated successors are not subject to any limitation on the number of generations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　被继承人的养子女、已形成扶养关系的继子女的生子女可以代位继承；被继承人亲生子女的养子女可以代位继承；被继承人养子女的养子女可以代位继承；与被继承人已形成扶养关系的继子女的养子女也可以代位继承。

**Article 15** The biological children of the deceased's adopted children and of stepchildren with whom the deceased has formed a maintenance relationship may inherit by subrogation; the adopted children of the deceased's biological children may inherit by subrogation; the adopted children of the deceased's adopted children may inherit by subrogation; and the adopted children of stepchildren with whom the deceased has formed a maintenance relationship may also inherit by subrogation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　代位继承人缺乏劳动能力又没有生活来源，或者对被继承人尽过主要赡养义务的，分配遗产时，可以多分。

**Article 16** Where a subrogated successor lacks the ability to work and has no source of income, or has performed the primary obligation of support for the deceased, the subrogated successor may be given a larger share when the estate is distributed.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　继承人丧失继承权的，其晚辈直系血亲不得代位继承。如该代位继承人缺乏劳动能力又没有生活来源，或者对被继承人尽赡养义务较多的，可以适当分给遗产。

**Article 17** Where a successor has lost the right of inheritance, his or her lineal descendants of younger generations shall not inherit by subrogation. If such a subrogated successor lacks the ability to work and has no source of income, or has performed a relatively greater obligation of support for the deceased, an appropriate share of the estate may be allocated to that person.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　丧偶儿媳对公婆、丧偶女婿对岳父母，无论其是否再婚，依照[民法典第一千一百二十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1129)规定作为第一顺序继承人时，不影响其子女代位继承。

**Article 18** Where a widowed daughter-in-law serves as a first-order successor for her parents-in-law, or a widowed son-in-law serves as a first-order successor for his parents-in-law, in accordance with Article 1,129 of the Civil Code, regardless of whether they remarry, this shall not affect the subrogation inheritance by their children.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对被继承人生活提供了主要经济来源，或者在劳务等方面给予了主要扶助的，应当认定其尽了主要赡养义务或主要扶养义务。

**Article 19** Where a person has provided the primary source of financial support for the deceased's livelihood, or has provided primary assistance in terms of labor or other aspects, that person shall be deemed to have fulfilled the primary obligation of support or primary obligation of maintenance.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　依照[民法典第一千一百三十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1131)规定可以分给适当遗产的人，分给他们遗产时，按具体情况可以多于或者少于继承人。

**Article 20** Where a person may be allocated an appropriate share of the estate in accordance with Article 1,131 of the Civil Code, the share allocated to that person may, depending on the specific circumstances, be larger or smaller than the shares of the successors.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　依照[民法典第一千一百三十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1131)规定可以分给适当遗产的人，在其依法取得被继承人遗产的权利受到侵犯时，本人有权以独立的诉讼主体资格向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 21** A person who may be given an appropriate share of the estate in accordance with Article 1131 of the Civil Code shall, when his or her right to lawfully obtain the estate of the decedent is infringed upon, have the right to file a lawsuit with the People's Court as an independent litigation subject.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　继承人有扶养能力和扶养条件，愿意尽扶养义务，但被继承人因有固定收入和劳动能力，明确表示不要求其扶养的，分配遗产时，一般不应因此而影响其继承份额。

**Article 22** Where a successor has the capacity and conditions to provide support and is willing to fulfill the obligation of support, but the decedent, having a stable income and the ability to work, expressly indicates that he or she does not require such support, the successor's share of inheritance shall generally not be affected thereby when the estate is distributed.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　有扶养能力和扶养条件的继承人虽然与被继承人共同生活，但对需要扶养的被继承人不尽扶养义务，分配遗产时，可以少分或者不分。  

**Article 23** Where a successor who has the capacity and conditions to provide support lives together with the decedent but fails to fulfill the obligation of support toward the decedent who is in need of support, the successor may be given a reduced share or no share when the estate is distributed.
III. Testamentary Succession and Legacy
三、遗嘱继承和遗赠

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　继承人、受遗赠人的债权人、债务人，共同经营的合伙人，也应当视为与继承人、受遗赠人有利害关系，不能作为遗嘱的见证人。

**Article 24** Creditors, debtors, and co-operating partners of the successor or legatee shall also be deemed to have an interest in the successor or legatee and shall not act as witnesses to a will.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　遗嘱人未保留缺乏劳动能力又没有生活来源的继承人的遗产份额，遗产处理时，应当为该继承人留下必要的遗产，所剩余的部分，才可参照遗嘱确定的分配原则处理。  

**Article 25** If a testator fails to reserve a share of the estate for a successor who lacks the ability to work and has no source of income, when the estate is administered, the necessary portion of the estate shall be set aside for that successor, and the remaining portion may only be disposed of in accordance with the distribution principles specified in the will.
Whether a successor lacks the ability to work and has no source of income shall be determined based on the specific circumstances of that successor at the time the will takes effect.
继承人是否缺乏劳动能力又没有生活来源，应当按遗嘱生效时该继承人的具体情况确定。

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　遗嘱人以遗嘱处分了国家、集体或者他人财产的，应当认定该部分遗嘱无效。

**Article 26** If a testator disposes of property owned by the state, a collective, or any other person by will, that part of the will shall be deemed invalid.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　自然人在遗书中涉及死后个人财产处分的内容，确为死者的真实意思表示，有本人签名并注明了年、月、日，又无相反证据的，可以按自书遗嘱对待。

**Article 27** Where a natural person's testamentary letter contains provisions for the disposition of the deceased's personal property after death, which truly represent the deceased's genuine intention, bear the deceased's signature and are dated with the year, month, and day, and where there is no contrary evidence, it may be treated as a holographic will.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　遗嘱人立遗嘱时必须具有完全民事行为能力。无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人所立的遗嘱，即使其本人后来具有完全民事行为能力，仍属无效遗嘱。遗嘱人立遗嘱时具有完全民事行为能力，后来成为无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人的，不影响遗嘱的效力。

**Article 28** A testator must have full civil capacity at the time of making a will. A will made by a person without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity shall be invalid, even if such person later acquires full civil capacity. If a testator has full civil capacity at the time of making the will but later becomes a person without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity, the validity of the will shall not be affected.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　附义务的遗嘱继承或者遗赠，如义务能够履行，而继承人、受遗赠人无正当理由不履行，经受益人或者其他继承人请求，人民法院可以取消其接受附义务部分遗产的权利，由提出请求的继承人或者受益人负责按遗嘱人的意愿履行义务，接受遗产。  

**Article 29** In the case of testamentary succession or legacy subject to an obligation, if the obligation is capable of being performed but the successor or legatee fails to perform it without justifiable grounds, upon the request of the beneficiary or another successor, the People's Court may revoke the right of the successor or legatee to accept the portion of the estate subject to the obligation. The successor or beneficiary making the request shall be responsible for performing the obligation in accordance with the testator's wishes and accepting the estate.
IV. Administration of the Estate
四、遗产的处理

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　人民法院在审理继承案件时，如果知道有继承人而无法通知的，分割遗产时，要保留其应继承的遗产，并确定该遗产的保管人或者保管单位。

**Article 30** When a People's Court hears an inheritance case, if it knows of the existence of a successor but cannot notify that successor, the court shall reserve the successor's rightful share of the estate when distributing the estate and designate a custodian or custodial entity for that share.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　应当为胎儿保留的遗产份额没有保留的，应从继承人所继承的遗产中扣回。  

**Article 31** If the share of the estate that should have been reserved for a fetus is not reserved, it shall be deducted from the estate inherited by the successors.
If the fetus is born alive but subsequently dies, the share of the estate reserved for the fetus shall be inherited by the fetus's successors; if the fetus is stillborn, the share shall be inherited by the successors of the decedent.
为胎儿保留的遗产份额，如胎儿出生后死亡的，由其继承人继承；如胎儿娩出时是死体的，由被继承人的继承人继承。

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　继承人因放弃继承权，致其不能履行法定义务的，放弃继承权的行为无效。

**Article 32** If a successor's renunciation of the right of inheritance results in the successor's inability to perform a statutory obligation, such renunciation shall be void.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　继承人放弃继承应当以书面形式向遗产管理人或者其他继承人表示。

**Article 33** A successor's renunciation of inheritance shall be communicated in writing to the estate administrator or other successors.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　在诉讼中，继承人向人民法院以口头方式表示放弃继承的，要制作笔录，由放弃继承的人签名。

**Article 34** If, during litigation, a successor orally expresses to the People's Court the renunciation of inheritance, a record of such expression shall be made and signed by the person renouncing the inheritance.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　继承人放弃继承的意思表示，应当在继承开始后、遗产分割前作出。遗产分割后表示放弃的不再是继承权，而是所有权。

**Article 35** A successor's expression of intention to renounce inheritance shall be made after the succession has commenced and before the estate is distributed. A renunciation expressed after the estate has been distributed shall pertain not to the right of inheritance but to the right of ownership.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　遗产处理前或者在诉讼进行中，继承人对放弃继承反悔的，由人民法院根据其提出的具体理由，决定是否承认。遗产处理后，继承人对放弃继承反悔的，不予承认。

**Article 36** If a successor retracts a renunciation of inheritance before the estate is administered or during the pendency of litigation, the People's Court shall, based on the specific reasons provided, decide whether to uphold the retraction. If a successor retracts a renunciation of inheritance after the estate has been administered, the retraction shall not be upheld.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　放弃继承的效力，追溯到继承开始的时间。

**Article 37** The effect of a renunciation of inheritance shall be retroactive to the time when the succession commenced.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　继承开始后，受遗赠人表示接受遗赠，并于遗产分割前死亡的，其接受遗赠的权利转移给他的继承人。

**Article 38** If, after the succession has commenced, a legatee indicates acceptance of the legacy but dies before the estate is distributed, the right to accept the legacy shall be transferred to the legatee's successors.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　由国家或者集体组织供给生活费用的烈属和享受社会救济的自然人，其遗产仍应准许合法继承人继承。

**Article 39** For family members of martyrs whose living expenses are provided by the state or collective organizations, and for natural persons receiving social relief, their lawful successors shall still be permitted to inherit their estates.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　继承人以外的组织或者个人与自然人签订遗赠扶养协议后，无正当理由不履行，导致协议解除的，不能享有受遗赠的权利，其支付的供养费用一般不予补偿；遗赠人无正当理由不履行，导致协议解除的，则应当偿还继承人以外的组织或者个人已支付的供养费用。

**Article 40** Where an organization or individual other than a successor enters into a legacy-support agreement with a natural person, and the organization or individual fails to perform the agreement without justifiable grounds, thereby causing the agreement to be rescinded, the organization or individual shall not be entitled to the right to receive the legacy, and the support expenses paid by it or him/her shall generally not be compensated. Where the legator fails to perform the agreement without justifiable grounds, thereby causing the agreement to be rescinded, the legator shall repay the support expenses already paid by the organization or individual other than the successor.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　遗产因无人继承又无人受遗赠归国家或者集体所有制组织所有时，按照[民法典第一千一百三十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1131)规定可以分给适当遗产的人提出取得遗产的诉讼请求，人民法院应当视情况适当分给遗产。

**Article 41** Where an estate devolves to the state or a collective ownership organization due to the absence of any heir or legatee, if a person who may be given an appropriate share of the estate under Article 1131 of the Civil Code brings a claim for obtaining the estate, the People's Court shall, based on the circumstances, give an appropriate share of the estate to such person.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　人民法院在分割遗产中的房屋、生产资料和特定职业所需要的财产时，应当依据有利于发挥其使用效益和继承人的实际需要，兼顾各继承人的利益进行处理。

**Article 42** When dividing the estate consisting of houses, means of production, and property required for a specific occupation, the People's Court shall conduct the division in a manner that facilitates the utilization of such property's value and the actual needs of the heirs, while taking into account the interests of all heirs.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　人民法院对故意隐匿、侵吞或者争抢遗产的继承人，可以酌情减少其应继承的遗产。

**Article 43** The People's Court may, at its discretion, reduce the share of the estate that a heir shall inherit if such heir intentionally conceals, misappropriates, or scrambles for the estate.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　继承诉讼开始后，如继承人、受遗赠人中有既不愿参加诉讼，又不表示放弃实体权利的，应当追加为共同原告；继承人已书面表示放弃继承、受遗赠人在知道受遗赠后六十日内表示放弃受遗赠或者到期没有表示的，不再列为当事人。  

**Article 44** After the commencement of an inheritance action, if any heir or legatee neither wishes to participate in the action nor expresses a waiver of substantive rights, such person shall be added as a co-plaintiff; if a heir has made a written statement of waiver of inheritance, or if a legatee has expressed a waiver of legacy within 60 days from the date of learning of the legacy or has failed to make any expression upon expiration of the said period, such person shall no longer be listed as a party.

五、附则
