# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》总则编若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法典  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕6号  
**公布日期**：2022-02-24  
**生效日期**：2022-03-01

> （2021年12月30日最高人民法院审判委员会第1861次会议通过，自2022年3月1日起施行） 为正确审理民事案件，依法保护民事主体的合法权益，维护社会和经济秩序，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等相关法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 一、一般规定 第一条　民法典第二编至第七编对民事关系有规定的，人民法院直接适用该规定；民法典第二编至第七编没有规定的，适用民法典第一编的规定，但是根据其性质不能适用的除外。 就同一民事关系，其他民事法律的规定属于对民法典相应规定的细化的，应当适用该民事法律的规定。民法典规定适用其他法律的，适用该法律的规定。 民法典及其他法律对民事关系没有具体规定的，可以遵循民法典关于基本原则的规定。 第二条　在一定地域、行业范围内长期为一般人从事民事活动时普遍遵守的民间习俗、惯常做法等，可以认定为民法典第十条规定的习惯。 当事人主张适用习惯的，应当就习惯及其具体内容提供相应证据；必要时，人民法院可以依职权查明。 适用习惯，不得违背社会主义核心价值观，不得违背公序良俗。 第三条　对于民法典第一百三十二条所称的滥用民事权利，人民法院可以根据权利行使的对象、目的、时间、方式、造成当事人之间利益失衡的程度等因素作出认定。 行为人以损害国家利益、社会公共利益、他人合法权益为主要目的行使民事权利的，人民法院应当认定构成滥用民事权利。 构成滥用民事权利的，人民法院应当认定该滥用行为不发生相应的法律效力。滥用民事权利造成损害的，依照民法典第七编等有关规定处理。 二、民事权利能力和民事行为能力 第四条　涉及遗产继承、接受赠与等胎儿利益保护，父母在胎儿娩出前作为法定代理人主张相应权利的，人民法院依法予以支持。 第五条　限制民事行为能力人实施的民事法律行为是否与其年龄、智力、精神健康状况相适应，人民法院可以从行为与本人生活相关联的程度，本人的智力、精神健康状况能否理解其行为并预见相应的后果，以及标的、数量、价款或者报酬等方面认定。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　民法典第二编至第七编对民事关系有规定的，人民法院直接适用该规定；民法典第二编至第七编没有规定的，适用民法典第一编的规定，但是根据其性质不能适用的除外。  
就同一民事关系，其他民事法律的规定属于对民法典相应规定的细化的，应当适用该民事法律的规定。民法典规定适用其他法律的，适用该法律的规定。  
民法典及其他法律对民事关系没有具体规定的，可以遵循民法典关于基本原则的规定。

**Article 1** Where the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, in Books 2 to 7, provides for a civil relation, the People's Court shall directly apply such provision; where Books 2 to 7 of the Civil Code have no provision, the provisions of Book 1 of the Civil Code shall apply, except where their nature precludes their application.  
For the same civil relation, if the provisions of other civil laws are refinements of the corresponding provisions of the Civil Code, the provisions of such civil laws shall apply. Where the Civil Code provides for the application of other laws, the provisions of such laws shall apply.  
Where the Civil Code and other laws have no specific provisions for a civil relation, the provisions on basic principles of the Civil Code may be followed.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在一定地域、行业范围内长期为一般人从事民事活动时普遍遵守的民间习俗、惯常做法等，可以认定为[民法典第十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-10)规定的习惯。  
当事人主张适用习惯的，应当就习惯及其具体内容提供相应证据；必要时，人民法院可以依职权查明。  
适用习惯，不得违背社会主义核心价值观，不得违背公序良俗。

**Article 2** Folk customs, conventional practices, etc., that have long been generally observed by ordinary persons when engaging in civil activities within a certain region or trade, may be recognized as customs under Article 10 of the Civil Code.  
Where a party claims the application of a custom, the party shall provide evidence regarding the custom and its specific content; if necessary, the People's Court may ex officio ascertain the custom.  
The application of customs shall not contravene the core socialist values or public order and good customs.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　对于[民法典第一百三十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-132)所称的滥用民事权利，人民法院可以根据权利行使的对象、目的、时间、方式、造成当事人之间利益失衡的程度等因素作出认定。  
行为人以损害国家利益、社会公共利益、他人合法权益为主要目的行使民事权利的，人民法院应当认定构成滥用民事权利。  
构成滥用民事权利的，人民法院应当认定该滥用行为不发生相应的法律效力。滥用民事权利造成损害的，依照民法典第七编等有关规定处理。  
二、民事权利能力和民事行为能力

**Article 3** With respect to abuse of civil rights as mentioned in Article 132 of the Civil Code, the People's Court may make a determination based on factors such as the object, purpose, time, manner of exercising the right, and the degree of imbalance of interests caused between the parties.  
Where an actor exercises civil rights with the primary purpose of harming national interests, public interests, or the lawful rights and interests of others, the People's Court shall determine that such exercise constitutes abuse of civil rights.  
Where abuse of civil rights is constituted, the People's Court shall determine that the abusive act does not have corresponding legal effect. Where abuse of civil rights causes damage, it shall be dealt with in accordance with the relevant provisions of Book 7 of the Civil Code and other provisions.  
II. Capacity for Civil Rights and Capacity for Civil Conduct

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　涉及遗产继承、接受赠与等胎儿利益保护，父母在胎儿娩出前作为法定代理人主张相应权利的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 4** Where the protection of the interests of a fetus, such as in the inheritance of estate or acceptance of gifts, is involved, and the parents, as legal representatives before the fetus is delivered, claim the corresponding rights, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　限制民事行为能力人实施的民事法律行为是否与其年龄、智力、精神健康状况相适应，人民法院可以从行为与本人生活相关联的程度，本人的智力、精神健康状况能否理解其行为并预见相应的后果，以及标的、数量、价款或者报酬等方面认定。  
三、监护

**Article 5** Whether a juridical act performed by a person with limited capacity for civil conduct is commensurate with his age, intelligence, and mental health, the People's Court may determine based on the degree of connection between the act and the person's own life, whether the person's intelligence and mental health enable him to understand his act and foresee the corresponding consequences, and the subject matter, quantity, price, or remuneration, etc.  
III. Guardianship

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院认定自然人的监护能力，应当根据其年龄、身心健康状况、经济条件等因素确定；认定有关组织的监护能力，应当根据其资质、信用、财产状况等因素确定。

**Article 6** The People's Court shall determine the guardianship capacity of a natural person based on factors such as his age, physical and mental health condition, and economic circumstances; and shall determine the guardianship capacity of a relevant organization based on factors such as its qualifications, credit, and property status.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　担任监护人的被监护人父母通过遗嘱指定监护人，遗嘱生效时被指定的人不同意担任监护人的，人民法院应当适用[民法典第二十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-27)、第二十八条的规定确定监护人。  
未成年人由父母担任监护人，父母中的一方通过遗嘱指定监护人，另一方在遗嘱生效时有监护能力，有关当事人对监护人的确定有争议的，人民法院应当适用[民法典第二十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-27)第一款的规定确定监护人。

**Article 7** Where the parents of the ward who act as guardians designate a guardian by will, and the designated person does not agree to serve as guardian when the will becomes effective, the People's Court shall apply Articles 27 and 28 of the Civil Code to determine the guardian.  
Where a minor has his parents as guardians, and one parent designates a guardian by will, while the other parent has guardianship capacity when the will becomes effective, and the relevant parties dispute the determination of the guardian, the People's Court shall apply paragraph 1 of Article 27 of the Civil Code to determine the guardian.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　未成年人的父母与其他依法具有监护资格的人订立协议，约定免除具有监护能力的父母的监护职责的，人民法院不予支持。协议约定在未成年人的父母丧失监护能力时由该具有监护资格的人担任监护人的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
依法具有监护资格的人之间依据[民法典第三十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-30)的规定，约定由[民法典第二十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-27)第二款、第二十八条规定的不同顺序的人共同担任监护人，或者由顺序在后的人担任监护人的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 8** Where the parents of a minor enter into an agreement with other persons legally qualified for guardianship to waive the guardianship duties of the parents who have guardianship capacity, the People's Court shall not support such agreement. Where the agreement provides that such persons with guardianship capacity shall serve as guardians when the parents lose their guardianship capacity, the People's Court shall support it according to law.  
Where persons legally qualified for guardianship, in accordance with Article 30 of the Civil Code, agree that persons of different priority orders as specified in paragraph 2 of Article 27 and Article 28 of the Civil Code shall serve as co-guardians, or that a person of a later priority order shall serve as guardian, the People's Court shall support it according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院依据[民法典第三十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-31)第二款、第三十六条第一款的规定指定监护人时，应当尊重被监护人的真实意愿，按照最有利于被监护人的原则指定，具体参考以下因素：  
（一）与被监护人生活、情感联系的密切程度；  
（二）依法具有监护资格的人的监护顺序；  
（三）是否有不利于履行监护职责的违法犯罪等情形；  
（四）依法具有监护资格的人的监护能力、意愿、品行等。  
人民法院依法指定的监护人一般应当是一人，由数人共同担任监护人更有利于保护被监护人利益的，也可以是数人。

**Article 9** When the People's Court designates a guardian in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 31 and paragraph 1 of Article 36 of the Civil Code, it shall respect the true will of the ward and designate the guardian in accordance with the principle of the best interests of the ward, with specific reference to the following factors:  
(1) the degree of close connection in life and emotional ties with the ward;  
(2) the priority order of guardianship among persons legally qualified for guardianship;  
(3) whether there are illegal or criminal circumstances that are detrimental to the performance of guardianship duties;  
(4) the guardianship capacity, willingness, and moral character of the persons legally qualified for guardianship, etc.  
The guardian designated by the People's Court according to law shall generally be one person; however, if having several persons serving as co-guardians is more conducive to protecting the interests of the ward, there may be several persons.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　有关当事人不服居民委员会、村民委员会或者民政部门的指定，在接到指定通知之日起三十日内向人民法院申请指定监护人的，人民法院经审理认为指定并无不当，依法裁定驳回申请；认为指定不当，依法判决撤销指定并另行指定监护人。  
有关当事人在接到指定通知之日起三十日后提出申请的，人民法院应当按照变更监护关系处理。

**Article 10** Where a relevant party, dissatisfied with the designation by the residents' committee, villagers' committee, or civil affairs department, applies to the People's Court for designation of a guardian within thirty days from the date of receiving the designation notice, the People's Court, after trial, if it finds the designation proper, shall rule to dismiss the application; if it finds the designation improper, shall render a judgment to revoke the designation and designate another guardian.  
Where a relevant party files an application after thirty days from the date of receiving the designation notice, the People's Court shall handle it as a change of guardianship.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　具有完全民事行为能力的成年人与他人依据[民法典第三十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-33)的规定订立书面协议事先确定自己的监护人后，协议的任何一方在该成年人丧失或者部分丧失民事行为能力前请求解除协议的，人民法院依法予以支持。该成年人丧失或者部分丧失民事行为能力后，协议确定的监护人无正当理由请求解除协议的，人民法院不予支持。  
该成年人丧失或者部分丧失民事行为能力后，协议确定的监护人有[民法典第三十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-36)第一款规定的情形之一，该条第二款规定的有关个人、组织申请撤销其监护人资格的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 11** After an adult with full capacity for civil conduct enters into a written agreement with another person to predetermine his guardian in accordance with Article 33 of the Civil Code, if either party to the agreement requests to rescind the agreement before the adult loses or partially loses his capacity for civil conduct, the People's Court shall support such request according to law. After the adult loses or partially loses his capacity for civil conduct, if the guardian determined by the agreement requests to rescind the agreement without justifiable cause, the People's Court shall not support such request.  
After the adult loses or partially loses his capacity for civil conduct, if the guardian determined by the agreement falls under any of the circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of Article 36 of the Civil Code, and the relevant individuals or organizations specified in paragraph 2 of that Article apply to revoke the guardian's qualification, the People's Court shall support such application according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　监护人、其他依法具有监护资格的人之间就监护人是否有[民法典第三十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-39)第一款第二项、第四项规定的应当终止监护关系的情形发生争议，申请变更监护人的，人民法院应当依法受理。经审理认为理由成立的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
被依法指定的监护人与其他具有监护资格的人之间协议变更监护人的，人民法院应当尊重被监护人的真实意愿，按照最有利于被监护人的原则作出裁判。

**Article 12** Where a dispute arises between the guardian and other persons legally qualified for guardianship as to whether the guardian has the circumstances under subparagraphs (2) and (4) of paragraph 1 of Article 39 of the Civil Code that should terminate the guardianship, and an application is made to change the guardian, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law. After trial, if it finds the reasons established, the People's Court shall support it according to law.  
Where the guardian designated according to law and other persons with guardianship qualification agree to change the guardian, the People's Court shall respect the true will of the ward and render a judgment in accordance with the principle of the best interests of the ward.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　监护人因患病、外出务工等原因在一定期限内不能完全履行监护职责，将全部或者部分监护职责委托给他人，当事人主张受托人因此成为监护人的，人民法院不予支持。  
四、宣告失踪和宣告死亡

**Article 13** Where the guardian, due to illness, working away from home, or other reasons, is unable to fully perform guardianship duties within a certain period, and entrusts all or part of the guardianship duties to another person, and the party claims that the trustee thereby becomes the guardian, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
IV. Declaration of Missing Persons and Declaration of Death

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院审理宣告失踪案件时，下列人员应当认定为[民法典第四十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-40)规定的利害关系人：  
（一）被申请人的近亲属；  
（二）依据[民法典第一千一百二十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1128)、第一千一百二十九条规定对被申请人有继承权的亲属；  
（三）债权人、债务人、合伙人等与被申请人有民事权利义务关系的民事主体，但是不申请宣告失踪不影响其权利行使、义务履行的除外。

**Article 14** When the People's Court tries a case concerning declaration of a missing person, the following persons shall be recognized as interested persons under Article 40 of the Civil Code:  
(1) the close relatives of the respondent;  
(2) the relatives who have inheritance rights to the respondent according to Articles 1128 and 1129 of the Civil Code;  
(3) creditors, debtors, partners, and other civil subjects who have civil rights and obligations with the respondent, except where failure to apply for declaration of missing person does not affect the exercise of their rights or performance of their obligations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　失踪人的财产代管人向失踪人的债务人请求偿还债务的，人民法院应当将财产代管人列为原告。  
债权人提起诉讼，请求失踪人的财产代管人支付失踪人所欠的债务和其他费用的，人民法院应当将财产代管人列为被告。经审理认为债权人的诉讼请求成立的，人民法院应当判决财产代管人从失踪人的财产中支付失踪人所欠的债务和其他费用。

**Article 15** Where the property custodian of a missing person requests the debtor of the missing person to repay a debt, the People's Court shall list the property custodian as the plaintiff.  
Where a creditor initiates a lawsuit requesting the property custodian of the missing person to pay the debts and other expenses owed by the missing person, the People's Court shall list the property custodian as the defendant. After trial, if it finds the creditor's claims established, the People's Court shall render a judgment ordering the property custodian to pay the debts and other expenses owed by the missing person from the missing person's property.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院审理宣告死亡案件时，被申请人的配偶、父母、子女，以及依据[民法典第一千一百二十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1129)规定对被申请人有继承权的亲属应当认定为[民法典第四十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-46)规定的利害关系人。  
符合下列情形之一的，被申请人的其他近亲属，以及依据[民法典第一千一百二十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1128)规定对被申请人有继承权的亲属应当认定为[民法典第四十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-46)规定的利害关系人：  
（一）被申请人的配偶、父母、子女均已死亡或者下落不明的；  
（二）不申请宣告死亡不能保护其相应合法权益的。  
被申请人的债权人、债务人、合伙人等民事主体不能认定为[民法典第四十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-46)规定的利害关系人，但是不申请宣告死亡不能保护其相应合法权益的除外。

**Article 16** When the People's Court tries a case concerning declaration of death, the spouse, parents, and children of the respondent, as well as the relatives who have inheritance rights to the respondent according to Article 1129 of the Civil Code, shall be recognized as interested persons under Article 46 of the Civil Code.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, the other close relatives of the respondent and the relatives who have inheritance rights to the respondent according to Article 1128 of the Civil Code shall be recognized as interested persons under Article 46 of the Civil Code:  
(1) the spouse, parents, and children of the respondent have all died or are missing;  
(2) failure to apply for declaration of death cannot protect their corresponding lawful rights and interests.  
Creditors, debtors, partners, and other civil subjects of the respondent shall not be recognized as interested persons under Article 46 of the Civil Code, except where failure to apply for declaration of death cannot protect their corresponding lawful rights and interests.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　自然人在战争期间下落不明的，利害关系人申请宣告死亡的期间适用[民法典第四十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-46)第一款第一项的规定，自战争结束之日或者有关机关确定的下落不明之日起计算。  
五、民事法律行为

**Article 17** Where a natural person goes missing during wartime, the period for an interested person to apply for declaration of death shall be governed by paragraph 1, subparagraph (1) of Article 46 of the Civil Code, calculated from the day the war ends or the day the relevant authority determines the date of disappearance.  
V. Juridical Acts

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　当事人未采用书面形式或者口头形式，但是实施的行为本身表明已经作出相应意思表示，并符合民事法律行为成立条件的，人民法院可以认定为[民法典第一百三十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-135)规定的采用其他形式实施的民事法律行为。

**Article 18** Where the parties do not adopt written form or oral form, but the act performed itself indicates that a corresponding declaration of will has been made, and the conditions for the formation of a juridical act are met, the People's Court may recognize it as a juridical act performed in another form as provided in Article 135 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　行为人对行为的性质、对方当事人或者标的物的品种、质量、规格、价格、数量等产生错误认识，按照通常理解如果不发生该错误认识行为人就不会作出相应意思表示的，人民法院可以认定为[民法典第一百四十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-147)规定的重大误解。  
行为人能够证明自己实施民事法律行为时存在重大误解，并请求撤销该民事法律行为的，人民法院依法予以支持；但是，根据交易习惯等认定行为人无权请求撤销的除外。

**Article 19** Where an actor has a mistaken understanding of the nature of the act, the other party, or the type, quality, specification, price, quantity, etc. of the subject matter, and according to common understanding, if such mistaken understanding had not occurred, the actor would not have made the corresponding declaration of will, the People's Court may recognize it as a major misunderstanding under Article 147 of the Civil Code.  
Where an actor can prove that he had a major misunderstanding when performing the juridical act, and requests to rescind the juridical act, the People's Court shall support it according to law; except where it is determined according to trade practices, etc. that the actor is not entitled to request rescission.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　行为人以其意思表示存在第三人转达错误为由请求撤销民事法律行为的，适用[本解释第十九条](#art-19)的规定。

**Article 20** Where an actor requests rescission of a juridical act on the ground that there was an error in the transmission of his declaration of will by a third party, the provisions of Article 19 of this Interpretation shall apply.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　故意告知虚假情况，或者负有告知义务的人故意隐瞒真实情况，致使当事人基于错误认识作出意思表示的，人民法院可以认定为[民法典第一百四十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-148)、第一百四十九条规定的欺诈。

**Article 21** Where a party intentionally informs a false situation, or a person obligated to inform intentionally conceals the true situation, causing the other party to make a manifestation of intention based on a mistaken understanding, the People's Court may determine it as fraud as stipulated in Articles 148 and 149 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　以给自然人及其近亲属等的人身权利、财产权利以及其他合法权益造成损害或者以给法人、非法人组织的名誉、荣誉、财产权益等造成损害为要挟，迫使其基于恐惧心理作出意思表示的，人民法院可以认定为[民法典第一百五十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-150)规定的胁迫。

**Article 22** Where a party threatens to cause damage to the personal rights, property rights, or other lawful rights and interests of a natural person or his close relatives, or to cause damage to the reputation, honor, property rights and interests, etc. of a legal person or an unincorporated organization, thereby compelling the other party to make a manifestation of intention out of fear, the People's Court may determine it as duress as stipulated in Article 150 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　民事法律行为不成立，当事人请求返还财产、折价补偿或者赔偿损失的，参照适用[民法典第一百五十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-157)的规定。

**Article 23** Where a juridical act is not established, and the party requests restitution of property, compensation in value, or damages, the provisions of Article 157 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　民事法律行为所附条件不可能发生，当事人约定为生效条件的，人民法院应当认定民事法律行为不发生效力；当事人约定为解除条件的，应当认定未附条件，民事法律行为是否失效，依照民法典和相关法律、行政法规的规定认定。  
六、代理

**Article 24** Where a condition attached to a juridical act is impossible to occur, if the parties have agreed that it shall be a condition precedent, the People's Court shall determine that the juridical act does not take effect; if the parties have agreed that it shall be a condition subsequent, it shall be determined that no condition is attached, and whether the juridical act ceases to have effect shall be determined in accordance with the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
VI. Agency

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　数个委托代理人共同行使代理权，其中一人或者数人未与其他委托代理人协商，擅自行使代理权的，依据[民法典第一百七十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-171)、第一百七十二条等规定处理。

**Article 25** Where several authorized agents jointly exercise the power of agency, and one or several of them, without consulting the other authorized agents, exercise the power of agency without authorization, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Articles 171 and 172 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, among others.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　由于急病、通讯联络中断、疫情防控等特殊原因，委托代理人自己不能办理代理事项，又不能与被代理人及时取得联系，如不及时转委托第三人代理，会给被代理人的利益造成损失或者扩大损失的，人民法院应当认定为[民法典第一百六十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-169)规定的紧急情况。

**Article 26** Where due to special reasons such as acute illness, interruption of communication, epidemic prevention and control, the authorized agent is unable to handle the agency matter himself and cannot promptly contact the principal, and if the agent fails to promptly sub-entrust a third party to act as agent, it would cause or increase damage to the interests of the principal, the People's Court shall determine it as an emergency situation as stipulated in Article 169 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　无权代理行为未被追认，相对人请求行为人履行债务或者赔偿损失的，由行为人就相对人知道或者应当知道行为人无权代理承担举证责任。行为人不能证明的，人民法院依法支持相对人的相应诉讼请求；行为人能够证明的，人民法院应当按照各自的过错认定行为人与相对人的责任。

**Article 27** Where an unauthorized agency act is not ratified, and the counterparty requests the actor to perform the obligation or compensate for losses, the actor shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that the counterparty knew or should have known that the actor lacked authority. If the actor fails to prove it, the People's Court shall support the counterparty's corresponding claim according to law; if the actor can prove it, the People's Court shall determine the respective liabilities of the actor and the counterparty according to their respective faults.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　同时符合下列条件的，人民法院可以认定为[民法典第一百七十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-172)规定的相对人有理由相信行为人有代理权：  
（一）存在代理权的外观；  
（二）相对人不知道行为人行为时没有代理权，且无过失。  
因是否构成表见代理发生争议的，相对人应当就无权代理符合前款第一项规定的条件承担举证责任；被代理人应当就相对人不符合前款第二项规定的条件承担举证责任。

**Article 28** Where the following conditions are concurrently satisfied, the People's Court may determine that the counterparty has reason to believe the actor has authority to act as agent as stipulated in Article 172 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) There exists an appearance of authority;  
(2) The counterparty did not know that the actor lacked authority at the time of the act, and is not at fault.  
In case of dispute over whether apparent agency is constituted, the counterparty shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that the unauthorized act meets the conditions set forth in paragraph (1) above; the principal shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that the counterparty does not meet the conditions set forth in paragraph (2) above.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　法定代理人、被代理人依据[民法典第一百四十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-145)、第一百七十一条的规定向相对人作出追认的意思表示的，人民法院应当依据[民法典第一百三十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-137)的规定确认其追认意思表示的生效时间。  
七、民事责任

**Article 29** Where a statutory agent or principal makes a manifestation of intention of ratification to the counterparty in accordance with the provisions of Articles 145 and 171 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 137 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, ascertain the time when the manifestation of intention of ratification takes effect.  
VII. Civil Liability

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　为了使国家利益、社会公共利益、本人或者他人的人身权利、财产权利以及其他合法权益免受正在进行的不法侵害，而针对实施侵害行为的人采取的制止不法侵害的行为，应当认定为[民法典第一百八十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-181)规定的正当防卫。

**Article 30** An act taken against the person committing the unlawful infringement for the purpose of preventing the national interest, public interest, or the personal rights, property rights, or other lawful rights and interests of oneself or another from an ongoing unlawful infringement shall be determined as justifiable defense as stipulated in Article 181 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对于正当防卫是否超过必要的限度，人民法院应当综合不法侵害的性质、手段、强度、危害程度和防卫的时机、手段、强度、损害后果等因素判断。  
经审理，正当防卫没有超过必要限度的，人民法院应当认定正当防卫人不承担责任。正当防卫超过必要限度的，人民法院应当认定正当防卫人在造成不应有的损害范围内承担部分责任;实施侵害行为的人请求正当防卫人承担全部责任的，人民法院不予支持。  
实施侵害行为的人不能证明防卫行为造成不应有的损害，仅以正当防卫人采取的反击方式和强度与不法侵害不相当为由主张防卫过当的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 31** Whether the justifiable defense exceeds the necessary limit shall be judged by the People's Court comprehensively considering factors such as the nature, means, intensity, and degree of harm of the unlawful infringement, as well as the timing, means, intensity, and harmful consequences of the defense.  
Upon adjudication, if the justifiable defense does not exceed the necessary limit, the People's Court shall determine that the person acting in justifiable defense does not bear liability. If the justifiable defense exceeds the necessary limit, the People's Court shall determine that the person acting in justifiable defense bears partial liability within the scope of the undue damage caused; if the person committing the infringement requests that the person acting in justifiable defense bear full liability, the People's Court shall not support it.  
If the person committing the infringement cannot prove that the defense caused undue damage, and only claims that the defense is excessive on the ground that the countermeasure and intensity adopted by the person acting in justifiable defense are disproportionate to the unlawful infringement, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　为了使国家利益、社会公共利益、本人或者他人的人身权利、财产权利以及其他合法权益免受正在发生的急迫危险，不得已而采取紧急措施的，应当认定为[民法典第一百八十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-182)规定的紧急避险。

**Article 32** An emergency measure taken out of necessity to prevent the national interest, public interest, or the personal rights, property rights, or other lawful rights and interests of oneself or another from an imminent danger that is occurring shall be determined as an act of rescue as stipulated in Article 182 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　对于紧急避险是否采取措施不当或者超过必要的限度，人民法院应当综合危险的性质、急迫程度、避险行为所保护的权益以及造成的损害后果等因素判断。  
经审理，紧急避险采取措施并无不当且没有超过必要限度的，人民法院应当认定紧急避险人不承担责任。紧急避险采取措施不当或者超过必要限度的，人民法院应当根据紧急避险人的过错程度、避险措施造成不应有的损害的原因力大小、紧急避险人是否为受益人等因素认定紧急避险人在造成的不应有的损害范围内承担相应的责任。

**Article 33** Whether the measures taken in an act of rescue are improper or exceed the necessary limit shall be judged by the People's Court comprehensively considering factors such as the nature and degree of urgency of the danger, the rights and interests protected by the act of rescue, and the harmful consequences caused.  
Upon adjudication, if the measures taken in the act of rescue are not improper and do not exceed the necessary limit, the People's Court shall determine that the person performing the act of rescue does not bear liability. If the measures taken in the act of rescue are improper or exceed the necessary limit, the People's Court shall, based on factors such as the degree of fault of the person performing the act of rescue, the causative potency of the rescue measures causing undue damage, and whether the person performing the act of rescue is a beneficiary, determine that the person bears corresponding liability within the scope of undue damage caused.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　因保护他人民事权益使自己受到损害，受害人依据[民法典第一百八十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-183)的规定请求受益人适当补偿的，人民法院可以根据受害人所受损失和已获赔偿的情况、受益人受益的多少及其经济条件等因素确定受益人承担的补偿数额。  
八、诉讼时效

**Article 34** Where a person suffers damage due to protecting the civil rights and interests of another, and the victim requests appropriate compensation from the beneficiary in accordance with Article 183 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court may determine the amount of compensation to be borne by the beneficiary based on factors such as the victim's loss and compensation already received, the extent of benefit received by the beneficiary, and the beneficiary's economic conditions.  
VIII. Limitation of Action

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　[民法典第一百八十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-188)第一款规定的三年诉讼时效期间，可以适用民法典有关诉讼时效中止、中断的规定，不适用延长的规定。该条第二款规定的二十年期间不适用中止、中断的规定。

**Article 35** The three-year limitation of action period provided in the first paragraph of Article 188 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China may be subject to the provisions of the Civil Code on suspension and interruption of the limitation of action, but shall not be subject to the provisions on extension. The twenty-year period provided in the second paragraph of that Article shall not be subject to the provisions on suspension or interruption.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人的权利受到损害的，诉讼时效期间自其法定代理人知道或者应当知道权利受到损害以及义务人之日起计算，但是法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 36** Where the rights of a person with no capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct are harmed, the limitation of action period shall run from the date when the statutory agent knew or should have known of the harm to the rights and the obligor, unless otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人的权利受到原法定代理人损害，且在取得、恢复完全民事行为能力或者在原法定代理终止并确定新的法定代理人后，相应民事主体才知道或者应当知道权利受到损害的，有关请求权诉讼时效期间的计算适用[民法典第一百八十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-188)第二款、[本解释第三十六条](#art-36)的规定。

**Article 37** Where the rights of a person with no capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct are harmed by the original statutory agent, and the relevant civil subject only knows or should have known of the harm to the rights after acquiring or regaining full capacity for civil conduct, or after the termination of the original statutory agency and the appointment of a new statutory agent, the calculation of the limitation of action period for the relevant claim shall be subject to the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 188 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and Article 36 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　诉讼时效依据[民法典第一百九十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-195)的规定中断后，在新的诉讼时效期间内，再次出现第一百九十五条规定的中断事由，可以认定为诉讼时效再次中断。  
权利人向义务人的代理人、财产代管人或者遗产管理人等提出履行请求的，可以认定为[民法典第一百九十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-195)规定的诉讼时效中断。  
九、附则

**Article 38** After the limitation of action is interrupted in accordance with Article 195 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, if a ground for interruption as provided in Article 195 occurs again within the new limitation period, it may be determined that the limitation of action is interrupted again.  
If the right holder makes a request for performance to the agent, property custodian, or estate administrator of the obligor, it may be determined as an interruption of the limitation of action as stipulated in Article 195 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China.  
IX. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本解释自2022年3月1日起施行。  
民法典施行后的法律事实引起的民事案件，本解释施行后尚未终审的，适用本解释；本解释施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的，不适用本解释。

**Article 39** This Interpretation shall come into force as of March 1, 2022.  
For civil cases arising from legal facts after the coming into force of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, if the case has not yet been finally adjudicated after the coming into force of this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall apply; if the case has been finally adjudicated before the coming into force of this Interpretation, and the party applies for a retrial or a retrial is decided in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, this Interpretation shall not apply.
