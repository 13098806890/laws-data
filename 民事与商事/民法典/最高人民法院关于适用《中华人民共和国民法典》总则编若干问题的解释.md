# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》总则编若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法典  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕6号  
**公布日期**：2022-02-24  
**生效日期**：2022-03-01

> （2021年12月30日最高人民法院审判委员会第1861次会议通过，自2022年3月1日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　民法典第二编至第七编对民事关系有规定的，人民法院直接适用该规定；民法典第二编至第七编没有规定的，适用民法典第一编的规定，但是根据其性质不能适用的除外。  



**Article 1** Where the Civil Code has provisions in Book Two through Book Seven on a civil relationship, the People's Court shall directly apply such provisions; where the Civil Code has no provisions in Book Two through Book Seven, the provisions in Book One of the Civil Code shall apply, except where such application is precluded by their nature. Where, for the same civil relationship, the provisions of other civil laws constitute a refinement of the corresponding provisions of the Civil Code, the provisions of such civil laws shall apply. Where the Civil Code provides that other laws shall apply, the provisions of such other laws shall apply. Where the Civil Code and other laws have no specific provisions on a civil relationship, the provisions on basic principles of the Civil Code may be followed.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在一定地域、行业范围内长期为一般人从事民事活动时普遍遵守的民间习俗、惯常做法等，可以认定为[民法典第十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-10)规定的习惯。  



**Article 2** Folk customs and customary practices that are generally observed by ordinary persons in civil activities within a certain region or industry for a long period may be recognized as customs as provided in Article 10 of the Civil Code.  
A party claiming the application of a custom shall provide corresponding evidence thereof and of its specific content; where necessary, the People's Court may investigate ex officio.  
The application of customs shall not contravene the core socialist values or public order and good customs.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　对于[民法典第一百三十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-132)所称的滥用民事权利，人民法院可以根据权利行使的对象、目的、时间、方式、造成当事人之间利益失衡的程度等因素作出认定。  



**Article 3** The People's Court may determine abuse of civil rights as referred to in Article 132 of the Civil Code based on factors including the object, purpose, time, and method of exercising the right, and the degree of imbalance of interests among the parties caused thereby. Where a person exercises a civil right with the primary purpose of harming national interests, social public interests, or the lawful rights and interests of others, the People's Court shall determine that such exercise constitutes abuse of civil rights. Where abuse of civil rights is constituted, the People's Court shall determine that such abusive act does not have the corresponding legal effect. If the abuse of civil rights causes damage, it shall be dealt with in accordance with the relevant provisions of Book Seven of the Civil Code, among others. II. Capacity for Civil Rights and Capacity for Civil Conduct

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　涉及遗产继承、接受赠与等胎儿利益保护，父母在胎儿娩出前作为法定代理人主张相应权利的，人民法院依法予以支持。



**Article 4** Where the protection of a fetus' interests, such as in inheritance and acceptance of gifts, is involved, and the parents, as legal representatives, claim corresponding rights before the fetus is delivered, the People's Court shall support such claims according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　限制民事行为能力人实施的民事法律行为是否与其年龄、智力、精神健康状况相适应，人民法院可以从行为与本人生活相关联的程度，本人的智力、精神健康状况能否理解其行为并预见相应的后果，以及标的、数量、价款或者报酬等方面认定。  



**Article 5** Whether a civil juristic act performed by a person with limited capacity for civil conduct is appropriate to his or her age, intelligence, and mental health condition may be determined by the People's Court based on the degree of relevance of the act to his or her life, whether his or her intelligence and mental health condition enable him or her to understand the act and foresee its corresponding consequences, and aspects such as the subject matter, quantity, price, or remuneration.  
III. Guardianship

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院认定自然人的监护能力，应当根据其年龄、身心健康状况、经济条件等因素确定；认定有关组织的监护能力，应当根据其资质、信用、财产状况等因素确定。



**Article 6** The People's Court shall determine the guardianship capacity of a natural person based on factors such as his or her age, physical and mental health condition, and financial circumstances; and shall determine the guardianship capacity of a relevant organization based on factors such as its qualifications, creditworthiness, and property status.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　担任监护人的被监护人父母通过遗嘱指定监护人，遗嘱生效时被指定的人不同意担任监护人的，人民法院应当适用[民法典第二十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-27)、第二十八条的规定确定监护人。  



**Article 7** Where parents who serve as guardians of a ward designate a guardian by will, and the designated person does not agree to serve as guardian when the will takes effect, the People's Court shall determine the guardian by applying Articles 27 and 28 of the Civil Code.  
Where the parents of a minor serve as guardians, one of the parents designates a guardian by will, the other parent has guardianship capacity when the will takes effect, and the relevant parties dispute the determination of the guardian, the People's Court shall determine the guardian by applying the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 27 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　未成年人的父母与其他依法具有监护资格的人订立协议，约定免除具有监护能力的父母的监护职责的，人民法院不予支持。协议约定在未成年人的父母丧失监护能力时由该具有监护资格的人担任监护人的，人民法院依法予以支持。  



**Article 8** Where parents of a minor enter into an agreement with other persons legally qualified for guardianship to exempt the parents who have guardianship capacity from their guardianship duties, the People's Court shall not support such agreement. Where an agreement provides that a person legally qualified for guardianship shall serve as guardian when the parents of the minor lose guardianship capacity, the People's Court shall support such agreement according to law. Where, among persons legally qualified for guardianship, they agree in accordance with Article 30 of the Civil Code that persons of different orders specified in paragraph 2 of Article 27 and Article 28 of the Civil Code shall jointly serve as guardians, or that a person of a later order shall serve as guardian, the People's Court shall support such agreement according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院依据[民法典第三十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-31)第二款、第三十六条第一款的规定指定监护人时，应当尊重被监护人的真实意愿，按照最有利于被监护人的原则指定，具体参考以下因素：  



**Article 9** When designating a guardian in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 31 and paragraph 1 of Article 36 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall respect the true wishes of the ward and designate the guardian in accordance with the principle of the best interests of the ward, with specific reference to the following factors: (1) the closeness of the living and emotional connection with the ward; (2) the order of guardianship of persons legally qualified for guardianship; (3) whether there are circumstances such as illegal or criminal acts that are detrimental to the performance of guardianship duties; (4) the guardianship capacity, willingness, and conduct of persons legally qualified for guardianship, among others. The guardian designated by the People's Court according to law shall generally be one person, but may also be more than one person if it is more conducive to protecting the interests of the ward for several persons to jointly serve as guardians.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　有关当事人不服居民委员会、村民委员会或者民政部门的指定，在接到指定通知之日起三十日内向人民法院申请指定监护人的，人民法院经审理认为指定并无不当，依法裁定驳回申请；认为指定不当，依法判决撤销指定并另行指定监护人。  



**Article 10** Where a relevant party, dissatisfied with the designation by the residents committee, villagers committee, or civil affairs department, applies to the People's Court for designation of a guardian within 30 days from the date of receiving the notice of designation, the People's Court shall, upon trial, rule to dismiss the application if it finds the designation not improper; and shall render a judgment to revoke the designation and designate another guardian if it finds the designation improper.  
Where a relevant party files an application after 30 days from the date of receiving the notice of designation, the People's Court shall handle it as a change of guardianship.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　具有完全民事行为能力的成年人与他人依据[民法典第三十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-33)的规定订立书面协议事先确定自己的监护人后，协议的任何一方在该成年人丧失或者部分丧失民事行为能力前请求解除协议的，人民法院依法予以支持。该成年人丧失或者部分丧失民事行为能力后，协议确定的监护人无正当理由请求解除协议的，人民法院不予支持。  



**Article 11** Where an adult with full capacity for civil conduct enters into a written agreement with another person in accordance with Article 33 of the Civil Code to designate his or her guardian in advance, and any party to the agreement requests to rescind the agreement before the adult loses or partially loses capacity for civil conduct, the People's Court shall support such request according to law. After the adult loses or partially loses capacity for civil conduct, if the guardian designated by the agreement requests to rescind the agreement without justifiable grounds, the People's Court shall not support such request. After the adult loses or partially loses capacity for civil conduct, where the guardian designated by the agreement falls under any of the circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of Article 36 of the Civil Code, and the relevant individual or organization specified in paragraph 2 of that Article applies to revoke the guardian's qualification, the People's Court shall support such application according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　监护人、其他依法具有监护资格的人之间就监护人是否有[民法典第三十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-39)第一款第二项、第四项规定的应当终止监护关系的情形发生争议，申请变更监护人的，人民法院应当依法受理。经审理认为理由成立的，人民法院依法予以支持。  



**Article 12** Where a guardian and another person legally qualified for guardianship dispute whether the guardian falls under the circumstances specified in paragraph 1, item 2 or item 4 of Article 39 of the Civil Code, which shall terminate the guardianship relationship, and apply for a change of guardian, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law. If upon trial the court finds the reasons to be valid, it shall support the application according to law. Where a guardian designated according to law and another person qualified for guardianship reach an agreement to change the guardian through consultation, the People's Court shall respect the true wishes of the ward and make a judgment in accordance with the principle of best interests of the ward.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　监护人因患病、外出务工等原因在一定期限内不能完全履行监护职责，将全部或者部分监护职责委托给他人，当事人主张受托人因此成为监护人的，人民法院不予支持。  



**Article 13** If a guardian, due to illness, employment away from home, or other reasons, is unable to fully perform guardianship duties within a certain period and entrusts all or part of the guardianship duties to another person, and a party claims that the trustee thereby becomes the guardian, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
IV. Declaration of Missing Person and Declaration of Death

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院审理宣告失踪案件时，下列人员应当认定为[民法典第四十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-40)规定的利害关系人：  



**Article 14** When the People's Court tries a case of declaration of a missing person, the following persons shall be identified as interested persons as specified in Article 40 of the Civil Code: (1) the close relatives of the respondent; (2) the relatives who have inheritance rights to the respondent under Articles 1128 and 1129 of the Civil Code; (3) civil subjects such as creditors, debtors, and partners who have a civil rights and obligations relationship with the respondent, except where failure to apply for declaration of missing person does not affect the exercise of their rights or the performance of their obligations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　失踪人的财产代管人向失踪人的债务人请求偿还债务的，人民法院应当将财产代管人列为原告。  



**Article 15** Where the property custodian of a missing person requests the debtor of the missing person to repay a debt, the People's Court shall list the property custodian as the plaintiff. Where a creditor initiates a lawsuit requesting the property custodian of the missing person to pay the debts and other expenses owed by the missing person, the People's Court shall list the property custodian as the defendant. If upon trial the court finds the creditor's claim to be valid, it shall order the property custodian to pay the debts and other expenses owed by the missing person from the missing person's property.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院审理宣告死亡案件时，被申请人的配偶、父母、子女，以及依据[民法典第一千一百二十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1129)规定对被申请人有继承权的亲属应当认定为[民法典第四十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-46)规定的利害关系人。  



**Article 16** When the People's Court tries a case of declaration of death, the spouse, parents, and children of the respondent, as well as the relatives who have inheritance rights to the respondent under Article 1129 of the Civil Code, shall be identified as interested persons as specified in Article 46 of the Civil Code. Under any of the following circumstances, other close relatives of the respondent, as well as the relatives who have inheritance rights to the respondent under Article 1128 of the Civil Code, shall be identified as interested persons as specified in Article 46 of the Civil Code: (1) the spouse, parents, and children of the respondent have all died or their whereabouts are unknown; or (2) failure to apply for declaration of death cannot protect their corresponding lawful rights and interests. Civil subjects such as creditors, debtors, and partners of the respondent shall not be identified as interested persons as specified in Article 46 of the Civil Code, except where failure to apply for declaration of death cannot protect their corresponding lawful rights and interests.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　自然人在战争期间下落不明的，利害关系人申请宣告死亡的期间适用[民法典第四十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-46)第一款第一项的规定，自战争结束之日或者有关机关确定的下落不明之日起计算。  



**Article 17** Where a natural person's whereabouts become unknown during a war, the period for an interested person to apply for declaration of death shall be governed by the provisions of paragraph 1, item 1 of Article 46 of the Civil Code, and shall be calculated from the date the war ends or from the date the relevant authority determines the person's whereabouts became unknown.  
V. Civil Juristic Acts

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　当事人未采用书面形式或者口头形式，但是实施的行为本身表明已经作出相应意思表示，并符合民事法律行为成立条件的，人民法院可以认定为[民法典第一百三十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-135)规定的采用其他形式实施的民事法律行为。



**Article 18** Where a party does not adopt a written or oral form, but the conduct performed itself indicates that a corresponding declaration of will has been made and meets the conditions for the formation of a civil juristic act, the People's Court may identify it as a civil juristic act performed by other forms as specified in Article 135 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　行为人对行为的性质、对方当事人或者标的物的品种、质量、规格、价格、数量等产生错误认识，按照通常理解如果不发生该错误认识行为人就不会作出相应意思表示的，人民法院可以认定为[民法典第一百四十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-147)规定的重大误解。  



**Article 19** Where the actor has a mistaken understanding of the nature of the act, the counterparty, or the type, quality, specification, price, quantity, etc., of the subject matter, and under common understanding, if such mistaken understanding had not occurred, the actor would not have made the corresponding declaration of will, the People's Court may identify it as a material mistake as specified in Article 147 of the Civil Code.  
If the actor can prove that there was a material mistake when performing the civil juristic act and requests to revoke such civil juristic act, the People's Court shall support such request according to law; except where the actor is determined to have no right to request revocation based on trading practices, etc.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　行为人以其意思表示存在第三人转达错误为由请求撤销民事法律行为的，适用[本解释第十九条](#art-19)的规定。



**Article 20** Where the actor requests to revoke a civil juristic act on the ground that there was an error in the transmission of his or her declaration of will by a third party, the provisions of Article 19 of this Interpretation shall apply.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　故意告知虚假情况，或者负有告知义务的人故意隐瞒真实情况，致使当事人基于错误认识作出意思表示的，人民法院可以认定为[民法典第一百四十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-148)、第一百四十九条规定的欺诈。



**Article 21** A party who intentionally provides false information, or a person obligated to disclose information who intentionally conceals the true situation, thereby causing the other party to make a manifestation of intention based on a mistaken understanding, may be determined by the People's Court to have committed fraud as stipulated in Articles 148 and 149 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　以给自然人及其近亲属等的人身权利、财产权利以及其他合法权益造成损害或者以给法人、非法人组织的名誉、荣誉、财产权益等造成损害为要挟，迫使其基于恐惧心理作出意思表示的，人民法院可以认定为[民法典第一百五十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-150)规定的胁迫。



**Article 22** Where a party threatens to cause harm to the personal rights, property rights, or other lawful rights and interests of a natural person and his near relatives, or threatens to cause harm to the reputation, honor, property rights and interests of a legal person or unincorporated organization, thereby compelling the other party to make a manifestation of intention based on fear, the People's Court may determine that duress as stipulated in Article 150 of the Civil Code has occurred.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　民事法律行为不成立，当事人请求返还财产、折价补偿或者赔偿损失的，参照适用[民法典第一百五十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-157)的规定。



**Article 23** Where a civil juristic act is not established, and a party requests the return of property, compensation at appraised value, or damages, the provisions of Article 157 of the Civil Code shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　民事法律行为所附条件不可能发生，当事人约定为生效条件的，人民法院应当认定民事法律行为不发生效力；当事人约定为解除条件的，应当认定未附条件，民事法律行为是否失效，依照民法典和相关法律、行政法规的规定认定。  



**Article 24** Where a condition attached to a civil juristic act is impossible to occur, and the parties have stipulated it as a condition precedent, the People's Court shall determine that the civil juristic act does not take effect. Where the parties have stipulated it as a condition subsequent, it shall be determined that no condition is attached, and whether the civil juristic act ceases to have effect shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Code and relevant laws and administrative regulations. VI. Agency

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　数个委托代理人共同行使代理权，其中一人或者数人未与其他委托代理人协商，擅自行使代理权的，依据[民法典第一百七十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-171)、第一百七十二条等规定处理。



**Article 25** Where several authorized agents jointly exercise the power of agency, and one or some of them, without consulting the other authorized agents, exercise the power of agency without authorization, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Articles 171 and 172 of the Civil Code, among others.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　由于急病、通讯联络中断、疫情防控等特殊原因，委托代理人自己不能办理代理事项，又不能与被代理人及时取得联系，如不及时转委托第三人代理，会给被代理人的利益造成损失或者扩大损失的，人民法院应当认定为[民法典第一百六十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-169)规定的紧急情况。



**Article 26** Where, due to special reasons such as sudden illness, interruption of communication, or epidemic prevention and control, an authorized agent is unable to handle the agency matters personally and cannot promptly contact the principal, and failing to promptly sub-entrust a third party with the agency would cause loss or increase loss to the principal's interests, the People's Court shall determine that an emergency situation as stipulated in Article 169 of the Civil Code has occurred.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　无权代理行为未被追认，相对人请求行为人履行债务或者赔偿损失的，由行为人就相对人知道或者应当知道行为人无权代理承担举证责任。行为人不能证明的，人民法院依法支持相对人的相应诉讼请求；行为人能够证明的，人民法院应当按照各自的过错认定行为人与相对人的责任。



**Article 27** Where an unauthorized agency act is not ratified, and the counterparty requests the actor to perform the obligation or compensate for losses, the actor shall bear the burden of proof that the counterparty knew or should have known that the actor was without authority to act as agent. If the actor fails to prove this, the People's Court shall support the counterparty's corresponding claims in accordance with law. If the actor can prove this, the People's Court shall determine the liability of the actor and the counterparty according to their respective fault.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　同时符合下列条件的，人民法院可以认定为[民法典第一百七十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-172)规定的相对人有理由相信行为人有代理权：  



**Article 28** Where the following conditions are concurrently satisfied, the People's Court may determine that the counterparty has reason to believe that the actor has the power of agency as stipulated in Article 172 of the Civil Code: (1) There is an appearance of the power of agency; (2) The counterparty did not know, and was not negligent in not knowing, that the actor had no power of agency at the time of the act. Where a dispute arises over whether apparent agency is constituted, the counterparty shall bear the burden of proof that the unauthorized agency meets the conditions specified in paragraph (1) of the preceding paragraph; the principal shall bear the burden of proof that the counterparty does not meet the conditions specified in paragraph (2) of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　法定代理人、被代理人依据[民法典第一百四十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-145)、第一百七十一条的规定向相对人作出追认的意思表示的，人民法院应当依据[民法典第一百三十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-137)的规定确认其追认意思表示的生效时间。  



**Article 29** Where a statutory agent or principal makes a manifestation of intention of ratification to the counterparty in accordance with Articles 145 and 171 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 137 of the Civil Code, determine the effective time of such manifestation of intention of ratification.  
VII. Civil Liability

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　为了使国家利益、社会公共利益、本人或者他人的人身权利、财产权利以及其他合法权益免受正在进行的不法侵害，而针对实施侵害行为的人采取的制止不法侵害的行为，应当认定为[民法典第一百八十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-181)规定的正当防卫。



**Article 30** Acts taken against the person committing the infringement to stop ongoing unlawful infringement, for the purpose of protecting the national interest, the public interest, or the personal rights, property rights, or other lawful rights and interests of oneself or others from such infringement, shall be determined as justifiable defense as stipulated in Article 181 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对于正当防卫是否超过必要的限度，人民法院应当综合不法侵害的性质、手段、强度、危害程度和防卫的时机、手段、强度、损害后果等因素判断。  



**Article 31** When determining whether justifiable defense has exceeded the necessary limits, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider factors such as the nature, means, intensity, and degree of harm of the unlawful infringement, as well as the timing, means, intensity, and consequences of damage of the defense. Upon trial, if the justifiable defense does not exceed the necessary limits, the People's Court shall determine that the person acting in justifiable defense bears no liability. If the justifiable defense exceeds the necessary limits, the People's Court shall determine that the person acting in justifiable defense bears partial liability to the extent of the undue damage caused. If the person who committed the infringement requests the person acting in justifiable defense to bear full liability, the People's Court shall not support such claim. If the person who committed the infringement cannot prove that the defensive act caused undue damage, but merely claims that the defense was excessive on the grounds that the countermeasures and intensity taken by the person acting in justifiable defense were disproportionate to the unlawful infringement, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　为了使国家利益、社会公共利益、本人或者他人的人身权利、财产权利以及其他合法权益免受正在发生的急迫危险，不得已而采取紧急措施的，应当认定为[民法典第一百八十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-182)规定的紧急避险。



**Article 32** Where a person takes emergency measures as a last resort to avert an imminent danger that is endangering the national interest, the public interest, or the personal rights, property rights, or other lawful rights and interests of oneself or others, such measures shall be determined as an act of rescue as stipulated in Article 182 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　对于紧急避险是否采取措施不当或者超过必要的限度，人民法院应当综合危险的性质、急迫程度、避险行为所保护的权益以及造成的损害后果等因素判断。  



**Article 33** When determining whether the measures taken in an act of rescue are improper or have exceeded the necessary limits, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider factors such as the nature and imminence of the danger, the rights and interests protected by the act of rescue, and the consequences of damage caused. Upon trial, if the measures taken in the act of rescue are not improper and do not exceed the necessary limits, the People's Court shall determine that the person who engaged in the act of rescue bears no liability. If the measures taken in the act of rescue are improper or exceed the necessary limits, the People's Court shall, based on factors such as the degree of fault of the person who engaged in the act of rescue, the causal contribution of the rescue measures to the undue damage caused, and whether the person who engaged in the act of rescue is a beneficiary, determine that such person bears corresponding liability to the extent of the undue damage caused.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　因保护他人民事权益使自己受到损害，受害人依据[民法典第一百八十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-183)的规定请求受益人适当补偿的，人民法院可以根据受害人所受损失和已获赔偿的情况、受益人受益的多少及其经济条件等因素确定受益人承担的补偿数额。  



**Article 34** Where a person suffers damage due to protecting the civil rights and interests of others, and the victim requests appropriate compensation from the beneficiary in accordance with Article 183 of the Civil Code, the People's Court may determine the amount of compensation to be borne by the beneficiary based on factors such as the loss suffered by the victim and any compensation already received, the extent of the benefit received by the beneficiary, and the beneficiary's economic conditions.  
VIII. Limitation of Action

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　[民法典第一百八十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-188)第一款规定的三年诉讼时效期间，可以适用民法典有关诉讼时效中止、中断的规定，不适用延长的规定。该条第二款规定的二十年期间不适用中止、中断的规定。



**Article 35** The three-year limitation of action period specified in paragraph 1 of Article 188 of the Civil Code is subject to the provisions of the Civil Code on the suspension and interruption of the limitation of action, but is not subject to the provisions on extension. The twenty-year period specified in paragraph 2 of that Article is not subject to the provisions on suspension or interruption.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人的权利受到损害的，诉讼时效期间自其法定代理人知道或者应当知道权利受到损害以及义务人之日起计算，但是法律另有规定的除外。



**Article 36** Where the rights of a person with no capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct are infringed, the limitation of action period shall run from the date on which his statutory agent knows or should have known of the infringement and the obligor, unless otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人的权利受到原法定代理人损害，且在取得、恢复完全民事行为能力或者在原法定代理终止并确定新的法定代理人后，相应民事主体才知道或者应当知道权利受到损害的，有关请求权诉讼时效期间的计算适用[民法典第一百八十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-188)第二款、[本解释第三十六条](#art-36)的规定。



**Article 37** Where the rights of a person with no capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct are infringed by the original statutory agent, and the relevant civil subject only knows or should have known of the infringement after acquiring or regaining full capacity for civil conduct, or after the termination of the original statutory agency and the appointment of a new statutory agent, the calculation of the limitation of action period for the relevant claims shall be governed by paragraph 2 of Article 188 of the Civil Code and Article 36 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　诉讼时效依据[民法典第一百九十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-195)的规定中断后，在新的诉讼时效期间内，再次出现第一百九十五条规定的中断事由，可以认定为诉讼时效再次中断。  



**Article 38** After the limitation of action is interrupted in accordance with Article 195 of the Civil Code, if a ground for interruption specified in Article 195 arises again within the new limitation of action period, it may be determined that the limitation of action is interrupted again.  
Where the right holder makes a request for performance to the obligor's agent, property custodian, or estate administrator, among others, it may be determined as an interruption of the limitation of action as stipulated in Article 195 of the Civil Code.  
IX. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本解释自2022年3月1日起施行。  



**Article 39** This Interpretation shall come into force as of March 1, 2022. For civil cases arising from legal facts after the Civil Code comes into force, if a final judgment has not been rendered before the date this Interpretation comes into force, this Interpretation shall apply. If a final judgment has been rendered before the date this Interpretation comes into force, this Interpretation shall not apply where a party applies for a retrial or where a retrial is decided in accordance with the trial supervision procedure.
