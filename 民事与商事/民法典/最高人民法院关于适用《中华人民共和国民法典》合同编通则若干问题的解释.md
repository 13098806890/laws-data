# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》合同编通则若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法典  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2023〕13号  
**公布日期**：2023-12-04  
**生效日期**：2023-12-05

> （2023年5月23日最高人民法院审判委员会第1889次会议通过，自2023年12月5日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院依据[民法典第一百四十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-142)第一款、第四百六十六条第一款的规定解释合同条款时，应当以词句的通常含义为基础，结合相关条款、合同的性质和目的、习惯以及诚信原则，参考缔约背景、磋商过程、履行行为等因素确定争议条款的含义。  



**Article 1** When a People's Court interprets contract clauses in accordance with Paragraph 1 of Article 142 and Paragraph 1 of Article 466 of the Civil Code, it shall take the ordinary meaning of the wording as the basis and determine the meaning of the disputed clauses by taking into account the relevant clauses, the nature and purpose of the contract, practices, and the principle of good faith, and by referring to factors such as the background of the contract formation, the negotiation process, and the conduct of performance.  
If there is evidence proving that the parties have a common understanding of the contract clauses different from the ordinary meaning of the wording, and one party claims that the contract clauses shall be understood based on the ordinary meaning of the wording, the People's Court shall not uphold such a claim.  
Where there are two or more interpretations of a contract clause that may affect its validity, the People's Court shall choose an interpretation that favors the validity of the clause; if the contract is a gratuitous contract, the interpretation that imposes a lighter burden on the debtor shall be chosen.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　下列情形，不违反法律、行政法规的强制性规定且不违背公序良俗的，人民法院可以认定为民法典所称的“交易习惯”：  



**Article 2** Under the following circumstances, where the conditions do not violate the mandatory provisions of laws or administrative regulations and do not contravene public order or good customs, the People's Court may recognize the existence of a "trading practice" as referred to in the Civil Code: (1) a habitual practice between the parties in their transactions; (2) a practice commonly adopted in the locality of the transaction or within a certain field or industry, which the counterparty knew or should have known at the time of concluding the contract. The party asserting the existence of a trading practice shall bear the burden of adducing evidence. II. Formation of Contracts

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　当事人对合同是否成立存在争议，人民法院能够确定当事人姓名或者名称、标的和数量的，一般应当认定合同成立。但是，法律另有规定或者当事人另有约定的除外。  



**Article 3** If a dispute arises between the parties regarding whether a contract is formed, and the People's Court can ascertain the name or title of the parties, the subject matter, and the quantity, the contract shall generally be deemed formed. However, this does not apply if otherwise provided by law or agreed by the parties. If the contract is deemed to have been formed under the preceding paragraph, the People's Court shall determine the missing contents of the contract in accordance with Articles 510 and 511 of the Civil Code and other relevant provisions. Where a party claims that the contract is void or requests revocation or dissolution of the contract, and the People's Court considers that the contract is not formed, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 53 of the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Evidence in Civil Actions, treat whether the contract is formed as a focal issue for trial, and may redesignate the time limit for adducing evidence based on the specific circumstances of the case.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　采取招标方式订立合同，当事人请求确认合同自中标通知书到达中标人时成立的，人民法院应予支持。合同成立后，当事人拒绝签订书面合同的，人民法院应当依据招标文件、投标文件和中标通知书等确定合同内容。  



**Article 4** Where a contract is concluded through a bidding process, and a party requests confirmation that the contract is formed when the notice of award reaches the winning bidder, the People's Court shall uphold such a request. If a party refuses to sign a written contract after the contract is formed, the People's Court shall determine the content of the contract based on the bidding documents, the bid documents, and the notice of award. Where a contract is concluded through public competitive methods such as on-site auctions or online auctions, and a party requests confirmation that the contract is formed when the auctioneer's hammer falls or the electronic transaction system confirms the transaction, the People's Court shall uphold such a request. If a party refuses to sign a transaction confirmation after the contract is formed, the People's Court shall determine the content of the contract based on the auction announcement and the bidders' offers. Where an institution such as a property rights exchange presides over an auction or listing transaction, and its published auction announcement, transaction rules, or other documents publicly establish the conditions required for the formation of the contract, and a party requests confirmation that the contract is formed when such conditions are met, the People's Court shall uphold such a request.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　第三人实施欺诈、胁迫行为，使当事人在违背真实意思的情况下订立合同，受到损失的当事人请求第三人承担赔偿责任的，人民法院依法予以支持；当事人亦有违背诚信原则的行为的，人民法院应当根据各自的过错确定相应的责任。但是，法律、司法解释对当事人与第三人的民事责任另有规定的，依照其规定。



**Article 5** Where a third party commits fraud or coercion, causing a party to enter into a contract contrary to its true intention, and the aggrieved party requests the third party to bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall uphold such a request in accordance with law; if the party also acts in violation of the principle of good faith, the People's Court shall determine the corresponding liability based on the respective fault of each party. However, if laws or judicial interpretations otherwise provide for the civil liability of the party and the third party, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　当事人以认购书、订购书、预订书等形式约定在将来一定期限内订立合同，或者为担保在将来一定期限内订立合同交付了定金，能够确定将来所要订立合同的主体、标的等内容的，人民法院应当认定预约合同成立。  



**Article 6** If a party uses a letter of intent, subscription agreement, booking agreement, or similar form to agree to enter into a contract within a certain future period, or pays a deposit as security for concluding a contract within a certain future period, and the subject matter and other contents of the contract to be concluded in the future can be ascertained, the People's Court shall find that a preliminary contract is formed.  
Where a party only expresses an intention to transact by signing a letter of intent or memorandum of understanding, without agreeing to enter into a contract within a certain future period, or although there is such an agreement, the subject matter and other contents of the contract to be concluded in the future are difficult to ascertain, and one party claims that a preliminary contract is formed, the People's Court shall not uphold such a claim.  
If the letter of intent, subscription agreement, booking agreement, or similar instruments signed by the parties have reached a consensus on the main contents such as the subject matter, quantity, price, or remuneration, and meet the conditions for the formation of a contract under Article 3, Paragraph 1 of this Interpretation, and there is no explicit agreement to separately conclude a contract within a certain future period, or although there is such an agreement, one party has already performed and the other party has accepted such performance, the People's Court shall find that a principal contract is formed.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　预约合同生效后，当事人一方拒绝订立本约合同或者在磋商订立本约合同时违背诚信原则导致未能订立本约合同的，人民法院应当认定该当事人不履行预约合同约定的义务。  



**Article 7** After a preliminary contract takes effect, if one party refuses to enter into the principal contract or acts in violation of the principle of good faith during the negotiation of the principal contract, resulting in the failure to conclude the principal contract, the People's Court shall find that such a party fails to perform its obligations under the preliminary contract.  
When determining whether a party has acted in violation of the principle of good faith during the negotiation of the principal contract, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider factors such as whether the conditions proposed by the party during negotiations are significantly inconsistent with the content agreed in the preliminary contract and whether the party has made reasonable efforts to negotiate.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　预约合同生效后，当事人一方不履行订立本约合同的义务，对方请求其赔偿因此造成的损失的，人民法院依法予以支持。  



**Article 8** After a preliminary contract takes effect, if one party fails to perform its obligation to enter into the principal contract, and the counterparty requests compensation for the losses thus caused, the People's Court shall uphold such a request in accordance with law.  
For the compensation for losses under the preceding paragraph, if the parties have agreed, such agreement shall prevail; in the absence of agreement, the People's Court shall, by comprehensively considering factors such as the completeness of the content of the preliminary contract and the extent to which the conditions for entering into the principal contract have matured, determine the amount of compensation as it deems appropriate.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　合同条款符合[民法典第四百九十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-496)第一款规定的情形，当事人仅以合同系依据合同示范文本制作或者双方已经明确约定合同条款不属于格式条款为由主张该条款不是格式条款的，人民法院不予支持。  



**Article 9** If a contract clause meets the circumstances specified in Article 496, Paragraph 1 of the Civil Code, and a party, solely on the grounds that the contract is made based on a standard model contract or that the parties have explicitly agreed that the clause is not a standard clause, claims that such clause is not a standard clause, the People's Court shall not uphold such a claim. If a party engaged in business operations claims, solely on the ground of no actual repeated use, that a clause it has pre-drafted and has not negotiated with the counterparty is not a standard clause, the People's Court shall not uphold such a claim. However, this does not apply if there is evidence proving that the clause was not pre-drafted for repeated use.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　提供格式条款的一方在合同订立时采用通常足以引起对方注意的文字、符号、字体等明显标识，提示对方注意免除或者减轻其责任、排除或者限制对方权利等与对方有重大利害关系的异常条款的，人民法院可以认定其已经履行[民法典第四百九十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-496)第二款规定的提示义务。  



**Article 10** If, at the time of entering into the contract, the party providing the standard clause uses obvious markings such as text, symbols, or font that are generally sufficient to attract the counterparty's attention to draw its attention to unusual clauses that have a material interest to the counterparty, such as clauses that relieve or reduce the provider's liability, or exclude or limit the counterparty's rights, the People's Court may find that the provider has fulfilled its duty to draw attention under Article 496, Paragraph 2 of the Civil Code. If the party providing the standard clause, at the request of the counterparty, provides an explanation in writing or orally regarding the concept, content, and legal consequences of the unusual clauses that have a material interest to the counterparty, in a manner generally understandable to the counterparty, the People's Court may find that the provider has fulfilled its duty of explanation under Article 496, Paragraph 2 of the Civil Code. The party providing the standard clause shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that it has fulfilled its duty to draw attention or duty of explanation. For electronic contracts concluded through information networks such as the Internet, if the party providing the standard clause claims to have fulfilled its duty to draw attention or duty of explanation solely by adopting methods such as setting checkboxes or pop-up windows, the People's Court shall not uphold such a claim, except where its evidence meets the requirements of the preceding two paragraphs. III. Validity of Contracts

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　当事人一方是自然人，根据该当事人的年龄、智力、知识、经验并结合交易的复杂程度，能够认定其对合同的性质、合同订立的法律后果或者交易中存在的特定风险缺乏应有的认知能力的，人民法院可以认定该情形构成[民法典第一百五十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-151)规定的“缺乏判断能力”。



**Article 11** Where one party is a natural person and, based on the party's age, intelligence, knowledge, and experience, combined with the complexity of the transaction, it can be determined that the party lacks the requisite cognitive ability to understand the nature of the contract, the legal consequences of concluding the contract, or the specific risks existing in the transaction, the People's Court may determine that such circumstance constitutes a "lack of judgment ability" as provided in Article 151 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　合同依法成立后，负有报批义务的当事人不履行报批义务或者履行报批义务不符合合同的约定或者法律、行政法规的规定，对方请求其继续履行报批义务的，人民法院应予支持；对方主张解除合同并请求其承担违反报批义务的赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 12** After a contract is legally formed, if the party obligated to apply for approval fails to perform such obligation or performs it in a manner inconsistent with the contract or with the provisions of laws or administrative regulations, and the other party requests that it continue to perform the obligation to apply for approval, the People's Court shall support such request; if the other party claims to rescind the contract and requests that it bear liability for damages for breaching the obligation to apply for approval, the People's Court shall support such claim. After the People's Court has rendered a judgment ordering one party to perform the obligation to apply for approval, if that party still fails to perform, and the other party claims to rescind the contract and requests that it bear liability for damages by reference to liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support such claim. Before the contract is approved, if one party brings an action requesting the other party to perform the principal obligations stipulated in the contract, and, after clarification, refuses to amend the claims, the People's Court shall render a judgment dismissing such claims, provided that this does not affect the party's right to file another action separately. If the party obligated to apply for approval has already completed procedures such as applying for approval or has performed the obligation to apply for approval as determined by an effective judgment, but the approving authority decides not to grant approval, and the other party requests that it bear liability for damages, the People's Court shall not support such request. However, if the contract fails to obtain approval due to reasons attributable to the party, such as delay in performing the obligation to apply for approval, and the other party requests compensation for the losses thereby incurred, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of Article 157 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　合同存在无效或者可撤销的情形，当事人以该合同已在有关行政管理部门办理备案、已经批准机关批准或者已依据该合同办理财产权利的变更登记、移转登记等为由主张合同有效的，人民法院不予支持。



**Article 13** Where a contract is void or voidable, and a party claims that the contract is valid on the grounds that it has been filed for record with the relevant administrative department, has been approved by the approving authority, or has been the basis for the registration of changes or transfer of property rights, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　当事人之间就同一交易订立多份合同，人民法院应当认定其中以虚假意思表示订立的合同无效。当事人为规避法律、行政法规的强制性规定，以虚假意思表示隐藏真实意思表示的，人民法院应当依据[民法典第一百五十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-153)第一款的规定认定被隐藏合同的效力；当事人为规避法律、行政法规关于合同应当办理批准等手续的规定，以虚假意思表示隐藏真实意思表示的，人民法院应当依据[民法典第五百零二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-502)第二款的规定认定被隐藏合同的效力。  



**Article 14** Where parties conclude multiple contracts for the same transaction, the People's Court shall determine that the contract concluded with a false expression of intent is void. Where a party, for the purpose of circumventing the mandatory provisions of laws or administrative regulations, uses a false expression of intent to conceal a true expression of intent, the People's Court shall determine the validity of the concealed contract in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 153 of the Civil Code; where a party, for the purpose of circumventing the provisions of laws or administrative regulations requiring contracts to undergo procedures such as approval, uses a false expression of intent to conceal a true expression of intent, the People's Court shall determine the validity of the concealed contract in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 502 of the Civil Code. If the concealed contract is determined to be void or to have no legal effect in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the People's Court shall, on the factual basis of the concealed contract, determine the parties' civil liability in accordance with the provisions of Article 157 of the Civil Code, unless otherwise provided by law. Where multiple contracts concluded by the parties for the same transaction all represent their true expressions of intent and there are no other circumstances affecting the validity of the contracts, the People's Court shall, on the basis of ascertaining the order in which each contract was concluded and the actual performance, determine whether the content of the contracts has been modified. Where laws or administrative regulations prohibit the modification of the contract's content, the People's Court shall determine that the corresponding modification to the contract is void.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民法院认定当事人之间的权利义务关系，不应当拘泥于合同使用的名称，而应当根据合同约定的内容。当事人主张的权利义务关系与根据合同内容认定的权利义务关系不一致的，人民法院应当结合缔约背景、交易目的、交易结构、履行行为以及当事人是否存在虚构交易标的等事实认定当事人之间的实际民事法律关系。



**Article 15** In determining the rights and obligations between parties, the People's Court shall not be confined to the name of the contract used but shall base its determination on the content agreed upon in the contract. Where the rights and obligations claimed by a party are inconsistent with the rights and obligations determined based on the content of the contract, the People's Court shall determine the actual civil legal relationship between the parties by taking into account such facts as the background of the contract formation, the purpose of the transaction, the structure of the transaction, the conduct of performance, and whether the parties have fabricated the subject matter of the transaction.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　合同违反法律、行政法规的强制性规定，有下列情形之一，由行为人承担行政责任或者刑事责任能够实现强制性规定的立法目的的，人民法院可以依据[民法典第一百五十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-153)第一款关于“该强制性规定不导致该民事法律行为无效的除外”的规定认定该合同不因违反强制性规定无效：  



**Article 16** Where a contract violates a mandatory provision of a law or an administrative regulation, and if the perpetrator assumes administrative or criminal liability can achieve the legislative purpose of such mandatory provision under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may determine that the contract is not void due to the violation of the mandatory provision in accordance with the provision of Article 153(1) of the Civil Code that "unless such mandatory provision does not render the civil juristic act void": (1) The mandatory provision is intended to maintain public order, but the actual performance of the contract has a significantly minor impact on public order, and holding the contract void will render the case outcome unfair and unjust; (2) The mandatory provision is intended to protect the state's interests such as tax revenue and land transfer fees, or the legitimate interests of other civil subjects, rather than the civil rights and interests of the parties to the contract, and holding the contract valid will not affect the realization of such normative purpose; (3) The mandatory provision is intended to require one party to strengthen risk control or internal management, and the other party is neither capable nor obligated to review whether the contract violates the mandatory provision, and holding the contract void will cause it to bear adverse consequences; (4) Although one party violated the mandatory provision at the time of concluding the contract, it has subsequently acquired the conditions to remedy such violation but fails to do so in bad faith; (5) Other circumstances as provided by laws or judicial interpretations. Where a mandatory provision of a law or an administrative regulation is intended to regulate the performance conduct after the contract is concluded, and a party requests a determination that the contract is void on the ground that it violates the mandatory provision, the People's Court shall not support such request, unless the performance of the contract inevitably leads to a violation of the mandatory provision, or unless otherwise provided by laws or judicial interpretations. Where the contract is found valid pursuant to the preceding two paragraphs, but the party's illegal act has not been dealt with, the People's Court shall issue a judicial recommendation to the relevant administrative department. Where the party's act is suspected of constituting a crime, the case clues shall be transferred to a criminal investigation authority; where it is a private prosecution case, the party shall be informed that it may separately initiate a lawsuit in the competent People's Court.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　合同虽然不违反法律、行政法规的强制性规定，但是有下列情形之一，人民法院应当依据[民法典第一百五十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-153)第二款的规定认定合同无效：  



**Article 17** Although a contract does not violate a mandatory provision of a law or an administrative regulation, the People's Court shall determine the contract to be void in accordance with Article 153(2) of the Civil Code under any of the following circumstances: (1) The contract affects national security such as political security, economic security, or military security; (2) The contract violates public order, such as affecting social stability or fair competition order, or damaging the public interest; (3) The contract contravenes good customs, such as deviating from social morals, family ethics, or harming human dignity. When determining whether a contract contravenes public order or good customs, the People's Court shall be guided by the core socialist values and comprehensively consider factors such as the subjective motive and transaction purpose of the parties, the intensity of government regulation, the frequency of similar transactions engaged in by the parties within a certain period, and the social consequences of the conduct, and shall provide sufficient reasoning in the judgment document. Where a party engages in a transaction genuinely for the needs of daily life, which does not cause a significant impact on public order, does not affect national security, and does not contravene good customs, the People's Court shall not determine the contract to be void.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　法律、行政法规的规定虽然有“应当”“必须”或者“不得”等表述，但是该规定旨在限制或者赋予民事权利，行为人违反该规定将构成无权处分、无权代理、越权代表等，或者导致合同相对人、第三人因此获得撤销权、解除权等民事权利的，人民法院应当依据法律、行政法规规定的关于违反该规定的民事法律后果认定合同效力。



**Article 18** Where a provision of a law or an administrative regulation contains expressions such as "shall", "must", or "shall not", but such provision is intended to restrict or grant a civil right, and a violator's act of violating such provision shall constitute disposal without right, agency without authority, representation beyond authority, or shall cause the counterparty or a third party to acquire civil rights such as the right of revocation or the right of dissolution, the People's Court shall determine the validity of the contract in accordance with the civil legal consequences provided by the law or administrative regulation for violating such provision.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　以转让或者设定财产权利为目的订立的合同，当事人或者真正权利人仅以让与人在订立合同时对标的物没有所有权或者处分权为由主张合同无效的，人民法院不予支持；因未取得真正权利人事后同意或者让与人事后未取得处分权导致合同不能履行，受让人主张解除合同并请求让与人承担违反合同的赔偿责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。  



**Article 19** Where a party or a true right holder claims that a contract concluded for the purpose of transferring or establishing a property right is void merely on the ground that the transferor had no ownership or right of disposal over the subject matter at the time of concluding the contract, the People's Court shall not support such claim. Where the contract cannot be performed due to the failure to obtain the subsequent consent of the true right holder or the subsequent acquisition of the right of disposal by the transferor, and the transferee claims to dissolve the contract and requests the transferor to bear liability for damages for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law. Where the contract as provided in the preceding paragraph is found valid and the transferor has delivered the property or transferred the registration to the transferee, the People's Court shall support the request of the true right holder to determine that the property right has not changed or to request the return of the property. However, this shall not apply where the transferee has acquired the property right in good faith in accordance with Article 311 of the Civil Code and other provisions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　法律、行政法规为限制法人的法定代表人或者非法人组织的负责人的代表权，规定合同所涉事项应当由法人、非法人组织的权力机构或者决策机构决议，或者应当由法人、非法人组织的执行机构决定，法定代表人、负责人未取得授权而以法人、非法人组织的名义订立合同，未尽到合理审查义务的相对人主张该合同对法人、非法人组织发生效力并由其承担违约责任的，人民法院不予支持，但是法人、非法人组织有过错的，可以参照[民法典第一百五十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-157)的规定判决其承担相应的赔偿责任。相对人已尽到合理审查义务，构成表见代表的，人民法院应当依据[民法典第五百零四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-504)的规定处理。  



**Article 20** Where a law or an administrative regulation, in order to restrict the representation authority of the legal representative of a legal person or the responsible person of an unincorporated organization, provides that matters involved in a contract shall be decided by a resolution of the authority or decision-making body of the legal person or unincorporated organization, or shall be decided by the executive body of the legal person or unincorporated organization, and the legal representative or responsible person concludes a contract in the name of the legal person or unincorporated organization without authorization, and the counterparty who fails to perform a reasonable duty of review claims that such contract is binding on the legal person or unincorporated organization and that it shall bear liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall not support such claim; however, if the legal person or unincorporated organization is at fault, the court may, by reference to Article 157 of the Civil Code, order it to bear corresponding compensatory liability. Where the counterparty has performed a reasonable duty of review, and the circumstances constitute apparent representation, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with Article 504 of the Civil Code. Where the matters involved in the contract do not exceed the representation authority of the legal representative or responsible person as provided by laws or administrative regulations, but exceed the restrictions on representation authority imposed by the articles of association of the legal person or unincorporated organization or by its authority body, and the counterparty claims that such contract is binding on the legal person or unincorporated organization and that it shall bear liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law, unless the legal person or unincorporated organization provides evidence that the counterparty knew or should have known of such restrictions. Where the legal person or unincorporated organization, after bearing civil liability, seeks recourse from the legal representative or responsible person who is at fault for losses caused by the act of representation beyond authority, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law. Where laws or judicial interpretations otherwise provide for the civil liability of the legal representative or responsible person, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　法人、非法人组织的工作人员就超越其职权范围的事项以法人、非法人组织的名义订立合同，相对人主张该合同对法人、非法人组织发生效力并由其承担违约责任的，人民法院不予支持。但是，法人、非法人组织有过错的，人民法院可以参照[民法典第一百五十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-157)的规定判决其承担相应的赔偿责任。前述情形，构成表见代理的，人民法院应当依据[民法典第一百七十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-172)的规定处理。  



**Article 21** Where a staff member of a legal person or an unincorporated organization concludes a contract in the name of the legal person or the unincorporated organization on matters exceeding the scope of his or her authority, and the counterparty claims that the contract shall take effect with respect to the legal person or the unincorporated organization and that it shall bear the liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall not support such claim. However, if the legal person or the unincorporated organization is at fault, the People's Court may, by reference to Article 157 of the Civil Code, render a judgment that it shall bear corresponding compensation liability. In the aforementioned circumstances, if an apparent agency is constituted, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with Article 172 of the Civil Code. The People's Court shall determine that a staff member of a legal person or an unincorporated organization exceeded the scope of his or her authority when concluding a contract if the matters covered by the contract fall under any of the following circumstances: (1) matters that shall be subject to resolution by the authority organ or decision-making organ of the legal person or the unincorporated organization according to law; (2) matters that shall be decided by the executive organ of the legal person or the unincorporated organization according to law; (3) matters that shall be implemented by the legal representative or the responsible person on behalf of the legal person or the unincorporated organization according to law; or (4) matters that are not ordinarily within the scope of authority that may be handled by such staff member. Where the matters covered by the contract do not exceed the scope of authority determined in accordance with the preceding paragraph, but exceed the restrictions imposed by the legal person or the unincorporated organization on the scope of authority of the staff member, and the counterparty claims that the contract shall take effect with respect to the legal person or the unincorporated organization and that it shall bear the liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support such claim. However, this shall not apply where the legal person or the unincorporated organization adduces evidence proving that the counterparty knew or ought to have known of such restrictions. Where the legal person or the unincorporated organization, after bearing civil liability, seeks recourse against a staff member who acted intentionally or with gross negligence, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　法定代表人、负责人或者工作人员以法人、非法人组织的名义订立合同且未超越权限，法人、非法人组织仅以合同加盖的印章不是备案印章或者系伪造的印章为由主张该合同对其不发生效力的，人民法院不予支持。  



**Article 22** Where a legal representative, a responsible person, or a staff member concludes a contract in the name of a legal person or an unincorporated organization without exceeding his or her authority, and the legal person or the unincorporated organization claims that the contract does not take effect with respect to it solely on the ground that the seal affixed to the contract is not the filed seal or is a forged seal, the People's Court shall not support such claim. Where a contract is concluded in the name of a legal person or an unincorporated organization, but only bears the signature or fingerprint of the legal representative, responsible person, or staff member without the seal of the legal person or the unincorporated organization, if the counterparty can prove that the legal representative, responsible person, or staff member did not exceed his or her authority when concluding the contract, the People's Court shall determine that the contract takes effect with respect to the legal person or the unincorporated organization. However, this shall not apply where the parties have agreed that affixing a seal shall be a condition for the formation of the contract. Where a contract bears only the seal of a legal person or an unincorporated organization without the signature or fingerprint of any person, if the counterparty can prove that the contract was concluded by the legal representative, responsible person, or staff member within the scope of his or her authority, the People's Court shall determine that the contract takes effect with respect to the legal person or the unincorporated organization. In the circumstances set forth in the preceding three paragraphs, if the legal representative, responsible person, or staff member, when concluding the contract, exceeds the authority of representation or agency, but constitutes apparent representation in accordance with Article 504 of the Civil Code, or constitutes apparent agency in accordance with Article 172 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall determine that the contract takes effect with respect to the legal person or the unincorporated organization.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　法定代表人、负责人或者代理人与相对人恶意串通，以法人、非法人组织的名义订立合同，损害法人、非法人组织的合法权益，法人、非法人组织主张不承担民事责任的，人民法院应予支持。法人、非法人组织请求法定代表人、负责人或者代理人与相对人对因此受到的损失承担连带赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 23** Where a legal representative, a responsible person, or an agent, in collusion with the counterparty with malicious intent, concludes a contract in the name of a legal person or an unincorporated organization, thereby harming the legitimate rights and interests of the legal person or the unincorporated organization, and the legal person or the unincorporated organization claims that it shall not bear civil liability, the People's Court shall support such claim. Where the legal person or the unincorporated organization requests that the legal representative, responsible person, or agent and the counterparty bear joint and several liability for compensation for the losses suffered as a result, the People's Court shall support such claim. If, based on the evidence adduced by the legal person or the unincorporated organization, and taking into account factors such as the trade practices between the parties, whether the contract was manifestly unfair at the time of its conclusion, whether the relevant persons obtained improper benefits, and the circumstances of the performance of the contract, the People's Court can determine that there is a high probability of malicious collusion between the legal representative, responsible person, or agent and the counterparty, it may require the aforesaid persons to make statements or adduce corresponding evidence regarding relevant facts such as the process of contract conclusion and performance. If such persons refuse to make statements without justifiable reasons, or their statements are unreasonable and they cannot adduce corresponding evidence, the People's Court may determine that the fact of malicious collusion is established.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　合同不成立、无效、被撤销或者确定不发生效力，当事人请求返还财产，经审查财产能够返还的，人民法院应当根据案件具体情况，单独或者合并适用返还占有的标的物、更正登记簿册记载等方式；经审查财产不能返还或者没有必要返还的，人民法院应当以认定合同不成立、无效、被撤销或者确定不发生效力之日该财产的市场价值或者以其他合理方式计算的价值为基准判决折价补偿。  



**Article 24** Where a contract is not formed, void, revoked, or determined to be ineffective, and a party requests the return of property, if upon examination the property can be returned, the People's Court shall, based on the specific circumstances of the case, apply individually or in combination the methods of returning the subject matter in possession or correcting the entry in the register; if upon examination the property cannot be returned or need not be returned, the People's Court shall render a judgment on compensation at a value converted based on the market value of the property on the date of determination that the contract is not formed, void, revoked, or ineffective, or a value calculated by other reasonable means. Except for the circumstances set forth in the preceding paragraph, if a party also requests compensation for losses, the People's Court shall, in consideration of the circumstances of property return or compensation at a converted value, comprehensively consider facts such as property value appreciation gains and depreciation losses, and the incurrence of transaction costs, and reasonably determine the amount of compensation for losses in accordance with the degree of fault and the extent of causation of the parties, based on the principle of good faith and the principle of fairness. Where a contract is not formed, void, revoked, or determined to be ineffective, and the conduct of the parties is suspected of being illegal and has not been dealt with, which may result in one or both parties obtaining improper benefits through illegal conduct, the People's Court shall submit a judicial recommendation to the relevant administrative department. If the conduct of the parties is suspected of constituting a crime, the case leads shall be transferred to a criminal investigation authority; if it falls within the category of a case of private prosecution, the parties shall be informed that they may separately file a lawsuit with the People's Court having jurisdiction.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　合同不成立、无效、被撤销或者确定不发生效力，有权请求返还价款或者报酬的当事人一方请求对方支付资金占用费的，人民法院应当在当事人请求的范围内按照中国人民银行授权全国银行间同业拆借中心公布的一年期贷款市场报价利率（LPR）计算。但是，占用资金的当事人对于合同不成立、无效、被撤销或者确定不发生效力没有过错的，应当以中国人民银行公布的同期同类存款基准利率计算。  



**Article 25** Where a contract is not formed, void, revoked, or determined to be ineffective, and a party who has the right to request the return of the purchase price or remuneration requests the other party to pay a fee for the use of funds, the People's Court shall calculate such fee within the scope of the party's request based on the one-year loan prime rate (LPR) published by the National Interbank Funding Center authorized by the People's Bank of China. However, if the party occupying the funds is not at fault for the contract not being formed, being void, being revoked, or being determined to be ineffective, the calculation shall be based on the benchmark interest rate for deposits of the same type and same term published by the People's Bank of China. Where both parties owe obligations of return, and one party claims the simultaneous performance thereof, the People's Court shall support such claim. If the party in possession of the subject matter uses or is entitled to use the subject matter according to law, and the other party requests the offsetting of the fee for the use of funds payable by it against the fee for the use of the subject matter receivable by it, the People's Court shall support such claim, except where the law provides otherwise. IV. Performance of Contracts

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　当事人一方未根据法律规定或者合同约定履行开具发票、提供证明文件等非主要债务，对方请求继续履行该债务并赔偿因怠于履行该债务造成的损失的，人民法院依法予以支持；对方请求解除合同的，人民法院不予支持，但是不履行该债务致使不能实现合同目的或者当事人另有约定的除外。



**Article 26** Where one party fails to perform a non-principal obligation, such as issuing an invoice or providing supporting documents, as required by law or contract, and the other party requests the continued performance of such obligation and compensation for losses caused by the failure to perform, the People's Court shall support such a request according to law. If the other party requests the dissolution of the contract, the People's Court shall not support such a request, unless the failure to perform such obligation frustrates the purpose of the contract or the parties have otherwise agreed.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　债务人或者第三人与债权人在债务履行期限届满后达成以物抵债协议，不存在影响合同效力情形的，人民法院应当认定该协议自当事人意思表示一致时生效。  



**Article 27** Where a debtor or a third party reaches an agreement with a creditor to transfer property in satisfaction of a debt after the debt performance period has expired, and there is no circumstance affecting the validity of the contract, the People's Court shall determine that the agreement takes effect at the time when the parties' intentions are mutually consistent. After the debtor or third party performs the agreement to transfer property in satisfaction of the debt, the People's Court shall determine that the corresponding original debt is simultaneously extinguished. If the debtor or third party fails to perform the agreement to transfer property in satisfaction of the debt as agreed, and, after being demanded, still fails to perform within a reasonable period, and the creditor elects to request the performance of the original debt or the agreement to transfer property in satisfaction of the debt, the People's Court shall support such a request, unless otherwise provided by law or agreed by the parties. Where the agreement to transfer property in satisfaction of the debt as provided in the preceding paragraph is confirmed by the People's Court or a conciliation statement is prepared by the People's Court based on the agreement to transfer property in satisfaction of the debt reached by the parties, and the creditor claims that the property right is modified upon the taking effect of the confirmation document or conciliation statement or has the effect of opposing a bona fide third party, the People's Court shall not support such a claim. Where a debtor or third party enters into an agreement to transfer property in satisfaction of a debt using a property right over which they do not have ownership or the right of disposal, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Article 19 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　债务人或者第三人与债权人在债务履行期限届满前达成以物抵债协议的，人民法院应当在审理债权债务关系的基础上认定该协议的效力。  



**Article 28** Where a debtor or a third party reaches an agreement with a creditor to transfer property in satisfaction of a debt before the debt performance period expires, the People's Court shall determine the validity of the agreement on the basis of examining the relationship of the creditor's rights and debts. If the parties agree that, upon the debtor's failure to discharge the debt at maturity, the creditor may auction, sell, or discount the property in satisfaction of the debt to realize the claim, the People's Court shall determine that such agreement is valid. If the parties agree that, upon the debtor's failure to discharge the debt at maturity, the property in satisfaction of the debt shall vest in the creditor, the People's Court shall determine that such agreement is void, provided that this shall not affect the validity of other parts; where the creditor requests the auction, sale, or discount of the property in satisfaction of the debt to realize the claim, the People's Court shall support such a request. After the parties enter into an agreement to transfer property in satisfaction of the debt as provided in the preceding paragraph, if the debtor or third party has not transferred the property right to the creditor's name, and the creditor claims a priority right to be repaid, the People's Court shall not support such a claim. If the debtor or third party has transferred the property right to the creditor's name, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Article 68 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Security System under the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　[民法典第五百二十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-522)第二款规定的第三人请求债务人向自己履行债务的，人民法院应予支持；请求行使撤销权、解除权等民事权利的，人民法院不予支持，但是法律另有规定的除外。  



**Article 29** Where a third party as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 522 of the Civil Code requests the debtor to perform the obligation to itself, the People's Court shall support such a request. If the third party requests to exercise civil rights such as the right of revocation or the right of dissolution, the People's Court shall not support such a request, unless otherwise provided by law. Where the contract is revoked or dissolved according to law, and the debtor requests the creditor to return the property, the People's Court shall support such a request. Where the debtor performs the obligation to a third party as agreed, but the third party refuses to accept the performance, and the creditor requests the debtor to perform the obligation to itself, the People's Court shall support such a request, unless the debtor has already extinguished the obligation by means such as deposit. Where the third party refuses to accept the performance or delays in acceptance, and the debtor requests the creditor to compensate for the losses caused thereby, the People's Court shall support such a request according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　下列民事主体，人民法院可以认定为[民法典第五百二十四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-524)第一款规定的对履行债务具有合法利益的第三人：  



**Article 30** The People's Court may determine that the following civil subjects are third parties with a legitimate interest in performing the obligation as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 524 of the Civil Code: (1) a surety or a third party providing security with property; (2) the transferee, usufructuary, or lawful possessor of the secured property; (3) a subsequent-ranking security interest holder on the secured property; (4) a third party who has a legitimate right or interest in the debtor's property and will lose such right or interest as a result of compulsory enforcement against the property; (5) if the debtor is a legal person or an unincorporated organization, its contributor or founder; (6) if the debtor is a natural person, his or her close relatives; and (7) other third parties with a legitimate interest in performing the obligation. The third party shall acquire the creditor's claim against the debtor to the extent of the performance it has made on behalf of the debtor, provided that the interests of the creditor shall not be prejudiced. Where a security provider, after acquiring the creditor's claim by performing the obligation on behalf of the debtor, claims security rights against other security providers, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Articles 13, 14, and paragraph 2 of Article 18 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Security System under the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, among other provisions.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　当事人互负债务，一方以对方没有履行非主要债务为由拒绝履行自己的主要债务的，人民法院不予支持。但是，对方不履行非主要债务致使不能实现合同目的或者当事人另有约定的除外。  



**Article 31** Where the parties owe debts to each other, and one party refuses to perform its own principal debt on the ground that the other party has failed to perform a non-principal debt, the People's Court shall not uphold such refusal. However, this exception shall not apply where such failure to perform the non-principal debt prevents the purpose of the contract from being achieved, or unless otherwise agreed by the parties. Where one party files a lawsuit requesting the other party to perform its debt, and the defendant pleads a defense of simultaneous performance by both parties under Article 525 of the Civil Code and such defense is established, if the defendant does not file a counterclaim, the People's Court shall adjudicate that the defendant shall perform its own debt concurrently with the plaintiff's performance, and shall specify in the judgment that where the plaintiff applies for compulsory execution, the People's Court shall take enforcement measures against the defendant after the plaintiff performs its own debt. If the defendant files a counterclaim, the People's Court shall adjudicate that both parties shall perform their own debts concurrently, and shall specify in the judgment that where either party applies for compulsory execution, the People's Court shall take enforcement measures against the other party after that party performs its own debt. Where one party files a lawsuit requesting the other party to perform its debt, and the defendant pleads a defense, under Article 526 of the Civil Code, that the plaintiff shall perform first, and such defense is established, the People's Court shall dismiss the plaintiff's claims; however, this shall not affect the plaintiff's right to file another lawsuit after performing its debt.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　合同成立后，因政策调整或者市场供求关系异常变动等原因导致价格发生当事人在订立合同时无法预见的、不属于商业风险的涨跌，继续履行合同对于当事人一方明显不公平的，人民法院应当认定合同的基础条件发生了[民法典第五百三十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-533)第一款规定的“重大变化”。但是，合同涉及市场属性活跃、长期以来价格波动较大的大宗商品以及股票、期货等风险投资型金融产品的除外。  



**Article 32** After the formation of a contract, if the price fluctuates due to policy adjustments or abnormal changes in market supply and demand, resulting in a rise or fall that was unforeseeable at the time of contract formation and does not constitute a commercial risk, and continued performance of the contract is manifestly unfair to one party, the People's Court shall determine that a "material change" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 533 of the Civil Code has occurred in the basic conditions of the contract. However, this exception shall not apply to contracts involving commodities with active market attributes and historically large price fluctuations, or risk-investment financial products such as stocks and futures. Where the basic conditions of the contract have undergone a material change as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 533 of the Civil Code, and a party requests modification of the contract, the People's Court shall not rescind the contract. Where one party requests modification of the contract and the other party requests rescission, or where one party requests rescission and the other party requests modification, the People's Court shall, based on the actual circumstances of the case and in accordance with the principle of fairness, adjudicate whether to modify or rescind the contract. Where the People's Court adjudicates to modify or rescind a contract under Article 533 of the Civil Code, it shall comprehensively consider factors such as the time when the material change in the basic conditions of the contract occurred, the circumstances of the parties' renegotiation, and the losses caused to the parties by the modification or rescission, and shall specify the time of modification or rescission in the judgment. Where the parties agree in advance to exclude the application of Article 533 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall determine that such agreement is invalid. V. Preservation of Contracts

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　债务人不履行其对债权人的到期债务，又不以诉讼或者仲裁方式向相对人主张其享有的债权或者与该债权有关的从权利，致使债权人的到期债权未能实现的，人民法院可以认定为[民法典第五百三十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-535)规定的“债务人怠于行使其债权或者与该债权有关的从权利，影响债权人的到期债权实现”。



**Article 33** Where the debtor fails to perform its due debt to the creditor and does not assert against the counterparty, by means of litigation or arbitration, its own claim or the accessory rights related to such claim, thereby causing the creditor's due claim to remain unsatisfied, the People's Court may determine this as falling under the provision of Article 535 of the Civil Code that "the debtor is remiss in exercising its claim or the accessory rights related to such claim, affecting the satisfaction of the creditor's due claim."

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　下列权利，人民法院可以认定为[民法典第五百三十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-535)第一款规定的专属于债务人自身的权利：  



**Article 34** The People's Court may determine the following rights as rights exclusively belonging to the debtor himself provided in paragraph 1 of Article 535 of the Civil Code: (1) Claims for child support, alimony, or spousal support; (2) Claims for compensation for personal injury; (3) Claims for labor remuneration, except for the portion exceeding the necessary living expenses of the debtor and his dependents; (4) Rights to claim payment of basic endowment insurance, unemployment insurance, minimum subsistence allowance, and other rights guaranteeing the party's basic living needs; and (5) Other rights exclusively belonging to the debtor himself.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　债权人依据[民法典第五百三十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-535)的规定对债务人的相对人提起代位权诉讼的，由被告住所地人民法院管辖，但是依法应当适用专属管辖规定的除外。  



**Article 35** Where a creditor initiates a subrogation action against the debtor's counterparty under Article 535 of the Civil Code, such action shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court at the domicile of the defendant, unless the application of exclusive jurisdiction provisions is required by law.  
Where the debtor or the counterparty raises an objection on the ground that there is a jurisdiction agreement concerning the claim and debt relationship between them, the People's Court shall not uphold such an objection.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　债权人提起代位权诉讼后，债务人或者相对人以双方之间的债权债务关系订有仲裁协议为由对法院主管提出异议的，人民法院不予支持。但是，债务人或者相对人在首次开庭前就债务人与相对人之间的债权债务关系申请仲裁的，人民法院可以依法中止代位权诉讼。



**Article 36** After the creditor initiates a subrogation action, if the debtor or the counterparty raises an objection to the court's jurisdiction on the ground that there is an arbitration agreement concerning the claim and debt relationship between them, the People's Court shall not uphold such an objection. However, if the debtor or the counterparty applies for arbitration regarding the claim and debt relationship between the debtor and the counterparty before the first hearing, the People's Court may suspend the subrogation action in accordance with law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　债权人以债务人的相对人为被告向人民法院提起代位权诉讼，未将债务人列为第三人的，人民法院应当追加债务人为第三人。  



**Article 37** Where a creditor initiates a subrogation action in the People's Court with the debtor's counterparty as the defendant but does not name the debtor as a third party, the People's Court shall add the debtor as a third party. Where two or more creditors initiate subrogation actions against the same counterparty of the debtor as the defendant, the People's Court may consolidate the hearings. If the claim that the debtor has against the counterparty is insufficient to satisfy the debts owed to two or more creditors, the People's Court shall determine each counterparty's performance share in proportion to the claims held by the creditors, unless otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　债权人向人民法院起诉债务人后，又向同一人民法院对债务人的相对人提起代位权诉讼，属于该人民法院管辖的，可以合并审理。不属于该人民法院管辖的，应当告知其向有管辖权的人民法院另行起诉；在起诉债务人的诉讼终结前，代位权诉讼应当中止。



**Article 38** After a creditor files a lawsuit against the debtor with the People's Court and subsequently initiates a subrogation action against the debtor's counterparty with the same People's Court, if the matter falls within the jurisdiction of that court, the actions may be consolidated for hearing. If it does not fall within the jurisdiction of that court, the court shall inform the creditor to file a separate lawsuit with the competent People's Court. Before the conclusion of the lawsuit against the debtor, the subrogation action shall be suspended.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　在代位权诉讼中，债务人对超过债权人代位请求数额的债权部分起诉相对人，属于同一人民法院管辖的，可以合并审理。不属于同一人民法院管辖的，应当告知其向有管辖权的人民法院另行起诉；在代位权诉讼终结前，债务人对相对人的诉讼应当中止。



**Article 39** In a subrogation action, if the debtor initiates a lawsuit against the counterparty for the portion of the claim exceeding the amount claimed by the creditor in the subrogation action, and the matter falls within the jurisdiction of the same People's Court, the actions may be consolidated for hearing. If it does not fall within the jurisdiction of the same People's Court, the court shall inform the debtor to file a separate lawsuit with the competent People's Court. Before the conclusion of the subrogation action, the debtor's lawsuit against the counterparty shall be suspended.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　代位权诉讼中，人民法院经审理认为债权人的主张不符合代位权行使条件的，应当驳回诉讼请求，但是不影响债权人根据新的事实再次起诉。  



**Article 40** In a subrogation action, if the People's Court, upon trial, finds that the creditor's claim does not satisfy the conditions for exercising the subrogation right, it shall dismiss the claims; however, this shall not affect the creditor's right to file another lawsuit based on new facts.  
Where the debtor's counterparty contends that the creditor's lawsuit does not satisfy the conditions for exercising the subrogation right solely on the ground that the claim and debt relationship between the creditor and the debtor has not been confirmed by an effective legal instrument at the time of filing the subrogation action, the People's Court shall not uphold such a contention.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　债权人提起代位权诉讼后，债务人无正当理由减免相对人的债务或者延长相对人的履行期限，相对人以此向债权人抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。



**Article 41** After a creditor initiates a subrogation action, if the debtor reduces or exempts the counterparty's debt or extends the counterparty's performance period without justifiable cause, and the counterparty uses this as a defense against the creditor, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　对于[民法典第五百三十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-539)规定的“明显不合理”的低价或者高价，人民法院应当按照交易当地一般经营者的判断，并参考交易时交易地的市场交易价或者物价部门指导价予以认定。  



**Article 42** For the "obviously unreasonable" low or high price specified in Article 539 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall determine it according to the judgment of a general local business operator at the place of transaction and with reference to the market transaction price at the place of transaction at the time of transaction or the guidance price of the price authority. If the transfer price is less than 70% of the market transaction price or guidance price at the place of transaction at the time of transaction, it may generally be deemed an "obviously unreasonable low price"; if the acquisition price exceeds 30% of the market transaction price or guidance price at the place of transaction at the time of transaction, it may generally be deemed an "obviously unreasonable high price." If the debtor and the counterparty are related by kinship or affiliated relationship, the limits of 70% and 30% as provided in the preceding paragraph shall not apply.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　债务人以明显不合理的价格，实施互易财产、以物抵债、出租或者承租财产、知识产权许可使用等行为，影响债权人的债权实现，债务人的相对人知道或者应当知道该情形，债权人请求撤销债务人的行为的，人民法院应当依据[民法典第五百三十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-539)的规定予以支持。



**Article 43** If the debtor, at an obviously unreasonable price, engages in acts such as bartering property, using property to offset debts, leasing or renting property, or licensing intellectual property, thereby affecting the realization of the creditor's claim, and the debtor's counterparty knows or ought to know of such circumstance, where the creditor requests the revocation of the debtor's act, the People's Court shall support such request in accordance with Article 539 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　债权人依据[民法典第五百三十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-538)、第五百三十九条的规定提起撤销权诉讼的，应当以债务人和债务人的相对人为共同被告，由债务人或者相对人的住所地人民法院管辖，但是依法应当适用专属管辖规定的除外。  



**Article 44** Where a creditor initiates a revocation action in accordance with Articles 538 and 539 of the Civil Code, the debtor and the debtor's counterparty shall be named as co-defendants, and the action shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court at the domicile of the debtor or the counterparty, unless exclusive jurisdiction provisions shall apply according to law.  
If two or more creditors initiate revocation actions regarding the same act of the debtor, the People's Court may consolidate the trials.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　在债权人撤销权诉讼中，被撤销行为的标的可分，当事人主张在受影响的债权范围内撤销债务人的行为的，人民法院应予支持；被撤销行为的标的不可分，债权人主张将债务人的行为全部撤销的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 45** In a creditor's revocation action, if the subject matter of the act to be revoked is divisible and a party claims to revoke the debtor's act within the scope of the affected claim, the People's Court shall support such claim; if the subject matter of the act to be revoked is indivisible and the creditor claims to revoke the debtor's entire act, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
The reasonable attorney agency fees, travel expenses, and other costs paid by the creditor in exercising the right of revocation may be deemed the "necessary expenses" as specified in Article 540 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　债权人在撤销权诉讼中同时请求债务人的相对人向债务人承担返还财产、折价补偿、履行到期债务等法律后果的，人民法院依法予以支持。  



**Article 46** Where the creditor, in a revocation action, simultaneously requests that the debtor's counterparty assume legal consequences such as returning property, making compensation at a discounted price, or performing a matured debt to the debtor, the People's Court shall support such request according to law. If the creditor requests the People's Court accepting the revocation action to concurrently adjudicate the creditor-debtor relationship between the creditor and the debtor, and such court has jurisdiction over the matter, the trials may be consolidated; if such court does not have jurisdiction, it shall inform the creditor to file a separate action with the competent People's Court. If the creditor applies for compulsory enforcement based on an effective legal document resulting from the action between the creditor and the debtor or the revocation action, the People's Court may take compulsory enforcement measures against the rights that the debtor enjoys against the counterparty in order to realize the creditor's claim. If the creditor applies for preservation measures against the counterparty's property in the revocation action, the People's Court shall grant such application according to law. VI. Modification and Assignment of Contracts

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　债权转让后，债务人向受让人主张其对让与人的抗辩的，人民法院可以追加让与人为第三人。  



**Article 47** After the assignment of a claim, if the debtor asserts against the assignee a defense that it has against the assignor, the People's Court may add the assignor as a third party.  
After the transfer of a debt, if the new debtor asserts a defense that the original debtor has against the creditor, the People's Court may add the original debtor as a third party.  
After one party assigns both the rights and obligations under a contract as a whole, if the other party asserts a defense against the assignee regarding the contractual rights and obligations or the assignee asserts a defense against the other party regarding the contractual rights and obligations, the People's Court may add the assignor as a third party.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　债务人在接到债权转让通知前已经向让与人履行，受让人请求债务人履行的，人民法院不予支持；债务人接到债权转让通知后仍然向让与人履行，受让人请求债务人履行的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 48** If the debtor has performed to the assignor before receiving the notice of assignment of claim, and the assignee requests the debtor to perform, the People's Court shall not support such request; if the debtor still performs to the assignor after receiving the notice of assignment of claim, and the assignee requests the debtor to perform, the People's Court shall support such request. If the assignor fails to notify the debtor, and the assignee directly sues the debtor to request performance of the debt, and the People's Court, upon trial, confirms the fact of the assignment of claim, the assignment of claim shall be deemed to take effect against the debtor from the time the copy of the statement of claim is served. If the debtor claims that the increased costs or losses incurred due to the lack of notification shall be deducted from the determined amount of the claim, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　债务人接到债权转让通知后，让与人以债权转让合同不成立、无效、被撤销或者确定不发生效力为由请求债务人向其履行的，人民法院不予支持。但是，该债权转让通知被依法撤销的除外。  



**Article 49** After the debtor receives the notice of assignment of claim, if the assignor requests the debtor to perform to it on the grounds that the contract for assignment of claim is not established, void, revoked, or definitively ineffective, the People's Court shall not support such request, unless the notice of assignment of claim is revoked according to law.  
If the assignee accepts the assignment of claim based on the debtor's confirmation of the claim's existence, and the debtor later refuses to perform to the assignee on the grounds that the claim does not exist, the People's Court shall not support such refusal, unless the assignee knew or ought to have known that the claim did not exist.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　让与人将同一债权转让给两个以上受让人，债务人以已经向最先通知的受让人履行为由主张其不再履行债务的，人民法院应予支持。债务人明知接受履行的受让人不是最先通知的受让人，最先通知的受让人请求债务人继续履行债务或者依据债权转让协议请求让与人承担违约责任的，人民法院应予支持；最先通知的受让人请求接受履行的受让人返还其接受的财产的，人民法院不予支持，但是接受履行的受让人明知该债权在其受让前已经转让给其他受让人的除外。  



**Article 50** If the assignor assigns the same claim to two or more assignees, and the debtor claims that it no longer needs to perform the debt because it has already performed to the assignee who received the earliest notice, the People's Court shall support such claim. If the debtor knows that the assignee accepting performance is not the assignee who received the earliest notice, and the assignee who received the earliest notice requests the debtor to continue performing the debt or requests the assignor to bear liability for breach of contract based on the assignment agreement, the People's Court shall support such request; if the assignee who received the earliest notice requests the assignee who accepted performance to return the property it accepted, the People's Court shall not support such request, unless the assignee who accepted performance knew that the claim had already been assigned to another assignee before its assignment. The assignee who received the earliest notice as referred to in the preceding paragraph means the assignee named in the notice of assignment that first reaches the debtor. If the parties dispute the time of arrival of the notice, the People's Court shall make a comprehensive judgment based on factors such as the method of notice, and shall not determine it solely based on the time of notice acknowledged by the debtor or the time recorded in the notice. If a party issues a notice by mail, electronic communication systems, or other means, the People's Court shall use the postmark time, the time recorded by the electronic communication system, or other similar evidence as the basis for determining the time of arrival of the notice.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　第三人加入债务并与债务人约定了追偿权，其履行债务后主张向债务人追偿的，人民法院应予支持；没有约定追偿权，第三人依照民法典关于不当得利等的规定，在其已经向债权人履行债务的范围内请求债务人向其履行的，人民法院应予支持，但是第三人知道或者应当知道加入债务会损害债务人利益的除外。  



**Article 51** Where a third party joins the debt and agrees with the debtor on the right of recourse, and after performing the debt, claims recourse against the debtor, the People's Court shall support such claim. Where there is no agreement on the right of recourse, and the third party, in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Code on unjust enrichment and other relevant provisions, requests the debtor to perform within the scope of the debt that the third party has already performed to the creditor, the People's Court shall support such request, except where the third party knew or should have known that joining the debt would harm the debtor's interests. Where the debtor asserts against the third party who joined the debt a defense that the debtor has against the creditor, the People's Court shall support such assertion. VII. Termination of Rights and Obligations under a Contract

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　当事人就解除合同协商一致时未对合同解除后的违约责任、结算和清理等问题作出处理，一方主张合同已经解除的，人民法院应予支持。但是，当事人另有约定的除外。  



**Article 52** Where the parties, upon reaching consensus on the dissolution of a contract, fail to address issues such as liability for breach of contract, settlement, and liquidation after the dissolution, and one party claims that the contract has been dissolved, the People's Court shall support such claim, unless the parties have otherwise agreed. Under any of the following circumstances, unless one party expresses a contrary intention, the People's Court may determine that the contract is dissolved: (1) One party claims to exercise the right to dissolve the contract provided by law or agreed in the contract, but upon trial, it is determined that the conditions for exercising such right are not met, yet the other party agrees to the dissolution; (2) Neither party meets the conditions for exercising the right to dissolve the contract, but both parties claim the dissolution of the contract. In the circumstances specified in the preceding two paragraphs, the People's Court shall handle issues such as liability for breach of contract, settlement, and liquidation in accordance with Articles 566 and 567 of the Civil Code and the provisions on liability for breach of contract.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　当事人一方以通知方式解除合同，并以对方未在约定的异议期限或者其他合理期限内提出异议为由主张合同已经解除的，人民法院应当对其是否享有法律规定或者合同约定的解除权进行审查。经审查，享有解除权的，合同自通知到达对方时解除；不享有解除权的，不发生合同解除的效力。



**Article 53** Where one party dissolves a contract by notice and claims that the contract has been dissolved on the ground that the other party failed to raise an objection within the agreed objection period or any other reasonable period, the People's Court shall review whether such party has the right to dissolve the contract as provided by law or agreed in the contract. If, upon review, it is determined that such party has the right to dissolve, the contract shall be dissolved upon the notice reaching the other party; if such party does not have the right to dissolve, the dissolution of the contract shall have no legal effect.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　当事人一方未通知对方，直接以提起诉讼的方式主张解除合同，撤诉后再次起诉主张解除合同，人民法院经审理支持该主张的，合同自再次起诉的起诉状副本送达对方时解除。但是，当事人一方撤诉后又通知对方解除合同且该通知已经到达对方的除外。



**Article 54** Where one party, without notifying the other party, directly claims dissolution of the contract by filing a lawsuit, and after withdrawing the lawsuit, files another lawsuit claiming dissolution, and the People's Court, upon trial, supports such claim, the contract shall be dissolved upon service of the copy of the complaint of the subsequent lawsuit on the other party, except where the party, after withdrawing the lawsuit, notifies the other party of the dissolution and such notice has reached the other party.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　当事人一方依据[民法典第五百六十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-568)的规定主张抵销，人民法院经审理认为抵销权成立的，应当认定通知到达对方时双方互负的主债务、利息、违约金或者损害赔偿金等债务在同等数额内消灭。



**Article 55** Where one party claims set-off in accordance with Article 568 of the Civil Code, and the People's Court, upon trial, determines that the right to set-off is established, it shall determine that upon the notice reaching the other party, the principal debts, interest, liquidated damages, damages for harm, and other debts owed by both parties to each other shall be extinguished to the extent of the same amount.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　行使抵销权的一方负担的数项债务种类相同，但是享有的债权不足以抵销全部债务，当事人因抵销的顺序发生争议的，人民法院可以参照[民法典第五百六十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-560)的规定处理。  



**Article 56** Where the party exercising the right to set-off owes multiple debts of the same type but the creditor's right it holds is insufficient to set off all the debts, and the parties dispute the order of set-off, the People's Court may handle the matter by reference to Article 560 of the Civil Code.  
Where the party exercising the right to set-off holds a creditor's right that is insufficient to set off all the debts it owes, including the principal debt, interest, and expenses related to realizing the creditor's right, and the parties dispute the order of set-off, the People's Court may handle the matter by reference to Article 561 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　因侵害自然人人身权益，或者故意、重大过失侵害他人财产权益产生的损害赔偿债务，侵权人主张抵销的，人民法院不予支持。



**Article 57** Where a debt of damages for harm arises from infringement upon a natural person's personal rights and interests, or from intentional or grossly negligent infringement upon another person's property rights and interests, and the tortfeasor claims set-off, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　当事人互负债务，一方以其诉讼时效期间已经届满的债权通知对方主张抵销，对方提出诉讼时效抗辩的，人民法院对该抗辩应予支持。一方的债权诉讼时效期间已经届满，对方主张抵销的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 58** Where the parties owe debts to each other, and one party notifies the other party of set-off based on a creditor's right for which the statute of limitations has already expired, and the other party raises a statute of limitations defense, the People's Court shall support such defense. Where the statute of limitations for one party's creditor's right has already expired, and the other party claims set-off, the People's Court shall support such claim. VIII. Liability for Breach of Contract

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　当事人一方依据[民法典第五百八十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-580)第二款的规定请求终止合同权利义务关系的，人民法院一般应当以起诉状副本送达对方的时间作为合同权利义务关系终止的时间。根据案件的具体情况，以其他时间作为合同权利义务关系终止的时间更加符合公平原则和诚信原则的，人民法院可以以该时间作为合同权利义务关系终止的时间，但是应当在裁判文书中充分说明理由。



**Article 59** Where one party requests termination of the contractual rights and obligations relationship in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 580 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall generally take the time when the copy of the complaint is served on the other party as the time of termination of the contractual rights and obligations relationship. If, based on the specific circumstances of the case, taking another time as the time of termination of the contractual rights and obligations relationship better conforms to the principles of fairness and good faith, the People's Court may take such time as the time of termination, provided that it fully states the reasons in the written judgment.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　人民法院依据[民法典第五百八十四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-584)的规定确定合同履行后可以获得的利益时，可以在扣除非违约方为订立、履行合同支出的费用等合理成本后，按照非违约方能够获得的生产利润、经营利润或者转售利润等计算。  



**Article 60** Where the People's Court determines the benefits that could have been obtained after performance of a contract in accordance with Article 584 of the Civil Code, it may calculate such benefits based on the production profits, business profits, or resale profits that the non-breaching party could have obtained, after deducting reasonable costs such as expenses incurred by the non-breaching party for concluding and performing the contract.  
Where the non-breaching party lawfully exercises the right to dissolve the contract and carries out a substitute transaction, and claims that the benefits obtainable after performance of the contract shall be determined based on the difference between the price of the substitute transaction and the contract price, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law; where the price of the substitute transaction clearly deviates from the local market price at the time of the substitute transaction, and the breaching party claims that the benefits obtainable after performance of the contract shall be determined based on the difference between the market price and the contract price, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
Where the non-breaching party lawfully exercises the right to dissolve the contract but does not carry out a substitute transaction, and claims that the benefits obtainable after performance of the contract shall be determined based on the difference between the market price at the place of performance within a reasonable period after the occurrence of the breach and the contract price, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　在以持续履行的债务为内容的定期合同中，一方不履行支付价款、租金等金钱债务，对方请求解除合同，人民法院经审理认为合同应当依法解除的，可以根据当事人的主张，参考合同主体、交易类型、市场价格变化、剩余履行期限等因素确定非违约方寻找替代交易的合理期限，并按照该期限对应的价款、租金等扣除非违约方应当支付的相应履约成本确定合同履行后可以获得的利益。  



**Article 61** In a fixed-term contract with continuous performance of obligations as its content, if one party fails to perform monetary obligations such as payment of price or rent, and the other party requests rescission of the contract, and the People's Court, upon trial, finds that the contract shall be rescinded according to law, the court may, based on the parties' claims and with reference to factors such as the subject of the contract, type of transaction, changes in market price, and remaining term of performance, determine a reasonable period for the non-breaching party to seek an alternative transaction, and determine the benefits obtainable after performance of the contract by deducting the corresponding performance costs payable by the non-breaching party from the price or rent corresponding to such period.  
If the non-breaching party claims to determine the benefits obtainable after performance of the contract by deducting performance costs from the price or rent corresponding to the remaining term of performance after rescission, the People's Court shall not support such claim, except where the remaining term of performance is shorter than the reasonable period for seeking an alternative transaction.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　非违约方在合同履行后可以获得的利益难以根据[本解释第六十条](#art-60)、第六十一条的规定予以确定的，人民法院可以综合考虑违约方因违约获得的利益、违约方的过错程度、其他违约情节等因素，遵循公平原则和诚信原则确定。



**Article 62** Where the benefits obtainable by the non-breaching party after performance of the contract are difficult to determine in accordance with Articles 60 and 61 of this Interpretation, the People's Court may, by comprehensively considering factors such as the benefits gained by the breaching party from the breach, the degree of fault of the breaching party, and other circumstances of the breach, determine such benefits in accordance with the principles of fairness and good faith.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　在认定[民法典第五百八十四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-584)规定的“违约一方订立合同时预见到或者应当预见到的因违约可能造成的损失”时，人民法院应当根据当事人订立合同的目的，综合考虑合同主体、合同内容、交易类型、交易习惯、磋商过程等因素，按照与违约方处于相同或者类似情况的民事主体在订立合同时预见到或者应当预见到的损失予以确定。  



**Article 63** When determining the "losses that may be caused by the breach and that the breaching party foresaw or ought to have foreseen at the time of entering into the contract" as provided in Article 584 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall, based on the purpose of the parties in entering into the contract, comprehensively consider factors such as the subject of the contract, content of the contract, type of transaction, trade practices, and negotiation process, and determine the losses in accordance with what a civil subject in the same or similar circumstances as the breaching party would have foreseen or ought to have foreseen at the time of entering into the contract.  
In addition to the benefits obtainable after performance of the contract, if the non-breaching party claims that there are other losses caused by the breach, such as additional expenses to be incurred in bearing contractual liability to a third party, and requests compensation from the breaching party, and if upon trial it is found that such losses were foreseen or ought to have been foreseen by the breaching party at the time of entering into the contract, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
When determining the amount of compensation for losses from breach of contract, if the breaching party claims to deduct the expanded losses resulting from the non-breaching party's failure to take appropriate measures, the corresponding losses caused by the fault of the non-breaching party, or the additional benefits obtained or necessary expenses reduced by the non-breaching party due to the breach, the People's Court shall support such claim according to law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　当事人一方通过反诉或者抗辩的方式，请求调整违约金的，人民法院依法予以支持。  



**Article 64** If a party requests adjustment of liquidated damages by means of a counterclaim or defense, the People's Court shall support such request according to law. If the breaching party claims that the agreed liquidated damages are excessively higher than the losses caused by the breach and requests an appropriate reduction, it shall bear the burden of adducing evidence. If the non-breaching party claims that the agreed liquidated damages are reasonable, it shall also provide corresponding evidence. If a party merely claims that liquidated damages shall not be adjusted on the ground that the contract stipulates that liquidated damages may not be adjusted, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　当事人主张约定的违约金过分高于违约造成的损失，请求予以适当减少的，人民法院应当以[民法典第五百八十四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-584)规定的损失为基础，兼顾合同主体、交易类型、合同的履行情况、当事人的过错程度、履约背景等因素，遵循公平原则和诚信原则进行衡量，并作出裁判。  



**Article 65** If a party claims that the agreed liquidated damages are excessively higher than the losses caused by the breach and requests an appropriate reduction, the People's Court shall, based on the losses provided in Article 584 of the Civil Code, take into account factors such as the subject of the contract, type of transaction, performance of the contract, degree of fault of the parties, and context of performance, and make a judgment in accordance with the principles of fairness and good faith.  
If the agreed liquidated damages exceed 30% of the losses caused, the People's Court may generally deem them excessively higher than the losses caused.  
If a party that has maliciously breached the contract requests a reduction of liquidated damages, the People's Court shall generally not support such request.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　当事人一方请求对方支付违约金，对方以合同不成立、无效、被撤销、确定不发生效力、不构成违约或者非违约方不存在损失等为由抗辩，未主张调整过高的违约金的，人民法院应当就若不支持该抗辩，当事人是否请求调整违约金进行释明。第一审人民法院认为抗辩成立且未予释明，第二审人民法院认为应当判决支付违约金的，可以直接释明，并根据当事人的请求，在当事人就是否应当调整违约金充分举证、质证、辩论后，依法判决适当减少违约金。  



**Article 66** If one party requests payment of liquidated damages from the other party, and the other party defends on grounds such as the contract not being established, being void, being revoked, being determined not to have taken effect, not constituting a breach, or the non-breaching party not having suffered losses, and does not claim adjustment of excessively high liquidated damages, the People's Court shall explain that, should the defense not be sustained, the party may request adjustment of liquidated damages. If the People's Court of first instance finds the defense sustainable and fails to provide such explanation, and the People's Court of second instance finds that a judgment for payment of liquidated damages shall be rendered, the court of second instance may directly provide the explanation and, based on the party's request and after the parties have fully adduced evidence, cross-examined, and debated on whether the liquidated damages shall be adjusted, render a judgment for appropriate reduction of liquidated damages according to law. If the defendant fails to appear in court in the first instance proceedings due to objective reasons but appears in the second instance proceedings and requests a reduction of liquidated damages, the People's Court of second instance may, after the parties have fully adduced evidence, cross-examined, and debated on whether the liquidated damages shall be adjusted, render a judgment for appropriate reduction of liquidated damages according to law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　当事人交付留置金、担保金、保证金、订约金、押金或者订金等，但是没有约定定金性质，一方主张适用[民法典第五百八十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-587)规定的定金罚则的，人民法院不予支持。当事人约定了定金性质，但是未约定定金类型或者约定不明，一方主张为违约定金的，人民法院应予支持。  



**Article 67** If a party delivers a retention sum, guarantee deposit, security deposit, bargain money, earnest money, or subscription money, but the parties do not agree on the nature of such as earnest money, and one party claims application of the earnest money forfeiture rule provided in Article 587 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall not support such claim. If the parties agree on the nature of earnest money but do not agree on the type of earnest money or the agreement is unclear, and one party claims it is earnest money for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support such claim. If the parties agree on the delivery of earnest money as security for entering into a contract, and one party refuses to enter into the contract or, in violation of the principle of good faith during negotiations, fails to enter into the contract, and the other party claims application of the earnest money forfeiture rule provided in Article 587 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall support such claim. If the parties agree on the delivery of earnest money as a condition for the establishment or effectiveness of a contract, and the party obligated to deliver the earnest money fails to do so, but the main obligations of the contract have been performed and accepted by the other party, the People's Court shall find that the contract has been established or has taken effect at the time of such acceptance. If the parties agree that the nature of the earnest money is earnest money for dissolution of the contract, and the party that delivered the earnest money claims to dissolve the contract at the cost of forfeiting the earnest money, or the party that received the earnest money claims to dissolve the contract at the cost of returning twice the earnest money, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　双方当事人均具有致使不能实现合同目的的违约行为，其中一方请求适用定金罚则的，人民法院不予支持。当事人一方仅有轻微违约，对方具有致使不能实现合同目的的违约行为，轻微违约方主张适用定金罚则，对方以轻微违约方也构成违约为由抗辩的，人民法院对该抗辩不予支持。  



**Article 68** If both parties have committed breaches of contract that render the purpose of the contract unattainable, and one party requests application of the earnest money forfeiture rule, the People's Court shall not support such request. If one party has only a minor breach and the other party has a breach that renders the purpose of the contract unattainable, and the party with the minor breach claims application of the earnest money forfeiture rule, while the other party defends on the ground that the party with the minor breach also constitutes a breach, the People's Court shall not support such defense. If one party has partially performed the contract, and the other party accepts the performance and claims application of the earnest money forfeiture rule in proportion to the unperformed part, the People's Court shall support such claim. If the other party claims application of the earnest money forfeiture rule with respect to the contract as a whole, the People's Court shall not support such claim, except where the partial non-performance renders the purpose of the contract unattainable. If the contract cannot be performed due to force majeure, and the non-breaching party claims application of the earnest money forfeiture rule, the People's Court shall not support such claim. IX. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　本解释自2023年12月5日起施行。  



**Article 69** This Interpretation shall come into force as of December 5, 2023.  
For civil cases arising from legal facts occurring after the implementation of the Civil Code, if the final judgment has not been rendered after the implementation of this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall apply; for cases where the final judgment has been rendered before the implementation of this Interpretation and the parties apply for a retrial or a retrial is decided upon in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, this Interpretation shall not apply.
