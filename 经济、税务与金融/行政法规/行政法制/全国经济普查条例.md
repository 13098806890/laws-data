# 全国经济普查条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2018-08-11  
**生效日期**：2018-08-11

> (2004年9月5日中华人民共和国国务院令第415号公布　根据2018年8月11日《国务院关于修改〈全国经济普查条例〉的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了科学、有效地组织实施全国经济普查，保障经济普查数据的准确性和及时性，根据《中华人民共和国统计法》，制定本条例。 第二条　经济普查的目的，是为了全面掌握我国第二产业、第三产业的发展规模、结构和效益等情况，建立健全基本单位名录库及其数据库系统，为研究制定国民经济和社会发展规划，提高决策和管理水平奠定基础。 第三条　经济普查工作按照全国统一领导、部门分工协作、地方分级负责、各方共同参与的原则组织实施。 第四条　国家机关、社会团体、企业事业单位、其他组织和个体经营户应当依照《中华人民共和国统计法》和本条例的规定，积极参与并密切配合经济普查工作。 第五条　各级宣传部门应当充分利用报刊、广播、电视、互联网和户外广告等媒体，认真做好经济普查的社会宣传、动员工作。 第六条　经济普查所需经费，由中央和地方各级人民政府共同负担，并列入相应年度的财政预算，按时拨付，确保到位。 经济普查经费应当统一管理、专款专用，从严控制支出。 第七条　经济普查每5年进行一次，标准时点为普查年份的12月31日。 第二章　经济普查对象、范围和方法 第八条　经济普查对象是在中华人民共和国境内从事第二产业、第三产业活动的全部法人单位、产业活动单位和个体经营户。 第九条　经济普查对象有义务接受经济普查机构和经济普查人员依法进行的调查。 经济普查对象应当如实、按时填报经济普查表，不得虚报、瞒报、拒报和迟报经济普查数据。 经济普查对象应当按照经济普查机构和经济普查人员的要求，及时提供与经济普查有关的资料。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了科学、有效地组织实施全国经济普查，保障经济普查数据的准确性和及时性，根据《中华人民共和国统计法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of scientifically and effectively organizing and implementing the national economic census, and ensuring the accuracy and timeliness of the economic census data.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　经济普查的目的，是为了全面掌握我国第二产业、第三产业的发展规模、结构和效益等情况，建立健全基本单位名录库及其数据库系统，为研究制定国民经济和社会发展规划，提高决策和管理水平奠定基础。

**Article 2** The purpose of the economic census is to comprehensively grasp the development scale, structure and efficiency of the secondary and tertiary industries in China, to establish and improve the basic unit name list database and its database system, and to lay a foundation for researching and formulating national economic and social development plans, and improving the level of decision-making and management.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　经济普查工作按照全国统一领导、部门分工协作、地方分级负责、各方共同参与的原则组织实施。

**Article 3** The economic census work shall be organized and implemented in accordance with the principles of unified national leadership, departmental division of labor and cooperation, hierarchical responsibilities at local levels, and joint participation of all parties.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家机关、社会团体、企业事业单位、其他组织和个体经营户应当依照《中华人民共和国统计法》和本条例的规定，积极参与并密切配合经济普查工作。

**Article 4** State organs, social groups, enterprises, institutions, other organizations, and individually-owned businesses shall actively participate in and closely cooperate with the economic census work in accordance with the provisions of the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China and these Regulations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　各级宣传部门应当充分利用报刊、广播、电视、互联网和户外广告等媒体，认真做好经济普查的社会宣传、动员工作。

**Article 5** The publicity departments at all levels shall make full use of media such as newspapers, periodicals, radio, television, the internet, and outdoor advertising, and shall earnestly carry out social publicity and mobilization work for the economic census.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　经济普查所需经费，由中央和地方各级人民政府共同负担，并列入相应年度的财政预算，按时拨付，确保到位。  
经济普查经费应当统一管理、专款专用，从严控制支出。

**Article 6** The funds required for the economic census shall be borne jointly by the central and local people's governments at all levels, and shall be included in the fiscal budgets of the corresponding years, and shall be allocated in a timely manner to ensure that they are in place.  
The funds for the economic census shall be managed uniformly, used only for designated purposes, and expenditures shall be strictly controlled.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　经济普查每5年进行一次，标准时点为普查年份的12月31日。

**Article 7** The economic census shall be conducted once every five years, with the reference date being December 31 of the census year.

### 第二章　经济普查对象、范围和方法

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　经济普查对象是在中华人民共和国境内从事第二产业、第三产业活动的全部法人单位、产业活动单位和个体经营户。

**Article 8** The objects of the economic census shall be all legal persons, industrial activity units, and individually-owned businesses engaged in the activities of the secondary and tertiary industries within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　经济普查对象有义务接受经济普查机构和经济普查人员依法进行的调查。  
经济普查对象应当如实、按时填报经济普查表，不得虚报、瞒报、拒报和迟报经济普查数据。  
经济普查对象应当按照经济普查机构和经济普查人员的要求，及时提供与经济普查有关的资料。

**Article 9** The objects of the economic census shall have the obligation to accept the surveys conducted by the economic census institutions and economic census personnel according to law.  
The objects of the economic census shall truthfully and timely fill in the economic census forms, and shall not make false reports, conceal reports, refuse to report, or delay the reporting of economic census data.  
The objects of the economic census shall, in accordance with the requirements of the economic census institutions and economic census personnel, promptly provide materials related to the economic census.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　经济普查的行业范围为第二产业、第三产业所涵盖的行业，具体行业分类依照以国家标准形式公布的《国民经济行业分类》执行。

**Article 10** The scope of industries covered by the economic census shall be the industries covered by the secondary and tertiary industries, and the specific industry classification shall be implemented in accordance with the "Classification of National Economic Industries" published in the form of national standards.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　经济普查采用全面调查的方法，但对小微企业和个体经营户的生产经营情况等可以采用抽样调查的方法。  
经济普查应当充分利用行政记录等资料。

**Article 11** Economic censuses shall adopt the method of comprehensive investigation, but for the production and operation conditions of small and micro enterprises and individual businesses, the method of sampling survey may be adopted.  
Economic censuses shall make full use of administrative records and other data.

### 第三章　经济普查表式、主要内容和标准

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　经济普查按照对象的不同类型，设置法人单位调查表、产业活动单位调查表和个体经营户调查表。

**Article 12** Economic censuses shall, in accordance with the different types of subjects, establish questionnaire forms for legal person entities, industrial activity entities, and individual businesses.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　经济普查的主要内容包括：单位基本属性、从业人员、财务状况、生产经营情况、生产能力、原材料和能源消耗、科技活动情况等。

**Article 13** The main contents of economic censuses include: basic attributes of entities, employees, financial status, production and operation conditions, production capacity, consumption of raw materials and energy, science and technology activities, etc.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　经济普查采用国家规定的统计分类标准和目录。

**Article 14** Economic censuses shall adopt the statistical classification standards and catalogues prescribed by the State.

### 第四章　经济普查的组织实施

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院设立经济普查领导小组及其办公室。国务院经济普查领导小组负责经济普查的组织和实施。领导小组办公室设在国家统计局，具体负责经济普查的日常组织和协调。  
国务院各有关部门应当各负其责、密切配合，认真做好相关工作。

**Article 15** The State Council shall set up an Economic Census Leading Group and its office. The Economic Census Leading Group of the State Council shall be responsible for the organization and implementation of economic censuses. The office of the Leading Group shall be located in the National Bureau of Statistics and shall be specifically responsible for the daily organization and coordination of economic censuses.  
All relevant departments under the State Council shall each shoulder their respective responsibilities, closely cooperate, and conscientiously perform the relevant work.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　地方各级人民政府设立经济普查领导小组及其办公室，按照国务院经济普查领导小组及其办公室的统一规定和要求，具体组织实施当地的经济普查工作。  
街道办事处和居(村)民委员会应当广泛动员和组织社会力量积极参与并认真做好经济普查工作。

**Article 16** Local people's governments at all levels shall set up Economic Census Leading Groups and their offices, and shall specifically organize and implement the local economic census work in accordance with the unified provisions and requirements of the Economic Census Leading Group of the State Council and its office.  
Sub-district offices and residents' (villagers') committees shall extensively mobilize and organize social forces to actively participate in and conscientiously perform the economic census work.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院和地方各级人民政府有关部门设立经济普查机构，负责完成国务院和本级地方人民政府经济普查领导小组办公室指定的经济普查任务。

**Article 17** The relevant departments of the State Council and local people's governments at all levels shall set up economic census agencies, which shall be responsible for completing the economic census tasks assigned by the offices of the Economic Census Leading Groups of the State Council and the local people's governments at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　大型企业应当设立经济普查机构，负责本企业经济普查表的填报工作。其他各类法人单位应当指定相关人员负责本单位经济普查表的填报工作。

**Article 18** Large enterprises shall set up economic census agencies and shall be responsible for filling in their own economic census forms. Other types of legal person entities shall designate relevant personnel to be responsible for filling in the economic census forms of their own entities.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　地方各级经济普查机构应当根据工作需要，聘用或者从有关单位商调普查指导员和普查员。各有关单位应当积极推荐符合条件的人员担任普查指导员和普查员。  
普查指导员和普查员应当身体健康、责任心强并具有相应的专业知识。

**Article 19** Local economic census agencies at all levels shall, according to work needs, employ or second census supervisors and enumerators from relevant entities. All relevant entities shall actively recommend qualified personnel to serve as census supervisors and enumerators.  
Census supervisors and enumerators shall be in good health, have a strong sense of responsibility, and possess the relevant professional knowledge.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　聘用人员应当由当地经济普查机构支付劳动报酬。商调人员的工资由原单位支付，其福利待遇保持不变。

**Article 20** The labor remuneration for employed personnel shall be paid by the local economic census agency. The wages of seconded personnel shall be paid by their original entities, and their welfare benefits shall remain unchanged.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　地方各级经济普查机构应当统一对普查指导员和普查员进行业务培训，并经考核合格后颁发普查指导员证或者普查员证。普查指导员和普查员在执行经济普查任务时，应当主动出示证件。  
普查员负责组织指导经济普查对象填报经济普查表，普查指导员负责指导、检查普查员的工作。

**Article 21** Local economic census offices at all levels shall uniformly conduct professional training for census supervisors and enumerators, and shall issue census supervisor certificates or enumerator certificates after passing the assessment. Census supervisors and enumerators shall proactively present their certificates when performing economic census tasks.  
The enumerators shall be responsible for organizing and guiding economic census respondents to fill in the economic census forms, and the census supervisors shall be responsible for guiding and inspecting the work of the enumerators.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　普查指导员和普查员有权查阅法人单位、产业活动单位和个体经营户与经济普查有关的财务会计、统计和业务核算等相关原始资料及有关经营证件，有权要求经济普查对象改正其经济普查表中不确实的内容。

**Article 22** Census supervisors and enumerators may inspect the original materials such as financial accounting, statistics, and business accounting records of legal persons, industrial activity units, and individual businesses that are related to the economic census, as well as relevant business licenses, and may require economic census respondents to correct inaccurate content in their economic census forms.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　各级经济普查机构在经济普查准备阶段应当进行单位清查，准确界定经济普查表的种类。  
各级编制、民政、税务、市场监管以及其他具有单位设立审批、登记职能的部门，负责向同级经济普查机构提供其审批或者登记的单位资料，并共同做好单位清查工作。  
县级经济普查机构以本地区现有基本单位名录库为基础，结合有关部门提供的单位资料，按照经济普查小区逐一核实清查，形成经济普查单位名录。

**Article 23** Local economic census offices at all levels shall conduct unit inventory during the preparatory stage of the economic census and accurately define the types of economic census forms.  
The departments for organization, civil affairs, taxation, market regulation, and other departments with functions of approving or registering the establishment of units at all levels shall be responsible for providing the economic census offices at the same level with the unit information they have approved or registered, and shall jointly carry out the unit inventory work.  
County-level economic census offices shall, based on the existing basic unit database of their region, combined with the unit information provided by relevant departments, verify and inventory one by one according to the economic census enumeration areas, and form a list of units for the economic census.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　各级经济普查机构应当按照清查形成的单位名录，做好经济普查数据的采集、审核和上报等工作。  
法人单位填报法人单位调查表，并负责组织其下属的产业活动单位填报产业活动单位调查表。

**Article 24** Local economic census offices at all levels shall, in accordance with the list of units formed through inventory, properly carry out the collection, review, and reporting of economic census data.  
Legal persons shall fill in the legal person survey forms, and shall be responsible for organizing their subordinate industrial activity units to fill in the industrial activity unit survey forms.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　各级经济普查机构和经济普查人员依法独立行使调查、报告、监督的职权，任何单位和个人不得干涉。  
各地方、各部门、各单位的领导人对经济普查机构和经济普查人员依法提供的经济普查资料不得自行修改，不得强令或者授意经济普查机构、经济普查人员篡改经济普查资料或者编造虚假数据。

**Article 25** Local economic census offices and economic census personnel shall, according to law, independently exercise their functions and powers of investigation, reporting, and supervision, and no unit or individual may interfere.  
Leaders of all localities, departments, and units shall not modify on their own the economic census data provided according to law by the economic census offices and economic census personnel, and shall not force or instruct the economic census offices or economic census personnel to tamper with economic census data or fabricate false data.

### 第五章　数据处理和质量控制

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　经济普查的数据处理工作由县级以上各级经济普查机构组织实施。  
国务院经济普查领导小组办公室负责提供各地方使用的数据处理标准和程序。  
地方各级经济普查机构按照国务院经济普查领导小组办公室的统一要求和标准进行数据处理，并上报经济普查数据。

**Article 26** The data processing work of the economic census shall be organized and implemented by the economic census offices at and above the county level.  
The Office of the Leading Group for Economic Census of the State Council shall be responsible for providing the data processing standards and procedures to be used by all localities.  
Local economic census offices at all levels shall carry out data processing in accordance with the unified requirements and standards of the Office of the Leading Group for Economic Census of the State Council, and shall report the economic census data.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　经济普查数据处理结束后，各级经济普查机构应当做好数据备份和数据入库工作，建立健全基本单位名录库及其数据库系统，并强化日常管理和维护更新。

**Article 27** After the completion of economic census data processing, economic census offices at all levels shall properly perform data backup and data storage, establish and improve the basic unit database and its database system, and strengthen daily management, maintenance, and updating.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　地方各级经济普查机构应当根据国务院经济普查领导小组办公室的统一规定，建立经济普查数据质量控制岗位责任制，并对经济普查实施中的每个环节实行质量控制和检查验收。

**Article 28** Local economic census offices at all levels shall, in accordance with the unified provisions of the Office of the Leading Group for Economic Census of the State Council, establish a post responsibility system for quality control of economic census data, and implement quality control and inspection and acceptance for each link in the implementation of the economic census.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国务院经济普查领导小组办公室统一组织经济普查数据的质量抽查工作，抽查结果作为评估全国及各地区经济普查数据质量的主要依据。  
各级经济普查机构应当对经济普查的汇总数据进行认真分析和综合评估。

**Article 29** The Office of the Leading Group for Economic Census of the State Council shall uniformly organize the quality spot-check of economic census data, and the spot-check results shall serve as the main basis for evaluating the quality of economic census data nationwide and in various regions.  
Economic census offices at all levels shall conduct careful analysis and comprehensive evaluation of the aggregated economic census data.

### 第六章　数据公布、资料管理和开发应用

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　各级经济普查机构应当按照国家规定发布经济普查公报。  
地方各级经济普查机构发布经济普查公报应当经上一级经济普查机构核准。

**Article 30** Economic census offices at all levels shall issue economic census bulletins in accordance with national regulations.  
Local economic census offices at all levels shall publish economic census bulletins subject to the approval of the economic census office at the next higher level.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　各级经济普查机构应当认真做好经济普查资料的保存、管理和对社会公众提供服务等项工作，并对经济普查资料进行开发和应用。

**Article 31** Economic census authorities at all levels shall properly preserve and manage the economic census data, provide services to the public, and develop and utilize the economic census data.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　各级经济普查机构及其工作人员对在经济普查中所知悉的国家秘密和经济普查对象的商业秘密、个人信息，应当依法履行保密义务。

**Article 32** Economic census authorities at all levels and their staff shall, according to law, fulfill the obligation of confidentiality regarding the state secrets and the trade secrets and personal information of the economic census objects that they have learned during the economic census.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　经济普查取得的单位和个人资料，严格限定用于经济普查的目的，不作为任何单位对经济普查对象实施处罚的依据。

**Article 33** The data of units and individuals obtained from the economic census shall be strictly limited to the purposes of the economic census and shall not be used as the basis for any unit to impose penalties on the economic census objects.

### 第七章　表彰和处罚

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　对在经济普查工作中贡献突出的先进集体和先进个人，由各级经济普查机构给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 34** The advanced collectives and advanced individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the economic census work shall be commended and rewarded by the economic census authorities at all levels.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　地方、部门、单位的领导人自行修改经济普查资料、编造虚假数据或者强令、授意经济普查机构、经济普查人员篡改经济普查资料或者编造虚假数据的，依法给予处分，并由县级以上人民政府统计机构予以通报。  
经济普查人员参与篡改经济普查资料、编造虚假数据的，由县级以上人民政府统计机构责令改正，依法给予处分，或者建议有关部门、单位依法给予处分。

**Article 35** Where leaders of local authorities, departments or units modify economic census data on their own, fabricate false data, or order or instruct economic census authorities or economic census personnel to alter economic census data or fabricate false data, they shall be given sanctions according to law, and shall be circulated a notice of criticism by the statistical institutions of the people's governments at or above the county level.  
Where economic census personnel participate in altering economic census data or fabricating false data, the statistical institutions of the people's governments at or above the county level shall order them to make corrections, give sanctions according to law, or suggest that the relevant departments or units impose sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　经济普查对象(个体经营户除外)有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府统计机构责令改正，给予警告，可以予以通报；其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员属于国家工作人员的，依法给予处分：  
(一)拒绝或者妨碍接受经济普查机构、经济普查人员依法进行的调查的；  
(二)提供虚假或者不完整的经济普查资料的；  
(三)未按时提供与经济普查有关的资料，经催报后仍未提供的。  
企业事业单位或者其他组织有前款所列行为之一的，可以并处5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款。  
个体经营户有本条第一款所列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府统计机构责令改正，给予警告，可以并处1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 36** Where any economic census object (except for individually-owned businesses) commits one of the following acts, the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections, give a warning, and may circulate a notice of criticism; if the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons are public servants, they shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) refusing or obstructing the survey lawfully conducted by the economic census authority or economic census personnel;  
(2) providing false or incomplete economic census data;  
(3) failing to provide data related to the economic census in a timely manner, and still failing to provide after being urged.  
Any enterprise, public institution or other organization that commits one of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph may be concurrently fined not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be concurrently fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.  
Any individually-owned business that commits one of the acts listed in the first paragraph of this Article shall be ordered by the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level to make corrections, given a warning, and may be concurrently fined not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　各级经济普查机构应当设立举报电话，接受社会各界对经济普查中单位和个人违法行为的检举和监督，并对举报有功人员给予奖励。

**Article 37** Economic census authorities at all levels shall set up reporting telephone numbers to receive reports and supervision from all sectors of society on illegal acts committed by units and individuals in the economic census, and shall reward those who have rendered meritorious service in reporting.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 38** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
