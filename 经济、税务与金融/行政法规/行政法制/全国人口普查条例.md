# 全国人口普查条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2010-05-24  
**生效日期**：2010-06-01

> (2010年5月12日国务院第111次常务会议通过　2010年5月24日中华人民共和国国务院令第576号公布　自2010年6月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了科学、有效地组织实施全国人口普查，保障人口普查数据的真实性、准确性、完整性和及时性，根据《中华人民共和国统计法》，制定本条例。 第二条　人口普查的目的是全面掌握全国人口的基本情况，为研究制定人口政策和经济社会发展规划提供依据，为社会公众提供人口统计信息服务。 第三条　人口普查工作按照全国统一领导、部门分工协作、地方分级负责、各方共同参与的原则组织实施。 国务院统一领导全国人口普查工作，研究决定人口普查中的重大问题。地方各级人民政府按照国务院的统一规定和要求，领导本行政区域的人口普查工作。 在人口普查工作期间，各级人民政府设立由统计机构和有关部门组成的人口普查机构(以下简称普查机构)，负责人口普查的组织实施工作。 村民委员会、居民委员会应当协助所在地人民政府动员和组织社会力量，做好本区域的人口普查工作。 国家机关、社会团体、企业事业单位应当按照《中华人民共和国统计法》和本条例的规定，参与并配合人口普查工作。 第四条　人口普查对象应当按照《中华人民共和国统计法》和本条例的规定，真实、准确、完整、及时地提供人口普查所需的资料。 人口普查对象提供的资料，应当依法予以保密。 第五条　普查机构和普查机构工作人员、普查指导员、普查员(以下统称普查人员)依法独立行使调查、报告、监督的职权，任何单位和个人不得干涉。 地方各级人民政府、各部门、各单位及其负责人，不得自行修改普查机构和普查人员依法搜集、整理的人口普查资料，不得以任何方式要求普查机构和普查人员及其他单位和个人伪造、篡改人口普查资料，不得对依法履行职责或者拒绝、抵制人口普查违法行为的普查人员打击报复。 第六条　各级人民政府应当利用报刊、广播、电视、互联网和户外广告等媒介，开展人口普查的宣传动员工作。 第七条　人口普查所需经费，由国务院和地方各级人民政府共同负担，并列入相应年度的财政预算，按时拨付，确保足额到位。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了科学、有效地组织实施全国人口普查，保障人口普查数据的真实性、准确性、完整性和及时性，根据《中华人民共和国统计法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated pursuant to the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of organizing and implementing the national population census scientifically and effectively, and ensuring the authenticity, accuracy, completeness and timeliness of population census data.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人口普查的目的是全面掌握全国人口的基本情况，为研究制定人口政策和经济社会发展规划提供依据，为社会公众提供人口统计信息服务。

**Article 2** The purpose of a population census is to comprehensively grasp the basic situation of the national population, to provide a basis for studying and formulating population policies and economic and social development plans, and to provide population statistical information services to the public.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人口普查工作按照全国统一领导、部门分工协作、地方分级负责、各方共同参与的原则组织实施。  
国务院统一领导全国人口普查工作，研究决定人口普查中的重大问题。地方各级人民政府按照国务院的统一规定和要求，领导本行政区域的人口普查工作。  
在人口普查工作期间，各级人民政府设立由统计机构和有关部门组成的人口普查机构(以下简称普查机构)，负责人口普查的组织实施工作。  
村民委员会、居民委员会应当协助所在地人民政府动员和组织社会力量，做好本区域的人口普查工作。  
国家机关、社会团体、企业事业单位应当按照《中华人民共和国统计法》和本条例的规定，参与并配合人口普查工作。

**Article 3** Population census work shall be organized and implemented pursuant to the principles of unified leadership by the State Council, division and cooperation among departments, hierarchical responsibility by local governments, and participation by all parties.  
The State Council shall exercise unified leadership over the national population census work, and shall study and decide on major issues in the population census. Local people's governments at all levels shall lead the population census work within their respective administrative areas in accordance with the unified provisions and requirements of the State Council.  
During the period of population census work, people's governments at all levels shall establish population census agencies (hereinafter referred to as census agencies) composed of statistical institutions and relevant departments, which shall be responsible for the organization and implementation of the population census work.  
Villagers' committees and residents' committees shall assist the local people's government in mobilizing and organizing social forces to properly carry out the population census work within their areas.  
State organs, social organizations, enterprises and institutions shall participate in and cooperate with the population census work in accordance with the provisions of the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China and these Regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人口普查对象应当按照《中华人民共和国统计法》和本条例的规定，真实、准确、完整、及时地提供人口普查所需的资料。  
人口普查对象提供的资料，应当依法予以保密。

**Article 4** Population census respondents shall provide the required information for the population census truthfully, accurately, completely and timely in accordance with the provisions of the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China and these Regulations.  
The information provided by population census respondents shall be kept confidential according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　普查机构和普查机构工作人员、普查指导员、普查员(以下统称普查人员)依法独立行使调查、报告、监督的职权，任何单位和个人不得干涉。  
地方各级人民政府、各部门、各单位及其负责人，不得自行修改普查机构和普查人员依法搜集、整理的人口普查资料，不得以任何方式要求普查机构和普查人员及其他单位和个人伪造、篡改人口普查资料，不得对依法履行职责或者拒绝、抵制人口普查违法行为的普查人员打击报复。

**Article 5** Census agencies and their staff, census supervisors, and enumerators (hereinafter collectively referred to as census personnel) shall independently exercise their powers of investigation, reporting, and supervision according to law, and no unit or individual shall interfere.  
Local people's governments at all levels, all departments, all units and their responsible persons shall not modify on their own the population census data lawfully collected and compiled by census agencies and census personnel, shall not request in any manner the census agencies and census personnel or other units or individuals to fabricate or tamper with population census data, and shall not retaliate against census personnel who lawfully perform their duties or refuse or resist illegal acts in the population census.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各级人民政府应当利用报刊、广播、电视、互联网和户外广告等媒介，开展人口普查的宣传动员工作。

**Article 6** People's governments at all levels shall use media such as newspapers, radio, television, the Internet and outdoor advertisements to carry out publicity and mobilization work for the population census.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人口普查所需经费，由国务院和地方各级人民政府共同负担，并列入相应年度的财政预算，按时拨付，确保足额到位。  
人口普查经费应当统一管理、专款专用，从严控制支出。

**Article 7** The funds required for the population census shall be borne jointly by the State Council and local people's governments at all levels, and shall be included in the fiscal budgets of the corresponding years, disbursed on time, and ensured to be fully in place.  
The population census funds shall be managed uniformly, used exclusively for designated purposes, and expenditures shall be strictly controlled.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人口普查每10年进行一次，尾数逢0的年份为普查年度，标准时点为普查年度的11月1日零时。

**Article 8** A population census shall be conducted once every 10 years, and the year ending with the digit 0 is the census year, with the standard reference time being zero hour of November 1 of the census year.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家统计局会同国务院有关部门制定全国人口普查方案(以下简称普查方案)，报国务院批准。  
人口普查应当按照普查方案的规定执行。

**Article 9** The National Bureau of Statistics shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate the national population census plan (hereinafter referred to as the census plan) and submit it to the State Council for approval.  
The population census shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the census plan.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对认真执行本条例，忠于职守、坚持原则，做出显著成绩的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 10** Units and individuals that earnestly implement these Regulations, are devoted to their duties, adhere to principles, and achieve remarkable results shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant state provisions.

### 第二章　人口普查的对象、内容和方法

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人口普查对象是指普查标准时点在中华人民共和国境内的自然人以及在中华人民共和国境外但未定居的中国公民，不包括在中华人民共和国境内短期停留的境外人员。

**Article 11** Population census respondents refer to natural persons who are within the territory of the People's Republic of China at the standard reference time of the census, and Chinese citizens who are outside the territory of the People's Republic of China but have not settled down, excluding overseas personnel who are staying in the territory of the People's Republic of China for a short period.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人口普查主要调查人口和住户的基本情况，内容包括姓名、性别、年龄、民族、国籍、受教育程度、行业、职业、迁移流动、社会保障、婚姻、生育、死亡、住房情况等。

**Article 12** The population census mainly surveys the basic conditions of the population and households, the content of which includes name, sex, age, ethnicity, nationality, educational level, industry, occupation, migration, social security, marriage, fertility, death, housing situation, etc.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人口普查采用全面调查的方法，以户为单位进行登记。

**Article 13** The population census shall adopt the method of complete enumeration and shall conduct registration on a household basis.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人口普查采用国家统计分类标准。

**Article 14** The population census shall adopt the national statistical classification standards.

### 第三章　人口普查的组织实施

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人口普查登记前，公安机关应当按照普查方案的规定完成户口整顿工作，并将有关资料提交本级人口普查机构。

**Article 15** Before the population census registration, the public security organs shall complete the household registration rectification work in accordance with the provisions of the census plan, and shall submit the relevant materials to the population census agency at the same level.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人口普查登记前应当划分普查区，普查区以村民委员会、居民委员会所辖区域为基础划分，每个普查区划分为若干普查小区。

**Article 16** Before the population census registration, census areas shall be divided. The census areas shall be divided on the basis of the areas under the jurisdiction of villagers' committees and residents' committees, and each census area shall be divided into several census enumeration districts.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　每个普查小区应当至少有一名普查员，负责入户登记等普查工作。每个普查区应当至少有一名普查指导员，负责安排、指导、督促和检查普查员的工作，也可以直接进行入户登记。

**Article 17** Each census enumeration district shall have at least one enumerator responsible for tasks such as door-to-door registration in the census. Each census area shall have at least one census supervisor responsible for arranging, guiding, supervising and inspecting the work of enumerators, and may directly conduct door-to-door registration.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　普查指导员和普查员应当具有初中以上文化水平，身体健康，责任心强。

**Article 18** Census supervisors and enumerators shall have an educational level of junior high school or above, be in good health, and have a strong sense of responsibility.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　普查指导员和普查员可以从国家机关、社会团体、企业事业单位借调，也可以从村民委员会、居民委员会或者社会招聘。借调和招聘工作由县级人民政府负责。  
国家鼓励符合条件的公民作为志愿者参与人口普查工作。

**Article 19** Census supervisors and enumerators may be seconded from state organs, social organizations, enterprises and institutions, or may be recruited from villagers' committees, residents' committees or from society. The work of secondment and recruitment shall be the responsibility of the people's governments at the county level.  
The State encourages eligible citizens to participate in the population census work as volunteers.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　借调的普查指导员和普查员的工资由原单位支付，其福利待遇保持不变，并保留其原有工作岗位。  
招聘的普查指导员和普查员的劳动报酬，在人口普查经费中予以安排，由聘用单位支付。

**Article 20** The wages of seconded census supervisors and enumerators shall be paid by their original units, their welfare benefits shall remain unchanged, and their original job positions shall be retained.  
The labor remuneration of recruited census supervisors and enumerators shall be arranged in the population census funds and paid by the employing units.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　普查机构应当对普查指导员和普查员进行业务培训，并对考核合格的人员颁发全国统一的普查指导员证或者普查员证。  
普查指导员和普查员执行人口普查任务时，应当出示普查指导员证或者普查员证。

**Article 21** Census agencies shall provide professional training to census supervisors and enumerators, and shall issue the nationally unified census supervisor certificate or enumerator certificate to those who pass the assessment.  
When performing population census tasks, census supervisors and enumerators shall present their census supervisor certificate or enumerator certificate.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　人口普查登记前，普查指导员、普查员应当绘制普查小区图，编制普查小区户主姓名底册。

**Article 22** Before the population census registration, census supervisors and enumerators shall draw maps of the census enumeration districts and compile the basic list of household heads' names for the census enumeration districts.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　普查指导员、普查员入户登记时，应当向人口普查对象说明人口普查的目的、法律依据以及人口普查对象的权利和义务。

**Article 23** When conducting door-to-door registration, census supervisors and enumerators shall explain to the population census respondents the purpose and legal basis of the population census, as well as the rights and obligations of the population census respondents.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　人口普查对象应当按时提供人口普查所需的资料，如实回答相关问题，不得隐瞒有关情况，不得提供虚假信息，不得拒绝或者阻碍人口普查工作。

**Article 24** Population census respondents shall provide the required information for the population census on time, answer related questions truthfully, and shall not conceal relevant information, provide false information, or refuse or obstruct the population census work.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　人口普查对象应当在普查表上签字或者盖章确认，并对其内容的真实性负责。

**Article 25** Population census respondents shall sign or affix their seals on the census questionnaire to confirm, and shall be responsible for the authenticity of its contents.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　普查人员应当坚持实事求是，恪守职业道德，拒绝、抵制人口普查工作中的违法行为。  
普查机构和普查人员不得伪造、篡改普查资料，不得以任何方式要求任何单位和个人提供虚假的普查资料。

**Article 26** Census personnel shall adhere to the principle of seeking truth from facts, abide by professional ethics, and refuse and resist illegal acts in the population census work.  
Census agencies and census personnel shall not fabricate or tamper with census data, and shall not request in any manner any unit or individual to provide false census data.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　人口普查实行质量控制岗位责任制，普查机构应当对人口普查实施中的每个环节实行质量控制和检查，对人口普查数据进行审核、复查和验收。

**Article 27** A post responsibility system for quality control shall be implemented in the population census. Census agencies shall exercise quality control and inspection over each link in the implementation of the population census, and shall examine, re-check and accept the population census data.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家统计局统一组织人口普查数据的事后质量抽查工作。

**Article 28** The National Bureau of Statistics shall uniformly organize the post-census quality spot-check work of population census data.

### 第四章　人口普查资料的管理和公布

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　地方各级普查机构应当按照普查方案的规定进行数据处理，并按时上报人口普查资料。

**Article 29** Local census agencies at all levels shall carry out data processing in accordance with the provisions of the census plan, and shall report population census data on time.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　人口普查汇总资料，除依法应当保密的外，应当予以公布。  
全国和各省、自治区、直辖市主要人口普查数据，由国家统计局以公报形式公布。  
地方人民政府统计机构公布本行政区域主要人口普查数据，应当报经上一级人民政府统计机构核准。

**Article 30** The aggregated data of the population census shall be made public, except for those that shall be kept confidential according to law.  
The main population census data of the whole country and of each province, autonomous region, and municipality directly under the Central Government shall be published by the National Bureau of Statistics in the form of a communiqué.  
When local people's government statistical institutions publish the main population census data of their administrative areas, they shall submit to the statistical institution of the people's government at the next higher level for approval.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　各级人民政府统计机构应当做好人口普查资料的管理、开发和应用，为社会公众提供查询、咨询等服务。

**Article 31** Statistical institutions of people's governments at all levels shall properly manage, develop and utilize population census data, and provide services such as inquiry and consultation to the public.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　人口普查中获得的原始普查资料，按照国家有关规定保存、销毁。

**Article 32** The original census data obtained in the population census shall be preserved and destroyed in accordance with the relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　人口普查中获得的能够识别或者推断单个普查对象身份的资料，任何单位和个人不得对外提供、泄露，不得作为对人口普查对象作出具体行政行为的依据，不得用于人口普查以外的目的。  
人口普查数据不得作为对地方人民政府进行政绩考核和责任追究的依据。

**Article 33** No unit or individual shall provide or disclose to the outside, use as the basis for making specific administrative acts against population census respondents, or use for purposes other than the population census, any data obtained in the population census that can identify or infer the identity of a single census respondent.  
Population census data shall not be used as the basis for performance assessment and accountability of local people's governments.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　地方人民政府、政府统计机构或者有关部门、单位的负责人有下列行为之一的，由任免机关或者监察机关依法给予处分，并由县级以上人民政府统计机构予以通报；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)自行修改人口普查资料、编造虚假人口普查数据的；  
(二)要求有关单位和个人伪造、篡改人口普查资料的；  
(三)不按照国家有关规定保存、销毁人口普查资料的；  
(四)违法公布人口普查资料的；  
(五)对依法履行职责或者拒绝、抵制人口普查违法行为的普查人员打击报复的；  
(六)对本地方、本部门、本单位发生的严重人口普查违法行为失察的。

**Article 34** Where the responsible persons of a local people's government, a governmental statistical institution or a relevant department or unit commit any of the following acts, they shall be given sanctions by the appointing authority or the disciplinary inspection authority according to law, and shall be notified by the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) modifying on their own the population census data or fabricating false population census data;  
(2) requesting relevant units or individuals to fabricate or tamper with population census data;  
(3) failing to preserve or destroy population census data in accordance with the relevant state provisions;  
(4) illegally publishing population census data;  
(5) retaliating against census personnel who lawfully perform their duties or refuse or resist illegal acts in the population census;  
(6) failing to detect serious illegal acts in the population census occurring within their locality, department or unit.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　普查机构在组织实施人口普查活动中有下列违法行为之一的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府统计机构责令改正，予以通报；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，由任免机关或者监察机关依法给予处分：  
(一)不执行普查方案的；  
(二)伪造、篡改人口普查资料的；  
(三)要求人口普查对象提供不真实的人口普查资料的；  
(四)未按照普查方案的规定报送人口普查资料的；  
(五)违反国家有关规定，造成人口普查资料毁损、灭失的；  
(六)泄露或者向他人提供能够识别或者推断单个普查对象身份的资料的。  
普查人员有前款所列行为之一的，责令其停止执行人口普查任务，予以通报，依法给予处分。

**Article 35** Where a census agency commits any of the following illegal acts in organizing and implementing the population census, the people's government at the same level or the statistical institution of the people's government at a higher level shall order it to make corrections and issue a circular; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions by the appointing authority or the disciplinary inspection authority according to law:  
(1) failing to implement the census plan;  
(2) fabricating or tampering with population census data;  
(3) requesting population census respondents to provide untrue population census data;  
(4) failing to submit population census data in accordance with the provisions of the census plan;  
(5) violating the relevant state provisions, causing damage or loss of population census data;  
(6) disclosing or providing to others data that can identify or infer the identity of a single census respondent.  
Where census personnel commit any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, they shall be ordered to stop performing the population census tasks, be issued a circular, and be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　人口普查对象拒绝提供人口普查所需的资料，或者提供不真实、不完整的人口普查资料的，由县级以上人民政府统计机构责令改正，予以批评教育。  
人口普查对象阻碍普查机构和普查人员依法开展人口普查工作，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予处罚。

**Article 36** Where a population census respondent refuses to provide the required information for the population census, or provides untrue or incomplete population census data, the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections and give criticism and education.  
Where a population census respondent obstructs the census agency and census personnel from lawfully carrying out the population census work, which constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a penalty according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　县级以上人民政府统计机构应当设立举报电话和信箱，接受社会各界对人口普查违法行为的检举和监督。

**Article 37** Statistical institutions of people's governments at or above the county level shall set up reporting telephone numbers and mailboxes to receive reports and supervision from all sectors of society on illegal acts in the population census.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　中国人民解放军现役军人、人民武装警察等人员的普查内容和方法，由国家统计局会同国务院有关部门、军队有关部门规定。  
交通极为不便地区的人口普查登记的时间和方法，由国家统计局会同国务院有关部门规定。

**Article 38** The census content and methods for active servicemen of the Chinese People's Liberation Army, the People's Armed Police, and other personnel shall be prescribed by the National Bureau of Statistics together with the relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant military departments.  
The time and methods for population census registration in areas with extremely inconvenient transportation shall be prescribed by the National Bureau of Statistics together with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的人口数，按照香港特别行政区政府、澳门特别行政区政府公布的资料计算。  
台湾地区的人口数，按照台湾地区有关主管部门公布的资料计算。

**Article 39** The population figures of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region shall be calculated based on the data published by the governments of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region.  
The population figure of the Taiwan region shall be calculated based on the data published by the relevant competent authorities of the Taiwan region.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　为及时掌握人口发展变化情况，在两次人口普查之间进行全国1%人口抽样调查。全国1%人口抽样调查参照本条例执行。

**Article 40** For the purpose of timely understanding the changes in population development, a national 1% population sample survey shall be conducted between two population censuses. The national 1% population sample survey shall be carried out by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　本条例自2010年6月1日起施行。

**Article 41** These Regulations shall come into force as of June 1, 2010.
