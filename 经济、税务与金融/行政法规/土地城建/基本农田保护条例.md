# 基本农田保护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：1999-01-01

> (1998年12月27日中华人民共和国国务院令第257号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了对基本农田实行特殊保护，促进农业生产和社会经济的可持续发展，根据《中华人民共和国农业法》和《中华人民共和国土地管理法》，制定本条例。 第二条　国家实行基本农田保护制度。 本条例所称基本农田，是指按照一定时期人口和社会经济发展对农产品的需求，依据土地利用总体规划确定的不得占用的耕地。 本条例所称基本农田保护区，是指为对基本农田实行特殊保护而依据土地利用总体规划和依照法定程序确定的特定保护区域。 第三条　基本农田保护实行全面规划、合理利用、用养结合、严格保护的方针。 第四条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当将基本农田保护工作纳入国民经济和社会发展计划，作为政府领导任期目标责任制的一项内容，并由上一级人民政府监督实施。 第五条　任何单位和个人都有保护基本农田的义务，并有权检举、控告侵占、破坏基本农田和其他违反本条例的行为。 第六条　国务院土地行政主管部门和农业行政主管部门按照国务院规定的职责分工，依照本条例负责全国的基本农田保护管理工作。 县级以上地方各级人民政府土地行政主管部门和农业行政主管部门按照本级人民政府规定的职责分工，依照本条例负责本行政区域内的基本农田保护管理工作。 乡(镇)人民政府负责本行政区域内的基本农田保护管理工作。 第七条　国家对在基本农田保护工作中取得显著成绩的单位和个人，给予奖励。 第二章　划定 第八条　各级人民政府在编制土地利用总体规划时，应当将基本农田保护作为规划的一项内容，明确基本农田保护的布局安排、数量指标和质量要求。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了对基本农田实行特殊保护，促进农业生产和社会经济的可持续发展，根据《中华人民共和国农业法》和《中华人民共和国土地管理法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Agriculture Law of the People's Republic of China and the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of implementing special protection for basic farmland and promoting the sustainable development of agricultural production and social economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家实行基本农田保护制度。  
本条例所称基本农田，是指按照一定时期人口和社会经济发展对农产品的需求，依据土地利用总体规划确定的不得占用的耕地。  
本条例所称基本农田保护区，是指为对基本农田实行特殊保护而依据土地利用总体规划和依照法定程序确定的特定保护区域。

**Article 2** The State applies the basic farmland protection system.  
The term 'basic farmland' as used in these Regulations refers to cultivated land which, according to the demand for agricultural products at a certain period of population and socio-economic development, shall not be occupied as determined pursuant to the overall plan for land utilization.  
The term 'basic farmland protection area' as used in these Regulations refers to a specific protection area determined pursuant to the overall plan for land utilization and in accordance with legal procedures for implementing special protection for basic farmland.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　基本农田保护实行全面规划、合理利用、用养结合、严格保护的方针。

**Article 3** The protection of basic farmland shall implement the policy of comprehensive planning, rational utilization, combination of use and cultivation, and stringent protection.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当将基本农田保护工作纳入国民经济和社会发展计划，作为政府领导任期目标责任制的一项内容，并由上一级人民政府监督实施。

**Article 4** Local people's governments at and above the county level shall incorporate the work of basic farmland protection into the plans for national economic and social development, take it as a component of the responsibility system for target achievements during the tenure of government leaders, and the people's government at the next higher level shall supervise the implementation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　任何单位和个人都有保护基本农田的义务，并有权检举、控告侵占、破坏基本农田和其他违反本条例的行为。

**Article 5** Any unit or individual shall have the obligation to protect basic farmland and shall have the right to report and accuse any acts of encroaching upon or destroying basic farmland or other acts violating these Regulations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院土地行政主管部门和农业行政主管部门按照国务院规定的职责分工，依照本条例负责全国的基本农田保护管理工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府土地行政主管部门和农业行政主管部门按照本级人民政府规定的职责分工，依照本条例负责本行政区域内的基本农田保护管理工作。  
乡(镇)人民政府负责本行政区域内的基本农田保护管理工作。

**Article 6** The competent administrative department for land and the competent administrative department for agriculture under the State Council shall, in accordance with the division of functions and duties prescribed by the State Council and in accordance with these Regulations, be responsible for the protection and administration of basic farmland nationwide.  
The competent administrative departments for land and for agriculture under the local people's governments at and above the county level shall, in accordance with the division of functions and duties prescribed by the people's governments at the corresponding level and in accordance with these Regulations, be responsible for the protection and administration of basic farmland within their respective administrative areas.  
The people's government of a township (town) shall be responsible for the protection and administration of basic farmland within its administrative area.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对在基本农田保护工作中取得显著成绩的单位和个人，给予奖励。

**Article 7** The State shall give awards to units and individuals that achieve remarkable successes in the work of basic farmland protection.

### 第二章　划定

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　各级人民政府在编制土地利用总体规划时，应当将基本农田保护作为规划的一项内容，明确基本农田保护的布局安排、数量指标和质量要求。  
县级和乡(镇)土地利用总体规划应当确定基本农田保护区。

**Article 8** When formulating the overall plan for land utilization, people's governments at various levels shall take the protection of basic farmland as a component of the plan, and specify the layout arrangements, quantity indicators, and quality requirements for the protection of basic farmland.  
The overall plans for land utilization at the county level and township (town) level shall determine the basic farmland protection areas.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　省、自治区、直辖市划定的基本农田应当占本行政区域内耕地总面积的80%以上，具体数量指标根据全国土地利用总体规划逐级分解下达。

**Article 9** The basic farmland designated by provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall account for not less than 80% of the total area of cultivated land within their respective administrative areas, and the specific quantity indicators shall be decomposed and assigned level by level according to the national overall plan for land utilization.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　下列耕地应当划入基本农田保护区，严格管理：  
(一)经国务院有关主管部门或者县级以上地方人民政府批准确定的粮、棉、油生产基地内的耕地；  
(二)有良好的水利与水土保持设施的耕地，正在实施改造计划以及可以改造的中、低产田；  
(三)蔬菜生产基地；  
(四)农业科研、教学试验田。  
根据土地利用总体规划，铁路、公路等交通沿线，城市和村庄、集镇建设用地区周边的耕地，应当优先划入基本农田保护区；需要退耕还林、还牧、还湖的耕地，不应当划入基本农田保护区。

**Article 10** The following cultivated land shall be delimited into the basic farmland protection areas and be strictly administered:  
(1) Cultivated land within the production bases of grain, cotton, and oil crops that have been determined upon approval by the relevant competent department under the State Council or the local people's government at or above the county level;  
(2) Cultivated land with good water conservancy and water and soil conservation facilities, and medium and low yield fields that are undergoing improvement plans or can be improved;  
(3) Vegetable production bases;  
(4) Agricultural research and teaching experimental fields.  
Pursuant to the overall plan for land utilization, cultivated land along traffic lines such as railways and highways, and around the construction land areas of cities, villages, and market towns shall be prioritized for delimitation into basic farmland protection areas; cultivated land that needs to be returned to forests, pastures, or lakes shall not be delimited into basic farmland protection areas.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　基本农田保护区以乡(镇)为单位划区定界，由县级人民政府土地行政主管部门会同同级农业行政主管部门组织实施。  
划定的基本农田保护区，由县级人民政府设立保护标志，予以公告，由县级人民政府土地行政主管部门建立档案，并抄送同级农业行政主管部门。任何单位和个人不得破坏或者擅自改变基本农田保护区的保护标志。  
基本农田划区定界后，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府组织土地行政主管部门和农业行政主管部门验收确认，或者由省、自治区人民政府授权设区的市、自治州人民政府组织土地行政主管部门和农业行政主管部门验收确认。

**Article 11** The protection areas of primary farmland shall be delimited by taking the township (town) as a unit, and the competent administrative department of land of the people's government at the county level shall, jointly with the competent administrative department of agriculture at the same level, organize the implementation.  
The delimited protection areas of primary farmland shall be provided with protection signs by the people's government at the county level, which shall be announced, and the competent administrative department of land of the people's government at the county level shall establish archives and send copies to the competent administrative department of agriculture at the same level. No unit or individual may damage or alter the protection signs of the protection areas of primary farmland without authorization.  
After the delimitation of primary farmland, the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the competent administrative department of land and the competent administrative department of agriculture to verify and confirm, or the people's government of a province or autonomous region may authorize the people's government of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture to organize the competent administrative department of land and the competent administrative department of agriculture to verify and confirm.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　划定基本农田保护区时，不得改变土地承包者的承包经营权。

**Article 12** When delimiting the protection areas of primary farmland, the contractual management right of the land contractor shall not be altered.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　划定基本农田保护区的技术规程，由国务院土地行政主管部门会同国务院农业行政主管部门制定。

**Article 13** The technical regulations for delimiting the protection areas of primary farmland shall be formulated by the competent administrative department of land of the State Council jointly with the competent administrative department of agriculture of the State Council.

### 第三章　保护

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　地方各级人民政府应当采取措施，确保土地利用总体规划确定的本行政区域内基本农田的数量不减少。

**Article 14** Local people's governments at all levels shall take measures to ensure that the quantity of primary farmland within their respective administrative areas as determined in the general plan for land utilization shall not be reduced.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　基本农田保护区经依法划定后，任何单位和个人不得改变或者占用。国家能源、交通、水利、军事设施等重点建设项目选址确实无法避开基本农田保护区，需要占用基本农田，涉及农用地转用或者征收土地的，必须经国务院批准。

**Article 15** After the protection areas of primary farmland are delimited according to law, no unit or individual may alter or occupy them. Where the site selection of a key construction project of the State such as energy, transportation, water conservancy, or military facilities cannot indeed avoid the protection areas of primary farmland and it is necessary to occupy primary farmland, involving the conversion of agricultural land or expropriation of land, it must be subject to the approval of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　经国务院批准占用基本农田的，当地人民政府应当按照国务院的批准文件修改土地利用总体规划，并补充划入数量和质量相当的基本农田。占用单位应当按照占多少、垦多少的原则，负责开垦与所占基本农田的数量与质量相当的耕地；没有条件开垦或者开垦的耕地不符合要求的，应当按照省、自治区、直辖市的规定缴纳耕地开垦费，专款用于开垦新的耕地。  
占用基本农田的单位应当按照县级以上地方人民政府的要求，将所占用基本农田耕作层的土壤用于新开垦耕地、劣质地或者其他耕地的土壤改良。

**Article 16** Where the occupation of primary farmland is approved by the State Council, the local people's government shall, in accordance with the approval document of the State Council, amend the general plan for land utilization and supplement and delimit primary farmland of equivalent quantity and quality. The occupying unit shall, in accordance with the principle of reclaiming as much land as is occupied, be responsible for reclaiming cultivated land equivalent to the quantity and quality of the occupied primary farmland; where there is no condition for reclamation or the reclaimed cultivated land does not meet the requirements, it shall, in accordance with the provisions of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, pay a fee for the reclamation of cultivated land, which shall be used exclusively for reclaiming new cultivated land.  
The unit occupying primary farmland shall, as required by the local people's government at or above the county level, use the soil of the plough layer of the occupied primary farmland for the soil improvement of newly reclaimed cultivated land, inferior land, or other cultivated land.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　禁止任何单位和个人在基本农田保护区内建窑、建房、建坟、挖砂、采石、采矿、取土、堆放固体废弃物或者进行其他破坏基本农田的活动。  
禁止任何单位和个人占用基本农田发展林果业和挖塘养鱼。

**Article 17** No unit or individual may construct kilns, houses, or graves, excavate sand, quarry stones, mine, extract soil, pile up solid wastes, or engage in other activities destroying primary farmland within the protection areas of primary farmland.  
No unit or individual may occupy primary farmland to develop forestry and fruit industry or to dig ponds to breed fish.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　禁止任何单位和个人闲置、荒芜基本农田。经国务院批准的重点建设项目占用基本农田的，满1年不使用而又可以耕种并收获的，应当由原耕种该幅基本农田的集体或者个人恢复耕种，也可以由用地单位组织耕种；1年以上未动工建设的，应当按照省、自治区、直辖市的规定缴纳闲置费；连续2年未使用的，经国务院批准，由县级以上人民政府无偿收回用地单位的土地使用权；该幅土地原为农民集体所有的，应当交由原农村集体经济组织恢复耕种，重新划入基本农田保护区。  
承包经营基本农田的单位或者个人连续2年弃耕抛荒的，原发包单位应当终止承包合同，收回发包的基本农田。

**Article 18** No unit or individual may leave primary farmland unused or abandon it. Where a key construction project approved by the State Council occupies primary farmland and the land is not used for one full year and can still be cultivated and harvested, the original collective or individual that cultivated the primary farmland shall resume cultivation, or the land user unit may organize cultivation; where construction has not started for more than one year, an idle fee shall be paid in accordance with the provisions of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; where the land has not been used for two consecutive years, the land use right of the land user unit shall be taken back without compensation by the people's government at or above the county level upon approval by the State Council; if the land was originally owned by a peasant collective, it shall be handed over to the original rural collective economic organization for resumption of cultivation and shall be re-delimited into the protection areas of primary farmland.  
Where a unit or individual that contracts for the cultivation of primary farmland abandons the land and leaves it uncultivated for two consecutive years, the original contract-issuing unit shall terminate the contract and take back the contracted primary farmland.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家提倡和鼓励农业生产者对其经营的基本农田施用有机肥料，合理施用化肥和农药。利用基本农田从事农业生产的单位和个人应当保持和培肥地力。

**Article 19** The State advocates and encourages agricultural producers to apply organic fertilizers to the primary farmland under their management and to apply chemical fertilizers and pesticides rationally. Units and individuals engaging in agricultural production using primary farmland shall maintain and enhance soil fertility.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级人民政府应当根据当地实际情况制定基本农田地力分等定级办法，由农业行政主管部门会同土地行政主管部门组织实施，对基本农田地力分等定级，并建立档案。

**Article 20** The people's government at the county level shall, in light of the actual local conditions, formulate measures for grading and classifying the soil fertility of primary farmland, which shall be implemented by the competent administrative department of agriculture jointly with the competent administrative department of land, so as to grade and classify the soil fertility of primary farmland and establish archives.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　农村集体经济组织或者村民委员会应当定期评定基本农田地力等级。

**Article 21** The rural collective economic organizations or villagers committees shall periodically assess the fertility grade of basic farmland.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级以上地方各级人民政府农业行政主管部门应当逐步建立基本农田地力与施肥效益长期定位监测网点，定期向本级人民政府提出基本农田地力变化状况报告以及相应的地力保护措施，并为农业生产者提供施肥指导服务。

**Article 22** The competent administrative departments for agriculture of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall gradually establish long-term fixed monitoring networks for the fertility of basic farmland and the effectiveness of fertilization, regularly submit reports on the changes in basic farmland fertility and corresponding soil protection measures to the people's government at the same level, and provide guidance services on fertilization for agricultural producers.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　县级以上人民政府农业行政主管部门应当会同同级环境保护行政主管部门对基本农田环境污染进行监测和评价，并定期向本级人民政府提出环境质量与发展趋势的报告。

**Article 23** The competent administrative departments for agriculture of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with the competent administrative departments for environmental protection at the same level, monitor and assess the environmental pollution of basic farmland, and regularly submit reports on environmental quality and development trends to the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　经国务院批准占用基本农田兴建国家重点建设项目的，必须遵守国家有关建设项目环境保护管理的规定。在建设项目环境影响报告书中，应当有基本农田环境保护方案。

**Article 24** Where the occupation of basic farmland for the construction of key national construction projects has been approved by the State Council, the provisions of the State on environmental protection management for construction projects must be complied with. The environmental impact report on a construction project shall include a plan for the environmental protection of basic farmland.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　向基本农田保护区提供肥料和作为肥料的城市垃圾、污泥的，应当符合国家有关标准。

**Article 25** Fertilizers and urban waste, sludge used as fertilizers supplied to basic farmland protection areas shall comply with the relevant national standards.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　因发生事故或者其他突然性事件，造成或者可能造成基本农田环境污染事故的，当事人必须立即采取措施处理，并向当地环境保护行政主管部门和农业行政主管部门报告，接受调查处理。

**Article 26** Where an accident or other sudden event causes or may cause environmental pollution of basic farmland, the parties must immediately take measures to deal with the situation, report to the local competent administrative departments for environmental protection and the competent administrative departments for agriculture, and accept investigation and handling.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　在建立基本农田保护区的地方，县级以上地方人民政府应当与下一级人民政府签订基本农田保护责任书；乡(镇)人民政府应当根据与县级人民政府签订的基本农田保护责任书的要求，与农村集体经济组织或者村民委员会签订基本农田保护责任书。  
基本农田保护责任书应当包括下列内容：  
(一)基本农田的范围、面积、地块；  
(二)基本农田的地力等级；  
(三)保护措施；  
(四)当事人的权利与义务；  
(五)奖励与处罚。

**Article 27** In localities where basic farmland protection areas have been established, the local people's governments at or above the county level shall sign a basic farmland protection responsibility agreement with the people's governments at the next lower level; the people's governments of townships (towns) shall, in accordance with the requirements of the basic farmland protection responsibility agreement signed with the people's government at the county level, sign basic farmland protection responsibility agreements with rural collective economic organizations or villagers committees.  
A basic farmland protection responsibility agreement shall include the following contents:  
(1) the scope, area, and plots of basic farmland;  
(2) the fertility grade of basic farmland;  
(3) protection measures;  
(4) the rights and obligations of the parties;  
(5) rewards and penalties.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县级以上地方人民政府应当建立基本农田保护监督检查制度，定期组织土地行政主管部门、农业行政主管部门以及其他有关部门对基本农田保护情况进行检查，将检查情况书面报告上一级人民政府。被检查的单位和个人应当如实提供有关情况和资料，不得拒绝。

**Article 28** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a supervision and inspection system for basic farmland protection, periodically organize the competent administrative departments for land, the competent administrative departments for agriculture, and other relevant departments to inspect the implementation of basic farmland protection, and report the inspection results in writing to the people's government at the next higher level. The entities and individuals under inspection shall truthfully provide relevant information and materials and shall not refuse to do so.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上地方人民政府土地行政主管部门、农业行政主管部门对本行政区域内发生的破坏基本农田的行为，有权责令纠正。

**Article 29** The competent administrative departments for land and the competent administrative departments for agriculture of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall have the power to order the rectification of any acts that damage basic farmland within their respective administrative areas.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，依照《中华人民共和国土地管理法》和《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》的有关规定，从重给予处罚：  
(一)未经批准或者采取欺骗手段骗取批准，非法占用基本农田的；  
(二)超过批准数量，非法占用基本农田的；  
(三)非法批准占用基本农田的；  
(四)买卖或者以其他形式非法转让基本农田的。

**Article 30** Any person who, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts shall be given a heavier punishment in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China and the Regulations for the Implementation of the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) illegally occupying basic farmland without approval or by obtaining approval through fraudulent means;  
(2) illegally occupying basic farmland in excess of the approved amount;  
(3) illegally approving the occupation of basic farmland;  
(4) buying, selling, or illegally transferring basic farmland in any other form.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　违反本条例规定，应当将耕地划入基本农田保护区而不划入的，由上一级人民政府责令限期改正；拒不改正的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分或者纪律处分。

**Article 31** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, fails to delimit cultivated land that shall be delimited into the basic farmland protection areas, the people's government at the next higher level shall order him to make corrections within a specified period; if he refuses to make corrections, the directly responsible person in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　违反本条例规定，破坏或者擅自改变基本农田保护区标志的，由县级以上地方人民政府土地行政主管部门或者农业行政主管部门责令恢复原状，可以处1000元以下罚款。

**Article 32** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, damages or alters without authorization the markers of basic farmland protection areas, the competent administrative department of land or the competent administrative department of agriculture of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order him to restore the markers to their original condition, and may impose a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违反本条例规定，占用基本农田建窑、建房、建坟、挖砂、采石、采矿、取土、堆放固体废弃物或者从事其他活动破坏基本农田，毁坏种植条件的，由县级以上人民政府土地行政主管部门责令改正或者治理，恢复原种植条件，处占用基本农田的耕地开垦费1倍以上2倍以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 33** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, occupies basic farmland to construct kilns, houses, graves, excavate sand, quarry, mine, extract soil, pile solid wastes, or engages in other activities that destroy basic farmland and damage the cultivation conditions, the competent administrative department of land of the people's government at or above the county level shall order him to make corrections or carry out remediation, restore the original cultivation conditions, and impose a fine of not less than one time and not more than two times the amount of the land reclamation fee for the occupied basic farmland; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　侵占、挪用基本农田的耕地开垦费，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分或者纪律处分。

**Article 34** Where anyone embezzles or misappropriates the land reclamation fee for basic farmland, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions shall be given according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据当地实际情况，将其他农业生产用地划为保护区。保护区内的其他农业生产用地的保护和管理，可以参照本条例执行。

**Article 35** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on the local actual conditions, delimit other agricultural production land as protected areas. The protection and management of other agricultural production land within such protected areas may be carried out by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本条例自1999年1月1日起施行。1994年8月18日国务院发布的《基本农田保护条例》同时废止。

**Article 36** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 1999. The Regulations on the Protection of Basic Farmland promulgated by the State Council on August 18, 1994 shall be abolished simultaneously.
