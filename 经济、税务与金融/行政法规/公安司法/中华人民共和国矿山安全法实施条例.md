### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国矿山安全法》(以下简称《矿山安全法》)，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Mine Safety Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Mine Safety Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　《矿山安全法》及本条例中下列用语的含义：  
矿山，是指在依法批准的矿区范围内从事矿产资源开采活动的场所及其附属设施。  
矿产资源开采活动，是指在依法批准的矿区范围内从事矿产资源勘探和矿山建设、生产、闭坑及有关活动。

**Article 2** For the purposes of the Mine Safety Law and these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:  
Mine means the site and its auxiliary facilities where mineral resources are exploited within a lawfully approved mining area.  
Mineral resources exploitation activities mean the exploration of mineral resources, construction, production, closure of mines and related activities carried out within a lawfully approved mining area.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家采取政策和措施，支持发展矿山安全教育，鼓励矿山安全开采技术、安全管理方法、安全设备与仪器的研究和推广，促进矿山安全科学技术进步。

**Article 3** The State shall adopt policies and measures to support the development of mine safety education, encourage the research and promotion of mine safe mining technologies, safety management methods, safety equipment and instruments, and promote progress in mine safety science and technology.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级人民政府、政府有关部门或者企业事业单位对有下列情形之一的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予奖励：  
(一)在矿山安全管理和监督工作中，忠于职守，作出显著成绩的；  
(二)防止矿山事故或者抢险救护有功的；  
(三)在推广矿山安全技术、改进矿山安全设施方面，作出显著成绩的；  
(四)在矿山安全生产方面提出合理化建议，效果显著的；  
(五)在改善矿山劳动条件或者预防矿山事故方面有发明创造和科研成果，效果显著的。

**Article 4** The people's governments at all levels, relevant government departments or enterprises and institutions shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, give rewards to units and individuals that fall under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) having performed their duties faithfully and achieved remarkable results in mine safety management and supervision;  
(2) having rendered meritorious service in preventing mine accidents or in emergency rescue and relief;  
(3) having made remarkable achievements in promoting mine safety technologies or improving mine safety facilities;  
(4) having put forward rational proposals on mine safety production with remarkable effects;  
(5) having made inventions, creations or scientific research achievements with remarkable effects in improving mine working conditions or preventing mine accidents.

### 第二章　矿山建设的安全保障

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　矿山设计使用的地质勘探报告书，应当包括下列技术资料：  
(一)较大的断层、破碎带、滑坡、泥石流的性质和规模；  
(二)含水层(包括溶洞)和隔水层的岩性、层厚、产状，含水层之间、地面水和地下水之间的水力联系，地下水的潜水位、水质、水量和流向，地面水流系统和有关水利工程的疏水能力以及当地历年降水量和最高洪水位；  
(三)矿山设计范围内原有小窑、老窑的分布范围、开采深度和积水情况；  
(四)沼气、二氧化碳赋存情况，矿物自然发火和矿尘爆炸的可能性；  
(五)对人体有害的矿物组份、含量和变化规律，勘探区至少一年的天然放射性本底数据；  
(六)地温异常和热水矿区的岩石热导率、地温梯度、热水来源、水温、水压和水量，以及圈定的热害区范围；  
(七)工业、生活用水的水源和水质；  
(八)钻孔封孔资料；  
(九)矿山设计需要的其他资料。

**Article 5** The geological exploration report used for mine design shall include the following technical data:  
(1) the nature and scale of major faults, fracture zones, landslides, and debris flows;  
(2) the lithology, thickness, and attitude of aquifers (including karst caves) and aquicludes, the hydraulic connection between aquifers and between surface water and groundwater, the phreatic water level, water quality, water volume, and flow direction of groundwater, the surface water flow system and the drainage capacity of relevant water conservancy projects, as well as the local annual precipitation and maximum flood level over the years;  
(3) the distribution range, mining depth, and water accumulation conditions of existing small pits and abandoned pits within the mine design area;  
(4) the occurrence of methane and carbon dioxide, and the possibility of spontaneous combustion of minerals and mineral dust explosion;  
(5) the mineral components harmful to human body, their contents and variation patterns, and the natural radioactive background data of the exploration area for at least one year;  
(6) the rock thermal conductivity, geothermal gradient, source of hot water, water temperature, water pressure, and water volume in areas with abnormal ground temperature and hot water mines, as well as the delineated range of heat hazard areas;  
(7) the water sources and water quality for industrial and domestic use;  
(8) borehole plugging data;  
(9) other data required for mine design.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　编制矿山建设项目的可行性研究报告和总体设计，应当对矿山开采的安全条件进行论证。  
矿山建设项目的初步设计，应当编制安全专篇。安全专篇的编写要求，由国务院劳动行政主管部门规定。

**Article 6** When preparing the feasibility study report and overall design of a mine construction project, the safety conditions for mining shall be demonstrated.  
The preliminary design of a mine construction project shall include a safety-specific chapter. The requirements for the preparation of the safety-specific chapter shall be prescribed by the competent administrative department for labour under the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　根据[《矿山安全法》第八条](../../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国矿山安全法.md#art-8)的规定，矿山建设单位在向管理矿山企业的主管部门报送审批矿山建设工程安全设施设计文件时，应当同时报送劳动行政主管部门审查；没有劳动行政主管部门的审查意见，管理矿山企业的主管部门不得批准。  
经批准的矿山建设工程安全设施设计需要修改时，应当征求原参加审查的劳动行政主管部门的意见。

**Article 7** In accordance with the provisions of Article 8 of the Mine Safety Law, when a mine construction unit submits the design documents for safety facilities of a mine construction project to the competent authority in charge of mining enterprises for examination and approval, it shall simultaneously submit the same to the labour administrative department for review; without the review opinions of the labour administrative department, the competent authority in charge of mining enterprises shall not grant approval.  
When the approved design of safety facilities for a mine construction project needs to be modified, the opinions of the labour administrative department that originally participated in the review shall be sought.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　矿山建设工程应当按照经批准的设计文件施工，保证施工质量；工程竣工后，应当按照国家有关规定申请验收。  
建设单位应当在验收前60日向管理矿山企业的主管部门、劳动行政主管部门报送矿山建设工程安全设施施工、竣工情况的综合报告。

**Article 8** A mine construction project shall be constructed in accordance with the approved design documents, and the construction quality shall be ensured; after the completion of the project, application for acceptance inspection shall be made in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The construction unit shall, 60 days before the acceptance inspection, submit a comprehensive report on the construction and completion of the safety facilities of the mine construction project to the competent authority in charge of mining enterprises and the labour administrative department.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　管理矿山企业的主管部门、劳动行政主管部门应当自收到建设单位报送的矿山建设工程安全设施施工、竣工情况的综合报告之日起30日内，对矿山建设工程的安全设施进行检查；不符合矿山安全规程、行业技术规范的，不得验收，不得投入生产或者使用。

**Article 9** The competent authority in charge of mining enterprises and the labour administrative department shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the comprehensive report on the construction and completion of the safety facilities of the mine construction project submitted by the construction unit, inspect the safety facilities of the mine construction project; if they do not conform to the mine safety regulations and industry technical specifications, they shall not be accepted, and the project shall not be put into production or use.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　矿山应当有保障安全生产、预防事故和职业危害的安全设施，并符合下列基本要求：  
(一)每个矿井至少有两个独立的能行人的直达地面的安全出口。矿井的每个生产水平(中段)和各个采区(盘区)至少有两个能行人的安全出口，并与直达地面的出口相通。  
(二)每个矿井有独立的采用机械通风的通风系统，保证井下作业场所有足够的风量；但是，小型非沼气矿井在保证井下作业场所所需风量的前提下，可以采用自然通风。  
(三)井巷断面能满足行人、运输、通风和安全设施、设备的安装、维修及施工需要。  
(四)井巷支护和采场顶板管理能保证作业场所的安全。  
(五)相邻矿井之间、矿井与露天矿之间、矿井与老窑之间留有足够的安全隔离矿柱。矿山井巷布置留有足够的保障井上和井下安全的矿柱或者岩柱。  
(六)露天矿山的阶段高度、平台宽度和边坡角能满足安全作业和边坡稳定的需要。船采沙矿的采池边界与地面建筑物、设备之间有足够的安全距离。  
(七)有地面和井下的防水、排水系统，有防止地表水泄入井下和露天采场的措施。  
(八)溜矿井有防止和处理堵塞的安全措施。  
(九)有自然发火可能性的矿井，主要运输巷道布置在岩层或者不易自然发火的矿层内，并采用预防性灌浆或者其他有效的预防自然发火的措施。  
(十)矿山地面消防设施符合国家有关消防的规定。矿井有防灭火设施和器材。  
(十一)地面及井下供配电系统符合国家有关规定。  
(十二)矿山提升运输设备、装置及设施符合下列要求：  
1．钢丝绳、连接装置、提升容器以及保险链有足够的安全系数；  
2．提升容器与井壁、罐道梁之间及两个提升容器之间有足够的间隙；  
3．提升绞车和提升容器有可靠的安全保护装置；  
4．电机车、架线、轨道的选型能满足安全要求；  
5．运送人员的机械设备有可靠的安全保护装置；  
6．提升运输设备有灵敏可靠的信号装置。  
(十三)每个矿井有防尘供水系统。地面和井下所有产生粉尘的作业地点有综合防尘措施。  
(十四)有瓦斯、矿尘爆炸可能性的矿井，采用防爆电器设备，并采取防尘和隔爆措施。  
(十五)开采放射性矿物的矿井，符合下列要求：  
1．矿井进风量和风质能满足降氡的需要，避免串联通风和污风循环；  
2．主要进风道开在矿脉之外，穿矿脉或者岩体裂隙发育的进风巷道有防止氡析出的措施；  
3．采用后退式回采；  
4．能防止井下污水散流，并采取封闭的排放污水系统。  
(十六)矿山储存爆破材料的场所符合国家有关规定。  
(十七)排土场、矸石山有防止发生泥石流和其他危害的安全措施，尾矿库有防止溃坝等事故的安全设施。  
(十八)有防止山体滑坡和因采矿活动引起地表塌陷造成危害的预防措施。  
(十九)每个矿井配置足够数量的通风检测仪表和有毒有害气体与井下环境检测仪器。开采有瓦斯突出的矿井，装备监测系统或者检测仪器。  
(二十)有与外界相通的、符合安全要求的运输设施和通讯设施。  
(二十一)有更衣室、浴室等设施。

**Article 10** A mine shall have safety facilities that ensure safe production and prevent accidents and occupational hazards, and shall meet the following basic requirements:  
(1) Each mine shaft shall have at least two independent safety exits accessible to pedestrians leading directly to the surface. Each production level (middle section) and each mining area (panel) of the mine shaft shall have at least two safety exits accessible to pedestrians, which shall connect with the exits leading directly to the surface.  
(2) Each mine shaft shall have an independent ventilation system using mechanical ventilation to ensure sufficient air volume in underground workplaces; however, small non-methane mines may use natural ventilation provided that the required air volume for underground workplaces is ensured.  
(3) The cross-section of mine roadways shall meet the needs for pedestrian passage, transportation, ventilation, and installation, maintenance, and construction of safety facilities and equipment.  
(4) Roadway support and stope roof management shall ensure the safety of workplaces.  
(5) Sufficient safety isolation pillars shall be left between adjacent mine shafts, between mine shafts and open-pit mines, and between mine shafts and abandoned pits. The layout of mine roadways shall leave sufficient pillars or rock pillars to ensure safety both above and underground.  
(6) The bench height, platform width, and slope angle of open-pit mines shall meet the requirements for safe operation and slope stability. For dredge mining of sand, sufficient safety distance shall be maintained between the mining pool boundary and surface buildings and equipment.  
(7) There shall be water prevention and drainage systems on the surface and underground, and measures to prevent surface water from flowing into underground mines and open-pit workings.  
(8) Ore passes shall have safety measures to prevent and deal with blockages.  
(9) For mines with a possibility of spontaneous combustion, main haulage roadways shall be arranged within rock strata or mineral seams not prone to spontaneous combustion, and preventive grouting or other effective measures to prevent spontaneous combustion shall be adopted.  
(10) Surface fire prevention facilities of the mine shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State on fire prevention. Mine shafts shall be equipped with fire prevention and extinguishing facilities and equipment.  
(11) The surface and underground power supply and distribution systems shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State.  
(12) Mine hoisting and transportation equipment, devices, and facilities shall meet the following requirements:  
1. Wire ropes, connecting devices, hoisting containers, and safety chains shall have sufficient safety factors;  
2. There shall be sufficient clearance between hoisting containers and the shaft wall, between hoisting containers and guide beam, and between two hoisting containers;  
3. Hoisting winches and hoisting containers shall have reliable safety protection devices;  
4. The selection of electric locomotives, overhead lines, and tracks shall meet safety requirements;  
5. Mechanical equipment for transporting personnel shall have reliable safety protection devices;  
6. Hoisting and transportation equipment shall have sensitive and reliable signaling devices.  
(13) Each mine shaft shall have a dust-prevention water supply system. Comprehensive dust prevention measures shall be taken at all workplaces where dust is generated, both on the surface and underground.  
(14) For mines with a possibility of gas or coal dust explosion, explosion-proof electrical equipment shall be used, and dust prevention and explosion isolation measures shall be taken.  
(15) Mines exploiting radioactive minerals shall meet the following requirements:  
1. The intake air volume and air quality of the mine shaft shall meet the needs of reducing radon, and series ventilation and recirculation of contaminated air shall be avoided;  
2. The main intake airway shall be driven outside the ore vein; intake roadways passing through ore veins or rock mass with developed fractures shall have measures to prevent radon exhalation;  
3. Retreat stoping method shall be adopted;  
4. Underground sewage shall be prevented from diffusing, and a closed sewage discharge system shall be adopted.  
(16) The storage site of blasting materials in the mine shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State.  
(17) Dumping grounds and waste rock piles shall have safety measures to prevent debris flows and other hazards, and tailings ponds shall have safety facilities to prevent dam break accidents.  
(18) Preventive measures shall be taken to prevent landslides and hazards caused by surface subsidence due to mining activities.  
(19) Each mine shaft shall be equipped with a sufficient number of ventilation detection instruments and toxic, harmful gas and underground environment detection instruments. Mines with a risk of gas outburst shall be equipped with monitoring systems or detection instruments.  
(20) There shall be transportation and communication facilities connected to the outside world that meet safety requirements.  
(21) There shall be facilities such as changing rooms, bathrooms, etc.

### 第三章　矿山开采的安全保障

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　采掘作业应当编制作业规程，规定保证作业人员安全的技术措施和组织措施，并在情况变化时及时予以修改和补充。

**Article 11** For excavation operations, an operating procedure shall be prepared, specifying technical and organizational measures to ensure the safety of personnel, and shall be promptly revised and supplemented when circumstances change.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　矿山开采应当有下列图纸资料：  
(一)地质图(包括水文地质图和工程地质图)；  
(二)矿山总布置图和矿井井上、井下对照图；  
(三)矿井、巷道、采场布置图；  
(四)矿山生产和安全保障的主要系统图。

**Article 12** Mining operations shall have the following drawings and data:  
(1) geological maps (including hydrogeological maps and engineering geological maps);  
(2) general layout maps of the mine and surface-underground comparison maps of the mine shaft;  
(3) layout maps of mine shafts, roadways, and stopes;  
(4) main system diagrams for mine production and safety assurance.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　矿山企业应当在采矿许可证批准的范围开采，禁止越层、越界开采。

**Article 13** A mining enterprise shall carry out mining within the scope approved by the mining license, and mining beyond the authorized level or boundary is prohibited.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　矿山使用的下列设备、器材、防护用品和安全检测仪器，应当符合国家安全标准或者行业安全标准；不符合国家安全标准或者行业安全标准的，不得使用：  
(一)采掘、支护、装载、运输、提升、通风、排水、瓦斯抽放、压缩空气和起重设备；  
(二)电动机、变压器、配电柜、电器开关、电控装置；  
(三)爆破器材、通讯器材、矿灯、电缆、钢丝绳、支护材料、防火材料；  
(四)各种安全卫生检测仪器仪表；  
(五)自救器、安全帽、防尘防毒口罩或者面罩、防护服、防护鞋等防护用品和救护设备；  
(六)经有关主管部门认定的其他有特殊安全要求的设备和器材。

**Article 14** The following equipment, materials, protective articles, and safety detection instruments used in a mine shall comply with the national safety standards or industrial safety standards; those that do not comply with the national safety standards or industrial safety standards shall not be used:  
(1) equipment for excavation, support, loading, transportation, hoisting, ventilation, drainage, gas drainage, compressed air, and hoisting;  
(2) electric motors, transformers, switchboards, electrical switches, and electric control devices;  
(3) blasting materials, communication equipment, miner's lamps, cables, wire ropes, support materials, and fireproof materials;  
(4) various safety and health detection instruments and meters;  
(5) self-rescuers, safety helmets, dust-proof and anti-toxic masks or face shields, protective clothing, protective shoes, and other protective articles and rescue equipment;  
(6) other equipment and materials with special safety requirements recognized by the relevant competent departments.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　矿山企业应当对机电设备及其防护装置、安全检测仪器定期检查、维修，并建立技术档案，保证使用安全。  
非负责设备运行的人员，不得操作设备。非值班电气人员，不得进行电气作业。操作电气设备的人员，应当有可靠的绝缘保护。检修电气设备时，不得带电作业。

**Article 15** A mining enterprise shall regularly inspect and maintain mechanical and electrical equipment and their protective devices, as well as safety detection instruments, and shall establish technical archives to ensure safe use.  
Personnel not responsible for equipment operation shall not operate the equipment. Non-shift electrical personnel shall not perform electrical work. Personnel operating electrical equipment shall have reliable insulation protection. When repairing electrical equipment, live work shall not be performed.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　矿山作业场所空气中的有毒有害物质的浓度，不得超过国家标准或者行业标准；矿山企业应当按照国家规定的方法，按照下列要求定期检测：  
(一)粉尘作业点，每月至少检测两次；  
(二)三硝基甲苯作业点，每月至少检测一次；  
(三)放射性物质作业点，每月至少检测三次；  
(四)其他有毒有害物质作业点，井下每月至少检测一次，地面每季度至少检测一次；  
(五)采用个体采样方法检测呼吸性粉尘的，每季度至少检测一次。

**Article 16** The concentration of toxic and harmful substances in the air of mine workplaces shall not exceed the national standards or industrial standards; the mining enterprise shall conduct regular tests in accordance with the methods prescribed by the State and the following requirements:  
(1) for dust work sites, at least twice per month;  
(2) for trinitrotoluene work sites, at least once per month;  
(3) for radioactive material work sites, at least three times per month;  
(4) for other toxic and harmful substance work sites, at least once per month underground and at least once per quarter on the surface;  
(5) where personal sampling method is used to test respirable dust, at least once per quarter.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　井下采掘作业，必须按照作业规程的规定管理顶帮。采掘作业通过地质破碎带或者其他顶帮破碎地点时，应当加强支护。  
露天采剥作业，应当按照设计规定，控制采剥工作面的阶段高度、宽度、边坡角和最终边坡角。采剥作业和排土作业，不得对深部或者邻近井巷造成危害。

**Article 17** Underground excavation operations must manage the roof and sides in accordance with the provisions of the operating procedures. When excavation operations pass through geological fracture zones or other locations with broken roof and sides, support shall be strengthened.  
Open-pit stripping operations shall control the bench height, width, slope angle, and final slope angle of the stripping face in accordance with the design provisions. Stripping operations and dumping operations shall not cause harm to deep or adjacent mine roadways.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　煤矿和其他有瓦斯爆炸可能性的矿井，应当严格执行瓦斯检查制度，任何人不得携带烟草和点火用具下井。

**Article 18** Coal mines and other mine shafts with a possibility of gas explosion shall strictly implement the gas inspection system; no one shall carry tobacco or ignition devices into the mine shaft.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　在下列条件下从事矿山开采，应当编制专门设计文件，并报管理矿山企业的主管部门批准：  
(一)有瓦斯突出的；  
(二)有冲击地压的；  
(三)在需要保护的建筑物、构筑物和铁路下面开采的；  
(四)在水体下面开采的；  
(五)在地温异常或者有热水涌出的地区开采的。

**Article 19** Mining operations under the following conditions shall be subject to the preparation of special design documents and submitted to the competent authority in charge of mining enterprises for approval:  
(1) where there is gas outburst;  
(2) where there is rock burst;  
(3) where mining is carried out beneath buildings, structures, and railways that need protection;  
(4) where mining is carried out beneath water bodies;  
(5) where mining is carried out in areas with abnormal ground temperature or hot water inflow.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　有自然发火可能性的矿井，应当采取下列措施：  
(一)及时清出采场浮矿和其他可燃物质，回采结束后及时封闭采空区；  
(二)采取防火灌浆或者其他有效的预防自然发火的措施；  
(三)定期检查井巷和采区封闭情况，测定可能自然发火地点的温度和风量；定期检测火区内的温度、气压和空气成份。

**Article 20** Mine shafts with a possibility of spontaneous combustion shall take the following measures:  
(1) timely removal of loose ore and other combustible materials from the stope, and timely sealing of the goaf after extraction;  
(2) adoption of fire prevention grouting or other effective measures to prevent spontaneous combustion;  
(3) regular inspection of the sealing conditions of roadways and mining areas, and measurement of temperature and air volume at locations that may spontaneously combust; regular detection of temperature, air pressure, and air composition in fire zones.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　井下采掘作业遇下列情形之一时，应当探水前进：  
(一)接近承压含水层或者含水的断层、流砂层、砾石层、溶洞、陷落柱时；  
(二)接近与地表水体相通的地质破碎带或者接近连通承压层的未封钻孔时；  
(三)接近积水的老窑、旧巷或者灌过泥浆的采空区时；  
(四)发现有出水征兆时；  
(五)掘开隔离矿柱或者岩柱放水时。

**Article 21** When underground excavation operations encounter any of the following circumstances, water exploration shall be conducted before advancing:  
(1) when approaching a confined aquifer or a water-bearing fault, quicksand layer, gravel layer, karst cave, or collapse column;  
(2) when approaching a geological fracture zone connected to a surface water body or an unsealed borehole connected to a confined aquifer;  
(3) when approaching a water-logged abandoned pit, old roadway, or goaf that has been grouted;  
(4) when signs of water inflow are detected;  
(5) when cutting through isolation pillars or rock pillars for water drainage.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　井下风量、风质、风速和作业环境的气候，必须符合矿山安全规程的规定。  
采掘工作面进风风流中，按照体积计算，氧气不得低于20%，二氧化碳不得超过0.5%。  
井下作业地点的空气温度不得超过28℃；超过时，应当采取降温或者其他防护措施。

**Article 22** The underground air volume, air quality, air velocity, and climate of the working environment must comply with the provisions of the mine safety regulations.  
In the intake air current of the excavation working face, by volume, oxygen shall not be less than 20% and carbon dioxide shall not exceed 0.5%.  
The air temperature at underground workplaces shall not exceed 28°C; if it exceeds, cooling or other protective measures shall be taken.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　开采放射性矿物的矿井，必须采取下列措施，减少氡气析出量：  
(一)及时封闭采空区和已经报废或者暂时不用的井巷；  
(二)用留矿法作业的采场采用下行通风；  
(三)严格管理井下污水。

**Article 23** Mines exploiting radioactive minerals must take the following measures to reduce the amount of radon exhalation:  
(1) timely sealing of goafs and abandoned or temporarily unused roadways;  
(2) adoption of downcast ventilation in stopes using the shrinkage mining method;  
(3) strict management of underground sewage.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　矿山的爆破作业和爆破材料的制造、储存、运输、试验及销毁，必须严格执行国家有关规定。

**Article 24** The blasting operations in a mine and the manufacture, storage, transportation, testing, and destruction of blasting materials must strictly comply with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　矿山企业对地面、井下产生粉尘的作业，应当采取综合防尘措施，控制粉尘危害。  
井下风动凿岩，禁止干打眼。

**Article 25** A mining enterprise shall adopt comprehensive dust prevention measures for operations that generate dust on the surface and underground to control dust hazards.  
Dry drilling is prohibited for underground pneumatic rock drilling.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　矿山企业应当建立、健全对地面陷落区、排土场、矸石山、尾矿库的检查和维护制度；对可能发生的危害，应当采取预防措施。

**Article 26** A mining enterprise shall establish and improve the inspection and maintenance system for surface subsidence areas, dumping grounds, waste rock piles, and tailings ponds; preventive measures shall be taken against possible hazards.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　矿山企业应当按照国家有关规定关闭矿山，对关闭矿山后可能引起的危害采取预防措施。关闭矿山报告应当包括下列内容：  
(一)采掘范围及采空区处理情况；  
(二)对矿井采取的封闭措施；  
(三)对其他不安全因素的处理办法。

**Article 27** A mining enterprise shall close the mine in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and take preventive measures against hazards that may be caused after the mine closure. The mine closure report shall include the following:  
(1) the scope of excavation and the treatment of goafs;  
(2) the sealing measures taken for the mine shaft;  
(3) the handling methods for other unsafe factors.

### 第四章　矿山企业的安全管理

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　矿山企业应当建立、健全下列安全生产责任制：  
(一)行政领导岗位安全生产责任制；  
(二)职能机构安全生产责任制；  
(三)岗位人员的安全生产责任制。

**Article 28** A mining enterprise shall establish and improve the following responsibility systems for work safety:  
(1) responsibility system for work safety of administrative leadership positions;  
(2) responsibility system for work safety of functional departments;  
(3) responsibility system for work safety of post personnel.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　矿长(含矿务局局长、矿山公司经理，下同)对本企业的安全生产工作负有下列责任：  
(一)认真贯彻执行《矿山安全法》和本条例以及其他法律、法规中有关矿山安全生产的规定；  
(二)制定本企业安全生产管理制度；  
(三)根据需要配备合格的安全工作人员，对每个作业场所进行跟班检查；  
(四)采取有效措施，改善职工劳动条件，保证安全生产所需要的材料、设备、仪器和劳动防护用品的及时供应；  
(五)依照本条例的规定，对职工进行安全教育、培训；  
(六)制定矿山灾害的预防和应急计划；  
(七)及时采取措施，处理矿山存在的事故隐患；  
(八)及时、如实向劳动行政主管部门和管理矿山企业的主管部门报告矿山事故。

**Article 29** The mine director (including the director of the mine bureau and the manager of the mining company, hereinafter the same) shall bear the following responsibilities for the work safety of the enterprise:  
(1) earnestly implement the provisions of the Mine Safety Law and these Regulations, as well as other laws and regulations concerning mine safety production;  
(2) formulate the work safety management system of the enterprise;  
(3) assign qualified safety personnel as needed, and conduct shift inspections at each workplace;  
(4) take effective measures to improve the working conditions of employees and ensure the timely supply of materials, equipment, instruments, and labor protection articles required for safe production;  
(5) provide safety education and training to employees in accordance with these Regulations;  
(6) formulate plans for the prevention of mine disasters and emergency responses;  
(7) take timely measures to deal with potential accident hazards existing in the mine;  
(8) report mine accidents to the labour administrative department and the competent authority in charge of mining enterprises in a timely and truthful manner.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　矿山企业应当根据需要，设置安全机构或者配备专职安全工作人员。专职安全工作人员应当经过培训，具备必要的安全专业知识和矿山安全工作经验，能胜任现场安全检查工作。

**Article 30** A mining enterprise shall, as needed, set up a safety organization or assign full-time safety personnel. Full-time safety personnel shall receive training, possess the necessary safety professional knowledge and mine safety work experience, and be competent to carry out on-site safety inspections.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　矿长应当定期向职工代表大会或者职工大会报告下列事项，接受民主监督：  
(一)企业安全生产重大决策；  
(二)企业安全技术措施计划及其执行情况；  
(三)职工安全教育、培训计划及其执行情况；  
(四)职工提出的改善劳动条件的建议和要求的处理情况；  
(五)重大事故处理情况；  
(六)有关安全生产的其他重要事项。

**Article 31** The mine director shall regularly report the following matters to the employees' congress or the general meeting of employees and accept democratic supervision:  
(1) major decisions on work safety of the enterprise;  
(2) the enterprise's safety technical measures plan and its implementation;  
(3) the plan for employee safety education and training and its implementation;  
(4) the handling of suggestions and demands raised by employees for improving working conditions;  
(5) the handling of major accidents;  
(6) other important matters concerning work safety.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　矿山企业职工享有下列权利：  
(一)有权获得作业场所安全与职业危害方面的信息；  
(二)有权向有关部门和工会组织反映矿山安全状况和存在的问题；  
(三)对任何危害职工安全健康的决定和行为，有权提出批评、检举和控告。

**Article 32** Employees of a mining enterprise shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) the right to obtain information on workplace safety and occupational hazards;  
(2) the right to report the mine safety conditions and existing problems to the relevant departments and trade union organizations;  
(3) the right to criticize, report, and accuse any decisions and acts that endanger the safety and health of employees.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　矿山企业职工应当履行下列义务：  
(一)遵守有关矿山安全的法律、法规和企业规章制度；  
(二)维护矿山企业的生产设备、设施；  
(三)接受安全教育和培训；  
(四)及时报告危险情况，参加抢险救护。

**Article 33** Employees of a mining enterprise shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) comply with the laws, regulations, and enterprise rules on mine safety;  
(2) maintain the production equipment and facilities of the mining enterprise;  
(3) receive safety education and training;  
(4) report dangerous situations in a timely manner and participate in emergency rescue and relief.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　矿山企业工会有权督促企业行政方面加强职工的安全教育、培训工作，开展安全宣传活动，提高职工的安全生产意识和技术素质。

**Article 34** The trade union of a mining enterprise shall have the right to urge the administrative side of the enterprise to strengthen safety education and training for employees, carry out safety publicity activities, and enhance employees' awareness of safe production and technical competence.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　矿山企业应当按照下列规定对职工进行安全教育、培训：  
(一)新进矿山的井下作业职工，接受安全教育、培训的时间不得少于72小时，考试合格后，必须在有安全工作经验的职工带领下工作满4个月，然后经再次考核合格，方可独立工作；  
(二)新进露天矿的职工，接受安全教育、培训的时间不得少于40小时，经考试合格后，方可上岗作业；  
(三)对调换工种和采用新工艺作业的人员，必须重新培训，经考试合格后，方可上岗作业；  
(四)所有生产作业人员，每年接受在职安全教育、培训的时间不少于20小时。  
职工安全教育、培训期间，矿山企业应当支付工资。  
职工安全教育、培训情况和考核结果，应当记录存档。

**Article 35** A mining enterprise shall provide safety education and training to employees in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) For newly recruited employees working underground in the mine, the time for receiving safety education and training shall not be less than 72 hours. After passing the examination, they must work under the guidance of an employee with safety work experience for a full 4 months, and then after passing another assessment, they may work independently.  
(2) For newly recruited employees in open-pit mines, the time for receiving safety education and training shall not be less than 40 hours. They may start work only after passing the examination.  
(3) Personnel who change their type of work or are engaged in operations using new processes must be retrained, and may start their post only after passing the examination.  
(4) All production and operation personnel shall receive on-the-job safety education and training for not less than 20 hours per year.  
During the period of safety education and training, the mining enterprise shall pay wages.  
The safety education and training status and assessment results of employees shall be recorded and archived.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　矿山企业对职工的安全教育、培训，应当包括下列内容：  
(一)《矿山安全法》及本条例赋予矿山职工的权利与义务；  
(二)矿山安全规程及矿山企业有关安全管理的规章制度；  
(三)与职工本职工作有关的安全知识；  
(四)各种事故征兆的识别、发生紧急危险情况时的应急措施和撤退路线；  
(五)自救装备的使用和有关急救方面的知识；  
(六)有关主管部门规定的其他内容。

**Article 36** The safety education and training provided by a mining enterprise to its employees shall include the following contents:  
(1) the rights and obligations of mine employees granted by the Mine Safety Law and these Regulations;  
(2) the mine safety regulations and the rules and systems of the mining enterprise concerning safety management;  
(3) safety knowledge related to the employee's own work;  
(4) identification of various accident precursors, emergency measures and evacuation routes in case of emergency danger;  
(5) the use of self-rescue equipment and knowledge of first aid;  
(6) other contents prescribed by the relevant competent departments.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　瓦斯检查工、爆破工、通风工、信号工、拥罐工、电工、金属焊接(切割)工、矿井泵工、瓦斯抽放工、主扇风机操作工、主提升机操作工、绞车操作工、输送机操作工、尾矿工、安全检查工和矿内机动车司机等特种作业人员应当接受专门技术培训，经考核合格取得操作资格证书后，方可上岗作业。特种作业人员的考核、发证工作按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 37** Special operation personnel, including gas inspectors, blasters, ventilation workers, signalmen, cage attendants, electricians, metal welding (cutting) workers, mine pump operators, gas drainage workers, main fan operators, main hoist operators, winch operators, conveyor operators, tailings workers, safety inspectors, and drivers of motor vehicles within the mine, shall receive specialized technical training, and may take up their posts only after passing the assessment and obtaining an operation qualification certificate. The assessment and certification of special operation personnel shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　对矿长安全资格的考核，应当包括下列内容：  
(一)《矿山安全法》和有关法律、法规及矿山安全规程；  
(二)矿山安全知识；  
(三)安全生产管理能力；  
(四)矿山事故处理能力；  
(五)安全生产业绩。

**Article 38** The assessment of the safety qualification of a mine director shall include the following contents:  
(1) the Mine Safety Law and relevant laws, regulations, and mine safety regulations;  
(2) mine safety knowledge;  
(3) safety production management capability;  
(4) ability to handle mine accidents;  
(5) safety production performance.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　矿山企业向职工发放的劳动防护用品应当是经过鉴定和检验合格的产品。劳动防护用品的发放标准由国务院劳动行政主管部门制定。

**Article 39** The labor protection articles distributed by a mining enterprise to its employees shall be products that have passed identification and inspection. The standards for the distribution of labor protection articles shall be formulated by the competent administrative department for labour under the State Council.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　矿山企业应当每年编制矿山灾害预防和应急计划；在每季度末，应当根据实际情况对计划及时进行修改，制定相应的措施。  
矿山企业应当使每个职工熟悉矿山灾害预防和应急计划，并且每年至少组织一次矿山救灾演习。  
矿山企业应当根据国家有关规定，按照不同作业场所的要求，设置矿山安全标志。

**Article 40** A mining enterprise shall prepare a mine disaster prevention and emergency plan annually; at the end of each quarter, the plan shall be promptly revised according to actual conditions and corresponding measures shall be formulated.  
The mining enterprise shall ensure that every employee is familiar with the mine disaster prevention and emergency plan, and shall organize at least one mine rescue drill each year.  
The mining enterprise shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the requirements of different workplaces, set up mine safety signs.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　矿山企业应当建立由专职的或者兼职的人员组成的矿山救护和医疗急救组织。不具备单独建立专业救护和医疗急救组织的小型矿山企业，除应当建立兼职的救护和医疗急救组织外，还应当与邻近的有专业的救护和医疗急救组织的矿山企业签订救护和急救协议，或者与邻近的矿山企业联合建立专业救护和医疗急救组织。  
矿山救护和医疗急救组织应当有固定场所、训练器械和训练场地。  
矿山救护和医疗急救组织的规模和装备标准，由国务院管理矿山企业的有关主管部门规定。

**Article 41** A mining enterprise shall establish a mine rescue and medical first aid organization composed of full-time or part-time personnel. Small mining enterprises that are unable to establish a specialized rescue and medical first aid organization alone shall, in addition to establishing a part-time rescue and medical first aid organization, sign rescue and first aid agreements with neighboring mining enterprises that have specialized rescue and medical first aid organizations, or jointly establish specialized rescue and medical first aid organizations with neighboring mining enterprises.  
The mine rescue and medical first aid organization shall have fixed premises, training equipment, and training grounds.  
The size and equipment standards of the mine rescue and medical first aid organization shall be prescribed by the relevant competent department for mine enterprises under the State Council.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　矿山企业必须按照国家规定的安全条件进行生产，并安排一部分资金，用于下列改善矿山安全生产条件的项目：  
(一)预防矿山事故的安全技术措施；  
(二)预防职业危害的劳动卫生技术措施；  
(三)职工的安全培训；  
(四)改善矿山安全生产条件的其他技术措施。  
前款所需资金，由矿山企业按矿山维简费的20%的比例具实列支；没有矿山维简费的矿山企业，按固定资产折旧费的20%的比例具实列支。

**Article 42** A mining enterprise must carry out production under the safety conditions prescribed by the State, and shall allocate a portion of funds for the following projects to improve mine safety production conditions:  
(1) safety technical measures to prevent mine accidents;  
(2) occupational health technical measures to prevent occupational hazards;  
(3) safety training of employees;  
(4) other technical measures to improve mine safety production conditions.  
The funds required for the preceding paragraph shall be actually disbursed by the mining enterprise at the rate of 20% of the mine maintenance and simple reproduction fee; for mining enterprises without a mine maintenance and simple reproduction fee, the funds shall be actually disbursed at the rate of 20% of the depreciation charge of fixed assets.

### 第五章　矿山安全的监督和管理

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　县级以上各级人民政府劳动行政主管部门，应当根据矿山安全监督工作的实际需要，配备矿山安全监督人员。  
矿山安全监督人员必须熟悉矿山安全技术知识，具有矿山安全工作经验，能胜任矿山安全检查工作。  
矿山安全监督证件和专用标志由国务院劳动行政主管部门统一制作。

**Article 43** The labour administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in light of the actual needs of mine safety supervision work, assign mine safety supervisors.  
Mine safety supervisors must be familiar with mine safety technical knowledge, have mine safety work experience, and be competent to carry out mine safety inspection work.  
The mine safety supervision certificates and special insignia shall be uniformly produced by the competent administrative department for labour under the State Council.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　矿山安全监督人员在执行职务时，有权进入现场检查，参加有关会议，无偿调阅有关资料，向有关单位和人员了解情况。  
矿山安全监督人员进入现场检查，发现有危及职工安全健康的情况时，有权要求矿山企业立即改正或者限期解决；情况紧急时，有权要求矿山企业立即停止作业，从危险区内撤出作业人员。  
劳动行政主管部门可以委托检测机构对矿山作业场所和危险性较大的在用设备、仪器、器材进行抽检。  
劳动行政主管部门对检查中发现的违反《矿山安全法》和本条例以及其他法律、法规有关矿山安全的规定的情况，应当依法提出处理意见。

**Article 44** When performing their duties, mine safety supervisors shall have the right to enter the site for inspection, attend relevant meetings, consult relevant materials free of charge, and inquire about the situation from relevant units and personnel.  
When entering the site for inspection and finding a situation that endangers the safety and health of employees, mine safety supervisors shall have the right to demand that the mining enterprise immediately rectify the situation or resolve it within a time limit; in an emergency, they shall have the right to demand that the mining enterprise immediately stop operations and evacuate the operating personnel from the dangerous area.  
The labour administrative department may entrust testing institutions to conduct spot checks on mine workplaces and on equipment, instruments, and materials in use that pose greater danger.  
For situations found during inspection that violate the provisions of the Mine Safety Law, these Regulations, and other laws and regulations concerning mine safety, the labour administrative department shall propose handling opinions according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　矿山安全监督人员执行公务时，应当出示矿山安全监督证件，秉公执法，并遵守有关规定。

**Article 45** When performing official duties, mine safety supervisors shall present their mine safety supervision certificates, enforce the law impartially, and comply with relevant provisions.

### 第六章　矿山事故处理

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　矿山发生事故后，事故现场有关人员应当立即报告矿长或者有关主管人员；矿长或者有关主管人员接到事故报告后，必须立即采取有效措施，组织抢救，防止事故扩大，尽力减少人员伤亡和财产损失。

**Article 46** After a mine accident occurs, the relevant personnel at the accident site shall immediately report it to the mine director or the relevant responsible person; upon receiving the accident report, the mine director or the relevant responsible person must immediately take effective measures to organize rescue, prevent the accident from escalating, and make every effort to reduce casualties and property losses.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　矿山发生重伤、死亡事故后，矿山企业应当在24小时内如实向劳动行政主管部门和管理矿山企业的主管部门报告。

**Article 47** After a serious injury or fatal accident occurs in a mine, the mining enterprise shall, within 24 hours, truthfully report it to the labour administrative department and the competent authority in charge of mining enterprises.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　劳动行政主管部门和管理矿山企业的主管部门接到死亡事故或者一次重伤3人以上的事故报告后，应当立即报告本级人民政府，并报各自的上一级主管部门。

**Article 48** After receiving a report of a fatal accident or an accident with three or more serious injuries at one time, the labour administrative department and the competent authority in charge of mining enterprises shall immediately report it to the people's government at the same level and to their respective higher-level competent authorities.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　发生伤亡事故，矿山企业和有关单位应当保护事故现场；因抢救事故，需要移动现场部分物品时，必须作出标志，绘制事故现场图，并详细记录；在消除现场危险，采取防范措施后，方可恢复生产。

**Article 49** When a casualty accident occurs, the mining enterprise and relevant units shall protect the accident scene; if it is necessary to move some items at the scene due to rescue operations, markings must be made, an accident scene diagram must be drawn, and detailed records must be made; production may be resumed only after the danger at the scene is eliminated and preventive measures are taken.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　矿山事故发生后，有关部门应当按照国家有关规定，进行事故调查处理。

**Article 50** After a mine accident occurs, the relevant departments shall conduct accident investigation and handling in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　矿山事故调查处理工作应当自事故发生之日起90日内结束；遇有特殊情况，可以适当延长，但是不得超过180日。矿山事故处理结案后，应当公布处理结果。

**Article 51** The investigation and handling of a mine accident shall be concluded within 90 days from the date of occurrence of the accident; under special circumstances, the period may be appropriately extended, but shall not exceed 180 days. After the conclusion of the handling of a mine accident, the handling results shall be made public.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　依照[《矿山安全法》第四十条](../../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国矿山安全法.md#art-40)规定处以罚款的，分别按照下列规定执行：  
(一)未对职工进行安全教育、培训，分配职工上岗作业的，处4万元以下的罚款；  
(二)使用不符合国家安全标准或者行业安全标准的设备、器材、防护用品和安全检测仪器的，处5万元以下的罚款；  
(三)未按照规定提取或者使用安全技术措施专项费用的，处5万元以下的罚款；  
(四)拒绝矿山安全监督人员现场检查或者在被检查时隐瞒事故隐患，不如实反映情况的，处2万元以下的罚款；  
(五)未按照规定及时、如实报告矿山事故的，处3万元以下的罚款。

**Article 52** Where a fine is imposed in accordance with Article 40 of the Mine Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, the fine shall be enforced respectively in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) where employees are assigned to work at posts without safety education or training, a fine of not more than 40,000 yuan shall be imposed;  
(2) where equipment, apparatus, protective articles or safety testing instruments that do not meet national safety standards or industrial safety standards are used, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed;  
(3) where special funds for safety technical measures are not allocated or used in accordance with the provisions, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed;  
(4) where a mine safety supervisor is refused on-site inspection or, during the inspection, hidden dangers of accidents are concealed or the true situation is not reflected, a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed;  
(5) where a mine accident is not reported promptly or truthfully in accordance with the provisions, a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　依照[《矿山安全法》第四十三条](../../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国矿山安全法.md#art-43)规定处以罚款的，罚款幅度为5万元以上10万元以下。

**Article 53** Where a fine is imposed in accordance with Article 43 of the Mine Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, the amount of the fine shall be not less than 50,000 yuan and not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反[本条例第十五条](#art-15)、第十六条、第十七条、第十八条、第十九条、第二十条、第二十一条、第二十二条、第二十三条、第二十五条规定的，由劳动行政主管部门责令改正，可以处2万元以下的罚款。

**Article 54** Where anyone violates the provisions of Articles 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23 and 25 of these Regulations, the administrative department of labor shall order it to make corrections, and may impose a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　当事人收到罚款通知书后，应当在15日内到指定的金融机构缴纳罚款；逾期不缴纳的，自逾期之日起每日加收3‰的滞纳金。

**Article 55** After receiving the notice of fine, the party shall pay the fine to the designated financial institution within 15 days; if it fails to pay within the time limit, a late fee of 3‰ per day shall be imposed from the date of default.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　矿山企业主管人员有下列行为之一，造成矿山事故的，按照规定给予纪律处分；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)违章指挥、强令工人违章、冒险作业的；  
(二)对工人屡次违章作业熟视无睹，不加制止的；  
(三)对重大事故预兆或者已发现的隐患不及时采取措施的；  
(四)不执行劳动行政主管部门的监督指令或者不采纳有关部门提出的整顿意见，造成严重后果的。

**Article 56** Where any person in charge of a mining enterprise commits any of the following acts, causing a mine accident, disciplinary sanction shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions; if the act constitutes a crime, the judicial organ shall investigate criminal liability according to law:  
(1) commanding in violation of rules, or forcing workers to operate in violation of rules or to work under hazardous conditions;  
(2) turning a blind eye to repeated violations of operating rules by workers without taking any preventive measures;  
(3) failing to take timely measures against signs of major accidents or discovered hidden dangers;  
(4) failing to implement supervision directives of the administrative department of labor or failing to adopt rectification opinions proposed by relevant departments, resulting in serious consequences.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国务院管理矿山企业的主管部门根据《矿山安全法》和本条例修订或者制定的矿山安全规程和行业技术规范，报国务院劳动行政主管部门备案。

**Article 57** The competent department of the State Council in charge of mining enterprises shall, in accordance with the Mine Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and these Regulations, revise or formulate mine safety procedures and industry technical specifications, and shall report them to the administrative department of labor under the State Council for record.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　石油天然气开采的安全规定，由国务院劳动行政主管部门会同石油工业主管部门制定，报国务院批准后施行。

**Article 58** Safety provisions for the exploitation of petroleum and natural gas shall be formulated by the administrative department of labor under the State Council jointly with the competent department of the petroleum industry, and shall come into force after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 59** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
