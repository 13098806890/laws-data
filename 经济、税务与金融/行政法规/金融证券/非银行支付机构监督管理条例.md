# 非银行支付机构监督管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2023-12-09  
**生效日期**：2024-05-01

> 第六条　设立非银行支付机构，应当经中国人民银行批准，取得支付业务许可。非银行支付机构的名称中应当标明“支付”字样。 未经依法批准，任何单位和个人不得从事或者变相从事支付业务，不得在单位名称和经营范围中使用“支付”字样，法律、行政法规和国家另有规定的除外。支付业务许可被依法注销后，该机构名称和经营范围中不得继续使用“支付”字样。 第七条　设立非银行支付机构，应当符合《中华人民共和国公司法》的规定，并具备以下条件： （一）有符合本条例规定的注册资本； （二）主要股东、实际控制人财务状况和诚信记录良好，最近3年无重大违法违规记录；主要股东、实际控制人为公司的，其股权结构应当清晰透明，不存在权属纠纷； （三）拟任董事、监事和高级管理人员熟悉相关法律法规，具有履行职责所需的经营管理能力，最近3年无重大违法违规记录； （四）有符合规定的经营场所、安全保障措施以及业务系统、设施和技术； （五）有健全的公司治理结构、内部控制和风险管理制度、退出预案以及用户权益保障机制； （六）法律、行政法规以及中国人民银行规章规定的其他审慎性条件。 第八条　设立非银行支付机构的注册资本最低限额为人民币1亿元，且应当为实缴货币资本。 中国人民银行根据非银行支付机构的业务类型、经营地域范围和业务规模等因素，可以提高前款规定的注册资本最低限额。 非银行支付机构的股东应当以自有资金出资，不得以委托资金、债务资金等非自有资金出资。 第九条　申请设立非银行支付机构，应当向中国人民银行提交申请书和证明其符合本条例第七条、第八条规定条件的材料。 第十条　中国人民银行应当自受理申请之日起6个月内作出批准或者不予批准的决定。决定批准的，颁发支付业务许可证并予以公告；决定不予批准的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。 支付业务许可证应当载明非银行支付机构可以从事的业务类型和经营地域范围。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　设立非银行支付机构，应当符合《中华人民共和国公司法》的规定，并具备以下条件：  
（一）有符合本条例规定的注册资本；  
（二）主要股东、实际控制人财务状况和诚信记录良好，最近3年无重大违法违规记录；主要股东、实际控制人为公司的，其股权结构应当清晰透明，不存在权属纠纷；  
（三）拟任董事、监事和高级管理人员熟悉相关法律法规，具有履行职责所需的经营管理能力，最近3年无重大违法违规记录；  
（四）有符合规定的经营场所、安全保障措施以及业务系统、设施和技术；  
（五）有健全的公司治理结构、内部控制和风险管理制度、退出预案以及用户权益保障机制；  
（六）法律、行政法规以及中国人民银行规章规定的其他审慎性条件。

**Article 7** The establishment of a non-bank payment institution shall comply with the provisions of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China and meet the following conditions:  
(1) having a registered capital that conforms to the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) the major shareholders and actual controllers have good financial conditions and integrity records, and have no record of major violations of laws or regulations in the past three years; if the major shareholder or actual controller is a company, its equity structure shall be clear and transparent, and there shall be no ownership dispute;  
(3) the proposed directors, supervisors, and senior officers are familiar with relevant laws and regulations, possess the operational and management capabilities required for performing their duties, and have no record of major violations of laws or regulations in the past three years;  
(4) having business premises, security measures, and business systems, facilities, and technologies that conform to regulations;  
(5) having a sound corporate governance structure, internal control and risk management systems, exit plan, and user rights protection mechanism;  
(6) other prudential conditions as prescribed by laws, administrative regulations, and the rules of the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　设立非银行支付机构的注册资本最低限额为人民币1亿元，且应当为实缴货币资本。  
中国人民银行根据非银行支付机构的业务类型、经营地域范围和业务规模等因素，可以提高前款规定的注册资本最低限额。  
非银行支付机构的股东应当以自有资金出资，不得以委托资金、债务资金等非自有资金出资。

**Article 8** The minimum registered capital for the establishment of a non-bank payment institution shall be RMB 100 million, and it shall be paid-in monetary capital.  
The People's Bank of China may, based on factors such as the business type, geographic scope of operations, and business scale of the non-bank payment institution, increase the minimum registered capital as specified in the preceding paragraph.  
The shareholders of a non-bank payment institution shall contribute with their own funds, and shall not contribute with entrusted funds, debt funds, or other non-own funds.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请设立非银行支付机构，应当向中国人民银行提交申请书和证明其符合[本条例第七条](#art-7)、第八条规定条件的材料。

**Article 9** To apply for the establishment of a non-bank payment institution, an applicant shall submit an application letter and materials proving that it meets the conditions prescribed in Articles 7 and 8 of these Regulations to the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　中国人民银行应当自受理申请之日起6个月内作出批准或者不予批准的决定。决定批准的，颁发支付业务许可证并予以公告；决定不予批准的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
支付业务许可证应当载明非银行支付机构可以从事的业务类型和经营地域范围。

**Article 10** The People's Bank of China shall, within six months from the date of accepting an application, make a decision of approval or disapproval. If it decides to approve, it shall issue a payment business permit and make an announcement; if it decides not to approve, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
The payment business permit shall specify the types of business that the non-bank payment institution may engage in and the geographic scope of operations.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请人收到支付业务许可证后，应当及时向市场监督管理部门办理登记手续，领取营业执照。  
非银行支付机构设立后无正当理由连续2年以上未开展支付业务的，由中国人民银行注销支付业务许可。

**Article 11** After receiving the payment business permit, the applicant shall promptly go through the registration procedures with the market supervision and administration department and obtain a business license.  
If a non-bank payment institution, after its establishment, fails to conduct payment business for two consecutive years or more without justifiable reasons, the People's Bank of China shall cancel the payment business permit.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　非银行支付机构的主要经营场所应当与登记的住所保持一致。非银行支付机构拟在住所所在地以外的省、自治区、直辖市为线下经营的特约商户提供支付服务的，应当按照规定设立分支机构，并向中国人民银行备案。  
本条例所称特约商户，是指与非银行支付机构签订支付服务协议，由非银行支付机构按照协议为其完成资金结算的经营主体。

**Article 12** The principal place of business of a non-bank payment institution shall be consistent with its registered domicile. If a non-bank payment institution intends to provide payment services to offline special merchants in provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government other than where its domicile is located, it shall establish branches in accordance with regulations and file a record with the People's Bank of China.  
The term "special merchant" as used in these Regulations refers to a business entity that enters into a payment service agreement with a non-bank payment institution, and the non-bank payment institution completes the fund settlement for it in accordance with the agreement.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　非银行支付机构办理下列事项，应当经中国人民银行批准：  
（一）变更名称、注册资本、业务类型或者经营地域范围；  
（二）跨省、自治区、直辖市变更住所；  
（三）变更主要股东或者实际控制人；  
（四）变更董事、监事或者高级管理人员；  
（五）合并或者分立。  
非银行支付机构申请变更名称、注册资本的，中国人民银行应当自受理申请之日起1个月内作出批准或者不予批准的书面决定；申请办理前款所列其他事项的，中国人民银行应当自受理申请之日起3个月内作出批准或者不予批准的书面决定。经批准后，非银行支付机构依法向市场监督管理部门办理相关登记手续。

**Article 13** A non-bank payment institution shall obtain the approval of the People's Bank of China for the following matters:  
(1) changing its name, registered capital, business type, or geographic scope of operations;  
(2) changing its domicile across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(3) changing its major shareholders or actual controllers;  
(4) changing its directors, supervisors, or senior officers;  
(5) merger or division.  
If a non-bank payment institution applies to change its name or registered capital, the People's Bank of China shall, within one month from the date of accepting the application, make a written decision of approval or disapproval; for an application to handle other matters listed in the preceding paragraph, the People's Bank of China shall, within three months from the date of accepting the application, make a written decision of approval or disapproval. After approval, the non-bank payment institution shall go through the relevant registration procedures with the market supervision and administration department according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　非银行支付机构拟终止支付业务的，应当向中国人民银行申请注销支付业务许可。非银行支付机构申请注销支付业务许可或者被中国人民银行吊销支付业务许可证、撤销支付业务许可的，应当按照规定制定切实保障用户资金和信息安全的方案，并向用户公告。非银行支付机构解散的，还应当依法进行清算，清算过程接受中国人民银行的监督。  
非银行支付机构办理支付业务许可注销手续后，方可向市场监督管理部门办理变更或者注销登记手续。

**Article 14** If a non-bank payment institution intends to terminate its payment business, it shall apply to the People's Bank of China for cancellation of the payment business permit. If a non-bank payment institution applies for cancellation of the payment business permit or if its payment business permit is revoked or the payment business approval is withdrawn by the People's Bank of China, it shall formulate a plan to effectively safeguard users' funds and information security in accordance with regulations and make an announcement to users. If a non-bank payment institution is dissolved, it shall also carry out liquidation according to law, and the liquidation process shall be subject to the supervision of the People's Bank of China.  
A non-bank payment institution may only complete the change or cancellation of registration procedures with the market supervision and administration department after completing the procedures for cancellation of the payment business permit.

### 第三章　支付业务规则

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　非银行支付业务根据能否接收付款人预付资金，分为储值账户运营和支付交易处理两种类型，但是单用途预付卡业务不属于本条例规定的支付业务。  
储值账户运营业务和支付交易处理业务的具体分类方式和监督管理规则由中国人民银行制定。

**Article 15** Non-bank payment businesses are classified into two types: stored-value account operation and payment transaction processing, based on whether they can accept prepaid funds from payers; however, single-purpose prepaid card business is not a payment business under these Regulations.  
The specific classification methods and supervisory and regulatory rules for stored-value account operation business and payment transaction processing business shall be formulated by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　非银行支付机构应当按照支付业务许可证载明的业务类型和经营地域范围从事支付业务，未经批准不得从事依法需经批准的其他业务。  
非银行支付机构不得涂改、倒卖、出租、出借支付业务许可证，或者以其他形式非法转让行政许可。

**Article 16** A non-bank payment institution shall conduct payment business in accordance with the business type and geographic scope of operations specified in the payment business permit, and shall not engage in other businesses that require approval according to law without approval.  
A non-bank payment institution shall not alter, resell, rent, lend, or otherwise illegally transfer the administrative license.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　非银行支付机构应当按照审慎经营要求，建立健全并落实合规管理制度、内部控制制度、业务管理制度、风险管理制度、突发事件应急预案以及用户权益保障机制。

**Article 17** A non-bank payment institution shall, in accordance with the requirements of prudent operation, establish, improve, and implement a compliance management system, internal control system, business management system, risk management system, emergency response plan, and user rights protection mechanism.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　非银行支付机构应当具备必要和独立的业务系统、设施和技术，按照强制性国家标准以及相关网络、数据安全管理要求，确保支付业务处理的及时性、准确性和支付业务的连续性、安全性、可溯源性。  
非银行支付机构的业务系统及其备份应当存放在境内。

**Article 18** A non-bank payment institution shall have necessary and independent business systems, facilities, and technologies, and shall ensure the timeliness and accuracy of payment business processing and the continuity, security, and traceability of payment businesses in accordance with mandatory national standards and relevant network and data security management requirements.  
The business systems and their backups of a non-bank payment institution shall be stored within the territory of China.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　非银行支付机构为境内交易提供支付服务的，应当在境内完成交易处理、资金结算和数据存储。  
非银行支付机构为跨境交易提供支付服务的，应当遵守跨境支付、跨境人民币业务、外汇管理以及数据跨境流动的有关规定。

**Article 19** If a non-bank payment institution provides payment services for domestic transactions, it shall complete transaction processing, fund settlement, and data storage within the territory of China.  
If a non-bank payment institution provides payment services for cross-border transactions, it shall comply with the relevant provisions on cross-border payment, cross-border RMB business, foreign exchange administration, and cross-border data flow.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　非银行支付机构应当与用户签订支付服务协议。非银行支付机构应当按照公平原则拟定协议条款，并在其经营场所、官方网站、移动互联网应用程序等的显著位置予以公示。  
支付服务协议应当明确非银行支付机构与用户的权利义务、支付业务流程、电子支付指令传输路径、资金结算、纠纷处理原则以及违约责任等事项，且不得包含排除、限制竞争以及不合理地免除或者减轻非银行支付机构责任、加重用户责任、限制或者排除用户主要权利等内容。对于协议中足以影响用户是否同意使用支付服务的条款，非银行支付机构应当采取合理方式提示用户注意，并按照用户的要求对该条款予以说明。  
非银行支付机构拟变更协议内容的，应当充分征求用户意见，并在本条第一款规定的显著位置公告满30日后方可变更。非银行支付机构应当以数据电文等书面形式与用户就变更的协议内容达成一致。

**Article 20** A non-bank payment institution shall sign a payment service agreement with its users. The non-bank payment institution shall draft the terms of the agreement in accordance with the principle of fairness and shall display them prominently at its business premises, official website, mobile internet applications, and other locations.  
The payment service agreement shall specify the rights and obligations of the non-bank payment institution and the user, the payment business process, the transmission path of electronic payment instructions, fund settlement, dispute resolution principles, and liability for breach of contract, among other matters, and shall not contain content that excludes or restricts competition, or that unreasonably exempts or mitigates the liability of the non-bank payment institution, increases the user's liability, or restricts or excludes the user's main rights. For terms that may affect the user's decision to agree to use the payment service, the non-bank payment institution shall draw the user's attention to them in a reasonable manner and shall explain such terms at the user's request.  
If a non-bank payment institution intends to change the content of the agreement, it shall fully solicit the opinions of users and shall only make the change after the notice has been displayed for at least 30 days in the prominent locations specified in the first paragraph of this Article. The non-bank payment institution shall reach an agreement with the user on the changed content of the agreement in writing, including in the form of data messages.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　非银行支付机构应当建立持续有效的用户尽职调查制度，按照规定识别并核实用户身份，了解用户交易背景和风险状况，并采取相应的风险管理措施。  
非银行支付机构不得将涉及资金安全、信息安全等的核心业务和技术服务委托第三方处理。

**Article 21** A non-bank payment institution shall establish a continuous and effective user due diligence system, identify and verify user identities in accordance with regulations, understand the background and risk status of user transactions, and take corresponding risk management measures.  
A non-bank payment institution shall not entrust a third party to handle core businesses and technical services related to fund security, information security, etc.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　非银行支付机构应当自行完成特约商户尽职调查、支付服务协议签订、持续风险监测等业务活动。非银行支付机构不得为未经依法设立或者从事非法经营活动的商户提供服务。

**Article 22** A non-bank payment institution shall independently complete business activities such as due diligence on special merchants, signing of payment service agreements, and continuous risk monitoring. A non-bank payment institution shall not provide services to merchants that have not been established according to law or that engage in illegal business activities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　从事储值账户运营业务的非银行支付机构为用户开立支付账户的，应当遵守法律、行政法规以及中国人民银行关于支付账户管理的规定。国家引导、鼓励非银行支付机构与商业银行开展合作，通过银行账户为单位用户提供支付服务。  
前款规定的非银行支付机构应当建立健全支付账户开立、使用、变更和撤销等业务管理和风险管理制度，防止开立匿名、假名支付账户，并采取有效措施保障支付账户安全，开展异常账户风险监测，防范支付账户被用于违法犯罪活动。  
本条例所称支付账户，是指根据用户真实意愿为其开立的，用于发起支付指令、反映交易明细、记录资金余额的电子簿记载体。支付账户应当以用户实名开立。  
任何单位和个人不得非法买卖、出租、出借支付账户。

**Article 23** If a non-bank payment institution engaged in stored-value account operation business opens a payment account for a user, it shall comply with laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of the People's Bank of China on the management of payment accounts. The State guides and encourages non-bank payment institutions to cooperate with commercial banks to provide payment services for institutional users through bank accounts.  
The non-bank payment institution specified in the preceding paragraph shall establish and improve business management and risk management systems for the opening, use, change, and cancellation of payment accounts, prevent the opening of anonymous or pseudonymous payment accounts, take effective measures to ensure the security of payment accounts, conduct risk monitoring of abnormal accounts, and prevent payment accounts from being used for illegal and criminal activities.  
The term "payment account" as used in these Regulations refers to an electronic book-entry carrier opened at the user's true will for initiating payment instructions, reflecting transaction details, and recording fund balances. Payment accounts shall be opened in the user's real name.  
No unit or individual shall illegally buy, sell, rent, or lend payment accounts.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　从事储值账户运营业务的非银行支付机构应当将从用户处获取的预付资金及时等值转换为支付账户余额或者预付资金余额。用户可以按照协议约定提取其持有的余额，但是非银行支付机构不得向用户支付与其持有的余额有关的利息等收益。

**Article 24** A non-bank payment institution engaged in stored-value account operation business shall promptly convert the prepaid funds obtained from users into payment account balances or prepaid fund balances at an equivalent value. Users may withdraw the balances they hold in accordance with the agreement, but the non-bank payment institution shall not pay users any interest or other income related to the balances they hold.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　非银行支付机构应当将收款人和付款人信息等必要信息包含在电子支付指令中，确保所传递的电子支付指令的完整性、一致性、可跟踪稽核和不可篡改。  
非银行支付机构不得伪造、变造电子支付指令。

**Article 25** A non-bank payment institution shall include necessary information such as the payee and payer information in the electronic payment instructions, and shall ensure the integrity, consistency, traceability, and non-tamperability of the transmitted electronic payment instructions.  
A non-bank payment institution shall not forge or alter electronic payment instructions.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　非银行支付机构应当以清算机构、银行业金融机构、其他非银行支付机构认可的安全认证方式访问账户，不得违反规定留存银行账户、支付账户敏感信息。

**Article 26** A non-bank payment institution shall access accounts using secure authentication methods recognized by clearing institutions, banking financial institutions, and other non-bank payment institutions, and shall not retain sensitive information of bank accounts or payment accounts in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　非银行支付机构应当根据用户发起的支付指令划转备付金，用户备付金被依法冻结、扣划的除外。  
本条例所称备付金，是指非银行支付机构为用户办理支付业务而实际收到的预收待付货币资金。  
非银行支付机构不得以任何形式挪用、占用、借用备付金，不得以备付金为自己或者他人提供担保。

**Article 27** A non-bank payment institution shall transfer the provident fund in accordance with the payment instructions initiated by the user, except where the user's provident fund is frozen or deducted according to law.  
The term "provident fund" as used in these Regulations refers to the monetary funds received in advance and to be paid later that a non-bank payment institution actually receives for handling payment business for users.  
A non-bank payment institution shall not misappropriate, occupy, or borrow the provident fund in any form, nor shall it use the provident fund to provide a guarantee for itself or others.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　非银行支付机构净资产与备付金日均余额的比例应当符合中国人民银行的规定。

**Article 28** The ratio of the net assets of a non-bank payment institution to the average daily balance of the provident fund shall comply with the provisions of the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　非银行支付机构应当将备付金存放在中国人民银行或者符合中国人民银行要求的商业银行。  
任何单位和个人不得对非银行支付机构存放备付金的账户申请冻结或者强制执行，法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 29** A non-bank payment institution shall deposit the provident fund with the People's Bank of China or with a commercial bank that meets the requirements of the People's Bank of China.  
No unit or individual shall apply for freezing or compulsory execution of the accounts where a non-bank payment institution deposits its provident fund, except as otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　非银行支付机构应当通过中国人民银行确定的清算机构处理与银行业金融机构、其他非银行支付机构之间合作开展的支付业务，遵守清算管理规定，不得从事或者变相从事清算业务。  
非银行支付机构应当向清算机构及时报送真实、准确、完整的交易信息。  
非银行支付机构应当按照结算管理规定为用户办理资金结算业务，采取风险管理措施。

**Article 30** A non-bank payment institution shall process payment business conducted in cooperation with banking financial institutions and other non-bank payment institutions through the clearing institution designated by the People's Bank of China, comply with the clearing management provisions, and shall not engage in or disguisedly engage in clearing business.  
A non-bank payment institution shall promptly submit true, accurate, and complete transaction information to the clearing institution.  
A non-bank payment institution shall handle fund settlement business for users in accordance with the settlement management provisions and take risk management measures.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　非银行支付机构应当妥善保存用户资料和交易记录。有关机关依照法律、行政法规的规定，查询用户资料、交易记录及其持有的支付账户余额或者预付资金余额，或者冻结、扣划用户资金的，非银行支付机构应当予以配合。

**Article 31** A non-bank payment institution shall properly preserve user materials and transaction records. If relevant authorities, in accordance with laws and administrative regulations, inquire about user materials, transaction records, and the payment account balance or prepaid fund balance held by the user, or freeze or deduct user funds, the non-bank payment institution shall cooperate.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　非银行支付机构处理用户信息，应当遵循合法、正当、必要和诚信原则，公开用户信息处理规则，明示处理用户信息的目的、方式和范围，并取得用户同意，法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。  
非银行支付机构应当依照法律、行政法规、国家有关规定和双方约定处理用户信息，不得收集与其提供的服务无关的用户信息，不得以用户不同意处理其信息或者撤回同意等为由拒绝提供服务，处理相关信息属于提供服务所必需的除外。  
非银行支付机构应当对用户信息严格保密，采取有效措施防止未经授权的访问以及用户信息泄露、篡改、丢失，不得非法买卖、提供或者公开用户信息。  
非银行支付机构与其关联公司共享用户信息的，应当告知用户该关联公司的名称和联系方式，并就信息共享的内容以及信息处理的目的、期限、方式、保护措施等取得用户单独同意。非银行支付机构还应当与关联公司就上述内容以及双方的权利义务等作出约定，并对关联公司的用户信息处理活动进行监督，确保用户信息处理活动依法合规、风险可控。  
用户发现非银行支付机构违反法律、行政法规、国家有关规定或者双方约定处理其信息的，有权要求非银行支付机构删除其信息并依法承担责任。用户发现其信息不准确或者不完整的，有权要求非银行支付机构更正、补充。

**Article 32** When processing user information, a non-bank payment institution shall follow the principles of legality, legitimacy, necessity, and good faith, make public the rules for processing user information, clearly indicate the purpose, method, and scope of processing user information, and obtain the user's consent, except as otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.  
A non-bank payment institution shall process user information in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, relevant national provisions, and the agreement between the parties, and shall not collect user information that is irrelevant to the services it provides; it shall not refuse to provide services on the grounds that the user does not consent to the processing of its information or withdraws consent, unless the processing of relevant information is necessary for providing the services.  
A non-bank payment institution shall keep user information strictly confidential, take effective measures to prevent unauthorized access and the leakage, tampering, or loss of user information, and shall not illegally buy, sell, provide, or disclose user information.  
If a non-bank payment institution shares user information with its affiliated companies, it shall inform the user of the name and contact information of the affiliated company, and obtain the user's separate consent regarding the content of the information sharing and the purpose, period, method, and protection measures for information processing. The non-bank payment institution shall also make agreements with the affiliated company on the above content and the rights and obligations of both parties, and supervise the affiliated company's user information processing activities to ensure that such activities are lawful and compliant and that risks are controllable.  
If a user discovers that a non-bank payment institution processes its information in violation of laws, administrative regulations, relevant national provisions, or the agreement between the parties, the user shall have the right to request the non-bank payment institution to delete its information and bear liability according to law. If a user discovers that its information is inaccurate or incomplete, it shall have the right to request the non-bank payment institution to correct or supplement it.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　非银行支付机构相关网络设施、信息系统等被依法认定为关键信息基础设施，或者处理个人信息达到国家网信部门规定数量的，其在境内收集和产生的个人信息的处理应当在境内进行。确需向境外提供的，应当符合法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，并取得用户单独同意。  
非银行支付机构在境内收集和产生的重要数据的出境安全管理，依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定执行。

**Article 33** If the relevant network facilities, information systems, etc. of a non-bank payment institution are legally identified as critical information infrastructure, or if the volume of personal information processed reaches the amount prescribed by the national cyberspace administration, the processing of personal information collected and generated within the territory shall be carried out within the territory. If it is truly necessary to provide such information abroad, it shall comply with laws, administrative regulations, and relevant national provisions, and obtain the user's separate consent.  
The security management of the outbound transfer of important data collected and generated by a non-bank payment institution within the territory shall be governed by laws, administrative regulations, and relevant national provisions.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　非银行支付机构应当依照有关价格法律、行政法规的规定，合理确定并公开支付业务的收费项目和收费标准，进行明码标价。  
非银行支付机构应当在经营场所的显著位置以及业务办理途径的关键节点，清晰、完整标明服务内容、收费项目、收费标准、限制条件以及相关要求等，保障用户知情权和选择权，不得收取任何未予标明的费用。

**Article 34** A non-bank payment institution shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant price laws and administrative regulations, reasonably determine and disclose the charging items and charging standards for payment business, and clearly mark the prices.  
A non-bank payment institution shall, at prominent places in its business premises and at key points in the business handling channels, clearly and completely indicate the service content, charging items, charging standards, restrictions, and relevant requirements, etc., to safeguard users' right to know and right to choose, and shall not charge any fees not indicated.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　非银行支付机构应当及时妥善处理与用户的争议，履行投诉处理主体责任，切实保护用户合法权益。  
国家鼓励用户和非银行支付机构之间运用调解、仲裁等方式解决纠纷。

**Article 35** A non-bank payment institution shall promptly and properly handle disputes with users, fulfill its primary responsibility for complaint handling, and effectively protect the legitimate rights and interests of users.  
The State encourages the use of mediation, arbitration, and other methods to resolve disputes between users and non-bank payment institutions.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　非银行支付机构的控股股东、实际控制人应当遵守非银行支付机构股权管理规定，不得存在以下情形：  
（一）通过特定目的载体或者委托他人持股等方式规避监管；  
（二）通过违规开展关联交易等方式损害非银行支付机构或者其用户的合法权益；  
（三）其他可能对非银行支付机构经营管理产生重大不利影响的情形。  
同一股东不得直接或者间接持有两个及以上同一业务类型的非银行支付机构10%以上股权或者表决权。同一实际控制人不得控制两个及以上同一业务类型的非银行支付机构，国家另有规定的除外。

**Article 36** The controlling shareholders and actual controllers of a non-bank payment institution shall comply with the provisions on equity management of non-bank payment institutions, and shall not engage in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) evading regulation through special purpose vehicles or entrusting others to hold shares;  
(2) damaging the legitimate rights and interests of the non-bank payment institution or its users through illegal related-party transactions or other means;  
(3) other circumstances that may have a material adverse impact on the operation and management of the non-bank payment institution.  
The same shareholder shall not directly or indirectly hold 10% or more of the equity or voting rights of two or more non-bank payment institutions of the same business type. The same actual controller shall not control two or more non-bank payment institutions of the same business type, unless otherwise provided by the State.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　非银行支付机构应当按照规定向中国人民银行报送支付业务信息、经审计的财务会计报告、经营数据报表、统计数据，以及中国人民银行要求报送的与公司治理、业务运营相关的其他资料。

**Article 37** A non-bank payment institution shall, in accordance with regulations, submit to the People's Bank of China payment business information, audited financial and accounting reports, business data statements, statistical data, and other materials related to corporate governance and business operations as required by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　中国人民银行按照规定对非银行支付机构进行分类评级，并根据分类评级结果实施分类监督管理。  
中国人民银行依法制定系统重要性非银行支付机构的认定标准和监督管理规则。

**Article 38** The People's Bank of China shall conduct classification and rating of non-bank payment institutions in accordance with regulations, and implement differentiated supervision and management based on the classification and rating results.  
The People's Bank of China shall, according to law, formulate the criteria for identifying systemically important non-bank payment institutions and the rules for their supervision and management.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　中国人民银行依法履行职责，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）对非银行支付机构进行现场检查和非现场监督管理；  
（二）进入涉嫌违法违规行为发生场所调查取证；  
（三）询问当事人和与被调查事件有关的单位和个人，要求其对与被调查事件有关的事项作出说明；  
（四）查阅、复制当事人和与被调查事件有关的单位和个人的相关文件、资料和业务系统；对可能被转移、隐匿或者毁损的文件、资料和业务系统，可以予以封存、扣押；  
（五）经中国人民银行或者其省一级派出机构负责人批准，查询当事人和与被调查事件有关的单位账户信息。  
为防范风险、维护市场秩序，中国人民银行可以采取责令改正、监管谈话、出具警示函、向社会发布风险提示等措施。

**Article 39** When performing its duties according to law, the People's Bank of China shall have the right to take the following measures:  
(1) conducting on-site inspections and off-site supervision and management of non-bank payment institutions;  
(2) entering the place where an alleged illegal or irregular act occurs to conduct investigation and collect evidence;  
(3) interrogating the parties and the units or individuals involved in the investigated incident, and requiring them to explain matters related to the investigated incident;  
(4) consulting and copying the relevant documents, materials, and business systems of the parties and the units or individuals involved in the investigated incident; sealing up or seizing documents, materials, and business systems that may be transferred, concealed, or destroyed;  
(5) upon approval by the person in charge of the People's Bank of China or its provincial-level branch, inquiring about the account information of the parties and the units involved in the investigated incident.  
To prevent risks and maintain market order, the People's Bank of China may take measures such as ordering correction, regulatory talks, issuing warning letters, and issuing risk warnings to the public.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　中国人民银行依法履行职责，进行现场检查或者调查，其现场检查、调查的人员不得少于2人，并应当出示合法证件和执法文书。现场检查、调查的人员少于2人或者未出示合法证件和执法文书的，被检查、调查的单位和个人有权拒绝。  
中国人民银行依法履行职责，被检查、调查的单位和个人应当配合，如实提供有关文件、资料和业务系统，不得拒绝、阻挠和隐瞒。

**Article 40** When performing its duties according to law and conducting on-site inspections or investigations, the People's Bank of China shall have no fewer than two personnel conducting the on-site inspection or investigation, and they shall present their legal credentials and enforcement documents. If fewer than two personnel conduct the on-site inspection or investigation, or if they fail to present their legal credentials and enforcement documents, the unit or individual being inspected or investigated shall have the right to refuse.  
When the People's Bank of China performs its duties according to law, the units and individuals being inspected or investigated shall cooperate, truthfully provide relevant documents, materials, and business systems, and shall not refuse, obstruct, or conceal.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　非银行支付机构发生对其经营发展、支付业务稳定性和连续性、用户合法权益产生重大影响事项的，应当按照规定向中国人民银行报告。  
非银行支付机构的主要股东拟质押非银行支付机构股权的，应当按照规定向中国人民银行报告，质押的股权不得超过该股东所持有该非银行支付机构股权总数的50%。

**Article 41** If a non-bank payment institution encounters a matter that has a material impact on its operation and development, the stability and continuity of its payment business, or the legitimate rights and interests of users, it shall report to the People's Bank of China in accordance with regulations.  
If a major shareholder of a non-bank payment institution intends to pledge the equity of the non-bank payment institution, it shall report to the People's Bank of China in accordance with regulations, and the pledged equity shall not exceed 50% of the total equity of the non-bank payment institution held by such shareholder.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　非银行支付机构不得实施垄断或者不正当竞争行为，妨害市场公平竞争秩序。  
中国人民银行在履行职责中发现非银行支付机构涉嫌垄断或者不正当竞争行为的，应当将相关线索移送有关执法部门，并配合其进行查处。

**Article 42** A non-bank payment institution shall not engage in monopolistic or unfair competition practices that impair fair market competition order.  
If, in the performance of its duties, the People's Bank of China discovers that a non-bank payment institution is suspected of engaging in monopolistic or unfair competition practices, it shall transfer the relevant clues to the relevant law enforcement departments and cooperate with them in investigating and handling the matter.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　非银行支付机构发生风险事件的，应当按照规定向中国人民银行报告。  
中国人民银行可以根据需要将风险情况通报非银行支付机构住所所在地地方人民政府。地方人民政府应当配合中国人民银行做好相关风险处置工作，维护社会稳定。

**Article 43** If a risk incident occurs at a non-bank payment institution, it shall report to the People's Bank of China in accordance with regulations.  
The People's Bank of China may, as necessary, notify the local people's government of the place where the non-bank payment institution's domicile is located of the risk situation. The local people's government shall cooperate with the People's Bank of China in carrying out relevant risk disposal work and maintaining social stability.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　非银行支付机构发生风险事件影响其正常运营、损害用户合法权益的，中国人民银行可以区分情形，对非银行支付机构采取下列措施：  
（一）责令主要股东履行补充资本的监管承诺；  
（二）限制重大资产交易；  
（三）责令调整董事、监事、高级管理人员或者限制其权利。

**Article 44** If a non-bank payment institution experiences a risk incident that affects its normal operation or damages the legitimate rights and interests of users, the People's Bank of China may, depending on the circumstances, take the following measures against the non-bank payment institution:  
(1) ordering major shareholders to fulfill their regulatory commitments to supplement capital;  
(2) restricting major asset transactions;  
(3) ordering the adjustment of directors, supervisors, or senior officers, or restricting their rights.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　中国人民银行及其工作人员对监督管理工作中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密和个人信息，应当予以保密。

**Article 45** The People's Bank of China and its staff members shall keep confidential the State secrets, trade secrets, and personal information learned in the course of supervision and management.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　中国人民银行应当依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，完善非银行支付机构行业风险防范化解措施，化解非银行支付机构风险。

**Article 46** The People's Bank of China shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and relevant national provisions, improve the measures for preventing and resolving risks in the non-bank payment institution industry, and mitigate risks of non-bank payment institutions.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　未经依法批准，擅自设立非银行支付机构、从事或者变相从事支付业务的，由中国人民银行依法予以取缔，没收违法所得，违法所得50万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足50万元的，单处或者并处50万元以上200万元以下罚款。对其法定代表人或者主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处10万元以上50万元以下罚款。地方人民政府应当予以配合。

**Article 47** If a non-bank payment institution is established without lawful approval, or if a person engages in or disguisedly engages in payment business without authorization, the People's Bank of China shall, according to law, ban it, confiscate the illegal gains, and if the illegal gains are more than RMB 500,000, impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 500,000, impose a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 2 million, either alone or in combination. It shall also give a warning to its legal representative or principal responsible person, the directly responsible person in charge, and other directly liable persons, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000. The local people's government shall cooperate.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　以欺骗、虚假出资、循环注资或者利用非自有资金出资等不正当手段申请设立、合并或者分立非银行支付机构、变更非银行支付机构主要股东或者实际控制人，未获批准的，申请人1年内不得再次申请或者参与申请相关许可。申请已获批准的，责令其终止支付业务，撤销相关许可，没收违法所得，违法所得50万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足50万元的，并处50万元以上200万元以下罚款；申请人3年内不得再次申请或者参与申请相关许可。

**Article 48** If a person applies for the establishment, merger, or division of a non-bank payment institution, or for a change of major shareholders or actual controllers of a non-bank payment institution, by fraudulent means, false capital contribution, circular capital injection, or using non-own funds for capital contribution, and the application is not approved, the applicant shall not apply for or participate in applying for the relevant license again within one year. If the application has been approved, the person shall be ordered to terminate the payment business, the relevant license shall be revoked, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and if the illegal gains are more than RMB 500,000, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed concurrently; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 500,000, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 2 million shall be imposed concurrently; the applicant shall not apply for or participate in applying for the relevant license again within three years.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　非银行支付机构违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，责令其限期改正，给予警告、通报批评，没收违法所得，违法所得10万元以上的，可以并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足10万元的，可以并处50万元以下罚款；情节严重或者逾期不改正的，限制部分支付业务或者责令停业整顿：  
（一）未在名称中使用“支付”字样；  
（二）未建立健全或者落实有关合规管理制度、内部控制制度、业务管理制度、风险管理制度、突发事件应急预案或者用户权益保障机制；  
（三）相关业务系统、设施或者技术不符合管理规定；  
（四）未按照规定报送、保存相关信息、资料或者公示相关事项、履行报告要求；  
（五）未经批准变更[本条例第十三条](#art-13)第一款第一项、第二项或者第四项规定的事项，或者未按照规定设立分支机构。

**Article 49** If a non-bank payment institution violates the provisions of these Regulations in any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit, given a warning, criticized in a circular, and its illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are more than RMB 100,000, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains may be imposed concurrently; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 100,000, a fine of not more than RMB 500,000 may be imposed concurrently; if the circumstances are serious or it fails to make corrections within the time limit, part of its payment business shall be restricted or it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification:  
(1) failing to use the word "payment" in its name;  
(2) failing to establish, improve, or implement the relevant compliance management system, internal control system, business management system, risk management system, emergency response plan, or user rights protection mechanism;  
(3) the relevant business systems, facilities, or technologies do not comply with management provisions;  
(4) failing to submit or retain relevant information or materials, or disclose relevant matters, or fulfill reporting requirements in accordance with regulations;  
(5) without approval, changing matters specified in items (1), (2), or (4) of paragraph 1 of Article 13 of these Regulations, or failing to establish branches in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　非银行支付机构违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，责令其限期改正，给予警告、通报批评，没收违法所得，违法所得50万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足50万元的，并处100万元以下罚款；情节严重或者逾期不改正的，限制部分支付业务或者责令停业整顿，直至吊销其支付业务许可证：  
（一）未按照规定与用户签订支付服务协议，办理资金结算，采取风险管理措施；  
（二）未按照规定完成特约商户尽职调查、支付服务协议签订、持续风险监测等业务活动；  
（三）将核心业务或者相关技术服务委托第三方处理；  
（四）违规开立支付账户，或者除非法买卖、出租、出借支付账户外，支付账户被违规使用；  
（五）违规向用户支付利息等收益，或者违规留存银行账户、支付账户敏感信息；  
（六）未按照规定存放、划转备付金；  
（七）未遵守跨境支付相关规定；  
（八）未按照规定终止支付业务。

**Article 50** If a non-bank payment institution violates the provisions of these Regulations in any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit, given a warning, criticized in a circular, and its illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are more than RMB 500,000, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed concurrently; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 500,000, a fine of not more than RMB 1,000,000 shall be imposed concurrently; if the circumstances are serious or it fails to make corrections within the time limit, part of its payment business shall be restricted or it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification, up to the revocation of its payment business permit:  
(1) failing to sign a payment service agreement with users, handle fund settlement, or take risk management measures in accordance with regulations;  
(2) failing to complete business activities such as due diligence on special merchants, signing of payment service agreements, and continuous risk monitoring in accordance with regulations;  
(3) entrusting a third party to handle core businesses or related technical services;  
(4) opening payment accounts in violation of regulations, or, except for illegal buying, selling, renting, or lending of payment accounts, payment accounts being used in violation of regulations;  
(5) illegally paying interest or other income to users, or illegally retaining sensitive information of bank accounts or payment accounts;  
(6) failing to deposit or transfer the provident fund in accordance with regulations;  
(7) failing to comply with relevant cross-border payment provisions;  
(8) failing to terminate payment business in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　非银行支付机构违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，责令其限期改正，给予警告、通报批评，没收违法所得，违法所得50万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足50万元的，并处50万元以上200万元以下罚款；情节严重或者逾期不改正的，限制部分支付业务或者责令停业整顿，直至吊销其支付业务许可证：  
（一）涂改、倒卖、出租、出借支付业务许可证，或者以其他形式非法转让行政许可；  
（二）超出经批准的业务类型或者经营地域范围开展支付业务；  
（三）为非法从事非银行支付业务的单位或者个人提供支付业务渠道；  
（四）未经批准变更主要股东或者实际控制人，合并或者分立；  
（五）挪用、占用、借用备付金，或者以备付金为自己或者他人提供担保；  
（六）无正当理由中断支付业务，或者未按照规定处理电子支付指令；  
（七）开展或者变相开展清算业务；  
（八）拒绝、阻挠、逃避检查或者调查，或者谎报、隐匿、销毁相关文件、资料或者业务系统。

**Article 51** If a non-bank payment institution violates the provisions of these Regulations in any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit, given a warning, criticized in a circular, and its illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are more than RMB 500,000, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed concurrently; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 500,000, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 2 million shall be imposed concurrently; if the circumstances are serious or it fails to make corrections within the time limit, part of its payment business shall be restricted or it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification, up to the revocation of its payment business permit:  
(1) altering, reselling, renting, lending, or otherwise illegally transferring the payment business permit;  
(2) conducting payment business beyond the approved business type or geographic scope of operations;  
(3) providing payment business channels for units or individuals that illegally engage in non-bank payment business;  
(4) changing major shareholders or actual controllers, or merging or dividing, without approval;  
(5) misappropriating, occupying, or borrowing the provident fund, or using the provident fund to provide a guarantee for itself or others;  
(6) interrupting payment business without justifiable reasons, or failing to process electronic payment instructions in accordance with regulations;  
(7) engaging in or disguisedly engaging in clearing business;  
(8) refusing, obstructing, or evading inspections or investigations, or making false reports, concealing, or destroying relevant documents, materials, or business systems.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　非银行支付机构违反本条例规定处理用户信息、业务数据的，依照《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》、《中华人民共和国网络安全法》、《中华人民共和国数据安全法》等有关规定进行处罚。

**Article 52** If a non-bank payment institution processes user information or business data in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, it shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Data Security Law of the People's Republic of China, and other regulations.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　非银行支付机构未按照规定建立用户尽职调查制度，履行相关义务，或者存在外汇、价格违法行为的，以及任何单位和个人非法买卖、出租、出借支付账户的，由有关主管部门依照有关法律、行政法规进行处罚。  
非银行支付机构未经批准从事依法需经批准的其他业务的，依照有关法律、行政法规进行处罚。

**Article 53** If a non-bank payment institution fails to establish a user due diligence system in accordance with regulations, fails to perform related obligations, or commits foreign exchange or price-related illegal acts, and if any unit or individual illegally buys, sells, rents, or lends payment accounts, the relevant competent authorities shall impose punishments in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
If a non-bank payment institution engages in other businesses that require approval according to law without approval, it shall be punished in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　非银行支付机构的控股股东、实际控制人违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，责令其限期改正，给予警告、通报批评，没收违法所得，违法所得10万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足10万元的，并处10万元以上50万元以下罚款：  
（一）通过特定目的载体或者委托他人持股等方式规避监管；  
（二）通过违规开展关联交易等方式损害非银行支付机构或者其用户的合法权益；  
（三）违反非银行支付机构股权管理规定。  
非银行支付机构的主要股东违反本条例关于股权质押等股权管理规定的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 54** If the controlling shareholder or actual controller of a non-bank payment institution violates the provisions of these Regulations in any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit, given a warning, criticized in a circular, and its illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are more than RMB 100,000, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed concurrently; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 100,000, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000 shall be imposed concurrently:  
(1) evading regulation through special purpose vehicles or entrusting others to hold shares;  
(2) damaging the legitimate rights and interests of the non-bank payment institution or its users through illegal related-party transactions or other means;  
(3) violating the provisions on equity management of non-bank payment institutions.  
If a major shareholder of a non-bank payment institution violates the provisions of these Regulations on equity pledge or other equity management matters, it shall be punished in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　依照本条例规定对非银行支付机构进行处罚的，根据具体情形，可以同时对负有直接责任的董事、监事、高级管理人员和其他人员给予警告、通报批评，单处或者并处5万元以上50万元以下罚款。  
非银行支付机构违反本条例规定，情节严重的，对负有直接责任的董事、监事、高级管理人员，可以禁止其在一定期限内担任或者终身禁止其担任非银行支付机构的董事、监事、高级管理人员。

**Article 55** When imposing a penalty on a non-bank payment institution in accordance with these Regulations, depending on the specific circumstances, a warning or criticism in a circular may also be given to the directors, supervisors, senior officers, and other persons who bear direct responsibility, and a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 500,000 may be imposed solely or concurrently.  
If a non-bank payment institution violates the provisions of these Regulations under serious circumstances, the directors, supervisors, or senior officers who bear direct responsibility may be prohibited from serving as directors, supervisors, or senior officers of a non-bank payment institution for a certain period or for life.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　中国人民银行工作人员有下列情形之一的，依法给予处分：  
（一）违反规定审查批准非银行支付机构的设立、变更、终止申请等事项；  
（二）泄露履行职责过程中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密或者个人信息；  
（三）滥用职权、玩忽职守的其他行为。

**Article 56** If a staff member of the People's Bank of China commits any of the following acts, disciplinary action shall be taken according to law:  
(1) reviewing and approving the establishment, change, or termination of a non-bank payment institution in violation of regulations;  
(2) disclosing State secrets, trade secrets, or personal information learned in the performance of duties;  
(3) other acts of abuse of power or dereliction of duty.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 57** Whoever violates these Regulations and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　支付清算行业自律组织依法开展行业自律管理活动，接受中国人民银行的指导和监督。  
支付清算行业自律组织可以制定非银行支付机构行业自律规范。

**Article 58** The payment and clearing industry self-regulatory organization shall carry out industry self-regulation management activities according to law and accept the guidance and supervision of the People's Bank of China.  
The payment and clearing industry self-regulatory organization may formulate industry self-regulatory norms for non-bank payment institutions.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　本条例施行前已按照有关规定设立的非银行支付机构的过渡办法，由中国人民银行规定。

**Article 59** The transitional measures for non-bank payment institutions that have been established in accordance with relevant provisions before these Regulations come into force shall be prescribed by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　本条例自2024年5月1日起施行。

**Article 60** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2024.
