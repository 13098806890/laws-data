### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了健全国有重点金融机构监督机制，加强对国有重点金融机构的监督，根据《中华人民共和国商业银行法》、《中华人民共和国保险法》等有关法律的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of improving the supervision mechanism of key State-owned financial institutions, strengthening the supervision over key State-owned financial institutions, pursuant to the provisions of the Commercial Bank Law of the People's Republic of China, the Insurance Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称国有重点金融机构，是指国务院派出监事会的国有政策性银行、商业银行、金融资产管理公司、证券公司、保险公司等(以下简称国有金融机构)。  
国务院派出监事会的国有金融机构名单，由国有金融机构监事会管理机构(以下简称监事会管理机构)提出建议，报国务院决定。

**Article 2** As used in these Regulations, "key State-owned financial institutions" means State-owned policy banks, commercial banks, financial asset management companies, securities companies, insurance companies, etc., to which the State Council dispatches a board of supervisors (hereinafter referred to as State-owned financial institutions).  
The list of State-owned financial institutions to which the State Council dispatches a board of supervisors shall be proposed by the administrative organ of the boards of supervisors of State-owned financial institutions (hereinafter referred to as the administrative organ of the boards of supervisors) and submitted to the State Council for decision.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国有金融机构监事会(以下简称监事会)由国务院派出，对国务院负责，代表国家对国有金融机构的资产质量及国有资产保值增值状况实施监督。

**Article 3** The board of supervisors of a State-owned financial institution (hereinafter referred to as the board of supervisors) is dispatched by the State Council, is responsible to the State Council, and shall, on behalf of the State, supervise the asset quality and the preservation and appreciation of State-owned assets of the State-owned financial institution.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　监事会的日常管理工作由监事会管理机构负责。

**Article 4** The routine administration of the board of supervisors shall be the responsibility of the administrative organ of the boards of supervisors.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　监事会以财务监督为核心，根据有关法律、行政法规和财政部的有关规定，对国有金融机构的财务活动及董事、行长(经理)等主要负责人的经营管理行为进行监督，确保国有资产及其权益不受侵犯。  
监事会与国有金融机构是监督与被监督的关系，不参与、不干预国有金融机构的经营决策和经营管理活动。

**Article 5** The board of supervisors shall, with financial supervision as the core, supervise the financial activities of the State-owned financial institution and the management conduct of the directors, president (manager) and other principal responsible persons pursuant to relevant laws, administrative regulations and the relevant provisions of the Ministry of Finance, to ensure that State-owned assets and their rights and interests are not infringed.  
The relationship between the board of supervisors and the State-owned financial institution is that of supervision and being supervised, and the board of supervisors shall not participate in or interfere with the business decisions and management activities of the State-owned financial institution.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　监事会履行下列职责：  
(一)检查国有金融机构贯彻执行国家有关金融、经济的法律、行政法规和规章制度的情况；  
(二)检查国有金融机构的财务，查阅其财务会计资料及与其经营管理活动有关的其他资料，验证其财务报告、资金营运报告的真实性、合法性；  
(三)检查国有金融机构的经营效益、利润分配、国有资产保值增值、资金营运等情况；  
(四)检查国有金融机构的董事、行长(经理)等主要负责人的经营行为，并对其经营管理业绩进行评价，提出奖惩、任免建议。

**Article 6** The board of supervisors shall perform the following duties:  
(1) To inspect the implementation of the State's financial and economic laws, administrative regulations and rules by the State-owned financial institution;  
(2) To inspect the financial affairs of the State-owned financial institution, examine its financial accounting materials and other materials related to its management activities, and verify the authenticity and legality of its financial reports and fund operation reports;  
(3) To inspect the operating results, profit distribution, preservation and appreciation of State-owned assets, and fund operation of the State-owned financial institution;  
(4) To inspect the management conduct of the directors, president (manager) and other principal responsible persons of the State-owned financial institution, evaluate their management performance, and propose recommendations on rewards, punishments, appointment and removal.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　监事会一般每年对国有金融机构定期检查两次，并可以根据实际需要不定期地对国有金融机构进行专项检查。

**Article 7** The board of supervisors shall generally conduct regular inspections of the State-owned financial institution twice a year, and may also conduct special inspections of the State-owned financial institution from time to time as needed.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　监事会开展监督检查，可以采取下列方式：  
(一)听取国有金融机构主要负责人有关财务、资金状况和经营管理情况的汇报，在国有金融机构召开有关监督检查事项的会议；  
(二)查阅国有金融机构的财务报告、会计凭证、会计账簿等财务会计资料以及与经营管理活动有关的其他资料；  
(三)核查国有金融机构的财务、资金状况，向职工了解情况、听取意见，必要时要求国有金融机构主要负责人作出说明；  
(四)向财政、工商、税务、审计、金融监管等有关部门调查了解国有金融机构的财务状况和经营管理情况。  
监事会主席根据监督检查的需要，可以列席或者委派监事会其他成员列席国有金融机构董事会会议和其他有关会议。

**Article 8** In conducting supervision and inspection, the board of supervisors may adopt the following methods:  
(1) To hear reports from the principal responsible persons of the State-owned financial institution on financial, fund conditions and management conditions, and to convene meetings in the State-owned financial institution on matters related to supervision and inspection;  
(2) To examine the financial reports, accounting vouchers, accounting books and other financial accounting materials of the State-owned financial institution, as well as other materials related to its management activities;  
(3) To check the financial and fund conditions of the State-owned financial institution, inquire of the employees and seek their opinions, and when necessary, require the principal responsible persons of the State-owned financial institution to provide explanations;  
(4) To investigate and learn about the financial conditions and management conditions of the State-owned financial institution from the finance, industry and commerce, taxation, audit, financial supervision and other relevant departments.  
The chairman of the board of supervisors may, based on the needs of supervision and inspection, attend or appoint other members of the board of supervisors to attend the board of directors meetings and other relevant meetings of the State-owned financial institution.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　监事会指导国有金融机构的内部审计、稽核、监察等内部监督部门的工作，国有金融机构内部监督部门应当协助监事会履行监督检查职责。

**Article 9** The board of supervisors shall guide the work of the internal supervision departments of the State-owned financial institution, such as internal audit, check, and supervision, and the internal supervision departments of the State-owned financial institution shall assist the board of supervisors in performing its duties of supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　监事会每次对国有金融机构进行检查后，应当及时作出检查报告。  
检查报告的内容包括：财务、资金分析以及经营管理评价；主要负责人的经营管理业绩评价以及奖惩、任免建议；存在问题的处理建议；国务院要求报告或者监事会认为需要报告的其他事项。  
监事会不得向国有金融机构透露前款所列检查报告内容。

**Article 10** After each inspection of a State-owned financial institution, the board of supervisors shall promptly make an inspection report.  
The contents of the inspection report shall include: financial and fund analysis and management evaluation; evaluation of the management performance of the principal responsible persons and recommendations on rewards, punishments, appointment and removal; recommendations on handling existing problems; and other matters required by the State Council to be reported or that the board of supervisors deems necessary to report.  
The board of supervisors shall not disclose the contents of the inspection report referred to in the preceding paragraph to the State-owned financial institution.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　检查报告经监事会成员审核，并征求有关部门意见后，由监事会主席签署，经监事会管理机构报国务院。  
监事会成员对检查报告有原则性不同意见的，应当在检查报告中说明。

**Article 11** The inspection report shall, after being examined by the members of the board of supervisors and after soliciting opinions from relevant departments, be signed by the chairman of the board of supervisors and submitted to the State Council via the administrative organ of the boards of supervisors.  
If a member of the board of supervisors has a principled difference of opinion on the inspection report, such difference shall be stated in the inspection report.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　监事会在监督检查中发现国有金融机构的经营行为有可能危及金融安全、造成国有资产流失或者侵害国有资产所有者权益以及监事会认为应当立即报告的其他紧急情况，应当及时向监事会管理机构提出专项报告，也可以直接向国务院报告。

**Article 12** If, during supervision and inspection, the board of supervisors discovers that the business conduct of a State-owned financial institution is likely to endanger financial safety, cause loss of State-owned assets, or infringe upon the rights and interests of the owner of State-owned assets, or other emergency situations that the board of supervisors deems should be reported immediately, the board of supervisors shall promptly submit a special report to the administrative organ of the boards of supervisors, and may also directly report to the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国有金融机构应当定期、如实向监事会报送财务报告、资金营运报告，并及时报告重大业务经营活动情况，不得拒绝、隐匿、伪报。

**Article 13** A State-owned financial institution shall regularly and truthfully submit financial reports and fund operation reports to the board of supervisors, and shall promptly report on major business activities, and shall not refuse, conceal, or make false reports.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　监事会根据对国有金融机构进行监督检查的情况，可以建议国务院责成审计署和财政部、中国人民银行、中国证券监督管理委员会、中国保险监督管理委员会依据各自的职权依法对国有金融机构进行审计或者检查。  
监事会应当加强同财政部、中国人民银行、中国证券监督管理委员会、中国保险监督管理委员会的联系，相互通报有关情况。

**Article 14** The board of supervisors may, based on the circumstances of supervision and inspection of a State-owned financial institution, recommend that the State Council instruct the National Audit Office, the Ministry of Finance, the People's Bank of China, the China Securities Regulatory Commission, and the China Insurance Regulatory Commission to conduct an audit or inspection of the State-owned financial institution according to law pursuant to their respective functions and powers.  
The board of supervisors shall strengthen contact with the Ministry of Finance, the People's Bank of China, the China Securities Regulatory Commission, and the China Insurance Regulatory Commission, and shall exchange relevant information with each other.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　监事会由主席一人、监事若干人组成。  
监事分为专职监事与兼职监事：从有关部门和单位选任的监事，为专职；监事会中财政部和中国人民银行、中国证券监督管理委员会、中国保险监督管理委员会等派出代表担任的监事，监事会管理机构聘请的经过资格认证的专业会计公司的专家和国有金融机构工作人员的代表担任的监事，为兼职。  
监事会可以聘请必要的工作人员。

**Article 15** A board of supervisors shall be composed of one chairman and a certain number of supervisors.  
Supervisors are divided into full-time supervisors and part-time supervisors: supervisors selected from relevant departments and units are full-time; supervisors appointed as representatives from the Ministry of Finance, the People's Bank of China, the China Securities Regulatory Commission, the China Insurance Regulatory Commission, etc., experts from professional accounting companies with certified qualifications engaged by the administrative organ of the boards of supervisors, and representatives of the staff of the State-owned financial institution are part-time.  
The board of supervisors may engage necessary working staff.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　监事会主席人选按照规定程序确定，由国务院任命。监事会主席由副部级国家工作人员担任，为专职，年龄一般在60周岁以下。  
专职监事由监事会管理机构任命。专职监事由司(局)、处级国家工作人员担任，年龄一般在55周岁以下。  
监事会成员每届任期3年，其中监事会主席和专职监事、派出监事不得在同一国有金融机构监事会连任。

**Article 16** The candidate for the chairman of the board of supervisors shall be determined according to the prescribed procedures and appointed by the State Council. The chairman of the board of supervisors shall be a State functionary at the vice-ministerial level, serve full-time, and shall generally be under 60 years of age.  
Full-time supervisors shall be appointed by the administrative organ of the boards of supervisors. Full-time supervisors shall be State functionaries at the director-general (bureau) or division level, and shall generally be under 55 years of age.  
Members of the board of supervisors shall serve a term of three years. The chairman of the board of supervisors, full-time supervisors, and dispatched supervisors shall not serve consecutive terms on the same board of supervisors of a State-owned financial institution.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　监事会主席应当具有较高的政策水平，坚持原则，廉洁自持，熟悉金融工作或者经济工作。  
监事会主席履行下列职责：  
(一)召集、主持监事会会议；  
(二)负责监事会的日常工作；  
(三)审定、签署监事会的报告和其他主要文件；  
(四)应当由监事会主席履行的其他职责。

**Article 17** The chairman of the board of supervisors shall have a high policy level, adhere to principles, be honest and self-disciplined, and be familiar with financial work or economic work.  
The chairman of the board of supervisors shall perform the following duties:  
(1) To convene and preside over meetings of the board of supervisors;  
(2) To be responsible for the routine work of the board of supervisors;  
(3) To review and sign reports and other major documents of the board of supervisors;  
(4) Other duties that shall be performed by the chairman of the board of supervisors.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　监事应当具备下列条件：  
(一)熟悉并能贯彻执行国家有关金融、经济的法律、行政法规和规章制度；  
(二)具有财务、金融、审计或者宏观经济等方面的专业知识，比较熟悉金融机构经营管理工作；  
(三)坚持原则，廉洁自持，忠于职守；  
(四)具有较强的综合分析和判断能力，并具备独立工作能力。

**Article 18** A supervisor shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) To be familiar with and be able to implement the State's financial and economic laws, administrative regulations and rules;  
(2) To have professional knowledge in finance, banking, audit or macro-economics, and be relatively familiar with the management of financial institutions;  
(3) To adhere to principles, be honest and self-disciplined, and be dedicated to duties;  
(4) To have strong comprehensive analysis and judgment ability, and possess independent work ability.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　监事会主席和专职监事、派出监事、专家监事实行回避原则，不得在其曾经工作过的或者其近亲属担任高级管理职务的国有金融机构的监事会中任职。

**Article 19** The principle of avoidance shall apply to the chairman of the board of supervisors, full-time supervisors, dispatched supervisors, and expert supervisors, and they shall not hold office on the board of supervisors of a State-owned financial institution in which they have previously worked or in which their close relatives hold senior management positions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　监事会开展监督检查工作所需费用由国家财政拨付，由监事会管理机构统一列支。

**Article 20** The expenses required for the board of supervisors to carry out supervision and inspection work shall be allocated by the State treasury and disbursed uniformly by the administrative organ of the boards of supervisors.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　监事会成员不得接受国有金融机构的任何馈赠，不得参加国有金融机构安排、组织或者支付费用的宴请、娱乐、旅游、出访等活动，不得在国有金融机构中为自己、亲友或者其他人谋取私利。  
监事会主席和专职监事、派出监事、专家监事不得接受国有金融机构的任何报酬或者福利待遇，不得在国有金融机构报销任何费用。

**Article 21** Members of the board of supervisors shall not accept any gifts from the State-owned financial institution, shall not participate in banquets, entertainments, tours, visits or other activities arranged, organized or paid for by the State-owned financial institution, and shall not seek personal benefits for themselves, relatives or other persons in the State-owned financial institution.  
The chairman of the board of supervisors, full-time supervisors, dispatched supervisors, and expert supervisors shall not accept any remuneration or welfare benefits from the State-owned financial institution, and shall not reimburse any expenses in the State-owned financial institution.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　监事会成员必须对检查报告内容保密，并不得泄露国有金融机构的商业秘密。

**Article 22** Members of the board of supervisors must keep the contents of inspection reports confidential, and shall not disclose the trade secrets of the State-owned financial institution.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　监事会成员在监督检查工作中成绩突出，为维护国家利益做出重要贡献的，给予奖励。

**Article 23** Members of the board of supervisors who achieve outstanding results in supervision and inspection work and make important contributions to safeguarding the interests of the State shall be rewarded.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　监事会成员有下列行为之一的，依法给予行政处分或者纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)对国有金融机构的重大违法违纪问题隐匿不报或者严重失职的；  
(二)与国有金融机构串通编造虚假报告的；  
(三)有违反[本条例第二十一条](#art-21)、第二十二条所列行为的。

**Article 24** Where a member of the board of supervisors commits any of the following acts, he or she shall be given an administrative sanction or disciplinary sanction according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Concealing or failing to report major illegal or disciplinary problems of a State-owned financial institution, or being seriously derelict in duty;  
(2) Colluding with the State-owned financial institution to fabricate false reports;  
(3) Committing acts listed in Articles 21 and 22 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国有金融机构有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予纪律处分，直至撤销职务；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)拒绝、阻碍监事会依法履行职责的；  
(二)拒绝、无故拖延向监事会提供财务状况和经营管理情况等有关资料的；  
(三)隐匿、伪报有关资料的；  
(四)有阻碍监事会监督检查的其他行为的。

**Article 25** Where a State-owned financial institution commits any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions according to law, up to removal from office; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Refusing or obstructing the board of supervisors from performing its duties according to law;  
(2) Refusing or, without justifiable reasons, delaying the provision of materials such as financial conditions and management conditions to the board of supervisors;  
(3) Concealing or making false reports of relevant materials;  
(4) Engaging in other acts that obstruct the supervision and inspection of the board of supervisors.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国有金融机构发现监事会成员有违反[本条例第二十一条](#art-21)、第二十二条所列行为时，有权向监事会管理机构报告，也可以直接向国务院报告。

**Article 26** Where a State-owned financial institution discovers that a member of the board of supervisors has committed an act listed in Articles 21 and 22 of these Regulations, it shall have the right to report to the administrative organ of the boards of supervisors, and may also directly report to the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本条例自发布之日起施行。1997年10月20日国务院批准、1997年11月12日中国人民银行发布的《国有独资商业银行监事会暂行规定》同时废止。

**Article 27** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Interim Provisions on the Board of Supervisors of Wholly State-owned Commercial Banks approved by the State Council on October 20, 1997 and promulgated by the People's Bank of China on November 12, 1997 shall be abolished simultaneously.
