# 国家自然科学基金条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2024-11-08  
**生效日期**：2025-01-01

> （2007年2月24日中华人民共和国国务院令第487号公布　2024年11月8日中华人民共和国国务院令第796号修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范国家自然科学基金的使用与管理，提高国家自然科学基金使用效能，加强基础研究，培养科学技术人才，增强自主创新能力，实施创新驱动发展战略，推动实现高水平科技自立自强，根据《中华人民共和国科学技术进步法》，制定本条例。 第二条　国家自然科学基金工作坚持党中央集中统一领导。 国家自然科学基金工作应当面向世界科技前沿、面向经济主战场、面向国家重大需求、面向人民生命健康。 第三条　国家设立国家自然科学基金，用于资助基础研究，支持人才培养和团队建设。 国家自然科学基金资助工作实行尊重科学、发扬民主、提倡竞争、促进合作、激励创新、引领未来的方针，培育和践行社会主义核心价值观，遵循公开、公平、公正的原则。 第四条　国家自然科学基金主要来源于中央预算拨款。 国家鼓励地方人民政府、企业和其他组织投入资金开展联合资助，建立科技创新合作机制。国家鼓励社会力量向国家自然科学基金捐赠。 国家自然科学基金的资金应当全额纳入预算管理。 第五条　国家自然科学基金资助项目（以下简称基金资助项目），应当推动基础研究自由探索和目标导向有机结合，根据基金发展规划和年度基金项目指南予以确定。 确定基金资助项目应当充分发挥专家的作用，采取宏观引导、自主申请、平等竞争、同行评审、择优支持的机制。 第六条　国家自然科学基金管理机构（以下简称基金管理机构）依法管理国家自然科学基金，制定国家自然科学基金资助与管理制度，负责资助计划、项目设置，以及评审、立项、监督等组织实施工作，提升国家自然科学基金资助效能。 国务院科学技术主管部门依法对国家自然科学基金工作进行宏观管理、统筹协调和监督检查。国务院财政部门依法对国家自然科学基金的预算、财务、会计进行管理和监督，对基金的运行情况开展绩效评价，根据评价结果调整完善政策、改进管理、优化预算安排。审计机关依法对国家自然科学基金的使用与管理进行审计监督。 第七条　基金管理机构应当加强国家自然科学基金工作信息化建设，推动完善科研诚信管理信息共享、基金资助项目成果共享等机制，加强基金资助项目与其他科学技术计划项目的衔接与协调。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范国家自然科学基金的使用与管理，提高国家自然科学基金使用效能，加强基础研究，培养科学技术人才，增强自主创新能力，实施创新驱动发展战略，推动实现高水平科技自立自强，根据《中华人民共和国科学技术进步法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on Scientific and Technological Progress to regulate the use and management of the National Natural Science Fund, improve the effectiveness of the use of the National Natural Science Fund, strengthen basic research, train scientific and technological personnel, enhance independent innovation capabilities, implement the innovation-driven development strategy, and promote the achievement of high-level scientific and technological self-reliance and self-strengthening.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家自然科学基金工作坚持党中央集中统一领导。  
国家自然科学基金工作应当面向世界科技前沿、面向经济主战场、面向国家重大需求、面向人民生命健康。

**Article 2** The work of the National Natural Science Fund shall adhere to the centralized and unified leadership of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China.  
The work of the National Natural Science Fund shall be oriented towards the frontiers of world science and technology, the main battlefield of the economy, the major needs of the country, and the life and health of the people.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家设立国家自然科学基金，用于资助基础研究，支持人才培养和团队建设。  
国家自然科学基金资助工作实行尊重科学、发扬民主、提倡竞争、促进合作、激励创新、引领未来的方针，培育和践行社会主义核心价值观，遵循公开、公平、公正的原则。

**Article 3** The State establishes the National Natural Science Fund to fund basic research and support personnel training and team building.  
The funding work of the National Natural Science Fund shall implement the policy of respecting science, promoting democracy, encouraging competition, facilitating cooperation, stimulating innovation, and leading the future, cultivate and practice the core socialist values, and follow the principles of openness, fairness, and justice.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家自然科学基金主要来源于中央预算拨款。  
国家鼓励地方人民政府、企业和其他组织投入资金开展联合资助，建立科技创新合作机制。国家鼓励社会力量向国家自然科学基金捐赠。  
国家自然科学基金的资金应当全额纳入预算管理。

**Article 4** The National Natural Science Fund mainly comes from appropriations from the central budget.  
The State encourages local people's governments, enterprises, and other organizations to invest funds in carrying out joint funding and establish scientific and technological innovation cooperation mechanisms. The State encourages social forces to make donations to the National Natural Science Fund.  
The funds of the National Natural Science Fund shall be fully included in budget management.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家自然科学基金资助项目（以下简称基金资助项目），应当推动基础研究自由探索和目标导向有机结合，根据基金发展规划和年度基金项目指南予以确定。  
确定基金资助项目应当充分发挥专家的作用，采取宏观引导、自主申请、平等竞争、同行评审、择优支持的机制。

**Article 5** The projects funded by the National Natural Science Fund (hereinafter referred to as funded projects) shall promote the organic integration of free exploration and goal orientation in basic research, and shall be determined in accordance with the fund development plan and the annual fund project guidelines.  
In determining funded projects, the role of experts shall be fully utilized, and the mechanism of macro guidance, independent application, equal competition, peer review, and merit-based support shall be adopted.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家自然科学基金管理机构（以下简称基金管理机构）依法管理国家自然科学基金，制定国家自然科学基金资助与管理制度，负责资助计划、项目设置，以及评审、立项、监督等组织实施工作，提升国家自然科学基金资助效能。  
国务院科学技术主管部门依法对国家自然科学基金工作进行宏观管理、统筹协调和监督检查。国务院财政部门依法对国家自然科学基金的预算、财务、会计进行管理和监督，对基金的运行情况开展绩效评价，根据评价结果调整完善政策、改进管理、优化预算安排。审计机关依法对国家自然科学基金的使用与管理进行审计监督。

**Article 6** The National Natural Science Foundation (hereinafter referred to as the Foundation) shall manage the National Natural Science Fund according to law, formulate the funding and management system of the National Natural Science Fund, and be responsible for the organizational implementation of funding plans, project setting, review, project approval, supervision, etc., to improve the funding effectiveness of the National Natural Science Fund.  
The competent department of science and technology under the State Council shall, according to law, carry out macro management, overall coordination, and supervision and inspection of the work of the National Natural Science Fund. The finance department under the State Council shall, according to law, manage and supervise the budget, finance, and accounting of the National Natural Science Fund, conduct performance evaluation on the operation of the Fund, and adjust and improve policies, improve management, and optimize budget arrangements based on the evaluation results. The auditing authority shall, according to law, conduct audit supervision over the use and management of the National Natural Science Fund.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　基金管理机构应当加强国家自然科学基金工作信息化建设，推动完善科研诚信管理信息共享、基金资助项目成果共享等机制，加强基金资助项目与其他科学技术计划项目的衔接与协调。

**Article 7** The Foundation shall strengthen the informatization construction of the work of the National Natural Science Fund, promote the improvement of mechanisms for information sharing on scientific research integrity management and sharing of results of funded projects, and strengthen the connection and coordination between funded projects and other scientific and technological plan projects.

### 第二章　组织与规划

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　基金管理机构应当根据国民经济和社会发展规划、科学技术发展规划以及科学技术发展状况，制定基金发展规划和年度基金项目指南。基金发展规划应当明确优先发展的领域，年度基金项目指南应当规定优先支持的项目范围。  
国家自然科学基金应当设立专项资金，用于培养青年科学技术人才，支持在科学技术领域取得突出成绩且具有明显创新潜力的青年人才。  
基金管理机构制定基金发展规划和年度基金项目指南，应当广泛听取高等学校、科学研究机构、学术团体和有关国家机关、企业的意见，组织有关专家进行科学论证。年度基金项目指南应当在受理基金资助项目申请起始之日30日前公布。

**Article 8** The Foundation shall formulate the fund development plan and the annual fund project guidelines based on the national economic and social development plan, the scientific and technological development plan, and the status of scientific and technological development. The fund development plan shall specify the priority development areas, and the annual fund project guidelines shall specify the scope of projects to be given priority support.  
The National Natural Science Fund shall set up a special fund to train young scientific and technological personnel and support young talents who have achieved outstanding results in the field of science and technology and have obvious innovation potential.  
When formulating the fund development plan and the annual fund project guidelines, the Foundation shall extensively solicit opinions from institutions of higher learning, scientific research institutions, academic societies, relevant state organs, and enterprises, and organize relevant experts to conduct scientific demonstration. The annual fund project guidelines shall be published 30 days before the date on which applications for funded projects begin to be accepted.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　基金管理机构应当确定依托单位作为基金资助项目及其资金管理的责任主体。  
中华人民共和国境内具有独立法人资格、具备基础研究能力的高等学校、科学研究机构和其他公益性机构，以及其他符合国家规定的单位，可以在基金管理机构注册为依托单位。基金管理机构应当定期公布已注册的依托单位名单。  
依托单位应当建立和完善基金资助项目管理、科研诚信、科技伦理管理等制度，遵守科学技术活动管理规范。

**Article 9** The Foundation shall designate supporting institutions as the entities responsible for funded projects and the management of their funds.  
Institutions of higher learning, scientific research institutions, and other public welfare institutions within the territory of the People's Republic of China that have independent legal personality and basic research capabilities, as well as other units meeting the requirements of the State, may register with the Foundation as supporting institutions. The Foundation shall regularly publish the list of registered supporting institutions.  
Supporting institutions shall establish and improve systems for the management of funded projects, scientific research integrity, and scientific and technological ethics management, and comply with the management norms for scientific and technological activities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　依托单位在基金资助管理工作中履行下列职责：  
（一）组织申请人申请国家自然科学基金资助；  
（二）审核申请人或者项目负责人所提交材料的真实性、完整性和合法性；  
（三）提供基金资助项目实施的条件，保障项目负责人和参与者实施基金资助项目的时间；  
（四）跟踪基金资助项目的实施，管理和监督基金资助资金的使用；  
（五）配合基金管理机构、科学技术主管部门和相关行业主管部门对基金资助项目的实施和资金的使用进行监督、检查和绩效评价；  
（六）加强基金资助项目研究成果的知识产权管理，支持研究成果开放获取，促进研究成果转化；  
（七）对违背科研诚信和科技伦理的行为进行调查处理；  
（八）完成基金管理机构委托的其他工作。  
基金管理机构、科学技术主管部门和相关行业主管部门对依托单位的基金资助管理工作进行指导、监督。

**Article 10** Supporting institutions shall perform the following duties in the management of funded projects:  
(1) Organizing applicants to apply for funding from the National Natural Science Fund;  
(2) Verifying the authenticity, completeness, and legality of the materials submitted by applicants or project leaders;  
(3) Providing conditions for the implementation of funded projects and ensuring the time for project leaders and participants to implement funded projects;  
(4) Tracking the implementation of funded projects, and managing and supervising the use of funds for funded projects;  
(5) Cooperating with the Foundation, the competent department of science and technology, and relevant competent industry departments in the supervision, inspection, and performance evaluation of the implementation of funded projects and the use of funds;  
(6) Strengthening the management of intellectual property rights in the research results of funded projects, supporting the open access to research results, and promoting the transformation of research results;  
(7) Investigating and handling acts that violate scientific research integrity and scientific and technological ethics;  
(8) Completing other work entrusted by the Foundation.  
The Foundation, the competent department of science and technology, and relevant competent industry departments shall guide and supervise the management of funded projects by supporting institutions.

### 第三章　申请与评审

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　依托单位的科学技术人员具备下列条件的，可以申请国家自然科学基金资助：  
（一）具有承担基础研究课题或者其他从事基础研究的经历；  
（二）具有高级专业技术职务（职称）或者具有博士学位，或者有2名与其研究领域相同、具有高级专业技术职务（职称）的科学技术人员推荐。  
符合条件的从事基础研究的科学技术人员无工作单位或者所在单位不是依托单位的，取得依托单位的同意，可以依照本条例规定申请国家自然科学基金资助。  
申请人应当是申请基金资助项目的负责人。

**Article 11** Scientific and technical personnel of supporting institutions who meet the following conditions may apply for funding from the National Natural Science Fund:  
(1) Having experience in undertaking basic research projects or otherwise engaging in basic research;  
(2) Having a senior professional technical position (title) or a doctoral degree, or having a recommendation from two scientific and technical personnel in the same research field who hold a senior professional technical position (title).  
Where a scientific or technical person engaged in basic research who meets the conditions has no work unit or whose work unit is not a supporting institution, that person may, with the consent of a supporting institution, apply for funding from the National Natural Science Fund in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
An applicant shall be the project leader of the funded project applied for.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　申请人申请国家自然科学基金资助，应当以年度基金项目指南为基础确定研究项目，在规定期限内通过依托单位向基金管理机构提出书面申请。  
申请人申请国家自然科学基金资助，应当提交证明申请人符合[本条例第十一条](#art-11)规定条件的材料。  
申请人申请基金资助的项目研究内容已获得其他资助的，应当在申请材料中说明资助情况。申请人应当对所提交申请材料的真实性、完整性和合法性负责。  
申请人、参与者及其依托单位应当遵守科研诚信和科技伦理要求，签署科研诚信和科技伦理承诺书，承诺不从事危害国家安全、损害社会公共利益、危害人体健康、违背科研诚信和科技伦理的科学技术研究开发和应用活动，没有虚构、伪造、剽窃、篡改等行为。

**Article 12** An applicant applying for funding from the National Natural Science Fund shall determine the research project based on the annual fund project guidelines, and submit a written application through the supporting institution to the Foundation within the prescribed time limit.  
An applicant applying for funding from the National Natural Science Fund shall submit materials proving that the applicant meets the conditions set out in Article 11 of these Regulations.  
Where an applicant's research content of the funded project applied for has already received other funding, the applicant shall state the funding situation in the application materials. The applicant shall be responsible for the authenticity, completeness, and legality of the submitted application materials.  
Applicants, participants, and their supporting institutions shall comply with the requirements of scientific research integrity and scientific and technological ethics, sign a commitment letter on scientific research integrity and scientific and technological ethics, and commit not to engage in scientific and technological research, development, and application activities that endanger national security, harm the public interest, endanger human health, or violate scientific research integrity and scientific and technological ethics, and shall not have any acts of fabrication, fraud, plagiarism, or alteration.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　基金管理机构应当自基金资助项目申请截止之日起45日内，完成对申请材料的初步审查。有下列情形之一的，不予受理，通过依托单位书面通知申请人，并说明理由：  
（一）申请人不符合本条例规定条件的；  
（二）申请材料不符合年度基金项目指南要求的；  
（三）申请人、参与者因有违背科研诚信和科技伦理等行为被禁止承担或者参与财政性资金支持的科学技术活动的；  
（四）申请人、参与者申请基金资助项目超过基金管理机构规定的数量的。

**Article 13** The Foundation shall complete the preliminary examination of application materials within 45 days from the closing date of applications for funded projects. In any of the following circumstances, the application shall not be accepted, and the Foundation shall notify the applicant in writing through the supporting institution and state the reasons:  
(1) The applicant does not meet the conditions specified in these Regulations;  
(2) The application materials do not meet the requirements of the annual fund project guidelines;  
(3) The applicant or participant is prohibited from undertaking or participating in scientific and technological activities supported by fiscal funds due to acts that violate scientific research integrity and scientific and technological ethics;  
(4) The number of funded projects applied for by the applicant or participant exceeds the number specified by the Foundation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　基金管理机构应当聘请具有较高的学术水平、良好的职业道德的同行专家，对已受理的基金资助项目申请进行评审。评审专家聘请、评审活动管理和相关监督保障机制的具体办法由基金管理机构制定。

**Article 14** The Foundation shall engage peer experts with a high academic level and good professional ethics to review accepted applications for funded projects. The specific measures for the engagement of review experts, management of review activities, and related supervision and guarantee mechanisms shall be formulated by the Foundation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　基金管理机构对已受理的基金资助项目申请，应当先从同行专家库中随机选择3名以上专家进行通讯评审。基金管理机构应当根据通讯评审评分等情况确定进入会议评审的项目申请，组织专家进行会议评审。对因国家经济、社会发展特殊需要或者其他特殊情况临时提出的基金资助项目申请，可以只进行通讯评审或者会议评审。  
评审专家对基金管理机构安排其评审的基金资助项目申请认为难以作出学术判断或者没有精力评审的，应当及时告知基金管理机构；基金管理机构应当依照本条例规定，选择其他评审专家进行评审。

**Article 15** The Foundation shall first randomly select no fewer than three experts from the peer expert database to conduct a correspondence review of accepted applications for funded projects. The Foundation shall determine the project applications to be submitted to a meeting review based on the correspondence review scores and other circumstances, and organize experts to conduct a meeting review. For applications for funded projects submitted temporarily due to special needs of national economic and social development or other special circumstances, only a correspondence review or a meeting review may be conducted.  
If a review expert considers it difficult to make an academic judgment on the application for a funded project assigned by the Foundation for review or lacks the energy to conduct the review, the expert shall promptly inform the Foundation; the Foundation shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, select other review experts to conduct the review.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　评审专家按照国家自然科学基金资助导向，对基金资助项目申请从科学价值、创新性、社会影响以及研究方案的可行性等方面进行独立判断和评价，提出评审意见。  
评审专家对基金资助项目申请提出评审意见，还应当考虑申请人和参与者的研究经历、基金资助资金使用计划的合理性、研究内容获得其他资助的情况、申请人实施基金资助项目的情况以及继续予以资助的必要性。  
会议评审提出的评审意见应当通过投票表决。

**Article 16** Review experts shall, in accordance with the funding orientation of the National Natural Science Fund, independently judge and evaluate applications for funded projects from the aspects of scientific value, innovation, social impact, and feasibility of the research plan, and provide review opinions.  
When providing review opinions on applications for funded projects, review experts shall also consider the research experience of the applicant and participants, the reasonableness of the plan for using the funds of the Fund, the situation of other funding received for the research content, the applicant's track record in implementing funded projects, and the necessity of continued funding.  
The review opinions made at the meeting review shall be adopted by vote.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　为了鼓励原创性基础研究工作，对重大原创性、交叉学科创新等基金资助项目，基金管理机构可以制定专门的申请与评审规定。

**Article 17** To encourage original basic research work, the Foundation may formulate special application and review provisions for funded projects such as major original and interdisciplinary innovations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　基金管理机构根据本条例的规定和专家提出的评审意见，决定予以资助的研究项目。基金管理机构不得以与评审专家有不同的学术观点为由否定专家的评审意见。  
基金管理机构在作出资助决定前，应当按照国家有关规定，通过科研诚信管理信息系统，及时开展严重失信行为数据比对、核查等工作。  
基金管理机构决定予以资助的，应当及时书面通知申请人和依托单位；决定不予资助的，应当及时书面通知申请人和依托单位，并说明理由。  
基金管理机构应当整理专家评审意见，并向申请人提供。

**Article 18** The Foundation shall decide on the research projects to be funded in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and the review opinions provided by experts. The Foundation shall not reject an expert's review opinion on the grounds of holding a different academic view from the expert.  
Before making a funding decision, the Foundation shall, in accordance with relevant provisions of the State, timely conduct data comparison and verification of serious dishonest acts through the scientific research integrity management information system.  
Where the Foundation decides to grant funding, it shall promptly notify the applicant and the supporting institution in writing; where it decides not to grant funding, it shall promptly notify the applicant and the supporting institution in writing and state the reasons.  
The Foundation shall compile the review opinions of experts and provide them to the applicant.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　申请人对基金管理机构作出的不予受理或者不予资助的决定不服的，可以自收到通知之日起15日内，向基金管理机构提出书面复审请求。对评审专家的学术判断有不同意见，不得作为提出复审请求的理由。  
基金管理机构对申请人提出的复审请求，应当自收到之日起60日内完成审查。基金管理机构认为原决定符合本条例规定的，予以维持，并书面通知申请人；认为原决定不符合本条例规定的，撤销原决定，重新对申请人的基金资助项目申请组织评审专家进行评审、作出决定，并书面通知申请人和依托单位。

**Article 19** If an applicant disagrees with the decision made by the Foundation to not accept the application or not to grant funding, the applicant may submit a written request for review to the Foundation within 15 days from the date of receiving the notice. Disagreement with the academic judgment of a review expert shall not be used as a reason for requesting review.  
The Foundation shall complete the review of a request for review submitted by an applicant within 60 days from the date of receipt. If the Foundation considers that the original decision complies with the provisions of these Regulations, it shall uphold the decision and notify the applicant in writing; if it considers that the original decision does not comply with the provisions of these Regulations, it shall revoke the original decision, organize review experts to re-evaluate the applicant's application for a funded project, make a new decision, and notify the applicant and the supporting institution in writing.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　在基金资助项目评审工作中，基金管理机构工作人员、评审专家有下列情形之一的，应当申请回避：  
（一）基金管理机构工作人员、评审专家是申请人、参与者近亲属，或者与其有其他关系可能影响公正评审的；  
（二）评审专家自己申请的基金资助项目与申请人申请的基金资助项目相同或者相近的；  
（三）评审专家与申请人、参与者属于同一法人单位的。  
基金管理机构根据申请，经审查作出是否回避的决定。基金管理机构作出是否回避的决定，应当说明理由。  
基金管理机构发现其工作人员、评审专家有本条第一款规定的需要回避情形的，可以不经申请直接作出回避决定。  
基金资助项目申请人可以向基金管理机构提供3名以内不适宜评审其申请的评审专家名单，基金管理机构在选择评审专家时应当根据实际情况予以考虑。

**Article 20** In the course of the review of funded projects, staff members of the Foundation or review experts shall apply for withdrawal if they fall under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The staff member or review expert is a close relative of the applicant or participant, or has any other relationship with them that may affect a fair review;  
(2) The funded project applied for by the review expert is the same as or similar to the funded project applied for by the applicant;  
(3) The review expert and the applicant or participant belong to the same legal person unit.  
The Foundation shall, upon application and after examination, decide whether or not to grant withdrawal. The Foundation shall state the reasons for its decision on withdrawal or non-withdrawal.  
Where the Foundation discovers that its staff member or a review expert falls under the circumstances requiring withdrawal as specified in Paragraph 1 of this Article, it may directly make a withdrawal decision without application.  
An applicant for a funded project may provide the Foundation with a list of no more than three review experts who are unsuitable to review the applicant's application, and the Foundation shall take such list into consideration based on the actual circumstances when selecting review experts.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　基金管理机构工作人员、评审专家均应当签署科研诚信承诺书。基金管理机构工作人员不得申请或者参与申请国家自然科学基金资助，不得干预评审专家的评审工作。评审专家应当遵守科研诚信要求和评审行为规范，独立、客观、公正开展评审工作，不得由他人代为评审，不得有接受请托、说情干预等不公正评审行为，不得利用工作便利谋取不正当利益。  
基金管理机构工作人员、评审专家均应当依法承担保密义务，不得以任何方式披露未公开的评审专家的基本情况、评审意见、评审结果等与评审有关的信息。

**Article 21** Staff members of the Foundation and review experts shall each sign a commitment letter on scientific research integrity. Staff members of the Foundation shall not apply for or participate in applying for funding from the National Natural Science Fund, and shall not interfere with the review work of review experts. Review experts shall comply with the requirements of scientific research integrity and the norms of review conduct, conduct the review work independently, objectively, and fairly, shall not have others conduct the review on their behalf, shall not engage in unfair review acts such as accepting requests or intervention through influence, and shall not use their work convenience to seek improper benefits.  
Staff members of the Foundation and review experts shall bear the obligation of confidentiality according to law, and shall not disclose in any way information related to the review, such as the basic information of review experts, review opinions, and review results, that is not public.

### 第四章　资助与实施

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　申请人收到基金管理机构的基金资助通知后，即作为基金资助项目的负责人组织开展研究工作。  
依托单位和项目负责人自收到基金资助通知之日起20日内，按照评审专家的评审意见、基金管理机构确定的基金资助额度填写项目计划书，报基金管理机构核准。  
依托单位和项目负责人填写项目计划书，除根据评审专家的评审意见和基金管理机构确定的基金资助额度对已提交的申请书内容进行调整外，不得对其他内容进行变更。

**Article 22** After receiving the funding notice from the Foundation, an applicant shall act as the project leader of the funded project and organize and carry out the research work.  
Within 20 days from the date of receiving the funding notice, the supporting institution and the project leader shall fill in the project plan in accordance with the review opinions of the review experts and the funding amount determined by the Foundation, and submit it to the Foundation for approval.  
When filling in the project plan, the supporting institution and the project leader shall not make changes to any content other than adjusting the submitted application content based on the review opinions of the review experts and the funding amount determined by the Foundation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　基金管理机构对本年度予以资助的研究项目，应当按照《中华人民共和国预算法》和国库集中支付制度等有关规定，办理基金资助项目拨款。  
项目负责人应当依法依规使用基金资助资金，依托单位应当对项目负责人使用基金资助资金的情况进行管理和监督，在其职责范围内及时审批项目资金调整事项。项目负责人、参与者及其依托单位不得以任何方式虚报、冒领、贪污、侵占、挪用、截留基金资助资金。  
基金资助资金使用与管理的具体办法由国务院财政部门会同基金管理机构制定。

**Article 23** The Foundation shall, in accordance with the Budget Law of the People's Republic of China and the relevant provisions of the centralized treasury payment system, handle the allocation of funds for funded projects for the current year.  
Project leaders shall use the funds of the Fund in accordance with laws and regulations, and supporting institutions shall manage and supervise the use of the Fund by project leaders, and promptly approve matters concerning the adjustment of project funds within the scope of their duties. Project leaders, participants, and their supporting institutions shall not, in any way, submit false reports, fraudulently claim, embezzle, misappropriate, divert, or withhold the funds of the Fund.  
The specific measures for the use and management of the funds of the Fund shall be formulated by the finance department under the State Council jointly with the Foundation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　项目负责人应当根据项目计划书组织开展研究工作，做好基金资助项目实施情况的原始记录，通过依托单位向基金管理机构提交项目年度进展报告。  
依托单位应当审核项目年度进展报告，查看基金资助项目实施情况的原始记录，并向基金管理机构提交年度基金资助项目管理报告。  
基金管理机构应当对项目年度进展报告和年度基金资助项目管理报告进行审查。

**Article 24** Project leaders shall organize and carry out research work in accordance with the project plan, keep original records of the implementation of funded projects, and submit annual progress reports on the projects to the Foundation through the supporting institution.  
Supporting institutions shall review the annual progress reports on the projects, check the original records of the implementation of funded projects, and submit an annual management report on funded projects to the Foundation.  
The Foundation shall examine the annual progress reports on the projects and the annual management reports on funded projects.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　基金资助项目实施中，依托单位不得擅自变更项目负责人。  
项目负责人有下列情形之一的，依托单位应当及时提出变更项目负责人或者终止基金资助项目实施的申请，报基金管理机构批准；基金管理机构也可以直接作出终止基金资助项目实施的决定：  
（一）不再是依托单位科学技术人员的；  
（二）不能继续开展研究工作的；  
（三）其他客观原因导致项目无法继续实施的。  
项目负责人调入另一依托单位工作的，经所在依托单位与原依托单位协商一致，由原依托单位提出变更依托单位的申请，报基金管理机构批准。协商不一致的，基金管理机构作出终止该项目负责人所负责的基金资助项目实施的决定。

**Article 25** During the implementation of a funded project, the supporting institution shall not change the project leader without authorization.  
Where a project leader falls under any of the following circumstances, the supporting institution shall promptly submit an application for changing the project leader or terminating the implementation of the funded project to the Foundation for approval; the Foundation may also directly make a decision to terminate the implementation of the funded project:  
(1) The project leader is no longer a scientific or technical person of the supporting institution;  
(2) The project leader is unable to continue carrying out the research work;  
(3) Other objective reasons that make the project impossible to continue implementing.  
Where a project leader is transferred to work at another supporting institution, upon consensus between the current supporting institution and the original supporting institution, the original supporting institution shall submit an application for changing the supporting institution to the Foundation for approval. If no consensus is reached, the Foundation shall make a decision to terminate the implementation of the funded project under the responsibility of that project leader.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　基金资助项目实施中，研究内容或者研究计划需要作出重大调整的，项目负责人应当及时提出申请，经依托单位审核后报基金管理机构批准。

**Article 26** Where major adjustments need to be made to the research content or research plan during the implementation of a funded project, the project leader shall promptly submit an application, which shall be reviewed by the supporting institution and then submitted to the Foundation for approval.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　自基金资助项目资助期满之日起60日内，项目负责人应当通过依托单位向基金管理机构提交结题报告；基金资助项目取得研究成果的，应当同时提交研究成果报告。  
依托单位应当对结题报告进行审核，建立基金资助项目档案。依托单位审核结题报告，应当查看基金资助项目实施情况的原始记录。

**Article 27** Within 60 days from the date on which the funding period of a funded project expires, the project leader shall submit a completion report to the Foundation through the supporting institution; where research results have been achieved in the funded project, a research results report shall be submitted at the same time.  
The supporting institution shall review the completion report and establish archives for the funded project. When reviewing the completion report, the supporting institution shall check the original records of the implementation of the funded project.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　基金管理机构应当及时审查结题报告。对不符合结题要求的，应当提出处理意见，并书面通知依托单位和项目负责人。  
基金管理机构应当按照国家科学技术报告制度的要求，将结题报告、研究成果报告和基金资助项目摘要予以公布，促进基金资助项目成果的传播与共享。

**Article 28** The Foundation shall promptly examine the completion report. If the completion report does not meet the requirements for completion, the Foundation shall propose handling opinions and notify the supporting institution and the project leader in writing.  
The Foundation shall, in accordance with the requirements of the national scientific and technical report system, publish the completion report, the research results report, and the summary of the funded project, so as to promote the dissemination and sharing of the results of the funded project.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　发表基金资助项目取得的研究成果，应当注明得到国家自然科学基金资助。  
基金资助项目所形成的科学数据，应当由依托单位按照国家科学数据管理的有关规定汇交到相关科学数据中心。

**Article 29** When publishing research results obtained from a funded project, it shall be indicated that they have received funding from the National Natural Science Fund.  
The scientific data generated by a funded project shall be submitted by the supporting institution to the relevant science data center in accordance with the relevant provisions on national science data management.

### 第五章　监督与管理

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　基金管理机构应当对基金资助项目实施情况、依托单位履行职责情况进行以抽查为主要方式的监督检查，确保监督检查全面覆盖。抽查时应当查看基金资助项目实施情况的原始记录和资金使用与管理情况。抽查结果应当予以记录并公布。  
基金管理机构应当建立项目负责人和依托单位的基金资助项目信誉档案。

**Article 30** The Foundation shall conduct supervision and inspection on the implementation of funded projects and the performance of duties by supporting institutions, mainly through spot checks, to ensure comprehensive coverage of supervision and inspection. During spot checks, the original records of the implementation of funded projects and the use and management of funds shall be examined. The results of spot checks shall be recorded and published.  
The Foundation shall establish credit files for project leaders and supporting institutions regarding funded projects.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　基金管理机构应当定期对评审专家履行评审职责情况进行评估，评估时应当参考申请人的意见；根据评估结果，建立评审专家信誉档案。

**Article 31** The Foundation shall regularly evaluate the performance of review experts in fulfilling their review duties, and the opinions of applicants shall be taken into account during the evaluation; based on the evaluation results, credit files for review experts shall be established.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　基金管理机构应当在每个会计年度结束时，公布本年度基金资助的项目、基金资助资金的拨付情况以及对违反本条例规定行为的处罚情况等。  
基金管理机构应当配合国务院财政部门和科学技术主管部门开展基金绩效评价，并应当按照全面实施预算绩效管理的要求，建立健全绩效管理制度，定期对基金使用情况开展绩效评价，强化绩效评价结果应用，加大绩效信息公开力度。

**Article 32** At the end of each fiscal year, the Foundation shall publish the projects funded in that year, the allocation of funds of the Fund, and the punishment of acts that violate the provisions of these Regulations.  
The Foundation shall cooperate with the finance department and the competent department of science and technology under the State Council in conducting performance evaluation of the Fund, and shall, in accordance with the requirements for comprehensive implementation of budget performance management, establish and improve the performance management system, regularly conduct performance evaluation on the use of the Fund, strengthen the application of performance evaluation results, and increase the disclosure of performance information.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　任何单位或者个人发现基金管理机构及其工作人员、评审专家、依托单位及其相关工作人员、申请人或者项目负责人、参与者有违反本条例规定行为的，可以检举或者控告。  
基金管理机构应当公布联系电话、通讯地址和电子邮件地址。

**Article 33** Any unit or individual that discovers any violation of the provisions of these Regulations by the Foundation, its staff members, review experts, a supporting institution or its relevant staff members, an applicant or project leader, or a participant may report or lodge a complaint.  
The Foundation shall publish its contact telephone number, correspondence address, and email address.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　基金管理机构依照本条例规定对外公开有关信息，应当遵守国家有关保密规定。  
基金管理机构应当健全科技安全制度和风险防范机制，加强科学技术研究、开发与应用活动的安全管理，支持国家安全领域科技创新，增强科技创新支撑国家安全的能力和水平。

**Article 34** When disclosing relevant information to the public in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the Foundation shall comply with the relevant State provisions on confidentiality.  
The Foundation shall improve the scientific and technological security system and risk prevention mechanisms, strengthen the safety management of scientific and technological research, development, and application activities, support scientific and technological innovation in the field of national security, and enhance the capacity and level of scientific and technological innovation to support national security.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　对违反本条例规定，有科研诚信严重失信行为的个人、组织，按照国家有关规定记入科研诚信严重失信行为数据库，实施联合惩戒。

**Article 35** Individuals or organizations that violate the provisions of these Regulations and have serious acts of dishonesty in scientific research integrity shall be recorded in the database of serious dishonest acts in scientific research integrity in accordance with relevant State provisions, and joint disciplinary action shall be taken.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　申请人、参与者有下列行为之一的，由基金管理机构给予警告，正在申请基金资助的，取消其当年申请或者参与申请国家自然科学基金资助的资格；其申请项目已实施资助的，撤销原资助决定，追回已拨付的基金资助资金；情节较重的，1至3年不得申请或者参与申请国家自然科学基金资助；情节严重的，3至5年不得申请或者参与申请国家自然科学基金资助：  
（一）虚构、伪造、剽窃、篡改申请材料的；  
（二）以请托、贿赂等不正当方式干预评审工作的；  
（三）有其他违背科研诚信和科技伦理行为的。  
申请人、参与者有前款所列行为的，由依托单位按照人事管理有关规定取消其在一定期限内参加专业技术职称评审的资格。

**Article 36** Where an applicant or participant commits any of the following acts, the Foundation shall give a warning; if the person is applying for funding, the qualification to apply for or participate in applying for funding from the National Natural Science Fund in the current year shall be cancelled; if the project applied for has been funded, the original funding decision shall be revoked and the funds already allocated shall be recovered; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the person shall not apply for or participate in applying for funding from the National Natural Science Fund for one to three years; if the circumstances are serious, the person shall not apply for or participate in applying for funding from the National Natural Science Fund for three to five years:  
(1) Fabricating, forging, plagiarizing, or altering application materials;  
(2) Interfering with the review work by improper means such as requests or bribery;  
(3) Having other acts that violate scientific research integrity and scientific and technological ethics.  
Where an applicant or participant commits any of the acts specified in the preceding paragraph, the supporting institution shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions on personnel management, cancel the person's qualification to participate in the evaluation of professional technical titles within a certain period.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　项目负责人、参与者有下列行为之一的，由基金管理机构给予警告，暂缓拨付基金资助资金，并责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，撤销原资助决定，追回已拨付的基金资助资金；情节较重的，1至5年不得申请或者参与申请国家自然科学基金资助；情节严重的，5至7年不得申请或者参与申请国家自然科学基金资助：  
（一）擅自变更研究方向或者降低申报指标的；  
（二）不依照本条例规定提交项目年度进展报告、结题报告或者研究成果报告的；  
（三）提交弄虚作假的报告、原始记录或者相关材料的；  
（四）成果发表署名不实或者虚假标注资助信息的；  
（五）不配合监督、检查或者绩效评价工作的；  
（六）虚报、冒领、贪污、侵占、挪用、截留基金资助资金的；  
（七）虚构、伪造、剽窃、篡改研究数据或者结果的；  
（八）有其他违背科研诚信和科技伦理行为的。  
项目负责人、参与者有前款所列行为的，由依托单位按照人事管理有关规定取消其在一定期限内参加专业技术职称评审的资格。

**Article 37** Where a project leader or participant commits any of the following acts, the Foundation shall give a warning, suspend the allocation of funds of the Fund, and order correction within a specified time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, the original funding decision shall be revoked and the funds already allocated shall be recovered; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the person shall not apply for or participate in applying for funding from the National Natural Science Fund for one to five years; if the circumstances are serious, the person shall not apply for or participate in applying for funding from the National Natural Science Fund for five to seven years:  
(1) Changing the research direction without authorization or lowering the declared indicators;  
(2) Failing to submit the annual progress report on the project, the completion report, or the research results report in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(3) Submitting false reports, original records, or relevant materials;  
(4) Having false signatures in the publication of results or falsely marking funding information;  
(5) Failing to cooperate with supervision, inspection, or performance evaluation work;  
(6) Submitting false reports, fraudulently claiming, embezzling, misappropriating, diverting, or withholding funds of the Fund;  
(7) Fabricating, forging, plagiarizing, or altering research data or results;  
(8) Having other acts that violate scientific research integrity and scientific and technological ethics.  
Where a project leader or participant commits any of the acts specified in the preceding paragraph, the supporting institution shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions on personnel management, cancel the person's qualification to participate in the evaluation of professional technical titles within a certain period.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　依托单位有下列情形之一的，由基金管理机构给予警告，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，核减基金资助资金，并可以暂停拨付或者追回已拨付的基金资助资金；情节较重的，1至3年不得作为依托单位；情节严重的，向社会公布其违法行为，3至5年不得作为依托单位：  
（一）不履行保障基金资助项目研究条件的职责的；  
（二）不对申请人或者项目负责人提交的材料的真实性、完整性和合法性进行审核的；  
（三）不依照本条例规定提交项目年度进展报告、年度基金资助项目管理报告、结题报告和研究成果报告的；  
（四）组织、参与、纵容、包庇申请人或者项目负责人、参与者弄虚作假的；  
（五）擅自变更项目负责人的；  
（六）不配合基金管理机构监督、检查基金资助项目实施的；  
（七）虚报、冒领、贪污、侵占、挪用、截留基金资助资金的；  
（八）违反保密规定或者管理严重失职，造成不良影响或者损失的；  
（九）以请托、贿赂等不正当方式干预评审工作的；  
（十）不履行科研诚信和科技伦理相关管理职责的。

**Article 38** Where a supporting institution falls under any of the following circumstances, the Foundation shall give a warning and order correction within a specified time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, the funds of the Fund shall be reduced, and the allocation of funds already allocated may be suspended or recovered; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the institution shall not act as a supporting institution for one to three years; if the circumstances are serious, the illegal acts shall be made public to the society, and the institution shall not act as a supporting institution for three to five years:  
(1) Failing to perform the duty of ensuring the research conditions for funded projects;  
(2) Failing to verify the authenticity, completeness, and legality of the materials submitted by the applicant or project leader;  
(3) Failing to submit the annual progress report on the project, the annual management report on funded projects, the completion report, and the research results report in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(4) Organizing, participating in, conniving at, or covering up the falsification by the applicant or project leader or participant;  
(5) Changing the project leader without authorization;  
(6) Failing to cooperate with the Foundation in supervising and inspecting the implementation of funded projects;  
(7) Submitting false reports, fraudulently claiming, embezzling, misappropriating, diverting, or withholding funds of the Fund;  
(8) Violating confidentiality provisions or seriously failing in management, causing adverse effects or losses;  
(9) Interfering with the review work by improper means such as requests or bribery;  
(10) Failing to perform the management duties related to scientific research integrity and scientific and technological ethics.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　评审专家有下列行为之一的，由基金管理机构给予警告，责令限期改正；情节较重的，2至7年不得聘请其为评审专家；情节严重的，向社会公布其违法行为，不得再聘请其为评审专家：  
（一）不履行基金管理机构规定的评审职责的；  
（二）未依照本条例规定申请回避的；  
（三）披露未公开的与评审有关的信息的；  
（四）对基金资助项目申请有接受请托、说情干预等不公正评审行为的；  
（五）利用工作便利谋取不正当利益的。

**Article 39** Where a review expert commits any of the following acts, the Foundation shall give a warning and order correction within a specified time limit; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the expert shall not be engaged as a review expert for two to seven years; if the circumstances are serious, the illegal acts shall be made public to the society, and the expert shall no longer be engaged as a review expert:  
(1) Failing to perform the review duties specified by the Foundation;  
(2) Failing to apply for withdrawal in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(3) Disclosing information related to the review that is not public;  
(4) Engaging in unfair review acts such as accepting requests or intervention through influence regarding applications for funded projects;  
(5) Using work convenience to seek improper benefits.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　基金管理机构工作人员有下列行为之一的，依法依规给予组织处理、处分：  
（一）未依照本条例规定申请回避的；  
（二）披露未公开的与评审有关的信息的；  
（三）干预评审专家评审工作的；  
（四）利用工作便利谋取不正当利益的；  
（五）有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为的。

**Article 40** Where a staff member of the Foundation commits any of the following acts, organizational handling or disciplinary action shall be taken according to laws and regulations:  
(1) Failing to apply for withdrawal in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) Disclosing information related to the review that is not public;  
(3) Interfering with the review work of review experts;  
(4) Using work convenience to seek improper benefits;  
(5) Having other acts of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反有关财政法律、行政法规规定的，依法依规予以处罚、处分。

**Article 41** Anyone who violates the provisions of relevant fiscal laws and administrative regulations shall be punished or disciplined according to laws and regulations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　申请人或者项目负责人、参与者违反本条例规定，情节特别严重的，或者从事危害国家安全、损害社会公共利益、危害人体健康、违背科研诚信和科技伦理的科学技术研究开发和应用活动的，终身不得申请或者参与申请国家自然科学基金资助。  
违反本条例规定受到处理的科学技术人员，在处理期内不得聘请其为评审专家；情节特别严重的，终身不得聘请其为评审专家。

**Article 42** Where an applicant, project leader, or participant violates the provisions of these Regulations with especially serious circumstances, or engages in scientific and technological research, development, and application activities that endanger national security, harm the public interest, endanger human health, or violate scientific research integrity and scientific and technological ethics, that person shall be permanently disqualified from applying for or participating in applying for funding from the National Natural Science Fund.  
A scientific or technical person who has been sanctioned for violating the provisions of these Regulations shall not be engaged as a review expert during the period of sanction; if the circumstances are especially serious, that person shall be permanently disqualified from being engaged as a review expert.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** Anyone who violates the provisions of these Regulations and constitutes a violation of public security administration shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　基金管理机构在基金资助工作中，涉及项目组织实施费和与基础研究有关的学术交流活动、基础研究环境建设活动的基金资助资金的使用与管理的，按照国务院财政部门的有关规定执行。

**Article 44** Where the Foundation, in the work of funding projects, involves the use and management of funds of the Fund for project organization and implementation expenses, academic exchange activities related to basic research, and basic research environment construction activities, the relevant provisions of the finance department under the State Council shall apply.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例自2025年1月1日起施行。

**Article 45** These Regulations shall come into force on January 1, 2025.
