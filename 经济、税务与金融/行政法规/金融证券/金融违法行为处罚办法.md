**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1999-02-22  
**生效日期**：1999-02-22

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了惩处金融违法行为，维护金融秩序，防范金融风险，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purpose of punishing financial violations, maintaining financial order, and preventing financial risks.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　金融机构违反国家有关金融管理的规定，有关法律、行政法规有处罚规定的，依照其规定给予处罚；有关法律、行政法规未作处罚规定或者有关行政法规的处罚规定与本办法不一致的，依照本办法给予处罚。  
本办法所称金融机构，是指在中华人民共和国境内依法设立和经营金融业务的机构，包括银行、信用合作社、财务公司、信托投资公司、金融租赁公司等。

**Article 2** Where a financial institution violates the provisions of the state on financial administration, if the relevant laws or administrative regulations provide for penalties, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with such provisions; if the relevant laws or administrative regulations do not provide for penalties or the penalty provisions of the relevant administrative regulations are inconsistent with these Measures, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with these Measures.  
For the purpose of these Measures, "financial institution" means an institution that is established and operates financial business according to law within the territory of the People's Republic of China, including banks, credit cooperatives, finance companies, trust and investment companies, financial leasing companies, etc.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本办法规定的行政处罚，由中国人民银行决定；但是[本办法第二十四条](#art-24)、第二十五条规定的行政处罚，由国家外汇管理机关决定。  
本办法规定的纪律处分，包括警告、记过、记大过、降级、撤职、留用察看、开除，由所在金融机构或者上级金融机构决定。  
金融机构的工作人员依照本办法受到开除的纪律处分的，终身不得在金融机构工作，由中国人民银行通知各金融机构不得任用，并在全国性报纸上公告。金融机构的高级管理人员依照本办法受到撤职的纪律处分的，由中国人民银行决定在一定期限内直至终身不得在任何金融机构担任高级管理职务或者与原职务相当的职务，通知各金融机构不得任用，并在全国性报纸上公告。  
本办法所称高级管理人员，是指金融机构的法定代表人和其他主要负责人，包括银行及其分支机构的董事长、副董事长、行长、副行长、主任、副主任；信用合作社的理事长、副理事长、主任、副主任；财务公司、信托投资公司、金融租赁公司等金融机构的董事长、副董事长、总经理、副总经理等。

**Article 3** The administrative penalties provided for in these Measures shall be decided by the People's Bank of China; however, the administrative penalties provided for in Articles 24 and 25 of these Measures shall be decided by the state foreign exchange administration authority.  
The disciplinary sanctions provided for in these Measures include warning, demerit recording, serious demerit recording, demotion, removal from office, probation, and dismissal, and shall be decided by the financial institution where the person works or by its superior financial institution.  
Where a staff member of a financial institution is subject to the disciplinary sanction of dismissal in accordance with these Measures, such person shall be permanently prohibited from working in any financial institution, and the People's Bank of China shall notify all financial institutions not to employ such person and shall make a public announcement in a national newspaper. Where a senior officer of a financial institution is subject to the disciplinary sanction of removal from office in accordance with these Measures, the People's Bank of China shall decide that such person shall be prohibited from holding any senior management position or a position equivalent to the original position in any financial institution for a certain period or permanently, notify all financial institutions not to employ such person, and make a public announcement in a national newspaper.  
For the purpose of these Measures, "senior officer" means the legal representative and other principal responsible persons of a financial institution, including the chairman, vice chairman, president, vice president, director, and deputy director of a bank and its branches; the chairman, vice chairman, director, and deputy director of a credit cooperative; the chairman, vice chairman, general manager, deputy general manager, etc. of financial institutions such as finance companies, trust and investment companies, and financial leasing companies.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　金融机构的工作人员离开该金融机构工作后，被发现在该金融机构工作期间违反国家有关金融管理规定的，仍然应当依法追究责任。

**Article 4** Where a staff member of a financial institution, after leaving the financial institution, is found to have violated the provisions of the state on financial administration during his or her work at the financial institution, liability shall still be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　金融机构设立、合并、撤销分支机构或者代表机构的，应当经中国人民银行批准。  
未经中国人民银行批准，金融机构擅自设立、合并、撤销分支机构或者代表机构的，给予警告，并处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员，给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分。

**Article 5** A financial institution shall obtain approval from the People's Bank of China for the establishment, merger, or dissolution of a branch or representative office.  
Where a financial institution, without approval from the People's Bank of China, establishes, merges, or dissolves a branch or representative office without authorization, it shall be given a warning and shall also be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　金融机构有下列情形之一的，应当经中国人民银行批准：  
(一)变更名称；  
(二)变更注册资本；  
(三)变更机构所在地；  
(四)更换高级管理人员；  
(五)中国人民银行规定的其他变更、更换情形。  
金融机构未经中国人民银行批准，有前款所列情形之一的，给予警告，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；有前款第(四)项所列情形的，对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员，给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分。

**Article 6** A financial institution shall obtain approval from the People's Bank of China under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) changing its name;  
(2) changing its registered capital;  
(3) changing the location of the institution;  
(4) replacing senior officers;  
(5) other circumstances of change or replacement specified by the People's Bank of China.  
Where a financial institution, without approval from the People's Bank of China, falls under any of the circumstances listed in the preceding paragraph, it shall be given a warning and shall also be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if it falls under the circumstance listed in item (4) of the preceding paragraph, the senior officer directly responsible of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　金融机构变更股东、转让股权或者调整股权结构的，应当经中国人民银行批准；涉及国有股权变动的，并应当按照规定经财政部门批准。  
未经依法批准，金融机构擅自变更股东、转让股权或者调整股权结构的，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员，给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分。

**Article 7** Where a financial institution changes its shareholders, transfers its equity, or adjusts its equity structure, it shall obtain approval from the People's Bank of China; if it involves a change in state-owned equity, it shall also obtain approval from the financial department in accordance with regulations.  
Where a financial institution, without approval according to law, changes its shareholders, transfers its equity, or adjusts its equity structure without authorization, it shall be given a warning, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, it shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　金融机构不得虚假出资或者抽逃出资。  
金融机构虚假出资或者抽逃出资的，责令停业整顿，并处虚假出资金额或者抽逃出资金额5%以上10%以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员给予开除的纪律处分，对其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员给予记过直至开除的纪律处分；情节严重的，吊销该金融机构的经营金融业务许可证；构成虚假出资、抽逃出资罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 8** A financial institution shall not make false capital contributions or withdraw capital contributions.  
Where a financial institution makes false capital contributions or withdraws capital contributions, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification and shall also be fined not less than 5% but not more than 10% of the amount of false capital contributions or withdrawn capital contributions; the senior officer directly responsible of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of dismissal; other directly responsible persons in charge and directly liable persons shall be given a disciplinary sanction of demerit recording up to dismissal; if the circumstances are serious, the financial institution's license for engaging in financial business shall be revoked; if a crime of making false capital contributions, withdrawing capital contributions, or any other crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　金融机构不得超出中国人民银行批准的业务范围从事金融业务活动。  
金融机构超出中国人民银行批准的业务范围从事金融业务活动的，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分，对其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员给予记过直至开除的纪律处分；情节严重的，责令该金融机构停业整顿或者吊销经营金融业务许可证；构成非法经营罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 9** A financial institution shall not engage in financial business activities beyond the scope of business approved by the People's Bank of China.  
Where a financial institution engages in financial business activities beyond the scope of business approved by the People's Bank of China, it shall be given a warning, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, it shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal; other directly responsible persons in charge and directly liable persons shall be given a disciplinary sanction of demerit recording up to dismissal; if the circumstances are serious, the financial institution shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification or its license for engaging in financial business shall be revoked; if a crime of illegal business operation or any other crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　金融机构的代表机构不得经营金融业务。  
金融机构的代表机构经营金融业务的，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分，对其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员给予降级直至开除的纪律处分；情节严重的，撤销该代表机构。

**Article 10** A representative office of a financial institution shall not engage in financial business.  
Where a representative office of a financial institution engages in financial business, it shall be given a warning, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, it shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal; other directly responsible persons in charge and directly liable persons shall be given a disciplinary sanction of demotion up to dismissal; if the circumstances are serious, the representative office shall be dissolved.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　金融机构不得以下列方式从事帐外经营行为：  
(一)办理存款、贷款等业务不按照会计制度记帐、登记，或者不在会计报表中反映；  
(二)将存款与贷款等不同业务在同一帐户内轧差处理；  
(三)经营收入未列入会计帐册；  
(四)其他方式的帐外经营行为。  
金融机构违反前款规定的，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员、其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员，给予开除的纪律处分；情节严重的，责令该金融机构停业整顿或者吊销经营金融业务许可证；构成用帐外客户资金非法拆借、发放贷款罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 11** A financial institution shall not engage in off-balance-sheet business activities in the following manners:  
(1) failing to record or register deposits, loans, and other businesses in accordance with accounting systems, or failing to reflect them in accounting statements;  
(2) netting different businesses such as deposits and loans within the same account;  
(3) failing to include operating income in accounting books;  
(4) other manners of off-balance-sheet business activities.  
Where a financial institution violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph, it shall be given a warning, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, it shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible, other directly responsible persons in charge, and directly liable persons of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of dismissal; if the circumstances are serious, the financial institution shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification or its license for engaging in financial business shall be revoked; if a crime of illegally lending or granting loans with off-balance-sheet customer funds or any other crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　金融机构不得提供虚假的或者隐瞒重要事实的财务会计报告、统计报告。  
金融机构提供虚假的或者隐瞒重要事实的财务会计报告、统计报告的，给予警告，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分，对其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员给予记大过直至开除的纪律处分；情节严重的，责令该金融机构停业整顿或者吊销经营金融业务许可证；构成提供虚假财会报告罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 12** A financial institution shall not provide false financial accounting reports or statistical reports, or those that conceal important facts.  
Where a financial institution provides false financial accounting reports or statistical reports, or those that conceal important facts, it shall be given a warning and shall also be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal; other directly responsible persons in charge and directly liable persons shall be given a disciplinary sanction of serious demerit recording up to dismissal; if the circumstances are serious, the financial institution shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification or its license for engaging in financial business shall be revoked; if a crime of providing false financial reports or any other crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　金融机构不得出具与事实不符的信用证、保函、票据、存单、资信证明等金融票证。  
金融机构弄虚作假，出具与事实不符的信用证、保函、票据、存单、资信证明等金融票证的，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员、其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员，给予开除的纪律处分；构成非法出具金融票证罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 13** A financial institution shall not issue financial instruments such as letters of credit, letters of guarantee, negotiable instruments, certificates of deposit, and credit certificates that are inconsistent with the facts.  
Where a financial institution engages in fraud and issues financial instruments such as letters of credit, letters of guarantee, negotiable instruments, certificates of deposit, and credit certificates that are inconsistent with the facts, it shall be given a warning, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, it shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible, other directly responsible persons in charge, and directly liable persons of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of dismissal; if a crime of illegally issuing financial instruments or any other crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　金融机构对违反票据法规定的票据，不得承兑、贴现、付款或者保证。  
金融机构对违反票据法规定的票据，予以承兑、贴现、付款或者保证的，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员、其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员，给予记大过直至开除的纪律处分；造成资金损失的，对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员，给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分；构成对违法票据承兑、付款、保证罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 14** A financial institution shall not accept, discount, pay, or guarantee any negotiable instrument that violates the provisions of the Negotiable Instruments Law.  
Where a financial institution accepts, discounts, pays, or guarantees any negotiable instrument that violates the provisions of the Negotiable Instruments Law, it shall be given a warning, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, it shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible, other directly responsible persons in charge, and directly liable persons of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of serious demerit recording up to dismissal; if a fund loss is caused, the senior officer directly responsible of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal; if a crime of accepting, paying, or guaranteeing illegal negotiable instruments or any other crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　金融机构办理存款业务，不得有下列行为：  
(一)擅自提高利率或者变相提高利率，吸收存款；  
(二)明知或者应知是单位资金，而允许以个人名义开立帐户存储；  
(三)擅自开办新的存款业务种类；  
(四)吸收存款不符合中国人民银行规定的客户范围、期限和最低限额；  
(五)违反规定为客户多头开立帐户；  
(六)违反中国人民银行规定的其他存款行为。  
金融机构有前款所列行为之一的，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分，对其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员给予降级直至开除的纪律处分；情节严重的，责令该金融机构停业整顿或者吊销经营金融业务许可证。

**Article 15** When handling deposit business, a financial institution shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) absorbing deposits by raising interest rates without authorization or by raising interest rates in a disguised form;  
(2) knowingly or should know that funds belong to a unit, yet allowing them to be deposited in an account opened in an individual's name;  
(3) opening new types of deposit business without authorization;  
(4) absorbing deposits that do not conform to the customer scope, term, and minimum limit prescribed by the People's Bank of China;  
(5) opening multiple accounts for a customer in violation of regulations;  
(6) other deposit acts in violation of the provisions of the People's Bank of China.  
Where a financial institution engages in any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, it shall be given a warning, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, it shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal; other directly responsible persons in charge and directly liable persons shall be given a disciplinary sanction of demotion up to dismissal; if the circumstances are serious, the financial institution shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification or its license for engaging in financial business shall be revoked.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　金融机构办理贷款业务，不得有下列行为：  
(一)向关系人发放信用贷款；  
(二)向关系人发放担保贷款的条件优于其他借款人同类贷款的条件；  
(三)违反规定提高或者降低利率以及采用其他不正当手段发放贷款；  
(四)违反中国人民银行规定的其他贷款行为。  
金融机构有前款所列行为之一的，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员、其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员，给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分；情节严重的，责令该金融机构停业整顿或者吊销经营金融业务许可证；构成违法向关系人发放贷款罪、违法发放贷款罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 16** When handling loan business, a financial institution shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) granting credit loans to related parties;  
(2) granting secured loans to related parties on terms more favorable than those for similar loans to other borrowers;  
(3) raising or lowering interest rates in violation of regulations or using other improper means to grant loans;  
(4) other loan acts in violation of the provisions of the People's Bank of China.  
Where a financial institution engages in any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, it shall be given a warning, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, it shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible, other directly responsible persons in charge, and directly liable persons of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal; if the circumstances are serious, the financial institution shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification or its license for engaging in financial business shall be revoked; if a crime of illegally granting loans to related parties, illegally granting loans, or any other crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　金融机构从事拆借活动，不得有下列行为：  
(一)拆借资金超过最高限额；  
(二)拆借资金超过最长期限；  
(三)不具有同业拆借业务资格而从事同业拆借业务；  
(四)在全国统一同业拆借网络之外从事同业拆借业务；  
(五)违反中国人民银行规定的其他拆借行为。  
金融机构有前款所列行为之一的，暂停或者停止该项业务，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员、其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员，给予记大过直至开除的纪律处分。

**Article 17** When engaging in interbank lending activities, a financial institution shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) borrowing funds exceeding the maximum limit;  
(2) borrowing funds exceeding the maximum term;  
(3) engaging in interbank lending business without having the qualification for interbank lending business;  
(4) engaging in interbank lending business outside the national unified interbank lending network;  
(5) other interbank lending acts in violation of the provisions of the People's Bank of China.  
Where a financial institution engages in any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, it shall be suspended or prohibited from engaging in such business, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, it shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible, other directly responsible persons in charge, and directly liable persons of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of serious demerit recording up to dismissal.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　金融机构不得违反国家规定从事证券、期货或者其他衍生金融工具交易，不得为证券、期货或者其他衍生金融工具交易提供信贷资金或者担保，不得违反国家规定从事非自用不动产、股权、实业等投资活动。  
金融机构违反前款规定的，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员给予开除的纪律处分，对其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分；情节严重的，责令该金融机构停业整顿或者吊销经营金融业务许可证；构成非法经营罪、违法发放贷款罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 18** A financial institution shall not engage in transactions of securities, futures, or other derivative financial instruments in violation of state regulations, shall not provide credit funds or guarantees for transactions of securities, futures, or other derivative financial instruments, and shall not engage in investment activities such as in non-self-use real estate, equity, or industrial enterprises in violation of state regulations.  
Where a financial institution violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph, it shall be given a warning, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, it shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of dismissal; other directly responsible persons in charge and directly liable persons shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal; if the circumstances are serious, the financial institution shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification or its license for engaging in financial business shall be revoked; if a crime of illegal business operation, illegally granting loans, or any other crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　金融机构应当遵守中国人民银行有关现金管理的规定，不得允许单位或者个人超限额提取现金。  
金融机构违反中国人民银行有关现金管理的规定，允许单位或者个人超限额提取现金的，给予警告，并处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员、其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员，给予记大过直至开除的纪律处分。

**Article 19** A financial institution shall comply with the provisions of the People's Bank of China on cash management, and shall not allow any unit or individual to withdraw cash in excess of the prescribed limit.  
Where a financial institution violates the provisions of the People's Bank of China on cash management and allows any unit or individual to withdraw cash in excess of the prescribed limit, it shall be given a warning and shall also be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible, other directly responsible persons in charge, and directly liable persons of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of serious demerit recording up to dismissal.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　金融机构应当遵守中国人民银行有关信用卡管理的规定，不得违反规定对持卡人透支或者帮助持卡人利用信用卡套取现金。  
金融机构违反中国人民银行有关信用卡管理的规定，对持卡人透支或者帮助持卡人利用信用卡套取现金的，给予警告，并处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员、其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员，给予记大过直至开除的纪律处分。

**Article 20** A financial institution shall comply with the provisions of the People's Bank of China on credit card management, and shall not, in violation of regulations, allow cardholders to overdraw or assist cardholders to use credit cards to obtain cash.  
Where a financial institution violates the provisions of the People's Bank of China on credit card management and allows cardholders to overdraw or assists cardholders to use credit cards to obtain cash, it shall be given a warning and shall also be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible, other directly responsible persons in charge, and directly liable persons of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of serious demerit recording up to dismissal.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　金融机构应当遵守中国人民银行有关资产负债比例管理的规定。  
金融机构违反中国人民银行有关资产负债比例管理规定的，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员，给予记大过直至开除的纪律处分。

**Article 21** A financial institution shall comply with the provisions of the People's Bank of China on asset-liability ratio management.  
Where a financial institution violates the provisions of the People's Bank of China on asset-liability ratio management, it shall be given a warning, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, it shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of serious demerit recording up to dismissal.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　金融机构不得占压财政存款或者资金。  
金融机构占压财政存款或者资金的，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分，对其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员给予降级直至开除的纪律处分。

**Article 22** A financial institution shall not retain fiscal deposits or funds without authorization.  
Where a financial institution retains fiscal deposits or funds without authorization, it shall be given a warning, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, it shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal; other directly responsible persons in charge and directly liable persons shall be given a disciplinary sanction of demotion up to dismissal.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　金融机构应当依法协助税务机关、海关办理对纳税人存款的冻结、扣划。  
金融机构违反前款规定，造成税款流失的，给予警告，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员、其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员，给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成妨害公务罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 23** A financial institution shall, according to law, assist tax authorities and customs in the freezing and deduction of taxpayers' deposits.  
Where a financial institution violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph, resulting in tax loss, it shall be given a warning and shall also be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible, other directly responsible persons in charge, and directly liable persons of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal; if an act violating public security administration is constituted, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime of obstructing public service or any other crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　经营外汇业务的金融机构应当遵守国家外汇管理规定。  
经营外汇业务的金融机构违反国家外汇管理规定的，依照外汇管理条例的规定，给予行政处罚；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员、其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员，给予记过直至开除的纪律处分；情节严重的，对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员，给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 24** A financial institution engaged in foreign exchange business shall comply with the state provisions on foreign exchange administration.  
Where a financial institution engaged in foreign exchange business violates the state provisions on foreign exchange administration, an administrative penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Foreign Exchange Administration; the senior officer directly responsible, other directly responsible persons in charge, and directly liable persons of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of demerit recording up to dismissal; if the circumstances are serious, the senior officer directly responsible of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　经营外汇业务的金融机构，不得有下列行为：  
(一)对大额购汇、频繁购汇、存取大额外币现钞等异常情况不及时报告；  
(二)未按照规定办理国际收支申报。  
经营外汇业务的金融机构有前款所列行为之一的，给予警告，并处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员、其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员，给予记过直至开除的纪律处分；情节严重的，对该金融机构直接负责的高级管理人员，给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分；构成签订、履行合同失职被骗罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 25** A financial institution engaged in foreign exchange business shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) failing to promptly report abnormal situations such as large-amount purchase of foreign exchange, frequent purchase of foreign exchange, and deposit or withdrawal of large-amount foreign currency cash;  
(2) failing to handle the declaration of balance of payments in accordance with regulations.  
Where a financial institution engaged in foreign exchange business engages in any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, it shall be given a warning and shall also be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible, other directly responsible persons in charge, and directly liable persons of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of demerit recording up to dismissal; if the circumstances are serious, the senior officer directly responsible of the financial institution shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal; if a crime of being defrauded due to dereliction of duty in signing or performing a contract or any other crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　商业银行不得为证券、期货交易资金清算透支或者为新股申购透支。  
商业银行为证券、期货交易资金清算透支或者为新股申购透支的，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；对该商业银行直接负责的高级管理人员给予开除的纪律处分，对其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分。

**Article 26** A commercial bank shall not overdraw for the settlement of funds for securities or futures transactions, or overdraw for the subscription of new shares.  
Where a commercial bank overdraws for the settlement of funds for securities or futures transactions, or overdraws for the subscription of new shares, it shall be given a warning, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, it shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible of the commercial bank shall be given a disciplinary sanction of dismissal; other directly responsible persons in charge and directly liable persons shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　财务公司不得有下列行为：  
(一)超过中国人民银行批准的规模发行财务公司债券；  
(二)吸收非集团成员单位存款或者向非集团成员单位发放贷款；  
(三)违反规定向非集团成员单位提供金融服务；  
(四)违反中国人民银行规定的其他行为。  
财务公司有前款所列行为之一的，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；对该财务公司直接负责的高级管理人员、其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员，给予记过直至开除的纪律处分；情节严重的，责令该财务公司停业整顿，对直接负责的高级管理人员给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分；构成非法吸收公众存款罪、擅自发行股票、公司企业债券罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 27** A finance company shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) issuing bonds of the finance company in an amount exceeding the scale approved by the People's Bank of China;  
(2) absorbing deposits from non-group member units or granting loans to non-group member units;  
(3) providing financial services to non-group member units in violation of regulations;  
(4) other acts in violation of the provisions of the People's Bank of China.  
Where a finance company engages in any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, it shall be given a warning, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, it shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible, other directly responsible persons in charge, and directly liable persons of the finance company shall be given a disciplinary sanction of demerit recording up to dismissal; if the circumstances are serious, the finance company shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, and the senior officer directly responsible shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal; if a crime of illegally absorbing public deposits, illegally issuing shares or corporate bonds, or any other crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　信托投资公司不得以办理委托、信托业务名义吸收公众存款、发放贷款，不得违反国家规定办理委托、信托业务。  
信托投资公司违反前款规定的，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；对该信托投资公司直接负责的高级管理人员、其他直接负责的主管人员和直接责任人员，给予记大过直至开除的纪律处分；情节严重的，暂停或者停止该项业务，对直接负责的高级管理人员给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分；构成非法吸收公众存款罪、集资诈骗罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** A trust and investment company shall not absorb public deposits or grant loans in the name of handling entrusted or trust business, and shall not handle entrusted or trust business in violation of state regulations.  
Where a trust and investment company violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph, it shall be given a warning, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, it shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; the senior officer directly responsible, other directly responsible persons in charge, and directly liable persons of the trust and investment company shall be given a disciplinary sanction of serious demerit recording up to dismissal; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be suspended or prohibited from engaging in such business, and the senior officer directly responsible shall be given a disciplinary sanction of removal from office up to dismissal; if a crime of illegally absorbing public deposits, fund-raising fraud, or any other crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　金融机构缴纳的罚款和被没收的违法所得，不得列入该金融机构的成本、费用。

**Article 29** Fines paid and illegal gains confiscated from a financial institution shall not be included in the costs or expenses of the financial institution.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对中国人民银行所属从事金融业务的机构的金融违法行为的处罚，适用本办法。

**Article 30** These Measures shall apply to the punishment of financial violations by institutions engaged in financial business under the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对证券违法行为的处罚，依照国家有关证券管理的法律、行政法规执行，不适用本办法。  
对保险违法行为的处罚，依照国家有关保险管理的法律、行政法规执行，不适用本办法。

**Article 31** The punishment of securities violations shall be implemented in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations of the state on securities administration, and these Measures shall not apply.  
The punishment of insurance violations shall be implemented in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations of the state on insurance administration, and these Measures shall not apply.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本办法自发布之日起施行。

**Article 32** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
