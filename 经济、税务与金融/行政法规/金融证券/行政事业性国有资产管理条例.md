# 行政事业性国有资产管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2021-02-01  
**生效日期**：2021-04-01

> 第二条　行政事业性国有资产，是指行政单位、事业单位通过以下方式取得或者形成的资产： （一）使用财政资金形成的资产； （二）接受调拨或者划转、置换形成的资产； （三）接受捐赠并确认为国有的资产； （四）其他国有资产。 第三条　行政事业性国有资产属于国家所有，实行政府分级监管、各部门及其所属单位直接支配的管理体制。 第四条　各级人民政府应当建立健全行政事业性国有资产管理机制，加强对本级行政事业性国有资产的管理，审查、批准重大行政事业性国有资产管理事项。 第五条　国务院财政部门负责制定行政事业单位国有资产管理规章制度并负责组织实施和监督检查，牵头编制行政事业性国有资产管理情况报告。 国务院机关事务管理部门和有关机关事务管理部门会同有关部门依法依规履行相关中央行政事业单位国有资产管理职责，制定中央行政事业单位国有资产管理具体制度和办法并组织实施，接受国务院财政部门的指导和监督检查。 相关部门根据职责规定，按照集中统一、分类分级原则，加强中央行政事业单位国有资产管理，优化管理手段，提高管理效率。 第六条　各部门根据职责负责本部门及其所属单位国有资产管理工作，应当明确管理责任，指导、监督所属单位国有资产管理工作。 各部门所属单位负责本单位行政事业性国有资产的具体管理，应当建立和完善内部控制管理制度。 第七条　各部门及其所属单位管理行政事业性国有资产应当遵循安全规范、节约高效、公开透明、权责一致的原则，实现实物管理与价值管理相统一，资产管理与预算管理、财务管理相结合。 第二章　资产配置、使用和处置 第八条　各部门及其所属单位应当根据依法履行职能和事业发展的需要，结合资产存量、资产配置标准、绩效目标和财政承受能力配置资产。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　行政事业性国有资产属于国家所有，实行政府分级监管、各部门及其所属单位直接支配的管理体制。

**Article 3** State-owned assets in administrative and public institutions shall belong to the State, and a management system under which the government exercises supervision at different levels and each department and its subordinate units have direct control shall be implemented.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级人民政府应当建立健全行政事业性国有资产管理机制，加强对本级行政事业性国有资产的管理，审查、批准重大行政事业性国有资产管理事项。

**Article 4** People's governments at all levels shall establish and improve the management mechanism for state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions, strengthen the management of such assets at their respective levels, and review and approve major matters concerning the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院财政部门负责制定行政事业单位国有资产管理规章制度并负责组织实施和监督检查，牵头编制行政事业性国有资产管理情况报告。  
国务院机关事务管理部门和有关机关事务管理部门会同有关部门依法依规履行相关中央行政事业单位国有资产管理职责，制定中央行政事业单位国有资产管理具体制度和办法并组织实施，接受国务院财政部门的指导和监督检查。  
相关部门根据职责规定，按照集中统一、分类分级原则，加强中央行政事业单位国有资产管理，优化管理手段，提高管理效率。

**Article 5** The finance department of the State Council shall be responsible for formulating the rules and regulations on the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions, organizing their implementation and supervising and inspecting them, and taking the lead in compiling reports on the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions.  
The government offices administration department of the State Council and relevant government offices administration departments shall, together with relevant departments, perform the duties for the management of state-owned assets in relevant central administrative and public institutions according to laws and regulations, formulate specific systems and measures for the management of state-owned assets in central administrative and public institutions, organize their implementation, and accept the guidance, supervision and inspection of the finance department of the State Council.  
Relevant departments shall, in accordance with their functions and duties and based on the principle of centralized and unified management, classified and hierarchical management, strengthen the management of state-owned assets in central administrative and public institutions, optimize management methods, and improve management efficiency.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各部门根据职责负责本部门及其所属单位国有资产管理工作，应当明确管理责任，指导、监督所属单位国有资产管理工作。  
各部门所属单位负责本单位行政事业性国有资产的具体管理，应当建立和完善内部控制管理制度。

**Article 6** Each department shall, in accordance with its functions, be responsible for the management of state-owned assets of its own department and its subordinate units, shall clearly define management responsibilities, and shall guide and supervise the management of state-owned assets by its subordinate units.  
Subordinate units of each department shall be responsible for the specific management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions of their own unit, and shall establish and improve internal control management systems.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各部门及其所属单位管理行政事业性国有资产应当遵循安全规范、节约高效、公开透明、权责一致的原则，实现实物管理与价值管理相统一，资产管理与预算管理、财务管理相结合。

**Article 7** In managing state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions, each department and its subordinate units shall follow the principles of safety and standardization, economy and efficiency, openness and transparency, and consistency of powers and responsibilities, achieve the unification of physical management and value management, and combine asset management with budget management and financial management.

### 第二章　资产配置、使用和处置

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　各部门及其所属单位应当根据依法履行职能和事业发展的需要，结合资产存量、资产配置标准、绩效目标和财政承受能力配置资产。

**Article 8** Each department and its subordinate units shall allocate assets based on the needs of performing functions according to law and the development of undertakings, taking into account the existing stock of assets, asset allocation standards, performance objectives, and fiscal affordability.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各部门及其所属单位应当合理选择资产配置方式，资产配置重大事项应当经可行性研究和集体决策，资产价值较高的按照国家有关规定进行资产评估，并履行审批程序。  
资产配置包括调剂、购置、建设、租用、接受捐赠等方式。

**Article 9** Each department and its subordinate units shall reasonably choose the means of asset allocation. Major matters of asset allocation shall be subject to feasibility studies and collective decision-making, and assets of high value shall undergo asset appraisal in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and go through approval procedures.  
Asset allocation includes such means as transfer, purchase, construction, lease, and acceptance of donations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上人民政府应当组织建立、完善资产配置标准体系，明确配置的数量、价值、等级、最低使用年限等标准。  
资产配置标准应当按照勤俭节约、讲求绩效和绿色环保的要求，根据国家有关政策、经济社会发展水平、市场价格变化、科学技术进步等因素适时调整。

**Article 10** People's governments at or above the county level shall organize the establishment and improvement of a system of standards for asset allocation, and specify such standards as the quantity, value, grade, and minimum useful life.  
The standards for asset allocation shall be adjusted in a timely manner in accordance with the requirements of thrift, performance orientation and environmental protection, based on such factors as relevant State policies, the level of economic and social development, changes in market prices, and scientific and technological progress.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　各部门及其所属单位应当优先通过调剂方式配置资产。不能调剂的，可以采用购置、建设、租用等方式。

**Article 11** Each department and its subordinate units shall give priority to allocating assets through transfer. Where transfer is not possible, they may adopt such means as purchase, construction, or lease.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　行政单位国有资产应当用于本单位履行职能的需要。  
除法律另有规定外，行政单位不得以任何形式将国有资产用于对外投资或者设立营利性组织。

**Article 12** State-owned assets of administrative units shall be used for the needs of performing the functions of the unit.  
Unless otherwise provided by law, an administrative unit shall not use state-owned assets for outward investment or to establish for-profit organizations in any form.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　事业单位国有资产应当用于保障事业发展、提供公共服务。

**Article 13** State-owned assets of public institutions shall be used to ensure the development of undertakings and provide public services.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　各部门及其所属单位应当加强对本单位固定资产、在建工程、流动资产、无形资产等各类国有资产的管理，明确管理责任，规范使用流程，加强产权保护，推进相关资产安全有效使用。

**Article 14** Each department and its subordinate units shall strengthen the management of various types of state-owned assets of the unit, including fixed assets, construction in progress, current assets, and intangible assets, clearly define management responsibilities, standardize usage procedures, strengthen property rights protection, and promote the safe and effective use of relevant assets.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　各部门及其所属单位应当明确资产使用人和管理人的岗位责任。  
资产使用人、管理人应当履行岗位责任，按照规程合理使用、管理资产，充分发挥资产效能。资产需要维修、保养、调剂、更新、报废的，资产使用人、管理人应当及时提出。  
资产使用人、管理人发生变化的，应当及时办理资产交接手续。

**Article 15** Each department and its subordinate units shall specify the post responsibilities of asset users and asset managers.  
Asset users and asset managers shall fulfill their post responsibilities, use and manage assets reasonably in accordance with procedures, and give full play to the effectiveness of assets. Where assets require maintenance, upkeep, transfer, renewal, or scrapping, the asset users or asset managers shall promptly raise such matters.  
Where a change occurs in asset users or asset managers, the handover procedures for assets shall be promptly completed.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　各部门及其所属单位接受捐赠的资产，应当按照捐赠约定的用途使用。捐赠人意愿不明确或者没有约定用途的，应当统筹安排使用。

**Article 16** Assets donated to a department or its subordinate units shall be used in accordance with the purposes agreed upon in the donation. Where the intention of the donor is unclear or no purpose is agreed upon, the assets shall be used in a coordinated arrangement.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　事业单位利用国有资产对外投资应当有利于事业发展和实现国有资产保值增值，符合国家有关规定，经可行性研究和集体决策，按照规定权限和程序进行。  
事业单位应当明确对外投资形成的股权及其相关权益管理责任，按照规定将对外投资形成的股权纳入经营性国有资产集中统一监管体系。

**Article 17** Outward investment by a public institution using state-owned assets shall be conducive to the development of undertakings and to the preservation and appreciation of the value of state-owned assets, comply with the relevant provisions of the State, undergo feasibility studies and collective decision-making, and be carried out in accordance with the prescribed authority and procedures.  
A public institution shall clearly define the management responsibilities for the equity and related rights and interests formed by outward investment, and incorporate the equity formed by outward investment into the centralized and unified supervision system for operating state-owned assets in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当建立健全国有资产共享共用机制，采取措施引导和鼓励国有资产共享共用，统筹规划有效推进国有资产共享共用工作。  
各部门及其所属单位应当在确保安全使用的前提下，推进本单位大型设备等国有资产共享共用工作，可以对提供方给予合理补偿。

**Article 18** People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall establish and improve the mechanism for the sharing and common use of state-owned assets, take measures to guide and encourage the sharing and common use of state-owned assets, and make overall plans to effectively promote such work.  
Each department and its subordinate units shall, on the premise of ensuring safe use, promote the sharing and common use of state-owned assets such as large equipment of the unit, and may provide reasonable compensation to the party that makes them available.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　各部门及其所属单位应当根据履行职能、事业发展需要和资产使用状况，经集体决策和履行审批程序，依据处置事项批复等相关文件及时处置行政事业性国有资产。

**Article 19** Each department and its subordinate units shall, based on the needs of performing functions, the development of undertakings, and the status of asset use, and upon collective decision-making and after going through approval procedures, promptly dispose of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions in accordance with relevant documents such as the approval of the disposal matter.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　各部门及其所属单位应当将依法罚没的资产按照国家规定公开拍卖或者按照国家有关规定处理，所得款项全部上缴国库。

**Article 20** Each department and its subordinate units shall, for assets confiscated according to law, either conduct a public auction in accordance with State provisions or handle them in accordance with relevant State provisions, and all proceeds therefrom shall be turned over to the State treasury.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　各部门及其所属单位应当对下列资产及时予以报废、报损：  
（一）因技术原因确需淘汰或者无法维修、无维修价值的资产；  
（二）涉及盘亏、坏账以及非正常损失的资产；  
（三）已超过使用年限且无法满足现有工作需要的资产；  
（四）因自然灾害等不可抗力造成毁损、灭失的资产。

**Article 21** Each department and its subordinate units shall promptly scrap or write off the following assets:  
(1) Assets that need to be eliminated for technical reasons or that cannot be repaired or have no repair value;  
(2) Assets involving inventory shortages, bad debts, and abnormal losses;  
(3) Assets that have exceeded their useful life and cannot meet current work needs;  
(4) Assets damaged or lost due to force majeure such as natural disasters.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　各部门及其所属单位发生分立、合并、改制、撤销、隶属关系改变或者部分职能、业务调整等情形，应当根据国家有关规定办理相关国有资产划转、交接手续。

**Article 22** Where a department or its subordinate units undergoes such circumstances as division, merger, restructuring, dissolution, change of subordination, or partial adjustment of functions or business, it shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, go through the procedures for the transfer and handover of relevant state-owned assets.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家设立的研究开发机构、高等院校对其持有的科技成果的使用和处置，依照《中华人民共和国促进科技成果转化法》、《中华人民共和国专利法》和国家有关规定执行。

**Article 23** The use and disposal of the scientific and technological achievements held by research and development institutions and institutions of higher education established by the State shall be governed by the Law of the People's Republic of China on Promoting the Transformation of Scientific and Technological Achievements, the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China, and the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第三章　预算管理

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　各部门及其所属单位购置、建设、租用资产应当提出资产配置需求，编制资产配置相关支出预算，并严格按照预算管理规定和财政部门批复的预算配置资产。

**Article 24** Where a department or its subordinate units purchases, constructs, or leases assets, it shall submit asset allocation requirements, prepare budgets for expenditures related to asset allocation, and allocate assets strictly in accordance with the budget management provisions and the budget approved by the finance department.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　行政单位国有资产出租和处置等收入，应当按照政府非税收入和国库集中收缴制度的有关规定管理。  
除国家另有规定外，事业单位国有资产的处置收入应当按照政府非税收入和国库集中收缴制度的有关规定管理。  
事业单位国有资产使用形成的收入，由本级人民政府财政部门规定具体管理办法。

**Article 25** Income from the lease and disposal of state-owned assets of administrative units shall be managed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the government non-tax revenue and centralized treasury collection system.  
Unless otherwise provided by the State, the disposal income from state-owned assets of public institutions shall be managed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the government non-tax revenue and centralized treasury collection system.  
The income arising from the use of state-owned assets of public institutions shall be subject to specific management measures prescribed by the finance department of the people's government at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　各部门及其所属单位应当及时收取各类资产收入，不得违反国家规定，多收、少收、不收、侵占、私分、截留、占用、挪用、隐匿、坐支。

**Article 26** Each department and its subordinate units shall promptly collect all types of asset income, and shall not, in violation of State provisions, overcharge, undercharge, fail to charge, embezzle, privately distribute, intercept, occupy, misappropriate, conceal, or directly use such income.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　各部门及其所属单位应当在决算中全面、真实、准确反映其国有资产收入、支出以及国有资产存量情况。

**Article 27** Each department and its subordinate units shall reflect in their final accounts comprehensively, truthfully, and accurately the income and expenditure of their state-owned assets as well as the stock of state-owned assets.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　各部门及其所属单位应当按照国家规定建立国有资产绩效管理制度，建立健全绩效指标和标准，有序开展国有资产绩效管理工作。

**Article 28** Each department and its subordinate units shall, in accordance with State provisions, establish a performance management system for state-owned assets, establish and improve performance indicators and standards, and carry out performance management of state-owned assets in an orderly manner.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上人民政府投资建设公共基础设施，应当依法落实资金来源，加强预算约束，防范政府债务风险，并明确公共基础设施的管理维护责任单位。

**Article 29** Where a people's government at or above the county level invests in the construction of public infrastructure, it shall ensure the source of funds according to law, strengthen budget constraints, prevent government debt risks, and specify the unit responsible for the management and maintenance of the public infrastructure.

### 第四章　基础管理

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　各部门及其所属单位应当按照国家规定设置行政事业性国有资产台账，依照国家统一的会计制度进行会计核算，不得形成账外资产。

**Article 30** Each department and its subordinate units shall, in accordance with State provisions, set up ledgers for state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions, conduct accounting in accordance with the uniform accounting system of the State, and shall not create off-book assets.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　各部门及其所属单位采用建设方式配置资产的，应当在建设项目竣工验收合格后及时办理资产交付手续，并在规定期限内办理竣工财务决算，期限最长不得超过1年。  
各部门及其所属单位对已交付但未办理竣工财务决算的建设项目，应当按照国家统一的会计制度确认资产价值。

**Article 31** Where a department or its subordinate units allocates assets by means of construction, it shall promptly go through the asset delivery procedures after the construction project passes the completion acceptance, and shall go through the final financial settlement of the completed project within the prescribed time limit, which shall not exceed one year at most.  
For a construction project that has been delivered but has not undergone final financial settlement of the completed project, the department or its subordinate units shall recognize the value of the assets in accordance with the uniform accounting system of the State.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　各部门及其所属单位对无法进行会计确认入账的资产，可以根据需要组织专家参照资产评估方法进行估价，并作为反映资产状况的依据。

**Article 32** For assets that cannot be recognized and recorded in accounting, each department and its subordinate units may, as needed, organize experts to estimate their value by reference to asset appraisal methods, and use such estimation as a basis for reflecting the condition of the assets.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　各部门及其所属单位应当明确资产的维护、保养、维修的岗位责任。因使用不当或者维护、保养、维修不及时造成资产损失的，应当依法承担责任。

**Article 33** Each department and its subordinate units shall specify the post responsibilities for the upkeep, maintenance, and repair of assets. Where asset losses are caused by improper use or failure to provide timely upkeep, maintenance, or repair, liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　各部门及其所属单位应当定期或者不定期对资产进行盘点、对账。出现资产盘盈盘亏的，应当按照财务、会计和资产管理制度有关规定处理，做到账实相符和账账相符。

**Article 34** Each department and its subordinate units shall conduct regular or irregular physical inventories and reconciliation of accounts for assets. Where asset surpluses or shortages occur, they shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the financial, accounting, and asset management systems, so as to ensure that the accounts match the actual assets and that the accounts are consistent with each other.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　各部门及其所属单位处置资产应当及时核销相关资产台账信息，同时进行会计处理。

**Article 35** Where a department or its subordinate units disposes of assets, it shall promptly write off the relevant ledger information for the assets and at the same time conduct accounting treatment.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　除国家另有规定外，各部门及其所属单位将行政事业性国有资产进行转让、拍卖、置换、对外投资等，应当按照国家有关规定进行资产评估。  
行政事业性国有资产以市场化方式出售、出租的，依照有关规定可以通过相应公共资源交易平台进行。

**Article 36** Unless otherwise provided by the State, where a department or its subordinate units transfers, auctions, swaps, or makes outward investment of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions, it shall conduct asset appraisal in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
State-owned assets in administrative and public institutions sold or leased through market-oriented means may, in accordance with relevant provisions, be conducted through the corresponding public resource trading platform.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　有下列情形之一的，各部门及其所属单位应当对行政事业性国有资产进行清查：  
（一）根据本级政府部署要求；  
（二）发生重大资产调拨、划转以及单位分立、合并、改制、撤销、隶属关系改变等情形；  
（三）因自然灾害等不可抗力造成资产毁损、灭失；  
（四）会计信息严重失真；  
（五）国家统一的会计制度发生重大变更，涉及资产核算方法发生重要变化；  
（六）其他应当进行资产清查的情形。

**Article 37** Under any of the following circumstances, each department and its subordinate units shall conduct a comprehensive inventory of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions:  
(1) As required by the deployment of the people's government at the corresponding level;  
(2) Where major asset allocation, transfer, or circumstances such as division, merger, restructuring, dissolution, or change of subordination occur;  
(3) Where assets are damaged or lost due to force majeure such as natural disasters;  
(4) Where accounting information is seriously distorted;  
(5) Where the uniform accounting system of the State undergoes major changes involving important changes in asset accounting methods;  
(6) Other circumstances where an asset inventory is required.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　各部门及其所属单位资产清查结果和涉及资产核实的事项，应当按照国务院财政部门的规定履行审批程序。

**Article 38** The results of the comprehensive inventory and matters involving asset verification of each department and its subordinate units shall be subject to approval procedures in accordance with the provisions of the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　各部门及其所属单位在资产清查中发现账实不符、账账不符的，应当查明原因予以说明，并随同清查结果一并履行审批程序。各部门及其所属单位应当根据审批结果及时调整资产台账信息，同时进行会计处理。  
由于资产使用人、管理人的原因造成资产毁损、灭失的，应当依法追究相关责任。

**Article 39** Where a department or its subordinate units discovers in the asset inventory that accounts do not match actual assets or accounts are inconsistent with each other, it shall identify the reasons, provide explanations, and go through the approval procedures together with the inventory results. The department or its subordinate units shall, based on the approval results, promptly adjust the asset ledger information and at the same time conduct accounting treatment.  
Where asset damage or loss is caused by the asset user or asset manager, the relevant liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　各部门及其所属单位对需要办理权属登记的资产应当依法及时办理。对有账簿记录但权证手续不全的行政事业性国有资产，可以向本级政府有关主管部门提出确认资产权属申请，及时办理权属登记。

**Article 40** Each department and its subordinate units shall, according to law, promptly handle the registration of ownership for assets requiring such registration. For state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions that have accounting records but incomplete title certificate procedures, an application for confirmation of asset ownership may be submitted to the relevant competent department of the people's government at the corresponding level, and ownership registration shall be promptly completed.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　各部门及其所属单位之间，各部门及其所属单位与其他单位和个人之间发生资产纠纷的，应当依照有关法律法规规定采取协商等方式处理。

**Article 41** Where an asset dispute arises between a department and its subordinate units, or between a department or its subordinate units and other entities or individuals, it shall be handled through consultation or other means in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国务院财政部门应当建立全国行政事业性国有资产管理信息系统，推行资产管理网上办理，实现信息共享。

**Article 42** The finance department of the State Council shall establish a national information system for the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions, promote online handling of asset management, and achieve information sharing.

### 第五章　资产报告

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家建立行政事业性国有资产管理情况报告制度。  
国务院向全国人民代表大会常务委员会报告全国行政事业性国有资产管理情况。  
县级以上地方人民政府按照规定向本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告行政事业性国有资产管理情况。

**Article 43** The State shall establish a system of reports on the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions.  
The State Council shall report to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions nationwide.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions, report to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level on the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　行政事业性国有资产管理情况报告，主要包括资产负债总量，相关管理制度建立和实施，资产配置、使用、处置和效益，推进管理体制机制改革等情况。  
行政事业性国有资产管理情况按照国家有关规定向社会公开。

**Article 44** The report on the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions shall mainly include such information as the total amount of assets and liabilities, the establishment and implementation of relevant management systems, the allocation, use, disposal, and efficiency of assets, and the promotion of reform of management systems and mechanisms.  
The information on the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions shall be disclosed to the public in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　各部门所属单位应当每年编制本单位行政事业性国有资产管理情况报告，逐级报送相关部门。  
各部门应当汇总编制本部门行政事业性国有资产管理情况报告，报送本级政府财政部门。

**Article 45** The subordinate units of each department shall prepare an annual report on the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions of their own unit, and submit it level by level to the relevant departments.  
Each department shall consolidate and prepare a report on the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions of its department, and submit it to the finance department of the people's government at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上地方人民政府财政部门应当每年汇总本级和下级行政事业性国有资产管理情况，报送本级政府和上一级政府财政部门。

**Article 46** The finance department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall annually consolidate the information on the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions at its own level and lower levels, and submit it to the people's government at the corresponding level and the finance department of the people's government at the next higher level.

### 第六章　监督

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上人民政府应当接受本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会对行政事业性国有资产管理情况的监督，组织落实本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会审议提出的整改要求，并向本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会报告整改情况。  
乡、民族乡、镇人民政府应当接受本级人民代表大会对行政事业性国有资产管理情况的监督。

**Article 47** People's governments at or above the county level shall accept the supervision of the people's congress at the corresponding level and its standing committee over the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions, organize the implementation of the rectification requirements raised during the review by the people's congress and its standing committee, and report to the people's congress at the corresponding level and its standing committee on the rectification situation.  
The people's governments of townships, ethnic townships, and towns shall accept the supervision of the people's congress at the corresponding level over the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上人民政府对下级政府的行政事业性国有资产管理情况进行监督。下级政府应当组织落实上一级政府提出的监管要求，并向上一级政府报告落实情况。

**Article 48** People's governments at or above the county level shall supervise the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions by governments at lower levels. A lower-level government shall organize the implementation of the regulatory requirements raised by the government at the next higher level, and report on the implementation to the government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上人民政府财政部门应当对本级各部门及其所属单位行政事业性国有资产管理情况进行监督检查，依法向社会公开检查结果。

**Article 49** The finance department of a people's government at or above the county level shall supervise and inspect the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions by the departments at the corresponding level and their subordinate units, and shall disclose the inspection results to the public according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　县级以上人民政府审计部门依法对行政事业性国有资产管理情况进行审计监督。

**Article 50** The audit department of a people's government at or above the county level shall, according to law, exercise audit supervision over the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　各部门应当建立健全行政事业性国有资产监督管理制度，根据职责对本行业行政事业性国有资产管理依法进行监督。  
各部门所属单位应当制定行政事业性国有资产内部控制制度，防控行政事业性国有资产管理风险。

**Article 51** Each department shall establish and improve a supervision and management system for state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions, and conduct supervision over the management of state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions in its industry according to law in accordance with its functions.  
The subordinate units of each department shall formulate internal control systems for state-owned assets in administrative and public institutions to prevent and control risks in the management of such assets.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　公民、法人或者其他组织发现违反本条例的行为，有权向有关部门进行检举、控告。接受检举、控告的有关部门应当依法进行处理，并为检举人、控告人保密。  
任何单位或者个人不得压制和打击报复检举人、控告人。

**Article 52** Citizens, legal persons, or other organizations that discover any act violating these Regulations shall have the right to report or accuse the act to the relevant department. The relevant department that receives the report or accusation shall handle it according to law and keep the reporter or accuser confidential.  
No unit or individual may suppress or retaliate against a reporter or accuser.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　各部门及其所属单位有下列行为之一的，责令改正，情节较重的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）配置、使用、处置国有资产未按照规定经集体决策或者履行审批程序；  
（二）超标准配置国有资产；  
（三）未按照规定办理国有资产调剂、调拨、划转、交接等手续；  
（四）未按照规定履行国有资产拍卖、报告、披露等程序；  
（五）未按照规定期限办理建设项目竣工财务决算；  
（六）未按照规定进行国有资产清查；  
（七）未按照规定设置国有资产台账；  
（八）未按照规定编制、报送国有资产管理情况报告。

**Article 53** Where any department or its subordinate unit commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the persons directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) allocating, using, or disposing of state-owned assets without collective decision-making or fulfilling the approval procedures as required;  
(2) allocating state-owned assets in excess of the standards;  
(3) failing to handle procedures for adjustment, allocation, transfer, handover, etc. of state-owned assets as required;  
(4) failing to perform procedures for auction, reporting, disclosure, etc. of state-owned assets as required;  
(5) failing to complete the final financial accounts for completed construction projects within the prescribed time limit;  
(6) failing to conduct a inventory of state-owned assets as required;  
(7) failing to set up ledgers for state-owned assets as required;  
(8) failing to prepare or submit reports on the management of state-owned assets as required.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　各部门及其所属单位有下列行为之一的，责令改正，有违法所得的没收违法所得，情节较重的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）非法占有、使用国有资产或者采用弄虚作假等方式低价处置国有资产；  
（二）违反规定将国有资产用于对外投资或者设立营利性组织；  
（三）未按照规定评估国有资产导致国家利益损失；  
（四）其他违反本条例规定造成国有资产损失的行为。

**Article 54** Where any department or its subordinate unit commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections; any illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the persons directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) illegally possessing or using state-owned assets, or disposing of state-owned assets at low prices by means such as fraud;  
(2) using state-owned assets for external investment or establishing profit-making organizations in violation of regulations;  
(3) failing to appraise state-owned assets as required, thereby causing losses to national interests;  
(4) other acts causing losses to state-owned assets in violation of these Regulations.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　各部门及其所属单位在国有资产管理工作中有违反预算管理规定行为的，依照《中华人民共和国预算法》及其实施条例、《财政违法行为处罚处分条例》等法律、行政法规追究责任。

**Article 55** Where any department or its subordinate unit, in the management of state-owned assets, commits any act in violation of the provisions on budget management, liability shall be investigated in accordance with the Budget Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing regulations, the Regulation on Penalties and Sanctions for Fiscal Violations, and other laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　各部门及其所属单位的工作人员在国有资产管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊或者有浪费国有资产等违法违规行为的，由有关部门依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 56** Where any staff member of a department or its subordinate unit abuses his or her power, neglects his or her duty, engages in malpractices for personal gain, or commits any other illegal or non-compliant act such as wasting state-owned assets in the management of state-owned assets, the relevant department shall impose sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　除国家另有规定外，社会组织直接支配的行政事业性国有资产管理，依照本条例执行。

**Article 57** Unless otherwise provided by the State, the management of administrative and institutional state-owned assets directly controlled by social organizations shall be implemented in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　货币形式的行政事业性国有资产管理，按照预算管理有关规定执行。  
执行企业财务、会计制度的事业单位以及事业单位对外投资的全资企业或者控股企业的资产管理，不适用本条例。

**Article 58** The management of administrative and institutional state-owned assets in the form of currency shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions on budget management.  
These Regulations shall not apply to the asset management of public institutions that implement enterprise financial and accounting systems, or of wholly-owned enterprises or holding enterprises invested in by public institutions.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　公共基础设施、政府储备物资、国有文物文化等行政事业性国有资产管理的具体办法，由国务院财政部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 59** Specific measures for the management of administrative and institutional state-owned assets such as public infrastructure, government reserve materials, and state-owned cultural relics and cultural assets shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队直接支配的行政事业性国有资产管理，依照中央军事委员会有关规定执行。

**Article 60** The management of administrative and institutional state-owned assets directly controlled by the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　本条例自2021年4月1日起施行。

**Article 61** These Regulations shall come into force as of April 1, 2021.
