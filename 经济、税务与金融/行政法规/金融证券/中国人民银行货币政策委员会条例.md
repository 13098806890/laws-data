### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了有助于国家货币政策的正确制定，根据中国人民银行法的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Bank of China in order to facilitate the correct formulation of national monetary policy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　货币政策委员会工作坚持中国共产党的领导，推动健全现代货币政策框架，重要事项报党中央、国务院。

**Article 2** The work of the Monetary Policy Committee shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, promote and improve the modern monetary policy framework, and report major matters to the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　货币政策委员会是中国人民银行制定货币政策的咨询议事机构。  
货币政策委员会依照本条例组成。

**Article 3** The Monetary Policy Committee is an advisory and deliberative body for the formulation of monetary policy by the People's Bank of China.  
The Monetary Policy Committee shall be formed in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　货币政策委员会的职责是，在综合分析宏观经济形势的基础上，依据国家的宏观经济调控目标，讨论下列货币政策事项，并提出建议：  
（一）货币政策的制定、调整；  
（二）一定时期内的货币政策控制目标；  
（三）货币政策工具的运用；  
（四）有关货币政策的重要措施；  
（五）货币政策与其他宏观经济政策的协调。

**Article 4** The functions of the Monetary Policy Committee shall be to discuss the following monetary policy matters on the basis of comprehensive analysis of the macroeconomic situation and in accordance with the national macroeconomic regulation and control targets, and to make recommendations:  
(1) formulation and adjustment of monetary policy;  
(2) monetary policy control targets within a certain period;  
(3) use of monetary policy instruments;  
(4) important measures relating to monetary policy;  
(5) coordination of monetary policy with other macroeconomic policies.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　货币政策委员会通过全体会议履行职责。

**Article 5** The Monetary Policy Committee shall perform its functions through plenary meetings.

### 第二章　组织机构

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　货币政策委员会由下列单位和人员组成：  
中国人民银行行长；  
国务院副秘书长1人；  
国家发展和改革委员会副主任1人；  
财政部副部长1人；  
中国人民银行副行长2人；  
国家金融监督管理总局局长；  
中国证券监督管理委员会主席；  
国家统计局局长；  
国家外汇管理局局长；  
中国银行业协会会长；  
专家委员3人。  
货币政策委员会组成单位和人员的调整，由国务院决定。

**Article 6** The Monetary Policy Committee shall be composed of the following entities and persons:  
the Governor of the People's Bank of China;  
one Deputy Secretary-General of the State Council;  
one Deputy Director of the National Development and Reform Commission;  
one Vice Minister of Finance;  
two Deputy Governors of the People's Bank of China;  
the Director of the National Financial Regulatory Administration;  
the Chairman of the China Securities Regulatory Commission;  
the Commissioner of the National Bureau of Statistics;  
the Administrator of the State Administration of Foreign Exchange;  
the President of the China Banking Association;  
three expert members.  
Adjustments to the constituent entities and persons of the Monetary Policy Committee shall be decided by the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　货币政策委员会委员包括当然委员和提名委员。中国人民银行行长、国家金融监督管理总局局长、中国证券监督管理委员会主席、国家外汇管理局局长为货币政策委员会的当然委员。货币政策委员会其他委员为提名委员，其人选由中国人民银行提名或者中国人民银行商有关部门提名，报请国务院任命。

**Article 7** The members of the Monetary Policy Committee shall include ex officio members and nominated members. The Governor of the People's Bank of China, the Director of the National Financial Regulatory Administration, the Chairman of the China Securities Regulatory Commission, and the Administrator of the State Administration of Foreign Exchange shall be ex officio members of the Monetary Policy Committee. The other members of the Monetary Policy Committee shall be nominated members, whose candidates shall be nominated by the People's Bank of China or nominated by the People's Bank of China in consultation with the relevant departments, and submitted to the State Council for appointment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　货币政策委员会设主席一人，副主席一人。主席由中国人民银行行长担任；副主席由主席指定。

**Article 8** The Monetary Policy Committee shall have one Chairperson and one Vice Chairperson. The Chairperson shall be assumed by the Governor of the People's Bank of China; the Vice Chairperson shall be designated by the Chairperson.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　货币政策委员会委员应当具备下列条件：  
（一）年龄一般在65周岁以下，具有中华人民共和国国籍；  
（二）公正廉洁，忠于职守，无违法、违纪记录；  
（三）具有宏观经济、金融等方面的专业知识和实践经验，熟悉有关法律、法规和政策。

**Article 9** The members of the Monetary Policy Committee shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) generally under the age of 65 years and possessing the nationality of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) being impartial and honest, dedicated to their duties, and without any record of illegal or disciplinary violations;  
(3) having professional knowledge and practical experience in macroeconomics, finance, and other relevant fields, and being familiar with relevant laws, regulations, and policies.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　货币政策委员会中的专家委员除应当符合[本条例第九条](#art-9)规定的条件外，还应当具备下列条件：  
（一）具有高级专业技术职称，从事经济金融工作10年以上，具有较高的学术水平；  
（二）非国家公务员，并且不在任何营利性机构任职。

**Article 10** In addition to meeting the conditions specified in Article 9 of these Regulations, the expert members of the Monetary Policy Committee shall also meet the following conditions:  
(1) holding a senior professional technical title, having been engaged in economic and financial work for more than 10 years, and possessing a relatively high academic level;  
(2) not being a civil servant, and not holding any position in any for-profit organization.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　货币政策委员会委员中的中国银行业协会会长任期一般不超过5年；专家委员一届任期不超过3年，最长任期一般不超过两届。

**Article 11** The term of office of the President of the China Banking Association who is a member of the Monetary Policy Committee shall generally not exceed five years; the term of office of expert members for one term shall not exceed three years, and the maximum term of office shall generally not exceed two terms.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　货币政策委员会委员有下列情形之一的，由中国人民银行报请国务院免去其货币政策委员会委员职务：  
（一）本人提出书面辞职申请的；  
（二）任职期间因职务变动，已经不能代表有关单位担任货币政策委员会委员的；  
（三）依照[本条例第十一条](#art-11)规定任期已满的；  
（四）不履行委员义务或者因各种原因不能胜任委员工作的。

**Article 12** Where a member of the Monetary Policy Committee falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Bank of China shall submit a request to the State Council to remove the member from the post of member of the Monetary Policy Committee:  
(1) the member has submitted a written resignation request;  
(2) during the term of office, due to a change in position, the member can no longer represent the relevant entity as a member of the Monetary Policy Committee;  
(3) the term of office has expired in accordance with Article 11 of these Regulations;  
(4) the member fails to fulfill the obligations of a member or is incompetent for the work of a member for various reasons.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　货币政策委员会设立秘书处，作为货币政策委员会的常设办事机构。

**Article 13** The Monetary Policy Committee shall establish a Secretariat as its permanent office.

### 第三章　委员的权利与义务

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　货币政策委员会委员具有同等的权利与义务。

**Article 14** The members of the Monetary Policy Committee shall have equal rights and obligations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　货币政策委员会委员为履行职责需要，享有下列权利：  
（一）了解经济、金融和货币政策方面的情况；  
（二）对货币政策委员会所讨论的问题发表意见；  
（三）向货币政策委员会就货币政策问题提出议案，并享有表决权。

**Article 15** For the purpose of performing their functions, the members of the Monetary Policy Committee shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) to learn about the circumstances in economic, financial and monetary policy aspects;  
(2) to express opinions on the issues discussed by the Monetary Policy Committee;  
(3) to submit proposals to the Monetary Policy Committee on monetary policy issues, and to have the right to vote.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　货币政策委员会委员应当出席货币政策委员会会议，并就有关货币政策事项提出意见和建议。

**Article 16** The members of the Monetary Policy Committee shall attend the meetings of the Monetary Policy Committee and put forward opinions and suggestions on relevant monetary policy matters.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　货币政策委员会委员应当恪尽职守，不得滥用职权、徇私舞弊。

**Article 17** The members of the Monetary Policy Committee shall perform their duties with due diligence, and shall not abuse their powers or engage in malpractice for personal gain.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　货币政策委员会委员应当保守国家秘密、商业秘密，遵守货币政策委员会的工作制度，不得违反规定透露货币政策及有关情况。  
货币政策委员会委员违反规定泄露国家秘密、商业秘密的，撤销货币政策委员会委员的职务，并依法追究法律责任。

**Article 18** The members of the Monetary Policy Committee shall keep state secrets and trade secrets, comply with the working system of the Monetary Policy Committee, and shall not disclose monetary policy and relevant information in violation of regulations.  
Where any member of the Monetary Policy Committee leaks state secrets or trade secrets in violation of regulations, the member's post as a member of the Monetary Policy Committee shall be removed, and legal liability shall be pursued according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　货币政策委员会委员在任职期内和离职以后一年内，不得公开反对已按法定程序制定的货币政策。

**Article 19** A member of the Monetary Policy Committee shall not publicly oppose the monetary policy that has been formulated in accordance with statutory procedures during his or her term of office and within one year after leaving office.

### 第四章　工作程序

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　货币政策委员会实行例会制度，每季度召开1次。  
货币政策委员会主席或者1/3以上委员联名，可以提议召开临时会议。  
中国人民银行在货币政策委员会例会召开后，采取多种方式加强预期引导和市场沟通。

**Article 20** The Monetary Policy Committee shall adopt a regular meeting system, with meetings held once every quarter.  
The Chairperson of the Monetary Policy Committee or more than one-third of the members jointly may propose to convene an interim meeting.  
After the regular meeting of the Monetary Policy Committee, the People's Bank of China shall adopt various methods to strengthen expectation guidance and market communication.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　货币政策委员会秘书处应当在货币政策委员会例会召开前，将会议议题及有关资料送达全部委员。

**Article 21** The Secretariat of the Monetary Policy Committee shall deliver the meeting agenda and relevant materials to all members before the convening of the regular meeting of the Monetary Policy Committee.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　货币政策委员会会议有2/3以上委员出席，方可举行。

**Article 22** A meeting of the Monetary Policy Committee may be held only when more than two-thirds of the members are present.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　货币政策委员会会议应当以会议纪要的形式记录各种意见。  
货币政策委员会委员提出的货币政策议案，经出席会议的2/3以上委员表决通过，形成货币政策委员会建议书。

**Article 23** The minutes of the meeting shall be adopted to record the various opinions of the meeting of the Monetary Policy Committee.  
A monetary policy proposal submitted by a member of the Monetary Policy Committee shall, after being voted and adopted by more than two-thirds of the members present at the meeting, form a recommendation letter of the Monetary Policy Committee.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　中国人民银行报请国务院批准有关利率、汇率或者其他货币政策重要事项的决定方案时，可以将货币政策委员会建议书或者会议纪要作为附件一并报送。

**Article 24** When the People's Bank of China submits a decision plan to the State Council for approval concerning matters related to interest rates, exchange rates or other important monetary policy matters, it may submit the recommendation letter of the Monetary Policy Committee or the minutes of the meeting as an attachment together with the plan.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　货币政策委员会的内部工作制度，由货币政策委员会制定。

**Article 25** The internal working system of the Monetary Policy Committee shall be formulated by the Monetary Policy Committee.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 26** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
