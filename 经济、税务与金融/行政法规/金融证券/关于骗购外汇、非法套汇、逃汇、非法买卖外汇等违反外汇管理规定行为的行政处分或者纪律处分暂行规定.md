# 关于骗购外汇、非法套汇、逃汇、非法买卖外汇等违反外汇管理规定行为的行政处分或者纪律处分暂行规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08

> (1998年12月16日国务院批准　1999年1月25日监察部、人事部、中国人民银行、海关总署、国家外汇管理局令第7号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了维护国家外汇管理秩序，惩处违反外汇管理规定的行为，防范金融风险，制定本规定。 第二条　本规定适用于国家公务员以及经批准经营外汇业务的金融机构、国有外经贸企业的工作人员。 本规定所称经批准经营外汇业务的金融机构，是指经批准经营外汇业务的中资银行、非银行金融机构及其分支机构。 本规定所称国有外经贸企业，是指国有外贸公司、自营进出口的国有生产企业、有进出口经营权的国有企业和国有资产占控股地位或者主导地位的企业。 第三条　有本规定所列违反外汇管理规定的行为的，除依法给予行政处罚外，对有关责任人员依照本规定给予行政处分或者纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。 第四条　经批准经营外汇业务的金融机构、国有外经贸企业的工作人员，有下列骗购外汇行为之一，数额不满10万美元的，给予留用察看处分；数额在10万美元以上的，给予开除处分。 (一)伪造、变造海关报关单、进口证明、外汇管理部门核准件等凭证和单据的； (二)使用、买卖伪造、变造的海关报关单、进口证明、外汇管理部门核准件等凭证和单据的； (三)重复使用海关报关单、进口证明、外汇管理部门核准件等凭证和单据的； (四)明知用于骗购外汇而提供人民币资金或者其他服务的； (五)以其他方式骗购外汇的。 单位有前款行为之一的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照前款规定给予纪律处分。 第五条　经批准经营外汇业务的金融机构、国有外经贸企业的工作人员，有下列非法套汇行为之一，数额不满10万美元的，给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；数额在10万美元以上不满100万美元的，给予降级或者撤职处分；数额在100万美元以上的，给予留用察看或者开除处分。 (一)违反国家规定，以人民币支付或者以实物偿付应当以外汇支付的进口货款或者其他类似支出的，但是合法的易货贸易除外；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护国家外汇管理秩序，惩处违反外汇管理规定的行为，防范金融风险，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of maintaining the order of national foreign exchange administration, punishing acts that violate the provisions on foreign exchange administration, and preventing financial risks.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定适用于国家公务员以及经批准经营外汇业务的金融机构、国有外经贸企业的工作人员。  
本规定所称经批准经营外汇业务的金融机构，是指经批准经营外汇业务的中资银行、非银行金融机构及其分支机构。  
本规定所称国有外经贸企业，是指国有外贸公司、自营进出口的国有生产企业、有进出口经营权的国有企业和国有资产占控股地位或者主导地位的企业。

**Article 2** These Provisions shall apply to State civil servants as well as the staff members of financial institutions approved to engage in foreign exchange business and State-owned foreign trade and economic cooperation enterprises.  
For the purpose of these Provisions, "financial institutions approved to engage in foreign exchange business" means Chinese-funded banks, non-bank financial institutions and their branches that are approved to engage in foreign exchange business.  
For the purpose of these Provisions, "State-owned foreign trade and economic cooperation enterprises" means State-owned foreign trade companies, State-owned production enterprises that engage in self-operated import and export, State-owned enterprises with the right to engage in import and export, and enterprises in which State-owned assets hold a controlling or dominant position.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　有本规定所列违反外汇管理规定的行为的，除依法给予行政处罚外，对有关责任人员依照本规定给予行政处分或者纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 3** Where any person commits any act of violating the provisions on foreign exchange administration as listed in these Provisions, if such act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; in addition to imposing administrative penalty according to law, the relevant responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions in accordance with these Provisions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　经批准经营外汇业务的金融机构、国有外经贸企业的工作人员，有下列骗购外汇行为之一，数额不满10万美元的，给予留用察看处分；数额在10万美元以上的，给予开除处分。  
(一)伪造、变造海关报关单、进口证明、外汇管理部门核准件等凭证和单据的；  
(二)使用、买卖伪造、变造的海关报关单、进口证明、外汇管理部门核准件等凭证和单据的；  
(三)重复使用海关报关单、进口证明、外汇管理部门核准件等凭证和单据的；  
(四)明知用于骗购外汇而提供人民币资金或者其他服务的；  
(五)以其他方式骗购外汇的。  
单位有前款行为之一的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照前款规定给予纪律处分。

**Article 4** Where any staff member of a financial institution approved to engage in foreign exchange business or a State-owned foreign trade and economic cooperation enterprise commits any of the following acts of fraudulently purchasing foreign exchange, if the amount is less than 100,000 US dollars, he/she shall be given the sanction of probation with retention of post; if the amount is 100,000 US dollars or more, he/she shall be given the sanction of discharge.  
(1) Forging or altering customs declaration forms, import certificates, approval documents of the foreign exchange administration department or other certificates or documents;  
(2) Using or trading forged or altered customs declaration forms, import certificates, approval documents of the foreign exchange administration department or other certificates or documents;  
(3) Repeatedly using customs declaration forms, import certificates, approval documents of the foreign exchange administration department or other certificates or documents;  
(4) Providing Renminbi funds or other services while knowing that they are used for fraudulently purchasing foreign exchange;  
(5) Fraudulently purchasing foreign exchange by other means.  
Where a unit commits any of the acts set forth in the preceding paragraph, the persons in charge with direct responsibility and other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　经批准经营外汇业务的金融机构、国有外经贸企业的工作人员，有下列非法套汇行为之一，数额不满10万美元的，给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；数额在10万美元以上不满100万美元的，给予降级或者撤职处分；数额在100万美元以上的，给予留用察看或者开除处分。  
(一)违反国家规定，以人民币支付或者以实物偿付应当以外汇支付的进口货款或者其他类似支出的，但是合法的易货贸易除外；  
(二)以人民币为他人支付在境内的费用，而由对方给付外汇的；  
(三)明知用于非法套汇而提供人民币资金或者其他服务的；  
(四)以其他方式非法套汇的。  
单位有前款行为之一的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照前款规定给予纪律处分。

**Article 5** Where any staff member of a financial institution approved to engage in foreign exchange business or a State-owned foreign trade and economic cooperation enterprise commits any of the following acts of illegal arbitrage of foreign exchange, if the amount is less than 100,000 US dollars, he/she shall be given a warning, a demerit or a serious demerit; if the amount is 100,000 US dollars or more but less than 1,000,000 US dollars, he/she shall be given demotion or removal from office; if the amount is 1,000,000 US dollars or more, he/she shall be given probation with retention of post or discharge.  
(1) In violation of State provisions, making payment in Renminbi or in kind for import payments or other similar expenditures that shall be paid in foreign exchange, except for legal barter trade;  
(2) Paying expenses in Renminbi for others within the territory, while the other party pays foreign exchange;  
(3) Providing Renminbi funds or other services while knowing that they are used for illegal foreign exchange arbitrage;  
(4) Engaging in illegal foreign exchange arbitrage by other means.  
Where a unit commits any of the acts set forth in the preceding paragraph, the persons in charge with direct responsibility and other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　经批准经营外汇业务的金融机构、国有外经贸企业的工作人员，有下列逃汇行为之一，数额不满10万美元的，给予撤职处分；数额在10万美元以上不满100万美元的，给予留用察看处分；数额在100万美元以上的，给予开除处分。  
(一)违反国家规定，擅自将外汇存放在境外的；  
(二)不按照国家规定将外汇卖给外汇指定银行的；  
(三)违反国家规定将外汇汇出或者携带出境的；  
(四)未经外汇管理部门批准，擅自将外币存款凭证、外币有价证券携带或者邮寄出境的；  
(五)明知用于逃汇而提供人民币资金或者其他服务的；  
(六)以其他方式逃汇的。  
单位有前款行为之一的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照前款规定给予纪律处分。

**Article 6** Where any staff member of a financial institution approved to engage in foreign exchange business or a State-owned foreign trade and economic cooperation enterprise commits any of the following acts of evasion of foreign exchange, if the amount is less than 100,000 US dollars, he/she shall be given removal from office; if the amount is 100,000 US dollars or more but less than 1,000,000 US dollars, he/she shall be given probation with retention of post; if the amount is 1,000,000 US dollars or more, he/she shall be given discharge.  
(1) In violation of State provisions, without authorization, depositing foreign exchange outside the territory;  
(2) Failing to sell foreign exchange to a designated foreign exchange bank in accordance with State provisions;  
(3) In violation of State provisions, remitting or carrying foreign exchange out of the territory;  
(4) Without approval from the foreign exchange administration department, without authorization, carrying or mailing foreign currency deposit certificates or foreign currency securities out of the territory;  
(5) Providing Renminbi funds or other services while knowing that they are used for evasion of foreign exchange;  
(6) Evading foreign exchange by other means.  
Where a unit commits any of the acts set forth in the preceding paragraph, the persons in charge with direct responsibility and other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　经批准经营外汇业务的金融机构、国有外经贸企业的工作人员，以营利为目的，在国家规定的交易场所以外非法买卖外汇，数额不满5万美元或者违法所得不满1万元人民币的，给予撤职处分；数额在5万美元以上不满10万美元或者违法所得在1万元人民币以上不满3万元人民币的，给予留用察看处分；数额在10万美元以上或者违法所得在3万元人民币以上的，给予开除处分。  
单位有前款所列行为的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照前款规定给予纪律处分。

**Article 7** Where any staff member of a financial institution approved to engage in foreign exchange business or a State-owned foreign trade and economic cooperation enterprise, for profit-making purposes, illegally buys or sells foreign exchange outside the trading places prescribed by the State, if the amount is less than 50,000 US dollars or the illegal income is less than 10,000 Renminbi, he/she shall be given removal from office; if the amount is 50,000 US dollars or more but less than 100,000 US dollars, or the illegal income is 10,000 Renminbi or more but less than 30,000 Renminbi, he/she shall be given probation with retention of post; if the amount is 100,000 US dollars or more, or the illegal income is 30,000 Renminbi or more, he/she shall be given discharge.  
Where a unit commits any of the acts set forth in the preceding paragraph, the persons in charge with direct responsibility and other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国有外经贸企业在代理进口业务中，因过失导致他人骗购外汇或者非法套汇，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予纪律处分，数额不满10万美元的，给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；数额在10万美元以上不满100万美元的，给予降级或者撤职处分；数额在100万美元以上的，给予留用察看或者开除处分。

**Article 8** Where a State-owned foreign trade and economic cooperation enterprise, in the course of agency import business, due to negligence, causes others to fraudulently purchase foreign exchange or illegally arbitrage foreign exchange, the persons in charge with direct responsibility and other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions; if the amount is less than 100,000 US dollars, they shall be given a warning, a demerit or a serious demerit; if the amount is 100,000 US dollars or more but less than 1,000,000 US dollars, they shall be given demotion or removal from office; if the amount is 1,000,000 US dollars or more, they shall be given probation with retention of post or discharge.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　经批准经营外汇业务的金融机构在办理结汇、售汇、付汇和开户业务中，因过失导致他人骗购外汇、非法套汇或者逃汇，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予纪律处分，数额不满10万美元的，给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；数额在10万美元以上不满100万美元的，给予降级或者撤职处分；数额在100万美元以上的，给予留用察看或者开除处分。

**Article 9** Where a financial institution approved to engage in foreign exchange business, in the course of handling settlement, sale, payment of foreign exchange and opening accounts, due to negligence, causes others to fraudulently purchase foreign exchange, illegally arbitrage foreign exchange or evade foreign exchange, the persons in charge with direct responsibility and other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions; if the amount is less than 100,000 US dollars, they shall be given a warning, a demerit or a serious demerit; if the amount is 100,000 US dollars or more but less than 1,000,000 US dollars, they shall be given demotion or removal from office; if the amount is 1,000,000 US dollars or more, they shall be given probation with retention of post or discharge.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家公务员有本规定所列骗购外汇、非法套汇、逃汇或者非法买卖外汇等违反外汇管理规定行为之一的，给予降级、撤职或者开除处分。

**Article 10** Where any State civil servant commits any of the acts of violating the provisions on foreign exchange administration, such as fraudulently purchasing foreign exchange, illegally arbitraging foreign exchange, evading foreign exchange or illegally buying or selling foreign exchange, as listed in these Provisions, he/she shall be given demotion, removal from office or discharge.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　海关、外汇管理等部门的国家公务员与骗购外汇、非法套汇、逃汇或者非法买卖外汇的行为人通谋，为其提供便利，或者明知是伪造、变造的凭证和单据而为其提供服务，或者有其他滥用职权、徇私舞弊行为造成他人骗购外汇、非法套汇或者逃汇后果的，给予开除处分。  
海关、外汇管理等部门的国家公务员，玩忽职守，造成他人骗购外汇、非法套汇或者逃汇的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分。

**Article 11** State functionaries of the customs, foreign exchange administration and other departments who collude with persons who fraudulently purchase foreign exchange, illegally arbitrage foreign exchange, evade foreign exchange, or illegally buy or sell foreign exchange, providing facilitation for them, or knowingly provide services for forged or altered certificates and documents, or otherwise abuse their power or engage in malpractice for personal benefit resulting in others fraudulently purchasing foreign exchange, illegally arbitraging foreign exchange, or evading foreign exchange, shall be given the sanction of dismissal.  
State functionaries of the customs, foreign exchange administration and other departments who, through dereliction of duty, cause others to fraudulently purchase foreign exchange, illegally arbitrage foreign exchange, or evade foreign exchange, shall be given the sanction of demotion or removal from office; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given the sanction of dismissal.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对本单位发生的违反外汇管理规定行为不制止、不查处，情节较重的，对负有直接责任的主管人员给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；情节严重的，给予降级或者撤职处分。

**Article 12** Where a person fails to stop or investigate acts violating the provisions on foreign exchange administration occurring within his or her own unit, if the circumstances are relatively serious, the person directly in charge who bears direct responsibility shall be given a warning, a demerit, or a serious demerit; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given demotion or removal from office.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家公务员利用职权，包庇违反外汇管理规定行为，或者有其他妨碍外汇管理执法监督、检查行为的，给予撤职或者开除处分。  
经批准经营外汇业务的金融机构、国有外经贸企业的工作人员有前款行为的，给予留用察看或者开除处分。  
单位有本条第一款所列行为的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，分别依照前两款规定给予行政处分或者纪律处分。

**Article 13** State functionaries who abuse their authority to cover up acts violating the provisions on foreign exchange administration, or otherwise obstruct law enforcement supervision or inspection of foreign exchange administration, shall be given the sanction of removal from office or dismissal.  
Where staff members of financial institutions approved to engage in foreign exchange business or state-owned foreign trade and economic cooperation enterprises commit the acts described in the preceding paragraph, they shall be given the sanction of probation or dismissal.  
Where a unit commits the acts listed in the first paragraph of this Article, the persons directly in charge who bear direct responsibility and other persons directly responsible shall, in accordance with the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs respectively, be given administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　主动交代违反外汇管理规定行为，并退出外汇和违法所得，或者主动采取措施避免损失，或者有立功表现的，可以从轻、减轻或者免予行政处分或者纪律处分。  
隐瞒事实真相，或者弄虚作假，出具伪证，或者隐匿、毁灭证据，或者拒绝提供有关文件、资料和证明材料的，应当从重或者加重行政处分或者纪律处分。

**Article 14** A person who voluntarily confesses acts violating the provisions on foreign exchange administration and returns the foreign exchange and illegal gains, or voluntarily takes measures to avoid losses, or has performed meritorious service, may be given a lighter or mitigated administrative sanction or disciplinary sanction, or may be exempted from such sanction.  
A person who conceals the true facts, or practices fraud, produces false evidence, or conceals or destroys evidence, or refuses to provide relevant documents, materials and evidentiary materials, shall be given a heavier or aggravated administrative sanction or disciplinary sanction.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　自营进出口的国有事业单位及其工作人员有本规定所列违反外汇管理规定行为的，参照本规定执行。

**Article 15** Where state-owned institutions with self-managed import and export operations and their staff members commit acts violating the provisions on foreign exchange administration as listed in these Provisions, these Provisions shall be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本规定自发布之日起施行。1996年4月1日《中华人民共和国外汇管理条例》施行后、本规定施行前发生的违反外汇管理规定的行为，尚未处理的，适用本规定。

**Article 16** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of issuance. For acts violating the provisions on foreign exchange administration that occurred after the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Foreign Exchange Administration came into force on April 1, 1996 but before these Provisions come into force, if such acts have not yet been dealt with, these Provisions shall apply.
