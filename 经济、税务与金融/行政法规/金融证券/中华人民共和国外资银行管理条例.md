# 中华人民共和国外资银行管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2019-09-30  
**生效日期**：2019-09-30

> (2006年11月11日中华人民共和国国务院令第478号公布　根据2014年7月29日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2014年11月27日《国务院关于修改〈中华人民共和国外资银行管理条例〉的决定》第二次修订　根据2019年9月30日《国务院关于修改〈中华人民共和国外资保险公司管理条例〉和〈中华人民共和国外资银行管理条例〉的决定》第三次修订)

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了适应对外开放和经济发展的需要，加强和完善对外资银行的监督管理，促进银行业的稳健运行，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of meeting the needs of opening up and economic development, strengthening and improving the supervision and administration of foreign-funded banks, and promoting the sound operation of the banking industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称外资银行，是指依照中华人民共和国有关法律、法规，经批准在中华人民共和国境内设立的下列机构：  
(一)1家外国银行单独出资或者1家外国银行与其他外国金融机构共同出资设立的外商独资银行；  
(二)外国金融机构与中国的公司、企业共同出资设立的中外合资银行；  
(三)外国银行分行；  
(四)外国银行代表处。  
前款第一项至第三项所列机构，以下统称外资银行营业性机构。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Regulations, a foreign-funded bank means any of the following institutions established within the territory of the People's Republic of China upon approval in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) a wholly foreign-owned bank funded solely by one foreign bank or jointly by one foreign bank and other foreign financial institutions;  
(2) a Sino-foreign equity joint venture bank funded jointly by foreign financial institutions and Chinese companies or enterprises;  
(3) a branch of a foreign bank; and  
(4) a representative office of a foreign bank.  
The institutions listed in Items (1) through (3) of the preceding paragraph are hereinafter collectively referred to as business-operating institutions of foreign-funded banks.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称外国金融机构，是指在中华人民共和国境外注册并经所在国家或者地区金融监管当局批准或者许可的金融机构。  
本条例所称外国银行，是指在中华人民共和国境外注册并经所在国家或者地区金融监管当局批准或者许可的商业银行。

**Article 3** For the purpose of these Regulations, a foreign financial institution means a financial institution registered outside the People's Republic of China and approved or licensed by the financial regulatory authority of its home country or region.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, a foreign bank means a commercial bank registered outside the People's Republic of China and approved or licensed by the financial regulatory authority of its home country or region.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　外资银行必须遵守中华人民共和国法律、法规，不得损害中华人民共和国的国家利益、社会公共利益。  
外资银行的正当活动和合法权益受中华人民共和国法律保护。

**Article 4** A foreign-funded bank must abide by the laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China and may not harm the national interests or public interests of the People's Republic of China.  
The legitimate activities and lawful rights and interests of a foreign-funded bank shall be protected by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院银行业监督管理机构及其派出机构(以下统称银行业监督管理机构)负责对外资银行及其活动实施监督管理。法律、行政法规规定其他监督管理部门或者机构对外资银行及其活动实施监督管理的，依照其规定。

**Article 5** The banking regulatory authority of the State Council and its local offices (hereinafter collectively referred to as the banking regulatory authorities) shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of foreign-funded banks and their activities. Where other regulatory departments or authorities are provided by laws or administrative regulations to conduct supervision and administration over foreign-funded banks and their activities, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院银行业监督管理机构根据国家区域经济发展战略及相关政策制定有关鼓励和引导的措施，报国务院批准后实施。

**Article 6** The banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall, in accordance with the national regional economic development strategies and relevant policies, formulate measures for encouragement and guidance, and shall implement them after submitting them to the State Council for approval.

### 第二章　设立与登记

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　设立外资银行及其分支机构，应当经银行业监督管理机构审查批准。

**Article 7** The establishment of a foreign-funded bank or its branch shall be subject to examination and approval by the banking regulatory authorities.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　外商独资银行、中外合资银行的注册资本最低限额为10亿元人民币或者等值的自由兑换货币。注册资本应当是实缴资本。  
外商独资银行、中外合资银行在中华人民共和国境内设立的分行，应当由其总行无偿拨给人民币或者自由兑换货币的营运资金。外商独资银行、中外合资银行拨给各分支机构营运资金的总和，不得超过总行资本金总额的60%。  
外国银行分行应当由其总行无偿拨给不少于2亿元人民币或者等值的自由兑换货币的营运资金。  
国务院银行业监督管理机构根据外资银行营业性机构的业务范围和审慎监管的需要，可以提高注册资本或者营运资金的最低限额，并规定其中的人民币份额。

**Article 8** The minimum registered capital for a wholly foreign-owned bank or a Sino-foreign equity joint venture bank is RMB 1 billion yuan or an equivalent amount in a freely convertible currency. The registered capital shall be paid-in capital.  
A branch established within the territory of the People's Republic of China by a wholly foreign-owned bank or a Sino-foreign equity joint venture bank shall be allocated operating funds in RMB or a freely convertible currency by its head office without compensation. The total operating funds allocated by a wholly foreign-owned bank or a Sino-foreign equity joint venture bank to all its branches shall not exceed 60 percent of the total capital of the head office.  
A branch of a foreign bank shall be allocated operating funds of not less than RMB 200 million yuan or an equivalent amount in a freely convertible currency by its head office without compensation.  
The banking regulatory authority of the State Council may, according to the business scope of the business-operating institution of a foreign-funded bank and the need for prudential supervision, raise the minimum amount of registered capital or operating funds and prescribe the RMB portion thereof.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　拟设外商独资银行、中外合资银行的股东或者拟设分行、代表处的外国银行应当具备下列条件：  
(一)具有持续盈利能力，信誉良好，无重大违法违规记录；  
(二)拟设外商独资银行的股东、中外合资银行的外方股东或者拟设分行、代表处的外国银行具有从事国际金融活动的经验；  
(三)具有有效的反洗钱制度；  
(四)拟设外商独资银行的股东、中外合资银行的外方股东或者拟设分行、代表处的外国银行受到所在国家或者地区金融监管当局的有效监管，并且其申请经所在国家或者地区金融监管当局同意；  
(五)国务院银行业监督管理机构规定的其他审慎性条件。  
拟设外商独资银行的股东、中外合资银行的外方股东或者拟设分行、代表处的外国银行所在国家或者地区应当具有完善的金融监督管理制度，并且其金融监管当局已经与国务院银行业监督管理机构建立良好的监督管理合作机制。

**Article 9** The shareholders of a proposed wholly foreign-owned bank or Sino-foreign equity joint venture bank, or the foreign bank applying to establish a branch or a representative office, shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having sustainable profitability, good reputation, and no records of major illegal or disciplinary violations;  
(2) the shareholders of the proposed wholly foreign-owned bank, the foreign shareholders of the proposed Sino-foreign equity joint venture bank, or the foreign bank applying to establish a branch or a representative office have experience in international financial activities;  
(3) having an effective anti-money laundering system;  
(4) the shareholders of the proposed wholly foreign-owned bank, the foreign shareholders of the proposed Sino-foreign equity joint venture bank, or the foreign bank applying to establish a branch or a representative office are subject to effective supervision by the financial regulatory authority of its home country or region, and its application is consented to by such financial regulatory authority; and  
(5) other prudential conditions prescribed by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The home country or region of the shareholders of the proposed wholly foreign-owned bank, the foreign shareholders of the proposed Sino-foreign equity joint venture bank, or the foreign bank applying to establish a branch or a representative office shall have a sound financial supervision and administration system, and its financial regulatory authority shall have established a sound mechanism for supervisory cooperation with the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　拟设外商独资银行的股东应当为金融机构，除应当具备[本条例第九条](#art-9)规定的条件外，其中唯一或者控股股东还应当具备下列条件：  
(一)为商业银行；  
(二)资本充足率符合所在国家或者地区金融监管当局以及国务院银行业监督管理机构的规定。

**Article 10** The shareholders of a proposed wholly foreign-owned bank shall be financial institutions. In addition to meeting the conditions prescribed in Article 9 of these Regulations, the sole or controlling shareholder therein shall also meet the following conditions:  
(1) being a commercial bank; and  
(2) having a capital adequacy ratio that complies with the provisions of the financial regulatory authority of its home country or region and the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　拟设中外合资银行的股东除应当具备[本条例第九条](#art-9)规定的条件外，其中外方股东应当为金融机构，且外方唯一或者主要股东还应当具备下列条件：  
(一)为商业银行；  
(二)资本充足率符合所在国家或者地区金融监管当局以及国务院银行业监督管理机构的规定。

**Article 11** In addition to meeting the conditions prescribed in Article 9 of these Regulations, the foreign shareholders of a proposed Sino-foreign equity joint venture bank shall be financial institutions, and the sole or principal foreign shareholder therein shall also meet the following conditions:  
(1) being a commercial bank; and  
(2) having a capital adequacy ratio that complies with the provisions of the financial regulatory authority of its home country or region and the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　拟设分行的外国银行除应当具备[本条例第九条](#art-9)规定的条件外，其资本充足率还应当符合所在国家或者地区金融监管当局以及国务院银行业监督管理机构的规定。

**Article 12** In addition to meeting the conditions prescribed in Article 9 of these Regulations, a foreign bank proposing to establish a branch shall also have a capital adequacy ratio that complies with the provisions of the financial regulatory authority of its home country or region and the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　外国银行在中华人民共和国境内设立营业性机构的，除已设立的代表处外，不得增设代表处，但符合国家区域经济发展战略及相关政策的地区除外。  
代表处经批准改制为营业性机构的，应当依法办理原代表处的注销登记手续。

**Article 13** Where a foreign bank establishes a business-operating institution within the territory of the People's Republic of China, it shall not establish any additional representative office except for the representative office already established, unless the area complies with the national regional economic development strategies and relevant policies.  
Where a representative office is restructured into a business-operating institution upon approval, the procedures for cancellation of registration of the original representative office shall be handled according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　设立外资银行营业性机构，应当先申请筹建，并将下列申请资料报送拟设机构所在地的银行业监督管理机构：  
(一)申请书，内容包括拟设机构的名称、所在地、注册资本或者营运资金、申请经营的业务种类等；  
(二)可行性研究报告；  
(三)拟设外商独资银行、中外合资银行的章程草案；  
(四)拟设外商独资银行、中外合资银行各方股东签署的经营合同；  
(五)拟设外商独资银行、中外合资银行的股东或者拟设分行的外国银行的章程；  
(六)拟设外商独资银行、中外合资银行的股东或者拟设分行的外国银行及其所在集团的组织结构图、主要股东名单、海外分支机构和关联企业名单；  
(七)拟设外商独资银行、中外合资银行的股东或者拟设分行的外国银行最近3年的年报；  
(八)拟设外商独资银行、中外合资银行的股东或者拟设分行的外国银行的反洗钱制度；  
(九)拟设外商独资银行的股东、中外合资银行的外方股东或者拟设分行的外国银行所在国家或者地区金融监管当局核发的营业执照或者经营金融业务许可文件的复印件及对其申请的意见书；  
(十)国务院银行业监督管理机构规定的其他资料。  
拟设机构所在地的银行业监督管理机构应当将申请资料连同审核意见，及时报送国务院银行业监督管理机构。

**Article 14** To establish a business-operating institution of a foreign-funded bank, an application for preparatory work shall be submitted first, and the following application documents shall be submitted to the banking regulatory authority of the place where the proposed institution is to be located:  
(1) a written application, specifying the name, location, registered capital or operating funds, and types of business applied for, among others;  
(2) a feasibility study report;  
(3) a draft of the articles of association of the proposed wholly foreign-owned bank or Sino-foreign equity joint venture bank;  
(4) the business contract signed by all shareholders of the proposed wholly foreign-owned bank or Sino-foreign equity joint venture bank;  
(5) the articles of association of the shareholder of the proposed wholly foreign-owned bank or Sino-foreign equity joint venture bank, or the foreign bank proposing to establish a branch;  
(6) organizational charts of the shareholder of the proposed wholly foreign-owned bank or Sino-foreign equity joint venture bank, or the foreign bank proposing to establish a branch and the group to which it belongs, a list of major shareholders, and a list of overseas branches and affiliated enterprises;  
(7) the annual reports for the latest three years of the shareholder of the proposed wholly foreign-owned bank or Sino-foreign equity joint venture bank, or the foreign bank proposing to establish a branch;  
(8) the anti-money laundering system of the shareholder of the proposed wholly foreign-owned bank or Sino-foreign equity joint venture bank, or the foreign bank proposing to establish a branch;  
(9) copies of the business license or the license document for engaging in financial business issued by the financial regulatory authority of the home country or region of the shareholder of the proposed wholly foreign-owned bank, the foreign shareholder of the proposed Sino-foreign equity joint venture bank, or the foreign bank proposing to establish a branch, and a written opinion on its application; and  
(10) other documents prescribed by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The banking regulatory authority of the place where the proposed institution is to be located shall promptly submit the application documents together with its review opinions to the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院银行业监督管理机构应当自收到设立外资银行营业性机构完整的申请资料之日起6个月内作出批准或者不批准筹建的决定，并书面通知申请人。决定不批准的，应当说明理由。  
特殊情况下，国务院银行业监督管理机构不能在前款规定期限内完成审查并作出批准或者不批准筹建决定的，可以适当延长审查期限，并书面通知申请人，但延长期限不得超过3个月。  
申请人凭批准筹建文件到拟设机构所在地的银行业监督管理机构领取开业申请表。

**Article 15** The banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall, within six months from the date of receipt of the complete application documents for the establishment of a business-operating institution of a foreign-funded bank, make a decision to approve or disapprove the preparatory work, and shall notify the applicant in writing. If the decision is disapproval, the reasons therefor shall be stated.  
Under special circumstances, if the banking regulatory authority of the State Council is unable to complete the review and make a decision to approve or disapprove the preparatory work within the time limit set forth in the preceding paragraph, it may appropriately extend the review period and notify the applicant in writing, but the extension shall not exceed three months.  
The applicant shall, based on the document approving the preparatory work, collect the application form for business commencement from the banking regulatory authority of the place where the proposed institution is to be located.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　申请人应当自获准筹建之日起6个月内完成筹建工作。在规定期限内未完成筹建工作的，应当说明理由，经拟设机构所在地的银行业监督管理机构批准，可以延长3个月。在延长期内仍未完成筹建工作的，国务院银行业监督管理机构作出的批准筹建决定自动失效。

**Article 16** The applicant shall complete the preparatory work within six months from the date of approval for preparatory work. If the preparatory work is not completed within the prescribed time limit, reasons shall be stated, and with the approval of the banking regulatory authority of the place where the proposed institution is to be located, an extension of three months may be granted. If the preparatory work is still not completed within the extended period, the decision approving the preparatory work made by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall automatically cease to have effect.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　经验收合格完成筹建工作的，申请人应当将填写好的开业申请表连同下列资料报送拟设机构所在地的银行业监督管理机构：  
(一)拟设机构的主要负责人名单及简历；  
(二)对拟任该机构主要负责人的授权书；  
(三)法定验资机构出具的验资证明；  
(四)安全防范措施和与业务有关的其他设施的资料；  
(五)设立分行的外国银行对该分行承担税务、债务的责任保证书；  
(六)国务院银行业监督管理机构规定的其他资料。  
拟设机构所在地的银行业监督管理机构应当将申请资料连同审核意见，及时报送国务院银行业监督管理机构。

**Article 17** If the preparatory work has been completed and passed the inspection, the applicant shall submit the completed application form for business commencement together with the following documents to the banking regulatory authority of the place where the proposed institution is to be located:  
(1) a list and resume of the principal responsible persons of the proposed institution;  
(2) a letter of authorization for the person proposed to be the principal responsible person of the institution;  
(3) a capital verification certificate issued by a statutory capital verification institution;  
(4) documents on security precautions and other facilities related to the business;  
(5) a letter of guarantee issued by the foreign bank establishing the branch to assume responsibility for the taxes and debts of such branch; and  
(6) other documents prescribed by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The banking regulatory authority of the place where the proposed institution is to be located shall promptly submit the application documents together with its review opinions to the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院银行业监督管理机构应当自收到完整的开业申请资料之日起2个月内，作出批准或者不批准开业的决定，并书面通知申请人。决定批准的，应当颁发金融许可证；决定不批准的，应当说明理由。

**Article 18** The banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall, within two months from the date of receipt of the complete application documents for business commencement, make a decision to approve or disapprove the commencement of business, and shall notify the applicant in writing. If the decision is approval, a financial license shall be issued; if the decision is disapproval, the reasons therefor shall be stated.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　经批准设立的外资银行营业性机构，应当凭金融许可证向市场监督管理部门办理登记，领取营业执照。

**Article 19** A business-operating institution of a foreign-funded bank established upon approval shall, on the strength of its financial license, register with the market regulatory authority and obtain a business license.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　设立外国银行代表处，应当将下列申请资料报送拟设代表处所在地的银行业监督管理机构：  
(一)申请书，内容包括拟设代表处的名称、所在地等；  
(二)可行性研究报告；  
(三)申请人的章程；  
(四)申请人及其所在集团的组织结构图、主要股东名单、海外分支机构和关联企业名单；  
(五)申请人最近3年的年报；  
(六)申请人的反洗钱制度；  
(七)拟任该代表处首席代表的身份证明和学历证明的复印件、简历以及拟任人有无不良记录的陈述书；  
(八)对拟任该代表处首席代表的授权书；  
(九)申请人所在国家或者地区金融监管当局核发的营业执照或者经营金融业务许可文件的复印件及对其申请的意见书；  
(十)国务院银行业监督管理机构规定的其他资料。  
拟设代表处所在地的银行业监督管理机构应当将申请资料连同审核意见，及时报送国务院银行业监督管理机构。

**Article 20** To establish a representative office of a foreign bank, the following application documents shall be submitted to the banking regulatory authority of the place where the proposed representative office is to be located:  
(1) a written application, specifying the name, location, and other details of the proposed representative office;  
(2) a feasibility study report;  
(3) the articles of association of the applicant;  
(4) organizational charts of the applicant and the group to which it belongs, a list of major shareholders, and a list of overseas branches and affiliated enterprises;  
(5) the annual reports for the latest three years of the applicant;  
(6) the anti-money laundering system of the applicant;  
(7) copies of the identity certificate and academic qualification certificate of the person proposed to be the chief representative of the representative office, a resume, and a statement on whether the proposed person has any adverse records;  
(8) a letter of authorization for the person proposed to be the chief representative of the representative office;  
(9) copies of the business license or the license document for engaging in financial business issued by the financial regulatory authority of the home country or region of the applicant, and a written opinion on its application; and  
(10) other documents prescribed by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The banking regulatory authority of the place where the proposed representative office is to be located shall promptly submit the application documents together with its review opinions to the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国务院银行业监督管理机构应当自收到设立外国银行代表处完整的申请资料之日起6个月内作出批准或者不批准设立的决定，并书面通知申请人。决定不批准的，应当说明理由。

**Article 21** The banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall, within six months from the date of receipt of the complete application materials for the establishment of a representative office of a foreign bank, make a decision to approve or disapprove the establishment, and shall notify the applicant in writing. If the decision is disapproval, the reasons shall be stated.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　经批准设立的外国银行代表处，应当凭批准文件向市场监督管理部门办理登记，领取外国企业常驻代表机构登记证。

**Article 22** A representative office of a foreign bank that has been approved for establishment shall, on the basis of the approval document, undergo registration with the market supervision and administration department and obtain the Registration Certificate of Resident Representative Office of Foreign Enterprise.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　[本条例第十四条](#art-14)、第十七条、第二十条所列资料，除年报外，凡用外文书写的，应当附有中文译本。

**Article 23** The materials listed in Articles 14, 17, and 20 of these Regulations, except for annual reports, if written in a foreign language, shall be accompanied by a Chinese translation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　按照合法性、审慎性和持续经营原则，经国务院银行业监督管理机构批准，外国银行可以将其在中华人民共和国境内设立的分行改制为由其单独出资的外商独资银行。申请人应当按照国务院银行业监督管理机构规定的审批条件、程序、申请资料提出设立外商独资银行的申请。

**Article 24** In accordance with the principles of legality, prudence, and going concern, and subject to approval by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, a foreign bank may convert its branch established within the territory of the People's Republic of China into a wholly foreign-funded bank solely invested by it. The applicant shall submit an application for the establishment of a wholly foreign-funded bank in accordance with the review conditions, procedures, and application materials prescribed by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　外国银行可以在中华人民共和国境内同时设立外商独资银行和外国银行分行，或者同时设立中外合资银行和外国银行分行。

**Article 25** A foreign bank may simultaneously establish a wholly foreign-funded bank and a branch of a foreign bank, or simultaneously establish a Sino-foreign joint venture bank and a branch of a foreign bank, within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　外资银行董事、高级管理人员、首席代表的任职资格应当符合国务院银行业监督管理机构规定的条件，并经国务院银行业监督管理机构核准。

**Article 26** The qualifications of directors, senior officers, and chief representatives of a foreign-funded bank shall meet the conditions prescribed by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, and shall be approved by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　外资银行有下列情形之一的，应当经国务院银行业监督管理机构批准，并按照规定提交申请资料，依法向市场监督管理部门办理有关登记：  
(一)变更注册资本或者营运资金；  
(二)变更机构名称、营业场所或者办公场所；  
(三)调整业务范围；  
(四)变更股东或者调整股东持股比例；  
(五)修改章程；  
(六)国务院银行业监督管理机构规定的其他情形。  
外资银行更换董事、高级管理人员、首席代表，应当报经国务院银行业监督管理机构核准其任职资格。

**Article 27** A foreign-funded bank that falls under any of the following circumstances shall obtain the approval of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, submit application materials in accordance with the provisions, and undergo relevant registration with the market supervision and administration department according to law:  
(1) Change of registered capital or operating funds;  
(2) Change of name, business premises, or office premises;  
(3) Adjustment of business scope;  
(4) Change of shareholders or adjustment of shareholding ratios of shareholders;  
(5) Amendment of the articles of association;  
(6) Other circumstances prescribed by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Where a foreign-funded bank replaces directors, senior officers, or chief representatives, it shall report to the banking regulatory authority of the State Council for approval of their qualifications.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　外商独资银行、中外合资银行变更股东的，变更后的股东应当符合[本条例第九条](#art-9)、第十条或者第十一条关于股东的条件。

**Article 28** Where a wholly foreign-funded bank or a Sino-foreign joint venture bank changes its shareholders, the shareholders after the change shall meet the conditions for shareholders as provided in Articles 9, 10, or 11 of these Regulations.

### 第三章　业务范围

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　外商独资银行、中外合资银行按照国务院银行业监督管理机构批准的业务范围，可以经营下列部分或者全部外汇业务和人民币业务：  
(一)吸收公众存款；  
(二)发放短期、中期和长期贷款；  
(三)办理票据承兑与贴现；  
(四)代理发行、代理兑付、承销政府债券；  
(五)买卖政府债券、金融债券，买卖股票以外的其他外币有价证券；  
(六)提供信用证服务及担保；  
(七)办理国内外结算；  
(八)买卖、代理买卖外汇；  
(九)代理收付款项及代理保险业务；  
(十)从事同业拆借；  
(十一)从事银行卡业务；  
(十二)提供保管箱服务；  
(十三)提供资信调查和咨询服务；  
(十四)经国务院银行业监督管理机构批准的其他业务。  
外商独资银行、中外合资银行经中国人民银行批准，可以经营结汇、售汇业务。

**Article 29** A wholly foreign-funded bank or a Sino-foreign joint venture bank may, within the business scope approved by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, engage in some or all of the following foreign exchange business and renminbi business:  
(1) Taking deposits from the public;  
(2) Granting short-term, medium-term, and long-term loans;  
(3) Handling acceptance and discount of negotiable instruments;  
(4) Acting as agent for issuance, agent for payment, and underwriting of government bonds;  
(5) Buying and selling government bonds and financial bonds, and buying and selling foreign currency securities other than stocks;  
(6) Providing letter of credit services and guarantees;  
(7) Handling domestic and international settlements;  
(8) Buying, selling, and acting as agent for the buying and selling of foreign exchange;  
(9) Acting as agent for receipts and payments and acting as agent for insurance business;  
(10) Engaging in interbank lending;  
(11) Engaging in bank card business;  
(12) Providing safe deposit box services;  
(13) Providing credit investigation and consulting services;  
(14) Other businesses approved by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.  
A wholly foreign-funded bank or a Sino-foreign joint venture bank may, with the approval of the People's Bank of China, engage in the business of exchange settlement and sale of foreign exchange.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　外商独资银行、中外合资银行的分支机构在总行授权范围内开展业务，其民事责任由总行承担。

**Article 30** The branches of a wholly foreign-funded bank or a Sino-foreign joint venture bank shall carry out business within the scope authorized by the head office, and their civil liability shall be borne by the head office.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　外国银行分行按照国务院银行业监督管理机构批准的业务范围，可以经营下列部分或者全部外汇业务以及对除中国境内公民以外客户的人民币业务：  
(一)吸收公众存款；  
(二)发放短期、中期和长期贷款；  
(三)办理票据承兑与贴现；  
(四)代理发行、代理兑付、承销政府债券；  
(五)买卖政府债券、金融债券，买卖股票以外的其他外币有价证券；  
(六)提供信用证服务及担保；  
(七)办理国内外结算；  
(八)买卖、代理买卖外汇；  
(九)代理收付款项及代理保险业务；  
(十)从事同业拆借；  
(十一)提供保管箱服务；  
(十二)提供资信调查和咨询服务；  
(十三)经国务院银行业监督管理机构批准的其他业务。  
外国银行分行可以吸收中国境内公民每笔不少于50万元人民币的定期存款。  
外国银行分行经中国人民银行批准，可以经营结汇、售汇业务。

**Article 31** A branch of a foreign bank may, within the business scope approved by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, engage in some or all of the following foreign exchange business and renminbi business for customers other than Chinese citizens within the territory of China:  
(1) Taking deposits from the public;  
(2) Granting short-term, medium-term, and long-term loans;  
(3) Handling acceptance and discount of negotiable instruments;  
(4) Acting as agent for issuance, agent for payment, and underwriting of government bonds;  
(5) Buying and selling government bonds and financial bonds, and buying and selling foreign currency securities other than stocks;  
(6) Providing letter of credit services and guarantees;  
(7) Handling domestic and international settlements;  
(8) Buying, selling, and acting as agent for the buying and selling of foreign exchange;  
(9) Acting as agent for receipts and payments and acting as agent for insurance business;  
(10) Engaging in interbank lending;  
(11) Providing safe deposit box services;  
(12) Providing credit investigation and consulting services;  
(13) Other businesses approved by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.  
A branch of a foreign bank may take fixed deposits of not less than RMB 500,000 per transaction from Chinese citizens within the territory of China.  
A branch of a foreign bank may, with the approval of the People's Bank of China, engage in the business of exchange settlement and sale of foreign exchange.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　外国银行分行及其分支机构的民事责任由其总行承担。

**Article 32** The civil liability of a branch of a foreign bank and its sub-branches shall be borne by its head office.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　外国银行代表处可以从事与其代表的外国银行业务相关的联络、市场调查、咨询等非经营性活动。  
外国银行代表处的行为所产生的民事责任，由其所代表的外国银行承担。

**Article 33** A representative office of a foreign bank may engage in non-operational activities such as liaison, market research, and consulting related to the business of the foreign bank it represents.  
The civil liability arising from the acts of the representative office of a foreign bank shall be borne by the foreign bank it represents.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　外资银行营业性机构经营[本条例第二十九条](#art-29)或者第三十一条规定业务范围内的人民币业务的，应当符合国务院银行业监督管理机构规定的审慎性要求。

**Article 34** Where a foreign-funded bank operating entity engages in renminbi business within the scope of business provided in Article 29 or Article 31 of these Regulations, it shall meet the prudential requirements prescribed by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　外资银行营业性机构应当按照有关规定，制定本行的业务规则，建立、健全风险管理和内部控制制度，并遵照执行。

**Article 35** A foreign-funded bank operating entity shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, formulate its own business rules, establish and improve risk management and internal control systems, and comply with them.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　外资银行营业性机构应当遵守国家统一的会计制度和国务院银行业监督管理机构有关信息披露的规定。

**Article 36** A foreign-funded bank operating entity shall comply with the uniform national accounting system and the provisions of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council on information disclosure.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　外资银行营业性机构举借外债，应当按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 37** A foreign-funded bank operating entity that borrows foreign debts shall do so in accordance with the relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　外资银行营业性机构应当按照有关规定确定存款、贷款利率及各种手续费率。

**Article 38** A foreign-funded bank operating entity shall determine deposit and loan interest rates as well as various fee rates in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　外资银行营业性机构经营存款业务，应当按照中国人民银行的规定交存存款准备金。

**Article 39** A foreign-funded bank operating entity engaged in deposit business shall deposit reserve funds in accordance with the provisions of the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　外商独资银行、中外合资银行应当遵守《中华人民共和国商业银行法》关于资产负债比例管理的规定。外国银行分行变更的由其总行单独出资的外商独资银行以及本条例施行前设立的外商独资银行、中外合资银行，其资产负债比例不符合规定的，应当在国务院银行业监督管理机构规定的期限内达到规定要求。  
国务院银行业监督管理机构可以要求风险较高、风险管理能力较弱的外商独资银行、中外合资银行提高资本充足率。

**Article 40** A wholly foreign-funded bank and a Sino-foreign joint venture bank shall comply with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Commercial Banks concerning the management of asset-liability ratios. Where a wholly foreign-funded bank converted from a branch of a foreign bank and solely invested by its head office, or a wholly foreign-funded bank or a Sino-foreign joint venture bank established before these Regulations come into force, does not conform to the provisions on asset-liability ratios, it shall meet the prescribed requirements within the time limit set by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The banking regulatory authority of the State Council may require a wholly foreign-funded bank or a Sino-foreign joint venture bank with higher risk and weaker risk management capability to increase its capital adequacy ratio.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　外资银行营业性机构应当按照规定计提呆账准备金。

**Article 41** A foreign-funded bank operating institution shall set aside provisions for doubtful accounts in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　外商独资银行、中外合资银行应当遵守国务院银行业监督管理机构有关公司治理的规定。

**Article 42** A wholly foreign-owned bank or Sino-foreign joint venture bank shall comply with the provisions of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council on corporate governance.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　外商独资银行、中外合资银行应当遵守国务院银行业监督管理机构有关关联交易的规定。

**Article 43** A wholly foreign-owned bank or Sino-foreign joint venture bank shall comply with the provisions of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council on connected transactions.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　外国银行分行应当按照国务院银行业监督管理机构的规定，持有一定比例的生息资产。

**Article 44** A branch of a foreign bank shall hold a certain proportion of interest-bearing assets in accordance with the provisions of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　外国银行分行营运资金加准备金等项之和中的人民币份额与其人民币风险资产的比例不得低于8%。  
资本充足率持续符合所在国家或者地区金融监管当局以及国务院银行业监督管理机构规定的外国银行，其分行不受前款规定的限制。  
国务院银行业监督管理机构可以要求风险较高、风险管理能力较弱的外国银行分行提高本条第一款规定的比例。

**Article 45** The proportion of the RMB portion in the sum of the operating funds plus provisions of a branch of a foreign bank to its RMB risk assets shall not be less than 8%.  
The branch of a foreign bank whose capital adequacy ratio continuously meets the requirements of the financial regulatory authority of the country or region where it is located and the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall not be subject to the restriction in the preceding paragraph.  
The banking regulatory authority of the State Council may require a foreign bank branch with higher risks and weaker risk management capabilities to increase the proportion specified in the first paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　外国银行分行应当确保其资产的流动性。流动性资产余额与流动性负债余额的比例不得低于25%。

**Article 46** A branch of a foreign bank shall ensure the liquidity of its assets. The ratio of the balance of liquid assets to the balance of liquid liabilities shall not be less than 25%.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　外国银行分行境内本外币资产余额不得低于境内本外币负债余额。

**Article 47** The balance of domestic assets denominated in local and foreign currencies of a branch of a foreign bank shall not be less than the balance of its domestic liabilities denominated in local and foreign currencies.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　在中华人民共和国境内设立2家及2家以上分行的外国银行，应当授权其中1家分行对其他分行实施统一管理。  
国务院银行业监督管理机构对外国银行在中华人民共和国境内设立的分行实行合并监管。

**Article 48** A foreign bank that has established two or more branches within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall authorize one of its branches to exercise unified management over the other branches.  
The banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall conduct consolidated supervision over the branches established by a foreign bank within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　外资银行营业性机构应当按照国务院银行业监督管理机构的有关规定，向其所在地的银行业监督管理机构报告跨境大额资金流动和资产转移情况。

**Article 49** A foreign-funded bank operating institution shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, report to the banking regulatory authority in its locality on cross-border large-amount fund flows and asset transfers.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国务院银行业监督管理机构根据外资银行营业性机构的风险状况，可以依法采取责令暂停部分业务、责令撤换高级管理人员等特别监管措施。

**Article 50** The banking regulatory authority of the State Council may, according to the risk status of a foreign-funded bank operating institution and in accordance with law, take special supervisory measures such as ordering the suspension of part of its business or ordering the removal of its senior officers.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　外资银行营业性机构应当聘请在中华人民共和国境内依法设立的会计师事务所对其财务会计报告进行审计，并应当向其所在地的银行业监督管理机构报告。解聘会计师事务所的，应当说明理由。

**Article 51** A foreign-funded bank operating institution shall engage an accounting firm established according to law within the territory of the People's Republic of China to audit its financial and accounting reports, and shall report to the banking regulatory authority in its locality. If it dismisses the accounting firm, it shall state the reasons therefor.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　外资银行营业性机构应当按照规定向银行业监督管理机构报送财务会计报告、报表和有关资料。  
外国银行代表处应当按照规定向银行业监督管理机构报送资料。

**Article 52** A foreign-funded bank operating institution shall, in accordance with the provisions, submit financial and accounting reports, statements and relevant materials to the banking regulatory authority.  
A representative office of a foreign bank shall, in accordance with the provisions, submit materials to the banking regulatory authority.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　外资银行应当接受银行业监督管理机构依法进行的监督检查，不得拒绝、阻碍。

**Article 53** A foreign-funded bank shall accept the supervision and inspection conducted by the banking regulatory authority in accordance with law, and shall not refuse or obstruct such supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　外商独资银行、中外合资银行应当设置独立的内部控制系统、风险管理系统、财务会计系统、计算机信息管理系统。

**Article 54** A wholly foreign-owned bank or Sino-foreign joint venture bank shall establish an independent internal control system, risk management system, financial accounting system and computer information management system.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　外国银行在中华人民共和国境内设立的外商独资银行、中外合资银行的董事长、高级管理人员和外国银行分行的高级管理人员不得相互兼职。

**Article 55** The chairman of the board of directors and senior officers of a wholly foreign-owned bank or Sino-foreign joint venture bank established by a foreign bank within the territory of the People's Republic of China, and the senior officers of a branch of a foreign bank, shall not hold concurrent positions in each other's institutions.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　外国银行在中华人民共和国境内设立的外商独资银行、中外合资银行与外国银行分行之间进行的交易必须符合商业原则，交易条件不得优于与非关联方进行交易的条件。外国银行对其在中华人民共和国境内设立的外商独资银行与外国银行分行之间的资金交易，应当提供全额担保。

**Article 56** Transactions between a wholly foreign-owned bank or Sino-foreign joint venture bank established by a foreign bank within the territory of the People's Republic of China and a branch of a foreign bank must comply with arm's length principles, and the terms of such transactions shall not be more favorable than those with unrelated parties. The foreign bank shall provide full guarantee for fund transactions between the wholly foreign-owned bank established by it within the territory of the People's Republic of China and its branch.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　外国银行代表处及其工作人员，不得从事任何形式的经营性活动。

**Article 57** A representative office of a foreign bank and its staff members shall not engage in any form of operational activities.

### 第五章　终止与清算

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　外资银行营业性机构自行终止业务活动的，应当在终止业务活动30日前以书面形式向国务院银行业监督管理机构提出申请，经审查批准予以解散或者关闭并进行清算。

**Article 58** If a foreign-funded bank operating institution voluntarily terminates its business activities, it shall submit a written application to the banking regulatory authority of the State Council 30 days prior to the termination of its business activities, and shall, upon examination and approval, be dissolved or closed and undergo liquidation.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　外资银行营业性机构已经或者可能发生信用危机，严重影响存款人和其他客户合法权益的，国务院银行业监督管理机构可以依法对该外资银行营业性机构实行接管或者促成机构重组。

**Article 59** If a foreign-funded bank operating institution has experienced or is likely to experience a credit crisis, which seriously affects the legitimate rights and interests of depositors and other clients, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council may, in accordance with law, take over the foreign-funded bank operating institution or facilitate its restructuring.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　外资银行营业性机构因解散、关闭、依法被撤销或者宣告破产而终止的，其清算的具体事宜，依照中华人民共和国有关法律、法规的规定办理。

**Article 60** Where a foreign-funded bank operating institution terminates due to dissolution, closure, revocation according to law, or declaration of bankruptcy, the specific matters of its liquidation shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　外资银行营业性机构清算终结，应当在法定期限内向原登记机关办理注销登记。

**Article 61** Where the liquidation of a business institution of a foreign-funded bank concludes, it shall undergo cancellation of registration with the original registration authority within the statutory time limit.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　外国银行代表处自行终止活动的，应当经国务院银行业监督管理机构批准予以关闭，并在法定期限内向原登记机关办理注销登记。

**Article 62** Where a representative office of a foreign bank terminates its activities on its own, it shall, upon approval of the closure by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, undergo cancellation of registration with the original registration authority within the statutory time limit.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　未经国务院银行业监督管理机构审查批准，擅自设立外资银行或者非法从事银行业金融机构的业务活动的，由国务院银行业监督管理机构予以取缔，自被取缔之日起5年内，国务院银行业监督管理机构不受理该当事人设立外资银行的申请；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由国务院银行业监督管理机构没收违法所得，违法所得50万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足50万元的，处50万元以上200万元以下罚款。

**Article 63** Where a foreign-funded bank is established without approval of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, or business activities of a banking financial institution are illegally engaged in, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall ban such establishment or activities; within five years from the date of the ban, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall not accept any application of the said party for establishing a foreign-funded bank; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall confiscate the illegal gains, and if the illegal gains amount to 500,000 yuan or more, concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 500,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　外资银行营业性机构有下列情形之一的，由国务院银行业监督管理机构责令改正，没收违法所得，违法所得50万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足50万元的，处50万元以上200万元以下罚款；情节特别严重或者逾期不改正的，可以责令停业整顿或者吊销其金融许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未经批准设立分支机构的；  
(二)未经批准变更、终止的；  
(三)违反规定从事未经批准的业务活动的；  
(四)违反规定提高或者降低存款利率、贷款利率的。

**Article 64** Where a business institution of a foreign-funded bank is under any of the following circumstances, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections, confiscate the illegal gains, and if the illegal gains amount to 500,000 yuan or more, concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 500,000 yuan, impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan; if the circumstances are especially serious or the institution fails to make corrections within the time limit, the authority may order it to suspend business for rectification or revoke its financial license; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) establishing a branch without approval;  
(2) altering or terminating without approval;  
(3) engaging in unauthorized business activities in violation of regulations;  
(4) raising or lowering deposit interest rates or loan interest rates in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　外资银行有下列情形之一的，由国务院银行业监督管理机构责令改正，处20万元以上50万元以下罚款；情节特别严重或者逾期不改正的，可以责令停业整顿、吊销其金融许可证、撤销代表处；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未按照有关规定进行信息披露的；  
(二)拒绝或者阻碍银行业监督管理机构依法进行的监督检查的；  
(三)提供虚假的或者隐瞒重要事实的财务会计报告、报表或者有关资料的；  
(四)隐匿、损毁监督检查所需的文件、证件、账簿、电子数据或者其他资料的；  
(五)未经任职资格核准任命董事、高级管理人员、首席代表的；  
(六)拒绝执行[本条例第五十条](#art-50)规定的特别监管措施的。

**Article 65** Where a foreign-funded bank is under any of the following circumstances, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are especially serious or the bank fails to make corrections within the time limit, the authority may order it to suspend business for rectification, revoke its financial license, or withdraw the representative office; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to disclose information in accordance with relevant provisions;  
(2) refusing or obstructing the supervision and inspection conducted by the banking regulatory authority according to law;  
(3) providing false financial accounting reports, statements or relevant materials, or concealing material facts;  
(4) concealing or destroying documents, certificates, account books, electronic data or other materials required for supervision and inspection;  
(5) appointing directors, senior officers or chief representatives without qualification approval;  
(6) refusing to implement special supervisory measures as provided for in Article 50 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　外资银行营业性机构违反本条例有关规定，未按期报送财务会计报告、报表或者有关资料，或者未按照规定制定有关业务规则、建立健全有关管理制度的，由国务院银行业监督管理机构责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处10万元以上30万元以下罚款。

**Article 66** Where a business institution of a foreign-funded bank violates relevant provisions of these Regulations by failing to submit financial accounting reports, statements or relevant materials within the prescribed time limit, or failing to formulate relevant business rules or establish and improve relevant management systems in accordance with regulations, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　外资银行营业性机构违反本条例第四章有关规定从事经营或者严重违反其他审慎经营规则的，由国务院银行业监督管理机构责令改正，处20万元以上50万元以下罚款；情节特别严重或者逾期不改正的，可以责令停业整顿或者吊销其金融许可证。

**Article 67** Where a business institution of a foreign-funded bank, in violation of the provisions of Chapter IV of these Regulations, engages in operations or seriously violates other prudent operation rules, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are especially serious or it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the authority may order it to suspend business for rectification or revoke its financial license.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　外资银行营业性机构违反本条例规定，国务院银行业监督管理机构除依照[本条例第六十三条](#art-63)至第六十七条规定处罚外，还可以区别不同情形，采取下列措施：  
(一)责令外资银行营业性机构撤换直接负责的董事、高级管理人员和其他直接责任人员；  
(二)外资银行营业性机构的行为尚不构成犯罪的，对直接负责的董事、高级管理人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处5万元以上50万元以下罚款；  
(三)取消直接负责的董事、高级管理人员一定期限直至终身在中华人民共和国境内的任职资格，禁止直接负责的董事、高级管理人员和其他直接责任人员一定期限直至终身在中华人民共和国境内从事银行业工作。

**Article 68** Where a business institution of a foreign-funded bank violates the provisions of these Regulations, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council may, in addition to imposing penalties under Articles 63 to 67 of these Regulations, take the following measures depending on the specific circumstances:  
(1) ordering the business institution of the foreign-funded bank to dismiss the directly responsible directors, senior officers and other directly liable persons;  
(2) if the act of the business institution of the foreign-funded bank does not constitute a crime, issuing a warning to the directly responsible directors, senior officers and other directly liable persons, and concurrently imposing a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan;  
(3) canceling the qualification of the directly responsible directors and senior officers to hold positions within the territory of the People's Republic of China for a fixed period up to life, and prohibiting the directly responsible directors, senior officers and other directly liable persons from engaging in banking business within the territory of the People's Republic of China for a fixed period up to life.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　外国银行代表处违反本条例规定，从事经营性活动的，由国务院银行业监督管理机构责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，违法所得50万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足50万元的，处50万元以上200万元以下罚款；情节严重的，由国务院银行业监督管理机构予以撤销；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 69** Where a representative office of a foreign bank, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, engages in operational activities, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections, issue a warning, confiscate the illegal gains, and if the illegal gains amount to 500,000 yuan or more, concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 500,000 yuan, impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall withdraw the representative office; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　外国银行代表处有下列情形之一的，由国务院银行业监督管理机构责令改正，给予警告，并处10万元以上30万元以下罚款；情节严重的，取消首席代表一定期限在中华人民共和国境内的任职资格或者要求其代表的外国银行撤换首席代表；情节特别严重的，由国务院银行业监督管理机构予以撤销：  
(一)未经批准变更办公场所的；  
(二)未按照规定向国务院银行业监督管理机构报送资料的；  
(三)违反本条例或者国务院银行业监督管理机构的其他规定的。

**Article 70** Where a representative office of a foreign bank is under any of the following circumstances, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections, issue a warning, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the authority shall cancel the qualification of the chief representative to hold a position within the territory of the People's Republic of China for a fixed period or require the foreign bank represented to replace the chief representative; if the circumstances are especially serious, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall withdraw the representative office:  
(1) changing the office premises without approval;  
(2) failing to submit materials to the banking regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with regulations;  
(3) violating these Regulations or other provisions of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　外资银行违反中华人民共和国其他法律、法规的，由有关主管机关依法处理。

**Article 71** Where a foreign-funded bank violates other laws or regulations of the People's Republic of China, the relevant competent authorities shall handle the matter according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区的金融机构在内地(大陆)设立的银行机构，比照适用本条例。国务院另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 72** Banking institutions established in the mainland (China) by financial institutions from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region and the Taiwan region shall apply these Regulations mutatis mutandis. Where the State Council has otherwise provided, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　本条例自2006年12月11日起施行。2001年12月20日国务院公布的《中华人民共和国外资金融机构管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 73** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 11, 2006. The Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Administration of Foreign-Funded Financial Institutions promulgated by the State Council on December 20, 2001 shall be abolished simultaneously.
