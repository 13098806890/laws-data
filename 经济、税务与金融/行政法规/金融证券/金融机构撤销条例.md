# 金融机构撤销条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2001-11-23  
**生效日期**：2001-12-15

> (2001年11月14日国务院第47次常务会议通过　2001年11月23日中华人民共和国国务院令第324号公布　自2001年12月15日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对金融活动的监督管理，维护金融秩序，保护国家利益和社会公众利益，制定本条例。 第二条　中国人民银行撤销金融机构，依照本条例执行。 本条例所称撤销，是指中国人民银行对经其批准设立的具有法人资格的金融机构依法采取行政强制措施，终止其经营活动，并予以解散。 第三条　中国人民银行及其工作人员以及其他有关人员依照本条例履行职责，应当依法为被撤销的金融机构保守秘密。 第四条　被撤销的金融机构所在地的地方人民政府应当组织有关部门，做好与撤销有关的工作。 第二章　撤销决定 第五条　金融机构有违法违规经营、经营管理不善等情形，不予撤销将严重危害金融秩序、损害社会公众利益的，应当依法撤销。 第六条　中国人民银行决定撤销金融机构，应当制作撤销决定书。 撤销决定自中国人民银行宣布之日起生效。 撤销决定应当在报纸上公告，并在被撤销的金融机构的营业场所张贴。 第七条　自撤销决定生效之日起，被撤销的金融机构必须立即停止经营活动，交回金融机构法人许可证及其分支机构营业许可证，其高级管理人员、董事会和股东大会必须立即停止行使职权。 第三章　撤销清算 第八条　商业银行依法被撤销的，由中国人民银行组织成立清算组；非银行金融机构依法被撤销的，由中国人民银行或者中国人民银行委托的有关地方人民政府组织成立清算组。清算自撤销决定生效之日起开始。清算组向中国人民银行负责并报告工作。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对金融活动的监督管理，维护金融秩序，保护国家利益和社会公众利益，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the supervision and administration of financial activities, maintaining financial order, and protecting the interests of the State and the public.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中国人民银行撤销金融机构，依照本条例执行。  
本条例所称撤销，是指中国人民银行对经其批准设立的具有法人资格的金融机构依法采取行政强制措施，终止其经营活动，并予以解散。

**Article 2** The revocation of a financial institution by the People's Bank of China shall be enforced in accordance with these Regulations.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "revocation" means that the People's Bank of China, according to law, takes administrative compulsion measures against a financial institution with the status of a legal person that was established upon its approval, terminates its business operations, and dissolves it.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中国人民银行及其工作人员以及其他有关人员依照本条例履行职责，应当依法为被撤销的金融机构保守秘密。

**Article 3** The People's Bank of China and its staff members as well as other relevant persons, when performing duties in accordance with these Regulations, shall keep secrets for the financial institution under revocation according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　被撤销的金融机构所在地的地方人民政府应当组织有关部门，做好与撤销有关的工作。

**Article 4** The local people's government of the place where the financial institution under revocation is located shall organize relevant departments to properly carry out the work related to the revocation.

### 第二章　撤销决定

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　金融机构有违法违规经营、经营管理不善等情形，不予撤销将严重危害金融秩序、损害社会公众利益的，应当依法撤销。

**Article 5** A financial institution that has illegal operations, poor management, or other circumstances, where failure to revoke it would seriously endanger the financial order and harm the public interests, shall be revoked according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　中国人民银行决定撤销金融机构，应当制作撤销决定书。  
撤销决定自中国人民银行宣布之日起生效。  
撤销决定应当在报纸上公告，并在被撤销的金融机构的营业场所张贴。

**Article 6** Where the People's Bank of China decides to revoke a financial institution, it shall produce a written decision of revocation.  
The decision of revocation shall take effect from the date when the People's Bank of China announces it.  
The decision of revocation shall be announced in a newspaper and shall be posted at the business premises of the financial institution under revocation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　自撤销决定生效之日起，被撤销的金融机构必须立即停止经营活动，交回金融机构法人许可证及其分支机构营业许可证，其高级管理人员、董事会和股东大会必须立即停止行使职权。

**Article 7** From the date when the decision of revocation takes effect, the financial institution under revocation must immediately cease its business operations, return the legal person permit for financial institutions and the business permits for its branches, and its senior officers, board of directors, and shareholders' general meeting must immediately cease exercising their functions and powers.

### 第三章　撤销清算

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　商业银行依法被撤销的，由中国人民银行组织成立清算组；非银行金融机构依法被撤销的，由中国人民银行或者中国人民银行委托的有关地方人民政府组织成立清算组。清算自撤销决定生效之日起开始。清算组向中国人民银行负责并报告工作。  
清算组由中国人民银行、财政、审计等有关部门、地方人民政府的代表和被撤销的金融机构股东的代表及有关专业人员组成。清算组组长及成员，由中国人民银行指定或者经中国人民银行同意。  
清算期间，清算组行使被撤销的金融机构的管理职权，清算组组长行使被撤销的金融机构的法定代表人职权。

**Article 8** Where a commercial bank is revoked according to law, the People's Bank of China shall organize the establishment of a liquidation team; where a non-bank financial institution is revoked according to law, the People's Bank of China or the relevant local people's government entrusted by the People's Bank of China shall organize the establishment of a liquidation team. The liquidation shall commence from the date when the decision of revocation takes effect. The liquidation team shall be responsible to the People's Bank of China and shall report on its work.  
The liquidation team shall be composed of representatives from the People's Bank of China, finance, audit, and other relevant departments, the local people's government, and representatives of the shareholders of the financial institution under revocation, as well as relevant professionals. The head and members of the liquidation team shall be designated by the People's Bank of China or be subject to its consent.  
During the liquidation period, the liquidation team shall exercise the management functions and powers of the financial institution under revocation, and the head of the liquidation team shall exercise the functions and powers of the legal representative of the financial institution under revocation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　清算组成立后，被撤销的金融机构的法定代表人及有关负责人应当将被撤销的金融机构的全部印章、账簿、单证、票据、文件、资料等移交清算组，并协助清算组进行清算。

**Article 9** After the establishment of the liquidation team, the legal representative and relevant responsible persons of the financial institution under revocation shall transfer all seals, account books, vouchers, instruments, documents, materials, etc., of the financial institution under revocation to the liquidation team, and shall assist the liquidation team in carrying out the liquidation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　清算期间，被撤销的金融机构的法定代表人、董事会和监事会成员、部门负责人以上高级管理人员、财务人员及其他有关人员，应当按照清算组的要求进行工作，不得擅离职守，不得自行出境。

**Article 10** During the liquidation period, the legal representative, members of the board of directors and board of supervisors, senior officers at or above the level of department head, financial personnel, and other relevant persons of the financial institution under revocation shall work in accordance with the requirements of the liquidation team, and shall not leave their posts without permission, nor leave the country without authorization.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　清算期间，清算组履行下列职责：  
(一)保管、清理被撤销的金融机构财产，编制资产负债表和财产清单；  
(二)通知、公告存款人及其他债权人，确认债权；  
(三)处理与清算被撤销的金融机构有关的未了结业务；  
(四)清理债权、债务，催收债权，处置资产；  
(五)制作清算方案，按照经批准的清算方案清偿债务；  
(六)清缴所欠税款；  
(七)处理被撤销的金融机构清偿债务后的剩余财产；  
(八)代表被撤销的金融机构参加诉讼、仲裁活动；  
(九)提请有关部门追究对金融机构被撤销负有直接责任的高级管理人员和其他有关人员的法律责任；  
(十)办理其他清算事务。

**Article 11** During the liquidation period, the liquidation team shall perform the following duties:  
(1) preserve and sort out the property of the financial institution under revocation, and prepare a balance sheet and a property inventory;  
(2) notify and announce to depositors and other creditors, and confirm claims;  
(3) handle the unfinished business related to the liquidation of the financial institution under revocation;  
(4) sort out claims and debts, collect claims, and dispose of assets;  
(5) formulate a liquidation plan, and repay debts in accordance with the approved liquidation plan;  
(6) pay off taxes owed;  
(7) handle the remaining property after the financial institution under revocation has repaid its debts;  
(8) represent the financial institution under revocation in litigation and arbitration activities;  
(9) request the relevant departments to investigate the legal liability of senior officers and other relevant persons who bear direct responsibility for the revocation of the financial institution;  
(10) handle other liquidation affairs.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　清算期间，清算组可以将清算事务委托中国人民银行指定的金融机构(以下简称托管机构)办理。  
托管机构不承担被撤销的金融机构债务，不垫付资金，不负责被撤销的金融机构人员安置。托管费用列入被撤销的金融机构清算费用。

**Article 12** During the liquidation period, the liquidation team may entrust the liquidation affairs to a financial institution designated by the People's Bank of China (hereinafter referred to as the custodian institution) for handling.  
The custodian institution shall not bear the debts of the financial institution under revocation, shall not advance funds, and shall not be responsible for the placement of personnel of the financial institution under revocation. The custodian fees shall be included in the liquidation expenses of the financial institution under revocation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　被撤销的金融机构所在地的地方人民政府应当成立撤销工作领导小组，组长由地方人民政府负责人担任。  
撤销工作领导小组应当支持、配合清算组催收债权和办理其他清算事务，并组织有关部门依法维护社会治安秩序，处理突发事件，查处违法行为，依法追究有关责任人员的法律责任。

**Article 13** The local people's government of the place where the financial institution under revocation is located shall establish a leading group for revocation work, with the head of the local people's government as its leader.  
The leading group for revocation work shall support and assist the liquidation team in collecting claims and handling other liquidation affairs, and shall organize relevant departments to maintain public security order according to law, handle emergencies, investigate and deal with illegal acts, and investigate the legal liability of relevant responsible persons according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　清算组应当自成立之日起10日内，书面通知债权人申报债权，并于60日内在报纸上至少公告3次。  
债权人应当自接到通知书之日起30日内，未接到通知书的债权人应当自第一次公告之日起90日内，向清算组申报债权。  
清算组可以决定小额储蓄存款人可以不申报债权，由清算组根据被撤销的金融机构会计账册和有关凭证，对储蓄存款予以确认和登记。

**Article 14** The liquidation team shall, within 10 days from the date of its establishment, notify creditors in writing to declare their claims, and shall, within 60 days, make at least 3 announcements in a newspaper.  
Creditors shall declare their claims to the liquidation team within 30 days from the date of receipt of the written notice, and creditors who have not received the written notice shall declare their claims within 90 days from the date of the first announcement.  
The liquidation team may decide that small-amount savings depositors are not required to declare their claims, and the liquidation team shall confirm and register the savings deposits based on the accounting books and relevant vouchers of the financial institution under revocation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　债权人申报债权，应当说明债权性质、数额和发生时间，并提供有关证明材料。清算组应当审查申报债权的证明材料，确认债权有无财产担保及数额，对有财产担保的债权和无财产担保的债权分别登记。

**Article 15** Creditors declaring claims shall state the nature, amount, and time of occurrence of the claims, and provide relevant evidentiary materials. The liquidation team shall examine the evidentiary materials of the declared claims, confirm whether the claims have property security and the amount thereof, and separately register the claims with property security and the claims without property security.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　债权人未在规定期限内申报债权的，按照下列规定处理：  
(一)已知债权人的债权，应当列入清算范围；  
(二)未知债权人的债权，在被撤销的金融机构的清算财产分配结束前，可以请求清偿；被撤销的金融机构的清算财产已经分配结束的，不再予以清偿。

**Article 16** Where a creditor fails to declare its claim within the prescribed time limit, it shall be dealt with according to the following provisions:  
(1) the claim of a known creditor shall be included in the scope of liquidation;  
(2) the claim of an unknown creditor may be requested for settlement before the distribution of the liquidation property of the financial institution under revocation is completed; where the distribution of liquidation property of the financial institution under revocation has been completed, settlement shall no longer be made.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　自撤销决定生效之日起，被撤销的金融机构债务停止计算利息。

**Article 17** From the date when the decision of revocation takes effect, the calculation of interest on the debts of the financial institution under revocation shall cease.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　被撤销的金融机构下列财产，作为清偿债务的清算财产：  
(一)清算开始之日起被撤销的金融机构全部财产，包括其股东的出资及其他权益、其全资子公司的财产和其投资入股的股份；  
(二)清算期间被撤销的金融机构依法取得的财产；  
(三)被撤销的金融机构的其他财产。  
撤销决定生效之日前，被撤销的金融机构恶意转移或者变相转移财产的行为无效；由此转移和变相转移的财产由清算组负责追回，并入清算财产。

**Article 18** The following property of a financial institution under revocation shall be the liquidation property for the repayment of debts:  
(1) all property of the financial institution under revocation from the date of commencement of liquidation, including the capital contributions and other rights and interests of its shareholders, the property of its wholly-owned subsidiaries, and the shares in which it has invested;  
(2) property lawfully obtained by the financial institution under revocation during the liquidation period;  
(3) other property of the financial institution under revocation.  
Any act of malicious transfer or disguised transfer of property by the financial institution under revocation before the date when the decision of revocation takes effect shall be void; the property so transferred or disguisedly transferred shall be recovered by the liquidation team and included in the liquidation property.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　清算组清理被撤销的金融机构财产时，应当依法评估其财产的实际价值；财产有损失的，应当核实损失数额。

**Article 19** When the liquidation team sorts out the property of the financial institution under revocation, it shall assess the actual value of the property according to law; where there is loss of property, it shall verify the amount of loss.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　清算组可以依法变卖被撤销的金融机构的有效资产；拍卖被撤销的金融机构有效资产的，应当按照具有资产评估业务资格的中介机构出具的评估结果确定拍卖底价。  
前款所称有效资产，是指被撤销的金融机构经清理、核实后具有实际价值的财产。

**Article 20** The liquidation team may sell the effective assets of the financial institution under revocation according to law; where the effective assets of the financial institution under revocation are auctioned, the reserve price for auction shall be determined in accordance with the appraisal results issued by an intermediary institution qualified for asset appraisal business.  
For the purposes of the preceding paragraph, "effective assets" means the property that has actual value after being sorted out and verified by the financial institution under revocation.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　被撤销的金融机构财产的清理和处置，免交税收和行政性收费。

**Article 21** The sorting out and disposal of the property of a financial institution under revocation shall be exempt from taxes and administrative charges.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　被撤销的金融机构财产经清理、核实后，清算组应当制作清算方案。  
清算方案应当包括债权人情况、债权数额、清算财产数额、支付个人储蓄存款的本金和合法利息的数额、清偿其他债务的数额等内容，并附资产负债表、财产清单、资产评估报告等材料。  
清算方案由清算组与债权人协商后，报中国人民银行确认。

**Article 22** After the property of the financial institution under revocation has been sorted out and verified, the liquidation team shall formulate a liquidation plan.  
The liquidation plan shall include the status of creditors, the amount of claims, the amount of liquidation property, the amount of principal and lawful interest to be paid on individual savings deposits, the amount to be used for repaying other debts, and other contents, and shall be accompanied by a balance sheet, property inventory, asset appraisal report, and other materials.  
The liquidation plan shall be submitted to the People's Bank of China for confirmation after consultation between the liquidation team and the creditors.

### 第四章　债务清偿

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　被撤销的金融机构清算财产，应当先支付个人储蓄存款的本金和合法利息。

**Article 23** The liquidation property of a financial institution under revocation shall first be used to pay the principal and lawful interest on individual savings deposits.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　被撤销的金融机构的清算财产支付个人储蓄存款的本金和合法利息后的剩余财产，应当清偿法人和其他组织的债务。

**Article 24** The remaining property after the payment of the principal and lawful interest on individual savings deposits from the liquidation property of a financial institution under revocation shall be used to repay the debts of legal persons and other organizations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　被撤销的金融机构的清算财产清偿债务后的剩余财产，经清算应当按照股东的出资比例或者持有的股份比例分配。

**Article 25** The remaining property after the liquidation property of a financial institution under revocation has repaid the debts shall, upon liquidation, be distributed in proportion to the capital contributions or the shares held by the shareholders.

### 第五章　注销登记

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　清算结束后，清算组应当制作清算报告、清算期内收支报表和各种财务账册，报中国人民银行确认。

**Article 26** After the conclusion of the liquidation, the liquidation team shall prepare a liquidation report, statements of income and expenditure during the liquidation period, and various financial books, and shall submit them to the People's Bank of China for confirmation.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　清算结束后，清算组应当向工商行政管理机关办理注销登记手续，被撤销的金融机构股东的资格终止，被撤销的金融机构即行解散，由中国人民银行予以公告。

**Article 27** After the conclusion of the liquidation, the liquidation team shall go through the formalities of cancellation of registration with the administrative authority for industry and commerce. The qualifications of the shareholders of the financial institution under revocation shall terminate, and the financial institution under revocation shall be dissolved immediately, and the People's Bank of China shall make an announcement.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　被撤销的金融机构的各种会计凭证、会计账册、会计报表等资料以及有关营业、清算的重要文件，应当在注销登记后由中国人民银行指定的机构负责保管。

**Article 28** Various accounting vouchers, accounting books, accounting statements, and other materials of the financial institution under revocation, as well as important documents related to its business and liquidation, shall be kept by an institution designated by the People's Bank of China after the cancellation of registration.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　审计机关应当对被撤销的金融机构负责人进行审计。

**Article 29** The audit authority shall audit the responsible persons of the financial institution under revocation.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　被撤销的金融机构的高级管理人员和其他有关人员，利用职务上的便利收受他人财物、违法发放贷款、非法出具金融票证、徇私舞弊造成该金融机构被撤销的，依照刑法关于受贿罪、违法发放贷款罪、非法出具金融票证罪、徇私舞弊造成破产、亏损罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分，并终身不得在任何金融机构担任高级管理职务或者与原职务相当的职务。

**Article 30** Where senior officers and other relevant persons of a financial institution under revocation, taking advantage of their positions, accept property from others, illegally grant loans, illegally issue financial instruments, or engage in malpractice for personal gain, thereby causing the revocation of the financial institution, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of accepting bribes, the crime of illegally granting loans, the crime of illegally issuing financial instruments, the crime of causing bankruptcy or losses through malpractice for personal gain, or other crimes; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, disciplinary sanctions of removal from office up to expulsion shall be imposed, and such persons shall be permanently prohibited from holding any senior management position in any financial institution or a position equivalent to the original position.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　中国人民银行的工作人员违法审批金融机构，对金融机构不依法实施监督管理、不依法查处违法行为，情节严重、导致金融机构被撤销的，依照刑法关于滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，给予记大过、降级或者撤职的行政处分。

**Article 31** Where staff members of the People's Bank of China illegally approve financial institutions, fail to exercise supervision and administration over financial institutions according to law, or fail to investigate and deal with illegal acts according to law, where the circumstances are serious and lead to the revocation of a financial institution, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty, or other crimes; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, administrative sanctions of recording a major demerit, demotion, or removal from office shall be imposed.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　任何国家机关工作人员非法干预金融机构的正常经营活动，对该金融机构被撤销负有直接责任的，依照刑法关于滥用职权罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，给予记大过、降级或者撤职的行政处分。

**Article 32** Where any state organ staff member illegally interferes with the normal business activities of a financial institution and bears direct responsibility for the revocation of the financial institution, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of abuse of power or other crimes; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, administrative sanctions of recording a major demerit, demotion, or removal from office shall be imposed.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　在撤销清算过程中，被撤销的金融机构工作人员有下列行为之一的，依照刑法关于妨害公务罪、妨害清算罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分：  
(一)阻挠清算组依法履行职责的；  
(二)拒绝提供情况或者提供虚假情况的；  
(三)抽逃资金、隐匿财产，逃避债务的；  
(四)恶意转移或者变相转移被撤销的金融机构财产的。

**Article 33** Where, during the process of revocation and liquidation, a staff member of the financial institution under revocation commits any of the following acts, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of obstructing public service, the crime of obstructing liquidation, or other crimes; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, disciplinary sanctions of removal from office up to expulsion shall be imposed:  
(1) obstructing the liquidation team from performing its duties according to law;  
(2) refusing to provide information or providing false information;  
(3) illegally withdrawing funds, concealing property, or evading debts;  
(4) maliciously transferring or disguisedly transferring the property of the financial institution under revocation.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　被撤销的金融机构在撤销决定生效后非法从事经营活动的，由中国人民银行依照《非法金融机构和非法金融业务活动取缔办法》予以取缔；依照刑法关于非法吸收公众存款罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予行政处罚。

**Article 34** Where a financial institution under revocation illegally engages in business activities after the decision of revocation takes effect, the People's Bank of China shall ban it in accordance with the Measures for Banning Illegal Financial Institutions and Illegal Financial Business Activities; criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegally absorbing public deposits or other crimes; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, administrative penalty shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　清算组的工作人员在清算过程中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，造成财产损失，损害债权人利益的，依照刑法关于滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，给予降级直至开除的行政处分或者纪律处分。

**Article 35** Where a staff member of the liquidation team, in the course of liquidation, abuses power, neglects duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, causing property losses and damaging the interests of creditors, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty, or other crimes; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions of demotion up to expulsion shall be imposed.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　中国人民银行工作人员及其他有关人员在依照本条例履行职责中，泄露国家秘密或者所知悉的商业秘密的，依照刑法关于泄露国家秘密罪、侵犯商业秘密罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，给予降级直至开除的行政处分或者纪律处分。

**Article 36** Where staff members of the People's Bank of China and other relevant persons, in the course of performing their duties in accordance with these Regulations, disclose State secrets or trade secrets known to them, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of disclosing State secrets, the crime of infringing upon trade secrets, or other crimes; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions of demotion up to expulsion shall be imposed.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　托管机构不履行托管职责，造成被撤销的金融机构财产损失的，应当依法承担民事责任，并对其负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予纪律处分。

**Article 37** Where a custodian institution fails to perform its custodial duties, causing property losses to the financial institution under revocation, it shall bear civil liability according to law, and disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed according to law on the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本条例自2001年12月15日起施行。

**Article 38** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 15, 2001.
