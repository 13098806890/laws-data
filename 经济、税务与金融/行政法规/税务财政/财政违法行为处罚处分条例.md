### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了纠正财政违法行为，维护国家财政经济秩序，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of rectifying illegal fiscal acts and maintaining the state's fiscal and economic order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　县级以上人民政府财政部门及审计机关在各自职权范围内，依法对财政违法行为作出处理、处罚决定。  
省级以上人民政府财政部门的派出机构，应当在规定职权范围内，依法对财政违法行为作出处理、处罚决定；审计机关的派出机构，应当根据审计机关的授权，依法对财政违法行为作出处理、处罚决定。  
根据需要，国务院可以依法调整财政部门及其派出机构(以下统称财政部门)、审计机关及其派出机构(以下统称审计机关)的职权范围。  
有财政违法行为的单位，其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，以及有财政违法行为的个人，属于国家公务员的，由监察机关及其派出机构(以下统称监察机关)或者任免机关依照人事管理权限，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 2** The finance departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and the audit organs shall, within their respective functions and powers, make decisions on handling and imposing penalties for illegal fiscal acts according to law.  
The dispatched agencies of the finance departments of the people's governments at or above the provincial level shall, within the prescribed functions and powers, make decisions on handling and imposing penalties for illegal fiscal acts according to law; the dispatched agencies of the audit organs shall, according to the authorization of the audit organs, make decisions on handling and imposing penalties for illegal fiscal acts according to law.  
When necessary, the State Council may, according to law, adjust the functions and powers of the finance departments and their dispatched agencies (hereinafter collectively referred to as the finance departments) and the audit organs and their dispatched agencies (hereinafter collectively referred to as the audit organs).  
Where a unit that has committed an illegal fiscal act, the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible, as well as an individual that has committed an illegal fiscal act, are State functionaries, the supervisory organs and their dispatched agencies (hereinafter collectively referred to as the supervisory organs) or the appointing and removing organs shall, according to law, impose administrative sanctions in accordance with the personnel management authority.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　财政收入执收单位及其工作人员有下列违反国家财政收入管理规定的行为之一的，责令改正，补收应当收取的财政收入，限期退还违法所得。对单位给予警告或者通报批评。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；情节严重的，给予降级或者撤职处分：  
(一)违反规定设立财政收入项目；  
(二)违反规定擅自改变财政收入项目的范围、标准、对象和期限；  
(三)对已明令取消、暂停执行或者降低标准的财政收入项目，仍然依照原定项目、标准征收或者变换名称征收；  
(四)缓收、不收财政收入；  
(五)擅自将预算收入转为预算外收入；  
(六)其他违反国家财政收入管理规定的行为。  
《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》等法律、行政法规另有规定的，依照其规定给予行政处分。

**Article 3** Where a unit responsible for collecting fiscal revenues or any of its staff members commits any of the following acts in violation of the state provisions on the administration of fiscal revenues, they shall be ordered to make corrections, collect the fiscal revenues that should have been collected, and return the illegal gains within a prescribed time limit. The unit shall be given a warning or a circulating notice of criticism. The person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a warning, a demerit record or a serious demerit record; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given a demotion or dismissal from post:  
(1) establishing items of fiscal revenues in violation of provisions;  
(2) arbitrarily changing the scope, standards, targets and time limits of items of fiscal revenues in violation of provisions;  
(3) continuing to levy according to the original items and standards or levying under changed names for items of fiscal revenues that have been explicitly abolished, suspended from implementation or reduced in standard;  
(4) deferring or not collecting fiscal revenues;  
(5) arbitrarily converting budgetary revenues into extra-budgetary revenues;  
(6) other acts in violation of the state provisions on the administration of fiscal revenues.  
Where laws and administrative regulations such as the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection provide otherwise, administrative sanctions shall be imposed in accordance therewith.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　财政收入执收单位及其工作人员有下列违反国家财政收入上缴规定的行为之一的，责令改正，调整有关会计账目，收缴应当上缴的财政收入，限期退还违法所得。对单位给予警告或者通报批评。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分：  
(一)隐瞒应当上缴的财政收入；  
(二)滞留、截留、挪用应当上缴的财政收入；  
(三)坐支应当上缴的财政收入；  
(四)不依照规定的财政收入预算级次、预算科目入库；  
(五)违反规定退付国库库款或者财政专户资金；  
(六)其他违反国家财政收入上缴规定的行为。  
《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》、《中华人民共和国预算法》等法律、行政法规另有规定的，依照其规定给予行政处分。

**Article 4** Where a unit responsible for collecting fiscal revenues or any of its staff members commits any of the following acts in violation of the state provisions on the turning over of fiscal revenues, they shall be ordered to make corrections, adjust the relevant accounting books and records, collect the fiscal revenues that should have been turned over, and return the illegal gains within a prescribed time limit. The unit shall be given a warning or a circulating notice of criticism. The person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a serious demerit record; if the circumstances are relatively serious, they shall be given a demotion or dismissal from post; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given discharge:  
(1) concealing fiscal revenues that should be turned over;  
(2) retaining, intercepting or misappropriating fiscal revenues that should be turned over;  
(3) using fiscal revenues that should be turned over without turning them over;  
(4) failing to turn over into the state treasury according to the prescribed budgetary levels and budgetary account items of fiscal revenues;  
(5) refunding state treasury funds or funds in fiscal accounts in violation of provisions;  
(6) other acts in violation of the state provisions on the turning over of fiscal revenues.  
Where laws and administrative regulations such as the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection and the Budget Law of the People's Republic of China provide otherwise, administrative sanctions shall be imposed in accordance therewith.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　财政部门、国库机构及其工作人员有下列违反国家有关上解、下拨财政资金规定的行为之一的，责令改正，限期退还违法所得。对单位给予警告或者通报批评。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记过或者记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分：  
(一)延解、占压应当上解的财政收入；  
(二)不依照预算或者用款计划核拨财政资金；  
(三)违反规定收纳、划分、留解、退付国库库款或者财政专户资金；  
(四)将应当纳入国库核算的财政收入放在财政专户核算；  
(五)擅自动用国库库款或者财政专户资金；  
(六)其他违反国家有关上解、下拨财政资金规定的行为。

**Article 5** Where the finance departments, state treasury organs or any of their staff members commit any of the following acts in violation of the state provisions on the upward remittance and downward allocation of fiscal funds, they shall be ordered to make corrections and return the illegal gains within a prescribed time limit. The unit shall be given a warning or a circulating notice of criticism. The person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a demerit record or a serious demerit record; if the circumstances are relatively serious, they shall be given a demotion or dismissal from post; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given discharge:  
(1) delaying or occupying the upward remittance of fiscal revenues;  
(2) failing to allocate fiscal funds in accordance with the budget or the plan for use of funds;  
(3) receiving, dividing, retaining, refunding state treasury funds or funds in fiscal accounts in violation of provisions;  
(4) accounting for fiscal revenues that should be accounted for in the state treasury in fiscal accounts;  
(5) arbitrarily using state treasury funds or funds in fiscal accounts;  
(6) other acts in violation of the state provisions on the upward remittance and downward allocation of fiscal funds.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家机关及其工作人员有下列违反规定使用、骗取财政资金的行为之一的，责令改正，调整有关会计账目，追回有关财政资金，限期退还违法所得。对单位给予警告或者通报批评。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分：  
(一)以虚报、冒领等手段骗取财政资金；  
(二)截留、挪用财政资金；  
(三)滞留应当下拨的财政资金；  
(四)违反规定扩大开支范围，提高开支标准；  
(五)其他违反规定使用、骗取财政资金的行为。

**Article 6** Where any state organ or any of its staff members commits any of the following acts in violation of provisions on the use or obtaining by fraud of fiscal funds, they shall be ordered to make corrections, adjust the relevant accounting books and records, recover the relevant fiscal funds, and return the illegal gains within a prescribed time limit. The unit shall be given a warning or a circulating notice of criticism. The person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a serious demerit record; if the circumstances are relatively serious, they shall be given a demotion or dismissal from post; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given discharge:  
(1) obtaining fiscal funds by fraud through means such as false reporting or false claiming;  
(2) intercepting or misappropriating fiscal funds;  
(3) retaining fiscal funds that should be allocated;  
(4) expanding the scope of expenditure or raising the standards of expenditure in violation of provisions;  
(5) other acts in violation of provisions on the use or obtaining by fraud of fiscal funds.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　财政预决算的编制部门和预算执行部门及其工作人员有下列违反国家有关预算管理规定的行为之一的，责令改正，追回有关款项，限期调整有关预算科目和预算级次。对单位给予警告或者通报批评。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级处分；情节严重的，给予撤职处分：  
(一)虚增、虚减财政收入或者财政支出；  
(二)违反规定编制、批复预算或者决算；  
(三)违反规定调整预算；  
(四)违反规定调整预算级次或者预算收支种类；  
(五)违反规定动用预算预备费或者挪用预算周转金；  
(六)违反国家关于转移支付管理规定的行为；  
(七)其他违反国家有关预算管理规定的行为。

**Article 7** Where the departments responsible for compiling fiscal budgets and final accounts and the departments responsible for budget execution or any of their staff members commit any of the following acts in violation of the state provisions on budget administration, they shall be ordered to make corrections, recover the relevant funds, and adjust the relevant budget account items and budget levels within a prescribed time limit. The unit shall be given a warning or a circulating notice of criticism. The person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a warning, a demerit record or a serious demerit record; if the circumstances are relatively serious, they shall be given a demotion; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given dismissal from post:  
(1) falsely increasing or falsely decreasing fiscal revenues or fiscal expenditures;  
(2) compiling, approving budgets or final accounts in violation of provisions;  
(3) adjusting budgets in violation of provisions;  
(4) adjusting budget levels or categories of budget revenues and expenditures in violation of provisions;  
(5) using budget reserve funds or misappropriating budget revolving funds in violation of provisions;  
(6) acts in violation of the state provisions on the administration of transfer payments;  
(7) other acts in violation of the state provisions on budget administration.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家机关及其工作人员违反国有资产管理的规定，擅自占有、使用、处置国有资产的，责令改正，调整有关会计账目，限期退还违法所得和被侵占的国有资产。对单位给予警告或者通报批评。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分。

**Article 8** Where any state organ or any of its staff members, in violation of the provisions on the administration of state-owned assets, arbitrarily takes possession, uses or disposes of state-owned assets, they shall be ordered to make corrections, adjust the relevant accounting books and records, and return the illegal gains and the occupied state-owned assets within a prescribed time limit. The unit shall be given a warning or a circulating notice of criticism. The person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a serious demerit record; if the circumstances are relatively serious, they shall be given a demotion or dismissal from post; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given discharge.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　单位和个人有下列违反国家有关投资建设项目规定的行为之一的，责令改正，调整有关会计账目，追回被截留、挪用、骗取的国家建设资金，没收违法所得，核减或者停止拨付工程投资。对单位给予警告或者通报批评，其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员属于国家公务员的，给予记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分：  
(一)截留、挪用国家建设资金；  
(二)以虚报、冒领、关联交易等手段骗取国家建设资金；  
(三)违反规定超概算投资；  
(四)虚列投资完成额；  
(五)其他违反国家投资建设项目有关规定的行为。  
《中华人民共和国政府采购法》、《中华人民共和国招标投标法》、《国家重点建设项目管理办法》等法律、行政法规另有规定的，依照其规定处理、处罚。

**Article 9** Where any unit or individual commits any of the following acts in violation of the state provisions on investment construction projects, they shall be ordered to make corrections, adjust the relevant accounting books and records, recover the intercepted, misappropriated or fraudulently obtained state construction funds, confiscate the illegal gains, and reduce or stop the allocation of project investment. The unit shall be given a warning or a circulating notice of criticism; if the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible are State functionaries, they shall be given a serious demerit record; if the circumstances are relatively serious, they shall be given a demotion or dismissal from post; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given discharge:  
(1) intercepting or misappropriating state construction funds;  
(2) obtaining state construction funds by fraud through means such as false reporting, false claiming or connected transactions;  
(3) investing in excess of the budget estimate in violation of provisions;  
(4) falsely listing the amount of completed investment;  
(5) other acts in violation of the relevant provisions on state investment construction projects.  
Where laws and administrative regulations such as the Government Procurement Law of the People's Republic of China, the Tendering and Bidding Law of the People's Republic of China and the Measures for the Administration of Key State Construction Projects provide otherwise, handling and penalties shall be imposed in accordance therewith.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家机关及其工作人员违反《中华人民共和国担保法》及国家有关规定，擅自提供担保的，责令改正，没收违法所得。对单位给予警告或者通报批评。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；造成损失的，给予降级或者撤职处分；造成重大损失的，给予开除处分。

**Article 10** Where any state organ or any of its staff members, in violation of the Guarantee Law of the People's Republic of China and the relevant state provisions, arbitrarily provides a guarantee, they shall be ordered to make corrections and the illegal gains shall be confiscated. The unit shall be given a warning or a circulating notice of criticism. The person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a warning, a demerit record or a serious demerit record; if losses are caused, they shall be given a demotion or dismissal from post; if heavy losses are caused, they shall be given discharge.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家机关及其工作人员违反国家有关账户管理规定，擅自在金融机构开立、使用账户的，责令改正，调整有关会计账目，追回有关财政资金，没收违法所得，依法撤销擅自开立的账户。对单位给予警告或者通报批评。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予降级处分；情节严重的，给予撤职或者开除处分。

**Article 11** Where any state organ or any of its staff members, in violation of the state provisions on the administration of accounts, arbitrarily opens or uses an account in a financial institution, they shall be ordered to make corrections, adjust the relevant accounting books and records, recover the relevant fiscal funds, confiscate the illegal gains, and revoke the arbitrarily opened account according to law. The unit shall be given a warning or a circulating notice of criticism. The person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a demotion; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given dismissal from post or discharge.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家机关及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，责令改正，调整有关会计账目，追回被挪用、骗取的有关资金，没收违法所得。对单位给予警告或者通报批评。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予降级处分；情节较重的，给予撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分：  
(一)以虚报、冒领等手段骗取政府承贷或者担保的外国政府贷款、国际金融组织贷款；  
(二)滞留政府承贷或者担保的外国政府贷款、国际金融组织贷款；  
(三)截留、挪用政府承贷或者担保的外国政府贷款、国际金融组织贷款；  
(四)其他违反规定使用、骗取政府承贷或者担保的外国政府贷款、国际金融组织贷款的行为。

**Article 12** Where any state organ or any of its staff members commits any of the following acts, they shall be ordered to make corrections, adjust the relevant accounting books and records, recover the misappropriated or fraudulently obtained relevant funds, and confiscate the illegal gains. The unit shall be given a warning or a circulating notice of criticism. The person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a demotion; if the circumstances are relatively serious, they shall be given dismissal from post; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given discharge:  
(1) obtaining by fraud loans from foreign governments or international financial organizations that are borrowed or guaranteed by the government through means such as false reporting or false claiming;  
(2) retaining loans from foreign governments or international financial organizations that are borrowed or guaranteed by the government;  
(3) intercepting or misappropriating loans from foreign governments or international financial organizations that are borrowed or guaranteed by the government;  
(4) other acts in violation of provisions on the use or obtaining by fraud of loans from foreign governments or international financial organizations that are borrowed or guaranteed by the government.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　企业和个人有下列不缴或者少缴财政收入行为之一的，责令改正，调整有关会计账目，收缴应当上缴的财政收入，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处不缴或者少缴财政收入10%以上30%以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处3000元以上5万元以下的罚款：  
(一)隐瞒应当上缴的财政收入；  
(二)截留代收的财政收入；  
(三)其他不缴或者少缴财政收入的行为。  
属于税收方面的违法行为，依照有关税收法律、行政法规的规定处理、处罚。

**Article 13** Where any enterprise or individual commits any of the following acts of non-payment or under-payment of fiscal revenues, they shall be ordered to make corrections, adjust the relevant accounting books and records, collect the fiscal revenues that should have been turned over, give a warning, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 10% but not more than 30% of the amount of non-payment or under-payment of fiscal revenues; the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be fined not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan:  
(1) concealing fiscal revenues that should be turned over;  
(2) intercepting fiscal revenues collected on behalf of others;  
(3) other acts of non-payment or under-payment of fiscal revenues.  
Where the illegal act pertains to taxation, it shall be handled and penalized in accordance with the relevant tax laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　企业和个人有下列行为之一的，责令改正，调整有关会计账目，追回违反规定使用、骗取的有关资金，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处被骗取有关资金10%以上50%以下的罚款或者被违规使用有关资金10%以上30%以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处3000元以上5万元以下的罚款：  
(一)以虚报、冒领等手段骗取财政资金以及政府承贷或者担保的外国政府贷款、国际金融组织贷款；  
(二)挪用财政资金以及政府承贷或者担保的外国政府贷款、国际金融组织贷款；  
(三)从无偿使用的财政资金以及政府承贷或者担保的外国政府贷款、国际金融组织贷款中非法获益；  
(四)其他违反规定使用、骗取财政资金以及政府承贷或者担保的外国政府贷款、国际金融组织贷款的行为。  
属于政府采购方面的违法行为，依照《中华人民共和国政府采购法》及有关法律、行政法规的规定处理、处罚。

**Article 14** Where any enterprise or individual commits any of the following acts, they shall be ordered to make corrections, adjust the relevant accounting books and records, recover the relevant funds used in violation of provisions or obtained by fraud, give a warning, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 10% but not more than 50% of the amount of funds obtained by fraud or a fine of not less than 10% but not more than 30% of the amount of funds used in violation of provisions; the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be fined not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan:  
(1) obtaining by fraud fiscal funds or loans from foreign governments or international financial organizations that are borrowed or guaranteed by the government through means such as false reporting or false claiming;  
(2) misappropriating fiscal funds or loans from foreign governments or international financial organizations that are borrowed or guaranteed by the government;  
(3) illegally benefiting from fiscal funds used without compensation or loans from foreign governments or international financial organizations that are borrowed or guaranteed by the government;  
(4) other acts in violation of provisions on the use or obtaining by fraud of fiscal funds or loans from foreign governments or international financial organizations that are borrowed or guaranteed by the government.  
Where the illegal act pertains to government procurement, it shall be handled and penalized in accordance with the Government Procurement Law of the People's Republic of China and the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　事业单位、社会团体、其他社会组织及其工作人员有财政违法行为的，依照本条例有关国家机关的规定执行；但其在经营活动中的财政违法行为，依照[本条例第十三条](#art-13)、第十四条的规定执行。

**Article 15** Where any institution, social organization, other social organization or any of their staff members commits an illegal fiscal act, the provisions of these Regulations concerning state organs shall apply; however, if such illegal fiscal act occurs in business operations, the provisions of Articles 13 and 14 of these Regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　单位和个人有下列违反财政收入票据管理规定的行为之一的，销毁非法印制的票据，没收违法所得和作案工具。对单位处5000元以上10万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处3000元以上5万元以下的罚款。属于国家公务员的，还应当给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分：  
(一)违反规定印制财政收入票据；  
(二)转借、串用、代开财政收入票据；  
(三)伪造、变造、买卖、擅自销毁财政收入票据；  
(四)伪造、使用伪造的财政收入票据监(印)制章；  
(五)其他违反财政收入票据管理规定的行为。  
属于税收收入票据管理方面的违法行为，依照有关税收法律、行政法规的规定处理、处罚。

**Article 16** Where any unit or individual commits any of the following acts in violation of the provisions on the administration of fiscal revenue invoices, the illegally printed invoices shall be destroyed, and the illegal gains and tools used for the commission of the act shall be confiscated. The unit shall be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be fined not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan. If they are State functionaries, they shall also be given a demotion or dismissal from post; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given discharge:  
(1) printing fiscal revenue invoices in violation of provisions;  
(2) transferring, using interchangeably, or issuing on behalf of others fiscal revenue invoices;  
(3) forging, altering, buying and selling, or arbitrarily destroying fiscal revenue invoices;  
(4) forging or using forged supervision (printing) seals for fiscal revenue invoices;  
(5) other acts in violation of the provisions on the administration of fiscal revenue invoices.  
Where the illegal act pertains to the administration of tax revenue invoices, it shall be handled and penalized in accordance with the relevant tax laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　单位和个人违反财务管理的规定，私存私放财政资金或者其他公款的，责令改正，调整有关会计账目，追回私存私放的资金，没收违法所得。对单位处3000元以上5万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处2000元以上2万元以下的罚款。属于国家公务员的，还应当给予记大过处分；情节严重的，给予降级或者撤职处分。

**Article 17** Where any unit or individual, in violation of the provisions on financial management, keeps fiscal funds or other public funds in private deposits or private placements, they shall be ordered to make corrections, adjust the relevant accounting books and records, recover the funds kept in private deposits or private placements, and confiscate the illegal gains. The unit shall be fined not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be fined not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan. If they are State functionaries, they shall also be given a serious demerit record; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given a demotion or dismissal from post.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　属于会计方面的违法行为，依照会计方面的法律、行政法规的规定处理、处罚。对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，属于国家公务员的，还应当给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分。

**Article 18** Where the illegal act pertains to accounting, it shall be handled and penalized in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations on accounting. If the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible are State functionaries, they shall also be given a warning, a demerit record or a serious demerit record; if the circumstances are relatively serious, they shall be given a demotion or dismissal from post; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given discharge.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　属于行政性收费方面的违法行为，《中华人民共和国行政许可法》、《违反行政事业性收费和罚没收入收支两条线管理规定行政处分暂行规定》等法律、行政法规及国务院另有规定的，有关部门依照其规定处理、处罚、处分。

**Article 19** Where the illegal act pertains to administrative charges, if laws and administrative regulations such as the Administrative License Law of the People's Republic of China and the Interim Provisions on Administrative Sanctions for Violations of the Management Rules on the Separation of Revenue and Expenditure of Administrative and Institutional Charges and Fines and Confiscated Properties, as well as the State Council, provide otherwise, the relevant departments shall handle, penalize and sanction in accordance therewith.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　单位和个人有本条例规定的财政违法行为，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 20** Where any unit or individual commits an illegal fiscal act as provided for in these Regulations, which constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　财政部门、审计机关、监察机关依法进行调查或者检查时，被调查、检查的单位和个人应当予以配合，如实反映情况，不得拒绝、阻挠、拖延。  
违反前款规定的，责令限期改正。逾期不改正的，对属于国家公务员的直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；情节严重的，给予降级或者撤职处分。

**Article 21** When the finance departments, audit organs and supervisory organs conduct investigations or inspections according to law, the units and individuals under investigation or inspection shall cooperate, truthfully report the situation, and shall not refuse, obstruct or delay.  
In case of violation of the preceding paragraph, the violator shall be ordered to make corrections within a prescribed time limit. If corrections are not made within the time limit, the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible who are State functionaries shall be given a warning, a demerit record or a serious demerit record; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given a demotion or dismissal from post.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　财政部门、审计机关、监察机关依法进行调查或者检查时，经县级以上人民政府财政部门、审计机关、监察机关的负责人批准，可以向与被调查、检查单位有经济业务往来的单位查询有关情况，可以向金融机构查询被调查、检查单位的存款，有关单位和金融机构应当配合。  
财政部门、审计机关、监察机关在依法进行调查或者检查时，执法人员不得少于2人，并应当向当事人或者有关人员出示证件；查询存款时，还应当持有县级以上人民政府财政部门、审计机关、监察机关签发的查询存款通知书，并负有保密义务。

**Article 22** When the finance departments, audit organs and supervisory organs conduct investigations or inspections according to law, with the approval of the persons in charge of the finance departments, audit organs or supervisory organs of the people's governments at or above the county level, they may inquire about the relevant information from units that have economic transactions with the unit under investigation or inspection, and may inquire about the deposits of the unit under investigation or inspection from financial institutions; the relevant units and financial institutions shall cooperate.  
When the finance departments, audit organs and supervisory organs conduct investigations or inspections according to law, there shall be no fewer than two law enforcement officers, and they shall present their identification documents to the party or the relevant personnel; when inquiring about deposits, they shall also hold a notice of inquiry about deposits issued by the finance department, audit organ or supervisory organ of the people's government at or above the county level, and shall have the obligation to maintain confidentiality.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　财政部门、审计机关、监察机关依法进行调查或者检查时，在有关证据可能灭失或者以后难以取得的情况下，经县级以上人民政府财政部门、审计机关、监察机关的负责人批准，可以先行登记保存，并应当在7日内及时作出处理决定。在此期间，当事人或者有关人员不得销毁或者转移证据。

**Article 23** When the finance departments, audit organs and supervisory organs conduct investigations or inspections according to law, if the relevant evidence may be destroyed or it may be difficult to obtain it later, with the approval of the persons in charge of the finance departments, audit organs or supervisory organs of the people's governments at or above the county level, they may register and preserve the evidence in advance, and shall promptly make a handling decision within seven days. During this period, the party or the relevant personnel shall not destroy or transfer the evidence.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对被调查、检查单位或者个人正在进行的财政违法行为，财政部门、审计机关应当责令停止。拒不执行的，财政部门可以暂停财政拨款或者停止拨付与财政违法行为直接有关的款项，已经拨付的，责令其暂停使用；审计机关可以通知财政部门或者其他有关主管部门暂停财政拨款或者停止拨付与财政违法行为直接有关的款项，已经拨付的，责令其暂停使用，财政部门和其他有关主管部门应当将结果书面告知审计机关。

**Article 24** Where a unit or individual under investigation or inspection is committing an illegal fiscal act, the finance departments and audit organs shall order it to be stopped. If the order is not complied with, the finance departments may suspend fiscal appropriations or stop disbursing the funds directly related to the illegal fiscal act; if the funds have already been disbursed, the finance departments shall order the suspension of their use; the audit organs may notify the finance departments or other competent departments to suspend fiscal appropriations or stop disbursing the funds directly related to the illegal fiscal act; if the funds have already been disbursed, the audit organs shall order the suspension of their use; the finance departments and other competent departments shall inform the audit organs in writing of the results.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　依照本条例规定限期退还的违法所得，到期无法退还的，应当收缴国库。

**Article 25** Where illegal gains are required to be returned within a prescribed time limit in accordance with these Regulations, and if they cannot be returned upon expiration of the time limit, they shall be turned over to the state treasury.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　单位和个人有本条例所列财政违法行为，财政部门、审计机关、监察机关可以公告其财政违法行为及处理、处罚、处分决定。

**Article 26** Where any unit or individual commits an illegal fiscal act listed in these Regulations, the finance departments, audit organs and supervisory organs may make public the illegal fiscal act and the decisions on handling, penalties and sanctions.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　单位和个人有本条例所列财政违法行为，弄虚作假骗取荣誉称号及其他有关奖励的，应当撤销其荣誉称号并收回有关奖励。

**Article 27** Where any unit or individual commits an illegal fiscal act listed in these Regulations and obtains honorary titles or other relevant rewards by fraud through deception, such honorary titles shall be revoked and the relevant rewards shall be recovered.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　财政部门、审计机关、监察机关的工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分。构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** Where any staff member of the finance departments, audit organs or supervisory organs abuses his power, neglects his duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, he shall be given a warning, a demerit record or a serious demerit record; if the circumstances are relatively serious, he shall be given a demotion or dismissal from post; if the circumstances are serious, he shall be given discharge. If a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　财政部门、审计机关、监察机关及其他有关监督检查机关对有关单位或者个人依法进行调查、检查后，应当出具调查、检查结论。有关监督检查机关已经作出的调查、检查结论能够满足其他监督检查机关履行本机关职责需要的，其他监督检查机关应当加以利用。

**Article 29** After the finance departments, audit organs, supervisory organs and other relevant supervisory inspection organs have conducted investigations or inspections on the relevant units or individuals according to law, they shall issue investigation or inspection conclusions. If the investigation or inspection conclusions already made by the relevant supervisory inspection organs can meet the needs of other supervisory inspection organs in performing their duties, such other supervisory inspection organs shall make use of them.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　财政部门、审计机关、监察机关及其他有关机关应当加强配合，对不属于其职权范围的事项，应当依法移送。受移送机关应当及时处理，并将结果书面告知移送机关。

**Article 30** The finance departments, audit organs, supervisory organs and other relevant organs shall strengthen coordination, and if a matter is not within their functions and powers, they shall transfer it to the competent organ according to law. The organ receiving the transfer shall handle the matter promptly and inform the transferring organ in writing of the results.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对财政违法行为作出处理、处罚和处分决定的程序，依照本条例和《中华人民共和国行政处罚法》、《中华人民共和国行政监察法》等有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 31** The procedures for making decisions on handling, penalties and sanctions for illegal fiscal acts shall be implemented in accordance with these Regulations and the relevant laws and administrative regulations such as the Administrative Penalty Law of the People's Republic of China and the Administrative Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　单位和个人对处理、处罚不服的，依照《中华人民共和国行政复议法》、《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》的规定申请复议或者提起诉讼。  
国家公务员对行政处分不服的，依照《中华人民共和国行政监察法》、《中华人民共和国公务员法》等法律、行政法规的规定提出申诉。

**Article 32** Where any unit or individual is dissatisfied with the handling or penalty, it may apply for reconsideration or file a lawsuit in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative Reconsideration Law of the People's Republic of China and the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where a State functionary is dissatisfied with the administrative sanction, he may file a petition in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations such as the Administrative Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China and the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　本条例所称“财政收入执收单位”，是指负责收取税收收入和各种非税收入的单位。

**Article 33** For the purpose of these Regulations, the term "unit responsible for collecting fiscal revenues" means a unit responsible for collecting tax revenues and various non-tax revenues.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　对法律、法规授权的具有管理公共事务职能的组织以及国家行政机关依法委托的组织及其工勤人员以外的工作人员，企业、事业单位、社会团体中由国家行政机关以委任、派遣等形式任命的人员以及其他人员有本条例规定的财政违法行为，需要给予处分的，参照本条例有关规定执行。

**Article 34** Where an organization authorized by laws or regulations to have the function of managing public affairs, or an organization entrusted by an administrative organ according to law and its staff other than service workers, or persons appointed by an administrative organ in the form of appointment or dispatch in enterprises, institutions or social organizations, or other persons, commit an illegal fiscal act as provided for in these Regulations and need to be given a sanction, the relevant provisions of these Regulations shall be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本条例自2005年2月1日起施行。1987年6月16日国务院发布的《国务院关于违反财政法规处罚的暂行规定》同时废止。

**Article 35** These Regulations shall come into force as of February 1, 2005. The Interim Provisions of the State Council on Punishment for Violations of Fiscal Laws and Regulations promulgated by the State Council on June 16, 1987 shall be abolished concurrently.
