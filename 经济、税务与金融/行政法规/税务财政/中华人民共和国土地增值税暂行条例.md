# 中华人民共和国土地增值税暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：1994-01-01

> (1993年12月13日中华人民共和国国务院令第138号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了规范土地、房地产市场交易秩序，合理调节土地增值收益，维护国家权益，制定本条例。 第二条　转让国有土地使用权、地上的建筑物及其附着物(以下简称转让房地产)并取得收入的单位和个人，为土地增值税的纳税义务人(以下简称纳税人)，应当依照本条例缴纳土地增值税。 第三条　土地增值税按照纳税人转让房地产所取得的增值额和本条例第七条规定的税率计算征收。 第四条　纳税人转让房地产所取得的收入减除本条例第六条规定扣除项目金额后的余额，为增值额。 第五条　纳税人转让房地产所取得的收入，包括货币收入、实物收入和其他收入。 第六条　计算增值额的扣除项目： (一)取得土地使用权所支付的金额； (二)开发土地的成本、费用； (三)新建房及配套设施的成本、费用，或者旧房及建筑物的评估价格； (四)与转让房地产有关的税金； (五)财政部规定的其他扣除项目。 第七条　土地增值税实行四级超率累进税率： 增值额未超过扣除项目金额50%的部分，税率为30%。 增值额超过扣除项目金额50%、未超过扣除项目金额100%的部分，税率为40%。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范土地、房地产市场交易秩序，合理调节土地增值收益，维护国家权益，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of regulating the order of land and real estate market transactions, rationally adjusting land value-added income, and safeguarding national rights and interests.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　转让国有土地使用权、地上的建筑物及其附着物(以下简称转让房地产)并取得收入的单位和个人，为土地增值税的纳税义务人(以下简称纳税人)，应当依照本条例缴纳土地增值税。

**Article 2** Units and individuals that transfer the right to use State-owned land, buildings on the land and their attachments (hereinafter referred to as transfer of real estate) and obtain income shall be the taxpayers of land value-added tax (hereinafter referred to as taxpayers) and shall pay land value-added tax in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　土地增值税按照纳税人转让房地产所取得的增值额和[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定的税率计算征收。

**Article 3** Land value-added tax shall be calculated and collected at the tax rates specified in Article 7 of these Regulations on the basis of the appreciation amount derived by the taxpayer from the transfer of real estate.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　纳税人转让房地产所取得的收入减除[本条例第六条](#art-6)规定扣除项目金额后的余额，为增值额。

**Article 4** The balance after deducting the amounts of deductible items specified in Article 6 of these Regulations from the income derived by the taxpayer from the transfer of real estate shall be the appreciation amount.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　纳税人转让房地产所取得的收入，包括货币收入、实物收入和其他收入。

**Article 5** The income derived by the taxpayer from the transfer of real estate shall include monetary income, income in kind, and other income.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　计算增值额的扣除项目：  
(一)取得土地使用权所支付的金额；  
(二)开发土地的成本、费用；  
(三)新建房及配套设施的成本、费用，或者旧房及建筑物的评估价格；  
(四)与转让房地产有关的税金；  
(五)财政部规定的其他扣除项目。

**Article 6** The deductible items for calculating the appreciation amount shall be:  
(1) the amount paid for obtaining the right to use land;  
(2) the costs and expenses of developing land;  
(3) the costs and expenses of new buildings and supporting facilities, or the assessed prices of old buildings and structures;  
(4) taxes related to the transfer of real estate;  
(5) other deductible items as prescribed by the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　土地增值税实行四级超率累进税率：  
增值额未超过扣除项目金额50%的部分，税率为30%。  
增值额超过扣除项目金额50%、未超过扣除项目金额100%的部分，税率为40%。  
增值额超过扣除项目金额100%、未超过扣除项目金额200%的部分，税率为50%。  
增值额超过扣除项目金额200%的部分，税率为60%。

**Article 7** Land value-added tax shall be subject to four progressive tax rates based on the ratio of appreciation amount to deductible items:  
where the appreciation amount does not exceed 50% of the total amount of deductible items, the tax rate shall be 30%;  
where the appreciation amount exceeds 50% but does not exceed 100% of the total amount of deductible items, the tax rate shall be 40%;  
where the appreciation amount exceeds 100% but does not exceed 200% of the total amount of deductible items, the tax rate shall be 50%;  
where the appreciation amount exceeds 200% of the total amount of deductible items, the tax rate shall be 60%.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　有下列情形之一的，免征土地增值税：  
(一)纳税人建造普通标准住宅出售，增值额未超过扣除项目金额20%的；  
(二)因国家建设需要依法征收、收回的房地产。

**Article 8** Land value-added tax shall be exempted in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) where the taxpayer constructs ordinary standard residential buildings for sale and the appreciation amount does not exceed 20% of the total amount of deductible items;  
(2) where the real estate is expropriated or taken over according to law due to the needs of national construction.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　纳税人有下列情形之一的，按照房地产评估价格计算征收：  
(一)隐瞒、虚报房地产成交价格的；  
(二)提供扣除项目金额不实的；  
(三)转让房地产的成交价格低于房地产评估价格，又无正当理由的。

**Article 9** In any of the following situations, the tax shall be calculated and collected on the basis of the assessed price of the real estate:  
(1) the taxpayer conceals or makes a false report on the transaction price of the real estate;  
(2) the taxpayer provides false information on the amounts of deductible items;  
(3) the transaction price of the transferred real estate is lower than the assessed price of the real estate without justifiable reasons.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　纳税人应当自转让房地产合同签订之日起7日内向房地产所在地主管税务机关办理纳税申报，并在税务机关核定的期限内缴纳土地增值税。

**Article 10** The taxpayer shall, within 7 days from the date of signing the real estate transfer contract, file a tax return with the competent tax authority of the locality where the real estate is located, and shall pay the land value-added tax within the time limit approved by the tax authority.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　土地增值税由税务机关征收。土地管理部门、房产管理部门应当向税务机关提供有关资料，并协助税务机关依法征收土地增值税。

**Article 11** Land value-added tax shall be collected by the tax authorities. The land administration departments and the real estate administration departments shall provide the relevant information to the tax authorities and assist the tax authorities in collecting land value-added tax according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　纳税人未按照本条例缴纳土地增值税的，土地管理部门、房产管理部门不得办理有关的权属变更手续。

**Article 12** Where the taxpayer fails to pay the land value-added tax in accordance with these Regulations, the land administration departments and the real estate administration departments shall not handle the relevant procedures for change of ownership.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　土地增值税的征收管理，依据《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》及本条例有关规定执行。

**Article 13** The collection and management of land value-added tax shall be carried out pursuant to the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection and the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本条例由财政部负责解释，实施细则由财政部制定。

**Article 14** The Ministry of Finance shall be responsible for the interpretation of these Regulations, and the Ministry of Finance shall formulate the implementation rules.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　本条例自1994年1月1日起施行。各地区的土地增值费征收办法，与本条例相抵触的，同时停止执行。

**Article 15** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 1994. The measures for the collection of land value-added fees in various regions that conflict with these Regulations shall cease to be implemented at the same time.
