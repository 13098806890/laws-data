# 中华人民共和国统计法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2017-05-28  
**生效日期**：2017-08-01

> (2017年4月12日国务院第168次常务会议通过　2017年5月28日中华人民共和国国务院令第681号公布　自2017年8月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国统计法》(以下简称统计法)，制定本条例。 第二条　统计资料能够通过行政记录取得的，不得组织实施调查。通过抽样调查、重点调查能够满足统计需要的，不得组织实施全面调查。 第三条　县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门应当加强统计规律研究，健全新兴产业等统计，完善经济、社会、科技、资源和环境统计，推进互联网、大数据、云计算等现代信息技术在统计工作中的应用，满足经济社会发展需要。 第四条　地方人民政府、县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门应当根据国家有关规定，明确本单位防范和惩治统计造假、弄虚作假的责任主体，严格执行统计法和本条例的规定。 地方人民政府、县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门及其负责人应当保障统计活动依法进行，不得侵犯统计机构、统计人员独立行使统计调查、统计报告、统计监督职权，不得非法干预统计调查对象提供统计资料，不得统计造假、弄虚作假。 统计调查对象应当依照统计法和国家有关规定，真实、准确、完整、及时地提供统计资料，拒绝、抵制弄虚作假等违法行为。 第五条　县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门不得组织实施营利性统计调查。 国家有计划地推进县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门通过向社会购买服务组织实施统计调查和资料开发。 第二章　统计调查项目 第六条　部门统计调查项目、地方统计调查项目的主要内容不得与国家统计调查项目的内容重复、矛盾。 第七条　统计调查项目的制定机关(以下简称制定机关)应当就项目的必要性、可行性、科学性进行论证，征求有关地方、部门、统计调查对象和专家的意见，并由制定机关按照会议制度集体讨论决定。 重要统计调查项目应当进行试点。 第八条　制定机关申请审批统计调查项目，应当以公文形式向审批机关提交统计调查项目审批申请表、项目的统计调查制度和工作经费来源说明。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国统计法》(以下简称统计法)，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Statistics Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　统计资料能够通过行政记录取得的，不得组织实施调查。通过抽样调查、重点调查能够满足统计需要的，不得组织实施全面调查。

**Article 2** If statistical data can be obtained through administrative records, no survey shall be organized and implemented. If statistical needs can be satisfied through sample surveys or key surveys, no comprehensive survey shall be organized and implemented.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门应当加强统计规律研究，健全新兴产业等统计，完善经济、社会、科技、资源和环境统计，推进互联网、大数据、云计算等现代信息技术在统计工作中的应用，满足经济社会发展需要。

**Article 3** The statistical institutions and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen research on statistical rules, improve statistics on emerging industries, etc., perfect statistics on economy, society, science and technology, resources and environment, and promote the application of modern information technologies such as the Internet, big data, and cloud computing in statistical work, so as to meet the needs of economic and social development.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　地方人民政府、县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门应当根据国家有关规定，明确本单位防范和惩治统计造假、弄虚作假的责任主体，严格执行统计法和本条例的规定。  
地方人民政府、县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门及其负责人应当保障统计活动依法进行，不得侵犯统计机构、统计人员独立行使统计调查、统计报告、统计监督职权，不得非法干预统计调查对象提供统计资料，不得统计造假、弄虚作假。  
统计调查对象应当依照统计法和国家有关规定，真实、准确、完整、及时地提供统计资料，拒绝、抵制弄虚作假等违法行为。

**Article 4** The local people's governments, the statistical institutions and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, clarify the responsible entities for preventing and punishing statistical fraud and falsification within their own units, and strictly implement the provisions of the Statistics Law and these Regulations.  
The local people's governments, the statistical institutions and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, and their responsible persons shall ensure that statistical activities are conducted according to law, shall not infringe upon the independent exercise by statistical institutions and statistical personnel of their functions and powers of statistical investigation, statistical reporting, and statistical supervision, shall not illegally interfere with the provision of statistical data by statistical respondents, and shall not engage in statistical fraud or falsification.  
The statistical respondents shall, in accordance with the Statistics Law and the relevant provisions of the State, provide statistical data in a truthful, accurate, complete and timely manner, and refuse to accept or resist illegal acts such as falsification.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门不得组织实施营利性统计调查。  
国家有计划地推进县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门通过向社会购买服务组织实施统计调查和资料开发。

**Article 5** The statistical institutions and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall not organize and implement profit-making statistical surveys.  
The State shall systematically promote the implementation of statistical surveys and data development by the statistical institutions and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level through purchasing services from society.

### 第二章　统计调查项目

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　部门统计调查项目、地方统计调查项目的主要内容不得与国家统计调查项目的内容重复、矛盾。

**Article 6** The main contents of departmental statistical survey projects and local statistical survey projects shall not duplicate or conflict with the contents of national statistical survey projects.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　统计调查项目的制定机关(以下简称制定机关)应当就项目的必要性、可行性、科学性进行论证，征求有关地方、部门、统计调查对象和专家的意见，并由制定机关按照会议制度集体讨论决定。  
重要统计调查项目应当进行试点。

**Article 7** The authority formulating a statistical survey project (hereinafter referred to as the formulating authority) shall demonstrate the necessity, feasibility and scientific nature of the project, solicit opinions from relevant localities, departments, statistical respondents and experts, and make a decision after collective discussion in accordance with the meeting system of the formulating authority.  
Key statistical survey projects shall be piloted.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　制定机关申请审批统计调查项目，应当以公文形式向审批机关提交统计调查项目审批申请表、项目的统计调查制度和工作经费来源说明。  
申请材料不齐全或者不符合法定形式的，审批机关应当一次性告知需要补正的全部内容，制定机关应当按照审批机关的要求予以补正。  
申请材料齐全、符合法定形式的，审批机关应当受理。

**Article 8** When applying for approval of a statistical survey project, the formulating authority shall submit to the approving authority, in the form of an official document, an application form for approval of the statistical survey project, the statistical survey system of the project, and a statement on the source of working funds.  
If the application materials are incomplete or do not conform to the statutory form, the approving authority shall, at one time, inform the formulating authority of all the contents that need to be supplemented or corrected, and the formulating authority shall supplement or correct them as required by the approving authority.  
If the application materials are complete and conform to the statutory form, the approving authority shall accept the application.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　统计调查项目符合下列条件的，审批机关应当作出予以批准的书面决定：  
(一)具有法定依据或者确为公共管理和服务所必需；  
(二)与已批准或者备案的统计调查项目的主要内容不重复、不矛盾；  
(三)主要统计指标无法通过行政记录或者已有统计调查资料加工整理取得；  
(四)统计调查制度符合统计法律法规规定，科学、合理、可行；  
(五)采用的统计标准符合国家有关规定；  
(六)制定机关具备项目执行能力。  
不符合前款规定条件的，审批机关应当向制定机关提出修改意见；修改后仍不符合前款规定条件的，审批机关应当作出不予批准的书面决定并说明理由。

**Article 9** If a statistical survey project meets the following conditions, the approving authority shall make a written decision to approve it:  
(1) It has a statutory basis or is indeed necessary for public administration and service;  
(2) Its main contents do not duplicate or conflict with those of approved or filed statistical survey projects;  
(3) The main statistical indicators cannot be obtained through administrative records or processing and compilation of existing statistical survey data;  
(4) The statistical survey system complies with the provisions of statistical laws and regulations, and is scientific, reasonable and feasible;  
(5) The statistical standards adopted conform to the relevant provisions of the State;  
(6) The formulating authority has the capability to implement the project.  
If the conditions specified in the preceding paragraph are not met, the approving authority shall propose modification opinions to the formulating authority; if after modification the conditions are still not met, the approving authority shall make a written decision of non-approval and state the reasons.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　统计调查项目涉及其他部门职责的，审批机关应当在作出审批决定前，征求相关部门的意见。

**Article 10** If a statistical survey project involves the functions and responsibilities of other departments, the approving authority shall solicit opinions from the relevant departments before making an approval decision.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　审批机关应当自受理统计调查项目审批申请之日起20日内作出决定。20日内不能作出决定的，经审批机关负责人批准可以延长10日，并应当将延长审批期限的理由告知制定机关。  
制定机关修改统计调查项目的时间，不计算在审批期限内。

**Article 11** The approving authority shall make a decision within 20 days from the date of accepting the application for approval of a statistical survey project. If a decision cannot be made within 20 days, the time limit may be extended by 10 days with the approval of the person in charge of the approving authority, and the reasons for the extension shall be notified to the formulating authority.  
The time taken by the formulating authority to modify the statistical survey project shall not be counted within the approval period.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　制定机关申请备案统计调查项目，应当以公文形式向备案机关提交统计调查项目备案申请表和项目的统计调查制度。  
统计调查项目的调查对象属于制定机关管辖系统，且主要内容与已批准、备案的统计调查项目不重复、不矛盾的，备案机关应当依法给予备案文号。

**Article 12** When applying for filing a statistical survey project, the formulating authority shall submit to the filing authority, in the form of an official document, an application form for filing the statistical survey project and the statistical survey system of the project.  
If the respondents of a statistical survey project are within the jurisdiction system of the formulating authority, and the main contents do not duplicate or conflict with those of approved or filed statistical survey projects, the filing authority shall, according to law, issue a filing document number.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　统计调查项目经批准或者备案的，审批机关或者备案机关应当及时公布统计调查项目及其统计调查制度的主要内容。涉及国家秘密的统计调查项目除外。

**Article 13** If a statistical survey project has been approved or filed, the approving authority or the filing authority shall promptly publish the main contents of the statistical survey project and its statistical survey system, except for statistical survey projects involving State secrets.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　统计调查项目有下列情形之一的，审批机关或者备案机关应当简化审批或者备案程序，缩短期限：  
(一)发生突发事件需要迅速实施统计调查；  
(二)统计调查制度内容未作变动，统计调查项目有效期届满需要延长期限。

**Article 14** In any of the following circumstances for a statistical survey project, the approving authority or the filing authority shall simplify the approval or filing procedures and shorten the time limit:  
(1) An emergency occurs and requires prompt implementation of a statistical survey;  
(2) The contents of the statistical survey system have not changed, and the validity period of the statistical survey project expires and needs to be extended.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　[统计法第十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国统计法.md#art-17)第二款规定的国家统计标准是强制执行标准。各级人民政府、县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门组织实施的统计调查活动，应当执行国家统计标准。  
制定国家统计标准，应当征求国务院有关部门的意见。

**Article 15** The national statistical standards as provided for in Paragraph 2 of Article 17 of the Statistics Law are compulsory enforcement standards. Statistical survey activities organized and implemented by people's governments at all levels, statistical institutions and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall implement the national statistical standards.  
When formulating national statistical standards, opinions shall be solicited from the relevant departments of the State Council.

### 第三章　统计调查的组织实施

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　统计机构、统计人员组织实施统计调查，应当就统计调查对象的法定填报义务、主要指标涵义和有关填报要求等，向统计调查对象作出说明。

**Article 16** When organizing and implementing a statistical survey, statistical institutions and statistical personnel shall explain to the statistical respondents the statutory filing obligations, the meanings of main indicators, and relevant filing requirements.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家机关、企业事业单位或者其他组织等统计调查对象提供统计资料，应当由填报人员和单位负责人签字，并加盖公章。个人作为统计调查对象提供统计资料，应当由本人签字。统计调查制度规定不需要签字、加盖公章的除外。  
统计调查对象使用网络提供统计资料的，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 17** When a statistical respondent such as a State organ, enterprise, public institution or other organization provides statistical data, the person filling in the data and the person in charge of the unit shall sign and affix the official seal. When an individual provides statistical data as a statistical respondent, the individual shall sign. Unless the statistical survey system provides that no signature or official seal is required.  
If a statistical respondent provides statistical data through a network, the relevant provisions of the State shall be followed.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上人民政府统计机构、有关部门推广使用网络报送统计资料，应当采取有效的网络安全保障措施。

**Article 18** When promoting the use of network submission of statistical data, the statistical institutions and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall take effective network security protection measures.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上人民政府统计机构、有关部门和乡、镇统计人员，应当对统计调查对象提供的统计资料进行审核。统计资料不完整或者存在明显错误的，应当由统计调查对象依法予以补充或者改正。

**Article 19** The statistical institutions and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, and the township or town statistical personnel shall examine the statistical data provided by the statistical respondents. If the statistical data are incomplete or contain obvious errors, the statistical respondents shall, according to law, supplement or correct them.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家统计局应当建立健全统计数据质量监控和评估制度，加强对各省、自治区、直辖市重要统计数据的监控和评估。

**Article 20** The National Bureau of Statistics shall establish and improve a system for monitoring and evaluating the quality of statistical data, and strengthen the monitoring and evaluation of important statistical data of all provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

### 第四章　统计资料的管理和公布

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上人民政府统计机构、有关部门和乡、镇人民政府应当妥善保管统计调查中取得的统计资料。  
国家建立统计资料灾难备份系统。

**Article 21** The statistical institutions and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, and the township or town people's governments shall properly keep the statistical data obtained in statistical surveys.  
The State shall establish a disaster backup system for statistical data.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　统计调查中取得的统计调查对象的原始资料，应当至少保存2年。  
汇总性统计资料应当至少保存10年，重要的汇总性统计资料应当永久保存。法律法规另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 22** The original data of statistical respondents obtained in statistical surveys shall be kept for at least 2 years.  
Aggregated statistical data shall be kept for at least 10 years, and important aggregated statistical data shall be kept permanently. Where laws and regulations provide otherwise, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　统计调查对象按照国家有关规定设置的原始记录和统计台账，应当至少保存2年。

**Article 23** The original records and statistical ledgers set up by statistical respondents in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State shall be kept for at least 2 years.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家统计局统计调查取得的全国性统计数据和分省、自治区、直辖市统计数据，由国家统计局公布或者由国家统计局授权其派出的调查机构或者省级人民政府统计机构公布。

**Article 24** The national statistical data and statistical data by province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government obtained from statistical surveys by the National Bureau of Statistics shall be published by the National Bureau of Statistics, or by the survey agencies dispatched by the National Bureau of Statistics or the statistical institutions of the provincial people's governments as authorized by the National Bureau of Statistics.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国务院有关部门统计调查取得的统计数据，由国务院有关部门按照国家有关规定和已批准或者备案的统计调查制度公布。  
县级以上地方人民政府有关部门公布其统计调查取得的统计数据，比照前款规定执行。

**Article 25** The statistical data obtained from statistical surveys by the relevant departments of the State Council shall be published by the relevant departments of the State Council in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the approved or filed statistical survey system.  
Where the relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level publish the statistical data obtained from their statistical surveys, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　已公布的统计数据按照国家有关规定需要进行修订的，县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门应当及时公布修订后的数据，并就修订依据和情况作出说明。

**Article 26** If published statistical data need to be revised in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, the statistical institutions and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall promptly publish the revised data and provide explanations on the basis and circumstances of the revision.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门应当及时公布主要统计指标涵义、调查范围、调查方法、计算方法、抽样调查样本量等信息，对统计数据进行解释说明。

**Article 27** The statistical institutions and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall promptly publish information such as the meanings of main statistical indicators, survey scope, survey methods, calculation methods, and sample size of sample surveys, and provide explanations on the statistical data.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　公布统计资料应当按照国家有关规定进行。公布前，任何单位和个人不得违反国家有关规定对外提供，不得利用尚未公布的统计资料谋取不正当利益。

**Article 28** The publication of statistical data shall be conducted in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. Before publication, no unit or individual shall provide them externally in violation of the relevant provisions of the State, nor shall they use unpublished statistical data to seek illegitimate interests.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　[统计法第二十五条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国统计法.md#art-25)规定的能够识别或者推断单个统计调查对象身份的资料包括：  
(一)直接标明单个统计调查对象身份的资料；  
(二)虽未直接标明单个统计调查对象身份，但是通过已标明的地址、编码等相关信息可以识别或者推断单个统计调查对象身份的资料；  
(三)可以推断单个统计调查对象身份的汇总资料。

**Article 29** The materials that can identify or infer the identity of a single statistical respondent as specified in Article 25 of the Statistics Law include:  
(1) Materials that directly indicate the identity of a single statistical respondent;  
(2) Materials that, although not directly indicating the identity of a single statistical respondent, can identify or infer the identity of a single statistical respondent through marked information such as addresses and codes;  
(3) Aggregated materials from which the identity of a single statistical respondent can be inferred.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　统计调查中获得的能够识别或者推断单个统计调查对象身份的资料应当依法严格管理，除作为统计执法依据外，不得直接作为对统计调查对象实施行政许可、行政处罚等具体行政行为的依据，不得用于完成统计任务以外的目的。

**Article 30** The materials that can identify or infer the identity of a single statistical respondent obtained in statistical surveys shall be strictly managed according to law. Except for being used as the basis for statistical law enforcement, such materials shall not be directly used as the basis for specific administrative acts such as administrative licensing or administrative penalties against the statistical respondent, and shall not be used for purposes other than completing statistical tasks.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家建立健全统计信息共享机制，实现县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门统计调查取得的资料共享。制定机关共同制定的统计调查项目，可以共同使用获取的统计资料。  
统计调查制度应当对统计信息共享的内容、方式、时限、渠道和责任等作出规定。

**Article 31** The State shall establish and improve a statistical information sharing mechanism to realize the sharing of data obtained from statistical surveys by the statistical institutions and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level. For statistical survey projects jointly formulated by formulating authorities, the statistical data obtained may be jointly used.  
The statistical survey system shall provide for the content, methods, time limits, channels and responsibilities of statistical information sharing.

### 第五章　统计机构和统计人员

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级以上地方人民政府统计机构受本级人民政府和上级人民政府统计机构的双重领导，在统计业务上以上级人民政府统计机构的领导为主。  
乡、镇人民政府应当设置统计工作岗位，配备专职或者兼职统计人员，履行统计职责，在统计业务上受上级人民政府统计机构领导。乡、镇统计人员的调动，应当征得县级人民政府统计机构的同意。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门在统计业务上受本级人民政府统计机构指导。

**Article 32** The statistical institutions of local people's governments at or above the county level are under the dual leadership of the people's government at the same level and the statistical institution of the people's government at the higher level, and in terms of statistical operations, the leadership of the statistical institution of the higher people's government shall prevail.  
The township or town people's government shall set up statistical posts, assign full-time or part-time statistical personnel to perform statistical duties, and shall, in terms of statistical operations, be under the leadership of the statistical institution of the higher people's government. The transfer of township or town statistical personnel shall be subject to the consent of the statistical institution of the county people's government.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in terms of statistical operations, be under the guidance of the statistical institution of the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门应当完成国家统计调查任务，执行国家统计调查项目的统计调查制度，组织实施本地方、本部门的统计调查活动。

**Article 33** The statistical institutions and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall fulfill the tasks of national statistical surveys, implement the statistical survey system of national statistical survey projects, and organize and implement the statistical survey activities within their respective localities or departments.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家机关、企业事业单位和其他组织应当加强统计基础工作，为履行法定的统计资料报送义务提供组织、人员和工作条件保障。

**Article 34** State organs, enterprises, public institutions and other organizations shall strengthen the basic work of statistics, and provide organizational, personnel and working conditions to guarantee the performance of the statutory obligation to submit statistical data.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　对在统计工作中做出突出贡献、取得显著成绩的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 35** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions and achieved remarkable results in statistical work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第六章　监督检查

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　县级以上人民政府统计机构从事统计执法工作的人员，应当具备必要的法律知识和统计业务知识，参加统计执法培训，并取得由国家统计局统一印制的统计执法证。

**Article 36** Personnel engaged in statistical law enforcement work in the statistical institutions of the people's governments at or above the county level shall possess the necessary legal knowledge and statistical professional knowledge, participate in statistical law enforcement training, and obtain the statistical law enforcement certificate uniformly printed by the National Bureau of Statistics.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　任何单位和个人不得拒绝、阻碍对统计工作的监督检查和对统计违法行为的查处工作，不得包庇、纵容统计违法行为。

**Article 37** No unit or individual may refuse to accept or obstruct the supervision and inspection of statistical work and the investigation and punishment of statistical violations, nor may they shelter or condone statistical violations.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　任何单位和个人有权向县级以上人民政府统计机构举报统计违法行为。  
县级以上人民政府统计机构应当公布举报统计违法行为的方式和途径，依法受理、核实、处理举报，并为举报人保密。

**Article 38** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report statistical violations to the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level.  
The statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level shall publish the methods and channels for reporting statistical violations, accept, verify and handle the reports according to law, and keep the informants confidential.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上人民政府统计机构负责查处统计违法行为；法律、行政法规对有关部门查处统计违法行为另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 39** The statistical institutions of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for investigating and punishing statistical violations; if laws and administrative regulations provide otherwise for the investigation and punishment of statistical violations by relevant departments, those provisions shall prevail.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　下列情形属于[统计法第三十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国统计法.md#art-37)第四项规定的对严重统计违法行为失察，对地方人民政府、政府统计机构或者有关部门、单位的负责人，由任免机关或者监察机关依法给予处分，并由县级以上人民政府统计机构予以通报：  
(一)本地方、本部门、本单位大面积发生或者连续发生统计造假、弄虚作假；  
(二)本地方、本部门、本单位统计数据严重失实，应当发现而未发现；  
(三)发现本地方、本部门、本单位统计数据严重失实不予纠正。

**Article 40** The following circumstances constitute the failure to detect serious statistical violations as specified in Item 4 of Article 37 of the Statistics Law, and the persons in charge of the local people's government, government statistical institution or relevant department or unit shall be given sanctions according to law by the appointing authority or the supervisory authority, and be notified by the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level:  
(1) Large-scale or successive occurrences of statistical fraud and falsification in the locality, department or unit;  
(2) The statistical data in the locality, department or unit are seriously untrue, which should have been discovered but were not;  
(3) The serious untruth of the statistical data in the locality, department or unit has been discovered but not corrected.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　县级以上人民政府统计机构或者有关部门组织实施营利性统计调查的，由本级人民政府、上级人民政府统计机构或者本级人民政府统计机构责令改正，予以通报；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 41** If the statistical institution or relevant department of the people's government at or above the county level organizes and implements profit-making statistical surveys, the people's government at the same level, the statistical institution of the higher people's government, or the statistical institution of the people's government at the same level shall order it to make corrections and give a notice of criticism; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　地方各级人民政府、县级以上人民政府统计机构或者有关部门及其负责人，侵犯统计机构、统计人员独立行使统计调查、统计报告、统计监督职权，或者采用下发文件、会议布置以及其他方式授意、指使、强令统计调查对象或者其他单位、人员编造虚假统计资料的，由上级人民政府、本级人民政府、上级人民政府统计机构或者本级人民政府统计机构责令改正，予以通报。

**Article 42** If a local people's government at any level, the statistical institution or relevant department of the people's government at or above the county level, or the person in charge thereof, infringes upon the independent exercise by statistical institutions and statistical personnel of their functions and powers of statistical investigation, statistical reporting and statistical supervision, or by issuing documents, arranging meetings or other means instigates, instructs or compels statistical respondents or other units or persons to fabricate false statistical data, the higher people's government, the people's government at the same level, the statistical institution of the higher people's government, or the statistical institution of the people's government at the same level shall order it to make corrections and give a notice of criticism.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　县级以上人民政府统计机构或者有关部门在组织实施统计调查活动中有下列行为之一的，由本级人民政府、上级人民政府统计机构或者本级人民政府统计机构责令改正，予以通报：  
(一)违法制定、审批或者备案统计调查项目；  
(二)未按照规定公布经批准或者备案的统计调查项目及其统计调查制度的主要内容；  
(三)未执行国家统计标准；  
(四)未执行统计调查制度；  
(五)自行修改单个统计调查对象的统计资料。  
乡、镇统计人员有前款第三项至第五项所列行为的，责令改正，依法给予处分。

**Article 43** If the statistical institution or relevant department of the people's government at or above the county level commits any of the following acts in organizing and implementing statistical survey activities, the people's government at the same level, the statistical institution of the higher people's government, or the statistical institution of the people's government at the same level shall order it to make corrections and give a notice of criticism:  
(1) Illegally formulating, approving or filing a statistical survey project;  
(2) Failing to publish the main contents of the approved or filed statistical survey project and its statistical survey system as required;  
(3) Failing to implement national statistical standards;  
(4) Failing to implement the statistical survey system;  
(5) Unauthorized modification of the statistical data of a single statistical respondent.  
If a township or town statistical personnel commits any of the acts listed in Items (3) to (5) of the preceding paragraph, the statistical personnel shall be ordered to make corrections and given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　县级以上人民政府统计机构或者有关部门违反[本条例第二十四条](#art-24)、第二十五条规定公布统计数据的，由本级人民政府、上级人民政府统计机构或者本级人民政府统计机构责令改正，予以通报。

**Article 44** If the statistical institution or relevant department of the people's government at or above the county level publishes statistical data in violation of the provisions of Articles 24 and 25 of these Regulations, the people's government at the same level, the statistical institution of the higher people's government, or the statistical institution of the people's government at the same level shall order it to make corrections and give a notice of criticism.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反国家有关规定对外提供尚未公布的统计资料或者利用尚未公布的统计资料谋取不正当利益的，由任免机关或者监察机关依法给予处分，并由县级以上人民政府统计机构予以通报。

**Article 45** If anyone, in violation of the relevant provisions of the State, provides unpublished statistical data externally or uses unpublished statistical data to seek illegitimate interests, the appointing authority or the supervisory authority shall give sanctions according to law, and the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level shall give a notice of criticism.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　统计机构及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府统计机构责令改正，予以通报：  
(一)拒绝、阻碍对统计工作的监督检查和对统计违法行为的查处工作；  
(二)包庇、纵容统计违法行为；  
(三)向有统计违法行为的单位或者个人通风报信，帮助其逃避查处；  
(四)未依法受理、核实、处理对统计违法行为的举报；  
(五)泄露对统计违法行为的举报情况。

**Article 46** If a statistical institution or its staff commits any of the following acts, the people's government at the same level or the statistical institution of the higher people's government shall order it to make corrections and give a notice of criticism:  
(1) Refusing to accept or obstructing the supervision and inspection of statistical work and the investigation and punishment of statistical violations;  
(2) Sheltering or condoning statistical violations;  
(3) Tipping off units or individuals with statistical violations and helping them evade investigation and punishment;  
(4) Failing to accept, verify and handle reports on statistical violations according to law;  
(5) Disclosing the information of reports on statistical violations.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　地方各级人民政府、县级以上人民政府有关部门拒绝、阻碍统计监督检查或者转移、隐匿、篡改、毁弃原始记录和凭证、统计台账、统计调查表及其他相关证明和资料的，由上级人民政府、上级人民政府统计机构或者本级人民政府统计机构责令改正，予以通报。

**Article 47** If a local people's government at any level or a relevant department of the people's government at or above the county level refuses or obstructs statistical supervision and inspection, or transfers, conceals, alters or destroys original records and vouchers, statistical ledgers, statistical survey forms and other related certifications and materials, the higher people's government, the statistical institution of the higher people's government, or the statistical institution of the people's government at the same level shall order it to make corrections and give a notice of criticism.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　地方各级人民政府、县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门有[本条例第四十一条](#art-41)至第四十七条所列违法行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，由任免机关或者监察机关依法给予处分。

**Article 48** If a local people's government at any level, the statistical institution or relevant department of the people's government at or above the county level commits any of the illegal acts listed in Articles 41 to 47 of these Regulations, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law by the appointing authority or the supervisory authority.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　乡、镇人民政府有[统计法第三十八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国统计法.md#art-38)第一款、第三十九条第一款所列行为之一的，依照[统计法第三十八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国统计法.md#art-38)、第三十九条的规定追究法律责任。

**Article 49** If a township or town people's government commits any of the acts listed in Paragraph 1 of Article 38 or Paragraph 1 of Article 39 of the Statistics Law, legal liability shall be pursued in accordance with the provisions of Articles 38 and 39 of the Statistics Law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　下列情形属于[统计法第四十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国统计法.md#art-41)第二款规定的情节严重行为：  
(一)使用暴力或者威胁方法拒绝、阻碍统计调查、统计监督检查；  
(二)拒绝、阻碍统计调查、统计监督检查，严重影响相关工作正常开展；  
(三)提供不真实、不完整的统计资料，造成严重后果或者恶劣影响；  
(四)有[统计法第四十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国统计法.md#art-41)第一款所列违法行为之一，1年内被责令改正3次以上。

**Article 50** The following circumstances constitute serious circumstances as specified in Paragraph 2 of Article 41 of the Statistics Law:  
(1) Using violence or threats to refuse or obstruct statistical surveys or statistical supervision and inspection;  
(2) Refusing or obstructing statistical surveys or statistical supervision and inspection, seriously affecting the normal conduct of relevant work;  
(3) Providing untrue or incomplete statistical data, causing serious consequences or adverse effects;  
(4) Having committed any of the illegal acts listed in Paragraph 1 of Article 41 of the Statistics Law and having been ordered to make corrections three times or more within one year.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　统计违法行为涉嫌犯罪的，县级以上人民政府统计机构应当将案件移送司法机关处理。

**Article 51** If a statistical violation is suspected of constituting a crime, the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level shall transfer the case to the judicial organ for handling.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　中华人民共和国境外的组织、个人需要在中华人民共和国境内进行统计调查活动的，应当委托中华人民共和国境内具有涉外统计调查资格的机构进行。涉外统计调查资格应当依法报经批准。统计调查范围限于省、自治区、直辖市行政区域内的，由省级人民政府统计机构审批；统计调查范围跨省、自治区、直辖市行政区域的，由国家统计局审批。  
涉外社会调查项目应当依法报经批准。统计调查范围限于省、自治区、直辖市行政区域内的，由省级人民政府统计机构审批；统计调查范围跨省、自治区、直辖市行政区域的，由国家统计局审批。

**Article 52** If an organization or individual outside the territory of the People's Republic of China needs to conduct statistical survey activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China, it shall entrust an institution within the territory of the People's Republic of China that has the qualification for foreign-related statistical surveys. The qualification for foreign-related statistical surveys shall be submitted for approval according to law. If the scope of the statistical survey is limited to the administrative area of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, it shall be examined and approved by the statistical institution of the provincial people's government; if the scope of the statistical survey spans across provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government, it shall be examined and approved by the National Bureau of Statistics.  
Foreign-related social survey projects shall be submitted for approval according to law. If the scope of the survey is limited to the administrative area of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, it shall be examined and approved by the statistical institution of the provincial people's government; if the scope of the survey spans across provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government, it shall be examined and approved by the National Bureau of Statistics.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家统计局或者省级人民政府统计机构对涉外统计违法行为进行调查，有权采取[统计法第三十五条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国统计法.md#art-35)规定的措施。

**Article 53** The National Bureau of Statistics or the statistical institution of the provincial people's government shall investigate foreign-related statistical violations and shall have the power to take the measures prescribed in Article 35 of the Statistics Law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　对违法从事涉外统计调查活动的单位、个人，由国家统计局或者省级人民政府统计机构责令改正或者责令停止调查，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；违法所得50万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款；违法所得不足50万元或者没有违法所得的，处200万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，暂停或者取消涉外统计调查资格，撤销涉外社会调查项目批准决定；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 54** For a unit or individual that illegally engages in foreign-related statistical survey activities, the National Bureau of Statistics or the statistical institution of the provincial people's government shall order it to make corrections or order the survey to be stopped; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are more than 500,000 yuan, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains shall be imposed concurrently; if the illegal gains are less than 500,000 yuan or there are no illegal gains, a fine of not more than 2 million yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the qualification for foreign-related statistical surveys shall be suspended or revoked, and the approval decision for the foreign-related social survey project shall be revoked; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　本条例自2017年8月1日起施行。1987年1月19日国务院批准、1987年2月15日国家统计局公布，2000年6月2日国务院批准修订、2000年6月15日国家统计局公布，2005年12月16日国务院修订的《中华人民共和国统计法实施细则》同时废止。

**Article 55** These Regulations shall come into force as of August 1, 2017. The Detailed Rules for the Implementation of the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China, approved by the State Council on January 19, 1987, promulgated by the National Bureau of Statistics on February 15, 1987, approved for revision by the State Council on June 2, 2000, promulgated by the National Bureau of Statistics on June 15, 2000, and revised by the State Council on December 16, 2005, shall be abolished simultaneously.
