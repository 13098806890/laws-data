# 国际收支统计申报办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2013-11-09  
**生效日期**：1996-01-01

> (1995年8月30日国务院批准　1995年9月14日中国人民银行发布　根据2013年11月9日《国务院关于修改〈国际收支统计申报办法〉的决定》修订) 第一条　为完善国际收支统计，根据《中华人民共和国统计法》，制定本办法。 第二条　国际收支统计申报范围为中国居民与非中国居民之间发生的一切经济交易以及中国居民对外金融资产、负债状况。 第三条　本办法所称中国居民，是指： (一)在中国境内居留1年以上的自然人，外国及香港、澳门、台湾地区在境内的留学生、就医人员、外国驻华使馆领馆外籍工作人员及其家属除外； (二)中国短期出国人员(在境外居留时间不满1年)、在境外留学人员、就医人员及中国驻外使馆领馆工作人员及其家属； (三)在中国境内依法成立的企业事业法人(含外商投资企业及外资金融机构)及境外法人的驻华机构(不含国际组织驻华机构、外国驻华使馆领馆)； (四)中国国家机关(含中国驻外使馆领馆)、团体、部队。 第四条　本办法适用于中国境内所有地区，包括在中国境内设立的保税区和保税仓库等。 第五条　国家外汇管理局按照《中华人民共和国统计法》规定的程序，负责组织实施国际收支统计申报，并进行监督、检查；统计、汇总并公布国际收支状况和国际投资状况；制定、修改本办法的实施细则；制发国际收支统计申报单及报表。政府有关部门应当协助国际收支统计申报工作。 第六条　国际收支统计申报实行交易主体申报的原则，采取间接申报与直接申报、逐笔申报与定期申报相结合的办法。 第七条　中国居民和在中国境内发生经济交易的非中国居民应当按照规定及时、准确、完整地申报国际收支信息。 第八条　中国居民通过境内金融机构与非中国居民进行交易的，应当通过该金融机构向国家外汇管理局或其分支局申报交易内容。 第九条　中国境内提供登记结算、托管等服务的机构和自营或者代理客户进行对外证券、期货、期权等交易的交易商，应当向国家外汇管理局或其分支局申报对外交易及相应的收支和分红派息情况。 第十条　中国境内各类金融机构应当直接向国家外汇管理局或其分支局申报其自营对外业务情况，包括其对外金融资产、负债及其变动情况，相应的利润、利息收支情况，以及对外金融服务收支和其他收支情况；并履行与中国居民和非中国居民通过其进行国际收支统计申报活动有关的义务。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为完善国际收支统计，根据《中华人民共和国统计法》，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of improving the statistics of the balance of payments.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国际收支统计申报范围为中国居民与非中国居民之间发生的一切经济交易以及中国居民对外金融资产、负债状况。

**Article 2** The scope of statistical declaration of the balance of payments shall cover all economic transactions between Chinese residents and non-Chinese residents, as well as the external financial assets and liabilities of Chinese residents.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本办法所称中国居民，是指：  
(一)在中国境内居留1年以上的自然人，外国及香港、澳门、台湾地区在境内的留学生、就医人员、外国驻华使馆领馆外籍工作人员及其家属除外；  
(二)中国短期出国人员(在境外居留时间不满1年)、在境外留学人员、就医人员及中国驻外使馆领馆工作人员及其家属；  
(三)在中国境内依法成立的企业事业法人(含外商投资企业及外资金融机构)及境外法人的驻华机构(不含国际组织驻华机构、外国驻华使馆领馆)；  
(四)中国国家机关(含中国驻外使馆领馆)、团体、部队。

**Article 3** For the purpose of these Measures, Chinese residents shall mean:  
(1) natural persons who reside in the territory of China for one year or more, excluding foreign students, medical patients, and foreign staff members of foreign embassies and consulates in China and their family members from foreign countries and the regions of Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan;  
(2) Chinese persons who go abroad for a short period (residing outside China for less than one year), Chinese persons studying or receiving medical treatment abroad, and staff members of Chinese embassies and consulates abroad and their family members;  
(3) enterprises and institutions as legal persons established according to law within the territory of China (including foreign-invested enterprises and foreign-funded financial institutions) and the offices of foreign legal persons stationed in China (excluding the offices of international organizations stationed in China and foreign embassies and consulates in China);  
(4) state organs of China (including Chinese embassies and consulates abroad), organizations, and the armed forces.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　本办法适用于中国境内所有地区，包括在中国境内设立的保税区和保税仓库等。

**Article 4** These Measures shall apply to all regions within the territory of China, including bonded zones and bonded warehouses established within the territory of China.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家外汇管理局按照《中华人民共和国统计法》规定的程序，负责组织实施国际收支统计申报，并进行监督、检查；统计、汇总并公布国际收支状况和国际投资状况；制定、修改本办法的实施细则；制发国际收支统计申报单及报表。政府有关部门应当协助国际收支统计申报工作。

**Article 5** The State Administration of Foreign Exchange shall, in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China, be responsible for organizing and implementing the statistical declaration of the balance of payments and conducting supervision and inspection; for collecting, compiling and publishing the status of the balance of payments and the status of international investment; for formulating and amending the implementing rules of these Measures; and for preparing and issuing the declaration forms and statements for the balance of payments statistics. Relevant government departments shall assist in the work of statistical declaration of the balance of payments.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国际收支统计申报实行交易主体申报的原则，采取间接申报与直接申报、逐笔申报与定期申报相结合的办法。

**Article 6** The statistical declaration of the balance of payments shall be based on the principle of declaration by the transaction parties and shall adopt a method combining indirect declaration with direct declaration, and declaration on a transaction-by-transaction basis with periodic declaration.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　中国居民和在中国境内发生经济交易的非中国居民应当按照规定及时、准确、完整地申报国际收支信息。

**Article 7** Chinese residents and non-Chinese residents that engage in economic transactions within the territory of China shall declare balance of payments information in a timely, accurate and complete manner in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　中国居民通过境内金融机构与非中国居民进行交易的，应当通过该金融机构向国家外汇管理局或其分支局申报交易内容。

**Article 8** Where a Chinese resident conducts a transaction with a non-Chinese resident through a financial institution within the territory of China, the Chinese resident shall declare the content of the transaction to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange or its branch offices through such financial institution.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　中国境内提供登记结算、托管等服务的机构和自营或者代理客户进行对外证券、期货、期权等交易的交易商，应当向国家外汇管理局或其分支局申报对外交易及相应的收支和分红派息情况。

**Article 9** Institutions that provide services such as registration, clearing and custody within the territory of China, and dealers that engage in external securities, futures, options and other transactions on a proprietary basis or as agents for clients, shall declare external transactions and the corresponding receipts and payments and dividend distributions to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange or its branch offices.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　中国境内各类金融机构应当直接向国家外汇管理局或其分支局申报其自营对外业务情况，包括其对外金融资产、负债及其变动情况，相应的利润、利息收支情况，以及对外金融服务收支和其他收支情况；并履行与中国居民和非中国居民通过其进行国际收支统计申报活动有关的义务。

**Article 10** All types of financial institutions within the territory of China shall directly declare to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange or its branch offices the status of their proprietary external business, including their external financial assets, liabilities and changes thereof, the corresponding revenues and expenditures of profits and interest, as well as the revenues and expenditures from external financial services and other revenues and expenditures; and shall perform the obligations relating to the statistical declaration activities of the balance of payments conducted by Chinese residents and non-Chinese residents through them.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　在中国境外开立账户的中国非金融机构，应当直接向国家外汇管理局或其分支局申报其通过境外账户与非中国居民发生的交易及账户余额。

**Article 11** Chinese non-financial institutions that maintain accounts outside China shall directly declare to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange or its branch offices the transactions with non-Chinese residents conducted through such accounts and the account balances.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　中国境内的外商投资企业、在境外有直接投资的企业及其他有对外金融资产、负债的非金融机构，必须直接向国家外汇管理局或其分支局申报其对外金融资产、负债及其变动情况和相应的利润、股息、利息收支情况。

**Article 12** Foreign-invested enterprises within the territory of China, enterprises with direct investment abroad, and other non-financial institutions that have external financial assets or liabilities must directly declare to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange or its branch offices their external financial assets, liabilities and changes thereof, and the corresponding revenues and expenditures of profits, dividends and interest.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　拥有对外金融资产、负债的中国居民个人，应当按照国家外汇管理局的规定申报其对外金融资产、负债的有关情况。

**Article 13** Chinese resident individuals who hold external financial assets or liabilities shall declare the relevant information on their external financial assets and liabilities in accordance with the provisions of the State Administration of Foreign Exchange.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家外汇管理局或其分支局可以就国际收支情况进行抽样调查或者普查。

**Article 14** The State Administration of Foreign Exchange or its branch offices may conduct sample surveys or general surveys on the situation of the balance of payments.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家外汇管理局或其分支局有权对中国居民和非中国居民申报的内容进行检查、核对，申报人及有关机构和个人应当提供检查、核对所需的资料和便利。

**Article 15** The State Administration of Foreign Exchange or its branch offices shall have the authority to inspect and verify the content declared by Chinese residents and non-Chinese residents, and the declarants and relevant institutions and individuals shall provide the information and facilities necessary for such inspection and verification.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家外汇管理局及其分支局应当对申报者申报的具体数据严格保密，只将其用于国际收支统计。除法律另有规定外，国际收支统计人员不得以任何形式向任何机构和个人提供申报者申报的具体数据。  
银行、交易商以及提供登记结算、托管等服务的机构应当对其在办理业务过程中知悉的申报者申报的具体数据严格保密。

**Article 16** The State Administration of Foreign Exchange and its branch offices shall strictly keep confidential the specific data declared by declarants, and shall only use such data for the statistics of the balance of payments. Unless otherwise provided by law, the personnel engaged in balance of payments statistics shall not provide the specific data declared by declarants to any institution or individual in any form.  
Banks, dealers and institutions that provide services such as registration, clearing and custody shall strictly keep confidential the specific data declared by declarants that they come to know in the course of handling business.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　中国居民、非中国居民未按照规定进行国际收支统计申报的，由国家外汇管理局或其分支局依照[《中华人民共和国外汇管理条例》第四十八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/金融证券/中华人民共和国外汇管理条例.md#art-48)的规定给予处罚。

**Article 17** Where a Chinese resident or a non-Chinese resident fails to make statistical declaration of the balance of payments in accordance with the provisions, the State Administration of Foreign Exchange or its branch offices shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of Article 48 of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Foreign Exchange Control.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国际收支统计人员违反[本办法第十六条](#art-16)规定的，依法给予处分。  
国家外汇管理局或其分支局，银行、交易商以及提供登记结算、托管等服务的机构违反[本办法第十六条](#art-16)规定的，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 18** Where a person engaged in balance of payments statistics violates the provisions of Article 16 of these Measures, a sanction shall be imposed according to law.  
Where the State Administration of Foreign Exchange or its branch offices, a bank, a dealer, or an institution that provides services such as registration, clearing and custody violates the provisions of Article 16 of these Measures, legal liability shall be pursued according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家外汇管理局根据本办法制定《国际收支统计申报办法实施细则》。

**Article 19** The State Administration of Foreign Exchange shall formulate the Implementing Rules for the Measures for the Statistical Declaration of the Balance of Payments in accordance with these Measures.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本办法自1996年1月1日起施行。

**Article 20** These Measures shall come into force on January 1, 1996.
