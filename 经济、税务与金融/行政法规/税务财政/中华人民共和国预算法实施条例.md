# 中华人民共和国预算法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2020-08-03  
**生效日期**：2020-10-01

> （1995年11月22日中华人民共和国国务院令第186号发布　2020年8月3日中华人民共和国国务院令第729号修订） 第一章　总则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国预算法》（以下简称预算法），制定本条例。 第二条　县级以上地方政府的派出机关根据本级政府授权进行预算管理活动，不作为一级预算，其收支纳入本级预算。 第三条　社会保险基金预算应当在精算平衡的基础上实现可持续运行，一般公共预算可以根据需要和财力适当安排资金补充社会保险基金预算。 第四条　预算法第六条第二款所称各部门，是指与本级政府财政部门直接发生预算缴拨款关系的国家机关、军队、政党组织、事业单位、社会团体和其他单位。 第五条　各部门预算应当反映一般公共预算、政府性基金预算、国有资本经营预算安排给本部门及其所属各单位的所有预算资金。 各部门预算收入包括本级财政安排给本部门及其所属各单位的预算拨款收入和其他收入。各部门预算支出为与部门预算收入相对应的支出，包括基本支出和项目支出。 本条第二款所称基本支出，是指各部门、各单位为保障其机构正常运转、完成日常工作任务所发生的支出，包括人员经费和公用经费；所称项目支出，是指各部门、各单位为完成其特定的工作任务和事业发展目标所发生的支出。 各部门及其所属各单位的本级预算拨款收入和其相对应的支出，应当在部门预算中单独反映。 部门预算编制、执行的具体办法，由本级政府财政部门依法作出规定。 第六条　一般性转移支付向社会公开应当细化到地区。专项转移支付向社会公开应当细化到地区和项目。 政府债务、机关运行经费、政府采购、财政专户资金等情况，按照有关规定向社会公开。 部门预算、决算应当公开基本支出和项目支出。部门预算、决算支出按其功能分类应当公开到项；按其经济性质分类，基本支出应当公开到款。 各部门所属单位的预算、决算及报表，应当在部门批复后20日内由单位向社会公开。单位预算、决算应当公开基本支出和项目支出。单位预算、决算支出按其功能分类应当公开到项；按其经济性质分类，基本支出应当公开到款。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国预算法》（以下简称预算法），制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Budget Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Budget Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　县级以上地方政府的派出机关根据本级政府授权进行预算管理活动，不作为一级预算，其收支纳入本级预算。

**Article 2** The agencies dispatched by local governments at or above the county level shall conduct budget management activities under the authorization of the governments at the same level, and shall not be treated as a level of budget, and their revenues and expenditures shall be incorporated into the budgets at the same level.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　社会保险基金预算应当在精算平衡的基础上实现可持续运行，一般公共预算可以根据需要和财力适当安排资金补充社会保险基金预算。

**Article 3** The social insurance fund budget shall achieve sustainable operation on the basis of actuarial balance. The general public budget may, according to needs and financial resources, appropriately arrange funds to supplement the social insurance fund budget.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　[预算法第六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-6)第二款所称各部门，是指与本级政府财政部门直接发生预算缴拨款关系的国家机关、军队、政党组织、事业单位、社会团体和其他单位。

**Article 4** The term "departments" as mentioned in Paragraph 2 of Article 6 of the Budget Law refers to state organs, the armed forces, political party organizations, public institutions, social organizations and other units that have a direct relationship with the finance department of the government at the same level regarding budget payments and allocations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　各部门预算应当反映一般公共预算、政府性基金预算、国有资本经营预算安排给本部门及其所属各单位的所有预算资金。  
各部门预算收入包括本级财政安排给本部门及其所属各单位的预算拨款收入和其他收入。各部门预算支出为与部门预算收入相对应的支出，包括基本支出和项目支出。  
本条第二款所称基本支出，是指各部门、各单位为保障其机构正常运转、完成日常工作任务所发生的支出，包括人员经费和公用经费；所称项目支出，是指各部门、各单位为完成其特定的工作任务和事业发展目标所发生的支出。  
各部门及其所属各单位的本级预算拨款收入和其相对应的支出，应当在部门预算中单独反映。  
部门预算编制、执行的具体办法，由本级政府财政部门依法作出规定。

**Article 5** The budgets of all departments shall reflect all budget funds arranged by the general public budget, government-managed fund budget, and state capital operation budget for the departments and their subordinate units.  
The budget revenues of all departments include the budgetary appropriation revenues and other revenues arranged by the finance at the same level for the departments and their subordinate units. The budget expenditures of all departments are the expenditures corresponding to the department budget revenues, including basic expenditures and project expenditures.  
The term "basic expenditures" as mentioned in paragraph 2 of this Article refers to the expenditures incurred by all departments and units to ensure the normal operation of their organs and to complete their daily work tasks, including personnel expenses and public expenses; the term "project expenditures" refers to the expenditures incurred by all departments and units to accomplish their specific work tasks and career development goals.  
The budgetary appropriation revenues at the same level and their corresponding expenditures of all departments and their subordinate units shall be separately reflected in the department budgets.  
The specific measures for the compilation and implementation of department budgets shall be formulated by the finance department of the government at the same level according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　一般性转移支付向社会公开应当细化到地区。专项转移支付向社会公开应当细化到地区和项目。  
政府债务、机关运行经费、政府采购、财政专户资金等情况，按照有关规定向社会公开。  
部门预算、决算应当公开基本支出和项目支出。部门预算、决算支出按其功能分类应当公开到项；按其经济性质分类，基本支出应当公开到款。  
各部门所属单位的预算、决算及报表，应当在部门批复后20日内由单位向社会公开。单位预算、决算应当公开基本支出和项目支出。单位预算、决算支出按其功能分类应当公开到项；按其经济性质分类，基本支出应当公开到款。

**Article 6** General transfer payments disclosed to the public shall be itemized by region. Special transfer payments disclosed to the public shall be itemized by region and project.  
Information on government debts, operating expenses of organs, government procurement, funds in fiscal accounts, etc. shall be disclosed to the public in accordance with the relevant provisions.  
Department budgets and final accounts shall disclose basic expenditures and project expenditures. The expenditures in department budgets and final accounts shall be disclosed by functional classification down to the item level; by economic nature classification, basic expenditures shall be disclosed down to the section level.  
The budgets, final accounts and statements of the units subordinate to all departments shall be disclosed to the public by the units within 20 days after the department approves them. The unit budgets and final accounts shall disclose basic expenditures and project expenditures. The expenditures in unit budgets and final accounts shall be disclosed by functional classification down to the item level; by economic nature classification, basic expenditures shall be disclosed down to the section level.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　[预算法第十五条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-15)所称中央和地方分税制，是指在划分中央与地方事权的基础上，确定中央与地方财政支出范围，并按税种划分中央与地方预算收入的财政管理体制。  
分税制财政管理体制的具体内容和实施办法，按照国务院的有关规定执行。

**Article 7** The term "tax-sharing system between the central and local governments" as mentioned in Article 15 of the Budget Law refers to the fiscal management system under which, on the basis of dividing the powers of the central and local governments, the scope of fiscal expenditures of the central and local governments is determined, and the budget revenues of the central and local governments are divided by tax categories.  
The specific contents and implementation measures of the tax-sharing fiscal management system shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上地方各级政府应当根据中央和地方分税制的原则和上级政府的有关规定，确定本级政府对下级政府的财政管理体制。

**Article 8** Local governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the principles of the tax-sharing system between the central and local governments and the relevant provisions of higher-level governments, determine the fiscal management system of the governments at the same level over lower-level governments.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　[预算法第十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-16)第二款所称一般性转移支付，包括：  
（一）均衡性转移支付；  
（二）对革命老区、民族地区、边疆地区、贫困地区的财力补助；  
（三）其他一般性转移支付。

**Article 9** The term "general transfer payments" as mentioned in Paragraph 2 of Article 16 of the Budget Law includes:  
(1) equalization transfer payments;  
(2) financial subsidies to old revolutionary base areas, ethnic minority areas, border areas and poverty-stricken areas;  
(3) other general transfer payments.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　[预算法第十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-16)第三款所称专项转移支付，是指上级政府为了实现特定的经济和社会发展目标给予下级政府，并由下级政府按照上级政府规定的用途安排使用的预算资金。  
县级以上各级政府财政部门应当会同有关部门建立健全专项转移支付定期评估和退出机制。对评估后的专项转移支付，按照下列情形分别予以处理：  
（一）符合法律、行政法规和国务院规定，有必要继续执行的，可以继续执行；  
（二）设立的有关要求变更，或者实际绩效与目标差距较大、管理不够完善的，应当予以调整；  
（三）设立依据失效或者废止的，应当予以取消。

**Article 10** The term "special transfer payments" as mentioned in Paragraph 3 of Article 16 of the Budget Law refers to budget funds granted by a higher-level government to a lower-level government for the purpose of achieving specific economic and social development goals, and used by the lower-level government according to the purposes prescribed by the higher-level government.  
The finance departments of governments at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments, establish and improve a regular evaluation and exit mechanism for special transfer payments. The evaluated special transfer payments shall be dealt with respectively according to the following circumstances:  
(1) if they comply with laws, administrative regulations and the provisions of the State Council and it is necessary to continue their implementation, they may continue to be implemented;  
(2) if the relevant requirements for their establishment have changed, or the actual performance deviates significantly from the goals, or the management is not sufficiently sound, they shall be adjusted;  
(3) if the basis for their establishment has ceased to be effective or has been abolished, they shall be cancelled.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　预算收入和预算支出以人民币元为计算单位。预算收支以人民币以外的货币收纳和支付的，应当折合成人民币计算。

**Article 11** Budget revenues and budget expenditures shall be calculated in Renminbi yuan. If budget revenues and expenditures are collected and paid in currencies other than Renminbi, they shall be converted into Renminbi for calculation.

### 第二章　预算收支范围

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　[预算法第二十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-27)第一款所称行政事业性收费收入，是指国家机关、事业单位等依照法律法规规定，按照国务院规定的程序批准，在实施社会公共管理以及在向公民、法人和其他组织提供特定公共服务过程中，按照规定标准向特定对象收取费用形成的收入。  
[预算法第二十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-27)第一款所称国有资源（资产）有偿使用收入，是指矿藏、水流、海域、无居民海岛以及法律规定属于国家所有的森林、草原等国有资源有偿使用收入，按照规定纳入一般公共预算管理的国有资产收入等。  
[预算法第二十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-27)第一款所称转移性收入，是指上级税收返还和转移支付、下级上解收入、调入资金以及按照财政部规定列入转移性收入的无隶属关系政府的无偿援助。

**Article 12** The term "revenue from administrative and institutional fees and charges" as mentioned in Paragraph 1 of Article 27 of the Budget Law refers to income generated from charges collected according to the prescribed standards from specific objects by state organs and public institutions, etc., in the process of implementing social public administration and providing specific public services to citizens, legal persons and other organizations, subject to approval in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the State Council under laws and regulations.  
The term "revenue from paid use of state-owned resources (assets)" as mentioned in Paragraph 1 of Article 27 of the Budget Law refers to income from the paid use of state-owned resources such as mineral deposits, water bodies, sea areas, unincorporated islands, and forests, grasslands that are owned by the state according to law, and income from state-owned assets that are included in the general public budget management according to regulations, etc.  
The term "transfer income" as mentioned in Paragraph 1 of Article 27 of the Budget Law refers to tax rebates and transfer payments from higher-level governments, contributions from lower-level governments, funds transferred in, and gratuitous assistance from governments without subordinate relationships that are listed as transfer income in accordance with the provisions of the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　转移性支出包括上解上级支出、对下级的税收返还和转移支付、调出资金以及按照财政部规定列入转移性支出的给予无隶属关系政府的无偿援助。

**Article 13** Transfer expenditures include expenditures turned over to higher-level governments, tax rebates and transfer payments to lower-level governments, funds transferred out, and gratuitous assistance to governments without subordinate relationships that are listed as transfer expenditures in accordance with the provisions of the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　政府性基金预算收入包括政府性基金各项目收入和转移性收入。  
政府性基金预算支出包括与政府性基金预算收入相对应的各项目支出和转移性支出。

**Article 14** Government-managed fund budget revenues include revenues from various items of government-managed funds and transfer income.  
Government-managed fund budget expenditures include expenditures on various items corresponding to government-managed fund budget revenues and transfer expenditures.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国有资本经营预算收入包括依照法律、行政法规和国务院规定应当纳入国有资本经营预算的国有独资企业和国有独资公司按照规定上缴国家的利润收入、从国有资本控股和参股公司获得的股息红利收入、国有产权转让收入、清算收入和其他收入。  
国有资本经营预算支出包括资本性支出、费用性支出、向一般公共预算调出资金等转移性支出和其他支出。

**Article 15** State capital operation budget revenues include the profit income that wholly state-owned enterprises and wholly state-owned companies shall turn over to the state according to regulations in accordance with laws, administrative regulations and the provisions of the State Council and that shall be included in the state capital operation budget, the dividend and bonus income obtained from state-controlled and state-invested companies, income from the transfer of state-owned property rights, liquidation income and other income.  
State capital operation budget expenditures include capital expenditures, expense-type expenditures, transfer expenditures such as funds transferred out to the general public budget, and other expenditures.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　社会保险基金预算收入包括各项社会保险费收入、利息收入、投资收益、一般公共预算补助收入、集体补助收入、转移收入、上级补助收入、下级上解收入和其他收入。  
社会保险基金预算支出包括各项社会保险待遇支出、转移支出、补助下级支出、上解上级支出和其他支出。

**Article 16** Social insurance fund budget revenues include revenues from various social insurance contributions, interest income, investment income, subsidies from the general public budget, collective subsidies, transfer income, subsidies from higher-level governments, contributions from lower-level governments and other income.  
Social insurance fund budget expenditures include expenditures on various social insurance benefits, transfer expenditures, subsidies to lower-level governments, payments to higher-level governments and other expenditures.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　地方各级预算上下级之间有关收入和支出项目的划分以及上解、返还或者转移支付的具体办法，由上级地方政府规定，报本级人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 17** The division of revenue and expenditure items between higher and lower levels of local budgets at all levels and the specific measures for turning over, refunding or transfer payments shall be prescribed by the higher-level local government and reported to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level for recordation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　地方各级社会保险基金预算上下级之间有关收入和支出项目的划分以及上解、补助的具体办法，按照统筹层次由上级地方政府规定，报本级人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 18** The division of revenue and expenditure items between higher and lower levels of local social insurance fund budgets at all levels and the specific measures for turning over and subsidizing shall be prescribed by the higher-level local government according to the pooling level and reported to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level for recordation.

### 第三章　预算编制

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　[预算法第三十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-31)所称预算草案，是指各级政府、各部门、各单位编制的未经法定程序审查和批准的预算。

**Article 19** The term "draft budget" as mentioned in Article 31 of the Budget Law refers to the budget compiled by all levels of government, all departments and all units that has not been examined and approved through legal procedures.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　[预算法第三十二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-32)第一款所称绩效评价，是指根据设定的绩效目标，依据规范的程序，对预算资金的投入、使用过程、产出与效果进行系统和客观的评价。  
绩效评价结果应当按照规定作为改进管理和编制以后年度预算的依据。

**Article 20** The term "performance evaluation" as mentioned in Paragraph 1 of Article 32 of the Budget Law refers to the systematic and objective evaluation of the input, use process, output and effect of budget funds based on the set performance objectives and in accordance with standardized procedures.  
The results of performance evaluation shall, according to regulations, serve as the basis for improving management and compiling budgets for subsequent years.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　[预算法第三十二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-32)第三款所称预算支出标准，是指对预算事项合理分类并分别规定的支出预算编制标准，包括基本支出标准和项目支出标准。  
地方各级政府财政部门应当根据财政部制定的预算支出标准，结合本地区经济社会发展水平、财力状况等，制定本地区或者本级的预算支出标准。

**Article 21** The term "budget expenditure standards" as mentioned in Paragraph 3 of Article 32 of the Budget Law refers to the standards for compiling expenditure budgets that are prescribed separately after reasonably classifying budget items, including standards for basic expenditures and standards for project expenditures.  
The finance departments of local governments at all levels shall, based on the budget expenditure standards formulated by the Ministry of Finance and taking into account the level of economic and social development and financial resources of the region, formulate the budget expenditure standards for the region or the same level.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　财政部于每年6月15日前部署编制下一年度预算草案的具体事项，规定报表格式、编报方法、报送期限等。

**Article 22** The Ministry of Finance shall, before June 15 each year, make arrangements for the specific matters concerning the compilation of the draft budget for the following year, and prescribe the formats of statements, methods of compilation and submission, and time limits for submission, etc.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　中央各部门应当按照国务院的要求和财政部的部署，结合本部门的具体情况，组织编制本部门及其所属各单位的预算草案。  
中央各部门负责本部门所属各单位预算草案的审核，并汇总编制本部门的预算草案，按照规定报财政部审核。

**Article 23** All central departments shall, in accordance with the requirements of the State Council and the arrangements of the Ministry of Finance, and taking into account the specific circumstances of their respective departments, organize the compilation of the draft budgets of their departments and their subordinate units.  
All central departments shall be responsible for the examination of the draft budgets of their subordinate units, and shall consolidate and compile the draft budget of their own departments, and report it to the Ministry of Finance for examination according to regulations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　财政部审核中央各部门的预算草案，具体编制中央预算草案；汇总地方预算草案或者地方预算，汇编中央和地方预算草案。

**Article 24** The Ministry of Finance shall examine the draft budgets of all central departments, and specifically compile the central draft budget; consolidate the local draft budgets or local budgets, and compile the consolidated central and local draft budgets.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　省、自治区、直辖市政府按照国务院的要求和财政部的部署，结合本地区的具体情况，提出本行政区域编制预算草案的要求。  
县级以上地方各级政府财政部门应当于每年6月30日前部署本行政区域编制下一年度预算草案的具体事项，规定有关报表格式、编报方法、报送期限等。

**Article 25** The governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the requirements of the State Council and the arrangements of the Ministry of Finance, and taking into account the specific circumstances of their regions, put forward the requirements for the compilation of draft budgets within their administrative regions.  
The finance departments of local governments at or above the county level shall, before June 30 each year, make arrangements for the specific matters concerning the compilation of the draft budget for the following year within their administrative regions, and prescribe the formats of the relevant statements, methods of compilation and submission, and time limits for submission, etc.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上地方各级政府各部门应当根据本级政府的要求和本级政府财政部门的部署，结合本部门的具体情况，组织编制本部门及其所属各单位的预算草案，按照规定报本级政府财政部门审核。

**Article 26** All departments of local governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the requirements of the government at the same level and the arrangements of the finance department of the government at the same level, and taking into account the specific circumstances of their respective departments, organize the compilation of the draft budgets of their departments and their subordinate units, and report them to the finance department of the government at the same level for examination according to regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上地方各级政府财政部门审核本级各部门的预算草案，具体编制本级预算草案，汇编本级总预算草案，经本级政府审定后，按照规定期限报上一级政府财政部门。  
省、自治区、直辖市政府财政部门汇总的本级总预算草案或者本级总预算，应当于下一年度1月10日前报财政部。

**Article 27** The finance departments of local governments at or above the county level shall examine the draft budgets of all departments at the same level, specifically compile the draft budget at the same level, and consolidate the draft general budget at the same level. After being examined and approved by the government at the same level, it shall be reported to the finance department of the higher-level government within the prescribed time limit.  
The draft general budget at the same level or the general budget at the same level consolidated by the finance departments of the governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be reported to the Ministry of Finance before January 10 of the following year.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县级以上各级政府财政部门审核本级各部门的预算草案时，发现不符合编制预算要求的，应当予以纠正；汇编本级总预算草案时，发现下级预算草案不符合上级政府或者本级政府编制预算要求的，应当及时向本级政府报告，由本级政府予以纠正。

**Article 28** When examining the draft budgets of all departments at the same level, the finance departments of governments at or above the county level shall, if they find any non-compliance with the requirements for budget compilation, correct them; when consolidating the draft general budget at the same level, if they find that the draft budgets of lower-level governments do not comply with the requirements for budget compilation of the higher-level government or the government at the same level, they shall promptly report to the government at the same level, and the government at the same level shall make corrections.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　各级政府财政部门编制收入预算草案时，应当征求税务、海关等预算收入征收部门和单位的意见。  
预算收入征收部门和单位应当按照财政部门的要求提供下一年度预算收入征收预测情况。

**Article 29** When compiling the draft revenue budget, the finance departments of all levels of government shall solicit the opinions of the departments and units that collect budget revenues, such as tax authorities and customs.  
The departments and units that collect budget revenues shall, in accordance with the requirements of the finance departments, provide the forecast of budget revenue collection for the following year.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　财政部门会同社会保险行政部门部署编制下一年度社会保险基金预算草案的具体事项。  
社会保险经办机构具体编制下一年度社会保险基金预算草案，报本级社会保险行政部门审核汇总。社会保险基金收入预算草案由社会保险经办机构会同社会保险费征收机构具体编制。财政部门负责审核并汇总编制社会保险基金预算草案。

**Article 30** The finance department shall, together with the social insurance administrative department, make arrangements for the specific matters concerning the compilation of the draft social insurance fund budget for the following year.  
The social insurance agency shall specifically compile the draft social insurance fund budget for the following year and report it to the social insurance administrative department at the same level for examination and consolidation. The draft revenue budget of the social insurance fund shall be specifically compiled by the social insurance agency together with the social insurance contribution collection agency. The finance department shall be responsible for examining and consolidating the draft social insurance fund budget.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　各级政府财政部门应当依照预算法和本条例规定，制定本级预算草案编制规程。

**Article 31** The finance departments of all levels of government shall, in accordance with the Budget Law and these Regulations, formulate the procedures for compiling the draft budget at the same level.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　各部门、各单位在编制预算草案时，应当根据资产配置标准，结合存量资产情况编制相关支出预算。

**Article 32** When compiling draft budgets, all departments and units shall, based on the standards for asset allocation and taking into account the existing assets, compile the relevant expenditure budgets.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　中央一般公共预算收入编制内容包括本级一般公共预算收入、从国有资本经营预算调入资金、地方上解收入、从预算稳定调节基金调入资金、其他调入资金。  
中央一般公共预算支出编制内容包括本级一般公共预算支出、对地方的税收返还和转移支付、补充预算稳定调节基金。  
中央政府债务余额的限额应当在本级预算中单独列示。

**Article 33** The compilation of central general public budget revenues includes the general public budget revenues at the same level, funds transferred in from the state capital operation budget, revenues turned over by local governments, funds transferred in from the budget stabilization and adjustment fund, and other funds transferred in.  
The compilation of central general public budget expenditures includes the general public budget expenditures at the same level, tax rebates and transfer payments to local governments, and supplements to the budget stabilization and adjustment fund.  
The limit on the outstanding balance of central government debts shall be separately presented in the budget at the same level.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　地方各级一般公共预算收入编制内容包括本级一般公共预算收入、从国有资本经营预算调入资金、上级税收返还和转移支付、下级上解收入、从预算稳定调节基金调入资金、其他调入资金。  
地方各级一般公共预算支出编制内容包括本级一般公共预算支出、上解上级支出、对下级的税收返还和转移支付、补充预算稳定调节基金。

**Article 34** The compilation of local general public budget revenues at all levels includes the general public budget revenues at the same level, funds transferred in from the state capital operation budget, tax rebates and transfer payments from higher-level governments, revenues turned over by lower-level governments, funds transferred in from the budget stabilization and adjustment fund, and other funds transferred in.  
The compilation of local general public budget expenditures at all levels includes the general public budget expenditures at the same level, expenditures turned over to higher-level governments, tax rebates and transfer payments to lower-level governments, and supplements to the budget stabilization and adjustment fund.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　中央政府性基金预算收入编制内容包括本级政府性基金各项目收入、上一年度结余、地方上解收入。  
中央政府性基金预算支出编制内容包括本级政府性基金各项目支出、对地方的转移支付、调出资金。

**Article 35** The compilation of central government-managed fund budget revenues includes revenues from various items of government-managed funds at the same level, the balance from the previous year, and revenues turned over by local governments.  
The compilation of central government-managed fund budget expenditures includes expenditures on various items of government-managed funds at the same level, transfer payments to local governments, and funds transferred out.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　地方政府性基金预算收入编制内容包括本级政府性基金各项目收入、上一年度结余、下级上解收入、上级转移支付。  
地方政府性基金预算支出编制内容包括本级政府性基金各项目支出、上解上级支出、对下级的转移支付、调出资金。

**Article 36** The compilation of local government-managed fund budget revenues includes revenues from various items of government-managed funds at the same level, the balance from the previous year, revenues turned over by lower-level governments, and transfer payments from higher-level governments.  
The compilation of local government-managed fund budget expenditures includes expenditures on various items of government-managed funds at the same level, expenditures turned over to higher-level governments, transfer payments to lower-level governments, and funds transferred out.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　中央国有资本经营预算收入编制内容包括本级收入、上一年度结余、地方上解收入。  
中央国有资本经营预算支出编制内容包括本级支出、向一般公共预算调出资金、对地方特定事项的转移支付。

**Article 37** The compilation of central state capital operation budget revenues includes revenues at the same level, the balance from the previous year, and revenues turned over by local governments.  
The compilation of central state capital operation budget expenditures includes expenditures at the same level, funds transferred out to the general public budget, and transfer payments to local governments for specific matters.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　地方国有资本经营预算收入编制内容包括本级收入、上一年度结余、上级对特定事项的转移支付、下级上解收入。  
地方国有资本经营预算支出编制内容包括本级支出、向一般公共预算调出资金、对下级特定事项的转移支付、上解上级支出。

**Article 38** The compilation of local state capital operation budget revenues includes revenues at the same level, the balance from the previous year, transfer payments from higher-level governments for specific matters, and revenues turned over by lower-level governments.  
The compilation of local state capital operation budget expenditures includes expenditures at the same level, funds transferred out to the general public budget, transfer payments to lower-level governments for specific matters, and expenditures turned over to higher-level governments.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　中央和地方社会保险基金预算收入、支出编制内容包括[本条例第十六条](#art-16)规定的各项收入和支出。

**Article 39** The compilation of central and local social insurance fund budget revenues and expenditures includes all items of revenues and expenditures as prescribed in Article 16 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　各部门、各单位预算收入编制内容包括本级预算拨款收入、预算拨款结转和其他收入。  
各部门、各单位预算支出编制内容包括基本支出和项目支出。  
各部门、各单位的预算支出，按其功能分类应当编列到项，按其经济性质分类应当编列到款。

**Article 40** The compilation of budget revenues of all departments and units includes the budgetary appropriation revenues at the same level, the carry-over of budgetary appropriations and other revenues.  
The compilation of budget expenditures of all departments and units includes basic expenditures and project expenditures.  
The budget expenditures of all departments and units shall be compiled down to the item level by functional classification, and down to the section level by economic nature classification.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　各级政府应当加强项目支出管理。各级政府财政部门应当建立和完善项目支出预算评审制度。各部门、各单位应当按照本级政府财政部门的规定开展预算评审。  
项目支出实行项目库管理，并建立健全项目入库评审机制和项目滚动管理机制。

**Article 41** All levels of government shall strengthen the management of project expenditures. The finance departments of all levels of government shall establish and improve the system for reviewing and evaluating project expenditure budgets. All departments and units shall carry out budget review and evaluation in accordance with the provisions of the finance department of the government at the same level.  
Project expenditures shall be subject to project database management, and a mechanism for reviewing projects before inclusion in the database and a mechanism for rolling management of projects shall be established and improved.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　[预算法第三十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-34)第二款所称余额管理，是指国务院在全国人民代表大会批准的中央一般公共预算债务的余额限额内，决定发债规模、品种、期限和时点的管理方式；所称余额，是指中央一般公共预算中举借债务未偿还的本金。

**Article 42** The term "outstanding balance management" as mentioned in Paragraph 2 of Article 34 of the Budget Law refers to the management method under which the State Council determines the scale, variety, maturity and timing of bond issuance within the limit on the outstanding balance of central general public budget debts approved by the National People's Congress; the term "outstanding balance" refers to the principal of the debts borrowed in the central general public budget that has not been repaid.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　地方政府债务余额实行限额管理。各省、自治区、直辖市的政府债务限额，由财政部在全国人民代表大会或者其常务委员会批准的总限额内，根据各地区债务风险、财力状况等因素，并考虑国家宏观调控政策等需要，提出方案报国务院批准。  
各省、自治区、直辖市的政府债务余额不得突破国务院批准的限额。

**Article 43** The outstanding balance of local government debts shall be subject to limit management. The government debt limits for each province, autonomous region and municipality directly under the Central Government shall be proposed by the Ministry of Finance within the total limit approved by the National People's Congress or its Standing Committee, based on factors such as the debt risk and financial resources of each region, and taking into account the needs of national macro-control policies, and shall be reported to the State Council for approval.  
The outstanding balance of government debts of each province, autonomous region and municipality directly under the Central Government shall not exceed the limit approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　[预算法第三十五条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-35)第二款所称举借债务的规模，是指各地方政府债务余额限额的总和，包括一般债务限额和专项债务限额。一般债务是指列入一般公共预算用于公益性事业发展的一般债券、地方政府负有偿还责任的外国政府和国际经济组织贷款转贷债务；专项债务是指列入政府性基金预算用于有收益的公益性事业发展的专项债券。

**Article 44** The term "scale of debts borrowed" as mentioned in Paragraph 2 of Article 35 of the Budget Law refers to the sum of the limits on the outstanding balance of debts of each local government, including the limit on general debts and the limit on special debts. General debts refer to general bonds included in the general public budget for the development of public welfare undertakings, and the debt from on-lending of loans from foreign governments and international economic organizations for which the local government bears the repayment responsibility; special debts refer to special bonds included in the government-managed fund budget for the development of revenue-generating public welfare undertakings.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　省、自治区、直辖市政府财政部门依照国务院下达的本地区地方政府债务限额，提出本级和转贷给下级政府的债务限额安排方案，报本级政府批准后，将增加举借的债务列入本级预算调整方案，报本级人民代表大会常务委员会批准。  
接受转贷并向下级政府转贷的政府应当将转贷债务纳入本级预算管理。使用转贷并负有直接偿还责任的政府，应当将转贷债务列入本级预算调整方案，报本级人民代表大会常务委员会批准。  
地方各级政府财政部门负责统一管理本地区政府债务。

**Article 45** The finance departments of the governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the local government debt limits for their regions issued by the State Council, propose a plan for the arrangement of debt limits at the same level and for on-lending to lower-level governments. After being approved by the government at the same level, the additional debts to be borrowed shall be included in the budget adjustment plan at the same level and submitted to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level for approval.  
A government that accepts on-lending and on-lends to a lower-level government shall incorporate the on-lent debt into its budget management at the same level. A government that uses on-lent funds and bears direct repayment responsibility shall include the on-lent debt in its budget adjustment plan at the same level and submit it to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level for approval.  
The finance departments of local governments at all levels shall be responsible for the unified management of government debts in their regions.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国务院可以将举借的外国政府和国际经济组织贷款转贷给省、自治区、直辖市政府。  
国务院向省、自治区、直辖市政府转贷的外国政府和国际经济组织贷款，省、自治区、直辖市政府负有直接偿还责任的，应当纳入本级预算管理。省、自治区、直辖市政府未能按时履行还款义务的，国务院可以相应抵扣对该地区的税收返还等资金。  
省、自治区、直辖市政府可以将国务院转贷的外国政府和国际经济组织贷款再转贷给下级政府。

**Article 46** The State Council may on-lend the loans borrowed from foreign governments and international economic organizations to the governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
If the governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government bear the direct repayment responsibility for the loans from foreign governments and international economic organizations on-lent by the State Council, such loans shall be incorporated into their budget management at the same level. If a government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government fails to perform its repayment obligations on time, the State Council may correspondingly deduct the tax rebates and other funds to that region.  
The governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government may re-on-lend the loans from foreign governments and international economic organizations on-lent by the State Council to lower-level governments.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　财政部和省、自治区、直辖市政府财政部门应当建立健全地方政府债务风险评估指标体系，组织评估地方政府债务风险状况，对债务高风险地区提出预警，并监督化解债务风险。

**Article 47** The Ministry of Finance and the finance departments of the governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall establish and improve a risk assessment indicator system for local government debts, organize assessments of the risk status of local government debts, issue early warnings to areas with high debt risks, and supervise the resolution of debt risks.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上各级政府应当按照本年度转移支付预计执行数的一定比例将下一年度转移支付预计数提前下达至下一级政府，具体下达事宜由本级政府财政部门办理。  
除据实结算等特殊项目的转移支付外，提前下达的一般性转移支付预计数的比例一般不低于90%；提前下达的专项转移支付预计数的比例一般不低于70%。其中，按照项目法管理分配的专项转移支付，应当一并明确下一年度组织实施的项目。

**Article 48** Governments at or above the county level shall, based on a certain proportion of the estimated implementation amount of transfer payments for the current year, issue in advance the estimated amount of transfer payments for the following year to the governments at the next lower level. The specific issuance matters shall be handled by the finance department of the government at the same level.  
Except for transfer payments for special items such as actual settlement, the proportion of the estimated amount of general transfer payments issued in advance shall generally not be less than 90%; the proportion of the estimated amount of special transfer payments issued in advance shall generally not be less than 70%. Among them, for special transfer payments allocated and managed according to the project method, the projects to be organized and implemented in the following year shall be specified together.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　经本级政府批准，各级政府财政部门可以设置预算周转金，额度不得超过本级一般公共预算支出总额的1%。年度终了时，各级政府财政部门可以将预算周转金收回并用于补充预算稳定调节基金。

**Article 49** With the approval of the government at the same level, the finance departments of all levels of government may set up a budget working capital, the amount of which shall not exceed 1% of the total general public budget expenditures at the same level. At the end of the year, the finance departments of all levels of government may withdraw the budget working capital and use it to supplement the budget stabilization and adjustment fund.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　[预算法第四十二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-42)第一款所称结转资金，是指预算安排项目的支出年度终了时尚未执行完毕，或者因故未执行但下一年度需要按原用途继续使用的资金；连续两年未用完的结转资金，是指预算安排项目的支出在下一年度终了时仍未用完的资金。  
[预算法第四十二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-42)第一款所称结余资金，是指年度预算执行终了时，预算收入实际完成数扣除预算支出实际完成数和结转资金后剩余的资金。

**Article 50** The term "carry-over funds" as mentioned in Paragraph 1 of Article 42 of the Budget Law refers to funds that, at the end of the year, have not been fully disbursed for the expenditures of budgeted projects, or have not been disbursed for some reason but need to be continued to be used for the original purposes in the following year; the term "carry-over funds that have not been used for two consecutive years" refers to funds that, at the end of the following year, have still not been fully used for the expenditures of budgeted projects.  
The term "surplus funds" as mentioned in Paragraph 1 of Article 42 of the Budget Law refers to the funds remaining after deducting the actual completed amount of budget expenditures and carry-over funds from the actual completed amount of budget revenues at the end of the annual budget implementation.

### 第四章　预算执行

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　预算执行中，政府财政部门的主要职责：  
（一）研究和落实财政税收政策措施，支持经济社会健康发展；  
（二）制定组织预算收入、管理预算支出以及相关财务、会计、内部控制、监督等制度和办法；  
（三）督促各预算收入征收部门和单位依法履行职责，征缴预算收入；  
（四）根据年度支出预算和用款计划，合理调度、拨付预算资金，监督各部门、各单位预算资金使用管理情况；  
（五）统一管理政府债务的举借、支出与偿还，监督债务资金使用情况；  
（六）指导和监督各部门、各单位建立健全财务制度和会计核算体系，规范账户管理，健全内部控制机制，按照规定使用预算资金；  
（七）汇总、编报分期的预算执行数据，分析预算执行情况，按照本级人民代表大会常务委员会、本级政府和上一级政府财政部门的要求定期报告预算执行情况，并提出相关政策建议；  
（八）组织和指导预算资金绩效监控、绩效评价；  
（九）协调预算收入征收部门和单位、国库以及其他有关部门的业务工作。

**Article 51** During the implementation of the budget, the main responsibilities of the government finance departments are:  
(1) to study and implement fiscal and tax policies and measures to support healthy economic and social development;  
(2) to formulate systems and methods for organizing budget revenues, managing budget expenditures, and related financial, accounting, internal control and supervision;  
(3) to supervise all budget revenue collection departments and units in performing their duties according to law and collecting and remitting budget revenues;  
(4) to reasonably allocate and disburse budget funds according to the annual expenditure budget and fund utilization plans, and supervise the use and management of budget funds by all departments and units;  
(5) to uniformly manage the borrowing, expenditure and repayment of government debts, and supervise the use of debt funds;  
(6) to guide and supervise all departments and units in establishing and improving financial systems and accounting systems, standardizing account management, improving internal control mechanisms, and using budget funds in accordance with regulations;  
(7) to consolidate and compile periodic budget implementation data, analyze the budget implementation situation, regularly report the budget implementation situation in accordance with the requirements of the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level, the government at the same level and the finance department of the higher-level government, and put forward relevant policy suggestions;  
(8) to organize and guide the performance monitoring and performance evaluation of budget funds;  
(9) to coordinate the operational work of budget revenue collection departments and units, the treasury and other relevant departments.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　[预算法第五十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-56)第二款所称财政专户，是指财政部门为履行财政管理职能，根据法律规定或者经国务院批准开设的用于管理核算特定专用资金的银行结算账户；所称特定专用资金，包括法律规定可以设立财政专户的资金，外国政府和国际经济组织的贷款、赠款，按照规定存储的人民币以外的货币，财政部会同有关部门报国务院批准的其他特定专用资金。  
开设、变更财政专户应当经财政部核准，撤销财政专户应当报财政部备案，中国人民银行应当加强对银行业金融机构开户的核准、管理和监督工作。  
财政专户资金由本级政府财政部门管理。除法律另有规定外，未经本级政府财政部门同意，任何部门、单位和个人都无权冻结、动用财政专户资金。  
财政专户资金应当由本级政府财政部门纳入统一的会计核算，并在预算执行情况、决算和政府综合财务报告中单独反映。

**Article 52** The term "fiscal account" as mentioned in Paragraph 2 of Article 56 of the Budget Law refers to a bank settlement account opened by the finance department for the purpose of performing fiscal management functions, in accordance with legal provisions or with the approval of the State Council, for managing and accounting specific dedicated funds; the term "specific dedicated funds" includes funds for which a fiscal account may be established under legal provisions, loans and grants from foreign governments and international economic organizations, currencies other than Renminbi stored in accordance with regulations, and other specific dedicated funds approved by the State Council upon the report of the Ministry of Finance together with the relevant departments.  
The opening and modification of a fiscal account shall be subject to the approval of the Ministry of Finance, and the cancellation of a fiscal account shall be reported to the Ministry of Finance for recordation. The People's Bank of China shall strengthen the approval, management and supervision of the opening of accounts by banking financial institutions.  
Funds in fiscal accounts shall be managed by the finance department of the government at the same level. Unless otherwise provided by law, no department, unit or individual has the right to freeze or use funds in fiscal accounts without the consent of the finance department of the government at the same level.  
Funds in fiscal accounts shall be incorporated into unified accounting by the finance department of the government at the same level, and shall be separately reflected in the budget implementation situation, final accounts and government comprehensive financial reports.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　预算执行中，各部门、各单位的主要职责：  
（一）制定本部门、本单位预算执行制度，建立健全内部控制机制；  
（二）依法组织收入，严格支出管理，实施绩效监控，开展绩效评价，提高资金使用效益；  
（三）对单位的各项经济业务进行会计核算；  
（四）汇总本部门、本单位的预算执行情况，定期向本级政府财政部门报送预算执行情况报告和绩效评价报告。

**Article 53** During the implementation of the budget, the main responsibilities of all departments and units are:  
(1) to formulate the budget implementation systems of their own departments and units, and establish and improve internal control mechanisms;  
(2) to organize revenues according to law, strictly manage expenditures, implement performance monitoring, carry out performance evaluations, and improve the efficiency of fund use;  
(3) to conduct accounting for various economic transactions of the units;  
(4) to consolidate the budget implementation situations of their own departments and units, and regularly submit budget implementation reports and performance evaluation reports to the finance department of the government at the same level.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　财政部门会同社会保险行政部门、社会保险费征收机构制定社会保险基金预算的收入、支出以及财务管理的具体办法。  
社会保险基金预算由社会保险费征收机构和社会保险经办机构具体执行，并按照规定向本级政府财政部门和社会保险行政部门报告执行情况。

**Article 54** The finance department shall, together with the social insurance administrative department and the social insurance contribution collection agency, formulate the specific measures for the revenues, expenditures and financial management of the social insurance fund budget.  
The social insurance fund budget shall be specifically implemented by the social insurance contribution collection agency and the social insurance agency, and they shall report the implementation situation to the finance department of the government at the same level and the social insurance administrative department according to regulations.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　各级政府财政部门和税务、海关等预算收入征收部门和单位必须依法组织预算收入，按照财政管理体制、征收管理制度和国库集中收缴制度的规定征收预算收入，除依法缴入财政专户的社会保险基金等预算收入外，应当及时将预算收入缴入国库。

**Article 55** The finance departments of all levels of government and the departments and units that collect budget revenues, such as tax authorities and customs, must organize budget revenues according to law, collect budget revenues in accordance with the provisions of the fiscal management system, the collection management system and the treasury centralized collection system, and shall, except for budget revenues such as social insurance funds that are paid into fiscal accounts according to law, promptly turn over the budget revenues to the treasury.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　除依法缴入财政专户的社会保险基金等预算收入外，一切有预算收入上缴义务的部门和单位，必须将应当上缴的预算收入，按照规定的预算级次、政府收支分类科目、缴库方式和期限缴入国库，任何部门、单位和个人不得截留、占用、挪用或者拖欠。

**Article 56** Except for budget revenues such as social insurance funds that are paid into fiscal accounts according to law, all departments and units that have the obligation to turn over budget revenues must turn over the budget revenues that should be turned over to the treasury in accordance with the prescribed budget levels, government revenue and expenditure classification subjects, treasury payment methods and time limits. No department, unit or individual may intercept, occupy, misappropriate or default on such revenues.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　各级政府财政部门应当加强对预算资金拨付的管理，并遵循下列原则：  
（一）按照预算拨付，即按照批准的年度预算和用款计划拨付资金。除[预算法第五十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-54)规定的在预算草案批准前可以安排支出的情形外，不得办理无预算、无用款计划、超预算或者超计划的资金拨付，不得擅自改变支出用途；  
（二）按照规定的预算级次和程序拨付，即根据用款单位的申请，按照用款单位的预算级次、审定的用款计划和财政部门规定的预算资金拨付程序拨付资金；  
（三）按照进度拨付，即根据用款单位的实际用款进度拨付资金。

**Article 57** The finance departments of all levels of government shall strengthen the management of budget fund disbursement and follow the following principles:  
(1) Disbursement according to the budget: funds shall be disbursed in accordance with the approved annual budget and fund utilization plans. Except for the circumstances provided for in Article 54 of the Budget Law where expenditures may be arranged before the draft budget is approved, no fund disbursement shall be made without a budget, without a fund utilization plan, in excess of the budget or the plan, and the use of expenditures shall not be changed without authorization;  
(2) Disbursement according to the prescribed budget levels and procedures: funds shall be disbursed based on the application of the fund-using unit, in accordance with the budget level of the fund-using unit, the approved fund utilization plan and the budget fund disbursement procedures prescribed by the finance department;  
(3) Disbursement according to progress: funds shall be disbursed based on the actual fund utilization progress of the fund-using unit.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　财政部应当根据全国人民代表大会批准的中央政府债务余额限额，合理安排发行国债的品种、结构、期限和时点。  
省、自治区、直辖市政府财政部门应当根据国务院批准的本地区政府债务限额，合理安排发行本地区政府债券的结构、期限和时点。

**Article 58** The Ministry of Finance shall, based on the limit on the outstanding balance of central government debts approved by the National People's Congress, reasonably arrange the varieties, structure, maturity and timing of the issuance of treasury bonds.  
The finance departments of the governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the government debt limits for their regions approved by the State Council, reasonably arrange the structure, maturity and timing of the issuance of local government bonds in their regions.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　转移支付预算下达和资金拨付应当由财政部门办理，其他部门和单位不得对下级政府部门和单位下达转移支付预算或者拨付转移支付资金。

**Article 59** The issuance of transfer payment budgets and the disbursement of funds shall be handled by the finance department. No other department or unit may issue transfer payment budgets or disburse transfer payment funds to lower-level government departments or units.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　各级政府、各部门、各单位应当加强对预算支出的管理，严格执行预算，遵守财政制度，强化预算约束，不得擅自扩大支出范围、提高开支标准；严格按照预算规定的支出用途使用资金，合理安排支出进度。

**Article 60** All levels of government, all departments and all units shall strengthen the management of budget expenditures, strictly implement the budget, abide by the fiscal system, strengthen budget constraints, and shall not arbitrarily expand the scope of expenditures or raise expenditure standards; they shall use funds strictly in accordance with the expenditure purposes prescribed in the budget, and reasonably arrange the progress of expenditures.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　财政部负责制定与预算执行有关的财务规则、会计准则和会计制度。各部门、各单位应当按照本级政府财政部门的要求建立健全财务制度，加强会计核算。

**Article 61** The Ministry of Finance is responsible for formulating financial rules, accounting standards and accounting systems related to budget implementation. All departments and units shall, in accordance with the requirements of the finance department of the government at the same level, establish and improve financial systems and strengthen accounting.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　国库是办理预算收入的收纳、划分、留解、退付和库款支拨的专门机构。国库分为中央国库和地方国库。  
中央国库业务由中国人民银行经理。未设中国人民银行分支机构的地区，由中国人民银行商财政部后，委托有关银行业金融机构办理。  
地方国库业务由中国人民银行分支机构经理。未设中国人民银行分支机构的地区，由上级中国人民银行分支机构商有关地方政府财政部门后，委托有关银行业金融机构办理。  
具备条件的乡、民族乡、镇，应当设立国库。具体条件和标准由省、自治区、直辖市政府财政部门确定。

**Article 62** The treasury is a specialized institution that handles the collection, division, retention, refund and disbursement of budget revenues and the disbursement of treasury funds. The treasury is divided into the central treasury and local treasuries.  
Central treasury operations are managed by the People's Bank of China. In areas where no branch of the People's Bank of China is established, the People's Bank of China shall, after consulting the Ministry of Finance, entrust the relevant banking financial institution to handle the operations.  
Local treasury operations are managed by the branches of the People's Bank of China. In areas where no branch of the People's Bank of China is established, the higher-level branch of the People's Bank of China shall, after consulting the relevant local government finance department, entrust the relevant banking financial institution to handle the operations.  
Townships, ethnic townships and towns that meet the conditions shall establish treasuries. The specific conditions and standards shall be determined by the finance departments of the governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　中央国库业务应当接受财政部的指导和监督，对中央财政负责。  
地方国库业务应当接受本级政府财政部门的指导和监督，对地方财政负责。  
省、自治区、直辖市制定的地方国库业务规程应当报财政部和中国人民银行备案。

**Article 63** Central treasury operations shall accept the guidance and supervision of the Ministry of Finance and be responsible to the central finance.  
Local treasury operations shall accept the guidance and supervision of the finance department of the government at the same level and be responsible to the local finance.  
The local treasury operation procedures formulated by provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be reported to the Ministry of Finance and the People's Bank of China for recordation.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　各级国库应当及时向本级政府财政部门编报预算收入入库、解库、库款拨付以及库款余额情况的日报、旬报、月报和年报。

**Article 64** All levels of treasury shall promptly compile and submit daily, ten-day, monthly and annual reports on the receipt, settlement, disbursement and balance of treasury funds to the finance department of the government at the same level.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　各级国库应当依照有关法律、行政法规、国务院以及财政部、中国人民银行的有关规定，加强对国库业务的管理，及时准确地办理预算收入的收纳、划分、留解、退付和预算支出的拨付。  
各级国库和有关银行业金融机构必须遵守国家有关预算收入缴库的规定，不得延解、占压应当缴入国库的预算收入和国库库款。

**Article 65** All levels of treasury shall, in accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations, and the relevant provisions of the State Council, the Ministry of Finance and the People's Bank of China, strengthen the management of treasury operations, and promptly and accurately handle the collection, division, retention, refund of budget revenues and the disbursement of budget expenditures.  
All levels of treasury and relevant banking financial institutions must comply with the state provisions on the payment of budget revenues into the treasury, and shall not delay or withhold the budget revenues that should be paid into the treasury or the treasury funds.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　各级国库必须凭本级政府财政部门签发的拨款凭证或者支付清算指令于当日办理资金拨付，并及时将款项转入收款单位的账户或者清算资金。  
各级国库和有关银行业金融机构不得占压财政部门拨付的预算资金。

**Article 66** All levels of treasury must, on the basis of the disbursement vouchers or payment settlement instructions issued by the finance department of the government at the same level, handle the fund disbursement on the same day, and promptly transfer the funds to the accounts of the receiving units or settle the funds.  
All levels of treasury and relevant banking financial institutions shall not withhold the budget funds disbursed by the finance department.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　各级政府财政部门、预算收入征收部门和单位、国库应当建立健全相互之间的预算收入对账制度，在预算执行中按月、按年核对预算收入的收纳以及库款拨付情况，保证预算收入的征收入库、库款拨付和库存金额准确无误。

**Article 67** The finance departments of all levels of government, the departments and units that collect budget revenues, and the treasury shall establish and improve a system for reconciling budget revenues among themselves, and reconcile the collection of budget revenues and the disbursement of treasury funds on a monthly and annual basis during the implementation of the budget, so as to ensure the accuracy of the collection and payment of budget revenues, the disbursement of treasury funds and the balance of treasury funds.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　中央预算收入、中央和地方预算共享收入退库的办法，由财政部制定。地方预算收入退库的办法，由省、自治区、直辖市政府财政部门制定。  
各级预算收入退库的审批权属于本级政府财政部门。中央预算收入、中央和地方预算共享收入的退库，由财政部或者财政部授权的机构批准。地方预算收入的退库，由地方政府财政部门或者其授权的机构批准。具体退库程序按照财政部的有关规定办理。  
办理预算收入退库，应当直接退给申请单位或者申请个人，按照国家规定用途使用。任何部门、单位和个人不得截留、挪用退库款项。

**Article 68** The measures for the refund of central budget revenues and revenues shared between the central and local budgets shall be formulated by the Ministry of Finance. The measures for the refund of local budget revenues shall be formulated by the finance departments of the governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
The power to approve the refund of budget revenues at all levels belongs to the finance department of the government at the same level. The refund of central budget revenues and revenues shared between the central and local budgets shall be approved by the Ministry of Finance or an institution authorized by the Ministry of Finance. The refund of local budget revenues shall be approved by the local government finance department or an institution authorized by it. The specific refund procedures shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Ministry of Finance.  
The refund of budget revenues shall be paid directly to the applying unit or individual, and shall be used in accordance with the purposes prescribed by the state. No department, unit or individual may intercept or misappropriate the refunded amounts.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　各级政府应当加强对本级国库的管理和监督，各级政府财政部门负责协调本级预算收入征收部门和单位与国库的业务工作。

**Article 69** All levels of government shall strengthen the management and supervision of the treasury at the same level. The finance departments of all levels of government shall be responsible for coordinating the business work between the departments and units that collect budget revenues at the same level and the treasury.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　国务院各部门制定的规章、文件，凡涉及减免应缴预算收入、设立和改变收入项目和标准、罚没财物处理、经费开支标准和范围、国有资产处置和收益分配以及会计核算等事项的，应当符合国家统一的规定；凡涉及增加或者减少财政收入或者支出的，应当征求财政部意见。

**Article 70** The rules and documents formulated by the departments of the State Council, if they involve matters such as the reduction or exemption of payable budget revenues, the establishment or change of revenue items and standards, the disposal of confiscated property, the standards and scope of expenditure, the disposal of state-owned assets and income distribution, and accounting, shall comply with the uniform provisions of the state; if they involve an increase or decrease in fiscal revenues or expenditures, the opinions of the Ministry of Finance shall be solicited.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　地方政府依据法定权限制定的规章和规定的行政措施，不得涉及减免中央预算收入、中央和地方预算共享收入，不得影响中央预算收入、中央和地方预算共享收入的征收；违反规定的，有关预算收入征收部门和单位有权拒绝执行，并应当向上级预算收入征收部门和单位以及财政部报告。

**Article 71** The rules and prescribed administrative measures formulated by local governments in accordance with their statutory powers shall not involve the reduction or exemption of central budget revenues or revenues shared between the central and local budgets, and shall not affect the collection of central budget revenues or revenues shared between the central and local budgets; if any provision is violated, the relevant departments and units that collect budget revenues shall have the right to refuse to implement it, and shall report to the higher-level departments and units that collect budget revenues and to the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　各级政府应当加强对预算执行工作的领导，定期听取财政部门有关预算执行情况的汇报，研究解决预算执行中出现的问题。

**Article 72** All levels of government shall strengthen the leadership over budget implementation, regularly listen to the reports of the finance department on the budget implementation situation, and study and solve problems arising in budget implementation.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　各级政府财政部门有权监督本级各部门及其所属各单位的预算管理有关工作，对各部门的预算执行情况和绩效进行评价、考核。  
各级政府财政部门有权对与本级各预算收入相关的征收部门和单位征收本级预算收入的情况进行监督，对违反法律、行政法规规定多征、提前征收、减征、免征、缓征或者退还预算收入的，责令改正。

**Article 73** The finance departments of all levels of government have the power to supervise the budget management related work of all departments at the same level and their subordinate units, and to evaluate and assess the budget implementation and performance of all departments.  
The finance departments of all levels of government have the power to supervise the collection of budget revenues at the same level by the collection departments and units related to the budget revenues at the same level, and to order the correction of any over-collection, advance collection, reduction, exemption, deferral or refund of budget revenues in violation of laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　各级政府财政部门应当每月向本级政府报告预算执行情况，具体报告内容、方式和期限由本级政府规定。

**Article 74** The finance departments of all levels of government shall report the budget implementation situation to the government at the same level on a monthly basis. The specific content, method and time limit of the report shall be prescribed by the government at the same level.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　地方各级政府财政部门应当定期向上一级政府财政部门报送本行政区域预算执行情况，包括预算执行旬报、月报、季报，政府债务余额统计报告，国库库款报告以及相关文字说明材料。具体报送内容、方式和期限由上一级政府财政部门规定。

**Article 75** The finance departments of local governments at all levels shall regularly submit the budget implementation situation of their administrative regions to the finance department of the higher-level government, including ten-day, monthly and quarterly reports on budget implementation, statistical reports on the outstanding balance of government debts, reports on treasury funds, and related written explanatory materials. The specific content, method and time limit for submission shall be prescribed by the finance department of the higher-level government.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　各级税务、海关等预算收入征收部门和单位应当按照财政部门规定的期限和要求，向财政部门和上级主管部门报送有关预算收入征收情况，并附文字说明材料。  
各级税务、海关等预算收入征收部门和单位应当与相关财政部门建立收入征管信息共享机制。

**Article 76** All levels of tax authorities, customs and other departments and units that collect budget revenues shall, in accordance with the time limits and requirements prescribed by the finance department, submit to the finance department and the higher-level competent department the information on the collection of budget revenues, together with written explanatory materials.  
All levels of tax authorities, customs and other departments and units that collect budget revenues shall establish an information sharing mechanism for revenue collection and management with the relevant finance departments.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　各部门应当按照本级政府财政部门规定的期限和要求，向本级政府财政部门报送本部门及其所属各单位的预算收支情况等报表和文字说明材料。

**Article 77** All departments shall, in accordance with the time limits and requirements prescribed by the finance department of the government at the same level, submit to the finance department of the government at the same level the statements and written explanatory materials on the budget revenues and expenditures of their departments and subordinate units.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　[预算法第六十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-66)第一款所称超收收入，是指年度本级一般公共预算收入的实际完成数超过经本级人民代表大会或者其常务委员会批准的预算收入数的部分。  
[预算法第六十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-66)第三款所称短收，是指年度本级一般公共预算收入的实际完成数小于经本级人民代表大会或者其常务委员会批准的预算收入数的情形。  
前两款所称实际完成数和预算收入数，不包括转移性收入和政府债务收入。  
省、自治区、直辖市政府依照[预算法第六十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-66)第三款规定增列的赤字，可以通过在国务院下达的本地区政府债务限额内发行地方政府一般债券予以平衡。  
设区的市、自治州以下各级一般公共预算年度执行中出现短收的，应当通过调入预算稳定调节基金或者其他预算资金、减少支出等方式实现收支平衡；采取上述措施仍不能实现收支平衡的，可以通过申请上级政府临时救助平衡当年预算，并在下一年度预算中安排资金归还。  
各级一般公共预算年度执行中厉行节约、节约开支，造成本级预算支出实际执行数小于预算总支出的，不属于预算调整的情形。  
各级政府性基金预算年度执行中有超收收入的，应当在下一年度安排使用并优先用于偿还相应的专项债务；出现短收的，应当通过减少支出实现收支平衡。国务院另有规定的除外。  
各级国有资本经营预算年度执行中有超收收入的，应当在下一年度安排使用；出现短收的，应当通过减少支出实现收支平衡。国务院另有规定的除外。

**Article 78** The term "excess revenue" as mentioned in Paragraph 1 of Article 66 of the Budget Law refers to the portion by which the actual completed amount of annual general public budget revenues at the same level exceeds the budgeted revenue amount approved by the people's congress at the same level or its standing committee.  
The term "revenue shortfall" as mentioned in Paragraph 3 of Article 66 of the Budget Law refers to the situation where the actual completed amount of annual general public budget revenues at the same level is less than the budgeted revenue amount approved by the people's congress at the same level or its standing committee.  
The actual completed amount and the budgeted revenue amount mentioned in the preceding two paragraphs do not include transfer income and government debt income.  
The deficit increased by the governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government in accordance with the provisions of Paragraph 3 of Article 66 of the Budget Law may be balanced by issuing local government general bonds within the government debt limits for their regions issued by the State Council.  
If a revenue shortfall occurs during the annual implementation of the general public budget at the level of a city divided into districts, an autonomous prefecture or below, it shall be balanced by transferring in funds from the budget stabilization and adjustment fund or other budget funds, reducing expenditures, etc.; if balance still cannot be achieved after taking the above measures, the current year's budget may be balanced by applying for temporary assistance from a higher-level government, and funds shall be arranged for repayment in the budget of the following year.  
If the actual implementation amount of budget expenditures at the same level is less than the total budgeted expenditures due to strict economy and thrift in the annual implementation of the general public budget at all levels, this shall not constitute a budget adjustment.  
If there is excess revenue during the annual implementation of the government-managed fund budget at all levels, it shall be arranged for use in the following year and given priority for repaying the corresponding special debts; if a revenue shortfall occurs, balance shall be achieved by reducing expenditures, unless otherwise provided by the State Council.  
If there is excess revenue during the annual implementation of the state capital operation budget at all levels, it shall be arranged for use in the following year; if a revenue shortfall occurs, balance shall be achieved by reducing expenditures, unless otherwise provided by the State Council.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　年度预算确定后，部门、单位改变隶属关系引起预算级次或者预算关系变化的，应当在改变财务关系的同时，相应办理预算、资产划转。

**Article 79** After the annual budget is determined, if a change in the affiliation of a department or unit results in a change in the budget level or budget relationship, the transfer of budgets and assets shall be handled accordingly at the same time as the change in financial relationship.

### 第五章　决算

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　[预算法第七十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-74)所称决算草案，是指各级政府、各部门、各单位编制的未经法定程序审查和批准的预算收支和结余的年度执行结果。

**Article 80** The term "draft final accounts" as mentioned in Article 74 of the Budget Law refers to the annual implementation results of budget revenues, expenditures and balances compiled by all levels of government, all departments and all units that have not been examined and approved through legal procedures.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　财政部应当在每年第四季度部署编制决算草案的原则、要求、方法和报送期限，制发中央各部门决算、地方决算以及其他有关决算的报表格式。  
省、自治区、直辖市政府按照国务院的要求和财政部的部署，结合本地区的具体情况，提出本行政区域编制决算草案的要求。  
县级以上地方政府财政部门根据财政部的部署和省、自治区、直辖市政府的要求，部署编制本级政府各部门和下级政府决算草案的原则、要求、方法和报送期限，制发本级政府各部门决算、下级政府决算以及其他有关决算的报表格式。

**Article 81** The Ministry of Finance shall, in the fourth quarter of each year, make arrangements for the principles, requirements, methods and time limits for submitting the compilation of draft final accounts, and issue the statement formats for the final accounts of central departments, local final accounts and other relevant final accounts.  
The governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the requirements of the State Council and the arrangements of the Ministry of Finance, and taking into account the specific circumstances of their regions, put forward the requirements for the compilation of draft final accounts within their administrative regions.  
The finance departments of local governments at or above the county level shall, based on the arrangements of the Ministry of Finance and the requirements of the governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, make arrangements for the principles, requirements, methods and time limits for submitting the compilation of draft final accounts of the departments of the government at the same level and lower-level governments, and issue the statement formats for the final accounts of the departments of the government at the same level, the final accounts of lower-level governments and other relevant final accounts.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　地方政府财政部门根据上级政府财政部门的部署，制定本行政区域决算草案和本级各部门决算草案的具体编制办法。  
各部门根据本级政府财政部门的部署，制定所属各单位决算草案的具体编制办法。

**Article 82** The finance departments of local governments shall, based on the arrangements of the finance department of the higher-level government, formulate the specific methods for compiling the draft final accounts of their administrative regions and the draft final accounts of the departments at the same level.  
All departments shall, based on the arrangements of the finance department of the government at the same level, formulate the specific methods for compiling the draft final accounts of their subordinate units.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　各级政府财政部门、各部门、各单位在每一预算年度终了时，应当清理核实全年预算收入、支出数据和往来款项，做好决算数据对账工作。  
决算各项数据应当以经核实的各级政府、各部门、各单位会计数据为准，不得以估计数据替代，不得弄虚作假。  
各部门、各单位决算应当列示结转、结余资金。

**Article 83** At the end of each budget year, the finance departments of all levels of government, all departments and all units shall clear and verify the annual budget revenue and expenditure data and the current account items, and properly carry out the reconciliation of final account data.  
All data in the final accounts shall be based on the verified accounting data of all levels of government, all departments and all units; estimated data shall not be used as substitutes, and no falsification shall be allowed.  
The final accounts of all departments and units shall show the carry-over funds and surplus funds.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　各单位应当按照主管部门的布置，认真编制本单位决算草案，在规定期限内上报。  
各部门在审核汇总所属各单位决算草案基础上，连同本部门自身的决算收入和支出数据，汇编成本部门决算草案并附详细说明，经部门负责人签章后，在规定期限内报本级政府财政部门审核。

**Article 84** All units shall, in accordance with the arrangements of the competent department, carefully compile their own draft final accounts and submit them within the prescribed time limit.  
All departments shall, on the basis of examining and consolidating the draft final accounts of their subordinate units, together with the final account revenue and expenditure data of their own departments, compile the draft final accounts of their own departments together with detailed explanations, and after being signed by the person in charge of the department, report them to the finance department of the government at the same level for examination within the prescribed time limit.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　各级预算收入征收部门和单位应当按照财政部门的要求，及时编制收入年报以及有关资料并报送财政部门。

**Article 85** All levels of departments and units that collect budget revenues shall, in accordance with the requirements of the finance department, promptly compile annual revenue reports and relevant materials and submit them to the finance department.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　各级政府财政部门应当根据本级预算、预算会计核算数据等相关资料编制本级决算草案。

**Article 86** The finance departments of all levels of government shall compile the draft final accounts at the same level based on the budget at the same level, budget accounting data and other relevant materials.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　年度预算执行终了，对于上下级财政之间按照规定需要清算的事项，应当在决算时办理结算。  
县级以上各级政府财政部门编制的决算草案应当及时报送本级政府审计部门审计。

**Article 87** At the end of the annual budget implementation, for matters that need to be settled between the higher and lower levels of finance according to regulations, the settlement shall be handled at the time of final accounts.  
The draft final accounts compiled by the finance departments of governments at or above the county level shall be promptly submitted to the audit department of the government at the same level for audit.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　县级以上地方各级政府应当自本级决算经批准之日起30日内，将本级决算以及下一级政府上报备案的决算汇总，报上一级政府备案；将下一级政府报送备案的决算汇总，报本级人民代表大会常务委员会备案。  
乡、民族乡、镇政府应当自本级决算经批准之日起30日内，将本级决算报上一级政府备案。

**Article 88** Local governments at or above the county level shall, within 30 days from the date their final accounts are approved, consolidate their own final accounts and the final accounts submitted by the governments at the next lower level for recordation, and report them to the higher-level government for recordation; and consolidate the final accounts submitted by the governments at the next lower level for recordation and report them to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level for recordation.  
The governments of townships, ethnic townships and towns shall, within 30 days from the date their final accounts are approved, report their own final accounts to the higher-level government for recordation.

### 第六章　监督

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　县级以上各级政府应当接受本级和上级人民代表大会及其常务委员会对预算执行情况和决算的监督，乡、民族乡、镇政府应当接受本级人民代表大会和上级人民代表大会及其常务委员会对预算执行情况和决算的监督；按照本级人民代表大会或者其常务委员会的要求，报告预算执行情况；认真研究处理本级人民代表大会代表或者其常务委员会组成人员有关改进预算管理的建议、批评和意见，并及时答复。

**Article 89** Governments at or above the county level shall accept the supervision of the people's congress at the same level and the higher-level people's congress and their standing committees over the budget implementation and final accounts. The governments of townships, ethnic townships and towns shall accept the supervision of the people's congress at the same level and the higher-level people's congress and its standing committee over the budget implementation and final accounts; they shall report the budget implementation situation in accordance with the requirements of the people's congress at the same level or its standing committee; they shall seriously study and handle the suggestions, criticisms and opinions of the deputies to the people's congress at the same level or the members of its standing committee on improving budget management, and shall promptly respond.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　各级政府应当加强对下级政府预算执行情况的监督，对下级政府在预算执行中违反预算法、本条例和国家方针政策的行为，依法予以制止和纠正；对本级预算执行中出现的问题，及时采取处理措施。  
下级政府应当接受上级政府对预算执行情况的监督；根据上级政府的要求，及时提供资料，如实反映情况，不得隐瞒、虚报；严格执行上级政府作出的有关决定，并将执行结果及时上报。

**Article 90** All levels of government shall strengthen the supervision over the budget implementation of lower-level governments, and shall, according to law, stop and correct the acts of lower-level governments that violate the Budget Law, these Regulations and the state's principles and policies during budget implementation; and shall promptly take measures to deal with problems arising in the budget implementation at the same level.  
Lower-level governments shall accept the supervision of higher-level governments over budget implementation; they shall, in accordance with the requirements of the higher-level government, promptly provide information and truthfully report the situation, and shall not conceal or make false reports; they shall strictly implement the relevant decisions made by the higher-level government, and shall promptly report the results of implementation to the higher-level government.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　各部门及其所属各单位应当接受本级政府财政部门对预算管理有关工作的监督。  
财政部派出机构根据职责和财政部的授权，依法开展工作。

**Article 91** All departments and their subordinate units shall accept the supervision of the finance department of the government at the same level over the work related to budget management.  
The dispatched agencies of the Ministry of Finance shall work according to law in accordance with their functions and the authorization of the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　各级政府审计部门应当依法对本级预算执行情况和决算草案，本级各部门、各单位和下级政府的预算执行情况和决算，进行审计监督。

**Article 92** The audit departments of all levels of government shall, according to law, conduct audit supervision over the budget implementation and draft final accounts at the same level, and over the budget implementation and final accounts of all departments and units at the same level and lower-level governments.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　[预算法第九十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-93)第六项所称违反本法规定冻结、动用国库库款或者以其他方式支配已入国库库款，是指：  
（一）未经有关政府财政部门同意，冻结、动用国库库款；  
（二）预算收入征收部门和单位违反规定将所收税款和其他预算收入存入国库之外的其他账户；  
（三）未经有关政府财政部门或者财政部门授权的机构同意，办理资金拨付和退付；  
（四）将国库库款挪作他用；  
（五）延解、占压国库库款；  
（六）占压政府财政部门拨付的预算资金。

**Article 93** The act of freezing or using treasury funds or otherwise disposing of funds already deposited in the treasury in violation of the provisions of the Budget Law as mentioned in Item 6 of Article 93 of the Budget Law refers to:  
(1) freezing or using treasury funds without the consent of the relevant government finance department;  
(2) the departments and units that collect budget revenues, in violation of regulations, depositing the collected taxes and other budget revenues into accounts other than the treasury;  
(3) handling fund disbursement and refund without the consent of the relevant government finance department or an institution authorized by the finance department;  
(4) misappropriating treasury funds for other purposes;  
(5) delaying or withholding treasury funds;  
(6) withholding the budget funds disbursed by the government finance department.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　各级政府、有关部门和单位有下列行为之一的，责令改正；对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分：  
（一）突破一般债务限额或者专项债务限额举借债务；  
（二）违反本条例规定下达转移支付预算或者拨付转移支付资金；  
（三）擅自开设、变更账户。

**Article 94** If any level of government, relevant department or unit commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to correct; and the person in charge with direct responsibility and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) borrowing debts exceeding the limit on general debts or the limit on special debts;  
(2) issuing transfer payment budgets or disbursing transfer payment funds in violation of the provisions of these Regulations;  
(3) opening or changing accounts without authorization.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　[预算法第九十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国预算法.md#art-97)所称政府综合财务报告，是指以权责发生制为基础编制的反映各级政府整体财务状况、运行情况和财政中长期可持续性的报告。政府综合财务报告包括政府资产负债表、收入费用表等财务报表和报表附注，以及以此为基础进行的综合分析等。

**Article 95** The term "government comprehensive financial report" as mentioned in Article 97 of the Budget Law refers to a report compiled on an accrual basis reflecting the overall financial position, operating performance and medium- and long-term fiscal sustainability of all levels of government. The government comprehensive financial report includes financial statements such as the government balance sheet and the income and expense statement, together with notes to the financial statements, as well as comprehensive analysis conducted on this basis, etc.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　政府投资年度计划应当和本级预算相衔接。政府投资决策、项目实施和监督管理按照政府投资有关行政法规执行。

**Article 96** The annual government investment plan shall be aligned with the budget at the same level. Government investment decision-making, project implementation and supervision and management shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant administrative regulations on government investment.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　本条例自2020年10月1日起施行。

**Article 97** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 1, 2020.
