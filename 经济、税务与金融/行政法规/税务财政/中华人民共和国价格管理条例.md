# 中华人民共和国价格管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1987-09-10

> (1987年9月11日国务院发布) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了贯彻执行国家的价格方针、政策，加强价格管理，保持市场价格的基本稳定，安定人民生活，保障经济体制改革的顺利进行，促进社会主义有计划商品经济的发展，制定本条例。 第二条　价格管理应当在保障国家利益的前提下，保护生产者、经营者和消费者的合法经济利益，正确处理中央、地方、部门、企业相互之间的经济利益关系。 第三条　国家对价格管理采取直接管理和间接控制相结合的原则，实行国家定价、国家指导价和市场调节价三种价格形式。 第四条　国家对价格工作实行统一领导、分级管理。 各级人民政府物价管理机关(以下简称物价部门)，各级人民政府有关业务主管部门以及企业、事业单位价格管理机构和人员，应当严格遵守国家价格法规和政策，做好价格管理和监督工作。 第二章　价格的制定和管理 第五条　本条例所指的价格包括： (一)各类商品的价格； (二)各种经营性服务的收费标准(以下简称收费标准)。 第六条　商品价格构成包括生产商品的社会平均成本、税金、利润以及正常的流通费用。 第七条　制定、调整实行国家定价和国家指导价的商品价格，应当接近商品价值，反映供求状况，符合国家政策要求，并且遵循下列原则： (一)各类商品价格应当保持合理的比价关系； (二)应当有明确的质量标准或者等级规格标准，实行按质定价；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了贯彻执行国家的价格方针、政策，加强价格管理，保持市场价格的基本稳定，安定人民生活，保障经济体制改革的顺利进行，促进社会主义有计划商品经济的发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of implementing the state's pricing principles and policies, strengthening price administration, maintaining basic stability of market prices, stabilizing the people's livelihood, ensuring the smooth progress of economic restructuring, and promoting the development of a socialist planned commodity economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　价格管理应当在保障国家利益的前提下，保护生产者、经营者和消费者的合法经济利益，正确处理中央、地方、部门、企业相互之间的经济利益关系。

**Article 2** Price administration shall, on the premise of safeguarding state interests, protect the lawful economic interests of producers, operators and consumers, and correctly handle the economic interest relations among the central authorities, local authorities, departments and enterprises.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对价格管理采取直接管理和间接控制相结合的原则，实行国家定价、国家指导价和市场调节价三种价格形式。

**Article 3** The state shall adopt the principle of combining direct administration with indirect control in price administration, and implement three price forms: state-fixed prices, state-guided prices and market-regulated prices.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对价格工作实行统一领导、分级管理。  
各级人民政府物价管理机关(以下简称物价部门)，各级人民政府有关业务主管部门以及企业、事业单位价格管理机构和人员，应当严格遵守国家价格法规和政策，做好价格管理和监督工作。

**Article 4** The state shall exercise unified leadership and administration at different levels over price work.  
The price administration authorities of the people's governments at various levels (hereinafter referred to as the price departments), the relevant competent business departments of the people's governments at various levels, and the price administration organs and personnel of enterprises and institutions shall strictly observe the state's price regulations and policies, and do a good job in price administration and supervision.

### 第二章　价格的制定和管理

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　本条例所指的价格包括：  
(一)各类商品的价格；  
(二)各种经营性服务的收费标准(以下简称收费标准)。

**Article 5** Prices referred to in these Regulations include:  
(1) prices of various commodities;  
(2) fees and charges for various operational services (hereinafter referred to as fee rates).

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　商品价格构成包括生产商品的社会平均成本、税金、利润以及正常的流通费用。

**Article 6** The components of commodity prices include the social average cost of production, taxes, profits and normal circulation expenses.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　制定、调整实行国家定价和国家指导价的商品价格，应当接近商品价值，反映供求状况，符合国家政策要求，并且遵循下列原则：  
(一)各类商品价格应当保持合理的比价关系；  
(二)应当有明确的质量标准或者等级规格标准，实行按质定价；  
(三)在减少流通环节、降低流通费用的前提下，实行合理的购销差价、批零差价、地区差价和季节差价。

**Article 7** The fixing and adjustment of commodity prices under state-fixed prices and state-guided prices shall be close to the value of the commodities, reflect supply and demand conditions, conform to the requirements of state policies, and observe the following principles:  
(1) prices of various commodities shall maintain reasonable price relations;  
(2) there shall be clear quality standards or grade specifications, and pricing according to quality shall be implemented;  
(3) on the premise of reducing circulation links and lowering circulation expenses, reasonable purchase-sale differentials, wholesale-retail differentials, regional differentials and seasonal differentials shall be implemented.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家定价是指由县级以上(含县级，以下同)各级人民政府物价部门、业务主管部门按照国家规定权限制定的商品价格和收费标准。  
国家指导价是指由县级以上各级人民政府物价部门、业务主管部门按照国家规定权限，通过规定基准价和浮动幅度、差率、利润率、最高限价和最低保护价等，指导企业制定的商品价格和收费标准。  
市场调节价是指由生产者、经营者制定的商品价格和收费标准。

**Article 8** State-fixed prices mean the commodity prices and fee rates fixed by the price departments and competent business departments of the people's governments at or above the county level (including the county level, the same below) within the limits of authority prescribed by the state.  
State-guided prices mean the commodity prices and fee rates guided by the price departments and competent business departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, within the limits of authority prescribed by the state, through stipulating base prices and floating ranges, differential ratios, profit rates, ceiling prices and floor protection prices, and guiding enterprises to fix the same.  
Market-regulated prices mean the commodity prices and fee rates fixed by producers and operators.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　实行国家定价、国家指导价的商品分工管理目录、收费项目分工管理目录，由国家物价部门和国家物价部门授权省、自治区、直辖市人民政府物价部门制定、调整。

**Article 9** The catalogs of commodities under the administration of state-fixed prices and state-guided prices managed separately by different departments and the catalogs of fee items managed separately by different departments shall be formulated and adjusted by the State Pricing Department and the pricing departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government authorized by the State Pricing Department.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　制定、调整商品价格和收费标准，必须按照国家规定的权限和程序执行。任何地区、部门、单位和个人，都不得超越权限擅自制定、调整商品价格和收费标准。

**Article 10** The fixing and adjustment of commodity prices and fee rates must be carried out according to the limits of authority and procedures prescribed by the state. No region, department, unit or individual may exceed the limits of authority and arbitrarily fix or adjust commodity prices or fee rates.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院有关业务主管部门、地方各级人民政府应当掌握市场商品价格信息，通过国营工商企业、物资供销企业、供销社组织货源，参与市场调节，平抑市场商品价格。在市场调节价出现暴涨暴落时，物价部门可以在一定时期内对部分商品价格规定最高限价、最低保护价和提价申报制度。

**Article 11** The relevant competent business departments under the State Council and the local people's governments at various levels shall keep abreast of market commodity price information, organize sources of goods through state-owned industrial and commercial enterprises, material supply and marketing enterprises, and supply and marketing cooperatives, participate in market regulation, and stabilize market commodity prices. When market-regulated prices skyrocket or plunge, the price departments may, within a certain period, prescribe ceiling prices, floor protection prices and a system of reporting price increases for some commodity prices.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　物价部门应当对城乡集贸市场和个体工商户的价格加强管理和监督。

**Article 12** The price departments shall strengthen the administration and supervision over the prices of urban and rural fairs and individual industrial and commercial households.

### 第三章　价格管理职责

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家物价部门在价格管理方面履行下列职责：  
(一)研究拟订国家的价格方针、政策、计划和改革方案，经国务院批准后组织实施；  
(二)研究拟订价格法规草案；  
(三)负责全国的价格管理和综合平衡工作；  
(四)按照价格管理权限，规定商品和收费的作价原则、作价办法，制定、调整分管的商品价格和收费标准，重要的商品价格和收费标准的制定、调整，应当会商国务院有关业务主管部门后报国务院批准；  
(五)指导、监督国务院业务主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的价格工作，检查、处理违反价格法规和政策的行为(以下简称价格违法行为)；  
(六)协调、处理国务院业务主管部门之间，省、自治区、直辖市之间，国务院业务主管部门与省、自治区、直辖市之间的价格争议；  
(七)建立全国价格信息网络，开展价格信息服务工作；  
(八)国务院赋予的其他职责。

**Article 13** The State Pricing Department shall perform the following functions in price administration:  
(1) studying and drafting the state's pricing principles, policies, plans and reform programs, and organizing their implementation after approval by the State Council;  
(2) studying and drafting drafts of price regulations;  
(3) being responsible for nationwide price administration and overall balance work;  
(4) stipulating, in accordance with the limits of authority over price administration, the pricing principles and methods for commodities and fees, fixing and adjusting the commodity prices and fee rates under its jurisdiction, and the fixing and adjustment of important commodity prices and fee rates shall be submitted to the State Council for approval after consultation with the relevant competent business departments under the State Council;  
(5) guiding and supervising the price work of the competent business departments under the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, inspecting and handling acts violating price regulations and policies (hereinafter referred to as illegal pricing acts);  
(6) coordinating and handling price disputes among the competent business departments under the State Council, among provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and between the competent business departments under the State Council and provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(7) establishing a nationwide price information network and carrying out price information services;  
(8) other functions assigned by the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院业务主管部门在价格管理方面履行下列职责：  
(一)负责组织、监督本系统、本行业贯彻执行国家的价格方针、政策和法规；  
(二)按照价格管理权限，规定商品和收费的作价原则、作价办法，制定、调整分管的商品价格和收费标准；  
(三)组织、监督本系统、本行业执行规定的商品价格和收费标准；  
(四)指导本系统、本行业价格工作，协调、处理本系统、本行业内的价格争议，协助物价检查机构查处价格违法行为；  
(五)对国家物价部门管理的商品价格和收费标准提供有关资料，提出价格调整方案；  
(六)建立本系统、本行业的价格信息网络，开展价格信息服务工作。

**Article 14** The competent business departments under the State Council shall perform the following functions in price administration:  
(1) being responsible for organizing and supervising the implementation of the state's pricing principles, policies and regulations by their own systems and trades;  
(2) stipulating, in accordance with the limits of authority over price administration, the pricing principles and methods for commodities and fees, and fixing and adjusting the commodity prices and fee rates under their jurisdiction;  
(3) organizing and supervising the implementation of the prescribed commodity prices and fee rates by their own systems and trades;  
(4) guiding the price work of their own systems and trades, coordinating and handling price disputes within their own systems and trades, and assisting price inspection organs in investigating and dealing with illegal pricing acts;  
(5) providing relevant data on commodity prices and fee rates administered by the State Pricing Department, and submitting proposals for price adjustment;  
(6) establishing price information networks for their own systems and trades, and carrying out price information services.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府物价部门在价格管理方面履行下列职责：  
(一)贯彻执行国家的价格方针、政策和法规；  
(二)组织、监督有关部门实施国家物价部门和国务院业务主管部门制定的商品价格和收费标准；  
(三)负责本地区的价格管理和综合平衡工作，会同有关部门拟订本地区价格计划草案，经批准后组织实施；  
(四)按照价格管理权限，规定商品和收费的作价原则、作价办法，制定、调整分管的商品价格和收费标准，重要的商品价格和收费标准应当报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，并报国家物价部门和国务院有关业务主管部门备案；  
(五)指导、监督同级业务主管部门、下级人民政府以及本地区内企业、事业单位的价格工作，检查、处理价格违法行为；  
(六)协调、处理本地区内的价格争议；  
(七)建立本地区价格信息网络，开展价格信息服务工作；  
(八)省、自治区、直辖市人民政府赋予的其他职责。

**Article 15** The pricing departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall perform the following functions in price administration:  
(1) implementing the state's pricing principles, policies and regulations;  
(2) organizing and supervising the relevant departments in implementing the commodity prices and fee rates fixed by the State Pricing Department and the competent business departments under the State Council;  
(3) being responsible for price administration and overall balance work in their own regions, drafting price plans for their own regions in conjunction with the relevant departments, and organizing implementation after approval;  
(4) stipulating, in accordance with the limits of authority over price administration, the pricing principles and methods for commodities and fees, fixing and adjusting the commodity prices and fee rates under their jurisdiction, and important commodity prices and fee rates shall be submitted to the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government for approval and reported to the State Pricing Department and the relevant competent business departments under the State Council for the record;  
(5) guiding and supervising the price work of the competent business departments at the same level, the people's governments at lower levels, and the enterprises and institutions in their own regions, inspecting and dealing with illegal pricing acts;  
(6) coordinating and handling price disputes within their own regions;  
(7) establishing price information networks for their own regions and carrying out price information services;  
(8) other functions assigned by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府业务主管部门和市、县人民政府的物价部门、业务主管部门的价格管理职责，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府参照本章的有关条款规定。

**Article 16** The price administration functions of the competent business departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the pricing departments and competent business departments of the people's governments of cities and counties shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government by reference to the relevant articles of this Chapter.

### 第四章　企业的价格权利和义务

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　企业在价格方面享有下列权利：  
(一)对实行国家指导价的商品和收费项目，按照有关规定制定商品价格和收费标准；  
(二)制定实行市场调节价的商品价格和收费标准；  
(三)对经有关部门鉴定确认、物价部门批准实行优质加价的产品，在规定的加价幅度内制定商品价格，按照规定权限确定残损废次商品的处理价格；  
(四)在国家规定期限内制定新产品的试销价格；  
(五)对实行国家定价、国家指导价的商品价格和收费标准的制定、调整提出建议。

**Article 17** Enterprises shall enjoy the following rights with respect to prices:  
(1) fixing commodity prices and fee rates for commodities and fee items under state-guided prices in accordance with relevant provisions;  
(2) fixing commodity prices and fee rates under market-regulated prices;  
(3) fixing commodity prices within the prescribed scope of price increases for products that have been verified and confirmed by the relevant departments and approved by the price departments for quality premium pricing, and determining the disposal prices of damaged, defective, spoiled and substandard commodities within the limits of authority prescribed;  
(4) fixing trial sale prices for new products within the period prescribed by the state;  
(5) making proposals on the fixing and adjustment of commodity prices and fee rates under state-fixed prices and state-guided prices.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　企业在价格方面应当履行下列义务：  
(一)遵照执行国家的价格方针、政策和法规，执行国家定价、国家指导价；  
(二)如实上报实行国家定价、国家指导价的商品和收费项目的有关定价资料；  
(三)服从物价部门的价格管理，接受价格监督检查，如实提供价格检查所必需的成本、帐簿等有关资料；  
(四)执行物价部门规定的商品价格和收费标准的申报、备案制度；  
(五)零售商业、饮食行业、服务行业等，必须按照规定明码标价。

**Article 18** Enterprises shall perform the following obligations with respect to prices:  
(1) observing and implementing the state's pricing principles, policies and regulations, and implementing state-fixed prices and state-guided prices;  
(2) truthfully reporting the relevant pricing data on commodities and fee items under state-fixed prices and state-guided prices;  
(3) submitting to the price administration of the price departments, accepting price supervision and inspection, and truthfully providing the cost, account books and other relevant data necessary for price inspection;  
(4) implementing the system of reporting and filing commodity prices and fee rates as prescribed by the price departments;  
(5) retail businesses, catering trades, service trades, etc. must clearly mark the prices according to regulations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　事业单位、个体工商户在价格方面的权利和义务，参照[本条例第十七条](#art-17)、第十八条的规定执行。

**Article 19** The rights and obligations of institutions, individual industrial and commercial households with respect to prices shall be implemented by reference to the provisions of Articles 17 and 18 of these Regulations.

### 第五章　价格监督检查

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　各级物价部门的物价检查机构，依法行使价格监督检查和处理价格违法行为的职权。对同级人民政府业务主管部门、下级人民政府以及本地区内的企业、事业单位和个体工商户执行价格法规、政策进行监督检查。

**Article 20** The price inspection organs of the price departments at various levels shall, according to law, exercise the power to supervise and inspect prices and deal with illegal pricing acts. They shall supervise and inspect the implementation of price regulations and policies by the competent business departments of the people's governments at the same level, the people's governments at lower levels, and the enterprises, institutions and individual industrial and commercial households within their regions.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　物价检查机构受上级物价检查机构的业务指导。地方各级物价检查机构主要负责人的任免，应当事前征得上一级物价部门的同意。

**Article 21** The price inspection organs shall be subject to the professional guidance of the superior price inspection organs. The appointment and removal of the principal leading members of the local price inspection organs at various levels shall be subject to the prior consent of the price departments at the next higher level.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　物价检查机构应当依靠和发动群众监督检查价格，协同工会和街道办事处组织职工价格监督站和群众价格监督站，开展群众性的价格监督检查活动。  
物价部门要发挥消费者协会监督价格的作用，依法查处消费者协会反映的价格违法行为。

**Article 22** The price inspection organs shall rely on and mobilize the masses to supervise and inspect prices, and shall, in coordination with the trade unions and subdistrict offices, organize workers' price supervision stations and mass price supervision stations, and carry out mass price supervision and inspection activities.  
The price departments shall bring into play the role of consumer associations in supervising prices, and according to law investigate and deal with illegal pricing acts reported by consumer associations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　群众价格监督组织监督检查的重点，应当是同人民生活关系密切的消费品价格和服务收费标准。  
群众价格监督人员进行价格监督检查活动时，应当佩带标志，出示价格检查证。

**Article 23** The focus of supervision and inspection by mass price supervision organizations shall be the prices of consumer goods and service fee rates that are closely related to the people's livelihood.  
When carrying out price supervision and inspection activities, mass price supervisors shall wear badges and present their price inspection certificates.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　地方各级人民政府应当加强对价格监督检查工作的领导，组织有关部门和社会有关方面人员定期或者不定期地对价格法规、政策的执行情况进行监督检查。

**Article 24** The local people's governments at various levels shall strengthen the leadership over price supervision and inspection work, and organize the relevant departments and relevant social personnel to conduct regular or ad hoc supervision and inspection of the implementation of price regulations and policies.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　工商行政管理、审计、财政、税务、公安、标准、计量以及银行等部门，应当积极配合物价检查机构做好价格监督检查和处理价格违法行为的工作。

**Article 25** The departments of industrial and commercial administration, audit, finance, taxation, public security, standards, metrology and banking shall actively cooperate with the price inspection organs in doing a good job of price supervision and inspection and dealing with illegal pricing acts.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对价格违法行为，任何单位和个人都有权检举揭发。物价检查机构应当为检举者保密，并按规定对检举揭发或者协助查处价格违法行为有功人员给予奖励。  
对群众价格监督组织中工作有成绩者，应当按规定给予奖励。

**Article 26** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report and expose illegal pricing acts. The price inspection organs shall keep the informants confidential, and shall, according to regulations, reward those who have rendered meritorious service in reporting or exposing illegal pricing acts or assisting in investigating and dealing with such acts.  
Those who have achieved successes in the mass price supervision organizations shall be rewarded according to regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对检举揭发或者查处价格违法行为者进行打击、报复的，依法追究责任。

**Article 27** Anyone who retaliates against or takes revenge on persons who report, expose or investigate and deal with illegal pricing acts shall be investigated for responsibility according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　物价检查人员必须依法办事。对滥用职权、贪污受贿、徇私舞弊、玩忽职守的，依照国家有关规定给予处分；情节严重，构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** Price inspection personnel must act according to law. Those who abuse their power, engage in embezzlement or bribery, practice favoritism or commit dereliction of duty shall be punished in accordance with the relevant state provisions; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated by the judicial organs according to law.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　下列行为属于价格违法行为：  
(一)不执行国家定价收购、销售商品或者收取费用的；  
(二)违反国家指导价的定价原则，制定、调整商品价格或者收费标准的；  
(三)抬级抬价、压级压价的；  
(四)违反规定将计划内生产资料转为计划外高价出售的；  
(五)将定量内供应城镇居民的商品按议价销售的；  
(六)违反规定层层加价销售商品的；  
(七)自立名目滥收费用的；  
(八)采取以次充好、短尺少秤、降低质量等手段，变相提高商品价格或者收费标准的；  
(九)企业之间或者行业组织商定垄断价格的；  
(十)不执行提价申报制度的；  
(十一)不按规定明码标价的；  
(十二)泄露国家价格机密的；  
(十三)其他违反价格法规、政策的行为。

**Article 29** The following acts constitute illegal pricing acts:  
(1) failing to implement state-fixed prices in purchasing or selling commodities or collecting fees;  
(2) violating the pricing principles of state-guided prices in fixing or adjusting commodity prices or fee rates;  
(3) raising prices by raising grades or lowering prices by lowering grades;  
(4) transferring means of production within the plan for sale at high prices outside the plan in violation of regulations;  
(5) selling commodities supplied to urban residents within the ration at negotiated prices;  
(6) selling commodities with increased prices layer by layer in violation of regulations;  
(7) arbitrarily charging fees under their own items;  
(8) adopting such means as passing off inferior goods as quality goods, giving short measures or short weights, or lowering quality to raise commodity prices or fee rates in a disguised form;  
(9) enterprises or trade organizations colluding to fix monopoly prices;  
(10) failing to implement the system of reporting price increases;  
(11) failing to clearly mark prices as required;  
(12) divulging state price secrets;  
(13) other acts violating price regulations and policies.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对有前条行为之一的，物价检查机构应当根据情节按照下列规定处罚：  
(一)通报批评；  
(二)责令将非法所得退还购买者或者用户；  
(三)不能退还的非法所得由物价检查机构予以没收；  
(四)罚款；  
(五)提请工商行政管理机关吊销营业执照；  
(六)对企业、事业单位的直接责任人员和主管人员处以罚款，并可以建议有关部门给予处分。  
以上处罚，可以并处。

**Article 30** For any of the acts listed in the preceding article, the price inspection organ shall, in light of the circumstances, impose penalties according to the following provisions:  
(1) circulating a notice of criticism;  
(2) ordering the illegal gains to be returned to the purchasers or users;  
(3) confiscating the illegal gains that cannot be returned by the price inspection organ;  
(4) imposing a fine;  
(5) referring the matter to the administrative organ for industry and commerce to revoke the business license;  
(6) imposing a fine on the persons directly responsible and the persons in charge of the enterprise or institution, and may recommend the relevant department to impose sanctions.  
The above penalties may be imposed concurrently.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对拒缴非法所得或者罚款的，物价检查机构可以按照有关规定通知其开户银行予以划拨。对没有银行账户或者银行帐户内无资金的，物价检查机构有权将其商品变卖抵缴。  
被处罚的单位和个体工商户，其退还或者被收缴的非法所得，应当抵减其结案年度的销售收入或者营业收入。企业、事业单位的罚款应当在自有资金、预算包干经费或者预算外资金中支出。

**Article 31** If a party refuses to surrender the illegal gains or pay the fine, the price inspection organ may, in accordance with the relevant provisions, notify the bank with which it has opened an account to effect the transfer. If the party has no bank account or has no funds in its bank account, the price inspection organ shall have the right to sell its commodities to offset the payment.  
The illegal gains returned or confiscated from the punished units and individual industrial and commercial households shall be deducted from their sales revenue or operating revenue for the year in which the case is closed. The fines imposed on enterprises and institutions shall be paid out of their own funds, funds under the lump-sum budget, or extra-budgetary funds.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　被处罚的单位和个人对处罚决定不服的，可以在收到处罚通知之日起十五日内，向上一级物价检查机构申请复议。上一级物价检查机构应当在收到复议申请之日起三十日内作出复议决定。复议期间，原处罚决定照常执行。申诉人对复议决定不服的，可以在收到复议通知之日起十五日内，向人民法院起诉。

**Article 32** If a unit or individual that is punished disagrees with the penalty decision, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice of penalty, apply to the price inspection organ at the next higher level for reconsideration. The price inspection organ at the next higher level shall, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the application for reconsideration, make a reconsideration decision. Pending the reconsideration, the original penalty decision shall remain in force. If the petitioner disagrees with the reconsideration decision, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice of reconsideration, institute a prosecution in the People's Court.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家物价部门对各级物价检查机构、上级物价检查机构对下级物价检查机构已经生效的处罚决定，如果发现确有错误，有权纠正或者责令重新处理。

**Article 33** If the State Pricing Department finds that a penalty decision made by a price inspection organ at any level which has already taken effect is indeed erroneous, it shall have the power to correct it or order a new handling. Similarly, if a superior price inspection organ finds that a penalty decision made by a subordinate price inspection organ which has already taken effect is indeed erroneous, it shall have the power to correct it or order a new handling.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　拒绝、阻碍物价检查人员依法执行职务的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚条例》的规定予以处罚；情节严重，构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 34** Anyone who refuses or obstructs a price inspection personnel in lawfully performing his duties shall be punished by the public security organ in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Administrative Penalties for Public Security of the People's Republic of China; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated by the judicial organs according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　各级人民政府物价部门、业务主管部门及其工作人员违反价格管理权限、程序，制定、调整商品价格或者收费标准的，由上级物价部门或者同级物价部门负责纠正，并按干部管理权限追究有关人员的责任。  
对泄露国家价格机密的，依法追究责任。

**Article 35** If the price departments, competent business departments of the people's governments at various levels or their staff members fix or adjust commodity prices or fee rates in violation of the limits of authority or procedures for price administration, the price department at the next higher level or the price department at the same level shall be responsible for correcting the same, and the responsibility of the persons concerned shall be investigated according to the limits of authority over the administration of cadres.  
Anyone who divulges state price secrets shall be investigated for responsibility according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　对行政性收费、事业性收费，物价部门应当根据国家的价格方针、政策进行管理和监督，并会同有关部门核定收费标准。

**Article 36** With respect to administrative fees and institutional fees, the price departments shall carry out administration and supervision in accordance with the state's pricing principles and policies, and shall jointly with the relevant departments approve the fee rates.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　在我国境内设立的外商投资企业价格管理按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 37** The price administration of foreign-invested enterprises established within the territory of China shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本条例由国家物价局负责解释；施行细则由国家物价局制定。

**Article 38** The power of interpretation of these Regulations shall be vested in the State Pricing Bureau. The rules for implementation shall be formulated by the State Pricing Bureau.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本条例自发布之日起施行，1982年7月7日国务院发布的《物价管理暂行条例》同时废止。

**Article 39** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Provisional Regulations on Price Administration promulgated by the State Council on July 7, 1982 shall be abolished simultaneously.
