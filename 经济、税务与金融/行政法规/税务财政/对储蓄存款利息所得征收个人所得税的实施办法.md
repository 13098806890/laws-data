# 对储蓄存款利息所得征收个人所得税的实施办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2007-07-20  
**生效日期**：1999-11-01

> (1999年9月30日中华人民共和国国务院令第272号发布　根据2007年7月20日《国务院关于修改〈对储蓄存款利息所得征收个人所得税的实施办法〉的决定》修订) 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国个人所得税法》第十二条的规定，制定本办法。 第二条　从中华人民共和国境内的储蓄机构取得人民币、外币储蓄存款利息所得的个人，应当依照本办法缴纳个人所得税。 第三条　对储蓄存款利息所得征收个人所得税的计税依据为纳税人取得的人民币、外币储蓄存款利息所得。 第四条　对储蓄存款利息所得征收个人所得税，减按5%的比例税率执行。减征幅度的调整由国务院决定。 第五条　对个人取得的教育储蓄存款利息所得以及国务院财政部门确定的其他专项储蓄存款或者储蓄性专项基金存款的利息所得，免征个人所得税。 前款所称教育储蓄是指个人按照国家有关规定在指定银行开户、存入规定数额资金、用于教育目的的专项储蓄。 第六条　对储蓄存款利息所得，按照每次取得的利息所得额计征个人所得税。 第七条　对储蓄存款利息所得征收个人所得税，以结付利息的储蓄机构为扣缴义务人，实行代扣代缴。 第八条　扣缴义务人在向储户结付利息时，依法代扣代缴税款。 前款所称结付利息，包括储户取款时结付利息、活期存款结息日结付利息和办理储蓄存款自动转存业务时结付利息等。 扣缴义务人代扣税款，应当在给储户的利息结付单上注明。 第九条　扣缴义务人每月代扣的税款，应当在次月7日内缴入中央国库，并向当地主管税务机关报送代扣代缴税款报告表；代扣的税款为外币的，应当折合成人民币缴入中央国库。 第十条　对扣缴义务人按照所扣缴的税款，付给2%的手续费。 第十一条　税务机关应当加强对扣缴义务人代扣代缴税款情况的监督和检查，扣缴义务人应当积极予以配合，如实反映情况，提供有关资料，不得拒绝、隐瞒。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据[《中华人民共和国个人所得税法》第十二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国个人所得税法.md#art-12)的规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated pursuant to the provisions of Article 12 of the Individual Income Tax Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　从中华人民共和国境内的储蓄机构取得人民币、外币储蓄存款利息所得的个人，应当依照本办法缴纳个人所得税。

**Article 2** Individuals who obtain interest income from savings deposits in Renminbi or foreign currency from savings institutions within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall pay individual income tax in accordance with these Measures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　对储蓄存款利息所得征收个人所得税的计税依据为纳税人取得的人民币、外币储蓄存款利息所得。

**Article 3** The tax base for the levy of individual income tax on interest income from savings deposits shall be the interest income from savings deposits in Renminbi or foreign currency obtained by taxpayers.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　对储蓄存款利息所得征收个人所得税，减按5%的比例税率执行。减征幅度的调整由国务院决定。

**Article 4** The individual income tax on interest income from savings deposits shall be levied at a reduced proportional tax rate of 5%. Any adjustment to the reduction shall be decided by the State Council.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对个人取得的教育储蓄存款利息所得以及国务院财政部门确定的其他专项储蓄存款或者储蓄性专项基金存款的利息所得，免征个人所得税。  
前款所称教育储蓄是指个人按照国家有关规定在指定银行开户、存入规定数额资金、用于教育目的的专项储蓄。

**Article 5** Interest income from education savings deposits obtained by individuals, as well as interest income from other special savings deposits or savings-type special fund deposits determined by the financial department of the State Council, shall be exempted from individual income tax.  
The education savings referred to in the preceding paragraph means special savings for educational purposes where an individual opens an account with a designated bank, deposits a specified amount of funds, and uses them for educational purposes in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　对储蓄存款利息所得，按照每次取得的利息所得额计征个人所得税。

**Article 6** The individual income tax on interest income from savings deposits shall be assessed and collected based on the amount of interest income obtained each time.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对储蓄存款利息所得征收个人所得税，以结付利息的储蓄机构为扣缴义务人，实行代扣代缴。

**Article 7** For the levy of individual income tax on interest income from savings deposits, the savings institution that pays the interest shall be the withholding agent and shall withhold and remit the tax.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　扣缴义务人在向储户结付利息时，依法代扣代缴税款。  
前款所称结付利息，包括储户取款时结付利息、活期存款结息日结付利息和办理储蓄存款自动转存业务时结付利息等。  
扣缴义务人代扣税款，应当在给储户的利息结付单上注明。

**Article 8** When paying interest to a depositor, the withholding agent shall, according to law, withhold and remit the tax.  
The term "pay interest" as used in the preceding paragraph includes paying interest when the depositor withdraws funds, paying interest on the interest settlement date for demand deposits, and paying interest when handling the automatic rollover business of savings deposits, among others.  
The withholding agent shall indicate the tax withheld on the interest payment slip given to the depositor.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　扣缴义务人每月代扣的税款，应当在次月7日内缴入中央国库，并向当地主管税务机关报送代扣代缴税款报告表；代扣的税款为外币的，应当折合成人民币缴入中央国库。

**Article 9** The tax withheld monthly by the withholding agent shall be paid into the central treasury within 7 days of the following month, and a statement of tax withheld and remitted shall be submitted to the local competent tax authority. If the tax withheld is in foreign currency, it shall be converted into Renminbi and paid into the central treasury.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对扣缴义务人按照所扣缴的税款，付给2%的手续费。

**Article 10** The withholding agent shall be paid a handling fee of 2% of the tax withheld.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　税务机关应当加强对扣缴义务人代扣代缴税款情况的监督和检查，扣缴义务人应当积极予以配合，如实反映情况，提供有关资料，不得拒绝、隐瞒。

**Article 11** The tax authority shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of the withholding agent's tax withholding and remittance. The withholding agent shall actively cooperate, truthfully reflect the situation, and provide relevant information, and shall not refuse or conceal.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对储蓄存款利息所得征收的个人所得税，由国家税务局依照《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》、《中华人民共和国个人所得税法》及本办法的规定负责征收管理。

**Article 12** The individual income tax on interest income from savings deposits shall be collected and administered by the State Tax Bureau in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection, the Individual Income Tax Law of the People's Republic of China, and these Measures.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本办法所称储蓄机构，是指经国务院银行业监督管理机构批准的商业银行、城市信用合作社和农村信用合作社等吸收公众存款的金融机构。

**Article 13** The term "savings institution" as used in these Measures means commercial banks, urban credit cooperatives, rural credit cooperatives, and other financial institutions that absorb public deposits, approved by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　储蓄存款在1999年10月31日前孳生的利息所得，不征收个人所得税；储蓄存款在1999年11月1日至2007年8月14日孳生的利息所得，按照20%的比例税率征收个人所得税；储蓄存款在2007年8月15日后孳生的利息所得，按照5%的比例税率征收个人所得税。

**Article 14** Interest income accrued from savings deposits before October 31, 1999 shall not be subject to individual income tax; interest income accrued from savings deposits from November 1, 1999 to August 14, 2007 shall be subject to individual income tax at a proportional tax rate of 20%; interest income accrued from savings deposits after August 15, 2007 shall be subject to individual income tax at a proportional tax rate of 5%.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　本办法自1999年11月1日起施行。

**Article 15** These Measures shall come into force on November 1, 1999.
