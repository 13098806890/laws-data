# 中华人民共和国房产税暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：1986-10-01

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　房产税在城市、县城、建制镇和工矿区征收。

**Article 1** House property tax shall be levied in cities, counties, towns, and industrial and mining areas.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　房产税由产权所有人缴纳。产权属于全民所有的，由经营管理的单位缴纳。产权出典的，由承典人缴纳。产权所有人、承典人不在房产所在地的，或者产权未确定及租典纠纷未解决的，由房产代管人或者使用人缴纳。  
前款列举的产权所有人、经营管理单位、承典人、房产代管人或者使用人，统称为纳税义务人(以下简称纳税人)。

**Article 2** House property tax shall be paid by the property owner. Where the property is owned by the whole people, the tax shall be paid by the unit that operates and manages the property. Where the property is pawned, the tax shall be paid by the pawnee. If the property owner or the pawnee is not located in the place where the house property is situated, or if the ownership of the property is not determined and the dispute over lease or pawn is not resolved, the tax shall be paid by the custodian or the user of the house property.  
The property owner, the operating and managing unit, the pawnee, the custodian or the user of the house property listed in the preceding paragraph are collectively referred to as the taxpayer (hereinafter referred to as the "taxpayer").

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　房产税依照房产原值一次减除10%至30%后的余值计算缴纳。具体减除幅度，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定。  
没有房产原值作为依据的，由房产所在地税务机关参考同类房产核定。  
房产出租的，以房产租金收入为房产税的计税依据。

**Article 3** House property tax shall be calculated and paid based on the balance obtained after deducting once 10% to 30% from the original value of the house property. The specific deduction percentage shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
In the absence of original value of the house property as a basis, the tax authority in the location of the house property shall assess the value by reference to similar house properties.  
If a house property is leased, the rental income from the house property shall serve as the tax base for the house property tax.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　房产税的税率，依照房产余值计算缴纳的，税率为1.2%；依照房产租金收入计算缴纳的，税率为12%。

**Article 4** The tax rate for house property tax shall be 1.2% for the tax calculated and paid based on the balance of the house property; and 12% for the tax calculated and paid based on the rental income of the house property.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　下列房产免纳房产税：  
一、国家机关、人民团体、军队自用的房产；  
二、由国家财政部门拨付事业经费的单位自用的房产；  
三、宗教寺庙、公园、名胜古迹自用的房产；  
四、个人所有非营业用的房产；  
五、经财政部批准免税的其他房产。

**Article 5** The following house properties shall be exempted from house property tax:  
1. house properties for the self-use of state organs, people's organizations, and the military;  
2. house properties for the self-use of units which receive operating funds from the financial departments of the state;  
3. house properties for the self-use of religious temples, parks, and scenic spots and historic sites;  
4. house properties owned by individuals for non-business use; and  
5. other house properties approved for tax exemption by the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　除[本条例第五条](#art-5)规定者外，纳税人纳税确有困难的，可由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定，定期减征或者免征房产税。

**Article 6** Except as provided in Article 5 of these Regulations, if a taxpayer truly has difficulties in paying taxes, the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may determine to periodically reduce or exempt the house property tax.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　房产税按年征收、分期缴纳。纳税期限由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定。

**Article 7** House property tax shall be collected on an annual basis and paid in installments. The tax payment period shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　房产税的征收管理，依照《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》的规定办理。

**Article 8** The collection and administration of house property tax shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the "Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection".

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　房产税由房产所在地的税务机关征收。

**Article 9** House property tax shall be collected by the tax authority in the location of the house property.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　本条例由财政部负责解释；施行细则由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定，抄送财政部备案。

**Article 10** The Ministry of Finance shall be responsible for the interpretation of these Regulations; the rules for implementation shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and submitted to the Ministry of Finance for record.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本条例自1986年10月1日起施行。

**Article 11** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 1, 1986.
