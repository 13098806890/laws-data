# 中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2024-12-06  
**生效日期**：2025-01-20

> （2007年12月6日中华人民共和国国务院令第512号公布　根据2019年4月23日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2024年12月6日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国企业所得税法》（以下简称企业所得税法）的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　企业所得税法第一条所称个人独资企业、合伙企业，是指依照中国法律、行政法规成立的个人独资企业、合伙企业。 第三条　企业所得税法第二条所称依法在中国境内成立的企业，包括依照中国法律、行政法规在中国境内成立的企业、事业单位、社会团体以及其他取得收入的组织。 企业所得税法第二条所称依照外国（地区）法律成立的企业，包括依照外国（地区）法律成立的企业和其他取得收入的组织。 第四条　企业所得税法第二条所称实际管理机构，是指对企业的生产经营、人员、账务、财产等实施实质性全面管理和控制的机构。 第五条　企业所得税法第二条第三款所称机构、场所，是指在中国境内从事生产经营活动的机构、场所，包括： （一）管理机构、营业机构、办事机构； （二）工厂、农场、开采自然资源的场所； （三）提供劳务的场所； （四）从事建筑、安装、装配、修理、勘探等工程作业的场所； （五）其他从事生产经营活动的机构、场所。 非居民企业委托营业代理人在中国境内从事生产经营活动的，包括委托单位或者个人经常代其签订合同，或者储存、交付货物等，该营业代理人视为非居民企业在中国境内设立的机构、场所。 第六条　企业所得税法第三条所称所得，包括销售货物所得、提供劳务所得、转让财产所得、股息红利等权益性投资所得、利息所得、租金所得、特许权使用费所得、接受捐赠所得和其他所得。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国企业所得税法》（以下简称企业所得税法）的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Enterprise Income Tax Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Enterprise Income Tax Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　[企业所得税法第一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-1)所称个人独资企业、合伙企业，是指依照中国法律、行政法规成立的个人独资企业、合伙企业。

**Article 2** For the purposes of Article 1 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the term "sole proprietorship enterprise" and "partnership enterprise" mean sole proprietorship enterprises and partnership enterprises established in accordance with Chinese laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　[企业所得税法第二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-2)所称依法在中国境内成立的企业，包括依照中国法律、行政法规在中国境内成立的企业、事业单位、社会团体以及其他取得收入的组织。  
[企业所得税法第二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-2)所称依照外国（地区）法律成立的企业，包括依照外国（地区）法律成立的企业和其他取得收入的组织。

**Article 3** For the purposes of Article 2 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the term "enterprises established within the territory of China according to law" includes enterprises, institutions, social organizations and other organizations that obtain income and are established within the territory of China in accordance with Chinese laws and administrative regulations.  
For the purposes of Article 2 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the term "enterprises established under the laws of a foreign country (region)" includes enterprises and other organizations that obtain income and are established under the laws of a foreign country (region).

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　[企业所得税法第二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-2)所称实际管理机构，是指对企业的生产经营、人员、账务、财产等实施实质性全面管理和控制的机构。

**Article 4** For the purposes of Article 2 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the term "de facto management body" means an institution that exercises substantive and overall management and control over the production and business operations, personnel, accounts, and assets of an enterprise.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　[企业所得税法第二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-2)第三款所称机构、场所，是指在中国境内从事生产经营活动的机构、场所，包括：  
（一）管理机构、营业机构、办事机构；  
（二）工厂、农场、开采自然资源的场所；  
（三）提供劳务的场所；  
（四）从事建筑、安装、装配、修理、勘探等工程作业的场所；  
（五）其他从事生产经营活动的机构、场所。  
非居民企业委托营业代理人在中国境内从事生产经营活动的，包括委托单位或者个人经常代其签订合同，或者储存、交付货物等，该营业代理人视为非居民企业在中国境内设立的机构、场所。

**Article 5** For the purposes of the third paragraph of Article 2 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the term "establishment or premises" means an establishment or premises engaged in production and business operations within the territory of China, including:  
(1) management offices, business offices, and representative offices;  
(2) factories, farms, and premises for the extraction of natural resources;  
(3) premises for the provision of labor services;  
(4) premises for engaging in construction, installation, assembly, repair, exploration and other engineering operations; and  
(5) other establishments or premises engaged in production and business operations.  
Where a non-resident enterprise entrusts a business agent to engage in production and business operations within the territory of China, including regularly signing contracts on its behalf or storing and delivering goods, the business agent shall be deemed as an establishment or premises established by the non-resident enterprise within the territory of China.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　[企业所得税法第三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-3)所称所得，包括销售货物所得、提供劳务所得、转让财产所得、股息红利等权益性投资所得、利息所得、租金所得、特许权使用费所得、接受捐赠所得和其他所得。

**Article 6** For the purposes of Article 3 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the term "income" includes income from the sale of goods, income from the provision of labor services, income from the transfer of property, income from equity investments such as dividends and bonuses, interest income, rental income, income from royalties, income from donations received, and other income.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　[企业所得税法第三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-3)所称来源于中国境内、境外的所得，按照以下原则确定：  
（一）销售货物所得，按照交易活动发生地确定；  
（二）提供劳务所得，按照劳务发生地确定；  
（三）转让财产所得，不动产转让所得按照不动产所在地确定，动产转让所得按照转让动产的企业或者机构、场所所在地确定，权益性投资资产转让所得按照被投资企业所在地确定；  
（四）股息、红利等权益性投资所得，按照分配所得的企业所在地确定；  
（五）利息所得、租金所得、特许权使用费所得，按照负担、支付所得的企业或者机构、场所所在地确定，或者按照负担、支付所得的个人的住所地确定；  
（六）其他所得，由国务院财政、税务主管部门确定。

**Article 7** For the purposes of Article 3 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, income derived from within and outside the territory of China shall be determined in accordance with the following principles:  
(1) income from the sale of goods shall be determined by the place where the transaction activities occur;  
(2) income from the provision of labor services shall be determined by the place where the labor services are provided;  
(3) income from the transfer of property: income from the transfer of immovable property shall be determined by the location of the immovable property; income from the transfer of movable property shall be determined by the location of the enterprise or establishment or premises that transfers the movable property; and income from the transfer of equity investment assets shall be determined by the location of the invested enterprise;  
(4) income from equity investments such as dividends and bonuses shall be determined by the location of the enterprise that distributes the income;  
(5) interest income, rental income, and royalty income shall be determined by the location of the enterprise or establishment or premises that bears or pays the income, or by the place of domicile of the individual who bears or pays the income; and  
(6) other income shall be determined by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　[企业所得税法第三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-3)所称实际联系，是指非居民企业在中国境内设立的机构、场所拥有据以取得所得的股权、债权，以及拥有、管理、控制据以取得所得的财产等。

**Article 8** For the purposes of Article 3 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the term "actual connection" means that the establishment or premises established by a non-resident enterprise within the territory of China holds the equities or creditor's rights giving rise to the income, or owns, manages or controls the property giving rise to the income.

### 第二章　应纳税所得额

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　企业应纳税所得额的计算，以权责发生制为原则，属于当期的收入和费用，不论款项是否收付，均作为当期的收入和费用；不属于当期的收入和费用，即使款项已经在当期收付，均不作为当期的收入和费用。本条例和国务院财政、税务主管部门另有规定的除外。

**Article 9** The computation of the taxable income amount of an enterprise shall be on the accrual basis. Income and expenses attributable to the current period shall be recognized as income and expenses of the current period regardless of whether the payment is received or made. Income and expenses not attributable to the current period shall not be recognized as income and expenses of the current period even if the payment has been received or made in the current period, unless these Regulations or the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council provide otherwise.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　[企业所得税法第五条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-5)所称亏损，是指企业依照企业所得税法和本条例的规定将每一纳税年度的收入总额减除不征税收入、免税收入和各项扣除后小于零的数额。

**Article 10** For the purposes of Article 5 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the term "loss" means the amount by which the total income of an enterprise for each tax year, after deducting non-taxable income, tax-exempt income and various deductions in accordance with the provisions of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and these Regulations, is less than zero.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　[企业所得税法第五十五条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-55)所称清算所得，是指企业的全部资产可变现价值或者交易价格减除资产净值、清算费用以及相关税费等后的余额。  
投资方企业从被清算企业分得的剩余资产，其中相当于从被清算企业累计未分配利润和累计盈余公积中应当分得的部分，应当确认为股息所得；剩余资产减除上述股息所得后的余额，超过或者低于投资成本的部分，应当确认为投资资产转让所得或者损失。

**Article 11** For the purposes of Article 55 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the term "liquidation income" means the balance of the total realizable value or transaction price of all assets of an enterprise after deducting the net asset value, liquidation expenses and relevant taxes and fees.  
Where an investing enterprise obtains residual assets from the enterprise being liquidated, the portion equivalent to the amount that should be distributed from the cumulative undistributed profits and cumulative surplus reserves of the enterprise being liquidated shall be recognized as dividend income; the balance of the residual assets after deducting the aforesaid dividend income, if it exceeds or falls short of the investment cost, shall be recognized as income or loss from the transfer of investment assets.

#### 第二节　收入

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　[企业所得税法第六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-6)所称企业取得收入的货币形式，包括现金、存款、应收账款、应收票据、准备持有至到期的债券投资以及债务的豁免等。  
[企业所得税法第六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-6)所称企业取得收入的非货币形式，包括固定资产、生物资产、无形资产、股权投资、存货、不准备持有至到期的债券投资、劳务以及有关权益等。

**Article 12** For the purposes of Article 6 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the monetary forms of income obtained by an enterprise include cash, deposits, accounts receivable, notes receivable, bond investments held to maturity, and debt forgiveness.  
For the purposes of Article 6 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the non-monetary forms of income obtained by an enterprise include fixed assets, biological assets, intangible assets, equity investments, inventory, bond investments not held to maturity, labor services, and related rights and interests.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　[企业所得税法第六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-6)所称企业以非货币形式取得的收入，应当按照公允价值确定收入额。  
前款所称公允价值，是指按照市场价格确定的价值。

**Article 13** For the purposes of Article 6 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, income obtained by an enterprise in non-monetary forms shall be determined at fair value.  
For the purposes of the preceding paragraph, "fair value" means the value determined in accordance with market prices.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　[企业所得税法第六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-6)第（一）项所称销售货物收入，是指企业销售商品、产品、原材料、包装物、低值易耗品以及其他存货取得的收入。

**Article 14** For the purposes of subparagraph (1) of Article 6 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "income from the sale of goods" means the income obtained by an enterprise from selling commodities, products, raw materials, packaging materials, low-value consumables and other inventory.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　[企业所得税法第六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-6)第（二）项所称提供劳务收入，是指企业从事建筑安装、修理修配、交通运输、仓储租赁、金融保险、邮电通信、咨询经纪、文化体育、科学研究、技术服务、教育培训、餐饮住宿、中介代理、卫生保健、社区服务、旅游、娱乐、加工以及其他劳务服务活动取得的收入。

**Article 15** For the purposes of subparagraph (2) of Article 6 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "income from the provision of labor services" means the income obtained by an enterprise from engaging in construction and installation, repair and maintenance, transportation, warehousing and leasing, finance and insurance, postal and telecommunications, consulting and brokerage, culture and sports, scientific research, technical services, education and training, catering and accommodation, intermediary agency, health care, community services, tourism, entertainment, processing and other labor service activities.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　[企业所得税法第六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-6)第（三）项所称转让财产收入，是指企业转让固定资产、生物资产、无形资产、股权、债权等财产取得的收入。

**Article 16** For the purposes of subparagraph (3) of Article 6 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "income from the transfer of property" means the income obtained by an enterprise from transferring fixed assets, biological assets, intangible assets, equity interests, creditor's rights and other property.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　[企业所得税法第六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-6)第（四）项所称股息、红利等权益性投资收益，是指企业因权益性投资从被投资方取得的收入。  
股息、红利等权益性投资收益，除国务院财政、税务主管部门另有规定外，按照被投资方作出利润分配决定的日期确认收入的实现。

**Article 17** For the purposes of subparagraph (4) of Article 6 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "income from equity investments such as dividends and bonuses" means the income obtained by an enterprise from the investee as a result of equity investments.  
Unless otherwise provided by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council, income from equity investments such as dividends and bonuses shall be recognized as realized on the date when the investee makes a decision on profit distribution.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　[企业所得税法第六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-6)第（五）项所称利息收入，是指企业将资金提供他人使用但不构成权益性投资，或者因他人占用本企业资金取得的收入，包括存款利息、贷款利息、债券利息、欠款利息等收入。  
利息收入，按照合同约定的债务人应付利息的日期确认收入的实现。

**Article 18** For the purposes of subparagraph (5) of Article 6 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "interest income" means the income obtained by an enterprise from providing funds to others for use but not constituting an equity investment, or from others using the enterprise's funds, including interest on deposits, interest on loans, interest on bonds, and interest on receivables.  
Interest income shall be recognized as realized on the date on which the debtor is obligated to pay interest as agreed in the contract.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　[企业所得税法第六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-6)第（六）项所称租金收入，是指企业提供固定资产、包装物或者其他有形资产的使用权取得的收入。  
租金收入，按照合同约定的承租人应付租金的日期确认收入的实现。

**Article 19** For the purposes of subparagraph (6) of Article 6 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "rental income" means the income obtained by an enterprise from providing the right to use fixed assets, packaging materials or other tangible assets.  
Rental income shall be recognized as realized on the date on which the lessee is obligated to pay rent as agreed in the contract.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　[企业所得税法第六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-6)第（七）项所称特许权使用费收入，是指企业提供专利权、非专利技术、商标权、著作权以及其他特许权的使用权取得的收入。  
特许权使用费收入，按照合同约定的特许权使用人应付特许权使用费的日期确认收入的实现。

**Article 20** For the purposes of subparagraph (7) of Article 6 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "royalty income" means the income obtained by an enterprise from providing the right to use patents, non-patented technology, trademark rights, copyrights and other franchises.  
Royalty income shall be recognized as realized on the date on which the franchisee is obligated to pay the royalty as agreed in the contract.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　[企业所得税法第六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-6)第（八）项所称接受捐赠收入，是指企业接受的来自其他企业、组织或者个人无偿给予的货币性资产、非货币性资产。  
接受捐赠收入，按照实际收到捐赠资产的日期确认收入的实现。

**Article 21** For the purposes of subparagraph (8) of Article 6 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "income from donations received" means the monetary or non-monetary assets received by an enterprise from other enterprises, organizations or individuals as gifts without compensation.  
Income from donations received shall be recognized as realized on the date when the donated assets are actually received.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　[企业所得税法第六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-6)第（九）项所称其他收入，是指企业取得的除[企业所得税法第六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-6)第（一）项至第（八）项规定的收入外的其他收入，包括企业资产溢余收入、逾期未退包装物押金收入、确实无法偿付的应付款项、已作坏账损失处理后又收回的应收款项、债务重组收入、补贴收入、违约金收入、汇兑收益等。

**Article 22** For the purposes of subparagraph (9) of Article 6 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "other income" means the income obtained by an enterprise other than the income specified in subparagraphs (1) through (8) of Article 6 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, including income from asset surplus, income from overdue packaging deposits not returned, accounts payable that are truly unpayable, accounts receivable recovered after being treated as bad debt losses, income from debt restructuring, subsidy income, liquidated damages income, and exchange gains.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　企业的下列生产经营业务可以分期确认收入的实现：  
（一）以分期收款方式销售货物的，按照合同约定的收款日期确认收入的实现；  
（二）企业受托加工制造大型机械设备、船舶、飞机，以及从事建筑、安装、装配工程业务或者提供其他劳务等，持续时间超过12个月的，按照纳税年度内完工进度或者完成的工作量确认收入的实现。

**Article 23** An enterprise may recognize the realization of income in installments for the following production and business operations:  
(1) where goods are sold by way of installment collection, income shall be recognized as realized in accordance with the date of collection agreed in the contract; and  
(2) where an enterprise is entrusted with the processing and manufacturing of large mechanical equipment, ships or aircraft, or engages in construction, installation, assembly engineering or provides other labor services, if the duration exceeds 12 months, income shall be recognized as realized in accordance with the progress of completion or the amount of work completed within the tax year.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　采取产品分成方式取得收入的，按照企业分得产品的日期确认收入的实现，其收入额按照产品的公允价值确定。

**Article 24** Where income is obtained by way of product sharing, the income shall be recognized as realized on the date when the enterprise receives the products, and the amount of income shall be determined at the fair value of the products.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　企业发生非货币性资产交换，以及将货物、财产、劳务用于捐赠、偿债、赞助、集资、广告、样品、职工福利或者利润分配等用途的，应当视同销售货物、转让财产或者提供劳务，但国务院财政、税务主管部门另有规定的除外。

**Article 25** Where an enterprise engages in a non-monetary asset exchange, or uses goods, property or labor services for donations, debt repayment, sponsorship, fundraising, advertising, samples, employee welfare or profit distribution, it shall be deemed as selling goods, transferring property or providing labor services, unless otherwise provided by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　[企业所得税法第七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-7)第（一）项所称财政拨款，是指各级人民政府对纳入预算管理的事业单位、社会团体等组织拨付的财政资金，但国务院和国务院财政、税务主管部门另有规定的除外。  
[企业所得税法第七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-7)第（二）项所称行政事业性收费，是指依照法律法规等有关规定，按照国务院规定程序批准，在实施社会公共管理，以及在向公民、法人或者其他组织提供特定公共服务过程中，向特定对象收取并纳入财政管理的费用。  
[企业所得税法第七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-7)第（二）项所称政府性基金，是指企业依照法律、行政法规等有关规定，代政府收取的具有专项用途的财政资金。  
[企业所得税法第七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-7)第（三）项所称国务院规定的其他不征税收入，是指企业取得的，由国务院财政、税务主管部门规定专项用途并经国务院批准的财政性资金。

**Article 26** For the purposes of subparagraph (1) of Article 7 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "financial allocation" means the fiscal funds allocated by people's governments at all levels to institutions, social organizations and other organizations that are included in budget management, unless otherwise provided by the State Council and its competent finance and tax departments.  
For the purposes of subparagraph (2) of Article 7 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "administrative and institutional charges" mean fees collected from specific targets and included in fiscal management in the course of implementing social public administration and providing specific public services to citizens, legal persons or other organizations, in accordance with the provisions of laws, regulations and other relevant rules and approved by the procedures prescribed by the State Council.  
For the purposes of subparagraph (2) of Article 7 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "government funds" mean fiscal funds with special purposes collected by enterprises on behalf of the government in accordance with the provisions of laws, administrative regulations and other relevant rules.  
For the purposes of subparagraph (3) of Article 7 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "other non-taxable income as prescribed by the State Council" means fiscal funds obtained by enterprises that are designated for special purposes by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council and approved by the State Council.

#### 第三节　扣除

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　[企业所得税法第八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-8)所称有关的支出，是指与取得收入直接相关的支出。  
[企业所得税法第八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-8)所称合理的支出，是指符合生产经营活动常规，应当计入当期损益或者有关资产成本的必要和正常的支出。

**Article 27** For the purposes of Article 8 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "relevant expenditures" mean expenditures directly related to the obtaining of income.  
For the purposes of Article 8 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "reasonable expenditures" mean necessary and normal expenditures that conform to the normal practices of production and business operations and shall be included in the current profit or loss or the cost of relevant assets.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　企业发生的支出应当区分收益性支出和资本性支出。收益性支出在发生当期直接扣除；资本性支出应当分期扣除或者计入有关资产成本，不得在发生当期直接扣除。  
企业的不征税收入用于支出所形成的费用或者财产，不得扣除或者计算对应的折旧、摊销扣除。  
除企业所得税法和本条例另有规定外，企业实际发生的成本、费用、税金、损失和其他支出，不得重复扣除。

**Article 28** Expenditures incurred by an enterprise shall be distinguished as revenue expenditures and capital expenditures. Revenue expenditures shall be directly deducted in the current period in which they are incurred; capital expenditures shall be deducted in installments or included in the cost of relevant assets and shall not be directly deducted in the current period in which they are incurred.  
Expenses or property formed by the use of non-taxable income of an enterprise for expenditures shall not be deducted, and corresponding depreciation or amortization shall not be calculated for deduction.  
Unless otherwise provided by the Enterprise Income Tax Law and these Regulations, the costs, expenses, taxes, losses and other expenditures actually incurred by an enterprise shall not be deducted repeatedly.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　[企业所得税法第八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-8)所称成本，是指企业在生产经营活动中发生的销售成本、销货成本、业务支出以及其他耗费。

**Article 29** For the purposes of Article 8 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "costs" mean the cost of sales, cost of goods sold, business expenses and other consumption incurred by an enterprise in the course of production and business operations.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　[企业所得税法第八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-8)所称费用，是指企业在生产经营活动中发生的销售费用、管理费用和财务费用，已经计入成本的有关费用除外。

**Article 30** For the purposes of Article 8 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "expenses" mean the selling expenses, administrative expenses and financial expenses incurred by an enterprise in the course of production and business operations, excluding relevant expenses that have been included in costs.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　[企业所得税法第八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-8)所称税金，是指企业发生的除企业所得税和允许抵扣的增值税以外的各项税金及其附加。

**Article 31** For the purposes of Article 8 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "taxes" mean various taxes and their surcharges incurred by an enterprise, excluding enterprise income tax and value-added tax that is deductible.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　[企业所得税法第八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-8)所称损失，是指企业在生产经营活动中发生的固定资产和存货的盘亏、毁损、报废损失，转让财产损失，呆账损失，坏账损失，自然灾害等不可抗力因素造成的损失以及其他损失。  
企业发生的损失，减除责任人赔偿和保险赔款后的余额，依照国务院财政、税务主管部门的规定扣除。  
企业已经作为损失处理的资产，在以后纳税年度又全部收回或者部分收回时，应当计入当期收入。

**Article 32** For the purposes of Article 8 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "losses" mean the losses from inventory shortages, damage and scrapping of fixed assets and inventory, losses from transfer of property, bad debt losses, losses from uncollectible accounts, losses caused by force majeure such as natural disasters, and other losses incurred by an enterprise in the course of production and business operations.  
The balance of losses incurred by an enterprise after deducting compensation from the responsible person and insurance indemnity shall be deducted in accordance with the provisions of the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council.  
Where assets that have been treated as losses by an enterprise are subsequently fully or partially recovered in a later tax year, the recovered amount shall be included in income for that year.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　[企业所得税法第八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-8)所称其他支出，是指除成本、费用、税金、损失外，企业在生产经营活动中发生的与生产经营活动有关的、合理的支出。

**Article 33** For the purposes of Article 8 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "other expenditures" mean expenditures, other than costs, expenses, taxes and losses, that are incurred by an enterprise in the course of production and business operations and are related to and reasonable for production and business operations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　企业发生的合理的工资薪金支出，准予扣除。  
前款所称工资薪金，是指企业每一纳税年度支付给在本企业任职或者受雇的员工的所有现金形式或者非现金形式的劳动报酬，包括基本工资、奖金、津贴、补贴、年终加薪、加班工资，以及与员工任职或者受雇有关的其他支出。

**Article 34** Reasonable wages and salaries expenses incurred by an enterprise shall be allowed for deduction.  
For the purposes of the preceding paragraph, "wages and salaries" mean all labor remuneration in cash or non-cash form paid by an enterprise to its employees who hold office or are employed by the enterprise in each tax year, including basic wages, bonuses, allowances, subsidies, year-end bonuses, overtime wages, and other expenditures related to the employment or service of employees.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　企业依照国务院有关主管部门或者省级人民政府规定的范围和标准为职工缴纳的基本养老保险费、基本医疗保险费、失业保险费、工伤保险费、生育保险费等基本社会保险费和住房公积金，准予扣除。  
企业为投资者或者职工支付的补充养老保险费、补充医疗保险费，在国务院财政、税务主管部门规定的范围和标准内，准予扣除。

**Article 35** Basic social insurance premiums, such as basic endowment insurance, basic medical insurance, unemployment insurance, work-related injury insurance and maternity insurance, as well as housing provident funds, paid by an enterprise for its employees within the scope and standards prescribed by the competent departments of the State Council or the people's governments at the provincial level, shall be allowed for deduction.  
Supplementary endowment insurance premiums and supplementary medical insurance premiums paid by an enterprise for its investors or employees, within the scope and standards prescribed by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council, shall be allowed for deduction.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　除企业依照国家有关规定为特殊工种职工支付的人身安全保险费和国务院财政、税务主管部门规定可以扣除的其他商业保险费外，企业为投资者或者职工支付的商业保险费，不得扣除。

**Article 36** Except for personal safety insurance premiums paid by an enterprise for employees in special types of work in accordance with relevant state provisions and other commercial insurance premiums that may be deducted as prescribed by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council, commercial insurance premiums paid by an enterprise for its investors or employees shall not be deducted.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　企业在生产经营活动中发生的合理的不需要资本化的借款费用，准予扣除。  
企业为购置、建造固定资产、无形资产和经过12个月以上的建造才能达到预定可销售状态的存货发生借款的，在有关资产购置、建造期间发生的合理的借款费用，应当作为资本性支出计入有关资产的成本，并依照本条例的规定扣除。

**Article 37** Reasonable borrowing expenses incurred by an enterprise in the course of production and business operations that do not need to be capitalized shall be allowed for deduction.  
Where an enterprise borrows funds for the acquisition or construction of fixed assets, intangible assets, and inventory that requires more than 12 months of construction to reach its intended saleable condition, the reasonable borrowing expenses incurred during the period of acquisition or construction of the relevant assets shall be treated as capital expenditures and included in the cost of the relevant assets, and shall be deducted in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　企业在生产经营活动中发生的下列利息支出，准予扣除：  
（一）非金融企业向金融企业借款的利息支出、金融企业的各项存款利息支出和同业拆借利息支出、企业经批准发行债券的利息支出；  
（二）非金融企业向非金融企业借款的利息支出，不超过按照金融企业同期同类贷款利率计算的数额的部分。

**Article 38** The following interest expenses incurred by an enterprise in the course of production and business operations shall be allowed for deduction:  
(1) interest expenses on borrowings by non-financial enterprises from financial enterprises, interest expenses on various deposits and interbank lending of financial enterprises, and interest expenses on bonds issued by enterprises with approval; and  
(2) interest expenses on borrowings by non-financial enterprises from non-financial enterprises, to the extent not exceeding the amount calculated at the interest rate for similar loans of financial enterprises for the same period.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　企业在货币交易中，以及纳税年度终了时将人民币以外的货币性资产、负债按照期末即期人民币汇率中间价折算为人民币时产生的汇兑损失，除已经计入有关资产成本以及与向所有者进行利润分配相关的部分外，准予扣除。

**Article 39** Exchange losses arising from currency transactions and from the conversion of monetary assets and liabilities denominated in currencies other than RMB into RMB at the central parity rate of the spot exchange rate at the end of the period on the closing date of the tax year shall be allowed for deduction, except for the portion that has been included in the cost of relevant assets or is related to profit distributions to owners.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　企业发生的职工福利费支出，不超过工资薪金总额14%的部分，准予扣除。

**Article 40** The portion of employee welfare expenses incurred by an enterprise that does not exceed 14% of the total wages and salaries shall be allowed for deduction.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　企业拨缴的工会经费，不超过工资薪金总额2%的部分，准予扣除。

**Article 41** The portion of trade union funds allocated and paid by an enterprise that does not exceed 2% of the total wages and salaries shall be allowed for deduction.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　除国务院财政、税务主管部门另有规定外，企业发生的职工教育经费支出，不超过工资薪金总额2.5%的部分，准予扣除；超过部分，准予在以后纳税年度结转扣除。

**Article 42** Unless otherwise provided by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council, the portion of employee education expenses incurred by an enterprise that does not exceed 2.5% of the total wages and salaries shall be allowed for deduction; the excess portion shall be allowed to be carried forward and deducted in subsequent tax years.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　企业发生的与生产经营活动有关的业务招待费支出，按照发生额的60%扣除，但最高不得超过当年销售（营业）收入的5‰。

**Article 43** Business entertainment expenses incurred by an enterprise related to production and business operations shall be deducted at 60% of the amount incurred, but the maximum shall not exceed 5‰ of the sales (operating) revenue of the current year.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　企业发生的符合条件的广告费和业务宣传费支出，除国务院财政、税务主管部门另有规定外，不超过当年销售（营业）收入15%的部分，准予扣除；超过部分，准予在以后纳税年度结转扣除。

**Article 44** Unless otherwise provided by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council, the portion of advertising expenses and business promotion expenses incurred by an enterprise that meets the conditions and does not exceed 15% of the sales (operating) revenue of the current year shall be allowed for deduction; the excess portion shall be allowed to be carried forward and deducted in subsequent tax years.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　企业依照法律、行政法规有关规定提取的用于环境保护、生态恢复等方面的专项资金，准予扣除。上述专项资金提取后改变用途的，不得扣除。

**Article 45** Special funds set aside by an enterprise in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations for environmental protection, ecological restoration and other purposes shall be allowed for deduction. If the use of the aforesaid special funds is changed after they are set aside, they shall not be deducted.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　企业参加财产保险，按照规定缴纳的保险费，准予扣除。

**Article 46** Insurance premiums paid by an enterprise for property insurance in accordance with the provisions shall be allowed for deduction.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　企业根据生产经营活动的需要租入固定资产支付的租赁费，按照以下方法扣除：  
（一）以经营租赁方式租入固定资产发生的租赁费支出，按照租赁期限均匀扣除；  
（二）以融资租赁方式租入固定资产发生的租赁费支出，按照规定构成融资租入固定资产价值的部分应当提取折旧费用，分期扣除。

**Article 47** Rental expenses paid by an enterprise for leasing in fixed assets as required by production and business operations shall be deducted in the following manner:  
(1) rental expenses incurred for leasing in fixed assets under an operating lease shall be deducted evenly over the lease term; and  
(2) rental expenses incurred for leasing in fixed assets under a finance lease, to the extent that they form part of the value of the fixed assets leased in under the finance lease according to the provisions, shall be subject to depreciation deductions and deducted in installments.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　企业发生的合理的劳动保护支出，准予扣除。

**Article 48** Reasonable labor protection expenses incurred by an enterprise shall be allowed for deduction.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　企业之间支付的管理费、企业内营业机构之间支付的租金和特许权使用费，以及非银行企业内营业机构之间支付的利息，不得扣除。

**Article 49** Management fees paid between enterprises, rental expenses and royalties paid between business establishments within an enterprise, and interest paid between business establishments within a non-bank enterprise shall not be deducted.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　非居民企业在中国境内设立的机构、场所，就其中国境外总机构发生的与该机构、场所生产经营有关的费用，能够提供总机构出具的费用汇集范围、定额、分配依据和方法等证明文件，并合理分摊的，准予扣除。

**Article 50** Where an establishment or premises established by a non-resident enterprise within the territory of China can provide supporting documents issued by its head office outside China on the scope, fixed amount, basis and method of allocation of expenses, and reasonably apportions the expenses incurred by the head office outside China that are related to the production and business operations of such establishment or premises, such expenses shall be allowed for deduction.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　[企业所得税法第九条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-9)所称公益性捐赠，是指企业通过公益性社会组织或者县级以上人民政府及其部门，用于符合法律规定的慈善活动、公益事业的捐赠。

**Article 51** For the purposes of Article 9 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "public welfare donations" mean donations made by an enterprise through public welfare social organizations or the people's governments at or above the county level and their departments for charitable activities and public welfare undertakings that comply with the provisions of laws.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　[本条例第五十一条](#art-51)所称公益性社会组织，是指同时符合下列条件的慈善组织以及其他社会组织：  
（一）依法登记，具有法人资格；  
（二）以发展公益事业为宗旨，且不以营利为目的；  
（三）全部资产及其增值为该法人所有；  
（四）收益和营运结余主要用于符合该法人设立目的的事业；  
（五）终止后的剩余财产不归属任何个人或者营利组织；  
（六）不经营与其设立目的无关的业务；  
（七）有健全的财务会计制度；  
（八）捐赠者不以任何形式参与该法人财产的分配；  
（九）国务院财政、税务主管部门会同国务院民政部门等登记管理部门规定的其他条件。

**Article 52** For the purposes of Article 51 of these Regulations, "public welfare social organizations" mean charitable organizations and other social organizations that meet all of the following conditions:  
(1) they are registered according to law and have the status of a legal person;  
(2) they take the development of public welfare undertakings as their purpose and do not aim at making profits;  
(3) all of their assets and the appreciation thereof are owned by the legal person;  
(4) their earnings and operating surpluses are mainly used for undertakings that are consistent with the purpose for which the legal person is established;  
(5) the remaining property after dissolution shall not belong to any individual or profit-making organization;  
(6) they do not engage in businesses unrelated to their establishment purpose;  
(7) they have sound financial accounting systems;  
(8) donors do not participate in the distribution of the property of the legal person in any form; and  
(9) other conditions prescribed by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council jointly with the civil affairs department and other registration administration departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　企业当年发生以及以前年度结转的公益性捐赠支出，不超过年度利润总额12%的部分，准予扣除。  
年度利润总额，是指企业依照国家统一会计制度的规定计算的年度会计利润。

**Article 53** The portion of public welfare donation expenses incurred by an enterprise in the current year and carried forward from previous years that does not exceed 12% of the total annual profit shall be allowed for deduction.  
"Total annual profit" means the annual accounting profit calculated by the enterprise in accordance with the provisions of the unified national accounting system.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　[企业所得税法第十条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-10)第（六）项所称赞助支出，是指企业发生的与生产经营活动无关的各种非广告性质支出。

**Article 54** For the purposes of subparagraph (6) of Article 10 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "sponsorship expenses" mean various non-advertising expenditures incurred by an enterprise that are unrelated to production and business operations.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　[企业所得税法第十条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-10)第（七）项所称未经核定的准备金支出，是指不符合国务院财政、税务主管部门规定的各项资产减值准备、风险准备等准备金支出。

**Article 55** For the purposes of subparagraph (7) of Article 10 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "unapproved reserve expenses" mean expenses for various reserves, such as asset impairment provisions and risk provisions, that do not comply with the provisions of the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council.

#### 第四节　资产的税务处理

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　企业的各项资产，包括固定资产、生物资产、无形资产、长期待摊费用、投资资产、存货等，以历史成本为计税基础。  
前款所称历史成本，是指企业取得该项资产时实际发生的支出。  
企业持有各项资产期间资产增值或者减值，除国务院财政、税务主管部门规定可以确认损益外，不得调整该资产的计税基础。

**Article 56** The tax basis of various assets of an enterprise, including fixed assets, biological assets, intangible assets, long-term deferred expenses, investment assets, and inventory, shall be the historical cost.  
For the purposes of the preceding paragraph, "historical cost" means the expenditure actually incurred by the enterprise when acquiring the asset.  
During the period when an enterprise holds various assets, any appreciation or depreciation in value shall not adjust the tax basis of the assets, unless the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council provide that gains or losses may be recognized.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　[企业所得税法第十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-11)所称固定资产，是指企业为生产产品、提供劳务、出租或者经营管理而持有的、使用时间超过12个月的非货币性资产，包括房屋、建筑物、机器、机械、运输工具以及其他与生产经营活动有关的设备、器具、工具等。

**Article 57** For the purposes of Article 11 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "fixed assets" mean non-monetary assets held by an enterprise for the production of products, provision of labor services, leasing or business management, with a useful life exceeding 12 months, including houses, buildings, machinery, mechanical equipment, means of transport, and other equipment, appliances and tools related to production and business operations.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　固定资产按照以下方法确定计税基础：  
（一）外购的固定资产，以购买价款和支付的相关税费以及直接归属于使该资产达到预定用途发生的其他支出为计税基础；  
（二）自行建造的固定资产，以竣工结算前发生的支出为计税基础；  
（三）融资租入的固定资产，以租赁合同约定的付款总额和承租人在签订租赁合同过程中发生的相关费用为计税基础，租赁合同未约定付款总额的，以该资产的公允价值和承租人在签订租赁合同过程中发生的相关费用为计税基础；  
（四）盘盈的固定资产，以同类固定资产的重置完全价值为计税基础；  
（五）通过捐赠、投资、非货币性资产交换、债务重组等方式取得的固定资产，以该资产的公允价值和支付的相关税费为计税基础；  
（六）改建的固定资产，除[企业所得税法第十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-13)第（一）项和第（二）项规定的支出外，以改建过程中发生的改建支出增加计税基础。

**Article 58** The tax basis of fixed assets shall be determined in the following manner:  
(1) for purchased fixed assets, the tax basis shall be the purchase price plus the relevant taxes and fees paid and other expenditures directly attributable to bringing the asset to its intended use;  
(2) for self-constructed fixed assets, the tax basis shall be the expenditures incurred before the completion of construction and final settlement;  
(3) for fixed assets acquired under a finance lease, the tax basis shall be the total payment amount agreed in the lease contract plus the relevant expenses incurred by the lessee in concluding the lease contract; if the total payment amount is not agreed in the lease contract, the tax basis shall be the fair value of the asset plus the relevant expenses incurred by the lessee in concluding the lease contract;  
(4) for fixed assets discovered upon inventory, the tax basis shall be the full replacement value of similar fixed assets;  
(5) for fixed assets acquired through donation, investment, non-monetary asset exchange, debt restructuring, or other methods, the tax basis shall be the fair value of the asset plus the relevant taxes and fees paid; and  
(6) for reconstructed fixed assets, except for expenditures specified in subparagraphs (1) and (2) of Article 13 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the tax basis shall be increased by the reconstruction expenditures incurred during the reconstruction process.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　固定资产按照直线法计算的折旧，准予扣除。  
企业应当自固定资产投入使用月份的次月起计算折旧；停止使用的固定资产，应当自停止使用月份的次月起停止计算折旧。  
企业应当根据固定资产的性质和使用情况，合理确定固定资产的预计净残值。固定资产的预计净残值一经确定，不得变更。

**Article 59** Depreciation calculated on fixed assets using the straight-line method shall be allowed for deduction.  
An enterprise shall begin calculating depreciation from the month following the month in which the fixed asset is put into use; for a fixed asset that ceases to be used, depreciation shall cease from the month following the month in which it ceases to be used.  
An enterprise shall reasonably determine the estimated net residual value of a fixed asset based on its nature and usage. Once the estimated net residual value of a fixed asset is determined, it shall not be changed.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　除国务院财政、税务主管部门另有规定外，固定资产计算折旧的最低年限如下：  
（一）房屋、建筑物，为20年；  
（二）飞机、火车、轮船、机器、机械和其他生产设备，为10年；  
（三）与生产经营活动有关的器具、工具、家具等，为5年；  
（四）飞机、火车、轮船以外的运输工具，为4年；  
（五）电子设备，为3年。

**Article 60** Unless otherwise provided by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council, the minimum depreciation periods for fixed assets are as follows:  
(1) 20 years for houses and buildings;  
(2) 10 years for aircraft, trains, ships, machinery, mechanical equipment and other production equipment;  
(3) 5 years for appliances, tools, furniture, etc. related to production and business operations;  
(4) 4 years for means of transport other than aircraft, trains and ships; and  
(5) 3 years for electronic equipment.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　从事开采石油、天然气等矿产资源的企业，在开始商业性生产前发生的费用和有关固定资产的折耗、折旧方法，由国务院财政、税务主管部门另行规定。

**Article 61** For enterprises engaged in the extraction of mineral resources such as oil and natural gas, the treatment of expenses incurred before the commencement of commercial production and the methods of depletion and depreciation for relevant fixed assets shall be separately prescribed by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　生产性生物资产按照以下方法确定计税基础：  
（一）外购的生产性生物资产，以购买价款和支付的相关税费为计税基础；  
（二）通过捐赠、投资、非货币性资产交换、债务重组等方式取得的生产性生物资产，以该资产的公允价值和支付的相关税费为计税基础。  
前款所称生产性生物资产，是指企业为生产农产品、提供劳务或者出租等而持有的生物资产，包括经济林、薪炭林、产畜和役畜等。

**Article 62** The tax basis of productive biological assets shall be determined in the following manner:  
(1) for purchased productive biological assets, the tax basis shall be the purchase price plus the relevant taxes and fees paid; and  
(2) for productive biological assets acquired through donation, investment, non-monetary asset exchange, debt restructuring, or other methods, the tax basis shall be the fair value of the asset plus the relevant taxes and fees paid.  
For the purposes of the preceding paragraph, "productive biological assets" mean biological assets held by an enterprise for the production of agricultural products, provision of labor services or leasing, including economic forests, fuel forests, productive livestock and draft animals.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　生产性生物资产按照直线法计算的折旧，准予扣除。  
企业应当自生产性生物资产投入使用月份的次月起计算折旧；停止使用的生产性生物资产，应当自停止使用月份的次月起停止计算折旧。  
企业应当根据生产性生物资产的性质和使用情况，合理确定生产性生物资产的预计净残值。生产性生物资产的预计净残值一经确定，不得变更。

**Article 63** Depreciation calculated on productive biological assets using the straight-line method shall be allowed for deduction.  
An enterprise shall begin calculating depreciation from the month following the month in which the productive biological asset is put into use; for a productive biological asset that ceases to be used, depreciation shall cease from the month following the month in which it ceases to be used.  
An enterprise shall reasonably determine the estimated net residual value of a productive biological asset based on its nature and usage. Once the estimated net residual value of a productive biological asset is determined, it shall not be changed.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　生产性生物资产计算折旧的最低年限如下：  
（一）林木类生产性生物资产，为10年；  
（二）畜类生产性生物资产，为3年。

**Article 64** The minimum depreciation periods for productive biological assets are as follows:  
(1) 10 years for forest-type productive biological assets; and  
(2) 3 years for livestock-type productive biological assets.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　[企业所得税法第十二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-12)所称无形资产，是指企业为生产产品、提供劳务、出租或者经营管理而持有的、没有实物形态的非货币性长期资产，包括专利权、商标权、著作权、土地使用权、非专利技术、商誉等。

**Article 65** For the purposes of Article 12 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "intangible assets" mean non-monetary long-term assets without physical substance held by an enterprise for the production of products, provision of labor services, leasing or business management, including patent rights, trademark rights, copyrights, land use rights, non-patented technology, and goodwill.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　无形资产按照以下方法确定计税基础：  
（一）外购的无形资产，以购买价款和支付的相关税费以及直接归属于使该资产达到预定用途发生的其他支出为计税基础；  
（二）自行开发的无形资产，以开发过程中该资产符合资本化条件后至达到预定用途前发生的支出为计税基础；  
（三）通过捐赠、投资、非货币性资产交换、债务重组等方式取得的无形资产，以该资产的公允价值和支付的相关税费为计税基础。

**Article 66** The tax basis of intangible assets shall be determined in the following manner:  
(1) for purchased intangible assets, the tax basis shall be the purchase price plus the relevant taxes and fees paid and other expenditures directly attributable to bringing the asset to its intended use;  
(2) for self-developed intangible assets, the tax basis shall be the expenditures incurred from the time the asset meets the capitalization conditions during the development process until it reaches its intended use; and  
(3) for intangible assets acquired through donation, investment, non-monetary asset exchange, debt restructuring, or other methods, the tax basis shall be the fair value of the asset plus the relevant taxes and fees paid.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　无形资产按照直线法计算的摊销费用，准予扣除。  
无形资产的摊销年限不得低于10年。  
作为投资或者受让的无形资产，有关法律规定或者合同约定了使用年限的，可以按照规定或者约定的使用年限分期摊销。  
外购商誉的支出，在企业整体转让或者清算时，准予扣除。

**Article 67** Amortization expenses calculated on intangible assets using the straight-line method shall be allowed for deduction.  
The amortization period of an intangible asset shall not be less than 10 years.  
For an intangible asset acquired as an investment or by transfer, if the relevant law or contract specifies a useful life, it may be amortized over the specified or agreed useful life in installments.  
Expenditures on purchased goodwill shall be allowed for deduction upon the overall transfer or liquidation of the enterprise.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　[企业所得税法第十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-13)第（一）项和第（二）项所称固定资产的改建支出，是指改变房屋或者建筑物结构、延长使用年限等发生的支出。  
[企业所得税法第十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-13)第（一）项规定的支出，按照固定资产预计尚可使用年限分期摊销；第（二）项规定的支出，按照合同约定的剩余租赁期限分期摊销。  
改建的固定资产延长使用年限的，除[企业所得税法第十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-13)第（一）项和第（二）项规定外，应当适当延长折旧年限。

**Article 68** For the purposes of subparagraphs (1) and (2) of Article 13 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "reconstruction expenditures on fixed assets" mean expenditures incurred for changing the structure of a house or building, extending the useful life, etc.  
Expenditures specified in subparagraph (1) of Article 13 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law shall be amortized over the estimated remaining useful life of the fixed asset; expenditures specified in subparagraph (2) shall be amortized over the remaining lease term as agreed in the contract.  
Where the useful life of a reconstructed fixed asset is extended, the depreciation period shall be appropriately extended, except as provided in subparagraphs (1) and (2) of Article 13 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　[企业所得税法第十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-13)第（三）项所称固定资产的大修理支出，是指同时符合下列条件的支出：  
（一）修理支出达到取得固定资产时的计税基础50%以上；  
（二）修理后固定资产的使用年限延长2年以上。  
[企业所得税法第十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-13)第（三）项规定的支出，按照固定资产尚可使用年限分期摊销。

**Article 69** For the purposes of subparagraph (3) of Article 13 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "major repair expenditures on fixed assets" mean expenditures that meet both of the following conditions:  
(1) the repair expenditure reaches 50% or more of the tax basis of the fixed asset at the time of acquisition; and  
(2) the useful life of the fixed asset after repair is extended by 2 years or more.  
Expenditures specified in subparagraph (3) of Article 13 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law shall be amortized over the remaining useful life of the fixed asset.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　[企业所得税法第十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-13)第（四）项所称其他应当作为长期待摊费用的支出，自支出发生月份的次月起，分期摊销，摊销年限不得低于3年。

**Article 70** For the purposes of subparagraph (4) of Article 13 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "other expenditures that shall be treated as long-term deferred expenses" shall be amortized in installments from the month following the month in which the expenditure is incurred, and the amortization period shall not be less than 3 years.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　[企业所得税法第十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-14)所称投资资产，是指企业对外进行权益性投资和债权性投资形成的资产。  
企业在转让或者处置投资资产时，投资资产的成本，准予扣除。  
投资资产按照以下方法确定成本：  
（一）通过支付现金方式取得的投资资产，以购买价款为成本；  
（二）通过支付现金以外的方式取得的投资资产，以该资产的公允价值和支付的相关税费为成本。

**Article 71** For the purposes of Article 14 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "investment assets" mean assets formed by an enterprise through equity investments and debt investments made to other entities.  
When an enterprise transfers or disposes of investment assets, the cost of the investment assets shall be allowed for deduction.  
The cost of investment assets shall be determined in the following manner:  
(1) for investment assets acquired by way of cash payment, the cost shall be the purchase price; and  
(2) for investment assets acquired by means other than cash payment, the cost shall be the fair value of the asset plus the relevant taxes and fees paid.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　[企业所得税法第十五条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-15)所称存货，是指企业持有以备出售的产品或者商品、处在生产过程中的在产品、在生产或者提供劳务过程中耗用的材料和物料等。  
存货按照以下方法确定成本：  
（一）通过支付现金方式取得的存货，以购买价款和支付的相关税费为成本；  
（二）通过支付现金以外的方式取得的存货，以该存货的公允价值和支付的相关税费为成本；  
（三）生产性生物资产收获的农产品，以产出或者采收过程中发生的材料费、人工费和分摊的间接费用等必要支出为成本。

**Article 72** For the purposes of Article 15 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "inventory" means products or goods held by an enterprise for sale, work in progress in the course of production, and materials and supplies consumed in the course of production or provision of labor services.  
The cost of inventory shall be determined in the following manner:  
(1) for inventory acquired by way of cash payment, the cost shall be the purchase price plus the relevant taxes and fees paid;  
(2) for inventory acquired by means other than cash payment, the cost shall be the fair value of the inventory plus the relevant taxes and fees paid; and  
(3) for agricultural products harvested from productive biological assets, the cost shall be the necessary expenditures incurred in the course of output or harvesting, such as material costs, labor costs and allocated overhead costs.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　企业使用或者销售的存货的成本计算方法，可以在先进先出法、加权平均法、个别计价法中选用一种。计价方法一经选用，不得随意变更。

**Article 73** An enterprise may choose one of the following methods for calculating the cost of inventory used or sold: the first-in-first-out method, the weighted average method, or the specific identification method. Once a valuation method is chosen, it shall not be changed arbitrarily.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　[企业所得税法第十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-16)所称资产的净值和第十九条所称财产净值，是指有关资产、财产的计税基础减除已经按照规定扣除的折旧、折耗、摊销、准备金等后的余额。

**Article 74** For the purposes of Article 16 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "net value of assets" and for the purposes of Article 19, "net value of property" mean the balance of the tax basis of the relevant assets or property after deducting depreciation, depletion, amortization, reserves, etc., that have been deducted in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　除国务院财政、税务主管部门另有规定外，企业在重组过程中，应当在交易发生时确认有关资产的转让所得或者损失，相关资产应当按照交易价格重新确定计税基础。

**Article 75** Unless otherwise provided by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council, in the course of reorganization, an enterprise shall recognize the gain or loss on the transfer of relevant assets at the time the transaction occurs, and the tax basis of the relevant assets shall be re-determined based on the transaction price.

### 第三章　应纳税额

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　[企业所得税法第二十二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-22)规定的应纳税额的计算公式为：  
应纳税额＝应纳税所得额×适用税率－减免税额－抵免税额  
公式中的减免税额和抵免税额，是指依照企业所得税法和国务院的税收优惠规定减征、免征和抵免的应纳税额。

**Article 76** The formula for calculating the tax payable as specified in Article 22 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law is:  
Tax payable = Taxable income × Applicable tax rate - Tax deducted and exempted - Tax credited  
In the formula, "tax deducted and exempted" and "tax credited" mean the tax payable that is reduced, exempted and credited in accordance with the Enterprise Income Tax Law and the tax preferential provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　[企业所得税法第二十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-23)所称已在境外缴纳的所得税税额，是指企业来源于中国境外的所得依照中国境外税收法律以及相关规定应当缴纳并已经实际缴纳的企业所得税性质的税款。

**Article 77** For the purposes of Article 23 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "income tax already paid abroad" means the tax of the nature of enterprise income tax that is payable and has been actually paid on the income of an enterprise derived from outside China in accordance with the tax laws and relevant provisions of the jurisdiction outside China.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　[企业所得税法第二十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-23)所称抵免限额，是指企业来源于中国境外的所得，依照企业所得税法和本条例的规定计算的应纳税额。除国务院财政、税务主管部门另有规定外，该抵免限额应当分国（地区）不分项计算，计算公式如下：  
抵免限额＝中国境内、境外所得依照企业所得税法和本条例的规定计算的应纳税总额×来源于某国（地区）的应纳税所得额÷中国境内、境外应纳税所得总额

**Article 78** For the purposes of Article 23 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "credit limit" means the amount of tax payable calculated in accordance with the Enterprise Income Tax Law and these Regulations on the income derived by an enterprise from outside China. Unless otherwise provided by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council, the credit limit shall be calculated on a country-by-country (region-by-region) basis without itemization, and the calculation formula is as follows:  
Credit limit = Total tax payable calculated on income from within and outside China in accordance with the Enterprise Income Tax Law and these Regulations × Taxable income derived from a particular country (region) ÷ Total taxable income from within and outside China

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　[企业所得税法第二十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-23)所称5个年度，是指从企业取得的来源于中国境外的所得，已经在中国境外缴纳的企业所得税性质的税额超过抵免限额的当年的次年起连续5个纳税年度。

**Article 79** For the purposes of Article 23 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "five years" means the five consecutive tax years commencing from the year following the year in which the amount of enterprise income tax-type tax paid abroad on the income derived by the enterprise from outside China exceeds the credit limit.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　[企业所得税法第二十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-24)所称直接控制，是指居民企业直接持有外国企业20%以上股份。  
[企业所得税法第二十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-24)所称间接控制，是指居民企业以间接持股方式持有外国企业20%以上股份，具体认定办法由国务院财政、税务主管部门另行制定。

**Article 80** For the purposes of Article 24 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "direct control" means that a resident enterprise directly holds 20% or more of the shares of a foreign enterprise.  
For the purposes of Article 24 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "indirect control" means that a resident enterprise holds 20% or more of the shares of a foreign enterprise through indirect shareholding. The specific method for determining indirect control shall be separately formulated by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　企业依照[企业所得税法第二十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-23)、第二十四条的规定抵免企业所得税税额时，应当提供中国境外税务机关出具的税款所属年度的有关纳税凭证。

**Article 81** When an enterprise credits enterprise income tax in accordance with the provisions of Articles 23 and 24 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, it shall provide the relevant tax payment certificates for the tax year issued by the tax authorities outside China.

### 第四章　税收优惠

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　[企业所得税法第二十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-26)第（一）项所称国债利息收入，是指企业持有国务院财政部门发行的国债取得的利息收入。

**Article 82** For the purposes of subparagraph (1) of Article 26 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "interest income from government bonds" means the interest income obtained by an enterprise from holding government bonds issued by the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　[企业所得税法第二十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-26)第（二）项所称符合条件的居民企业之间的股息、红利等权益性投资收益，是指居民企业直接投资于其他居民企业取得的投资收益。[企业所得税法第二十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-26)第（二）项和第（三）项所称股息、红利等权益性投资收益，不包括连续持有居民企业公开发行并上市流通的股票不足12个月取得的投资收益。

**Article 83** For the purposes of subparagraph (2) of Article 26 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "income from equity investments such as dividends and bonuses between qualified resident enterprises" means the investment income obtained by a resident enterprise from directly investing in another resident enterprise. The income from equity investments such as dividends and bonuses referred to in subparagraphs (2) and (3) of Article 26 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law does not include investment income obtained from holding shares of a resident enterprise that are publicly issued and listed for trading for less than 12 consecutive months.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　[企业所得税法第二十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-26)第（四）项所称符合条件的非营利组织，是指同时符合下列条件的组织：  
（一）依法履行非营利组织登记手续；  
（二）从事公益性或者非营利性活动；  
（三）取得的收入除用于与该组织有关的、合理的支出外，全部用于登记核定或者章程规定的公益性或者非营利性事业；  
（四）财产及其孳息不用于分配；  
（五）按照登记核定或者章程规定，该组织注销后的剩余财产用于公益性或者非营利性目的，或者由登记管理机关转赠给与该组织性质、宗旨相同的组织，并向社会公告；  
（六）投入人对投入该组织的财产不保留或者享有任何财产权利；  
（七）工作人员工资福利开支控制在规定的比例内，不变相分配该组织的财产。  
前款规定的非营利组织的认定管理办法由国务院财政、税务主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 84** For the purposes of subparagraph (4) of Article 26 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "qualified non-profit organizations" mean organizations that meet all of the following conditions:  
(1) they have fulfilled the registration procedures for non-profit organizations according to law;  
(2) they engage in public welfare or non-profit activities;  
(3) the income obtained, except for that used for reasonable expenses related to the organization, is entirely used for the public welfare or non-profit undertakings as approved in the registration or specified in the articles of association;  
(4) their property and its proceeds are not used for distribution;  
(5) in accordance with the approved registration or the provisions of the articles of association, the remaining property after the organization is dissolved shall be used for public welfare or non-profit purposes, or be transferred by the registration authority to an organization with the same nature and purpose, and the transfer shall be announced to the public;  
(6) the contributors do not retain or enjoy any property rights over the property contributed to the organization; and  
(7) the wages and welfare expenses of the staff are controlled within the prescribed proportion, and the property of the organization is not distributed in disguised form.  
The administrative measures for the recognition of non-profit organizations as specified in the preceding paragraph shall be formulated by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　[企业所得税法第二十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-26)第（四）项所称符合条件的非营利组织的收入，不包括非营利组织从事营利性活动取得的收入，但国务院财政、税务主管部门另有规定的除外。

**Article 85** For the purposes of subparagraph (4) of Article 26 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "income of qualified non-profit organizations" does not include income obtained by non-profit organizations from engaging in profit-making activities, unless otherwise provided by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　[企业所得税法第二十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-27)第（一）项规定的企业从事农、林、牧、渔业项目的所得，可以免征、减征企业所得税，是指：  
（一）企业从事下列项目的所得，免征企业所得税：  
1.蔬菜、谷物、薯类、油料、豆类、棉花、麻类、糖料、水果、坚果的种植；  
2.农作物新品种的选育；  
3.中药材的种植；  
4.林木的培育和种植；  
5.牲畜、家禽的饲养；  
6.林产品的采集；  
7.灌溉、农产品初加工、兽医、农技推广、农机作业和维修等农、林、牧、渔服务业项目；  
8.远洋捕捞。  
（二）企业从事下列项目的所得，减半征收企业所得税：  
1.花卉、茶以及其他饮料作物和香料作物的种植；  
2.海水养殖、内陆养殖。  
企业从事国家限制和禁止发展的项目，不得享受本条规定的企业所得税优惠。

**Article 86** The provisions of subparagraph (1) of Article 27 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law that the income of an enterprise from engaging in agriculture, forestry, animal husbandry and fishery projects may be exempted from or reduced on enterprise income tax mean:  
(1) The income derived by an enterprise from engaging in the following projects shall be exempted from enterprise income tax:  
1. cultivation of vegetables, grains, tubers, oil crops, beans, cotton, hemp, sugar crops, fruits and nuts;  
2. breeding of new crop varieties;  
3. cultivation of Chinese herbal medicines;  
4. cultivation and planting of trees;  
5. raising of livestock and poultry;  
6. collection of forest products;  
7. projects in agriculture, forestry, animal husbandry and fishery service industries such as irrigation, primary processing of agricultural products, veterinary services, agricultural technology extension, agricultural machinery operations and maintenance; and  
8. offshore fishing.  
(2) The income derived by an enterprise from engaging in the following projects shall be subject to enterprise income tax at half the rate:  
1. cultivation of flowers, tea and other beverage crops and spice crops; and  
2. mariculture and inland aquaculture.  
An enterprise engaged in projects the development of which is restricted or prohibited by the state shall not enjoy the enterprise income tax benefits provided in this Article.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　[企业所得税法第二十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-27)第（二）项所称国家重点扶持的公共基础设施项目，是指《公共基础设施项目企业所得税优惠目录》规定的港口码头、机场、铁路、公路、城市公共交通、电力、水利等项目。  
企业从事前款规定的国家重点扶持的公共基础设施项目的投资经营的所得，自项目取得第一笔生产经营收入所属纳税年度起，第一年至第三年免征企业所得税，第四年至第六年减半征收企业所得税。  
企业承包经营、承包建设和内部自建自用本条规定的项目，不得享受本条规定的企业所得税优惠。

**Article 87** For the purposes of subparagraph (2) of Article 27 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "key state-supported public infrastructure projects" mean projects such as ports and docks, airports, railways, highways, urban public transportation, electric power, and water conservancy as specified in the Public Infrastructure Projects Enterprise Income Tax Concessions Catalogue.  
The income derived by an enterprise from investing in and operating the key state-supported public infrastructure projects specified in the preceding paragraph shall be exempt from enterprise income tax for the first to third years and subject to enterprise income tax at half the rate for the fourth to sixth years, commencing from the tax year in which the project obtains its first production and business operation income.  
An enterprise that contracts to operate, contracts to construct, or internally builds for its own use the projects specified in this Article shall not enjoy the enterprise income tax benefits provided in this Article.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　[企业所得税法第二十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-27)第（三）项所称符合条件的环境保护、节能节水项目，包括公共污水处理、公共垃圾处理、沼气综合开发利用、节能减排技术改造、海水淡化等。项目的具体条件和范围由国务院财政、税务主管部门商国务院有关部门制订，报国务院批准后公布施行。  
企业从事前款规定的符合条件的环境保护、节能节水项目的所得，自项目取得第一笔生产经营收入所属纳税年度起，第一年至第三年免征企业所得税，第四年至第六年减半征收企业所得税。

**Article 88** For the purposes of subparagraph (3) of Article 27 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "qualified environmental protection, energy conservation and water conservation projects" include public sewage treatment, public garbage treatment, comprehensive development and utilization of biogas, technical transformation for energy conservation and emission reduction, and seawater desalination. The specific conditions and scope of the projects shall be formulated by the competent finance and tax departments of the State Council in consultation with the relevant departments of the State Council, and shall be published and implemented after approval by the State Council.  
The income derived by an enterprise from engaging in the qualified environmental protection, energy conservation and water conservation projects specified in the preceding paragraph shall be exempt from enterprise income tax for the first to third years and subject to enterprise income tax at half the rate for the fourth to sixth years, commencing from the tax year in which the project obtains its first production and business operation income.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　依照[本条例第八十七条](#art-87)和第八十八条规定享受减免税优惠的项目，在减免税期限内转让的，受让方自受让之日起，可以在剩余期限内享受规定的减免税优惠；减免税期限届满后转让的，受让方不得就该项目重复享受减免税优惠。

**Article 89** Where a project that enjoys tax reduction or exemption benefits in accordance with Articles 87 and 88 of these Regulations is transferred within the tax reduction or exemption period, the transferee may, from the date of transfer, enjoy the prescribed tax reduction or exemption benefits for the remaining period. Where the project is transferred after the expiration of the tax reduction or exemption period, the transferee shall not enjoy tax reduction or exemption benefits again for that project.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　[企业所得税法第二十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-27)第（四）项所称符合条件的技术转让所得免征、减征企业所得税，是指一个纳税年度内，居民企业技术转让所得不超过500万元的部分，免征企业所得税；超过500万元的部分，减半征收企业所得税。

**Article 90** For the purposes of subparagraph (4) of Article 27 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the exemption or reduction of enterprise income tax on qualified technology transfer income means that, within a single tax year, the portion of a resident enterprise's technology transfer income that does not exceed RMB 5 million shall be exempt from enterprise income tax, and the portion exceeding RMB 5 million shall be subject to enterprise income tax at half the rate.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　非居民企业取得[企业所得税法第二十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-27)第（五）项规定的所得，减按10%的税率征收企业所得税。  
下列所得可以免征企业所得税：  
（一）外国政府向中国政府提供贷款取得的利息所得；  
（二）国际金融组织向中国政府和居民企业提供优惠贷款取得的利息所得；  
（三）经国务院批准的其他所得。

**Article 91** Where a non-resident enterprise obtains the income specified in subparagraph (5) of Article 27 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, enterprise income tax shall be levied at a reduced rate of 10%.  
The following income may be exempt from enterprise income tax:  
(1) interest income obtained by a foreign government from providing loans to the Chinese government;  
(2) interest income obtained by an international financial organization from providing preferential loans to the Chinese government and resident enterprises; and  
(3) other income approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　[企业所得税法第二十八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-28)第一款所称符合条件的小型微利企业，是指从事国家非限制和禁止行业，并符合下列条件的企业：  
（一）工业企业，年度应纳税所得额不超过30万元，从业人数不超过100人，资产总额不超过3000万元；  
（二）其他企业，年度应纳税所得额不超过30万元，从业人数不超过80人，资产总额不超过1000万元。

**Article 92** For the purposes of the first paragraph of Article 28 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "qualified small low-profit enterprises" mean enterprises engaged in industries not restricted or prohibited by the state and meeting the following conditions:  
(1) for industrial enterprises, the annual taxable income amount does not exceed RMB 300,000, the number of employees does not exceed 100, and the total assets do not exceed RMB 30 million; and  
(2) for other enterprises, the annual taxable income amount does not exceed RMB 300,000, the number of employees does not exceed 80, and the total assets do not exceed RMB 10 million.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　[企业所得税法第二十八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-28)第二款所称国家需要重点扶持的高新技术企业，是指拥有核心自主知识产权，并同时符合下列条件的企业：  
（一）产品（服务）属于《国家重点支持的高新技术领域》规定的范围；  
（二）研究开发费用占销售收入的比例不低于规定比例；  
（三）高新技术产品（服务）收入占企业总收入的比例不低于规定比例；  
（四）科技人员占企业职工总数的比例不低于规定比例；  
（五）高新技术企业认定管理办法规定的其他条件。  
《国家重点支持的高新技术领域》和高新技术企业认定管理办法由国务院工业和信息化、科技、财政、税务主管部门商国务院有关部门制订，报国务院批准后公布施行。

**Article 93** For the purposes of the second paragraph of Article 28 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "high-tech enterprises requiring key state support" mean enterprises that have core independent intellectual property rights and meet all of the following conditions:  
(1) the products (services) fall within the scope specified in the State Key Supported High-Tech Fields;  
(2) the ratio of research and development expenses to sales revenue is not lower than the prescribed ratio;  
(3) the ratio of high-tech product (service) income to the total income of the enterprise is not lower than the prescribed ratio;  
(4) the ratio of scientific and technical personnel to the total number of employees of the enterprise is not lower than the prescribed ratio; and  
(5) other conditions specified in the administrative measures for the recognition of high-tech enterprises.  
The State Key Supported High-Tech Fields and the administrative measures for the recognition of high-tech enterprises shall be formulated by the competent departments of industry and information technology, science and technology, finance, and taxation under the State Council in consultation with the relevant departments of the State Council, and shall be published and implemented after approval by the State Council.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　[企业所得税法第二十九条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-29)所称民族自治地方，是指依照《中华人民共和国民族区域自治法》的规定，实行民族区域自治的自治区、自治州、自治县。  
对民族自治地方内国家限制和禁止行业的企业，不得减征或者免征企业所得税。

**Article 94** For the purposes of Article 29 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "ethnic autonomous areas" mean autonomous regions, autonomous prefectures and autonomous counties that practice ethnic regional autonomy in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Regional Ethnic Autonomy.  
For enterprises in ethnic autonomous areas that are engaged in industries restricted or prohibited by the state, enterprise income tax shall not be reduced or exempted.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　[企业所得税法第三十条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-30)第（一）项所称研究开发费用的加计扣除，是指企业为开发新技术、新产品、新工艺发生的研究开发费用，未形成无形资产计入当期损益的，在按照规定据实扣除的基础上，按照研究开发费用的50%加计扣除；形成无形资产的，按照无形资产成本的150%摊销。

**Article 95** For the purposes of subparagraph (1) of Article 30 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "additional deduction of research and development expenses" means that, for research and development expenses incurred by an enterprise for the development of new technologies, new products, or new processes, if no intangible asset has been formed and such expenses are included in the current profit or loss, an additional deduction of 50% of the research and development expenses shall be allowed on the basis of the actual deduction according to the provisions; if an intangible asset is formed, the intangible asset shall be amortized at 150% of its cost.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　[企业所得税法第三十条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-30)第（二）项所称企业安置残疾人员所支付的工资的加计扣除，是指企业安置残疾人员的，在按照支付给残疾职工工资据实扣除的基础上，按照支付给残疾职工工资的100%加计扣除。残疾人员的范围适用《中华人民共和国残疾人保障法》的有关规定。  
[企业所得税法第三十条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-30)第（二）项所称企业安置国家鼓励安置的其他就业人员所支付的工资的加计扣除办法，由国务院另行规定。

**Article 96** For the purposes of subparagraph (2) of Article 30 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "additional deduction of wages paid by an enterprise for employing disabled persons" means that if an enterprise employs disabled persons, an additional deduction of 100% of the wages paid to disabled employees shall be allowed on the basis of the actual deduction of such wages according to the provisions. The scope of disabled persons shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Persons with Disabilities.  
The method for the additional deduction of wages paid by an enterprise for employing other persons whose employment is encouraged by the state, as referred to in subparagraph (2) of Article 30 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, shall be separately prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　[企业所得税法第三十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-31)所称抵扣应纳税所得额，是指创业投资企业采取股权投资方式投资于未上市的中小高新技术企业2年以上的，可以按照其投资额的70%在股权持有满2年的当年抵扣该创业投资企业的应纳税所得额；当年不足抵扣的，可以在以后纳税年度结转抵扣。

**Article 97** For the purposes of Article 31 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "deduction of taxable income" means that if a venture capital enterprise adopts the equity investment method and invests in a medium or small high-tech enterprise that is not listed for a period of not less than 2 years, it may deduct 70% of its investment amount from its taxable income in the year in which the equity is held for a full 2 years; if the deduction is insufficient in that year, the remaining amount may be carried forward and deducted in subsequent tax years.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　[企业所得税法第三十二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-32)所称可以采取缩短折旧年限或者采取加速折旧的方法的固定资产，包括：  
（一）由于技术进步，产品更新换代较快的固定资产；  
（二）常年处于强震动、高腐蚀状态的固定资产。  
采取缩短折旧年限方法的，最低折旧年限不得低于[本条例第六十条](#art-60)规定折旧年限的60%；采取加速折旧方法的，可以采取双倍余额递减法或者年数总和法。

**Article 98** For the purposes of Article 32 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the fixed assets for which the method of shortening the depreciation period or adopting accelerated depreciation may be applied include:  
(1) fixed assets that, due to technological progress, have rapid product replacement; and  
(2) fixed assets that are constantly in a state of strong vibration or high corrosion.  
Where the method of shortening the depreciation period is adopted, the minimum depreciation period shall not be less than 60% of the depreciation period specified in Article 60 of these Regulations. Where accelerated depreciation is adopted, the double-declining balance method or the sum-of-the-years'-digits method may be used.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　[企业所得税法第三十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-33)所称减计收入，是指企业以《资源综合利用企业所得税优惠目录》规定的资源作为主要原材料，生产国家非限制和禁止并符合国家和行业相关标准的产品取得的收入，减按90%计入收入总额。  
前款所称原材料占生产产品材料的比例不得低于《资源综合利用企业所得税优惠目录》规定的标准。

**Article 99** For the purposes of Article 33 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "reduced calculation of income" means that where an enterprise, using the resources specified in the Enterprise Income Tax Concessions Catalogue for Comprehensive Resource Utilization as the main raw materials, produces products that are not restricted or prohibited by the state and comply with relevant national and industry standards, 90% of the income from such products shall be included in the total income.  
The proportion of the raw materials referred to in the preceding paragraph in the materials used for producing the products shall not be lower than the standard specified in the Enterprise Income Tax Concessions Catalogue for Comprehensive Resource Utilization.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　[企业所得税法第三十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-34)所称税额抵免，是指企业购置并实际使用《环境保护专用设备企业所得税优惠目录》、《节能节水专用设备企业所得税优惠目录》和《安全生产专用设备企业所得税优惠目录》规定的环境保护、节能节水、安全生产等专用设备的，该专用设备的投资额的10%可以从企业当年的应纳税额中抵免；当年不足抵免的，可以在以后5个纳税年度结转抵免。  
享受前款规定的企业所得税优惠的企业，应当实际购置并自身实际投入使用前款规定的专用设备；企业购置上述专用设备在5年内转让、出租的，应当停止享受企业所得税优惠，并补缴已经抵免的企业所得税税款。

**Article 100** For the purposes of Article 34 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, "tax credit" means that if an enterprise purchases and actually uses special equipment for environmental protection, energy conservation and water conservation, and safe production as specified in the Enterprise Income Tax Concessions Catalogue for Special Equipment for Environmental Protection, the Enterprise Income Tax Concessions Catalogue for Special Equipment for Energy Conservation and Water Conservation, and the Enterprise Income Tax Concessions Catalogue for Special Equipment for Safe Production, 10% of the investment amount in such special equipment may be credited against the enterprise's tax payable for the current year; if the credit is insufficient in that year, the remaining amount may be carried forward and credited in the following 5 tax years.  
An enterprise that enjoys the enterprise income tax benefits specified in the preceding paragraph shall actually purchase and itself actually put into use the special equipment specified in the preceding paragraph. If the enterprise transfers or leases out the aforesaid special equipment within 5 years, it shall cease to enjoy the enterprise income tax benefits and pay back the enterprise income tax that has been credited.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　本章第八十七条、第九十九条、第一百条规定的企业所得税优惠目录，由国务院财政、税务主管部门商国务院有关部门制订，报国务院批准后公布施行。

**Article 101** The preferential catalogue for enterprise income tax as prescribed in Articles 87, 99 and 100 of this Chapter shall be formulated by the finance and taxation departments of the State Council in consultation with the relevant departments of the State Council, and shall be published and come into force after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　企业同时从事适用不同企业所得税待遇的项目的，其优惠项目应当单独计算所得，并合理分摊企业的期间费用；没有单独计算的，不得享受企业所得税优惠。

**Article 102** Where an enterprise concurrently operates projects subject to different enterprise income tax treatments, the preferential items shall separately calculate the income and reasonably apportion the period expenses of the enterprise; if no separate calculation is made, the enterprise shall not enjoy the enterprise income tax preferences.

### 第五章　源泉扣缴

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　依照企业所得税法对非居民企业应当缴纳的企业所得税实行源泉扣缴的，应当依照[企业所得税法第十九条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-19)的规定计算应纳税所得额。  
[企业所得税法第十九条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-19)所称收入全额，是指非居民企业向支付人收取的全部价款和价外费用。

**Article 103** Where withholding at source is implemented for the enterprise income tax payable by a non-resident enterprise in accordance with the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the taxable income amount shall be calculated in accordance with the provisions of Article 19 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law.  
The term "total income" as mentioned in Article 19 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law means the total consideration and other charges collected by the non-resident enterprise from the payer.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　[企业所得税法第三十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-37)所称支付人，是指依照有关法律规定或者合同约定对非居民企业直接负有支付相关款项义务的单位或者个人。

**Article 104** The term "payer" as mentioned in Article 37 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law means the unit or individual that has the obligation to directly pay the relevant amount to the non-resident enterprise in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws or the agreement of the contract.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　[企业所得税法第三十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-37)所称支付，包括现金支付、汇拨支付、转账支付和权益兑价支付等货币支付和非货币支付。  
[企业所得税法第三十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-37)所称到期应支付的款项，是指支付人按照权责发生制原则应当计入相关成本、费用的应付款项。

**Article 105** The term "payment" as mentioned in Article 37 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law includes monetary payments and non-monetary payments such as cash payment, remittance payment, transfer payment and equity consideration payment.  
The term "amounts due and payable" as mentioned in Article 37 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law means the amounts payable that shall be included in the relevant costs and expenses by the payer in accordance with the accrual basis principle.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　[企业所得税法第三十八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-38)规定的可以指定扣缴义务人的情形，包括：  
（一）预计工程作业或者提供劳务期限不足一个纳税年度，且有证据表明不履行纳税义务的；  
（二）没有办理税务登记或者临时税务登记，且未委托中国境内的代理人履行纳税义务的；  
（三）未按照规定期限办理企业所得税纳税申报或者预缴申报的。  
前款规定的扣缴义务人，由县级以上税务机关指定，并同时告知扣缴义务人所扣税款的计算依据、计算方法、扣缴期限和扣缴方式。

**Article 106** The circumstances under which a withholding agent may be designated as provided in Article 38 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law include:  
(1) The expected period for engineering operations or provision of services is less than one tax year, and there is evidence showing that the taxpayer fails to perform the tax obligation;  
(2) The taxpayer has not undergone tax registration or temporary tax registration and has not entrusted an agent within the territory of China to perform the tax obligation;  
(3) The taxpayer fails to file the enterprise income tax return or the prepayment return within the prescribed time limit.  
The withholding agent specified in the preceding paragraph shall be designated by the tax authority at or above the county level, and the tax authority shall simultaneously inform the withholding agent of the basis for calculating the tax withheld, the calculation method, the withholding period and the withholding method.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　[企业所得税法第三十九条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-39)所称所得发生地，是指依照[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定的原则确定的所得发生地。在中国境内存在多处所得发生地的，由纳税人选择其中之一申报缴纳企业所得税。

**Article 107** The term "place where the income arises" as mentioned in Article 39 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law means the place where the income arises as determined in accordance with the principles prescribed in Article 7 of these Regulations. Where there are multiple places of income arising within the territory of China, the taxpayer may select one of them to declare and pay the enterprise income tax.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　[企业所得税法第三十九条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-39)所称该纳税人在中国境内其他收入，是指该纳税人在中国境内取得的其他各种来源的收入。  
税务机关在追缴该纳税人应纳税款时，应当将追缴理由、追缴数额、缴纳期限和缴纳方式等告知该纳税人。

**Article 108** The term "other income of the taxpayer within the territory of China" as mentioned in Article 39 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law means the income from various other sources obtained by the taxpayer within the territory of China.  
When the tax authority recovers the tax payable from the taxpayer, it shall inform the taxpayer of the reasons for recovery, the amount recovered, the payment period and the payment method.

### 第六章　特别纳税调整

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　[企业所得税法第四十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-41)所称关联方，是指与企业有下列关联关系之一的企业、其他组织或者个人：  
（一）在资金、经营、购销等方面存在直接或者间接的控制关系；  
（二）直接或者间接地同为第三者控制；  
（三）在利益上具有相关联的其他关系。

**Article 109** The term "related party" as mentioned in Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law means an enterprise, other organization or individual that has any of the following affiliated relationships with an enterprise:  
(1) There is a direct or indirect control relationship in terms of funds, operations, purchases and sales, etc.;  
(2) They are directly or indirectly under the control of a third party;  
(3) There are other relationships that are associated in terms of interests.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　[企业所得税法第四十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-41)所称独立交易原则，是指没有关联关系的交易各方，按照公平成交价格和营业常规进行业务往来遵循的原则。

**Article 110** The term "arm's length principle" as mentioned in Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law means the principle that transaction parties without an affiliated relationship shall conduct business transactions in accordance with fair transaction prices and normal business practices.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　[企业所得税法第四十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-41)所称合理方法，包括：  
（一）可比非受控价格法，是指按照没有关联关系的交易各方进行相同或者类似业务往来的价格进行定价的方法；  
（二）再销售价格法，是指按照从关联方购进商品再销售给没有关联关系的交易方的价格，减除相同或者类似业务的销售毛利进行定价的方法；  
（三）成本加成法，是指按照成本加合理的费用和利润进行定价的方法；  
（四）交易净利润法，是指按照没有关联关系的交易各方进行相同或者类似业务往来取得的净利润水平确定利润的方法；  
（五）利润分割法，是指将企业与其关联方的合并利润或者亏损在各方之间采用合理标准进行分配的方法；  
（六）其他符合独立交易原则的方法。

**Article 111** The term "reasonable methods" as mentioned in Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law includes:  
(1) The comparable uncontrolled price method, which means a pricing method based on the prices at which unrelated parties conduct the same or similar business transactions;  
(2) The resale price method, which means a pricing method based on the price at which goods purchased from a related party are resold to an unrelated party, deducting the gross profit margin of the same or similar business;  
(3) The cost-plus method, which means a pricing method based on cost plus reasonable expenses and profits;  
(4) The transactional net margin method, which means a method for determining profits based on the net profit level obtained from the same or similar business transactions conducted by unrelated parties;  
(5) The profit split method, which means a method for allocating the combined profits or losses of an enterprise and its related parties among them using reasonable standards;  
(6) Other methods that conform to the arm's length principle.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　企业可以依照[企业所得税法第四十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-41)第二款的规定，按照独立交易原则与其关联方分摊共同发生的成本，达成成本分摊协议。  
企业与其关联方分摊成本时，应当按照成本与预期收益相配比的原则进行分摊，并在税务机关规定的期限内，按照税务机关的要求报送有关资料。  
企业与其关联方分摊成本时违反本条第一款、第二款规定的，其自行分摊的成本不得在计算应纳税所得额时扣除。

**Article 112** An enterprise may, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, apportion jointly incurred costs with its related parties in accordance with the arm's length principle and conclude a cost-sharing agreement.  
When apportioning costs with its related parties, an enterprise shall make the apportionment in accordance with the principle of matching costs with expected benefits, and shall submit relevant materials within the time limit prescribed by the tax authority as required by the tax authority.  
Where an enterprise violates the provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article when apportioning costs with its related parties, the costs apportioned by itself shall not be deducted in calculating the taxable income amount.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　[企业所得税法第四十二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-42)所称预约定价安排，是指企业就其未来年度关联交易的定价原则和计算方法，向税务机关提出申请，与税务机关按照独立交易原则协商、确认后达成的协议。

**Article 113** The term "advance pricing arrangement" as mentioned in Article 42 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law means an agreement reached after an enterprise applies to the tax authority for the pricing principles and calculation methods for its future related party transactions and negotiates and confirms with the tax authority in accordance with the arm's length principle.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　[企业所得税法第四十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-43)所称相关资料，包括：  
（一）与关联业务往来有关的价格、费用的制定标准、计算方法和说明等同期资料；  
（二）关联业务往来所涉及的财产、财产使用权、劳务等的再销售（转让）价格或者最终销售（转让）价格的相关资料；  
（三）与关联业务调查有关的其他企业应当提供的与被调查企业可比的产品价格、定价方式以及利润水平等资料；  
（四）其他与关联业务往来有关的资料。  
[企业所得税法第四十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-43)所称与关联业务调查有关的其他企业，是指与被调查企业在生产经营内容和方式上相类似的企业。  
企业应当在税务机关规定的期限内提供与关联业务往来有关的价格、费用的制定标准、计算方法和说明等资料。关联方以及与关联业务调查有关的其他企业应当在税务机关与其约定的期限内提供相关资料。

**Article 114** The term "relevant materials" as mentioned in Article 43 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law includes:  
(1) Contemporaneous documents such as the pricing standards, calculation methods and explanations related to the prices and expenses of related party transactions;  
(2) Materials related to the resale (transfer) price or the final sale (transfer) price of the property, the right to use property, labor services, etc. involved in the related party transactions;  
(3) Materials such as product prices, pricing methods and profit levels comparable to those of the investigated enterprise that shall be provided by other enterprises related to the related party investigation;  
(4) Other materials related to related party transactions.  
The term "other enterprises related to the related party investigation" as mentioned in Article 43 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law means enterprises that are similar to the investigated enterprise in terms of production and operation content and methods.  
An enterprise shall provide, within the time limit prescribed by the tax authority, materials such as the pricing standards, calculation methods and explanations related to the prices and expenses of related party transactions. The related parties and other enterprises related to the related party investigation shall provide relevant materials within the time limit agreed with the tax authority.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　税务机关依照[企业所得税法第四十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-44)的规定核定企业的应纳税所得额时，可以采用下列方法：  
（一）参照同类或者类似企业的利润率水平核定；  
（二）按照企业成本加合理的费用和利润的方法核定；  
（三）按照关联企业集团整体利润的合理比例核定；  
（四）按照其他合理方法核定。  
企业对税务机关按照前款规定的方法核定的应纳税所得额有异议的，应当提供相关证据，经税务机关认定后，调整核定的应纳税所得额。

**Article 115** Where the tax authority determines the taxable income amount of an enterprise by assessment in accordance with Article 44 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, it may adopt the following methods:  
(1) Assessment by reference to the profit rate level of the same or similar enterprises;  
(2) Assessment by the method of cost plus reasonable expenses and profits;  
(3) Assessment by a reasonable proportion of the overall profit of the group of related enterprises;  
(4) Assessment by other reasonable methods.  
Where an enterprise has objections to the taxable income amount determined by the tax authority by assessment in accordance with the method prescribed in the preceding paragraph, it shall provide relevant evidence, and the assessed taxable income amount shall be adjusted after being confirmed by the tax authority.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　[企业所得税法第四十五条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-45)所称中国居民，是指根据《中华人民共和国个人所得税法》的规定，就其从中国境内、境外取得的所得在中国缴纳个人所得税的个人。

**Article 116** The term "Chinese resident" as mentioned in Article 45 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law means an individual who pays individual income tax in China on income derived from within and outside the territory of China in accordance with the provisions of the Individual Income Tax Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　[企业所得税法第四十五条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-45)所称控制，包括：  
（一）居民企业或者中国居民直接或者间接单一持有外国企业10%以上有表决权股份，且由其共同持有该外国企业50%以上股份；  
（二）居民企业，或者居民企业和中国居民持股比例没有达到第（一）项规定的标准，但在股份、资金、经营、购销等方面对该外国企业构成实质控制。

**Article 117** The term "control" as mentioned in Article 45 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law includes:  
(1) A resident enterprise or a Chinese resident directly or indirectly holds alone more than 10% of the voting shares of a foreign enterprise, and jointly holds more than 50% of the shares of the foreign enterprise;  
(2) The shareholding proportion of the resident enterprise, or of the resident enterprise and the Chinese resident, does not meet the standard specified in item (1), but constitutes substantive control over the foreign enterprise in terms of shares, funds, operations, purchases and sales, etc.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　[企业所得税法第四十五条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-45)所称实际税负明显低于[企业所得税法第四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-4)第一款规定税率水平，是指低于[企业所得税法第四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-4)第一款规定税率的50%。

**Article 118** The term "actual tax burden is obviously lower than the tax rate level prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 4 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law" as mentioned in Article 45 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law means less than 50% of the tax rate prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 4 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　[企业所得税法第四十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-46)所称债权性投资，是指企业直接或者间接从关联方获得的，需要偿还本金和支付利息或者需要以其他具有支付利息性质的方式予以补偿的融资。  
企业间接从关联方获得的债权性投资，包括：  
（一）关联方通过无关联第三方提供的债权性投资；  
（二）无关联第三方提供的、由关联方担保且负有连带责任的债权性投资；  
（三）其他间接从关联方获得的具有负债实质的债权性投资。  
[企业所得税法第四十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-46)所称权益性投资，是指企业接受的不需要偿还本金和支付利息，投资人对企业净资产拥有所有权的投资。  
[企业所得税法第四十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-46)所称标准，由国务院财政、税务主管部门另行规定。

**Article 119** The term "debt investment" as mentioned in Article 46 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law means financing obtained by an enterprise directly or indirectly from a related party, which requires repayment of principal and payment of interest or compensation in other ways with the nature of paying interest.  
Debt investments obtained indirectly by an enterprise from a related party include:  
(1) Debt investment provided by a related party through an unrelated third party;  
(2) Debt investment provided by an unrelated third party, guaranteed by a related party and subject to joint and several liability;  
(3) Other debt investments obtained indirectly from a related party that have the substance of liabilities.  
The term "equity investment" as mentioned in Article 46 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law means an investment accepted by an enterprise that does not require repayment of principal or payment of interest, and the investor has ownership of the enterprise's net assets.  
The term "standards" as mentioned in Article 46 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law shall be separately prescribed by the finance and taxation departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　[企业所得税法第四十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-47)所称不具有合理商业目的，是指以减少、免除或者推迟缴纳税款为主要目的。

**Article 120** The term "without reasonable commercial purpose" as mentioned in Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law means having the reduction, exemption or deferral of tax payment as the main purpose.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　税务机关根据税收法律、行政法规的规定，对企业作出特别纳税调整的，应当对补征的税款，自税款所属纳税年度的次年6月1日起至补缴税款之日止的期间，按日加收利息。  
前款规定加收的利息，不得在计算应纳税所得额时扣除。

**Article 121** Where the tax authority makes a special tax adjustment to an enterprise in accordance with the provisions of tax laws and administrative regulations, it shall charge interest on a daily basis on the tax recovered from June 1 of the year following the tax year to which the tax relates to the date of payment of the recovered tax.  
The interest charged as prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall not be deducted in calculating the taxable income amount.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　[企业所得税法第四十八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-48)所称利息，应当按照税款所属纳税年度中国人民银行公布的与补税期间同期的人民币贷款基准利率加5个百分点计算。  
企业依照[企业所得税法第四十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-43)和本条例的规定提供有关资料的，可以只按前款规定的人民币贷款基准利率计算利息。

**Article 122** The term "interest" as mentioned in Article 48 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law shall be calculated at the benchmark interest rate for RMB loans published by the People's Bank of China for the same period as the tax recovery period plus 5 percentage points in the tax year to which the tax relates.  
Where an enterprise provides relevant materials in accordance with Article 43 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and the provisions of these Regulations, the interest may be calculated only at the benchmark interest rate for RMB loans as prescribed in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　企业与其关联方之间的业务往来，不符合独立交易原则，或者企业实施其他不具有合理商业目的安排的，税务机关有权在该业务发生的纳税年度起10年内，进行纳税调整。

**Article 123** Where the business transactions between an enterprise and its related parties do not conform to the arm's length principle, or the enterprise implements other arrangements without reasonable commercial purpose, the tax authority has the right to make tax adjustments within 10 years from the tax year in which such transactions occur.

### 第七章　征收管理

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　[企业所得税法第五十条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-50)所称企业登记注册地，是指企业依照国家有关规定登记注册的住所地。

**Article 124** The term "place of registration of an enterprise" as mentioned in Article 50 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law means the place of domicile where the enterprise is registered in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　企业汇总计算并缴纳企业所得税时，应当统一核算应纳税所得额，具体办法由国务院财政、税务主管部门另行制定。

**Article 125** Where an enterprise calculates and pays enterprise income tax on a consolidated basis, it shall uniformly calculate the taxable income amount. The specific measures shall be separately formulated by the finance and taxation departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条　[企业所得税法第五十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-51)所称主要机构、场所，应当同时符合下列条件：  
（一）对其他各机构、场所的生产经营活动负有监督管理责任；  
（二）设有完整的账簿、凭证，能够准确反映各机构、场所的收入、成本、费用和盈亏情况。

**Article 126** The term "principal institution or establishment" as mentioned in Article 51 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law shall meet the following conditions at the same time:  
(1) It is responsible for the supervision and management of the production and operation activities of other institutions or establishments;  
(2) It maintains complete account books and vouchers, and can accurately reflect the income, costs, expenses and profit or loss of each institution or establishment.

<a id="art-127"></a>第一百二十七条　企业所得税分月或者分季预缴，由税务机关具体核定。  
企业根据[企业所得税法第五十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-54)规定分月或者分季预缴企业所得税时，应当按照月度或者季度的实际利润额预缴；按照月度或者季度的实际利润额预缴有困难的，可以按照上一纳税年度应纳税所得额的月度或者季度平均额预缴，或者按照经税务机关认可的其他方法预缴。预缴方法一经确定，该纳税年度内不得随意变更。

**Article 127** The prepayment of enterprise income tax on a monthly or quarterly basis shall be specifically determined by the tax authority.  
Where an enterprise prepays enterprise income tax on a monthly or quarterly basis in accordance with Article 54 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, it shall prepay tax based on the actual profit amount of the month or quarter; if it is difficult to prepay tax based on the actual profit amount of the month or quarter, it may prepay tax based on the monthly or quarterly average amount of the taxable income amount of the preceding tax year, or prepay tax by other methods approved by the tax authority. Once the prepayment method is determined, it shall not be changed arbitrarily during that tax year.

<a id="art-128"></a>第一百二十八条　企业在纳税年度内无论盈利或者亏损，都应当依照[企业所得税法第五十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-54)规定的期限，向税务机关报送预缴企业所得税纳税申报表、年度企业所得税纳税申报表、财务会计报告和税务机关规定应当报送的其他有关资料。

**Article 128** Regardless of whether an enterprise has profit or loss in a tax year, it shall, within the time limit prescribed in Article 54 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, submit to the tax authority the prepayment return for enterprise income tax, the annual enterprise income tax return, the financial accounting report and other relevant materials that the tax authority requires to be submitted.

<a id="art-129"></a>第一百二十九条　企业所得以人民币以外的货币计算的，预缴企业所得税时，应当按照月度或者季度最后一日的人民币汇率中间价，折合成人民币计算应纳税所得额。年度终了汇算清缴时，对已经按照月度或者季度预缴税款的，不再重新折合计算，只就该纳税年度内未缴纳企业所得税的部分，按照纳税年度最后一日的人民币汇率中间价，折合成人民币计算应纳税所得额。  
经税务机关检查确认，企业少计或者多计前款规定的所得的，应当按照检查确认补税或者退税时的上一个月最后一日的人民币汇率中间价，将少计或者多计的所得折合成人民币计算应纳税所得额，再计算应补缴或者应退的税款。

**Article 129** Where an enterprise's income is calculated in a currency other than RMB, when prepaying enterprise income tax, the taxable income amount shall be converted into RMB at the central parity rate of RMB on the last day of the month or quarter. At the end of the year for final settlement, the tax already prepaid on a monthly or quarterly basis shall not be reconverted; only for the part of the enterprise income tax that has not been paid in that tax year, the taxable income amount shall be converted into RMB at the central parity rate of RMB on the last day of the tax year.  
Where it is confirmed by the tax authority upon inspection that the enterprise has understated or overstated the income as prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the understated or overstated income shall be converted into RMB at the central parity rate of RMB on the last day of the month preceding the month in which the supplementary tax payment or tax refund confirmed by the inspection is made, to calculate the taxable income amount, and then calculate the amount of tax to be supplemented or refunded.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-130"></a>第一百三十条　[企业所得税法第五十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-57)第一款所称本法公布前已经批准设立的企业，是指企业所得税法公布前已经完成登记注册的企业。

**Article 130** The term "enterprises that have been approved to be established before the promulgation of this Law" as mentioned in paragraph 1 of Article 57 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law means enterprises that have completed registration before the promulgation of the Enterprise Income Tax Law.

<a id="art-131"></a>第一百三十一条　在香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区成立的企业，参照适用[企业所得税法第二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法.md#art-2)第二款、第三款的有关规定。

**Article 131** For enterprises established in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region and the Taiwan region, the relevant provisions of paragraph 2 and paragraph 3 of Article 2 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-132"></a>第一百三十二条　本条例自2008年1月1日起施行。1991年6月30日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国外商投资企业和外国企业所得税法实施细则》和1994年2月4日财政部发布的《中华人民共和国企业所得税暂行条例实施细则》同时废止。

**Article 132** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2008. The Detailed Rules for the Implementation of the Income Tax Law of the People's Republic of China for Enterprises with Foreign Investment and Foreign Enterprises promulgated by the State Council on June 30, 1991, and the Detailed Rules for the Implementation of the Provisional Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Enterprise Income Tax promulgated by the Ministry of Finance on February 4, 1994, shall be abolished simultaneously.
