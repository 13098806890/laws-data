# 保税区海关监管办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08

> (1997年6月10日国务院批准　1997年8月1日海关总署令第65号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强与完善海关对保税区的监管，促进保税区的健康发展，根据海关法和其他有关法律的规定，制定本办法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内设立保税区，必须经国务院批准。 第三条　保税区是海关监管的特定区域。海关依照本办法对进出保税区的货物、运输工具、个人携带物品实施监管。 保税区与中华人民共和国境内的其他地区(以下简称非保税区)之间，应当设置符合海关监管要求的隔离设施。 第四条　保税区内仅设置保税区行政管理机构和企业。除安全保卫人员外，其他人员不得在保税区内居住。 第五条　在保税区内设立的企业(以下简称区内企业)，应当向海关办理注册手续。 区内企业应当依照国家有关法律、行政法规的规定设置账簿、编制报表，凭合法、有效凭证记账并进行核算，记录有关进出保税区货物和物品的库存、转让、转移、销售、加工、使用和损耗等情况。 第六条　保税区实行海关稽查制度。 区内企业应当与海关实行电子计算机联网，进行电子数据交换。 第七条　海关对进出保税区的货物、物品、运输工具、人员及区内有关场所，有权依照海关法的规定进行检查、查验。 第八条　国家禁止进出口的货物、物品，不得进出保税区。 第二章　对保税区与境外之间进出货物的监管 第九条　海关对保税区与境外之间进出的货物，实施简便、有效的监管。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强与完善海关对保税区的监管，促进保税区的健康发展，根据海关法和其他有关法律的规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Customs Law and the provisions of other relevant laws for the purposes of strengthening and improving the Customs supervision and control over the bonded areas and promoting the healthy development of the bonded areas.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内设立保税区，必须经国务院批准。

**Article 2** The establishment of a bonded area within the territory of the People's Republic of China must be approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　保税区是海关监管的特定区域。海关依照本办法对进出保税区的货物、运输工具、个人携带物品实施监管。  
保税区与中华人民共和国境内的其他地区(以下简称非保税区)之间，应当设置符合海关监管要求的隔离设施。

**Article 3** A bonded area is a specific area under Customs supervision and control. The Customs shall, in accordance with these Measures, exercise supervision and control over goods, means of transport, and articles carried by individuals entering or leaving the bonded area.  
Between a bonded area and other areas within the territory of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as non-bonded areas), isolation facilities that meet the requirements of Customs supervision and control shall be installed.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　保税区内仅设置保税区行政管理机构和企业。除安全保卫人员外，其他人员不得在保税区内居住。

**Article 4** Only the administrative organs of the bonded area and enterprises shall be established within the bonded area. Except for security guards, no other persons shall reside in the bonded area.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在保税区内设立的企业(以下简称区内企业)，应当向海关办理注册手续。  
区内企业应当依照国家有关法律、行政法规的规定设置账簿、编制报表，凭合法、有效凭证记账并进行核算，记录有关进出保税区货物和物品的库存、转让、转移、销售、加工、使用和损耗等情况。

**Article 5** An enterprise established in a bonded area (hereinafter referred to as an in-area enterprise) shall undergo registration formalities with the Customs.  
An in-area enterprise shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant State laws and administrative regulations, set up account books, prepare statements, keep accounts and conduct accounting based on lawful and valid vouchers, and record information regarding the storage, transfer, assignment, sale, processing, use, and wastage of goods and articles entering or leaving the bonded area.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　保税区实行海关稽查制度。  
区内企业应当与海关实行电子计算机联网，进行电子数据交换。

**Article 6** The Customs inspection system shall be implemented in the bonded area.  
An in-area enterprise shall be linked with the Customs through computer networks for electronic data exchange.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　海关对进出保税区的货物、物品、运输工具、人员及区内有关场所，有权依照海关法的规定进行检查、查验。

**Article 7** The Customs shall have the authority, in accordance with the provisions of the Customs Law, to examine and inspect the goods, articles, means of transport, persons entering or leaving the bonded area, and relevant places within the bonded area.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家禁止进出口的货物、物品，不得进出保税区。

**Article 8** Goods and articles whose import or export is prohibited by the State shall not enter or leave the bonded area.

### 第二章　对保税区与境外之间进出货物的监管

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　海关对保税区与境外之间进出的货物，实施简便、有效的监管。

**Article 9** The Customs shall exercise simple and effective supervision and control over goods entering or leaving the bonded area from or to areas outside the territory.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　保税区与境外之间进出的货物，由货物的收货人、发货人或其代理人向海关备案。

**Article 10** Goods entering or leaving the bonded area from or to areas outside the territory shall be recorded with the Customs by the consignee, consignor, or their agents.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对保税区与境外之间进出的货物，除实行出口被动配额管理的外，不实行进出口配额、许可证管理。

**Article 11** For goods entering and leaving between the bonded area and the territory outside China, except those subject to export passive quota administration, no import and export quota or license administration shall be applied.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　从境外进入保税区的货物，其进口关税和进口环节税收，除法律、行政法规另有规定外，按照下列规定办理：  
(一)区内生产性的基础设施建设项目所需的机器、设备和其他基建物资，予以免税；  
(二)区内企业自用的生产、管理设备和自用合理数量的办公用品及其所需的维修零配件，生产用燃料，建设生产厂房、仓储设施所需的物资、设备，予以免税；  
(三)保税区行政管理机构自用合理数量的管理设备和办公用品及其所需的维修零配件，予以免税；  
(四)区内企业为加工出口产品所需的原材料、零部件、元器件、包装物件，予以保税。  
前款第(一)项至第(四)项规定范围以外的货物或者物品从境外进入保税区，应当依法纳税。  
转口货物和在保税区内储存的货物按照保税货物管理。

**Article 12** For goods entering the bonded area from outside China, the import customs duties and import-link taxes shall be handled in accordance with the following provisions, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations:  
(1) Machinery, equipment and other capital construction materials required for productive infrastructure construction projects within the area shall be exempted from duties;  
(2) Production and management equipment for self-use by enterprises within the area, office supplies in reasonable quantities for self-use, and the necessary maintenance spare parts, fuel for production, and materials and equipment required for the construction of production workshops and storage facilities shall be exempted from duties;  
(3) Management equipment and office supplies in reasonable quantities for self-use by the administrative authorities of the bonded area, and the necessary maintenance spare parts shall be exempted from duties;  
(4) Raw materials, spare parts, components, and packaging materials required by enterprises within the area for processing export products shall be kept under bonded status.  
Goods or articles entering the bonded area from outside China that are not covered by the provisions of paragraphs (1) to (4) of the preceding paragraph shall be subject to tax payment according to law.  
Transshipment goods and goods stored in the bonded area shall be managed as bonded goods.

### 第三章　对保税区与非保税区之间进出货物的监管

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　从保税区进入非保税区的货物，按照进口货物办理手续；从非保税区进入保税区的货物，按照出口货物办理手续，出口退税按照国家有关规定办理。  
海关对保税区与非保税区之间进出的货物，按照国家有关进出口管理的规定实施监管。

**Article 13** Goods entering the non-bonded area from the bonded area shall go through procedures as import goods; goods entering the bonded area from the non-bonded area shall go through procedures as export goods, and export tax rebates shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The Customs shall exercise supervision over goods entering and leaving between the bonded area and the non-bonded area in accordance with the provisions of the State on import and export administration.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　从非保税区进入保税区供区内使用的机器、设备、基建物资和物品，使用单位应当向海关提供上述货物或者物品的清单，经海关查验后放行。  
前款货物或者物品，已经缴纳进口关税和进口环节税收的，已纳税款不予退还。

**Article 14** For machinery, equipment, capital construction materials and articles entering the bonded area from the non-bonded area for use within the area, the user unit shall provide the Customs with a list of the said goods or articles, which shall be released after Customs inspection.  
For the goods or articles mentioned in the preceding paragraph, if the import customs duties and import-link taxes have already been paid, the taxes paid shall not be refunded.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　保税区的货物需从非保税区口岸进出口或者保税区内的货物运往另一保税区的，应当事先向海关提出书面申请，经海关批准后，按照海关转关运输及有关规定办理。

**Article 15** Where goods in the bonded area need to be imported or exported through a port in the non-bonded area, or goods in the bonded area need to be transported to another bonded area, a written application shall be submitted to the Customs in advance, and after approval by the Customs, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Customs transit transportation and relevant provisions.

### 第四章　对保税区内货物的监管

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　保税区内的货物可以在区内企业之间转让、转移；双方当事人应当就转让、转移事项向海关备案。

**Article 16** Goods within the bonded area may be transferred or moved between enterprises within the area; both parties shall file with the Customs for the record regarding the transfer or movement.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　保税区内的转口货物可以在区内仓库或者区内其他场所进行分级、挑选、刷贴标志、改换包装形式等简单加工。

**Article 17** Transshipment goods within the bonded area may undergo simple processing such as grading, sorting, labeling, and changing packaging forms in warehouses or other places within the area.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　区内企业在保税区内举办境外商品和非保税区商品的展示活动，展示的商品应当接受海关监管。

**Article 18** Enterprises within the area that hold exhibition activities of goods from outside China and goods from the non-bonded area within the bonded area shall have the exhibited goods subject to Customs supervision.

### 第五章　对保税区加工贸易货物的管理

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　区内加工企业应当向海关办理所需料、件进出保税区备案手续。

**Article 19** Processing enterprises within the area shall go through the filing procedures with the Customs for the entry and exit of required materials and parts in and out of the bonded area.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　区内加工企业生产属于被动配额管理的出口产品，应当事先经国务院有关主管部门批准。

**Article 20** Where processing enterprises within the area produce export products subject to passive quota administration, approval shall be obtained in advance from the relevant competent department of the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　区内加工企业加工的制成品及其在加工过程中产生的边角余料运往境外时，应当按照国家有关规定向海关办理手续；除法律、行政法规另有规定外，免征出口关税。  
区内加工企业将区内加工的制成品、副次品或者在加工过程中产生的边角余料运往非保税区时，应当按照国家有关规定向海关办理进口报关手续，并依法纳税。

**Article 21** When the finished products processed by processing enterprises in the zone and the leftover materials generated during the processing are transported outside the territory, the enterprises shall go through the Customs formalities in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; unless otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations, export duties shall be exempted.  
When the processing enterprises in the zone transport finished products, substandard products, or leftover materials generated during the processing to non-bonded areas, they shall go through the import declaration formalities with the Customs in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and pay taxes according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　区内加工企业全部用境外运入料、件加工的制成品销往非保税区时，海关按照进口制成品征税。  
用含有境外运入料、件加工的制成品销往非保税区时，海关对其制成品按照所含境外运入料、件征税；对所含境外运入料、件的品名、数量、价值申报不实的，海关按照进口制成品征税。

**Article 22** When processing enterprises in the zone sell finished products processed entirely from materials and parts transported into the zone from outside the territory to non-bonded areas, the Customs shall levy duties according to imported finished products.  
When finished products processed with materials and parts containing those transported from outside the territory are sold to non-bonded areas, the Customs shall levy duties on such finished products according to the content of materials and parts transported from outside the territory; if the declaration of the name, quantity, or value of the materials and parts transported from outside the territory is false, the Customs shall levy duties according to imported finished products.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　区内加工企业委托非保税区企业或者接受非保税区企业委托进行加工业务，应当事先经海关批准，并符合下列条件：  
(一)在区内拥有生产场所，并已经正式开展加工业务；  
(二)委托非保税区企业的加工业务，主要工序应当在区内进行；  
(三)委托非保税区企业加工业务的期限为6个月；有特殊情况需要延长期限的，应当向海关申请展期，展期期限为6个月。在非保税区加工完毕的产品应当运回保税区；需要从非保税区直接出口的，应当向海关办理核销手续；  
(四)接受非保税区企业委托加工的，由区内加工企业向海关办理委托加工料、件的备案手续，委托加工的料、件及产品应当与区内企业的料、件及产品分别建立账册并分别使用。加工完毕的产品应当运回非保税区企业，并由区内加工企业向海关销案。

**Article 23** A processing enterprise in the zone that entrusts a non-bonded area enterprise to carry out processing operations or accepts entrustment from a non-bonded area enterprise to carry out processing operations shall obtain prior approval from the Customs and meet the following conditions:  
(1) it has a production site in the zone and has formally commenced processing operations;  
(2) for processing operations entrusted to a non-bonded area enterprise, the main processes shall be carried out in the zone;  
(3) the period for processing operations entrusted to a non-bonded area enterprise shall be six months; if an extension is needed due to special circumstances, an application for extension shall be submitted to the Customs, and the extension period shall be six months. Products processed in the non-bonded area shall be transported back to the bonded zone; if they need to be directly exported from the non-bonded area, the enterprise shall go through the cancellation formalities with the Customs;  
(4) where the processing enterprise in the zone accepts entrustment from a non-bonded area enterprise for processing, it shall go through the filing formalities for the entrusted processing materials and parts with the Customs, and separate accounts shall be established and separately used for the entrusted processing materials, parts, and products from those of the zone enterprise. Processed products shall be transported back to the non-bonded area enterprise, and the processing enterprise in the zone shall close the case with the Customs.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　海关对区内加工企业进料加工、来料加工业务，不实行加工贸易银行保证金台账制度。  
委托非保税区企业进行加工业务的，由非保税区企业向当地海关办理合同登记备案手续，并实行加工贸易银行保证金台账制度。

**Article 24** For the processing with imported materials and processing with supplied materials operations of processing enterprises in the zone, the Customs shall not implement the processing trade bank guarantee ledger system.  
Where processing operations are entrusted to a non-bonded area enterprise, the non-bonded area enterprise shall go through the contract registration and filing formalities with the local Customs, and the processing trade bank guarantee ledger system shall be implemented.

### 第六章　对进出保税区运输工具和个人携带物品的监管

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　运输工具和人员进出保税区，应当经由海关指定的专用通道，并接受海关检查。

**Article 25** Means of transport and personnel entering or leaving the bonded zone shall pass through the special passages designated by the Customs and shall be subject to Customs inspection.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　进出保税区的运输工具的负责人，应当持保税区主管机关批准的证件连同运输工具的名称、数量、牌照号码及驾驶员姓名等清单，向海关办理登记备案手续。

**Article 26** The person in charge of a means of transport entering or leaving the bonded zone shall present the documents approved by the competent authority of the bonded zone together with a list of the name, quantity, license plate number, and driver's name of the means of transport, and go through the registration and filing formalities with the Customs.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　未经海关批准，从保税区到非保税区的运输工具和人员不得运输、携带保税区内的免税货物、物品，保税货物，以及用保税料、件生产的产品。

**Article 27** Without Customs approval, means of transport and personnel moving from the bonded zone to non-bonded areas shall not transport or carry duty-free goods, articles, bonded goods, or products manufactured with bonded materials and parts within the bonded zone.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　违反本办法规定的，由海关依照《中华人民共和国海关法》及《中华人民共和国海关行政处罚实施条例》的规定处理；情节严重的，海关可以取消区内企业在海关的注册资格。

**Article 28** Anyone who violates these Measures shall be dealt with by the Customs in accordance with the provisions of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China and the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Implementation of Customs Administrative Penalties; if the circumstances are serious, the Customs may cancel the registration qualification of the enterprise in the zone with the Customs.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本办法规定的有关备案的具体办法，由海关总署制定。

**Article 29** The specific measures for filing as provided for in these Measures shall be formulated by the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本办法自发布之日起施行。《中华人民共和国海关对进出上海外高桥保税区货物、运输工具和个人携带物品的管理办法》同时废止。

**Article 30** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Measures of the Customs of the People's Republic of China for the Administration of Goods, Means of Transport, and Articles carried by Individuals Entering and Leaving the Shanghai Waigaoqiao Bonded Zone shall be abolished simultaneously.
