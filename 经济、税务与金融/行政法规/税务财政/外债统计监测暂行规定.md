# 外债统计监测暂行规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2020-11-29  
**生效日期**：2020-11-29

> (1987年6月17日国务院批准　1987年8月27日国家外汇管理局发布  根据2020年11月29日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了准确、及时、全面地集中全国的外债信息，有效地控制对外借款规模，提高利用国外资金的效益，促进国民经济的发展，特制定本规定。 第二条　国家对外债实行登记管理制度。 国家外汇管理局负责建立和健全全国外债统计监测系统，对外公布外债数字。 第三条　本规定所称的外债是指中国境内的机关、团体、企业、事业单位、金融机构或者其他机构(以下统称借款单位)对中国境外的国际金融组织、外国政府、金融机构、企业或者其他机构用外国货币承担的具有契约性偿还义务的全部债务，包括： (一)国际金融组织贷款； (二)外国政府贷款； (三)外国银行和金融机构贷款； (四)买方信贷； (五)外国企业贷款； (六)发行外币债券； (七)国际金融租赁； (八)延期付款； (九)补偿贸易中直接以现汇偿还的债务； (十)其他形式的对外债务。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了准确、及时、全面地集中全国的外债信息，有效地控制对外借款规模，提高利用国外资金的效益，促进国民经济的发展，特制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of accurately, timely and comprehensively collecting national information on external debts, effectively controlling the scale of foreign borrowing, improving the efficiency of utilizing foreign funds, and promoting the development of the national economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家对外债实行登记管理制度。  
国家外汇管理局负责建立和健全全国外债统计监测系统，对外公布外债数字。

**Article 2** The State shall implement a registration administration system for external debts.  
The State Administration of Foreign Exchange shall be responsible for establishing and perfecting the national statistical monitoring system for external debts, and for publishing the figures of external debts.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本规定所称的外债是指中国境内的机关、团体、企业、事业单位、金融机构或者其他机构(以下统称借款单位)对中国境外的国际金融组织、外国政府、金融机构、企业或者其他机构用外国货币承担的具有契约性偿还义务的全部债务，包括：  
(一)国际金融组织贷款；  
(二)外国政府贷款；  
(三)外国银行和金融机构贷款；  
(四)买方信贷；  
(五)外国企业贷款；  
(六)发行外币债券；  
(七)国际金融租赁；  
(八)延期付款；  
(九)补偿贸易中直接以现汇偿还的债务；  
(十)其他形式的对外债务。  
借款单位向在中国境内注册的外资银行借入的外汇资金视同外债。  
在中国境内注册的外资银行向外借入的外汇资金不视为外债。

**Article 3** "External debts" as mentioned in these Provisions refer to all debts with contractual repayment obligations in foreign currency assumed by organs, organizations, enterprises, institutions, financial institutions or other entities within the territory of China (hereinafter collectively referred to as "borrowing entities") to international financial organizations, foreign governments, financial institutions, enterprises or other entities outside the territory of China, including:  
(1) loans from international financial organizations;  
(2) loans from foreign governments;  
(3) loans from foreign banks and financial institutions;  
(4) buyer's credit;  
(5) loans from foreign enterprises;  
(6) issuance of bonds in foreign currency;  
(7) international financial leasing;  
(8) deferred payment;  
(9) debts directly repaid in cash in compensation trade;  
(10) other forms of external debts.  
Foreign exchange funds borrowed by a borrowing entity from a foreign-funded bank registered within the territory of China shall be regarded as external debts.  
Foreign exchange funds borrowed from abroad by a foreign-funded bank registered within the territory of China shall not be regarded as external debts.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　外债登记分为逐笔登记和定期登记。  
国家外汇管理局统一制定和签发《外债登记证》。

**Article 4** The registration of external debts shall be divided into registration item by item and periodic registration.  
The State Administration of Foreign Exchange shall uniformly formulate and issue the Registration Certificate for External Debts.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　外商投资企业的对外借款，借款单位应当在正式签订借款合同后15天内，持借款合同副本向所在地外汇管理局办理登记手续并领取逐笔登记的《外债登记证》。  
国际金融组织贷款、外国政府贷款、中国银行或者经批准的其他银行和金融机构的对外借款，借款单位应当向所在地外汇管理局办理登记手续，领取定期登记的《外债登记证》。上述登记，不包括转贷款。  
除上述两款规定以外的其他借款单位应当在正式签订借款合同后15天内，持对外借款批件和借款合同副本向所在地外汇管理局办理登记手续并领取逐笔登记的《外债登记证》。

**Article 5** For foreign borrowings by foreign-invested enterprises, the borrowing entity shall, within 15 days after formally signing the loan contract, go through the registration formalities with the local administration of foreign exchange by presenting a copy of the loan contract, and obtain the Registration Certificate for External Debts for registration item by item.  
For loans from international financial organizations, loans from foreign governments, and foreign borrowings by Bank of China or other approved banks and financial institutions, the borrowing entity shall go through the registration formalities with the local administration of foreign exchange and obtain the Registration Certificate for External Debts for periodic registration. The above registration shall not include on-lending.  
For borrowing entities other than those specified in the preceding two paragraphs, they shall, within 15 days after formally signing the loan contract, go through the registration formalities with the local administration of foreign exchange by presenting the approval document for foreign borrowing and a copy of the loan contract, and obtain the Registration Certificate for External Debts for registration item by item.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　借款单位调入国外借款时，凭《外债登记证》在中国银行或者经国家外汇管理局批准的其他银行(以下简称银行)开立外债专用现汇账户。经批准将借款存放境外的借款单位以及其他非调入形式的外债的借款单位，凭《外债登记证》在银行开立还本付息外债专用现汇账户。  
对于未按规定领取《外债登记证》的借款单位，银行不得为其开立外债专用现汇账户或者还本付息外债专用现汇账户，其本息不准汇出境外。

**Article 6** When a borrowing entity transfers the borrowed funds into China, it shall, by presenting the Registration Certificate for External Debts, open a special foreign exchange account for external debts with Bank of China or other banks approved by the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (hereinafter collectively referred to as "banks"). A borrowing entity that has been approved to deposit the borrowed funds abroad or a borrowing entity of external debts not in the form of transfer into China shall, by presenting the Registration Certificate for External Debts, open a special foreign exchange account for repayment of principal and interest of external debts with a bank.  
For a borrowing entity that fails to obtain the Registration Certificate for External Debts as required, banks shall not open a special foreign exchange account for external debts or a special foreign exchange account for repayment of principal and interest of external debts for it, and its principal and interest shall not be remitted abroad.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　实行逐笔登记的借款单位还本付息时，开户银行应当凭借款单位提供的外汇管理局的核准证件和《外债登记证》，通过外债专用现汇账户或者还本付息外债专用现汇账户办理收付。借款单位应当按照银行的收付凭证，将收付款项记入《外债变动反馈表》并将该表的副本报送签发《外债登记证》的外汇管理局。  
实行定期登记的借款单位，应当按月向发证的外汇管理局报送其外债的签约、提款、使用和还本付息等情况。  
经批准将借款存放境外的借款单位，应当定期向原批准的外汇管理局报送其存款的变动情况。

**Article 7** When a borrowing entity subject to registration item by item repays principal and interest, the account-opening bank shall, based on the verification documents issued by the administration of foreign exchange and the Registration Certificate for External Debts provided by the borrowing entity, handle receipts and payments through the special foreign exchange account for external debts or the special foreign exchange account for repayment of principal and interest of external debts. The borrowing entity shall, in accordance with the receipt and payment vouchers of the bank, record the receipts and payments into the Feedback Form for Changes in External Debts and submit a copy of such form to the administration of foreign exchange that issued the Registration Certificate for External Debts.  
A borrowing entity subject to periodic registration shall report to the issuing administration of foreign exchange on a monthly basis the signing, drawing, utilization, and repayment of principal and interest of its external debts.  
A borrowing entity that has been approved to deposit the borrowed funds abroad shall periodically report to the administration of foreign exchange that originally approved such deposit on the changes in its deposits.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　借款单位全部偿清《外债登记证》所载明的外债后，银行应即注销其外债专用现汇账户或者还本付息外债专用现汇账户，借款单位应当在15天内向发证的外汇管理局缴销《外债登记证》。

**Article 8** After a borrowing entity has fully repaid the external debts recorded in the Registration Certificate for External Debts, the bank shall immediately cancel its special foreign exchange account for external debts or special foreign exchange account for repayment of principal and interest of external debts, and the borrowing entity shall, within 15 days, surrender the Registration Certificate for External Debts to the issuing administration of foreign exchange for cancellation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　凡违反本规定有下列行为之一的，所在地外汇管理局可根据情节处以最高不超过所涉及外债金额3%的罚款：  
(一)故意不办理或者拖延办理外债登记手续的；  
(二)拒绝向外汇管理局报送或者隐瞒、虚报《外债变动反馈表》，或者并无特殊原因屡次迟报的；  
(三)伪造、涂改《外债登记证》的；  
(四)擅自开立、保留外债专用现汇账户或者还本付息外债专用现汇账户的。  
当事人对外汇管理局的处罚决定不服的，可以向上一级外汇管理局提出申诉。

**Article 9** Whoever violates these Provisions and commits any of the following acts shall, depending on the circumstances, be imposed a fine by the local administration of foreign exchange of not more than 3% of the amount of external debts involved:  
(1) intentionally failing to go through or delaying the registration formalities for external debts;  
(2) refusing to submit, concealing, or making false statements in the Feedback Form for Changes in External Debts to the administration of foreign exchange, or repeatedly delaying submission without special reasons;  
(3) forging or altering the Registration Certificate for External Debts; or  
(4) opening or retaining a special foreign exchange account for external debts or a special foreign exchange account for repayment of principal and interest of external debts without authorization.  
If a party is dissatisfied with the penalty decision of the administration of foreign exchange, it may file a petition with the administration of foreign exchange at the next higher level.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　本规定由国家外汇管理局负责解释。

**Article 10** These Provisions shall be interpreted by the State Administration of Foreign Exchange.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本规定自发布之日起施行。  
本规定发布时，已借外债尚未清偿完毕的借款单位，应当在本规定发布后30天内向所在地外汇管理局办理外债登记手续。

**Article 11** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.  
Borrowing units that have borrowed foreign debts that have not been fully repaid prior to the promulgation of these Provisions shall, within 30 days after the promulgation of these Provisions, go through the foreign debt registration formalities with the local foreign exchange administration.
