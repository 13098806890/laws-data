# 关于统计报表管理的暂行规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1980-11-17

> (1)全国性的社会经济情况基本统计报表(包括基层表和综合表)，由国家统计局制订，并统一下达，或者由国家统计局与有关业务部门联合制订下达。重要的统计报表，应报请国务院批准下达。发往农村人民公社、生产大队和生产队的报表，尤应严格控制。凡国家统计局已经统一下达或与有关业务部门联合下达的报表和指标，各级业务部门都不得重复制发；因特殊需要必须补充某些报表或指标时，须经同级政府统计部门核准。 (2)国务院各业务部门制订的专业统计报表，是对国家统计局制订的社会经济情况基本统计报表的必要补充，必须由各该部门的综合统计机构统一组织、统一审查、统一管理，坚决改变一个部门内各职能机构自行制发统计报表的现象。发到本部门直属和本系统管辖的企业、事业单位的统计报表，由本部门负责人批准下达，并送国家统计局备案；发到非本系统所管辖的企业、事业单位的统计报表，由主办部门负责人签署，送国家统计局核批。 (3)省、市、自治区统计局制订的地区性的统计报表，应报国家统计局备案。重要的统计报表，应报请省、市、自治区人民政府批准下达。 (4)省、市、自治区各业务部门制订的专业统计报表，必须由各该部门的综合统计机构统一组织、统一审查、统一管理。发到本部门直属和本系统管辖的企业、事业单位的统计报表，由本部门负责人批准下达，并送省、市、自治区统计局备案；发到非本系统所管辖的企业、事业单位的统计报表，由主办部门负责人签署，送省、市、自治区统计局核批。 (5)专、市、县统计部门与业务部门制订和审批统计报表的程序，由省、市、自治区统计局根据各该地区的具体情况拟订，报省、市、自治区人民政府批准下达，并送国家统计局备案。 (6)国务院各部门和省、市、自治区领导机关设立的中心工作办公室、各种临时办公室，一般不要直接发统计报表，工作上必需的统计资料，可向有关部门搜集整理。如果确实需要制发少数统计报表，应按上述规定报经同级政府统计部门进行审批。 (7)人民团体、科研机关制发统计报表的审批程序，亦按上述规定办理。 五、各级业务部门制发的专业统计报表，其报表内容、指标解释、计算方法、完成期限等，不得与各级政府统计部门制发的有关统计报表相矛盾，并应避免重复。 国家统计局制订的或者与有关业务部门联合制订的全国性的社会经济情况基本统计报表所规定的统计概念、范围、方法、分类、表式、编号等，各地政府统计部门和各业务部门不得擅自修改变动，以利于全国统一实施。如确需作某些增减变动时，应经国家统计局核准。 六、经批准或备案的统计报表，必须在报表的右上角标明制表机关、批准机关或者备案机关以及批准文号，以便进行管理和监督。 七、各级政府统计部门审批报表，必须认真负责，严格掌握。对送批和报请备案的统计报表，如有不符合本规定第三条至第六条的，各级政府统计部门有权通知制表机关修改或者废止。 八、凡经批准下达的统计报表，有关单位都应认真按照各项规定填报。如有意见，可向制表机关反映，但在未修改变动前，仍应按原规定执行。 凡未按本规定审批和备案，未在报表右上角标明制表机关、批准机关或者备案机关以及批准文号的统计报表，填报单位可拒绝填报，并予揭发检举。 各级领导机关和政府统计部门，对于违反本规定滥发报表的单位，必须认真检查，严肃处理，并制止未经批准的报表的继续执行。 凡滥发统计报表给基层造成损失的，各级政府统计部门应根据情节轻重，给予通报批评，追究责任。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>五、各级业务部门制发的专业统计报表，其报表内容、指标解释、计算方法、完成期限等，不得与各级政府统计部门制发的有关统计报表相矛盾，并应避免重复。  
国家统计局制订的或者与有关业务部门联合制订的全国性的社会经济情况基本统计报表所规定的统计概念、范围、方法、分类、表式、编号等，各地政府统计部门和各业务部门不得擅自修改变动，以利于全国统一实施。如确需作某些增减变动时，应经国家统计局核准。

**Article 1** V. Professional statistical forms issued by various business departments shall not contradict the relevant statistical forms issued by government statistical departments at all levels in terms of content, interpretation of indicators, calculation methods, deadlines, etc., and duplication shall be avoided.  
The statistical concepts, scope, methods, classifications, forms, numbering, etc., stipulated in the national basic statistical forms on social and economic conditions formulated by the National Bureau of Statistics or jointly formulated with relevant business departments, shall not be modified or altered without authorization by local government statistical departments and business departments, in order to facilitate unified implementation nationwide. If certain additions or deletions are indeed necessary, approval from the National Bureau of Statistics shall be obtained.

<a id="art-2"></a>六、经批准或备案的统计报表，必须在报表的右上角标明制表机关、批准机关或者备案机关以及批准文号，以便进行管理和监督。

**Article 2** VI. For statistical forms that have been approved or filed for record, the form-making organ, the approving organ or the filing organ, and the approval document number must be indicated in the upper right corner of the form to facilitate management and supervision.

<a id="art-3"></a>七、各级政府统计部门审批报表，必须认真负责，严格掌握。对送批和报请备案的统计报表，如有不符合本规定第三条至第六条的，各级政府统计部门有权通知制表机关修改或者废止。

**Article 3** VII. Government statistical departments at all levels, when examining and approving forms, must be conscientious and responsible, and strictly control. For statistical forms submitted for approval or for record, if they do not comply with Articles III to VI of these Provisions, the government statistical departments at all levels shall have the right to notify the form-making organ to revise or abolish them.

<a id="art-4"></a>八、凡经批准下达的统计报表，有关单位都应认真按照各项规定填报。如有意见，可向制表机关反映，但在未修改变动前，仍应按原规定执行。  
凡未按本规定审批和备案，未在报表右上角标明制表机关、批准机关或者备案机关以及批准文号的统计报表，填报单位可拒绝填报，并予揭发检举。  
各级领导机关和政府统计部门，对于违反本规定滥发报表的单位，必须认真检查，严肃处理，并制止未经批准的报表的继续执行。  
凡滥发统计报表给基层造成损失的，各级政府统计部门应根据情节轻重，给予通报批评，追究责任。

**Article 4** VIII. For all approved and issued statistical forms, the relevant units shall fill them out conscientiously in accordance with the provisions. If there are any opinions, they may reflect them to the form-making organ, but before the revision or change, the original provisions shall still be implemented.  
For statistical forms that have not been examined, approved, or filed for record in accordance with these Provisions, and that do not indicate the form-making organ, the approving organ or the filing organ, and the approval document number in the upper right corner, the reporting units may refuse to fill them out and shall expose and report them.  
Leading organs and government statistical departments at all levels must seriously inspect and strictly handle units that violate these Provisions by indiscriminately issuing forms, and shall stop the continued implementation of unauthorized forms.  
Where indiscriminate issuance of statistical forms causes losses to the grassroots, government statistical departments at all levels shall, depending on the severity of the circumstances, give a notice of criticism and investigate the responsibility.

<a id="art-5"></a>九、各级政府统计部门和业务部门应定期检查和清理统计报表，凡是已经过时的和不适用的统计报表、指标等，都应该及时废止或者修订。每年检查和清理完毕，应将清理结果报告上一级政府统计部门和同级人民政府。

**Article 5** IX. Government statistical departments and business departments at all levels shall regularly inspect and clean up statistical forms. Any statistical forms, indicators, etc., that are outdated or inapplicable shall be promptly abolished or revised. After the annual inspection and cleanup, the cleanup results shall be reported to the government statistical department at the next higher level and the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-6"></a>十、国务院各业务部门和各省、市、自治区统计局应根据本规定的原则，拟订实施细则，报国家统计局备案。  
附：  
国务院批转国家统计局《关于加强统计报表管理的  
报告》和《关于统计报表管理的暂行规定》  
(一九八○年十一月十七日)  
国务院同意国家统计局《关于加强统计报表管理的报告》和《关于统计报表管理的暂行规定》，现发给你们，望认真贯彻执行。  
统计报表是了解国民经济情况的一个重要手段。但是，现在统计报表多、乱的情况是比较严重的，如不及时加以制止，势必泛滥成灾，这一点必须引起各级领导机关的密切注意。报表繁多，不仅使基层单位负担过重，造成人力、物力、财力的浪费，而且容易助长官僚主义作风。  
为了克服报表多、乱，减轻基层负担，保证党和国家必需的统计资料准确及时地上报，对于那些重复、烦琐、互相矛盾，或者不符合新的情况和新的经济管理体制需要的报表和指标，必须认真加以精简。任何统计调查都要注意用尽可能少的人力、物力、财力，取得尽可能好的调查效果。为此，国务院重申：统计报表要由各级统计部门统一管理，严禁滥发报表，并责成国家统计局负责全国统计报表的管理工作，各地统计部门和各级业务主管部门的统计机构也要把本地区、本部门的统计报表切实管起来。  
现在，统计部门力量薄弱，同他们担负的任务很不适应。各地区、各部门要抓紧落实国务院一九七九年十月二十日《关于加强统计工作充实统计机构的决定》加强对统计工作的领导，健全统计机构，充实统计人员，包括管理报表的人员，以利工作的开展。  
国家统计局关于加强统计报表管理的报告  
(一九八○年十一月三日)  
多年来，由于林彪、“四人帮”的干扰，统计工作受到严重削弱，一些合理的规章制度遭到破坏，因而统计报表多、乱的情况，至今在不少地区和部门仍然存在，最近又有发展之势，主要表现在：  
（一）发往基层的统计表格越来越多，指标越来越细，加重了基层负担。一九七九年统计报表，除了国家统计局制发的一百六十七种以外，国务院六十四个部门下达的统计报表达二千六百三十三种。这些表格发到各地后，层层加码，因此发到基层特别是农村人民公社的报表更多更繁。据河北省调查，有的县向农村发的报表多达一百三十余种，五千多个项目。辽宁省有的地方发往人民公社的各种报表二百零四种，四千九百多个项目，其中经国家统计局和各级政府统计部门批准的只占百分之十五，其余百分之八十五都是各部门自行制发的。  
（二）各部门制发报表，自成体系，各搞一套，致使统计报表指标相互重复矛盾。例如许多工业企业要同时填报两、三套大同小异的经济技术指标月报，一套是政府统计部门发的，另一套是国务院有关工业部门发的，还有一套是根据本地要求另搞的。同样，农村人民公社也要填两套大同小异的农业统计年报，一套是政府统计部门发的，另一套是农、林、水有关业务主管部门发的。这些大同小异的报表，指标重复，计算方法和口径不尽统一，报送期限前后不一，严重增加了基层工作量。  
（三）有些单位用电话向下边要数字很多，不仅次数频繁，而且急如星火。如有的县用电话向社、队要早稻的生产进度数字，从备耕、栽插到收获，就达九十多次，平均一、两天就要报一次。有的县在大部分农作物尚未抽穗的情况下，就要求下边预计产量。群众很有意见，批评这种做法是：“禾未插到田，进度报在前，禾穗未长成，估产就先行！”  
（四）有些报表的指标十分烦琐，脱离实际。有的公社向生产大队布置了“灭鼠情况调查表”，要求上报：发给耗子药多少，有耗子洞多少，药死耗子多少等等，根本无法填报。  
统计报表多、乱，造成了大量人力、物力、财力的浪费，妨碍正常的统计工作开展，影响党和国家必需的统计资料的准确和及时提供，并带来数出多门的恶果。  
造成统计报表多、乱的原因，主要是林彪、“四人帮”的严重干扰，统一管理和统计报表的规章制度受到破坏，统计机构大大削弱，管理报表人员被撤减。同时，有些机关习惯于靠发报表来了解情况，不认真研究所发的报表是否切合实际，也是造成报表多、乱的一个重要原因。  
为了加强统计报表的管理，有效地克服报表多、乱，减轻基层负担，经我局与各省、市、自治区统计局和国务院各部门协商，拟订了《关于统计报表管理的暂行规定》，拟请各省、市、自治区和国务院各部门，在今冬和明年内，根据“暂行规定”的要求，对现行的统计报表制度进行清理整顿，坚决废除一切不必要的重复、烦琐的，或者与新的情况和新的经济管理体制不适应的统计报表和指标。国家统计局对下达的报表，也要听取各方意见，总结实践的经验，进行清理和整顿。各地区、各部门清理的结果，请函告国家统计局，以便汇总上报国务院。今后每年都要对统计报表进行一次检查和清理。各地区、各部门要继续贯彻执行国务院《关于加强统计工作充实统计机构的决定》，充实统计人员，包括配备统计报表管理人员，改变目前许多地区统计部门力量薄弱、无人管理报表的不正常状态。  
以上报告，如可行，请连同《国家统计局关于统计报表管理的暂行规定》，批转各省、市、自治区和国务院各部门贯彻执行。

**Article 6** X. The business departments of the State Council and the statistical bureaus of all provinces, municipalities, and autonomous regions shall, in accordance with the principles of these Provisions, formulate implementing rules and report them to the National Bureau of Statistics for record.  
Appendix:  
State Council Approving and Forwarding the "Report on Strengthening the Management of Statistical Forms" and the "Interim Provisions on the Management of Statistical Forms" of the National Bureau of Statistics  
(November 17, 1980)  
The State Council agrees to the "Report on Strengthening the Management of Statistical Forms" and the "Interim Provisions on the Management of Statistical Forms" of the National Bureau of Statistics, and hereby issues them to you, hoping that you will earnestly implement them.  
Statistical forms are an important means of understanding the national economic situation. However, the current situation where there are too many and disorderly statistical forms is relatively serious. If not promptly stopped, it will inevitably become a disaster. This point must attract the close attention of leading organs at all levels. An excessive number of forms not only overburdens grassroots units, causing waste of manpower, material resources, and financial resources, but also easily fosters bureaucratic tendencies.  
In order to overcome the problem of too many and disorderly forms, reduce the burden on the grassroots, and ensure that the statistical data necessary for the Party and the state are reported accurately and timely, it is necessary to earnestly streamline those forms and indicators that are repetitive, cumbersome, contradictory, or not suited to the new situation and the new economic management system. Any statistical survey shall pay attention to achieving the best possible survey results with as little manpower, material resources, and financial resources as possible. To this end, the State Council reiterates: Statistical forms shall be uniformly managed by statistical departments at all levels; indiscriminate issuance of forms is strictly prohibited; and the National Bureau of Statistics is charged with the management of national statistical forms. The statistical departments of all localities and the statistical organs of business departments at all levels shall also effectively manage the statistical forms of their own regions and departments.  
Currently, the statistical departments are weak in strength, which is not well suited to the tasks they undertake. All localities and departments shall promptly implement the "Decision on Strengthening Statistical Work and Enriching Statistical Organs" of the State Council dated October 20, 1979, strengthen leadership over statistical work, improve statistical organs, and enrich statistical personnel, including those managing forms, to facilitate the development of work.  
Report of the National Bureau of Statistics on Strengthening the Management of Statistical Forms  
(November 3, 1980)  
Over the years, due to the interference of Lin Biao and the "Gang of Four," statistical work has been seriously weakened, and some reasonable rules and regulations have been destroyed. As a result, the problem of too many and disorderly statistical forms still exists in many regions and departments, and has recently shown a trend of development, mainly manifested in:  
(1) The statistical forms sent to the grassroots are increasingly numerous, and the indicators are increasingly detailed, aggravating the burden on the grassroots. In 1979, in addition to 167 types of statistical forms formulated by the National Bureau of Statistics, the 64 departments of the State Council issued as many as 2,633 types of statistical forms. After these forms were sent to various localities, layers of additional requirements were added, making the forms sent to the grassroots, especially rural people's communes, even more numerous and complicated. According to a survey in Hebei Province, some counties sent as many as 130 types of forms to rural areas, with over 5,000 items. In some places in Liaoning Province, 204 types of forms with 4,900 items were sent to people's communes, of which only 15% were approved by the National Bureau of Statistics and government statistical departments at all levels, and the remaining 85% were formulated by various departments themselves.  
(2) Departments formulate and issue forms on their own systems, each doing its own thing, resulting in overlapping and contradictory indicators among statistical forms. For example, many industrial enterprises have to fill out two or three sets of similar monthly reports on economic and technical indicators: one set issued by the government statistical department, another set issued by the relevant industrial department of the State Council, and a third set based on local requirements. Similarly, rural people's communes have to fill out two sets of similar annual agricultural statistical reports: one set issued by the government statistical department and another set issued by the relevant business departments of agriculture, forestry, and water conservancy. These similar forms have overlapping indicators, inconsistent calculation methods and caliber, and varying submission deadlines, seriously increasing the workload of the grassroots.  
(3) Some units make many requests for figures by telephone, not only frequently but also urgently. For example, some counties used the telephone to request production progress figures for early rice from communes and production brigades, which reached over 90 times from preparation for cultivation, transplanting to harvesting, averaging one or two reports per day. Some counties, when most crops had not yet headed, demanded yield estimates from below. The masses were very dissatisfied and criticized this practice as: "Before the seedlings are planted, the progress report comes first; before the ears of grain have grown, the yield estimate is made first!"  
(4) Some forms have extremely cumbersome indicators that are divorced from reality. Some communes assigned a "Mouse Eradication Situation Survey Form" to production brigades, requiring reports on: how much rat poison was distributed, how many rat holes there were, how many rats were poisoned to death, etc., which were completely impossible to fill out.  
The excessive number and disorderly state of statistical forms have caused a great waste of manpower, material resources, and financial resources, hampered the normal conduct of statistical work, affected the accurate and timely provision of statistical data necessary for the Party and the state, and led to the evil consequence of multiple sources of data.  
The main reasons for the excessive number and disorderly state of statistical forms are the serious interference of Lin Biao and the "Gang of Four," the destruction of uniform management and rules and regulations for statistical forms, the significant weakening of statistical organs, and the removal or reduction of personnel managing forms. At the same time, the habit of some organs relying on issuing forms to understand the situation and failing to seriously study whether the forms issued are practical is also an important reason for the excessive number and disorderly state of forms.  
In order to strengthen the management of statistical forms, effectively overcome the problem of too many and disorderly forms, and reduce the burden on the grassroots, our Bureau, in consultation with the statistical bureaus of all provinces, municipalities, and autonomous regions and the departments of the State Council, has formulated the "Interim Provisions on the Management of Statistical Forms." It is proposed that all provinces, municipalities, and autonomous regions and the departments of the State Council, within this winter and the next year, in accordance with the requirements of the "Interim Provisions," carry out a cleanup and rectification of the current statistical form system, and resolutely abolish all unnecessary, repetitive, cumbersome, or forms and indicators that are not suitable for the new situation and the new economic management system. The National Bureau of Statistics shall also listen to opinions from all sides, summarize practical experience, and carry out a cleanup and rectification of the forms it issues. The results of the cleanup by various regions and departments shall be reported in writing to the National Bureau of Statistics for compilation and submission to the State Council. In the future, statistical forms shall be inspected and cleaned up once a year. All regions and departments shall continue to implement the State Council's "Decision on Strengthening Statistical Work and Enriching Statistical Organs," enrich statistical personnel, including assigning personnel to manage statistical forms, and change the current abnormal situation in many regions where statistical departments are weak and no one manages forms.  
The above report, if feasible, is requested to be approved and forwarded together with the "Interim Provisions on the Management of Statistical Forms of the National Bureau of Statistics" to all provinces, municipalities, and autonomous regions and the departments of the State Council for implementation.
