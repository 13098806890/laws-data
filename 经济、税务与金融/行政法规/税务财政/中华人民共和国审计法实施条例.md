# 中华人民共和国审计法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2010-02-11  
**生效日期**：2010-05-01

> (1997年10月21日中华人民共和国国务院令第231号公布　2010年2月2日国务院第100次常务会议修订通过　2010年2月11日中华人民共和国国务院令第571号公布　自2010年5月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国审计法》(以下简称审计法)的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　审计法所称审计，是指审计机关依法独立检查被审计单位的会计凭证、会计账簿、财务会计报告以及其他与财政收支、财务收支有关的资料和资产，监督财政收支、财务收支真实、合法和效益的行为。 第三条　审计法所称财政收支，是指依照《中华人民共和国预算法》和国家其他有关规定，纳入预算管理的收入和支出，以及下列财政资金中未纳入预算管理的收入和支出： (一)行政事业性收费； (二)国有资源、国有资产收入； (三)应当上缴的国有资本经营收益； (四)政府举借债务筹措的资金； (五)其他未纳入预算管理的财政资金。 第四条　审计法所称财务收支，是指国有的金融机构、企业事业组织以及依法应当接受审计机关审计监督的其他单位，按照国家财务会计制度的规定，实行会计核算的各项收入和支出。 第五条　审计机关依照审计法和本条例以及其他有关法律、法规规定的职责、权限和程序进行审计监督。 审计机关依照有关财政收支、财务收支的法律、法规，以及国家有关政策、标准、项目目标等方面的规定进行审计评价，对被审计单位违反国家规定的财政收支、财务收支行为，在法定职权范围内作出处理、处罚的决定。 第六条　任何单位和个人对依法应当接受审计机关审计监督的单位违反国家规定的财政收支、财务收支行为，有权向审计机关举报。审计机关接到举报，应当依法及时处理。 第二章　审计机关和审计人员

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国审计法》(以下简称审计法)的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Audit Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Audit Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　审计法所称审计，是指审计机关依法独立检查被审计单位的会计凭证、会计账簿、财务会计报告以及其他与财政收支、财务收支有关的资料和资产，监督财政收支、财务收支真实、合法和效益的行为。

**Article 2** For the purposes of the Audit Law, "audit" means the acts of audit institutions independently inspecting, according to law, the accounting vouchers, accounting books, financial accounting reports, and other information and assets related to fiscal revenues and expenditures and financial revenues and expenditures of the auditees, and supervising the truthfulness, lawfulness, and efficiency of fiscal revenues and expenditures and financial revenues and expenditures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　审计法所称财政收支，是指依照《中华人民共和国预算法》和国家其他有关规定，纳入预算管理的收入和支出，以及下列财政资金中未纳入预算管理的收入和支出：  
(一)行政事业性收费；  
(二)国有资源、国有资产收入；  
(三)应当上缴的国有资本经营收益；  
(四)政府举借债务筹措的资金；  
(五)其他未纳入预算管理的财政资金。

**Article 3** For the purposes of the Audit Law, "fiscal revenues and expenditures" means the revenues and expenditures included in budgetary management in accordance with the Budget Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant provisions of the State, as well as the revenues and expenditures of the following fiscal funds not included in budgetary management:  
(1) administrative fees;  
(2) revenues from state-owned resources and state-owned assets;  
(3) earnings from state-owned capital operations that shall be turned over;  
(4) funds raised by the government through borrowing;  
(5) other fiscal funds not included in budgetary management.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　审计法所称财务收支，是指国有的金融机构、企业事业组织以及依法应当接受审计机关审计监督的其他单位，按照国家财务会计制度的规定，实行会计核算的各项收入和支出。

**Article 4** For the purposes of the Audit Law, "financial revenues and expenditures" means the various revenues and expenditures subject to accounting treatment by state-owned financial institutions, enterprises, public institutions, and other entities that shall accept audit supervision by audit institutions according to law, in accordance with the provisions of the state financial accounting system.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　审计机关依照审计法和本条例以及其他有关法律、法规规定的职责、权限和程序进行审计监督。  
审计机关依照有关财政收支、财务收支的法律、法规，以及国家有关政策、标准、项目目标等方面的规定进行审计评价，对被审计单位违反国家规定的财政收支、财务收支行为，在法定职权范围内作出处理、处罚的决定。

**Article 5** Audit institutions shall conduct audit supervision in accordance with the duties, powers, and procedures prescribed by the Audit Law, these Regulations, and other relevant laws and regulations.  
Audit institutions shall conduct audit evaluation in accordance with the laws and regulations on fiscal revenues and expenditures and financial revenues and expenditures, as well as the provisions of the state on policies, standards, project objectives, etc., and shall make decisions on handling and punishment within the scope of their statutory functions and powers for acts of the auditees that violate the provisions of the state on fiscal revenues and expenditures and financial revenues and expenditures.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何单位和个人对依法应当接受审计机关审计监督的单位违反国家规定的财政收支、财务收支行为，有权向审计机关举报。审计机关接到举报，应当依法及时处理。

**Article 6** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report to audit institutions any acts of entities that shall accept audit supervision by audit institutions according to law that violate the provisions of the state on fiscal revenues and expenditures and financial revenues and expenditures. Upon receipt of such a report, the audit institution shall handle it promptly according to law.

### 第二章　审计机关和审计人员

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　审计署在国务院总理领导下，主管全国的审计工作，履行审计法和国务院规定的职责。  
地方各级审计机关在本级人民政府行政首长和上一级审计机关的领导下，负责本行政区域的审计工作，履行法律、法规和本级人民政府规定的职责。

**Article 7** The National Audit Office shall, under the leadership of the Premier of the State Council, be in charge of the audit work nationwide and perform the duties prescribed by the Audit Law and the State Council.  
Local audit institutions at all levels shall, under the leadership of the chief executive of the people's government at the corresponding level and the audit institution at the next higher level, be responsible for the audit work within their respective administrative regions and perform the duties prescribed by laws, regulations, and the people's government at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　省、自治区人民政府设有派出机关的，派出机关的审计机关对派出机关和省、自治区人民政府审计机关负责并报告工作，审计业务以省、自治区人民政府审计机关领导为主。

**Article 8** Where a provincial or autonomous regional people's government has established a agency, the audit institution of such agency shall be responsible to and report work to the agency and the audit institution of the provincial or autonomous regional people's government, and the audit business shall be mainly under the leadership of the audit institution of the provincial or autonomous regional people's government.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　审计机关派出机构依照法律、法规和审计机关的规定，在审计机关的授权范围内开展审计工作，不受其他行政机关、社会团体和个人的干涉。

**Article 9** The dispatched offices of audit institutions shall carry out audit work within the scope authorized by the audit institutions in accordance with laws, regulations, and the provisions of the audit institutions, and shall be free from interference by other administrative organs, social organizations, or individuals.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　审计机关编制年度经费预算草案的依据主要包括：  
(一)法律、法规；  
(二)本级人民政府的决定和要求；  
(三)审计机关的年度审计工作计划；  
(四)定员定额标准；  
(五)上一年度经费预算执行情况和本年度的变化因素。

**Article 10** The basis for audit institutions to prepare draft annual budgets for funds mainly includes:  
(1) laws and regulations;  
(2) the decisions and requirements of the people's government at the corresponding level;  
(3) the annual audit work plan of the audit institution;  
(4) standards for staffing and quotas;  
(5) the implementation of the fund budget for the previous year and the factors of change in the current year.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　审计人员实行审计专业技术资格制度，具体按照国家有关规定执行。  
审计机关根据工作需要，可以聘请具有与审计事项相关专业知识的人员参加审计工作。

**Article 11** A system of professional technical qualifications for auditors shall be implemented, and the specifics shall be executed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Audit institutions may, based on work needs, engage persons with professional knowledge related to audit matters to participate in audit work.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　审计人员办理审计事项，有下列情形之一的，应当申请回避，被审计单位也有权申请审计人员回避：  
(一)与被审计单位负责人或者有关主管人员有夫妻关系、直系血亲关系、三代以内旁系血亲或者近姻亲关系的；  
(二)与被审计单位或者审计事项有经济利益关系的；  
(三)与被审计单位、审计事项、被审计单位负责人或者有关主管人员有其他利害关系，可能影响公正执行公务的。  
审计人员的回避，由审计机关负责人决定；审计机关负责人办理审计事项时的回避，由本级人民政府或者上一级审计机关负责人决定。

**Article 12** Where an auditor, in handling an audit matter, falls under any of the following circumstances, the auditor shall apply for withdrawal, and the auditee shall also have the right to apply for the auditor's withdrawal:  
(1) being a spouse, a lineal relative by blood, a collateral relative by blood within three generations, or a close relative by marriage of the person in charge of the auditee or the relevant responsible person;  
(2) having an economic interest relationship with the auditee or the audit matter;  
(3) having any other interest relationship with the auditee, the audit matter, the person in charge of the auditee, or the relevant responsible person that may affect the impartial performance of official duties.  
A withdrawal of an auditor shall be decided by the person in charge of the audit institution; a withdrawal of the person in charge of an audit institution when handling an audit matter shall be decided by the people's government at the corresponding level or by the person in charge of the audit institution at the next higher level.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　地方各级审计机关正职和副职负责人的任免，应当事先征求上一级审计机关的意见。

**Article 13** The appointment and removal of the heads and deputy heads of local audit institutions at all levels shall be subject to prior consultation with the audit institution at the next higher level.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　审计机关负责人在任职期间没有下列情形之一的，不得随意撤换：  
(一)因犯罪被追究刑事责任的；  
(二)因严重违法、失职受到处分，不适宜继续担任审计机关负责人的；  
(三)因健康原因不能履行职责1年以上的；  
(四)不符合国家规定的其他任职条件的。

**Article 14** The person in charge of an audit institution shall not be removed at will during the term of office unless any of the following circumstances exists:  
(1) being subject to criminal liability due to a crime;  
(2) being subject to disciplinary sanction due to serious violation of law or dereliction of duty, and being unsuitable to continue to serve as the person in charge of an audit institution;  
(3) being unable to perform duties for more than one year due to health reasons;  
(4) failing to meet other conditions for holding the office as prescribed by the State.

### 第三章　审计机关职责

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　审计机关对本级人民政府财政部门具体组织本级预算执行的情况，本级预算收入征收部门征收预算收入的情况，与本级人民政府财政部门直接发生预算缴款、拨款关系的部门、单位的预算执行情况和决算，下级人民政府的预算执行情况和决算，以及其他财政收支情况，依法进行审计监督。经本级人民政府批准，审计机关对其他取得财政资金的单位和项目接受、运用财政资金的真实、合法和效益情况，依法进行审计监督。

**Article 15** Audit institutions shall, according to law, conduct audit supervision over: the specific organization of the implementation of the budget at the corresponding level by the finance department of the people's government at the corresponding level; the collection of budget revenues by the departments responsible for collecting budget revenues at the corresponding level; the budget implementation and final accounts of departments and entities that directly have budget payment and appropriation relationships with the finance department of the people's government at the corresponding level; the budget implementation and final accounts of people's governments at lower levels; and other fiscal revenues and expenditures. With the approval of the people's government at the corresponding level, audit institutions shall, according to law, conduct audit supervision over the truthfulness, lawfulness, and efficiency of the receipt and use of fiscal funds by other entities and projects that obtain fiscal funds.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　审计机关对本级预算收入和支出的执行情况进行审计监督的内容包括：  
(一)财政部门按照本级人民代表大会批准的本级预算向本级各部门(含直属单位)批复预算的情况、本级预算执行中调整情况和预算收支变化情况；  
(二)预算收入征收部门依照法律、行政法规的规定和国家其他有关规定征收预算收入情况；  
(三)财政部门按照批准的年度预算、用款计划，以及规定的预算级次和程序，拨付本级预算支出资金情况；  
(四)财政部门依照法律、行政法规的规定和财政管理体制，拨付和管理政府间财政转移支付资金情况以及办理结算、结转情况；  
(五)国库按照国家有关规定办理预算收入的收纳、划分、留解情况和预算支出资金的拨付情况；  
(六)本级各部门(含直属单位)执行年度预算情况；  
(七)依照国家有关规定实行专项管理的预算资金收支情况；  
(八)法律、法规规定的其他预算执行情况。

**Article 16** The contents of audit supervision conducted by audit institutions over the implementation of revenues and expenditures of the budget at the corresponding level include:  
(1) the situation where the finance department approves budgets to various departments (including directly subordinate entities) at the corresponding level in accordance with the budget approved by the people's congress at the corresponding level, the adjustments made during the implementation of the budget, and the changes in budget revenues and expenditures;  
(2) the situation where the departments responsible for collecting budget revenues collect budget revenues in accordance with the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, and other relevant provisions of the State;  
(3) the situation where the finance department allocates funds for budget expenditures at the corresponding level in accordance with the approved annual budget, expenditure plan, and the prescribed budget levels and procedures;  
(4) the situation where the finance department allocates and manages intergovernmental fiscal transfer payment funds and handles settlements and carry-overs in accordance with the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, and the fiscal management system;  
(5) the situation where the state treasury handles the receipt, division, retention, and remittance of budget revenues and the allocation of budget expenditure funds in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(6) the situation where various departments (including directly subordinate entities) at the corresponding level implement the annual budget;  
(7) the revenues and expenditures of budget funds subject to special management in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(8) other situations of budget implementation as prescribed by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　[审计法第十七条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国审计法.md#art-17)所称审计结果报告，应当包括下列内容：  
(一)本级预算执行和其他财政收支的基本情况；  
(二)审计机关对本级预算执行和其他财政收支情况作出的审计评价；  
(三)本级预算执行和其他财政收支中存在的问题以及审计机关依法采取的措施；  
(四)审计机关提出的改进本级预算执行和其他财政收支管理工作的建议；  
(五)本级人民政府要求报告的其他情况。

**Article 17** The audit result report referred to in Article 17 of the Audit Law shall include the following:  
(1) the basic situation of the implementation of the budget at the corresponding level and other fiscal revenues and expenditures;  
(2) the audit evaluation made by the audit institution on the implementation of the budget at the corresponding level and other fiscal revenues and expenditures;  
(3) the problems existing in the implementation of the budget at the corresponding level and other fiscal revenues and expenditures and the measures taken by the audit institution according to law;  
(4) the suggestions put forward by the audit institution for improving the management of the implementation of the budget at the corresponding level and other fiscal revenues and expenditures;  
(5) other situations required to be reported by the people's government at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　审计署对中央银行及其分支机构履行职责所发生的各项财务收支，依法进行审计监督。  
审计署向国务院总理提出的中央预算执行和其他财政收支情况审计结果报告，应当包括对中央银行的财务收支的审计情况。

**Article 18** The National Audit Office shall, according to law, conduct audit supervision over the various financial revenues and expenditures incurred by the central bank and its branch offices in performing their duties.  
The audit result report on the implementation of the central budget and other fiscal revenues and expenditures submitted by the National Audit Office to the Premier of the State Council shall include the audit situation of the financial revenues and expenditures of the central bank.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　[审计法第二十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国审计法.md#art-21)所称国有资本占控股地位或者主导地位的企业、金融机构，包括：  
(一)国有资本占企业、金融机构资本(股本)总额的比例超过50%的；  
(二)国有资本占企业、金融机构资本(股本)总额的比例在50%以下，但国有资本投资主体拥有实际控制权的。  
审计机关对前款规定的企业、金融机构，除国务院另有规定外，比照[审计法第十八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国审计法.md#art-18)第二款、第二十条规定进行审计监督。

**Article 19** The enterprises and financial institutions in which state-owned capital holds a controlling or dominant position referred to in Article 21 of the Audit Law include:  
(1) those where the proportion of state-owned capital in the total capital (equity) of the enterprise or financial institution exceeds 50%;  
(2) those where the proportion of state-owned capital in the total capital (equity) of the enterprise or financial institution is below 50%, but the state-owned capital investor has de facto control.  
Audit institutions shall, unless otherwise provided by the State Council, conduct audit supervision over the enterprises and financial institutions specified in the preceding paragraph by analogy with the provisions of paragraphs 2 of Article 18 and Article 20 of the Audit Law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　[审计法第二十二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国审计法.md#art-22)所称政府投资和以政府投资为主的建设项目，包括：  
(一)全部使用预算内投资资金、专项建设基金、政府举借债务筹措的资金等财政资金的；  
(二)未全部使用财政资金，财政资金占项目总投资的比例超过50%，或者占项目总投资的比例在50%以下，但政府拥有项目建设、运营实际控制权的。  
审计机关对前款规定的建设项目的总预算或者概算的执行情况、年度预算的执行情况和年度决算、单项工程结算、项目竣工决算，依法进行审计监督；对前款规定的建设项目进行审计时，可以对直接有关的设计、施工、供货等单位取得建设项目资金的真实性、合法性进行调查。

**Article 20** The construction projects invested by the government or mainly invested by the government referred to in Article 22 of the Audit Law include:  
(1) those entirely using fiscal funds such as budgetary investment funds, special construction funds, and funds raised by the government through borrowing;  
(2) those not entirely using fiscal funds, but where the proportion of fiscal funds in the total investment of the project exceeds 50%, or where the proportion is below 50% but the government has de facto control over the construction and operation of the project.  
Audit institutions shall, according to law, conduct audit supervision over the implementation of the total budget or budgetary estimate, the implementation of the annual budget and annual final accounts, the settlement of individual projects, and the final accounts of completed projects for the construction projects specified in the preceding paragraph; when auditing the construction projects specified in the preceding paragraph, they may investigate the truthfulness and lawfulness of the acquisition of construction project funds by directly related design, construction, and supply entities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　[审计法第二十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国审计法.md#art-23)所称社会保障基金，包括社会保险、社会救助、社会福利基金以及发展社会保障事业的其他专项基金；所称社会捐赠资金，包括来源于境内外的货币、有价证券和实物等各种形式的捐赠。

**Article 21** The social security funds referred to in Article 23 of the Audit Law include social insurance, social assistance, social welfare funds, and other special funds for the development of social security undertakings; the social donation funds referred to therein include donations in various forms such as currency, negotiable securities, and in-kind from domestic and foreign sources.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　[审计法第二十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国审计法.md#art-24)所称国际组织和外国政府援助、贷款项目，包括：  
(一)国际组织、外国政府及其机构向中国政府及其机构提供的贷款项目；  
(二)国际组织、外国政府及其机构向中国企业事业组织以及其他组织提供的由中国政府及其机构担保的贷款项目；  
(三)国际组织、外国政府及其机构向中国政府及其机构提供的援助和赠款项目；  
(四)国际组织、外国政府及其机构向受中国政府委托管理有关基金、资金的单位提供的援助和赠款项目；  
(五)国际组织、外国政府及其机构提供援助、贷款的其他项目。

**Article 22** The projects of assistance and loans from international organizations and foreign governments referred to in Article 24 of the Audit Law include:  
(1) loan projects provided by international organizations, foreign governments, and their agencies to the Chinese government and its agencies;  
(2) loan projects provided by international organizations, foreign governments, and their agencies to Chinese enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations, guaranteed by the Chinese government and its agencies;  
(3) assistance and grant projects provided by international organizations, foreign governments, and their agencies to the Chinese government and its agencies;  
(4) assistance and grant projects provided by international organizations, foreign governments, and their agencies to entities entrusted by the Chinese government to manage relevant funds;  
(5) other projects of assistance and loans provided by international organizations, foreign governments, and their agencies.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　审计机关可以依照审计法和本条例规定的审计程序、方法以及国家其他有关规定，对预算管理或者国有资产管理使用等与国家财政收支有关的特定事项，向有关地方、部门、单位进行专项审计调查。

**Article 23** Audit institutions may, in accordance with the audit procedures and methods prescribed by the Audit Law and these Regulations, as well as other relevant provisions of the State, conduct special audit investigations on specific matters related to the state's fiscal revenues and expenditures, such as budget management or the management and use of state-owned assets, with respect to relevant localities, departments, and entities.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　审计机关根据被审计单位的财政、财务隶属关系，确定审计管辖范围；不能根据财政、财务隶属关系确定审计管辖范围的，根据国有资产监督管理关系，确定审计管辖范围。  
两个以上国有资本投资主体投资的金融机构、企业事业组织和建设项目，由对主要投资主体有审计管辖权的审计机关进行审计监督。

**Article 24** Audit institutions shall determine the audit jurisdiction based on the fiscal and financial subordination relationship of the auditee; if the audit jurisdiction cannot be determined based on the fiscal and financial subordination relationship, it shall be determined based on the relationship of supervision and administration of state-owned assets.  
For financial institutions, enterprises, public institutions, and construction projects invested by two or more state-owned capital investors, the audit institution with audit jurisdiction over the main investor shall conduct audit supervision.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　各级审计机关应当按照确定的审计管辖范围进行审计监督。

**Article 25** Audit institutions at all levels shall conduct audit supervision within the determined audit jurisdiction.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　依法属于审计机关审计监督对象的单位的内部审计工作，应当接受审计机关的业务指导和监督。  
依法属于审计机关审计监督对象的单位，可以根据内部审计工作的需要，参加依法成立的内部审计自律组织。审计机关可以通过内部审计自律组织，加强对内部审计工作的业务指导和监督。

**Article 26** The internal audit work of entities that are subject to audit supervision by audit institutions according to law shall accept the professional guidance and supervision of audit institutions.  
Entities that are subject to audit supervision by audit institutions according to law may, based on the needs of internal audit work, participate in internal audit self-regulatory organizations established according to law. Audit institutions may, through internal audit self-regulatory organizations, strengthen professional guidance and supervision over internal audit work.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　审计机关进行审计或者专项审计调查时，有权对社会审计机构出具的相关审计报告进行核查。  
审计机关核查社会审计机构出具的相关审计报告时，发现社会审计机构存在违反法律、法规或者执业准则等情况的，应当移送有关主管机关依法追究责任。

**Article 27** When conducting an audit or special audit investigation, audit institutions shall have the power to verify the relevant audit reports issued by social audit institutions.  
When verifying the relevant audit reports issued by social audit institutions, if audit institutions discover that the social audit institutions have violated laws, regulations, or professional standards, they shall transfer the matter to the competent authority to investigate liability according to law.

### 第四章　审计机关权限

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　审计机关依法进行审计监督时，被审计单位应当依照[审计法第三十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国审计法.md#art-31)规定，向审计机关提供与财政收支、财务收支有关的资料。被审计单位负责人应当对本单位提供资料的真实性和完整性作出书面承诺。

**Article 28** When audit institutions conduct audit supervision according to law, the auditee shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 31 of the Audit Law, provide the audit institution with information related to fiscal revenues and expenditures and financial revenues and expenditures. The person in charge of the auditee shall make a written commitment to the truthfulness and completeness of the information provided by the entity.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　各级人民政府财政、税务以及其他部门(含直属单位)应当向本级审计机关报送下列资料：  
(一)本级人民代表大会批准的本级预算和本级人民政府财政部门向本级各部门(含直属单位)批复的预算，预算收入征收部门的年度收入计划，以及本级各部门(含直属单位)向所属各单位批复的预算；  
(二)本级预算收支执行和预算收入征收部门的收入计划完成情况月报、年报，以及决算情况；  
(三)综合性财政税务工作统计年报、情况简报，财政、预算、税务、财务和会计等规章制度；  
(四)本级各部门(含直属单位)汇总编制的本部门决算草案。

**Article 29** The finance, tax, and other departments (including directly subordinate entities) of people's governments at all levels shall submit the following materials to the audit institution at the corresponding level:  
(1) the budget approved by the people's congress at the corresponding level and the budget approved by the finance department of the people's government at the corresponding level to various departments (including directly subordinate entities) at the corresponding level, the annual revenue plans of the departments responsible for collecting budget revenues, and the budgets approved by various departments (including directly subordinate entities) at the corresponding level to their subordinate units;  
(2) monthly and annual reports on the implementation of budget revenues and expenditures at the corresponding level and the completion of revenue plans by the departments responsible for collecting budget revenues, as well as the final accounts situation;  
(3) comprehensive annual statistical reports on fiscal and tax work, briefing reports, and rules and regulations on finance, budget, tax, financial management, and accounting;  
(4) the draft final accounts of each department (including directly subordinate entities) at the corresponding level compiled on a consolidated basis.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　审计机关依照[审计法第三十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国审计法.md#art-33)规定查询被审计单位在金融机构的账户的，应当持县级以上人民政府审计机关负责人签发的协助查询单位账户通知书；查询被审计单位以个人名义在金融机构的存款的，应当持县级以上人民政府审计机关主要负责人签发的协助查询个人存款通知书。有关金融机构应当予以协助，并提供证明材料，审计机关和审计人员负有保密义务。

**Article 30** Where an audit institution, in accordance with the provisions of Article 33 of the Audit Law, inquires about the account of an auditee in a financial institution, it shall hold a notice of assistance in inquiring about the entity's account signed by the person in charge of the audit institution of the people's government at or above the county level; where it inquires about the deposits of the auditee in a financial institution in the name of an individual, it shall hold a notice of assistance in inquiring about individual deposits signed by the chief person in charge of the audit institution of the people's government at or above the county level. The relevant financial institution shall provide assistance and provide evidential materials, and the audit institution and auditors shall bear the obligation of confidentiality.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　[审计法第三十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国审计法.md#art-34)所称违反国家规定取得的资产，包括：  
(一)弄虚作假骗取的财政拨款、实物以及金融机构贷款；  
(二)违反国家规定享受国家补贴、补助、贴息、免息、减税、免税、退税等优惠政策取得的资产；  
(三)违反国家规定向他人收取的款项、有价证券、实物；  
(四)违反国家规定处分国有资产取得的收益；  
(五)违反国家规定取得的其他资产。

**Article 31** The assets obtained in violation of the provisions of the State referred to in Article 34 of the Audit Law include:  
(1) fiscal appropriations, physical objects, and loans from financial institutions obtained through fraud or deception;  
(2) assets obtained through preferential policies such as state subsidies, allowances, interest subsidies, interest-free treatment, tax reductions, tax exemptions, and tax refunds enjoyed in violation of the provisions of the State;  
(3) monies, negotiable securities, and physical objects collected from others in violation of the provisions of the State;  
(4) income obtained from the disposal of state-owned assets in violation of the provisions of the State;  
(5) other assets obtained in violation of the provisions of the State.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　审计机关依照[审计法第三十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国审计法.md#art-34)规定封存被审计单位有关资料和违反国家规定取得的资产的，应当持县级以上人民政府审计机关负责人签发的封存通知书，并在依法收集与审计事项相关的证明材料或者采取其他措施后解除封存。封存的期限为7日以内；有特殊情况需要延长的，经县级以上人民政府审计机关负责人批准，可以适当延长，但延长的期限不得超过7日。  
对封存的资料、资产，审计机关可以指定被审计单位负责保管，被审计单位不得损毁或者擅自转移。

**Article 32** Where an audit institution, in accordance with the provisions of Article 34 of the Audit Law, seals up relevant materials and assets obtained in violation of the provisions of the State from the auditee, it shall hold a sealing-up notice signed by the person in charge of the audit institution of the people's government at or above the county level, and shall lift the sealing after collecting evidential materials related to the audit matter according to law or taking other measures. The period of sealing shall be no more than 7 days; if extension is necessary due to special circumstances, with the approval of the person in charge of the audit institution of the people's government at or above the county level, the extension may be appropriately extended, but the extended period shall not exceed 7 days.  
For the sealed materials and assets, the audit institution may designate the auditee to be responsible for their custody, and the auditee shall not damage or transfer them without authorization.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　审计机关依照[审计法第三十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国审计法.md#art-36)规定，可以就有关审计事项向政府有关部门通报或者向社会公布对被审计单位的审计、专项审计调查结果。  
审计机关经与有关主管机关协商，可以在向社会公布的审计、专项审计调查结果中，一并公布对社会审计机构相关审计报告核查的结果。  
审计机关拟向社会公布对上市公司的审计、专项审计调查结果的，应当在5日前将拟公布的内容告知上市公司。

**Article 33** Audit institutions may, in accordance with the provisions of Article 36 of the Audit Law, notify the relevant government departments or publish to the public the results of audits or special audit investigations on auditees concerning relevant audit matters.  
After consultation with the relevant competent authority, audit institutions may, in the results of audits or special audit investigations published to the public, simultaneously publish the results of the verification of relevant audit reports of social audit institutions.  
Where an audit institution intends to publish to the public the results of an audit or special audit investigation on a listed company, it shall notify the listed company of the content to be published 5 days in advance.

### 第五章　审计程序

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　审计机关应当根据法律、法规和国家其他有关规定，按照本级人民政府和上级审计机关的要求，确定年度审计工作重点，编制年度审计项目计划。  
审计机关在年度审计项目计划中确定对国有资本占控股地位或者主导地位的企业、金融机构进行审计的，应当自确定之日起7日内告知列入年度审计项目计划的企业、金融机构。

**Article 34** Audit institutions shall, based on laws, regulations, and other relevant provisions of the State, and in accordance with the requirements of the people's government at the corresponding level and the audit institution at the next higher level, determine the priorities for annual audit work and prepare the annual audit project plan.  
Where an audit institution determines in the annual audit project plan to audit enterprises or financial institutions in which state-owned capital holds a controlling or dominant position, it shall notify the enterprises or financial institutions included in the annual audit project plan within 7 days from the date of such determination.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　审计机关应当根据年度审计项目计划，组成审计组，调查了解被审计单位的有关情况，编制审计方案，并在实施审计3日前，向被审计单位送达审计通知书。

**Article 35** Audit institutions shall, based on the annual audit project plan, form an audit team, investigate and understand the relevant situation of the auditee, prepare an audit plan, and deliver the audit notice to the auditee 3 days before the implementation of the audit.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　[审计法第三十八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国审计法.md#art-38)所称特殊情况，包括：  
(一)办理紧急事项的；  
(二)被审计单位涉嫌严重违法违规的；  
(三)其他特殊情况。

**Article 36** The special circumstances referred to in Article 38 of the Audit Law include:  
(1) handling urgent matters;  
(2) the auditee is suspected of serious violations of laws or regulations;  
(3) other special circumstances.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　审计人员实施审计时，应当按照下列规定办理：  
(一)通过检查、查询、监督盘点、发函询证等方法实施审计；  
(二)通过收集原件、原物或者复制、拍照等方法取得证明材料；  
(三)对与审计事项有关的会议和谈话内容作出记录，或者要求被审计单位提供会议记录材料；  
(四)记录审计实施过程和查证结果。

**Article 37** When implementing an audit, auditors shall handle the matter in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) implement the audit through methods such as inspection, inquiry, supervised counting, and correspondence confirmation;  
(2) obtain evidential materials through methods such as collecting originals or original objects, or making copies or photographs;  
(3) make records of meetings and conversations related to the audit matter, or require the auditee to provide meeting record materials;  
(4) record the audit implementation process and the findings of verification.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　审计人员向有关单位和个人调查取得的证明材料，应当有提供者的签名或者盖章；不能取得提供者签名或者盖章的，审计人员应当注明原因。

**Article 38** The evidential materials obtained by auditors through investigation from relevant entities and individuals shall bear the signature or seal of the provider; if the signature or seal of the provider cannot be obtained, the auditor shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　审计组向审计机关提出审计报告前，应当书面征求被审计单位意见。被审计单位应当自接到审计组的审计报告之日起10日内，提出书面意见；10日内未提出书面意见的，视同无异议。  
审计组应当针对被审计单位提出的书面意见，进一步核实情况，对审计组的审计报告作必要修改，连同被审计单位的书面意见一并报送审计机关。

**Article 39** Before the audit team submits the audit report to the audit institution, it shall solicit the opinions of the auditee in writing. The auditee shall submit written opinions within 10 days from the date of receiving the audit report of the audit team; if no written opinions are submitted within 10 days, it shall be deemed as having no objection.  
The audit team shall, in response to the written opinions submitted by the auditee, further verify the facts, make necessary modifications to the audit report of the audit team, and submit it together with the written opinions of the auditee to the audit institution.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　审计机关有关业务机构和专门机构或者人员对审计组的审计报告以及相关审计事项进行复核、审理后，由审计机关按照下列规定办理：  
(一)提出审计机关的审计报告，内容包括：对审计事项的审计评价，对违反国家规定的财政收支、财务收支行为提出的处理、处罚意见，移送有关主管机关、单位的意见，改进财政收支、财务收支管理工作的意见；  
(二)对违反国家规定的财政收支、财务收支行为，依法应当给予处理、处罚的，在法定职权范围内作出处理、处罚的审计决定；  
(三)对依法应当追究有关人员责任的，向有关主管机关、单位提出给予处分的建议；对依法应当由有关主管机关处理、处罚的，移送有关主管机关；涉嫌犯罪的，移送司法机关。

**Article 40** After the relevant operational departments and specialized institutions or personnel of the audit institution have conducted review and adjudication of the audit report of the audit team and the related audit matters, the audit institution shall handle the matter in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) issue the audit report of the audit institution, which includes: the audit evaluation of the audit matter, opinions on handling and punishment for acts violating the provisions of the State on fiscal revenues and expenditures and financial revenues and expenditures, opinions on transfer to relevant competent authorities or entities, and opinions on improving the management of fiscal revenues and expenditures and financial revenues and expenditures;  
(2) where handling or punishment shall be given according to law for acts violating the provisions of the State on fiscal revenues and expenditures and financial revenues and expenditures, make an audit decision on handling or punishment within the scope of statutory functions and powers;  
(3) where the liability of relevant persons shall be investigated according to law, make suggestions to the relevant competent authorities or entities to impose disciplinary sanctions; where the matter shall be handled or punished by the relevant competent authority according to law, transfer it to the relevant competent authority; if a crime is suspected, transfer it to the judicial organ.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　审计机关在审计中发现损害国家利益和社会公共利益的事项，但处理、处罚依据又不明确的，应当向本级人民政府和上一级审计机关报告。

**Article 41** Where an audit institution discovers matters that damage the interests of the State and the public interest during an audit, but the basis for handling or punishment is unclear, it shall report to the people's government at the corresponding level and the audit institution at the next higher level.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　被审计单位应当按照审计机关规定的期限和要求执行审计决定。对应当上缴的款项，被审计单位应当按照财政管理体制和国家有关规定缴入国库或者财政专户。审计决定需要有关主管机关、单位协助执行的，审计机关应当书面提请协助执行。

**Article 42** The auditee shall execute the audit decision within the time limit and in accordance with the requirements prescribed by the audit institution. For amounts that shall be turned over, the auditee shall pay them into the state treasury or a fiscal special account in accordance with the fiscal management system and the relevant provisions of the State. Where the audit decision requires the assistance of relevant competent authorities or entities in enforcement, the audit institution shall request such assistance in writing.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　上级审计机关应当对下级审计机关的审计业务依法进行监督。  
下级审计机关作出的审计决定违反国家有关规定的，上级审计机关可以责成下级审计机关予以变更或者撤销，也可以直接作出变更或者撤销的决定；审计决定被撤销后需要重新作出审计决定的，上级审计机关可以责成下级审计机关在规定的期限内重新作出审计决定，也可以直接作出审计决定。  
下级审计机关应当作出而没有作出审计决定的，上级审计机关可以责成下级审计机关在规定的期限内作出审计决定，也可以直接作出审计决定。

**Article 43** The audit institution at the higher level shall supervise the audit business of the audit institution at the lower level according to law.  
Where an audit decision made by the audit institution at the lower level violates the relevant provisions of the State, the audit institution at the higher level may instruct the audit institution at the lower level to modify or revoke it, or may directly make a decision to modify or revoke it; where a new audit decision needs to be made after the audit decision is revoked, the audit institution at the higher level may instruct the audit institution at the lower level to make a new audit decision within a prescribed time limit, or may directly make the audit decision.  
Where the audit institution at the lower level should have made an audit decision but fails to do so, the audit institution at the higher level may instruct the audit institution at the lower level to make an audit decision within a prescribed time limit, or may directly make the audit decision.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　审计机关进行专项审计调查时，应当向被调查的地方、部门、单位出示专项审计调查的书面通知，并说明有关情况；有关地方、部门、单位应当接受调查，如实反映情况，提供有关资料。  
在专项审计调查中，依法属于审计机关审计监督对象的部门、单位有违反国家规定的财政收支、财务收支行为或者其他违法违规行为的，专项审计调查人员和审计机关可以依照审计法和本条例的规定提出审计报告，作出审计决定，或者移送有关主管机关、单位依法追究责任。

**Article 44** When conducting a special audit investigation, audit institutions shall present a written notice of the special audit investigation to the investigated localities, departments, and entities, and explain the relevant circumstances; the relevant localities, departments, and entities shall accept the investigation, truthfully report the situation, and provide relevant materials.  
In a special audit investigation, if a department or entity that is subject to audit supervision by audit institutions according to law has committed acts violating the provisions of the State on fiscal revenues and expenditures and financial revenues and expenditures or other illegal or non-compliant acts, the special audit investigators and the audit institution may, in accordance with the Audit Law and these Regulations, issue an audit report, make an audit decision, or transfer the matter to the relevant competent authority or entity to investigate liability according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　审计机关应当按照国家有关规定建立、健全审计档案制度。

**Article 45** Audit institutions shall establish and improve the audit archive system in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　审计机关送达审计文书，可以直接送达，也可以邮寄送达或者以其他方式送达。直接送达的，以被审计单位在送达回证上注明的签收日期或者见证人证明的收件日期为送达日期；邮寄送达的，以邮政回执上注明的收件日期为送达日期；以其他方式送达的，以签收或者收件日期为送达日期。  
审计机关的审计文书的种类、内容和格式，由审计署规定。

**Article 46** Audit institutions may serve audit documents by direct service, postal service, or other methods. In the case of direct service, the date of service shall be the date of receipt indicated by the auditee on the proof of service or the date of receipt certified by a witness; in the case of postal service, the date of service shall be the date of receipt indicated on the postal receipt; in the case of service by other methods, the date of service shall be the date of signing or receiving.  
The types, contents, and formats of audit documents of audit institutions shall be prescribed by the National Audit Office.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　被审计单位违反审计法和本条例的规定，拒绝、拖延提供与审计事项有关的资料，或者提供的资料不真实、不完整，或者拒绝、阻碍检查的，由审计机关责令改正，可以通报批评，给予警告；拒不改正的，对被审计单位可以处5万元以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，可以处2万元以下的罚款，审计机关认为应当给予处分的，向有关主管机关、单位提出给予处分的建议；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 47** Where an auditee violates the provisions of the Audit Law and these Regulations by refusing or delaying to provide information related to the audit matter, or providing information that is untrue or incomplete, or refusing or obstructing inspection, the audit institution shall order it to make corrections, and may circulate a notice of criticism or give a warning; if it refuses to make corrections, the auditee may be fined not more than 50,000 yuan, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons may be fined not more than 20,000 yuan; if the audit institution considers that disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed, it shall make suggestions to the relevant competent authority or entity; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　对本级各部门(含直属单位)和下级人民政府违反预算的行为或者其他违反国家规定的财政收支行为，审计机关在法定职权范围内，依照法律、行政法规的规定，区别情况采取[审计法第四十五条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国审计法.md#art-45)规定的处理措施。

**Article 48** For acts violating the budget or other acts violating the provisions of the State on fiscal revenues and expenditures committed by various departments (including directly subordinate entities) at the corresponding level and people's governments at lower levels, audit institutions shall, within the scope of their statutory functions and powers and in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, take the handling measures prescribed in Article 45 of the Audit Law depending on the circumstances.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　对被审计单位违反国家规定的财务收支行为，审计机关在法定职权范围内，区别情况采取[审计法第四十五条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国审计法.md#art-45)规定的处理措施，可以通报批评，给予警告；有违法所得的，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，可以处5万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，可以处2万元以下的罚款，审计机关认为应当给予处分的，向有关主管机关、单位提出给予处分的建议；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
法律、行政法规对被审计单位违反国家规定的财务收支行为处理、处罚另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 49** For acts of the auditee violating the provisions of the State on financial revenues and expenditures, audit institutions shall, within the scope of their statutory functions and powers, take the handling measures prescribed in Article 45 of the Audit Law depending on the circumstances, and may circulate a notice of criticism or give a warning; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may be imposed; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons may be fined not more than 20,000 yuan; if the audit institution considers that disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed, it shall make suggestions to the relevant competent authority or entity; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise for the handling or punishment of acts of auditees violating the provisions of the State on financial revenues and expenditures, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　审计机关在作出较大数额罚款的处罚决定前，应当告知被审计单位和有关人员有要求举行听证的权利。较大数额罚款的具体标准由审计署规定。

**Article 50** Before making a decision on a relatively large amount of fine, audit institutions shall inform the auditee and the relevant persons of their right to request a hearing. The specific standard for a relatively large amount of fine shall be prescribed by the National Audit Office.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　审计机关提出的对被审计单位给予处理、处罚的建议以及对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予处分的建议，有关主管机关、单位应当依法及时作出决定，并将结果书面通知审计机关。

**Article 51** For the suggestions made by audit institutions on handling or punishment of the auditee and on imposing disciplinary sanctions on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons, the relevant competent authority or entity shall make a decision promptly according to law and notify the audit institution of the result in writing.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　被审计单位对审计机关依照[审计法第十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国审计法.md#art-16)、第十七条和[本条例第十五条](#art-15)规定进行审计监督作出的审计决定不服的，可以自审计决定送达之日起60日内，提请审计机关的本级人民政府裁决，本级人民政府的裁决为最终决定。  
审计机关应当在审计决定中告知被审计单位提请裁决的途径和期限。  
裁决期间，审计决定不停止执行。但是，有下列情形之一的，可以停止执行：  
(一)审计机关认为需要停止执行的；  
(二)受理裁决的人民政府认为需要停止执行的；  
(三)被审计单位申请停止执行，受理裁决的人民政府认为其要求合理，决定停止执行的。  
裁决由本级人民政府法制机构办理。裁决决定应当自接到提请之日起60日内作出；有特殊情况需要延长的，经法制机构负责人批准，可以适当延长，并告知审计机关和提请裁决的被审计单位，但延长的期限不得超过30日。

**Article 52** Where an auditee is not satisfied with an audit decision made by an audit institution pursuant to audit supervision conducted in accordance with Articles 16 and 17 of the Audit Law and Article 15 of these Regulations, it may, within 60 days from the date of service of the audit decision, request a ruling from the people's government at the corresponding level of the audit institution, and the ruling of the people's government at the corresponding level shall be final.  
The audit institution shall inform the auditee of the channels and time limit for requesting a ruling in the audit decision.  
During the ruling period, the enforcement of the audit decision shall not be suspended. However, under any of the following circumstances, enforcement may be suspended:  
(1) the audit institution considers that suspension is necessary;  
(2) the people's government accepting the ruling considers that suspension is necessary;  
(3) the auditee applies for suspension of enforcement, and the people's government accepting the ruling considers the request reasonable and decides to suspend enforcement.  
The ruling shall be handled by the legal affairs institution of the people's government at the corresponding level. The ruling decision shall be made within 60 days from the date of receiving the request; if extension is necessary due to special circumstances, with the approval of the person in charge of the legal affairs institution, the extension may be appropriately extended, and the audit institution and the auditee requesting the ruling shall be notified, but the extended period shall not exceed 30 days.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　除[本条例第五十二条](#art-52)规定的可以提请裁决的审计决定外，被审计单位对审计机关作出的其他审计决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。  
审计机关应当在审计决定中告知被审计单位申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼的途径和期限。

**Article 53** Except for the audit decisions for which a ruling may be requested as prescribed in Article 52 of these Regulations, if an auditee is not satisfied with any other audit decision made by an audit institution, it may apply for administrative reconsideration or institute an administrative action according to law.  
The audit institution shall inform the auditee of the channels and time limit for applying for administrative reconsideration or instituting an administrative action in the audit decision.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　被审计单位应当将审计决定执行情况书面报告审计机关。审计机关应当检查审计决定的执行情况。  
被审计单位不执行审计决定的，审计机关应当责令限期执行；逾期仍不执行的，审计机关可以申请人民法院强制执行，建议有关主管机关、单位对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予处分。

**Article 54** The auditee shall report the implementation of the audit decision to the audit institution in writing. The audit institution shall inspect the implementation of the audit decision.  
Where the auditee fails to implement the audit decision, the audit institution shall order it to implement within a time limit; if it still fails to implement after the expiration of the time limit, the audit institution may apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution, and suggest that the relevant competent authority or entity impose disciplinary sanctions on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　审计人员滥用职权、徇私舞弊、玩忽职守，或者泄露所知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
审计人员违法违纪取得的财物，依法予以追缴、没收或者责令退赔。

**Article 55** Where an auditor abuses power, engages in malpractice for personal gain, neglects duties, or discloses state secrets or trade secrets known to him, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Property obtained by auditors through illegal or disciplinary violations shall be recovered, confiscated, or ordered to be returned or compensated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　本条例所称以上、以下，包括本数。  
[本条例第五十二条](#art-52)规定的期间的最后一日是法定节假日的，以节假日后的第一个工作日为期间届满日。审计法和本条例规定的其他期间以工作日计算，不含法定节假日。

**Article 56** The terms "not less than," "not more than," "within," and "not exceeding" as used in these Regulations include the figure itself.  
Where the last day of a time period prescribed in Article 52 of these Regulations falls on a legal holiday, the first working day after the holiday shall be deemed the expiry date. Other time periods prescribed in the Audit Law and these Regulations shall be calculated in working days, excluding legal holidays.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　实施经济责任审计的规定，另行制定。

**Article 57** Provisions on conducting audits of economic accountability shall be formulated separately.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　本条例自2010年5月1日起施行。

**Article 58** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2010.
