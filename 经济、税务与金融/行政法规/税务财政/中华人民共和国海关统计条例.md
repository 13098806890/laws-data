# 中华人民共和国海关统计条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2022-03-29  
**生效日期**：2022-05-01

> （2005年12月25日中华人民共和国国务院令第454号公布　根据2022年3月29日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订） 第一条　为了科学、有效地开展海关统计工作，保障海关统计的准确性、及时性、完整性，根据《中华人民共和国海关法》和《中华人民共和国统计法》的有关规定，制定本条例。 第二条　海关统计是海关依法对进出口货物贸易的统计，是国民经济统计的组成部分。 海关统计的任务是对进出口货物贸易进行统计调查、统计分析和统计监督，进行进出口监测预警，编制、管理和公布海关统计资料，提供统计服务。 第三条　海关总署负责组织、管理全国海关统计工作。 海关统计机构、统计人员应当依照《中华人民共和国统计法》、《中华人民共和国统计法实施条例》及本条例的规定履行职责。 第四条　实际进出境并引起境内物质存量增加或者减少的货物，列入海关统计。 进出境物品超过自用、合理数量的，列入海关统计。 第五条　下列进出口货物不列入海关统计： （一）过境、转运和通运货物； （二）暂时进出口货物； （三）货币及货币用黄金； （四）租赁期1年以下的租赁进出口货物； （五）因残损、短少、品质不良或者规格不符而免费补偿或者更换的进出口货物； （六）海关总署规定的不列入海关统计的其他货物。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了科学、有效地开展海关统计工作，保障海关统计的准确性、及时性、完整性，根据《中华人民共和国海关法》和《中华人民共和国统计法》的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China and the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of conducting customs statistics work in a scientific and effective manner and ensuring the accuracy, timeliness, and completeness of customs statistics.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　海关统计是海关依法对进出口货物贸易的统计，是国民经济统计的组成部分。  
海关统计的任务是对进出口货物贸易进行统计调查、统计分析和统计监督，进行进出口监测预警，编制、管理和公布海关统计资料，提供统计服务。

**Article 2** Customs statistics are statistics conducted by the Customs according to law on the import and export of goods and constitute a component of national economic statistics.  
The tasks of customs statistics are to conduct statistical investigation, statistical analysis, and statistical supervision on the import and export of goods, to carry out import and export monitoring and early warning, to compile, manage, and publish customs statistical data, and to provide statistical services.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　海关总署负责组织、管理全国海关统计工作。  
海关统计机构、统计人员应当依照《中华人民共和国统计法》、《中华人民共和国统计法实施条例》及本条例的规定履行职责。

**Article 3** The General Administration of Customs shall be responsible for organizing and managing customs statistics work nationwide.  
Customs statistical institutions and statistical personnel shall perform their duties in accordance with the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China, the Implementing Regulations of the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China, and these Regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　实际进出境并引起境内物质存量增加或者减少的货物，列入海关统计。  
进出境物品超过自用、合理数量的，列入海关统计。

**Article 4** Goods that actually enter or exit the territory and cause an increase or decrease in the material stock within the territory shall be included in customs statistics.  
Articles entering or exiting the territory in quantities exceeding the self-use or reasonable quantities shall be included in customs statistics.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　下列进出口货物不列入海关统计：  
（一）过境、转运和通运货物；  
（二）暂时进出口货物；  
（三）货币及货币用黄金；  
（四）租赁期1年以下的租赁进出口货物；  
（五）因残损、短少、品质不良或者规格不符而免费补偿或者更换的进出口货物；  
（六）海关总署规定的不列入海关统计的其他货物。

**Article 5** The following import and export goods shall not be included in customs statistics:  
(1) goods in transit, transshipment, and through transport;  
(2) temporary import or export goods;  
(3) currency and monetary gold;  
(4) imported or exported goods on lease with a lease term of less than one year;  
(5) imported or exported goods that are compensated or replaced free of charge due to damage, shortage, poor quality, or specification discrepancy;  
(6) other goods that the General Administration of Customs specifies shall not be included in customs statistics.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　进出口货物的统计项目包括：  
（一）品名及编码；  
（二）数量、价格；  
（三）进出口货物收发货人；  
（四）贸易方式；  
（五）运输方式；  
（六）进口货物的原产国（地区）、启运国（地区）、境内目的地；  
（七）出口货物的最终目的国（地区）、运抵国（地区）、境内货源地；  
（八）进出口日期；  
（九）关别；  
（十）海关总署规定的其他统计项目。  
根据国民经济发展和海关监管需要，海关总署可以对统计项目进行调整。

**Article 6** The statistical items for import and export goods include:  
(1) description and code;  
(2) quantity and price;  
(3) import or export goods consignor or consignee;  
(4) trade mode;  
(5) mode of transport;  
(6) country (region) of origin, country (region) of shipment, and domestic destination for imported goods;  
(7) country (region) of final destination, country (region) of arrival, and domestic place of origin for exported goods;  
(8) import or export date;  
(9) customs office;  
(10) other statistical items specified by the General Administration of Customs.  
The General Administration of Customs may adjust the statistical items based on the needs of national economic development and customs supervision.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　进出口货物的品名及编码，按照《中华人民共和国海关统计商品目录》归类统计。  
进出口货物的数量，按照《中华人民共和国海关统计商品目录》规定的计量单位统计。  
《中华人民共和国海关统计商品目录》由海关总署公布。

**Article 7** The description and code of import and export goods shall be classified and statistically recorded in accordance with the Customs Statistical Commodity Catalogue of the People's Republic of China.  
The quantity of import and export goods shall be statistically recorded in accordance with the unit of measurement prescribed in the Customs Statistical Commodity Catalogue of the People's Republic of China.  
The Customs Statistical Commodity Catalogue of the People's Republic of China shall be published by the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　进口货物的价格，按照货价、货物运抵中华人民共和国境内输入地点起卸前的运输及其相关费用、保险费之和统计。  
出口货物的价格，按照货价、货物运抵中华人民共和国境内输出地点装卸前的运输及其相关费用、保险费之和统计，其中包含的出口关税税额，应当予以扣除。

**Article 8** The price of imported goods shall be statistically recorded as the sum of the cost of the goods, transport and related expenses, and insurance premiums incurred before unloading at the place of entry within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
The price of exported goods shall be statistically recorded as the sum of the cost of the goods, transport and related expenses, and insurance premiums incurred before loading at the place of exit within the territory of the People's Republic of China, and the amount of export customs duties included therein shall be deducted.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　进口货物，应当分别统计其原产国（地区）、启运国（地区）和境内目的地。  
出口货物，应当分别统计其最终目的国（地区）、运抵国（地区）和境内货源地。

**Article 9** For imported goods, their country (region) of origin, country (region) of shipment, and domestic destination shall be statistically recorded separately.  
For exported goods, their country (region) of final destination, country (region) of arrival, and domestic place of origin shall be statistically recorded separately.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　进出口货物收发货人，按照从事进出口经营活动的法人、其他组织或者个人统计。

**Article 10** The import or export goods consignor or consignee shall be statistically recorded according to the legal person, other organization, or individual engaged in import and export business activities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　进出口货物的贸易方式，按照海关监管要求分类统计。

**Article 11** The trade modes of import and export goods shall be classified and compiled in accordance with the requirements of customs control.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　进出口货物的运输方式，按照货物进出境时的运输方式统计，包括水路运输、铁路运输、公路运输、航空运输及其他运输方式。

**Article 12** The means of transportation of import and export goods shall be compiled by the means of transportation when the goods enter or leave the territory, including waterway transportation, railway transportation, highway transportation, air transportation and other means of transportation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　进口货物的日期，按照海关放行的日期统计；出口货物的日期，按照办结海关手续的日期统计。

**Article 13** The date of import goods shall be compiled by the date of customs release; the date of export goods shall be compiled by the date of completion of customs formalities.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　进出口货物由接受申报的海关负责统计。

**Article 14** Import and export goods shall be compiled by the customs office that accepts the declaration.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　海关统计资料包括海关统计原始资料以及以原始资料为基础采集、整理的相关统计信息。  
前款所称海关统计原始资料，是指经海关确认的进出口货物报关单及其他有关单证。

**Article 15** Customs statistical data include original customs statistical data and relevant statistical information collected and organized on the basis thereof.  
The "original customs statistical data" as referred to in the preceding paragraph means the customs declaration forms for import and export goods confirmed by the customs and other relevant documents.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　海关总署应当定期、无偿地向国务院有关部门提供有关综合统计资料。  
直属海关应当定期、无偿地向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关部门提供有关综合统计资料。

**Article 16** The General Administration of Customs shall regularly and free of charge provide relevant comprehensive statistical data to the relevant departments of the State Council.  
The directly subordinate customs offices shall regularly and free of charge provide relevant comprehensive statistical data to the relevant departments of the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government where they are located.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　海关应当建立统计资料定期公布制度，向社会公布海关统计信息。  
海关可以根据社会公众的需要，提供统计服务。

**Article 17** The customs shall establish a system for regular publication of statistical data and release customs statistical information to the public.  
The customs may provide statistical services in accordance with the needs of the public.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　海关统计人员对在统计过程中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密负有保密义务。

**Article 18** Customs statistical personnel shall have the duty of confidentiality with respect to state secrets and trade secrets known to them during the statistical process.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　当事人有权在保存期限内查询自己申报的海关统计原始资料及相关信息，对查询结果有疑问的，可以向海关申请核实，海关应当予以核实，并解答有关问题。

**Article 19** A party shall have the right to query the original customs statistical data and related information declared by itself within the retention period; if it has doubts about the query results, it may apply to the customs for verification, and the customs shall verify and answer the relevant questions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　海关对当事人依法应当申报的项目有疑问的，可以向当事人提出查询，当事人应当及时作出答复。

**Article 20** If the customs has doubts about the items that the party shall declare according to law, it may make an inquiry to the party, and the party shall make a timely response.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　依法应当申报的项目未申报或者申报不实影响海关统计准确性的，海关应当责令当事人予以更正，需要予以行政处罚的，依照《中华人民共和国海关行政处罚实施条例》的规定予以处罚。

**Article 21** Items that shall be declared according to law but are not declared or are falsely declared, affecting the accuracy of customs statistics, the customs shall order the party to make corrections; if an administrative penalty is necessary, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Implementation of Customs Administrative Penalties.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本条例自2006年3月1日起施行。

**Article 22** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 2006.
