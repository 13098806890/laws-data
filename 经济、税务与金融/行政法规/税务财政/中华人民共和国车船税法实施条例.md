# 中华人民共和国车船税法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2012-01-01

> (2011年12月5日中华人民共和国国务院令第611号公布　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国车船税法》(以下简称车船税法)的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　车船税法第一条所称车辆、船舶，是指： (一)依法应当在车船登记管理部门登记的机动车辆和船舶； (二)依法不需要在车船登记管理部门登记的在单位内部场所行驶或者作业的机动车辆和船舶。 第三条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据车船税法所附《车船税税目税额表》确定车辆具体适用税额，应当遵循以下原则： (一)乘用车依排气量从小到大递增税额； (二)客车按照核定载客人数20人以下和20人(含)以上两档划分，递增税额。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的车辆具体适用税额，应当报国务院备案。 第四条　机动船舶具体适用税额为： (一)净吨位不超过200吨的，每吨3元； (二)净吨位超过200吨但不超过2000吨的，每吨4元； (三)净吨位超过2000吨但不超过10000吨的，每吨5元； (四)净吨位超过10000吨的，每吨6元。 拖船按照发动机功率每1千瓦折合净吨位0.67吨计算征收车船税。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国车船税法》(以下简称车船税法)的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Vehicle and Vessel Tax Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Vehicle and Vessel Tax Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　[车船税法第一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国车船税法.md#art-1)所称车辆、船舶，是指：  
(一)依法应当在车船登记管理部门登记的机动车辆和船舶；  
(二)依法不需要在车船登记管理部门登记的在单位内部场所行驶或者作业的机动车辆和船舶。

**Article 2** The term "vehicles and vessels" as mentioned in Article 1 of the Vehicle and Vessel Tax Law shall refer to:  
(1) motor vehicles and vessels that shall be registered with the vehicle and vessel registration administration department according to law;  
(2) motor vehicles and vessels that are not required to be registered with the vehicle and vessel registration administration department according to law but are operated or used within the premises of a unit.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据车船税法所附《车船税税目税额表》确定车辆具体适用税额，应当遵循以下原则：  
(一)乘用车依排气量从小到大递增税额；  
(二)客车按照核定载客人数20人以下和20人(含)以上两档划分，递增税额。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的车辆具体适用税额，应当报国务院备案。

**Article 3** The specific applicable tax amounts for vehicles determined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government in accordance with the Schedule of Vehicle and Vessel Tax Items and Tax Amounts attached to the Vehicle and Vessel Tax Law shall follow the following principles:  
(1) for passenger cars, the tax amount shall increase progressively from low to high based on engine displacement;  
(2) for buses, the tax amount shall increase progressively by two categories: those with a certified passenger capacity of fewer than 20 persons and those with a certified passenger capacity of 20 persons or more.  
The specific applicable tax amounts for vehicles determined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be reported to the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　机动船舶具体适用税额为：  
(一)净吨位不超过200吨的，每吨3元；  
(二)净吨位超过200吨但不超过2000吨的，每吨4元；  
(三)净吨位超过2000吨但不超过10000吨的，每吨5元；  
(四)净吨位超过10000吨的，每吨6元。  
拖船按照发动机功率每1千瓦折合净吨位0.67吨计算征收车船税。

**Article 4** The specific applicable tax amounts for motor vessels are as follows:  
(1) for those with a net tonnage not exceeding 200 tons, 3 yuan per ton;  
(2) for those with a net tonnage exceeding 200 tons but not exceeding 2,000 tons, 4 yuan per ton;  
(3) for those with a net tonnage exceeding 2,000 tons but not exceeding 10,000 tons, 5 yuan per ton;  
(4) for those with a net tonnage exceeding 10,000 tons, 6 yuan per ton.  
For tugboats, the vehicle and vessel tax shall be calculated and levied based on a conversion of 0.67 tons of net tonnage per kilowatt of engine power.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　游艇具体适用税额为：  
(一)艇身长度不超过10米的，每米600元；  
(二)艇身长度超过10米但不超过18米的，每米900元；  
(三)艇身长度超过18米但不超过30米的，每米1300元；  
(四)艇身长度超过30米的，每米2000元；  
(五)辅助动力帆艇，每米600元。

**Article 5** The specific applicable tax amounts for yachts are as follows:  
(1) for those with a hull length not exceeding 10 meters, 600 yuan per meter;  
(2) for those with a hull length exceeding 10 meters but not exceeding 18 meters, 900 yuan per meter;  
(3) for those with a hull length exceeding 18 meters but not exceeding 30 meters, 1,300 yuan per meter;  
(4) for those with a hull length exceeding 30 meters, 2,000 yuan per meter;  
(5) for auxiliary-powered sailing yachts, 600 yuan per meter.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　车船税法和本条例所涉及的排气量、整备质量、核定载客人数、净吨位、千瓦、艇身长度，以车船登记管理部门核发的车船登记证书或者行驶证所载数据为准。  
依法不需要办理登记的车船和依法应当登记而未办理登记或者不能提供车船登记证书、行驶证的车船，以车船出厂合格证明或者进口凭证标注的技术参数、数据为准；不能提供车船出厂合格证明或者进口凭证的，由主管税务机关参照国家相关标准核定，没有国家相关标准的参照同类车船核定。

**Article 6** The engine displacement, curb weight, certified passenger capacity, net tonnage, kilowatts, and hull length involved in the Vehicle and Vessel Tax Law and these Regulations shall be based on the data recorded in the vehicle and vessel registration certificate or the driving permit issued by the vehicle and vessel registration administration department.  
For vehicles and vessels that are not required to undergo registration according to law, and for those that shall be registered according to law but have not been registered or cannot provide a vehicle and vessel registration certificate or driving permit, the technical parameters and data indicated in the vehicle and vessel factory qualification certificate or import certificate shall prevail; where the vehicle and vessel factory qualification certificate or import certificate cannot be provided, the competent tax authority shall determine them by reference to the relevant national standards, or in the absence of relevant national standards, by reference to similar vehicles and vessels.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　[车船税法第三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国车船税法.md#art-3)第一项所称的捕捞、养殖渔船，是指在渔业船舶登记管理部门登记为捕捞船或者养殖船的船舶。

**Article 7** The term "fishing and aquaculture vessels" as mentioned in Item 1 of Article 3 of the Vehicle and Vessel Tax Law shall refer to vessels that are registered as fishing vessels or aquaculture vessels with the fishery vessel registration administration department.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　[车船税法第三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国车船税法.md#art-3)第二项所称的军队、武装警察部队专用的车船，是指按照规定在军队、武装警察部队车船登记管理部门登记，并领取军队、武警牌照的车船。

**Article 8** The term "vehicles and vessels exclusively used by the military and the armed police forces" as mentioned in Item 2 of Article 3 of the Vehicle and Vessel Tax Law shall refer to vehicles and vessels that are registered with the vehicle and vessel registration administration departments of the military and the armed police forces and have obtained military or armed police license plates in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　[车船税法第三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国车船税法.md#art-3)第三项所称的警用车船，是指公安机关、国家安全机关、监狱、劳动教养管理机关和人民法院、人民检察院领取警用牌照的车辆和执行警务的专用船舶。

**Article 9** The term "police vehicles and vessels" as mentioned in Item 3 of Article 3 of the Vehicle and Vessel Tax Law shall refer to vehicles that have obtained police license plates and special vessels for performing police duties of the public security organs, state security organs, prisons, reeducation-through-labor administrative organs, and the People's Courts and People's Procuratorates.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　节约能源、使用新能源的车船可以免征或者减半征收车船税。免征或者减半征收车船税的车船的范围，由国务院财政、税务主管部门商国务院有关部门制订，报国务院批准。  
对受地震、洪涝等严重自然灾害影响纳税困难以及其他特殊原因确需减免税的车船，可以在一定期限内减征或者免征车船税。具体减免期限和数额由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定，报国务院备案。

**Article 10** Vehicles and vessels that save energy or use new energy may be exempted from or subject to a 50% reduction in vehicle and vessel tax. The scope of vehicles and vessels eligible for such exemption or 50% reduction shall be formulated by the fiscal and tax departments of the State Council in consultation with the relevant departments of the State Council, and shall be reported to the State Council for approval.  
For vehicles and vessels that have difficulty in paying taxes due to severe natural disasters such as earthquakes and floods, or for other special reasons that genuinely require tax reduction or exemption, the vehicle and vessel tax may be reduced or exempted within a certain period. The specific reduction or exemption period and amount shall be determined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and shall be reported to the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　车船税由税务机关负责征收。

**Article 11** Vehicle and vessel tax shall be collected by the tax authorities.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　机动车车船税扣缴义务人在代收车船税时，应当在机动车交通事故责任强制保险的保险单以及保费发票上注明已收税款的信息，作为代收税款凭证。

**Article 12** When a motor vehicle and vessel tax withholding agent collects the vehicle and vessel tax on behalf of the tax authority, it shall indicate the information on the tax collected on the insurance policy of compulsory motor vehicle traffic accident liability insurance and the premium invoice, as a certificate of tax collection on behalf of the tax authority.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　已完税或者依法减免税的车辆，纳税人应当向扣缴义务人提供登记地的主管税务机关出具的完税凭证或者减免税证明。

**Article 13** For vehicles for which tax has been paid or tax has been reduced or exempted according to law, the taxpayer shall provide the withholding agent with the tax payment certificate or tax reduction or exemption certificate issued by the competent tax authority of the place of registration.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　纳税人没有按照规定期限缴纳车船税的，扣缴义务人在代收代缴税款时，可以一并代收代缴欠缴税款的滞纳金。

**Article 14** Where a taxpayer fails to pay vehicle and vessel tax within the prescribed time limit, the withholding agent, when collecting and paying the tax on behalf of the taxpayer, may collect and pay the overdue fine for the tax in arrears at the same time.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　扣缴义务人已代收代缴车船税的，纳税人不再向车辆登记地的主管税务机关申报缴纳车船税。  
没有扣缴义务人的，纳税人应当向主管税务机关自行申报缴纳车船税。

**Article 15** Where a withholding agent has collected and paid the vehicle and vessel tax on behalf of the taxpayer, the taxpayer shall no longer declare and pay the vehicle and vessel tax to the competent tax authority of the place of vehicle registration.  
Where there is no withholding agent, the taxpayer shall declare and pay the vehicle and vessel tax to the competent tax authority on its own.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　纳税人缴纳车船税时，应当提供反映排气量、整备质量、核定载客人数、净吨位、千瓦、艇身长度等与纳税相关信息的相应凭证以及税务机关根据实际需要要求提供的其他资料。  
纳税人以前年度已经提供前款所列资料信息的，可以不再提供。

**Article 16** When paying vehicle and vessel tax, the taxpayer shall provide the corresponding certificates reflecting the information related to tax payment such as displacement, curb weight, approved passenger capacity, net tonnage, kilowatts, hull length, etc., and other materials required by the tax authorities according to actual needs.  
Where the taxpayer has already provided the materials and information listed in the preceding paragraph in previous years, it may no longer provide them.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　车辆车船税的纳税人按照纳税地点所在的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的具体适用税额缴纳车船税。

**Article 17** The taxpayer of vehicle and vessel tax shall pay the vehicle and vessel tax in accordance with the specific applicable tax amount determined by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the tax payment place is located.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　扣缴义务人应当及时解缴代收代缴的税款和滞纳金，并向主管税务机关申报。扣缴义务人向税务机关解缴税款和滞纳金时，应当同时报送明细的税款和滞纳金扣缴报告。扣缴义务人解缴税款和滞纳金的具体期限，由省、自治区、直辖市税务机关依照法律、行政法规的规定确定。

**Article 18** A withholding agent shall, in a timely manner, turn over the tax and overdue fine collected and paid on behalf of the taxpayer, and declare to the competent tax authority. When turning over the tax and overdue fine to the tax authority, the withholding agent shall at the same time submit a detailed report on the withholding of the tax and overdue fine. The specific time limit for the withholding agent to turn over the tax and overdue fine shall be determined by the tax authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　购置的新车船，购置当年的应纳税额自纳税义务发生的当月起按月计算。应纳税额为年应纳税额除以12再乘以应纳税月份数。  
在一个纳税年度内，已完税的车船被盗抢、报废、灭失的，纳税人可以凭有关管理机关出具的证明和完税凭证，向纳税所在地的主管税务机关申请退还自被盗抢、报废、灭失月份起至该纳税年度终了期间的税款。  
已办理退税的被盗抢车船失而复得的，纳税人应当从公安机关出具相关证明的当月起计算缴纳车船税。

**Article 19** For a newly purchased vehicle or vessel, the taxable amount for the year of purchase shall be calculated on a monthly basis from the month in which the tax liability arises. The taxable amount shall be the annual taxable amount divided by 12 and multiplied by the number of taxable months.  
Within a tax year, where a vehicle or vessel on which tax has been paid is stolen, robbed, scrapped, or lost, the taxpayer may, on the basis of the certificate issued by the relevant administrative authority and the tax payment certificate, apply to the competent tax authority at the place of tax payment for a refund of the tax for the period from the month of theft, robbery, scrapping, or loss to the end of the tax year.  
Where a stolen or robbed vehicle or vessel for which tax has been refunded is recovered, the taxpayer shall calculate and pay the vehicle and vessel tax from the month in which the public security organ issues the relevant certificate.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　已缴纳车船税的车船在同一纳税年度内办理转让过户的，不另纳税，也不退税。

**Article 20** Where a vehicle or vessel on which vehicle and vessel tax has been paid is transferred or undergoes change of ownership within the same tax year, no additional tax shall be paid, nor shall a tax refund be made.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　[车船税法第八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/税收与财政/中华人民共和国车船税法.md#art-8)所称取得车船所有权或者管理权的当月，应当以购买车船的发票或者其他证明文件所载日期的当月为准。

**Article 21** The month in which the ownership or management right of a vehicle or vessel is obtained as mentioned in Article 8 of the Vehicle and Vessel Tax Law shall be the month in which the date specified in the invoice or other supporting documents for the purchase of the vehicle or vessel falls.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　税务机关可以在车船登记管理部门、车船检验机构的办公场所集中办理车船税征收事宜。  
公安机关交通管理部门在办理车辆相关登记和定期检验手续时，经核查，对没有提供依法纳税或者免税证明的，不予办理相关手续。

**Article 22** The tax authorities may centrally handle the collection of vehicle and vessel tax at the office premises of the vehicle and vessel registration administration departments and vehicle and vessel inspection institutions.  
When handling vehicle registration and periodic inspection procedures, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall, upon verification, not handle the relevant procedures for those that fail to provide certificates of tax payment or tax exemption according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　车船税按年申报，分月计算，一次性缴纳。纳税年度为公历1月1日至12月31日。

**Article 23** Vehicle and vessel tax shall be declared annually, calculated monthly, and paid in a lump sum. The tax year shall be from January 1 to December 31 of the Gregorian calendar.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　临时入境的外国车船和香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区的车船，不征收车船税。

**Article 24** Vehicle and vessel tax shall not be levied on vehicles and vessels of foreign countries and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Macao Special Administrative Region, and Taiwan region that temporarily enter the country.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　按照规定缴纳船舶吨税的机动船舶，自车船税法实施之日起5年内免征车船税。  
依法不需要在车船登记管理部门登记的机场、港口、铁路站场内部行驶或者作业的车船，自车船税法实施之日起5年内免征车船税。

**Article 25** Power-driven vessels that pay tonnage tax in accordance with the regulations shall be exempted from vehicle and vessel tax for five years from the date of implementation of the Vehicle and Vessel Tax Law.  
Vehicles and vessels that are not required to be registered with the vehicle and vessel registration administration department according to law and that operate or travel within airports, ports, or railway stations and yards shall be exempted from vehicle and vessel tax for five years from the date of implementation of the Vehicle and Vessel Tax Law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　车船税法所附《车船税税目税额表》中车辆、船舶的含义如下：  
乘用车，是指在设计和技术特性上主要用于载运乘客及随身行李，核定载客人数包括驾驶员在内不超过9人的汽车。  
商用车，是指除乘用车外，在设计和技术特性上用于载运乘客、货物的汽车，划分为客车和货车。  
半挂牵引车，是指装备有特殊装置用于牵引半挂车的商用车。  
三轮汽车，是指最高设计车速不超过每小时50公里，具有三个车轮的货车。  
低速载货汽车，是指以柴油机为动力，最高设计车速不超过每小时70公里，具有四个车轮的货车。  
挂车，是指就其设计和技术特性需由汽车或者拖拉机牵引，才能正常使用的一种无动力的道路车辆。  
专用作业车，是指在其设计和技术特性上用于特殊工作的车辆。  
轮式专用机械车，是指有特殊结构和专门功能，装有橡胶车轮可以自行行驶，最高设计车速大于每小时20公里的轮式工程机械车。  
摩托车，是指无论采用何种驱动方式，最高设计车速大于每小时50公里，或者使用内燃机，其排量大于50毫升的两轮或者三轮车辆。  
船舶，是指各类机动、非机动船舶以及其他水上移动装置，但是船舶上装备的救生艇筏和长度小于5米的艇筏除外。其中，机动船舶是指用机器推进的船舶；拖船是指专门用于拖(推)动运输船舶的专业作业船舶；非机动驳船，是指在船舶登记管理部门登记为驳船的非机动船舶；游艇是指具备内置机械推进动力装置，长度在90米以下，主要用于游览观光、休闲娱乐、水上体育运动等活动，并应当具有船舶检验证书和适航证书的船舶。

**Article 26** The meanings of vehicles and vessels in the "Vehicle and Vessel Tax Tax Items and Tax Rates Table" attached to the Vehicle and Vessel Tax Law are as follows:  
Passenger vehicles refer to motor vehicles that are mainly designed and technically characterized for carrying passengers and their personal luggage, with an approved number of passengers including the driver not exceeding nine persons.  
Commercial vehicles refer to motor vehicles other than passenger vehicles that are designed and technically characterized for carrying passengers or goods, and are divided into buses and trucks.  
Semi-trailer towing vehicles refer to commercial vehicles equipped with special devices for towing semi-trailers.  
Three-wheeled vehicles refer to trucks with three wheels and a maximum design speed not exceeding 50 kilometers per hour.  
Low-speed cargo vehicles refer to trucks powered by diesel engines with a maximum design speed not exceeding 70 kilometers per hour and having four wheels.  
Trailers refer to non-powered road vehicles that, by design and technical characteristics, require towing by a motor vehicle or tractor for normal use.  
Special purpose vehicles refer to vehicles designed and technically characterized for special work.  
Wheeled special machinery vehicles refer to wheeled engineering machinery vehicles with special structures and functions, equipped with rubber tires, capable of self-propulsion, and with a maximum design speed exceeding 20 kilometers per hour.  
Motorcycles refer to two-wheeled or three-wheeled vehicles, regardless of the driving method, with a maximum design speed exceeding 50 kilometers per hour, or using an internal combustion engine with a displacement exceeding 50 milliliters.  
Vessels refer to various types of power-driven and non-power-driven vessels and other floating devices on water, except for lifeboats and rafts equipped on vessels and boats with a length of less than 5 meters. Among them, power-driven vessels refer to vessels propelled by machinery; tugboats refer to special-purpose vessels specifically used for towing (or pushing) transport vessels; non-power-driven barges refer to non-power-driven vessels registered as barges with the vessel registration administration department; yachts refer to vessels with built-in mechanical propulsion power devices, with a length of less than 90 meters, mainly used for sightseeing, leisure and entertainment, water sports activities, etc., and shall have a vessel inspection certificate and a seaworthiness certificate.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本条例自2012年1月1日起施行。

**Article 27** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2012.
