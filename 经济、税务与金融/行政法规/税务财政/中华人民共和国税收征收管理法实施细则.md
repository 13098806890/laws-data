# 中华人民共和国税收征收管理法实施细则

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2016-02-06  
**生效日期**：2002-10-15

> (2002年9月7日中华人民共和国国务院令第362号公布　根据2012年11月9日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2013年7月18日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》(以下简称税收征管法)的规定，制定本细则。 第二条　凡依法由税务机关征收的各种税收的征收管理，均适用税收征管法及本细则；税收征管法及本细则没有规定的，依照其他有关税收法律、行政法规的规定执行。 第三条　任何部门、单位和个人作出的与税收法律、行政法规相抵触的决定一律无效，税务机关不得执行，并应当向上级税务机关报告。 纳税人应当依照税收法律、行政法规的规定履行纳税义务；其签订的合同、协议等与税收法律、行政法规相抵触的，一律无效。 第四条　国家税务总局负责制定全国税务系统信息化建设的总体规划、技术标准、技术方案与实施办法；各级税务机关应当按照国家税务总局的总体规划、技术标准、技术方案与实施办法，做好本地区税务系统信息化建设的具体工作。 地方各级人民政府应当积极支持税务系统信息化建设，并组织有关部门实现相关信息的共享。 第五条　税收征管法第八条所称为纳税人、扣缴义务人保密的情况，是指纳税人、扣缴义务人的商业秘密及个人隐私。纳税人、扣缴义务人的税收违法行为不属于保密范围。 第六条　国家税务总局应当制定税务人员行为准则和服务规范。 上级税务机关发现下级税务机关的税收违法行为，应当及时予以纠正；下级税务机关应当按照上级税务机关的决定及时改正。 下级税务机关发现上级税务机关的税收违法行为，应当向上级税务机关或者有关部门报告。 第七条　税务机关根据检举人的贡献大小给予相应的奖励，奖励所需资金列入税务部门年度预算，单项核定。奖励资金具体使用办法以及奖励标准，由国家税务总局会同财政部制定。 第八条　税务人员在核定应纳税额、调整税收定额、进行税务检查、实施税务行政处罚、办理税务行政复议时，与纳税人、扣缴义务人或者其法定代表人、直接责任人有下列关系之一的，应当回避： (一)夫妻关系；

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》(以下简称税收征管法)的规定，制定本细则。

**Article 1** These Detailed Rules are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection (hereinafter referred to as the Tax Collection and Administration Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　凡依法由税务机关征收的各种税收的征收管理，均适用税收征管法及本细则；税收征管法及本细则没有规定的，依照其他有关税收法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 2** The collection and administration of all types of taxes levied by tax authorities according to law shall be governed by the Tax Collection and Administration Law and these Detailed Rules; matters not covered by the Tax Collection and Administration Law and these Detailed Rules shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of other relevant tax laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　任何部门、单位和个人作出的与税收法律、行政法规相抵触的决定一律无效，税务机关不得执行，并应当向上级税务机关报告。  
纳税人应当依照税收法律、行政法规的规定履行纳税义务；其签订的合同、协议等与税收法律、行政法规相抵触的，一律无效。

**Article 3** Any decision made by any department, entity or individual that contradicts tax laws and administrative regulations shall be null and void, and the tax authority shall not implement it and shall report to the tax authority at a higher level.  
A taxpayer shall perform its tax payment obligations in accordance with tax laws and administrative regulations; any contract or agreement signed by it that contradicts tax laws and administrative regulations shall be null and void.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家税务总局负责制定全国税务系统信息化建设的总体规划、技术标准、技术方案与实施办法；各级税务机关应当按照国家税务总局的总体规划、技术标准、技术方案与实施办法，做好本地区税务系统信息化建设的具体工作。  
地方各级人民政府应当积极支持税务系统信息化建设，并组织有关部门实现相关信息的共享。

**Article 4** The State Administration of Taxation shall be responsible for formulating the overall plan, technical standards, technical solutions and implementation measures for the informatization construction of the national tax system; tax authorities at all levels shall, in accordance with the overall plan, technical standards, technical solutions and implementation measures of the State Administration of Taxation, carry out the specific work of informatization construction of the tax system in their respective regions.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall actively support the informatization construction of the tax system and organize relevant departments to achieve the sharing of relevant information.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　税收征管法第八条所称为纳税人、扣缴义务人保密的情况，是指纳税人、扣缴义务人的商业秘密及个人隐私。纳税人、扣缴义务人的税收违法行为不属于保密范围。

**Article 5** The information to be kept confidential for taxpayers and withholding agents as referred to in Article 8 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law means the trade secrets and personal privacy of taxpayers and withholding agents. Tax illegal acts of taxpayers and withholding agents shall not fall within the scope of confidentiality.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家税务总局应当制定税务人员行为准则和服务规范。  
上级税务机关发现下级税务机关的税收违法行为，应当及时予以纠正；下级税务机关应当按照上级税务机关的决定及时改正。  
下级税务机关发现上级税务机关的税收违法行为，应当向上级税务机关或者有关部门报告。

**Article 6** The State Administration of Taxation shall formulate code of conduct and service standards for tax personnel.  
If a tax authority at a higher level discovers a tax illegal act committed by a tax authority at a lower level, it shall promptly rectify it; the tax authority at a lower level shall promptly make corrections in accordance with the decision of the tax authority at a higher level.  
If a tax authority at a lower level discovers a tax illegal act committed by a tax authority at a higher level, it shall report to the tax authority at a higher level or to relevant departments.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　税务机关根据检举人的贡献大小给予相应的奖励，奖励所需资金列入税务部门年度预算，单项核定。奖励资金具体使用办法以及奖励标准，由国家税务总局会同财政部制定。

**Article 7** A tax authority shall grant corresponding rewards to a whistleblower based on the extent of his contribution; the funds required for such rewards shall be included in the annual budget of the tax department and approved separately. The specific measures for the use of reward funds and the reward standards shall be formulated by the State Administration of Taxation jointly with the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　税务人员在核定应纳税额、调整税收定额、进行税务检查、实施税务行政处罚、办理税务行政复议时，与纳税人、扣缴义务人或者其法定代表人、直接责任人有下列关系之一的，应当回避：  
(一)夫妻关系；  
(二)直系血亲关系；  
(三)三代以内旁系血亲关系；  
(四)近姻亲关系；  
(五)可能影响公正执法的其他利害关系。

**Article 8** When a tax official is determining the amount of tax payable, adjusting tax quotas, conducting tax inspections, imposing tax administrative penalties, or handling tax administrative reconsideration, if he has any of the following relationships with the taxpayer, withholding agent, or their legal representative or directly responsible person, he shall withdraw:  
(1) Spouse relationship;  
(2) Direct blood relationship;  
(3) Collateral blood relationship within three generations;  
(4) Close in-law relationship;  
(5) Other interests that may affect impartial law enforcement.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　税收征管法第十四条所称按照国务院规定设立的并向社会公告的税务机构，是指省以下税务局的稽查局。稽查局专司偷税、逃避追缴欠税、骗税、抗税案件的查处。  
国家税务总局应当明确划分税务局和稽查局的职责，避免职责交叉。

**Article 9** The tax authorities established in accordance with the provisions of the State Council and announced to the public as referred to in Article 14 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law mean the inspection bureaus of the tax bureaus at or below the provincial level. The inspection bureau shall be exclusively responsible for investigating and handling cases of tax evasion, avoidance of recovery of tax arrears, tax fraud, and tax resistance.  
The State Administration of Taxation shall clearly define the functions of tax bureaus and inspection bureaus to avoid overlapping functions.

### 第二章　税务登记

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家税务局、地方税务局对同一纳税人的税务登记应当采用同一代码，信息共享。  
税务登记的具体办法由国家税务总局制定。

**Article 10** The State Tax Bureau and the Local Tax Bureau shall use the same code for the tax registration of the same taxpayer and share information.  
The specific measures for tax registration shall be formulated by the State Administration of Taxation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　各级工商行政管理机关应当向同级国家税务局和地方税务局定期通报办理开业、变更、注销登记以及吊销营业执照的情况。  
通报的具体办法由国家税务总局和国家工商行政管理总局联合制定。

**Article 11** The administrative departments for industry and commerce at all levels shall regularly notify the state tax bureaus and local tax bureaus at the same level of the situations of handling registration for commencement, modification, cancellation, and revocation of business licenses.  
The specific measures for notification shall be jointly formulated by the State Administration of Taxation and the State Administration for Industry and Commerce.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　从事生产、经营的纳税人应当自领取营业执照之日起30日内，向生产、经营地或者纳税义务发生地的主管税务机关申报办理税务登记，如实填写税务登记表，并按照税务机关的要求提供有关证件、资料。  
前款规定以外的纳税人，除国家机关和个人外，应当自纳税义务发生之日起30日内，持有关证件向所在地的主管税务机关申报办理税务登记。  
个人所得税的纳税人办理税务登记的办法由国务院另行规定。  
税务登记证件的式样，由国家税务总局制定。

**Article 12** Taxpayers engaged in production or business operations shall, within 30 days from the date of obtaining the business license, apply to the competent tax authority in the place of production or business operation or the place where the tax obligation arises for tax registration, truthfully fill in the tax registration form, and provide relevant certificates and materials as required by the tax authority.  
Taxpayers other than those specified in the preceding paragraph, except state organs and individuals, shall, within 30 days from the date the tax obligation arises, apply to the competent tax authority at their locality for tax registration with relevant certificates.  
The measures for tax registration by taxpayers of individual income tax shall be separately prescribed by the State Council.  
The format of tax registration certificates shall be prescribed by the State Administration of Taxation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　扣缴义务人应当自扣缴义务发生之日起30日内，向所在地的主管税务机关申报办理扣缴税款登记，领取扣缴税款登记证件；税务机关对已办理税务登记的扣缴义务人，可以只在其税务登记证件上登记扣缴税款事项，不再发给扣缴税款登记证件。

**Article 13** Withholding agents shall, within 30 days from the date the withholding obligation arises, apply to the competent tax authority at their locality for withholding tax registration and obtain the withholding tax registration certificate; the tax authority may, for withholding agents that have already completed tax registration, only record the withholding tax matters on their tax registration certificates and no longer issue withholding tax registration certificates.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　纳税人税务登记内容发生变化的，应当自工商行政管理机关或者其他机关办理变更登记之日起30日内，持有关证件向原税务登记机关申报办理变更税务登记。  
纳税人税务登记内容发生变化，不需要到工商行政管理机关或者其他机关办理变更登记的，应当自发生变化之日起30日内，持有关证件向原税务登记机关申报办理变更税务登记。

**Article 14** Where the contents of a taxpayer's tax registration change, the taxpayer shall, within 30 days from the date of handling the modification registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce or other authorities, apply to the original tax registration authority for modification of tax registration with relevant certificates.  
Where the contents of a taxpayer's tax registration change but there is no need to handle modification registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce or other authorities, the taxpayer shall, within 30 days from the date of such change, apply to the original tax registration authority for modification of tax registration with relevant certificates.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　纳税人发生解散、破产、撤销以及其他情形，依法终止纳税义务的，应当在向工商行政管理机关或者其他机关办理注销登记前，持有关证件向原税务登记机关申报办理注销税务登记；按照规定不需要在工商行政管理机关或者其他机关办理注册登记的，应当自有关机关批准或者宣告终止之日起15日内，持有关证件向原税务登记机关申报办理注销税务登记。  
纳税人因住所、经营地点变动，涉及改变税务登记机关的，应当在向工商行政管理机关或者其他机关申请办理变更或者注销登记前或者住所、经营地点变动前，向原税务登记机关申报办理注销税务登记，并在30日内向迁达地税务机关申报办理税务登记。  
纳税人被工商行政管理机关吊销营业执照或者被其他机关予以撤销登记的，应当自营业执照被吊销或者被撤销登记之日起15日内，向原税务登记机关申报办理注销税务登记。

**Article 15** Where a taxpayer is dissolved, bankrupt, cancelled, or under other circumstances, and lawfully terminates its tax obligation, it shall, before handling cancellation registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce or other authorities, apply to the original tax registration authority for cancellation of tax registration with relevant certificates; where there is no need to handle registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce or other authorities according to regulations, it shall, within 15 days from the date of approval or declaration of termination by the relevant authority, apply to the original tax registration authority for cancellation of tax registration with relevant certificates.  
Where a taxpayer changes its domicile or place of business operation, involving a change of tax registration authority, it shall, before applying to the administrative department for industry and commerce or other authorities for modification or cancellation registration, or before the change of domicile or place of business operation, apply to the original tax registration authority for cancellation of tax registration, and within 30 days apply to the tax registration authority at the new location for tax registration.  
Where a taxpayer's business license is revoked by the administrative department for industry and commerce, or its registration is cancelled by other authorities, it shall, within 15 days from the date of revocation of the business license or cancellation of registration, apply to the original tax registration authority for cancellation of tax registration.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　纳税人在办理注销税务登记前，应当向税务机关结清应纳税款、滞纳金、罚款，缴销发票、税务登记证件和其他税务证件。

**Article 16** Before handling cancellation of tax registration, a taxpayer shall settle all taxes payable, overdue fines, and penalties with the tax authority, and hand over invoices, tax registration certificates, and other tax documents for cancellation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　从事生产、经营的纳税人应当自开立基本存款账户或者其他存款账户之日起15日内，向主管税务机关书面报告其全部账号；发生变化的，应当自变化之日起15日内，向主管税务机关书面报告。

**Article 17** Taxpayers engaged in production or business operations shall, within 15 days from the date of opening a basic deposit account or other deposit accounts, report in writing all their account numbers to the competent tax authority; if any change occurs, they shall report in writing to the competent tax authority within 15 days from the date of such change.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　除按照规定不需要发给税务登记证件的外，纳税人办理下列事项时，必须持税务登记证件：  
(一)开立银行账户；  
(二)申请减税、免税、退税；  
(三)申请办理延期申报、延期缴纳税款；  
(四)领购发票；  
(五)申请开具外出经营活动税收管理证明；  
(六)办理停业、歇业；  
(七)其他有关税务事项。

**Article 18** Except where tax registration certificates are not required to be issued according to regulations, a taxpayer must hold the tax registration certificate when handling the following matters:  
(1) opening a bank account;  
(2) applying for tax reduction, exemption, or refund;  
(3) applying for deferred declaration or deferred payment of tax;  
(4) purchasing invoices;  
(5) applying for issuance of a tax administration certificate for out-of-business activities;  
(6) handling suspension or cessation of business;  
(7) other tax-related matters.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　税务机关对税务登记证件实行定期验证和换证制度。纳税人应当在规定的期限内持有关证件到主管税务机关办理验证或者换证手续。

**Article 19** The tax authority shall implement a system of periodic verification and renewal of tax registration certificates. Taxpayers shall, within the prescribed time limit, go to the competent tax authority with relevant certificates to go through the formalities of verification or renewal.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　纳税人应当将税务登记证件正本在其生产、经营场所或者办公场所公开悬挂，接受税务机关检查。  
纳税人遗失税务登记证件的，应当在15日内书面报告主管税务机关，并登报声明作废。

**Article 20** A taxpayer shall hang the original of the tax registration certificate publicly at its production, business, or office premises, subject to inspection by the tax authority.  
If a taxpayer loses its tax registration certificate, it shall, within 15 days, report in writing to the competent tax authority and make a public announcement of invalidation in a newspaper.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　从事生产、经营的纳税人到外县(市)临时从事生产、经营活动的，应当持税务登记证副本和所在地税务机关填开的外出经营活动税收管理证明，向营业地税务机关报验登记，接受税务管理。  
从事生产、经营的纳税人外出经营，在同一地累计超过180天的，应当在营业地办理税务登记手续。

**Article 21** A taxpayer engaged in production or business operations who temporarily conducts production or business operations in another county (city) shall present a duplicate of the tax registration certificate and the tax administration certificate for out-of-county business activities issued by the tax authority of the locality where it is headquartered to the tax authority of the place of business for registration and inspection, and shall accept tax administration.  
Where a taxpayer engaged in production or business operations conducts business operations outside its registered locality and, in the same place, the accumulated period exceeds 180 days, it shall undergo tax registration formalities at the place of business.

### 第三章　账簿、凭证管理

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　从事生产、经营的纳税人应当自领取营业执照或者发生纳税义务之日起15日内，按照国家有关规定设置账簿。  
前款所称账簿，是指总账、明细账、日记账以及其他辅助性账簿。总账、日记账应当采用订本式。

**Article 22** A taxpayer engaged in production or business operations shall, within 15 days from the date of obtaining the business license or the occurrence of the tax liability, set up account books in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The term "account books" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph refers to general ledgers, subsidiary ledgers, journals and other auxiliary account books. The general ledger and journal shall be bound volumes.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　生产、经营规模小又确无建账能力的纳税人，可以聘请经批准从事会计代理记账业务的专业机构或者财会人员代为建账和办理账务。

**Article 23** A taxpayer with a small scale of production or business operations who indeed does not have the ability to set up account books may engage a professional institution or accounting personnel approved to engage in agency bookkeeping business to set up account books and handle accounting matters on its behalf.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　从事生产、经营的纳税人应当自领取税务登记证件之日起15日内，将其财务、会计制度或者财务、会计处理办法报送主管税务机关备案。  
纳税人使用计算机记账的，应当在使用前将会计电算化系统的会计核算软件、使用说明书及有关资料报送主管税务机关备案。  
纳税人建立的会计电算化系统应当符合国家有关规定，并能正确、完整核算其收入或者所得。

**Article 24** A taxpayer engaged in production or business operations shall, within 15 days from the date of obtaining the tax registration certificate, submit its financial and accounting systems or its financial and accounting treatment methods to the competent tax authority for archival filing.  
Where a taxpayer uses a computer for bookkeeping, it shall, before using the computer, submit the accounting software of the computerized accounting system, user manual, and relevant materials to the competent tax authority for archival filing.  
The computerized accounting system established by the taxpayer shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State and be capable of correctly and completely accounting for its income or gains.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　扣缴义务人应当自税收法律、行政法规规定的扣缴义务发生之日起10日内，按照所代扣、代收的税种，分别设置代扣代缴、代收代缴税款账簿。

**Article 25** A withholding agent shall, within 10 days from the date of occurrence of the withholding obligation as prescribed by tax laws and administrative regulations, set up account books for taxes withheld and collected separately according to the types of taxes withheld and collected.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　纳税人、扣缴义务人会计制度健全，能够通过计算机正确、完整计算其收入和所得或者代扣代缴、代收代缴税款情况的，其计算机输出的完整的书面会计记录，可视同会计账簿。  
纳税人、扣缴义务人会计制度不健全，不能通过计算机正确、完整计算其收入和所得或者代扣代缴、代收代缴税款情况的，应当建立总账及与纳税或者代扣代缴、代收代缴税款有关的其他账簿。

**Article 26** Where a taxpayer or withholding agent has a sound accounting system and is able to correctly and completely calculate its income and gains or the taxes withheld and collected through a computer, the complete written accounting records output by the computer may be regarded as account books.  
Where a taxpayer or withholding agent does not have a sound accounting system and is unable to correctly and completely calculate its income and gains or the taxes withheld and collected through a computer, it shall establish a general ledger and other account books related to tax payment or to taxes withheld and collected.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　账簿、会计凭证和报表，应当使用中文。民族自治地方可以同时使用当地通用的一种民族文字。外商投资企业和外国企业可以同时使用一种外国文字。

**Article 27** Account books, accounting vouchers and statements shall be in the Chinese language. In national autonomous areas, a local commonly used national language may be used simultaneously. Enterprises with foreign investment and foreign enterprises may use a foreign language simultaneously.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　纳税人应当按照税务机关的要求安装、使用税控装置，并按照税务机关的规定报送有关数据和资料。  
税控装置推广应用的管理办法由国家税务总局另行制定，报国务院批准后实施。

**Article 28** A taxpayer shall install and use tax control devices as required by the tax authority, and shall submit relevant data and materials in accordance with the provisions of the tax authority.  
The administrative measures for the promotion and application of tax control devices shall be separately formulated by the State Administration of Taxation and implemented upon approval by the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　账簿、记账凭证、报表、完税凭证、发票、出口凭证以及其他有关涉税资料应当合法、真实、完整。  
账簿、记账凭证、报表、完税凭证、发票、出口凭证以及其他有关涉税资料应当保存10年；但是，法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 29** Account books, accounting vouchers, statements, tax payment receipts, invoices, export vouchers, and other relevant tax-related materials shall be lawful, authentic and complete.  
Account books, accounting vouchers, statements, tax payment receipts, invoices, export vouchers, and other relevant tax-related materials shall be kept for 10 years, except where otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.

### 第四章　纳税申报

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　税务机关应当建立、健全纳税人自行申报纳税制度。纳税人、扣缴义务人可以采取邮寄、数据电文方式办理纳税申报或者报送代扣代缴、代收代缴税款报告表。  
数据电文方式，是指税务机关确定的电话语音、电子数据交换和网络传输等电子方式。

**Article 30** The tax authority shall establish and improve the system of taxpayers' self-declaration of tax payments. A taxpayer or withholding agent may adopt the methods of mail or electronic data messages to handle tax declaration or submit the reporting forms for taxes withheld and collected.  
The method of electronic data messages refers to electronic methods such as telephone voice, electronic data interchange, and network transmission as determined by the tax authority.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　纳税人采取邮寄方式办理纳税申报的，应当使用统一的纳税申报专用信封，并以邮政部门收据作为申报凭据。邮寄申报以寄出的邮戳日期为实际申报日期。  
纳税人采取电子方式办理纳税申报的，应当按照税务机关规定的期限和要求保存有关资料，并定期书面报送主管税务机关。

**Article 31** Where a taxpayer handles tax declaration by post, it shall use a uniform special envelope for tax declaration and shall use the receipt from the postal department as the evidence of declaration. The postmark date of mailing shall be the actual declaration date.  
Where a taxpayer handles tax declaration electronically, it shall preserve relevant documents within the time limit and in accordance with the requirements prescribed by the tax authorities, and shall periodically submit them in writing to the competent tax authorities.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　纳税人在纳税期内没有应纳税款的，也应当按照规定办理纳税申报。  
纳税人享受减税、免税待遇的，在减税、免税期间应当按照规定办理纳税申报。

**Article 32** Where a taxpayer has no tax payable during the tax period, it shall nevertheless handle tax declaration in accordance with the provisions.  
Where a taxpayer enjoys the treatment of tax reduction or exemption, it shall, during the period of tax reduction or exemption, handle tax declaration in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　纳税人、扣缴义务人的纳税申报或者代扣代缴、代收代缴税款报告表的主要内容包括：税种、税目，应纳税项目或者应代扣代缴、代收代缴税款项目，计税依据，扣除项目及标准，适用税率或者单位税额，应退税项目及税额、应减免税项目及税额，应纳税额或者应代扣代缴、代收代缴税额，税款所属期限、延期缴纳税款、欠税、滞纳金等。

**Article 33** The main contents of the tax declaration form or the tax withholding and collection report form of a taxpayer or withholding agent shall include: tax categories and tax items; taxable items or items subject to tax withholding and collection; tax calculation basis; deduction items and standards; applicable tax rates or unit tax amounts; items and amounts eligible for tax refund; items and amounts eligible for tax reduction or exemption; tax payable or tax to be withheld and collected; tax period; deferred tax payment; tax arrears; and late payment surcharges, etc.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　纳税人办理纳税申报时，应当如实填写纳税申报表，并根据不同的情况相应报送下列有关证件、资料：  
(一)财务会计报表及其说明材料；  
(二)与纳税有关的合同、协议书及凭证；  
(三)税控装置的电子报税资料；  
(四)外出经营活动税收管理证明和异地完税凭证；  
(五)境内或者境外公证机构出具的有关证明文件；  
(六)税务机关规定应当报送的其他有关证件、资料。

**Article 34** When handling tax declaration, a taxpayer shall truthfully fill out the tax declaration form and, according to different circumstances, correspondingly submit the following relevant documents and materials:  
(1) financial accounting statements and explanatory materials;  
(2) contracts, agreements, and vouchers related to taxation;  
(3) electronic tax reporting data from tax control devices;  
(4) tax administration certificates for outbound business activities and tax payment certificates from other localities;  
(5) relevant certification documents issued by domestic or foreign notarization institutions;  
(6) other relevant documents and materials as prescribed by the tax authorities to be submitted.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　扣缴义务人办理代扣代缴、代收代缴税款报告时，应当如实填写代扣代缴、代收代缴税款报告表，并报送代扣代缴、代收代缴税款的合法凭证以及税务机关规定的其他有关证件、资料。

**Article 35** When handling the report of tax withholding and collection, a withholding agent shall truthfully fill out the tax withholding and collection report form and submit the lawful vouchers for tax withholding and collection as well as other relevant documents and materials as prescribed by the tax authorities.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　实行定期定额缴纳税款的纳税人，可以实行简易申报、简并征期等申报纳税方式。

**Article 36** A taxpayer that pays tax on a fixed periodic basis may adopt simplified tax declaration and combined tax period methods.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　纳税人、扣缴义务人按照规定的期限办理纳税申报或者报送代扣代缴、代收代缴税款报告表确有困难，需要延期的，应当在规定的期限内向税务机关提出书面延期申请，经税务机关核准，在核准的期限内办理。  
纳税人、扣缴义务人因不可抗力，不能按期办理纳税申报或者报送代扣代缴、代收代缴税款报告表的，可以延期办理；但是，应当在不可抗力情形消除后立即向税务机关报告。税务机关应当查明事实，予以核准。

**Article 37** Where a taxpayer or withholding agent genuinely has difficulty in handling tax declaration or submitting the tax withholding and collection report form within the prescribed time limit and needs an extension, it shall submit a written application for extension to the tax authorities within the prescribed time limit, and shall handle the matter within the extended time limit approved by the tax authorities.  
Where a taxpayer or withholding agent is unable to handle tax declaration or submit the tax withholding and collection report form on time due to force majeure, it may handle it with an extension; provided that it shall immediately report to the tax authorities after the force majeure situation is eliminated. The tax authorities shall ascertain the facts and grant approval.

### 第五章　税款征收

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　税务机关应当加强对税款征收的管理，建立、健全责任制度。  
税务机关根据保证国家税款及时足额入库、方便纳税人、降低税收成本的原则，确定税款征收的方式。  
税务机关应当加强对纳税人出口退税的管理，具体管理办法由国家税务总局会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 38** Tax authorities shall strengthen the management of tax collection and establish and improve responsibility systems.  
Tax authorities shall, based on the principles of ensuring timely and full collection of state taxes, facilitating taxpayers, and reducing tax collection costs, determine the methods of tax collection.  
Tax authorities shall strengthen the management of export tax refunds for taxpayers, and the specific administrative measures shall be formulated by the State Administration of Taxation jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　税务机关应当将各种税收的税款、滞纳金、罚款，按照国家规定的预算科目和预算级次及时缴入国库，税务机关不得占压、挪用、截留，不得缴入国库以外或者国家规定的税款账户以外的任何账户。  
已缴入国库的税款、滞纳金、罚款，任何单位和个人不得擅自变更预算科目和预算级次。

**Article 39** Tax authorities shall timely remit the tax payments, late payment surcharges, and fines of various taxes into the state treasury in accordance with the budget categories and budget levels prescribed by the state. Tax authorities shall not retain, misappropriate, or withhold them, nor shall they remit them into any account other than the state treasury or the tax accounts prescribed by the state.  
No entity or individual shall alter the budget categories or budget levels of tax payments, late payment surcharges, and fines that have been remitted into the state treasury without authorization.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　税务机关应当根据方便、快捷、安全的原则，积极推广使用支票、银行卡、电子结算方式缴纳税款。

**Article 40** Tax authorities shall, based on the principles of convenience, speed, and safety, actively promote the use of checks, bank cards, and electronic settlement methods for tax payment.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　纳税人有下列情形之一的，属于税收征管法第三十一条所称特殊困难：  
(一)因不可抗力，导致纳税人发生较大损失，正常生产经营活动受到较大影响的；  
(二)当期货币资金在扣除应付职工工资、社会保险费后，不足以缴纳税款的。  
计划单列市国家税务局、地方税务局可以参照税收征管法第三十一条第二款的批准权限，审批纳税人延期缴纳税款。

**Article 41** Where a taxpayer falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall constitute the special difficulties referred to in Article 31 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection:  
(1) Where force majeure causes the taxpayer to suffer relatively large losses and materially affects its normal production and business operations; or  
(2) Where the current monetary funds, after deducting the payable职工的 wages and social insurance premiums, are insufficient to pay the taxes.  
The State Tax Bureau and Local Tax Bureau of a city with separate planning status may, by reference to the approval authority under the second paragraph of Article 31 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, examine and approve the taxpayer's application for deferring tax payment.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　纳税人需要延期缴纳税款的，应当在缴纳税款期限届满前提出申请，并报送下列材料：申请延期缴纳税款报告，当期货币资金余额情况及所有银行存款账户的对账单，资产负债表，应付职工工资和社会保险费等税务机关要求提供的支出预算。  
税务机关应当自收到申请延期缴纳税款报告之日起20日内作出批准或者不予批准的决定；不予批准的，从缴纳税款期限届满之日起加收滞纳金。

**Article 42** Where a taxpayer needs to defer tax payment, it shall submit an application before the expiry of the tax payment deadline and submit the following materials: a report on the application for deferring tax payment, the current monetary fund balance and bank statements for all bank deposit accounts, a balance sheet, and an expenditure budget for payable wages of employees and social insurance premiums, which the tax authority requires to be provided.  
The tax authority shall, within 20 days from the date of receiving the report on the application for deferring tax payment, make a decision to approve or disapprove; if the application is disapproved, a surcharge for overdue tax payment shall be imposed from the date of expiry of the tax payment deadline.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　享受减税、免税优惠的纳税人，减税、免税期满，应当自期满次日起恢复纳税；减税、免税条件发生变化的，应当在纳税申报时向税务机关报告；不再符合减税、免税条件的，应当依法履行纳税义务；未依法纳税的，税务机关应当予以追缴。

**Article 43** For a taxpayer that enjoys tax reduction or exemption preferences, upon the expiry of the tax reduction or exemption period, it shall resume tax payment from the day following the expiry date; if the conditions for tax reduction or exemption change, it shall report to the tax authority at the time of tax declaration; if it no longer meets the conditions for tax reduction or exemption, it shall perform its tax payment obligation according to law; if it fails to pay taxes according to law, the tax authority shall pursue the collection.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　税务机关根据有利于税收控管和方便纳税的原则，可以按照国家有关规定委托有关单位和人员代征零星分散和异地缴纳的税收，并发给委托代征证书。受托单位和人员按照代征证书的要求，以税务机关的名义依法征收税款，纳税人不得拒绝；纳税人拒绝的，受托代征单位和人员应当及时报告税务机关。

**Article 44** In accordance with the principles of facilitating tax control and taxpayer convenience, the tax authority may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, entrust relevant entities and individuals to collect on a commission basis the taxes that are scattered and paid in different localities, and shall issue a certificate of entrustment for commission collection. The entrusted entities and individuals shall collect taxes in the name of the tax authority according to law in accordance with the requirements of the commission collection certificate, and taxpayers shall not refuse; if a taxpayer refuses, the entrusted entity or individual shall promptly report to the tax authority.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　税收征管法第三十四条所称完税凭证，是指各种完税证、缴款书、印花税票、扣(收)税凭证以及其他完税证明。  
未经税务机关指定，任何单位、个人不得印制完税凭证。完税凭证不得转借、倒卖、变造或者伪造。  
完税凭证的式样及管理办法由国家税务总局制定。

**Article 45** The tax payment receipts referred to in Article 34 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection mean various tax payment certificates, payment books, stamp tax tickets, tax withholding (collection) certificates, and other tax payment proofs.  
Without being designated by the tax authority, no entity or individual may print tax payment receipts. Tax payment receipts shall not be lent, resold, altered, or forged.  
The format and administrative measures for tax payment receipts shall be formulated by the State Administration of Taxation.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　税务机关收到税款后，应当向纳税人开具完税凭证。纳税人通过银行缴纳税款的，税务机关可以委托银行开具完税凭证。

**Article 46** After receiving the tax payment, the tax authority shall issue a tax payment receipt to the taxpayer. Where a taxpayer pays taxes through a bank, the tax authority may entrust the bank to issue the tax payment receipt.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　纳税人有税收征管法第三十五条或者第三十七条所列情形之一的，税务机关有权采用下列任何一种方法核定其应纳税额：  
(一)参照当地同类行业或者类似行业中经营规模和收入水平相近的纳税人的税负水平核定；  
(二)按照营业收入或者成本加合理的费用和利润的方法核定；  
(三)按照耗用的原材料、燃料、动力等推算或者测算核定；  
(四)按照其他合理方法核定。  
采用前款所列一种方法不足以正确核定应纳税额时，可以同时采用两种以上的方法核定。  
纳税人对税务机关采取本条规定的方法核定的应纳税额有异议的，应当提供相关证据，经税务机关认定后，调整应纳税额。

**Article 47** Where a taxpayer falls under any of the circumstances specified in Article 35 or Article 37 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, the tax authority has the right to assess the amount of tax payable by using any of the following methods:  
(1) Assess by reference to the tax burden level of taxpayers with similar business scale and income level in the same or similar industry in the local area;  
(2) Assess based on the business revenue or the cost plus reasonable expenses and profit method;  
(3) Assess by extrapolating or calculating based on the consumption of raw materials, fuel, power, etc.;  
(4) Assess by other reasonable methods.  
Where one of the methods listed in the preceding paragraph is insufficient to correctly assess the amount of tax payable, two or more methods may be used concurrently for assessment.  
If a taxpayer disagrees with the amount of tax payable assessed by the tax authority using the methods specified in this Article, it shall provide relevant evidence, and the amount of tax payable shall be adjusted after confirmation by the tax authority.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　税务机关负责纳税人纳税信誉等级评定工作。纳税人纳税信誉等级的评定办法由国家税务总局制定。

**Article 48** The tax authority is responsible for the assessment of taxpayers' tax credit ratings. The measures for assessing taxpayers' tax credit ratings shall be formulated by the State Administration of Taxation.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　承包人或者承租人有独立的生产经营权，在财务上独立核算，并定期向发包人或者出租人上缴承包费或者租金的，承包人或者承租人应当就其生产、经营收入和所得纳税，并接受税务管理；但是，法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。  
发包人或者出租人应当自发包或者出租之日起30日内将承包人或者承租人的有关情况向主管税务机关报告。发包人或者出租人不报告的，发包人或者出租人与承包人或者承租人承担纳税连带责任。

**Article 49** Where a contractor or lessee has independent production and business operation rights, conducts independent financial accounting, and regularly pays contract fees or rent to the contract-issuing party or lessor, the contractor or lessee shall pay taxes on its production and business operation income and earnings, and shall be subject to tax administration; except where otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations.  
The contract-issuing party or lessor shall, within 30 days from the date of issuing the contract or leasing, report the relevant information of the contractor or lessee to the competent tax authority. If the contract-issuing party or lessor fails to report, the contract-issuing party or lessor shall bear joint and several liability for tax payment with the contractor or lessee.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　纳税人有解散、撤销、破产情形的，在清算前应当向其主管税务机关报告；未结清税款的，由其主管税务机关参加清算。

**Article 50** Where a taxpayer is subject to dissolution, cancellation, or bankruptcy, it shall report to its competent tax authority before liquidation; where taxes are not settled, the competent tax authority shall participate in the liquidation.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　税收征管法第三十六条所称关联企业，是指有下列关系之一的公司、企业和其他经济组织：  
(一)在资金、经营、购销等方面，存在直接或者间接的拥有或者控制关系；  
(二)直接或者间接地同为第三者所拥有或者控制；  
(三)在利益上具有相关联的其他关系。  
纳税人有义务就其与关联企业之间的业务往来，向当地税务机关提供有关的价格、费用标准等资料。具体办法由国家税务总局制定。

**Article 51** "Associated enterprises" as mentioned in Article 36 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection means companies, enterprises and other economic organizations that have one of the following relationships:  
(1) They have direct or indirect ownership or control relationships in terms of funds, operation, purchase and sales, etc.;  
(2) They are directly or indirectly owned or controlled by the same third party;  
(3) They have other relationships that are associated in terms of interests.  
A taxpayer shall have the obligation to provide the local tax authorities with relevant materials on prices, fee standards, etc., concerning business transactions between the taxpayer and its associated enterprises. Specific measures shall be formulated by the State Administration of Taxation.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　税收征管法第三十六条所称独立企业之间的业务往来，是指没有关联关系的企业之间按照公平成交价格和营业常规所进行的业务往来。

**Article 52** "Business transactions between independent enterprises" as mentioned in Article 36 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection means business transactions carried out between enterprises that have no associated relationships, in accordance with arm's length prices and normal business practices.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　纳税人可以向主管税务机关提出与其关联企业之间业务往来的定价原则和计算方法，主管税务机关审核、批准后，与纳税人预先约定有关定价事项，监督纳税人执行。

**Article 53** A taxpayer may propose to the competent tax authorities the pricing principles and calculation methods for business transactions between the taxpayer and its associated enterprises. After review and approval by the competent tax authorities, the taxpayer shall agree in advance on relevant pricing matters, and the tax authorities shall supervise the taxpayer's implementation.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　纳税人与其关联企业之间的业务往来有下列情形之一的，税务机关可以调整其应纳税额：  
(一)购销业务未按照独立企业之间的业务往来作价；  
(二)融通资金所支付或者收取的利息超过或者低于没有关联关系的企业之间所能同意的数额，或者利率超过或者低于同类业务的正常利率；  
(三)提供劳务，未按照独立企业之间业务往来收取或者支付劳务费用；  
(四)转让财产、提供财产使用权等业务往来，未按照独立企业之间业务往来作价或者收取、支付费用；  
(五)未按照独立企业之间业务往来作价的其他情形。

**Article 54** Where any of the following circumstances exists in business transactions between a taxpayer and its associated enterprises, the tax authorities may adjust the taxpayer's taxable amount:  
(1) The pricing of purchase and sales transactions is not based on arm's length prices between independent enterprises;  
(2) The interest paid or received for financing exceeds or is lower than the amount that would be agreed upon between enterprises without associated relationships, or the interest rate exceeds or is lower than the normal interest rate for similar transactions;  
(3) Services are provided without charging or paying service fees based on arm's length transactions between independent enterprises;  
(4) The transfer of property, provision of property use rights, or other transactions are not priced or charged/payed on an arm's length basis between independent enterprises;  
(5) Other circumstances where pricing is not based on arm's length transactions between independent enterprises.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　纳税人有本细则第五十四条所列情形之一的，税务机关可以按照下列方法调整计税收入额或者所得额：  
(一)按照独立企业之间进行的相同或者类似业务活动的价格；  
(二)按照再销售给无关联关系的第三者的价格所应取得的收入和利润水平；  
(三)按照成本加合理的费用和利润；  
(四)按照其他合理的方法。

**Article 55** Where a taxpayer falls under any of the circumstances listed in Article 54 of these Rules, the tax authorities may adjust the taxable revenue or income amount by the following methods:  
(1) At the price of the same or similar business activities between independent enterprises;  
(2) Based on the income and profit level that would be obtained from resale to an unrelated third party at a certain price;  
(3) Based on cost plus reasonable expenses and profit;  
(4) By other reasonable methods.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　纳税人与其关联企业未按照独立企业之间的业务往来支付价款、费用的，税务机关自该业务往来发生的纳税年度起3年内进行调整；有特殊情况的，可以自该业务往来发生的纳税年度起10年内进行调整。

**Article 56** Where a taxpayer and its associated enterprises fail to pay prices or fees in accordance with arm's length transactions between independent enterprises, the tax authorities shall make adjustments within 3 years from the tax year in which such transaction occurred; in special circumstances, the tax authorities may make adjustments within 10 years from the tax year in which such transaction occurred.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　税收征管法第三十七条所称未按照规定办理税务登记从事生产、经营的纳税人，包括到外县(市)从事生产、经营而未向营业地税务机关报验登记的纳税人。

**Article 57** "Taxpayers who engage in production or business operations without undergoing tax registration in accordance with the provisions" as mentioned in Article 37 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection includes taxpayers who go to other counties (cities) to engage in production or business operations but fail to report to the local tax authorities in the place of business for registration.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　税务机关依照税收征管法第三十七条的规定，扣押纳税人商品、货物的，纳税人应当自扣押之日起15日内缴纳税款。  
对扣押的鲜活、易腐烂变质或者易失效的商品、货物，税务机关根据被扣押物品的保质期，可以缩短前款规定的扣押期限。

**Article 58** Where the tax authorities, in accordance with Article 37 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, seize the taxpayer's goods and commodities, the taxpayer shall pay the tax within 15 days from the date of seizure.  
For seized fresh, perishable or easily deteriorated goods and commodities, the tax authorities may, according to the shelf life of the seized items, shorten the seizure period as specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　税收征管法第三十八条、第四十条所称其他财产，包括纳税人的房地产、现金、有价证券等不动产和动产。  
机动车辆、金银饰品、古玩字画、豪华住宅或者一处以外的住房不属于税收征管法第三十八条、第四十条、第四十二条所称个人及其所扶养家属维持生活必需的住房和用品。  
税务机关对单价5000元以下的其他生活用品，不采取税收保全措施和强制执行措施。

**Article 59** "Other property" as mentioned in Articles 38 and 40 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection includes the taxpayer's real estate, cash, securities and other immovable and movable property.  
Motor vehicles, gold and silver ornaments, antiques and calligraphy/paintings, luxury residences, or housing other than one residential property shall not be considered as housing and articles necessary for the daily life of the individual and his/her dependents as mentioned in Articles 38, 40 and 42 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection.  
The tax authorities shall not take tax preservation measures or compulsory enforcement measures against other daily necessities with a unit price below 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　税收征管法第三十八条、第四十条、第四十二条所称个人所扶养家属，是指与纳税人共同居住生活的配偶、直系亲属以及无生活来源并由纳税人扶养的其他亲属。

**Article 60** "Dependents of an individual" as mentioned in Articles 38, 40 and 42 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection means the spouse, lineal relatives who live together with the taxpayer, and other relatives who have no source of income and are supported by the taxpayer.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　税收征管法第三十八条、第八十八条所称担保，包括经税务机关认可的纳税保证人为纳税人提供的纳税保证，以及纳税人或者第三人以其未设置或者未全部设置担保物权的财产提供的担保。  
纳税保证人，是指在中国境内具有纳税担保能力的自然人、法人或者其他经济组织。  
法律、行政法规规定的没有担保资格的单位和个人，不得作为纳税担保人。

**Article 61** The guarantees referred to in Articles 38 and 88 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection include the tax payment guarantee provided by a tax payment guarantor recognized by the tax authority for the taxpayer, and the guarantee provided by the taxpayer or a third party with property on which no security interest or not all security interests have been created.  
A tax payment guarantor means a natural person, legal person or other economic organization within the territory of China that has the capacity to provide a tax payment guarantee.  
Units and individuals that are not qualified to provide guarantees as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations shall not act as tax payment guarantors.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　纳税担保人同意为纳税人提供纳税担保的，应当填写纳税担保书，写明担保对象、担保范围、担保期限和担保责任以及其他有关事项。担保书须经纳税人、纳税担保人签字盖章并经税务机关同意，方为有效。  
纳税人或者第三人以其财产提供纳税担保的，应当填写财产清单，并写明财产价值以及其他有关事项。纳税担保财产清单须经纳税人、第三人签字盖章并经税务机关确认，方为有效。

**Article 62** If a tax payment guarantor agrees to provide a tax payment guarantee for the taxpayer, it shall fill in a tax payment guarantee deed specifying the guarantee object, the scope of guarantee, the guarantee period, the guarantee liability and other relevant matters. The guarantee deed shall become valid only after being signed and sealed by the taxpayer and the tax payment guarantor and approved by the tax authority.  
If a taxpayer or a third party provides a tax payment guarantee with its property, it shall fill in a property list specifying the value of the property and other relevant matters. The property list for tax payment guarantee shall become valid only after being signed and sealed by the taxpayer and the third party and confirmed by the tax authority.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　税务机关执行扣押、查封商品、货物或者其他财产时，应当由两名以上税务人员执行，并通知被执行人。被执行人是自然人的，应当通知被执行人本人或者其成年家属到场；被执行人是法人或者其他组织的，应当通知其法定代表人或者主要负责人到场；拒不到场的，不影响执行。

**Article 63** When the tax authority carries out seizure or sealing up of commodities, goods or other property, it shall be executed by two or more tax officials and the person subjected to enforcement shall be notified. If the person subjected to enforcement is a natural person, the person himself or his adult family member shall be notified to be present; if the person subjected to enforcement is a legal person or other organization, its legal representative or principal person in charge shall be notified to be present. If they refuse to be present, the enforcement shall not be affected.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　税务机关执行税收征管法第三十七条、第三十八条、第四十条的规定，扣押、查封价值相当于应纳税款的商品、货物或者其他财产时，参照同类商品的市场价、出厂价或者评估价估算。  
税务机关按照前款方法确定应扣押、查封的商品、货物或者其他财产的价值时，还应当包括滞纳金和拍卖、变卖所发生的费用。

**Article 64** When the tax authority, in executing the provisions of Articles 37, 38 and 40 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, seizes or seals up commodities, goods or other property with a value equivalent to the tax payable, it shall estimate the value by reference to the market price, ex-factory price or assessed price of similar commodities.  
When the tax authority determines the value of the commodities, goods or other property to be seized or sealed up in accordance with the method set forth in the preceding paragraph, it shall also include the surcharge for overdue payment and the expenses incurred in auction or sale.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　对价值超过应纳税额且不可分割的商品、货物或者其他财产，税务机关在纳税人、扣缴义务人或者纳税担保人无其他可供强制执行的财产的情况下，可以整体扣押、查封、拍卖。

**Article 65** With respect to commodities, goods or other property whose value exceeds the amount of tax payable and which are indivisible, the tax authority may seize, seal up or auction them as a whole if the taxpayer, withholding agent or tax payment guarantor has no other property available for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　税务机关执行税收征管法第三十七条、第三十八条、第四十条的规定，实施扣押、查封时，对有产权证件的动产或者不动产，税务机关可以责令当事人将产权证件交税务机关保管，同时可以向有关机关发出协助执行通知书，有关机关在扣押、查封期间不再办理该动产或者不动产的过户手续。

**Article 66** When the tax authority, in executing the provisions of Articles 37, 38 and 40 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, carries out seizure or sealing up, with respect to movable or immovable property that has title documents, the tax authority may order the party to deliver the title documents to the tax authority for custody, and may also issue a notice of assistance in enforcement to the relevant authority, which shall not handle the transfer of ownership of such movable or immovable property during the period of seizure or sealing up.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　对查封的商品、货物或者其他财产，税务机关可以指令被执行人负责保管，保管责任由被执行人承担。  
继续使用被查封的财产不会减少其价值的，税务机关可以允许被执行人继续使用；因被执行人保管或者使用的过错造成的损失，由被执行人承担。

**Article 67** With respect to the sealed-up commodities, goods or other property, the tax authority may instruct the person subjected to enforcement to take custody thereof, and the custody liability shall be borne by the person subjected to enforcement.  
If continued use of the sealed-up property will not reduce its value, the tax authority may permit the person subjected to enforcement to continue using it; any loss caused by the fault of the person subjected to enforcement in custody or use shall be borne by the person subjected to enforcement.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　纳税人在税务机关采取税收保全措施后，按照税务机关规定的期限缴纳税款的，税务机关应当自收到税款或者银行转回的完税凭证之日起1日内解除税收保全。

**Article 68** If, after the tax authority has taken tax preservation measures, the taxpayer pays the tax within the time limit prescribed by the tax authority, the tax authority shall release the tax preservation within one day from the date of receiving the tax payment or the tax payment certificate returned by the bank.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　税务机关将扣押、查封的商品、货物或者其他财产变价抵缴税款时，应当交由依法成立的拍卖机构拍卖；无法委托拍卖或者不适于拍卖的，可以交由当地商业企业代为销售，也可以责令纳税人限期处理；无法委托商业企业销售，纳税人也无法处理的，可以由税务机关变价处理，具体办法由国家税务总局规定。国家禁止自由买卖的商品，应当交由有关单位按照国家规定的价格收购。  
拍卖或者变卖所得抵缴税款、滞纳金、罚款以及拍卖、变卖等费用后，剩余部分应当在3日内退还被执行人。

**Article 69** When the tax authority converts the seized or sealed-up commodities, goods or other property into money to offset the tax, it shall have them auctioned by an auction institution established according to law; if it is impossible to entrust an auction or the property is not suitable for auction, it may entrust a local commercial enterprise to sell them on commission, or it may order the taxpayer to dispose of them within a specified time limit; if it is impossible to entrust a commercial enterprise for sale and the taxpayer is also unable to dispose of them, the tax authority may dispose of them and convert them into money, and the specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Administration of Taxation. Commodities whose free trading is prohibited by the State shall be purchased by the relevant units at the price prescribed by the State.  
After the proceeds from auction or sale are used to offset the tax, surcharge for overdue payment, fine and the expenses of auction or sale, the remaining part shall be returned to the person subjected to enforcement within three days.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　税收征管法第三十九条、第四十三条所称损失，是指因税务机关的责任，使纳税人、扣缴义务人或者纳税担保人的合法利益遭受的直接损失。

**Article 70** The losses referred to in Articles 39 and 43 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection mean the direct losses suffered by the legitimate interests of the taxpayer, withholding agent or tax payment guarantor due to the fault of the tax authority.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　税收征管法所称其他金融机构，是指信托投资公司、信用合作社、邮政储蓄机构以及经中国人民银行、中国证券监督管理委员会等批准设立的其他金融机构。

**Article 71** The term "other financial institutions" as used in the Tax Collection and Administration Law shall mean trust and investment companies, credit cooperatives, postal savings institutions, and other financial institutions approved to be established by the People's Bank of China, the China Securities Regulatory Commission, etc.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　税收征管法所称存款，包括独资企业投资人、合伙企业合伙人、个体工商户的储蓄存款以及股东资金账户中的资金等。

**Article 72** The term "deposits" as used in the Tax Collection and Administration Law shall include the savings deposits of investors in sole proprietorship enterprises, partners in partnership enterprises, self-employed industrial and commercial households, as well as funds in shareholders' capital accounts, etc.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　从事生产、经营的纳税人、扣缴义务人未按照规定的期限缴纳或者解缴税款的，纳税担保人未按照规定的期限缴纳所担保的税款的，由税务机关发出限期缴纳税款通知书，责令缴纳或者解缴税款的最长期限不得超过15日。

**Article 73** If a taxpayer or withholding agent engaged in production or business operations fails to pay or remit tax within the prescribed time limit, or if a tax guarantor fails to pay the guaranteed tax within the prescribed time limit, the tax authority shall issue a notice of a time limit for tax payment, ordering the payment or remittance of tax, and the maximum period shall not exceed 15 days.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　欠缴税款的纳税人或者其法定代表人在出境前未按照规定结清应纳税款、滞纳金或者提供纳税担保的，税务机关可以通知出入境管理机关阻止其出境。阻止出境的具体办法，由国家税务总局会同公安部制定。

**Article 74** If a taxpayer who owes tax or his legal representative fails to settle the tax payable, late payment surcharge, or provide a tax guarantee in accordance with the provisions before leaving the country, the tax authority may notify the exit-entry administration authority to prevent him from leaving the country. The specific measures for preventing departure shall be formulated by the State Administration of Taxation jointly with the Ministry of Public Security.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　税收征管法第三十二条规定的加收滞纳金的起止时间，为法律、行政法规规定或者税务机关依照法律、行政法规的规定确定的税款缴纳期限届满次日起至纳税人、扣缴义务人实际缴纳或者解缴税款之日止。

**Article 75** The start and end time for the imposition of late payment surcharge as provided in Article 32 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law shall be from the day following the expiration of the tax payment period as prescribed by laws, administrative regulations, or as determined by the tax authority in accordance with laws and administrative regulations, until the day when the taxpayer or withholding agent actually pays or remits the tax.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　县级以上各级税务机关应当将纳税人的欠税情况，在办税场所或者广播、电视、报纸、期刊、网络等新闻媒体上定期公告。  
对纳税人欠缴税款的情况实行定期公告的办法，由国家税务总局制定。

**Article 76** The tax authorities at or above the county level shall periodically publish the tax arrears of taxpayers in tax handling venues or in news media such as radio, television, newspapers, periodicals, and the Internet.  
The measures for periodically publishing the tax arrears of taxpayers shall be formulated by the State Administration of Taxation.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　税收征管法第四十九条所称欠缴税款数额较大，是指欠缴税款5万元以上。

**Article 77** The term "relatively large amount of tax arrears" as used in Article 49 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law shall mean tax arrears of 50,000 yuan or more.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　税务机关发现纳税人多缴税款的，应当自发现之日起10日内办理退还手续；纳税人发现多缴税款，要求退还的，税务机关应当自接到纳税人退还申请之日起30日内查实并办理退还手续。  
税收征管法第五十一条规定的加算银行同期存款利息的多缴税款退税，不包括依法预缴税款形成的结算退税、出口退税和各种减免退税。  
退税利息按照税务机关办理退税手续当天中国人民银行规定的活期存款利率计算。

**Article 78** If the tax authority discovers that a taxpayer has overpaid tax, it shall go through the refund formalities within 10 days from the date of discovery; if the taxpayer discovers the overpayment and requests a refund, the tax authority shall verify and go through the refund formalities within 30 days from the date of receiving the taxpayer's refund application.  
The refund of overpaid tax with interest calculated based on the bank deposit interest rate for the same period as provided in Article 51 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law shall not include the settlement refund, export tax refund, and various tax reduction and exemption refunds formed from advance tax payments made according to law.  
The refund interest shall be calculated at the demand deposit interest rate prescribed by the People's Bank of China on the day when the tax authority goes through the refund formalities.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　当纳税人既有应退税款又有欠缴税款的，税务机关可以将应退税款和利息先抵扣欠缴税款；抵扣后有余额的，退还纳税人。

**Article 79** If a taxpayer has both tax refundable and tax arrears, the tax authority may first offset the tax arrears with the refundable tax and interest; if there is a balance after the offset, the balance shall be refunded to the taxpayer.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　税收征管法第五十二条所称税务机关的责任，是指税务机关适用税收法律、行政法规不当或者执法行为违法。

**Article 80** The term "liability of the tax authority" as used in Article 52 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law shall mean the improper application of tax laws and administrative regulations by the tax authority or the illegality of its law enforcement acts.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　税收征管法第五十二条所称纳税人、扣缴义务人计算错误等失误，是指非主观故意的计算公式运用错误以及明显的笔误。

**Article 81** The "mistakes such as miscalculation" by a taxpayer or withholding agent as mentioned in Article 52 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection refers to errors in the use of calculation formulas that are not intentional and obvious clerical errors.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　税收征管法第五十二条所称特殊情况，是指纳税人或者扣缴义务人因计算错误等失误，未缴或者少缴、未扣或者少扣、未收或者少收税款，累计数额在10万元以上的。

**Article 82** The "special circumstances" as mentioned in Article 52 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection refers to circumstances where the taxpayer or withholding agent, due to mistakes such as miscalculation, fails to pay or underpays, fails to withhold or under-withholds, or fails to collect or under-collect tax, with the cumulative amount exceeding 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　税收征管法第五十二条规定的补缴和追征税款、滞纳金的期限，自纳税人、扣缴义务人应缴未缴或者少缴税款之日起计算。

**Article 83** The time limit for the supplementary payment and recovery of taxes and surcharges for overdue payment as provided in Article 52 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection shall be calculated from the date on which the taxpayer or withholding agent should have paid but failed to pay or underpaid the tax.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　审计机关、财政机关依法进行审计、检查时，对税务机关的税收违法行为作出的决定，税务机关应当执行；发现被审计、检查单位有税收违法行为的，向被审计、检查单位下达决定、意见书，责成被审计、检查单位向税务机关缴纳应当缴纳的税款、滞纳金。税务机关应当根据有关机关的决定、意见书，依照税收法律、行政法规的规定，将应收的税款、滞纳金按照国家规定的税收征收管理范围和税款入库预算级次缴入国库。  
税务机关应当自收到审计机关、财政机关的决定、意见书之日起30日内将执行情况书面回复审计机关、财政机关。  
有关机关不得将其履行职责过程中发现的税款、滞纳金自行征收入库或者以其他款项的名义自行处理、占压。

**Article 84** Where an audit authority or financial authority, when conducting an audit or inspection according to law, makes a decision on a tax-related illegal act of a tax authority, the tax authority shall implement the decision; if it discovers that the unit under audit or inspection has committed a tax-related illegal act, it shall issue a decision or opinion letter to the unit under audit or inspection, requiring the unit to pay to the tax authority the tax and surcharges for overdue payment that shall be paid. The tax authority shall, based on the decision or opinion letter of the relevant authority and in accordance with the tax laws and administrative regulations, turn over the tax and surcharges for overdue payment that shall be collected to the state treasury in accordance with the state-prescribed scope of tax collection administration and the budgetary level for tax revenue.  
The tax authority shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the decision or opinion letter from the audit authority or financial authority, reply in writing to the audit authority or financial authority regarding the implementation.  
The relevant authority shall not collect and turn over the tax and surcharges for overdue payment discovered during the performance of its duties on its own, or dispose of or retain them under other names.

### 第六章　税务检查

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　税务机关应当建立科学的检查制度，统筹安排检查工作，严格控制对纳税人、扣缴义务人的检查次数。  
税务机关应当制定合理的税务稽查工作规程，负责选案、检查、审理、执行的人员的职责应当明确，并相互分离、相互制约，规范选案程序和检查行为。  
税务检查工作的具体办法，由国家税务总局制定。

**Article 85** The tax authority shall establish a scientific inspection system, make overall arrangements for inspection work, and strictly control the number of inspections on taxpayers and withholding agents.  
The tax authority shall formulate reasonable procedures for tax inspection work, and the responsibilities of personnel responsible for case selection, inspection, adjudication, and enforcement shall be clearly defined, and they shall be separated from and check each other, standardizing case selection procedures and inspection acts.  
The specific measures for tax inspection work shall be formulated by the State Administration of Taxation.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　税务机关行使税收征管法第五十四条第(一)项职权时，可以在纳税人、扣缴义务人的业务场所进行；必要时，经县以上税务局(分局)局长批准，可以将纳税人、扣缴义务人以前会计年度的账簿、记账凭证、报表和其他有关资料调回税务机关检查，但是税务机关必须向纳税人、扣缴义务人开付清单，并在3个月内完整退还；有特殊情况的，经设区的市、自治州以上税务局局长批准，税务机关可以将纳税人、扣缴义务人当年的账簿、记账凭证、报表和其他有关资料调回检查，但是税务机关必须在30日内退还。

**Article 86** When exercising the authority under subparagraph (1) of Article 54 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, the tax authority may conduct the inspection at the business premises of the taxpayer or withholding agent; when necessary, upon approval by the director of the tax bureau (sub-bureau) at or above the county level, the tax authority may bring back to the tax authority for inspection the account books, vouchers, statements, and other relevant materials of the taxpayer or withholding agent from previous fiscal years, provided that the tax authority shall issue a receipt to the taxpayer or withholding agent and return all materials within three months; under special circumstances, upon approval by the director of the tax bureau at the level of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture or above, the tax authority may bring back to the tax authority for inspection the account books, vouchers, statements, and other relevant materials of the taxpayer or withholding agent for the current year, provided that the tax authority shall return them within 30 days.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　税务机关行使税收征管法第五十四条第(六)项职权时，应当指定专人负责，凭全国统一格式的检查存款账户许可证明进行，并有责任为被检查人保守秘密。  
检查存款账户许可证明，由国家税务总局制定。  
税务机关查询的内容，包括纳税人存款账户余额和资金往来情况。

**Article 87** When exercising the authority under subparagraph (6) of Article 54 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, the tax authority shall designate a specific person to be responsible, carry out the inspection with a permit for inspecting deposit accounts in a uniform format throughout the country, and have the duty to keep confidentiality for the person under inspection.  
The permit for inspecting deposit accounts shall be formulated by the State Administration of Taxation.  
The content of the tax authority's inquiry includes the balance of the taxpayer's deposit account and the flow of funds.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　依照税收征管法第五十五条规定，税务机关采取税收保全措施的期限一般不得超过6个月；重大案件需要延长的，应当报国家税务总局批准。

**Article 88** The period for which the tax authority takes tax preservation measures in accordance with Article 55 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection shall generally not exceed six months; if an extension is necessary for a major case, it shall be reported to the State Administration of Taxation for approval.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　税务机关和税务人员应当依照税收征管法及本细则的规定行使税务检查职权。  
税务人员进行税务检查时，应当出示税务检查证和税务检查通知书；无税务检查证和税务检查通知书的，纳税人、扣缴义务人及其他当事人有权拒绝检查。税务机关对集贸市场及集中经营业户进行检查时，可以使用统一的税务检查通知书。  
税务检查证和税务检查通知书的式样、使用和管理的具体办法，由国家税务总局制定。

**Article 89** The tax authority and tax personnel shall exercise the tax inspection authority in accordance with the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection and these Rules.  
When conducting tax inspection, tax personnel shall present their tax inspection certificate and tax inspection notice; if no tax inspection certificate or tax inspection notice is presented, the taxpayer, withholding agent, and other parties shall have the right to refuse inspection. When conducting inspection at a market or centralized business premises, the tax authority may use a unified tax inspection notice.  
The specific measures for the format, use, and management of the tax inspection certificate and tax inspection notice shall be formulated by the State Administration of Taxation.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　纳税人未按照规定办理税务登记证件验证或者换证手续的，由税务机关责令限期改正，可以处2000元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处2000元以上1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 90** If a taxpayer fails to go through the formalities for verification or renewal of the tax registration certificate according to the provisions, the tax authority shall order it to make corrections within a specified period, and may impose a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　非法印制、转借、倒卖、变造或者伪造完税凭证的，由税务机关责令改正，处2000元以上1万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 91** Where any person illegally prints, transfers, sells, alters or forges tax payment certificates, the tax authority shall order the person to make correction and impose a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　银行和其他金融机构未依照税收征管法的规定在从事生产、经营的纳税人的账户中登录税务登记证件号码，或者未按规定在税务登记证件中登录从事生产、经营的纳税人的账户账号的，由税务机关责令其限期改正，处2000元以上2万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 92** Where a bank or any other financial institution fails to enter the tax registration certificate number in the accounts of a taxpayer engaged in production or business operations in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, or fails to enter the account numbers of a taxpayer engaged in production or business operations in the tax registration certificate as required, the tax authority shall order it to make correction within a specified time limit and impose a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　为纳税人、扣缴义务人非法提供银行账户、发票、证明或者其他方便，导致未缴、少缴税款或者骗取国家出口退税款的，税务机关除没收其违法所得外，可以处未缴、少缴或者骗取的税款1倍以下的罚款。

**Article 93** Where any person illegally provides a taxpayer or a withholding agent with bank accounts, invoices, certificates or other conveniences, resulting in failure to pay or underpayment of taxes, or fraudulent obtaining of export tax refunds from the State, the tax authority shall, in addition to confiscating its illegal gains, may impose a fine of not more than one time the amount of taxes unpaid, underpaid or fraudulently obtained.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　纳税人拒绝代扣、代收税款的，扣缴义务人应当向税务机关报告，由税务机关直接向纳税人追缴税款、滞纳金；纳税人拒不缴纳的，依照税收征管法第六十八条的规定执行。

**Article 94** Where a taxpayer refuses to have taxes withheld or collected on its behalf, the withholding agent shall report to the tax authority, and the tax authority shall directly recover the taxes and late payment surcharges from the taxpayer; if the taxpayer refuses to pay, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 68 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　税务机关依照税收征管法第五十四条第(五)项的规定，到车站、码头、机场、邮政企业及其分支机构检查纳税人有关情况时，有关单位拒绝的，由税务机关责令改正，可以处1万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 95** Where, when the tax authority, in accordance with the provisions of Article 54(5) of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, goes to a railway station, port, airport, postal enterprise or its branch to inspect relevant information of a taxpayer, the relevant entity refuses to cooperate, the tax authority shall order it to make correction and may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　纳税人、扣缴义务人有下列情形之一的，依照税收征管法第七十条的规定处罚：  
(一)提供虚假资料，不如实反映情况，或者拒绝提供有关资料的；  
(二)拒绝或者阻止税务机关记录、录音、录像、照相和复制与案件有关的情况和资料的；  
(三)在检查期间，纳税人、扣缴义务人转移、隐匿、销毁有关资料的；  
(四)有不依法接受税务检查的其他情形的。

**Article 96** Where a taxpayer or withholding agent falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 70 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection:  
(1) providing false information, failing to truthfully reflect the situation, or refusing to provide relevant information;  
(2) refusing or preventing the tax authority from recording, audio-recording, video-recording, photographing, or reproducing circumstances and materials related to the case;  
(3) during the inspection period, the taxpayer or withholding agent transfers, conceals, or destroys relevant materials;  
(4) having other circumstances of failing to accept tax inspection according to law.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　税务人员私分扣押、查封的商品、货物或者其他财产，情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 97** Where a tax official privately distributes seized or distrained goods, merchandise or other property, if the circumstances are serious and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if no crime is constituted, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　税务代理人违反税收法律、行政法规，造成纳税人未缴或者少缴税款的，除由纳税人缴纳或者补缴应纳税款、滞纳金外，对税务代理人处纳税人未缴或者少缴税款50%以上3倍以下的罚款。

**Article 98** Where a tax agent violates tax laws or administrative regulations, causing a taxpayer to fail to pay or underpay taxes, in addition to the taxpayer paying or making up the taxes payable and late payment surcharges, the tax agent shall be imposed a fine of not less than 50% but not more than three times the amount of taxes unpaid or underpaid by the taxpayer.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　税务机关对纳税人、扣缴义务人及其他当事人处以罚款或者没收违法所得时，应当开付罚没凭证；未开付罚没凭证的，纳税人、扣缴义务人以及其他当事人有权拒绝给付。

**Article 99** When the tax authority imposes a fine or confiscates illegal gains on a taxpayer, withholding agent or other party, it shall issue a receipt for the fine or confiscation; if no such receipt is issued, the taxpayer, withholding agent or other party shall have the right to refuse to pay.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　税收征管法第八十八条规定的纳税争议，是指纳税人、扣缴义务人、纳税担保人对税务机关确定纳税主体、征税对象、征税范围、减税、免税及退税、适用税率、计税依据、纳税环节、纳税期限、纳税地点以及税款征收方式等具体行政行为有异议而发生的争议。

**Article 100** The tax disputes as referred to in Article 88 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection mean disputes arising from a taxpayer, withholding agent or tax guarantor having objections to the specific administrative acts of the tax authority in determining the taxpayer subject, object of taxation, scope of taxation, tax reduction, tax exemption and tax refund, applicable tax rate, tax basis, tax link, tax payment period, tax payment place, and method of tax collection, among others.

### 第八章　文书送达

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　税务机关送达税务文书，应当直接送交受送达人。  
受送达人是公民的，应当由本人直接签收；本人不在的，交其同住成年家属签收。  
受送达人是法人或者其他组织的，应当由法人的法定代表人、其他组织的主要负责人或者该法人、组织的财务负责人、负责收件的人签收。受送达人有代理人的，可以送交其代理人签收。

**Article 101** A tax authority that serves tax documents shall deliver them directly to the recipient.  
Where the recipient is a citizen, the documents shall be signed for by the citizen himself; if the citizen is absent, they may be signed for by an adult family member living with him.  
Where the recipient is a legal person or any other organization, the documents shall be signed for by the legal representative of the legal person, the principal leading person of the other organization, or the person in charge of financial affairs or the person responsible for receiving documents of such legal person or organization. Where the recipient has an agent, the documents may be delivered to the agent for signature.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　送达税务文书应当有送达回证，并由受送达人或者本细则规定的其他签收人在送达回证上记明收到日期，签名或者盖章，即为送达。

**Article 102** The service of tax documents shall be evidenced by a certificate of service, and the service shall be considered effected upon the recipient or other person designated to sign for receipt under these Rules noting the date of receipt on the certificate of service and affixing their signature or seal.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　受送达人或者本细则规定的其他签收人拒绝签收税务文书的，送达人应当在送达回证上记明拒收理由和日期，并由送达人和见证人签名或者盖章，将税务文书留在受送达人处，即视为送达。

**Article 103** Where the recipient or other person designated to sign for receipt under these Rules refuses to sign for tax documents, the person serving the documents shall note on the certificate of service the reason for refusal and the date, and the person serving the documents and a witness shall affix their signatures or seals. The tax documents shall then be left at the premise of the recipient, and the service shall be deemed effected.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　直接送达税务文书有困难的，可以委托其他有关机关或者其他单位代为送达，或者邮寄送达。

**Article 104** Where direct service of tax documents is difficult, service may be entrusted to another relevant authority or other unit, or effected by mail.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　直接或者委托送达税务文书的，以签收人或者见证人在送达回证上的签收或者注明的收件日期为送达日期；邮寄送达的，以挂号函件回执上注明的收件日期为送达日期，并视为已送达。

**Article 105** For direct service or service by entrustment of tax documents, the date of service shall be the date noted on the certificate of service by the person signing for receipt or the witness; for service by mail, the date of service shall be the date of receipt noted on the receipt of the registered mail, and the service shall be deemed effected.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　有下列情形之一的，税务机关可以公告送达税务文书，自公告之日起满30日，即视为送达：  
(一)同一送达事项的受送达人众多；  
(二)采用本章规定的其他送达方式无法送达。

**Article 106** Under any of the following circumstances, a tax authority may serve tax documents by public announcement, and service shall be deemed effected upon expiry of 30 days from the date of announcement:  
(1) there are numerous recipients for the same service matter;  
(2) service cannot be effected by other methods provided in this Chapter.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　税务文书的格式由国家税务总局制定。本细则所称税务文书，包括：  
(一)税务事项通知书；  
(二)责令限期改正通知书；  
(三)税收保全措施决定书；  
(四)税收强制执行决定书；  
(五)税务检查通知书；  
(六)税务处理决定书；  
(七)税务行政处罚决定书；  
(八)行政复议决定书；  
(九)其他税务文书。

**Article 107** The format of tax documents shall be formulated by the State Administration of Taxation. For the purposes of these Rules, 'tax documents' include:  
(1) Notice of Tax Matters;  
(2) Notice of Order for Correction within a Time Limit;  
(3) Decision on Tax Preservation Measures;  
(4) Decision on Compulsory Tax Enforcement;  
(5) Notice of Tax Inspection;  
(6) Decision on Tax Treatment;  
(7) Decision on Tax Administrative Penalty;  
(8) Decision on Administrative Reconsideration;  
(9) other tax documents.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　税收征管法及本细则所称“以上”、“以下”、“日内”、“届满”均含本数。

**Article 108** The terms 'or above', 'or below', 'within', and 'upon expiry' as used in the Tax Collection Administration Law and these Rules include the specified number.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　税收征管法及本细则所规定期限的最后一日是法定休假日的，以休假日期满的次日为期限的最后一日；在期限内有连续3日以上法定休假日的，按休假日天数顺延。

**Article 109** If the last day of a time limit prescribed in the Tax Collection Administration Law and these Rules falls on a legal holiday, the day immediately following the expiry of the holiday shall be deemed the last day of the time limit; if there are three or more consecutive legal holidays within the time limit, the time limit shall be extended by the number of holidays.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　税收征管法第三十条第三款规定的代扣、代收手续费，纳入预算管理，由税务机关依照法律、行政法规的规定付给扣缴义务人。

**Article 110** The handling fees for withholding and collection as provided for in Article 30, paragraph 3 of the Tax Collection Administration Law shall be included in budgetary management and shall be paid to the withholding agent by the tax authority in accordance with laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　纳税人、扣缴义务人委托税务代理人代为办理税务事宜的办法，由国家税务总局规定。

**Article 111** The measures for taxpayers and withholding agents to entrust tax agents to handle tax matters on their behalf shall be prescribed by the State Administration of Taxation.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　耕地占用税、契税、农业税、牧业税的征收管理，按照国务院的有关规定执行。

**Article 112** The collection and administration of farmland occupation tax, deed tax, agricultural tax, and animal husbandry tax shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　本细则自2002年10月15日起施行。1993年8月4日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法实施细则》同时废止。

**Article 113** These Implementing Rules shall come into force as of October 15, 2002. The Implementing Rules of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection, promulgated by the State Council on August 4, 1993, shall be abolished simultaneously.
