# 价格违法行为行政处罚规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2010-12-04  
**生效日期**：2010-12-04

> (1999年7月10日国务院批准　1999年8月1日国家发展计划委员会发布　根据2006年2月21日《国务院关于修改〈价格违法行为行政处罚规定〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2008年1月13日《国务院关于修改〈价格违法行为行政处罚规定〉的决定》第二次修订　根据2010年12月4日《国务院关于修改〈价格违法行为行政处罚规定〉的决定》第三次修订) 第一条　为了依法惩处价格违法行为，维护正常的价格秩序，保护消费者和经营者的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国价格法》(以下简称价格法)的有关规定，制定本规定。 第二条　县级以上各级人民政府价格主管部门依法对价格活动进行监督检查，并决定对价格违法行为的行政处罚。 第三条　价格违法行为的行政处罚由价格违法行为发生地的地方人民政府价格主管部门决定；国务院价格主管部门规定由其上级价格主管部门决定的，从其规定。 第四条　经营者违反价格法第十四条的规定，有下列行为之一的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，处10万元以上100万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，或者由工商行政管理机关吊销营业执照： (一)除依法降价处理鲜活商品、季节性商品、积压商品等商品外，为了排挤竞争对手或者独占市场，以低于成本的价格倾销，扰乱正常的生产经营秩序，损害国家利益或者其他经营者的合法权益的； (二)提供相同商品或者服务，对具有同等交易条件的其他经营者实行价格歧视的。 第五条　经营者违反价格法第十四条的规定，相互串通，操纵市场价格，造成商品价格较大幅度上涨的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，处10万元以上100万元以下的罚款，情节较重的处100万元以上500万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，或者由工商行政管理机关吊销营业执照。 除前款规定情形外，经营者相互串通，操纵市场价格，损害其他经营者或者消费者合法权益的，依照本规定第四条的规定处罚。 行业协会或者其他单位组织经营者相互串通，操纵市场价格的，对经营者依照前两款的规定处罚；对行业协会或者其他单位，可以处50万元以下的罚款，情节严重的，由登记管理机关依法撤销登记、吊销执照。 第六条　经营者违反价格法第十四条的规定，有下列推动商品价格过快、过高上涨行为之一的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款，情节较重的处50万元以上300万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，或者由工商行政管理机关吊销营业执照： (一)捏造、散布涨价信息，扰乱市场价格秩序的； (二)除生产自用外，超出正常的存储数量或者存储周期，大量囤积市场供应紧张、价格发生异常波动的商品，经价格主管部门告诫仍继续囤积的； (三)利用其他手段哄抬价格，推动商品价格过快、过高上涨的。 行业协会或者为商品交易提供服务的单位有前款规定的违法行为的，可以处50万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由登记管理机关依法撤销登记、吊销执照。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了依法惩处价格违法行为，维护正常的价格秩序，保护消费者和经营者的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国价格法》(以下简称价格法)的有关规定，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Price Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Price Law) for the purpose of punishing illegal pricing acts according to law, maintaining normal pricing order, and protecting the legitimate rights and interests of consumers and business operators.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　县级以上各级人民政府价格主管部门依法对价格活动进行监督检查，并决定对价格违法行为的行政处罚。

**Article 2** The competent pricing departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, supervise and inspect pricing activities and decide on administrative penalties for illegal pricing acts.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　价格违法行为的行政处罚由价格违法行为发生地的地方人民政府价格主管部门决定；国务院价格主管部门规定由其上级价格主管部门决定的，从其规定。

**Article 3** The administrative penalty for an illegal pricing act shall be decided by the competent pricing department of the local people's government at the place where the illegal pricing act occurs; if the competent pricing department of the State Council stipulates that the decision shall be made by the competent pricing department at a higher level, such stipulation shall prevail.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　经营者违反[价格法第十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国价格法.md#art-14)的规定，有下列行为之一的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，处10万元以上100万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，或者由工商行政管理机关吊销营业执照：  
(一)除依法降价处理鲜活商品、季节性商品、积压商品等商品外，为了排挤竞争对手或者独占市场，以低于成本的价格倾销，扰乱正常的生产经营秩序，损害国家利益或者其他经营者的合法权益的；  
(二)提供相同商品或者服务，对具有同等交易条件的其他经营者实行价格歧视的。

**Article 4** Where a business operator, in violation of the provisions of Article 14 of the Price Law, commits any of the following acts, he/she shall be ordered to make corrections, his/her illegal gains shall be confiscated, and concurrently a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, he/she shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, or his/her business license shall be revoked by the administrative authority for industry and commerce:  
(1) Dumping at a price lower than cost in order to exclude competitors or monopolize the market, thereby disrupting the normal production and operation order and harming the legitimate rights and interests of the State or other business operators, except for the lawful reduction in price of fresh goods, seasonal goods, overstocked goods, etc.;  
(2) Practicing price discrimination against other business operators with identical transaction conditions in the supply of the same commodity or service.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　经营者违反[价格法第十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国价格法.md#art-14)的规定，相互串通，操纵市场价格，造成商品价格较大幅度上涨的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，处10万元以上100万元以下的罚款，情节较重的处100万元以上500万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，或者由工商行政管理机关吊销营业执照。  
除前款规定情形外，经营者相互串通，操纵市场价格，损害其他经营者或者消费者合法权益的，依照[本规定第四条](#art-4)的规定处罚。  
行业协会或者其他单位组织经营者相互串通，操纵市场价格的，对经营者依照前两款的规定处罚；对行业协会或者其他单位，可以处50万元以下的罚款，情节严重的，由登记管理机关依法撤销登记、吊销执照。

**Article 5** Where a business operator, in violation of the provisions of Article 14 of the Price Law, colludes with others to manipulate the market price, causing a relatively large increase in commodity prices, he/she shall be ordered to make corrections, his/her illegal gains shall be confiscated, and concurrently a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a fine of not less than 1,000,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, he/she shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, or his/her business license shall be revoked by the administrative authority for industry and commerce.  
Except for the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, where business operators collude to manipulate market prices, harming the legitimate rights and interests of other business operators or consumers, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 4 of these Provisions.  
Where a trade association or any other unit organizes business operators to collude in manipulating market prices, the business operators shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs; the trade association or other unit may be imposed a fine of not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the registration authority shall revoke the registration and cancel the license according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　经营者违反[价格法第十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国价格法.md#art-14)的规定，有下列推动商品价格过快、过高上涨行为之一的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款，情节较重的处50万元以上300万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，或者由工商行政管理机关吊销营业执照：  
(一)捏造、散布涨价信息，扰乱市场价格秩序的；  
(二)除生产自用外，超出正常的存储数量或者存储周期，大量囤积市场供应紧张、价格发生异常波动的商品，经价格主管部门告诫仍继续囤积的；  
(三)利用其他手段哄抬价格，推动商品价格过快、过高上涨的。  
行业协会或者为商品交易提供服务的单位有前款规定的违法行为的，可以处50万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由登记管理机关依法撤销登记、吊销执照。  
前两款规定以外的其他单位散布虚假涨价信息，扰乱市场价格秩序，依法应当由其他主管机关查处的，价格主管部门可以提出依法处罚的建议，有关主管机关应当依法处罚。

**Article 6** Where a business operator, in violation of the provisions of Article 14 of the Price Law, commits any of the following acts that promote excessively fast and high increases in commodity prices, he/she shall be ordered to make corrections, his/her illegal gains shall be confiscated, and concurrently a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 3,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, he/she shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, or his/her business license shall be revoked by the administrative authority for industry and commerce:  
(1) Fabricating and spreading information about price increases to disrupt the market pricing order;  
(2) Hoarding large quantities of commodities that are in short supply or whose prices are experiencing abnormal fluctuations beyond normal storage quantities or storage periods (except for self-use in production), and continuing to hoard after being warned by the competent pricing department;  
(3) Using other means to drive up prices, pushing commodity prices to rise excessively fast and high.  
Where a trade association or a unit providing services for commodity transactions commits any of the illegal acts specified in the preceding paragraph, a fine of not more than 500,000 yuan may be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the registration authority shall revoke the registration and cancel the license according to law.  
Where any other unit other than those specified in the preceding two paragraphs spreads false information about price increases to disrupt market pricing order, which shall be investigated and dealt with by other competent authorities according to law, the competent pricing department may propose a suggestion of penalty according to law, and the relevant competent authority shall impose a penalty according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　经营者违反[价格法第十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国价格法.md#art-14)的规定，利用虚假的或者使人误解的价格手段，诱骗消费者或者其他经营者与其进行交易的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，或者由工商行政管理机关吊销营业执照。

**Article 7** Where a business operator, in violation of the provisions of Article 14 of the Price Law, uses false or misleading pricing methods to trick consumers or other business operators into transactions with him/her, he/she shall be ordered to make corrections, his/her illegal gains shall be confiscated, and concurrently a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, he/she shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, or his/her business license shall be revoked by the administrative authority for industry and commerce.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　经营者违反[价格法第十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国价格法.md#art-14)的规定，采取抬高等级或者压低等级等手段销售、收购商品或者提供服务，变相提高或者压低价格的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，或者由工商行政管理机关吊销营业执照。

**Article 8** Where a business operator, in violation of the provisions of Article 14 of the Price Law, raises or lowers grades or other means to sell or purchase commodities or provide services, thereby raising or lowering prices in disguised form, he/she shall be ordered to make corrections, his/her illegal gains shall be confiscated, and concurrently a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, he/she shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, or his/her business license shall be revoked by the administrative authority for industry and commerce.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　经营者不执行政府指导价、政府定价，有下列行为之一的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款，情节较重的处50万元以上200万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿：  
(一)超出政府指导价浮动幅度制定价格的；  
(二)高于或者低于政府定价制定价格的；  
(三)擅自制定属于政府指导价、政府定价范围内的商品或者服务价格的；  
(四)提前或者推迟执行政府指导价、政府定价的；  
(五)自立收费项目或者自定标准收费的；  
(六)采取分解收费项目、重复收费、扩大收费范围等方式变相提高收费标准的；  
(七)对政府明令取消的收费项目继续收费的；  
(八)违反规定以保证金、抵押金等形式变相收费的；  
(九)强制或者变相强制服务并收费的；  
(十)不按照规定提供服务而收取费用的；  
(十一)不执行政府指导价、政府定价的其他行为。

**Article 9** Where a business operator fails to implement government-guided prices or government-fixed prices and commits any of the following acts, he/she shall be ordered to make corrections, his/her illegal gains shall be confiscated, and concurrently a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, he/she shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification:  
(1) Setting prices beyond the floating range of government-guided prices;  
(2) Setting prices higher or lower than government-fixed prices;  
(3) Unauthorized setting of prices for commodities or services that fall within the scope of government-guided prices or government-fixed prices;  
(4) Implementing government-guided prices or government-fixed prices ahead of schedule or later than the prescribed time;  
(5) Collecting fees for self-established items or at self-determined rates;  
(6) Raising fee standards in disguised form by means such as splitting fee items, charging repeatedly, expanding the scope of charges, etc.;  
(7) Continuing to charge for fee items explicitly abolished by the government;  
(8) Charging fees in disguised form in the name of deposits, mortgages, etc., in violation of regulations;  
(9) Forcing or imposing services in disguised form and collecting fees;  
(10) Collecting fees without providing services as required;  
(11) Other acts of failing to implement government-guided prices or government-fixed prices.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　经营者不执行法定的价格干预措施、紧急措施，有下列行为之一的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，处10万元以上100万元以下的罚款，情节较重的处100万元以上500万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿：  
(一)不执行提价申报或者调价备案制度的；  
(二)超过规定的差价率、利润率幅度的；  
(三)不执行规定的限价、最低保护价的；  
(四)不执行集中定价权限措施的；  
(五)不执行冻结价格措施的；  
(六)不执行法定的价格干预措施、紧急措施的其他行为。

**Article 10** Where a business operator fails to implement statutory price intervention measures or emergency measures and commits any of the following acts, he/she shall be ordered to make corrections, his/her illegal gains shall be confiscated, and concurrently a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a fine of not less than 1,000,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, he/she shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification:  
(1) Failing to implement the system of reporting price increases or filing price adjustments;  
(2) Exceeding the prescribed ranges of price difference percentage or profit rate percentage;  
(3) Failing to implement the prescribed price limits or minimum protection prices;  
(4) Failing to implement the measures for centralized pricing authority;  
(5) Failing to implement measures for freezing prices;  
(6) Other acts of failing to implement statutory price intervention measures or emergency measures.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　[本规定第四条](#art-4)、第七条至第九条规定中经营者为个人的，对其没有违法所得的价格违法行为，可以处10万元以下的罚款。  
[本规定第五条](#art-5)、第六条、第十条规定中经营者为个人的，对其没有违法所得的价格违法行为，按照前款规定处罚；情节严重的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 11** Where the business operator specified in the provisions of Articles 4, 7 through 9 of these Provisions is an individual, a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan may be imposed on him/her for an illegal pricing act without illegal gains.  
Where the business operator specified in the provisions of Articles 5, 6 and 10 of these Provisions is an individual, a penalty shall be imposed on him/her for an illegal pricing act without illegal gains in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　经营者违反法律、法规的规定牟取暴利的，责令改正，没收违法所得，可以并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，或者由工商行政管理机关吊销营业执照。

**Article 12** Where a business operator, in violation of the provisions of laws or regulations, seeks exorbitant profits, he/she shall be ordered to make corrections, his/her illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains may be imposed concurrently; if the circumstances are serious, he/she shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, or his/her business license shall be revoked by the administrative authority for industry and commerce.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　经营者违反明码标价规定，有下列行为之一的，责令改正，没收违法所得，可以并处5000元以下的罚款：  
(一)不标明价格的；  
(二)不按照规定的内容和方式明码标价的；  
(三)在标价之外加价出售商品或者收取未标明的费用的；  
(四)违反明码标价规定的其他行为。

**Article 13** Where a business operator violates the provisions on clearly marked prices and commits any of the following acts, he/she shall be ordered to make corrections, his/her illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan may be imposed concurrently:  
(1) Failing to indicate prices;  
(2) Failing to clearly mark prices in the prescribed content and manner;  
(3) Selling commodities at a price higher than the marked price or collecting fees not indicated;  
(4) Other acts that violate the provisions on clearly marked prices.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　拒绝提供价格监督检查所需资料或者提供虚假资料的，责令改正，给予警告；逾期不改正的，可以处10万元以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予纪律处分。

**Article 14** Where a party refuses to provide materials required for price supervision and inspection or provides false materials, he/she shall be ordered to make corrections and given a warning; if no correction is made within the time limit, a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan may be imposed, and the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　政府价格主管部门进行价格监督检查时，发现经营者的违法行为同时具有下列三种情形的，可以依照[价格法第三十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国价格法.md#art-34)第(三)项的规定责令其暂停相关营业：  
(一)违法行为情节复杂或者情节严重，经查明后可能给予较重处罚的；  
(二)不暂停相关营业，违法行为将继续的；  
(三)不暂停相关营业，可能影响违法事实的认定，采取其他措施又不足以保证查明的。  
政府价格主管部门进行价格监督检查时，执法人员不得少于2人，并应当向经营者或者有关人员出示证件。

**Article 15** Where, during price supervision and inspection, the competent pricing department of the government finds that the illegal act of a business operator simultaneously meets the following three conditions, it may, in accordance with the provisions of Item (3) of Article 34 of the Price Law, order the operator to suspend the relevant business:  
(1) The illegal act is complex or serious in circumstances, and may be subject to a relatively heavy penalty after investigation;  
(2) Without suspending the relevant business, the illegal act will continue;  
(3) Without suspending the relevant business, it may affect the determination of the illegal facts, and other measures are insufficient to ensure verification.  
When the competent pricing department of the government conducts price supervision and inspection, there shall be at least two law enforcement officers, who shall produce their credentials to the business operator or relevant persons.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　[本规定第四条](#art-4)至第十三条规定中的违法所得，属于[价格法第四十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国价格法.md#art-41)规定的消费者或者其他经营者多付价款的，责令经营者限期退还。难以查找多付价款的消费者或者其他经营者的，责令公告查找。  
经营者拒不按照前款规定退还消费者或者其他经营者多付的价款，以及期限届满没有退还消费者或者其他经营者多付的价款，由政府价格主管部门予以没收，消费者或者其他经营者要求退还时，由经营者依法承担民事责任。

**Article 16** Where the illegal gains specified in the provisions of Articles 4 through 13 of these Provisions are the overpaid amounts by consumers or other business operators as provided for in Article 41 of the Price Law, the business operator shall be ordered to refund such amounts within a prescribed time limit. If it is difficult to locate the consumers or other business operators who overpaid, the business operator shall be ordered to make a public notice to locate them.  
If the business operator refuses to refund the overpaid amounts to consumers or other business operators in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, or fails to refund such amounts within the prescribed time limit, the competent pricing department of the government shall confiscate such amounts. Where consumers or other business operators demand a refund, the business operator shall bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　经营者有[《中华人民共和国行政处罚法》第二十七条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政处罚法.md#art-27)所列情形的，应当依法从轻或者减轻处罚。  
经营者有下列情形之一的，应当从重处罚：  
(一)价格违法行为严重或者社会影响较大的；  
(二)屡查屡犯的；  
(三)伪造、涂改或者转移、销毁证据的；  
(四)转移与价格违法行为有关的资金或者商品的；  
(五)经营者拒不按照[本规定第十六条](#art-16)第一款规定退还消费者或者其他经营者多付价款的；  
(六)应予从重处罚的其他价格违法行为。

**Article 17** Where a business operator falls under the circumstances listed in Article 27 of the Administrative Penalty Law of the People's Republic of China, a lighter or mitigated penalty shall be imposed according to law.  
Where a business operator falls under any of the following circumstances, a severe penalty shall be imposed:  
(1) The illegal pricing act is serious or has a relatively large social impact;  
(2) Repeatedly committing violations after being investigated;  
(3) Forging, altering, transferring, or destroying evidence;  
(4) Transferring funds or commodities related to the illegal pricing act;  
(5) The business operator refuses to refund overpaid amounts to consumers or other business operators in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 16 of these Provisions;  
(6) Other illegal pricing acts that shall be subject to a severe penalty.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本规定中以违法所得计算罚款数额的，违法所得无法确定时，按照没有违法所得的规定处罚。

**Article 18** Where the amount of a fine is calculated on the basis of illegal gains under these Provisions, if the illegal gains cannot be determined, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions for the case where there are no illegal gains.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　有本规定所列价格违法行为严重扰乱市场秩序，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 19** Where an illegal pricing act listed in these Provisions seriously disrupts the market order and constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　经营者对政府价格主管部门作出的处罚决定不服的，应当先依法申请行政复议；对行政复议决定不服的，可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 20** Where a business operator disagrees with a penalty decision made by the competent pricing department of the government, he/she shall first apply for administrative reconsideration according to law; if he/she disagrees with the decision on administrative reconsideration, he/she may institute legal proceedings with the People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　逾期不缴纳罚款的，每日按罚款数额的3%加处罚款；逾期不缴纳违法所得的，每日按违法所得数额的2‰加处罚款。

**Article 21** A fine at the rate of 3% of the amount of the fine shall be imposed for each day of delay in paying the fine; a fine at the rate of 2‰ of the amount of illegal gains shall be imposed for each day of delay in turning over illegal gains.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　任何单位和个人有本规定所列价格违法行为，情节严重，拒不改正的，政府价格主管部门除依照本规定给予处罚外，可以公告其价格违法行为，直至其改正。

**Article 22** Where any unit or individual commits a price-related illegal act listed in these Provisions, and if the circumstances are serious and the party refuses to rectify, the government price competent department may, in addition to imposing a penalty in accordance with these Provisions, publicize the price-related illegal act until rectification is made.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　有关法律对[价格法第十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国价格法.md#art-14)所列行为的处罚及处罚机关另有规定的，可以依照有关法律的规定执行。

**Article 23** Where other laws provide otherwise on the penalties for the acts listed in Article 14 of the Price Law and the penalty-imposing authorities, such provisions may be executed in accordance with the relevant laws.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　价格执法人员泄露国家秘密、经营者的商业秘密或者滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 24** Where a price law enforcement officer divulges State secrets or trade secrets of business operators, or abuses power, neglects duties, or engages in malpractices for personal gain, which constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, disciplinary action shall be taken according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本规定自公布之日起施行。

**Article 25** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
