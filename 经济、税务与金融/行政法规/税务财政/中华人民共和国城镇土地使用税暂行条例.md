### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了合理利用城镇土地，调节土地级差收入，提高土地使用效益，加强土地管理，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of rationally utilizing urban land, regulating differential land income, improving the efficiency of land use, and strengthening land administration.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在城市、县城、建制镇、工矿区范围内使用土地的单位和个人，为城镇土地使用税(以下简称土地使用税)的纳税人，应当依照本条例的规定缴纳土地使用税。  
前款所称单位，包括国有企业、集体企业、私营企业、股份制企业、外商投资企业、外国企业以及其他企业和事业单位、社会团体、国家机关、军队以及其他单位；所称个人，包括个体工商户以及其他个人。

**Article 2** Units and individuals that use land within the scope of cities, county towns, established towns, and industrial and mining areas shall be the taxpayers of urban land use tax (hereinafter referred to as land use tax) and shall pay land use tax in accordance with these Regulations.  
The units mentioned in the preceding paragraph include State-owned enterprises, collective enterprises, private enterprises, joint-stock enterprises, enterprises with foreign investment, foreign enterprises and other enterprises, as well as institutions, social organizations, State organs, the armed forces and other units; the individuals mentioned include individual industrial and commercial households and other individuals.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　土地使用税以纳税人实际占用的土地面积为计税依据，依照规定税额计算征收。  
前款土地占用面积的组织测量工作，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据实际情况确定。

**Article 3** Land use tax shall be calculated and levied on the basis of the actual land area occupied by the taxpayer, in accordance with the prescribed tax amount.  
The work of organizing the measurement of the land area occupied as mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be determined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government according to the actual circumstances.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　土地使用税每平方米年税额如下：  
(一)大城市1.5元至30元；  
(二)中等城市1.2元至24元；  
(三)小城市0.9元至18元；  
(四)县城、建制镇、工矿区0.6元至12元。

**Article 4** The annual tax amount per square meter for land use tax is as follows:  
(1) for large cities, from 1.5 yuan to 30 yuan;  
(2) for medium-sized cities, from 1.2 yuan to 24 yuan;  
(3) for small cities, from 0.9 yuan to 18 yuan;  
(4) for county towns, established towns, and industrial and mining areas, from 0.6 yuan to 12 yuan.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，应当在[本条例第四条](#art-4)规定的税额幅度内，根据市政建设状况、经济繁荣程度等条件，确定所辖地区的适用税额幅度。  
市、县人民政府应当根据实际情况，将本地区土地划分为若干等级，在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的税额幅度内，制定相应的适用税额标准，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准执行。  
经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，经济落后地区土地使用税的适用税额标准可以适当降低，但降低额不得超过[本条例第四条](#art-4)规定最低税额的30%。经济发达地区土地使用税的适用税额标准可以适当提高，但须报经财政部批准。

**Article 5** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, within the tax amount range prescribed in Article 4 of these Regulations, determine the applicable tax amount range for their respective administered areas based on conditions such as municipal construction status and the degree of economic prosperity.  
The people's governments of cities or counties shall, according to actual circumstances, divide the land in their respective areas into several grades and, within the tax amount range determined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, formulate corresponding applicable tax amount standards, and submit them to the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government for approval before implementation.  
Upon approval by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the applicable tax amount standard for land use tax in economically underdeveloped areas may be appropriately reduced, but the reduction shall not exceed 30% of the minimum tax amount prescribed in Article 4 of these Regulations. The applicable tax amount standard for land use tax in economically developed areas may be appropriately increased, but such increase shall be subject to approval by the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　下列土地免缴土地使用税：  
(一)国家机关、人民团体、军队自用的土地；  
(二)由国家财政部门拨付事业经费的单位自用的土地；  
(三)宗教寺庙、公园、名胜古迹自用的土地；  
(四)市政街道、广场、绿化地带等公共用地；  
(五)直接用于农、林、牧、渔业的生产用地；  
(六)经批准开山填海整治的土地和改造的废弃土地，从使用的月份起免缴土地使用税5年至10年；  
(七)由财政部另行规定免税的能源、交通、水利设施用地和其他用地。

**Article 6** The following land shall be exempted from land use tax:  
(1) land used by State organs, people's organizations, and the armed forces for their own use;  
(2) land used by units that receive operating expenses allocated by the State financial department for their own use;  
(3) land used by religious temples, parks, and places of historic interest and scenic beauty for their own use;  
(4) public land such as municipal streets, squares, and green belts;  
(5) production land directly used for agriculture, forestry, animal husbandry, and fishery;  
(6) land that has been approved for reclamation by leveling mountains and filling seas and transformed abandoned land, which shall be exempted from land use tax for a period of 5 to 10 years starting from the month of use; and  
(7) land used for energy, transportation, and water conservancy facilities and other land that are separately stipulated by the Ministry of Finance as tax-exempt.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　除[本条例第六条](#art-6)规定外，纳税人缴纳土地使用税确有困难需要定期减免的，由县以上税务机关批准。

**Article 7** Except as provided in Article 6 of these Regulations, if a taxpayer has genuine difficulty in paying land use tax and needs periodic reduction or exemption, such reduction or exemption shall be approved by the tax authority at or above the county level.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　土地使用税按年计算、分期缴纳。缴纳期限由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定。

**Article 8** Land use tax shall be calculated on an annual basis and paid in installments. The payment period shall be determined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　新征收的土地，依照下列规定缴纳土地使用税：  
(一)征收的耕地，自批准征收之日起满1年时开始缴纳土地使用税；  
(二)征收的非耕地，自批准征收次月起缴纳土地使用税。

**Article 9** For newly requisitioned land, land use tax shall be paid in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) for requisitioned cultivated land, land use tax shall be paid starting from one year after the date of approval of the requisition; and  
(2) for requisitioned non-cultivated land, land use tax shall be paid starting from the month following the approval of the requisition.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　土地使用税由土地所在地的税务机关征收。土地管理机关应当向土地所在地的税务机关提供土地使用权属资料。

**Article 10** Land use tax shall be collected by the tax authority in the place where the land is located. The land administration authority shall provide the tax authority in the place where the land is located with the materials on the ownership of land use rights.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　土地使用税的征收管理，依照《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》及本条例的规定执行。

**Article 11** The collection and administration of land use tax shall be implemented in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection and the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　土地使用税收入纳入财政预算管理。

**Article 12** The revenue from land use tax shall be incorporated into the financial budget management.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本条例的实施办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。

**Article 13** The implementing measures of these Regulations shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本条例自1988年11月1日起施行，各地制定的土地使用费办法同时停止执行。

**Article 14** These Regulations shall come into force on November 1, 1988, and the measures on land use fees formulated by various localities shall cease to be implemented at the same time.
