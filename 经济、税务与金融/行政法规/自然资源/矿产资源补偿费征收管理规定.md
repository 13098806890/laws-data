# 矿产资源补偿费征收管理规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1997-07-03  
**生效日期**：1994-04-01

> (1994年2月27日中华人民共和国国务院令第150号发布　根据1997年7月3日《国务院关于修改〈矿产资源补偿费征收管理规定〉的决定》修订) 第一条　为了保障和促进矿产资源的勘查、保护与合理开发，维护国家对矿产资源的财产权益，根据《中华人民共和国矿产资源法》的有关规定，制定本规定。 第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和其他管辖海域开采矿产资源，应当依照本规定缴纳矿产资源补偿费；法律、行政法规另有规定的，从其规定。 第三条　矿产资源补偿费按照矿产品销售收入的一定比例计征。企业缴纳的矿产资源补偿费列入管理费用。 采矿权人对矿产品自行加工的，按照国家规定价格计算销售收入；国家没有规定价格的，按照征收时矿产品的当地市场平均价格计算销售收入。 采矿权人向境外销售矿产品的，按照国际市场销售价格计算销售收入。 本规定所称矿产品，是指矿产资源经过开采或者采选后，脱离自然赋存状态的产品。 第四条　矿产资源补偿费由采矿权人缴纳。 矿产资源补偿费以矿产品销售时使用的货币结算；采矿权人对矿产品自行加工的，以其销售最终产品时使用的货币结算。 第五条　矿产资源补偿费按照下列方式计算： 征收矿产资源补偿费金额＝矿产品销售收入×补偿费费率×开采回采率系数 核定开采回采率 开采回采率系数＝ 实际开采回采率 核定开采回采率，以按照国家有关规定经批准的矿山设计为准；按照国家有关规定，只要求有开采方案，不要求有矿山设计的矿山企业，其开采回采率由县级以上地方人民政府负责地质矿产管理工作的部门会同同级有关部门核定。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障和促进矿产资源的勘查、保护与合理开发，维护国家对矿产资源的财产权益，根据《中华人民共和国矿产资源法》的有关规定，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Mineral Resources Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of ensuring and promoting the exploration, protection and rational development of mineral resources and safeguarding the State's property rights and interests in mineral resources.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和其他管辖海域开采矿产资源，应当依照本规定缴纳矿产资源补偿费；法律、行政法规另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 2** Anyone who exploits mineral resources within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction shall pay mineral resources compensation fees in accordance with these Provisions; where laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　矿产资源补偿费按照矿产品销售收入的一定比例计征。企业缴纳的矿产资源补偿费列入管理费用。  
采矿权人对矿产品自行加工的，按照国家规定价格计算销售收入；国家没有规定价格的，按照征收时矿产品的当地市场平均价格计算销售收入。  
采矿权人向境外销售矿产品的，按照国际市场销售价格计算销售收入。  
本规定所称矿产品，是指矿产资源经过开采或者采选后，脱离自然赋存状态的产品。

**Article 3** Mineral resources compensation fees shall be levied at a certain percentage of the sales revenue of mineral products. The mineral resources compensation fees paid by an enterprise shall be included in administrative expenses.  
Where a mining right holder processes mineral products on its own, the sales revenue shall be calculated at the prices prescribed by the State; where no price is prescribed by the State, the sales revenue shall be calculated at the local average market price of the mineral products at the time of levy.  
Where a mining right holder sells mineral products abroad, the sales revenue shall be calculated at the international market sales price.  
For the purpose of these Provisions, "mineral products" means products that have been separated from their natural state after extraction or extraction and dressing of mineral resources.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　矿产资源补偿费由采矿权人缴纳。  
矿产资源补偿费以矿产品销售时使用的货币结算；采矿权人对矿产品自行加工的，以其销售最终产品时使用的货币结算。

**Article 4** Mineral resources compensation fees shall be paid by the mining right holder.  
Mineral resources compensation fees shall be settled in the currency used at the time of sale of the mineral products; where a mining right holder processes mineral products on its own, settlement shall be made in the currency used at the time of sale of the final products.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　矿产资源补偿费按照下列方式计算：  
征收矿产资源补偿费金额＝矿产品销售收入×补偿费费率×开采回采率系数  
核定开采回采率  
开采回采率系数＝  
实际开采回采率  
核定开采回采率，以按照国家有关规定经批准的矿山设计为准；按照国家有关规定，只要求有开采方案，不要求有矿山设计的矿山企业，其开采回采率由县级以上地方人民政府负责地质矿产管理工作的部门会同同级有关部门核定。  
不能按照本条第一款、第二款规定的方式计算矿产资源补偿费的矿种，由国务院地质矿产主管部门会同国务院财政部门另行制定计算方式。

**Article 5** Mineral resources compensation fees shall be calculated in the following manner:  
Amount of mineral resources compensation fee levied = Sales revenue of mineral products × Compensation fee rate × Mining recovery coefficient  
Approved mining recovery rate  
Mining recovery coefficient =  
Actual mining recovery rate  
The approved mining recovery rate shall be based on the mine design approved in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; for a mining enterprise that, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, is only required to have a mining plan but not a mine design, its mining recovery rate shall be verified by the department responsible for geological and mineral management under the local people's government at or above the county level in conjunction with the relevant departments at the same level.  
For mineral types for which the mineral resources compensation fee cannot be calculated in the manner specified in paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 of this Article, the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council shall, in conjunction with the finance department under the State Council, formulate a calculation method separately.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　矿产资源补偿费依照本规定附录所规定的费率征收。  
矿产资源补偿费费率的调整，由国务院财政部门、国务院地质矿产主管部门、国务院计划主管部门共同确定，报国务院批准施行。

**Article 6** Mineral resources compensation fees shall be levied at the rates specified in the appendix to these Provisions.  
The adjustment of the rates of mineral resources compensation fees shall be jointly determined by the finance department under the State Council, the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council, and the competent department of planning under the State Council, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval before coming into force.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　矿产资源补偿费由地质矿产主管部门会同财政部门征收。  
矿区在县级行政区域内的，矿产资源补偿费由矿区所在地的县级人民政府负责地质矿产管理工作的部门负责征收。  
矿区范围跨县级以上行政区域的，矿产资源补偿费由所涉及行政区域的共同上一级人民政府负责地质矿产管理工作的部门负责征收。  
矿区范围跨省级行政区域和在中华人民共和国领海与其他管辖海域的，矿产资源补偿费由国务院地质矿产主管部门授权的省级人民政府地质矿产主管部门负责征收。

**Article 7** Mineral resources compensation fees shall be collected by the competent department of geology and mineral resources in conjunction with the finance department.  
Where the mining area is within a county-level administrative region, the mineral resources compensation fees shall be collected by the department responsible for geological and mineral management under the people's government of the county where the mining area is located.  
Where the mining area spans administrative regions at or above the county level, the mineral resources compensation fees shall be collected by the department responsible for geological and mineral management under the common higher-level people's government of the administrative regions involved.  
Where the mining area spans provincial-level administrative regions or is within the territorial sea of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction, the mineral resources compensation fees shall be collected by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the provincial people's government authorized by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　采矿权人应当于每年的7月31日前缴纳上半年的矿产资源补偿费；于下一年度1月31日前缴纳上一年度下半年的矿产资源补偿费。  
采矿权人在中止或者终止采矿活动时，应当结缴矿产资源补偿费。

**Article 8** A mining right holder shall pay the mineral resources compensation fee for the first half of the year before July 31 of each year; and shall pay the mineral resources compensation fee for the second half of the preceding year before January 31 of the following year.  
When a mining right holder suspends or terminates mining activities, it shall settle and pay the mineral resources compensation fee.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　采矿权人在缴纳矿产资源补偿费时，应当同时提交已采出的矿产品的矿种、产量、销售数量、销售价格和实际开采回采率等资料。

**Article 9** When paying the mineral resources compensation fee, a mining right holder shall simultaneously submit information such as the type, output, sales volume, sales price and actual mining recovery rate of the mineral products already extracted.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　征收的矿产资源补偿费，应当及时全额上缴，并按照下款规定的中央与省、自治区、直辖市的分成比例分别入库，年终不再结算。  
中央与省、直辖市矿产资源补偿费的分成比例为5∶5；中央与自治区矿产资源补偿费的分成比例为4∶6。

**Article 10** The collected mineral resources compensation fees shall be turned over in full in a timely manner and shall be deposited into the respective treasuries according to the sharing ratios between the central government and the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government as prescribed in the following paragraph, and no year-end settlement shall be made.  
The sharing ratio of mineral resources compensation fees between the central government and a province or municipality directly under the Central Government is 5:5; the sharing ratio between the central government and an autonomous region is 4:6.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　矿产资源补偿费纳入国家预算，实行专项管理，主要用于矿产资源勘查。  
中央所得的矿产资源补偿费的具体使用管理办法，由国务院财政部门、国务院地质矿产主管部门、国务院计划主管部门共同制定。  
地方所得的矿产资源补偿费的具体使用管理办法，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。

**Article 11** Mineral resources compensation fees shall be included in the State budget, subject to special management, and mainly used for mineral resources exploration.  
The specific measures for the management of the use of the mineral resources compensation fees obtained by the central government shall be jointly formulated by the finance department under the State Council, the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council, and the competent department of planning under the State Council.  
The specific measures for the management of the use of the mineral resources compensation fees obtained by local governments shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　采矿权人有下列情形之一的，经省级人民政府地质矿产主管部门会同同级财政部门批准，可以免缴矿产资源补偿费：  
(一)从废石(矸石)中回收矿产品的；  
(二)按照国家有关规定经批准开采已关闭矿山的非保安残留矿体的；  
(三)国务院地质矿产主管部门会同国务院财政部门认定免缴的其他情形。

**Article 12** A mining right holder may be exempted from paying mineral resources compensation fees with the approval of the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the provincial people's government in conjunction with the finance department at the same level under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) recovering mineral products from waste rock (gangue);  
(2) mining non-safety residual ore bodies of closed mines with approval in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; or  
(3) other circumstances recognized by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council in conjunction with the finance department under the State Council as qualifying for exemption.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　采矿权人有下列情况之一的，经省级人民政府地质矿产主管部门会同同级财政部门批准，可以减缴矿产资源补偿费：  
(一)从尾矿中回收矿产品的；  
(二)开采未达到工业品位或者未计算储量的低品位矿产资源的；  
(三)依法开采水体下、建筑物下、交通要道下的矿产资源的；  
(四)由于执行国家定价而形成政策性亏损的；  
(五)国务院地质矿产主管部门会同国务院财政部门认定减缴的其他情形。  
采矿权人减缴的矿产资源补偿费超过应当缴纳的矿产资源补偿费50%的，须经省级人民政府批准。  
批准减缴矿产资源补偿费的，应当报国务院地质矿产主管部门和国务院财政部门备案。

**Article 13** A mining right holder may have the mineral resources compensation fee reduced with the approval of the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the provincial people's government in conjunction with the finance department at the same level under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) recovering mineral products from tailings;  
(2) mining low-grade mineral resources that do not reach industrial grades or for which reserves have not been calculated;  
(3) lawfully mining mineral resources under water bodies, buildings, or major traffic routes;  
(4) incurring policy-related losses due to implementation of State-fixed prices; or  
(5) other circumstances recognized by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council in conjunction with the finance department under the State Council as qualifying for reduction.  
Where the reduction of the mineral resources compensation fee for a mining right holder exceeds 50% of the amount otherwise payable, approval by the provincial people's government is required.  
Where a reduction of the mineral resources compensation fee is approved, it shall be reported to the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council and the finance department under the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　采矿权人在规定期限内未足额缴纳矿产资源补偿费的，由征收机关责令限期缴纳，并从滞纳之日起按日加收滞纳补偿费2‰的滞纳金。  
采矿权人未按照前款规定缴纳矿产资源补偿费和滞纳金的，由征收机关处以应当缴纳的矿产资源补偿费3倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，由采矿许可证颁发机关吊销其采矿许可证。

**Article 14** Where a mining right holder fails to pay the full amount of the mineral resources compensation fee within the prescribed time limit, the collecting authority shall order it to pay within a specified period, and shall impose a late payment surcharge of 2‰ of the overdue compensation fee per day from the date the payment becomes overdue.  
Where a mining right holder fails to pay the mineral resources compensation fee and the late payment surcharge in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the collecting authority shall impose a fine of not more than three times the amount of the mineral resources compensation fee payable; if the circumstances are serious, the mining license issuing authority shall revoke its mining license.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　采矿权人采取伪报矿种，隐匿产量、销售数量，或者伪报销售价格、实际开采回采率等手段，不缴或者少缴矿产资源补偿费的，由征收机关追缴应当缴纳的矿产资源补偿费，并处以应当缴纳的矿产资源补偿费5倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，由采矿许可证颁发机关吊销其采矿许可证。

**Article 15** Where a mining right holder fails to pay or underpays the mineral resources compensation fee by means such as falsely reporting the mineral type, concealing the output or sales volume, or falsely reporting the sales price or actual mining recovery rate, the collecting authority shall recover the mineral resources compensation fee payable and impose a fine of not more than five times the amount of the mineral resources compensation fee payable; if the circumstances are serious, the mining license issuing authority shall revoke its mining license.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　采矿权人未按照[本规定第九条](#art-9)的规定报送有关资料的，由征收机关责令限期报送；逾期不报送的，处以5,000元以下罚款；仍不报送的，采矿许可证颁发机关可以吊销其采矿许可证。

**Article 16** Where a mining right holder fails to submit the relevant information in accordance with Article 9 of these Provisions, the collecting authority shall order it to do so within a specified period; if it fails to submit within the specified period, a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed; if it still fails to submit, the mining license issuing authority may revoke its mining license.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　依照本规定对采矿权人处以的罚款、加收的滞纳金应当上缴国库。

**Article 17** Fines imposed and late payment surcharges collected from mining right holders in accordance with these Provisions shall be turned over to the State treasury.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　当事人对行政处罚决定不服的，可以自接到处罚决定通知之日起15日内向作出处罚决定的机关的上一级机关申请复议；当事人也可以自接到处罚决定通知之日起15日内直接向人民法院起诉。  
当事人逾期不申请复议也不向人民法院起诉、又不履行处罚决定的，作出处罚决定的机关可以申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 18** Where a party is dissatisfied with an administrative penalty decision, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice of the penalty decision, apply for reconsideration to the authority at the next higher level of the authority that made the penalty decision; the party may also, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice of the penalty decision, directly initiate a prosecution in the People's Court.  
If a party neither applies for reconsideration nor initiates a prosecution in the People's Court within the time limit, nor complies with the penalty decision, the authority that made the penalty decision may apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本规定发布前的地方性法规和地方人民政府发布的规章及行政性文件的内容，与本规定相抵触的，以本规定为准。

**Article 19** Where the contents of local regulations and rules and administrative documents issued by local people's governments prior to the promulgation of these Provisions are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本规定制定实施办法。

**Article 20** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate implementing measures in accordance with these Provisions.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本规定由地质矿产部负责解释。

**Article 21** These Provisions shall be interpreted by the Ministry of Geology and Mineral Resources.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本规定自1994年4月1日起施行。

**Article 22** These Provisions shall come into force as of April 1, 1994.
