# 农田水利条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2016-05-17  
**生效日期**：2016-07-01

> (2016年4月27日国务院第131次常务会议通过　2016年5月17日中华人民共和国国务院令第669号公布　自2016年7月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加快农田水利发展，提高农业综合生产能力，保障国家粮食安全，制定本条例。 第二条　农田水利规划的编制实施、农田水利工程建设和运行维护、农田灌溉和排水等活动，适用本条例。 本条例所称农田水利，是指为防治农田旱、涝、渍和盐碱灾害，改善农业生产条件，采取的灌溉、排水等工程措施和其他相关措施。 第三条　发展农田水利，坚持政府主导、科学规划、因地制宜、节水高效、建管并重的原则。 县级以上人民政府应当加强对农田水利工作的组织领导，采取措施保障农田水利发展。 第四条　国务院水行政主管部门负责全国农田水利的管理和监督工作。国务院有关部门按照职责分工做好农田水利相关工作。 县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门负责本行政区域农田水利的管理和监督工作。县级以上地方人民政府有关部门按照职责分工做好农田水利相关工作。 乡镇人民政府应当协助上级人民政府及其有关部门做好本行政区域农田水利工程建设和运行维护等方面的工作。 第五条　国家鼓励和引导农村集体经济组织、农民用水合作组织、农民和其他社会力量进行农田水利工程建设、经营和运行维护，保护农田水利工程设施，节约用水，保护生态环境。 国家依法保护农田水利工程投资者的合法权益。 第二章　规划 第六条　国务院水行政主管部门负责编制全国农田水利规划，征求国务院有关部门意见后，报国务院或者国务院授权的部门批准公布。 县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门负责编制本行政区域农田水利规划，征求本级人民政府有关部门意见后，报本级人民政府批准公布。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加快农田水利发展，提高农业综合生产能力，保障国家粮食安全，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of accelerating the development of farmland water conservancy, improving comprehensive agricultural production capacity, and ensuring national food security.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　农田水利规划的编制实施、农田水利工程建设和运行维护、农田灌溉和排水等活动，适用本条例。  
本条例所称农田水利，是指为防治农田旱、涝、渍和盐碱灾害，改善农业生产条件，采取的灌溉、排水等工程措施和其他相关措施。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the formulation and implementation of farmland water conservancy plans, the construction, operation and maintenance of farmland water conservancy projects, and farmland irrigation and drainage activities.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "farmland water conservancy" means engineering measures such as irrigation and drainage and other relevant measures taken to prevent and control drought, waterlogging, waterlogging-induced salinization, and alkali damage on farmland, and to improve agricultural production conditions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　发展农田水利，坚持政府主导、科学规划、因地制宜、节水高效、建管并重的原则。  
县级以上人民政府应当加强对农田水利工作的组织领导，采取措施保障农田水利发展。

**Article 3** The development of farmland water conservancy shall adhere to the principles of government leadership, scientific planning, adaptation to local conditions, water-saving and high efficiency, and equal emphasis on construction and management.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen organizational leadership over farmland water conservancy work and take measures to ensure the development of farmland water conservancy.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院水行政主管部门负责全国农田水利的管理和监督工作。国务院有关部门按照职责分工做好农田水利相关工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门负责本行政区域农田水利的管理和监督工作。县级以上地方人民政府有关部门按照职责分工做好农田水利相关工作。  
乡镇人民政府应当协助上级人民政府及其有关部门做好本行政区域农田水利工程建设和运行维护等方面的工作。

**Article 4** The water administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the administration and supervision of farmland water conservancy nationwide. The relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, properly carry out relevant work on farmland water conservancy.  
The water administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the administration and supervision of farmland water conservancy within their respective administrative regions. The relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, properly carry out relevant work on farmland water conservancy.  
Township people's governments shall assist the people's governments at higher levels and their relevant departments in properly carrying out work such as the construction, operation and maintenance of farmland water conservancy projects within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家鼓励和引导农村集体经济组织、农民用水合作组织、农民和其他社会力量进行农田水利工程建设、经营和运行维护，保护农田水利工程设施，节约用水，保护生态环境。  
国家依法保护农田水利工程投资者的合法权益。

**Article 5** The State shall encourage and guide rural collective economic organizations, farmers' water user cooperative organizations, farmers, and other social forces to construct, operate, and maintain farmland water conservancy projects, protect farmland water conservancy project facilities, conserve water, and protect the ecological environment.  
The State shall, according to law, protect the lawful rights and interests of investors in farmland water conservancy projects.

### 第二章　规划

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院水行政主管部门负责编制全国农田水利规划，征求国务院有关部门意见后，报国务院或者国务院授权的部门批准公布。  
县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门负责编制本行政区域农田水利规划，征求本级人民政府有关部门意见后，报本级人民政府批准公布。

**Article 6** The water administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for formulating a national farmland water conservancy plan. After soliciting opinions from the relevant departments of the State Council, it shall submit the plan to the State Council or a department authorized by the State Council for approval and promulgation.  
The water administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for formulating farmland water conservancy plans for their respective administrative regions. After soliciting opinions from the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level, they shall submit the plans to the people's government at the same level for approval and promulgation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　编制农田水利规划应当统筹考虑经济社会发展水平、水土资源供需平衡、农业生产需求、灌溉排水发展需求、环境保护等因素。  
农田水利规划应当包括发展思路、总体任务、区域布局、保障措施等内容；县级农田水利规划还应当包括水源保障、工程布局、工程规模、生态环境影响、工程建设和运行维护、技术推广、资金筹措等内容。

**Article 7** In formulating a farmland water conservancy plan, overall consideration shall be given to factors such as the level of economic and social development, the balance between supply and demand of water and soil resources, agricultural production needs, the development needs for irrigation and drainage, and environmental protection.  
A farmland water conservancy plan shall include contents such as development ideas, overall tasks, regional layout, and safeguard measures; a county-level farmland water conservancy plan shall also include contents such as water source assurance, project layout, project scale, ecological and environmental impact, project construction, operation and maintenance, technology promotion, and fund raising.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上人民政府应当组织开展农田水利调查。农田水利调查结果是编制农田水利规划的依据。  
县级人民政府水行政主管部门编制农田水利规划，应当征求农村集体经济组织、农民用水合作组织、农民等方面的意见。

**Article 8** People's governments at or above the county level shall organize and carry out farmland water conservancy surveys. The results of farmland water conservancy surveys shall serve as the basis for formulating farmland water conservancy plans.  
When a water administrative department of a county people's government formulates a farmland water conservancy plan, it shall solicit opinions from rural collective economic organizations, farmers' water user cooperative organizations, farmers, and other parties.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　下级农田水利规划应当根据上级农田水利规划编制，并向上一级人民政府水行政主管部门备案。  
经批准的农田水利规划是农田水利建设和管理的依据。农田水利规划确需修改的，应当按照原审批程序报送审批。

**Article 9** A lower-level farmland water conservancy plan shall be formulated in accordance with the higher-level farmland water conservancy plan and shall be filed with the water administrative department of the people's government at the next higher level.  
The approved farmland water conservancy plan shall serve as the basis for farmland water conservancy construction and management. If a farmland water conservancy plan truly needs to be revised, it shall be submitted for approval in accordance with the original approval procedure.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门和其他有关部门按照职责分工负责实施农田水利规划。  
县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门应当会同本级人民政府有关部门对农田水利规划实施情况进行评估，并将评估结果向本级人民政府报告。

**Article 10** The water administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for implementing the farmland water conservancy plan.  
The water administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level, evaluate the implementation of the farmland water conservancy plan and report the evaluation results to the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　编制土地整治、农业综合开发等规划涉及农田水利，应当与农田水利规划相衔接，并征求本级人民政府水行政主管部门的意见。

**Article 11** When formulating plans for land consolidation, comprehensive agricultural development, and other matters that involve farmland water conservancy, such plans shall be consistent with the farmland water conservancy plan, and opinions of the water administrative department of the people's government at the same level shall be solicited.

### 第三章　工程建设

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级人民政府应当根据农田水利规划组织制定农田水利工程建设年度实施计划，统筹协调有关部门和单位安排的与农田水利有关的各类工程建设项目。  
乡镇人民政府应当协调农村集体经济组织、农民用水合作组织以及其他社会力量开展农田水利工程建设的有关工作。

**Article 12** The people's government at the county level shall, based on the farmland water conservancy plan, organize the formulation of an annual implementation plan for construction of farmland water conservancy projects, and shall coordinate, under an overall plan, various types of construction projects related to farmland water conservancy arranged by relevant departments and entities.  
The people's government at the township level shall coordinate rural collective economic organizations, farmers' water use cooperative organizations, and other social forces in carrying out relevant work for the construction of farmland water conservancy projects.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　农田水利工程建设应当符合国家有关农田水利标准。  
农田水利标准由国务院标准化主管部门、水行政主管部门以及省、自治区、直辖市人民政府标准化主管部门、水行政主管部门依照法定程序和权限组织制定。

**Article 13** Construction of farmland water conservancy projects shall comply with the relevant national standards for farmland water conservancy.  
The standards for farmland water conservancy shall be formulated by the standardization administrative department and the water administrative department of the State Council, as well as the standardization administrative departments and water administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, in accordance with statutory procedures and authority.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　农田水利工程建设应当节约集约使用土地。县级以上人民政府应当根据农田水利规划，保障农田水利工程建设用地需求。

**Article 14** Construction of farmland water conservancy projects shall make economical and intensive use of land. The people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the farmland water conservancy plan, ensure the land use demand for construction of farmland water conservancy projects.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　农田水利工程建设单位应当建立健全工程质量安全管理制度，对工程质量安全负责，并公示工程建设情况。  
县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门和其他有关部门应当按照职责分工加强对农田水利工程建设的监督管理。

**Article 15** The entity responsible for construction of a farmland water conservancy project shall establish and improve a quality and safety management system for the project, be responsible for the quality and safety of the project, and shall disclose the project construction information.  
The water administrative department and other relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective duties, strengthen supervision and administration over the construction of farmland water conservancy projects.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　政府投资建设的农田水利工程由县级以上人民政府有关部门组织竣工验收，并邀请有关专家和农村集体经济组织、农民用水合作组织、农民代表参加。社会力量投资建设的农田水利工程由投资者或者受益者组织竣工验收。政府与社会力量共同投资的农田水利工程，由县级以上人民政府有关部门、社会投资者或者受益者共同组织竣工验收。  
大中型农田水利工程应当按照水利建设工程验收规程组织竣工验收。小型农田水利工程验收办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门会同有关部门制定。  
农田水利工程验收合格后，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门组织造册存档。

**Article 16** For a farmland water conservancy project invested and constructed by the government, the relevant department of the people's government at or above the county level shall organize the completion and acceptance inspection, and shall invite relevant experts and representatives of rural collective economic organizations, farmers' water use cooperative organizations, and farmers to participate. For a farmland water conservancy project invested and constructed by social forces, the investor or beneficiary shall organize the completion and acceptance inspection. For a farmland water conservancy project jointly invested by the government and social forces, the relevant department of the people's government at or above the county level, together with the social investor or beneficiary, shall jointly organize the completion and acceptance inspection.  
Large and medium-sized farmland water conservancy projects shall undergo completion and acceptance inspection in accordance with the procedures for acceptance inspection of water conservancy construction projects. The acceptance inspection measures for small farmland water conservancy projects shall be formulated by the water administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with relevant departments.  
After a farmland water conservancy project passes the acceptance inspection, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall make a file and keep it on record.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门应当会同有关部门加强农田水利信息系统建设，收集与发布农田水利规划、农田水利工程建设和运行维护等信息。

**Article 17** The water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, jointly with relevant departments, strengthen the construction of the farmland water conservancy information system, and collect and release information on farmland water conservancy plans, construction of farmland water conservancy projects, operation and maintenance, and the like.

### 第四章　工程运行维护

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　农田水利工程按照下列规定确定运行维护主体：  
(一)政府投资建设的大中型农田水利工程，由县级以上人民政府按照工程管理权限确定的单位负责运行维护，鼓励通过政府购买服务等方式引进社会力量参与运行维护；  
(二)政府投资建设或者财政补助建设的小型农田水利工程，按照规定交由受益农村集体经济组织、农民用水合作组织、农民等使用和管理的，由受益者或者其委托的单位、个人负责运行维护；  
(三)农村集体经济组织筹资筹劳建设的农田水利工程，由农村集体经济组织或者其委托的单位、个人负责运行维护；  
(四)农民或者其他社会力量投资建设的农田水利工程，由投资者或者其委托的单位、个人负责运行维护；  
(五)政府与社会力量共同投资建设的农田水利工程，由投资者按照约定确定运行维护主体。  
农村土地承包经营权依法流转的，应当同时明确该土地上农田水利工程的运行维护主体。

**Article 18** The operation and maintenance entity for a farmland water conservancy project shall be determined according to the following provisions:  
(1) For a large or medium-sized farmland water conservancy project invested and constructed by the government, the entity designated by the people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the project management authority shall be responsible for operation and maintenance. The introduction of social forces to participate in operation and maintenance through means such as government procurement of services shall be encouraged.  
(2) For a small farmland water conservancy project invested and constructed by the government or funded by government subsidies, which is, in accordance with the provisions, transferred to and used and managed by the benefiting rural collective economic organization, farmers' water use cooperative organization, or farmers, the beneficiary or the entity or individual entrusted thereby shall be responsible for operation and maintenance.  
(3) For a farmland water conservancy project constructed through fund-raising and labor contributions by a rural collective economic organization, the rural collective economic organization or the entity or individual entrusted thereby shall be responsible for operation and maintenance.  
(4) For a farmland water conservancy project invested and constructed by a farmer or other social force, the investor or the entity or individual entrusted thereby shall be responsible for operation and maintenance.  
(5) For a farmland water conservancy project jointly invested by the government and a social force, the investor shall determine the operation and maintenance entity by agreement.  
Where the right to the contractual management of rural land is transferred according to law, the operation and maintenance entity for the farmland water conservancy project on the land shall be specified at the same time.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　灌区农田水利工程实行灌区管理单位管理与受益农村集体经济组织、农民用水合作组织、农民等管理相结合的方式。灌区管理办法由国务院水行政主管部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 19** Farmland water conservancy projects in irrigation districts shall be managed by combining the management of the irrigation district management entity with the management of the benefiting rural collective economic organizations, farmers' water use cooperative organizations, farmers, and others. The measures for the administration of irrigation districts shall be formulated by the water administrative department of the State Council jointly with relevant departments.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级以上人民政府应当建立农田水利工程运行维护经费合理负担机制。  
农田水利工程所有权人应当落实农田水利工程运行维护经费，保障运行维护工作正常进行。

**Article 20** The people's government at or above the county level shall establish a mechanism for reasonably sharing the costs of operation and maintenance of farmland water conservancy projects.  
The owner of a farmland water conservancy project shall ensure the appropriation of funds for the operation and maintenance of the project, and guarantee the normal conduct of operation and maintenance.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　负责农田水利工程运行维护的单位和个人应当建立健全运行维护制度，加强对农田水利工程的日常巡查、维修和养护，按照有关规定进行调度，保障农田水利工程正常运行。  
农田水利工程水量调度涉及航道通航的，应当符合《中华人民共和国航道法》的有关规定。

**Article 21** The entities and individuals responsible for the operation and maintenance of farmland water conservancy projects shall establish and improve the operation and maintenance system, strengthen the daily inspection, repair and maintenance of farmland water conservancy projects, and carry out dispatching in accordance with the relevant provisions to ensure the normal operation of farmland water conservancy projects.  
Where water quantity dispatching of a farmland water conservancy project involves navigation of waterways, it shall comply with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Waterways.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门和农田水利工程所有权人应当加强对农田水利工程运行维护工作的监督，督促负责运行维护的单位和个人履行运行维护责任。  
农村集体经济组织、农民用水合作组织、农民等发现影响农田水利工程正常运行的情形的，有权向县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门和农田水利工程所有权人报告。接到报告的县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门和农田水利工程所有权人应当督促负责运行维护的单位和个人及时处理。

**Article 22** The water administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and the owners of farmland water conservancy projects shall strengthen the supervision over the operation and maintenance of farmland water conservancy projects, and urge the entities and individuals responsible for operation and maintenance to perform their operation and maintenance responsibilities.  
Where rural collective economic organizations, farmers' water user associations, farmers, etc. discover any circumstances affecting the normal operation of a farmland water conservancy project, they shall have the right to report to the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level and the owner of the farmland water conservancy project. The water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level and the owner of the farmland water conservancy project that receive the report shall urge the entities and individuals responsible for operation and maintenance to handle the matter in a timely manner.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　禁止危害农田水利工程设施的下列行为：  
(一)侵占、损毁农田水利工程设施；  
(二)危害农田水利工程设施安全的爆破、打井、采石、取土等活动；  
(三)堆放阻碍蓄水、输水、排水的物体；  
(四)建设妨碍蓄水、输水、排水的建筑物和构筑物；  
(五)向塘坝、沟渠排放污水、倾倒垃圾以及其他废弃物。

**Article 23** The following acts that endanger farmland water conservancy project facilities are prohibited:  
(1) Encroachment upon or damage to farmland water conservancy project facilities;  
(2) Activities such as blasting, well digging, quarrying, and soil extraction that endanger the safety of farmland water conservancy project facilities;  
(3) Stacking objects that impede water storage, water conveyance, or drainage;  
(4) Constructing buildings and structures that obstruct water storage, water conveyance, or drainage;  
(5) Discharging sewage, dumping garbage, or other wastes into ponds, dams, or ditches.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　任何单位和个人不得擅自占用农业灌溉水源、农田水利工程设施。  
新建、改建、扩建建设工程确需占用农业灌溉水源、农田水利工程设施的，应当与取用水的单位、个人或者农田水利工程所有权人协商，并报经有管辖权的县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门同意。  
占用者应当建设与被占用的农田水利工程设施效益和功能相当的替代工程；不具备建设替代工程条件的，应当按照建设替代工程的总投资额支付占用补偿费；造成运行成本增加等其他损失的，应当依法给予补偿。补偿标准由省、自治区、直辖市制定。

**Article 24** No entity or individual may occupy agricultural irrigation water sources or farmland water conservancy project facilities without authorization.  
Where the construction of a new project, the reconstruction or expansion of an existing project indeed requires the occupation of agricultural irrigation water sources or farmland water conservancy project facilities, the entity or individual shall negotiate with the entity or individual that uses the water or the owner of the farmland water conservancy project, and shall report to the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level with jurisdiction for approval.  
The occupier shall construct a substitute project with benefits and functions equivalent to those of the occupied farmland water conservancy project facilities; if the conditions for constructing a substitute project are not met, the occupier shall pay an occupation compensation fee based on the total investment amount for constructing such a substitute project; if other losses such as increased operation costs are caused, compensation shall be made according to law. The compensation standards shall be formulated by the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　农田水利工程设施因超过设计使用年限、灌溉排水功能基本丧失或者严重毁坏而无法继续使用的，工程所有权人或者管理单位应当按照有关规定及时处置，消除安全隐患，并将相关情况告知县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门。

**Article 25** Where farmland water conservancy project facilities can no longer be used due to exceeding their designed service life, basic loss of irrigation and drainage functions, or severe damage, the project owner or the management unit shall dispose of them in a timely manner in accordance with the relevant provisions, eliminate safety hazards, and inform the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level of the relevant information.

### 第五章　灌溉排水管理

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门应当加强对农田灌溉排水的监督和指导，做好技术服务。

**Article 26** The water administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the supervision and guidance over agricultural irrigation and drainage, and provide technical services.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　农田灌溉用水实行总量控制和定额管理相结合的制度。  
农作物灌溉用水定额依照《中华人民共和国水法》规定的权限和程序制定并公布。  
农田灌溉用水应当合理确定水价，实行有偿使用、计量收费。

**Article 27** A system combining total quantity control and quota management shall be implemented for agricultural irrigation water use.  
The irrigation water quotas for crops shall be formulated and published in accordance with the authority and procedures stipulated in the Water Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The water price for agricultural irrigation shall be reasonably determined, and a system of paid use and metered charging shall be implemented.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　灌区管理单位应当根据有管辖权的县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门核定的年度取用水计划，制定灌区内用水计划和调度方案，与用水户签订用水协议。

**Article 28** The management unit of an irrigation district shall, based on the annual water intake and use plan verified by the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level with jurisdiction, formulate a water use plan and a dispatching scheme within the irrigation district, and sign water use agreements with water users.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　农田灌溉用水应当符合相应的水质标准。县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门应当会同水行政主管部门、农业主管部门加强对农田灌溉用水的水质监测。

**Article 29** Agricultural irrigation water shall meet the corresponding water quality standards. The environmental protection departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with the water administrative departments and agricultural departments, strengthen the water quality monitoring of agricultural irrigation water.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家鼓励采取先进适用的农田排水技术和措施，促进盐碱地和中低产田改造；控制和合理利用农田排水，减少肥料流失，防止农业面源污染。

**Article 30** The State encourages the adoption of advanced and applicable farmland drainage technologies and measures to promote the improvement of saline-alkali land and low- and medium-yield farmland; control and rationally utilize farmland drainage to reduce fertilizer loss and prevent agricultural non-point source pollution.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门应当组织做好本行政区域农田灌溉排水试验工作。灌溉试验站应当做好农田灌溉排水试验研究，加强科技成果示范推广，指导用水户科学灌溉排水。

**Article 31** The water administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the work of farmland irrigation and drainage experiments in its administrative region. Irrigation experiment stations shall conduct research on farmland irrigation and drainage experiments, strengthen the demonstration and promotion of scientific and technological achievements, and guide water users in scientific irrigation and drainage.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家鼓励推广应用喷灌、微灌、管道输水灌溉、渠道防渗输水灌溉等节水灌溉技术，以及先进的农机、农艺和生物技术等，提高灌溉用水效率。

**Article 32** The state encourages the popularization and application of water-saving irrigation technologies such as sprinkler irrigation, micro-irrigation, pipeline water conveyance irrigation, canal seepage control irrigation, as well as advanced agricultural machinery, agronomy, and biotechnology, to improve irrigation water use efficiency.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　粮食主产区和严重缺水、生态环境脆弱地区以及地下水超采地区应当优先发展节水灌溉。  
国家鼓励企业、农村集体经济组织、农民用水合作组织等单位和个人投资建设节水灌溉设施，采取财政补助等方式鼓励购买节水灌溉设备。

**Article 33** Major grain-producing areas, areas with severe water shortage, ecologically fragile areas, and areas with over-extraction of groundwater shall prioritize the development of water-saving irrigation.  
The state encourages enterprises, rural collective economic organizations, farmers' water user cooperative organizations, and other entities and individuals to invest in the construction of water-saving irrigation facilities, and adopts financial subsidies and other means to encourage the purchase of water-saving irrigation equipment.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　规划建设商品粮、棉、油、菜等农业生产基地，应当充分考虑当地水资源条件。水资源短缺地区，限制发展高耗水作物；地下水超采区，禁止农田灌溉新增取用地下水。

**Article 34** The planning and construction of agricultural production bases for commercial grain, cotton, oil, vegetables, etc., shall give full consideration to local water resource conditions. In areas with water shortage, the development of crops with high water consumption shall be restricted; in areas with over-extraction of groundwater, new extraction of groundwater for farmland irrigation shall be prohibited.

### 第六章　保障与扶持

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　农田水利工程建设实行政府投入和社会力量投入相结合的方式。  
县级以上人民政府应当多渠道筹措农田水利工程建设资金，保障农田水利建设投入。

**Article 35** The construction of farmland water conservancy projects shall adopt a combination of government investment and social investment.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall raise funds for the construction of farmland water conservancy projects through multiple channels to ensure investment in farmland water conservancy construction.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　县级人民政府应当及时公布农田水利工程建设年度实施计划、建设条件、补助标准等信息，引导社会力量参与建设农田水利工程。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当支持社会力量通过提供农田灌溉服务、收取供水水费等方式，开展农田水利工程经营活动，保障其合法经营收益。  
县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门应当为社会力量参与建设、经营农田水利工程提供指导和技术支持。

**Article 36** The people's government at the county level shall promptly publish information such as the annual implementation plan, construction conditions, and subsidy standards for farmland water conservancy project construction, and guide social forces to participate in the construction of farmland water conservancy projects.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall support social forces in carrying out business activities of farmland water conservancy projects by providing farmland irrigation services, collecting water supply fees, etc., and ensure their legitimate business income.  
The water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall provide guidance and technical support for social forces to participate in the construction and operation of farmland water conservancy projects.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家引导金融机构推出符合农田水利工程项目特点的金融产品和服务方式，加大对农田水利工程建设的信贷支持力度。  
农田灌溉和排水的用电执行农业生产用电价格。

**Article 37** The state guides financial institutions to launch financial products and service methods that conform to the characteristics of farmland water conservancy projects, and increases credit support for the construction of farmland water conservancy projects.  
The electricity used for farmland irrigation and drainage shall be charged at the price for agricultural production electricity.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　县级人民政府应当建立健全基层水利服务体系，将基层水利服务机构公益性业务经费纳入本级政府预算。基层水利服务机构应当履行农田水利建设管理、科技推广等公益性职能。  
国家通过政府购买服务等方式，支持专业化服务组织开展农田灌溉和排水、农田水利工程设施维修等公益性工作。

**Article 38** The people's government at the county level shall establish and improve the grassroots water conservancy service system, and incorporate the public service business funds of grassroots water conservancy service institutions into the government budget at the same level. Grassroots water conservancy service institutions shall perform public service functions such as farmland water conservancy construction management and science and technology promotion.  
The state supports professional service organizations in carrying out public service work such as farmland irrigation and drainage, and maintenance of farmland water conservancy project facilities through government procurement of services and other means.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门应当会同本级人民政府有关部门，制定农田水利新技术推广目录和培训计划，加强对基层水利服务人员和农民的培训。

**Article 39** The water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level, formulate a directory for the promotion of new farmland water conservancy technologies and training plans, and strengthen training for grassroots water conservancy service personnel and farmers.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　对农田水利工作中成绩显著的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰。

**Article 40** Units and individuals that have achieved outstanding results in farmland water conservancy work shall be commended in accordance with the relevant regulations of the state.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本条例规定，县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门和其他有关部门不依法履行农田水利管理和监督职责的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分；负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 41** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the competent water administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level and other relevant departments do not perform their duties of farmland water conservancy management and supervision according to law, the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本条例规定，县级以上人民政府确定的农田水利工程运行维护单位不按照规定进行维修养护和调度、不执行年度取用水计划的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门责令改正；发生责任事故或者造成其他重大损失的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 42** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the operation and maintenance unit of farmland water conservancy projects determined by a people's government at or above the county level fails to carry out maintenance, repair, and scheduling in accordance with the provisions, or fails to implement the annual water intake and use plan, the competent water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections; if a liability accident occurs or causes other major losses, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状或者采取补救措施；逾期不恢复原状或者采取补救措施的，依法强制执行；造成损失的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)堆放阻碍农田水利工程设施蓄水、输水、排水的物体；  
(二)建设妨碍农田水利工程设施蓄水、输水、排水的建筑物和构筑物；  
(三)擅自占用农业灌溉水源、农田水利工程设施。

**Article 43** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any of the following acts is committed, the competent water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act, and order restoration to the original condition or adoption of remedial measures within a specified time limit; if the original condition is not restored or remedial measures are not taken within the time limit, compulsory execution shall be carried out according to law; if losses are caused, civil liability shall be borne according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be given according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Stacking objects that impede water storage, water conveyance, or drainage of farmland water conservancy projects and facilities;  
(2) Constructing buildings and structures that hinder water storage, water conveyance, or drainage of farmland water conservancy projects and facilities;  
(3) Unauthorized occupation of agricultural irrigation water sources or farmland water conservancy projects and facilities.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反本条例规定，侵占、损毁农田水利工程设施，以及有危害农田水利工程设施安全的爆破、打井、采石、取土等行为的，依照《中华人民共和国水法》的规定处理。  
违反本条例规定，向塘坝、沟渠排放污水、倾倒垃圾以及其他废弃物的，依照环境保护有关法律、行政法规的规定处理。

**Article 44** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any person encroaches upon or damages farmland water conservancy projects and facilities, or commits acts such as blasting, well-drilling, quarrying, or earth-taking that endanger the safety of farmland water conservancy projects and facilities, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Water Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, any person discharges sewage, dumps garbage or other waste into ponds, dams, or ditches, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant environmental protection laws and administrative regulations.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例自2016年7月1日起施行。

**Article 45** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 2016.
