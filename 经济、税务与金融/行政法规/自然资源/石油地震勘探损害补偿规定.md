# 石油地震勘探损害补偿规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1989-10-17  
**生效日期**：1989-10-17

> (1989年9月26日国务院批准　1989年10月17日中华人民共和国能源部令第1号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了做好石油地震勘探损害补偿工作，正确处理国家、集体、个人三者利益关系，确保石油勘探工作的顺利进行，制定本规定。 第二条　本规定适用于中华人民共和国陆地(包括岛屿、海滩、水深五米的浅海在内)石油地震勘探作业和作业地震波的损害补偿。 第三条　石油地震勘探是沿一定测线通过式作业，在测线每个观测点上通过时间不超过四十八小时，在特殊情况下不超过九十六小时的，免于办理临时用地手续。 第四条　石油地震勘探按照实际损害程度由地震作业队给予受损害的个人或者单位一次性补偿，补偿费用应当及时支付。任何单位或者个人不得截留、回扣补偿费用，或者向地震作业队索要补偿费用之外的费用或者实物。 第五条　地震作业队的主管部门，应当在开工前三十日内将本年度的石油地震勘探施工设计和作业计划向作业区所在地的县级人民政府有关主管部门报告，取得支持和帮助。县级人民政府有关主管部门应当在地震作业队作业前将作业区的地面、地下设施、农田作物等有关资料提供给地震作业队主管部门。 第六条　地震作业队的作业，应当按照国家有关规定在城市区、工矿区、居民点、车站、码头、港口、铁路、军用基地、人防工程、大中型桥涵、水利工程、电力设施、输油输气管线、国家测量标志、重点文物、通讯设施、广播设施的安全距离之外放炮。特殊情况下，如需在上述安全距离内放炮作业，地震作业队应当征得有关部门同意，采取安全措施后，方可施工。 第七条　当事人双方对石油地震勘探损害补偿问题发生纠纷时，首先应当协商解决，协商不成的，提请当地县级人民政府及其指定的部门调解处理；对调解处理决定不服的，任何一方可在接到处理决定之日起十五日内向所在地人民法院起诉，但在调解和诉讼期间，应当保证地震作业队正常作业。 第二章　石油地震勘探作业损害补偿 第八条　石油地震勘探作业(以下简称地震作业)车辆沿测线通过农田，对粮食作物和其他经济作物造成损害的，作物受害量以当地县级人民政府统计部门前三年上报国家产量的年平均数、实际损害面积和实际损害程度计算；作物补偿价格按国家规定的价格和当地市场价格的加权平均值计算，其加权平均值参照定购合同等因素，由双方协商确定，作业车辆损害农田面积以实际碾压宽度和长度计算。 第九条　地震作业车辆沿测线通过造成的农田地面设施损害，根据实际损害程度，由双方协商，给予适当补偿。 第十条　地震作业对封冻期的青苗损害的补偿标准，按作业车辆实际碾压损害程度确定，一般不超过对该地非封冻期作物损害补偿标准的百分之三十，但个别地方青苗碾压严重的，经当事人双方认定，可酌情提高补偿标准。封冻期的具体期限由当地省级以上人民政府确定。 第十一条　地震作业对无封冻期的已耕待种地造成损害的，应按照作业车辆实际碾压面积，参照当地劳务费用和机耕费用的标准补偿复耕费。 第十二条　地震作业对牧区人工种植的草场造成损害的，应按照草场实际损害面积和亩产量，及当地省级人民政府规定的牧草收购价格，予以补偿。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了做好石油地震勘探损害补偿工作，正确处理国家、集体、个人三者利益关系，确保石油勘探工作的顺利进行，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of properly carrying out the damage compensation work for petroleum seismic exploration, correctly handling the interest relations among the state, the collective and the individual, and ensuring the smooth progress of petroleum exploration.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定适用于中华人民共和国陆地(包括岛屿、海滩、水深五米的浅海在内)石油地震勘探作业和作业地震波的损害补偿。

**Article 2** These Provisions shall apply to the damage compensation for petroleum seismic exploration operations and the seismic waves generated thereby on the land (including islands, beaches, and shallow seas with a water depth of five meters) of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　石油地震勘探是沿一定测线通过式作业，在测线每个观测点上通过时间不超过四十八小时，在特殊情况下不超过九十六小时的，免于办理临时用地手续。

**Article 3** Petroleum seismic exploration is a transit operation along a certain survey line. Where the transit time at each observation point on the survey line does not exceed forty-eight hours, or under special circumstances does not exceed ninety-six hours, the procedures for temporary land use shall be exempted.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　石油地震勘探按照实际损害程度由地震作业队给予受损害的个人或者单位一次性补偿，补偿费用应当及时支付。任何单位或者个人不得截留、回扣补偿费用，或者向地震作业队索要补偿费用之外的费用或者实物。

**Article 4** The seismic operation team shall give one-time compensation to the damaged individual or unit according to the actual extent of damage caused by the petroleum seismic exploration, and the compensation fees shall be paid in a timely manner. No unit or individual may withhold or deduct the compensation fees, or demand any fees or materials beyond the compensation fees from the seismic operation team.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　地震作业队的主管部门，应当在开工前三十日内将本年度的石油地震勘探施工设计和作业计划向作业区所在地的县级人民政府有关主管部门报告，取得支持和帮助。县级人民政府有关主管部门应当在地震作业队作业前将作业区的地面、地下设施、农田作物等有关资料提供给地震作业队主管部门。

**Article 5** The competent department of the seismic operation team shall, within thirty days before the commencement of operations, report the annual petroleum seismic exploration construction design and operation plan to the relevant competent department of the people's government at the county level where the operation area is located, so as to obtain support and assistance. The relevant competent department of the people's government at the county level shall, before the seismic operation team commences operations, provide the competent department of the seismic operation team with relevant information on the surface and underground facilities, farmland crops, etc. in the operation area.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　地震作业队的作业，应当按照国家有关规定在城市区、工矿区、居民点、车站、码头、港口、铁路、军用基地、人防工程、大中型桥涵、水利工程、电力设施、输油输气管线、国家测量标志、重点文物、通讯设施、广播设施的安全距离之外放炮。特殊情况下，如需在上述安全距离内放炮作业，地震作业队应当征得有关部门同意，采取安全措施后，方可施工。

**Article 6** The operations of the seismic operation team shall set off explosive charges outside the safety distances from urban areas, industrial and mining areas, residential areas, railway stations, docks, ports, railways, military bases, civil air defense works, large and medium-sized bridges and culverts, water conservancy projects, electric power facilities, oil and gas pipelines, national survey markers, key cultural relics, communication facilities, and broadcasting facilities, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state. Under special circumstances, if it is necessary to set off explosive charges within the aforesaid safety distances, the seismic operation team shall obtain the consent of the relevant departments, and may only commence operations after taking safety measures.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　当事人双方对石油地震勘探损害补偿问题发生纠纷时，首先应当协商解决，协商不成的，提请当地县级人民政府及其指定的部门调解处理；对调解处理决定不服的，任何一方可在接到处理决定之日起十五日内向所在地人民法院起诉，但在调解和诉讼期间，应当保证地震作业队正常作业。

**Article 7** Where a dispute arises between the parties over the damage compensation for petroleum seismic exploration, they shall first attempt to resolve it through negotiation. If negotiation fails, the matter shall be submitted to the local people's government at the county level and the department designated by it for mediation and disposition. If any party is dissatisfied with the decision on mediation and disposition, it may file a prosecution with the local People's Court within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the decision. However, during the period of mediation and litigation, the normal operations of the seismic operation team shall be ensured.

### 第二章　石油地震勘探作业损害补偿

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　石油地震勘探作业(以下简称地震作业)车辆沿测线通过农田，对粮食作物和其他经济作物造成损害的，作物受害量以当地县级人民政府统计部门前三年上报国家产量的年平均数、实际损害面积和实际损害程度计算；作物补偿价格按国家规定的价格和当地市场价格的加权平均值计算，其加权平均值参照定购合同等因素，由双方协商确定，作业车辆损害农田面积以实际碾压宽度和长度计算。

**Article 8** Where vehicles used in petroleum seismic exploration operations (hereinafter referred to as seismic operations) cause damage to grain crops and other cash crops while passing through farmland along the survey line, the amount of crop damage shall be calculated based on the average annual output reported to the state by the statistics department of the local people's government at the county level in the preceding three years, the actual damaged area, and the actual extent of damage. The compensation price for crops shall be calculated based on the weighted average of the price prescribed by the state and the local market price, and the weighted average shall be determined by the parties through negotiation with reference to factors such as the fixed procurement contract. The area of farmland damaged by the operation vehicles shall be calculated based on the actual rolling width and length.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　地震作业车辆沿测线通过造成的农田地面设施损害，根据实际损害程度，由双方协商，给予适当补偿。

**Article 9** For damage to farmland surface facilities caused by seismic operation vehicles passing along the survey line, appropriate compensation shall be given through negotiation between the parties based on the actual extent of damage.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　地震作业对封冻期的青苗损害的补偿标准，按作业车辆实际碾压损害程度确定，一般不超过对该地非封冻期作物损害补偿标准的百分之三十，但个别地方青苗碾压严重的，经当事人双方认定，可酌情提高补偿标准。封冻期的具体期限由当地省级以上人民政府确定。

**Article 10** The compensation standard for damage to young crops during the freezing period caused by seismic operations shall be determined according to the actual rolling damage extent of the operation vehicles, and shall generally not exceed thirty percent of the compensation standard for crop damage in the non-freezing period of the same area. However, where the rolling damage to young crops in a particular area is severe, the compensation standard may be appropriately increased upon confirmation by both parties. The specific period of the freezing period shall be determined by the people's government at or above the provincial level of the locality.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　地震作业对无封冻期的已耕待种地造成损害的，应按照作业车辆实际碾压面积，参照当地劳务费用和机耕费用的标准补偿复耕费。

**Article 11** Where seismic operations cause damage to ploughed land awaiting planting during the non-freezing period, the re-tillage fees shall be compensated according to the actual rolling area of the operation vehicles, with reference to the local labor fees and mechanized plowing fee standards.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　地震作业对牧区人工种植的草场造成损害的，应按照草场实际损害面积和亩产量，及当地省级人民政府规定的牧草收购价格，予以补偿。  
对已承包给个人或集体的天然草场造成损害的，应根据该天然草场的实际产草情况和损害程度，由当事人双方协商补偿办法。补偿费的标准，由当地省级人民政府确定。

**Article 12** Where seismic operations cause damage to artificially planted grasslands in pastoral areas, compensation shall be given according to the actual damaged area and per-mu yield of the grassland, as well as the forage grass procurement price prescribed by the local people's government at the provincial level.  
Where damage is caused to natural grasslands that have been contracted to individuals or collectives, the compensation method shall be negotiated between the parties based on the actual grass production situation and the extent of damage of such natural grasslands. The standard of compensation fees shall be determined by the local people's government at the provincial level.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　地震作业对竹木造成损害的，应对实际损害株数逐株计算，进行补偿。竹木补偿标准由当地省级人民政府确定，被损竹木归原竹木所有者。

**Article 13** Where seismic operations cause damage to bamboos and trees, compensation shall be made by calculating each damaged plant individually based on the actual number of damaged plants. The compensation standard for bamboos and trees shall be determined by the local people's government at the provincial level, and the damaged bamboos and trees shall belong to their original owners.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　地震作业后的炮眼，应当由地震作业队负责回填。地震作业队不回填的，应按照当地省级人民政府规定的标准，支付炮眼回填劳务费。

**Article 14** Shot holes after seismic operations shall be backfilled by the seismic operation team. If the seismic operation team fails to backfill them, it shall pay labor fees for backfilling the shot holes according to the standard prescribed by the local people's government at the provincial level.

### 第三章　地震作业地震波造成损害的补偿

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　地震作业队井中放炮，对其地震作业地震波(以下简称地震波)造成损害的补偿范围规定如下：  
(一)对土坯结构和砖、土坯混合结构的房屋、窑洞、厂房、正常生产的砖窑，炸药量在一至三公斤，地震波损害的补偿范围为距炮点半径四十米以内，炸药量在四至六公斤，地震波损害的补偿范围为距炮点半径五十米以内；炸药量在七至十公斤，地震波损害的补偿范围为距炮点半径七十米以内；炸药量在十一至十五公斤，地震波损害的补偿范围为距炮点半径八十米以内。  
(二)对混凝土结构的厂房建筑、机井、一般桥梁和水闸的地震波损害的补偿范围，比照本条(一)项规定的范围缩小四分之一。

**Article 15** The scope of compensation for damage caused by seismic waves (hereinafter referred to as seismic waves) from in-well detonation by the seismic operation team is stipulated as follows:  
(1) For houses, cave dwellings, factory buildings, and normally operating brick kilns of adobe structure or brick-adobe mixed structure, where the amount of explosives is between one and three kilograms, the scope of compensation for seismic wave damage shall be within a radius of forty meters from the shot point; where the amount of explosives is between four and six kilograms, the scope of compensation for seismic wave damage shall be within a radius of fifty meters from the shot point; where the amount of explosives is between seven and ten kilograms, the scope of compensation for seismic wave damage shall be within a radius of seventy meters from the shot point; where the amount of explosives is between eleven and fifteen kilograms, the scope of compensation for seismic wave damage shall be within a radius of eighty meters from the shot point.  
(2) For factory buildings of concrete structure, motor-pumped wells, ordinary bridges and sluice gates, the scope of compensation for seismic wave damage shall be reduced by one quarter compared to the scope specified in item (1) of this Article.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　在地震波损害的补偿范围内的地面设施，根据地震作业放炮后所造成的损害的程度，参考原造价和使用年限计算补偿费用，予以补偿。

**Article 16** For surface facilities within the scope of compensation for seismic wave damage, compensation fees shall be calculated and compensation shall be given based on the extent of damage caused after the detonation of the seismic operation, with reference to the original cost and service life.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对地震波损害补偿范围内的机井的损害补偿，由当事人双方共同核实损害程度，并参照当地县级人民政府有关部门提供的机井档案资料、造价、使用年限，计算补偿费用予以补偿。

**Article 17** For compensation for damage to motor-pumped wells within the scope of compensation for seismic wave damage, both parties shall jointly verify the extent of damage, and compensation fees shall be calculated and compensation given with reference to the motor-pumped well archive materials, cost, and service life provided by the relevant department of the local people's government at the county level.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　在海滩和浅海区进行地震作业，应当采用非炸药震源；在陆地进行地震作业，应当尽量避开鱼塘或养殖场。  
必须在鱼塘或养殖场内及其附近地区进行地震作业的，炮眼井底至鱼塘或养殖场水底的深度不得小于五米，炸药量不得大于十公斤。  
因地震作业对鱼类和其它水产类造成损害的，应当根据实际损害情况给予一次性补偿，具体补偿办法由国务院石油工业主管部门和农业主管部门另行规定。

**Article 18** Seismic operations conducted on beaches and in shallow sea areas shall use non-explosive sources. Seismic operations conducted on land shall avoid fish ponds or aquaculture farms as far as possible.  
Where it is necessary to conduct seismic operations within or in the vicinity of fish ponds or aquaculture farms, the depth from the bottom of the shot hole to the bottom of the fish pond or aquaculture farm shall not be less than five meters, and the amount of explosives shall not exceed ten kilograms.  
Where damage is caused to fish or other aquatic products due to seismic operations, one-time compensation shall be given according to the actual damage situation. The specific compensation method shall be separately prescribed by the petroleum industry administrative department and the agricultural administrative department under the State Council.

### 第四章　不予补偿的范围

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　地震作业队在没有种植农作物的荒岭、荒坡、荒地、荒漠，以及封冻期的无种植耕地、草原进行石油勘探地震作业的，不予补偿，但对作业区的耕地应当负责复垦，恢复利用。

**Article 19** Where the seismic operation team conducts petroleum seismic exploration operations on barren hills, barren slopes, wasteland, deserts without planted crops, as well as on uncultivated farmland and grasslands during the freezing period, no compensation shall be given. However, the team shall be responsible for reclaiming the farmland in the operation area to restore its use.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　地震作业队在国家和集体所有的闲置地，没有承包的沙滩、沙漠、河滩、湖滩、海滩、森林中的空地进行地震作业的，不予补偿。

**Article 20** Where the seismic operation team conducts seismic operations on idle land owned by the state or the collective, or on un-contracted sandy beaches, deserts, river beaches, lake beaches, sea beaches, or vacant land in forests, no compensation shall be given.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　地震作业队主管部门向地震作业区所在地人民政府提交作业计划后，任何单位和个人故意在作业线上抢种作物、苗木和突击设置地面设施的，其在地震作业后受到损害的，地震作业队不予补偿。

**Article 21** Where, after the competent department of the seismic operation team submits the operation plan to the people's government of the locality where the seismic operation area is located, any unit or individual intentionally plants crops or seedlings in a rush or hastily installs surface facilities on the operation line, and such crops, seedlings or facilities suffer damage after the seismic operations, the seismic operation team shall not give compensation.

### 第五章　罚则

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　地震作业队违反第六条规定，擅自在安全距离内放炮的，应当根据国家有关法律、法规由有关部门对地震作业队责任人员追究行政责任或民事责任；对情节严重构成犯罪的，依法追究其刑事责任。

**Article 22** Where a seismic operation team violates the provisions of Article 6 and arbitrarily sets off explosive charges within the safety distances, the relevant departments shall investigate the administrative liability or civil liability of the responsible personnel of the seismic operation team in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of the state; if the circumstances are serious enough to constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　有下列行为之一的单位和个人，视其情节轻重，由当地人民政府有关部门依法给予行政处罚，并责令其赔偿造成的损失；构成犯罪的，由司法部门依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)扣押、损坏、偷盗地震作业队的设备和器材的；  
(二)采取挖路、放水、扣车、扣人、围攻及殴打地震作业人员、煽动群众起哄闹事、阻碍地震作业队正常施工作业的；  
(三)采用欺骗的方法，利用损害补偿敲诈勒索的。

**Article 23** Any unit or individual that commits any of the following acts shall, depending on the severity of the circumstances, be given an administrative penalty by the relevant department of the local people's government in accordance with the law, and shall be ordered to compensate for the losses caused; if the act constitutes a crime, the judicial department shall investigate criminal liability in accordance with the law:  
(1) detaining, damaging, or stealing the equipment and materials of the seismic operation team;  
(2) digging up roads, draining water, detaining vehicles, detaining persons, surrounding and assaulting seismic operation personnel, inciting the masses to create disturbances, or obstructing the normal construction operations of the seismic operation team;  
(3) using deceptive methods to extort or blackmail by exploiting damage compensation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　违反第四条规定，截留、回扣补偿费用的单位和个人，由监察部门责令其退回被截留、回扣的补偿费用，并可予以行政处罚。

**Article 24** Where any unit or individual violates the provisions of Article 4 and withholds or deducts compensation fees, the supervisory department shall order it to return the withheld or deducted compensation fees, and may impose an administrative penalty.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　其它行业和部门因地震勘探作业所造成的损害，可参照本规定进行补偿。

**Article 25** Where damage is caused by seismic exploration operations in other industries or departments, compensation may be made by reference to these Provisions.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本规定由国务院石油工业主管部门负责解释。

**Article 26** These Provisions shall be interpreted by the petroleum industry administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 27** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
