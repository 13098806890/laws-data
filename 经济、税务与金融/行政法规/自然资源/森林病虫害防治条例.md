# 森林病虫害防治条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1989-12-18  
**生效日期**：1989-12-18

> (1989年11月17日国务院第50次常务会议通过　1989年12月18日中华人民共和国国务院令第46号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为有效防治森林病虫害，保护森林资源，促进林业发展，维护自然生态平衡，根据《中华人民共和国森林法》有关规定，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称森林病虫害防治，是指对森林、林木、林木种苗及木材、竹材的病害和虫害的预防和除治。 第三条　森林病虫害防治实行“预防为主，综合治理”的方针。 第四条　森林病虫害防治实行“谁经营、谁防治”的责任制度。 地方各级人民政府应当制定措施和制度，加强对森林病虫害防治工作的领导。 第五条　国务院林业主管部门主管全国森林病虫害防治工作。 县级以上地方各级人民政府林业主管部门主管本行政区域内的森林病虫害防治工作，其所属的森林病虫害防治机构负责森林病虫害防治的具体组织工作。 区、乡林业工作站负责组织本区、乡的森林病虫害防治工作。 第六条　国家鼓励和支持森林病虫害防治科学研究，推广和应用先进技术，提高科学防治水平。 第二章　森林病虫害的预防 第七条　森林经营单位和个人在森林的经营活动中应当遵守下列规定： (一)植树造林应当适地适树，提倡营造混交林，合理搭配树种，依照国家规定选用林木良种；造林设计方案必须有森林病虫害防治措施； (二)禁止使用带有危险性病虫害的林木种苗进行育苗或者造林；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为有效防治森林病虫害，保护森林资源，促进林业发展，维护自然生态平衡，根据《中华人民共和国森林法》有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Forest Law of the People's Republic of China to effectively prevent and control forest pests and diseases, protect forest resources, promote forestry development, and maintain natural ecological balance.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称森林病虫害防治，是指对森林、林木、林木种苗及木材、竹材的病害和虫害的预防和除治。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, prevention and control of forest pests and diseases means the prevention and elimination of diseases and insect pests of forests, trees, forest seedlings, timber and bamboo.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　森林病虫害防治实行“预防为主，综合治理”的方针。

**Article 3** The policy of 'prevention first, integrated management' shall be implemented for the prevention and control of forest pests and diseases.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　森林病虫害防治实行“谁经营、谁防治”的责任制度。  
地方各级人民政府应当制定措施和制度，加强对森林病虫害防治工作的领导。

**Article 4** The responsibility system of 'whoever manages, prevents and controls' shall be implemented for the prevention and control of forest pests and diseases.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall formulate measures and systems to strengthen leadership over the prevention and control of forest pests and diseases.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院林业主管部门主管全国森林病虫害防治工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府林业主管部门主管本行政区域内的森林病虫害防治工作，其所属的森林病虫害防治机构负责森林病虫害防治的具体组织工作。  
区、乡林业工作站负责组织本区、乡的森林病虫害防治工作。

**Article 5** The forestry department under the State Council shall be in charge of the prevention and control of forest pests and diseases nationwide.  
The forestry departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of the prevention and control of forest pests and diseases within their respective administrative regions, and the forest pest and disease prevention and control institutions affiliated to them shall be responsible for the specific organizational work of forest pest and disease prevention and control.  
Forestry workstations of districts and townships shall be responsible for organizing the prevention and control of forest pests and diseases in their respective districts and townships.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励和支持森林病虫害防治科学研究，推广和应用先进技术，提高科学防治水平。

**Article 6** The State shall encourage and support scientific research on the prevention and control of forest pests and diseases, and promote and apply advanced technologies to improve the level of scientific prevention and control.

### 第二章　森林病虫害的预防

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　森林经营单位和个人在森林的经营活动中应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)植树造林应当适地适树，提倡营造混交林，合理搭配树种，依照国家规定选用林木良种；造林设计方案必须有森林病虫害防治措施；  
(二)禁止使用带有危险性病虫害的林木种苗进行育苗或者造林；  
(三)对幼龄林和中龄林应当及时进行抚育管理，清除已经感染病虫害的林木；  
(四)有计划地实行封山育林，改变纯林生态环境；  
(五)及时清理火烧迹地，伐除受害严重的过火林木；  
(六)采伐后的林木应当及时运出伐区并清理现场。

**Article 7** Forest management entities and individuals shall observe the following provisions in their forest management activities:  
(1) Afforestation shall be carried out with species suitable to the site, mixed forests shall be encouraged, tree species shall be rationally matched, and improved forest tree varieties shall be selected in accordance with state regulations; afforestation design plans shall include measures for preventing and controlling forest pests and diseases;  
(2) It shall be prohibited to use forest seedlings with dangerous pests and diseases for seedling cultivation or afforestation;  
(3) Young and middle-aged forests shall be timely tended and managed, and trees infected with pests and diseases shall be removed;  
(4) Mountain closure for afforestation shall be carried out in a planned manner to change the ecological environment of pure forests;  
(5) Burned areas shall be timely cleared, and severely damaged fire-scorched trees shall be felled;  
(6) After felling, trees shall be promptly transported out of the cutting area and the site shall be cleaned.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　各级人民政府林业主管部门应当有计划地组织建立无检疫对象的林木种苗基地。各级森林病虫害防治机构应当依法对林木种苗和木材、竹材进行产地和调运检疫；发现新传入的危险性病虫害，应当及时采取严密封锁、扑灭措施，不得将危险性病虫害传出。  
各口岸动植物检疫机构，应当按照国家有关进出境动植物检疫的法律规定，加强进境林木种苗和木材、竹材的检疫工作，防止境外森林病虫害传入。

**Article 8** The forestry departments of people's governments at all levels shall, in a planned manner, organize the establishment of forest seedling bases free of quarantine objects. The forest pest and disease prevention and control institutions at all levels shall, according to law, carry out origin and transit quarantine for forest seedlings, timber and bamboo; where newly introduced dangerous pests and diseases are discovered, they shall promptly take strict measures for sealing off and eradication, and shall not transfer the dangerous pests and diseases outside.  
Animal and plant quarantine institutions at various ports shall, in accordance with the legal provisions of the State on the quarantine of animals and plants entering and leaving the country, strengthen the quarantine of inbound forest seedlings, timber and bamboo, and prevent the introduction of forest pests and diseases from abroad.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各级人民政府林业主管部门应当组织和监督森林经营单位和个人，采取有效措施，保护好林内各种有益生物，并有计划地进行繁殖和培养，发挥生物防治作用。

**Article 9** The forestry departments of people's governments at all levels shall organize and supervise forest management entities and individuals to take effective measures to protect various beneficial organisms in forests, and to propagate and cultivate them in a planned manner, so as to give full play to the role of biological control.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院林业主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业主管部门的森林病虫害防治机构，应当综合分析各地测报数据，定期分别发布全国和本行政区域的森林病虫害中、长期趋势预报，并提出防治方案。  
县、市、自治州人民政府林业主管部门或者其所属的森林病虫害防治机构，应当综合分析基层单位测报数据，发布当地森林病虫害短、中期预报，并提出防治方案。  
全民所有的森林和林木，由国营林业局、国营林场或者其他经营单位组织森林病虫害情况调查。  
集体和个人所有的森林和林木，由区、乡林业工作站或者县森林病虫害防治机构组织森林病虫害情况调查。  
各调查单位应当按照规定向上一级林业主管部门或者其森林病虫害防治机构报告森林病虫害的调查情况。

**Article 10** The forest pest and disease prevention and control institutions of the forestry department under the State Council and of the forestry departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall comprehensively analyze the forecast data from various sources, regularly issue respectively national and regional medium- and long-term trend forecasts for forest pests and diseases, and propose prevention and control plans.  
The forestry departments of the people's governments of counties, cities and autonomous prefectures, or the forest pest and disease prevention and control institutions affiliated to them, shall comprehensively analyze the forecast data from grass-roots units, issue local short- and medium-term forecasts for forest pests and diseases, and propose prevention and control plans.  
For forests and trees owned by the whole people, the state-owned forestry bureaus, state-owned forest farms or other management entities shall organize investigations into the situation of forest pests and diseases.  
For forests and trees owned by collectives or individuals, the forestry workstations of districts and townships or the county forest pest and disease prevention and control institutions shall organize investigations into the situation of forest pests and diseases.  
Each investigating unit shall report the investigation results on forest pests and diseases to the superior forestry department or its forest pest and disease prevention and control institution in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院林业主管部门负责制定主要森林病虫害的测报对象及测报办法；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业主管部门可以根据本行政区域的情况作出补充规定，并报国务院林业主管部门备案。  
国务院林业主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业主管部门的森林病虫害防治机构可以在不同地区根据实际需要建立中心测报点，对测报对象进行调查与监测。

**Article 11** The forestry department under the State Council shall be responsible for formulating the forecast objects and methods for major forest pests and diseases; the forestry departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government may make supplementary provisions in light of the conditions in their respective administrative regions, and shall submit them to the forestry department under the State Council for recordation.  
The forest pest and disease prevention and control institutions of the forestry department under the State Council and of the forestry departments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government may establish central forecast stations in different areas according to actual needs to conduct investigation and monitoring of forecast objects.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　地方各级人民政府林业主管部门应当对经常发生森林病虫害的地区，实施以营林措施为主，生物、化学和物理防治相结合的综合治理措施，逐步改变森林生态环境，提高森林抗御自然灾害的能力。

**Article 12** The forestry departments of local people's governments at all levels shall, in areas where forest pests and diseases frequently occur, implement comprehensive management measures that combine forestry measures with biological, chemical and physical control, gradually improve the forest ecological environment, and enhance the forest's ability to resist natural disasters.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　各级人民政府林业主管部门可以根据森林病虫害防治的实际需要，建设下列设施：  
(一)药剂、器械及其储备仓库；  
(二)临时简易机场；  
(三)测报试验室、检疫检验室、检疫隔离试种苗圃；  
(四)林木种苗及木材熏蒸除害设施。

**Article 13** The forestry departments of people's governments at all levels may, in light of the actual needs for prevention and control of forest pests and diseases, construct the following facilities:  
(1) Pharmacies, equipment and their storage warehouses;  
(2) Temporary simple airfields;  
(3) Forecast laboratories, quarantine inspection rooms, quarantine isolation trial nurseries;  
(4) Fumigation and treatment facilities for forest seedlings and timber.

### 第三章　森林病虫害的除治

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　发现严重森林病虫害的单位和个人，应当及时向当地人民政府或者林业主管部门报告。  
当地人民政府或者林业主管部门接到报告后，应当及时组织除治，同时报告所在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业主管部门。  
发生大面积暴发性或者危险性森林病虫害时，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业主管部门应当及时报告国务院林业主管部门。

**Article 14** Entities and individuals that discover severe forest pests and diseases shall promptly report to the local people's government or forestry department.  
Upon receiving the report, the local people's government or forestry department shall promptly organize elimination and simultaneously report to the forestry department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
Where a large-scale outbreak or dangerous forest pests and diseases occur, the forestry department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall promptly report to the forestry department under the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　发生暴发性或者危险性的森林病虫害时，当地人民政府应当根据实际需要，组织有关部门建立森林病虫害防治临时指挥机构，负责制定紧急除治措施，协调解决工作中的重大问题。

**Article 15** Where outbreak or dangerous forest pests and diseases occur, the local people's government shall, according to actual needs, organize relevant departments to establish a temporary command organization for prevention and control of forest pests and diseases, which shall be responsible for formulating emergency elimination measures and coordinating to resolve major problems in the work.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　县级以上地方人民政府或者其林业主管部门应当制定除治森林病虫害的实施计划，并组织好交界地区的联防联治，对除治情况定期检查。

**Article 16** Local people's governments at or above the county level or their forestry departments shall formulate implementation plans for the elimination of forest pests and diseases, organize joint prevention and control in border areas, and conduct regular inspections of the elimination situation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　施药必须遵守有关规定，防止环境污染，保证人畜安全，减少杀伤有益生物。  
使用航空器施药时，当地人民政府林业主管部门应当事先进行调查设计，做好地面准备工作；林业、民航、气象部门应当密切配合，保证作业质量。

**Article 17** The application of pesticides must comply with relevant regulations to prevent environmental pollution, ensure the safety of humans and livestock, and reduce the killing of beneficial organisms.  
Where aircraft are used for pesticide application, the local forestry department of the people's government shall conduct prior investigation and design, and make proper ground preparations; the forestry, civil aviation and meteorological departments shall closely cooperate to ensure the quality of operations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　发生严重森林病虫害时，所需的防治药剂、器械、油料等，商业、供销、物资、石油化工等部门应当优先供应，铁路、交通、民航部门应当优先承运，民航部门应当优先安排航空器施药。

**Article 18** When severe forest pests and diseases occur, commercial, supply and marketing, materials, petrochemical and other departments shall give priority to the supply of necessary pesticides, equipment, fuel, etc.; railway, transportation and civil aviation departments shall give priority to transportation; and civil aviation departments shall give priority to arranging aircraft for pesticide application.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　森林病虫害防治费用，全民所有的森林和林木，依照国家有关规定，分别从育林基金、木竹销售收入、多种经营收入和事业费中解决；集体和个人所有的森林和林木，由经营者负担，地方各级人民政府可以给予适当扶持。  
对暂时没有经济收入的森林、林木和长期没有经济收入的防护林、水源林、特种用途林的森林经营单位和个人，其所需的森林病虫害防治费用由地方各级人民政府给予适当扶持。  
发生大面积暴发性或者危险性病虫害，森林经营单位或者个人确实无力负担全部防治费用的，各级人民政府应当给予补助。

**Article 19** For expenses on prevention and control of forest pests and diseases, for forests and trees owned by the whole people, they shall be met from the forest cultivation fund, timber and bamboo sales revenue, diversified business income and operating expenses in accordance with relevant state regulations; for forests and trees owned by collectives or individuals, the expenses shall be borne by the operators, and local people's governments at all levels may provide appropriate support.  
For forest management entities and individuals whose forests and trees have no economic income temporarily or for a long period, such as protection forests, water source forests and forests for special uses, the expenses required for prevention and control of forest pests and diseases shall be appropriately supported by local people's governments at all levels.  
Where a large-scale outbreak or dangerous pests and diseases occur, and forest management entities or individuals are truly unable to bear all the prevention and control expenses, people's governments at all levels shall provide subsidies.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家在重点林区逐步实行森林病虫害保险制度，具体办法由中国人民保险公司会同国务院林业主管部门制定。

**Article 20** The State shall gradually implement a forest pest and disease insurance system in key forest areas, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the People's Insurance Company of China in conjunction with the forestry department under the State Council.

### 第四章　奖励和惩罚

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　有下列成绩之一的单位和个人，由人民政府或者林业主管部门给予奖励：  
(一)严格执行森林病虫害防治法规，预防和除治措施得力，在本地区或者经营区域内，连续五年没有发生森林病虫害的；  
(二)预报病情、虫情及时准确，并提出防治森林病虫害的合理化建议，被有关部门采纳，获得显著效益的；  
(三)在森林病虫害防治科学研究中取得成果或者在应用推广科研成果中获得重大效益的；  
(四)在林业基层单位连续从事森林病虫害防治工作满十年，工作成绩较好的；  
(五)在森林病虫害防治工作中有其他显著成绩的。

**Article 21** Entities and individuals that achieve any of the following accomplishments shall be rewarded by the people's government or the forestry department:  
(1) Strictly implementing the laws and regulations on prevention and control of forest pests and diseases, taking effective prevention and elimination measures, and no forest pests or diseases occurring within their region or area of operation for five consecutive years;  
(2) Providing timely and accurate forecasts of disease and insect situations, and proposing rational suggestions for prevention and control that are adopted by relevant departments, resulting in significant benefits;  
(3) Achieving results in scientific research on prevention and control of forest pests and diseases or obtaining significant benefits in the application and dissemination of scientific research results;  
(4) Having been continuously engaged in the prevention and control of forest pests and diseases at grass-roots forestry units for a full ten years with good work performance;  
(5) Having made other notable achievements in the prevention and control of forest pests and diseases.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　有下列行为之一的，责令限期除治、赔偿损失，可以并处一百元至二千元的罚款：  
(一)用带有危险性病虫害的林木种苗进行育苗或者造林的；  
(二)发生森林病虫害不除治或者除治不力，造成森林病虫害蔓延成灾的；  
(三)隐瞒或者虚报森林病虫害情况，造成森林病虫害蔓延成灾的。

**Article 22** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be ordered to eliminate the pests and diseases within a time limit, compensate for losses, and may also be fined not less than 100 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan:  
(1) Using forest seedlings with dangerous pests and diseases for seedling cultivation or afforestation;  
(2) Failing to eliminate or failing to effectively eliminate forest pests and diseases after their occurrence, causing the spread and disaster of forest pests and diseases;  
(3) Concealing or falsely reporting the situation of forest pests and diseases, causing the spread and disaster of forest pests and diseases.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　违反植物检疫法规调运林木种苗或者木材的，除依照植物检疫法规处罚外，并可处五十元至二千元的罚款。

**Article 23** Whoever, in violation of plant quarantine laws and regulations, transports forest seedlings or timber shall, in addition to being punished in accordance with the plant quarantine laws and regulations, also be fined not less than 50 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　有[本条例第二十二条](#art-22)、第二十三条规定行为的责任人员或者在森林病虫害防治工作中有失职行为的国家工作人员，由其所在单位或者上级机关给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 24** The persons responsible for the acts specified in Articles 22 and 23 of these Regulations, or State functionaries who have neglected their duties in the prevention and control of forest pests and diseases, shall be given administrative sanctions by their units or higher authorities; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated by the judicial organs according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　被责令限期除治森林病虫害者不除治的，林业主管部门或者其授权的单位可以代为除治，由被责令限期除治者承担全部防治费用。  
代为除治森林病虫害的工作，不因被责令限期除治者申请复议或者起诉而停止执行。

**Article 25** If the person ordered to eliminate forest pests and diseases within a time limit fails to do so, the forestry department or an authorized unit may eliminate them on their behalf, and the person ordered to eliminate shall bear all prevention and control expenses.  
The work of eliminating forest pests and diseases on behalf of the person shall not be suspended due to the person's application for reconsideration or prosecution.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例规定的行政处罚，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门或其授权的单位决定。  
当事人对行政处罚决定不服的，可以在接到处罚通知之日起十五日内向作出处罚决定机关的上一级机关申请复议；对复议决定不服的，可以在接到复议决定书之日起十五日内向人民法院起诉。当事人也可以在接到处罚通知之日起十五日内直接向人民法院起诉。期满不申请复议或者不起诉又不履行处罚决定的，由作出处罚决定的机关申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 26** The administrative penalties prescribed in these Regulations shall be decided by the forestry departments of people's governments at or above the county level or by units authorized by them.  
If a party is dissatisfied with the decision on administrative penalty, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the penalty notification, apply for reconsideration to the authority at the next higher level over the authority that made the decision; if dissatisfied with the reconsideration decision, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration decision document, file a lawsuit with the People's Court. The party may also directly file a lawsuit with the People's Court within 15 days from the date of receipt of the penalty notification. If, upon expiration of the time limit, no application for reconsideration or lawsuit has been filed and the penalty decision is not complied with, the authority that made the penalty decision shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本条例由国务院林业主管部门负责解释。

**Article 27** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the forestry department under the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本条例结合本地实际情况，制定实施办法。

**Article 28** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in light of these Regulations and their local actual conditions, formulate implementing measures.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　城市园林管理部门管理的森林和林木，其病虫害防治工作由城市园林管理部门参照本条例执行。

**Article 29** For forests and trees managed by urban landscape management departments, the prevention and control of pests and diseases shall be implemented by the urban landscape management departments with reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 30** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
