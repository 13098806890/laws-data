# 地质灾害防治条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2003-11-24  
**生效日期**：2004-03-01

> (2003年11月19日国务院第29次常务会议通过　2003年11月24日中华人民共和国国务院令第394号公布　自2004年3月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了防治地质灾害，避免和减轻地质灾害造成的损失，维护人民生命和财产安全，促进经济和社会的可持续发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称地质灾害，包括自然因素或者人为活动引发的危害人民生命和财产安全的山体崩塌、滑坡、泥石流、地面塌陷、地裂缝、地面沉降等与地质作用有关的灾害。 第三条　地质灾害防治工作，应当坚持预防为主、避让与治理相结合和全面规划、突出重点的原则。 第四条　地质灾害按照人员伤亡、经济损失的大小，分为四个等级： (一)特大型：因灾死亡30人以上或者直接经济损失1000万元以上的； (二)大型：因灾死亡10人以上30人以下或者直接经济损失500万元以上1000万元以下的； (三)中型：因灾死亡3人以上10人以下或者直接经济损失100万元以上500万元以下的； (四)小型：因灾死亡3人以下或者直接经济损失100万元以下的。 第五条　地质灾害防治工作，应当纳入国民经济和社会发展计划。 因自然因素造成的地质灾害的防治经费，在划分中央和地方事权和财权的基础上，分别列入中央和地方有关人民政府的财政预算。具体办法由国务院财政部门会同国务院国土资源主管部门制定。 因工程建设等人为活动引发的地质灾害的治理费用，按照谁引发、谁治理的原则由责任单位承担。 第六条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对地质灾害防治工作的领导，组织有关部门采取措施，做好地质灾害防治工作。 县级以上人民政府应当组织有关部门开展地质灾害防治知识的宣传教育，增强公众的地质灾害防治意识和自救、互救能力。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了防治地质灾害，避免和减轻地质灾害造成的损失，维护人民生命和财产安全，促进经济和社会的可持续发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of preventing and controlling geological disasters, avoiding and mitigating losses caused by geological disasters, safeguarding people's lives and property safety, and promoting sustainable economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称地质灾害，包括自然因素或者人为活动引发的危害人民生命和财产安全的山体崩塌、滑坡、泥石流、地面塌陷、地裂缝、地面沉降等与地质作用有关的灾害。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, geological disasters include disasters related to geological processes, such as mountain collapses, landslides, debris flows, ground subsidence, ground fissures, and land subsidence, which are caused by natural factors or human activities and endanger people's lives and property safety.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　地质灾害防治工作，应当坚持预防为主、避让与治理相结合和全面规划、突出重点的原则。

**Article 3** The work of preventing and controlling geological disasters shall adhere to the principles of focusing on prevention, combining avoidance with control, and making comprehensive plans with emphasis on key areas.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　地质灾害按照人员伤亡、经济损失的大小，分为四个等级：  
(一)特大型：因灾死亡30人以上或者直接经济损失1000万元以上的；  
(二)大型：因灾死亡10人以上30人以下或者直接经济损失500万元以上1000万元以下的；  
(三)中型：因灾死亡3人以上10人以下或者直接经济损失100万元以上500万元以下的；  
(四)小型：因灾死亡3人以下或者直接经济损失100万元以下的。

**Article 4** Geological disasters are classified into four grades according to the magnitude of casualties and economic losses:  
(1) Extra-large: 30 or more deaths caused by the disaster, or direct economic losses of RMB 10 million or more;  
(2) Large: between 10 and 30 deaths caused by the disaster, or direct economic losses of between RMB 5 million and 10 million;  
(3) Medium: between 3 and 10 deaths caused by the disaster, or direct economic losses of between RMB 1 million and 5 million;  
(4) Small: fewer than 3 deaths caused by the disaster, or direct economic losses of less than RMB 1 million.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　地质灾害防治工作，应当纳入国民经济和社会发展计划。  
因自然因素造成的地质灾害的防治经费，在划分中央和地方事权和财权的基础上，分别列入中央和地方有关人民政府的财政预算。具体办法由国务院财政部门会同国务院国土资源主管部门制定。  
因工程建设等人为活动引发的地质灾害的治理费用，按照谁引发、谁治理的原则由责任单位承担。

**Article 5** The work of preventing and controlling geological disasters shall be incorporated into the national economic and social development plan.  
The funds for preventing and controlling geological disasters caused by natural factors shall, on the basis of the division of central and local responsibilities and financial powers, be included in the fiscal budgets of the relevant central and local people's governments. Specific measures shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council jointly with the competent department of land and resources of the State Council.  
The costs of controlling geological disasters triggered by human activities such as engineering construction shall be borne by the responsible unit according to the principle of "who triggers, who controls."

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对地质灾害防治工作的领导，组织有关部门采取措施，做好地质灾害防治工作。  
县级以上人民政府应当组织有关部门开展地质灾害防治知识的宣传教育，增强公众的地质灾害防治意识和自救、互救能力。

**Article 6** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen leadership over the work of preventing and controlling geological disasters, organize relevant departments to take measures, and ensure the proper performance of geological disaster prevention and control work.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall organize relevant departments to carry out publicity and education on geological disaster prevention and control knowledge, and enhance the public's awareness of geological disaster prevention and control and their ability to self-rescue and mutual rescue.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院国土资源主管部门负责全国地质灾害防治的组织、协调、指导和监督工作。国务院其他有关部门按照各自的职责负责有关的地质灾害防治工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门负责本行政区域内地质灾害防治的组织、协调、指导和监督工作。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门按照各自的职责负责有关的地质灾害防治工作。

**Article 7** The competent department of land and resources of the State Council shall be responsible for the organization, coordination, guidance, and supervision of the national geological disaster prevention and control work. Other relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for relevant geological disaster prevention and control work within their respective functions.  
The competent departments of land and resources of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the organization, coordination, guidance, and supervision of geological disaster prevention and control work within their respective administrative regions. Other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for relevant geological disaster prevention and control work within their respective functions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励和支持地质灾害防治科学技术研究，推广先进的地质灾害防治技术，普及地质灾害防治的科学知识。

**Article 8** The State shall encourage and support scientific and technological research on geological disaster prevention and control, promote advanced geological disaster prevention and control technologies, and popularize scientific knowledge on geological disaster prevention and control.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何单位和个人对地质灾害防治工作中的违法行为都有权检举和控告。  
在地质灾害防治工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，由人民政府给予奖励。

**Article 9** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report and accuse any illegal act in the work of geological disaster prevention and control.  
Units and individuals that make outstanding contributions to the work of geological disaster prevention and control shall be rewarded by the people's governments.

### 第二章　地质灾害防治规划

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家实行地质灾害调查制度。  
国务院国土资源主管部门会同国务院建设、水利、铁路、交通等部门结合地质环境状况组织开展全国的地质灾害调查。  
县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门会同同级建设、水利、交通等部门结合地质环境状况组织开展本行政区域的地质灾害调查。

**Article 10** The State shall implement a geological disaster survey system.  
The competent department of land and resources of the State Council shall, jointly with the departments of construction, water resources, railways, and transportation of the State Council, organize and carry out national geological disaster surveys in light of the geological environment conditions.  
The competent departments of land and resources of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, jointly with the departments of construction, water resources, and transportation at the same level, organize and carry out geological disaster surveys within their respective administrative regions in light of the geological environment conditions.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院国土资源主管部门会同国务院建设、水利、铁路、交通等部门，依据全国地质灾害调查结果，编制全国地质灾害防治规划，经专家论证后报国务院批准公布。  
县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门会同同级建设、水利、交通等部门，依据本行政区域的地质灾害调查结果和上一级地质灾害防治规划，编制本行政区域的地质灾害防治规划，经专家论证后报本级人民政府批准公布，并报上一级人民政府国土资源主管部门备案。  
修改地质灾害防治规划，应当报经原批准机关批准。

**Article 11** The competent department of land and resources of the State Council shall, jointly with the departments of construction, water resources, railways, and transportation of the State Council, formulate a national geological disaster prevention and control plan based on the results of the national geological disaster survey, which shall be submitted to the State Council for approval and promulgation after expert review.  
The competent departments of land and resources of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, jointly with the departments of construction, water resources, and transportation at the same level, formulate geological disaster prevention and control plans for their respective administrative regions based on the results of the geological disaster survey within their administrative regions and the prevention and control plan at the next higher level, which shall be submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval and promulgation after expert review, and shall be reported to the competent department of land and resources of the people's government at the next higher level for recordation.  
Any amendment to a geological disaster prevention and control plan shall be subject to the approval of the original approving authority.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　地质灾害防治规划包括以下内容：  
(一)地质灾害现状和发展趋势预测；  
(二)地质灾害的防治原则和目标；  
(三)地质灾害易发区、重点防治区；  
(四)地质灾害防治项目；  
(五)地质灾害防治措施等。  
县级以上人民政府应当将城镇、人口集中居住区、风景名胜区、大中型工矿企业所在地和交通干线、重点水利电力工程等基础设施作为地质灾害重点防治区中的防护重点。

**Article 12** A geological disaster prevention and control plan shall include the following:  
(1) the current status of geological disasters and predictions of development trends;  
(2) the principles and objectives for geological disaster prevention and control;  
(3) areas prone to geological disasters and key prevention and control areas;  
(4) geological disaster prevention and control projects;  
(5) geological disaster prevention and control measures, etc.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall designate towns, densely populated residential areas, scenic spots, locations of large and medium-sized industrial and mining enterprises, and infrastructure such as major transportation routes and key water conservancy and power projects as key protection targets within key prevention and control areas.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　编制和实施土地利用总体规划、矿产资源规划以及水利、铁路、交通、能源等重大建设工程项目规划，应当充分考虑地质灾害防治要求，避免和减轻地质灾害造成的损失。  
编制城市总体规划、村庄和集镇规划，应当将地质灾害防治规划作为其组成部分。

**Article 13** When formulating and implementing overall plans for land use, mineral resource plans, and plans for major construction projects such as water conservancy, railways, transportation, and energy, full consideration shall be given to the requirements of geological disaster prevention and control, so as to avoid and mitigate losses caused by geological disasters.  
When formulating overall urban plans and plans for villages and towns, the geological disaster prevention and control plan shall be taken as an integral part thereof.

### 第三章　地质灾害预防

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家建立地质灾害监测网络和预警信息系统。  
县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门应当会同建设、水利、交通等部门加强对地质灾害险情的动态监测。  
因工程建设可能引发地质灾害的，建设单位应当加强地质灾害监测。

**Article 14** The State shall establish a geological disaster monitoring network and an early warning information system.  
The competent departments of land and resources of people's governments at or above the county level shall, jointly with the departments of construction, water resources, and transportation, strengthen dynamic monitoring of potential geological disaster risks.  
Where engineering construction may trigger geological disasters, the construction unit shall strengthen geological disaster monitoring.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　地质灾害易发区的县、乡、村应当加强地质灾害的群测群防工作。在地质灾害重点防范期内，乡镇人民政府、基层群众自治组织应当加强地质灾害险情的巡回检查，发现险情及时处理和报告。  
国家鼓励单位和个人提供地质灾害前兆信息。

**Article 15** Counties, townships, and villages in areas prone to geological disasters shall strengthen the work of mass monitoring and mass prevention of geological disasters. During the key prevention period for geological disasters, township people's governments and grassroots self-governing mass organizations shall strengthen patrol inspections of potential geological disaster risks, and promptly handle and report any risks discovered.  
The State shall encourage units and individuals to provide information on precursor signs of geological disasters.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家保护地质灾害监测设施。任何单位和个人不得侵占、损毁、损坏地质灾害监测设施。

**Article 16** The State shall protect geological disaster monitoring facilities. No unit or individual may occupy, damage, or destroy geological disaster monitoring facilities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家实行地质灾害预报制度。预报内容主要包括地质灾害可能发生的时间、地点、成灾范围和影响程度等。  
地质灾害预报由县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门会同气象主管机构发布。  
任何单位和个人不得擅自向社会发布地质灾害预报。

**Article 17** The State shall implement a geological disaster forecasting system. The forecasting content mainly includes the possible time, location, disaster range, and impact degree of geological disasters.  
Geological disaster forecasts shall be issued by the competent departments of land and resources of people's governments at or above the county level jointly with the meteorological authorities.  
No unit or individual may issue geological disaster forecasts to the public without authorization.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门会同同级建设、水利、交通等部门依据地质灾害防治规划，拟订年度地质灾害防治方案，报本级人民政府批准后公布。  
年度地质灾害防治方案包括下列内容：  
(一)主要灾害点的分布；  
(二)地质灾害的威胁对象、范围；  
(三)重点防范期；  
(四)地质灾害防治措施；  
(五)地质灾害的监测、预防责任人。

**Article 18** The competent departments of land and resources of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, jointly with the departments of construction, water resources, and transportation at the same level, draft annual geological disaster prevention and control plans based on the geological disaster prevention and control plan, which shall be published after approval by the people's government at the same level.  
An annual geological disaster prevention and control plan shall include the following:  
(1) distribution of major disaster points;  
(2) objects and scope threatened by geological disasters;  
(3) key prevention period;  
(4) geological disaster prevention and control measures;  
(5) persons responsible for monitoring and preventing geological disasters.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对出现地质灾害前兆、可能造成人员伤亡或者重大财产损失的区域和地段，县级人民政府应当及时划定为地质灾害危险区，予以公告，并在地质灾害危险区的边界设置明显警示标志。  
在地质灾害危险区内，禁止爆破、削坡、进行工程建设以及从事其他可能引发地质灾害的活动。  
县级以上人民政府应当组织有关部门及时采取工程治理或者搬迁避让措施，保证地质灾害危险区内居民的生命和财产安全。

**Article 19** For areas and sections where precursor signs of geological disasters appear and which may cause casualties or significant property losses, the county people's government shall promptly designate them as geological disaster risk zones, make a public announcement, and set up conspicuous warning signs at the boundaries of the geological disaster risk zones.  
Within geological disaster risk zones, blasting, slope cutting, engineering construction, and other activities that may trigger geological disasters are prohibited.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall organize relevant departments to promptly take engineering control measures or relocation and avoidance measures to ensure the life and property safety of residents within geological disaster risk zones.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　地质灾害险情已经消除或者得到有效控制的，县级人民政府应当及时撤销原划定的地质灾害危险区，并予以公告。

**Article 20** Where a geological disaster risk has been eliminated or effectively controlled, the county people's government shall promptly revoke the originally designated geological disaster risk zone and make a public announcement.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　在地质灾害易发区内进行工程建设应当在可行性研究阶段进行地质灾害危险性评估，并将评估结果作为可行性研究报告的组成部分；可行性研究报告未包含地质灾害危险性评估结果的，不得批准其可行性研究报告。  
编制地质灾害易发区内的城市总体规划、村庄和集镇规划时，应当对规划区进行地质灾害危险性评估。

**Article 21** When conducting engineering construction in areas prone to geological disasters, a geological hazard risk assessment shall be carried out at the feasibility study stage, and the assessment results shall be taken as a component of the feasibility study report; where the feasibility study report does not contain the geological hazard risk assessment results, the feasibility study report shall not be approved.  
When formulating overall urban plans and plans for villages and towns within areas prone to geological disasters, a geological hazard risk assessment shall be carried out for the planning area.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家对从事地质灾害危险性评估的单位实行资质管理制度。地质灾害危险性评估单位应当具备下列条件，经省级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门资质审查合格，取得国土资源主管部门颁发的相应等级的资质证书后，方可在资质等级许可的范围内从事地质灾害危险性评估业务：  
(一)有独立的法人资格；  
(二)有一定数量的工程地质、环境地质和岩土工程等相应专业的技术人员；  
(三)有相应的技术装备。  
地质灾害危险性评估单位进行评估时，应当对建设工程遭受地质灾害危害的可能性和该工程建设中、建成后引发地质灾害的可能性做出评价，提出具体的预防治理措施，并对评估结果负责。

**Article 22** The State shall establish a qualification management system for units engaged in geological hazard risk assessment. A geological hazard risk assessment unit shall meet the following conditions, pass the qualification examination by the competent department of land and resources of the people's government at or above the provincial level, and obtain the qualification certificate of the corresponding grade issued by the competent department of land and resources before it may engage in geological hazard risk assessment business within the scope permitted by its qualification grade:  
(1) having independent legal person status;  
(2) having a certain number of professional technical personnel in corresponding specialties such as engineering geology, environmental geology, and geotechnical engineering;  
(3) having corresponding technical equipment.  
When conducting an assessment, a geological hazard risk assessment unit shall evaluate the likelihood of the construction project being endangered by geological disasters and the likelihood of triggering geological disasters during and after the construction of the project, put forward specific prevention and control measures, and be responsible for the assessment results.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　禁止地质灾害危险性评估单位超越其资质等级许可的范围或者以其他地质灾害危险性评估单位的名义承揽地质灾害危险性评估业务。  
禁止地质灾害危险性评估单位允许其他单位以本单位的名义承揽地质灾害危险性评估业务。  
禁止任何单位和个人伪造、变造、买卖地质灾害危险性评估资质证书。

**Article 23** It shall be prohibited for a geological hazard risk assessment unit to undertake geological hazard risk assessment business beyond the scope permitted by its qualification grade or in the name of another geological hazard risk assessment unit.  
It shall be prohibited for a geological hazard risk assessment unit to allow other units to undertake geological hazard risk assessment business in its name.  
It shall be prohibited for any unit or individual to forge, alter, or trade geological hazard risk assessment qualification certificates.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对经评估认为可能引发地质灾害或者可能遭受地质灾害危害的建设工程，应当配套建设地质灾害治理工程。地质灾害治理工程的设计、施工和验收应当与主体工程的设计、施工、验收同时进行。  
配套的地质灾害治理工程未经验收或者经验收不合格的，主体工程不得投入生产或者使用。

**Article 24** For construction projects that are assessed as likely to trigger geological disasters or may suffer from geological disaster hazards, supporting geological disaster control projects shall be constructed. The design, construction, and acceptance of geological disaster control projects shall be carried out simultaneously with the design, construction, and acceptance of the main project.  
Where the supporting geological disaster control project has not been accepted or has been accepted but found to be unqualified, the main project shall not be put into production or use.

### 第四章　地质灾害应急

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国务院国土资源主管部门会同国务院建设、水利、铁路、交通等部门拟订全国突发性地质灾害应急预案，报国务院批准后公布。  
县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门会同同级建设、水利、交通等部门拟订本行政区域的突发性地质灾害应急预案，报本级人民政府批准后公布。

**Article 25** The competent department of land and resources of the State Council shall, jointly with the departments of construction, water resources, railways, and transportation of the State Council, draft a national emergency response plan for sudden geological disasters, which shall be published after approval by the State Council.  
The competent departments of land and resources of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, jointly with the departments of construction, water resources, and transportation at the same level, draft emergency response plans for sudden geological disasters within their respective administrative regions, which shall be published after approval by the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　突发性地质灾害应急预案包括下列内容：  
(一)应急机构和有关部门的职责分工；  
(二)抢险救援人员的组织和应急、救助装备、资金、物资的准备；  
(三)地质灾害的等级与影响分析准备；  
(四)地质灾害调查、报告和处理程序；  
(五)发生地质灾害时的预警信号、应急通信保障；  
(六)人员财产撤离、转移路线、医疗救治、疾病控制等应急行动方案。

**Article 26** An emergency response plan for sudden geological disasters shall include the following:  
(1) the division of functions and responsibilities of emergency response agencies and relevant departments;  
(2) the organization of emergency rescue personnel and the preparation of emergency and relief equipment, funds, and materials;  
(3) the preparation for analysis of the grade and impact of geological disasters;  
(4) procedures for geological disaster investigation, reporting, and handling;  
(5) early warning signals and emergency communication support in the event of a geological disaster;  
(6) emergency action plans for evacuation and transfer of persons and property, medical treatment, disease control, etc.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　发生特大型或者大型地质灾害时，有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当成立地质灾害抢险救灾指挥机构。必要时，国务院可以成立地质灾害抢险救灾指挥机构。  
发生其他地质灾害或者出现地质灾害险情时，有关市、县人民政府可以根据地质灾害抢险救灾工作的需要，成立地质灾害抢险救灾指挥机构。  
地质灾害抢险救灾指挥机构由政府领导负责、有关部门组成，在本级人民政府的领导下，统一指挥和组织地质灾害的抢险救灾工作。

**Article 27** When an extra-large or large geological disaster occurs, the relevant people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall establish a geological disaster emergency rescue command organization. When necessary, the State Council may establish a geological disaster emergency rescue command organization.  
When other geological disasters occur or potential geological disaster risks appear, the relevant city or county people's government may establish a geological disaster emergency rescue command organization as needed for the emergency rescue work.  
The geological disaster emergency rescue command organization shall be headed by a government leader and composed of relevant departments. Under the leadership of the people's government at the same level, it shall exercise unified command and organization of the emergency rescue work for geological disasters.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　发现地质灾害险情或者灾情的单位和个人，应当立即向当地人民政府或者国土资源主管部门报告。其他部门或者基层群众自治组织接到报告的，应当立即转报当地人民政府。  
当地人民政府或者县级人民政府国土资源主管部门接到报告后，应当立即派人赶赴现场，进行现场调查，采取有效措施，防止灾害发生或者灾情扩大，并按照国务院国土资源主管部门关于地质灾害灾情分级报告的规定，向上级人民政府和国土资源主管部门报告。

**Article 28** Any unit or individual that discovers a potential geological disaster risk or disaster situation shall immediately report to the local people's government or the competent department of land and resources. Where another department or a grassroots self-governing mass organization receives the report, it shall immediately forward the report to the local people's government.  
Upon receiving the report, the local people's government or the competent department of land and resources of the county people's government shall immediately dispatch personnel to the scene, conduct an on-site investigation, take effective measures to prevent the occurrence of the disaster or the expansion of the disaster situation, and report to the people's government at the next higher level and the competent department of land and resources in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of land and resources of the State Council on graded reporting of geological disaster situations.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　接到地质灾害险情报告的当地人民政府、基层群众自治组织应当根据实际情况，及时动员受到地质灾害威胁的居民以及其他人员转移到安全地带；情况紧急时，可以强行组织避灾疏散。

**Article 29** Upon receiving a report of a potential geological disaster risk, the local people's government and grassroots self-governing mass organization shall, based on the actual circumstances, promptly mobilize residents and other persons threatened by the geological disaster to evacuate to a safe area; in case of an emergency, they may forcibly organize evacuation for disaster avoidance.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　地质灾害发生后，县级以上人民政府应当启动并组织实施相应的突发性地质灾害应急预案。有关地方人民政府应当及时将灾情及其发展趋势等信息报告上级人民政府。  
禁止隐瞒、谎报或者授意他人隐瞒、谎报地质灾害灾情。

**Article 30** After a geological disaster occurs, the people's government at or above the county level shall activate and organize the implementation of the corresponding emergency response plan for sudden geological disasters. The relevant local people's government shall promptly report information such as the disaster situation and its development trend to the people's government at the next higher level.  
It shall be prohibited to conceal, make false reports, or instigate others to conceal or make false reports about the geological disaster situation.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　县级以上人民政府有关部门应当按照突发性地质灾害应急预案的分工，做好相应的应急工作。  
国土资源主管部门应当会同同级建设、水利、交通等部门尽快查明地质灾害发生原因、影响范围等情况，提出应急治理措施，减轻和控制地质灾害灾情。  
民政、卫生、食品药品监督管理、商务、公安部门，应当及时设置避难场所和救济物资供应点，妥善安排灾民生活，做好医疗救护、卫生防疫、药品供应、社会治安工作；气象主管机构应当做好气象服务保障工作；通信、航空、铁路、交通部门应当保证地质灾害应急的通信畅通和救灾物资、设备、药物、食品的运送。

**Article 31** The relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall perform the corresponding emergency work in accordance with the division of responsibilities in the emergency response plan for sudden geological disasters.  
The competent department of land and resources shall, jointly with the departments of construction, water resources, and transportation at the same level, promptly ascertain the causes and scope of impact of the geological disaster, propose emergency control measures, and mitigate and control the geological disaster situation.  
The civil affairs, health, food and drug supervision and administration, commerce, and public security departments shall promptly set up shelters and relief supply points, properly arrange the lives of disaster victims, and carry out medical rescue, health and epidemic prevention, drug supply, and public security work; the meteorological authority shall provide meteorological service support; the communications, aviation, railway, and transportation departments shall ensure the smooth flow of communications for geological disaster emergency response and the transportation of relief materials, equipment, medicines, and food.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　根据地质灾害应急处理的需要，县级以上人民政府应当紧急调集人员，调用物资、交通工具和相关的设施、设备；必要时，可以根据需要在抢险救灾区域范围内采取交通管制等措施。  
因救灾需要，临时调用单位和个人的物资、设施、设备或者占用其房屋、土地的，事后应当及时归还；无法归还或者造成损失的，应当给予相应的补偿。

**Article 32** Based on the needs of geological disaster emergency response, people's governments at or above the county level shall urgently assemble personnel and requisition materials, means of transportation, and related facilities and equipment; when necessary, they may take measures such as traffic control within the emergency rescue area as needed.  
Where, for the needs of disaster relief, materials, facilities, or equipment of units or individuals are temporarily requisitioned, or their houses or land are occupied, they shall be promptly returned after the event; where return is impossible or losses are caused, corresponding compensation shall be given.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据地质灾害灾情和地质灾害防治需要，统筹规划、安排受灾地区的重建工作。

**Article 33** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the geological disaster situation and the needs of geological disaster prevention and control, make overall plans and arrangements for the reconstruction work in the disaster-affected areas.

### 第五章　地质灾害治理

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　因自然因素造成的特大型地质灾害，确需治理的，由国务院国土资源主管部门会同灾害发生地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府组织治理。  
因自然因素造成的其他地质灾害，确需治理的，在县级以上地方人民政府的领导下，由本级人民政府国土资源主管部门组织治理。  
因自然因素造成的跨行政区域的地质灾害，确需治理的，由所跨行政区域的地方人民政府国土资源主管部门共同组织治理。

**Article 34** For extra-large geological disasters caused by natural factors that indeed require control, the competent department of land and resources of the State Council shall, jointly with the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the disaster occurred, organize the control.  
For other geological disasters caused by natural factors that indeed require control, under the leadership of the local people's government at or above the county level, the competent department of land and resources of the people's government at the same level shall organize the control.  
For geological disasters caused by natural factors that cross administrative regions and indeed require control, the competent departments of land and resources of the local people's governments across the administrative regions shall jointly organize the control.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　因工程建设等人为活动引发的地质灾害，由责任单位承担治理责任。  
责任单位由地质灾害发生地的县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门负责组织专家对地质灾害的成因进行分析论证后认定。  
对地质灾害的治理责任认定结果有异议的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 35** Geological disasters triggered by human activities such as engineering construction shall be controlled by the responsible unit, which shall bear the control responsibility.  
The responsible unit shall be determined by the competent department of land and resources of the people's government at or above the county level where the geological disaster occurred, after organizing experts to analyze and demonstrate the causes of the geological disaster.  
If a party disagrees with the determination of control responsibility for the geological disaster, it may apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate administrative action according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　地质灾害治理工程的确定，应当与地质灾害形成的原因、规模以及对人民生命和财产安全的危害程度相适应。  
承担专项地质灾害治理工程勘查、设计、施工和监理的单位，应当具备下列条件，经省级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门资质审查合格，取得国土资源主管部门颁发的相应等级的资质证书后，方可在资质等级许可的范围内从事地质灾害治理工程的勘查、设计、施工和监理活动，并承担相应的责任：  
(一)有独立的法人资格；  
(二)有一定数量的水文地质、环境地质、工程地质等相应专业的技术人员；  
(三)有相应的技术装备；  
(四)有完善的工程质量管理制度。  
地质灾害治理工程的勘查、设计、施工和监理应当符合国家有关标准和技术规范。

**Article 36** The determination of a geological disaster control project shall be commensurate with the causes and scale of the geological disaster and the degree of harm to people's lives and property.  
Units undertaking the survey, design, construction, and supervision of specialized geological disaster control projects shall meet the following conditions, pass the qualification examination by the competent department of land and resources of the people's government at or above the provincial level, and obtain the qualification certificate of the corresponding grade issued by the competent department of land and resources before they may engage in the survey, design, construction, and supervision activities of geological disaster control projects within the scope permitted by their qualification grade, and shall bear corresponding responsibilities:  
(1) having independent legal person status;  
(2) having a certain number of professional technical personnel in corresponding specialties such as hydrogeology, environmental geology, and engineering geology;  
(3) having corresponding technical equipment;  
(4) having a sound project quality management system.  
The survey, design, construction, and supervision of geological disaster control projects shall comply with relevant national standards and technical specifications.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　禁止地质灾害治理工程勘查、设计、施工和监理单位超越其资质等级许可的范围或者以其他地质灾害治理工程勘查、设计、施工和监理单位的名义承揽地质灾害治理工程勘查、设计、施工和监理业务。  
禁止地质灾害治理工程勘查、设计、施工和监理单位允许其他单位以本单位的名义承揽地质灾害治理工程勘查、设计、施工和监理业务。  
禁止任何单位和个人伪造、变造、买卖地质灾害治理工程勘查、设计、施工和监理资质证书。

**Article 37** It shall be prohibited for a unit undertaking the survey, design, construction, or supervision of a geological disaster control project to undertake such business beyond the scope permitted by its qualification grade or in the name of another unit undertaking the survey, design, construction, or supervision of geological disaster control projects.  
It shall be prohibited for a unit undertaking the survey, design, construction, or supervision of a geological disaster control project to allow other units to undertake such business in its name.  
It shall be prohibited for any unit or individual to forge, alter, or trade qualification certificates for the survey, design, construction, or supervision of geological disaster control projects.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　政府投资的地质灾害治理工程竣工后，由县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门组织竣工验收。其他地质灾害治理工程竣工后，由责任单位组织竣工验收；竣工验收时，应当有国土资源主管部门参加。

**Article 38** After the completion of a geological disaster control project invested by the government, the competent department of land and resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall organize the completion acceptance. After the completion of other geological disaster control projects, the responsible unit shall organize the completion acceptance; during the completion acceptance, the competent department of land and resources shall participate.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　政府投资的地质灾害治理工程经竣工验收合格后，由县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门指定的单位负责管理和维护；其他地质灾害治理工程经竣工验收合格后，由负责治理的责任单位负责管理和维护。  
任何单位和个人不得侵占、损毁、损坏地质灾害治理工程设施。

**Article 39** After a geological disaster control project invested by the government passes the completion acceptance, it shall be managed and maintained by the unit designated by the competent department of land and resources of the people's government at or above the county level; after other geological disaster control projects pass the completion acceptance, they shall be managed and maintained by the responsible unit that carried out the control.  
No unit or individual may occupy, damage, or destroy the facilities of a geological disaster control project.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本条例规定，有关县级以上地方人民政府、国土资源主管部门和其他有关部门有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予降级或者撤职的行政处分；造成地质灾害导致人员伤亡和重大财产损失的，依法给予开除的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未按照规定编制突发性地质灾害应急预案，或者未按照突发性地质灾害应急预案的要求采取有关措施、履行有关义务的；  
(二)在编制地质灾害易发区内的城市总体规划、村庄和集镇规划时，未按照规定对规划区进行地质灾害危险性评估的；  
(三)批准未包含地质灾害危险性评估结果的可行性研究报告的；  
(四)隐瞒、谎报或者授意他人隐瞒、谎报地质灾害灾情，或者擅自发布地质灾害预报的；  
(五)给不符合条件的单位颁发地质灾害危险性评估资质证书或者地质灾害治理工程勘查、设计、施工、监理资质证书的；  
(六)在地质灾害防治工作中有其他渎职行为的。

**Article 40** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a relevant local people's government at or above the county level, the competent department of land and resources, or another relevant department commits any of the following acts, the person directly in charge and the other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions of demotion or removal from office according to law; if the geological disaster caused results in casualties and significant property losses, they shall be given administrative sanctions of dismissal according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to formulate an emergency response plan for sudden geological disasters in accordance with the provisions, or failing to take relevant measures or perform relevant obligations in accordance with the requirements of the emergency response plan for sudden geological disasters;  
(2) when formulating overall urban plans or plans for villages and towns within areas prone to geological disasters, failing to carry out a geological hazard risk assessment for the planning area in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) approving a feasibility study report that does not contain the geological hazard risk assessment results;  
(4) concealing, making false reports, or instigating others to conceal or make false reports about the geological disaster situation, or issuing geological disaster forecasts without authorization;  
(5) issuing geological hazard risk assessment qualification certificates or qualification certificates for the survey, design, construction, or supervision of geological disaster control projects to units that do not meet the conditions;  
(6) committing other acts of dereliction of duty in the work of geological disaster prevention and control.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本条例规定，建设单位有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令停止生产、施工或者使用，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未按照规定对地质灾害易发区内的建设工程进行地质灾害危险性评估的；  
(二)配套的地质灾害治理工程未经验收或者经验收不合格，主体工程即投入生产或者使用的。

**Article 41** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a construction unit commits any of the following acts, the competent department of land and resources of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to stop production, construction, or use, and a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000 shall be imposed; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to carry out a geological hazard risk assessment for a construction project in an area prone to geological disasters in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) putting the main project into production or use when the supporting geological disaster control project has not been accepted or has been accepted but found to be unqualified.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本条例规定，对工程建设等人为活动引发的地质灾害不予治理的，由县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门责令限期治理；逾期不治理或者治理不符合要求的，由责令限期治理的国土资源主管部门组织治理，所需费用由责任单位承担，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 42** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a geological disaster triggered by human activities such as engineering construction is not controlled, the competent department of land and resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall order control within a prescribed time limit; if control is not carried out within the time limit or the control does not meet the requirements, the competent department of land and resources that issued the order shall organize the control, and the necessary expenses shall be borne by the responsible unit, and a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000 shall be imposed; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反本条例规定，在地质灾害危险区内爆破、削坡、进行工程建设以及从事其他可能引发地质灾害活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门责令停止违法行为，对单位处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款，对个人处l万元以上5万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 43** Where, in violation of these Regulations, blasting, slope cutting, engineering construction, or other activities that may trigger geological disasters are carried out within a geological disaster risk zone, the competent department of land and resources of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act, impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 200,000 on the unit, and a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 50,000 on the individual; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门或者其他部门依据职责责令停止违法行为，对地质灾害危险性评估单位、地质灾害治理工程勘查、设计或者监理单位处合同约定的评估费、勘查费、设计费或者监理酬金1倍以上2倍以下的罚款，对地质灾害治理工程施工单位处工程价款2%以上4%以下的罚款，并可以责令停业整顿，降低资质等级；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，吊销其资质证书；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
(一)在地质灾害危险性评估中弄虚作假或者故意隐瞒地质灾害真实情况的；  
(二)在地质灾害治理工程勘查、设计、施工以及监理活动中弄虚作假、降低工程质量的；  
(三)无资质证书或者超越其资质等级许可的范围承揽地质灾害危险性评估、地质灾害治理工程勘查、设计、施工及监理业务的；  
(四)以其他单位的名义或者允许其他单位以本单位的名义承揽地质灾害危险性评估、地质灾害治理工程勘查、设计、施工和监理业务的。

**Article 44** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any of the following acts is committed, the competent department of land and resources of the people's government at or above the county level or another department shall, according to its functions, order cessation of the illegal act, impose on the geological hazard risk assessment unit or the unit undertaking survey, design, or supervision of a geological disaster control project a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the assessment fee, survey fee, design fee, or supervision fee as stipulated in the contract, and impose on the unit undertaking construction of a geological disaster control project a fine of not less than 2% but not more than 4% of the project cost; it may also order suspension of business for rectification and reduction of the qualification grade; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification certificate shall be revoked; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law:  
(1) practicing fraud or intentionally concealing the true situation of geological disasters in a geological hazard risk assessment;  
(2) practicing fraud or reducing project quality in the survey, design, construction, or supervision of a geological disaster control project;  
(3) undertaking geological hazard risk assessment or survey, design, construction, or supervision of a geological disaster control project without a qualification certificate or beyond the scope permitted by its qualification grade;  
(4) undertaking geological hazard risk assessment or survey, design, construction, or supervision of a geological disaster control project in the name of another unit, or allowing another unit to undertake such business in its own name.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反本条例规定，伪造、变造、买卖地质灾害危险性评估资质证书、地质灾害治理工程勘查、设计、施工和监理资质证书的，由省级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门收缴或者吊销其资质证书，没收违法所得，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 45** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a geological hazard risk assessment qualification certificate or a qualification certificate for the survey, design, construction, or supervision of a geological disaster control project is forged, altered, or traded, the competent department of land and resources of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall confiscate or revoke the qualification certificate, confiscate any illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 100,000; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本条例规定，侵占、损毁、损坏地质灾害监测设施或者地质灾害治理工程设施的，由县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状或者采取补救措施，可以处5万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 46** Where, in violation of these Regulations, geological disaster monitoring facilities or geological disaster control project facilities are occupied, damaged, or destroyed, the competent department of land and resources of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act, order restoration to the original condition or adoption of remedial measures within a specified time limit, and may impose a fine of not more than RMB 50,000; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　在地质灾害防治工作中形成的地质资料，应当按照《地质资料管理条例》的规定汇交。

**Article 47** Geological data formed in the course of geological disaster prevention and control work shall be submitted for collection in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on the Management of Geological Data.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　地震灾害的防御和减轻依照防震减灾的法律、行政法规的规定执行。  
防洪法律、行政法规对洪水引发的崩塌、滑坡、泥石流的防治有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 48** The prevention and mitigation of earthquake disasters shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on earthquake prevention and disaster reduction.  
Where laws and administrative regulations on flood control have provisions on the prevention and control of collapses, landslides, and debris flows triggered by floods, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　本条例自2004年3月1日起施行。

**Article 49** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 2004.
