# 矿产资源监督管理暂行办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1987-04-28  
**生效日期**：1987-04-29

> (1987年4月29日国务院发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一条　为加强对矿山企业的矿产资源开发利用和保护工作的监督管理，根据《中华人民共和国矿产资源法》的有关规定，制定本办法。 第二条　本办法适用于在中华人民共和国领域及管辖海域内从事采矿生产的矿山企业(包括有矿山的单位，下同)，但本办法另有规定的除外。 第三条　国务院地质矿产主管部门对执行本办法负有下列职责： 一、制定有关矿产资源开发利用与保护的监督管理规章； 二、监督、检查矿产资源管理法规的执行情况； 三、会同有关部门建立矿产资源合理开发利用的考核指标体系及定期报表制度； 四、会同有关主管部门负责大型矿山企业的非正常储量报销的审批工作； 五、组织或者参与矿产资源开发利用与保护工作的调查研究，总结交流经验。 第四条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门对执行本办法负有下列职责： 一、根据本办法和有关法规，对本地区矿山企业的矿产资源开发利用与保护工作进行监督管理和指导； 二、根据需要向重点矿山企业派出矿产督察员，向矿山企业集中的地区派出巡回矿产督察员； 派出督察员的具体办法，由国务院地质矿产主管部门会同有关部门另行制定。 第五条　国务院和各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的有关主管部门对贯彻执行本办法负有下列职责： 一、制定本部门矿产资源开发利用和保护工作的规章、规定，并报同级地质矿产主管部门备案；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为加强对矿山企业的矿产资源开发利用和保护工作的监督管理，根据《中华人民共和国矿产资源法》的有关规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Mineral Resources Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of strengthening the supervision and management of the exploitation, utilization and protection of mineral resources by mining enterprises.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本办法适用于在中华人民共和国领域及管辖海域内从事采矿生产的矿山企业(包括有矿山的单位，下同)，但本办法另有规定的除外。

**Article 2** These Measures shall apply to mining enterprises engaged in mining production within the territory of the People's Republic of China and the sea areas under its jurisdiction (including units that own mines, similarly hereinafter), except as otherwise provided for in these Measures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院地质矿产主管部门对执行本办法负有下列职责：  
一、制定有关矿产资源开发利用与保护的监督管理规章；  
二、监督、检查矿产资源管理法规的执行情况；  
三、会同有关部门建立矿产资源合理开发利用的考核指标体系及定期报表制度；  
四、会同有关主管部门负责大型矿山企业的非正常储量报销的审批工作；  
五、组织或者参与矿产资源开发利用与保护工作的调查研究，总结交流经验。

**Article 3** The competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council shall have the following responsibilities for implementing these Measures:  
1. To formulate supervisory and administrative regulations concerning the exploitation, utilization and protection of mineral resources;  
2. To supervise and inspect the implementation of laws and regulations on mineral resources management;  
3. To establish, jointly with relevant departments, an assessment indicator system for the rational exploitation and utilization of mineral resources and a system for regular reporting;  
4. To be responsible, jointly with the relevant competent departments, for the examination and approval of abnormal write-offs of mineral reserves of large mining enterprises;  
5. To organize or participate in investigations and studies on the exploitation, utilization and protection of mineral resources, and to summarize and exchange experience.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门对执行本办法负有下列职责：  
一、根据本办法和有关法规，对本地区矿山企业的矿产资源开发利用与保护工作进行监督管理和指导；  
二、根据需要向重点矿山企业派出矿产督察员，向矿山企业集中的地区派出巡回矿产督察员；  
派出督察员的具体办法，由国务院地质矿产主管部门会同有关部门另行制定。

**Article 4** The competent departments of geology and mineral resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall have the following responsibilities for implementing these Measures:  
1. To supervise, manage and guide the exploitation, utilization and protection of mineral resources by mining enterprises in their respective regions in accordance with these Measures and relevant laws and regulations;  
2. To station mineral supervisors at key mining enterprises, and to dispatch itinerant mineral supervisors to areas where mining enterprises are concentrated, as required.  
Specific measures for dispatching supervisors shall be formulated separately by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院和各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的有关主管部门对贯彻执行本办法负有下列职责：  
一、制定本部门矿产资源开发利用和保护工作的规章、规定，并报同级地质矿产主管部门备案；  
二、根据本办法和有关法规，协助地质矿产主管部门对本部门矿山企业的矿产资源开发利用与保护工作进行监督管理；  
三、负责所属矿山企业的矿产储量管理，严格执行矿产储量核减的审批规定；  
四、总结和交流本部门矿山企业矿产资源合理开发利用和保护工作的经验。

**Article 5** The relevant competent departments of the State Council and of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall have the following responsibilities for implementing these Measures:  
1. To formulate regulations and provisions concerning the exploitation, utilization and protection of mineral resources within their respective departments, and to report them to the competent departments of geology and mineral resources at the same level for record;  
2. To assist the competent departments of geology and mineral resources in supervising and managing the exploitation, utilization and protection of mineral resources by mining enterprises within their respective departments in accordance with these Measures and relevant laws and regulations;  
3. To be responsible for the management of mineral reserves of mining enterprises under their jurisdiction, and to strictly implement the approval provisions for the reduction of mineral reserves;  
4. To summarize and exchange experience in the rational exploitation, utilization and protection of mineral resources by mining enterprises within their respective departments.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　矿山企业的地质测量机构是本企业矿产资源开发利用与保护工作的监督管理机构，对执行本办法负有以下职责：  
一、做好生产勘探工作，提高矿产储量级别，为开采提供可靠地质依据；  
二、对矿产资源开采的损失、贫化以及矿产资源综合开采利用进行监督；  
三、对矿山企业的矿产储量进行管理；  
四、对违反矿产资源管理法规的行为及其责任者提出处理意见并可越级上报。

**Article 6** The geological survey agency of a mining enterprise shall be the supervisory and administrative organ for the exploitation, utilization and protection of mineral resources by the enterprise, and shall have the following responsibilities for implementing these Measures:  
1. To conduct production exploration work properly, upgrade the grade of mineral reserves, and provide reliable geological basis for mining;  
2. To supervise the loss and dilution of mineral resources during mining, as well as the comprehensive exploitation and utilization of mineral resources;  
3. To manage the mineral reserves of the mining enterprise;  
4. To propose handling opinions on acts violating laws and regulations on mineral resources management and the persons responsible therefor, and may report to higher authorities by bypassing immediate superiors.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　矿山企业开发利用矿产资源，应当加强开采管理，选择合理的采矿方法和选矿方法，推广先进工艺技术，提高矿产资源利用水平。

**Article 7** In exploiting and utilizing mineral resources, mining enterprises shall strengthen mining management, select rational mining and beneficiation methods, promote advanced technologies, and raise the level of utilization of mineral resources.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　矿山企业在基建施工至矿山关闭的生产全过程中，都应当加强矿产资源的保护工作。

**Article 8** Mining enterprises shall strengthen the protection of mineral resources throughout the entire production process from capital construction to the closure of the mine.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　矿山企业应当按照国家有关法规及其主管部门的有关规章、规定，建立、健全本企业开发利用和保护矿产资源的各项制度，并切实加以贯彻落实。

**Article 9** Mining enterprises shall, in accordance with the relevant state laws and regulations and the relevant rules and provisions of their competent departments, establish and perfect various systems for the exploitation, utilization and protection of mineral resources, and shall earnestly implement them.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　矿山开采设计要求的回采率、采矿贫化率和选矿回收率，应当列为考核矿山企业的重要年度计划指标。

**Article 10** The recovery rate, mining dilution rate and beneficiation recovery rate required by the mining design shall be listed as important annual plan indicators for assessing mining enterprises.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　矿山企业应当加强生产勘探，提高矿床勘探程度，为开采设计提供可靠依据；对具有工业价值的共生、伴生矿产应当系统查定和评价。

**Article 11** Mining enterprises shall strengthen production exploration, improve the exploration degree of mineral deposits, and provide reliable basis for mining design; they shall systematically verify and evaluate co-existing and associated mineral resources of industrial value.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　矿山企业的开采设计应当在可靠地质资料基础上进行。中段(或阶段)开采应当有总体设计，块段开采应当有采矿设计。

**Article 12** The mining design of a mining enterprise shall be conducted on the basis of reliable geological data. For mining by sublevel (or stage), an overall design shall be prepared; for mining by block, a mining design shall be prepared.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　矿山的开拓、采准及采矿工程，必须按照开采设计进行施工。应当建立严格的施工验收制度，防止资源丢失。

**Article 13** The development, preparation and stoping works of a mine must be carried out in accordance with the mining design. A strict construction acceptance system shall be established to prevent loss of resources.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　矿山企业必须按照设计进行开采，不准任意丢掉矿体。对开采应当加强监督检查，严防不应有的开采损失。

**Article 14** Mining enterprises must carry out mining in accordance with the design and shall not discard ore bodies arbitrarily. Supervision and inspection of mining shall be strengthened to strictly prevent undue mining losses.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　矿山企业在开采中必须加强对矿石损失、贫化的管理，建立定期检查制度，分析造成非正常损失、贫化的原因，制定措施，提高资源的回采率，降低贫化率。

**Article 15** In the course of mining, mining enterprises must strengthen the management of ore loss and dilution, establish a system of regular inspection, analyze the causes of abnormal loss and dilution, formulate measures to increase the recovery rate of resources and reduce the dilution rate.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　选矿(煤)厂应当根据设计要求定期进行选矿流程考察；对选矿回收率和精矿(洗精煤)质量没有达到设计指标的，应当查明原因，提出改进措施。

**Article 16** A concentrator (coal preparation plant) shall, in accordance with the design requirements, regularly conduct investigations of the beneficiation process; where the beneficiation recovery rate and the quality of concentrate (washed coal) fail to meet the design targets, the causes shall be identified and improvement measures shall be proposed.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在采、选主要矿产的同时，对具有工业价值的共生、伴生矿产，在技术可行、经济合理的条件下，必须综合回收；对暂时不能综合回收利用的矿产，应当采取有效的保护措施。

**Article 17** While mining and processing major mineral resources, comprehensive recovery must be carried out for co-existing and associated mineral resources of industrial value, provided that such recovery is technically feasible and economically reasonable; for mineral resources that cannot be comprehensively recovered and utilized for the time being, effective protective measures shall be taken.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　矿山企业应当加强对滞销矿石、粉矿、中矿、尾矿、废石和煤矸石的管理，积极研究其利用途径；暂时不能利用的，应当在节约土地的原则下，妥善堆放保存，防止其流失及污染环境。

**Article 18** Mining enterprises shall strengthen the management of unsalable ores, fine ores, middlings, tailings, waste rock and coal gangue, and actively study ways for their utilization; those that cannot be utilized for the time being shall be properly stacked and stored under the principle of land conservation, to prevent their loss and environmental pollution.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　矿山企业对矿产储量的圈定、计算及开采，必须以批准的计算矿产储量的工业指标为依据，不得随意变动。需要变动的，应当上报实际资料，经主管部门审核同意后，报原审批单位批准。

**Article 19** In the delineation, calculation and mining of mineral reserves, mining enterprises must take the approved industrial indicators for calculating mineral reserves as the basis, and shall not make changes arbitrarily. Where changes are necessary, actual data shall be submitted, and after being examined and approved by the competent department, the matter shall be submitted to the original approving authority for approval.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　报销矿产储量，应当经矿山企业地质测量机构检查鉴定后，向矿山企业的主管部门提出申请。  
属正常报销的矿产储量，由矿山企业的主管部门审批。  
属非正常报销和转出的矿产储量，由矿山企业的主管部门会同同级地质矿产主管部门审批。  
同一采区应当一次申请报销的矿产储量，不得化整为零，分几次申请报销。

**Article 20** To write off mineral reserves, an application shall be made to the competent department of the mining enterprise after being examined and verified by the geological survey agency of the mining enterprise.  
The write-off of mineral reserves that is normal shall be examined and approved by the competent department of the mining enterprise.  
The write-off and reclassification of mineral reserves that is abnormal shall be examined and approved by the competent department of the mining enterprise jointly with the competent department of geology and mineral resources at the same level.  
Mineral reserves that shall be applied for write-off in a single mining area shall not be divided into several applications for write-off in separate batches.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　地下开采的中段(水平)或露天采矿场内尚有未采完的保有矿产储量，未经地质测量机构检查验收和报销申请尚未批准之前，不准擅自废除坑道和其他工程。

**Article 21** Where there are remaining mineral reserves not yet fully mined in a sublevel (level) of underground mining or in an open pit, it shall not be permitted to abolish tunnels and other works without authorization before the geological survey agency has inspected and accepted the same and the application for write-off has been approved.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　矿山企业应当向其上级主管部门和地质矿产主管部门上报矿产资源开发利用情况报表。

**Article 22** Mining enterprises shall submit reports on the exploitation and utilization of mineral resources to their superior competent departments and the competent departments of geology and mineral resources.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　矿山企业有下列情形之一的，应当追究有关人员的责任，或者由地质矿产主管部门责令其限期改正，并可处以相当于矿石损失50%以下的罚款，情节严重的，应当责令停产整顿或者吊销采矿许可证：  
一、因开采设计、采掘计划的决策错误，造成资源损失的；  
二、开采回采率、采矿贫化率和选矿回收率长期达不到设计要求，造成资源破坏损失的；  
三、违反[本办法第十三条](#art-13)、第十四条、第十七条、第十九条、第二十一条的规定，造成资源破坏损失的。

**Article 23** Where a mining enterprise falls under any of the following circumstances, the relevant persons shall be held responsible, or the competent department of geology and mineral resources shall order the enterprise to make corrections within a specified time limit and may also impose a fine of not more than 50% of the value of the ore lost; if the circumstances are serious, the enterprise shall be ordered to suspend production for rectification or its mining permit shall be revoked:  
1. Causing resource losses due to incorrect decisions in mining design or extraction plans;  
2. Failing to meet the design requirements for mining recovery rate, mining dilution rate and beneficiation recovery rate over a long period of time, thereby causing damage and loss to resources;  
3. Violating the provisions of Article 13, Article 14, Article 17, Article 19 or Article 21 of these Measures, thereby causing damage and loss to resources.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　当事人对行政处罚决定不服的，可以在收到处罚通知之日起15日内，向人民法院起诉。对罚款的行政处罚决定期满不起诉又不履行的，由作出处罚决定的机关申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 24** Any party that refuses to accept the decision on administrative penalty may file a complaint with the People's Court within 15 days from the date of receipt of the decision on penalty. If, upon expiration of the time limit, no prosecution has been instituted against the decision on administrative penalty of fine and the decision has not been complied with, the organ that made the decision on penalty shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　矿山企业上报的矿产资源开发利用资料数据必须准确可靠。虚报瞒报的，依照《中华人民共和国统计法》的有关规定追究责任。对保密资料，应当按照国家有关保密规定执行。

**Article 25** The data and materials on the exploitation and utilization of mineral resources submitted by mining enterprises must be accurate and reliable. A person who makes false reports or conceals information shall be held accountable in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China. Confidential materials shall be handled in accordance with the relevant state provisions on secrecy.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对乡镇集体矿山企业和个体采矿的矿产资源开发利用与保护工作的监督管理办法，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府参照本办法制定。

**Article 26** The supervisory and administrative measures for the exploitation, utilization and protection of mineral resources by collectively-owned mining enterprises in townships and towns and by individual mining shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government with reference to these Measures.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本办法由国务院地质矿产主管部门负责解释。

**Article 27** These Measures shall be interpreted by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本办法自发布之日起施行。

**Article 28** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
