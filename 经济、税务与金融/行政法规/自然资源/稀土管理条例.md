# 稀土管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2024-06-22  
**生效日期**：2024-10-01

> 第八条　国务院工业和信息化主管部门会同国务院有关部门确定稀土开采企业和稀土冶炼分离企业，并向社会公布。 除依照本条第一款确定的企业外，其他组织和个人不得从事稀土开采和稀土冶炼分离。 第九条　稀土开采企业应当依照矿产资源管理法律、行政法规和国家有关规定取得采矿权、采矿许可证。 投资稀土开采、冶炼分离等项目，应当遵守投资项目管理的法律、行政法规和国家有关规定。 第十条　国家根据稀土资源储量和种类差异、产业发展、生态保护、市场需求等因素，对稀土开采和稀土冶炼分离实行总量调控，并优化动态管理。具体办法由国务院工业和信息化主管部门会同国务院自然资源、发展改革等部门制定。 稀土开采企业和稀土冶炼分离企业应当严格遵守国家有关总量调控管理规定。 第十一条　国家鼓励和支持企业利用先进适用技术、工艺，对稀土二次资源进行综合利用。 稀土综合利用企业不得以稀土矿产品为原料从事生产活动。 第十二条　从事稀土开采、冶炼分离、金属冶炼、综合利用的企业，应当遵守有关矿产资源、节能环保、清洁生产、安全生产和消防的法律法规，采取合理的环境风险防范、生态保护、污染防治和安全防护措施，有效防止环境污染和生产安全事故。 第十三条　任何组织和个人不得收购、加工、销售、出口非法开采或者非法冶炼分离的稀土产品。 第十四条　国务院工业和信息化主管部门会同国务院自然资源、商务、海关、税务等部门建立稀土产品追溯信息系统，加强对稀土产品全过程追溯管理，推进有关部门数据共享。 从事稀土开采、冶炼分离、金属冶炼、综合利用和稀土产品出口的企业应当建立稀土产品流向记录制度，如实记录稀土产品流向信息并录入稀土产品追溯信息系统。 第十五条　稀土产品及相关技术、工艺、装备的进出口，应当遵守有关对外贸易、进出口管理法律、行政法规的规定。属于出口管制物项的，还应当遵守出口管制法律、行政法规的规定。 第十六条　国家按照实物储备和矿产地储备相结合的方式，完善稀土储备体系。 稀土实物储备实行政府储备与企业储备相结合，不断优化储备品种结构数量。具体办法由国务院发展改革、财政部门会同工业和信息化主管部门、粮食和物资储备部门制定。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　稀土开采企业应当依照矿产资源管理法律、行政法规和国家有关规定取得采矿权、采矿许可证。  
投资稀土开采、冶炼分离等项目，应当遵守投资项目管理的法律、行政法规和国家有关规定。

**Article 9** : A rare earth mining enterprise shall obtain the mining right and mining license in accordance with the laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions governing mineral resources management.  
Investment in projects such as rare earth mining, smelting, and separation shall comply with the laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions on investment project management.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家根据稀土资源储量和种类差异、产业发展、生态保护、市场需求等因素，对稀土开采和稀土冶炼分离实行总量调控，并优化动态管理。具体办法由国务院工业和信息化主管部门会同国务院自然资源、发展改革等部门制定。  
稀土开采企业和稀土冶炼分离企业应当严格遵守国家有关总量调控管理规定。

**Article 10** : The State shall, based on factors such as differences in rare earth resource reserves and types, industrial development, ecological protection, and market demand, implement aggregate control over rare earth mining and rare earth smelting and separation, and optimize dynamic management. Specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of industry and information technology of the State Council jointly with the departments of natural resources, development and reform, and other relevant departments under the State Council.  
Rare earth mining enterprises and rare earth smelting and separation enterprises shall strictly comply with the state's relevant provisions on aggregate control management.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家鼓励和支持企业利用先进适用技术、工艺，对稀土二次资源进行综合利用。  
稀土综合利用企业不得以稀土矿产品为原料从事生产活动。

**Article 11** : The State encourages and supports enterprises in utilizing advanced and applicable technologies and processes for the comprehensive utilization of rare earth secondary resources.  
A rare earth comprehensive utilization enterprise shall not engage in production activities using rare earth mineral products as raw materials.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　从事稀土开采、冶炼分离、金属冶炼、综合利用的企业，应当遵守有关矿产资源、节能环保、清洁生产、安全生产和消防的法律法规，采取合理的环境风险防范、生态保护、污染防治和安全防护措施，有效防止环境污染和生产安全事故。

**Article 12** : Enterprises engaged in rare earth mining, smelting and separation, metal smelting, or comprehensive utilization shall comply with the laws and regulations concerning mineral resources, energy conservation and environmental protection, cleaner production, work safety, and fire protection, and shall adopt reasonable environmental risk prevention, ecological protection, pollution prevention and control, and safety protection measures to effectively prevent environmental pollution and production safety accidents.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　任何组织和个人不得收购、加工、销售、出口非法开采或者非法冶炼分离的稀土产品。

**Article 13** : No organization or individual may purchase, process, sell, or export rare earth products mined illegally or smelted and separated illegally.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院工业和信息化主管部门会同国务院自然资源、商务、海关、税务等部门建立稀土产品追溯信息系统，加强对稀土产品全过程追溯管理，推进有关部门数据共享。  
从事稀土开采、冶炼分离、金属冶炼、综合利用和稀土产品出口的企业应当建立稀土产品流向记录制度，如实记录稀土产品流向信息并录入稀土产品追溯信息系统。

**Article 14** : The competent department of industry and information technology of the State Council shall, jointly with the departments of natural resources, commerce, customs, taxation, and other relevant departments under the State Council, establish a rare earth product traceability information system to strengthen the traceability management of rare earth products throughout the entire process, and promote data sharing among relevant departments.  
Enterprises engaged in rare earth mining, smelting and separation, metal smelting, comprehensive utilization, and export of rare earth products shall establish a record-keeping system for the flow direction of rare earth products, truthfully record the flow direction information of rare earth products, and input such information into the rare earth product traceability information system.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　稀土产品及相关技术、工艺、装备的进出口，应当遵守有关对外贸易、进出口管理法律、行政法规的规定。属于出口管制物项的，还应当遵守出口管制法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 15** : The import and export of rare earth products and related technologies, processes, and equipment shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on foreign trade and import/export management. Where such items are subject to export control, the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on export control shall also be complied with.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家按照实物储备和矿产地储备相结合的方式，完善稀土储备体系。  
稀土实物储备实行政府储备与企业储备相结合，不断优化储备品种结构数量。具体办法由国务院发展改革、财政部门会同工业和信息化主管部门、粮食和物资储备部门制定。  
国务院自然资源主管部门会同国务院有关部门根据保障稀土资源安全需要，结合资源储量、分布情况、重要程度等因素，划定稀土资源储备地，依法加强监管和保护。具体办法由国务院自然资源主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 16** : The State shall improve the rare earth reserve system by combining physical reserves with mineral reserve land.  
The physical reserve of rare earth shall be carried out by combining government reserves with enterprise reserves, and the variety, structure, and quantity of reserves shall be continuously optimized. Specific measures shall be formulated by the departments of development and reform and finance under the State Council jointly with the competent department of industry and information technology and the department of grain and material reserves.  
The competent department of natural resources under the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council, delimit rare earth resource reserve land in accordance with the need to ensure the security of rare earth resources, taking into account factors such as resource reserves, distribution, and importance, and shall strengthen supervision and protection according to law. Specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of natural resources under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　稀土行业组织应当建立健全行业规范，加强行业自律管理，引导企业守法、诚信经营，促进公平竞争。

**Article 17** : Rare earth industry organizations shall establish and improve industry norms, strengthen industry self-discipline management, guide enterprises to operate in accordance with the law and with integrity, and promote fair competition.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　工业和信息化主管部门和其他有关部门（以下统称监督检查部门）应当依照有关法律法规和本条例规定，按照职责分工对稀土的开采、冶炼分离、金属冶炼、综合利用、产品流通、进出口等活动进行监督检查，对违法行为及时依法处理。  
监督检查部门进行监督检查，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）要求被检查单位提供有关文件和资料；  
（二）询问被检查单位及其有关人员，要求其对与监督检查事项有关的情况作出说明；  
（三）进入涉嫌违法活动的场所进行调查和取证；  
（四）扣押违法活动相关的稀土产品及工具、设备，查封违法活动的场所；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他措施。  
被检查单位及其有关人员应当予以配合，如实提供有关文件和资料，不得拒绝、阻碍。

**Article 18** : The competent department of industry and information technology and other relevant departments (hereinafter collectively referred to as the supervision and inspection departments) shall, in accordance with relevant laws, regulations, and the provisions of these Regulations, conduct supervision and inspection of activities such as rare earth mining, smelting and separation, metal smelting, comprehensive utilization, product circulation, and import and export, according to their respective functions and duties, and shall promptly handle illegal acts in accordance with the law.  
When conducting supervision and inspection, the supervision and inspection departments shall have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) Require the unit under inspection to provide relevant documents and materials;  
(2) Inquire with the unit under inspection and its relevant personnel, requiring them to make explanations regarding matters related to the supervision and inspection;  
(3) Enter the premises suspected of engaging in illegal activities to investigate and collect evidence;  
(4) Seize rare earth products, tools, and equipment related to the illegal activities, and seal up the premises of the illegal activities;  
(5) Other measures as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
The unit under inspection and its relevant personnel shall cooperate, truthfully provide relevant documents and materials, and shall not refuse or obstruct.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　监督检查部门进行监督检查，监督检查人员不得少于2人，并应当出示有效的行政执法证件。  
监督检查部门的工作人员，对监督检查中获悉的国家秘密、商业秘密和个人信息负有保密义务。

**Article 19** : When conducting supervision and inspection, the supervision and inspection departments shall have no fewer than two supervision and inspection personnel, and shall present valid administrative law enforcement credentials.  
The staff members of the supervision and inspection departments shall bear the obligation of confidentiality regarding state secrets, trade secrets, and personal information learned during supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由自然资源主管部门依法予以处罚：  
（一）稀土开采企业未取得采矿权、采矿许可证开采稀土资源，或者超出采矿权登记的开采区域开采稀土资源；  
（二）稀土开采企业之外的组织和个人从事稀土开采。

**Article 20** : Whoever violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts shall be punished by the competent department of natural resources in accordance with the law:  
(1) A rare earth mining enterprise exploits rare earth resources without obtaining the mining right or mining license, or exploits rare earth resources beyond the mining area registered for the mining right;  
(2) An organization or individual other than a rare earth mining enterprise engages in rare earth mining.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　稀土开采企业和稀土冶炼分离企业违反总量调控管理规定进行稀土开采、冶炼分离的，由自然资源、工业和信息化主管部门按照职责分工责令改正，没收违法生产的稀土产品和违法所得，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足50万元的，并处100万元以上500万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停产停业，对主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 21** : Where a rare earth mining enterprise or a rare earth smelting and separation enterprise violates the provisions on aggregate control management and conducts rare earth mining or smelting and separation, the competent departments of natural resources and industry and information technology shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order it to make corrections, confiscate the illegally produced rare earth products and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 500,000, a fine of not less than RMB 1,000,000 but not more than RMB 5,000,000 shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend production and business operations, and the principal responsible person, the directly responsible supervisor, and other directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由工业和信息化主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法生产的稀土产品和违法所得以及直接用于违法活动的工具、设备，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足50万元的，并处200万元以上500万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由市场监督管理部门吊销其营业执照：  
（一）稀土冶炼分离企业之外的组织和个人从事冶炼分离；  
（二）稀土综合利用企业以稀土矿产品为原料从事生产活动。

**Article 22** : Whoever violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the competent department of industry and information technology to cease the illegal act, confiscate the illegally produced rare earth products and illegal gains as well as the tools and equipment directly used for the illegal activity, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 500,000, a fine of not less than RMB 2,000,000 but not more than RMB 5,000,000 shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the market supervision and administration department shall revoke its business license:  
(1) An organization or individual other than a rare earth smelting and separation enterprise engages in smelting and separation;  
(2) A rare earth comprehensive utilization enterprise uses rare earth mineral products as raw materials to engage in production activities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　违反本条例规定，收购、加工、销售非法开采或者非法冶炼分离的稀土产品的，由工业和信息化主管部门会同有关部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法收购、加工、销售的稀土产品和违法所得以及直接用于违法活动的工具、设备，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足50万元的，并处50万元以上200万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由市场监督管理部门吊销其营业执照。

**Article 23** : Whoever, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, purchases, processes, or sells rare earth products mined illegally or smelted and separated illegally shall be ordered by the competent department of industry and information technology jointly with the relevant departments to cease the illegal act, confiscate the illegally purchased, processed, or sold rare earth products and illegal gains as well as the tools and equipment directly used for the illegal activity, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 500,000, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 2,000,000 shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the market supervision and administration department shall revoke its business license.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　进出口稀土产品及相关技术、工艺、装备，违反有关法律、行政法规和本条例规定的，由商务主管部门、海关等有关部门按照职责依法予以处罚。

**Article 24** : Where the import or export of rare earth products and related technologies, processes, or equipment violates the provisions of relevant laws, administrative regulations, and these Regulations, the competent department of commerce, customs, and other relevant departments shall impose penalties in accordance with the law according to their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　从事稀土开采、冶炼分离、金属冶炼、综合利用和稀土产品出口的企业不如实记录稀土产品流向信息并录入稀土产品追溯信息系统的，由工业和信息化主管部门和其他有关部门按照职责分工责令改正，对企业处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停产停业，并对主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款，对企业处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款。

**Article 25** : Where an enterprise engaged in rare earth mining, smelting and separation, metal smelting, comprehensive utilization, or export of rare earth products fails to truthfully record and input the flow direction information of rare earth products into the rare earth product traceability information system, the competent department of industry and information technology and other relevant departments shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order it to make corrections, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 200,000 on the enterprise; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be ordered to suspend production and business operations, and the principal responsible person, the directly responsible supervisor, and other directly responsible personnel shall each be fined not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 50,000, and a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000 shall be imposed on the enterprise.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　拒绝、阻碍监督检查部门依法履行监督检查职责的，由监督检查部门责令改正，对主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，对企业处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停产停业，并对主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款，对企业处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 26** : Where any person refuses or obstructs the supervision and inspection departments from lawfully performing their supervision and inspection duties, the supervision and inspection departments shall order it to make corrections, issue a warning to the principal responsible person, the directly responsible supervisor, and other directly responsible personnel, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 on the enterprise; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be ordered to suspend production and business operations, and the principal responsible person, the directly responsible supervisor, and other directly responsible personnel shall each be fined not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 50,000, and a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000 shall be imposed on the enterprise.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　从事稀土开采、冶炼分离、金属冶炼、综合利用的企业，违反有关节能环保、清洁生产、安全生产和消防法律法规的，由相关部门按照职责依法予以处罚。  
从事稀土开采、冶炼分离、金属冶炼、综合利用和稀土产品进出口企业的违法违规行为，由相关部门依法记入信用记录，纳入国家有关信用信息系统。

**Article 27** : Where an enterprise engaged in rare earth mining, smelting and separation, metal smelting, or comprehensive utilization violates the laws and regulations on energy conservation and environmental protection, cleaner production, work safety, and fire protection, the relevant departments shall impose penalties in accordance with the law according to their respective functions and duties.  
The illegal or non-compliant acts of enterprises engaged in rare earth mining, smelting and separation, metal smelting, comprehensive utilization, and import/export of rare earth products shall be recorded in their credit records by the relevant departments according to law and included in the national credit information system.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　监督检查部门工作人员在稀土管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 28** : Where any staff member of a supervision and inspection department abuses his power, neglects his duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain in the management of rare earth, he shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 29** : Where a violation of the provisions of these Regulations constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
稀土，指镧、铈、镨、钕、钷、钐、铕、钆、铽、镝、钬、铒、铥、镱、镥、钪、钇等元素的总称。  
冶炼分离，指将稀土矿产品加工生成各类单一或者混合稀土氧化物、盐类以及其他化合物的生产过程。  
金属冶炼，指以单一或者混合稀土氧化物、盐类及其他化合物为原料制得稀土金属或者合金的生产过程。  
稀土二次资源，指经加工可使含有的稀土元素重新具有使用价值的固体废物，包括但不限于稀土永磁废料、废旧永磁体以及其他含稀土废弃物。  
稀土产品，包括稀土矿产品、各类稀土化合物、各类稀土金属及合金等。

**Article 30** : For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:  
Rare earth refers to the collective term for elements such as lanthanum, cerium, praseodymium, neodymium, promethium, samarium, europium, gadolinium, terbium, dysprosium, holmium, erbium, thulium, ytterbium, lutetium, scandium, and yttrium.  
Smelting and separation refers to the production process of processing rare earth mineral products to generate various single or mixed rare earth oxides, salts, and other compounds.  
Metal smelting refers to the production process of using single or mixed rare earth oxides, salts, and other compounds as raw materials to produce rare earth metals or alloys.  
Rare earth secondary resources refer to solid wastes that, through processing, can render the rare earth elements contained therein reusable, including but not limited to rare earth permanent magnet scrap, waste permanent magnets, and other waste materials containing rare earth.  
Rare earth products include rare earth mineral products, various rare earth compounds, various rare earth metals and alloys, and the like.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对稀土之外的其他稀有金属的管理，国务院相关主管部门可以参照本条例的有关规定执行。

**Article 31** : For the management of other rare metals other than rare earth, the relevant competent departments of the State Council may implement it by reference to the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本条例自2024年10月1日起施行。

**Article 32** : These Regulations shall come into force as of October 1, 2024.
