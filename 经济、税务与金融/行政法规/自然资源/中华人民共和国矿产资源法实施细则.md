**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1994-03-26  
**生效日期**：1994-03-26

> (1994年3月26日中华人民共和国国务院令第152号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国矿产资源法》，制定本细则。 第二条　矿产资源是指由地质作用形成的，具有利用价值的，呈固态、液态、气态的自然资源。 矿产资源的矿种和分类见本细则所附《矿产资源分类细目》。新发现的矿种由国务院地质矿产主管部门报国务院批准后公布。 第三条　矿产资源属于国家所有，地表或者地下的矿产资源的国家所有权，不因其所依附的土地的所有权或者使用权的不同而改变。 国务院代表国家行使矿产资源的所有权。国务院授权国务院地质矿产主管部门对全国矿产资源分配实施统一管理。 第四条　在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域勘查、开采矿产资源，必须遵守《中华人民共和国矿产资源法》(以下简称《矿产资源法》)和本细则。 第五条　国家对矿产资源的勘查、开采实行许可证制度。勘查矿产资源，必须依法申请登记，领取勘查许可证，取得探矿权；开采矿产资源，必须依法申请登记，领取采矿许可证，取得采矿权。 矿产资源勘查工作区范围和开采矿区范围，以经纬度划分的区块为基本单位。具体办法由国务院地质矿产主管部门制定。 第六条　《矿产资源法》及本细则中下列用语的含义： 探矿权，是指在依法取得的勘查许可证规定的范围内，勘查矿产资源的权利。取得勘查许可证的单位或者个人称为探矿权人。 采矿权，是指在依法取得的采矿许可证规定的范围内，开采矿产资源和获得所开采的矿产品的权利。取得采矿许可证的单位或者个人称为采矿权人。 国家规定实行保护性开采的特定矿种，是指国务院根据国民经济建设和高科技发展的需要，以及资源稀缺、贵重程度确定的，由国务院有关主管部门按照国家计划批准开采的矿种。 国家规划矿区，是指国家根据建设规划和矿产资源规划，为建设大、中型矿山划定的矿产资源分布区域。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国矿产资源法》，制定本细则。

**Article 1** These Detailed Rules are formulated in accordance with the Mineral Resources Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　矿产资源是指由地质作用形成的，具有利用价值的，呈固态、液态、气态的自然资源。  
矿产资源的矿种和分类见本细则所附《矿产资源分类细目》。新发现的矿种由国务院地质矿产主管部门报国务院批准后公布。

**Article 2** Mineral resources refer to natural resources formed by geological processes, having utilization value, and occurring in solid, liquid, or gaseous states.  
The types and classification of mineral resources are set out in the Classification List of Mineral Resources attached to these Detailed Rules. Newly discovered types of minerals shall be reported by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council to the State Council for approval and then be published.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　矿产资源属于国家所有，地表或者地下的矿产资源的国家所有权，不因其所依附的土地的所有权或者使用权的不同而改变。  
国务院代表国家行使矿产资源的所有权。国务院授权国务院地质矿产主管部门对全国矿产资源分配实施统一管理。

**Article 3** Mineral resources shall be owned by the State. The State ownership of mineral resources, whether on the surface or underground, shall not change due to differences in the ownership or right to use of the land to which they are attached.  
The State Council shall exercise the ownership of mineral resources on behalf of the State. The State Council authorizes the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council to exercise unified management over the allocation of mineral resources nationwide.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域勘查、开采矿产资源，必须遵守《中华人民共和国矿产资源法》(以下简称《矿产资源法》)和本细则。

**Article 4** Anyone who explores for or mines mineral resources within the territory of the People's Republic of China or in other sea areas under its jurisdiction must comply with the Mineral Resources Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Mineral Resources Law) and these Detailed Rules.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家对矿产资源的勘查、开采实行许可证制度。勘查矿产资源，必须依法申请登记，领取勘查许可证，取得探矿权；开采矿产资源，必须依法申请登记，领取采矿许可证，取得采矿权。  
矿产资源勘查工作区范围和开采矿区范围，以经纬度划分的区块为基本单位。具体办法由国务院地质矿产主管部门制定。

**Article 5** The State shall apply a licensing system for the exploration and mining of mineral resources. To explore for mineral resources, one must apply for registration according to law, obtain an exploration license, and acquire the right of exploration; to mine mineral resources, one must apply for registration according to law, obtain a mining license, and acquire the right of mining.  
The area covered by mineral resource exploration operations and the area of a mining district shall have blocks delimited by latitude and longitude as the basic units. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　《矿产资源法》及本细则中下列用语的含义：  
探矿权，是指在依法取得的勘查许可证规定的范围内，勘查矿产资源的权利。取得勘查许可证的单位或者个人称为探矿权人。  
采矿权，是指在依法取得的采矿许可证规定的范围内，开采矿产资源和获得所开采的矿产品的权利。取得采矿许可证的单位或者个人称为采矿权人。  
国家规定实行保护性开采的特定矿种，是指国务院根据国民经济建设和高科技发展的需要，以及资源稀缺、贵重程度确定的，由国务院有关主管部门按照国家计划批准开采的矿种。  
国家规划矿区，是指国家根据建设规划和矿产资源规划，为建设大、中型矿山划定的矿产资源分布区域。  
对国民经济具有重要价值的矿区，是指国家根据国民经济发展需要划定的，尚未列入国家建设规划的，储量大、质量好、具有开发前景的矿产资源保护区域。

**Article 6** For the purposes of the Mineral Resources Law and these Detailed Rules, the following terms shall have the meanings assigned to them:  
“Right of exploration” means the right to explore for mineral resources within the area specified in an exploration license lawfully obtained. The entity or individual that obtains an exploration license shall be called the exploration right holder.  
“Right of mining” means the right to mine mineral resources and obtain the mineral products extracted within the area specified in a mining license lawfully obtained. The entity or individual that obtains a mining license shall be called the mining right holder.  
“Specific types of minerals subject to protective mining as prescribed by the State” means types of minerals determined by the State Council on the basis of the needs of national economic construction and high-tech development, as well as the scarcity and preciousness of resources, and approved for mining by the relevant competent department under the State Council in accordance with the state plan.  
“State-planned mining area” means an area of mineral resource distribution designated by the State for the construction of large and medium-sized mines in accordance with the construction plan and the mineral resource plan.  
“Mining area of significant value to the national economy” means a protected area of mineral resources with large reserves, good quality, and development prospects, designated by the State according to the needs of national economic development, which has not yet been included in the state construction plan.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家允许外国的公司、企业和其他经济组织以及个人依照中华人民共和国有关法律、行政法规的规定，在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域投资勘查、开采矿产资源。

**Article 7** The State permits foreign companies, enterprises, other economic organizations, and individuals to invest in the exploration and mining of mineral resources within the territory of the People's Republic of China or in other sea areas under its jurisdiction in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院地质矿产主管部门主管全国矿产资源勘查、开采的监督管理工作。国务院有关主管部门按照国务院规定的职责分工，协助国务院地质矿产主管部门进行矿产资源勘查、开采的监督管理工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门主管本行政区域内矿产资源勘查、开采的监督管理工作。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关主管部门，协助同级地质矿产主管部门进行矿产资源勘查、开采的监督管理工作。  
设区的市人民政府、自治州人民政府和县级人民政府及其负责管理矿产资源的部门，依法对本级人民政府批准开办的国有矿山企业和本行政区域内的集体所有制矿山企业、私营矿山企业、个体采矿者以及在本行政区域内从事勘查施工的单位和个人进行监督管理，依法保护探矿权人、采矿权人的合法权益。  
上级地质矿产主管部门有权对下级地质矿产主管部门违法的或者不适当的矿产资源勘查、开采管理行政行为予以改变或者撤销。

**Article 8** The competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of mineral resource exploration and mining nationwide. The relevant competent departments under the State Council shall, according to the division of functions and responsibilities prescribed by the State Council, assist the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council in carrying out the supervision and administration of mineral resource exploration and mining.  
The competent departments of geology and mineral resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of mineral resource exploration and mining within their respective administrative regions. The relevant competent departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall assist the competent departments of geology and mineral resources at the same level in carrying out the supervision and administration of mineral resource exploration and mining.  
The people's governments of cities divided into districts, autonomous prefectures, and counties, and their departments responsible for the administration of mineral resources, shall, according to law, exercise supervision and administration over state-owned mining enterprises approved to be established by the people's governments at the corresponding level, collectively-owned mining enterprises, privately-owned mining enterprises, individual miners within their respective administrative regions, and entities and individuals engaged in exploration operations within their respective administrative regions, and shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of exploration right holders and mining right holders according to law.  
The competent department of geology and mineral resources at a higher level shall have the authority to modify or revoke illegal or inappropriate administrative acts of the competent department of geology and mineral resources at a lower level concerning the administration of mineral resource exploration and mining.

### 第二章　矿产资源勘查登记和开采审批

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　勘查矿产资源，应当按照国务院关于矿产资源勘查登记管理的规定，办理申请、审批和勘查登记。  
勘查特定矿种，应当按照国务院有关规定办理申请、审批和勘查登记。

**Article 9** To explore mineral resources, one shall go through the procedures of application, examination and approval, and exploration registration in accordance with the provisions of the State Council on the registration and administration of mineral resource exploration.  
To explore specific types of minerals, one shall go through the procedures of application, examination and approval, and exploration registration in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国有矿山企业开采矿产资源，应当按照国务院关于采矿登记管理的规定，办理申请、审批和采矿登记。开采国家规划矿区、对国民经济具有重要价值矿区的矿产和国家规定实行保护性开采的特定矿种，办理申请、审批和采矿登记时，应当持有国务院有关主管部门批准的文件。  
开采特定矿种，应当按照国务院有关规定办理申请、审批和采矿登记。

**Article 10** State-owned mining enterprises mining mineral resources shall go through the procedures of application, examination and approval, and mining registration in accordance with the provisions of the State Council on the registration and administration of mining. When applying, undergoing examination and approval, and conducting mining registration for the mining of minerals in state-planned mining areas, mining areas of significant value to the national economy, and specific types of minerals subject to protective mining as prescribed by the State, they shall hold documents approved by the relevant competent department under the State Council.  
To mine specific types of minerals, one shall go through the procedures of application, examination and approval, and mining registration in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　开办国有矿山企业，除应当具备有关法律、法规规定的条件外，并应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有供矿山建设使用的矿产勘查报告；  
(二)有矿山建设项目的可行性研究报告(含资源利用方案和矿山环境影响报告)；  
(三)有确定的矿区范围和开采范围；  
(四)有矿山设计；  
(五)有相应的生产技术条件。  
国务院、国务院有关主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，按照国家有关固定资产投资管理的规定，对申请开办的国有矿山企业根据前款所列条件审查合格后，方予批准。

**Article 11** To establish a state-owned mining enterprise, in addition to meeting the conditions prescribed by relevant laws and regulations, the following conditions shall also be satisfied:  
(1) Having a mineral exploration report available for mine construction;  
(2) Having a feasibility study report for the mine construction project (including a resource utilization plan and a mine environmental impact report);  
(3) Having a defined mining area and extraction scope;  
(4) Having a mine design;  
(5) Having corresponding production technical conditions.  
The State Council, the relevant competent departments under the State Council, and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the state provisions on the administration of fixed asset investment, approve the establishment of a state-owned mining enterprise only after verifying that the conditions listed in the preceding paragraph are met.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　申请开办集体所有制矿山企业、私营矿山企业及个体采矿的审查批准、采矿登记，按照省、自治区、直辖市的有关规定办理。

**Article 12** The examination and approval, and mining registration for the application to establish collectively-owned mining enterprises, privately-owned mining enterprises, and individual mining shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　申请开办集体所有制矿山企业或者私营矿山企业，除应当具备有关法律、法规规定的条件外，并应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有供矿山建设使用的与开采规模相适应的矿产勘查资料；  
(二)有经过批准的无争议的开采范围；  
(三)有与所建矿山规模相适应的资金、设备和技术人员；  
(四)有与所建矿山规模相适应的，符合国家产业政策和技术规范的可行性研究报告、矿山设计或者开采方案；  
(五)矿长具有矿山生产、安全管理和环境保护的基本知识。

**Article 13** To apply for establishing a collectively-owned mining enterprise or a privately-owned mining enterprise, in addition to meeting the conditions prescribed by relevant laws and regulations, the following conditions shall also be satisfied:  
(1) Having mineral exploration data corresponding to the mining scale and available for mine construction;  
(2) Having an approved and uncontested extraction scope;  
(3) Having funds, equipment, and technical personnel commensurate with the scale of the mine to be constructed;  
(4) Having a feasibility study report, mine design, or extraction plan that conforms to the state industrial policy and technical specifications and is commensurate with the scale of the mine to be constructed;  
(5) The mine manager possessing basic knowledge of mine production, safety management, and environmental protection.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　申请个体采矿应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有经过批准的无争议的开采范围；  
(二)有与采矿规模相适应的资金、设备和技术人员；  
(三)有相应的矿产勘查资料和经批准的开采方案；  
(四)有必要的安全生产条件和环境保护措施。

**Article 14** To apply for individual mining, the following conditions shall be satisfied:  
(1) Having an approved and uncontested extraction scope;  
(2) Having funds, equipment, and technical personnel commensurate with the mining scale;  
(3) Having corresponding mineral exploration data and an approved extraction plan;  
(4) Having necessary workplace safety conditions and environmental protection measures.

### 第三章　矿产资源的勘查

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家对矿产资源勘查实行统一规划。全国矿产资源中、长期勘查规划，在国务院计划行政主管部门指导下，由国务院地质矿产主管部门根据国民经济和社会发展中、长期规划，在国务院有关主管部门勘查规划的基础上组织编制。  
全国矿产资源年度勘查计划和省、自治区、直辖市矿产资源年度勘查计划，分别由国务院地质矿产主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门组织有关主管部门，根据全国矿产资源中、长期勘查规划编制，经同级人民政府计划行政主管部门批准后施行。  
法律对勘查规划的审批权另有规定的，依照有关法律的规定执行。

**Article 15** The State shall implement unified planning for mineral resource exploration. The national medium and long-term exploration plan for mineral resources shall be organized and formulated by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council, under the guidance of the competent department of planning under the State Council, on the basis of the medium and long-term plans for national economic and social development and on the basis of the exploration plans of the relevant competent departments under the State Council.  
The national annual exploration plan for mineral resources and the annual exploration plans for mineral resources of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be formulated respectively by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council and the competent departments of geology and mineral resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, organizing the relevant competent departments, on the basis of the national medium and long-term exploration plan for mineral resources, and shall be implemented after approval by the competent departments of planning of the people's governments at the same level.  
Where the law provides otherwise for the examination and approval authority of exploration plans, the provisions of the relevant laws shall prevail.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　探矿权人享有下列权利：  
(一)按照勘查许可证规定的区域、期限、工作对象进行勘查；  
(二)在勘查作业区及相邻区域架设供电、供水、通讯管线，但是不得影响或者损害原有的供电、供水设施和通讯管线；  
(三)在勘查作业区及相邻区域通行；  
(四)根据工程需要临时使用土地；  
(五)优先取得勘查作业区内新发现矿种的探矿权；  
(六)优先取得勘查作业区内矿产资源的采矿权；  
(七)自行销售勘查中按照批准的工程设计施工回收的矿产品，但是国务院规定由指定单位统一收购的矿产品除外。  
探矿权人行使前款所列权利时，有关法律、法规规定应当经过批准或者履行其他手续的，应当遵守有关法律、法规的规定。

**Article 16** An exploration right holder shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) To explore in accordance with the area, time limit, and work objects specified in the exploration license;  
(2) To erect power supply, water supply, and communication lines in the exploration operation area and adjacent areas, provided that they shall not affect or damage existing power supply, water supply facilities, and communication lines;  
(3) To have passage through the exploration operation area and adjacent areas;  
(4) To use land temporarily as required by the project;  
(5) To have the priority right to obtain the exploration right over newly discovered types of minerals within the exploration operation area;  
(6) To have the priority right to obtain the mining right over mineral resources within the exploration operation area;  
(7) To sell on their own the mineral products recovered during exploration in accordance with the approved engineering design and construction, except for those mineral products which the State Council prescribes shall be purchased exclusively by designated entities.  
When exercising the rights listed in the preceding paragraph, an exploration right holder shall, if the relevant laws and regulations provide that approval or other procedures are required, comply with the provisions of the relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　探矿权人应当履行下列义务：  
(一)在规定的期限内开始施工，并在勘查许可证规定的期限内完成勘查工作；  
(二)向勘查登记管理机关报告开工等情况；  
(三)按照探矿工程设计施工，不得擅自进行采矿活动；  
(四)在查明主要矿种的同时，对共生、伴生矿产资源进行综合勘查、综合评价；  
(五)编写矿产资源勘查报告，提交有关部门审批；  
(六)按照国务院有关规定汇交矿产资源勘查成果档案资料；  
(七)遵守有关法律、法规关于劳动安全、土地复垦和环境保护的规定；  
(八)勘查作业完毕，及时封、填探矿作业遗留的井、硐或者采取其他措施，消除安全隐患。

**Article 17** An exploration right holder shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) To commence construction within the prescribed time limit and complete the exploration work within the time limit specified in the exploration license;  
(2) To report to the exploration registration administration authority on matters such as the commencement of work;  
(3) To carry out construction in accordance with the exploration project design and shall not engage in mining activities without authorization;  
(4) While ascertaining the main types of minerals, to conduct comprehensive exploration and comprehensive assessment of associated and coexisting mineral resources;  
(5) To compile mineral resource exploration reports and submit them to the relevant departments for examination and approval;  
(6) To hand over the archives and data of mineral resource exploration results in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council;  
(7) To comply with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations on labor safety, land reclamation, and environmental protection;  
(8) Upon completion of exploration operations, to promptly seal or backfill shafts, adits, or other openings left by exploration operations, or take other measures to eliminate safety hazards.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　探矿权人可以对符合国家边探边采规定要求的复杂类型矿床进行开采；但是，应当向原颁发勘查许可证的机关、矿产储量审批机构和勘查项目主管部门提交论证材料，经审核同意后，按照国务院关于采矿登记管理法规的规定，办理采矿登记。

**Article 18** An exploration right holder may mine complex types of mineral deposits that meet the requirements of the state provisions on simultaneous exploration and mining; however, he/she shall submit substantiation materials to the authority that originally issued the exploration license, the mineral reserve examination and approval authority, and the competent department for the exploration project, and after examination and approval, shall undergo mining registration in accordance with the provisions of the State Council's regulations on mining registration administration.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　矿产资源勘查报告按照下列规定审批：  
(一)供矿山建设使用的重要大型矿床勘查报告和供大型水源地建设使用的地下水勘查报告，由国务院矿产储量审批机构审批；  
(二)供矿山建设使用的一般大型、中型、小型矿床勘查报告和供中型、小型水源地建设使用的地下水勘查报告，由省、自治区、直辖市矿产储量审批机构审批；  
矿产储量审批机构和勘查单位的主管部门应当自收到矿产资源勘查报告之日起六个月内作出批复。

**Article 19** Mineral resource exploration reports shall be examined and approved according to the following provisions:  
(1) Exploration reports for important large mineral deposits used for mine construction and groundwater exploration reports for large water source construction shall be examined and approved by the mineral reserve examination and approval authority under the State Council;  
(2) Exploration reports for general large, medium, and small mineral deposits used for mine construction and groundwater exploration reports for medium and small water source construction shall be examined and approved by the mineral reserve examination and approval authorities of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
The mineral reserve examination and approval authority and the competent department of the exploration entity shall give a reply within six months from the date of receipt of the mineral resource exploration report.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　矿产资源勘查报告及其他有价值的勘查资料，按照国务院有关规定实行有偿使用。

**Article 20** Mineral resource exploration reports and other valuable exploration data shall be subject to compensated use in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　探矿权人取得临时使用土地权后，在勘查过程中给他人造成财产损害的，按照下列规定给以补偿：  
(一)对耕地造成损害的，根据受损害的耕地面积前三年平均年产量，以补偿时当地市场平均价格计算，逐年给以补偿，并负责恢复耕地的生产条件，及时归还；  
(二)对牧区草场造成损害的，按照前项规定逐年给以补偿，并负责恢复草场植被，及时归还；  
(三)对耕地上的农作物、经济作物造成损害的，根据受损害的耕地面积前三年平均年产量，以补偿时当地市场平均价格计算，给以补偿；  
(四)对竹木造成损害的，根据实际损害株数，以补偿时当地市场平均价格逐株计算，给以补偿；  
(五)对土地上的附着物造成损害的，根据实际损害的程度，以补偿时当地市场价格，给以适当补偿。

**Article 21** Where an exploration right holder, after obtaining the right to temporarily use land, causes property damage to others during exploration, compensation shall be made according to the following provisions:  
(1) For damage caused to cultivated land, compensation shall be made year by year based on the average annual output of the damaged cultivated land in the preceding three years, calculated at the average local market price at the time of compensation, and the exploration right holder shall be responsible for restoring the production conditions of the cultivated land and returning it in a timely manner;  
(2) For damage caused to grassland in pastoral areas, compensation shall be made year by year in accordance with the provisions of the preceding item, and the exploration right holder shall be responsible for restoring the grassland vegetation and returning it in a timely manner;  
(3) For damage caused to crops and cash crops on cultivated land, compensation shall be made based on the average annual output of the damaged cultivated land in the preceding three years, calculated at the average local market price at the time of compensation;  
(4) For damage caused to bamboo and trees, compensation shall be made on a per-tree basis based on the actual number of trees damaged, calculated at the average local market price at the time of compensation;  
(5) For damage caused to attachments on the land, appropriate compensation shall be made based on the extent of actual damage and at the local market price at the time of compensation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　探矿权人在没有农作物和其他附着物的荒岭、荒坡、荒地、荒漠、沙滩、河滩、湖滩、海滩上进行勘查的，不予补偿；但是，勘查作业不得阻碍或者损害航运、灌溉、防洪等活动或者设施，勘查作业结束后应当采取措施，防止水土流失，保护生态环境。

**Article 22** No compensation shall be made if an exploration right holder carries out exploration on barren hills, barren slopes, barren land, deserts, sand beaches, river beaches, lake beaches, or sea beaches that have no crops or other attachments; provided that the exploration operations shall not obstruct or damage activities or facilities such as navigation, irrigation, and flood control, and after the exploration operations are completed, measures shall be taken to prevent soil erosion and protect the ecological environment.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　探矿权人之间对勘查范围发生争议时，由当事人协商解决；协商不成的，由勘查作业区所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门裁决；跨省、自治区、直辖市的勘查范围争议，当事人协商不成的，由有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府协商解决；协商不成的，由国务院地质矿产主管部门裁决。特定矿种的勘查范围争议，当事人协商不成的，由国务院授权的有关主管部门裁决。

**Article 23** Where a dispute arises between exploration right holders over the exploration area, it shall be settled by the parties through consultation; if consultation fails, it shall be adjudicated by the competent department of geology and mineral resources of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the exploration operation area is located; for a dispute over the exploration area that spans provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, if consultation between the parties fails, it shall be settled through consultation by the people's governments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government; if consultation fails, it shall be adjudicated by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council. For a dispute over the exploration area of specific types of minerals, if consultation between the parties fails, it shall be adjudicated by the relevant competent department authorized by the State Council.

### 第四章　矿产资源的开采

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　全国矿产资源的分配和开发利用，应当兼顾当前和长远、中央和地方的利益，实行统一规划、有效保护、合理开采、综合利用。

**Article 24** The allocation, development, and utilization of mineral resources nationwide shall take into account both current and long-term interests and the interests of the central and local authorities, and shall implement unified planning, effective protection, rational mining, and comprehensive utilization.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　全国矿产资源规划，在国务院计划行政主管部门指导下，由国务院地质矿产主管部门根据国民经济和社会发展中、长期规划，组织国务院有关主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府编制，报国务院批准后施行。  
全国矿产资源规划应当对全国矿产资源的分配作出统筹安排，合理划定中央与省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审批、开发矿产资源的范围。

**Article 25** The national mineral resource plan shall be formulated by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council, under the guidance of the competent department of planning under the State Council, on the basis of the medium and long-term plans for national economic and social development, organizing the relevant competent departments under the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and shall be implemented after being submitted to the State Council for approval.  
The national mineral resource plan shall make overall arrangements for the allocation of mineral resources nationwide and reasonably define the scope of examination, approval, and development of mineral resources by the central government and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　矿产资源开发规划是对矿区的开发建设布局进行统筹安排的规划。  
矿产资源开发规划分为行业开发规划和地区开发规划。  
矿产资源行业开发规划由国务院有关主管部门根据全国矿产资源规划中分配给本部门的矿产资源编制实施。  
矿产资源地区开发规划由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据全国矿产资源规划中分配给本省、自治区、直辖市的矿产资源编制实施；并作出统筹安排，合理划定省、市、县级人民政府审批、开发矿产资源的范围。  
矿产资源行业开发规划和地区开发规划应当报送国务院计划行政主管部门、地质矿产主管部门备案。  
国务院计划行政主管部门、地质矿产主管部门，对不符合全国矿产资源规划的行业开发规划和地区开发规划，应当予以纠正。

**Article 26** A mineral resource development plan is a plan for making overall arrangements for the development and construction layout of mining areas.  
Mineral resource development plans are divided into industrial development plans and regional development plans.  
The industrial development plan for mineral resources shall be formulated and implemented by the relevant competent department under the State Council on the basis of the mineral resources allocated to that department in the national mineral resource plan.  
The regional development plan for mineral resources shall be formulated and implemented by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government on the basis of the mineral resources allocated to that province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in the national mineral resource plan; and overall arrangements shall be made to reasonably define the scope of examination, approval, and development of mineral resources by the people's governments at the provincial, city, and county levels.  
The industrial development plan and the regional development plan for mineral resources shall be submitted to the competent department of planning under the State Council and the competent department of geology and mineral resources for the record.  
The competent department of planning under the State Council and the competent department of geology and mineral resources shall correct any industrial development plan or regional development plan that is inconsistent with the national mineral resource plan.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　设立、变更或者撤销国家规划矿区、对国民经济具有重要价值的矿区，由国务院有关主管部门提出，并附具矿产资源详查报告及论证材料，经国务院计划行政主管部门和地质矿产主管部门审定，并联合书面通知有关县级人民政府。县级人民政府应当自收到通知之日起一个月内予以公告，并报国务院计划行政主管部门、地质矿产主管部门备案。

**Article 27** The establishment, modification, or cancellation of a state-planned mining area or a mining area of significant value to the national economy shall be proposed by the relevant competent department under the State Council, accompanied by a detailed mineral resource survey report and substantiation materials, and shall be examined and finalized by the competent department of planning under the State Council and the competent department of geology and mineral resources, which shall jointly issue a written notice to the relevant county people's government. The county people's government shall, within one month from the date of receipt of the notice, make a public announcement and report to the competent department of planning under the State Council and the competent department of geology and mineral resources for the record.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　确定或者撤销国家规定实行保护性开采的特定矿种，由国务院有关主管部门提出，并附具论证材料，经国务院计划行政主管部门和地质矿产主管部门审核同意后，报国务院批准。

**Article 28** The determination or cancellation of specific types of minerals subject to protective mining as prescribed by the State shall be proposed by the relevant competent department under the State Council, accompanied by substantiation materials, and after examination and consent by the competent department of planning under the State Council and the competent department of geology and mineral resources, shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　单位或者个人开采矿产资源前，应当委托持有相应矿山设计证书的单位进行可行性研究和设计。开采零星分散矿产资源和用作建筑材料的砂、石、粘土的，可以不进行可行性研究和设计，但是应当有开采方案和环境保护措施。  
矿山设计必须依据设计任务书，采用合理的开采顺序、开采方法和选矿工艺。  
矿山设计必须按照国家有关规定审批；未经批准，不得施工。

**Article 29** Before mining mineral resources, an entity or individual shall entrust a unit holding a corresponding mine design certificate to conduct a feasibility study and design. For mining scattered and dispersed mineral resources and sand, stone, and clay used as building materials, a feasibility study and design may be omitted, but an extraction plan and environmental protection measures shall be in place.  
A mine design must be based on a design task document and adopt a reasonable extraction sequence, extraction method, and mineral processing technology.  
A mine design must be examined and approved in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; no construction shall be carried out without approval.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　采矿权人享有下列权利：  
(一)按照采矿许可证规定的开采范围和期限从事开采活动；  
(二)自行销售矿产品，但是国务院规定由指定的单位统一收购的矿产品除外；  
(三)在矿区范围内建设采矿所需的生产和生活设施；  
(四)根据生产建设的需要依法取得土地使用权；  
(五)法律、法规规定的其他权利。  
采矿权人行使前款所列权利时，法律、法规规定应当经过批准或者履行其他手续的，依照有关法律、法规的规定办理。

**Article 30** A mining right holder shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) To engage in mining activities within the extraction scope and time limit specified in the mining license;  
(2) To sell mineral products on their own, except for those mineral products which the State Council prescribes shall be purchased exclusively by designated entities;  
(3) To construct production and living facilities necessary for mining within the mining area;  
(4) To obtain the right to use land according to law as required by production and construction;  
(5) Other rights provided by laws and regulations.  
When exercising the rights listed in the preceding paragraph, a mining right holder shall, if the laws and regulations provide that approval or other procedures are required, handle them in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　采矿权人应当履行下列义务：  
(一)在批准的期限内进行矿山建设或者开采；  
(二)有效保护、合理开采、综合利用矿产资源；  
(三)依法缴纳资源税和矿产资源补偿费；  
(四)遵守国家有关劳动安全、水土保持、土地复垦和环境保护的法律、法规；  
(五)接受地质矿产主管部门和有关主管部门的监督管理，按照规定填报矿产储量表和矿产资源开发利用情况统计报告。

**Article 31** A mining right holder shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) To carry out mine construction or mining within the approved time limit;  
(2) To effectively protect, rationally mine, and comprehensively utilize mineral resources;  
(3) To pay resource tax and mineral resource compensation fees according to law;  
(4) To comply with the laws and regulations of the State on labor safety, water and soil conservation, land reclamation, and environmental protection;  
(5) To accept supervision and administration by the competent department of geology and mineral resources and other relevant competent departments, and to fill in mineral reserve statements and statistical reports on the development and utilization of mineral resources as prescribed.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　采矿权人在采矿许可证有效期满或者在有效期内，停办矿山而矿产资源尚未采完的，必须采取措施将资源保持在能够继续开采的状态，并事先完成下列工作：  
(一)编制矿山开采现状报告及实测图件；  
(二)按照有关规定报销所消耗的储量；  
(三)按照原设计实际完成相应的有关劳动安全、水土保持、土地复垦和环境保护工作，或者缴清土地复垦和环境保护的有关费用。  
采矿权人停办矿山的申请，须经原批准开办矿山的主管部门批准、原颁发采矿许可证的机关验收合格后，方可办理有关证、照注销手续。

**Article 32** Where a mining right holder suspends mining operations before the expiration of the mining license or within its validity period while the mineral resources have not been fully extracted, the mining right holder must take measures to keep the resources in a state that allows continued mining, and shall complete the following work in advance:  
(1) Prepare a report on the current status of mining and measured maps;  
(2) Write off the consumed reserves in accordance with the relevant provisions;  
(3) Actually complete the corresponding work concerning labor safety, water and soil conservation, land reclamation, and environmental protection in accordance with the original design, or pay off the relevant fees for land reclamation and environmental protection.  
The application of a mining right holder for suspending mining operations shall be approved by the competent department that originally approved the establishment of the mine and shall pass the acceptance inspection by the authority that originally issued the mining license before the procedures for canceling the relevant certificates and licenses may be handled.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　矿山企业关闭矿山，应当按照下列程序办理审批手续：  
(一)开采活动结束的前一年，向原批准开办矿山的主管部门提出关闭矿山申请，并提交闭坑地质报告；  
(二)闭坑地质报告经原批准开办矿山的主管部门审核同意后，报地质矿产主管部门会同矿产储量审批机构批准；  
(三)闭坑地质报告批准后，采矿权人应当编写关闭矿山报告，报请原批准开办矿山的主管部门会同同级地质矿产主管部门和有关主管部门按照有关行业规定批准。

**Article 33** Where a mining enterprise closes a mine, it shall go through the examination and approval procedures according to the following procedures:  
(1) One year before the end of mining activities, submit an application for mine closure to the competent department that originally approved the establishment of the mine, and submit a geological report on mine closure;  
(2) After the geological report on mine closure has been examined and agreed upon by the competent department that originally approved the establishment of the mine, it shall be submitted to the competent department of geology and mineral resources together with the mineral reserve examination and approval authority for approval;  
(3) After the geological report on mine closure is approved, the mining right holder shall prepare a report on mine closure and submit it to the competent department that originally approved the establishment of the mine, which, together with the competent department of geology and mineral resources at the same level and other relevant competent departments, shall approve it in accordance with the relevant trade provisions.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　关闭矿山报告批准后，矿山企业应当完成下列工作：  
(一)按照国家有关规定将地质、测量、采矿资料整理归档，并汇交闭坑地质报告、关闭矿山报告及其他有关资料；  
(二)按照批准的关闭矿山报告，完成有关劳动安全、水土保持、土地复垦和环境保护工作，或者缴清土地复垦和环境保护的有关费用。  
矿山企业凭关闭矿山报告批准文件和有关部门对完成上述工作提供的证明，报请原颁发采矿许可证的机关办理采矿许可证注销手续。

**Article 34** After the report on mine closure is approved, the mining enterprise shall complete the following work:  
(1) Organize and archive the geological, survey, and mining data in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and hand over the geological report on mine closure, the report on mine closure, and other relevant data;  
(2) Complete the relevant work concerning labor safety, water and soil conservation, land reclamation, and environmental protection in accordance with the approved report on mine closure, or pay off the relevant fees for land reclamation and environmental protection.  
The mining enterprise shall, on the basis of the approval document for the report on mine closure and the certificates provided by the relevant departments for the completion of the above work, apply to the authority that originally issued the mining license for the cancellation of the mining license.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　建设单位在建设铁路、公路、工厂、水库、输油管道、输电线路和各种大型建筑物前，必须向所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门了解拟建工程所在地区的矿产资源分布情况，并在建设项目设计任务书报请审批时附具地质矿产主管部门的证明。在上述建设项目与重要矿床的开采发生矛盾时，由国务院有关主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府提出方案，经国务院地质矿产主管部门提出意见后，报国务院计划行政主管部门决定。

**Article 35** Before constructing railways, highways, factories, reservoirs, oil pipelines, power transmission lines, and various large buildings, a construction entity must ascertain from the competent department of geology and mineral resources of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the proposed project is located the distribution of mineral resources in that area, and shall attach the certificate from the competent department of geology and mineral resources when submitting the design task document for the construction project for examination and approval. Where the above-mentioned construction project conflicts with the mining of important mineral deposits, the relevant competent department under the State Council or the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall propose a plan, and after the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council provides its opinion, the matter shall be submitted to the competent department of planning under the State Council for decision.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　采矿权人之间对矿区范围发生争议时，由当事人协商解决；协商不成的，由矿产资源所在地的县级以上地方人民政府根据依法核定的矿区范围处理；跨省、自治区、直辖市的矿区范围争议，当事人协商不成的，由有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府协商解决；协商不成的，由国务院地质矿产主管部门提出处理意见，报国务院决定。

**Article 36** Where a dispute arises between mining right holders over the scope of a mining area, it shall be settled by the parties through consultation; if consultation fails, it shall be handled by the local people's government at or above the county level where the mineral resources are located on the basis of the mining area scope verified according to law; for a dispute over the scope of a mining area that spans provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, if consultation between the parties fails, it shall be settled through consultation by the people's governments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government; if consultation fails, the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council shall put forward a handling opinion and submit it to the State Council for decision.

### 第五章　集体所有制矿山企业、私营矿山企业和个体采矿者

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家依法保护集体所有制矿山企业、私营矿山企业和个体采矿者的合法权益，依法对集体所有制矿山企业、私营矿山企业和个体采矿者进行监督管理。

**Article 37** The State shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of collectively-owned mining enterprises, privately-owned mining enterprises, and individual miners according to law, and shall exercise supervision and administration over collectively-owned mining enterprises, privately-owned mining enterprises, and individual miners according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　集体所有制矿山企业可以开采下列矿产资源：  
(一)不适于国家建设大、中型矿山的矿床及矿点；  
(二)经国有矿山企业同意，并经其上级主管部门批准，在其矿区范围内划出的边缘零星矿产；  
(三)矿山闭坑后，经原矿山企业主管部门确认可以安全开采并不会引起严重环境后果的残留矿体；  
(四)国家规划可以由集体所有制矿山企业开采的其他矿产资源。  
集体所有制矿山企业开采前款第(二)项所列矿产资源时，必须与国有矿山企业签定合理开发利用矿产资源和矿山安全协议，不得浪费和破坏矿产资源，并不得影响国有矿山企业的生产安全。

**Article 38** Collectively-owned mining enterprises may mine the following mineral resources:  
(1) Mineral deposits and occurrences that are not suitable for the construction of large and medium-sized state mines;  
(2) Marginal and scattered minerals delineated within the mining area of a state-owned mining enterprise with the consent of that enterprise and approval of its superior competent department;  
(3) Residual ore bodies that, after the closure of a mine, are confirmed by the competent department of the original mining enterprise to be capable of being safely mined without causing serious environmental consequences;  
(4) Other mineral resources that the state plans to be mined by collectively-owned mining enterprises.  
When a collectively-owned mining enterprise mines the mineral resources listed in item (2) of the preceding paragraph, it must sign an agreement with the state-owned mining enterprise on the rational development and utilization of mineral resources and mine safety, shall not waste or damage mineral resources, and shall not affect the production safety of the state-owned mining enterprise.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　私营矿山企业开采矿产资源的范围参照本细则第三十八条的规定执行。

**Article 39** The scope of mineral resources mined by privately-owned mining enterprises shall be implemented by reference to the provisions of Article 38 of these Detailed Rules.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　个体采矿者可以采挖下列矿产资源：  
(一)零星分散的小矿体或者矿点；  
(二)只能用作普通建筑材料的砂、石、粘土。

**Article 40** Individual miners may extract the following mineral resources:  
(1) Scattered and dispersed small ore bodies or mineral occurrences;  
(2) Sand, stone, and clay that can only be used as ordinary building materials.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家设立国家规划矿区、对国民经济具有重要价值的矿区时，对应当撤出的原采矿权人，国家按照有关规定给予合理补偿。

**Article 41** When the State establishes a state-planned mining area or a mining area of significant value to the national economy, the State shall give reasonable compensation to the original mining right holders that are required to withdraw in accordance with the relevant provisions.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　依照[《矿产资源法》第三十九条](../../../经济、税务与金融/农业、资源与能源/中华人民共和国矿产资源法.md#art-39)、第四十条、第四十二条、第四十三条、第四十四条规定处以罚款的，分别按照下列规定执行：  
(一)未取得采矿许可证擅自采矿的，擅自进入国家规划矿区、对国民经济具有重要价值的矿区和他人矿区范围采矿的，擅自开采国家规定实行保护性开采的特定矿种的，处以违法所得50%以下的罚款；  
(二)超越批准的矿区范围采矿的，处以违法所得30%以下的罚款；  
(三)买卖、出租或者以其他形式转让矿产资源的，买卖、出租采矿权的，对卖方、出租方、出让方处以违法所得一倍以下的罚款；  
(四)非法用采矿权作抵押的，处以5000元以下的罚款；  
(五)违反规定收购和销售国家规定统一收购的矿产品的，处以违法所得一倍以下的罚款；  
(六)采取破坏性的开采方法开采矿产资源，造成矿产资源严重破坏的，处以相当于矿产资源损失价值50%以下的罚款。

**Article 42** Where a fine is imposed in accordance with Articles 39, 40, 42, 43, and 44 of the Mineral Resources Law, the fine shall be executed respectively according to the following provisions:  
(1) For mining without a mining license, for entering a state-planned mining area, a mining area of significant value to the national economy, or another person's mining area without authorization to mine, or for mining specific types of minerals subject to protective mining as prescribed by the State without authorization, a fine of not more than 50% of the illegal income shall be imposed;  
(2) For mining beyond the approved mining area, a fine of not more than 30% of the illegal income shall be imposed;  
(3) For selling, leasing, or transferring mineral resources by other means, or for selling or leasing the mining right, a fine of not more than one time the illegal income shall be imposed on the seller, lessor, or transferor;  
(4) For illegally using the mining right as a mortgage, a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed;  
(5) For purchasing or selling mineral products that the State prescribes shall be purchased exclusively by designated entities in violation of regulations, a fine of not more than one time the illegal income shall be imposed;  
(6) For adopting destructive mining methods to mine mineral resources, causing serious damage to mineral resources, a fine of not more than 50% of the value of the mineral resource loss shall be imposed.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反本细则规定，有下列行为之一的，对主管人员和直接责任人员给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)批准不符合办矿条件的单位或者个人开办矿山的；  
(二)对未经依法批准的矿山企业或者个人颁发采矿许可证的。

**Article 43** Where any of the following acts is committed in violation of these Detailed Rules, the persons in charge and the persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Approving the establishment of a mine by an entity or individual that does not meet the conditions for mine operation;  
(2) Issuing a mining license to a mining enterprise or individual that has not been approved according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　地下水资源具有水资源和矿产资源的双重属性。地下水资源的勘查，适用《矿产资源法》和本细则；地下水资源的开发、利用、保护和管理，适用《水法》和有关的行政法规。

**Article 44** Groundwater resources have the dual attributes of water resources and mineral resources. The exploration of groundwater resources shall be governed by the Mineral Resources Law and these Detailed Rules; the development, utilization, protection, and management of groundwater resources shall be governed by the Water Law and relevant administrative regulations.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本细则由地质矿产部负责解释。

**Article 45** These Detailed Rules shall be interpreted by the Ministry of Geology and Mineral Resources.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本细则自发布之日起施行。  
附件：矿产资源分类细目

**Article 46** These Detailed Rules shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
