# 矿产资源勘查区块登记管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2014-07-29

> (1998年2月12日中华人民共和国国务院令第240号发布　根据2014年7月29日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了加强对矿产资源勘查的管理，保护探矿权人的合法权益，维护矿产资源勘查秩序，促进矿业发展，根据《中华人民共和国矿产资源法》，制定本办法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域勘查矿产资源，必须遵守本办法。 第三条　国家对矿产资源勘查实行统一的区块登记管理制度。矿产资源勘查工作区范围以经纬度1′×1′划分的区块为基本单位区块。每个勘查项目允许登记的最大范围： (一)矿泉水为10个基本单位区块； (二)金属矿产、非金属矿产、放射性矿产为40个基本单位区块； (三)地热、煤、水气矿产为200个基本单位区块； (四)石油、天然气矿产为2500个基本单位区块。 第四条　勘查下列矿产资源，由国务院地质矿产主管部门审批登记，颁发勘查许可证： (一)跨省、自治区、直辖市的矿产资源； (二)领海及中国管辖的其他海域的矿产资源； (三)外商投资勘查的矿产资源； (四)本办法附录所列的矿产资源。 勘查石油、天然气矿产的，经国务院指定的机关审查同意后，由国务院地质矿产主管部门登记，颁发勘查许可证。 勘查下列矿产资源，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门审批登记，颁发勘查许可证，并应当自发证之日起10日内，向国务院地质矿产主管部门备案：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对矿产资源勘查的管理，保护探矿权人的合法权益，维护矿产资源勘查秩序，促进矿业发展，根据《中华人民共和国矿产资源法》，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Mineral Resources Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of strengthening the administration of mineral resources exploration, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of exploration right holders, maintaining the order of mineral resources exploration, and promoting the development of the mining industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域勘查矿产资源，必须遵守本办法。

**Article 2** Any person that explores for mineral resources within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction must comply with these Measures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对矿产资源勘查实行统一的区块登记管理制度。矿产资源勘查工作区范围以经纬度1′×1′划分的区块为基本单位区块。每个勘查项目允许登记的最大范围：  
(一)矿泉水为10个基本单位区块；  
(二)金属矿产、非金属矿产、放射性矿产为40个基本单位区块；  
(三)地热、煤、水气矿产为200个基本单位区块；  
(四)石油、天然气矿产为2500个基本单位区块。

**Article 3** The State shall implement a unified block registration administration system for mineral resources exploration. The scope of the mineral resources exploration working area shall take a block divided by longitude and latitude of 1′×1′ as the basic unit block. The maximum scope permitted for registration of each exploration project:  
(1) mineral water: 10 basic unit blocks;  
(2) metallic minerals, non-metallic minerals, and radioactive minerals: 40 basic unit blocks;  
(3) geothermal, coal, and water-gas minerals: 200 basic unit blocks;  
(4) petroleum and natural gas minerals: 2,500 basic unit blocks.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　勘查下列矿产资源，由国务院地质矿产主管部门审批登记，颁发勘查许可证：  
(一)跨省、自治区、直辖市的矿产资源；  
(二)领海及中国管辖的其他海域的矿产资源；  
(三)外商投资勘查的矿产资源；  
(四)本办法附录所列的矿产资源。  
勘查石油、天然气矿产的，经国务院指定的机关审查同意后，由国务院地质矿产主管部门登记，颁发勘查许可证。  
勘查下列矿产资源，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门审批登记，颁发勘查许可证，并应当自发证之日起10日内，向国务院地质矿产主管部门备案：  
(一)本条第一款、第二款规定以外的矿产资源；  
(二)国务院地质矿产主管部门授权省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门审批登记的矿产资源。

**Article 4** The exploration of the following mineral resources shall be subject to examination, approval, registration and the issuance of exploration licenses by the competent geology and mineral resources department of the State Council:  
(1) mineral resources spanning provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(2) mineral resources in the territorial sea and other sea areas under China's jurisdiction;  
(3) mineral resources explored with foreign investment;  
(4) mineral resources listed in the appendix to these Measures.  
For the exploration of petroleum and natural gas minerals, after examination and consent by the organ designated by the State Council, the competent geology and mineral resources department of the State Council shall register and issue an exploration license.  
The exploration of the following mineral resources shall be subject to examination, approval, registration and the issuance of exploration licenses by the competent geology and mineral resources department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and such department shall, within 10 days from the date of issuance, report to the competent geology and mineral resources department of the State Council for record:  
(1) mineral resources other than those specified in paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article;  
(2) mineral resources for which the competent geology and mineral resources department of the State Council has authorized the competent geology and mineral resources department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to examine, approve, and register.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　勘查出资人为探矿权申请人；但是，国家出资勘查的，国家委托勘查的单位为探矿权申请人。

**Article 5** The person that provides funds for exploration shall be the applicant for an exploration right; provided that, for exploration funded by the State, the entity commissioned by the State for exploration shall be the applicant for the exploration right.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　探矿权申请人申请探矿权时，应当向登记管理机关提交下列资料：  
(一)申请登记书和申请的区块范围图；  
(二)勘查单位的资格证书复印件；  
(三)勘查工作计划、勘查合同或者委托勘查的证明文件；  
(四)勘查实施方案及附件；  
(五)勘查项目资金来源证明；  
(六)国务院地质矿产主管部门规定提交的其他资料。  
申请勘查石油、天然气的，还应当提交国务院批准设立石油公司或者同意进行石油、天然气勘查的批准文件以及勘查单位法人资格证明。

**Article 6** When applying for an exploration right, the applicant shall submit the following documents to the registration authority:  
(1) a registration application form and a map of the applied block scope;  
(2) a copy of the qualification certificate of the exploration entity;  
(3) the exploration work plan, the exploration contract, or the certifying documents for commissioned exploration;  
(4) the exploration implementation plan and its appendices;  
(5) certifying documents for the source of funds for the exploration project;  
(6) other documents as required by the competent geology and mineral resources department of the State Council.  
In applying for the exploration of petroleum and natural gas, the applicant shall also submit the approval document of the State Council for the establishment of a petroleum company or for consent to the exploration of petroleum and natural gas, and the certifying documents for the legal person status of the exploration entity.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请石油、天然气滚动勘探开发的，应当向登记管理机关提交下列资料，经批准，办理登记手续，领取滚动勘探开发的采矿许可证：  
(一)申请登记书和滚动勘探开发矿区范围图；  
(二)国务院计划主管部门批准的项目建议书；  
(三)需要进行滚动勘探开发的论证材料；  
(四)经国务院矿产储量审批机构批准进行石油、天然气滚动勘探开发的储量报告；  
(五)滚动勘探开发利用方案。

**Article 7** Any person that applies for progressive exploration and development of petroleum and natural gas shall submit the following documents to the registration authority, and after approval, go through the registration formalities and obtain the mining license for progressive exploration and development:  
(1) a registration application form and a map of the mining area for progressive exploration and development;  
(2) the project proposal approved by the competent planning department of the State Council;  
(3) demonstration materials showing the need for progressive exploration and development;  
(4) the reserve report approved by the mineral reserve approval authority of the State Council for the progressive exploration and development of petroleum and natural gas;  
(5) the plan for the utilization of progressive exploration and development.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　登记管理机关应当自收到申请之日起40日内，按照申请在先的原则作出准予登记或者不予登记的决定，并通知探矿权申请人。对申请勘查石油、天然气的，登记管理机关还应当在收到申请后及时予以公告或者提供查询。  
登记管理机关应当保证国家地质勘查计划一类项目的登记，具体办法由国务院地质矿产主管部门会同国务院计划主管部门制定。  
需要探矿权申请人修改或者补充[本办法第六条](#art-6)规定的资料的，登记管理机关应当通知探矿权申请人限期修改或者补充。  
准予登记时，探矿权申请人应当自收到通知之日起30日内，依照[本办法第十二条](#art-12)的规定缴纳探矿权使用费，并依照[本办法第十三条](#art-13)的规定缴纳国家出资勘查形成的探矿权价款，办理登记手续，领取勘查许可证，成为探矿权人。  
不予登记的，登记管理机关应当向探矿权申请人说明理由。

**Article 8** The registration authority shall, within 40 days from the date of receipt of the application, make a decision to grant or deny registration on a first-come, first-served basis, and notify the applicant for the exploration right. In the case of an application for the exploration of petroleum and natural gas, the registration authority shall also promptly make a public announcement or provide inquiry services after receiving the application.  
The registration authority shall ensure the registration of Category I projects under the national geological exploration plan. The specific measures therefor shall be formulated by the competent geology and mineral resources department of the State Council jointly with the competent planning department of the State Council.  
If it is necessary for the applicant for the exploration right to modify or supplement the documents specified in Article 6 of these Measures, the registration authority shall notify the applicant to make such modification or supplement within a specified time limit.  
Upon granting the registration, the applicant for the exploration right shall, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the notice, pay the exploration right user fee in accordance with Article 12 of these Measures, and pay the exploration right price formed by State-funded exploration in accordance with Article 13 of these Measures, go through the registration formalities, obtain the exploration license, and become the exploration right holder.  
If registration is denied, the registration authority shall explain the reasons to the applicant for the exploration right.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　禁止任何单位和个人进入他人依法取得探矿权的勘查作业区内进行勘查或者采矿活动。  
探矿权人与采矿权人对勘查作业区范围和矿区范围发生争议的，由当事人协商解决；协商不成的，由发证的登记管理机关中级别高的登记管理机关裁决。

**Article 9** No entity or individual may enter an exploration working area for which another person has lawfully obtained an exploration right to carry out exploration or mining activities.  
If a dispute arises between an exploration right holder and a mining right holder over the scope of the exploration working area and the mining area, the parties shall settle such dispute through consultation; if consultation fails, the dispute shall be adjudicated by the registration authority at a higher level among the registration authorities that issued the licenses.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　勘查许可证有效期最长为3年；但是，石油、天然气勘查许可证有效期最长为7年。需要延长勘查工作时间的，探矿权人应当在勘查许可证有效期届满的30日前，到登记管理机关办理延续登记手续，每次延续时间不得超过2年。  
探矿权人逾期不办理延续登记手续的，勘查许可证自行废止。  
石油、天然气滚动勘探开发的采矿许可证有效期最长为15年；但是，探明储量的区块，应当申请办理采矿许可证。

**Article 10** The validity period of an exploration license shall be a maximum of 3 years; however, the validity period of an exploration license for petroleum and natural gas shall be a maximum of 7 years. If it is necessary to extend the exploration work period, the exploration right holder shall, 30 days prior to the expiry of the validity period of the exploration license, go to the registration authority to go through the extension registration formalities, and each extension shall not exceed 2 years.  
If the exploration right holder fails to go through the extension registration formalities within the prescribed time limit, the exploration license shall be automatically abolished.  
The validity period of a mining license for progressive exploration and development of petroleum and natural gas shall be a maximum of 15 years; however, for a block with proven reserves, an application shall be filed to go through the formalities for obtaining a mining license.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　登记管理机关应当自颁发勘查许可证之日起10日内，将登记发证项目的名称、探矿权人、区块范围和勘查许可证期限等事项，通知勘查项目所在地的县级人民政府负责地质矿产管理工作的部门。  
登记管理机关对勘查区块登记发证情况，应当定期予以公告。

**Article 11** The registration authority shall, within 10 days from the date of issuance of the exploration license, notify the department responsible for geology and mineral resources administration of the people's government at the county level where the exploration project is located of the name of the registered and licensed project, the exploration right holder, the block scope, and the term of the exploration license.  
The registration authority shall regularly make a public announcement of the registration and licensing status of exploration blocks.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家实行探矿权有偿取得的制度。探矿权使用费以勘查年度计算，逐年缴纳。  
探矿权使用费标准：第一个勘查年度至第三个勘查年度，每平方公里每年缴纳100元；从第四个勘查年度起，每平方公里每年增加100元，但是最高不得超过每平方公里每年500元。

**Article 12** The State shall implement a system of obtaining exploration rights with consideration. The exploration right user fee shall be calculated on the basis of the exploration year and paid on an annual basis.  
The standard for the exploration right user fee: from the first exploration year to the third exploration year, 100 yuan per square kilometer per year; starting from the fourth exploration year, an additional 100 yuan per square kilometer per year shall be added, provided that the maximum shall not exceed 500 yuan per square kilometer per year.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　申请国家出资勘查并已经探明矿产地的区块的探矿权的，探矿权申请人除依照[本办法第十二条](#art-12)的规定缴纳探矿权使用费外，还应当缴纳国家出资勘查形成的探矿权价款；探矿权价款按照国家有关规定，可以一次缴纳，也可以分期缴纳。  
国家出资勘查形成的探矿权价款，由具有矿业权评估资质的评估机构进行评估；评估报告报登记管理机关备案。

**Article 13** Where an applicant applies for an exploration right over a block that has been explored with State funds and where a mineral deposit has already been identified, the applicant for the exploration right shall, in addition to paying the exploration right user fee in accordance with Article 12 of these Measures, also pay the exploration right price formed by the State-funded exploration; the exploration right price may be paid in a lump sum or in installments in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The exploration right price formed by State-funded exploration shall be assessed by an assessment institution with the qualification for mining rights assessment; the assessment report shall be submitted to the registration authority for record.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　探矿权使用费和国家出资勘查形成的探矿权价款，由登记管理机关收取，全部纳入国家预算管理。具体管理、使用办法，由国务院地质矿产主管部门会同国务院财政部门、计划主管部门制定。

**Article 14** The exploration right user fee and the exploration right price formed by State-funded exploration shall be collected by the registration authority and shall be fully incorporated into the State budget management. The specific measures for the management and use thereof shall be formulated by the competent geology and mineral resources department of the State Council jointly with the competent finance department and the competent planning department of the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　有下列情形之一的，由探矿权人提出申请，经登记管理机关按照国务院地质矿产主管部门会同国务院财政部门制定的探矿权使用费和探矿权价款的减免办法审查批准，可以减缴、免缴探矿权使用费和探矿权价款：  
(一)国家鼓励勘查的矿种；  
(二)国家鼓励勘查的区域；  
(三)国务院地质矿产主管部门会同国务院财政部门规定的其他情形。

**Article 15** Under any of the following circumstances, upon application by the exploration right holder, and after examination and approval by the registration authority in accordance with the measures for reduction and exemption of exploration right user fees and exploration right prices formulated by the competent geology and mineral resources department of the State Council jointly with the competent finance department of the State Council, the exploration right user fee and the exploration right price may be reduced or exempted:  
(1) mineral varieties the exploration of which is encouraged by the State;  
(2) regions the exploration of which is encouraged by the State;  
(3) other circumstances as prescribed by the competent geology and mineral resources department of the State Council jointly with the competent finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　探矿权可以通过招标投标的方式有偿取得。  
登记管理机关依照[本办法第四条](#art-4)规定的权限确定招标区块，发布招标公告，提出投标要求和截止日期；但是，对境外招标的区块由国务院地质矿产主管部门确定。  
登记管理机关组织评标，采取择优原则确定中标人。中标人缴纳[本办法第十二条](#art-12)、第十三条规定的费用后，办理登记手续，领取勘查许可证，成为探矿权人，并履行标书中承诺的义务。

**Article 16** An exploration right may be obtained with consideration through tender and bidding.  
The registration authority shall, in accordance with the authority provided in Article 4 of these Measures, determine the tender blocks, issue a tender announcement, and set forth the bidding requirements and the deadline; however, blocks offered for tender to overseas bidders shall be determined by the competent geology and mineral resources department of the State Council.  
The registration authority shall organize the evaluation of bids and determine the winning bidder based on the principle of selecting the best. After paying the fees specified in Articles 12 and 13 of these Measures, the winning bidder shall go through the registration formalities, obtain an exploration license, become an exploration right holder, and perform the obligations undertaken in the bid.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　探矿权人应当自领取勘查许可证之日起，按照下列规定完成最低勘查投入：  
(一)第一个勘查年度，每平方公里2000元；  
(二)第二个勘查年度，每平方公里5000元；  
(三)从第三个勘查年度起，每个勘查年度每平方公里10000元。  
探矿权人当年度的勘查投入高于最低勘查投入标准的，高于的部分可以计入下一个勘查年度的勘查投入。  
因自然灾害等不可抗力的原因，致使勘查工作不能正常进行的，探矿权人应当自恢复正常勘查工作之日起30日内，向登记管理机关提交申请核减相应的最低勘查投入的报告；登记管理机关应当自收到报告之日起30日内予以批复。

**Article 17** The exploration right holder shall, from the date of obtaining the exploration license, complete the minimum exploration input in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) for the first exploration year, 2,000 yuan per square kilometer;  
(2) for the second exploration year, 5,000 yuan per square kilometer;  
(3) starting from the third exploration year, 10,000 yuan per square kilometer per exploration year.  
If the exploration input of the exploration right holder in the current year exceeds the minimum exploration input standard, the excess portion may be counted toward the exploration input of the next exploration year.  
If exploration work cannot be carried out normally due to force majeure causes such as natural disasters, the exploration right holder shall, within 30 days from the date of resumption of normal exploration work, submit to the registration authority a report applying for a reduction of the corresponding minimum exploration input; the registration authority shall, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the report, give a reply.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　探矿权人应当自领取勘查许可证之日起6个月内开始施工；在开始勘查工作时，应当向勘查项目所在地的县级人民政府负责地质矿产管理工作的部门报告，并向登记管理机关报告开工情况。

**Article 18** The exploration right holder shall commence operations within 6 months from the date of obtaining the exploration license; when commencing exploration work, it shall report to the department responsible for geology and mineral resources administration of the people's government at the county level where the exploration project is located, and shall report the commencement of work to the registration authority.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　探矿权人在勘查许可证有效期内进行勘查时，发现符合国家边探边采规定要求的复杂类型矿床的，可以申请开采，经登记管理机关批准，办理采矿登记手续。

**Article 19** If, during the exploration within the validity period of the exploration license, an exploration right holder discovers a complex type mineral deposit that meets the requirements of the national provisions on concurrent exploration and mining, it may apply for mining, and after approval by the registration authority, go through the mining registration formalities.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　探矿权人在勘查石油、天然气等流体矿产期间，需要试采的，应当向登记管理机关提交试采申请，经批准后可以试采1年；需要延长试采时间的，必须办理登记手续。

**Article 20** If, during the exploration of fluid minerals such as petroleum and natural gas, the exploration right holder needs to carry out trial production, it shall submit an application for trial production to the registration authority, and after approval, it may conduct trial production for one year; if it is necessary to extend the trial production period, it must go through the registration formalities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　探矿权人在勘查许可证有效期内探明可供开采的矿体后，经登记管理机关批准，可以停止相应区块的最低勘查投入，并可以在勘查许可证有效期届满的30日前，申请保留探矿权。但是，国家为了公共利益或者因技术条件暂时难以利用等情况，需要延期开采的除外。  
保留探矿权的期限，最长不得超过2年，需要延长保留期的，可以申请延长2次，每次不得超过2年；保留探矿权的范围为可供开采的矿体范围。  
在停止最低勘查投入期间或者探矿权保留期间，探矿权人应当依照本办法的规定，缴纳探矿权使用费。  
探矿权保留期届满，勘查许可证应当予以注销。

**Article 21** 第二十一条 After the prospecting right holder has discovered a mineable ore body within the valid term of the exploration license, subject to approval by the registration authority, it may suspend the minimum exploration investment in the corresponding block and may apply for retention of the prospecting right 30 days prior to the expiration of the valid term of the exploration license, unless the State needs to extend the exploitation due to public interest or temporary technical difficulties in utilization.  
The retention period of the prospecting right shall not exceed two years at maximum; if an extension of the retention period is needed, an application may be filed for two extensions, each not exceeding two years; the scope of the retained prospecting right shall be the scope of the mineable ore body.  
During the period of suspension of minimum exploration investment or the period of retention of the prospecting right, the prospecting right holder shall pay the prospecting right use fee in accordance with these Measures.  
Upon expiration of the retention period of the prospecting right, the exploration license shall be cancelled.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　有下列情形之一的，探矿权人应当在勘查许可证有效期内，向登记管理机关申请变更登记：  
(一)扩大或者缩小勘查区块范围的；  
(二)改变勘查工作对象的；  
(三)经依法批准转让探矿权的；  
(四)探矿权人改变名称或者地址的。

**Article 22** 第二十二条 Where any of the following circumstances occurs, the prospecting right holder shall apply to the registration authority for modification registration within the valid term of the exploration license:  
(1) expanding or reducing the scope of the exploration block;  
(2) changing the object of exploration work;  
(3) transferring the prospecting right upon approval according to law; or  
(4) changing the name or address of the prospecting right holder.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　探矿权延续登记和变更登记，其勘查年度、探矿权使用费和最低勘查投入连续计算。

**Article 23** 第二十三条 For the renewal registration and modification registration of the prospecting right, the exploration year, prospecting right use fee and minimum exploration investment shall be calculated consecutively.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　有下列情形之一的，探矿权人应当在勘查许可证有效期内，向登记管理机关递交勘查项目完成报告或者勘查项目终止报告，报送资金投入情况报表和有关证明文件，由登记管理机关核定其实际勘查投入后，办理勘查许可证注销登记手续：  
(一)勘查许可证有效期届满，不办理延续登记或者不申请保留探矿权的；  
(二)申请采矿权的；  
(三)因故需要撤销勘查项目的。  
自勘查许可证注销之日起90日内，原探矿权人不得申请已经注销的区块范围内的探矿权。

**Article 24** 第二十四条 Where any of the following circumstances occurs, the prospecting right holder shall submit a report on completion of the exploration project or a report on termination of the exploration project to the registration authority within the valid term of the exploration license, together with a statement of capital input and relevant supporting documents, and shall undergo the formalities for cancellation registration of the exploration license after the registration authority verifies its actual exploration input:  
(1) the valid term of the exploration license expires and no renewal registration is handled or no application for retention of the prospecting right is filed;  
(2) an application for mining right is filed; or  
(3) the exploration project needs to be withdrawn for some reason.  
Within 90 days from the date of cancellation of the exploration license, the original prospecting right holder shall not apply for the prospecting right in the block scope that has been cancelled.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　登记管理机关需要调查勘查投入、勘查工作进展情况，探矿权人应当如实报告并提供有关资料，不得虚报、瞒报，不得拒绝检查。  
对探矿权人要求保密的申请登记资料、勘查工作成果资料和财务报表，登记管理机关应当予以保密。

**Article 25** 第二十五条 Where the registration authority needs to investigate the exploration input and the progress of exploration work, the prospecting right holder shall report truthfully and provide relevant materials, and shall not make false reports, conceal information, or refuse inspection.  
With respect to the application registration materials, exploration work result materials and financial statements that the prospecting right holder requires to be kept confidential, the registration authority shall maintain confidentiality.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反本办法规定，未取得勘查许可证擅自进行勘查工作的，超越批准的勘查区块范围进行勘查工作的，由县级以上人民政府负责地质矿产管理工作的部门按照国务院地质矿产主管部门规定的权限，责令停止违法行为，予以警告，可以并处10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 26** 第二十六条 In violation of these Measures, whoever engages in exploration work without obtaining an exploration license, or engages in exploration work beyond the scope of the approved exploration block, shall be ordered by the department responsible for geological and mineral resources management under the people's government at or above the county level, within the authority prescribed by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council, to cease the illegal act, be given a warning, and may also be imposed a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反本办法规定，未经批准，擅自进行滚动勘探开发、边探边采或者试采的，由县级以上人民政府负责地质矿产管理工作的部门按照国务院地质矿产主管部门规定的权限，责令停止违法行为，予以警告，没收违法所得，可以并处10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 27** 第二十七条 In violation of these Measures, whoever, without approval, engages in rolling exploration and development, prospecting while mining, or trial mining, shall be ordered by the department responsible for geological and mineral resources management under the people's government at or above the county level, within the authority prescribed by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council, to cease the illegal act, be given a warning, have the illegal gains confiscated, and may also be imposed a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　违反本办法规定，擅自印制或者伪造、冒用勘查许可证的，由县级以上人民政府负责地质矿产管理工作的部门按照国务院地质矿产主管部门规定的权限，没收违法所得，可以并处10万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** 第二十八条 In violation of these Measures, whoever prints, forges or fraudulently uses an exploration license without authorization, shall be subject to confiscation of illegal gains by the department responsible for geological and mineral resources management under the people's government at or above the county level, within the authority prescribed by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council, and may also be imposed a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　违反本办法规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府负责地质矿产管理工作的部门按照国务院地质矿产主管部门规定的权限，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，原发证机关可以吊销勘查许可证：  
(一)不按照本办法的规定备案、报告有关情况、拒绝接受监督检查或者弄虚作假的；  
(二)未完成最低勘查投入的；  
(三)已经领取勘查许可证的勘查项目，满6个月未开始施工，或者施工后无故停止勘查工作满6个月的。

**Article 29** 第二十九条 In violation of these Measures, whoever commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the department responsible for geological and mineral resources management under the people's government at or above the county level, within the authority prescribed by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council, to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if no correction is made within the time limit, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the original issuing authority may revoke the exploration license:  
(1) failing to file for record, report relevant information in accordance with these Measures, refusing to accept supervision and inspection, or practicing fraud;  
(2) failing to complete the minimum exploration investment; or  
(3) for an exploration project that has obtained an exploration license, failing to commence construction within six months, or suspending exploration work without just cause for six months after commencement.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　违反本办法规定，不办理勘查许可证变更登记或者注销登记手续的，由登记管理机关责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，由原发证机关吊销勘查许可证。

**Article 30** 第三十条 In violation of these Measures, whoever fails to go through the formalities for modification registration or cancellation registration of the exploration license, shall be ordered by the registration authority to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if no correction is made within the time limit, the original issuing authority shall revoke the exploration license.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　违反本办法规定，不按期缴纳本办法规定应当缴纳的费用的，由登记管理机关责令限期缴纳，并从滞纳之日起每日加收2‰的滞纳金；逾期仍不缴纳的，由原发证机关吊销勘查许可证。

**Article 31** 第三十一条 In violation of these Measures, whoever fails to pay the fees due under these Measures on time shall be ordered by the registration authority to pay within a prescribed time limit and shall be charged an additional daily late fee of 2‰ from the date of default; if still not paid after the time limit, the original issuing authority shall revoke the exploration license.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　违反本办法规定勘查石油、天然气矿产的，由国务院地质矿产主管部门按照本办法的有关规定给予行政处罚。

**Article 32** 第三十二条 In violation of these Measures, whoever explores for oil or natural gas minerals shall be given administrative penalties by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Measures.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　探矿权人被吊销勘查许可证的，自勘查许可证被吊销之日起6个月内，不得再申请探矿权。

**Article 33** 第三十三条 If the prospecting right holder's exploration license is revoked, it shall not apply for the prospecting right again within six months from the date of revocation of the exploration license.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　登记管理机关工作人员徇私舞弊、滥用职权、玩忽职守，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 34** 第三十四条 Where any staff member of the registration authority engages in malpractice for personal gain, abuses power, or neglects duty, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　勘查许可证由国务院地质矿产主管部门统一印制。申请登记书、变更申请登记书、探矿权保留申请登记书和注销申请登记书的格式，由国务院地质矿产主管部门统一制定。

**Article 35** 第三十五条 The exploration license shall be uniformly printed by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council. The formats of the application registration form, modification application registration form, prospecting right retention application registration form and cancellation application registration form shall be uniformly formulated by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　办理勘查登记手续，应当按照规定缴纳登记费。收费标准和管理、使用办法，由国务院物价主管部门会同国务院地质矿产主管部门、财政部门规定。

**Article 36** 第三十六条 When undergoing the formalities for exploration registration, the registration fee shall be paid in accordance with the provisions. The charging standards and the measures for management and use shall be prescribed by the competent department of price under the State Council jointly with the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council and the financial department.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　外商投资勘查矿产资源的，依照本办法的规定办理；法律、行政法规另有特别规定的，从其规定。

**Article 37** 第三十七条 Where foreign investment is used to explore for mineral resources, the matter shall be handled in accordance with these Measures; if there are special provisions in laws or administrative regulations, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　中外合作勘查矿产资源的，中方合作者应当在签订合同后，将合同向原发证机关备案。

**Article 38** 第三十八条 Where Chinese and foreign parties cooperate in exploring for mineral resources, the Chinese party shall, after signing the contract, file the contract with the original issuing authority for record.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本办法施行前已经取得勘查许可证的，由国务院地质矿产主管部门统一组织换领新的勘查许可证。探矿权使用费、最低勘查投入按照重新登记后的第一个勘查年度计算，并可以依照本办法的规定申请减缴、免缴。

**Article 39** 第三十九条 Where an exploration license has been obtained before these Measures come into force, the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council shall uniformly organize the exchange for a new exploration license. The prospecting right use fee and the minimum exploration investment shall be calculated based on the first exploration year after re-registration, and may be applied for reduction or exemption in accordance with the provisions of these Measures.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　本办法附录的修改，由国务院地质矿产主管部门报国务院批准后公布。

**Article 40** 第四十条 Amendments to the appendix of these Measures shall be reported by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council to the State Council for approval and then promulgated.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　本办法自发布之日起施行。1987年4月29日国务院发布的《矿产资源勘查登记管理暂行办法》和1987年12月16日国务院批准、石油工业部发布的《石油及天然气勘查、开采登记管理暂行办法》同时废止。

**Article 41** These Measures shall come into force from the date of issuance. The Interim Procedures for the Registration and Management of Mineral Resources Exploration issued by the State Council on April 29, 1987 and the Interim Procedures for the Registration and Management of Petroleum and Natural Gas Exploration and Production approved by the State Council and issued by the Ministry of Petroleum Industry on December 16, 1987 shall be abolished simultaneously.
