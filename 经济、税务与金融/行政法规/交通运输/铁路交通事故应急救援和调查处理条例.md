# 铁路交通事故应急救援和调查处理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2012-11-09  
**生效日期**：2007-09-01

> (2007年7月11日中华人民共和国国务院令第501号公布　根据2012年11月9日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强铁路交通事故的应急救援工作，规范铁路交通事故调查处理，减少人员伤亡和财产损失，保障铁路运输安全和畅通，根据《中华人民共和国铁路法》和其他有关法律的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　铁路机车车辆在运行过程中与行人、机动车、非机动车、牲畜及其他障碍物相撞，或者铁路机车车辆发生冲突、脱轨、火灾、爆炸等影响铁路正常行车的铁路交通事故(以下简称事故)的应急救援和调查处理，适用本条例。 第三条　国务院铁路主管部门应当加强铁路运输安全监督管理，建立健全事故应急救援和调查处理的各项制度，按照国家规定的权限和程序，负责组织、指挥、协调事故的应急救援和调查处理工作。 第四条　铁路管理机构应当加强日常的铁路运输安全监督检查，指导、督促铁路运输企业落实事故应急救援的各项规定，按照规定的权限和程序，组织、参与、协调本辖区内事故的应急救援和调查处理工作。 第五条　国务院其他有关部门和有关地方人民政府应当按照各自的职责和分工，组织、参与事故的应急救援和调查处理工作。 第六条　铁路运输企业和其他有关单位、个人应当遵守铁路运输安全管理的各项规定，防止和避免事故的发生。 事故发生后，铁路运输企业和其他有关单位应当及时、准确地报告事故情况，积极开展应急救援工作，减少人员伤亡和财产损失，尽快恢复铁路正常行车。 第七条　任何单位和个人不得干扰、阻碍事故应急救援、铁路线路开通、列车运行和事故调查处理。 第二章　事故等级 第八条　根据事故造成的人员伤亡、直接经济损失、列车脱轨辆数、中断铁路行车时间等情形，事故等级分为特别重大事故、重大事故、较大事故和一般事故。 第九条　有下列情形之一的，为特别重大事故： (一)造成30人以上死亡，或者100人以上重伤(包括急性工业中毒，下同)，或者1亿元以上直接经济损失的； (二)繁忙干线客运列车脱轨18辆以上并中断铁路行车48小时以上的；

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强铁路交通事故的应急救援工作，规范铁路交通事故调查处理，减少人员伤亡和财产损失，保障铁路运输安全和畅通，根据《中华人民共和国铁路法》和其他有关法律的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** For the purposes of strengthening the emergency rescue work for railway traffic accidents, regulating the investigation and handling of railway traffic accidents, reducing casualties and property losses, and ensuring the safety and smoothness of railway transportation, these Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Railway Law of the People's Republic of China and the provisions of other relevant laws.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　铁路机车车辆在运行过程中与行人、机动车、非机动车、牲畜及其他障碍物相撞，或者铁路机车车辆发生冲突、脱轨、火灾、爆炸等影响铁路正常行车的铁路交通事故(以下简称事故)的应急救援和调查处理，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the emergency rescue, investigation and handling of railway traffic accidents (hereinafter referred to as accidents) where railway locomotives or rolling stock collide with pedestrians, motor vehicles, non-motor vehicles, livestock or other obstacles during operation, or where railway locomotives or rolling stock are involved in collisions, derailments, fires, explosions or other incidents that affect normal railway operation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院铁路主管部门应当加强铁路运输安全监督管理，建立健全事故应急救援和调查处理的各项制度，按照国家规定的权限和程序，负责组织、指挥、协调事故的应急救援和调查处理工作。

**Article 3** The competent department of railway under the State Council shall strengthen the supervision and administration of railway transportation safety, establish and perfect various systems for accident emergency rescue, investigation and handling, and shall be responsible for organizing, commanding and coordinating the emergency rescue, investigation and handling of accidents in accordance with the limits of authority and procedures prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　铁路管理机构应当加强日常的铁路运输安全监督检查，指导、督促铁路运输企业落实事故应急救援的各项规定，按照规定的权限和程序，组织、参与、协调本辖区内事故的应急救援和调查处理工作。

**Article 4** The railway administration authority shall strengthen daily supervision and inspection of railway transportation safety, guide and urge railway transport enterprises to implement various provisions on accident emergency rescue, and shall organize, participate in and coordinate the emergency rescue, investigation and handling of accidents within its jurisdiction in accordance with the prescribed limits of authority and procedures.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院其他有关部门和有关地方人民政府应当按照各自的职责和分工，组织、参与事故的应急救援和调查处理工作。

**Article 5** Other relevant departments of the State Council and relevant local people's governments shall organize and participate in the emergency rescue, investigation and handling of accidents in accordance with their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　铁路运输企业和其他有关单位、个人应当遵守铁路运输安全管理的各项规定，防止和避免事故的发生。  
事故发生后，铁路运输企业和其他有关单位应当及时、准确地报告事故情况，积极开展应急救援工作，减少人员伤亡和财产损失，尽快恢复铁路正常行车。

**Article 6** Railway transport enterprises and other relevant entities and individuals shall abide by the provisions on railway transportation safety management to prevent and avoid the occurrence of accidents.  
After an accident occurs, the railway transport enterprises and other relevant entities shall report the accident situation timely and accurately, actively carry out emergency rescue work, reduce casualties and property losses, and restore normal railway operation as soon as possible.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　任何单位和个人不得干扰、阻碍事故应急救援、铁路线路开通、列车运行和事故调查处理。

**Article 7** No entity or individual may interfere with or impede accident emergency rescue, opening of railway lines, train operation, or accident investigation and handling.

### 第二章　事故等级

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　根据事故造成的人员伤亡、直接经济损失、列车脱轨辆数、中断铁路行车时间等情形，事故等级分为特别重大事故、重大事故、较大事故和一般事故。

**Article 8** According to the casualties, direct economic losses, number of derailed trains, duration of interruption of railway operation and other circumstances caused by an accident, the accident grade shall be classified into particularly major accident, major accident, relatively major accident and ordinary accident.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　有下列情形之一的，为特别重大事故：  
(一)造成30人以上死亡，或者100人以上重伤(包括急性工业中毒，下同)，或者1亿元以上直接经济损失的；  
(二)繁忙干线客运列车脱轨18辆以上并中断铁路行车48小时以上的；  
(三)繁忙干线货运列车脱轨60辆以上并中断铁路行车48小时以上的。

**Article 9** Any of the following circumstances shall constitute a particularly major accident:  
(1) causing death of 30 or more persons, or serious injury (including acute industrial poisoning, the same below) of 100 or more persons, or direct economic losses of RMB 100 million yuan or more;  
(2) 18 or more passenger train cars derailed on busy main lines and interruption of railway operation for 48 hours or more;  
(3) 60 or more freight train cars derailed on busy main lines and interruption of railway operation for 48 hours or more.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　有下列情形之一的，为重大事故：  
(一)造成10人以上30人以下死亡，或者50人以上100人以下重伤，或者5000万元以上1亿元以下直接经济损失的；  
(二)客运列车脱轨18辆以上的；  
(三)货运列车脱轨60辆以上的；  
(四)客运列车脱轨2辆以上18辆以下，并中断繁忙干线铁路行车24小时以上或者中断其他线路铁路行车48小时以上的；  
(五)货运列车脱轨6辆以上60辆以下，并中断繁忙干线铁路行车24小时以上或者中断其他线路铁路行车48小时以上的。

**Article 10** Any of the following circumstances shall constitute a major accident:  
(1) causing death of 10 or more but less than 30 persons, or serious injury of 50 or more but less than 100 persons, or direct economic losses of RMB 50 million yuan or more but less than RMB 100 million yuan;  
(2) 18 or more passenger train cars derailed;  
(3) 60 or more freight train cars derailed;  
(4) 2 or more but less than 18 passenger train cars derailed, and interruption of railway operation on busy main lines for 24 hours or more, or interruption of railway operation on other lines for 48 hours or more;  
(5) 6 or more but less than 60 freight train cars derailed, and interruption of railway operation on busy main lines for 24 hours or more, or interruption of railway operation on other lines for 48 hours or more.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　有下列情形之一的，为较大事故：  
(一)造成3人以上10人以下死亡，或者10人以上50人以下重伤，或者1000万元以上5000万元以下直接经济损失的；  
(二)客运列车脱轨2辆以上18辆以下的；  
(三)货运列车脱轨6辆以上60辆以下的；  
(四)中断繁忙干线铁路行车6小时以上的；  
(五)中断其他线路铁路行车10小时以上的。

**Article 11** Any of the following circumstances shall constitute a relatively major accident:  
(1) causing death of 3 or more but less than 10 persons, or serious injury of 10 or more but less than 50 persons, or direct economic losses of RMB 10 million yuan or more but less than RMB 50 million yuan;  
(2) 2 or more but less than 18 passenger train cars derailed;  
(3) 6 or more but less than 60 freight train cars derailed;  
(4) interruption of railway operation on busy main lines for 6 hours or more;  
(5) interruption of railway operation on other lines for 10 hours or more.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　造成3人以下死亡，或者10人以下重伤，或者1000万元以下直接经济损失的，为一般事故。  
除前款规定外，国务院铁路主管部门可以对一般事故的其他情形作出补充规定。

**Article 12** An accident that causes death of less than 3 persons, or serious injury of less than 10 persons, or direct economic losses of less than RMB 10 million yuan shall be an ordinary accident.  
In addition to the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the competent department of railway under the State Council may formulate supplementary provisions on other circumstances of ordinary accidents.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本章所称的“以上”包括本数，所称的“以下”不包括本数。

**Article 13** The term "or more" as used in this Chapter includes the number itself, and the term "or less" as used in this Chapter does not include the number itself.

### 第三章　事故报告

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　事故发生后，事故现场的铁路运输企业工作人员或者其他人员应当立即报告邻近铁路车站、列车调度员或者公安机关。有关单位和人员接到报告后，应当立即将事故情况报告事故发生地铁路管理机构。

**Article 14** After an accident occurs, the staff of the railway transport enterprise at the accident site or other persons shall immediately report to the adjacent railway station, train dispatcher or public security organ. After receiving the report, the relevant entity or person shall immediately report the accident situation to the railway administration authority at the place where the accident occurred.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　铁路管理机构接到事故报告，应当尽快核实有关情况，并立即报告国务院铁路主管部门；对特别重大事故、重大事故，国务院铁路主管部门应当立即报告国务院并通报国家安全生产监督管理等有关部门。  
发生特别重大事故、重大事故、较大事故或者有人员伤亡的一般事故，铁路管理机构还应当通报事故发生地县级以上地方人民政府及其安全生产监督管理部门。

**Article 15** Upon receiving the accident report, the railway administration authority shall verify the relevant situation as soon as possible and immediately report to the competent department of railway under the State Council; for a particularly major accident or a major accident, the competent department of railway under the State Council shall immediately report to the State Council and notify the State Administration of Work Safety and other relevant departments.  
When a particularly major accident, a major accident, a relatively major accident or an ordinary accident involving casualties occurs, the railway administration authority shall also notify the local people's government at or above the county level at the place where the accident occurred and its work safety supervision and administration department.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　事故报告应当包括下列内容：  
(一)事故发生的时间、地点、区间(线名、公里、米)、事故相关单位和人员；  
(二)发生事故的列车种类、车次、部位、计长、机车型号、牵引辆数、吨数；  
(三)承运旅客人数或者货物品名、装载情况；  
(四)人员伤亡情况，机车车辆、线路设施、道路车辆的损坏情况，对铁路行车的影响情况；  
(五)事故原因的初步判断；  
(六)事故发生后采取的措施及事故控制情况；  
(七)具体救援请求。  
事故报告后出现新情况的，应当及时补报。

**Article 16** The accident report shall include the following:  
(1) the time, place, section (line name, kilometer, meter), entities and persons involved in the accident;  
(2) the type, train number, location, length, locomotive model, number of cars hauled, and tonnage of the train involved in the accident;  
(3) the number of passengers carried or the name and loading condition of the goods;  
(4) the casualty situation, damage to locomotives, rolling stock, line facilities and road vehicles, and the impact on railway operation;  
(5) preliminary judgment on the cause of the accident;  
(6) measures taken after the accident and the control of the accident;  
(7) specific rescue requests.  
If new circumstances arise after the accident report is made, a supplementary report shall be made promptly.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院铁路主管部门、铁路管理机构和铁路运输企业应当向社会公布事故报告值班电话，受理事故报告和举报。

**Article 17** The competent department of railway under the State Council, the railway administration authority and railway transport enterprises shall make public the duty telephone numbers for accident reports to the public and accept accident reports and tip-offs.

### 第四章　事故应急救援

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　事故发生后，列车司机或者运转车长应当立即停车，采取紧急处置措施；对无法处置的，应当立即报告邻近铁路车站、列车调度员进行处置。  
为保障铁路旅客安全或者因特殊运输需要不宜停车的，可以不停车；但是，列车司机或者运转车长应当立即将事故情况报告邻近铁路车站、列车调度员，接到报告的邻近铁路车站、列车调度员应当立即进行处置。

**Article 18** After an accident occurs, the train driver or the train commander shall immediately stop the train and take emergency measures; if the situation cannot be handled, they shall immediately report to the adjacent railway station or train dispatcher for disposition.  
For the purpose of ensuring the safety of railway passengers or due to special transportation needs, the train may not be stopped; however, the train driver or the train commander shall immediately report the accident situation to the adjacent railway station or train dispatcher, and the adjacent railway station or train dispatcher that receives the report shall immediately take disposition.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　事故造成中断铁路行车的，铁路运输企业应当立即组织抢修，尽快恢复铁路正常行车；必要时，铁路运输调度指挥部门应当调整运输径路，减少事故影响。

**Article 19** If an accident causes interruption of railway operation, the railway transport enterprise shall immediately organize emergency repair to restore normal railway operation as soon as possible; if necessary, the railway transportation dispatching and command department shall adjust the transportation route to reduce the impact of the accident.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　事故发生后，国务院铁路主管部门、铁路管理机构、事故发生地县级以上地方人民政府或者铁路运输企业应当根据事故等级启动相应的应急预案；必要时，成立现场应急救援机构。

**Article 20** After an accident occurs, the competent department of railway under the State Council, the railway administration authority, the local people's government at or above the county level at the place where the accident occurred, or the railway transport enterprise shall activate the corresponding emergency plan according to the accident grade; if necessary, an on-site emergency rescue organization shall be established.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　现场应急救援机构根据事故应急救援工作的实际需要，可以借用有关单位和个人的设施、设备和其他物资。借用单位使用完毕应当及时归还，并支付适当费用；造成损失的，应当赔偿。  
有关单位和个人应当积极支持、配合救援工作。

**Article 21** The on-site emergency rescue organization may, based on the actual needs of the accident emergency rescue work, borrow facilities, equipment and other materials from relevant entities and individuals. The borrowing entity shall return them promptly after use and pay appropriate fees; if losses are caused, compensation shall be made.  
Relevant entities and individuals shall actively support and cooperate with the rescue work.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　事故造成重大人员伤亡或者需要紧急转移、安置铁路旅客和沿线居民的，事故发生地县级以上地方人民政府应当及时组织开展救治和转移、安置工作。

**Article 22** If an accident causes heavy casualties or requires the emergency transfer and resettlement of railway passengers and residents along the line, the local people's government at or above the county level at the place where the accident occurred shall promptly organize and carry out the treatment, transfer and resettlement work.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院铁路主管部门、铁路管理机构或者事故发生地县级以上地方人民政府根据事故救援的实际需要，可以请求当地驻军、武装警察部队参与事故救援。

**Article 23** The competent department of railway under the State Council, the railway administration authority or the local people's government at or above the county level at the place where the accident occurred may, according to the actual needs of accident rescue, request local military forces or armed police forces to participate in the accident rescue.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　有关单位和个人应当妥善保护事故现场以及相关证据，并在事故调查组成立后将相关证据移交事故调查组。因事故救援、尽快恢复铁路正常行车需要改变事故现场的，应当做出标记、绘制现场示意图、制作现场视听资料，并做出书面记录。  
任何单位和个人不得破坏事故现场，不得伪造、隐匿或者毁灭相关证据。

**Article 24** Relevant entities and individuals shall properly protect the accident scene and relevant evidence, and shall hand over the relevant evidence to the accident investigation team after its establishment. If it is necessary to alter the accident scene for the purpose of accident rescue or restoring normal railway operation as soon as possible, markings shall be made, a sketch of the scene shall be drawn, audio-visual materials of the scene shall be produced, and a written record shall be made.  
No entity or individual may destroy the accident scene, or forge, conceal or destroy relevant evidence.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　事故中死亡人员的尸体经法定机构鉴定后，应当及时通知死者家属认领；无法查找死者家属的，按照国家有关规定处理。

**Article 25** After the bodies of persons deceased in the accident have been identified by a statutory authority, the families of the deceased shall be notified promptly to claim the bodies; if the families cannot be located, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第五章　事故调查处理

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　特别重大事故由国务院或者国务院授权的部门组织事故调查组进行调查。  
重大事故由国务院铁路主管部门组织事故调查组进行调查。  
较大事故和一般事故由事故发生地铁路管理机构组织事故调查组进行调查；国务院铁路主管部门认为必要时，可以组织事故调查组对较大事故和一般事故进行调查。  
根据事故的具体情况，事故调查组由有关人民政府、公安机关、安全生产监督管理部门、监察机关等单位派人组成，并应当邀请人民检察院派人参加。事故调查组认为必要时，可以聘请有关专家参与事故调查。

**Article 26** A particularly major accident shall be investigated by an accident investigation team organized by the State Council or a department authorized by the State Council.  
A major accident shall be investigated by an accident investigation team organized by the competent department of railway under the State Council.  
A relatively major accident and an ordinary accident shall be investigated by an accident investigation team organized by the railway administration authority at the place where the accident occurred; when the competent department of railway under the State Council deems it necessary, it may organize an accident investigation team to investigate a relatively major accident or an ordinary accident.  
In light of the specific circumstances of the accident, the accident investigation team shall be composed of persons dispatched by the relevant people's government, public security organ, work safety supervision and administration department, supervisory authority and other units, and shall invite persons from the People's Procuratorate to participate. When the accident investigation team deems it necessary, it may engage relevant experts to participate in the accident investigation.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　事故调查组应当按照国家有关规定开展事故调查，并在下列调查期限内向组织事故调查组的机关或者铁路管理机构提交事故调查报告：  
(一)特别重大事故的调查期限为60日；  
(二)重大事故的调查期限为30日；  
(三)较大事故的调查期限为20日；  
(四)一般事故的调查期限为10日。  
事故调查期限自事故发生之日起计算。

**Article 27** The accident investigation team shall carry out the accident investigation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and shall submit an accident investigation report to the authority or railway administration authority that organized the investigation team within the following investigation periods:  
(1) 60 days for a particularly major accident;  
(2) 30 days for a major accident;  
(3) 20 days for a relatively major accident;  
(4) 10 days for an ordinary accident.  
The accident investigation period shall be calculated from the date of the accident.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　事故调查处理，需要委托有关机构进行技术鉴定或者对铁路设备、设施及其他财产损失状况以及中断铁路行车造成的直接经济损失进行评估的，事故调查组应当委托具有国家规定资质的机构进行技术鉴定或者评估。技术鉴定或者评估所需时间不计入事故调查期限。

**Article 28** If the accident investigation and handling requires entrusting a relevant institution to conduct a technical appraisal or to assess the loss of railway equipment, facilities and other property as well as the direct economic losses caused by the interruption of railway operation, the accident investigation team shall entrust an institution with the qualifications prescribed by the State to conduct the technical appraisal or assessment. The time needed for the technical appraisal or assessment shall not be counted in the accident investigation period.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　事故调查报告形成后，报经组织事故调查组的机关或者铁路管理机构同意，事故调查组工作即告结束。组织事故调查组的机关或者铁路管理机构应当自事故调查组工作结束之日起15日内，根据事故调查报告，制作事故认定书。  
事故认定书是事故赔偿、事故处理以及事故责任追究的依据。

**Article 29** After the accident investigation report is formed and submitted to the authority or railway administration authority that organized the investigation team for approval, the work of the accident investigation team shall be concluded. The authority or railway administration authority that organized the investigation team shall, within 15 days from the date of conclusion of the work of the accident investigation team, produce an accident determination letter based on the accident investigation report.  
The accident determination letter shall be the basis for accident compensation, accident handling and accident liability accountability.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　事故责任单位和有关人员应当认真吸取事故教训，落实防范和整改措施，防止事故再次发生。  
国务院铁路主管部门、铁路管理机构以及其他有关行政机关应当对事故责任单位和有关人员落实防范和整改措施的情况进行监督检查。

**Article 30** The entity responsible for the accident and relevant persons shall earnestly learn from the accident lessons, implement preventive and corrective measures to prevent the recurrence of accidents.  
The competent department of railway under the State Council, the railway administration authority and other relevant administrative authorities shall supervise and inspect the implementation of preventive and corrective measures by the entity responsible for the accident and relevant persons.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　事故的处理情况，除依法应当保密的外，应当由组织事故调查组的机关或者铁路管理机构向社会公布。

**Article 31** The handling of the accident shall, except where confidentiality is required according to law, be made public by the authority or railway administration authority that organized the accident investigation team.

### 第六章　事故赔偿

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　事故造成人身伤亡的，铁路运输企业应当承担赔偿责任；但是人身伤亡是不可抗力或者受害人自身原因造成的，铁路运输企业不承担赔偿责任。  
违章通过平交道口或者人行过道，或者在铁路线路上行走、坐卧造成的人身伤亡，属于受害人自身的原因造成的人身伤亡。

**Article 32** If an accident causes personal injury or death, the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability; however, if the personal injury or death is caused by force majeure or by the victim's own fault, the railway transport enterprise shall not bear compensation liability.  
Personal injury or death caused by crossing a level crossing or pedestrian crossing in violation of regulations, or by walking, sitting or lying on railway lines shall be deemed as personal injury or death caused by the victim's own fault.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　事故造成铁路旅客人身伤亡和自带行李损失的，铁路运输企业对每名铁路旅客人身伤亡的赔偿责任限额为人民币15万元，对每名铁路旅客自带行李损失的赔偿责任限额为人民币2000元。  
铁路运输企业与铁路旅客可以书面约定高于前款规定的赔偿责任限额。(2012年11月9日删除)

**Article 33** If an accident causes personal injury or death to a railway passenger or loss of his/her carry-on luggage, the limit of compensation liability of the railway transport enterprise for personal injury or death of each railway passenger shall be RMB 150,000 yuan, and the limit of compensation liability for loss of carry-on luggage of each railway passenger shall be RMB 2,000 yuan.  
The railway transport enterprise and the railway passenger may agree in writing on a limit of compensation liability higher than that prescribed in the preceding paragraph. (Deleted on November 9, 2012)

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　事故造成铁路运输企业承运的货物、包裹、行李损失的，铁路运输企业应当依照《中华人民共和国铁路法》的规定承担赔偿责任。

**Article 34** If an accident causes loss of goods, parcels or luggage consigned to the railway transport enterprise for carriage, the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability in accordance with the provisions of the Railway Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　除[本条例第三十三条](#art-33)、第三十四条的规定外，事故造成其他人身伤亡或者财产损失的，依照国家有关法律、行政法规的规定赔偿。

**Article 35** In addition to the provisions of Articles 33 and 34 of these Regulations, if an accident causes other personal injury or death or property loss, compensation shall be made in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations of the State.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　事故当事人对事故损害赔偿有争议的，可以通过协商解决，或者请求组织事故调查组的机关或者铁路管理机构组织调解，也可以直接向人民法院提起民事诉讼。

**Article 36** If a party to an accident disputes the damages for the accident, the dispute may be resolved through negotiation, or the party may request the authority or railway administration authority that organized the accident investigation team to conduct mediation, or may directly file a civil action with a People's Court.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　铁路运输企业及其职工违反法律、行政法规的规定，造成事故的，由国务院铁路主管部门或者铁路管理机构依法追究行政责任。

**Article 37** If a railway transport enterprise or its employee violates the provisions of laws or administrative regulations and causes an accident, the competent department of railway under the State Council or the railway administration authority shall investigate the administrative liability according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反本条例的规定，铁路运输企业及其职工不立即组织救援，或者迟报、漏报、瞒报、谎报事故的，对单位，由国务院铁路主管部门或者铁路管理机构处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；对个人，由国务院铁路主管部门或者铁路管理机构处4000元以上2万元以下的罚款；属于国家工作人员的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 38** If, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a railway transport enterprise or its employee fails to immediately organize rescue, or delays, omits, conceals or falsely reports an accident, the entity shall be fined by the competent department of railway under the State Council or the railway administration authority not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan; the individual shall be fined not less than RMB 4,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan; if the individual is a state functionary, disciplinary sanction shall be imposed according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反本条例的规定，国务院铁路主管部门、铁路管理机构以及其他行政机关未立即启动应急预案，或者迟报、漏报、瞒报、谎报事故的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 39** If, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the competent department of railway under the State Council, the railway administration authority or other administrative authorities fail to immediately activate an emergency plan, or delay, omit, conceal or falsely report an accident, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given disciplinary sanction according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本条例的规定，干扰、阻碍事故救援、铁路线路开通、列车运行和事故调查处理的，对单位，由国务院铁路主管部门或者铁路管理机构处4万元以上20万元以下的罚款；对个人，由国务院铁路主管部门或者铁路管理机构处2000元以上1万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，对单位，由国务院铁路主管部门或者铁路管理机构处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款；对个人，由国务院铁路主管部门或者铁路管理机构处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；属于国家工作人员的，依法给予处分；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 40** If, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, any entity or individual interferes with or impedes accident rescue, opening of railway lines, train operation or accident investigation and handling, the entity shall be fined by the competent department of railway under the State Council or the railway administration authority not less than RMB 40,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan; the individual shall be fined not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the entity shall be fined not less than RMB 200,000 yuan but not more than RMB 1,000,000 yuan; the individual shall be fined not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan; if the person is a state functionary, disciplinary sanction shall be imposed according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administrative penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　本条例于2007年9月1日起施行。1979年7月16日国务院批准发布的《火车与其他车辆碰撞和铁路路外人员伤亡事故处理暂行规定》和1994年8月13日国务院批准发布的《铁路旅客运输损害赔偿规定》同时废止。

**Article 41** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2007. The Interim Provisions on the Handling of Collisions Between Trains and Other Vehicles and Casualty Accidents of Persons Outside the Railway, approved and promulgated by the State Council on July 16, 1979, and the Provisions on Compensation for Damages in Railway Passenger Transport, approved and promulgated by the State Council on August 13, 1994, shall be abolished simultaneously.
