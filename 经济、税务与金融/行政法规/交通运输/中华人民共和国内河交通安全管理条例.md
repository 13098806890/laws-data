### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强内河交通安全管理，维护内河交通秩序，保障人民群众生命、财产安全，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the administration of inland river traffic safety, maintaining the order of inland river traffic, and ensuring the safety of life and property of the people.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国内河通航水域从事航行、停泊和作业以及与内河交通安全有关的活动，必须遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Any vessel engaging in navigation, berthing, operations, and activities related to inland river traffic safety within the navigable waters of inland rivers of the People's Republic of China must comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　内河交通安全管理遵循安全第一、预防为主、方便群众、依法管理的原则，保障内河交通安全、有序、畅通。

**Article 3** The administration of inland river traffic safety shall follow the principles of safety first, prevention as the priority, convenience for the public, and administration according to law, so as to ensure safe, orderly and unobstructed inland river traffic.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院交通主管部门主管全国内河交通安全管理工作。国家海事管理机构在国务院交通主管部门的领导下，负责全国内河交通安全监督管理工作。  
国务院交通主管部门在中央管理水域设立的海事管理机构和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府在中央管理水域以外的其他水域设立的海事管理机构(以下统称海事管理机构)依据各自的职责权限，对所辖内河通航水域实施水上交通安全监督管理。

**Article 4** The competent transport authority under the State Council shall be in charge of the administration of inland river traffic safety nationwide. The national maritime safety administration, under the leadership of the competent transport authority under the State Council, shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of inland river traffic safety nationwide.  
The maritime safety administrations established by the competent transport authority under the State Council in the waters under central administration and those established by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government in waters other than those under central administration (hereinafter collectively referred to as the maritime safety administrations) shall, pursuant to their respective functions and powers, carry out water traffic safety supervision and administration over the navigable waters of inland rivers under their jurisdiction.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当加强本行政区域内的内河交通安全管理工作，建立、健全内河交通安全管理责任制。  
乡(镇)人民政府对本行政区域内的内河交通安全管理履行下列职责：  
(一)建立、健全行政村和船主的船舶安全责任制；  
(二)落实渡口船舶、船员、旅客定额的安全管理责任制；  
(三)落实船舶水上交通安全管理的专门人员；  
(四)督促船舶所有人、经营人和船员遵守有关内河交通安全的法律、法规和规章。

**Article 5** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the administration of inland river traffic safety within their respective administrative regions, and shall establish and improve the responsibility system for inland river traffic safety administration.  
The people's government of a township (town) shall perform the following duties in respect of inland river traffic safety administration within its administrative region:  
(1) establishing and improving the safety responsibility system for vessels of administrative villages and shipowners;  
(2) implementing the safety management responsibility system for ferry vessels, crew members and passenger quotas;  
(3) designating specialized personnel for the safety management of water traffic of vessels;  
(4) urging vessel owners, operators and crew members to observe the relevant laws, regulations and rules on inland river traffic safety.

### 第二章　船舶、浮动设施和船员

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　船舶具备下列条件，方可航行：  
(一)经海事管理机构认可的船舶检验机构依法检验并持有合格的船舶检验证书；  
(二)经海事管理机构依法登记并持有船舶登记证书；  
(三)配备符合国务院交通主管部门规定的船员；  
(四)配备必要的航行资料。

**Article 6** A vessel may navigate only when it meets the following conditions:  
(1) it has been surveyed according to law by a vessel survey institution recognized by the maritime safety administration and holds a valid vessel survey certificate;  
(2) it has been registered according to law with the maritime safety administration and holds a vessel registration certificate;  
(3) it is manned with crew members meeting the requirements prescribed by the competent transport authority under the State Council; and  
(4) it is equipped with the necessary navigational materials.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　浮动设施具备下列条件，方可从事有关活动：  
(一)经海事管理机构认可的船舶检验机构依法检验并持有合格的检验证书；  
(二)经海事管理机构依法登记并持有登记证书；  
(三)配备符合国务院交通主管部门规定的掌握水上交通安全技能的船员。

**Article 7** A floating facility may engage in relevant activities only when it meets the following conditions:  
(1) it has been surveyed according to law by a vessel survey institution recognized by the maritime safety administration and holds a valid survey certificate;  
(2) it has been registered according to law with the maritime safety administration and holds a registration certificate; and  
(3) it is manned with crew members meeting the requirements prescribed by the competent transport authority under the State Council who possess the skills in water traffic safety.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　船舶、浮动设施应当保持适于安全航行、停泊或者从事有关活动的状态。  
船舶、浮动设施的配载和系固应当符合国家安全技术规范。

**Article 8** Vessels and floating facilities shall be maintained in a condition suitable for safe navigation, berthing or engagement in relevant activities.  
The stowage and securing of vessels and floating facilities shall comply with the national safety technical specifications.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　船员经水上交通安全专业培训，其中客船和载运危险货物船舶的船员还应当经相应的特殊培训，并经海事管理机构考试合格，取得相应的适任证书或者其他适任证件，方可担任船员职务。严禁未取得适任证书或者其他适任证件的船员上岗。  
船员应当遵守职业道德，提高业务素质，严格依法履行职责。

**Article 9** A person may serve as a crew member only after he has received professional training in water traffic safety, and, in the case of crew members on passenger vessels and vessels carrying dangerous cargo, they shall also have received corresponding special training, and have passed the examinations conducted by the maritime safety administration, and obtained the corresponding certificate of competency or other qualification document. It is strictly prohibited for crew members who have not obtained the certificate of competency or other qualification document to work on board.  
Crew members shall abide by professional ethics, improve their professional competence, and strictly perform their duties according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　船舶、浮动设施的所有人或者经营人，应当加强对船舶、浮动设施的安全管理，建立、健全相应的交通安全管理制度，并对船舶、浮动设施的交通安全负责；不得聘用无适任证书或者其他适任证件的人员担任船员；不得指使、强令船员违章操作。

**Article 10** The owner or operator of a vessel or floating facility shall strengthen the safety management of the vessel or floating facility, establish and improve the corresponding traffic safety management system, and be responsible for the traffic safety of the vessel or floating facility. He shall not employ any person without a certificate of competency or other qualification document as a crew member, nor instruct or compel any crew member to operate in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　船舶、浮动设施的所有人或者经营人，应当根据船舶、浮动设施的技术性能、船员状况、水域和水文气象条件，合理调度船舶或者使用浮动设施。

**Article 11** The owner or operator of a vessel or floating facility shall rationally dispatch the vessel or use the floating facility in light of its technical performance, the condition of the crew, and the water area and hydrometeorological conditions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　按照国家规定必须取得船舶污染损害责任、沉船打捞责任的保险文书或者财务保证书的船舶，其所有人或者经营人必须取得相应的保险文书或者财务担保证明，并随船携带其副本。

**Article 12** For a vessel which is, in accordance with state regulations, required to obtain insurance documents or financial guarantees for liability for pollution damage and for salvage of sunken vessels, the owner or operator thereof must obtain the corresponding insurance documents or financial guarantee certificates, and carry a copy thereof on board.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　禁止伪造、变造、买卖、租借、冒用船舶检验证书、船舶登记证书、船员适任证书或者其他适任证件。

**Article 13** It is prohibited to forge, alter, buy, sell, lease, lend or fraudulently use vessel survey certificates, vessel registration certificates, crew competency certificates or other qualification documents.

### 第三章　航行、停泊和作业

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　船舶在内河航行，应当悬挂国旗，标明船名、船籍港、载重线。  
按照国家规定应当报废的船舶、浮动设施，不得航行或者作业。

**Article 14** A vessel navigating on inland rivers shall fly the national flag, and indicate the vessel's name, port of registry, and load line.  
Vessels and floating facilities that should be scrapped in accordance with state regulations shall not navigate or operate.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　船舶在内河航行，应当保持瞭望，注意观察，并采用安全航速航行。船舶安全航速应当根据能见度、通航密度、船舶操纵性能和风、浪、水流、航路状况以及周围环境等主要因素决定。使用雷达的船舶，还应当考虑雷达设备的特性、效率和局限性。  
船舶在限制航速的区域和汛期高水位期间，应当按照海事管理机构规定的航速航行。

**Article 15** A vessel navigating on inland rivers shall keep a lookout, pay attention to observation, and navigate at a safe speed. The safe speed of a vessel shall be determined based on such main factors as visibility, traffic density, vessel maneuverability, wind, waves, current, channel conditions and the surrounding environment. A vessel using radar shall also take into account the characteristics, efficiency and limitations of the radar equipment.  
A vessel navigating in areas with speed restrictions and during high water periods in the flood season shall navigate at the speed prescribed by the maritime safety administration.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　船舶在内河航行时，上行船舶应当沿缓流或者航路一侧航行，下行船舶应当沿主流或者航路中间航行；在潮流河段、湖泊、水库、平流区域，应当尽可能沿本船右舷一侧航路航行。

**Article 16** When navigating on inland rivers, an upbound vessel shall navigate along the slack water or one side of the channel, and a downbound vessel shall navigate along the main current or the middle of the channel. In tidal river sections, lakes, reservoirs, and areas of still water, a vessel shall navigate as far as possible along the channel on the starboard side of the vessel.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　船舶在内河航行时，应当谨慎驾驶，保障安全；对来船动态不明、声号不统一或者遇有紧迫情况时，应当减速、停车或者倒车，防止碰撞。  
船舶相遇，各方应当注意避让。按照船舶航行规则应当让路的船舶，必须主动避让被让路船舶；被让路船舶应当注意让路船舶的行动，并适时采取措施，协助避让。  
船舶避让时，各方避让意图经统一后，任何一方不得擅自改变避让行动。  
船舶航行、避让和信号显示的具体规则，由国务院交通主管部门制定。

**Article 17** A vessel navigating on inland rivers shall navigate with caution to ensure safety. When the movement of an oncoming vessel is unclear, when sound signals are inconsistent, or when encountering an urgent situation, the vessel shall reduce speed, stop or go astern to avoid collision.  
When vessels meet, each party shall pay attention to give way. A vessel that should give way in accordance with the navigation rules must actively keep out of the way of the vessel being given way to; the vessel being given way to shall pay attention to the actions of the give-way vessel and take timely measures to assist in the avoidance.  
When vessels are taking avoiding action, once the intentions of the parties are unified, no party shall arbitrarily change the avoiding action.  
The specific rules for vessel navigation, avoidance and signal display shall be formulated by the competent transport authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　船舶进出内河港口，应当向海事管理机构报告船舶的航次计划、适航状态、船员配备和载货载客等情况。

**Article 18** A vessel entering or leaving an inland river port shall report to the maritime safety administration on such matters as the vessel's voyage plan, seaworthiness status, crew manning, and cargo and passenger carrying conditions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　下列船舶在内河航行，应当向引航机构申请引航：  
(一)外国籍船舶；  
(二)1000总吨以上的海上机动船舶，但船长驾驶同一类型的海上机动船舶在同一内河通航水域航行与上一航次间隔2个月以内的除外；  
(三)通航条件受限制的船舶；  
(四)国务院交通主管部门规定应当申请引航的客船、载运危险货物的船舶。

**Article 19** The following vessels shall apply to a pilotage organization for pilotage when navigating on inland rivers:  
(1) vessels of foreign nationality;  
(2) sea-going power-driven vessels of 1,000 gross tonnage or above, except that the master has navigated a sea-going power-driven vessel of the same type within the same inland river navigable waters within an interval of two months from the previous voyage;  
(3) vessels whose navigational conditions are restricted; and  
(4) passenger vessels and vessels carrying dangerous cargo for which the competent transport authority under the State Council prescribes that pilotage shall be applied for.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　船舶进出港口和通过交通管制区、通航密集区或者航行条件受限制的区域，应当遵守海事管理机构发布的有关通航规定。  
任何船舶不得擅自进入或者穿越海事管理机构公布的禁航区。

**Article 20** A vessel entering or leaving a port or passing through a traffic control area, a congested navigable area, or an area where navigational conditions are restricted, shall comply with the relevant navigation regulations issued by the maritime safety administration.  
No vessel may enter or cross a prohibited navigation area published by the maritime safety administration without authorization.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　从事货物或者旅客运输的船舶，必须符合船舶强度、稳性、吃水、消防和救生等安全技术要求和国务院交通主管部门规定的载货或者载客条件。  
任何船舶不得超载运输货物或者旅客。

**Article 21** A vessel engaged in the transport of cargo or passengers must comply with the safety technical requirements for vessel strength, stability, draught, fire-fighting, lifesaving, etc., and the cargo or passenger carrying conditions prescribed by the competent transport authority under the State Council.  
No vessel may carry cargo or passengers in excess of the load line.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　船舶在内河通航水域载运或者拖带超重、超长、超高、超宽、半潜的物体，必须在装船或者拖带前24小时报海事管理机构核定拟航行的航路、时间，并采取必要的安全措施，保障船舶载运或者拖带安全。船舶需要护航的，应当向海事管理机构申请护航。

**Article 22** When a vessel carries or tows overweight, overlength, overheight, overwidth, or semi-submerged objects in inland river navigable waters, it must, 24 hours before loading or towing, report to the maritime safety administration for verification of the intended route and time for navigation, and take necessary safety measures to ensure the safety of the vessel's carriage or towing. If the vessel needs an escort, it shall apply to the maritime safety administration for an escort.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　遇有下列情形之一时，海事管理机构可以根据情况采取限时航行、单航、封航等临时性限制、疏导交通的措施，并予公告：  
(一)恶劣天气；  
(二)大范围水上施工作业；  
(三)影响航行的水上交通事故；  
(四)水上大型群众性活动或者体育比赛；  
(五)对航行安全影响较大的其他情形。

**Article 23** When any of the following circumstances occurs, the maritime safety administration may, depending on the circumstances, take temporary restrictive or traffic diversion measures such as time-limited navigation, one-way navigation or navigation closure, and shall give public notice thereof:  
(1) severe weather;  
(2) large-scale water construction operations;  
(3) water traffic accidents affecting navigation;  
(4) large-scale mass activities or sports competitions on water; or  
(5) other circumstances having a significant impact on navigation safety.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　船舶应当在码头、泊位或者依法公布的锚地、停泊区、作业区停泊；遇有紧急情况，需要在其他水域停泊的，应当向海事管理机构报告。  
船舶停泊，应当按照规定显示信号，不得妨碍或者危及其他船舶航行、停泊或者作业的安全。  
船舶停泊，应当留有足以保证船舶安全的船员值班。

**Article 24** A vessel shall berth at a wharf, berth, or at an anchorage, mooring area or operation area published according to law. In case of emergency requiring berthing in other waters, it shall report to the maritime safety administration.  
When berthing, a vessel shall display signals as required, and shall not obstruct or endanger the safety of navigation, berthing or operations of other vessels.  
When berthing, a vessel shall leave on board a sufficient number of crew members on watch to ensure the safety of the vessel.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　在内河通航水域或者岸线上进行下列可能影响通航安全的作业或者活动的，应当在进行作业或者活动前报海事管理机构批准：  
(一)勘探、采掘、爆破；  
(二)构筑、设置、维修、拆除水上水下构筑物或者设施；  
(三)架设桥梁、索道；  
(四)铺设、检修、拆除水上水下电缆或者管道；  
(五)设置系船浮筒、浮趸、缆桩等设施；  
(六)航道建设，航道、码头前沿水域疏浚；  
(七)举行大型群众性活动、体育比赛。  
进行前款所列作业或者活动，需要进行可行性研究的，在进行可行性研究时应当征求海事管理机构的意见；依照法律、行政法规的规定，需经其他有关部门审批的，还应当依法办理有关审批手续。

**Article 25** Where any of the following operations or activities that may affect navigation safety is carried out in inland river navigable waters or on their banks, approval from the maritime safety administration shall be obtained before such operation or activity is conducted:  
(1) prospecting, excavation or blasting;  
(2) construction, installation, repair or demolition of above-water or underwater structures or facilities;  
(3) erection of bridges or cableways;  
(4) laying, repair or removal of above-water or underwater cables or pipelines;  
(5) installation of mooring buoys, pontoons, bollards and other facilities;  
(6) waterway construction, dredging of waterways or waters in front of wharves; or  
(7) holding of large-scale mass activities or sports competitions.  
Where a feasibility study is required for an operation or activity listed in the preceding paragraph, the views of the maritime safety administration shall be sought during the feasibility study; and if approval by other relevant departments is required in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, the relevant approval procedures shall be conducted according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　海事管理机构审批[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)规定的作业或者活动，应当自收到申请之日起30日内作出批准或者不批准的决定，并书面通知申请人。  
遇有紧急情况，需要对航道进行修复或者对航道、码头前沿水域进行疏浚的，作业人可以边申请边施工。

**Article 26** When the maritime safety administration examines and approves an operation or activity provided for in Article 25 of these Regulations, it shall make a decision of approval or disapproval within 30 days from the date of receipt of the application, and shall notify the applicant in writing.  
In case of emergency requiring repair of a waterway or dredging of a waterway or of waters in front of a wharf, the operator may carry out the construction while applying.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　航道内不得养殖、种植植物、水生物和设置永久性固定设施。  
划定航道，涉及水产养殖区的，航道主管部门应当征求渔业行政主管部门的意见；设置水产养殖区，涉及航道的，渔业行政主管部门应当征求航道主管部门和海事管理机构的意见。

**Article 27** No cultivation, planting of plants or aquatic organisms, or installation of permanent fixed facilities shall be allowed within the waterway.  
Where the delineation of a waterway involves an aquaculture area, the waterway authority shall seek the views of the competent fisheries administrative department. Where the establishment of an aquaculture area involves a waterway, the competent fisheries administrative department shall seek the views of the waterway authority and the maritime safety administration.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　在内河通航水域进行下列可能影响通航安全的作业，应当在进行作业前向海事管理机构备案：  
(一)气象观测、测量、地质调查；  
(二)航道日常养护；  
(三)大面积清除水面垃圾；  
(四)可能影响内河通航水域交通安全的其他行为。

**Article 28** Where any of the following operations that may affect navigation safety is carried out in inland river navigable waters, it shall be filed with the maritime safety administration before the operation is conducted:  
(1) meteorological observation, surveying, or geological survey;  
(2) routine maintenance of waterways;  
(3) large-scale removal of refuse from the water surface; or  
(4) other acts that may affect the traffic safety of inland river navigable waters.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　进行[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)、第二十八条规定的作业或者活动时，应当在作业或者活动区域设置标志和显示信号，并按照海事管理机构的规定，采取相应的安全措施，保障通航安全。  
前款作业或者活动完成后，不得遗留任何妨碍航行的物体。

**Article 29** When carrying out an operation or activity provided for in Article 25 or Article 28 of these Regulations, signs shall be set up and signals displayed in the operation or activity area, and corresponding safety measures shall be taken in accordance with the provisions of the maritime safety administration to ensure navigation safety.  
After the completion of the operation or activity referred to in the preceding paragraph, no object obstructing navigation shall be left behind.

### 第四章　危险货物监管

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　从事危险货物装卸的码头、泊位，必须符合国家有关安全规范要求，并征求海事管理机构的意见，经验收合格后，方可投入使用。  
禁止在内河运输法律、行政法规以及国务院交通主管部门规定禁止运输的危险货物。

**Article 30** Wharves and berths used for the loading and unloading of dangerous cargo must comply with the relevant state safety specifications, and after seeking the views of the maritime safety administration and passing acceptance inspection, they may be put into use.  
It is prohibited to transport dangerous cargo whose transport is prohibited by laws, administrative regulations or by the competent transport authority under the State Council on inland rivers.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　载运危险货物的船舶，必须持有经海事管理机构认可的船舶检验机构依法检验并颁发的危险货物适装证书，并按照国家有关危险货物运输的规定和安全技术规范进行配载和运输。

**Article 31** A vessel carrying dangerous cargo must hold a dangerous cargo suitability certificate issued after a survey conducted according to law by a vessel survey institution recognized by the maritime safety administration, and shall stow and transport the cargo in accordance with the relevant state regulations on the transport of dangerous cargo and the safety technical specifications.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　船舶装卸、过驳危险货物或者载运危险货物进出港口，应当将危险货物的名称、特性、包装、装卸或者过驳的时间、地点以及进出港时间等事项，事先报告海事管理机构和港口管理机构，经其同意后，方可进行装卸、过驳作业或者进出港口；但是，定船、定线、定货的船舶可以定期报告。

**Article 32** When a vessel loads, unloads or transships dangerous cargo or enters or leaves a port carrying such cargo, it shall report in advance to the maritime safety administration and the port administration on such matters as the name, properties, packaging, time and place of loading, unloading or transshipment, and time of entry and exit, and may carry out the loading, unloading or transshipment operation or enter or leave the port only after obtaining their consent. However, vessels operating on a fixed vessel, fixed route and fixed cargo basis may report periodically.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　载运危险货物的船舶，在航行、装卸或者停泊时，应当按照规定显示信号；其他船舶应当避让。

**Article 33** A vessel carrying dangerous cargo shall display signals as required when navigating, loading, unloading or berthing; other vessels shall keep clear of it.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　从事危险货物装卸的码头、泊位和载运危险货物的船舶，必须编制危险货物事故应急预案，并配备相应的应急救援设备和器材。

**Article 34** Wharves and berths used for the loading and unloading of dangerous cargo and vessels carrying dangerous cargo must formulate emergency response plans for dangerous cargo accidents, and be equipped with corresponding emergency rescue equipment and appliances.

### 第五章　渡口管理

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　设置或者撤销渡口，应当经渡口所在地的县级人民政府审批；县级人民政府审批前，应当征求当地海事管理机构的意见。

**Article 35** The establishment or cancellation of a ferry shall be subject to the examination and approval of the people's government of the county where the ferry is located; before granting approval, the county people's government shall seek the views of the local maritime safety administration.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　渡口的设置应当具备下列条件：  
(一)选址应当在水流平缓、水深足够、坡岸稳定、视野开阔、适宜船舶停靠的地点，并远离危险物品生产、堆放场所；  
(二)具备货物装卸、旅客上下的安全设施；  
(三)配备必要的救生设备和专门管理人员。

**Article 36** The establishment of a ferry shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) the site shall be selected at a location with gentle current, sufficient water depth, stable banks, open view, and suitable for vessel berthing, and shall be far away from places where dangerous articles are produced or stored;  
(2) it shall be equipped with safety facilities for cargo loading and unloading and passenger embarkation and disembarkation; and  
(3) it shall be equipped with necessary lifesaving equipment and specialized management personnel.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　渡口经营者应当在渡口设置明显的标志，维护渡运秩序，保障渡运安全。  
渡口所在地县级人民政府应当建立、健全渡口安全管理责任制，指定有关部门负责对渡口和渡运安全实施监督检查。

**Article 37** The ferry operator shall set up conspicuous signs at the ferry, maintain the order of ferry operations, and ensure ferry safety.  
The people's government of the county where the ferry is located shall establish and improve the responsibility system for ferry safety management, and designate relevant departments to carry out supervision and inspection of ferry and ferry operation safety.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　渡口工作人员应当经培训、考试合格，并取得渡口所在地县级人民政府指定的部门颁发的合格证书。  
渡口船舶应当持有合格的船舶检验证书和船舶登记证书。

**Article 38** Ferry staff shall undergo training and pass examinations, and obtain a qualification certificate issued by the department designated by the people's government of the county where the ferry is located.  
Ferry vessels shall hold valid vessel survey certificates and vessel registration certificates.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　渡口载客船舶应当有符合国家规定的识别标志，并在明显位置标明载客定额、安全注意事项。  
渡口船舶应当按照渡口所在地的县级人民政府核定的路线渡运，并不得超载；渡运时，应当注意避让过往船舶，不得抢航或者强行横越。  
遇有洪水或者大风、大雾、大雪等恶劣天气，渡口应当停止渡运。

**Article 39** A passenger-carrying ferry vessel shall have identification marks conforming to state regulations, and shall clearly indicate the passenger quota and safety precautions in a conspicuous position.  
Ferry vessels shall operate on the routes approved by the people's government of the county where the ferry is located, and shall not be overloaded. When operating, they shall pay attention to keeping clear of passing vessels, and shall not engage in racing or forcible crossing.  
In case of flood or severe weather such as strong wind, heavy fog or heavy snow, the ferry shall suspend operations.

### 第六章　通航保障

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　内河通航水域的航道、航标和其他标志的规划、建设、设置、维护，应当符合国家规定的通航安全要求。

**Article 40** The planning, construction, installation and maintenance of waterways, navigation marks and other signs in inland river navigable waters shall comply with the state-prescribed requirements for navigation safety.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　内河航道发生变迁，水深、宽度发生变化，或者航标发生位移、损坏、灭失，影响通航安全的，航道、航标主管部门必须及时采取措施，使航道、航标保持正常状态。

**Article 41** If changes occur in an inland waterway, the water depth or width changes, or a navigation mark is displaced, damaged or lost, thereby affecting navigation safety, the competent authority for waterways and navigation marks must take timely measures to keep the waterway and navigation marks in a normal condition.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　内河通航水域内可能影响航行安全的沉没物、漂流物、搁浅物，其所有人和经营人，必须按照国家有关规定设置标志，向海事管理机构报告，并在海事管理机构限定的时间内打捞清除；没有所有人或者经营人的，由海事管理机构打捞清除或者采取其他相应措施，保障通航安全。

**Article 42** With respect to sunken, drifting or grounded objects that may affect navigation safety in inland river navigable waters, the owner and operator thereof must set up signs in accordance with the relevant state regulations, report to the maritime safety administration, and salvage and remove the objects within the time limit prescribed by the maritime safety administration. If there is no owner or operator, the maritime safety administration shall salvage and remove them or take other corresponding measures to ensure navigation safety.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　在内河通航水域中拖放竹、木等物体，应当在拖放前24小时报经海事管理机构同意，按照核定的时间、路线拖放，并采取必要的安全措施，保障拖放安全。

**Article 43** When towing or launching bamboo, timber or other objects in inland river navigable waters, approval from the maritime safety administration shall be obtained 24 hours before the towing or launching, and the towing or launching shall be carried out in accordance with the approved time and route, and necessary safety measures shall be taken to ensure the safety of the towing or launching.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　任何单位和个人发现下列情况，应当迅速向海事管理机构报告：  
(一)航道变迁，航道水深、宽度发生变化；  
(二)妨碍通航安全的物体；  
(三)航标发生位移、损坏、灭失；  
(四)妨碍通航安全的其他情况。  
海事管理机构接到报告后，应当根据情况发布航行通告或者航行警告，并通知航道、航标主管部门。

**Article 44** Any unit or individual that discovers any of the following circumstances shall promptly report it to the maritime safety administration:  
(1) changes in the waterway, or changes in water depth or width;  
(2) objects obstructing navigation safety;  
(3) displacement, damage or loss of navigation marks; or  
(4) other circumstances obstructing navigation safety.  
After receiving a report, the maritime safety administration shall, depending on the circumstances, issue a navigation notice or navigation warning, and notify the competent authority for waterways and navigation marks.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　海事管理机构划定或者调整禁航区、交通管制区、港区外锚地、停泊区和安全作业区，以及对进行[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)、第二十八条规定的作业或者活动，需要发布航行通告、航行警告的，应当及时发布。

**Article 45** When the maritime safety administration designates or adjusts prohibited navigation areas, traffic control areas, anchorages outside port areas, mooring areas and safe operation areas, or when it is necessary to issue navigation notices or navigation warnings for the operations or activities provided for in Articles 25 and 28 of these Regulations, it shall issue them in a timely manner.

### 第七章　救助

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　船舶、浮动设施遇险，应当采取一切有效措施进行自救。  
船舶、浮动设施发生碰撞等事故，任何一方应当在不危及自身安全的情况下，积极救助遇险的他方，不得逃逸。  
船舶、浮动设施遇险，必须迅速将遇险的时间、地点、遇险状况、遇险原因、救助要求，向遇险地海事管理机构以及船舶、浮动设施所有人、经营人报告。

**Article 46** When a vessel or floating facility is in distress, it shall take all effective measures for self-rescue.  
In the event of a collision or other accident involving a vessel or floating facility, any party shall, without endangering its own safety, actively rescue the other party in distress, and shall not flee.  
When a vessel or floating facility is in distress, it must promptly report the time, location, situation, cause of the distress and rescue requirements to the maritime safety administration at the place of distress and to the owner and operator of the vessel or floating facility.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　船员、浮动设施上的工作人员或者其他人员发现其他船舶、浮动设施遇险，或者收到求救信号后，必须尽力救助遇险人员，并将有关情况及时向遇险地海事管理机构报告。

**Article 47** After discovering that another vessel or floating facility is in distress, or after receiving a distress signal, crew members, workers on floating facilities or other persons must do their utmost to rescue the persons in distress, and promptly report the relevant information to the maritime safety administration at the place of distress.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　海事管理机构收到船舶、浮动设施遇险求救信号或者报告后，必须立即组织力量救助遇险人员，同时向遇险地县级以上地方人民政府和上级海事管理机构报告。  
遇险地县级以上地方人民政府收到海事管理机构的报告后，应当对救助工作进行领导和协调，动员各方力量积极参与救助。

**Article 48** After receiving a distress signal or report from a vessel or floating facility in distress, the maritime safety administration must immediately organize forces to rescue the persons in distress, and at the same time report to the local people's government at or above the county level at the place of distress and to the maritime safety administration at a higher level.  
After receiving a report from the maritime safety administration, the local people's government at or above the county level at the place of distress shall lead and coordinate the rescue efforts, and mobilize all forces to actively participate in the rescue.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　船舶、浮动设施遇险时，有关部门和人员必须积极协助海事管理机构做好救助工作。  
遇险现场和附近的船舶、人员，必须服从海事管理机构的统一调度和指挥。

**Article 49** When a vessel or floating facility is in distress, the relevant departments and persons must actively assist the maritime safety administration in carrying out the rescue.  
The vessels and persons at or near the scene of the distress must obey the unified dispatch and command of the maritime safety administration.

### 第八章　事故调查处理

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　船舶、浮动设施发生交通事故，其所有人或者经营人必须立即向交通事故发生地海事管理机构报告，并做好现场保护工作。

**Article 50** When a traffic accident occurs involving a vessel or floating facility, the owner or operator thereof must immediately report to the maritime safety administration at the place where the traffic accident occurred, and properly protect the scene.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　海事管理机构接到内河交通事故报告后，必须立即派员前往现场，进行调查和取证。  
海事管理机构进行内河交通事故调查和取证，应当全面、客观、公正。

**Article 51** After receiving a report of an inland river traffic accident, the maritime safety administration must immediately send persons to the scene to conduct investigation and collect evidence.  
The maritime safety administration shall conduct the investigation and evidence collection for an inland river traffic accident comprehensively, objectively and impartially.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　接受海事管理机构调查、取证的有关人员，应当如实提供有关情况和证据，不得谎报或者隐匿、毁灭证据。

**Article 52** The relevant persons who accept investigation and evidence collection by the maritime safety administration shall truthfully provide relevant information and evidence, and shall not make false reports or conceal or destroy evidence.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　海事管理机构应当在内河交通事故调查、取证结束后30日内，依据调查事实和证据作出调查结论，并书面告知内河交通事故当事人。

**Article 53** The maritime safety administration shall, within 30 days after the conclusion of the investigation and evidence collection for an inland river traffic accident, make an investigation conclusion based on the facts and evidence, and notify the parties to the inland river traffic accident in writing.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　海事管理机构在调查处理内河交通事故过程中，应当采取有效措施，保证航路畅通，防止发生其他事故。

**Article 54** In the process of investigating and handling an inland river traffic accident, the maritime safety administration shall take effective measures to ensure the smooth flow of the channel and prevent the occurrence of other accidents.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　地方人民政府应当依照国家有关规定积极做好内河交通事故的善后工作。

**Article 55** Local people's governments shall, in accordance with the relevant state regulations, actively carry out the aftermath work of inland river traffic accidents.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　特大内河交通事故的报告、调查和处理，按照国务院有关规定执行。

**Article 56** The reporting, investigation and handling of extraordinarily serious inland river traffic accidents shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

### 第九章　监督检查

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　在旅游、交通运输繁忙的湖泊、水库，在气候恶劣的季节，在法定或者传统节日、重大集会、集市、农忙、学生放学放假等交通高峰期间，县级以上地方各级人民政府应当加强对维护内河交通安全的组织、协调工作。

**Article 57** In lakes and reservoirs with heavy tourism and transport traffic, in seasons with severe weather, and during peak traffic periods such as statutory or traditional holidays, major gatherings, market days, busy farming seasons, and school dismissal and holidays, local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen their organization and coordination work for maintaining inland river traffic safety.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　海事管理机构必须建立、健全内河交通安全监督检查制度，并组织落实。

**Article 58** The maritime safety administration must establish and improve the system of supervision and inspection over inland river traffic safety, and shall organize its implementation.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　海事管理机构必须依法履行职责，加强对船舶、浮动设施、船员和通航安全环境的监督检查。发现内河交通安全隐患时，应当责令有关单位和个人立即消除或者限期消除；有关单位和个人不立即消除或者逾期不消除的，海事管理机构必须采取责令其临时停航、停止作业，禁止进港、离港等强制性措施。

**Article 59** The maritime safety administration must perform its duties according to law, and strengthen supervision and inspection over vessels, floating facilities, crew members and the navigation safety environment. When discovering potential inland river traffic safety hazards, it shall order the relevant units or individuals to eliminate them immediately or within a time limit; if the relevant units or individuals fail to eliminate them immediately or within the time limit, the maritime safety administration must take compulsory measures such as ordering temporary suspension of navigation or operations, or prohibiting entry into or departure from port.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　对内河交通密集区域、多发事故水域以及货物装卸、乘客上下比较集中的港口，对客渡船、滚装客船、高速客轮、旅游船和载运危险货物的船舶，海事管理机构必须加强安全巡查。

**Article 60** The maritime safety administration must strengthen safety patrols in areas with dense inland river traffic, waters with frequent accidents, and ports where cargo loading and unloading and passenger embarkation and disembarkation are concentrated, as well as for passenger ferries, ro-ro passenger vessels, high-speed passenger vessels, tourist vessels and vessels carrying dangerous cargo.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　海事管理机构依照本条例实施监督检查时，可以根据情况对违反本条例有关规定的船舶，采取责令临时停航、驶向指定地点，禁止进港、离港，强制卸载、拆除动力装置、暂扣船舶等保障通航安全的措施。

**Article 61** When carrying out supervision and inspection in accordance with these Regulations, the maritime safety administration may, depending on the circumstances, take measures to ensure navigation safety against vessels that violate the relevant provisions of these Regulations, such as ordering temporary suspension of navigation, ordering to proceed to a designated place, prohibiting entry into or departure from port, compulsory unloading, removal of propulsion devices, or temporary seizure of the vessel.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　海事管理机构的工作人员依法在内河通航水域对船舶、浮动设施进行内河交通安全监督检查，任何单位和个人不得拒绝或者阻挠。  
有关单位或者个人应当接受海事管理机构依法实施的安全监督检查，并为其提供方便。  
海事管理机构的工作人员依照本条例实施监督检查时，应当出示执法证件，表明身份。

**Article 62** When staff members of the maritime safety administration carry out supervision and inspection over inland river traffic safety concerning vessels and floating facilities in inland river navigable waters according to law, no unit or individual may refuse or obstruct them.  
The relevant units or individuals shall accept the safety supervision and inspection carried out by the maritime safety administration according to law, and shall provide convenience therefor.  
When carrying out supervision and inspection in accordance with these Regulations, staff members of the maritime safety administration shall produce their law enforcement credentials to show their identity.

### 第十章　法律责任

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本条例的规定，应当报废的船舶、浮动设施在内河航行或者作业的，由海事管理机构责令停航或者停止作业，并对船舶、浮动设施予以没收。

**Article 63** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a vessel or floating facility that should be scrapped navigates or operates on inland rivers, the maritime safety administration shall order the suspension of navigation or operations, and shall confiscate the vessel or floating facility.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反本条例的规定，船舶、浮动设施未持有合格的检验证书、登记证书或者船舶未持有必要的航行资料，擅自航行或者作业的，由海事管理机构责令停止航行或者作业；拒不停止的，暂扣船舶、浮动设施；情节严重的，予以没收。

**Article 64** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a vessel or floating facility navigates or operates without holding valid survey certificates or registration certificates, or a vessel navigates or operates without holding the necessary navigational materials, the maritime safety administration shall order the cessation of navigation or operations; if the vessel or floating facility refuses to cease, the maritime safety administration shall temporarily seize the vessel or floating facility; if the circumstances are serious, it shall confiscate the vessel or floating facility.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本条例的规定，船舶未按照国务院交通主管部门的规定配备船员擅自航行，或者浮动设施未按照国务院交通主管部门的规定配备掌握水上交通安全技能的船员擅自作业的，由海事管理机构责令限期改正，对船舶、浮动设施所有人或者经营人处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停航或者停止作业。

**Article 65** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a vessel navigates without being manned with crew members as prescribed by the competent transport authority under the State Council, or a floating facility operates without being manned with crew members possessing skills in water traffic safety as prescribed by the competent transport authority under the State Council, the maritime safety administration shall order rectification within a time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the owner or operator of the vessel or floating facility; if the vessel or floating facility fails to rectify within the time limit, the maritime safety administration shall order the suspension of navigation or operations.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本条例的规定，未经考试合格并取得适任证书或者其他适任证件的人员擅自从事船舶航行的，由海事管理机构责令其立即离岗，对直接责任人员处2000元以上2万元以下的罚款，并对聘用单位处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 66** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a person who has not passed the examination and obtained a certificate of competency or other qualification document engages in vessel navigation without authorization, the maritime safety administration shall order him to leave his post immediately, impose a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan on the person directly responsible, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the employing unit.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　违反本条例的规定，按照国家规定必须取得船舶污染损害责任、沉船打捞责任的保险文书或者财务保证书的船舶的所有人或者经营人，未取得船舶污染损害责任、沉船打捞责任保险文书或者财务担保证明的，由海事管理机构责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令停航，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 67** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the owner or operator of a vessel that is required by state regulations to obtain insurance documents or financial guarantees for liability for pollution damage and for salvage of sunken vessels fails to obtain such documents or guarantees, the maritime safety administration shall order rectification within a time limit; if the owner or operator fails to rectify within the time limit, the maritime safety administration shall order the vessel to suspend navigation, and shall impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　违反本条例的规定，船舶在内河航行时，有下列情形之一的，由海事管理机构责令改正，处5000元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，禁止船舶进出港口或者责令停航，并可以对责任船员给予暂扣适任证书或者其他适任证件3个月至6个月的处罚：  
(一)未按照规定悬挂国旗，标明船名、船籍港、载重线的；  
(二)未按照规定向海事管理机构报告船舶的航次计划、适航状态、船员配备和载货载客等情况的；  
(三)未按照规定申请引航的；  
(四)擅自进出内河港口，强行通过交通管制区、通航密集区、航行条件受限制区域或者禁航区的；  
(五)载运或者拖带超重、超长、超高、超宽、半潜的物体，未申请或者未按照核定的航路、时间航行的。

**Article 68** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a vessel navigating on inland rivers falls under any of the following circumstances, the maritime safety administration shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the maritime safety administration shall prohibit the vessel from entering or leaving the port or order the suspension of navigation, and may impose a penalty of temporary seizure of the certificate of competency or other qualification document of the responsible crew member for a period of not less than 3 months but not more than 6 months:  
(1) failing to fly the national flag or indicate the vessel's name, port of registry and load line as required;  
(2) failing to report to the maritime safety administration on the vessel's voyage plan, seaworthiness status, crew manning and cargo and passenger carrying conditions as required;  
(3) failing to apply for pilotage as required;  
(4) entering or leaving an inland river port without authorization, or forcibly passing through a traffic control area, a congested navigable area, an area with restricted navigational conditions or a prohibited navigation area; or  
(5) carrying or towing overweight, overlength, overheight, overwidth or semi-submerged objects without applying for or navigating in accordance with the approved route and time.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　违反本条例的规定，船舶未在码头、泊位或者依法公布的锚地、停泊区、作业区停泊的，由海事管理机构责令改正；拒不改正的，予以强行拖离，因拖离发生的费用由船舶所有人或者经营人承担。

**Article 69** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a vessel fails to berth at a wharf, berth, or at an anchorage, mooring area or operation area published according to law, the maritime safety administration shall order rectification; if the vessel refuses to rectify, the maritime safety administration shall forcibly tow it away, and the expenses incurred from the towing shall be borne by the owner or operator of the vessel.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　违反本条例的规定，在内河通航水域或者岸线上进行有关作业或者活动未经批准或者备案，或者未设置标志、显示信号的，由海事管理机构责令改正，处5000元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 70** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a relevant operation or activity is carried out in inland river navigable waters or on their banks without approval or filing, or without setting up signs or displaying signals, the maritime safety administration shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　违反本条例的规定，从事危险货物作业，有下列情形之一的，由海事管理机构责令停止作业或者航行，对负有责任的主管人员或者其他直接责任人员处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；属于船员的，并给予暂扣适任证书或者其他适任证件6个月以上直至吊销适任证书或者其他适任证件的处罚：  
(一)从事危险货物运输的船舶，未编制危险货物事故应急预案或者未配备相应的应急救援设备和器材的；  
(二)船舶装卸、过驳危险货物或者载运危险货物进出港口未经海事管理机构、港口管理机构同意的。  
未持有危险货物适装证书擅自载运危险货物或者未按照安全技术规范进行配载和运输的，依照《危险化学品安全管理条例》的规定处罚。

**Article 71** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, any of the following circumstances occurs in the course of dangerous cargo operations, the maritime safety administration shall order the suspension of operations or navigation, impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the responsible persons in charge or other persons directly responsible; if the person is a crew member, a penalty of temporary seizure of the certificate of competency or other qualification document for a period of not less than 6 months up to revocation thereof shall also be imposed:  
(1) a vessel engaged in the transport of dangerous cargo fails to formulate an emergency response plan for dangerous cargo accidents or fails to be equipped with corresponding emergency rescue equipment and appliances; or  
(2) the loading, unloading or transshipment of dangerous cargo or the entry or departure of a vessel carrying dangerous cargo is not approved by the maritime safety administration or the port administration.  
Where a vessel carries dangerous cargo without holding a dangerous cargo suitability certificate or fails to stow and transport the cargo in accordance with the safety technical specifications, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on the Safety Management of Hazardous Chemicals.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　违反本条例的规定，未经批准擅自设置或者撤销渡口的，由渡口所在地县级人民政府指定的部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，予以强制拆除或者恢复，因强制拆除或者恢复发生的费用分别由设置人、撤销人承担。

**Article 72** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a ferry is established or cancelled without approval, the department designated by the people's government of the county where the ferry is located shall order rectification within a time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, the ferry shall be forcibly demolished or restored, and the expenses incurred from the forcible demolition or restoration shall be borne by the person who established or cancelled the ferry respectively.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　违反本条例的规定，渡口船舶未标明识别标志、载客定额、安全注意事项的，由渡口所在地县级人民政府指定的部门责令改正，处2000元以上1万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停航。

**Article 73** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a ferry vessel fails to indicate identification marks, passenger quota or safety precautions, the department designated by the people's government of the county where the ferry is located shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan; if rectification is not made within the time limit, the department shall order the suspension of navigation.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　违反本条例的规定，在内河通航水域的航道内养殖、种植植物、水生物或者设置永久性固定设施的，由海事管理机构责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，予以强制清除，因清除发生的费用由其所有人或者经营人承担。

**Article 74** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, cultivation, planting of plants or aquatic organisms, or installation of permanent fixed facilities is carried out within the waterway of inland river navigable waters, the maritime safety administration shall order rectification within a time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, the objects shall be forcibly removed, and the expenses incurred from the removal shall be borne by the owner or operator thereof.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反本条例的规定，内河通航水域中的沉没物、漂流物、搁浅物的所有人或者经营人，未按照国家有关规定设置标志或者未在规定的时间内打捞清除的，由海事管理机构责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，海事管理机构强制设置标志或者组织打捞清除；需要立即组织打捞清除的，海事管理机构应当及时组织打捞清除。海事管理机构因设置标志或者打捞清除发生的费用，由沉没物、漂流物、搁浅物的所有人或者经营人承担。

**Article 75** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the owner or operator of a sunken, drifting or grounded object in inland river navigable waters fails to set up signs in accordance with the relevant state regulations or fails to salvage and remove the object within the prescribed time limit, the maritime safety administration shall order rectification within a time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, the maritime safety administration shall forcibly set up signs or organize salvage and removal. If it is necessary to organize salvage and removal immediately, the maritime safety administration shall organize salvage and removal in a timely manner. The expenses incurred by the maritime safety administration for setting up signs or for salvage and removal shall be borne by the owner or operator of the sunken, drifting or grounded object.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　违反本条例的规定，船舶、浮动设施遇险后未履行报告义务或者不积极施救的，由海事管理机构给予警告，并可以对责任船员给予暂扣适任证书或者其他适任证件3个月至6个月直至吊销适任证书或者其他适任证件的处罚。

**Article 76** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a vessel or floating facility fails to perform the reporting obligation or fails to actively rescue after being in distress, the maritime safety administration shall give a warning, and may impose a penalty of temporary seizure of the certificate of competency or other qualification document of the responsible crew member for a period of not less than 3 months but not more than 6 months up to revocation thereof.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　违反本条例的规定，船舶、浮动设施发生内河交通事故的，除依法承担相应的法律责任外，由海事管理机构根据调查结论，对责任船员给予暂扣适任证书或者其他适任证件6个月以上直至吊销适任证书或者其他适任证件的处罚。

**Article 77** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a vessel or floating facility is involved in an inland river traffic accident, in addition to bearing the corresponding legal liability according to law, the maritime safety administration shall, based on the investigation conclusion, impose a penalty of temporary seizure of the certificate of competency or other qualification document of the responsible crew member for a period of not less than 6 months up to revocation thereof.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　违反本条例的规定，遇险现场和附近的船舶、船员不服从海事管理机构的统一调度和指挥的，由海事管理机构给予警告，并可以对责任船员给予暂扣适任证书或者其他适任证件3个月至6个月直至吊销适任证书或者其他适任证件的处罚。

**Article 78** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a vessel or crew member at or near the scene of a distress does not obey the unified dispatch and command of the maritime safety administration, the maritime safety administration shall give a warning, and may impose a penalty of temporary seizure of the certificate of competency or other qualification document of the responsible crew member for a period of not less than 3 months but not more than 6 months up to revocation thereof.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　违反本条例的规定，伪造、变造、买卖、转借、冒用船舶检验证书、船舶登记证书、船员适任证书或者其他适任证件的，由海事管理机构没收有关的证书或者证件；有违法所得的，没收违法所得，并处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足2万元的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；触犯刑律的，依照刑法关于伪造、变造、买卖国家机关公文、证件罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 79** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a person forges, alters, buys, sells, transfers, lends or fraudulently uses a vessel survey certificate, vessel registration certificate, crew competency certificate or other qualification document, the maritime safety administration shall confiscate the relevant certificate or document; if there is any illegal income, the illegal income shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 2 times but not more than 5 times the illegal income shall be imposed; if there is no illegal income or the illegal income is less than 20,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of forging, altering or buying or selling official documents or certificates of a state organ or other crimes.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　违反本条例的规定，船舶、浮动设施的所有人或者经营人指使、强令船员违章操作的，由海事管理机构给予警告，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款，并可以责令停航或者停止作业；造成重大伤亡事故或者严重后果的，依照刑法关于重大责任事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 80** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the owner or operator of a vessel or floating facility instructs or compels a crew member to operate in violation of regulations, the maritime safety administration shall give a warning, impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan, and may order the suspension of navigation or operations; if a major casualty accident or other serious consequences are caused, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of causing a major accident due to neglect of duty or other crimes.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　违反本条例的规定，船舶在内河航行、停泊或者作业，不遵守航行、避让和信号显示规则的，由海事管理机构责令改正，处1000元以上1万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，对责任船员给予暂扣适任证书或者其他适任证件3个月至6个月直至吊销适任证书或者其他适任证件的处罚；造成重大内河交通事故的，依照刑法关于交通肇事罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 81** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a vessel navigating, berthing or operating on inland rivers fails to comply with the rules on navigation, avoidance and signal display, the maritime safety administration shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a penalty of temporary seizure of the certificate of competency or other qualification document of the responsible crew member for a period of not less than 3 months but not more than 6 months up to revocation thereof shall be imposed; if a major inland river traffic accident is caused, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of causing a traffic accident or other crimes.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　违反本条例的规定，船舶不具备安全技术条件从事货物、旅客运输，或者超载运输货物、旅客的，由海事管理机构责令改正，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款，可以对责任船员给予暂扣适任证书或者其他适任证件6个月以上直至吊销适任证书或者其他适任证件的处罚，并对超载运输的船舶强制卸载，因卸载而发生的卸货费、存货费、旅客安置费和船舶监管费由船舶所有人或者经营人承担；发生重大伤亡事故或者造成其他严重后果的，依照刑法关于重大劳动安全事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 82** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a vessel that does not meet the safety technical conditions engages in the transport of cargo or passengers, or carries cargo or passengers in excess of the load line, the maritime safety administration shall order rectification, impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan, may impose a penalty of temporary seizure of the certificate of competency or other qualification document of the responsible crew member for a period of not less than 6 months up to revocation thereof, and shall forcibly unload the overloaded vessel. The expenses for unloading, storage, passenger accommodation and vessel supervision incurred from the unloading shall be borne by the owner or operator of the vessel. If a major casualty accident or other serious consequences are caused, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of causing a major labor safety accident or other crimes.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　违反本条例的规定，船舶、浮动设施发生内河交通事故后逃逸的，由海事管理机构对责任船员给予吊销适任证书或者其他适任证件的处罚；证书或者证件吊销后，5年内不得重新从业；触犯刑律的，依照刑法关于交通肇事罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 83** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a vessel or floating facility flees after an inland river traffic accident, the maritime safety administration shall impose a penalty of revocation of the certificate of competency or other qualification document of the responsible crew member; after the certificate or document is revoked, the person shall not be re-employed within 5 years; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of causing a traffic accident or other crimes.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　违反本条例的规定，阻碍、妨碍内河交通事故调查取证，或者谎报、隐匿、毁灭证据的，由海事管理机构给予警告，并对直接责任人员处1000元以上1万元以下的罚款；属于船员的，并给予暂扣适任证书或者其他适任证件12个月以上直至吊销适任证书或者其他适任证件的处罚；以暴力、威胁方法阻碍内河交通事故调查取证的，依照刑法关于妨害公务罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 84** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a person obstructs or hinders the investigation and evidence collection of an inland river traffic accident, or makes false reports or conceals or destroys evidence, the maritime safety administration shall give a warning, and impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan on the person directly responsible; if the person is a crew member, a penalty of temporary seizure of the certificate of competency or other qualification document for a period of not less than 12 months up to revocation thereof shall also be imposed; if the person obstructs the investigation and evidence collection of an inland river traffic accident by means of violence or threat, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of disrupting public service.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　违反本条例的规定，海事管理机构不依据法定的安全条件进行审批、许可的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员根据不同情节，给予降级或者撤职的行政处分；造成重大内河交通事故或者致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失的，依照刑法关于滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 85** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the maritime safety administration fails to conduct examination, approval or licensing in accordance with the statutory safety conditions, the responsible persons in charge and other persons directly responsible shall, depending on the circumstances, be given administrative sanctions of demotion or removal from office; if a major inland river traffic accident is caused or public property, state interests or the interests of the people suffer major losses, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty or other crimes.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　违反本条例的规定，海事管理机构对审批、许可的安全事项不实施监督检查的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员根据不同情节，给予记大过、降级或者撤职的行政处分；造成重大内河交通事故或者致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失的，依照刑法关于滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 86** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the maritime safety administration fails to carry out supervision and inspection over the safety matters that have been examined, approved or licensed, the responsible persons in charge and other persons directly responsible shall, depending on the circumstances, be given administrative sanctions of recording of a major demerit, demotion or removal from office; if a major inland river traffic accident is caused or public property, state interests or the interests of the people suffer major losses, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty or other crimes.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　违反本条例的规定，海事管理机构发现船舶、浮动设施不再具备安全航行、停泊、作业条件而不及时撤销批准或者许可并予以处理的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员根据不同情节，给予记大过、降级或者撤职的行政处分；造成重大内河交通事故或者致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失的，依照刑法关于滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 87** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the maritime safety administration discovers that a vessel or floating facility no longer meets the conditions for safe navigation, berthing or operations but fails to timely revoke the approval or license and deal with the matter, the responsible persons in charge and other persons directly responsible shall, depending on the circumstances, be given administrative sanctions of recording of a major demerit, demotion or removal from office; if a major inland river traffic accident is caused or public property, state interests or the interests of the people suffer major losses, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty or other crimes.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　违反本条例的规定，海事管理机构对未经审批、许可擅自从事旅客、危险货物运输的船舶不实施监督检查，或者发现内河交通安全隐患不及时依法处理，或者对违法行为不依法予以处罚的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员根据不同情节，给予降级或者撤职的行政处分；造成重大内河交通事故或者致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失的，依照刑法关于滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 88** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the maritime safety administration fails to carry out supervision and inspection over a vessel that engages in the transport of passengers or dangerous cargo without examination, approval or licensing, or fails to timely deal with potential inland river traffic safety hazards according to law, or fails to impose penalties on illegal acts according to law, the responsible persons in charge and other persons directly responsible shall, depending on the circumstances, be given administrative sanctions of demotion or removal from office; if a major inland river traffic accident is caused or public property, state interests or the interests of the people suffer major losses, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty or other crimes.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　违反本条例的规定，渡口所在地县级人民政府指定的部门，有下列情形之一的，根据不同情节，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，给予降级或者撤职的行政处分；造成重大内河交通事故或者致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失的，依照刑法关于滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)对县级人民政府批准的渡口不依法实施监督检查的；  
(二)对未经县级人民政府批准擅自设立的渡口不予以查处的；  
(三)对渡船超载、人与大牲畜混载、人与爆炸品、压缩气体和液化气体、易燃液体、易燃固体、自燃物品和遇湿易燃物品、氧化剂和有机过氧化物、有毒品和腐蚀品等危险品混载以及其他危及安全的行为不及时纠正并依法处理的。

**Article 89** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the department designated by the people's government of the county where the ferry is located falls under any of the following circumstances, the responsible persons in charge and other persons directly responsible shall, depending on the circumstances, be given administrative sanctions of demotion or removal from office; if a major inland river traffic accident is caused or public property, state interests or the interests of the people suffer major losses, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty or other crimes:  
(1) failing to carry out supervision and inspection according to law over a ferry approved by the county people's government;  
(2) failing to investigate and deal with a ferry established without approval from the county people's government; or  
(3) failing to timely rectify and handle according to law such acts as overloading of ferry vessels, mixing of persons with large livestock, mixing of persons with dangerous goods such as explosives, compressed gases and liquefied gases, flammable liquids, flammable solids, substances liable to spontaneous combustion and substances which, in contact with water, emit flammable gases, oxidizing agents and organic peroxides, toxic substances and corrosives, and other acts endangering safety.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　违反本条例的规定，触犯《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 90** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a person contravenes the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments and constitutes an act violating public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration punishment.

### 第十一章　附则

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
(一)内河通航水域，是指由海事管理机构认定的可供船舶航行的江、河、湖泊、水库、运河等水域。  
(二)船舶，是指各类排水或者非排水的船、艇、筏、水上飞行器、潜水器、移动式平台以及其他水上移动装置。  
(三)浮动设施，是指采用缆绳或者锚链等非刚性固定方式系固并漂浮或者潜于水中的建筑、装置。  
(四)交通事故，是指船舶、浮动设施在内河通航水域发生的碰撞、触碰、触礁、浪损、搁浅、火灾、爆炸、沉没等引起人身伤亡和财产损失的事件。

**Article 91** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms have the meanings ascribed to them below:  
(1) "Inland river navigable waters" means such waters as rivers, lakes, reservoirs and canals that are recognized by the maritime safety administration as navigable for vessels.  
(2) "Vessel" means any type of ship, boat, raft, watercraft, submersible, mobile platform or other mobile device on water, whether self-propelled or not, and whether displacing water or not.  
(3) "Floating facility" means a structure or device that is moored by non-rigid means such as cables or anchor chains and floats or is submerged in water.  
(4) "Traffic accident" means an event such as collision, striking, grounding, swell damage, stranding, fire, explosion or sinking that occurs to a vessel or floating facility in inland river navigable waters and causes personal injury or death or property loss.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　军事船舶在内河通航水域航行，应当遵守内河航行、避让和信号显示规则。军事船舶的检验、登记和船员的考试、发证等管理办法，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 92** Military vessels navigating in inland river navigable waters shall comply with the rules on inland river navigation, avoidance and signal display. The administrative measures for the survey and registration of military vessels and the examination and certification of their crew members shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　渔船的登记以及进出渔港报告，渔船船员的考试、发证，渔船之间交通事故的调查处理，以及渔港水域内渔船的交通安全管理办法，由国务院渔业行政主管部门依据本条例另行规定。  
渔业船舶的检验及相关监督管理，由国务院交通运输主管部门按照相关渔业船舶检验的行政法规执行。

**Article 93** The administrative measures for the registration of fishing vessels and reporting of entry and exit of fishing ports, the examination and certification of crew members of fishing vessels, the investigation and handling of traffic accidents between fishing vessels, and the traffic safety management of fishing vessels in the waters of fishing ports shall be separately formulated by the competent fisheries administrative department under the State Council in accordance with these Regulations.  
The survey and related supervision and administration of fishing vessels shall be carried out by the competent transport authority under the State Council in accordance with the relevant administrative regulations on the survey of fishing vessels.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　城市园林水域水上交通安全管理的具体办法，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定；但是，有关船舶检验、登记和船员管理，依照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 94** The specific measures for the administration of water traffic safety in urban garden waters shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government; however, the relevant provisions on vessel survey, registration and crew management shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　本条例自2002年8月1日起施行。1986年12月16日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国内河交通安全管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 95** These Regulations shall come into force as of August 1, 2002. The Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Inland River Traffic Safety promulgated by the State Council on December 16, 1986 shall be abolished simultaneously.
