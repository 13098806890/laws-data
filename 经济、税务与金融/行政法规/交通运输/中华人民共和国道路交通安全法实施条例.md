# 中华人民共和国道路交通安全法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2017-10-07  
**生效日期**：2017-10-07

> (2004年4月30日中华人民共和国国务院令第405号公布　根据2017年10月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法》(以下简称道路交通安全法)的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　中华人民共和国境内的车辆驾驶人、行人、乘车人以及与道路交通活动有关的单位和个人，应当遵守道路交通安全法和本条例。 第三条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当建立、健全道路交通安全工作协调机制，组织有关部门对城市建设项目进行交通影响评价，制定道路交通安全管理规划，确定管理目标，制定实施方案。 第二章　车辆和驾驶人 第一节　机动车 第四条　机动车的登记，分为注册登记、变更登记、转移登记、抵押登记和注销登记。 第五条　初次申领机动车号牌、行驶证的，应当向机动车所有人住所地的公安机关交通管理部门申请注册登记。 申请机动车注册登记，应当交验机动车，并提交以下证明、凭证： (一)机动车所有人的身份证明； (二)购车发票等机动车来历证明； (三)机动车整车出厂合格证明或者进口机动车进口凭证； (四)车辆购置税完税证明或者免税凭证； (五)机动车第三者责任强制保险凭证；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法》(以下简称道路交通安全法)的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Road Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Road Traffic Safety Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国境内的车辆驾驶人、行人、乘车人以及与道路交通活动有关的单位和个人，应当遵守道路交通安全法和本条例。

**Article 2** Drivers of vehicles, pedestrians, passengers, and units and individuals involved in road traffic activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall comply with the Road Traffic Safety Law and these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当建立、健全道路交通安全工作协调机制，组织有关部门对城市建设项目进行交通影响评价，制定道路交通安全管理规划，确定管理目标，制定实施方案。

**Article 3** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve a coordination mechanism for road traffic safety work, organize relevant departments to conduct traffic impact assessments for urban construction projects, formulate road traffic safety management plans, set management objectives, and formulate implementation plans.

### 第二章　车辆和驾驶人

#### 第一节　机动车

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　机动车的登记，分为注册登记、变更登记、转移登记、抵押登记和注销登记。

**Article 4** The registration of motor vehicles is categorized into initial registration, change registration, transfer registration, mortgage registration, and cancellation registration.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　初次申领机动车号牌、行驶证的，应当向机动车所有人住所地的公安机关交通管理部门申请注册登记。  
申请机动车注册登记，应当交验机动车，并提交以下证明、凭证：  
(一)机动车所有人的身份证明；  
(二)购车发票等机动车来历证明；  
(三)机动车整车出厂合格证明或者进口机动车进口凭证；  
(四)车辆购置税完税证明或者免税凭证；  
(五)机动车第三者责任强制保险凭证；  
(六)法律、行政法规规定应当在机动车注册登记时提交的其他证明、凭证。  
不属于国务院机动车产品主管部门规定免予安全技术检验的车型的，还应当提供机动车安全技术检验合格证明。

**Article 5** For the first application for a motor vehicle license plate and driving permit, the motor vehicle owner shall apply for initial registration to the traffic management department of the public security organ at the place of domicile of the motor vehicle owner.  
When applying for motor vehicle initial registration, the motor vehicle shall be inspected and the following certificates and documents shall be submitted:  
(1) Identity certificate of the motor vehicle owner;  
(2) Proof of the motor vehicle's origin, such as purchase invoice;  
(3) Certificate of conformity for the complete motor vehicle produced by the manufacturer or import certificate for imported motor vehicles;  
(4) Certificate of payment of vehicle purchase tax or tax exemption certificate;  
(5) Compulsory insurance certificate for third-party liability of motor vehicle;  
(6) Other certificates and documents that shall be submitted upon motor vehicle initial registration as provided by laws and administrative regulations.  
Where the motor vehicle type is not exempted from safety technical inspection as prescribed by the competent product administration department of the State Council, a certificate of passing the safety technical inspection of the motor vehicle shall also be provided.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　已注册登记的机动车有下列情形之一的，机动车所有人应当向登记该机动车的公安机关交通管理部门申请变更登记：  
(一)改变机动车车身颜色的；  
(二)更换发动机的；  
(三)更换车身或者车架的；  
(四)因质量有问题，制造厂更换整车的；  
(五)营运机动车改为非营运机动车或者非营运机动车改为营运机动车的；  
(六)机动车所有人的住所迁出或者迁入公安机关交通管理部门管辖区域的。  
申请机动车变更登记，应当提交下列证明、凭证，属于前款第(一)项、第(二)项、第(三)项、第(四)项、第(五)项情形之一的，还应当交验机动车；属于前款第(二)项、第(三)项情形之一的，还应当同时提交机动车安全技术检验合格证明：  
(一)机动车所有人的身份证明；  
(二)机动车登记证书；  
(三)机动车行驶证。  
机动车所有人的住所在公安机关交通管理部门管辖区域内迁移、机动车所有人的姓名(单位名称)或者联系方式变更的，应当向登记该机动车的公安机关交通管理部门备案。

**Article 6** Where a registered motor vehicle falls under any of the following circumstances, the motor vehicle owner shall apply for change registration to the traffic management department of the public security organ that registered the motor vehicle:  
(1) Change of the motor vehicle body color;  
(2) Replacement of the engine;  
(3) Replacement of the body or frame;  
(4) Replacement of the complete vehicle by the manufacturer due to quality problems;  
(5) Change of a commercial motor vehicle to a non-commercial motor vehicle, or a non-commercial motor vehicle to a commercial motor vehicle;  
(6) Relocation of the domicile of the motor vehicle owner out of or into the jurisdiction area of the traffic management department of the public security organ.  
When applying for motor vehicle change registration, the following certificates and documents shall be submitted. Where the circumstance is under items (1), (2), (3), (4), or (5) of the preceding paragraph, the motor vehicle shall also be inspected; where the circumstance is under items (2) or (3) of the preceding paragraph, a certificate of passing the safety technical inspection of the motor vehicle shall also be submitted simultaneously:  
(1) Identity certificate of the motor vehicle owner;  
(2) Motor vehicle registration certificate;  
(3) Motor vehicle driving permit.  
Where the domicile of the motor vehicle owner is relocated within the jurisdiction area of the traffic management department of the public security organ, or the name (unit name) or contact information of the motor vehicle owner is changed, the motor vehicle owner shall file a record with the traffic management department of the public security organ that registered the motor vehicle.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　已注册登记的机动车所有权发生转移的，应当及时办理转移登记。  
申请机动车转移登记，当事人应当向登记该机动车的公安机关交通管理部门交验机动车，并提交以下证明、凭证：  
(一)当事人的身份证明；  
(二)机动车所有权转移的证明、凭证；  
(三)机动车登记证书；  
(四)机动车行驶证。

**Article 7** Where the ownership of a registered motor vehicle is transferred, transfer registration shall be processed in a timely manner.  
When applying for motor vehicle transfer registration, the party shall submit the motor vehicle for inspection to the traffic management department of the public security organ that registered the motor vehicle, and provide the following certificates and documents:  
(1) Identity certificate of the party;  
(2) Certificate and document evidencing the transfer of motor vehicle ownership;  
(3) Motor vehicle registration certificate;  
(4) Motor vehicle driving permit.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　机动车所有人将机动车作为抵押物抵押的，机动车所有人应当向登记该机动车的公安机关交通管理部门申请抵押登记。

**Article 8** Where a motor vehicle owner mortgages the motor vehicle as collateral, the motor vehicle owner shall apply for mortgage registration to the traffic management department of the public security organ that registered the motor vehicle.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　已注册登记的机动车达到国家规定的强制报废标准的，公安机关交通管理部门应当在报废期满的2个月前通知机动车所有人办理注销登记。机动车所有人应当在报废期满前将机动车交售给机动车回收企业，由机动车回收企业将报废的机动车登记证书、号牌、行驶证交公安机关交通管理部门注销。机动车所有人逾期不办理注销登记的，公安机关交通管理部门应当公告该机动车登记证书、号牌、行驶证作废。  
因机动车灭失申请注销登记的，机动车所有人应当向公安机关交通管理部门提交本人身份证明，交回机动车登记证书。

**Article 9** Where a registered motor vehicle meets the mandatory scrapping standards prescribed by the State, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall notify the motor vehicle owner to undergo cancellation registration two months before the expiry of the scrapping period. The motor vehicle owner shall sell the motor vehicle to a motor vehicle recycling enterprise before the expiry of the scrapping period, and the motor vehicle recycling enterprise shall deliver the scrapped motor vehicle registration certificate, license plate, and driving permit to the traffic management department of the public security organ for cancellation. If the motor vehicle owner fails to undergo cancellation registration within the prescribed time limit, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall announce the motor vehicle registration certificate, license plate, and driving permit as void.  
Where an application for cancellation registration is made due to the loss of the motor vehicle, the motor vehicle owner shall submit his/her identity certificate to the traffic management department of the public security organ and return the motor vehicle registration certificate.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　办理机动车登记的申请人提交的证明、凭证齐全、有效的，公安机关交通管理部门应当当场办理登记手续。  
人民法院、人民检察院以及行政执法部门依法查封、扣押的机动车，公安机关交通管理部门不予办理机动车登记。

**Article 10** Where the certificates and documents submitted by an applicant for motor vehicle registration are complete and valid, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall process the registration formalities on the spot.  
For a motor vehicle that has been sealed up or seized by a People's Court, a People's Procuratorate, or an administrative law enforcement department according to law, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall not process the motor vehicle registration.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　机动车登记证书、号牌、行驶证丢失或者损毁，机动车所有人申请补发的，应当向公安机关交通管理部门提交本人身份证明和申请材料。公安机关交通管理部门经与机动车登记档案核实后，在收到申请之日起15日内补发。

**Article 11** Where the motor vehicle registration certificate, license plate, or driving permit is lost or damaged, the owner of the motor vehicle applying for reissuance shall submit his or her identity certificate and application materials to the traffic management department of the public security organ. The traffic management department of the public security organ shall, after verifying with the motor vehicle registration archives, reissue them within 15 days from the date of receiving the application.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　税务部门、保险机构可以在公安机关交通管理部门的办公场所集中办理与机动车有关的税费缴纳、保险合同订立等事项。

**Article 12** Tax authorities and insurance institutions may, at the office premises of the traffic management department of the public security organ, centrally handle matters such as the payment of taxes and fees related to motor vehicles and the conclusion of insurance contracts.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　机动车号牌应当悬挂在车前、车后指定位置，保持清晰、完整。重型、中型载货汽车及其挂车、拖拉机及其挂车的车身或者车厢后部应当喷涂放大的牌号，字样应当端正并保持清晰。  
机动车检验合格标志、保险标志应当粘贴在机动车前窗右上角。  
机动车喷涂、粘贴标识或者车身广告的，不得影响安全驾驶。

**Article 13** The license plate of a motor vehicle shall be hung at the designated positions at the front and rear of the vehicle, and shall be kept clear and intact. For heavy and medium-duty trucks and their trailers, tractors and their trailers, the rear part of the body or compartment shall be spray-painted with an enlarged plate number, and the characters shall be upright and kept clear.  
The motor vehicle inspection qualification mark and insurance mark shall be affixed to the upper right corner of the front windshield of the motor vehicle.  
Where logos or body advertisements are spray-painted on or affixed to a motor vehicle, safe driving shall not be affected.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　用于公路营运的载客汽车、重型载货汽车、半挂牵引车应当安装、使用符合国家标准的行驶记录仪。交通警察可以对机动车行驶速度、连续驾驶时间以及其他行驶状态信息进行检查。安装行驶记录仪可以分步实施，实施步骤由国务院机动车产品主管部门会同有关部门规定。

**Article 14** Passenger vehicles used for highway operations, heavy-duty trucks, and semi-trailer tractors shall be installed with and use traveling recorders that conform to national standards. Traffic police may inspect the traveling speed, continuous driving time, and other traveling status information of motor vehicles. The installation of traveling recorders may be carried out in stages, and the implementation steps shall be prescribed by the competent department of motor vehicle products of the State Council in conjunction with the relevant departments.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　机动车安全技术检验由机动车安全技术检验机构实施。机动车安全技术检验机构应当按照国家机动车安全技术检验标准对机动车进行检验，对检验结果承担法律责任。  
质量技术监督部门负责对机动车安全技术检验机构实行计量认证管理，对机动车安全技术检验设备进行检定，对执行国家机动车安全技术检验标准的情况进行监督。  
机动车安全技术检验项目由国务院公安部门会同国务院质量技术监督部门规定。

**Article 15** Safety technical inspection of motor vehicles shall be carried out by motor vehicle safety technical inspection institutions. A motor vehicle safety technical inspection institution shall inspect motor vehicles in accordance with the national standards for safety technical inspection of motor vehicles, and shall bear legal liability for the inspection results.  
The quality and technical supervision department shall be responsible for implementing measurement certification management for motor vehicle safety technical inspection institutions, verifying motor vehicle safety technical inspection equipment, and supervising the implementation of national standards for safety technical inspection of motor vehicles.  
The items for safety technical inspection of motor vehicles shall be prescribed by the public security department of the State Council in conjunction with the quality and technical supervision department of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　机动车应当从注册登记之日起，按照下列期限进行安全技术检验：  
(一)营运载客汽车5年以内每年检验1次；超过5年的，每6个月检验1次；  
(二)载货汽车和大型、中型非营运载客汽车10年以内每年检验1次；超过10年的，每6个月检验1次；  
(三)小型、微型非营运载客汽车6年以内每2年检验1次；超过6年的，每年检验1次；超过15年的，每6个月检验1次；  
(四)摩托车4年以内每2年检验1次；超过4年的，每年检验1次；  
(五)拖拉机和其他机动车每年检验1次。  
营运机动车在规定检验期限内经安全技术检验合格的，不再重复进行安全技术检验。

**Article 16** A motor vehicle shall, from the date of registration, undergo safety technical inspection in accordance with the following periods:  
(1) For operating passenger vehicles, once a year within five years, and once every six months after five years;  
(2) For trucks and large and medium-sized non-operating passenger vehicles, once a year within ten years, and once every six months after ten years;  
(3) For small and mini non-operating passenger vehicles, once every two years within six years, once a year after six years, and once every six months after fifteen years;  
(4) For motorcycles, once every two years within four years, and once a year after four years;  
(5) For tractors and other motor vehicles, once a year.  
Where an operating motor vehicle passes the safety technical inspection within the prescribed inspection period, no repeated safety technical inspection shall be conducted.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　已注册登记的机动车进行安全技术检验时，机动车行驶证记载的登记内容与该机动车的有关情况不符，或者未按照规定提供机动车第三者责任强制保险凭证的，不予通过检验。

**Article 17** Where a registered motor vehicle undergoes safety technical inspection, if the registration particulars recorded in the motor vehicle driving permit do not match the relevant information of the motor vehicle, or if the motor vehicle compulsory third-party liability insurance certificate is not provided in accordance with the provisions, the inspection shall not be passed.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　警车、消防车、救护车、工程救险车标志图案的喷涂以及警报器、标志灯具的安装、使用规定，由国务院公安部门制定。

**Article 18** The provisions on the spray-painting of emblem patterns for police cars, fire engines, ambulances, and engineering rescue vehicles, as well as the installation and use of sirens and warning lights, shall be formulated by the public security department of the State Council.

#### 第二节　机动车驾驶人

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　符合国务院公安部门规定的驾驶许可条件的人，可以向公安机关交通管理部门申请机动车驾驶证。  
机动车驾驶证由国务院公安部门规定式样并监制。

**Article 19** A person who meets the driving license requirements prescribed by the public security department of the State Council may apply to the traffic management department of the public security organ for a motor vehicle driving license.  
The form of the motor vehicle driving license shall be prescribed and supervised by the public security department of the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　学习机动车驾驶，应当先学习道路交通安全法律、法规和相关知识，考试合格后，再学习机动车驾驶技能。  
在道路上学习驾驶，应当按照公安机关交通管理部门指定的路线、时间进行。在道路上学习机动车驾驶技能应当使用教练车，在教练员随车指导下进行，与教学无关的人员不得乘坐教练车。学员在学习驾驶中有道路交通安全违法行为或者造成交通事故的，由教练员承担责任。

**Article 20** To learn to drive a motor vehicle, one shall first learn the laws, regulations, and relevant knowledge on road traffic safety, and after passing the examination, then learn the skills of driving a motor vehicle.  
Learning to drive on the road shall be conducted on the routes and at the times designated by the traffic management department of the public security organ. Learning driving skills on the road shall be conducted in a training vehicle under the guidance of an instructor accompanying the vehicle, and persons unrelated to the teaching shall not ride in the training vehicle. Where a student commits a road traffic safety violation or causes a traffic accident while learning to drive, the instructor shall bear the liability.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　公安机关交通管理部门应当对申请机动车驾驶证的人进行考试，对考试合格的，在5日内核发机动车驾驶证；对考试不合格的，书面说明理由。

**Article 21** The traffic management department of the public security organ shall conduct examinations for applicants for motor vehicle driving licenses. For those who pass the examination, it shall issue a motor vehicle driving license within five days; for those who fail the examination, it shall provide a written explanation of the reasons.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　机动车驾驶证的有效期为6年，本条例另有规定的除外。  
机动车驾驶人初次申领机动车驾驶证后的12个月为实习期。在实习期内驾驶机动车的，应当在车身后部粘贴或者悬挂统一式样的实习标志。  
机动车驾驶人在实习期内不得驾驶公共汽车、营运客车或者执行任务的警车、消防车、救护车、工程救险车以及载有爆炸物品、易燃易爆化学物品、剧毒或者放射性等危险物品的机动车；驾驶的机动车不得牵引挂车。

**Article 22** The validity period of a motor vehicle driving license is six years, unless otherwise provided in these Regulations.  
The 12 months following the initial application for a motor vehicle driving license by a motor vehicle driver shall be the probationary period. A motor vehicle driver who drives a motor vehicle during the probationary period shall affix or hang a probationary sign of uniform pattern at the rear of the vehicle.  
A motor vehicle driver shall not, during the probationary period, drive a bus, a commercial passenger vehicle, or a police vehicle, fire engine, ambulance, or engineering rescue vehicle on duty, or a motor vehicle carrying dangerous articles such as explosive items, flammable and explosive chemicals, highly toxic substances, or radioactive materials; the motor vehicle driven shall not tow a trailer.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　公安机关交通管理部门对机动车驾驶人的道路交通安全违法行为除给予行政处罚外，实行道路交通安全违法行为累积记分(以下简称记分)制度，记分周期为12个月。对在一个记分周期内记分达到12分的，由公安机关交通管理部门扣留其机动车驾驶证，该机动车驾驶人应当按照规定参加道路交通安全法律、法规的学习并接受考试。考试合格的，记分予以清除，发还机动车驾驶证；考试不合格的，继续参加学习和考试。  
应当给予记分的道路交通安全违法行为及其分值，由国务院公安部门根据道路交通安全违法行为的危害程度规定。  
公安机关交通管理部门应当提供记分查询方式供机动车驾驶人查询。

**Article 23** In addition to imposing administrative penalties for road traffic safety violations committed by motor vehicle drivers, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall implement a system of cumulative demerit points (hereinafter referred to as demerit points) for road traffic safety violations, with a demerit point cycle of 12 months. For a driver who accumulates demerit points up to 12 within one demerit point cycle, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall impound his/her motor vehicle driving license, and the motor vehicle driver shall participate in the study of road traffic safety laws and regulations and take an examination in accordance with the provisions. If the examination is passed, the demerit points shall be cleared and the motor vehicle driving license shall be returned; if the examination is failed, the driver shall continue to study and take the examination.  
The road traffic safety violations that shall be subject to demerit points and the demerit point values thereof shall be prescribed by the public security department of the State Council based on the degree of harm of the road traffic safety violations.  
The traffic management department of the public security organ shall provide a method for inquiring about demerit points for motor vehicle drivers to consult.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　机动车驾驶人在一个记分周期内记分未达到12分，所处罚款已经缴纳的，记分予以清除；记分虽未达到12分，但尚有罚款未缴纳的，记分转入下一记分周期。  
机动车驾驶人在一个记分周期内记分2次以上达到12分的，除按照第二十三条的规定扣留机动车驾驶证、参加学习、接受考试外，还应当接受驾驶技能考试。考试合格的，记分予以清除，发还机动车驾驶证；考试不合格的，继续参加学习和考试。  
接受驾驶技能考试的，按照本人机动车驾驶证载明的最高准驾车型考试。

**Article 24** Where a motor vehicle driver has not reached 12 demerit points within a demerit point cycle and the fine imposed has been paid, the demerit points shall be cleared; where the demerit points have not reached 12 but there are still unpaid fines, the demerit points shall be carried over to the next demerit point cycle.  
Where a motor vehicle driver has reached 12 demerit points two or more times within one demerit point cycle, in addition to impounding the motor vehicle driving license, participating in study, and taking the examination as provided in Article 23, he/she shall also take a driving skill test. If the test is passed, the demerit points shall be cleared and the motor vehicle driving license shall be returned; if the test is failed, the driver shall continue to study and take the examination.  
When taking a driving skill test, the driver shall be tested according to the highest permitted driving type indicated on his/her motor vehicle driving license.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　机动车驾驶人记分达到12分，拒不参加公安机关交通管理部门通知的学习，也不接受考试的，由公安机关交通管理部门公告其机动车驾驶证停止使用。

**Article 25** Where a motor vehicle driver whose demerit points reach 12 refuses to participate in the study notified by the traffic management department of the public security organ and also refuses to take the examination, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall announce that the motor vehicle driving license is suspended from use.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　机动车驾驶人在机动车驾驶证的6年有效期内，每个记分周期均未达到12分的，换发10年有效期的机动车驾驶证；在机动车驾驶证的10年有效期内，每个记分周期均未达到12分的，换发长期有效的机动车驾驶证。  
换发机动车驾驶证时，公安机关交通管理部门应当对机动车驾驶证进行审验。

**Article 26** Where a motor vehicle driver has not reached 12 demerit points in any demerit point cycle within the six-year validity period of the motor vehicle driving license, a motor vehicle driving license with a validity period of ten years shall be issued upon renewal; where the driver has not reached 12 demerit points in any demerit point cycle within the ten-year validity period, a motor vehicle driving license with indefinite validity shall be issued upon renewal.  
When renewing a motor vehicle driving license, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall inspect the motor vehicle driving license.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　机动车驾驶证丢失、损毁，机动车驾驶人申请补发的，应当向公安机关交通管理部门提交本人身份证明和申请材料。公安机关交通管理部门经与机动车驾驶证档案核实后，在收到申请之日起3日内补发。

**Article 27** Where a motor vehicle driving license is lost or damaged, and the motor vehicle driver applies for a replacement, he/she shall submit his/her identity certificate and application materials to the traffic management department of the public security organ. After verifying with the motor vehicle driving license archives, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall issue a replacement within three days from the date of receiving the application.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　机动车驾驶人在机动车驾驶证丢失、损毁、超过有效期或者被依法扣留、暂扣期间以及记分达到12分的，不得驾驶机动车。

**Article 28** A motor vehicle driver shall not drive a motor vehicle when the motor vehicle driving license is lost, damaged, expired, or impounded or temporarily seized according to law, or when the driver's demerit points have reached 12.

### 第三章　道路通行条件

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　交通信号灯分为：机动车信号灯、非机动车信号灯、人行横道信号灯、车道信号灯、方向指示信号灯、闪光警告信号灯、道路与铁路平面交叉道口信号灯。

**Article 29** Traffic signal lights are classified into: motor vehicle signal lights, non-motor vehicle signal lights, pedestrian crosswalk signal lights, lane signal lights, directional indicator signal lights, flashing warning signal lights, and signal lights at level crossings of roads and railways.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　交通标志分为：指示标志、警告标志、禁令标志、指路标志、旅游区标志、道路施工安全标志和辅助标志。  
道路交通标线分为：指示标线、警告标线、禁止标线。

**Article 30** Traffic signs are classified into: directional signs, warning signs, prohibitory signs, guide signs, tourist area signs, road construction safety signs, and auxiliary signs.  
Road traffic markings are classified into: directional markings, warning markings, and prohibitory markings.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　交通警察的指挥分为：手势信号和使用器具的交通指挥信号。

**Article 31** The directions of traffic police shall be divided into: hand signal and traffic direction signal using instruments.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　道路交叉路口和行人横过道路较为集中的路段应当设置人行横道、过街天桥或者过街地下通道。  
在盲人通行较为集中的路段，人行横道信号灯应当设置声响提示装置。

**Article 32** At road intersections and sections where pedestrians cross the road in a relatively concentrated manner, pedestrian crosswalks, footbridges or pedestrian underpasses shall be installed.  
On sections where blind persons travel in a relatively concentrated manner, pedestrian crosswalk signal lights shall be equipped with audible warning devices.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　城市人民政府有关部门可以在不影响行人、车辆通行的情况下，在城市道路上施划停车泊位，并规定停车泊位的使用时间。

**Article 33** The relevant departments of the people's government of a city may, provided that the passage of pedestrians and vehicles is not affected, mark parking spaces on urban roads and prescribe the use hours of the parking spaces.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　开辟或者调整公共汽车、长途汽车的行驶路线或者车站，应当符合交通规划和安全、畅通的要求。

**Article 34** The opening or adjustment of bus or long-distance bus routes or stops shall comply with the requirements of traffic planning, safety, and smooth traffic flow.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　道路养护施工单位在道路上进行养护、维修时，应当按照规定设置规范的安全警示标志和安全防护设施。道路养护施工作业车辆、机械应当安装示警灯，喷涂明显的标志图案，作业时应当开启示警灯和危险报警闪光灯。对未中断交通的施工作业道路，公安机关交通管理部门应当加强交通安全监督检查。发生交通阻塞时，及时做好分流、疏导，维护交通秩序。  
道路施工需要车辆绕行的，施工单位应当在绕行处设置标志；不能绕行的，应当修建临时通道，保证车辆和行人通行。需要封闭道路中断交通的，除紧急情况外，应当提前5日向社会公告。

**Article 35** When a road maintenance construction unit carries out maintenance or repair on a road, it shall, in accordance with the regulations, set up standardized safety warning signs and safety protection facilities. Road maintenance construction operation vehicles and machinery shall be installed with warning lights and painted with conspicuous marking patterns, and during operations, warning lights and hazard warning flashers shall be turned on. For construction operation roads where traffic is not interrupted, the public security organ's traffic management department shall strengthen traffic safety supervision and inspection. In the event of traffic congestion, it shall timely carry out diversion and guidance to maintain traffic order.  
If road construction requires vehicles to detour, the construction unit shall set up signs at the detour point; if detour is not possible, a temporary passage shall be constructed to ensure the passage of vehicles and pedestrians. Where road closure to interrupt traffic is necessary, except in an emergency, an announcement shall be made to the public five days in advance.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　道路或者交通设施养护部门、管理部门应当在急弯、陡坡、临崖、临水等危险路段，按照国家标准设置警告标志和安全防护设施。

**Article 36** The road or traffic facility maintenance department and management department shall, on dangerous sections such as sharp curves, steep slopes, sections adjacent to cliffs, or sections adjacent to water, set up warning signs and safety protection facilities in accordance with national standards.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　道路交通标志、标线不规范，机动车驾驶人容易发生辨认错误的，交通标志、标线的主管部门应当及时予以改善。  
道路照明设施应当符合道路建设技术规范，保持照明功能完好。

**Article 37** Where road traffic signs or markings are not standardized, making it easy for motor vehicle drivers to make recognition errors, the competent department in charge of traffic signs and markings shall timely make improvements.  
Road lighting facilities shall comply with road construction technical specifications and maintain intact lighting functions.

### 第四章　道路通行规定

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　机动车信号灯和非机动车信号灯表示：  
(一)绿灯亮时，准许车辆通行，但转弯的车辆不得妨碍被放行的直行车辆、行人通行；  
(二)黄灯亮时，已越过停止线的车辆可以继续通行；  
(三)红灯亮时，禁止车辆通行。  
在未设置非机动车信号灯和人行横道信号灯的路口，非机动车和行人应当按照机动车信号灯的表示通行。  
红灯亮时，右转弯的车辆在不妨碍被放行的车辆、行人通行的情况下，可以通行。

**Article 38** The meanings of motor vehicle signal lights and non-motor vehicle signal lights are as follows:  
(1) When the green light is illuminated, vehicles are permitted to pass, but turning vehicles shall not impede the passage of straight-moving vehicles and pedestrians that have been given the right of way;  
(2) When the yellow light is illuminated, vehicles that have already crossed the stop line may continue to pass;  
(3) When the red light is illuminated, vehicles are prohibited from passing.  
At intersections where no non-motor vehicle signal lights or pedestrian crosswalk signal lights are installed, non-motor vehicles and pedestrians shall pass in accordance with the indications of the motor vehicle signal lights.  
When the red light is illuminated, a vehicle making a right turn may pass provided that it does not impede the passage of vehicles and pedestrians that have been given the right of way.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　人行横道信号灯表示：  
(一)绿灯亮时，准许行人通过人行横道；  
(二)红灯亮时，禁止行人进入人行横道，但是已经进入人行横道的，可以继续通过或者在道路中心线处停留等候。

**Article 39** The meanings of pedestrian crosswalk signal lights are as follows:  
(1) When the green light is illuminated, pedestrians are permitted to cross the pedestrian crosswalk;  
(2) When the red light is illuminated, pedestrians are prohibited from entering the pedestrian crosswalk, but those who have already entered the crosswalk may continue to cross or stop and wait at the center line of the road.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　车道信号灯表示：  
(一)绿色箭头灯亮时，准许本车道车辆按指示方向通行；  
(二)红色叉形灯或者箭头灯亮时，禁止本车道车辆通行。

**Article 40** The meanings of lane signal lights are as follows:  
(1) When the green arrow light is illuminated, vehicles in this lane are permitted to pass in the indicated direction;  
(2) When the red cross light or arrow light is illuminated, vehicles in this lane are prohibited from passing.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　方向指示信号灯的箭头方向向左、向上、向右分别表示左转、直行、右转。

**Article 41** The arrow directions of the direction indication signal lights, pointing left, upward, and right, indicate turning left, going straight, and turning right respectively.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　闪光警告信号灯为持续闪烁的黄灯，提示车辆、行人通行时注意瞭望，确认安全后通过。

**Article 42** The flashing warning signal light is a continuously flashing yellow light, reminding vehicles and pedestrians to watch carefully when passing and to pass only after confirming safety.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　道路与铁路平面交叉道口有两个红灯交替闪烁或者一个红灯亮时，表示禁止车辆、行人通行；红灯熄灭时，表示允许车辆、行人通行。

**Article 43** At a level crossing of a road and a railway, when two red lights flash alternately or one red light is on, it indicates that vehicles and pedestrians are prohibited from passing; when the red light is off, it indicates that vehicles and pedestrians are allowed to pass.

#### 第二节　机动车通行规定

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　在道路同方向划有2条以上机动车道的，左侧为快速车道，右侧为慢速车道。在快速车道行驶的机动车应当按照快速车道规定的速度行驶，未达到快速车道规定的行驶速度的，应当在慢速车道行驶。摩托车应当在最右侧车道行驶。有交通标志标明行驶速度的，按照标明的行驶速度行驶。慢速车道内的机动车超越前车时，可以借用快速车道行驶。  
在道路同方向划有2条以上机动车道的，变更车道的机动车不得影响相关车道内行驶的机动车的正常行驶。

**Article 44** Where there are two or more motor vehicle lanes marked in the same direction on a road, the left side shall be the fast lane and the right side shall be the slow lane. A motor vehicle traveling in the fast lane shall drive at the speed specified for the fast lane; if it does not reach the specified speed for the fast lane, it shall travel in the slow lane. Motorcycles shall travel in the rightmost lane. Where there are traffic signs indicating the speed, vehicles shall drive at the indicated speed. When a motor vehicle in the slow lane overtakes the vehicle ahead, it may use the fast lane for overtaking.  
Where there are two or more motor vehicle lanes marked in the same direction on a road, a motor vehicle changing lanes shall not affect the normal driving of motor vehicles in the relevant lanes.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　机动车在道路上行驶不得超过限速标志、标线标明的速度。在没有限速标志、标线的道路上，机动车不得超过下列最高行驶速度：  
(一)没有道路中心线的道路，城市道路为每小时30公里，公路为每小时40公里；  
(二)同方向只有1条机动车道的道路，城市道路为每小时50公里，公路为每小时70公里。

**Article 45** A motor vehicle traveling on a road shall not exceed the speed indicated by speed limit signs or markings. On a road without speed limit signs or markings, a motor vehicle shall not exceed the following maximum speeds:  
(1) On a road without a central dividing line, 30 kilometers per hour on urban roads and 40 kilometers per hour on highways;  
(2) On a road with only one motor vehicle lane in the same direction, 50 kilometers per hour on urban roads and 70 kilometers per hour on highways.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　机动车行驶中遇有下列情形之一的，最高行驶速度不得超过每小时30公里，其中拖拉机、电瓶车、轮式专用机械车不得超过每小时15公里：  
(一)进出非机动车道，通过铁路道口、急弯路、窄路、窄桥时；  
(二)掉头、转弯、下陡坡时；  
(三)遇雾、雨、雪、沙尘、冰雹，能见度在50米以内时；  
(四)在冰雪、泥泞的道路上行驶时；  
(五)牵引发生故障的机动车时。

**Article 46** When a motor vehicle is driving under any of the following circumstances, the maximum speed shall not exceed 30 kilometers per hour, and for tractors, battery cars, and wheeled special machinery vehicles, it shall not exceed 15 kilometers per hour:  
(1) When entering or leaving a non-motor vehicle lane, passing a railway crossing, a sharp bend, a narrow road, or a narrow bridge;  
(2) When making a U-turn, turning, or going down a steep slope;  
(3) When encountering fog, rain, snow, sandstorm, or hail with visibility within 50 meters;  
(4) When driving on icy or muddy roads;  
(5) When towing a motor vehicle that has broken down.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　机动车超车时，应当提前开启左转向灯、变换使用远、近光灯或者鸣喇叭。在没有道路中心线或者同方向只有1条机动车道的道路上，前车遇后车发出超车信号时，在条件许可的情况下，应当降低速度、靠右让路。后车应当在确认有充足的安全距离后，从前车的左侧超越，在与被超车辆拉开必要的安全距离后，开启右转向灯，驶回原车道。

**Article 47** When overtaking, a motor vehicle shall, in advance, turn on the left turn signal, alternate between high and low beam headlights, or sound the horn. On a road without a central dividing line or with only one motor vehicle lane in the same direction, when the vehicle ahead receives an overtaking signal from the vehicle behind, it shall, where conditions permit, reduce speed and move to the right to give way. The overtaking vehicle shall, after confirming that there is sufficient safe distance, overtake from the left side of the vehicle ahead, and after creating the necessary safe distance from the overtaken vehicle, turn on the right turn signal and return to the original lane.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　在没有中心隔离设施或者没有中心线的道路上，机动车遇相对方向来车时应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)减速靠右行驶，并与其他车辆、行人保持必要的安全距离；  
(二)在有障碍的路段，无障碍的一方先行；但有障碍的一方已驶入障碍路段而无障碍的一方未驶入时，有障碍的一方先行；  
(三)在狭窄的坡路，上坡的一方先行；但下坡的一方已行至中途而上坡的一方未上坡时，下坡的一方先行；  
(四)在狭窄的山路，不靠山体的一方先行；  
(五)夜间会车应当在距相对方向来车150米以外改用近光灯，在窄路、窄桥与非机动车会车时应当使用近光灯。

**Article 48** On a road without a central isolation facility or a central line, when a motor vehicle meets an oncoming vehicle, it shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) Slow down and keep to the right, and maintain the necessary safe distance from other vehicles and pedestrians;  
(2) On a section with an obstacle, the side without the obstacle shall go first; but if the side with the obstacle has already entered the obstructed section and the side without the obstacle has not yet entered, the side with the obstacle shall go first;  
(3) On a narrow slope, the vehicle going uphill shall go first; but if the vehicle going downhill has already reached the middle and the vehicle going uphill has not yet started climbing, the vehicle going downhill shall go first;  
(4) On a narrow mountain road, the vehicle not on the mountainside shall go first;  
(5) When meeting at night, low beam headlights shall be used when the distance from the oncoming vehicle is 150 meters or more; when meeting non-motor vehicles on narrow roads or narrow bridges, low beam headlights shall be used.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　机动车在有禁止掉头或者禁止左转弯标志、标线的地点以及在铁路道口、人行横道、桥梁、急弯、陡坡、隧道或者容易发生危险的路段，不得掉头。  
机动车在没有禁止掉头或者没有禁止左转弯标志、标线的地点可以掉头，但不得妨碍正常行驶的其他车辆和行人的通行。

**Article 49** A motor vehicle shall not make a U-turn at a location with signs or markings prohibiting U-turns or left turns, nor at railway crossings, pedestrian crosswalks, bridges, sharp bends, steep slopes, tunnels, or sections prone to danger.  
A motor vehicle may make a U-turn at a location without signs or markings prohibiting U-turns or left turns, provided that it does not obstruct the passage of other vehicles or pedestrians that are normally passing.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　机动车倒车时，应当察明车后情况，确认安全后倒车。不得在铁路道口、交叉路口、单行路、桥梁、急弯、陡坡或者隧道中倒车。

**Article 50** When reversing, a motor vehicle shall check the situation behind the vehicle and reverse only after confirming safety. It shall not reverse at railway crossings, intersections, one-way roads, bridges, sharp bends, steep slopes, or tunnels.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　机动车通过有交通信号灯控制的交叉路口，应当按照下列规定通行：  
(一)在划有导向车道的路口，按所需行进方向驶入导向车道；  
(二)准备进入环形路口的让已在路口内的机动车先行；  
(三)向左转弯时，靠路口中心点左侧转弯。转弯时开启转向灯，夜间行驶开启近光灯；  
(四)遇放行信号时，依次通过；  
(五)遇停止信号时，依次停在停止线以外。没有停止线的，停在路口以外；  
(六)向右转弯遇有同车道前车正在等候放行信号时，依次停车等候；  
(七)在没有方向指示信号灯的交叉路口，转弯的机动车让直行的车辆、行人先行。相对方向行驶的右转弯机动车让左转弯车辆先行。

**Article 51** When a motor vehicle passes through an intersection controlled by traffic signal lights, it shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) At an intersection with designated lanes, enter the designated lane according to the intended direction of travel;  
(2) When preparing to enter a roundabout, yield to motor vehicles already within the roundabout;  
(3) When making a left turn, turn on the left side of the center point of the intersection. During a turn, shall use the turn signal, and when driving at night, use the low beam headlights;  
(4) When encountering a proceed signal, pass in sequence;  
(5) When encountering a stop signal, stop sequentially beyond the stop line. If there is no stop line, stop beyond the intersection;  
(6) When making a right turn and encountering a vehicle ahead in the same lane waiting for the proceed signal, stop and wait in sequence;  
(7) At an intersection without directional signal lights, a turning motor vehicle shall yield to straight-going vehicles and pedestrians. A right-turning motor vehicle traveling from the opposite direction shall yield to a left-turning vehicle.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　机动车通过没有交通信号灯控制也没有交通警察指挥的交叉路口，除应当遵守第五十一条第(二)项、第(三)项的规定外，还应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)有交通标志、标线控制的，让优先通行的一方先行；  
(二)没有交通标志、标线控制的，在进入路口前停车瞭望，让右方道路的来车先行；  
(三)转弯的机动车让直行的车辆先行；  
(四)相对方向行驶的右转弯的机动车让左转弯的车辆先行。

**Article 52** When a motor vehicle passes through an intersection that is neither controlled by traffic signal lights nor directed by a traffic police officer, in addition to complying with the provisions of Article 51, items (2) and (3), it shall also comply with the following provisions:  
(1) If controlled by traffic signs or markings, yield to the party with the right-of-way;  
(2) If not controlled by traffic signs or markings, stop and look before entering the intersection, and yield to vehicles approaching from the right-hand road;  
(3) A turning motor vehicle shall yield to straight-going vehicles;  
(4) A right-turning motor vehicle traveling from the opposite direction shall yield to a left-turning vehicle.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　机动车遇有前方交叉路口交通阻塞时，应当依次停在路口以外等候，不得进入路口。  
机动车在遇有前方机动车停车排队等候或者缓慢行驶时，应当依次排队，不得从前方车辆两侧穿插或者超越行驶，不得在人行横道、网状线区域内停车等候。  
机动车在车道减少的路口、路段，遇有前方机动车停车排队等候或者缓慢行驶的，应当每车道一辆依次交替驶入车道减少后的路口、路段。

**Article 53** When a motor vehicle encounters traffic congestion at an intersection ahead, it shall stop sequentially beyond the intersection and wait, and shall not enter the intersection.  
When a motor vehicle encounters queuing or slow-moving vehicles ahead, it shall queue up sequentially, and shall not cut in or overtake from both sides of the vehicle ahead, nor stop and wait within crosswalks or grid areas.  
At an intersection or section of road where lanes are reduced, if a motor vehicle encounters queuing or slow-moving vehicles ahead, it shall, one vehicle per lane, alternately merge into the reduced-lane intersection or section.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　机动车载物不得超过机动车行驶证上核定的载质量，装载长度、宽度不得超出车厢，并应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)重型、中型载货汽车，半挂车载物，高度从地面起不得超过4米，载运集装箱的车辆不得超过4.2米；  
(二)其他载货的机动车载物，高度从地面起不得超过2.5米；  
(三)摩托车载物，高度从地面起不得超过1.5米，长度不得超出车身0.2米。两轮摩托车载物宽度左右各不得超出车把0.15米；三轮摩托车载物宽度不得超过车身。  
载客汽车除车身外部的行李架和内置的行李箱外，不得载货。载客汽车行李架载货，从车顶起高度不得超过0.5米，从地面起高度不得超过4米。

**Article 54** The load carried by a motor vehicle shall not exceed the load capacity specified in the motor vehicle's driving permit, and the length and width of the load shall not extend beyond the body, and shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) For heavy and medium trucks and semi-trailers, the height of the load from the ground shall not exceed 4 meters; for vehicles carrying containers, it shall not exceed 4.2 meters;  
(2) For other cargo motor vehicles, the height of the load from the ground shall not exceed 2.5 meters;  
(3) For motorcycles, the height of the load from the ground shall not exceed 1.5 meters, and the length shall not extend beyond the vehicle body by 0.2 meters. For two-wheeled motorcycles, the width of the load on each side shall not extend beyond the handlebars by 0.15 meters; for three-wheeled motorcycles, the width of the load shall not exceed the vehicle body.  
Passenger vehicles shall not carry cargo except for external roof racks and built-in trunks. Cargo carried on a roof rack of a passenger vehicle shall not exceed 0.5 meters in height from the roof, and the total height from the ground shall not exceed 4 meters.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　机动车载人应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)公路载客汽车不得超过核定的载客人数，但按照规定免票的儿童除外，在载客人数已满的情况下，按照规定免票的儿童不得超过核定载客人数的10%；  
(二)载货汽车车厢不得载客。在城市道路上，货运机动车在留有安全位置的情况下，车厢内可以附载临时作业人员1人至5人；载物高度超过车厢栏板时，货物上不得载人；  
(三)摩托车后座不得乘坐未满12周岁的未成年人，轻便摩托车不得载人。

**Article 55** A motor vehicle carrying passengers shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) A highway passenger vehicle shall not exceed the approved number of passengers, except for children who are exempt from fare according to regulations; when the approved number of passengers is full, the number of fare-exempt children shall not exceed 10% of the approved passenger capacity;  
(2) The cargo compartment of a truck shall not carry passengers. On urban roads, a cargo motor vehicle may, if there is a safe position, carry 1 to 5 temporary workers in the compartment; when the height of the load exceeds the side panels of the compartment, no person shall be carried on top of the load;  
(3) A motorcycle shall not carry a minor under the age of 12 on the rear seat, and a moped shall not carry any passengers.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　机动车牵引挂车应当符合下列规定：  
(一)载货汽车、半挂牵引车、拖拉机只允许牵引1辆挂车。挂车的灯光信号、制动、连接、安全防护等装置应当符合国家标准；  
(二)小型载客汽车只允许牵引旅居挂车或者总质量700千克以下的挂车。挂车不得载人；  
(三)载货汽车所牵引挂车的载质量不得超过载货汽车本身的载质量。  
大型、中型载客汽车，低速载货汽车，三轮汽车以及其他机动车不得牵引挂车。

**Article 56** Towing a trailer by a motor vehicle shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) A truck, semi-trailer tractor, or tractor may only tow one trailer. The lighting, braking, coupling, and safety protection devices of the trailer shall conform to national standards;  
(2) A small passenger vehicle may only tow a caravan trailer or a trailer with a gross mass of less than 700 kilograms. The trailer shall not carry passengers;  
(3) The load capacity of a trailer towed by a truck shall not exceed the load capacity of the truck itself.  
Large and medium-sized passenger vehicles, low-speed trucks, three-wheeled vehicles, and other motor vehicles shall not tow trailers.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　机动车应当按照下列规定使用转向灯：  
(一)向左转弯、向左变更车道、准备超车、驶离停车地点或者掉头时，应当提前开启左转向灯；  
(二)向右转弯、向右变更车道、超车完毕驶回原车道、靠路边停车时，应当提前开启右转向灯。

**Article 57** A motor vehicle shall use its turn signals in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) When turning left, changing lanes to the left, preparing to overtake, leaving a parking spot, or making a U-turn, it shall turn on the left turn signal in advance;  
(2) When turning right, changing lanes to the right, returning to the original lane after overtaking, or pulling over to the side of the road, it shall turn on the right turn signal in advance.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　机动车在夜间没有路灯、照明不良或者遇有雾、雨、雪、沙尘、冰雹等低能见度情况下行驶时，应当开启前照灯、示廓灯和后位灯，但同方向行驶的后车与前车近距离行驶时，不得使用远光灯。机动车雾天行驶应当开启雾灯和危险报警闪光灯。

**Article 58** When a motor vehicle is driving at night on a road without streetlights or with poor lighting, or in low-visibility conditions such as fog, rain, snow, sandstorms, or hail, it shall turn on its headlights, clearance lights, and tail lights; however, when a following vehicle is driving close to the vehicle ahead, it shall not use high beams. When driving in foggy weather, a motor vehicle shall turn on its fog lights and hazard warning flashers.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　机动车在夜间通过急弯、坡路、拱桥、人行横道或者没有交通信号灯控制的路口时，应当交替使用远近光灯示意。  
机动车驶近急弯、坡道顶端等影响安全视距的路段以及超车或者遇有紧急情况时，应当减速慢行，并鸣喇叭示意。

**Article 59** When a motor vehicle passes through a sharp curve, slope, arch bridge, crosswalk, or an intersection not controlled by traffic signal lights at night, it shall alternately use its high and low beams to signal.  
When approaching a sharp curve, the top of a slope, or any section affecting safe sight distance, or when overtaking or encountering an emergency, a motor vehicle shall reduce speed and sound its horn to signal.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　机动车在道路上发生故障或者发生交通事故，妨碍交通又难以移动的，应当按照规定开启危险报警闪光灯并在车后50米至100米处设置警告标志，夜间还应当同时开启示廓灯和后位灯。

**Article 60** If a motor vehicle breaks down or is involved in a traffic accident on the road, obstructing traffic and being difficult to move, it shall, in accordance with regulations, turn on its hazard warning flashers and place a warning sign 50 to 100 meters behind the vehicle. At night, it shall also turn on its clearance lights and tail lights.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　牵引故障机动车应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)被牵引的机动车除驾驶人外不得载人，不得拖带挂车；  
(二)被牵引的机动车宽度不得大于牵引机动车的宽度；  
(三)使用软连接牵引装置时，牵引车与被牵引车之间的距离应当大于4米小于10米；  
(四)对制动失效的被牵引车，应当使用硬连接牵引装置牵引；  
(五)牵引车和被牵引车均应当开启危险报警闪光灯。  
汽车吊车和轮式专用机械车不得牵引车辆。摩托车不得牵引车辆或者被其他车辆牵引。  
转向或者照明、信号装置失效的故障机动车，应当使用专用清障车拖曳。

**Article 61** The towing of a disabled motor vehicle shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) The towed motor vehicle shall not carry any person other than the driver, and shall not tow a trailer;  
(2) The width of the towed motor vehicle shall not be greater than the width of the towing vehicle;  
(3) When a flexible coupling device is used, the distance between the towing vehicle and the towed vehicle shall be more than 4 meters and less than 10 meters;  
(4) For a towed vehicle with brake failure, a rigid coupling device shall be used for towing;  
(5) Both the towing vehicle and the towed vehicle shall turn on their hazard warning flashers.  
A motor vehicle crane and a wheeled special-purpose mechanical vehicle shall not tow any vehicle. A motorcycle shall not tow any vehicle or be towed by any other vehicle.  
For a disabled motor vehicle whose steering or lighting or signal devices have failed, a special breakdown recovery vehicle shall be used for towing.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　驾驶机动车不得有下列行为：  
(一)在车门、车厢没有关好时行车；  
(二)在机动车驾驶室的前后窗范围内悬挂、放置妨碍驾驶人视线的物品；  
(三)拨打接听手持电话、观看电视等妨碍安全驾驶的行为；  
(四)下陡坡时熄火或者空挡滑行；  
(五)向道路上抛撒物品；  
(六)驾驶摩托车手离车把或者在车把上悬挂物品；  
(七)连续驾驶机动车超过4小时未停车休息或者停车休息时间少于20分钟；  
(八)在禁止鸣喇叭的区域或者路段鸣喇叭。

**Article 62** A person driving a motor vehicle shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) Driving when the doors or the cargo compartment are not properly closed;  
(2) Hanging or placing articles within the front or rear window range of the driver's cab that obstruct the driver's view;  
(3) Engaging in acts that impede safe driving, such as making or answering hand-held phone calls, or watching television;  
(4) Turning off the engine or coasting in neutral while descending a steep slope;  
(5) Throwing objects onto the road;  
(6) Riding a motorcycle with hands off the handlebars or hanging objects on the handlebars;  
(7) Driving a motor vehicle continuously for more than 4 hours without stopping for rest, or stopping for rest for less than 20 minutes;  
(8) Sounding the horn in an area or on a section of road where honking is prohibited.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　机动车在道路上临时停车，应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)在设有禁停标志、标线的路段，在机动车道与非机动车道、人行道之间设有隔离设施的路段以及人行横道、施工地段，不得停车；  
(二)交叉路口、铁路道口、急弯路、宽度不足4米的窄路、桥梁、陡坡、隧道以及距离上述地点50米以内的路段，不得停车；  
(三)公共汽车站、急救站、加油站、消防栓或者消防队(站)门前以及距离上述地点30米以内的路段，除使用上述设施的以外，不得停车；  
(四)车辆停稳前不得开车门和上下人员，开关车门不得妨碍其他车辆和行人通行；  
(五)路边停车应当紧靠道路右侧，机动车驾驶人不得离车，上下人员或者装卸物品后，立即驶离；  
(六)城市公共汽车不得在站点以外的路段停车上下乘客。

**Article 63** When a motor vehicle stops temporarily on the road, it shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) No parking is allowed on sections of road where no-stopping signs or markings are installed, on sections of road where separation facilities are installed between the motor vehicle lane and the non-motor vehicle lane or the sidewalk, as well as on pedestrian crossings and construction sections;  
(2) No parking is allowed at intersections, railway crossings, sharp bends, narrow roads with a width of less than 4 meters, bridges, steep slopes, tunnels, and within 50 meters of the aforesaid locations;  
(3) No parking is allowed in front of bus stops, emergency stations, gas stations, fire hydrants or fire stations, and within 30 meters of the aforesaid locations, except for vehicles using such facilities;  
(4) Doors shall not be opened and persons shall not board or alight until the vehicle has come to a complete stop; opening or closing doors shall not hinder the passage of other vehicles or pedestrians;  
(5) When parking at the roadside, the vehicle shall be kept as close as possible to the right side of the road; the driver of the motor vehicle shall not leave the vehicle and shall depart immediately after boarding or alighting of persons or loading or unloading of articles;  
(6) City buses shall not stop to pick up or drop off passengers on sections of road other than at stops.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　机动车行经漫水路或者漫水桥时，应当停车察明水情，确认安全后，低速通过。

**Article 64** When a motor vehicle passes through a flooded road or a flooded bridge, it shall stop to ascertain the water conditions, and after confirming safety, it shall pass at a low speed.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　机动车载运超限物品行经铁路道口的，应当按照当地铁路部门指定的铁路道口、时间通过。  
机动车行经渡口，应当服从渡口管理人员指挥，按照指定地点依次待渡。机动车上下渡船时，应当低速慢行。

**Article 65** When a motor vehicle carrying articles exceeding the prescribed limits passes through a railway crossing, it shall pass at the railway crossing and time designated by the local railway authority.  
When a motor vehicle passes through a ferry, it shall obey the directions of the ferry管理人员 and wait in turn at the designated place for crossing. When a motor vehicle boards or leaves a ferry, it shall proceed at a low speed.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　警车、消防车、救护车、工程救险车在执行紧急任务遇交通受阻时，可以断续使用警报器，并遵守下列规定：  
(一)不得在禁止使用警报器的区域或者路段使用警报器；  
(二)夜间在市区不得使用警报器；  
(三)列队行驶时，前车已经使用警报器的，后车不再使用警报器。

**Article 66** When police vehicles, fire engines, ambulances, and engineering rescue vehicles encounter traffic obstruction while on emergency missions, they may use their sirens intermittently and shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) Sirens shall not be used in areas or on sections of road where the use of sirens is prohibited;  
(2) Sirens shall not be used at night in urban areas;  
(3) When traveling in a convoy, if the vehicle ahead has already used its siren, the following vehicle shall not use its siren.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　在单位院内、居民居住区内，机动车应当低速行驶，避让行人；有限速标志的，按照限速标志行驶。

**Article 67** Within the courtyards of work units or within residential areas, motor vehicles shall drive at a low speed and yield to pedestrians; if there are speed limit signs, they shall drive in accordance with the speed limit signs.

#### 第三节　非机动车通行规定

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　非机动车通过有交通信号灯控制的交叉路口，应当按照下列规定通行：  
(一)转弯的非机动车让直行的车辆、行人优先通行；  
(二)遇有前方路口交通阻塞时，不得进入路口；  
(三)向左转弯时，靠路口中心点的右侧转弯；  
(四)遇有停止信号时，应当依次停在路口停止线以外。没有停止线的，停在路口以外；  
(五)向右转弯遇有同方向前车正在等候放行信号时，在本车道内能够转弯的，可以通行；不能转弯的，依次等候。

**Article 68** When a non-motor vehicle passes through an intersection controlled by traffic signal lights, it shall proceed in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) A non-motor vehicle making a turn shall give way to vehicles and pedestrians going straight;  
(2) When there is a traffic jam at the intersection ahead, it shall not enter the intersection;  
(3) When making a left turn, it shall turn to the right side of the center point of the intersection;  
(4) When encountering a stop signal, it shall stop in turn beyond the stop line at the intersection. If there is no stop line, it shall stop beyond the intersection;  
(5) When making a right turn and encountering a vehicle ahead in the same direction waiting for the green light, if it can turn within its lane, it may proceed; if it cannot turn, it shall wait in turn.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　非机动车通过没有交通信号灯控制也没有交通警察指挥的交叉路口，除应当遵守第六十八条第(一)项、第(二)项和第(三)项的规定外，还应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)有交通标志、标线控制的，让优先通行的一方先行；  
(二)没有交通标志、标线控制的，在路口外慢行或者停车瞭望，让右方道路的来车先行；  
(三)相对方向行驶的右转弯的非机动车让左转弯的车辆先行。

**Article 69** When a non-motor vehicle passes through an intersection that is neither controlled by traffic signal lights nor directed by a traffic police officer, in addition to complying with the provisions of Article 68, subparagraphs (1), (2) and (3), it shall also comply with the following provisions:  
(1) If there are traffic signs or markings controlling the intersection, it shall yield to the party that has the right of way;  
(2) If there are no traffic signs or markings controlling the intersection, it shall slow down outside the intersection or stop to look around, and yield to vehicles approaching from the right side of the road;  
(3) A non-motor vehicle making a right turn from the opposite direction shall yield to a vehicle making a left turn.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　驾驶自行车、电动自行车、三轮车在路段上横过机动车道，应当下车推行，有人行横道或者行人过街设施的，应当从人行横道或者行人过街设施通过；没有人行横道、没有行人过街设施或者不便使用行人过街设施的，在确认安全后直行通过。  
因非机动车道被占用无法在本车道内行驶的非机动车，可以在受阻的路段借用相邻的机动车道行驶，并在驶过被占用路段后迅速驶回非机动车道。机动车遇此情况应当减速让行。

**Article 70** When riding a bicycle, an electric bicycle, or a tricycle across a motor vehicle lane on a road section, the rider shall dismount and push the vehicle. If there is a pedestrian crosswalk or a pedestrian overpass or underpass, the rider shall use such facility to cross. If there is no pedestrian crosswalk, no pedestrian overpass or underpass, or it is inconvenient to use such facilities, the rider shall cross straight after confirming safety.  
Where a non-motor vehicle cannot travel within its own lane because the non-motor vehicle lane is occupied, it may temporarily use the adjacent motor vehicle lane on the obstructed section, and shall quickly return to the non-motor vehicle lane after passing the occupied section. Motor vehicles shall slow down and yield in such circumstances.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　非机动车载物，应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)自行车、电动自行车、残疾人机动轮椅车载物，高度从地面起不得超过1.5米，宽度左右各不得超出车把0.15米，长度前端不得超出车轮，后端不得超出车身0.3米；  
(二)三轮车、人力车载物，高度从地面起不得超过2米，宽度左右各不得超出车身0.2米，长度不得超出车身1米；  
(三)畜力车载物，高度从地面起不得超过2.5米，宽度左右各不得超出车身0.2米，长度前端不得超出车辕，后端不得超出车身1米。  
自行车载人的规定，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据当地实际情况制定。

**Article 71** Where a non-motor vehicle carries goods, it shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) For bicycles, electric bicycles, and motorized wheelchairs for the disabled, the height from the ground shall not exceed 1.5 meters, the width on each side shall not exceed 0.15 meters beyond the handlebars, the length in front shall not extend beyond the wheel, and the length behind shall not exceed 0.3 meters beyond the body of the vehicle;  
(2) For tricycles and rickshaws, the height from the ground shall not exceed 2 meters, the width on each side shall not exceed 0.2 meters beyond the body of the vehicle, and the length shall not exceed 1 meter beyond the body of the vehicle;  
(3) For animal-drawn carts, the height from the ground shall not exceed 2.5 meters, the width on each side shall not exceed 0.2 meters beyond the body of the vehicle, the length in front shall not extend beyond the shafts, and the length behind shall not exceed 1 meter beyond the body of the vehicle.  
Provisions on carrying passengers on bicycles shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government based on local conditions.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　在道路上驾驶自行车、三轮车、电动自行车、残疾人机动轮椅车应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)驾驶自行车、三轮车必须年满12周岁；  
(二)驾驶电动自行车和残疾人机动轮椅车必须年满16周岁；  
(三)不得醉酒驾驶；  
(四)转弯前应当减速慢行，伸手示意，不得突然猛拐，超越前车时不得妨碍被超越的车辆行驶；  
(五)不得牵引、攀扶车辆或者被其他车辆牵引，不得双手离把或者手中持物；  
(六)不得扶身并行、互相追逐或者曲折竞驶；  
(七)不得在道路上骑独轮自行车或者2人以上骑行的自行车；  
(八)非下肢残疾的人不得驾驶残疾人机动轮椅车；  
(九)自行车、三轮车不得加装动力装置；  
(十)不得在道路上学习驾驶非机动车。

**Article 72** Driving a bicycle, tricycle, electric bicycle, or motorized wheelchair for the disabled on the road shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) A person must be at least 12 years old to drive a bicycle or tricycle;  
(2) A person must be at least 16 years old to drive an electric bicycle or a motorized wheelchair for the disabled;  
(3) A person shall not drive while intoxicated;  
(4) Before turning, a person shall slow down, extend an arm to signal, and shall not make a sudden sharp turn; when overtaking a vehicle ahead, a person shall not obstruct the overtaken vehicle from moving;  
(5) A person shall not tow or cling to another vehicle, or be towed by another vehicle, and shall not take both hands off the handlebars or hold objects in hands;  
(6) A person shall not ride side by side with others while leaning on each other, chase one another, or race in a zigzag manner;  
(7) A person shall not ride a unicycle or a bicycle designed for two or more riders on the road;  
(8) A person without a lower limb disability shall not drive a motorized wheelchair for the disabled;  
(9) Bicycles and tricycles shall not be fitted with power devices;  
(10) A person shall not learn to drive a non-motor vehicle on the road.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　在道路上驾驭畜力车应当年满16周岁，并遵守下列规定：  
(一)不得醉酒驾驭；  
(二)不得并行，驾驭人不得离开车辆；  
(三)行经繁华路段、交叉路口、铁路道口、人行横道、急弯路、宽度不足4米的窄路或者窄桥、陡坡、隧道或者容易发生危险的路段，不得超车。驾驭两轮畜力车应当下车牵引牲畜；  
(四)不得使用未经驯服的牲畜驾车，随车幼畜须拴系；  
(五)停放车辆应当拉紧车闸，拴系牲畜。

**Article 73** A person driving an animal-drawn cart on the road shall be at least 16 years old and shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) A person shall not drive while intoxicated;  
(2) A person shall not drive side by side with another cart, and the driver shall not leave the vehicle;  
(3) When passing through busy sections, intersections, railway crossings, pedestrian crosswalks, sharp bends, narrow roads or narrow bridges with a width of less than 4 meters, steep slopes, tunnels, or sections prone to danger, a person shall not overtake. For a two-wheeled animal-drawn cart, the driver shall dismount and lead the animal;  
(4) A person shall not use untamed animals to drive the cart, and young animals accompanying the cart shall be tethered;  
(5) When parking the vehicle, a person shall tighten the brake and tether the animals.

#### 第四节　行人和乘车人通行规定

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　行人不得有下列行为：  
(一)在道路上使用滑板、旱冰鞋等滑行工具；  
(二)在车行道内坐卧、停留、嬉闹；  
(三)追车、抛物击车等妨碍道路交通安全的行为。

**Article 74** Pedestrians shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) Using skateboards, roller skates, or other sliding tools on the road;  
(2) Sitting, lying down, stopping, or playing in the carriageway;  
(3) Chasing vehicles, throwing objects at vehicles, or other acts that obstruct road traffic safety.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　行人横过机动车道，应当从行人过街设施通过；没有行人过街设施的，应当从人行横道通过；没有人行横道的，应当观察来往车辆的情况，确认安全后直行通过，不得在车辆临近时突然加速横穿或者中途倒退、折返。

**Article 75** When crossing a motor vehicle lane, a pedestrian shall use a pedestrian crossing facility; if there is no pedestrian crossing facility, the pedestrian shall use a crosswalk; if there is no crosswalk, the pedestrian shall observe the oncoming traffic, ensure safety, and then cross straight through, and shall not suddenly accelerate to cross when vehicles are approaching or retreat or turn back midway.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　行人列队在道路上通行，每横列不得超过2人，但在已经实行交通管制的路段不受限制。

**Article 76** When pedestrians walk on the road in a formation, each row shall not exceed two persons, except on sections where traffic control has been imposed.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　乘坐机动车应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)不得在机动车道上拦乘机动车；  
(二)在机动车道上不得从机动车左侧上下车；  
(三)开关车门不得妨碍其他车辆和行人通行；  
(四)机动车行驶中，不得干扰驾驶，不得将身体任何部分伸出车外，不得跳车；  
(五)乘坐两轮摩托车应当正向骑坐。

**Article 77** Passengers in a motor vehicle shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) A person shall not hail a motor vehicle in the motor vehicle lane;  
(2) A person shall not get on or off a motor vehicle from the left side in the motor vehicle lane;  
(3) When opening or closing the door, a person shall not obstruct other vehicles and pedestrians;  
(4) While the motor vehicle is moving, a person shall not interfere with driving, extend any part of the body outside the vehicle, or jump out of the vehicle;  
(5) When riding a two-wheeled motorcycle, a person shall sit astride facing forward.

#### 第五节　高速公路的特别规定

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　高速公路应当标明车道的行驶速度，最高车速不得超过每小时120公里，最低车速不得低于每小时60公里。  
在高速公路上行驶的小型载客汽车最高车速不得超过每小时120公里，其他机动车不得超过每小时100公里，摩托车不得超过每小时80公里。  
同方向有2条车道的，左侧车道的最低车速为每小时100公里；同方向有3条以上车道的，最左侧车道的最低车速为每小时110公里，中间车道的最低车速为每小时90公里。道路限速标志标明的车速与上述车道行驶车速的规定不一致的，按照道路限速标志标明的车速行驶。

**Article 78** On expressways, the driving speed for each lane shall be marked, with a maximum speed not exceeding 120 kilometers per hour and a minimum speed not less than 60 kilometers per hour.  
For small passenger vehicles traveling on expressways, the maximum speed shall not exceed 120 kilometers per hour; for other motor vehicles, the maximum speed shall not exceed 100 kilometers per hour; for motorcycles, the maximum speed shall not exceed 80 kilometers per hour.  
Where there are two lanes in the same direction, the minimum speed for the left lane shall be 100 kilometers per hour; where there are three or more lanes in the same direction, the minimum speed for the leftmost lane shall be 110 kilometers per hour, and the minimum speed for the middle lane shall be 90 kilometers per hour. If the speed indicated by road speed limit signs is inconsistent with the above provisions on lane driving speed, the speed indicated by the road speed limit signs shall be followed.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　机动车从匝道驶入高速公路，应当开启左转向灯，在不妨碍已在高速公路内的机动车正常行驶的情况下驶入车道。  
机动车驶离高速公路时，应当开启右转向灯，驶入减速车道，降低车速后驶离。

**Article 79** When a motor vehicle enters an expressway from a ramp, it shall turn on the left turn signal and merge into the lane without obstructing the normal driving of motor vehicles already on the expressway.  
When a motor vehicle exits an expressway, it shall turn on the right turn signal, enter the deceleration lane, reduce speed, and then exit.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　机动车在高速公路上行驶，车速超过每小时100公里时，应当与同车道前车保持100米以上的距离，车速低于每小时100公里时，与同车道前车距离可以适当缩短，但最小距离不得少于50米。

**Article 80** When a motor vehicle is traveling on an expressway at a speed exceeding 100 kilometers per hour, it shall maintain a distance of more than 100 meters from the vehicle ahead in the same lane; when the speed is lower than 100 kilometers per hour, the distance from the vehicle ahead in the same lane may be appropriately shortened, but the minimum distance shall not be less than 50 meters.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　机动车在高速公路上行驶，遇有雾、雨、雪、沙尘、冰雹等低能见度气象条件时，应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)能见度小于200米时，开启雾灯、近光灯、示廓灯和前后位灯，车速不得超过每小时60公里，与同车道前车保持100米以上的距离；  
(二)能见度小于100米时，开启雾灯、近光灯、示廓灯、前后位灯和危险报警闪光灯，车速不得超过每小时40公里，与同车道前车保持50米以上的距离；  
(三)能见度小于50米时，开启雾灯、近光灯、示廓灯、前后位灯和危险报警闪光灯，车速不得超过每小时20公里，并从最近的出口尽快驶离高速公路。  
遇有前款规定情形时，高速公路管理部门应当通过显示屏等方式发布速度限制、保持车距等提示信息。

**Article 81** When driving on an expressway, a motor vehicle shall, under low visibility weather conditions such as fog, rain, snow, sandstorm, hail, etc., comply with the following provisions:  
(1) When visibility is less than 200 meters, turn on fog lamps, low-beam headlamps, width lamps, and front and rear position lamps; the speed shall not exceed 60 kilometers per hour, and a distance of more than 100 meters from the vehicle ahead in the same lane shall be maintained;  
(2) When visibility is less than 100 meters, turn on fog lamps, low-beam headlamps, width lamps, front and rear position lamps, and hazard warning flashers; the speed shall not exceed 40 kilometers per hour, and a distance of more than 50 meters from the vehicle ahead in the same lane shall be maintained;  
(3) When visibility is less than 50 meters, turn on fog lamps, low-beam headlamps, width lamps, front and rear position lamps, and hazard warning flashers; the speed shall not exceed 20 kilometers per hour, and leave the expressway as soon as possible from the nearest exit.  
When encountering the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, the expressway management department shall issue prompt information, such as speed limits and maintaining safe distance, through display screens or other means.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　机动车在高速公路上行驶，不得有下列行为：  
(一)倒车、逆行、穿越中央分隔带掉头或者在车道内停车；  
(二)在匝道、加速车道或者减速车道上超车；  
(三)骑、轧车行道分界线或者在路肩上行驶；  
(四)非紧急情况时在应急车道行驶或者停车；  
(五)试车或者学习驾驶机动车。

**Article 82** When driving on an expressway, a motor vehicle shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) Reversing, driving in the opposite direction, making a U-turn across the central dividing strip, or parking within a lane;  
(2) Overtaking on a ramp, acceleration lane, or deceleration lane;  
(3) Riding or driving on lane demarcation lines or driving on the shoulder;  
(4) Driving or parking on the emergency lane in non-emergency situations;  
(5) Test-driving or learning to drive a motor vehicle.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　在高速公路上行驶的载货汽车车厢不得载人。两轮摩托车在高速公路行驶时不得载人。

**Article 83** The cargo compartment of a truck driving on an expressway shall not carry passengers. A two-wheeled motorcycle shall not carry passengers when driving on an expressway.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　机动车通过施工作业路段时，应当注意警示标志，减速行驶。

**Article 84** When passing through a construction work section, a motor vehicle shall pay attention to warning signs and drive at a reduced speed.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　城市快速路的道路交通安全管理，参照本节的规定执行。  
高速公路、城市快速路的道路交通安全管理工作，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公安机关交通管理部门可以指定设区的市人民政府公安机关交通管理部门或者相当于同级的公安机关交通管理部门承担。

**Article 85** The road traffic safety management of urban expressways shall be implemented with reference to the provisions of this Section.  
For the road traffic safety management of expressways and urban expressways, the traffic management department of the public security organ of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may designate the traffic management department of the public security organ of the people's government of a city divided into districts, or a traffic management department of the public security organ at an equivalent level, to undertake such work.

### 第五章　交通事故处理

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　机动车与机动车、机动车与非机动车在道路上发生未造成人身伤亡的交通事故，当事人对事实及成因无争议的，在记录交通事故的时间、地点、对方当事人的姓名和联系方式、机动车牌号、驾驶证号、保险凭证号、碰撞部位，并共同签名后，撤离现场，自行协商损害赔偿事宜。当事人对交通事故事实及成因有争议的，应当迅速报警。

**Article 86** Where a traffic accident occurs between motor vehicles or between a motor vehicle and a non-motor vehicle on the road, causing no personal injury, and the parties have no dispute over the facts and causes thereof, they shall, after recording the time, location, name and contact information of the opposing party, vehicle license plate number, driver's license number, insurance certificate number, and collision points, and jointly signing, leave the scene and negotiate on the matters of damage compensation by themselves. Where the parties have a dispute over the facts and causes of the traffic accident, they shall promptly report to the police.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　非机动车与非机动车或者行人在道路上发生交通事故，未造成人身伤亡，且基本事实及成因清楚的，当事人应当先撤离现场，再自行协商处理损害赔偿事宜。当事人对交通事故事实及成因有争议的，应当迅速报警。

**Article 87** Where a traffic accident occurs between non-motor vehicles or between a non-motor vehicle and a pedestrian on the road, causing no personal injury, and the basic facts and causes are clear, the parties shall first leave the scene, and then negotiate on the matters of damage compensation by themselves. Where the parties have a dispute over the facts and causes of the traffic accident, they shall promptly report to the police.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　机动车发生交通事故，造成道路、供电、通讯等设施损毁的，驾驶人应当报警等候处理，不得驶离。机动车可以移动的，应当将机动车移至不妨碍交通的地点。公安机关交通管理部门应当将事故有关情况通知有关部门。

**Article 88** Where a traffic accident involving a motor vehicle causes damage to facilities such as roads, power supply, or communications, the driver shall report to the police and wait for handling, and shall not drive away. If the motor vehicle can be moved, it shall be moved to a location that does not obstruct traffic. The traffic management department of the public security organ shall notify the relevant departments of the circumstances related to the accident.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　公安机关交通管理部门或者交通警察接到交通事故报警，应当及时赶赴现场，对未造成人身伤亡，事实清楚，并且机动车可以移动的，应当在记录事故情况后责令当事人撤离现场，恢复交通。对拒不撤离现场的，予以强制撤离。  
对属于前款规定情况的道路交通事故，交通警察可以适用简易程序处理，并当场出具事故认定书。当事人共同请求调解的，交通警察可以当场对损害赔偿争议进行调解。  
对道路交通事故造成人员伤亡和财产损失需要勘验、检查现场的，公安机关交通管理部门应当按照勘查现场工作规范进行。现场勘查完毕，应当组织清理现场，恢复交通。

**Article 89** Upon receiving a traffic accident report, the traffic management department of the public security organ or a traffic police officer shall promptly rush to the scene. For an accident causing no personal injury, with clear facts, and where the motor vehicle can be moved, they shall, after recording the accident circumstances, order the parties to leave the scene and restore traffic. For those who refuse to leave the scene, compulsory removal shall be carried out.  
For a road traffic accident falling under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, the traffic police officer may handle the case by using summary procedures and issue an accident confirmation certificate on the spot. If the parties jointly request mediation, the traffic police officer may conduct mediation on the damage compensation dispute on the spot.  
For a road traffic accident causing personal injury or death and property loss that requires inspection or examination of the scene, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall conduct it in accordance with the scene investigation work regulations. After the scene investigation is completed, it shall organize the cleaning of the scene and restore traffic.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　投保机动车第三者责任强制保险的机动车发生交通事故，因抢救受伤人员需要保险公司支付抢救费用的，由公安机关交通管理部门通知保险公司。  
抢救受伤人员需要道路交通事故救助基金垫付费用的，由公安机关交通管理部门通知道路交通事故社会救助基金管理机构。

**Article 90** Where a traffic accident occurs involving a motor vehicle covered by compulsory third-party liability insurance, and the insurance company needs to pay rescue expenses for rescuing the injured, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall notify the insurance company.  
Where the rescue of the injured requires advance payment from the road traffic accident relief fund, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall notify the management institution of the road traffic accident social relief fund.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　公安机关交通管理部门应当根据交通事故当事人的行为对发生交通事故所起的作用以及过错的严重程度，确定当事人的责任。

**Article 91** The traffic management department of the public security organ shall, based on the role of the conduct of the parties to a traffic accident in causing the traffic accident and the severity of their fault, determine the liability of the parties.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　发生交通事故后当事人逃逸的，逃逸的当事人承担全部责任。但是，有证据证明对方当事人也有过错的，可以减轻责任。  
当事人故意破坏、伪造现场、毁灭证据的，承担全部责任。

**Article 92** Where a party flees after causing a traffic accident, the fleeing party shall bear full liability. However, if there is evidence proving that the other party was also at fault, the liability may be mitigated.  
Where a party intentionally damages the scene, falsifies the scene, or destroys evidence, the party shall bear full liability.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　公安机关交通管理部门对经过勘验、检查现场的交通事故应当在勘查现场之日起10日内制作交通事故认定书。对需要进行检验、鉴定的，应当在检验、鉴定结果确定之日起5日内制作交通事故认定书。

**Article 93** The traffic management department of the public security organ shall, for a traffic accident that has undergone inspection and examination of the scene, produce a traffic accident confirmation document within 10 days from the date of investigating the scene. Where inspection and expert evaluation are required, the traffic accident confirmation document shall be produced within 5 days from the date when the results of the inspection and expert evaluation are confirmed.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　当事人对交通事故损害赔偿有争议，各方当事人一致请求公安机关交通管理部门调解的，应当在收到交通事故认定书之日起10日内提出书面调解申请。  
对交通事故致死的，调解从办理丧葬事宜结束之日起开始；对交通事故致伤的，调解从治疗终结或者定残之日起开始；对交通事故造成财产损失的，调解从确定损失之日起开始。

**Article 94** Where a party has a dispute over compensation for traffic accident damages, and all parties unanimously request mediation by the traffic management department of the public security organ, they shall submit a written mediation application within 10 days from the date of receiving the traffic accident confirmation document.  
For a traffic accident causing death, mediation shall commence from the date when funeral arrangements are completed; for a traffic accident causing injury, mediation shall commence from the date when treatment is concluded or disability is determined; for a traffic accident causing property damage, mediation shall commence from the date when the loss is determined.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　公安机关交通管理部门调解交通事故损害赔偿争议的期限为10日。调解达成协议的，公安机关交通管理部门应当制作调解书送交各方当事人，调解书经各方当事人共同签字后生效；调解未达成协议的，公安机关交通管理部门应当制作调解终结书送交各方当事人。  
交通事故损害赔偿项目和标准依照有关法律的规定执行。

**Article 95** The time limit for the traffic management department of the public security organ to mediate disputes over compensation for traffic accident damages is 10 days. If an agreement is reached through mediation, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall produce a mediation document and deliver it to all parties, and the mediation document shall take effect upon being signed by all parties jointly; if no agreement is reached through mediation, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall produce a mediation termination document and deliver it to all parties.  
The items and standards for compensation for traffic accident damages shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　对交通事故损害赔偿的争议，当事人向人民法院提起民事诉讼的，公安机关交通管理部门不再受理调解申请。  
公安机关交通管理部门调解期间，当事人向人民法院提起民事诉讼的，调解终止。

**Article 96** Where a party initiates a civil action in a People's Court regarding a dispute over compensation for traffic accident damages, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall no longer accept the mediation application.  
Where, during the mediation by the traffic management department of the public security organ, a party initiates a civil action in a People's Court, the mediation shall terminate.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　车辆在道路以外发生交通事故，公安机关交通管理部门接到报案的，参照道路交通安全法和本条例的规定处理。  
车辆、行人与火车发生的交通事故以及在渡口发生的交通事故，依照国家有关规定处理。

**Article 97** Where a traffic accident occurs outside the road and the traffic management department of the public security organ receives a report, it shall handle the matter by reference to the provisions of the Road Traffic Safety Law and these Regulations.  
Traffic accidents involving vehicles or pedestrians and trains, as well as traffic accidents occurring at ferries, shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第六章　执法监督

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　公安机关交通管理部门应当公开办事制度、办事程序，建立警风警纪监督员制度，自觉接受社会和群众的监督。

**Article 98** The traffic management department of the public security organ shall make public its work systems and procedures, establish a system of supervisors for police conduct and discipline, and consciously accept supervision by society and the public.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　公安机关交通管理部门及其交通警察办理机动车登记，发放号牌，对驾驶人考试、发证，处理道路交通安全违法行为，处理道路交通事故，应当严格遵守有关规定，不得越权执法，不得延迟履行职责，不得擅自改变处罚的种类和幅度。

**Article 99** The traffic management department of the public security organ and its traffic police, when handling motor vehicle registration, issuing license plates, conducting driving tests and issuing driving licenses, handling road traffic safety violations, and handling road traffic accidents, shall strictly observe the relevant provisions, shall not exercise powers beyond their authority, shall not delay in performing their duties, and shall not arbitrarily change the types or ranges of penalties.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　公安机关交通管理部门应当公布举报电话，受理群众举报投诉，并及时调查核实，反馈查处结果。

**Article 100** The traffic management department of the public security organ shall publish a reporting hotline, accept reports and complaints from the public, and timely investigate and verify them, and provide feedback on the results of the investigation and handling.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　公安机关交通管理部门应当建立执法质量考核评议、执法责任制和执法过错追究制度，防止和纠正道路交通安全执法中的错误或者不当行为。

**Article 101** : The public security organ's traffic administrative department shall establish a system for performance quality assessment and evaluation, a law enforcement responsibility system, and a system for investigating law enforcement errors, in order to prevent and correct errors or improper actions in road traffic safety law enforcement.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　违反本条例规定的行为，依照道路交通安全法和本条例的规定处罚。

**Article 102** : Any act that violates the provisions of these Regulations shall be punished in accordance with the Road Traffic Safety Law and the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　以欺骗、贿赂等不正当手段取得机动车登记或者驾驶许可的，收缴机动车登记证书、号牌、行驶证或者机动车驾驶证，撤销机动车登记或者机动车驾驶许可；申请人在3年内不得申请机动车登记或者机动车驾驶许可。

**Article 103** : Whoever obtains motor vehicle registration or driving permit by deceit, bribery or other improper means shall have the motor vehicle registration certificate, number plate, driving permit or motor vehicle driver's license confiscated, and the motor vehicle registration or motor vehicle driving permit shall be revoked; the applicant shall not apply for motor vehicle registration or motor vehicle driving permit within three years.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　机动车驾驶人有下列行为之一，又无其他机动车驾驶人即时替代驾驶的，公安机关交通管理部门除依法给予处罚外，可以将其驾驶的机动车移至不妨碍交通的地点或者有关部门指定的地点停放：  
(一)不能出示本人有效驾驶证的；  
(二)驾驶的机动车与驾驶证载明的准驾车型不符的；  
(三)饮酒、服用国家管制的精神药品或者麻醉药品、患有妨碍安全驾驶的疾病，或者过度疲劳仍继续驾驶的；  
(四)学习驾驶人员没有教练人员随车指导单独驾驶的。

**Article 104** : If a motor vehicle driver commits any of the following acts and there is no other motor vehicle driver to immediately take over the driving, the public security organ's traffic administrative department may, in addition to imposing a penalty according to law, move the motor vehicle driven by the driver to a location that does not obstruct traffic or to a location designated by the relevant department for parking:  
(1) Failing to produce a valid driver's license;  
(2) Driving a motor vehicle that does not conform to the type of vehicle for which the driver's license permits driving;  
(3) Driving while under the influence of alcohol, taking psychotropic substances or narcotic drugs controlled by the state, suffering from a disease that impairs safe driving, or continuing to drive while excessively fatigued;  
(4) A learner driver driving alone without the guidance of a coach in the vehicle.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　机动车驾驶人有饮酒、醉酒、服用国家管制的精神药品或者麻醉药品嫌疑的，应当接受测试、检验。

**Article 105** : If a motor vehicle driver is suspected of having consumed alcohol, being intoxicated, or having taken psychotropic substances or narcotic drugs controlled by the state, he or she shall undergo testing and examination.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　公路客运载客汽车超过核定乘员、载货汽车超过核定载质量的，公安机关交通管理部门依法扣留机动车后，驾驶人应当将超载的乘车人转运、将超载的货物卸载，费用由超载机动车的驾驶人或者所有人承担。

**Article 106** : Where a highway passenger vehicle carries more passengers than the approved number, or a cargo vehicle exceeds the approved load mass, after the public security organ's traffic administrative department detains the motor vehicle according to law, the driver shall transfer the overloaded passengers or unload the overloaded cargo, and the costs shall be borne by the driver or owner of the overloaded motor vehicle.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　依照[道路交通安全法第九十二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国道路交通安全法.md#art-92)、第九十五条、第九十六条、第九十八条的规定被扣留的机动车，驾驶人或者所有人、管理人30日内没有提供被扣留机动车的合法证明，没有补办相应手续，或者不前来接受处理，经公安机关交通管理部门通知并且经公告3个月仍不前来接受处理的，由公安机关交通管理部门将该机动车送交有资格的拍卖机构拍卖，所得价款上缴国库；非法拼装的机动车予以拆除；达到报废标准的机动车予以报废；机动车涉及其他违法犯罪行为的，移交有关部门处理。

**Article 107** : If a motor vehicle is detained pursuant to Articles 92, 95, 96 and 98 of the Road Traffic Safety Law, and the driver or owner or manager fails to provide legal certificates for the detained motor vehicle, fails to complete the relevant procedures, or fails to come forward to accept handling within 30 days, and after notification by the public security organ's traffic administrative department and a public announcement for three months still fails to come forward to accept handling, the public security organ's traffic administrative department shall deliver the motor vehicle to a qualified auction institution for auction, and the proceeds shall be turned over to the state treasury; illegally assembled motor vehicles shall be dismantled; motor vehicles that have reached the scrap standard shall be scrapped; if the motor vehicle is involved in other illegal or criminal acts, it shall be transferred to the relevant department for handling.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　交通警察按照简易程序当场作出行政处罚的，应当告知当事人道路交通安全违法行为的事实、处罚的理由和依据，并将行政处罚决定书当场交付被处罚人。

**Article 108** : Where a traffic police officer imposes an administrative penalty on the spot in accordance with the summary procedure, he or she shall inform the party of the facts of the illegal road traffic safety act, the reasons and legal basis for the penalty, and shall deliver the written decision on administrative penalty to the person being penalized on the spot.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　对道路交通安全违法行为人处以罚款或者暂扣驾驶证处罚的，由违法行为发生地的县级以上人民政府公安机关交通管理部门或者相当于同级的公安机关交通管理部门作出决定；对处以吊销机动车驾驶证处罚的，由设区的市人民政府公安机关交通管理部门或者相当于同级的公安机关交通管理部门作出决定。  
公安机关交通管理部门对非本辖区机动车的道路交通安全违法行为没有当场处罚的，可以由机动车登记地的公安机关交通管理部门处罚。

**Article 109** : The decision to impose a fine or a suspension of the driver's license on a person who commits an illegal act against road traffic safety shall be made by the public security organ's traffic administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level in the place where the illegal act occurs, or by a public security organ's traffic administrative department of equivalent level. The decision to impose a revocation of the motor vehicle driver's license shall be made by the public security organ's traffic administrative department of the people's government of a city with districts, or by a public security organ's traffic administrative department of equivalent level.  
Where the public security organ's traffic administrative department does not impose an on-the-spot penalty on a motor vehicle not within its jurisdiction for an illegal act against road traffic safety, the penalty may be imposed by the public security organ's traffic administrative department in the place where the motor vehicle is registered.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　当事人对公安机关交通管理部门及其交通警察的处罚有权进行陈述和申辩，交通警察应当充分听取当事人的陈述和申辩，不得因当事人陈述、申辩而加重其处罚。

**Article 110** : A party shall have the right to make statements and defenses against the penalty imposed by the public security organ's traffic administrative department or its traffic police officers. The traffic police officer shall fully hear the party's statements and defenses and shall not increase the penalty because the party makes statements or defenses.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　本条例所称上道路行驶的拖拉机，是指手扶拖拉机等最高设计行驶速度不超过每小时20公里的轮式拖拉机和最高设计行驶速度不超过每小时40公里、牵引挂车方可从事道路运输的轮式拖拉机。

**Article 111** As used in these Regulations, tractors running on roads shall mean walking tractors and other wheeled tractors with a maximum design speed of no more than 20 kilometers per hour, and wheeled tractors with a maximum design speed of no more than 40 kilometers per hour that may engage in road transportation only when towing a trailer.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　农业(农业机械)主管部门应当定期向公安机关交通管理部门提供拖拉机登记、安全技术检验以及拖拉机驾驶证发放的资料、数据。公安机关交通管理部门对拖拉机驾驶人作出暂扣、吊销驾驶证处罚或者记分处理的，应当定期将处罚决定书和记分情况通报有关的农业(农业机械)主管部门。吊销驾驶证的，还应当将驾驶证送交有关的农业(农业机械)主管部门。

**Article 112** The competent department of agriculture (agricultural machinery) shall periodically provide to the traffic management department of the public security organ the materials and data on tractor registration, safety technical inspection, and issuance of tractor driving licenses. Where the traffic management department of the public security organ imposes a penalty of suspending or revoking the driving license on a tractor driver or administers demerit points, it shall periodically notify the relevant competent department of agriculture (agricultural machinery) of the decision on penalty and the demerit point situation. If the driving license is revoked, the license shall also be delivered to the relevant competent department of agriculture (agricultural machinery).

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　境外机动车入境行驶，应当向入境地的公安机关交通管理部门申请临时通行号牌、行驶证。临时通行号牌、行驶证应当根据行驶需要，载明有效日期和允许行驶的区域。  
入境的境外机动车申请临时通行号牌、行驶证以及境外人员申请机动车驾驶许可的条件、考试办法由国务院公安部门规定。

**Article 113** A motor vehicle from outside the territory that enters the territory for driving shall apply to the traffic management department of the public security organ at the entry place for a temporary passage plate and a driving permit. The temporary passage plate and driving permit shall, based on driving needs, specify the validity period and the area where driving is permitted.  
The conditions and examination methods for an imported motor vehicle from outside the territory to apply for a temporary passage plate and driving permit, and for a person from outside the territory to apply for a motor vehicle driving permit, shall be prescribed by the public security department of the State Council.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　机动车驾驶许可考试的收费标准，由国务院价格主管部门规定。

**Article 114** The fee standards for the motor vehicle driving license examination shall be prescribed by the competent department of pricing of the State Council.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　本条例自2004年5月1日起施行。1960年2月11日国务院批准、交通部发布的《机动车管理办法》，1988年3月9日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国道路交通管理条例》，1991年9月22日国务院发布的《道路交通事故处理办法》，同时废止。

**Article 115** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2004. The Measures for the Administration of Motor Vehicles approved by the State Council on February 11, 1960 and promulgated by the Ministry of Communications, the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Road Traffic Administration promulgated by the State Council on March 9, 1988, and the Measures for Handling Road Traffic Accidents promulgated by the State Council on September 22, 1991 shall be abolished simultaneously.
