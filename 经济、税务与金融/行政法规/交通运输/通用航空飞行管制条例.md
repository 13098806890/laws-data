# 通用航空飞行管制条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2003-01-10  
**生效日期**：2003-05-01

> (2003年1月10日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第371号公布　自2003年5月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了促进通用航空事业的发展，规范通用航空飞行活动，保证飞行安全，根据《中华人民共和国民用航空法》和《中华人民共和国飞行基本规则》，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事通用航空飞行活动，必须遵守本条例。 在中华人民共和国境内从事升放无人驾驶自由气球和系留气球活动，适用本条例的有关规定。 第三条　本条例所称通用航空，是指除军事、警务、海关缉私飞行和公共航空运输飞行以外的航空活动，包括从事工业、农业、林业、渔业、矿业、建筑业的作业飞行和医疗卫生、抢险救灾、气象探测、海洋监测、科学实验、遥感测绘、教育训练、文化体育、旅游观光等方面的飞行活动。 第四条　从事通用航空飞行活动的单位、个人，必须按照《中华人民共和国民用航空法》的规定取得从事通用航空活动的资格，并遵守国家有关法律、行政法规的规定。 第五条　飞行管制部门按照职责分工，负责对通用航空飞行活动实施管理，提供空中交通管制服务。相关飞行保障单位应当积极协调配合，做好有关服务保障工作，为通用航空飞行活动创造便利条件。 第二章　飞行空域的划设与使用 第六条　从事通用航空飞行活动的单位、个人使用机场飞行空域、航路、航线，应当按照国家有关规定向飞行管制部门提出申请，经批准后方可实施。 第七条　从事通用航空飞行活动的单位、个人，根据飞行活动要求，需要划设临时飞行空域的，应当向有关飞行管制部门提出划设临时飞行空域的申请。 划设临时飞行空域的申请应当包括下列内容： (一)临时飞行空域的水平范围、高度； (二)飞入和飞出临时飞行空域的方法； (三)使用临时飞行空域的时间；

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了促进通用航空事业的发展，规范通用航空飞行活动，保证飞行安全，根据《中华人民共和国民用航空法》和《中华人民共和国飞行基本规则》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Civil Aviation Law of the People's Republic of China and the Basic Rules for Flights of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of promoting the development of general aviation, regulating general aviation flight activities, and ensuring flight safety.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事通用航空飞行活动，必须遵守本条例。  
在中华人民共和国境内从事升放无人驾驶自由气球和系留气球活动，适用本条例的有关规定。

**Article 2** Any person engaging in general aviation flight activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China must comply with these Regulations.  
The relevant provisions of these Regulations shall apply to activities of releasing unmanned free balloons and tethered balloons within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称通用航空，是指除军事、警务、海关缉私飞行和公共航空运输飞行以外的航空活动，包括从事工业、农业、林业、渔业、矿业、建筑业的作业飞行和医疗卫生、抢险救灾、气象探测、海洋监测、科学实验、遥感测绘、教育训练、文化体育、旅游观光等方面的飞行活动。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Regulations, "general aviation" means aviation activities other than military, police, customs anti-smuggling flights and public air transport flights, including operational flights in industry, agriculture, forestry, fishery, mining, construction, and flights for medical and health care, emergency rescue and disaster relief, meteorological observation, marine monitoring, scientific experiments, remote sensing and mapping, education and training, culture and sports, tourism, etc.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　从事通用航空飞行活动的单位、个人，必须按照《中华人民共和国民用航空法》的规定取得从事通用航空活动的资格，并遵守国家有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 4** Any unit or individual engaging in general aviation flight activities must obtain the qualification for engaging in general aviation activities in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Aviation Law of the People's Republic of China, and shall comply with the provisions of relevant national laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　飞行管制部门按照职责分工，负责对通用航空飞行活动实施管理，提供空中交通管制服务。相关飞行保障单位应当积极协调配合，做好有关服务保障工作，为通用航空飞行活动创造便利条件。

**Article 5** The flight control departments shall, according to their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the management of general aviation flight activities and the provision of air traffic control services. Relevant flight support units shall actively coordinate and cooperate, perform relevant service and support work well, and create convenient conditions for general aviation flight activities.

### 第二章　飞行空域的划设与使用

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　从事通用航空飞行活动的单位、个人使用机场飞行空域、航路、航线，应当按照国家有关规定向飞行管制部门提出申请，经批准后方可实施。

**Article 6** Any unit or individual engaging in general aviation flight activities that intends to use airport flight airspace, air routes, or airways shall submit an application to the flight control department in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and may implement the flight only after obtaining approval.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　从事通用航空飞行活动的单位、个人，根据飞行活动要求，需要划设临时飞行空域的，应当向有关飞行管制部门提出划设临时飞行空域的申请。  
划设临时飞行空域的申请应当包括下列内容：  
(一)临时飞行空域的水平范围、高度；  
(二)飞入和飞出临时飞行空域的方法；  
(三)使用临时飞行空域的时间；  
(四)飞行活动性质；  
(五)其他有关事项。

**Article 7** Any unit or individual engaging in general aviation flight activities that needs to designate temporary flight airspace based on flight activity requirements shall submit an application for designating temporary flight airspace to the relevant flight control department.  
An application for designating temporary flight airspace shall include the following:  
(1) the horizontal range and altitude of the temporary flight airspace;  
(2) the method of entering and exiting the temporary flight airspace;  
(3) the time for using the temporary flight airspace;  
(4) the nature of the flight activity;  
(5) other relevant matters.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　划设临时飞行空域，按照下列规定的权限批准：  
(一)在机场区域内划设的，由负责该机场飞行管制的部门批准；  
(二)超出机场区域在飞行管制分区内划设的，由负责该分区飞行管制的部门批准；  
(三)超出飞行管制分区在飞行管制区内划设的，由负责该管制区飞行管制的部门批准；  
(四)在飞行管制区间划设的，由中国人民解放军空军批准。  
批准划设临时飞行空域的部门应当将划设的临时飞行空域报上一级飞行管制部门备案，并通报有关单位。

**Article 8** Designation of temporary flight airspace shall be approved according to the following authority:  
(1) if designated within an airport area, it shall be approved by the flight control department responsible for that airport;  
(2) if designated beyond the airport area but within a flight control sub-zone, it shall be approved by the flight control department responsible for that sub-zone;  
(3) if designated beyond a flight control sub-zone but within a flight control zone, it shall be approved by the flight control department responsible for that zone;  
(4) if designated between flight control zones, it shall be approved by the Air Force of the People's Liberation Army.  
The department that approves the designation of temporary flight airspace shall report the designated temporary flight airspace to the superior flight control department for record, and shall notify the relevant units.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　划设临时飞行空域的申请，应当在拟使用临时飞行空域7个工作日前向有关飞行管制部门提出；负责批准该临时飞行空域的飞行管制部门应当在拟使用临时飞行空域3个工作日前作出批准或者不予批准的决定，并通知申请人。

**Article 9** An application for designating temporary flight airspace shall be submitted to the relevant flight control department at least 7 working days before the intended use of the temporary flight airspace; the flight control department responsible for approving such temporary flight airspace shall make a decision on whether to approve or disapprove and notify the applicant at least 3 working days before the intended use of the temporary flight airspace.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　临时飞行空域的使用期限应当根据通用航空飞行的性质和需要确定，通常不得超过12个月。  
因飞行任务的要求，需要延长临时飞行空域使用期限的，应当报经批准该临时飞行空域的飞行管制部门同意。  
通用航空飞行任务完成后，从事通用航空飞行活动的单位、个人应当及时报告有关飞行管制部门，其申请划设的临时飞行空域即行撤销。

**Article 10** The period of use of temporary flight airspace shall be determined based on the nature and needs of general aviation flights, and shall generally not exceed 12 months.  
If it is necessary to extend the period of use of temporary flight airspace due to flight mission requirements, the approval of the flight control department that approved the temporary flight airspace shall be obtained.  
After the completion of general aviation flight missions, the unit or individual engaging in general aviation flight activities shall promptly report to the relevant flight control department, and the temporary flight airspace for which they applied shall be immediately revoked.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　已划设的临时飞行空域，从事通用航空飞行活动的其他单位、个人因飞行需要，经批准划设该临时飞行空域的飞行管制部门同意，也可以使用。

**Article 11** Other units or individuals engaging in general aviation flight activities may also use the temporary flight airspace that has already been designated, subject to the consent of the flight control department that approved the designation of such temporary flight airspace, when needed for flight.

### 第三章　飞行活动的管理

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　从事通用航空飞行活动的单位、个人实施飞行前，应当向当地飞行管制部门提出飞行计划申请，按照批准权限，经批准后方可实施。

**Article 12** Before conducting a flight, a unit or individual engaging in general aviation flight activities shall submit a flight plan application to the local flight control department, and may conduct the flight only after obtaining approval according to the approval authority.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　飞行计划申请应当包括下列内容：  
(一)飞行单位；  
(二)飞行任务性质；  
(三)机长(飞行员)姓名、代号(呼号)和空勤组人数；  
(四)航空器型别和架数；  
(五)通信联络方法和二次雷达应答机代码；  
(六)起飞、降落机场和备降场；  
(七)预计飞行开始、结束时间；  
(八)飞行气象条件；  
(九)航线、飞行高度和飞行范围；  
(十)其他特殊保障需求。

**Article 13** A flight plan application shall include the following:  
(1) the flight unit;  
(2) the nature of the flight mission;  
(3) the name, code (call sign) of the captain (pilot) and the number of the crew;  
(4) the type and number of aircraft;  
(5) the method of communication and the secondary surveillance radar transponder code;  
(6) the take-off and landing airports and alternate airports;  
(7) the estimated start and end time of the flight;  
(8) the meteorological conditions for the flight;  
(9) the route, flight altitude and flight range;  
(10) other special support requirements.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　从事通用航空飞行活动的单位、个人有下列情形之一的，必须在提出飞行计划申请时，提交有效的任务批准文件：  
(一)飞出或者飞入我国领空的(公务飞行除外)；  
(二)进入空中禁区或者国(边)界线至我方一侧10公里之间地带上空飞行的；  
(三)在我国境内进行航空物探或者航空摄影活动的；  
(四)超出领海(海岸)线飞行的；  
(五)外国航空器或者外国人使用我国航空器在我国境内进行通用航空飞行活动的。

**Article 14** Where a unit or individual engaging in general aviation flight activities falls under any of the following circumstances, it must submit valid mission approval documents when filing a flight plan application:  
(1) flying out of or into the airspace of the People's Republic of China (except for official flights);  
(2) entering a prohibited airspace or flying over the area between the national (border) line and a distance of 10 kilometers on the side of the People's Republic of China;  
(3) conducting aerial geophysical prospecting or aerial photography activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China;  
(4) flying beyond the territorial sea (coast) line;  
(5) a foreign aircraft or a foreigner using an aircraft of the People's Republic of China to conduct general aviation flight activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　使用机场飞行空域、航路、航线进行通用航空飞行活动，其飞行计划申请由当地飞行管制部门批准或者由当地飞行管制部门报经上级飞行管制部门批准。  
使用临时飞行空域、临时航线进行通用航空飞行活动，其飞行计划申请按照下列规定的权限批准：  
(一)在机场区域内的，由负责该机场飞行管制的部门批准；  
(二)超出机场区域在飞行管制分区内的，由负责该分区飞行管制的部门批准；  
(三)超出飞行管制分区在飞行管制区内的，由负责该区域飞行管制的部门批准；  
(四)超出飞行管制区的，由中国人民解放军空军批准。

**Article 15** For general aviation flight activities using airport flight airspace, air routes or airways, the flight plan application shall be approved by the local flight control department or be submitted by the local flight control department to the superior flight control department for approval.  
For general aviation flight activities using temporary flight airspace or temporary routes, the flight plan application shall be approved according to the following authority:  
(1) if within the airport area, it shall be approved by the flight control department responsible for that airport;  
(2) if beyond the airport area but within a flight control sub-zone, it shall be approved by the flight control department responsible for that sub-zone;  
(3) if beyond a flight control sub-zone but within a flight control zone, it shall be approved by the flight control department responsible for that zone;  
(4) if beyond a flight control zone, it shall be approved by the Air Force of the People's Liberation Army.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　飞行计划申请应当在拟飞行前1天15时前提出；飞行管制部门应当在拟飞行前1天21时前作出批准或者不予批准的决定，并通知申请人。  
执行紧急救护、抢险救灾、人工影响天气或者其他紧急任务的，可以提出临时飞行计划申请。临时飞行计划申请最迟应当在拟飞行1小时前提出；飞行管制部门应当在拟起飞时刻15分钟前作出批准或者不予批准的决定，并通知申请人。

**Article 16** A flight plan application shall be submitted before 15:00 on the day prior to the intended flight; the flight control department shall make a decision on whether to approve or disapprove before 21:00 on the day prior to the intended flight, and shall notify the applicant.  
A temporary flight plan application may be submitted for emergency rescue, disaster relief, weather modification or other emergency missions. A temporary flight plan application shall be submitted at the latest one hour before the intended flight; the flight control department shall make a decision on whether to approve or disapprove 15 minutes before the intended take-off time, and shall notify the applicant.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在划设的临时飞行空域内实施通用航空飞行活动的，可以在申请划设临时飞行空域时一并提出15天以内的短期飞行计划申请，不再逐日申请；但是每日飞行开始前和结束后，应当及时报告飞行管制部门。

**Article 17** Where general aviation flight activities are conducted within a designated temporary flight airspace, a short-term flight plan application for a period of up to 15 days may be submitted together with the application for designating the temporary flight airspace, without the need for daily applications; however, before and after each day's flight, a report shall be promptly made to the flight control department.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　使用临时航线转场飞行的，其飞行计划申请应当在拟飞行2天前向当地飞行管制部门提出；飞行管制部门应当在拟飞行前1天18时前作出批准或者不予批准的决定，并通知申请人，同时按照规定通报有关单位。

**Article 18** For cross-country flights using temporary routes, the flight plan application shall be submitted to the local flight control department two days before the intended flight; the flight control department shall make a decision on whether to approve or disapprove before 18:00 on the day prior to the intended flight, notify the applicant, and at the same time notify the relevant units in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　飞行管制部门对违反飞行管制规定的航空器，可以根据情况责令改正或者停止其飞行。

**Article 19** The flight control department may, depending on the circumstances, order an aircraft that violates the provisions on flight control to make corrections or stop its flight.

### 第四章　飞行保障

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　通信、导航、雷达、气象、航行情报和其他飞行保障部门应当认真履行职责，密切协同，统筹兼顾，合理安排，提高飞行空域和时间的利用率，保障通用航空飞行顺利实施。

**Article 20** Communication, navigation, radar, meteorological, aeronautical information and other flight support departments shall earnestly perform their duties, closely coordinate, make overall plans and reasonable arrangements, improve the utilization rate of flight airspace and time, and ensure the smooth implementation of general aviation flights.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　通信、导航、雷达、气象、航行情报和其他飞行保障部门对于紧急救护、抢险救灾、人工影响天气等突发性任务的飞行，应当优先安排。

**Article 21** Communication, navigation, radar, meteorological, aeronautical information and other flight support departments shall give priority to flights for emergency rescue, disaster relief, weather modification and other emergent missions.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　从事通用航空飞行活动的单位、个人组织各类飞行活动，应当制定安全保障措施，严格按照批准的飞行计划组织实施，并按照要求报告飞行动态。

**Article 22** Units or individuals engaging in general aviation flight activities that organize various flight activities shall formulate safety assurance measures, strictly organize and implement the flights in accordance with the approved flight plan, and report flight dynamics as required.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　从事通用航空飞行活动的单位、个人，应当与有关飞行管制部门建立可靠的通信联络。  
在划设的临时飞行空域内从事通用航空飞行活动时，应当保持空地联络畅通。

**Article 23** Units or individuals engaging in general aviation flight activities shall establish reliable communication links with the relevant flight control departments.  
When conducting general aviation flight activities within a designated temporary flight airspace, they shall maintain unobstructed air-ground communication.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　在临时飞行空域内进行通用航空飞行活动，通常由从事通用航空飞行活动的单位、个人负责组织实施，并对其安全负责。

**Article 24** General aviation flight activities conducted within temporary flight airspace shall generally be organized and implemented by the units or individuals engaging in such activities, and they shall be responsible for the safety thereof.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　飞行管制部门应当按照职责分工或者协议，为通用航空飞行活动提供空中交通管制服务。

**Article 25** The flight control departments shall, according to their respective functions and duties or agreements, provide air traffic control services for general aviation flight activities.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　从事通用航空飞行活动需要使用军用机场的，应当将使用军用机场的申请和飞行计划申请一并向有关部队司令机关提出，由有关部队司令机关作出批准或者不予批准的决定，并通知申请人。

**Article 26** Where a general aviation flight activity requires the use of a military airport, the application for using the military airport and the flight plan application shall be submitted together to the relevant military command organ, which shall make a decision on whether to approve or disapprove and notify the applicant.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　从事通用航空飞行活动的航空器转场飞行，需要使用军用或者民用机场的，由该机场管理机构按照规定或者协议提供保障；使用军民合用机场的，由从事通用航空飞行活动的单位、个人与机场有关部门协商确定保障事宜。

**Article 27** Where the cross-country flight of an aircraft engaging in general aviation flight activities requires the use of a military or civil airport, the airport management authority shall provide support in accordance with the provisions or agreements; where a joint military-civilian airport is used, the unit or individual engaging in the general aviation flight activities shall negotiate with the relevant airport departments to determine support matters.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　在临时机场或者起降点飞行的组织指挥，通常由从事通用航空飞行活动的单位、个人负责。

**Article 28** The organization and command of flights at temporary airports or landing sites shall generally be the responsibility of the units or individuals engaging in the general aviation flight activities.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　从事通用航空飞行活动的民用航空器能否起飞、着陆和飞行，由机长(飞行员)根据适航标准和气象条件等最终确定，并对此决定负责。

**Article 29** Whether a civil aircraft engaging in general aviation flight activities may take off, land and fly shall be finally determined by the captain (pilot) based on airworthiness standards, meteorological conditions, etc., and he/she shall be responsible for such determination.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　通用航空飞行保障收费标准，按照国家有关国内机场收费标准执行。

**Article 30** The charging standards for general aviation flight support shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant national standards for domestic airport charges.

### 第五章　升放和系留气球的规定

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　升放无人驾驶自由气球或者系留气球，不得影响飞行安全。  
本条例所称无人驾驶自由气球，是指无动力驱动、无人操纵、轻于空气、总质量大于4千克自由飘移的充气物体。  
本条例所称系留气球，是指系留于地面物体上、直径大于1.8米或者体积容量大于3.2立方米、轻于空气的充气物体。

**Article 31** The release of unmanned free balloons or tethered balloons shall not affect flight safety.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "unmanned free balloon" means an inflatable object that is not power-driven, not manned, lighter than air, with a total mass greater than 4 kilograms, and capable of free drifting.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "tethered balloon" means an inflatable object that is tethered to a ground object, with a diameter greater than 1.8 meters or a volume capacity greater than 3.2 cubic meters, and lighter than air.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　无人驾驶自由气球和系留气球的分类、识别标志和升放条件等，应当符合国家有关规定。

**Article 32** The classification, identification marks and release conditions of unmanned free balloons and tethered balloons shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　进行升放无人驾驶自由气球或者系留气球活动，必须经设区的市级以上气象主管机构会同有关部门批准。具体办法由国务院气象主管机构制定。

**Article 33** Activities of releasing unmanned free balloons or tethered balloons must be approved by the meteorological authority at or above the city with districts level jointly with the relevant departments. The specific measures shall be formulated by the meteorological authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　升放无人驾驶自由气球，应当在拟升放2天前持[本条例第三十三条](#art-33)规定的批准文件向当地飞行管制部门提出升放申请；飞行管制部门应当在拟升放1天前作出批准或者不予批准的决定，并通知申请人。

**Article 34** For the release of an unmanned free balloon, the release application shall be submitted to the local flight control department with the approval documents specified in Article 33 of these Regulations two days before the intended release; the flight control department shall make a decision on whether to approve or disapprove one day before the intended release, and notify the applicant.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　升放无人驾驶自由气球的申请，通常应当包括下列内容：  
(一)升放的单位、个人和联系方法；  
(二)气球的类型、数量、用途和识别标志；  
(三)升放地点和计划回收区；  
(四)预计升放和回收(结束)的时间；  
(五)预计飘移方向、上升的速度和最大高度。

**Article 35** An application for releasing an unmanned free balloon shall generally include the following:  
(1) the unit or individual releasing the balloon and the contact method;  
(2) the type, quantity, purpose and identification marks of the balloon;  
(3) the release location and planned recovery area;  
(4) the estimated time of release and recovery (end);  
(5) the estimated drift direction, ascending speed and maximum altitude.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　升放无人驾驶自由气球，应当按照批准的申请升放，并及时向有关飞行管制部门报告升放动态；取消升放时，应当及时报告有关飞行管制部门。

**Article 36** Unmanned free balloons shall be released in accordance with the approved application, and the release dynamics shall be promptly reported to the relevant flight control department; if the release is cancelled, the relevant flight control department shall be promptly notified.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　升放系留气球，应当确保系留牢固，不得擅自释放。  
系留气球升放的高度不得高于地面150米，但是低于距其水平距离50米范围内建筑物顶部的除外。  
系留气球升放的高度超过地面50米的，必须加装快速放气装置，并设置识别标志。

**Article 37** When releasing a tethered balloon, it shall be ensured that the tether is secure, and the balloon shall not be released without authorization.  
The altitude at which a tethered balloon is released shall not exceed 150 meters above the ground, except when it is lower than the top of buildings within a horizontal distance of 50 meters.  
If the altitude at which a tethered balloon is released exceeds 50 meters above the ground, a quick-release device must be installed and identification marks must be set.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　升放的无人驾驶自由气球或者系留气球中发生下列可能危及飞行安全的情况时，升放单位、个人应当及时报告有关飞行管制部门和当地气象主管机构：  
(一)无人驾驶自由气球非正常运行的；  
(二)系留气球意外脱离系留的；  
(三)其他可能影响飞行安全的异常情况。  
加装快速放气装置的系留气球意外脱离系留时，升放系留气球的单位、个人应当在保证地面人员、财产安全的条件下，快速启动放气装置。

**Article 38** Where any of the following situations that may endanger flight safety occurs in the released unmanned free balloon or tethered balloon, the releasing unit or individual shall promptly report to the relevant flight control department and the local meteorological authority:  
(1) abnormal operation of the unmanned free balloon;  
(2) accidental detachment of the tethered balloon from its tether;  
(3) other abnormal situations that may affect flight safety.  
When a tethered balloon equipped with a quick-release device accidentally detaches from its tether, the unit or individual releasing the tethered balloon shall quickly activate the release device under the condition of ensuring the safety of ground personnel and property.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　禁止在依法划设的机场范围内和机场净空保护区域内升放无人驾驶自由气球或者系留气球，但是国家另有规定的除外。

**Article 39** The release of unmanned free balloons or tethered balloons is prohibited within the scope of airports designated according to law and within airport obstacle limitation surfaces, unless otherwise provided by the State.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本条例规定，《中华人民共和国民用航空法》、《中华人民共和国飞行基本规则》及有关行政法规对其处罚有规定的，从其规定；没有规定的，适用本章规定。

**Article 40** Where a violation of these Regulations is provided with penalties in the Civil Aviation Law of the People's Republic of China, the Basic Rules for Flights of the People's Republic of China or relevant administrative regulations, those provisions shall prevail; where there are no such provisions, the provisions of this Chapter shall apply.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　从事通用航空飞行活动的单位、个人违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由有关部门按照职责分工责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，处2万元以上10万元以下罚款，并可给予责令停飞1个月至3个月、暂扣直至吊销经营许可证、飞行执照的处罚；造成重大事故或者严重后果的，依照刑法关于重大飞行事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未经批准擅自飞行的；  
(二)未按批准的飞行计划飞行的；  
(三)不及时报告或者漏报飞行动态的；  
(四)未经批准飞入空中限制区、空中危险区的。

**Article 41** Where a unit or individual engaging in general aviation flight activities violates these Regulations and falls under any of the following circumstances, the relevant department shall, according to its functions and duties, order corrections and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a penalty of ordering a suspension of flights for 1 to 3 months, or temporary seizure up to revocation of the operation license or pilot license may also be imposed; if a major accident or serious consequence is caused, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of major flight accident or other crimes:  
(1) flying without authorization;  
(2) failing to fly in accordance with the approved flight plan;  
(3) failing to report or omitting to report flight dynamics in a timely manner;  
(4) flying into a restricted airspace or a dangerous airspace without approval.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本条例规定，未经批准飞入空中禁区的，由有关部门按照国家有关规定处置。

**Article 42** Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, flies into a prohibited airspace without approval, such person shall be dealt with by the relevant department in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反本条例规定，升放无人驾驶自由气球或者系留气球，有下列情形之一的，由气象主管机构或者有关部门按照职责分工责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，处1万元以上5万元以下罚款；造成重大事故或者严重后果的，依照刑法关于重大责任事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未经批准擅自升放的；  
(二)未按照批准的申请升放的；  
(三)未按照规定设置识别标志的；  
(四)未及时报告升放动态或者系留气球意外脱离时未按照规定及时报告的；  
(五)在规定的禁止区域内升放的。

**Article 43** Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, releases an unmanned free balloon or a tethered balloon and falls under any of the following circumstances, the meteorological authority or the relevant department shall, according to its functions and duties, order corrections and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if a major accident or serious consequence is caused, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of major liability accident or other crimes:  
(1) releasing without authorization;  
(2) failing to release in accordance with the approved application;  
(3) failing to set identification marks as required;  
(4) failing to report release dynamics in a timely manner or failing to report timely when a tethered balloon accidentally detaches;  
(5) releasing in a prohibited area.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　按照本条例实施的罚款，应当全额上缴财政。

**Article 44** Fines imposed in accordance with these Regulations shall be turned over to the State treasury in full.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例自2003年5月1日起施行。

**Article 45** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2003.
