# 中华人民共和国海上航行警告和航行通告管理规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1993-01-11  
**生效日期**：1993-02-01

> (1992年12月22日国务院批准  1993年1月11日交通部令第44号发布)

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强海上航行警告和航行通告的管理，保障船舶、设施的航行和作业安全，根据《中华人民共和国海上交通安全法》有关规定，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Maritime Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of strengthening the administration of maritime navigation warnings and navigation notices, and ensuring the safety of navigation and operations of vessels and installations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定适用于在中华人民共和国沿海水域从事影响或者可能影响海上交通安全的各种活动的船舶、设施和人员，以及负责发布海上航行警告、航行通告的有关单位和人员。

**Article 2** These Provisions shall apply to vessels, installations and persons engaged in various activities that affect or may affect maritime traffic safety in the coastal waters of the People's Republic of China, as well as the relevant units and persons responsible for issuing maritime navigation warnings and navigation notices.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国港务监督机构(以下简称国家主管机关)主管全国海上航行警告和航行通告的统一发布工作。  
沿海水域港务监督机构(以下简称区域主管机关)主管本管辖区域内海上航行警告和航行通告的统一发布工作。  
沿海水域港务监督机构的管辖区域由国家主管机关确定。

**Article 3** The Harbour Superintendency Administration of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the National Competent Authority) shall be responsible for the unified issuance of maritime navigation warnings and navigation notices throughout the country.  
The Harbour Superintendency Administration of the coastal waters (hereinafter referred to as the Regional Competent Authority) shall be responsible for the unified issuance of maritime navigation warnings and navigation notices within its area of jurisdiction.  
The area of jurisdiction of the Harbour Superintendency Administration of the coastal waters shall be determined by the National Competent Authority.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　海上航行警告由国家主管机关或者其授权的机关以无线电报或者无线电话的形式发布。  
海上航行通告由国家主管机关或者区域主管机关以书面形式或者通过报纸、广播、电视等新闻媒介发布。

**Article 4** Maritime navigation warnings shall be issued by the National Competent Authority or an authority authorized by it in the form of radiotelegraph or radiotelephone.  
Maritime navigation notices shall be issued by the National Competent Authority or the Regional Competent Authority in written form or through news media such as newspapers, radio, and television.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在中华人民共和国沿海水域从事下列活动，必须事先向所涉及的海区的区域主管机关申请发布海上航行警告、航行通告：  
(一)改变航道、航槽；  
(二)划定、改动或者撤销禁航区、抛泥区、水产养殖区、测速区、水上娱乐区；  
(三)设置或者撤除公用罗经标、消磁场；  
(四)打捞沉船、沉物；  
(五)铺设、撤除、检修电缆和管道；  
(六)设置、撤除系船浮筒及其他建筑物；  
(七)设置、撤除用于海上勘探开发的设施和其安全区；  
(八)从事扫海、疏浚、爆破、打桩、拔桩、起重、钻探等作业；  
(九)进行使船舶航行能力受到限制的超长、超高、笨重拖带作业；  
(十)进行有碍海上航行安全的海洋地质调查、勘探和水文测量；  
(十一)进行其他影响海上航行和作业安全的活动。  
军事单位划定、改动或者撤销军事禁航区、军事训练区，由国家主管机关或者区域主管机关发布海上航行警告、航行通告。

**Article 5** Any person engaging in the following activities in the coastal waters of the People's Republic of China must apply in advance to the Regional Competent Authority of the sea area concerned for the issuance of a maritime navigation warning or navigation notice:  
(1) altering a fairway or channel;  
(2) delimiting, altering or cancelling a prohibited navigation area, dumping area, aquaculture area, speed trial area or water recreation area;  
(3) installing or removing a public compass datum or degaussing range;  
(4) salving a sunken ship or sunken object;  
(5) laying, removing or repairing cables or pipelines;  
(6) installing or removing mooring buoys or other structures;  
(7) installing or removing facilities for offshore exploration and exploitation and their safety zones;  
(8) carrying out operations such as sweeping, dredging, blasting, pile driving, pile extraction, hoisting or drilling;  
(9) carrying out towing operations involving excessive length, excessive height or heavy objects which restrict the navigability of vessels;  
(10) conducting marine geological surveys, exploration or hydrographic surveys that obstruct the safety of maritime navigation;  
(11) carrying out other activities affecting the safety of maritime navigation and operations.  
Where a military unit delimits, alters or cancels a military prohibited navigation area or military training area, the National Competent Authority or the Regional Competent Authority shall issue a maritime navigation warning or navigation notice.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　组织或者从事[本规定第五条](#art-5)第一款所列各项活动的单位，应当在活动开始之日的七天前向该项活动所涉及海区的区域主管机关递交发布海上航行警告、航行通告的书面申请。但是，有特殊情况，经区域主管机关认定，需要立即发布海上航行警告、航行通告的除外。从事[本规定第五条](#art-5)第一款第(九)项所列活动的，依照[本规定第七条](#art-7)执行。  
书面申请应当包括下列内容：  
(一)活动起止日期和海日活动时间；  
(二)活动内容和活动方式；  
(三)参加活动的船舶、设施和单位的名称；  
(四)活动区域；  
(五)安全措施。

**Article 6** A unit that organizes or engages in any of the activities listed in paragraph 1 of Article 5 of these Provisions shall submit a written application for the issuance of a maritime navigation warning or navigation notice to the Regional Competent Authority of the sea area involved in such activity seven days before the date of commencement of the activity, except where, due to special circumstances, the Regional Competent Authority determines that an immediate issuance of a maritime navigation warning or navigation notice is necessary. Where a unit engages in an activity listed in item (9) of paragraph 1 of Article 5 of these Provisions, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Article 7 of these Provisions.  
The written application shall include the following particulars:  
(1) the start and end dates of the activity and the specific time of activity each day;  
(2) the content and method of the activity;  
(3) the names of vessels, installations and units participating in the activity;  
(4) the area of the activity; and  
(5) safety measures.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　船舶从事[本规定第五条](#art-5)第(九)项所列活动的，应当在启拖开始之日的三天前向启拖地所在海区的区域主管机关递交发布海上航行警告、航行通告的书面申请。  
书面申请应当包括下列内容：  
(一)拖船、被拖船或者被拖物的名称；  
(二)启拖时间；  
(三)启始位置、终到位置及主要转向点位置；  
(四)拖带总长度；  
(五)航速。

**Article 7** Where a vessel engages in an activity listed in item (9) of Article 5 of these Provisions, it shall submit a written application for the issuance of a maritime navigation warning or navigation notice to the Regional Competent Authority of the sea area where the towing commencement location is situated three days before the date of commencement of the towing.  
The written application shall include the following particulars:  
(1) the names of the tug, the vessel or object being towed;  
(2) the time of commencement of towing;  
(3) the starting position, final position and positions of major turning points;  
(4) the total length of the tow; and  
(5) the towing speed.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　海上航行警告、航行通告发布后，申请人必须在国家主管机关或者区域主管机关核准的时间和区域内进行活动；需要变更活动时间或者改换活动区域的，应当依照本规定，重新申请发布海上航行警告、航行通告。

**Article 8** After the issuance of a maritime navigation warning or navigation notice, the applicant must carry out the activity within the time and area approved by the National Competent Authority or the Regional Competent Authority; if it is necessary to change the activity time or alter the activity area, the applicant shall re-apply for the issuance of a maritime navigation warning or navigation notice in accordance with these Provisions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　船舶、设施在海上发现下列情形，应当尽快向就近的区域主管机关报告：  
(一)航海图书上未载明的浅滩、礁石；  
(二)异常磁区或者海水变色；  
(三)沉船、沉物、危险物、碍航漂流物；  
(四)助航标志或者导航设施变异、失常；  
(五)其他有碍海上航行安全的情形。  
报告内容应当包括：发现时间、地点和被发现物的状况。

**Article 9** Where a vessel or installation discovers any of the following situations at sea, it shall report to the nearest Regional Competent Authority as soon as possible:  
(1) shoals or reefs not indicated on nautical charts or publications;  
(2) anomalous magnetic areas or discoloration of seawater;  
(3) sunken ships, sunken objects, dangerous objects or drifting objects obstructing navigation;  
(4) alterations or malfunctions of aids to navigation or navigational facilities; or  
(5) other situations detrimental to the safety of maritime navigation.  
The report shall include the time and place of discovery and the condition of the object discovered.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　区域主管机关收到有碍海上航行安全的报告或者发布海上航行警告、航行通告的申请后，应当立即核实有关资料，根据实际需要和接收范围，决定发布海上航行警告、航通告。

**Article 10** After receiving a report on a situation detrimental to the safety of maritime navigation or an application for the issuance of a maritime navigation warning or navigation notice, the Regional Competent Authority shall immediately verify the relevant information and, based on the actual needs and the scope of reception, decide on the issuance of a maritime navigation warning or navigation notice.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　区域主管机关对本管辖区域内的下列情形，应当发布海上航行警告、航行通告：  
(一)设置、调整或者撤销锚地；  
(二)设置或者撤销海难救助区、防污作业区、海上作业重大事故区；  
(三)设置、变更或者撤销分道通航制；  
(四)设置、撤除、改建、变更或者恢复助航标志和导航设施；  
(五)其他有碍海上航行和作业安全的情形。

**Article 11** The Regional Competent Authority shall issue a maritime navigation warning or navigation notice in respect of the following situations within its area of jurisdiction:  
(1) the establishment, adjustment or cancellation of an anchorage;  
(2) the establishment or cancellation of a maritime salvage area, an anti-pollution operation area or a major maritime operation accident area;  
(3) the establishment, modification or cancellation of a traffic separation scheme;  
(4) the establishment, removal, reconstruction, modification or restoration of aids to navigation and navigational facilities; or  
(5) other situations detrimental to the safety of maritime navigation and operations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家主管机关或者区域主管机关在发布海上航行警告、航行通告以及收到[本规定第九条](#art-9)规定的各项报告时，应当及时向海军航海保证部门提供有关资料并通报有关情况。

**Article 12** When issuing a maritime navigation warning or navigation notice, or when receiving any report prescribed in Article 9 of these Provisions, the National Competent Authority or the Regional Competent Authority shall promptly provide the relevant information to the naval navigation assurance department and notify it of the relevant circumstances.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　海岸电台负责并按照规定的时间、频率和要求播发海上航行警告。播发的程序和办法由国务院交通主管部门另行制定。

**Article 13** Coast radio stations shall be responsible for broadcasting maritime navigation warnings at the prescribed times, frequencies and in accordance with the requirements. The procedures and methods for broadcasting shall be separately formulated by the competent department of transportation under the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　船舶、设施的有关人员必须按照规定抄收海岸电台播发的海上航行警告。

**Article 14** The relevant personnel of vessels and installations must, in accordance with the regulations, copy and receive the maritime navigation warnings broadcast by coast radio stations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　有关单位收到海上航行通告后，必须采取有效手段，及时通知所属船舶、设施。

**Article 15** After receiving a maritime navigation notice, the relevant unit must take effective measures to promptly notify the vessels and installations under its command.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　对于执行本规定做出显著成绩的单位和个人，由国家主管机关或者区域主管机关给予奖励。

**Article 16** Units and individuals that achieve outstanding results in implementing these Provisions shall be rewarded by the National Competent Authority or the Regional Competent Authority.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　违反[本规定第五条](#art-5)第一款、第八条规定的，由国家主管机关或者区域主管机关责令其停止活动，并可以处二千元以下罚款。

**Article 17** Any person who violates the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 5 or Article 8 of these Provisions shall be ordered by the National Competent Authority or the Regional Competent Authority to cease the activity, and may be fined not more than 2,000 yuan.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　未依照[本规定第六条](#art-6)、第七条规定的时间申请发布海上航行警告、航行通告的，国家主管机关或者区域主管机关可以给予警告，可以并处八百元以下罚款。

**Article 18** Where an application for the issuance of a maritime navigation warning or navigation notice is not made within the time limit prescribed in Article 6 or Article 7 of these Provisions, the National Competent Authority or the Regional Competent Authority may give a warning, and may also impose a fine of not more than 800 yuan.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对违反[本规定第十四条](#art-14)规定的责任人员，根据情节，国家主管机关或者区域主管机关可以给予警告、扣留职务证书或者吊销职务证书。

**Article 19** The National Competent Authority or the Regional Competent Authority may, depending on the circumstances, give a warning, detain the certificate of competency or revoke the certificate of competency in respect of the responsible person who violates the provisions of Article 14 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　违反本规定，造成海上交通事故的，除依法承担民事赔偿责任外，国家主管机关或者区域主管机关可以根据情节给予罚款、扣留职务证书或者吊销职务证书；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 20** Any person who violates these Provisions and causes a maritime traffic accident shall, in addition to bearing civil liability for compensation according to law, be subject to a fine, detention of the certificate of competency or revocation of the certificate of competency by the National Competent Authority or the Regional Competent Authority according to the circumstances; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　当事人对罚款、扣留职务证书或者吊销职务证书的处罚决定不服的，可以自接到处罚决定通知之日起十五日内向中华人民共和国港务监督机构申请复议，可也以直接向人民法院提起诉讼；期满不申请复议也不提起诉讼又不履行的，作出处罚决定的主管机关可以申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 21** A party dissatisfied with a penalty decision of fine, suspension of service certificate or revocation of service certificate may, within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the notice of the penalty decision, apply to the Harbor Superintendency Agency of the People's Republic of China for reconsideration, or may directly initiate a prosecution in a People's Court; if the party fails to apply for reconsideration or initiate a prosecution within the time limit and fails to comply with the decision, the competent authority that made the penalty decision may apply to a People's Court for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　在渔港水域内新建、改建、扩建各种设施或者进行其他施工作业，由渔政渔港监督管理机关根据本规定和国家其他有关规定发布海上航行通告。

**Article 22** Where any facilities are newly built, rebuilt or expanded or any other construction operations are carried out in the waters of fishing ports, the fishery administration and fishing port superintendency agencies shall issue a maritime navigation notice in accordance with these Provisions and other relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　军事单位涉及海上航行警告、航行通告事宜的管理办法。根据《中华人民共和国海上交通安全法》有关规定，另行制定。

**Article 23** The measures for the administration of maritime navigation warnings and navigation notices involving military units shall be separately formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Maritime Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本规定由交通部负责解释。

**Article 24** These Provisions shall be interpreted by the Ministry of Communications.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本规定自一九九三年二月一日起施行。

**Article 25** These Provisions shall come into force as of February 1, 1993.
