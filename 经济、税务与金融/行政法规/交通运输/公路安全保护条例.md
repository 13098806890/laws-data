**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2011-03-07  
**生效日期**：2011-07-01

> (2011年2月16日国务院第144次常务会议通过　2011年3月7日中华人民共和国国务院令第593号公布　自2011年7月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强公路保护，保障公路完好、安全和畅通，根据《中华人民共和国公路法》，制定本条例。 第二条　各级人民政府应当加强对公路保护工作的领导，依法履行公路保护职责。 第三条　国务院交通运输主管部门主管全国公路保护工作。 县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门主管本行政区域的公路保护工作；但是，县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门对国道、省道的保护职责，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定。 公路管理机构依照本条例的规定具体负责公路保护的监督管理工作。 第四条　县级以上各级人民政府发展改革、工业和信息化、公安、工商、质检等部门按照职责分工，依法开展公路保护的相关工作。 第五条　县级以上各级人民政府应当将政府及其有关部门从事公路管理、养护所需经费以及公路管理机构行使公路行政管理职能所需经费纳入本级人民政府财政预算。但是，专用公路的公路保护经费除外。 第六条　县级以上各级人民政府交通运输主管部门应当综合考虑国家有关车辆技术标准、公路使用状况等因素，逐步提高公路建设、管理和养护水平，努力满足国民经济和社会发展以及人民群众生产、生活需要。 第七条　县级以上各级人民政府交通运输主管部门应当依照《中华人民共和国突发事件应对法》的规定，制定地震、泥石流、雨雪冰冻灾害等损毁公路的突发事件(以下简称公路突发事件)应急预案，报本级人民政府批准后实施。 公路管理机构、公路经营企业应当根据交通运输主管部门制定的公路突发事件应急预案，组建应急队伍，并定期组织应急演练。 第八条　国家建立健全公路突发事件应急物资储备保障制度，完善应急物资储备、调配体系，确保发生公路突发事件时能够满足应急处置工作的需要。 第九条　任何单位和个人不得破坏、损坏、非法占用或者非法利用公路、公路用地和公路附属设施。 第二章　公路线路

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强公路保护，保障公路完好、安全和畅通，根据《中华人民共和国公路法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** For the purposes of strengthening highway protection, ensuring the integrity, safety and smoothness of highways, these Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Highway Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　各级人民政府应当加强对公路保护工作的领导，依法履行公路保护职责。

**Article 2** The people's governments at all levels shall strengthen leadership over highway protection work and perform their highway protection duties according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院交通运输主管部门主管全国公路保护工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门主管本行政区域的公路保护工作；但是，县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门对国道、省道的保护职责，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定。  
公路管理机构依照本条例的规定具体负责公路保护的监督管理工作。

**Article 3** The competent transport department of the State Council shall take charge of highway protection work nationwide.  
The competent transport departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall take charge of highway protection work within their respective administrative regions; provided that the protection duties of the competent transport departments of local people's governments at or above the county level with respect to national and provincial highways shall be determined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
The highway management institution shall be specifically responsible for the supervision and administration of highway protection in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上各级人民政府发展改革、工业和信息化、公安、工商、质检等部门按照职责分工，依法开展公路保护的相关工作。

**Article 4** The development and reform, industry and information technology, public security, industry and commerce, quality inspection and other departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective division of responsibilities, carry out relevant highway protection work according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上各级人民政府应当将政府及其有关部门从事公路管理、养护所需经费以及公路管理机构行使公路行政管理职能所需经费纳入本级人民政府财政预算。但是，专用公路的公路保护经费除外。

**Article 5** The people's governments at or above the county level shall include in the fiscal budgets of the people's governments at the corresponding level the funds required by the governments and their relevant departments for highway administration and maintenance, as well as the funds required by the highway management institution for exercising highway administrative functions, except for the highway protection funds for highways for special use.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上各级人民政府交通运输主管部门应当综合考虑国家有关车辆技术标准、公路使用状况等因素，逐步提高公路建设、管理和养护水平，努力满足国民经济和社会发展以及人民群众生产、生活需要。

**Article 6** The competent transport departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall comprehensively consider factors such as relevant national vehicle technical standards and highway usage conditions, gradually improve the level of highway construction, administration and maintenance, and strive to meet the needs of national economic and social development and the production and living needs of the people.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上各级人民政府交通运输主管部门应当依照《中华人民共和国突发事件应对法》的规定，制定地震、泥石流、雨雪冰冻灾害等损毁公路的突发事件(以下简称公路突发事件)应急预案，报本级人民政府批准后实施。  
公路管理机构、公路经营企业应当根据交通运输主管部门制定的公路突发事件应急预案，组建应急队伍，并定期组织应急演练。

**Article 7** The competent transport departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Emergency Response Law of the People's Republic of China, formulate emergency response plans for emergencies that damage highways such as earthquakes, mudslides, rain, snow and ice disasters (hereinafter referred to as highway emergencies), and submit them to the people's governments at the corresponding level for approval before implementation.  
The highway management institution and highway operating enterprises shall, based on the highway emergency response plans formulated by the competent transport departments, establish emergency response teams and organize emergency drills on a regular basis.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家建立健全公路突发事件应急物资储备保障制度，完善应急物资储备、调配体系，确保发生公路突发事件时能够满足应急处置工作的需要。

**Article 8** The State shall establish and improve the guarantee system for emergency material reserves for highway emergencies, and improve the system for the reserve and deployment of emergency materials, to ensure that the needs of emergency response work can be met when a highway emergency occurs.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何单位和个人不得破坏、损坏、非法占用或者非法利用公路、公路用地和公路附属设施。

**Article 9** No unit or individual may destroy, damage, illegally occupy or illegally use highways, highway land and highway auxiliary facilities.

### 第二章　公路线路

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　公路管理机构应当建立健全公路管理档案，对公路、公路用地和公路附属设施调查核实、登记造册。

**Article 10** The highway management institution shall establish and improve highway management archives, investigate, verify and register highways, highway land and highway auxiliary facilities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据保障公路运行安全和节约用地的原则以及公路发展的需要，组织交通运输、国土资源等部门划定公路建筑控制区的范围。  
公路建筑控制区的范围，从公路用地外缘起向外的距离标准为：  
(一)国道不少于20米；  
(二)省道不少于15米；  
(三)县道不少于10米；  
(四)乡道不少于5米。  
属于高速公路的，公路建筑控制区的范围从公路用地外缘起向外的距离标准不少于30米。  
公路弯道内侧、互通立交以及平面交叉道口的建筑控制区范围根据安全视距等要求确定。

**Article 11** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the principles of ensuring highway operation safety and land conservation as well as the needs of highway development, organize the transport, land and resources and other departments to delimit the scope of highway building control areas.  
The distance standard for the scope of highway building control areas, measured outward from the outer edge of the highway land, is as follows:  
(1) not less than 20 meters for national highways;  
(2) not less than 15 meters for provincial highways;  
(3) not less than 10 meters for county highways;  
(4) not less than 5 meters for township highways.  
For expressways, the distance standard for the scope of highway building control areas, measured outward from the outer edge of the highway land, is not less than 30 meters.  
The scope of building control areas on the inner side of highway curves, interchange interchanges and level intersections shall be determined based on requirements such as safe sight distance.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　新建、改建公路的建筑控制区的范围，应当自公路初步设计批准之日起30日内，由公路沿线县级以上地方人民政府依照本条例划定并公告。  
公路建筑控制区与铁路线路安全保护区、航道保护范围、河道管理范围或者水工程管理和保护范围重叠的，经公路管理机构和铁路管理机构、航道管理机构、水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构协商后划定。

**Article 12** The scope of building control areas for new or reconstructed highways shall be delimited and announced by the local people's governments at or above the county level along the highway within 30 days from the date of approval of the preliminary design of the highway, in accordance with these Regulations.  
Where the highway building control area overlaps with railway line safety protection zones, navigation channel protection ranges, river course management ranges, or water project administration and protection ranges, it shall be delimited after consultation between the highway management institution and the railway management institution, navigation channel management institution, water administrative department or river basin management institution.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　在公路建筑控制区内，除公路保护需要外，禁止修建建筑物和地面构筑物；公路建筑控制区划定前已经合法修建的不得扩建，因公路建设或者保障公路运行安全等原因需要拆除的应当依法给予补偿。  
在公路建筑控制区外修建的建筑物、地面构筑物以及其他设施不得遮挡公路标志，不得妨碍安全视距。

**Article 13** Within the highway building control areas, except as required for highway protection, the construction of buildings and surface structures is prohibited; buildings and structures lawfully constructed before the delimitation of the highway building control areas shall not be expanded, and if removal is necessary due to highway construction or ensuring highway operation safety and other reasons, compensation shall be given according to law.  
Buildings, surface structures and other facilities constructed outside the highway building control areas shall not obstruct highway signs or hinder safe sight distance.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　新建村镇、开发区、学校和货物集散地、大型商业网点、农贸市场等公共场所，与公路建筑控制区边界外缘的距离应当符合下列标准，并尽可能在公路一侧建设：  
(一)国道、省道不少于50米；  
(二)县道、乡道不少于20米。

**Article 14** The distance of newly built villages and towns, development zones, schools, goods distribution centers, large commercial outlets, farmers' markets and other public places from the outer edge of the boundary of highway building control areas shall meet the following standards, and they shall, as far as possible, be built on one side of the highway:  
(1) not less than 50 meters for national and provincial highways;  
(2) not less than 20 meters for county and township highways.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　新建、改建公路与既有城市道路、铁路、通信等线路交叉或者新建、改建城市道路、铁路、通信等线路与既有公路交叉的，建设费用由新建、改建单位承担；城市道路、铁路、通信等线路的管理部门、单位或者公路管理机构要求提高既有建设标准而增加的费用，由提出要求的部门或者单位承担。  
需要改变既有公路与城市道路、铁路、通信等线路交叉方式的，按照公平合理的原则分担建设费用。

**Article 15** Where a new or reconstructed highway crosses an existing urban road, railway, communication line, etc., or where a new or reconstructed urban road, railway, communication line, etc. crosses an existing highway, the construction costs shall be borne by the unit undertaking the new construction or reconstruction; if the department or unit managing the urban road, railway, communication line, etc., or the highway management institution requests to raise the existing construction standards, the additional costs shall be borne by the department or unit making the request.  
If it is necessary to change the crossing mode between the existing highway and urban road, railway, communication line, etc., the construction costs shall be shared in accordance with the principle of fairness and reasonableness.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　禁止将公路作为检验车辆制动性能的试车场地。  
禁止在公路、公路用地范围内摆摊设点、堆放物品、倾倒垃圾、设置障碍、挖沟引水、打场晒粮、种植作物、放养牲畜、采石、取土、采空作业、焚烧物品、利用公路边沟排放污物或者进行其他损坏、污染公路和影响公路畅通的行为。

**Article 16** It is prohibited to use highways as test sites for inspecting vehicle braking performance.  
It is prohibited to set up stalls, pile up articles, dump garbage, set up obstacles, dig ditches to divert water, thresh and sun grain, plant crops, graze livestock, quarry, extract soil, conduct mining activities, burn articles, discharge pollutants using highway side ditches, or engage in other acts that damage or pollute highways or affect highway smoothness within the scope of highways or highway land.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　禁止在下列范围内从事采矿、采石、取土、爆破作业等危及公路、公路桥梁、公路隧道、公路渡口安全的活动：  
(一)国道、省道、县道的公路用地外缘起向外100米，乡道的公路用地外缘起向外50米；  
(二)公路渡口和中型以上公路桥梁周围200米；  
(三)公路隧道上方和洞口外100米。  
在前款规定的范围内，因抢险、防汛需要修筑堤坝、压缩或者拓宽河床的，应当经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通运输主管部门会同水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构批准，并采取安全防护措施方可进行。

**Article 17** It is prohibited to engage in mining, quarrying, earth extraction, blasting operations and other activities that endanger the safety of highways, highway bridges, highway tunnels, and highway ferries within the following ranges:  
(1) 100 meters outward from the outer edge of highway land for national, provincial and county highways, and 50 meters outward from the outer edge of highway land for township highways;  
(2) 200 meters around highway ferries and medium-sized and above highway bridges;  
(3) 100 meters above highway tunnels and outside tunnel entrances.  
If it is necessary to construct dams, compress or widen riverbeds due to emergency rescue and flood control within the ranges specified in the preceding paragraph, approval shall be obtained from the competent transport department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the water administrative department or the river basin management institution, and safety protection measures shall be taken before carrying out such activities.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　除按照国家有关规定设立的为车辆补充燃料的场所、设施外，禁止在下列范围内设立生产、储存、销售易燃、易爆、剧毒、放射性等危险物品的场所、设施：  
(一)公路用地外缘起向外100米；  
(二)公路渡口和中型以上公路桥梁周围200米；  
(三)公路隧道上方和洞口外100米。

**Article 18** Except for places and facilities established for refueling vehicles in accordance with relevant State regulations, it is prohibited to establish places and facilities for the production, storage or sale of dangerous articles such as flammable, explosive, highly toxic or radioactive substances within the following ranges:  
(1) 100 meters outward from the outer edge of highway land;  
(2) 200 meters around highway ferries and medium-sized and above highway bridges;  
(3) 100 meters above highway tunnels and outside tunnel entrances.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　禁止擅自在中型以上公路桥梁跨越的河道上下游各1000米范围内抽取地下水、架设浮桥以及修建其他危及公路桥梁安全的设施。  
在前款规定的范围内，确需进行抽取地下水、架设浮桥等活动的，应当经水行政主管部门、流域管理机构等有关单位会同公路管理机构批准，并采取安全防护措施方可进行。

**Article 19** It is prohibited, without authorization, to extract groundwater, install floating bridges or construct other facilities that endanger the safety of highway bridges within 1,000 meters upstream and downstream of the river crossed by a medium-sized or above highway bridge.  
If it is indeed necessary to carry out activities such as groundwater extraction or floating bridge installation within the ranges specified in the preceding paragraph, approval shall be obtained from the water administrative department, river basin management institution and other relevant units jointly with the highway management institution, and safety protection measures shall be taken before carrying out such activities.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　禁止在公路桥梁跨越的河道上下游的下列范围内采砂：  
(一)特大型公路桥梁跨越的河道上游500米，下游3000米；  
(二)大型公路桥梁跨越的河道上游500米，下游2000米；  
(三)中小型公路桥梁跨越的河道上游500米，下游1000米。

**Article 20** It is prohibited to extract sand within the following ranges upstream and downstream of the river crossed by a highway bridge:  
(1) 500 meters upstream and 3,000 meters downstream for rivers crossed by extra-large highway bridges;  
(2) 500 meters upstream and 2,000 meters downstream for rivers crossed by large highway bridges;  
(3) 500 meters upstream and 1,000 meters downstream for rivers crossed by medium and small highway bridges.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　在公路桥梁跨越的河道上下游各500米范围内依法进行疏浚作业的，应当符合公路桥梁安全要求，经公路管理机构确认安全方可作业。

**Article 21** Any dredging operation carried out according to law within 500 meters upstream and downstream of the river crossed by a highway bridge shall comply with the safety requirements for the highway bridge, and the operation may only be conducted after the highway management institution confirms safety.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　禁止利用公路桥梁进行牵拉、吊装等危及公路桥梁安全的施工作业。  
禁止利用公路桥梁(含桥下空间)、公路隧道、涵洞堆放物品，搭建设施以及铺设高压电线和输送易燃、易爆或者其他有毒有害气体、液体的管道。

**Article 22** It is prohibited to use highway bridges for construction operations such as pulling and hoisting that endanger the safety of highway bridges.  
It is prohibited to use highway bridges (including the space under the bridge), highway tunnels, and culverts for piling up articles, erecting facilities, laying high-voltage wires, and laying pipelines for conveying flammable, explosive or other toxic and harmful gases or liquids.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　公路桥梁跨越航道的，建设单位应当按照国家有关规定设置桥梁航标、桥柱标、桥梁水尺标，并按照国家标准、行业标准设置桥区水上航标和桥墩防撞装置。桥区水上航标由航标管理机构负责维护。  
通过公路桥梁的船舶应当符合公路桥梁通航净空要求，严格遵守航行规则，不得在公路桥梁下停泊或者系缆。

**Article 23** Where a highway bridge crosses a navigation channel, the construction unit shall, in accordance with relevant State regulations, set up bridge navigation marks, bridge pier marks, and bridge water gauge marks, and set up bridge area water navigation marks and bridge pier anti-collision devices in accordance with national standards and industry standards. The bridge area water navigation marks shall be maintained by the navigation mark management institution.  
Ships passing through a highway bridge shall meet the navigable clearance requirements of the highway bridge, strictly comply with navigation rules, and shall not moor or tie up under the highway bridge.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　重要的公路桥梁和公路隧道按照《中华人民共和国人民武装警察法》和国务院、中央军委的有关规定由中国人民武装警察部队守护。

**Article 24** Important highway bridges and highway tunnels shall be guarded by the Chinese People's Armed Police Force in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the People's Armed Police Force and the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　禁止损坏、擅自移动、涂改、遮挡公路附属设施或者利用公路附属设施架设管道、悬挂物品。

**Article 25** It is prohibited to damage, move without authorization, alter, or obscure highway auxiliary facilities, or to use highway auxiliary facilities to erect pipelines or hang articles.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　禁止破坏公路、公路用地范围内的绿化物。需要更新采伐护路林的，应当向公路管理机构提出申请，经批准方可更新采伐，并及时补种；不能及时补种的，应当交纳补种所需费用，由公路管理机构代为补种。

**Article 26** It is prohibited to destroy greenery within the scope of highways or highway land. If it is necessary to renew or cut road protection forests, an application shall be submitted to the highway management institution, and the renewal or cutting may only be carried out after approval, and replanting shall be done promptly; if replanting cannot be done promptly, the costs required for replanting shall be paid, and the highway management institution shall replant on behalf.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　进行下列涉路施工活动，建设单位应当向公路管理机构提出申请：  
(一)因修建铁路、机场、供电、水利、通信等建设工程需要占用、挖掘公路、公路用地或者使公路改线；  
(二)跨越、穿越公路修建桥梁、渡槽或者架设、埋设管道、电缆等设施；  
(三)在公路用地范围内架设、埋设管道、电缆等设施；  
(四)利用公路桥梁、公路隧道、涵洞铺设电缆等设施；  
(五)利用跨越公路的设施悬挂非公路标志；  
(六)在公路上增设或者改造平面交叉道口；  
(七)在公路建筑控制区内埋设管道、电缆等设施。

**Article 27** For the following road-related construction activities, the construction unit shall submit an application to the highway management institution:  
(1) occupying or excavating highways or highway land, or altering the alignment of highways, due to the need for construction projects such as railways, airports, power supply, water conservancy, communications, etc.;  
(2) constructing bridges, aqueducts or erecting or burying pipelines, cables and other facilities across or through highways;  
(3) erecting or burying pipelines, cables and other facilities within the scope of highway land;  
(4) using highway bridges, highway tunnels, and culverts to lay cables and other facilities;  
(5) using facilities that span highways to hang non-highway signs;  
(6) adding or modifying level intersections on highways;  
(7) burying pipelines, cables and other facilities within highway building control areas.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　申请进行涉路施工活动的建设单位应当向公路管理机构提交下列材料：  
(一)符合有关技术标准、规范要求的设计和施工方案；  
(二)保障公路、公路附属设施质量和安全的技术评价报告；  
(三)处置施工险情和意外事故的应急方案。  
公路管理机构应当自受理申请之日起20日内作出许可或者不予许可的决定；影响交通安全的，应当征得公安机关交通管理部门的同意；涉及经营性公路的，应当征求公路经营企业的意见；不予许可的，公路管理机构应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 28** The construction unit applying for road-related construction activities shall submit the following materials to the highway management institution:  
(1) design and construction plans that comply with the requirements of relevant technical standards and specifications;  
(2) a technical evaluation report ensuring the quality and safety of the highway and its auxiliary facilities;  
(3) an emergency plan for dealing with construction hazards and accidents.  
The highway management institution shall make a decision to grant or not grant permission within 20 days from the date of accepting the application; if traffic safety is affected, the consent of the traffic management department of the public security organ shall be obtained; if an operational highway is involved, the opinions of the highway operating enterprise shall be solicited; if permission is not granted, the highway management institution shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　建设单位应当按照许可的设计和施工方案进行施工作业，并落实保障公路、公路附属设施质量和安全的防护措施。  
涉路施工完毕，公路管理机构应当对公路、公路附属设施是否达到规定的技术标准以及施工是否符合保障公路、公路附属设施质量和安全的要求进行验收；影响交通安全的，还应当经公安机关交通管理部门验收。  
涉路工程设施的所有人、管理人应当加强维护和管理，确保工程设施不影响公路的完好、安全和畅通。

**Article 29** The construction unit shall carry out construction operations in accordance with the approved design and construction plans, and implement protective measures to ensure the quality and safety of the highway and its auxiliary facilities.  
Upon completion of the road-related construction, the highway management institution shall carry out acceptance inspection on whether the highway and its auxiliary facilities meet the prescribed technical standards and whether the construction meets the requirements for ensuring the quality and safety of the highway and its auxiliary facilities; if traffic safety is affected, the acceptance inspection shall also be carried out by the traffic management department of the public security organ.  
The owner and manager of the road-related engineering facilities shall strengthen maintenance and management to ensure that the engineering facilities do not affect the integrity, safety and smoothness of the highway.

### 第三章　公路通行

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　车辆的外廓尺寸、轴荷和总质量应当符合国家有关车辆外廓尺寸、轴荷、质量限值等机动车安全技术标准，不符合标准的不得生产、销售。

**Article 30** The overall dimensions, axle load and gross mass of a vehicle shall comply with the national motor vehicle safety technical standards concerning overall dimensions, axle load, mass limits, etc.; vehicles that do not meet the standards shall not be produced or sold.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　公安机关交通管理部门办理车辆登记，应当当场查验，对不符合机动车国家安全技术标准的车辆不予登记。

**Article 31** The traffic management department of the public security organ shall, when handling vehicle registration, inspect the vehicle on the spot, and shall not register vehicles that do not comply with the national safety technical standards for motor vehicles.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　运输不可解体物品需要改装车辆的，应当由具有相应资质的车辆生产企业按照规定的车型和技术参数进行改装。

**Article 32** If vehicle modification is necessary for the transport of indivisible articles, it shall be carried out by a vehicle manufacturer with corresponding qualifications in accordance with the prescribed vehicle model and technical parameters.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　超过公路、公路桥梁、公路隧道限载、限高、限宽、限长标准的车辆，不得在公路、公路桥梁或者公路隧道行驶；超过汽车渡船限载、限高、限宽、限长标准的车辆，不得使用汽车渡船。  
公路、公路桥梁、公路隧道限载、限高、限宽、限长标准调整的，公路管理机构、公路经营企业应当及时变更限载、限高、限宽、限长标志；需要绕行的，还应当标明绕行路线。

**Article 33** Vehicles exceeding the load limit, height limit, width limit or length limit of highways, highway bridges or highway tunnels shall not travel on such highways, bridges or tunnels; vehicles exceeding the load limit, height limit, width limit or length limit of automobile ferries shall not use such ferries.  
If the load limit, height limit, width limit or length limit standards of highways, highway bridges or highway tunnels are adjusted, the highway management institution and the highway operating enterprise shall promptly change the signs for such limits; if a detour is necessary, the detour route shall also be clearly indicated.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级人民政府交通运输主管部门或者乡级人民政府可以根据保护乡道、村道的需要，在乡道、村道的出入口设置必要的限高、限宽设施，但是不得影响消防和卫生急救等应急通行需要，不得向通行车辆收费。

**Article 34** The competent transport department of the county people's government or the township people's government may, based on the need to protect township and village highways, set up necessary height limit and width limit facilities at the entrances and exits of township and village highways, provided that they do not affect the emergency passage needs for fire fighting and medical first aid, and no charge shall be made to passing vehicles.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　车辆载运不可解体物品，车货总体的外廓尺寸或者总质量超过公路、公路桥梁、公路隧道的限载、限高、限宽、限长标准，确需在公路、公路桥梁、公路隧道行驶的，从事运输的单位和个人应当向公路管理机构申请公路超限运输许可。

**Article 35** If the overall dimensions or gross mass of the vehicle and cargo exceed the load limit, height limit, width limit or length limit of the highway, highway bridge or highway tunnel, and it is indeed necessary to travel on the highway, bridge or tunnel, the unit or individual engaged in transport shall apply to the highway management institution for a highway oversize/overweight transport permit.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　申请公路超限运输许可按照下列规定办理：  
(一)跨省、自治区、直辖市进行超限运输的，向公路沿线各省、自治区、直辖市公路管理机构提出申请，由起运地省、自治区、直辖市公路管理机构统一受理，并协调公路沿线各省、自治区、直辖市公路管理机构对超限运输申请进行审批，必要时可以由国务院交通运输主管部门统一协调处理；  
(二)在省、自治区范围内跨设区的市进行超限运输，或者在直辖市范围内跨区、县进行超限运输的，向省、自治区、直辖市公路管理机构提出申请，由省、自治区、直辖市公路管理机构受理并审批；  
(三)在设区的市范围内跨区、县进行超限运输的，向设区的市公路管理机构提出申请，由设区的市公路管理机构受理并审批；  
(四)在区、县范围内进行超限运输的，向区、县公路管理机构提出申请，由区、县公路管理机构受理并审批。  
公路超限运输影响交通安全的，公路管理机构在审批超限运输申请时，应当征求公安机关交通管理部门意见。

**Article 36** Applications for highway oversize/overweight transport permits shall be handled in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) For oversize/overweight transport across provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government, an application shall be submitted to the highway management institution of each province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government along the highway, and the highway management institution of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the transport originates shall accept the application uniformly and coordinate with the highway management institutions of other provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government along the highway to review and approve the application; if necessary, the competent transport department of the State Council may coordinate and handle it uniformly;  
(2) For oversize/overweight transport across cities divided into districts within a province or autonomous region, or across districts or counties within a municipality directly under the Central Government, an application shall be submitted to the highway management institution of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, which shall accept and approve the application;  
(3) For oversize/overweight transport across districts or counties within a city divided into districts, an application shall be submitted to the highway management institution of the city divided into districts, which shall accept and approve the application;  
(4) For oversize/overweight transport within a district or county, an application shall be submitted to the highway management institution of the district or county, which shall accept and approve the application.  
If an oversize/overweight transport affects traffic safety, the highway management institution shall, when reviewing and approving the application, seek the opinions of the traffic management department of the public security organ.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　公路管理机构审批超限运输申请，应当根据实际情况勘测通行路线，需要采取加固、改造措施的，可以与申请人签订有关协议，制定相应的加固、改造方案。  
公路管理机构应当根据其制定的加固、改造方案，对通行的公路桥梁、涵洞等设施进行加固、改造；必要时应当对超限运输车辆进行监管。

**Article 37** When reviewing and approving an oversize/overweight transport application, the highway management institution shall survey the passage route based on the actual circumstances, and if reinforcement or modification measures are necessary, it may sign a relevant agreement with the applicant and formulate a corresponding reinforcement or modification plan.  
The highway management institution shall reinforce or modify the highway bridges, culverts and other facilities on the passage route according to the reinforcement or modification plan formulated by it; if necessary, it shall supervise the oversize/overweight transport vehicles.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　公路管理机构批准超限运输申请的，应当为超限运输车辆配发国务院交通运输主管部门规定式样的超限运输车辆通行证。  
经批准进行超限运输的车辆，应当随车携带超限运输车辆通行证，按照指定的时间、路线和速度行驶，并悬挂明显标志。  
禁止租借、转让超限运输车辆通行证。禁止使用伪造、变造的超限运输车辆通行证。

**Article 38** If the highway management institution approves an oversize/overweight transport application, it shall issue to the oversize/overweight transport vehicle a permit in the form prescribed by the competent transport department of the State Council.  
A vehicle approved for oversize/overweight transport shall carry the permit in the vehicle, travel at the designated time, along the designated route and at the designated speed, and display conspicuous signs.  
It is prohibited to lend or transfer the permit. It is prohibited to use a forged or altered permit.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，有关交通运输主管部门可以设立固定超限检测站点，配备必要的设备和人员。  
固定超限检测站点应当规范执法，并公布监督电话。公路管理机构应当加强对固定超限检测站点的管理。

**Article 39** With the approval of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, the relevant competent transport department may set up fixed oversize/overweight inspection stations and equip them with necessary equipment and personnel.  
Fixed oversize/overweight inspection stations shall enforce the law in a standardized manner and publish a supervision telephone number. The highway management institution shall strengthen the management of fixed oversize/overweight inspection stations.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　公路管理机构在监督检查中发现车辆超过公路、公路桥梁、公路隧道或者汽车渡船的限载、限高、限宽、限长标准的，应当就近引导至固定超限检测站点进行处理。  
车辆应当按照超限检测指示标志或者公路管理机构监督检查人员的指挥接受超限检测，不得故意堵塞固定超限检测站点通行车道、强行通过固定超限检测站点或者以其他方式扰乱超限检测秩序，不得采取短途驳载等方式逃避超限检测。  
禁止通过引路绕行等方式为不符合国家有关载运标准的车辆逃避超限检测提供便利。

**Article 40** If, during supervision and inspection, the highway management institution discovers that a vehicle exceeds the load limit, height limit, width limit or length limit of a highway, highway bridge, highway tunnel or automobile ferry, it shall guide the vehicle to a nearby fixed oversize/overweight inspection station for handling.  
Vehicles shall submit to oversize/overweight inspection in accordance with the inspection indication signs or the instructions of the highway management institution's supervision and inspection personnel; they shall not intentionally block the traffic lanes of fixed oversize/overweight inspection stations, force their way through fixed oversize/overweight inspection stations or otherwise disrupt the inspection order; they shall not take measures such as short-distance transshipment to evade inspection.  
It is prohibited to provide convenience for vehicles that do not meet the relevant national carriage standards to evade oversize/overweight inspection by means such as guiding them on detours.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　煤炭、水泥等货物集散地以及货运站等场所的经营人、管理人应当采取有效措施，防止不符合国家有关载运标准的车辆出场(站)。  
道路运输管理机构应当加强对煤炭、水泥等货物集散地以及货运站等场所的监督检查，制止不符合国家有关载运标准的车辆出场(站)。  
任何单位和个人不得指使、强令车辆驾驶人超限运输货物，不得阻碍道路运输管理机构依法进行监督检查。

**Article 41** Operators and managers of coal, cement and other goods distribution centers, as well as freight stations and other places, shall take effective measures to prevent vehicles that do not meet the relevant national carriage standards from leaving the premises (stations).  
The road transport administration institution shall strengthen supervision and inspection over coal, cement and other goods distribution centers, freight stations and other places, and stop vehicles that do not meet the relevant national carriage standards from leaving the premises (stations).  
No unit or individual may instigate or compel a vehicle driver to transport goods in an oversize/overweight manner, or obstruct the road transport administration institution from carrying out supervision and inspection according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　载运易燃、易爆、剧毒、放射性等危险物品的车辆，应当符合国家有关安全管理规定，并避免通过特大型公路桥梁或者特长公路隧道；确需通过特大型公路桥梁或者特长公路隧道的，负责审批易燃、易爆、剧毒、放射性等危险物品运输许可的机关应当提前将行驶时间、路线通知特大型公路桥梁或者特长公路隧道的管理单位，并对在特大型公路桥梁或者特长公路隧道行驶的车辆进行现场监管。

**Article 42** Vehicles carrying dangerous articles such as flammable, explosive, highly toxic or radioactive substances shall comply with the relevant national safety management provisions and avoid passing through extra-large highway bridges or extra-long highway tunnels; if it is indeed necessary to pass through such bridges or tunnels, the authority responsible for approving permits for the transport of such dangerous articles shall notify the management unit of the extra-large bridge or extra-long tunnel in advance of the travel time and route, and shall carry out on-site supervision over the vehicles traveling on such bridges or tunnels.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　车辆应当规范装载，装载物不得触地拖行。车辆装载物易掉落、遗洒或者飘散的，应当采取厢式密闭等有效防护措施方可在公路上行驶。  
公路上行驶车辆的装载物掉落、遗洒或者飘散的，车辆驾驶人、押运人员应当及时采取措施处理；无法处理的，应当在掉落、遗洒或者飘散物来车方向适当距离外设置警示标志，并迅速报告公路管理机构或者公安机关交通管理部门。其他人员发现公路上有影响交通安全的障碍物的，也应当及时报告公路管理机构或者公安机关交通管理部门。公安机关交通管理部门应当责令改正车辆装载物掉落、遗洒、飘散等违法行为；公路管理机构、公路经营企业应当及时清除掉落、遗洒、飘散在公路上的障碍物。  
车辆装载物掉落、遗洒、飘散后，车辆驾驶人、押运人员未及时采取措施处理，造成他人人身、财产损害的，道路运输企业、车辆驾驶人应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 43** Vehicles shall be loaded in a standardized manner, and the loaded materials shall not drag on the ground. If the loaded materials are prone to fall, leak or scatter, effective protective measures such as enclosed compartment shall be taken before the vehicle may travel on the highway.  
If the loaded materials fall, leak or scatter from a vehicle traveling on the highway, the driver and the escort shall promptly take measures to deal with it; if it cannot be dealt with, they shall set up warning signs at an appropriate distance from the direction of oncoming traffic from the fallen, leaked or scattered materials, and promptly report to the highway management institution or the traffic management department of the public security organ. Other persons who discover obstacles affecting traffic safety on the highway shall also promptly report to the highway management institution or the traffic management department of the public security organ. The traffic management department of the public security organ shall order the correction of the illegal act of falling, leaking or scattering of loaded materials; the highway management institution and the highway operating enterprise shall promptly remove the obstacles fallen, leaked or scattered on the highway.  
If the driver and escort fail to take timely measures to deal with the fallen, leaked or scattered loaded materials, causing personal injury or property damage to others, the road transport enterprise and the vehicle driver shall bear compensation liability according to law.

### 第四章　公路养护

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　公路管理机构、公路经营企业应当加强公路养护，保证公路经常处于良好技术状态。  
前款所称良好技术状态，是指公路自身的物理状态符合有关技术标准的要求，包括路面平整，路肩、边坡平顺，有关设施完好。

**Article 44** The highway management institution and highway operating enterprises shall strengthen highway maintenance to ensure that the highway is constantly in good technical condition.  
The term 'good technical condition' as used in the preceding paragraph means that the physical condition of the highway itself meets the requirements of relevant technical standards, including smooth pavement, even road shoulders and slopes, and intact relevant facilities.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　公路养护应当按照国务院交通运输主管部门规定的技术规范和操作规程实施作业。

**Article 45** Highway maintenance operations shall be carried out in accordance with the technical specifications and operating procedures prescribed by the competent transport department of the State Council.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　从事公路养护作业的单位应当具备下列资质条件：  
(一)有一定数量的符合要求的技术人员；  
(二)有与公路养护作业相适应的技术设备；  
(三)有与公路养护作业相适应的作业经历；  
(四)国务院交通运输主管部门规定的其他条件。  
公路养护作业单位资质管理办法由国务院交通运输主管部门另行制定。

**Article 46** Units engaged in highway maintenance operations shall meet the following qualification requirements:  
(1) having a certain number of qualified technical personnel;  
(2) having technical equipment commensurate with the highway maintenance operations;  
(3) having operational experience commensurate with the highway maintenance operations;  
(4) other conditions prescribed by the competent transport department of the State Council.  
The administrative measures for qualifications of highway maintenance operating units shall be separately formulated by the competent transport department of the State Council.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　公路管理机构、公路经营企业应当按照国务院交通运输主管部门的规定对公路进行巡查，并制作巡查记录；发现公路坍塌、坑槽、隆起等损毁的，应当及时设置警示标志，并采取措施修复。  
公安机关交通管理部门发现公路坍塌、坑槽、隆起等损毁，危及交通安全的，应当及时采取措施，疏导交通，并通知公路管理机构或者公路经营企业。  
其他人员发现公路坍塌、坑槽、隆起等损毁的，应当及时向公路管理机构、公安机关交通管理部门报告。

**Article 47** The highway management institution and highway operating enterprises shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent transport department of the State Council, conduct patrol inspections of highways and make patrol records; if they discover any damages such as collapse, potholes or bulging of the highway, they shall promptly set up warning signs and take measures to repair them.  
If the traffic management department of the public security organ discovers any damages such as collapse, potholes or bulging of the highway that endanger traffic safety, it shall promptly take measures to divert traffic and notify the highway management institution or the highway operating enterprise.  
Other persons who discover any damages such as collapse, potholes or bulging of the highway shall promptly report to the highway management institution or the traffic management department of the public security organ.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　公路管理机构、公路经营企业应当定期对公路、公路桥梁、公路隧道进行检测和评定，保证其技术状态符合有关技术标准；对经检测发现不符合车辆通行安全要求的，应当进行维修，及时向社会公告，并通知公安机关交通管理部门。

**Article 48** The highway management institution and highway operating enterprises shall regularly carry out testing and assessment of highways, highway bridges and highway tunnels to ensure that their technical conditions comply with relevant technical standards; if any conditions failing to meet the safety requirements for vehicle passage are found through testing, they shall carry out repairs, promptly make a public announcement, and notify the traffic management department of the public security organ.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　公路管理机构、公路经营企业应当定期检查公路隧道的排水、通风、照明、监控、报警、消防、救助等设施，保持设施处于完好状态。

**Article 49** The highway management institution and highway operating enterprises shall regularly inspect the drainage, ventilation, lighting, monitoring, alarm, fire fighting, rescue and other facilities of highway tunnels, and keep the facilities in good condition.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　公路管理机构应当统筹安排公路养护作业计划，避免集中进行公路养护作业造成交通堵塞。  
在省、自治区、直辖市交界区域进行公路养护作业，可能造成交通堵塞的，有关公路管理机构、公安机关交通管理部门应当事先书面通报相邻的省、自治区、直辖市公路管理机构、公安机关交通管理部门，共同制定疏导预案，确定分流路线。

**Article 50** The highway management institution shall make overall arrangements for highway maintenance operation plans to avoid traffic congestion caused by concentrated highway maintenance operations.  
If highway maintenance operations in the border areas of provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government may cause traffic congestion, the relevant highway management institution and traffic management department of the public security organ shall notify in writing in advance the highway management institutions and traffic management departments of the public security organs of the neighboring provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government, and jointly formulate diversion plans and determine detour routes.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　公路养护作业需要封闭公路的，或者占用半幅公路进行作业，作业路段长度在2公里以上，并且作业期限超过30日的，除紧急情况外，公路养护作业单位应当在作业开始之日前5日向社会公告，明确绕行路线，并在绕行处设置标志；不能绕行的，应当修建临时道路。

**Article 51** If highway maintenance operations require the closure of a highway, or the occupation of half of the highway for operations with a road section length of more than 2 kilometers and an operation period exceeding 30 days, except in emergency situations, the highway maintenance operation unit shall make a public announcement 5 days before the start of the operations, clearly indicate the detour route, and set up signs at the detour point; if a detour is not possible, a temporary road shall be constructed.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　公路养护作业人员作业时，应当穿着统一的安全标志服。公路养护车辆、机械设备作业时，应当设置明显的作业标志，开启危险报警闪光灯。

**Article 52** Highway maintenance operation personnel shall wear uniform safety marking clothing when working. When highway maintenance vehicles and mechanical equipment are operating, conspicuous operation signs shall be set up and hazard warning flashers shall be turned on.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　发生公路突发事件影响通行的，公路管理机构、公路经营企业应当及时修复公路、恢复通行。设区的市级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门应当根据修复公路、恢复通行的需要，及时调集抢修力量，统筹安排有关作业计划，下达路网调度指令，配合有关部门组织绕行、分流。  
设区的市级以上公路管理机构应当按照国务院交通运输主管部门的规定收集、汇总公路损毁、公路交通流量等信息，开展公路突发事件的监测、预报和预警工作，并利用多种方式及时向社会发布有关公路运行信息。

**Article 53** If a highway emergency occurs affecting passage, the highway management institution and the highway operating enterprise shall promptly repair the highway and restore passage. The competent transport departments of the people's governments at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall, in accordance with the need to repair the highway and restore passage, promptly mobilize emergency repair forces, make overall arrangements for relevant operation plans, issue road network dispatching instructions, and coordinate with relevant departments to organize detours and diversions.  
The highway management institutions at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent transport department of the State Council, collect and summarize information on highway damage, highway traffic flow, etc., carry out monitoring, forecasting and early warning of highway emergencies, and promptly release relevant highway operation information to the public through multiple means.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　中国人民武装警察交通部队按照国家有关规定承担公路、公路桥梁、公路隧道等设施的抢修任务。

**Article 54** The Chinese People's Armed Police Traffic Forces shall, in accordance with relevant State regulations, undertake the emergency repair tasks for highways, highway bridges, highway tunnels and other facilities.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　公路永久性停止使用的，应当按照国务院交通运输主管部门规定的程序核准后作报废处理，并向社会公告。  
公路报废后的土地使用管理依照有关土地管理的法律、行政法规执行。

**Article 55** If a highway is permanently discontinued from use, it shall be approved as scrap and disposed of as abandoned after approval in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the competent transport department of the State Council, and a public announcement shall be made.  
The land use management after the scrapping of the highway shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations on land management.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由公路管理机构责令限期拆除，可以处5万元以下的罚款。逾期不拆除的，由公路管理机构拆除，有关费用由违法行为人承担：  
(一)在公路建筑控制区内修建、扩建建筑物、地面构筑物或者未经许可埋设管道、电缆等设施的；  
(二)在公路建筑控制区外修建的建筑物、地面构筑物以及其他设施遮挡公路标志或者妨碍安全视距的。

**Article 56** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, under any of the following circumstances, the highway management institution shall order removal within a prescribed time limit and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan. If the removal is not carried out within the time limit, the highway management institution shall carry out the removal, and the relevant expenses shall be borne by the violator:  
(1) constructing or expanding buildings or surface structures within a highway building control area, or burying pipelines, cables or other facilities without permission;  
(2) buildings, surface structures or other facilities constructed outside the highway building control area that obstruct highway signs or hinder safe sight distance.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反[本条例第十八条](#art-18)、第十九条、第二十三条规定的，由安全生产监督管理部门、水行政主管部门、流域管理机构、海事管理机构等有关单位依法处理。

**Article 57** In violation of the provisions of Articles 18, 19 and 23 of these Regulations, the safety production supervision and administration department, water administrative department, river basin management institution, maritime administration institution and other relevant units shall handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反[本条例第二十条](#art-20)规定的，由水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构责令改正，可以处3万元以下的罚款。

**Article 58** In violation of the provisions of Article 20 of these Regulations, the water administrative department or the river basin management institution shall order correction and may impose a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反[本条例第二十二条](#art-22)规定的，由公路管理机构责令改正，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 59** In violation of the provisions of Article 22 of these Regulations, the highway management institution shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由公路管理机构责令改正，可以处3万元以下的罚款：  
(一)损坏、擅自移动、涂改、遮挡公路附属设施或者利用公路附属设施架设管道、悬挂物品，可能危及公路安全的；  
(二)涉路工程设施影响公路完好、安全和畅通的。

**Article 60** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, under any of the following circumstances, the highway management institution shall order correction and may impose a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan:  
(1) damaging, moving without authorization, altering or obscuring highway auxiliary facilities, or using highway auxiliary facilities to erect pipelines or hang articles, which may endanger highway safety;  
(2) road-related engineering facilities affecting the integrity, safety and smoothness of the highway.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反本条例的规定，未经批准更新采伐护路林的，由公路管理机构责令补种，没收违法所得，并处采伐林木价值3倍以上5倍以下的罚款。

**Article 61** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if road protection forests are renewed or cut without approval, the highway management institution shall order replanting, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the value of the cut trees.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反本条例的规定，未经许可进行[本条例第二十七条](#art-27)第一项至第五项规定的涉路施工活动的，由公路管理机构责令改正，可以处3万元以下的罚款；未经许可进行[本条例第二十七条](#art-27)第六项规定的涉路施工活动的，由公路管理机构责令改正，处5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 62** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a road-related construction activity specified in items (1) to (5) of Article 27 of these Regulations is carried out without permission, the highway management institution shall order correction and may impose a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan; if a road-related construction activity specified in item (6) of Article 27 of these Regulations is carried out without permission, the highway management institution shall order correction and impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本条例的规定，非法生产、销售外廓尺寸、轴荷、总质量不符合国家有关车辆外廓尺寸、轴荷、质量限值等机动车安全技术标准的车辆的，依照《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法》的有关规定处罚。  
具有国家规定资质的车辆生产企业未按照规定车型和技术参数改装车辆的，由原发证机关责令改正，处4万元以上20万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，吊销其资质证书。

**Article 63** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if vehicles are illegally produced or sold with overall dimensions, axle load or gross mass that do not comply with the relevant national motor vehicle safety technical standards concerning overall dimensions, axle load, mass limits, etc., the punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Road Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If a vehicle manufacturer with qualifications prescribed by the State fails to modify vehicles in accordance with the prescribed vehicle model and technical parameters, the original issuing authority shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 40,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if it refuses to correct, its qualification certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反本条例的规定，在公路上行驶的车辆，车货总体的外廓尺寸、轴荷或者总质量超过公路、公路桥梁、公路隧道、汽车渡船限定标准的，由公路管理机构责令改正，可以处3万元以下的罚款。

**Article 64** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a vehicle traveling on a highway, the overall dimensions, axle load or gross mass of the vehicle and cargo exceed the prescribed limits of highways, highway bridges, highway tunnels or automobile ferries, the highway management institution shall order correction and may impose a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本条例的规定，经批准进行超限运输的车辆，未按照指定时间、路线和速度行驶的，由公路管理机构或者公安机关交通管理部门责令改正；拒不改正的，公路管理机构或者公安机关交通管理部门可以扣留车辆。  
未随车携带超限运输车辆通行证的，由公路管理机构扣留车辆，责令车辆驾驶人提供超限运输车辆通行证或者相应的证明。  
租借、转让超限运输车辆通行证的，由公路管理机构没收超限运输车辆通行证，处1000元以上5000元以下的罚款。使用伪造、变造的超限运输车辆通行证的，由公路管理机构没收伪造、变造的超限运输车辆通行证，处3万元以下的罚款。

**Article 65** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a vehicle approved for oversize/overweight transport fails to travel at the designated time, along the designated route and at the designated speed, the highway management institution or the traffic management department of the public security organ shall order correction; if it refuses to correct, the highway management institution or the traffic management department of the public security organ may detain the vehicle.  
If the oversize/overweight transport permit is not carried in the vehicle, the highway management institution shall detain the vehicle and order the driver to provide the permit or corresponding evidence.  
If the oversize/overweight transport permit is lent or transferred, the highway management institution shall confiscate the permit and impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan. If a forged or altered permit is used, the highway management institution shall confiscate the forged or altered permit and impose a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　对1年内违法超限运输超过3次的货运车辆，由道路运输管理机构吊销其车辆营运证；对1年内违法超限运输超过3次的货运车辆驾驶人，由道路运输管理机构责令其停止从事营业性运输；道路运输企业1年内违法超限运输的货运车辆超过本单位货运车辆总数10%的，由道路运输管理机构责令道路运输企业停业整顿；情节严重的，吊销其道路运输经营许可证，并向社会公告。

**Article 66** For a freight vehicle that has been involved in illegal oversize/overweight transport more than three times within one year, the road transport administration institution shall revoke its vehicle operation certificate; for a driver of a freight vehicle who has been involved in illegal oversize/overweight transport more than three times within one year, the road transport administration institution shall order the driver to cease engaging in commercial transport; if the freight vehicles of a road transport enterprise involved in illegal oversize/overweight transport within one year account for more than 10% of the total freight vehicles of the enterprise, the road transport administration institution shall order the enterprise to suspend operations for rectification; if the circumstances are serious, the enterprise's road transport operation license shall be revoked and a public announcement shall be made.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由公路管理机构强制拖离或者扣留车辆，处3万元以下的罚款：  
(一)采取故意堵塞固定超限检测站点通行车道、强行通过固定超限检测站点等方式扰乱超限检测秩序的；  
(二)采取短途驳载等方式逃避超限检测的。

**Article 67** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, under any of the following circumstances, the highway management institution shall forcibly tow away or detain the vehicle, and impose a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan:  
(1) disrupting the order of oversize/overweight inspection by means such as intentionally blocking the traffic lanes of a fixed oversize/overweight inspection station, forcing through a fixed oversize/overweight inspection station, etc.;  
(2) evading oversize/overweight inspection by means such as short-distance transshipment.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　违反本条例的规定，指使、强令车辆驾驶人超限运输货物的，由道路运输管理机构责令改正，处3万元以下的罚款。

**Article 68** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a person instigates or compels a vehicle driver to transport goods in an oversize/overweight manner, the road transport administration institution shall order correction and impose a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　车辆装载物触地拖行、掉落、遗洒或者飘散，造成公路路面损坏、污染的，由公路管理机构责令改正，处5000元以下的罚款。

**Article 69** If the loaded materials of a vehicle drag on the ground, fall, leak or scatter, causing damage or pollution to the highway pavement, the highway management institution shall order correction and impose a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　违反本条例的规定，公路养护作业单位未按照国务院交通运输主管部门规定的技术规范和操作规程进行公路养护作业的，由公路管理机构责令改正，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，吊销其资质证书。

**Article 70** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a highway maintenance operation unit fails to carry out highway maintenance operations in accordance with the technical specifications and operating procedures prescribed by the competent transport department of the State Council, the highway management institution shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if it refuses to correct, its qualification certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　造成公路、公路附属设施损坏的单位和个人应当立即报告公路管理机构，接受公路管理机构的现场调查处理；危及交通安全的，还应当设置警示标志或者采取其他安全防护措施，并迅速报告公安机关交通管理部门。  
发生交通事故造成公路、公路附属设施损坏的，公安机关交通管理部门在处理交通事故时应当及时通知有关公路管理机构到场调查处理。

**Article 71** Units and individuals that cause damage to highways or highway auxiliary facilities shall immediately report to the highway management institution and accept on-site investigation and handling by the highway management institution; if traffic safety is endangered, they shall also set up warning signs or take other safety protection measures, and promptly report to the traffic management department of the public security organ.  
If a traffic accident causes damage to highways or highway auxiliary facilities, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall, when handling the traffic accident, promptly notify the relevant highway management institution to appear on site for investigation and handling.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　造成公路、公路附属设施损坏，拒不接受公路管理机构现场调查处理的，公路管理机构可以扣留车辆、工具。  
公路管理机构扣留车辆、工具的，应当当场出具凭证，并告知当事人在规定期限内到公路管理机构接受处理。逾期不接受处理，并且经公告3个月仍不来接受处理的，对扣留的车辆、工具，由公路管理机构依法处理。  
公路管理机构对被扣留的车辆、工具应当妥善保管，不得使用。

**Article 72** If a person causes damage to highways or highway auxiliary facilities and refuses to accept on-site investigation and handling by the highway management institution, the highway management institution may detain the vehicle or tools.  
If the highway management institution detains a vehicle or tools, it shall issue a receipt on the spot and inform the party to appear at the highway management institution for handling within a prescribed time limit. If the party fails to appear for handling within the time limit and still fails to appear after a public announcement for three months, the detained vehicle or tools shall be disposed of by the highway management institution according to law.  
The highway management institution shall properly keep the detained vehicle or tools and shall not use them.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　违反本条例的规定，公路管理机构工作人员有下列行为之一的，依法给予处分：  
(一)违法实施行政许可的；  
(二)违反规定拦截、检查正常行驶的车辆的；  
(三)未及时采取措施处理公路坍塌、坑槽、隆起等损毁的；  
(四)违法扣留车辆、工具或者使用依法扣留的车辆、工具的；  
(五)有其他玩忽职守、徇私舞弊、滥用职权行为的。  
公路管理机构有前款所列行为之一的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 73** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a staff member of the highway management institution commits any of the following acts, he or she shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) illegally implementing administrative licensing;  
(2) intercepting or inspecting normally traveling vehicles in violation of regulations;  
(3) failing to take timely measures to deal with damages such as collapse, potholes or bulging of highways;  
(4) illegally detaining vehicles or tools, or using vehicles or tools detained according to law;  
(5) engaging in other acts of dereliction of duty, engaging in malpractice for personal gain, or abusing power.  
If the highway management institution commits any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　违反本条例的规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 74** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　村道的管理和养护工作，由乡级人民政府参照本条例的规定执行。  
专用公路的保护不适用本条例。

**Article 75** The administration and maintenance of village highways shall be carried out by the township people's government with reference to the provisions of these Regulations.  
These Regulations shall not apply to the protection of highways for special use.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　军事运输使用公路按照国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 76** The use of highways for military transport shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　本条例自2011年7月1日起施行。1987年10月13日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国公路管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 77** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 2011. The Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Highway Administration promulgated by the State Council on October 13, 1987 shall be abolished simultaneously.
