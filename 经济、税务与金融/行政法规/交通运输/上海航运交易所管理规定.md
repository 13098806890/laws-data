# 上海航运交易所管理规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1996-10-03  
**生效日期**：1996-10-03

> (1996年9月19日经国务院批准　1996年10月3日中华人民共和国交通部令第8号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对上海航运交易所的管理，维护航运交易秩序，促进水路货物运输业的发展，制定本规定。 第二条　上海航运交易所(以下简称航运交易所)是不以营利为目的，为航运业务提供交易场所、设施、信息的事业法人。 第三条　国务院交通主管部门和上海市人民政府，依照本规定对航运交易所进行管理和监督。 第四条　航运交易所应当提供公开、公平、公正的交易环境和便利的交易条件，保证航运交易的正常进行。 第二章　组织机构 第五条　航运交易所实行理事会领导下的总裁负责制。 第六条　理事会是航运交易所的权力机构。 理事会由理事长一人、副理事长一至二人和理事五至七人组成，总人数为单数。理事会应当有二至三名会员理事。 理事长、副理事长由上海市人民政府和国务院交通主管部门共同任命。理事由理事长提名，上海市人民政府批准。 第七条　理事会行使下列职权： (一)制定航运交易所章程、交易规则及其他有关规定； (二)审议航运交易所的工作计划和工作报告； (三)决定、处理航运交易所运作中的重大问题；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对上海航运交易所的管理，维护航运交易秩序，促进水路货物运输业的发展，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated to strengthen the administration of Shanghai Shipping Exchange, maintain the order of shipping transactions, and promote the development of waterway cargo transportation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　上海航运交易所(以下简称航运交易所)是不以营利为目的，为航运业务提供交易场所、设施、信息的事业法人。

**Article 2** Shanghai Shipping Exchange (hereinafter referred to as the Shipping Exchange) is a public institution legal person that, without the purpose of profit-making, provides trading venues, facilities, and information for shipping business.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院交通主管部门和上海市人民政府，依照本规定对航运交易所进行管理和监督。

**Article 3** The competent transportation department under the State Council and the Shanghai Municipal People's Government shall administer and supervise the Shipping Exchange in accordance with these Provisions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　航运交易所应当提供公开、公平、公正的交易环境和便利的交易条件，保证航运交易的正常进行。

**Article 4** The Shipping Exchange shall provide an open, fair, and just trading environment and convenient trading conditions to ensure the normal operation of shipping transactions.

### 第二章　组织机构

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　航运交易所实行理事会领导下的总裁负责制。

**Article 5** The Shipping Exchange shall implement a system of president responsibility under the leadership of the Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　理事会是航运交易所的权力机构。  
理事会由理事长一人、副理事长一至二人和理事五至七人组成，总人数为单数。理事会应当有二至三名会员理事。  
理事长、副理事长由上海市人民政府和国务院交通主管部门共同任命。理事由理事长提名，上海市人民政府批准。

**Article 6** The Council is the power organ of the Shipping Exchange.  
The Council shall be composed of one chairperson, one or two vice chairpersons, and five to seven council members, with the total number being an odd number. The Council shall have two to three member council members.  
The chairperson and vice chairpersons shall be jointly appointed by the Shanghai Municipal People's Government and the competent transportation department under the State Council. Council members shall be nominated by the chairperson and approved by the Shanghai Municipal People's Government.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　理事会行使下列职权：  
(一)制定航运交易所章程、交易规则及其他有关规定；  
(二)审议航运交易所的工作计划和工作报告；  
(三)决定、处理航运交易所运作中的重大问题；  
(四)聘任、解聘航运交易所总裁、副总裁；  
(五)决定取消会员资格。

**Article 7** The Council shall exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) To formulate the articles of association, trading rules, and other relevant provisions of the Shipping Exchange;  
(2) To review the work plans and work reports of the Shipping Exchange;  
(3) To decide and handle major issues in the operation of the Shipping Exchange;  
(4) To appoint and dismiss the president and vice presidents of the Shipping Exchange;  
(5) To decide on the cancellation of membership.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　理事会会议由理事长召集；理事长因故不能召集会议时，由其指定的副理事长代为召集。  
理事会会议的决议必须经理事会2/3以上成员表决同意。

**Article 8** Meetings of the Council shall be convened by the chairperson; if the chairperson is unable to convene a meeting due to any reason, the vice chairperson designated by the chairperson shall convene the meeting on his or her behalf.  
Resolutions at Council meetings must be approved by a vote of more than two-thirds of the members of the Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　航运交易所设总裁一人、副总裁若干人。  
总裁由理事长从理事中提名，理事会聘任。副总裁由总裁提名，理事会聘任。  
总裁是航运交易所的法定代表人，主持航运交易所的日常工作，对理事会负责。

**Article 9** The Shipping Exchange shall have one president and several vice presidents.  
The president shall be nominated by the chairperson from among the council members and appointed by the Council. The vice presidents shall be nominated by the president and appointed by the Council.  
The president is the legal representative of the Shipping Exchange, shall preside over the daily work of the Shipping Exchange, and shall be responsible to the Council.

### 第三章　会员

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　取得会员资格，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)具有中国法人资格的经营水路货物运输业务、水路货物运输代理业务、船舶代理业务和港口业务的企业及其他有关企业；  
(二)遵守航运交易所章程；  
(三)有良好的商业信誉。  
外国企业申请取得会员资格的条件，由国务院交通主管部门另行规定。

**Article 10** To obtain membership, the following conditions must be met:  
(1) The enterprise shall be qualified as a Chinese legal person engaged in waterway cargo transportation business, waterway cargo transportation agency business, ship agency business, and port business, and other relevant enterprises;  
(2) To abide by the articles of association of the Shipping Exchange;  
(3) To have good commercial reputation.  
The conditions for foreign enterprises applying for membership shall be separately prescribed by the competent transportation department under the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请取得会员资格，应当经航运交易所审查批准，并按照航运交易所章程办理入会手续。  
会员退会，应当按照航运交易所章程办理退会手续。

**Article 11** An application for membership shall be examined and approved by the Shipping Exchange, and the admission procedures shall be handled in accordance with the articles of association of the Shipping Exchange.  
A member withdrawing from membership shall handle the withdrawal procedures in accordance with the articles of association of the Shipping Exchange.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　会员分为正式会员和临时会员。  
会员资格期限三个月以上的为正式会员，会员资格期限不超过三个月的为临时会员。

**Article 12** Members are divided into formal members and temporary members.  
Those whose membership period exceeds three months are formal members, and those whose membership period does not exceed three months are temporary members.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　会员享有下列权利：  
(一)对航运交易所的工作提出批评和建议；  
(二)推荐会员理事和被推荐为会员理事；  
(三)在场内进行交易；  
(四)使用航运交易所提供的设施；  
(五)获取航运交易所提供的信息。  
前款第(二)项规定不适用于临时会员。

**Article 13** Members shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) To raise criticisms and suggestions regarding the work of the Shipping Exchange;  
(2) To recommend member council members and be recommended as a member council member;  
(3) To conduct transactions on the floor;  
(4) To use the facilities provided by the Shipping Exchange;  
(5) To obtain information provided by the Shipping Exchange.  
The provision of subparagraph (2) of the preceding paragraph shall not apply to temporary members.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　会员应当履行下列义务：  
(一)遵守航运交易所章程和交易规则；  
(二)按时缴纳会费；  
(三)如实向航运交易所提供本企业与航运交易有关的价格及其他信息；  
(四)不得向新闻媒介及公众泄露或者传播从航运交易所获取的航运信息；  
(五)在航运交易中接受航运交易所的管理和监督。  
前款第(三)项规定的信息，不包括会员的商业秘密。

**Article 14** Members shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) To abide by the articles of association and trading rules of the Shipping Exchange;  
(2) To pay membership fees on time;  
(3) To truthfully provide the Shipping Exchange with prices and other information related to the shipping transactions of the enterprise;  
(4) Not to disclose or disseminate to the news media or the public the shipping information obtained from the Shipping Exchange;  
(5) To accept the administration and supervision of the Shipping Exchange in shipping transactions.  
The information specified in subparagraph (3) of the preceding paragraph shall not include the trade secrets of members.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　会员可以通过会员会议对航运交易所的工作提出批评和建议。  
经2/3以上的会员提议，可以召开会员会议。

**Article 15** Members may raise criticisms and suggestions regarding the work of the Shipping Exchange through member meetings.  
Upon the proposal of more than two-thirds of the members, a member meeting may be convened.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　会员在航运交易所内从事交易的人员为出市代表，出市代表须经航运交易所培训。

**Article 16** The personnel of members who engage in transactions within the Shipping Exchange shall be floor representatives, and floor representatives must be trained by the Shipping Exchange.

### 第四章　交易管理

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　航运交易所的交易范围：  
(一)水路货物运输；  
(二)港口业务；  
(三)船舶租赁；  
(四)船舶买卖；  
(五)国务院交通主管部门允许进场交易的其他航运业务。

**Article 17** The scope of transactions of the Shipping Exchange is as follows:  
(1) Waterway cargo transportation;  
(2) Port business;  
(3) Ship chartering;  
(4) Ship sale and purchase;  
(5) Other shipping businesses permitted by the competent transportation department under the State Council to be traded on the floor.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　会员的出市代表，只能代表本企业进行交易；但是，从事水路货物运输代理业务、船舶代理业务的会员除外。

**Article 18** Floor representatives of members may only represent their own enterprises in transactions; however, members engaged in waterway cargo transportation agency business or ship agency business are excepted.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　非会员企业可以委托从事水路货物运输代理或者船舶代理业务的会员代表本企业进场交易。

**Article 19** Non-member enterprises may entrust members engaged in waterway cargo transportation agency or ship agency business to represent their enterprises in floor transactions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　会员不得有下列行为：  
(一)利用内幕信息进行交易或者利用从航运交易所获取的信息进行场外交易；  
(二)制造或者散布虚假信息或者以其他手段扰乱交易秩序；  
(三)超越经营范围交易；  
(四)法律、法规禁止的其他行为。

**Article 20** Members shall not engage in the following acts:  
(1) To trade using inside information or to trade off the floor using information obtained from the Shipping Exchange;  
(2) To create or disseminate false information or to disrupt trading order by other means;  
(3) To trade beyond the scope of business;  
(4) Other acts prohibited by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　以竞价方式进行交易和以协商方式进行船舶买卖交易的，交易双方应当向航运交易所提供信用保证。

**Article 21** Where transactions are conducted through competitive bidding or ship sale and purchase transactions are conducted through negotiation, both parties to the transaction shall provide credit guarantees to the Shipping Exchange.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　从事国际班轮运输的会员应当将本企业的运价报航运交易所备案。

**Article 22** Members engaged in international liner shipping shall report their enterprise's freight rates to the Shipping Exchange for record.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　交易双方成交后，应当向航运交易所缴纳手续费。  
手续费的标准由航运交易所提出方案，报上海市人民政府交通主管部门会同物价主管部门批准。

**Article 23** After a transaction is concluded, both parties shall pay handling fees to the Shipping Exchange.  
The standard for handling fees shall be proposed by the Shipping Exchange and submitted to the competent transportation department of the Shanghai Municipal People's Government, which shall approve it jointly with the competent price department.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　除国家另有规定外，航运交易所内的交易价格由交易双方自主决定。  
航运交易所可以制定交易指导价格；在交易价格暴涨或者暴跌时，航运交易所可以限定最高价或者最低价，必要时可以宣布暂停交易。

**Article 24** Unless otherwise provided by the State, the transaction price within the Shipping Exchange shall be independently determined by the parties to the transaction.  
The Shipping Exchange may formulate guiding transaction prices; when transaction prices soar or plummet, the Shipping Exchange may set maximum or minimum prices, and may declare a suspension of trading when necessary.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　航运交易所应当及时、准确地向会员发布航运交易信息。

**Article 25** The Shipping Exchange shall promptly and accurately release shipping transaction information to members.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　航运交易所工作人员应当廉洁自律、奉公守法，不得以任何方式从航运交易所会员或者其他有关单位获取利益，不得泄露内幕信息。

**Article 26** Staff members of the Shipping Exchange shall be honest and self-disciplined, abide by the law, shall not obtain benefits from members of the Shipping Exchange or other relevant units in any manner, and shall not disclose inside information.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　因突发事件导致航运交易所停市时，理事会应当及时向上海市人民政府和国务院交通主管部门报告。

**Article 27** When a sudden event causes the suspension of trading at the Shipping Exchange, the Council shall promptly report to the Shanghai Municipal People's Government and the competent transportation department under the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　会员和出市代表违反本规定、航运交易所章程和交易规则的，航运交易所有权责令当事人停止违法行为，并可以视情节给予警告、暂停进场交易或者取消会员资格的处理。

**Article 28** If a member or floor representative violates these Provisions, the articles of association of the Shipping Exchange, or the trading rules, the Shipping Exchange shall have the authority to order the party to cease the illegal act, and may, depending on the circumstances, impose a warning, suspend floor trading, or cancel membership.

### 第五章　争议处理与法律责任

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　交易双方在交易过程中发生争议，可以自行协商解决或者申请航运交易所调解，也可以依照有关法律、行政法规的规定申请仲裁或者提起诉讼。

**Article 29** If a dispute arises between the parties during a transaction, they may resolve it through negotiation or apply to the Shipping Exchange for mediation, or may apply for arbitration or initiate litigation in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　会员有[本规定第二十条](#art-20)第(一)、(二)项所列行为之一的，由上海市人民政府交通主管部门根据不同情况给予警告、没收违法所得；情节严重的，并处2万元以下的罚款。  
会员有[本规定第二十条](#art-20)第(三)、(四)项所列行为之一的，由有关部门依照有关法律、法规予以处罚。

**Article 30** If a member commits any of the acts listed in subparagraphs (1) and (2) of Article 20 of these Provisions, the competent transportation department of the Shanghai Municipal People's Government shall, depending on the circumstances, give a warning and confiscate the illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan shall also be imposed.  
If a member commits any of the acts listed in subparagraphs (3) and (4) of Article 20 of these Provisions, the relevant department shall impose a penalty in accordance with relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　违反[本规定第二十二条](#art-22)规定，不将运价报备案或者不执行报备案的运价的，由上海市人民政府交通主管部门根据情节给予警告、责令停开航线，处5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 31** If a party violates the provisions of Article 22 of these Provisions by failing to report freight rates for record or failing to implement the reported freight rates, the competent transportation department of the Shanghai Municipal People's Government shall, depending on the circumstances, give a warning, order the suspension of the shipping line, and impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　航运交易所违反本规定，接收不符合会员条件的企业入会的，随意开除会员的，或者明知会员超经营范围从事交易而不予制止的，由国务院交通主管部门给予警告；情节严重的，处2万元以下的罚款。

**Article 32** If the Shipping Exchange violates these Provisions by accepting enterprises that do not meet the membership conditions, arbitrarily expelling members, or failing to stop members from engaging in transactions beyond the scope of business while knowing the circumstances, the competent transportation department under the State Council shall give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　航运交易所违反[本规定第十七条](#art-17)规定，擅自扩大交易范围的，由国务院交通主管部门给予警告；情节严重的，处5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 33** If the Shipping Exchange violates the provisions of Article 17 of these Provisions by arbitrarily expanding the scope of transactions, the competent transportation department under the State Council shall give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　航运交易所违反[本规定第二十七条](#art-27)规定，停市时不报告的，由国务院交通主管部门处2万元以下的罚款。

**Article 34** If the Shipping Exchange violates the provisions of Article 27 of these Provisions by failing to report when trading is suspended, the competent transportation department under the State Council shall impose a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　航运交易所违反本规定，给会员造成经济损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 35** If the Shipping Exchange violates these Provisions and causes economic losses to members, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 36** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
