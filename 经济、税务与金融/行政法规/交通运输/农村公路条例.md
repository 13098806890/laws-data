# 农村公路条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2025-07-16  
**生效日期**：2025-09-15

> 国家鼓励通过慈善和社会捐助以及综合开发运营农村公路相关资源、权益等方式筹集农村公路发展资金，支持金融机构按照市场化、法治化原则提供与农村公路发展相适应的金融服务，引导社会资本依法参与农村公路发展。 第七条　农村公路建设应当以提升路网质量为重点，推动农村公路路网提档升级、优化结构。 农村公路路网密度不足的偏远山区、边疆地区等，应当根据实际需要有序推进路网延伸，提高路网通达水平。 第八条　新建农村公路应当符合规定的公路技术等级要求。 现有农村公路不符合最低技术等级要求的，应当升级改造为符合技术等级要求的公路。县级人民政府应当根据本地区实际情况组织实施农村公路升级改造。 农村公路建设选用的具体技术等级及其主要技术指标，应当根据其功能定位，结合地形条件、预计交通流量等因素确定。国务院交通运输主管部门应当健全与农村公路特点相适应的技术等级及其技术指标体系。 第九条　农村公路建设应当与国道、省道建设衔接协调，促进城乡交通运输一体化。 地方各级人民政府应当采取措施，推进农村公路与沿线配套设施、产业园区、旅游景区等一体化建设，统筹农业生产、乡村旅游等需求，促进农村公路建设与乡村产业发展有机融合。 鼓励在农村公路建设中推广以工代赈方式。 第十条　农村公路建设涉及土地和空间使用的合理需求纳入国土空间规划实施监督系统统筹保障，按照国家有关规定落实用地管理要求。 农村公路建设应当依据国土空间规划科学合理选址，优先利用现有道路改建或者扩建，不占或者少占耕地，节约集约用地。 第十一条　对符合投资规模较小、技术方案简单等条件的农村公路建设项目，可以按照国家有关规定简化相关程序。 第十二条　村道两侧的建筑控制区的范围由县级人民政府按照省、自治区、直辖市的规定划定，建筑控制区管理依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。 县道、乡道两侧的建筑控制区依照有关法律、行政法规的规定划定范围并实行管理。 第十三条　县级人民政府交通运输主管部门应当依法采取有效措施，加大对农村公路超限运输行为的治理力度，防止超限运输车辆违法在农村公路上行驶。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　农村公路建设应当以提升路网质量为重点，推动农村公路路网提档升级、优化结构。  
农村公路路网密度不足的偏远山区、边疆地区等，应当根据实际需要有序推进路网延伸，提高路网通达水平。

**Article 7** Rural road construction shall focus on improving the quality of the road network, promoting the upgrading and optimization of the rural road network structure.  
In remote mountainous areas, border areas, and other regions where the density of the rural road network is insufficient, the extension of the road network shall be promoted in an orderly manner according to actual needs to enhance the level of road network accessibility.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　新建农村公路应当符合规定的公路技术等级要求。  
现有农村公路不符合最低技术等级要求的，应当升级改造为符合技术等级要求的公路。县级人民政府应当根据本地区实际情况组织实施农村公路升级改造。  
农村公路建设选用的具体技术等级及其主要技术指标，应当根据其功能定位，结合地形条件、预计交通流量等因素确定。国务院交通运输主管部门应当健全与农村公路特点相适应的技术等级及其技术指标体系。

**Article 8** Newly built rural roads shall meet the prescribed technical grade requirements for highways.  
Where existing rural roads do not meet the minimum technical grade requirements, they shall be upgraded and reconstructed to conform to the technical grade requirements. The county people's government shall, based on the actual conditions of its region, organize and implement the upgrading and reconstruction of rural roads.  
The specific technical grade and its main technical indicators selected for rural road construction shall be determined according to the functional positioning, taking into account factors such as terrain conditions and expected traffic volume. The competent transportation department of the State Council shall improve the technical grades and the technical indicator system that are compatible with the characteristics of rural roads.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　农村公路建设应当与国道、省道建设衔接协调，促进城乡交通运输一体化。  
地方各级人民政府应当采取措施，推进农村公路与沿线配套设施、产业园区、旅游景区等一体化建设，统筹农业生产、乡村旅游等需求，促进农村公路建设与乡村产业发展有机融合。  
鼓励在农村公路建设中推广以工代赈方式。

**Article 9** Rural road construction shall be connected and coordinated with the construction of national and provincial highways to promote the integration of urban and rural transportation.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall take measures to promote the integrated construction of rural roads with supporting facilities along the route, industrial parks, tourist attractions, etc., coordinate the needs of agricultural production, rural tourism, etc., and promote the organic integration of rural road construction with the development of rural industries.  
The adoption of the work-relief approach in rural road construction is encouraged.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　农村公路建设涉及土地和空间使用的合理需求纳入国土空间规划实施监督系统统筹保障，按照国家有关规定落实用地管理要求。  
农村公路建设应当依据国土空间规划科学合理选址，优先利用现有道路改建或者扩建，不占或者少占耕地，节约集约用地。

**Article 10** Reasonable demands for land and space use involved in rural road construction shall be incorporated into the territorial space planning implementation supervision system for overall guarantee, and land use management requirements shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Rural road construction shall, based on the territorial space planning, select sites scientifically and rationally, give priority to the renovation or expansion of existing roads, avoid or minimize the occupation of arable land, and use land in a conservation and intensive manner.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对符合投资规模较小、技术方案简单等条件的农村公路建设项目，可以按照国家有关规定简化相关程序。

**Article 11** For rural road construction projects that meet conditions such as a relatively small investment scale and simple technical schemes, the relevant procedures may be simplified in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　村道两侧的建筑控制区的范围由县级人民政府按照省、自治区、直辖市的规定划定，建筑控制区管理依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。  
县道、乡道两侧的建筑控制区依照有关法律、行政法规的规定划定范围并实行管理。

**Article 12** The scope of the building control zone on both sides of a village road shall be delimited by the county people's government in accordance with the provisions of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The management of the building control zone shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
The building control zone on both sides of a county road and a township road shall be delimited and managed in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级人民政府交通运输主管部门应当依法采取有效措施，加大对农村公路超限运输行为的治理力度，防止超限运输车辆违法在农村公路上行驶。  
不属于超限运输的建设工程重型载货汽车确需将农村公路作为施工便道或者在一定时期内集中通行农村公路，可能造成农村公路损坏的，建设单位应当与县级人民政府交通运输主管部门或者乡镇人民政府签订协议，采取合理规划行车线路、控制车辆载荷、加固改造通行路段等防护措施；造成损坏的，应当按照不低于该段公路原有的技术标准及时予以修复、改建或者给予相应的经济补偿。

**Article 13** The competent transportation department of the county people's government shall, according to law, take effective measures to strengthen the control of over-limit transport on rural roads and prevent over-limit transport vehicles from illegally traveling on rural roads.  
Where a heavy-duty truck for construction projects that does not fall under over-limit transport indeed needs to use a rural road as a construction access road or intensively travel on a rural road within a certain period, which may cause damage to the rural road, the construction unit shall sign an agreement with the competent transportation department of the county people's government or the township people's government, and take protective measures such as rationally planning the driving route, controlling the vehicle load, and reinforcing and reconstructing the traveled sections; if damage is caused, the construction unit shall promptly repair or reconstruct the road in accordance with a standard not lower than the original technical standard of that road section, or provide corresponding economic compensation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　根据农村公路保护管理需要，县级人民政府交通运输主管部门可以在农村公路沿线设置相应的电子技术监控设备。设置电子技术监控设备应当经过法制和技术审核，确保电子技术监控设备符合标准、设置合理、标志明显，投入使用前设置地点应当及时向社会公布。

**Article 14** Based on the needs of rural road protection and management, the competent transportation department of the county people's government may install corresponding electronic technical monitoring equipment along rural roads. The installation of electronic technical monitoring equipment shall undergo legal and technical review to ensure that the equipment meets standards, is reasonably located, and has prominent signs. Before being put into use, the locations of the installations shall be promptly announced to the public.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级人民政府应当组织交通运输等有关部门、乡镇人民政府依法采取措施，开展农村公路路域环境综合整治，持续优化农村公路路域环境。

**Article 15** The county people's government shall organize the relevant departments such as transportation and the township people's government to take measures according to law, carry out comprehensive remediation of the rural road roadside environment, and continuously optimize the rural road roadside environment.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　县级人民政府应当建立健全农村公路管理养护责任制，明确并支持、督促交通运输等有关部门、乡镇人民政府履行农村公路管理养护职责。  
地方各级人民政府应当按照国家有关规定，落实农村公路养护资金财政支出责任，加强农村公路资产管理。农村公路与乡村旅游等经营性项目一体化开发的收益可以用于农村公路养护。

**Article 16** The county people's government shall establish and improve the responsibility system for the management and maintenance of rural roads, specify and support, supervise the competent transportation department and other relevant departments and the township people's government in performing their duties of rural road management and maintenance.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, fulfill the fiscal expenditure responsibilities for rural road maintenance funds and strengthen the management of rural road assets. The proceeds from the integrated development of rural roads and commercial projects such as rural tourism may be used for rural road maintenance.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级人民政府交通运输主管部门应当定期组织开展农村公路技术状况检测和评定，并将检测和评定结果作为实施农村公路养护的重要依据。

**Article 17** The competent department of transportation of the people's government at the county level shall regularly organize the detection and assessment of the technical condition of rural highways, and shall take the detection and assessment results as an important basis for implementing rural highway maintenance.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　农村公路养护按作业性质分为日常养护和养护工程。  
县道的养护由县级人民政府交通运输主管部门组织实施。  
乡道、村道的日常养护由乡镇人民政府组织实施。乡道、村道的养护工程由县级人民政府交通运输主管部门组织实施，或者交由具备条件的乡镇人民政府组织实施。

**Article 18** Rural highway maintenance is divided into routine maintenance and maintenance engineering according to the nature of operations.  
Maintenance of county roads shall be organized and implemented by the competent department of transportation of the people's government at the county level.  
Routine maintenance of township roads and village roads shall be organized and implemented by the people's government of the township (town). Maintenance engineering of township roads and village roads shall be organized and implemented by the competent department of transportation of the people's government at the county level, or be assigned to the people's government of the township (town) that meets the conditions for implementation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　农村公路的日常养护可以通过政府购买服务等方式吸纳沿线农村居民参与，保洁、绿化等相关工作可以由农村居民或者家庭承包，统筹用好农村公路管理养护领域公益性岗位，优先吸纳就业困难人员。  
国家鼓励、引导专业公路养护作业单位参与农村公路养护，推动建立健全农村公路养护长效机制，提高养护专业化水平。

**Article 19** Routine maintenance of rural highways may absorb the participation of rural residents along the route through means such as government purchase of services, and cleaning, greening and other related work may be contracted by rural residents or families, and public welfare positions in the field of rural highway management and maintenance shall be comprehensively utilized, with priority given to absorbing persons with employment difficulties.  
The State encourages and guides professional highway maintenance units to participate in rural highway maintenance, promotes the establishment and improvement of a long-term mechanism for rural highway maintenance, and improves the professional level of maintenance.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　农村公路养护应当按照相应的技术规范和操作规程实施，保证公路经常处于良好的技术状态。

**Article 20** Rural highway maintenance shall be carried out in accordance with the corresponding technical specifications and operating procedures, ensuring that the highway is constantly in good technical condition.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级人民政府交通运输主管部门、乡镇人民政府应当组织开展农村公路日常巡查；发现公路坍塌或者有坑槽、隆起等影响安全通行情形的，应当及时设置警示标志并组织修复。

**Article 21** The competent department of transportation of the people's government at the county level and the people's government of the township (town) shall organize and carry out routine inspections of rural highways; where a collapse or potholes, bulges or other circumstances affecting safe passage are discovered, they shall promptly set up warning signs and organize repairs.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级人民政府应当建立健全农村公路安全隐患排查治理机制，以农村公路急弯陡坡、临水临崖、平交路口、桥梁隧道、穿村过镇等路段为重点，组织开展安全隐患排查和治理。  
交通事故易发的农村公路平交路口、穿村过镇路段等地点应当设置醒目的安全警示标志，必要时设置车辆减速装置等设施。

**Article 22** The people's government at the county level shall establish and improve a mechanism for investigating and rectifying potential safety hazards of rural highways, focusing on sections such as sharp bends and steep slopes, areas adjacent to water and cliffs, level crossings, bridges and tunnels, and sections passing through villages and towns, and organize the investigation and rectification of potential safety hazards.  
At locations such as level crossings and sections passing through villages and towns of rural highways where traffic accidents are prone to occur, conspicuous safety warning signs shall be set up, and where necessary, facilities such as vehicle deceleration devices shall be installed.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　县级人民政府应当加强农村公路应急保障和防灾抗灾能力建设，组织落实农村公路突发事件应急预案制定、应急物资储备、灾害监测预警等方面的责任。  
因自然灾害或者其他突发事件造成农村公路中断的，县级人民政府应当组织交通运输等有关部门及时进行抢通；难以及时抢通的，应当设立警示标志并发布绕行路线。

**Article 23** The people's government at the county level shall strengthen the construction of emergency support and disaster prevention and resistance capabilities for rural highways, and organize the implementation of responsibilities in aspects such as the formulation of emergency plans for rural highway emergencies, storage of emergency supplies, and disaster monitoring and early warning.  
Where rural highways are interrupted due to natural disasters or other emergencies, the people's government at the county level shall organize the relevant departments such as transportation to promptly carry out emergency repair; where prompt emergency repair is difficult, warning signs shall be set up and detour routes shall be published.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　县级人民政府及其有关部门、乡镇人民政府应当采取灵活有效的方式，对农村公路沿线居民开展道路交通安全法律法规和爱路护路宣传教育，鼓励、引导将爱路护路有关要求纳入村规民约，增强农村居民的道路交通安全和爱路护路意识。

**Article 24** The people's government at the county level and its relevant departments, and the people's government of the township (town) shall adopt flexible and effective methods to carry out publicity and education on road traffic safety laws and regulations and on loving and protecting roads among residents along rural highways, encourage and guide the incorporation of relevant requirements for loving and protecting roads into village rules and conventions, and enhance the awareness of road traffic safety and loving and protecting roads among rural residents.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　地方各级人民政府应当完善政策措施，健全激励机制，加快农村货运物流等相关基础设施建设，保障农村客运可持续运营，促进农村客运、货运物流、邮政快递融合发展，拓展农村公路服务功能，提升农村公路服务畅通城乡经济循环的能力。

**Article 25** Local people's governments at all levels shall improve policies and measures, perfect incentive mechanisms, accelerate the construction of relevant infrastructure such as rural freight logistics, ensure the sustainable operation of rural passenger transport, promote the integrated development of rural passenger transport, freight logistics, and postal express delivery, expand the service functions of rural highways, and enhance the capacity of rural highway services to smooth the urban-rural economic cycle.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国务院有关部门、县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门、乡镇人民政府在农村公路工作中不履行或者不正确履行职责的，依照法律法规和国家有关规定追究责任，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 26** Where the relevant departments of the State Council, the local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments, and the people's governments of the townships (towns) fail to perform or fail to correctly perform their duties in rural highway work, they shall be held accountable in accordance with laws, regulations, and the relevant provisions of the State, and the person directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　村道除适用本条例规定外，参照适用《中华人民共和国公路法》、《公路安全保护条例》的有关规定。

**Article 27** In addition to the application of the provisions of these Regulations, village roads shall apply mutatis mutandis the relevant provisions of the Highway Law of the People's Republic of China and the Regulations on the Protection of Highway Safety.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本条例自2025年9月15日起施行。

**Article 28** These Regulations shall come into force on September 15, 2025.
