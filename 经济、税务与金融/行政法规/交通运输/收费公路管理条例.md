# 收费公路管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2004-09-13  
**生效日期**：2004-11-01

> (2004年8月18日国务院第61次常务会议通过　2004年9月13日中华人民共和国国务院令第417号公布　自2004年11月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对收费公路的管理，规范公路收费行为，维护收费公路的经营管理者和使用者的合法权益，促进公路事业的发展，根据《中华人民共和国公路法》(以下简称公路法)，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称收费公路，是指符合公路法和本条例规定，经批准依法收取车辆通行费的公路(含桥梁和隧道)。 第三条　各级人民政府应当采取积极措施，支持、促进公路事业的发展。公路发展应当坚持非收费公路为主，适当发展收费公路。 第四条　全部由政府投资或者社会组织、个人捐资建设的公路，不得收取车辆通行费。 第五条　任何单位或者个人不得违反公路法和本条例的规定，在公路上设站(卡)收取车辆通行费。 第六条　对在公路上非法设立收费站(卡)收取车辆通行费的，任何单位和个人都有权拒绝交纳。 任何单位或者个人对在公路上非法设立收费站(卡)、非法收取或者使用车辆通行费、非法转让收费公路权益或者非法延长收费期限等行为，都有权向交通、价格、财政等部门举报。收到举报的部门应当按照职责分工依法及时查处；无权查处的，应当及时移送有权查处的部门。受理的部门必须自收到举报或者移送材料之日起10日内进行查处。 第七条　收费公路的经营管理者，经依法批准有权向通行收费公路的车辆收取车辆通行费。 军队车辆、武警部队车辆，公安机关在辖区内收费公路上处理交通事故、执行正常巡逻任务和处置突发事件的统一标志的制式警车，以及经国务院交通主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准执行抢险救灾任务的车辆，免交车辆通行费。 进行跨区作业的联合收割机、运输联合收割机(包括插秧机)的车辆，免交车辆通行费。联合收割机不得在高速公路上通行。 第八条　任何单位或者个人不得以任何形式非法干预收费公路的经营管理，挤占、挪用收费公路经营管理者依法收取的车辆通行费。 第二章　收费公路建设和收费站的设置 第九条　建设收费公路，应当符合国家和省、自治区、直辖市公路发展规划，符合本条例规定的收费公路的技术等级和规模。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对收费公路的管理，规范公路收费行为，维护收费公路的经营管理者和使用者的合法权益，促进公路事业的发展，根据《中华人民共和国公路法》(以下简称公路法)，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Highway Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Highway Law) for the purposes of strengthening the administration of toll roads, regulating the toll collection activities on highways, protecting the lawful rights and interests of the operators and users of toll roads, and promoting the development of the highway industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称收费公路，是指符合公路法和本条例规定，经批准依法收取车辆通行费的公路(含桥梁和隧道)。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "toll road" means a highway (including bridges and tunnels) that meets the requirements of the Highway Law and these Regulations and is approved to collect vehicle tolls according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　各级人民政府应当采取积极措施，支持、促进公路事业的发展。公路发展应当坚持非收费公路为主，适当发展收费公路。

**Article 3** People's governments at all levels shall take active measures to support and promote the development of the highway industry. Highway development shall adhere to the principle of giving priority to non-toll roads while appropriately developing toll roads.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　全部由政府投资或者社会组织、个人捐资建设的公路，不得收取车辆通行费。

**Article 4** No vehicle tolls may be collected on highways wholly invested by the government or donated by social organizations or individuals.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　任何单位或者个人不得违反公路法和本条例的规定，在公路上设站(卡)收取车辆通行费。

**Article 5** No unit or individual may, in violation of the provisions of the Highway Law and these Regulations, set up a station (checkpoint) on a highway to collect vehicle tolls.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　对在公路上非法设立收费站(卡)收取车辆通行费的，任何单位和个人都有权拒绝交纳。  
任何单位或者个人对在公路上非法设立收费站(卡)、非法收取或者使用车辆通行费、非法转让收费公路权益或者非法延长收费期限等行为，都有权向交通、价格、财政等部门举报。收到举报的部门应当按照职责分工依法及时查处；无权查处的，应当及时移送有权查处的部门。受理的部门必须自收到举报或者移送材料之日起10日内进行查处。

**Article 6** Any unit or individual shall have the right to refuse to pay vehicle tolls collected at illegally set up toll stations (checkpoints) on highways.  
Any unit or individual shall have the right to report to the competent departments of transport, pricing, finance, and others regarding any act of illegally setting up toll stations (checkpoints) on highways, illegally collecting or using vehicle tolls, illegally transferring toll road rights, or illegally extending the toll period. The department receiving the report shall investigate and deal with the matter in a timely manner according to law based on its functions and duties; if it has no authority to handle the matter, it shall promptly transfer the case to the department with the authority. The department that accepts the case must complete the investigation and disposition within 10 days from the date of receiving the report or the transferred materials.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　收费公路的经营管理者，经依法批准有权向通行收费公路的车辆收取车辆通行费。  
军队车辆、武警部队车辆，公安机关在辖区内收费公路上处理交通事故、执行正常巡逻任务和处置突发事件的统一标志的制式警车，以及经国务院交通主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准执行抢险救灾任务的车辆，免交车辆通行费。  
进行跨区作业的联合收割机、运输联合收割机(包括插秧机)的车辆，免交车辆通行费。联合收割机不得在高速公路上通行。

**Article 7** The operator of a toll road shall, upon approval according to law, have the right to collect vehicle tolls from vehicles passing through the toll road.  
Vehicles of the armed forces, vehicles of the armed police forces, standard police vehicles with unified markings of the public security organs used for handling traffic accidents, performing routine patrols, and responding to emergencies on toll roads within their jurisdiction, and vehicles approved by the competent transport department of the State Council or the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to perform disaster relief and emergency rescue tasks shall be exempted from vehicle tolls.  
Combine harvesters engaged in cross-region operations and vehicles transporting combine harvesters (including rice transplanters) shall be exempted from vehicle tolls. Combine harvesters shall not travel on expressways.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　任何单位或者个人不得以任何形式非法干预收费公路的经营管理，挤占、挪用收费公路经营管理者依法收取的车辆通行费。

**Article 8** No unit or individual may illegally interfere in the operation and management of a toll road in any form, or misappropriate or divert the vehicle tolls lawfully collected by the operator of the toll road.

### 第二章　收费公路建设和收费站的设置

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　建设收费公路，应当符合国家和省、自治区、直辖市公路发展规划，符合本条例规定的收费公路的技术等级和规模。

**Article 9** The construction of toll roads shall conform to the national and provincial (autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government) highway development plans, and meet the technical grades and scales for toll roads as prescribed by these Regulations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上地方人民政府交通主管部门利用贷款或者向企业、个人有偿集资建设的公路(以下简称政府还贷公路)，国内外经济组织投资建设或者依照公路法的规定受让政府还贷公路收费权的公路(以下简称经营性公路)，经依法批准后，方可收取车辆通行费。

**Article 10** A highway constructed by the competent transport department of a local people's government at or above the county level with loans or paid funds raised from enterprises or individuals (hereinafter referred to as a government-financed repayment highway), and a highway invested and constructed by domestic or foreign economic organizations or whose toll collection right for a government-financed repayment highway is transferred in accordance with the provisions of the Highway Law (hereinafter referred to as a commercial highway), may collect vehicle tolls only after being approved according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　建设和管理政府还贷公路，应当按照政事分开的原则，依法设立专门的不以营利为目的的法人组织。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门对本行政区域内的政府还贷公路，可以实行统一管理、统一贷款、统一还款。  
经营性公路建设项目应当向社会公布，采用招标投标方式选择投资者。  
经营性公路由依法成立的公路企业法人建设、经营和管理。

**Article 11** For the construction and management of government-financed repayment highways, a specialized non-profit legal person organization shall be established according to law in accordance with the principle of separation of government and enterprise functions.  
The competent transport department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may implement unified management, unified borrowing, and unified repayment for government-financed repayment highways within its administrative region.  
Commercial highway construction projects shall be made public to the community, and investors shall be selected through bidding.  
Commercial highways shall be constructed, operated, and managed by highway enterprise legal persons established according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　收费公路收费站的设置，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府按照下列规定审查批准：  
(一)高速公路以及其他封闭式的收费公路，除两端出入口外，不得在主线上设置收费站。但是，省、自治区、直辖市之间确需设置收费站的除外。  
(二)非封闭式的收费公路的同一主线上，相邻收费站的间距不得少于50公里。

**Article 12** The establishment of toll stations on toll roads shall be examined and approved by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) For expressways and other closed toll roads, no toll station may be set up on the main line except at the two entrance and exit points; however, this shall not apply where it is truly necessary to set up a toll station between provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
(2) For non-closed toll roads on the same main line, the distance between adjacent toll stations shall not be less than 50 kilometers.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　高速公路以及其他封闭式的收费公路，应当实行计算机联网收费，减少收费站点，提高通行效率。联网收费的具体办法由国务院交通主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 13** Expressways and other closed toll roads shall adopt a computer network toll collection system to reduce toll stations and increase traffic efficiency. The specific measures for network toll collection shall be formulated by the competent transport department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　收费公路的收费期限，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府按照下列标准审查批准：  
(一)政府还贷公路的收费期限，按照用收费偿还贷款、偿还有偿集资款的原则确定，最长不得超过15年。国家确定的中西部省、自治区、直辖市的政府还贷公路收费期限，最长不得超过20年。  
(二)经营性公路的收费期限，按照收回投资并有合理回报的原则确定，最长不得超过25年。国家确定的中西部省、自治区、直辖市的经营性公路收费期限，最长不得超过30年。

**Article 14** The toll period of a toll road shall be examined and approved by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the following standards:  
(1) The toll period for a government-financed repayment highway shall be determined based on the principle of using toll revenues to repay loans and paid funds raised, and shall not exceed 15 years at most. For government-financed repayment highways in the central and western provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government designated by the State, the maximum toll period shall not exceed 20 years.  
(2) The toll period for a commercial highway shall be determined based on the principle of recovering the investment and achieving a reasonable return, and shall not exceed 25 years at most. For commercial highways in the central and western provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government designated by the State, the maximum toll period shall not exceed 30 years.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　车辆通行费的收费标准，应当依照价格法律、行政法规的规定进行听证，并按照下列程序审查批准：  
(一)政府还贷公路的收费标准，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门会同同级价格主管部门、财政部门审核后，报本级人民政府审查批准。  
(二)经营性公路的收费标准，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门会同同级价格主管部门审核后，报本级人民政府审查批准。

**Article 15** The toll rate for vehicle passage shall be subject to a hearing in accordance with the provisions of price laws and administrative regulations, and shall be examined and approved through the following procedures:  
(1) The toll rate for a government-financed repayment highway shall be examined by the competent transport department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the competent price department and the finance department at the same level, and then submitted to the people's government at the same level for examination and approval.  
(2) The toll rate for a commercial highway shall be examined by the competent transport department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the competent price department at the same level, and then submitted to the people's government at the same level for examination and approval.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　车辆通行费的收费标准，应当根据公路的技术等级、投资总额、当地物价指数、偿还贷款或者有偿集资款的期限和收回投资的期限以及交通量等因素计算确定。对在国家规定的绿色通道上运输鲜活农产品的车辆，可以适当降低车辆通行费的收费标准或者免交车辆通行费。  
修建与收费公路经营管理无关的设施、超标准修建的收费公路经营管理设施和服务设施，其费用不得作为确定收费标准的因素。  
车辆通行费的收费标准需要调整的，应当依照[本条例第十五条](#art-15)规定的程序办理。

**Article 16** The toll rate for vehicle passage shall be calculated and determined based on factors such as the technical grade of the highway, total investment amount, local price index, the period for repaying loans or paid funds raised, the period for recovering investment, and traffic volume. For vehicles transporting fresh agricultural products on the green channel designated by the State, the toll rate may be appropriately reduced or the toll may be exempted.  
The costs of constructing facilities unrelated to the operation and management of a toll road, or constructing operation and management facilities and service facilities in excess of standards, shall not be considered as factors in determining the toll rate.  
Where the toll rate for vehicle passage needs to be adjusted, the procedures set forth in Article 15 of these Regulations shall be followed.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　依照本条例规定的程序审查批准的收费公路收费站、收费期限、车辆通行费收费标准或者收费标准的调整方案，审批机关应当自审查批准之日起10日内将有关文件向国务院交通主管部门和国务院价格主管部门备案；其中属于政府还贷公路的，还应当自审查批准之日起10日内向国务院财政部门备案。

**Article 17** For a toll road toll station, toll period, vehicle toll rate, or adjustment plan for the toll rate that has been examined and approved in accordance with the procedures prescribed in these Regulations, the approving authority shall, within 10 days from the date of examination and approval, file the relevant documents with the competent transport department of the State Council and the competent price department of the State Council; for a government-financed repayment highway, the approving authority shall also, within 10 days from the date of examination and approval, file the documents with the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　建设收费公路，应当符合下列技术等级和规模：  
(一)高速公路连续里程30公里以上。但是，城市市区至本地机场的高速公路除外。  
(二)一级公路连续里程50公里以上。  
(三)二车道的独立桥梁、隧道，长度800米以上；四车道的独立桥梁、隧道，长度500米以上。  
技术等级为二级以下(含二级)的公路不得收费。但是，在国家确定的中西部省、自治区、直辖市建设的二级公路，其连续里程60公里以上的，经依法批准，可以收取车辆通行费。

**Article 18** The construction of a toll road shall meet the following technical grades and scales:  
(1) The continuous mileage of an expressway shall be 30 kilometers or more, except for expressways from urban districts to local airports.  
(2) The continuous mileage of a Class I highway shall be 50 kilometers or more.  
(3) A two-lane independent bridge or tunnel shall have a length of 800 meters or more; a four-lane independent bridge or tunnel shall have a length of 500 meters or more.  
No toll may be collected on highways with a technical grade of Class II or below (including Class II). However, for Class II highways constructed in the central and western provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government designated by the State, if the continuous mileage is 60 kilometers or more, vehicle tolls may be collected upon approval according to law.

### 第三章　收费公路权益的转让

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　依照本条例的规定转让收费公路权益的，应当向社会公布，采用招标投标的方式，公平、公正、公开地选择经营管理者，并依法订立转让协议。

**Article 19** Where the rights and interests of a toll road are transferred in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the matter shall be made public to the community, the operator shall be selected through bidding in a fair, just and open manner, and a transfer agreement shall be concluded according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　收费公路的权益，包括收费权、广告经营权、服务设施经营权。  
转让收费公路权益的，应当依法保护投资者的合法利益。

**Article 20** The rights and interests of a toll road include the toll collection right, the right to operate advertisements, and the right to operate service facilities.  
Where the rights and interests of a toll road are transferred, the lawful interests of the investors shall be protected according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　转让政府还贷公路权益中的收费权，可以申请延长收费期限，但延长的期限不得超过5年。  
转让经营性公路权益中的收费权，不得延长收费期限。

**Article 21** Where the toll collection right in the rights and interests of a government-financed repayment highway is transferred, an application may be made to extend the toll period, but the extension shall not exceed five years.  
Where the toll collection right in the rights and interests of a commercial highway is transferred, the toll period may not be extended.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　有下列情形之一的，收费公路权益中的收费权不得转让：  
(一)长度小于1000米的二车道独立桥梁和隧道；  
(二)二级公路；  
(三)收费时间已超过批准收费期限2/3。

**Article 22** The toll collection right in the rights and interests of a toll road may not be transferred under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) A two-lane independent bridge or tunnel with a length of less than 1,000 meters;  
(2) A Class II highway;  
(3) The toll collection time has exceeded two-thirds of the approved toll period.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　转让政府还贷公路权益的收入，必须缴入国库，除用于偿还贷款和有偿集资款外，必须用于公路建设。

**Article 23** The income from the transfer of the rights and interests of a government-financed repayment highway must be turned over to the state treasury. Apart from being used to repay loans and paid funds raised, such income must be used for highway construction.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　收费公路权益转让的具体办法，由国务院交通主管部门会同国务院发展改革部门和财政部门制定。

**Article 24** The specific measures for the transfer of the rights and interests of toll roads shall be formulated by the competent transport department of the State Council jointly with the development and reform department of the State Council and the finance department of the State Council.

### 第四章　收费公路的经营管理

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　收费公路建成后，应当按照国家有关规定进行验收；验收合格的，方可收取车辆通行费。  
收费公路不得边建设边收费。

**Article 25** After a toll road is completed, it shall be inspected and accepted in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. Vehicle tolls may be collected only if it passes the inspection and acceptance.  
No toll may be collected on a toll road while it is under construction.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　收费公路经营管理者应当按照国家规定的标准和规范，对收费公路及沿线设施进行日常检查、维护，保证收费公路处于良好的技术状态，为通行车辆及人员提供优质服务。  
收费公路的养护应当严格按照工期施工、竣工，不得拖延工期，不得影响车辆安全通行。

**Article 26** The operator of a toll road shall, in accordance with the standards and specifications prescribed by the State, conduct daily inspections and maintenance of the toll road and its roadside facilities to ensure that the toll road is in good technical condition and provide quality services to passing vehicles and persons.  
The maintenance of a toll road shall be constructed and completed strictly in accordance with the construction schedule, and the schedule may not be delayed, nor may it affect the safe passage of vehicles.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　收费公路经营管理者应当在收费站的显著位置，设置载有收费站名称、审批机关、收费单位、收费标准、收费起止年限和监督电话等内容的公告牌，接受社会监督。

**Article 27** The operator of a toll road shall set up a bulletin board at a conspicuous location at the toll station, containing information such as the name of the toll station, the approving authority, the toll collection entity, the toll rate, the start and end years of toll collection, and the supervision telephone number, to accept public supervision.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　收费公路经营管理者应当按照国家规定的标准，结合公路交通状况、沿线设施等情况，设置交通标志、标线。  
交通标志、标线必须清晰、准确、易于识别。重要的通行信息应当重复提示。

**Article 28** The operator of a toll road shall set up traffic signs and markings in accordance with the standards prescribed by the State, taking into account the traffic conditions and roadside facilities.  
Traffic signs and markings must be clear, accurate, and easy to identify. Important traffic information shall be repeatedly indicated.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　收费道口的设置，应当符合车辆行驶安全的要求；收费道口的数量，应当符合车辆快速通过的需要，不得造成车辆堵塞。

**Article 29** The setting of toll lanes shall meet the requirements for safe vehicle movement; the number of toll lanes shall meet the need for vehicles to pass through quickly and shall not cause traffic congestion.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　收费站工作人员的配备，应当与收费道口的数量、车流量相适应，不得随意增加人员。  
收费公路经营管理者应当加强对收费站工作人员的业务培训和职业道德教育，收费人员应当做到文明礼貌，规范服务。

**Article 30** The staffing of toll station personnel shall be commensurate with the number of toll lanes and traffic volume, and personnel shall not be increased arbitrarily.  
The operator of a toll road shall strengthen the professional training and professional ethics education of toll station personnel. Toll collectors shall be civil and polite and provide standardized services.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　遇有公路损坏、施工或者发生交通事故等影响车辆正常安全行驶的情形时，收费公路经营管理者应当在现场设置安全防护设施，并在收费公路出入口进行限速、警示提示，或者利用收费公路沿线可变信息板等设施予以公告；造成交通堵塞时，应当及时报告有关部门并协助疏导交通。  
遇有公路严重损毁、恶劣气象条件或者重大交通事故等严重影响车辆安全通行的情形时，公安机关应当根据情况，依法采取限速通行、关闭公路等交通管制措施。收费公路经营管理者应当积极配合公安机关，及时将有关交通管制的信息向通行车辆进行提示。

**Article 31** Where a situation such as highway damage, construction, or a traffic accident occurs that affects the normal and safe driving of vehicles, the operator of the toll road shall set up safety protection facilities at the scene, and post speed limit and warning signs at the entrances and exits of the toll road, or use variable message signs along the toll road to provide notice; if traffic congestion is caused, the operator shall promptly report to the relevant departments and assist in traffic diversion.  
Where a situation such as severe highway damage, adverse weather conditions, or a major traffic accident seriously affects the safe passage of vehicles, the public security organ shall, based on the circumstances, take traffic control measures such as speed restriction or highway closure according to law. The operator of the toll road shall actively cooperate with the public security organ and promptly inform passing vehicles of the relevant traffic control information.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　收费公路经营管理者收取车辆通行费，必须向收费公路使用者开具收费票据。政府还贷公路的收费票据，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府财政部门统一印(监)制。经营性公路的收费票据，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府税务部门统一印(监)制。

**Article 32** When collecting vehicle tolls, the operator of a toll road must issue a toll receipt to the user of the toll road. The toll receipts for government-financed repayment highways shall be uniformly printed (or supervised) by the finance department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The toll receipts for commercial highways shall be uniformly printed (or supervised) by the tax department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　收费公路经营管理者对依法应当交纳而拒交、逃交、少交车辆通行费的车辆，有权拒绝其通行，并要求其补交应交纳的车辆通行费。  
任何人不得为拒交、逃交、少交车辆通行费而故意堵塞收费道口、强行冲卡、殴打收费公路管理人员、破坏收费设施或者从事其他扰乱收费公路经营管理秩序的活动。  
发生前款规定的扰乱收费公路经营管理秩序行为时，收费公路经营管理者应当及时报告公安机关，由公安机关依法予以处理。

**Article 33** The operator of a toll road shall have the right to refuse passage to vehicles that, being lawfully liable to pay vehicle tolls, refuse to pay, evade payment, or underpay vehicle tolls, and shall have the right to demand that they make up the vehicle tolls payable.  
No person may intentionally block toll lanes, forcibly rush through toll stations, assault toll road management personnel, damage toll facilities, or engage in other activities that disrupt the operation and management order of toll roads for the purpose of refusing to pay, evading payment, or underpaying vehicle tolls.  
Where conduct disrupting the operation and management order of a toll road as specified in the preceding paragraph occurs, the operator of the toll road shall promptly report to the public security organ, which shall handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　在收费公路上行驶的车辆不得超载。  
发现车辆超载时，收费公路经营管理者应当及时报告公安机关，由公安机关依法予以处理。

**Article 34** Vehicles traveling on a toll road shall not be overloaded.  
When an overloaded vehicle is discovered, the operator of the toll road shall promptly report it to the public security organ, which shall handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　收费公路经营管理者不得有下列行为：  
(一)擅自提高车辆通行费收费标准；  
(二)在车辆通行费收费标准之外加收或者代收任何其他费用；  
(三)强行收取或者以其他不正当手段按车辆收取某一期间的车辆通行费；  
(四)不开具收费票据，开具未经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府财政、税务部门统一印(监)制的收费票据或者开具已经过期失效的收费票据。  
有前款所列行为之一的，通行车辆有权拒绝交纳车辆通行费。

**Article 35** The operator of a toll road shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) Unilaterally raising the toll rate for vehicle passage;  
(2) Charging or collecting on behalf of others any other fees in addition to the toll rate for vehicle passage;  
(3) Forcibly collecting, or collecting by other improper means, vehicle tolls for a certain period on a per-vehicle basis;  
(4) Failing to issue a toll receipt, issuing a toll receipt that has not been uniformly printed (or supervised) by the finance or tax department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or issuing an expired or invalid toll receipt.  
Where any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph is committed, passing vehicles shall have the right to refuse to pay the vehicle toll.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　政府还贷公路的管理者收取的车辆通行费收入，应当全部存入财政专户，严格实行收支两条线管理。  
政府还贷公路的车辆通行费，除必要的管理、养护费用从财政部门批准的车辆通行费预算中列支外，必须全部用于偿还贷款和有偿集资款，不得挪作他用。

**Article 36** The vehicle toll revenue collected by the manager of a government-financed repayment highway shall be deposited in a special financial account, and shall be strictly managed under the system of separation of revenue and expenditure.  
The vehicle tolls of a government-financed repayment highway, except for necessary management and maintenance expenses to be disbursed from the vehicle toll budget approved by the finance department, must be used entirely for repaying loans and paid funds raised, and shall not be diverted to any other purpose.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　收费公路的收费期限届满，必须终止收费。  
政府还贷公路在批准的收费期限届满前已经还清贷款、还清有偿集资款的，必须终止收费。  
依照本条前两款的规定，收费公路终止收费的，有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当向社会公告，明确规定终止收费的日期，接受社会监督。

**Article 37** Upon expiration of the toll period of a toll road, toll collection must cease.  
If a government-financed repayment highway has fully repaid its loans and paid funds raised before the expiration of the approved toll period, toll collection must cease.  
Where toll collection on a toll road ceases in accordance with the preceding two paragraphs of this Article, the relevant people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall make a public announcement, clearly specifying the date of toll cessation, to accept public supervision.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　收费公路终止收费前6个月，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门应当对收费公路进行鉴定和验收。经鉴定和验收，公路符合取得收费公路权益时核定的技术等级和标准的，收费公路经营管理者方可按照国家有关规定向交通主管部门办理公路移交手续；不符合取得收费公路权益时核定的技术等级和标准的，收费公路经营管理者应当在交通主管部门确定的期限内进行养护，达到要求后，方可按照规定办理公路移交手续。

**Article 38** Six months before the cessation of toll collection on a toll road, the competent transport department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall conduct an appraisal and acceptance inspection of the toll road. If, after appraisal and acceptance, the highway meets the technical grades and standards verified at the time the toll road rights and interests were acquired, the operator of the toll road may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, go through the procedures for handing over the highway to the competent transport department; if the highway does not meet the technical grades and standards verified at the time the toll road rights and interests were acquired, the operator of the toll road shall carry out maintenance within the time limit determined by the competent transport department, and may go through the procedures for handing over the highway only after meeting the requirements.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　收费公路终止收费后，收费公路经营管理者应当自终止收费之日起15日内拆除收费设施。

**Article 39** After the cessation of toll collection on a toll road, the operator of the toll road shall dismantle the toll facilities within 15 days from the date of toll cessation.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　任何单位或者个人不得通过封堵非收费公路或者在非收费公路上设卡收费等方式，强迫车辆通行收费公路。

**Article 40** No unit or individual may compel vehicles to travel on a toll road by blocking a non-toll road or setting up a toll station on a non-toll road, or by other means.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　收费公路经营管理者应当按照国务院交通主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门的要求，及时提供统计资料和有关情况。

**Article 41** The operator of a toll road shall, upon request by the competent transport department of the State Council and the competent transport department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, promptly provide statistical data and relevant information.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　收费公路的养护、绿化和公路用地范围内的水土保持及路政管理，依照公路法的有关规定执行。

**Article 42** The maintenance, greening, soil and water conservation within the highway land area, and highway administration of toll roads shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Highway Law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国务院交通主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门应当对收费公路实施监督检查，督促收费公路经营管理者依法履行公路养护、绿化和公路用地范围内的水土保持义务。

**Article 43** The competent transport department of the State Council and the competent transport departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall supervise and inspect toll roads, and urge the operators of toll roads to perform their obligations of highway maintenance, greening, and soil and water conservation within the highway land area according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　审计机关应当依法加强收费公路的审计监督，对违法行为依法进行查处。

**Article 44** The audit authorities shall strengthen the audit supervision of toll roads according to law, and shall investigate and deal with illegal acts according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　行政执法机关依法对收费公路实施监督检查时，不得向收费公路经营管理者收取任何费用。

**Article 45** When an administrative law enforcement agency conducts supervision and inspection of a toll road according to law, it shall not charge any fees to the operator of the toll road.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当将本行政区域内收费公路及收费站名称、收费单位、收费标准、收费期限等信息向社会公布，接受社会监督。

**Article 46** The people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall make public to the community information about toll roads and toll station names, toll collection entities, toll rates, and toll periods within its administrative region, to accept public supervision.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反本条例的规定，擅自批准收费公路建设、收费站、收费期限、车辆通行费收费标准或者收费公路权益转让的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府责令改正；对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予记大过直至开除的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 47** Where a person, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, unauthorizedly approves the construction of a toll road, a toll station, a toll period, a vehicle toll rate, or the transfer of the rights and interests of a toll road, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order rectification; the person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions ranging from a serious demerit to dismissal according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本条例的规定，地方人民政府或者有关部门及其工作人员非法干预收费公路经营管理，或者挤占、挪用收费公路经营管理者收取的车辆通行费的，由上级人民政府或者有关部门责令停止非法干预，退回挤占、挪用的车辆通行费；对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予记大过直至开除的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 48** Where a local people's government or relevant department or its functionary, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, illegally interferes in the operation and management of a toll road, or misappropriates or diverts the vehicle tolls collected by the operator of the toll road, the people's government at a higher level or the relevant department shall order the cessation of the illegal interference and the return of the misappropriated or diverted vehicle tolls; the person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions ranging from a serious demerit to dismissal according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　违反本条例的规定，擅自在公路上设立收费站(卡)收取车辆通行费或者应当终止收费而不终止的，由国务院交通主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门依据职权，责令改正，强制拆除收费设施；有违法所得的，没收违法所得，并处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，处l万元以上5万元以下的罚款；负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员属于国家工作人员的，依法给予记大过直至开除的行政处分。

**Article 49** Where a person, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, unauthorizedly sets up a toll station (checkpoint) on a highway to collect vehicle tolls, or fails to cease toll collection when required, the competent transport department of the State Council or the competent transport department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within its functions and powers, order rectification and forcibly dismantle the toll facilities; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the person in charge and other directly responsible persons are state functionaries, they shall be given administrative sanctions ranging from a serious demerit to dismissal according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由国务院交通主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门依据职权，责令改正，并根据情节轻重，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款：  
(一)收费站的设置不符合标准或者擅自变更收费站位置的；  
(二)未按照国家规定的标准和规范对收费公路及沿线设施进行日常检查、维护的；  
(三)未按照国家有关规定合理设置交通标志、标线的；  
(四)道口设置不符合车辆行驶安全要求或者道口数量不符合车辆快速通过需要的；  
(五)遇有公路损坏、施工或者发生交通事故等影响车辆正常安全行驶的情形，未按照规定设置安全防护设施或者未进行提示、公告，或者遇有交通堵塞不及时疏导交通的；  
(六)应当公布有关限速通行或者关闭收费公路的信息而未及时公布的。

**Article 50** Where a person, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the competent transport department of the State Council or the competent transport department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within its functions and powers, order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan depending on the seriousness of the circumstances:  
(1) The setting of the toll station fails to meet the standards, or the location of the toll station is unauthorizedly changed;  
(2) Failing to conduct daily inspections and maintenance of the toll road and its roadside facilities in accordance with the standards and specifications prescribed by the State;  
(3) Failing to set up traffic signs and markings reasonably in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(4) The setting of toll lanes fails to meet the safety requirements for vehicle movement, or the number of toll lanes fails to meet the need for vehicles to pass through quickly;  
(5) Where a situation such as highway damage, construction, or a traffic accident occurs that affects the normal and safe driving of vehicles, failing to set up safety protection facilities or make warnings or announcements as required, or failing to promptly divert traffic when congestion occurs;  
(6) Failing to promptly publish information about speed restrictions or closure of the toll road that should be published.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本条例的规定，收费公路经营管理者收费时不开具票据，开具未经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府财政、税务部门统一印(监)制的票据，或者开具已经过期失效的票据的，由财政部门或者税务部门责令改正，并根据情节轻重，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员属于国家工作人员的，依法给予记大过直至开除的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 51** Where a toll road business operator, in violation of these Regulations, fails to issue a receipt when collecting tolls, issues a receipt not uniformly printed (or supervised) by the financial or tax department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or issues an expired or invalid receipt, the financial department or the tax department shall order it to make corrections and, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons are state functionaries, administrative sanctions ranging from a major demerit up to removal from office shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例的规定，政府还贷公路的管理者未将车辆通行费足额存入财政专户或者未将转让政府还贷公路权益的收入全额缴入国库的，由财政部门予以追缴、补齐；对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予记过直至开除的行政处分。  
违反本条例的规定，财政部门未将政府还贷公路的车辆通行费或者转让政府还贷公路权益的收入用于偿还贷款、偿还有偿集资款，或者将车辆通行费、转让政府还贷公路权益的收入挪作他用的，由本级人民政府责令偿还贷款、偿还有偿集资款，或者责令退还挪用的车辆通行费和转让政府还贷公路权益的收入；对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予记过直至开除的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 52** Where an administrator of a government loan-repaid toll road, in violation of these Regulations, fails to deposit the full amount of vehicle tolls into the special financial account or fails to transfer the full amount of income from the transfer of government loan-repaid toll road rights into the state treasury, the financial department shall recover and make up the shortfall; administrative sanctions ranging from a demerit up to removal from office shall be imposed according of law on the responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons.  
Where a financial department, in violation of these Regulations, fails to use the vehicle tolls of a government loan-repaid toll road or the income from the transfer of government loan-repaid toll road rights for repaying loans or repaying funds raised with compensation, or misappropriates such vehicle tolls or income for other purposes, the people's government at the corresponding level shall order it to repay the loans or the funds raised with compensation, or order it to return the misappropriated vehicle tolls and income from the transfer of government loan-repaid toll road rights; administrative sanctions ranging from a demerit up to removal from office shall be imposed according of law on the responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反本条例的规定，收费公路终止收费后，收费公路经营管理者不及时拆除收费设施的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门责令限期拆除；逾期不拆除的，强制拆除，拆除费用由原收费公路经营管理者承担。

**Article 53** Where a toll road business operator, in violation of these Regulations, fails to promptly remove the toll collection facilities after the cessation of toll collection on the toll road, the competent transportation department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order it to remove them within a specified period; if it fails to remove them within the specified period, compulsory demolition shall be carried out, and the demolition costs shall be borne by the original toll road business operator.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本条例的规定，收费公路经营管理者未按照国务院交通主管部门规定的技术规范和操作规程进行收费公路养护的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，责令停止收费。责令停止收费后30日内仍未履行公路养护义务的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门指定其他单位进行养护，养护费用由原收费公路经营管理者承担。拒不承担的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 54** Where a toll road business operator, in violation of these Regulations, fails to carry out the maintenance of the toll road in accordance with the technical specifications and operating procedures prescribed by the competent transportation department under the State Council, the competent transportation department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be ordered to cease toll collection. If, within 30 days after the order to cease toll collection, it still fails to perform the road maintenance obligation, the competent transportation department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall designate another entity to carry out the maintenance, and the maintenance costs shall be borne by the original toll road business operator. If it refuses to bear the costs, the competent transportation department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall apply to a People's Court for compulsory enforcement.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本条例的规定，收费公路经营管理者未履行公路绿化和水土保持义务的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门责令改正，并可以对原收费公路经营管理者处履行绿化、水土保持义务所需费用1倍至2倍的罚款。

**Article 55** Where a toll road business operator, in violation of these Regulations, fails to perform the obligations of road greening and water and soil conservation, the competent transportation department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order it to make corrections, and may impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the costs necessary for performing the greening and water and soil conservation obligations on the original toll road business operator.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国务院价格主管部门或者县级以上地方人民政府价格主管部门对违反本条例的价格违法行为，应当依据价格管理的法律、法规和规章的规定予以处罚。

**Article 56** The competent price department under the State Council or the competent price department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall impose penalties on illegal pricing acts in violation of these Regulations in accordance with the provisions of laws, regulations, and rules on price administration.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本条例的规定，为拒交、逃交、少交车辆通行费而故意堵塞收费道口、强行冲卡、殴打收费公路管理人员、破坏收费设施或者从事其他扰乱收费公路经营管理秩序活动，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；给收费公路经营管理者造成损失或者造成人身损害的，依法承担民事赔偿责任。

**Article 57** Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, intentionally blocks a toll gate, forcibly breaks through a toll barrier, assaults a toll road manager, damages toll collection facilities, or engages in other activities disrupting the management order of a toll road for the purpose of refusing to pay, evading, or paying less vehicle tolls, if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if losses are caused to a toll road business operator or personal injury is caused, civil liability for compensation shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本条例的规定，假冒军队车辆、武警部队车辆、公安机关统一标志的制式警车和抢险救灾车辆逃交车辆通行费的，由有关机关依法予以处理。

**Article 58** Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, impersonates a military vehicle, an armed police vehicle, a standard police vehicle with a unified mark of a public security organ, or an emergency rescue and disaster relief vehicle to evade payment of vehicle tolls, the relevant organ shall deal with the matter according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　本条例施行前在建的和已投入运行的收费公路，由国务院交通主管部门会同国务院发展改革部门和财政部门依照本条例规定的原则进行规范。具体办法由国务院交通主管部门制定。

**Article 59** Toll roads under construction or already in operation before these Regulations come into force shall be regulated by the competent transportation department under the State Council together with the development and reform department under the State Council and the financial department under the State Council in accordance with the principles set forth in these Regulations. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent transportation department under the State Council.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　本条例自2004年11月1日起施行。

**Article 60** These Regulations shall come into force as of November 1, 2004.
