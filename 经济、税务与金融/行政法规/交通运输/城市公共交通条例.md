# 城市公共交通条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2024-10-17  
**生效日期**：2024-12-01

> 第十一条　新建、改建、扩建居住区、交通枢纽、学校、医院、体育场馆、商业中心等大型建设项目，应当统筹考虑公共交通出行需求；建设项目批准、核准文件要求配套建设城市公共交通基础设施的，建设单位应当按照要求建设相关设施并同步投入使用。 城市公共交通基础设施建设应当符合无障碍环境建设要求，并与适老化改造相结合。 第十二条　城市人民政府应当依法保障城市公共交通基础设施用地。城市公共交通基础设施用地符合规定条件的，可以以划拨、协议出让等方式供给。 在符合国土空间规划和用途管制要求且不影响城市公共交通功能和规模的前提下，对城市公共交通基础设施用地可以按照国家有关规定实施综合开发，支持城市公共交通发展。 第十三条　城市人民政府应当根据城市公共交通实际和财政承受能力安排城市公共交通发展所需经费，并纳入本级预算。 国家鼓励、引导金融机构提供与城市公共交通发展相适应的金融服务，加大对城市公共交通发展的融资支持力度。 国家鼓励和支持社会资本依法参与城市公共交通基础设施建设运营，保障其合法权益。 第十四条　城市公共交通票价依法实行政府定价或者政府指导价，并建立动态调整机制。鼓励根据城市公共交通服务质量、运输距离以及换乘方式等因素，建立多层次、差别化的城市公共交通票价体系。 制定、调整城市公共交通票价，应当统筹考虑企业运营成本、社会承受能力、交通供求状况等因素，并依法履行定价成本监审等程序。 第十五条　城市公共交通企业在保障公众基本出行的前提下，可以开展定制化出行服务业务。定制化出行服务业务可以实行市场调节价。 第十六条　城市人民政府应当组织有关部门，在对城市公共交通企业开展运营服务质量评价和成本费用年度核算报告审核的基础上，综合考虑财政承受能力、企业增收节支空间等因素，按照规定及时给予补贴补偿。 第十七条　城市人民政府可以根据实际情况和需要，按照统筹公共交通效率和整体交通效率、集约利用城市道路资源的原则，设置公共交通专用车道，并实行科学管理和动态调整。 第三章　运营服务 第十八条　城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门应当通过与城市公共交通企业签订运营服务协议等方式，明确城市公共交通运营有关服务标准、规范、要求以及运营服务质量评价等事项。 城市公共交通企业应当遵守城市公共交通运营有关服务标准、规范、要求等，加强企业内部管理，不断提高运营服务质量和效率。

### 正文

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　城市人民政府应当依法保障城市公共交通基础设施用地。城市公共交通基础设施用地符合规定条件的，可以以划拨、协议出让等方式供给。  
在符合国土空间规划和用途管制要求且不影响城市公共交通功能和规模的前提下，对城市公共交通基础设施用地可以按照国家有关规定实施综合开发，支持城市公共交通发展。

**Article 12** The people's government of a city shall, according to law, guarantee the land for urban public transport infrastructure. Where the land for urban public transport infrastructure meets the prescribed conditions, it may be supplied by means of allocation, negotiated assignment, etc.  
On the premise of complying with the requirements of territorial space planning and use control and without affecting the function and scale of urban public transport, comprehensive development may be carried out on the land for urban public transport infrastructure in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State to support the development of urban public transport.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　城市人民政府应当根据城市公共交通实际和财政承受能力安排城市公共交通发展所需经费，并纳入本级预算。  
国家鼓励、引导金融机构提供与城市公共交通发展相适应的金融服务，加大对城市公共交通发展的融资支持力度。  
国家鼓励和支持社会资本依法参与城市公共交通基础设施建设运营，保障其合法权益。

**Article 13** The people's government of a city shall, based on the actual conditions of urban public transport and its fiscal affordability, arrange the funds needed for the development of urban public transport and incorporate them into its budget at the corresponding level.  
The State encourages and guides financial institutions to provide financial services commensurate with the development of urban public transport and increase the financing support for the development of urban public transport.  
The State encourages and supports social capital to participate in the construction and operation of urban public transport infrastructure according to law, and safeguards its lawful rights and interests.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　城市公共交通票价依法实行政府定价或者政府指导价，并建立动态调整机制。鼓励根据城市公共交通服务质量、运输距离以及换乘方式等因素，建立多层次、差别化的城市公共交通票价体系。  
制定、调整城市公共交通票价，应当统筹考虑企业运营成本、社会承受能力、交通供求状况等因素，并依法履行定价成本监审等程序。

**Article 14** The fares for urban public transport shall, according to law, be subject to government pricing or government-guided pricing, and a dynamic adjustment mechanism shall be established. The establishment of a multi-level and differentiated fare system for urban public transport is encouraged based on factors such as the quality of urban public transport services, transport distance, and transfer modes.  
When setting or adjusting fares for urban public transport, comprehensive consideration shall be given to factors such as enterprise operation costs, social affordability, and transport supply and demand conditions, and procedures such as pricing cost supervision and review shall be performed according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　城市公共交通企业在保障公众基本出行的前提下，可以开展定制化出行服务业务。定制化出行服务业务可以实行市场调节价。

**Article 15** On the premise of ensuring the basic travel of the public, an urban public transport enterprise may carry out customized travel service business. The customized travel service business may be subject to market-adjusted prices.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　城市人民政府应当组织有关部门，在对城市公共交通企业开展运营服务质量评价和成本费用年度核算报告审核的基础上，综合考虑财政承受能力、企业增收节支空间等因素，按照规定及时给予补贴补偿。

**Article 16** The people's government of a city shall organize relevant departments to, on the basis of conducting operation service quality evaluation and reviewing annual cost accounting reports of urban public transport enterprises, comprehensively consider factors such as fiscal affordability and the enterprise's capacity to increase revenue and reduce expenditure, and grant subsidies and compensations in a timely manner in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　城市人民政府可以根据实际情况和需要，按照统筹公共交通效率和整体交通效率、集约利用城市道路资源的原则，设置公共交通专用车道，并实行科学管理和动态调整。

**Article 17** The people's government of a city may, based on the actual circumstances and needs, set up dedicated lanes for public transport in accordance with the principle of coordinating public transport efficiency and overall traffic efficiency and intensive use of urban road resources, and implement scientific management and dynamic adjustment.

### 第三章　运营服务

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门应当通过与城市公共交通企业签订运营服务协议等方式，明确城市公共交通运营有关服务标准、规范、要求以及运营服务质量评价等事项。  
城市公共交通企业应当遵守城市公共交通运营有关服务标准、规范、要求等，加强企业内部管理，不断提高运营服务质量和效率。  
城市公共交通企业不得将其运营的城市公共交通线路转让、出租或者变相转让、出租给他人运营。

**Article 18** The competent department of urban public transport under the people's government of a city shall, by means of signing operation service agreements with urban public transport enterprises, specify matters such as the service standards, norms, and requirements related to the operation of urban public transport, as well as the evaluation of operation service quality.  
An urban public transport enterprise shall comply with the service standards, norms, and requirements related to the operation of urban public transport, strengthen its internal management, and continuously improve the quality and efficiency of operation services.  
An urban public transport enterprise shall not transfer, lease, or transfer or lease in a disguised form the urban public transport lines it operates to others for operation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　城市公共交通企业应当按照运营服务协议或者城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门的要求配备城市公共交通车辆，并按照规定设置车辆运营服务标识。

**Article 19** An urban public transport enterprise shall equip urban public transport vehicles in accordance with the operation service agreement or the requirements of the competent department of urban public transport under the people's government of a city, and set up vehicle operation service identification in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　城市公共交通企业应当通过便于公众知晓的方式，及时公开运营线路、停靠站点、运营时间、发车间隔、票价等信息。鼓励城市公共交通企业通过电子站牌、出行信息服务系统等信息化手段为公众提供信息查询服务。

**Article 20** An urban public transport enterprise shall, by means convenient for the public to know, timely disclose information such as operation routes, stops, operation hours, departure intervals, and fares. An urban public transport enterprise is encouraged to provide information inquiry services to the public through information-based means such as electronic stop signs and travel information service systems.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　城市公共交通企业应当加强运营调度管理，在保障安全的前提下提高运行准点率和运行效率。

**Article 21** An urban public transport enterprise shall strengthen operation dispatching management, and improve on-time performance and operation efficiency on the premise of ensuring safety.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　城市公共交通企业不得擅自变更运营线路、停靠站点、运营时间或者中断运营服务；因特殊原因需要临时变更运营线路、停靠站点、运营时间或者暂时中断运营服务的，除发生突发事件或者为保障运营安全等采取紧急措施外，应当提前向社会公告，并向城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门报告。

**Article 22** An urban public transport enterprise shall not change operating routes, stops, operating hours, or interrupt operating services without authorization; where it is necessary to temporarily change operating routes, stops, operating hours, or temporarily interrupt operating services due to special reasons, except for emergency measures taken due to the occurrence of emergencies or to ensure operational safety, it shall make a public announcement in advance and report to the competent urban public transport department of the people's government of the city.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　因大型群众性活动等情形出现公共交通客流集中、正常运营服务安排难以满足需求的，城市公共交通企业应当按照城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门的要求，及时采取增开临时班次、缩短发车间隔、延长运营时间等措施，保障运营服务。

**Article 23** Where, due to large-scale mass events or other circumstances, public transport passenger flow is concentrated and normal operating service arrangements cannot meet the demand, the urban public transport enterprise shall, according to the requirements of the competent urban public transport department of the people's government of the city, promptly take measures such as adding temporary trips, shortening departure intervals, and extending operating hours to ensure the operating service.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　乘客应当按照票价支付票款；对拒不支付票款的，城市公共交通企业可以拒绝其进站乘车。  
城市公共交通企业应当依照法律、法规和国家有关规定，对相关群体乘坐公共交通工具提供便利和优待。

**Article 24** Passengers shall pay the fare according to the ticket price; if a passenger refuses to pay the fare, the urban public transport enterprise may refuse to allow the passenger to enter the station and board the vehicle.  
The urban public transport enterprise shall, in accordance with laws, regulations, and the relevant provisions of the State, provide convenience and preferential treatment to relevant groups for riding public transport vehicles.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　城市公共交通企业应当建立运营服务质量投诉处理机制并向社会公布，及时妥善处理乘客提出的投诉，并向乘客反馈处理结果；乘客对处理结果不满意的，可以向城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门申诉，城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门应当及时作出答复。乘客也可以直接就运营服务质量问题向城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门投诉。

**Article 25** The urban public transport enterprise shall establish a complaint handling mechanism regarding operating service quality and make it public, handle complaints raised by passengers in a timely and proper manner, and provide feedback to passengers on the handling results; if a passenger is dissatisfied with the handling result, the passenger may petition to the competent urban public transport department of the people's government of the city, and the competent urban public transport department of the people's government of the city shall make a reply promptly. Passengers may also directly lodge complaints regarding operating service quality issues with the competent urban public transport department of the people's government of the city.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门应当定期组织开展城市公共交通企业运营服务质量评价，并将评价结果向社会公布。

**Article 26** The competent urban public transport department of the people's government of the city shall periodically organize evaluations of the operating service quality of urban public transport enterprises and publish the evaluation results to the public.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　未经城市人民政府同意，城市公共交通企业不得终止运营服务；因破产、解散终止运营服务的，应当提前30日向城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门报告，城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门应当及时采取指定临时运营服务企业、调配运营车辆等措施，确保运营服务不中断；需要重新确定承担城市公共交通运营服务企业的，城市人民政府或者其城市公共交通主管部门应当按照规定及时确定。

**Article 27** Without the consent of the people's government of the city, the urban public transport enterprise shall not terminate the operating service; if the operating service is terminated due to bankruptcy or dissolution, it shall report to the competent urban public transport department of the people's government of the city 30 days in advance, and the competent urban public transport department of the people's government of the city shall promptly take measures such as designating a temporary operating service enterprise and allocating operating vehicles to ensure that the operating service is not interrupted; if it is necessary to re-designate an enterprise to undertake the urban public transport operating service, the people's government of the city or its competent urban public transport department shall promptly make the designation in accordance with the provisions.

### 第四章　安全管理

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　城市公共交通企业应当遵守有关安全生产的法律、法规和标准，落实全员安全生产责任，建立健全安全生产管理制度和安全生产责任制，保障安全经费投入，构建安全风险分级管控和隐患排查治理双重预防机制，增强突发事件防范和应急能力。

**Article 28** The urban public transport enterprise shall comply with the laws, regulations, and standards concerning work safety, implement the work safety responsibility of all employees, establish and improve the work safety management system and the work safety responsibility system, ensure the investment of safety funds, establish a dual prevention mechanism for hierarchical management and control of safety risks and investigation and management of hidden dangers, and enhance the capability to prevent and respond to emergencies.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　城市公共交通建设工程的勘察、设计、施工、监理应当遵守有关建设工程管理的法律、法规和标准。  
城市公共交通建设工程涉及公共安全的设施应当与主体工程同步规划、同步建设、同步投入使用。

**Article 29** The survey, design, construction, and supervision of urban public transport construction projects shall comply with the laws, regulations, and standards concerning construction project management.  
The facilities involving public safety in urban public transport construction projects shall be planned, constructed, and put into use simultaneously with the main project.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　城市公共交通企业投入运营的车辆应当依法经检验合格，并按照国家有关标准配备灭火器、安全锤以及安全隔离、紧急报警、车门紧急开启等安全设备，设置明显的安全警示标志。  
城市公共交通企业应当按照国家有关标准对车辆和有关系统、设施设备进行维护、保养，确保性能良好和安全运行。  
利用城市公共交通车辆或者设施设备设置广告的，应当遵守有关广告管理的法律、法规，不得影响城市公共交通运营安全。

**Article 30** The vehicles put into operation by the urban public transport enterprise shall be inspected and passed in accordance with the law, and shall be equipped with fire extinguishers, safety hammers, and safety equipment such as safety isolation, emergency alarm, emergency door opening, etc., and shall be set with obvious safety warning signs in accordance with the relevant national standards.  
The urban public transport enterprise shall maintain and service the vehicles and related systems, facilities, and equipment in accordance with the relevant national standards to ensure good performance and safe operation.  
Where advertisements are placed using urban public transport vehicles or facilities and equipment, the laws and regulations concerning advertising management shall be complied with, and the safety of urban public transport operation shall not be affected.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　城市公共交通企业直接涉及运营安全的驾驶员、乘务员、调度员、值班员、信号工、通信工等重点岗位人员（以下统称重点岗位人员），应当符合下列条件：  
（一）具有履行岗位职责的能力；  
（二）无可能危及运营安全的疾病；  
（三）无暴力犯罪和吸毒行为记录；  
（四）国务院城市公共交通主管部门规定的其他条件。  
除符合前款规定条件外，城市公共汽电车驾驶员还应当取得相应准驾车型机动车驾驶证，城市轨道交通列车驾驶员还应当按照国家有关规定取得相应职业准入资格。

**Article 31** Key position personnel directly involved in the operational safety of an urban public transport enterprise, such as drivers, conductors, dispatchers, duty officers, signalmen, and telecommunications workers (hereinafter collectively referred to as key position personnel), shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having the ability to perform the duties of the position;  
(2) having no diseases that may endanger operational safety;  
(3) having no record of violent crime or drug use;  
(4) other conditions prescribed by the competent urban public transport department of the State Council.  
In addition to meeting the conditions set forth in the preceding paragraph, drivers of urban public buses and trolleybuses shall also obtain a motor vehicle driver's license for the corresponding type of vehicle, and drivers of urban rail transit trains shall also obtain corresponding vocational access qualifications in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　城市公共交通企业应当定期对重点岗位人员进行岗位职责、操作规程、服务规范、安全防范和应急处置基本知识等方面的培训和考核，经考核合格的方可上岗作业。培训和考核情况应当建档备查。  
城市公共交通企业应当关注重点岗位人员的身体、心理状况和行为习惯，对重点岗位人员定期组织体检，加强心理疏导，及时采取有效措施防范重点岗位人员身体、心理状况或者行为异常导致运营安全事故发生。  
城市公共交通企业应当合理安排驾驶员工作时间，防止疲劳驾驶。

**Article 32** Urban public transport enterprises shall regularly provide training and assessment to personnel in key positions regarding job responsibilities, operating procedures, service standards, basic knowledge of safety prevention and emergency response, and only those who pass the assessment may take up their posts. The training and assessment records shall be kept on file for future reference.  
Urban public transport enterprises shall pay attention to the physical and psychological conditions and behavioral habits of personnel in key positions, regularly arrange physical examinations for such personnel, strengthen psychological counseling, and take timely and effective measures to prevent operational safety accidents caused by abnormal physical or psychological conditions or behaviors of personnel in key positions.  
Urban public transport enterprises shall reasonably arrange the working hours of drivers to prevent fatigue driving.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　城市公共交通企业应当依照有关法律、法规的规定，落实对相关人员进行安全背景审查、配备安保人员和相应设施设备等安全防范责任。

**Article 33** Urban public transport enterprises shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, fulfill security prevention responsibilities such as conducting security background checks on relevant personnel and deploying security personnel and corresponding facilities and equipment.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　城市公共交通企业应当加强对客流状况的日常监测；出现或者可能出现客流大量积压时，应当及时采取疏导措施，必要时可以采取临时限制客流或者临时封站等措施，确保运营安全。  
因突发事件或者设施设备故障等原因危及运营安全的，城市公共交通企业可以暂停部分区段或者全线网运营服务，并做好乘客疏导和现场秩序维护等工作。乘客应当按照城市公共交通企业工作人员的指挥和引导有序疏散。

**Article 34** Urban public transport enterprises shall strengthen daily monitoring of passenger flow conditions; when a large buildup of passenger flow occurs or is likely to occur, they shall promptly take diversion measures, and when necessary, may take measures such as temporarily restricting passenger flow or temporarily closing stations to ensure operational safety.  
Where the safety of operations is endangered due to emergencies or facility or equipment failures, urban public transport enterprises may suspend operation services on some sections or the entire network, and shall properly handle passenger diversion and on-site order maintenance. Passengers shall evacuate in an orderly manner under the command and guidance of the staff of the urban public transport enterprise.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　乘客应当遵守乘车规范，维护乘车秩序。  
乘客不得携带易燃、易爆、毒害性、放射性、腐蚀性以及其他可能危及人身和财产安全的危险物品进站乘车；乘客坚持携带的，城市公共交通企业应当拒绝其进站乘车。  
城市轨道交通运营单位应当按照国家有关规定，对进入城市轨道交通车站的人员及其携带物品进行安全检查；对拒不接受安全检查的，应当拒绝其进站乘车。安全检查应当遵守有关操作规范，提高质量和效率。

**Article 35** Passengers shall comply with riding codes and maintain riding order.  
Passengers shall not carry flammable, explosive, toxic, radioactive, corrosive, or other dangerous articles that may endanger personal or property safety into stations or onto vehicles; if a passenger insists on carrying such articles, the urban public transport enterprise shall refuse to allow the passenger to enter the station or board the vehicle.  
Urban rail transit operation entities shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, conduct security checks on persons entering urban rail transit stations and their carry-on items; for those who refuse to undergo security checks, the entity shall refuse to allow them to enter the station or board the vehicle. Security checks shall comply with relevant operating procedures and shall improve quality and efficiency.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　任何单位和个人不得实施下列危害城市公共交通运营安全的行为：  
（一）非法拦截或者强行上下城市公共交通车辆；  
（二）非法占用城市公共交通场站或者出入口；  
（三）擅自进入城市轨道交通线路、车辆基地、控制中心、列车驾驶室或者其他禁止非工作人员进入的区域；  
（四）向城市公共交通车辆投掷物品或者在城市轨道交通线路上放置障碍物；  
（五）故意损坏或者擅自移动、遮挡城市公共交通站牌、安全警示标志、监控设备、安全防护设备；  
（六）在非紧急状态下擅自操作有安全警示标志的安全设备；  
（七）干扰、阻碍城市公共交通车辆驾驶员安全驾驶；  
（八）其他危害城市公共交通运营安全的行为。  
城市公共交通企业发现前款规定行为的，应当及时予以制止，并采取措施消除安全隐患，必要时报请有关部门依法处理。

**Article 36** No unit or individual may commit any of the following acts endangering the operational safety of urban public transport:  
(1) illegally intercepting or forcibly boarding or alighting from urban public transport vehicles;  
(2) illegally occupying urban public transport stations or entrances and exits;  
(3) entering, without authorization, areas such as urban rail transit lines, vehicle depots, control centers, train cabs, or other areas where non-staff members are prohibited from entering;  
(4) throwing objects at urban public transport vehicles or placing obstacles on urban rail transit lines;  
(5) intentionally damaging, or without authorization moving or obscuring, urban public transport station signs, safety warning signs, surveillance equipment, or safety protection equipment;  
(6) without authorization operating safety equipment with safety warning signs in non-emergency situations;  
(7) interfering with or obstructing the safe driving of urban public transport vehicle drivers;  
(8) other acts endangering the operational safety of urban public transport.  
Where an urban public transport enterprise discovers any of the acts specified in the preceding paragraph, it shall promptly stop such acts and take measures to eliminate safety hazards, and when necessary, report the matter to the relevant departments for handling according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　城市人民政府有关部门应当按照职责分工，加强对城市公共交通运营安全的监督管理，建立城市公共交通运营安全工作协作机制。

**Article 37** The relevant departments of the people's government of a city shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, strengthen supervision and administration over the operational safety of urban public transport and establish a coordination mechanism for urban public transport operational safety work.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门应当会同有关部门制定城市公共交通应急预案，报城市人民政府批准。  
城市公共交通企业应当根据城市公共交通应急预案，制定本单位应急预案，报城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门、应急管理部门备案，并定期组织演练。  
城市人民政府应当加强城市公共交通应急能力建设，组织有关部门、城市公共交通企业和其他有关单位联合开展城市公共交通应急处置演练，提高突发事件应急处置能力。

**Article 38** The urban public transport主管部门 of the people's government of a city shall, together with relevant departments, formulate an urban public transport emergency response plan, which shall be submitted to the people's government of the city for approval.  
Urban public transport enterprises shall, in accordance with the urban public transport emergency response plan, formulate their own emergency response plans, report them to the urban public transport主管部门 and the emergency management department of the people's government of the city for recordation, and regularly organize drills.  
The people's government of a city shall strengthen the emergency response capacity building for urban public transport, organize the relevant departments, urban public transport enterprises, and other relevant units to jointly conduct urban public transport emergency response drills, and improve the capacity for emergency response to emergencies.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　城市人民政府应当健全有关部门与城市公共交通企业之间的信息共享机制。城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门、城市公共交通企业应当加强与有关部门的沟通，及时掌握气象、自然灾害、公共安全等方面可能影响城市公共交通运营安全的信息，并采取有针对性的安全防范措施。有关部门应当予以支持、配合。

**Article 39** The people's government of a city shall improve the information sharing mechanism between relevant departments and urban public transport enterprises. The urban public transport主管部门 of the people's government of a city and urban public transport enterprises shall strengthen communication with relevant departments, promptly obtain information on meteorology, natural disasters, public security, and other aspects that may affect the operational safety of urban public transport, and take targeted safety prevention measures. The relevant departments shall provide support and cooperation.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　城市人民政府应当将城市轨道交通纳入城市防灾减灾规划，完善城市轨道交通防范水淹、火灾、冰雪、雷击、风暴等设计和论证，提高城市轨道交通灾害防范应对能力。

**Article 40** The people's government of a city shall incorporate urban rail transit into the city's disaster prevention and mitigation plan, improve the design and demonstration of urban rail transit against flooding, fire, ice and snow, lightning strikes, storms, etc., and enhance the capacity of urban rail transit to prevent and respond to disasters.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　城市轨道交通建设单位组织编制城市轨道交通建设工程可行性研究报告和初步设计文件，应当落实国家有关公共安全和运营服务的要求。

**Article 41** When organizing the preparation of feasibility study reports and preliminary design documents for urban rail transit construction projects, the construction entity of urban rail transit shall implement the relevant national requirements for public safety and operational services.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　城市轨道交通建设工程项目依法经验收合格后，城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门应当组织开展运营前安全评估，通过安全评估的方可投入运营。城市轨道交通建设单位和运营单位应当按照国家有关规定办理建设和运营交接手续。  
城市轨道交通建设工程项目验收以及建设和运营交接的管理办法由国务院住房城乡建设主管部门会同国务院城市公共交通主管部门制定。

**Article 42** After an urban rail transit construction project has been accepted as qualified according to law, the competent department of urban public transport of the municipal people's government shall organize and conduct a pre-operation safety assessment, and only after passing such assessment may it be put into operation. The construction unit and the operation unit of urban rail transit shall go through the formalities for the handover of construction and operation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The administrative measures for the acceptance inspection of urban rail transit construction projects and the handover of construction and operation shall be formulated by the competent department of housing and urban-rural development of the State Council jointly with the competent department of urban public transport of the State Council.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　城市人民政府应当组织有关部门划定城市轨道交通线路安全保护区，制定安全保护区管理制度。  
在城市轨道交通线路安全保护区内进行作业的，应当征得城市轨道交通运营单位同意。作业单位应当制定和落实安全防护方案，并在作业过程中对作业影响区域进行动态监测，及时发现并消除安全隐患。城市轨道交通运营单位可以进入作业现场进行巡查，发现作业危及或者可能危及城市轨道交通运营安全的，应当要求作业单位采取措施消除安全隐患或者停止作业。

**Article 43** The municipal people's government shall organize the relevant departments to delineate safety protection zones for urban rail transit lines and formulate a management system for the safety protection zones.  
Where any operation is conducted within a safety protection zone of an urban rail transit line, the consent of the urban rail transit operation unit shall be obtained. The operation unit shall formulate and implement a safety protection plan, and shall conduct dynamic monitoring of the area affected by the operation during the operation process, and timely discover and eliminate safety hazards. The urban rail transit operation unit may enter the operation site for inspection, and if it finds that the operation endangers or may endanger the safety of urban rail transit operation, it shall require the operation unit to take measures to eliminate the safety hazards or cease the operation.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门应当定期组织开展城市轨道交通运营安全第三方评估，督促运营单位及时发现并消除安全隐患。

**Article 44** The competent department of urban public transport of the municipal people's government shall regularly organize and conduct third-party evaluation on the safety of urban rail transit operation, and supervise the operation unit to timely discover and eliminate safety hazards.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　城市公共交通企业以外的单位或者个人擅自从事城市公共交通线路运营的，由城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门责令停止运营，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。  
城市公共交通企业将其运营的城市公共交通线路转让、出租或者变相转让、出租给他人运营的，由城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门责令改正，并依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 45** Where any unit or individual other than an urban public transport enterprise engages in the operation of urban public transport lines without authorization, the competent department of urban public transport of the municipal people's government shall order it to cease operation, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where an urban public transport enterprise transfers, leases, or in disguised form transfers or leases the urban public transport lines it operates to others for operation, the competent department of urban public transport of the municipal people's government shall order it to make corrections, and it shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　城市公共交通企业有下列行为之一的，由城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未遵守城市公共交通运营有关服务标准、规范、要求；  
（二）未按照规定配备城市公共交通车辆或者设置车辆运营服务标识；  
（三）未公开运营线路、停靠站点、运营时间、发车间隔、票价等信息。

**Article 46** Where an urban public transport enterprise has any of the following acts, the competent department of urban public transport of the municipal people's government shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) failing to comply with the relevant service standards, specifications, and requirements for urban public transport operation;  
(2) failing to equip urban public transport vehicles or set vehicle operation service identification in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) failing to disclose information such as operation routes, stops, operation hours, departure intervals, and ticket prices.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　城市公共交通企业擅自变更运营线路、停靠站点、运营时间的，由城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。  
城市公共交通企业擅自中断运营服务的，由城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款。  
城市公共交通企业因特殊原因变更运营线路、停靠站点、运营时间或者暂时中断运营服务，未按照规定向社会公告并向城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门报告的，由城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门责令改正，可以处1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 47** Where an urban public transport enterprise alters the operation routes, stops, or operation hours without authorization, the competent department of urban public transport of the municipal people's government shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where an urban public transport enterprise suspends operation services without authorization, the competent department of urban public transport of the municipal people's government shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where an urban public transport enterprise, due to special reasons, alters the operation routes, stops, operation hours or temporarily suspends operation services, and fails to make a public announcement to the society and report to the competent department of urban public transport of the municipal people's government in accordance with the provisions, the competent department of urban public transport of the municipal people's government shall order it to make corrections, and may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　城市公共交通企业违反本条例规定，未经城市人民政府同意终止运营服务的，由城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 48** Where an urban public transport enterprise, in violation of these Regulations, terminates operation services without the consent of the municipal people's government, the competent department of urban public transport of the municipal people's government shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　城市公共交通企业有下列行为之一的，由城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门责令改正，可以处5万元以下的罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；拒不改正的，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款：  
（一）利用城市公共交通车辆或者设施设备设置广告，影响城市公共交通运营安全；  
（二）重点岗位人员不符合规定条件或者未按照规定对重点岗位人员进行培训和考核，或者安排考核不合格的重点岗位人员上岗作业。

**Article 49** Where an urban public transport enterprise has any of the following acts, the competent department of urban public transport of the municipal people's government shall order it to make corrections, may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan, and if there are illegal gains, confiscate the illegal gains; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) using urban public transport vehicles or facilities and equipment to set up advertisements, thereby affecting the safety of urban public transport operation;  
(2) the personnel in key positions do not meet the prescribed conditions, or failing to conduct training and assessment for personnel in key positions in accordance with the provisions, or arranging for personnel in key positions who have failed the assessment to work on duty.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　在城市轨道交通线路安全保护区内进行作业的单位有下列行为之一的，由城市人民政府城市公共交通主管部门责令改正，暂时停止作业，可以处5万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停止作业，并处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；造成城市轨道交通设施损坏或者影响运营安全的，并处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未征得城市轨道交通运营单位同意进行作业；  
（二）未制定和落实安全防护方案；  
（三）未在作业过程中对作业影响区域进行动态监测或者未及时消除发现的安全隐患。

**Article 50** Where a unit conducting operations within a safety protection zone of an urban rail transit line has any of the following acts, the competent department of urban public transport of the municipal people's government shall order it to make corrections, temporarily cease operations, and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be ordered to cease operations, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed concurrently; if it causes damage to urban rail transit facilities or affects operation safety, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed concurrently:  
(1) conducting operations without obtaining the consent of the urban rail transit operation unit;  
(2) failing to formulate and implement a safety protection plan;  
(3) failing to conduct dynamic monitoring of the area affected by operations during the operation process, or failing to timely eliminate discovered safety hazards.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　城市人民政府及其城市公共交通主管部门、其他有关部门的工作人员在城市公共交通工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 51** Where the staff members of the municipal people's government and its competent department of urban public transport and other relevant departments abuse their power, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractices for personal gain in urban public transport work, they shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 52** Where a violation of these Regulations constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administrative penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　用于公共交通服务的城市轮渡，参照本条例的有关规定执行。

**Article 53** Urban ferries used for public transport services shall be executed with reference to the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　城市人民政府根据城乡融合和区域协调发展需要，统筹推进城乡之间、区域之间公共交通一体化发展。

**Article 54** The people's government of a city shall, in accordance with the needs of urban-rural integration and coordinated regional development, coordinate and promote the integrated development of public transport between urban and rural areas and among regions.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　本条例自2024年12月1日起施行。

**Article 55** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 2024.
