# 铁路安全管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2013-08-17  
**生效日期**：2014-01-01

> (2013年7月24日国务院第18次常务会议通过　2013年8月17日中华人民共和国国务院令第639号公布　自2014年1月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强铁路安全管理，保障铁路运输安全和畅通，保护人身安全和财产安全，制定本条例。 第二条　铁路安全管理坚持安全第一、预防为主、综合治理的方针。 第三条　国务院铁路行业监督管理部门负责全国铁路安全监督管理工作，国务院铁路行业监督管理部门设立的铁路监督管理机构负责辖区内的铁路安全监督管理工作。国务院铁路行业监督管理部门和铁路监督管理机构统称铁路监管部门。 国务院有关部门依照法律和国务院规定的职责，负责铁路安全管理的有关工作。 第四条　铁路沿线地方各级人民政府和县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当按照各自职责，加强保障铁路安全的教育，落实护路联防责任制，防范和制止危害铁路安全的行为，协调和处理保障铁路安全的有关事项，做好保障铁路安全的有关工作。 第五条　从事铁路建设、运输、设备制造维修的单位应当加强安全管理，建立健全安全生产管理制度，落实企业安全生产主体责任，设置安全管理机构或者配备安全管理人员，执行保障生产安全和产品质量安全的国家标准、行业标准，加强对从业人员的安全教育培训，保证安全生产所必需的资金投入。 铁路建设、运输、设备制造维修单位的工作人员应当严格执行规章制度，实行标准化作业，保证铁路安全。 第六条　铁路监管部门、铁路运输企业等单位应当按照国家有关规定制定突发事件应急预案，并组织应急演练。 第七条　禁止扰乱铁路建设、运输秩序。禁止损坏或者非法占用铁路设施设备、铁路标志和铁路用地。 任何单位或者个人发现损坏或者非法占用铁路设施设备、铁路标志、铁路用地以及其他影响铁路安全的行为，有权报告铁路运输企业，或者向铁路监管部门、公安机关或者其他有关部门举报。接到报告的铁路运输企业、接到举报的部门应当根据各自职责及时处理。 对维护铁路安全作出突出贡献的单位或者个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰奖励。 第二章　铁路建设质量安全 第八条　铁路建设工程的勘察、设计、施工、监理以及建设物资、设备的采购，应当依法进行招标。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强铁路安全管理，保障铁路运输安全和畅通，保护人身安全和财产安全，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen railway safety management, ensure the safety and smoothness of railway transportation, and protect personal safety and property safety.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　铁路安全管理坚持安全第一、预防为主、综合治理的方针。

**Article 2** Railway safety management shall adhere to the policy of safety first, prevention primarily, and comprehensive governance.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院铁路行业监督管理部门负责全国铁路安全监督管理工作，国务院铁路行业监督管理部门设立的铁路监督管理机构负责辖区内的铁路安全监督管理工作。国务院铁路行业监督管理部门和铁路监督管理机构统称铁路监管部门。  
国务院有关部门依照法律和国务院规定的职责，负责铁路安全管理的有关工作。

**Article 3** The railway industry regulatory department of the State Council shall be responsible for national railway safety supervision and administration, and the railway regulatory institutions established by the railway industry regulatory department of the State Council shall be responsible for railway safety supervision and administration within their respective jurisdictions. The railway industry regulatory department of the State Council and the railway regulatory institutions are collectively referred to as railway regulatory authorities.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with the law and the duties prescribed by the State Council, be responsible for the relevant work of railway safety management.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　铁路沿线地方各级人民政府和县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当按照各自职责，加强保障铁路安全的教育，落实护路联防责任制，防范和制止危害铁路安全的行为，协调和处理保障铁路安全的有关事项，做好保障铁路安全的有关工作。

**Article 4** The local people's governments at all levels along the railway and the relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective duties, strengthen education on ensuring railway safety, implement the responsibility system for joint defense of railway protection, prevent and stop acts endangering railway safety, coordinate and handle matters related to ensuring railway safety, and properly carry out the relevant work of ensuring railway safety.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　从事铁路建设、运输、设备制造维修的单位应当加强安全管理，建立健全安全生产管理制度，落实企业安全生产主体责任，设置安全管理机构或者配备安全管理人员，执行保障生产安全和产品质量安全的国家标准、行业标准，加强对从业人员的安全教育培训，保证安全生产所必需的资金投入。  
铁路建设、运输、设备制造维修单位的工作人员应当严格执行规章制度，实行标准化作业，保证铁路安全。

**Article 5** Units engaged in railway construction, transportation, and equipment manufacturing and maintenance shall strengthen safety management, establish and improve the safety production management system, implement the principal responsibility of the enterprise for safety production, set up safety management institutions or equip safety management personnel, implement national and industry standards for ensuring production safety and product quality safety, strengthen safety education and training for employees, and ensure the necessary capital investment for safety production.  
The staff of units engaged in railway construction, transportation, and equipment manufacturing and maintenance shall strictly implement rules and regulations, carry out standardized operations, and ensure railway safety.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　铁路监管部门、铁路运输企业等单位应当按照国家有关规定制定突发事件应急预案，并组织应急演练。

**Article 6** Railway regulatory authorities, railway transport enterprises, and other units shall formulate emergency response plans for emergencies in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and organize emergency drills.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　禁止扰乱铁路建设、运输秩序。禁止损坏或者非法占用铁路设施设备、铁路标志和铁路用地。  
任何单位或者个人发现损坏或者非法占用铁路设施设备、铁路标志、铁路用地以及其他影响铁路安全的行为，有权报告铁路运输企业，或者向铁路监管部门、公安机关或者其他有关部门举报。接到报告的铁路运输企业、接到举报的部门应当根据各自职责及时处理。  
对维护铁路安全作出突出贡献的单位或者个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰奖励。

**Article 7** It is prohibited to disrupt the order of railway construction and transportation. It is prohibited to damage or illegally occupy railway facilities and equipment, railway signs, and railway land.  
Any unit or individual that discovers acts of damaging or illegally occupying railway facilities and equipment, railway signs, railway land, or other acts affecting railway safety shall have the right to report to the railway transport enterprise, or to report to the railway regulatory authority, the public security organ, or other relevant departments. The railway transport enterprise receiving the report and the department receiving the report shall handle the matter promptly according to their respective duties.  
Units or individuals that have made outstanding contributions to maintaining railway safety shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　铁路建设质量安全

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　铁路建设工程的勘察、设计、施工、监理以及建设物资、设备的采购，应当依法进行招标。

**Article 8** The survey, design, construction, supervision, and procurement of construction materials and equipment for railway construction projects shall be subject to tender according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　从事铁路建设工程勘察、设计、施工、监理活动的单位应当依法取得相应资质，并在其资质等级许可的范围内从事铁路工程建设活动。

**Article 9** Units engaged in the survey, design, construction, and supervision activities of railway construction projects shall obtain corresponding qualifications according to law, and shall carry out railway construction activities within the scope permitted by their qualification grades.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　铁路建设单位应当选择具备相应资质等级的勘察、设计、施工、监理单位进行工程建设，并对建设工程的质量安全进行监督检查，制作检查记录留存备查。

**Article 10** A railway construction unit shall select survey, design, construction, and supervision units with corresponding qualification grades for the project construction, and shall supervise and inspect the quality and safety of the construction project, and produce inspection records for future reference.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　铁路建设工程的勘察、设计、施工、监理应当遵守法律、行政法规关于建设工程质量和安全管理的规定，执行国家标准、行业标准和技术规范。  
铁路建设工程的勘察、设计、施工单位依法对勘察、设计、施工的质量负责，监理单位依法对施工质量承担监理责任。  
高速铁路和地质构造复杂的铁路建设工程实行工程地质勘察监理制度。

**Article 11** The survey, design, construction, and supervision of railway construction projects shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on the quality and safety management of construction projects, and implement national standards, industry standards, and technical specifications.  
The survey, design, and construction units of railway construction projects shall be responsible for the quality of survey, design, and construction according to law, and the supervision unit shall bear supervision responsibility for the construction quality according to law.  
The system of engineering geological survey supervision shall be implemented for high-speed railways and railway construction projects with complex geological structures.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　铁路建设工程的安全设施应当与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投入使用。安全设施投资应当纳入建设项目概算。

**Article 12** The safety facilities of a railway construction project shall be designed, constructed, and put into use simultaneously with the main project. The investment in safety facilities shall be included in the budget estimate of the construction project.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　铁路建设工程使用的材料、构件、设备等产品，应当符合有关产品质量的强制性国家标准、行业标准。

**Article 13** Products such as materials, components, and equipment used in railway construction projects shall conform to the mandatory national standards and industry standards on product quality.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　铁路建设工程的建设工期，应当根据工程地质条件、技术复杂程度等因素，按照国家标准、行业标准和技术规范合理确定、调整。  
任何单位和个人不得违反前款规定要求铁路建设、设计、施工单位压缩建设工期。

**Article 14** The construction period of a railway construction project shall be reasonably determined and adjusted according to factors such as the engineering geological conditions and the degree of technical complexity, in accordance with national standards, industry standards, and technical specifications.  
No unit or individual may, in violation of the preceding paragraph, require railway construction, design, or construction units to shorten the construction period.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　铁路建设工程竣工，应当按照国家有关规定组织验收，并由铁路运输企业进行运营安全评估。经验收、评估合格，符合运营安全要求的，方可投入运营。

**Article 15** Upon completion of a railway construction project, the acceptance inspection shall be organized in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and the railway transport enterprise shall conduct an operational safety assessment. Only after passing the acceptance inspection and assessment and meeting the operational safety requirements may it be put into operation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　在铁路线路及其邻近区域进行铁路建设工程施工，应当执行铁路营业线施工安全管理规定。铁路建设单位应当会同相关铁路运输企业和工程设计、施工单位制定安全施工方案，按照方案进行施工。施工完毕应当及时清理现场，不得影响铁路运营安全。

**Article 16** Construction of railway projects on or adjacent to railway lines shall implement the safety management provisions for construction on operating railway lines. The railway construction unit shall, together with the relevant railway transport enterprise and the engineering design and construction units, formulate a safe construction plan and carry out the construction in accordance with the plan. Upon completion of the construction, the site shall be cleared promptly, and the safety of railway operations shall not be affected.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　新建、改建设计开行时速120公里以上列车的铁路或者设计运输量达到国务院铁路行业监督管理部门规定的较大运输量标准的铁路，需要与道路交叉的，应当设置立体交叉设施。  
新建、改建高速公路、一级公路或者城市道路中的快速路，需要与铁路交叉的，应当设置立体交叉设施，并优先选择下穿铁路的方案。  
已建成的属于前两款规定情形的铁路、道路为平面交叉的，应当逐步改造为立体交叉。  
新建、改建高速铁路需要与普通铁路、道路、渡槽、管线等设施交叉的，应当优先选择高速铁路上跨方案。

**Article 17** Where a railway designed for trains running at a speed of more than 120 kilometers per hour, or a railway with a designed transport volume reaching the standard of a relatively large transport volume prescribed by the railway industry regulatory department of the State Council, is newly built or rebuilt and needs to intersect with roads, a grade-separated crossing facility shall be installed.  
Where an expressway, a first-class highway, or an express road in an urban road is newly built or rebuilt and needs to intersect with a railway, a grade-separated crossing facility shall be installed, and the scheme of crossing under the railway shall be given priority.  
Where an existing railway or road falling under the circumstances specified in the preceding two paragraphs is at-grade crossing, it shall be gradually converted into a grade-separated crossing.  
Where a newly built or rebuilt high-speed railway needs to intersect with ordinary railways, roads, aqueducts, pipelines, or other facilities, the scheme of crossing over by the high-speed railway shall be given priority.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　设置铁路与道路立体交叉设施及其附属安全设施所需费用的承担，按照下列原则确定：  
(一)新建、改建铁路与既有道路交叉的，由铁路方承担建设费用；道路方要求超过既有道路建设标准建设所增加的费用，由道路方承担；  
(二)新建、改建道路与既有铁路交叉的，由道路方承担建设费用；铁路方要求超过既有铁路线路建设标准建设所增加的费用，由铁路方承担；  
(三)同步建设的铁路和道路需要设置立体交叉设施以及既有铁路道口改造为立体交叉的，由铁路方和道路方按照公平合理的原则分担建设费用。

**Article 18** The bearing of costs required for installing grade-separated crossing facilities between railways and roads and their ancillary safety facilities shall be determined in accordance with the following principles:  
(1) Where a newly built or rebuilt railway crosses an existing road, the railway party shall bear the construction costs; if the road party requires construction exceeding the existing road construction standards, the increased costs shall be borne by the road party;  
(2) Where a newly built or rebuilt road crosses an existing railway, the road party shall bear the construction costs; if the railway party requires construction exceeding the existing railway line construction standards, the increased costs shall be borne by the railway party;  
(3) Where a railway and a road constructed simultaneously need grade-separated crossing facilities, or where an existing railway level crossing is converted into a grade-separated crossing, the railway party and the road party shall share the construction costs according to the principle of fairness and reasonableness.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　铁路与道路立体交叉设施及其附属安全设施竣工验收合格后，应当按照国家有关规定移交有关单位管理、维护。

**Article 19** After the completion acceptance inspection of grade-separated crossing facilities between railways and roads and their ancillary safety facilities, they shall be handed over to the relevant units for management and maintenance in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　专用铁路、铁路专用线需要与公用铁路网接轨的，应当符合国家有关铁路建设、运输的安全管理规定。

**Article 20** Where a special railway or a railway siding needs to be connected to the public railway network, it shall comply with the national safety management provisions on railway construction and transportation.

### 第三章　铁路专用设备质量安全

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　设计、制造、维修或者进口新型铁路机车车辆，应当符合国家标准、行业标准，并分别向国务院铁路行业监督管理部门申请领取型号合格证、制造许可证、维修许可证或者进口许可证，具体办法由国务院铁路行业监督管理部门制定。  
铁路机车车辆的制造、维修、使用单位应当遵守有关产品质量的法律、行政法规以及国家其他有关规定，确保投入使用的机车车辆符合安全运营要求。

**Article 21** The design, manufacture, maintenance, or import of new types of railway locomotives and rolling stock shall conform to national standards and industry standards, and an application shall be made to the railway industry regulatory department of the State Council for obtaining a type certificate, manufacturing license, maintenance license, or import license respectively. The specific measures shall be formulated by the railway industry regulatory department of the State Council.  
The units manufacturing, maintaining, and using railway locomotives and rolling stock shall comply with the laws and administrative regulations on product quality and other relevant provisions of the State, and ensure that the locomotives and rolling stock put into use meet the operational safety requirements.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　生产铁路道岔及其转辙设备、铁路信号控制软件和控制设备、铁路通信设备、铁路牵引供电设备的企业，应当符合下列条件并经国务院铁路行业监督管理部门依法审查批准：  
(一)有按照国家标准、行业标准检测、检验合格的专业生产设备；  
(二)有相应的专业技术人员；  
(三)有完善的产品质量保证体系和安全管理制度；  
(四)法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 22** Enterprises that produce railway switches and their switch machines, railway signal control software and control equipment, railway communication equipment, and railway traction power supply equipment shall meet the following conditions and be subject to examination and approval by the railway industry regulatory department of the State Council according to law:  
(1) Having specialized production equipment that has passed testing and inspection in accordance with national standards and industry standards;  
(2) Having corresponding specialized technical personnel;  
(3) Having a complete product quality assurance system and safety management system;  
(4) Other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　铁路机车车辆以外的直接影响铁路运输安全的铁路专用设备，依法应当进行产品认证的，经认证合格方可出厂、销售、进口、使用。

**Article 23** For railway special equipment other than railway locomotives and rolling stock that directly affects railway transport safety and is subject to product certification according to law, such equipment may be released, sold, imported, or used only after passing the certification.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　用于危险化学品和放射性物品运输的铁路罐车、专用车辆以及其他容器的生产和检测、检验，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 24** The production and testing and inspection of railway tank cars, special vehicles, and other containers for the transport of dangerous chemicals and radioactive substances shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　用于铁路运输的安全检测、监控、防护设施设备，集装箱和集装化用具等运输器具，专用装卸机械、索具、篷布、装载加固材料或者装置，以及运输包装、货物装载加固等，应当符合国家标准、行业标准和技术规范。

**Article 25** Safety detection, monitoring, and protection facilities and equipment used for railway transport, containers and containerized appliances and other transport implements, specialized loading and unloading machinery, rigging, tarpaulins, loading and securing materials or devices, as well as transport packaging and cargo loading and securing, shall conform to national standards, industry standards, and technical specifications.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　铁路机车车辆以及其他铁路专用设备存在缺陷，即由于设计、制造、标识等原因导致同一批次、型号或者类别的铁路专用设备普遍存在不符合保障人身、财产安全的国家标准、行业标准的情形或者其他危及人身、财产安全的不合理危险的，应当立即停止生产、销售、进口、使用；设备制造者应当召回缺陷产品，采取措施消除缺陷。具体办法由国务院铁路行业监督管理部门制定。

**Article 26** Where railway locomotives and rolling stock or other railway special equipment have defects, that is, due to reasons such as design, manufacture, or labeling, the same batch, model, or category of railway special equipment generally fails to comply with national standards or industry standards for ensuring personal and property safety, or has other unreasonable risks endangering personal and property safety, production, sale, import, and use of such equipment shall be immediately stopped; the equipment manufacturer shall recall the defective products and take measures to eliminate the defects. Specific measures shall be formulated by the railway industry regulatory department of the State Council.

### 第四章　铁路线路安全

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　铁路线路两侧应当设立铁路线路安全保护区。铁路线路安全保护区的范围，从铁路线路路堤坡脚、路堑坡顶或者铁路桥梁(含铁路、道路两用桥，下同)外侧起向外的距离分别为：  
(一)城市市区高速铁路为10米，其他铁路为8米；  
(二)城市郊区居民居住区高速铁路为12米，其他铁路为10米；  
(三)村镇居民居住区高速铁路为15米，其他铁路为12米；  
(四)其他地区高速铁路为20米，其他铁路为15米。  
前款规定距离不能满足铁路运输安全保护需要的，由铁路建设单位或者铁路运输企业提出方案，铁路监督管理机构或者县级以上地方人民政府依照本条第三款规定程序划定。  
在铁路用地范围内划定铁路线路安全保护区的，由铁路监督管理机构组织铁路建设单位或者铁路运输企业划定并公告。在铁路用地范围外划定铁路线路安全保护区的，由县级以上地方人民政府根据保障铁路运输安全和节约用地的原则，组织有关铁路监督管理机构、县级以上地方人民政府国土资源等部门划定并公告。  
铁路线路安全保护区与公路建筑控制区、河道管理范围、水利工程管理和保护范围、航道保护范围或者石油、电力以及其他重要设施保护区重叠的，由县级以上地方人民政府组织有关部门依照法律、行政法规的规定协商划定并公告。  
新建、改建铁路的铁路线路安全保护区范围，应当自铁路建设工程初步设计批准之日起30日内，由县级以上地方人民政府依照本条例的规定划定并公告。铁路建设单位或者铁路运输企业应当根据工程竣工资料进行勘界，绘制铁路线路安全保护区平面图，并根据平面图设立标桩。

**Article 27** Railway line safety protection areas shall be established on both sides of railway lines. The scope of the railway line safety protection area, measured outward from the toe of the railway embankment, the top of the cutting, or the outer edge of the railway bridge (including bridges used jointly by railways and roads, hereinafter the same), shall be as follows:  
(1) In urban areas: 10 meters for high-speed railways and 8 meters for other railways;  
(2) In suburban residential areas: 12 meters for high-speed railways and 10 meters for other railways;  
(3) In village and town residential areas: 15 meters for high-speed railways and 12 meters for other railways;  
(4) In other areas: 20 meters for high-speed railways and 15 meters for other railways.  
Where the distances specified in the preceding paragraph cannot meet the needs of railway transport safety protection, a proposal shall be made by the railway construction unit or the railway transport enterprise, and the railway regulatory institution or the local people's government at or above the county level shall delimit the area in accordance with the procedures specified in paragraph 3 of this Article.  
Where the railway line safety protection area is delimited within the railway land area, the railway regulatory institution shall organize the railway construction unit or the railway transport enterprise to delimit it and make a public announcement. Where the railway line safety protection area is delimited outside the railway land area, the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the principles of ensuring railway transport safety and economizing on land, organize the relevant railway regulatory institution, the land and resources department of the local people's government at or above the county level, and other departments to delimit it and make a public announcement.  
Where the railway line safety protection area overlaps with the highway building control area, the river management scope, the water conservancy project management and protection scope, the navigation channel protection scope, or the oil, electric power, or other important facility protection areas, the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize the relevant departments to negotiate and delimit the area in accordance with laws and administrative regulations and make a public announcement.  
The scope of the railway line safety protection area for a newly built or rebuilt railway shall be delimited and publicly announced by the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with these Regulations within 30 days from the date of approval of the preliminary design of the railway construction project. The railway construction unit or the railway transport enterprise shall, based on the project completion data, conduct boundary survey, draw a plan of the railway line safety protection area, and set up boundary markers based on the plan.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　设计开行时速120公里以上列车的铁路应当实行全封闭管理。铁路建设单位或者铁路运输企业应当按照国务院铁路行业监督管理部门的规定在铁路用地范围内设置封闭设施和警示标志。

**Article 28** Railways designed for trains running at a speed of more than 120 kilometers per hour shall implement fully enclosed management. The railway construction unit or the railway transport enterprise shall, in accordance with the provisions of the railway industry regulatory department of the State Council, install enclosure facilities and warning signs within the railway land area.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　禁止在铁路线路安全保护区内烧荒、放养牲畜、种植影响铁路线路安全和行车瞭望的树木等植物。  
禁止向铁路线路安全保护区排污、倾倒垃圾以及其他危害铁路安全的物质。

**Article 29** It is prohibited to burn grass, graze livestock, or plant trees or other plants that affect railway line safety or train observation within the railway line safety protection area.  
It is prohibited to discharge pollutants, dump garbage, or other substances that endanger railway safety into the railway line safety protection area.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　在铁路线路安全保护区内建造建筑物、构筑物等设施，取土、挖砂、挖沟、采空作业或者堆放、悬挂物品，应当征得铁路运输企业同意并签订安全协议，遵守保证铁路安全的国家标准、行业标准和施工安全规范，采取措施防止影响铁路运输安全。铁路运输企业应当派员对施工现场实行安全监督。

**Article 30** Where buildings, structures, or other facilities are constructed, earth is taken, sand is excavated, ditches are dug, mining activities are carried out, or articles are stacked or hung within the railway line safety protection area, the consent of the railway transport enterprise shall be obtained and a safety agreement shall be signed, and the national standards, industry standards, and construction safety specifications for ensuring railway safety shall be complied with, and measures shall be taken to prevent affecting railway transport safety. The railway transport enterprise shall dispatch personnel to carry out safety supervision over the construction site.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　铁路线路安全保护区内既有的建筑物、构筑物危及铁路运输安全的，应当采取必要的安全防护措施；采取安全防护措施后仍不能保证安全的，依照有关法律的规定拆除。  
拆除铁路线路安全保护区内的建筑物、构筑物，清理铁路线路安全保护区内的植物，或者对他人在铁路线路安全保护区内已依法取得的采矿权等合法权利予以限制，给他人造成损失的，应当依法给予补偿或者采取必要的补救措施。但是，拆除非法建设的建筑物、构筑物的除外。

**Article 31** Where existing buildings or structures within the railway line safety protection area endanger railway transport safety, necessary safety protection measures shall be taken; where safety still cannot be ensured after taking such measures, the buildings or structures shall be demolished in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws.  
Where the demolition of buildings or structures within the railway line safety protection area, the clearing of plants within the railway line safety protection area, or the restriction of the lawful rights of others, such as mining rights that have been lawfully obtained within the railway line safety protection area, causes losses to others, compensation shall be given according to law or necessary remedial measures shall be taken. However, this shall not apply to the demolition of illegally constructed buildings or structures.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　在铁路线路安全保护区及其邻近区域建造或者设置的建筑物、构筑物、设备等，不得进入国家规定的铁路建筑限界。

**Article 32** Buildings, structures, equipment, or other items constructed or installed within the railway line safety protection area or its adjacent areas shall not intrude into the railway construction clearance prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　在铁路线路两侧建造、设立生产、加工、储存或者销售易燃、易爆或者放射性物品等危险物品的场所、仓库，应当符合国家标准、行业标准规定的安全防护距离。

**Article 33** Where places or warehouses for producing, processing, storing, or selling dangerous substances such as flammable, explosive, or radioactive articles are constructed or established on both sides of a railway line, they shall comply with the safety protection distances prescribed by national standards and industry standards.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　在铁路线路两侧从事采矿、采石或者爆破作业，应当遵守有关采矿和民用爆破的法律法规，符合国家标准、行业标准和铁路安全保护要求。  
在铁路线路路堤坡脚、路堑坡顶、铁路桥梁外侧起向外各1000米范围内，以及在铁路隧道上方中心线两侧各1000米范围内，确需从事露天采矿、采石或者爆破作业的，应当与铁路运输企业协商一致，依照有关法律法规的规定报县级以上地方人民政府有关部门批准，采取安全防护措施后方可进行。

**Article 34** Mining, quarrying, or blasting operations carried out on both sides of a railway line shall comply with the laws and regulations on mining and civil blasting, and meet national standards, industry standards, and railway safety protection requirements.  
Where it is truly necessary to carry out open-pit mining, quarrying, or blasting operations within a range of 1,000 meters outward from the toe of the railway embankment, the top of the cutting, or the outer edge of the railway bridge, and within a range of 1,000 meters on both sides of the centerline above the railway tunnel, the matter shall be agreed upon with the railway transport enterprise, and shall be submitted for approval to the relevant department of the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, and such operations may be carried out only after safety protection measures are taken.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　高速铁路线路路堤坡脚、路堑坡顶或者铁路桥梁外侧起向外各200米范围内禁止抽取地下水。  
在前款规定范围外，高速铁路线路经过的区域属于地面沉降区域，抽取地下水危及高速铁路安全的，应当设置地下水禁止开采区或者限制开采区，具体范围由铁路监督管理机构会同县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门提出方案，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准并公告。

**Article 35** Extraction of groundwater is prohibited within a range of 200 meters outward from the toe of the high-speed railway embankment, the top of the cutting, or the outer edge of the railway bridge.  
Outside the range specified in the preceding paragraph, where the area through which the high-speed railway passes is a land subsidence area and groundwater extraction endangers the safety of the high-speed railway, a groundwater prohibition zone or a restricted extraction zone shall be established. The specific scope shall be proposed by the railway regulatory institution jointly with the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level, and submitted to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval and public announcement.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　在电气化铁路附近从事排放粉尘、烟尘及腐蚀性气体的生产活动，超过国家规定的排放标准，危及铁路运输安全的，由县级以上地方人民政府有关部门依法责令整改，消除安全隐患。

**Article 36** Where production activities involving the discharge of dust, smoke, or corrosive gases near electrified railways exceed the emission standards prescribed by the State and endanger railway transport safety, the relevant department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification according to law to eliminate the safety hazard.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　任何单位和个人不得擅自在铁路桥梁跨越处河道上下游各1000米范围内围垦造田、拦河筑坝、架设浮桥或者修建其他影响铁路桥梁安全的设施。  
因特殊原因确需在前款规定的范围内进行围垦造田、拦河筑坝、架设浮桥等活动的，应当进行安全论证，负责审批的机关在批准前应当征求有关铁路运输企业的意见。

**Article 37** No unit or individual may, without authorization, reclaim land for farming, build dams across rivers, erect floating bridges, or construct other facilities affecting the safety of railway bridges within a range of 1,000 meters upstream and downstream of the river at the railway bridge crossing.  
Where it is truly necessary due to special reasons to carry out activities such as land reclamation for farming, building dams across rivers, or erecting floating bridges within the scope specified in the preceding paragraph, a safety demonstration shall be conducted, and the approving authority shall, before granting approval, seek the opinions of the relevant railway transport enterprise.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　禁止在铁路桥梁跨越处河道上下游的下列范围内采砂、淘金：  
(一)跨河桥长500米以上的铁路桥梁，河道上游500米，下游3000米；  
(二)跨河桥长100米以上不足500米的铁路桥梁，河道上游500米，下游2000米；  
(三)跨河桥长不足100米的铁路桥梁，河道上游500米，下游1000米。  
有关部门依法在铁路桥梁跨越处河道上下游划定的禁采范围大于前款规定的禁采范围的，按照划定的禁采范围执行。  
县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门、国土资源主管部门应当按照各自职责划定禁采区域、设置禁采标志，制止非法采砂、淘金行为。

**Article 38** Sand mining and gold panning are prohibited within the following ranges upstream and downstream of the river at the railway bridge crossing:  
(1) For railway bridges with a river-crossing bridge length of 500 meters or more: 500 meters upstream and 3,000 meters downstream;  
(2) For railway bridges with a river-crossing bridge length of 100 meters or more but less than 500 meters: 500 meters upstream and 2,000 meters downstream;  
(3) For railway bridges with a river-crossing bridge length of less than 100 meters: 500 meters upstream and 1,000 meters downstream.  
Where the prohibited mining range delimited by the relevant department according to law upstream and downstream of the river at the railway bridge crossing is larger than the prohibited mining range specified in the preceding paragraph, the delimited prohibited mining range shall prevail.  
The water administrative department and the land and resources administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to their respective duties, delimit prohibited mining areas, set up prohibited mining signs, and stop illegal sand mining and gold panning activities.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　在铁路桥梁跨越处河道上下游各500米范围内进行疏浚作业，应当进行安全技术评价，有关河道、航道管理部门应当征求铁路运输企业的意见，确认安全或者采取安全技术措施后，方可批准进行疏浚作业。但是，依法进行河道、航道日常养护、疏浚作业的除外。

**Article 39** Dredging operations carried out within a range of 500 meters upstream and downstream of the river at the railway bridge crossing shall undergo a safety technical assessment. The relevant river and navigation channel management departments shall seek the opinions of the railway transport enterprise, and may approve the dredging operations only after confirming safety or taking safety technical measures. However, this shall not apply to routine maintenance and dredging operations of rivers and navigation channels carried out according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　铁路、道路两用桥由所在地铁路运输企业和道路管理部门或者道路经营企业定期检查、共同维护，保证桥梁处于安全的技术状态。  
铁路、道路两用桥的墩、梁等共用部分的检测、维修由铁路运输企业和道路管理部门或者道路经营企业共同负责，所需费用按照公平合理的原则分担。

**Article 40** A bridge used jointly by railways and roads shall be periodically inspected and jointly maintained by the local railway transport enterprise and the road management department or road operating enterprise to ensure that the bridge is in a safe technical condition.  
The testing and repair of common parts such as piers and beams of the bridge used jointly by railways and roads shall be jointly undertaken by the railway transport enterprise and the road management department or road operating enterprise, and the necessary costs shall be shared according to the principle of fairness and reasonableness.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　铁路的重要桥梁和隧道按照国家有关规定由中国人民武装警察部队负责守卫。

**Article 41** Important railway bridges and tunnels shall be guarded by the Chinese People's Armed Police Force in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　船舶通过铁路桥梁应当符合桥梁的通航净空高度并遵守航行规则。  
桥区航标中的桥梁航标、桥柱标、桥梁水尺标由铁路运输企业负责设置、维护，水面航标由铁路运输企业负责设置，航道管理部门负责维护。

**Article 42** Ships passing through a railway bridge shall comply with the navigable clearance height of the bridge and abide by the navigation rules.  
Among the navigation marks in the bridge area, the bridge navigation marks, bridge pier marks, and bridge water gauge marks shall be installed and maintained by the railway transport enterprise; water surface navigation marks shall be installed by the railway transport enterprise and maintained by the navigation channel management department.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　下穿铁路桥梁、涵洞的道路应当按照国家标准设置车辆通过限高、限宽标志和限高防护架。城市道路的限高、限宽标志由当地人民政府指定的部门设置并维护，公路的限高、限宽标志由公路管理部门设置并维护。限高防护架在铁路桥梁、涵洞、道路建设时设置，由铁路运输企业负责维护。  
机动车通过下穿铁路桥梁、涵洞的道路，应当遵守限高、限宽规定。  
下穿铁路涵洞的管理单位负责涵洞的日常管理、维护，防止淤塞、积水。

**Article 43** Roads passing under railway bridges or culverts shall be equipped with height and width limit signs for vehicle passage and height limit protective frames in accordance with national standards. The height and width limit signs for urban roads shall be installed and maintained by the department designated by the local people's government, and those for highways shall be installed and maintained by the highway management department. The height limit protective frames shall be installed during the construction of the railway bridge, culvert, or road, and shall be maintained by the railway transport enterprise.  
Motor vehicles passing under railway bridges or culverts shall comply with the height and width limit provisions.  
The management unit of a culvert passing under a railway shall be responsible for the daily management and maintenance of the culvert to prevent silting and water accumulation.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　铁路线路安全保护区内的道路和铁路线路路堑上的道路、跨越铁路线路的道路桥梁，应当按照国家有关规定设置防止车辆以及其他物体进入、坠入铁路线路的安全防护设施和警示标志，并由道路管理部门或者道路经营企业维护、管理。

**Article 44** Roads within the railway line safety protection area, roads on railway cuttings, and road bridges crossing railway lines shall be equipped with safety protection facilities and warning signs to prevent vehicles and other objects from entering or falling onto the railway line in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and shall be maintained and managed by the road management department or the road operating enterprise.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　架设、铺设铁路信号和通信线路、杆塔应当符合国家标准、行业标准和铁路安全防护要求。铁路运输企业、为铁路运输提供服务的电信企业应当加强对铁路信号和通信线路、杆塔的维护和管理。

**Article 45** The erection and laying of railway signal and communication lines and poles/towers shall conform to national standards, industry standards, and railway safety protection requirements. Railway transport enterprises and telecommunications enterprises providing services for railway transport shall strengthen the maintenance and management of railway signal and communication lines and poles/towers.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　设置或者拓宽铁路道口、铁路人行过道，应当征得铁路运输企业的同意。

**Article 46** The installation or widening of a railway level crossing or a pedestrian crossing over a railway shall obtain the consent of the railway transport enterprise.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　铁路与道路交叉的无人看守道口应当按照国家标准设置警示标志；有人看守道口应当设置移动栏杆、列车接近报警装置、警示灯、警示标志、铁路道口路段标线等安全防护设施。  
道口移动栏杆、列车接近报警装置、警示灯等安全防护设施由铁路运输企业设置、维护；警示标志、铁路道口路段标线由铁路道口所在地的道路管理部门设置、维护。

**Article 47** Unguarded level crossings where a railway and a road intersect shall be equipped with warning signs in accordance with national standards; guarded level crossings shall be equipped with safety protection facilities such as movable barriers, train approach warning devices, warning lights, warning signs, and road markings at the railway crossing.  
Safety protection facilities such as movable barriers, train approach warning devices, and warning lights at the crossing shall be installed and maintained by the railway transport enterprise; warning signs and road markings at the railway crossing shall be installed and maintained by the road management department at the location of the railway level crossing.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　机动车或者非机动车在铁路道口内发生故障或者装载物掉落的，应当立即将故障车辆或者掉落的装载物移至铁路道口停止线以外或者铁路线路最外侧钢轨5米以外的安全地点。无法立即移至安全地点的，应当立即报告铁路道口看守人员；在无人看守道口，应当立即在道口两端采取措施拦停列车，并就近通知铁路车站或者公安机关。

**Article 48** Where a motor vehicle or non-motor vehicle breaks down or its loaded articles fall within a railway level crossing, the broken-down vehicle or fallen loaded articles shall be immediately moved to a safe place beyond the stop line of the railway level crossing or at least 5 meters away from the outermost rail of the railway line. If it cannot be moved to a safe place immediately, the crossing attendant shall be immediately notified; at an unguarded crossing, measures shall be immediately taken at both ends of the crossing to stop trains, and the nearest railway station or public security organ shall be notified.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　履带车辆等可能损坏铁路设施设备的车辆、物体通过铁路道口，应当提前通知铁路道口管理单位，在其协助、指导下通过，并采取相应的安全防护措施。

**Article 49** Vehicles or objects such as tracked vehicles that may damage railway facilities or equipment, when passing through a railway level crossing, shall notify the railway crossing management unit in advance, pass under its assistance and guidance, and take corresponding safety protection measures.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　在下列地点，铁路运输企业应当按照国家标准、行业标准设置易于识别的警示、保护标志：  
(一)铁路桥梁、隧道的两端；  
(二)铁路信号、通信光(电)缆的埋设、铺设地点；  
(三)电气化铁路接触网、自动闭塞供电线路和电力贯通线路等电力设施附近易发生危险的地点。

**Article 50** At the following locations, the railway transport enterprise shall install easily identifiable warning and protection signs in accordance with national standards and industry standards:  
(1) At both ends of railway bridges and tunnels;  
(2) At locations where railway signal and communication optical (electric) cables are buried or laid;  
(3) At locations prone to danger near power facilities such as electrified railway catenary, automatic block power supply lines, and power through lines.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　禁止毁坏铁路线路、站台等设施设备和铁路路基、护坡、排水沟、防护林木、护坡草坪、铁路线路封闭网及其他铁路防护设施。

**Article 51** It shall be prohibited to damage railway lines, platforms and other facilities and equipment, as well as railway subgrades, protective slopes, drainage ditches, protective trees, slope-protecting lawns, railway line enclosures and other railway protective facilities.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　禁止实施下列危及铁路通信、信号设施安全的行为：  
(一)在埋有地下光(电)缆设施的地面上方进行钻探，堆放重物、垃圾，焚烧物品，倾倒腐蚀性物质；  
(二)在地下光(电)缆两侧各1米的范围内建造、搭建建筑物、构筑物等设施；  
(三)在地下光(电)缆两侧各1米的范围内挖砂、取土；  
(四)在过河光(电)缆两侧各100米的范围内挖砂、抛锚或者进行其他危及光(电)缆安全的作业。

**Article 52** It shall be prohibited to commit the following acts that endanger the safety of railway communication and signaling facilities:  
(1) drilling, piling heavy objects or garbage, burning articles, or dumping corrosive substances above the ground where underground optical (electrical) cables are buried;  
(2) constructing or erecting buildings, structures or other facilities within a range of 1 meter on each side of underground optical (electrical) cables;  
(3) dredging sand or excavating soil within a range of 1 meter on each side of underground optical (electrical) cables;  
(4) dredging sand, dropping anchor, or carrying out other operations that endanger the safety of optical (electrical) cables within a range of 100 meters on each side of river-crossing optical (electrical) cables.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　禁止实施下列危害电气化铁路设施的行为：  
(一)向电气化铁路接触网抛掷物品；  
(二)在铁路电力线路导线两侧各500米的范围内升放风筝、气球等低空飘浮物体；  
(三)攀登铁路电力线路杆塔或者在杆塔上架设、安装其他设施设备；  
(四)在铁路电力线路杆塔、拉线周围20米范围内取土、打桩、钻探或者倾倒有害化学物品；  
(五)触碰电气化铁路接触网。

**Article 53** It shall be prohibited to commit the following acts that endanger electrified railway facilities:  
(1) throwing or casting objects at the overhead contact lines of electrified railways;  
(2) flying kites, balloons or other low-altitude floating objects within a range of 500 meters on each side of railway power line conductors;  
(3) climbing railway power line poles and towers, or erecting or installing other facilities and equipment on poles and towers;  
(4) excavating soil, driving piles, drilling, or dumping harmful chemicals within a 20-meter radius of railway power line poles, towers, or guy wires;  
(5) touching the overhead contact lines of electrified railways.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　县级以上各级人民政府及其有关部门、铁路运输企业应当依照地质灾害防治法律法规的规定，加强铁路沿线地质灾害的预防、治理和应急处理等工作。

**Article 54** The people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments, and railway transport enterprises shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations on geological disaster prevention and control, strengthen the prevention, control, and emergency response work regarding geological disasters along railway lines.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　铁路运输企业应当对铁路线路、铁路防护设施和警示标志进行经常性巡查和维护；对巡查中发现的安全问题应当立即处理，不能立即处理的应当及时报告铁路监督管理机构。巡查和处理情况应当记录留存。

**Article 55** Railway transport enterprises shall conduct regular inspection and maintenance of railway lines, railway protective facilities, and warning signs; they shall immediately address safety problems discovered during inspections, and those that cannot be immediately addressed shall be promptly reported to the railway supervision and administration authority. The inspection and handling situations shall be recorded and retained.

### 第五章　铁路运营安全

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　铁路运输企业应当依照法律、行政法规和国务院铁路行业监督管理部门的规定，制定铁路运输安全管理制度，完善相关作业程序，保障铁路旅客和货物运输安全。

**Article 56** Railway transport enterprises shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations and the provisions of the railway industry regulatory authority of the State Council, formulate railway transport safety management systems, improve relevant operating procedures, and ensure the safety of railway passenger and freight transport.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　铁路机车车辆的驾驶人员应当参加国务院铁路行业监督管理部门组织的考试，考试合格方可上岗。具体办法由国务院铁路行业监督管理部门制定。

**Article 57** Drivers of railway locomotives and rolling stock shall take the examinations organized by the railway industry regulatory authority of the State Council; they may only take up their posts after passing the examinations. The specific measures shall be formulated by the railway industry regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　铁路运输企业应当加强铁路专业技术岗位和主要行车工种岗位从业人员的业务培训和安全培训，提高从业人员的业务技能和安全意识。

**Article 58** Railway transport enterprises shall strengthen the professional training and safety training of employees in railway professional technical positions and main train operation positions, and improve the professional skills and safety awareness of the employees.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　铁路运输企业应当加强运输过程中的安全防护，使用的运输工具、装载加固设备以及其他专用设施设备应当符合国家标准、行业标准和安全要求。

**Article 59** Railway transport enterprises shall strengthen safety protection during the transport process, and the transport vehicles, loading and securing equipment, and other special facilities and equipment used shall comply with national standards, industry standards, and safety requirements.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　铁路运输企业应当建立健全铁路设施设备的检查防护制度，加强对铁路设施设备的日常维护检修，确保铁路设施设备性能完好和安全运行。  
铁路运输企业的从业人员应当按照操作规程使用、管理铁路设施设备。

**Article 60** Railway transport enterprises shall establish and improve the inspection and protection system for railway facilities and equipment, strengthen the daily maintenance and repair of railway facilities and equipment, and ensure that the performance of railway facilities and equipment is sound and safe operation.  
Employees of railway transport enterprises shall use and manage railway facilities and equipment in accordance with operating procedures.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　在法定假日和传统节日等铁路运输高峰期或者恶劣气象条件下，铁路运输企业应当采取必要的安全应急管理措施，加强铁路运输安全检查，确保运输安全。

**Article 61** During peak periods of railway transport such as statutory holidays and traditional festivals, or under adverse weather conditions, railway transport enterprises shall take necessary safety emergency management measures, strengthen railway transport safety inspection, and ensure transport safety.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　铁路运输企业应当在列车、车站等场所公告旅客、列车工作人员以及其他进站人员遵守的安全管理规定。

**Article 62** Artigo 62 Railway transport enterprises shall announce in trains, stations and other places the safety management regulations that passengers, train staff, and other persons entering the station shall comply with.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　公安机关应当按照职责分工，维护车站、列车等铁路场所和铁路沿线的治安秩序。

**Article 63** Public security organs shall, according to their division of responsibilities, maintain public security order in railway places such as stations and trains, and along railway lines.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　铁路运输企业应当按照国务院铁路行业监督管理部门的规定实施火车票实名购买、查验制度。  
实施火车票实名购买、查验制度的，旅客应当凭有效身份证件购票乘车；对车票所记载身份信息与所持身份证件或者真实身份不符的持票人，铁路运输企业有权拒绝其进站乘车。  
铁路运输企业应当采取有效措施为旅客实名购票、乘车提供便利，并加强对旅客身份信息的保护。铁路运输企业工作人员不得窃取、泄露旅客身份信息。

**Article 64** Railway transport enterprises shall, in accordance with the provisions of the railway industry regulatory authority of the State Council, implement a real-name ticket purchasing and inspection system for train tickets.  
Where a real-name ticket purchasing and inspection system is implemented, passengers shall purchase tickets and board trains with valid identity documents; for a ticket holder whose identity information recorded on the ticket does not match the identity document held or the true identity, the railway transport enterprise shall have the right to refuse their entry into the station and boarding.  
Railway transport enterprises shall take effective measures to facilitate passengers' real-name ticket purchasing and boarding, and shall strengthen the protection of passengers' identity information. Staff of railway transport enterprises shall not steal or disclose passengers' identity information.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　铁路运输企业应当依照法律、行政法规和国务院铁路行业监督管理部门的规定，对旅客及其随身携带、托运的行李物品进行安全检查。  
从事安全检查的工作人员应当佩戴安全检查标志，依法履行安全检查职责，并有权拒绝不接受安全检查的旅客进站乘车和托运行李物品。

**Article 65** Railway transport enterprises shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations and the provisions of the railway industry regulatory authority of the State Council, conduct safety inspections on passengers and their carry-on and checked baggage.  
Staff engaged in safety inspections shall wear safety inspection signs, perform safety inspection duties according to law, and shall have the right to refuse entry into the station, boarding, and checked baggage for passengers who do not accept safety inspections.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　旅客应当接受并配合铁路运输企业在车站、列车实施的安全检查，不得违法携带、夹带管制器具，不得违法携带、托运烟花爆竹、枪支弹药等危险物品或者其他违禁物品。  
禁止或者限制携带的物品种类及其数量由国务院铁路行业监督管理部门会同公安机关规定，并在车站、列车等场所公布。

**Article 66** Passengers shall accept and cooperate with the safety inspections conducted by railway transport enterprises in stations and trains, and shall not illegally carry or conceal controlled instruments, and shall not illegally carry or check dangerous goods such as fireworks, firecrackers, firearms, ammunition, or other prohibited articles.  
The types and quantities of articles that are prohibited or restricted from being carried shall be prescribed by the railway industry regulatory authority of the State Council in conjunction with the public security organs, and shall be published in stations, trains and other places.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　铁路运输托运人托运货物、行李、包裹，不得有下列行为：  
(一)匿报、谎报货物品名、性质、重量；  
(二)在普通货物中夹带危险货物，或者在危险货物中夹带禁止配装的货物；  
(三)装车、装箱超过规定重量。

**Article 67** When consigning goods, luggage, or parcels for railway transport, the consignor shall not commit the following acts:  
(1) concealing or misrepresenting the name, nature, or weight of the goods;  
(2) inserting dangerous goods into ordinary goods, or inserting goods that are prohibited from being loaded together into dangerous goods;  
(3) loading vehicles or containers exceeding the prescribed weight.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　铁路运输企业应当对承运的货物进行安全检查，并不得有下列行为：  
(一)在非危险货物办理站办理危险货物承运手续；  
(二)承运未接受安全检查的货物；  
(三)承运不符合安全规定、可能危害铁路运输安全的货物。

**Article 68** Railway transport enterprises shall conduct safety inspections on the goods they accept for carriage, and shall not commit the following acts:  
(1) undertaking the carriage procedures for dangerous goods at non-dangerous goods handling stations;  
(2) accepting goods that have not undergone safety inspection;  
(3) accepting goods that do not comply with safety regulations and may endanger railway transport safety.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　运输危险货物应当依照法律法规和国家其他有关规定使用专用的设施设备，托运人应当配备必要的押运人员和应急处理器材、设备以及防护用品，并使危险货物始终处于押运人员的监管之下；危险货物发生被盗、丢失、泄漏等情况，应当按照国家有关规定及时报告。

**Article 69** For the transport of dangerous goods, special facilities and equipment shall be used in accordance with laws, regulations and other relevant state provisions; the consignor shall equip necessary escort personnel, emergency response materials, equipment and protective articles, and shall ensure that the dangerous goods are always under the supervision of the escort personnel; if dangerous goods are stolen, lost, leaked, etc., it shall be promptly reported in accordance with relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　办理危险货物运输业务的工作人员和装卸人员、押运人员，应当掌握危险货物的性质、危害特性、包装容器的使用特性和发生意外的应急措施。

**Article 70** The staff handling dangerous goods transport business, loading and unloading personnel, and escort personnel shall master the nature, hazardous characteristics of dangerous goods, the usage characteristics of packaging containers, and emergency measures in the event of accidents.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　铁路运输企业和托运人应当按照操作规程包装、装卸、运输危险货物，防止危险货物泄漏、爆炸。

**Article 71** Railway transport enterprises and consignors shall package, load, unload, and transport dangerous goods in accordance with operating procedures to prevent leakage or explosion of dangerous goods.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　铁路运输企业和托运人应当依照法律法规和国家其他有关规定包装、装载、押运特殊药品，防止特殊药品在运输过程中被盗、被劫或者发生丢失。

**Article 72** Railway transport enterprises and consignors shall package, load, and escort special pharmaceuticals in accordance with laws, regulations and other relevant state provisions to prevent such pharmaceuticals from being stolen, robbed, or lost during transport.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　铁路管理信息系统及其设施的建设和使用，应当符合法律法规和国家其他有关规定的安全技术要求。  
铁路运输企业应当建立网络与信息安全应急保障体系，并配备相应的专业技术人员负责网络和信息系统的安全管理工作。

**Article 73** The construction and use of railway management information systems and their facilities shall comply with the safety technical requirements of laws, regulations and other relevant state provisions.  
Railway transport enterprises shall establish a network and information security emergency support system, and shall equip corresponding professional technical personnel to be responsible for the safety management of networks and information systems.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　禁止使用无线电台(站)以及其他仪器、装置干扰铁路运营指挥调度无线电频率的正常使用。  
铁路运营指挥调度无线电频率受到干扰的，铁路运输企业应当立即采取排查措施并报告无线电管理机构、铁路监管部门；无线电管理机构、铁路监管部门应当依法排除干扰。

**Article 74** It shall be prohibited to use radio stations or other instruments or devices to interfere with the normal use of radio frequencies for railway operation command and dispatch.  
If the radio frequencies for railway operation command and dispatch are interfered, railway transport enterprises shall immediately take investigation measures and report to the radio regulatory authority and the railway regulatory department; the radio regulatory authority and the railway regulatory department shall eliminate the interference according to law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　电力企业应当依法保障铁路运输所需电力的持续供应，并保证供电质量。  
铁路运输企业应当加强用电安全管理，合理配置供电电源和应急自备电源。  
遇有特殊情况影响铁路电力供应的，电力企业和铁路运输企业应当按照各自职责及时组织抢修，尽快恢复正常供电。

**Article 75** Power enterprises shall, according to law, ensure the continuous supply of electricity required for railway transport and guarantee the quality of power supply.  
Railway transport enterprises shall strengthen electricity safety management, and reasonably configure power supply sources and emergency self-contained power supplies.  
In the event of special circumstances affecting railway power supply, power enterprises and railway transport enterprises shall, according to their respective duties, promptly organize emergency repairs to restore normal power supply as soon as possible.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　铁路运输企业应当加强铁路运营食品安全管理，遵守有关食品安全管理的法律法规和国家其他有关规定，保证食品安全。

**Article 76** Railway transport enterprises shall strengthen food safety management in railway operations, comply with laws, regulations and other relevant state provisions on food safety management, and ensure food safety.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　禁止实施下列危害铁路安全的行为：  
(一)非法拦截列车、阻断铁路运输；  
(二)扰乱铁路运输指挥调度机构以及车站、列车的正常秩序；  
(三)在铁路线路上放置、遗弃障碍物；  
(四)击打列车；  
(五)擅自移动铁路线路上的机车车辆，或者擅自开启列车车门、违规操纵列车紧急制动设备；  
(六)拆盗、损毁或者擅自移动铁路设施设备、机车车辆配件、标桩、防护设施和安全标志；  
(七)在铁路线路上行走、坐卧或者在未设道口、人行过道的铁路线路上通过；  
(八)擅自进入铁路线路封闭区域或者在未设置行人通道的铁路桥梁、隧道通行；  
(九)擅自开启、关闭列车的货车阀、盖或者破坏施封状态；  
(十)擅自开启列车中的集装箱箱门，破坏箱体、阀、盖或者施封状态；  
(十一)擅自松动、拆解、移动列车中的货物装载加固材料、装置和设备；  
(十二)钻车、扒车、跳车；  
(十三)从列车上抛扔杂物；  
(十四)在动车组列车上吸烟或者在其他列车的禁烟区域吸烟；  
(十五)强行登乘或者以拒绝下车等方式强占列车；  
(十六)冲击、堵塞、占用进出站通道或者候车区、站台。

**Article 77** It shall be prohibited to commit the following acts that endanger railway safety:  
(1) illegally intercepting trains or blocking railway transport;  
(2) disrupting the normal order of railway transport command and dispatch organizations, stations, or trains;  
(3) placing or abandoning obstacles on railway lines;  
(4) striking trains;  
(5) moving locomotives or rolling stock on railway lines without authorization, or opening train doors without authorization, or illegally operating train emergency braking equipment;  
(6) dismantling, stealing, damaging, or moving railway facilities and equipment, locomotive and rolling stock parts, markers, protective facilities, and safety signs without authorization;  
(7) walking, sitting, or lying on railway lines, or crossing railway lines where there are no level crossings or pedestrian crossings;  
(8) entering enclosed areas of railway lines without authorization, or passing through railway bridges or tunnels where no pedestrian passages are provided;  
(9) opening or closing the valves or covers of freight cars, or breaking the seal status without authorization;  
(10) opening the doors of containers in trains, or damaging the containers, valves, covers, or seal status without authorization;  
(11) loosening, disassembling, or moving cargo loading and securing materials, devices, and equipment in trains without authorization;  
(12) boring under trains, clinging to trains, or jumping from trains;  
(13) throwing debris from trains;  
(14) smoking on EMU trains or in the non-smoking areas of other trains;  
(15) forcibly boarding or occupying trains by refusing to get off;  
(16) charging, blocking, or occupying access passages to stations, waiting areas, or platforms.

### 第六章　监督检查

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　铁路监管部门应当对从事铁路建设、运输、设备制造维修的企业执行本条例的情况实施监督检查，依法查处违反本条例规定的行为，依法组织或者参与铁路安全事故的调查处理。  
铁路监管部门应当建立企业违法行为记录和公告制度，对违反本条例被依法追究法律责任的从事铁路建设、运输、设备制造维修的企业予以公布。

**Article 78** The railway regulatory department shall supervise and inspect the implementation of these Regulations by enterprises engaged in railway construction, transport, and equipment manufacturing and maintenance, investigate and deal with acts violating the provisions of these Regulations according to law, and organize or participate in the investigation and handling of railway safety accidents according to law.  
The railway regulatory department shall establish a system for recording and publicizing enterprise illegal acts, and shall publish the enterprises engaged in railway construction, transport, and equipment manufacturing and maintenance that have been investigated for legal liability according to law for violating these Regulations.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　铁路监管部门应当加强对铁路运输高峰期和恶劣气象条件下运输安全的监督管理，加强对铁路运输的关键环节、重要设施设备的安全状况以及铁路运输突发事件应急预案的建立和落实情况的监督检查。

**Article 79** The railway regulatory department shall strengthen supervision and management of transport safety during peak periods of railway transport and under adverse weather conditions, and shall strengthen supervision and inspection of the safety status of key links and important facilities and equipment of railway transport, as well as the establishment and implementation of emergency response plans for railway transport emergencies.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　铁路监管部门和县级以上人民政府安全生产监督管理部门应当建立信息通报制度和运输安全生产协调机制。发现重大安全隐患，铁路运输企业难以自行排除的，应当及时向铁路监管部门和有关地方人民政府报告。地方人民政府获悉铁路沿线有危及铁路运输安全的重要情况，应当及时通报有关的铁路运输企业和铁路监管部门。

**Article 80** The railway regulatory department and the work safety supervision and management departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall establish an information notification system and a coordination mechanism for transport safety production. If a major safety hazard is discovered and it is difficult for the railway transport enterprise to eliminate it on its own, it shall promptly report to the railway regulatory department and the relevant local people's government. If the local people's government learns of important circumstances along the railway that endanger railway transport safety, it shall promptly notify the relevant railway transport enterprise and the railway regulatory department.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　铁路监管部门发现安全隐患，应当责令有关单位立即排除。重大安全隐患排除前或者排除过程中无法保证安全的，应当责令从危险区域内撤出人员、设备，停止作业；重大安全隐患排除后方可恢复作业。

**Article 81** If the railway regulatory department discovers a safety hazard, it shall order the relevant unit to immediately eliminate it. If safety cannot be guaranteed before or during the elimination of a major safety hazard, it shall order the withdrawal of personnel and equipment from the hazardous area, and cessation of operations; operations may only resume after the major safety hazard has been eliminated.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　实施铁路安全监督检查的人员执行监督检查任务时，应当佩戴标志或者出示证件。任何单位和个人不得阻碍、干扰安全监督检查人员依法履行安全检查职责。

**Article 82** Personnel conducting railway safety supervision and inspection shall wear badges or produce identification when performing supervision and inspection tasks. No unit or individual shall obstruct or interfere with safety supervision and inspection personnel in performing their safety inspection duties according to law.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　铁路建设单位和铁路建设的勘察、设计、施工、监理单位违反本条例关于铁路建设质量安全管理的规定的，由铁路监管部门依照有关工程建设、招标投标管理的法律、行政法规的规定处罚。

**Article 83** Where the railway construction unit and the surveying, design, construction, and supervision units of railway construction violate the provisions of these Regulations on railway construction quality and safety management, the railway regulatory department shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on engineering construction and bid invitation and bidding management.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　铁路建设单位未对高速铁路和地质构造复杂的铁路建设工程实行工程地质勘察监理，或者在铁路线路及其邻近区域进行铁路建设工程施工不执行铁路营业线施工安全管理规定，影响铁路运营安全的，由铁路监管部门责令改正，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 84** Where the railway construction unit fails to implement engineering geological survey supervision for high-speed railways and railway construction projects with complex geological structures, or fails to implement the safety management provisions for construction on railway operating lines when carrying out railway construction projects on or adjacent to railway lines, thereby affecting railway operation safety, the railway regulatory department shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　依法应当进行产品认证的铁路专用设备未经认证合格，擅自出厂、销售、进口、使用的，依照《中华人民共和国认证认可条例》的规定处罚。

**Article 85** Where railway special equipment that shall undergo product certification according to law is manufactured, sold, imported, or used without passing certification, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Certification and Accreditation.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　铁路机车车辆以及其他专用设备制造者未按规定召回缺陷产品，采取措施消除缺陷的，由国务院铁路行业监督管理部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处缺陷产品货值金额1%以上10%以下的罚款；情节严重的，由国务院铁路行业监督管理部门吊销相应的许可证件。

**Article 86** Where a manufacturer of railway locomotives, rolling stock, or other special equipment fails to recall defective products or take measures to eliminate defects in accordance with regulations, the railway industry regulatory authority of the State Council shall order rectification; if the manufacturer refuses to rectify, a fine of not less than 1% but not more than 10% of the value of the defective products shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the railway industry regulatory authority of the State Council shall revoke the relevant license.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　有下列情形之一的，由铁路监督管理机构责令改正，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款：  
(一)用于铁路运输的安全检测、监控、防护设施设备，集装箱和集装化用具等运输器具、专用装卸机械、索具、篷布、装载加固材料或者装置、运输包装、货物装载加固等，不符合国家标准、行业标准和技术规范；  
(二)不按照国家有关规定和标准设置、维护铁路封闭设施、安全防护设施；  
(三)架设、铺设铁路信号和通信线路、杆塔不符合国家标准、行业标准和铁路安全防护要求，或者未对铁路信号和通信线路、杆塔进行维护和管理；  
(四)运输危险货物不依照法律法规和国家其他有关规定使用专用的设施设备。

**Article 87** In any of the following circumstances, the railway regulatory department shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan:  
(1) the safety inspection, monitoring, and protection facilities and equipment for railway transport, containers and containerized appliances and other transport utensils, special loading and unloading machinery, slings, tarpaulins, loading and securing materials or devices, transport packaging, cargo loading and securing, etc., do not comply with national standards, industry standards, and technical specifications;  
(2) railway enclosure facilities and safety protection facilities are not installed or maintained in accordance with relevant state regulations and standards;  
(3) the erection or laying of railway signal and communication lines and poles and towers does not comply with national standards, industry standards, and railway safety protection requirements, or the railway signal and communication lines and poles and towers are not maintained and managed;  
(4) dangerous goods are transported without using special facilities and equipment in accordance with laws, regulations, and other relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　在铁路线路安全保护区内烧荒、放养牲畜、种植影响铁路线路安全和行车瞭望的树木等植物，或者向铁路线路安全保护区排污、倾倒垃圾以及其他危害铁路安全的物质的，由铁路监督管理机构责令改正，对单位可以处5万元以下的罚款，对个人可以处2000元以下的罚款。

**Article 88** Where any person engages in burning grass, grazing livestock, planting trees or other plants that affect railway line safety and sight distance for train operation, or discharges sewage, dumps garbage, or other substances endangering railway safety into the railway line safety protection zone, the railway regulatory department shall order rectification, and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan on a unit, or a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan on an individual.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　未经铁路运输企业同意或者未签订安全协议，在铁路线路安全保护区内建造建筑物、构筑物等设施，取土、挖砂、挖沟、采空作业或者堆放、悬挂物品，或者违反保证铁路安全的国家标准、行业标准和施工安全规范，影响铁路运输安全的，由铁路监督管理机构责令改正，可以处10万元以下的罚款。  
铁路运输企业未派员对铁路线路安全保护区内施工现场进行安全监督的，由铁路监督管理机构责令改正，可以处3万元以下的罚款。

**Article 89** Where, without the consent of the railway transport enterprise or without signing a safety agreement, any person builds buildings, structures or other facilities, excavates soil, dredges sand, digs ditches, conducts mining operations, or piles or hangs articles within the railway line safety protection zone, or violates national standards, industry standards, and construction safety specifications that ensure railway safety, thereby affecting railway transport safety, the railway regulatory department shall order rectification and may impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan.  
If the railway transport enterprise fails to send personnel to conduct safety supervision on the construction site within the railway line safety protection zone, the railway regulatory department shall order rectification and may impose a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　在铁路线路安全保护区及其邻近区域建造或者设置的建筑物、构筑物、设备等进入国家规定的铁路建筑限界，或者在铁路线路两侧建造、设立生产、加工、储存或者销售易燃、易爆或者放射性物品等危险物品的场所、仓库不符合国家标准、行业标准规定的安全防护距离的，由铁路监督管理机构责令改正，对单位处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款，对个人处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 90** Where buildings, structures, equipment, etc., constructed or installed in or adjacent to the railway line safety protection zone intrude into the national prescribed railway clearance limit, or where places or warehouses for producing, processing, storing, or selling dangerous goods such as flammable, explosive, or radioactive articles are constructed or established on both sides of the railway line and do not comply with the safety protection distance specified in national standards or industry standards, the railway regulatory department shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan on a unit, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on an individual.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　有下列行为之一的，分别由铁路沿线所在地县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门、国土资源主管部门或者无线电管理机构等依照有关水资源管理、矿产资源管理、无线电管理等法律、行政法规的规定处罚：  
(一)未经批准在铁路线路两侧各1000米范围内从事露天采矿、采石或者爆破作业；  
(二)在地下水禁止开采区或者限制开采区抽取地下水；  
(三)在铁路桥梁跨越处河道上下游各1000米范围内围垦造田、拦河筑坝、架设浮桥或者修建其他影响铁路桥梁安全的设施；  
(四)在铁路桥梁跨越处河道上下游禁止采砂、淘金的范围内采砂、淘金；  
(五)干扰铁路运营指挥调度无线电频率正常使用。

**Article 91** Where any of the following acts is committed, the water administrative department, the land and resources administrative department, or the radio regulatory authority of the local people's government at or above the county level along the railway line shall, respectively, impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on water resource management, mineral resource management, radio management, etc.:  
(1) engaging in open-pit mining, quarrying, or blasting operations within a range of 1,000 meters on each side of the railway line without approval;  
(2) extracting groundwater in areas where groundwater extraction is prohibited or restricted;  
(3) reclaiming land, building dams across rivers, constructing floating bridges, or building other facilities that affect the safety of railway bridges within a range of 1,000 meters upstream and downstream of the river at the location of a railway bridge crossing;  
(4) dredging sand or panning for gold within the range where sand dredging and gold panning are prohibited upstream and downstream of the river at the location of a railway bridge crossing;  
(5) interfering with the normal use of radio frequencies for railway operation command and dispatch.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　铁路运输企业、道路管理部门或者道路经营企业未履行铁路、道路两用桥检查、维护职责的，由铁路监督管理机构或者上级道路管理部门责令改正；拒不改正的，由铁路监督管理机构或者上级道路管理部门指定其他单位进行养护和维修，养护和维修费用由拒不履行义务的铁路运输企业、道路管理部门或者道路经营企业承担。

**Article 92** Where a railway transport enterprise, a road administration department, or a road operating enterprise fails to perform its inspection and maintenance duties for a combined railway and road bridge, the railway regulatory department or the superior road administration department shall order rectification; if the party refuses to rectify, the railway regulatory department or the superior road administration department shall designate another unit to carry out maintenance and repair, and the costs of maintenance and repair shall be borne by the railway transport enterprise, road administration department, or road operating enterprise that refuses to perform its obligations.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　机动车通过下穿铁路桥梁、涵洞的道路未遵守限高、限宽规定的，由公安机关依照道路交通安全管理法律、行政法规的规定处罚。

**Article 93** Where a motor vehicle fails to comply with the height and width restrictions when passing through a road under a railway bridge or culvert, the public security organ shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on road traffic safety management.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　违反[本条例第四十八条](#art-48)、第四十九条关于铁路道口安全管理的规定的，由铁路监督管理机构责令改正，处1000元以上5000元以下的罚款。

**Article 94** Where any person violates the provisions of Articles 48 and 49 of these Regulations on the safety management of railway crossings, the railway regulatory department shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　违反[本条例第五十一条](#art-51)、第五十二条、第五十三条、第七十七条规定的，由公安机关责令改正，对单位处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款，对个人处500元以上2000元以下的罚款。

**Article 95** Where any person violates the provisions of Articles 51, 52, 53, and 77 of these Regulations, the public security organ shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on a unit, and a fine of not less than 500 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan on an individual.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　铁路运输托运人托运货物、行李、包裹时匿报、谎报货物品名、性质、重量，或者装车、装箱超过规定重量的，由铁路监督管理机构责令改正，可以处2000元以下的罚款；情节较重的，处2000元以上2万元以下的罚款；将危险化学品谎报或者匿报为普通货物托运的，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款。  
铁路运输托运人在普通货物中夹带危险货物，或者在危险货物中夹带禁止配装的货物的，由铁路监督管理机构责令改正，处3万元以上20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 96** Where a railway transport consignor, when consigning goods, luggage, or parcels, conceals or misrepresents the name, nature, or weight of the goods, or loads vehicles or containers exceeding the prescribed weight, the railway regulatory department shall order rectification and may impose a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed; if dangerous chemicals are misrepresented or concealed as ordinary goods for consignment, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where a railway transport consignor inserts dangerous goods into ordinary goods, or inserts goods that are prohibited from being loaded together into dangerous goods, the railway regulatory department shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　铁路运输托运人运输危险货物未配备必要的应急处理器材、设备、防护用品，或者未按照操作规程包装、装卸、运输危险货物的，由铁路监督管理机构责令改正，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 97** Where a railway transport consignor, when transporting dangerous goods, fails to equip necessary emergency response materials, equipment, and protective articles, or fails to package, load, unload, or transport dangerous goods in accordance with operating procedures, the railway regulatory department shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　铁路运输托运人运输危险货物不按照规定配备必要的押运人员，或者发生危险货物被盗、丢失、泄漏等情况不按照规定及时报告的，由公安机关责令改正，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 98** Where a railway transport consignor, when transporting dangerous goods, fails to equip necessary escort personnel in accordance with regulations, or fails to promptly report in accordance with regulations when dangerous goods are stolen, lost, or leaked, the public security organ shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　旅客违法携带、夹带管制器具或者违法携带、托运烟花爆竹、枪支弹药等危险物品或者其他违禁物品的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 99** Where a passenger illegally carries or conceals controlled instruments, or illegally carries or checks dangerous goods such as fireworks, firearms, ammunition, or other prohibited articles, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　铁路运输企业有下列情形之一的，由铁路监管部门责令改正，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款：  
(一)在非危险货物办理站办理危险货物承运手续；  
(二)承运未接受安全检查的货物；  
(三)承运不符合安全规定、可能危害铁路运输安全的货物；  
(四)未按照操作规程包装、装卸、运输危险货物。

**Article 100** Where a railway transport enterprise is in any of the following circumstances, the railway regulatory department shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan:  
(1) undertaking the carriage procedures for dangerous goods at a non-dangerous goods handling station;  
(2) accepting goods that have not undergone safety inspection;  
(3) accepting goods that do not comply with safety regulations and may endanger railway transport safety;  
(4) failing to package, load, unload, or transport dangerous goods in accordance with operating procedures.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　铁路监管部门及其工作人员应当严格按照本条例规定的处罚种类和幅度，根据违法行为的性质和具体情节行使行政处罚权，具体办法由国务院铁路行业监督管理部门制定。

**Article 101** Railway regulatory authorities and their staff shall exercise the power of administrative penalties strictly in accordance with the types and ranges of penalties prescribed in these Regulations, and based on the nature and specific circumstances of the illegal acts. The specific measures shall be formulated by the railway industry regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　铁路运输企业工作人员窃取、泄露旅客身份信息的，由公安机关依法处罚。

**Article 102** Where an employee of a railway transport enterprise steals or discloses passenger identity information, the public security organ shall impose penalties according to law.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　从事铁路建设、运输、设备制造维修的单位违反本条例规定，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 103** Where a unit engaged in railway construction, transport, equipment manufacturing or maintenance violates the provisions of these Regulations, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　铁路监管部门及其工作人员不依照本条例规定履行职责的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 104** Where railway regulatory authorities and their staff fail to perform their duties in accordance with these Regulations, the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　违反本条例规定，给铁路运输企业或者其他单位、个人财产造成损失的，依法承担民事责任。  
违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 105** Anyone who violates the provisions of these Regulations and causes damage to the property of a railway transport enterprise or any other unit or individual shall bear civil liability according to law.  
Anyone who violates the provisions of these Regulations and constitutes a violation of public security administration shall be given a public security administration penalty by the public security organ according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　专用铁路、铁路专用线的安全管理参照本条例的规定执行。

**Article 106** The safety management of special railways and railway sidings shall be implemented by reference to the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　本条例所称高速铁路，是指设计开行时速250公里以上(含预留)，并且初期运营时速200公里以上的客运列车专线铁路。

**Article 107** For the purpose of these Regulations, "high-speed railway" means a passenger train dedicated line railway designed for a running speed of more than 250 kilometers per hour (including reserved speed) and with an initial operating speed of more than 200 kilometers per hour.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　本条例自2014年1月1日起施行。2004年12月27日国务院公布的《铁路运输安全保护条例》同时废止。

**Article 108** These Regulations shall come into force on January 1, 2014. The Regulations on the Protection of Railway Transport Safety promulgated by the State Council on December 27, 2004 shall be abolished simultaneously.
