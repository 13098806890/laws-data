# 国内交通卫生检疫条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1998-11-28  
**生效日期**：1999-03-01

> (1998年11月28日中华人民共和国国务院令第254号发布　自1999年3月1日起施行) 第一条　为了控制检疫传染病通过交通工具及其乘运的人员、物资传播，防止检疫传染病流行，保障人体健康，依照《中华人民共和国传染病防治法》(以下简称传染病防治法)的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　列车、船舶、航空器和其他车辆(以下简称交通工具)出入检疫传染病疫区和在非检疫传染病疫区的交通工具上发现检疫传染病疫情时，依照本条例对交通工具及其乘运的人员、物资实施交通卫生检疫。 在中华人民共和国国际通航的港口、机场以及陆地边境和国界江河口岸的国境卫生检疫，依照《中华人民共和国国境卫生检疫法》的规定执行。 第三条　本条例所称检疫传染病，是指鼠疫、霍乱以及国务院确定并公布的其他传染病。 检疫传染病的诊断标准，按照国家有关卫生标准和国务院卫生行政部门的规定执行。 第四条　国务院卫生行政部门主管全国国内交通卫生检疫监督管理工作。 县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门负责本行政区域内的国内交通卫生检疫监督管理工作。 铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门的卫生主管机构，根据有关法律、法规和国务院卫生行政部门分别会同国务院铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门规定的职责划分，负责各自职责范围内的国内交通卫生检疫工作。 第五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府依照传染病防治法的规定，确定检疫传染病疫区，并决定对出入疫区的交通工具及其乘运的人员、物资实施交通卫生检疫。 第六条　对出入检疫传染病疫区的交通工具及其乘运的人员、物资，县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门或者铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门的卫生主管机构根据各自的职责，有权采取下列相应的交通卫生检疫措施： (一)对出入检疫传染病疫区的人员、交通工具及其承运的物资进行查验； (二)对检疫传染病病人、病原携带者、疑似检疫传染病病人和与其密切接触者，实施临时隔离、医学检查及其他应急医学措施； (三)对被检疫传染病病原体污染或者可能被污染的物品，实施控制和卫生处理； (四)对通过该疫区的交通工具及其停靠场所，实施紧急卫生处理；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了控制检疫传染病通过交通工具及其乘运的人员、物资传播，防止检疫传染病流行，保障人体健康，依照《中华人民共和国传染病防治法》(以下简称传染病防治法)的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases (hereinafter referred to as the Law on the Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases) for the purposes of controlling the spread of quarantine infectious diseases through means of transport and the persons and materials carried thereby, preventing the prevalence of quarantine infectious diseases, and protecting human health.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　列车、船舶、航空器和其他车辆(以下简称交通工具)出入检疫传染病疫区和在非检疫传染病疫区的交通工具上发现检疫传染病疫情时，依照本条例对交通工具及其乘运的人员、物资实施交通卫生检疫。  
在中华人民共和国国际通航的港口、机场以及陆地边境和国界江河口岸的国境卫生检疫，依照《中华人民共和国国境卫生检疫法》的规定执行。

**Article 2** When trains, ships, aircraft and other vehicles (hereinafter referred to as means of transport) enter or leave an epidemic area of a quarantine infectious disease, or when an epidemic situation of a quarantine infectious disease is discovered on a means of transport in a non-epidemic area, traffic health quarantine shall be implemented on such means of transport and the persons and materials carried thereby in accordance with these Regulations.  
Frontier health quarantine at ports, airports and land border and national boundary river ports open to international navigation in the People's Republic of China shall be executed in accordance with the provisions of the Frontier Health and Quarantine Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称检疫传染病，是指鼠疫、霍乱以及国务院确定并公布的其他传染病。  
检疫传染病的诊断标准，按照国家有关卫生标准和国务院卫生行政部门的规定执行。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Regulations, "quarantine infectious diseases" means plague, cholera and other infectious diseases determined and announced by the State Council.  
The diagnostic criteria for quarantine infectious diseases shall be executed in accordance with the relevant national health standards and the provisions of the health administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院卫生行政部门主管全国国内交通卫生检疫监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门负责本行政区域内的国内交通卫生检疫监督管理工作。  
铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门的卫生主管机构，根据有关法律、法规和国务院卫生行政部门分别会同国务院铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门规定的职责划分，负责各自职责范围内的国内交通卫生检疫工作。

**Article 4** The health administration department of the State Council shall be in charge of the supervision and administration of domestic traffic health quarantine throughout the country.  
The health administration departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of domestic traffic health quarantine within their respective administrative areas.  
The health competent authorities of the administrative departments of railways, communications and civil aviation shall, in accordance with the division of duties provided for by relevant laws and regulations and by the health administration department of the State Council jointly with the administrative departments of railways, communications and civil aviation of the State Council respectively, be responsible for domestic traffic health quarantine work within their respective duties.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府依照传染病防治法的规定，确定检疫传染病疫区，并决定对出入疫区的交通工具及其乘运的人员、物资实施交通卫生检疫。

**Article 5** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases, determine the epidemic areas of quarantine infectious diseases and decide to implement traffic health quarantine on means of transport entering or leaving the epidemic areas and the persons and materials carried thereby.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　对出入检疫传染病疫区的交通工具及其乘运的人员、物资，县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门或者铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门的卫生主管机构根据各自的职责，有权采取下列相应的交通卫生检疫措施：  
(一)对出入检疫传染病疫区的人员、交通工具及其承运的物资进行查验；  
(二)对检疫传染病病人、病原携带者、疑似检疫传染病病人和与其密切接触者，实施临时隔离、医学检查及其他应急医学措施；  
(三)对被检疫传染病病原体污染或者可能被污染的物品，实施控制和卫生处理；  
(四)对通过该疫区的交通工具及其停靠场所，实施紧急卫生处理；  
(五)需要采取的其他卫生检疫措施。  
采取前款所列交通卫生检疫措施的期间自决定实施时起至决定解除时止。

**Article 6** With regard to means of transport entering or leaving an epidemic area of a quarantine infectious disease and the persons and materials carried thereby, the health administration departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level or the health competent authorities of the administrative departments of railways, communications and civil aviation shall, according to their respective duties, have the authority to take the following corresponding traffic health quarantine measures:  
(1) inspecting the persons, means of transport and materials carried thereby that enter or leave an epidemic area of a quarantine infectious disease;  
(2) implementing temporary isolation, medical examination and other emergency medical measures on patients with quarantine infectious diseases, pathogen carriers, suspected patients with quarantine infectious diseases and their close contacts;  
(3) implementing control and sanitary treatment on articles contaminated or likely to be contaminated by the pathogens of quarantine infectious diseases;  
(4) implementing emergency sanitary treatment on means of transport passing through such an epidemic area and their stopping places;  
(5) other health quarantine measures that need to be taken.  
The period for taking the traffic health quarantine measures listed in the preceding paragraph shall be from the time when the decision to implement is made to the time when the decision to lift is made.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　非检疫传染病疫区的交通工具上发现下列情形之一时，县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门或者铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门的卫生主管机构根据各自的职责，有权对交通工具及其乘运的人员、物资实施交通卫生检疫：  
(一)发现有感染鼠疫的啮齿类动物或者啮齿类动物反常死亡，并且死因不明；  
(二)发现鼠疫、霍乱病人、病原携带者和疑似鼠疫、霍乱病人；  
(三)发现国务院确定并公布的需要实施国内交通卫生检疫的其他传染病。  
跨省、自治区、直辖市在非检疫传染病疫区运行的列车、船舶、航空器上发现前款所列情形之一时，国务院卫生行政部门分别会同国务院铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门，可以决定对该列车、船舶、航空器实施交通卫生检疫和指令列车、船舶、航空器不得停靠或者通过港口、机场、车站；但是，因实施交通卫生检疫导致中断干线交通或者封锁国境的，须由国务院决定。

**Article 7** When any of the following circumstances is discovered on a means of transport in a non-epidemic area of quarantine infectious diseases, the health administration departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level or the health competent authorities of the administrative departments of railways, communications and civil aviation shall, according to their respective duties, have the authority to implement traffic health quarantine on such means of transport and the persons and materials carried thereby:  
(1) rodents infected with plague are found or rodents die abnormally for unknown reasons;  
(2) patients with plague or cholera, pathogen carriers and suspected patients with plague or cholera are found;  
(3) other infectious diseases determined and announced by the State Council that require domestic traffic health quarantine are found.  
When any of the circumstances listed in the preceding paragraph is discovered on a train, ship or aircraft operating in a non-epidemic area of quarantine infectious diseases across provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the health administration department of the State Council, jointly with the administrative departments of railways, communications and civil aviation of the State Council respectively, may decide to implement traffic health quarantine on such train, ship or aircraft and order that the train, ship or aircraft shall not stop at or pass through ports, airports or stations; however, if the implementation of traffic health quarantine leads to interruption of trunk line traffic or blockade of the national border, a decision by the State Council shall be required.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　在非检疫传染病疫区的交通工具上，发现检疫传染病病人、病原携带者、疑似检疫传染病病人时，交通工具负责人应当组织有关人员采取下列临时措施：  
(一)以最快的方式通知前方停靠点，并向交通工具营运单位的主管部门报告；  
(二)对检疫传染病病人、病原携带者、疑似检疫传染病病人和与其密切接触者实施隔离；  
(三)封锁已经污染或者可能污染的区域，采取禁止向外排放污物等卫生处理措施；  
(四)在指定的停靠点将检疫传染病病人、病原携带者、疑似检疫传染病病人和与其密切接触者以及其他需要跟踪观察的旅客名单，移交当地县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门；  
(五)对承运过检疫传染病病人、病原携带者、疑似检疫传染病病人的交通工具和可能被污染的环境实施卫生处理。  
交通工具停靠地的县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门或者铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门的卫生主管机构，应当根据各自的职责，依照传染病防治法的规定，采取控制措施。

**Article 8** When a patient with a quarantine infectious disease, a pathogen carrier or a suspected patient with a quarantine infectious disease is discovered on a means of transport in a non-epidemic area of quarantine infectious diseases, the person in charge of the means of transport shall organize relevant personnel to take the following temporary measures:  
(1) notify the next stopping point in the fastest manner and report to the competent department of the operator of the means of transport;  
(2) isolate the patient with a quarantine infectious disease, pathogen carrier, suspected patient with a quarantine infectious disease and their close contacts;  
(3) blockade the contaminated or potentially contaminated area and take sanitary treatment measures such as prohibiting the discharge of pollutants outward;  
(4) at the designated stopping point, hand over the patient with a quarantine infectious disease, pathogen carrier, suspected patient with a quarantine infectious disease and their close contacts, as well as the list of other passengers requiring follow-up observation, to the local health administration department of the people's government at or above the county level;  
(5) implement sanitary treatment on the means of transport that has carried the patient with a quarantine infectious disease, pathogen carrier or suspected patient with a quarantine infectious disease, and on the potentially contaminated environment.  
The health administration departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level at the stopping place of the means of transport or the health competent authorities of the administrative departments of railways, communications and civil aviation shall, according to their respective duties and in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases, take control measures.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门或者铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门的卫生主管机构，根据各自的职责，对出入检疫传染病疫区的或者在非检疫传染病疫区发现检疫传染病疫情的交通工具及其乘运的人员、物资，实施交通卫生检疫；经检疫合格的，签发检疫合格证明。交通工具及其乘运的人员、物资凭检疫合格证明，方可通行。  
检疫合格证明的格式，由国务院卫生行政部门商国务院铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门制定。

**Article 9** The health administration departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level or the health competent authorities of the administrative departments of railways, communications and civil aviation shall, according to their respective duties, implement traffic health quarantine on means of transport that enter or leave an epidemic area of a quarantine infectious disease or on which an epidemic situation of a quarantine infectious disease is discovered in a non-epidemic area, and on the persons and materials carried thereby; if the quarantine passes, a quarantine certificate shall be issued. The means of transport and the persons and materials carried thereby may pass only upon presentation of the quarantine certificate.  
The format of the quarantine certificate shall be formulated by the health administration department of the State Council in consultation with the administrative departments of railways, communications and civil aviation of the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对拒绝隔离、治疗、留验的检疫传染病病人、病原携带者、疑似检疫传染病病人和与其密切接触者，以及拒绝检查和卫生处理的可能传播检疫传染病的交通工具、停靠场所及物资，县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门或者铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门的卫生主管机构根据各自的职责，应当依照传染病防治法的规定，采取强制检疫措施；必要时，由当地县级以上人民政府组织公安部门予以协助。

**Article 10** With respect to patients with quarantine infectious diseases, pathogen carriers, suspected patients with quarantine infectious diseases and their close contacts who refuse isolation, treatment or medical observation, as well as means of transport, stopping places and materials that may spread quarantine infectious diseases and refuse inspection and sanitary treatment, the health administration departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level or the health competent authorities of the administrative departments of railways, communications and civil aviation shall, according to their respective duties and in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases, take compulsory quarantine measures; when necessary, the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize the public security department to provide assistance.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　检疫传染病疫情发生后，疫区所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门应当向有关铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门的卫生主管机构通报疫情。铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门的卫生主管机构接到疫情通报后，应当及时通知有关交通工具的营运单位。  
检疫传染病疫情的报告、通报和公布，依照传染病防治法及其实施办法的规定执行。

**Article 11** After the occurrence of an epidemic situation of a quarantine infectious disease, the health administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the epidemic area is located shall notify the epidemic situation to the health competent authorities of the relevant administrative departments of railways, communications and civil aviation. Upon receiving the notification of the epidemic situation, the health competent authorities of the administrative departments of railways, communications and civil aviation shall promptly notify the operators of the relevant means of transport.  
The reporting, notification and publication of epidemic situations of quarantine infectious diseases shall be executed in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases and its implementing measures.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院卫生行政部门应当依照传染病防治法的规定，加强对检疫传染病防治的监督管理，会同国务院铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门，依照本条例的规定，拟订国内交通卫生检疫实施方案。

**Article 12** The health administration department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases, strengthen the supervision and administration of the prevention and control of quarantine infectious diseases, and jointly with the administrative departments of railways, communications and civil aviation of the State Council, formulate implementation plans for domestic traffic health quarantine in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　检疫传染病病人、病原携带者、疑似检疫传染病病人和与其密切接触者隐瞒真实情况、逃避交通卫生检疫的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门或者铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门的卫生主管机构，根据各自的职责分工，责令限期改正，给予警告，可以并处1000元以下的罚款；拒绝接受查验和卫生处理的，给予警告，并处1000元以上5000元以下的罚款；情节严重，引起检疫传染病传播或者有传播严重危险，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 13** If a patient with a quarantine infectious disease, a pathogen carrier, a suspected patient with a quarantine infectious disease or their close contacts conceals the true situation or evades traffic health quarantine, the health administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the health competent authority of the administrative department of railways, communications or civil aviation shall, according to the division of duties, order rectification within a time limit, give a warning, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan; if they refuse to accept inspection and sanitary treatment, a warning shall be given and a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed concurrently; if the circumstances are serious, causing the spread of a quarantine infectious disease or creating a serious risk of spread, and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　在非检疫传染病疫区的交通工具上发现检疫传染病病人、病原携带者、疑似检疫传染病病人时，交通工具负责人未依照本条例规定采取措施的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门或者铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门的卫生主管机构，根据各自的职责，责令改正，给予警告，并处1000元以上5000元以下的罚款；情节严重，引起检疫传染病传播或者有传播严重危险，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 14** If, when a patient with a quarantine infectious disease, a pathogen carrier or a suspected patient with a quarantine infectious disease is discovered on a means of transport in a non-epidemic area of quarantine infectious diseases, the person in charge of the means of transport fails to take measures in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the health administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the health competent authority of the administrative department of railways, communications or civil aviation shall, according to their respective duties, order rectification, give a warning, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, causing the spread of a quarantine infectious disease or creating a serious risk of spread, and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门或者铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门的卫生主管机构，对发现的检疫传染病病人、病原携带者、疑似检疫传染病病人和与其密切接触者，未依法实施临时隔离、医学检查和其他应急医学措施的，以及对被检疫传染病病原体污染或者可能被污染的物品、交通工具及其停靠场所未依法进行必要的控制和卫生处理的，由其上级行政主管部门责令限期改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；情节严重，引起检疫传染病传播或者有传播严重危险，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 15** If the health administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the health competent authority of the administrative department of railways, communications or civil aviation fails to implement temporary isolation, medical examination and other emergency medical measures according to law on discovered patients with quarantine infectious diseases, pathogen carriers, suspected patients with quarantine infectious diseases and their close contacts, or fails to carry out necessary control and sanitary treatment according to law on articles, means of transport and their stopping places contaminated or likely to be contaminated by the pathogens of quarantine infectious diseases, the competent administrative department at a higher level shall order rectification within a time limit and impose administrative sanctions according to law on the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, causing the spread of a quarantine infectious disease or creating a serious risk of spread, and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本条例自1999年3月1日起施行。1985年9月19日国务院批准、1985年10月12日铁道部、卫生部公布的《铁路交通检疫管理办法》同时废止。

**Article 16** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 1999. The Measures for the Administration of Railway Traffic Quarantine approved by the State Council on September 19, 1985 and promulgated by the Ministry of Railways and the Ministry of Health on October 12, 1985 shall be abolished simultaneously.
