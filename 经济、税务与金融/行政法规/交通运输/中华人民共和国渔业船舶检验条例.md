# 中华人民共和国渔业船舶检验条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2003-06-27  
**生效日期**：2003-08-01

> (2003年6月11日国务院第11次常务会议通过　2003年6月27日中华人民共和国国务院令第383号公布　自2003年8月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范渔业船舶的检验，保证渔业船舶具备安全航行和作业的条件，保障渔业船舶和渔民生命财产的安全，防止污染环境，依照《中华人民共和国渔业法》，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国登记和将要登记的渔业船舶(以下简称渔业船舶)的检验，适用本条例。从事国际航运的渔业辅助船舶除外。 第三条　国务院渔业行政主管部门主管全国渔业船舶检验及其监督管理工作。 中华人民共和国渔业船舶检验局(以下简称国家渔业船舶检验机构)行使渔业船舶检验及其监督管理职能。 地方渔业船舶检验机构依照本条例规定，负责有关的渔业船舶检验工作。 各级公安边防、质量监督和工商行政管理等部门，应当在各自的职责范围内对渔业船舶检验和监督管理工作予以协助。 第四条　国家对渔业船舶实行强制检验制度。强制检验分为初次检验、营运检验和临时检验。 第五条　渔业船舶检验，应当遵循安全第一、保证质量和方便渔民的原则。 第二章　初次检验 第六条　渔业船舶的初次检验，是指渔业船舶检验机构在渔业船舶投入营运前对其所实施的全面检验。 第七条　下列渔业船舶的所有者或者经营者应当申报初次检验： (一)制造的渔业船舶； (二)改造的渔业船舶(包括非渔业船舶改为渔业船舶、国内作业的渔业船舶改为远洋作业的渔业船舶)；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范渔业船舶的检验，保证渔业船舶具备安全航行和作业的条件，保障渔业船舶和渔民生命财产的安全，防止污染环境，依照《中华人民共和国渔业法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Fisheries Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of regulating the inspection of fishing vessels, ensuring that fishing vessels meet the conditions for safe navigation and operation, safeguarding the safety of life and property of fishing vessels and fishermen, and preventing environmental pollution.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国登记和将要登记的渔业船舶(以下简称渔业船舶)的检验，适用本条例。从事国际航运的渔业辅助船舶除外。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the inspection of fishing vessels registered and to be registered in the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as fishing vessels), with the exception of fishing auxiliary vessels engaged in international navigation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院渔业行政主管部门主管全国渔业船舶检验及其监督管理工作。  
中华人民共和国渔业船舶检验局(以下简称国家渔业船舶检验机构)行使渔业船舶检验及其监督管理职能。  
地方渔业船舶检验机构依照本条例规定，负责有关的渔业船舶检验工作。  
各级公安边防、质量监督和工商行政管理等部门，应当在各自的职责范围内对渔业船舶检验和监督管理工作予以协助。

**Article 3** The competent fisheries administrative department under the State Council shall be in charge of the inspection and supervision of fishing vessels nationwide.  
The Fishing Vessel Inspection Bureau of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the national fishing vessel inspection authority) shall exercise the functions of fishing vessel inspection and supervision thereof.  
Local fishing vessel inspection authorities shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, be responsible for the relevant fishing vessel inspection work.  
The public security border control, quality supervision, and administrative departments for industry and commerce at all levels shall, within their respective functions and duties, assist in the inspection and supervision of fishing vessels.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对渔业船舶实行强制检验制度。强制检验分为初次检验、营运检验和临时检验。

**Article 4** The State shall implement a compulsory inspection system for fishing vessels. Compulsory inspection shall be divided into initial inspection, operational inspection, and temporary inspection.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　渔业船舶检验，应当遵循安全第一、保证质量和方便渔民的原则。

**Article 5** The inspection of fishing vessels shall follow the principles of safety first, quality assurance, and convenience for fishermen.

### 第二章　初次检验

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　渔业船舶的初次检验，是指渔业船舶检验机构在渔业船舶投入营运前对其所实施的全面检验。

**Article 6** Initial inspection of a fishing vessel means the comprehensive inspection carried out by the fishing vessel inspection authority on the fishing vessel before it is put into operation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　下列渔业船舶的所有者或者经营者应当申报初次检验：  
(一)制造的渔业船舶；  
(二)改造的渔业船舶(包括非渔业船舶改为渔业船舶、国内作业的渔业船舶改为远洋作业的渔业船舶)；  
(三)进口的渔业船舶。

**Article 7** The owner or operator of any of the following fishing vessels shall apply for initial inspection:  
(1) fishing vessels constructed;  
(2) fishing vessels converted (including those converted from non-fishing vessels to fishing vessels, and fishing vessels operating domestically converted to fishing vessels operating on the high seas);  
(3) imported fishing vessels.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　制造、改造的渔业船舶，其设计图纸、技术文件应当经渔业船舶检验机构审查批准，并在开工制造、改造前申报初次检验。渔业船舶检验机构应当自收到设计图纸、技术文件之日起20个工作日内作出审查决定，并书面通知当事人。  
设计、制造、改造渔业船舶的单位应当符合国家规定的条件，并遵守国家渔业船舶技术规则。

**Article 8** For fishing vessels to be constructed or converted, the design drawings and technical documents shall be examined and approved by the fishing vessel inspection authority, and an application for initial inspection shall be made before construction or conversion commences. The fishing vessel inspection authority shall, within 20 working days from the date of receipt of the design drawings and technical documents, make a decision on examination and notify the party in writing.  
The unit designing, constructing, or converting a fishing vessel shall meet the conditions prescribed by the State and comply with the national technical rules for fishing vessels.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　制造、改造的渔业船舶的初次检验，应当与渔业船舶的制造、改造同时进行。  
用于制造、改造渔业船舶的有关航行、作业和人身财产安全以及防止污染环境的重要设备、部件和材料，在使用前应当经渔业船舶检验机构检验，检验合格的方可使用。  
前款规定必须检验的重要设备、部件和材料的目录，由国务院渔业行政主管部门制定。

**Article 9** The initial inspection of a fishing vessel to be constructed or converted shall be carried out simultaneously with the construction or conversion of the fishing vessel.  
The important equipment, components, and materials used for constructing or converting a fishing vessel and relating to navigation, operation, safety of person and property, and environmental pollution prevention shall be inspected by the fishing vessel inspection authority before use; only those passing the inspection may be used.  
The catalog of the important equipment, components, and materials that must be inspected as prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be formulated by the competent fisheries administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　进口的渔业船舶，其设计图纸、技术文件应当经渔业船舶检验机构审查确认，并在投入营运前申报初次检验。进口旧渔业船舶，进口前还应当取得国家渔业船舶检验机构出具的旧渔业船舶技术评定证书。

**Article 10** For an imported fishing vessel, its design drawings and technical documents shall be examined and confirmed by the fishing vessel inspection authority, and an application for initial inspection shall be made before it is put into operation. For an imported second-hand fishing vessel, a technical assessment certificate for the second-hand fishing vessel issued by the national fishing vessel inspection authority shall also be obtained before import.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　渔业船舶检验机构对检验合格的渔业船舶，应当自检验完毕之日起5个工作日内签发渔业船舶检验证书；经检验不合格的，应当书面通知当事人，并说明理由。  
经检验合格的渔业船舶，任何单位和个人不得擅自改变其吨位、载重线、主机功率、人员定额和适航区域；不得擅自拆除其有关航行、作业和人身财产安全以及防止污染环境的重要设备、部件。确需改变或者拆除的，应当经原渔业船舶检验机构核准。

**Article 11** The fishing vessel inspection authority shall, within 5 working days from the date of completion of inspection, issue a fishing vessel inspection certificate for a fishing vessel that passes the inspection; if the inspection is failed, it shall notify the party in writing and state the reasons.  
No unit or individual may, without authorization, alter the tonnage, load line, main engine power, manning quota, or navigation area of a fishing vessel that has passed the inspection; nor may they remove, without authorization, the important equipment or components thereof relating to navigation, operation, safety of person and property, and environmental pollution prevention. Where alteration or removal is genuinely necessary, approval from the original fishing vessel inspection authority shall be obtained.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　进口的渔业船舶和远洋渔业船舶的初次检验，由国家渔业船舶检验机构统一组织实施。其他渔业船舶的初次检验，由船籍港渔业船舶检验机构负责实施；渔业船舶的制造地或者改造地与船籍港不一致的，初次检验由制造地或者改造地渔业船舶检验机构实施；该渔业船舶检验机构应当自检验完毕之日起5个工作日内，将检验报告、检验记录等技术资料移交船籍港渔业船舶检验机构。

**Article 12** The initial inspection of imported fishing vessels and deep-sea fishing vessels shall be organized and carried out uniformly by the national fishing vessel inspection authority. The initial inspection of other fishing vessels shall be carried out by the fishing vessel inspection authority at the port of registry; where the place of construction or conversion of a fishing vessel is different from its port of registry, the initial inspection shall be carried out by the fishing vessel inspection authority at the place of construction or conversion; such fishing vessel inspection authority shall, within 5 working days from the date of completion of inspection, transfer the technical materials such as inspection report and inspection records to the fishing vessel inspection authority at the port of registry.

### 第三章　营运检验

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　渔业船舶的营运检验，是指渔业船舶检验机构对营运中的渔业船舶所实施的常规性检验。

**Article 13** Operational inspection of a fishing vessel means the routine inspection carried out by the fishing vessel inspection authority on a fishing vessel in operation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　营运中的渔业船舶的所有者或者经营者应当按照国务院渔业行政主管部门规定的时间申报营运检验。  
渔业船舶检验机构应当按照国务院渔业行政主管部门的规定，根据渔业船舶运行年限和安全要求对下列项目实施检验：  
(一)渔业船舶的结构和机电设备；  
(二)与渔业船舶安全有关的设备、部件；  
(三)与防止污染环境有关的设备、部件；  
(四)国务院渔业行政主管部门规定的其他检验项目。

**Article 14** The owner or operator of a fishing vessel in operation shall apply for operational inspection within the time prescribed by the competent fisheries administrative department under the State Council.  
The fishing vessel inspection authority shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent fisheries administrative department under the State Council, inspect the following items based on the years of service and safety requirements of the fishing vessel:  
(1) the structure and mechanical and electrical equipment of the fishing vessel;  
(2) equipment and components relating to the safety of the fishing vessel;  
(3) equipment and components relating to environmental pollution prevention;  
(4) other inspection items prescribed by the competent fisheries administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　渔业船舶检验机构应当自申报营运检验的渔业船舶到达受检地之日起3个工作日内实施检验。经检验合格的，应当自检验完毕之日起5个工作日内在渔业船舶检验证书上签署意见或者签发渔业船舶检验证书；签发境外受检的远洋渔业船舶的检验证书，可以延长至15个工作日。经检验不合格的，应当书面通知当事人，并说明理由。

**Article 15** The fishing vessel inspection authority shall carry out the inspection within 3 working days from the date when the fishing vessel applying for operational inspection arrives at the place of inspection. If the inspection is passed, it shall, within 5 working days from the date of completion of inspection, endorse the fishing vessel inspection certificate or issue a fishing vessel inspection certificate; for issuing an inspection certificate for a deep-sea fishing vessel inspected abroad, the period may be extended to 15 working days. If the inspection is failed, it shall notify the party in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　渔业船舶经检验需要维修的，该船舶的所有者或者经营者应当选择符合国家规定条件的维修单位。维修渔业船舶应当遵守国家渔业船舶技术规则。  
用于维修渔业船舶的有关航行、作业和人身财产安全以及防止污染环境的重要设备、部件和材料，在使用前应当经渔业船舶检验机构检验，检验合格的方可使用。

**Article 16** Where a fishing vessel requires repair after inspection, the owner or operator of such vessel shall select a repair unit that meets the conditions prescribed by the State. Repair of a fishing vessel shall comply with the national technical rules for fishing vessels.  
The important equipment, components, and materials used for repairing a fishing vessel and relating to navigation, operation, safety of person and property, and environmental pollution prevention shall be inspected by the fishing vessel inspection authority before use; only those passing the inspection may be used.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　营运中的渔业船舶需要更换有关航行、作业和人身财产安全以及防止污染环境的重要设备、部件和材料的，该船舶的所有者或者经营者应当遵守[本条例第十六条](#art-16)第二款的规定。

**Article 17** Where a fishing vessel in operation needs to replace important equipment, components, and materials relating to navigation, operation, safety of person and property, and environmental pollution prevention, the owner or operator of such vessel shall comply with the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 16 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　远洋渔业船舶的营运检验，由国家渔业船舶检验机构统一组织实施。其他渔业船舶的营运检验，由船籍港渔业船舶检验机构负责实施；因故不能回船籍港进行营运检验的渔业船舶，由船籍港渔业船舶检验机构委托船舶的营运地或者维修地渔业船舶检验机构实施检验；实施检验的渔业船舶检验机构应当自检验完毕之日起5个工作日内将检验报告、检验记录等技术资料移交船籍港渔业船舶检验机构。

**Article 18** The operational inspection of deep-sea fishing vessels shall be organized and carried out uniformly by the national fishing vessel inspection authority. The operational inspection of other fishing vessels shall be carried out by the fishing vessel inspection authority at the port of registry; where a fishing vessel cannot return to its port of registry for operational inspection for any reason, the fishing vessel inspection authority at the port of registry shall entrust the inspection to the fishing vessel inspection authority at the place of operation or repair; the fishing vessel inspection authority carrying out the inspection shall, within 5 working days from the date of completion of inspection, transfer the technical materials such as inspection report and inspection records to the fishing vessel inspection authority at the port of registry.

### 第四章　临时检验

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　渔业船舶的临时检验，是指渔业船舶检验机构对营运中的渔业船舶出现特定情形时所实施的非常规性检验。

**Article 19** Temporary inspection of a fishing vessel means the non-routine inspection carried out by the fishing vessel inspection authority on a fishing vessel in operation when specific circumstances arise.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　有下列情形之一的渔业船舶，其所有者或者经营者应当申报临时检验：  
(一)因检验证书失效而无法及时回船籍港的；  
(二)因不符合水上交通安全或者环境保护法律、法规的有关要求被责令检验的；  
(三)具有国务院渔业行政主管部门规定的其他特定情形的。

**Article 20** The owner or operator of a fishing vessel shall apply for temporary inspection under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the fishing vessel cannot return to its port of registry in time due to the invalidity of its inspection certificate;  
(2) the fishing vessel is ordered to undergo inspection for failing to meet the relevant requirements of laws and regulations on maritime traffic safety or environmental protection;  
(3) other specific circumstances as prescribed by the competent fisheries administrative department under the State Council exist.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　渔业船舶检验机构应当自申报临时检验的渔业船舶到达受检地之日起2个工作日内实施检验。经检验合格的，应当自检验完毕之日起3个工作日内在渔业船舶检验证书上签署意见或者签发渔业船舶检验证书；经检验不合格的，应当书面通知当事人，并说明理由。

**Article 21** The fishing vessel inspection authority shall carry out the inspection within 2 working days from the date when the fishing vessel applying for temporary inspection arrives at the place of inspection. If the inspection is passed, it shall, within 3 working days from the date of completion of inspection, endorse the fishing vessel inspection certificate or issue a fishing vessel inspection certificate; if the inspection is failed, it shall notify the party in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　渔业船舶临时检验的管辖权限划分，依照[本条例第十八条](#art-18)关于营运检验管辖权限的规定执行。

**Article 22** The division of jurisdiction for temporary inspection of fishing vessels shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of Article 18 of these Regulations on the jurisdiction of operational inspection.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　有下列情形之一的渔业船舶，渔业船舶检验机构不得受理检验：  
(一)设计图纸、技术文件未经渔业船舶检验机构审查批准或者确认的；  
(二)违反[本条例第八条](#art-8)第二款和第九条第二款规定制造、改造的；  
(三)违反[本条例第十六条](#art-16)、第十七条规定维修的；  
(四)按照国家有关规定应当报废的。

**Article 23** The fishing vessel inspection authority shall not accept the application for inspection of a fishing vessel under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the design drawings and technical documents have not been examined, approved, or confirmed by the fishing vessel inspection authority;  
(2) the fishing vessel has been constructed or converted in violation of the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 8 and the second paragraph of Article 9 of these Regulations;  
(3) the fishing vessel has been repaired in violation of the provisions of Article 16 or Article 17 of these Regulations;  
(4) the fishing vessel shall be scrapped in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　地方渔业船舶检验机构应当在国家渔业船舶检验机构核定的范围内开展检验业务。

**Article 24** Local fishing vessel inspection authorities shall carry out inspection business within the scope approved by the national fishing vessel inspection authority.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　从事渔业船舶检验的人员应当经国家渔业船舶检验机构考核合格后，方可从事相应的渔业船舶检验工作。

**Article 25** Personnel engaged in fishing vessel inspection shall pass the examination conducted by the national fishing vessel inspection authority before they may engage in the corresponding fishing vessel inspection work.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　渔业船舶检验机构及其检验人员应当严格遵守渔业船舶检验规则，实施现场检验，并对检验结论负责。  
渔业船舶检验规则由国家渔业船舶检验机构制定，经国务院渔业行政主管部门批准后公布实施。  
对具有新颖性的渔业船舶或者船用产品，国家尚未制定相应的检验规则的，可以适用国家渔业船舶检验机构认可的检验规则。

**Article 26** Fishing vessel inspection authorities and their inspectors shall strictly abide by the rules for fishing vessel inspection, conduct on-site inspection, and be responsible for the inspection conclusions.  
The rules for fishing vessel inspection shall be formulated by the national fishing vessel inspection authority and promulgated for implementation after approval by the competent fisheries administrative department under the State Council.  
For fishing vessels or marine products with novelty for which the State has not yet formulated corresponding inspection rules, the inspection rules recognized by the national fishing vessel inspection authority may be applied.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　当事人对地方渔业船舶检验机构的检验结论有异议的，可以按照国务院渔业行政主管部门的规定申请复验。

**Article 27** If a party disagrees with the inspection conclusion of a local fishing vessel inspection authority, it may apply for a re-inspection in accordance with the provisions of the competent fisheries administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　渔业船舶的检验收费，按照国务院价格主管部门、财政部门规定的收费标准执行。

**Article 28** The fees for fishing vessel inspection shall be charged in accordance with the fee standards prescribed by the competent pricing department and the finance department under the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　渔业船舶的检验证书、检验记录、检验报告的式样和检验业务印章，由国家渔业船舶检验机构统一规定。

**Article 29** The formats of fishing vessel inspection certificates, inspection records, inspection reports, and the seals for inspection business shall be uniformly prescribed by the national fishing vessel inspection authority.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　渔业船舶检验人员依法履行职能时，有权对渔业船舶的检验证书和技术状况进行检查，有关单位和个人应当给予配合。  
重大渔业船舶海损事故的调查处理，应当有渔业船舶检验机构的检验人员参加。

**Article 30** When performing their functions according to law, fishing vessel inspection personnel shall have the authority to inspect the inspection certificates and technical condition of fishing vessels, and the relevant units and individuals shall cooperate.  
The investigation and handling of major marine accidents involving fishing vessels shall involve the participation of inspectors from the fishing vessel inspection authority.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　有下列情形之一的渔业船舶，其所有者或者经营者应当在渔业船舶报废、改籍、改造之日前7个工作日内或者自渔业船舶灭失之日起20个工作日内，向渔业船舶检验机构申请注销其渔业船舶检验证书；逾期不申请的，渔业船舶检验证书自渔业船舶改籍、改造完毕之日起或者渔业船舶报废、灭失之日起失效，并由渔业船舶检验机构注销渔业船舶检验证书：  
(一)按照国家有关规定报废的；  
(二)中国籍改为外国籍的；  
(三)渔业船舶改为非渔业船舶的；  
(四)因沉没等原因灭失的。

**Article 31** For a fishing vessel under any of the following circumstances, the owner or operator shall apply to the fishing vessel inspection authority for cancellation of its fishing vessel inspection certificate within 7 working days before the date of scrapping, change of registry, or conversion of the fishing vessel, or within 20 working days from the date of loss of the fishing vessel; if no application is made within the time limit, the fishing vessel inspection certificate shall cease to have effect from the date of completion of change of registry or conversion, or from the date of scrapping or loss of the fishing vessel, and the fishing vessel inspection authority shall cancel the fishing vessel inspection certificate:  
(1) the fishing vessel is scrapped in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(2) the nationality of the fishing vessel is changed from Chinese to foreign;  
(3) the fishing vessel is converted into a non-fishing vessel;  
(4) the fishing vessel is lost due to sinking or other reasons.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　违反本条例规定，渔业船舶未经检验、未取得渔业船舶检验证书擅自下水作业的，没收该渔业船舶。  
按照规定应当报废的渔业船舶继续作业的，责令立即停止作业，收缴失效的渔业船舶检验证书，强制拆解应当报废的渔业船舶，并处2000元以上5万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 32** Where a fishing vessel, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, is launched for operation without inspection or without obtaining a fishing vessel inspection certificate, such fishing vessel shall be confiscated.  
Where a fishing vessel that shall be scrapped in accordance with the provisions continues to operate, an order shall be issued to immediately cease operation, the invalid fishing vessel inspection certificate shall be confiscated, the fishing vessel that shall be scrapped shall be forcibly dismantled, and a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违反本条例规定，渔业船舶应当申报营运检验或者临时检验而不申报的，责令立即停止作业，限期申报检验；逾期仍不申报检验的，处1000元以上1万元以下的罚款，并可以暂扣渔业船舶检验证书。

**Article 33** Where a fishing vessel, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, fails to apply for operational inspection or temporary inspection when required to do so, an order shall be issued to immediately cease operation and to apply for inspection within a specified time limit; if the inspection is still not applied for upon expiration of the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 1,000 yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the fishing vessel inspection certificate may be temporarily detained.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，责令立即改正，处2000元以上2万元以下的罚款；正在作业的，责令立即停止作业；拒不改正或者拒不停止作业的，强制拆除非法使用的重要设备、部件和材料或者暂扣渔业船舶检验证书；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)使用未经检验合格的有关航行、作业和人身财产安全以及防止污染环境的重要设备、部件和材料，制造、改造、维修渔业船舶的；  
(二)擅自拆除渔业船舶上有关航行、作业和人身财产安全以及防止污染环境的重要设备、部件的；  
(三)擅自改变渔业船舶的吨位、载重线、主机功率、人员定额和适航区域的。

**Article 34** Where any of the following acts is committed in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, an order shall be issued to immediately rectify the act, and a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the vessel is in operation, an order shall be issued to immediately cease operation; if the party refuses to rectify or cease operation, the important equipment, components, and materials illegally used shall be forcibly dismantled, or the fishing vessel inspection certificate shall be temporarily detained; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) using important equipment, components, and materials relating to navigation, operation, safety of person and property, and environmental pollution prevention that have not passed inspection to construct, convert, or repair a fishing vessel;  
(2) removing, without authorization, important equipment or components relating to navigation, operation, safety of person and property, and environmental pollution prevention on a fishing vessel;  
(3) altering, without authorization, the tonnage, load line, main engine power, manning quota, or navigation area of a fishing vessel.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　渔业船舶检验机构的工作人员未经考核合格从事渔业船舶检验工作的，责令其立即停止检验工作，处1000元以上5000元以下的罚款。

**Article 35** Where a staff member of a fishing vessel inspection authority engages in fishing vessel inspection work without passing the examination, an order shall be issued to immediately cease the inspection work, and a fine of not less than RMB 1,000 yuan but not more than RMB 5,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，责令立即改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予降级、撤职、取消检验资格的处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；已签发的渔业船舶检验证书无效：  
(一)未按照国务院渔业行政主管部门的有关规定实施检验的；  
(二)所签发的渔业船舶检验证书或者检验记录、检验报告与渔业船舶实际情况不相符的；  
(三)超越规定的权限进行渔业船舶检验的。

**Article 36** Where any of the following circumstances occurs in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, an order shall be issued to immediately rectify; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions such as demotion, removal from office, or disqualification from inspection according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; the fishing vessel inspection certificate already issued shall be invalid:  
(1) inspection is not carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the competent fisheries administrative department under the State Council;  
(2) the fishing vessel inspection certificate or inspection records or inspection report issued does not conform to the actual condition of the fishing vessel;  
(3) fishing vessel inspection is carried out beyond the prescribed authority.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　伪造、变造渔业船舶检验证书、检验记录和检验报告，或者私刻渔业船舶检验业务印章的，应当予以没收；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 37** Where a fishing vessel inspection certificate, inspection records, or inspection report is forged or altered, or the seal for fishing vessel inspection business is privately engraved, such items shall be confiscated; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本条例规定的行政处罚，由县级以上人民政府渔业行政主管部门或者其所属的渔业行政执法机构依据职权决定。  
前款规定的行政处罚决定机关及其工作人员利用职务上的便利收取他人财物、其他好处，或者不履行监督职责、发现违法行为不予查处，或者有其他玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊行为，构成犯罪的，依法追究直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员的刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 38** The administrative penalties provided for in these Regulations shall be decided by the competent fisheries administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the fisheries administrative law enforcement organ affiliated therewith in accordance with their functions.  
Where the administrative penalty decision authority prescribed in the preceding paragraph or its staff members take advantage of their positions to accept property or other benefits from others, or fail to perform their supervisory duties, or fail to investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered, or commit other acts of dereliction of duty, abuse of power, or favoritism and malpractice, if a crime is constituted, criminal liability of the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be investigated according to law; if no crime is constituted, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　外国籍渔业船舶，其船旗国委托中华人民共和国检验的，依照本条例的规定执行。

**Article 39** For a foreign-registered fishing vessel whose flag State entrusts the inspection to the People's Republic of China, the provisions of these Regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　本条例自2003年8月1日起施行。

**Article 40** These Regulations shall come into force as of August 1, 2003.
