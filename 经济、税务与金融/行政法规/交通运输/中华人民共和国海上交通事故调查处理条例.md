# 中华人民共和国海上交通事故调查处理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1990-03-03  
**生效日期**：1990-03-03

> (1990年1月11日国务院批准　1990年3月3日交通部令第14号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强海上交通安全管理，及时调查处理海上交通事故，根据《中华人民共和国海上交通安全法》的有关规定，制定本条例。 第二条　中华人民共和国港务监督机构是本条例的实施机关。 第三条　本条例适用于船舶、设施在中华人民共和国沿海水域内发生的海上交通事故。 以渔业为主的渔港水域内发生的海上交通事故和沿海水域内渔业船舶之间、军用船舶之间发生的海上交通事故的调查处理，国家法律、行政法规另有专门规定的，从其规定。 第四条　本条例所称海上交通事故是指船舶、设施发生的下列事故： (一)碰撞、触碰或浪损； (二)触礁或搁浅； (三)火灾或爆炸； (四)沉没； (五)在航行中发生影响适航性能的机件或重要属具的损坏或灭失； (六)其他引起财产损失和人身伤亡的海上交通事故。 第二章　报告 第五条　船舶、设施发生海上交通事故，必须立即用甚高频电话、无线电报或其他有效手段向就近港口的港务监督报告。报告的内容应当包括：船舶或设施的名称、呼号、国籍、起迄港，船舶或设施的所有人或经营人名称，事故发生的时间、地点、海况以及船舶、设施的损害程度、救助要求等。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强海上交通安全管理，及时调查处理海上交通事故，根据《中华人民共和国海上交通安全法》的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Maritime Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of strengthening the management of maritime traffic safety and timely investigating and handling maritime traffic accidents.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国港务监督机构是本条例的实施机关。

**Article 2** The port superintendency agency of the People's Republic of China shall be the implementing authority of these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例适用于船舶、设施在中华人民共和国沿海水域内发生的海上交通事故。  
以渔业为主的渔港水域内发生的海上交通事故和沿海水域内渔业船舶之间、军用船舶之间发生的海上交通事故的调查处理，国家法律、行政法规另有专门规定的，从其规定。

**Article 3** These Regulations shall apply to maritime traffic accidents involving vessels and installations within the coastal waters of the People's Republic of China.  
Where there are special provisions in national laws and administrative regulations regarding the investigation and handling of maritime traffic accidents occurring in fishing port waters mainly used for fisheries, and maritime traffic accidents between fishing vessels or between military vessels within coastal waters, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　本条例所称海上交通事故是指船舶、设施发生的下列事故：  
(一)碰撞、触碰或浪损；  
(二)触礁或搁浅；  
(三)火灾或爆炸；  
(四)沉没；  
(五)在航行中发生影响适航性能的机件或重要属具的损坏或灭失；  
(六)其他引起财产损失和人身伤亡的海上交通事故。

**Article 4** For the purpose of these Regulations, "maritime traffic accidents" means the following accidents involving vessels or installations:  
(1) Collision, striking or damage by waves;  
(2) Running aground or stranding;  
(3) Fire or explosion;  
(4) Sinking;  
(5) Damage or loss of machinery or important appurtenances affecting seaworthiness during navigation;  
(6) Other maritime traffic accidents causing property damage or personal injury.

### 第二章　报告

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　船舶、设施发生海上交通事故，必须立即用甚高频电话、无线电报或其他有效手段向就近港口的港务监督报告。报告的内容应当包括：船舶或设施的名称、呼号、国籍、起迄港，船舶或设施的所有人或经营人名称，事故发生的时间、地点、海况以及船舶、设施的损害程度、救助要求等。

**Article 5** Where a vessel or installation is involved in a maritime traffic accident, it must immediately report to the port superintendency agency at the nearest port by VHF radiotelephone, radiogram, or other effective means. The report shall include: the name, call sign, nationality, port of departure and destination of the vessel or installation; the name of the owner or operator of the vessel or installation; the time, location and sea conditions of the accident; the extent of damage to the vessel or installation; and the request for assistance.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　船舶、设施发生海上交通事故，除应按第五条规定立即提出扼要报告外，还必须按下列规定向港务监督提交《海上交通事故报告书》和必要的文书资料：  
(一)船舶、设施在港区水域内发生海上交通事故，必须在事故发生后二十四小时内向当地港务监督提交。  
(二)船舶、设施在港区水域以外的沿海水域发生海上交通事故，船舶必须在到达中华人民共和国的第一个港口后四十八小时内向港务监督提交；设施必须在事故发生后四十八小时内用电报向就近港口的港务监督报告《海上交通事故报告书》要求的内容。  
(三)引航员在引领船舶的过程中发生海上交通事故，应当在返港后二十四小时内向当地港务监督提交《海上交通事故报告书》。  
前款(一)、(二)项因特殊情况不能按规定时间提交《海上交通事故报告书》的，在征得港务监督同意后可予以适当延迟。

**Article 6** Where a vessel or installation is involved in a maritime traffic accident, in addition to making a brief report immediately in accordance with Article 5, it must also submit a "Maritime Traffic Accident Report" and necessary documents to the port superintendency agency in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) If the maritime traffic accident occurs within the port area waters, the report must be submitted to the local port superintendency agency within twenty-four hours after the accident.  
(2) If the maritime traffic accident occurs in coastal waters outside the port area, the vessel must submit the report to the port superintendency agency within forty-eight hours after arriving at the first port of the People's Republic of China; the installation must report by radiogram the contents required in the "Maritime Traffic Accident Report" to the port superintendency agency at the nearest port within forty-eight hours after the accident.  
(3) If a pilot is involved in a maritime traffic accident while piloting a vessel, the pilot shall submit a "Maritime Traffic Accident Report" to the local port superintendency agency within twenty-four hours after returning to port.  
If, due to special circumstances, the "Maritime Traffic Accident Report" cannot be submitted within the time limit specified in items (1) and (2) of the preceding paragraph, an appropriate delay may be granted with the consent of the port superintendency agency.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　《海上交通事故报告书》应当如实写明下列情况：  
(一)船舶、设施概况和主要性能数据；  
(二)船舶、设施所有人或经营人的名称、地址；  
(三)事故发生的时间和地点；  
(四)事故发生时的气象和海况；  
(五)事故发生的详细经过(碰撞事故应附相对运动示意图)；  
(六)损害情况(附船舶、设施受损部位简图。难以在规定时间内查清的，应于检验后补报)；  
(七)船舶、设施沉没的，其沉没概位；  
(八)与事故有关的其他情况。

**Article 7** The "Maritime Traffic Accident Report" shall truthfully state the following:  
(1) General description and main performance data of the vessel or installation;  
(2) Name and address of the owner or operator of the vessel or installation;  
(3) Time and place of the accident;  
(4) Weather and sea conditions at the time of the accident;  
(5) Detailed account of the accident (a sketch of relative motion shall be attached for collision accidents);  
(6) Damage details (a sketch of damaged parts of the vessel or installation shall be attached; if it is difficult to ascertain within the specified time, a supplementary report shall be made after survey);  
(7) Approximate position of sinking if the vessel or installation sank;  
(8) Other circumstances related to the accident.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　海上交通事故报告必须真实，不得隐瞒或捏造。

**Article 8** The report of a maritime traffic accident must be truthful and shall not be concealed or fabricated.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　因海上交通事故致使船舶、设施发生损害，船长、设施负责人应申请中国当地或船舶第一到达港地的检验部门进行检验或鉴定，并应将检验报告副本送交港务监督备案。  
前款检验、鉴定事项，港务监督可委托有关单位或部门进行，其费用由船舶、设施所有人或经营人承担。  
船舶、设施发生火灾、爆炸等事故，船长、设施负责人必须申请公安消防监督机关鉴定，并将鉴定书副本送交港务监督备案。

**Article 9** Where a vessel or installation is damaged due to a maritime traffic accident, the master or the person in charge of the installation shall apply to the local survey department in China or at the first port of call for survey or expert evaluation, and shall send a copy of the survey report to the port superintendency agency for record.  
The port superintendency agency may entrust the survey or expert evaluation referred to in the preceding paragraph to a relevant unit or department, and the expenses shall be borne by the owner or operator of the vessel or installation.  
In the event of a fire, explosion or other accident on a vessel or installation, the master or the person in charge of the installation must apply to the public security fire supervision authority for expert evaluation, and shall send a copy of the expert evaluation report to the port superintendency agency for record.

### 第三章　调查

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在港区水域内发生的海上交通事故，由港区地的港务监督进行调查。  
在港区水域外发生的海上交通事故，由就近港口的港务监督或船舶到达的中华人民共和国的第一个港口的港务监督进行调查。必要时，由中华人民共和国港务监督局指定的港务监督进行调查。  
港务监督认为必要时，可以通知有关机关和社会组织参加事故调查。

**Article 10** A maritime traffic accident occurring within the port area waters shall be investigated by the port superintendency agency of that port area.  
A maritime traffic accident occurring outside the port area waters shall be investigated by the port superintendency agency at the nearest port or the port superintendency agency at the first port of call of the vessel in the People's Republic of China. Where necessary, the investigation shall be carried out by a port superintendency agency designated by the Maritime Safety Administration of the People's Republic of China.  
If the port superintendency agency deems it necessary, it may notify relevant authorities and social organizations to participate in the accident investigation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　港务监督在接到事故报告后，应及时进行调查。调查应客观、全面，不受事故当事人提供材料的限制。根据调查工作的需要，港务监督有权：  
(一)询问有关人员；  
(二)要求被调查人员提供书面材料和证明；  
(三)要求有关当事人提供航海日志、轮机日志、车钟记录、报务日志、航向记录、海图、船舶资料、航行设备仪器的性能以及其他必要的原始文书资料；  
(四)检查船舶、设施及有关设备的证书、人员证书和核实事故发生前船舶的适航状态、设施的技术状态；  
(五)检查船舶、设施及其货物的损害情况和人员伤亡情况；  
(六)勘查事故现场，搜集有关物证。  
港务监督在调查中，可以使用录音、照相、录相等设备，并可采取法律允许的其他调查手段。

**Article 11** Upon receiving a report of an accident, the port superintendency agency shall promptly conduct an investigation. The investigation shall be objective and comprehensive, and shall not be limited by the materials provided by the parties involved in the accident. According to the needs of the investigation, the port superintendency agency shall have the authority to:  
(1) Interview relevant persons;  
(2) Require the persons under investigation to provide written materials and evidence;  
(3) Require relevant parties to provide deck logs, engine room logs, bell book records, radio logs, course records, charts, vessel information, performance of navigation equipment and instruments, and other necessary original documents;  
(4) Inspect certificates of the vessel, installation and related equipment, personnel certificates, and verify the seaworthiness of the vessel and the technical condition of the installation before the accident;  
(5) Inspect the damage to the vessel, installation and their cargo, and personal injuries;  
(6) Survey the accident scene and collect relevant physical evidence.  
During the investigation, the port superintendency agency may use audio recording, photography, video recording and other equipment, and may adopt other investigation means permitted by law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　被调查人必须接受调查，如实陈述事故的有关情节，并提供真实的文书资料。  
港务监督人员在执行调查任务时，应当向被调查人员出示证件。

**Article 12** The person under investigation must accept the investigation, truthfully state the relevant circumstances of the accident, and provide authentic documents and materials.  
When performing investigation tasks, officers of the port superintendency agency shall produce their credentials to the person under investigation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　港务监督因调查海上交通事故的需要，可以令当事船舶驶抵指定地点接受调查。当事船舶在不危及自身安全的情况下，未经港务监督同意，不得离开指定地点。

**Article 13** For the purpose of investigating a maritime traffic accident, the port superintendency agency may order the vessel involved to proceed to a designated location for investigation. Without endangering its own safety, the vessel involved shall not leave the designated location without the consent of the port superintendency agency.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　港务监督的海上交通事故调查材料，公安机关、国家安全机关、监察机关、检察机关、审判机关和海事仲裁委员会及法律规定的其他机关和人员因办案需要可以查阅、摘录或复制，审判机关确因开庭需要可以借用。

**Article 14** The investigation materials of the port superintendency agency concerning maritime traffic accidents may be consulted, excerpted or copied by public security organs, state security organs, supervisory organs, procuratorial organs, judicial organs and maritime arbitration commissions, as well as other organs and persons as provided by law, for the purpose of handling cases; judicial organs may borrow such materials if truly necessary for court hearings.

### 第四章　处理

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　港务监督应当根据对海上交通事故的调查，作出《海上交通事故调查报告书》，查明事故发生的原因，判明当事人的责任；构成重大事故的，通报当地检察机关。

**Article 15** The port superintendency agency shall, based on the investigation of the maritime traffic accident, produce a "Maritime Traffic Accident Investigation Report", ascertain the cause of the accident, and determine the liability of the parties; if the accident constitutes a major accident, it shall notify the local procuratorial organ.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　《海上交通事故调查报告书》应包括以下内容：  
(一)船舶、设施的概况和主要数据；  
(二)船舶、设施所有人或经营人的名称和地址；  
(三)事故发生的时间、地点、过程、气象海况、损害情况等；  
(四)事故发生的原因及依据；  
(五)当事人各方的责任及依据；  
(六)其他有关情况。

**Article 16** The "Maritime Traffic Accident Investigation Report" shall include the following:  
(1) General description and main data of the vessel or installation;  
(2) Name and address of the owner or operator of the vessel or installation;  
(3) Time, location, process, weather and sea conditions, and damage details of the accident;  
(4) Cause of the accident and the basis therefor;  
(5) Liability of each party and the basis therefor;  
(6) Other relevant circumstances.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对海上交通事故的发生负有责任的人员，港务监督可以根据其责任的性质和程度依法给予下列处罚：  
(一)对中国籍船员、引航员或设施上的工作人员，可以给予警告、罚款或扣留、吊销职务证书；  
(二)对外国籍船员或设施上的工作人员，可以给予警告、罚款或将其过失通报其所属国家的主管机关。

**Article 17** The port superintendency agency may, according to law, impose the following penalties on persons liable for the occurrence of a maritime traffic accident, depending on the nature and degree of their liability:  
(1) For Chinese crew members, pilots, or staff on installations, a warning, fine, or detention or revocation of their service certificates may be imposed;  
(2) For foreign crew members or staff on installations, a warning, fine, or the notification of their faults to the competent authority of their country may be imposed.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对海上交通事故的发生负有责任的人员及船舶、设施的所有人或经营人，需要追究其行政责任的，由港务监督提交其主管机关或行政监察机关处理；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 18** Where administrative liability is to be investigated against persons liable for the occurrence of a maritime traffic accident and the owners or operators of vessels or installations, the port superintendency agency shall refer the matter to their competent authorities or administrative supervisory organs for handling; if the act constitutes a crime, the judicial organ shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　根据海上交通事故发生的原因，港务监督可责令有关船舶、设施的所有人、经营人限期加强对所属船舶、设施的安全管理。对拒不加强安全管理或在限期内达不到安全要求的，港务监督有权责令其停航、改航、停止作业，并可采取其他必要的强制性处置措施。

**Article 19** Based on the cause of the maritime traffic accident, the port superintendency agency may order the owners or operators of the relevant vessels or installations to strengthen the safety management of their vessels or installations within a specified time limit. If they refuse to strengthen safety management or fail to meet safety requirements within the time limit, the port superintendency agency shall have the authority to order them to suspend navigation, change course, cease operations, and may take other necessary compulsory measures.

### 第五章　调解

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对船舶、设施发生海上交通事故引起的民事侵权赔偿纠纷，当事人可以申请港务监督调解。  
调解必须遵循自愿、公平的原则，不得强迫。

**Article 20** For disputes over civil tort compensation arising from a maritime traffic accident involving a vessel or installation, the parties may apply to the port superintendency agency for mediation.  
Mediation must follow the principles of voluntariness and fairness, and shall not be coerced.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　前条民事纠纷，凡已向海事法院起诉或申请海事仲裁机构仲裁的，当事人不得再申请港务监督调解。

**Article 21** For the civil disputes referred to in the preceding Article, if the parties have already filed a prosecution with a maritime court or applied for arbitration with a maritime arbitration institution, they shall not apply to the port superintendency agency for mediation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　调解由当事人各方在事故发生之日起三十日内向负责该事故调查的港务监督提交书面申请。港务监督要求提供担保的，当事人应附经济赔偿担保证明文件。

**Article 22** Mediation shall be initiated by the parties by submitting a written application to the port superintendency agency responsible for investigating the accident within thirty days from the date of the accident. If the port superintendency agency requires the provision of security, the parties shall attach documents evidencing security for economic compensation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　经调解达成协议的，港务监督应制作调解书。调解书应当写明当事人的姓名或名称、住所、法定代表人或代理人的姓名及职务、纠纷的主要事实、当事人的责任、协议的内容、调解费的承担、调解协议履行的期限。调解书由当事人各方共同签字，并经港务监督盖印确认。调解书应交当事方各持一份，港务监督留存一份。

**Article 23** If an agreement is reached through mediation, the port superintendency agency shall prepare a mediation statement. The mediation statement shall set forth the names or titles and domiciles of the parties, the names and positions of their legal representatives or agents, the main facts of the dispute, the liability of the parties, the content of the agreement, the allocation of mediation fees, and the time limit for performance of the mediation agreement. The mediation statement shall be signed jointly by all parties and confirmed by the seal of the port superintendency agency. Each party shall hold one copy of the mediation statement, and the port superintendency agency shall retain one copy.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　调解达成协议的，当事人各方应当自动履行。达成协议后当事人翻悔的或逾期不履行协议的，视为调解不成。

**Article 24** If an agreement is reached through mediation, the parties shall voluntarily perform it. If a party repudiates the agreement after it is reached or fails to perform it within the prescribed time limit, the mediation shall be deemed to have failed.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　凡向港务监督申请调解的民事纠纷，当事人中途不愿调解的，应当向港务监督递交撤销调解的书面申请，并通知对方当事人。

**Article 25** For a civil dispute for which mediation has been applied to the port superintendency agency, if a party no longer wishes to mediate midway, it shall submit a written application for withdrawal of mediation to the port superintendency agency and notify the other party.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　港务监督自收到调解申请书之日起三个月内未能使当事人各方达成调解协议的，可以宣布调解不成。

**Article 26** If the port superintendency agency fails to bring the parties to a mediation agreement within three months from the date of receiving the mediation application, it may declare the mediation unsuccessful.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　不愿意调解或调解不成的，当事人可以向海事法院起诉或申请海事仲裁机构仲裁。

**Article 27** If a party is unwilling to mediate or mediation fails, the party may file a prosecution with a maritime court or apply for arbitration with a maritime arbitration institution.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　凡申请港务监督调解的，应向港务监督缴纳调解费。调解的收费标准，由交通部会同国家物价局、财政部制定。  
经调解达成协议的，调解费用按当事人过失比例或约定的数额分摊；调解不成的，由当事人各方平均分摊。

**Article 28** Anyone who applies to the port superintendency agency for mediation shall pay mediation fees. The standards for mediation fees shall be formulated by the Ministry of Communications jointly with the State Pricing Bureau and the Ministry of Finance.  
If an agreement is reached through mediation, the mediation fees shall be apportioned according to the proportions of fault of the parties or the amount agreed upon; if mediation fails, the fees shall be apportioned equally among the parties.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，港务监督可视情节对有关当事人(自然人)处以警告或者二百元以下罚款；对船舶所有人、经营人处以警告或者五千元以下罚款：  
(一)未按规定的时间向港务监督报告事故或提交《海上交通事故报告书》或[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)要求的判决书、裁决书、调解书的副本的；  
(二)未按港务监督要求驶往指定地点，或在未出现危及船舶安全的情况下未经港务监督同意擅自驶离指定地点的；  
(三)事故报告或《海上交通事故报告书》的内容不符合规定要求或不真实，影响调查工作进行或给有关部门造成损失的；  
(四)违反第九条规定，影响事故调查的；  
(五)拒绝接受调查或无理阻挠、干扰港务监督进行调查的；  
(六)在受调查时故意隐瞒事实或提供虚假证明的。  
前款第(五)、(六)项行为构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 29** Any person who violates these Regulations by committing any of the following acts shall, depending on the circumstances, be given a warning or a fine of not more than 200 yuan by the port superintendency agency if the party (natural person) is an individual; or be given a warning or a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan if the party is a shipowner or operator:  
(1) Failing to report the accident or submit the "Maritime Traffic Accident Report" or the copies of judgments, awards or mediation statements required under Article 32 of these Regulations to the port superintendency agency within the prescribed time;  
(2) Failing to proceed to the designated location as required by the port superintendency agency, or leaving the designated location without the consent of the port superintendency agency when there is no danger to the safety of the vessel;  
(3) The contents of the accident report or the "Maritime Traffic Accident Report" do not meet the prescribed requirements or are untruthful, thereby affecting the investigation or causing losses to relevant departments;  
(4) Violating the provisions of Article 9, thereby affecting the accident investigation;  
(5) Refusing to accept the investigation or unreasonably obstructing or interfering with the investigation by the port superintendency agency;  
(6) Intentionally concealing facts or providing false evidence during the investigation.  
If the acts specified in items (5) and (6) of the preceding paragraph constitute a crime, the judicial organ shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对违反本条例规定，玩忽职守、滥用职权、营私舞弊、索贿受贿的港务监督人员，由行政监察机关或其所在单位给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 30** Any officer of the port superintendency agency who, in violation of these Regulations, neglects duty, abuses power, engages in malpractice for personal gain, or solicits or accepts bribes shall be given an administrative sanction by the administrative supervisory organ or the unit to which he belongs; if the act constitutes a crime, the judicial organ shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　当事人对港务监督依据本条例给予的处罚不服的，可以依法向人民法院提起行政诉讼。

**Article 31** If a party is dissatisfied with a penalty imposed by the port superintendency agency pursuant to these Regulations, the party may, according to law, file an administrative action with a People's Court.

### 第七章　特别规定

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　中国籍船舶在中华人民共和国沿海水域以外发生的海上交通事故，其所有人或经营人应当向船籍港的港务监督报告，并于事故发生之日起六十日内提交《海上交通事故报告书》。如果事故在国外诉讼、仲裁或调解，船舶所有人或经营人应在诉讼、仲裁或调解结束后六十日内将判决书、裁决书或调解书的副本或影印件报船籍港的港务监督备案。

**Article 32** For a maritime traffic accident occurring outside the coastal waters of the People's Republic of China involving a Chinese vessel, the owner or operator shall report to the port superintendency agency at the port of registry and submit a "Maritime Traffic Accident Report" within sixty days from the date of the accident. If the accident is subject to litigation, arbitration or mediation abroad, the owner or operator of the vessel shall, within sixty days after the conclusion of the litigation, arbitration or mediation, report copies or reproductions of the judgment, award or mediation statement to the port superintendency agency at the port of registry for record.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　派往外国籍船舶任职的持有中华人民共和国船员职务证书的中国籍船员对海上交通事故的发生负有责任的，其派出单位应当在事故发生之日起六十日内向签发该职务证书的港务监督提交《海上交通事故报告书》。  
本条第一款和第三十二条的海上交通事故的调查处理，按本条例的有关规定办理。

**Article 33** Where a Chinese crew member holding a certificate of competency of the People's Republic of China assigned to serve on a foreign vessel is liable for the occurrence of a maritime traffic accident, the dispatching unit shall submit a "Maritime Traffic Accident Report" to the port superintendency agency that issued the certificate of competency within sixty days from the date of the accident.  
The investigation and handling of the maritime traffic accidents referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article and Article 32 shall be conducted in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　对违反海上交通安全管理法规进行违章操作，虽未造成直接的交通事故，但构成重大潜在事故隐患的，港务监督可以依据本条例进行调查和处罚。

**Article 34** Where a person violates the laws and regulations on maritime traffic safety management and conducts illegal operations, which, although not directly causing a traffic accident, constitutes a major potential accident hazard, the port superintendency agency may investigate and impose penalties in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　因海上交通事故产生的海洋环境污染，按照我国海洋环境保护的有关法律、法规处理。

**Article 35** Marine environmental pollution caused by a maritime traffic accident shall be handled in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of China on marine environmental protection.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本条例由交通部负责解释。

**Article 36** The Ministry of Communications shall be responsible for the interpretation of these Regulations.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 37** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
