# 放射性物品运输安全管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2009-09-14  
**生效日期**：2010-01-01

> (2009年9月7日国务院第80次常务会议通过　2009年9月14日中华人民共和国国务院令第562号公布　自2010年1月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对放射性物品运输的安全管理，保障人体健康，保护环境，促进核能、核技术的开发与和平利用，根据《中华人民共和国放射性污染防治法》，制定本条例。 第二条　放射性物品的运输和放射性物品运输容器的设计、制造等活动，适用本条例。 本条例所称放射性物品，是指含有放射性核素，并且其活度和比活度均高于国家规定的豁免值的物品。 第三条　根据放射性物品的特性及其对人体健康和环境的潜在危害程度，将放射性物品分为一类、二类和三类。 一类放射性物品，是指Ⅰ类放射源、高水平放射性废物、乏燃料等释放到环境后对人体健康和环境产生重大辐射影响的放射性物品。 二类放射性物品，是指Ⅱ类和Ⅲ类放射源、中等水平放射性废物等释放到环境后对人体健康和环境产生一般辐射影响的放射性物品。 三类放射性物品，是指Ⅳ类和Ⅴ类放射源、低水平放射性废物、放射性药品等释放到环境后对人体健康和环境产生较小辐射影响的放射性物品。 放射性物品的具体分类和名录，由国务院核安全监管部门会同国务院公安、卫生、海关、交通运输、铁路、民航、核工业行业主管部门制定。 第四条　国务院核安全监管部门对放射性物品运输的核与辐射安全实施监督管理。 国务院公安、交通运输、铁路、民航等有关主管部门依照本条例规定和各自的职责，负责放射性物品运输安全的有关监督管理工作。 县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门和公安、交通运输等有关主管部门，依照本条例规定和各自的职责，负责本行政区域放射性物品运输安全的有关监督管理工作。 第五条　运输放射性物品，应当使用专用的放射性物品运输包装容器(以下简称运输容器)。 放射性物品的运输和放射性物品运输容器的设计、制造，应当符合国家放射性物品运输安全标准。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对放射性物品运输的安全管理，保障人体健康，保护环境，促进核能、核技术的开发与和平利用，根据《中华人民共和国放射性污染防治法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law on Prevention and Control of Radioactive Pollution of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of strengthening the safety management of the transport of radioactive articles, protecting human health, protecting the environment, and promoting the development and peaceful use of nuclear energy and nuclear technology.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　放射性物品的运输和放射性物品运输容器的设计、制造等活动，适用本条例。  
本条例所称放射性物品，是指含有放射性核素，并且其活度和比活度均高于国家规定的豁免值的物品。

**Article 2** These Regulations apply to the transport of radioactive articles and the design, manufacture and other activities of transport packages for radioactive articles.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, radioactive articles refer to articles that contain radionuclides and whose activity and specific activity are both higher than the exemption levels prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　根据放射性物品的特性及其对人体健康和环境的潜在危害程度，将放射性物品分为一类、二类和三类。  
一类放射性物品，是指Ⅰ类放射源、高水平放射性废物、乏燃料等释放到环境后对人体健康和环境产生重大辐射影响的放射性物品。  
二类放射性物品，是指Ⅱ类和Ⅲ类放射源、中等水平放射性废物等释放到环境后对人体健康和环境产生一般辐射影响的放射性物品。  
三类放射性物品，是指Ⅳ类和Ⅴ类放射源、低水平放射性废物、放射性药品等释放到环境后对人体健康和环境产生较小辐射影响的放射性物品。  
放射性物品的具体分类和名录，由国务院核安全监管部门会同国务院公安、卫生、海关、交通运输、铁路、民航、核工业行业主管部门制定。

**Article 3** Radioactive articles are divided into Category I, Category II and Category III according to the characteristics of radioactive articles and their potential harm to human health and the environment.  
Category I radioactive articles refer to radioactive articles that, when released to the environment, have significant radiation impacts on human health and the environment, such as Category I radioactive sources, high-level radioactive waste and spent nuclear fuel.  
Category II radioactive articles refer to radioactive articles that, when released to the environment, have general radiation impacts on human health and the environment, such as Category II and III radioactive sources and intermediate-level radioactive waste.  
Category III radioactive articles refer to radioactive articles that, when released to the environment, have relatively minor radiation impacts on human health and the environment, such as Category IV and V radioactive sources, low-level radioactive waste and radioactive drugs.  
The specific classification and list of radioactive articles shall be formulated by the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council jointly with the competent departments of public security, health, customs, transport, railway, civil aviation and nuclear industry under the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院核安全监管部门对放射性物品运输的核与辐射安全实施监督管理。  
国务院公安、交通运输、铁路、民航等有关主管部门依照本条例规定和各自的职责，负责放射性物品运输安全的有关监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门和公安、交通运输等有关主管部门，依照本条例规定和各自的职责，负责本行政区域放射性物品运输安全的有关监督管理工作。

**Article 4** The nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall exercise supervision and administration over the nuclear and radiation safety of the transport of radioactive articles.  
The competent departments of public security, transport, railway, civil aviation and other relevant departments under the State Council shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant supervision and administration of the safety of the transport of radioactive articles.  
The environmental protection departments and the competent departments of public security, transport and other relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant supervision and administration of the safety of the transport of radioactive articles within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　运输放射性物品，应当使用专用的放射性物品运输包装容器(以下简称运输容器)。  
放射性物品的运输和放射性物品运输容器的设计、制造，应当符合国家放射性物品运输安全标准。  
国家放射性物品运输安全标准，由国务院核安全监管部门制定，由国务院核安全监管部门和国务院标准化主管部门联合发布。国务院核安全监管部门制定国家放射性物品运输安全标准，应当征求国务院公安、卫生、交通运输、铁路、民航、核工业行业主管部门的意见。

**Article 5** Dedicated transport packages for radioactive articles (hereinafter referred to as transport packages) shall be used for the transport of radioactive articles.  
The transport of radioactive articles and the design and manufacture of transport packages for radioactive articles shall comply with the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles.  
The national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles shall be formulated by the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council, and shall be jointly issued by the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council and the competent department of standardization under the State Council. When formulating the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles, the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall solicit opinions from the competent departments of public security, health, transport, railway, civil aviation and nuclear industry under the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　放射性物品运输容器的设计、制造单位应当建立健全责任制度，加强质量管理，并对所从事的放射性物品运输容器的设计、制造活动负责。  
放射性物品的托运人(以下简称托运人)应当制定核与辐射事故应急方案，在放射性物品运输中采取有效的辐射防护和安全保卫措施，并对放射性物品运输中的核与辐射安全负责。

**Article 6** The unit designing or manufacturing transport packages for radioactive articles shall establish and improve the responsibility system, strengthen quality management, and be responsible for the design or manufacture of transport packages for radioactive articles that it undertakes.  
The consignor of radioactive articles (hereinafter referred to as the consignor) shall formulate a nuclear and radiation accident emergency plan, take effective radiation protection and security measures in the transport of radioactive articles, and be responsible for the nuclear and radiation safety during the transport of radioactive articles.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　任何单位和个人对违反本条例规定的行为，有权向国务院核安全监管部门或者其他依法履行放射性物品运输安全监督管理职责的部门举报。  
接到举报的部门应当依法调查处理，并为举报人保密。

**Article 7** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report any act violating the provisions of these Regulations to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council or other departments that perform the duties of supervision and administration over the safety of the transport of radioactive articles according to law.  
The department receiving the report shall investigate and handle it according to law, and shall keep the informant confidential.

### 第二章　放射性物品运输容器的设计

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　放射性物品运输容器设计单位应当建立健全和有效实施质量保证体系，按照国家放射性物品运输安全标准进行设计，并通过试验验证或者分析论证等方式，对设计的放射性物品运输容器的安全性能进行评价。

**Article 8** The unit designing transport packages for radioactive articles shall establish, improve and effectively implement a quality assurance system, conduct the design in accordance with the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles, and evaluate the safety performance of the designed transport packages for radioactive articles through test verification or analysis and demonstration.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　放射性物品运输容器设计单位应当建立健全档案制度，按照质量保证体系的要求，如实记录放射性物品运输容器的设计和安全性能评价过程。  
进行一类放射性物品运输容器设计，应当编制设计安全评价报告书；进行二类放射性物品运输容器设计，应当编制设计安全评价报告表。

**Article 9** The unit designing transport packages for radioactive articles shall establish and improve the archives system, and truthfully record the process of design and safety performance evaluation of transport packages for radioactive articles in accordance with the requirements of the quality assurance system.  
For the design of a Category I transport package for radioactive articles, a design safety evaluation report shall be prepared; for the design of a Category II transport package for radioactive articles, a design safety evaluation report form shall be prepared.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　一类放射性物品运输容器的设计，应当在首次用于制造前报国务院核安全监管部门审查批准。  
申请批准一类放射性物品运输容器的设计，设计单位应当向国务院核安全监管部门提出书面申请，并提交下列材料：  
(一)设计总图及其设计说明书；  
(二)设计安全评价报告书；  
(三)质量保证大纲。

**Article 10** The design of a Category I transport package for radioactive articles shall be submitted to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for examination and approval before being used for manufacture for the first time.  
To apply for approval of the design of a Category I transport package for radioactive articles, the design unit shall submit a written application to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council, and shall provide the following materials:  
(1) the general design drawings and their design specifications;  
(2) the design safety evaluation report;  
(3) the quality assurance program.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院核安全监管部门应当自受理申请之日起45个工作日内完成审查，对符合国家放射性物品运输安全标准的，颁发一类放射性物品运输容器设计批准书，并公告批准文号；对不符合国家放射性物品运输安全标准的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。

**Article 11** The nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall complete the examination within 45 working days from the date of accepting the application. If the design complies with the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles, it shall issue a design approval certificate for the Category I transport package for radioactive articles and publish the approval number; if it does not comply with the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles, it shall notify the applicant unit in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　设计单位修改已批准的一类放射性物品运输容器设计中有关安全内容的，应当按照原申请程序向国务院核安全监管部门重新申请领取一类放射性物品运输容器设计批准书。

**Article 12** If the design unit modifies the safety-related content of the approved design of a Category I transport package for radioactive articles, it shall re-apply to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for a design approval certificate for the Category I transport package for radioactive articles in accordance with the original application procedure.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　二类放射性物品运输容器的设计，设计单位应当在首次用于制造前，将设计总图及其设计说明书、设计安全评价报告表报国务院核安全监管部门备案。

**Article 13** For the design of a Category II transport package for radioactive articles, the design unit shall, before it is used for manufacture for the first time, submit the general design drawings and their design specifications and the design safety evaluation report form to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　三类放射性物品运输容器的设计，设计单位应当编制设计符合国家放射性物品运输安全标准的证明文件并存档备查。

**Article 14** For the design of a Category III transport package for radioactive articles, the design unit shall prepare a certificate document proving that the design complies with the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles and shall keep it on file for future reference.

### 第三章　放射性物品运输容器的制造与使用

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　放射性物品运输容器制造单位，应当按照设计要求和国家放射性物品运输安全标准，对制造的放射性物品运输容器进行质量检验，编制质量检验报告。  
未经质量检验或者经检验不合格的放射性物品运输容器，不得交付使用。

**Article 15** The unit manufacturing transport packages for radioactive articles shall, in accordance with the design requirements and the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles, conduct quality inspection on the manufactured transport packages for radioactive articles and prepare a quality inspection report.  
No transport package for radioactive articles that has not undergone quality inspection or has been found unqualified upon inspection may be delivered for use.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　从事一类放射性物品运输容器制造活动的单位，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有与所从事的制造活动相适应的专业技术人员；  
(二)有与所从事的制造活动相适应的生产条件和检测手段；  
(三)有健全的管理制度和完善的质量保证体系。

**Article 16** A unit engaged in the manufacture of Category I transport packages for radioactive articles shall satisfy the following conditions:  
(1) having professional and technical personnel suitable for the manufacture activity it undertakes;  
(2) having production conditions and testing means suitable for the manufacture activity it undertakes;  
(3) having a sound management system and a complete quality assurance system.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　从事一类放射性物品运输容器制造活动的单位，应当申请领取一类放射性物品运输容器制造许可证(以下简称制造许可证)。  
申请领取制造许可证的单位，应当向国务院核安全监管部门提出书面申请，并提交其符合[本条例第十六条](#art-16)规定条件的证明材料和申请制造的运输容器型号。  
禁止无制造许可证或者超出制造许可证规定的范围从事一类放射性物品运输容器的制造活动。

**Article 17** A unit engaged in the manufacture of Category I transport packages for radioactive articles shall apply for and obtain a manufacturing license for Category I transport packages for radioactive articles (hereinafter referred to as the manufacturing license).  
A unit applying for a manufacturing license shall submit a written application to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council, and shall provide evidentiary materials proving that it satisfies the conditions specified in Article 16 of these Regulations and the type of transport package to be manufactured.  
It is prohibited to engage in the manufacture of Category I transport packages for radioactive articles without a manufacturing license or beyond the scope specified in the manufacturing license.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院核安全监管部门应当自受理申请之日起45个工作日内完成审查，对符合条件的，颁发制造许可证，并予以公告；对不符合条件的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。

**Article 18** The nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall complete the examination within 45 working days from the date of accepting the application. If the conditions are met, it shall issue a manufacturing license and make a public announcement; if the conditions are not met, it shall notify the applicant unit in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　制造许可证应当载明下列内容：  
(一)制造单位名称、住所和法定代表人；  
(二)许可制造的运输容器的型号；  
(三)有效期限；  
(四)发证机关、发证日期和证书编号。

**Article 19** A manufacturing license shall specify the following:  
(1) the name, domicile and legal representative of the manufacturing unit;  
(2) the type of transport package authorized to be manufactured;  
(3) the period of validity;  
(4) the issuing authority, date of issuance and certificate number.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　一类放射性物品运输容器制造单位变更单位名称、住所或者法定代表人的，应当自工商变更登记之日起20日内，向国务院核安全监管部门办理制造许可证变更手续。  
一类放射性物品运输容器制造单位变更制造的运输容器型号的，应当按照原申请程序向国务院核安全监管部门重新申请领取制造许可证。

**Article 20** If a unit manufacturing Category I transport packages for radioactive articles changes its name, domicile or legal representative, it shall, within 20 days from the date of the change registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce, go through the formalities for modification of the manufacturing license with the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council.  
If a unit manufacturing Category I transport packages for radioactive articles changes the type of transport package to be manufactured, it shall re-apply to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for a manufacturing license in accordance with the original application procedure.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　制造许可证有效期为5年。  
制造许可证有效期届满，需要延续的，一类放射性物品运输容器制造单位应当于制造许可证有效期届满6个月前，向国务院核安全监管部门提出延续申请。  
国务院核安全监管部门应当在制造许可证有效期届满前作出是否准予延续的决定。

**Article 21** The period of validity of a manufacturing license is five years.  
If a manufacturing license needs to be renewed upon expiry, the unit manufacturing Category I transport packages for radioactive articles shall submit an application for renewal to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council six months before the expiry of the manufacturing license.  
The nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall make a decision on whether to approve the renewal before the expiry of the manufacturing license.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　从事二类放射性物品运输容器制造活动的单位，应当在首次制造活动开始30日前，将其具备与所从事的制造活动相适应的专业技术人员、生产条件、检测手段，以及具有健全的管理制度和完善的质量保证体系的证明材料，报国务院核安全监管部门备案。

**Article 22** A unit engaged in the manufacture of Category II transport packages for radioactive articles shall, 30 days before the commencement of the first manufacture activity, submit to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for recordation the evidentiary materials proving that it has professional and technical personnel, production conditions and testing means suitable for the manufacture activity it undertakes, and has a sound management system and a complete quality assurance system.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　一类、二类放射性物品运输容器制造单位，应当按照国务院核安全监管部门制定的编码规则，对其制造的一类、二类放射性物品运输容器统一编码，并于每年1月31日前将上一年度的运输容器编码清单报国务院核安全监管部门备案。

**Article 23** A unit manufacturing Category I or Category II transport packages for radioactive articles shall, in accordance with the coding rules formulated by the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council, uniformly code the Category I or Category II transport packages for radioactive articles it manufactures, and shall, before January 31 of each year, submit the list of codes of transport packages for the preceding year to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　从事三类放射性物品运输容器制造活动的单位，应当于每年1月31日前将上一年度制造的运输容器的型号和数量报国务院核安全监管部门备案。

**Article 24** A unit engaged in the manufacture of Category III transport packages for radioactive articles shall, before January 31 of each year, submit to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for recordation the types and quantities of transport packages manufactured in the preceding year.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　放射性物品运输容器使用单位应当对其使用的放射性物品运输容器定期进行保养和维护，并建立保养和维护档案；放射性物品运输容器达到设计使用年限，或者发现放射性物品运输容器存在安全隐患的，应当停止使用，进行处理。  
一类放射性物品运输容器使用单位还应当对其使用的一类放射性物品运输容器每两年进行一次安全性能评价，并将评价结果报国务院核安全监管部门备案。

**Article 25** The unit using transport packages for radioactive articles shall regularly maintain and service the transport packages for radioactive articles it uses, and shall establish archives of maintenance and service; if a transport package for radioactive articles reaches its design service life, or if a potential safety hazard is found in the transport package for radioactive articles, it shall cease use and handle it.  
The unit using Category I transport packages for radioactive articles shall also conduct a safety performance evaluation on the Category I transport packages for radioactive articles it uses every two years, and shall submit the evaluation results to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　使用境外单位制造的一类放射性物品运输容器的，应当在首次使用前报国务院核安全监管部门审查批准。  
申请使用境外单位制造的一类放射性物品运输容器的单位，应当向国务院核安全监管部门提出书面申请，并提交下列材料：  
(一)设计单位所在国核安全监管部门颁发的设计批准文件的复印件；  
(二)设计安全评价报告书；  
(三)制造单位相关业绩的证明材料；  
(四)质量合格证明；  
(五)符合中华人民共和国法律、行政法规规定，以及国家放射性物品运输安全标准或者经国务院核安全监管部门认可的标准的说明材料。  
国务院核安全监管部门应当自受理申请之日起45个工作日内完成审查，对符合国家放射性物品运输安全标准的，颁发使用批准书；对不符合国家放射性物品运输安全标准的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。

**Article 26** If a Category I transport package for radioactive articles manufactured by an overseas unit is to be used, the matter shall be submitted to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for examination and approval before the first use.  
A unit applying to use a Category I transport package for radioactive articles manufactured by an overseas unit shall submit a written application to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council, and shall provide the following materials:  
(1) a copy of the design approval document issued by the nuclear safety regulatory department of the country where the design unit is located;  
(2) the design safety evaluation report;  
(3) evidentiary materials on the relevant performance of the manufacturing unit;  
(4) a certificate of quality conformity;  
(5) explanatory materials demonstrating compliance with the provisions of the laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China and the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles or the standards recognized by the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council.  
The nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall complete the examination within 45 working days from the date of accepting the application. If the requirements comply with the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles, it shall issue a use approval certificate; if they do not comply with the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles, it shall notify the applicant unit in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　使用境外单位制造的二类放射性物品运输容器的，应当在首次使用前将运输容器质量合格证明和符合中华人民共和国法律、行政法规规定，以及国家放射性物品运输安全标准或者经国务院核安全监管部门认可的标准的说明材料，报国务院核安全监管部门备案。

**Article 27** If a Category II transport package for radioactive articles manufactured by an overseas unit is to be used, the certificate of quality conformity of the transport package and the explanatory materials demonstrating compliance with the provisions of the laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China and the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles or the standards recognized by the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall be submitted to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for recordation before the first use.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院核安全监管部门办理使用境外单位制造的一类、二类放射性物品运输容器审查批准和备案手续，应当同时为运输容器确定编码。

**Article 28** When handling the examination, approval and recordation procedures for the use of Category I or Category II transport packages for radioactive articles manufactured by an overseas unit, the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall simultaneously determine the codes for the transport packages.

### 第四章　放射性物品的运输

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　托运放射性物品的，托运人应当持有生产、销售、使用或者处置放射性物品的有效证明，使用与所托运的放射性物品类别相适应的运输容器进行包装，配备必要的辐射监测设备、防护用品和防盗、防破坏设备，并编制运输说明书、核与辐射事故应急响应指南、装卸作业方法、安全防护指南。  
运输说明书应当包括放射性物品的品名、数量、物理化学形态、危害风险等内容。

**Article 29** When consigning radioactive articles for transport, the consignor shall hold a valid certificate for the production, sale, use or disposal of radioactive articles, use a transport package suitable for the category of radioactive articles being consigned for packaging, be equipped with necessary radiation monitoring equipment, protective articles and anti-theft and anti-sabotage equipment, and prepare a transport description, a nuclear and radiation accident emergency response guide, a loading and unloading operation method, and a safety protection guide.  
The transport description shall include the name, quantity, physical and chemical form, hazard risk and other contents of the radioactive articles.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　托运一类放射性物品的，托运人应当委托有资质的辐射监测机构对其表面污染和辐射水平实施监测，辐射监测机构应当出具辐射监测报告。  
托运二类、三类放射性物品的，托运人应当对其表面污染和辐射水平实施监测，并编制辐射监测报告。  
监测结果不符合国家放射性物品运输安全标准的，不得托运。

**Article 30** When consigning Category I radioactive articles for transport, the consignor shall entrust a qualified radiation monitoring institution to monitor the surface contamination and radiation level thereof, and the radiation monitoring institution shall issue a radiation monitoring report.  
When consigning Category II or Category III radioactive articles for transport, the consignor shall monitor the surface contamination and radiation level thereof and prepare a radiation monitoring report.  
If the monitoring results do not comply with the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles, the articles shall not be consigned for transport.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　承运放射性物品应当取得国家规定的运输资质。承运人的资质管理，依照有关法律、行政法规和国务院交通运输、铁路、民航、邮政主管部门的规定执行。

**Article 31** A carrier of radioactive articles shall obtain the transport qualification prescribed by the State. The qualification management of carriers shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations and the provisions of the competent departments of transport, railway, civil aviation and postal services under the State Council.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　托运人和承运人应当对直接从事放射性物品运输的工作人员进行运输安全和应急响应知识的培训，并进行考核；考核不合格的，不得从事相关工作。  
托运人和承运人应当按照国家放射性物品运输安全标准和国家有关规定，在放射性物品运输容器和运输工具上设置警示标志。  
国家利用卫星定位系统对一类、二类放射性物品运输工具的运输过程实行在线监控。具体办法由国务院核安全监管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 32** The consignor and the carrier shall provide training on transport safety and emergency response knowledge to the personnel directly engaged in the transport of radioactive articles, and shall conduct assessment; those who fail the assessment shall not engage in the relevant work.  
The consignor and the carrier shall, in accordance with the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles and the relevant provisions of the State, set up warning signs on the transport packages for radioactive articles and the means of transport.  
The State shall implement online monitoring of the transport process of means of transport for Category I and Category II radioactive articles by using a satellite positioning system. The specific measures shall be formulated by the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　托运人和承运人应当按照国家职业病防治的有关规定，对直接从事放射性物品运输的工作人员进行个人剂量监测，建立个人剂量档案和职业健康监护档案。

**Article 33** The consignor and the carrier shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State on prevention and control of occupational diseases, conduct individual dose monitoring on the personnel directly engaged in the transport of radioactive articles, and establish individual dose archives and occupational health surveillance archives.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　托运人应当向承运人提交运输说明书、辐射监测报告、核与辐射事故应急响应指南、装卸作业方法、安全防护指南，承运人应当查验、收存。托运人提交文件不齐全的，承运人不得承运。

**Article 34** The consignor shall submit to the carrier the transport description, radiation monitoring report, nuclear and radiation accident emergency response guide, loading and unloading operation method and safety protection guide. The carrier shall check and keep such documents. If the documents submitted by the consignor are incomplete, the carrier shall not carry the articles.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　托运一类放射性物品的，托运人应当编制放射性物品运输的核与辐射安全分析报告书，报国务院核安全监管部门审查批准。  
放射性物品运输的核与辐射安全分析报告书应当包括放射性物品的品名、数量、运输容器型号、运输方式、辐射防护措施、应急措施等内容。  
国务院核安全监管部门应当自受理申请之日起45个工作日内完成审查，对符合国家放射性物品运输安全标准的，颁发核与辐射安全分析报告批准书；对不符合国家放射性物品运输安全标准的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。

**Article 35** When consigning Category I radioactive articles for transport, the consignor shall prepare a nuclear and radiation safety analysis report for the transport of radioactive articles, and submit it to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for examination and approval.  
The nuclear and radiation safety analysis report for the transport of radioactive articles shall include the name, quantity, type of transport package, mode of transport, radiation protection measures, emergency measures and other contents of the radioactive articles.  
The nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall complete the examination within 45 working days from the date of accepting the application. If the report complies with the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles, it shall issue an approval certificate for the nuclear and radiation safety analysis report; if it does not comply with the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles, it shall notify the applicant unit in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　放射性物品运输的核与辐射安全分析报告批准书应当载明下列主要内容：  
(一)托运人的名称、地址、法定代表人；  
(二)运输放射性物品的品名、数量；  
(三)运输放射性物品的运输容器型号和运输方式；  
(四)批准日期和有效期限。

**Article 36** An approval certificate for a nuclear and radiation safety analysis report for the transport of radioactive articles shall specify the following main contents:  
(1) the name, address and legal representative of the consignor;  
(2) the name and quantity of the radioactive articles to be transported;  
(3) the type of transport package and mode of transport for the radioactive articles;  
(4) the date of approval and the period of validity.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　一类放射性物品启运前，托运人应当将放射性物品运输的核与辐射安全分析报告批准书、辐射监测报告，报启运地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护主管部门备案。  
收到备案材料的环境保护主管部门应当及时将有关情况通报放射性物品运输的途经地和抵达地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护主管部门。

**Article 37** Before the dispatch of Category I radioactive articles, the consignor shall submit the approval certificate for the nuclear and radiation safety analysis report and the radiation monitoring report for the transport of radioactive articles to the environmental protection department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the dispatch takes place for recordation.  
The environmental protection department receiving the recordation materials shall promptly notify the environmental protection departments of the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government of the places through which the radioactive articles pass and the places of arrival of the relevant information.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　通过道路运输放射性物品的，应当经公安机关批准，按照指定的时间、路线、速度行驶，并悬挂警示标志，配备押运人员，使放射性物品处于押运人员的监管之下。  
通过道路运输核反应堆乏燃料的，托运人应当报国务院公安部门批准。通过道路运输其他放射性物品的，托运人应当报启运地县级以上人民政府公安机关批准。具体办法由国务院公安部门商国务院核安全监管部门制定。

**Article 38** When transporting radioactive articles by road, the transport shall be approved by the public security organ, shall be conducted at the designated time, on the designated route and at the designated speed, and warning signs shall be displayed and escorts shall be assigned to keep the radioactive articles under the supervision of the escorts.  
When transporting spent nuclear fuel from nuclear reactors by road, the consignor shall report to the public security department of the State Council for approval. When transporting other radioactive articles by road, the consignor shall report to the public security organ of the people's government at or above the county level of the place of dispatch for approval. The specific measures shall be formulated by the public security department of the State Council in consultation with the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　通过水路运输放射性物品的，按照水路危险货物运输的法律、行政法规和规章的有关规定执行。  
通过铁路、航空运输放射性物品的，按照国务院铁路、民航主管部门的有关规定执行。  
禁止邮寄一类、二类放射性物品。邮寄三类放射性物品的，按照国务院邮政管理部门的有关规定执行。

**Article 39** The transport of radioactive articles by water shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the laws, administrative regulations and rules on the transport of dangerous goods by water.  
The transport of radioactive articles by rail or air shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the competent departments of railway and civil aviation under the State Council.  
It is prohibited to mail Category I or Category II radioactive articles. The mailing of Category III radioactive articles shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the competent department of postal services under the State Council.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　生产、销售、使用或者处置放射性物品的单位，可以依照《中华人民共和国道路运输条例》的规定，向设区的市级人民政府道路运输管理机构申请非营业性道路危险货物运输资质，运输本单位的放射性物品，并承担本条例规定的托运人和承运人的义务。  
申请放射性物品非营业性道路危险货物运输资质的单位，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)持有生产、销售、使用或者处置放射性物品的有效证明；  
(二)有符合本条例规定要求的放射性物品运输容器；  
(三)有具备辐射防护与安全防护知识的专业技术人员和经考试合格的驾驶人员；  
(四)有符合放射性物品运输安全防护要求，并经检测合格的运输工具、设施和设备；  
(五)配备必要的防护用品和依法经定期检定合格的监测仪器；  
(六)有运输安全和辐射防护管理规章制度以及核与辐射事故应急措施。  
放射性物品非营业性道路危险货物运输资质的具体条件，由国务院交通运输主管部门会同国务院核安全监管部门制定。

**Article 40** A unit that produces, sells, uses or disposes of radioactive articles may, in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Road Transport of the People's Republic of China, apply to the road transport administration institution of the people's government of a city divided into districts for a non-operational road dangerous goods transport qualification, transport its own radioactive articles, and assume the obligations of the consignor and the carrier as specified in these Regulations.  
A unit applying for a non-operational road dangerous goods transport qualification for radioactive articles shall satisfy the following conditions:  
(1) holding a valid certificate for the production, sale, use or disposal of radioactive articles;  
(2) having transport packages for radioactive articles that comply with the requirements of these Regulations;  
(3) having professional and technical personnel with knowledge of radiation protection and safety protection and drivers who have passed the examination;  
(4) having means of transport, facilities and equipment that comply with the requirements of safety protection for the transport of radioactive articles and have passed inspection;  
(5) being equipped with necessary protective articles and monitoring instruments that have undergone regular verification according to law;  
(6) having management rules and regulations on transport safety and radiation protection as well as nuclear and radiation accident emergency measures.  
The specific conditions for the non-operational road dangerous goods transport qualification for radioactive articles shall be formulated by the competent department of transport under the State Council jointly with the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　一类放射性物品从境外运抵中华人民共和国境内，或者途经中华人民共和国境内运输的，托运人应当编制放射性物品运输的核与辐射安全分析报告书，报国务院核安全监管部门审查批准。审查批准程序依照[本条例第三十五条](#art-35)第三款的规定执行。  
二类、三类放射性物品从境外运抵中华人民共和国境内，或者途经中华人民共和国境内运输的，托运人应当编制放射性物品运输的辐射监测报告，报国务院核安全监管部门备案。  
托运人、承运人或者其代理人向海关办理有关手续，应当提交国务院核安全监管部门颁发的放射性物品运输的核与辐射安全分析报告批准书或者放射性物品运输的辐射监测报告备案证明。

**Article 41** If Category I radioactive articles are transported from abroad to the territory of the People's Republic of China, or are transported through the territory of the People's Republic of China, the consignor shall prepare a nuclear and radiation safety analysis report for the transport of radioactive articles and submit it to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for examination and approval. The examination and approval procedure shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 35 of these Regulations.  
If Category II or Category III radioactive articles are transported from abroad to the territory of the People's Republic of China, or are transported through the territory of the People's Republic of China, the consignor shall prepare a radiation monitoring report for the transport of radioactive articles and submit it to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for recordation.  
When the consignor, carrier or their agent goes through the relevant formalities with the customs, they shall submit the approval certificate for the nuclear and radiation safety analysis report for the transport of radioactive articles or the recordation certificate of the radiation monitoring report for the transport of radioactive articles issued by the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　县级以上人民政府组织编制的突发环境事件应急预案，应当包括放射性物品运输中可能发生的核与辐射事故应急响应的内容。

**Article 42** The emergency plans for sudden environmental incidents formulated by the people's governments at or above the county level shall include the content of emergency response to nuclear and radiation accidents that may occur during the transport of radioactive articles.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　放射性物品运输中发生核与辐射事故的，承运人、托运人应当按照核与辐射事故应急响应指南的要求，做好事故应急工作，并立即报告事故发生地的县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门。接到报告的环境保护主管部门应当立即派人赶赴现场，进行现场调查，采取有效措施控制事故影响，并及时向本级人民政府报告，通报同级公安、卫生、交通运输等有关主管部门。  
接到报告的县级以上人民政府及其有关主管部门应当按照应急预案做好应急工作，并按照国家突发事件分级报告的规定及时上报核与辐射事故信息。  
核反应堆乏燃料运输的核事故应急准备与响应，还应当遵守国家核应急的有关规定。

**Article 43** If a nuclear and radiation accident occurs during the transport of radioactive articles, the carrier and the consignor shall, in accordance with the requirements of the nuclear and radiation accident emergency response guide, properly handle the accident emergency work, and immediately report to the environmental protection department of the people's government at or above the county level of the place where the accident occurred. The environmental protection department receiving the report shall immediately dispatch personnel to the scene, conduct on-site investigation, take effective measures to control the impact of the accident, and promptly report to the people's government at the same level, and notify the relevant competent departments at the same level, such as public security, health and transport.  
The people's government at or above the county level receiving the report and its relevant competent departments shall properly carry out the emergency work in accordance with the emergency plan, and promptly report the information on the nuclear and radiation accident in accordance with the provisions of the State on graded reporting of emergencies.  
The emergency preparedness and response for nuclear accidents in the transport of spent nuclear fuel from nuclear reactors shall also comply with the relevant provisions of the State on nuclear emergency.

### 第五章　监督检查

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国务院核安全监管部门和其他依法履行放射性物品运输安全监督管理职责的部门，应当依据各自职责对放射性物品运输安全实施监督检查。  
国务院核安全监管部门应当将其已批准或者备案的一类、二类、三类放射性物品运输容器的设计、制造情况和放射性物品运输情况通报设计、制造单位所在地和运输途经地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护主管部门。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护主管部门应当加强对本行政区域放射性物品运输安全的监督检查和监督性监测。  
被检查单位应当予以配合，如实反映情况，提供必要的资料，不得拒绝和阻碍。

**Article 44** The nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council and other departments that perform the duties of supervision and administration over the safety of the transport of radioactive articles according to law shall, in accordance with their respective duties, conduct inspection and supervision over the safety of the transport of radioactive articles.  
The nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall notify the environmental protection departments of the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government where the design or manufacture units are located and through which the transport passes of the design and manufacture of Category I, Category II and Category III transport packages for radioactive articles that have been approved or recorded, and the transport of radioactive articles. The environmental protection departments of the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall strengthen the inspection, supervision and supervisory monitoring over the safety of the transport of radioactive articles within their respective administrative regions.  
The unit under inspection shall cooperate, truthfully reflect the situation, provide necessary materials, and shall not refuse or obstruct the inspection.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国务院核安全监管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护主管部门以及其他依法履行放射性物品运输安全监督管理职责的部门进行监督检查，监督检查人员不得少于2人，并应当出示有效的行政执法证件。  
国务院核安全监管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护主管部门以及其他依法履行放射性物品运输安全监督管理职责的部门的工作人员，对监督检查中知悉的商业秘密负有保密义务。

**Article 45** When the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council, the environmental protection departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and other departments that perform the duties of supervision and administration over the safety of the transport of radioactive articles according to law conduct supervision and inspection, there shall be no fewer than two inspectors, and they shall produce valid administrative law enforcement certificates.  
The staff members of the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council, the environmental protection departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and other departments that perform the duties of supervision and administration over the safety of the transport of radioactive articles according to law shall have the duty to keep confidential the trade secrets learned during the supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　监督检查中发现经批准的一类放射性物品运输容器设计确有重大设计安全缺陷的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令停止该型号运输容器的制造或者使用，撤销一类放射性物品运输容器设计批准书。

**Article 46** If, during supervision and inspection, it is found that the approved design of a Category I transport package for radioactive articles indeed has major design safety defects, the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall order the cessation of the manufacture or use of such type of transport package, and revoke the design approval certificate for the Category I transport package for radioactive articles.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　监督检查中发现放射性物品运输活动有不符合国家放射性物品运输安全标准情形的，或者一类放射性物品运输容器制造单位有不符合制造许可证规定条件情形的，应当责令限期整改；发现放射性物品运输活动可能对人体健康和环境造成核与辐射危害的，应当责令停止运输。

**Article 47** If, during supervision and inspection, it is found that the transport activity of radioactive articles does not comply with the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles, or that a unit manufacturing Category I transport packages for radioactive articles does not meet the conditions specified in the manufacturing license, it shall be ordered to rectify within a time limit; if it is found that the transport activity of radioactive articles may cause nuclear and radiation hazards to human health and the environment, the transport shall be ordered to cease.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国务院核安全监管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护主管部门以及其他依法履行放射性物品运输安全监督管理职责的部门，对放射性物品运输活动实施监测，不得收取监测费用。  
国务院核安全监管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护主管部门以及其他依法履行放射性物品运输安全监督管理职责的部门，应当加强对监督管理人员辐射防护与安全防护知识的培训。

**Article 48** When implementing monitoring of the transport activity of radioactive articles, the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council, the environmental protection departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and other departments that perform the duties of supervision and administration over the safety of the transport of radioactive articles according to law shall not charge monitoring fees.  
The nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council, the environmental protection departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and other departments that perform the duties of supervision and administration over the safety of the transport of radioactive articles according to law shall strengthen the training on knowledge of radiation protection and safety protection for supervision and administration personnel.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国务院核安全监管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护主管部门或者其他依法履行放射性物品运输安全监督管理职责的部门有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未依照本条例规定作出行政许可或者办理批准文件的；  
(二)发现违反本条例规定的行为不予查处，或者接到举报不依法处理的；  
(三)未依法履行放射性物品运输核与辐射事故应急职责的；  
(四)对放射性物品运输活动实施监测收取监测费用的；  
(五)其他不依法履行监督管理职责的行为。

**Article 49** If the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council, the environmental protection departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, or other departments that perform the duties of supervision and administration over the safety of the transport of radioactive articles according to law commit any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons commit a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to make an administrative license or handle an approval document in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) failing to investigate and deal with acts violating the provisions of these Regulations when discovered, or failing to deal with reports according to law;  
(3) failing to perform the duties of nuclear and radiation accident emergency for the transport of radioactive articles according to law;  
(4) charging monitoring fees for monitoring the transport activity of radioactive articles;  
(5) other acts of failing to perform supervision and administration duties according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　放射性物品运输容器设计、制造单位有下列行为之一的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令停止违法行为，处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得：  
(一)将未取得设计批准书的一类放射性物品运输容器设计用于制造的；  
(二)修改已批准的一类放射性物品运输容器设计中有关安全内容，未重新取得设计批准书即用于制造的。

**Article 50** If a unit designing or manufacturing transport packages for radioactive articles commits any of the following acts, the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal act, impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) using a design of a Category I transport package for radioactive articles that has not obtained a design approval certificate for manufacture;  
(2) modifying the safety-related content of the approved design of a Category I transport package for radioactive articles and using it for manufacture without re-obtaining a design approval certificate.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　放射性物品运输容器设计、制造单位有下列行为之一的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令停止违法行为，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得：  
(一)将不符合国家放射性物品运输安全标准的二类、三类放射性物品运输容器设计用于制造的；  
(二)将未备案的二类放射性物品运输容器设计用于制造的。

**Article 51** If a unit designing or manufacturing transport packages for radioactive articles commits any of the following acts, the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal act, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) using a design of a Category II or Category III transport package for radioactive articles that does not comply with the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles for manufacture;  
(2) using a design of a Category II transport package for radioactive articles that has not been recorded for manufacture.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　放射性物品运输容器设计单位有下列行为之一的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款：  
(一)未对二类、三类放射性物品运输容器的设计进行安全性能评价的；  
(二)未如实记录二类、三类放射性物品运输容器设计和安全性能评价过程的；  
(三)未编制三类放射性物品运输容器设计符合国家放射性物品运输安全标准的证明文件并存档备查的。

**Article 52** If a unit designing transport packages for radioactive articles commits any of the following acts, the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) failing to conduct safety performance evaluation on the design of Category II or Category III transport packages for radioactive articles;  
(2) failing to truthfully record the process of design and safety performance evaluation of Category II or Category III transport packages for radioactive articles;  
(3) failing to prepare a certificate document proving that the design of a Category III transport package for radioactive articles complies with the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles and keep it on file for future reference.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　放射性物品运输容器制造单位有下列行为之一的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令停止违法行为，处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得：  
(一)未取得制造许可证从事一类放射性物品运输容器制造活动的；  
(二)制造许可证有效期届满，未按照规定办理延续手续，继续从事一类放射性物品运输容器制造活动的；  
(三)超出制造许可证规定的范围从事一类放射性物品运输容器制造活动的；  
(四)变更制造的一类放射性物品运输容器型号，未按照规定重新领取制造许可证的；  
(五)将未经质量检验或者经检验不合格的一类放射性物品运输容器交付使用的。  
有前款第(三)项、第(四)项和第(五)项行为之一，情节严重的，吊销制造许可证。

**Article 53** If a unit manufacturing transport packages for radioactive articles commits any of the following acts, the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal act, impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) engaging in the manufacture of Category I transport packages for radioactive articles without obtaining a manufacturing license;  
(2) continuing to engage in the manufacture of Category I transport packages for radioactive articles after the expiry of the manufacturing license without going through the renewal procedures according to the provisions;  
(3) engaging in the manufacture of Category I transport packages for radioactive articles beyond the scope specified in the manufacturing license;  
(4) changing the type of Category I transport package for radioactive articles manufactured without re-obtaining a manufacturing license according to the provisions;  
(5) delivering for use a Category I transport package for radioactive articles that has not undergone quality inspection or has been found unqualified upon inspection.  
If any of the acts specified in items (3), (4) and (5) of the preceding paragraph is committed under serious circumstances, the manufacturing license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　一类放射性物品运输容器制造单位变更单位名称、住所或者法定代表人，未依法办理制造许可证变更手续的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处2万元的罚款。

**Article 54** If a unit manufacturing Category I transport packages for radioactive articles changes its name, domicile or legal representative and fails to go through the formalities for modification of the manufacturing license according to law, the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, a fine of 20,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　放射性物品运输容器制造单位有下列行为之一的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令停止违法行为，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得：  
(一)在二类放射性物品运输容器首次制造活动开始前，未按照规定将有关证明材料报国务院核安全监管部门备案的；  
(二)将未经质量检验或者经检验不合格的二类、三类放射性物品运输容器交付使用的。

**Article 55** If a unit manufacturing transport packages for radioactive articles commits any of the following acts, the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal act, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) failing to submit the relevant evidentiary materials to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for recordation in accordance with the provisions before the commencement of the first manufacture activity of a Category II transport package for radioactive articles;  
(2) delivering for use a Category II or Category III transport package for radioactive articles that has not undergone quality inspection or has been found unqualified upon inspection.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　放射性物品运输容器制造单位有下列行为之一的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款：  
(一)未按照规定对制造的一类、二类放射性物品运输容器统一编码的；  
(二)未按照规定将制造的一类、二类放射性物品运输容器编码清单报国务院核安全监管部门备案的；  
(三)未按照规定将制造的三类放射性物品运输容器的型号和数量报国务院核安全监管部门备案的。

**Article 56** If a unit manufacturing transport packages for radioactive articles commits any of the following acts, the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) failing to uniformly code the manufactured Category I or Category II transport packages for radioactive articles in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) failing to submit the list of codes of the manufactured Category I or Category II transport packages for radioactive articles to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for recordation in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) failing to submit the types and quantities of the manufactured Category III transport packages for radioactive articles to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for recordation in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　放射性物品运输容器使用单位未按照规定对使用的一类放射性物品运输容器进行安全性能评价，或者未将评价结果报国务院核安全监管部门备案的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 57** If a unit using transport packages for radioactive articles fails to conduct safety performance evaluation on the Category I transport packages for radioactive articles it uses in accordance with the provisions, or fails to submit the evaluation results to the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council for recordation, the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　未按照规定取得使用批准书使用境外单位制造的一类放射性物品运输容器的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令停止违法行为，处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款。  
未按照规定办理备案手续使用境外单位制造的二类放射性物品运输容器的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令停止违法行为，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 58** If a Category I transport package for radioactive articles manufactured by an overseas unit is used without obtaining a use approval certificate in accordance with the provisions, the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal act and impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan.  
If a Category II transport package for radioactive articles manufactured by an overseas unit is used without going through the recordation procedures in accordance with the provisions, the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal act and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　托运人未按照规定编制放射性物品运输说明书、核与辐射事故应急响应指南、装卸作业方法、安全防护指南的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。  
托运人未按照规定将放射性物品运输的核与辐射安全分析报告批准书、辐射监测报告备案的，由启运地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 59** If the consignor fails to prepare a transport description, a nuclear and radiation accident emergency response guide, a loading and unloading operation method, and a safety protection guide for the transport of radioactive articles in accordance with the provisions, the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
If the consignor fails to have the approval certificate for the nuclear and radiation safety analysis report for the transport of radioactive articles and the radiation monitoring report recorded in accordance with the provisions, the environmental protection department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government of the place of dispatch shall order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　托运人或者承运人在放射性物品运输活动中，有违反有关法律、行政法规关于危险货物运输管理规定行为的，由交通运输、铁路、民航等有关主管部门依法予以处罚。  
违反有关法律、行政法规规定邮寄放射性物品的，由公安机关和邮政管理部门依法予以处罚。在邮寄进境物品中发现放射性物品的，由海关依照有关法律、行政法规的规定处理。

**Article 60** If the consignor or the carrier, in the transport activity of radioactive articles, commits any act violating the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations on the transport management of dangerous goods, the competent departments of transport, railway, civil aviation and other relevant departments shall impose penalties according to law.  
If radioactive articles are mailed in violation of the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations, the public security organs and the postal administration departments shall impose penalties according to law. If radioactive articles are found in incoming mailed items, the customs shall handle them in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　托运人未取得放射性物品运输的核与辐射安全分析报告批准书托运一类放射性物品的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令停止违法行为，处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款。

**Article 61** If the consignor consigns Category I radioactive articles for transport without obtaining an approval certificate for the nuclear and radiation safety analysis report for the transport of radioactive articles, the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal act and impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　通过道路运输放射性物品，有下列行为之一的，由公安机关责令限期改正，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未经公安机关批准通过道路运输放射性物品的；  
(二)运输车辆未按照指定的时间、路线、速度行驶或者未悬挂警示标志的；  
(三)未配备押运人员或者放射性物品脱离押运人员监管的。

**Article 62** If any of the following acts occurs in the transport of radioactive articles by road, the public security organ shall order correction within a time limit and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) transporting radioactive articles by road without approval from the public security organ;  
(2) the transport vehicle does not travel at the designated time, on the designated route or at the designated speed, or fails to display warning signs;  
(3) failing to assign escorts or the radioactive articles are out of the supervision of the escorts.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　托运人有下列行为之一的，由启运地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护主管部门责令停止违法行为，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款：  
(一)未按照规定对托运的放射性物品表面污染和辐射水平实施监测的；  
(二)将经监测不符合国家放射性物品运输安全标准的放射性物品交付托运的；  
(三)出具虚假辐射监测报告的。

**Article 63** If the consignor commits any of the following acts, the environmental protection department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government of the place of dispatch shall order cessation of the illegal act and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan:  
(1) failing to monitor the surface contamination and radiation level of the radioactive articles being consigned in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) delivering for consignment radioactive articles that, upon monitoring, do not comply with the national safety standards for the transport of radioactive articles;  
(3) issuing a false radiation monitoring report.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　未取得放射性物品运输的核与辐射安全分析报告批准书或者放射性物品运输的辐射监测报告备案证明，将境外的放射性物品运抵中华人民共和国境内，或者途经中华人民共和国境内运输的，由海关责令托运人退运该放射性物品，并依照海关法律、行政法规给予处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。托运人不明的，由承运人承担退运该放射性物品的责任，或者承担该放射性物品的处置费用。

**Article 64** If radioactive articles from abroad are transported into the territory of the People's Republic of China, or are transported through the territory of the People's Republic of China, without obtaining an approval certificate for the nuclear and radiation safety analysis report for the transport of radioactive articles or a recordation certificate of the radiation monitoring report for the transport of radioactive articles, the customs shall order the consignor to return the radioactive articles and impose penalties in accordance with the customs laws and administrative regulations; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law. If the consignor is unknown, the carrier shall bear the responsibility of returning the radioactive articles or bear the disposal costs of the radioactive articles.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本条例规定，在放射性物品运输中造成核与辐射事故的，由县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门处以罚款，罚款数额按照核与辐射事故造成的直接损失的20%计算；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
托运人、承运人未按照核与辐射事故应急响应指南的要求，做好事故应急工作并报告事故的，由县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款。  
因核与辐射事故造成他人损害的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 65** If a nuclear and radiation accident is caused during the transport of radioactive articles in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the environmental protection department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall impose a fine, the amount of which shall be calculated at 20% of the direct losses caused by the nuclear and radiation accident; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If the consignor or the carrier fails to properly carry out the accident emergency work and report the accident in accordance with the requirements of the nuclear and radiation accident emergency response guide, the environmental protection department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.  
If damage is caused to others by a nuclear and radiation accident, civil liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　拒绝、阻碍国务院核安全监管部门或者其他依法履行放射性物品运输安全监督管理职责的部门进行监督检查，或者在接受监督检查时弄虚作假的，由监督检查部门责令改正，处1万元以上2万元以下的罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 66** If any entity or individual refuses or obstructs the supervision and inspection conducted by the nuclear safety regulatory department of the State Council or other departments that perform the duties of supervision and administration over the safety of the transport of radioactive articles according to law, or practices fraud when undergoing supervision and inspection, the supervision and inspection department shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　军用放射性物品运输安全的监督管理，依照[《中华人民共和国放射性污染防治法》第六十条](../../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国放射性污染防治法.md#art-60)的规定执行。

**Article 67** The supervision and administration of the transport safety of military radioactive articles shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of Article 60 of the Law on Prevention and Control of Radioactive Pollution of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　本条例自2010年1月1日起施行。

**Article 68** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2010.
