# 中华人民共和国渔港水域交通安全管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：1989-08-01

> (1989年7月3日中华人民共和国国务院令第38号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2017年10月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订) 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国海上交通安全法》第四十八条的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于在中华人民共和国沿海以渔业为主的渔港和渔港水域(以下简称“渔港”和“渔港水域”)航行、停泊、作业的船舶、设施和人员以及船舶、设施的所有者、经营者。 第三条　中华人民共和国渔政渔港监督管理机关是对渔港水域交通安全实施监督管理的主管机关，并负责沿海水域渔业船舶之间交通事故的调查处理。 第四条　本条例下列用语的含义是： 渔港是指主要为渔业生产服务和供渔业船舶停泊、避风、装卸渔获物和补充渔需物资的人工港口或者自然港湾。 渔港水域是指渔港的港池、锚地、避风湾和航道。 渔业船舶是指从事渔业生产的船舶以及属于水产系统为渔业生产服务的船舶，包括捕捞船、养殖船、水产运销船、冷藏加工船、油船、供应船、渔业指导船、科研调查船、教学实习船、渔港工程船、拖轮、交通船、驳船、渔政船和渔监船。 第五条　对渔港认定有不同意见的，依照港口隶属关系由县级以上人民政府确定。 第六条　船舶进出渔港必须遵守渔港管理章程以及国际海上避碰规则，并依照规定向渔政渔港监督管理机关报告，接受安全检查。 渔港内的船舶必须服从渔政渔港监督管理机关对水域交通安全秩序的管理。 第七条　船舶在渔港内停泊、避风和装卸物资，不得损坏渔港的设施装备；造成损坏的应当向渔政渔港监督管理机关报告，并承担赔偿责任。 第八条　船舶在渔港内装卸易燃、易爆、有毒等危险货物，必须遵守国家关于危险货物管理的规定，并事先向渔政渔港监督管理机关提出申请，经批准后在指定的安全地点装卸。 第九条　在渔港内新建、改建、扩建各种设施，或者进行其他水上、水下施工作业，除依照国家规定履行审批手续外，应当报请渔政渔港监督管理机关批准。渔政渔港监督管理机关批准后，应当事先发布航行通告。 第十条　在渔港内的航道、港池、锚地和停泊区，禁止从事有碍海上交通安全的捕捞、养殖等生产活动。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据[《中华人民共和国海上交通安全法》第四十八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国海上交通安全法.md#art-48)的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of Article 48 of the Maritime Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于在中华人民共和国沿海以渔业为主的渔港和渔港水域(以下简称“渔港”和“渔港水域”)航行、停泊、作业的船舶、设施和人员以及船舶、设施的所有者、经营者。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the vessels, installations and personnel, as well as the owners and operators of vessels and installations, navigating, mooring and operating in the fishing ports and fishing port waters (hereinafter referred to as "fishing ports" and "fishing port waters") along the coast of the People's Republic of China which are mainly used for fisheries.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国渔政渔港监督管理机关是对渔港水域交通安全实施监督管理的主管机关，并负责沿海水域渔业船舶之间交通事故的调查处理。

**Article 3** The Fishery Administration and Fishing Port Supervisory Authority of the People's Republic of China shall be the competent authority for the supervision and administration of traffic safety in fishing port waters, and shall be responsible for the investigation and handling of traffic accidents between fishing vessels in coastal waters.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　本条例下列用语的含义是：  
渔港是指主要为渔业生产服务和供渔业船舶停泊、避风、装卸渔获物和补充渔需物资的人工港口或者自然港湾。  
渔港水域是指渔港的港池、锚地、避风湾和航道。  
渔业船舶是指从事渔业生产的船舶以及属于水产系统为渔业生产服务的船舶，包括捕捞船、养殖船、水产运销船、冷藏加工船、油船、供应船、渔业指导船、科研调查船、教学实习船、渔港工程船、拖轮、交通船、驳船、渔政船和渔监船。

**Article 4** For the purpose of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:  
"Fishing ports" means artificial harbors or natural harbors mainly servicing fishery production and providing for the mooring, sheltering from wind, loading and unloading of catches, and replenishment of fishery supplies for fishing vessels.  
"Fishing port waters" means the harbor basins, anchorages, sheltering bays and fairways within fishing ports.  
"Fishing vessels" means vessels engaged in fishery production and vessels belonging to the aquatic products system that serve fishery production, including fishing boats, aquaculture boats, aquatic product transport ships, refrigerated processing ships, oil tankers, supply ships, fishery guidance ships, scientific research survey ships, teaching practice ships, fishing port engineering ships, tugboats, traffic boats, barges, fishery administration ships and fishery supervision ships.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对渔港认定有不同意见的，依照港口隶属关系由县级以上人民政府确定。

**Article 5** In case of disagreement on the determination of fishing ports, the determination shall be made by the people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the affiliation of the port.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　船舶进出渔港必须遵守渔港管理章程以及国际海上避碰规则，并依照规定向渔政渔港监督管理机关报告，接受安全检查。  
渔港内的船舶必须服从渔政渔港监督管理机关对水域交通安全秩序的管理。

**Article 6** Vessels entering or leaving a fishing port must comply with the fishing port management regulations and the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea, and shall report to the Fishery Administration and Fishing Port Supervisory Authority in accordance with the provisions and undergo safety inspections.  
Vessels within a fishing port must obey the management of the Fishery Administration and Fishing Port Supervisory Authority with respect to the order of water traffic safety.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　船舶在渔港内停泊、避风和装卸物资，不得损坏渔港的设施装备；造成损坏的应当向渔政渔港监督管理机关报告，并承担赔偿责任。

**Article 7** When mooring, sheltering from wind and loading or unloading materials in a fishing port, a vessel shall not damage the facilities and equipment of the fishing port; in case such damage is caused, it shall report to the Fishery Administration and Fishing Port Supervisory Authority and bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　船舶在渔港内装卸易燃、易爆、有毒等危险货物，必须遵守国家关于危险货物管理的规定，并事先向渔政渔港监督管理机关提出申请，经批准后在指定的安全地点装卸。

**Article 8** Vessels loading or unloading flammable, explosive, toxic or other dangerous goods in a fishing port must comply with the State regulations on the management of dangerous goods, and shall file an application with the Fishery Administration and Fishing Port Supervisory Authority in advance, and only after approval shall they load or unload at a designated safe location.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　在渔港内新建、改建、扩建各种设施，或者进行其他水上、水下施工作业，除依照国家规定履行审批手续外，应当报请渔政渔港监督管理机关批准。渔政渔港监督管理机关批准后，应当事先发布航行通告。

**Article 9** Where any facility is newly built, rebuilt or expanded in a fishing port, or any other water or underwater construction operation is carried out, in addition to going through the formalities of examination and approval in accordance with the State provisions, the matter shall be submitted to the Fishery Administration and Fishing Port Supervisory Authority for approval. After approval by the Fishery Administration and Fishing Port Supervisory Authority, a navigation notice shall be issued in advance.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在渔港内的航道、港池、锚地和停泊区，禁止从事有碍海上交通安全的捕捞、养殖等生产活动。

**Article 10** Fishing, aquaculture or other production activities that hinder maritime traffic safety shall be prohibited in the fairways, harbor basins, anchorages and berthing areas within a fishing port.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家公务船舶在执行公务时进出渔港，经通报渔政渔港监督管理机关，可免于检查。渔政渔港监督管理机关应当对执行海上巡视任务的国家公务船舶的靠岸、停泊和补给提供方便。

**Article 11** National public service vessels, when entering or leaving a fishing port in the execution of official duties, may be exempted from inspection upon notification to the administrative organ for fishery administration and fishing port superintendence. The administrative organ for fishery administration and fishing port superintendence shall provide convenience for the berthing, mooring and replenishment of national public service vessels performing maritime patrol tasks.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　渔业船舶在向渔政渔港监督管理机关申请船舶登记，并取得渔业船舶国籍证书或者渔业船舶登记证书后，方可悬挂中华人民共和国国旗航行。

**Article 12** A fishing vessel may sail flying the national flag of the People's Republic of China only after it has applied for vessel registration with the administrative organ for fishery administration and fishing port superintendence and has obtained either a certificate of nationality for the fishing vessel or a certificate of registration for the fishing vessel.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　渔业船舶必须经船舶检验部门检验合格，取得船舶技术证书，方可从事渔业生产。

**Article 13** A fishing vessel must pass inspection by the vessel inspection authority and obtain a vessel technical certificate before it may engage in fishery production.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　渔业船舶的船长、轮机长、驾驶员、轮机员、电机员、无线电报务员、话务员，必须经渔政渔港监督管理机关考核合格，取得职务证书，其他人员应当经过相应的专业训练。

**Article 14** The captain, chief engineer, navigator, engineer, electrician, radio operator, and telephone operator of a fishing vessel must pass assessment by the administrative organ for fishery administration and fishing port superintendence and obtain a certificate of competency; other personnel shall undergo appropriate professional training.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　地方各级人民政府应当加强本行政区域内渔业船舶船员的技术培训工作。国营、集体所有的渔业船舶，其船员的技术培训由渔业船舶所属单位负责；个人所有的渔业船舶，其船员的技术培训由当地人民政府渔业行政主管部门负责。

**Article 15** Local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen technical training for crew members of fishing vessels within their respective administrative regions. For fishing vessels owned by the state or collectively, the technical training of their crew members shall be the responsibility of the unit to which the fishing vessel belongs; for fishing vessels owned by individuals, the technical training of their crew members shall be the responsibility of the competent administrative department for fisheries of the local people's government.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　渔业船舶之间发生交通事故，应当向就近的渔政渔港监督管理机关报告，并在进入第一个港口48小时之内向渔政渔港监督管理机关递交事故报告书和有关材料，接受调查处理。

**Article 16** In the event of a traffic accident between fishing vessels, a report shall be made to the nearest administrative organ for fishery administration and fishing port superintendence, and within 48 hours after entering the first port, a written report of the accident and relevant materials shall be submitted to the said organ for investigation and handling.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　渔政渔港监督管理机关对渔港水域内的交通事故和其他沿海水域渔业船舶之间的交通事故，应当及时查明原因，判明责任，作出处理决定。

**Article 17** The administrative organ for fishery administration and fishing port superintendence shall promptly ascertain the causes, determine liability, and make a handling decision with respect to traffic accidents occurring within the waters of fishing ports and traffic accidents between fishing vessels in other coastal waters.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　渔港内的船舶、设施有下列情形之一的，渔政渔港监督管理机关有权禁止其离港，或者令其停航、改航、停止作业：  
(一)违反中华人民共和国法律、法规或者规章的；  
(二)处于不适航或者不适拖状态的；  
(三)发生交通事故，手续未清的；  
(四)未向渔政渔港监督管理机关或者有关部门交付应当承担的费用，也未提供担保的；  
(五)渔政渔港监督管理机关认为有其他妨害或者可能妨害海上交通安全的。

**Article 18** Where any vessel or installation in a fishing port falls under any of the following circumstances, the administrative organ for fishery administration and fishing port superintendence shall have the power to prohibit it from leaving the port, or to order it to suspend sailing, change its course, or cease operations:  
(1) violating the laws, regulations, or rules of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) being in an unseaworthy or untowable condition;  
(3) having been involved in a traffic accident with unsettled formalities;  
(4) failing to pay the fees that shall be borne to the administrative organ for fishery administration and fishing port superintendence or the relevant departments, or failing to provide a guarantee;  
(5) being deemed by the administrative organ for fishery administration and fishing port superintendence as having other hazards or potential hazards to maritime traffic safety.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　渔港内的船舶、设施发生事故，对海上交通安全造成或者可能造成危害，渔政渔港监督管理机关有权对其采取强制性处置措施。

**Article 19** Where any vessel or installation in a fishing port has an accident causing or likely to cause harm to maritime traffic safety, the administrative organ for fishery administration and fishing port superintendence shall have the power to take compulsory disposal measures against it.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　船舶进出渔港依照规定应当向渔政渔港监督管理机关报告而未报告的，或者在渔港内不服从渔政渔港监督管理机关对水域交通安全秩序管理的，由渔政渔港监督管理机关责令改正，可以并处警告、罚款；情节严重的，扣留或者吊销船长职务证书(扣留职务证书时间最长不超过6个月，下同)。

**Article 20** Where a vessel, when entering or leaving a fishing port, fails to report to the administrative organ for fishery administration and fishing port superintendence as required, or where it fails to comply with the administration of the said organ over the order of water traffic safety within the fishing port, the administrative organ for fishery administration and fishing port superintendence shall order it to make corrections, and may concurrently impose a warning or a fine; if the circumstances are serious, the certificate of competency of the captain shall be detained or revoked (the period of detention of the certificate of competency shall not exceed six months at the maximum, the same below).

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由渔政渔港监督管理机关责令停止违法行为，可以并处警告、罚款；造成损失的，应当承担赔偿责任；对直接责任人员由其所在单位或者上级主管机关给予行政处分：  
(一)未经渔政渔港监督管理机关批准或者未按照批准文件的规定，在渔港内装卸易燃、易爆、有毒等危险货物的；  
(二)未经渔政渔港监督管理机关批准，在渔港内新建、改建、扩建各种设施或者进行其他水上、水下施工作业的；  
(三)在渔港内的航道、港池、锚地和停泊区从事有碍海上交通安全的捕捞、养殖等生产活动的。

**Article 21** Anyone who violates these Regulations and commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the fishery administration and fishing port superintendency authority to cease the illegal act, and may be concurrently given a warning or imposed a fine; if losses are caused, the person shall bear compensation liability; the directly responsible person shall be given an administrative sanction by the entity to which he belongs or by the competent authority at a higher level:  
(1) loading or unloading dangerous goods such as inflammable, explosive or toxic substances in a fishing port without the approval of the fishery administration and fishing port superintendency authority or not in accordance with the provisions of the approval document;  
(2) constructing, reconstructing or expanding various facilities or carrying out other water or underwater construction operations in a fishing port without the approval of the fishery administration and fishing port superintendency authority;  
(3) engaging in fishing, aquaculture or other production activities that impede maritime traffic safety in the navigation channels, harbor basins, anchorages or mooring areas within a fishing port.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　违反本条例规定，未持有船舶证书或者未配齐船员的，由渔政渔港监督管理机关责令改正，可以并处罚款。

**Article 22** Anyone who violates these Regulations by failing to hold a ship certificate or failing to have a full crew complement shall be ordered by the fishery administration and fishing port superintendency authority to make corrections, and may be concurrently imposed a fine.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　违反本条例规定，不执行渔政渔港监督管理机关作出的离港、停航、改航、停止作业的决定，或者在执行中违反上述决定的，由渔政渔港监督管理机关责令改正，可以并处警告、罚款；情节严重的，扣留或者吊销船长职务证书。

**Article 23** Anyone who violates these Regulations by failing to comply with the decisions on departure, suspension, diversion or cessation of operations made by the fishery administration and fishing port superintendency authority, or by violating the aforesaid decisions during implementation, shall be ordered by the fishery administration and fishing port superintendency authority to make corrections, and may be concurrently given a warning or imposed a fine; if the circumstances are serious, the certificate of competency of the master shall be detained or revoked.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　当事人对渔政渔港监督管理机关作出的行政处罚决定不服的，可以在接到处罚通知之日起15日内向人民法院起诉；期满不起诉又不履行的，由渔政渔港监督管理机关申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 24** A party that is dissatisfied with the administrative penalty decision made by the fishery administration and fishing port superintendency authority may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the penalty notice, bring a prosecution in a People's Court; if no prosecution is filed within the time limit and the decision is not complied with, the fishery administration and fishing port superintendency authority shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　因渔港水域内发生的交通事故或者其他沿海水域发生的渔业船舶之间的交通事故引起的民事纠纷，可以由渔政渔港监督管理机关调解处理；调解不成或者不愿意调解的，当事人可以向人民法院起诉。

**Article 25** Civil disputes arising from traffic accidents occurring in the waters of a fishing port or from traffic accidents between fishery vessels occurring in other coastal waters may be mediated and handled by the fishery administration and fishing port superintendency authority; if mediation fails or the parties are unwilling to mediate, the parties may bring a prosecution in a People's Court.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　拒绝、阻碍渔政渔港监督管理工作人员依法执行公务，应当给予治安管理处罚的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》有关规定处罚；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 26** Where anyone refuses or obstructs the fishery administration and fishing port superintendency staff from performing their duties according to law, and if the act warrants a public security penalty, the public security organ shall impose a penalty in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Penalties; if the act constitutes a crime, the judicial authority shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　渔政渔港监督管理工作人员，在渔港和渔港水域交通安全监督管理工作中，玩忽职守，滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，由其所在单位或者上级主管机关给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 27** Where the fishery administration and fishing port superintendency staff, in the work of traffic safety supervision and administration of fishing ports and fishing port waters, neglect their duties, abuse their powers, or engage in malpractices for personal gain, they shall be given an administrative sanction by the entity to which they belong or by the competent authority at a higher level; if the act constitutes a crime, the judicial authority shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本条例实施细则由农业农村部制定。

**Article 28** The detailed implementing rules for these Regulations shall be formulated by the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本条例自1989年8月1日起施行。

**Article 29** These Regulations shall come into force as of August 1, 1989.
