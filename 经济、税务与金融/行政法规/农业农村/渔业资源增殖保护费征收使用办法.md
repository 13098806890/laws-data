# 渔业资源增殖保护费征收使用办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：1989-01-01

> (1988年10月9日国务院批准　1988年10月31日农业部、财政部、国家物价局令第1号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国渔业法》的有关规定，制定本办法。 第二条　凡在中华人民共和国的内水、滩涂、领海以及中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域采捕天然生长和人工增殖水生动植物的单位和个人，必须依照本办法缴纳渔业资源增殖保护费(以下简称“渔业资源费”)。 第三条　渔业资源费的征收和使用，实行取之于渔、用之于渔的原则。 第四条　渔业资源费由县级以上人民政府渔业行政主管部门及其授权单位依照批准发放捕捞许可证的权限征收。由国务院渔业行政主管部门批准发放捕捞许可证的，渔业资源费由国务院渔业行政主管部门所属的海区渔政监督管理机构(以下称“海区渔政监督管理机构”)征收。 第五条　渔业资源费分为海洋渔业资源费和内陆水域渔业资源费。 海洋渔业资源费年征收金额，由沿海省级人民政府渔业行政主管部门或者海区渔政监督管理机构，在其批准发放捕捞许可证的渔船前3年采捕水产品的平均年总产值(不含专项采捕经济价值较高的渔业资源品种产值)1%至3%的幅度内确定。 内陆水域渔业资源费年征收金额由省级人民政府确定。 专项采捕经济价值较高的渔业资源品种，渔业资源费年征收金额，由省级人民政府渔业行政主管部门或者海区渔政监督管理机构，在其批准发放捕捞许可证的渔船前3年采捕该品种的平均年总产值3%至5%的幅度内确定。 经济价值较高的渔业资源品种名录，由国务院渔业行政主管部门确定。 第六条　渔业资源费的具体征收标准，由省级人民政府渔业行政主管部门或者海区渔政监督管理机构，在本办法第五条确定的渔业资源费年征收金额幅度内，依照下列原则制定： (一)从事外海捕捞、有利于渔业资源保护或者国家鼓励开发的作业的，其渔业资源费征收标准应当低于平均征收标准，也可以在一定时期内免征渔业资源费。 (二)从事应当淘汰、不利于渔业资源保护或者国家限制发展的作业的，或者持临时捕捞许可证进行采捕作业的，其渔业资源费征收标准应当高于平均征收标准，但最高不得超过平均征收标准金额的3倍。 (三)依法经批准采捕珍稀水生动植物的，依照专项采捕经济价值较高的渔业资源品种适用的征收标准，加倍征收渔业资源费，但最高不得超过上述征收标准金额的3倍。因从事科研活动的需要，依据有关规定经批准采捕珍稀水生动植物的除外。 渔业资源费的具体征收标准，省级人民政府渔业行政主管部门制定的，由省级人民政府物价部门核定；海区渔政监督管理机构制定的，报国务院渔业行政主管部门审查后，由国务院物价部门核定。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国渔业法》的有关规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Fisheries Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　凡在中华人民共和国的内水、滩涂、领海以及中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域采捕天然生长和人工增殖水生动植物的单位和个人，必须依照本办法缴纳渔业资源增殖保护费(以下简称“渔业资源费”)。

**Article 2** All units and individuals that engage in the catching of naturally growing and artificially propagated aquatic animals and plants in the inland waters, beaches, territorial seas and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China must pay the fishery resources增殖 protection fees (hereinafter referred to as "fishery resources fees") in accordance with these Measures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　渔业资源费的征收和使用，实行取之于渔、用之于渔的原则。

**Article 3** The collection and use of fishery resources fees shall be based on the principle of taking from fishing and using for fishing.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　渔业资源费由县级以上人民政府渔业行政主管部门及其授权单位依照批准发放捕捞许可证的权限征收。由国务院渔业行政主管部门批准发放捕捞许可证的，渔业资源费由国务院渔业行政主管部门所属的海区渔政监督管理机构(以下称“海区渔政监督管理机构”)征收。

**Article 4** Fishery resources fees shall be collected by the competent administrative departments for fisheries of the people's governments at or above the county level and their authorized units in accordance with the authority for approving and issuing fishing licenses. For fishing licenses approved and issued by the competent administrative department for fisheries of the State Council, the fishery resources fees shall be collected by the fisheries administration and supervision agencies in the sea areas under the said department (hereinafter referred to as "sea area fisheries administration and supervision agencies").

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　渔业资源费分为海洋渔业资源费和内陆水域渔业资源费。  
海洋渔业资源费年征收金额，由沿海省级人民政府渔业行政主管部门或者海区渔政监督管理机构，在其批准发放捕捞许可证的渔船前3年采捕水产品的平均年总产值(不含专项采捕经济价值较高的渔业资源品种产值)1%至3%的幅度内确定。  
内陆水域渔业资源费年征收金额由省级人民政府确定。  
专项采捕经济价值较高的渔业资源品种，渔业资源费年征收金额，由省级人民政府渔业行政主管部门或者海区渔政监督管理机构，在其批准发放捕捞许可证的渔船前3年采捕该品种的平均年总产值3%至5%的幅度内确定。  
经济价值较高的渔业资源品种名录，由国务院渔业行政主管部门确定。

**Article 5** Fishery resources fees shall be divided into marine fishery resources fees and inland waters fishery resources fees.  
The annual amount of marine fishery resources fees to be collected shall be determined by the competent administrative departments for fisheries of the coastal provincial people's governments or the sea area fisheries administration and supervision agencies within a range of 1% to 3% of the average annual total output value (excluding the output value of fishery resources species with high economic value subject to special catching) of aquatic products caught by the fishing vessels to which they approve and issue fishing licenses in the three years prior to the year of collection.  
The annual amount of inland waters fishery resources fees to be collected shall be determined by the provincial people's governments.  
For special catching of fishery resources species with high economic value, the annual amount of fishery resources fees to be collected shall be determined by the competent administrative departments for fisheries of the provincial people's governments or the sea area fisheries administration and supervision agencies within a range of 3% to 5% of the average annual total output value of such species caught by the fishing vessels to which they approve and issue fishing licenses in the three years prior to the year of collection.  
The list of fishery resources species with high economic value shall be determined by the competent administrative department for fisheries of the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　渔业资源费的具体征收标准，由省级人民政府渔业行政主管部门或者海区渔政监督管理机构，在[本办法第五条](#art-5)确定的渔业资源费年征收金额幅度内，依照下列原则制定：  
(一)从事外海捕捞、有利于渔业资源保护或者国家鼓励开发的作业的，其渔业资源费征收标准应当低于平均征收标准，也可以在一定时期内免征渔业资源费。  
(二)从事应当淘汰、不利于渔业资源保护或者国家限制发展的作业的，或者持临时捕捞许可证进行采捕作业的，其渔业资源费征收标准应当高于平均征收标准，但最高不得超过平均征收标准金额的3倍。  
(三)依法经批准采捕珍稀水生动植物的，依照专项采捕经济价值较高的渔业资源品种适用的征收标准，加倍征收渔业资源费，但最高不得超过上述征收标准金额的3倍。因从事科研活动的需要，依据有关规定经批准采捕珍稀水生动植物的除外。  
渔业资源费的具体征收标准，省级人民政府渔业行政主管部门制定的，由省级人民政府物价部门核定；海区渔政监督管理机构制定的，报国务院渔业行政主管部门审查后，由国务院物价部门核定。

**Article 6** The specific collection standards for fishery resources fees shall be formulated by the competent administrative departments for fisheries of the provincial people's governments or the sea area fisheries administration and supervision agencies within the range of the annual amount of fishery resources fees determined in Article 5 of these Measures, in accordance with the following principles:  
(1) For operations engaging in offshore fishing, which are conducive to the protection of fishery resources or are encouraged by the State for development, the collection standard for fishery resources fees shall be lower than the average collection standard, and may also be exempted from fishery resources fees for a certain period.  
(2) For operations engaging in activities that shall be eliminated, are not conducive to the protection of fishery resources or are restricted by the State for development, or for catching operations conducted with a temporary fishing license, the collection standard for fishery resources fees shall be higher than the average collection standard, but the maximum shall not exceed three times the amount of the average collection standard.  
(3) For catching rare aquatic animals and plants approved according to law, fishery resources fees shall be collected at double the collection standard applicable to special catching of fishery resources species with high economic value, but the maximum shall not exceed three times the amount of the aforesaid collection standard. This shall not apply to catching rare aquatic animals and plants approved in accordance with relevant provisions for the purpose of scientific research activities.  
Where the specific collection standards for fishery resources fees are formulated by the competent administrative department for fisheries of a provincial people's government, they shall be verified by the pricing department of the said provincial people's government; where formulated by a sea area fisheries administration and supervision agency, they shall be submitted to the competent administrative department for fisheries of the State Council for examination and then verified by the pricing department of the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上地方人民政府渔业行政主管部门或者海区渔政监督管理机构，根据[本办法第六条](#art-6)规定的渔业资源费征收标准，依照作业单位的船只、功率和网具数量，确定应当缴纳的渔业资源费金额。

**Article 7** The competent administrative departments for fisheries of the local people's governments at or above the county level or the sea area fisheries administration and supervision agencies shall, in accordance with the collection standards for fishery resources fees provided in Article 6 of these Measures, determine the amount of fishery resources fees to be paid by the operating units based on their vessels, power and number of nets.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　持有广东省和香港、澳门地区双重户籍的流动渔船，由广东省人民政府渔业行政主管部门征收渔业资源费。

**Article 8** For mobile fishing vessels holding dual household registrations in Guangdong Province and the regions of Hong Kong and Macao, the fishery resources fees shall be collected by the competent administrative department for fisheries of the people's government of Guangdong Province.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上地方人民政府渔业行政主管部门或者海区渔政监督管理机构，在批准发放捕捞许可证的同时征收渔业资源费，并在捕捞许可证上注明缴纳金额，加盖印章。  
征收渔业资源费时，必须出具收费的收据。

**Article 9** The competent administrative departments for fisheries of the local people's governments at or above the county level or the sea area fisheries administration and supervision agencies shall collect fishery resources fees at the same time as approving and issuing fishing licenses, and shall indicate the amount paid on the fishing license and affix a seal.  
When collecting fishery resources fees, a receipt for the fees collected must be issued.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　渔业资源费列入当年生产成本。

**Article 10** Fishery resources fees shall be included in the production costs of the current year.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　省级人民政府渔业行政主管部门征收的渔业资源费(含市、县上缴部分，下同)，实行按比例留成和上缴一部分统筹使用的办法。  
沿海省级人民政府渔业行政主管部门征收的海洋渔业资源费，90%由其留用；10%上缴海区渔政监督管理机构，用于大范围洄游性渔业资源增殖保护项目。省级人民政府渔业行政主管部门征收的内陆水域渔业资源费全部由其安排使用。  
市、县人民政府渔业行政主管部门征收的渔业资源费上缴省、自治区、直辖市的比例，由省级人民政府渔业行政主管部门商同级财政部门确定。

**Article 11** The fishery resources fees collected by the competent administrative departments for fisheries of the provincial people's governments (including the portion turned over by cities and counties, hereinafter the same) shall be subject to the method of retaining a proportion and turning over a portion for unified use.  
For marine fishery resources fees collected by the competent administrative departments for fisheries of the coastal provincial people's governments, 90% shall be retained by them for their own use and 10% shall be turned over to the sea area fisheries administration and supervision agencies for use in projects for the proliferation and protection of large-range migratory fishery resources. The inland waters fishery resources fees collected by the competent administrative departments for fisheries of the provincial people's governments shall all be arranged for use by them.  
The proportion of fishery resources fees collected by the competent administrative departments for fisheries of the people's governments of cities and counties to be turned over to the provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be determined by the competent administrative departments for fisheries of the provincial people's governments in consultation with the financial departments at the same level.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　渔业资源费用于渔业资源的增殖、保护，使用范围是：  
(一)购买增殖放流用的苗种和培育苗种所需的配套设施，修建近海和内陆水域人工鱼礁、鱼巢等增殖设施；  
(二)为保护特定的渔业资源品种，借给渔民用于转业或者转产的生产周转金(不得作为生活补助和流动资金)；  
(三)为增殖渔业资源提供科学研究经费补助；  
(四)为改善渔业资源增殖保护管理手段和监测渔业资源提供经费补助。  
渔业资源费用于渔业资源增殖与保护之间的比例，属于海区掌握的，由海区渔政监督管理机构确定；属于省、自治区、直辖市掌握的，由省级人民政府渔业行政主管部门商同级财政部门确定。

**Article 12** Fishery resources fees shall be used for the proliferation and protection of fishery resources, and the scope of use shall be:  
(1) Purchasing fry and fingerlings for proliferation and release, and supporting facilities needed for cultivating fry and fingerlings, and constructing proliferation facilities such as artificial reefs and fish nests in offshore and inland waters;  
(2) Providing production revolving funds (which shall not be used as living subsidies or working capital) to fishermen for their change of profession or conversion of production for the purpose of protecting specific fishery resources species;  
(3) Providing subsidies for scientific research funds for the proliferation of fishery resources;  
(4) Providing subsidies for funds for improving the management means of fishery resources proliferation and protection and for monitoring fishery resources.  
The proportion of fishery resources fees used for fishery resources proliferation and protection shall, for the portion under the control of sea areas, be determined by the sea area fisheries administration and supervision agencies; for the portion under the control of provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government, be determined by the competent administrative departments for fisheries of the provincial people's governments in consultation with the financial departments at the same level.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　跨省、自治区、直辖市的大江、大河的人工增殖放流，由江河流经的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门根据国务院渔业行政主管部门的统一规划，从其征收的内陆水域渔业资源费中提取经费。

**Article 13** For artificial proliferation and release in large rivers crossing provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the competent administrative departments for fisheries of the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government through which the rivers flow shall, in accordance with the unified plan of the competent administrative department for fisheries of the State Council, extract funds from the inland waters fishery resources fees collected by them.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　渔业资源费按预算外资金管理。  
县级以上人民政府渔业行政主管部门征收的渔业资源费应当交同级财政部门在银行开设专户储存，依照规定用途专款专用，不得挪用。

**Article 14** Fishery resources fees shall be managed as extra-budgetary funds.  
Fishery resources fees collected by the competent administrative departments for fisheries of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be submitted to the financial departments at the same level for depositing in special accounts opened with banks, and shall be used exclusively for the specified purposes in accordance with the provisions, and shall not be misappropriated.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级以上地方人民政府渔业行政主管部门以及渔业资源费的其他使用单位，应当在年初编制渔业资源费收支计划，在年终编制决算，报同级财政部门审批，并报上一级渔业行政主管部门备案。  
海区渔政监督管理机构编制的渔业资源费收支计划和年终决算，由国务院渔业行政主管部门审查汇总后，报财政部门审批。  
收支计划和决算报表的格式由国务院渔业行政主管部门统一制订。

**Article 15** The competent administrative departments for fisheries of the local people's governments at or above the county level and other units using fishery resources fees shall compile annual revenue and expenditure plans for fishery resources fees at the beginning of the year and final accounts at the end of the year, submit them to the financial departments at the same level for examination and approval, and report them to the competent administrative departments for fisheries at the next higher level for record.  
The revenue and expenditure plans and annual final accounts for fishery resources fees compiled by the sea area fisheries administration and supervision agencies shall be submitted by the competent administrative department for fisheries of the State Council, after examination and compilation, to the financial department for examination and approval.  
The formats of the revenue and expenditure plans and final account statements shall be uniformly formulated by the competent administrative department for fisheries of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　各级财政、物价和审计部门，应当加强对渔业资源费征收使用工作的监督检查，对挪用、浪费渔业资源费的行为，应当依照国家有关规定查处。

**Article 16** The financial, pricing and auditing departments at all levels shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of the collection and use of fishery resources fees, and shall investigate and deal with any misappropriation or waste of fishery resources fees in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本办法由国务院渔业行政主管部门会同财政、物价部门解释。

**Article 17** These Measures shall be interpreted by the competent administrative department for fisheries of the State Council jointly with the financial and pricing departments.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本办法自1989年1月1日起施行。

**Article 18** These Measures shall come into force as of January 1, 1989.
