### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对饲料、饲料添加剂的管理，提高饲料、饲料添加剂的质量，保障动物产品质量安全，维护公众健康，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the administration of feed and feed additives, improve the quality of feed and feed additives, ensure the quality and safety of animal products, and safeguard public health.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称饲料，是指经工业化加工、制作的供动物食用的产品，包括单一饲料、添加剂预混合饲料、浓缩饲料、配合饲料和精料补充料。  
本条例所称饲料添加剂，是指在饲料加工、制作、使用过程中添加的少量或者微量物质，包括营养性饲料添加剂和一般饲料添加剂。  
饲料原料目录和饲料添加剂品种目录由国务院农业行政主管部门制定并公布。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "feed" means products processed and manufactured by industrial methods for animal consumption, including single feed, additive premix, concentrate feed, compound feed, and supplementary concentrate.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "feed additives" means substances added in small or trace amounts during the processing, manufacturing, and use of feed, including nutritional feed additives and general feed additives.  
The catalogue of feed materials and the catalogue of feed additive varieties shall be formulated and published by the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院农业行政主管部门负责全国饲料、饲料添加剂的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责饲料、饲料添加剂管理的部门(以下简称饲料管理部门)，负责本行政区域饲料、饲料添加剂的监督管理工作。

**Article 3** The administrative department of agriculture under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of feed and feed additives nationwide.  
The department responsible for the administration of feed and feed additives under the local people's government at or above the county level (hereinafter referred to as the feed administrative department) shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of feed and feed additives within its administrative region.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上地方人民政府统一领导本行政区域饲料、饲料添加剂的监督管理工作，建立健全监督管理机制，保障监督管理工作的开展。

**Article 4** The local people's government at or above the county level shall exercise unified leadership over the supervision and administration of feed and feed additives within its administrative region, establish and improve the supervision and administration mechanism, and ensure the development of supervision and administration work.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　饲料、饲料添加剂生产企业、经营者应当建立健全质量安全制度，对其生产、经营的饲料、饲料添加剂的质量安全负责。

**Article 5** Producers and business operators of feed and feed additives shall establish and improve the quality and safety system, and be responsible for the quality and safety of the feed and feed additives they produce or operate.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何组织或者个人有权举报在饲料、饲料添加剂生产、经营、使用过程中违反本条例的行为，有权对饲料、饲料添加剂监督管理工作提出意见和建议。

**Article 6** Any organization or individual shall have the right to report any act violating these Regulations in the production, operation, or use of feed and feed additives, and shall have the right to put forward opinions and suggestions on the supervision and administration of feed and feed additives.

### 第二章　审定和登记

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励研制新饲料、新饲料添加剂。  
研制新饲料、新饲料添加剂，应当遵循科学、安全、有效、环保的原则，保证新饲料、新饲料添加剂的质量安全。

**Article 7** The State encourages the research and development of new feed and new feed additives.  
The research and development of new feed and new feed additives shall follow the principles of science, safety, effectiveness, and environmental protection, and ensure the quality and safety of new feed and new feed additives.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　研制的新饲料、新饲料添加剂投入生产前，研制者或者生产企业应当向国务院农业行政主管部门提出审定申请，并提供该新饲料、新饲料添加剂的样品和下列资料：  
(一)名称、主要成分、理化性质、研制方法、生产工艺、质量标准、检测方法、检验报告、稳定性试验报告、环境影响报告和污染防治措施；  
(二)国务院农业行政主管部门指定的试验机构出具的该新饲料、新饲料添加剂的饲喂效果、残留消解动态以及毒理学安全性评价报告。  
申请新饲料添加剂审定的，还应当说明该新饲料添加剂的添加目的、使用方法，并提供该饲料添加剂残留可能对人体健康造成影响的分析评价报告。

**Article 8** Before the newly developed feed and new feed additive are put into production, the developer or the production enterprise shall submit an application for approval to the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council, and provide samples of such new feed and new feed additive and the following materials:  
(1) name, main ingredients, physicochemical properties, research and development methods, production techniques, quality standards, testing methods, inspection reports, stability test reports, environmental impact reports, and pollution prevention measures;  
(2) reports on the feeding effect, residue depletion dynamics, and toxicological safety evaluation of such new feed and new feed additive issued by the testing institution designated by the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council.  
When applying for approval of a new feed additive, the applicant shall also state the purpose and usage of such new feed additive, and provide an analytical evaluation report on the possible impact of residues of such feed additive on human health.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院农业行政主管部门应当自受理申请之日起5个工作日内，将新饲料、新饲料添加剂的样品和申请资料交全国饲料评审委员会，对该新饲料、新饲料添加剂的安全性、有效性及其对环境的影响进行评审。  
全国饲料评审委员会由养殖、饲料加工、动物营养、毒理、药理、代谢、卫生、化工合成、生物技术、质量标准、环境保护、食品安全风险评估等方面的专家组成。全国饲料评审委员会对新饲料、新饲料添加剂的评审采取评审会议的形式，评审会议应当有9名以上全国饲料评审委员会专家参加，根据需要也可以邀请1至2名全国饲料评审委员会专家以外的专家参加，参加评审的专家对评审事项具有表决权。评审会议应当形成评审意见和会议纪要，并由参加评审的专家审核签字；有不同意见的，应当注明。参加评审的专家应当依法公平、公正履行职责，对评审资料保密，存在回避事由的，应当主动回避。  
全国饲料评审委员会应当自收到新饲料、新饲料添加剂的样品和申请资料之日起9个月内出具评审结果并提交国务院农业行政主管部门；但是，全国饲料评审委员会决定由申请人进行相关试验的，经国务院农业行政主管部门同意，评审时间可以延长3个月。  
国务院农业行政主管部门应当自收到评审结果之日起10个工作日内作出是否核发新饲料、新饲料添加剂证书的决定；决定不予核发的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 9** The administrative department of agriculture under the State Council shall, within 5 working days from the date of accepting the application, submit the samples and application materials of the new feed and new feed additive to the National Feed Evaluation Committee, which shall evaluate the safety, effectiveness, and environmental impact of such new feed and new feed additive.  
The National Feed Evaluation Committee shall be composed of experts in fields such as breeding, feed processing, animal nutrition, toxicology, pharmacology, metabolism, hygiene, chemical synthesis, biotechnology, quality standards, environmental protection, and food safety risk assessment. The National Feed Evaluation Committee shall conduct the evaluation of new feed and new feed additives in the form of evaluation meetings. An evaluation meeting shall be attended by at least nine experts from the National Feed Evaluation Committee, and, if necessary, one or two experts who are not members of the National Feed Evaluation Committee may also be invited. The experts participating in the evaluation shall have the right to vote on the matters to be evaluated. An evaluation meeting shall form an evaluation opinion and meeting minutes, which shall be reviewed and signed by the experts participating in the evaluation; if there are dissenting opinions, they shall be noted. The experts participating in the evaluation shall perform their duties fairly and impartially according to law, keep the evaluation materials confidential, and if there are grounds for withdrawal, they shall voluntarily withdraw.  
The National Feed Evaluation Committee shall, within 9 months from the date of receiving the samples and application materials of the new feed and new feed additive, issue the evaluation results and submit them to the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council; however, if the National Feed Evaluation Committee decides that the applicant shall conduct relevant tests, the evaluation period may be extended by 3 months with the consent of the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council.  
The administrative department of agriculture under the State Council shall, within 10 working days from the date of receiving the evaluation results, make a decision on whether to issue a new feed or new feed additive certificate; if it decides not to issue the certificate, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院农业行政主管部门核发新饲料、新饲料添加剂证书，应当同时按照职责权限公布该新饲料、新饲料添加剂的产品质量标准。

**Article 10** When issuing a new feed or new feed additive certificate, the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council shall simultaneously publish the product quality standards of such new feed or new feed additive in accordance with its functions and powers.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　新饲料、新饲料添加剂的监测期为5年。新饲料、新饲料添加剂处于监测期的，不受理其他就该新饲料、新饲料添加剂的生产申请和进口登记申请，但超过3年不投入生产的除外。  
生产企业应当收集处于监测期的新饲料、新饲料添加剂的质量稳定性及其对动物产品质量安全的影响等信息，并向国务院农业行政主管部门报告；国务院农业行政主管部门应当对新饲料、新饲料添加剂的质量安全状况组织跟踪监测，证实其存在安全问题的，应当撤销新饲料、新饲料添加剂证书并予以公告。

**Article 11** The monitoring period for new feed and new feed additives shall be 5 years. Where a new feed or new feed additive is within the monitoring period, no production application or import registration application by other parties with respect to such new feed or new feed additive shall be accepted, unless it has not been put into production for more than 3 years.  
The production enterprise shall collect information on the quality stability of the new feed or new feed additive within the monitoring period and its impact on the quality and safety of animal products, and report to the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council; the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council shall organize follow-up monitoring of the quality and safety status of the new feed or new feed additive, and if it is confirmed that there is a safety problem, it shall revoke the new feed or new feed additive certificate and make a public announcement.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　向中国出口中国境内尚未使用但出口国已经批准生产和使用的饲料、饲料添加剂的，由出口方驻中国境内的办事机构或者其委托的中国境内代理机构向国务院农业行政主管部门申请登记，并提供该饲料、饲料添加剂的样品和下列资料：  
(一)商标、标签和推广应用情况；  
(二)生产地批准生产、使用的证明和生产地以外其他国家、地区的登记资料；  
(三)主要成分、理化性质、研制方法、生产工艺、质量标准、检测方法、检验报告、稳定性试验报告、环境影响报告和污染防治措施；  
(四)国务院农业行政主管部门指定的试验机构出具的该饲料、饲料添加剂的饲喂效果、残留消解动态以及毒理学安全性评价报告。  
申请饲料添加剂进口登记的，还应当说明该饲料添加剂的添加目的、使用方法，并提供该饲料添加剂残留可能对人体健康造成影响的分析评价报告。  
国务院农业行政主管部门应当依照[本条例第九条](#art-9)规定的新饲料、新饲料添加剂的评审程序组织评审，并决定是否核发饲料、饲料添加剂进口登记证。  
首次向中国出口中国境内已经使用且出口国已经批准生产和使用的饲料、饲料添加剂的，应当依照本条第一款、第二款的规定申请登记。国务院农业行政主管部门应当自受理申请之日起10个工作日内对申请资料进行审查；审查合格的，将样品交由指定的机构进行复核检测；复核检测合格的，国务院农业行政主管部门应当在10个工作日内核发饲料、饲料添加剂进口登记证。  
饲料、饲料添加剂进口登记证有效期为5年。进口登记证有效期满需要继续向中国出口饲料、饲料添加剂的，应当在有效期届满6个月前申请续展。  
禁止进口未取得饲料、饲料添加剂进口登记证的饲料、饲料添加剂。

**Article 12** If feed or feed additives that have not been used in China but have been approved for production and use in the exporting country are imported into China, the office of the exporter stationed in China or the agent in China authorized by the exporter shall apply for registration with the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council, and provide samples of such feed or feed additive and the following materials:  
(1) trademarks, labels, and information on promotion and application;  
(2) certificates of approval for production and use in the place of production, and registration materials from countries or regions other than the place of production;  
(3) name, main ingredients, physicochemical properties, research and development methods, production techniques, quality standards, testing methods, inspection reports, stability test reports, environmental impact reports, and pollution prevention measures;  
(4) reports on the feeding effect, residue depletion dynamics, and toxicological safety evaluation of such feed or feed additive issued by the testing institution designated by the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council.  
When applying for import registration of a feed additive, the applicant shall also state the purpose and usage of such feed additive, and provide an analytical evaluation report on the possible impact of residues of such feed additive on human health.  
The administrative department of agriculture under the State Council shall organize the evaluation in accordance with the evaluation procedures for new feed and new feed additives as provided in Article 9 of these Regulations, and decide whether to issue an import registration certificate for feed or feed additives.  
Where feed or feed additives that have already been used in China and have been approved for production and use in the exporting country are imported into China for the first time, registration shall be applied for in accordance with paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article. The administrative department of agriculture under the State Council shall, within 10 working days from the date of accepting the application, review the application materials; if the review is passed, the samples shall be submitted to the designated institution for verification testing; if the verification testing is passed, the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council shall issue the import registration certificate for feed or feed additives within 10 working days.  
The import registration certificate for feed or feed additives shall be valid for 5 years. If it is necessary to continue exporting feed or feed additives to China after the expiration of the validity period of the import registration certificate, an application for renewal shall be made 6 months before the expiration of the validity period.  
Import of feed or feed additives without obtaining an import registration certificate for feed or feed additives is prohibited.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家对已经取得新饲料、新饲料添加剂证书或者饲料、饲料添加剂进口登记证的、含有新化合物的饲料、饲料添加剂的申请人提交的其自己所取得且未披露的试验数据和其他数据实施保护。  
自核发证书之日起6年内，对其他申请人未经已取得新饲料、新饲料添加剂证书或者饲料、饲料添加剂进口登记证的申请人同意，使用前款规定的数据申请新饲料、新饲料添加剂审定或者饲料、饲料添加剂进口登记的，国务院农业行政主管部门不予审定或者登记；但是，其他申请人提交其自己所取得的数据的除外。  
除下列情形外，国务院农业行政主管部门不得披露本条第一款规定的数据：  
(一)公共利益需要；  
(二)已采取措施确保该类信息不会被不正当地进行商业使用。

**Article 13** The State shall protect the test data and other data that are independently obtained and not disclosed by applicants of feed or feed additives containing new compounds who have already obtained new feed or new feed additive certificates or import registration certificates for feed or feed additives.  
Within 6 years from the date of issuance of the certificate, if another applicant, without the consent of the applicant who has obtained a new feed or new feed additive certificate or import registration certificate for feed or feed additives, uses the data specified in the preceding paragraph to apply for approval of new feed or new feed additives or import registration of feed or feed additives, the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council shall not approve or register; except where the other applicant submits data independently obtained by itself.  
The administrative department of agriculture under the State Council shall not disclose the data specified in paragraph 1 of this Article, except under the following circumstances:  
(1) public interest requires disclosure;  
(2) measures have been taken to ensure that such information will not be improperly used for commercial purposes.

### 第三章　生产、经营和使用

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　设立饲料、饲料添加剂生产企业，应当符合饲料工业发展规划和产业政策，并具备下列条件：  
(一)有与生产饲料、饲料添加剂相适应的厂房、设备和仓储设施；  
(二)有与生产饲料、饲料添加剂相适应的专职技术人员；  
(三)有必要的产品质量检验机构、人员、设施和质量管理制度；  
(四)有符合国家规定的安全、卫生要求的生产环境；  
(五)有符合国家环境保护要求的污染防治措施；  
(六)国务院农业行政主管部门制定的饲料、饲料添加剂质量安全管理规范规定的其他条件。

**Article 14** To establish a feed or feed additive production enterprise, it shall meet the development plan and industrial policy of the feed industry, and meet the following conditions:  
(1) having production plants, equipment, and storage facilities suitable for producing feed or feed additives;  
(2) having full-time technical personnel suitable for producing feed or feed additives;  
(3) having necessary product quality inspection institutions, personnel, facilities, and quality management systems;  
(4) having a production environment that meets the national safety and hygiene requirements;  
(5) having pollution prevention measures that meet the national environmental protection requirements;  
(6) other conditions specified in the quality and safety management norms for feed and feed additives formulated by the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　申请从事饲料、饲料添加剂生产的企业，申请人应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府饲料管理部门提出申请。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府饲料管理部门应当自受理申请之日起10个工作日内进行书面审查；审查合格的，组织进行现场审核，并根据审核结果在10个工作日内作出是否核发生产许可证的决定。  
生产许可证有效期为5年。生产许可证有效期满需要继续生产饲料、饲料添加剂的，应当在有效期届满6个月前申请续展。

**Article 15** An enterprise applying to engage in the production of feed or feed additives shall submit an application to the feed administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The feed administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 10 working days from the date of accepting the application, conduct a written review; if the review is passed, it shall organize an on-site inspection, and within 10 working days, based on the inspection results, decide whether to issue a production license.  
The production license shall be valid for 5 years. If it is necessary to continue production of feed or feed additives after the expiration of the validity period of the production license, an application for renewal shall be made 6 months before the expiration of the validity period.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　饲料添加剂、添加剂预混合饲料生产企业取得生产许可证后，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府饲料管理部门按照国务院农业行政主管部门的规定，核发相应的产品批准文号。

**Article 16** After a feed additive or additive premix production enterprise obtains a production license, the feed administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the provisions of the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council, issue the corresponding product approval number.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　饲料、饲料添加剂生产企业应当按照国务院农业行政主管部门的规定和有关标准，对采购的饲料原料、单一饲料、饲料添加剂、药物饲料添加剂、添加剂预混合饲料和用于饲料添加剂生产的原料进行查验或者检验。  
饲料生产企业使用限制使用的饲料原料、单一饲料、饲料添加剂、药物饲料添加剂、添加剂预混合饲料生产饲料的，应当遵守国务院农业行政主管部门的限制性规定。禁止使用国务院农业行政主管部门公布的饲料原料目录、饲料添加剂品种目录和药物饲料添加剂品种目录以外的任何物质生产饲料。  
饲料、饲料添加剂生产企业应当如实记录采购的饲料原料、单一饲料、饲料添加剂、药物饲料添加剂、添加剂预混合饲料和用于饲料添加剂生产的原料的名称、产地、数量、保质期、许可证明文件编号、质量检验信息、生产企业名称或者供货者名称及其联系方式、进货日期等。记录保存期限不得少于2年。

**Article 17** A feed or feed additive production enterprise shall, in accordance with the provisions of the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council and relevant standards, inspect or test the purchased feed materials, single feed, feed additives, medicated feed additives, additive premixes, and raw materials used for the production of feed additives.  
Where a feed production enterprise uses restricted feed materials, single feed, feed additives, medicated feed additives, or additive premixes to produce feed, it shall comply with the restrictive provisions of the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council. It is prohibited to use any substance other than those included in the catalogue of feed materials, the catalogue of feed additive varieties, and the catalogue of medicated feed additive varieties published by the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council to produce feed.  
A feed or feed additive production enterprise shall truthfully record the name, origin, quantity, shelf life, number of the license document, quality inspection information, name of the production enterprise or supplier and its contact information, purchase date, etc., of the purchased feed materials, single feed, feed additives, medicated feed additives, additive premixes, and raw materials used for the production of feed additives. The record retention period shall not be less than 2 years.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　饲料、饲料添加剂生产企业，应当按照产品质量标准以及国务院农业行政主管部门制定的饲料、饲料添加剂质量安全管理规范和饲料添加剂安全使用规范组织生产，对生产过程实施有效控制并实行生产记录和产品留样观察制度。

**Article 18** A feed or feed additive production enterprise shall organize production in accordance with product quality standards and the quality and safety management norms for feed and feed additives and the safety use norms for feed additives formulated by the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council, implement effective control over the production process, and implement a production record and product sample retention observation system.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　饲料、饲料添加剂生产企业应当对生产的饲料、饲料添加剂进行产品质量检验；检验合格的，应当附具产品质量检验合格证。未经产品质量检验、检验不合格或者未附具产品质量检验合格证的，不得出厂销售。  
饲料、饲料添加剂生产企业应当如实记录出厂销售的饲料、饲料添加剂的名称、数量、生产日期、生产批次、质量检验信息、购货者名称及其联系方式、销售日期等。记录保存期限不得少于2年。

**Article 19** A feed or feed additive production enterprise shall conduct product quality inspection on the feed or feed additives it produces; if the inspection is passed, it shall attach a product quality inspection certificate. If the product has not undergone product quality inspection, has failed the inspection, or has not been attached with a product quality inspection certificate, it shall not be delivered from the factory or sold.  
A feed or feed additive production enterprise shall truthfully record the name, quantity, production date, production batch, quality inspection information, name of the purchaser and its contact information, sales date, etc., of the feed or feed additives delivered from the factory or sold. The record retention period shall not be less than 2 years.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　出厂销售的饲料、饲料添加剂应当包装，包装应当符合国家有关安全、卫生的规定。  
饲料生产企业直接销售给养殖者的饲料可以使用罐装车运输。罐装车应当符合国家有关安全、卫生的规定，并随罐装车附具符合[本条例第二十一条](#art-21)规定的标签。  
易燃或者其他特殊的饲料、饲料添加剂的包装应当有警示标志或者说明，并注明储运注意事项。

**Article 20** Feed and feed additives delivered from the factory or sold shall be packaged, and the packaging shall comply with the national safety and hygiene provisions.  
Feed directly sold by feed production enterprises to breeders may be transported by tank trucks. The tank trucks shall comply with the national safety and hygiene provisions, and the labels in accordance with Article 21 of these Regulations shall be attached to the tank trucks.  
The packaging of flammable or other special feed or feed additives shall have warning signs or descriptions, and indicate storage and transportation precautions.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　饲料、饲料添加剂的包装上应当附具标签。标签应当以中文或者适用符号标明产品名称、原料组成、产品成分分析保证值、净重或者净含量、贮存条件、使用说明、注意事项、生产日期、保质期、生产企业名称以及地址、许可证明文件编号和产品质量标准等。加入药物饲料添加剂的，还应当标明“加入药物饲料添加剂”字样，并标明其通用名称、含量和休药期。乳和乳制品以外的动物源性饲料，还应当标明“本产品不得饲喂反刍动物”字样。

**Article 21** A label shall be attached to the package of feed and feed additives. The label shall indicate, in Chinese or with applicable symbols, the product name, ingredient composition, guaranteed analysis of product ingredients, net weight or net content, storage conditions, directions for use, precautions, date of manufacture, shelf life, name and address of the manufacturer, number of the license document, and product quality standards, among others. Where medicated feed additives are added, the words "medicated feed additive added" shall be indicated, and the generic name, content, and withdrawal period thereof shall be indicated. For animal-derived feed other than milk and dairy products, the words "This product shall not be fed to ruminants" shall be indicated.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　饲料、饲料添加剂经营者应当符合下列条件：  
(一)有与经营饲料、饲料添加剂相适应的经营场所和仓储设施；  
(二)有具备饲料、饲料添加剂使用、贮存等知识的技术人员；  
(三)有必要的产品质量管理和安全管理制度。

**Article 22** A feed and feed additive distributor shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having business premises and storage facilities commensurate with the distribution of feed and feed additives;  
(2) having technical personnel with knowledge of the use and storage of feed and feed additives; and  
(3) having the necessary product quality management and safety management systems.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　饲料、饲料添加剂经营者进货时应当查验产品标签、产品质量检验合格证和相应的许可证明文件。  
饲料、饲料添加剂经营者不得对饲料、饲料添加剂进行拆包、分装，不得对饲料、饲料添加剂进行再加工或者添加任何物质。  
禁止经营用国务院农业行政主管部门公布的饲料原料目录、饲料添加剂品种目录和药物饲料添加剂品种目录以外的任何物质生产的饲料。  
饲料、饲料添加剂经营者应当建立产品购销台账，如实记录购销产品的名称、许可证明文件编号、规格、数量、保质期、生产企业名称或者供货者名称及其联系方式、购销时间等。购销台账保存期限不得少于2年。

**Article 23** When purchasing feed and feed additives, a feed and feed additive distributor shall inspect the product label, product quality inspection certificate, and the corresponding license documents.  
A feed and feed additive distributor shall not unpack or repackage feed and feed additives, and shall not reprocess feed and feed additives or add any substance thereto.  
It is prohibited to distribute feed produced with any substance other than those listed in the catalogue of feed ingredients, the catalogue of feed additive varieties, and the catalogue of medicated feed additive varieties published by the administrative department of agriculture of the State Council.  
A feed and feed additive distributor shall establish purchase and sale ledgers, truthfully recording the name of the purchased or sold product, number of the license document, specification, quantity, shelf life, name of the manufacturer or supplier and its contact information, time of purchase or sale, etc. The retention period of the purchase and sale ledgers shall not be less than two years.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　向中国出口的饲料、饲料添加剂应当包装，包装应当符合中国有关安全、卫生的规定，并附具符合[本条例第二十一条](#art-21)规定的标签。  
向中国出口的饲料、饲料添加剂应当符合中国有关检验检疫的要求，由出入境检验检疫机构依法实施检验检疫，并对其包装和标签进行核查。包装和标签不符合要求的，不得入境。  
境外企业不得直接在中国销售饲料、饲料添加剂。境外企业在中国销售饲料、饲料添加剂的，应当依法在中国境内设立销售机构或者委托符合条件的中国境内代理机构销售。

**Article 24** Feed and feed additives exported to China shall be packaged, and the packaging shall comply with the relevant safety and hygiene provisions of China, and shall have a label attached that complies with the provisions of Article 21 of these Regulations.  
Feed and feed additives exported to China shall comply with the relevant inspection and quarantine requirements of China, and shall be subject to inspection and quarantine by the exit and entry inspection and quarantine institutions according to law, which shall also check the packaging and labels thereof. Where the packaging and labels do not meet the requirements, they shall not be imported.  
An overseas enterprise shall not directly sell feed and feed additives in China. Where an overseas enterprise sells feed and feed additives in China, it shall, according to law, establish a sales institution within China or entrust a qualified agent institution within China to sell them.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　养殖者应当按照产品使用说明和注意事项使用饲料。在饲料或者动物饮用水中添加饲料添加剂的，应当符合饲料添加剂使用说明和注意事项的要求，遵守国务院农业行政主管部门制定的饲料添加剂安全使用规范。  
养殖者使用自行配制的饲料的，应当遵守国务院农业行政主管部门制定的自行配制饲料使用规范，并不得对外提供自行配制的饲料。  
使用限制使用的物质养殖动物的，应当遵守国务院农业行政主管部门的限制性规定。禁止在饲料、动物饮用水中添加国务院农业行政主管部门公布禁用的物质以及对人体具有直接或者潜在危害的其他物质，或者直接使用上述物质养殖动物。禁止在反刍动物饲料中添加乳和乳制品以外的动物源性成分。

**Article 25** A breeder shall use feed in accordance with the product use instructions and precautions. Where feed additives are added to feed or animal drinking water, the requirements of the feed additive use instructions and precautions shall be met, and the safe use specifications for feed additives formulated by the administrative department of agriculture of the State Council shall be complied with.  
Where a breeder uses self-formulated feed, it shall comply with the use specifications for self-formulated feed formulated by the administrative department of agriculture of the State Council, and shall not provide self-formulated feed externally.  
Where a substance subject to restricted use is used for breeding animals, the restrictive provisions of the administrative department of agriculture of the State Council shall be complied with. It is prohibited to add substances banned by the administrative department of agriculture of the State Council or other substances that are directly or potentially harmful to human health to feed or animal drinking water, or to directly use the aforesaid substances for breeding animals. It is prohibited to add animal-derived ingredients other than milk and dairy products to ruminant feed.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国务院农业行政主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门应当加强饲料、饲料添加剂质量安全知识的宣传，提高养殖者的质量安全意识，指导养殖者安全、合理使用饲料、饲料添加剂。

**Article 26** The administrative department of agriculture of the State Council and the feed administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the dissemination of knowledge on the quality and safety of feed and feed additives, enhance the quality and safety awareness of breeders, and guide breeders to use feed and feed additives safely and reasonably.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　饲料、饲料添加剂在使用过程中被证实对养殖动物、人体健康或者环境有害的，由国务院农业行政主管部门决定禁用并予以公布。

**Article 27** Where feed or feed additives are proven to be harmful to breeding animals, human health, or the environment during use, the administrative department of agriculture of the State Council shall decide to ban them and announce such a decision.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　饲料、饲料添加剂生产企业发现其生产的饲料、饲料添加剂对养殖动物、人体健康有害或者存在其他安全隐患的，应当立即停止生产，通知经营者、使用者，向饲料管理部门报告，主动召回产品，并记录召回和通知情况。召回的产品应当在饲料管理部门监督下予以无害化处理或者销毁。  
饲料、饲料添加剂经营者发现其销售的饲料、饲料添加剂具有前款规定情形的，应当立即停止销售，通知生产企业、供货者和使用者，向饲料管理部门报告，并记录通知情况。  
养殖者发现其使用的饲料、饲料添加剂具有本条第一款规定情形的，应当立即停止使用，通知供货者，并向饲料管理部门报告。

**Article 28** Where a feed or feed additive manufacturer discovers that the feed or feed additive it produces is harmful to breeding animals or human health or has any other safety hazard, it shall immediately cease production, notify the distributors and users, report to the feed administrative department, voluntarily recall the products, and record the recall and notification. The recalled products shall be rendered harmless or destroyed under the supervision of the feed administrative department.  
Where a feed or feed additive distributor discovers that the feed or feed additive it sells falls under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, it shall immediately stop selling, notify the manufacturer, supplier, and users, report to the feed administrative department, and record the notification.  
Where a breeder discovers that the feed or feed additive it uses falls under the circumstances specified in the first paragraph of this Article, it shall immediately stop using, notify the supplier, and report to the feed administrative department.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　禁止生产、经营、使用未取得新饲料、新饲料添加剂证书的新饲料、新饲料添加剂以及禁用的饲料、饲料添加剂。  
禁止经营、使用无产品标签、无生产许可证、无产品质量标准、无产品质量检验合格证的饲料、饲料添加剂。禁止经营、使用无产品批准文号的饲料添加剂、添加剂预混合饲料。禁止经营、使用未取得饲料、饲料添加剂进口登记证的进口饲料、进口饲料添加剂。

**Article 29** It is prohibited to produce, distribute, or use new feed or new feed additives for which no new feed or new feed additive certificate has been obtained, or prohibited feed or feed additives.  
It is prohibited to distribute or use feed or feed additives without a product label, without a production license, without product quality standards, or without a product quality inspection certificate. It is prohibited to distribute or use feed additives or additive premixed feed without a product approval number. It is prohibited to distribute or use imported feed or imported feed additives for which no import registration certificate for feed or feed additives has been obtained.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　禁止对饲料、饲料添加剂作具有预防或者治疗动物疾病作用的说明或者宣传。但是，饲料中添加药物饲料添加剂的，可以对所添加的药物饲料添加剂的作用加以说明。

**Article 30** No person shall make any statement or advertisement that feed or feed additives have the effect of preventing or treating animal diseases. However, where medicated feed additives are added to feed, the effect of the added medicated feed additives may be explained.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国务院农业行政主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府饲料管理部门应当按照职责权限对全国或者本行政区域饲料、饲料添加剂的质量安全状况进行监测，并根据监测情况发布饲料、饲料添加剂质量安全预警信息。

**Article 31** The administrative department of agriculture of the State Council and the feed administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, according to their functions and powers, monitor the quality and safety status of feed and feed additives nationwide or within their respective administrative regions, and issue early warning information on the quality and safety of feed and feed additives based on the monitoring results.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院农业行政主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门，应当根据需要定期或者不定期组织实施饲料、饲料添加剂监督抽查；饲料、饲料添加剂监督抽查检测工作由国务院农业行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府饲料管理部门指定的具有相应技术条件的机构承担。饲料、饲料添加剂监督抽查不得收费。  
国务院农业行政主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府饲料管理部门应当按照职责权限公布监督抽查结果，并可以公布具有不良记录的饲料、饲料添加剂生产企业、经营者名单。

**Article 32** The administrative department of agriculture of the State Council and the feed administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, as needed, periodically or irregularly organize and implement supervisory inspections on feed and feed additives; the testing for such supervisory inspections shall be undertaken by institutions with appropriate technical conditions designated by the administrative department of agriculture of the State Council or the feed administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government. No fees shall be charged for the supervisory inspections on feed and feed additives.  
The administrative department of agriculture of the State Council and the feed administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, according to their functions and powers, publish the results of supervisory inspections, and may publish the list of feed and feed additive manufacturers and distributors with adverse records.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门应当建立饲料、饲料添加剂监督管理档案，记录日常监督检查、违法行为查处等情况。

**Article 33** The feed administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish supervision and management files for feed and feed additives, recording routine supervision and inspection, investigation and punishment of illegal acts, and other matters.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院农业行政主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门在监督检查中可以采取下列措施：  
(一)对饲料、饲料添加剂生产、经营、使用场所实施现场检查；  
(二)查阅、复制有关合同、票据、账簿和其他相关资料；  
(三)查封、扣押有证据证明用于违法生产饲料的饲料原料、单一饲料、饲料添加剂、药物饲料添加剂、添加剂预混合饲料，用于违法生产饲料添加剂的原料，用于违法生产饲料、饲料添加剂的工具、设施，违法生产、经营、使用的饲料、饲料添加剂；  
(四)查封违法生产、经营饲料、饲料添加剂的场所。

**Article 34** In the course of supervision and inspection, the administrative department of agriculture of the State Council and the feed administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level may take the following measures:  
(1) conducting on-site inspections of premises where feed and feed additives are produced, distributed, or used;  
(2) consulting and making copies of relevant contracts, invoices, account books, and other relevant materials;  
(3) sealing up or seizing feed ingredients, single feeds, feed additives, medicated feed additives, additive premixed feed used for the illegal production of feed, raw materials used for the illegal production of feed additives, tools and facilities used for the illegal production of feed and feed additives, and feed and feed additives that are illegally produced, distributed, or used, for which there is evidence; and  
(4) sealing up premises where feed and feed additives are illegally produced or distributed.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国务院农业行政主管部门、县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门或者其他依照本条例规定行使监督管理权的部门及其工作人员，不履行本条例规定的职责或者滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 35** Where the administrative department of agriculture of the State Council, the feed administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level, or any other department exercising supervision and administrative powers in accordance with these Regulations, or any staff member thereof, fails to perform the duties prescribed in these Regulations, or abuses power, neglects duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; where the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　提供虚假的资料、样品或者采取其他欺骗方式取得许可证明文件的，由发证机关撤销相关许可证明文件，处5万元以上10万元以下罚款，申请人3年内不得就同一事项申请行政许可。以欺骗方式取得许可证明文件给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 36** Where any entity or individual obtains a license document by providing false information or samples or by other fraudulent means, the issuing authority shall revoke the relevant license document, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan, and the applicant shall not apply for an administrative license for the same matter within three years. Where a license document obtained by fraudulent means causes losses to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　假冒、伪造或者买卖许可证明文件的，由国务院农业行政主管部门或者县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门按照职责权限收缴或者吊销、撤销相关许可证明文件；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 37** Where any entity or individual counterfeits, forges, or buys or sells license documents, the administrative department of agriculture of the State Council or the feed administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to their functions and powers, confiscate or revoke the relevant license documents; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　未取得生产许可证生产饲料、饲料添加剂的，由县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门责令停止生产，没收违法所得、违法生产的产品和用于违法生产饲料的饲料原料、单一饲料、饲料添加剂、药物饲料添加剂、添加剂预混合饲料以及用于违法生产饲料添加剂的原料，违法生产的产品货值金额不足1万元的，并处1万元以上5万元以下罚款，货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额5倍以上10倍以下罚款；情节严重的，没收其生产设备，生产企业的主要负责人和直接负责的主管人员10年内不得从事饲料、饲料添加剂生产、经营活动。  
已经取得生产许可证，但不再具备[本条例第十四条](#art-14)规定的条件而继续生产饲料、饲料添加剂的，由县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门责令停止生产、限期改正，并处1万元以上5万元以下罚款；逾期不改正的，由发证机关吊销生产许可证。  
已经取得生产许可证，但未取得产品批准文号而生产饲料添加剂、添加剂预混合饲料的，由县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门责令停止生产，没收违法所得、违法生产的产品和用于违法生产饲料的饲料原料、单一饲料、饲料添加剂、药物饲料添加剂以及用于违法生产饲料添加剂的原料，限期补办产品批准文号，并处违法生产的产品货值金额1倍以上3倍以下罚款；情节严重的，由发证机关吊销生产许可证。

**Article 38** Where any entity produces feed or feed additives without obtaining a production license, the feed administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to stop production, confiscate the illegal gains, illegally produced products, and the feed ingredients, single feeds, feed additives, medicated feed additives, additive premixed feed used for the illegal production of feed, and the raw materials used for the illegal production of feed additives; where the value of the illegally produced products is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall also be imposed; where the value is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the value of the illegally produced products shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the production equipment shall be confiscated, and the principal responsible person and the directly responsible persons in charge of the manufacturer shall not engage in the production or distribution of feed and feed additives within ten years.  
Where a manufacturer that has already obtained a production license continues to produce feed or feed additives while no longer meeting the conditions prescribed in Article 14 of these Regulations, the feed administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to stop production and rectify within a specified period, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if it fails to rectify within the specified period, the issuing authority shall revoke the production license.  
Where a manufacturer that has already obtained a production license produces feed additives or additive premixed feed without obtaining a product approval number, the feed administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to stop production, confiscate the illegal gains, illegally produced products, and the feed ingredients, single feeds, feed additives, medicated feed additives used for the illegal production of feed, and the raw materials used for the illegal production of feed additives, order it to obtain the product approval number within a specified period, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the value of the illegally produced products; if the circumstances are serious, the issuing authority shall revoke the production license.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　饲料、饲料添加剂生产企业有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得、违法生产的产品和用于违法生产饲料的饲料原料、单一饲料、饲料添加剂、药物饲料添加剂、添加剂预混合饲料以及用于违法生产饲料添加剂的原料，违法生产的产品货值金额不足1万元的，并处1万元以上5万元以下罚款，货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额5倍以上10倍以下罚款；情节严重的，由发证机关吊销、撤销相关许可证明文件，生产企业的主要负责人和直接负责的主管人员10年内不得从事饲料、饲料添加剂生产、经营活动；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)使用限制使用的饲料原料、单一饲料、饲料添加剂、药物饲料添加剂、添加剂预混合饲料生产饲料，不遵守国务院农业行政主管部门的限制性规定的；  
(二)使用国务院农业行政主管部门公布的饲料原料目录、饲料添加剂品种目录和药物饲料添加剂品种目录以外的物质生产饲料的；  
(三)生产未取得新饲料、新饲料添加剂证书的新饲料、新饲料添加剂或者禁用的饲料、饲料添加剂的。

**Article 39** Where a feed or feed additive manufacturer commits any of the following acts, the feed administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections, confiscate the illegal gains, illegally produced products, and the feed ingredients, single feeds, feed additives, medicated feed additives, additive premixed feed used for the illegal production of feed, and the raw materials used for the illegal production of feed additives; where the value of the illegally produced products is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall also be imposed; where the value is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the value of the illegally produced products shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the issuing authority shall revoke or cancel the relevant license documents, and the principal responsible person and the directly responsible persons in charge of the manufacturer shall not engage in the production or distribution of feed and feed additives within ten years; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) using restricted feed ingredients, single feeds, feed additives, medicated feed additives, or additive premixed feed to produce feed, but failing to comply with the restrictive provisions of the administrative department of agriculture of the State Council;  
(2) using substances other than those listed in the catalogue of feed ingredients, the catalogue of feed additive varieties, and the catalogue of medicated feed additive varieties published by the administrative department of agriculture of the State Council to produce feed; or  
(3) producing new feed or new feed additives for which no new feed or new feed additive certificate has been obtained, or prohibited feed or feed additives.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　饲料、饲料添加剂生产企业有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门责令改正，处1万元以上2万元以下罚款；拒不改正的，没收违法所得、违法生产的产品和用于违法生产饲料的饲料原料、单一饲料、饲料添加剂、药物饲料添加剂、添加剂预混合饲料以及用于违法生产饲料添加剂的原料，并处5万元以上10万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停止生产，可以由发证机关吊销、撤销相关许可证明文件：  
(一)不按照国务院农业行政主管部门的规定和有关标准对采购的饲料原料、单一饲料、饲料添加剂、药物饲料添加剂、添加剂预混合饲料和用于饲料添加剂生产的原料进行查验或者检验的；  
(二)饲料、饲料添加剂生产过程中不遵守国务院农业行政主管部门制定的饲料、饲料添加剂质量安全管理规范和饲料添加剂安全使用规范的；  
(三)生产的饲料、饲料添加剂未经产品质量检验的。

**Article 40** Where a feed or feed additive manufacturer commits any of the following acts, the feed administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan; if it refuses to make corrections, the illegal gains, illegally produced products, and the feed ingredients, single feeds, feed additives, medicated feed additives, additive premixed feed used for the illegal production of feed, and the raw materials used for the illegal production of feed additives shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to stop production, and the issuing authority may revoke or cancel the relevant license documents:  
(1) failing to check or test the purchased feed ingredients, single feeds, feed additives, medicated feed additives, additive premixed feed, and raw materials used for producing feed additives in accordance with the provisions of the administrative department of agriculture of the State Council and relevant standards;  
(2) failing to comply with the quality and safety management specifications for feed and feed additives and the safe use specifications for feed additives formulated by the administrative department of agriculture of the State Council during the production process of feed and feed additives; or  
(3) failing to carry out product quality inspection on the feed or feed additives produced.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　饲料、饲料添加剂生产企业不依照本条例规定实行采购、生产、销售记录制度或者产品留样观察制度的，由县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门责令改正，处1万元以上2万元以下罚款；拒不改正的，没收违法所得、违法生产的产品和用于违法生产饲料的饲料原料、单一饲料、饲料添加剂、药物饲料添加剂、添加剂预混合饲料以及用于违法生产饲料添加剂的原料，处2万元以上5万元以下罚款，并可以由发证机关吊销、撤销相关许可证明文件。  
饲料、饲料添加剂生产企业销售的饲料、饲料添加剂未附具产品质量检验合格证或者包装、标签不符合规定的，由县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门责令改正；情节严重的，没收违法所得和违法销售的产品，可以处违法销售的产品货值金额30%以下罚款。

**Article 41** Where a feed or feed additive production enterprise fails to implement the procurement, production and sales record system or the product sample retention observation system in accordance with these Regulations, the feed administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan; if it refuses to make corrections, its illegal gains, the illegally produced products, and the feed materials, single feeds, feed additives, medicated feed additives, additive premixes used for the illegal production of feed, as well as the raw materials used for the illegal production of feed additives shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the licensing authority may revoke or cancel the relevant license documents.  
Where the feed or feed additive sold by a feed or feed additive production enterprise is not accompanied by a product quality inspection certificate, or the packaging and label do not conform to the provisions, the feed administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, the illegal gains and the illegally sold products shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than 30% of the value of the illegally sold products may be imposed.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　不符合[本条例第二十二条](#art-22)规定的条件经营饲料、饲料添加剂的，由县级人民政府饲料管理部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，没收违法所得和违法经营的产品，违法经营的产品货值金额不足1万元的，并处2000元以上2万元以下罚款，货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额2倍以上5倍以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停止经营，并通知工商行政管理部门，由工商行政管理部门吊销营业执照。

**Article 42** Where any person engages in the operation of feed or feed additives without meeting the conditions set forth in Article 22 of these Regulations, the feed administrative department of the people's government at the county level shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, its illegal gains and the illegally operated products shall be confiscated, and, if the value of the illegally operated products is less than RMB 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value of the illegally operated products is not less than RMB 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the value of the illegally operated products shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to cease business operations, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall be notified, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke its business license.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　饲料、饲料添加剂经营者有下列行为之一的，由县级人民政府饲料管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得和违法经营的产品，违法经营的产品货值金额不足1万元的，并处2000元以上2万元以下罚款，货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额2倍以上5倍以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停止经营，并通知工商行政管理部门，由工商行政管理部门吊销营业执照；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)对饲料、饲料添加剂进行再加工或者添加物质的；  
(二)经营无产品标签、无生产许可证、无产品质量检验合格证的饲料、饲料添加剂的；  
(三)经营无产品批准文号的饲料添加剂、添加剂预混合饲料的；  
(四)经营用国务院农业行政主管部门公布的饲料原料目录、饲料添加剂品种目录和药物饲料添加剂品种目录以外的物质生产的饲料的；  
(五)经营未取得新饲料、新饲料添加剂证书的新饲料、新饲料添加剂或者未取得饲料、饲料添加剂进口登记证的进口饲料、进口饲料添加剂以及禁用的饲料、饲料添加剂的。

**Article 43** Where any feed or feed additive operator commits any of the following acts, the feed administrative department of the people's government at the county level shall order it to make corrections, confiscate its illegal gains and the illegally operated products; if the value of the illegally operated products is less than RMB 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value of the illegally operated products is not less than RMB 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the value of the illegally operated products shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to cease business operations, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall be notified, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke its business license; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Further processing or adding substances to feed or feed additives;  
(2) Operating feed or feed additives without product labels, without production licenses, or without product quality inspection certificates;  
(3) Operating feed additives or additive premixes without product approval numbers;  
(4) Operating feed produced from substances other than those listed in the feed materials catalogue, feed additives catalogue and medicated feed additives catalogue published by the administrative department for agriculture of the State Council;  
(5) Operating new feed or new feed additives for which a new feed or new feed additive certificate has not been obtained, or imported feed or imported feed additives for which an import registration certificate for feed or feed additives has not been obtained, as well as prohibited feed or feed additives.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　饲料、饲料添加剂经营者有下列行为之一的，由县级人民政府饲料管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得和违法经营的产品，并处2000元以上1万元以下罚款：  
(一)对饲料、饲料添加剂进行拆包、分装的；  
(二)不依照本条例规定实行产品购销台账制度的；  
(三)经营的饲料、饲料添加剂失效、霉变或者超过保质期的。

**Article 44** Where any feed or feed additive operator commits any of the following acts, the feed administrative department of the people's government at the county level shall order it to make corrections, confiscate its illegal gains and the illegally operated products, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 yuan:  
(1) Unpacking or repackaging feed or feed additives;  
(2) Failing to implement the product purchase and sales ledger system in accordance with these Regulations;  
(3) Operating feed or feed additives that are ineffective, moldy, or beyond their shelf life.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　对[本条例第二十八条](#art-28)规定的饲料、饲料添加剂，生产企业不主动召回的，由县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门责令召回，并监督生产企业对召回的产品予以无害化处理或者销毁；情节严重的，没收违法所得，并处应召回的产品货值金额1倍以上3倍以下罚款，可以由发证机关吊销、撤销相关许可证明文件；生产企业对召回的产品不予以无害化处理或者销毁的，由县级人民政府饲料管理部门代为销毁，所需费用由生产企业承担。  
对[本条例第二十八条](#art-28)规定的饲料、饲料添加剂，经营者不停止销售的，由县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门责令停止销售；拒不停止销售的，没收违法所得，处1000元以上5万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停止经营，并通知工商行政管理部门，由工商行政管理部门吊销营业执照。

**Article 45** Where for the feed or feed additives specified in Article 28 of these Regulations, the production enterprise fails to voluntarily recall them, the feed administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to recall them, and supervise the production enterprise in carrying out harmless treatment or destruction of the recalled products; if the circumstances are serious, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the value of the products that shall be recalled shall be imposed, and the licensing authority may revoke or cancel the relevant license documents; if the production enterprise fails to carry out harmless treatment or destruction of the recalled products, the feed administrative department of the people's government at the county level shall destroy them on its behalf, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the production enterprise.  
Where for the feed or feed additives specified in Article 28 of these Regulations, the operator fails to stop selling them, the feed administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to stop selling; if it refuses to stop selling, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than RMB 1,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to cease business operations, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall be notified, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke its business license.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　饲料、饲料添加剂生产企业、经营者有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门责令停止生产、经营，没收违法所得和违法生产、经营的产品，违法生产、经营的产品货值金额不足1万元的，并处2000元以上2万元以下罚款，货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额2倍以上5倍以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)在生产、经营过程中，以非饲料、非饲料添加剂冒充饲料、饲料添加剂或者以此种饲料、饲料添加剂冒充他种饲料、饲料添加剂的；  
(二)生产、经营无产品质量标准或者不符合产品质量标准的饲料、饲料添加剂的；  
(三)生产、经营的饲料、饲料添加剂与标签标示的内容不一致的。  
饲料、饲料添加剂生产企业有前款规定的行为，情节严重的，由发证机关吊销、撤销相关许可证明文件；饲料、饲料添加剂经营者有前款规定的行为，情节严重的，通知工商行政管理部门，由工商行政管理部门吊销营业执照。

**Article 46** Where any feed or feed additive production enterprise or operator commits any of the following acts, the feed administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to cease production or business operations, confiscate its illegal gains and the illegally produced or operated products; if the value of the illegally produced or operated products is less than RMB 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value of the illegally produced or operated products is not less than RMB 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the value of the illegally produced or operated products shall also be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) During the production or business process, passing off non-feed or non-feed additive substances as feed or feed additives, or passing off one type of feed or feed additive as another type of feed or feed additive;  
(2) Producing or operating feed or feed additives without product quality standards or that do not conform to product quality standards;  
(3) Producing or operating feed or feed additives whose contents are inconsistent with the label markings.  
Where a feed or feed additive production enterprise commits any act provided for in the preceding paragraph and the circumstances are serious, the licensing authority shall revoke or cancel the relevant license documents; where a feed or feed additive operator commits any act provided for in the preceding paragraph and the circumstances are serious, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall be notified, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke its business license.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　养殖者有下列行为之一的，由县级人民政府饲料管理部门没收违法使用的产品和非法添加物质，对单位处1万元以上5万元以下罚款，对个人处5000元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)使用未取得新饲料、新饲料添加剂证书的新饲料、新饲料添加剂或者未取得饲料、饲料添加剂进口登记证的进口饲料、进口饲料添加剂的；  
(二)使用无产品标签、无生产许可证、无产品质量标准、无产品质量检验合格证的饲料、饲料添加剂的；  
(三)使用无产品批准文号的饲料添加剂、添加剂预混合饲料的；  
(四)在饲料或者动物饮用水中添加饲料添加剂，不遵守国务院农业行政主管部门制定的饲料添加剂安全使用规范的；  
(五)使用自行配制的饲料，不遵守国务院农业行政主管部门制定的自行配制饲料使用规范的；  
(六)使用限制使用的物质养殖动物，不遵守国务院农业行政主管部门的限制性规定的；  
(七)在反刍动物饲料中添加乳和乳制品以外的动物源性成分的。  
在饲料或者动物饮用水中添加国务院农业行政主管部门公布禁用的物质以及对人体具有直接或者潜在危害的其他物质，或者直接使用上述物质养殖动物的，由县级以上地方人民政府饲料管理部门责令其对饲喂了违禁物质的动物进行无害化处理，处3万元以上10万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 47** Where any breeder commits any of the following acts, the feed administrative department of the people's government at the county level shall confiscate the illegally used products and the illegally added substances, impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan on the entity, and a fine of not more than RMB 5,000 yuan on the individual; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Using new feed or new feed additives for which a new feed or new feed additive certificate has not been obtained, or using imported feed or imported feed additives for which an import registration certificate for feed or feed additives has not been obtained;  
(2) Using feed or feed additives without product labels, without production licenses, without product quality standards, or without product quality inspection certificates;  
(3) Using feed additives or additive premixes without product approval numbers;  
(4) Adding feed additives to feed or drinking water for animals without complying with the safety specifications for the use of feed additives formulated by the administrative department for agriculture of the State Council;  
(5) Using self-formulated feed without complying with the specifications for the use of self-formulated feed formulated by the administrative department for agriculture of the State Council;  
(6) Using restricted substances for breeding animals without complying with the restrictive provisions of the administrative department for agriculture of the State Council;  
(7) Adding animal-derived ingredients other than milk and dairy products to feed for ruminants.  
Where any person adds to feed or drinking water for animals any substance prohibited by the administrative department for agriculture of the State Council or any other substance that is directly or potentially harmful to the human body, or directly uses the said substances to breed animals, the feed administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the person to carry out harmless treatment of the animals that have been fed with prohibited substances, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 30,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　养殖者对外提供自行配制的饲料的，由县级人民政府饲料管理部门责令改正，处2000元以上2万元以下罚款。

**Article 48** Where any breeder provides self-formulated feed to outsiders, the feed administrative department of the people's government at the county level shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
(一)饲料原料，是指来源于动物、植物、微生物或者矿物质，用于加工制作饲料但不属于饲料添加剂的饲用物质。  
(二)单一饲料，是指来源于一种动物、植物、微生物或者矿物质，用于饲料产品生产的饲料。  
(三)添加剂预混合饲料，是指由两种(类)或者两种(类)以上营养性饲料添加剂为主，与载体或者稀释剂按照一定比例配制的饲料，包括复合预混合饲料、微量元素预混合饲料、维生素预混合饲料。  
(四)浓缩饲料，是指主要由蛋白质、矿物质和饲料添加剂按照一定比例配制的饲料。  
(五)配合饲料，是指根据养殖动物营养需要，将多种饲料原料和饲料添加剂按照一定比例配制的饲料。  
(六)精料补充料，是指为补充草食动物的营养，将多种饲料原料和饲料添加剂按照一定比例配制的饲料。  
(七)营养性饲料添加剂，是指为补充饲料营养成分而掺入饲料中的少量或者微量物质，包括饲料级氨基酸、维生素、矿物质微量元素、酶制剂、非蛋白氮等。  
(八)一般饲料添加剂，是指为保证或者改善饲料品质、提高饲料利用率而掺入饲料中的少量或者微量物质。  
(九)药物饲料添加剂，是指为预防、治疗动物疾病而掺入载体或者稀释剂的兽药的预混合物质。  
(十)许可证明文件，是指新饲料、新饲料添加剂证书，饲料、饲料添加剂进口登记证，饲料、饲料添加剂生产许可证，饲料添加剂、添加剂预混合饲料产品批准文号。

**Article 49** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) "Feed materials" means substances intended for use in the processing and production of feed, derived from animals, plants, microorganisms or minerals, but not belonging to feed additives.  
(2) "Single feed" means feed derived from one kind of animal, plant, microorganism or mineral, used for the production of feed products.  
(3) "Additive premix" means feed prepared mainly from two or more types of nutritional feed additives, mixed with a carrier or diluent in a certain proportion, including compound premix, trace mineral premix, and vitamin premix.  
(4) "Concentrated feed" means feed mainly prepared from protein, minerals and feed additives in a certain proportion.  
(5) "Formula feed" means feed prepared by mixing a variety of feed materials and feed additives in a certain proportion according to the nutritional needs of breeding animals.  
(6) "Concentrate supplement" means feed prepared by mixing a variety of feed materials and feed additives in a certain proportion to supplement the nutrition of herbivores.  
(7) "Nutritional feed additives" means small or trace amounts of substances added to feed to supplement its nutritional components, including feed-grade amino acids, vitamins, trace minerals, enzyme preparations, non-protein nitrogen, etc.  
(8) "General feed additives" means small or trace amounts of substances added to feed to ensure or improve the quality of feed and increase feed utilization rate.  
(9) "Medicated feed additives" means premixed substances of veterinary drugs that are mixed with carriers or diluents for the prevention or treatment of animal diseases.  
(10) "License documents" means the new feed or new feed additive certificate, the import registration certificate for feed or feed additives, the production license for feed or feed additives, and the product approval number for feed additives or additive premixes.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　药物饲料添加剂的管理，依照《兽药管理条例》的规定执行。

**Article 50** The administration of medicated feed additives shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on the Administration of Veterinary Drugs.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　本条例自2012年5月1日起施行。

**Article 51** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2012.
