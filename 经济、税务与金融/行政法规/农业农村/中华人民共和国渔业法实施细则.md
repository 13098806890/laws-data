### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国渔业法》(以下简称《渔业法》)第三十四条的规定，制定本实施细则。

**Article 1** These Detailed Implementation Rules are formulated in accordance with the provisions of Article 34 of the Fisheries Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Fisheries Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　《渔业法》及本实施细则中下列用语的含义是：  
(一)“中华人民共和国的内水”，是指中华人民共和国领海基线向陆一侧的海域和江河、湖泊等内陆水域。  
(二)“中华人民共和国管辖的一切其他海域”，是指根据中华人民共和国法律，中华人民共和国缔结、参加的国际条约、协定或者其他有关国际法，而由中华人民共和国管辖的海域。  
(三)“渔业水域”，是指中华人民共和国管辖水域中鱼、虾、蟹、贝类的产卵场、索饵场、越冬场、洄游通道和鱼、虾、蟹、贝、藻类及其他水生动植物的养殖场所。

**Article 2** For the purposes of the Fisheries Law and these Detailed Implementation Rules, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them below:  
(1) "Inland waters of the People's Republic of China" means the sea areas on the landward side of the baseline of the territorial sea of the People's Republic of China and the inland waters such as rivers and lakes.  
(2) "All other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China" means the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China in accordance with the laws of the People's Republic of China, the international treaties and agreements concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, or other relevant international law.  
(3) "Fishery waters" means the spawning grounds, feeding grounds, wintering grounds, migration channels of fish, shrimp, crab, and shellfish, and the breeding grounds of fish, shrimp, crab, shellfish, algae, and other aquatic animals and plants within the waters under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.

### 第二章　渔业的监督管理

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对渔业的监督管理，实行统一领导、分级管理。  
国务院划定的“机动渔船底拖网禁渔区线”外侧，属于中华人民共和国管辖海域的渔业，由国务院渔业行政主管部门及其所属的海区渔政管理机构监督管理；“机动渔船底拖网禁渔区线”内侧海域的渔业，除国家另有规定者外，由毗邻海域的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门监督管理。  
内陆水域渔业，按照行政区划由当地县级以上地方人民政府渔业行政主管部门监督管理；跨行政区域的内陆水域渔业，由有关县级以上地方人民政府协商制定管理办法，或者由上一级人民政府渔业行政主管部门及其所属的渔政监督管理机构监督管理；跨省、自治区、直辖市的大型江河的渔业，可以由国务院渔业行政主管部门监督管理。  
重要的、洄游性的共用渔业资源，由国家统一管理；定居性的、小宗的渔业资源，由地方人民政府渔业行政主管部门管理。

**Article 3** The State shall exercise unified leadership and graded administration over the supervision and administration of fisheries.  
The fisheries in the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China outside the "Demarcation Line for Motor Trawl Prohibited Fishing Areas" delineated by the State Council shall be supervised and administered by the competent fisheries administrative department of the State Council and its regional fishery administrative agencies; the fisheries in the sea areas inside the said demarcation line, unless otherwise provided by the State, shall be supervised and administered by the competent fisheries administrative departments of the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government adjacent to such sea areas.  
The fisheries in inland waters shall be supervised and administered by the competent fisheries administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level according to the administrative divisions; the fisheries in inland waters crossing administrative divisions shall be supervised and administered by the relevant local people's governments at or above the county level through consultation to formulate management measures, or by the competent fisheries administrative department of the people's government at the next higher level and its fishery supervision and administration agency; the fisheries in large rivers crossing provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government may be supervised and administered by the competent fisheries administrative department of the State Council.  
Important and migratory shared fishery resources shall be managed uniformly by the State; sedentary and minor fishery resources shall be managed by the competent fisheries administrative departments of local people's governments.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　“机动渔船底拖网禁渔区线”内侧海域的渔业，由有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门协商划定监督管理范围；划定监督管理范围有困难的，可划叠区或者共管区管理，必要时由国务院渔业行政主管部门决定。

**Article 4** The fisheries in the sea areas inside the "Demarcation Line for Motor Trawl Prohibited Fishing Areas" shall be supervised and administered by the competent fisheries administrative departments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government through consultation to delineate the scope of supervision and administration; where difficulties arise in delineating the scope, overlapping areas or joint management areas may be designated, and if necessary, the decision shall be made by the competent fisheries administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　渔场和渔汛生产，应当以渔业资源可捕量为依据，按照有利于保护、增殖和合理利用渔业资源，优先安排邻近地区、兼顾其他地区的原则，统筹安排。  
舟山渔场冬季带鱼汛，浙江渔场大黄鱼汛，闽东、闽中渔场大黄鱼汛，吕泗渔场大黄鱼、小黄鱼、鲳鱼汛，渤海渔场秋季对虾汛等主要渔场、渔汛和跨海区管理线的捕捞作业，由国务院渔业行政主管部门或其授权单位安排。

**Article 5** The production in fishing grounds and fishing seasons shall be based on the allowable catch of fishery resources, and shall be arranged in an overall manner in accordance with the principles of facilitating the protection, enhancement, and rational utilization of fishery resources, giving priority to adjacent areas while taking other areas into consideration.  
The fishing operations in major fishing grounds and fishing seasons such as the winter hairtail season in the Zhoushan Fishing Ground, the large yellow croaker season in the Zhejiang Fishing Ground, the large yellow croaker season in the Mindong and Minzhong Fishing Grounds, the large yellow croaker, small yellow croaker, and pomfret season in the Lusi Fishing Ground, and the autumn prawn season in the Bohai Fishing Ground, as well as those crossing the management lines of different sea areas, shall be arranged by the competent fisheries administrative department of the State Council or its authorized units.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院渔业行政主管部门的渔政渔港监督管理机构，代表国家行使渔政渔港监督管理权。  
国务院渔业行政主管部门在黄渤海、东海、南海三个海区设渔政监督管理机构；在重要渔港、边境水域和跨省、自治区、直辖市的大型江河，根据需要设渔政渔港监督管理机构。

**Article 6** The fishery administration and fishing port supervision and administration agency of the competent fisheries administrative department of the State Council shall, on behalf of the State, exercise the power of fishery administration and fishing port supervision and administration.  
The competent fisheries administrative department of the State Council shall set up fishery administration agencies in the three sea areas of Huang-Bohai, East China Sea, and South China Sea; and shall set up fishery administration and fishing port supervision and administration agencies in important fishing ports, border waters, and large rivers crossing provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government as necessary.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　渔政检查人员有权对各种渔业及渔业船舶的证件、渔船、渔具、渔获物和捕捞方法，进行检查。  
渔政检查人员经国务院渔业行政主管部门或者省级人民政府渔业行政主管部门考核，合格者方可执行公务。

**Article 7** Fishery inspection personnel shall have the right to inspect the certificates, fishing vessels, fishing gear, catches, and fishing methods of various fisheries and fishing vessels.  
Fishery inspection personnel shall be assessed by the competent fisheries administrative department of the State Council or the competent fisheries administrative department of the provincial people's government, and only those who pass the assessment may perform their duties.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　渔业行政主管部门及其所属的渔政监督管理机构，应当与公安、海监、交通、环保、工商行政管理等有关部门相互协作，监督检查渔业法规的施行。

**Article 8** The competent fisheries administrative departments and their fishery supervision and administration agencies shall cooperate with the public security, maritime surveillance, transportation, environmental protection, and administrative departments for industry and commerce and other relevant departments to supervise and inspect the implementation of fisheries laws and regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　群众性护渔管理组织，应当在当地县级以上人民政府渔业行政主管部门的业务指导下，依法开展护渔管理工作。

**Article 9** Mass organizations for fishery protection and management shall, under the professional guidance of the competent fisheries administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level, carry out fishery protection and management work according to law.

### 第三章　养殖业

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　使用全民所有的水面、滩涂，从事养殖生产的全民所有制单位和集体所有制单位，应当向县级以上地方人民政府申请养殖使用证。  
全民所有的水面、滩涂在一县行政区域内的，由该县人民政府核发养殖使用证；跨县的，由有关县协商核发养殖使用证，必要时由上级人民政府决定核发养殖使用证。

**Article 10** Units under ownership by the whole people and units under collective ownership that use water surfaces and tidal flats owned by the whole people for aquaculture production shall apply to the local people's government at or above the county level for an aquaculture use permit.  
Where the water surfaces and tidal flats owned by the whole people are within the administrative area of one county, the people's government of that county shall issue the aquaculture use permit; where they cross counties, the relevant counties shall issue the permit through consultation, and if necessary, the people's government at the higher level shall decide to issue the permit.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　领取养殖使用证的单位，无正当理由未从事养殖生产，或者放养量低于当地同类养殖水域平均放养量60%的，应当视为荒芜。

**Article 11** Any unit that has obtained an aquaculture use permit but fails to engage in aquaculture production without justifiable reasons, or whose stocking density is less than 60% of the average stocking density of similar aquaculture waters in the local area, shall be deemed as having abandoned the waters.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　全民所有的水面、滩涂中的鱼、虾、蟹、贝、藻类的自然产卵场、繁殖场、索饵场及重要的洄游通道必须予以保护，不得划作养殖场所。

**Article 12** The natural spawning grounds, breeding grounds, feeding grounds, and important migration passages of fish, shrimp, crab, shellfish, and algae in water surfaces and tidal flats owned by the whole people must be protected and shall not be designated as aquaculture sites.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家建设征用集体所有的水面、滩涂，按照国家土地管理法规办理。

**Article 13** The requisition of water surfaces and tidal flats owned by collectives for state construction shall be handled in accordance with the state's land management laws and regulations.

### 第四章　捕捞业

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　近海渔场与外海渔场的划分：  
(一)渤海、黄海为近海渔场。  
(二)下列四个基点之间连线内侧海域为东海近海渔场；四个基点之间连线外侧海域为东海外海渔场。四个基点是：  
1.北纬33度，东经125度；  
2.北纬29度，东经125度；  
3.北纬28度，东经124度30分；  
4.北纬27度，东经123度。  
(三)下列两条等深线之内侧海域为南海近海渔场；两条等深线之外侧海域为南海外海渔场。两条等深线是：  
1.东经112度以东之80米等深线；  
2.东经112度以西之100米等深线。

**Article 14** Division of inshore and offshore fishing grounds:  
(1) The Bohai Sea and the Yellow Sea are inshore fishing grounds.  
(2) The sea areas inside the connecting lines among the following four base points are the East China Sea inshore fishing grounds; the sea areas outside the connecting lines among the four base points are the East China Sea offshore fishing grounds. The four base points are:  
1. 33°N, 125°E;  
2. 29°N, 125°E;  
3. 28°N, 124°30'E;  
4. 27°N, 123°E.  
(3) The sea areas inside the following two isobaths are the South China Sea inshore fishing grounds; the sea areas outside the two isobaths are the South China Sea offshore fishing grounds. The two isobaths are:  
1. The 80-meter isobath east of 112°E;  
2. The 100-meter isobath west of 112°E.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家对捕捞业，实行捕捞许可制度。  
从事外海、远洋捕捞业的，由经营者提出申请，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门审核后，报国务院渔业行政主管部门批准。从事外海生产的渔船，必须按照批准的海域和渔期作业，不得擅自进入近海捕捞。  
近海大型拖网、围网作业的捕捞许可证，由国务院渔业行政主管部门批准发放；近海其他作业的捕捞许可证，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门按照国家下达的船网工具控制指标批准发放。  
内陆水域的捕捞许可证，由县级以上地方人民政府渔业行政主管部门批准发放。  
捕捞许可证的格式，由国务院渔业行政主管部门制定。

**Article 15** The state shall implement a fishing license system for the fishing industry.  
For those engaged in offshore and distant-water fishing, the operator shall submit an application, which shall be examined by the competent fishery administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and then reported to the competent fishery administrative department of the State Council for approval. Fishing vessels engaged in offshore production must operate in accordance with the approved sea areas and fishing seasons, and shall not enter inshore waters for fishing without authorization.  
Fishing licenses for inshore large-scale trawl and purse seine operations shall be approved and issued by the competent fishery administrative department of the State Council; fishing licenses for other inshore operations shall be approved and issued by the competent fishery administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the vessel and gear control targets assigned by the state.  
Fishing licenses for inland waters shall be approved and issued by the competent fishery administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level.  
The format of fishing licenses shall be formulated by the competent fishery administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　在中华人民共和国管辖水域，外商投资的渔业企业，未经国务院有关主管部门批准，不得从事近海捕捞业。

**Article 16** Foreign-invested fishery enterprises in the waters under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China shall not engage in inshore fishing without the approval of the relevant competent department of the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　有下列情形之一的，不得发放捕捞许可证：  
(一)使用破坏渔业资源、被明令禁止使用的渔具或者捕捞方法的；  
(二)未按国家规定办理批准手续，制造、更新改造、购置或者进口捕捞渔船的；  
(三)未按国家规定领取渔业船舶证书、航行签证簿、职务船员证书、船舶户口簿、渔民证等证件的。

**Article 17** A fishing license shall not be issued under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The use of fishing gear or fishing methods that damage fishery resources and are expressly prohibited;  
(2) Manufacturing, renovating, purchasing, or importing fishing vessels without going through the approval procedures as required by state regulations;  
(3) Failing to obtain, as required by state regulations, such documents as the fishing vessel certificate, navigation visa book, certificate for officer crew members, vessel household register, and fisherman certificate.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　娱乐性游钓和在尚未养殖、管理的滩涂手工采集零星水产品的，不必申请捕捞许可证，但应当加强管理，防止破坏渔业资源。具体管理办法由县级以上人民政府制定。

**Article 18** Those engaged in recreational angling and the manual collection of sporadic aquatic products on tidal flats that have not yet been used for aquaculture or managed shall not be required to apply for a fishing license, but management shall be strengthened to prevent damage to fishery resources. The specific management measures shall be formulated by the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　因科学研究等特殊需要，在禁渔区、禁渔期捕捞，或者使用禁用的渔具、捕捞方法，或者捕捞重点保护的渔业资源品种，必须经省级以上人民政府渔业行政主管部门批准。

**Article 19** Where, due to special needs such as scientific research, it is necessary to fish in a closed fishing area or during a closed fishing season, or to use prohibited fishing gear or methods, or to catch fishery resources species under special protection, approval must be obtained from the competent fishery administrative department of the people's government at or above the provincial level.

### 第五章　渔业资源的增殖和保护

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　禁止使用电力、鱼鹰捕鱼和敲䑩作业。在特定水域确有必要使用电力或者鱼鹰捕鱼时，必须经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门批准。

**Article 20** Fishing with electric current, cormorants, and the operation of beating boats (qiao gu) are prohibited. Where it is truly necessary in specific waters to use electric current or cormorants for fishing, approval must be obtained from the competent fishery administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上人民政府渔业行政主管部门，应当依照本实施细则第三条规定的管理权限，确定重点保护的渔业资源品种及采捕标准。在重要鱼、虾、蟹、贝、藻类，以及其他重要水生生物的产卵场、索饵场、越冬场和洄游通道，规定禁渔区和禁渔期，禁止使用或者限制使用的渔具和捕捞方法，最小网目尺寸，以及制定其他保护渔业资源的措施。

**Article 21** The competent administrative department for fisheries of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the administrative competence provided in Article 3 of these Detailed Rules, determine the species of fishery resources to be given priority for protection and the standards for catching and collecting. In the spawning grounds, feeding grounds, wintering grounds and migration passages of important fish, shrimp, crab, shellfish, algae and other important aquatic organisms, it shall prescribe no-fishing zones and closed seasons, prohibit or restrict the use of fishing gear and fishing methods, specify minimum mesh sizes, and formulate other measures for the protection of fishery resources.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　在“机动渔船底拖网禁渔区线”内侧建造人工鱼礁的，必须经有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门或其授权单位批准。  
建造人工鱼礁，应当避开主要航道和重要锚地，并通知有关交通和海洋管理部门。

**Article 22** The construction of artificial reefs inside the 'prohibited fishing area line for motorized bottom trawlers' must be approved by the competent administrative department for fisheries of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or its authorized entity.  
The construction of artificial reefs shall avoid main navigational channels and important anchorages, and the relevant transportation and marine administration departments shall be notified.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　定置渔业一般不得跨县作业。县级以上人民政府渔业行政主管部门应当限制其网桩数量、作业场所，并规定禁渔期。海洋定置渔业，不得越出“机动渔船底拖网禁渔区线”。

**Article 23** Fixed fisheries shall generally not operate across counties. The competent administrative department for fisheries of the people's government at or above the county level shall restrict the number of net stakes and operating sites thereof, and prescribe closed seasons. Marine fixed fisheries shall not cross the 'prohibited fishing area line for motorized bottom trawlers'.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　因养殖或者其他特殊需要，捕捞鳗鲡、鲥鱼、中华绒螯蟹、真鲷、石斑鱼等有重要经济价值的水生动物苗种或者禁捕的怀卵亲体的，必须经国务院渔业行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门批准，并领取专项许可证件，方可在指定区域和时间内，按照批准限额捕捞。捕捞其他有重要经济价值的水生动物苗种的批准权，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门规定。

**Article 24** Where, for aquaculture or other special needs, the catching of fingerlings of aquatic animals of important economic value such as eel, Reeves shad, Chinese mitten crab, red sea bream, grouper, or the catching of gravid parent bodies that are prohibited from being caught must be approved by the competent administrative department for fisheries of the State Council or the competent administrative department for fisheries of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and a special permit shall be obtained before the catching may be conducted in the designated areas and periods and within the approved quota. The authority to approve the catching of fingerlings of other aquatic animals of important economic value shall be prescribed by the competent administrative department for fisheries of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　禁止捕捞中国对虾苗种和春季亲虾。因养殖需要中国对虾怀卵亲体的，应当限期由养殖单位自行培育，期限及管理办法由国务院渔业行政主管部门制定。

**Article 25** The catching of fingerlings of Chinese prawn and parent prawns in spring shall be prohibited. Where gravid parent bodies of Chinese prawn are needed for aquaculture, they shall be cultivated by the aquaculture unit within a prescribed time limit, and the time limit and the management measures shall be formulated by the competent administrative department for fisheries of the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　任何单位和个人，在鱼、虾、蟹、贝幼苗的重点产区直接引水、用水的，应当采取避开幼苗的密集期、密集区，或者设置网栅等保护措施。

**Article 26** Any unit or individual that directly diverts or uses water in the key production areas of fish, shrimp, crab, and shellfish seedlings shall adopt protective measures such as avoiding peak periods and dense areas of seedlings or installing net screens.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　各级渔业行政主管部门，应当对渔业水域污染情况进行监测；渔业环境保护监测网，应当纳入全国环境监测网络。因污染造成渔业损失的，应当由渔政渔港监督管理部门协同环保部门调查处理。

**Article 27** The competent administrative departments for fisheries at all levels shall monitor the pollution of fishery waters; the fishery environmental protection monitoring network shall be incorporated into the national environmental monitoring network. Where fishery losses are caused by pollution, the fishery administration and fishing port supervision and administration departments shall, in coordination with the environmental protection departments, investigate and handle the matter.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　在重点渔业水域不得从事拆船业。在其他渔业水域从事拆船业，造成渔业资源损害的，由拆船单位依照有关规定负责赔偿。

**Article 28** No shipbreaking industry shall be engaged in key fishery waters. Where shipbreaking is engaged in other fishery waters and causes damage to fishery resources, the shipbreaking unit shall be liable for compensation in accordance with relevant provisions.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　依照[《渔业法》第二十八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/农业、资源与能源/中华人民共和国渔业法.md#art-28)规定处以罚款的，按下列规定执行：  
(一)炸鱼、毒鱼的，违反关于禁渔区、禁渔期的规定进行捕捞的，擅自捕捞国家规定禁止捕捞的珍贵水生动物的，在内陆水域处五十元至五千元罚款，在海洋处五百元至五万元罚款；  
(二)敲䑩作业的，处一千元至五万元罚款；  
(三)未经批准使用鱼鹰捕鱼的，处五十元至二百元罚款；  
(四)未经批准使用电力捕鱼的，在内陆水域处二百元至一千元罚款，在海洋处五百元至三千元罚款；  
(五)使用小于规定的最小网目尺寸的网具进行捕捞的，处五十元至一千元罚款。

**Article 29** Where a fine is imposed in accordance with Article 28 of the Fisheries Law, it shall be executed according to the following provisions:  
(1) For blasting fish, poisoning fish, fishing in violation of the provisions on no-fishing zones and closed seasons, or unauthorized catching of precious aquatic animals that the State prohibits from being caught, a fine of 50 to 5,000 yuan shall be imposed in inland waters, and a fine of 500 to 50,000 yuan shall be imposed in marine waters;  
(2) For the operation of knocking fishing, a fine of 1,000 to 50,000 yuan shall be imposed;  
(3) For unauthorized use of cormorants to catch fish, a fine of 50 to 200 yuan shall be imposed;  
(4) For unauthorized use of electric power to catch fish, a fine of 200 to 1,000 yuan shall be imposed in inland waters, and a fine of 500 to 3,000 yuan shall be imposed in marine waters;  
(5) For using nets with a mesh size smaller than the prescribed minimum, a fine of 50 to 1,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　依照[《渔业法》第二十九条](../../../经济、税务与金融/农业、资源与能源/中华人民共和国渔业法.md#art-29)规定处以罚款的，按罚款一千元以下执行。

**Article 30** Where a fine is imposed in accordance with Article 29 of the Fisheries Law, it shall be executed by imposing a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　依照[《渔业法》第三十条](../../../经济、税务与金融/农业、资源与能源/中华人民共和国渔业法.md#art-30)规定需处以罚款的，按下列规定执行：  
(一)内陆渔业非机动渔船，处五十元至一百五十元罚款；  
(二)内陆渔业机动渔船和海洋渔业非机动渔船，处一百元至五百元罚款；  
(三)海洋渔业机动渔船，处二百元至二万元罚款。

**Article 31** Where a fine is to be imposed in accordance with Article 30 of the Fisheries Law, the following provisions shall be executed:  
(1) For inland fishery non-motorized fishing vessels, a fine of not less than 50 yuan but not more than 150 yuan shall be imposed;  
(2) For inland fishery motorized fishing vessels and marine fishery non-motorized fishing vessels, a fine of not less than 100 yuan but not more than 500 yuan shall be imposed;  
(3) For marine fishery motorized fishing vessels, a fine of not less than 200 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　依照[《渔业法》第三十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/农业、资源与能源/中华人民共和国渔业法.md#art-31)规定需处以罚款的，按下列规定执行：  
(一)内陆渔业非机动渔船，处二十五元至五十元罚款；  
(二)内陆渔业机动渔船和海洋渔业非机动渔船，处五十元至一百元罚款；  
(三)海洋渔业机动渔船，处五十元至三千元罚款；  
(四)外海渔船擅自进入近海捕捞的，处三千元至二万元罚款。

**Article 32** Where a fine is to be imposed in accordance with Article 31 of the Fisheries Law, the following provisions shall be executed:  
(1) For inland fishery non-motorized fishing vessels, a fine of not less than 25 yuan but not more than 50 yuan shall be imposed;  
(2) For inland fishery motorized fishing vessels and marine fishery non-motorized fishing vessels, a fine of not less than 50 yuan but not more than 100 yuan shall be imposed;  
(3) For marine fishery motorized fishing vessels, a fine of not less than 50 yuan but not more than 3,000 yuan shall be imposed;  
(4) For offshore fishing vessels that enter inshore waters without permission to engage in fishing, a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　买卖、出租或者以其他形式非法转让以及涂改捕捞许可证的，没收违法所得，吊销捕捞许可证，可以并处一百元至一千元罚款。

**Article 33** Whoever purchases, leases, or illegally transfers in any other form, or alters a fishing license shall have the illegal gains confiscated, the fishing license revoked, and may also be fined not less than 100 yuan but not more than 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　依照[《渔业法》第二十八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/农业、资源与能源/中华人民共和国渔业法.md#art-28)、第三十条、第三十一条、第三十二条规定需处以罚款的，对船长或者单位负责人可以视情节另处一百元至五百元罚款。

**Article 34** Where a fine is to be imposed in accordance with Articles 28, 30, 31, and 32 of the Fisheries Law, the master of the vessel or the person in charge of the unit may, depending on the circumstances, be additionally fined not less than 100 yuan but not more than 500 yuan.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　未按《渔业法》和本实施细则有关规定，采取保护措施，造成渔业资源损失的，围湖造田或者未经批准围垦沿海滩涂的，应当依法承担责任。

**Article 35** Any person who fails to take protective measures in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Fisheries Law and these Implementing Rules, thereby causing damage to fishery resources, or who reclaims land from lakes or reclaims coastal tidelands without approval, shall bear liability according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　外商投资的渔业企业，违反本实施细则第十六条规定，没收渔获物和违法所得，可以并处三千元至五万元罚款。

**Article 36** Where a fishery enterprise with foreign investment violates the provisions of Article 16 of these Implementing Rules, the catch and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan may also be imposed.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　外国人、外国渔船违反[《渔业法》第八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/农业、资源与能源/中华人民共和国渔业法.md#art-8)规定，擅自进入中华人民共和国管辖水域从事渔业生产或者渔业资源调查活动的，渔业行政主管部门或其所属的渔政监督管理机构应当令其离开或者将其驱逐，并可处以罚款和没收渔获物、渔具。

**Article 37** Where any foreigner or foreign fishing vessel violates the provisions of Article 8 of the Fisheries Law by entering the waters under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China without permission to engage in fishery production or fishery resource investigation activities, the competent fishery administrative department or its subordinate fishery supervision and administration agency shall order them to leave or expel them, and may impose a fine and confiscate the catch and fishing gear.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　渔业行政主管部门或其所属的渔政监督管理机构进行处罚时，应当填发处罚决定书；处以罚款及没收渔具、渔获物和违法所得的，应当开具凭证，并在捕捞许可证上载明。

**Article 38** When imposing a penalty, the competent fishery administrative department or its subordinate fishery supervision and administration agency shall issue a penalty decision; where a fine is imposed and fishing gear, catch, and illegal gains are confiscated, a receipt shall be issued and the matter shall be recorded in the fishing license.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　有下列行为之一的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚条例》的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)拒绝、阻碍渔政检查人员依法执行职务的；  
(二)偷窃、哄抢或者破坏渔具、渔船、渔获物的。

**Article 39** Any person who commits one of the following acts shall be punished by the public security organ in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Administrative Penalties for Public Security; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated by the judicial organ according to law:  
(1) Refusing or obstructing a fishery inspection officer from lawfully performing his or her duties;  
(2) Stealing, plundering, or damaging fishing gear, fishing vessels, or catch.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　渔政检查人员玩忽职守或者徇私枉法的，由其所在单位或者上级主管部门给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 40** Where a fishery inspection officer neglects his or her duties or engages in malpractice for personal gain, an administrative sanction shall be imposed by the unit to which he or she belongs or by the competent authority at a higher level; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　本实施细则由农牧渔业部负责解释。

**Article 41** The Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Husbandry and Fisheries shall be responsible for the interpretation of these Implementing Rules.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　本实施细则自发布之日起施行。

**Article 42** These Implementing Rules shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
