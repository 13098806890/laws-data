**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2006-08-23  
**生效日期**：2006-08-23

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了科学、有效地组织实施全国农业普查，保障农业普查数据的准确性和及时性，根据《中华人民共和国统计法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of organizing and implementing the national agricultural census scientifically and effectively, and ensuring the accuracy and timeliness of agricultural census data.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　农业普查的目的，是全面掌握我国农业、农村和农民的基本情况，为研究制定经济社会发展战略、规划、政策和科学决策提供依据，并为农业生产经营者和社会公众提供统计信息服务。

**Article 2** The purpose of the agricultural census is to comprehensively grasp the basic situation of agriculture, rural areas, and farmers in China, to provide a basis for researching and formulating economic and social development strategies, plans, policies, and scientific decision-making, and to provide statistical information services for agricultural producers and operators and the public.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　农业普查工作按照全国统一领导、部门分工协作、地方分级负责的原则组织实施。

**Article 3** The agricultural census work shall be organized and implemented in accordance with the principles of unified national leadership, division of labor and cooperation among departments, and hierarchical responsibility at local levels.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家机关、社会团体以及与农业普查有关的单位和个人，应当依照《中华人民共和国统计法》和本条例的规定，积极参与并密切配合农业普查工作。

**Article 4** State organs, social organizations, and units and individuals related to the agricultural census shall, in accordance with the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China and these Regulations, actively participate in and closely cooperate with the agricultural census work.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　各级农业普查领导小组办公室(以下简称普查办公室)和普查办公室工作人员、普查指导员、普查员(以下统称普查人员)依法独立行使调查、报告、监督的职权，任何单位和个人不得干涉。  
各地方、各部门、各单位的领导人对普查办公室和普查人员依法提供的农业普查资料不得自行修改，不得强令、授意普查办公室、普查人员和普查对象篡改农业普查资料或者编造虚假数据，不得对拒绝、抵制篡改农业普查资料或者拒绝、抵制编造虚假数据的人员打击报复。

**Article 5** Agricultural census leading group offices at all levels (hereinafter referred to as census offices) and their staff, census supervisors, and enumerators (hereinafter collectively referred to as census personnel) shall independently exercise the powers of investigation, reporting, and supervision according to law, and no unit or individual shall interfere.  
Leaders of various localities, departments, and units shall not modify the agricultural census data lawfully provided by census offices and census personnel on their own, shall not compel or instruct census offices, census personnel, or census respondents to falsify agricultural census data or fabricate false data, and shall not retaliate against persons who refuse or resist the falsification of agricultural census data or the fabrication of false data.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各级宣传部门应当充分利用报刊、广播、电视、互联网和户外广告等媒体，采取多种形式，认真做好农业普查的宣传动员工作。

**Article 6** Publicity departments at all levels shall make full use of media such as newspapers, radio, television, the Internet, and outdoor advertising, adopt various forms, and earnestly carry out the publicity and mobilization work for the agricultural census.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　农业普查所需经费，由中央和地方各级人民政府共同负担，并列入相应年度的财政预算，按时拨付，确保足额到位。  
农业普查经费应当统一管理、专款专用、从严控制支出。

**Article 7** The funds required for the agricultural census shall be jointly borne by the Central and local people's governments at all levels, included in the fiscal budgets of the corresponding years, and allocated on time to ensure full availability.  
The agricultural census funds shall be uniformly managed, used only for designated purposes, and expenditures shall be strictly controlled.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　农业普查每10年进行一次，尾数逢6的年份为普查年度，标准时点为普查年度的12月31日24时。特殊地区的普查登记时间经国务院农业普查领导小组办公室批准，可以适当调整。

**Article 8** The agricultural census shall be conducted once every 10 years, with years ending in the digit 6 as census years, and the standard reference time shall be 24:00 on December 31 of the census year. The census registration time in special regions may be appropriately adjusted with the approval of the Office of the Leading Group for the Agricultural Census of the State Council.

### 第二章　农业普查的对象、范围和内容

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　农业普查对象是在中华人民共和国境内的下列个人和单位：  
(一)农村住户，包括农村农业生产经营户和其他住户；  
(二)城镇农业生产经营户；  
(三)农业生产经营单位；  
(四)村民委员会；  
(五)乡镇人民政府。

**Article 9** The respondents of the agricultural census are the following individuals and units within the territory of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) rural households, including rural agricultural production and operation households and other households;  
(2) urban agricultural production and operation households;  
(3) agricultural production and operation units;  
(4) villagers' committees;  
(5) township people's governments.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　农业普查对象应当如实回答普查人员的询问，按时填报农业普查表，不得虚报、瞒报、拒报和迟报。  
农业普查对象应当配合县级以上人民政府统计机构和国家统计局派出的调查队依法进行的监督检查，如实反映情况，提供有关资料，不得拒绝、推诿和阻挠检查，不得转移、隐匿、篡改、毁弃原始记录、统计台账、普查表、会计资料及其他相关资料。

**Article 10** Agricultural census respondents shall truthfully answer the inquiries of census personnel, fill in the agricultural census forms on time, and shall not make false reports, conceal reports, refuse reports, or delay reports.  
Agricultural census respondents shall cooperate with the supervision and inspection lawfully conducted by the statistical institutions of the people's governments at or above the county level and the survey teams dispatched by the National Bureau of Statistics, truthfully reflect the situation, provide relevant materials, and shall not refuse, prevaricate, or obstruct the inspection, and shall not transfer, conceal, tamper with, or destroy original records, statistical ledgers, census forms, accounting materials, and other relevant materials.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　农业普查行业范围包括：农作物种植业、林业、畜牧业、渔业和农林牧渔服务业。

**Article 11** The industry scope of the agricultural census includes: crop cultivation, forestry, animal husbandry, fishery, and services for agriculture, forestry, animal husbandry, and fishery.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　农业普查内容包括：农业生产条件、农业生产经营活动、农业土地利用、农村劳动力及就业、农村基础设施、农村社会服务、农民生活，以及乡镇、村民委员会和社区环境等情况。  
前款规定的农业普查内容，国务院农业普查领导小组办公室可以根据具体情况进行调整。

**Article 12** The contents of the agricultural census include: agricultural production conditions, agricultural production and operation activities, agricultural land use, rural labor force and employment, rural infrastructure, rural social services, farmers' livelihood, and the circumstances of townships, villagers' committees, and community environment, etc.  
The agricultural census contents specified in the preceding paragraph may be adjusted by the Office of the Leading Group for the Agricultural Census of the State Council according to specific circumstances.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　农业普查采用全面调查的方法。国务院农业普查领导小组办公室可以决定对特定内容采用抽样调查的方法。

**Article 13** The agricultural census shall adopt the method of comprehensive survey. The Office of the Leading Group for the Agricultural Census of the State Council may decide to adopt the method of sample survey for specific contents.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　农业普查采用国家统计分类标准。

**Article 14** The agricultural census shall adopt the national statistical classification standards.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　农业普查方案由国务院农业普查领导小组办公室统一制订。  
省级普查办公室可以根据需要增设农业普查附表，报经国务院农业普查领导小组办公室批准后实施。

**Article 15** The agricultural census plan shall be uniformly formulated by the Office of the Leading Group for the Agricultural Census of the State Council.  
Provincial census offices may, as needed, add supplementary tables for the agricultural census, and implement them after submitting them to the Office of the Leading Group for the Agricultural Census of the State Council for approval.

### 第三章　农业普查的组织实施

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院设立农业普查领导小组及其办公室。国务院农业普查领导小组负责组织和领导全国农业普查工作。国务院农业普查领导小组办公室设在国家统计局，具体负责农业普查日常工作的组织和协调。

**Article 16** The State Council shall establish a leading group for the agricultural census and its office. The Leading Group for the Agricultural Census of the State Council shall be responsible for organizing and leading the national agricultural census work. The Office of the Leading Group for the Agricultural Census of the State Council shall be located in the National Bureau of Statistics and shall be specifically responsible for the organization and coordination of the daily work of the agricultural census.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　地方各级人民政府设立农业普查领导小组及其办公室，按照国务院农业普查领导小组及其办公室的统一规定和要求，负责本行政区域内农业普查的组织实施工作。国家统计局派出的调查队作为农业普查领导小组及其办公室的成员单位，参与农业普查的组织实施工作。  
村民委员会应当在乡镇人民政府的指导下做好本区域内的农业普查工作。

**Article 17** Local people's governments at all levels shall establish leading groups for the agricultural census and their offices, and shall be responsible for the organization and implementation of the agricultural census within their respective administrative regions in accordance with the unified provisions and requirements of the Leading Group for the Agricultural Census of the State Council and its office. The survey teams dispatched by the National Bureau of Statistics shall, as member units of the leading groups for the agricultural census and their offices, participate in the organization and implementation of the agricultural census.  
Villagers' committees shall, under the guidance of township people's governments, do a good job in the agricultural census within their respective areas.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院和地方各级人民政府的有关部门应当积极参与并密切配合普查办公室开展农业普查工作。  
军队、武警部队所属农业生产单位的农业普查工作，由军队、武警部队分别负责组织实施。  
新疆生产建设兵团的农业普查工作，由新疆生产建设兵团农业普查领导小组及其办公室负责组织实施。

**Article 18** Relevant departments of the State Council and local people's governments at all levels shall actively participate in and closely cooperate with the census offices in carrying out the agricultural census work.  
The agricultural census work of agricultural production units under the armed forces and the armed police forces shall be organized and implemented by the armed forces and the armed police forces respectively.  
The agricultural census work of the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps shall be organized and implemented by the leading group for the agricultural census of the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps and its office.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　农村的普查现场登记按普查区进行。普查区以村民委员会管理地域为基础划分，每个普查区可以划分为若干个普查小区。  
城镇的普查现场登记，按照普查方案的规定进行。

**Article 19** The on-site registration of the agricultural census in rural areas shall be conducted by census areas. Census areas shall be divided on the basis of the management territories of villagers' committees, and each census area may be divided into several enumeration districts.  
The on-site registration of the agricultural census in urban areas shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions of the census plan.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　每个普查小区配备一名普查员，负责普查的访问登记工作。每个普查区至少配备一名普查指导员，负责安排、指导和督促检查普查员的工作，也可以直接进行访问登记。  
普查指导员和普查员主要由有较高文化水平的乡村干部、村民小组长和其他当地居民担任。  
普查指导员和普查员应当身体健康、责任心强。

**Article 20** Each enumeration district shall be equipped with one enumerator, responsible for the interview and registration work of the census. Each census area shall be equipped with at least one census supervisor, responsible for arranging, guiding, and supervising the work of the enumerators, and may also conduct direct interview and registration.  
Census supervisors and enumerators shall be mainly composed of village and township cadres, leaders of villagers' groups, and other local residents with a relatively high level of education.  
Census supervisors and enumerators shall be in good health and have a strong sense of responsibility.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　普查办公室根据工作需要，可以聘用或者从其他有关单位借调人员从事农业普查工作。有关单位应当积极推荐符合条件的人员从事农业普查工作。  
聘用人员应当由聘用单位支付劳动报酬。借调人员的工资由原单位支付，其福利待遇保持不变。  
农业普查经费中应当对村普查指导员、普查员安排适当的工作补贴。

**Article 21** Census offices may, based on work needs, employ or second personnel from other relevant units to engage in agricultural census work. Relevant units shall actively recommend qualified personnel to engage in agricultural census work.  
Employed personnel shall have their labor remuneration paid by the employing unit. The salaries of seconded personnel shall be paid by their original units, and their welfare benefits shall remain unchanged.  
Appropriate work subsidies shall be arranged in the agricultural census funds for village census supervisors and enumerators.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　地方普查办公室应当对普查指导员和普查员进行业务培训，并对考核合格的人员颁发全国统一的普查指导员证或者普查员证。

**Article 22** Local census offices shall provide professional training to census supervisors and enumerators, and shall issue nationally unified census supervisor certificates or enumerator certificates to those who have passed the assessments.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　普查人员有权就与农业普查有关的问题询问有关单位和个人，要求有关单位和个人如实提供有关情况和资料、修改不真实的资料。

**Article 23** Census personnel shall have the right to inquire of relevant units and individuals about matters related to the agricultural census, and request that such units and individuals truthfully provide relevant information and materials, and correct untruthful materials.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　普查人员应当坚持实事求是，恪守职业道德，拒绝、抵制农业普查工作中的违法行为。  
普查人员应当严格执行普查方案，不得伪造、篡改普查资料，不得强令、授意普查对象提供虚假的普查资料。  
普查指导员和普查员执行农业普查任务时，应当出示普查指导员证或者普查员证。

**Article 24** Census personnel shall adhere to the principle of seeking truth from facts, abide by professional ethics, and refuse and resist illegal acts in the agricultural census work.  
Census personnel shall strictly implement the census plan, and shall not forge or tamper with census data, and shall not compel or instruct census respondents to provide false census data.  
When carrying out agricultural census tasks, census supervisors and enumerators shall present the census supervisor certificate or enumerator certificate.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　普查员应当依法直接访问普查对象，当场进行询问、填报。普查表填写完成后，应当由普查对象签字或者盖章确认。普查对象应当对其签字或者盖章的普查资料的真实性负责。  
普查人员应当对其负责登记、审核、录入的普查资料与普查对象签字或者盖章的普查资料的一致性负责。  
普查办公室应当对其加工、整理的普查资料的准确性负责。

**Article 25** Enumerators shall, according to law, directly visit the census respondents, conduct inquiries and fill in the forms on the spot. After the census forms are completed, they shall be confirmed by the signature or seal of the census respondent. The census respondent shall be responsible for the authenticity of the census data he or she has signed or sealed.  
Census personnel shall be responsible for the consistency of the census data they are responsible for registering, reviewing, and entering with the census data signed or sealed by the census respondent.  
Census offices shall be responsible for the accuracy of the census data they process and compile.

### 第四章　数据处理和质量控制

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　农业普查数据处理方案和实施办法，由国务院农业普查领导小组办公室制订。  
地方普查办公室应当按照数据处理方案和实施办法的规定进行数据处理，并按时上报普查数据。

**Article 26** The data processing plan and implementation measures for the agricultural census shall be formulated by the Office of the Leading Group for the Agricultural Census of the State Council.  
Local census offices shall carry out data processing in accordance with the provisions of the data processing plan and implementation measures, and shall report the census data on time.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　农业普查的数据处理工作由设区的市级以上普查办公室组织实施。

**Article 27** The data processing work of the agricultural census shall be organized and implemented by the census offices at or above the level of a city divided into districts.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　普查办公室应当做好数据备份和加载入库工作，建立健全农业普查数据库系统，并加强日常管理和维护更新。

**Article 28** Census offices shall do a good job in data backup and loading into the database, establish and improve the agricultural census database system, and strengthen routine management and maintenance updates.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家建立农业普查数据质量控制制度。  
普查办公室应当对普查实施中的每个环节实行质量控制和检查验收。

**Article 29** The State shall establish a quality control system for agricultural census data.  
Census offices shall implement quality control and inspection acceptance for each link in the implementation of the census.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　普查人员实行质量控制工作责任制。  
普查人员应当按照普查方案的规定对普查数据进行审核、复查和验收。

**Article 30** Census personnel shall implement a responsibility system for quality control work.  
Census personnel shall review, recheck, and accept census data in accordance with the provisions of the census plan.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国务院农业普查领导小组办公室统一组织农业普查数据的事后质量抽查工作。抽查结果作为评估全国或者各省、自治区、直辖市农业普查数据质量的重要依据。

**Article 31** The Office of the Leading Group for the Agricultural Census of the State Council shall uniformly organize the post-enumeration quality check of agricultural census data. The results of the spot checks shall serve as an important basis for evaluating the quality of agricultural census data of the whole country or of each province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

### 第五章　数据公布、资料管理和开发应用

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家建立农业普查资料公布制度。  
农业普查汇总资料，除依法予以保密的外，应当及时向社会公布。  
全国农业普查数据和各省、自治区、直辖市的主要农业普查数据，由国务院农业普查领导小组办公室审定并会同国务院有关部门公布。  
地方普查办公室发布普查公报，应当报经上一级普查办公室核准。

**Article 32** The State shall establish a system for the publication of agricultural census data.  
Summary data of the agricultural census shall be promptly published to the public, except for those kept confidential according to law.  
The national agricultural census data and the main agricultural census data of each province, autonomous region, and municipality directly under the Central Government shall be reviewed and approved by the Office of the Leading Group for the Agricultural Census of the State Council and published jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.  
When local census offices issue census bulletins, they shall report to the census office at the next higher level for approval.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　普查办公室和普查人员对在农业普查工作中搜集的单个普查对象的资料，应予保密，不得用于普查以外的目的。

**Article 33** Census offices and census personnel shall keep confidential the data of individual census respondents collected in the agricultural census work, and shall not use them for purposes other than the census.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　普查办公室应当做好农业普查资料的保存、管理和为社会公众提供服务等工作，并对农业普查资料进行开发和应用。

**Article 34** Census offices shall do a good job in the preservation, management, and provision of services to the public regarding agricultural census data, and shall develop and apply the agricultural census data.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上各级人民政府统计机构和有关部门可以根据农业普查结果，对有关常规统计的历史数据进行修正，具体办法由国家统计局规定。

**Article 35** The statistical institutions of the people's governments at or above the county level and relevant departments may, based on the results of the agricultural census, revise the historical data of relevant routine statistics. The specific measures shall be formulated by the National Bureau of Statistics.

### 第六章　表彰和处罚

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　对认真执行本条例，忠于职守，坚持原则，做出显著成绩的单位和个人，应当给予奖励。

**Article 36** Units and individuals that earnestly implement these Regulations, are devoted to their duties, adhere to principles, and have made outstanding achievements shall be given awards.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　地方、部门、单位的领导人自行修改农业普查资料，强令、授意普查办公室、普查人员和普查对象篡改农业普查资料或者编造虚假数据，对拒绝、抵制篡改农业普查资料或者拒绝、抵制编造虚假数据的人员打击报复的，依法给予行政处分或者纪律处分，并由县级以上人民政府统计机构或者国家统计局派出的调查队给予通报批评；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 37** Where leaders of localities, departments, or units modify agricultural census data on their own, compel or instruct census offices, census personnel, or census respondents to tamper with agricultural census data or fabricate false data, or retaliate against persons who refuse or resist tampering with agricultural census data or refuse or resist fabricating false data, they shall be given administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions according to law, and shall be given a notice of criticism by the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level or the survey team dispatched by the National Bureau of Statistics; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　普查人员不执行普查方案，伪造、篡改普查资料，强令、授意普查对象提供虚假普查资料的，由县级以上人民政府统计机构或者国家统计局派出的调查队责令改正，依法给予行政处分或者纪律处分，并可以给予通报批评。

**Article 38** Where census personnel fail to implement the census plan, forge or tamper with census data, or compel or instruct census respondents to provide false census data, the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level or the survey team dispatched by the National Bureau of Statistics shall order corrections, give administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions according to law, and may also give a notice of criticism.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　农业普查对象有下列违法行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府统计机构或者国家统计局派出的调查队责令改正，给予通报批评；情节严重的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分或者纪律处分：  
(一)拒绝或者妨碍普查办公室、普查人员依法进行调查的；  
(二)提供虚假或者不完整的农业普查资料的；  
(三)未按时提供与农业普查有关的资料，经催报后仍未提供的；  
(四)拒绝、推诿和阻挠依法进行的农业普查执法检查的；  
(五)在接受农业普查执法检查时，转移、隐匿、篡改、毁弃原始记录、统计台账、普查表、会计资料及其他相关资料的。  
农业生产经营单位有前款所列违法行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府统计机构或者国家统计局派出的调查队予以警告，并可以处5万元以下罚款；农业生产经营户有前款所列违法行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府统计机构或者国家统计局派出的调查队予以警告，并可以处1万元以下罚款。  
农业普查对象有本条第一款第(一)、(四)项所列违法行为之一的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 39** Where an agricultural census respondent commits any of the following illegal acts, the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level or the survey team dispatched by the National Bureau of Statistics shall order corrections and give a notice of criticism; if the circumstances are serious, administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions shall be given according to law to the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons:  
(1) refusing or obstructing the lawful investigation by census offices or census personnel;  
(2) providing false or incomplete agricultural census data;  
(3) failing to provide materials related to the agricultural census on time and still failing to do so after being urged;  
(4) refusing, prevaricating, or obstructing the lawful agricultural census law enforcement inspection;  
(5) transferring, concealing, tampering with, or destroying original records, statistical ledgers, census forms, accounting materials, or other relevant materials during the agricultural census law enforcement inspection.  
Where an agricultural production and operation unit commits any of the illegal acts listed in the preceding paragraph, the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level or the survey team dispatched by the National Bureau of Statistics shall give a warning and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; where an agricultural production and operation household commits any of the illegal acts listed in the preceding paragraph, the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level or the survey team dispatched by the National Bureau of Statistics shall give a warning and may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan.  
Where an agricultural census respondent commits any of the illegal acts listed in items (1) and (4) of the first paragraph of this Article, the public security organ shall give a public security penalty according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　普查人员失职、渎职等造成严重后果的，应当依法给予行政处分或者纪律处分，并可以由县级以上人民政府统计机构或者国家统计局派出的调查队给予通报批评。

**Article 40** Where census personnel are derelict of duty or commit malfeasance causing serious consequences, they shall be given administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions according to law, and may also be given a notice of criticism by the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level or the survey team dispatched by the National Bureau of Statistics.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　普查办公室应当设立举报电话和信箱，接受社会各界对农业普查违法行为的检举和监督，并对举报有功人员给予奖励。

**Article 41** Census offices shall set up reporting telephone numbers and mailboxes to accept reports and supervision from all sectors of society regarding illegal acts in the agricultural census, and shall give awards to persons who have rendered meritorious services in reporting.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 42** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
