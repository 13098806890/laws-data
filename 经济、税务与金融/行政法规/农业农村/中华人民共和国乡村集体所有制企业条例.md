# 中华人民共和国乡村集体所有制企业条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：1990-07-01

> (1990年6月3日中华人民共和国国务院令第59号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了保障乡村集体所有制企业的合法权益，引导其健康发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于由乡(含镇，下同)村(含村民小组，下同)农民集体举办的企业。 农业生产合作社、农村供销合作社、农村信用社不适用本条例。 第三条　乡村集体所有制企业是我国社会主义公有制经济的组成部分。 国家对乡村集体所有制企业实行积极扶持，合理规划，正确引导，加强管理的方针。 第四条　乡村集体所有制企业的主要任务是：发展商品生产和服务业，满足社会日益增长的物质和文化生活的需要；调整农村产业结构，合理利用农村劳动力；支援农业生产和农村建设，增加国家财政和农民的收入；积极发展出口创汇生产；为大工业配套和服务。 第五条　国家保护乡村集体所有制企业的合法权益，禁止任何组织和个人侵犯其财产。 第六条　乡村集体所有制企业实行自主经营，独立核算，自负盈亏。 乡村集体所有制企业实行多种形式的经营责任制。 乡村集体所有制企业可以在不改变集体所有制性质的前提下，吸收投资入股。 第七条　国家鼓励和扶持乡村集体所有制企业采用先进适用的科学技术和经营管理方法，加速企业现代化。 第八条　国家鼓励和保护乡村集体所有制企业依照平等互利、自愿协商、等价有偿的原则，进行多种形式的经济技术合作。 第九条　国家鼓励和支持乡村集体所有制企业依法利用自然资源，因地制宜发展符合国家产业政策和市场需要的产业和产品，增加社会有效供给。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障乡村集体所有制企业的合法权益，引导其健康发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to protect the legitimate rights and interests of township and village collective enterprises and to guide their healthy development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于由乡(含镇，下同)村(含村民小组，下同)农民集体举办的企业。  
农业生产合作社、农村供销合作社、农村信用社不适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to enterprises organized by the farmers' collectives of towns (including towns, the same below) and villages (including villagers' groups, the same below).  
Agricultural production cooperatives, rural supply and marketing cooperatives, and rural credit cooperatives shall not be subject to these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　乡村集体所有制企业是我国社会主义公有制经济的组成部分。  
国家对乡村集体所有制企业实行积极扶持，合理规划，正确引导，加强管理的方针。

**Article 3** Township and village collective enterprises are a component of the socialist public-owned economy of China.  
The State shall adopt a policy of actively supporting, rationally planning, correctly guiding, and strengthening the administration of township and village collective enterprises.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　乡村集体所有制企业的主要任务是：发展商品生产和服务业，满足社会日益增长的物质和文化生活的需要；调整农村产业结构，合理利用农村劳动力；支援农业生产和农村建设，增加国家财政和农民的收入；积极发展出口创汇生产；为大工业配套和服务。

**Article 4** The main tasks of township and village collective enterprises shall be: to develop commodity production and service industries to meet the growing material and cultural needs of society; to adjust the rural industrial structure and make rational use of the rural labor force; to support agricultural production and rural construction; to increase state revenue and farmers' income; to actively develop export-oriented production; and to provide supporting services for large-scale industry.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家保护乡村集体所有制企业的合法权益，禁止任何组织和个人侵犯其财产。

**Article 5** The State shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of township and village collective enterprises and shall prohibit any organization or individual from infringing upon their property.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　乡村集体所有制企业实行自主经营，独立核算，自负盈亏。  
乡村集体所有制企业实行多种形式的经营责任制。  
乡村集体所有制企业可以在不改变集体所有制性质的前提下，吸收投资入股。

**Article 6** Township and village collective enterprises shall practice self-management, independent accounting, and responsibility for their own profits and losses.  
Township and village collective enterprises shall adopt various forms of the operational responsibility system.  
Township and village collective enterprises may, on the premise of not altering the nature of collective ownership, absorb investment for shares.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励和扶持乡村集体所有制企业采用先进适用的科学技术和经营管理方法，加速企业现代化。

**Article 7** The State shall encourage and support township and village collective enterprises in adopting advanced and applicable science and technology and management methods so as to accelerate the modernization of enterprises.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励和保护乡村集体所有制企业依照平等互利、自愿协商、等价有偿的原则，进行多种形式的经济技术合作。

**Article 8** The State shall encourage and protect township and village collective enterprises in conducting various forms of economic and technological cooperation in accordance with the principles of equality and mutual benefit, voluntary consultation, and exchange of equal values.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家鼓励和支持乡村集体所有制企业依法利用自然资源，因地制宜发展符合国家产业政策和市场需要的产业和产品，增加社会有效供给。

**Article 9** The State shall encourage and support township and village collective enterprises in utilizing natural resources according to law, developing industries and products that are in conformity with the national industrial policies and market needs, in light of local conditions, so as to increase effective social supply.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　乡村集体所有制企业经依法审查，具备法人条件的，登记后取得法人资格，厂长(经理)为企业的法定代表人。

**Article 10** After being examined according to law and meeting the conditions for legal person status, a township and village collective enterprise shall acquire the status of a legal person upon registration, and the factory director (manager) shall be the legal representative of the enterprise.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　乡村集体所有制企业职工有返回其所属的农民集体经济组织从事农业生产的权利。

**Article 11** Workers of rural collective ownership enterprises shall have the right to return to the farmer collective economic organization to which they belong to engage in agricultural production.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院乡镇企业行政主管部门主管全国乡村集体所有制企业。地方人民政府乡镇企业行政主管部门主管本行政区域内的乡村集体所有制企业(以下简称企业)。

**Article 12** The competent administrative department for township enterprises under the State Council shall be in charge of rural collective ownership enterprises nationwide. The competent administrative department for township enterprises under the local people's government shall be in charge of rural collective ownership enterprises within its administrative area (hereinafter referred to as enterprises).

### 第二章　企业的设立、变更和终止

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　设立企业应当具备下列条件：  
(一)产品和提供的服务为社会所需要，并符合国家法律、法规和政策规定；  
(二)有自己的名称、组织机构和生产经营场所；  
(三)有确定的经营范围；  
(四)有与生产经营和服务规模相适应的资金、设备、从业人员和必要的原材料条件；  
(五)有必要的劳动卫生、安全生产条件和环境保护措施；  
(六)符合当地乡村建设规划，合理利用土地。

**Article 13** The establishment of an enterprise shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) The products and services provided are needed by society and comply with national laws, regulations, and policies;  
(2) It has its own name, organizational structure, and place of production and operation;  
(3) It has a defined business scope;  
(4) It has funds, equipment, employees, and necessary raw material conditions commensurate with the scale of production, operation, and services;  
(5) It has necessary labor hygiene, safe production conditions, and environmental protection measures;  
(6) It complies with local rural construction planning and makes rational use of land.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　设立企业必须依照法律、法规，经乡级人民政府审核后，报请县级人民政府乡镇企业主管部门以及法律、法规规定的有关部门批准，持有关批准文件向企业所在地工商行政管理机关办理登记，经核准领取《企业法人营业执照》或者《营业执照》后始得营业，并向税务机关办理税务登记。  
企业应当在核准登记的经营范围内从事生产经营活动。

**Article 14** The establishment of an enterprise must, in accordance with laws and regulations, be examined by the township people's government and then submitted to the competent administrative department for township enterprises of the county people's government and the relevant departments prescribed by laws and regulations for approval. With the relevant approval documents, it shall register with the administrative authority for industry and commerce at the place where the enterprise is located, and may commence business only after approval and obtaining the "Business License for Enterprise Legal Person" or "Business License", and shall register with the tax authorities for tax registration.  
The enterprise shall engage in production and operation activities within the approved and registered business scope.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　企业分立、合并、迁移、停业、终止以及改变名称、经营范围等，须经原批准企业设立的机关核准，向当地工商行政管理机关和税务机关办理变更或者注销登记，并通知开户银行。

**Article 15** The division, merger, relocation, suspension of business, termination, or change of name or business scope of an enterprise shall be subject to the verification of the authority that originally approved the establishment of the enterprise, and the enterprise shall undergo change or cancellation registration with the local administrative authority for industry and commerce and tax authorities, and shall notify the bank with which it has opened an account.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　企业分立、合并、停业或者终止时，必须保护其财产，依法清理债权、债务。

**Article 16** When an enterprise divides, merges, suspends business, or terminates, it must protect its property and liquidate its claims and debts according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　企业破产应当进行破产清算，法人以企业的财产对企业债权人清偿债务。

**Article 17** An enterprise that becomes bankrupt shall undergo bankruptcy liquidation, and the legal person shall use the enterprise's property to repay debts to the enterprise's creditors.

### 第三章　企业的所有者和经营者

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　企业财产属于举办该企业的乡或者村范围内的全体农民集体所有，由乡或者村的农民大会(农民代表会议)或者代表全体农民的集体经济组织行使企业财产的所有权。  
企业实行承包、租赁制或者与其他所有制企业联营的，企业财产的所有权不变。

**Article 18** The property of an enterprise shall belong to the collective ownership of all farmers within the township or village that established the enterprise, and the farmers' assembly (farmers' representative meeting) of the township or village or the collective economic organization representing all farmers shall exercise the ownership of the enterprise's property.  
If an enterprise adopts a contract, leasing system, or jointly operates with enterprises of other ownerships, the ownership of the enterprise's property shall remain unchanged.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　企业所有者依法决定企业的经营方向、经营形式、厂长(经理)人选或者选聘方式，依法决定企业税后利润在其与企业之间的具体分配比例，有权作出关于企业分立、合并、迁移、停业、终止、申请破产等决议。  
企业所有者应当为企业的生产、供应、销售提供服务，并尊重企业的自主权。

**Article 19** The owner of an enterprise shall, according to law, decide on the enterprise's operational direction, operational form, the person to serve as the factory director (manager) or the method of selection and appointment, and shall, according to law, decide on the specific proportion of after-tax profits distributed between itself and the enterprise, and shall have the right to make resolutions on the division, merger, relocation, suspension of business, termination, or application for bankruptcy of the enterprise.  
The owner of an enterprise shall provide services for the enterprise's production, supply, and sales, and shall respect the enterprise's autonomy.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　实行承包或者租赁制的企业，企业所有者应当采取公开招标方式确定经营者，不具备条件的，也可以采取招聘、推荐等方式选用经营者。  
招标可以在企业内部或者企业外部进行。投标者可以是经营集团或者个人。经营集团中标后，必须确定企业经营者。  
企业所有者应当对投标者全面评审，择优选定。

**Article 20** For an enterprise that adopts a contract or leasing system, the owner of the enterprise shall adopt public bidding to determine the operator; if conditions are not met, the owner may also adopt methods such as recruitment or recommendation to select the operator.  
Bidding may be conducted within or outside the enterprise. Bidders may be management groups or individuals. After a management group wins the bid, it must designate the enterprise operator.  
The owner of the enterprise shall conduct a comprehensive review of the bidders and select the best candidate.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　实行承包或者租赁制的企业，企业经营者应当具备下列条件：  
(一)坚持四项基本原则和改革开放，遵纪守法；  
(二)必要的文化知识和专业技术知识；  
(三)必要的企业经营管理能力；  
(四)提供必要的财产担保或者保证人；  
(五)企业所有者提供的其他合法条件。

**Article 21** An enterprise that implements the contract or lease system shall have its operator meeting the following conditions:  
(1) Adhering to the Four Cardinal Principles and the reform and opening-up policy, abiding by laws and disciplines;  
(2) Possessing necessary cultural knowledge and professional technical knowledge;  
(3) Possessing necessary capability of enterprise management;  
(4) Providing necessary property guarantee or guarantor;  
(5) Other lawful conditions provided by the enterprise owner.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　企业经营者是企业的厂长(经理)。企业实行厂长(经理)负责制。厂长(经理)对企业全面负责，代表企业行使职权。

**Article 22** The operator of an enterprise shall be the director (manager) of the enterprise. The enterprise shall implement the system of director (manager) responsibility. The director (manager) shall be fully responsible for the enterprise and exercise the powers and functions on behalf of the enterprise.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　实行承包或者租赁制的企业，订立承包或者租赁合同时，应当坚持平等、自愿、协商的原则，兼顾国家、集体和个人的利益。

**Article 23** When concluding a contract or lease agreement, an enterprise that implements the contract or lease system shall adhere to the principles of equality, voluntariness, and consultation, and take into account the interests of the State, the collective, and individuals.

### 第四章　企业的权利和义务

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　企业在生产经营活动中享有下列权利：  
(一)占有和使用企业资产，依照国家规定筹集资金；  
(二)在核准登记的范围内自主安排生产经营活动；  
(三)确定企业内部机构设置和人员配备；依法招聘、辞退职工，并确定工资形式和奖惩办法；  
(四)有权自行销售本企业的产品，但国务院另有规定的除外；  
(五)有权自行确定本企业的产品价格、劳务价格，但国务院规定由物价部门和有关主管部门控制价格的除外；  
(六)自愿参加行业协会和产品评比；  
(七)依照国家规定自愿参加各种招标、投标活动，申请产品定点生产，取得生产许可证；  
(八)自主订立经济合同，开展经济技术合作；  
(九)依法开发和利用自然资源；  
(十)依法利用外资、引进先进技术和设备，开展进出口贸易等涉外经济活动，并依照国家规定提留企业的外汇收入；  
(十一)拒绝摊派和非法罚款，但法律、法规规定应当提供财力、物力、人力的除外。

**Article 24** An enterprise shall enjoy the following rights in its production and operation activities:  
(1) Possessing and using the enterprise's assets, and raising funds in accordance with state provisions;  
(2) Independently arranging production and operation activities within the approved and registered scope;  
(3) Determining the internal organizational structure and staffing of the enterprise; recruiting and dismissing employees according to law, and determining the forms of wages and measures for rewards and penalties;  
(4) Having the right to sell the products of the enterprise on its own, except as otherwise provided by the State Council;  
(5) Having the right to determine the prices of the enterprise's products and services on its own, except those prices controlled by the price control departments and relevant competent departments as stipulated by the State Council;  
(6) Voluntarily participating in industry associations and product evaluations;  
(7) Voluntarily participating in various bidding and tendering activities in accordance with state provisions, applying for designated production of products, and obtaining production licenses;  
(8) Independently concluding economic contracts and carrying out economic and technological cooperation;  
(9) Developing and utilizing natural resources according to law;  
(10) Utilizing foreign capital, introducing advanced technology and equipment, engaging in import and export trade and other foreign-related economic activities according to law, and retaining the enterprise's foreign exchange earnings in accordance with state provisions;  
(11) Refusing unauthorized levies and illegal fines, unless laws or regulations require the provision of financial, material, or human resources.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　企业在生产经营活动中应当履行下列义务：  
(一)依法缴纳税金；  
(二)依照国家以及省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的规定，上交支农资金和管理费；  
(三)依法建立健全财务会计、审计、统计等制度，按期编报财务、统计报表；  
(四)保护自然资源和环境，防止和治理污染；  
(五)努力降低原材料和能源消耗，发展符合国家产业政策的产品；  
(六)做好劳动保护工作，实行安全生产；  
(七)保证产品质量和服务质量；  
(八)依法履行合同；  
(九)对职工进行政治思想、科学文化、技术业务和职业道德等方面的教育；  
(十)遵守法律、法规和政策的其他规定。

**Article 25** An enterprise shall perform the following obligations in its production and operation activities:  
(1) Paying taxes according to law;  
(2) Handing over funds for supporting agriculture and management fees in accordance with the provisions of the State and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(3) Establishing and perfecting financial accounting, auditing, statistics and other systems according to law, and compiling and submitting financial and statistical reports on schedule;  
(4) Protecting natural resources and the environment, preventing and controlling pollution;  
(5) Striving to reduce consumption of raw materials and energy, and developing products that conform to the national industrial policy;  
(6) Carrying out labor protection work and implementing safe production;  
(7) Ensuring product quality and service quality;  
(8) Performing contracts according to law;  
(9) Educating employees in political ideology, science and culture, technical and professional skills, and professional ethics;  
(10) Complying with other provisions of laws, regulations, and policies.

### 第五章　企业的管理

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　企业职工有参加企业民主管理，对厂长(经理)和其他管理人员提出批评和控告的权利。  
企业职工大会或者职工代表大会有权对企业经营管理中的问题提出意见和建议，评议、监督厂长(经理)和其他管理人员，维护职工的合法权益。

**Article 26** The employees of an enterprise shall have the right to participate in the democratic management of the enterprise and to criticize and accuse the director (manager) and other management personnel.  
The general meeting of employees or the representative meeting of employees shall have the right to raise opinions and suggestions on issues in the operation and management of the enterprise, to appraise and supervise the director (manager) and other management personnel, and to protect the lawful rights and interests of the employees.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　企业应当兼顾国家、集体和个人的利益，合理安排积累与消费的比例，对职工实行各尽所能、按劳分配的原则。  
男工与女工应当同工同酬。

**Article 27** An enterprise shall take into account the interests of the State, the collective, and individuals, and reasonably arrange the proportion of accumulation and consumption, and apply the principle of "from each according to his ability, to each according to his work" to the employees.  
Male and female workers shall receive equal pay for equal work.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　企业招用职工应当依法签订劳动合同，实行灵活的用工形式和办法。  
对技术要求高的企业，应当逐步形成专业化的技术职工队伍。

**Article 28** When recruiting employees, an enterprise shall conclude labor contracts according to law and adopt flexible forms and methods of employment.  
For enterprises with high technical requirements, a specialized technical workforce shall be gradually formed.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　企业不得招用未满16周岁的童工。

**Article 29** An enterprise shall not recruit child labor under the age of 16.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　企业对从事高度危险作业的职工，必须依照国家规定向保险公司投保。  
有条件的企业，应当参照国家有关规定实行职工社会保险。

**Article 30** An enterprise must insure with an insurance company in accordance with state provisions for employees engaged in high-risk operations.  
An enterprise with the conditions shall, by referring to relevant state provisions, implement social insurance for employees.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　企业发生劳动争议，可以依照《中华人民共和国劳动争议调解仲裁法》处理。

**Article 31** Where a labor dispute occurs in an enterprise, it may be handled in accordance with the Labor Dispute Mediation and Arbitration Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　企业税后利润，留给企业的部分不应低于60%，由企业自主安排，主要用作增加生产发展基金，进行技术改造和扩大再生产，适当增加福利基金和奖励基金。  
企业税后利润交给企业所有者的部分，主要用于扶持农业基本建设、农业技术服务、农村公益事业、企业更新改造或者发展新企业。

**Article 32** The portion of after-tax profits retained by the enterprise shall not be less than 60%, and shall be arranged independently by the enterprise, mainly for increasing the production development fund, carrying out technical transformation and expanded reproduction, and appropriately increasing the welfare fund and incentive fund.  
The portion of after-tax profits delivered to the enterprise owner shall be mainly used for supporting agricultural infrastructure, agricultural technical services, rural public welfare undertakings, enterprise renovation and upgrading, or developing new enterprises.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　企业应当根据国家有关规定，加强本企业的各项基础管理和合同管理。

**Article 33** An enterprise shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, strengthen its basic management and contract management.

### 第六章　企业与政府有关部门的关系

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　各级人民政府乡镇企业行政主管部门根据国家的法律、法规和政策，加强对企业的指导、管理、监督、协调和服务：  
(一)监督检查企业执行国家法律、法规和政策；  
(二)制订企业发展规划，协同有关部门制定农村剩余劳动力就业规划；  
(三)会同有关部门指导企业的计划、统计、财务、审计、价格、物资、质量、设备、技术、劳动、安全生产、环境保护等管理工作；  
(四)组织和指导企业的技术进步、职工教育和培训；  
(五)向企业提供经济、技术咨询和信息服务；  
(六)协调企业与有关方面的关系，帮助企业开展经济技术合作；  
(七)总结推广企业发展的经验；  
(八)组织和指导企业的思想政治工作，促进企业的社会主义精神文明建设。

**Article 31** The administrative departments for township enterprises of the people's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the laws, regulations and policies of the State, strengthen the guidance, management, supervision, coordination and services for enterprises:  
(1) supervise and inspect the implementation by enterprises of the laws, regulations and policies of the State;  
(2) formulate development plans for enterprises and assist relevant departments in formulating employment plans for rural surplus labor;  
(3) in conjunction with relevant departments, guide the management work of enterprises in respect of planning, statistics, finance, auditing, pricing, materials, quality, equipment, technology, labor, safe production and environmental protection;  
(4) organize and guide the technological progress of enterprises, and the education and training of employees;  
(5) provide enterprises with economic and technical consultancy and information services;  
(6) coordinate the relationship between enterprises and the relevant parties, and assist enterprises in developing economic and technical cooperation;  
(7) summarize and disseminate the experience of enterprise development;  
(8) organize and guide the ideological and political work of enterprises, and promote the socialist spiritual civilization construction of enterprises.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　各级人民政府有关行业管理部门应当根据国家的产业政策和行业发展规划，对企业的发展方向进行指导和监督；对企业开展技术指导、人才培训和经济、技术信息服务；指导、帮助和监督企业开展劳动保护、环境保护等工作。

**Article 35** The relevant industry administrative departments of the people's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the national industrial policies and industry development plans, guide and supervise the development direction of enterprises; carry out technical guidance, personnel training and economic and technical information services for enterprises; and guide, assist and supervise enterprises in carrying out labor protection, environmental protection and other work.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　政府有关部门应当为符合国家产业政策，经济和社会效益好的企业创造发展条件：  
(一)对企业所需的能源、原材料、资金等，计划、物资、金融等部门应当积极帮助解决；生产纳入国家指令性计划的产品所需的能源、原材料等，由安排生产任务的部门和单位组织供应；(2011年1月8日删除)  
(二)根据国家有关规定，对生产名、优产品和出口创汇产品的企业，应当在信贷、能源、原材料和运输等方面给予扶持；  
(三)为企业培训、招用专业技术人才和引进先进技术创造条件。

**Article 36** The relevant government departments shall create conditions for development for enterprises which conform to the national industrial policies and have good economic and social benefits:  
(1) The planning, materials, financial and other departments shall actively assist in solving the energy, raw materials, funds, etc., needed by enterprises; the energy, raw materials, etc., needed for the production of products included in the mandatory state plan shall be supplied by the departments and units which assign the production tasks; (deleted on January 8, 2011)  
(2) In accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, support shall be given to enterprises producing famous and high-quality products and products for export in terms of credit, energy, raw materials and transportation;  
(3) Create conditions for enterprises to train and recruit specialized technical personnel and introduce advanced technology.

### 第七章　奖励与处罚

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　对在企业经营管理、科技进步、劳动保护、环境保护和思想政治工作等方面作出显著成绩的企业和个人，由人民政府给予奖励。

**Article 37** Enterprises and individuals that have made remarkable achievements in enterprise management, scientific and technological progress, labor protection, environmental protection, ideological and political work and other aspects shall be rewarded by the people's governments.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　企业产品质量达不到国家规定标准的，企业所有者和企业主管部门应当责令其限期整顿，经整顿无效者，应当责令其停产或者转产，直至建议有关机关撤销生产许可证，吊销营业执照。  
企业因生产、销售前款所指的产品，给用户和消费者造成财产损失、人身损害的，应当承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 38** Where the quality of an enterprise's products fails to meet the standards prescribed by the State, the enterprise owner and the competent department of the enterprise shall order it to rectify within a time limit; if the rectification is ineffective, it shall be ordered to suspend production or change its product line, until the relevant authorities are recommended to revoke the production license and cancel the business license.  
Where an enterprise causes property losses or personal injury to users and consumers due to the production or sale of the products referred to in the preceding paragraph, it shall bear compensation liability; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　企业厂长(经理)侵犯职工合法权益，情节严重的，由企业所有者给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 39** Where the factory director (manager) of an enterprise infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of the employees, and the circumstances are serious, the enterprise owner shall impose administrative sanctions; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　对向企业摊派的单位和个人，企业可以向审计机关或者其他有关部门控告、检举。经审计机关确认是摊派行为的，由审计机关通知摊派单位停止摊派行为，限期退回摊派财物；对摊派单位的负责人和直接责任人员，监察机关或者有关主管部门可以根据情节轻重，给予行政处分。

**Article 40** For units and individuals that impose apportionment on enterprises, the enterprises may lodge accusations or reports with the auditing authorities or other relevant departments. Where the auditing authority confirms the act as apportionment, the auditing authority shall notify the apportioning unit to cease the apportionment and return the apportioned property within a time limit; for the person in charge and the person directly responsible of the apportioning unit, the supervisory authority or the relevant competent department may, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, impose administrative sanctions.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　政府部门的工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权，使企业合法权益遭受损害的，由其所在单位或者上级主管机关给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 41** Where a staff member of a government department neglects their duty or abuses their authority, causing damage to the lawful rights and interests of an enterprise, the entity to which they belong or the competent authority at a higher level shall impose administrative sanctions; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　企业违反财政、税收、劳动、工商行政、价格、资源、环境保护等法律、法规的，依照有关法律、法规处理。

**Article 42** Where an enterprise violates laws and regulations on finance, taxation, labor, industry and commerce administration, pricing, resources, environmental protection, etc., the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　本条例由国务院乡镇企业行政主管部门负责解释，并组织实施。

**Article 43** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the administrative department in charge of township enterprises under the State Council, which shall also organize their implementation.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本条例制定实施办法。

**Article 44** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate implementing measures in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例自1990年7月1日起施行。

**Article 45** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 1990.
