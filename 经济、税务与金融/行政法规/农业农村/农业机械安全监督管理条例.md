# 农业机械安全监督管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2009-11-01

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强农业机械安全监督管理，预防和减少农业机械事故，保障人民生命和财产安全，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the safety supervision and administration of agricultural machinery, preventing and reducing agricultural machinery accidents, and protecting the safety of people's lives and property.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事农业机械的生产、销售、维修、使用操作以及安全监督管理等活动，应当遵守本条例。  
本条例所称农业机械，是指用于农业生产及其产品初加工等相关农事活动的机械、设备。

**Article 2** Any person engaged in the production, sale, repair, use and operation of agricultural machinery and the safety supervision and administration thereof within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall comply with these Regulations.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, agricultural machinery means machinery and equipment used for agricultural production and the primary processing of agricultural products and other related agricultural activities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　农业机械安全监督管理应当遵循以人为本、预防事故、保障安全、促进发展的原则。

**Article 3** The safety supervision and administration of agricultural machinery shall follow the principles of putting people first, preventing accidents, ensuring safety, and promoting development.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对农业机械安全监督管理工作的领导，完善农业机械安全监督管理体系，增加对农民购买农业机械的补贴，保障农业机械安全的财政投入，建立健全农业机械安全生产责任制。

**Article 4** The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the leadership over the safety supervision and administration of agricultural machinery, improve the system for the safety supervision and administration of agricultural machinery, increase subsidies for farmers to purchase agricultural machinery, ensure financial investment in the safety of agricultural machinery, and establish and improve the accountability system for the safe production of agricultural machinery.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院有关部门和地方各级人民政府、有关部门应当加强农业机械安全法律、法规、标准和知识的宣传教育。  
农业生产经营组织、农业机械所有人应当对农业机械操作人员及相关人员进行农业机械安全使用教育，提高其安全意识。

**Article 5** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at all levels and the relevant departments shall strengthen the publicity and education on the laws, regulations, standards and knowledge concerning the safety of agricultural machinery.  
Agricultural production and business operation organizations and agricultural machinery owners shall conduct education on the safe use of agricultural machinery for agricultural machinery operators and relevant personnel, so as to improve their safety awareness.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励和支持开发、生产、推广、应用先进适用、安全可靠、节能环保的农业机械，建立健全农业机械安全技术标准和安全操作规程。

**Article 6** The State encourages and supports the development, production, promotion and application of agricultural machinery that is advanced, applicable, safe, reliable, energy-saving and environmentally friendly, and the establishment and improvement of safety technical standards and safe operation procedures for agricultural machinery.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励农业机械操作人员、维修技术人员参加职业技能培训和依法成立安全互助组织，提高农业机械安全操作水平。

**Article 7** The State encourages agricultural machinery operators and maintenance technicians to participate in vocational skill training and to establish safety mutual assistance organizations according to law, so as to improve the level of safe operation of agricultural machinery.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家建立落后农业机械淘汰制度和危及人身财产安全的农业机械报废制度，并对淘汰和报废的农业机械依法实行回收。

**Article 8** The State establishes a system for the elimination of outdated agricultural machinery and a system for the scrapping of agricultural machinery that endangers personal and property safety, and shall implement the recovery of eliminated and scrapped agricultural machinery according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院农业机械化主管部门、工业主管部门、市场监督管理部门等有关部门依照本条例和国务院规定的职责，负责农业机械安全监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府农业机械化主管部门、工业主管部门和市场监督管理部门等有关部门按照各自职责，负责本行政区域的农业机械安全监督管理工作。

**Article 9** The competent department of agricultural mechanization of the State Council, the competent department of industry and the market supervision and administration department and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with these Regulations and the functions specified by the State Council, be responsible for the safety supervision and administration of agricultural machinery.  
The competent department of agricultural mechanization, the competent department of industry and the market supervision and administration department and other relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions, be responsible for the safety supervision and administration of agricultural machinery within their respective administrative regions.

### 第二章　生产、销售和维修

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院工业主管部门负责制定并组织实施农业机械工业产业政策和有关规划。  
国务院标准化主管部门负责制定发布农业机械安全技术国家标准，并根据实际情况及时修订。农业机械安全技术标准是强制执行的标准。

**Article 10** The competent department of industry of the State Council shall be responsible for formulating and organizing the implementation of industrial policies and relevant plans for the agricultural machinery industry.  
The competent department of standardization of the State Council shall be responsible for formulating and publishing national safety technical standards for agricultural machinery, and shall revise them in a timely manner in light of actual circumstances. The safety technical standards for agricultural machinery are mandatory standards.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　农业机械生产者应当依据农业机械工业产业政策和有关规划，按照农业机械安全技术标准组织生产，并建立健全质量保障控制体系。  
对依法实行工业产品生产许可证管理的农业机械，其生产者应当取得相应资质，并按照许可的范围和条件组织生产。

**Article 11** Agricultural machinery producers shall, pursuant to the industrial policies and relevant plans for the agricultural machinery industry, organize production in accordance with the safety technical standards for agricultural machinery, and shall establish and improve the quality assurance and control system.  
For agricultural machinery subject to the administration of industrial product production licenses according to law, the producers thereof shall obtain the corresponding qualifications and organize production within the scope and conditions of the license.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　农业机械生产者应当按照农业机械安全技术标准对生产的农业机械进行检验；农业机械经检验合格并附具详尽的安全操作说明书和标注安全警示标志后，方可出厂销售；依法必须进行认证的农业机械，在出厂前应当标注认证标志。  
上道路行驶的拖拉机，依法必须经过认证的，在出厂前应当标注认证标志，并符合机动车国家安全技术标准。  
农业机械生产者应当建立产品出厂记录制度，如实记录农业机械的名称、规格、数量、生产日期、生产批号、检验合格证号、购货者名称及联系方式、销售日期等内容。出厂记录保存期限不得少于3年。

**Article 12** Agricultural machinery producers shall inspect the agricultural machinery they produce in accordance with the safety technical standards for agricultural machinery; the agricultural machinery may leave the factory for sale only after it passes the inspection and is accompanied by detailed safe operation instructions and marked with safety warning signs; for agricultural machinery that must be certified according to law, it shall be marked with certification marks before leaving the factory.  
Tractors that travel on roads and must be certified according to law shall be marked with certification marks before leaving the factory, and shall comply with the national safety technical standards for motor vehicles.  
Agricultural machinery producers shall establish a product ex-factory recording system, and truthfully record the name, specifications, quantity, date of manufacture, production batch number, inspection certificate number, name and contact information of the purchaser, date of sale and other contents. The retention period of ex-factory records shall not be less than three years.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　进口的农业机械应当符合我国农业机械安全技术标准，并依法由出入境检验检疫机构检验合格。依法必须进行认证的农业机械，还应当由出入境检验检疫机构进行入境验证。

**Article 13** Imported agricultural machinery shall conform to the safety technical standards for agricultural machinery of China, and shall be inspected and deemed qualified by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine authority according to law. For agricultural machinery that must be certified according to law, it shall also be subject to entry verification by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine authority.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　农业机械销售者对购进的农业机械应当查验产品合格证明。对依法实行工业产品生产许可证管理、依法必须进行认证的农业机械，还应当验明相应的证明文件或者标志。  
农业机械销售者应当建立销售记录制度，如实记录农业机械的名称、规格、生产批号、供货者名称及联系方式、销售流向等内容。销售记录保存期限不得少于3年。  
农业机械销售者应当向购买者说明农业机械操作方法和安全注意事项，并依法开具销售发票。

**Article 14** Agricultural machinery sellers shall inspect the product conformity certificates for the agricultural machinery they purchase. For agricultural machinery subject to the administration of industrial product production licenses according to law or must be certified according to law, they shall also verify the corresponding certification documents or marks.  
Agricultural machinery sellers shall establish a sales recording system, and truthfully record the name, specifications, production batch number, name and contact information of the supplier, sales destination and other contents. The retention period of sales records shall not be less than three years.  
Agricultural machinery sellers shall explain the operation methods and safety precautions of agricultural machinery to purchasers, and issue sales invoices according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　农业机械生产者、销售者应当建立健全农业机械销售服务体系，依法承担产品质量责任。

**Article 15** Agricultural machinery producers and sellers shall establish and improve the agricultural machinery sales service system, and bear product quality liability according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　农业机械生产者、销售者发现其生产、销售的农业机械存在设计、制造等缺陷，可能对人身财产安全造成损害的，应当立即停止生产、销售，及时报告当地市场监督管理部门，通知农业机械使用者停止使用。农业机械生产者应当及时召回存在设计、制造等缺陷的农业机械。  
农业机械生产者、销售者不履行本条第一款义务的，市场监督管理部门可以责令生产者召回农业机械，责令销售者停止销售农业机械。

**Article 16** If an agricultural machinery producer or seller discovers that the agricultural machinery it produces or sells has defects in design or manufacture, which may cause damage to personal or property safety, it shall immediately stop production and sale, promptly report to the local market supervision and administration department, and notify the agricultural machinery users to stop using the agricultural machinery. The agricultural machinery producer shall promptly recall the agricultural machinery with defects in design or manufacture.  
If the agricultural machinery producer or seller fails to perform the obligations specified in the first paragraph of this Article, the market supervision and administration department may order the producer to recall the agricultural machinery and order the seller to stop selling the agricultural machinery.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　禁止生产、销售下列农业机械：  
(一)不符合农业机械安全技术标准的；  
(二)依法实行工业产品生产许可证管理而未取得许可证的；  
(三)依法必须进行认证而未经认证的；  
(四)利用残次零配件或者报废农业机械的发动机、方向机、变速器、车架等部件拼装的；  
(五)国家明令淘汰的。

**Article 17** The production and sale of the following agricultural machinery are prohibited:  
(1) those that do not conform to the safety technical standards for agricultural machinery;  
(2) those subject to the administration of industrial product production licenses according to law but without obtaining a license;  
(3) those that must be certified according to law but have not been certified;  
(4) those assembled from defective parts or components, or engines, steering gears, transmissions, frames and other parts of scrapped agricultural machinery;  
(5) those explicitly eliminated by the State.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　从事农业机械维修经营，应当有必要的维修场地，有必要的维修设施、设备和检测仪器，有相应的维修技术人员，有安全防护和环境保护措施。

**Article 18** Any person engaged in the maintenance business of agricultural machinery shall have necessary maintenance premises, necessary maintenance facilities, equipment and testing instruments, corresponding maintenance technicians, and safety protection and environmental protection measures.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　农业机械维修经营者应当遵守国家有关维修质量安全技术规范和维修质量保证期的规定，确保维修质量。  
从事农业机械维修不得有下列行为：  
(一)使用不符合农业机械安全技术标准的零配件；  
(二)拼装、改装农业机械整机；  
(三)承揽维修已经达到报废条件的农业机械；  
(四)法律、法规和国务院农业机械化主管部门规定的其他禁止性行为。

**Article 19** Agricultural machinery maintenance business operators shall comply with the relevant state provisions on maintenance quality safety technical specifications and the warranty period for maintenance quality, and shall ensure the maintenance quality.  
In engaging in the maintenance of agricultural machinery, the following acts are prohibited:  
(1) using parts and accessories that do not conform to the safety technical standards for agricultural machinery;  
(2) assembling or modifying complete agricultural machinery;  
(3) undertaking the maintenance of agricultural machinery that has reached the scrapping conditions;  
(4) other prohibited acts specified by laws, regulations and the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the State Council.

### 第三章　使用操作

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　农业机械操作人员可以参加农业机械操作人员的技能培训，可以向有关农业机械化主管部门、人力资源和社会保障部门申请职业技能鉴定，获取相应等级的国家职业资格证书。

**Article 20** Agricultural machinery operators may participate in skill training for agricultural machinery operators, and may apply to the relevant competent department of agricultural mechanization or the human resources and social security department for vocational skill appraisal, so as to obtain the national vocational qualification certificate of the corresponding level.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　拖拉机、联合收割机投入使用前，其所有人应当按照国务院农业机械化主管部门的规定，持本人身份证明和机具来源证明，向所在地县级人民政府农业机械化主管部门申请登记。拖拉机、联合收割机经安全检验合格的，农业机械化主管部门应当在2个工作日内予以登记并核发相应的证书和牌照。  
拖拉机、联合收割机使用期间登记事项发生变更的，其所有人应当按照国务院农业机械化主管部门的规定申请变更登记。

**Article 21** Before tractors and combine harvesters are put into use, their owners shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the State Council, apply to the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the people's government at the county level where the owner is located for registration, by presenting their identity certificates and the source certificates of the machinery. If tractors and combine harvesters pass the safety inspection, the competent department of agricultural mechanization shall, within two working days, register them and issue the corresponding certificates and license plates.  
If the registered matters of a tractor or combine harvester change during the period of use, its owner shall apply for change registration in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　拖拉机、联合收割机操作人员经过培训后，应当按照国务院农业机械化主管部门的规定，参加县级人民政府农业机械化主管部门组织的考试。考试合格的，农业机械化主管部门应当在2个工作日内核发相应的操作证件。  
拖拉机、联合收割机操作证件有效期为6年；有效期满，拖拉机、联合收割机操作人员可以向原发证机关申请续展。未满18周岁不得操作拖拉机、联合收割机。操作人员年满70周岁的，县级人民政府农业机械化主管部门应当注销其操作证件。

**Article 22** After receiving training, tractor and combine harvester operators shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the State Council, take the examination organized by the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the people's government at the county level. Those who pass the examination shall, within two working days, be issued the corresponding operation certificates by the competent department of agricultural mechanization.  
The validity period of tractor and combine harvester operation certificates is six years; upon expiry of the validity period, tractor and combine harvester operators may apply to the original issuing authority for renewal. No person under the age of 18 may operate tractors or combine harvesters. For operators who have reached the age of 70, the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the people's government at the county level shall cancel their operation certificates.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　拖拉机、联合收割机应当悬挂牌照。拖拉机上道路行驶，联合收割机因转场作业、维修、安全检验等需要转移的，其操作人员应当携带操作证件。  
拖拉机、联合收割机操作人员不得有下列行为：  
(一)操作与本人操作证件规定不相符的拖拉机、联合收割机；  
(二)操作未按照规定登记、检验或者检验不合格、安全设施不全、机件失效的拖拉机、联合收割机；  
(三)使用国家管制的精神药品、麻醉品后操作拖拉机、联合收割机；  
(四)患有妨碍安全操作的疾病操作拖拉机、联合收割机；  
(五)国务院农业机械化主管部门规定的其他禁止行为。  
禁止使用拖拉机、联合收割机违反规定载人。

**Article 23** Tractors and combine harvesters shall display license plates. When a tractor travels on a road, or when a combine harvester needs to be moved due to field transfer operations, maintenance, safety inspection, etc., its operator shall carry the operation certificate.  
Tractor and combine harvester operators shall not commit any of the following acts:  
(1) operating a tractor or combine harvester that does not match the specifications of the operator's own operation certificate;  
(2) operating a tractor or combine harvester that has not been registered or inspected in accordance with regulations, or has been inspected and found unqualified, has incomplete safety facilities, or has failed parts;  
(3) operating a tractor or combine harvester after using psychotropic drugs or narcotics controlled by the State;  
(4) operating a tractor or combine harvester while suffering from a disease that impairs safe operation;  
(5) other prohibited acts specified by the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the State Council.  
It is prohibited to use tractors or combine harvesters to carry passengers in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　农业机械操作人员作业前，应当对农业机械进行安全查验；作业时，应当遵守国务院农业机械化主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业机械化主管部门制定的安全操作规程。

**Article 24** Before starting operations, agricultural machinery operators shall conduct a safety inspection of the agricultural machinery; during operations, they shall comply with the safe operation procedures formulated by the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the State Council and the competent departments of agricultural mechanization of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

### 第四章　事故处理

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上地方人民政府农业机械化主管部门负责农业机械事故责任的认定和调解处理。  
本条例所称农业机械事故，是指农业机械在作业或者转移等过程中造成人身伤亡、财产损失的事件。  
农业机械在道路上发生的交通事故，由公安机关交通管理部门依照道路交通安全法律、法规处理；拖拉机在道路以外通行时发生的事故，公安机关交通管理部门接到报案的，参照道路交通安全法律、法规处理。农业机械事故造成公路及其附属设施损坏的，由交通主管部门依照公路法律、法规处理。

**Article 25** The competent department of agricultural mechanization of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the determination of liability for agricultural machinery accidents and for the mediation and handling thereof.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, agricultural machinery accident means an incident that causes personal injury, death or property damage during the operation or movement of agricultural machinery or in other processes.  
Traffic accidents involving agricultural machinery on roads shall be handled by the traffic management department of the public security organ in accordance with road traffic safety laws and regulations; for accidents occurring when tractors are traveling outside roads, if the traffic management department of the public security organ receives a report, it shall handle them with reference to road traffic safety laws and regulations. Where an agricultural machinery accident causes damage to highways and their ancillary facilities, the competent department of transportation shall handle it in accordance with highway laws and regulations.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　在道路以外发生的农业机械事故，操作人员和现场其他人员应当立即停止作业或者停止农业机械的转移，保护现场，造成人员伤害的，应当向事故发生地农业机械化主管部门报告；造成人员死亡的，还应当向事故发生地公安机关报告。造成人身伤害的，应当立即采取措施，抢救受伤人员。因抢救受伤人员变动现场的，应当标明位置。  
接到报告的农业机械化主管部门和公安机关应当立即派人赶赴现场进行勘验、检查，收集证据，组织抢救受伤人员，尽快恢复正常的生产秩序。

**Article 26** For an agricultural machinery accident occurring outside a road, the operator and other persons at the scene shall immediately stop the operation or the movement of the agricultural machinery, protect the scene, and if personal injury is caused, shall report to the competent department of agricultural mechanization at the place where the accident occurred; if death is caused, they shall also report to the public security organ at the place where the accident occurred. If personal injury is caused, they shall immediately take measures to rescue the injured persons. If the scene is altered due to the rescue of the injured persons, the position shall be marked.  
The competent department of agricultural mechanization and the public security organ that receive the report shall immediately send personnel to the scene to conduct inspection and examination, collect evidence, organize the rescue of the injured persons, and restore normal production order as soon as possible.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对经过现场勘验、检查的农业机械事故，农业机械化主管部门应当在10个工作日内制作完成农业机械事故认定书；需要进行农业机械鉴定的，应当自收到农业机械鉴定机构出具的鉴定结论之日起5个工作日内制作农业机械事故认定书。  
农业机械事故认定书应当载明农业机械事故的基本事实、成因和当事人的责任，并在制作完成农业机械事故认定书之日起3个工作日内送达当事人。

**Article 27** For an agricultural machinery accident that has been inspected and examined at the scene, the competent department of agricultural mechanization shall, within ten working days, complete the preparation of the agricultural machinery accident confirmation document; if an expert evaluation of the agricultural machinery is required, it shall, within five working days from the date of receipt of the appraisal conclusion issued by the agricultural machinery appraisal institution, prepare the agricultural machinery accident confirmation document.  
The agricultural machinery accident confirmation document shall specify the basic facts, causes, and liability of the parties involved in the agricultural machinery accident, and shall be served to the parties within three working days from the date of completion of the preparation of the agricultural machinery accident confirmation document.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　当事人对农业机械事故损害赔偿有争议，请求调解的，应当自收到事故认定书之日起10个工作日内向农业机械化主管部门书面提出调解申请。  
调解达成协议的，农业机械化主管部门应当制作调解书送交各方当事人。调解书经各方当事人共同签字后生效。调解不能达成协议或者当事人向人民法院提起诉讼的，农业机械化主管部门应当终止调解并书面通知当事人。调解达成协议后当事人反悔的，可以向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 28** If a party disputes the compensation for damage arising from an agricultural machinery accident and requests mediation, it shall, within ten working days from the receipt of the accident confirmation document, submit a written mediation application to the competent department of agricultural mechanization.  
If an agreement is reached through mediation, the competent department of agricultural mechanization shall prepare a mediation letter and deliver it to the parties concerned. The mediation letter shall take effect after being jointly signed by the parties. If no agreement can be reached through mediation or if a party files a lawsuit with the People's Court, the competent department of agricultural mechanization shall terminate the mediation and notify the parties in writing. If a party reneges after a mediation agreement is reached, it may file a lawsuit with the People's Court.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　农业机械化主管部门应当为当事人处理农业机械事故损害赔偿等后续事宜提供帮助和便利。因农业机械产品质量原因导致事故的，农业机械化主管部门应当依法出具有关证明材料。  
农业机械化主管部门应当定期将农业机械事故统计情况及说明材料报送上级农业机械化主管部门并抄送同级安全生产监督管理部门。  
农业机械事故构成生产安全事故的，应当依照相关法律、行政法规的规定调查处理并追究责任。

**Article 29** The competent department of agricultural mechanization shall provide assistance and convenience for the parties to handle subsequent matters such as compensation for damage arising from an agricultural machinery accident. If the accident is caused by the quality of the agricultural machinery product, the competent department of agricultural mechanization shall issue relevant evidentiary materials according to law.  
The competent department of agricultural mechanization shall regularly submit statistical information on agricultural machinery accidents and explanatory materials to the superior competent department of agricultural mechanization, and send a copy to the work safety supervision and administration department at the same level.  
If an agricultural machinery accident constitutes a work safety accident, it shall be investigated and handled and liability shall be pursued in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations.

### 第五章　服务与监督

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上地方人民政府农业机械化主管部门应当定期对危及人身财产安全的农业机械进行免费实地安全检验。但是道路交通安全法律对拖拉机的安全检验另有规定的，从其规定。  
拖拉机、联合收割机的安全检验为每年1次。  
实施安全技术检验的机构应当对检验结果承担法律责任。

**Article 30** The competent department of agricultural mechanization of the local people's government at or above the county level shall regularly conduct free on-site safety inspections of agricultural machinery that endangers personal and property safety. However, if the road traffic safety law has other provisions on the safety inspection of tractors, those provisions shall prevail.  
The safety inspection of tractors and combine harvesters shall be conducted once a year.  
The institution implementing the safety technical inspection shall bear legal liability for the inspection results.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　农业机械化主管部门在安全检验中发现农业机械存在事故隐患的，应当告知其所有人停止使用并及时排除隐患。  
实施安全检验的农业机械化主管部门应当对安全检验情况进行汇总，建立农业机械安全监督管理档案。

**Article 31** If, in the course of safety inspection, the competent department of agricultural mechanization discovers that an agricultural machine has potential accident hazards, it shall inform its owner to stop using it and eliminate the hazards in a timely manner.  
The competent department of agricultural mechanization that conducts the safety inspection shall summarize the safety inspection situation and establish a safety supervision and administration archive for agricultural machinery.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　联合收割机跨行政区域作业前，当地县级人民政府农业机械化主管部门应当会同有关部门，对跨行政区域作业的联合收割机进行必要的安全检查，并对操作人员进行安全教育。

**Article 32** Before a combine harvester operates across administrative regions, the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the local people's government at the county level shall, together with the relevant departments, conduct necessary safety inspections on the combine harvester operating across administrative regions and conduct safety education for the operators.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院农业机械化主管部门应当定期对农业机械安全使用状况进行分析评估，发布相关信息。

**Article 33** The competent department of agricultural mechanization of the State Council shall regularly analyze and assess the safe use status of agricultural machinery and publish relevant information.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院工业主管部门应当定期对农业机械生产行业运行态势进行监测和分析，并按照先进适用、安全可靠、节能环保的要求，会同国务院农业机械化主管部门、市场监督管理部门等有关部门制定、公布国家明令淘汰的农业机械产品目录。

**Article 34** The competent department of industry of the State Council shall regularly monitor and analyze the operational trend of the agricultural machinery manufacturing industry, and, in accordance with the requirements of being advanced and applicable, safe and reliable, energy-saving and environmentally friendly, shall, together with the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the State Council and the market supervision and administration department and other relevant departments, formulate and publish the catalogue of agricultural machinery products explicitly eliminated by the State.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　危及人身财产安全的农业机械达到报废条件的，应当停止使用，予以报废。农业机械的报废条件由国务院农业机械化主管部门会同国务院市场监督管理部门、工业主管部门规定。  
县级人民政府农业机械化主管部门对达到报废条件的危及人身财产安全的农业机械，应当书面告知其所有人。

**Article 35** If agricultural machinery that endangers personal and property safety reaches the scrapping conditions, it shall be stopped from use and scrapped. The scrapping conditions for agricultural machinery shall be prescribed by the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the State Council together with the market supervision and administration department and the competent department of industry of the State Council.  
The competent department of agricultural mechanization of the people's government at the county level shall notify the owner in writing of agricultural machinery that endangers personal and property safety and has reached the scrapping conditions.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家对达到报废条件或者正在使用的国家已经明令淘汰的农业机械实行回收。农业机械回收办法由国务院农业机械化主管部门会同国务院财政部门、商务主管部门制定。

**Article 36** The State implements recovery for agricultural machinery that has reached the scrapping conditions or that is in use and has been explicitly eliminated by the State. The measures for the recovery of agricultural machinery shall be formulated by the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the State Council together with the finance department and the commerce department of the State Council.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　回收的农业机械由县级人民政府农业机械化主管部门监督回收单位进行解体或者销毁。

**Article 37** Recovered agricultural machinery shall be dismantled or destroyed by the recovery unit under the supervision of the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the people's government at the county level.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　使用操作过程中发现农业机械存在产品质量、维修质量问题的，当事人可以向县级以上地方人民政府农业机械化主管部门或者市场监督管理部门投诉。接到投诉的部门对属于职责范围内的事项，应当依法及时处理；对不属于职责范围内的事项，应当及时移交有权处理的部门，有权处理的部门应当立即处理，不得推诿。  
县级以上地方人民政府农业机械化主管部门和市场监督管理部门应当定期汇总农业机械产品质量、维修质量投诉情况并逐级上报。

**Article 38** If, during the use and operation, problems with product quality or maintenance quality of agricultural machinery are discovered, the party may file a complaint with the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the local people's government at or above the county level or the market supervision and administration department. The department receiving the complaint shall, if the matter falls within its scope of functions, handle it according to law in a timely manner; if the matter does not fall within its scope of functions, it shall promptly transfer it to the department with the authority to handle it, and the department with the authority shall handle it immediately and shall not prevaricate.  
The competent department of agricultural mechanization and the market supervision and administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall regularly summarize the complaints about the product quality and maintenance quality of agricultural machinery and report them level by level.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国务院农业机械化主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业机械化主管部门应当根据投诉情况和农业安全生产需要，组织开展在用的特定种类农业机械的安全鉴定和重点检查，并公布结果。

**Article 39** The competent department of agricultural mechanization of the State Council and the competent departments of agricultural mechanization of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the complaint situation and the needs of agricultural work safety, organize the safety appraisal and key inspection of specific types of agricultural machinery in use, and publish the results.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　农业机械安全监督管理执法人员在农田、场院等场所进行农业机械安全监督检查时，可以采取下列措施：  
(一)向有关单位和个人了解情况，查阅、复制有关资料；  
(二)查验拖拉机、联合收割机证书、牌照及有关操作证件；  
(三)检查危及人身财产安全的农业机械的安全状况，对存在重大事故隐患的农业机械，责令当事人立即停止作业或者停止农业机械的转移，并进行维修；  
(四)责令农业机械操作人员改正违规操作行为。

**Article 40** When conducting safety supervision and inspection of agricultural machinery in farmland, yards and other places, law enforcement personnel for the safety supervision and administration of agricultural machinery may take the following measures:  
(1) inquiring about the situation from relevant entities and individuals, and consulting and copying relevant materials;  
(2) inspecting the certificates, license plates and relevant operation certificates of tractors and combine harvesters;  
(3) inspecting the safety status of agricultural machinery that endangers personal and property safety, and for agricultural machinery with major accident hazards, ordering the party to immediately stop the operation or the movement of the agricultural machinery and carry out maintenance;  
(4) ordering agricultural machinery operators to rectify illegal operation acts.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　发生农业机械事故后企图逃逸的、拒不停止存在重大事故隐患农业机械的作业或者转移的，县级以上地方人民政府农业机械化主管部门可以扣押有关农业机械及证书、牌照、操作证件。案件处理完毕或者农业机械事故肇事方提供担保的，县级以上地方人民政府农业机械化主管部门应当及时退还被扣押的农业机械及证书、牌照、操作证件。存在重大事故隐患的农业机械，其所有人或者使用人排除隐患前不得继续使用。

**Article 41** If a person attempts to flee after an agricultural machinery accident, or refuses to stop the operation or movement of agricultural machinery with major accident hazards, the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the local people's government at or above the county level may impound the relevant agricultural machinery and its certificates, license plates, and operation certificates. After the case is handled or the party at fault in the agricultural machinery accident provides a guarantee, the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the local people's government at or above the county level shall promptly return the impounded agricultural machinery and its certificates, license plates, and operation certificates. For agricultural machinery with major accident hazards, its owner or user shall not continue to use it until the hazards are eliminated.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　农业机械安全监督管理执法人员进行安全监督检查时，应当佩戴统一标志，出示行政执法证件。农业机械安全监督检查、事故勘察车辆应当在车身喷涂统一标识。

**Article 42** When carrying out safety supervision and inspection, law enforcement personnel for the safety supervision and administration of agricultural machinery shall wear uniform signs and present administrative law enforcement credentials. Vehicles used for agricultural machinery safety supervision and inspection and accident investigation shall display uniform identification marks on their bodies.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　农业机械化主管部门不得为农业机械指定维修经营者。

**Article 43** The competent department of agricultural mechanization shall not designate maintenance operators for agricultural machinery.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　农业机械化主管部门应当定期向同级公安机关交通管理部门通报拖拉机登记、检验以及有关证书、牌照、操作证件发放情况。公安机关交通管理部门应当定期向同级农业机械化主管部门通报农业机械在道路上发生的交通事故及处理情况。

**Article 44** The competent department of agricultural mechanization shall regularly notify the traffic management department of the public security organ at the same level of the registration, inspection, and issuance of relevant certificates, license plates, and operation certificates for tractors. The traffic management department of the public security organ shall regularly notify the competent department of agricultural mechanization at the same level of the traffic accidents involving agricultural machinery on roads and their handling.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上地方人民政府农业机械化主管部门、工业主管部门、市场监督管理部门及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)不依法对拖拉机、联合收割机实施安全检验、登记，或者不依法核发拖拉机、联合收割机证书、牌照的；  
(二)对未经考试合格者核发拖拉机、联合收割机操作证件，或者对经考试合格者拒不核发拖拉机、联合收割机操作证件的；  
(三)不依法处理农业机械事故，或者不依法出具农业机械事故认定书和其他证明材料的；  
(四)在农业机械生产、销售等过程中不依法履行监督管理职责的；  
(五)其他未依照本条例的规定履行职责的行为。

**Article 45** If the competent department of agricultural mechanization, the competent department of industry, or the market supervision and administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level, or any of their staff members, commits any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a sanction according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to conduct safety inspection or registration of tractors or combine harvesters according to law, or failing to issue certificates or license plates for tractors or combine harvesters according to law;  
(2) issuing operation certificates for tractors or combine harvesters to persons who have not passed the examination, or refusing to issue operation certificates for tractors or combine harvesters to persons who have passed the examination;  
(3) failing to handle agricultural machinery accidents according to law, or failing to issue agricultural machinery accident confirmation documents and other evidentiary materials according to law;  
(4) failing to perform the duty of supervision and administration according to law in the production, sale, or other processes of agricultural machinery;  
(5) other acts of failing to perform duties in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　生产、销售利用残次零配件或者报废农业机械的发动机、方向机、变速器、车架等部件拼装的农业机械的，由县级以上人民政府市场监督管理部门责令停止生产、销售，没收违法所得和违法生产、销售的农业机械，并处违法产品货值金额1倍以上3倍以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销营业执照。  
农业机械生产者、销售者违反工业产品生产许可证管理、认证认可管理、安全技术标准管理以及产品质量管理的，依照有关法律、行政法规处罚。

**Article 46** If any person produces or sells agricultural machinery assembled from defective parts or components, or engines, steering gears, transmissions, frames and other parts of scrapped agricultural machinery, the market supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of production and sale, confiscate the illegal gains and the illegally produced or sold agricultural machinery, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the value of the illegal products; if the circumstances are serious, the business license shall be revoked.  
If an agricultural machinery producer or seller violates the administration of industrial product production licenses, certification and accreditation, safety technical standards, or product quality, it shall be penalized in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　农业机械销售者未依照本条例的规定建立、保存销售记录的，由县级以上人民政府市场监督管理部门责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正的，处1000元以上1万元以下罚款，并责令停业整顿；情节严重的，吊销营业执照。

**Article 47** If an agricultural machinery seller fails to establish or maintain sales records in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the market supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections and give it a warning; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be fined not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan, and be ordered to suspend business for rectification; if the circumstances are serious, the business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　从事农业机械维修经营不符合[本条例第十八条](#art-18)规定的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业机械化主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处5000元以上1万元以下罚款。

**Article 48** If a person engaged in the maintenance business of agricultural machinery does not meet the requirements of Article 18 of these Regulations, the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order him or her to make corrections; if he or she refuses to make corrections, he or she shall be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　农业机械维修经营者使用不符合农业机械安全技术标准的配件维修农业机械，或者拼装、改装农业机械整机，或者承揽维修已经达到报废条件的农业机械的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业机械化主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法经营额1倍以上2倍以下罚款；拒不改正的，处违法经营额2倍以上5倍以下罚款。

**Article 49** If an agricultural machinery maintenance business operator uses parts and accessories that do not conform to the safety technical standards for agricultural machinery to maintain agricultural machinery, or assembles or modifies complete agricultural machinery, or undertakes the maintenance of agricultural machinery that has reached the scrapping conditions, the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order him or her to make corrections, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the illegal business amount; if he or she refuses to make corrections, he or she shall be fined not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal business amount.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　未按照规定办理登记手续并取得相应的证书和牌照，擅自将拖拉机、联合收割机投入使用，或者未按照规定办理变更登记手续的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业机械化主管部门责令限期补办相关手续；逾期不补办的，责令停止使用；拒不停止使用的，扣押拖拉机、联合收割机，并处200元以上2000元以下罚款。  
当事人补办相关手续的，应当及时退还扣押的拖拉机、联合收割机。

**Article 50** If any person uses a tractor or combine harvester without completing registration procedures and obtaining the corresponding certificates and license plates in accordance with regulations, or fails to complete change registration procedures in accordance with regulations, the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the person to complete the relevant procedures within a specified period; if the person fails to complete them within the specified period, the person shall be ordered to stop using the machinery; if the person refuses to stop using it, the tractor or combine harvester shall be impounded, and a fine of not less than 200 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
If the party completes the relevant procedures, the impounded tractor or combine harvester shall be promptly returned.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　伪造、变造或者使用伪造、变造的拖拉机、联合收割机证书和牌照的，或者使用其他拖拉机、联合收割机的证书和牌照的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业机械化主管部门收缴伪造、变造或者使用的证书和牌照，对违法行为人予以批评教育，并处200元以上2000元以下罚款。

**Article 51** If any person forges, alters, or uses forged or altered certificates or license plates for tractors or combine harvesters, or uses certificates or license plates of other tractors or combine harvesters, the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the local people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the forged, altered or used certificates and license plates, criticize and educate the person who commits the illegal act, and impose a fine of not less than 200 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　未取得拖拉机、联合收割机操作证件而操作拖拉机、联合收割机的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业机械化主管部门责令改正，处100元以上500元以下罚款。

**Article 52** If any person operates a tractor or combine harvester without obtaining an operation certificate for tractors or combine harvesters, the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the person to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 100 yuan but not more than 500 yuan.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　拖拉机、联合收割机操作人员操作与本人操作证件规定不相符的拖拉机、联合收割机，或者操作未按照规定登记、检验或者检验不合格、安全设施不全、机件失效的拖拉机、联合收割机，或者使用国家管制的精神药品、麻醉品后操作拖拉机、联合收割机，或者患有妨碍安全操作的疾病操作拖拉机、联合收割机的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业机械化主管部门对违法行为人予以批评教育，责令改正；拒不改正的，处100元以上500元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销有关人员的操作证件。

**Article 53** If a tractor or combine harvester operator operates a tractor or combine harvester that does not match the specifications of the operator's own operation certificate, or operates a tractor or combine harvester that has not been registered or inspected in accordance with regulations, or has been inspected and found unqualified, has incomplete safety facilities, or has failed parts, or operates a tractor or combine harvester after using psychotropic drugs or narcotics controlled by the State, or operates a tractor or combine harvester while suffering from a disease that impairs safe operation, the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the local people's government at or above the county level shall criticize and educate the person who commits the illegal act and order him or her to make corrections; if he or she refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 100 yuan but not more than 500 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the operation certificate of the person concerned shall be revoked.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　使用拖拉机、联合收割机违反规定载人的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业机械化主管部门对违法行为人予以批评教育，责令改正；拒不改正的，扣押拖拉机、联合收割机的证书、牌照；情节严重的，吊销有关人员的操作证件。非法从事经营性道路旅客运输的，由交通主管部门依照道路运输管理法律、行政法规处罚。  
当事人改正违法行为的，应当及时退还扣押的拖拉机、联合收割机的证书、牌照。

**Article 54** If any person uses a tractor or combine harvester to carry passengers in violation of regulations, the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the local people's government at or above the county level shall criticize and educate the person who commits the illegal act and order him or her to make corrections; if he or she refuses to make corrections, the certificates and license plates of the tractor or combine harvester shall be impounded; if the circumstances are serious, the operation certificate of the person concerned shall be revoked. If any person illegally engages in commercial road passenger transport, the competent department of transportation shall impose penalties in accordance with laws and administrative regulations on road transport administration.  
If the party corrects the illegal act, the impounded certificates and license plates of the tractor or combine harvester shall be promptly returned.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　经检验、检查发现农业机械存在事故隐患，经农业机械化主管部门告知拒不排除并继续使用的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业机械化主管部门对违法行为人予以批评教育，责令改正；拒不改正的，责令停止使用；拒不停止使用的，扣押存在事故隐患的农业机械。  
事故隐患排除后，应当及时退还扣押的农业机械。

**Article 55** If, after inspection or examination, an agricultural machine is found to have potential accident hazards, and the owner is informed by the competent department of agricultural mechanization but refuses to eliminate the hazards and continues to use it, the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the local people's government at or above the county level shall criticize and educate the person who commits the illegal act and order him or her to make corrections; if he or she refuses to make corrections, the person shall be ordered to stop using the machinery; if he or she refuses to stop using it, the agricultural machinery with potential accident hazards shall be impounded.  
After the potential accident hazards are eliminated, the impounded agricultural machinery shall be promptly returned.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本条例规定，造成他人人身伤亡或者财产损失的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 56** Any person who violates the provisions of these Regulations and causes personal injury, death or property damage to others shall bear civil liability according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration punishment shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　本条例所称危及人身财产安全的农业机械，是指对人身财产安全可能造成损害的农业机械，包括拖拉机、联合收割机、机动植保机械、机动脱粒机、饲料粉碎机、插秧机、铡草机等。

**Article 57** For the purpose of these Regulations, agricultural machinery that endangers personal and property safety means agricultural machinery that may cause damage to personal and property safety, including tractors, combine harvesters, motorized plant protection machinery, motorized threshers, feed grinders, rice transplanters, straw choppers, and the like.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　本条例规定的农业机械证书、牌照、操作证件，由国务院农业机械化主管部门会同国务院有关部门统一规定式样，由国务院农业机械化主管部门监制。

**Article 58** The styles of the agricultural machinery certificates, license plates, and operation certificates specified in these Regulations shall be uniformly prescribed by the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the State Council together with the relevant departments of the State Council, and shall be produced under the supervision of the competent department of agricultural mechanization of the State Council.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　拖拉机操作证件考试收费、安全技术检验收费和牌证的工本费，应当严格执行国务院价格主管部门核定的收费标准。

**Article 59** The fees for tractor operation certificate examinations, safety technical inspections, and the cost of license plates and certificates shall be strictly implemented in accordance with the fee standards approved by the competent department of pricing of the State Council.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　本条例自2009年11月1日起施行。

**Article 60** These Regulations shall come into force as of November 1, 2009.
