# 农业转基因生物安全管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2017-10-07  
**生效日期**：2017-10-07

> (2001年5月23日中华人民共和国国务院令第304号公布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2017年10月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强农业转基因生物安全管理，保障人体健康和动植物、微生物安全，保护生态环境，促进农业转基因生物技术研究，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事农业转基因生物的研究、试验、生产、加工、经营和进口、出口活动，必须遵守本条例。 第三条　本条例所称农业转基因生物，是指利用基因工程技术改变基因组构成，用于农业生产或者农产品加工的动植物、微生物及其产品，主要包括： (一)转基因动植物(含种子、种畜禽、水产苗种)和微生物； (二)转基因动植物、微生物产品； (三)转基因农产品的直接加工品； (四)含有转基因动植物、微生物或者其产品成份的种子、种畜禽、水产苗种、农药、兽药、肥料和添加剂等产品。 本条例所称农业转基因生物安全，是指防范农业转基因生物对人类、动植物、微生物和生态环境构成的危险或者潜在风险。 第四条　国务院农业行政主管部门负责全国农业转基因生物安全的监督管理工作。 县级以上地方各级人民政府农业行政主管部门负责本行政区域内的农业转基因生物安全的监督管理工作。 县级以上各级人民政府有关部门依照《中华人民共和国食品安全法》的有关规定，负责转基因食品安全的监督管理工作。 第五条　国务院建立农业转基因生物安全管理部际联席会议制度。 农业转基因生物安全管理部际联席会议由农业、科技、环境保护、卫生、外经贸、检验检疫等有关部门的负责人组成，负责研究、协调农业转基因生物安全管理工作中的重大问题。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强农业转基因生物安全管理，保障人体健康和动植物、微生物安全，保护生态环境，促进农业转基因生物技术研究，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the safety management of agricultural genetically modified organisms, protect human health, the safety of animals, plants and microorganisms, safeguard the ecological environment, and promote research on agricultural genetically modified organism technologies.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事农业转基因生物的研究、试验、生产、加工、经营和进口、出口活动，必须遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Any entity or individual engaged in research, testing, production, processing, operation, import or export of agricultural genetically modified organisms within the territory of the People's Republic of China must comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称农业转基因生物，是指利用基因工程技术改变基因组构成，用于农业生产或者农产品加工的动植物、微生物及其产品，主要包括：  
(一)转基因动植物(含种子、种畜禽、水产苗种)和微生物；  
(二)转基因动植物、微生物产品；  
(三)转基因农产品的直接加工品；  
(四)含有转基因动植物、微生物或者其产品成份的种子、种畜禽、水产苗种、农药、兽药、肥料和添加剂等产品。  
本条例所称农业转基因生物安全，是指防范农业转基因生物对人类、动植物、微生物和生态环境构成的危险或者潜在风险。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Regulations, "agricultural genetically modified organisms" (hereinafter referred to as "Agricultural GMOs") refer to animals, plants, microorganisms and their products, the genomic composition of which has been altered through the use of genetic engineering technologies, which are used for agricultural production or the processing of agricultural products. They mainly include:  
(1) transgenic animals, plants (including seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, and aquatic fry) and microorganisms;  
(2) products of transgenic animals, plants and microorganisms;  
(3) directly processed products of transgenic agricultural products;  
(4) products such as seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, aquatic fry, pesticides, veterinary drugs, fertilizers and additives containing components of transgenic animals, plants, microorganisms or their products.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, the safety of Agricultural GMOs refers to preventing the danger or potential risk posed by Agricultural GMOs to humans, animals, plants, microorganisms and the ecological environment.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院农业行政主管部门负责全国农业转基因生物安全的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府农业行政主管部门负责本行政区域内的农业转基因生物安全的监督管理工作。  
县级以上各级人民政府有关部门依照《中华人民共和国食品安全法》的有关规定，负责转基因食品安全的监督管理工作。

**Article 4** The competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the safety of Agricultural GMOs nationwide.  
The competent administrative departments for agriculture of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the safety of Agricultural GMOs within their respective administrative regions.  
The relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, be responsible for the supervision and administration of the safety of genetically modified food.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院建立农业转基因生物安全管理部际联席会议制度。  
农业转基因生物安全管理部际联席会议由农业、科技、环境保护、卫生、外经贸、检验检疫等有关部门的负责人组成，负责研究、协调农业转基因生物安全管理工作中的重大问题。

**Article 5** The State Council shall establish an inter-ministerial joint conference system for the safety management of Agricultural GMOs.  
The inter-ministerial joint conference for the safety management of Agricultural GMOs shall be composed of responsible persons from the relevant departments such as agriculture, science and technology, environmental protection, health, foreign trade and economic cooperation, and inspection and quarantine, and shall be responsible for studying and coordinating major issues in the safety management of Agricultural GMOs.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家对农业转基因生物安全实行分级管理评价制度。  
农业转基因生物按照其对人类、动植物、微生物和生态环境的危险程度，分为I、Ⅱ、Ⅲ、Ⅳ四个等级。具体划分标准由国务院农业行政主管部门制定。

**Article 6** The State shall implement a graded management and evaluation system for the safety of Agricultural GMOs.  
Agricultural GMOs shall be classified into four grades (I, II, III and IV) according to the degree of danger they pose to humans, animals, plants, microorganisms and the ecological environment. The specific classification standards shall be formulated by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家建立农业转基因生物安全评价制度。  
农业转基因生物安全评价的标准和技术规范，由国务院农业行政主管部门制定。

**Article 7** The State shall establish a safety assessment system for Agricultural GMOs.  
The standards and technical specifications for the safety assessment of Agricultural GMOs shall be formulated by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家对农业转基因生物实行标识制度。  
实施标识管理的农业转基因生物目录，由国务院农业行政主管部门商国务院有关部门制定、调整并公布。

**Article 8** The State shall implement a labeling system for Agricultural GMOs.  
The catalogue of Agricultural GMOs subject to labeling management shall be formulated, adjusted and published by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council in consultation with the relevant departments of the State Council.

### 第二章　研究与试验

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院农业行政主管部门应当加强农业转基因生物研究与试验的安全评价管理工作，并设立农业转基因生物安全委员会，负责农业转基因生物的安全评价工作。  
农业转基因生物安全委员会由从事农业转基因生物研究、生产、加工、检验检疫以及卫生、环境保护等方面的专家组成。

**Article 9** The competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council shall strengthen the safety assessment management of research and testing of Agricultural GMOs, and shall establish a Safety Committee for Agricultural GMOs, which shall be responsible for the safety assessment of Agricultural GMOs.  
The Safety Committee for Agricultural GMOs shall be composed of experts engaged in research, production, processing, inspection and quarantine of Agricultural GMOs, as well as health, environmental protection and other related fields.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院农业行政主管部门根据农业转基因生物安全评价工作的需要，可以委托具备检测条件和能力的技术检测机构对农业转基因生物进行检测。

**Article 10** The competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council may, as needed for the safety assessment of Agricultural GMOs, entrust technical testing institutions with the necessary testing conditions and capabilities to test Agricultural GMOs.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　从事农业转基因生物研究与试验的单位，应当具备与安全等级相适应的安全设施和措施，确保农业转基因生物研究与试验的安全，并成立农业转基因生物安全小组，负责本单位农业转基因生物研究与试验的安全工作。

**Article 11** Units engaged in research and testing of Agricultural GMOs shall have safety facilities and measures commensurate with the safety grade, ensure the safety of research and testing of Agricultural GMOs, and establish a Safety Group for Agricultural GMOs to be responsible for the safety work of the unit's research and testing of Agricultural GMOs.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　从事Ⅲ、Ⅳ级农业转基因生物研究的，应当在研究开始前向国务院农业行政主管部门报告。

**Article 12** Anyone engaged in research on Agricultural GMOs of Grade III or IV shall report to the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council before the start of the research.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　农业转基因生物试验，一般应当经过中间试验、环境释放和生产性试验三个阶段。  
中间试验，是指在控制系统内或者控制条件下进行的小规模试验。  
环境释放，是指在自然条件下采取相应安全措施所进行的中规模的试验。  
生产性试验，是指在生产和应用前进行的较大规模的试验。

**Article 13** Testing of Agricultural GMOs shall generally undergo three stages: intermediate testing, environmental release, and production testing.  
Intermediate testing means small-scale testing carried out within a control system or under controlled conditions.  
Environmental release means medium-scale testing carried out under natural conditions with corresponding safety measures.  
Production testing means large-scale testing carried out before production and application.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　农业转基因生物在实验室研究结束后，需要转入中间试验的，试验单位应当向国务院农业行政主管部门报告。

**Article 14** After the completion of laboratory research on Agricultural GMOs, if it is necessary to proceed to intermediate testing, the testing unit shall report to the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　农业转基因生物试验需要从上一试验阶段转入下一试验阶段的，试验单位应当向国务院农业行政主管部门提出申请；经农业转基因生物安全委员会进行安全评价合格的，由国务院农业行政主管部门批准转入下一试验阶段。  
试验单位提出前款申请，应当提供下列材料：  
(一)农业转基因生物的安全等级和确定安全等级的依据；  
(二)农业转基因生物技术检测机构出具的检测报告；  
(三)相应的安全管理、防范措施；  
(四)上一试验阶段的试验报告。

**Article 15** If it is necessary to proceed from a previous testing stage to the next testing stage for Agricultural GMOs, the testing unit shall submit an application to the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council; after passing the safety assessment by the Safety Committee for Agricultural GMOs, the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council shall approve the move to the next testing stage.  
When submitting the application referred to in the preceding paragraph, the testing unit shall provide the following materials:  
(1) the safety grade of the Agricultural GMO and the basis for determining the safety grade;  
(2) a test report issued by a technical testing institution for Agricultural GMOs;  
(3) corresponding safety management and preventive measures;  
(4) a testing report from the previous testing stage.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　从事农业转基因生物试验的单位在生产性试验结束后，可以向国务院农业行政主管部门申请领取农业转基因生物安全证书。  
试验单位提出前款申请，应当提供下列材料：  
(一)农业转基因生物的安全等级和确定安全等级的依据；  
(二)生产性试验的总结报告；  
(三)国务院农业行政主管部门规定的试验材料、检测方法等其他材料。  
国务院农业行政主管部门收到申请后，应当委托具备检测条件和能力的技术检测机构进行检测，并组织农业转基因生物安全委员会进行安全评价；安全评价合格的，方可颁发农业转基因生物安全证书。

**Article 16** After the completion of production testing, a unit engaged in testing of Agricultural GMOs may apply to the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council for a Safety Certificate for Agricultural GMOs.  
When submitting the application referred to in the preceding paragraph, the testing unit shall provide the following materials:  
(1) the safety grade of the Agricultural GMO and the basis for determining the safety grade;  
(2) a summary report of the production testing;  
(3) other materials such as testing materials and testing methods as prescribed by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council.  
Upon receipt of the application, the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council shall entrust a technical testing institution with the necessary testing conditions and capabilities to conduct testing, and organize the Safety Committee for Agricultural GMOs to carry out a safety assessment; only after passing the safety assessment may a Safety Certificate for Agricultural GMOs be issued.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　转基因植物种子、种畜禽、水产苗种，利用农业转基因生物生产的或者含有农业转基因生物成份的种子、种畜禽、水产苗种、农药、兽药、肥料和添加剂等，在依照有关法律、行政法规的规定进行审定、登记或者评价、审批前，应当依照[本条例第十六条](#art-16)的规定取得农业转基因生物安全证书。

**Article 17** Transgenic plant seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, and aquatic fry, as well as seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, aquatic fry, pesticides, veterinary drugs, fertilizers, additives, etc., produced using Agricultural GMOs or containing components of Agricultural GMOs, shall, before undergoing examination, registration or evaluation, or approval in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations, obtain a Safety Certificate for Agricultural GMOs in accordance with the provisions of Article 16 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　中外合作、合资或者外方独资在中华人民共和国境内从事农业转基因生物研究与试验的，应当经国务院农业行政主管部门批准。

**Article 18** Entities engaged in research and testing of Agricultural GMOs within the territory of the People's Republic of China through Sino-foreign cooperation, joint ventures or wholly foreign-owned enterprises shall be approved by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council.

### 第三章　生产与加工

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　生产转基因植物种子、种畜禽、水产苗种，应当取得国务院农业行政主管部门颁发的种子、种畜禽、水产苗种生产许可证。  
生产单位和个人申请转基因植物种子、种畜禽、水产苗种生产许可证，除应当符合有关法律、行政法规规定的条件外，还应当符合下列条件：  
(一)取得农业转基因生物安全证书并通过品种审定；  
(二)在指定的区域种植或者养殖；  
(三)有相应的安全管理、防范措施；  
(四)国务院农业行政主管部门规定的其他条件。

**Article 19** To produce transgenic plant seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, or aquatic fry, a production license for seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, or aquatic fry issued by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council shall be obtained.  
When applying for a production license for transgenic plant seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, or aquatic fry, in addition to meeting the conditions stipulated by the relevant laws and administrative regulations, the production unit or individual shall also meet the following conditions:  
(1) having obtained a Safety Certificate for Agricultural GMOs and passed variety examination;  
(2) planting or breeding in designated areas;  
(3) having corresponding safety management and preventive measures;  
(4) other conditions stipulated by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　生产转基因植物种子、种畜禽、水产苗种的单位和个人，应当建立生产档案，载明生产地点、基因及其来源、转基因的方法以及种子、种畜禽、水产苗种流向等内容。

**Article 20** Units or individuals producing transgenic plant seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, or aquatic fry shall establish production archives, recording the production site, gene and its source, method of transgenesis, and the flow direction of seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, and aquatic fry.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　单位和个人从事农业转基因生物生产、加工的，应当由国务院农业行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业行政主管部门批准。具体办法由国务院农业行政主管部门制定。

**Article 21** Units or individuals engaged in the production or processing of Agricultural GMOs shall obtain approval from the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council or the competent administrative department for agriculture of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　从事农业转基因生物生产、加工的单位和个人，应当按照批准的品种、范围、安全管理要求和相应的技术标准组织生产、加工，并定期向所在地县级人民政府农业行政主管部门提供生产、加工、安全管理情况和产品流向的报告。

**Article 22** Units or individuals engaged in the production or processing of Agricultural GMOs shall organize production and processing in accordance with the approved varieties, scope, safety management requirements and corresponding technical standards, and shall periodically provide a report on production, processing, safety management and product flow direction to the competent administrative department for agriculture of the local people's government at the county level.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　农业转基因生物在生产、加工过程中发生基因安全事故时，生产、加工单位和个人应当立即采取安全补救措施，并向所在地县级人民政府农业行政主管部门报告。

**Article 23** In the event of a genetic safety incident during the production or processing of Agricultural GMOs, the production or processing unit or individual shall immediately take safety remedial measures and report to the competent administrative department for agriculture of the local people's government at the county level.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　从事农业转基因生物运输、贮存的单位和个人，应当采取与农业转基因生物安全等级相适应的安全控制措施，确保农业转基因生物运输、贮存的安全。

**Article 24** Units or individuals engaged in the transportation or storage of Agricultural GMOs shall take safety control measures commensurate with the safety grade of the Agricultural GMOs to ensure the safety of their transportation and storage.

### 第四章　经营

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　经营转基因植物种子、种畜禽、水产苗种的单位和个人，应当取得国务院农业行政主管部门颁发的种子、种畜禽、水产苗种经营许可证。  
经营单位和个人申请转基因植物种子、种畜禽、水产苗种经营许可证，除应当符合有关法律、行政法规规定的条件外，还应当符合下列条件：  
(一)有专门的管理人员和经营档案；  
(二)有相应的安全管理、防范措施；  
(三)国务院农业行政主管部门规定的其他条件。

**Article 25** Units or individuals operating transgenic plant seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, or aquatic fry shall obtain a business license for seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, or aquatic fry issued by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council.  
When applying for a business license for transgenic plant seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, or aquatic fry, in addition to meeting the conditions stipulated by the relevant laws and administrative regulations, the business unit or individual shall also meet the following conditions:  
(1) having specialized management personnel and business archives;  
(2) having corresponding safety management and preventive measures;  
(3) other conditions stipulated by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　经营转基因植物种子、种畜禽、水产苗种的单位和个人，应当建立经营档案，载明种子、种畜禽、水产苗种的来源、贮存、运输和销售去向等内容。

**Article 26** Units or individuals operating transgenic plant seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, or aquatic fry shall establish business archives, recording the source, storage, transportation and sales destination of the seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, or aquatic fry.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　在中华人民共和国境内销售列入农业转基因生物目录的农业转基因生物，应当有明显的标识。  
列入农业转基因生物目录的农业转基因生物，由生产、分装单位和个人负责标识；未标识的，不得销售。经营单位和个人在进货时，应当对货物和标识进行核对。经营单位和个人拆开原包装进行销售的，应当重新标识。

**Article 27** Any Agricultural GMO listed in the catalogue of Agricultural GMOs sold within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall have a clear label.  
The Agricultural GMOs listed in the catalogue of Agricultural GMOs shall be labeled by the production unit or individual or the repackaging unit or individual; those not labeled shall not be sold. When purchasing goods, business units or individuals shall check the goods and labels. If business units or individuals break the original packaging for sale, they shall re-label.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　农业转基因生物标识应当载明产品中含有转基因成份的主要原料名称；有特殊销售范围要求的，还应当载明销售范围，并在指定范围内销售。

**Article 28** The label of an Agricultural GMO shall indicate the name of the main raw material containing transgenic components in the product; if there are special sales scope requirements, the sales scope shall also be indicated, and the product shall be sold within the designated scope.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　农业转基因生物的广告，应当经国务院农业行政主管部门审查批准后，方可刊登、播放、设置和张贴。

**Article 29** Advertisements for Agricultural GMOs may be published, broadcast, displayed or posted only after examination and approval by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council.

### 第五章　进口与出口

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　从中华人民共和国境外引进农业转基因生物用于研究、试验的，引进单位应当向国务院农业行政主管部门提出申请；符合下列条件的，国务院农业行政主管部门方可批准：  
(一)具有国务院农业行政主管部门规定的申请资格；  
(二)引进的农业转基因生物在国(境)外已经进行了相应的研究、试验；  
(三)有相应的安全管理、防范措施。

**Article 30** To introduce Agricultural GMOs from outside the territory of the People's Republic of China for research or testing, the introducing unit shall submit an application to the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council; the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council may approve the application only if the following conditions are met:  
(1) the applicant has the qualifications for application prescribed by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council;  
(2) the introduced Agricultural GMOs have already undergone corresponding research and testing in the foreign country (or region);  
(3) corresponding safety management and preventive measures are in place.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　境外公司向中华人民共和国出口转基因植物种子、种畜禽、水产苗种和利用农业转基因生物生产的或者含有农业转基因生物成份的植物种子、种畜禽、水产苗种、农药、兽药、肥料和添加剂的，应当向国务院农业行政主管部门提出申请；符合下列条件的，国务院农业行政主管部门方可批准试验材料入境并依照本条例的规定进行中间试验、环境释放和生产性试验：  
(一)输出国家或者地区已经允许作为相应用途并投放市场；  
(二)输出国家或者地区经过科学试验证明对人类、动植物、微生物和生态环境无害；  
(三)有相应的安全管理、防范措施。  
生产性试验结束后，经安全评价合格，并取得农业转基因生物安全证书后，方可依照有关法律、行政法规的规定办理审定、登记或者评价、审批手续。

**Article 31** If an overseas company exports to the People's Republic of China transgenic plant seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, aquatic fry, or plant seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, aquatic fry, pesticides, veterinary drugs, fertilizers, additives, etc., produced using or containing components of Agricultural GMOs, it shall submit an application to the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council; the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council may approve the entry of testing materials and, in accordance with these Regulations, carry out intermediate testing, environmental release and production testing only if the following conditions are met:  
(1) the exporting country or region has already permitted them for corresponding use and placed them on the market;  
(2) scientific tests in the exporting country or region have proven them harmless to humans, animals, plants, microorganisms and the ecological environment;  
(3) corresponding safety management and preventive measures are in place.  
After the completion of production testing, upon passing the safety assessment and obtaining a Safety Certificate for Agricultural GMOs, the procedures for examination, registration or evaluation, and approval may be completed in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　境外公司向中华人民共和国出口农业转基因生物用作加工原料的，应当向国务院农业行政主管部门提出申请，提交国务院农业行政主管部门要求的试验材料、检测方法等材料；符合下列条件，经国务院农业行政主管部门委托的、具备检测条件和能力的技术检测机构检测确认对人类、动植物、微生物和生态环境不存在危险，并经安全评价合格的，由国务院农业行政主管部门颁发农业转基因生物安全证书：  
(一)输出国家或者地区已经允许作为相应用途并投放市场；  
(二)输出国家或者地区经过科学试验证明对人类、动植物、微生物和生态环境无害；  
(三)有相应的安全管理、防范措施。

**Article 32** If an overseas company exports to the People's Republic of China Agricultural GMOs to be used as processing raw materials, it shall submit an application to the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council, providing testing materials, testing methods and other materials as required by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council; only if the following conditions are met, and after a technical testing institution entrusted by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council and having the necessary testing conditions and capabilities confirms through testing that they pose no danger to humans, animals, plants, microorganisms and the ecological environment, and after passing the safety assessment, shall the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council issue a Safety Certificate for Agricultural GMOs:  
(1) the exporting country or region has already permitted them for corresponding use and placed them on the market;  
(2) scientific tests in the exporting country or region have proven them harmless to humans, animals, plants, microorganisms and the ecological environment;  
(3) corresponding safety management and preventive measures are in place.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　从中华人民共和国境外引进农业转基因生物的，或者向中华人民共和国出口农业转基因生物的，引进单位或者境外公司应当凭国务院农业行政主管部门颁发的农业转基因生物安全证书和相关批准文件，向口岸出入境检验检疫机构报检；经检疫合格后，方可向海关申请办理有关手续。

**Article 33** When introducing Agricultural GMOs from outside the territory of the People's Republic of China or exporting Agricultural GMOs to the People's Republic of China, the introducing unit or overseas company shall, with the Safety Certificate for Agricultural GMOs and relevant approval documents issued by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council, apply for inspection at the entry-exit inspection and quarantine authority at the port; only after passing the inspection may it apply to the Customs for relevant procedures.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　农业转基因生物在中华人民共和国过境转移的，应当遵守中华人民共和国有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 34** The transit and transfer of Agricultural GMOs within the People's Republic of China shall comply with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国务院农业行政主管部门应当自收到申请人申请之日起270日内作出批准或者不批准的决定，并通知申请人。

**Article 35** The competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council shall, within 270 days from the date of receiving an application, make a decision on approval or disapproval and notify the applicant.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　向中华人民共和国境外出口农产品，外方要求提供非转基因农产品证明的，由口岸出入境检验检疫机构根据国务院农业行政主管部门发布的转基因农产品信息，进行检测并出具非转基因农产品证明。

**Article 36** If, when exporting agricultural products to a destination outside the territory of the People's Republic of China, the foreign party requires a certificate proving that the products are non-transgenic, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine authority at the port shall, based on the information on transgenic agricultural products published by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council, conduct testing and issue a certificate proving that the products are non-transgenic.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　进口农业转基因生物，没有国务院农业行政主管部门颁发的农业转基因生物安全证书和相关批准文件的，或者与证书、批准文件不符的，作退货或者销毁处理。进口农业转基因生物不按照规定标识的，重新标识后方可入境。

**Article 37** If imported Agricultural GMOs are not accompanied by a Safety Certificate for Agricultural GMOs and relevant approval documents issued by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council, or if they do not conform to the certificate or documents, they shall be returned or destroyed. If imported Agricultural GMOs are not labeled in accordance with the regulations, they may be allowed entry only after being re-labeled.

### 第六章　监督检查

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　农业行政主管部门履行监督检查职责时，有权采取下列措施：  
(一)询问被检查的研究、试验、生产、加工、经营或者进口、出口的单位和个人、利害关系人、证明人，并要求其提供与农业转基因生物安全有关的证明材料或者其他资料；  
(二)查阅或者复制农业转基因生物研究、试验、生产、加工、经营或者进口、出口的有关档案、账册和资料等；  
(三)要求有关单位和个人就有关农业转基因生物安全的问题作出说明；  
(四)责令违反农业转基因生物安全管理的单位和个人停止违法行为；  
(五)在紧急情况下，对非法研究、试验、生产、加工、经营或者进口、出口的农业转基因生物实施封存或者扣押。

**Article 38** When performing the duties of supervision and inspection, the competent administrative department for agriculture shall have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) to inquire into the inspected units or individuals engaged in research, testing, production, processing, operation or import or export, interested parties, and witnesses, and to require them to provide evidentiary materials or other information related to the safety of Agricultural GMOs;  
(2) to inspect or copy relevant archives, account books, materials, etc., concerning the research, testing, production, processing, operation or import or export of Agricultural GMOs;  
(3) to require the relevant units or individuals to provide explanations on matters concerning the safety of Agricultural GMOs;  
(4) to order units or individuals that violate the safety management of Agricultural GMOs to cease their illegal acts;  
(5) in an emergency, to seal up or seize Agricultural GMOs that are illegally researched, tested, produced, processed, operated or imported or exported.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　农业行政主管部门工作人员在监督检查时，应当出示执法证件。

**Article 39** When conducting supervision and inspection, the staff members of the competent administrative department for agriculture shall present their law enforcement credentials.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　有关单位和个人对农业行政主管部门的监督检查，应当予以支持、配合，不得拒绝、阻碍监督检查人员依法执行职务。

**Article 40** The relevant units or individuals shall support and cooperate with the supervision and inspection conducted by the competent administrative department for agriculture, and shall not refuse or obstruct the law enforcement personnel from performing their duties according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　发现农业转基因生物对人类、动植物和生态环境存在危险时，国务院农业行政主管部门有权宣布禁止生产、加工、经营和进口，收回农业转基因生物安全证书，销毁有关存在危险的农业转基因生物。

**Article 41** When it is found that an Agricultural GMO poses a threat to humans, animals, plants or the ecological environment, the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council shall have the authority to declare the prohibition of its production, processing, operation and import, to revoke the Safety Certificate for Agricultural GMOs, and to destroy the Agricultural GMO that poses the threat.

### 第七章　罚则

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本条例规定，从事Ⅲ、Ⅳ级农业转基因生物研究或者进行中间试验，未向国务院农业行政主管部门报告的，由国务院农业行政主管部门责令暂停研究或者中间试验，限期改正。

**Article 42** In case of a violation of these Regulations by engaging in research on Agricultural GMOs of Grade III or IV or conducting intermediate testing without reporting to the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council, the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council shall order the suspension of research or intermediate testing and impose a time limit for correction.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反本条例规定，未经批准擅自从事环境释放、生产性试验的，已获批准但未按照规定采取安全管理、防范措施的，或者超过批准范围进行试验的，由国务院农业行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业行政主管部门依据职权，责令停止试验，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 43** In case of a violation of these Regulations by engaging in environmental release or production testing without approval, by failing to take safety management and preventive measures as required despite approval having been obtained, or by conducting testing beyond the approved scope, the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council or the competent administrative department for agriculture of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within the scope of its authority, order the cessation of testing and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反本条例规定，在生产性试验结束后，未取得农业转基因生物安全证书，擅自将农业转基因生物投入生产和应用的，由国务院农业行政主管部门责令停止生产和应用，并处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 44** In case of a violation of these Regulations by, after the completion of production testing and without obtaining a Safety Certificate for Agricultural GMOs, putting an Agricultural GMO into production and application without authorization, the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council shall order the cessation of production and application and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反[本条例第十八条](#art-18)规定，未经国务院农业行政主管部门批准，从事农业转基因生物研究与试验的，由国务院农业行政主管部门责令立即停止研究与试验，限期补办审批手续。

**Article 45** In case of a violation of the provisions of Article 18 of these Regulations by engaging in research and testing of Agricultural GMOs without the approval of the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council, the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council shall order the immediate cessation of research and testing and impose a time limit for completing the approval procedures retroactively.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本条例规定，未经批准生产、加工农业转基因生物或者未按照批准的品种、范围、安全管理要求和技术标准生产、加工的，由国务院农业行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业行政主管部门依据职权，责令停止生产或者加工，没收违法生产或者加工的产品及违法所得；违法所得10万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足10万元的，并处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 46** In case of a violation of these Regulations by producing or processing Agricultural GMOs without approval, or by producing or processing not in accordance with the approved varieties, scope, safety management requirements and technical standards, the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council or the competent administrative department for agriculture of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within the scope of its authority, order the cessation of production or processing, confiscate the illegally produced or processed products and illegal gains; if the illegal gains are not less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反本条例规定，转基因植物种子、种畜禽、水产苗种的生产、经营单位和个人，未按照规定制作、保存生产、经营档案的，由县级以上人民政府农业行政主管部门依据职权，责令改正，处1000元以上1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 47** In case of a violation of these Regulations by production or business units or individuals of transgenic plant seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, or aquatic fry failing to create or preserve production or business archives in accordance with the regulations, the competent administrative department for agriculture of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within the scope of its authority, order a correction and impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本条例规定，未经国务院农业行政主管部门批准，擅自进口农业转基因生物的，由国务院农业行政主管部门责令停止进口，没收已进口的产品和违法所得；违法所得10万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足10万元的，并处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 48** In case of a violation of these Regulations by importing Agricultural GMOs without the approval of the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council, the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council shall order the cessation of import, confiscate the imported products and illegal gains; if the illegal gains are not less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　违反本条例规定，进口、携带、邮寄农业转基因生物未向口岸出入境检验检疫机构报检的，由口岸出入境检验检疫机构比照进出境动植物检疫法的有关规定处罚。

**Article 49** In case of a violation of these Regulations by importing, carrying or sending by post Agricultural GMOs without applying for inspection at the entry-exit inspection and quarantine authority at the port, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine authority at the port shall impose a penalty by reference to the relevant provisions of the Law on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本条例关于农业转基因生物标识管理规定的，由县级以上人民政府农业行政主管部门依据职权，责令限期改正，可以没收非法销售的产品和违法所得，并可以处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 50** In case of a violation of the provisions of these Regulations on the labeling management of Agricultural GMOs, the competent administrative department for agriculture of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within the scope of its authority, order a correction within a time limit, may confiscate the illegally sold products and illegal gains, and may impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　假冒、伪造、转让或者买卖农业转基因生物有关证明文书的，由县级以上人民政府农业行政主管部门依据职权，收缴相应的证明文书，并处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 51** In case of counterfeiting, forging, transferring or buying or selling relevant certification documents for Agricultural GMOs, the competent administrative department for agriculture of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within the scope of its authority, confiscate the relevant certification documents and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例规定，在研究、试验、生产、加工、贮存、运输、销售或者进口、出口农业转基因生物过程中发生基因安全事故，造成损害的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 52** In case of a violation of these Regulations by causing damage due to a genetic safety incident occurring in the course of research, testing, production, processing, storage, transportation, sale, import or export of Agricultural GMOs, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国务院农业行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业行政主管部门违反本条例规定核发许可证、农业转基因生物安全证书以及其他批准文件的，或者核发许可证、农业转基因生物安全证书以及其他批准文件后不履行监督管理职责的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 53** If the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council or the competent administrative department for agriculture of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government issues a license, a Safety Certificate for Agricultural GMOs or other approval documents in violation of these Regulations, or fails to perform its supervision and administration duties after issuing such documents, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 54** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
