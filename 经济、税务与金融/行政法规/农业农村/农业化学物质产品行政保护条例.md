# 农业化学物质产品行政保护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2020-11-29  
**生效日期**：2020-11-29

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了扩大对外经济技术合作与交流，对外国农业化学物质产品独占权人的合法权益给予行政保护，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of expanding foreign economic and technological cooperation and exchanges and providing administrative protection for the lawful rights and interests of exclusive rights holders of foreign agrochemical products.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称农业化学物质产品，是指用于农业生产的化学合成农药，即用化学合成方法生产的除草剂、杀虫剂、杀菌剂、灭鼠剂、植物生长调节剂。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "agrochemical products" means chemically synthesized pesticides used in agricultural production, namely herbicides, insecticides, fungicides, rodenticides, and plant growth regulators produced by chemical synthesis.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　凡与中华人民共和国缔结有关农业化学物质产品行政保护双边条约或者协定的国家、地区的企业和其他组织以及个人，都可以依照本条例申请农业化学物质产品行政保护。

**Article 3** Any enterprise, other organization, or individual from a country or region that has concluded a bilateral treaty or agreement with the People's Republic of China on administrative protection of agrochemical products may apply for administrative protection of agrochemical products in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院化学工业行政主管部门受理和审查农业化学物质产品行政保护的申请，对符合本条例规定的农业化学物质产品给予行政保护，对申请人颁发农业化学物质产品行政保护证书。

**Article 4** The administrative department for chemical industry under the State Council shall accept and examine applications for administrative protection of agrochemical products, grant administrative protection to agrochemical products meeting the requirements of these Regulations, and issue a certificate of administrative protection for agrochemical products to the applicant.

### 第二章　行政保护的申请

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　申请行政保护的农业化学物质产品应当具备下列条件：  
(一)1993年1月1日前依照中国专利法的规定其独占权不受保护的；  
(二)1986年1月1日至1993年1月1日期间，获得禁止他人在申请人所在国制造、使用或者销售的独占权的；  
(三)提出行政保护申请日前尚未在中国销售的。

**Article 5** An agrochemical product for which administrative protection is applied shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) its exclusive right was not protected under the provisions of the Patent Law of China before January 1, 1993;  
(2) during the period from January 1, 1986 to January 1, 1993, the exclusive right to prohibit others from manufacturing, using, or selling the product in the applicant's country was obtained;  
(3) the product has not been sold in China before the date of filing the application for administrative protection.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　农业化学物质产品行政保护的申请权属于该产品的独占权人。

**Article 6** The right to apply for administrative protection of an agrochemical product shall belong to the exclusive rights holder of such product.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　外国农业化学物质产品独占权人申请行政保护，应当委托国务院化学工业行政主管部门指定的代理机构办理。

**Article 7** Where a foreign exclusive rights holder of an agrochemical product applies for administrative protection, it shall appoint an agency designated by the administrative department for chemical industry under the State Council to handle the application.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请人应当报送下列文件的中文、外文对照本：  
(一)农业化学物质产品行政保护申请书；  
(二)申请人所在国有关主管部门颁发的证明申请人享有该农业化学物质产品独占权的文件副本；  
(三)申请人所在国有关主管部门颁发的准许制造或者销售该农业化学物质产品的文件副本；  
(四)申请人与中国企业正式签订的在中国境内制造或者销售该产品的合同副本。

**Article 8** The applicant shall submit the following documents in both Chinese and foreign language versions:  
(1) a written application for administrative protection of an agrochemical product;  
(2) a copy of the document issued by the competent authority in the applicant's country certifying that the applicant enjoys the exclusive right to the agrochemical product;  
(3) a copy of the document issued by the competent authority in the applicant's country permitting the manufacture or sale of the agrochemical product;  
(4) a copy of the contract formally signed by the applicant and a Chinese enterprise for the manufacture or sale of the product within the territory of China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　外国农业化学物质产品独占权人在申请农业化学物质产品行政保护之前或者之后，应当依照中国有关法律、法规向国务院农业行政主管部门申请办理登记手续。

**Article 9** Before or after applying for administrative protection of an agrochemical product, a foreign exclusive rights holder of an agrochemical product shall apply to the administrative department for agriculture under the State Council for registration formalities in accordance with relevant Chinese laws and regulations.

### 第三章　行政保护的审查和批准

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院化学工业行政主管部门自收到行政保护申请文件之日起十五日内，进行初步审查，并分别情况作出以下处理：  
(一)申请文件符合[本条例第八条](#art-8)规定的，发给受理通知书，并予以公告。  
(二)申请文件不符合[本条例第八条](#art-8)规定的，要求申请人限期补正；过期不补正的，视为未申请。

**Article 10** The administrative department for chemical industry under the State Council shall, within fifteen days from the date of receiving the application documents for administrative protection, conduct a preliminary examination and take the following actions depending on the circumstances:  
(1) if the application documents comply with the provisions of Article 8 of these Regulations, it shall issue a notice of acceptance and make a public announcement;  
(2) if the application documents do not comply with the provisions of Article 8 of these Regulations, it shall require the applicant to make supplements within a time limit; if no supplements are made within the time limit, the application shall be deemed as not having been filed.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院化学工业行政主管部门应当自收到申请文件之日起，或者依照[本条例第十条](#art-10)第(二)项的规定，自收到补正文件之日起，六个月内审查完毕。因特殊情况不能在六个月内审查完毕的，国务院化学工业行政主管部门应当及时通知申请人，并告之理由，适当延长审查时间。  
经审查，符合本条例规定的，给予行政保护；不符合本条例规定的，不给予行政保护，并告之理由。

**Article 11** The administrative department for chemical industry under the State Council shall complete the examination within six months from the date of receiving the application documents or, in cases as specified in Article 10(2) of these Regulations, within six months from the date of receiving the supplementary documents. If the examination cannot be completed within six months due to special circumstances, the administrative department for chemical industry under the State Council shall promptly notify the applicant, inform it of the reasons, and appropriately extend the examination period.  
If, upon examination, the product meets the requirements of these Regulations, administrative protection shall be granted; if it does not meet the requirements, administrative protection shall not be granted and the reasons shall be stated.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院化学工业行政主管部门批准给予农业化学物质产品行政保护的，颁发农业化学物质产品行政保护证书，并予以公告。

**Article 12** If the administrative department for chemical industry under the State Council approves the grant of administrative protection for an agrochemical product, it shall issue a certificate of administrative protection for agrochemical products and make a public announcement.

### 第四章　行政保护的期限、终止、撤销和效力

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　农业化学物质产品的行政保护期为七年零六个月，自农业化学物质产品行政保护证书颁发之日起计算。

**Article 13** The term of administrative protection for an agrochemical product shall be seven years and six months, calculated from the date of issuance of the certificate of administrative protection for agrochemical products.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　外国农业化学物质产品独占权人应当自农业化学物质产品行政保护证书颁发的当年，开始缴纳年费。

**Article 14** A foreign exclusive rights holder of an agrochemical product shall begin to pay annual fees in the year when the certificate of administrative protection for agrochemical products is issued.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　有下列情形之一的，行政保护在期限届满前终止：  
(一)农业化学物质产品独占权在申请人所在国无效或者失效的；  
(二)农业化学物质产品独占权人没有按照规定缴纳行政保护年费的；  
(三)农业化学物质产品独占权人以书面形式声明放弃行政保护的；  
(四)农业化学物质产品独占权人自农业化学物质产品行政保护证书颁发之日起一年内未向国务院农业行政主管部门申请办理登记手续的。

**Article 15** Administrative protection shall terminate before the expiration of the term under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the exclusive right to the agrochemical product is invalid or has ceased to have effect in the applicant's country;  
(2) the exclusive rights holder of the agrochemical product fails to pay the annual fees for administrative protection in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) the exclusive rights holder of the agrochemical product renounces administrative protection in writing;  
(4) the exclusive rights holder of the agrochemical product fails to apply to the administrative department for agriculture under the State Council for registration formalities within one year from the date of issuance of the certificate of administrative protection for agrochemical products.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　农业化学物质产品行政保护证书颁发后，任何组织或者个人认为给予该产品行政保护不符合本条例规定的，都可以请求国务院化学工业行政主管部门撤销对该产品的行政保护；该产品独占权人对国务院化学工业行政主管部门的撤销决定不服的，可以向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 16** After the certificate of administrative protection for an agrochemical product is issued, any organization or individual that considers the grant of administrative protection to the product to be inconsistent with the provisions of these Regulations may request the administrative department for chemical industry under the State Council to revoke the administrative protection of the product. If the exclusive rights holder of the product does not accept the revocation decision of the administrative department for chemical industry under the State Council, it may bring a lawsuit in the People's Court.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　农业化学物质产品行政保护的终止或者撤销，由国务院化学工业行政主管部门予以公告。

**Article 17** Any termination or revocation of administrative protection of an agrochemical product shall be publicly announced by the administrative department for chemical industry under the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　未经获得农业化学物质产品行政保护的独占权人的许可，制造或者销售该农业化学物质产品的，农业化学物质产品独占权人可以请求国务院化学工业行政主管部门制止侵权行为；农业化学物质产品独占权人要求经济赔偿的，可以向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 18** If any person manufactures or sells an agrochemical product without the permission of the exclusive rights holder who has obtained administrative protection for such product, the exclusive rights holder of the agrochemical product may request the administrative department for chemical industry under the State Council to stop the infringing act. If the exclusive rights holder of the agrochemical product seeks economic compensation, it may bring a lawsuit in the People's Court.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国务院化学工业行政主管部门对申请人提供的需要保密的资料，应当采取保密措施。

**Article 19** The administrative department for chemical industry under the State Council shall take confidentiality measures for the materials requiring confidentiality provided by the applicant.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　向国务院化学工业行政主管部门申请行政保护和办理有关手续，应当按照规定缴纳费用。

**Article 20** Any application for administrative protection and handling of relevant procedures with the administrative department for chemical industry under the State Council shall be subject to the payment of fees in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本条例的实施细则由国务院化学工业行政主管部门制定。

**Article 21** Rules for the implementation of these Regulations shall be formulated by the administrative department for chemical industry under the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本条例由国务院化学工业行政主管部门负责解释。

**Article 22** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the administrative department for chemical industry under the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本条例自1993年1月1日起施行。

**Article 23** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 1993.
