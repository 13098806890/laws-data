# 自然灾害救助条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2010-09-01

> (2010年7月8日中华人民共和国国务院令第577号公布　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范自然灾害救助工作，保障受灾人员基本生活，制定本条例。 第二条　自然灾害救助工作遵循以人为本、政府主导、分级管理、社会互助、灾民自救的原则。 第三条　自然灾害救助工作实行各级人民政府行政领导负责制。 国家减灾委员会负责组织、领导全国的自然灾害救助工作，协调开展重大自然灾害救助活动。国务院应急管理部门负责全国的自然灾害救助工作，承担国家减灾委员会的具体工作。国务院有关部门按照各自职责做好全国的自然灾害救助相关工作。 县级以上地方人民政府或者人民政府的自然灾害救助应急综合协调机构，组织、协调本行政区域的自然灾害救助工作。县级以上地方人民政府应急管理部门负责本行政区域的自然灾害救助工作。县级以上地方人民政府有关部门按照各自职责做好本行政区域的自然灾害救助相关工作。 第四条　县级以上人民政府应当将自然灾害救助工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，建立健全与自然灾害救助需求相适应的资金、物资保障机制，将人民政府安排的自然灾害救助资金和自然灾害救助工作经费纳入财政预算。 第五条　村民委员会、居民委员会以及红十字会、慈善会和公募基金会等社会组织，依法协助人民政府开展自然灾害救助工作。 国家鼓励和引导单位和个人参与自然灾害救助捐赠、志愿服务等活动。 第六条　各级人民政府应当加强防灾减灾宣传教育，提高公民的防灾避险意识和自救互救能力。 村民委员会、居民委员会、企业事业单位应当根据所在地人民政府的要求，结合各自的实际情况，开展防灾减灾应急知识的宣传普及活动。 第七条　对在自然灾害救助中作出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。 第二章　救助准备 第八条　县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当根据有关法律、法规、规章，上级人民政府及其有关部门的应急预案以及本行政区域的自然灾害风险调查情况，制定相应的自然灾害救助应急预案。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范自然灾害救助工作，保障受灾人员基本生活，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of regulating natural disaster relief work and ensuring the basic living of the affected persons.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　自然灾害救助工作遵循以人为本、政府主导、分级管理、社会互助、灾民自救的原则。

**Article 2** Natural disaster relief work shall follow the principles of putting people first, government leadership, hierarchical management, social mutual assistance, and disaster victims' self-rescue.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　自然灾害救助工作实行各级人民政府行政领导负责制。  
国家减灾委员会负责组织、领导全国的自然灾害救助工作，协调开展重大自然灾害救助活动。国务院应急管理部门负责全国的自然灾害救助工作，承担国家减灾委员会的具体工作。国务院有关部门按照各自职责做好全国的自然灾害救助相关工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府或者人民政府的自然灾害救助应急综合协调机构，组织、协调本行政区域的自然灾害救助工作。县级以上地方人民政府应急管理部门负责本行政区域的自然灾害救助工作。县级以上地方人民政府有关部门按照各自职责做好本行政区域的自然灾害救助相关工作。

**Article 3** Natural disaster relief work shall implement the administrative leader responsibility system of the people's governments at all levels.  
The National Disaster Reduction Committee shall be responsible for organizing and leading the national natural disaster relief work and coordinating major natural disaster relief activities. The emergency management department of the State Council shall be responsible for the national natural disaster relief work and undertake the specific work of the National Disaster Reduction Committee. The relevant departments of the State Council shall properly perform the work related to national natural disaster relief in accordance with their respective functions and duties.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level or their comprehensive coordination bodies for natural disaster relief emergency response shall organize and coordinate the natural disaster relief work within their respective administrative regions. The emergency management departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the natural disaster relief work within their respective administrative regions. The relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall properly perform the work related to natural disaster relief within their respective administrative regions in accordance with their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府应当将自然灾害救助工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，建立健全与自然灾害救助需求相适应的资金、物资保障机制，将人民政府安排的自然灾害救助资金和自然灾害救助工作经费纳入财政预算。

**Article 4** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate natural disaster relief work into the national economic and social development plans, establish and improve a fund and material guarantee mechanism commensurate with the needs of natural disaster relief, and include the natural disaster relief funds and the working funds for natural disaster relief arranged by the people's governments into the fiscal budgets.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　村民委员会、居民委员会以及红十字会、慈善会和公募基金会等社会组织，依法协助人民政府开展自然灾害救助工作。  
国家鼓励和引导单位和个人参与自然灾害救助捐赠、志愿服务等活动。

**Article 5** Villagers' committees, residents' committees, and social organizations such as the Red Cross Society, charitable organizations, and public fundraising foundations shall assist the people's governments in carrying out natural disaster relief work according to law.  
The State encourages and guides entities and individuals to participate in activities such as donations and volunteer services for natural disaster relief.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各级人民政府应当加强防灾减灾宣传教育，提高公民的防灾避险意识和自救互救能力。  
村民委员会、居民委员会、企业事业单位应当根据所在地人民政府的要求，结合各自的实际情况，开展防灾减灾应急知识的宣传普及活动。

**Article 6** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen publicity and education on disaster prevention and mitigation, and enhance citizens' awareness of disaster prevention and risk avoidance, as well as their ability of self-rescue and mutual rescue.  
Villagers' committees, residents' committees, enterprises, and public institutions shall, in accordance with the requirements of the local people's government and in light of their respective actual conditions, carry out activities to publicize and popularize emergency knowledge on disaster prevention and mitigation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对在自然灾害救助中作出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 7** Entities and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in natural disaster relief shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　救助准备

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当根据有关法律、法规、规章，上级人民政府及其有关部门的应急预案以及本行政区域的自然灾害风险调查情况，制定相应的自然灾害救助应急预案。  
自然灾害救助应急预案应当包括下列内容：  
(一)自然灾害救助应急组织指挥体系及其职责；  
(二)自然灾害救助应急队伍；  
(三)自然灾害救助应急资金、物资、设备；  
(四)自然灾害的预警预报和灾情信息的报告、处理；  
(五)自然灾害救助应急响应的等级和相应措施；  
(六)灾后应急救助和居民住房恢复重建措施。

**Article 8** Local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, based on the relevant laws, regulations, and rules, the emergency plans of the higher-level people's governments and their relevant departments, and the survey of natural disaster risks in their respective administrative regions, formulate corresponding natural disaster relief emergency plans.  
The natural disaster relief emergency plan shall include the following contents:  
(1) The organizational command system for natural disaster relief emergency response and its functions;  
(2) The natural disaster relief emergency response teams;  
(3) The funds, materials, and equipment for natural disaster relief emergency response;  
(4) The early warning and forecast of natural disasters and the reporting and handling of disaster information;  
(5) The levels of natural disaster relief emergency response and corresponding measures;  
(6) Post-disaster emergency relief and measures for the restoration and reconstruction of residential housing.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上人民政府应当建立健全自然灾害救助应急指挥技术支撑系统，并为自然灾害救助工作提供必要的交通、通信等装备。

**Article 9** People's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve the technical support system for the command of natural disaster relief emergency response, and provide necessary equipment such as transportation and communication for natural disaster relief work.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家建立自然灾害救助物资储备制度，由国务院应急管理部门分别会同国务院财政部门、发展改革部门、工业和信息化部门、粮食和物资储备部门制定全国自然灾害救助物资储备规划和储备库规划，并组织实施。其中，由国务院粮食和物资储备部门会同相关部门制定中央救灾物资储备库规划，并组织实施。  
设区的市级以上人民政府和自然灾害多发、易发地区的县级人民政府应当根据自然灾害特点、居民人口数量和分布等情况，按照布局合理、规模适度的原则，设立自然灾害救助物资储备库。

**Article 10** The State shall establish a system for the storage of natural disaster relief materials. The emergency management department of the State Council, in conjunction with the finance department, the development and reform department, the industry and information technology department, and the grain and material reserves department of the State Council, shall formulate the national plan for the storage of natural disaster relief materials and the plan for storage depots, and organize their implementation. Among these, the grain and material reserves department of the State Council, in conjunction with the relevant departments, shall formulate the plan for the central disaster relief material storage depots and organize its implementation.  
People's governments in cities divided into districts or above and the people's governments of counties prone to frequent or easy occurrence of natural disasters shall, based on the characteristics of natural disasters, the population and distribution of residents, and in accordance with the principles of reasonable layout and appropriate scale, establish natural disaster relief material storage depots.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据当地居民人口数量和分布等情况，利用公园、广场、体育场馆等公共设施，统筹规划设立应急避难场所，并设置明显标志。  
启动自然灾害预警响应或者应急响应，需要告知居民前往应急避难场所的，县级以上地方人民政府或者人民政府的自然灾害救助应急综合协调机构应当通过广播、电视、手机短信、电子显示屏、互联网等方式，及时公告应急避难场所的具体地址和到达路径。

**Article 11** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the population and distribution of local residents, utilize public facilities such as parks, squares, and stadiums to make comprehensive plans to establish emergency shelters and set up clear signs.  
When a natural disaster early warning response or emergency response is activated and residents need to be informed to go to emergency shelters, the local people's government at or above the county level or the comprehensive coordination body for natural disaster relief emergency response of the people's government shall promptly announce the specific addresses and access routes of the emergency shelters through broadcasting, television, mobile phone text messages, electronic display screens, the Internet, and other means.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强自然灾害救助人员的队伍建设和业务培训，村民委员会、居民委员会和企业事业单位应当设立专职或者兼职的自然灾害信息员。

**Article 12** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the team building and professional training of natural disaster relief personnel. Villagers' committees, residents' committees, enterprises, and public institutions shall establish full-time or part-time natural disaster information officers.

### 第三章　应急救助

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上人民政府或者人民政府的自然灾害救助应急综合协调机构应当根据自然灾害预警预报启动预警响应，采取下列一项或者多项措施：  
(一)向社会发布规避自然灾害风险的警告，宣传避险常识和技能，提示公众做好自救互救准备；  
(二)开放应急避难场所，疏散、转移易受自然灾害危害的人员和财产，情况紧急时，实行有组织的避险转移；  
(三)加强对易受自然灾害危害的乡村、社区以及公共场所的安全保障；  
(四)责成应急管理等部门做好基本生活救助的准备。

**Article 13** The people's government at or above the county level or the comprehensive coordination body for natural disaster relief emergency response of the people's government shall, based on the natural disaster early warning and forecast, activate an early warning response and take one or more of the following measures:  
(1) Issue warnings to the public about avoiding natural disaster risks, publicize knowledge and skills of risk avoidance, and remind the public to prepare for self-rescue and mutual rescue;  
(2) Open emergency shelters, evacuate and relocate persons and property that are susceptible to natural disasters, and, in case of emergency, carry out organized risk-avoidance relocation;  
(3) Strengthen the security protection of villages, communities, and public places that are susceptible to natural disasters;  
(4) Instruct the emergency management department and other relevant departments to make preparations for basic living assistance.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　自然灾害发生并达到自然灾害救助应急预案启动条件的，县级以上人民政府或者人民政府的自然灾害救助应急综合协调机构应当及时启动自然灾害救助应急响应，采取下列一项或者多项措施：  
(一)立即向社会发布政府应对措施和公众防范措施；  
(二)紧急转移安置受灾人员；  
(三)紧急调拨、运输自然灾害救助应急资金和物资，及时向受灾人员提供食品、饮用水、衣被、取暖、临时住所、医疗防疫等应急救助，保障受灾人员基本生活；  
(四)抚慰受灾人员，处理遇难人员善后事宜；  
(五)组织受灾人员开展自救互救；  
(六)分析评估灾情趋势和灾区需求，采取相应的自然灾害救助措施；  
(七)组织自然灾害救助捐赠活动。  
对应急救助物资，各交通运输主管部门应当组织优先运输。

**Article 14** When a natural disaster occurs and the conditions for activating the natural disaster relief emergency plan are met, the people's government at or above the county level or the comprehensive coordination body for natural disaster relief emergency response of the people's government shall promptly activate the natural disaster relief emergency response and take one or more of the following measures:  
(1) Immediately release to the public the government's response measures and public prevention measures;  
(2) Carry out emergency relocation and resettlement of the affected persons;  
(3) Urgently allocate and transport emergency funds and materials for natural disaster relief, and promptly provide the affected persons with emergency assistance such as food, drinking water, clothing and bedding, heating, temporary shelter, medical treatment and epidemic prevention, so as to ensure the basic living of the affected persons;  
(4) Console the affected persons and handle the aftermath of the deceased;  
(5) Organize the affected persons to carry out self-rescue and mutual rescue;  
(6) Analyze and assess the disaster situation trend and the needs of the disaster area, and take corresponding natural disaster relief measures;  
(7) Organize donation activities for natural disaster relief.  
With respect to emergency relief materials, the competent departments of transportation shall organize priority transportation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　在自然灾害救助应急期间，县级以上地方人民政府或者人民政府的自然灾害救助应急综合协调机构可以在本行政区域内紧急征用物资、设备、交通运输工具和场地，自然灾害救助应急工作结束后应当及时归还，并按照国家有关规定给予补偿。

**Article 15** During the period of natural disaster relief emergency response, the local people's government at or above the county level or the comprehensive coordination body for natural disaster relief emergency response of the people's government may urgently requisition materials, equipment, means of transportation, and sites within its administrative region. After the natural disaster relief emergency work concludes, the requisitioned items shall be promptly returned and compensation shall be given in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　自然灾害造成人员伤亡或者较大财产损失的，受灾地区县级人民政府应急管理部门应当立即向本级人民政府和上一级人民政府应急管理部门报告。  
自然灾害造成特别重大或者重大人员伤亡、财产损失的，受灾地区县级人民政府应急管理部门应当按照有关法律、行政法规和国务院应急预案规定的程序及时报告，必要时可以直接报告国务院。

**Article 16** Where a natural disaster causes casualties or relatively heavy property losses, the emergency management department of the people's government at the county level in the affected area shall immediately report to the people's government at the same level and the emergency management department of the people's government at the next higher level.  
Where a natural disaster causes especially heavy or heavy casualties or property losses, the emergency management department of the people's government at the county level in the affected area shall promptly report in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the relevant laws, administrative regulations, and the emergency plan of the State Council, and may, when necessary, directly report to the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　灾情稳定前，受灾地区人民政府应急管理部门应当每日逐级上报自然灾害造成的人员伤亡、财产损失和自然灾害救助工作动态等情况，并及时向社会发布。  
灾情稳定后，受灾地区县级以上人民政府或者人民政府的自然灾害救助应急综合协调机构应当评估、核定并发布自然灾害损失情况。

**Article 17** Before the disaster situation stabilizes, the emergency management department of the people's government in the affected area shall, on a daily basis, report step by step the casualties, property losses, and the dynamics of natural disaster relief work caused by the natural disaster, and promptly release such information to the public.  
After the disaster situation stabilizes, the local people's government at or above the county level in the affected area or the comprehensive coordination body for natural disaster relief emergency response of the people's government shall assess, verify, and release the situation of natural disaster losses.

### 第四章　灾后救助

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　受灾地区人民政府应当在确保安全的前提下，采取就地安置与异地安置、政府安置与自行安置相结合的方式，对受灾人员进行过渡性安置。  
就地安置应当选择在交通便利、便于恢复生产和生活的地点，并避开可能发生次生自然灾害的区域，尽量不占用或者少占用耕地。  
受灾地区人民政府应当鼓励并组织受灾群众自救互救，恢复重建。

**Article 18** The people's government in the affected area shall, on the premise of ensuring safety, adopt a combination of on-site resettlement and off-site resettlement, government resettlement and self-resettlement, to provide transitional resettlement for the affected persons.  
On-site resettlement shall be selected in locations with convenient transportation, conducive to the resumption of production and living, and shall avoid areas where secondary natural disasters may occur, and shall try not to occupy or occupy as little cultivated land as possible.  
The people's government in the affected area shall encourage and organize the affected masses to carry out self-rescue and mutual rescue and to restore and rebuild.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　自然灾害危险消除后，受灾地区人民政府应当统筹研究制订居民住房恢复重建规划和优惠政策，组织重建或者修缮因灾损毁的居民住房，对恢复重建确有困难的家庭予以重点帮扶。  
居民住房恢复重建应当因地制宜、经济实用，确保房屋建设质量符合防灾减灾要求。  
受灾地区人民政府应急管理等部门应当向经审核确认的居民住房恢复重建补助对象发放补助资金和物资，住房城乡建设等部门应当为受灾人员重建或者修缮因灾损毁的居民住房提供必要的技术支持。

**Article 19** After the danger of natural disasters is eliminated, the people's government in the affected area shall make overall plans to study and formulate plans for the restoration and reconstruction of residential housing and preferential policies, organize the reconstruction or repair of residential housing damaged by the disaster, and provide key assistance to families that indeed have difficulties in restoration and reconstruction.  
The restoration and reconstruction of residential housing shall be adapted to local conditions, be economical and practical, and ensure that the quality of house construction meets the requirements for disaster prevention and mitigation.  
The emergency management department and other relevant departments of the people's government in the affected area shall issue subsidy funds and materials to the recipients of subsidies for the restoration and reconstruction of residential housing that have been verified and confirmed, and the housing and urban-rural construction department and other relevant departments shall provide necessary technical support to the affected persons for the reconstruction or repair of residential housing damaged by the disaster.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　居民住房恢复重建补助对象由受灾人员本人申请或者由村民小组、居民小组提名。经村民委员会、居民委员会民主评议，符合救助条件的，在自然村、社区范围内公示；无异议或者经村民委员会、居民委员会民主评议异议不成立的，由村民委员会、居民委员会将评议意见和有关材料提交乡镇人民政府、街道办事处审核，报县级人民政府应急管理等部门审批。

**Article 20** The recipients of subsidies for the restoration and reconstruction of residential housing shall be applied for by the affected persons themselves or nominated by villagers' groups or residents' groups. After democratic appraisal by the villagers' committee or residents' committee, those who meet the conditions for assistance shall be publicized within the natural village or community; if there is no objection or if the objection is not sustained after democratic appraisal by the villagers' committee or residents' committee, the villagers' committee or residents' committee shall submit the appraisal opinions and relevant materials to the township people's government or sub-district office for review, and report them to the emergency management department and other relevant departments of the people's government at the county level for approval.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　自然灾害发生后的当年冬季、次年春季，受灾地区人民政府应当为生活困难的受灾人员提供基本生活救助。  
受灾地区县级人民政府应急管理部门应当在每年10月底前统计、评估本行政区域受灾人员当年冬季、次年春季的基本生活困难和需求，核实救助对象，编制工作台账，制定救助工作方案，经本级人民政府批准后组织实施，并报上一级人民政府应急管理部门备案。

**Article 21** In the winter of the year following a natural disaster and the spring of the next year, the people's government in the affected area shall provide basic living assistance to the affected persons who have difficulties in living.  
The emergency management department of the people's government at the county level in the affected area shall, before the end of October each year, collect statistics and assess the basic living difficulties and needs of the affected persons in its administrative region for the winter of that year and the spring of the next year, verify the recipients of assistance, prepare work ledgers, formulate assistance work plans, organize the implementation after approval by the people's government at the same level, and report them to the emergency management department of the people's government at the next higher level for recordation.

### 第五章　救助款物管理

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级以上人民政府财政部门、应急管理部门负责自然灾害救助资金的分配、管理并监督使用情况。  
县级以上人民政府应急管理部门负责调拨、分配、管理自然灾害救助物资。

**Article 22** The finance department and the emergency management department of the people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the allocation, management, and supervision of the use of natural disaster relief funds.  
The emergency management department of the people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the allocation, distribution, and management of natural disaster relief materials.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　人民政府采购用于自然灾害救助准备和灾后恢复重建的货物、工程和服务，依照有关政府采购和招标投标的法律规定组织实施。自然灾害应急救助和灾后恢复重建中涉及紧急抢救、紧急转移安置和临时性救助的紧急采购活动，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 23** The procurement by the people's government of goods, projects, and services for the preparation of natural disaster relief and post-disaster restoration and reconstruction shall be organized and implemented in accordance with the relevant legal provisions on government procurement and tendering and bidding. The emergency procurement activities involving urgent rescue, emergency relocation and resettlement, and temporary assistance in natural disaster emergency relief and post-disaster restoration and reconstruction shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　自然灾害救助款物专款(物)专用，无偿使用。  
定向捐赠的款物，应当按照捐赠人的意愿使用。政府部门接受的捐赠人无指定意向的款物，由县级以上人民政府应急管理部门统筹安排用于自然灾害救助；社会组织接受的捐赠人无指定意向的款物，由社会组织按照有关规定用于自然灾害救助。

**Article 24** Natural disaster relief funds and materials shall be used exclusively for their designated purposes and shall be used free of charge.  
Funds and materials donated with specific directions shall be used in accordance with the donor's wishes. Where government departments accept funds and materials from donors without specified intentions, the emergency management department of the people's government at or above the county level shall make overall arrangements for their use in natural disaster relief; where social organizations accept funds and materials from donors without specified intentions, the social organizations shall use them for natural disaster relief in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　自然灾害救助款物应当用于受灾人员的紧急转移安置，基本生活救助，医疗救助，教育、医疗等公共服务设施和住房的恢复重建，自然灾害救助物资的采购、储存和运输，以及因灾遇难人员亲属的抚慰等项支出。

**Article 25** Natural disaster relief funds and materials shall be used for the emergency relocation and resettlement of affected persons, basic living assistance, medical assistance, the restoration and reconstruction of public service facilities such as education and medical care and housing, the procurement, storage, and transportation of natural disaster relief materials, and the consolation of the relatives of persons deceased due to the disaster, among other expenditures.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　受灾地区人民政府应急管理、财政等部门和有关社会组织应当通过报刊、广播、电视、互联网，主动向社会公开所接受的自然灾害救助款物和捐赠款物的来源、数量及其使用情况。  
受灾地区村民委员会、居民委员会应当公布救助对象及其接受救助款物数额和使用情况。

**Article 26** The emergency management department, the finance department, and other relevant departments of the people's government in the affected area, as well as the relevant social organizations, shall proactively disclose to the public through newspapers, broadcasting, television, and the Internet the sources, quantities, and usage of the natural disaster relief funds and materials and donation funds and materials they have received.  
The villagers' committees and residents' committees in the affected area shall publish the recipients of assistance and the amounts of relief funds and materials they have received and their usage.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　各级人民政府应当建立健全自然灾害救助款物和捐赠款物的监督检查制度，并及时受理投诉和举报。

**Article 27** People's governments at all levels shall establish and improve the supervision and inspection system for natural disaster relief funds and materials and donation funds and materials, and shall promptly accept complaints and reports.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县级以上人民政府监察机关、审计机关应当依法对自然灾害救助款物和捐赠款物的管理使用情况进行监督检查，应急管理、财政等部门和有关社会组织应当予以配合。

**Article 28** The supervisory organs and audit organs of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, carry out supervision and inspection over the management and use of natural disaster relief funds and materials and donation funds and materials, and the emergency management department, the finance department, and other relevant departments and relevant social organizations shall cooperate.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　行政机关工作人员违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由任免机关或者监察机关依照法律法规给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)迟报、谎报、瞒报自然灾害损失情况，造成后果的；  
(二)未及时组织受灾人员转移安置，或者在提供基本生活救助、组织恢复重建过程中工作不力，造成后果的；  
(三)截留、挪用、私分自然灾害救助款物或者捐赠款物的；  
(四)不及时归还征用的财产，或者不按照规定给予补偿的；  
(五)有滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的其他行为的。

**Article 29** Where any functionary of an administrative organ violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the appointing and removing organ or the supervisory organ shall impose a sanction in accordance with laws and regulations; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Delaying reporting, misreporting, or concealing the situation of natural disaster losses, causing consequences;  
(2) Failing to timely organize the relocation and resettlement of affected persons, or being ineffective in the process of providing basic living assistance or organizing restoration and reconstruction, causing consequences;  
(3) Intercepting, misappropriating, or privately dividing natural disaster relief funds and materials or donation funds and materials;  
(4) Failing to promptly return requisitioned property, or failing to give compensation in accordance with the provisions;  
(5) Committing other acts of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　采取虚报、隐瞒、伪造等手段，骗取自然灾害救助款物或者捐赠款物的，由县级以上人民政府应急管理部门责令限期退回违法所得的款物；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 30** Where anyone uses means such as false reporting, concealment, or forgery to defraud natural disaster relief funds and materials or donation funds and materials, the emergency management department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the return of the illegally obtained funds and materials within a specified time limit; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　抢夺或者聚众哄抢自然灾害救助款物或者捐赠款物的，由县级以上人民政府应急管理部门责令停止违法行为；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 31** Where anyone seizes or gathers a crowd to plunder natural disaster relief funds and materials or donation funds and materials, the emergency management department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　以暴力、威胁方法阻碍自然灾害救助工作人员依法执行职务，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 32** Where anyone obstructs a natural disaster relief staff member from lawfully performing their duties by means of violence or threat, if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　发生事故灾难、公共卫生事件、社会安全事件等突发事件，需要由县级以上人民政府应急管理部门开展生活救助的，参照本条例执行。

**Article 33** Where emergencies such as accident disasters, public health incidents, and social security incidents occur and the emergency management department of the people's government at or above the county level needs to carry out living assistance, these Regulations shall be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　法律、行政法规对防灾、抗灾、救灾另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 34** Where there are other provisions in laws or administrative regulations on disaster prevention, resistance, and relief, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本条例自2010年9月1日起施行。

**Article 35** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2010.
