# 排污许可管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2021-01-24  
**生效日期**：2021-03-01

> (2020年12月9日国务院第117次常务会议通过　2021年1月24日中华人民共和国国务院令第736号公布　自2021年3月1日起施行) 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强排污许可管理，规范企业事业单位和其他生产经营者排污行为，控制污染物排放，保护和改善生态环境，根据《中华人民共和国环境保护法》等有关法律，制定本条例。 第二条　依照法律规定实行排污许可管理的企业事业单位和其他生产经营者(以下称排污单位)，应当依照本条例规定申请取得排污许可证；未取得排污许可证的，不得排放污染物。 根据污染物产生量、排放量、对环境的影响程度等因素，对排污单位实行排污许可分类管理： (一)污染物产生量、排放量或者对环境的影响程度较大的排污单位，实行排污许可重点管理； (二)污染物产生量、排放量和对环境的影响程度都较小的排污单位，实行排污许可简化管理。 实行排污许可管理的排污单位范围、实施步骤和管理类别名录，由国务院生态环境主管部门拟订并报国务院批准后公布实施。制定实行排污许可管理的排污单位范围、实施步骤和管理类别名录，应当征求有关部门、行业协会、企业事业单位和社会公众等方面的意见。 第三条　国务院生态环境主管部门负责全国排污许可的统一监督管理。 设区的市级以上地方人民政府生态环境主管部门负责本行政区域排污许可的监督管理。 第四条　国务院生态环境主管部门应当加强全国排污许可证管理信息平台建设和管理，提高排污许可在线办理水平。 排污许可证审查与决定、信息公开等应当通过全国排污许可证管理信息平台办理。 第五条　设区的市级以上人民政府应当将排污许可管理工作所需经费列入本级预算。 第二章　申请与审批 第六条　排污单位应当向其生产经营场所所在地设区的市级以上地方人民政府生态环境主管部门(以下称审批部门)申请取得排污许可证。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强排污许可管理，规范企业事业单位和其他生产经营者排污行为，控制污染物排放，保护和改善生态环境，根据《中华人民共和国环境保护法》等有关法律，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the management of pollutant discharge permits, regulating the pollutant discharge behavior of enterprises, public institutions and other producers and business operators, controlling pollutant discharge, protecting and improving the ecological environment, and in accordance with the Environmental Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　依照法律规定实行排污许可管理的企业事业单位和其他生产经营者(以下称排污单位)，应当依照本条例规定申请取得排污许可证；未取得排污许可证的，不得排放污染物。  
根据污染物产生量、排放量、对环境的影响程度等因素，对排污单位实行排污许可分类管理：  
(一)污染物产生量、排放量或者对环境的影响程度较大的排污单位，实行排污许可重点管理；  
(二)污染物产生量、排放量和对环境的影响程度都较小的排污单位，实行排污许可简化管理。  
实行排污许可管理的排污单位范围、实施步骤和管理类别名录，由国务院生态环境主管部门拟订并报国务院批准后公布实施。制定实行排污许可管理的排污单位范围、实施步骤和管理类别名录，应当征求有关部门、行业协会、企业事业单位和社会公众等方面的意见。

**Article 2** Enterprises, public institutions and other producers and business operators that are subject to pollutant discharge permit management according to law (hereinafter referred to as "pollutant discharging entities") shall apply for and obtain a pollutant discharge permit in accordance with these Regulations; those that have not obtained a pollutant discharge permit shall not discharge pollutants.  
Based on factors such as the amount of pollutants generated, the amount discharged, and the degree of impact on the environment, a classified management of pollutant discharge permits shall be implemented for pollutant discharging entities:  
(1) Pollutant discharging entities that generate or discharge a relatively large amount of pollutants or have a relatively large impact on the environment shall be subject to key management of pollutant discharge permits;  
(2) Pollutant discharging entities that generate or discharge a small amount of pollutants and have a small impact on the environment shall be subject to simplified management of pollutant discharge permits.  
The scope of pollutant discharging entities subject to pollutant discharge permit management, the implementation steps, and the list of management categories shall be formulated by the competent department of ecology and environment under the State Council and shall be published and implemented after being submitted to the State Council for approval. In formulating the scope of pollutant discharging entities subject to pollutant discharge permit management, the implementation steps, and the list of management categories, opinions shall be solicited from relevant departments, industry associations, enterprises, public institutions, and the public.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院生态环境主管部门负责全国排污许可的统一监督管理。  
设区的市级以上地方人民政府生态环境主管部门负责本行政区域排污许可的监督管理。

**Article 3** The competent department of ecology and environment under the State Council shall be responsible for the unified supervision and administration of pollutant discharge permits nationwide.  
The competent departments of ecology and environment of the local people's governments at or above the city level with districts shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of pollutant discharge permits within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院生态环境主管部门应当加强全国排污许可证管理信息平台建设和管理，提高排污许可在线办理水平。  
排污许可证审查与决定、信息公开等应当通过全国排污许可证管理信息平台办理。

**Article 4** The competent department of ecology and environment under the State Council shall strengthen the construction and management of the national pollutant discharge permit management information platform and improve the level of online handling of pollutant discharge permits.  
The review and decision of pollutant discharge permits, information disclosure, and other matters shall be handled through the national pollutant discharge permit management information platform.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　设区的市级以上人民政府应当将排污许可管理工作所需经费列入本级预算。

**Article 5** The people's governments at or above the city level with districts shall include the funds required for the work of pollutant discharge permit management in their respective budgets.

### 第二章　申请与审批

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　排污单位应当向其生产经营场所所在地设区的市级以上地方人民政府生态环境主管部门(以下称审批部门)申请取得排污许可证。  
排污单位有两个以上生产经营场所排放污染物的，应当按照生产经营场所分别申请取得排污许可证。

**Article 6** A pollutant discharging entity shall apply for a pollutant discharge permit to the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government at or above the city level with districts where its production and business premises are located (hereinafter referred to as the "approval department").  
If a pollutant discharging entity discharges pollutants from two or more production and business premises, it shall separately apply for pollutant discharge permits for each production and business premises.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请取得排污许可证，可以通过全国排污许可证管理信息平台提交排污许可证申请表，也可以通过信函等方式提交。  
排污许可证申请表应当包括下列事项：  
(一)排污单位名称、住所、法定代表人或者主要负责人、生产经营场所所在地、统一社会信用代码等信息；  
(二)建设项目环境影响报告书(表)批准文件或者环境影响登记表备案材料；  
(三)按照污染物排放口、主要生产设施或者车间、厂界申请的污染物排放种类、排放浓度和排放量，执行的污染物排放标准和重点污染物排放总量控制指标；  
(四)污染防治设施、污染物排放口位置和数量，污染物排放方式、排放去向、自行监测方案等信息；  
(五)主要生产设施、主要产品及产能、主要原辅材料、产生和排放污染物环节等信息，及其是否涉及商业秘密等不宜公开情形的情况说明。

**Article 7** An application for a pollutant discharge permit may be submitted through the national pollutant discharge permit management information platform by submitting a pollutant discharge permit application form, or by letter or other means.  
The pollutant discharge permit application form shall include the following:  
(1) Information such as the name, domicile, legal representative or principal person in charge, location of production and business premises, and unified social credit code of the pollutant discharging entity;  
(2) The approval document for the environmental impact assessment report (form) of the construction project or the filing materials for the environmental impact registration form;  
(3) The types, concentrations, and amounts of pollutants applied for discharge at each pollutant discharge outlet, major production facility or workshop, or plant boundary, the pollutant discharge standards implemented, and the total emission control targets for key pollutants;  
(4) Information on pollution prevention facilities, the location and number of pollutant discharge outlets, the manner and destination of pollutant discharge, and the self-monitoring plan;  
(5) Information on major production facilities, main products and production capacity, main raw and auxiliary materials, and links where pollutants are generated and discharged, as well as a statement on whether the information involves trade secrets or other circumstances unsuitable for disclosure.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　有下列情形之一的，申请取得排污许可证还应当提交相应材料：  
(一)属于实行排污许可重点管理的，排污单位在提出申请前已通过全国排污许可证管理信息平台公开单位基本信息、拟申请许可事项的说明材料；  
(二)属于城镇和工业污水集中处理设施的，排污单位的纳污范围、管网布置、最终排放去向等说明材料；  
(三)属于排放重点污染物的新建、改建、扩建项目以及实施技术改造项目的，排污单位通过污染物排放量削减替代获得重点污染物排放总量控制指标的说明材料。

**Article 8** Under any of the following circumstances, the applicant for a pollutant discharge permit shall also submit the corresponding materials:  
(1) For a pollutant discharging entity subject to key management of pollutant discharge permits, the materials demonstrating that before filing the application, the entity has disclosed its basic information and the description of the matters for which the permit is sought through the national pollutant discharge permit management information platform;  
(2) For urban and industrial centralized wastewater treatment facilities, the materials demonstrating the scope of waste collection, the layout of the pipeline network, and the final discharge destination of the pollutant discharging entity;  
(3) For new construction, renovation, or expansion projects that discharge key pollutants, as well as projects implementing technological transformation, the materials demonstrating that the pollutant discharging entity has obtained the total emission control targets for key pollutants through pollutant emission reduction offsets.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　审批部门对收到的排污许可证申请，应当根据下列情况分别作出处理：  
(一)依法不需要申请取得排污许可证的，应当即时告知不需要申请取得排污许可证；  
(二)不属于本审批部门职权范围的，应当即时作出不予受理的决定，并告知排污单位向有审批权的生态环境主管部门申请；  
(三)申请材料存在可以当场更正的错误的，应当允许排污单位当场更正；  
(四)申请材料不齐全或者不符合法定形式的，应当当场或者在3日内出具告知单，一次性告知排污单位需要补正的全部材料；逾期不告知的，自收到申请材料之日起即视为受理；  
(五)属于本审批部门职权范围，申请材料齐全、符合法定形式，或者排污单位按照要求补正全部申请材料的，应当受理。  
审批部门应当在全国排污许可证管理信息平台上公开受理或者不予受理排污许可证申请的决定，同时向排污单位出具加盖本审批部门专用印章和注明日期的书面凭证。

**Article 9** The approval department shall, upon receipt of an application for a pollutant discharge permit, handle it separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If an application for a pollutant discharge permit is not required according to law, the applicant shall be immediately informed that an application is not required;  
(2) If the application does not fall within the functions and powers of this approval department, a decision of non-acceptance shall be immediately made, and the pollutant discharging entity shall be informed to apply to the competent department of ecology and environment with the authority to accept the application;  
(3) If the application materials contain errors that can be corrected on the spot, the pollutant discharging entity shall be allowed to correct them on the spot;  
(4) If the application materials are incomplete or do not conform to the statutory form, a notice shall be issued on the spot or within three days, informing the pollutant discharging entity of all materials that need to be supplemented at one time; if no notice is given within the time limit, the application shall be deemed to have been accepted from the date of receipt of the application materials;  
(5) If the application falls within the functions and powers of this approval department, and the application materials are complete and conform to the statutory form, or the pollutant discharging entity has supplemented all the application materials as required, the application shall be accepted.  
The approval department shall publicly disclose its decision on acceptance or non-acceptance of the pollutant discharge permit application on the national pollutant discharge permit management information platform, and shall simultaneously issue to the pollutant discharging entity a written certificate stamped with the special seal of the approval department and indicating the date.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　审批部门应当对排污单位提交的申请材料进行审查，并可以对排污单位的生产经营场所进行现场核查。  
审批部门可以组织技术机构对排污许可证申请材料进行技术评估，并承担相应费用。  
技术机构应当对其提出的技术评估意见负责，不得向排污单位收取任何费用。

**Article 10** The approval department shall review the application materials submitted by the pollutant discharging entity, and may conduct on-site inspections of the production and business premises of the pollutant discharging entity.  
The approval department may organize technical institutions to conduct technical assessments of the pollutant discharge permit application materials, and shall bear the corresponding costs.  
Technical institutions shall be responsible for the technical assessment opinions they provide, and shall not charge any fees to the pollutant discharging entity.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对具备下列条件的排污单位，颁发排污许可证：  
(一)依法取得建设项目环境影响报告书(表)批准文件，或者已经办理环境影响登记表备案手续；  
(二)污染物排放符合污染物排放标准要求，重点污染物排放符合排污许可证申请与核发技术规范、环境影响报告书(表)批准文件、重点污染物排放总量控制要求；其中，排污单位生产经营场所位于未达到国家环境质量标准的重点区域、流域的，还应当符合有关地方人民政府关于改善生态环境质量的特别要求；  
(三)采用污染防治设施可以达到许可排放浓度要求或者符合污染防治可行技术；  
(四)自行监测方案的监测点位、指标、频次等符合国家自行监测规范。

**Article 11** A pollutant discharge permit shall be issued to a pollutant discharge unit that meets the following conditions:  
(1) having lawfully obtained the approval document for the environmental impact report (form) of the construction project, or having completed the filing procedures for the environmental impact registration form;  
(2) the discharge of pollutants meets the requirements of pollutant discharge standards, and the discharge of key pollutants meets the requirements of the technical specifications for application and issuance of pollutant discharge permits, the approval document for the environmental impact report (form), and the total discharge control requirements for key pollutants; where the place of production and business operation of the pollutant discharge unit is located in a key region or river basin that has not yet reached the national environmental quality standards, it shall also meet the special requirements of the relevant local people's government for improving the ecological environment quality;  
(3) using pollution prevention and control facilities that can meet the permitted discharge concentration requirements or that comply with feasible pollution prevention and control technologies;  
(4) the monitoring points, indicators, frequency, etc. of the self-monitoring plan comply with the national self-monitoring specifications.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对实行排污许可简化管理的排污单位，审批部门应当自受理申请之日起20日内作出审批决定；对符合条件的颁发排污许可证，对不符合条件的不予许可并书面说明理由。  
对实行排污许可重点管理的排污单位，审批部门应当自受理申请之日起30日内作出审批决定；需要进行现场核查的，应当自受理申请之日起45日内作出审批决定；对符合条件的颁发排污许可证，对不符合条件的不予许可并书面说明理由。  
审批部门应当通过全国排污许可证管理信息平台生成统一的排污许可证编号。

**Article 12** For a pollutant discharge unit subject to simplified management of pollutant discharge permits, the approving department shall make an approval decision within 20 days from the date of accepting the application; it shall issue a pollutant discharge permit to those that meet the conditions, and shall refuse to grant the permit to those that do not meet the conditions and give reasons in writing.  
For a pollutant discharge unit subject to key management of pollutant discharge permits, the approving department shall make an approval decision within 30 days from the date of accepting the application; if on-site verification is required, it shall make an approval decision within 45 days from the date of accepting the application; it shall issue a pollutant discharge permit to those that meet the conditions, and shall refuse to grant the permit to those that do not meet the conditions and give reasons in writing.  
The approving department shall generate a unified pollutant discharge permit number through the National Pollutant Discharge Permit Management Information Platform.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　排污许可证应当记载下列信息：  
(一)排污单位名称、住所、法定代表人或者主要负责人、生产经营场所所在地等；  
(二)排污许可证有效期限、发证机关、发证日期、证书编号和二维码等；  
(三)产生和排放污染物环节、污染防治设施等；  
(四)污染物排放口位置和数量、污染物排放方式和排放去向等；  
(五)污染物排放种类、许可排放浓度、许可排放量等；  
(六)污染防治设施运行和维护要求、污染物排放口规范化建设要求等；  
(七)特殊时段禁止或者限制污染物排放的要求；  
(八)自行监测、环境管理台账记录、排污许可证执行报告的内容和频次等要求；  
(九)排污单位环境信息公开要求；  
(十)存在大气污染物无组织排放情形时的无组织排放控制要求；  
(十一)法律法规规定排污单位应当遵守的其他控制污染物排放的要求。

**Article 13** A pollutant discharge permit shall record the following information:  
(1) the name, domicile, legal representative or principal responsible person of the pollutant discharge unit, and the location of its production and business operation premises;  
(2) the validity period of the pollutant discharge permit, the issuing authority, the date of issuance, the certificate number, and the QR code;  
(3) the links where pollutants are generated and discharged, and the pollution prevention and control facilities;  
(4) the location and number of pollutant discharge outlets, and the methods and directions of pollutant discharge;  
(5) the types of pollutants discharged, the permitted discharge concentration, and the permitted discharge amount;  
(6) the operation and maintenance requirements for pollution prevention and control facilities, and the requirements for standardized construction of pollutant discharge outlets;  
(7) the requirements for prohibiting or restricting pollutant discharge during special periods;  
(8) the requirements for the contents and frequency of self-monitoring, environmental management ledger records, and the pollutant discharge permit implementation report;  
(9) the requirements for the disclosure of environmental information by the pollutant discharge unit;  
(10) the fugitive emission control requirements when there are fugitive emissions of air pollutants;  
(11) other requirements for controlling pollutant discharge that the pollutant discharge unit shall comply with as provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　排污许可证有效期为5年。  
排污许可证有效期届满，排污单位需要继续排放污染物的，应当于排污许可证有效期届满60日前向审批部门提出申请。审批部门应当自受理申请之日起20日内完成审查；对符合条件的予以延续，对不符合条件的不予延续并书面说明理由。  
排污单位变更名称、住所、法定代表人或者主要负责人的，应当自变更之日起30日内，向审批部门申请办理排污许可证变更手续。

**Article 14** The validity period of a pollutant discharge permit shall be five years.  
Where a pollutant discharge unit needs to continue discharging pollutants upon the expiry of the validity period of the pollutant discharge permit, it shall submit an application to the approving department 60 days before the expiry of the validity period. The approving department shall complete the review within 20 days from the date of accepting the application; it shall renew the permit for those that meet the conditions, and shall refuse to renew the permit for those that do not meet the conditions and give reasons in writing.  
Where a pollutant discharge unit changes its name, domicile, legal representative or principal responsible person, it shall, within 30 days from the date of change, apply to the approving department for going through the formalities for alteration of the pollutant discharge permit.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　在排污许可证有效期内，排污单位有下列情形之一的，应当重新申请取得排污许可证：  
(一)新建、改建、扩建排放污染物的项目；  
(二)生产经营场所、污染物排放口位置或者污染物排放方式、排放去向发生变化；  
(三)污染物排放口数量或者污染物排放种类、排放量、排放浓度增加。

**Article 15** Within the validity period of a pollutant discharge permit, if a pollutant discharge unit falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall re-apply for a pollutant discharge permit:  
(1) a new construction, renovation, or expansion project that discharges pollutants;  
(2) the place of production and business operation, the location of pollutant discharge outlets, or the methods or directions of pollutant discharge have changed;  
(3) the number of pollutant discharge outlets, or the types, discharge amounts, or discharge concentrations of pollutants have increased.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　排污单位适用的污染物排放标准、重点污染物总量控制要求发生变化，需要对排污许可证进行变更的，审批部门可以依法对排污许可证相应事项进行变更。

**Article 16** Where the pollutant discharge standards applicable to a pollutant discharge unit or the total discharge control requirements for key pollutants change, and the pollutant discharge permit needs to be altered, the approving department may, according to law, alter the corresponding items of the pollutant discharge permit.

### 第三章　排污管理

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　排污许可证是对排污单位进行生态环境监管的主要依据。  
排污单位应当遵守排污许可证规定，按照生态环境管理要求运行和维护污染防治设施，建立环境管理制度，严格控制污染物排放。

**Article 17** The pollutant discharge permit shall be the main basis for ecological and environmental supervision of a pollutant discharge unit.  
A pollutant discharge unit shall comply with the provisions of the pollutant discharge permit, operate and maintain pollution prevention and control facilities in accordance with ecological and environmental management requirements, establish an environmental management system, and strictly control pollutant discharge.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　排污单位应当按照生态环境主管部门的规定建设规范化污染物排放口，并设置标志牌。  
污染物排放口位置和数量、污染物排放方式和排放去向应当与排污许可证规定相符。  
实施新建、改建、扩建项目和技术改造的排污单位，应当在建设污染防治设施的同时，建设规范化污染物排放口。

**Article 18** A pollutant discharge unit shall construct standardized pollutant discharge outlets and set up signs in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of ecological environment.  
The location and number of pollutant discharge outlets, and the methods and directions of pollutant discharge shall be consistent with the provisions of the pollutant discharge permit.  
A pollutant discharge unit that carries out new construction, renovation or expansion projects and technological transformation shall construct standardized pollutant discharge outlets at the same time as constructing pollution prevention and control facilities.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　排污单位应当按照排污许可证规定和有关标准规范，依法开展自行监测，并保存原始监测记录。原始监测记录保存期限不得少于5年。  
排污单位应当对自行监测数据的真实性、准确性负责，不得篡改、伪造。

**Article 19** A pollutant discharge unit shall, in accordance with the provisions of the pollutant discharge permit and relevant standards and specifications, lawfully carry out self-monitoring and preserve original monitoring records. The retention period of the original monitoring records shall not be less than five years.  
A pollutant discharge unit shall be responsible for the authenticity and accuracy of the self-monitoring data, and shall not tamper with or falsify them.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　实行排污许可重点管理的排污单位，应当依法安装、使用、维护污染物排放自动监测设备，并与生态环境主管部门的监控设备联网。  
排污单位发现污染物排放自动监测设备传输数据异常的，应当及时报告生态环境主管部门，并进行检查、修复。

**Article 20** A pollutant discharge unit subject to key management of pollutant discharge permits shall lawfully install, use, and maintain automatic monitoring equipment for pollutant discharge, and connect it to the monitoring equipment of the competent department of ecological environment.  
Where a pollutant discharge unit discovers anomalies in the data transmitted by the automatic monitoring equipment for pollutant discharge, it shall promptly report to the competent department of ecological environment, and carry out inspection and repair.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　排污单位应当建立环境管理台账记录制度，按照排污许可证规定的格式、内容和频次，如实记录主要生产设施、污染防治设施运行情况以及污染物排放浓度、排放量。环境管理台账记录保存期限不得少于5年。  
排污单位发现污染物排放超过污染物排放标准等异常情况时，应当立即采取措施消除、减轻危害后果，如实进行环境管理台账记录，并报告生态环境主管部门，说明原因。超过污染物排放标准等异常情况下的污染物排放计入排污单位的污染物排放量。

**Article 21** The pollutant discharging unit shall establish an environmental management ledger record system, and truthfully record the operation of main production facilities and pollution prevention facilities, as well as the pollutant discharge concentration and discharge volume, in accordance with the format, content and frequency specified in the pollutant discharge permit. The retention period of the environmental management ledger records shall not be less than 5 years.  
When a pollutant discharging unit discovers abnormal situations such as pollutant discharge exceeding the pollutant discharge standards, it shall immediately take measures to eliminate or mitigate the harmful consequences, truthfully make records in the environmental management ledger, and report to the competent department of ecological environment, explaining the reasons. The pollutant discharge under abnormal situations such as exceeding the pollutant discharge standards shall be included in the pollutant discharge volume of the pollutant discharging unit.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　排污单位应当按照排污许可证规定的内容、频次和时间要求，向审批部门提交排污许可证执行报告，如实报告污染物排放行为、排放浓度、排放量等。  
排污许可证有效期内发生停产的，排污单位应当在排污许可证执行报告中如实报告污染物排放变化情况并说明原因。  
排污许可证执行报告中报告的污染物排放量可以作为年度生态环境统计、重点污染物排放总量考核、污染源排放清单编制的依据。

**Article 22** A pollutant discharging unit shall submit an implementation report of the pollutant discharge permit to the approving department in accordance with the content, frequency and time requirements specified in the pollutant discharge permit, and truthfully report the pollutant discharge behaviors, discharge concentrations, discharge volumes, etc.  
If the production is suspended during the validity period of the pollutant discharge permit, the pollutant discharging unit shall truthfully report the changes in pollutant discharge in the implementation report of the pollutant discharge permit and explain the reasons.  
The pollutant discharge volume reported in the implementation report of the pollutant discharge permit may serve as the basis for annual ecological environment statistics, assessment of the total discharge of key pollutants, and compilation of the pollution source discharge inventory.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　排污单位应当按照排污许可证规定，如实在全国排污许可证管理信息平台上公开污染物排放信息。  
污染物排放信息应当包括污染物排放种类、排放浓度和排放量，以及污染防治设施的建设运行情况、排污许可证执行报告、自行监测数据等；其中，水污染物排入市政排水管网的，还应当包括污水接入市政排水管网位置、排放方式等信息。

**Article 23** A pollutant discharging unit shall truthfully disclose pollutant discharge information on the national pollutant discharge permit management information platform in accordance with the provisions of the pollutant discharge permit.  
The pollutant discharge information shall include the types, discharge concentrations and discharge volumes of pollutants, as well as the construction and operation of pollution prevention facilities, the implementation report of the pollutant discharge permit, self-monitoring data, etc.; among which, if water pollutants are discharged into the municipal drainage pipe network, the information shall also include the location where the sewage is connected to the municipal drainage pipe network, the discharge method, and other information.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　污染物产生量、排放量和对环境的影响程度都很小的企业事业单位和其他生产经营者，应当填报排污登记表，不需要申请取得排污许可证。  
需要填报排污登记表的企业事业单位和其他生产经营者范围名录，由国务院生态环境主管部门制定并公布。制定需要填报排污登记表的企业事业单位和其他生产经营者范围名录，应当征求有关部门、行业协会、企业事业单位和社会公众等方面的意见。  
需要填报排污登记表的企业事业单位和其他生产经营者，应当在全国排污许可证管理信息平台上填报基本信息、污染物排放去向、执行的污染物排放标准以及采取的污染防治措施等信息；填报的信息发生变动的，应当自发生变动之日起20日内进行变更填报。

**Article 24** Enterprises, institutions and other producers and operators whose pollutant generation volume, discharge volume and impact on the environment are all very small shall fill in a pollutant discharge registration form, and are not required to apply for a pollutant discharge permit.  
The list of the scope of enterprises, institutions and other producers and operators that need to fill in a pollutant discharge registration form shall be formulated and published by the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council. When formulating the list, opinions from relevant departments, industry associations, enterprises, institutions and the public shall be solicited.  
Enterprises, institutions and other producers and operators that need to fill in a pollutant discharge registration form shall fill in basic information, the destination of pollutant discharge, the implemented pollutant discharge standards, and the pollution prevention measures taken on the national pollutant discharge permit management information platform; if the information filled in changes, the registration shall be updated within 20 days from the date of change.

### 第四章　监督检查

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　生态环境主管部门应当加强对排污许可的事中事后监管，将排污许可执法检查纳入生态环境执法年度计划，根据排污许可管理类别、排污单位信用记录和生态环境管理需要等因素，合理确定检查频次和检查方式。  
生态环境主管部门应当在全国排污许可证管理信息平台上记录执法检查时间、内容、结果以及处罚决定，同时将处罚决定纳入国家有关信用信息系统向社会公布。

**Article 25** The competent department of ecological environment shall strengthen the supervision during and after the issuance of the pollutant discharge permit, incorporate the law enforcement inspection of pollutant discharge permits into the annual plan of ecological environment law enforcement, and rationally determine the inspection frequency and method based on factors such as the management category of the pollutant discharge permit, the credit record of the pollutant discharging unit, and the needs of ecological environment management.  
The competent department of ecological environment shall record the time, content, results and punishment decisions of law enforcement inspections on the national pollutant discharge permit management information platform, and simultaneously incorporate the punishment decisions into the relevant national credit information system for disclosure to the public.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　排污单位应当配合生态环境主管部门监督检查，如实反映情况，并按照要求提供排污许可证、环境管理台账记录、排污许可证执行报告、自行监测数据等相关材料。  
禁止伪造、变造、转让排污许可证。

**Article 26** A pollutant discharging unit shall cooperate with the supervision and inspection of the competent department of ecological environment, truthfully reflect the situation, and provide the pollutant discharge permit, environmental management ledger records, implementation report of the pollutant discharge permit, self-monitoring data and other relevant materials as required.  
It is prohibited to forge, alter or transfer the pollutant discharge permit.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　生态环境主管部门可以通过全国排污许可证管理信息平台监控排污单位的污染物排放情况，发现排污单位的污染物排放浓度超过许可排放浓度的，应当要求排污单位提供排污许可证、环境管理台账记录、排污许可证执行报告、自行监测数据等相关材料进行核查，必要时可以组织开展现场监测。

**Article 27** The competent department of ecological environment may monitor the pollutant discharge situation of the pollutant discharging unit through the national pollutant discharge permit management information platform. If it finds that the pollutant discharge concentration of the pollutant discharging unit exceeds the permitted discharge concentration, it shall require the pollutant discharging unit to provide the pollutant discharge permit, environmental management ledger records, implementation report of the pollutant discharge permit, self-monitoring data and other relevant materials for verification, and may organize on-site monitoring when necessary.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　生态环境主管部门根据行政执法过程中收集的监测数据，以及排污单位的排污许可证、环境管理台账记录、排污许可证执行报告、自行监测数据等相关材料，对排污单位在规定周期内的污染物排放量，以及排污单位污染防治设施运行和维护是否符合排污许可证规定进行核查。

**Article 28** The competent department of ecological environment shall, based on the monitoring data collected in the process of administrative law enforcement, as well as the pollutant discharge permit, environmental management ledger records, implementation report of the pollutant discharge permit, self-monitoring data and other relevant materials of the pollutant discharging unit, verify the amount of pollutant discharge by the pollutant discharging unit within the specified period and whether the operation and maintenance of the pollution prevention facilities of the pollutant discharging unit comply with the provisions of the pollutant discharge permit.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　生态环境主管部门依法通过现场监测、排污单位污染物排放自动监测设备、全国排污许可证管理信息平台获得的排污单位污染物排放数据，可以作为判定污染物排放浓度是否超过许可排放浓度的证据。  
排污单位自行监测数据与生态环境主管部门及其所属监测机构在行政执法过程中收集的监测数据不一致的，以生态环境主管部门及其所属监测机构收集的监测数据作为行政执法依据。

**Article 29** The pollutant discharge data obtained by the competent department of ecological environment according to law through on-site monitoring, automatic monitoring equipment of the pollutant discharging unit, and the national pollutant discharge permit management information platform may serve as evidence for determining whether the pollutant discharge concentration exceeds the permitted discharge concentration.  
If the self-monitoring data of the pollutant discharging unit is inconsistent with the monitoring data collected by the competent department of ecological environment and its monitoring institutions in the process of administrative law enforcement, the monitoring data collected by the competent department of ecological environment and its monitoring institutions shall be used as the basis for administrative law enforcement.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家鼓励排污单位采用污染防治可行技术。国务院生态环境主管部门制定并公布污染防治可行技术指南。  
排污单位未采用污染防治可行技术的，生态环境主管部门应当根据排污许可证、环境管理台账记录、排污许可证执行报告、自行监测数据等相关材料，以及生态环境主管部门及其所属监测机构在行政执法过程中收集的监测数据，综合判断排污单位采用的污染防治技术能否稳定达到排污许可证规定；对不能稳定达到排污许可证规定的，应当提出整改要求，并可以增加检查频次。  
制定污染防治可行技术指南，应当征求有关部门、行业协会、企业事业单位和社会公众等方面的意见。

**Article 30** The State encourages pollutant discharging units to adopt feasible technologies for pollution prevention and control. The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council formulates and publishes technical guidelines for feasible pollution prevention and control technologies.  
If a pollutant discharging unit fails to adopt feasible pollution prevention and control technologies, the competent department of ecological environment shall, based on the pollutant discharge permit, environmental management ledger records, implementation report of the pollutant discharge permit, self-monitoring data and other relevant materials, as well as the monitoring data collected by the competent department of ecological environment and its monitoring institutions in the process of administrative law enforcement, comprehensively judge whether the pollution prevention and control technology adopted by the pollutant discharging unit can stably meet the requirements of the pollutant discharge permit; if it fails to stably meet the requirements, the competent department shall propose rectification requirements and may increase the frequency of inspections.  
When formulating the technical guidelines for feasible pollution prevention and control technologies, opinions from relevant departments, industry associations, enterprises, institutions and the public shall be solicited.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　任何单位和个人对排污单位违反本条例规定的行为，均有向生态环境主管部门举报的权利。  
接到举报的生态环境主管部门应当依法处理，按照有关规定向举报人反馈处理结果，并为举报人保密。

**Article 31** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report to the competent department of ecological environment any act of a pollutant discharge unit that violates the provisions of these Regulations.  
The competent department of ecological environment that receives the report shall handle it according to law, provide feedback on the handling results to the informant in accordance with the relevant provisions, and keep the informant confidential.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　违反本条例规定，生态环境主管部门在排污许可证审批或者监督管理中有下列行为之一的，由上级机关责令改正；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
(一)对符合法定条件的排污许可证申请不予受理或者不在法定期限内审批；  
(二)向不符合法定条件的排污单位颁发排污许可证；  
(三)违反审批权限审批排污许可证；  
(四)发现违法行为不予查处；  
(五)不依法履行监督管理职责的其他行为。

**Article 32** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the competent department of ecological environment commits any of the following acts in the approval or supervision and management of pollutant discharge permits, the superior organ shall order it to make corrections; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to accept an application for a pollutant discharge permit that meets the statutory conditions or failing to approve it within the statutory time limit;  
(2) issuing a pollutant discharge permit to a pollutant discharge unit that does not meet the statutory conditions;  
(3) approving a pollutant discharge permit in violation of the approval authority;  
(4) failing to investigate and punish illegal acts discovered;  
(5) other acts of failing to perform supervision and management duties according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违反本条例规定，排污单位有下列行为之一的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正或者限制生产、停产整治，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业、关闭：  
(一)未取得排污许可证排放污染物；  
(二)排污许可证有效期届满未申请延续或者延续申请未经批准排放污染物；  
(三)被依法撤销、注销、吊销排污许可证后排放污染物；  
(四)依法应当重新申请取得排污许可证，未重新申请取得排污许可证排放污染物。

**Article 33** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a pollutant discharge unit commits any of the following acts, the competent department of ecological environment shall order it to make corrections or restrict production, suspend production for remediation, and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall, after reporting to and obtaining the approval of the people's government with the approval authority, order the suspension of business or closure:  
(1) discharging pollutants without obtaining a pollutant discharge permit;  
(2) discharging pollutants after the validity period of the pollutant discharge permit expires without applying for extension or without approval of the extension application;  
(3) discharging pollutants after the pollutant discharge permit has been revoked, cancelled, or withdrawn according to law;  
(4) discharging pollutants without reapplying for a pollutant discharge permit that should be reapplied for according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反本条例规定，排污单位有下列行为之一的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正或者限制生产、停产整治，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销排污许可证，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业、关闭：  
(一)超过许可排放浓度、许可排放量排放污染物；  
(二)通过暗管、渗井、渗坑、灌注或者篡改、伪造监测数据，或者不正常运行污染防治设施等逃避监管的方式违法排放污染物。

**Article 34** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a pollutant discharge unit commits any of the following acts, the competent department of ecological environment shall order it to make corrections or restrict production, suspend production for remediation, and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall revoke the pollutant discharge permit, and after reporting to and obtaining the approval of the people's government with the approval authority, order the suspension of business or closure:  
(1) discharging pollutants exceeding the permitted discharge concentration or the permitted discharge amount;  
(2) illegally discharging pollutants through means of evading supervision such as underground pipes, seepage wells, seepage pits,灌注 (injection), or tampering with or falsifying monitoring data, or abnormal operation of pollution prevention facilities.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反本条例规定，排污单位有下列行为之一的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款，责令限制生产、停产整治：  
(一)未按照排污许可证规定控制大气污染物无组织排放；  
(二)特殊时段未按照排污许可证规定停止或者限制排放污染物。

**Article 35** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a pollutant discharge unit commits any of the following acts, the competent department of ecological environment shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan and order restriction of production or suspension of production for remediation:  
(1) failing to control fugitive emissions of atmospheric pollutants in accordance with the provisions of the pollutant discharge permit;  
(2) failing to stop or restrict the discharge of pollutants during special periods in accordance with the provisions of the pollutant discharge permit.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本条例规定，排污单位有下列行为之一的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停产整治：  
(一)污染物排放口位置或者数量不符合排污许可证规定；  
(二)污染物排放方式或者排放去向不符合排污许可证规定；  
(三)损毁或者擅自移动、改变污染物排放自动监测设备；  
(四)未按照排污许可证规定安装、使用污染物排放自动监测设备并与生态环境主管部门的监控设备联网，或者未保证污染物排放自动监测设备正常运行；  
(五)未按照排污许可证规定制定自行监测方案并开展自行监测；  
(六)未按照排污许可证规定保存原始监测记录；  
(七)未按照排污许可证规定公开或者不如实公开污染物排放信息；  
(八)发现污染物排放自动监测设备传输数据异常或者污染物排放超过污染物排放标准等异常情况不报告；  
(九)违反法律法规规定的其他控制污染物排放要求的行为。

**Article 36** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a pollutant discharge unit commits any of the following acts, the competent department of ecological environment shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall order suspension of production for remediation:  
(1) the location or number of pollutant discharge outlets does not comply with the provisions of the pollutant discharge permit;  
(2) the method or direction of pollutant discharge does not comply with the provisions of the pollutant discharge permit;  
(3) damaging or without authorization moving or altering the automatic monitoring equipment for pollutant discharge;  
(4) failing to install or use automatic monitoring equipment for pollutant discharge in accordance with the provisions of the pollutant discharge permit and connect it with the monitoring equipment of the competent department of ecological environment, or failing to ensure the normal operation of the automatic monitoring equipment for pollutant discharge;  
(5) failing to formulate a self-monitoring plan and carry out self-monitoring in accordance with the provisions of the pollutant discharge permit;  
(6) failing to preserve original monitoring records in accordance with the provisions of the pollutant discharge permit;  
(7) failing to disclose or not truthfully disclosing pollutant discharge information in accordance with the provisions of the pollutant discharge permit;  
(8) failing to report abnormal situations such as abnormal data transmitted by automatic monitoring equipment for pollutant discharge or pollutant discharge exceeding the pollutant discharge standard;  
(9) other acts violating other requirements for controlling pollutant discharge as provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　违反本条例规定，排污单位有下列行为之一的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，处每次5千元以上2万元以下的罚款；法律另有规定的，从其规定：  
(一)未建立环境管理台账记录制度，或者未按照排污许可证规定记录；  
(二)未如实记录主要生产设施及污染防治设施运行情况或者污染物排放浓度、排放量；  
(三)未按照排污许可证规定提交排污许可证执行报告；  
(四)未如实报告污染物排放行为或者污染物排放浓度、排放量。

**Article 37** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a pollutant discharge unit commits any of the following acts, the competent department of ecological environment shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan for each such act; if there are other provisions in the law, those provisions shall prevail:  
(1) failing to establish an environmental management ledger recording system, or failing to make records in accordance with the provisions of the pollutant discharge permit;  
(2) failing to truthfully record the operation of main production facilities and pollution prevention facilities or the concentration and amount of pollutant discharge;  
(3) failing to submit a pollutant discharge permit implementation report in accordance with the provisions of the pollutant discharge permit;  
(4) failing to truthfully report the pollutant discharge behavior or the concentration and amount of pollutant discharge.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　排污单位违反本条例规定排放污染物，受到罚款处罚，被责令改正的，生态环境主管部门应当组织复查，发现其继续实施该违法行为或者拒绝、阻挠复查的，依照《中华人民共和国环境保护法》的规定按日连续处罚。

**Article 38** Where a pollutant discharge unit, in violation of these Regulations, discharges pollutants and is subject to a fine penalty and ordered to make corrections, the competent department of ecological environment shall organize a review. If it discovers that the unit continues to commit the illegal act or refuses or obstructs the review, it shall impose a daily recurring penalty in accordance with the provisions of the Environmental Protection Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　排污单位拒不配合生态环境主管部门监督检查，或者在接受监督检查时弄虚作假的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 39** Where a pollutant discharge unit refuses to cooperate with the supervision and inspection of the competent department of ecological environment, or practices fraud when accepting supervision and inspection, the competent department of ecological environment shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　排污单位以欺骗、贿赂等不正当手段申请取得排污许可证的，由审批部门依法撤销其排污许可证，处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款，3年内不得再次申请排污许可证。

**Article 40** Where a pollutant discharge unit obtains a pollutant discharge permit by means of deception, bribery or other illegitimate means, the approving department shall revoke its pollutant discharge permit according to law, impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and the unit shall not apply for a pollutant discharge permit again within three years.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本条例规定，伪造、变造、转让排污许可证的，由生态环境主管部门没收相关证件或者吊销排污许可证，处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款，3年内不得再次申请排污许可证。

**Article 41** Whoever violates these Regulations by forging, altering, or transferring a pollutant discharge permit shall be subject to confiscation of the relevant documents or revocation of the pollutant discharge permit by the competent department of ecological environment, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan, and shall not apply for a pollutant discharge permit again within three years.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本条例规定，接受审批部门委托的排污许可技术机构弄虚作假的，由审批部门解除委托关系，将相关信息记入其信用记录，在全国排污许可证管理信息平台上公布，同时纳入国家有关信用信息系统向社会公布；情节严重的，禁止从事排污许可技术服务。

**Article 42** Where a pollutant discharge permit technical institution entrusted by the approving department practices fraud in violation of these Regulations, the approving department shall terminate the entrustment relationship, record the relevant information in its credit record, publish it on the national pollutant discharge permit management information platform, and simultaneously incorporate it into the relevant national credit information system for public disclosure; if the circumstances are serious, the institution shall be prohibited from engaging in pollutant discharge permit technical services.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　需要填报排污登记表的企业事业单位和其他生产经营者，未依照本条例规定填报排污信息的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，可以处5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 43** Where an enterprise, public institution, or other business operator required to fill in a pollutant discharge registration form fails to fill in pollutant discharge information in accordance with these Regulations, the competent department of ecological environment shall order it to make corrections and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　排污单位有下列行为之一，尚不构成犯罪的，除依照本条例规定予以处罚外，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照《中华人民共和国环境保护法》的规定处以拘留：  
(一)未取得排污许可证排放污染物，被责令停止排污，拒不执行；  
(二)通过暗管、渗井、渗坑、灌注或者篡改、伪造监测数据，或者不正常运行污染防治设施等逃避监管的方式违法排放污染物。

**Article 44** Where a pollutant discharge unit commits any of the following acts, which does not yet constitute a crime, in addition to being penalized in accordance with these Regulations, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be detained in accordance with the provisions of the Environmental Protection Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) discharging pollutants without obtaining a pollutant discharge permit, being ordered to cease such discharge but refusing to comply;  
(2) illegally discharging pollutants through means of evading supervision, such as using underground pipes, seepage wells, seepage pits, injection, or tampering with or falsifying monitoring data, or improperly operating pollution prevention facilities.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 45** Whoever violates these Regulations, where the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, shall be given a public security administrative penalty according to law; where the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本条例施行前已经实际排放污染物的排污单位，不符合本条例规定条件的，应当在国务院生态环境主管部门规定的期限内进行整改，达到本条例规定的条件并申请取得排污许可证；逾期未取得排污许可证的，不得继续排放污染物。整改期限内，生态环境主管部门应当向其下达排污限期整改通知书，明确整改内容、整改期限等要求。

**Article 46** A pollutant discharge unit that has actually discharged pollutants before the coming into force of these Regulations but does not meet the requirements of these Regulations shall, within the time limit prescribed by the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council, make rectifications to meet the requirements specified in these Regulations and apply for a pollutant discharge permit; if it fails to obtain the pollutant discharge permit after the expiration of the time limit, it shall not continue to discharge pollutants. During the rectification period, the competent department of ecological environment shall issue to it a notice of pollutant discharge rectification with a time limit, specifying the rectification contents, rectification time limit, and other requirements.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　排污许可证申请表、环境管理台账记录、排污许可证执行报告等文件的格式和内容要求，以及排污许可证申请与核发技术规范等，由国务院生态环境主管部门制定。

**Article 47** The format and content requirements for documents such as the pollutant discharge permit application form, environmental management ledger records, and pollutant discharge permit implementation report, as well as technical specifications for the application and issuance of pollutant discharge permits, shall be formulated by the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　企业事业单位和其他生产经营者涉及国家秘密的，其排污许可、监督管理等应当遵守保密法律法规的规定。

**Article 48** Where enterprises, public institutions, or other business operators involve state secrets, their pollutant discharge permits, supervision, and management shall comply with the provisions of the laws and regulations on confidentiality.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　飞机、船舶、机动车、列车等移动污染源的污染物排放管理，依照相关法律法规的规定执行。

**Article 49** The management of pollutant discharge from mobile sources such as aircraft, ships, motor vehicles, and trains shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　排污单位应当遵守安全生产规定，按照安全生产管理要求运行和维护污染防治设施，建立安全生产管理制度。  
在运行和维护污染防治设施过程中违反安全生产规定，发生安全生产事故的，对负有责任的排污单位依照《中华人民共和国安全生产法》的有关规定予以处罚。

**Article 50** A pollutant discharge unit shall comply with the safety production regulations, operate and maintain pollution prevention facilities in accordance with the safety production management requirements, and establish a safety production management system.  
Where a safety production accident occurs due to violation of safety production regulations during the operation and maintenance of pollution prevention facilities, the pollutant discharge unit that bears responsibility shall be penalized in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Work Safety Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　本条例自2021年3月1日起施行。

**Article 51** These Regulations shall come into force on March 1, 2021.
