# 中华人民共和国海洋石油勘探开发环境保护管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1983-12-29  
**生效日期**：1983-12-29

> (1983年12月29日国务院发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一条　为实施《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》，防止海洋石油勘探开发对海洋环境的污染损害，特制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于在中华人民共和国管辖海域从事石油勘探开发的企业、事业单位、作业者和个人，以及他们所使用的固定式和移动式平台及其他有关设施。 第三条　海洋石油勘探开发环境保护管理的主管部门是中华人民共和国国家海洋局及其派出机构，以下称“主管部门”。 第四条　企业或作业者在编制油(气)田总体开发方案的同时，必须编制海洋环境影响报告书，报中华人民共和国城乡建设环境保护部。城乡建设环境保护部会同国家海洋局和石油工业部，按照国家基本建设项目环境保护管理的规定组织审批。 第五条　海洋环境影响报告书应包括以下内容： (一)油田名称、地理位置、规模； (二)油田所处海域的自然环境和海洋资源状况； (三)油田开发中需要排放的废弃物种类、成分、数量、处理方式； (四)对海洋环境影响的评价；海洋石油开发对周围海域自然环境、海洋资源可能发生的影响；对海洋渔业、航运、其他海上活动可能产生的影响；为避免、减轻各种有害影响，拟采取的环境保护措施； (五)最终不可避免的影响、影响程度及原因； (六)防范重大油污染事故的措施：防范组织，人员配备，技术装备，通信联络等。 第六条　企业、事业单位、作业者应具备防治油污染事故的应急能力，制定应急计划，配备与其所从事的海洋石油勘探开发规模相适应的油回收设施和围油、消油器材。 配备化学消油剂，应将其牌号、成分报告主管部门核准。 第七条　固定式和移动式平台的防污设备的要求：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为实施《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》，防止海洋石油勘探开发对海洋环境的污染损害，特制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of implementing the Marine Environment Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and preventing pollution damage to the marine environment from offshore oil exploration and development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于在中华人民共和国管辖海域从事石油勘探开发的企业、事业单位、作业者和个人，以及他们所使用的固定式和移动式平台及其他有关设施。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to enterprises, institutions, operators and individuals engaged in oil exploration and development in the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, as well as the fixed and mobile platforms and other related facilities used by them.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　海洋石油勘探开发环境保护管理的主管部门是中华人民共和国国家海洋局及其派出机构，以下称“主管部门”。

**Article 3** The competent authority for environmental protection management of offshore oil exploration and development shall be the State Oceanic Administration of the People's Republic of China and its local offices, hereinafter referred to as the 'competent authority'.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　企业或作业者在编制油(气)田总体开发方案的同时，必须编制海洋环境影响报告书，报中华人民共和国城乡建设环境保护部。城乡建设环境保护部会同国家海洋局和石油工业部，按照国家基本建设项目环境保护管理的规定组织审批。

**Article 4** When preparing the overall development plan for an oil (gas) field, an enterprise or operator must prepare a marine environmental impact report and submit it to the Ministry of Urban and Rural Construction and Environmental Protection of the People's Republic of China. The Ministry of Urban and Rural Construction and Environmental Protection shall, together with the State Oceanic Administration and the Ministry of Petroleum Industry, organize the examination and approval in accordance with the provisions of the State on environmental protection management for capital construction projects.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　海洋环境影响报告书应包括以下内容：  
(一)油田名称、地理位置、规模；  
(二)油田所处海域的自然环境和海洋资源状况；  
(三)油田开发中需要排放的废弃物种类、成分、数量、处理方式；  
(四)对海洋环境影响的评价；海洋石油开发对周围海域自然环境、海洋资源可能发生的影响；对海洋渔业、航运、其他海上活动可能产生的影响；为避免、减轻各种有害影响，拟采取的环境保护措施；  
(五)最终不可避免的影响、影响程度及原因；  
(六)防范重大油污染事故的措施：防范组织，人员配备，技术装备，通信联络等。

**Article 5** A marine environmental impact report shall include the following contents:  
(1) Name, geographical location and scale of the oilfield;  
(2) Natural environment and marine resources status of the sea area where the oilfield is located;  
(3) Types, components, quantities and treatment methods of wastes to be discharged during the development of the oilfield;  
(4) Assessment of the impact on the marine environment; the impacts that offshore oil development may have on the natural environment and marine resources of the surrounding sea areas; the impacts that may be caused to marine fisheries, navigation and other offshore activities; the environmental protection measures to be taken to avoid or mitigate various harmful effects;  
(5) Ultimately unavoidable impacts, their degree and causes;  
(6) Measures to prevent major oil pollution accidents: prevention organization, staffing, technical equipment, communication and liaison, etc.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　企业、事业单位、作业者应具备防治油污染事故的应急能力，制定应急计划，配备与其所从事的海洋石油勘探开发规模相适应的油回收设施和围油、消油器材。  
配备化学消油剂，应将其牌号、成分报告主管部门核准。

**Article 6** Enterprises, institutions and operators shall have the emergency response capability to prevent and control oil pollution accidents, formulate emergency plans, and equip with oil recovery facilities and oil containment and dispersant equipment commensurate with the scale of offshore oil exploration and development they engage in.  
When equipping chemical dispersants, the brand and composition shall be reported to the competent authority for approval.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　固定式和移动式平台的防污设备的要求：  
(一)应设置油水分离设备；  
(二)采油平台应设置含油污水处理设备，该设备处理后的污水含油量应达到国家排放标准；  
(三)应设置排油监控装置；  
(四)应设置残油、废油回收设施；  
(五)应设置垃圾粉碎设备；  
(六)上述设备应经中华人民共和国船舶检验机关检验合格，并获得有效证书。

**Article 7** Requirements for anti-pollution equipment on fixed and mobile platforms:  
(1) Oil-water separation equipment shall be installed;  
(2) Production platforms shall be equipped with oily wastewater treatment equipment, and the oil content of the wastewater treated by such equipment shall meet the national discharge standards;  
(3) Oil discharge monitoring devices shall be installed;  
(4) Facilities for recovering residual oil and waste oil shall be installed;  
(5) Garbage crushing equipment shall be installed;  
(6) The above equipment shall be inspected and approved by the Ship Inspection Authority of the People's Republic of China and obtain valid certificates.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　一九八三年三月一日以前，已经在中华人民共和国管辖海域从事石油勘探开发的固定式和移动式平台，防污设备达不到规定要求的，应采取有效措施，防止污染，并在本条例颁布后三年内使防污设备达到规定的要求。

**Article 8** Before March 1, 1983, fixed and mobile platforms already engaged in offshore oil exploration and development in the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, if their anti-pollution equipment does not meet the specified requirements, shall take effective measures to prevent pollution, and shall bring the anti-pollution equipment into compliance with the specified requirements within three years after the promulgation of these Regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　企业、事业单位和作业者应具有有关污染损害民事责任保险或其他财务保证。

**Article 9** Enterprises, institutions and operators shall have civil liability insurance for pollution damage or other financial guarantees.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　固定式和移动式平台应备有由主管部门批准格式的防污记录簿。

**Article 10** Fixed and mobile platforms shall be provided with anti-pollution record books in the format approved by the competent authority.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　固定式和移动式平台的含油污水，不得直接或稀释排放。经过处理后排放的污水，含油量必须符合国家有关含油污水排放标准。

**Article 11** Oily wastewater from fixed and mobile platforms shall not be discharged directly or after dilution. For wastewater discharged after treatment, the oil content must comply with the relevant national standards for oily wastewater discharge.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对其他废弃物的管理要求：  
(一)残油、废油、油基泥浆、含油垃圾和其他有毒残液残渣，必须回收，不得排放或弃置入海；  
(二)大量工业垃圾的弃置，按照海洋倾废的规定管理；零星工业垃圾，不得投弃于渔业水域和航道；  
(三)生活垃圾，需要在距最近陆地十二海里以内投弃的，应经粉碎处理，粒径应小于二十五毫米。

**Article 12** Requirements for management of other wastes:  
(1) Residual oil, waste oil, oil-based mud, oil-bearing garbage and other toxic residual liquids and residues must be recovered and shall not be discharged or discarded into the sea;  
(2) The disposal of large quantities of industrial garbage shall be managed in accordance with the provisions on marine dumping; sporadic industrial garbage shall not be cast into fishery waters and navigation channels;  
(3) Domestic waste, if it needs to be discarded within 12 nautical miles from the nearest land, shall be crushed and the particle size shall be less than 25 millimeters.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　海洋石油勘探开发需要在重要渔业水域进行炸药爆破或其他对渔业资源有损害的作业时，应采取有效措施，避开主要经济鱼虾类的产卵、繁殖和捕捞季节，作业前报告主管部门，作业时并应有明显的标志、信号。  
主管部门接到报告后，应及时将作业地点、时间等通告有关单位。

**Article 13** When offshore oil exploration and development requires explosive blasting or other operations harmful to fishery resources in important fishery waters, effective measures shall be taken to avoid the spawning, breeding and fishing seasons of major economic fish and shrimp species; the competent authority shall be reported before the operation, and conspicuous signs and signals shall be provided during the operation.  
After receiving the report, the competent authority shall promptly notify the relevant units of the location and time of the operation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　海上储油设施、输油管线应符合防渗、防漏、防腐蚀的要求，并应经常检查，保持良好状态，防止发生漏油事故。

**Article 14** Offshore oil storage facilities and oil pipelines shall meet the requirements for seepage prevention, leakage prevention and corrosion prevention, and shall be inspected regularly to maintain good condition to prevent oil leakage accidents.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　海上试油应使油气通过燃烧器充分燃烧。对试油中落海的油类和油性混合物，应采取有效措施处理，并如实记录。

**Article 15** Offshore well testing shall ensure that oil and gas are fully burned through burners. Effective measures shall be taken to treat oil and oily mixtures that fall into the sea during well testing, and records shall be made truthfully.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　企业、事业单位及作业者在作业中发生溢油、漏油等污染事故，应迅速采取围油、回收油的措施，控制、减轻和消除污染。  
发生大量溢油、漏油和井喷等重大油污染事故，应立即报告主管部门，并采取有效措施，控制和消除油污染，接受主管部门的调查处理。

**Article 16** When oil spill, leakage or other pollution accidents occur during operations, enterprises, institutions and operators shall promptly take measures to contain and recover oil, and to control, mitigate and eliminate pollution.  
When major oil pollution accidents such as large-scale oil spill, leakage or blowout occur, they shall immediately report to the competent authority, take effective measures to control and eliminate oil pollution, and accept investigation and handling by the competent authority.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　化学消油剂要控制使用：  
(一)在发生油污染事故时，应采取回收措施，对少量确实无法回收的油，准许使用少量的化学消油剂。  
(二)一次性使用化学消油剂的数量(包括溶剂在内)，应根据不同海域等情况，由主管部门另作具体规定。作业者应按规定向主管部门报告，经准许后方可使用。  
(三)在海面浮油可能发生火灾或者严重危及人命和财产安全，又无法使用回收方法处理，而使用化学消油剂可以减轻污染和避免扩大事故后果的紧急情况下，使用化学消油剂的数量和报告程序可不受本条(二)项规定限制。但事后，应将事故情况和使用化学消油剂情况详细报告主管部门。  
(四)必须使用经主管部门核准的化学消油剂。

**Article 17** Chemical dispersants shall be used in a controlled manner:  
(1) When oil pollution accidents occur, recovery measures shall be taken; for a small amount of oil that is truly unrecoverable, a small amount of chemical dispersant is permitted.  
(2) The quantity of chemical dispersant used at one time (including solvent) shall be specifically prescribed by the competent authority according to different sea areas and other conditions. The operator shall report to the competent authority as prescribed and may use it only after approval.  
(3) In emergency situations where floating oil on the sea surface may cause fire or seriously endanger life and property safety, and recovery methods cannot be used, and the use of chemical dispersant can mitigate pollution and avoid expanding the consequences of the accident, the quantity and reporting procedures for the use of chemical dispersant may be exempted from the restrictions of paragraph (2) of this Article. However, after the event, the accident situation and the use of chemical dispersant shall be reported in detail to the competent authority.  
(4) Chemical dispersants that have been approved by the competent authority must be used.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　作业者应将下列情况详细地、如实地记载于平台防污记录簿：  
(一)防污设备、设施的运行情况；  
(二)含油污水处理和排放情况；  
(三)其他废弃物的处理、排放和投弃情况；  
(四)发生溢油、漏油、井喷等油污染事故及处理情况；  
(五)进行爆破作业情况；  
(六)使用化学消油剂的情况；  
(七)主管部门规定的其他事项。

**Article 18** The operator shall record the following situations in detail and truthfully in the platform anti-pollution record book:  
(1) Operation of anti-pollution equipment and facilities;  
(2) Treatment and discharge of oily wastewater;  
(3) Treatment, discharge and disposal of other wastes;  
(4) Occurrence and handling of oil pollution accidents such as oil spill, leakage and blowout;  
(5) Blasting operations;  
(6) Use of chemical dispersants;  
(7) Other matters prescribed by the competent authority.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　企业和作业者在每季度末后十五日内，应按主管部门批准的格式，向主管部门综合报告该季度防污染情况及污染事故的情况。  
固定式平台和移动式平台的位置，应及时通知主管部门。

**Article 19** Enterprises and operators shall, within fifteen days after the end of each quarter, submit a comprehensive report to the competent authority on the pollution prevention situation and pollution accidents of that quarter in the format approved by the competent authority.  
The positions of fixed and mobile platforms shall be promptly notified to the competent authority.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　主管部门的公务人员或指派的人员，有权登临固定式和移动式平台以及其他有关设施，进行监测和检查。包括：  
(一)采集各类样品；  
(二)检查各项防污设备、设施和器材的装备、运行或使用情况；  
(三)检查有关的文书、证件；  
(四)检查防污记录簿及有关的操作记录，必要时可进行复制和摘录，并要求平台负责人签证该复制和摘录件为正确无误的副本；  
(五)向有关人员调查污染事故；  
(六)其他有关的事项。

**Article 20** Public servants or designated personnel of the competent authority shall have the right to board fixed and mobile platforms and other related facilities to conduct monitoring and inspection. Including:  
(1) Collecting various samples;  
(2) Inspecting the equipment, operation or use of anti-pollution equipment, facilities and appliances;  
(3) Inspecting relevant documents and certificates;  
(4) Inspecting the anti-pollution record book and relevant operation records, and may make copies and extracts when necessary, and request the person in charge of the platform to certify that such copies and extracts are correct and true copies;  
(5) Investigating pollution accidents with relevant personnel;  
(6) Other relevant matters.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　主管部门的公务船舶应有明显标志。公务人员或指派的人员执行公务时，必须穿着公务制服，携带证件。  
被检查者应为上述公务船舶、公务人员和指派人员提供方便，并如实提供材料，陈述情况。

**Article 21** Public service vessels of the competent authority shall have conspicuous markings. When performing official duties, public servants or designated personnel must wear official uniforms and carry identification.  
The person being inspected shall provide convenience to the above-mentioned public service vessels, public servants and designated personnel, and truthfully provide materials and state the situation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　受到海洋石油勘探开发污染损害，要求赔偿的单位和个人，应按照[《中华人民共和国环境保护法》第三十二条](../../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国环境保护法.md#art-32)的规定及[《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》第四十二条](../../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法.md#art-42)的规定，申请主管部门处理，要求造成污染损害的一方赔偿损失。受损害一方应提交污染损害索赔报告书，报告书应包括以下内容：  
(一)受石油勘探开发污染损害的时间、地点、范围、对象；  
(二)受污染损害的损失清单，包括品名、数量、单价、计算方法，以及养殖或自然等情况；  
(三)有关科研部门鉴定或公证机关对损害情况的签证；  
(四)尽可能提供受污染损害的原始单证，有关情况的照片，其他有关索赔的证明单据、材料。

**Article 22** Units and individuals that have suffered pollution damage from offshore oil exploration and development and claim compensation shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 32 of the Environmental Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and Article 42 of the Marine Environment Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, apply to the competent authority for handling, and demand the party causing the pollution damage to compensate for the losses. The damaged party shall submit a pollution damage claim report, which shall include the following contents:  
(1) Time, location, scope and object of the pollution damage from oil exploration and development;  
(2) List of losses caused by pollution damage, including names, quantities, unit prices, calculation methods, and conditions of aquaculture or nature;  
(3) Expert evaluation by relevant scientific research departments or certification by notarial authorities of the damage situation;  
(4) To the extent possible, original documents of pollution damage, photographs of relevant situations, and other supporting documents and materials related to the claim.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　因清除海洋石油勘探开发污染物，需要索取清除污染物费用的单位和个人(有商业合同者除外)，在申请主管部门处理时，应向主管部门提交索取清除费用报告书。该报告书应包括以下内容：  
(一)清除污染物的时间、地点、对象；  
(二)投入的人力、机具、船只、清除材料的数量、单价、计算方法；  
(三)组织清除的管理费、交通费及其他有关费用；  
(四)清除效果及情况；  
(五)其他有关的证据和证明材料。

**Article 23** Units and individuals that need to claim expenses for the removal of pollutants from offshore oil exploration and development (except those with commercial contracts) shall, when applying to the competent authority for handling, submit a report for claiming removal expenses. The report shall include the following contents:  
(1) Time, location and object of pollutant removal;  
(2) Quantity, unit price and calculation method of labor, machinery, vessels and removal materials invested;  
(3) Management fees, transportation fees and other related expenses for organizing the removal;  
(4) Removal effect and situation;  
(5) Other relevant evidence and supporting materials.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　由于不可抗力发生污染损害事故的企业、事业单位、作业者，要求免于承担赔偿责任的，应向主管部门提交报告。该报告应能证实污染损害确实属于[《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》第四十三条](../../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法.md#art-43)所列的情况之一，并经过及时采取合理措施仍不能避免的。

**Article 24** Enterprises, institutions and operators that have caused pollution damage accidents due to force majeure and seek exemption from compensation liability shall submit a report to the competent authority. The report shall be able to prove that the pollution damage indeed falls within one of the circumstances listed in Article 43 of the Marine Environment Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and could not be avoided despite timely and reasonable measures.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　主管部门受理的海洋石油勘探开发污染损害赔偿责任和赔偿金额纠纷，在调查了解的基础上，可以进行调解处理。  
当事人不愿调解或对主管部门的调解处理不服的，可以按[《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》第四十二条](../../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法.md#art-42)的规定办理。

**Article 25** For disputes over liability for pollution damage compensation and the amount of compensation from offshore oil exploration and development accepted by the competent authority, mediation may be conducted on the basis of investigation and understanding.  
If the parties are unwilling to mediate or are dissatisfied with the mediation handling by the competent authority, they may handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of Article 42 of the Marine Environment Protection Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　主管部门对违反《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》和本条例的企业、事业单位、作业者，可以责令其限期治理，支付消除污染费用，赔偿国家损失；超过标准排放污染物的，可以责令其交纳排污费。

**Article 26** The competent authority may, for enterprises, institutions and operators that violate the Marine Environment Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and these Regulations, order them to carry out treatment within a time limit, pay pollution elimination costs, and compensate for the losses of the State; for those discharging pollutants exceeding the standards, may order them to pay pollutant discharge fees.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　主管部门对违反《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》和本条例的企业、事业单位、作业者和个人，可视其情节轻重，予以警告或罚款处分。  
罚款分为以下几种：  
(一)对造成海洋环境污染的企业、事业单位、作业者的罚款，最高额为人民币十万元。  
(二)对企业、事业单位、作业者的下列违法行为，罚款最高额为人民币五千元：  
1．不按规定向主管部门报告重大油污染事故；  
2．不按规定使用化学消油剂。  
(三)对企业、事业单位、作业者的下列违法行为，罚款最高额为人民币一千元：  
1．不按规定配备防污记录簿；  
2．防污记录簿的记载非正规化或者伪造；  
3．不按规定报告或通知有关情况；  
4．阻挠公务人员或指派人员执行公务。  
(四)对有直接责任的个人，可根据情节轻重，酌情处以罚款。

**Article 27** The competent authority may, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, impose a warning or a fine on enterprises, institutions, operators and individuals that violate the Marine Environment Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and these Regulations.  
The fines are divided into the following categories:  
(1) For enterprises, institutions and operators that cause marine environmental pollution, the maximum fine shall be RMB 100,000.  
(2) For the following illegal acts of enterprises, institutions and operators, the maximum fine shall be RMB 5,000:  
1. Failing to report major oil pollution accidents to the competent authority as prescribed;  
2. Failing to use chemical dispersants as prescribed.  
(3) For the following illegal acts of enterprises, institutions and operators, the maximum fine shall be RMB 1,000:  
1. Failing to equip anti-pollution record books as prescribed;  
2. Irregular entries in the anti-pollution record book or forgery thereof;  
3. Failing to report or notify relevant information as prescribed;  
4. Obstructing public servants or designated personnel from performing official duties.  
(4) For individuals with direct responsibility, a fine may be imposed as appropriate according to the seriousness of the circumstances.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　当事人对主管部门的处罚决定不服的，按[《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》第四十一条](../../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法.md#art-41)的规定处理。

**Article 28** If a party is dissatisfied with a penalty decision of the competent authority, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 41 of the Marine Environment Protection Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　主管部门对主动检举、揭发企业、事业单位、作业者匿报石油勘探开发污染损害事故，或者提供证据，或者采取措施减轻污染损害的单位和个人，给予表扬和奖励。

**Article 29** The competent authority shall give commendation and awards to units and individuals that voluntarily report or expose enterprises, institutions or operators for concealing pollution damage accidents from oil exploration and development, or provide evidence, or take measures to mitigate pollution damage.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本条例中下列用语的含义是：  
(一)“固定式和移动式平台”，即《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》中所称的钻井船、钻井平台和采油平台，并包括其他平台。  
(二)“海洋石油勘探开发”，是指海洋石油勘探、开发、生产储存和管线输送等作业活动。  
(三)“作业者”，是指实施海洋石油勘探开发作业的实体。

**Article 30** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms mean:  
(1) "Fixed and mobile platforms" means the drilling ships, drilling platforms and production platforms referred to in the Marine Environment Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, and includes other platforms.  
(2) "Offshore oil exploration and development" means operational activities such as offshore oil exploration, development, production storage and pipeline transportation.  
(3) "Operator" means the entity that implements offshore oil exploration and development operations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 31** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
