# 全国污染源普查条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2019-03-02

> (2007年10月9日中华人民共和国国务院令第508号公布　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了科学、有效地组织实施全国污染源普查，保障污染源普查数据的准确性和及时性，根据《中华人民共和国统计法》和《中华人民共和国环境保护法》，制定本条例。 第二条　污染源普查的任务是，掌握各类污染源的数量、行业和地区分布情况，了解主要污染物的产生、排放和处理情况，建立健全重点污染源档案、污染源信息数据库和环境统计平台，为制定经济社会发展和环境保护政策、规划提供依据。 第三条　本条例所称污染源，是指因生产、生活和其他活动向环境排放污染物或者对环境产生不良影响的场所、设施、装置以及其他污染发生源。 第四条　污染源普查按照全国统一领导、部门分工协作、地方分级负责、各方共同参与的原则组织实施。 第五条　污染源普查所需经费，由中央和地方各级人民政府共同负担，并列入相应年度的财政预算，按时拨付，确保足额到位。 污染源普查经费应当统一管理，专款专用，严格控制支出。 第六条　全国污染源普查每10年进行1次，标准时点为普查年份的12月31日。 第七条　报刊、广播、电视和互联网等新闻媒体，应当及时开展污染源普查工作的宣传报道。 第二章　污染源普查的对象、范围、内容和方法 第八条　污染源普查的对象是中华人民共和国境内有污染源的单位和个体经营户。 第九条　污染源普查对象有义务接受污染源普查领导小组办公室、普查人员依法进行的调查，并如实反映情况，提供有关资料，按照要求填报污染源普查表。 污染源普查对象不得迟报、虚报、瞒报和拒报普查数据；不得推诿、拒绝和阻挠调查；不得转移、隐匿、篡改、毁弃原材料消耗记录、生产记录、污染物治理设施运行记录、污染物排放监测记录以及其他与污染物产生和排放有关的原始资料。 第十条　污染源普查范围包括：工业污染源，农业污染源，生活污染源，集中式污染治理设施和其他产生、排放污染物的设施。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了科学、有效地组织实施全国污染源普查，保障污染源普查数据的准确性和及时性，根据《中华人民共和国统计法》和《中华人民共和国环境保护法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China and the Environmental Protection Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of organizing and implementing a national pollution source census in a scientific and effective manner, and ensuring the accuracy and timeliness of the data of the pollution source census.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　污染源普查的任务是，掌握各类污染源的数量、行业和地区分布情况，了解主要污染物的产生、排放和处理情况，建立健全重点污染源档案、污染源信息数据库和环境统计平台，为制定经济社会发展和环境保护政策、规划提供依据。

**Article 2** The tasks of a pollution source census are to ascertain the quantity, industrial and regional distribution of various types of pollution sources, to understand the generation, discharge and treatment of major pollutants, to establish and improve the archives of key pollution sources, the pollution source information database and the environmental statistics platform, so as to provide a basis for formulating policies and plans for economic and social development and environmental protection.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称污染源，是指因生产、生活和其他活动向环境排放污染物或者对环境产生不良影响的场所、设施、装置以及其他污染发生源。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Regulations, “pollution source” means any site, facility, installation or other source that discharges pollutants into the environment or causes adverse effects on the environment due to production, living or other activities.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　污染源普查按照全国统一领导、部门分工协作、地方分级负责、各方共同参与的原则组织实施。

**Article 4** A pollution source census shall be organized and implemented under the principles of unified leadership by the State, division of work and cooperation among departments, hierarchical responsibility of local authorities, and joint participation of all parties.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　污染源普查所需经费，由中央和地方各级人民政府共同负担，并列入相应年度的财政预算，按时拨付，确保足额到位。  
污染源普查经费应当统一管理，专款专用，严格控制支出。

**Article 5** The funds required for a pollution source census shall be shared by the Central People's Government and the local people's governments at all levels, and shall be included in the fiscal budgets of the corresponding fiscal years, allocated in a timely manner, and ensured to be fully in place.  
The funds for a pollution source census shall be managed in a unified manner, used only for the designated purpose, and the expenditures shall be strictly controlled.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　全国污染源普查每10年进行1次，标准时点为普查年份的12月31日。

**Article 6** A national pollution source census shall be conducted once every ten years, and the standard reference date shall be December 31 of the census year.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　报刊、广播、电视和互联网等新闻媒体，应当及时开展污染源普查工作的宣传报道。

**Article 7** Newspapers, periodicals, radio, television, the Internet and other news media shall promptly carry out publicity and coverage of the pollution source census work.

### 第二章　污染源普查的对象、范围、内容和方法

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　污染源普查的对象是中华人民共和国境内有污染源的单位和个体经营户。

**Article 8** The subjects of a pollution source census are the entities and individual business operators within the territory of the People's Republic of China that have pollution sources.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　污染源普查对象有义务接受污染源普查领导小组办公室、普查人员依法进行的调查，并如实反映情况，提供有关资料，按照要求填报污染源普查表。  
污染源普查对象不得迟报、虚报、瞒报和拒报普查数据；不得推诿、拒绝和阻挠调查；不得转移、隐匿、篡改、毁弃原材料消耗记录、生产记录、污染物治理设施运行记录、污染物排放监测记录以及其他与污染物产生和排放有关的原始资料。

**Article 9** The subjects of a pollution source census have the obligation to accept the investigations conducted by the office of the leading group for the pollution source census and the census takers according to law, truthfully reflect the situation, provide relevant materials, and fill in the pollution source census forms as required.  
No subject of a pollution source census shall report census data later than the prescribed time, make false reports, conceal data, or refuse to report; nor shall they prevaricate, refuse or obstruct the investigation; nor shall they transfer, conceal, tamper with or destroy the records of consumption of raw materials, production records, records of operation of pollutant treatment facilities, records of monitoring of pollutant discharge, and other original materials relating to the generation and discharge of pollutants.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　污染源普查范围包括：工业污染源，农业污染源，生活污染源，集中式污染治理设施和其他产生、排放污染物的设施。

**Article 10** The scope of a pollution source census includes: industrial pollution sources, agricultural pollution sources, domestic pollution sources, centralized pollution treatment facilities, and other facilities that generate or discharge pollutants.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　工业污染源普查的主要内容包括：企业基本登记信息，原材料消耗情况，产品生产情况，产生污染的设施情况，各类污染物产生、治理、排放和综合利用情况，各类污染防治设施建设、运行情况等。  
农业污染源普查的主要内容包括：农业生产规模，用水、排水情况，化肥、农药、饲料和饲料添加剂以及农用薄膜等农业投入品使用情况，秸秆等种植业剩余物处理情况以及养殖业污染物产生、治理情况等。  
生活污染源普查的主要内容包括：从事第三产业的单位的基本情况和污染物的产生、排放、治理情况，机动车污染物排放情况，城镇生活能源结构和能源消费量，生活用水量、排水量以及污染物排放情况等。  
集中式污染治理设施普查的主要内容包括：设施基本情况和运行状况，污染物的处理处置情况，渗滤液、污泥、焚烧残渣和废气的产生、处置以及利用情况等。

**Article 11** The main contents of the industrial pollution source census shall include: basic registration information of enterprises, consumption of raw materials, production of products, facilities generating pollution, generation, treatment, discharge and comprehensive utilization of various pollutants, and construction and operation of various pollution prevention and control facilities.  
The main contents of the agricultural pollution source census shall include: scale of agricultural production, water use and drainage, use of agricultural inputs such as chemical fertilizers, pesticides, feed and feed additives, and agricultural films, treatment of crop residues such as straw, and generation and treatment of pollutants from aquaculture.  
The main contents of the domestic pollution source census shall include: basic information of units engaged in the tertiary industry and the generation, discharge and treatment of pollutants, pollutant discharge from motor vehicles, urban domestic energy structure and energy consumption, domestic water consumption and drainage, and pollutant discharge.  
The main contents of the centralized pollution treatment facility census shall include: basic information and operation status of facilities, treatment and disposal of pollutants, generation, disposal and utilization of leachate, sludge, incineration residues and waste gases.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　每次污染源普查的具体范围和内容，由国务院批准的普查方案确定。

**Article 12** The specific scope and content of each pollution source census shall be determined by the census plan approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　污染源普查采用全面调查的方法，必要时可以采用抽样调查的方法。  
污染源普查采用全国统一的标准和技术要求。

**Article 13** The pollution source census shall adopt a comprehensive survey method, and a sampling survey method may be adopted when necessary.  
The pollution source census shall adopt unified national standards and technical requirements.

### 第三章　污染源普查的组织实施

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　全国污染源普查领导小组负责领导和协调全国污染源普查工作。  
全国污染源普查领导小组办公室设在国务院生态环境主管部门，负责全国污染源普查日常工作。

**Article 14** The National Pollution Source Census Leading Group shall be responsible for leading and coordinating the national pollution source census work.  
The office of the National Pollution Source Census Leading Group shall be established within the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council and shall be responsible for the daily work of the national pollution source census.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级以上地方人民政府污染源普查领导小组，按照全国污染源普查领导小组的统一规定和要求，领导和协调本行政区域的污染源普查工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府污染源普查领导小组办公室设在同级生态环境主管部门，负责本行政区域的污染源普查日常工作。  
乡(镇)人民政府、街道办事处和村(居)民委员会应当广泛动员和组织社会力量积极参与并认真做好污染源普查工作。

**Article 15** The pollution source census leading groups of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the unified provisions and requirements of the National Pollution Source Census Leading Group, lead and coordinate the pollution source census work within their respective administrative regions.  
The office of the pollution source census leading group of a local people's government at or above the county level shall be established within the competent department of ecological environment at the same level and shall be responsible for the daily work of the pollution source census within its administrative region.  
Township (town) people's governments, sub-district offices, and villagers' (residents') committees shall extensively mobilize and organize social forces to actively participate in and diligently carry out the pollution source census work.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门和其他有关部门，按照职责分工和污染源普查领导小组的统一要求，做好污染源普查相关工作。

**Article 16** The competent departments of ecological environment and other relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties and the unified requirements of the pollution source census leading group, properly carry out relevant work concerning the pollution source census.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　全国污染源普查方案由全国污染源普查领导小组办公室拟订，经全国污染源普查领导小组审核同意，报国务院批准。  
全国污染源普查方案应当包括：普查的具体范围和内容、普查的主要污染物、普查方法、普查的组织实施以及经费预算等。  
拟订全国污染源普查方案，应当充分听取有关部门和专家的意见。

**Article 17** The national pollution source census plan shall be drafted by the office of the National Pollution Source Census Leading Group, reviewed and approved by the National Pollution Source Census Leading Group, and submitted to the State Council for approval.  
The national pollution source census plan shall include: the specific scope and content of the census, the main pollutants to be surveyed, the census methods, the organization and implementation of the census, and the budget.  
When drafting the national pollution source census plan, full consideration shall be given to the opinions of relevant departments and experts.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　全国污染源普查领导小组办公室根据全国污染源普查方案拟订污染源普查表，报国家统计局审定。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府污染源普查领导小组办公室，可以根据需要增设本行政区域污染源普查附表，报全国污染源普查领导小组办公室批准后使用。

**Article 18** The office of the National Pollution Source Census Leading Group shall, based on the national pollution source census plan, draft the pollution source census forms and submit them to the National Bureau of Statistics for verification.  
The office of the pollution source census leading group of a people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, as needed, add supplementary pollution source census forms for its administrative region, which shall be used after being submitted to the office of the National Pollution Source Census Leading Group for approval.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　在普查启动阶段，污染源普查领导小组办公室应当进行单位清查。  
县级以上人民政府机构编制、民政、市场监督管理以及其他具有设立审批、登记职能的部门，应当向同级污染源普查领导小组办公室提供其审批或者登记的单位资料，并协助做好单位清查工作。  
污染源普查领导小组办公室应当以本行政区域现有的基本单位名录库为基础，按照全国污染源普查方案确定的污染源普查的具体范围，结合有关部门提供的单位资料，对污染源逐一核实清查，形成污染源普查单位名录。

**Article 19** At the initiation stage of the census, the office of the pollution source census leading group shall conduct an inventory of units.  
The departments of organizational establishment, civil affairs, market supervision and administration, and other departments with functions of establishment approval or registration of people's governments at or above the county level shall provide the office of the pollution source census leading group at the same level with information on units approved or registered by them, and shall assist in properly carrying out the unit inventory work.  
The office of the pollution source census leading group shall, based on the existing basic unit directory database within its administrative region and in accordance with the specific scope of the pollution source census determined by the national pollution source census plan, and in conjunction with the unit information provided by relevant departments, verify and inventory each pollution source one by one, and form a list of units for the pollution source census.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　列入污染源普查范围的大、中型工业企业，应当明确相关机构负责本企业污染源普查表的填报工作，其他单位应当指定人员负责本单位污染源普查表的填报工作。

**Article 20** Large and medium-sized industrial enterprises included in the scope of the pollution source census shall designate relevant institutions to be responsible for filling in the pollution source census forms of their enterprises, and other units shall designate personnel to be responsible for filling in the pollution source census forms of their units.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　污染源普查领导小组办公室可以根据工作需要，聘用或者从有关单位借调人员从事污染源普查工作。  
污染源普查领导小组办公室应当与聘用人员依法签订劳动合同，支付劳动报酬，并为其办理社会保险。借调人员的工资由原单位支付，其福利待遇保持不变。

**Article 21** The Leading Group Office for Pollution Source Census may, based on work needs, employ or second personnel from relevant units to engage in the pollution source census work.  
The Leading Group Office for Pollution Source Census shall conclude a labor contract with the employed personnel according to law, pay labor remuneration, and arrange social insurance for them. The salary of seconded personnel shall be paid by their original units, and their welfare benefits shall remain unchanged.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　普查人员应当坚持实事求是，恪守职业道德，具有执行普查任务所需要的专业知识。  
污染源普查领导小组办公室应当对普查人员进行业务培训，对考核合格的颁发全国统一的普查员工作证。

**Article 22** Census personnel shall adhere to the principle of seeking truth from facts, abide by professional ethics, and possess the professional knowledge required for performing the census task.  
The Leading Group Office for Pollution Source Census shall provide professional training to census personnel, and issue the nationally unified census worker certificate to those who pass the assessment.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　普查人员依法独立行使调查、报告、监督和检查的职权，有权查阅普查对象的原材料消耗记录、生产记录、污染物治理设施运行记录、污染物排放监测记录以及其他与污染物产生和排放有关的原始资料，并有权要求普查对象改正其填报的污染源普查表中不真实、不完整的内容。

**Article 23** Census personnel shall, according to law, independently exercise the functions and powers of investigation, reporting, supervision and examination, and have the right to consult the raw material consumption records, production records, pollutant treatment facility operation records, pollutant discharge monitoring records, and other original materials related to the generation and discharge of pollutants of the census object, and have the right to request the census object to correct the untrue or incomplete contents in the pollution source census form filled by it.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　普查人员应当严格执行全国污染源普查方案，不得伪造、篡改普查资料，不得强令、授意普查对象提供虚假普查资料。  
普查人员执行污染源调查任务，不得少于2人，并应当出示普查员工作证；未出示普查员工作证的，普查对象可以拒绝接受调查。

**Article 24** Census personnel shall strictly implement the National Pollution Source Census Plan, and shall not falsify or tamper with census data, and shall not coerce or instruct the census object to provide false census data.  
When performing the pollution source investigation task, the number of census personnel shall not be fewer than two, and they shall present the census worker certificate; if the census worker certificate is not presented, the census object may refuse to accept the investigation.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　普查人员应当依法直接访问普查对象，指导普查对象填报污染源普查表。污染源普查表填写完成后，应当由普查对象签字或者盖章确认。普查对象应当对其签字或者盖章的普查资料的真实性负责。  
污染源普查领导小组办公室对其登记、录入的普查资料与普查对象填报的普查资料的一致性负责，并对其加工、整理的普查资料的准确性负责。  
污染源普查领导小组办公室在登记、录入、加工和整理普查资料过程中，对普查资料有疑义的，应当向普查对象核实，普查对象应当如实说明或者改正。

**Article 25** Census personnel shall according to law directly visit the census object and guide the census object to fill in the pollution source census form. After the pollution source census form is completed, it shall be signed or sealed by the census object for confirmation. The census object shall be responsible for the authenticity of the census data signed or sealed by it.  
The Leading Group Office for Pollution Source Census shall be responsible for the consistency between the census data registered and entered by it and the census data filled by the census object, and shall be responsible for the accuracy of the census data processed and sorted by it.  
If the Leading Group Office for Pollution Source Census has doubts about the census data during the process of registration, entry, processing and sorting, it shall verify with the census object, and the census object shall truthfully explain or correct.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　各地方、各部门、各单位的负责人不得擅自修改污染源普查领导小组办公室、普查人员依法取得的污染源普查资料；不得强令或者授意污染源普查领导小组办公室、普查人员伪造或者篡改普查资料；不得对拒绝、抵制伪造或者篡改普查资料的普查人员打击报复。

**Article 26** The persons in charge of all localities, departments and units shall not unilaterally modify the pollution source census data lawfully obtained by the Leading Group Office for Pollution Source Census and census personnel; shall not coerce or instruct the Leading Group Office for Pollution Source Census or census personnel to falsify or tamper with census data; and shall not retaliate against census personnel who refuse or resist falsification or tampering of census data.

### 第四章　数据处理和质量控制

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　污染源普查领导小组办公室应当按照全国污染源普查方案和有关标准、技术要求进行数据处理，并按时上报普查数据。

**Article 27** The Leading Group Office for Pollution Source Census shall process data in accordance with the National Pollution Source Census Plan and relevant standards and technical requirements, and shall report the census data on time.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　污染源普查领导小组办公室应当做好污染源普查数据备份和数据入库工作，建立健全污染源信息数据库，并加强日常管理和维护更新。

**Article 28** The Leading Group Office for Pollution Source Census shall properly perform the work of data backup and data warehousing for pollution source census, establish and improve the pollution source information database, and strengthen routine management, maintenance and updating.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　污染源普查领导小组办公室应当按照全国污染源普查方案，建立污染源普查数据质量控制岗位责任制，并对普查中的每个环节进行质量控制和检查验收。  
污染源普查数据不符合全国污染源普查方案或者有关标准、技术要求的，上一级污染源普查领导小组办公室可以要求下一级污染源普查领导小组办公室重新调查，确保普查数据的一致性、真实性和有效性。

**Article 29** The Leading Group Office for Pollution Source Census shall, in accordance with the National Pollution Source Census Plan, establish a post responsibility system for quality control of pollution source census data, and conduct quality control, inspection and acceptance for each link in the census.  
If the pollution source census data do not comply with the National Pollution Source Census Plan or relevant standards and technical requirements, the Leading Group Office for Pollution Source Census at the higher level may require the Leading Group Office for Pollution Source Census at the lower level to re-investigate to ensure the consistency, authenticity and validity of the census data.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　全国污染源普查领导小组办公室统一组织对污染源普查数据的质量核查。核查结果作为评估全国或者各省、自治区、直辖市污染源普查数据质量的重要依据。  
污染源普查数据的质量达不到规定要求的，有关污染源普查领导小组办公室应当在全国污染源普查领导小组办公室规定的时间内重新进行污染源普查。

**Article 30** The National Leading Group Office for Pollution Source Census shall uniformly organize the quality inspection of pollution source census data. The inspection results shall serve as an important basis for evaluating the quality of pollution source census data of the whole country or of each province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
If the quality of pollution source census data fails to meet the prescribed requirements, the relevant Leading Group Office for Pollution Source Census shall re-conduct the pollution source census within the time limit prescribed by the National Leading Group Office for Pollution Source Census.

### 第五章　数据发布、资料管理和开发应用

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　全国污染源普查公报，根据全国污染源普查领导小组的决定发布。  
地方污染源普查公报，经上一级污染源普查领导小组办公室核准发布。

**Article 31** The national communiqué on the pollution source census shall be published in accordance with the decision of the National Pollution Source Census Leading Group.  
The local communiqué on the pollution source census shall be published upon the verification and approval of the office of the pollution source census leading group at the next higher level.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　普查对象提供的资料和污染源普查领导小组办公室加工、整理的资料属于国家秘密的，应当注明秘密的等级，并按照国家有关保密规定处理。  
污染源普查领导小组办公室、普查人员对在污染源普查中知悉的普查对象的商业秘密，负有保密义务。

**Article 32** Where the data provided by the census subjects and the data processed and sorted out by the office of the pollution source census leading group are state secrets, the classification of secrecy shall be indicated and shall be handled in accordance with the relevant state provisions on secrecy.  
The office of the pollution source census leading group and the census takers shall have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets of the census subjects that they have learned of during the pollution source census.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　污染源普查领导小组办公室应当建立污染源普查资料档案管理制度。污染源普查资料档案的保管、调用和移交应当遵守国家有关档案管理规定。

**Article 33** The office of the pollution source census leading group shall establish a management system for the archives of pollution source census materials. The custody, access, and transfer of the archives of pollution source census materials shall comply with the relevant state provisions on archive management.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家建立污染源普查资料信息共享制度。  
污染源普查领导小组办公室应当在污染源信息数据库的基础上，建立污染源普查资料信息共享平台，促进普查成果的开发和应用。

**Article 34** The State shall establish a system for sharing information of pollution source census materials.  
The office of the pollution source census leading group shall, on the basis of the pollution source information database, establish a platform for sharing information of pollution source census materials to promote the development and application of the census results.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　污染源普查取得的单个普查对象的资料严格限定用于污染源普查目的，不得作为考核普查对象是否完成污染物总量削减计划的依据，不得作为依照其他法律、行政法规对普查对象实施行政处罚和征收排污费的依据。

**Article 35** The data of individual census subjects obtained from the pollution source census shall be strictly limited to the purpose of the pollution source census and shall not be used as the basis for assessing whether the census subjects have fulfilled the plan for total pollutant reduction, nor as the basis for imposing administrative penalties on or collecting pollutant discharge fees from the census subjects in accordance with other laws and administrative regulations.

### 第六章　表彰和处罚

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　对在污染源普查工作中做出突出贡献的集体和个人，应当给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 36** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in the pollution source census work shall be given commendation and rewards.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　地方、部门、单位的负责人有下列行为之一的，依法给予处分，并由县级以上人民政府统计机构予以通报批评；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)擅自修改污染源普查资料的；  
(二)强令、授意污染源普查领导小组办公室、普查人员伪造或者篡改普查资料的；  
(三)对拒绝、抵制伪造或者篡改普查资料的普查人员打击报复的。

**Article 37** Where the person in charge of a local government, department, or entity commits any of the following acts, he or she shall be given a sanction according to law and shall be subject to circulation of a notice of criticism by the statistical agency of the people's government at or above the county level; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) altering the pollution source census data without authorization;  
(2) compelling or instructing the office of the pollution source census leading group or the census takers to falsify or tamper with the census data;  
(3) retaliating against census takers who refuse to or resist falsifying or tampering with the census data.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　普查人员不执行普查方案，或者伪造、篡改普查资料，或者强令、授意普查对象提供虚假普查资料的，依法给予处分。  
污染源普查领导小组办公室、普查人员泄露在普查中知悉的普查对象商业秘密的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；对普查对象造成损害的，应当依法承担民事责任。

**Article 38** Where a census taker fails to implement the census plan, or falsifies or tampers with the census data, or compels or instructs the census subjects to provide false census data, he or she shall be given a sanction according to law.  
Where the office of the pollution source census leading group or the census takers disclose the trade secrets of the census subjects that they have learned of during the census, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given a sanction according to law; if damage is caused to the census subjects, they shall bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　污染源普查对象有下列行为之一的，污染源普查领导小组办公室应当及时向同级人民政府统计机构通报有关情况，提出处理意见，由县级以上人民政府统计机构责令改正，予以通报批评；情节严重的，可以建议对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
(一)迟报、虚报、瞒报或者拒报污染源普查数据的；  
(二)推诿、拒绝或者阻挠普查人员依法进行调查的；  
(三)转移、隐匿、篡改、毁弃原材料消耗记录、生产记录、污染物治理设施运行记录、污染物排放监测记录以及其他与污染物产生和排放有关的原始资料的。  
单位有本条第一款所列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府统计机构予以警告，可以处5万元以下的罚款。  
个体经营户有本条第一款所列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府统计机构予以警告，可以处1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 39** Where a pollution source census subject commits any of the following acts, the office of the pollution source census leading group shall promptly report the relevant circumstances to the statistical agency of the people's government at the same level, put forward suggestions for handling, and the statistical agency of the people's government at or above the county level shall order correction and circulate a notice of criticism; if the circumstances are serious, it may recommend that the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons be given a sanction according to law:  
(1) reporting late, making false reports, concealing reports, or refusing to report pollution source census data;  
(2) shirking, refusing, or obstructing the lawful investigation by the census takers;  
(3) transferring, concealing, tampering with, or destroying original records of raw material consumption, production records, operation records of pollutant treatment facilities, monitoring records of pollutant discharge, and other original materials related to the generation and discharge of pollutants.  
Where a unit commits any of the acts listed in the first paragraph of this Article, the statistical agency of the people's government at or above the county level shall give a warning and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.  
Where an individual business commits any of the acts listed in the first paragraph of this Article, the statistical agency of the people's government at or above the county level shall give a warning and may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　污染源普查领导小组办公室应当设立举报电话和信箱，接受社会各界对污染源普查工作的监督和对违法行为的检举，并对检举有功的人员依法给予奖励，对检举的违法行为，依法予以查处。

**Article 40** The office of the pollution source census leading group shall set up a tip-off telephone number and a mailbox to accept supervision by all sectors of society over the pollution source census work and to receive reports on illegal acts, and shall give rewards to persons who have rendered meritorious service in reporting according to law, and shall investigate and deal with the reported illegal acts according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　军队、武装警察部队的污染源普查工作，由中国人民解放军总后勤部按照国家统一规定和要求组织实施。  
新疆生产建设兵团的污染源普查工作，由新疆生产建设兵团按照国家统一规定和要求组织实施。

**Article 41** The pollution source survey work of the military and the armed police forces shall be organized and implemented by the General Logistics Department of the People's Liberation Army in accordance with the unified provisions and requirements of the State.  
The pollution source survey work of the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps shall be organized and implemented by the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps in accordance with the unified provisions and requirements of the State.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 42** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
