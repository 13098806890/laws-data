# 血吸虫病防治条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2006-05-01

> (2006年4月1日中华人民共和国国务院令第463号公布　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了预防、控制和消灭血吸虫病，保障人体健康、动物健康和公共卫生，促进经济社会发展，根据传染病防治法、动物防疫法，制定本条例。 第二条　国家对血吸虫病防治实行预防为主的方针，坚持防治结合、分类管理、综合治理、联防联控，人与家畜同步防治，重点加强对传染源的管理。 第三条　国务院卫生主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定全国血吸虫病防治规划并组织实施。国务院卫生、农业、水利、林业主管部门依照本条例规定的职责和全国血吸虫病防治规划，制定血吸虫病防治专项工作计划并组织实施。 有血吸虫病防治任务的地区(以下称血吸虫病防治地区)县级以上地方人民政府卫生、农业或者兽医、水利、林业主管部门依照本条例规定的职责，负责本行政区域内的血吸虫病防治及其监督管理工作。 第四条　血吸虫病防治地区县级以上地方人民政府统一领导本行政区域内的血吸虫病防治工作；根据全国血吸虫病防治规划，制定本行政区域的血吸虫病防治计划并组织实施；建立健全血吸虫病防治工作协调机制和工作责任制，对有关部门承担的血吸虫病防治工作进行综合协调和考核、监督。 第五条　血吸虫病防治地区村民委员会、居民委员会应当协助地方各级人民政府及其有关部门开展血吸虫病防治的宣传教育，组织村民、居民参与血吸虫病防治工作。 第六条　国家鼓励血吸虫病防治地区的村民、居民积极参与血吸虫病防治的有关活动；鼓励共产主义青年团等社会组织动员青年团员等积极参与血吸虫病防治的有关活动。 血吸虫病防治地区地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当完善有关制度，方便单位和个人参与血吸虫病防治的宣传教育、捐赠等活动。 第七条　国务院有关部门、血吸虫病防治地区县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门对在血吸虫病防治工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，给予表彰或者奖励。 第二章　预防 第八条　血吸虫病防治地区根据血吸虫病预防控制标准，划分为重点防治地区和一般防治地区。具体办法由国务院卫生主管部门会同国务院农业主管部门制定。 第九条　血吸虫病防治地区县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当组织各类新闻媒体开展公益性血吸虫病防治宣传教育。各类新闻媒体应当开展公益性血吸虫病防治宣传教育。 血吸虫病防治地区县级以上地方人民政府教育主管部门应当组织各级各类学校对学生开展血吸虫病防治知识教育。各级各类学校应当对学生开展血吸虫病防治知识教育。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了预防、控制和消灭血吸虫病，保障人体健康、动物健康和公共卫生，促进经济社会发展，根据传染病防治法、动物防疫法，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of preventing, controlling and eliminating schistosomiasis, safeguarding human health, animal health and public health, promoting economic and social development, and in accordance with the Law on the Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases and the Animal Epidemic Prevention Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家对血吸虫病防治实行预防为主的方针，坚持防治结合、分类管理、综合治理、联防联控，人与家畜同步防治，重点加强对传染源的管理。

**Article 2** The State shall implement the policy of prevention first in the prevention and control of schistosomiasis, adhere to the combination of prevention and control, classified management, comprehensive treatment, joint prevention and control, synchronous prevention and control of humans and domestic animals, and focus on strengthening the management of infection sources.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院卫生主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定全国血吸虫病防治规划并组织实施。国务院卫生、农业、水利、林业主管部门依照本条例规定的职责和全国血吸虫病防治规划，制定血吸虫病防治专项工作计划并组织实施。  
有血吸虫病防治任务的地区(以下称血吸虫病防治地区)县级以上地方人民政府卫生、农业或者兽医、水利、林业主管部门依照本条例规定的职责，负责本行政区域内的血吸虫病防治及其监督管理工作。

**Article 3** The competent health department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate the national schistosomiasis prevention and control plan and organize its implementation. The competent departments of health, agriculture, water resources, and forestry of the State Council shall, in accordance with the functions and duties prescribed in these Regulations and the national schistosomiasis prevention and control plan, formulate special work plans for schistosomiasis prevention and control and organize their implementation.  
In areas with schistosomiasis prevention and control tasks (hereinafter referred to as schistosomiasis prevention and control areas), the competent departments of health, agriculture or veterinary, water resources, and forestry of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the functions and duties prescribed in these Regulations, be responsible for schistosomiasis prevention and control and the supervision and administration thereof within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　血吸虫病防治地区县级以上地方人民政府统一领导本行政区域内的血吸虫病防治工作；根据全国血吸虫病防治规划，制定本行政区域的血吸虫病防治计划并组织实施；建立健全血吸虫病防治工作协调机制和工作责任制，对有关部门承担的血吸虫病防治工作进行综合协调和考核、监督。

**Article 4** The local people's governments at or above the county level in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall exercise unified leadership over the schistosomiasis prevention and control work within their respective administrative regions; formulate schistosomiasis prevention and control plans for their respective administrative regions based on the national schistosomiasis prevention and control plan and organize their implementation; establish and improve the coordination mechanism and work responsibility system for schistosomiasis prevention and control work, and conduct comprehensive coordination, assessment, and supervision of the schistosomiasis prevention and control work undertaken by relevant departments.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　血吸虫病防治地区村民委员会、居民委员会应当协助地方各级人民政府及其有关部门开展血吸虫病防治的宣传教育，组织村民、居民参与血吸虫病防治工作。

**Article 5** The villagers' committees and residents' committees in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall assist the local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments in carrying out publicity and education on schistosomiasis prevention and control, and organize villagers and residents to participate in schistosomiasis prevention and control work.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励血吸虫病防治地区的村民、居民积极参与血吸虫病防治的有关活动；鼓励共产主义青年团等社会组织动员青年团员等积极参与血吸虫病防治的有关活动。  
血吸虫病防治地区地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当完善有关制度，方便单位和个人参与血吸虫病防治的宣传教育、捐赠等活动。

**Article 6** The State encourages villagers and residents in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas to actively participate in relevant activities of schistosomiasis prevention and control; encourages social organizations such as the Communist Youth League to mobilize youth league members and others to actively participate in relevant activities of schistosomiasis prevention and control.  
The local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall improve relevant systems to facilitate the participation of units and individuals in publicity and education, donations, and other activities for schistosomiasis prevention and control.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院有关部门、血吸虫病防治地区县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门对在血吸虫病防治工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，给予表彰或者奖励。

**Article 7** The relevant departments of the State Council, the local people's governments at or above the county level in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas and their relevant departments shall give commendations or rewards to units and individuals that have made remarkable achievements in schistosomiasis prevention and control work.

### 第二章　预防

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　血吸虫病防治地区根据血吸虫病预防控制标准，划分为重点防治地区和一般防治地区。具体办法由国务院卫生主管部门会同国务院农业主管部门制定。

**Article 8** Schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall be divided into key prevention and control areas and general prevention and control areas in accordance with the schistosomiasis prevention and control standards. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent health department of the State Council jointly with the competent agricultural department of the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　血吸虫病防治地区县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当组织各类新闻媒体开展公益性血吸虫病防治宣传教育。各类新闻媒体应当开展公益性血吸虫病防治宣传教育。  
血吸虫病防治地区县级以上地方人民政府教育主管部门应当组织各级各类学校对学生开展血吸虫病防治知识教育。各级各类学校应当对学生开展血吸虫病防治知识教育。  
血吸虫病防治地区的机关、团体、企业事业单位、个体经济组织应当组织本单位人员学习血吸虫病防治知识。

**Article 9** The local people's governments at or above the county level in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas and their relevant departments shall organize various news media to carry out public welfare publicity and education on schistosomiasis prevention and control. All types of news media shall carry out public welfare publicity and education on schistosomiasis prevention and control.  
The competent education departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall organize schools of all levels and types to conduct education on schistosomiasis prevention and control knowledge among students. Schools of all levels and types shall conduct education on schistosomiasis prevention and control knowledge among students.  
State organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and individual economic organizations in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall organize their personnel to study schistosomiasis prevention and control knowledge.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　处于同一水系或者同一相对独立地理环境的血吸虫病防治地区各地方人民政府应当开展血吸虫病联防联控，组织有关部门和机构同步实施下列血吸虫病防治措施：  
(一)在农业、兽医、水利、林业等工程项目中采取与血吸虫病防治有关的工程措施；  
(二)进行人和家畜的血吸虫病筛查、治疗和管理；  
(三)开展流行病学调查和疫情监测；  
(四)调查钉螺分布，实施药物杀灭钉螺；  
(五)防止未经无害化处理的粪便直接进入水体；  
(六)其他防治措施。

**Article 10** The local people's governments in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas that are located in the same water system or the same relatively independent geographical environment shall carry out joint prevention and control of schistosomiasis, and organize relevant departments and institutions to simultaneously implement the following schistosomiasis prevention and control measures:  
(1) adopt engineering measures related to schistosomiasis prevention and control in agriculture, veterinary, water resources, forestry and other engineering projects;  
(2) carry out screening, treatment and management of schistosomiasis in humans and domestic animals;  
(3) conduct epidemiological investigations and epidemic surveillance;  
(4) investigate the distribution of Oncomelania snails and implement drug mollusciciding;  
(5) prevent untreated feces from directly entering water bodies;  
(6) other prevention and control measures.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　血吸虫病防治地区县级人民政府应当制定本行政区域的血吸虫病联防联控方案，组织乡(镇)人民政府同步实施。  
血吸虫病防治地区两个以上的县、不设区的市、市辖区或者两个以上设区的市需要同步实施血吸虫病防治措施的，其共同的上一级人民政府应当制定血吸虫病联防联控方案，并组织实施。  
血吸虫病防治地区两个以上的省、自治区、直辖市需要同步实施血吸虫病防治措施的，有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当共同制定血吸虫病联防联控方案，报国务院卫生、农业主管部门备案，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府组织实施。

**Article 11** The county people's governments in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall formulate schistosomiasis joint prevention and control plans for their respective administrative regions and organize the township (town) people's governments to implement them simultaneously.  
Where two or more counties, cities without districts, municipal districts, or two or more cities divided into districts in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas need to simultaneously implement schistosomiasis prevention and control measures, the common people's government at the next higher level shall formulate a schistosomiasis joint prevention and control plan and organize its implementation.  
Where two or more provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas need to simultaneously implement schistosomiasis prevention and control measures, the relevant people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall jointly formulate a schistosomiasis joint prevention and control plan, submit it to the competent health and agricultural departments of the State Council for record, and the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall organize its implementation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在血吸虫病防治地区实施农业、兽医、水利、林业等工程项目以及开展人、家畜血吸虫病防治工作，应当符合相关血吸虫病防治技术规范的要求。相关血吸虫病防治技术规范由国务院卫生、农业、水利、林业主管部门分别制定。

**Article 12** The implementation of agriculture, veterinary, water resources, forestry and other engineering projects and the carrying out of schistosomiasis prevention and control work for humans and domestic animals in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall comply with the requirements of the relevant technical specifications for schistosomiasis prevention and control. The relevant technical specifications for schistosomiasis prevention and control shall be formulated respectively by the competent health, agriculture, water resources, and forestry departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　血吸虫病重点防治地区县级以上地方人民政府应当在渔船集中停靠地设点发放抗血吸虫基本预防药物；按照无害化要求和血吸虫病防治技术规范修建公共厕所；推行在渔船和水上运输工具上安装和使用粪便收集容器，并采取措施，对所收集的粪便进行集中无害化处理。

**Article 13** The local people's governments at or above the county level in key schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall set up points at concentrated berthing places of fishing boats to distribute basic preventive anti-schistosome drugs; construct public toilets in accordance with the requirements of harmlessness and the technical specifications for schistosomiasis prevention and control; promote the installation and use of feces collection containers on fishing boats and water transport vehicles, and take measures to conduct centralized harmlessness treatment of the collected feces.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门在血吸虫病重点防治地区，应当安排并组织实施农业机械化推广、农村改厕、沼气池建设以及人、家畜饮用水设施建设等项目。  
国务院有关主管部门安排农业机械化推广、农村改厕、沼气池建设以及人、家畜饮用水设施建设等项目，应当优先安排血吸虫病重点防治地区的有关项目。

**Article 14** The local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments in key schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall arrange and organize the implementation of projects such as agricultural mechanization promotion, rural toilet renovation, biogas digester construction, and construction of drinking water facilities for humans and domestic animals.  
When arranging projects such as agricultural mechanization promotion, rural toilet renovation, biogas digester construction, and construction of drinking water facilities for humans and domestic animals, the relevant competent departments of the State Council shall give priority to the relevant projects in key schistosomiasis prevention and control areas.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　血吸虫病防治地区县级以上地方人民政府卫生、农业主管部门组织实施农村改厕、沼气池建设项目，应当按照无害化要求和血吸虫病防治技术规范，保证厕所和沼气池具备杀灭粪便中血吸虫卵的功能。  
血吸虫病防治地区的公共厕所应当具备杀灭粪便中血吸虫卵的功能。

**Article 15** When the competent health and agricultural departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas organize the implementation of rural toilet renovation and biogas digester construction projects, they shall, in accordance with the requirements of harmlessness and the technical specifications for schistosomiasis prevention and control, ensure that the toilets and biogas digesters have the function of killing schistosome eggs in feces.  
Public toilets in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall have the function of killing schistosome eggs in feces.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　县级以上人民政府农业主管部门在血吸虫病重点防治地区应当适应血吸虫病防治工作的需要，引导和扶持农业种植结构的调整，推行以机械化耕作代替牲畜耕作的措施。  
县级以上人民政府农业或者兽医主管部门在血吸虫病重点防治地区应当引导和扶持养殖结构的调整，推行对牛、羊、猪等家畜的舍饲圈养，加强对圈养家畜粪便的无害化处理，开展对家畜的血吸虫病检查和对感染血吸虫的家畜的治疗、处理。

**Article 16** The competent agricultural departments of the people's governments at or above the county level in key schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall, in order to meet the needs of schistosomiasis prevention and control work, guide and support the adjustment of agricultural planting structure, and promote measures to replace livestock farming with mechanized farming.  
The competent agricultural or veterinary departments of the people's governments at or above the county level in key schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall guide and support the adjustment of breeding structure, promote the pen-feeding and enclosure of cattle, sheep, pigs and other domestic animals, strengthen the harmlessness treatment of feces from pen-fed animals, and carry out schistosomiasis examination of domestic animals as well as treatment and disposal of domestic animals infected with schistosomiasis.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　禁止在血吸虫病防治地区施用未经无害化处理的粪便。

**Article 17** It is prohibited to apply untreated feces in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上人民政府水利主管部门在血吸虫病防治地区进行水利建设项目，应当同步建设血吸虫病防治设施；结合血吸虫病防治地区的江河、湖泊治理工程和人畜饮水、灌区改造等水利工程项目，改善水环境，防止钉螺孳生。

**Article 18** When carrying out water resources construction projects in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas, the competent water resources departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall simultaneously construct schistosomiasis prevention and control facilities; and in combination with water resources projects such as river and lake regulation projects, drinking water projects for humans and animals, and irrigation district renovation in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas, improve the water environment and prevent the breeding of Oncomelania snails.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上人民政府林业主管部门在血吸虫病防治地区应当结合退耕还林、长江防护林建设、野生动物植物保护、湿地保护以及自然保护区建设等林业工程，开展血吸虫病综合防治。  
县级以上人民政府交通主管部门在血吸虫病防治地区应当结合航道工程建设，开展血吸虫病综合防治。

**Article 19** The competent forestry departments of the people's governments at or above the county level in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall carry out comprehensive schistosomiasis prevention and control in conjunction with forestry projects such as converting farmland to forests, construction of the Yangtze River shelterbelt, wildlife and plant protection, wetland protection and nature reserve construction.  
The competent transportation departments of the people's governments at or above the county level in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall carry out comprehensive schistosomiasis prevention and control in conjunction with waterway engineering construction.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院卫生主管部门应当根据血吸虫病流行病学资料、钉螺分布以及孳生环境的特点、药物特性，制定药物杀灭钉螺工作规范。  
血吸虫病防治地区县级人民政府及其卫生主管部门应当根据药物杀灭钉螺工作规范，组织实施本行政区域内的药物杀灭钉螺工作。  
血吸虫病防治地区乡(镇)人民政府应当在实施药物杀灭钉螺7日前，公告施药的时间、地点、种类、方法、影响范围和注意事项。有关单位和个人应当予以配合。  
杀灭钉螺严禁使用国家明令禁止使用的药物。

**Article 20** The competent health department of the State Council shall, based on schistosomiasis epidemiological data, distribution and breeding environment characteristics of Oncomelania snails, and drug properties, formulate work specifications for drug mollusciciding.  
The county people's governments and their competent health departments in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall, in accordance with the work specifications for drug mollusciciding, organize and implement drug mollusciciding within their respective administrative regions.  
The township (town) people's governments in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall, 7 days before implementing drug mollusciciding, announce the time, location, type, method, affected scope and precautions of drug application. The relevant units and individuals shall cooperate.  
Drugs explicitly prohibited by the State shall not be used for mollusciciding.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　血吸虫病防治地区县级人民政府卫生主管部门会同同级人民政府农业或者兽医、水利、林业主管部门，根据血吸虫病监测等流行病学资料，划定、变更有钉螺地带，并报本级人民政府批准。县级人民政府应当及时公告有钉螺地带。  
禁止在有钉螺地带放养牛、羊、猪等家畜，禁止引种在有钉螺地带培育的芦苇等植物和农作物的种子、种苗等繁殖材料。  
乡(镇)人民政府应当在有钉螺地带设立警示标志，并在县级人民政府作出解除有钉螺地带决定后予以撤销。警示标志由乡(镇)人民政府负责保护，所在地村民委员会、居民委员会应当予以协助。任何单位或者个人不得损坏或者擅自移动警示标志。  
在有钉螺地带完成杀灭钉螺后，由原批准机关决定并公告解除本条第二款规定的禁止行为。

**Article 21** The competent health department of the county people's government in a schistosomiasis prevention and control area shall, jointly with the competent agricultural or veterinary, water resources, and forestry departments of the same level people's government, based on schistosomiasis surveillance and other epidemiological data, delimit or change areas with Oncomelania snails, and submit to the same level people's government for approval. The county people's government shall promptly announce the areas with snails.  
It is prohibited to graze cattle, sheep, pigs and other domestic animals in areas with snails, and it is prohibited to introduce reeds and other plants, and seeds, seedlings and other propagation materials of crops cultivated in areas with snails.  
The township (town) people's government shall set up warning signs in areas with snails, and shall remove them after the county people's government makes a decision to lift the designation of snail areas. The warning signs shall be protected by the township (town) people's government, and the local villagers' committee or residents' committee shall provide assistance. No unit or individual may damage or move the warning signs without authorization.  
After the completion of mollusciciding in areas with snails, the original approving authority shall decide and announce the lifting of the prohibitions provided for in paragraph 2 of this Article.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　医疗机构、疾病预防控制机构、动物防疫监督机构和植物检疫机构应当根据血吸虫病防治技术规范，在各自的职责范围内，开展血吸虫病的监测、筛查、预测、流行病学调查、疫情报告和处理工作，开展杀灭钉螺、血吸虫病防治技术指导以及其他防治工作。  
血吸虫病防治地区的医疗机构、疾病预防控制机构、动物防疫监督机构和植物检疫机构应当定期对其工作人员进行血吸虫病防治知识、技能的培训和考核。

**Article 22** Medical institutions, disease prevention and control institutions, animal epidemic prevention supervision institutions, and plant quarantine institutions shall, in accordance with the technical specifications for schistosomiasis prevention and control and within their respective functions and duties, carry out schistosomiasis surveillance, screening, prediction, epidemiological investigation, epidemic reporting and handling, and carry out mollusciciding, technical guidance for schistosomiasis prevention and control, and other prevention and control work.  
Medical institutions, disease prevention and control institutions, animal epidemic prevention supervision institutions, and plant quarantine institutions in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall regularly conduct training and assessment of their staff on schistosomiasis prevention and control knowledge and skills.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　建设单位在血吸虫病防治地区兴建水利、交通、旅游、能源等大型建设项目，应当事先提请省级以上疾病预防控制机构对施工环境进行卫生调查，并根据疾病预防控制机构的意见，采取必要的血吸虫病预防、控制措施。施工期间，建设单位应当设专人负责工地上的血吸虫病防治工作；工程竣工后，应当告知当地县级疾病预防控制机构，由其对该地区的血吸虫病进行监测。

**Article 23** Where a construction unit constructs large-scale construction projects such as water resources, transportation, tourism, and energy in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas, it shall, in advance, request a disease prevention and control institution at or above the provincial level to conduct a sanitary survey of the construction environment, and take necessary schistosomiasis prevention and control measures in accordance with the opinions of the disease prevention and control institution. During the construction period, the construction unit shall assign special personnel to be responsible for schistosomiasis prevention and control work at the construction site; after the completion of the project, it shall inform the local county-level disease prevention and control institution, which shall monitor schistosomiasis in the area.

### 第三章　疫情控制

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　血吸虫病防治地区县级以上地方人民政府应当根据有关法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，结合本地实际，制定血吸虫病应急预案。

**Article 24** The local people's governments at or above the county level in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall formulate schistosomiasis emergency plans in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, and relevant provisions of the State, taking into account local conditions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　急性血吸虫病暴发、流行时，县级以上地方人民政府应当根据控制急性血吸虫病暴发、流行的需要，依照传染病防治法和其他有关法律的规定采取紧急措施，进行下列应急处理：  
(一)组织医疗机构救治急性血吸虫病病人；  
(二)组织疾病预防控制机构和动物防疫监督机构分别对接触疫水的人和家畜实施预防性服药；  
(三)组织有关部门和单位杀灭钉螺和处理疫水；  
(四)组织乡(镇)人民政府在有钉螺地带设置警示标志，禁止人和家畜接触疫水。

**Article 25** When acute schistosomiasis outbreaks or epidemics occur, the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the need to control the outbreak or epidemic of acute schistosomiasis, take emergency measures in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases and other relevant laws, and carry out the following emergency response:  
(1) organize medical institutions to treat patients with acute schistosomiasis;  
(2) organize disease prevention and control institutions and animal epidemic prevention supervision institutions to administer prophylactic medication respectively to persons and domestic animals that have been exposed to infested water;  
(3) organize relevant departments and units to kill snails and treat infested water;  
(4) organize township (town) people's governments to set up warning signs in areas with snails, prohibiting humans and domestic animals from contacting infested water.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　疾病预防控制机构发现急性血吸虫病疫情或者接到急性血吸虫病暴发、流行报告时，应当及时采取下列措施：  
(一)进行现场流行病学调查；  
(二)提出疫情控制方案，明确有钉螺地带范围、预防性服药的人和家畜范围，以及采取杀灭钉螺和处理疫水的措施；  
(三)指导医疗机构和下级疾病预防控制机构处理疫情；  
(四)卫生主管部门要求采取的其他措施。

**Article 26** When a disease prevention and control institution discovers an acute schistosomiasis epidemic or receives a report of an acute schistosomiasis outbreak or epidemic, it shall promptly take the following measures:  
(1) conduct on-site epidemiological investigation;  
(2) propose an epidemic control plan, specifying the scope of areas with snails, the scope of persons and domestic animals to receive prophylactic medication, and the measures to be taken for snail killing and treatment of infested water;  
(3) guide medical institutions and lower-level disease prevention and control institutions in handling the epidemic;  
(4) other measures required by the competent health department.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　有关单位对因生产、工作必须接触疫水的人员应当按照疾病预防控制机构的要求采取防护措施，并定期组织进行血吸虫病的专项体检。  
血吸虫病防治地区地方各级人民政府及其有关部门对因防汛、抗洪抢险必须接触疫水的人员，应当按照疾病预防控制机构的要求采取防护措施。血吸虫病防治地区县级人民政府对参加防汛、抗洪抢险的人员，应当及时组织有关部门和机构进行血吸虫病的专项体检。

**Article 27** Relevant units shall, in accordance with the requirements of the disease prevention and control institution, take protective measures for personnel who must come into contact with infested water due to production or work, and regularly organize special medical examinations for schistosomiasis.  
The local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall, in accordance with the requirements of the disease prevention and control institution, take protective measures for personnel who must come into contact with infested water due to flood control or flood fighting and emergency rescue. The county people's governments in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall promptly organize relevant departments and institutions to conduct special medical examinations for schistosomiasis for personnel participating in flood control or flood fighting and emergency rescue.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　血吸虫病防治地区县级以上地方人民政府卫生、农业或者兽医主管部门应当根据血吸虫病防治技术规范，组织开展对本地村民、居民和流动人口血吸虫病以及家畜血吸虫病的筛查、治疗和预防性服药工作。  
血吸虫病防治地区省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当采取措施，组织对晚期血吸虫病病人的治疗。

**Article 28** The competent health, agriculture, or veterinary departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall, in accordance with the technical specifications for schistosomiasis prevention and control, organize and carry out screening, treatment and prophylactic medication for schistosomiasis among local villagers, residents, and mobile population, as well as for domestic animals.  
The people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall take measures to organize the treatment of patients with advanced schistosomiasis.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　血吸虫病防治地区的动物防疫监督机构、植物检疫机构应当加强对本行政区域内的家畜和植物的血吸虫病检疫工作。动物防疫监督机构对经检疫发现的患血吸虫病的家畜，应当实施药物治疗；植物检疫机构对发现的携带钉螺的植物，应当实施杀灭钉螺。  
凡患血吸虫病的家畜、携带钉螺的植物，在血吸虫病防治地区未经检疫的家畜、植物，一律不得出售、外运。

**Article 29** The animal epidemic prevention supervision institutions and plant quarantine institutions in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall strengthen the quarantine work for schistosomiasis in domestic animals and plants within their respective administrative regions. The animal epidemic prevention supervision institution shall administer drug treatment to domestic animals found to be infected with schistosomiasis through quarantine; the plant quarantine institution shall implement snail killing for plants found to carry Oncomelania snails.  
Domestic animals infected with schistosomiasis, plants carrying snails, and domestic animals or plants that have not been quarantined in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall not be sold or transported out.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　血吸虫病疫情的报告、通报和公布，依照传染病防治法和动物防疫法的有关规定执行。

**Article 30** The reporting, notification, and publication of schistosomiasis epidemic information shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law on the Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases and the Animal Epidemic Prevention Law.

### 第四章　保障措施

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　血吸虫病防治地区县级以上地方人民政府应当根据血吸虫病防治规划、计划，安排血吸虫病防治经费和基本建设投资，纳入同级财政预算。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和设区的市级人民政府根据血吸虫病防治工作需要，对经济困难的县级人民政府开展血吸虫病防治工作给予适当补助。  
国家对经济困难地区的血吸虫病防治经费、血吸虫病重大疫情应急处理经费给予适当补助，对承担血吸虫病防治任务的机构的基本建设和跨地区的血吸虫病防治重大工程项目给予必要支持。

**Article 31** The local people's governments at or above the county level in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall, in accordance with the schistosomiasis prevention and control plans, allocate funds for schistosomiasis prevention and control and capital construction investment, and include them in the fiscal budgets at the same level.  
The people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government and the people's governments of cities divided into districts shall, based on the needs of schistosomiasis prevention and control work, provide appropriate subsidies to economically disadvantaged county people's governments for carrying out schistosomiasis prevention and control work.  
The State shall provide appropriate subsidies for schistosomiasis prevention and control funds and emergency response funds for major schistosomiasis epidemics in economically disadvantaged areas, and provide necessary support for the capital construction of institutions undertaking schistosomiasis prevention and control tasks and for major cross-regional schistosomiasis prevention and control projects.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　血吸虫病防治地区县级以上地方人民政府编制或者审批血吸虫病防治地区的农业、兽医、水利、林业等工程项目，应当将有关血吸虫病防治的工程措施纳入项目统筹安排。

**Article 32** When the local people's governments at or above the county level in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas prepare or approve agriculture, veterinary, water resources, forestry and other engineering projects in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas, they shall incorporate the relevant engineering measures for schistosomiasis prevention and control into the overall arrangement of the projects.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家对农民免费提供抗血吸虫基本预防药物，对经济困难农民的血吸虫病治疗费用予以减免。  
因工作原因感染血吸虫病的，依照《工伤保险条例》的规定，享受工伤待遇。参加城镇职工基本医疗保险的血吸虫病病人，不属于工伤的，按照国家规定享受医疗保险待遇。对未参加工伤保险、医疗保险的人员因防汛、抗洪抢险患血吸虫病的，按照县级以上地方人民政府的规定解决所需的检查、治疗费用。

**Article 33** The State shall provide basic preventive anti-schistosome drugs to farmers free of charge, and reduce or exempt the treatment costs for schistosomiasis for economically disadvantaged farmers.  
Persons who contract schistosomiasis due to work shall enjoy work-related injury benefits in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Work-Related Injury Insurance. Patients with schistosomiasis who participate in the basic medical insurance for urban workers and whose condition is not a work-related injury shall enjoy medical insurance benefits in accordance with state regulations. For persons who have not participated in work-related injury insurance or medical insurance and contract schistosomiasis due to flood control or flood fighting and emergency rescue, the required examination and treatment costs shall be resolved in accordance with the provisions of the local people's governments at or above the county level.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　血吸虫病防治地区县级以上地方人民政府民政、医疗保障部门对符合救助条件的血吸虫病病人进行救助。

**Article 34** The civil affairs and medical security departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall provide assistance to schistosomiasis patients who meet the conditions for assistance.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家对家畜免费实施血吸虫病检查和治疗，免费提供抗血吸虫基本预防药物。

**Article 35** The State shall provide free schistosomiasis examination and treatment for domestic animals, and provide basic preventive anti-schistosome drugs free of charge.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　血吸虫病防治地区县级以上地方人民政府应当根据血吸虫病防治工作需要和血吸虫病流行趋势，储备血吸虫病防治药物、杀灭钉螺药物和有关防护用品。

**Article 36** The local people's governments at or above the county level in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall, in accordance with the needs of schistosomiasis prevention and control work and the epidemiological trend of schistosomiasis, stockpile schistosomiasis prevention and control drugs, molluscicides, and relevant protective supplies.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　血吸虫病防治地区县级以上地方人民政府应当加强血吸虫病防治网络建设，将承担血吸虫病防治任务的机构所需基本建设投资列入基本建设计划。

**Article 37** The local people's governments at or above the county level in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall strengthen the construction of the schistosomiasis prevention and control network, and include the capital construction investment required by institutions undertaking schistosomiasis prevention and control tasks in the capital construction plan.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　血吸虫病防治地区省、自治区、直辖市人民政府在制定和实施本行政区域的血吸虫病防治计划时，应当统筹协调血吸虫病防治项目和资金，确保实现血吸虫病防治项目的综合效益。  
血吸虫病防治经费应当专款专用，严禁截留或者挪作他用。严禁倒买倒卖、挪用国家免费供应的防治血吸虫病药品和其他物品。有关单位使用血吸虫病防治经费应当依法接受审计机关的审计监督。

**Article 38** When formulating and implementing schistosomiasis prevention and control plans for their respective administrative regions, the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas shall make overall coordination of schistosomiasis prevention and control projects and funds to ensure the realization of the comprehensive benefits of the schistosomiasis prevention and control projects.  
Schistosomiasis prevention and control funds shall be used for specific purposes only, and shall not be withheld or diverted to other uses. It is strictly prohibited to resell or misuse the drugs and other articles for schistosomiasis prevention and control supplied free of charge by the State. Relevant units using schistosomiasis prevention and control funds shall, in accordance with the law, accept the audit supervision of the audit institutions.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门负责血吸虫病监测、预防、控制、治疗和疫情的管理工作，对杀灭钉螺药物的使用情况进行监督检查。

**Article 39** The competent health departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the management of schistosomiasis surveillance, prevention, control, treatment, and epidemic situation, and shall supervise and inspect the use of molluscicides.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　县级以上人民政府农业或者兽医主管部门对下列事项进行监督检查：  
(一)[本条例第十六条](#art-16)规定的血吸虫病防治措施的实施情况；  
(二)家畜血吸虫病监测、预防、控制、治疗和疫情管理工作情况；  
(三)治疗家畜血吸虫病药物的管理、使用情况；  
(四)农业工程项目中执行血吸虫病防治技术规范情况。

**Article 40** The competent agricultural or veterinary departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall supervise and inspect the following matters:  
(1) the implementation of the schistosomiasis prevention and control measures provided for in Article 16 of these Regulations;  
(2) the situation of schistosomiasis surveillance, prevention, control, treatment, and epidemic management in domestic animals;  
(3) the management and use of drugs for treating schistosomiasis in domestic animals;  
(4) the implementation of technical specifications for schistosomiasis prevention and control in agricultural engineering projects.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　县级以上人民政府水利主管部门对[本条例第十八条](#art-18)规定的血吸虫病防治措施的实施情况和水利工程项目中执行血吸虫病防治技术规范情况进行监督检查。

**Article 41** The competent water resources departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall supervise and inspect the implementation of the schistosomiasis prevention and control measures provided for in Article 18 of these Regulations and the implementation of the technical specifications for schistosomiasis prevention and control in water resources engineering projects.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　县级以上人民政府林业主管部门对血吸虫病防治地区的林业工程项目的实施情况和林业工程项目中执行血吸虫病防治技术规范情况进行监督检查。

**Article 42** The competent forestry departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall supervise and inspect the implementation of forestry engineering projects in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas and the implementation of the technical specifications for schistosomiasis prevention and control in forestry engineering projects.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　县级以上人民政府卫生、农业或者兽医、水利、林业主管部门在监督检查过程中，发现违反或者不执行本条例规定的，应当责令有关单位和个人及时改正并依法予以处理；属于其他部门职责范围的，应当移送有监督管理职责的部门依法处理；涉及多个部门职责的，应当共同处理。

**Article 43** Where, during the process of supervision and inspection, the competent health, agriculture or veterinary, water resources, and forestry departments of the people's governments at or above the county level discover any violation of or non-compliance with the provisions of these Regulations, they shall order the relevant units and individuals to make corrections promptly and handle the matter in accordance with the law; if the matter falls within the scope of functions and duties of another department, they shall transfer it to the department with supervision and administration functions for handling in accordance with the law; if the matter involves the functions and duties of multiple departments, they shall handle it jointly.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　县级以上人民政府卫生、农业或者兽医、水利、林业主管部门在履行血吸虫病防治监督检查职责时，有权进入被检查单位和血吸虫病疫情发生现场调查取证，查阅、复制有关资料和采集样本。被检查单位应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻挠。

**Article 44** When performing their duties of supervision and inspection for schistosomiasis prevention and control, the competent health, agriculture or veterinary, water resources, and forestry departments of the people's governments at or above the county level have the right to enter the unit under inspection and the scene of the schistosomiasis epidemic to investigate and collect evidence, examine and copy relevant materials, and collect samples. The unit under inspection shall cooperate and shall not refuse or obstruct.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　血吸虫病防治地区县级以上动物防疫监督机构对在有钉螺地带放养的牛、羊、猪等家畜，有权予以暂扣并进行强制检疫。

**Article 45** The animal epidemic prevention supervision institutions at or above the county level in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas have the right to temporarily impound cattle, sheep, pigs and other domestic animals grazed in areas with snails and conduct compulsory quarantine.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　上级主管部门发现下级主管部门未及时依照本条例的规定处理职责范围内的事项，应当责令纠正，或者直接处理下级主管部门未及时处理的事项。

**Article 46** Where a superior competent department discovers that a subordinate competent department has failed to handle matters within its functions and duties in a timely manner in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, it shall order the subordinate to make corrections, or directly handle the matters that the subordinate competent department has failed to handle in a timely manner.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上地方各级人民政府有下列情形之一的，由上级人民政府责令改正，通报批评；造成血吸虫病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，对负有责任的主管人员，依法给予行政处分；负有责任的主管人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未依照本条例的规定开展血吸虫病联防联控的；  
(二)急性血吸虫病暴发、流行时，未依照本条例的规定采取紧急措施、进行应急处理的；  
(三)未履行血吸虫病防治组织、领导、保障职责的；  
(四)未依照本条例的规定采取其他血吸虫病防治措施的。  
乡(镇)人民政府未依照本条例的规定采取血吸虫病防治措施的，由上级人民政府责令改正，通报批评；造成血吸虫病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，对负有责任的主管人员，依法给予行政处分；负有责任的主管人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 47** Where a local people's government at or above the county level falls under any of the following circumstances, the people's government at the next higher level shall order it to make corrections and circulate a notice of criticism; if the circumstances cause the spread or epidemic of schistosomiasis or other serious consequences, the competent person in charge shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if the competent person in charge commits a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to carry out joint prevention and control of schistosomiasis in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) when acute schistosomiasis outbreaks or epidemics occur, failing to take emergency measures or carry out emergency response in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(3) failing to perform the duties of organizing, leading, and guaranteeing schistosomiasis prevention and control;  
(4) failing to take other schistosomiasis prevention and control measures in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
Where a township (town) people's government fails to take schistosomiasis prevention and control measures in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the people's government at the next higher level shall order it to make corrections and circulate a notice of criticism; if the circumstances cause the spread or epidemic of schistosomiasis or other serious consequences, the competent person in charge shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if the competent person in charge commits a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上人民政府有关主管部门违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府有关主管部门责令改正，通报批评；造成血吸虫病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)在组织实施农村改厕、沼气池建设项目时，未按照无害化要求和血吸虫病防治技术规范，保证厕所或者沼气池具备杀灭粪便中血吸虫卵功能的；  
(二)在血吸虫病重点防治地区未开展家畜血吸虫病检查，或者未对感染血吸虫的家畜进行治疗、处理的；  
(三)在血吸虫病防治地区进行水利建设项目，未同步建设血吸虫病防治设施，或者未结合血吸虫病防治地区的江河、湖泊治理工程和人畜饮水、灌区改造等水利工程项目，改善水环境，导致钉螺孳生的；  
(四)在血吸虫病防治地区未结合退耕还林、长江防护林建设、野生动物植物保护、湿地保护以及自然保护区建设等林业工程，开展血吸虫病综合防治的；  
(五)未制定药物杀灭钉螺规范，或者未组织实施本行政区域内药物杀灭钉螺工作的；  
(六)未组织开展血吸虫病筛查、治疗和预防性服药工作的；  
(七)未依照本条例规定履行监督管理职责，或者发现违法行为不及时查处的；  
(八)有违反本条例规定的其他失职、渎职行为的。

**Article 48** Where a relevant competent department of a people's government at or above the county level violates the provisions of these Regulations and falls under any of the following circumstances, the people's government at the same level or the relevant competent department of the people's government at the next higher level shall order it to make corrections and circulate a notice of criticism; if the circumstances cause the spread or epidemic of schistosomiasis or other serious consequences, the competent person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if the competent person in charge and other directly responsible persons commit a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) when organizing the implementation of rural toilet renovation or biogas digester construction projects, failing to ensure that the toilets or biogas digesters have the function of killing schistosome eggs in feces in accordance with the requirements of harmlessness and the technical specifications for schistosomiasis prevention and control;  
(2) in key schistosomiasis prevention and control areas, failing to carry out schistosomiasis examination of domestic animals, or failing to treat or dispose of domestic animals infected with schistosomiasis;  
(3) when carrying out water resources construction projects in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas, failing to simultaneously construct schistosomiasis prevention and control facilities, or failing to improve the water environment in conjunction with river and lake regulation projects, drinking water projects for humans and animals, irrigation district renovation and other water resources projects in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas, resulting in the breeding of Oncomelania snails;  
(4) in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas, failing to carry out comprehensive schistosomiasis prevention and control in conjunction with forestry projects such as converting farmland to forests, construction of the Yangtze River shelterbelt, wildlife and plant protection, wetland protection, and nature reserve construction;  
(5) failing to formulate specifications for drug mollusciciding, or failing to organize and implement drug mollusciciding within the administrative region;  
(6) failing to organize and carry out screening, treatment and prophylactic medication for schistosomiasis;  
(7) failing to perform supervision and administration duties in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, or failing to promptly investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered;  
(8) other acts of dereliction of duty or malfeasance in violation of the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　医疗机构、疾病预防控制机构、动物防疫监督机构或者植物检疫机构违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门、农业或者兽医主管部门依据各自职责责令限期改正，通报批评，给予警告；逾期不改正，造成血吸虫病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予降级、撤职、开除的处分，并可以依法吊销有关责任人员的执业证书；负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未依照本条例规定开展血吸虫病防治工作的；  
(二)未定期对其工作人员进行血吸虫病防治知识、技能培训和考核的；  
(三)发现急性血吸虫病疫情或者接到急性血吸虫病暴发、流行报告时，未及时采取措施的；  
(四)未对本行政区域内出售、外运的家畜或者植物进行血吸虫病检疫的；  
(五)未对经检疫发现的患血吸虫病的家畜实施药物治疗，或者未对发现的携带钉螺的植物实施杀灭钉螺的。

**Article 49** Where a medical institution, disease prevention and control institution, animal epidemic prevention supervision institution, or plant quarantine institution violates the provisions of these Regulations and falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent health department or the competent agriculture or veterinary department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, order it to make corrections within a time limit, circulate a notice of criticism, and give a warning; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit and causes the spread or epidemic of schistosomiasis or other serious consequences, the competent person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions such as demotion, removal from office, or expulsion according to law, and the practice certificates of the relevant responsible persons may be revoked according to law; if the competent person in charge and other directly responsible persons commit a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to carry out schistosomiasis prevention and control work in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) failing to regularly conduct training and assessment of their staff on schistosomiasis prevention and control knowledge and skills;  
(3) upon discovering an acute schistosomiasis epidemic or receiving a report of an acute schistosomiasis outbreak or epidemic, failing to take timely measures;  
(4) failing to quarantine domestic animals or plants sold or transported out within the administrative region for schistosomiasis;  
(5) failing to administer drug treatment to domestic animals found to be infected with schistosomiasis through quarantine, or failing to kill snails on plants found to carry Oncomelania snails.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　建设单位在血吸虫病防治地区兴建水利、交通、旅游、能源等大型建设项目，未事先提请省级以上疾病预防控制机构进行卫生调查，或者未根据疾病预防控制机构的意见，采取必要的血吸虫病预防、控制措施的，由县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告，处5000元以上3万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，处3万元以上10万元以下的罚款，并可以提请有关人民政府依据职责权限，责令停建、关闭；造成血吸虫病疫情扩散或者其他严重后果的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 50** Where a construction unit constructing large-scale construction projects such as water resources, transportation, tourism, and energy in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas fails to request in advance a disease prevention and control institution at or above the provincial level to conduct a sanitary survey, or fails to take necessary schistosomiasis prevention and control measures in accordance with the opinions of the disease prevention and control institution, the competent health department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit, give it a warning, and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the matter may be submitted to the relevant people's government to order suspension of construction or closure in accordance with its functions and powers; if the circumstances cause the spread of the schistosomiasis epidemic or other serious consequences, the competent person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　单位和个人损坏或者擅自移动有钉螺地带警示标志的，由乡(镇)人民政府责令修复或者赔偿损失，给予警告；情节严重的，对单位处1000元以上3000元以下的罚款，对个人处50元以上200元以下的罚款。

**Article 51** Any entity or individual that damages or without authorization removes warning signs in snail-infested areas shall be ordered by the people's government of the township (town) to make restoration or compensate for the losses, and shall be given a warning; if the circumstances are serious, the entity shall be fined not less than RMB 1,000 but not more than RMB 3,000, and the individual shall be fined not less than RMB 50 but not more than RMB 200.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府卫生、农业或者兽医、水利、林业主管部门依据各自职责责令改正，给予警告，对单位处1000元以上1万元以下的罚款，对个人处50元以上500元以下的罚款，并没收用于违法活动的工具和物品；造成血吸虫病疫情扩散或者其他严重后果的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
(一)单位未依照本条例的规定对因生产、工作必须接触疫水的人员采取防护措施，或者未定期组织进行血吸虫病的专项体检的；  
(二)对政府有关部门采取的预防、控制措施不予配合的；  
(三)使用国家明令禁止使用的药物杀灭钉螺的；  
(四)引种在有钉螺地带培育的芦苇等植物或者农作物的种子、种苗等繁殖材料的；  
(五)在血吸虫病防治地区施用未经无害化处理粪便的。

**Article 52** Anyone who, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the competent departments of health, agriculture or veterinary, water resources, or forestry of the people's government at or above the county level to make corrections according to their respective duties, given a warning, and fined not less than RMB 1,000 but not more than RMB 10,000 for the entity, or not less than RMB 50 but not more than RMB 500 for the individual, and the tools and articles used for the illegal activities shall be confiscated; if the spread of schistosomiasis epidemic or other serious consequences are caused, the persons in charge and other directly responsible persons who are liable shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) An entity fails to take protection measures for persons who must contact infected water due to production or work, or fails to organize regular special physical examinations for schistosomiasis, in accordance with these Regulations;  
(2) Failing to cooperate with the prevention and control measures taken by the relevant government departments;  
(3) Using drugs prohibited by the state to kill snails;  
(4) Introducing seeds, seedlings or other propagation materials of reeds or other plants or crops cultivated in snail-infested areas;  
(5) Applying untreated manure in schistosomiasis prevention and control areas.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
血吸虫病，是血吸虫寄生于人体或者哺乳动物体内，导致其发病的一种寄生虫病。  
疫水，是指含有血吸虫尾蚴的水体。

**Article 53** For the purpose of these Regulations, the following terms have the meanings as defined below:  
Schistosomiasis means a parasitic disease caused by schistosomes parasitizing the human body or mammals and causing illness.  
Infected water means water containing schistosome cercariae.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　本条例自2006年5月1日起施行。

**Article 54** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2006.
