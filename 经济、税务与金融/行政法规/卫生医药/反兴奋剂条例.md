# 反兴奋剂条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2018-09-18  
**生效日期**：2004-03-01

> (2004年1月13日中华人民共和国国务院令第398号公布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2014年7月29日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　根据2018年9月18日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了防止在体育运动中使用兴奋剂，保护体育运动参加者的身心健康，维护体育竞赛的公平竞争，根据《中华人民共和国体育法》和其他有关法律，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称兴奋剂，是指兴奋剂目录所列的禁用物质等。兴奋剂目录由国务院体育主管部门会同国务院药品监督管理部门、国务院卫生主管部门、国务院商务主管部门和海关总署制定、调整并公布。 第三条　国家提倡健康、文明的体育运动，加强反兴奋剂的宣传、教育和监督管理，坚持严格禁止、严格检查、严肃处理的反兴奋剂工作方针，禁止使用兴奋剂。 任何单位和个人不得向体育运动参加者提供或者变相提供兴奋剂。 第四条　国务院体育主管部门负责并组织全国的反兴奋剂工作。 县级以上人民政府负责药品监督管理的部门和卫生、教育等有关部门，在各自职责范围内依照本条例和有关法律、行政法规的规定负责反兴奋剂工作。 第五条　县级以上人民政府体育主管部门，应当加强反兴奋剂宣传、教育工作，提高体育运动参加者和公众的反兴奋剂意识。 广播电台、电视台、报刊媒体以及互联网信息服务提供者应当开展反兴奋剂的宣传。 第六条　任何单位和个人发现违反本条例规定行为的，有权向体育主管部门和其他有关部门举报。 第二章　兴奋剂管理 第七条　国家对兴奋剂目录所列禁用物质实行严格管理，任何单位和个人不得非法生产、销售、进出口。 第八条　生产兴奋剂目录所列蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素(以下简称蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素)，应当依照《中华人民共和国药品管理法》(以下简称药品管理法)的规定取得《药品生产许可证》、药品批准文号。 生产企业应当记录蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素的生产、销售和库存情况，并保存记录至超过蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素有效期2年。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了防止在体育运动中使用兴奋剂，保护体育运动参加者的身心健康，维护体育竞赛的公平竞争，根据《中华人民共和国体育法》和其他有关法律，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Sports Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws, for the purposes of preventing the use of doping in sports, protecting the physical and mental health of sports participants, and maintaining fair competition in sports contests.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称兴奋剂，是指兴奋剂目录所列的禁用物质等。兴奋剂目录由国务院体育主管部门会同国务院药品监督管理部门、国务院卫生主管部门、国务院商务主管部门和海关总署制定、调整并公布。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "doping" means the prohibited substances listed in the List of Doping Substances. The List of Doping Substances shall be formulated, adjusted and published by the competent sports department of the State Council jointly with the drug regulatory department of the State Council, the health department of the State Council, the commerce department of the State Council and the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家提倡健康、文明的体育运动，加强反兴奋剂的宣传、教育和监督管理，坚持严格禁止、严格检查、严肃处理的反兴奋剂工作方针，禁止使用兴奋剂。  
任何单位和个人不得向体育运动参加者提供或者变相提供兴奋剂。

**Article 3** The State advocates healthy and civilized sports, strengthens anti-doping publicity, education and supervision and management, adheres to the working policy of strict prohibition, strict inspection and strict handling of anti-doping, and prohibits the use of doping.  
No unit or individual may provide or provide in disguised form doping to sports participants.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院体育主管部门负责并组织全国的反兴奋剂工作。  
县级以上人民政府负责药品监督管理的部门和卫生、教育等有关部门，在各自职责范围内依照本条例和有关法律、行政法规的规定负责反兴奋剂工作。

**Article 4** The competent sports department of the State Council shall be responsible for and organize the national anti-doping work.  
The departments of drug regulation and the departments of health, education and other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for anti-doping work within their respective functions and duties in accordance with these Regulations and the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府体育主管部门，应当加强反兴奋剂宣传、教育工作，提高体育运动参加者和公众的反兴奋剂意识。  
广播电台、电视台、报刊媒体以及互联网信息服务提供者应当开展反兴奋剂的宣传。

**Article 5** The competent sports departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen anti-doping publicity and education, and enhance the anti-doping awareness of sports participants and the public.  
Radio stations, television stations, press media and Internet information service providers shall conduct anti-doping publicity.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何单位和个人发现违反本条例规定行为的，有权向体育主管部门和其他有关部门举报。

**Article 6** Any unit or individual that discovers any act violating the provisions of these Regulations shall have the right to report it to the competent sports department or other relevant departments.

### 第二章　兴奋剂管理

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对兴奋剂目录所列禁用物质实行严格管理，任何单位和个人不得非法生产、销售、进出口。

**Article 7** The State shall exercise strict control over the prohibited substances listed in the List of Doping Substances, and no unit or individual may illegally produce, sell, import or export them.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　生产兴奋剂目录所列蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素(以下简称蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素)，应当依照《中华人民共和国药品管理法》(以下简称药品管理法)的规定取得《药品生产许可证》、药品批准文号。  
生产企业应当记录蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素的生产、销售和库存情况，并保存记录至超过蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素有效期2年。

**Article 8** The production of anabolic agents and peptide hormones (hereinafter referred to as anabolic agents and peptide hormones) listed in the List of Doping Substances shall be subject to obtaining the Drug Manufacturing Certificate and the drug approval number in accordance with the Drug Administration Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Drug Administration Law).  
A manufacturing enterprise shall record the production, sale and inventory status of anabolic agents and peptide hormones, and shall keep the records for at least 2 years beyond the validity period of such anabolic agents and peptide hormones.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　依照药品管理法的规定取得《药品经营许可证》的药品批发企业，具备下列条件，并经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准，方可经营蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素：  
(一)有专门的管理人员；  
(二)有专储仓库或者专储药柜；  
(三)有专门的验收、检查、保管、销售和出入库登记制度；  
(四)法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。  
蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素的验收、检查、保管、销售和出入库登记记录应当保存至超过蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素有效期2年。

**Article 9** A drug wholesale enterprise that has obtained the Drug Distribution Permit in accordance with the Drug Administration Law may operate anabolic agents and peptide hormones only if it meets the following conditions and is approved by the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government:  
(1) having specialized management personnel;  
(2) having a dedicated warehouse or dedicated drug cabinet;  
(3) having a dedicated system for acceptance, inspection, storage, sale, and inbound and outbound registration;  
(4) other conditions as provided by laws and administrative regulations.  
Records of acceptance, inspection, storage, sale, and inbound and outbound registration of anabolic agents and peptide hormones shall be kept for at least 2 years beyond the validity period of such anabolic agents and peptide hormones.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　除胰岛素外，药品零售企业不得经营蛋白同化制剂或者其他肽类激素。

**Article 10** Except for insulin, drug retail enterprises may not operate anabolic agents or other peptide hormones.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　进口蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素，除依照药品管理法及其实施条例的规定取得国务院药品监督管理部门发给的进口药品注册证书外，还应当取得省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门颁发的进口准许证。  
申请进口蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素，应当说明其用途。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门应当自收到申请之日起15个工作日内作出决定；对用途合法的，应当予以批准，发给进口准许证。海关凭进口准许证放行。

**Article 11** For importing anabolic agents and peptide hormones, in addition to obtaining the Import Drug Registration Certificate issued by the drug regulatory department of the State Council in accordance with the Drug Administration Law and its implementing regulations, an import permit issued by the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall also be obtained.  
When applying for import of anabolic agents and peptide hormones, the purpose of use shall be stated. The drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall make a decision within 15 working days from the date of receipt of the application; if the purpose of use is lawful, it shall approve the application and issue the import permit. Customs shall release the goods upon presentation of the import permit.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　申请出口蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素，应当说明供应对象并提交进口国政府主管部门的相关证明文件等资料。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门应当自收到申请之日起15个工作日内作出决定；提交进口国政府主管部门的相关证明文件等资料的，应当予以批准，发给出口准许证。海关凭出口准许证放行。

**Article 12** When applying for export of anabolic agents and peptide hormones, the recipient shall be stated and relevant certifying documents from the competent authority of the importing country shall be submitted. The drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall make a decision within 15 working days from the date of receipt of the application; if the relevant certifying documents from the competent authority of the importing country are submitted, it shall approve the application and issue the export permit. Customs shall release the goods upon presentation of the export permit.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　境内企业接受境外企业委托生产蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素，应当签订书面委托生产合同，并将委托生产合同报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门备案。委托生产合同应当载明委托企业的国籍、委托生产的蛋白同化制剂或者肽类激素的品种、数量、生产日期等内容。  
境内企业接受境外企业委托生产的蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素不得在境内销售。

**Article 13** A domestic enterprise that accepts a commission from an overseas enterprise to produce anabolic agents and peptide hormones shall conclude a written commission production contract and shall file the commission production contract with the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for record. The commission production contract shall specify the nationality of the commissioning enterprise, the variety, quantity, date of production and other contents of the anabolic agents or peptide hormones to be produced under commission.  
Anabolic agents and peptide hormones produced by a domestic enterprise under commission from an overseas enterprise may not be sold within the territory.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素的生产企业只能向医疗机构、符合[本条例第九条](#art-9)规定的药品批发企业和其他同类生产企业供应蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素。  
蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素的批发企业只能向医疗机构、蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素的生产企业和其他同类批发企业供应蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素。  
蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素的进口单位只能向蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素的生产企业、医疗机构和符合[本条例第九条](#art-9)规定的药品批发企业供应蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素。  
肽类激素中的胰岛素除依照本条第一款、第二款、第三款的规定供应外，还可以向药品零售企业供应。

**Article 14** Manufacturers of anabolic agents and peptide hormones may only supply anabolic agents and peptide hormones to medical institutions, drug wholesale enterprises that meet the requirements of Article 9 of these Regulations, and other similar manufacturers.  
Wholesale enterprises of anabolic agents and peptide hormones may only supply anabolic agents and peptide hormones to medical institutions, manufacturers of anabolic agents and peptide hormones, and other similar wholesale enterprises.  
Import units of anabolic agents and peptide hormones may only supply anabolic agents and peptide hormones to manufacturers of anabolic agents and peptide hormones, medical institutions, and drug wholesale enterprises that meet the requirements of Article 9 of these Regulations.  
Insulin, being a peptide hormone, may, in addition to being supplied in accordance with paragraphs 1, 2 and 3 of this Article, also be supplied to drug retail enterprises.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　医疗机构只能凭依法享有处方权的执业医师开具的处方向患者提供蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素。处方应当保存2年。

**Article 15** A medical institution may only provide anabolic agents and peptide hormones to patients on the basis of prescriptions issued by licensed physicians who have the right to prescribe according to law. Prescriptions shall be kept for 2 years.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　兴奋剂目录所列禁用物质属于麻醉药品、精神药品、医疗用毒性药品和易制毒化学品的，其生产、销售、进口、运输和使用，依照药品管理法和有关行政法规的规定实行特殊管理。  
蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素和前款规定以外的兴奋剂目录所列其他禁用物质，实行处方药管理。

**Article 16** Where a prohibited substance listed in the List of Doping Substances is a narcotic drug, psychotropic substance, toxic drug for medical use or precursor chemical, its production, sale, import, transport and use shall be subject to special control in accordance with the Drug Administration Law and relevant administrative regulations.  
Anabolic agents, peptide hormones and other prohibited substances listed in the List of Doping Substances other than those specified in the preceding paragraph shall be managed as prescription drugs.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　药品、食品中含有兴奋剂目录所列禁用物质的，生产企业应当在包装标识或者产品说明书上用中文注明“运动员慎用”字样。

**Article 17** Where a drug or food contains a prohibited substance listed in the List of Doping Substances, the manufacturer shall indicate the words "Caution for Athletes" in Chinese on the package label or product instructions.

### 第三章　反兴奋剂义务

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　实施运动员注册管理的体育社会团体(以下简称体育社会团体)应当加强对在本体育社会团体注册的运动员和教练、领队、队医等运动员辅助人员的监督管理和反兴奋剂的教育、培训。  
运动员管理单位应当加强对其所属的运动员和运动员辅助人员的监督管理和反兴奋剂的教育、培训。

**Article 18** Sports social organizations that implement athlete registration management (hereinafter referred to as sports social organizations) shall strengthen the supervision and management of athletes registered with them and athlete support personnel such as coaches, team leaders and team doctors, as well as anti-doping education and training.  
Athlete management units shall strengthen the supervision and management of the athletes and athlete support personnel under them, as well as anti-doping education and training.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　体育社会团体、运动员管理单位和其他单位，不得向运动员提供兴奋剂，不得组织、强迫、欺骗运动员在体育运动中使用兴奋剂。  
科研单位不得为使用兴奋剂或者逃避兴奋剂检查提供技术支持。

**Article 19** Sports social organizations, athlete management units and other units may not provide doping to athletes, nor organize, force or deceive athletes into using doping in sports.  
Scientific research units may not provide technical support for the use of doping or evasion of doping tests.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　运动员管理单位应当为其所属运动员约定医疗机构，指导运动员因医疗目的合理使用药物；应当记录并按照兴奋剂检查规则的规定向相关体育社会团体提供其所属运动员的医疗信息和药物使用情况。

**Article 20** An athlete management unit shall designate medical institutions for the athletes under its management, and guide the athletes in the rational use of drugs for medical purposes; it shall record and, in accordance with the provisions of the doping test rules, provide the relevant sports social organizations with the medical information and drug use status of the athletes under its management.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　体育社会团体、运动员管理单位，应当按照兴奋剂检查规则的规定提供运动员名单和每名运动员的教练、所从事的运动项目以及运动成绩等相关信息，并为兴奋剂检查提供便利。

**Article 21** Sports social organizations and athlete management units shall, in accordance with the provisions of the doping test rules, provide the list of athletes and information on each athlete's coach, sport event and sports performance, and shall facilitate doping tests.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　全国性体育社会团体应当对在本体育社会团体注册的成员的下列行为规定处理措施和处理程序：  
(一)运动员使用兴奋剂的；  
(二)运动员辅助人员、运动员管理单位向运动员提供兴奋剂的；  
(三)运动员、运动员辅助人员、运动员管理单位拒绝、阻挠兴奋剂检查的。  
前款所指的处理程序还应当规定当事人的抗辩权和申诉权。全国性体育社会团体应当将处理措施和处理程序报国务院体育主管部门备案。

**Article 22** National sports social organizations shall prescribe handling measures and handling procedures for the following acts committed by their registered members:  
(1) athletes using doping;  
(2) athlete support personnel or athlete management units providing doping to athletes;  
(3) athletes, athlete support personnel or athlete management units refusing or obstructing doping tests.  
The handling procedures referred to in the preceding paragraph shall also provide for the parties' right to defense and right to petition. The national sports social organizations shall report the handling measures and handling procedures to the competent sports department of the State Council for record.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　运动员辅助人员应当教育、提示运动员不得使用兴奋剂，并向运动员提供有关反兴奋剂规则的咨询。  
运动员辅助人员不得向运动员提供兴奋剂，不得组织、强迫、欺骗、教唆、协助运动员在体育运动中使用兴奋剂，不得阻挠兴奋剂检查，不得实施影响采样结果的行为。  
运动员发现运动员辅助人员违反前款规定的，有权检举、控告。

**Article 23** Athlete support personnel shall educate and remind athletes not to use doping, and shall provide athletes with consultation on anti-doping rules.  
Athlete support personnel may not provide doping to athletes, nor organize, force, deceive, instigate or assist athletes in using doping in sports, nor obstruct doping tests, nor commit any act affecting the results of sample collection.  
If an athlete discovers that athlete support personnel violate the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the athlete shall have the right to report and accuse.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　运动员不得在体育运动中使用兴奋剂。

**Article 24** Athletes may not use doping in sports.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　在体育社会团体注册的运动员、运动员辅助人员凭依法享有处方权的执业医师开具的处方，方可持有含有兴奋剂目录所列禁用物质的药品。  
在体育社会团体注册的运动员接受医疗诊断时，应当按照兴奋剂检查规则的规定向医师说明其运动员身份。医师对其使用药品时，应当首先选择不含兴奋剂目录所列禁用物质的药品；确需使用含有这类禁用物质的药品的，应当告知其药品性质和使用后果。

**Article 25** Athletes and athlete support personnel registered with sports social organizations may only possess drugs containing prohibited substances listed in the List of Doping Substances on the basis of prescriptions issued by licensed physicians who have the right to prescribe according to law.  
When an athlete registered with a sports social organization receives medical diagnosis, he or she shall inform the physician of his or her status as an athlete in accordance with the provisions of the doping test rules. When prescribing drugs for such an athlete, the physician shall first choose drugs that do not contain prohibited substances listed in the List of Doping Substances; if it is truly necessary to use drugs containing such prohibited substances, the physician shall inform the athlete of the nature of the drugs and the consequences of their use.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　在全国性体育社会团体注册的运动员，因医疗目的确需使用含有兴奋剂目录所列禁用物质的药品的，应当按照兴奋剂检查规则的规定申请核准后方可使用。

**Article 26** An athlete registered with a national sports social organization who truly needs to use drugs containing prohibited substances listed in the List of Doping Substances for medical purposes may only use them after applying for and obtaining approval in accordance with the provisions of the doping test rules.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　运动员应当接受兴奋剂检查，不得实施影响采样结果的行为。

**Article 27** Athletes shall submit to doping tests and may not commit any act affecting the results of sample collection.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　在全国性体育社会团体注册的运动员离开运动员驻地的，应当按照兴奋剂检查规则的规定报告。

**Article 28** An athlete registered with a national sports social organization who leaves the athlete's residence shall report in accordance with the provisions of the doping test rules.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　实施中等及中等以上教育的学校和其他教育机构应当加强反兴奋剂教育，提高学生的反兴奋剂意识，并采取措施防止在学校体育活动中使用兴奋剂；发现学生使用兴奋剂，应当予以制止。  
体育专业教育应当包括反兴奋剂的教学内容。

**Article 29** Schools and other educational institutions providing secondary education or above shall strengthen anti-doping education, enhance students' anti-doping awareness, and take measures to prevent the use of doping in school sports activities; if they discover that a student uses doping, they shall stop it.  
Physical education programs shall include anti-doping teaching content.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　体育健身活动经营单位及其专业指导人员，不得向体育健身活动参加者提供含有禁用物质的药品、食品。

**Article 30** Business units of sports fitness activities and their professional instructors may not provide drugs or foods containing prohibited substances to participants in sports fitness activities.

### 第四章　兴奋剂检查与检测

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国务院体育主管部门应当制定兴奋剂检查规则和兴奋剂检查计划并组织实施。

**Article 31** The competent sports department of the State Council shall formulate doping test rules and doping test plans and organize their implementation.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院体育主管部门应当根据兴奋剂检查计划，决定对全国性体育竞赛的参赛运动员实施赛内兴奋剂检查；并可以决定对省级体育竞赛的参赛运动员实施赛内兴奋剂检查。  
其他体育竞赛需要进行赛内兴奋剂检查的，由竞赛组织者决定。

**Article 32** The competent sports department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the doping test plan, decide to conduct in-competition doping tests on athletes participating in national sports competitions; and it may decide to conduct in-competition doping tests on athletes participating in provincial sports competitions.  
If in-competition doping tests are required for other sports competitions, the competition organizer shall decide.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院体育主管部门应当根据兴奋剂检查计划，决定对在全国性体育社会团体注册的运动员实施赛外兴奋剂检查。

**Article 33** The competent sports department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the doping test plan, decide to conduct out-of-competition doping tests on athletes registered with national sports social organizations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　兴奋剂检查工作人员(以下简称检查人员)应当按照兴奋剂检查规则实施兴奋剂检查。

**Article 34** Doping test personnel (hereinafter referred to as test personnel) shall conduct doping tests in accordance with the doping test rules.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　实施兴奋剂检查，应当有2名以上检查人员参加。检查人员履行兴奋剂检查职责时，应当出示兴奋剂检查证件；向运动员采集受检样本时，还应当出示按照兴奋剂检查规则签发的一次性兴奋剂检查授权书。  
检查人员履行兴奋剂检查职责时，有权进入体育训练场所、体育竞赛场所和运动员驻地。有关单位和人员应当对检查人员履行兴奋剂检查职责予以配合，不得拒绝、阻挠。

**Article 35** For conducting a doping test, at least two test personnel shall participate. When performing doping test duties, test personnel shall present their doping test credentials; when collecting a sample from an athlete, they shall also present a one-time doping test authorization document issued in accordance with the doping test rules.  
When performing doping test duties, test personnel shall have the right to enter sports training venues, sports competition venues and athletes' residences. Relevant units and individuals shall cooperate with test personnel in performing doping test duties and may not refuse or obstruct them.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　受检样本由国务院体育主管部门确定的符合兴奋剂检测条件的检测机构检测。  
兴奋剂检测机构及其工作人员，应当按照兴奋剂检查规则规定的范围和标准对受检样本进行检测。

**Article 36** Samples collected shall be tested by testing institutions that meet the conditions for doping testing and are designated by the competent sports department of the State Council.  
Doping testing institutions and their staff shall test samples within the scope and according to the standards prescribed by the doping test rules.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　体育主管部门和其他行政机关及其工作人员不履行职责，或者包庇、纵容非法使用、提供兴奋剂，或者有其他违反本条例行为的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 37** Where the competent sports departments and other administrative organs and their staff members fail to perform their duties, or shield or connive at the illegal use or provision of doping, or commit other acts violating these Regulations, the persons in charge with responsibility and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府负责药品监督管理的部门按照国务院药品监督管理部门规定的职责分工，没收非法生产、经营的蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素和违法所得，并处违法生产、经营药品货值金额2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，由发证机关吊销《药品生产许可证》、《药品经营许可证》；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)生产企业擅自生产蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素，或者未按照本条例规定渠道供应蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素的；  
(二)药品批发企业擅自经营蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素，或者未按照本条例规定渠道供应蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素的；  
(三)药品零售企业擅自经营蛋白同化制剂、肽类激素的。

**Article 38** Where any of the following acts is committed in violation of these Regulations, the drug regulatory department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the division of functions and duties prescribed by the drug regulatory department of the State Council, confiscate the illegally produced or operated anabolic agents and peptide hormones and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 2 times but not more than 5 times the value of the illegally produced or operated drugs; if the circumstances are serious, the issuing authority shall revoke the Drug Manufacturing Certificate or Drug Distribution Permit; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) a manufacturer producing anabolic agents or peptide hormones without authorization, or failing to supply anabolic agents or peptide hormones through the channels prescribed by these Regulations;  
(2) a drug wholesale enterprise operating anabolic agents or peptide hormones without authorization, or failing to supply anabolic agents or peptide hormones through the channels prescribed by these Regulations;  
(3) a drug retail enterprise operating anabolic agents or peptide hormones without authorization.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　体育社会团体、运动员管理单位向运动员提供兴奋剂或者组织、强迫、欺骗运动员在体育运动中使用兴奋剂的，由国务院体育主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府体育主管部门收缴非法持有的兴奋剂；负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员4年内不得从事体育管理工作和运动员辅助工作；情节严重的，终身不得从事体育管理工作和运动员辅助工作；造成运动员人身损害的，依法承担民事赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
体育社会团体、运动员管理单位未履行本条例规定的其他义务的，由国务院体育主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府体育主管部门责令改正；造成严重后果的，负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员2年内不得从事体育管理工作和运动员辅助工作。

**Article 39** Where a sports social organization or athlete management unit provides doping to athletes, or organizes, forces or deceives athletes into using doping in sports, the competent sports department of the State Council or the competent sports department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall confiscate the illegally held doping; the persons in charge with responsibility and other directly responsible persons shall be prohibited from engaging in sports management work and athlete support work for 4 years; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be permanently prohibited from engaging in sports management work and athlete support work; if personal injury is caused to athletes, they shall bear civil liability for compensation according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a sports social organization or athlete management unit fails to perform other obligations prescribed by these Regulations, the competent sports department of the State Council or the competent sports department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order it to make corrections; if serious consequences are caused, the persons in charge with responsibility and other directly responsible persons shall be prohibited from engaging in sports management work and athlete support work for 2 years.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　运动员辅助人员组织、强迫、欺骗、教唆运动员在体育运动中使用兴奋剂的，由国务院体育主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府体育主管部门收缴非法持有的兴奋剂；4年内不得从事运动员辅助工作和体育管理工作；情节严重的，终身不得从事运动员辅助工作和体育管理工作；造成运动员人身损害的，依法承担民事赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
运动员辅助人员向运动员提供兴奋剂，或者协助运动员在体育运动中使用兴奋剂，或者实施影响采样结果行为的，由国务院体育主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府体育主管部门收缴非法持有的兴奋剂；2年内不得从事运动员辅助工作和体育管理工作；情节严重的，终身不得从事运动员辅助工作和体育管理工作；造成运动员人身损害的，依法承担民事赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 40** Where athlete support personnel organize, force, deceive or instigate athletes to use doping in sports, the competent sports department of the State Council or the competent sports department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall confiscate the illegally held doping; such personnel shall be prohibited from engaging in athlete support work and sports management work for 4 years; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be permanently prohibited from engaging in athlete support work and sports management work; if personal injury is caused to athletes, they shall bear civil liability for compensation according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where athlete support personnel provide doping to athletes, or assist athletes in using doping in sports, or commit any act affecting the results of sample collection, the competent sports department of the State Council or the competent sports department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall confiscate the illegally held doping; such personnel shall be prohibited from engaging in athlete support work and sports management work for 2 years; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be permanently prohibited from engaging in athlete support work and sports management work; if personal injury is caused to athletes, they shall bear civil liability for compensation according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　运动员辅助人员非法持有兴奋剂的，由国务院体育主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府体育主管部门收缴非法持有的兴奋剂；情节严重的，2年内不得从事运动员辅助工作。

**Article 41** Where athlete support personnel illegally hold doping, the competent sports department of the State Council or the competent sports department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall confiscate the illegally held doping; if the circumstances are serious, such personnel shall be prohibited from engaging in athlete support work for 2 years.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　体育社会团体、运动员管理单位违反本条例规定，负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员属于国家工作人员的，还应当依法给予撤职、开除的行政处分。  
运动员辅助人员违反本条例规定，属于国家工作人员的，还应当依法给予撤职、开除的行政处分。

**Article 42** Where a sports social organization or athlete management unit violates these Regulations, if the persons in charge with responsibility and other directly responsible persons are public servants, they shall also be given the administrative sanctions of removal from office or expulsion according to law.  
Where athlete support personnel violate these Regulations, if they are public servants, they shall also be given the administrative sanctions of removal from office or expulsion according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　按照[本条例第三十九条](#art-39)、第四十条、第四十一条规定作出的处理决定应当公开，公众有权查阅。

**Article 43** The handling decisions made in accordance with Articles 39, 40 and 41 of these Regulations shall be made public, and the public shall have the right to inspect them.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　医师未按照本条例的规定使用药品，或者未履行告知义务的，由县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门给予警告；造成严重后果的，责令暂停6个月以上1年以下执业活动。

**Article 44** Where a physician fails to use drugs in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, or fails to fulfill the obligation of disclosure, the health department of the people's government at or above the county level shall give him or her a warning; if serious consequences are caused, he or she shall be ordered to suspend his or her practice for not less than 6 months but not more than 1 year.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　体育健身活动经营单位向体育健身活动参加者提供含有禁用物质的药品、食品的，由负责药品监督管理的部门、食品安全监督管理部门依照药品管理法、《中华人民共和国食品安全法》和有关行政法规的规定予以处罚。

**Article 45** Where a business unit of sports fitness activities provides drugs or foods containing prohibited substances to participants in sports fitness activities, the drug regulatory department and the food safety supervision and administration department shall impose penalties in accordance with the Drug Administration Law, the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and relevant administrative regulations.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　运动员违反本条例规定的，由有关体育社会团体、运动员管理单位、竞赛组织者作出取消参赛资格、取消比赛成绩或者禁赛的处理。  
运动员因受到前款规定的处理不服的，可以向体育仲裁机构申请仲裁。

**Article 46** Where an athlete violates the provisions of these Regulations, the relevant sports social organization, athlete management unit or competition organizer shall impose a sanction of disqualification, annulment of results or suspension.  
If an athlete is dissatisfied with the sanction imposed under the preceding paragraph, he or she may apply for arbitration to a sports arbitration institution.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　本条例自2004年3月1日起施行。

**Article 47** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 2004.
