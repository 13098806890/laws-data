### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国海关关衔条例》的规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Customs Ranks.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　海关工作人员佩带的关衔标志必须与所授予的关衔相符。

**Article 2** The rank insignia worn by Customs personnel must correspond to the rank conferred.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　海关关衔标志：海关总监、海关副总监关衔标志由橄榄叶环绕的海关关徽和五角星下衬五边形的星花组成；关务监督关衔标志由橄榄叶、星花和海关关徽组成；关务督察、关务督办、关务员关衔标志由横杠、星花和海关关徽组成。海关关衔标志为金色。

**Article 3** Customs rank insignia: The rank insignia for Customs Director General and Deputy Customs Director General shall consist of the Customs emblem surrounded by olive leaves and star flowers with a pentagon beneath a five-pointed star; the rank insignia for Customs Supervisors shall consist of olive leaves, star flowers, and the Customs emblem; the rank insignia for Customs Inspectors, Customs Superintendents, and Customs Attendants shall consist of horizontal bars, star flowers, and the Customs emblem. All Customs rank insignia shall be gold in color.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　关衔标志缀钉在肩章上，肩章为剑形，版面为黑色，分为硬肩章和软肩章。  
海关工作人员着制式上衣、制式大衣时，佩带硬肩章；着制式短袖衫、制式长袖衫、查验服时，佩带软肩章。

**Article 4** The rank insignia shall be affixed to epaulettes, which shall be sword-shaped with a black background, and shall be divided into hard epaulettes and soft epaulettes.  
When Customs personnel wear the standard uniform jacket or standard overcoat, they shall wear hard epaulettes; when wearing the standard short-sleeved shirt, standard long-sleeved shirt, or inspection attire, they shall wear soft epaulettes.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　海关总监关衔标志为一枚橄榄叶环绕的海关关徽和三枚星花；海关副总监关衔标志为一枚橄榄叶环绕的海关关徽和二枚星花。  
关务监督关衔标志为一枚橄榄叶和一枚海关关徽，一级关务监督关衔标志为三枚星花；二级关务监督关衔标志为二枚星花；三级关务监督关衔标志为一枚星花。  
关务督察关衔标志为三道横杠和一枚海关关徽，一级关务督察关衔标志为三枚星花；二级关务督察关衔标志为二枚星花；三级关务督察关衔标志为一枚星花。  
关务督办关衔标志为二道横杠和一枚海关关徽，一级关务督办关衔标志为三枚星花；二级关务督办关衔标志为二枚星花；三级关务督办关衔标志为一枚星花。  
关务员关衔标志为一道横杠和一枚海关关徽，一级关务员关衔标志为三枚星花；二级关务员关衔标志为二枚星花。

**Article 5** The rank insignia for Customs Director General shall be one Customs emblem surrounded by olive leaves and three star flowers; the rank insignia for Deputy Customs Director General shall be one Customs emblem surrounded by olive leaves and two star flowers.  
The rank insignia for Customs Supervisors shall be one olive leaf and one Customs emblem; the rank insignia for Grade 1 Customs Supervisor shall be three star flowers; the rank insignia for Grade 2 Customs Supervisor shall be two star flowers; the rank insignia for Grade 3 Customs Supervisor shall be one star flower.  
The rank insignia for Customs Inspectors shall be three horizontal bars and one Customs emblem; the rank insignia for Grade 1 Customs Inspector shall be three star flowers; the rank insignia for Grade 2 Customs Inspector shall be two star flowers; the rank insignia for Grade 3 Customs Inspector shall be one star flower.  
The rank insignia for Customs Superintendents shall be two horizontal bars and one Customs emblem; the rank insignia for Grade 1 Customs Superintendent shall be three star flowers; the rank insignia for Grade 2 Customs Superintendent shall be two star flowers; the rank insignia for Grade 3 Customs Superintendent shall be one star flower.  
The rank insignia for Customs Attendants shall be one horizontal bar and one Customs emblem; the rank insignia for Grade 1 Customs Attendant shall be three star flowers; the rank insignia for Grade 2 Customs Attendant shall be two star flowers.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　海关工作人员晋升或者降低关衔的，由批准机关更换其关衔标志；取消或者不予保留关衔的，由批准机关收回其关衔标志。

**Article 6** Where a Customs staff member's rank is promoted or demoted, the approving authority shall replace the rank insignia; where the rank is revoked or not retained, the approving authority shall recall the rank insignia.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　海关关衔标志由海关总署负责制作和管理。其他单位和个人不得制作、仿造、伪造、变造和买卖、使用关衔标志，也不得使用与关衔标志相类似的标志。

**Article 7** The Customs rank insignia shall be manufactured and managed by the General Administration of Customs. No other entity or individual may manufacture, imitate, forge, alter, buy, sell, or use rank insignia, nor use any insignia similar to the rank insignia.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　本办法自公布之日起施行。
