# 中华人民共和国海关稽查条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2022-03-29  
**生效日期**：2022-05-01

> （1997年1月3日中华人民共和国国务院令第209号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2016年6月19日《国务院关于修改〈中华人民共和国海关稽查条例〉的决定》第二次修订　根据2022年3月29日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了建立、健全海关稽查制度，加强海关监督管理，维护正常的进出口秩序和当事人的合法权益，保障国家税收收入，促进对外贸易的发展，根据《中华人民共和国海关法》（以下简称海关法），制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称海关稽查，是指海关自进出口货物放行之日起3年内或者在保税货物、减免税进口货物的海关监管期限内及其后的3年内，对与进出口货物直接有关的企业、单位的会计账簿、会计凭证、报关单证以及其他有关资料（以下统称账簿、单证等有关资料）和有关进出口货物进行核查，监督其进出口活动的真实性和合法性。 第三条　海关对下列与进出口货物直接有关的企业、单位实施海关稽查： （一）从事对外贸易的企业、单位； （二）从事对外加工贸易的企业； （三）经营保税业务的企业； （四）使用或者经营减免税进口货物的企业、单位； （五）从事报关业务的企业； （六）海关总署规定的与进出口货物直接有关的其他企业、单位。 第四条　海关根据稽查工作需要，可以向有关行业协会、政府部门和相关企业等收集特定商品、行业与进出口活动有关的信息。收集的信息涉及商业秘密的，海关应当予以保密。 第五条　海关和海关工作人员执行海关稽查职务，应当客观公正，实事求是，廉洁奉公，保守被稽查人的商业秘密，不得侵犯被稽查人的合法权益。 第二章　账簿、单证等有关资料的管理 第六条　与进出口货物直接有关的企业、单位所设置、编制的会计账簿、会计凭证、会计报表和其他会计资料，应当真实、准确、完整地记录和反映进出口业务的有关情况。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了建立、健全海关稽查制度，加强海关监督管理，维护正常的进出口秩序和当事人的合法权益，保障国家税收收入，促进对外贸易的发展，根据《中华人民共和国海关法》（以下简称海关法），制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Customs Law) for the purposes of establishing and improving the customs audit system, strengthening customs supervision and administration, maintaining the normal order of import and export and the legitimate rights and interests of the parties, safeguarding the state tax revenue, and promoting the development of foreign trade.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称海关稽查，是指海关自进出口货物放行之日起3年内或者在保税货物、减免税进口货物的海关监管期限内及其后的3年内，对与进出口货物直接有关的企业、单位的会计账簿、会计凭证、报关单证以及其他有关资料（以下统称账簿、单证等有关资料）和有关进出口货物进行核查，监督其进出口活动的真实性和合法性。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "customs audit" means the customs authority, within 3 years from the date of release of import or export goods, or within the period of customs supervision of bonded goods or goods imported with reduced or exempted duties and within 3 years thereafter, verifies the accounting books, accounting vouchers, customs declaration documents and other relevant materials (hereinafter collectively referred to as books, documents and other relevant materials) of enterprises and units directly related to the import or export goods, as well as the relevant import or export goods, to supervise the authenticity and legality of their import or export activities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　海关对下列与进出口货物直接有关的企业、单位实施海关稽查：  
（一）从事对外贸易的企业、单位；  
（二）从事对外加工贸易的企业；  
（三）经营保税业务的企业；  
（四）使用或者经营减免税进口货物的企业、单位；  
（五）从事报关业务的企业；  
（六）海关总署规定的与进出口货物直接有关的其他企业、单位。

**Article 3** The customs authority shall carry out customs audit on the following enterprises and units directly related to the import or export goods:  
(1) enterprises and units engaged in foreign trade;  
(2) enterprises engaged in outward processing trade;  
(3) enterprises operating bonded business;  
(4) enterprises and units that use or operate goods imported with reduced or exempted duties;  
(5) enterprises engaged in customs declaration business;  
(6) other enterprises and units directly related to the import or export goods as prescribed by the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　海关根据稽查工作需要，可以向有关行业协会、政府部门和相关企业等收集特定商品、行业与进出口活动有关的信息。收集的信息涉及商业秘密的，海关应当予以保密。

**Article 4** The customs authority may, based on the needs of audit work, collect information relating to specific goods, industries and import or export activities from relevant trade associations, government departments and relevant enterprises, etc. If the collected information involves trade secrets, the customs authority shall keep it confidential.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　海关和海关工作人员执行海关稽查职务，应当客观公正，实事求是，廉洁奉公，保守被稽查人的商业秘密，不得侵犯被稽查人的合法权益。

**Article 5** The customs authority and its staff members shall, when performing customs audit duties, be objective and fair, seek truth from facts, be honest and upright, keep the trade secrets of the auditee, and shall not infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of the auditee.

### 第二章　账簿、单证等有关资料的管理

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　与进出口货物直接有关的企业、单位所设置、编制的会计账簿、会计凭证、会计报表和其他会计资料，应当真实、准确、完整地记录和反映进出口业务的有关情况。

**Article 6** The accounting books, accounting vouchers, accounting statements and other accounting materials set up and prepared by enterprises and units directly related to the import or export goods shall truly, accurately and completely record and reflect the relevant information of the import or export business.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　与进出口货物直接有关的企业、单位应当依照有关法律、行政法规规定的保管期限，保管会计账簿、会计凭证、会计报表和其他会计资料。  
报关单证、进出口单证、合同以及与进出口业务直接有关的其他资料，应当在[本条例第二条](#art-2)规定的期限内保管。

**Article 7** Enterprises and units directly related to the import or export goods shall keep accounting books, accounting vouchers, accounting statements and other accounting materials in accordance with the retention periods prescribed by relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
Customs declaration documents, import and export documents, contracts and other materials directly related to the import or export business shall be kept within the period specified in Article 2 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　与进出口货物直接有关的企业、单位会计制度健全，能够通过计算机正确、完整地记账、核算的，其计算机储存和输出的会计记录视同会计资料。

**Article 8** If enterprises and units directly related to the import or export goods have sound accounting systems and can correctly and completely record and calculate accounts through computers, the accounting records stored and output by their computers shall be deemed as accounting materials.

### 第三章　海关稽查的实施

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　海关应当按照海关监管的要求，根据与进出口货物直接有关的企业、单位的进出口信用状况和风险状况以及进出口货物的具体情况，确定海关稽查重点。

**Article 9** The customs authority shall, in accordance with the requirements of customs supervision, determine the key points of customs audit based on the import-export credit status and risk status of enterprises and units directly related to the import or export goods, as well as the specific circumstances of the import or export goods.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　海关进行稽查时，应当在实施稽查的3日前，书面通知被稽查人。在被稽查人有重大违法嫌疑，其账簿、单证等有关资料以及进出口货物可能被转移、隐匿、毁弃等紧急情况下，经直属海关关长或者其授权的隶属海关关长批准，海关可以不经事先通知进行稽查。

**Article 10** When conducting an audit, the customs authority shall notify the auditee in writing 3 days before carrying out the audit. In emergency situations where the auditee is suspected of serious violations and its books, documents and other relevant materials as well as import or export goods may be transferred, concealed, destroyed, etc., the customs authority may conduct the audit without prior notification upon approval by the director of the directly subordinate customs or the authorized director of the subordinate customs.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　海关进行稽查时，应当组成稽查组。稽查组的组成人员不得少于2人。

**Article 11** When conducting an audit, the customs authority shall form an audit team. The audit team shall consist of no fewer than 2 members.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　海关进行稽查时，海关工作人员应当出示海关稽查证。  
海关稽查证，由海关总署统一制发。

**Article 12** When conducting an audit, the customs staff shall present the customs audit certificate.  
The customs audit certificate shall be uniformly issued by the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　海关进行稽查时，海关工作人员与被稽查人有直接利害关系的，应当回避。

**Article 13** When conducting an audit, if a customs staff member has a direct interest in the auditee, that staff member shall withdraw.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　海关进行稽查时，可以行使下列职权：  
（一）查阅、复制被稽查人的账簿、单证等有关资料；  
（二）进入被稽查人的生产经营场所、货物存放场所，检查与进出口活动有关的生产经营情况和货物；  
（三）询问被稽查人的法定代表人、主要负责人员和其他有关人员与进出口活动有关的情况和问题；  
（四）经直属海关关长或者其授权的隶属海关关长批准，查询被稽查人在商业银行或者其他金融机构的存款账户。

**Article 14** When conducting an audit, the customs authority may exercise the following powers:  
(1) to consult and copy the books, documents and other relevant materials of the auditee;  
(2) to enter the business premises and goods storage places of the auditee to inspect the production and operation conditions and goods related to import or export activities;  
(3) to inquire the legal representative, principal responsible persons and other relevant persons of the auditee about the situations and issues related to import or export activities;  
(4) upon approval by the director of the directly subordinate customs or the authorized director of the subordinate customs, to inquire about the deposit accounts of the auditee in commercial banks or other financial institutions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　海关进行稽查时，发现被稽查人有可能转移、隐匿、篡改、毁弃账簿、单证等有关资料的，经直属海关关长或者其授权的隶属海关关长批准，可以查封、扣押其账簿、单证等有关资料以及相关电子数据存储介质。采取该项措施时，不得妨碍被稽查人正常的生产经营活动。  
海关对有关情况查明或者取证后，应当立即解除对账簿、单证等有关资料以及相关电子数据存储介质的查封、扣押。

**Article 15** When conducting an audit, if the customs authority finds that the auditee may transfer, conceal, tamper with, or destroy books, documents and other relevant materials, it may, upon approval by the director of the directly subordinate customs or the authorized director of the subordinate customs, seal up or seize the books, documents and other relevant materials as well as the related electronic data storage media. When taking such measures, the customs authority shall not hinder the normal production and operation activities of the auditee.  
After the customs authority has clarified the relevant circumstances or obtained evidence, it shall promptly lift the sealing up or seizure of the books, documents and other relevant materials as well as the related electronic data storage media.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　海关进行稽查时，发现被稽查人的进出口货物有违反海关法和其他有关法律、行政法规规定的嫌疑的，经直属海关关长或者其授权的隶属海关关长批准，可以查封、扣押有关进出口货物。

**Article 16** When conducting an audit, if the customs authority finds that the import or export goods of the auditee are suspected of violating the Customs Law and other relevant laws and administrative regulations, it may, upon approval by the director of the directly subordinate customs or the authorized director of the subordinate customs, seal up or seize the relevant import or export goods.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　被稽查人应当配合海关稽查工作，并提供必要的工作条件。

**Article 17** The auditee shall cooperate with the customs audit work and provide necessary working conditions.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　被稽查人应当接受海关稽查，如实反映情况，提供账簿、单证等有关资料，不得拒绝、拖延、隐瞒。  
被稽查人使用计算机记账的，应当向海关提供记账软件、使用说明书及有关资料。

**Article 18** The auditee shall accept the customs audit, truthfully reflect the situation, and provide books, documents and other relevant materials, and shall not refuse, delay, or conceal.  
If the auditee uses a computer to keep accounts, it shall provide the accounting software, user manuals and relevant materials to the customs authority.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　海关查阅、复制被稽查人的账簿、单证等有关资料或者进入被稽查人的生产经营场所、货物存放场所检查时，被稽查人的法定代表人或者主要负责人员或者其指定的代表应当到场，并按照海关的要求清点账簿、打开货物存放场所、搬移货物或者开启货物包装。

**Article 19** When the customs authority consults, copies the books, documents and other relevant materials of the auditee or enters the business premises or goods storage places of the auditee for inspection, the legal representative or principal responsible person of the auditee, or a designated representative shall be present, and shall count the books, open the goods storage places, move the goods, or open the goods packaging as required by the customs authority.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　海关进行稽查时，与被稽查人有财务往来或者其他商务往来的企业、单位应当向海关如实反映被稽查人的有关情况，提供有关资料和证明材料。

**Article 20** When the customs authority conducts an audit, enterprises and units that have financial transactions or other commercial transactions with the auditee shall truthfully reflect to the customs authority the relevant information about the auditee and provide relevant materials and evidentiary documents.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　海关进行稽查时，可以委托会计、税务等方面的专业机构就相关问题作出专业结论。  
被稽查人委托会计、税务等方面的专业机构作出的专业结论，可以作为海关稽查的参考依据。

**Article 21** When conducting an audit, the customs authority may entrust professional institutions in accounting, taxation, etc. to make professional conclusions on relevant issues.  
Professional conclusions made by professional institutions in accounting, taxation, etc. entrusted by the auditee may serve as reference basis for customs audit.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　海关稽查组实施稽查后，应当向海关报送稽查报告。稽查报告认定被稽查人涉嫌违法的，在报送海关前应当就稽查报告认定的事实征求被稽查人的意见，被稽查人应当自收到相关材料之日起7日内，将其书面意见送交海关。

**Article 22** After carrying out the audit, the customs audit team shall submit the audit report to the customs authority. If the audit report finds that the auditee is suspected of violating the law, before submitting the report to the customs authority, the audit team shall solicit the auditee's opinions on the facts determined in the audit report, and the auditee shall, within 7 days from the date of receiving the relevant materials, submit its written opinions to the customs authority.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　海关应当自收到稽查报告之日起30日内，作出海关稽查结论并送达被稽查人。  
海关应当在稽查结论中说明作出结论的理由，并告知被稽查人的权利。

**Article 23** The customs authority shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the audit report, make a customs audit conclusion and serve it on the auditee.  
The customs authority shall state the reasons for making the conclusion in the audit conclusion and inform the auditee of its rights.

### 第四章　海关稽查的处理

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　经海关稽查，发现关税或者其他进口环节的税收少征或者漏征的，由海关依照海关法和有关税收法律、行政法规的规定向被稽查人补征；因被稽查人违反规定而造成少征或者漏征的，由海关依照海关法和有关税收法律、行政法规的规定追征。  
被稽查人在海关规定的期限内仍未缴纳税款的，海关可以依照[海关法第六十条](../../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国海关法.md#art-60)第一款、第二款的规定采取强制执行措施。

**Article 24** If, upon customs audit, it is found that customs duties or other taxes at the import stage are under-collected or omitted, the customs authority shall, in accordance with the Customs Law and relevant tax laws and administrative regulations, make a supplementary collection from the auditee; if the under-collection or omission is caused by the auditee's violation of regulations, the customs authority shall, in accordance with the Customs Law and relevant tax laws and administrative regulations, make a retroactive collection.  
If the auditee still fails to pay the tax within the time limit prescribed by the customs authority, the customs authority may take compulsory enforcement measures in accordance with Article 60, paragraphs 1 and 2 of the Customs Law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　依照[本条例第十六条](#art-16)的规定查封、扣押的有关进出口货物，经海关稽查排除违法嫌疑的，海关应当立即解除查封、扣押；经海关稽查认定违法的，由海关依照海关法和海关行政处罚实施条例的规定处理。

**Article 25** If the relevant import or export goods sealed up or seized in accordance with Article 16 of these Regulations are cleared of suspicion of violation upon customs audit, the customs authority shall immediately lift the sealing up or seizure; if they are found to be illegal upon customs audit, the customs authority shall handle them in accordance with the Customs Law and the Regulations of the Customs on Implementation of Administrative Penalties.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　经海关稽查，认定被稽查人有违反海关监管规定的行为的，由海关依照海关法和海关行政处罚实施条例的规定处理。  
与进出口货物直接有关的企业、单位主动向海关报告其违反海关监管规定的行为，并接受海关处理的，应当从轻或者减轻行政处罚。

**Article 26** If, upon customs audit, the auditee is found to have violated customs supervision regulations, the customs authority shall handle the matter in accordance with the Customs Law and the Regulations of the Customs on Implementation of Administrative Penalties.  
If enterprises and units directly related to the import or export goods voluntarily report to the customs authority their violations of customs supervision regulations and accept the handling by the customs authority, they shall be given a lighter or mitigated administrative penalty.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　经海关稽查，发现被稽查人有走私行为，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由海关依照海关法和海关行政处罚实施条例的规定处理。

**Article 27** If, upon customs audit, the auditee is found to have engaged in smuggling activities that constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, the customs authority shall handle the matter in accordance with the Customs Law and the Regulations of the Customs on Implementation of Administrative Penalties.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　海关通过稽查决定补征或者追征的税款、没收的走私货物和违法所得以及收缴的罚款，全部上缴国库。

**Article 28** The taxes decided by the customs authority to be supplementary collected or retroactively collected through audit, the smuggled goods and illegal gains confiscated, and the fines collected shall all be turned over to the state treasury.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　被稽查人同海关发生纳税争议的，依照[海关法第六十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国海关法.md#art-64)的规定办理。

**Article 29** If the auditee has a tax dispute with the customs authority, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Article 64 of the Customs Law.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　被稽查人有下列行为之一的，由海关责令限期改正，逾期不改正的，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，禁止其从事报关活动；对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处5000元以上5万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）向海关提供虚假情况或者隐瞒重要事实；  
（二）拒绝、拖延向海关提供账簿、单证等有关资料以及相关电子数据存储介质；  
（三）转移、隐匿、篡改、毁弃报关单证、进出口单证、合同、与进出口业务直接有关的其他资料以及相关电子数据存储介质。

**Article 30** If an auditee commits any of the following acts, the customs authority shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be prohibited from engaging in customs declaration activities; the person in charge directly responsible and other persons directly responsible shall be fined not less than RMB 5,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) providing false information or concealing important facts to the customs authority;  
(2) refusing or delaying to provide the customs authority with books, documents and other relevant materials and related electronic data storage media;  
(3) transferring, concealing, tampering with, or destroying customs declaration documents, import and export documents, contracts, other materials directly related to import or export business, and related electronic data storage media.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　被稽查人未按照规定编制或者保管报关单证、进出口单证、合同以及与进出口业务直接有关的其他资料的，由海关责令限期改正，逾期不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，禁止其从事报关活动；对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处1000元以上5000元以下的罚款。

**Article 31** If an auditee fails to prepare or keep customs declaration documents, import and export documents, contracts, and other materials directly related to the import or export business in accordance with the regulations, the customs authority shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be prohibited from engaging in customs declaration activities; the person in charge directly responsible and other persons directly responsible shall be fined not less than RMB 1,000 yuan but not more than RMB 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　被稽查人未按照规定设置或者编制账簿，或者转移、隐匿、篡改、毁弃账簿的，依照会计法的有关规定追究法律责任。

**Article 32** If an auditee fails to set up or prepare books in accordance with the regulations, or transfers, conceals, tampers with, or destroys books, legal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Accounting Law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　海关工作人员在稽查中玩忽职守、徇私舞弊、滥用职权，或者利用职务上的便利，收受、索取被稽查人的财物，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 33** If a customs staff member neglects duty, engages in malpractices for personal gain, abuses power, or uses the position to accept or extort property from the auditee, and if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, a sanction shall be given according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 34** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
