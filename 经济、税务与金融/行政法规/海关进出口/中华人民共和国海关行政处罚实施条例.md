**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2022-03-29  
**生效日期**：2022-05-01

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范海关行政处罚，保障海关依法行使职权，保护公民、法人或者其他组织的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国海关法》（以下简称海关法）及其他有关法律的规定，制定本实施条例。

**Article 1** These implementing regulations are formulated in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Customs Law) and the provisions of other relevant laws, for the purpose of regulating customs administrative penalties, ensuring that customs exercise their functions and powers according to law, and protecting the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons or other organizations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　依法不追究刑事责任的走私行为和违反海关监管规定的行为，以及法律、行政法规规定由海关实施行政处罚的行为的处理，适用本实施条例。

**Article 2** These implementing regulations shall apply to the handling of smuggling acts for which criminal liability is not investigated according to law, acts in violation of customs supervision and control regulations, and acts subject to administrative penalties imposed by the customs as stipulated by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　海关行政处罚由发现违法行为的海关管辖，也可以由违法行为发生地海关管辖。  
2个以上海关都有管辖权的案件，由最先发现违法行为的海关管辖。  
管辖不明确的案件，由有关海关协商确定管辖，协商不成的，报请共同的上级海关指定管辖。  
重大、复杂的案件，可以由海关总署指定管辖。

**Article 3** Customs administrative penalties shall be under the jurisdiction of the customs that discovers the illegal act, or under the jurisdiction of the customs at the place where the illegal act occurs.  
A case under the jurisdiction of two or more customs shall be under the jurisdiction of the customs that first discovers the illegal act.  
For a case with unclear jurisdiction, the relevant customs shall determine the jurisdiction through consultation; if consultation fails, they shall report to the common superior customs for designation of jurisdiction.  
Major or complicated cases may be subject to the designation of jurisdiction by the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　海关发现的依法应当由其他行政机关处理的违法行为，应当移送有关行政机关处理；违法行为涉嫌犯罪的，应当移送海关侦查走私犯罪公安机构、地方公安机关依法办理。

**Article 4** Where the customs discovers an illegal act that shall be handled by another administrative organ according to law, it shall transfer the case to the relevant administrative organ for handling; where the illegal act is suspected of constituting a crime, it shall transfer the case to the customs investigation organ for smuggling crimes or the local public security organ for handling according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　依照本实施条例处以警告、罚款等行政处罚，但不没收进出境货物、物品、运输工具的，不免除有关当事人依法缴纳税款、提交进出口许可证件、办理有关海关手续的义务。

**Article 5** The imposition of administrative penalties such as warnings or fines in accordance with these implementing regulations, without confiscation of imported or exported goods, articles, or means of transport, shall not exempt the relevant party from the obligations to pay taxes, submit import or export licenses, or go through relevant customs formalities according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　抗拒、阻碍海关侦查走私犯罪公安机构依法执行职务的，由设在直属海关、隶属海关的海关侦查走私犯罪公安机构依照治安管理处罚的有关规定给予处罚。  
抗拒、阻碍其他海关工作人员依法执行职务的，应当报告地方公安机关依法处理。

**Article 6** Where a person resists or obstructs the customs investigation organ for smuggling crimes in performing its duties according to law, the customs investigation organ for smuggling crimes established in the directly subordinate customs or subsidiary customs shall impose a penalty in accordance with the relevant provisions on public security administration penalties.  
Where a person resists or obstructs other customs staff in performing their duties according to law, the matter shall be reported to the local public security organ for handling according to law.

### 第二章　走私行为及其处罚

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　违反海关法及其他有关法律、行政法规，逃避海关监管，偷逃应纳税款、逃避国家有关进出境的禁止性或者限制性管理，有下列情形之一的，是走私行为：  
（一）未经国务院或者国务院授权的机关批准，从未设立海关的地点运输、携带国家禁止或者限制进出境的货物、物品或者依法应当缴纳税款的货物、物品进出境的；  
（二）经过设立海关的地点，以藏匿、伪装、瞒报、伪报或者其他方式逃避海关监管，运输、携带、邮寄国家禁止或者限制进出境的货物、物品或者依法应当缴纳税款的货物、物品进出境的；  
（三）使用伪造、变造的手册、单证、印章、账册、电子数据或者以其他方式逃避海关监管，擅自将海关监管货物、物品、进境的境外运输工具，在境内销售的；  
（四）使用伪造、变造的手册、单证、印章、账册、电子数据或者以伪报加工贸易制成品单位耗料量等方式，致使海关监管货物、物品脱离监管的；  
（五）以藏匿、伪装、瞒报、伪报或者其他方式逃避海关监管，擅自将保税区、出口加工区等海关特殊监管区域内的海关监管货物、物品，运出区外的；  
（六）有逃避海关监管，构成走私的其他行为的。

**Article 7** Any of the following acts committed in violation of the Customs Law and other relevant laws and administrative regulations, evading customs supervision, evading payable duties, or evading the State's prohibitive or restrictive management on import or export, constitutes a smuggling act:  
(1) Transporting or carrying goods or articles the import or export of which is prohibited or restricted by the State, or goods or articles on which duties shall be paid according to law, into or out of the territory from a place without a customs office established, without approval of the State Council or an organ authorized by the State Council;  
(2) Transporting, carrying, or sending by post goods or articles the import or export of which is prohibited or restricted by the State, or goods or articles on which duties shall be paid according to law, into or out of a place with a customs office established, by means of concealment, camouflage, false declaration, misrepresentation, or any other means to evade customs supervision;  
(3) Using forged or altered handbooks, documents, seals, account books, or electronic data, or by any other means to evade customs supervision, and selling without authorization within the territory goods or articles under customs supervision or foreign means of transport that have entered the territory;  
(4) Using forged or altered handbooks, documents, seals, account books, or electronic data, or by means of falsely reporting the unit material consumption of processed products, etc., to cause goods or articles under customs supervision to be out of supervision;  
(5) Transporting without authorization goods or articles under customs supervision out of a special customs supervision area such as a bonded area or export processing zone, by means of concealment, camouflage, false declaration, misrepresentation, or any other means to evade customs supervision;  
(6) Other acts that evade customs supervision and constitute smuggling.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　有下列行为之一的，按走私行为论处：  
（一）明知是走私进口的货物、物品，直接向走私人非法收购的；  
（二）在内海、领海、界河、界湖，船舶及所载人员运输、收购、贩卖国家禁止或者限制进出境的货物、物品，或者运输、收购、贩卖依法应当缴纳税款的货物，没有合法证明的。

**Article 8** Any of the following acts shall be dealt with as a smuggling act:  
(1) Knowingly purchasing smuggled imported goods or articles directly from the smuggler;  
(2) Transporting, purchasing, or selling goods or articles the import or export of which is prohibited or restricted by the State, or transporting, purchasing, or selling goods on which duties shall be paid according to law, by ships or persons on board in inland waters, territorial seas, boundary rivers, or boundary lakes, without lawful certificates.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　有本实施条例第七条、第八条所列行为之一的，依照下列规定处罚：  
（一）走私国家禁止进出口的货物的，没收走私货物及违法所得，可以并处100万元以下罚款；走私国家禁止进出境的物品的，没收走私物品及违法所得，可以并处10万元以下罚款；  
（二）应当提交许可证件而未提交但未偷逃税款，走私国家限制进出境的货物、物品的，没收走私货物、物品及违法所得，可以并处走私货物、物品等值以下罚款；  
（三）偷逃应纳税款但未逃避许可证件管理，走私依法应当缴纳税款的货物、物品的，没收走私货物、物品及违法所得，可以并处偷逃应纳税款3倍以下罚款。  
专门用于走私的运输工具或者用于掩护走私的货物、物品，2年内3次以上用于走私的运输工具或者用于掩护走私的货物、物品，应当予以没收。藏匿走私货物、物品的特制设备、夹层、暗格，应当予以没收或者责令拆毁。使用特制设备、夹层、暗格实施走私的，应当从重处罚。

**Article 9** Any person committing any of the acts listed in Articles 7 and 8 of these implementing regulations shall be punished in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) Where goods whose import or export is prohibited by the State are smuggled, the smuggled goods and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than 1 million yuan may also be imposed; where articles whose import or export is prohibited by the State are smuggled, the smuggled articles and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan may also be imposed;  
(2) Where goods or articles whose import or export is restricted by the State are smuggled, where licenses should have been submitted but are not submitted and no duties are evaded, the smuggled goods or articles and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than the equivalent value of the smuggled goods or articles may also be imposed;  
(3) Where goods or articles on which duties shall be paid according to law are smuggled, where payable duties are evaded but license management is not evaded, the smuggled goods or articles and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than three times the evaded duties may also be imposed.  
Means of transport specially used for smuggling, or goods or articles used to cover up smuggling, and means of transport or goods or articles used for smuggling three or more times within two years, shall be confiscated. Special equipment, interlayers, or concealed compartments used to conceal smuggled goods or articles shall be confiscated or ordered to be dismantled. Where special equipment, interlayers, or concealed compartments are used to commit smuggling, severe punishment shall be imposed.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　与走私人通谋为走私人提供贷款、资金、账号、发票、证明、海关单证的，与走私人通谋为走私人提供走私货物、物品的提取、发运、运输、保管、邮寄或者其他方便的，以走私的共同当事人论处，没收违法所得，并依照本实施条例第九条的规定予以处罚。

**Article 10** Where a person colludes with a smuggler to provide loans, funds, account numbers, invoices, certificates, or customs documents to the smuggler, or colludes with a smuggler to provide convenience for the extraction, dispatch, transport, storage, or mailing of smuggled goods or articles, such person shall be dealt with as a joint party to the smuggling, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 9 of these implementing regulations.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　海关准予从事海关监管货物的运输、储存、加工、装配、寄售、展示等业务的企业，构成走私犯罪或者1年内有2次以上走私行为的，海关可以撤销其注册登记；报关企业、报关人员有上述情形的，禁止其从事报关活动。

**Article 11** Where an enterprise permitted by the Customs to engage in the transportation, storage, processing, assembly, consignment, exhibition or other businesses of goods under Customs supervision constitutes a smuggling crime or commits smuggling acts twice or more within one year, the Customs may revoke its registration; where a declaration enterprise or a declaration person falls under the above circumstances, it or he shall be prohibited from engaging in declaration activities.

### 第三章　违反海关监管规定的行为及其处罚

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　违反海关法及其他有关法律、行政法规和规章但不构成走私行为的，是违反海关监管规定的行为。

**Article 12** Any act that violates the Customs Law and other relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules but does not constitute a smuggling act shall be an act in violation of Customs supervision and control regulations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　违反国家进出口管理规定，进出口国家禁止进出口的货物的，责令退运，处100万元以下罚款。

**Article 13** Where anyone, in violation of the national provisions on the administration of import and export, imports or exports goods the import or export of which is prohibited by the State, it shall be ordered to return the goods and a fine of not more than 1 million yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　违反国家进出口管理规定，进出口国家限制进出口的货物，进出口货物的收发货人向海关申报时不能提交许可证件的，进出口货物不予放行，处货物价值30%以下罚款。  
违反国家进出口管理规定，进出口属于自动进出口许可管理的货物，进出口货物的收发货人向海关申报时不能提交自动许可证明的，进出口货物不予放行。

**Article 14** Where anyone, in violation of the national provisions on the administration of import and export, imports or exports goods the import or export of which is restricted by the State, and the consignee or consignor of the import or export goods fails to submit the license documents when making declaration to the Customs, the import or export goods shall not be released, and a fine of not more than 30% of the value of the goods shall be imposed.  
Where anyone, in violation of the national provisions on the administration of import and export, imports or exports goods subject to automatic import or export licensing administration, and the consignee or consignor of the import or export goods fails to submit the automatic license certificate when making declaration to the Customs, the import or export goods shall not be released.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　进出口货物的品名、税则号列、数量、规格、价格、贸易方式、原产地、启运地、运抵地、最终目的地或者其他应当申报的项目未申报或者申报不实的，分别依照下列规定予以处罚，有违法所得的，没收违法所得：  
（一）影响海关统计准确性的，予以警告或者处1000元以上1万元以下罚款；  
（二）影响海关监管秩序的，予以警告或者处1000元以上3万元以下罚款；  
（三）影响国家许可证件管理的，处货物价值5%以上30%以下罚款；  
（四）影响国家税款征收的，处漏缴税款30%以上2倍以下罚款；  
（五）影响国家外汇、出口退税管理的，处申报价格10%以上50%以下罚款。

**Article 15** Where the description, tariff heading, quantity, specification, price, trade mode, place of origin, place of departure, place of arrival, final destination or other items that shall be declared of the import or export goods are not declared or are falsely declared, the party shall be punished respectively according to the following provisions; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) if the accuracy of Customs statistics is affected, a warning shall be given or a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed;  
(2) if the order of Customs supervision and control is affected, a warning shall be given or a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan shall be imposed;  
(3) if the administration of national license documents is affected, a fine of not less than 5% but not more than 30% of the value of the goods shall be imposed;  
(4) if the collection of national taxes is affected, a fine of not less than 30% but not more than two times the amount of taxes underpaid shall be imposed;  
(5) if the administration of national foreign exchange or export tax rebates is affected, a fine of not less than 10% but not more than 50% of the declared price shall be imposed.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　进出口货物收发货人未按照规定向报关企业提供所委托报关事项的真实情况，致使发生本实施条例第十五条规定情形的，对委托人依照本实施条例第十五条的规定予以处罚。

**Article 16** Where a consignee or consignor of import or export goods fails to provide the declaration enterprise with the true information on the entrusted declaration matters in accordance with the provisions, thereby causing the circumstances specified in Article 15 of these Implementing Regulations to occur, the client shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 15 of these Implementing Regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　报关企业、报关人员对委托人所提供情况的真实性未进行合理审查，或者因工作疏忽致使发生本实施条例第十五条规定情形的，可以对报关企业处货物价值10%以下罚款，暂停其6个月以内从事报关活动；情节严重的，禁止其从事报关活动。

**Article 17** Where a declaration enterprise or a declaration person fails to conduct a reasonable examination of the truthfulness of the information provided by the client, or causes the circumstances specified in Article 15 of these Implementing Regulations to occur due to negligence in work, the declaration enterprise may be imposed a fine of not more than 10% of the value of the goods, and its engagement in declaration activities shall be suspended for not more than six months; if the circumstances are serious, it or he shall be prohibited from engaging in declaration activities.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　有下列行为之一的，处货物价值5%以上30%以下罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得：  
（一）未经海关许可，擅自将海关监管货物开拆、提取、交付、发运、调换、改装、抵押、质押、留置、转让、更换标记、移作他用或者进行其他处置的；  
（二）未经海关许可，在海关监管区以外存放海关监管货物的；  
（三）经营海关监管货物的运输、储存、加工、装配、寄售、展示等业务，有关货物灭失、数量短少或者记录不真实，不能提供正当理由的；  
（四）经营保税货物的运输、储存、加工、装配、寄售、展示等业务，不依照规定办理收存、交付、结转、核销等手续，或者中止、延长、变更、转让有关合同不依照规定向海关办理手续的；  
（五）未如实向海关申报加工贸易制成品单位耗料量的；  
（六）未按照规定期限将过境、转运、通运货物运输出境，擅自留在境内的；  
（七）未按照规定期限将暂时进出口货物复运出境或者复运进境，擅自留在境内或者境外的；  
（八）有违反海关监管规定的其他行为，致使海关不能或者中断对进出口货物实施监管的。  
前款规定所涉货物属于国家限制进出口需要提交许可证件，当事人在规定期限内不能提交许可证件的，另处货物价值30%以下罚款；漏缴税款的，可以另处漏缴税款1倍以下罚款。

**Article 18** Any person who commits one of the following acts shall be imposed a fine of not less than 5% but not more than 30% of the value of the goods; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) without permission of the Customs, arbitrarily opening, removing, delivering, dispatching, exchanging, modifying, mortgaging, pledging, retaining, transferring, changing marks, diverting to other uses or otherwise disposing of goods under Customs supervision;  
(2) without permission of the Customs, storing goods under Customs supervision outside the Customs surveillance zone;  
(3) engaging in the transportation, storage, processing, assembly, consignment, exhibition or other businesses of goods under Customs supervision, where the relevant goods are lost, short in quantity or the records are untrue, and no justifiable reason can be provided;  
(4) engaging in the transportation, storage, processing, assembly, consignment, exhibition or other businesses of bonded goods, failing to go through the procedures of receipt, delivery, carry-over, verification and cancellation, etc. in accordance with the provisions, or failing to go through the formalities with the Customs in accordance with the provisions for the suspension, extension, modification or transfer of the relevant contracts;  
(5) failing to truthfully declare to the Customs the unit consumption of materials for finished products under processing trade;  
(6) failing to transport goods in transit, transshipment or through transport out of the territory within the prescribed time limit, and arbitrarily leaving them within the territory;  
(7) failing to re-export or re-import temporarily imported or exported goods within the prescribed time limit, and arbitrarily leaving them within or outside the territory;  
(8) committing other acts in violation of Customs supervision and control regulations, causing the Customs to be unable or interrupted to exercise supervision and control over import or export goods.  
Where the goods involved in the preceding paragraph are subject to the submission of license documents because they are restricted by the State for import or export, and the party fails to submit the license documents within the prescribed time limit, an additional fine of not more than 30% of the value of the goods shall be imposed; if taxes are underpaid, an additional fine of not more than one time the underpaid tax may be imposed.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　有下列行为之一的，予以警告，可以处物品价值20%以下罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得：  
（一）未经海关许可，擅自将海关尚未放行的进出境物品开拆、交付、投递、转移或者进行其他处置的；  
（二）个人运输、携带、邮寄超过合理数量的自用物品进出境未向海关申报的；  
（三）个人运输、携带、邮寄超过规定数量但仍属自用的国家限制进出境物品进出境，未向海关申报但没有以藏匿、伪装等方式逃避海关监管的；  
（四）个人运输、携带、邮寄物品进出境，申报不实的；  
（五）经海关登记准予暂时免税进境或者暂时免税出境的物品，未按照规定复带出境或者复带进境的；  
（六）未经海关批准，过境人员将其所带物品留在境内的。

**Article 19** Any person who commits one of the following acts shall be given a warning, and may be imposed a fine of not more than 20% of the value of the articles; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) without permission of the Customs, arbitrarily opening, delivering, posting, transferring or otherwise disposing of inbound or outbound articles that have not yet been released by the Customs;  
(2) an individual transporting, carrying or mailing personal articles in a quantity exceeding a reasonable amount for self-use into or out of the territory without declaring to the Customs;  
(3) an individual transporting, carrying or mailing into or out of the territory articles restricted by the State for entry or exit in a quantity exceeding the prescribed amount but still for self-use, without declaring to the Customs but not concealing or disguising such articles to evade Customs supervision;  
(4) an individual transporting, carrying or mailing articles into or out of the territory with false declarations;  
(5) failing to re-carry out or re-carry in articles that have been registered by the Customs and permitted to be temporarily exempted from duty upon entry or exit, in accordance with the provisions;  
(6) without approval of the Customs, a person in transit leaving his or her articles within the territory.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　运输、携带、邮寄国家禁止进出境的物品进出境，未向海关申报但没有以藏匿、伪装等方式逃避海关监管的，予以没收，或者责令退回，或者在海关监管下予以销毁或者进行技术处理。

**Article 20** Where anyone transports, carries or mails articles the entry or exit of which is prohibited by the State into or out of the territory, without declaring to the Customs but without concealing or disguising such articles to evade Customs supervision, such articles shall be confiscated, or ordered to be returned, or destroyed or technically treated under Customs supervision.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　有下列行为之一的，予以警告，可以处10万元以下罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得：  
（一）运输工具不经设立海关的地点进出境的；  
（二）在海关监管区停留的进出境运输工具，未经海关同意擅自驶离的；  
（三）进出境运输工具从一个设立海关的地点驶往另一个设立海关的地点，尚未办结海关手续又未经海关批准，中途改驶境外或者境内未设立海关的地点的；  
（四）进出境运输工具到达或者驶离设立海关的地点，未按照规定向海关申报、交验有关单证或者交验的单证不真实的。

**Article 21** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be given a warning and may be fined not more than 100,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) a means of transport entering or leaving the territory at a place without a customs office;  
(2) a means of transport entering or leaving the territory staying in the customs surveillance zone, departing without the consent of the customs;  
(3) a means of transport entering or leaving the territory traveling from one place with a customs office to another place with a customs office, before completing the customs formalities and without customs approval, changing its route mid-way to a place outside the territory or to a place inside the territory without a customs office;  
(4) a means of transport entering or leaving the territory arriving at or departing from a place with a customs office, failing to declare to the customs or submit relevant documents in accordance with the provisions, or submitting untrue documents.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　有下列行为之一的，予以警告，可以处5万元以下罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得：  
（一）未经海关同意，进出境运输工具擅自装卸进出境货物、物品或者上下进出境旅客的；  
（二）未经海关同意，进出境运输工具擅自兼营境内客货运输或者用于进出境运输以外的其他用途的；  
（三）未按照规定办理海关手续，进出境运输工具擅自改营境内运输的；  
（四）未按照规定期限向海关传输舱单等电子数据、传输的电子数据不准确或者未按照规定期限保存相关电子数据，影响海关监管的；  
（五）进境运输工具在进境以后向海关申报以前，出境运输工具在办结海关手续以后出境以前，不按照交通主管部门或者海关指定的路线行进的；  
（六）载运海关监管货物的船舶、汽车不按照海关指定的路线行进的；  
（七）进出境船舶和航空器，由于不可抗力被迫在未设立海关的地点停泊、降落或者在境内抛掷、起卸货物、物品，无正当理由不向附近海关报告的；  
（八）无特殊原因，未将进出境船舶、火车、航空器到达的时间、停留的地点或者更换的时间、地点事先通知海关的；  
（九）不按照规定接受海关对进出境运输工具、货物、物品进行检查、查验的。

**Article 22** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be given a warning and may be fined not more than 50,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) without the consent of the customs, a means of transport entering or leaving the territory loads or unloads inward/outward goods or articles, or embarks or disembarks inward/outward passengers;  
(2) without the consent of the customs, a means of transport entering or leaving the territory concurrently engages in domestic passenger or cargo transport or is used for purposes other than inward/outward transport;  
(3) without going through customs formalities in accordance with the provisions, a means of transport entering or leaving the territory changes to domestic transport;  
(4) failing to transmit electronic data such as cargo manifests to the customs within the prescribed time limit, transmitting inaccurate electronic data, or failing to retain relevant electronic data within the prescribed time limit, thereby affecting customs supervision;  
(5) an inward means of transport, after entering the territory but before declaring to the customs, or an outward means of transport, after completing customs formalities but before leaving the territory, does not follow the route designated by the competent transport authority or the customs;  
(6) a vessel or motor vehicle carrying goods under customs supervision does not follow the route designated by the customs;  
(7) an inward/outward vessel or aircraft, forced by force majeure to anchor or land at a place without a customs office, or to jettison or unload goods or articles within the territory, fails to report to the nearest customs office without justifiable reasons;  
(8) without special reasons, fails to notify the customs in advance of the arrival time, place of stay, or time and place of change of an inward/outward vessel, train, or aircraft;  
(9) fails to accept customs inspection or examination of inward/outward means of transport, goods, or articles in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　有下列行为之一的，予以警告，可以处3万元以下罚款：  
（一）擅自开启或者损毁海关封志的；  
（二）遗失海关制发的监管单证、手册等凭证，妨碍海关监管的；  
（三）有违反海关监管规定的其他行为，致使海关不能或者中断对进出境运输工具、物品实施监管的。

**Article 23** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be given a warning and may be fined not more than 30,000 yuan:  
(1) opening or damaging a customs seal without authorization;  
(2) losing customs-issued supervision documents, manuals, or other credentials, thereby obstructing customs supervision;  
(3) committing other acts in violation of customs supervision provisions, causing the customs to be unable or interrupted in exercising supervision over inward/outward means of transport or articles.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　伪造、变造、买卖海关单证的，处5万元以上50万元以下罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 24** Whoever forges, alters, or buys or sells customs documents shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　进出口侵犯中华人民共和国法律、行政法规保护的知识产权的货物的，没收侵权货物，并处货物价值30%以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
需要向海关申报知识产权状况，进出口货物收发货人及其代理人未按照规定向海关如实申报有关知识产权状况，或者未提交合法使用有关知识产权的证明文件的，可以处5万元以下罚款。

**Article 25** Whoever imports or exports goods that infringe upon intellectual property rights protected by the laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China shall have the infringing goods confiscated and shall also be fined not more than 30% of the value of the goods; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where the declaration of intellectual property status to the customs is required, if the consignee or consignor of import or export goods or their agent fails to truthfully declare the relevant intellectual property status to the customs in accordance with the provisions, or fails to submit documents evidencing the lawful use of the relevant intellectual property rights, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may be imposed.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　海关准予从事海关监管货物的运输、储存、加工、装配、寄售、展示等业务的企业，有下列情形之一的，责令改正，给予警告，可以暂停其6个月以内从事有关业务：  
（一）拖欠税款或者不履行纳税义务的；  
（二）损坏或者丢失海关监管货物，不能提供正当理由的；  
（三）有需要暂停其从事有关业务的其他违法行为的。

**Article 26** Where an enterprise permitted by the customs to engage in the transport, storage, processing, assembly, consignment, exhibition, or other business operations of goods under customs supervision falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, and may be suspended from engaging in the relevant business for a period of not more than six months:  
(1) being in arrears with tax payments or failing to perform tax payment obligations;  
(2) damaging or losing goods under customs supervision without being able to provide justifiable reasons;  
(3) committing other illegal acts that necessitate the suspension of its engagement in the relevant business.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　海关准予从事海关监管货物的运输、储存、加工、装配、寄售、展示等业务的企业，有下列情形之一的，海关可以撤销其注册登记：  
（一）被海关暂停从事有关业务，恢复从事有关业务后1年内再次发生本实施条例第二十六条规定情形的；  
（二）有需要撤销其注册登记的其他违法行为的。

**Article 27** Where an enterprise permitted by the customs to engage in the transport, storage, processing, assembly, consignment, exhibition, or other business operations of goods under customs supervision falls under any of the following circumstances, the customs may revoke its registration:  
(1) after being suspended by the customs from engaging in the relevant business and having resumed such business, it again commits any of the acts specified in Article 26 of these Implementing Regulations within one year;  
(2) committing other illegal acts that necessitate the revocation of its registration.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　报关企业、报关人员非法代理他人报关的，责令改正，处5万元以下罚款；情节严重的，禁止其从事报关活动。

**Article 28** Where a customs declaration enterprise or a customs declarant illegally acts as an agent for others in customs declaration, it/he/she shall be ordered to make corrections and fined not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it/he/she shall be prohibited from engaging in customs declaration activities.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　进出口货物收发货人、报关企业、报关人员向海关工作人员行贿的，由海关禁止其从事报关活动，并处10万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 29** Where a consignee or consignor of import or export goods, a customs declaration enterprise, or a customs declarant bribes a customs officer, the customs shall prohibit it/him/her from engaging in customs declaration activities and shall also impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　未经海关备案从事报关活动的，责令改正，没收违法所得，可以并处10万元以下罚款。

**Article 30** Whoever engages in customs declaration activities without having filed a record with the customs shall be ordered to make corrections, have the illegal gains confiscated, and may also be fined not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　提供虚假资料骗取海关注册登记，撤销其注册登记，并处30万元以下罚款。

**Article 31** Where anyone provides false information to fraudulently obtain Customs registration, its registration shall be revoked, and a fine of not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　法人或者其他组织有违反海关法的行为，除处罚该法人或者组织外，对其主管人员和直接责任人员予以警告，可以处5万元以下罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 32** Where a legal person or any other organization commits an act violating the Customs law, in addition to punishing the legal person or organization, a warning shall be given to its person in charge and the person directly responsible, and a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

### 第四章　对违反海关法行为的调查

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　海关发现公民、法人或者其他组织有依法应当由海关给予行政处罚的行为的，应当立案调查。

**Article 33** Where the Customs discovers that a citizen, legal person, or any other organization has committed an act for which the Customs shall impose an administrative penalty according to law, the Customs shall open a case for investigation.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　海关立案后，应当全面、客观、公正、及时地进行调查、收集证据。  
海关调查、收集证据，应当按照法律、行政法规及其他有关规定的要求办理。  
海关调查、收集证据时，海关工作人员不得少于2人，并应当向被调查人出示证件。  
调查、收集的证据涉及国家秘密、商业秘密或者个人隐私的，海关应当保守秘密。

**Article 34** After opening a case, the Customs shall conduct investigation and collect evidence in a comprehensive, objective, fair, and timely manner.  
When investigating and collecting evidence, the Customs shall handle the matter in accordance with the requirements of laws, administrative regulations, and other relevant provisions.  
When investigating and collecting evidence, there shall be no fewer than two Customs officers, and they shall present their credentials to the person under investigation.  
If the evidence investigated and collected involves State secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy, the Customs shall keep the secrets.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　海关依法检查走私嫌疑人的身体，应当在隐蔽的场所或者非检查人员的视线之外，由2名以上与被检查人同性别的海关工作人员执行。  
走私嫌疑人应当接受检查，不得阻挠。

**Article 35** When the Customs examines the body of a smuggling suspect according to law, the examination shall be conducted in a concealed place or out of the sight of non-examiners, by no fewer than two Customs officers of the same sex as the person under examination.  
The smuggling suspect shall submit to the examination and shall not obstruct it.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　海关依法检查运输工具和场所，查验货物、物品，应当制作检查、查验记录。

**Article 36** When the Customs inspects means of transport and places, or examines goods or articles according to law, it shall make inspection and examination records.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　海关依法扣留走私犯罪嫌疑人，应当制发扣留走私犯罪嫌疑人决定书。对走私犯罪嫌疑人，扣留时间不超过24小时，在特殊情况下可以延长至48小时。  
海关应当在法定扣留期限内对被扣留人进行审查。排除犯罪嫌疑或者法定扣留期限届满的，应当立即解除扣留，并制发解除扣留决定书。

**Article 37** When the Customs detains a criminal suspect of smuggling according to law, it shall issue a decision on the detention of the criminal suspect of smuggling. The period of detention for the criminal suspect of smuggling shall not exceed 24 hours, and may be extended to 48 hours under special circumstances.  
The Customs shall conduct a review of the detained person within the statutory detention period. If the suspicion of crime is eliminated or the statutory detention period expires, the detention shall be immediately lifted, and a decision to lift the detention shall be issued.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　下列货物、物品、运输工具及有关账册、单据等资料，海关可以依法扣留：  
（一）有走私嫌疑的货物、物品、运输工具；  
（二）违反海关法或者其他有关法律、行政法规的货物、物品、运输工具；  
（三）与违反海关法或者其他有关法律、行政法规的货物、物品、运输工具有牵连的账册、单据等资料；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定可以扣留的其他货物、物品、运输工具及有关账册、单据等资料。

**Article 38** The Customs may detain according to law the following goods, articles, means of transport, and relevant account books, documents, and other materials:  
(1) Goods, articles, and means of transport suspected of being involved in smuggling;  
(2) Goods, articles, and means of transport that violate the Customs Law or other relevant laws or administrative regulations;  
(3) Account books, documents, and other materials that are connected with goods, articles, or means of transport that violate the Customs Law or other relevant laws or administrative regulations;  
(4) Other goods, articles, means of transport, and relevant account books, documents, and other materials that may be detained as provided by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　有违法嫌疑的货物、物品、运输工具无法或者不便扣留的，当事人或者运输工具负责人应当向海关提供等值的担保，未提供等值担保的，海关可以扣留当事人等值的其他财产。

**Article 39** If goods, articles, or means of transport suspected of being illegal cannot be detained or are not convenient to detain, the party or the person in charge of the means of transport shall provide a guarantee of equivalent value to the Customs. If no guarantee of equivalent value is provided, the Customs may detain other property of the party of equivalent value.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　海关扣留货物、物品、运输工具以及账册、单据等资料的期限不得超过1年。因案件调查需要，经直属海关关长或者其授权的隶属海关关长批准，可以延长，延长期限不得超过1年。但复议、诉讼期间不计算在内。

**Article 40** The period of detention of goods, articles, means of transport, and account books, documents, and other materials by the Customs shall not exceed one year. If necessary for case investigation, with the approval of the director of the directly affiliated Customs or the authorized director of a subordinate Customs, the period may be extended, and the extended period shall not exceed one year. However, the period for reconsideration or litigation shall not be counted.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　有下列情形之一的，海关应当及时解除扣留：  
（一）排除违法嫌疑的；  
（二）扣留期限、延长期限届满的；  
（三）已经履行海关行政处罚决定的；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定应当解除扣留的其他情形。

**Article 41** : Customs shall promptly lift the detention in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) where the suspicion of illegality is eliminated;  
(2) where the period of detention or the extended period expires;  
(3) where the decision on Customs administrative penalty has been performed;  
(4) other circumstances where the detention shall be lifted as provided for by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　海关依法扣留货物、物品、运输工具、其他财产以及账册、单据等资料，应当制发海关扣留凭单，由海关工作人员、当事人或者其代理人、保管人、见证人签字或者盖章，并可以加施海关封志。加施海关封志的，当事人或者其代理人、保管人应当妥善保管。  
海关解除对货物、物品、运输工具、其他财产以及账册、单据等资料的扣留，或者发还等值的担保，应当制发海关解除扣留通知书、海关解除担保通知书，并由海关工作人员、当事人或者其代理人、保管人、见证人签字或者盖章。

**Article 42** : Where Customs seizes goods, articles, means of transportation, other property, and account books, documents, and other materials according to law, it shall issue a Customs seizure receipt, which shall be signed or affixed with a seal by Customs officers, the party or his/her agent, the custodian, and witnesses, and may affix a Customs seal. If a Customs seal is affixed, the party or his/her agent and the custodian shall properly keep it.  
Where Customs lifts the seizure of goods, articles, means of transportation, other property, and account books, documents, and other materials, or releases equivalent security, it shall issue a Customs notice of lifting seizure and a Customs notice of lifting security, which shall be signed or affixed with a seal by Customs officers, the party or his/her agent, the custodian, and witnesses.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　海关查问违法嫌疑人或者询问证人，应当个别进行，并告知其权利和作伪证应当承担的法律责任。违法嫌疑人、证人必须如实陈述、提供证据。  
海关查问违法嫌疑人或者询问证人应当制作笔录，并当场交其辨认，没有异议的，立即签字确认；有异议的，予以更正后签字确认。  
严禁刑讯逼供或者以威胁、引诱、欺骗等非法手段收集证据。  
海关查问违法嫌疑人，可以到违法嫌疑人的所在单位或者住处进行，也可以要求其到海关或者海关指定的地点进行。

**Article 43** : Customs shall interrogate a suspect of illegality or question a witness individually, and shall inform him/her of his/her rights and the legal liability for giving false testimony. The suspect of illegality or the witness must truthfully make statements and provide evidence.  
When interrogating a suspect of illegality or questioning a witness, Customs shall make a record of the interrogation or questioning, and present it to the person for examination on the spot; if there is no objection, the person shall immediately sign for confirmation; if there is an objection, the record shall be corrected and then signed for confirmation.  
It is strictly prohibited to collect evidence through torture or by illegal means such as threats, enticement, or deception.  
Customs may interrogate a suspect of illegality at the suspect's place of work or residence, or may require the suspect to come to Customs or a place designated by Customs.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　海关收集的物证、书证应当是原物、原件。收集原物、原件确有困难的，可以拍摄、复制，并可以指定或者委托有关单位或者个人对原物、原件予以妥善保管。  
海关收集物证、书证，应当开列清单，注明收集的日期，由有关单位或者个人确认后签字或者盖章。  
海关收集电子数据或者录音、录像等视听资料，应当收集原始载体。收集原始载体确有困难的，可以收集复制件，注明制作方法、制作时间、制作人等，并由有关单位或者个人确认后签字或者盖章。

**Article 44** : The physical evidence and documentary evidence collected by Customs shall be the original objects and original documents. If it is truly difficult to collect the original objects or documents, photographs or copies may be taken or made, and the relevant units or individuals may be designated or entrusted to properly keep the original objects or documents.  
When collecting physical evidence or documentary evidence, Customs shall make a list, indicate the date of collection, and obtain the signature or seal of the relevant unit or individual after confirmation.  
When collecting electronic data or audio-visual materials such as audio recordings or video recordings, Customs shall collect the original carrier. If it is truly difficult to collect the original carrier, copies may be collected, with the method of production, time of production, producer, etc. indicated, and the relevant unit or individual shall sign or affix a seal after confirmation.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　根据案件调查需要，海关可以对有关货物、物品进行取样化验、鉴定。  
海关提取样品时，当事人或者其代理人应当到场；当事人或者其代理人未到场的，海关应当邀请见证人到场。提取的样品，海关应当予以加封，并由海关工作人员及当事人或者其代理人、见证人确认后签字或者盖章。  
化验、鉴定应当交由海关化验鉴定机构或者委托国家认可的其他机构进行。  
化验人、鉴定人进行化验、鉴定后，应当出具化验报告、鉴定结论，并签字或者盖章。

**Article 45** : Based on the needs of case investigation, Customs may take samples of relevant goods or articles for testing or expert evaluation.  
When Customs takes samples, the party or his/her agent shall be present; if the party or his/her agent is not present, Customs shall invite witnesses to be present. Collected samples shall be sealed by Customs, and Customs officers, the party or his/her agent, and the witnesses shall sign or affix seals after confirmation.  
Testing or expert evaluation shall be conducted by Customs testing and appraisal institutions or entrusted to other institutions recognized by the State.  
After conducting testing or expert evaluation, the tester or appraiser shall issue a testing report or appraisal conclusion, and sign or affix a seal.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　根据海关法有关规定，海关可以查询案件涉嫌单位和涉嫌人员在金融机构、邮政企业的存款、汇款。  
海关查询案件涉嫌单位和涉嫌人员在金融机构、邮政企业的存款、汇款，应当出示海关协助查询通知书。

**Article 46** : According to the relevant provisions of the Customs Law, Customs may inquire into the deposits and remittances of units and individuals suspected of being involved in a case in financial institutions or postal enterprises.  
When Customs inquires into the deposits and remittances of units and individuals suspected of being involved in a case in financial institutions or postal enterprises, it shall present the Customs assistance inquiry notice.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　海关依法扣留的货物、物品、运输工具，在人民法院判决或者海关行政处罚决定作出之前，不得处理。但是，危险品或者鲜活、易腐、易烂、易失效、易变质等不宜长期保存的货物、物品以及所有人申请先行变卖的货物、物品、运输工具，经直属海关关长或者其授权的隶属海关关长批准，可以先行依法变卖，变卖所得价款由海关保存，并通知其所有人。

**Article 47** : Goods, articles, and means of transportation detained by Customs according to law shall not be disposed of before a decision of a People's Court or a decision on Customs administrative penalty is made. However, dangerous goods, or goods or articles that are fresh, perishable, easily rotted, easily invalidated, easily deteriorated, or otherwise not suitable for long-term storage, as well as goods, articles, or means of transportation that the owner applies to sell in advance, may be sold in advance according to law with the approval of the director of the directly subordinate Customs or the authorized director of a subordinate Customs. The proceeds from the sale shall be kept by Customs, and the owner shall be notified.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　当事人有权根据海关法的规定要求海关工作人员回避。

**Article 48** : A party shall have the right to request the withdrawal of a Customs officer in accordance with the provisions of the Customs Law.

### 第五章　海关行政处罚的决定和执行

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　海关作出暂停从事有关业务、撤销海关注册登记、禁止从事报关活动、对公民处1万元以上罚款、对法人或者其他组织处10万元以上罚款、没收有关货物、物品、走私运输工具等行政处罚决定之前，应当告知当事人有要求举行听证的权利；当事人要求听证的，海关应当组织听证。  
海关行政处罚听证办法由海关总署制定。

**Article 49** : Before making a decision on an administrative penalty such as suspending relevant business, revoking Customs registration, prohibiting engaging in customs declaration activities, imposing a fine of more than 10,000 yuan on a citizen, imposing a fine of more than 100,000 yuan on a legal person or other organization, or confiscating relevant goods, articles, or smuggling means of transportation, Customs shall inform the party of the right to request a hearing; if the party requests a hearing, Customs shall organize a hearing.  
The measures for hearings on Customs administrative penalties shall be formulated by the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　案件调查终结，海关关长应当对调查结果进行审查，根据不同情况，依法作出决定。  
对情节复杂或者重大违法行为给予较重的行政处罚，应当由海关案件审理委员会集体讨论决定。

**Article 50** : After the investigation of a case is concluded, the director of Customs shall review the investigation results and, based on the different circumstances, make a decision according to law.  
For complicated cases or serious illegal acts for which a heavier administrative penalty is to be imposed, the decision shall be made after collective discussion by the Customs case review committee.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　同一当事人实施了走私和违反海关监管规定的行为且二者之间有因果关系的，依照本实施条例对走私行为的规定从重处罚，对其违反海关监管规定的行为不再另行处罚。  
同一当事人就同一批货物、物品分别实施了2个以上违反海关监管规定的行为且二者之间有因果关系的，依照本实施条例分别规定的处罚幅度，择其重者处罚。

**Article 51** Where the same party has committed both smuggling and violation of Customs surveillance regulations and there is a causal relationship between the two, the party shall be given a severe punishment in accordance with the provisions on smuggling under these Implementing Regulations, and no separate punishment shall be imposed for the violation of Customs surveillance regulations.  
Where the same party has separately committed two or more acts in violation of Customs surveillance regulations in respect of the same batch of goods or articles and there is a causal relationship between such acts, the punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the respective punishment ranges stipulated in these Implementing Regulations, and the heavier punishment shall be selected.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　对2个以上当事人共同实施的违法行为，应当区别情节及责任，分别给予处罚。

**Article 52** Where an illegal act is committed jointly by two or more parties, punishments shall be imposed respectively in light of the circumstances and liabilities of each party.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　有下列情形之一的，应当从重处罚：  
（一）因走私被判处刑罚或者被海关行政处罚后在2年内又实施走私行为的；  
（二）因违反海关监管规定被海关行政处罚后在1年内又实施同一违反海关监管规定的行为的；  
（三）有其他依法应当从重处罚的情形的。

**Article 53** A severe punishment shall be imposed under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) having been sentenced to a criminal penalty for smuggling or having been subjected to a Customs administrative penalty and committing smuggling again within two years;  
(2) having been subjected to a Customs administrative penalty for violating Customs surveillance regulations and committing the same violation of Customs surveillance regulations again within one year;  
(3) other circumstances under which a severe punishment shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　海关对当事人违反海关法的行为依法给予行政处罚的，应当制作行政处罚决定书。  
对同一当事人实施的2个以上违反海关法的行为，可以制发1份行政处罚决定书。  
对2个以上当事人分别实施的违反海关法的行为，应当分别制发行政处罚决定书。  
对2个以上当事人共同实施的违反海关法的行为，应当制发1份行政处罚决定书，区别情况对各当事人分别予以处罚，但需另案处理的除外。

**Article 54** Where the Customs imposes an administrative penalty according to law on a party for an act violating the Customs Law, it shall make a written decision on administrative penalty.  
For two or more acts violating the Customs Law committed by the same party, one written decision on administrative penalty may be issued.  
For acts violating the Customs Law committed separately by two or more parties, separate written decisions on administrative penalty shall be issued.  
For an act violating the Customs Law committed jointly by two or more parties, one written decision on administrative penalty shall be issued, and each party shall be punished respectively based on the specific circumstances, unless separate case handling is required.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　行政处罚决定书应当依照有关法律规定送达当事人。  
依法予以公告送达的，海关应当将行政处罚决定书的正本张贴在海关公告栏内，并在报纸上刊登公告。

**Article 55** The written decision on administrative penalty shall be served on the party in accordance with relevant legal provisions.  
Where service by announcement is carried out according to law, the Customs shall post the original copy of the written decision on administrative penalty on the Customs bulletin board and publish an announcement in a newspaper.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　海关作出没收货物、物品、走私运输工具的行政处罚决定，有关货物、物品、走私运输工具无法或者不便没收的，海关应当追缴上述货物、物品、走私运输工具的等值价款。

**Article 56** Where the Customs makes an administrative penalty decision to confiscate goods, articles, or smuggling means of transport, but such goods, articles, or smuggling means of transport cannot be confiscated or are inconvenient to confiscate, the Customs shall recover the equivalent value of the aforesaid goods, articles, or smuggling means of transport.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　法人或者其他组织实施违反海关法的行为后，有合并、分立或者其他资产重组情形的，海关应当以原法人、组织作为当事人。  
对原法人、组织处以罚款、没收违法所得或者依法追缴货物、物品、走私运输工具的等值价款的，应当以承受其权利义务的法人、组织作为被执行人。

**Article 57** Where, after a legal person or other organization has committed an act violating the Customs Law, there is a merger, division, or other asset restructuring, the Customs shall take the original legal person or organization as the party.  
Where a fine, confiscation of illegal gains, or recovery of the equivalent value of goods, articles, or smuggling means of transport according to law is imposed on the original legal person or organization, the legal person or organization that succeeds to its rights and obligations shall be the person subject to enforcement.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　罚款、违法所得和依法追缴的货物、物品、走私运输工具的等值价款，应当在海关行政处罚决定规定的期限内缴清。  
当事人按期履行行政处罚决定、办结海关手续的，海关应当及时解除其担保。

**Article 58** The fine, illegal gains, and the equivalent value of goods, articles, or smuggling means of transport recovered according to law shall be paid in full within the time limit specified in the Customs administrative penalty decision.  
Where the party performs the administrative penalty decision on time and completes the Customs formalities, the Customs shall promptly release the guarantee provided by the party.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　受海关处罚的当事人或者其法定代表人、主要负责人应当在出境前缴清罚款、违法所得和依法追缴的货物、物品、走私运输工具的等值价款。在出境前未缴清上述款项的，应当向海关提供相当于上述款项的担保。未提供担保，当事人是自然人的，海关可以通知出境管理机关阻止其出境；当事人是法人或者其他组织的，海关可以通知出境管理机关阻止其法定代表人或者主要负责人出境。

**Article 59** A party subjected to Customs punishment, or its legal representative or principal responsible person, shall pay in full the fine, illegal gains, and the equivalent value of goods, articles, or smuggling means of transport recovered according to law before leaving the country. If the aforesaid amounts are not paid in full before leaving the country, a guarantee equivalent to the aforesaid amounts shall be provided to the Customs. If no guarantee is provided, where the party is a natural person, the Customs may notify the exit administration authority to prevent him or her from leaving the country; where the party is a legal person or other organization, the Customs may notify the exit administration authority to prevent its legal representative or principal responsible person from leaving the country.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　当事人逾期不履行行政处罚决定的，海关可以采取下列措施：  
（一）到期不缴纳罚款的，每日按罚款数额的3%加处罚款；  
（二）根据海关法规定，将扣留的货物、物品、运输工具变价抵缴，或者以当事人提供的担保抵缴；  
（三）申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 60** Where a party fails to perform the administrative penalty decision within the time limit, the Customs may take the following measures:  
(1) if the fine is not paid when due, an additional fine of 3% of the fine amount shall be imposed for each day of delay;  
(2) in accordance with the Customs Law, convert the detained goods, articles, or means of transport into money to offset the penalty, or offset the penalty with the guarantee provided by the party;  
(3) apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　当事人确有经济困难，申请延期或者分期缴纳罚款的，经海关批准，可以暂缓或者分期缴纳罚款。  
当事人申请延期或者分期缴纳罚款的，应当以书面形式提出，海关收到申请后，应当在10个工作日内作出决定，并通知申请人。海关同意当事人暂缓或者分期缴纳的，应当及时通知收缴罚款的机构。

**Article 61** Where a party truly has financial difficulties and applies for an extension or installment payment of the fine, with the approval of the Customs, the party may defer or pay the fine in installments.  
Where a party applies for an extension or installment payment of the fine, the application shall be made in writing. After receiving the application, the Customs shall make a decision within 10 working days and notify the applicant. Where the Customs approves the deferment or installment payment, it shall promptly notify the institution collecting the fine.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　有下列情形之一的，有关货物、物品、违法所得、运输工具、特制设备由海关予以收缴：  
（一）依照[《中华人民共和国行政处罚法》第三十条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政处罚法.md#art-30)、第三十一条规定不予行政处罚的当事人携带、邮寄国家禁止进出境的货物、物品进出境的；  
（二）散发性邮寄国家禁止、限制进出境的物品进出境或者携带数量零星的国家禁止进出境的物品进出境，依法可以不予行政处罚的；  
（三）依法应当没收的货物、物品、违法所得、走私运输工具、特制设备，在海关作出行政处罚决定前，作为当事人的自然人死亡或者作为当事人的法人、其他组织终止，且无权利义务承受人的；  
（四）走私违法事实基本清楚，但当事人无法查清，自海关公告之日起满3个月的；  
（五）有违反法律、行政法规，应当予以收缴的其他情形的。  
海关收缴前款规定的货物、物品、违法所得、运输工具、特制设备，应当制发清单，由被收缴人或者其代理人、见证人签字或者盖章。被收缴人无法查清且无见证人的，应当予以公告。

**Article 62** In any of the following circumstances, the relevant goods, articles, illegal gains, means of transport, or specially designed equipment shall be confiscated by the Customs:  
(1) where a party who is not subject to an administrative penalty in accordance with Articles 30 and 31 of the Administrative Penalty Law of the People's Republic of China carries or mails goods or articles that are prohibited from entering or leaving the country into or out of the country;  
(2) where articles that are prohibited or restricted from entering or leaving the country are mailed into or out of the country in a sporadic manner or a small quantity of articles that are prohibited from entering or leaving the country are carried into or out of the country, and no administrative penalty may be imposed according to law;  
(3) where goods, articles, illegal gains, smuggling means of transport, or specially designed equipment that shall be confiscated according to law, but before the Customs makes a decision on administrative penalty, the natural person who is the party dies or the legal person or other organization that is the party terminates, and there is no successor of rights and obligations;  
(4) where the facts of the smuggling violation are basically clear, but the party cannot be identified, and three months have elapsed from the date of the Customs announcement;  
(5) where there are other circumstances that violate laws or administrative regulations and shall be subject to confiscation.  
For the confiscation of goods, articles, illegal gains, means of transport, or specially designed equipment as specified in the preceding paragraph, the Customs shall prepare a list and have it signed or sealed by the person from whom the items are confiscated or his/her agent or witness. If the person from whom the items are confiscated cannot be identified and there is no witness, an announcement shall be made.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　人民法院判决没收的走私货物、物品、违法所得、走私运输工具、特制设备，或者海关决定没收、收缴的货物、物品、违法所得、走私运输工具、特制设备，由海关依法统一处理，所得价款和海关收缴的罚款，全部上缴中央国库。

**Article 63** Smuggled goods, articles, illegal gains, smuggling means of transport, or specially designed equipment that are ordered to be confiscated by a People's Court, or goods, articles, illegal gains, smuggling means of transport, or specially designed equipment that are decided to be confiscated or forfeited by the Customs, shall be uniformly disposed of by the Customs according to law. The proceeds therefrom and the fines collected by the Customs shall be turned over to the central treasury in full.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　本实施条例下列用语的含义是：  
“设立海关的地点”，指海关在港口、车站、机场、国界孔道、国际邮件互换局（交换站）等海关监管区设立的卡口，海关在保税区、出口加工区等海关特殊监管区域设立的卡口，以及海关在海上设立的中途监管站。  
“许可证件”，指依照国家有关规定，当事人应当事先申领，并由国家有关主管部门颁发的准予进口或者出口的证明、文件。  
“合法证明”，指船舶及所载人员依照国家有关规定或者依照国际运输惯例所必须持有的证明其运输、携带、收购、贩卖所载货物、物品真实、合法、有效的商业单证、运输单证及其他有关证明、文件。  
“物品”，指个人以运输、携带等方式进出境的行李物品、邮寄进出境的物品，包括货币、金银等。超出自用、合理数量的，视为货物。  
“自用”，指旅客或者收件人本人自用、馈赠亲友而非为出售或者出租。  
“合理数量”，指海关根据旅客或者收件人的情况、旅行目的和居留时间所确定的正常数量。  
“货物价值”，指进出口货物的完税价格、关税、进口环节海关代征税之和。  
“物品价值”，指进出境物品的完税价格、进口税之和。  
“应纳税款”，指进出口货物、物品应当缴纳的进出口关税、进口环节海关代征税之和。  
“专门用于走私的运输工具”，指专为走私而制造、改造、购买的运输工具。  
“以上”、“以下”、“以内”、“届满”，均包括本数在内。

**Article 64** For the purposes of these Implementing Regulations, the meanings of the following terms are:  
"Place where Customs is established" means the checkpoints set up by the Customs in Customs surveillance zones such as ports, railway stations, airports, border passages, international mail exchange offices (exchange stations), the checkpoints set up by the Customs in special Customs supervision areas such as bonded areas and export processing zones, and the mid-way surveillance stations set up by the Customs at sea.  
"License or permit" means the certificate or document issued by the relevant competent authority of the State which a party shall obtain in advance in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State for import or export.  
"Legal certificate" means the commercial documents, transport documents and other relevant certificates and documents that a vessel and the persons on board shall hold in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State or international transport practices to prove that the goods or articles carried, transported, purchased, or sold are genuine, legal, and valid.  
"Articles" means luggage articles carried or transported into or out of the country by individuals, and articles mailed into or out of the country, including currency, gold, silver, etc. If the quantity exceeds the amount for personal use or reasonable quantity, they shall be deemed as goods.  
"For personal use" means for the passenger or consignee's own use or as gifts to relatives or friends, not for sale or lease.  
"Reasonable quantity" means the normal quantity determined by the Customs based on the passenger or consignee's circumstances, purpose of travel, and length of stay.  
"Value of goods" means the sum of the dutiable value, customs duties, and taxes collected by the Customs on behalf of the State at the time of import or export.  
"Value of articles" means the sum of the dutiable value and import duties of incoming or outgoing articles.  
"Taxes payable" means the sum of the import or export duties and taxes collected by the Customs on behalf of the State that shall be paid for imported or exported goods or articles.  
"Means of transport specially used for smuggling" means means of transport manufactured, modified, or purchased specifically for smuggling.  
The terms "or more", "or less", "within", and "upon expiry" all include the number in question.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　海关对外国人、无国籍人、外国企业或者其他组织给予行政处罚的，适用本实施条例。

**Article 65** Where the Customs imposes an administrative penalty on a foreign national, stateless person, foreign enterprise or other organization, these Implementing Regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家禁止或者限制进出口的货物目录，由国务院对外贸易主管部门依照《中华人民共和国对外贸易法》的规定办理；国家禁止或者限制进出境的物品目录，由海关总署公布。

**Article 66** The catalogue of goods whose import or export is prohibited or restricted by the State shall be handled by the competent department of foreign trade under the State Council in accordance with the provisions of the Foreign Trade Law of the People's Republic of China. The catalogue of articles whose entry or exit is prohibited or restricted by the State shall be published by the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　依照海关规章给予行政处罚的，应当遵守本实施条例规定的程序。

**Article 67** Where an administrative penalty is imposed in accordance with the Customs regulations, the procedures prescribed in these Implementing Regulations shall be followed.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　本实施条例自2004年11月1日起施行。1993年2月17日国务院批准修订、1993年4月1日海关总署发布的《中华人民共和国海关法行政处罚实施细则》同时废止。

**Article 68** These Implementing Regulations shall come into force as of November 1, 2004. The Detailed Rules for the Implementation of Administrative Penalties under the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, approved by the State Council for revision on February 17, 1993 and promulgated by the General Administration of Customs on April 1, 1993, shall be abolished simultaneously.
