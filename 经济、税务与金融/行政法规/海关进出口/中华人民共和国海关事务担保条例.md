# 中华人民共和国海关事务担保条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2018-03-19  
**生效日期**：2011-01-01

> (2010年9月14日中华人民共和国国务院令第581号公布　根据2018年3月19日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了规范海关事务担保，提高通关效率，保障海关监督管理，根据《中华人民共和国海关法》及其他有关法律的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　当事人向海关申请提供担保，承诺履行法律义务，海关为当事人办理海关事务担保，适用本条例。 第三条　海关事务担保应当遵循合法、诚实信用、权责统一的原则。 第四条　有下列情形之一的，当事人可以在办结海关手续前向海关申请提供担保，要求提前放行货物： (一)进出口货物的商品归类、完税价格、原产地尚未确定的； (二)有效报关单证尚未提供的； (三)在纳税期限内税款尚未缴纳的； (四)滞报金尚未缴纳的； (五)其他海关手续尚未办结的。 国家对进出境货物、物品有限制性规定，应当提供许可证件而不能提供的，以及法律、行政法规规定不得担保的其他情形，海关不予办理担保放行。 第五条　当事人申请办理下列特定海关业务的，按照海关规定提供担保： (一)运输企业承担来往内地与港澳公路货物运输、承担海关监管货物境内公路运输的； (二)货物、物品暂时进出境的； (三)货物进境修理和出境加工的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范海关事务担保，提高通关效率，保障海关监督管理，根据《中华人民共和国海关法》及其他有关法律的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China and the provisions of other relevant laws for the purpose of regulating customs security, improving customs clearance efficiency, and ensuring customs supervision and administration.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人向海关申请提供担保，承诺履行法律义务，海关为当事人办理海关事务担保，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply where a party applies to the Customs for providing security and undertakes to perform legal obligations, and the Customs handles customs security for such party.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　海关事务担保应当遵循合法、诚实信用、权责统一的原则。

**Article 3** Customs security shall follow the principles of legality, good faith, and unity of rights and responsibilities.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　有下列情形之一的，当事人可以在办结海关手续前向海关申请提供担保，要求提前放行货物：  
(一)进出口货物的商品归类、完税价格、原产地尚未确定的；  
(二)有效报关单证尚未提供的；  
(三)在纳税期限内税款尚未缴纳的；  
(四)滞报金尚未缴纳的；  
(五)其他海关手续尚未办结的。  
国家对进出境货物、物品有限制性规定，应当提供许可证件而不能提供的，以及法律、行政法规规定不得担保的其他情形，海关不予办理担保放行。

**Article 4** In any of the following circumstances, a party may apply to the Customs for providing security before completing customs formalities and request early release of the goods:  
(1) The classification, dutiable value, or origin of import or export goods has not yet been determined;  
(2) Valid customs declaration documents have not yet been provided;  
(3) The tax has not yet been paid within the tax payment period;  
(4) The fee for delayed declaration has not yet been paid;  
(5) Other customs formalities have not yet been completed.  
Where the State has restrictive provisions on entry and exit goods and articles, and the party is unable to provide the required license documents, or other circumstances where security is not permitted by laws and administrative regulations, the Customs shall not handle the release with security.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　当事人申请办理下列特定海关业务的，按照海关规定提供担保：  
(一)运输企业承担来往内地与港澳公路货物运输、承担海关监管货物境内公路运输的；  
(二)货物、物品暂时进出境的；  
(三)货物进境修理和出境加工的；  
(四)租赁货物进口的；  
(五)货物和运输工具过境的；  
(六)将海关监管货物暂时存放在海关监管区外的；  
(七)将海关监管货物向金融机构抵押的；  
(八)为保税货物办理有关海关业务的。  
当事人不提供或者提供的担保不符合规定的，海关不予办理前款所列特定海关业务。

**Article 5** Where a party applies to handle the following specific customs business, it shall provide security in accordance with the Customs provisions:  
(1) A transportation enterprise undertakes road cargo transportation between the mainland and Hong Kong/Macau, or undertakes domestic road transportation of goods under customs supervision;  
(2) Goods or articles are temporarily entering or leaving the territory;  
(3) Goods enter the territory for repair or leave the territory for processing;  
(4) Leased goods are imported;  
(5) Goods and means of transport are in transit;  
(6) Goods under customs supervision are temporarily stored outside the customs surveillance zone;  
(7) Goods under customs supervision are mortgaged to financial institutions;  
(8) Handling relevant customs business for bonded goods.  
If the party fails to provide security or the security provided does not comply with the requirements, the Customs shall not handle the specific customs business listed in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　进出口货物的纳税义务人在规定的纳税期限内有明显的转移、藏匿其应税货物以及其他财产迹象的，海关可以责令纳税义务人提供担保；纳税义务人不能提供担保的，海关依法采取税收保全措施。

**Article 6** Where the taxpayer of import or export goods apparently transfers or conceals its dutiable goods or other properties within the prescribed tax payment period, the Customs may order the taxpayer to provide security; if the taxpayer fails to provide security, the Customs shall take tax preservation measures according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　有违法嫌疑的货物、物品、运输工具应当或者已经被海关依法扣留、封存的，当事人可以向海关提供担保，申请免予或者解除扣留、封存。  
有违法嫌疑的货物、物品、运输工具无法或者不便扣留的，当事人或者运输工具负责人应当向海关提供等值的担保；未提供等值担保的，海关可以扣留当事人等值的其他财产。  
有违法嫌疑的货物、物品、运输工具属于禁止进出境，或者必须以原物作为证据，或者依法应当予以没收的，海关不予办理担保。

**Article 7** Where goods, articles, or means of transport suspected of being illegal have been or should be detained or sealed up according to law by the Customs, the party may provide security to the Customs and apply for exemption from or release of detention or sealing up.  
If the goods, articles, or means of transport suspected of being illegal cannot be or are inconvenient to be detained, the party or the person in charge of the means of transport shall provide equivalent security to the Customs; if equivalent security is not provided, the Customs may detain other equivalent property of the party.  
If the goods, articles, or means of transport suspected of being illegal are prohibited from entry or exit, or must be used as evidence in original form, or shall be confiscated according to law, the Customs shall not handle the security.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　法人、其他组织受到海关处罚，在罚款、违法所得或者依法应当追缴的货物、物品、走私运输工具的等值价款未缴清前，其法定代表人、主要负责人出境的，应当向海关提供担保；未提供担保的，海关可以通知出境管理机关阻止其法定代表人、主要负责人出境。  
受海关处罚的自然人出境的，适用前款规定。

**Article 8** Where a legal person or other organization is subject to Customs penalty, and before the fine, illegal gains, or the equivalent value of goods, articles, or smuggling means of transport that shall be recovered according to law is fully paid, its legal representative or principal responsible person leaves the territory, the said person shall provide security to the Customs; if security is not provided, the Customs may notify the exit administration authority to prevent the legal representative or principal responsible person from leaving the territory.  
Where a natural person subject to Customs penalty leaves the territory, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　进口已采取临时反倾销措施、临时反补贴措施的货物应当提供担保的，或者进出口货物收发货人、知识产权权利人申请办理知识产权海关保护相关事务等，依照本条例的规定办理海关事务担保。法律、行政法规有特别规定的，从其规定。

**Article 9** Where security shall be provided for the import of goods subject to provisional anti-dumping or countervailing measures, or where the consignee or consignor of import or export goods or the holder of intellectual property rights applies to handle matters related to intellectual property customs protection, etc., customs security shall be handled in accordance with these Regulations. If there are special provisions in laws or administrative regulations, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　按照海关总署的规定经海关认定的高级认证企业可以申请免除担保，并按照海关规定办理有关手续。

**Article 10** An advanced certified enterprise recognized by the Customs in accordance with the provisions of the General Administration of Customs may apply for exemption from security and shall handle relevant procedures in accordance with the Customs provisions.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　当事人在一定期限内多次办理同一类海关事务的，可以向海关申请提供总担保。海关接受总担保的，当事人办理该类海关事务，不再单独提供担保。  
总担保的适用范围、担保金额、担保期限、终止情形等由海关总署规定。

**Article 11** Where a party repeatedly handles the same type of customs matters within a certain period, it may apply to the Customs for providing a master security. If the Customs accepts the master security, the party shall not provide separate security for handling such customs matters.  
The scope of application, security amount, term of validity, and circumstances for termination of the master security shall be prescribed by the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　当事人可以以海关依法认可的财产、权利提供担保，担保财产、权利的具体范围由海关总署规定。

**Article 12** A party may provide security with property or rights recognized by the Customs according to law, and the specific scope of such property or rights shall be prescribed by the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　当事人以保函向海关提供担保的，保函应当以海关为受益人，并且载明下列事项：  
(一)担保人、被担保人的基本情况；  
(二)被担保的法律义务；  
(三)担保金额；  
(四)担保期限；  
(五)担保责任；  
(六)需要说明的其他事项。  
担保人应当在保函上加盖印章，并注明日期。

**Article 13** Where a party provides security to the Customs by a letter of guarantee, the letter of guarantee shall take the Customs as the beneficiary and shall contain the following items:  
(1) Basic information of the guarantor and the guaranteed party;  
(2) The legal obligation being guaranteed;  
(3) The amount of security;  
(4) The term of validity of the security;  
(5) The liability under the security;  
(6) Other matters requiring explanation.  
The guarantor shall affix its seal to the letter of guarantee and indicate the date.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　当事人提供的担保应当与其需要履行的法律义务相当，除[本条例第七条](#art-7)第二款规定的情形外，担保金额按照下列标准确定：  
(一)为提前放行货物提供的担保，担保金额不得超过可能承担的最高税款总额；  
(二)为办理特定海关业务提供的担保，担保金额不得超过可能承担的最高税款总额或者海关总署规定的金额；  
(三)因有明显的转移、藏匿应税货物以及其他财产迹象被责令提供的担保，担保金额不得超过可能承担的最高税款总额；  
(四)为有关货物、物品、运输工具免予或者解除扣留、封存提供的担保，担保金额不得超过该货物、物品、运输工具的等值价款；  
(五)为罚款、违法所得或者依法应当追缴的货物、物品、走私运输工具的等值价款未缴清前出境提供的担保，担保金额应当相当于罚款、违法所得数额或者依法应当追缴的货物、物品、走私运输工具的等值价款。

**Article 14** The security provided by a party shall be commensurate with the legal obligation it needs to perform. Except for the circumstances provided in the second paragraph of Article 7 of these Regulations, the amount of security shall be determined according to the following standards:  
(1) For security provided for early release of goods, the amount shall not exceed the maximum total tax that may be payable;  
(2) For security provided for handling specific customs business, the amount shall not exceed the maximum total tax that may be payable or the amount prescribed by the General Administration of Customs;  
(3) For security ordered to be provided due to apparent signs of transfer or concealment of dutiable goods or other properties, the amount shall not exceed the maximum total tax that may be payable;  
(4) For security provided for exemption from or release of detention or sealing up of relevant goods, articles, or means of transport, the amount shall not exceed the equivalent value of such goods, articles, or means of transport;  
(5) For security provided for leaving the territory before the fine, illegal gains, or the equivalent value of goods, articles, or smuggling means of transport that shall be recovered according to law is fully paid, the amount shall be equivalent to the amount of the fine or illegal gains, or the equivalent value of the goods, articles, or smuggling means of transport that shall be recovered according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　办理担保，当事人应当提交书面申请以及真实、合法、有效的财产、权利凭证和身份或者资格证明等材料。

**Article 15** To handle security, the party shall submit a written application and authentic, lawful, and valid certificates of property or rights, as well as identity or qualification documents.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　海关应当自收到当事人提交的材料之日起5个工作日内对相关财产、权利等进行审核，并决定是否接受担保。当事人申请办理总担保的，海关应当在10个工作日内审核并决定是否接受担保。  
符合规定的担保，自海关决定接受之日起生效。对不符合规定的担保，海关应当书面通知当事人不予接受，并说明理由。

**Article 16** The Customs shall, within 5 working days from the date of receipt of the materials submitted by the party, review the relevant property or rights and decide whether to accept the security. Where the party applies for a master security, the Customs shall review and decide whether to accept the security within 10 working days.  
Security that complies with the provisions shall take effect from the date the Customs decides to accept it. For security that does not comply with the provisions, the Customs shall notify the party in writing of non-acceptance and state the reasons.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　被担保人履行法律义务期限届满前，担保人和被担保人因特殊原因要求变更担保内容的，应当向接受担保的海关提交书面申请以及有关证明材料。海关应当自收到当事人提交的材料之日起5个工作日内作出是否同意变更的决定，并书面通知当事人，不同意变更的，应当说明理由。

**Article 17** Where before the expiry of the period for the guaranteed party to perform the legal obligation, the guarantor and the guaranteed party, due to special reasons, request to change the content of the security, they shall submit a written application and relevant evidentiary materials to the Customs that accepted the security. The Customs shall, within 5 working days from the date of receipt of the materials submitted by the party, decide whether to approve the change and notify the party in writing; if it does not approve the change, it shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　被担保人在规定的期限内未履行有关法律义务的，海关可以依法从担保财产、权利中抵缴。当事人以保函提供担保的，海关可以直接要求承担连带责任的担保人履行担保责任。  
担保人履行担保责任的，不免除被担保人办理有关海关手续的义务。海关应当及时为被担保人办理有关海关手续。

**Article 18** Where the guaranteed party fails to perform the relevant legal obligation within the prescribed period, the Customs may, according to law, satisfy the obligation from the security property or rights. Where the party provides security by a letter of guarantee, the Customs may directly require the guarantor that bears joint and several liability to perform the security liability.  
The performance of the security liability by the guarantor shall not exempt the guaranteed party from the obligation to handle relevant customs formalities. The Customs shall promptly handle the relevant customs formalities for the guaranteed party.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　担保财产、权利不足以抵偿被担保人有关法律义务的，海关应当书面通知被担保人另行提供担保或者履行法律义务。

**Article 19** Where the security property or rights are insufficient to satisfy the relevant legal obligation of the guaranteed party, the Customs shall notify the guaranteed party in writing to provide additional security or perform the legal obligation.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　有下列情形之一的，海关应当书面通知当事人办理担保财产、权利退还手续：  
(一)当事人已经履行有关法律义务的；  
(二)当事人不再从事特定海关业务的；  
(三)担保财产、权利被海关采取抵缴措施后仍有剩余的；  
(四)其他需要退还的情形。

**Article 20** In any of the following circumstances, the Customs shall notify the party in writing to handle the return procedures for the security property or rights:  
(1) The party has performed the relevant legal obligation;  
(2) The party no longer engages in specific customs business;  
(3) There is a surplus after the Customs takes satisfaction measures from the security property or rights;  
(4) Other circumstances requiring return.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　自海关要求办理担保财产、权利退还手续的书面通知送达之日起3个月内，当事人无正当理由未办理退还手续的，海关应当发布公告。  
自海关公告发布之日起1年内，当事人仍未办理退还手续的，海关应当将担保财产、权利依法变卖或者兑付后，上缴国库。

**Article 21** If, within 3 months from the date of service of the written notice from the Customs requiring the party to handle the return procedures for the security property or rights, the party fails to handle the return procedures without justifiable reasons, the Customs shall issue a public announcement.  
If, within 1 year from the date of the announcement by the Customs, the party still fails to handle the return procedures, the Customs shall, after selling or cashing the security property or rights according to law, turn the proceeds over to the State Treasury.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　海关履行职责，金融机构等有关单位应当依法予以协助。

**Article 22** When the Customs performs its duties, financial institutions and other relevant entities shall provide assistance according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　担保人、被担保人违反本条例，使用欺骗、隐瞒等手段提供担保的，由海关责令其继续履行法律义务，处5000元以上50000元以下的罚款；情节严重的，可以暂停被担保人从事有关海关业务或者撤销其从事有关海关业务的注册登记。

**Article 23** Where a guarantor or guaranteed party violates these Regulations and provides security by means of deception or concealment, the Customs shall order it to continue to perform the legal obligation and impose a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the Customs may suspend the guaranteed party from engaging in relevant customs business or revoke its registration for engaging in relevant customs business.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　海关工作人员有下列行为之一的，给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)违法处分担保财产、权利；  
(二)对不符合担保规定的，违法办理有关手续致使国家利益遭受损失；  
(三)对符合担保规定的，不予办理有关手续；  
(四)与海关事务担保有关的其他违法行为。

**Article 24** Where a Customs officer commits any of the following acts, disciplinary sanction shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Illegally disposing of security property or rights;  
(2) Illegally handling relevant procedures for security that does not comply with the provisions, causing losses to the national interests;  
(3) Refusing to handle relevant procedures for security that complies with the provisions;  
(4) Other illegal acts related to customs security.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　担保人、被担保人对海关有关海关事务担保的具体行政行为不服的，可以依法向上一级海关申请行政复议或者向人民法院提起行政诉讼。

**Article 25** Where a guarantor or guaranteed party is dissatisfied with a specific administrative act of the Customs concerning customs security, it may apply to the Customs at the next higher level for administrative reconsideration or file an administrative action with the People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例自2011年1月1日起施行。

**Article 26** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2011.
