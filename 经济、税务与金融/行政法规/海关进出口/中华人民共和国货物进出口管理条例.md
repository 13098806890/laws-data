# 中华人民共和国货物进出口管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2024-03-10  
**生效日期**：2024-05-01

> （2001年12月10日中华人民共和国国务院令第332号公布　根据2024年3月10日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范货物进出口管理，维护货物进出口秩序，促进对外贸易健康发展，根据《中华人民共和国对外贸易法》（以下简称对外贸易法）的有关规定，制定本条例。 第二条　从事将货物进口到中华人民共和国关境内或者将货物出口到中华人民共和国关境外的贸易活动，应当遵守本条例。 第三条　国家对货物进出口实行统一的管理制度。 第四条　国家准许货物的自由进出口，依法维护公平、有序的货物进出口贸易。 除法律、行政法规明确禁止或者限制进出口的外，任何单位和个人均不得对货物进出口设置、维持禁止或者限制措施。 第五条　中华人民共和国在货物进出口贸易方面根据所缔结或者参加的国际条约、协定，给予其他缔约方、参加方最惠国待遇、国民待遇，或者根据互惠、对等原则给予对方最惠国待遇、国民待遇。 第六条　任何国家或者地区在货物进出口贸易方面对中华人民共和国采取歧视性的禁止、限制或者其他类似措施的，中华人民共和国可以根据实际情况对该国家或者地区采取相应的措施。 第七条　国务院对外经济贸易主管部门（以下简称国务院外经贸主管部门）依照对外贸易法和本条例的规定，主管全国货物进出口贸易工作。 国务院有关部门按照国务院规定的职责，依照本条例的规定负责货物进出口贸易管理的有关工作。 第二章　货物进口管理 第一节　禁止进口的货物 第八条　有对外贸易法第十六条规定情形之一的货物，禁止进口。其他法律、行政法规规定禁止进口的，依照其规定。 禁止进口的货物目录由国务院外经贸主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定、调整并公布。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范货物进出口管理，维护货物进出口秩序，促进对外贸易健康发展，根据《中华人民共和国对外贸易法》（以下简称对外贸易法）的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Foreign Trade Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Foreign Trade Law) for the purposes of regulating the administration of import and export of goods, maintaining the order of import and export of goods, and promoting the healthy development of foreign trade.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　从事将货物进口到中华人民共和国关境内或者将货物出口到中华人民共和国关境外的贸易活动，应当遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Any trade activity that imports goods into or exports goods out of the customs territory of the People's Republic of China shall comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对货物进出口实行统一的管理制度。

**Article 3** The State shall implement a unified administration system for the import and export of goods.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家准许货物的自由进出口，依法维护公平、有序的货物进出口贸易。  
除法律、行政法规明确禁止或者限制进出口的外，任何单位和个人均不得对货物进出口设置、维持禁止或者限制措施。

**Article 4** The State permits the free import and export of goods, and safeguards fair and orderly import and export trade according to law.  
Unless expressly prohibited or restricted by laws or administrative regulations, no unit or individual may establish or maintain any prohibition or restriction measures on the import or export of goods.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中华人民共和国在货物进出口贸易方面根据所缔结或者参加的国际条约、协定，给予其他缔约方、参加方最惠国待遇、国民待遇，或者根据互惠、对等原则给予对方最惠国待遇、国民待遇。

**Article 5** In respect of import and export trade in goods, the People's Republic of China shall, in accordance with the international treaties or agreements it has concluded or acceded to, grant most-favored-nation treatment and national treatment to other contracting parties or acceding parties, or grant most-favored-nation treatment and national treatment to the other party based on the principle of reciprocity and mutual benefit.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何国家或者地区在货物进出口贸易方面对中华人民共和国采取歧视性的禁止、限制或者其他类似措施的，中华人民共和国可以根据实际情况对该国家或者地区采取相应的措施。

**Article 6** If any country or region adopts discriminatory prohibitions, restrictions or other similar measures against the People's Republic of China in respect of import and export trade in goods, the People's Republic of China may, according to the actual circumstances, take corresponding measures against that country or region.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院对外经济贸易主管部门（以下简称国务院外经贸主管部门）依照对外贸易法和本条例的规定，主管全国货物进出口贸易工作。  
国务院有关部门按照国务院规定的职责，依照本条例的规定负责货物进出口贸易管理的有关工作。

**Article 7** The competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council (hereinafter referred to as the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council) shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Foreign Trade Law and these Regulations, take charge of the work of import and export trade in goods nationwide.  
The relevant departments under the State Council shall, in accordance with the functions and duties prescribed by the State Council, be responsible for relevant work concerning the administration of import and export trade in goods in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

### 第二章　货物进口管理

#### 第一节　禁止进口的货物

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　有[对外贸易法第十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国对外贸易法.md#art-16)规定情形之一的货物，禁止进口。其他法律、行政法规规定禁止进口的，依照其规定。  
禁止进口的货物目录由国务院外经贸主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定、调整并公布。

**Article 8** Goods that fall under any of the circumstances specified in Article 16 of the Foreign Trade Law shall be prohibited from import. Where other laws or administrative regulations provide for prohibition of import, such provisions shall prevail.  
The catalogue of goods prohibited from import shall be formulated, adjusted and published by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　属于禁止进口的货物，不得进口。

**Article 9** Goods that are prohibited from import shall not be imported.

#### 第二节　限制进口的货物

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　有[对外贸易法第十五条](../../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国对外贸易法.md#art-15)第（一）、（四）、（五）、（六）、（七）项规定情形之一的货物，限制进口。其他法律、行政法规规定限制进口的，依照其规定。  
限制进口的货物目录由国务院外经贸主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定、调整并公布。  
限制进口的货物目录，应当至少在实施前21天公布；在紧急情况下，应当不迟于实施之日公布。

**Article 10** Goods that fall under any of the circumstances specified in subparagraphs (1), (4), (5), (6) and (7) of Article 15 of the Foreign Trade Law shall be restricted from import. Where other laws or administrative regulations provide for restriction of import, such provisions shall prevail.  
The catalogue of goods restricted from import shall be formulated, adjusted and published by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council.  
The catalogue of goods restricted from import shall be published at least 21 days before the implementation; in an emergency, it shall be published no later than the date of implementation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家规定有数量限制的限制进口货物，实行配额管理；其他限制进口货物，实行许可证管理。  
实行关税配额管理的进口货物，依照本章第四节的规定执行。

**Article 11** For restricted import goods for which the State prescribes quantity restrictions, quota administration shall be implemented; for other restricted import goods, license administration shall be implemented.  
Import goods subject to tariff quota administration shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Section 4 of this Chapter.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　实行配额管理的限制进口货物，由国务院外经贸主管部门和国务院有关经济管理部门（以下统称进口配额管理部门）按照国务院规定的职责划分进行管理。

**Article 12** The restricted import goods subject to quota administration shall be administered by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council and the relevant economic administration departments under the State Council (hereinafter collectively referred to as the import quota administration departments) in accordance with the division of functions and duties prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对实行配额管理的限制进口货物，进口配额管理部门应当在每年7月31日前公布下一年度进口配额总量。  
配额申请人应当在每年8月1日至8月31日向进口配额管理部门提出下一年度进口配额的申请。  
进口配额管理部门应当在每年10月31日前将下一年度的配额分配给配额申请人。  
进口配额管理部门可以根据需要对年度配额总量进行调整，并在实施前21天予以公布。

**Article 13** For restricted import goods subject to quota administration, the import quota administration departments shall publish the total amount of import quotas for the next year before July 31 each year.  
Quota applicants shall submit applications for import quotas for the next year to the import quota administration departments from August 1 to August 31 each year.  
The import quota administration departments shall allocate the quotas for the next year to the quota applicants before October 31 each year.  
The import quota administration departments may adjust the total annual quota amount as necessary and shall publish such adjustment 21 days before implementation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　配额可以按照对所有申请统一办理的方式分配。

**Article 14** Quotas may be allocated by way of unified handling of all applications.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　按照对所有申请统一办理的方式分配配额的，进口配额管理部门应当自规定的申请期限截止之日起60天内作出是否发放配额的决定。

**Article 15** Where quotas are allocated by way of unified handling of all applications, the import quota administration departments shall make a decision on whether to grant the quotas within 60 days from the date of expiry of the prescribed application period.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　进口配额管理部门分配配额时，应当考虑下列因素：  
（一）申请人的进口实绩；  
（二）以往分配的配额是否得到充分使用；  
（三）申请人的生产能力、经营规模、销售状况；  
（四）新的进口经营者的申请情况；  
（五）申请配额的数量情况；  
（六）需要考虑的其他因素。

**Article 16** When allocating quotas, the import quota administration departments shall take into account the following factors:  
(1) the applicant's import performance;  
(2) whether the quotas previously allocated have been fully utilized;  
(3) the applicant's production capacity, business scale and sales status;  
(4) applications from new import operators;  
(5) the quantity of quotas applied for;  
(6) other factors that need to be taken into account.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　进口经营者凭进口配额管理部门发放的配额证明，向国务院外经贸主管部门申领进口配额许可证。国务院外经贸主管部门应当自收到申请之日起3个工作日内发放进口配额许可证。  
进口经营者凭国务院外经贸主管部门发放的进口配额许可证，向海关办理报关验放手续。

**Article 17** The import operator shall, on the strength of the quota certificate issued by the import quota administration departments, apply to the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council for an import quota license. The competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council shall issue the import quota license within three working days from the date of receipt of the application.  
The import operator shall, on the strength of the import quota license issued by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council, go through the customs declaration and inspection procedures at the customs.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　配额持有者未使用完其持有的年度配额的，应当在当年9月1日前将未使用的配额交还进口配额管理部门；未按期交还并且在当年年底前未使用完的，进口配额管理部门可以在下一年度对其扣减相应的配额。

**Article 18** If a quota holder does not fully utilize the annual quota it holds, it shall return the unused quota to the import quota administration departments before September 1 of that year; if it fails to return the quota within the prescribed time and does not fully utilize it before the end of that year, the import quota administration departments may deduct a corresponding amount from its quota in the next year.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　实行许可证管理的限制进口货物，进口经营者应当向国务院外经贸主管部门或者国务院有关部门（以下统称进口许可证管理部门）提出申请。进口许可证管理部门应当自收到申请之日起30天内决定是否许可。  
进口经营者凭进口许可证管理部门发放的进口许可证，向海关办理报关验放手续。  
前款所称进口许可证，包括法律、行政法规规定的各种具有许可进口性质的证明、文件。

**Article 19** For restricted import goods subject to license administration, the import operator shall submit an application to the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council or the relevant departments under the State Council (hereinafter collectively referred to as the import license administration departments). The import license administration departments shall decide whether to grant the license within 30 days from the date of receipt of the application.  
The import operator shall, on the strength of the import license issued by the import license administration departments, go through the customs declaration and inspection procedures at the customs.  
The term "import license" in the preceding paragraph includes all types of certificates and documents that have the nature of permitting import as prescribed by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　进口配额管理部门和进口许可证管理部门应当根据本条例的规定制定具体管理办法，对申请人的资格、受理申请的部门、审查的原则和程序等事项作出明确规定并在实施前予以公布。  
受理申请的部门一般为一个部门。  
进口配额管理部门和进口许可证管理部门要求申请人提交的文件，应当限于为保证实施管理所必需的文件和资料，不得仅因细微的、非实质性的错讹拒绝接受申请。

**Article 20** The import quota administration departments and the import license administration departments shall formulate specific administrative measures in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, clearly stipulate the qualifications of applicants, the department accepting applications, the principles and procedures for examination, and other matters, and publish them before implementation.  
The department accepting applications shall generally be one department.  
The documents required by the import quota administration departments and the import license administration departments to be submitted by the applicant shall be limited to the documents and materials necessary for ensuring the implementation of administration, and the application shall not be rejected solely due to minor or non-substantive errors.

#### 第三节　自由进口的货物

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　进口属于自由进口的货物，不受限制。

**Article 21** Import of goods that are freely importable shall not be subject to restriction.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　基于监测货物进口情况的需要，国务院外经贸主管部门和国务院有关经济管理部门可以按照国务院规定的职责划分，对部分属于自由进口的货物实行自动进口许可管理。  
实行自动进口许可管理的货物目录，应当至少在实施前21天公布。

**Article 22** Based on the need to monitor the import situation of goods, the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council and the relevant economic administration departments under the State Council may, in accordance with the division of functions and duties prescribed by the State Council, implement automatic import license administration for some goods that are freely importable.  
The catalogue of goods subject to automatic import license administration shall be published at least 21 days before implementation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　进口属于自动进口许可管理的货物，均应当给予许可。

**Article 23** Import of goods subject to automatic import license administration shall be granted licenses.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　进口属于自动进口许可管理的货物，进口经营者应当在办理海关报关手续前，向国务院外经贸主管部门或者国务院有关经济管理部门提交自动进口许可申请。  
国务院外经贸主管部门或者国务院有关经济管理部门应当在收到申请后，立即发放自动进口许可证明；在特殊情况下，最长不得超过10天。  
进口经营者凭国务院外经贸主管部门或者国务院有关经济管理部门发放的自动进口许可证明，向海关办理报关验放手续。

**Article 24** For import of goods subject to automatic import license administration, the import operator shall, before going through customs declaration procedures, submit an application for automatic import license to the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council or the relevant economic administration departments under the State Council.  
The competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council or the relevant economic administration departments under the State Council shall, upon receipt of the application, immediately issue an automatic import license certificate; under special circumstances, the time limit shall not exceed ten days.  
The import operator shall, on the strength of the automatic import license certificate issued by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council or the relevant economic administration departments under the State Council, go through the customs declaration and inspection procedures at the customs.

#### 第四节　关税配额管理的货物

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　实行关税配额管理的进口货物目录，由国务院外经贸主管部门会同国务院有关经济管理部门制定、调整并公布。

**Article 25** The catalogue of imported goods subject to tariff quota administration shall be formulated, adjusted and published by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council jointly with the relevant economic administration departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　属于关税配额内进口的货物，按照配额内税率缴纳关税；属于关税配额外进口的货物，按照配额外税率缴纳关税。

**Article 26** Goods imported within the tariff quota shall pay customs duties at the within-quota tariff rate; goods imported outside the tariff quota shall pay customs duties at the out-of-quota tariff rate.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　进口配额管理部门应当在每年9月15日至10月14日公布下一年度的关税配额总量。  
配额申请人应当在每年10月15日至10月30日向进口配额管理部门提出关税配额的申请。

**Article 27** The import quota administration departments shall publish the total amount of tariff quotas for the next year between September 15 and October 14 each year.  
Quota applicants shall submit applications for tariff quotas to the import quota administration departments between October 15 and October 30 each year.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　关税配额可以按照对所有申请统一办理的方式分配。

**Article 28** Tariff quotas may be allocated by way of unified handling of all applications.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　按照对所有申请统一办理的方式分配关税配额的，进口配额管理部门应当在每年12月31日前作出是否发放配额的决定。

**Article 29** Where tariff quotas are allocated by way of unified handling of all applications, the import quota administration departments shall make a decision on whether to grant the quotas before December 31 each year.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　进口经营者凭进口配额管理部门发放的关税配额证明，向海关办理关税配额内货物的报关验放手续。  
国务院有关经济管理部门应当及时将年度关税配额总量、分配方案和关税配额证明实际发放的情况向国务院外经贸主管部门备案。

**Article 30** The import operator shall, on the strength of the tariff quota certificate issued by the import quota administration departments, go through the customs declaration and inspection procedures for goods within the tariff quota at the customs.  
The relevant economic administration departments under the State Council shall timely file the total annual tariff quota amount, allocation plan, and the actual issuance of tariff quota certificates with the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council for record.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　关税配额持有者未使用完其持有的年度配额的，应当在当年9月15日前将未使用的配额交还进口配额管理部门；未按期交还并且在当年年底前未使用完的，进口配额管理部门可以在下一年度对其扣减相应的配额。

**Article 31** If a tariff quota holder does not fully utilize the annual quota it holds, it shall return the unused quota to the import quota administration departments before September 15 of that year; if it fails to return the quota within the prescribed time and does not fully utilize it before the end of that year, the import quota administration departments may deduct a corresponding amount from its quota in the next year.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　进口配额管理部门应当根据本条例的规定制定有关关税配额的具体管理办法，对申请人的资格、受理申请的部门、审查的原则和程序等事项作出明确规定并在实施前予以公布。  
受理申请的部门一般为一个部门。  
进口配额管理部门要求关税配额申请人提交的文件，应当限于为保证实施关税配额管理所必需的文件和资料，不得仅因细微的、非实质性的错讹拒绝接受关税配额申请。

**Article 32** The import quota administration departments shall formulate specific administrative measures for tariff quotas in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, clearly stipulate the qualifications of applicants, the department accepting applications, the principles and procedures for examination, and other matters, and publish them before implementation.  
The department accepting applications shall generally be one department.  
The documents required by the import quota administration departments to be submitted by the tariff quota applicant shall be limited to the documents and materials necessary for ensuring the implementation of tariff quota administration, and the tariff quota application shall not be rejected solely due to minor or non-substantive errors.

### 第三章　货物出口管理

#### 第一节　禁止出口的货物

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　有[对外贸易法第十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国对外贸易法.md#art-16)规定情形之一的货物，禁止出口。其他法律、行政法规规定禁止出口的，依照其规定。  
禁止出口的货物目录由国务院外经贸主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定、调整并公布。

**Article 33** Goods that fall under any of the circumstances specified in Article 16 of the Foreign Trade Law shall be prohibited from export. Where other laws or administrative regulations provide for prohibition of export, such provisions shall prevail.  
The catalogue of goods prohibited from export shall be formulated, adjusted and published by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　属于禁止出口的货物，不得出口。

**Article 34** Goods that are prohibited from export shall not be exported.

#### 第二节　限制出口的货物

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　有[对外贸易法第十五条](../../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国对外贸易法.md#art-15)第（一）、（二）、（三）、（七）项规定情形之一的货物，限制出口。其他法律、行政法规规定限制出口的，依照其规定。  
限制出口的货物目录由国务院外经贸主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定、调整并公布。  
限制出口的货物目录，应当至少在实施前21天公布；在紧急情况下，应当不迟于实施之日公布。

**Article 35** Goods that fall under any of the circumstances specified in subparagraphs (1), (2), (3) and (7) of Article 15 of the Foreign Trade Law shall be restricted from export. Where other laws or administrative regulations provide for restriction of export, such provisions shall prevail.  
The catalogue of goods restricted from export shall be formulated, adjusted and published by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council.  
The catalogue of goods restricted from export shall be published at least 21 days before implementation; in an emergency, it shall be published no later than the date of implementation.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家规定有数量限制的限制出口货物，实行配额管理；其他限制出口货物，实行许可证管理。

**Article 36** For restricted export goods for which the State prescribes quantity restrictions, quota administration shall be implemented; for other restricted export goods, license administration shall be implemented.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　实行配额管理的限制出口货物，由国务院外经贸主管部门和国务院有关经济管理部门（以下统称出口配额管理部门）按照国务院规定的职责划分进行管理。

**Article 37** The restricted export goods subject to quota administration shall be administered by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council and the relevant economic administration departments under the State Council (hereinafter collectively referred to as the export quota administration departments) in accordance with the division of functions and duties prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　对实行配额管理的限制出口货物，出口配额管理部门应当在每年10月31日前公布下一年度出口配额总量。  
配额申请人应当在每年11月1日至11月15日向出口配额管理部门提出下一年度出口配额的申请。  
出口配额管理部门应当在每年12月15日前将下一年度的配额分配给配额申请人。

**Article 38** For restricted export goods subject to quota administration, the export quota administration departments shall publish the total amount of export quotas for the next year before October 31 each year.  
Quota applicants shall submit applications for export quotas for the next year to the export quota administration departments from November 1 to November 15 each year.  
The export quota administration departments shall allocate the quotas for the next year to the quota applicants before December 15 each year.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　配额可以通过直接分配的方式分配，也可以通过招标等方式分配。

**Article 39** Quotas may be allocated by way of direct allocation, or by way of bidding or other methods.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　出口配额管理部门应当自收到申请之日起30天内并不晚于当年12月15日作出是否发放配额的决定。

**Article 40** The export quota administration departments shall make a decision on whether to grant the quotas within 30 days from the date of receipt of the application and no later than December 15 of that year.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　出口经营者凭出口配额管理部门发放的配额证明，向国务院外经贸主管部门申领出口配额许可证。国务院外经贸主管部门应当自收到申请之日起3个工作日内发放出口配额许可证。  
出口经营者凭国务院外经贸主管部门发放的出口配额许可证，向海关办理报关验放手续。

**Article 41** The export operator shall, on the strength of the quota certificate issued by the export quota administration departments, apply to the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council for an export quota license. The competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council shall issue the export quota license within three working days from the date of receipt of the application.  
The export operator shall, on the strength of the export quota license issued by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council, go through the customs declaration and inspection procedures at the customs.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　配额持有者未使用完其持有的年度配额的，应当在当年10月31日前将未使用的配额交还出口配额管理部门；未按期交还并且在当年年底前未使用完的，出口配额管理部门可以在下一年度对其扣减相应的配额。

**Article 42** If a quota holder does not fully utilize the annual quota it holds, it shall return the unused quota to the export quota administration departments before October 31 of that year; if it fails to return the quota within the prescribed time and does not fully utilize it before the end of that year, the export quota administration departments may deduct a corresponding amount from its quota in the next year.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　实行许可证管理的限制出口货物，出口经营者应当向国务院外经贸主管部门或者国务院有关部门（以下统称出口许可证管理部门）提出申请，出口许可证管理部门应当自收到申请之日起30天内决定是否许可。  
出口经营者凭出口许可证管理部门发放的出口许可证，向海关办理报关验放手续。  
前款所称出口许可证，包括法律、行政法规规定的各种具有许可出口性质的证明、文件。

**Article 43** For restricted export goods subject to license administration, the export operator shall submit an application to the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council or the relevant departments under the State Council (hereinafter collectively referred to as the export license administration departments). The export license administration departments shall decide whether to grant the license within 30 days from the date of receipt of the application.  
The export operator shall, on the strength of the export license issued by the export license administration departments, go through the customs declaration and inspection procedures at the customs.  
The term "export license" in the preceding paragraph includes all types of certificates and documents that have the nature of permitting export as prescribed by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　出口配额管理部门和出口许可证管理部门应当根据本条例的规定制定具体管理办法，对申请人的资格、受理申请的部门、审查的原则和程序等事项作出明确规定并在实施前予以公布。  
受理申请的部门一般为一个部门。  
出口配额管理部门和出口许可证管理部门要求申请人提交的文件，应当限于为保证实施管理所必需的文件和资料，不得仅因细微的、非实质性的错讹拒绝接受申请。

**Article 44** The export quota administration departments and the export license administration departments shall formulate specific administrative measures in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, clearly stipulate the qualifications of applicants, the department accepting applications, the principles and procedures for examination, and other matters, and publish them before implementation.  
The department accepting applications shall generally be one department.  
The documents required by the export quota administration departments and the export license administration departments to be submitted by the applicant shall be limited to the documents and materials necessary for ensuring the implementation of administration, and the application shall not be rejected solely due to minor or non-substantive errors.

### 第四章　国营贸易和指定经营

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家可以对部分货物的进出口实行国营贸易管理。  
实行国营贸易管理的进出口货物目录由国务院外经贸主管部门会同国务院有关经济管理部门制定、调整并公布。

**Article 45** The State may implement state trading administration for the import and export of certain goods.  
The catalogue of import and export goods subject to state trading administration shall be formulated, adjusted and published by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council jointly with the relevant economic administration departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国务院外经贸主管部门和国务院有关经济管理部门按照国务院规定的职责划分确定国营贸易企业名录并予以公布。

**Article 46** The competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council and the relevant economic administration departments under the State Council shall, in accordance with the division of functions and duties prescribed by the State Council, determine and publish the list of state trading enterprises.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　实行国营贸易管理的货物，国家允许非国营贸易企业从事部分数量的进出口。

**Article 47** For goods subject to state trading administration, the State allows non-state trading enterprises to engage in the import and export of a portion of the quantity.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国营贸易企业应当每半年向国务院外经贸主管部门提供实行国营贸易管理的货物的购买价格、销售价格等有关信息。

**Article 48** State trading enterprises shall provide the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council with information on the purchase prices, sales prices, and other relevant information of goods subject to state trading administration on a semi-annual basis.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国务院外经贸主管部门基于维护进出口经营秩序的需要，可以在一定期限内对部分货物实行指定经营管理。  
实行指定经营管理的进出口货物目录由国务院外经贸主管部门制定、调整并公布。

**Article 49** The competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council may, based on the need to maintain the order of import and export operations, implement designated operation administration for certain goods for a specific period.  
The catalogue of import and export goods subject to designated operation administration shall be formulated, adjusted and published by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　确定指定经营企业的具体标准和程序，由国务院外经贸主管部门制定并在实施前公布。  
指定经营企业名录由国务院外经贸主管部门公布。

**Article 50** The specific standards and procedures for determining designated operation enterprises shall be formulated by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council and published before implementation.  
The list of designated operation enterprises shall be published by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　除[本条例第四十七条](#art-47)规定的情形外，未列入国营贸易企业名录和指定经营企业名录的企业或者其他组织，不得从事实行国营贸易管理、指定经营管理的货物的进出口贸易。

**Article 51** Except for the circumstances specified in Article 47 of these Regulations, enterprises or other organizations that are not included in the list of state trading enterprises and the list of designated operation enterprises shall not engage in import and export trade of goods subject to state trading administration or designated operation administration.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国营贸易企业和指定经营企业应当根据正常的商业条件从事经营活动，不得以非商业因素选择供应商，不得以非商业因素拒绝其他企业或者组织的委托。

**Article 52** State trading enterprises and designated operation enterprises shall conduct business activities in accordance with normal commercial conditions, and shall not select suppliers based on non-commercial factors, nor refuse entrustment by other enterprises or organizations based on non-commercial factors.

### 第五章　进出口监测和临时措施

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国务院外经贸主管部门负责对货物进出口情况进行监测、评估，并定期向国务院报告货物进出口情况，提出建议。

**Article 53** The competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council shall be responsible for monitoring and evaluating the import and export situation of goods, and shall periodically report the import and export situation of goods to the State Council and make recommendations.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家为维护国际收支平衡，包括国际收支发生严重失衡或者受到严重失衡威胁时，或者为维持与实施经济发展计划相适应的外汇储备水平，可以对进口货物的价值或者数量采取临时限制措施。

**Article 54** For the purpose of safeguarding the balance of international payments, including when serious imbalance occurs or is threatened in the balance of international payments, or for maintaining a level of foreign exchange reserves appropriate to the implementation of economic development plans, the State may adopt temporary restrictive measures on the value or quantity of imported goods.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家为建立或者加快建立国内特定产业，在采取现有措施无法实现的情况下，可以采取限制或者禁止进口的临时措施。

**Article 55** For the purpose of establishing or accelerating the establishment of a specific domestic industry, where the existing measures are insufficient to achieve the goal, the State may adopt temporary restrictive or prohibitive measures on imports.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国家为执行下列一项或者数项措施，必要时可以对任何形式的农产品水产品采取限制进口的临时措施：  
（一）对相同产品或者直接竞争产品的国内生产或者销售采取限制措施；  
（二）通过补贴消费的形式，消除国内过剩的相同产品或者直接竞争产品；  
（三）对完全或者主要依靠该进口农产品水产品形成的动物产品采取限产措施。

**Article 56** For the purpose of implementing one or more of the following measures, the State may, when necessary, adopt temporary restrictive measures on the import of agricultural products and aquatic products of any form:  
(1) adopting restrictive measures on domestic production or sale of like products or directly competitive products;  
(2) eliminating surpluses of like products or directly competitive products in the domestic market by way of subsidizing consumption;  
(3) adopting production restriction measures on animal products that are wholly or mainly derived from such imported agricultural products or aquatic products.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　有下列情形之一的，国务院外经贸主管部门可以对特定货物的出口采取限制或者禁止的临时措施：  
（一）发生严重自然灾害等异常情况，需要限制或者禁止出口的；  
（二）出口经营秩序严重混乱，需要限制出口的；  
（三）依照[对外贸易法第十五条](../../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国对外贸易法.md#art-15)、第十六条的规定，需要限制或者禁止出口的。

**Article 57** Under any of the following circumstances, the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council may adopt temporary restrictive or prohibitive measures on the export of specific goods:  
(1) where serious natural disasters or other unusual events occur, making it necessary to restrict or prohibit export;  
(2) where the order of export operations is severely disrupted, making it necessary to restrict export;  
(3) where it is necessary to restrict or prohibit export in accordance with the provisions of Article 15 or Article 16 of the Foreign Trade Law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　对进出口货物采取限制或者禁止的临时措施的，国务院外经贸主管部门应当在实施前予以公告。

**Article 58** Where temporary restrictive or prohibitive measures are adopted on import or export goods, the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council shall make an announcement before implementation.

### 第六章　对外贸易促进

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国家采取出口信用保险、出口信贷、出口退税、设立外贸发展基金等措施，促进对外贸易发展。

**Article 59** The State adopts measures such as export credit insurance, export credit, export tax rebates, and establishment of foreign trade development funds to promote the development of foreign trade.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国家采取有效措施，促进企业的技术创新和技术进步，提高企业的国际竞争能力。

**Article 60** The State adopts effective measures to promote technological innovation and technological progress of enterprises, and to enhance the international competitiveness of enterprises.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家通过提供信息咨询服务，帮助企业开拓国际市场。

**Article 61** The State helps enterprises to explore international markets by providing information and consulting services.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　货物进出口经营者可以依法成立和参加进出口商会，实行行业自律和协调。

**Article 62** Import and export operators of goods may, according to law, establish and join import and export chambers of commerce to practice industry self-regulation and coordination.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国家鼓励企业积极应对国外歧视性反倾销、反补贴、保障措施及其他限制措施，维护企业的正当贸易权利。

**Article 63** The State encourages enterprises to actively respond to foreign discriminatory anti-dumping, countervailing, safeguard, and other restrictive measures to protect the legitimate trade rights of enterprises.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　进口或者出口属于禁止进出口的货物，或者未经批准、许可擅自进口或者出口属于限制进出口的货物的，依照刑法关于走私罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依照海关法的有关规定处罚。

**Article 64** Whoever imports or exports goods that are prohibited from import or export, or without approval or license, imports or exports goods that are restricted from import or export, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of smuggling; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　擅自超出批准、许可的范围进口或者出口属于限制进出口的货物的，依照刑法关于走私罪或者非法经营罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依照海关法的有关规定处罚。

**Article 65** Whoever, without authorization, imports or exports goods that are restricted from import or export beyond the scope of the approval or license, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of smuggling or the crime of illegal business operations; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　伪造、变造或者买卖货物进出口配额证明、批准文件、许可证或者自动进口许可证明的，依照刑法关于非法经营罪或者伪造、变造、买卖国家机关公文、证件、印章罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依照海关法的有关规定处罚。

**Article 66** Whoever forges, alters, or buys or sells import or export quota certificates, approval documents, licenses, or automatic import license certificates, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operations or the crime of forging, altering, or buying or selling official documents, certificates, or seals of state organs; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　进出口经营者以欺骗或者其他不正当手段获取货物进出口配额、批准文件、许可证或者自动进口许可证明的，依法收缴其货物进出口配额、批准文件、许可证或者自动进口许可证明。

**Article 67** If an import or export operator obtains import or export quotas, approval documents, licenses, or automatic import license certificates by deception or other illegitimate means, the said quotas, approval documents, licenses, or automatic import license certificates shall be confiscated according to law.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　违反[本条例第五十一条](#art-51)规定，擅自从事实行国营贸易管理或者指定经营管理的货物进出口贸易，扰乱市场秩序，情节严重的，依照刑法关于非法经营罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由市场监督管理部门依法给予行政处罚。

**Article 68** Whoever, in violation of Article 51 of these Regulations, without authorization, engages in import or export trade of goods subject to state trading administration or designated operation administration, thereby disrupting market order, if the circumstances are serious, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operations; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, the market supervision and administration department shall impose an administrative penalty according to law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　国营贸易企业或者指定经营企业违反[本条例第四十八条](#art-48)、第五十二条规定的，由国务院外经贸主管部门予以警告；情节严重的，可以暂停直至取消其国营贸易企业或者指定经营企业资格。

**Article 69** If a state trading enterprise or a designated operation enterprise violates the provisions of Article 48 or Article 52 of these Regulations, the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council shall give it a warning; if the circumstances are serious, it may suspend or even revoke its qualification as a state trading enterprise or designated operation enterprise.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　货物进出口管理工作人员在履行货物进出口管理职责中，滥用职权、玩忽职守或者利用职务上的便利收受、索取他人财物的，依照刑法关于滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪、受贿罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 70** Where a staff member engaged in the administration of import and export of goods, in the performance of his duties, abuses his power, neglects his duties, or takes advantage of his position to accept or extort property from others, he shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty, the crime of bribery, or other crimes; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, he shall be given an administrative sanction according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　对本条例规定的行政机关发放配额、关税配额、许可证或者自动许可证明的决定不服的，对确定国营贸易企业或者指定经营企业资格的决定不服的，或者对行政处罚的决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议，也可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 71** Any person who is dissatisfied with a decision made by an administrative organ under these Regulations on granting quotas, tariff quotas, licenses, or automatic license certificates, or with a decision on determining the qualification of a state trading enterprise or designated operation enterprise, or with a decision on administrative penalty, may apply for administrative reconsideration according to law, or may file a lawsuit with a People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　本条例的规定不妨碍依据法律、行政法规对进出口货物采取的关税、检验检疫、安全、环保、知识产权保护等措施。

**Article 72** The provisions of these Regulations shall not prejudice the measures taken in accordance with laws and administrative regulations on the import and export of goods, such as customs duties, inspection and quarantine, safety, environmental protection, and intellectual property protection.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　出口核用品、核两用品、监控化学品、军品等出口管制货物的，依照有关行政法规的规定办理。

**Article 73** The export of export-controlled goods such as nuclear materials, dual-use nuclear goods, monitoring chemicals, and military products shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant administrative regulations.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　对进口货物需要采取反倾销措施、反补贴措施、保障措施的，依照对外贸易法和有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 74** Where it is necessary to take anti-dumping, countervailing, or safeguard measures on imported goods, such measures shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the Foreign Trade Law and other relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　法律、行政法规对保税区、出口加工区等特殊经济区的货物进出口管理另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 75** Where laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise for the administration of import and export of goods in special economic zones such as bonded zones and export processing zones, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　国务院外经贸主管部门负责有关货物进出口贸易的双边或者多边磋商、谈判，并负责贸易争端解决的有关事宜。

**Article 76** The competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council shall be responsible for bilateral or multilateral consultations and negotiations concerning import and export trade in goods, and shall be responsible for relevant matters concerning the settlement of trade disputes.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　本条例自2002年1月1日起施行。1984年1月10日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国进口货物许可制度暂行条例》，1992年12月21日国务院批准、1992年12月29日对外经济贸易部发布的《出口商品管理暂行办法》，1993年9月22日国务院批准、1993年10月7日国家经济贸易委员会、对外贸易经济合作部发布的《机电产品进口管理暂行办法》，1993年12月22日国务院批准、1993年12月29日国家计划委员会、对外贸易经济合作部发布的《一般商品进口配额管理暂行办法》，1994年6月13日国务院批准、1994年7月19日对外贸易经济合作部、国家计划委员会发布的《进口商品经营管理暂行办法》，同时废止。

**Article 77** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2002. The Interim Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Licensing System for Imported Goods promulgated by the State Council on January 10, 1984, the Interim Measures for the Control of Exported Commodities approved by the State Council on December 21, 1992 and issued by the Ministry of Foreign Economic Relations and Trade on December 29, 1992, the Interim Measures for the Administration of Import of Mechanical and Electronic Products approved by the State Council on September 22, 1993 and issued by the State Economic and Trade Commission and the Ministry of Foreign Trade and Economic Cooperation on October 7, 1993, the Interim Measures for the Administration of Import Quotas for General Commodities approved by the State Council on December 22, 1993 and issued by the State Planning Commission and the Ministry of Foreign Trade and Economic Cooperation on December 29, 1993, and the Interim Measures for the Administration of Imported Goods approved by the State Council on June 13, 1994 and issued by the Ministry of Foreign Trade and Economic Cooperation and the State Planning Commission on July 19, 1994 shall be abolished simultaneously.
