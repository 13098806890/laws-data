# 海关工作人员使用武器和警械的规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保证海关工作人员依法使用武器和警械，履行职责，根据[《中华人民共和国海关法》第六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国海关法.md#art-6)的规定，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated in accordance with the provisions of Article 6 of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China to ensure that customs staff members use weapons and police implements according to law and perform their duties.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　海关工作人员使用的武器和警械，经当地公安机关同意后由海关总署统一配发。海关工作人员执行缉私任务时，应当依照本规定使用武器和警械。  
海关工作人员使用的武器和警械包括：轻型枪支、电警棍、手铐及其他经批准列装的武器和警械。

**Article 2** The weapons and police implements used by customs staff members shall be uniformly distributed by the General Administration of Customs after obtaining the consent of the local public security organ. When performing anti-smuggling duties, customs staff members shall use weapons and police implements in accordance with these Provisions.  
The weapons and police implements used by customs staff members include: light firearms, electric batons, handcuffs, and other weapons and police implements approved for listing.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　配发给海关的武器和警械一律公用，不配发个人专用的武器和警械。  
海关工作人员持枪执行缉私任务时，应当随身携带当地公安机关核发的持枪证或者持枪通行证。

**Article 3** All weapons and police implements distributed to customs shall be for official use, and no weapons or police implements for personal use shall be distributed.  
When carrying firearms to perform anti-smuggling duties, customs staff members shall carry with them the firearm license or firearm pass issued by the local public security organ.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　海关工作人员执行缉私任务，遇有下列情形之一的，可以开枪射击：  
(一)追缉逃跑的走私团伙或者遭遇武装掩护走私，非开枪不足以制服时；  
(二)走私分子或者走私嫌疑人以暴力抗拒检查，抢夺武器或者警械，威胁海关工作人员生命安全，非开枪不能自卫时；  
(三)走私分子或者走私嫌疑人以暴力劫夺查扣的走私货物、物品和其他证据，非开枪不能制止时。

**Article 4** Customs staff members performing anti-smuggling duties may fire their firearms under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) When pursuing an escaping smuggling gang or encountering armed escort of smuggling and the situation cannot be controlled without firing;  
(2) When smugglers or suspected smugglers violently resist inspection, seize weapons or police implements, threaten the life safety of customs staff members, and self-defense cannot be achieved without firing;  
(3) When smugglers or suspected smugglers violently seize the confiscated smuggled goods, articles and other evidence, and the situation cannot be stopped without firing.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　海关工作人员执行缉私任务，遇有下列情形之一的，可以使用警械：  
(一)走私分子或者走私嫌疑人以暴力抗拒检查或者逃跑时；  
(二)走私分子或者走私嫌疑人以暴力抗拒查扣走私货物、物品和其他证据时；  
(三)执行缉私任务受到袭击需要自卫时；  
(四)遇有其他需要使用警械的情形时。

**Article 5** Customs staff members performing anti-smuggling duties may use police implements under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) When smugglers or suspected smugglers violently resist inspection or escape;  
(2) When smugglers or suspected smugglers violently resist the seizure of smuggled goods, articles and other evidence;  
(3) When they are attacked while performing anti-smuggling duties and need to defend themselves;  
(4) Under other circumstances where the use of police implements is necessary.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　海关工作人员使用武器或者警械时，应当以制服对方为限度。海关工作人员依照[本规定第四条](#art-4)的规定开枪射击时，除特别紧迫的情况外，应当先口头警告或者鸣枪警告，对方一有畏服表现，应当立即停止射击。开枪射击造成人员伤亡的，应当保护现场，并立即向上级海关和当地公安机关报告。

**Article 6** When using weapons or police implements, customs staff members shall limit themselves to subduing the opposing party. When firing firearms in accordance with Article 4 of these Provisions, except under particularly urgent circumstances, they shall first give a verbal warning or fire a warning shot, and once the opposing party shows signs of submission, they shall immediately cease firing. If the firing causes casualties, they shall protect the scene and immediately report to the superior customs and the local public security organ.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　海关工作人员违反本规定，滥用武器或者警械的，应当根据情节，依法追究有关人员的责任。

**Article 7** Where a customs staff member violates these Provisions and abuses weapons or police implements, the relevant person shall be held liable according to law based on the circumstances.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 8** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
