# 中华人民共和国海关对出口加工区监管的暂行办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：2000-05-24

> (2000年4月27日国务院批准　2000年5月24日海关总署令第81号公布　2002年6月21日国务院批准第一次修订　根据2003年9月2日《国务院关于修改〈中华人民共和国海关对出口加工区监管的暂行办法〉的决定》第二次修订　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为加强与完善加工贸易管理，规范海关对出口加工区的监管，促进出口加工区的健康发展，鼓励扩大外贸出口，根据《中华人民共和国海关法》和国家有关法律、法规，制定本办法。 第二条　为防止重复建设，在中华人民共和国境内设立出口加工区(以下简称加工区)，只能设在已经国务院批准的现有经济技术开发区内，并由省(自治区、直辖市)人民政府报国务院批准。 第三条　加工区是海关监管的特定区域。海关在加工区内设立机构，并依照本办法，对进、出加工区的货物及区内相关场所实行24小时监管。 第四条　加工区与中华人民共和国境内的其他地区(以下简称区外)之间，须设置符合海关监管要求的隔离设施及闭路电视监控系统。经海关总署对加工区的隔离设施验收合格后，方可开展加工区有关业务。 第五条　区内设置加工区管理委员会和出口加工企业、专为出口加工企业生产提供服务的仓储企业以及经海关核准专门从事加工区内货物进、出的运输企业。 除安全保卫人员和企业值班人员外，其他人员不得在加工区内居住。不得建立营业性的生活消费设施。 第六条　区内不得经营商业零售、一般贸易、转口贸易及其他与加工区无关的业务。 第七条　在加工区内设立的企业(以下简称区内企业)，应向海关办理注册手续。 第八条　区内企业应当依据《中华人民共和国会计法》及国家有关法律、法规的规定，设置符合海关监管要求的账簿、报表。凭合法、有效凭证记账并进行核算，记录本企业有关进、出加工区货物和物品的库存、转让、转移、销售、加工、使用和损耗等情况。 第九条　加工区实行计算机联网管理和海关稽查制度。 区内企业应建立符合海关监管要求的电子计算机管理数据库，并与海关实行电子计算机联网，进行电子数据交换。 第十条　区内企业开展加工贸易业务不实行加工贸易银行保证金台账制度，海关不实行《加工贸易登记手册》管理。 第十一条　海关对进、出加工区的货物、物品、运输工具、人员及区内有关场所，有权依照《中华人民共和国海关法》的规定进行检查、查验。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为加强与完善加工贸易管理，规范海关对出口加工区的监管，促进出口加工区的健康发展，鼓励扩大外贸出口，根据《中华人民共和国海关法》和国家有关法律、法规，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws and regulations of the State for the purposes of strengthening and improving the administration of processing trade, regulating the customs supervision over export processing zones, promoting the sound development of export processing zones, and encouraging the expansion of foreign trade export.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　为防止重复建设，在中华人民共和国境内设立出口加工区(以下简称加工区)，只能设在已经国务院批准的现有经济技术开发区内，并由省(自治区、直辖市)人民政府报国务院批准。

**Article 2** To prevent redundant construction, export processing zones (hereinafter referred to as "processing zones") established within the territory of the People's Republic of China may only be located within existing economic and technological development zones that have been approved by the State Council, and shall be submitted by the people's government of the province (autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government) to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　加工区是海关监管的特定区域。海关在加工区内设立机构，并依照本办法，对进、出加工区的货物及区内相关场所实行24小时监管。

**Article 3** A processing zone is a specific area under customs supervision. The customs shall set up offices within the processing zone and, in accordance with these Measures, exercise 24-hour supervision over goods entering or leaving the processing zone and over relevant premises within the zone.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　加工区与中华人民共和国境内的其他地区(以下简称区外)之间，须设置符合海关监管要求的隔离设施及闭路电视监控系统。经海关总署对加工区的隔离设施验收合格后，方可开展加工区有关业务。

**Article 4** Between a processing zone and other areas within the territory of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as "outside the zone"), isolation facilities and closed-circuit television monitoring systems that meet the customs supervision requirements shall be installed. Relevant businesses of the processing zone may be conducted only after the General Administration of Customs has inspected and accepted the isolation facilities of the processing zone as qualified.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　区内设置加工区管理委员会和出口加工企业、专为出口加工企业生产提供服务的仓储企业以及经海关核准专门从事加工区内货物进、出的运输企业。  
除安全保卫人员和企业值班人员外，其他人员不得在加工区内居住。不得建立营业性的生活消费设施。

**Article 5** The following shall be established within a processing zone: the administrative committee of the processing zone, export processing enterprises, warehousing enterprises that provide services exclusively for the production of export processing enterprises, and transport enterprises specially engaged in the entry and exit of goods within the processing zone upon customs approval.  
Except for security personnel and enterprise personnel on duty, no other persons may reside within the processing zone. No commercial living or consumption facilities may be established.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　区内不得经营商业零售、一般贸易、转口贸易及其他与加工区无关的业务。

**Article 6** No commercial retail, general trade, transit trade, or other businesses unrelated to the processing zone may be operated within the zone.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　在加工区内设立的企业(以下简称区内企业)，应向海关办理注册手续。

**Article 7** Enterprises established within a processing zone (hereinafter referred to as "enterprises within the zone") shall go through the registration procedures with the customs.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　区内企业应当依据《中华人民共和国会计法》及国家有关法律、法规的规定，设置符合海关监管要求的账簿、报表。凭合法、有效凭证记账并进行核算，记录本企业有关进、出加工区货物和物品的库存、转让、转移、销售、加工、使用和损耗等情况。

**Article 8** Enterprises within the zone shall, in accordance with the Accounting Law of the People's Republic of China and relevant laws and regulations of the State, set up account books and statements that meet the customs supervision requirements, keep accounts and conduct accounting on the basis of lawful and valid vouchers, and record the inventory, transfer, movement, sale, processing, use, and wastage of goods and articles entering or leaving the processing zone.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　加工区实行计算机联网管理和海关稽查制度。  
区内企业应建立符合海关监管要求的电子计算机管理数据库，并与海关实行电子计算机联网，进行电子数据交换。

**Article 9** A processing zone shall implement computer network management and a customs inspection system.  
Enterprises within the zone shall establish electronic computer management databases that meet the customs supervision requirements, connect electronically with the customs, and conduct electronic data interchange.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　区内企业开展加工贸易业务不实行加工贸易银行保证金台账制度，海关不实行《加工贸易登记手册》管理。

**Article 10** In conducting processing trade business, enterprises within the zone shall not be subject to the processing trade bank guarantee ledger system, and the customs shall not implement the management of the "Registration Manual for Processing Trade."

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　海关对进、出加工区的货物、物品、运输工具、人员及区内有关场所，有权依照《中华人民共和国海关法》的规定进行检查、查验。

**Article 11** The Customs has the right to inspect and examine goods, articles, means of transport, and personnel entering or leaving the processing zone, as well as relevant places within the zone, in accordance with the provisions of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家对区内加工产品不征收增值税。

**Article 12** The State shall not levy value-added tax on products processed within the zone.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家禁止进、出口的货物、物品，不得进、出加工区。

**Article 13** Goods and articles the import or export of which is prohibited by the State shall not be brought into or taken out of the processing zone.

### 第二章　对加工区与境外之间进出货物的监管

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　加工区与境外之间进、出的货物，由货主或其代理人根据加工区管理委员会的批件，填写进、出境货物备案清单，向主管海关备案。备案清单由海关总署统一制发。

**Article 14** For goods entering or leaving the processing zone from or to outside the territory, the owner of the goods or his agent shall, on the basis of the approval document of the Administrative Committee of the Processing Zone, fill in a record-filing list for import or export goods and file it with the competent customs. The record-filing list shall be uniformly formulated by the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　海关对加工区与境外之间进、出的货物，按照直通式或转关运输的办法进行监管。

**Article 15** The Customs shall supervise the goods entering or leaving the processing zone from or to outside the territory by means of direct clearance or transit transport.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　加工区与境外之间进、出的货物，除实行出口被动配额管理的外，不实行进出口配额、许可证件管理。

**Article 16** Goods entering or leaving the processing zone from or to outside the territory shall not be subject to import and export quota or license management, except those subject to passive export quota management.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　从境外进入加工区的货物，其进口关税和进口环节税，除法律、法规另有规定外，按照下列规定办理：  
(一)区内生产性的基础设施建设项目所需的机器、设备和建设生产厂房、仓储设施所需的基建物资，予以免税；  
(二)区内企业生产所需的机器、设备、模具及其维修用零配件，予以免税；  
(三)区内企业为加工出口产品所需的原材料、零部件、元器件、包装物料及消耗性材料，予以保税；  
(四)区内企业和行政管理机构自用合理数量的办公用品，予以免税；  
(五)区内企业和行政管理机构自用的交通运输工具、生活消费用品，按进口货物的有关规定办理报关手续，海关予以照章征税。

**Article 17** For goods entering the processing zone from outside the territory, the import customs duty and import stage taxes shall be handled in accordance with the following provisions, unless otherwise provided by laws or regulations:  
(1) Machinery and equipment needed for productive infrastructure construction projects within the zone, and capital construction materials needed for the construction of production workshops and storage facilities, shall be exempted from duty;  
(2) Machinery, equipment, molds and their spare parts for maintenance needed for production by enterprises within the zone shall be exempted from duty;  
(3) Raw materials, spare parts, components, packaging materials and consumables needed by enterprises within the zone for processing export products shall be placed under bonded supervision;  
(4) Office supplies for self-use in reasonable quantities by enterprises and administrative organs within the zone shall be exempted from duty;  
(5) Means of transport and consumer goods for daily use for self-use by enterprises and administrative organs within the zone shall undergo customs declaration procedures in accordance with the relevant provisions on import goods, and the Customs shall levy duty in accordance with the regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　除法律、法规另有规定外，区内企业加工的制成品及其在加工生产过程中产生的边角料、余料、残次品、废品等销往境外的，免征出口关税。

**Article 18** Unless otherwise provided by laws or regulations, finished products processed by enterprises within the zone, as well as leftovers, surplus materials, defective products, and waste generated in the process of processing and production that are sold outside the territory, shall be exempted from export customs duty.

### 第三章　对加工区与区外之间进出货物的监管

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对加工区运往区外的货物，海关按照对进口货物的有关规定办理报关手续，并按照制成品征税。如属许可证件管理商品，还应向海关出具有效的进口许可证件。

**Article 19** For goods transported from the processing zone to outside the zone, the Customs shall handle customs declaration procedures in accordance with the relevant provisions on import goods and shall levy duty on the finished products. If the goods are subject to license management, a valid import license shall also be presented to the Customs.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　区内企业的加工产品和在加工生产过程中产生的边角料、残次品、废品等应复运出境。因特殊情况需要运往区外时，由企业申请，经主管海关核准后，按内销时的状态确定归类并征税。如属进口许可证件管理商品，免领进口许可证件。如属《限制进口类可用作原料的废物目录》所列商品，应按现行规定向环保部门申领进口许可证件。对无商业价值的边角料和废品，需运往区外销毁的，应凭加工区管理委员会和环保部门的批件，向主管海关办理出区手续，海关予以免进口许可证件、免税。

**Article 20** Processed products of enterprises within the zone, as well as leftovers, defective products, waste, etc. generated in the process of processing and production, shall be shipped out of the territory. Where it is necessary to transport them to outside the zone due to special circumstances, the enterprise shall apply, and after approval by the competent customs, the classification and duty shall be determined based on the condition at the time of domestic sale. If the goods are subject to import license management, an import license shall be exempted. If the goods are listed in the Catalogue of Restricted Imports of Waste that Can Be Used as Raw Materials, an import license shall be obtained from the environmental protection department in accordance with the current provisions. For leftovers and waste that have no commercial value and need to be transported outside the zone for destruction, the exit formalities shall be handled with the competent customs on the basis of the approval documents of the Administrative Committee of the Processing Zone and the environmental protection department, and the Customs shall exempt them from import license and duty.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　区内企业在确有需要时，可将有关模具、半成品等运往区外进行加工。经加工区主管海关关长批准，由接受委托的区外企业向加工区主管海关缴纳货物应征关税和进口环节增值税等值保证金或保函后办理出区手续。  
委托区外企业加工的期限由加工区主管海关参照合同期限核定。货物加工完毕后应按期运回区内。区内企业凭出区时填写的委托区外加工申请书及有关单证，向加工区主管海关办理验放核销手续。加工区主管海关在办理验放核销手续后，应及时退还保证金或保函。

**Article 21** Where an enterprise within the zone has a genuine need, it may transport relevant moulds, semi-finished products, etc., outside the zone for processing. Upon approval by the chief of the customs office in charge of the processing zone, the entrusted enterprise outside the zone shall pay a guarantee deposit or provide a letter of guarantee equivalent to the customs duties and import value-added taxes payable on the goods to the customs office in charge of the processing zone, and then go through the formalities for exit from the zone.  
The time limit for processing entrusted to an enterprise outside the zone shall be verified by the customs office in charge of the processing zone by reference to the contract term. After the processing of the goods is completed, they shall be transported back into the zone within the prescribed time limit. The enterprise within the zone shall, by presenting the application for entrusted processing outside the zone filled out at the time of exit and relevant documents, go through the formalities for inspection, release, and verification with the customs office in charge of the processing zone. After completing the formalities for inspection, release, and verification, the customs office in charge of the processing zone shall promptly refund the guarantee deposit or release the letter of guarantee.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　区内企业销往区外的机器、设备、模具等，按照国家现行进口政策及有关规定办理。

**Article 22** Machines, equipment, moulds, etc., sold by an enterprise within the zone to an entity outside the zone shall be handled in accordance with the current State import policies and relevant regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　区内企业经主管海关批准，可在区外进行产品的测试、检验和展示活动。测试、检验和展示的产品，应比照海关对暂时进口货物的管理规定办理出区手续。

**Article 23** An enterprise within the zone may, with the approval of the competent customs office, conduct product testing, inspection, and display activities outside the zone. For products subject to testing, inspection, and display, the formalities for exit from the zone shall be handled by analogy with the customs provisions on the administration of temporary importation of goods.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　区内使用的机器、设备、模具和办公用品等，须运往区外进行维修、测试或检验时，区内企业或管理机构应填写《出口加工区货物运往区外维修查验联系单》，向主管海关提出申请，并经主管海关核准、登记、查验后，方可将机器、设备、模具和办公用品等运往区外维修、测试或检验。  
区内企业将模具运往区外维修、测试或检验时，应留存模具所生产产品的样品，以备海关对运回区内的模具进行核查。  
运往区外维修、测试或检验的机器、设备、模具和办公用品等，不得用于区外加工生产和使用。

**Article 24** Where machines, equipment, moulds, office supplies, etc., used within the zone need to be transported outside the zone for repair, testing, or inspection, the enterprise or administrative organ within the zone shall fill out the "Contact Sheet for Transportation of Goods from the Export Processing Zone to Outside the Zone for Repair and Inspection", submit an application to the competent customs office, and only after approval, registration, and inspection by the competent customs office may the machines, equipment, moulds, office supplies, etc., be transported outside the zone for repair, testing, or inspection.  
Where an enterprise within the zone transports moulds outside the zone for repair, testing, or inspection, it shall retain samples of the products produced by such moulds for customs verification of the moulds upon their return to the zone.  
Machines, equipment, moulds, office supplies, etc., transported outside the zone for repair, testing, or inspection shall not be used for processing, production, or any other use outside the zone.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　运往区外维修、测试或检验的机器、设备、模具和办公用品等，应自运出之日起2个月内运回加工区。因特殊情况不能如期运回的，区内企业应于期限届满前7天内，向主管海关说明情况，并申请延期。申请延期以1次为限，延长期限不得超过1个月。

**Article 25** Machines, equipment, moulds, office supplies, etc., transported outside the zone for repair, testing, or inspection shall be transported back into the processing zone within two months from the date of exit. If, due to special circumstances, they cannot be returned within the time limit, the enterprise within the zone shall, within seven days prior to the expiry of the time limit, explain the situation to the competent customs office and apply for an extension. The application for extension shall be limited to one time, and the extension period shall not exceed one month.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　运往区外维修的机器、设备、模具和办公用品等，运回区内时，要以海关能辨认其为原物或同一规格的新零件、配件或附件为限，但更换新零件、配件或附件的，原零件、配件或附件应一并运回区内。

**Article 26** Where machines, equipment, moulds, office supplies, etc., transported outside the zone for repair are returned to the zone, they shall be limited to such items that the customs can identify as the original items or new parts, fittings, or accessories of the same specifications; provided that if new parts, fittings, or accessories are replaced, the original parts, fittings, or accessories shall be transported back into the zone together with them.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　从区外进入加工区的货物视同出口，办理出口报关手续。其出口退税，除法律、法规另有规定外，按照以下规定办理：  
(一)从区外进入加工区供区内企业使用的国产机器、设备、原材料、零部件、元器件、包装物料以及建造基础设施、加工企业和行政管理部门生产、办公用房所需合理数量的基建物资等，海关按照对出口货物的有关规定办理报关手续，并签发出口退税报关单。区外企业凭报关单出口退税联向税务部门申请办理出口退(免)税手续，具体退(免)税管理办法由国家税务总局另行下达。  
(二)从区外进入加工区供区内企业和行政管理机构使用的生活消费用品、交通运输工具等，海关不予签发出口退税报关单。  
(三)从区外进入加工区的进口机器、设备、原材料、零部件、元器件、包装物料、基建物资等，区外企业应当向海关提供上述货物或物品的清单，并办理出口报关手续，经海关查验后放行。上述货物或物品，已经缴纳的进口环节税，不予退还。  
(四)因国内技术无法达到产品要求、须将国家禁止出口或统一经营商品运至加工区内进行某项工序加工的，应报经商务部批准，海关比照出料加工管理办法进行监管，其运入加工区的货物，不予签发出口退税报关单。

**Article 27** Goods entering the processing zone from outside the zone shall be deemed as exports and shall go through export customs declaration formalities. Unless otherwise provided by laws or regulations, the export tax rebate (exemption) for such goods shall be handled in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) For domestic machines, equipment, raw materials, parts, components, packaging materials, and a reasonable quantity of infrastructure materials for the construction of infrastructure, processing enterprises, and office buildings for administrative departments, which enter the processing zone from outside the zone for use by enterprises within the zone, the customs shall go through the customs declaration formalities in accordance with the relevant provisions on export goods, and shall issue an export tax rebate customs declaration form. The enterprise outside the zone shall, by presenting the export tax rebate copy of the customs declaration form, apply to the tax authority for export tax rebate (exemption) formalities. The specific measures for the administration of tax rebate (exemption) shall be separately issued by the State Administration of Taxation.  
(2) For consumer goods, means of transport, etc., which enter the processing zone from outside the zone for use by enterprises and administrative organs within the zone, the customs shall not issue an export tax rebate customs declaration form.  
(3) For imported machines, equipment, raw materials, parts, components, packaging materials, infrastructure materials, etc., which enter the processing zone from outside the zone, the enterprise outside the zone shall provide the customs with a list of the aforesaid goods or articles, go through export customs declaration formalities, and shall be released by the customs after inspection. The customs duties and taxes that have already been paid on the aforesaid goods or articles at the import stage shall not be refunded.  
(4) Where, because domestic technology cannot meet product requirements, commodities that are prohibited from export or subject to centralized operation by the State must be transported into the processing zone for certain processing procedures, prior approval from the Ministry of Commerce shall be obtained. The customs shall supervise such goods by analogy with the administrative measures for outward processing, and no export tax rebate customs declaration form shall be issued for such goods transported into the processing zone.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　从区外进入加工区的货物、物品，应运入加工区内海关指定仓库或地点，区外企业填写出口报关单，并持境内购货发票、装箱单，向加工区的主管海关办理报关手续。

**Article 28** Goods and articles entering the processing zone from outside the zone shall be transported to the warehouse or location designated by the customs within the processing zone. The enterprise outside the zone shall fill out an export customs declaration form and, together with the domestic purchase invoice and packing list, go through the customs declaration formalities with the competent customs office of the processing zone.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　从区外进入加工区的货物，须经区内企业进行实质性加工后，方可运出境外。

**Article 29** Goods entering the processing zone from outside the zone shall be subject to substantive processing by an enterprise within the zone before they may be transported out of the country.

### 第四章　对加工区内货物的监管

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　区内企业进、出加工区的货物须向其主管海关如实申报，海关依据备案清单及有关单证，对区内企业进、出加工区的货物进行查验、放行和核销。  
海关对进、出加工区货物的备案、报关、查验、放行、核销手续应在区内办理。

**Article 30** Goods entering or leaving the processing zone by an enterprise within the zone shall be truthfully declared to the competent customs office. The customs shall, based on the filing list and relevant documents, inspect, release, and verify the goods entering or leaving the processing zone by the enterprise within the zone.  
The customs formalities for filing, declaration, inspection, release, and verification of goods entering or leaving the processing zone shall be handled within the zone.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　加工区内的货物可在区内企业之间转让、转移，双方当事人须事先将转让、转移货物的具体品名、数量、金额等有关事项向海关备案。

**Article 31** Goods within the processing zone may be transferred or shifted among enterprises in the zone. The parties shall file with the Customs in advance the specific names, quantities, amounts, and other relevant matters regarding the transferred or shifted goods.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　区内加工企业，不得将未经实质性加工的进口原材料、零部件销往区外。区内从事仓储服务的企业，不得将仓储的原材料、零部件提供给区外企业。

**Article 32** Processing enterprises within the zone shall not sell imported raw materials or parts that have not undergone substantive processing to entities outside the zone. Enterprises providing warehousing services within the zone shall not provide warehoused raw materials or parts to enterprises outside the zone.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　区内企业自开展出口加工业务或仓储业务之日起，每半年持本企业账册和有关单据，向其主管海关办理一次核销手续。

**Article 33** From the date on which an enterprise within the zone commences export processing business or warehousing business, it shall, every six months, present its own account books and relevant documents to the Customs office in charge to undergo verification and cancellation procedures once.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　进入加工区的货物，在加工、储存期间，因不可抗力造成短少、损毁的，区内加工企业或仓储企业应自发现之日起10日内报告主管海关，并说明理由。经海关核实确认后，准其在账册内减除。

**Article 34** Where goods entering the processing zone are, during processing or storage, short-delivered or damaged due to force majeure, the processing enterprise or warehousing enterprise within the zone shall, within 10 days from the date of discovery, report to the Customs office in charge and state the reasons. After verification and confirmation by the Customs, the enterprise shall be permitted to deduct the goods from its account books.

### 第五章　对加工区之间往来货物的监管

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　加工区之间货物的往来，应由收、发货物双方联名向转出区主管海关提出申请。经海关核准后，按照转关运输的有关规定办理。

**Article 35** The movement of goods between processing zones shall be applied for jointly by both the receiving and the sending parties to the Customs office in charge of the transshipping zone. Upon approval by the Customs, it shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions on transit transport.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　货物转关至其他加工区时，转入区主管海关在核对封志完整及单货相符后，即予放行入厂或入库。

**Article 36** When goods are transshipped to another processing zone, the Customs office in charge of the receiving zone shall, after verifying that the seals are intact and that the documents correspond to the goods, promptly release the goods for entry into the factory or warehouse.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　加工区之间往来的货物不能按照转关运输办理的，转入区主管海关应向收货企业收取货物等值的担保金。货物运抵转入区并经海关核对无误后，主管海关应在10个工作日内，将担保金退还企业。

**Article 37** Where goods moving between processing zones cannot be handled in accordance with transit transport, the Customs office in charge of the receiving zone shall collect from the receiving enterprise a deposit equal to the value of the goods. After the goods arrive in the receiving zone and are verified by the Customs to be correct, the Customs office in charge shall, within 10 working days, return the deposit to the enterprise.

### 第六章　对进、出加工区运输工具和个人携带物品的监管

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　运输工具和人员应经海关指定的专用通道进、出加工区。

**Article 38** Means of transport and persons shall enter and exit the processing zone through the dedicated passage designated by the Customs.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　从加工区运往境外的加工产品及由加工区运往区外的货物，经海关查验放行后，应交由经海关核准、并由设立于区内的专营运输企业承运。下列货物经主管海关查验后，可由企业指派专人携带或自行运输：  
(一)价值1万美元及以下的小额物品；  
(二)因品质不合格复运区外退换的物品；  
(三)已办理进口纳税手续的物品；  
(四)其他经海关核准的物品。

**Article 39** Processed products shipped from the processing zone to destinations outside the territory, and goods shipped from the processing zone to places outside the zone, after being inspected and released by the Customs, shall be entrusted for carriage to a specialized transport enterprise approved by the Customs and established within the zone. The following goods, after being inspected by the Customs office in charge, may be carried or transported by the enterprise itself with a designated person:  
(1) small-value items with a value of USD 10,000 or less;  
(2) items returned to outside the zone for exchange due to substandard quality;  
(3) items for which import duty payment procedures have been completed; and  
(4) other items approved by the Customs.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　进、出加工区货物的运输工具的负责人，应持企业法人营业执照和运输工具的名称、数量、牌照号码及驾驶员姓名等清单，向海关办理登记备案手续。  
承运加工区货物进、出加工区或转关运输的所有运输企业的经营人，应遵守海关有关运输工具及其所载货物的管理规定，并承担相关的法律责任。

**Article 40** The person in charge of the means of transport for goods entering or exiting the processing zone shall, holding the enterprise's business license of legal person and a list including the names, quantities, license plate numbers of the means of transport and the names of drivers, undergo registration and filing procedures with the Customs.  
All operators of transport enterprises that carry goods into or out of the processing zone or that provide transit transport of goods in the processing zone shall comply with the Customs' administrative provisions on means of transport and the goods carried thereon, and shall assume the relevant legal liability.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　未经海关批准，从加工区到区外的运输工具和人员不得运输、携带加工区内货物出区。

**Article 41** Without the approval of the Customs, transport vehicles and personnel from the processing zone to outside the zone shall not transport or carry goods from within the processing zone out of the zone.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　从境外运入加工区的货物和从加工区运出境外的货物列入进、出口统计。从区外运入加工区和从加工区运往区外的货物，实施单项统计。统计办法由海关总署另行制定。

**Article 42** Goods transported into the processing zone from outside the territory and goods transported out of the processing zone to outside the territory shall be included in import and export statistics. Goods transported into the processing zone from outside the zone and goods transported from the processing zone to outside the zone shall be subject to single-item statistics. The statistical measures shall be separately formulated by the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反本办法规定的，由海关依照《中华人民共和国海关法》及《中华人民共和国海关行政处罚实施条例》的有关规定进行处理。

**Article 43** Anyone who violates the provisions of these Measures shall be dealt with by the Customs in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China and the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Implementation of Customs Administrative Penalties.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　本办法自2000年5月24日起施行。

**Article 44** These Measures shall come into force as of May 24, 2000.
