# 中华人民共和国反补贴条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2004-03-31

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护对外贸易秩序和公平竞争，根据《中华人民共和国对外贸易法》的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Foreign Trade Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of maintaining the order of foreign trade and fair competition.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　进口产品存在补贴，并对已经建立的国内产业造成实质损害或者产生实质损害威胁，或者对建立国内产业造成实质阻碍的，依照本条例的规定进行调查，采取反补贴措施。

**Article 2** Where an imported product is subsidized and causes material injury to a domestic industry already established, or threatens to cause material injury, or materially retards the establishment of a domestic industry, an investigation shall be conducted and countervailing measures shall be taken in accordance with these Regulations.

### 第二章　补贴与损害

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　补贴，是指出口国（地区）政府或者其任何公共机构提供的并为接受者带来利益的财政资助以及任何形式的收入或者价格支持。  
出口国（地区）政府或者其任何公共机构，以下统称出口国（地区）政府。  
本条第一款所称财政资助，包括：  
（一）出口国（地区）政府以拨款、贷款、资本注入等形式直接提供资金，或者以贷款担保等形式潜在地直接转让资金或者债务；  
（二）出口国（地区）政府放弃或者不收缴应收收入；  
（三）出口国（地区）政府提供除一般基础设施以外的货物、服务，或者由出口国（地区）政府购买货物；  
（四）出口国（地区）政府通过向筹资机构付款，或者委托、指令私营机构履行上述职能。

**Article 3** A subsidy means a financial contribution or any form of income or price support provided by the government of an exporting country (region) or any public body thereof which confers a benefit to the recipient.  
The government of an exporting country (region) or any public body thereof is hereinafter collectively referred to as the government of the exporting country (region).  
The term 'financial contribution' in the first paragraph of this Article includes:  
(1) direct transfers of funds by the government of the exporting country (region) in the form of grants, loans, capital injection, etc., or potential direct transfers of funds or liabilities in the form of loan guarantees, etc.;  
(2) government revenue that is otherwise due is foregone or not collected by the government of the exporting country (region);  
(3) the government of the exporting country (region) provides goods or services other than general infrastructure, or purchases goods from the government of the exporting country (region);  
(4) the government of the exporting country (region) makes payments to a funding mechanism, or entrusts or directs a private body to carry out the functions described above.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　依照本条例进行调查、采取反补贴措施的补贴，必须具有专向性。  
具有下列情形之一的补贴，具有专向性：  
（一）由出口国（地区）政府明确确定的某些企业、产业获得的补贴；  
（二）由出口国（地区）法律、法规明确规定的某些企业、产业获得的补贴；  
（三）指定特定区域内的企业、产业获得的补贴；  
（四）以出口实绩为条件获得的补贴，包括本条例所附出口补贴清单列举的各项补贴；  
（五）以使用本国（地区）产品替代进口产品为条件获得的补贴。  
在确定补贴专向性时，还应当考虑受补贴企业的数量和企业受补贴的数额、比例、时间以及给与补贴的方式等因素。

**Article 4** Subsidies subject to investigation and countervailing measures in accordance with these Regulations must be specific.  
A subsidy falls within the following circumstances shall be deemed specific:  
(1) the subsidy is explicitly limited to certain enterprises or industries by the government of the exporting country (region);  
(2) the subsidy is explicitly limited to certain enterprises or industries by the laws or regulations of the exporting country (region);  
(3) the subsidy is limited to enterprises or industries within a designated geographical region;  
(4) the subsidy is contingent upon export performance, including the subsidies listed in the Illustrative List of Export Subsidies annexed to these Regulations;  
(5) the subsidy is contingent upon the use of domestic (regional) products over imported products.  
When determining the specificity of a subsidy, factors such as the number of subsidized enterprises, the amount, proportion, and duration of subsidies received by enterprises, and the method of granting the subsidy shall also be considered.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对补贴的调查和确定，由商务部负责。

**Article 5** The Ministry of Commerce shall be responsible for the investigation and determination of subsidies.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　进口产品的补贴金额，应当区别不同情况，按照下列方式计算：  
（一）以无偿拨款形式提供补贴的，补贴金额以企业实际接受的金额计算；  
（二）以贷款形式提供补贴的，补贴金额以接受贷款的企业在正常商业贷款条件下应支付的利息与该项贷款的利息差额计算；  
（三）以贷款担保形式提供补贴的，补贴金额以在没有担保情况下企业应支付的利息与有担保情况下企业实际支付的利息之差计算；  
（四）以注入资本形式提供补贴的，补贴金额以企业实际接受的资本金额计算；  
（五）以提供货物或者服务形式提供补贴的，补贴金额以该项货物或者服务的正常市场价格与企业实际支付的价格之差计算；  
（六）以购买货物形式提供补贴的，补贴金额以政府实际支付价格与该项货物正常市场价格之差计算；  
（七）以放弃或者不收缴应收收入形式提供补贴的，补贴金额以依法应缴金额与企业实际缴纳金额之差计算。  
对前款所列形式以外的其他补贴，按照公平、合理的方式确定补贴金额。

**Article 6** The amount of a subsidy on an imported product shall be calculated in the following manner, depending on the circumstances:  
(1) where the subsidy is granted in the form of a grant, the amount of the subsidy shall be the amount actually received by the enterprise;  
(2) where the subsidy is granted in the form of a loan, the amount of the subsidy shall be the difference between the interest that the enterprise receiving the loan would have paid under normal commercial loan conditions and the interest actually paid on the loan;  
(3) where the subsidy is granted in the form of a loan guarantee, the amount of the subsidy shall be the difference between the interest that the enterprise would have paid without the guarantee and the interest actually paid by the enterprise with the guarantee;  
(4) where the subsidy is granted in the form of a capital injection, the amount of the subsidy shall be the amount of capital actually received by the enterprise;  
(5) where the subsidy is granted in the form of provision of goods or services, the amount of the subsidy shall be the difference between the normal market price of such goods or services and the price actually paid by the enterprise;  
(6) where the subsidy is granted in the form of purchase of goods, the amount of the subsidy shall be the difference between the price actually paid by the government and the normal market price of such goods;  
(7) where the subsidy is granted in the form of foregoing or not collecting revenue that is otherwise due, the amount of the subsidy shall be the difference between the amount payable according to law and the amount actually paid by the enterprise.  
For subsidies other than those listed in the preceding paragraph, the amount of the subsidy shall be determined in a fair and reasonable manner.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　损害，是指补贴对已经建立的国内产业造成实质损害或者产生实质损害威胁，或者对建立国内产业造成实质阻碍。  
对损害的调查和确定，由商务部负责；其中，涉及农产品的反补贴国内产业损害调查，由商务部会同农业部进行。

**Article 7** Injury means material injury to a domestic industry already established, threat of material injury, or material retardation of the establishment of a domestic industry, caused by the subsidy.  
The Ministry of Commerce shall be responsible for the investigation and determination of injury; where the investigation of injury to the domestic industry for countervailing measures involves agricultural products, the Ministry of Commerce shall conduct the investigation jointly with the Ministry of Agriculture.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　在确定补贴对国内产业造成的损害时，应当审查下列事项：  
（一）补贴可能对贸易造成的影响；  
（二）补贴进口产品的数量，包括补贴进口产品的绝对数量或者相对于国内同类产品生产或者消费的数量是否大量增加，或者补贴进口产品大量增加的可能性；  
（三）补贴进口产品的价格，包括补贴进口产品的价格削减或者对国内同类产品的价格产生大幅度抑制、压低等影响；  
（四）补贴进口产品对国内产业的相关经济因素和指标的影响；  
（五）补贴进口产品出口国（地区）、原产国（地区）的生产能力、出口能力，被调查产品的库存情况；  
（六）造成国内产业损害的其他因素。  
对实质损害威胁的确定，应当依据事实，不得仅依据指控、推测或者极小的可能性。  
在确定补贴对国内产业造成的损害时，应当依据肯定性证据，不得将造成损害的非补贴因素归因于补贴。

**Article 8** When determining the injury caused by a subsidy to a domestic industry, the following matters shall be examined:  
(1) the effect of the subsidy on trade;  
(2) the volume of the subsidized imported products, including whether there has been a significant increase in the absolute volume of subsidized imports or relative to production or consumption of the like domestic product, or the likelihood of a significant increase in subsidized imports;  
(3) the price of the subsidized imported products, including whether there has been price undercutting or significant price depression or suppression of prices of like domestic products;  
(4) the impact of subsidized imports on the relevant economic factors and indices of the domestic industry;  
(5) the production capacity and export capacity of the exporting country (region) or the country (region) of origin of the subsidized imports, and the inventory situation of the products under investigation;  
(6) other factors causing injury to the domestic industry.  
A determination of threat of material injury shall be based on facts and not merely on allegation, conjecture, or remote possibility.  
When determining injury caused by a subsidy to the domestic industry, positive evidence shall be relied upon, and the injury caused by non-subsidy factors shall not be attributed to the subsidy.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　补贴进口产品来自两个以上国家（地区），并且同时满足下列条件的，可以就补贴进口产品对国内产业造成的影响进行累积评估：  
（一）来自每一国家（地区）的补贴进口产品的补贴金额不属于微量补贴，并且其进口量不属于可忽略不计的；  
（二）根据补贴进口产品之间的竞争条件以及补贴进口产品与国内同类产品之间的竞争条件，进行累积评估是适当的。  
微量补贴，是指补贴金额不足产品价值1%的补贴；但是，来自发展中国家（地区）的补贴进口产品的微量补贴，是指补贴金额不足产品价值2%的补贴。

**Article 9** Where subsidized imports from more than two countries (regions) simultaneously meet the following conditions, a cumulative assessment of the impact of such subsidized imports on the domestic industry may be conducted:  
(1) the amount of the subsidy from each country (region) is not de minimis and the volume of imports from each country (region) is not negligible;  
(2) it is appropriate to conduct a cumulative assessment in light of the conditions of competition between the subsidized imports and the conditions of competition between the subsidized imports and the like domestic product.  
A de minimis subsidy means a subsidy that is less than 1% ad valorem; however, for subsidized imports from a developing country (region), a de minimis subsidy means a subsidy that is less than 2% ad valorem.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　评估补贴进口产品的影响，应当对国内同类产品的生产进行单独确定。不能对国内同类产品的生产进行单独确定的，应当审查包括国内同类产品在内的最窄产品组或者范围的生产。

**Article 10** When assessing the impact of subsidized imports, the production of the like domestic product shall be identified separately. Where it is not possible to separately identify the production of the like domestic product, the production of the narrowest group or range of products that includes the like domestic product shall be examined.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国内产业，是指中华人民共和国国内同类产品的全部生产者，或者其总产量占国内同类产品全部总产量的主要部分的生产者；但是，国内生产者与出口经营者或者进口经营者有关联的，或者其本身为补贴产品或者同类产品的进口经营者的，应当除外。  
在特殊情形下，国内一个区域市场中的生产者，在该市场中销售其全部或者几乎全部的同类产品，并且该市场中同类产品的需求主要不是由国内其他地方的生产者供给的，可以视为一个单独产业。

**Article 11** 'Domestic industry' means all domestic producers of like products in the People's Republic of China, or those whose collective output constitutes a major proportion of the total domestic production of like products; provided that, domestic producers that are related to exporters or importers, or are themselves importers of the subsidized product or like product, shall be excluded.  
Under exceptional circumstances, the producers within a regional market in China who sell all or almost all of their like product in that market, and where the demand for the like product in that market is not mainly supplied by producers elsewhere in China, may be regarded as a separate industry.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　同类产品，是指与补贴进口产品相同的产品；没有相同产品的，以与补贴进口产品的特性最相似的产品为同类产品。

**Article 12** 'Like product' means a product which is identical to the subsidized imported product; in the absence of such identical product, a product which has characteristics most closely resembling that of the subsidized imported product.

### 第三章　反补贴调查

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国内产业或者代表国内产业的自然人、法人或者有关组织（以下统称申请人），可以依照本条例的规定向商务部提出反补贴调查的书面申请。

**Article 13** The domestic industry, or a natural person, legal person, or relevant organization representing the domestic industry (hereinafter collectively referred to as the 'applicant'), may submit a written application for a countervailing investigation to the Ministry of Commerce in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　申请书应当包括下列内容：  
（一）申请人的名称、地址及有关情况；  
（二）对申请调查的进口产品的完整说明，包括产品名称、所涉及的出口国（地区）或者原产国（地区）、已知的出口经营者或者生产者等；  
（三）对国内同类产品生产的数量和价值的说明；  
（四）申请调查进口产品的数量和价格对国内产业的影响；  
（五）申请人认为需要说明的其他内容。

**Article 14** The application shall include the following:  
(1) the name, address, and relevant information of the applicant;  
(2) a complete description of the imported product subject to the application for investigation, including the product name, the exporting country (region) or country (region) of origin involved, known exporters or producers, etc.;  
(3) a description of the volume and value of domestic production of the like product;  
(4) a description of the impact of the volume and price of the imported product subject to the application for investigation on the domestic industry;  
(5) any other content that the applicant considers necessary to explain.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　申请书应当附具下列证据：  
（一）申请调查的进口产品存在补贴；  
（二）对国内产业的损害；  
（三）补贴与损害之间存在因果关系。

**Article 15** The application shall be accompanied by the following evidence:  
(1) evidence of the existence of a subsidy on the imported product subject to the application for investigation;  
(2) evidence of injury to the domestic industry;  
(3) evidence of a causal link between the subsidy and the injury.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　商务部应当自收到申请人提交的申请书及有关证据之日起60天内，对申请是否由国内产业或者代表国内产业提出、申请书内容及所附具的证据等进行审查，并决定立案调查或者不立案调查。在特殊情形下，可以适当延长审查期限。  
在决定立案调查前，应当就有关补贴事项向产品可能被调查的国家（地区）政府发出进行磋商的邀请。

**Article 16** The Ministry of Commerce shall, within 60 days from the date of receipt of the application and the relevant evidence submitted by the applicant, examine whether the application is made by or on behalf of the domestic industry, the content of the application and the evidence attached, and decide to initiate or not to initiate an investigation. Under exceptional circumstances, the period for examination may be extended appropriately.  
Before deciding to initiate an investigation, the Ministry of Commerce shall invite the government of the country (region) whose products may be subject to investigation for consultations on the matters relating to the subsidy.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在表示支持申请或者反对申请的国内产业中，支持者的产量占支持者和反对者的总产量的50%以上的，应当认定申请是由国内产业或者代表国内产业提出，可以启动反补贴调查；但是，表示支持申请的国内生产者的产量不足国内同类产品总产量的25%的，不得启动反补贴调查。

**Article 17** Where the output of those who express support for the application accounts for more than 50% of the total output of both supporters and opposers among the domestic industry expressing support or opposition, the application shall be deemed to be made by or on behalf of the domestic industry, and a countervailing investigation may be initiated; provided that, where the output of the domestic producers expressing support for the application accounts for less than 25% of the total output of the like domestic product, no countervailing investigation shall be initiated.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　在特殊情形下，商务部没有收到反补贴调查的书面申请，但有充分证据认为存在补贴和损害以及二者之间有因果关系的，可以决定立案调查。

**Article 18** Under exceptional circumstances, the Ministry of Commerce may decide to initiate an investigation even if it has not received a written application for a countervailing investigation, provided that it has sufficient evidence of the existence of a subsidy, injury, and a causal link between them.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　立案调查的决定，由商务部予以公告，并通知申请人、已知的出口经营者、进口经营者以及其他有利害关系的组织、个人（以下统称利害关系方）和出口国（地区）政府。  
立案调查的决定一经公告，商务部应当将申请书文本提供给已知的出口经营者和出口国（地区）政府。

**Article 19** The decision to initiate an investigation shall be announced by the Ministry of Commerce, and shall be notified to the applicant, known exporters, importers, and other interested organizations and individuals (hereinafter collectively referred to as 'interested parties') and the government of the exporting country (region).  
Once the decision to initiate an investigation is announced, the Ministry of Commerce shall provide the text of the application to known exporters and the government of the exporting country (region).

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　商务部可以采用问卷、抽样、听证会、现场核查等方式向利害关系方了解情况，进行调查。  
商务部应当为有关利害关系方、利害关系国（地区）政府提供陈述意见和论据的机会。  
商务部认为必要时，可以派出工作人员赴有关国家（地区）进行调查；但是，有关国家（地区）提出异议的除外。

**Article 20** The Ministry of Commerce may use methods such as questionnaires, sampling, hearings, and on-site verification to gather information and conduct investigations from the interested parties.  
The Ministry of Commerce shall provide the relevant interested parties and the governments of interested countries (regions) with the opportunity to present their views and arguments.  
When the Ministry of Commerce considers it necessary, it may send its staff to conduct investigations in the relevant country (region); except where the relevant country (region) raises objections.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　商务部进行调查时，利害关系方、利害关系国（地区）政府应当如实反映情况，提供有关资料。利害关系方、利害关系国（地区）政府不如实反映情况、提供有关资料的，或者没有在合理时间内提供必要信息的，或者以其他方式严重妨碍调查的，商务部可以根据可获得的事实作出裁定。

**Article 21** When the Ministry of Commerce conducts an investigation, interested parties and the governments of interested countries (regions) shall truthfully reflect the situation and provide relevant information. If an interested party or the government of an interested country (region) fails to truthfully reflect the situation or provide relevant information, or fails to provide necessary information within a reasonable time, or otherwise seriously impedes the investigation, the Ministry of Commerce may make a determination based on the facts available.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　利害关系方、利害关系国（地区）政府认为其提供的资料泄露后将产生严重不利影响的，可以向商务部申请对该资料按保密资料处理。  
商务部认为保密申请有正当理由的，应当对利害关系方、利害关系国（地区）政府提供的资料按保密资料处理，同时要求利害关系方、利害关系国（地区）政府提供一份非保密的该资料概要。  
按保密资料处理的资料，未经提供资料的利害关系方、利害关系国（地区）政府同意，不得泄露。

**Article 22** Where an interested party or the government of an interested country (region) considers that disclosure of the information it provides will cause serious adverse effects, it may apply to the Ministry of Commerce for confidential treatment of such information.  
If the Ministry of Commerce considers the application for confidential treatment to be justified, it shall treat the information provided by the interested party or the government of the interested country (region) as confidential, and shall at the same time require the interested party or the government of the interested country (region) to provide a non-confidential summary of such information.  
Information treated as confidential shall not be disclosed without the consent of the interested party or the government of the interested country (region) that provided the information.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　商务部应当允许申请人、利害关系方和利害关系国（地区）政府查阅本案有关资料；但是，属于按保密资料处理的除外。

**Article 23** The Ministry of Commerce shall allow the applicant, interested parties, and the governments of interested countries (regions) to inspect the relevant information in the case; except for information treated as confidential.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　在反补贴调查期间，应当给予产品被调查的国家（地区）政府继续进行磋商的合理机会。磋商不妨碍商务部根据本条例的规定进行调查，并采取反补贴措施。

**Article 24** During the countervailing investigation, the government of the country (region) whose product is subject to investigation shall be given a reasonable opportunity to continue consultations. Such consultations shall not preclude the Ministry of Commerce from conducting the investigation and taking countervailing measures in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　商务部根据调查结果，就补贴、损害和二者之间的因果关系是否成立作出初裁决定，并予以公告。

**Article 25** The Ministry of Commerce shall, based on the results of the investigation, make a preliminary determination as to whether the subsidy, injury, and the causal link between them exist, and shall announce such determination.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　初裁决定确定补贴、损害以及二者之间的因果关系成立的，商务部应当对补贴及补贴金额、损害及损害程度继续进行调查，并根据调查结果作出终裁决定，予以公告。  
在作出终裁决定前，应当由商务部将终裁决定所依据的基本事实通知所有已知的利害关系方、利害关系国（地区）政府。

**Article 26** Where a preliminary determination establishes the existence of a subsidy, injury, and a causal link between them, the Ministry of Commerce shall continue the investigation into the subsidy and the amount thereof, as well as the injury and the degree thereof, and shall make a final determination based on the results of the investigation, and announce such determination.  
Before making a final determination, the Ministry of Commerce shall notify all known interested parties and the governments of interested countries (regions) of the essential facts on which the final determination is based.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　反补贴调查，应当自立案调查决定公告之日起12个月内结束；特殊情况下可以延长，但延长期不得超过6个月。

**Article 27** A countervailing investigation shall be concluded within 12 months from the date of announcement of the decision to initiate the investigation; under special circumstances, it may be extended, but the extension shall not exceed 6 months.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　有下列情形之一的，反补贴调查应当终止，并由商务部予以公告：  
（一）申请人撤销申请的；  
（二）没有足够证据证明存在补贴、损害或者二者之间有因果关系的；  
（三）补贴金额为微量补贴的；  
（四）补贴进口产品实际或者潜在的进口量或者损害属于可忽略不计的；  
（五）通过与有关国家（地区）政府磋商达成协议，不需要继续进行反补贴调查的；  
（六）商务部认为不适宜继续进行反补贴调查的。  
来自一个或者部分国家（地区）的被调查产品有前款第（二）、（三）、（四）、（五）项所列情形之一的，针对所涉产品的反补贴调查应当终止。

**Article 28** A countervailing investigation shall be terminated in any of the following circumstances, and the Ministry of Commerce shall announce such termination:  
(1) the applicant withdraws the application;  
(2) there is insufficient evidence to prove the existence of a subsidy, injury, or a causal link between them;  
(3) the amount of the subsidy is de minimis;  
(4) the actual or potential volume of subsidized imports or the injury is negligible;  
(5) an agreement has been reached through consultations with the relevant government of a country (region) such that it is unnecessary to continue the countervailing investigation;  
(6) the Ministry of Commerce considers it inappropriate to continue the countervailing investigation.  
Where the investigated products from one or some countries (regions) fall under any of the circumstances specified in subparagraphs (2), (3), (4), or (5) of the preceding paragraph, the countervailing investigation concerning such products shall be terminated.

### 第四章　反补贴措施

#### 第一节　临时措施

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　初裁决定确定补贴成立，并由此对国内产业造成损害的，可以采取临时反补贴措施。  
临时反补贴措施采取以保证金或者保函作为担保的征收临时反补贴税的形式。

**Article 29** Where a preliminary determination establishes the existence of a subsidy and injury to the domestic industry caused thereby, provisional countervailing measures may be taken.  
Provisional countervailing measures shall take the form of the imposition of provisional countervailing duties secured by a cash deposit or a bond.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　采取临时反补贴措施，由商务部提出建议，国务院关税税则委员会根据商务部的建议作出决定，由商务部予以公告。海关自公告规定实施之日起执行。

**Article 30** The Ministry of Commerce shall make a recommendation for the adoption of provisional countervailing measures, and the Customs Tariff Commission of the State Council shall make a decision based on the recommendation of the Ministry of Commerce, and the Ministry of Commerce shall announce the decision. The customs shall enforce the decision from the date specified in the announcement.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　临时反补贴措施实施的期限，自临时反补贴措施决定公告规定实施之日起，不超过4个月。  
自反补贴立案调查决定公告之日起60天内，不得采取临时反补贴措施。

**Article 31** The period of application of provisional countervailing measures shall not exceed four months from the date specified in the announcement of the decision on provisional countervailing measures.  
No provisional countervailing measures shall be taken within 60 days from the date of announcement of the decision to initiate a countervailing investigation.

#### 第二节　承诺

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　在反补贴调查期间，出口国（地区）政府提出取消、限制补贴或者其他有关措施的承诺，或者出口经营者提出修改价格的承诺的，商务部应当予以充分考虑。  
商务部可以向出口经营者或者出口国（地区）政府提出有关价格承诺的建议。  
商务部不得强迫出口经营者作出承诺。

**Article 32** During the countervailing investigation, if the government of the exporting country (region) offers undertakings to eliminate or limit the subsidy or take other measures, or if the exporter offers undertakings to revise the price, the Ministry of Commerce shall give due consideration to such undertakings.  
The Ministry of Commerce may suggest price undertakings to the exporter or the government of the exporting country (region).  
The Ministry of Commerce shall not compel the exporter to make an undertaking.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　出口经营者、出口国（地区）政府不作出承诺或者不接受有关价格承诺的建议的，不妨碍对反补贴案件的调查和确定。出口经营者继续补贴进口产品的，商务部有权确定损害威胁更有可能出现。

**Article 33** The failure of the exporter or the government of the exporting country (region) to make an undertaking or accept a suggestion for a price undertaking shall not preclude the investigation and determination of the countervailing case. If the exporter continues to subsidize the imported product, the Ministry of Commerce shall have the right to determine that the threat of injury is more likely to materialize.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　商务部认为承诺能够接受并符合公共利益的，可以决定中止或者终止反补贴调查，不采取临时反补贴措施或者征收反补贴税。中止或者终止反补贴调查的决定由商务部予以公告。  
商务部不接受承诺的，应当向有关出口经营者说明理由。  
商务部对补贴以及由补贴造成的损害作出肯定的初裁决定前，不得寻求或者接受承诺。在出口经营者作出承诺的情况下，未经其本国（地区）政府同意的，商务部不得寻求或者接受承诺。

**Article 34** Where the Ministry of Commerce considers that the undertaking is acceptable and in the public interest, it may decide to suspend or terminate the countervailing investigation without taking provisional countervailing measures or imposing countervailing duties. The decision to suspend or terminate the countervailing investigation shall be announced by the Ministry of Commerce.  
If the Ministry of Commerce does not accept the undertaking, it shall provide reasons to the relevant exporter.  
The Ministry of Commerce shall not seek or accept an undertaking before making a preliminary affirmative determination of the subsidy and injury caused thereby. Where an undertaking is made by the exporter, the Ministry of Commerce shall not seek or accept the undertaking without the consent of the government of its own country (region).

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　依照[本条例第三十四条](#art-34)第一款规定中止或者终止调查后，应出口国（地区）政府请求，商务部应当对补贴和损害继续进行调查；或者商务部认为有必要的，可以对补贴和损害继续进行调查。  
根据调查结果，作出补贴或者损害的否定裁定的，承诺自动失效；作出补贴和损害的肯定裁定的，承诺继续有效。

**Article 35** After the investigation is suspended or terminated in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 34 of these Regulations, the Ministry of Commerce shall, at the request of the government of the exporting country (region), continue the investigation into the subsidy and injury; or the Ministry of Commerce may, if it considers necessary, continue the investigation into the subsidy and injury.  
Based on the results of the investigation, if a negative determination of the subsidy or injury is made, the undertaking shall automatically lapse; if an affirmative determination of both the subsidy and injury is made, the undertaking shall remain in effect.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　商务部可以要求承诺已被接受的出口经营者或者出口国（地区）政府定期提供履行其承诺的有关情况、资料，并予以核实。

**Article 36** The Ministry of Commerce may require the exporter or the government of the exporting country (region) whose undertaking has been accepted to periodically provide information and materials relevant to the fulfillment of its undertaking, and shall verify such information.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　对违反承诺的，商务部依照本条例的规定，可以立即决定恢复反补贴调查；根据可获得的最佳信息，可以决定采取临时反补贴措施，并可以对实施临时反补贴措施前90天内进口的产品追溯征收反补贴税，但违反承诺前进口的产品除外。

**Article 37** Where an undertaking is violated, the Ministry of Commerce may, in accordance with these Regulations, immediately decide to resume the countervailing investigation; may decide to take provisional countervailing measures based on the best information available; and may impose countervailing duties retroactively on products imported within 90 days prior to the application of provisional countervailing measures, except for products imported before the violation of the undertaking.

#### 第三节　反补贴税

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　在为完成磋商的努力没有取得效果的情况下，终裁决定确定补贴成立，并由此对国内产业造成损害的，可以征收反补贴税。征收反补贴税应当符合公共利益。

**Article 38** Where the efforts to complete consultations have not produced results, and the final determination establishes the existence of a subsidy and injury to the domestic industry caused thereby, countervailing duties may be imposed. The imposition of countervailing duties shall be in the public interest.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　征收反补贴税，由商务部提出建议，国务院关税税则委员会根据商务部的建议作出决定，由商务部予以公告。海关自公告规定实施之日起执行。

**Article 39** The Ministry of Commerce shall make a recommendation for the imposition of countervailing duties, and the Customs Tariff Commission of the State Council shall make a decision based on the recommendation of the Ministry of Commerce, and the Ministry of Commerce shall announce the decision. The customs shall enforce the decision from the date specified in the announcement.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　反补贴税适用于终裁决定公告之日后进口的产品，但属于[本条例第三十七条](#art-37)、第四十四条、第四十五条规定的情形除外。

**Article 40** Countervailing duties shall apply to products imported after the date of announcement of the final determination, except for the circumstances specified in Articles 37, 44, and 45 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　反补贴税的纳税人为补贴进口产品的进口经营者。

**Article 41** The taxpayer for countervailing duties shall be the importer of the subsidized imported products.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　反补贴税应当根据不同出口经营者的补贴金额，分别确定。对实际上未被调查的出口经营者的补贴进口产品，需要征收反补贴税的，应当迅速审查，按照合理的方式确定对其适用的反补贴税。

**Article 42** Countervailing duties shall be determined separately based on the amount of subsidy for each exporter. Where countervailing duties need to be imposed on subsidized imported products from an exporter who was not actually investigated, a prompt review shall be conducted and the countervailing duty applicable to such exporter shall be determined in a reasonable manner.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　反补贴税税额不得超过终裁决定确定的补贴金额。

**Article 43** The amount of countervailing duties shall not exceed the amount of the subsidy determined in the final determination.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　终裁决定确定存在实质损害，并在此前已经采取临时反补贴措施的，反补贴税可以对已经实施临时反补贴措施的期间追溯征收。  
终裁决定确定存在实质损害威胁，在先前不采取临时反补贴措施将会导致后来作出实质损害裁定的情况下已经采取临时反补贴措施的，反补贴税可以对已经实施临时反补贴措施的期间追溯征收。  
终裁决定确定的反补贴税，高于保证金或者保函所担保的金额的，差额部分不予收取；低于保证金或者保函所担保的金额的，差额部分应当予以退还。

**Article 44** Where a final determination establishes the existence of material injury and provisional countervailing measures have been taken prior thereto, countervailing duties may be imposed retroactively on the period for which provisional countervailing measures have been applied.  
Where a final determination establishes a threat of material injury, and provisional countervailing measures have been taken in circumstances where, had such provisional measures not been taken, a determination of material injury would subsequently have been made, countervailing duties may be imposed retroactively on the period for which provisional countervailing measures have been applied.  
If the countervailing duties determined in the final determination are higher than the amount secured by the cash deposit or bond, the difference shall not be collected; if they are lower than the amount secured by the cash deposit or bond, the difference shall be refunded.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　下列三种情形并存的，必要时可以对实施临时反补贴措施之日前90天内进口的产品追溯征收反补贴税：  
（一）补贴进口产品在较短的时间内大量增加；  
（二）此种增加对国内产业造成难以补救的损害；  
（三）此种产品得益于补贴。

**Article 45** Where the following three circumstances coexist, countervailing duties may, when necessary, be imposed retroactively on products imported within 90 days prior to the application of provisional countervailing measures:  
(1) there is a massive increase in subsidized imports over a relatively short period;  
(2) such increase causes injury to the domestic industry which is difficult to remedy;  
(3) such product benefits from the subsidy.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　终裁决定确定不征收反补贴税的，或者终裁决定未确定追溯征收反补贴税的，对实施临时反补贴措施期间已收取的保证金应当予以退还，保函应当予以解除。

**Article 46** Where a final determination decides not to impose countervailing duties, or does not provide for retroactive imposition of countervailing duties, any cash deposit already collected during the period of application of provisional countervailing measures shall be refunded, and any bond shall be released.

### 第五章　反补贴税和承诺的期限与复审

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　反补贴税的征收期限和承诺的履行期限不超过5年；但是，经复审确定终止征收反补贴税有可能导致补贴和损害的继续或者再度发生的，反补贴税的征收期限可以适当延长。

**Article 47** The period of imposition of countervailing duties and the period for fulfillment of undertakings shall not exceed five years; provided that, if a review establishes that termination of the imposition of countervailing duties would be likely to lead to continuation or recurrence of the subsidy and injury, the period of imposition may be extended appropriately.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　反补贴税生效后，商务部可以在有正当理由的情况下，决定对继续征收反补贴税的必要性进行复审；也可以在经过一段合理时间，应利害关系方的请求并对利害关系方提供的相应证据进行审查后，决定对继续征收反补贴税的必要性进行复审。  
承诺生效后，商务部可以在有正当理由的情况下，决定对继续履行承诺的必要性进行复审；也可以在经过一段合理时间，应利害关系方的请求并对利害关系方提供的相应证据进行审查后，决定对继续履行承诺的必要性进行复审。

**Article 48** After countervailing duties take effect, the Ministry of Commerce may, where justified, decide to conduct a review of the need for the continued imposition of the countervailing duties; it may also, after a reasonable period of time, upon request by an interested party and after examining the relevant evidence provided by the interested party, decide to conduct a review of the need for the continued imposition of the countervailing duties.  
After an undertaking takes effect, the Ministry of Commerce may, where justified, decide to conduct a review of the need for the continued fulfillment of the undertaking; it may also, after a reasonable period of time, upon request by an interested party and after examining the relevant evidence provided by the interested party, decide to conduct a review of the need for the continued fulfillment of the undertaking.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　根据复审结果，由商务部依照本条例的规定提出保留、修改或者取消反补贴税的建议，国务院关税税则委员会根据商务部的建议作出决定，由商务部予以公告；或者由商务部依照本条例的规定，作出保留、修改或者取消承诺的决定并予以公告。

**Article 49** Based on the results of the review, the Ministry of Commerce shall, in accordance with these Regulations, make a recommendation to maintain, modify, or abolish the countervailing duties, and the Customs Tariff Commission of the State Council shall make a decision based on the recommendation of the Ministry of Commerce, and the Ministry of Commerce shall announce the decision; or the Ministry of Commerce shall, in accordance with these Regulations, make a decision to maintain, modify, or abolish the undertaking and shall announce such decision.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　复审程序参照本条例关于反补贴调查的有关规定执行。  
复审期限自决定复审开始之日起，不超过12个月。

**Article 50** The procedures for review shall be implemented with reference to the relevant provisions of these Regulations on countervailing investigations.  
The period for review shall not exceed 12 months from the date of the decision to initiate the review.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　在复审期间，复审程序不妨碍反补贴措施的实施。

**Article 51** During the period of review, the review procedure shall not impede the implementation of countervailing measures.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　对依照[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)作出的终裁决定不服的，对依照本条例第四章作出的是否征收反补贴税的决定以及追溯征收的决定不服的，或者对依照本条例第五章作出的复审决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议，也可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 52** Any party that does not accept the final determination made in accordance with Article 26 of these Regulations, or the decision on whether to impose a countervailing duty or the decision on retroactive imposition made in accordance with Chapter IV of these Regulations, or the review decision made in accordance with Chapter V of these Regulations, may apply for administrative reconsideration according to law, or may initiate a prosecution in the People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　依照本条例作出的公告，应当载明重要的情况、事实、理由、依据、结果和结论等内容。

**Article 53** A public notice issued in accordance with these Regulations shall include important information, facts, reasons, basis, results, conclusions and other content.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　商务部可以采取适当措施，防止规避反补贴措施的行为。

**Article 54** The Ministry of Commerce may take appropriate measures to prevent acts of circumvention of countervailing measures.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　任何国家（地区）对中华人民共和国的出口产品采取歧视性反补贴措施的，中华人民共和国可以根据实际情况对该国家（地区）采取相应的措施。

**Article 55** If any country (region) adopts discriminatory countervailing measures against export products of the People's Republic of China, the People's Republic of China may, in light of the actual circumstances, take corresponding measures against that country (region).

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　商务部负责与反补贴有关的对外磋商、通知和争端解决事宜。

**Article 56** The Ministry of Commerce shall be responsible for external consultations, notifications and dispute settlement matters related to countervailing.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　商务部可以根据本条例制定有关具体实施办法。

**Article 57** The Ministry of Commerce may, in accordance with these Regulations, formulate specific implementation measures.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　本条例自2002年1月1日起施行。1997年3月25日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国反倾销和反补贴条例》中关于反补贴的规定同时废止。

**Article 58** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2002. The provisions on countervailing in the Anti-dumping and Countervailing Regulations of the People's Republic of China promulgated by the State Council on March 25, 1997 shall be abolished simultaneously.
