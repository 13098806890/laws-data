# 中华人民共和国海关总署关于外国驻中国使馆和使馆人员进出境物品的规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08

> (1986年10月31日国务院批准　1986年12月1日海关总署发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国外交特权与豁免条例》，制定本规定。 第二条　外国驻中国使馆(以下简称使馆)运进运出公务用品，使馆人员运进运出自用物品，除有双边协议按协议执行外，应当按照本规定办理。 前款中，“公务用品”系指使馆执行职务直接需用的物品，包括家具、陈设品、办公用品、招待用品和机动车辆等；“自用物品”系指使馆人员和与其共同生活的配偶及未成年子女在中国居留期间直接需用的生活用品，包括家具、家用电器和机动车辆等。 第三条　使馆运进运出公务用品，外交代表以托运或者邮寄方式运进运出自用物品，应当书面向海关申报。 外交代表进出境时有随身携带的或者附载于同一运输工具上的私人行李物品，应当口头向海关申报，海关予以免验放行。但具有重大理由推定其中装有非公务用品、非自用物品，或者装有中国法律和规章禁止进出口的物品时，海关有权查验。查验时，必须有外交代表或者其授权人员在场。 第四条　使馆申报运进的公务用品和外交代表申报运进的自用物品，经海关审核在直接需用数量范围内的，予以免税。 申报运出公务用品和自用物品，由海关审核后予以放行。 第五条　使馆和使馆人员不得携运中华人民共和国法律和规章禁止进出口的物品进出境。因特殊情况需要运进运出上述物品，必须事先得到中国政府有关部门的批准，并按中国政府有关规定办理。 运进无线电收发信机及其器材，必须事先以书面申请报经中国外交部批准。使馆和使馆人员应当向海关申报并提供有关批准文件，海关予以审核放行。 携运文物出境，必须事先向海关申报，经国家文化行政管理部门指定的省、自治区、直辖市文化行政管理部门鉴定，并发给许可出境凭证。使馆和使馆人员应当向海关提供有关凭证，海关予以审核放行。 携运枪支、子弹进出境，按照《中华人民共和国枪支管理法》的规定办理。 携运属于中国检疫法规规定管制的物品进出境，海关按有关法规办理。 第六条　使馆和使馆人员申报属于中国法律和规章禁止进出口的物品，除经中国政府有关部门批准的以外，海关予以扣留，有关使馆或者使馆人员应当在90天内退运。逾期未退运的，由海关变价上缴国库。 第七条　使馆和使馆人员免税运进的物品，不得转让。确有特殊原因需要转让的，必须报经海关批准。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国外交特权与豁免条例》，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated in accordance with the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Diplomatic Privileges and Immunities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　外国驻中国使馆(以下简称使馆)运进运出公务用品，使馆人员运进运出自用物品，除有双边协议按协议执行外，应当按照本规定办理。  
前款中，“公务用品”系指使馆执行职务直接需用的物品，包括家具、陈设品、办公用品、招待用品和机动车辆等；“自用物品”系指使馆人员和与其共同生活的配偶及未成年子女在中国居留期间直接需用的生活用品，包括家具、家用电器和机动车辆等。

**Article 2** The import and export of official supplies by foreign embassies in China (hereinafter referred to as “embassies”) and the import and export of personal effects by members of embassies shall be handled in accordance with these Provisions, unless a bilateral agreement provides otherwise, in which case the agreement shall prevail.  
In the preceding paragraph, “official supplies” means articles directly needed by an embassy in performing its functions, including furniture, furnishings, office supplies, reception supplies and motor vehicles, etc.; “personal effects” means articles for daily use directly needed by members of an embassy and their spouses and minor children living with them during their stay in China, including furniture, household electrical appliances and motor vehicles, etc.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　使馆运进运出公务用品，外交代表以托运或者邮寄方式运进运出自用物品，应当书面向海关申报。  
外交代表进出境时有随身携带的或者附载于同一运输工具上的私人行李物品，应当口头向海关申报，海关予以免验放行。但具有重大理由推定其中装有非公务用品、非自用物品，或者装有中国法律和规章禁止进出口的物品时，海关有权查验。查验时，必须有外交代表或者其授权人员在场。

**Article 3** An embassy that imports or exports official supplies, and a diplomatic agent who imports or exports personal effects by consignment or by post, shall make a written declaration to the Customs.  
When a diplomatic agent enters or leaves the country, his personal baggage carried with him or loaded on the same means of transport shall be declared orally to the Customs, and the Customs shall release it without examination. However, where there are substantial grounds for presuming that such baggage contains articles other than official supplies or personal effects, or articles the import or export of which is prohibited by Chinese laws and regulations, the Customs shall have the right to inspect it. Such inspection shall be conducted in the presence of the diplomatic agent or his authorized representative.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　使馆申报运进的公务用品和外交代表申报运进的自用物品，经海关审核在直接需用数量范围内的，予以免税。  
申报运出公务用品和自用物品，由海关审核后予以放行。

**Article 4** Official supplies declared for import by an embassy and personal effects declared for import by a diplomatic agent shall, upon Customs verification that they are within the quantity directly needed, be exempted from Customs duties.  
Official supplies and personal effects declared for export shall be released by the Customs after verification.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　使馆和使馆人员不得携运中华人民共和国法律和规章禁止进出口的物品进出境。因特殊情况需要运进运出上述物品，必须事先得到中国政府有关部门的批准，并按中国政府有关规定办理。  
运进无线电收发信机及其器材，必须事先以书面申请报经中国外交部批准。使馆和使馆人员应当向海关申报并提供有关批准文件，海关予以审核放行。  
携运文物出境，必须事先向海关申报，经国家文化行政管理部门指定的省、自治区、直辖市文化行政管理部门鉴定，并发给许可出境凭证。使馆和使馆人员应当向海关提供有关凭证，海关予以审核放行。  
携运枪支、子弹进出境，按照《中华人民共和国枪支管理法》的规定办理。  
携运属于中国检疫法规规定管制的物品进出境，海关按有关法规办理。

**Article 5** An embassy and its members shall not carry into or out of the country any articles the import or export of which is prohibited by the laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China. Where special circumstances require the import or export of such articles, prior approval must be obtained from the competent authorities of the Chinese Government, and the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Chinese Government.  
The import of radio transceivers and their equipment must be approved in advance by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of China through a written application. The embassy and its members shall declare to the Customs and provide the relevant approval documents, and the Customs shall release them after verification.  
The export of cultural relics must be declared to the Customs in advance, and shall be subject to expert evaluation by the cultural administrative department of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government designated by the State cultural administrative department, and a permit for export shall be issued. The embassy and its members shall provide the relevant permit to the Customs, and the Customs shall release them after verification.  
The import and export of firearms and ammunition shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Control of Firearms.  
The import and export of articles subject to quarantine control under Chinese quarantine regulations shall be handled by the Customs in accordance with the relevant regulations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　使馆和使馆人员申报属于中国法律和规章禁止进出口的物品，除经中国政府有关部门批准的以外，海关予以扣留，有关使馆或者使馆人员应当在90天内退运。逾期未退运的，由海关变价上缴国库。

**Article 6** Where an embassy or a member thereof declares articles the import or export of which is prohibited by Chinese laws and regulations, the Customs shall detain them unless the articles have been approved by the competent authorities of the Chinese Government. The embassy or the member concerned shall re-export or re-ship the articles within 90 days. If they fail to do so within the time limit, the Customs shall sell them and turn the proceeds over to the State Treasury.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　使馆和使馆人员免税运进的物品，不得转让。确有特殊原因需要转让的，必须报经海关批准。  
经批准转让的物品，应当由受让人或者出让人按规定向海关办理纳税或者免税手续。

**Article 7** Articles imported duty-free by an embassy or its members shall not be transferred. Where transfer is necessary for special reasons, approval must be obtained from the Customs.  
For articles approved for transfer, the transferee or the transferor shall go through the formalities of paying Customs duties or obtaining duty exemption with the Customs in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　使馆发送或者收受的外交邮袋，海关予以免验放行。外交邮袋应予加封，附有可资识别的外部标记，并以装载外交文件或者公务用品为限。  
外交邮袋由外交信使转递时，必须持有派遣国主管机关出具的信使证明书。商业飞机机长可以受委托为使馆转递外交邮袋，但机长必须持有委托国的官方证明文件(注明邮袋件数)。由商业飞机机长转递或者托运的外交邮袋，应当由使馆派使馆人员办理接交、提取或者发运手续。

**Article 8** Diplomatic bags sent or received by an embassy shall be released by the Customs without examination. Diplomatic bags shall be sealed and bear visible external marks and may contain only diplomatic documents or official supplies.  
When diplomatic bags are transmitted by a diplomatic courier, the courier must be in possession of a courier certificate issued by the competent authorities of the sending state. A commercial aircraft pilot may be entrusted to transmit diplomatic bags on behalf of an embassy, but the pilot must hold an official document from the entrusting state (indicating the number of bags). Diplomatic bags transmitted or consigned by a commercial aircraft pilot shall be received, collected or dispatched by embassy personnel designated by the embassy.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　使馆的行政技术人员和服务人员，如非中国公民或者在中国的永久居留者，携运进境自用物品，包括到任后半年内运进安家物品，应当书面向海关申报。上述物品经海关审核在直接需用数量范围内(其中汽车每户限1辆)的，海关予以查验免税放行。申报携运出境的自用物品，海关予以审核查验放行。  
前款所述人员寄进或者寄出的个人自用物品，海关按照个人邮递物品的有关规定办理。  
本条第一款所述人员任职期间托运进境的自用物品，海关比照本条第二款办理。

**Article 9** Administrative and technical staff and service staff of an embassy who are not Chinese citizens or permanent residents of China shall declare in writing to the Customs when carrying into the country personal effects, including household effects brought in within six months after their arrival. If the Customs verifies that the articles are within the quantity directly needed (with a limit of one motor vehicle per household), the Customs shall examine and release them duty-free. Personal effects declared for export shall be examined and released by the Customs after verification.  
Personal effects sent or received by the persons mentioned in the preceding paragraph by post shall be handled by the Customs in accordance with the relevant provisions on personal postal articles.  
Personal effects imported by consignment by the persons mentioned in paragraph 1 of this Article during their term of office shall be handled by the Customs mutatis mutandis in accordance with paragraph 2 of this Article.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　联合国及其专门机构和其他国际组织驻中国代表机构运进运出公务用品和邮袋，代表机构的代表、行政技术人员、服务人员和与其共同生活的配偶及未成年子女运进运出自用物品，海关根据中国已加入的有关国际公约和中国与有关国际组织签订的协议办理；遇有公约和协议未涉及的情况，参照本规定有关条款办理。

**Article 10** For the import and export of official supplies and bags by the representative offices of the United Nations, its specialized agencies and other international organizations in China, and the import and export of personal effects by their representatives, administrative and technical staff, service staff, and their spouses and minor children living with them, the Customs shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant international conventions to which China has acceded and the agreements concluded between China and the relevant international organizations. Where a matter is not covered by such conventions or agreements, it shall be handled by reference to the relevant provisions of these Provisions.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　外国驻中国领事馆运进运出公务用品和领事邮袋，领事官员、领馆工作人员和与其共同生活的配偶及未成年子女运进运出自用物品，海关根据中国已加入的有关国际公约和中国与有关国家签订的协议办理；遇有公约和协议未涉及的情况，根据互惠的原则，参照本规定有关条款办理。

**Article 11** For the import and export of official supplies and consular bags by foreign consulates in China, and the import and export of articles for personal use by consular officers, consular staff, and their spouses and minor children living with them, the Customs shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant international conventions to which China has acceded and the agreements signed between China and the countries concerned; in cases not covered by such conventions or agreements, the matter shall be handled on the basis of reciprocity, with reference to the relevant provisions of these Provisions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 12** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
