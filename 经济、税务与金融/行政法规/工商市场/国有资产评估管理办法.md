# 国有资产评估管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2020-11-29  
**生效日期**：2020-11-29

> (1991年11月16日中华人民共和国国务院令第91号发布　根据2020年11月29日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了正确体现国有资产的价值量，保护国有资产所有者和经营者、使用者的合法权益，制定本办法。 第二条　国有资产评估，除法律、法规另有规定外，适用本办法。 第三条　国有资产占有单位(以下简称占有单位)有下列情形之一的，应当进行资产评估： (一)资产拍卖、转让； (二)企业兼并、出售、联营、股份经营； (三)与外国公司、企业和其他经济组织或者个人开办外商投资企业； (四)企业清算； (五)依照国家有关规定需要进行资产评估的其他情形。 第四条　占有单位有下列情形之一，当事人认为需要的，可以进行资产评估： (一)资产抵押及其他担保； (二)企业租赁； (三)需要进行资产评估的其他情形。 第五条　全国或者特定行业的国有资产评估，由国务院决定。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了正确体现国有资产的价值量，保护国有资产所有者和经营者、使用者的合法权益，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the correct reflection of the value of state-owned assets and for the protection of the legitimate rights and interests of the owners, operators, and users of state-owned assets.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国有资产评估，除法律、法规另有规定外，适用本办法。

**Article 2** These Measures shall apply to the appraisal of state-owned assets, unless otherwise provided by laws or regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国有资产占有单位(以下简称占有单位)有下列情形之一的，应当进行资产评估：  
(一)资产拍卖、转让；  
(二)企业兼并、出售、联营、股份经营；  
(三)与外国公司、企业和其他经济组织或者个人开办外商投资企业；  
(四)企业清算；  
(五)依照国家有关规定需要进行资产评估的其他情形。

**Article 3** A unit possessing state-owned assets (hereinafter referred to as the possessing unit) shall conduct asset appraisal under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Auction or transfer of assets;  
(2) Merger, sale, joint operation, or shareholding operation of an enterprise;  
(3) Establishment of a foreign-invested enterprise with a foreign company, enterprise, other economic organization or individual;  
(4) Liquidation of an enterprise;  
(5) Other circumstances requiring asset appraisal in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　占有单位有下列情形之一，当事人认为需要的，可以进行资产评估：  
(一)资产抵押及其他担保；  
(二)企业租赁；  
(三)需要进行资产评估的其他情形。

**Article 4** A possessing unit may conduct asset appraisal if the parties deem it necessary under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Mortgage of assets and other guarantees;  
(2) Leasing of an enterprise;  
(3) Other circumstances requiring asset appraisal.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　全国或者特定行业的国有资产评估，由国务院决定。

**Article 5** The appraisal of state-owned assets throughout the country or in a specific industry shall be decided by the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国有资产评估范围包括：固定资产、流动资产、无形资产和其他资产。

**Article 6** The scope of appraisal of state-owned assets includes: fixed assets, current assets, intangible assets, and other assets.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国有资产评估应当遵循真实性、科学性、可行性原则，依照国家规定的标准、程序和方法进行评定和估算。

**Article 7** The appraisal of state-owned assets shall follow the principles of truthfulness, scientificity, and feasibility, and shall be assessed and estimated in accordance with the standards, procedures, and methods prescribed by the State.

### 第二章　组织管理

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国有资产评估工作，按照国有资产管理权限，由国有资产管理行政主管部门负责管理和监督。  
国有资产评估组织工作，按照占有单位的隶属关系，由行业主管部门负责。  
国有资产管理行政主管部门和行业主管部门不直接从事国有资产评估业务。

**Article 8** The work of appraising state-owned assets shall be managed and supervised by the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management according to the authority of state-owned assets management.  
The organization of state-owned assets appraisal shall be handled by the competent industry department in accordance with the subordination relationship of the possessing unit.  
The administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management and the competent industry department shall not directly engage in the business of appraising state-owned assets.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　持有国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府国有资产管理行政主管部门颁发的国有资产评估资格证书的资产评估公司、会计师事务所、审计事务所、财务咨询公司，经国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府国有资产管理行政主管部门认可的临时评估机构(以下统称资产评估机构)，可以接受占有单位的委托，从事国有资产评估业务。  
前款所列资产评估机构的管理办法，由国务院国有资产管理行政主管部门制定。

**Article 9** Asset appraisal companies, accounting firms, auditing firms, and financial consulting companies that hold a certificate of qualification for appraising state-owned assets issued by the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management of the State Council or of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, as well as temporary appraisal institutions recognized by the said administrative departments (hereinafter collectively referred to as asset appraisal institutions), may accept the entrustment of possessing units to engage in the business of appraising state-owned assets.  
The measures for the administration of the asset appraisal institutions listed in the preceding paragraph shall be formulated by the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management under the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　占有单位委托资产评估机构进行资产评估时，应当如实提供有关情况和资料。资产评估机构应当对占有单位提供的有关情况和资料保守秘密。

**Article 10** When a possessing unit entrusts an asset appraisal institution to conduct asset appraisal, it shall truthfully provide the relevant information and materials. The asset appraisal institution shall keep confidential the relevant information and materials provided by the possessing unit.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　资产评估机构进行资产评估，实行有偿服务。资产评估收费办法，由国务院国有资产管理行政主管部门会同财政部门、物价主管部门制定。

**Article 11** Asset appraisal institutions shall provide paid services when conducting asset appraisal. The measures for charging fees for asset appraisal shall be formulated by the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management under the State Council together with the financial department and the competent department for pricing.

### 第三章　评估程序

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国有资产评估按照下列程序进行：  
(一)申请立项；  
(二)资产清查；  
(三)评定估算；  
(四)验证确认。

**Article 12** The appraisal of state-owned assets shall be carried out in accordance with the following procedures:  
(1) Application for project initiation;  
(2) Asset verification;  
(3) Assessment and estimation;  
(4) Verification and confirmation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　依照[本办法第三条](#art-3)、第四条规定进行资产评估的占有单位，经其主管部门审查同意后，应当向同级国有资产管理行政主管部门提交资产评估立项申请书，并附财产目录和有关会计报表等资料。  
经国有资产管理行政主管部门授权或者委托，占有单位的主管部门可以审批资产评估立项申请。

**Article 13** A possessing unit that conducts asset appraisal in accordance with Articles 3 and 4 of these Measures shall, after examination and approval by its competent department, submit an application for project initiation of asset appraisal to the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management at the same level, together with a property list and relevant accounting statements and other materials.  
Upon authorization or entrustment by the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management, the competent department of the possessing unit may examine and approve the application for project initiation of asset appraisal.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国有资产管理行政主管部门应当自收到资产评估立项申请书之日起十日内进行审核，并作出是否准予资产评估立项的决定，通知申请单位及其主管部门。

**Article 14** The administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management shall, within ten days from the date of receiving the application for project initiation of asset appraisal, conduct examination and make a decision on whether to approve the project initiation of asset appraisal, and shall notify the applying unit and its competent department.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院决定对全国或者特定行业进行国有资产评估的，视为已经准予资产评估立项。

**Article 15** Where the State Council decides to conduct an appraisal of state-owned assets throughout the country or in a specific industry, it shall be deemed that the project initiation of asset appraisal has been approved.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　申请单位收到准予资产评估立项通知书后，可以委托资产评估机构评估资产。

**Article 16** After receiving the notice of approval for project initiation of asset appraisal, the applying unit may entrust an asset appraisal institution to appraise the assets.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　受占有单位委托的资产评估机构应当在对委托单位的资产、债权、债务进行全面清查的基础上，核实资产账面与实际是否相符，经营成果是否真实，据以作出鉴定。

**Article 17** The asset appraisal institution entrusted by the possessing unit shall, on the basis of a comprehensive check of the assets, creditor's rights, and debts of the entrusting unit, verify whether the book value of the assets is consistent with the actual value and whether the business results are true, and shall make an appraisal accordingly.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　受占有单位委托的资产评估机构应当根据本办法的规定，对委托单位被评估资产的价值进行评定和估算，并向委托单位提出资产评估结果报告书。  
委托单位收到资产评估机构的资产评估结果报告书后，应当报其主管部门审查；主管部门审查同意后，报同级国有资产管理行政主管部门确认资产评估结果。  
经国有资产管理行政主管部门授权或者委托，占有单位的主管部门可以确认资产评估结果。

**Article 18** The asset appraisal institution entrusted by the possessing unit shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Measures, assess and estimate the value of the assets of the entrusting unit to be appraised, and shall submit a report on the results of asset appraisal to the entrusting unit.  
After receiving the report on the results of asset appraisal from the asset appraisal institution, the entrusting unit shall submit it to its competent department for examination; after examination and approval, the competent department shall submit it to the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management at the same level for confirmation of the asset appraisal results.  
Upon authorization or entrustment by the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management, the competent department of the possessing unit may confirm the asset appraisal results.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国有资产管理行政主管部门应当自收到占有单位报送的资产评估结果报告书之日起四十五日内组织审核、验证、协商，确认资产评估结果，并下达确认通知书。

**Article 19** The administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management shall, within 45 days from the date of receiving the report on the results of asset appraisal submitted by the possessing unit, organize examination, verification, and consultation, confirm the asset appraisal results, and issue a notice of confirmation.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　占有单位对确认通知书有异议的，可以自收到通知书之日起十五日内向上一级国有资产管理行政主管部门申请复核。上一级国有资产管理行政主管部门应当自收到复核申请之日起三十日内作出裁定，并下达裁定通知书。

**Article 20** If a possessing unit disagrees with the notice of confirmation, it may, within 15 days from the date of receiving the notice, apply to the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management at the next higher level for review. The administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management at the next higher level shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the application for review, make a ruling and issue a notice of ruling.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　占有单位收到确认通知书或者裁定通知书后，应当根据国家有关财务、会计制度进行账务处理。

**Article 21** After receiving the notice of confirmation or the notice of ruling, the possessing unit shall make accounting treatment in accordance with the relevant financial and accounting systems of the State.

### 第四章　评估方法

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国有资产重估价值，根据资产原值、净值、新旧程度、重置成本、获利能力等因素和本办法规定的资产评估方法评定。

**Article 22** The revalued amount of state-owned assets shall be assessed on the basis of such factors as original value, net value, degree of wear and tear, replacement cost, profitability, and the asset appraisal methods prescribed in these Measures.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国有资产评估方法包括：  
(一)收益现值法；  
(二)重置成本法；  
(三)现行市价法；  
(四)清算价格法；  
(五)国务院国有资产管理行政主管部门规定的其他评估方法。

**Article 23** The methods for appraising state-owned assets include:  
(1) The income capitalization method;  
(2) The replacement cost method;  
(3) The current market price method;  
(4) The liquidation price method;  
(5) Other appraisal methods prescribed by the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management under the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　用收益现值法进行资产评估的，应当根据被评估资产合理的预期获利能力和适当的折现率，计算出资产的现值，并以此评定重估价值。

**Article 24** Where the income capitalization method is used for asset appraisal, the present value of the asset shall be calculated on the basis of the reasonable expected profitability of the asset being appraised and an appropriate discount rate, and the revalued amount shall be assessed accordingly.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　用重置成本法进行资产评估的，应当根据该项资产在全新情况下的重置成本，减去按重置成本计算的已使用年限的累积折旧额，考虑资产功能变化、成新率等因素，评定重估价值；或者根据资产的使用期限，考虑资产功能变化等因素重新确定成新率，评定重估价值。

**Article 25** Where the replacement cost method is used for asset appraisal, the revalued amount shall be assessed on the basis of the replacement cost of the asset in its new condition, minus the accumulated depreciation for the years of use calculated at the replacement cost, and taking into account such factors as changes in the function of the asset and the condition ratio; or, the revalued amount shall be assessed by re-determining the condition ratio on the basis of the service life of the asset and taking into account such factors as changes in the function of the asset.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　用现行市价法进行资产评估的，应当参照相同或者类似资产的市场价格，评定重估价值。

**Article 26** Where the current market price method is used for asset appraisal, the revalued amount shall be assessed by referring to the market prices of the same or similar assets.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　用清算价格法进行资产评估的，应当根据企业清算时其资产可变现的价值，评定重估价值。

**Article 27** Where the liquidation price method is used for asset appraisal, the revalued amount shall be assessed on the basis of the realizable value of the assets of the enterprise at the time of liquidation.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对流动资产中的原材料、在制品、协作件、库存商品、低值易耗品等进行评估时，应当根据该项资产的现行市场价格、计划价格，考虑购置费用、产品完工程度、损耗等因素，评定重估价值。

**Article 28** Where the appraisal is conducted on raw materials, work in process, cooperative parts, inventory goods, low-value consumables, etc. in current assets, the revalued amount shall be assessed on the basis of the current market price or planned price of the asset, taking into account such factors as acquisition costs, degree of completion of the product, and wear and tear.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　对有价证券的评估，参照市场价格评定重估价值；没有市场价格的，考虑票面价值、预期收益等因素，评定重估价值。

**Article 29** For the appraisal of marketable securities, the revalued amount shall be assessed by referring to the market price; if there is no market price, the revalued amount shall be assessed by taking into account such factors as the face value and expected income.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对占有单位的无形资产，区别下列情况评定重估价值：  
(一)外购的无形资产，根据购入成本及该项资产具有的获利能力；  
(二)自创或者自身拥有的无形资产，根据其形成时所需实际成本及该项资产具有的获利能力；  
(三)自创或者自身拥有的未单独计算成本的无形资产，根据该项资产具有的获利能力。

**Article 30** For intangible assets of a possessing unit, the revalued amount shall be assessed by distinguishing among the following circumstances:  
(1) For purchased intangible assets, on the basis of the acquisition cost and the profitability of such assets;  
(2) For self-developed or self-owned intangible assets, on the basis of the actual cost required at the time of their formation and the profitability of such assets;  
(3) For self-developed or self-owned intangible assets for which the cost is not separately calculated, on the basis of the profitability of such assets.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　占有单位违反本办法的规定，提供虚假情况和资料，或者与资产评估机构串通作弊，致使资产评估结果失实的，国有资产管理行政主管部门可以宣布资产评估结果无效，并可以根据情节轻重，单处或者并处下列处罚：  
(一)通报批评；  
(二)限期改正，并可以处以相当于评估费用以下的罚款；  
(三)提请有关部门对单位主管人员和直接责任人员给予行政处分，并可以处以相当于本人三个月基本工资以下的罚款。

**Article 31** Where a possessing unit violates the provisions of these Measures by providing false information and materials, or colluding with an asset appraisal institution to commit fraud, thereby causing the asset appraisal results to be untrue, the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management may declare the asset appraisal results invalid, and may, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, impose one or more of the following penalties:  
(1) Circular criticism;  
(2) Ordering correction within a time limit, and may also impose a fine not exceeding the equivalent of the appraisal fee;  
(3) Requesting the relevant department to impose administrative sanctions on the person in charge of the unit and the persons directly responsible, and may also impose a fine not exceeding the equivalent of three months' basic salary of the said persons.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　资产评估机构作弊或者玩忽职守，致使资产评估结果失实的，国有资产管理行政主管部门可以宣布资产评估结果无效，并可以根据情节轻重，对该资产评估机构给予下列处罚：  
(一)警告；  
(二)停业整顿；  
(三)吊销国有资产评估资格证书。

**Article 32** Where an asset appraisal institution commits fraud or neglects its duty, thereby causing the asset appraisal results to be untrue, the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management may declare the asset appraisal results invalid, and may, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, impose the following penalties on the asset appraisal institution:  
(1) Warning;  
(2) Suspension of business for rectification;  
(3) Revocation of the certificate of qualification for appraising state-owned assets.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　被处罚的单位和个人对依照[本办法第三十一条](#art-31)、第三十二条规定作出的处罚决定不服的，可以在收到处罚通知之日起十五日内，向上一级国有资产管理行政主管部门申请复议。上一级国有资产管理行政主管部门应当自收到复议申请之日起六十日内作出复议决定。申请人对复议决定不服的，可以自收到复议通知之日起十五日内，向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 33** If the unit or individual subjected to a penalty disagrees with the decision on penalty made in accordance with Articles 31 and 32 of these Measures, it/he may, within 15 days from the date of receiving the notice of penalty, apply to the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management at the next higher level for reconsideration. The administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management at the next higher level shall, within 60 days from the date of receiving the application for reconsideration, make a reconsideration decision. If the applicant disagrees with the reconsideration decision, it/he may, within 15 days from the date of receiving the notice of reconsideration, institute legal proceedings in a People's Court.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国有资产管理行政主管部门或者行业主管部门工作人员违反本办法，利用职权谋取私利，或者玩忽职守，造成国有资产损失的，国有资产管理行政主管部门或者行业主管部门可以按照干部管理权限，给予行政处分，并可以处以相当于本人三个月基本工资以下的罚款。  
违反本办法，利用职权谋取私利的，由有查处权的部门依法追缴其非法所得。

**Article 34** Where a staff member of the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management or the competent industry department violates these Measures by taking advantage of his position to seek personal gains, or neglects his duty, thereby causing losses to state-owned assets, the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management or the competent industry department may, in accordance with the authority for personnel management, impose administrative sanctions, and may also impose a fine not exceeding the equivalent of three months' basic salary of the said staff member.  
Where he violates these Measures by taking advantage of his position to seek personal gains, the department with the power of investigation shall, according to law, recover the illegal gains.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反本办法，情节严重，构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 35** Where a violation of these Measures is serious enough to constitute a crime, the judicial authorities shall, according to law, investigate the criminal liability.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　境外国有资产的评估，不适用本办法。

**Article 36** These Measures shall not apply to the appraisal of state-owned assets outside China.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　有关国有自然资源有偿使用、开采的评估办法，由国务院另行规定。

**Article 37** The appraisal measures concerning the paid use and exploitation of state-owned natural resources shall be separately prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本办法由国务院国有资产管理行政主管部门负责解释。本办法的施行细则由国务院国有资产管理行政主管部门制定。

**Article 38** These Measures shall be interpreted by the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management under the State Council. The detailed rules for the implementation of these Measures shall be formulated by the administrative department in charge of state-owned assets management under the State Council.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本办法自发布之日起施行。

**Article 39** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
