# 企业国有资产监督管理暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2019-03-02

> (2003年5月27日中华人民共和国国务院令第378号公布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为建立适应社会主义市场经济需要的国有资产监督管理体制，进一步搞好国有企业，推动国有经济布局和结构的战略性调整，发展和壮大国有经济，实现国有资产保值增值，制定本条例。 第二条　国有及国有控股企业、国有参股企业中的国有资产的监督管理，适用本条例。 金融机构中的国有资产的监督管理，不适用本条例。 第三条　本条例所称企业国有资产，是指国家对企业各种形式的投资和投资所形成的权益，以及依法认定为国家所有的其他权益。 第四条　企业国有资产属于国家所有。国家实行由国务院和地方人民政府分别代表国家履行出资人职责，享有所有者权益，权利、义务和责任相统一，管资产和管人、管事相结合的国有资产管理体制。 第五条　国务院代表国家对关系国民经济命脉和国家安全的大型国有及国有控股、国有参股企业，重要基础设施和重要自然资源等领域的国有及国有控股、国有参股企业，履行出资人职责。国务院履行出资人职责的企业，由国务院确定、公布。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和设区的市、自治州级人民政府分别代表国家对由国务院履行出资人职责以外的国有及国有控股、国有参股企业，履行出资人职责。其中，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府履行出资人职责的国有及国有控股、国有参股企业，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定、公布，并报国务院国有资产监督管理机构备案；其他由设区的市、自治州级人民政府履行出资人职责的国有及国有控股、国有参股企业，由设区的市、自治州级人民政府确定、公布，并报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府国有资产监督管理机构备案。 国务院，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，设区的市、自治州级人民政府履行出资人职责的企业，以下统称所出资企业。 第六条　国务院，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，设区的市、自治州级人民政府，分别设立国有资产监督管理机构。国有资产监督管理机构根据授权，依法履行出资人职责，依法对企业国有资产进行监督管理。 企业国有资产较少的设区的市、自治州，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，可以不单独设立国有资产监督管理机构。 第七条　各级人民政府应当严格执行国有资产管理法律、法规，坚持政府的社会公共管理职能与国有资产出资人职能分开，坚持政企分开，实行所有权与经营权分离。 国有资产监督管理机构不行使政府的社会公共管理职能，政府其他机构、部门不履行企业国有资产出资人职责。 第八条　国有资产监督管理机构应当依照本条例和其他有关法律、行政法规的规定，建立健全内部监督制度，严格执行法律、行政法规。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为建立适应社会主义市场经济需要的国有资产监督管理体制，进一步搞好国有企业，推动国有经济布局和结构的战略性调整，发展和壮大国有经济，实现国有资产保值增值，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of establishing a state-owned assets supervision and administration system that meets the needs of the socialist market economy, further improving state-owned enterprises, promoting strategic adjustment of the layout and structure of the state-owned economy, developing and strengthening the state-owned economy, and preserving and increasing the value of state-owned assets.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国有及国有控股企业、国有参股企业中的国有资产的监督管理，适用本条例。  
金融机构中的国有资产的监督管理，不适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the supervision and administration of state-owned assets in wholly state-owned enterprises, state-controlled enterprises, and state-invested enterprises.  
The supervision and administration of state-owned assets in financial institutions shall not be governed by these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称企业国有资产，是指国家对企业各种形式的投资和投资所形成的权益，以及依法认定为国家所有的其他权益。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Regulations, state-owned assets of enterprises refer to the various forms of investment made by the State in enterprises and the rights and interests arising from such investments, as well as other rights and interests recognized as owned by the State in accordance with law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　企业国有资产属于国家所有。国家实行由国务院和地方人民政府分别代表国家履行出资人职责，享有所有者权益，权利、义务和责任相统一，管资产和管人、管事相结合的国有资产管理体制。

**Article 4** State-owned assets of enterprises shall be owned by the State. The State shall implement a state-owned assets supervision and administration system under which the State Council and local people's governments respectively act as contributors on behalf of the State, enjoy owner's rights and interests, unify rights, obligations and responsibilities, and combine the management of assets with the management of personnel and affairs.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院代表国家对关系国民经济命脉和国家安全的大型国有及国有控股、国有参股企业，重要基础设施和重要自然资源等领域的国有及国有控股、国有参股企业，履行出资人职责。国务院履行出资人职责的企业，由国务院确定、公布。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和设区的市、自治州级人民政府分别代表国家对由国务院履行出资人职责以外的国有及国有控股、国有参股企业，履行出资人职责。其中，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府履行出资人职责的国有及国有控股、国有参股企业，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定、公布，并报国务院国有资产监督管理机构备案；其他由设区的市、自治州级人民政府履行出资人职责的国有及国有控股、国有参股企业，由设区的市、自治州级人民政府确定、公布，并报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府国有资产监督管理机构备案。  
国务院，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，设区的市、自治州级人民政府履行出资人职责的企业，以下统称所出资企业。

**Article 5** The State Council shall, on behalf of the State, perform the responsibilities of a contributor in respect of large wholly state-owned enterprises, state-controlled enterprises, and state-invested enterprises that relate to the lifeline of the national economy and national security, and in respect of wholly state-owned enterprises, state-controlled enterprises, and state-invested enterprises in the fields of important infrastructure and important natural resources. The enterprises for which the State Council performs the responsibilities of a contributor shall be determined and announced by the State Council.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the people's governments of cities with districts and autonomous prefectures shall, on behalf of the State, perform the responsibilities of a contributor in respect of wholly state-owned enterprises, state-controlled enterprises, and state-invested enterprises other than those for which the State Council performs the responsibilities of a contributor. Among them, the wholly state-owned enterprises, state-controlled enterprises, and state-invested enterprises for which the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government perform the responsibilities of a contributor shall be determined and announced by the said people's governments and reported to the state-owned assets supervision and administration institution of the State Council for the record; and the other wholly state-owned enterprises, state-controlled enterprises, and state-invested enterprises for which the people's governments of cities with districts and autonomous prefectures perform the responsibilities of a contributor shall be determined and announced by the said people's governments and reported to the state-owned assets supervision and administration institution of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government for the record.  
The enterprises for which the State Council, the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the people's governments of cities with districts and autonomous prefectures perform the responsibilities of a contributor shall be collectively referred to as invested enterprises hereinafter.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，设区的市、自治州级人民政府，分别设立国有资产监督管理机构。国有资产监督管理机构根据授权，依法履行出资人职责，依法对企业国有资产进行监督管理。  
企业国有资产较少的设区的市、自治州，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，可以不单独设立国有资产监督管理机构。

**Article 6** The State Council, the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the people's governments of cities with districts and autonomous prefectures shall respectively establish state-owned assets supervision and administration institutions. The state-owned assets supervision and administration institutions shall, according to authorization, perform the responsibilities of a contributor in accordance with law and supervise and administer state-owned assets of enterprises in accordance with law.  
In cities with districts and autonomous prefectures where state-owned assets of enterprises are relatively small, with the approval of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, a separate state-owned assets supervision and administration institution may not be established.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级人民政府应当严格执行国有资产管理法律、法规，坚持政府的社会公共管理职能与国有资产出资人职能分开，坚持政企分开，实行所有权与经营权分离。  
国有资产监督管理机构不行使政府的社会公共管理职能，政府其他机构、部门不履行企业国有资产出资人职责。

**Article 7** People's governments at all levels shall strictly implement the laws and regulations on state-owned assets administration, adhere to the separation of the government's social public administration functions from the functions as a contributor of state-owned assets, adhere to the separation of government functions from enterprise functions, and implement the separation of ownership from the right of operation.  
The state-owned assets supervision and administration institutions shall not exercise the government's social public administration functions, and other government institutions and departments shall not perform the responsibilities of a contributor of state-owned assets of enterprises.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国有资产监督管理机构应当依照本条例和其他有关法律、行政法规的规定，建立健全内部监督制度，严格执行法律、行政法规。

**Article 8** The state-owned assets supervision and administration institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and other relevant laws and administrative regulations, establish and improve the internal supervision system, and strictly implement the laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　发生战争、严重自然灾害或者其他重大、紧急情况时，国家可以依法统一调用、处置企业国有资产。

**Article 9** In the event of war, serious natural disasters, or other major emergencies, the State may, in accordance with law, uniformly deploy and dispose of state-owned assets of enterprises.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　所出资企业及其投资设立的企业，享有有关法律、行政法规规定的企业经营自主权。  
国有资产监督管理机构应当支持企业依法自主经营，除履行出资人职责以外，不得干预企业的生产经营活动。

**Article 10** The invested enterprises and the enterprises invested and established by them shall enjoy the autonomy of enterprise operation as prescribed by relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
The state-owned assets supervision and administration institutions shall support the enterprises in operating independently in accordance with law, and shall not intervene in the production and operation activities of the enterprises except when performing the responsibilities of a contributor.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　所出资企业应当努力提高经济效益，对其经营管理的企业国有资产承担保值增值责任。  
所出资企业应当接受国有资产监督管理机构依法实施的监督管理，不得损害企业国有资产所有者和其他出资人的合法权益。

**Article 11** The invested enterprises shall make efforts to improve economic returns and shall assume the responsibility for preserving and increasing the value of the State-owned assets of enterprises under their management.  
The invested enterprises shall accept the supervision and administration conducted by the State-owned assets supervision and administration institution according to law, and shall not impair the legitimate rights and interests of the owners of State-owned assets of enterprises and other investors.

### 第二章　国有资产监督管理机构

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院国有资产监督管理机构是代表国务院履行出资人职责、负责监督管理企业国有资产的直属特设机构。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府国有资产监督管理机构，设区的市、自治州级人民政府国有资产监督管理机构是代表本级政府履行出资人职责、负责监督管理企业国有资产的直属特设机构。  
上级政府国有资产监督管理机构依法对下级政府的国有资产监督管理工作进行指导和监督。

**Article 12** The State-owned assets supervision and administration institution of the State Council is a direct special institution under the State Council, which performs the functions of a contributor on behalf of the State Council and is responsible for supervising and administering the State-owned assets of enterprises.  
The State-owned assets supervision and administration institution of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and the State-owned assets supervision and administration institution of the people's government of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture, are direct special institutions under the respective governments, which perform the functions of a contributor on behalf of the governments at the corresponding levels and are responsible for supervising and administering the State-owned assets of enterprises.  
The State-owned assets supervision and administration institution of a higher-level government shall, according to law, provide guidance and supervision over the State-owned assets supervision and administration work of the lower-level government.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国有资产监督管理机构的主要职责是：  
(一)依照《中华人民共和国公司法》等法律、法规，对所出资企业履行出资人职责，维护所有者权益；  
(二)指导推进国有及国有控股企业的改革和重组；  
(三)依照规定向所出资企业委派监事；  
(四)依照法定程序对所出资企业的企业负责人进行任免、考核，并根据考核结果对其进行奖惩；  
(五)通过统计、稽核等方式对企业国有资产的保值增值情况进行监管；  
(六)履行出资人的其他职责和承办本级政府交办的其他事项。  
国务院国有资产监督管理机构除前款规定职责外，可以制定企业国有资产监督管理的规章、制度。

**Article 13** The main functions of the State-owned assets supervision and administration institution are:  
(1) to perform the functions of a contributor with respect to the invested enterprises, in accordance with the Company Law of the People's Republic of China and other laws and regulations, and to safeguard the rights and interests of the owners;  
(2) to guide and promote the reform and reorganization of State-owned and State-controlled enterprises;  
(3) to appoint supervisors to the invested enterprises in accordance with the provisions;  
(4) to appoint and remove the responsible persons of the invested enterprises and assess their performance in accordance with statutory procedures, and to give them rewards or impose punishments based on the assessment results;  
(5) to supervise the preservation and increase of the value of State-owned assets of enterprises through methods such as statistics and auditing;  
(6) to perform other functions of a contributor and to handle other matters assigned by the government at the corresponding level.  
In addition to the functions specified in the preceding paragraph, the State-owned assets supervision and administration institution of the State Council may formulate rules and systems for the supervision and administration of State-owned assets of enterprises.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国有资产监督管理机构的主要义务是：  
(一)推进国有资产合理流动和优化配置，推动国有经济布局和结构的调整；  
(二)保持和提高关系国民经济命脉和国家安全领域国有经济的控制力和竞争力，提高国有经济的整体素质；  
(三)探索有效的企业国有资产经营体制和方式，加强企业国有资产监督管理工作，促进企业国有资产保值增值，防止企业国有资产流失；  
(四)指导和促进国有及国有控股企业建立现代企业制度，完善法人治理结构，推进管理现代化；  
(五)尊重、维护国有及国有控股企业经营自主权，依法维护企业合法权益，促进企业依法经营管理，增强企业竞争力；  
(六)指导和协调解决国有及国有控股企业改革与发展中的困难和问题。

**Article 14** The main obligations of the State-owned assets supervision and administration institution are:  
(1) to promote the rational flow and optimal allocation of State-owned assets, and to facilitate the adjustment of the layout and structure of the State-owned economy;  
(2) to maintain and enhance the controlling power and competitiveness of the State-owned economy in sectors vital to the lifeblood of the national economy and national security, and to improve the overall quality of the State-owned economy;  
(3) to explore effective operational systems and methods for State-owned assets of enterprises, to strengthen the supervision and administration of State-owned assets of enterprises, to promote the preservation and increase of the value of State-owned assets of enterprises, and to prevent the loss of State-owned assets of enterprises;  
(4) to guide and promote State-owned and State-controlled enterprises in establishing a modern enterprise system, improving the corporate governance structure, and advancing management modernization;  
(5) to respect and safeguard the operational autonomy of State-owned and State-controlled enterprises, to protect the legitimate rights and interests of enterprises according to law, to promote enterprises in operating and managing according to law, and to enhance their competitiveness;  
(6) to guide and coordinate the resolution of difficulties and problems in the reform and development of State-owned and State-controlled enterprises.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国有资产监督管理机构应当向本级政府报告企业国有资产监督管理工作、国有资产保值增值状况和其他重大事项。

**Article 15** The State-owned assets supervision and administration institution shall report to the government at the corresponding level on the supervision and administration of State-owned assets of enterprises, the status of preservation and increase of value of State-owned assets, and other major matters.

### 第三章　企业负责人管理

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国有资产监督管理机构应当建立健全适应现代企业制度要求的企业负责人的选用机制和激励约束机制。

**Article 16** The State-owned assets supervision and administration institution shall establish and improve a selection and appointment mechanism and an incentive and restraint mechanism for responsible persons of enterprises that meet the requirements of the modern enterprise system.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国有资产监督管理机构依照有关规定，任免或者建议任免所出资企业的企业负责人：  
(一)任免国有独资企业的总经理、副总经理、总会计师及其他企业负责人；  
(二)任免国有独资公司的董事长、副董事长、董事，并向其提出总经理、副总经理、总会计师等的任免建议；  
(三)依照公司章程，提出向国有控股的公司派出的董事、监事人选，推荐国有控股的公司的董事长、副董事长和监事会主席人选，并向其提出总经理、副总经理、总会计师人选的建议；  
(四)依照公司章程，提出向国有参股的公司派出的董事、监事人选。  
国务院，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，设区的市、自治州级人民政府，对所出资企业的企业负责人的任免另有规定的，按照有关规定执行。

**Article 17** The State-owned assets supervision and administration institution shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, appoint or remove, or propose the appointment or removal of, the responsible persons of the invested enterprises:  
(1) to appoint or remove the general manager, deputy general manager, chief accountant, and other responsible persons of wholly State-owned enterprises;  
(2) to appoint or remove the chairman, vice chairman, and directors of wholly State-owned companies, and to propose the appointment or removal of the general manager, deputy general manager, chief accountant, and others to the company;  
(3) to propose candidates for directors and supervisors to be dispatched to State-controlled companies, to recommend candidates for chairman, vice chairman, and chairman of the board of supervisors of State-controlled companies, and to propose candidates for general manager, deputy general manager, and chief accountant to the company, in accordance with the company's articles of association;  
(4) to propose candidates for directors and supervisors to be dispatched to State-invested companies, in accordance with the company's articles of association.  
Where the State Council, the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or the people's government of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture has other provisions on the appointment or removal of the responsible persons of the invested enterprises, such provisions shall be followed.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国有资产监督管理机构应当建立企业负责人经营业绩考核制度，与其任命的企业负责人签订业绩合同，根据业绩合同对企业负责人进行年度考核和任期考核。

**Article 18** The State-owned assets supervision and administration institution shall establish a system for assessing the business performance of responsible persons of enterprises, shall sign performance contracts with the responsible persons appointed by it, and shall conduct annual and term assessments of the responsible persons based on the performance contracts.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国有资产监督管理机构应当依照有关规定，确定所出资企业中的国有独资企业、国有独资公司的企业负责人的薪酬；依据考核结果，决定其向所出资企业派出的企业负责人的奖惩。

**Article 19** The State-owned assets supervision and administration institution shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, determine the remuneration of the responsible persons of wholly State-owned enterprises and wholly State-owned companies among the invested enterprises; and shall, based on the assessment results, decide on the rewards or punishments for the responsible persons dispatched by it to the invested enterprises.

### 第四章　企业重大事项管理

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国有资产监督管理机构负责指导国有及国有控股企业建立现代企业制度，审核批准其所出资企业中的国有独资企业、国有独资公司的重组、股份制改造方案和所出资企业中的国有独资公司的章程。

**Article 20** The State-owned assets supervision and administration institution shall be responsible for guiding State-owned and State-controlled enterprises in establishing a modern enterprise system, and shall examine and approve the restructuring and shareholding reform plans of wholly State-owned enterprises and wholly State-owned companies among the invested enterprises, as well as the articles of association of wholly State-owned companies among the invested enterprises.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国有资产监督管理机构依照法定程序决定其所出资企业中的国有独资企业、国有独资公司的分立、合并、破产、解散、增减资本、发行公司债券等重大事项。其中，重要的国有独资企业、国有独资公司分立、合并、破产、解散的，应当由国有资产监督管理机构审核后，报本级人民政府批准。  
国有资产监督管理机构依照法定程序审核、决定国防科技工业领域其所出资企业中的国有独资企业、国有独资公司的有关重大事项时，按照国家有关法律、规定执行。

**Article 21** The state-owned assets supervision and administration authority shall, according to statutory procedures, decide on major matters such as split, merger, bankruptcy, dissolution, increase or decrease of capital, and issuance of corporate bonds of wholly state-owned enterprises and wholly state-owned companies in which it contributes capital. Among these, if important wholly state-owned enterprises and wholly state-owned companies undergo split, merger, bankruptcy, or dissolution, such matters shall be subject to review by the state-owned assets supervision and administration authority and then submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval.  
When the state-owned assets supervision and administration authority reviews and decides on relevant major matters concerning wholly state-owned enterprises and wholly state-owned companies in the defense science, technology and industry sector in which it contributes capital, it shall act in accordance with the relevant state laws and regulations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国有资产监督管理机构依照公司法的规定，派出股东代表、董事，参加国有控股的公司、国有参股的公司的股东会、董事会。  
国有控股的公司、国有参股的公司的股东会、董事会决定公司的分立、合并、破产、解散、增减资本、发行公司债券、任免企业负责人等重大事项时，国有资产监督管理机构派出的股东代表、董事，应当按照国有资产监督管理机构的指示发表意见、行使表决权。  
国有资产监督管理机构派出的股东代表、董事，应当将其履行职责的有关情况及时向国有资产监督管理机构报告。

**Article 22** The state-owned assets supervision and administration authority shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Company Law, send shareholder representatives and directors to participate in the shareholders' meetings and board of directors meetings of state-controlled companies and state-invested companies.  
When the shareholders' meeting or board of directors of a state-controlled company or state-invested company decides on major matters such as split, merger, bankruptcy, dissolution, increase or decrease of capital, issuance of corporate bonds, appointment or removal of enterprise leaders, the shareholder representatives and directors sent by the state-owned assets supervision and administration authority shall express opinions and exercise voting rights in accordance with the instructions of the state-owned assets supervision and administration authority.  
The shareholder representatives and directors sent by the state-owned assets supervision and administration authority shall promptly report to the state-owned assets supervision and administration authority the relevant circumstances of their performance of duties.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国有资产监督管理机构决定其所出资企业的国有股权转让。其中，转让全部国有股权或者转让部分国有股权致使国家不再拥有控股地位的，报本级人民政府批准。

**Article 23** The state-owned assets supervision and administration authority shall decide on the transfer of state-owned equity in the enterprises in which it contributes capital. Among these, if the transfer of all state-owned equity or the transfer of part of the state-owned equity results in the state no longer having a controlling position, such transfer shall be submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　所出资企业投资设立的重要子企业的重大事项，需由所出资企业报国有资产监督管理机构批准的，管理办法由国务院国有资产监督管理机构另行制定，报国务院批准。

**Article 24** Where major matters of important subsidiary enterprises established with investment by enterprises in which capital is contributed require approval by the state-owned assets supervision and administration authority upon submission by such enterprises, the administrative measures shall be separately formulated by the state-owned assets supervision and administration authority of the State Council and submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国有资产监督管理机构依照国家有关规定组织协调所出资企业中的国有独资企业、国有独资公司的兼并破产工作，并配合有关部门做好企业下岗职工安置等工作。

**Article 25** The state-owned assets supervision and administration authority shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, organize and coordinate the merger and bankruptcy work of wholly state-owned enterprises and wholly state-owned companies among the enterprises in which capital is contributed, and shall cooperate with relevant departments in properly handling matters such as the placement of laid-off workers.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国有资产监督管理机构依照国家有关规定拟订所出资企业收入分配制度改革的指导意见，调控所出资企业工资分配的总体水平。

**Article 26** The state-owned assets supervision and administration authority shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, formulate guiding opinions on the reform of the income distribution system of enterprises in which capital is contributed, and shall regulate the overall level of wage distribution in such enterprises.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国有资产监督管理机构可以对所出资企业中具备条件的国有独资企业、国有独资公司进行国有资产授权经营。  
被授权的国有独资企业、国有独资公司对其全资、控股、参股企业中国家投资形成的国有资产依法进行经营、管理和监督。

**Article 27** The state-owned assets supervision and administration authority may grant authorized operation of state-owned assets to eligible wholly state-owned enterprises and wholly state-owned companies among the enterprises in which capital is contributed.  
The authorized wholly state-owned enterprises and wholly state-owned companies shall, according to law, operate, manage and supervise the state-owned assets formed by state investment in their wholly-owned, controlled and invested enterprises.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　被授权的国有独资企业、国有独资公司应当建立和完善规范的现代企业制度，并承担企业国有资产的保值增值责任。

**Article 28** The authorized wholly state-owned enterprises and wholly state-owned companies shall establish and improve a standardized modern enterprise system, and shall assume the responsibility for maintaining and increasing the value of the state-owned assets of the enterprise.

### 第五章　企业国有资产管理

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国有资产监督管理机构依照国家有关规定，负责企业国有资产的产权界定、产权登记、资产评估监管、清产核资、资产统计、综合评价等基础管理工作。  
国有资产监督管理机构协调其所出资企业之间的企业国有资产产权纠纷。

**Article 29** The state-owned assets supervision and administration authority shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, be responsible for basic management work such as definition of property rights, registration of property rights, supervision of asset appraisal, verification of assets, asset statistics, and comprehensive evaluation of state-owned assets of enterprises.  
The state-owned assets supervision and administration authority shall coordinate disputes over property rights of state-owned assets of enterprises among the enterprises in which it contributes capital.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国有资产监督管理机构应当建立企业国有资产产权交易监督管理制度，加强企业国有资产产权交易的监督管理，促进企业国有资产的合理流动，防止企业国有资产流失。

**Article 30** The state-owned assets supervision and administration authority shall establish a supervision and management system for the transaction of property rights of state-owned assets of enterprises, strengthen the supervision and management of the transaction of property rights of state-owned assets of enterprises, promote the rational flow of state-owned assets of enterprises, and prevent the loss of state-owned assets of enterprises.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国有资产监督管理机构对其所出资企业的企业国有资产收益依法履行出资人职责；对其所出资企业的重大投融资规划、发展战略和规划，依照国家发展规划和产业政策履行出资人职责。

**Article 31** The State-owned assets supervision and administration institution shall, according to law, perform the duties of a contributor in respect of the earnings from the State-owned assets of the enterprise in which it has made capital contribution; and shall, in accordance with the national development plan and industrial policies, perform the duties of a contributor in respect of the major investment and financing plans, development strategies and plans of the enterprise in which it has made capital contribution.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　所出资企业中的国有独资企业、国有独资公司的重大资产处置，需由国有资产监督管理机构批准的，依照有关规定执行。

**Article 32** Where the disposition of major assets of a wholly State-owned enterprise or wholly State-owned company among the enterprises in which capital has been contributed requires approval by the State-owned assets supervision and administration institution, it shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions.

### 第六章　企业国有资产监督

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国有资产监督管理机构依法对所出资企业财务进行监督，建立和完善国有资产保值增值指标体系，维护国有资产出资人的权益。

**Article 33** The State-owned assets supervision and administration institution shall, according to law, supervise the finance of the enterprises in which it has made capital contribution, establish and improve the indicator system for maintaining and increasing the value of State-owned assets, and safeguard the rights and interests of the contributor of State-owned assets.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国有及国有控股企业应当加强内部监督和风险控制，依照国家有关规定建立健全财务、审计、企业法律顾问和职工民主监督等制度。

**Article 34** State-owned enterprises and State-controlled enterprises shall strengthen internal supervision and risk control, and establish and improve the systems of finance, audit, corporate legal counsel, and democratic supervision by employees, etc. in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　所出资企业中的国有独资企业、国有独资公司应当按照规定定期向国有资产监督管理机构报告财务状况、生产经营状况和国有资产保值增值状况。

**Article 35** A wholly State-owned enterprise or wholly State-owned company among the enterprises in which capital has been contributed shall, as prescribed, regularly report to the State-owned assets supervision and administration institution on its financial status, production and operation status, and the status of maintaining and increasing the value of State-owned assets.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国有资产监督管理机构不按规定任免或者建议任免所出资企业的企业负责人，或者违法干预所出资企业的生产经营活动，侵犯其合法权益，造成企业国有资产损失或者其他严重后果的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 36** Where the State-owned assets supervision and administration institution fails to appoint or remove, or to recommend or nominate, the persons-in-charge of the enterprises in which it has made capital contribution as prescribed, or illegally intervenes in the production and operation activities of the enterprise in which it has made capital contribution, infringes upon its lawful rights and interests, and causes losses to the State-owned assets of the enterprise or other serious consequences, the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　所出资企业中的国有独资企业、国有独资公司未按照规定向国有资产监督管理机构报告财务状况、生产经营状况和国有资产保值增值状况的，予以警告；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予纪律处分。

**Article 37** Where a wholly State-owned enterprise or wholly State-owned company among the enterprises in which capital has been contributed fails to report to the State-owned assets supervision and administration institution on its financial status, production and operation status, and the status of maintaining and increasing the value of State-owned assets as prescribed, a warning shall be given; if the circumstances are serious, the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国有及国有控股企业的企业负责人滥用职权、玩忽职守，造成企业国有资产损失的，应负赔偿责任，并对其依法给予纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 38** Where a person-in-charge of a State-owned enterprise or State-controlled enterprise abuses his or her power or neglects his or her duty, causing losses to the State-owned assets of the enterprise, he or she shall bear liability for compensation, and shall be given disciplinary sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　对企业国有资产损失负有责任受到撤职以上纪律处分的国有及国有控股企业的企业负责人，5年内不得担任任何国有及国有控股企业的企业负责人；造成企业国有资产重大损失或者被判处刑罚的，终身不得担任任何国有及国有控股企业的企业负责人。

**Article 39** A person-in-charge of a State-owned enterprise or State-controlled enterprise who is held responsible for losses of State-owned assets of the enterprise and has received disciplinary sanctions of dismissal from post or above shall not, within five years, serve as a person-in-charge of any State-owned enterprise or State-controlled enterprise; if he or she has caused major losses to the State-owned assets of the enterprise or has been sentenced to criminal punishment, he or she shall be permanently disqualified from serving as a person-in-charge of any State-owned enterprise or State-controlled enterprise.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国有及国有控股企业、国有参股企业的组织形式、组织机构、权利和义务等，依照《中华人民共和国公司法》等法律、行政法规和本条例的规定执行。

**Article 40** The organizational form, organizational structure, rights and obligations, etc. of State-owned enterprises, State-controlled enterprises, and enterprises in which the State holds participating shares shall be implemented in accordance with the Company Law of the People's Republic of China and other laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国有及国有控股企业、国有参股企业中中国共产党基层组织建设、社会主义精神文明建设和党风廉政建设，依照《中国共产党章程》和有关规定执行。  
国有及国有控股企业、国有参股企业中工会组织依照《中华人民共和国工会法》和《中国工会章程》的有关规定执行。

**Article 41** In wholly state-owned enterprises, state-controlled enterprises, and enterprises with state-owned shares, the construction of primary organizations of the Communist Party of China, socialist spiritual civilization, and Party conduct and integrity building shall be carried out in accordance with the Constitution of the Communist Party of China and relevant provisions.  
In wholly state-owned enterprises, state-controlled enterprises, and enterprises with state-owned shares, trade union organizations shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Trade Union Law of the People's Republic of China and the Constitution of the Chinese Trade Union.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国务院国有资产监督管理机构，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以依据本条例制定实施办法。

**Article 42** The State Council's State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Institution and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate implementation measures in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　本条例施行前制定的有关企业国有资产监督管理的行政法规与本条例不一致的，依照本条例的规定执行。

**Article 43** Where administrative regulations concerning the supervision and administration of state-owned assets of enterprises formulated before these Regulations come into force are inconsistent with these Regulations, they shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　政企尚未分开的单位，应当按照国务院的规定，加快改革，实现政企分开。政企分开后的企业，由国有资产监督管理机构依法履行出资人职责，依法对企业国有资产进行监督管理。

**Article 44** Entities where the separation of government functions from enterprise management has not yet been achieved shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council, accelerate reform to achieve such separation. After separation, enterprises shall be subject to the supervision and administration of the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Institution, which shall perform the duties of an investor according to law and supervise and administer the state-owned assets of the enterprise according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 45** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
