# 企业国有资产产权登记管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1996-01-25  
**生效日期**：1996-01-25

> (1996年1月25日中华人民共和国国务院令第192号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一条　为了加强企业国有资产产权登记管理，健全国有资产基础管理制度，防止国有资产流失，制定本办法。 第二条　本办法所称企业国有资产产权登记(以下简称产权登记)，是指国有资产管理部门代表政府对占有国有资产的各类企业的资产、负债、所有者权益等产权状况进行登记，依法确认产权归属关系的行为。 第三条　国有企业、国有独资公司、持有国家股权的单位以及以其他形式占有国有资产的企业(以下统称企业)，应当依照本办法的规定办理产权登记。 第四条　企业产权归属关系不清楚或者发生产权纠纷的，可以申请暂缓办理产权登记。 企业应当在经批准的暂缓办理产权登记期限内，将产权界定清楚、产权纠纷处理完毕，并及时办理产权登记。 第五条　县级以上各级人民政府国有资产管理部门，按照产权归属关系办理产权登记。 国有资产管理部门根据工作需要，可以按照产权归属关系委托政府有关部门或者机构办理产权登记。 第六条　产权登记分为占有产权登记、变动产权登记和注销产权登记。 第七条　企业应当依照本办法向国有资产管理部门办理占有产权登记。 占有产权登记的主要内容： (一)出资人名称、住所、出资金额及法定代表人； (二)企业名称、住所及法定代表人； (三)企业的资产、负债及所有者权益； (四)企业实收资本、国有资本；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强企业国有资产产权登记管理，健全国有资产基础管理制度，防止国有资产流失，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the registration management of property rights of state-owned assets in enterprises, perfecting the basic management system for state-owned assets, and preventing the loss of state-owned assets.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本办法所称企业国有资产产权登记(以下简称产权登记)，是指国有资产管理部门代表政府对占有国有资产的各类企业的资产、负债、所有者权益等产权状况进行登记，依法确认产权归属关系的行为。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Measures, the term "registration of property rights of state-owned assets in enterprises" (hereinafter referred to as "property rights registration") refers to the act whereby the administrative department of state-owned assets registers the property rights status, such as assets, liabilities, and owners' equity, of various types of enterprises that possess state-owned assets on behalf of the government, and confirms the attribution of property rights according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国有企业、国有独资公司、持有国家股权的单位以及以其他形式占有国有资产的企业(以下统称企业)，应当依照本办法的规定办理产权登记。

**Article 3** State-owned enterprises, wholly state-owned companies, units holding state equity, and enterprises that possess state-owned assets in other forms (hereinafter collectively referred to as "enterprises") shall undergo property rights registration in accordance with the provisions of these Measures.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　企业产权归属关系不清楚或者发生产权纠纷的，可以申请暂缓办理产权登记。  
企业应当在经批准的暂缓办理产权登记期限内，将产权界定清楚、产权纠纷处理完毕，并及时办理产权登记。

**Article 4** Where the attribution of property rights of an enterprise is unclear or a property rights dispute arises, the enterprise may apply for a temporary suspension of property rights registration.  
The enterprise shall, within the approved period of temporary suspension, clarify the property rights, settle the property rights dispute, and promptly undergo property rights registration.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上各级人民政府国有资产管理部门，按照产权归属关系办理产权登记。  
国有资产管理部门根据工作需要，可以按照产权归属关系委托政府有关部门或者机构办理产权登记。

**Article 5** The administrative departments of state-owned assets of the people's governments at or above the county level shall handle property rights registration in accordance with the attribution of property rights.  
The administrative department of state-owned assets may, based on work requirements, entrust relevant government departments or agencies with handling property rights registration in accordance with the attribution of property rights.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　产权登记分为占有产权登记、变动产权登记和注销产权登记。

**Article 6** Property rights registration is classified into possession property rights registration, change property rights registration, and cancellation property rights registration.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　企业应当依照本办法向国有资产管理部门办理占有产权登记。  
占有产权登记的主要内容：  
(一)出资人名称、住所、出资金额及法定代表人；  
(二)企业名称、住所及法定代表人；  
(三)企业的资产、负债及所有者权益；  
(四)企业实收资本、国有资本；  
(五)企业投资情况；  
(六)国务院国有资产管理部门规定的其他事项。  
国有资产管理部门向企业核发的国有资产产权登记表，是企业的资信证明文件。

**Article 7** An enterprise shall undergo possession property rights registration with the administrative department of state-owned assets in accordance with these Measures.  
The main contents of possession property rights registration:  
(1) Name, domicile, amount of capital contribution, and legal representative of the contributor;  
(2) Name, domicile, and legal representative of the enterprise;  
(3) Assets, liabilities, and owners' equity of the enterprise;  
(4) Paid-in capital and state-owned capital of the enterprise;  
(5) Investment situation of the enterprise;  
(6) Other matters prescribed by the administrative department of state-owned assets of the State Council.  
The state-owned assets property rights registration form issued by the administrative department of state-owned assets to the enterprise shall be a certificate of credit standing of the enterprise.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　企业发生下列变动情形之一的，应当自变动之日起30日内办理变动产权登记：  
(一)企业名称、住所或者法定代表人改变的；  
(二)国有资本占企业实收资本比例发生变化的；  
(三)企业分立、合并或者改变经营形式的；  
(四)有国务院国有资产管理部门规定的其他变动情形的。

**Article 8** Where an enterprise undergoes any of the following changes, it shall undergo change property rights registration within 30 days from the date of such change:  
(1) The name, domicile, or legal representative of the enterprise is changed;  
(2) The proportion of state-owned capital in the paid-in capital of the enterprise is changed;  
(3) The enterprise is divided, merged, or its operating form is changed;  
(4) There are other change circumstances prescribed by the administrative department of state-owned assets of the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　企业发生下列情形之一的，应当自各该情形发生之日起30日内办理注销产权登记：  
(一)企业解散、被依法撤销或者被依法宣告破产的；  
(二)企业转让全部产权或者企业被划转的；  
(三)有国务院国有资产管理部门规定的其他情形的。

**Article 9** Where an enterprise undergoes any of the following circumstances, it shall undergo cancellation property rights registration within 30 days from the date of such circumstance:  
(1) The enterprise is dissolved, revoked according to law, or declared bankrupt according to law;  
(2) The enterprise transfers all property rights or the enterprise is transferred in whole;  
(3) There are other circumstances prescribed by the administrative department of state-owned assets of the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　企业办理产权登记，应当按照规定填报国有资产产权登记表并提交有关文件、凭证、报表等。填报的内容或者提交的文件、凭证、报表等不符合规定的，国有资产管理部门有权要求企业补正。

**Article 10** When undergoing property rights registration, an enterprise shall fill in the state-owned assets property rights registration form as prescribed and submit relevant documents, vouchers, statements, etc. If the filled contents or the submitted documents, vouchers, statements, etc., do not conform to the provisions, the administrative department of state-owned assets shall have the right to require the enterprise to make corrections.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　产权登记实行年度检查制度。  
企业应当于每一年度终了后90日内，办理产权年度检查登记，向国有资产管理部门提交财务报告和国有资产经营年度报告书，报告下列主要内容：  
(一)出资人的资金实际到位情况；  
(二)企业国有资产的结构变化，包括企业对外投资情况；  
(三)国有资产增减、变动情况；  
(四)国务院国有资产管理部门规定的其他事项。

**Article 11** A system of annual inspection for property rights registration shall be implemented.  
An enterprise shall, within 90 days after the end of each fiscal year, undergo the annual inspection registration of property rights, and submit to the administrative department of state-owned assets a financial report and an annual report on the operation of state-owned assets, reporting the following main contents:  
(1) The actual capital contribution of the contributor;  
(2) The structural changes of state-owned assets of the enterprise, including the enterprise's outward investment;  
(3) The increase, decrease, or change of state-owned assets;  
(4) Other matters prescribed by the administrative department of state-owned assets of the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国有资产产权登记表由国务院国有资产管理部门统一制定。  
任何单位和个人不得伪造、涂改、出卖或者出借国有资产产权登记表。  
国有资产产权登记表遗失或者毁坏的，应当按照规定申请补领。

**Article 12** The state-owned assets property rights registration form shall be uniformly formulated by the administrative department of state-owned assets of the State Council.  
No unit or individual shall forge, alter, sell, or lend the state-owned assets property rights registration form.  
Where the state-owned assets property rights registration form is lost or damaged, the enterprise shall apply for a replacement in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国有资产管理部门应当建立健全产权登记档案制度，并定期分析和报告国有资产产权状况。

**Article 13** The administrative department of state-owned assets shall establish and improve the archives system for property rights registration, and regularly analyze and report on the status of property rights of state-owned assets.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　企业违反本办法规定，有下列行为之一的，由国有资产管理部门责令改正、通报批评，可以处以10万元以下的罚款，并提请政府有关部门对企业领导人员和直接责任人员按照规定给予纪律处分：  
(一)在规定期限内不办理产权登记的；  
(二)隐瞒真实情况、未如实办理产权登记的；  
(三)不按照规定办理产权年度检查登记的；  
(四)伪造、涂改、出卖或者出借国有资产产权登记表的。

**Article 14** Where an enterprise violates the provisions of these Measures and commits any of the following acts, the administrative department of state-owned assets shall order it to make corrections, circulate a notice of criticism, may impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan, and shall request relevant government departments to impose disciplinary sanctions on the leading personnel and the persons directly responsible in accordance with the provisions:  
(1) Failing to undergo property rights registration within the prescribed time limit;  
(2) Concealing the true situation and failing to undergo property rights registration truthfully;  
(3) Failing to undergo the annual inspection registration of property rights as prescribed;  
(4) Forging, altering, selling, or lending the state-owned assets property rights registration form.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国有资产管理部门工作人员在办理产权登记中玩忽职守、徇私舞弊、滥用职权、谋取私利，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 15** Where a staff member of the administrative department of state-owned assets, in handling property rights registration, neglects his or her duty, engages in malpractice for personal gain, abuses his or her power, or seeks personal benefits, and such act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　军队企业的国有资产产权登记管理办法，由中国人民解放军总后勤部会同国务院国有资产管理部门参照本办法制定。

**Article 16** The measures for the registration administration of property rights of state-owned assets in military enterprises shall be formulated by the General Logistics Department of the People's Liberation Army of China jointly with the administrative department of state-owned assets of the State Council by reference to these Measures.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本办法自发布之日起施行。国家国有资产管理局、财政部、国家工商行政管理局1992年5月11日发布的《国有资产产权登记管理试行办法》同时废止。

**Article 17** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Interim Measures for the Registration Administration of Property Rights of State-owned Assets promulgated by the State Administration of State-owned Assets, the Ministry of Finance, and the State Administration for Industry and Commerce on May 11, 1992 shall be abolished simultaneously.
