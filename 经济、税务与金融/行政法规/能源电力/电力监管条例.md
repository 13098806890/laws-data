# 电力监管条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2005-02-15  
**生效日期**：2005-05-01

> (2005年2月2日国务院第80次常务会议通过　2005年2月15日中华人民共和国国务院令第432号公布　自2005年5月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强电力监管，规范电力监管行为，完善电力监管制度，制定本条例。 第二条　电力监管的任务是维护电力市场秩序，依法保护电力投资者、经营者、使用者的合法权益和社会公共利益，保障电力系统安全稳定运行，促进电力事业健康发展。 第三条　电力监管应当依法进行，并遵循公开、公正和效率的原则。 第四条　国务院电力监管机构依照本条例和国务院有关规定，履行电力监管和行政执法职能；国务院有关部门依照有关法律、行政法规和国务院有关规定，履行相关的监管职能和行政执法职能。 第五条　任何单位和个人对违反本条例和国家有关电力监管规定的行为有权向电力监管机构和政府有关部门举报，电力监管机构和政府有关部门应当及时处理，并依照有关规定对举报有功人员给予奖励。 第二章　监管机构 第六条　国务院电力监管机构根据履行职责的需要，经国务院批准，设立派出机构。国务院电力监管机构对派出机构实行统一领导和管理。 国务院电力监管机构的派出机构在国务院电力监管机构的授权范围内，履行电力监管职责。 第七条　电力监管机构从事监管工作的人员，应当具备与电力监管工作相适应的专业知识和业务工作经验。 第八条　电力监管机构从事监管工作的人员，应当忠于职守，依法办事，公正廉洁，不得利用职务便利谋取不正当利益，不得在电力企业、电力调度交易机构兼任职务。 第九条　电力监管机构应当建立监管责任制度和监管信息公开制度。 第十条　电力监管机构及其从事监管工作的人员依法履行电力监管职责，有关单位和人员应当予以配合和协助。 第十一条　电力监管机构应当接受国务院财政、监察、审计等部门依法实施的监督。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强电力监管，规范电力监管行为，完善电力监管制度，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening electricity regulation, standardizing electricity regulatory acts, and improving the electricity regulatory system.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　电力监管的任务是维护电力市场秩序，依法保护电力投资者、经营者、使用者的合法权益和社会公共利益，保障电力系统安全稳定运行，促进电力事业健康发展。

**Article 2** The task of electricity regulation is to maintain the order of the electricity market, protect according to law the legitimate rights and interests of electricity investors, operators and users as well as the public interest, ensure the safe and stable operation of the electricity system, and promote the healthy development of the electricity industry.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　电力监管应当依法进行，并遵循公开、公正和效率的原则。

**Article 3** Electricity regulation shall be carried out according to law and shall follow the principles of openness, fairness and efficiency.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院电力监管机构依照本条例和国务院有关规定，履行电力监管和行政执法职能；国务院有关部门依照有关法律、行政法规和国务院有关规定，履行相关的监管职能和行政执法职能。

**Article 4** The electricity regulatory institution of the State Council shall, in accordance with these Regulations and the relevant provisions of the State Council, perform the functions of electricity regulation and administrative law enforcement; the relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations and the relevant provisions of the State Council, perform the relevant regulatory functions and administrative law enforcement functions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　任何单位和个人对违反本条例和国家有关电力监管规定的行为有权向电力监管机构和政府有关部门举报，电力监管机构和政府有关部门应当及时处理，并依照有关规定对举报有功人员给予奖励。

**Article 5** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report to the electricity regulatory institution and the relevant government departments any act that violates these Regulations and the relevant provisions of the State on electricity regulation, and the electricity regulatory institution and the relevant government departments shall handle the matter in a timely manner and, in accordance with the relevant provisions, give rewards to the persons who have rendered meritorious service in reporting.

### 第二章　监管机构

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院电力监管机构根据履行职责的需要，经国务院批准，设立派出机构。国务院电力监管机构对派出机构实行统一领导和管理。  
国务院电力监管机构的派出机构在国务院电力监管机构的授权范围内，履行电力监管职责。

**Article 6** The electricity regulatory institution of the State Council shall, as required by the performance of its duties and with the approval of the State Council, establish dispatched agencies. The electricity regulatory institution of the State Council shall exercise unified leadership and management over the dispatched agencies.  
The dispatched agencies of the electricity regulatory institution of the State Council shall, within the scope of authorization granted by the electricity regulatory institution of the State Council, perform the electricity regulatory duties.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　电力监管机构从事监管工作的人员，应当具备与电力监管工作相适应的专业知识和业务工作经验。

**Article 7** Persons engaged in regulatory work in the electricity regulatory institution shall possess professional knowledge and operational work experience commensurate with the electricity regulatory work.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　电力监管机构从事监管工作的人员，应当忠于职守，依法办事，公正廉洁，不得利用职务便利谋取不正当利益，不得在电力企业、电力调度交易机构兼任职务。

**Article 8** Persons engaged in regulatory work in the electricity regulatory institution shall be devoted to their duties, act according to law, be fair and honest, shall not take advantage of their positions to seek illegitimate interests, and shall not hold concurrent posts in electricity enterprises or electricity dispatch and trading institutions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　电力监管机构应当建立监管责任制度和监管信息公开制度。

**Article 9** The electricity regulatory institution shall establish a regulatory responsibility system and a system for the disclosure of regulatory information.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　电力监管机构及其从事监管工作的人员依法履行电力监管职责，有关单位和人员应当予以配合和协助。

**Article 10** Where the electricity regulatory institution and the persons engaged in regulatory work therein perform the electricity regulatory duties according to law, the relevant entities and persons shall cooperate and provide assistance.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　电力监管机构应当接受国务院财政、监察、审计等部门依法实施的监督。

**Article 11** The electricity regulatory institution shall accept the supervision exercised according to law by the financial, supervisory, auditing and other departments of the State Council.

### 第三章　监管职责

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院电力监管机构依照有关法律、行政法规和本条例的规定，在其职责范围内制定并发布电力监管规章、规则。

**Article 12** The electricity regulatory institution of the State Council shall, in accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations and the provisions of these Regulations, formulate and issue electricity regulatory rules and regulations within the scope of its duties.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　电力监管机构依照有关法律和国务院有关规定，颁发和管理电力业务许可证。

**Article 13** The electricity regulatory institution shall, in accordance with the relevant laws and the relevant provisions of the State Council, issue and manage electricity business licenses.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　电力监管机构按照国家有关规定，对发电企业在各电力市场中所占份额的比例实施监管。

**Article 14** The electricity regulatory institution shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, supervise the proportion of shares held by power generation enterprises in each electricity market.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　电力监管机构对发电厂并网、电网互联以及发电厂与电网协调运行中执行有关规章、规则的情况实施监管。

**Article 15** The electricity regulatory institution shall supervise the implementation of the relevant regulations and rules in the grid connection of power plants, interconnection of power grids, and coordinated operation between power plants and power grids.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　电力监管机构对电力市场向从事电力交易的主体公平、无歧视开放的情况以及输电企业公平开放电网的情况依法实施监管。

**Article 16** The electricity regulatory institution shall, according to law, supervise the fair and non-discriminatory opening of the electricity market to entities engaged in electricity trading and the fair opening of power grids by transmission enterprises.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　电力监管机构对电力企业、电力调度交易机构执行电力市场运行规则的情况，以及电力调度交易机构执行电力调度规则的情况实施监管。

**Article 17** The electricity regulatory institution shall supervise the implementation of electricity market operation rules by electricity enterprises and electricity dispatch and trading institutions, as well as the implementation of electricity dispatch rules by electricity dispatch and trading institutions.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　电力监管机构对供电企业按照国家规定的电能质量和供电服务质量标准向用户提供供电服务的情况实施监管。

**Article 18** The electricity regulatory institution shall supervise the provision of power supply services by power supply enterprises to users in accordance with the national standards for electric energy quality and power supply service quality.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　电力监管机构具体负责电力安全监督管理工作。  
国务院电力监管机构经商国务院发展改革部门、国务院安全生产监督管理部门等有关部门后，制订重大电力生产安全事故处置预案，建立重大电力生产安全事故应急处置制度。

**Article 19** The electricity regulatory institution shall be specifically responsible for the supervision and administration of electricity safety.  
The electricity regulatory institution of the State Council shall, after consulting with the development and reform department of the State Council, the work safety supervision and administration department of the State Council and other relevant departments, formulate contingency plans for handling major electricity production safety accidents and establish an emergency response system for major electricity production safety accidents.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院价格主管部门、国务院电力监管机构依照法律、行政法规和国务院的规定，对电价实施监管。

**Article 20** The competent pricing department of the State Council and the electricity regulatory institution of the State Council shall, in accordance with the laws, administrative regulations and the provisions of the State Council, supervise electricity prices.

### 第四章　监管措施

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　电力监管机构根据履行监管职责的需要，有权要求电力企业、电力调度交易机构报送与监管事项相关的文件、资料。  
电力企业、电力调度交易机构应当如实提供有关文件、资料。

**Article 21** The electricity regulatory institution shall have the authority, as required by the performance of its regulatory duties, to require electricity enterprises and electricity dispatch and trading institutions to submit documents and materials relating to the regulatory matters.  
Electricity enterprises and electricity dispatch and trading institutions shall truthfully provide the relevant documents and materials.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国务院电力监管机构应当建立电力监管信息系统。  
电力企业、电力调度交易机构应当按照国务院电力监管机构的规定将与监管相关的信息系统接入电力监管信息系统。

**Article 22** The electricity regulatory institution of the State Council shall establish an electricity regulatory information system.  
Electricity enterprises and electricity dispatch and trading institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of the electricity regulatory institution of the State Council, connect their information systems related to regulation to the electricity regulatory information system.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　电力监管机构有权责令电力企业、电力调度交易机构按照国家有关电力监管规章、规则的规定如实披露有关信息。

**Article 23** The electricity regulatory institution shall have the authority to order electricity enterprises and electricity dispatch and trading institutions to truthfully disclose relevant information in accordance with the provisions of the relevant regulations and rules of the State on electricity regulation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　电力监管机构依法履行职责，可以采取下列措施，进行现场检查：  
(一)进入电力企业、电力调度交易机构进行检查；  
(二)询问电力企业、电力调度交易机构的工作人员，要求其对有关检查事项作出说明；  
(三)查阅、复制与检查事项有关的文件、资料，对可能被转移、隐匿、损毁的文件、资料予以封存；  
(四)对检查中发现的违法行为，有权当场予以纠正或者要求限期改正。

**Article 24** The electricity regulatory institution, when performing its duties according to law, may take the following measures to conduct on-site inspections:  
(1) entering electricity enterprises and electricity dispatch and trading institutions for inspection;  
(2) questioning the staff members of electricity enterprises and electricity dispatch and trading institutions and requiring them to give explanations on matters relating to the inspection;  
(3) consulting and making copies of documents and materials relating to the matters under inspection, and sealing up documents and materials that may be transferred, concealed or destroyed;  
(4) having the authority to correct on the spot or order rectification within a time limit any illegal acts discovered during the inspection.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　依法从事电力监管工作的人员在进行现场检查时，应当出示有效执法证件；未出示有效执法证件的，电力企业、电力调度交易机构有权拒绝检查。

**Article 25** Persons engaged in electricity regulatory work according to law shall produce valid law enforcement credentials when conducting on-site inspections; if they fail to produce valid law enforcement credentials, electricity enterprises and electricity dispatch and trading institutions shall have the right to refuse the inspection.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　发电厂与电网并网、电网与电网互联，并网双方或者互联双方达不成协议，影响电力交易正常进行的，电力监管机构应当进行协调；经协调仍不能达成协议的，由电力监管机构作出裁决。

**Article 26** Where, in the case of grid connection between a power plant and a power grid or interconnection between power grids, the parties to the grid connection or interconnection fail to reach an agreement, thereby affecting the normal conduct of electricity trading, the electricity regulatory institution shall conduct coordination; if no agreement can still be reached after coordination, the electricity regulatory institution shall make an award.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　电力企业发生电力生产安全事故，应当及时采取措施，防止事故扩大，并向电力监管机构和其他有关部门报告。电力监管机构接到发生重大电力生产安全事故报告后，应当按照重大电力生产安全事故处置预案，及时采取处置措施。  
电力监管机构按照国家有关规定组织或者参加电力生产安全事故的调查处理。

**Article 27** Where an electricity production safety accident occurs in an electricity enterprise, the enterprise shall promptly take measures to prevent the accident from escalating and report to the electricity regulatory institution and other relevant departments. After receiving a report on the occurrence of a major electricity production safety accident, the electricity regulatory institution shall, in accordance with the contingency plan for handling major electricity production safety accidents, promptly take handling measures.  
The electricity regulatory institution shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, organize or participate in the investigation and handling of electricity production safety accidents.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　电力监管机构对电力企业、电力调度交易机构违反有关电力监管的法律、行政法规或者有关电力监管规章、规则，损害社会公共利益的行为及其处理情况，可以向社会公布。

**Article 28** The electricity regulatory institution may disclose to the public the acts of electricity enterprises or electricity dispatch and trading institutions that violate the relevant laws or administrative regulations on electricity regulation or the relevant electricity regulatory regulations or rules and harm the public interest, as well as the handling of such acts.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　电力监管机构从事监管工作的人员有下列情形之一的，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)违反有关法律和国务院有关规定颁发电力业务许可证的；  
(二)发现未经许可擅自经营电力业务的行为，不依法进行处理的；  
(三)发现违法行为或者接到对违法行为的举报后，不及时进行处理的；  
(四)利用职务便利谋取不正当利益的。  
电力监管机构从事监管工作的人员在电力企业、电力调度交易机构兼任职务的，由电力监管机构责令改正，没收兼职所得；拒不改正的，予以辞退或者开除。

**Article 29** Where any person engaged in regulatory work in the electricity regulatory institution falls under any of the following circumstances, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) issuing an electricity business license in violation of the relevant laws and the relevant provisions of the State Council;  
(2) discovering an act of engaging in electricity business without a license but failing to handle it according to law;  
(3) failing to handle an illegal act in a timely manner after discovering it or receiving a report on an illegal act;  
(4) taking advantage of his or her position to seek illegitimate interests.  
Where a person engaged in regulatory work in the electricity regulatory institution holds a concurrent post in an electricity enterprise or an electricity dispatch and trading institution, the electricity regulatory institution shall order him or her to rectify the situation and confiscate the income from the concurrent post; if he or she refuses to rectify, he or she shall be dismissed or discharged.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　 违反规定未取得电力业务许可证擅自经营电力业务的，由电力监管机构责令改正，没收违法所得，可以并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 30** Where any entity or individual, in violation of the provisions, engages in electricity business without obtaining an electricity business license, the electricity regulatory institution shall order rectification, confiscate the illegal gains, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　电力企业违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由电力监管机构责令改正；拒不改正的，处10万元以上100万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；情节严重的，可以吊销电力业务许可证：  
(一)不遵守电力市场运行规则的；  
(二)发电厂并网、电网互联不遵守有关规章、规则的；  
(三)不向从事电力交易的主体公平、无歧视开放电力市场或者不按照规定公平开放电网的。

**Article 31** Where an electricity enterprise violates the provisions of these Regulations and falls under any of the following circumstances, the electricity regulatory institution shall order rectification; if it refuses to rectify, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 1 million yuan shall be imposed; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law; if the circumstances are serious, the electricity business license may be revoked:  
(1) failing to comply with the electricity market operation rules;  
(2) failing to comply with the relevant regulations and rules concerning grid connection of power plants or interconnection of power grids;  
(3) failing to open the electricity market to entities engaged in electricity trading in a fair and non-discriminatory manner, or failing to open the power grid fairly in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　供电企业未按照国家规定的电能质量和供电服务质量标准向用户提供供电服务的，由电力监管机构责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 32** Where a power supply enterprise fails to provide power supply services to users in accordance with the national standards for electric energy quality and power supply service quality, the electricity regulatory institution shall order rectification and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　电力调度交易机构违反本条例规定，不按照电力市场运行规则组织交易的，由电力监管机构责令改正；拒不改正的，处10万元以上100万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。  
电力调度交易机构工作人员泄露电力交易内幕信息的，由电力监管机构责令改正，并依法给予处分。

**Article 33** Where an electricity dispatch and trading institution violates the provisions of these Regulations by failing to organize trading in accordance with the electricity market operation rules, the electricity regulatory institution shall order rectification; if it refuses to rectify, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 1 million yuan shall be imposed; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.  
Where a staff member of an electricity dispatch and trading institution divulges inside information on electricity trading, the electricity regulatory institution shall order rectification and shall impose sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　电力企业、电力调度交易机构有下列情形之一的，由电力监管机构责令改正；拒不改正的，处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)拒绝或者阻碍电力监管机构及其从事监管工作的人员依法履行监管职责的；  
(二)提供虚假或者隐瞒重要事实的文件、资料的；  
(三)未按照国家有关电力监管规章、规则的规定披露有关信息的。

**Article 34** Where an electricity enterprise or an electricity dispatch and trading institution falls under any of the following circumstances, the electricity regulatory institution shall order rectification; if it refuses to rectify, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) refusing or obstructing the electricity regulatory institution and the persons engaged in regulatory work therein from lawfully performing their regulatory duties;  
(2) providing documents or materials that are false or conceal important facts;  
(3) failing to disclose relevant information in accordance with the provisions of the relevant regulations and rules of the State on electricity regulation.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本条例规定的罚款和没收的违法所得，按照国家有关规定上缴国库。

**Article 35** The fines and confiscated illegal gains provided for in these Regulations shall be turned over to the State treasury in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　电力企业应当按照国务院价格主管部门、财政部门的有关规定缴纳电力监管费。

**Article 36** Electricity enterprises shall pay electricity regulatory fees in accordance with the relevant provisions of the competent pricing department and the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　本条例自2005年5月1日起施行。

**Article 37** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2005.
