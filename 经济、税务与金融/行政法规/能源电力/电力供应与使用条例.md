# 电力供应与使用条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：1996-09-01

> (1996年4月17日中华人民共和国国务院令第196号发布　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强电力供应与使用的管理，保障供电、用电双方的合法权益，维护供电、用电秩序，安全、经济、合理地供电和用电，根据《中华人民共和国电力法》制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内，电力供应企业(以下称供电企业)和电力使用者(以下称用户)以及与电力供应、使用有关的单位和个人，必须遵守本条例。 第三条　国务院电力管理部门负责全国电力供应与使用的监督管理工作。 县级以上地方人民政府电力管理部门负责本行政区域内电力供应与使用的监督管理工作。 第四条　电网经营企业依法负责本供区内的电力供应与使用的业务工作，并接受电力管理部门的监督。 第五条　国家对电力供应和使用实行安全用电、节约用电、计划用电的管理原则。 供电企业和用户应当遵守国家有关规定，采取有效措施，做好安全用电、节约用电、计划用电工作。 第六条　供电企业和用户应当根据平等自愿、协商一致的原则签订供用电合同。 第七条　电力管理部门应当加强对供用电的监督管理，协调供用电各方关系，禁止危害供用电安全和非法侵占电能的行为。 第二章　供电营业区 第八条　供电企业在批准的供电营业区内向用户供电。 供电营业区的划分，应当考虑电网的结构和供电合理性等因素。一个供电营业区内只设立一个供电营业机构。 第九条　供电营业区的设立、变更，由供电企业提出申请，电力管理部门依据职责和管理权限，会同同级有关部门审查批准后，发给《电力业务许可证》。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强电力供应与使用的管理，保障供电、用电双方的合法权益，维护供电、用电秩序，安全、经济、合理地供电和用电，根据《中华人民共和国电力法》制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Electricity Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of strengthening the management of power supply and consumption, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of both power supply and consumption parties, maintaining the order of power supply and consumption, and ensuring safe, economical and rational power supply and consumption.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内，电力供应企业(以下称供电企业)和电力使用者(以下称用户)以及与电力供应、使用有关的单位和个人，必须遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Within the territory of the People's Republic of China, power supply enterprises (hereinafter referred to as power supply enterprises) and power consumers (hereinafter referred to as users), as well as entities and individuals related to power supply and consumption, must comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院电力管理部门负责全国电力供应与使用的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府电力管理部门负责本行政区域内电力供应与使用的监督管理工作。

**Article 3** The power administrative department under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of power supply and consumption nationwide.  
The power administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of power supply and consumption within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　电网经营企业依法负责本供区内的电力供应与使用的业务工作，并接受电力管理部门的监督。

**Article 4** Power grid enterprises shall, according to law, be responsible for the business work of power supply and consumption within their respective supply areas, and shall accept the supervision of the power administrative departments.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家对电力供应和使用实行安全用电、节约用电、计划用电的管理原则。  
供电企业和用户应当遵守国家有关规定，采取有效措施，做好安全用电、节约用电、计划用电工作。

**Article 5** The State adopts the management principles of safe electricity use, economical electricity use, and planned electricity use for power supply and consumption.  
Power supply enterprises and users shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State and take effective measures to ensure safe, economical and planned electricity use.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　供电企业和用户应当根据平等自愿、协商一致的原则签订供用电合同。

**Article 6** Power supply enterprises and users shall conclude power supply and consumption contracts based on the principles of equality, voluntariness, and consensus through consultation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　电力管理部门应当加强对供用电的监督管理，协调供用电各方关系，禁止危害供用电安全和非法侵占电能的行为。

**Article 7** The power administrative departments shall strengthen the supervision and management of power supply and consumption, coordinate the relationships among all parties involved in power supply and consumption, and prohibit acts that endanger the safety of power supply and consumption or illegally embezzle electric energy.

### 第二章　供电营业区

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　供电企业在批准的供电营业区内向用户供电。  
供电营业区的划分，应当考虑电网的结构和供电合理性等因素。一个供电营业区内只设立一个供电营业机构。

**Article 8** A power supply enterprise shall supply power to users within its approved power supply business area.  
The division of power supply business areas shall take into account factors such as the structure of the power grid and the rationality of power supply. Only one power supply business institution shall be established within a single power supply business area.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　供电营业区的设立、变更，由供电企业提出申请，电力管理部门依据职责和管理权限，会同同级有关部门审查批准后，发给《电力业务许可证》。  
电网经营企业应当根据电网结构和供电合理性的原则协助电力管理部门划分供电营业区。  
供电营业区的划分和管理办法，由国务院电力管理部门制定。

**Article 9** For the establishment or change of a power supply business area, the power supply enterprise shall submit an application, which shall, after being examined and approved by the power administrative department in conjunction with the relevant departments at the same level according to its functions and administrative authority, be issued a 'Electric Power Business License'.  
Power grid enterprises shall assist the power administrative departments in dividing power supply business areas based on the principles of grid structure and rationality of power supply.  
The measures for the division and administration of power supply business areas shall be formulated by the power administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　并网运行的电力生产企业按照并网协议运行后，送入电网的电力、电量由供电营业机构统一经销。

**Article 10** After a power generation enterprise operating in parallel with the grid operates according to the grid connection agreement, the power and electricity delivered to the grid shall be uniformly marketed by the power supply business institution.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　用户用电容量超过其所在的供电营业区内供电企业供电能力的，由省级以上电力管理部门指定的其他供电企业供电。

**Article 11** Where a user's electricity consumption capacity exceeds the supply capacity of the power supply enterprise within its power supply business area, the power shall be supplied by another power supply enterprise designated by the power administrative department at or above the provincial level.

### 第三章　供电设施

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上各级人民政府应当将城乡电网的建设与改造规划，纳入城市建设和乡村建设的总体规划。各级电力管理部门应当会同有关行政主管部门和电网经营企业做好城乡电网建设和改造的规划。供电企业应当按照规划做好供电设施建设和运行管理工作。

**Article 12** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the plans for the construction and renovation of urban and rural power grids into the overall plans for urban construction and rural construction. The power administrative departments at all levels shall, together with the relevant administrative departments and power grid enterprises, make plans for the construction and renovation of urban and rural power grids. Power supply enterprises shall, in accordance with the plans, carry out the construction and operation management of power supply facilities.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　地方各级人民政府应当按照城市建设和乡村建设的总体规划统筹安排城乡供电线路走廊、电缆通道、区域变电所、区域配电所和营业网点的用地。  
供电企业可以按照国家有关规定在规划的线路走廊、电缆通道、区域变电所、区域配电所和营业网点的用地上，架线、敷设电缆和建设公用供电设施。

**Article 13** Local people's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the overall plans for urban construction and rural construction, make overall arrangements for the land use for urban and rural power line corridors, cable channels, regional substations, regional distribution stations and business outlets.  
Power supply enterprises may, according to the relevant provisions of the State, erect lines, lay cables and construct public power supply facilities on the land planned for line corridors, cable channels, regional substations, regional distribution stations and business outlets.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　公用路灯由乡、民族乡、镇人民政府或者县级以上地方人民政府有关部门负责建设，并负责运行维护和交付电费，也可以委托供电企业代为有偿设计、施工和维护管理。

**Article 14** Public street lamps shall be constructed by the people's governments of townships, ethnic townships, and towns or the relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level, which shall also be responsible for their operation, maintenance and payment of electricity charges. Alternatively, they may entrust power supply enterprises to carry out design, construction and maintenance management for a fee.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　供电设施、受电设施的设计、施工、试验和运行，应当符合国家标准或者电力行业标准。

**Article 15** The design, construction, testing and operation of power supply facilities and power receiving facilities shall comply with national standards or power industry standards.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　供电企业和用户对供电设施、受电设施进行建设和维护时，作业区域内的有关单位和个人应当给予协助，提供方便；因作业对建筑物或者农作物造成损坏的，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定负责修复或者给予合理的补偿。

**Article 16** When power supply enterprises and users construct and maintain power supply facilities and power receiving facilities, the relevant entities and individuals within the operation area shall provide assistance and convenience; if damage is caused to buildings or crops due to the operations, they shall be responsible for repair or provide reasonable compensation in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公用供电设施建成投产后，由供电单位统一维护管理。经电力管理部门批准，供电企业可以使用、改造、扩建该供电设施。  
共用供电设施的维护管理，由产权单位协商确定，产权单位可自行维护管理，也可以委托供电企业维护管理。  
用户专用的供电设施建成投产后，由用户维护管理或者委托供电企业维护管理。

**Article 17** After public power supply facilities are constructed and put into operation, they shall be uniformly maintained and managed by the power supply unit. With the approval of the power administrative department, a power supply enterprise may use, renovate or expand such power supply facilities.  
The maintenance and management of shared power supply facilities shall be determined through consultation by the property rights units, which may maintain and manage them by themselves or entrust the power supply enterprise to do so.  
After user-dedicated power supply facilities are constructed and put into operation, they shall be maintained and managed by the user or entrusted to the power supply enterprise.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　因建设需要，必须对已建成的供电设施进行迁移、改造或者采取防护措施时，建设单位应当事先与该供电设施管理单位协商，所需工程费用由建设单位负担。

**Article 18** Where it is necessary to relocate, renovate or take protective measures for existing power supply facilities due to construction needs, the construction unit shall consult with the management unit of the power supply facilities in advance, and the required project costs shall be borne by the construction unit.

### 第四章　电力供应

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　用户受电端的供电质量应当符合国家标准或者电力行业标准。

**Article 19** The quality of power supply at the user's receiving end shall comply with national standards or power industry standards.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　供电方式应当按照安全、可靠、经济、合理和便于管理的原则，由电力供应与使用双方根据国家有关规定以及电网规划、用电需求和当地供电条件等因素协商确定。  
在公用供电设施未到达的地区，供电企业可以委托有供电能力的单位就近供电。非经供电企业委托，任何单位不得擅自向外供电。

**Article 20** The power supply mode shall be determined through consultation between the power supply and consumption parties based on the principles of safety, reliability, economy, rationality and ease of management, taking into account the relevant national provisions, grid planning, electricity demand and local power supply conditions.  
In areas where public power supply facilities are not available, the power supply enterprise may entrust a unit with power supply capacity to supply power nearby. Without entrustment by the power supply enterprise, no unit may supply power to others without authorization.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　因抢险救灾需要紧急供电时，供电企业必须尽速安排供电。所需工程费用和应付电费由有关地方人民政府有关部门从抢险救灾经费中支出，但是抗旱用电应当由用户交付电费。

**Article 21** Where emergency power supply is needed for rescue and disaster relief, the power supply enterprise must arrange power supply as quickly as possible. The required project costs and electricity charges payable shall be disbursed by the relevant departments of the local people's government from the rescue and disaster relief funds; however, electricity used for drought relief shall be paid by the user.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　用户对供电质量有特殊要求的，供电企业应当根据其必要性和电网的可能，提供相应的电力。

**Article 22** Where a user has special requirements for power supply quality, the power supply enterprise shall, based on the necessity and the capability of the power grid, provide corresponding electric power.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　申请新装用电、临时用电、增加用电容量、变更用电和终止用电，均应当到当地供电企业办理手续，并按照国家有关规定交付费用；供电企业没有不予供电的合理理由的，应当供电。供电企业应当在其营业场所公告用电的程序、制度和收费标准。

**Article 23** Applications for new electricity installation, temporary electricity use, increase of electricity capacity, change of electricity use, and termination of electricity use shall all go through procedures at the local power supply enterprise and pay fees according to the relevant provisions of the State; if there is no reasonable ground for not supplying power, the power supply enterprise shall supply power. The power supply enterprise shall publicly announce the procedures, standards and fees for electricity use at its business premises.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　供电企业应当按照国家标准或者电力行业标准参与用户受送电装置设计图纸的审核，对用户受送电装置隐蔽工程的施工过程实施监督，并在该受送电装置工程竣工后进行检验；检验合格的，方可投入使用。

**Article 24** The power supply enterprise shall, in accordance with national standards or power industry standards, participate in the review of design drawings for the user's power receiving and transmitting devices, supervise the construction process of concealed works of the user's power receiving and transmitting devices, and carry out inspection after the completion of the project; only after passing the inspection may they be put into use.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　供电企业应当按照国家有关规定实行分类电价、分时电价。

**Article 25** Power supply enterprises shall implement classified electricity prices and time-of-use electricity prices in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　用户应当安装用电计量装置。用户使用的电力、电量，以计量检定机构依法认可的用电计量装置的记录为准。用电计量装置，应当安装在供电设施与受电设施的产权分界处。  
安装在用户处的用电计量装置，由用户负责保护。

**Article 26** Users shall install electricity metering devices. The electric power and electricity consumed by a user shall be based on the records of the electricity metering devices verified by metrology verification institutions according to law. Electricity metering devices shall be installed at the boundary of property rights between power supply facilities and power receiving facilities.  
Electricity metering devices installed at the user's premises shall be protected by the user.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　供电企业应当按照国家核准的电价和用电计量装置的记录，向用户计收电费。  
用户应当按照国家批准的电价，并按照规定的期限、方式或者合同约定的办法，交付电费。

**Article 27** Power supply enterprises shall calculate and collect electricity charges from users in accordance with the electricity prices approved by the State and the records of the electricity metering devices.  
Users shall pay electricity charges in accordance with the electricity prices approved by the State and within the prescribed time limits, methods, or the methods agreed upon in the contract.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　除本条例另有规定外，在发电、供电系统正常运行的情况下，供电企业应当连续向用户供电；因故需要停止供电时，应当按照下列要求事先通知用户或者进行公告：  
(一)因供电设施计划检修需要停电时，供电企业应当提前7天通知用户或者进行公告；  
(二)因供电设施临时检修需要停止供电时，供电企业应当提前24小时通知重要用户；  
(三)因发电、供电系统发生故障需要停电、限电时，供电企业应当按照事先确定的限电序位进行停电或者限电。引起停电或者限电的原因消除后，供电企业应当尽快恢复供电。

**Article 28** Unless otherwise provided in these Regulations, under normal operation of the power generation and supply systems, the power supply enterprise shall supply power continuously to users; if it is necessary to cut off power supply for cause, it shall notify users in advance or make a public announcement in accordance with the following requirements:  
(1) Where power cut is needed for planned maintenance of power supply facilities, the power supply enterprise shall notify users 7 days in advance or make a public announcement;  
(2) Where power cut is needed for temporary maintenance of power supply facilities, the power supply enterprise shall notify important users 24 hours in advance;  
(3) Where power cut or load shedding is needed due to failure of the power generation or supply systems, the power supply enterprise shall perform power cut or load shedding according to the predetermined order. After the cause of the power cut or load shedding is eliminated, the power supply enterprise shall restore power supply as soon as possible.

### 第五章　电力使用

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上人民政府电力管理部门应当遵照国家产业政策，按照统筹兼顾、保证重点、择优供应的原则，做好计划用电工作。  
供电企业和用户应当制订节约用电计划，推广和采用节约用电的新技术、新材料、新工艺、新设备，降低电能消耗。  
供电企业和用户应当采用先进技术、采取科学管理措施，安全供电、用电，避免发生事故，维护公共安全。

**Article 29** The power administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall follow the national industrial policies and do a good job in planned electricity use based on the principles of overall consideration, ensuring key points, and supplying power to the best.  
Power supply enterprises and users shall formulate plans for economical electricity use, promote and adopt new technologies, new materials, new processes and new equipment for economical electricity use to reduce electricity consumption.  
Power supply enterprises and users shall adopt advanced technologies and scientific management measures to ensure safe power supply and consumption, avoid accidents and maintain public safety.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　用户不得有下列危害供电、用电安全，扰乱正常供电、用电秩序的行为：  
(一)擅自改变用电类别；  
(二)擅自超过合同约定的容量用电；  
(三)擅自超过计划分配的用电指标的；  
(四)擅自使用已经在供电企业办理暂停使用手续的电力设备，或者擅自启用已经被供电企业查封的电力设备；  
(五)擅自迁移、更动或者擅自操作供电企业的用电计量装置、电力负荷控制装置、供电设施以及约定由供电企业调度的用户受电设备；  
(六)未经供电企业许可，擅自引入、供出电源或者将自备电源擅自并网。

**Article 30** Users shall not engage in any of the following acts that endanger the safety of power supply and consumption or disrupt the normal order of power supply and consumption:  
(1) Changing the type of electricity use without authorization;  
(2) Exceeding the contracted capacity for electricity use without authorization;  
(3) Exceeding the planned allocation of electricity consumption targets without authorization;  
(4) Using power equipment that has been placed on suspended use with the power supply enterprise, or activating power equipment that has been sealed by the power supply enterprise without authorization;  
(5) Relocating, altering or operating the power supply enterprise's electricity metering devices, power load control devices, power supply facilities, or user's power receiving equipment that has been agreed to be dispatched by the power supply enterprise without authorization;  
(6) Introducing or supplying power from external sources or connecting self-owned power sources to the grid without the permission of the power supply enterprise.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　禁止窃电行为。窃电行为包括：  
(一)在供电企业的供电设施上，擅自接线用电；  
(二)绕越供电企业的用电计量装置用电；  
(三)伪造或者开启法定的或者授权的计量检定机构加封的用电计量装置封印用电；  
(四)故意损坏供电企业用电计量装置；  
(五)故意使供电企业的用电计量装置计量不准或者失效；  
(六)采用其他方法窃电。

**Article 31** Electricity theft is prohibited. Acts of electricity theft include:  
(1) Connecting to power supply facilities of the power supply enterprise without authorization to use electricity;  
(2) Bypassing the electricity metering devices of the power supply enterprise to use electricity;  
(3) Forging or opening the seals of electricity metering devices affixed by statutory or authorized metrology verification institutions to use electricity;  
(4) Intentionally damaging the electricity metering devices of the power supply enterprise;  
(5) Intentionally causing the electricity metering devices of the power supply enterprise to measure inaccurately or become invalid;  
(6) Using other methods to steal electricity.

### 第六章　供用电合同

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　供电企业和用户应当在供电前根据用户需要和供电企业的供电能力签订供用电合同。

**Article 32** Power supply enterprises and users shall, before power supply, conclude a power supply and consumption contract based on the user's needs and the supply capacity of the power supply enterprise.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　供用电合同应当具备以下条款：  
(一)供电方式、供电质量和供电时间；  
(二)用电容量和用电地址、用电性质；  
(三)计量方式和电价、电费结算方式；  
(四)供用电设施维护责任的划分；  
(五)合同的有效期限；  
(六)违约责任；  
(七)双方共同认为应当约定的其他条款。

**Article 33** A power supply and consumption contract shall contain the following clauses:  
(1) Power supply mode, power supply quality and power supply time;  
(2) Electricity capacity, electricity consumption address and nature of electricity use;  
(3) Metering method, electricity price and settlement method for electricity charges;  
(4) Division of maintenance responsibilities for power supply and consumption facilities;  
(5) Term of validity of the contract;  
(6) Liability for breach of contract;  
(7) Other clauses that both parties deem necessary to stipulate.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　供电企业应当按照合同约定的数量、质量、时间、方式，合理调度和安全供电。  
用户应当按照合同约定的数量、条件用电，交付电费和国家规定的其他费用。

**Article 34** The power supply enterprise shall dispatch reasonably and supply power safely in accordance with the quantity, quality, time and method agreed in the contract.  
The user shall use electricity in accordance with the quantity and conditions agreed in the contract, and pay the electricity charges and other fees prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　供用电合同的变更或者解除，应当依照有关法律、行政法规和本条例的规定办理。

**Article 35** The modification or rescission of a power supply and consumption contract shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws, administrative regulations and these Regulations.

### 第七章　监督与管理

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　电力管理部门应当加强对供电、用电的监督和管理。供电、用电监督检查工作人员必须具备相应的条件。供电、用电监督检查工作人员执行公务时，应当出示证件。  
供电、用电监督检查管理的具体办法，由国务院电力管理部门另行制定。

**Article 36** The power administrative departments shall strengthen the supervision and management of power supply and consumption. The personnel engaged in supervision and inspection of power supply and consumption shall meet the necessary qualifications. When performing official duties, such personnel shall present their credentials.  
The specific measures for the supervision, inspection and management of power supply and consumption shall be separately formulated by the power administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　承装、承修、承试供电设施和受电设施的单位，必须经电力管理部门审核合格，取得电力管理部门颁发的《承装(修)电力设施许可证》。

**Article 37** Units undertaking installation, repair, and testing of power supply facilities and power receiving facilities must pass the examination of the power administrative department and obtain a 'Power Facility Installation (Repair) Permit' issued by the power administrative department.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由电力管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得，可以并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款：  
(一)未按照规定取得《电力业务许可证》，从事电力供应业务的；  
(二)擅自伸入或者跨越供电营业区供电的；  
(三)擅自向外转供电的。

**Article 38** Where any of the following acts is committed in violation of these Regulations, the power administrative department shall order rectification, confiscate illegal gains, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains:  
(1) Engaging in power supply business without obtaining a 'Electric Power Business License' as required;  
(2) Extending or crossing power supply business areas to supply power without authorization;  
(3) Transferring power to others without authorization.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反[本条例第二十七条](#art-27)规定，逾期未交付电费的，供电企业可以从逾期之日起，每日按照电费总额的1‰至3‰加收违约金，具体比例由供用电双方在供用电合同中约定；自逾期之日起计算超过30日，经催交仍未交付电费的，供电企业可以按照国家规定的程序停止供电。

**Article 39** Where, in violation of Article 27 of these Regulations, a user fails to pay electricity charges within the prescribed time limit, the power supply enterprise may, from the date of default, charge liquidated damages at a daily rate of 1‰ to 3‰ of the total electricity charges, the specific rate shall be agreed upon by both parties in the power supply and consumption contract; if the user fails to pay the electricity charges after 30 days from the default date and after being urged, the power supply enterprise may cut off power supply in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反[本条例第三十条](#art-30)规定，违章用电的，供电企业可以根据违章事实和造成的后果追缴电费，并按照国务院电力管理部门的规定加收电费和国家规定的其他费用；情节严重的，可以按照国家规定的程序停止供电。

**Article 40** Where, in violation of Article 30 of these Regulations, a user commits illegal electricity use, the power supply enterprise may recover the electricity charges based on the facts and consequences of the violation, and additionally charge electricity fees and other fees prescribed by the State in accordance with the provisions of the power administrative department under the State Council; if the circumstances are serious, it may cut off power supply in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反[本条例第三十一条](#art-31)规定，盗窃电能的，由电力管理部门责令停止违法行为，追缴电费并处应交电费5倍以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 41** Where, in violation of Article 31 of these Regulations, electric energy is stolen, the power administrative department shall order cessation of the illegal act, recover the electricity charges and impose a fine of not more than five times the electricity charges payable; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　供电企业或者用户违反供用电合同，给对方造成损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 42** Where a power supply enterprise or a user breaches the power supply and consumption contract and causes losses to the other party, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　因电力运行事故给用户或者第三人造成损害的，供电企业应当依法承担赔偿责任。  
因用户或者第三人的过错给供电企业或者其他用户造成损害的，该用户或者第三人应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 43** Where damage is caused to a user or a third party due to a power operation accident, the power supply enterprise shall bear compensation liability according to law.  
Where damage is caused to a power supply enterprise or other users due to the fault of a user or a third party, such user or third party shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　供电企业职工违反规章制度造成供电事故的，或者滥用职权、利用职务之便谋取私利的，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 44** Where an employee of a power supply enterprise causes a power supply accident due to violation of rules and regulations, or abuses authority or uses his position to seek private gains, he shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例自1996年9月1日起施行。

**Article 45** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 1996.
