# 电力设施保护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08

> (1987年9月15日国务院发布　根据1998年1月7日《国务院关于修改〈电力设施保护条例〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为保障电力生产和建设的顺利进行，维护公共安全，特制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于中华人民共和国境内已建或在建的电力设施(包括发电设施、变电设施和电力线路设施及其有关辅助设施，下同)。 第三条　电力设施的保护，实行电力管理部门、公安部门、电力企业和人民群众相结合的原则。 第四条　电力设施受国家法律保护，禁止任何单位或个人从事危害电力设施的行为。任何单位和个人都有保护电力设施的义务，对危害电力设施的行为，有权制止并向电力管理部门、公安部门报告。 电力企业应加强对电力设施的保护工作，对危害电力设施安全的行为，应采取适当措施，予以制止。 第五条　国务院电力管理部门对电力设施的保护负责监督、检查、指导和协调。 第六条　县以上地方各级电力管理部门保护电力设施的职责是： (一)监督、检查本条例及根据本条例制定的规章的贯彻执行； (二)开展保护电力设施的宣传教育工作； (三)会同有关部门及沿电力线路各单位，建立群众护线组织并健全责任制； (四)会同当地公安部门，负责所辖地区电力设施的安全保卫工作。 第七条　各级公安部门负责依法查处破坏电力设施或哄抢、盗窃电力设施器材的案件。 第二章　电力设施的保护范围和保护区

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保障电力生产和建设的顺利进行，维护公共安全，特制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of ensuring the smooth progress of electric power production and construction, and safeguarding public security.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于中华人民共和国境内已建或在建的电力设施(包括发电设施、变电设施和电力线路设施及其有关辅助设施，下同)。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to existing or under-construction power facilities (including power generation facilities, power transformation facilities, power line facilities and their related auxiliary facilities, the same below) within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　电力设施的保护，实行电力管理部门、公安部门、电力企业和人民群众相结合的原则。

**Article 3** The protection of power facilities shall be carried out under the principle of combining the efforts of the electric power administration departments, public security departments, power enterprises, and the general public.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　电力设施受国家法律保护，禁止任何单位或个人从事危害电力设施的行为。任何单位和个人都有保护电力设施的义务，对危害电力设施的行为，有权制止并向电力管理部门、公安部门报告。  
电力企业应加强对电力设施的保护工作，对危害电力设施安全的行为，应采取适当措施，予以制止。

**Article 4** Power facilities shall be protected by national laws, and no unit or individual shall engage in any act that endangers power facilities. Every unit and individual shall have the obligation to protect power facilities, and shall have the right to stop any act endangering power facilities and report it to the electric power administration department or the public security department.  
Power enterprises shall strengthen the protection of power facilities and shall take appropriate measures to stop any act endangering the safety of power facilities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院电力管理部门对电力设施的保护负责监督、检查、指导和协调。

**Article 5** The electric power administration department of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision, inspection, guidance, and coordination of the protection of power facilities.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县以上地方各级电力管理部门保护电力设施的职责是：  
(一)监督、检查本条例及根据本条例制定的规章的贯彻执行；  
(二)开展保护电力设施的宣传教育工作；  
(三)会同有关部门及沿电力线路各单位，建立群众护线组织并健全责任制；  
(四)会同当地公安部门，负责所辖地区电力设施的安全保卫工作。

**Article 6** The duties of local electric power administration departments at or above the county level in protecting power facilities are:  
(1) to supervise and inspect the implementation of these Regulations and of the rules formulated in accordance with these Regulations;  
(2) to carry out publicity and education on the protection of power facilities;  
(3) to work together with relevant departments and units along power lines to establish mass line-protection organizations and improve the responsibility system;  
(4) to work together with the local public security departments to be responsible for the security and protection of power facilities within their respective jurisdictions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级公安部门负责依法查处破坏电力设施或哄抢、盗窃电力设施器材的案件。

**Article 7** Public security departments at all levels shall be responsible for investigating and dealing with, according to law, cases of damaging power facilities or making off with or stealing power facility equipment and materials.

### 第二章　电力设施的保护范围和保护区

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　发电设施、变电设施的保护范围：  
(一)发电厂、变电站、换流站、开关站等厂、站内的设施；  
(二)发电厂、变电站外各种专用的管道(沟)、储灰场、水井、泵站、冷却水塔、油库、堤坝、铁路、道路、桥梁、码头、燃料装卸设施、避雷装置、消防设施及其有关辅助设施；  
(三)水力发电厂使用的水库、大坝、取水口、引水隧洞(含支洞口)、引水渠道、调压井(塔)、露天高压管道、厂房、尾水渠、厂房与大坝间的通信设施及其有关辅助设施。

**Article 8** The scope of protection for power generation facilities and power transformation facilities:  
(1) facilities inside power plants, substations, converter stations, switchyards and similar plants or stations;  
(2) various dedicated pipelines (trenches), ash storage yards, wells, pumping stations, cooling towers, oil depots, dikes, railways, roads, bridges, docks, fuel loading and unloading facilities, lightning arresters, fire-fighting facilities and their related auxiliary facilities outside power plants or substations;  
(3) reservoirs, dams, intakes, diversion tunnels (including adit entrances), diversion channels, surge chambers (towers), exposed high-pressure pipelines, power houses, tailraces, communication facilities between the power house and the dam, and their related auxiliary facilities used by hydropower plants.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　电力线路设施的保护范围：  
(一)架空电力线路：杆塔、基础、拉线、接地装置、导线、避雷线、金具、绝缘子、登杆塔的爬梯和脚钉，导线跨越航道的保护设施，巡(保)线站，巡视检修专用道路、船舶和桥梁，标志牌及其有关辅助设施；  
(二)电力电缆线路：架空、地下、水底电力电缆和电缆联结装置，电缆管道、电缆隧道、电缆沟、电缆桥，电缆井、盖板、入孔、标石、水线标志牌及其有关辅助设施；  
(三)电力线路上的变压器、电容器、电抗器、断路器、隔离开关、避雷器、互感器、熔断器、计量仪表装置、配电室、箱式变电站及其有关辅助设施；  
(四)电力调度设施：电力调度场所、电力调度通信设施、电网调度自动化设施、电网运行控制设施。

**Article 9** The scope of protection for power line facilities:  
(1) overhead power lines: towers, foundations, guy wires, grounding devices, conductors, ground wires, fittings, insulators, climbing ladders and step bolts of towers, protection facilities for conductors crossing waterways, patrol (maintenance) stations, dedicated patrol and maintenance roads, vessels and bridges, signboards and their related auxiliary facilities;  
(2) power cable lines: overhead, underground, and submarine power cables and cable connecting devices, cable ducts, cable tunnels, cable trenches, cable bridges, cable manholes, covers, access holes, marker stones, submarine cable markers and their related auxiliary facilities;  
(3) transformers, capacitors, reactors, circuit breakers, disconnectors, surge arresters, instrument transformers, fuses, metering devices, distribution rooms, box-type substations and their related auxiliary facilities on power lines;  
(4) power dispatching facilities: power dispatching premises, power dispatching communication facilities, power grid dispatching automation facilities, and power grid operation control facilities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　电力线路保护区：  
(一)架空电力线路保护区：导线边线向外侧水平延伸并垂直于地面所形成的两平行面内的区域，在一般地区各级电压导线的边线延伸距离如下：  
1—10千伏       5米  
35—110千伏     10米  
154—330千伏    15米  
500千伏         20米  
在厂矿、城镇等人口密集地区，架空电力线路保护区的区域可略小于上述规定。但各级电压导线边线延伸的距离，不应小于导线边线在最大计算弧垂及最大计算风偏后的水平距离和风偏后距建筑物的安全距离之和。  
(二)电力电缆线路保护区：地下电缆为电缆线路地面标桩两侧各0.75米所形成的两平行线内的区域；海底电缆一般为线路两侧各2海里(港内为两侧各100米)，江河电缆一般不小于线路两侧各100米(中、小河流一般不小于各50米)所形成的两平行线内的水域。

**Article 10** Protected areas for power lines:  
(1) Protected area for overhead power lines: the area within two parallel planes formed by extending the side edges of conductors horizontally outward and perpendicular to the ground. In general areas, the distances for the extension of side edges of conductors at various voltage levels are as follows:  
1-10 kV 5 meters  
35-110 kV 10 meters  
154-330 kV 15 meters  
500 kV 20 meters  
In densely populated areas such as industrial and mining areas and towns, the protected area for overhead power lines may be slightly smaller than the above specifications. However, the distance of extension of the side edges of conductors at each voltage level shall not be less than the sum of the horizontal distance after the maximum calculated sag and the maximum calculated wind deflection of the conductor side edges and the safe distance from buildings after wind deflection.  
(2) Protected area for power cable lines: for underground cables, the area within two parallel lines formed by 0.75 meters on each side of the ground markers along the cable line; for submarine cables, generally the waters within two parallel lines formed by 2 nautical miles on each side of the line (100 meters on each side within harbors); for river cables, generally the waters within two parallel lines formed by not less than 100 meters on each side of the line (generally not less than 50 meters on each side for medium and small rivers).

### 第三章　电力设施的保护

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县以上地方各级电力管理部门应采取以下措施，保护电力设施：  
(一)在必要的架空电力线路保护区的区界上，应设立标志，并标明保护区的宽度和保护规定；  
(二)在架空电力线路导线跨越重要公路和航道的区段，应设立标志，并标明导线距穿越物体之间的安全距离；  
(三)地下电缆铺设后，应设立永久性标志，并将地下电缆所在位置书面通知有关部门；  
(四)水底电缆敷设后，应设立永久性标志，并将水底电缆所在位置书面通知有关部门。

**Article 11** The local power administrative departments at or above the county level shall take the following measures to protect power facilities:  
(1) At the boundaries of necessary overhead power line protection zones, signs shall be erected indicating the width of the protection zone and the protection regulations;  
(2) At sections where overhead power line conductors cross important highways and waterways, signs shall be erected indicating the safe distance between the conductors and the objects crossed;  
(3) After underground cables are laid, permanent signs shall be erected, and the location of the underground cables shall be notified to the relevant departments in writing;  
(4) After submarine cables are laid, permanent signs shall be erected, and the location of the submarine cables shall be notified to the relevant departments in writing.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　任何单位或个人在电力设施周围进行爆破作业，必须按照国家有关规定，确保电力设施的安全。

**Article 12** Any unit or individual that conducts blasting operations around power facilities must ensure the safety of the power facilities in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　任何单位或个人不得从事下列危害发电设施、变电设施的行为：  
(一)闯入发电厂、变电站内扰乱生产和工作秩序，移动、损害标志物；  
(二)危及输水、输油、供热、排灰等管道(沟)的安全运行；  
(三)影响专用铁路、公路、桥梁、码头的使用；  
(四)在用于水力发电的水库内，进入距水工建筑物300米区域内炸鱼、捕鱼、游泳、划船及其他可能危及水工建筑物安全的行为；  
(五)其他危害发电、变电设施的行为。

**Article 13** No unit or individual shall engage in any of the following acts that endanger power generation or transformation facilities:  
(1) Entering a power plant or substation to disrupt production or work order, or moving or damaging markers;  
(2) Endangering the safe operation of pipelines (channels) for water transmission, oil transmission, heat supply, ash discharge, etc.;  
(3) Affecting the use of specialized railways, highways, bridges, or docks;  
(4) Within reservoirs used for hydroelectric power generation, entering areas within 300 meters of hydraulic structures to blast fish, catch fish, swim, row boats, or engage in other acts that may endanger the safety of hydraulic structures;  
(5) Other acts that endanger power generation or transformation facilities.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　任何单位或个人，不得从事下列危害电力线路设施的行为：  
(一)向电力线路设施射击；  
(二)向导线抛掷物体；  
(三)在架空电力线路导线两侧各300米的区域内放风筝；  
(四)擅自在导线上接用电器设备；  
(五)擅自攀登杆塔或在杆塔上架设电力线、通信线、广播线，安装广播喇叭；  
(六)利用杆塔、拉线作起重牵引地锚；  
(七)在杆塔、拉线上拴牲畜、悬挂物体、攀附农作物；  
(八)在杆塔、拉线基础的规定范围内取土、打桩、钻探、开挖或倾倒酸、碱、盐及其他有害化学物品；  
(九)在杆塔内(不含杆塔与杆塔之间)或杆塔与拉线之间修筑道路；  
(十)拆卸杆塔或拉线上的器材，移动、损坏永久性标志或标志牌；  
(十一)其他危害电力线路设施的行为。

**Article 14** No unit or individual shall engage in any of the following acts that endanger power line facilities:  
(1) Shooting at power line facilities;  
(2) Throwing objects at conductors;  
(3) Flying kites within an area of 300 meters on each side of overhead power line conductors;  
(4) Without authorization, connecting electrical equipment to conductors;  
(5) Without authorization, climbing towers or poles, or installing power lines, communication lines, broadcast lines, or broadcast speakers on towers or poles;  
(6) Using towers or poles or their guy wires as anchor points for lifting or traction;  
(7) Tethering livestock, hanging objects, or attaching crops to towers or poles or their guy wires;  
(8) Excavating soil, driving piles, drilling, digging, or dumping acids, alkalis, salts, or other harmful chemicals within the prescribed scope of the foundations of towers or poles or their guy wires;  
(9) Building roads within the space between towers or poles (excluding the area between towers or poles) or between towers or poles and their guy wires;  
(10) Dismantling equipment on towers or poles or their guy wires, or moving or damaging permanent signs or signboards;  
(11) Other acts that endanger power line facilities.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　任何单位或个人在架空电力线路保护区内，必须遵守下列规定：  
(一)不得堆放谷物、草料、垃圾、矿渣、易燃物、易爆物及其他影响安全供电的物品；  
(二)不得烧窑、烧荒；  
(三)不得兴建建筑物、构筑物；  
(四)不得种植可能危及电力设施安全的植物。

**Article 15** Any unit or individual within an overhead power line protection zone must comply with the following provisions:  
(1) No stacking of grains, fodder, garbage, slag, flammable materials, explosive materials, or other items that affect safe power supply;  
(2) No kiln firing or slash burning;  
(3) No construction of buildings or structures;  
(4) No planting of plants that may endanger the safety of power facilities.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　任何单位或个人在电力电缆线路保护区内，必须遵守下列规定：  
(一)不得在地下电缆保护区内堆放垃圾、矿渣、易燃物、易爆物，倾倒酸、碱、盐及其他有害化学物品，兴建建筑物、构筑物或种植树木、竹子；  
(二)不得在海底电缆保护区内抛锚、拖锚；  
(三)不得在江河电缆保护区内抛锚、拖锚、炸鱼、挖沙。

**Article 16** Any unit or individual within a power cable protection zone must comply with the following provisions:  
(1) No stacking of garbage, slag, flammable materials, or explosive materials, no dumping of acids, alkalis, salts, or other harmful chemicals, no construction of buildings or structures, and no planting of trees or bamboo within the underground cable protection zone;  
(2) No anchoring or dragging of anchors within the submarine cable protection zone;  
(3) No anchoring, dragging of anchors, blast fishing, or sand excavation within the river cable protection zone.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　任何单位或个人必须经县级以上地方电力管理部门批准，并采取安全措施后，方可进行下列作业或活动：  
(一)在架空电力线路保护区内进行农田水利基本建设工程及打桩、钻探、开挖等作业；  
(二)起重机械的任何部位进入架空电力线路保护区进行施工；  
(三)小于导线距穿越物体之间的安全距离，通过架空电力线路保护区；  
(四)在电力电缆线路保护区内进行作业。

**Article 17** Any unit or individual may carry out the following operations or activities only after obtaining approval from the local power administrative department at or above the county level and taking safety measures:  
(1) Carrying out farmland water conservancy infrastructure construction projects and operations such as pile driving, drilling, and excavation within the overhead power line protection zone;  
(2) Any part of a crane entering the overhead power line protection zone for construction;  
(3) Passing through the overhead power line protection zone at a distance less than the safe distance between the conductor and the objects crossed;  
(4) Carrying out operations within the power cable protection zone.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　任何单位或个人不得从事下列危害电力设施建设的行为：  
(一)非法侵占电力设施建设项目依法征收的土地；  
(二)涂改、移动、损害、拔除电力设施建设的测量标桩和标记；  
(三)破坏、封堵施工道路，截断施工水源或电源。

**Article 18** No unit or individual shall engage in any of the following acts that endanger the construction of power facilities:  
(1) Illegally encroaching upon land lawfully requisitioned for power facility construction projects;  
(2) Altering, moving, damaging, or removing survey stakes and markers for power facility construction;  
(3) Damaging or blocking construction roads, or cutting off construction water or power supply.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　未经有关部门依照国家有关规定批准，任何单位和个人不得收购电力设施器材。

**Article 19** No unit or individual may purchase materials and equipment of power facilities without approval from the relevant department in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第四章　对电力设施与其他设施互相妨碍的处理

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　电力设施的建设和保护应尽量避免或减少给国家、集体和个人造成的损失。

**Article 20** The construction and protection of power facilities shall avoid or minimize losses caused to the State, collectives, and individuals.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　新建架空电力线路不得跨越储存易燃、易爆物品仓库的区域；一般不得跨越房屋，特殊情况需要跨越房屋时，电力建设企业应采取安全措施，并与有关单位达成协议。

**Article 21** New overhead power lines shall not cross areas where warehouses storing inflammable and explosive goods are located; they shall generally not cross houses. Where special circumstances require crossing houses, the electric power construction enterprise shall take safety measures and reach an agreement with the relevant units.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　公用工程、城市绿化和其他工程在新建、改建或扩建中妨碍电力设施时，或电力设施在新建、改建或扩建中妨碍公用工程、城市绿化和其他工程时，双方有关单位必须按照本条例和国家有关规定协商，就迁移、采取必要的防护措施和补偿等问题达成协议后方可施工。

**Article 22** Where public works, urban greening, and other projects, during new construction, reconstruction or expansion, interfere with electric power facilities, or where electric power facilities, during new construction, reconstruction or expansion, interfere with public works, urban greening, and other projects, the relevant units of both parties shall negotiate in accordance with these Regulations and the relevant provisions of the State, and construction may commence only after an agreement is reached on relocation, necessary protective measures, compensation, and other matters.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　电力管理部门应将经批准的电力设施新建、改建或扩建的规划和计划通知城乡建设规划主管部门，并划定保护区域。  
城乡建设规划主管部门应将电力设施的新建、改建或扩建的规划和计划纳入城乡建设规划。

**Article 23** The electric power administrative department shall notify the competent department of urban and rural construction planning of the approved plans and programs for new construction, reconstruction or expansion of electric power facilities, and shall demarcate protected areas.  
The competent department of urban and rural construction planning shall incorporate the plans and programs for new construction, reconstruction or expansion of electric power facilities into the urban and rural construction plan.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　新建、改建或扩建电力设施，需要损害农作物，砍伐树木、竹子，或拆迁建筑物及其他设施的，电力建设企业应按照国家有关规定给予一次性补偿。  
在依法划定的电力设施保护区内种植的或自然生长的可能危及电力设施安全的树木、竹子，电力企业应依法予以修剪或砍伐。

**Article 24** Where new construction, reconstruction or expansion of electric power facilities requires damaging crops, felling trees or bamboos, or demolishing buildings or other structures, the electric power construction enterprise shall provide one-time compensation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Trees or bamboos planted or naturally grown within the legally demarcated protected areas of electric power facilities that may endanger the safety of the electric power facilities shall be trimmed or felled according to law by the electric power enterprise.

### 第五章　奖励与惩罚

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　任何单位或个人有下列行为之一，电力管理部门应给予表彰或一次性物质奖励：  
(一)对破坏电力设施或哄抢、盗窃电力设施器材的行为检举、揭发有功；  
(二)对破坏电力设施或哄抢、盗窃电力设施器材的行为进行斗争，有效地防止事故发生；  
(三)为保护电力设施而同自然灾害作斗争，成绩突出；  
(四)为维护电力设施安全，做出显著成绩。

**Article 25** Any unit or individual that commits one of the following acts shall be commended or given a one-time material reward by the electric power administrative department:  
(1) Having rendered meritorious service in reporting or exposing acts of destroying electric power facilities or seizing or stealing electric power facility equipment;  
(2) Having fought against acts of destroying electric power facilities or seizing or stealing electric power facility equipment, effectively preventing accidents;  
(3) Having fought against natural disasters to protect electric power facilities with outstanding achievements;  
(4) Having made notable contributions to maintaining the safety of electric power facilities.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反本条例规定，未经批准或未采取安全措施，在电力设施周围或在依法划定的电力设施保护区内进行爆破或其他作业，危及电力设施安全的，由电力管理部门责令停止作业、恢复原状并赔偿损失。

**Article 26** Where, in violation of these Regulations, blasting or other operations are carried out without approval or without taking safety measures around electric power facilities or within the legally demarcated protected areas of electric power facilities, endangering the safety of the electric power facilities, the electric power administrative department shall order the cessation of the operations, restoration to the original state, and compensation for losses.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反本条例规定，危害发电设施、变电设施和电力线路设施的，由电力管理部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 27** Where, in violation of these Regulations, generating facilities, transformer facilities, or power line facilities are endangered, the electric power administrative department shall order rectification; if the party refuses to rectify, a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　违反本条例规定，在依法划定的电力设施保护区内进行烧窑、烧荒、抛锚、拖锚、炸鱼、挖沙作业，危及电力设施安全的，由电力管理部门责令停止作业、恢复原状并赔偿损失。

**Article 28** Where, in violation of these Regulations, operations such as kiln firing, burning wasteland, dropping anchor, dragging anchor, fish bombing, or sand excavation are carried out within the legally demarcated protected areas of electric power facilities, endangering the safety of the electric power facilities, the electric power administrative department shall order the cessation of the operations, restoration to the original state, and compensation for losses.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　违反本条例规定，危害电力设施建设的，由电力管理部门责令改正、恢复原状并赔偿损失。

**Article 29** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the construction of electric power facilities is endangered, the electric power administrative department shall order rectification, restoration to the original state, and compensation for losses.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　凡违反本条例规定而构成违反治安管理行为的单位或个人，由公安部门根据《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》予以处罚；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 30** Any unit or individual that violates these Regulations and thereby constitutes a violation of public security administration shall be punished by the public security department in accordance with the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law by the judicial organ.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国务院电力管理部门可以会同国务院有关部门制定本条例的实施细则。

**Article 31** The power regulatory department of the State Council may, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate detailed rules for the implementation of these Regulations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 32** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
