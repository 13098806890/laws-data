# 中华人民共和国对外合作开采陆上石油资源条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2013-07-18  
**生效日期**：2013-07-18

> (1993年10月7日中华人民共和国国务院令第131号发布　根据2001年9月23日《国务院关于修改〈中华人民共和国对外合作开采陆上石油资源条例〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2007年9月18日《国务院关于修改〈中华人民共和国对外合作开采陆上石油资源条例〉的决定》第二次修订　根据2011年9月30日《国务院关于修改〈中华人民共和国对外合作开采陆上石油资源条例〉的决定》第三次修订　根据2013年7月18日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第四次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为保障石油工业的发展，促进国际经济合作和技术交流，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事中外合作开采陆上石油资源活动，必须遵守本条例。 第三条　中华人民共和国境内的石油资源属于中华人民共和国国家所有。 第四条　中国政府依法保护参加合作开采陆上石油资源的外国企业的合作开采活动及其投资、利润和其他合法权益。 在中华人民共和国境内从事中外合作开采陆上石油资源活动，必须遵守中华人民共和国的有关法律、法规和规章，并接受中国政府有关机关的监督管理。 第五条　国家对参加合作开采陆上石油资源的外国企业的投资和收益不实行征收。在特殊情况下，根据社会公共利益的需要，可以对外国企业在合作开采中应得石油的一部分或者全部，依照法律程序实行征收，并给予相应的补偿。 第六条　国务院指定的部门负责在国务院批准的合作区域内，划分合作区块，确定合作方式，组织制定有关规划和政策，审批对外合作油(气)田总体开发方案。 第七条　中国石油天然气集团公司、中国石油化工集团公司(以下简称中方石油公司)负责对外合作开采陆上石油资源的经营业务；负责与外国企业谈判、签订、执行合作开采陆上石油资源的合同；在国务院批准的对外合作开采陆上石油资源的区域内享有与外国企业合作进行石油勘探、开发、生产的专营权。 第八条　中方石油公司在国务院批准的对外合作开采陆上石油资源的区域内，按划分的合作区块，通过招标或者谈判，确定合作开采陆上石油资源的外国企业，签订合作开采石油合同或者其他合作合同，并向中华人民共和国商务部报送合同有关情况。 第九条　对外合作区块公布后，除中方石油公司与外国企业进行合作开采陆上石油资源活动外，其他企业不得进入该区块内进行石油勘查活动，也不得与外国企业签订在该区块内进行石油开采的经济技术合作协议。 对外合作区块公布前，已进入该区块进行石油勘查(尚处于区域评价勘查阶段)的企业，在中方石油公司与外国企业签订合同后，应当撤出。该企业所取得的勘查资料，由中方石油公司负责销售，以适当补偿其投资。该区块发现有商业开采价值的油(气)田后，从该区块撤出的企业可以通过投资方式参与开发。 国务院指定的部门应当根据合同的签订和执行情况，定期对所确定的对外合作区块进行调整。 第十条　对外合作开采陆上石油资源，应当遵循兼顾中央与地方利益的原则，通过吸收油(气)田所在地的资金对有商业开采价值的油(气)田的开发进行投资等方式，适当照顾地方利益。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保障石油工业的发展，促进国际经济合作和技术交流，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to ensure the development of the petroleum industry and to promote international economic cooperation and technical exchanges.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事中外合作开采陆上石油资源活动，必须遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Any person engaged in activities of Chinese-foreign cooperative exploitation of onshore petroleum resources within the territory of the People's Republic of China must comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国境内的石油资源属于中华人民共和国国家所有。

**Article 3** The petroleum resources within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be owned by the State of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　中国政府依法保护参加合作开采陆上石油资源的外国企业的合作开采活动及其投资、利润和其他合法权益。  
在中华人民共和国境内从事中外合作开采陆上石油资源活动，必须遵守中华人民共和国的有关法律、法规和规章，并接受中国政府有关机关的监督管理。

**Article 4** The Chinese Government shall protect, according to law, the cooperative exploitation activities of foreign enterprises participating in cooperative exploitation of onshore petroleum resources, as well as their investments, profits and other lawful rights and interests.  
Any person engaged in activities of Chinese-foreign cooperative exploitation of onshore petroleum resources within the territory of the People's Republic of China must comply with the relevant laws, regulations and rules of the People's Republic of China, and shall accept the supervision and administration of the relevant authorities of the Chinese Government.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家对参加合作开采陆上石油资源的外国企业的投资和收益不实行征收。在特殊情况下，根据社会公共利益的需要，可以对外国企业在合作开采中应得石油的一部分或者全部，依照法律程序实行征收，并给予相应的补偿。

**Article 5** The State shall not expropriate the investments and earnings of foreign enterprises participating in cooperative exploitation of onshore petroleum resources. Under special circumstances, when required by the public interest, the State may expropriate part or all of the petroleum due to foreign enterprises in cooperative exploitation in accordance with legal procedures, and shall grant corresponding compensation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院指定的部门负责在国务院批准的合作区域内，划分合作区块，确定合作方式，组织制定有关规划和政策，审批对外合作油(气)田总体开发方案。

**Article 6** The department designated by the State Council shall be responsible for, within the cooperative areas approved by the State Council, delineating cooperative blocks, determining cooperative modes, organizing the formulation of relevant plans and policies, and examining and approving the overall development plans of foreign cooperative oil (gas) fields.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　中国石油天然气集团公司、中国石油化工集团公司(以下简称中方石油公司)负责对外合作开采陆上石油资源的经营业务；负责与外国企业谈判、签订、执行合作开采陆上石油资源的合同；在国务院批准的对外合作开采陆上石油资源的区域内享有与外国企业合作进行石油勘探、开发、生产的专营权。

**Article 7** China National Petroleum Corporation and China Petrochemical Corporation (hereinafter referred to as Chinese petroleum companies) shall be responsible for the business operations of foreign cooperative exploitation of onshore petroleum resources; shall be responsible for negotiating, signing and executing contracts for cooperative exploitation of onshore petroleum resources with foreign enterprises; and shall enjoy the exclusive right to conduct petroleum exploration, development and production in cooperation with foreign enterprises within the areas for foreign cooperative exploitation of onshore petroleum resources approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　中方石油公司在国务院批准的对外合作开采陆上石油资源的区域内，按划分的合作区块，通过招标或者谈判，确定合作开采陆上石油资源的外国企业，签订合作开采石油合同或者其他合作合同，并向中华人民共和国商务部报送合同有关情况。

**Article 8** Within the areas for foreign cooperative exploitation of onshore petroleum resources approved by the State Council, Chinese petroleum companies shall, in accordance with the divided cooperative blocks, determine foreign enterprises for cooperative exploitation of onshore petroleum resources through bidding or negotiation, sign contracts for cooperative exploitation of petroleum or other cooperative contracts, and submit relevant information on such contracts to the Ministry of Commerce of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对外合作区块公布后，除中方石油公司与外国企业进行合作开采陆上石油资源活动外，其他企业不得进入该区块内进行石油勘查活动，也不得与外国企业签订在该区块内进行石油开采的经济技术合作协议。  
对外合作区块公布前，已进入该区块进行石油勘查(尚处于区域评价勘查阶段)的企业，在中方石油公司与外国企业签订合同后，应当撤出。该企业所取得的勘查资料，由中方石油公司负责销售，以适当补偿其投资。该区块发现有商业开采价值的油(气)田后，从该区块撤出的企业可以通过投资方式参与开发。  
国务院指定的部门应当根据合同的签订和执行情况，定期对所确定的对外合作区块进行调整。

**Article 9** After the foreign cooperative blocks are announced, no enterprise other than the Chinese petroleum companies engaged in cooperative exploitation of onshore petroleum resources with foreign enterprises may enter such blocks for petroleum exploration activities, nor may it sign economic and technological cooperation agreements with foreign enterprises for petroleum exploitation within such blocks.  
Before the announcement of foreign cooperative blocks, enterprises that have already entered such blocks for petroleum exploration (still in the stage of regional evaluation exploration) shall withdraw after the Chinese petroleum companies sign contracts with foreign enterprises. The exploration data obtained by such enterprises shall be sold by the Chinese petroleum companies to appropriately compensate their investments. If oil (gas) fields with commercial exploitation value are discovered in such blocks, enterprises that have withdrawn from such blocks may participate in the development by means of investment.  
The department designated by the State Council shall, in light of the signing and execution status of contracts, periodically adjust the designated foreign cooperative blocks.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对外合作开采陆上石油资源，应当遵循兼顾中央与地方利益的原则，通过吸收油(气)田所在地的资金对有商业开采价值的油(气)田的开发进行投资等方式，适当照顾地方利益。  
有关地方人民政府应当依法保护合作区域内正常的生产经营活动，并在土地使用、道路通行、生活服务等方面给予有效协助。

**Article 10** Foreign cooperative exploitation of onshore petroleum resources shall follow the principle of balancing the interests of the central and local authorities, and shall give due consideration to local interests by means such as absorbing funds from the localities where the oil (gas) fields are located and investing in the development of oil (gas) fields with commercial exploitation value.  
The relevant local people's governments shall protect, according to law, the normal production and business operations within the cooperative areas, and shall provide effective assistance in land use, road access, and living services.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对外合作开采陆上石油资源，应当依法纳税。

**Article 11** Foreign cooperative exploitation of onshore petroleum resources shall pay taxes according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　为执行合同所进口的设备和材料，按照国家有关规定给予减税、免税或者给予税收方面的其他优惠。具体办法由财政部会同海关总署制定。

**Article 12** The equipment and materials imported for the execution of contracts shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, be granted tax reduction, exemption or other tax preferences. The specific measures shall be formulated by the Ministry of Finance jointly with the General Administration of Customs.

### 第二章　外国合同者的权利和义务

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　中方石油公司与外国企业合作开采陆上石油资源必须订立合同，除法律、法规另有规定或者合同另有约定外，应当由签订合同的外国企业(以下简称外国合同者)单独投资进行勘探，负责勘探作业，并承担勘探风险；发现有商业开采价值的油(气)田后，由外国合同者与中方石油公司共同投资合作开发；外国合同者并应承担开发作业和生产作业，直至中方石油公司按照合同约定接替生产作业为止。

**Article 13** Chinese petroleum companies and foreign enterprises must conclude contracts for cooperative exploitation of onshore petroleum resources. Unless otherwise provided by laws or regulations or otherwise agreed in the contracts, the foreign enterprise that signs the contract (hereinafter referred to as the foreign contractor) shall make independent investment in exploration, be responsible for exploration operations, and bear the exploration risks; after discovering oil (gas) fields with commercial exploitation value, the foreign contractor and the Chinese petroleum company shall jointly invest in cooperative development; the foreign contractor shall also undertake development operations and production operations until the Chinese petroleum company takes over the production operations in accordance with the contract.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　外国合同者可以按照合同约定，从生产的石油中回收其投资和费用，并取得报酬。

**Article 14** The foreign contractor may, in accordance with the contract, recover its investments and costs from the petroleum produced and obtain remuneration.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　外国合同者根据国家有关规定和合同约定，可以将其应得的石油和购买的石油运往国外，也可以依法将其回收的投资、利润和其他合法收益汇往国外。  
外国合同者在中华人民共和国境内销售其应得的石油，一般由中方石油公司收购，也可以采取合同双方约定的其他方式销售，但是不得违反国家有关在中华人民共和国境内销售石油产品的规定。

**Article 15** The foreign contractor may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the contract, ship abroad the petroleum to which it is entitled and the petroleum it has purchased, and may also remit abroad its recovered investments, profits and other lawful earnings according to law.  
When the foreign contractor sells the petroleum to which it is entitled within the territory of the People's Republic of China, such petroleum shall generally be purchased by the Chinese petroleum company, or may be sold in other ways agreed upon by both parties to the contract; however, the provisions of the State concerning the sale of petroleum products within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall not be violated.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　外国合同者开立外汇账户和办理其他外汇事宜，应当遵守《中华人民共和国外汇管理条例》和国家有关外汇管理的其他规定。  
外国合同者的投资，应当采用美元或者其他可自由兑换货币。

**Article 16** The foreign contractor, when opening foreign exchange accounts and handling other foreign exchange matters, shall comply with the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Foreign Exchange Control and other provisions of the State concerning foreign exchange control.  
The investment of the foreign contractor shall be in US dollars or other freely convertible currencies.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　外国合同者应当依法在中华人民共和国境内设立分公司、子公司或者代表机构。  
前款机构的设立地点由外国合同者与中方石油公司协商确定。

**Article 17** The foreign contractor shall establish a branch, subsidiary or representative office within the territory of the People's Republic of China according to law.  
The location of the establishment of the institution mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be determined through negotiation between the foreign contractor and the Chinese petroleum company.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　外国合同者在执行合同的过程中，应当及时地、准确地向中方石油公司报告石油作业情况，完整地、准确地取得各项石油作业的数据、记录、样品、凭证和其他原始资料，并按规定向中方石油公司提交资料和样品以及技术、经济、财会、行政方面的各种报告。

**Article 18** During the execution of the contract, the foreign contractor shall timely and accurately report the petroleum operations to the Chinese petroleum company, shall completely and accurately obtain all data, records, samples, vouchers and other original materials of petroleum operations, and shall submit to the Chinese petroleum company the data and samples as well as various reports on technical, economic, financial and administrative aspects in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　外国合同者执行合同，除租用第三方的设备外，按照计划和预算所购置和建造的全部资产，在其投资按照合同约定得到补偿或者该油(气)田生产期期满后，所有权属于中方石油公司。在合同期内，外国合同者可以按照合同约定使用这些资产。

**Article 19** All assets purchased and constructed by the foreign contractor in executing the contract, except for equipment leased from a third party, shall, in accordance with plans and budgets, become owned by the Chinese petroleum company after the investment of the foreign contractor is compensated in accordance with the contract or upon expiration of the production period of the oil (gas) field. During the term of the contract, the foreign contractor may use such assets in accordance with the contract.

### 第三章　石油作业

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　作业者必须根据国家有关开采石油资源的规定，制订油(气)田总体开发方案，并经国务院指定的部门批准后，实施开发作业和生产作业。

**Article 20** The operator must, in accordance with the provisions of the State concerning the exploitation of petroleum resources, formulate an overall development plan for the oil (gas) field, and after the approval of the department designated by the State Council, carry out development operations and production operations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　石油合同可以约定石油作业所需的人员，作业者可以优先录用中国公民。

**Article 21** A petroleum contract may stipulate the personnel required for petroleum operations, and the operator may give priority to employing Chinese citizens.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　作业者和承包者在实施石油作业中，应当遵守国家有关环境保护和安全作业方面的法律、法规和标准，并按照国际惯例进行作业，保护农田、水产、森林资源和其他自然资源，防止对大气、海洋、河流、湖泊、地下水和陆地其他环境的污染和损害。

**Article 22** In carrying out petroleum operations, the operator and the contractor shall comply with the state laws, regulations and standards relating to environmental protection and safe operations, and shall conduct operations in accordance with international practices, protect farmland, aquatic, forest resources and other natural resources, and prevent pollution and damage to the atmosphere, oceans, rivers, lakes, groundwater and other terrestrial environments.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　在实施石油作业中使用土地的，应当依照《中华人民共和国土地管理法》和国家其他有关规定办理。

**Article 23** Where land is used in carrying out petroleum operations, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　[本条例第十八条](#art-18)规定的各项石油作业的数据、记录、样品、凭证和其他原始资料，所有权属于中方石油公司。  
前款所列数据、记录、样品、凭证和其他原始资料的使用、转让、赠与、交换、出售、发表以及运出、传送到中华人民共和国境外，必须按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 24** The ownership of the various petroleum operation data, records, samples, vouchers and other original materials specified in Article 18 of these Regulations shall belong to the Chinese petroleum company.  
The use, transfer, donation, exchange, sale, publication, as well as the transportation or transmission out of or to outside the territory of the People's Republic of China of the data, records, samples, vouchers and other original materials listed in the preceding paragraph must be carried out in accordance with relevant state provisions.

### 第四章　争议的解决

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　合作开采陆上石油资源合同的当事人因执行合同发生争议时，应当通过协商或者调解解决；不愿协商、调解，或者协商、调解不成的，可以根据合同中的仲裁条款或者事后达成的书面仲裁协议，提交中国仲裁机构或者其他仲裁机构仲裁。  
当事人未在合同中订立仲裁条款，事后又没有达成书面仲裁协议的，可以向中国人民法院起诉。

**Article 25** Where a dispute arises between the parties to a contract for cooperative exploitation of onshore petroleum resources in the execution of the contract, the dispute shall be settled through consultation or mediation; if the parties are unwilling to consult or mediate, or if consultation or mediation fails, the dispute may be submitted for arbitration to a Chinese arbitration institution or another arbitration institution in accordance with the arbitration clause in the contract or a written arbitration agreement reached afterwards.  
Where the parties have neither included an arbitration clause in the contract nor reached a written arbitration agreement afterwards, they may institute prosecution in a Chinese People's Court.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由国务院指定的部门依据职权责令限期改正，给予警告；在限期内不改正的，可以责令其停止实施石油作业；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
(一)违反[本条例第九条](#art-9)第一款规定，擅自进入对外合作区块进行石油勘查活动或者与外国企业签订在对外合作区块内进行石油开采合作协议的；  
(二)违反[本条例第十八条](#art-18)规定，在执行合同的过程中，未向中方石油公司及时、准确地报告石油作业情况的，未按规定向中方石油公司提交资料和样品以及技术、经济、财会、行政方面的各种报告的；  
(三)违反[本条例第二十条](#art-20)规定，油(气)田总体开发方案未经批准，擅自实施开发作业和生产作业的；  
(四)违反[本条例第二十四条](#art-24)第二款规定，擅自使用石油作业的数据、记录、样品、凭证和其他原始资料或者将其转让、赠与、交换、出售、发表以及运出、传送到中华人民共和国境外的。

**Article 26** Anyone who violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the department designated by the State Council, according to its functions, to make corrections within a specified period and shall be given a warning; if no correction is made within the specified period, the violator may be ordered to cease petroleum operations; if the violation constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) entering, without authorization, a foreign cooperation block to conduct petroleum exploration activities, or signing an agreement with a foreign enterprise on petroleum exploitation cooperation within a foreign cooperation block, in violation of the provisions of Article 9, paragraph 1 of these Regulations;  
(2) failing to report petroleum operation situations to the Chinese petroleum company in a timely and accurate manner, or failing to submit data, samples and various reports on technical, economic, financial and administrative aspects to the Chinese petroleum company as required, during the execution of the contract, in violation of the provisions of Article 18 of these Regulations;  
(3) implementing, without authorization, development operations and production operations before the overall development plan for the oil (gas) field is approved, in violation of the provisions of Article 20 of these Regulations;  
(4) using, without authorization, the petroleum operation data, records, samples, vouchers and other original materials, or transferring, donating, exchanging, selling, publishing, transporting or transmitting them out of or to outside the territory of the People's Republic of China, in violation of the provisions of Article 24, paragraph 2 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反[本条例第十一条](#art-11)、第十六条、第二十二条、第二十三条规定的，由国家有关主管部门依照有关法律、法规的规定予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 27** Anyone who violates the provisions of Articles 11, 16, 22 and 23 of these Regulations shall be punished by the relevant competent department of the state in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations; if the violation constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
(一)“石油”，是指蕴藏在地下的、正在采出的和已经采出的原油和天然气。  
(二)“陆上石油资源”，是指蕴藏在陆地全境(包括海滩、岛屿及向外延伸至5米水深处的海域)的范围内的地下石油资源。  
(三)“开采”，是指石油的勘探、开发、生产和销售及其有关的活动。  
(四)“石油作业”，是指为执行合同而进行的勘探、开发和生产作业及其有关的活动。  
(五)“勘探作业”，是指用地质、地球物理、地球化学和包括钻探井等各种方法寻找储藏石油圈闭所做的全部工作，以及在已发现石油的圈闭上为确定它有无商业价值所做的钻评价井、可行性研究和编制油(气)田的总体开发方案等全部工作。  
(六)“开发作业”，是指自油(气)田总体开发方案被批准之日起，为实现石油生产所进行的设计、建造、安装、钻井工程等及其相应的研究工作，包括商业性生产开始之前的生产活动。  
(七)“生产作业”，是指一个油(气)田从开始商业性生产之日起，为生产石油所进行的全部作业以及与其有关的活动。

**Article 28** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings hereby assigned to them:  
(1) "Petroleum" means the crude oil and natural gas that are stored underground, being extracted, or have been extracted.  
(2) "Onshore petroleum resources" means the underground petroleum resources stored within the entire territory of the land (including beaches, islands and the sea area extending outward to a water depth of 5 meters).  
(3) "Exploitation" means the exploration, development, production and sale of petroleum and the activities related thereto.  
(4) "Petroleum operations" means the exploration, development and production operations carried out for the execution of a contract and the activities related thereto.  
(5) "Exploration operations" means all work done to search for petroleum-bearing traps using geological, geophysical, geochemical methods and various methods including drilling exploratory wells, and all work done on traps where petroleum has been discovered to determine their commercial value, including drilling evaluation wells, feasibility studies and preparation of the overall development plan for the oil (gas) field.  
(6) "Development operations" means the design, construction, installation, drilling engineering and corresponding research work carried out to achieve petroleum production from the date of approval of the overall development plan for the oil (gas) field, including production activities before the commencement of commercial production.  
(7) "Production operations" means all operations carried out to produce petroleum from the date of commencement of commercial production of an oil (gas) field and the activities related thereto.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　[本条例第四条](#art-4)、第十一条、第十二条、第十五条、第十六条、第十七条、第二十一条的规定，适用于外国承包者。

**Article 29** The provisions of Articles 4, 11, 12, 15, 16, 17 and 21 of these Regulations shall apply to foreign contractors.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对外合作开采煤层气资源由中联煤层气有限责任公司、国务院指定的其他公司实施专营，并参照本条例执行。

**Article 30** The cooperative exploitation of coalbed methane resources with foreign parties shall be operated exclusively by China United Coalbed Methane Co., Ltd. and other companies designated by the State Council, and shall be implemented by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 31** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
