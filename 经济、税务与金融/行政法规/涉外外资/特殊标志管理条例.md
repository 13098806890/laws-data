# 特殊标志管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1996-07-13  
**生效日期**：1996-07-13

> (1996年7月13日中华人民共和国国务院令第202号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对特殊标志的管理，推动文化、体育、科学研究及其他社会公益活动的发展，保护特殊标志所有人、使用人和消费者的合法权益，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称特殊标志，是指经国务院批准举办的全国性和国际性的文化、体育、科学研究及其他社会公益活动所使用的，由文字、图形组成的名称及缩写、会徽、吉祥物等标志。 第三条　经国务院工商行政管理部门核准登记的特殊标志，受本条例保护。 第四条　含有下列内容的文字、图形组成的特殊标志，不予登记： (一)有损于国家或者国际组织的尊严或者形象的； (二)有害于社会善良习俗和公共秩序的； (三)带有民族歧视性，不利于民族团结的； (四)缺乏显著性，不便于识别的； (五)法律、行政法规禁止的其他内容。 第五条　特殊标志所有人使用或者许可他人使用特殊标志所募集的资金，必须用于特殊标志所服务的社会公益事业，并接受国务院财政部门、审计部门的监督。 第二章　特殊标志的登记 第六条　举办社会公益活动的组织者或者筹备者对其使用的名称、会徽、吉祥物等特殊标志，需要保护的，应当向国务院工商行政管理部门提出登记申请。 登记申请可以直接办理，也可以委托他人代理。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对特殊标志的管理，推动文化、体育、科学研究及其他社会公益活动的发展，保护特殊标志所有人、使用人和消费者的合法权益，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the administration of special signs, promoting the development of cultural, sports, scientific research and other social welfare activities, and protecting the legitimate rights and interests of owners, users and consumers of special signs.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称特殊标志，是指经国务院批准举办的全国性和国际性的文化、体育、科学研究及其他社会公益活动所使用的，由文字、图形组成的名称及缩写、会徽、吉祥物等标志。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "special signs" refer to signs such as names and abbreviations, emblems, mascots, etc., composed of words and graphics, which are used in national and international cultural, sports, scientific research and other social welfare activities approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　经国务院工商行政管理部门核准登记的特殊标志，受本条例保护。

**Article 3** Special signs that have been approved and registered by the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council shall be protected under these Regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　含有下列内容的文字、图形组成的特殊标志，不予登记：  
(一)有损于国家或者国际组织的尊严或者形象的；  
(二)有害于社会善良习俗和公共秩序的；  
(三)带有民族歧视性，不利于民族团结的；  
(四)缺乏显著性，不便于识别的；  
(五)法律、行政法规禁止的其他内容。

**Article 4** No special sign composed of words or graphics containing any of the following contents shall be registered:  
(1) those harming the dignity or image of the State or an international organization;  
(2) those harming good social customs and public order;  
(3) those with ethnic discrimination or detrimental to national unity;  
(4) those lacking distinctiveness or being difficult to identify;  
(5) other contents prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　特殊标志所有人使用或者许可他人使用特殊标志所募集的资金，必须用于特殊标志所服务的社会公益事业，并接受国务院财政部门、审计部门的监督。

**Article 5** The funds raised by the owner of a special sign through the use or licensing of the special sign to others must be used for the social welfare undertakings to which the special sign is dedicated, and shall be subject to the supervision of the finance department and the audit department under the State Council.

### 第二章　特殊标志的登记

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　举办社会公益活动的组织者或者筹备者对其使用的名称、会徽、吉祥物等特殊标志，需要保护的，应当向国务院工商行政管理部门提出登记申请。  
登记申请可以直接办理，也可以委托他人代理。

**Article 6** Where the organizer or preparatory body of a social welfare activity needs protection for the special sign, such as name, emblem or mascot, used by it, it shall file an application for registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council.  
The application for registration may be made directly or through an agent.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请特殊标志登记，应当填写特殊标志登记申请书并提交下列文件：  
(一)国务院批准举办该社会公益活动的文件；  
(二)准许他人使用特殊标志的条件及管理办法；  
(三)特殊标志图样5份，黑白墨稿1份。图样应当清晰，便于粘贴，用光洁耐用的纸张印制或者用照片代替，长和宽不大于10厘米、不小于5厘米；  
(四)委托他人代理的，应当附代理人委托书，注明委托事项和权限；  
(五)国务院工商行政管理部门认为应当提交的其他文件。

**Article 7** To apply for registration of a special sign, a Special Sign Registration Application Form shall be filled in and the following documents shall be submitted:  
(1) the document issued by the State Council approving the holding of such social welfare activity;  
(2) the conditions and management measures for permitting others to use the special sign;  
(3) five copies of the design of the special sign and one copy of a black-and-white ink drawing. The design shall be clear and easy to affix, printed on smooth and durable paper or replaced by photographs, with a length and width not more than 10 cm and not less than 5 cm;  
(4) where an agent is entrusted, a power of attorney specifying the entrusted matters and scope of authority shall be attached;  
(5) other documents that the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council deems necessary to submit.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院工商行政管理部门收到申请后，按照以下规定处理：  
(一)符合本条例有关规定，申请文件齐备无误的，自收到申请之日起15日内，发给特殊标志登记申请受理通知书，并在发出通知之日起2个月内，将特殊标志有关事项、图样和核准使用的商品和服务项目，在特殊标志登记簿上登记，发给特殊标志登记证书。  
特殊标志经核准登记后，由国务院工商行政管理部门公告。  
(二)申请文件不齐备或者有误的，自收到申请之日起10日内发给特殊标志登记申请补正通知书，并限其自收到通知之日起15日内予以补正；期满不补正或者补正仍不符合规定的，发给特殊标志登记申请不予受理通知书。  
(三)违反[本条例第四条](#art-4)规定的，自收到申请之日起15日内发给特殊标志登记申请驳回通知书。申请人对驳回通知不服的，可以自收到驳回通知之日起15日内，向国务院工商行政管理部门申请复议。  
前款所列各类通知书，由国务院工商行政管理部门送达申请人或者其代理人。因故不能直接送交的，以国务院工商行政管理部门公告或者邮寄之日起的20日为送达日期。

**Article 8** Upon receipt of an application, the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council shall handle it according to the following provisions:  
(1) If the application complies with the relevant provisions of these Regulations and the application documents are complete and correct, it shall, within 15 days from the date of receiving the application, issue a Notice of Acceptance of Special Sign Registration Application, and within 2 months from the date of issuing such notice, register the relevant matters and designs of the special sign as well as the approved goods and services in the Special Sign Register, and issue a Special Sign Registration Certificate.  
Upon approval and registration of the special sign, the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council shall publish an announcement.  
(2) If the application documents are incomplete or contain errors, it shall, within 10 days from the date of receiving the application, issue a Notice of Correction of Special Sign Registration Application, and set a time limit of 15 days from the date of receipt of such notice for the applicant to make corrections; if the applicant fails to make corrections within the time limit or the corrections still do not comply with the requirements, it shall issue a Notice of Non-Acceptance of Special Sign Registration Application.  
(3) If the application violates the provisions of Article 4 of these Regulations, it shall, within 15 days from the date of receiving the application, issue a Notice of Rejection of Special Sign Registration Application. If the applicant disagrees with the rejection notice, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the rejection notice, apply to the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council for reconsideration.  
The various notices listed in the preceding paragraph shall be served by the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council on the applicant or its agent. If direct service is not possible for any reason, the date of service shall be the 20th day after the announcement or mailing by the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　特殊标志有效期为4年，自核准登记之日起计算。  
特殊标志所有人可以在有效期满前3个月内提出延期申请，延长的期限由国务院工商行政管理部门根据实际情况和需要决定。  
特殊标志所有人变更地址，应当自变更之日起1个月内报国务院工商行政管理部门备案。

**Article 9** The term of validity of a special sign shall be four years, calculated from the date of approval and registration.  
The owner of a special sign may file an application for renewal within three months before the expiry of the term of validity. The extended period shall be determined by the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council in light of the actual circumstances and needs.  
If the owner of a special sign changes its address, it shall report to the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council for record within one month from the date of such change.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　已获准登记的特殊标志有下列情形之一的，任何单位和个人可以在特殊标志公告刊登之日至其有效期满的期间，向国务院工商行政管理部门申明理由并提供相应证据，请求宣告特殊标志登记无效：  
(一)同已在先申请的特殊标志相同或者近似的；  
(二)同已在先申请注册的商标或者已获得注册的商标相同或者近似的；  
(三)同已在先申请外观设计专利或者已依法取得专利权的外观设计专利相同或者近似的；  
(四)侵犯他人著作权的。

**Article 10** Where a registered special sign falls under any of the following circumstances, any entity or individual may, during the period from the date of publication of the special sign announcement to the expiry of its term of validity, state reasons and provide corresponding evidence to the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council, requesting a declaration of invalidity of the special sign registration:  
(1) it is identical or similar to a special sign for which an application has previously been filed;  
(2) it is identical or similar to a trademark for which an application has previously been filed or which has been registered;  
(3) it is identical or similar to a design patent for which an application has previously been filed or which has been lawfully granted;  
(4) it infringes another person's copyright.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院工商行政管理部门自收到特殊标志登记无效申请之日起10日内，通知被申请人并限其自收到通知之日起15日内作出答辩。  
被申请人拒绝答辩或者无正当理由超过答辩期限的，视为放弃答辩的权利。

**Article 11** The administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council shall, within 10 days from the date of receiving the application for invalidity of special sign registration, notify the respondent and set a time limit of 15 days from the date of receipt of such notice for the respondent to file a defense.  
If the respondent refuses to file a defense or fails to file a defense within the time limit without justifiable reasons, it shall be deemed to have waived its right to defense.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院工商行政管理部门自收到特殊标志登记无效申请之日起3个月内作出裁定，并通知当事人；当事人对裁定不服的，可以自收到通知之日起15日内，向国务院工商行政管理部门申请复议。

**Article 12** The administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council shall, within three months from the date of receiving the application for invalidity of special sign registration, make a ruling and notify the parties; if a party disagrees with the ruling, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notification, apply to the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council for reconsideration.

### 第三章　特殊标志的使用与保护

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　特殊标志所有人可以在与其公益活动相关的广告、纪念品及其他物品上使用该标志，并许可他人在国务院工商行政管理部门核准使用该标志的商品或者服务项目上使用。

**Article 13** The owner of a special sign may use the sign on advertisements, souvenirs and other articles related to its public welfare activities, and may license others to use the sign on the goods or service items approved by the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council for using such sign.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　特殊标志的使用人应当是依法成立的企业、事业单位、社会团体、个体工商户。  
特殊标志使用人应当同所有人签订书面使用合同。  
特殊标志使用人应当自合同签订之日起1个月内，将合同副本报国务院工商行政管理部门备案，并报使用人所在地县级以上人民政府工商行政管理部门存查。

**Article 14** The user of a special sign shall be an enterprise, institution, social organization or individual industrial or commercial household established according to law.  
The user of a special sign shall enter into a written use contract with the owner.  
The user of a special sign shall, within one month from the date of signing the contract, submit a duplicate of the contract to the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council for record, and also to the administrative department for industry and commerce of the local people's government at or above the county level where the user is located for reference.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　特殊标志所有人或者使用人有下列行为之一的，由其所在地或者行为发生地县级以上人民政府工商行政管理部门责令改正，可以处5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由县级以上人民政府工商行政管理部门责令使用人停止使用该特殊标志，由国务院工商行政管理部门撤销所有人的特殊标志登记：  
(一)擅自改变特殊标志文字、图形的；  
(二)许可他人使用特殊标志，未签订使用合同，或者使用人在规定期限内未报国务院工商行政管理部门备案或者未报所在地县级以上人民政府工商行政管理机关存查的；  
(三)超出核准登记的商品或者服务范围使用的。

**Article 15** Where the owner or user of a special sign commits any of the following acts, the administrative department for industry and commerce of the local people's government at or above the county level where the party is located or where the act occurs shall order correction, and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the administrative department for industry and commerce of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the user to stop using the special sign, and the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council shall cancel the registration of the special sign of the owner:  
(1) altering the words or graphics of the special sign without authorization;  
(2) without entering into a use contract when licensing others to use the special sign, or the user fails to report to the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council for record or to the administrative department for industry and commerce of the local people's government at or above the county level for reference within the prescribed time limit;  
(3) using the special sign beyond the approved scope of goods or services.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府工商行政管理部门责令侵权人立即停止侵权行为，没收侵权商品，没收违法所得，并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处1万元以下的罚款：  
(一)擅自使用与所有人的特殊标志相同或者近似的文字、图形或者其组合的；  
(二)未经特殊标志所有人许可，擅自制造、销售其特殊标志或者将其特殊标志用于商业活动的；  
(三)有给特殊标志所有人造成经济损失的其他行为的。

**Article 16** Where any of the following acts is committed, the administrative department for industry and commerce of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the infringer to immediately cease the infringing act, confiscate the infringing goods, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) using, without authorization, words or graphics identical or similar to those of a special sign owned by another person, or a combination thereof;  
(2) manufacturing or selling another person's special sign, or using such special sign in commercial activities, without the permission of the owner of the special sign;  
(3) other acts causing economic losses to the owner of the special sign.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　特殊标志所有人或者使用人发现特殊标志所有权或者使用权被侵害时，可以向侵权人所在地或者侵权行为发生地县级以上人民政府工商行政管理部门投诉；也可以直接向人民法院起诉。  
工商行政管理部门受理特殊标志侵权案件投诉的，应当依特殊标志所有人的请求，就侵权的民事赔偿主持调解；调解不成的，特殊标志所有人可以向人民法院起诉。

**Article 17** Where the owner or user of a special sign finds that the ownership or right to use of the special sign is infringed, it may lodge a complaint with the administrative department for industry and commerce of the people's government at or above the county level in the place where the infringer is located or where the infringing act occurs; it may also directly file a lawsuit with the People's Court.  
Where the administrative department for industry and commerce accepts a complaint regarding a special sign infringement case, it shall, upon the request of the owner of the special sign, preside over mediation on the civil compensation for the infringement; if mediation fails, the owner of the special sign may file a lawsuit with the People's Court.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　工商行政管理部门受理特殊标志侵权案件，在调查取证时，可以行使下列职权，有关当事人应当予以协助，不得拒绝：  
(一)询问有关当事人；  
(二)检查与侵权活动有关的物品；  
(三)调查与侵权活动有关的行为；  
(四)查阅、复制与侵权活动有关的合同、账册等业务资料。

**Article 18** When the administrative department for industry and commerce accepts a special sign infringement case and conducts investigation and evidence collection, it may exercise the following powers, and the relevant parties shall provide assistance and may not refuse:  
(1) interrogating the relevant parties;  
(2) inspecting articles related to the infringing activities;  
(3) investigating acts related to the infringing activities;  
(4) consulting and copying contracts, account books and other business materials related to the infringing activities.

### 第四章　附则

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　特殊标志申请费、公告费、登记费的收费标准，由国务院财政部门、物价部门会同国务院工商行政管理部门制定。

**Article 19** The fee standards for application, announcement and registration of special signs shall be formulated by the finance department and the pricing department under the State Council jointly with the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　申请特殊标志登记有关文书格式由国务院工商行政管理部门制定。

**Article 20** The format of the relevant documents for applying for registration of special signs shall be prescribed by the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　经国务院批准代表中国参加国际性文化、体育、科学研究等活动的组织所使用的名称、徽记、吉祥物等标志的保护，参照本条例的规定施行。

**Article 21** The protection of signs, such as names, logos and mascots, used by organizations that, with the approval of the State Council, represent China in international cultural, sports, scientific research and other activities, shall be implemented with reference to the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 22** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
