# 中华人民共和国对外合作开采海洋石油资源条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2013-07-18  
**生效日期**：2013-07-18

> (1982年1月30日国务院发布　根据2001年9月23日《国务院关于修改〈中华人民共和国对外合作开采海洋石油资源条例〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　根据2011年9月30日《国务院关于修改〈中华人民共和国对外合作开采海洋石油资源条例〉的决定》第三次修订　根据2013年7月18日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第四次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为促进国民经济的发展，扩大国际经济技术合作，在维护国家主权和经济利益的前提下允许外国企业参与合作开采中华人民共和国海洋石油资源，特制定本条例。 第二条　中华人民共和国的内海、领海、大陆架以及其他属于中华人民共和国海洋资源管辖海域的石油资源，都属于中华人民共和国国家所有。 在前款海域内，为开采石油而设置的建筑物、构筑物、作业船舶，以及相应的陆岸油(气)集输终端和基地，都受中华人民共和国管辖。 第三条　中国政府依法保护参与合作开采海洋石油资源的外国企业的投资、应得利润和其他合法权益，依法保护外国企业的合作开采活动。 在本条例范围内，合作开采海洋石油资源的一切活动，都应当遵守中华人民共和国的法律、法令和国家的有关规定；参与实施石油作业的企业和个人，都应当受中国法律的约束，接受中国政府有关主管部门的检查、监督。 第四条　国家对参加合作开采海洋石油资源的外国企业的投资和收益不实行征收。在特殊情况下，根据社会公共利益的需要，可以对外国企业在合作开采中应得石油的一部分或者全部，依照法律程序实行征收，并给予相应的补偿。 第五条　国务院指定的部门依据国家确定的合作海区、面积，决定合作方式，划分合作区块；依据国家规定制定同外国企业合作开采海洋石油资源的规划；制定对外合作开采海洋石油资源的业务政策和审批海上油(气)田的总体开发方案。 第六条　中华人民共和国对外合作开采海洋石油资源的业务，由中国海洋石油总公司全面负责。 中国海洋石油总公司是具有法人资格的国家公司，享有在对外合作海区内进行石油勘探、开发、生产和销售的专营权。 中国海洋石油总公司根据工作需要，可以设立地区公司、专业公司、驻外代表机构，执行总公司交付的任务。 第七条　中国海洋石油总公司就对外合作开采石油的海区、面积、区块，通过组织招标，确定合作开采海洋石油资源的外国企业，签订合作开采石油合同或者其他合作合同，并向中华人民共和国商务部报送合同有关情况。 第二章　石油合同各方的权利和义务 第八条　中国海洋石油总公司通过订立石油合同同外国企业合作开采海洋石油资源，除法律、行政法规另有规定或者石油合同另有约定外，应当由石油合同中的外国企业一方(以下称外国合同者)投资进行勘探，负责勘探作业，并承担全部勘探风险；发现商业性油(气)田后，由外国合同者同中国海洋石油总公司双方投资合作开发，外国合同者并应负责开发作业和生产作业，直至中国海洋石油总公司按照石油合同规定在条件具备的情况下接替生产作业。外国合同者可以按照石油合同规定，从生产的石油中回收其投资和费用，并取得报酬。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为促进国民经济的发展，扩大国际经济技术合作，在维护国家主权和经济利益的前提下允许外国企业参与合作开采中华人民共和国海洋石油资源，特制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of promoting the development of the national economy, expanding international economic and technical cooperation, and permitting foreign enterprises to participate in the cooperative exploitation of offshore petroleum resources of the People's Republic of China on the premise of safeguarding national sovereignty and economic interests.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国的内海、领海、大陆架以及其他属于中华人民共和国海洋资源管辖海域的石油资源，都属于中华人民共和国国家所有。  
在前款海域内，为开采石油而设置的建筑物、构筑物、作业船舶，以及相应的陆岸油(气)集输终端和基地，都受中华人民共和国管辖。

**Article 2** The petroleum resources in the internal waters, territorial sea, continental shelf and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the marine resources of the People's Republic of China shall belong to the State of the People's Republic of China.  
Within the sea areas mentioned in the preceding paragraph, the buildings, structures, operating vessels, and corresponding onshore oil (gas) gathering and transmission terminals and bases established for the exploitation of petroleum shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中国政府依法保护参与合作开采海洋石油资源的外国企业的投资、应得利润和其他合法权益，依法保护外国企业的合作开采活动。  
在本条例范围内，合作开采海洋石油资源的一切活动，都应当遵守中华人民共和国的法律、法令和国家的有关规定；参与实施石油作业的企业和个人，都应当受中国法律的约束，接受中国政府有关主管部门的检查、监督。

**Article 3** The Chinese Government shall, according to law, protect the investments, due profits and other lawful rights and interests of foreign enterprises participating in the cooperative exploitation of offshore petroleum resources, and shall, according to law, protect the cooperative exploitation activities of foreign enterprises.  
Within the scope of these Regulations, all activities of cooperative exploitation of offshore petroleum resources shall comply with the laws, decrees and relevant state provisions of the People's Republic of China; enterprises and individuals participating in the implementation of petroleum operations shall be bound by Chinese laws and shall accept the inspection and supervision of the competent authorities of the Chinese Government.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对参加合作开采海洋石油资源的外国企业的投资和收益不实行征收。在特殊情况下，根据社会公共利益的需要，可以对外国企业在合作开采中应得石油的一部分或者全部，依照法律程序实行征收，并给予相应的补偿。

**Article 4** The State shall not nationalize the investments and profits of foreign enterprises participating in the cooperative exploitation of offshore petroleum resources. Under special circumstances, where required by the public interest, a part or all of the petroleum due to a foreign enterprise in cooperative exploitation may be expropriated through legal procedures, and appropriate compensation shall be given.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院指定的部门依据国家确定的合作海区、面积，决定合作方式，划分合作区块；依据国家规定制定同外国企业合作开采海洋石油资源的规划；制定对外合作开采海洋石油资源的业务政策和审批海上油(气)田的总体开发方案。

**Article 5** The department designated by the State Council shall, pursuant to the cooperative sea areas and areas determined by the State, decide on the modes of cooperation, and delineate cooperative blocks; formulate plans for the cooperative exploitation of offshore petroleum resources with foreign enterprises in accordance with state provisions; formulate business policies for foreign cooperative exploitation of offshore petroleum resources, and approve the overall development plans of offshore oil (gas) fields.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　中华人民共和国对外合作开采海洋石油资源的业务，由中国海洋石油总公司全面负责。  
中国海洋石油总公司是具有法人资格的国家公司，享有在对外合作海区内进行石油勘探、开发、生产和销售的专营权。  
中国海洋石油总公司根据工作需要，可以设立地区公司、专业公司、驻外代表机构，执行总公司交付的任务。

**Article 6** The business of foreign cooperative exploitation of offshore petroleum resources of the People's Republic of China shall be wholly undertaken by the China National Offshore Oil Corporation.  
China National Offshore Oil Corporation is a state company with the status of a legal person, and enjoys the exclusive right to conduct petroleum exploration, development, production and sales within the foreign cooperative sea areas.  
China National Offshore Oil Corporation may, in light of work requirements, establish regional companies, specialized companies and overseas representative offices to carry out tasks assigned by the head office.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　中国海洋石油总公司就对外合作开采石油的海区、面积、区块，通过组织招标，确定合作开采海洋石油资源的外国企业，签订合作开采石油合同或者其他合作合同，并向中华人民共和国商务部报送合同有关情况。

**Article 7** China National Offshore Oil Corporation shall, in respect of the sea areas, areas and blocks for foreign cooperative exploitation of petroleum, organize tenders to determine the foreign enterprises for cooperative exploitation of offshore petroleum resources, conclude cooperative exploitation petroleum contracts or other cooperative contracts, and submit to the Ministry of Commerce of the People's Republic of China the relevant information on the contracts.

### 第二章　石油合同各方的权利和义务

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　中国海洋石油总公司通过订立石油合同同外国企业合作开采海洋石油资源，除法律、行政法规另有规定或者石油合同另有约定外，应当由石油合同中的外国企业一方(以下称外国合同者)投资进行勘探，负责勘探作业，并承担全部勘探风险；发现商业性油(气)田后，由外国合同者同中国海洋石油总公司双方投资合作开发，外国合同者并应负责开发作业和生产作业，直至中国海洋石油总公司按照石油合同规定在条件具备的情况下接替生产作业。外国合同者可以按照石油合同规定，从生产的石油中回收其投资和费用，并取得报酬。

**Article 8** When China National Offshore Oil Corporation cooperates with foreign enterprises in the exploitation of offshore petroleum resources through concluding petroleum contracts, unless otherwise provided by laws, administrative regulations or otherwise agreed in the petroleum contract, the foreign enterprise party to the petroleum contract (hereinafter referred to as the foreign contractor) shall invest in exploration, be responsible for exploration operations, and bear all exploration risks; after a commercial oil (gas) field is discovered, both the foreign contractor and China National Offshore Oil Corporation shall invest in cooperative development, and the foreign contractor shall be responsible for development operations and production operations until China National Offshore Oil Corporation, under the conditions stipulated in the petroleum contract, takes over production operations when conditions are met. The foreign contractor may, in accordance with the provisions of the petroleum contract, recover its investment and expenses from the petroleum produced and obtain remuneration.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　外国合同者可以将其应得的石油和购买的石油运往国外，也可以依法将其回收的投资、利润和其他正当收益汇往国外。

**Article 9** Foreign contractors may transport abroad the petroleum due to them and the petroleum purchased by them, and may also, according to law, remit abroad their recovered investments, profits and other legitimate gains.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　参与合作开采海洋石油资源的中国企业、外国企业，都应当依法纳税。

**Article 10** All Chinese enterprises and foreign enterprises participating in the cooperative exploitation of offshore petroleum resources shall pay taxes according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　为执行石油合同所进口的设备和材料，按照国家规定给予减税、免税，或者给予税收方面的其他优惠。

**Article 11** Equipment and materials imported for the implementation of petroleum contracts shall be granted tax reduction, tax exemption or other tax preferences in accordance with state provisions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　外国合同者开立外汇账户和办理其他外汇事宜，应当遵守《中华人民共和国外汇管理条例》和国家有关外汇管理的其他规定。

**Article 12** When opening foreign exchange accounts and handling other foreign exchange matters, foreign contractors shall comply with the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Foreign Exchange Control and other relevant state provisions on foreign exchange administration.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　石油合同可以约定石油作业所需的人员，作业者可以优先录用中国公民。

**Article 13** A petroleum contract may stipulate the personnel required for petroleum operations, and the operator may give priority to employing Chinese citizens.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　外国合同者在执行石油合同从事开发、生产作业过程中，必须及时地、准确地向中国海洋石油总公司报告石油作业情况；完整地、准确地取得各项石油作业的数据、记录、样品、凭证和其他原始资料，并定期向中国海洋石油总公司提交必要的资料和样品以及技术、经济、财会、行政方面的各种报告。

**Article 14** In the process of carrying out development and production operations under a petroleum contract, a foreign contractor must report to China National Offshore Oil Corporation on the petroleum operations in a timely and accurate manner; obtain complete and accurate data, records, samples, vouchers and other original materials related to various petroleum operations, and periodically submit to China National Offshore Oil Corporation the necessary data and samples, as well as various technical, economic, financial, accounting and administrative reports.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　外国合同者为执行石油合同从事开发、生产作业，应当在中华人民共和国境内设立分支机构或者代表机构，并依法履行登记手续。  
前款机构的住所地应当同中国海洋石油总公司共同商量确定。

**Article 15** A foreign contractor carrying out development and production operations for the implementation of a petroleum contract shall establish a branch or representative office within the territory of the People's Republic of China and go through registration formalities according to law.  
The domicile of the institution mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be determined through consultation with China National Offshore Oil Corporation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　[本条例第三条](#art-3)、第九条、第十条、第十一条、第十五条的规定，对向石油作业提供服务的外国承包者，类推适用。

**Article 16** The provisions of Articles 3, 9, 10, 11 and 15 of these Regulations shall apply mutatis mutandis to foreign contractors providing services for petroleum operations.

### 第三章　石油作业

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　作业者必须根据本条例和国家有关开采石油资源的规定，参照国际惯例，制定油(气)田总体开发方案和实施生产作业，以达到尽可能高的石油采收率。

**Article 17** The operator must, in accordance with these Regulations and the state provisions on the exploitation of petroleum resources, and with reference to international practice, formulate the overall development plan of the oil (gas) field and carry out production operations so as to achieve the highest possible recovery factor.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　外国合同者为执行石油合同从事开发、生产作业，应当使用中华人民共和国境内现有的基地；如需设立新基地，必须位于中华人民共和国境内。  
前款新基地的具体地点，以及在特殊情况下需要采取的其他措施，都必须经中国海洋石油总公司书面同意。

**Article 18** Foreign contractors carrying out development and production operations for the implementation of a petroleum contract shall use existing bases within the territory of the People's Republic of China; if it is necessary to establish a new base, it must be located within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
The specific location of the new base mentioned in the preceding paragraph, as well as any other measures required under special circumstances, must be subject to the written consent of China National Offshore Oil Corporation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　中国海洋石油总公司有权派人参加外国作业者为执行石油合同而进行的总体设计和工程设计。

**Article 19** China National Offshore Oil Corporation shall have the right to send personnel to participate in the overall design and engineering design carried out by the foreign operator for the implementation of the petroleum contract.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　外国合同者为执行石油合同，除租用第三方的设备外，按计划和预算所购置和建造的全部资产，当外国合同者的投资按照规定得到补偿后，其所有权属于中国海洋石油总公司，在合同期内，外国合同者仍然可以依据合同的规定使用这些资产。

**Article 20** All assets purchased and constructed by a foreign contractor in accordance with plans and budgets for the implementation of a petroleum contract, except for equipment leased from a third party, shall, after the foreign contractor's investment has been compensated in accordance with the provisions, belong to China National Offshore Oil Corporation by title, and during the contract period, the foreign contractor may still use these assets in accordance with the provisions of the contract.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　为执行石油合同所取得的各项石油作业的数据、记录、样品、凭证和其他原始资料，其所有权属于中国海洋石油总公司。  
前款数据、记录、样品、凭证和其他原始资料的使用和转让、赠与、交换、出售、公开发表以及运出、传送出中华人民共和国，都必须按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 21** The ownership of all data, records, samples, vouchers and other original materials related to petroleum operations obtained for the implementation of a petroleum contract shall belong to China National Offshore Oil Corporation.  
The use, transfer, donation, exchange, sale, public disclosure, and transportation or transmission out of the People's Republic of China of the data, records, samples, vouchers and other original materials mentioned in the preceding paragraph must be carried out in accordance with relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　作业者和承包者在实施石油作业中，应当遵守中华人民共和国有关环境保护和安全方面的法律规定，并参照国际惯例进行作业，保护渔业资源和其他自然资源，防止对大气、海洋、河流、湖泊和陆地等环境的污染和损害。

**Article 22** In carrying out petroleum operations, the operator and contractors shall comply with the legal provisions of the People's Republic of China on environmental protection and safety, and conduct operations with reference to international practice, protect fishery resources and other natural resources, and prevent pollution and damage to the environment such as the atmosphere, oceans, rivers, lakes and land.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　石油合同区产出的石油，应当在中华人民共和国登陆，也可以在海上油(气)外输计量点运出。如需在中华人民共和国以外的地点登陆，必须经国务院指定的部门批准。

**Article 23** The petroleum produced from a petroleum contract area shall be landed in the People's Republic of China, or may be shipped out at offshore oil (gas) export metering points. If it is necessary to land at a location outside the People's Republic of China, approval must be obtained from the department designated by the State Council.

### 第四章　附则

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　在合作开采海洋石油资源活动中，外国企业和中国企业间发生的争执，应当通过友好协商解决。通过协商不能解决的，由中华人民共和国仲裁机构进行调解、仲裁，也可以由合同双方协议在其他仲裁机构仲裁。

**Article 24** Any dispute arising between a foreign enterprise and a Chinese enterprise in the activities of cooperative exploitation of offshore petroleum resources shall be settled through friendly consultations. If it cannot be settled through consultations, it shall be submitted to an arbitration institution of the People's Republic of China for mediation or arbitration, or may be submitted for arbitration to another arbitration institution agreed upon by both parties to the contract.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　作业者、承包者违反本条例规定实施石油作业的，由国务院指定的部门依据职权责令限期改正，给予警告；在限期内不改正的，可以责令其停止实施石油作业。由此造成的一切经济损失，由责任方承担。

**Article 25** If an operator or contractor carries out petroleum operations in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the department designated by the State Council shall, within its authority, order it to make corrections within a specified time limit and give it a warning; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it may be ordered to cease the petroleum operations. All economic losses caused thereby shall be borne by the responsible party.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例所用的术语，其定义如下：  
(一)“石油”是指蕴藏在地下的、正在采出的和已经采出的原油和天然气。  
(二)“开采”是泛指石油的勘探、开发、生产和销售及其有关的活动。  
(三)“石油合同”是指中国海洋石油总公司同外国企业为合作开采中华人民共和国海洋石油资源，依法订立的包括石油勘探、开发和生产的合同。  
(四)“合同区”是指在石油合同中为合作开采石油资源以地理坐标圈定的海域面积。  
(五)“石油作业”是指为执行石油合同而进行的勘探、开发和生产作业及其有关的活动。  
(六)“勘探作业”是指用地质、地球物理、地球化学和包括钻勘探井等各种方法寻找储藏石油的圈闭所做的全部工作，以及在已发现石油的圈闭上为确定它有无商业价值所做的钻评价井、可行性研究和编制油(气)田的总体开发方案等全部工作。  
(七)“开发作业”是指从国务院指定的部门批准油(气)田的总体开发方案之日起，为实现石油生产所进行的设计、建造、安装、钻井工程等及其相应的研究工作，并包括商业性生产开始之前的生产活动。  
(八)“生产作业”是指一个油(气)田从开始商业性生产之日起，为生产石油所进行的全部作业以及与其有关的活动，诸如采出、注入、增产、处理、贮运和提取等作业。  
(九)“外国合同者”是指同中国海洋石油总公司签订石油合同的外国企业。外国企业可以是公司，也可以是公司集团。  
(十)“作业者”是指按照石油合同的规定负责实施作业的实体。  
(十一)“承包者”是指向作业者提供服务的实体。

**Article 26** For the purpose of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them below:  
(1) "Petroleum" means crude oil and natural gas that are underground, being extracted or already extracted.  
(2) "Exploitation" generally refers to petroleum exploration, development, production and sales and related activities.  
(3) "Petroleum contract" means a contract concluded according to law between China National Offshore Oil Corporation and a foreign enterprise for the cooperative exploitation of offshore petroleum resources of the People's Republic of China, including petroleum exploration, development and production.  
(4) "Contract area" means the sea area delineated by geographical coordinates in the petroleum contract for the cooperative exploitation of petroleum resources.  
(5) "Petroleum operations" means exploration, development and production operations carried out for the implementation of a petroleum contract and related activities.  
(6) "Exploration operations" means all work performed by using methods such as geology, geophysics, geochemistry and drilling of exploration wells to find traps that store petroleum, and all work such as drilling evaluation wells, feasibility studies and preparation of overall development plans of oil (gas) fields on discovered petroleum traps to determine whether they have commercial value.  
(7) "Development operations" means the design, construction, installation, drilling engineering and corresponding research work carried out for achieving petroleum production, starting from the date of approval of the overall development plan of an oil (gas) field by the department designated by the State Council, and includes production activities before the commencement of commercial production.  
(8) "Production operations" means all operations and related activities carried out for producing petroleum, such as recovery, injection, stimulation, processing, storage, transportation and extraction, starting from the date of commencement of commercial production of an oil (gas) field.  
(9) "Foreign contractor" means a foreign enterprise that enters into a petroleum contract with China National Offshore Oil Corporation. A foreign enterprise may be a company or a group of companies.  
(10) "Operator" means the entity responsible for carrying out operations in accordance with the provisions of the petroleum contract.  
(11) "Contractor" means an entity that provides services to the operator.

**Article 27** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
