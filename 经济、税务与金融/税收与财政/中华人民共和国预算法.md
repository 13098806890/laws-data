# 中华人民共和国预算法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2018-12-29  
**生效日期**：2018-12-29

> （1994年3月22日第八届全国人民代表大会第二次会议通过　根据2014年8月31日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国预算法〉的决定》第一次修正　根据2018年12月29日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第七次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国产品质量法〉等五部法律的决定》第二次修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　预算管理职权 第三章　预算收支范围 第四章　预算编制 第五章　预算审查和批准 第六章　预算执行 第七章　预算调整 第八章　决　　算 第九章　监　　督 第十章　法律责任 第十一章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范政府收支行为，强化预算约束，加强对预算的管理和监督，建立健全全面规范、公开透明的预算制度，保障经济社会的健康发展，根据宪法，制定本法。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范政府收支行为，强化预算约束，加强对预算的管理和监督，建立健全全面规范、公开透明的预算制度，保障经济社会的健康发展，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution to regulate government revenue and expenditure, strengthen budgetary constraints, enhance budget management and supervision, establish and improve a comprehensive, standardized, open and transparent budget system, and ensure the healthy development of the economy and society.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　预算、决算的编制、审查、批准、监督，以及预算的执行和调整，依照本法规定执行。

**Article 2** The formulation, examination, approval, and supervision of budgets and final accounts, as well as the execution and adjustment of budgets, shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家实行一级政府一级预算，设立中央，省、自治区、直辖市，设区的市、自治州，县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区，乡、民族乡、镇五级预算。  
全国预算由中央预算和地方预算组成。地方预算由各省、自治区、直辖市总预算组成。  
地方各级总预算由本级预算和汇总的下一级总预算组成；下一级只有本级预算的，下一级总预算即指下一级的本级预算。没有下一级预算的，总预算即指本级预算。

**Article 3** The State shall practice budget management at each level of government, establishing five levels of budgets: central; provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government; cities with districts and autonomous prefectures; counties, autonomous counties, cities without districts, and municipal districts; and townships, ethnic townships, and towns.  
The national budget consists of the central budget and local budgets. Local budgets consist of the general budgets of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
The general budget at each local level consists of the budget at that level and the aggregated general budget at the next lower level; if the next lower level has only its own budget, the general budget at the next lower level means that level's own budget. Where there is no budget at the next lower level, the general budget means the budget at that level.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　预算由预算收入和预算支出组成。  
政府的全部收入和支出都应当纳入预算。

**Article 4** A budget consists of budgetary revenue and budgetary expenditure.  
All government revenue and expenditure shall be included in the budget.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　预算包括一般公共预算、政府性基金预算、国有资本经营预算、社会保险基金预算。  
一般公共预算、政府性基金预算、国有资本经营预算、社会保险基金预算应当保持完整、独立。政府性基金预算、国有资本经营预算、社会保险基金预算应当与一般公共预算相衔接。

**Article 5** Budgets include general public budgets, government fund budgets, state capital operation budgets, and social insurance fund budgets.  
The general public budget, government fund budget, state capital operation budget, and social insurance fund budget shall maintain completeness and independence. The government fund budget, state capital operation budget, and social insurance fund budget shall be connected with the general public budget.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　一般公共预算是对以税收为主体的财政收入，安排用于保障和改善民生、推动经济社会发展、维护国家安全、维持国家机构正常运转等方面的收支预算。  
中央一般公共预算包括中央各部门（含直属单位，下同）的预算和中央对地方的税收返还、转移支付预算。  
中央一般公共预算收入包括中央本级收入和地方向中央的上解收入。中央一般公共预算支出包括中央本级支出、中央对地方的税收返还和转移支付。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-4" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第4条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 6** A general public budget is the revenue and expenditure budget for fiscal revenue mainly from taxes, arranged for ensuring and improving people's livelihood, promoting economic and social development, maintaining national security, and sustaining the normal operation of state institutions.  
The central general public budget includes the budgets of all central departments (including directly subordinate units, the same below) and the central budget for tax rebates and transfer payments to local governments.  
Central general public budget revenue includes central-level revenue and revenue turned over by local governments to the central government. Central general public budget expenditure includes central-level expenditure and tax rebates and transfer payments from the central government to local governments.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　地方各级一般公共预算包括本级各部门（含直属单位，下同）的预算和税收返还、转移支付预算。  
地方各级一般公共预算收入包括地方本级收入、上级政府对本级政府的税收返还和转移支付、下级政府的上解收入。地方各级一般公共预算支出包括地方本级支出、对上级政府的上解支出、对下级政府的税收返还和转移支付。

**Article 7** The general public budget at each local level includes the budgets of all departments at that level (including directly subordinate units, the same below) and budgets for tax rebates and transfer payments.  
General public budget revenue at each local level includes revenue at that level, tax rebates and transfer payments from the government at the next higher level to the government at that level, and revenue turned over by governments at the next lower level. General public budget expenditure at each local level includes expenditure at that level, expenditure turned over to the government at the next higher level, and tax rebates and transfer payments to governments at the next lower level.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　各部门预算由本部门及其所属各单位预算组成。

**Article 8** The budget of each department consists of the budgets of that department and all units under it.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　政府性基金预算是对依照法律、行政法规的规定在一定期限内向特定对象征收、收取或者以其他方式筹集的资金，专项用于特定公共事业发展的收支预算。  
政府性基金预算应当根据基金项目收入情况和实际支出需要，按基金项目编制，做到以收定支。

**Article 9** A government fund budget is the revenue and expenditure budget for funds collected from specific targets by levy, charge, or other means within a certain period in accordance with laws and administrative regulations, to be used exclusively for the development of specific public undertakings.  
Government fund budgets shall be compiled on a fund project basis based on the revenue circumstances of each fund project and the actual expenditure needs, to ensure expenditure is determined by revenue.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国有资本经营预算是对国有资本收益作出支出安排的收支预算。  
国有资本经营预算应当按照收支平衡的原则编制，不列赤字，并安排资金调入一般公共预算。

**Article 10** A state capital operation budget is the revenue and expenditure budget that makes expenditure arrangements for state capital earnings.  
The state capital operation budget shall be compiled in accordance with the principle of balancing revenue and expenditure, shall not show a deficit, and shall arrange for funds to be transferred into the general public budget.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　社会保险基金预算是对社会保险缴款、一般公共预算安排和其他方式筹集的资金，专项用于社会保险的收支预算。  
社会保险基金预算应当按照统筹层次和社会保险项目分别编制，做到收支平衡。

**Article 11** A social insurance fund budget is the revenue and expenditure budget for funds raised from social insurance contributions, allocations from the general public budget, and other means, to be used exclusively for social insurance.  
Social insurance fund budgets shall be compiled separately according to the pooling level and social insurance item, so as to achieve a balance between revenue and expenditure.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　各级预算应当遵循统筹兼顾、勤俭节约、量力而行、讲求绩效和收支平衡的原则。  
各级政府应当建立跨年度预算平衡机制。

**Article 12** Budgets at all levels shall follow the principles of overall planning, diligence and thrift, acting within capacity, focusing on performance, and balancing revenue and expenditure.  
Governments at all levels shall establish a mechanism for balancing budgets across fiscal years.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　经人民代表大会批准的预算，非经法定程序，不得调整。各级政府、各部门、各单位的支出必须以经批准的预算为依据，未列入预算的不得支出。

**Article 13** A budget approved by the People's Congress shall not be adjusted without going through the statutory procedures. The expenditure of governments at all levels, all departments, and all units must be based on the approved budget, and no expenditure shall be made for items not included in the budget.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　经本级人民代表大会或者本级人民代表大会常务委员会批准的预算、预算调整、决算、预算执行情况的报告及报表，应当在批准后二十日内由本级政府财政部门向社会公开，并对本级政府财政转移支付安排、执行的情况以及举借债务的情况等重要事项作出说明。  
经本级政府财政部门批复的部门预算、决算及报表，应当在批复后二十日内由各部门向社会公开，并对部门预算、决算中机关运行经费的安排、使用情况等重要事项作出说明。  
各级政府、各部门、各单位应当将政府采购的情况及时向社会公开。  
本条前三款规定的公开事项，涉及国家秘密的除外。

**Article 14** The budget, budget adjustment, final accounts, and reports and statements on budget execution approved by the People's Congress at the corresponding level or its Standing Committee shall be disclosed to the public by the finance department of the government at that level within 20 days after approval, and explanations shall be made on important matters such as the arrangement and execution of fiscal transfer payments by the government at that level and the situation of borrowing debts.  
Department budgets, final accounts, and statements approved by the finance department of the government at that level shall be disclosed to the public by each department within 20 days after approval, and explanations shall be made on important matters such as the arrangement and use of government operational expenses in the department budgets and final accounts.  
Governments at all levels, all departments, and all units shall promptly disclose to the public the circumstances of government procurement.  
The matters subject to disclosure as provided in the preceding three paragraphs of this Article shall not include those involving State secrets.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家实行中央和地方分税制。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-7" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第7条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 15** The State shall practice a system of tax sharing between the central and local governments.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家实行财政转移支付制度。财政转移支付应当规范、公平、公开，以推进地区间基本公共服务均等化为主要目标。  
财政转移支付包括中央对地方的转移支付和地方上级政府对下级政府的转移支付，以为均衡地区间基本财力、由下级政府统筹安排使用的一般性转移支付为主体。  
按照法律、行政法规和国务院的规定可以设立专项转移支付，用于办理特定事项。建立健全专项转移支付定期评估和退出机制。市场竞争机制能够有效调节的事项不得设立专项转移支付。  
上级政府在安排专项转移支付时，不得要求下级政府承担配套资金。但是，按照国务院的规定应当由上下级政府共同承担的事项除外。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-9" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第9条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-10" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第10条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 16** The State shall practice a system of fiscal transfer payments. Fiscal transfer payments shall be standardized, fair, and open, with the main objective of promoting equalization of basic public services among regions.  
Fiscal transfer payments include transfer payments from the central government to local governments and from higher-level local governments to lower-level local governments, with general transfer payments as the main form, which aim to balance basic fiscal capacity among regions and are to be arranged and used in an integrated manner by lower-level governments.  
Special transfer payments may be established in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and provisions of the State Council, for handling specific matters. A mechanism for regular evaluation and withdrawal of special transfer payments shall be established and improved. No special transfer payment shall be established for matters that can be effectively regulated by market competition mechanisms.  
When arranging special transfer payments, a higher-level government shall not require the lower-level government to provide matching funds, except for matters that, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council, shall be borne jointly by the higher-level and lower-level governments.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　各级预算的编制、执行应当建立健全相互制约、相互协调的机制。

**Article 17** The formulation and execution of budgets at all levels shall establish and improve mechanisms of mutual checks and mutual coordination.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　预算年度自公历一月一日起，至十二月三十一日止。

**Article 18** The fiscal year begins on January 1 and ends on December 31 of the Gregorian calendar.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　预算收入和预算支出以人民币元为计算单位。

**Article 19** Budgetary revenue and budgetary expenditure shall be calculated in Renminbi yuan.

### 第二章　预算管理职权

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　全国人民代表大会审查中央和地方预算草案及中央和地方预算执行情况的报告；批准中央预算和中央预算执行情况的报告；改变或者撤销全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于预算、决算的不适当的决议。  
全国人民代表大会常务委员会监督中央和地方预算的执行；审查和批准中央预算的调整方案；审查和批准中央决算；撤销国务院制定的同宪法、法律相抵触的关于预算、决算的行政法规、决定和命令；撤销省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会及其常务委员会制定的同宪法、法律和行政法规相抵触的关于预算、决算的地方性法规和决议。

**Article 20** The National People's Congress shall examine the draft central and local budgets and the reports on the execution of the central and local budgets; approve the central budget and the report on the execution of the central budget; and alter or annul inappropriate resolutions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress concerning budgets and final accounts.  
The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall supervise the execution of the central and local budgets; examine and approve the adjustment plan for the central budget; examine and approve the central final accounts; annul administrative regulations, decisions, and orders of the State Council concerning budgets and final accounts that contravene the Constitution or laws; and annul local regulations and resolutions concerning budgets and final accounts formulated by the people's congresses and their standing committees of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government that contravene the Constitution, laws, or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上地方各级人民代表大会审查本级总预算草案及本级总预算执行情况的报告；批准本级预算和本级预算执行情况的报告；改变或者撤销本级人民代表大会常务委员会关于预算、决算的不适当的决议；撤销本级政府关于预算、决算的不适当的决定和命令。  
县级以上地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会监督本级总预算的执行；审查和批准本级预算的调整方案；审查和批准本级决算；撤销本级政府和下一级人民代表大会及其常务委员会关于预算、决算的不适当的决定、命令和决议。  
乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会审查和批准本级预算和本级预算执行情况的报告；监督本级预算的执行；审查和批准本级预算的调整方案；审查和批准本级决算；撤销本级政府关于预算、决算的不适当的决定和命令。

**Article 21** The local people's congresses at or above the county level shall examine the draft general budgets of their respective levels and the reports on the implementation of such general budgets; approve the budgets of their respective levels and the reports on the implementation of such budgets; alter or annul inappropriate resolutions of the standing committees of their respective levels on budgets and final accounts; and annul inappropriate decisions and orders of the governments of their respective levels on budgets and final accounts.  
The standing committees of the local people's congresses at or above the county level shall supervise the implementation of the general budgets of their respective levels; examine and approve the adjustment plans for the budgets of their respective levels; examine and approve the final accounts of their respective levels; and annul inappropriate decisions, orders and resolutions of the governments of their respective levels and of the people's congresses and their standing committees at the next lower level on budgets and final accounts.  
The people's congresses of townships, nationality townships, and towns shall examine and approve the budgets of their respective levels and the reports on the implementation of such budgets; supervise the implementation of the budgets of their respective levels; examine and approve the adjustment plans for their respective budgets; examine and approve their respective final accounts; and annul inappropriate decisions and orders of the governments of their respective levels on budgets and final accounts.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会对中央预算草案初步方案及上一年预算执行情况、中央预算调整初步方案和中央决算草案进行初步审查，提出初步审查意见。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会有关专门委员会对本级预算草案初步方案及上一年预算执行情况、本级预算调整初步方案和本级决算草案进行初步审查，提出初步审查意见。  
设区的市、自治州人民代表大会有关专门委员会对本级预算草案初步方案及上一年预算执行情况、本级预算调整初步方案和本级决算草案进行初步审查，提出初步审查意见，未设立专门委员会的，由本级人民代表大会常务委员会有关工作机构研究提出意见。  
县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民代表大会常务委员会对本级预算草案初步方案及上一年预算执行情况进行初步审查，提出初步审查意见。县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民代表大会常务委员会有关工作机构对本级预算调整初步方案和本级决算草案研究提出意见。  
设区的市、自治州以上各级人民代表大会有关专门委员会进行初步审查、常务委员会有关工作机构研究提出意见时，应当邀请本级人民代表大会代表参加。  
对依照本条第一款至第四款规定提出的意见，本级政府财政部门应当将处理情况及时反馈。  
依照本条第一款至第四款规定提出的意见以及本级政府财政部门反馈的处理情况报告，应当印发本级人民代表大会代表。  
全国人民代表大会常务委员会和省、自治区、直辖市、设区的市、自治州人民代表大会常务委员会有关工作机构，依照本级人民代表大会常务委员会的决定，协助本级人民代表大会财政经济委员会或者有关专门委员会承担审查预算草案、预算调整方案、决算草案和监督预算执行等方面的具体工作。

**Article 22** The Financial and Economic Affairs Committee of the National People's Congress shall conduct preliminary examination of the preliminary plan of the central budget, the implementation of the budget of the previous year, the preliminary plan of the central budget adjustment and the draft central final accounts, and provide preliminary examination opinions.  
The relevant special committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall conduct preliminary examination of the preliminary plan of the budgets of their respective levels, the implementation of the budgets of the previous year, the preliminary plans for budget adjustment of their respective levels and the draft final accounts of their respective levels, and provide preliminary examination opinions.  
The relevant special committees of the people's congresses of cities divided into districts and autonomous prefectures shall conduct preliminary examination of the preliminary plan of the budgets of their respective levels, the implementation of the budgets of the previous year, the preliminary plans for budget adjustment of their respective levels and the draft final accounts of their respective levels, and provide preliminary examination opinions; where no special committee is established, the relevant working bodies of the standing committee of the people's congress of the same level shall study and provide opinions.  
The standing committees of the people's congresses of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, and municipal districts shall conduct preliminary examination of the preliminary plan of the budgets of their respective levels and the implementation of the budgets of the previous year, and provide preliminary examination opinions. The relevant working bodies of the standing committees of the people's congresses of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, and municipal districts shall study and provide opinions on the preliminary plans for budget adjustment of their respective levels and the draft final accounts of their respective levels.  
When conducting preliminary examination by the relevant special committees of the people's congresses at or above the level of cities divided into districts or autonomous prefectures, or when studying and providing opinions by the relevant working bodies of the standing committees, representatives of the people's congresses of the same level shall be invited to participate.  
The finance departments of the governments of the same level shall promptly provide feedback on the handling of opinions provided in accordance with the provisions of paragraphs 1 to 4 of this Article.  
The opinions provided in accordance with the provisions of paragraphs 1 to 4 of this Article and the reports on the handling feedback from the finance departments of the governments of the same level shall be distributed to the representatives of the people's congresses of the same level.  
The relevant working bodies of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress and the standing committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, cities divided into districts, and autonomous prefectures shall, in accordance with the decisions of the standing committees of the people's congresses of the same level, assist the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee or the relevant special committees of the people's congresses of the same level in undertaking specific work such as examining the draft budgets, budget adjustment plans, draft final accounts, and supervising budget implementation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院编制中央预算、决算草案；向全国人民代表大会作关于中央和地方预算草案的报告；将省、自治区、直辖市政府报送备案的预算汇总后报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案；组织中央和地方预算的执行；决定中央预算预备费的动用；编制中央预算调整方案；监督中央各部门和地方政府的预算执行；改变或者撤销中央各部门和地方政府关于预算、决算的不适当的决定、命令；向全国人民代表大会、全国人民代表大会常务委员会报告中央和地方预算的执行情况。

**Article 23** The State Council shall prepare the draft central budget and final accounts; submit a report on the central and local draft budgets to the National People's Congress; compile the budgets submitted by the governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government for recordation and report them to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for recordation; organize the implementation of the central and local budgets; decide on the use of the central budget reserve fund; prepare the central budget adjustment plan; supervise the budget implementation of the central departments and local governments; alter or annul inappropriate decisions and orders of the central departments and local governments on budgets and final accounts; and report on the implementation of the central and local budgets to the National People's Congress and the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　县级以上地方各级政府编制本级预算、决算草案；向本级人民代表大会作关于本级总预算草案的报告；将下一级政府报送备案的预算汇总后报本级人民代表大会常务委员会备案；组织本级总预算的执行；决定本级预算预备费的动用；编制本级预算的调整方案；监督本级各部门和下级政府的预算执行；改变或者撤销本级各部门和下级政府关于预算、决算的不适当的决定、命令；向本级人民代表大会、本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告本级总预算的执行情况。  
乡、民族乡、镇政府编制本级预算、决算草案；向本级人民代表大会作关于本级预算草案的报告；组织本级预算的执行；决定本级预算预备费的动用；编制本级预算的调整方案；向本级人民代表大会报告本级预算的执行情况。  
经省、自治区、直辖市政府批准，乡、民族乡、镇本级预算草案、预算调整方案、决算草案，可以由上一级政府代编，并依照[本法第二十一条](#art-21)的规定报乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会审查和批准。

**Article 24** The local governments at or above the county level shall prepare the draft budgets and final accounts of their respective levels; submit a report on the draft general budgets of their respective levels to the people's congresses of the same level; compile the budgets submitted by the governments at the next lower level for recordation and report them to the standing committees of the people's congresses of the same level for recordation; organize the implementation of the general budgets of their respective levels; decide on the use of the budget reserve fund of their respective levels; prepare the budget adjustment plans of their respective levels; supervise the budget implementation of the departments of the same level and the governments at lower levels; alter or annul inappropriate decisions and orders of the departments of the same level and the governments at lower levels on budgets and final accounts; and report on the implementation of the general budgets of their respective levels to the people's congresses and the standing committees of the people's congresses of the same level.  
The governments of townships, nationality townships, and towns shall prepare the draft budgets and final accounts of their respective levels; submit a report on the draft budgets of their respective levels to the people's congresses of the same level; organize the implementation of the budgets of their respective levels; decide on the use of the budget reserve fund of their respective levels; prepare the budget adjustment plans of their respective levels; and report on the implementation of the budgets of their respective levels to the people's congresses of the same level.  
With the approval of the governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, the draft budgets, budget adjustment plans, and draft final accounts of townships, nationality townships, and towns may be prepared on their behalf by the governments at the next higher level and shall be submitted to the people's congresses of townships, nationality townships, and towns for examination and approval in accordance with Article 21 of this Law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国务院财政部门具体编制中央预算、决算草案；具体组织中央和地方预算的执行；提出中央预算预备费动用方案；具体编制中央预算的调整方案；定期向国务院报告中央和地方预算的执行情况。  
地方各级政府财政部门具体编制本级预算、决算草案；具体组织本级总预算的执行；提出本级预算预备费动用方案；具体编制本级预算的调整方案；定期向本级政府和上一级政府财政部门报告本级总预算的执行情况。

**Article 25** The finance department of the State Council shall specifically prepare the draft central budget and final accounts; specifically organize the implementation of the central and local budgets; submit a proposal on the use of the central budget reserve fund; specifically prepare the central budget adjustment plan; and periodically report to the State Council on the implementation of the central and local budgets.  
The finance departments of local governments at all levels shall specifically prepare the draft budgets and final accounts of their respective levels; specifically organize the implementation of the general budgets of their respective levels; submit proposals on the use of the budget reserve funds of their respective levels; specifically prepare the budget adjustment plans of their respective levels; and periodically report to the governments of the same level and the finance departments of the governments at the next higher level on the implementation of the general budgets of their respective levels.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　各部门编制本部门预算、决算草案；组织和监督本部门预算的执行；定期向本级政府财政部门报告预算的执行情况。  
各单位编制本单位预算、决算草案；按照国家规定上缴预算收入，安排预算支出，并接受国家有关部门的监督。

**Article 26** All departments shall prepare the draft budgets and final accounts of their respective departments; organize and supervise the budget implementation of their respective departments; and periodically report to the finance departments of the governments of the same level on the budget implementation.  
All units shall prepare the draft budgets and final accounts of their respective units; turn over budget revenues, arrange budget expenditures in accordance with the provisions of the State; and accept supervision by the relevant State departments.

### 第三章　预算收支范围

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　一般公共预算收入包括各项税收收入、行政事业性收费收入、国有资源（资产）有偿使用收入、转移性收入和其他收入。  
一般公共预算支出按照其功能分类，包括一般公共服务支出，外交、公共安全、国防支出，农业、环境保护支出，教育、科技、文化、卫生、体育支出，社会保障及就业支出和其他支出。  
一般公共预算支出按照其经济性质分类，包括工资福利支出、商品和服务支出、资本性支出和其他支出。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-12" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第12条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 27** General public budget revenues include all types of tax revenues, administrative and institutional fee revenues, revenues from compensated use of State-owned resources (assets), transfer revenues, and other revenues.  
General public budget expenditures, classified by function, include expenditures for general public services, expenditures for diplomacy, public security, and national defense, expenditures for agriculture and environmental protection, expenditures for education, science and technology, culture, health, and sports, expenditures for social security and employment, and other expenditures.  
General public budget expenditures, classified by economic nature, include expenditures for wages and benefits, expenditures for goods and services, capital expenditures, and other expenditures.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　政府性基金预算、国有资本经营预算和社会保险基金预算的收支范围，按照法律、行政法规和国务院的规定执行。

**Article 28** The scope of revenues and expenditures of government-managed fund budgets, State-owned capital operation budgets, and social insurance fund budgets shall be executed in accordance with the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, and the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　中央预算与地方预算有关收入和支出项目的划分、地方向中央上解收入、中央对地方税收返还或者转移支付的具体办法，由国务院规定，报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 29** The specific measures for the division of revenue and expenditure items between the central budget and local budgets, the transfer of revenues from local governments to the central government, and the tax refunds or transfer payments from the central government to local governments shall be prescribed by the State Council and reported to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for recordation.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　上级政府不得在预算之外调用下级政府预算的资金。下级政府不得挤占或者截留属于上级政府预算的资金。

**Article 30** A government at a higher level shall not, outside the budget, transfer funds from the budget of a government at a lower level. A government at a lower level shall not misappropriate or intercept funds belonging to the budget of a government at a higher level.

### 第四章　预算编制

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国务院应当及时下达关于编制下一年预算草案的通知。编制预算草案的具体事项由国务院财政部门部署。  
各级政府、各部门、各单位应当按照国务院规定的时间编制预算草案。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-19" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 31** The State Council shall promptly issue a notice on the preparation of the draft budget for the next year. The specific matters for preparing the draft budget shall be arranged by the finance department of the State Council.  
All governments, departments, and units shall prepare draft budgets within the time limits prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　各级预算应当根据年度经济社会发展目标、国家宏观调控总体要求和跨年度预算平衡的需要，参考上一年预算执行情况、有关支出绩效评价结果和本年度收支预测，按照规定程序征求各方面意见后，进行编制。  
各级政府依据法定权限作出决定或者制定行政措施，凡涉及增加或者减少财政收入或者支出的，应当在预算批准前提出并在预算草案中作出相应安排。  
各部门、各单位应当按照国务院财政部门制定的政府收支分类科目、预算支出标准和要求，以及绩效目标管理等预算编制规定，根据其依法履行职能和事业发展的需要以及存量资产情况，编制本部门、本单位预算草案。  
前款所称政府收支分类科目，收入分为类、款、项、目；支出按其功能分类分为类、款、项，按其经济性质分类分为类、款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-20" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第20条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-21" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第21条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 32** Budgets at all levels shall be prepared in accordance with the annual economic and social development objectives, the overall requirements of national macro-control, and the needs of cross-year budget balance, with reference to the implementation of the budget of the previous year, the results of performance evaluation of relevant expenditures, and the revenue and expenditure forecasts for the current year, and after soliciting opinions from all sides in accordance with the prescribed procedures.  
Where a government at any level makes a decision or formulates administrative measures in accordance with statutory authority that involve an increase or decrease in fiscal revenues or expenditures, it shall propose such decisions or measures before the budget is approved and make corresponding arrangements in the draft budget.  
All departments and units shall prepare their respective drafts of budgets in accordance with the government revenue and expenditure classification subjects, budget expenditure standards and requirements, and budget preparation regulations such as performance target management formulated by the finance department of the State Council, and based on the needs of performing their functions according to law and developing their undertakings, as well as the status of existing assets.  
The government revenue and expenditure classification subjects referred to in the preceding paragraph classify revenues into classes, categories, items, and sub-items; expenditures are classified by function into classes, categories, and items, and by economic nature into classes and categories.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　省、自治区、直辖市政府应当按照国务院规定的时间，将本级总预算草案报国务院审核汇总。

**Article 33** The governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, within the time limits prescribed by the State Council, submit the drafts of their general budgets to the State Council for examination and compilation.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　中央一般公共预算中必需的部分资金，可以通过举借国内和国外债务等方式筹措，举借债务应当控制适当的规模，保持合理的结构。  
对中央一般公共预算中举借的债务实行余额管理，余额的规模不得超过全国人民代表大会批准的限额。  
国务院财政部门具体负责对中央政府债务的统一管理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-42" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第42条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 34** The necessary portion of funds in the central general public budget may be raised by means of borrowing domestic and foreign debts, and the borrowing of debts shall be controlled within an appropriate scale and maintain a reasonable structure.  
A balance management system shall be implemented for debts borrowed in the central general public budget, and the scale of the balance shall not exceed the limit approved by the National People's Congress.  
The finance department of the State Council shall specifically be responsible for the unified management of central government debts.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　地方各级预算按照量入为出、收支平衡的原则编制，除本法另有规定外，不列赤字。  
经国务院批准的省、自治区、直辖市的预算中必需的建设投资的部分资金，可以在国务院确定的限额内，通过发行地方政府债券举借债务的方式筹措。举借债务的规模，由国务院报全国人民代表大会或者全国人民代表大会常务委员会批准。省、自治区、直辖市依照国务院下达的限额举借的债务，列入本级预算调整方案，报本级人民代表大会常务委员会批准。举借的债务应当有偿还计划和稳定的偿还资金来源，只能用于公益性资本支出，不得用于经常性支出。  
除前款规定外，地方政府及其所属部门不得以任何方式举借债务。  
除法律另有规定外，地方政府及其所属部门不得为任何单位和个人的债务以任何方式提供担保。  
国务院建立地方政府债务风险评估和预警机制、应急处置机制以及责任追究制度。国务院财政部门对地方政府债务实施监督。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-44" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第44条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 35** Local budgets at all levels shall be prepared on the principle of living within means and balancing revenues and expenditures, and shall not include deficits except as otherwise provided in this Law.  
With the approval of the State Council, part of the funds necessary for construction investment in the budgets of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may be raised by means of borrowing debts through the issuance of local government bonds within the limits determined by the State Council. The scale of borrowing debts shall be submitted by the State Council to the National People's Congress or its Standing Committee for approval. The debts borrowed by provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government in accordance with the limits issued by the State Council shall be included in the budget adjustment plans of their respective levels and submitted to the standing committees of the people's congresses of the same level for approval. The borrowed debts shall have a repayment plan and stable sources of repayment funds, and may only be used for public welfare capital expenditures and not for recurrent expenditures.  
Except as provided in the preceding paragraph, local governments and their subordinate departments shall not borrow debts in any manner.  
Except as otherwise provided by law, local governments and their subordinate departments shall not provide guarantees in any manner for the debts of any units or individuals.  
The State Council shall establish a risk assessment and early warning mechanism, an emergency response mechanism, and an accountability system for local government debts. The finance department of the State Council shall supervise local government debts.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　各级预算收入的编制，应当与经济社会发展水平相适应，与财政政策相衔接。  
各级政府、各部门、各单位应当依照本法规定，将所有政府收入全部列入预算，不得隐瞒、少列。

**Article 36** The preparation of budget revenues at all levels shall be compatible with the level of economic and social development and consistent with fiscal policies.  
All governments, departments, and units shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law, include all government revenues in the budgets and shall not conceal or understate them.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　各级预算支出应当依照本法规定，按其功能和经济性质分类编制。  
各级预算支出的编制，应当贯彻勤俭节约的原则，严格控制各部门、各单位的机关运行经费和楼堂馆所等基本建设支出。  
各级一般公共预算支出的编制，应当统筹兼顾，在保证基本公共服务合理需要的前提下，优先安排国家确定的重点支出。

**Article 37** Budget expenditures at all levels shall be classified and prepared according to their functions and economic nature in accordance with the provisions of this Law.  
The preparation of budget expenditures at all levels shall implement the principle of diligence and thrift, and strictly control the operating expenses of organs of all departments and units and capital construction expenditures such as on office buildings and auditoriums.  
The preparation of general public budget expenditures at all levels shall be coordinated and balanced, and on the premise of ensuring reasonable needs for basic public services, priority shall be given to arranging key expenditures determined by the State.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　一般性转移支付应当按照国务院规定的基本标准和计算方法编制。专项转移支付应当分地区、分项目编制。  
县级以上各级政府应当将对下级政府的转移支付预计数提前下达下级政府。  
地方各级政府应当将上级政府提前下达的转移支付预计数编入本级预算。

**Article 38** General transfer payments shall be prepared in accordance with the basic standards and calculation methods prescribed by the State Council. Special transfer payments shall be prepared by region and by project.  
Governments at or above the county level shall issue in advance the estimated amounts of transfer payments to governments at lower levels.  
Local governments at all levels shall include in their own budgets the estimated amounts of transfer payments issued in advance by governments at higher levels.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　中央预算和有关地方预算中应当安排必要的资金，用于扶助革命老区、民族地区、边疆地区、贫困地区发展经济社会建设事业。

**Article 39** Necessary funds shall be arranged in the central budget and the relevant local budgets to support the development of economic and social construction in old revolutionary base areas, ethnic minority areas, border areas, and poverty-stricken areas.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　各级一般公共预算应当按照本级一般公共预算支出额的百分之一至百分之三设置预备费，用于当年预算执行中的自然灾害等突发事件处理增加的支出及其他难以预见的开支。

**Article 40** A reserve fund shall be set up in each general public budget at any level at one percent to three percent of the amount of general public budget expenditures of the same level, to be used for increased expenditures arising from the handling of natural disasters and other emergencies during the budget implementation of the current year, as well as other unforeseeable expenses.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　各级一般公共预算按照国务院的规定可以设置预算周转金，用于本级政府调剂预算年度内季节性收支差额。  
各级一般公共预算按照国务院的规定可以设置预算稳定调节基金，用于弥补以后年度预算资金的不足。

**Article 41** : At all levels, general public budgets may, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council, establish budget revolving funds for the purpose of adjusting seasonal differences in revenue and expenditure within the budget year at the corresponding level of government.  
At all levels, general public budgets may, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council, establish budget stabilization and regulation funds for the purpose of covering deficiencies in budget funds in subsequent years.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　各级政府上一年预算的结转资金，应当在下一年用于结转项目的支出；连续两年未用完的结转资金，应当作为结余资金管理。  
各部门、各单位上一年预算的结转、结余资金按照国务院财政部门的规定办理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-50" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第50条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 42** : The carryover funds from the previous year's budget of all levels of government shall be used for expenditures on carryover projects in the following year; carryover funds that remain unused for two consecutive years shall be managed as surplus funds.  
The carryover and surplus funds from the previous year's budget of all departments and units shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the finance department of the State Council.

### 第五章　预算审查和批准

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　中央预算由全国人民代表大会审查和批准。  
地方各级预算由本级人民代表大会审查和批准。

**Article 43** : The central budget shall be examined and approved by the National People's Congress.  
Local budgets at all levels shall be examined and approved by the people's congresses at the corresponding levels.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国务院财政部门应当在每年全国人民代表大会会议举行的四十五日前，将中央预算草案的初步方案提交全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会进行初步审查。  
省、自治区、直辖市政府财政部门应当在本级人民代表大会会议举行的三十日前，将本级预算草案的初步方案提交本级人民代表大会有关专门委员会进行初步审查。  
设区的市、自治州政府财政部门应当在本级人民代表大会会议举行的三十日前，将本级预算草案的初步方案提交本级人民代表大会有关专门委员会进行初步审查，或者送交本级人民代表大会常务委员会有关工作机构征求意见。  
县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区政府应当在本级人民代表大会会议举行的三十日前，将本级预算草案的初步方案提交本级人民代表大会常务委员会进行初步审查。

**Article 44** : The finance department of the State Council shall, 45 days before the annual session of the National People's Congress, submit the preliminary draft of the central budget to the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee of the National People's Congress for preliminary examination.  
The finance departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, 30 days before the session of the people's congress at the corresponding level, submit the preliminary draft of the budget at the corresponding level to the relevant special committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level for preliminary examination.  
The finance departments of cities divided into districts and autonomous prefectures shall, 30 days before the session of the people's congress at the corresponding level, submit the preliminary draft of the budget at the corresponding level to the relevant special committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level for preliminary examination, or deliver it to the relevant working body of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level for comments.  
The governments of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, and municipal districts shall, 30 days before the session of the people's congress at the corresponding level, submit the preliminary draft of the budget at the corresponding level to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level for preliminary examination.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区、乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会举行会议审查预算草案前，应当采用多种形式，组织本级人民代表大会代表，听取选民和社会各界的意见。

**Article 45** : Before the people's congresses of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, municipal districts, townships, ethnic townships, and towns hold meetings to examine the draft budget, they shall adopt various forms to organize the deputies to the people's congress at the corresponding level to solicit opinions from the electorate and from all sectors of society.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　报送各级人民代表大会审查和批准的预算草案应当细化。本级一般公共预算支出，按其功能分类应当编列到项；按其经济性质分类，基本支出应当编列到款。本级政府性基金预算、国有资本经营预算、社会保险基金预算支出，按其功能分类应当编列到项。

**Article 46** : The draft budget submitted to the people's congress at all levels for examination and approval shall be itemized in detail. Expenditures of the general public budget at the corresponding level shall, by functional classification, be compiled to the item level; and by economic classification, basic expenditures shall be compiled to the section level. Expenditures of the government-managed fund budget, the state capital operation budget, and the social insurance fund budget at the corresponding level shall, by functional classification, be compiled to the item level.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国务院在全国人民代表大会举行会议时，向大会作关于中央和地方预算草案以及中央和地方预算执行情况的报告。  
地方各级政府在本级人民代表大会举行会议时，向大会作关于总预算草案和总预算执行情况的报告。

**Article 47** : The State Council shall, when the National People's Congress is in session, submit to the Congress a report on the draft central and local budgets and on the implementation of the central and local budgets.  
Local governments at all levels shall, when the people's congress at the corresponding level is in session, submit to the congress a report on the draft total budget and on the implementation of the total budget.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　全国人民代表大会和地方各级人民代表大会对预算草案及其报告、预算执行情况的报告重点审查下列内容：  
（一）上一年预算执行情况是否符合本级人民代表大会预算决议的要求；  
（二）预算安排是否符合本法的规定；  
（三）预算安排是否贯彻国民经济和社会发展的方针政策，收支政策是否切实可行；  
（四）重点支出和重大投资项目的预算安排是否适当；  
（五）预算的编制是否完整，是否符合[本法第四十六条](#art-46)的规定；  
（六）对下级政府的转移性支出预算是否规范、适当；  
（七）预算安排举借的债务是否合法、合理，是否有偿还计划和稳定的偿还资金来源；  
（八）与预算有关重要事项的说明是否清晰。

**Article 48** : The National People's Congress and local people's congresses at all levels shall focus on examining the following contents with respect to the draft budget and its report, and the report on the implementation of the budget:  
(1) Whether the implementation of the budget in the previous year conforms to the requirements of the budget resolution of the people's congress at the corresponding level;  
(2) Whether the budget arrangements comply with the provisions of this Law;  
(3) Whether the budget arrangements implement the guidelines and policies for national economic and social development, and whether the revenue and expenditure policies are practical and feasible;  
(4) Whether the budget arrangements for key expenditures and major investment projects are appropriate;  
(5) Whether the compilation of the budget is complete and complies with the provisions of Article 46 of this Law;  
(6) Whether the budget for transfer expenditures to lower-level governments is standardized and appropriate;  
(7) Whether the debts incurred under the budget arrangements are lawful and reasonable, and whether there are repayment plans and stable sources of repayment funds;  
(8) Whether the explanations of important matters related to the budget are clear.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会向全国人民代表大会主席团提出关于中央和地方预算草案及中央和地方预算执行情况的审查结果报告。  
省、自治区、直辖市、设区的市、自治州人民代表大会有关专门委员会，县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民代表大会常务委员会，向本级人民代表大会主席团提出关于总预算草案及上一年总预算执行情况的审查结果报告。  
审查结果报告应当包括下列内容：  
（一）对上一年预算执行和落实本级人民代表大会预算决议的情况作出评价；  
（二）对本年度预算草案是否符合本法的规定，是否可行作出评价；  
（三）对本级人民代表大会批准预算草案和预算报告提出建议；  
（四）对执行年度预算、改进预算管理、提高预算绩效、加强预算监督等提出意见和建议。

**Article 49** : The Financial and Economic Affairs Committee of the National People's Congress shall submit to the Presidium of the National People's Congress a report on the results of the examination of the draft central and local budgets and the implementation of the central and local budgets.  
The relevant special committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, cities divided into districts, and autonomous prefectures, and the standing committees of the people's congresses of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, and municipal districts, shall submit to the presidium of the people's congress at the corresponding level a report on the results of the examination of the draft total budget and the implementation of the total budget in the previous year.  
The report on the results of the examination shall include the following contents:  
(1) Evaluating the implementation of the budget in the previous year and the implementation of the budget resolution of the people's congress at the corresponding level;  
(2) Evaluating whether the draft budget for the current year complies with the provisions of this Law and whether it is feasible;  
(3) Proposing suggestions on approving the draft budget and the budget report by the people's congress at the corresponding level;  
(4) Proposing opinions and suggestions on implementing the annual budget, improving budget management, enhancing budget performance, and strengthening budget supervision.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　乡、民族乡、镇政府应当及时将经本级人民代表大会批准的本级预算报上一级政府备案。县级以上地方各级政府应当及时将经本级人民代表大会批准的本级预算及下一级政府报送备案的预算汇总，报上一级政府备案。  
县级以上地方各级政府将下一级政府依照前款规定报送备案的预算汇总后，报本级人民代表大会常务委员会备案。国务院将省、自治区、直辖市政府依照前款规定报送备案的预算汇总后，报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 50** : The governments of townships, ethnic townships, and towns shall promptly report the budgets approved by the people's congress at the corresponding level to the government at the next higher level for recordation. Local governments at or above the county level shall promptly consolidate the budgets approved by the people's congress at the corresponding level and the budgets reported for recordation by the governments at the next lower level, and report them to the government at the next higher level for recordation.  
After consolidating the budgets reported for recordation by the governments at the next lower level in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, local governments at or above the county level shall report them to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level for recordation. After consolidating the budgets reported for recordation by the governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the State Council shall report them to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for recordation.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国务院和县级以上地方各级政府对下一级政府依照[本法第五十条](#art-50)规定报送备案的预算，认为有同法律、行政法规相抵触或者有其他不适当之处，需要撤销批准预算的决议的，应当提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会审议决定。

**Article 51** : If the State Council and local governments at or above the county level consider that the budgets reported for recordation by the governments at the next lower level in accordance with the provisions of Article 50 of this Law conflict with laws or administrative regulations, or have other improprieties, and that it is necessary to revoke the resolution approving the budget, they shall submit the matter to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level for deliberation and decision.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　各级预算经本级人民代表大会批准后，本级政府财政部门应当在二十日内向本级各部门批复预算。各部门应当在接到本级政府财政部门批复的本部门预算后十五日内向所属各单位批复预算。  
中央对地方的一般性转移支付应当在全国人民代表大会批准预算后三十日内正式下达。中央对地方的专项转移支付应当在全国人民代表大会批准预算后九十日内正式下达。  
省、自治区、直辖市政府接到中央一般性转移支付和专项转移支付后，应当在三十日内正式下达到本行政区域县级以上各级政府。  
县级以上地方各级预算安排对下级政府的一般性转移支付和专项转移支付，应当分别在本级人民代表大会批准预算后的三十日和六十日内正式下达。  
对自然灾害等突发事件处理的转移支付，应当及时下达预算；对据实结算等特殊项目的转移支付，可以分期下达预算，或者先预付后结算。  
县级以上各级政府财政部门应当将批复本级各部门的预算和批复下级政府的转移支付预算，抄送本级人民代表大会财政经济委员会、有关专门委员会和常务委员会有关工作机构。

**Article 52** : After a budget at any level is approved by the people's congress at the corresponding level, the finance department of the government at that level shall approve the budget to the departments at the corresponding level within 20 days. Each department shall approve the budget to its subordinate units within 15 days after receiving the approved budget from the finance department of the government at the corresponding level.  
General transfer payments from the central government to local governments shall be formally issued within 30 days after the National People's Congress approves the budget. Special transfer payments from the central government to local governments shall be formally issued within 90 days after the National People's Congress approves the budget.  
After receiving the general transfer payments and special transfer payments from the central government, the governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formally issue them to governments at or above the county level within their administrative areas within 30 days.  
General transfer payments and special transfer payments arranged in local budgets at or above the county level for governments at the next lower level shall be formally issued within 30 days and 60 days, respectively, after the people's congress at the corresponding level approves the budget.  
Transfer payments for handling emergencies such as natural disasters shall be promptly issued; for special projects such as those settled based on actual amounts, transfer payments may be issued in installments, or advance payments may be made followed by settlement.  
The finance departments of governments at or above the county level shall send copies of the budgets approved to the departments at the corresponding level and the transfer payment budgets approved to governments at lower levels to the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee, relevant special committees, and relevant working bodies of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level.

### 第六章　预算执行

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　各级预算由本级政府组织执行，具体工作由本级政府财政部门负责。  
各部门、各单位是本部门、本单位的预算执行主体，负责本部门、本单位的预算执行，并对执行结果负责。

**Article 53** : Budgets at all levels shall be organized for implementation by the government at the corresponding level, and the specific work shall be the responsibility of the finance department of the government at that level.  
All departments and units are the entities responsible for the implementation of their respective budgets, and shall be responsible for the implementation of their budgets and be accountable for the results of such implementation.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　预算年度开始后，各级预算草案在本级人民代表大会批准前，可以安排下列支出：  
（一）上一年度结转的支出；  
（二）参照上一年同期的预算支出数额安排必须支付的本年度部门基本支出、项目支出，以及对下级政府的转移性支出；  
（三）法律规定必须履行支付义务的支出，以及用于自然灾害等突发事件处理的支出。  
根据前款规定安排支出的情况，应当在预算草案的报告中作出说明。  
预算经本级人民代表大会批准后，按照批准的预算执行。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-57" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第57条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 54** : After the start of the budget year, before the draft budget at any level is approved by the people's congress at the corresponding level, the following expenditures may be arranged:  
(1) Carryover expenditures from the previous year;  
(2) Expenditures for departmental basic expenditures, project expenditures, and transfer expenditures to lower-level governments for the current year that must be paid by reference to the amount of budget expenditures in the corresponding period of the previous year;  
(3) Expenditures that must be paid in accordance with legal provisions, and expenditures for handling emergencies such as natural disasters.  
The circumstances in which expenditures are arranged in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall be explained in the report on the draft budget.  
After the budget is approved by the people's congress at the corresponding level, it shall be implemented in accordance with the approved budget.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　预算收入征收部门和单位，必须依照法律、行政法规的规定，及时、足额征收应征的预算收入。不得违反法律、行政法规规定，多征、提前征收或者减征、免征、缓征应征的预算收入，不得截留、占用或者挪用预算收入。  
各级政府不得向预算收入征收部门和单位下达收入指标。

**Article 55** : The departments and units responsible for collecting budget revenues must collect the budget revenues due in a timely manner and in full amount in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations. They shall not, in violation of the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, collect more, collect in advance, or reduce, exempt, or defer the collection of budget revenues due, and shall not intercept, retain, or misappropriate budget revenues.  
All levels of government shall not issue revenue targets to the departments and units responsible for collecting budget revenues.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　政府的全部收入应当上缴国家金库（以下简称国库），任何部门、单位和个人不得截留、占用、挪用或者拖欠。  
对于法律有明确规定或者经国务院批准的特定专用资金，可以依照国务院的规定设立财政专户。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-52" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第52条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 56** : All revenues of the government shall be turned over to the National Treasury (hereinafter referred to as the Treasury), and no department, unit, or individual may intercept, retain, misappropriate, or withhold them.  
For specific dedicated funds explicitly provided for by law or approved by the State Council, special fiscal accounts may be established in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　各级政府财政部门必须依照法律、行政法规和国务院财政部门的规定，及时、足额地拨付预算支出资金，加强对预算支出的管理和监督。  
各级政府、各部门、各单位的支出必须按照预算执行，不得虚假列支。  
各级政府、各部门、各单位应当对预算支出情况开展绩效评价。

**Article 57** : The finance departments of governments at all levels must allocate budget expenditure funds in a timely manner and in full amount in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of the finance department of the State Council, and shall strengthen the management and supervision of budget expenditures.  
Expenditures of governments at all levels, all departments, and all units must be implemented in accordance with the budget, and no false expenditures may be recorded.  
Governments at all levels, all departments, and all units shall carry out performance evaluation on the use of budget expenditures.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　各级预算的收入和支出实行收付实现制。  
特定事项按照国务院的规定实行权责发生制的有关情况，应当向本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告。

**Article 58** : The revenues and expenditures of budgets at all levels shall be accounted for on a cash basis.  
For specific matters to which an accrual basis is applied in accordance with the provisions of the State Council, the relevant circumstances shall be reported to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　县级以上各级预算必须设立国库；具备条件的乡、民族乡、镇也应当设立国库。  
中央国库业务由中国人民银行经理，地方国库业务依照国务院的有关规定办理。  
各级国库应当按照国家有关规定，及时准确地办理预算收入的收纳、划分、留解、退付和预算支出的拨付。  
各级国库库款的支配权属于本级政府财政部门。除法律、行政法规另有规定外，未经本级政府财政部门同意，任何部门、单位和个人都无权冻结、动用国库库款或者以其他方式支配已入国库的库款。  
各级政府应当加强对本级国库的管理和监督，按照国务院的规定完善国库现金管理，合理调节国库资金余额。

**Article 59** : Treasuries must be established for budgets at or above the county level; townships, ethnic townships, and towns that meet the conditions shall also establish treasuries.  
The central treasury business shall be managed by the People's Bank of China, and local treasury business shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.  
Treasuries at all levels shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, promptly and accurately handle the receipt, segregation, retention, release, and refund of budget revenues and the allocation of budget expenditures.  
The right to control treasury funds at all levels belongs to the finance department of the government at the corresponding level. Unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations, no department, unit, or individual may freeze, use, or otherwise control treasury funds that have been paid into the treasury without the consent of the finance department of the government at the corresponding level.  
Governments at all levels shall strengthen the management and supervision of the treasury at the corresponding level, improve treasury cash management in accordance with the provisions of the State Council, and reasonably adjust the balance of treasury funds.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　已经缴入国库的资金，依照法律、行政法规的规定或者国务院的决定需要退付的，各级政府财政部门或者其授权的机构应当及时办理退付。按照规定应当由财政支出安排的事项，不得用退库处理。

**Article 60** : Where funds that have been paid into the treasury need to be refunded in accordance with the provisions of laws or administrative regulations or a decision of the State Council, the finance department of the government at the corresponding level or the institution authorized by it shall promptly handle the refund. Matters that should be arranged through fiscal expenditures in accordance with regulations shall not be handled by means of treasury refunds.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家实行国库集中收缴和集中支付制度，对政府全部收入和支出实行国库集中收付管理。

**Article 61** The State shall implement a centralized treasury collection and centralized payment system, and shall carry out centralized treasury collection and payment management for all government revenues and expenditures.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　各级政府应当加强对预算执行的领导，支持政府财政、税务、海关等预算收入的征收部门依法组织预算收入，支持政府财政部门严格管理预算支出。  
财政、税务、海关等部门在预算执行中，应当加强对预算执行的分析；发现问题时应当及时建议本级政府采取措施予以解决。

**Article 62** Governments at all levels shall strengthen their leadership over budget execution, support the departments responsible for collecting budgetary revenues, such as the government finance, taxation, and customs departments, in organizing budgetary revenues according to law, and support the government finance departments in strictly managing budgetary expenditures.  
The finance, taxation, and customs departments shall, during budget execution, strengthen the analysis of budget execution; where problems are discovered, they shall promptly propose that the government at the same level take measures to resolve them.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　各部门、各单位应当加强对预算收入和支出的管理，不得截留或者动用应当上缴的预算收入，不得擅自改变预算支出的用途。

**Article 63** All departments and units shall strengthen the management of budgetary revenues and expenditures, and shall not intercept or divert budgetary revenues that shall be turned over, nor shall they change the purpose of budgetary expenditures without authorization.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　各级预算预备费的动用方案，由本级政府财政部门提出，报本级政府决定。

**Article 64** The plan for the use of the budgetary reserve fund at each level shall be proposed by the government finance department at the same level and shall be submitted to the government at the same level for decision.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　各级预算周转金由本级政府财政部门管理，不得挪作他用。

**Article 65** The budgetary revolving fund at each level shall be managed by the government finance department at the same level and shall not be diverted to other purposes.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　各级一般公共预算年度执行中有超收收入的，只能用于冲减赤字或者补充预算稳定调节基金。  
各级一般公共预算的结余资金，应当补充预算稳定调节基金。  
省、自治区、直辖市一般公共预算年度执行中出现短收，通过调入预算稳定调节基金、减少支出等方式仍不能实现收支平衡的，省、自治区、直辖市政府报本级人民代表大会或者其常务委员会批准，可以增列赤字，报国务院财政部门备案，并应当在下一年度预算中予以弥补。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-78" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第78条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 66** Where there is excess revenue during the annual execution of a general public budget at any level, it shall only be used to offset deficits or to supplement the budget stabilization and adjustment fund.  
The surplus funds of a general public budget at any level shall be used to supplement the budget stabilization and adjustment fund.  
Where, during the annual execution of a general public budget of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, a revenue shortfall occurs and cannot be resolved by transferring in funds from the budget stabilization and adjustment fund or reducing expenditures to achieve a balance, the government of that province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, with the approval of the people's congress at the same level or its standing committee, increase the deficit, report to the finance department of the State Council for recordation, and shall make up for it in the next year's budget.

### 第七章　预算调整

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　经全国人民代表大会批准的中央预算和经地方各级人民代表大会批准的地方各级预算，在执行中出现下列情况之一的，应当进行预算调整：  
（一）需要增加或者减少预算总支出的；  
（二）需要调入预算稳定调节基金的；  
（三）需要调减预算安排的重点支出数额的；  
（四）需要增加举借债务数额的。

**Article 67** Where, during budget execution, any of the following circumstances occurs in the central budget approved by the National People's Congress or in the local budgets at all levels approved by the local people's congresses at the corresponding levels, a budget adjustment shall be made:  
(1) The total budgetary expenditures need to be increased or decreased;  
(2) Funds need to be transferred in from the budget stabilization and adjustment fund;  
(3) The amount of key expenditures arranged in the budget needs to be reduced;  
(4) The amount of debt to be borrowed needs to be increased.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　在预算执行中，各级政府一般不制定新的增加财政收入或者支出的政策和措施，也不制定减少财政收入的政策和措施；必须作出并需要进行预算调整的，应当在预算调整方案中作出安排。

**Article 68** During budget execution, governments at all levels shall generally not formulate new policies or measures to increase fiscal revenues or expenditures, nor shall they formulate policies or measures to reduce fiscal revenues; where it is necessary to do so and a budget adjustment is required, arrangements shall be made in the budget adjustment plan.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　在预算执行中，各级政府对于必须进行的预算调整，应当编制预算调整方案。预算调整方案应当说明预算调整的理由、项目和数额。  
在预算执行中，由于发生自然灾害等突发事件，必须及时增加预算支出的，应当先动支预备费；预备费不足支出的，各级政府可以先安排支出，属于预算调整的，列入预算调整方案。  
国务院财政部门应当在全国人民代表大会常务委员会举行会议审查和批准预算调整方案的三十日前，将预算调整初步方案送交全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会进行初步审查。  
省、自治区、直辖市政府财政部门应当在本级人民代表大会常务委员会举行会议审查和批准预算调整方案的三十日前，将预算调整初步方案送交本级人民代表大会有关专门委员会进行初步审查。  
设区的市、自治州政府财政部门应当在本级人民代表大会常务委员会举行会议审查和批准预算调整方案的三十日前，将预算调整初步方案送交本级人民代表大会有关专门委员会进行初步审查，或者送交本级人民代表大会常务委员会有关工作机构征求意见。  
县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区政府财政部门应当在本级人民代表大会常务委员会举行会议审查和批准预算调整方案的三十日前，将预算调整初步方案送交本级人民代表大会常务委员会有关工作机构征求意见。  
中央预算的调整方案应当提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会审查和批准。县级以上地方各级预算的调整方案应当提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会审查和批准；乡、民族乡、镇预算的调整方案应当提请本级人民代表大会审查和批准。未经批准，不得调整预算。

**Article 69** During budget execution, where a budget adjustment is necessary for a government at any level, it shall prepare a budget adjustment plan. The budget adjustment plan shall state the reasons, items, and amounts involved in the budget adjustment.  
During budget execution, where an emergency caused by a natural disaster or other unexpected event necessitates an immediate increase in budgetary expenditures, the reserve fund shall be used first; where the reserve fund is insufficient for the expenditures, the government at the corresponding level may arrange the expenditures first, and if it falls within the scope of budget adjustment, it shall be included in the budget adjustment plan.  
The finance department of the State Council shall, thirty days before the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress holds a meeting to examine and approve the budget adjustment plan, submit the preliminary budget adjustment plan to the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee of the National People's Congress for preliminary examination.  
The finance department of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, thirty days before the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level holds a meeting to examine and approve the budget adjustment plan, submit the preliminary budget adjustment plan to the relevant special committee of the people's congress at the same level for preliminary examination.  
The finance department of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture shall, thirty days before the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level holds a meeting to examine and approve the budget adjustment plan, submit the preliminary budget adjustment plan to the relevant special committee of the people's congress at the same level for preliminary examination, or submit it to the relevant working body of the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level for opinions.  
The finance department of a county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall, thirty days before the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level holds a meeting to examine and approve the budget adjustment plan, submit the preliminary budget adjustment plan to the relevant working body of the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level for opinions.  
The budget adjustment plan for the central budget shall be submitted to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for examination and approval. The budget adjustment plan for a local budget at or above the county level shall be submitted to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level for examination and approval; the budget adjustment plan for the budget of a township, ethnic township, or town shall be submitted to the people's congress at the same level for examination and approval. Without approval, no budget adjustment shall be made.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　经批准的预算调整方案，各级政府应当严格执行。未经[本法第六十九条](#art-69)规定的程序，各级政府不得作出预算调整的决定。  
对违反前款规定作出的决定，本级人民代表大会、本级人民代表大会常务委员会或者上级政府应当责令其改变或者撤销。

**Article 70** Governments at all levels shall strictly implement the approved budget adjustment plan. Without going through the procedures prescribed in Article 69 of this Law, no government at any level shall make a decision on budget adjustment.  
Any decision made in violation of the preceding paragraph shall be ordered to be changed or revoked by the people's congress at the same level, its standing committee, or the government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　在预算执行中，地方各级政府因上级政府增加不需要本级政府提供配套资金的专项转移支付而引起的预算支出变化，不属于预算调整。  
接受增加专项转移支付的县级以上地方各级政府应当向本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告有关情况；接受增加专项转移支付的乡、民族乡、镇政府应当向本级人民代表大会报告有关情况。

**Article 71** During budget execution, changes in budgetary expenditures caused by the government at the next higher level increasing special transfer payments that do not require the government at the same level to provide matching funds shall not be considered a budget adjustment.  
A local government at or above the county level that receives increased special transfer payments shall report the relevant situation to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level; a township, ethnic township, or town government that receives increased special transfer payments shall report the relevant situation to the people's congress at the same level.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　各部门、各单位的预算支出应当按照预算科目执行。严格控制不同预算科目、预算级次或者项目间的预算资金的调剂，确需调剂使用的，按照国务院财政部门的规定办理。

**Article 72** The budgetary expenditures of all departments and units shall be executed in accordance with the budget accounts. Adjustments of budgetary funds among different budget accounts, budget levels, or items shall be strictly controlled; where adjustments are genuinely necessary, they shall be handled in accordance with the regulations of the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　地方各级预算的调整方案经批准后，由本级政府报上一级政府备案。

**Article 73** After the approval of the adjustment plan for a local budget at any level, the government at the same level shall report it to the government at the next higher level for recordation.

### 第八章　决算

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　决算草案由各级政府、各部门、各单位，在每一预算年度终了后按照国务院规定的时间编制。  
编制决算草案的具体事项，由国务院财政部门部署。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-80" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第80条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 74** The final accounts draft shall be prepared by all governments, departments, and units within the time limit prescribed by the State Council after the end of each budget year.  
The specific matters concerning the preparation of the final accounts draft shall be arranged by the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　编制决算草案，必须符合法律、行政法规，做到收支真实、数额准确、内容完整、报送及时。  
决算草案应当与预算相对应，按预算数、调整预算数、决算数分别列出。一般公共预算支出应当按其功能分类编列到项，按其经济性质分类编列到款。

**Article 75** In preparing the final accounts draft, the provisions of laws and administrative regulations must be complied with, and the revenues and expenditures shall be truthful, the amounts accurate, the content complete, and the submission timely.  
The final accounts draft shall correspond to the budget and shall list separately the budgeted figures, the adjusted budgeted figures, and the actual final accounts figures. General public budget expenditures shall be itemized by function classification and by economic classification.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　各部门对所属各单位的决算草案，应当审核并汇总编制本部门的决算草案，在规定的期限内报本级政府财政部门审核。  
各级政府财政部门对本级各部门决算草案审核后发现有不符合法律、行政法规规定的，有权予以纠正。

**Article 76** Each department shall examine and compile the final accounts drafts of its subordinate units into its own departmental final accounts draft, and submit it to the government finance department at the same level for examination within the prescribed time limit.  
Where a government finance department at any level, upon examination, finds that the final accounts drafts of the departments at the same level do not comply with the provisions of laws or administrative regulations, it shall have the authority to correct them.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　国务院财政部门编制中央决算草案，经国务院审计部门审计后，报国务院审定，由国务院提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会审查和批准。  
县级以上地方各级政府财政部门编制本级决算草案，经本级政府审计部门审计后，报本级政府审定，由本级政府提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会审查和批准。  
乡、民族乡、镇政府编制本级决算草案，提请本级人民代表大会审查和批准。

**Article 77** The finance department of the State Council shall prepare the central final accounts draft, which shall be audited by the audit department of the State Council, submitted to the State Council for approval, and then submitted by the State Council to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for examination and approval.  
The finance department of a local government at or above the county level shall prepare the final accounts draft of the same level, which shall be audited by the audit department of the government at the same level, submitted to the government at the same level for approval, and then submitted by the government at the same level to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level for examination and approval.  
The government of a township, ethnic township, or town shall prepare the final accounts draft of the same level and submit it to the people's congress at the same level for examination and approval.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　国务院财政部门应当在全国人民代表大会常务委员会举行会议审查和批准中央决算草案的三十日前，将上一年度中央决算草案提交全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会进行初步审查。  
省、自治区、直辖市政府财政部门应当在本级人民代表大会常务委员会举行会议审查和批准本级决算草案的三十日前，将上一年度本级决算草案提交本级人民代表大会有关专门委员会进行初步审查。  
设区的市、自治州政府财政部门应当在本级人民代表大会常务委员会举行会议审查和批准本级决算草案的三十日前，将上一年度本级决算草案提交本级人民代表大会有关专门委员会进行初步审查，或者送交本级人民代表大会常务委员会有关工作机构征求意见。  
县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区政府财政部门应当在本级人民代表大会常务委员会举行会议审查和批准本级决算草案的三十日前，将上一年度本级决算草案送交本级人民代表大会常务委员会有关工作机构征求意见。  
全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会和省、自治区、直辖市、设区的市、自治州人民代表大会有关专门委员会，向本级人民代表大会常务委员会提出关于本级决算草案的审查结果报告。

**Article 78** The finance department of the State Council shall, thirty days before the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress holds a meeting to examine and approve the central final accounts draft, submit the final accounts draft of the preceding year to the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee of the National People's Congress for preliminary examination.  
The finance department of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, thirty days before the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level holds a meeting to examine and approve the final accounts draft of the same level, submit the final accounts draft of the preceding year to the relevant special committee of the people's congress at the same level for preliminary examination.  
The finance department of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture shall, thirty days before the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level holds a meeting to examine and approve the final accounts draft of the same level, submit the final accounts draft of the preceding year to the relevant special committee of the people's congress at the same level for preliminary examination, or submit it to the relevant working body of the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level for opinions.  
The finance department of a county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall, thirty days before the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level holds a meeting to examine and approve the final accounts draft of the same level, submit the final accounts draft of the preceding year to the relevant working body of the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level for opinions.  
The Financial and Economic Affairs Committee of the National People's Congress and the relevant special committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, cities divided into districts, and autonomous prefectures shall submit to the standing committees of the people's congresses at the same level a report on the results of the examination of the final accounts draft at the same level.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　县级以上各级人民代表大会常务委员会和乡、民族乡、镇人民代表大会对本级决算草案，重点审查下列内容：  
（一）预算收入情况；  
（二）支出政策实施情况和重点支出、重大投资项目资金的使用及绩效情况；  
（三）结转资金的使用情况；  
（四）资金结余情况；  
（五）本级预算调整及执行情况；  
（六）财政转移支付安排执行情况；  
（七）经批准举借债务的规模、结构、使用、偿还等情况；  
（八）本级预算周转金规模和使用情况；  
（九）本级预备费使用情况；  
（十）超收收入安排情况，预算稳定调节基金的规模和使用情况；  
（十一）本级人民代表大会批准的预算决议落实情况；  
（十二）其他与决算有关的重要情况。  
县级以上各级人民代表大会常务委员会应当结合本级政府提出的上一年度预算执行和其他财政收支的审计工作报告，对本级决算草案进行审查。

**Article 79** The standing committees of the people's congresses at or above the county level and the people's congresses of townships, ethnic townships, and towns shall focus their examination of the final accounts draft at the same level on the following:  
(1) The status of budgetary revenues;  
(2) The implementation of expenditure policies and the use and performance of key expenditures and funds for major investment projects;  
(3) The use of carryover funds;  
(4) The status of fund balances;  
(5) The adjustment and execution of the budget at the same level;  
(6) The arrangement and implementation of fiscal transfer payments;  
(7) The scale, structure, use, and repayment of approved borrowings;  
(8) The scale and use of the budgetary revolving fund at the same level;  
(9) The use of the reserve fund at the same level;  
(10) The arrangement of excess revenue and the scale and use of the budget stabilization and adjustment fund;  
(11) The implementation of the budget resolution adopted by the people's congress at the same level;  
(12) Other important matters related to the final accounts.  
The standing committees of the people's congresses at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with the audit work report on the implementation of the budget for the preceding year and other fiscal revenues and expenditures submitted by the government at the same level, examine the final accounts draft at the same level.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　各级决算经批准后，财政部门应当在二十日内向本级各部门批复决算。各部门应当在接到本级政府财政部门批复的本部门决算后十五日内向所属单位批复决算。

**Article 80** After the final accounts at each level are approved, the finance department shall approve and issue the final accounts to the departments at the same level within twenty days. Each department shall, within fifteen days after receiving the approval of its departmental final accounts from the government finance department at the same level, approve and issue the final accounts to its subordinate units.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　地方各级政府应当将经批准的决算及下一级政府上报备案的决算汇总，报上一级政府备案。  
县级以上各级政府应当将下一级政府报送备案的决算汇总后，报本级人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 81** Local governments at all levels shall summarize the approved final accounts and the final accounts submitted for record by the next lower level government, and submit them to the next higher level government for record.  
Governments at and above the county level shall, after summarizing the final accounts submitted for record by the next lower level government, submit them to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level for record.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　国务院和县级以上地方各级政府对下一级政府依照[本法第八十一条](#art-81)规定报送备案的决算，认为有同法律、行政法规相抵触或者有其他不适当之处，需要撤销批准该项决算的决议的，应当提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会审议决定；经审议决定撤销的，该下级人民代表大会常务委员会应当责成本级政府依照本法规定重新编制决算草案，提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会审查和批准。

**Article 82** If the State Council and local governments at and above the county level consider that the final accounts submitted for record by the next lower level government in accordance with the provisions of Article 81 of this Law are in conflict with laws or administrative regulations or are otherwise inappropriate, and a decision to approve the said final accounts needs to be revoked, they shall submit the matter to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level for deliberation and decision; if it is decided to revoke the approval after deliberation, the standing committee of the people's congress at the lower level shall instruct the government at that level to re-prepare a draft final accounts in accordance with the provisions of this Law, and submit it to the standing committee of the people's congress at that level for examination and approval.

### 第九章　监督

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会对中央和地方预算、决算进行监督。  
县级以上地方各级人民代表大会及其常务委员会对本级和下级预算、决算进行监督。  
乡、民族乡、镇人民代表大会对本级预算、决算进行监督。

**Article 83** The National People's Congress and its Standing Committee shall exercise supervision over the central and local budgets and final accounts.  
The local people's congresses at and above the county level and their standing committees shall exercise supervision over the budgets and final accounts at the corresponding level and at lower levels.  
The people's congresses of townships, nationality townships, and towns shall exercise supervision over the budgets and final accounts at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　各级人民代表大会和县级以上各级人民代表大会常务委员会有权就预算、决算中的重大事项或者特定问题组织调查，有关的政府、部门、单位和个人应当如实反映情况和提供必要的材料。

**Article 84** The people's congresses at all levels and the standing committees of the people's congresses at and above the county level may organize investigations into major matters or specific issues in budgets and final accounts, and the relevant governments, departments, entities, and individuals shall truthfully reflect the situation and provide necessary materials.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　各级人民代表大会和县级以上各级人民代表大会常务委员会举行会议时，人民代表大会代表或者常务委员会组成人员，依照法律规定程序就预算、决算中的有关问题提出询问或者质询，受询问或者受质询的有关的政府或者财政部门必须及时给予答复。

**Article 85** When the people's congresses at all levels and the standing committees of the people's congresses at and above the county level hold meetings, deputies to the people's congresses or members of the standing committees may, in accordance with the procedures prescribed by law, raise inquiries or questions on relevant issues in budgets and final accounts, and the relevant governments or finance departments that are inquired or questioned must give timely replies.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　国务院和县级以上地方各级政府应当在每年六月至九月期间向本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告预算执行情况。

**Article 86** The State Council and local governments at and above the county level shall, during the period from June to September each year, report to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level on the implementation of the budget.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　各级政府监督下级政府的预算执行；下级政府应当定期向上一级政府报告预算执行情况。

**Article 87** Governments at all levels shall supervise the implementation of budgets by lower level governments; lower level governments shall regularly report to the next higher level government on the implementation of the budget.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　各级政府财政部门负责监督本级各部门及其所属各单位预算管理有关工作，并向本级政府和上一级政府财政部门报告预算执行情况。

**Article 88** The finance departments of governments at all levels shall be responsible for supervising the relevant work of budget management of departments at the corresponding level and their subordinate entities, and shall report to the government at the corresponding level and the finance department of the next higher level government on the implementation of the budget.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　县级以上政府审计部门依法对预算执行、决算实行审计监督。  
对预算执行和其他财政收支的审计工作报告应当向社会公开。

**Article 89** The audit departments of governments at and above the county level shall, according to law, exercise audit supervision over budget implementation and final accounts.  
Audit work reports on budget implementation and other fiscal revenues and expenditures shall be made public to society.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　政府各部门负责监督检查所属各单位的预算执行，及时向本级政府财政部门反映本部门预算执行情况，依法纠正违反预算的行为。

**Article 90** All government departments shall be responsible for supervising and inspecting the implementation of budgets by entities under them, and shall promptly report to the finance department of the government at the corresponding level on the implementation of the budget of their department, and correct any acts violating the budget according to law.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　公民、法人或者其他组织发现有违反本法的行为，可以依法向有关国家机关进行检举、控告。  
接受检举、控告的国家机关应当依法进行处理，并为检举人、控告人保密。任何单位或者个人不得压制和打击报复检举人、控告人。

**Article 91** Citizens, legal persons, or other organizations that discover any acts violating this Law may, according to law, report or accuse them to the relevant state organs.  
The state organs that receive such reports or accusations shall, according to law, handle the matter and keep the reporters or accusers confidential. No entity or individual shall suppress or retaliate against reporters or accusers.

### 第十章　法律责任

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　各级政府及有关部门有下列行为之一的，责令改正，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员追究行政责任：  
（一）未依照本法规定，编制、报送预算草案、预算调整方案、决算草案和部门预算、决算以及批复预算、决算的；  
（二）违反本法规定，进行预算调整的；  
（三）未依照本法规定对有关预算事项进行公开和说明的；  
（四）违反规定设立政府性基金项目和其他财政收入项目的；  
（五）违反法律、法规规定使用预算预备费、预算周转金、预算稳定调节基金、超收收入的；  
（六）违反本法规定开设财政专户的。

**Article 92** Where governments at all levels and relevant departments commit any of the following acts, they shall be ordered to make corrections, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be subject to administrative liability:  
(1) failing to prepare, submit, and approve the draft budget, budget adjustment plan, draft final accounts, and departmental budgets and final accounts in accordance with the provisions of this Law;  
(2) making budget adjustments in violation of the provisions of this Law;  
(3) failing to disclose and explain relevant budget matters in accordance with the provisions of this Law;  
(4) establishing government fund items and other fiscal revenue items in violation of regulations;  
(5) using budget reserves, budget working capital, budget stabilization and adjustment funds, and excess revenue in violation of the provisions of laws and regulations;  
(6) opening special fiscal accounts in violation of the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　各级政府及有关部门、单位有下列行为之一的，责令改正，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予降级、撤职、开除的处分：  
（一）未将所有政府收入和支出列入预算或者虚列收入和支出的；  
（二）违反法律、行政法规的规定，多征、提前征收或者减征、免征、缓征应征预算收入的；  
（三）截留、占用、挪用或者拖欠应当上缴国库的预算收入的；  
（四）违反本法规定，改变预算支出用途的；  
（五）擅自改变上级政府专项转移支付资金用途的；  
（六）违反本法规定拨付预算支出资金，办理预算收入收纳、划分、留解、退付，或者违反本法规定冻结、动用国库库款或者以其他方式支配已入国库库款的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-93" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第93条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 93** Where governments at all levels, relevant departments, or entities commit any of the following acts, they shall be ordered to make corrections, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions such as demotion, removal from office, or expulsion according to law:  
(1) failing to include all government revenues and expenditures in the budget or fabricating revenues and expenditures;  
(2) in violation of the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, excessively collecting, collecting in advance, or reducing, exempting, or deferring the collection of budget revenues that should be levied;  
(3) withholding, occupying, misappropriating, or defaulting on budget revenues that should be turned over to the state treasury;  
(4) in violation of the provisions of this Law, changing the use of budget expenditures;  
(5) without authorization, changing the use of special transfer payment funds from higher level governments;  
(6) in violation of the provisions of this Law, allocating budget expenditure funds, handling the collection, division, retention, and refund of budget revenues, or in violation of the provisions of this Law, freezing, using treasury funds, or otherwise controlling funds already deposited in the treasury.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　各级政府、各部门、各单位违反本法规定举借债务或者为他人债务提供担保，或者挪用重点支出资金，或者在预算之外及超预算标准建设楼堂馆所的，责令改正，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予撤职、开除的处分。

**Article 94** Where governments at all levels, all departments, and all entities, in violation of the provisions of this Law, incur debts or provide guarantees for the debts of others, or misappropriate funds for key expenditures, or construct office buildings and guesthouses outside the budget or in excess of budgetary standards, they shall be ordered to make corrections, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions of removal from office or expulsion.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　各级政府有关部门、单位及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，责令改正，追回骗取、使用的资金，有违法所得的没收违法所得，对单位给予警告或者通报批评；对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）违反法律、法规的规定，改变预算收入上缴方式的；  
（二）以虚报、冒领等手段骗取预算资金的；  
（三）违反规定扩大开支范围、提高开支标准的；  
（四）其他违反财政管理规定的行为。

**Article 95** Where relevant departments, entities, and their staff members of governments at all levels commit any of the following acts, they shall be ordered to make corrections, and the funds fraudulently obtained or used shall be recovered, and any illegal gains shall be confiscated; the entity shall be given a warning or criticized in a circular; and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) in violation of the provisions of laws and regulations, changing the method of turning over budget revenues;  
(2) using fraudulent reporting, false claiming, or other means to obtain budget funds;  
(3) in violation of regulations, expanding the scope of expenditures or raising expenditure standards;  
(4) other acts in violation of financial management regulations.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　[本法第九十二条](#art-92)、第九十三条、第九十四条、第九十五条所列违法行为，其他法律对其处理、处罚另有规定的，依照其规定。  
违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 96** Where there are other provisions in other laws on the handling and punishment of the illegal acts listed in Articles 92, 93, 94, and 95 of this Law, those provisions shall apply.  
Where an act in violation of the provisions of this Law constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十一章　附则

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　各级政府财政部门应当按年度编制以权责发生制为基础的政府综合财务报告，报告政府整体财务状况、运行情况和财政中长期可持续性，报本级人民代表大会常务委员会备案。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国预算法实施条例.md#art-95" title="被《中华人民共和国预算法实施条例》第95条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 97** The finance departments of governments at all levels shall, on an annual basis, prepare comprehensive government financial reports on an accrual basis, reporting on the overall financial position, operational status, and medium- to long-term fiscal sustainability of the government, and submit them to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level for record.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　国务院根据本法制定实施条例。

**Article 98** The State Council shall formulate implementing regulations in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　民族自治地方的预算管理，依照民族区域自治法的有关规定执行；民族区域自治法没有规定的，依照本法和国务院的有关规定执行。

**Article 99** The budget management of national autonomous areas shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law on Regional National Autonomy; where there are no provisions in the Law on Regional National Autonomy, it shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会或者其常务委员会根据本法，可以制定有关预算审查监督的决定或者地方性法规。

**Article 100** The people's congresses or their standing committees of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with this Law, formulate decisions or local regulations on budget examination and supervision.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　本法自1995年1月1日起施行。1991年10月21日国务院发布的《国家预算管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 101** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 1995. The Regulations on the Administration of the State Budget promulgated by the State Council on October 21, 1991 shall be abolished at the same time.
