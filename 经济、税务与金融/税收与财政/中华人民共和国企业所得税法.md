# 中华人民共和国企业所得税法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2018-12-29  
**生效日期**：2018-12-29

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　在中华人民共和国境内，企业和其他取得收入的组织（以下统称企业）为企业所得税的纳税人，依照本法的规定缴纳企业所得税。  
个人独资企业、合伙企业不适用本法。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-2" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 1** Within the territory of the People's Republic of China, enterprises and other organizations that obtain income (hereinafter collectively referred to as "enterprises") shall be taxpayers of enterprise income tax, and shall pay enterprise income tax in accordance with the provisions of this Law.  
Individual proprietorship enterprises and partnership enterprises shall not be subject to this Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　企业分为居民企业和非居民企业。  
本法所称居民企业，是指依法在中国境内成立，或者依照外国（地区）法律成立但实际管理机构在中国境内的企业。  
本法所称非居民企业，是指依照外国（地区）法律成立且实际管理机构不在中国境内，但在中国境内设立机构、场所的，或者在中国境内未设立机构、场所，但有来源于中国境内所得的企业。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-3" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第3条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-4" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第4条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-5" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第5条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-131" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第131条引用">[4]</a></sup>

**Article 2** Enterprises shall be classified into resident enterprises and non-resident enterprises.  
For the purposes of this Law, a "resident enterprise" means an enterprise that is established within the territory of China according to law, or an enterprise that is established under the laws of a foreign country (region) but whose actual management body is located within the territory of China.  
For the purposes of this Law, a "non-resident enterprise" means an enterprise that is established under the laws of a foreign country (region) and whose actual management body is not located within the territory of China, but which has established an institution or site within the territory of China, or an enterprise that, without having established an institution or site within the territory of China, has income derived from sources within the territory of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　居民企业应当就其来源于中国境内、境外的所得缴纳企业所得税。  
非居民企业在中国境内设立机构、场所的，应当就其所设机构、场所取得的来源于中国境内的所得，以及发生在中国境外但与其所设机构、场所有实际联系的所得，缴纳企业所得税。  
非居民企业在中国境内未设立机构、场所的，或者虽设立机构、场所但取得的所得与其所设机构、场所没有实际联系的，应当就其来源于中国境内的所得缴纳企业所得税。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-6" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第6条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-7" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第7条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-8" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第8条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 3** A resident enterprise shall pay enterprise income tax on its income derived from sources within and outside the territory of China.  
A non-resident enterprise that has established an institution or site within the territory of China shall pay enterprise income tax on its income derived from sources within the territory of China from such institution or site, as well as on its income derived from sources outside the territory of China that is actually connected with such institution or site.  
A non-resident enterprise that has not established an institution or site within the territory of China, or that has so established but the income obtained is not actually connected with such institution or site, shall pay enterprise income tax on its income derived from sources within the territory of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　企业所得税的税率为25％。  
非居民企业取得[本法第三条](#art-3)第三款规定的所得，适用税率为20％。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-118" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第118条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 4** The enterprise income tax rate shall be 25%.  
For the income obtained by a non-resident enterprise as specified in Paragraph 3 of Article 3 of this Law, the applicable tax rate shall be 20%.

### 第二章　应纳税所得额

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　企业每一纳税年度的收入总额，减除不征税收入、免税收入、各项扣除以及允许弥补的以前年度亏损后的余额，为应纳税所得额。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-10" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第10条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 5** The taxable income of an enterprise for each tax year shall be the total income for that year, less the non-taxable income, tax-exempt income, various deductions, and the losses of prior years that are permitted to be offset.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　企业以货币形式和非货币形式从各种来源取得的收入，为收入总额。包括：  
（一）销售货物收入；  
（二）提供劳务收入；  
（三）转让财产收入；  
（四）股息、红利等权益性投资收益；  
（五）利息收入；  
（六）租金收入；  
（七）特许权使用费收入；  
（八）接受捐赠收入；  
（九）其他收入。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-12" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第12条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-13" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第13条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-14" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第14条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-15" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第15条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-16" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第16条引用">[5]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-17" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第17条引用">[6]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-18" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第18条引用">[7]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-19" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第19条引用">[8]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-20" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第20条引用">[9]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-21" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第21条引用">[10]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-22" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第22条引用">[11]</a></sup>

**Article 6** The total income of an enterprise shall be the income obtained from various sources in monetary form and non-monetary form, including:  
(1) income from the sale of goods;  
(2) income from the provision of services;  
(3) income from the transfer of property;  
(4) income from equity investments such as dividends and bonuses;  
(5) interest income;  
(6) rental income;  
(7) income from royalties;  
(8) income from donations received;  
(9) other income.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　收入总额中的下列收入为不征税收入：  
（一）财政拨款；  
（二）依法收取并纳入财政管理的行政事业性收费、政府性基金；  
（三）国务院规定的其他不征税收入。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-26" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第26条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 7** The following income within the total income shall be non-taxable income:  
(1) fiscal appropriations;  
(2) administrative and institutional charges and government funds that are collected according to law and incorporated into fiscal management;  
(3) other non-taxable income as prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　企业实际发生的与取得收入有关的、合理的支出，包括成本、费用、税金、损失和其他支出，准予在计算应纳税所得额时扣除。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-27" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第27条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-29" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第29条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-30" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第30条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-31" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第31条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-32" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第32条引用">[5]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-33" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第33条引用">[6]</a></sup>

**Article 8** The actual and reasonable expenditures incurred by an enterprise in connection with the obtaining of income, including costs, expenses, taxes, losses, and other expenditures, shall be permitted to be deducted when calculating the taxable income.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　企业发生的公益性捐赠支出，在年度利润总额12％以内的部分，准予在计算应纳税所得额时扣除；超过年度利润总额12％的部分，准予结转以后三年内在计算应纳税所得额时扣除。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-51" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第51条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 9** The portion of public welfare donation expenditures incurred by an enterprise that is within 12% of the total annual profit shall be permitted to be deducted when calculating the taxable income; the portion exceeding 12% of the total annual profit shall be permitted to be carried forward and deducted when calculating the taxable income within the following three years.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在计算应纳税所得额时，下列支出不得扣除：  
（一）向投资者支付的股息、红利等权益性投资收益款项；  
（二）企业所得税税款；  
（三）税收滞纳金；  
（四）罚金、罚款和被没收财物的损失；  
（五）[本法第九条](#art-9)规定以外的捐赠支出；  
（六）赞助支出；  
（七）未经核定的准备金支出；  
（八）与取得收入无关的其他支出。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-54" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第54条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-55" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第55条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 10** The following expenditures shall not be deducted when calculating the taxable income:  
(1) payments on account of equity investments such as dividends and bonuses paid to investors;  
(2) enterprise income tax payments;  
(3) tax surcharges for late payment;  
(4) fines, penalties, and losses from confiscated property;  
(5) donation expenditures other than those specified in Article 9 of this Law;  
(6) sponsorship expenditures;  
(7) expenditures on unverified reserve funds;  
(8) other expenditures unrelated to the obtaining of income.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　在计算应纳税所得额时，企业按照规定计算的固定资产折旧，准予扣除。  
下列固定资产不得计算折旧扣除：  
（一）房屋、建筑物以外未投入使用的固定资产；  
（二）以经营租赁方式租入的固定资产；  
（三）以融资租赁方式租出的固定资产；  
（四）已足额提取折旧仍继续使用的固定资产；  
（五）与经营活动无关的固定资产；  
（六）单独估价作为固定资产入账的土地；  
（七）其他不得计算折旧扣除的固定资产。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-57" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第57条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 11** When calculating the taxable income, the depreciation of fixed assets calculated by an enterprise in accordance with the provisions shall be permitted to be deducted.  
Depreciation shall not be calculated and deducted for the following fixed assets:  
(1) fixed assets other than houses and buildings that are not put into use;  
(2) fixed assets leased in under an operating lease;  
(3) fixed assets leased out under a finance lease;  
(4) fixed assets that are still in use after full depreciation has been withdrawn;  
(5) fixed assets unrelated to operating activities;  
(6) land that is separately valued and recorded as a fixed asset;  
(7) other fixed assets for which depreciation shall not be calculated and deducted.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在计算应纳税所得额时，企业按照规定计算的无形资产摊销费用，准予扣除。  
下列无形资产不得计算摊销费用扣除：  
（一）自行开发的支出已在计算应纳税所得额时扣除的无形资产；  
（二）自创商誉；  
（三）与经营活动无关的无形资产；  
（四）其他不得计算摊销费用扣除的无形资产。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-65" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第65条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 12** When calculating the taxable income, the amortization expenses of intangible assets calculated by an enterprise in accordance with the provisions shall be permitted to be deducted.  
Amortization expenses shall not be calculated and deducted for the following intangible assets:  
(1) intangible assets for which the expenditure on self-development has already been deducted when calculating the taxable income;  
(2) self-created goodwill;  
(3) intangible assets unrelated to operating activities;  
(4) other intangible assets for which amortization expenses shall not be calculated and deducted.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　在计算应纳税所得额时，企业发生的下列支出作为长期待摊费用，按照规定摊销的，准予扣除：  
（一）已足额提取折旧的固定资产的改建支出；  
（二）租入固定资产的改建支出；  
（三）固定资产的大修理支出；  
（四）其他应当作为长期待摊费用的支出。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-58" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第58条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-68" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第68条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-69" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第69条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-70" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第70条引用">[4]</a></sup>

**Article 13** When calculating the taxable income, the following expenditures incurred by an enterprise, which shall be treated as long-term deferred expenses and amortized in accordance with the provisions, shall be permitted to be deducted:  
(1) expenditures on the renovation of fixed assets from which full depreciation has been withdrawn;  
(2) expenditures on the renovation of leased-in fixed assets;  
(3) major repair expenditures on fixed assets;  
(4) other expenditures that shall be treated as long-term deferred expenses.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　企业对外投资期间，投资资产的成本在计算应纳税所得额时不得扣除。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-71" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第71条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 14** During the period of outward investment by an enterprise, the cost of the investment assets shall not be deducted when calculating the taxable income.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　企业使用或者销售存货，按照规定计算的存货成本，准予在计算应纳税所得额时扣除。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-72" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第72条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 15** When an enterprise uses or sells inventory, the cost of the inventory calculated in accordance with the provisions shall be permitted to be deducted when calculating the taxable income.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　企业转让资产，该项资产的净值，准予在计算应纳税所得额时扣除。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-74" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第74条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 16** When an enterprise transfers assets, the net value of the assets shall be permitted to be deducted when calculating the taxable income.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　企业在汇总计算缴纳企业所得税时，其境外营业机构的亏损不得抵减境内营业机构的盈利。

**Article 17** When an enterprise calculates and pays enterprise income tax on a consolidated basis, the losses of its operating institutions outside the territory of China shall not be used to offset the profits of its operating institutions within the territory of China.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　企业纳税年度发生的亏损，准予向以后年度结转，用以后年度的所得弥补，但结转年限最长不得超过五年。

**Article 18** Losses incurred by an enterprise in a tax year shall be permitted to be carried forward to subsequent years and offset against the income of those subsequent years, but the carry-forward period shall not exceed five years at the maximum.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　非居民企业取得[本法第三条](#art-3)第三款规定的所得，按照下列方法计算其应纳税所得额：  
（一）股息、红利等权益性投资收益和利息、租金、特许权使用费所得，以收入全额为应纳税所得额；  
（二）转让财产所得，以收入全额减除财产净值后的余额为应纳税所得额；  
（三）其他所得，参照前两项规定的方法计算应纳税所得额。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-103" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第103条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 19** For the income obtained by a non-resident enterprise as specified in Paragraph 3 of Article 3 of this Law, its taxable income shall be calculated in accordance with the following methods:  
(1) for income from equity investments such as dividends and bonuses, and income from interest, rent, and royalties, the total amount of income shall be the taxable income;  
(2) for income from the transfer of property, the balance after deducting the net value of the property from the total amount of income shall be the taxable income;  
(3) for other income, the taxable income shall be calculated by reference to the methods prescribed in the preceding two items.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本章规定的收入、扣除的具体范围、标准和资产的税务处理的具体办法，由国务院财政、税务主管部门规定。

**Article 20** The specific scope and standards of income and deductions, as well as the specific measures for the tax treatment of assets, as provided for in this Chapter, shall be prescribed by the finance and tax administrative departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　在计算应纳税所得额时，企业财务、会计处理办法与税收法律、行政法规的规定不一致的，应当依照税收法律、行政法规的规定计算。

**Article 21** When calculating the taxable income, if the financial and accounting treatment methods of an enterprise are inconsistent with the provisions of tax laws and administrative regulations, the calculation shall be made in accordance with the provisions of tax laws and administrative regulations.

### 第三章　应纳税额

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　企业的应纳税所得额乘以适用税率，减除依照本法关于税收优惠的规定减免和抵免的税额后的余额，为应纳税额。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-76" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第76条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 22** The balance obtained after multiplying the taxable income of an enterprise by the applicable tax rate and deducting the amount of tax reduced, exempted, or credited in accordance with the provisions of this Law on tax incentives shall be the tax payable.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　企业取得的下列所得已在境外缴纳的所得税税额，可以从其当期应纳税额中抵免，抵免限额为该项所得依照本法规定计算的应纳税额；超过抵免限额的部分，可以在以后五个年度内，用每年度抵免限额抵免当年应抵税额后的余额进行抵补：  
（一）居民企业来源于中国境外的应税所得；  
（二）非居民企业在中国境内设立机构、场所，取得发生在中国境外但与该机构、场所有实际联系的应税所得。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-77" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第77条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-78" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第78条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-79" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第79条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-81" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第81条引用">[4]</a></sup>

**Article 23** The amount of enterprise income tax that an enterprise has paid outside the territory of China on the following income may be credited against its current period tax payable, with the credit limit being the amount of tax payable on such income calculated in accordance with the provisions of this Law; the portion exceeding the credit limit may be offset within the following five tax years against the balance of the credit limit for each year after deducting the amount of tax that shall be credited in that year:  
(1) taxable income derived by a resident enterprise from sources outside the territory of China;  
(2) taxable income derived by a non-resident enterprise that has established an institution or site within the territory of China from sources outside the territory of China that is actually connected with such institution or site.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　居民企业从其直接或者间接控制的外国企业分得的来源于中国境外的股息、红利等权益性投资收益，外国企业在境外实际缴纳的所得税税额中属于该项所得负担的部分，可以作为该居民企业的可抵免境外所得税税额，在[本法第二十三条](#art-23)规定的抵免限额内抵免。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-80" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第80条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 24** For a resident enterprise that receives income from equity investments such as dividends and bonuses from a foreign enterprise it directly or indirectly controls, which are derived from sources outside the territory of China, the portion of the foreign enterprise's actual enterprise income tax paid outside the territory of China that is attributable to such income may be treated as the creditable foreign income tax amount of the resident enterprise, and may be credited within the credit limit provided for in Article 23 of this Law.

### 第四章　税收优惠

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家对重点扶持和鼓励发展的产业和项目，给予企业所得税优惠。

**Article 25** The State shall grant enterprise income tax incentives to industries and projects that are key supported and encouraged for development.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　企业的下列收入为免税收入：  
（一）国债利息收入；  
（二）符合条件的居民企业之间的股息、红利等权益性投资收益；  
（三）在中国境内设立机构、场所的非居民企业从居民企业取得与该机构、场所有实际联系的股息、红利等权益性投资收益；  
（四）符合条件的非营利组织的收入。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-82" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第82条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-83" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第83条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-84" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第84条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-85" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第85条引用">[4]</a></sup>

**Article 26** The following income of an enterprise shall be tax-exempt income:  
(1) interest income from government bonds;  
(2) income from equity investments such as dividends and bonuses between eligible resident enterprises;  
(3) income from equity investments such as dividends and bonuses that is actually connected with an institution or site, obtained from a resident enterprise by a non-resident enterprise that has established an institution or site within the territory of China;  
(4) income of eligible non-profit organizations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　企业的下列所得，可以免征、减征企业所得税：  
（一）从事农、林、牧、渔业项目的所得；  
（二）从事国家重点扶持的公共基础设施项目投资经营的所得；  
（三）从事符合条件的环境保护、节能节水项目的所得；  
（四）符合条件的技术转让所得；  
（五）[本法第三条](#art-3)第三款规定的所得。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-86" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第86条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-87" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第87条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-88" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第88条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-90" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第90条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-91" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第91条引用">[5]</a></sup>

**Article 27** The following income of an enterprise may be exempted from or reduced for enterprise income tax:  
(1) income from projects in agriculture, forestry, animal husbandry, and fisheries;  
(2) income from investment and operation of public infrastructure projects that are key supported by the State;  
(3) income from projects that meet the conditions for environmental protection, energy conservation, and water conservation;  
(4) income from technology transfer that meets the conditions;  
(5) income as specified in Paragraph 3 of Article 3 of this Law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　符合条件的小型微利企业，减按20％的税率征收企业所得税。  
国家需要重点扶持的高新技术企业，减按15％的税率征收企业所得税。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-92" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第92条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-93" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第93条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 28** For eligible small low-profit enterprises, the enterprise income tax shall be levied at a reduced rate of 20%.  
For high and new technology enterprises that require key support by the State, the enterprise income tax shall be levied at a reduced rate of 15%.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　民族自治地方的自治机关对本民族自治地方的企业应缴纳的企业所得税中属于地方分享的部分，可以决定减征或者免征。自治州、自治县决定减征或者免征的，须报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-94" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第94条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 29** The autonomous authorities of national autonomous areas may decide to reduce or exempt the portion of enterprise income tax payable by enterprises in their own national autonomous areas that belongs to the local share. A decision to reduce or exempt by an autonomous prefecture or autonomous county shall be subject to the approval of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　企业的下列支出，可以在计算应纳税所得额时加计扣除：  
（一）开发新技术、新产品、新工艺发生的研究开发费用；  
（二）安置残疾人员及国家鼓励安置的其他就业人员所支付的工资。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-95" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第95条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-96" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第96条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 30** The following expenditures of an enterprise may be additionally deducted when calculating the taxable income:  
(1) research and development expenses incurred in the development of new technologies, new products, and new processes;  
(2) wages paid for employing disabled persons and other employees whose employment is encouraged by the State.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　创业投资企业从事国家需要重点扶持和鼓励的创业投资，可以按投资额的一定比例抵扣应纳税所得额。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-97" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第97条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 31** Venture capital enterprises engaged in venture capital investment that requires key support and encouragement by the State may deduct a certain percentage of their investment amount from their taxable income.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　企业的固定资产由于技术进步等原因，确需加速折旧的，可以缩短折旧年限或者采取加速折旧的方法。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-98" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第98条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 32** Where, due to technological progress or other reasons, the fixed assets of an enterprise indeed need to be depreciated at an accelerated rate, the depreciation period may be shortened or the accelerated depreciation method may be adopted.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　企业综合利用资源，生产符合国家产业政策规定的产品所取得的收入，可以在计算应纳税所得额时减计收入。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-99" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第99条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 33** The income obtained by an enterprise from comprehensively utilizing resources to produce products that comply with the provisions of the national industrial policy may be reduced when calculating the taxable income.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　企业购置用于环境保护、节能节水、安全生产等专用设备的投资额，可以按一定比例实行税额抵免。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-100" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第100条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 34** The investment amount of an enterprise in the purchase of special equipment for environmental protection, energy conservation, water conservation, and safe production may be credited against the tax payable at a certain percentage.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本法规定的税收优惠的具体办法，由国务院规定。

**Article 35** The specific measures for tax incentives provided for in this Law shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　根据国民经济和社会发展的需要，或者由于突发事件等原因对企业经营活动产生重大影响的，国务院可以制定企业所得税专项优惠政策，报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 36** Where necessary due to the needs of national economic and social development, or where significant impacts on enterprise business operations are caused by unexpected events or other reasons, the State Council may formulate special preferential policies for enterprise income tax, and shall report them to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for recordation.

### 第五章　源泉扣缴

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　对非居民企业取得[本法第三条](#art-3)第三款规定的所得应缴纳的所得税，实行源泉扣缴，以支付人为扣缴义务人。税款由扣缴义务人在每次支付或者到期应支付时，从支付或者到期应支付的款项中扣缴。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-104" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第104条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-105" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第105条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 37** For the income tax payable by a non-resident enterprise on the income obtained as specified in Paragraph 3 of Article 3 of this Law, withholding at source shall be implemented, with the payer acting as the withholding agent. The tax shall be withheld by the withholding agent from the amount paid or due to be paid at the time of each payment or when the payment becomes due.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　对非居民企业在中国境内取得工程作业和劳务所得应缴纳的所得税，税务机关可以指定工程价款或者劳务费的支付人为扣缴义务人。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-106" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第106条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 38** For the income tax payable by a non-resident enterprise on income from engineering operations and labor services obtained within the territory of China, the tax authorities may designate the payer of the engineering price or labor service fee as the withholding agent.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　依照[本法第三十七条](#art-37)、第三十八条规定应当扣缴的所得税，扣缴义务人未依法扣缴或者无法履行扣缴义务的，由纳税人在所得发生地缴纳。纳税人未依法缴纳的，税务机关可以从该纳税人在中国境内其他收入项目的支付人应付的款项中，追缴该纳税人的应纳税款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-107" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第107条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-108" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第108条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 39** Where the income tax that shall be withheld in accordance with the provisions of Articles 37 and 38 of this Law is not withheld according to law by the withholding agent, or the withholding agent is unable to perform the withholding obligation, the taxpayer shall pay the tax at the place where the income arose. If the taxpayer fails to pay the tax according to law, the tax authorities may recover the tax payable by the taxpayer from the amounts payable by the payer of other income items of the taxpayer within the territory of China.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　扣缴义务人每次代扣的税款，应当自代扣之日起七日内缴入国库，并向所在地的税务机关报送扣缴企业所得税报告表。

**Article 40** The tax withheld each time by the withholding agent shall be paid into the State Treasury within seven days from the date of withholding, and the withholding agent shall submit the withholding enterprise income tax report form to the tax authority at its location.

### 第六章　特别纳税调整

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　企业与其关联方之间的业务往来，不符合独立交易原则而减少企业或者其关联方应纳税收入或者所得额的，税务机关有权按照合理方法调整。  
企业与其关联方共同开发、受让无形资产，或者共同提供、接受劳务发生的成本，在计算应纳税所得额时应当按照独立交易原则进行分摊。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-109" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第109条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-110" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第110条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-111" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第111条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-112" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第112条引用">[4]</a></sup>

**Article 41** Where business transactions between an enterprise and its related parties do not comply with the arm's length principle and reduce the taxable income or profits of the enterprise or its related parties, the tax authorities shall have the right to make adjustments using reasonable methods.  
Where an enterprise and its related parties jointly develop or transfer intangible assets, or jointly provide or receive services, the costs incurred shall be apportioned in accordance with the arm's length principle when calculating the taxable income.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　企业可以向税务机关提出与其关联方之间业务往来的定价原则和计算方法，税务机关与企业协商、确认后，达成预约定价安排。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-113" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第113条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 42** An enterprise may propose to the tax authorities its pricing principles and calculation methods for business transactions with its related parties. After negotiation and confirmation between the tax authorities and the enterprise, a advance pricing arrangement may be reached.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　企业向税务机关报送年度企业所得税纳税申报表时，应当就其与关联方之间的业务往来，附送年度关联业务往来报告表。  
税务机关在进行关联业务调查时，企业及其关联方，以及与关联业务调查有关的其他企业，应当按照规定提供相关资料。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-114" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第114条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-122" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第122条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 43** When an enterprise submits its annual enterprise income tax return to the tax authorities, it shall attach an annual report on related party transactions with respect to its business transactions with related parties.  
When the tax authorities conduct investigations on related party transactions, the enterprise, its related parties, and other enterprises involved in the investigation shall provide relevant materials in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　企业不提供与其关联方之间业务往来资料，或者提供虚假、不完整资料，未能真实反映其关联业务往来情况的，税务机关有权依法核定其应纳税所得额。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-115" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第115条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 44** Where an enterprise fails to provide materials on its business transactions with related parties, or provides false or incomplete materials, and thus fails to truthfully reflect its related party transactions, the tax authorities shall have the right to determine its taxable income according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　由居民企业，或者由居民企业和中国居民控制的设立在实际税负明显低于[本法第四条](#art-4)第一款规定税率水平的国家（地区）的企业，并非由于合理的经营需要而对利润不作分配或者减少分配的，上述利润中应归属于该居民企业的部分，应当计入该居民企业的当期收入。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-116" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第116条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-117" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第117条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-118" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第118条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 45** Where an enterprise established in a country (region) where the actual tax burden is significantly lower than the tax rate level specified in paragraph 1 of Article 4 of this Law is controlled by a resident enterprise, or by a resident enterprise and a Chinese resident, and the profits are not distributed or are under-distributed without reasonable business needs, the portion of such profits attributable to the resident enterprise shall be included in the current income of that resident enterprise.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　企业从其关联方接受的债权性投资与权益性投资的比例超过规定标准而发生的利息支出，不得在计算应纳税所得额时扣除。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-119" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第119条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 46** No interest expense incurred by an enterprise on debt investment received from its related parties that exceeds the prescribed ratio of debt-to-equity investment may be deducted when calculating the taxable income.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　企业实施其他不具有合理商业目的的安排而减少其应纳税收入或者所得额的，税务机关有权按照合理方法调整。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-120" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第120条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 47** Where an enterprise implements other arrangements without reasonable commercial purposes and thereby reduces its taxable income or profits, the tax authorities shall have the right to make adjustments using reasonable methods.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　税务机关依照本章规定作出纳税调整，需要补征税款的，应当补征税款，并按照国务院规定加收利息。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-122" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第122条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 48** Where the tax authorities make tax adjustments in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter and additional tax is required to be collected, the tax authorities shall collect the additional tax and charge interest in accordance with the regulations of the State Council.

### 第七章　征收管理

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　企业所得税的征收管理除本法规定外，依照《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》的规定执行。

**Article 49** The collection and administration of enterprise income tax shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection, except as otherwise provided in this Law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　除税收法律、行政法规另有规定外，居民企业以企业登记注册地为纳税地点；但登记注册地在境外的，以实际管理机构所在地为纳税地点。  
居民企业在中国境内设立不具有法人资格的营业机构的，应当汇总计算并缴纳企业所得税。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-124" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第124条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 50** Except as otherwise provided by tax laws and administrative regulations, the place of tax payment for a resident enterprise shall be the place where the enterprise is registered; however, if the place of registration is outside China, the place of its actual management institution shall be the place of tax payment.  
Where a resident enterprise establishes a business establishment without legal person status within China, it shall calculate and pay enterprise income tax on a consolidated basis.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　非居民企业取得[本法第三条](#art-3)第二款规定的所得，以机构、场所所在地为纳税地点。非居民企业在中国境内设立两个或者两个以上机构、场所，符合国务院税务主管部门规定条件的，可以选择由其主要机构、场所汇总缴纳企业所得税。  
非居民企业取得[本法第三条](#art-3)第三款规定的所得，以扣缴义务人所在地为纳税地点。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-126" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第126条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 51** For a non-resident enterprise that derives income as specified in paragraph 2 of Article 3 of this Law, the place of tax payment shall be the location of its establishment or site. Where a non-resident enterprise has two or more establishments or sites within China and meets the conditions prescribed by the tax authorities under the State Council, it may elect to pay enterprise income tax on a consolidated basis through its principal establishment or site.  
For a non-resident enterprise that derives income as specified in paragraph 3 of Article 3 of this Law, the place of tax payment shall be the location of the withholding agent.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　除国务院另有规定外，企业之间不得合并缴纳企业所得税。

**Article 52** Except as otherwise provided by the State Council, enterprises may not be subject to consolidated payment of enterprise income tax.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　企业所得税按纳税年度计算。纳税年度自公历1月1日起至12月31日止。  
企业在一个纳税年度中间开业，或者终止经营活动，使该纳税年度的实际经营期不足十二个月的，应当以其实际经营期为一个纳税年度。  
企业依法清算时，应当以清算期间作为一个纳税年度。

**Article 53** Enterprise income tax shall be calculated on a tax year basis. The tax year shall begin on January 1 and end on December 31 of the Gregorian calendar.  
Where an enterprise commences operations or terminates its business activities in the middle of a tax year, resulting in an actual business period of less than twelve months in that tax year, the enterprise shall take the actual business period as a tax year.  
When an enterprise undergoes liquidation according to law, the liquidation period shall be taken as a tax year.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　企业所得税分月或者分季预缴。  
企业应当自月份或者季度终了之日起十五日内，向税务机关报送预缴企业所得税纳税申报表，预缴税款。  
企业应当自年度终了之日起五个月内，向税务机关报送年度企业所得税纳税申报表，并汇算清缴，结清应缴应退税款。  
企业在报送企业所得税纳税申报表时，应当按照规定附送财务会计报告和其他有关资料。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-127" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第127条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-128" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第128条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 54** Enterprise income tax shall be prepaid on a monthly or quarterly basis.  
An enterprise shall, within 15 days from the end of a month or quarter, submit a prepayment return for enterprise income tax to the tax authorities and prepay the tax.  
An enterprise shall, within five months from the end of a year, submit an annual enterprise income tax return to the tax authorities, carry out a final settlement, and settle the tax payable or refundable.  
When submitting the enterprise income tax return, the enterprise shall attach its financial accounting reports and other relevant materials in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　企业在年度中间终止经营活动的，应当自实际经营终止之日起六十日内，向税务机关办理当期企业所得税汇算清缴。  
企业应当在办理注销登记前，就其清算所得向税务机关申报并依法缴纳企业所得税。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-11" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第11条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 55** Where an enterprise terminates its business activities in the middle of a year, it shall, within 60 days from the date of actual termination of business, carry out a final settlement of enterprise income tax with the tax authorities for the current period.  
Before completing the cancellation of registration, the enterprise shall declare its liquidation income to the tax authorities and pay enterprise income tax according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　依照本法缴纳的企业所得税，以人民币计算。所得以人民币以外的货币计算的，应当折合成人民币计算并缴纳税款。

**Article 56** Enterprise income tax paid in accordance with this Law shall be calculated in Renminbi. Where income is calculated in a currency other than Renminbi, it shall be converted into Renminbi for calculation and tax payment.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　本法公布前已经批准设立的企业，依照当时的税收法律、行政法规规定，享受低税率优惠的，按照国务院规定，可以在本法施行后五年内，逐步过渡到本法规定的税率；享受定期减免税优惠的，按照国务院规定，可以在本法施行后继续享受到期满为止，但因未获利而尚未享受优惠的，优惠期限从本法施行年度起计算。  
法律设置的发展对外经济合作和技术交流的特定地区内，以及国务院已规定执行上述地区特殊政策的地区内新设立的国家需要重点扶持的高新技术企业，可以享受过渡性税收优惠，具体办法由国务院规定。  
国家已确定的其他鼓励类企业，可以按照国务院规定享受减免税优惠。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例.md#art-130" title="被《中华人民共和国企业所得税法实施条例》第130条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 57** Enterprises that were approved for establishment before the promulgation of this Law and enjoyed low tax rate preferences under the then-effective tax laws and administrative regulations may, in accordance with the regulations of the State Council, gradually transition to the tax rates provided for in this Law within five years after this Law comes into force; and those that enjoyed periodic tax reduction or exemption preferences may, in accordance with the regulations of the State Council, continue to enjoy such preferences until the expiration of the period. However, for enterprises that have not yet enjoyed the preferences due to lack of profitability, the preference period shall be calculated from the year this Law comes into force.  
Newly established high-tech enterprises that require key state support within specific areas designated by law for the development of foreign economic cooperation and technological exchanges, and within areas where the State Council has prescribed special policies to implement those applicable in the aforementioned areas, may enjoy transitional tax preferences. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
Other encouraged enterprises already determined by the state may enjoy tax reduction or exemption preferences in accordance with the regulations of the State Council.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　中华人民共和国政府同外国政府订立的有关税收的协定与本法有不同规定的，依照协定的规定办理。

**Article 58** Where a tax agreement concluded between the government of the People's Republic of China and a foreign government contains provisions different from those of this Law, the provisions of the agreement shall prevail.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国务院根据本法制定实施条例。

**Article 59** The State Council shall formulate implementing regulations in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　本法自2008年1月1日起施行。1991年4月9日第七届全国人民代表大会第四次会议通过的《中华人民共和国外商投资企业和外国企业所得税法》和1993年12月13日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国企业所得税暂行条例》同时废止。

**Article 60** This Law shall come into force on January 1, 2008. The Law of the People's Republic of China on Income Tax of Enterprises with Foreign Investment and Foreign Enterprises, adopted at the Fourth Session of the Seventh National People's Congress on April 9, 1991, and the Interim Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Enterprise Income Tax, promulgated by the State Council on December 13, 1993, shall be simultaneously abolished.
