### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　在中国境内有住所，或者无住所而一个纳税年度内在中国境内居住累计满一百八十三天的个人，为居民个人。居民个人从中国境内和境外取得的所得，依照本法规定缴纳个人所得税。  
在中国境内无住所又不居住，或者无住所而一个纳税年度内在中国境内居住累计不满一百八十三天的个人，为非居民个人。非居民个人从中国境内取得的所得，依照本法规定缴纳个人所得税。  
纳税年度，自公历一月一日起至十二月三十一日止。

**Article 1** An individual who has a domicile in China, or who has no domicile but has resided in China for an aggregate of 183 days or more in a tax year, shall be a resident individual. Income derived by a resident individual from within or outside China shall be subject to individual income tax in accordance with the provisions of this Law.  
An individual who has no domicile in China and does not reside in China, or who has no domicile but has resided in China for an aggregate of less than 183 days in a tax year, shall be a non-resident individual. Income derived by a non-resident individual from within China shall be subject to individual income tax in accordance with the provisions of this Law.  
A tax year shall commence on January 1 and end on December 31 of the Gregorian calendar.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　下列各项个人所得，应当缴纳个人所得税：  
（一）工资、薪金所得；  
（二）劳务报酬所得；  
（三）稿酬所得；  
（四）特许权使用费所得；  
（五）经营所得；  
（六）利息、股息、红利所得；  
（七）财产租赁所得；  
（八）财产转让所得；  
（九）偶然所得。  
居民个人取得前款第一项至第四项所得（以下称综合所得），按纳税年度合并计算个人所得税；非居民个人取得前款第一项至第四项所得，按月或者按次分项计算个人所得税。纳税人取得前款第五项至第九项所得，依照本法规定分别计算个人所得税。

**Article 2** The following categories of individual income shall be subject to individual income tax:  
(1) income from wages and salaries;  
(2) income from remuneration for personal services;  
(3) income from author's remuneration;  
(4) income from royalties;  
(5) income from business operations;  
(6) income from interest, dividends and bonuses;  
(7) income from lease of property;  
(8) income from transfer of property;  
(9) incidental income.  
A resident individual who derives income under items (1) through (4) of the preceding paragraph (hereinafter referred to as comprehensive income) shall calculate individual income tax on a consolidated basis for the tax year; a non-resident individual who derives income under items (1) through (4) of the preceding paragraph shall calculate individual income tax on a monthly or per-transaction basis by item. A taxpayer who derives income under items (5) through (9) of the preceding paragraph shall calculate individual income tax separately in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　个人所得税的税率：  
（一）综合所得，适用百分之三至百分之四十五的超额累进税率（税率表附后）；  
（二）经营所得，适用百分之五至百分之三十五的超额累进税率（税率表附后）；  
（三）利息、股息、红利所得，财产租赁所得，财产转让所得和偶然所得，适用比例税率，税率为百分之二十。

**Article 3** The tax rates for individual income tax shall be:  
(1) comprehensive income shall be subject to progressive tax rates ranging from three percent to forty-five percent (a tax rate schedule is attached);  
(2) income from business operations shall be subject to progressive tax rates ranging from five percent to thirty-five percent (a tax rate schedule is attached);  
(3) income from interest, dividends and bonuses, income from lease of property, income from transfer of property and incidental income shall be subject to a proportional tax rate of twenty percent.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　下列各项个人所得，免征个人所得税：  
（一）省级人民政府、国务院部委和中国人民解放军军以上单位，以及外国组织、国际组织颁发的科学、教育、技术、文化、卫生、体育、环境保护等方面的奖金；  
（二）国债和国家发行的金融债券利息；  
（三）按照国家统一规定发给的补贴、津贴；  
（四）福利费、抚恤金、救济金；  
（五）保险赔款；  
（六）军人的转业费、复员费、退役金；  
（七）按照国家统一规定发给干部、职工的安家费、退职费、基本养老金或者退休费、离休费、离休生活补助费；  
（八）依照有关法律规定应予免税的各国驻华使馆、领事馆的外交代表、领事官员和其他人员的所得；  
（九）中国政府参加的国际公约、签订的协议中规定免税的所得；  
（十）国务院规定的其他免税所得。  
前款第十项免税规定，由国务院报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例.md#art-9" title="被《中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例》第9条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例.md#art-10" title="被《中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例》第10条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例.md#art-11" title="被《中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例》第11条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例.md#art-12" title="被《中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例》第12条引用">[4]</a></sup>

**Article 4** The following categories of individual income shall be exempt from individual income tax:  
(1) prizes awarded by the people's governments at the provincial level, ministries and commissions of the State Council, units of the Chinese People's Liberation Army at or above the level of corps, and foreign or international organizations in the fields of science, education, technology, culture, health, sports, environmental protection, etc.;  
(2) interest on treasury bonds and on financial bonds issued by the State;  
(3) subsidies and allowances issued in accordance with the uniform provisions of the State;  
(4) welfare benefits, pensions for the disabled and survivors, and relief payments;  
(5) insurance compensation;  
(6) severance pay, demobilization pay, and retirement pay for military personnel;  
(7) settlement fees, severance pay, basic pensions or retirement pay, and living allowances for retirees and those who have left their posts, as issued to cadres and staff and workers in accordance with the uniform provisions of the State;  
(8) income of diplomatic representatives, consular officers and other personnel of foreign embassies and consulates in China that is exempt from tax in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws;  
(9) income that is exempt from tax as provided for in international conventions to which the Chinese government is a party or agreements it has signed;  
(10) other income exempt from tax as prescribed by the State Council.  
The exemption provisions in item (10) of the preceding paragraph shall be reported by the State Council to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for recordation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　有下列情形之一的，可以减征个人所得税，具体幅度和期限，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定，并报同级人民代表大会常务委员会备案：  
（一）残疾、孤老人员和烈属的所得；  
（二）因自然灾害遭受重大损失的。  
国务院可以规定其他减税情形，报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 5** Under any of the following circumstances, individual income tax may be reduced, and the specific reduction amounts and periods shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and reported to the standing committees of the people's congresses at the corresponding level for recordation:  
(1) income of disabled persons, orphans, elderly persons without family, and family members of martyrs;  
(2) income of persons who have suffered major losses due to natural disasters.  
The State Council may prescribe other circumstances for tax reduction, and shall report them to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for recordation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　应纳税所得额的计算：  
（一）居民个人的综合所得，以每一纳税年度的收入额减除费用六万元以及专项扣除、专项附加扣除和依法确定的其他扣除后的余额，为应纳税所得额。  
（二）非居民个人的工资、薪金所得，以每月收入额减除费用五千元后的余额为应纳税所得额；劳务报酬所得、稿酬所得、特许权使用费所得，以每次收入额为应纳税所得额。  
（三）经营所得，以每一纳税年度的收入总额减除成本、费用以及损失后的余额，为应纳税所得额。  
（四）财产租赁所得，每次收入不超过四千元的，减除费用八百元；四千元以上的，减除百分之二十的费用，其余额为应纳税所得额。  
（五）财产转让所得，以转让财产的收入额减除财产原值和合理费用后的余额，为应纳税所得额。  
（六）利息、股息、红利所得和偶然所得，以每次收入额为应纳税所得额。  
劳务报酬所得、稿酬所得、特许权使用费所得以收入减除百分之二十的费用后的余额为收入额。稿酬所得的收入额减按百分之七十计算。  
个人将其所得对教育、扶贫、济困等公益慈善事业进行捐赠，捐赠额未超过纳税人申报的应纳税所得额百分之三十的部分，可以从其应纳税所得额中扣除；国务院规定对公益慈善事业捐赠实行全额税前扣除的，从其规定。  
本条第一款第一项规定的专项扣除，包括居民个人按照国家规定的范围和标准缴纳的基本养老保险、基本医疗保险、失业保险等社会保险费和住房公积金等；专项附加扣除，包括子女教育、继续教育、大病医疗、住房贷款利息或者住房租金、赡养老人等支出，具体范围、标准和实施步骤由国务院确定，并报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例.md#art-13" title="被《中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例》第13条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例.md#art-14" title="被《中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例》第14条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例.md#art-15" title="被《中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例》第15条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例.md#art-16" title="被《中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例》第16条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例.md#art-19" title="被《中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例》第19条引用">[5]</a></sup>

**Article 6** The calculation of taxable income shall be:  
(1) For a resident individual, the taxable income from comprehensive income shall be the balance of the income amount for each tax year after deducting RMB 60,000 and special deductions, special additional deductions, and other deductions determined in accordance with law.  
(2) For a non-resident individual, the taxable income from wages and salaries shall be the balance of the monthly income amount after deducting RMB 5,000; the taxable income from remuneration for personal services, author's remuneration, and royalties shall be the amount of each income.  
(3) For income from business operations, the taxable income shall be the balance of the total income amount for each tax year after deducting costs, expenses, and losses.  
(4) For income from lease of property, if the income from each transaction does not exceed RMB 4,000, the deductible expense shall be RMB 800; if it exceeds RMB 4,000, twenty percent of the expenses shall be deductible, and the balance shall be the taxable income.  
(5) For income from transfer of property, the taxable income shall be the balance of the income amount from the transfer after deducting the original value of the property and reasonable expenses.  
(6) For income from interest, dividends and bonuses, and incidental income, the taxable income shall be the amount of each income.  
The income amount from remuneration for personal services, author's remuneration, and royalties shall be the balance after deducting twenty percent of the expenses from the income. The income amount from author's remuneration shall be further calculated at seventy percent.  
Where an individual makes donations from his or her income to public welfare and charitable undertakings such as education, poverty alleviation, and assistance for the needy, the portion of donations that does not exceed thirty percent of the taxable income declared by the taxpayer may be deducted from his or her taxable income; if the State Council prescribes full pre-tax deduction for donations to public welfare and charitable undertakings, such provisions shall prevail.  
The special deductions specified in item (1) of the first paragraph of this Article shall include the basic pension insurance, basic medical insurance, unemployment insurance and other social insurance premiums and housing provident funds paid by a resident individual in accordance with the scope and standards prescribed by the State; special additional deductions shall include expenditures on children's education, continuing education, serious illness medical treatment, housing loan interest or housing rent, and care for elderly parents, and the specific scope, standards, and implementation steps shall be determined by the State Council and reported to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for recordation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　居民个人从中国境外取得的所得，可以从其应纳税额中抵免已在境外缴纳的个人所得税税额，但抵免额不得超过该纳税人境外所得依照本法规定计算的应纳税额。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例.md#art-21" title="被《中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例》第21条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 7** A resident individual who derives income from outside China may credit the amount of individual income tax already paid outside China against his or her tax payable, but the credit amount shall not exceed the tax payable calculated on the income from outside China of that taxpayer in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　有下列情形之一的，税务机关有权按照合理方法进行纳税调整：  
（一）个人与其关联方之间的业务往来不符合独立交易原则而减少本人或者其关联方应纳税额，且无正当理由；  
（二）居民个人控制的，或者居民个人和居民企业共同控制的设立在实际税负明显偏低的国家（地区）的企业，无合理经营需要，对应当归属于居民个人的利润不作分配或者减少分配；  
（三）个人实施其他不具有合理商业目的的安排而获取不当税收利益。  
税务机关依照前款规定作出纳税调整，需要补征税款的，应当补征税款，并依法加收利息。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例.md#art-23" title="被《中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例》第23条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 8** Under any of the following circumstances, the tax authorities shall have the power to make tax adjustments using reasonable methods:  
(1) business transactions between an individual and a related party do not comply with the arm's length principle and result in a reduction of the tax payable of the individual or his or her related party, without justifiable reasons;  
(2) an enterprise controlled by a resident individual, or jointly controlled by a resident individual and a resident enterprise, which is established in a country (region) with a clearly lower effective tax burden, fails to distribute or reduces the distribution of profits that should be attributable to the resident individual without a reasonable business need;  
(3) an individual implements other arrangements without reasonable commercial purposes to obtain improper tax benefits.  
Where the tax authorities make tax adjustments in accordance with the preceding paragraph and additional tax needs to be collected, the additional tax shall be collected, and interest shall be added in accordance with law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　个人所得税以所得人为纳税人，以支付所得的单位或者个人为扣缴义务人。  
纳税人有中国公民身份号码的，以中国公民身份号码为纳税人识别号；纳税人没有中国公民身份号码的，由税务机关赋予其纳税人识别号。扣缴义务人扣缴税款时，纳税人应当向扣缴义务人提供纳税人识别号。

**Article 9** The individual who derives income shall be the taxpayer for individual income tax, and the unit or individual that pays the income shall be the withholding agent.  
Where the taxpayer has a Chinese citizen identity number, the Chinese citizen identity number shall be the taxpayer identification number; where the taxpayer does not have a Chinese citizen identity number, the tax authority shall assign a taxpayer identification number to him or her. When the withholding agent withholds tax, the taxpayer shall provide the taxpayer identification number to the withholding agent.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　有下列情形之一的，纳税人应当依法办理纳税申报：  
（一）取得综合所得需要办理汇算清缴；  
（二）取得应税所得没有扣缴义务人；  
（三）取得应税所得，扣缴义务人未扣缴税款；  
（四）取得境外所得；  
（五）因移居境外注销中国户籍；  
（六）非居民个人在中国境内从两处以上取得工资、薪金所得；  
（七）国务院规定的其他情形。  
扣缴义务人应当按照国家规定办理全员全额扣缴申报，并向纳税人提供其个人所得和已扣缴税款等信息。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例.md#art-26" title="被《中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例》第26条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 10** Under any of the following circumstances, the taxpayer shall file a tax return in accordance with law:  
(1) deriving comprehensive income and needing to go through the annual reconciliation;  
(2) deriving taxable income without a withholding agent;  
(3) deriving taxable income that has not been withheld by the withholding agent;  
(4) deriving income from outside China;  
(5) canceling Chinese household registration due to emigration outside China;  
(6) a non-resident individual deriving income from wages and salaries from two or more sources within China;  
(7) other circumstances prescribed by the State Council.  
The withholding agent shall handle full withholding and filing for all employees in accordance with State regulations, and shall provide the taxpayer with information on his or her individual income and the tax withheld.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　居民个人取得综合所得，按年计算个人所得税；有扣缴义务人的，由扣缴义务人按月或者按次预扣预缴税款；需要办理汇算清缴的，应当在取得所得的次年三月一日至六月三十日内办理汇算清缴。预扣预缴办法由国务院税务主管部门制定。  
居民个人向扣缴义务人提供专项附加扣除信息的，扣缴义务人按月预扣预缴税款时应当按照规定予以扣除，不得拒绝。  
非居民个人取得工资、薪金所得，劳务报酬所得，稿酬所得和特许权使用费所得，有扣缴义务人的，由扣缴义务人按月或者按次代扣代缴税款，不办理汇算清缴。

**Article 11** Where a resident individual derives comprehensive income, the individual income tax shall be calculated on an annual basis; if there is a withholding agent, the withholding agent shall withhold and remit the tax on a monthly or per-transaction basis in advance; if an annual reconciliation is required, it shall be handled within the period from March 1 to June 30 of the year following the year in which the income is derived. The detailed measures for advance withholding and remittance shall be formulated by the competent tax authority under the State Council.  
Where a resident individual provides information on special additional deductions to the withholding agent, the withholding agent shall make the deductions in accordance with the regulations when withholding and remitting tax on a monthly basis, and shall not refuse to do so.  
Where a non-resident individual derives income from wages and salaries, remuneration for personal services, author's remuneration, and royalties, and there is a withholding agent, the withholding agent shall withhold and remit the tax on a monthly or per-transaction basis, and no annual reconciliation shall be handled.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　纳税人取得经营所得，按年计算个人所得税，由纳税人在月度或者季度终了后十五日内向税务机关报送纳税申报表，并预缴税款；在取得所得的次年三月三十一日前办理汇算清缴。  
纳税人取得利息、股息、红利所得，财产租赁所得，财产转让所得和偶然所得，按月或者按次计算个人所得税，有扣缴义务人的，由扣缴义务人按月或者按次代扣代缴税款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/对储蓄存款利息所得征收个人所得税的实施办法.md#art-1" title="被《对储蓄存款利息所得征收个人所得税的实施办法》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 12** Where a taxpayer derives income from business operations, the individual income tax shall be calculated on an annual basis, and the taxpayer shall submit a tax return to the tax authority and make advance tax payment within fifteen days after the end of each month or quarter; the annual reconciliation shall be handled before March 31 of the year following the year in which the income is derived.  
Where a taxpayer derives income from interest, dividends and bonuses, income from lease of property, income from transfer of property, and incidental income, the individual income tax shall be calculated on a monthly or per-transaction basis, and if there is a withholding agent, the withholding agent shall withhold and remit the tax on a monthly or per-transaction basis.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　纳税人取得应税所得没有扣缴义务人的，应当在取得所得的次月十五日内向税务机关报送纳税申报表，并缴纳税款。  
纳税人取得应税所得，扣缴义务人未扣缴税款的，纳税人应当在取得所得的次年六月三十日前，缴纳税款；税务机关通知限期缴纳的，纳税人应当按照期限缴纳税款。  
居民个人从中国境外取得所得的，应当在取得所得的次年三月一日至六月三十日内申报纳税。  
非居民个人在中国境内从两处以上取得工资、薪金所得的，应当在取得所得的次月十五日内申报纳税。  
纳税人因移居境外注销中国户籍的，应当在注销中国户籍前办理税款清算。

**Article 13** Where a taxpayer derives taxable income without a withholding agent, the taxpayer shall submit a tax return to the tax authority and pay tax within fifteen days of the month following the month in which the income is derived.  
Where a taxpayer derives taxable income but the withholding agent fails to withhold tax, the taxpayer shall pay the tax before June 30 of the year following the year in which the income is derived; if the tax authority notifies the taxpayer to pay within a specified time limit, the taxpayer shall pay the tax within that time limit.  
Where a resident individual derives income from outside China, he or she shall file a tax return and pay tax within the period from March 1 to June 30 of the year following the year in which the income is derived.  
Where a non-resident individual derives income from wages and salaries from two or more sources within China, he or she shall file a tax return and pay tax within fifteen days of the month following the month in which the income is derived.  
Where a taxpayer cancels Chinese household registration due to emigration outside China, he or she shall settle the tax before canceling the Chinese household registration.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　扣缴义务人每月或者每次预扣、代扣的税款，应当在次月十五日内缴入国库，并向税务机关报送扣缴个人所得税申报表。  
纳税人办理汇算清缴退税或者扣缴义务人为纳税人办理汇算清缴退税的，税务机关审核后，按照国库管理的有关规定办理退税。

**Article 14** The tax withheld or remitted in advance by a withholding agent on a monthly or per-transaction basis shall be paid into the State Treasury within fifteen days of the following month, and a withholding return for individual income tax shall be submitted to the tax authority.  
Where a taxpayer applies for a tax refund from the annual reconciliation, or the withholding agent applies for a tax refund on behalf of the taxpayer, after examination by the tax authority, the refund shall be processed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Treasury management.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　公安、人民银行、金融监督管理等相关部门应当协助税务机关确认纳税人的身份、金融账户信息。教育、卫生、医疗保障、民政、人力资源社会保障、住房城乡建设、公安、人民银行、金融监督管理等相关部门应当向税务机关提供纳税人子女教育、继续教育、大病医疗、住房贷款利息、住房租金、赡养老人等专项附加扣除信息。  
个人转让不动产的，税务机关应当根据不动产登记等相关信息核验应缴的个人所得税，登记机构办理转移登记时，应当查验与该不动产转让相关的个人所得税的完税凭证。个人转让股权办理变更登记的，市场主体登记机关应当查验与该股权交易相关的个人所得税的完税凭证。  
有关部门依法将纳税人、扣缴义务人遵守本法的情况纳入信用信息系统，并实施联合激励或者惩戒。

**Article 15** The public security, people's bank, financial supervision and administration, and other relevant departments shall assist the tax authorities in verifying the taxpayer's identity and financial account information. The education, health, medical security, civil affairs, human resources and social security, housing and urban-rural construction, public security, people's bank, financial supervision and administration, and other relevant departments shall provide the tax authorities with information on the taxpayer's special additional deductions for children's education, continuing education, serious illness medical treatment, housing loan interest, housing rent, care for elderly parents, etc.  
Where an individual transfers immovable property, the tax authority shall verify the individual income tax payable based on immovable property registration and other relevant information, and the registration agency shall, when handling the transfer registration, check the tax payment certificate for individual income tax related to the transfer of the immovable property. Where an individual transfers equity and handles change registration, the market entity registration authority shall check the tax payment certificate for individual income tax related to the equity transaction.  
The relevant departments shall, in accordance with law, incorporate the compliance of taxpayers and withholding agents with this Law into the credit information system, and implement joint incentives or disciplinary measures.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　各项所得的计算，以人民币为单位。所得为人民币以外的货币的，按照人民币汇率中间价折合成人民币缴纳税款。

**Article 16** The computation of all categories of income shall be in Renminbi. If income is in a currency other than Renminbi, it shall be converted into Renminbi at the central parity rate for tax payment.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对扣缴义务人按照所扣缴的税款，付给百分之二的手续费。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例.md#art-33" title="被《中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例》第33条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 17** A handling fee of two percent of the tax withheld shall be paid to the withholding agent.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对储蓄存款利息所得开征、减征、停征个人所得税及其具体办法，由国务院规定，并报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 18** The imposition, reduction, or exemption of individual income tax on interest income from savings deposits, and the specific measures therefor, shall be prescribed by the State Council and reported to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for recordation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　纳税人、扣缴义务人和税务机关及其工作人员违反本法规定的，依照《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》和有关法律法规的规定追究法律责任。

**Article 19** Where a taxpayer, withholding agent, or tax authority or its staff member violates the provisions of this Law, legal liability shall be pursued in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection and relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　个人所得税的征收管理，依照本法和《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》的规定执行。

**Article 20** The collection and administration of individual income tax shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国务院根据本法制定实施条例。

**Article 21** The State Council shall formulate implementing regulations in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本法自公布之日起施行。
