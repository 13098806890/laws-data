**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：经济法

**发文字号**：法释〔2004〕18号

### 最高人民法院关于审理出口退税托管帐户质押贷款案件有关问题的规定

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于审理出口退税托管帐户质押贷款案件有关问题的规定  
  
法释〔2004〕18号  
  
为正确审理涉及出口退税专用帐户质押贷款纠纷案件，维护相关当事人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》、《中华人民共和国合同法》、《中华人民共和国担保法》等有关规定，结合人民法院审判实践，制定本规定。  
  
第一条 本规定适用于审理、执行涉及出口退税专用帐户质押贷款的案件。  
  
本规定所称出口退税专用帐户质押贷款，是指借款人将出口退税专用帐户托管给贷款银行，并承诺以该帐户中的退税款作为还款保证的贷款。  
  
第二条 以出口退税专用帐户质押方式贷款的，应当签订书面质押贷款合同。质押贷款合同自贷款银行实际托管借款人出口退税专用帐户时生效。  
  
第三条 出口退税专用帐户质押贷款银行，对质押帐户内的退税款享有优先受偿权。  
  
第四条 人民法院审理和执行案件时，不得对已设质的出口退税专用帐户内的款项采取财产保全措施或者执行措施。  
  
第五条 借款人进入破产程序时，贷款银行对已经设质的出口退税专用帐户内的款项享有优先受偿权，但应以被担保债权尚未受偿的数额为限。  
  
第六条 有下列情形之一的，不受本《规定》第三、四、五条规定的限制，人民法院可以采取财产保全或者执行措施：  
  
（一）借款人将非退税款存入出口退税专用帐户的；  
  
（二）贷款银行将出口退税专用帐户内的退税款扣还其他贷款，且数额已经超出质押贷款金额的；  
  
（三）贷款银行同意税务部门转移出口退税专用帐户的；  
  
（四）贷款银行有其他违背退税帐户专用性质，损害其他债权人利益行为的。  
  
第七条 本规定自公布之日起施行。

**Article 1** Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Trial of Cases Involving Pledge Loans on Export Tax Rebate Custody Accounts  
  
Supreme People's Court  
  
Provisions on Several Issues Concerning the Trial of Cases Involving Pledge Loans on Export Tax Rebate Custody Accounts  
  
Fa Shi [2004] No. 18  
  
These Provisions are formulated in accordance with the General Principles of the Civil Law of the People's Republic of China, the Contract Law of the People's Republic of China, the Security Law of the People's Republic of China, and other relevant regulations, in light of the trial practices of the people's courts, for the purpose of correctly adjudicating disputes arising from pledge loans on export tax rebate special accounts and protecting the lawful rights and interests of the relevant parties.  
  
Article 1 These Provisions shall apply to the trial and enforcement of cases involving pledge loans on export tax rebate special accounts.  
  
The term "pledge loan on export tax rebate special account" as used in these Provisions refers to a loan in which the borrower entrusts the export tax rebate special account to the lending bank for custody and promises to use the tax rebate funds in such account as a guarantee for repayment.  
  
Article 2 Where a loan is made by means of pledging an export tax rebate special account, a written pledge loan contract shall be concluded. The pledge loan contract shall become effective when the lending bank actually takes custody of the borrower's export tax rebate special account.  
  
Article 3 The lending bank of a pledge loan on an export tax rebate special account shall have the priority right to be repaid from the tax rebate funds in the pledged account.  
  
Article 4 When trying or enforcing a case, the people's court shall not adopt property preservation measures or enforcement measures against the funds in an export tax rebate special account that has been pledged.  
  
Article 5 Where the borrower enters into bankruptcy proceedings, the lending bank shall have the priority right to be repaid from the funds in the pledged export tax rebate special account, but only to the extent of the amount of the secured claim that has not yet been satisfied.  
  
Article 6 Under any of the following circumstances, the restrictions under Articles 3, 4, and 5 of these Provisions shall not apply, and the people's court may adopt property preservation or enforcement measures:  
  
(1) The borrower deposits non-tax-rebate funds into the export tax rebate special account;  
  
(2) The lending bank deducts the tax rebate funds in the export tax rebate special account to repay other loans, and the amount has exceeded the amount of the pledged loan;  
  
(3) The lending bank agrees to the transfer of the export tax rebate special account by the tax authority;  
  
(4) The lending bank engages in other acts that violate the special nature of the tax rebate account and harm the interests of other creditors.  
  
Article 7 These Provisions shall come into effect as of the date of promulgation.
