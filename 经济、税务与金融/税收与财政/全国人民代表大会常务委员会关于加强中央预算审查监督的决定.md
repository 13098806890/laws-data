# 全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于加强中央预算审查监督的决定

**分类**：有关法律问题和重大问题的决定（部分）  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2021-04-29  
**生效日期**：2021-04-29

> （1999年12月25日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十三次会议通过　2021年4月29日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十八次会议修订） 为履行宪法法律赋予全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会的预算审查监督职责，贯彻落实党中央关于加强人大预算决算审查监督职能的部署要求，推进全面依法治国，健全完善中国特色社会主义预算审查监督制度，规范预算行为，提高预算绩效，厉行节约，更好地发挥中央预算在推进国家治理体系和治理能力现代化、推动高质量发展、促进社会进步、改善人民生活和全面深化改革开放中的重要作用，必须进一步加强对中央预算的审查监督。为此，特作如下决定： 一、加强全口径审查和全过程监管。全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会对政府预算决算开展全口径审查和全过程监管，坚持党中央集中统一领导，坚持围绕服务党和国家工作大局，坚持以人民为中心，坚持依法审查监督，聚焦重点，注重实效，保障宪法和法律贯彻实施，保障国家方针政策和决策部署贯彻落实。 （一）加强财政政策审查监督。审查监督重点包括：财政政策贯彻落实国家方针政策和决策部署的情况；与经济社会发展目标和宏观调控总体要求相衔接的情况；加强中期财政规划管理工作，对国家重大战略任务保障的情况；财政政策制定过程中充分听取人大代表与社会各界意见建议的情况；财政政策的合理性、可行性、可持续性等情况。 （二）加强一般公共预算审查监督。审查监督一般公共预算支出总量和结构的重点包括：支出总量和结构贯彻落实国家方针政策和决策部署的情况；支出总量及其增减的情况，财政赤字规模及其占年度预计国内生产总值比重的情况；调整优化支出结构，严格控制一般性支出，提高财政资金配置效率和使用绩效等情况。 审查监督重点支出与重大投资项目的重点包括：重点支出预算和支出政策相衔接的情况；重点支出规模变化和结构优化的情况；重点支出决策论证、政策目标和绩效的情况。重大投资项目与国民经济和社会发展规划相衔接的情况；重大投资项目决策论证、投资安排和实施效果的情况。 审查监督部门预算的重点包括：部门各项收支全部纳入预算的情况；部门预算与支出政策、部门职责衔接匹配的情况；项目库建设情况；部门重点项目预算安排和绩效的情况；新增资产配置情况；结转资金使用情况；审计查出问题整改落实等情况。 审查监督中央对地方转移支付的重点包括：各类转移支付保障中央财政承担的财政事权和支出责任的情况；促进地区间财力均衡及增强基层公共服务保障能力的情况；健全规范转移支付制度、优化转移支付结构的情况；专项转移支付定期评估和退出的情况；转移支付预算下达和使用的情况；转移支付绩效的情况。 审查监督一般公共预算收入的重点包括：预算收入安排与经济社会发展目标、国家宏观调控总体要求相适应的情况；各项税收收入与对应税基相协调的情况；预算收入依法依规征收、真实完整的情况；预算收入结构优化、质量提高的情况；依法规范非税收入管理等情况。 （三）加强政府债务审查监督。审查监督中央政府债务重点包括：根据中央财政赤字规模和上年末国债余额限额，科学确定当年国债余额限额，合理控制国债余额与限额之间的差额；评估政府债务风险水平情况，推进实现稳增长和防风险的长期均衡。审查监督地方政府债务重点包括：地方政府债务纳入预算管理的情况；根据债务率、利息支出率等指标评估地方政府债务风险水平，审查地方政府新增一般债务限额和专项债务限额的合理性情况；地方政府专项债务偿还的情况；积极稳妥化解地方政府债务风险等情况。 （四）加强政府性基金预算审查监督。审查监督重点包括：基金项目设立、征收、使用和期限符合法律法规规定的情况；收支政策和预算安排的合理性、可行性、可持续性的情况；政府性基金支出使用情况；政府性基金项目绩效和评估调整等情况。 （五）加强国有资本经营预算审查监督。审查监督重点包括：预算范围完整、制度规范的情况；国有资本足额上缴收益和产权转让等收入的情况；支出使用方向和项目符合法律法规规定和政策的情况；国有资本经营预算调入一般公共预算的情况；政府投资基金管理的情况；发挥优化国有资本布局、与国资国企改革相衔接等情况。 （六）加强社会保险基金预算审查监督。审查监督重点包括：各项基金收支安排、财政补助和预算平衡的情况；预算安排贯彻落实社会保障政策的情况；推进基本养老保险全国统筹的情况；基金绩效和运营投资的情况；中长期收支预测及可持续运行等情况。 （七）进一步推进预算决算公开，提高预算决算透明度。以公开为常态、不公开为例外，监督中央政府及其部门依法及时公开预算决算信息，主动回应社会普遍关注的问题，接受社会监督。 二、加强中央预算编制的监督工作。坚持先有预算、后有支出、严格按预算支出的原则，细化预算和提前编制预算。按预算法规定的时间将中央预算草案全部编制完毕。中央预算应当按照宪法和法律规定，贯彻落实国家方针政策和决策部署，做到政策明确、标准科学、安排合理，增强可读性和可审性。

**Article 1** I. Strengthening comprehensive review and whole-process oversight. The National People's Congress and its Standing Committee shall conduct comprehensive review and whole-process oversight of government budgets and final accounts, adhere to the centralized and unified leadership of the Party Central Committee, focus on serving the overall work of the Party and the state, take the people as the center, conduct review and oversight according to law, focus on key points, emphasize practical results, ensure the implementation of the Constitution and laws, and ensure the implementation of the state's policies, decisions, and arrangements.  
(1) Strengthening review and oversight of fiscal policies. The key points of review and oversight include: the implementation of state policies, decisions, and arrangements by fiscal policies; the alignment with economic and social development goals and the overall requirements of macro-control; the enhancement of medium-term fiscal planning management and the guarantee of major national strategic tasks; the full solicitation of opinions and suggestions from deputies to the National People's Congress and all sectors of society during the formulation of fiscal policies; and the reasonableness, feasibility, and sustainability of fiscal policies.  
(2) Strengthening review and oversight of general public budgets. The key points of review and oversight of the total amount and structure of general public budget expenditures include: the implementation of state policies, decisions, and arrangements by the total amount and structure of expenditures; the total amount of expenditures and its increase or decrease, the size of the fiscal deficit and its proportion to the annual estimated gross domestic product; the adjustment and optimization of the expenditure structure, strict control over general expenditures, and improvement of the efficiency of fiscal fund allocation and use performance.  
The key points of review and oversight of key expenditures and major investment projects include: the alignment of key expenditure budgets with expenditure policies; changes in the scale and optimization of the structure of key expenditures; the decision-making justification, policy objectives, and performance of key expenditures. The alignment of major investment projects with the national economic and social development plan; the decision-making justification, investment arrangement, and implementation effects of major investment projects.  
The key points of review and oversight of departmental budgets include: the inclusion of all revenues and expenditures of each department into the budget; the alignment and matching of departmental budgets with expenditure policies and departmental functions; the construction of project pools; the budget arrangements and performance of key departmental projects; the allocation of new assets; the use of carry-over funds; and the rectification and implementation of issues identified in audits.  
The key points of review and oversight of central-to-local transfer payments include: the guarantee by various types of transfer payments of the fiscal powers and expenditure responsibilities borne by the central finance; the promotion of balanced financial resources among regions and the enhancement of the capacity of basic public services at the grassroots level; the improvement and standardization of the transfer payment system and optimization of the transfer payment structure; the periodic evaluation and withdrawal of special transfer payments; the issuance and use of transfer payment budgets; and the performance of transfer payments.  
The key points of review and oversight of general public budget revenues include: the adaptation of budget revenue arrangements to economic and social development goals and the overall requirements of national macro-control; the coordination of various tax revenues with corresponding tax bases; the collection of budget revenues in accordance with laws and regulations, and their truthfulness and completeness; the optimization of the budget revenue structure and improvement of quality; and the management of non-tax revenues according to law.  
(3) Strengthening review and oversight of government debt. The key points of review and oversight of central government debt include: scientifically determining the balance limit of national debt for the current year based on the central fiscal deficit scale and the balance limit of national debt at the end of the previous year, and reasonably controlling the difference between the balance and the limit of national debt; assessing the level of government debt risk, and promoting the long-term balance between stable growth and risk prevention. The key points of review and oversight of local government debt include: the inclusion of local government debt into budget management; assessing the level of local government debt risk based on indicators such as debt ratio and interest expenditure ratio, and reviewing the reasonableness of the new general debt limit and special debt limit for local governments; the repayment of local government special debt; and the active and steady resolution of local government debt risks.  
(4) Strengthening review and oversight of government-managed fund budgets. The key points of review and oversight include: the establishment, collection, use, and duration of fund projects in compliance with laws and regulations; the reasonableness, feasibility, and sustainability of revenue and expenditure policies and budget arrangements; the use of government-managed fund expenditures; and the performance, evaluation, and adjustment of government-managed fund projects.  
(5) Strengthening review and oversight of state capital management budgets. The key points of review and oversight include: the completeness of the budget scope and standardized systems; the full contribution of state capital income and income from property rights transfers; the compliance of expenditure use directions and projects with laws, regulations, and policies; the transfer of state capital management budgets into general public budgets; the management of government investment funds; and the optimization of the layout of state capital and alignment with the reform of state-owned assets and enterprises.  
(6) Strengthening review and oversight of social insurance fund budgets. The key points of review and oversight include: the arrangement of revenues and expenditures of various funds, fiscal subsidies, and budget balance; the implementation of social security policies through budget arrangements; the promotion of national pooling of basic old-age insurance; fund performance and operational investment; and medium- and long-term revenue and expenditure forecasts and sustainable operation.  
(7) Further promoting the disclosure of budgets and final accounts to enhance transparency. With disclosure as the norm and non-disclosure as the exception, supervise the central government and its departments to disclose budget and final account information in a timely manner according to law, proactively respond to issues of general public concern, and accept social supervision.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、加强中央预算编制的监督工作。坚持先有预算、后有支出、严格按预算支出的原则，细化预算和提前编制预算。按预算法规定的时间将中央预算草案全部编制完毕。中央预算应当按照宪法和法律规定，贯彻落实国家方针政策和决策部署，做到政策明确、标准科学、安排合理，增强可读性和可审性。  
中央一般公共预算草案，应当列示预算收支情况表、转移支付预算表、基本建设支出表、政府债务情况表等，说明收支预算安排及转移支付绩效目标情况。中央政府性基金预算草案应当按基金项目分别编列、分别说明。政府性基金支出编列到资金使用的具体项目，说明结转结余和绩效目标情况。中央国有资本经营预算草案收入编列到行业或企业，说明纳入预算的企业单位的上年总体经营财务状况；支出编列到使用方向和用途，说明项目安排的依据和绩效目标。中央社会保险基金预算草案应当按保险项目编制，反映基本养老保险全国统筹推进情况，说明社会保险基金可持续运行情况。

**Article 2** II. Strengthening oversight of central budget preparation. Adhere to the principles of having the budget first, then expenditures, and strictly implementing expenditures according to the budget; refine the budget and prepare it in advance. Complete the entire central budget draft within the time limits prescribed by the Budget Law. The central budget shall, in accordance with the Constitution and laws, implement state policies, decisions, and arrangements, ensuring clear policies, scientific standards, and reasonable arrangements, and enhancing readability and auditability.  
The draft central general public budget shall include statements on budget revenue and expenditure, transfer payment budgets, basic construction expenditure statements, and government debt statements, and shall explain the budget revenue and expenditure arrangements and the performance targets for transfer payments. The draft central government-managed fund budget shall be prepared and explained separately for each fund item. Government-managed fund expenditures shall be itemized to specific projects for the use of funds, explaining carry-over balances and performance targets. The draft central state capital management budget shall have revenues itemized by industry or enterprise, explaining the overall operating financial status of the previous year for the enterprise units included in the budget; expenditures shall be itemized by use direction and purpose, explaining the basis for project arrangements and performance targets. The draft central social insurance fund budget shall be prepared by insurance item, reflecting the progress of national pooling of basic old-age insurance and explaining the sustainable operation of social insurance funds.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、加强和改善中央预算的初步审查工作。国务院财政部门应当及时向全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会和全国人民代表大会常务委员会预算工作委员会通报有关中央预算编制的情况。预算工作委员会应当结合听取全国人大代表和社会各界意见建议情况，与国务院财政等部门密切沟通，研究提出关于年度预算的分析报告。在全国人民代表大会会议举行的四十五日前，国务院财政部门应当将中央预算草案初步方案提交财政经济委员会，由财政经济委员会对中央预算草案初步方案进行初步审查，并就有关重点问题开展专题审议，提出初步审查意见。  
财政经济委员会开展初步审查阶段，全国人民代表大会有关专门委员会围绕国家方针政策和决策部署，对相关领域部门预算初步方案、转移支付资金和政策开展专项审查，提出专项审查意见。专项审查意见中增加相关支出预算的建议，应当与减少其他支出预算的建议同时提出，以保持预算的平衡性、完整性和统一性。有关专门委员会的专项审查意见，送财政经济委员会、预算工作委员会研究处理，必要时作为初步审查意见的附件印发全国人民代表大会会议。

**Article 3** III. Strengthening and improving the preliminary review of the central budget. The fiscal department of the State Council shall promptly inform the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee of the National People's Congress and the Budget Affairs Commission of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress of the situation concerning the preparation of the central budget. The Budget Affairs Commission shall, based on listening to the opinions and suggestions of deputies to the National People's Congress and all sectors of society, closely communicate with the fiscal and other departments of the State Council, and study and propose an analysis report on the annual budget. Forty-five days before the convening of the session of the National People's Congress, the fiscal department of the State Council shall submit the preliminary plan of the central budget draft to the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee, which shall conduct a preliminary review of the preliminary plan of the central budget draft, carry out special deliberation on relevant key issues, and put forward preliminary review opinions.  
During the preliminary review stage conducted by the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee, the relevant special committees of the National People's Congress shall, focusing on state policies, decisions, and arrangements, conduct special reviews of the preliminary plans of departmental budgets in related fields, transfer payment funds, and policies, and put forward special review opinions. In the special review opinions, suggestions to increase relevant expenditure budgets shall be put forward simultaneously with suggestions to reduce other expenditure budgets, so as to maintain the balance, completeness, and unity of the budget. The special review opinions of the relevant special committees shall be sent to the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee and the Budget Affairs Commission for study and processing, and when necessary, shall be attached to the preliminary review opinions and distributed at the session of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、加强中央预算执行情况的监督工作。在全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会领导下，财政经济委员会和预算工作委员会应当做好有关工作。国务院有关部门应当及时向财政经济委员会、预算工作委员会提交落实全国人民代表大会关于预算决议的情况。国务院财政部门应当定期提供全国、中央和地方的预算执行报表，反映预算收支、政府债务等相关情况。国务院有关部门应当通过国家电子政务网等平台，定期提供部门预算执行、宏观经济、金融、审计、税务、海关、社会保障、国有资产等方面政策制度和数据信息。  
全国人民代表大会常务委员会通过听取和审议专项工作报告、执法检查、专题调研等监督方式，加强对重点收支政策贯彻实施、重点领域财政资金分配和使用、重大财税改革和政策调整、重大投资项目落实情况的监督。国务院在每年八月向全国人民代表大会常务委员会报告当年预算执行情况。国务院财政部门及相关主管部门每季度提供预算执行、有关政策实施和重点项目进展情况。  
全国人民代表大会常务委员会利用现代信息技术开展预算联网监督，提高预算审查监督效能，实现预算审查监督的网络化、智能化。对预算联网监督发现的问题，适时向国务院有关部门通报，有关部门应当核实处理并反馈处理情况。

**Article 4** IV. Strengthening oversight of central budget implementation. Under the leadership of the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee, the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee and the Budget Affairs Commission shall perform relevant duties. The relevant departments of the State Council shall promptly submit to the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee and the Budget Affairs Commission the situation concerning the implementation of the budget resolution of the National People's Congress. The fiscal department of the State Council shall regularly provide statements on the implementation of the national, central, and local budgets, reflecting the budget revenues and expenditures, government debt, and other relevant information. The relevant departments of the State Council shall, through platforms such as the national e-government network, regularly provide policy systems and data information on departmental budget implementation, macroeconomics, finance, audit, taxation, customs, social security, and state-owned assets.  
The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall strengthen oversight of the implementation of key revenue and expenditure policies, the allocation and use of fiscal funds in key areas, major fiscal and tax reforms and policy adjustments, and the implementation of major investment projects through means such as hearing and deliberating special work reports, law enforcement inspections, and special research. In August each year, the State Council shall report to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on the budget implementation for that year. The fiscal department of the State Council and relevant competent departments shall provide, on a quarterly basis, information on budget implementation, the implementation of relevant policies, and the progress of key projects.  
The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall use modern information technology to carry out online budget oversight, improve the efficiency of budget review and oversight, and achieve networked and intelligent budget review and oversight. Issues discovered through online budget oversight shall be communicated to the relevant departments of the State Council in a timely manner, and the relevant departments shall verify, handle, and report on the handling situation.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、加强中央预算调整方案的审查工作。中央预算执行中，农业、教育、科技、社会保障等重点领域支出的调减，新增发行特别国债，增加地方政府举借债务规模，须经全国人民代表大会常务委员会审查和批准。中央预算执行中必须作出预算调整的，国务院应当编制中央预算调整方案，一般于当年六月至十月期间提交全国人民代表大会常务委员会。严格控制预算调剂，各部门、各单位的预算支出应当按照预算执行，因重大事项确需调剂的，严格按照规定程序办理。中央预算执行中出台重要的增加财政收入或者支出的政策措施，调入全国社会保障基金，或者预算收支结构发生重要变化的情况，国务院财政部门应当及时向预算工作委员会通报。预算工作委员会及时将有关情况向财政经济委员会通报，必要时向全国人民代表大会常务委员会报告。

**Article 5** V. Strengthening the review of central budget adjustment plans. During the implementation of the central budget, reductions in expenditures in key areas such as agriculture, education, science and technology, and social security, new issuance of special government bonds, and increases in the scale of local government borrowing shall be subject to review and approval by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress. If a budget adjustment must be made during the implementation of the central budget, the State Council shall prepare a central budget adjustment plan, generally submitted to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress between June and October of the current year. Strictly control budget transfers. Budget expenditures of each department and unit shall be implemented according to the budget. If a transfer is truly necessary due to major matters, it shall be handled strictly in accordance with the prescribed procedures. If important policy measures to increase fiscal revenues or expenditures are introduced during the implementation of the central budget, funds are transferred into the National Social Security Fund, or significant changes occur in the structure of budget revenues and expenditures, the fiscal department of the State Council shall promptly inform the Budget Affairs Commission. The Budget Affairs Commission shall promptly inform the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee of the relevant situation, and when necessary, report to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、加强中央决算的审查工作。中央决算草案应当按照全国人民代表大会批准的预算所列科目编制，按预算数、调整预算数以及决算数分别列出，对重要变化应当作出说明。一般公共预算支出应当按功能分类编列到项，按经济性质分类编列到款。政府性基金预算支出、国有资本经营预算支出、社会保险基金预算支出，应当按功能分类编列到项。按照国务院规定实行权责发生制的特定事项，在审查中央决算草案前向全国人民代表大会常务委员会报告。中央决算草案应当在全国人民代表大会常务委员会举行会议审查和批准的三十日前，提交财政经济委员会，由财政经济委员会结合审计工作报告进行初步审查。

**Article 6** VI. Strengthening the review of central final accounts. The draft central final accounts shall be prepared in accordance with the headings listed in the budget approved by the National People's Congress, showing separately the budgeted figures, adjusted budget figures, and actual final account figures, and explanations shall be provided for significant changes. General public budget expenditures shall be itemized by functional classification to the item level and by economic nature to the section level. Government-managed fund budget expenditures, state capital management budget expenditures, and social insurance fund budget expenditures shall be itemized by functional classification to the item level. Specific matters for which the accrual basis is applied pursuant to the regulations of the State Council shall be reported to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress before the review of the draft central final accounts. The draft central final accounts shall be submitted to the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee thirty days before the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress meets for review and approval, and the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee shall conduct a preliminary review in conjunction with the audit work report.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、加强预算绩效的审查监督工作。各部门、各单位应当实施全面预算绩效管理，强化事前绩效评估，严格绩效目标管理，完善预算绩效指标体系，提升绩效评价质量。加强绩效评价结果运用，促进绩效评价结果与完善政策、安排预算和改进管理相结合，推进预算绩效信息公开，将重要绩效评价结果与决算草案同步报送全国人民代表大会常务委员会审查。全国人民代表大会常务委员会加强对重点支出和重大项目绩效目标、绩效评价结果的审查监督。必要时，召开预算绩效听证会。

**Article 7** VII. Strengthening the review and oversight of budget performance. All departments and units shall implement comprehensive budget performance management, strengthen pre-performance evaluation, strictly manage performance targets, improve the budget performance indicator system, and enhance the quality of performance evaluation. Strengthen the application of performance evaluation results, promote the integration of performance evaluation results with policy improvement, budget arrangement, and management enhancement, advance the disclosure of budget performance information, and submit important performance evaluation results together with the draft final accounts to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for review. The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall strengthen the review and oversight of performance targets and performance evaluation results of key expenditures and major projects. When necessary, hold budget performance hearings.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、加强对中央预算执行和决算的审计监督。审计机关应当按照真实、合法和效益的要求，对中央预算执行和其他财政收支情况以及决算草案进行审计监督，为全国人民代表大会常务委员会开展预算执行、决算审查监督提供支持服务。国务院应当在每年六月向全国人民代表大会常务委员会提出对上一年度中央预算执行和其他财政收支的审计工作报告。审计工作报告应当重点报告上一年度中央预算执行和决算草案、重要政策实施、财政资金绩效的审计情况，全面客观反映审计查出的问题，揭示问题产生的原因，提出改进工作的建议。审计查出的问题要依法纠正、处理，加强审计结果运用，强化责任追究，完善审计查出问题整改工作机制，健全整改情况公开机制。必要时，全国人民代表大会常务委员会可以对审计工作报告作出决议。

**Article 8** VIII. Strengthening audit oversight of central budget implementation and final accounts. Audit institutions shall, in accordance with the requirements of truthfulness, legality, and efficiency, conduct audit oversight of the implementation of the central budget and other fiscal revenues and expenditures, as well as the draft final accounts, and provide support services for the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress in conducting budget implementation and final account review and oversight. The State Council shall, in June of each year, submit to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress an audit work report on the implementation of the central budget and other fiscal revenues and expenditures for the previous year. The audit work report shall focus on reporting the audit of the previous year's central budget implementation and draft final accounts, the implementation of important policies, and the performance of fiscal funds, comprehensively and objectively reflect the problems found in the audit, reveal the causes of the problems, and put forward suggestions for improving work. The problems found in the audit shall be corrected and handled according to law, the application of audit results shall be strengthened, accountability shall be reinforced, the working mechanism for rectifying problems found in audits shall be improved, and the mechanism for disclosing rectification information shall be perfected. When necessary, the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress may adopt a resolution on the audit work report.

<a id="art-9"></a>九、加强审计查出问题整改情况的监督工作。全国人民代表大会常务委员会对审计查出突出问题整改情况开展跟踪监督。综合运用听取和审议专项工作报告、专题询问等方式开展跟踪监督，加大监督力度，增强监督效果，推动建立健全整改长效机制，完善预算管理制度。健全人大预算审查监督与纪检监察监督、审计监督的贯通协调机制，加强信息共享，形成监督合力。  
全国人民代表大会常务委员会在每年十二月听取和审议国务院关于审计查出问题整改情况的报告，根据需要可以听取审计查出突出问题相关责任部门单位的单项整改情况报告。有关责任部门单位负责人应当到会听取意见，回答询问。国务院提交的整改情况报告，应当与审计工作报告揭示的问题和提出的建议相对应，重点反映审计查出突出问题的整改情况，并提供审计查出突出问题的单项整改结果和中央部门预算执行审计查出问题整改情况清单。必要时，全国人民代表大会常务委员会可以对审计查出问题整改情况报告作出决议。

**Article 9** IX. Strengthening oversight of the rectification of problems found in audits. The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall conduct follow-up oversight of the rectification of prominent problems found in audits. It shall comprehensively use methods such as hearing and deliberating special work reports and special inquiries to carry out follow-up oversight, increase oversight intensity, enhance oversight effectiveness, promote the establishment and improvement of a long-term rectification mechanism, and improve the budget management system. Strengthen the connection and coordination mechanism between the National People's Congress's budget review and oversight, disciplinary inspection and supervision, and audit supervision, enhance information sharing, and form a joint oversight force.  
The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall, in December of each year, hear and deliberate the report of the State Council on the rectification of problems found in audits. It may, as needed, hear individual rectification reports from the responsible departments and units for prominent problems found in audits. The responsible persons of the relevant departments and units shall attend the meeting to hear opinions and answer inquiries. The rectification report submitted by the State Council shall correspond to the problems revealed and suggestions made in the audit work report, focus on reflecting the rectification of prominent problems found in audits, and provide individual rectification results for prominent problems found in audits and a list of rectification situations for problems found in the audit of central department budget implementation. When necessary, the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress may adopt a resolution on the report on the rectification of problems found in audits.

<a id="art-10"></a>十、依法执行备案制度、强化预算法律责任。国务院应当将有关预算的法规及规范性文件，中央预算与地方预算有关收入和支出项目的划分、地方向中央上解收入、中央对地方税收返还或者转移支付的具体办法，省、自治区、直辖市政府报送国务院备案的预算决算的汇总，中央政府综合财务报告，以及其他应当报送的事项，及时报送全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。  
全国人民代表大会常务委员会开展预算决算审查监督工作发现的问题，相关机关、部门单位和地方应当及时研究处理，对违反预算法等法律规定的，依法追究法律责任；需要给予政务处分的，全国人民代表大会常务委员会有关工作机构及时通报监察机关。

**Article 10** X. Implementing the filing system and strengthening budget legal liability according to law. The State Council shall promptly file with the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress the following: regulations and normative documents concerning budgets; the division of revenue and expenditure items between the central and local budgets; specific measures for local governments' revenue contributions to the central government and the central government's tax rebates or transfer payments to local governments; summaries of budgets and final accounts submitted by the governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the central government to the State Council for filing; the comprehensive financial report of the central government; and other matters that shall be filed.  
For problems discovered by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress during budget and final account review and oversight, the relevant organs, departments, units, and localities shall promptly study and handle them. Where laws such as the Budget Law are violated, legal liability shall be pursued according to law. If disciplinary sanctions are required, the relevant working bodies of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall promptly notify the supervisory authorities.

<a id="art-11"></a>十一、更好发挥全国人大代表作用。国务院财政等部门应当通过座谈会、通报会、专题调研、办理议案建议和邀请全国人大代表视察等方式，在编制预算、制定政策、推进改革过程中，认真听取全国人大代表意见建议，主动回应全国人大代表关切。全国人民代表大会有关专门委员会、常务委员会有关工作机构应当加强与全国人大代表的沟通联系，更好发挥代表作用。健全预算审查联系代表工作机制。

**Article 11** XI. Better leveraging the role of deputies to the National People's Congress. The fiscal and other departments of the State Council shall, through methods such as symposiums, briefing sessions, special research, handling of proposals and suggestions, and inviting deputies to the National People's Congress for inspections, earnestly listen to the opinions and suggestions of deputies to the National People's Congress during the processes of budget preparation, policy formulation, and reform promotion, and proactively respond to the concerns of deputies to the National People's Congress. The relevant special committees of the National People's Congress and the relevant working bodies of the Standing Committee shall strengthen communication and contact with deputies to the National People's Congress to better leverage their role. Improve the working mechanism for contacting deputies in budget review.

<a id="art-12"></a>十二、预算工作委员会职责。预算工作委员会是全国人民代表大会常务委员会的工作机构，协助财政经济委员会承担全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会审查预算决算、审查预算调整方案和监督预算执行方面的具体工作；承担国有资产管理情况监督、审计查出突出问题整改情况跟踪监督方面的具体工作；承担预算、国有资产联网监督方面的具体工作；受委员长会议委托，承担有关法律草案的起草工作，协助财政经济委员会承担有关法律草案审议方面的具体工作；以及承办本决定规定的和常务委员会、委员长会议交办以及财政经济委员会需要协助办理的其他有关财政预算的具体事项。经委员长会议同意，预算工作委员会可以要求政府有关部门和单位提供预算情况，并获取相关信息资料及说明。经委员长会议批准，可以对各部门、各预算单位、重大建设项目的预算资金使用和专项资金的使用进行调查，政府有关部门和单位应积极协助、配合。  
本决定自公布之日起施行。

**Article 12** XII. Duties of the Budget Affairs Commission. The Budget Affairs Commission is a working body of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, assisting the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee in undertaking specific work on the review of budgets and final accounts, the review of budget adjustment plans, and the oversight of budget implementation by the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee; undertaking specific work on the oversight of the management of state-owned assets and the follow-up oversight of the rectification of prominent problems found in audits; undertaking specific work on online oversight of budgets and state-owned assets; entrusted by the Council of Chairpersons, undertaking the drafting of relevant legal drafts, and assisting the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee in undertaking specific work on the deliberation of relevant legal drafts; and undertaking other specific matters concerning fiscal budgets as provided for in this Decision, as assigned by the Standing Committee and the Council of Chairpersons, and as needed by the Financial and Economic Affairs Committee. With the consent of the Council of Chairpersons, the Budget Affairs Commission may request relevant government departments and units to provide budget information and obtain relevant information, materials, and explanations. With the approval of the Council of Chairpersons, it may investigate the use of budget funds and special funds by various departments, budget units, and major construction projects, and the relevant government departments and units shall actively assist and cooperate.  
This Decision shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
