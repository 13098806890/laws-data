# 最高人民法院关于对因政府调整划转企业国有资产引起的纠纷是否受理问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法复〔1996〕4号  
**公布日期**：1996-04-02

---

### 正文

一、因政府及其所属主管部门在对企业国有资产调整、划转过程中引起相关国有企业之间的纠纷，应由政府或所属国有资产管理部门处理。国有企业作为当事人向人民法院提起民事诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article None** 1. Disputes between relevant state-owned enterprises arising from the government and its competent authorities in the process of adjustment and transfer of state-owned assets of enterprises shall be handled by the government or the competent state-owned assets management department. Where a state-owned enterprise, as a party, brings a civil action with a People's Court, the People's Court shall not accept the case.

二、当事人不服政府及其所属主管部门依据有关行政法规作出的调整、划转企业国有资产决定，向人民法院提起行政诉讼，凡符合法定起诉条件的，人民法院应当受理。  
1996年4月2日

**Article None** 2. Where a party, not satisfied with a decision on adjustment or transfer of state-owned assets of enterprises made by the government or its competent authorities pursuant to relevant administrative regulations, brings an administrative action with a People's Court, and if the statutory conditions for prosecution are met, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
April 2, 1996
