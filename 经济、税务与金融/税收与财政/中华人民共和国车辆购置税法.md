# 中华人民共和国车辆购置税法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2018-12-29  
**生效日期**：2019-07-01

> （2018年12月29日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第七次会议通过） 第一条　在中华人民共和国境内购置汽车、有轨电车、汽车挂车、排气量超过一百五十毫升的摩托车（以下统称应税车辆）的单位和个人，为车辆购置税的纳税人，应当依照本法规定缴纳车辆购置税。 第二条　本法所称购置，是指以购买、进口、自产、受赠、获奖或者其他方式取得并自用应税车辆的行为。 第三条　车辆购置税实行一次性征收。购置已征车辆购置税的车辆，不再征收车辆购置税。 第四条　车辆购置税的税率为百分之十。 第五条　车辆购置税的应纳税额按照应税车辆的计税价格乘以税率计算。 第六条　应税车辆的计税价格，按照下列规定确定： （一）纳税人购买自用应税车辆的计税价格，为纳税人实际支付给销售者的全部价款，不包括增值税税款； （二）纳税人进口自用应税车辆的计税价格，为关税完税价格加上关税和消费税； （三）纳税人自产自用应税车辆的计税价格，按照纳税人生产的同类应税车辆的销售价格确定，不包括增值税税款； （四）纳税人以受赠、获奖或者其他方式取得自用应税车辆的计税价格，按照购置应税车辆时相关凭证载明的价格确定，不包括增值税税款。 第七条　纳税人申报的应税车辆计税价格明显偏低，又无正当理由的，由税务机关依照《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》的规定核定其应纳税额。 第八条　纳税人以外汇结算应税车辆价款的，按照申报纳税之日的人民币汇率中间价折合成人民币计算缴纳税款。 第九条　下列车辆免征车辆购置税： （一）依照法律规定应当予以免税的外国驻华使馆、领事馆和国际组织驻华机构及其有关人员自用的车辆；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　在中华人民共和国境内购置汽车、有轨电车、汽车挂车、排气量超过一百五十毫升的摩托车（以下统称应税车辆）的单位和个人，为车辆购置税的纳税人，应当依照本法规定缴纳车辆购置税。

**Article 1** Units and individuals that purchase automobiles, trams, automobile trailers, and motorcycles with a displacement exceeding 150 milliliters (hereinafter collectively referred to as "taxable vehicles") within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be taxpayers of vehicle purchase tax and shall pay vehicle purchase tax in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称购置，是指以购买、进口、自产、受赠、获奖或者其他方式取得并自用应税车辆的行为。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "purchase" means the act of acquiring and using taxable vehicles for personal use by means of buying, importing, self-producing, receiving as gifts, winning prizes, or other means.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　车辆购置税实行一次性征收。购置已征车辆购置税的车辆，不再征收车辆购置税。

**Article 3** Vehicle purchase tax shall be levied on a one-time basis. No vehicle purchase tax shall be levied on the purchase of a vehicle on which vehicle purchase tax has already been levied.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　车辆购置税的税率为百分之十。

**Article 4** The tax rate for vehicle purchase tax is 10 percent.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　车辆购置税的应纳税额按照应税车辆的计税价格乘以税率计算。

**Article 5** The amount of vehicle purchase tax payable shall be calculated by multiplying the taxable price of a taxable vehicle by the tax rate.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　应税车辆的计税价格，按照下列规定确定：  
（一）纳税人购买自用应税车辆的计税价格，为纳税人实际支付给销售者的全部价款，不包括增值税税款；  
（二）纳税人进口自用应税车辆的计税价格，为关税完税价格加上关税和消费税；  
（三）纳税人自产自用应税车辆的计税价格，按照纳税人生产的同类应税车辆的销售价格确定，不包括增值税税款；  
（四）纳税人以受赠、获奖或者其他方式取得自用应税车辆的计税价格，按照购置应税车辆时相关凭证载明的价格确定，不包括增值税税款。

**Article 6** The taxable price of a taxable vehicle shall be determined in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) For a taxable vehicle purchased by a taxpayer for personal use, the taxable price shall be the total price actually paid by the taxpayer to the seller, excluding value-added tax;  
(2) For a taxable vehicle imported by a taxpayer for personal use, the taxable price shall be the customs value plus customs duties and consumption tax;  
(3) For a taxable vehicle self-produced by a taxpayer for personal use, the taxable price shall be determined based on the selling price of similar taxable vehicles produced by the taxpayer, excluding value-added tax;  
(4) For a taxable vehicle acquired by a taxpayer for personal use through gifts, prizes, or other means, the taxable price shall be determined based on the price stated in the relevant documents at the time of purchasing the taxable vehicle, excluding value-added tax.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　纳税人申报的应税车辆计税价格明显偏低，又无正当理由的，由税务机关依照《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》的规定核定其应纳税额。

**Article 7** Where the taxable price declared by a taxpayer for a taxable vehicle is obviously low and without justifiable reasons, the tax authority shall determine the amount of tax payable in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　纳税人以外汇结算应税车辆价款的，按照申报纳税之日的人民币汇率中间价折合成人民币计算缴纳税款。

**Article 8** Where a taxpayer settles the price of a taxable vehicle in foreign exchange, the tax payable shall be calculated and paid by converting the amount into RMB at the central parity rate of the RMB exchange rate on the date of tax declaration.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　下列车辆免征车辆购置税：  
（一）依照法律规定应当予以免税的外国驻华使馆、领事馆和国际组织驻华机构及其有关人员自用的车辆；  
（二）中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队列入装备订货计划的车辆；  
（三）悬挂应急救援专用号牌的国家综合性消防救援车辆；  
（四）设有固定装置的非运输专用作业车辆；  
（五）城市公交企业购置的公共汽电车辆。  
根据国民经济和社会发展的需要，国务院可以规定减征或者其他免征车辆购置税的情形，报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 9** The following vehicles shall be exempt from vehicle purchase tax:  
(1) Vehicles for personal use by foreign embassies, consulates, and offices of international organizations in China and their relevant personnel, which shall be exempted in accordance with legal provisions;  
(2) Vehicles included in the equipment ordering plans of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force;  
(3) National comprehensive fire rescue vehicles bearing special license plates for emergency rescue;  
(4) Non-transport special operation vehicles equipped with fixed devices;  
(5) Public bus and trolley vehicles purchased by urban public transport enterprises.  
Based on the needs of the national economy and social development, the State Council may prescribe circumstances for reduction or other exemptions from vehicle purchase tax, and shall report such prescriptions to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for recordation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　车辆购置税由税务机关负责征收。

**Article 10** Vehicle purchase tax shall be collected by the tax authorities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　纳税人购置应税车辆，应当向车辆登记地的主管税务机关申报缴纳车辆购置税；购置不需要办理车辆登记的应税车辆的，应当向纳税人所在地的主管税务机关申报缴纳车辆购置税。

**Article 11** A taxpayer who purchases a taxable vehicle shall declare and pay vehicle purchase tax to the competent tax authority at the place of vehicle registration; if the taxpayer purchases a taxable vehicle for which no vehicle registration is required, the taxpayer shall declare and pay vehicle purchase tax to the competent tax authority at the place where the taxpayer is located.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　车辆购置税的纳税义务发生时间为纳税人购置应税车辆的当日。纳税人应当自纳税义务发生之日起六十日内申报缴纳车辆购置税。

**Article 12** The tax liability for vehicle purchase tax shall arise on the date when the taxpayer purchases the taxable vehicle. The taxpayer shall declare and pay vehicle purchase tax within 60 days from the date on which the tax liability arises.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　纳税人应当在向公安机关交通管理部门办理车辆注册登记前，缴纳车辆购置税。  
公安机关交通管理部门办理车辆注册登记，应当根据税务机关提供的应税车辆完税或者免税电子信息对纳税人申请登记的车辆信息进行核对，核对无误后依法办理车辆注册登记。

**Article 13** A taxpayer shall pay the vehicle purchase tax before applying for vehicle registration with the traffic management department of the public security organ.  
When handling vehicle registration, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall verify the vehicle information declared by the taxpayer for registration against the electronic information of tax payment or tax exemption for taxable vehicles provided by the tax authority, and after confirming that the information is correct, shall handle the vehicle registration according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　免税、减税车辆因转让、改变用途等原因不再属于免税、减税范围的，纳税人应当在办理车辆转移登记或者变更登记前缴纳车辆购置税。计税价格以免税、减税车辆初次办理纳税申报时确定的计税价格为基准，每满一年扣减百分之十。

**Article 14** Where a tax-exempt or tax-reduced vehicle is no longer within the scope of exemption or reduction due to transfer, change of use, or other reasons, the taxpayer shall pay vehicle purchase tax before handling the vehicle transfer registration or alteration registration. The taxable price shall be based on the taxable price determined at the time of the initial tax declaration for the exempted or reduced vehicle, with a reduction of 10 percent for each full year.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　纳税人将已征车辆购置税的车辆退回车辆生产企业或者销售企业的，可以向主管税务机关申请退还车辆购置税。退税额以已缴税款为基准，自缴纳税款之日至申请退税之日，每满一年扣减百分之十。

**Article 15** Where a taxpayer returns a vehicle on which vehicle purchase tax has already been paid to the vehicle manufacturer or seller, the taxpayer may apply to the competent tax authority for a refund of the vehicle purchase tax. The refund amount shall be based on the tax paid, with a reduction of 10 percent for each full year from the date of tax payment to the date of application for refund.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　税务机关和公安、商务、海关、工业和信息化等部门应当建立应税车辆信息共享和工作配合机制，及时交换应税车辆和纳税信息资料。

**Article 16** The tax authority and the public security, commerce, customs, industry and information technology, and other departments shall establish a mechanism for sharing information on taxable vehicles and for work coordination, and shall promptly exchange information and data on taxable vehicles and tax payments.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　车辆购置税的征收管理，依照本法和《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》的规定执行。

**Article 17** The collection and management of vehicle purchase tax shall be governed by this Law and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　纳税人、税务机关及其工作人员违反本法规定的，依照《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》和有关法律法规的规定追究法律责任。

**Article 18** Where a taxpayer or a tax authority or its staff member violates the provisions of this Law, legal liability shall be pursued in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection and relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本法自2019年7月1日起施行。2000年10月22日国务院公布的《中华人民共和国车辆购置税暂行条例》同时废止。

**Article 19** This Law shall come into force on July 1, 2019. The Interim Regulation of the People's Republic of China on Vehicle Purchase Tax promulgated by the State Council on October 22, 2000 shall be abolished simultaneously.
