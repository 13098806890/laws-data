# 中华人民共和国增值税法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2024-12-25  
**生效日期**：2026-01-01

> （2024年12月25日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十三次会议通过）

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了健全有利于高质量发展的增值税制度，规范增值税的征收和缴纳，保护纳税人的合法权益，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purposes of improving the value-added tax system conducive to high-quality development, regulating the collection and payment of value-added tax, and protecting the legitimate rights and interests of taxpayers.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　增值税税收工作应当贯彻落实党和国家路线方针政策、决策部署，为国民经济和社会发展服务。

**Article 2** The value-added tax work shall implement the line, principles, policies, and decisions of the Party and the State, and serve the national economic and social development.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　在中华人民共和国境内（以下简称境内）销售货物、服务、无形资产、不动产（以下称应税交易），以及进口货物的单位和个人（包括个体工商户），为增值税的纳税人，应当依照本法规定缴纳增值税。  
销售货物、服务、无形资产、不动产，是指有偿转让货物、不动产的所有权，有偿提供服务，有偿转让无形资产的所有权或者使用权。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-2" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-3" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第3条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-22" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第22条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 3** Units and individuals (including individual businesses) that sell goods, services, intangible assets, or immovable property (hereinafter referred to as taxable transactions) within the territory of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as within the territory), or import goods, shall be taxpayers of value-added tax and shall pay value-added tax in accordance with the provisions of this Law.  
The sale of goods, services, intangible assets, or immovable property means the transfer of ownership of goods or immovable property, provision of services, or transfer of ownership or right to use of intangible assets for a consideration.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　在境内发生应税交易，是指下列情形：  
（一）销售货物的，货物的起运地或者所在地在境内；  
（二）销售或者租赁不动产、转让自然资源使用权的，不动产、自然资源所在地在境内；  
（三）销售金融商品的，金融商品在境内发行，或者销售方为境内单位和个人；  
（四）除本条第二项、第三项规定外，销售服务、无形资产的，服务、无形资产在境内消费，或者销售方为境内单位和个人。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-4" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第4条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 4** The occurrence of a taxable transaction within the territory means any of the following circumstances:  
(1) where goods are sold, the place of dispatch or the location of the goods is within the territory;  
(2) where immovable property is sold or leased, or the right to use natural resources is transferred, the location of the immovable property or natural resources is within the territory;  
(3) where financial products are sold, the financial products are issued within the territory, or the seller is a unit or individual within the territory;  
(4) except as provided in subparagraphs (2) and (3) of this Article, where services or intangible assets are sold, the services or intangible assets are consumed within the territory, or the seller is a unit or individual within the territory.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　有下列情形之一的，视同应税交易，应当依照本法规定缴纳增值税：  
（一）单位和个体工商户将自产或者委托加工的货物用于集体福利或者个人消费；  
（二）单位和个体工商户无偿转让货物；  
（三）单位和个人无偿转让无形资产、不动产或者金融商品。

**Article 5** Under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as a taxable transaction, and value-added tax shall be paid in accordance with the provisions of this Law:  
(1) where a unit or individual business uses goods produced or processed on commission by itself for collective welfare or personal consumption;  
(2) where a unit or individual business transfers goods without consideration;  
(3) where a unit or individual transfers intangible assets, immovable property, or financial products without consideration.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　有下列情形之一的，不属于应税交易，不征收增值税：  
（一）员工为受雇单位或者雇主提供取得工资、薪金的服务；  
（二）收取行政事业性收费、政府性基金；  
（三）依照法律规定被征收、征用而取得补偿；  
（四）取得存款利息收入。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-22" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第22条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 6** Under any of the following circumstances, it shall not be considered a taxable transaction, and value-added tax shall not be levied:  
(1) where an employee provides services to obtain wages or salaries from the employing unit or employer;  
(2) where administrative fees or government funds are collected;  
(3) where compensation is obtained for expropriation or requisition in accordance with the provisions of laws;  
(4) where interest income from deposits is obtained.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　增值税为价外税，应税交易的销售额不包括增值税税额。增值税税额，应当按照国务院的规定在交易凭证上单独列明。

**Article 7** Value-added tax is an off-price tax, and the sales amount of a taxable transaction shall not include the value-added tax amount. The value-added tax amount shall be separately stated in the transaction voucher in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　纳税人发生应税交易，应当按照一般计税方法，通过销项税额抵扣进项税额计算应纳税额的方式，计算缴纳增值税；本法另有规定的除外。  
小规模纳税人可以按照销售额和征收率计算应纳税额的简易计税方法，计算缴纳增值税。  
中外合作开采海洋石油、天然气增值税的计税方法等，按照国务院的有关规定执行。

**Article 8** Where a taxpayer engages in a taxable transaction, he/it shall calculate and pay value-added tax by using the general tax calculation method, i.e., calculating the tax payable by deducting input tax from output tax; unless otherwise provided in this Law.  
Small-scale taxpayers may calculate and pay value-added tax by using the simplified tax calculation method, i.e., calculating the tax payable based on the sales amount and the levy rate.  
The tax calculation methods for value-added tax on the Sino-foreign cooperative exploitation of offshore oil and natural gas shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　本法所称小规模纳税人，是指年应征增值税销售额未超过五百万元的纳税人。  
小规模纳税人会计核算健全，能够提供准确税务资料的，可以向主管税务机关办理登记，按照本法规定的一般计税方法计算缴纳增值税。  
根据国民经济和社会发展的需要，国务院可以对小规模纳税人的标准作出调整，报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-36" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第36条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 9** For the purposes of this Law, 'small-scale taxpayer' means a taxpayer whose annual sales amount subject to value-added tax does not exceed five million yuan.  
Where a small-scale taxpayer has a sound accounting system and can provide accurate tax information, he/it may register with the competent tax authority and calculate and pay value-added tax by using the general tax calculation method as provided in this Law.  
Based on the needs of national economic and social development, the State Council may adjust the criteria for small-scale taxpayers and report to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for record.

### 第二章　税率

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　增值税税率：  
（一）纳税人销售货物、加工修理修配服务、有形动产租赁服务，进口货物，除本条第二项、第四项、第五项规定外，税率为百分之十三。  
（二）纳税人销售交通运输、邮政、基础电信、建筑、不动产租赁服务，销售不动产，转让土地使用权，销售或者进口下列货物，除本条第四项、第五项规定外，税率为百分之九：  
1.农产品、食用植物油、食用盐；  
2.自来水、暖气、冷气、热水、煤气、石油液化气、天然气、二甲醚、沼气、居民用煤炭制品；  
3.图书、报纸、杂志、音像制品、电子出版物；  
4.饲料、化肥、农药、农机、农膜。  
（三）纳税人销售服务、无形资产，除本条第一项、第二项、第五项规定外，税率为百分之六。  
（四）纳税人出口货物，税率为零；国务院另有规定的除外。  
（五）境内单位和个人跨境销售国务院规定范围内的服务、无形资产，税率为零。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-8" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第8条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 10** Value-added tax rates:  
(1) For taxpayers selling goods, processing, repair and fitting services, or tangible movable property leasing services, or importing goods, the tax rate is 13 percent, except as provided in subparagraphs (2), (4), and (5) of this Article.  
(2) For taxpayers selling transportation, postal, basic telecommunications, construction, or immovable property leasing services, selling immovable property, transferring land use rights, or selling or importing the following goods, the tax rate is 9 percent, except as provided in subparagraphs (4) and (5) of this Article:  
1. agricultural products, edible vegetable oil, and edible salt;  
2. tap water, heating, cooling, hot water, coal gas, liquefied petroleum gas, natural gas, dimethyl ether, biogas, and coal products for residential use;  
3. books, newspapers, magazines, audio-visual products, and electronic publications;  
4. feed, fertilizers, pesticides, agricultural machinery, and agricultural film.  
(3) For taxpayers selling services or intangible assets, the tax rate is 6 percent, except as provided in subparagraphs (1), (2), and (5) of this Article.  
(4) For taxpayers exporting goods, the tax rate is zero unless otherwise provided by the State Council.  
(5) For units and individuals within the territory engaging in cross-border sales of services or intangible assets within the scope specified by the State Council, the tax rate is zero.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　适用简易计税方法计算缴纳增值税的征收率为百分之三。

**Article 11** The levy rate for calculating and paying value-added tax by using the simplified tax calculation method is 3 percent.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　纳税人发生两项以上应税交易涉及不同税率、征收率的，应当分别核算适用不同税率、征收率的销售额；未分别核算的，从高适用税率。

**Article 12** Where a taxpayer engages in two or more taxable transactions involving different tax rates or levy rates, he/it shall separately account for the sales amounts applicable to different tax rates or levy rates; if not separately accounted for, the higher rate shall apply.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　纳税人发生一项应税交易涉及两个以上税率、征收率的，按照应税交易的主要业务适用税率、征收率。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-10" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第10条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 13** Where a taxpayer engages in one taxable transaction involving two or more tax rates or levy rates, the tax rate or levy rate applicable to the main business of the taxable transaction shall apply.

### 第三章　应纳税额

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　按照一般计税方法计算缴纳增值税的，应纳税额为当期销项税额抵扣当期进项税额后的余额。  
按照简易计税方法计算缴纳增值税的，应纳税额为当期销售额乘以征收率。  
进口货物，按照本法规定的组成计税价格乘以适用税率计算缴纳增值税。组成计税价格，为关税计税价格加上关税和消费税；国务院另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 14** For value-added tax calculated and paid by using the general tax calculation method, the tax payable is the balance after deducting the current input tax from the current output tax.  
For value-added tax calculated and paid by using the simplified tax calculation method, the tax payable is the current sales amount multiplied by the levy rate.  
For imported goods, value-added tax shall be calculated and paid by multiplying the composite taxable price as provided in this Law by the applicable tax rate. The composite taxable price is the customs duty taxable price plus customs duty and consumption tax; where the State Council provides otherwise, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　境外单位和个人在境内发生应税交易，以购买方为扣缴义务人；按照国务院的规定委托境内代理人申报缴纳税款的除外。  
扣缴义务人依照本法规定代扣代缴税款的，按照销售额乘以税率计算应扣缴税额。

**Article 15** Where a foreign unit or individual engages in a taxable transaction within the territory, the purchaser shall be the withholding agent; unless a domestic agent is entrusted to declare and pay the tax in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.  
Where a withholding agent withholds and pays tax in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the amount to be withheld shall be calculated by multiplying the sales amount by the tax rate.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　销项税额，是指纳税人发生应税交易，按照销售额乘以本法规定的税率计算的增值税税额。  
进项税额，是指纳税人购进货物、服务、无形资产、不动产支付或者负担的增值税税额。  
纳税人应当凭法律、行政法规或者国务院规定的增值税扣税凭证从销项税额中抵扣进项税额。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-11" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第11条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 16** 'Output tax' means the value-added tax amount calculated by multiplying the sales amount by the tax rate provided in this Law when a taxpayer engages in a taxable transaction.  
'Input tax' means the value-added tax amount paid or borne by a taxpayer when purchasing goods, services, intangible assets, or immovable property.  
A taxpayer shall deduct input tax from output tax on the basis of value-added tax deduction vouchers as prescribed by laws, administrative regulations, or the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　销售额，是指纳税人发生应税交易取得的与之相关的价款，包括货币和非货币形式的经济利益对应的全部价款，不包括按照一般计税方法计算的销项税额和按照简易计税方法计算的应纳税额。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-15" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第15条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 17** 'Sales amount' means the price obtained by a taxpayer in connection with a taxable transaction, including the total price corresponding to economic benefits in monetary or non-monetary forms, but excluding the output tax calculated by using the general tax calculation method and the tax payable calculated by using the simplified tax calculation method.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　销售额以人民币计算。纳税人以人民币以外的货币结算销售额的，应当折合成人民币计算。

**Article 18** The sales amount shall be calculated in Renminbi. Where a taxpayer settles the sales amount in a currency other than Renminbi, it shall be converted into Renminbi for calculation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　发生[本法第五条](#art-5)规定的视同应税交易以及销售额为非货币形式的，纳税人应当按照市场价格确定销售额。

**Article 19** Where a transaction is deemed as a taxable transaction under Article 5 of this Law, or the sales amount is in a non-monetary form, the taxpayer shall determine the sales amount based on the market price.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　销售额明显偏低或者偏高且无正当理由的，税务机关可以依照《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》和有关行政法规的规定核定销售额。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-18" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第18条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 20** Where the sales amount is obviously low or high without justifiable reasons, the tax authority may determine the sales amount in accordance with the provisions of the Tax Collection and Administration Law of the People's Republic of China and relevant administrative regulations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　当期进项税额大于当期销项税额的部分，纳税人可以按照国务院的规定选择结转下期继续抵扣或者申请退还。

**Article 21** Where the current input tax exceeds the current output tax, the taxpayer may, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council, choose to carry forward the excess to the next period for deduction or apply for a refund.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　纳税人的下列进项税额不得从其销项税额中抵扣：  
（一）适用简易计税方法计税项目对应的进项税额；  
（二）免征增值税项目对应的进项税额；  
（三）非正常损失项目对应的进项税额；  
（四）购进并用于集体福利或者个人消费的货物、服务、无形资产、不动产对应的进项税额；  
（五）购进并直接用于消费的餐饮服务、居民日常服务和娱乐服务对应的进项税额；  
（六）国务院规定的其他进项税额。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-19" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-24" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第24条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 22** The following input tax of a taxpayer shall not be deducted from its output tax:  
(1) input tax corresponding to items taxed under the simplified tax calculation method;  
(2) input tax corresponding to items exempt from VAT;  
(3) input tax corresponding to items of abnormal loss;  
(4) input tax corresponding to goods, services, intangible assets, and immovable property purchased for collective welfare or personal consumption;  
(5) input tax corresponding to catering services, daily services for residents, and entertainment services purchased for direct consumption;  
(6) other input tax as prescribed by the State Council.

### 第四章　税收优惠

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　小规模纳税人发生应税交易，销售额未达到起征点的，免征增值税；达到起征点的，依照本法规定全额计算缴纳增值税。  
前款规定的起征点标准由国务院规定，报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 23** Where a small-scale taxpayer engages in taxable transactions and the sales amount does not reach the threshold, VAT shall be exempted; where the threshold is reached, VAT shall be calculated and paid on the full amount in accordance with this Law.  
The threshold standard referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be prescribed by the State Council and reported to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for recordation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　下列项目免征增值税：  
（一）农业生产者销售的自产农产品，农业机耕、排灌、病虫害防治、植物保护、农牧保险以及相关技术培训业务，家禽、牲畜、水生动物的配种和疾病防治；  
（二）医疗机构提供的医疗服务；  
（三）古旧图书，自然人销售的自己使用过的物品；  
（四）直接用于科学研究、科学试验和教学的进口仪器、设备；  
（五）外国政府、国际组织无偿援助的进口物资和设备；  
（六）由残疾人的组织直接进口供残疾人专用的物品，残疾人个人提供的服务；  
（七）托儿所、幼儿园、养老机构、残疾人服务机构提供的育养服务，婚姻介绍服务，殡葬服务；  
（八）学校提供的学历教育服务，学生勤工俭学提供的服务；  
（九）纪念馆、博物馆、文化馆、文物保护单位管理机构、美术馆、展览馆、书画院、图书馆举办文化活动的门票收入，宗教场所举办文化、宗教活动的门票收入。  
前款规定的免税项目具体标准由国务院规定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-26" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第26条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-27" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第27条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-28" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第28条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-29" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第29条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-30" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第30条引用">[5]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-31" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第31条引用">[6]</a></sup>

**Article 24** The following items shall be exempt from VAT:  
(1) self-produced agricultural products sold by agricultural producers, agricultural machinery ploughing, irrigation, pest control, plant protection, agricultural and livestock insurance, and related technical training services, as well as breeding and disease prevention for poultry, livestock, and aquatic animals;  
(2) medical services provided by medical institutions;  
(3) ancient and old books, and items used by natural persons themselves and sold by them;  
(4) imported instruments and equipment directly used for scientific research, scientific experiments, and education;  
(5) imported materials and equipment donated gratuitously by foreign governments and international organizations;  
(6) goods directly imported by organizations of disabled persons for the exclusive use of disabled persons, and services provided by disabled persons themselves;  
(7) childcare services provided by nurseries, kindergartens, elderly care institutions, and disabled persons service institutions, marriage introduction services, and funeral services;  
(8) academic education services provided by schools, and services provided by students under work-study programs;  
(9) ticket income from cultural activities held by memorial halls, museums, cultural centers, administrative bodies of cultural relic protection units, art galleries, exhibition halls, painting and calligraphy academies, and libraries, as well as ticket income from cultural and religious activities held by religious venues.  
The specific standards for the tax-exempt items referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　根据国民经济和社会发展的需要，国务院对支持小微企业发展、扶持重点产业、鼓励创新创业就业、公益事业捐赠等情形可以制定增值税专项优惠政策，报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。  
国务院应当对增值税优惠政策适时开展评估、调整。

**Article 25** Based on the needs of national economic and social development, the State Council may, for circumstances such as supporting the development of small and micro enterprises, supporting key industries, encouraging innovation, entrepreneurship, and employment, and donations to public welfare undertakings, formulate special preferential policies for VAT and report them to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for recordation.  
The State Council shall conduct timely evaluation and adjustment of the VAT preferential policies.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　纳税人兼营增值税优惠项目的，应当单独核算增值税优惠项目的销售额；未单独核算的项目，不得享受税收优惠。

**Article 26** Where a taxpayer concurrently engages in items eligible for VAT preferences, it shall separately account for the sales amount of the VAT-preferred items; items not separately accounted for shall not enjoy the tax preferences.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　纳税人可以放弃增值税优惠；放弃优惠的，在三十六个月内不得享受该项税收优惠，小规模纳税人除外。

**Article 27** A taxpayer may waive VAT preferences; after waiving such preferences, the taxpayer shall not enjoy such tax preferences for 36 months, except for small-scale taxpayers.

### 第五章　征收管理

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　增值税纳税义务发生时间，按照下列规定确定：  
（一）发生应税交易，纳税义务发生时间为收讫销售款项或者取得销售款项索取凭据的当日；先开具发票的，为开具发票的当日。  
（二）发生视同应税交易，纳税义务发生时间为完成视同应税交易的当日。  
（三）进口货物，纳税义务发生时间为货物报关进口的当日。  
增值税扣缴义务发生时间为纳税人增值税纳税义务发生的当日。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-39" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第39条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-40" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第40条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-41" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第41条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 28** The time when the obligation to pay VAT arises shall be determined according to the following provisions:  
(1) for taxable transactions, the obligation arises on the day when the sales proceeds are received or the document evidencing the right to collect the sales proceeds is obtained; where an invoice is issued first, on the day when the invoice is issued;  
(2) for transactions deemed as taxable transactions, the obligation arises on the day when the deemed taxable transaction is completed;  
(3) for imported goods, the obligation arises on the day when the goods are declared for import.  
The time when the withholding obligation arises is the day when the taxpayer's obligation to pay VAT arises.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　增值税纳税地点，按照下列规定确定：  
（一）有固定生产经营场所的纳税人，应当向其机构所在地或者居住地主管税务机关申报纳税。总机构和分支机构不在同一县（市）的，应当分别向各自所在地的主管税务机关申报纳税；经省级以上财政、税务主管部门批准，可以由总机构汇总向总机构所在地的主管税务机关申报纳税。  
（二）无固定生产经营场所的纳税人，应当向其应税交易发生地主管税务机关申报纳税；未申报纳税的，由其机构所在地或者居住地主管税务机关补征税款。  
（三）自然人销售或者租赁不动产，转让自然资源使用权，提供建筑服务，应当向不动产所在地、自然资源所在地、建筑服务发生地主管税务机关申报纳税。  
（四）进口货物的纳税人，应当按照海关规定的地点申报纳税。  
（五）扣缴义务人，应当向其机构所在地或者居住地主管税务机关申报缴纳扣缴的税款；机构所在地或者居住地在境外的，应当向应税交易发生地主管税务机关申报缴纳扣缴的税款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-42" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第42条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 29** The place for VAT payment shall be determined according to the following provisions:  
(1) a taxpayer with a fixed place of production or business operations shall declare and pay tax to the competent tax authority at its place of establishment or residence. Where the head office and branch are not in the same county (city), they shall declare and pay tax separately to the competent tax authorities at their respective locations; upon approval by the financial and tax authorities at or above the provincial level, the head office may aggregate and declare and pay tax to the competent tax authority at its place of establishment;  
(2) a taxpayer without a fixed place of production or business operations shall declare and pay tax to the competent tax authority at the place where the taxable transaction occurs; if it fails to declare and pay tax, the competent tax authority at its place of establishment or residence shall collect the tax in arrears;  
(3) a natural person who sells or leases immovable property, transfers the right to use natural resources, or provides construction services shall declare and pay tax to the competent tax authority at the place where the immovable property, natural resources, or construction services are located;  
(4) a taxpayer importing goods shall declare and pay tax at the place prescribed by the customs authorities;  
(5) a withholding agent shall declare and pay the withheld tax to the competent tax authority at its place of establishment or residence; if its place of establishment or residence is outside the territory of China, it shall declare and pay the withheld tax to the competent tax authority at the place where the taxable transaction occurs.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　增值税的计税期间分别为十日、十五日、一个月或者一个季度。纳税人的具体计税期间，由主管税务机关根据纳税人应纳税额的大小分别核定。不经常发生应税交易的纳税人，可以按次纳税。  
纳税人以一个月或者一个季度为一个计税期间的，自期满之日起十五日内申报纳税；以十日或者十五日为一个计税期间的，自次月一日起十五日内申报纳税。  
扣缴义务人解缴税款的计税期间和申报纳税期限，依照前两款规定执行。  
纳税人进口货物，应当按照海关规定的期限申报并缴纳税款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-43" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第43条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 30** The tax calculation periods for VAT shall be 10 days, 15 days, one month, or one quarter. The specific tax calculation period for a taxpayer shall be determined by the competent tax authority based on the taxpayer's tax payable. Taxpayers that do not frequently engage in taxable transactions may pay tax on a per-transaction basis.  
Where a taxpayer uses one month or one quarter as a tax calculation period, it shall declare and pay tax within 15 days from the end of the period; where it uses 10 days or 15 days as a tax calculation period, it shall declare and pay tax within 15 days from the first day of the following month.  
The tax calculation period and the deadline for declaring and paying tax for the withholding agent to remit the withheld tax shall be in accordance with the preceding two paragraphs.  
Where a taxpayer imports goods, it shall declare and pay tax within the time limit prescribed by the customs authorities.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　纳税人以十日或者十五日为一个计税期间的，应当自期满之日起五日内预缴税款。  
法律、行政法规对纳税人预缴税款另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 31** Where a taxpayer uses 10 days or 15 days as a tax calculation period, it shall prepay tax within 5 days from the end of the period.  
Where laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise for the taxpayer's prepayment of tax, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　增值税由税务机关征收，进口货物的增值税由海关代征。  
海关应当将代征增值税和货物出口报关的信息提供给税务机关。  
个人携带或者寄递进境物品增值税的计征办法由国务院制定，报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 32** VAT shall be collected by the tax authorities, and the VAT on imported goods shall be collected by the customs authorities.  
The customs authorities shall provide the tax authorities with information on the VAT collected and the export declaration of goods.  
The method of calculating and collecting VAT on articles carried or mailed into the territory of China by individuals shall be prescribed by the State Council and reported to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for recordation.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　纳税人出口货物或者跨境销售服务、无形资产，适用零税率的，应当向主管税务机关申报办理退（免）税。出口退（免）税的具体办法，由国务院制定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例.md#art-47" title="被《中华人民共和国增值税法实施条例》第47条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 33** Where a taxpayer exports goods or cross-border sells services or intangible assets and applies the zero tax rate, it shall apply to the competent tax authority for refund (exemption) of tax. The specific measures for export refund (exemption) shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　纳税人应当依法开具和使用增值税发票。增值税发票包括纸质发票和电子发票。电子发票与纸质发票具有同等法律效力。  
国家积极推广使用电子发票。

**Article 34** A taxpayer shall issue and use VAT invoices according to law. VAT invoices include paper invoices and electronic invoices. Electronic invoices shall have the same legal effect as paper invoices.  
The State actively promotes the use of electronic invoices.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　税务机关与工业和信息化、公安、海关、市场监督管理、人民银行、金融监督管理等部门建立增值税涉税信息共享机制和工作配合机制。  
有关部门应当依照法律、行政法规，在各自职责范围内，支持、协助税务机关开展增值税征收管理。

**Article 35** The tax authorities shall establish a mechanism for sharing VAT-related information and a mechanism for cooperation with the departments of industry and information technology, public security, customs, market regulation, the People's Bank of China, and financial regulation.  
The relevant departments shall, in accordance with laws and administrative regulations, support and assist the tax authorities in carrying out VAT collection and administration within their respective functions and responsibilities.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　增值税的征收管理依照本法和《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》的规定执行。

**Article 36** The collection and administration of VAT shall be governed by this Law and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　纳税人、扣缴义务人、税务机关及其工作人员违反本法规定的，依照《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》和有关法律、行政法规的规定追究法律责任。

**Article 37** Where a taxpayer, a withholding agent, tax authorities, or their staff violate the provisions of this Law, they shall be subject to legal liability in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection and the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本法自2026年1月1日起施行。《中华人民共和国增值税暂行条例》同时废止。

**Article 38** This Law shall come into force on January 1, 2026. The Interim Regulation of the People's Republic of China on Value-Added Tax shall be abolished simultaneously.
