<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民检察院  
2000年10月16日  
最高人民检察院关于擅自销售进料  
加工保税货物的行为法律适用问题的解释  
（2000年9月29日最高人民检察院第九届检察委员会  
第七十次会议通过）  
高检发释字〔2000〕3号  
为依法办理走私犯罪案件，根据海关法等法律的有关规定，对擅自销售进料加工保税货物的行为法律适用问题解释如下：  
保税货物是指经海关批准未办理纳税手续进境，在境内储存、加工、装配后复运出境的货物。经海关批准进口的进料加工的货物属于保税货物。未经海关许可并且未补缴应缴税额，擅自将批准进口的进料加工的原材料、零件、制成品、设备等保税货物，在境内销售牟利，偷逃应缴税额在五万元以上的，依照[刑法第一百五十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-154)、第一百五十三条的规定，以走私普通货物、物品罪追究刑事责任。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Procuratorate  
October 16, 2000  
Interpretation of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on the Issues Concerning the Application of Law to the Act of Unauthorized Sale of Bonded Goods for Inward Processing  
(Adopted at the 70th Session of the Procuratorial Committee of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on September 29, 2000)  
Prosecutor's Interpretation No. 3 [2000]  
For the purpose of handling smuggling crime cases according to law, and in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law and other laws, the issues concerning the application of law to the act of unauthorized sale of bonded goods for inward processing are interpreted as follows:  
Bonded goods refer to goods that are brought into the territory with the approval of the Customs without going through the tax payment procedures, and are to be re-exported after storage, processing, or assembly within the territory. Goods imported with the approval of the Customs for inward processing are bonded goods. Whoever, without permission from the Customs and without making up the payable tax amount, sells for profit within the territory the approved imported raw materials, parts, finished products, equipment, and other bonded goods for inward processing, and evades the payable tax amount of 50,000 RMB yuan or more, shall be investigated for criminal liability for the crime of smuggling ordinary goods or articles in accordance with Articles 154 and 153 of the Criminal Law.
