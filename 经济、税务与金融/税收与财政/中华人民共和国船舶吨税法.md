# 中华人民共和国船舶吨税法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2018-10-26  
**生效日期**：2018-10-26

> （2017年12月27日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十一次会议通过　根据2018年10月26日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第六次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国野生动物保护法〉等十五部法律的决定》修正） 第一条　自中华人民共和国境外港口进入境内港口的船舶（以下称应税船舶），应当依照本法缴纳船舶吨税（以下简称吨税）。 第二条　吨税的税目、税率依照本法所附的《吨税税目税率表》执行。 第三条　吨税设置优惠税率和普通税率。 中华人民共和国籍的应税船舶，船籍国（地区）与中华人民共和国签订含有相互给予船舶税费最惠国待遇条款的条约或者协定的应税船舶，适用优惠税率。 其他应税船舶，适用普通税率。 第四条　吨税按照船舶净吨位和吨税执照期限征收。 应税船舶负责人在每次申报纳税时，可以按照《吨税税目税率表》选择申领一种期限的吨税执照。 第五条　吨税的应纳税额按照船舶净吨位乘以适用税率计算。 第六条　吨税由海关负责征收。海关征收吨税应当制发缴款凭证。 应税船舶负责人缴纳吨税或者提供担保后，海关按照其申领的执照期限填发吨税执照。 第七条　应税船舶在进入港口办理入境手续时，应当向海关申报纳税领取吨税执照，或者交验吨税执照（或者申请核验吨税执照电子信息）。应税船舶在离开港口办理出境手续时，应当交验吨税执照（或者申请核验吨税执照电子信息）。 应税船舶负责人申领吨税执照时，应当向海关提供下列文件： （一）船舶国籍证书或者海事部门签发的船舶国籍证书收存证明； （二）船舶吨位证明。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　自中华人民共和国境外港口进入境内港口的船舶（以下称应税船舶），应当依照本法缴纳船舶吨税（以下简称吨税）。

**Article 1** Ships entering domestic ports from ports outside the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as "dutiable ships") shall pay the ship tonnage tax (hereinafter referred to as "tonnage tax") in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　吨税的税目、税率依照本法所附的《吨税税目税率表》执行。

**Article 2** The taxable items and tax rates for the tonnage tax shall be implemented in accordance with the Table of Tonnage Tax Items and Tax Rates attached to this Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　吨税设置优惠税率和普通税率。  
中华人民共和国籍的应税船舶，船籍国（地区）与中华人民共和国签订含有相互给予船舶税费最惠国待遇条款的条约或者协定的应税船舶，适用优惠税率。  
其他应税船舶，适用普通税率。

**Article 3** A preferential tax rate and a general tax rate are set for the tonnage tax.  
Dutiable ships of the nationality of the People's Republic of China, and dutiable ships whose country (region) of registry has concluded with the People's Republic of China a treaty or agreement containing a clause on mutual most-favored-nation treatment in respect of ship taxes and fees, shall apply the preferential tax rate.  
Other dutiable ships shall apply the general tax rate.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　吨税按照船舶净吨位和吨税执照期限征收。  
应税船舶负责人在每次申报纳税时，可以按照《吨税税目税率表》选择申领一种期限的吨税执照。

**Article 4** The tonnage tax shall be levied based on the net tonnage of the ship and the period of the tonnage tax license.  
When declaring and paying tax each time, the responsible person of a dutiable ship may, in accordance with the Table of Tonnage Tax Items and Tax Rates, choose to apply for a tonnage tax license for a certain period.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　吨税的应纳税额按照船舶净吨位乘以适用税率计算。

**Article 5** The amount of tonnage tax payable shall be calculated by multiplying the net tonnage of the ship by the applicable tax rate.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　吨税由海关负责征收。海关征收吨税应当制发缴款凭证。  
应税船舶负责人缴纳吨税或者提供担保后，海关按照其申领的执照期限填发吨税执照。

**Article 6** The tonnage tax shall be levied by the Customs. The Customs shall issue a payment receipt when collecting the tonnage tax.  
After the responsible person of a dutiable ship pays the tonnage tax or provides a guarantee, the Customs shall issue a tonnage tax license in accordance with the period of the license applied for.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　应税船舶在进入港口办理入境手续时，应当向海关申报纳税领取吨税执照，或者交验吨税执照（或者申请核验吨税执照电子信息）。应税船舶在离开港口办理出境手续时，应当交验吨税执照（或者申请核验吨税执照电子信息）。  
应税船舶负责人申领吨税执照时，应当向海关提供下列文件：  
（一）船舶国籍证书或者海事部门签发的船舶国籍证书收存证明；  
（二）船舶吨位证明。  
应税船舶因不可抗力在未设立海关地点停泊的，船舶负责人应当立即向附近海关报告，并在不可抗力原因消除后，依照本法规定向海关申报纳税。

**Article 7** When entering the port and going through entry formalities, a dutiable ship shall declare tax to the Customs and obtain a tonnage tax license, or present the tonnage tax license for inspection (or apply for verification of the electronic information of the tonnage tax license). When leaving the port and going through exit formalities, a dutiable ship shall present the tonnage tax license for inspection (or apply for verification of the electronic information of the tonnage tax license).  
When applying for a tonnage tax license, the responsible person of a dutiable ship shall provide the following documents to the Customs:  
(1) the ship's nationality certificate or the receipt certificate of the ship's nationality certificate issued by the maritime authority;  
(2) the ship's tonnage certificate.  
If a dutiable ship anchors at a place where no Customs office is established due to force majeure, the ship's responsible person shall immediately report to the nearest Customs office and, after the cause of force majeure is eliminated, declare tax to the Customs in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　吨税纳税义务发生时间为应税船舶进入港口的当日。  
应税船舶在吨税执照期满后尚未离开港口的，应当申领新的吨税执照，自上一次执照期满的次日起续缴吨税。

**Article 8** The time when the obligation to pay the tonnage tax arises shall be the date on which the dutiable ship enters the port.  
If a dutiable ship has not left the port after the expiration of its tonnage tax license, it shall apply for a new tonnage tax license and make supplementary payment of the tonnage tax from the day following the expiration of the previous license.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　下列船舶免征吨税：  
（一）应纳税额在人民币五十元以下的船舶；  
（二）自境外以购买、受赠、继承等方式取得船舶所有权的初次进口到港的空载船舶；  
（三）吨税执照期满后二十四小时内不上下客货的船舶；  
（四）非机动船舶（不包括非机动驳船）；  
（五）捕捞、养殖渔船；  
（六）避难、防疫隔离、修理、改造、终止运营或者拆解，并不上下客货的船舶；  
（七）军队、武装警察部队专用或者征用的船舶；  
（八）警用船舶；  
（九）依照法律规定应当予以免税的外国驻华使领馆、国际组织驻华代表机构及其有关人员的船舶；  
（十）国务院规定的其他船舶。  
前款第十项免税规定，由国务院报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 9** The following ships shall be exempted from tonnage tax:  
(1) ships with a payable tax amount of less than RMB 50 yuan;  
(2) ships that have obtained ownership from abroad by purchase, gift, succession, etc. and are entering the port for the first time and are empty;  
(3) ships that do not embark or disembark passengers or load or unload cargo within 24 hours after the expiration of the tonnage tax license;  
(4) non-motorized ships (excluding non-motorized barges);  
(5) fishing and aquaculture vessels;  
(6) ships for refuge, epidemic prevention isolation, repair, refitting, termination of operation or scrapping, and which do not embark or disembark passengers or load or unload cargo;  
(7) ships exclusively used or requisitioned by the armed forces or the armed police forces;  
(8) police ships;  
(9) ships of foreign embassies and consulates in China, representative offices of international organizations in China, and their relevant personnel that shall be exempted from tax according to law;  
(10) other ships as prescribed by the State Council.  
The exemption provisions in item (10) of the preceding paragraph shall be submitted by the State Council to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for recordation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在吨税执照期限内，应税船舶发生下列情形之一的，海关按照实际发生的天数批注延长吨税执照期限：  
（一）避难、防疫隔离、修理、改造，并不上下客货；  
（二）军队、武装警察部队征用。

**Article 10** If, within the period of the tonnage tax license, a dutiable ship falls under any of the following circumstances, the Customs shall endorse the extension of the period of the tonnage tax license according to the actual number of days:  
(1) refuge, epidemic prevention isolation, repair, or refitting, and it does not embark or disembark passengers or load or unload cargo;  
(2) requisition by the armed forces or the armed police forces.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　符合[本法第九条](#art-9)第一款第五项至第九项、第十条规定的船舶，应当提供海事部门、渔业船舶管理部门等部门、机构出具的具有法律效力的证明文件或者使用关系证明文件，申明免税或者延长吨税执照期限的依据和理由。

**Article 11** Ships that meet the provisions of items (5) to (9) of the first paragraph of Article 9 and Article 10 of this Law shall provide legally effective supporting documents or certificates of usage relations issued by the maritime authority, fishery ship administration department, or other departments or agencies, stating the basis and reasons for exemption from tax or extension of the period of the tonnage tax license.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　应税船舶负责人应当自海关填发吨税缴款凭证之日起十五日内缴清税款。未按期缴清税款的，自滞纳税款之日起至缴清税款之日止，按日加收滞纳税款万分之五的税款滞纳金。

**Article 12** The responsible person of a dutiable ship shall pay the tax in full within 15 days from the date on which the Customs issues the tonnage tax payment receipt. If the tax is not paid in full within the prescribed time limit, a late payment surcharge of 0.05% of the overdue tax shall be imposed per day from the date on which the tax becomes overdue to the date on which it is paid in full.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　应税船舶到达港口前，经海关核准先行申报并办结出入境手续的，应税船舶负责人应当向海关提供与其依法履行吨税缴纳义务相适应的担保；应税船舶到达港口后，依照本法规定向海关申报纳税。  
下列财产、权利可以用于担保：  
（一）人民币、可自由兑换货币；  
（二）汇票、本票、支票、债券、存单；  
（三）银行、非银行金融机构的保函；  
（四）海关依法认可的其他财产、权利。

**Article 13** If, before a dutiable ship arrives at the port, it has been approved by the Customs to make an advance declaration and complete the entry and exit formalities, the responsible person of the dutiable ship shall provide the Customs with a guarantee commensurate with its performance of the obligation to pay the tonnage tax according to law; after the dutiable ship arrives at the port, it shall declare tax to the Customs in accordance with this Law.  
The following property and rights may be used for guarantee:  
(1) Renminbi or freely convertible currencies;  
(2) drafts, promissory notes, checks, bonds, and certificates of deposit;  
(3) letters of guarantee issued by banks or non-bank financial institutions;  
(4) other property and rights recognized by the Customs according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　应税船舶在吨税执照期限内，因修理、改造导致净吨位变化的，吨税执照继续有效。应税船舶办理出入境手续时，应当提供船舶经过修理、改造的证明文件。

**Article 14** If, within the period of the tonnage tax license, the net tonnage of a dutiable ship changes due to repair or refitting, the tonnage tax license shall remain valid. When going through entry and exit formalities, the dutiable ship shall provide supporting documents proving that the ship has been repaired or refitted.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　应税船舶在吨税执照期限内，因税目税率调整或者船籍改变而导致适用税率变化的，吨税执照继续有效。  
因船籍改变而导致适用税率变化的，应税船舶在办理出入境手续时，应当提供船籍改变的证明文件。

**Article 15** If, within the period of the tonnage tax license, the applicable tax rate changes due to adjustment of tax items or tax rates or change of ship registry, the tonnage tax license shall remain valid.  
If the applicable tax rate changes due to a change of ship registry, the dutiable ship shall, when going through entry and exit formalities, provide supporting documents proving the change of ship registry.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　吨税执照在期满前毁损或者遗失的，应当向原发照海关书面申请核发吨税执照副本，不再补税。

**Article 16** If a tonnage tax license is damaged or lost before its expiration, the person concerned shall apply in writing to the original Customs office that issued the license for a duplicate of the tonnage tax license, and no additional tax shall be paid.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　海关发现少征或者漏征税款的，应当自应税船舶应当缴纳税款之日起一年内，补征税款。但因应税船舶违反规定造成少征或者漏征税款的，海关可以自应当缴纳税款之日起三年内追征税款，并自应当缴纳税款之日起按日加征少征或者漏征税款万分之五的税款滞纳金。  
海关发现多征税款的，应当在二十四小时内通知应税船舶办理退还手续，并加算银行同期活期存款利息。  
应税船舶发现多缴税款的，可以自缴纳税款之日起三年内以书面形式要求海关退还多缴的税款并加算银行同期活期存款利息；海关应当自受理退税申请之日起三十日内查实并通知应税船舶办理退还手续。  
应税船舶应当自收到本条第二款、第三款规定的通知之日起三个月内办理有关退还手续。

**Article 17** If the Customs discovers that a tax has been under-levied or omitted, it shall recover the tax within one year from the date on which the tax should have been paid by the dutiable ship. However, if the under-levy or omission is caused by the dutiable ship's violation of regulations, the Customs may recover the tax within three years from the date on which the tax should have been paid, and shall, from the date on which the tax should have been paid, impose a late payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on the under-levied or omitted tax.  
If the Customs discovers that a tax has been over-levied, it shall notify the dutiable ship to go through the refund formalities within 24 hours, and shall add interest calculated at the current deposit interest rate of banks for the same period.  
If a dutiable ship discovers that it has overpaid tax, it may, within three years from the date of tax payment, request in writing the Customs to refund the overpaid tax with interest calculated at the current deposit interest rate of banks for the same period; the Customs shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the refund application, verify the matter and notify the dutiable ship to go through the refund formalities.  
A dutiable ship shall go through the relevant refund formalities within three months from the date of receiving the notice provided for in paragraphs 2 and 3 of this Article.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　应税船舶有下列行为之一的，由海关责令限期改正，处二千元以上三万元以下的罚款；不缴或者少缴应纳税款的，处不缴或者少缴税款百分之五十以上五倍以下的罚款，但罚款不得低于二千元：  
（一）未按照规定申报纳税、领取吨税执照；  
（二）未按照规定交验吨税执照（或者申请核验吨税执照电子信息）以及提供其他证明文件。

**Article 18** If a dutiable ship commits any of the following acts, the Customs shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit and impose a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 30,000 yuan; if it fails to pay or underpays the tax payable, a fine of not less than 50% but not more than five times the amount of tax unpaid or underpaid shall be imposed, provided that the fine shall not be less than RMB 2,000 yuan:  
(1) failing to declare tax and obtain a tonnage tax license in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) failing to present the tonnage tax license for inspection (or apply for verification of the electronic information of the tonnage tax license) or provide other supporting documents in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　吨税税款、税款滞纳金、罚款以人民币计算。

**Article 19** The tonnage tax, late payment surcharge, and fines shall be calculated in Renminbi.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　吨税的征收，本法未作规定的，依照有关税收征收管理的法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 20** If no provisions are made in this Law regarding the collection of the tonnage tax, the relevant laws and administrative regulations on tax collection and administration shall apply.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本法及所附《吨税税目税率表》下列用语的含义：  
净吨位，是指由船籍国（地区）政府签发或者授权签发的船舶吨位证明书上标明的净吨位。  
非机动船舶，是指自身没有动力装置，依靠外力驱动的船舶。  
非机动驳船，是指在船舶登记机关登记为驳船的非机动船舶。  
捕捞、养殖渔船，是指在中华人民共和国渔业船舶管理部门登记为捕捞船或者养殖船的船舶。  
拖船，是指专门用于拖（推）动运输船舶的专业作业船舶。  
吨税执照期限，是指按照公历年、日计算的期间。

**Article 21** For the purposes of this Law and the attached Table of Tonnage Tax Items and Tax Rates, the following terms have the meanings as defined below:  
"Net tonnage" means the net tonnage specified in the ship tonnage certificate issued or authorized to be issued by the government of the country (region) of registry.  
"Non-motorized ship" means a ship that has no power device of its own and is driven by external force.  
"Non-motorized barge" means a non-motorized ship registered as a barge with the ship registration authority.  
"Fishing and aquaculture vessel" means a vessel registered as a fishing vessel or an aquaculture vessel with the fishery ship administration department of the People's Republic of China.  
"Tugboat" means a specialized operational vessel used exclusively for towing (pushing) transport ships.  
"Period of tonnage tax license" means the period calculated according to the calendar year or calendar day.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本法自2018年7月1日起施行。2011年12月5日国务院公布的《中华人民共和国船舶吨税暂行条例》同时废止。
