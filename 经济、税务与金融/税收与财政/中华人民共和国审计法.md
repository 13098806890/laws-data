# 中华人民共和国审计法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2021-10-23  
**生效日期**：2022-01-01

> （1994年8月31日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第九次会议通过　根据2006年2月28日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国审计法〉的决定》第一次修正　根据2021年10月23日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十一次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国审计法〉的决定》第二次修正）

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强国家的审计监督，维护国家财政经济秩序，提高财政资金使用效益，促进廉政建设，保障国民经济和社会健康发展，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution to strengthen the state's audit supervision, maintain the order of national fiscal and economic affairs, improve the efficiency of fiscal fund utilization, promote integrity building, and ensure the healthy development of the national economy and society.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家实行审计监督制度。坚持中国共产党对审计工作的领导，构建集中统一、全面覆盖、权威高效的审计监督体系。  
国务院和县级以上地方人民政府设立审计机关。  
国务院各部门和地方各级人民政府及其各部门的财政收支，国有的金融机构和企业事业组织的财务收支，以及其他依照本法规定应当接受审计的财政收支、财务收支，依照本法规定接受审计监督。  
审计机关对前款所列财政收支或者财务收支的真实、合法和效益，依法进行审计监督。

**Article 2** The state applies an audit supervision system. It shall uphold the leadership of the Communist Party of China over audit work and establish a centralized, unified, comprehensive, authoritative, and efficient audit supervision system.  
The State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish audit authorities.  
The fiscal revenues and expenditures of all departments of the State Council and local people's governments at all levels and their departments, the financial revenues and expenditures of state-owned financial institutions, enterprises, and public institutions, as well as other fiscal revenues and expenditures and financial revenues and expenditures that shall be subject to audit in accordance with this Law, shall accept audit supervision in accordance with this Law.  
Audit authorities shall, according to law, conduct audit supervision over the truthfulness, legality, and efficiency of the fiscal revenues and expenditures or financial revenues and expenditures listed in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　审计机关依照法律规定的职权和程序，进行审计监督。  
审计机关依据有关财政收支、财务收支的法律、法规和国家其他有关规定进行审计评价，在法定职权范围内作出审计决定。

**Article 3** Audit authorities shall conduct audit supervision in accordance with the functions, powers, and procedures prescribed by law.  
Audit authorities shall make audit evaluations based on laws, regulations, and other relevant state provisions concerning fiscal revenues and expenditures and financial revenues and expenditures, and shall make audit decisions within the statutory scope of functions and powers.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院和县级以上地方人民政府应当每年向本级人民代表大会常务委员会提出审计工作报告。审计工作报告应当报告审计机关对预算执行、决算草案以及其他财政收支的审计情况，重点报告对预算执行及其绩效的审计情况，按照有关法律、行政法规的规定报告对国有资源、国有资产的审计情况。必要时，人民代表大会常务委员会可以对审计工作报告作出决议。  
国务院和县级以上地方人民政府应当将审计工作报告中指出的问题的整改情况和处理结果向本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告。

**Article 4** The State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall each year submit an audit work report to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level. The audit work report shall report the audit findings of audit authorities on budget implementation, draft final accounts, and other fiscal revenues and expenditures, with a focus on reporting the audit findings on budget implementation and its performance, and shall report the audit findings on state resources and state assets in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations. When necessary, the standing committee of the people's congress may make a resolution on the audit work report.  
The State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall report to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level on the rectification status and handling results of the issues pointed out in the audit work report.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　审计机关依照法律规定独立行使审计监督权，不受其他行政机关、社会团体和个人的干涉。

**Article 5** Audit authorities shall independently exercise their power of audit supervision in accordance with the provisions of law and shall not be subject to interference by any other administrative organs, social organizations, or individuals.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　审计机关和审计人员办理审计事项，应当客观公正，实事求是，廉洁奉公，保守秘密。

**Article 6** Audit authorities and auditors, when handling audit matters, shall be objective and fair, seek truth from facts, be honest and upright, and maintain confidentiality.

### 第二章　审计机关和审计人员

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院设立审计署，在国务院总理领导下，主管全国的审计工作。审计长是审计署的行政首长。

**Article 7** The State Council establishes the National Audit Office, which, under the leadership of the Premier of the State Council, shall take charge of the national audit work. The Auditor General shall be the administrative head of the National Audit Office.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　省、自治区、直辖市、设区的市、自治州、县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府的审计机关，分别在省长、自治区主席、市长、州长、县长、区长和上一级审计机关的领导下，负责本行政区域内的审计工作。

**Article 8** The audit authorities of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, cities divided into districts, autonomous prefectures, counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, and municipal districts shall, under the respective leadership of the governor, chairman of the autonomous region, mayor, prefect, county head, district head and the audit authority at the next higher level, be responsible for the audit work within their respective administrative areas.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　地方各级审计机关对本级人民政府和上一级审计机关负责并报告工作，审计业务以上级审计机关领导为主。

**Article 9** Local audit authorities at all levels shall be responsible to and report on their work to the people's government at the corresponding level and the audit authority at the next higher level, and their audit operations shall be primarily subject to the leadership of the audit authority at the higher level.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　审计机关根据工作需要，经本级人民政府批准，可以在其审计管辖范围内设立派出机构。  
派出机构根据审计机关的授权，依法进行审计工作。

**Article 10** Audit authorities may, according to work needs and with the approval of the people's government at the corresponding level, establish representative offices within their audit jurisdiction.  
Representative offices shall conduct audit work in accordance with law based on the authorization of the audit authority.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　审计机关履行职责所必需的经费，应当列入预算予以保证。

**Article 11** The necessary funds for audit authorities to perform their duties shall be included in the budget and guaranteed.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　审计机关应当建设信念坚定、为民服务、业务精通、作风务实、敢于担当、清正廉洁的高素质专业化审计队伍。  
审计机关应当加强对审计人员遵守法律和执行职务情况的监督，督促审计人员依法履职尽责。  
审计机关和审计人员应当依法接受监督。

**Article 12** Audit authorities shall build a highly competent and professional audit team that is firm in conviction, serves the people, proficient in business, pragmatic in work style, bold in taking responsibility, and honest and upright.  
Audit authorities shall strengthen supervision over auditors' compliance with the law and performance of their duties, and urge auditors to perform their duties and responsibilities according to law.  
Audit authorities and auditors shall accept supervision according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　审计人员应当具备与其从事的审计工作相适应的专业知识和业务能力。  
审计机关根据工作需要，可以聘请具有与审计事项相关专业知识的人员参加审计工作。

**Article 13** Auditors shall possess professional knowledge and competence commensurate with the audit work they engage in.  
Audit authorities may, according to work needs, engage persons with professional knowledge relevant to audit matters to participate in audit work.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　审计机关和审计人员不得参加可能影响其依法独立履行审计监督职责的活动，不得干预、插手被审计单位及其相关单位的正常生产经营和管理活动。

**Article 14** Audit authorities and auditors shall not participate in any activities that may affect their independent performance of audit supervision duties in accordance with law, and shall not intervene in or meddle with the normal production, operation, and management activities of the auditees and their related units.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　审计人员办理审计事项，与被审计单位或者审计事项有利害关系的，应当回避。

**Article 15** Where an auditor, when handling an audit matter, has an interest in the auditee or the audit matter, such auditor shall withdraw.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　审计机关和审计人员对在执行职务中知悉的国家秘密、工作秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私和个人信息，应当予以保密，不得泄露或者向他人非法提供。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国审计法实施条例.md#art-52" title="被《中华人民共和国审计法实施条例》第52条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 16** Audit authorities and auditors shall keep confidential any state secrets, work secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, and personal information known to them in the course of performing their duties, and shall not disclose or illegally provide them to others.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　审计人员依法执行职务，受法律保护。  
任何组织和个人不得拒绝、阻碍审计人员依法执行职务，不得打击报复审计人员。  
审计机关负责人依照法定程序任免。审计机关负责人没有违法失职或者其他不符合任职条件的情况的，不得随意撤换。  
地方各级审计机关负责人的任免，应当事先征求上一级审计机关的意见。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国审计法实施条例.md#art-17" title="被《中华人民共和国审计法实施条例》第17条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 17** Auditors' performance of their duties according to law shall be protected by law.  
No organization or individual may refuse or obstruct an auditor from performing duties according to law, or retaliate against an auditor.  
Heads of audit authorities shall be appointed and removed in accordance with statutory procedures. Where a head of an audit authority has no illegal or derelict conduct or other circumstances that do not meet the conditions for holding the position, such head shall not be removed arbitrarily.  
The appointment and removal of heads of local audit authorities at all levels shall be subject to prior consultation with the audit authority at the next higher level.

### 第三章　审计机关职责

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　审计机关对本级各部门（含直属单位）和下级政府预算的执行情况和决算以及其他财政收支情况，进行审计监督。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国审计法实施条例.md#art-19" title="被《中华人民共和国审计法实施条例》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 18** Audit authorities shall conduct audit supervision over the implementation of budgets and final accounts of all departments at the corresponding level (including directly subordinate units) and governments at lower levels, as well as other fiscal revenues and expenditures.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　审计署在国务院总理领导下，对中央预算执行情况、决算草案以及其他财政收支情况进行审计监督，向国务院总理提出审计结果报告。  
地方各级审计机关分别在省长、自治区主席、市长、州长、县长、区长和上一级审计机关的领导下，对本级预算执行情况、决算草案以及其他财政收支情况进行审计监督，向本级人民政府和上一级审计机关提出审计结果报告。

**Article 19** The National Audit Office shall, under the leadership of the Premier of the State Council, conduct audit supervision over the implementation of the central budget, draft final accounts, and other fiscal revenues and expenditures, and shall submit an audit findings report to the Premier of the State Council.  
Local audit authorities at all levels shall, under the respective leadership of the governor, chairman of the autonomous region, mayor, prefect, county head, district head and the audit authority at the next higher level, conduct audit supervision over the implementation of the budget at the corresponding level, draft final accounts, and other fiscal revenues and expenditures, and shall submit audit findings reports to the people's government at the corresponding level and the audit authority at the next higher level.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　审计署对中央银行的财务收支，进行审计监督。

**Article 20** The National Audit Office shall conduct audit supervision over the financial revenues and expenditures of the central bank.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　审计机关对国家的事业组织和使用财政资金的其他事业组织的财务收支，进行审计监督。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国审计法实施条例.md#art-19" title="被《中华人民共和国审计法实施条例》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 21** Audit institutions shall conduct audit supervision over the financial revenues and expenditures of state institutions and other public institutions that use fiscal funds.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　审计机关对国有企业、国有金融机构和国有资本占控股地位或者主导地位的企业、金融机构的资产、负债、损益以及其他财务收支情况，进行审计监督。  
遇有涉及国家财政金融重大利益情形，为维护国家经济安全，经国务院批准，审计署可以对前款规定以外的金融机构进行专项审计调查或者审计。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国审计法实施条例.md#art-20" title="被《中华人民共和国审计法实施条例》第20条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 22** Audit institutions shall conduct audit supervision over the assets, liabilities, profits and losses, and other financial revenues and expenditures of state-owned enterprises, state-owned financial institutions, and enterprises and financial institutions in which state-owned capital holds a controlling or dominant position.  
In cases involving vital interests of the state's fiscal and financial sectors, to safeguard national economic security, and upon approval by the State Council, the National Audit Office may conduct special audit investigations or audits on financial institutions other than those specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　审计机关对政府投资和以政府投资为主的建设项目的预算执行情况和决算，对其他关系国家利益和公共利益的重大公共工程项目的资金管理使用和建设运营情况，进行审计监督。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国审计法实施条例.md#art-21" title="被《中华人民共和国审计法实施条例》第21条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 23** Audit institutions shall conduct audit supervision over the budget implementation and final accounts of government-invested and government-investment-dominated construction projects, and over the fund management, use, construction and operation of other major public works projects that concern the national interests and public interests.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　审计机关对国有资源、国有资产，进行审计监督。  
审计机关对政府部门管理的和其他单位受政府委托管理的社会保险基金、全国社会保障基金、社会捐赠资金以及其他公共资金的财务收支，进行审计监督。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国审计法实施条例.md#art-22" title="被《中华人民共和国审计法实施条例》第22条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 24** Audit institutions shall conduct audit supervision over state-owned resources and state-owned assets.  
Audit institutions shall conduct audit supervision over the financial revenues and expenditures of social insurance funds, the National Social Security Fund, social donation funds, and other public funds managed by government departments or entrusted by the government to other entities.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　审计机关对国际组织和外国政府援助、贷款项目的财务收支，进行审计监督。

**Article 25** Audit institutions shall conduct audit supervision over the financial revenues and expenditures of projects receiving aid or loans from international organizations and foreign governments.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　根据经批准的审计项目计划安排，审计机关可以对被审计单位贯彻落实国家重大经济社会政策措施情况进行审计监督。

**Article 26** In accordance with the approved audit project plan arrangements, audit institutions may conduct audit supervision over the implementation of major national economic and social policies and measures by entities under audit.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　除本法规定的审计事项外，审计机关对其他法律、行政法规规定应当由审计机关进行审计的事项，依照本法和有关法律、行政法规的规定进行审计监督。

**Article 27** In addition to the audit matters specified in this Law, audit institutions shall conduct audit supervision over matters that shall be audited by audit institutions as required by other laws and administrative regulations, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　审计机关可以对被审计单位依法应当接受审计的事项进行全面审计，也可以对其中的特定事项进行专项审计。

**Article 28** Audit institutions may conduct a comprehensive audit of matters that an entity under audit shall accept audit according to law, or may conduct a special audit of specific matters therein.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　审计机关有权对与国家财政收支有关的特定事项，向有关地方、部门、单位进行专项审计调查，并向本级人民政府和上一级审计机关报告审计调查结果。

**Article 29** Audit institutions shall have the power to conduct special audit investigations on specific matters related to state fiscal revenues and expenditures with relevant localities, departments, and entities, and shall report the audit investigation results to the people's government at the same level and the audit institution at the next higher level.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　审计机关履行审计监督职责，发现经济社会运行中存在风险隐患的，应当及时向本级人民政府报告或者向有关主管机关、单位通报。

**Article 30** Audit institutions, in performing their audit supervision duties, shall promptly report to the people's government at the same level or notify the relevant competent authorities or entities if they discover potential risks in the operation of the economy and society.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　审计机关根据被审计单位的财政、财务隶属关系或者国有资源、国有资产监督管理关系，确定审计管辖范围。  
审计机关之间对审计管辖范围有争议的，由其共同的上级审计机关确定。  
上级审计机关对其审计管辖范围内的审计事项，可以授权下级审计机关进行审计，但[本法第十八条](#art-18)至第二十条规定的审计事项不得进行授权；上级审计机关对下级审计机关审计管辖范围内的重大审计事项，可以直接进行审计，但是应当防止不必要的重复审计。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国审计法实施条例.md#art-28" title="被《中华人民共和国审计法实施条例》第28条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 31** Audit institutions shall determine audit jurisdiction based on the fiscal and financial subordination relationship or the state-owned resources and state-owned assets supervision and management relationship of the entity under audit.  
If there is a dispute over the scope of audit jurisdiction between audit institutions, it shall be determined by their common higher-level audit institution.  
A higher-level audit institution may authorize a lower-level audit institution to audit matters within its audit jurisdiction, except for the audit matters specified in Articles 18 to 20 of this Law; a higher-level audit institution may directly audit major audit matters within the audit jurisdiction of a lower-level audit institution, but shall avoid unnecessary duplication of audits.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　被审计单位应当加强对内部审计工作的领导，按照国家有关规定建立健全内部审计制度。  
审计机关应当对被审计单位的内部审计工作进行业务指导和监督。

**Article 32** Entities under audit shall strengthen leadership over internal audit work and establish and improve internal audit systems in accordance with relevant state regulations.  
Audit institutions shall provide professional guidance and supervision over the internal audit work of entities under audit.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　社会审计机构审计的单位依法属于被审计单位的，审计机关按照国务院的规定，有权对该社会审计机构出具的相关审计报告进行核查。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国审计法实施条例.md#art-30" title="被《中华人民共和国审计法实施条例》第30条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 33** If an entity audited by a social audit institution is an entity under audit as prescribed by this Law, the audit institution shall, in accordance with the regulations of the State Council, have the power to verify the relevant audit reports issued by such social audit institution.

### 第四章　审计机关权限

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　审计机关有权要求被审计单位按照审计机关的规定提供财务、会计资料以及与财政收支、财务收支有关的业务、管理等资料，包括电子数据和有关文档。被审计单位不得拒绝、拖延、谎报。  
被审计单位负责人应当对本单位提供资料的及时性、真实性和完整性负责。  
审计机关对取得的电子数据等资料进行综合分析，需要向被审计单位核实有关情况的，被审计单位应当予以配合。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国审计法实施条例.md#art-31" title="被《中华人民共和国审计法实施条例》第31条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国审计法实施条例.md#art-32" title="被《中华人民共和国审计法实施条例》第32条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 34** Audit institutions shall have the power to require entities under audit to provide, in accordance with the regulations of the audit institution, financial and accounting materials as well as business, management and other materials related to fiscal and financial revenues and expenditures, including electronic data and relevant documents. Entities under audit shall not refuse, delay, or make false reports.  
The person in charge of the entity under audit shall be responsible for the timeliness, authenticity and completeness of the materials provided by the entity.  
If the audit institution, after conducting comprehensive analysis of the acquired electronic data and other materials, needs to verify relevant information with the entity under audit, the entity under audit shall cooperate.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家政务信息系统和数据共享平台应当按照规定向审计机关开放。  
审计机关通过政务信息系统和数据共享平台取得的电子数据等资料能够满足需要的，不得要求被审计单位重复提供。

**Article 35** The national government affairs information system and data sharing platform shall be opened to audit institutions in accordance with regulations.  
If the electronic data and other materials obtained by the audit institution through the government affairs information system and data sharing platform are sufficient to meet the needs, the audit institution shall not require the entity under audit to provide them repeatedly.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　审计机关进行审计时，有权检查被审计单位的财务、会计资料以及与财政收支、财务收支有关的业务、管理等资料和资产，有权检查被审计单位信息系统的安全性、可靠性、经济性，被审计单位不得拒绝。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国审计法实施条例.md#art-33" title="被《中华人民共和国审计法实施条例》第33条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 36** When conducting an audit, audit institutions shall have the power to inspect the financial and accounting materials, as well as the business, management and other materials and assets related to fiscal and financial revenues and expenditures of the entity under audit, and shall have the power to inspect the security, reliability and economy of the information system of the entity under audit; the entity under audit shall not refuse.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　审计机关进行审计时，有权就审计事项的有关问题向有关单位和个人进行调查，并取得有关证明材料。有关单位和个人应当支持、协助审计机关工作，如实向审计机关反映情况，提供有关证明材料。  
审计机关经县级以上人民政府审计机关负责人批准，有权查询被审计单位在金融机构的账户。  
审计机关有证据证明被审计单位违反国家规定将公款转入其他单位、个人在金融机构账户的，经县级以上人民政府审计机关主要负责人批准，有权查询有关单位、个人在金融机构与审计事项相关的存款。

**Article 37** When conducting an audit, audit institutions shall have the power to investigate relevant issues concerning the audit matters with relevant entities and individuals, and to obtain relevant evidentiary materials. Relevant entities and individuals shall support and assist the work of the audit institution, truthfully report the situation to the audit institution, and provide relevant evidentiary materials.  
Upon approval by the person in charge of the audit institution of the people's government at or above the county level, an audit institution shall have the power to inquire into the accounts of the entity under audit with financial institutions.  
If the audit institution has evidence proving that the entity under audit, in violation of state regulations, has transferred public funds to accounts of other entities or individuals with financial institutions, upon approval by the principal person in charge of the audit institution of the people's government at or above the county level, the audit institution shall have the power to inquire into the deposits of such relevant entities or individuals with financial institutions that are related to the audit matters.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　审计机关进行审计时，被审计单位不得转移、隐匿、篡改、毁弃财务、会计资料以及与财政收支、财务收支有关的业务、管理等资料，不得转移、隐匿、故意毁损所持有的违反国家规定取得的资产。  
审计机关对被审计单位违反前款规定的行为，有权予以制止；必要时，经县级以上人民政府审计机关负责人批准，有权封存有关资料和违反国家规定取得的资产；对其中在金融机构的有关存款需要予以冻结的，应当向人民法院提出申请。  
审计机关对被审计单位正在进行的违反国家规定的财政收支、财务收支行为，有权予以制止；制止无效的，经县级以上人民政府审计机关负责人批准，通知财政部门和有关主管机关、单位暂停拨付与违反国家规定的财政收支、财务收支行为直接有关的款项，已经拨付的，暂停使用。  
审计机关采取前两款规定的措施不得影响被审计单位合法的业务活动和生产经营活动。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国审计法实施条例.md#art-36" title="被《中华人民共和国审计法实施条例》第36条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 38** When an audit institution is conducting an audit, the entity under audit shall not transfer, conceal, alter, or destroy financial and accounting materials, or business, management and other materials related to fiscal and financial revenues and expenditures, and shall not transfer, conceal, or intentionally damage assets held that were obtained in violation of state regulations.  
An audit institution shall have the power to stop any act of the entity under audit that violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph; if necessary, upon approval by the person in charge of the audit institution of the people's government at or above the county level, the audit institution shall have the power to seal up the relevant materials and the assets obtained in violation of state regulations; if the relevant deposits with financial institutions need to be frozen, the audit institution shall apply to a People's Court.  
An audit institution shall have the power to stop any ongoing act of the entity under audit that violates state regulations on fiscal revenues and expenditures or financial revenues and expenditures; if the stop is ineffective, upon approval by the person in charge of the audit institution of the people's government at or above the county level, the audit institution shall notify the fiscal department and relevant competent authorities or entities to suspend the allocation of funds directly related to the act that violates state regulations on fiscal revenues and expenditures or financial revenues and expenditures, and if disbursed, to suspend the use thereof.  
The measures taken by the audit institution as specified in the preceding two paragraphs shall not affect the lawful business activities and production and operation activities of the entity under audit.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　审计机关认为被审计单位所执行的上级主管机关、单位有关财政收支、财务收支的规定与法律、行政法规相抵触的，应当建议有关主管机关、单位纠正；有关主管机关、单位不予纠正的，审计机关应当提请有权处理的机关、单位依法处理。

**Article 39** If an audit institution considers that the provisions on fiscal revenues and expenditures or financial revenues and expenditures of the higher-level competent authorities or entities implemented by the entity under audit contravene laws or administrative regulations, the audit institution shall suggest that the relevant competent authorities or entities make corrections; if the relevant competent authorities or entities fail to make corrections, the audit institution shall submit the matter to the authorities or entities with the power to handle it according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　审计机关可以向政府有关部门通报或者向社会公布审计结果。  
审计机关通报或者公布审计结果，应当保守国家秘密、工作秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私和个人信息，遵守法律、行政法规和国务院的有关规定。

**Article 40** Audit institutions may notify the relevant government departments or announce the audit results to the public.  
When notifying or announcing audit results, audit institutions shall maintain state secrets, work secrets, trade secrets, individual privacy and personal information, and comply with laws, administrative regulations and relevant regulations of the State Council.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　审计机关履行审计监督职责，可以提请公安、财政、自然资源、生态环境、海关、税务、市场监督管理等机关予以协助。有关机关应当依法予以配合。

**Article 41** : When performing the duty of audit supervision, an audit authority may request assistance from public security, finance, natural resources, ecological environment, customs, taxation, market regulation and other authorities. The relevant authorities shall cooperate according to law.

### 第五章　审计程序

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　审计机关根据经批准的审计项目计划确定的审计事项组成审计组，并应当在实施审计三日前，向被审计单位送达审计通知书；遇有特殊情况，经县级以上人民政府审计机关负责人批准，可以直接持审计通知书实施审计。  
被审计单位应当配合审计机关的工作，并提供必要的工作条件。  
审计机关应当提高审计工作效率。

**Article 42** : An audit authority shall form an audit team based on the audit matters determined in the approved audit project plan, and shall serve an audit notice on the auditee three days before implementing the audit; under special circumstances, with the approval of the head of the audit authority of the people's government at or above the county level, it may directly implement the audit with the audit notice.  
The auditee shall cooperate with the work of the audit authority and provide necessary working conditions.  
The audit authority shall improve the efficiency of audit work.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　审计人员通过审查财务、会计资料，查阅与审计事项有关的文件、资料，检查现金、实物、有价证券和信息系统，向有关单位和个人调查等方式进行审计，并取得证明材料。  
向有关单位和个人进行调查时，审计人员应当不少于二人，并出示其工作证件和审计通知书副本。

**Article 43** : Audit personnel shall conduct audits by examining financial and accounting materials, consulting documents and materials related to audit matters, inspecting cash, physical assets, securities and information systems, and investigating relevant entities and individuals, and shall obtain evidentiary materials.  
When investigating relevant entities and individuals, there shall be no fewer than two audit personnel, and they shall present their work credentials and a copy of the audit notice.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　审计组对审计事项实施审计后，应当向审计机关提出审计组的审计报告。审计组的审计报告报送审计机关前，应当征求被审计单位的意见。被审计单位应当自接到审计组的审计报告之日起十日内，将其书面意见送交审计组。审计组应当将被审计单位的书面意见一并报送审计机关。

**Article 44** : After implementing an audit on an audit matter, the audit team shall submit an audit report of the audit team to the audit authority. Before submitting the audit report of the audit team to the audit authority, the audit team shall seek the opinions of the auditee. The auditee shall, within ten days from the date of receiving the audit report of the audit team, deliver its written opinions to the audit team. The audit team shall submit the written opinions of the auditee together with its audit report to the audit authority.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　审计机关按照审计署规定的程序对审计组的审计报告进行审议，并对被审计单位对审计组的审计报告提出的意见一并研究后，出具审计机关的审计报告。对违反国家规定的财政收支、财务收支行为，依法应当给予处理、处罚的，审计机关在法定职权范围内作出审计决定；需要移送有关主管机关、单位处理、处罚的，审计机关应当依法移送。  
审计机关应当将审计机关的审计报告和审计决定送达被审计单位和有关主管机关、单位，并报上一级审计机关。审计决定自送达之日起生效。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国审计法实施条例.md#art-48" title="被《中华人民共和国审计法实施条例》第48条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国审计法实施条例.md#art-49" title="被《中华人民共和国审计法实施条例》第49条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 45** : The audit authority shall review the audit report of the audit team in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the National Audit Office, and after studying the opinions of the auditee on the audit report of the audit team, shall issue an audit report of the audit authority. If an act of financial revenues and expenditures or financial revenues and expenditures that violates state regulations shall be subject to sanctions or penalties according to law, the audit authority shall make an audit decision within its statutory authority; if the matter needs to be transferred to a competent authority or entity for sanctions or penalties, the audit authority shall transfer it according to law.  
The audit authority shall serve the audit report and audit decision of the audit authority on the auditee and the relevant competent authorities or entities, and shall report them to the audit authority at the next higher level. The audit decision shall take effect from the date of service.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　上级审计机关认为下级审计机关作出的审计决定违反国家有关规定的，可以责成下级审计机关予以变更或者撤销，必要时也可以直接作出变更或者撤销的决定。

**Article 46** : If the audit authority at a higher level considers that an audit decision made by the audit authority at a lower level violates relevant state regulations, it may order the lower-level audit authority to modify or revoke the decision, or may directly make a decision to modify or revoke it when necessary.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　被审计单位违反本法规定，拒绝、拖延提供与审计事项有关的资料的，或者提供的资料不真实、不完整的，或者拒绝、阻碍检查、调查、核实有关情况的，由审计机关责令改正，可以通报批评，给予警告；拒不改正的，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 47** : If an auditee, in violation of the provisions of this Law, refuses or delays providing materials related to audit matters, or provides materials that are not truthful or incomplete, or refuses or obstructs inspection, investigation or verification of relevant circumstances, the audit authority shall order it to make corrections, may circulate a notice of criticism or give a warning; if it refuses to make corrections, legal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　被审计单位违反本法规定，转移、隐匿、篡改、毁弃财务、会计资料以及与财政收支、财务收支有关的业务、管理等资料，或者转移、隐匿、故意毁损所持有的违反国家规定取得的资产，审计机关认为对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法应当给予处分的，应当向被审计单位提出处理建议，或者移送监察机关和有关主管机关、单位处理，有关机关、单位应当将处理结果书面告知审计机关；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 48** : If an auditee, in violation of the provisions of this Law, transfers, conceals, alters, or destroys financial and accounting materials, and business, management and other materials related to financial revenues and expenditures or financial revenues and expenditures, or transfers, conceals or intentionally damages assets held by it that have been obtained in violation of state regulations, and the audit authority considers that the person directly in charge and the other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law, it shall propose handling suggestions to the auditee, or transfer the matter to the supervisory authority or the competent authority or entity for handling; the relevant authority or entity shall inform the audit authority in writing of the handling result; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　对本级各部门（含直属单位）和下级政府违反预算的行为或者其他违反国家规定的财政收支行为，审计机关、人民政府或者有关主管机关、单位在法定职权范围内，依照法律、行政法规的规定，区别情况采取下列处理措施：  
（一）责令限期缴纳应当上缴的款项；  
（二）责令限期退还被侵占的国有资产；  
（三）责令限期退还违法所得；  
（四）责令按照国家统一的财务、会计制度的有关规定进行处理；  
（五）其他处理措施。

**Article 49** : For acts of violating the budget or other acts of financial revenues and expenditures that violate state regulations committed by the departments (including directly subordinate units) at the corresponding level and by lower-level governments, the audit authority, the people's government or the relevant competent authority or entity shall, within its statutory authority, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations and according to the circumstances, take the following handling measures:  
(1) Order the payment of amounts that shall be turned over within a specified period;  
(2) Order the return of the encroached state-owned assets within a specified period;  
(3) Order the return of illegal gains within a specified period;  
(4) Order handling in accordance with the relevant provisions of the unified state financial and accounting system;  
(5) Other handling measures.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　对被审计单位违反国家规定的财务收支行为，审计机关、人民政府或者有关主管机关、单位在法定职权范围内，依照法律、行政法规的规定，区别情况采取前条规定的处理措施，并可以依法给予处罚。

**Article 50** : For acts of financial revenues and expenditures that violate state regulations committed by the auditee, the audit authority, the people's government or the relevant competent authority or entity shall, within its statutory authority, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations and according to the circumstances, take the handling measures prescribed in the preceding article, and may impose penalties according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　审计机关在法定职权范围内作出的审计决定，被审计单位应当执行。  
审计机关依法责令被审计单位缴纳应当上缴的款项，被审计单位拒不执行的，审计机关应当通报有关主管机关、单位，有关主管机关、单位应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定予以扣缴或者采取其他处理措施，并将处理结果书面告知审计机关。

**Article 51** : The auditee shall execute the audit decision made by the audit authority within its statutory authority.  
If the audit authority orders the auditee according to law to pay amounts that shall be turned over, and the auditee refuses to execute, the audit authority shall notify the relevant competent authority or entity; the relevant competent authority or entity shall, in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations, withhold the amounts or take other handling measures, and shall inform the audit authority in writing of the handling result.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　被审计单位应当按照规定时间整改审计查出的问题，将整改情况报告审计机关，同时向本级人民政府或者有关主管机关、单位报告，并按照规定向社会公布。  
各级人民政府和有关主管机关、单位应当督促被审计单位整改审计查出的问题。审计机关应当对被审计单位整改情况进行跟踪检查。  
审计结果以及整改情况应当作为考核、任免、奖惩领导干部和制定政策、完善制度的重要参考；拒不整改或者整改时弄虚作假的，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 52** : The auditee shall, within the prescribed time, rectify the problems identified in the audit, report the rectification situation to the audit authority, and at the same time report it to the people's government at the corresponding level or the relevant competent authority or entity, and shall make it public to the society in accordance with regulations.  
The people's governments at all levels and the relevant competent authorities or entities shall urge the auditee to rectify the problems identified in the audit. The audit authority shall conduct follow-up inspections on the rectification situation of the auditee.  
The audit results and rectification situation shall serve as an important reference for the assessment, appointment and removal, rewards and penalties of leading cadres, and for policy formulation and system improvement; if the auditee refuses to rectify or practices fraud during rectification, legal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　被审计单位对审计机关作出的有关财务收支的审计决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。  
被审计单位对审计机关作出的有关财政收支的审计决定不服的，可以提请审计机关的本级人民政府裁决，本级人民政府的裁决为最终决定。

**Article 53** : If the auditee is dissatisfied with the audit decision made by the audit authority concerning financial revenues and expenditures, it may apply for administrative reconsideration or file an administrative action according to law.  
If the auditee is dissatisfied with the audit decision made by the audit authority concerning financial revenues and expenditures, it may request the people's government at the same level as the audit authority to make a ruling; the ruling of the people's government at the same level shall be final.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　被审计单位的财政收支、财务收支违反国家规定，审计机关认为对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法应当给予处分的，应当向被审计单位提出处理建议，或者移送监察机关和有关主管机关、单位处理，有关机关、单位应当将处理结果书面告知审计机关。

**Article 54** : If the financial revenues and expenditures or financial revenues and expenditures of the auditee violate state regulations, and the audit authority considers that the person directly in charge and the other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law, it shall propose handling suggestions to the auditee, or transfer the matter to the supervisory authority or the competent authority or entity for handling; the relevant authority or entity shall inform the audit authority in writing of the handling result.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　被审计单位的财政收支、财务收支违反法律、行政法规的规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 55** : If the financial revenues and expenditures or financial revenues and expenditures of the auditee violate the provisions of laws and administrative regulations and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　报复陷害审计人员的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 56** : Anyone who retaliates against or frames audit personnel shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　审计人员滥用职权、徇私舞弊、玩忽职守或者泄露、向他人非法提供所知悉的国家秘密、工作秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私和个人信息的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 57** : If an audit personnel abuses power, engages in malpractices for personal gain, neglects duties, or discloses or illegally provides to others state secrets, work secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy or personal information known to them, they shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　领导干部经济责任审计和自然资源资产离任审计，依照本法和国家有关规定执行。

**Article 58** : The audit of economic responsibility of leading cadres and the audit of natural resources assets when leaving office shall be implemented in accordance with this Law and relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队审计工作的规定，由中央军事委员会根据本法制定。  
审计机关和军队审计机构应当建立健全协作配合机制，按照国家有关规定对涉及军地经济事项实施联合审计。

**Article 59** : The regulations on the audit work of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be formulated by the Central Military Commission in accordance with this Law.  
Audit authorities and military audit institutions shall establish and improve a coordination and cooperation mechanism, and implement joint audits on matters involving military and local economic affairs in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　本法自1995年1月1日起施行。1988年11月30日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国审计条例》同时废止。

**Article 60** : This Law shall come into force on January 1, 1995. The "Audit Regulations of the People's Republic of China" promulgated by the State Council on November 30, 1988 shall be abolished simultaneously.
