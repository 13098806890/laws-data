# 中华人民共和国车船税法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2019-04-23  
**生效日期**：2019-04-23

> （2011年2月25日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十九次会议通过　根据2019年4月23日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国建筑法〉等八部法律的决定》修正） 第一条　在中华人民共和国境内属于本法所附《车船税税目税额表》规定的车辆、船舶（以下简称车船）的所有人或者管理人，为车船税的纳税人，应当依照本法缴纳车船税。 第二条　车船的适用税额依照本法所附《车船税税目税额表》执行。 车辆的具体适用税额由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府依照本法所附《车船税税目税额表》规定的税额幅度和国务院的规定确定。 船舶的具体适用税额由国务院在本法所附《车船税税目税额表》规定的税额幅度内确定。 第三条　下列车船免征车船税： （一）捕捞、养殖渔船； （二）军队、武装警察部队专用的车船； （三）警用车船； （四）悬挂应急救援专用号牌的国家综合性消防救援车辆和国家综合性消防救援专用船舶； （五）依照法律规定应当予以免税的外国驻华使领馆、国际组织驻华代表机构及其有关人员的车船。 第四条　对节约能源、使用新能源的车船可以减征或者免征车船税；对受严重自然灾害影响纳税困难以及有其他特殊原因确需减税、免税的，可以减征或者免征车船税。具体办法由国务院规定，并报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。 第五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据当地实际情况，可以对公共交通车船，农村居民拥有并主要在农村地区使用的摩托车、三轮汽车和低速载货汽车定期减征或者免征车船税。 第六条　从事机动车第三者责任强制保险业务的保险机构为机动车车船税的扣缴义务人，应当在收取保险费时依法代收车船税，并出具代收税款凭证。 第七条　车船税的纳税地点为车船的登记地或者车船税扣缴义务人所在地。依法不需要办理登记的车船，车船税的纳税地点为车船的所有人或者管理人所在地。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　在中华人民共和国境内属于本法所附《车船税税目税额表》规定的车辆、船舶（以下简称车船）的所有人或者管理人，为车船税的纳税人，应当依照本法缴纳车船税。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国车船税法实施条例.md#art-2" title="被《中华人民共和国车船税法实施条例》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 1** The owner or manager of a vehicle or vessel (hereinafter referred to as a vehicle or vessel) within the territory of the People's Republic of China that falls under the provisions of the "Vehicle and Vessel Tax Items and Rates Table" attached to this Law shall be a taxpayer of vehicle and vessel tax and shall pay vehicle and vessel tax in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　车船的适用税额依照本法所附《车船税税目税额表》执行。  
车辆的具体适用税额由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府依照本法所附《车船税税目税额表》规定的税额幅度和国务院的规定确定。  
船舶的具体适用税额由国务院在本法所附《车船税税目税额表》规定的税额幅度内确定。

**Article 2** The applicable tax rates for vehicles and vessels shall be enforced in accordance with the "Vehicle and Vessel Tax Items and Rates Table" attached to this Law.  
The specific applicable tax rates for vehicles shall be determined by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government within the range of tax rates prescribed in the "Vehicle and Vessel Tax Items and Rates Table" attached to this Law and in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.  
The specific applicable tax rates for vessels shall be determined by the State Council within the range of tax rates prescribed in the "Vehicle and Vessel Tax Items and Rates Table" attached to this Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　下列车船免征车船税：  
（一）捕捞、养殖渔船；  
（二）军队、武装警察部队专用的车船；  
（三）警用车船；  
（四）悬挂应急救援专用号牌的国家综合性消防救援车辆和国家综合性消防救援专用船舶；  
（五）依照法律规定应当予以免税的外国驻华使领馆、国际组织驻华代表机构及其有关人员的车船。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国车船税法实施条例.md#art-7" title="被《中华人民共和国车船税法实施条例》第7条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国车船税法实施条例.md#art-8" title="被《中华人民共和国车船税法实施条例》第8条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国车船税法实施条例.md#art-9" title="被《中华人民共和国车船税法实施条例》第9条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 3** The following vehicles and vessels shall be exempted from vehicle and vessel tax:  
(1) Fishing boats and aquaculture boats;  
(2) Vehicles and vessels exclusively used by the military and armed police forces;  
(3) Police vehicles and vessels;  
(4) National comprehensive fire rescue vehicles and fire rescue vessels bearing special emergency rescue license plates;  
(5) Vehicles and vessels of foreign embassies and consulates in China, representative offices of international organizations in China, and their relevant persons that shall be exempted from tax according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　对节约能源、使用新能源的车船可以减征或者免征车船税；对受严重自然灾害影响纳税困难以及有其他特殊原因确需减税、免税的，可以减征或者免征车船税。具体办法由国务院规定，并报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 4** Vehicle and vessel tax may be reduced or exempted for vehicles and vessels that save energy or use new energy; vehicle and vessel tax may be reduced or exempted for those that are affected by severe natural disasters, causing difficulty in tax payment, or for other special reasons that indeed require tax reduction or exemption. The specific measures shall be formulated by the State Council and reported to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for record.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据当地实际情况，可以对公共交通车船，农村居民拥有并主要在农村地区使用的摩托车、三轮汽车和低速载货汽车定期减征或者免征车船税。

**Article 5** The people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, based on local actual conditions, grant periodic reduction or exemption of vehicle and vessel tax for public transport vehicles and vessels, as well as for motorcycles, three-wheeled vehicles, and low-speed trucks owned by rural residents and mainly used in rural areas.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　从事机动车第三者责任强制保险业务的保险机构为机动车车船税的扣缴义务人，应当在收取保险费时依法代收车船税，并出具代收税款凭证。

**Article 6** An insurance institution engaged in the business of compulsory insurance for motor vehicle third-party liability shall be the withholding agent for motor vehicle and vessel tax, and shall collect the vehicle and vessel tax according to law when collecting insurance premiums, and issue a certificate of tax collection.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　车船税的纳税地点为车船的登记地或者车船税扣缴义务人所在地。依法不需要办理登记的车船，车船税的纳税地点为车船的所有人或者管理人所在地。

**Article 7** The place of tax payment for vehicle and vessel tax shall be the place of registration of the vehicle or vessel, or the place where the withholding agent for vehicle and vessel tax is located. For vehicles and vessels that are not required to undergo registration according to law, the place of tax payment for vehicle and vessel tax shall be the place where the owner or manager of the vehicle or vessel is located.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　车船税纳税义务发生时间为取得车船所有权或者管理权的当月。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国车船税法实施条例.md#art-21" title="被《中华人民共和国车船税法实施条例》第21条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 8** The time when the obligation to pay vehicle and vessel tax arises shall be the month in which the ownership or management right of the vehicle or vessel is acquired.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　车船税按年申报缴纳。具体申报纳税期限由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定。

**Article 9** Vehicle and vessel tax shall be declared and paid on an annual basis. The specific time limit for declaration and payment shall be prescribed by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　公安、交通运输、农业、渔业等车船登记管理部门、船舶检验机构和车船税扣缴义务人的行业主管部门应当在提供车船有关信息等方面，协助税务机关加强车船税的征收管理。  
车辆所有人或者管理人在申请办理车辆相关登记、定期检验手续时，应当向公安机关交通管理部门提交依法纳税或者免税证明。公安机关交通管理部门核查后办理相关手续。

**Article 10** The competent departments for vehicle and vessel registration management such as public security, transportation, agriculture, and fisheries, the vessel inspection institutions, and the industry competent authorities of the withholding agents for vehicle and vessel tax shall assist the tax authorities in strengthening the collection and administration of vehicle and vessel tax by providing information relevant to vehicles and vessels, among other means.  
When the owner or manager of a vehicle applies for vehicle registration or periodic inspection procedures, they shall submit to the traffic management department of the public security organ a certificate showing that tax has been paid according to law or that the vehicle is tax-exempt. The traffic management department of the public security organ shall handle the relevant procedures after verification.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　车船税的征收管理，依照本法和《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》的规定执行。

**Article 11** The collection and administration of vehicle and vessel tax shall be enforced in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the "Tax Collection and Administration Law of the People's Republic of China".

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院根据本法制定实施条例。

**Article 12** The State Council shall formulate implementing regulations in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本法自2012年1月1日起施行。2006年12月29日国务院公布的《中华人民共和国车船税暂行条例》同时废止。

**Article 13** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2012. The "Interim Regulations on Vehicle and Vessel Tax of the People's Republic of China" promulgated by the State Council on December 29, 2006 shall be abolished simultaneously.
