### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了促进矿产资源合理开发利用，加强矿产资源和生态环境保护，维护矿产资源国家所有者权益和矿业权人合法权益，推动矿业高质量发展，保障国家矿产资源安全，适应全面建设社会主义现代化国家的需要，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of promoting the rational exploitation and utilization of mineral resources, strengthening the protection of mineral resources and the ecological environment, safeguarding the rights and interests of the State as owner of mineral resources and the legitimate rights and interests of mineral rights holders, promoting high-quality development of the mining industry, ensuring national mineral resources security, and meeting the needs of comprehensively building a modern socialist country.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域勘查、开采矿产资源，开展矿区生态修复等活动，适用本法。  
本法所称矿产资源，是指由地质作用形成、具有利用价值的，呈固态、液态、气态等形态的自然资源。矿产资源目录由国务院确定并调整。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the prospecting and mining of mineral resources and the carrying out of ecological restoration of mining areas and other activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction.  
For the purposes of this Law, "mineral resources" mean natural resources formed by geological processes, having use value, and existing in solid, liquid, gaseous or other forms. The catalogue of mineral resources shall be determined and adjusted by the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　矿产资源开发利用和保护工作应当坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻总体国家安全观，统筹发展和安全，统筹国内国际，坚持开发利用与保护并重，遵循保障安全、节约集约、科技支撑、绿色发展的原则。

**Article 3** The work of exploitation, utilization and protection of mineral resources shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, implement a holistic approach to national security, coordinate development and security, balance domestic and international considerations, attach equal importance to exploitation, utilization and protection, and follow the principles of ensuring safety, conservation and intensive use, scientific and technological support, and green development.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　矿产资源属于国家所有，由国务院代表国家行使矿产资源的所有权。地表或者地下的矿产资源的国家所有权，不因其所依附的土地的所有权或者使用权的不同而改变。  
各级人民政府应当加强矿产资源保护工作。禁止任何单位和个人以任何手段侵占或者破坏矿产资源。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/农业、资源与能源/最高人民法院关于审理矿产资源纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-3" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理矿产资源纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第3条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 4** Mineral resources shall be owned by the State, and the State Council shall exercise the ownership of mineral resources on behalf of the State. The State ownership of mineral resources on or under the surface shall not change by reason of any difference in the ownership or right to use of the land to which they are attached.  
People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the protection of mineral resources. No unit or individual may, by any means, encroach upon or destroy mineral resources.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　勘查、开采矿产资源应当依法分别取得探矿权、采矿权，本法另有规定的除外。  
国家保护依法取得的探矿权、采矿权不受侵犯，维护矿产资源勘查、开采区域的生产秩序、工作秩序。

**Article 5** The prospecting and mining of mineral resources shall, respectively, require the acquisition of prospecting rights and mining rights in accordance with law, unless otherwise provided for in this Law.  
The State shall protect lawfully acquired prospecting rights and mining rights from infringement, and maintain the production order and work order in areas where mineral resources are prospected and mined.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　勘查、开采矿产资源应当按照国家有关规定缴纳费用。国务院可以根据不同情况规定减收或者免收有关费用。  
开采矿产资源应当依法缴纳资源税。

**Article 6** Fees shall be paid in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State for the prospecting and mining of mineral resources. The State Council may, depending on the circumstances, provide for reduction or exemption of relevant fees.  
Resource tax shall be paid in accordance with law for the mining of mineral resources.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家建立健全地质调查制度，加强基础性地质调查工作，为矿产资源勘查、开采和保护等提供基础地质资料。

**Article 7** The State shall establish and improve a geological survey system, strengthen basic geological survey work, and provide basic geological data for the prospecting, mining and protection of mineral resources.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家完善政策措施，加大对战略性矿产资源勘查、开采、贸易、储备等的支持力度，推动战略性矿产资源增加储量和提高产能，推进战略性矿产资源产业优化升级，提升矿产资源安全保障水平。  
战略性矿产资源目录由国务院确定并调整。  
对国务院确定的特定战略性矿产资源，按照国家有关规定实行保护性开采。

**Article 8** The State shall improve policies and measures, increase support for the prospecting, mining, trade, and reserves of strategic mineral resources, promote the increase of reserves and production capacity of strategic mineral resources, advance the optimization and upgrading of the strategic mineral resources industry, and enhance the level of security assurance for mineral resources.  
The catalogue of strategic mineral resources shall be determined and adjusted by the State Council.  
A protective mining system shall be implemented for specific strategic mineral resources determined by the State Council, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家对矿产资源勘查、开采实行统一规划、合理布局、综合勘查、合理开采和综合利用的方针。  
国务院自然资源主管部门会同国务院发展改革、应急管理、生态环境、工业和信息化、水行政、能源、矿山安全监察等有关部门，依据国家发展规划、全国国土空间规划、地质调查成果等，编制全国矿产资源规划，报国务院或者其授权的部门批准后实施。  
省级人民政府自然资源主管部门会同有关部门编制本行政区域矿产资源规划，经本级人民政府同意后，报国务院自然资源主管部门批准后实施。  
设区的市级、县级人民政府自然资源主管部门会同有关部门根据本行政区域内矿产资源状况和实际需要，编制本行政区域矿产资源规划，经本级人民政府同意后，报上一级人民政府自然资源主管部门批准后实施。

**Article 9** The State shall implement the policy of unified planning, rational layout, comprehensive prospecting, rational mining and comprehensive utilization for the prospecting and mining of mineral resources.  
The competent department of natural resources under the State Council shall, together with the competent departments of development and reform, emergency management, ecological environment, industry and information technology, water administration, energy, and mine safety supervision under the State Council and other relevant departments, prepare the national mineral resources plan based on the national development plan, the national territorial space plan, and the results of geological surveys, and submit it to the State Council or its authorized department for approval before implementation.  
The competent department of natural resources of a provincial people's government shall, together with relevant departments, prepare the mineral resources plan for its administrative region, which, after being approved by the people's government at the same level, shall be submitted to the competent department of natural resources under the State Council for approval before implementation.  
The competent department of natural resources of a people's government of a city divided into districts or a county shall, together with relevant departments, prepare the mineral resources plan for its administrative region based on the mineral resources situation and actual needs within its administrative region, which, after being approved by the people's government at the same level, shall be submitted to the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at the next higher level for approval before implementation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家加强战略性矿产资源储备体系和矿产资源应急体系建设，提升矿产资源应急保供能力和水平。

**Article 10** The State shall strengthen the strategic mineral resources reserve system and the mineral resources emergency response system, and enhance the capability and level of emergency supply assurance of mineral resources.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家鼓励、支持矿产资源勘查、开采、保护和矿区生态修复等领域的科技创新、科技成果应用推广，推动数字化、智能化、绿色化建设，提高矿产资源相关领域的科学技术水平。

**Article 11** The State shall encourage and support scientific and technological innovation, and the application and promotion of scientific and technological achievements, in the fields of prospecting, mining, protection and ecological restoration of mining areas of mineral resources, promote digital, intelligent and green construction, and raise the level of science and technology in the relevant fields of mineral resources.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对在矿产资源勘查、开采、保护和矿区生态修复工作中做出突出贡献以及在矿产资源相关领域科技创新等方面取得显著成绩的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 12** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in the work of prospecting, mining, protection and ecological restoration of mining areas of mineral resources, or have achieved remarkable results in scientific and technological innovation in the relevant fields of mineral resources, shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家在民族自治地方开采矿产资源，应当照顾民族自治地方的利益，作出有利于民族自治地方经济建设的安排，照顾当地群众的生产和生活。  
民族自治地方的自治机关根据法律规定和国家的统一规划，对可以由本地方开发的矿产资源，优先合理开发利用。

**Article 13** Where the State mines mineral resources in national autonomous areas, it shall give consideration to the interests of the national autonomous areas, make arrangements conducive to the economic construction of the national autonomous areas, and take care of the production and livelihood of the local people.  
The organs of self-government of national autonomous areas may, in accordance with legal provisions and the unified planning of the State, give priority to the rational exploitation and utilization of mineral resources that may be developed by such localities.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院自然资源主管部门会同有关部门负责全国矿产资源勘查、开采和矿区生态修复等活动的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府自然资源主管部门会同有关部门负责本行政区域内矿产资源勘查、开采和矿区生态修复等活动的监督管理工作。  
国务院授权的机构对省、自治区、直辖市人民政府矿产资源开发利用和监督管理情况进行督察。

**Article 14** The competent department of natural resources under the State Council shall, together with relevant departments, be responsible for the supervision and administration of prospecting, mining and ecological restoration of mining areas of mineral resources nationwide.  
The competent departments of natural resources of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with relevant departments, be responsible for the supervision and administration of prospecting, mining and ecological restoration of mining areas within their respective administrative regions.  
An institution authorized by the State Council shall supervise the exploitation, utilization and supervision and administration of mineral resources by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家坚持平等互利、合作共赢的方针，积极促进矿产资源领域国际合作。

**Article 15** The State shall adhere to the principle of equality, mutual benefit and win-win cooperation, and actively promote international cooperation in the field of mineral resources.

### 第二章　矿业权

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家实行探矿权、采矿权有偿取得的制度。  
探矿权、采矿权统称矿业权。

**Article 16** The State shall implement a system of compensated acquisition of prospecting rights and mining rights.  
Prospecting rights and mining rights are collectively referred to as mineral rights.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　矿业权应当通过招标、拍卖、挂牌等竞争性方式出让，法律、行政法规或者国务院规定可以通过协议出让或者其他方式设立的除外。  
矿业权出让权限划分由国务院规定。县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门按照规定权限组织矿业权出让。  
矿业权出让应当按照国家规定纳入统一的公共资源交易平台体系。

**Article 17** Mineral rights shall be granted through competitive methods such as invitation to bid, auction, or listing, unless otherwise provided by laws, administrative regulations or the State Council that they may be established through agreement or other means.  
The division of authority for granting mineral rights shall be prescribed by the State Council. The competent departments of natural resources of people's governments at or above the county level shall organize the grant of mineral rights within their prescribed authority.  
The grant of mineral rights shall be incorporated into a unified public resource trading platform system in accordance with the provisions of the State.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门应当加强对矿业权出让工作的统筹安排，优化矿业权出让工作流程，提高工作效率，保障矿业权出让工作与加强矿产资源勘查、开采的实际需要相适应。矿业权出让应当考虑不同矿产资源特点、矿山最低开采规模、生态环境保护和安全要求等因素。  
国家鼓励单位和个人向县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门提供可供出让的探矿权区块来源；对符合出让条件的，有关人民政府自然资源主管部门应当及时安排出让。  
国务院自然资源主管部门应当加强对矿业权出让工作的指导和监督。  
法律、行政法规规定在一定区域范围内禁止或者限制开采矿产资源的，应当遵守相关规定。

**Article 18** The competent departments of natural resources of people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the overall arrangement of the work of granting mineral rights, optimize the work flow of granting mineral rights, improve work efficiency, and ensure that the work of granting mineral rights is adapted to the actual needs of strengthening the prospecting and mining of mineral resources. The grant of mineral rights shall take into account factors such as the characteristics of different mineral resources, the minimum mining scale of mines, ecological and environmental protection requirements, and safety requirements.  
The State shall encourage units and individuals to provide the competent departments of natural resources of people's governments at or above the county level with sources of prospecting blocks that may be granted; where conditions for grant are met, the relevant competent department of natural resources of the people's government shall arrange the grant in a timely manner.  
The competent department of natural resources under the State Council shall strengthen the guidance and supervision of the work of granting mineral rights.  
Where laws or administrative regulations prohibit or restrict the mining of mineral resources within a certain area, the relevant provisions shall be complied with.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　通过竞争性方式出让矿业权的，出让矿业权的自然资源主管部门（以下称矿业权出让部门）应当提前公告拟出让矿业权的基本情况、竞争规则、受让人的技术能力等条件及其权利义务等事项，不得以不合理的条件对市场主体实行差别待遇或者歧视待遇。

**Article 19** Where mineral rights are granted through competitive methods, the competent department of natural resources granting the mineral rights (hereinafter referred to as the mineral rights granting department) shall announce in advance the basic information of the mineral rights to be granted, the competition rules, the conditions such as the technical capability of the transferee, and the rights and obligations thereof, and shall not impose differential treatment or discriminatory treatment on market entities with unreasonable conditions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　出让矿业权的，矿业权出让部门应当与依法确定的受让人以书面形式签订矿业权出让合同。  
矿业权出让合同应当明确勘查或者开采的矿种、区域，勘查、开采、矿区生态修复和安全要求，矿业权出让收益数额与缴纳方式、矿业权的期限等事项；涉及特定战略性矿产资源的，还应当明确保护性开采的有关要求。矿业权出让合同示范文本由国务院自然资源主管部门制定。

**Article 20** Where mineral rights are granted, the mineral rights granting department shall enter into a written mineral rights grant contract with the transferee determined in accordance with law.  
The mineral rights grant contract shall specify the type of mineral to be prospected or mined, the area, the prospecting, mining, ecological restoration of mining areas and safety requirements, the amount and payment method of the mineral rights grant proceeds, the term of the mineral rights, and other matters; where specific strategic mineral resources are involved, the relevant requirements for protective mining shall also be specified. A model text of the mineral rights grant contract shall be formulated by the competent department of natural resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　矿业权出让合同约定的矿业权出让收益数额与缴纳方式等，应当符合国家有关矿业权出让收益征收的规定。  
矿业权出让收益征收办法由国务院财政部门会同国务院自然资源主管部门、国务院税务主管部门制定，报国务院批准后执行。制定矿业权出让收益征收办法，应当根据不同矿产资源特点，遵循有利于维护国家权益、调动矿产资源勘查积极性、促进矿业可持续发展的原则，并广泛听取各有关方面的意见和建议。

**Article 21** The amount and payment method of the mineral rights grant proceeds agreed upon in the mineral rights grant contract shall comply with the provisions of the State on the levy of mineral rights grant proceeds.  
Measures for the levy of mineral rights grant proceeds shall be formulated by the competent department of finance under the State Council together with the competent department of natural resources under the State Council and the competent tax department under the State Council, and shall be implemented after being approved by the State Council. In formulating the measures for the levy of mineral rights grant proceeds, the characteristics of different mineral resources shall be taken into account, and the principles of being conducive to safeguarding the rights and interests of the State, mobilizing the enthusiasm for mineral resources prospecting, and promoting the sustainable development of the mining industry shall be followed, and opinions and suggestions from relevant parties shall be extensively solicited.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　设立矿业权的，应当向矿业权出让部门申请矿业权登记。符合登记条件的，矿业权出让部门应当将相关事项记载于矿业权登记簿，并向矿业权人发放矿业权证书。  
矿业权变更、转让、抵押和消灭的，应当依法办理登记。  
矿业权的设立、变更、转让、抵押和消灭，经依法登记，发生效力；未经登记，不发生效力，法律另有规定的除外。  
矿业权登记的具体办法由国务院自然资源主管部门制定。

**Article 22** Where a mineral right is established, an application for registration of the mineral right shall be made to the mineral rights granting department. Where the conditions for registration are met, the mineral rights granting department shall record the relevant matters in the mineral rights register and issue a mineral rights certificate to the mineral rights holder.  
Any modification, transfer, mortgage or extinguishment of a mineral right shall be registered in accordance with law.  
The establishment, modification, transfer, mortgage or extinguishment of a mineral right shall take effect upon registration in accordance with law; without registration, it shall not take effect, unless otherwise provided by law.  
Specific measures for mineral rights registration shall be formulated by the competent department of natural resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　探矿权人在登记的勘查区域内，享有勘查有关矿产资源并依法取得采矿权的权利。  
采矿权人在登记的开采区域内，享有开采有关矿产资源并获得采出的矿产品的权利。  
矿业权人有权依法优先取得登记的勘查、开采区域内新发现的其他矿产资源的矿业权，具体办法由国务院自然资源主管部门制定。  
在已经登记的勘查、开采区域内，不得设立其他矿业权，国务院和国务院自然资源主管部门规定可以按照不同矿种分别设立矿业权的除外。

**Article 23** A prospecting right holder shall, within the registered prospecting area, have the right to prospect the relevant mineral resources and to obtain mining rights in accordance with law.  
A mining right holder shall, within the registered mining area, have the right to mine the relevant mineral resources and to obtain the mineral products mined.  
A mineral rights holder shall have the right to obtain, by priority and in accordance with law, the mineral rights of other newly discovered mineral resources within the registered prospecting or mining area; specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of natural resources under the State Council.  
No other mineral rights may be established within a registered prospecting or mining area, unless otherwise prescribed by the State Council or the competent department of natural resources under the State Council that mineral rights may be established separately according to different types of minerals.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　探矿权的期限为五年。探矿权期限届满，可以续期，续期最多不超过三次，每次期限为五年；续期时应当按照规定核减勘查区域面积。法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。  
探矿权人应当按照探矿权出让合同的约定及时开展勘查工作，并每年向原矿业权出让部门报告有关情况；无正当理由未开展或者未实质性开展勘查工作的，探矿权期限届满时不予续期。  
采矿权的期限结合矿产资源储量和矿山建设规模确定，最长不超过三十年。采矿权期限届满，登记的开采区域内仍有可供开采的矿产资源的，可以续期；法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。  
期限届满未申请续期或者依法不予续期的，矿业权消灭。

**Article 24** The term of a prospecting right shall be five years. Upon expiry of the term of a prospecting right, it may be renewed, but the renewal shall not exceed three times, each time for a period of five years; upon renewal, the prospecting area shall be reduced in accordance with the provisions. Unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.  
A prospecting right holder shall carry out prospecting work in a timely manner as agreed in the prospecting right grant contract, and shall report the relevant situation to the original mineral rights granting department each year; where no prospecting work has been carried out or no substantive prospecting work has been carried out without justifiable reasons, the prospecting right shall not be renewed upon expiry of its term.  
The term of a mining right shall be determined by taking into account the mineral resources reserves and the scale of mine construction, and shall not exceed thirty years. Upon expiry of the term of a mining right, if there are still mineral resources available for mining within the registered mining area, the term may be renewed; unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.  
Where no application for renewal is made upon expiry of the term or the renewal is not granted in accordance with law, the mineral right shall be extinguished.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　探矿权人探明可供开采的矿产资源后可以在探矿权期限内申请将其探矿权转为采矿权；法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。原矿业权出让部门应当与该探矿权人签订采矿权出让合同，设立采矿权。  
为了公共利益的需要，或者因不可抗力或者其他特殊情形，探矿权暂时不能转为采矿权的，探矿权人可以申请办理探矿权保留，原矿业权出让部门应当为其办理。探矿权保留期间，探矿权期限中止计算。

**Article 25** After a prospecting right holder has verified mineral resources that are available for mining, it may, within the term of the prospecting right, apply to convert its prospecting right into a mining right; unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations. The original mineral rights granting department shall enter into a mining right grant contract with such prospecting right holder and establish a mining right.  
Where, for the needs of public interest, or due to force majeure or other special circumstances, a prospecting right cannot be temporarily converted into a mining right, the prospecting right holder may apply for the retention of the prospecting right, and the original mineral rights granting department shall handle it. During the period of retention of the prospecting right, the calculation of the term of the prospecting right shall be suspended.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　矿业权期限届满前，为了公共利益的需要，原矿业权出让部门可以依法收回矿业权；矿业权被收回的，应当依法给予公平、合理的补偿。  
自然保护地范围内，可以依法进行符合管控要求的勘查、开采活动，已设立的矿业权不符合管控要求的，应当依法有序退出。

**Article 26** Before the expiry of the term of a mineral right, the original mineral rights granting department may, for the needs of public interest, recover the mineral right in accordance with law; where a mineral right is recovered, fair and reasonable compensation shall be given in accordance with law.  
Within the scope of natural protected areas, prospecting and mining activities that meet the requirements of control may be carried out in accordance with law; where an established mineral right does not meet the requirements of control, it shall be withdrawn in an orderly manner in accordance with law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　矿业权可以依法转让或者出资、抵押等，国家另有规定或者矿业权出让合同另有约定的除外。  
矿业权转让的，矿业权出让合同和矿业权登记簿所载明的权利、义务随之转移，国家另有规定或者矿业权出让、转让合同另有约定的除外。  
矿业权转让的具体管理办法由国务院制定。

**Article 27** Mineral rights may be transferred, contributed as capital, mortgaged, etc., in accordance with law, unless otherwise provided by the State or otherwise agreed in the mineral rights grant contract.  
Where a mineral right is transferred, the rights and obligations specified in the mineral rights grant contract and the mineral rights register shall be transferred therewith, unless otherwise provided by the State or otherwise agreed in the mineral rights grant or transfer contract.  
Specific measures for the administration of mineral rights transfer shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　有下列情形之一的，无需取得探矿权：  
（一）国家出资勘查矿产资源；  
（二）采矿权人在登记的开采区域内为开采活动需要进行勘查；  
（三）国务院和国务院自然资源主管部门规定的其他情形。

**Article 28** A prospecting right shall not be required under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the State finances the prospecting of mineral resources;  
(2) a mining right holder needs to carry out prospecting for mining activities within the registered mining area; or  
(3) other circumstances provided by the State Council or the competent department of natural resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　有下列情形之一的，无需取得采矿权：  
（一）个人为生活自用采挖只能用作普通建筑材料的砂、石、黏土；  
（二）建设项目施工单位在批准的作业区域和建设工期内，因施工需要采挖只能用作普通建筑材料的砂、石、黏土；  
（三）国务院和国务院自然资源主管部门规定的其他情形。  
有前款第一项、第二项规定情形的，应当遵守省、自治区、直辖市规定的监督管理要求。

**Article 29** A mining right shall not be required under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) an individual digs sand, stone or clay that can only be used as ordinary construction materials for personal livelihood purposes;  
(2) a construction project construction unit digs sand, stone or clay that can only be used as ordinary construction materials for construction needs within the approved operation area and construction period; or  
(3) other circumstances provided by the State Council or the competent department of natural resources under the State Council.  
Where the circumstances specified in subparagraph (1) or (2) of the preceding paragraph exist, the supervision and management requirements prescribed by provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be complied with.

### 第三章　矿产资源勘查、开采

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门会同有关部门组织开展基础性地质调查；省级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门会同有关部门组织开展战略性矿产资源、重点成矿区远景调查和潜力评价。

**Article 30** The competent departments of natural resources of people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with relevant departments, organize and carry out basic geological surveys; the competent departments of natural resources of people's governments at or above the provincial level shall, together with relevant departments, organize and carry out surveys of the prospect and potential assessment of strategic mineral resources and key metallogenic areas.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　开展地质调查和矿产资源勘查、开采活动，应当按照国家有关规定及时汇交原始地质资料、实物地质资料和成果地质资料。  
汇交的地质资料应当依法保管、利用和保护。

**Article 31** In carrying out geological survey and mineral resources prospecting and mining activities, original geological data, physical geological data and resultant geological data shall be submitted in a timely manner in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The submitted geological data shall be preserved, utilized and protected in accordance with law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　编制国土空间规划应当合理规划建设项目的空间布局，避免、减少压覆矿产资源。  
建设项目论证时，建设单位应当查询占地范围内矿产资源分布和矿业权设置情况。省级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门应当为建设单位提供查询服务。  
建设项目确需压覆已经设置矿业权的矿产资源，对矿业权行使造成直接影响的，建设单位应当在压覆前与矿业权人协商，并依法给予公平、合理的补偿。  
战略性矿产资源原则上不得压覆；确需压覆的，应当经国务院自然资源主管部门或者其授权的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府自然资源主管部门批准。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/农业、资源与能源/最高人民法院关于审理矿产资源纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-15" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理矿产资源纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第15条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 32** In preparing territorial space plans, the spatial layout of construction projects shall be reasonably planned to avoid or reduce the covering of mineral resources.  
When demonstrating a construction project, the construction entity shall inquire about the distribution of mineral resources and the establishment of mineral rights within the area occupied by land. The competent departments of natural resources of people's governments at or above the provincial level shall provide inquiry services for construction entities.  
Where a construction project indeed needs to cover mineral resources over which mineral rights have been established, causing direct impact on the exercise of mineral rights, the construction entity shall negotiate with the mineral rights holder before covering, and give fair and reasonable compensation in accordance with law.  
Strategic mineral resources shall not be covered in principle; where covering is indeed necessary, approval shall be obtained from the competent department of natural resources under the State Council or the competent department of natural resources of the provincial-level people's government authorized thereby.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　矿业权人依照本法有关规定取得矿业权后，进行矿产资源勘查、开采作业前，应当按照矿业权出让合同以及相关标准、技术规范等，分别编制勘查方案、开采方案，报原矿业权出让部门批准，取得勘查许可证、采矿许可证；未取得许可证的，不得进行勘查、开采作业。  
矿业权人应当按照经批准的勘查方案、开采方案进行勘查、开采作业；勘查方案、开采方案需要作重大调整的，应当按照规定报原矿业权出让部门批准。

**Article 33** After obtaining mineral rights in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Law, and before carrying out prospecting or mining operations of mineral resources, a mineral rights holder shall, in accordance with the mineral rights grant contract and relevant standards and technical specifications, respectively prepare a prospecting plan and a mining plan, submit them to the original mineral rights granting department for approval, and obtain a prospecting permit and a mining permit; without obtaining the permits, no prospecting or mining operations may be carried out.  
A mineral rights holder shall carry out prospecting or mining operations in accordance with the approved prospecting plan or mining plan; where significant adjustments to the prospecting plan or mining plan are necessary, approval shall be obtained from the original mineral rights granting department in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家完善与矿产资源勘查、开采相适应的矿业用地制度。编制国土空间规划应当考虑矿产资源勘查、开采用地实际需求。勘查、开采矿产资源应当节约集约使用土地。  
县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门应当保障矿业权人依法通过出让、租赁、作价出资等方式使用土地。开采战略性矿产资源确需使用农民集体所有土地的，可以依法实施征收。  
勘查矿产资源可以依照土地管理法律、行政法规的规定临时使用土地。露天开采战略性矿产资源占用土地，经科学论证，具备边开采、边复垦条件的，报省级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门批准后，可以临时使用土地；临时使用农用地的，还应当按照国家有关规定及时恢复种植条件、耕地质量或者恢复植被、生产条件，确保原地类数量不减少、质量不下降、农民利益有保障。  
勘查、开采矿产资源用地的范围和使用期限应当根据需要确定，使用期限最长不超过矿业权期限。

**Article 34** The State shall improve the mining land use system suitable for the prospecting and mining of mineral resources. The actual land use needs for prospecting and mining of mineral resources shall be taken into account when preparing territorial space plans. The prospecting and mining of mineral resources shall use land in a conservation and intensive manner.  
The competent departments of natural resources of people's governments at or above the county level shall ensure that mineral rights holders use land through means such as grant, lease, or contribution as capital in accordance with law. Where the mining of strategic mineral resources indeed requires the use of land owned by peasant collectives, expropriation may be carried out in accordance with law.  
Land may be used temporarily for prospecting of mineral resources in accordance with the provisions of land management laws and administrative regulations. Where open-pit mining of strategic mineral resources occupies land, and upon scientific demonstration, if the conditions for simultaneous mining and reclamation are met, the land may be used temporarily after approval by the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the provincial level; where agricultural land is temporarily used, planting conditions, cultivated land quality, or vegetation and production conditions shall be restored in a timely manner in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, so as to ensure that the quantity of the original land type is not reduced, the quality is not degraded, and the interests of peasants are protected.  
The scope and term of land use for prospecting and mining of mineral resources shall be determined according to needs, and the term of use shall not exceed the term of the mineral right.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　矿业权所在地的县级人民政府自然资源主管部门应当公告矿业权人勘查、开采区域范围。矿业权人在勘查、开采区域内勘查、开采矿产资源，可以依法在相邻区域通行，架设供电、供水、通讯等相关设施。  
任何单位和个人不得实施下列行为：  
（一）进入他人的勘查、开采区域勘查、开采矿产资源；  
（二）扰乱勘查、开采区域的生产秩序、工作秩序；  
（三）侵占、哄抢矿业权人依法开采的矿产品；  
（四）其他干扰、破坏矿产资源勘查、开采活动正常进行的行为。

**Article 35** The competent department of natural resources of the county people's government where the mineral right is located shall announce the scope of the prospecting or mining area of the mineral rights holder. When prospecting or mining mineral resources within the prospecting or mining area, a mineral rights holder may, in accordance with law, pass through adjacent areas and erect relevant facilities such as power supply, water supply, and communication facilities.  
No unit or individual may commit any of the following acts:  
(1) entering another person's prospecting or mining area to prospect or mine mineral resources;  
(2) disturbing the production order or work order of the prospecting or mining area;  
(3) encroaching upon or scrambling for mineral products lawfully mined by a mineral rights holder; or  
(4) other acts that interfere with or disrupt the normal conduct of mineral resources prospecting or mining activities.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　石油、天然气等矿产资源勘查过程中发现可供开采的石油、天然气等矿产资源的，探矿权人依法履行相关程序后，可以进行开采，但应当在国务院自然资源主管部门规定的期限内依法取得采矿权和采矿许可证。

**Article 36** Where, during the prospecting of mineral resources such as petroleum and natural gas, petroleum, natural gas or other mineral resources that are available for mining are discovered, the prospecting right holder may, after going through the relevant procedures in accordance with law, carry out mining, but shall obtain the mining right and mining permit in accordance with law within the time limit prescribed by the competent department of natural resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家鼓励、支持矿业绿色低碳转型发展，加强绿色矿山建设。  
勘查、开采矿产资源，应当采用先进适用、符合生态环境保护和安全生产要求的工艺、设备、技术，不得使用国家明令淘汰的工艺、设备、技术。  
开采矿产资源应当采取有效措施，避免、减少对矿区森林、草原、耕地、湿地、河湖、海洋等生态系统的破坏，并加强对尾矿库建设、运行、闭库等活动的管理，防范生态环境和安全风险。

**Article 37** The State shall encourage and support the green and low-carbon transformation and development of the mining industry, and strengthen the construction of green mines.  
In prospecting and mining mineral resources, advanced and applicable technologies, equipment, and techniques that meet the requirements of ecological environmental protection and safe production shall be adopted, and technologies, equipment, and techniques that are expressly eliminated by the State shall not be used.  
In mining mineral resources, effective measures shall be taken to avoid or reduce damage to ecosystems such as forests, grasslands, cultivated land, wetlands, rivers, lakes and oceans in mining areas, and the management of activities such as the construction, operation and closure of tailings ponds shall be strengthened to prevent ecological, environmental and safety risks.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　勘查活动结束后，探矿权人应当及时对勘查区域进行清理，清除可能危害公共安全的设施、设备等，对废弃的探坑、探井等实施回填、封堵；破坏地表植被的，应当及时恢复。  
勘查活动临时占用耕地的，应当及时恢复种植条件和耕地质量；临时占用林地、草地的，应当及时恢复植被和生产条件。

**Article 38** After the completion of prospecting activities, the prospecting right holder shall promptly clean up the prospecting area, remove facilities and equipment that may endanger public safety, and backfill and seal abandoned prospecting pits, wells, etc.; where surface vegetation has been damaged, it shall be promptly restored.  
Where prospecting activities temporarily occupy cultivated land, the planting conditions and the quality of the cultivated land shall be promptly restored; where forest land or grassland is temporarily occupied, the vegetation and production conditions shall be promptly restored.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　开采矿产资源，应当采取合理的开采顺序、开采方法，并采取有效措施确保矿产资源开采回采率、选矿回收率和综合利用率达到有关国家标准的要求。  
开采矿产资源，应当采取有效措施保护地下水资源，并优先使用矿井水。  
采矿权人在开采主要矿种的同时，对具有工业价值的共生和伴生矿产应当综合开采、综合利用，防止浪费；对暂时不能综合开采或者必须同时采出但暂时不能综合利用的矿产以及含有有用组分的尾矿，应当采取有效的保护措施，防止损失破坏。  
国家制定和完善提高矿产资源开采回采率、选矿回收率、综合利用率的激励性政策措施。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/自然资源/中华人民共和国矿产资源法实施细则.md#art-42" title="被《中华人民共和国矿产资源法实施细则》第42条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 39** In mining mineral resources, reasonable mining sequence and mining methods shall be adopted, and effective measures shall be taken to ensure that the mining recovery rate, mineral processing recovery rate and comprehensive utilization rate meet the requirements of the relevant national standards.  
In mining mineral resources, effective measures shall be taken to protect groundwater resources, and mine water shall be used in priority.  
While mining the main mineral species, a mining right holder shall comprehensively mine and comprehensively utilize associated and intergrown minerals of industrial value, so as to prevent waste; for minerals that cannot be comprehensively mined temporarily or must be mined simultaneously but cannot be comprehensively utilized temporarily, as well as for tailings containing useful components, effective protection measures shall be taken to prevent loss and damage.  
The State shall formulate and improve incentive policies and measures for increasing the mining recovery rate, mineral processing recovery rate and comprehensive utilization rate of mineral resources.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家建立矿产资源储量管理制度，具体办法由国务院制定。  
矿业权人查明可供开采的矿产资源或者发现矿产资源储量发生重大变化的，应当按照规定编制矿产资源储量报告并报送县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门。矿业权人应当对矿产资源储量报告的真实性负责。

**Article 40** The State shall establish a mineral resources reserve management system, and specific measures shall be formulated by the State Council.  
Where a mineral rights holder has verified mineral resources that are available for mining or has found that the mineral resources reserves have undergone significant changes, it shall prepare a mineral resources reserve report in accordance with the provisions and submit it to the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level. The mineral rights holder shall be responsible for the authenticity of the mineral resources reserve report.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　采矿权人应当按照国家有关规定将闭坑地质报告报送县级以上地方人民政府自然资源主管部门。  
采矿权人应当在矿山闭坑前或者闭坑后的合理期限内采取安全措施、防治环境污染和生态破坏。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当组织有关部门加强闭坑的监督管理。

**Article 41** A mining right holder shall submit a mine closure geological report to the competent department of natural resources of the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
A mining right holder shall, before the closure of a mine or within a reasonable period after the closure, take safety measures, prevent and control environmental pollution and ecological damage.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize relevant departments to strengthen the supervision and administration of mine closure.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　勘查、开采矿产资源，应当遵守有关生态环境保护、安全生产、职业病防治等法律、法规的规定，防止污染环境、破坏生态，预防和减少生产安全事故，预防发生职业病。

**Article 42** In prospecting and mining mineral resources, the provisions of laws and regulations on ecological environmental protection, safe production, and prevention and control of occupational diseases shall be complied with, environmental pollution and ecological damage shall be prevented, production safety accidents shall be prevented and reduced, and the occurrence of occupational diseases shall be prevented.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　勘查、开采矿产资源时发现重要地质遗迹、古生物化石和文物的，应当加以保护并及时报告有关部门。

**Article 43** Where important geological relics, paleontological fossils or cultural relics are discovered during the prospecting or mining of mineral resources, they shall be protected and promptly reported to the relevant departments.

### 第四章　矿区生态修复

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　矿区生态修复应当坚持自然恢复与人工修复相结合，遵循因地制宜、科学规划、系统治理、合理利用的原则，采取工程、技术、生物等措施，做好地质环境恢复治理、地貌重塑、植被恢复、土地复垦等。涉及矿区污染治理的，应当遵守相关法律法规和技术标准等要求。  
国务院自然资源主管部门会同国务院生态环境主管部门等有关部门制定矿区生态修复技术规范。  
国务院生态环境主管部门指导、协调和监督矿区生态修复工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对矿区生态修复工作的统筹和监督，保障矿区生态修复与污染防治、水土保持、植被恢复等协同实施，提升矿区生态环境保护和恢复效果。

**Article 44** Ecological restoration of mining areas shall adhere to the combination of natural restoration and artificial restoration, follow the principles of adapting measures to local conditions, scientific planning, systematic governance and rational utilization, and take engineering, technical and biological measures to carry out geological environment restoration and management, landform reconstruction, vegetation restoration, land reclamation, etc. Where pollution control in mining areas is involved, the relevant laws, regulations and technical standards shall be complied with.  
The competent department of natural resources under the State Council shall, together with the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council and other relevant departments, formulate technical specifications for ecological restoration of mining areas.  
The competent department of ecological environment under the State Council shall guide, coordinate and supervise the work of ecological restoration of mining areas.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the overall planning and supervision of the work of ecological restoration of mining areas, ensure the coordinated implementation of ecological restoration of mining areas with pollution prevention and control, water and soil conservation, vegetation restoration, etc., and improve the effect of ecological environment protection and restoration of mining areas.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　因开采矿产资源导致矿区生态破坏的，采矿权人应当依法履行生态修复义务。采矿权人的生态修复义务不因采矿权消灭而免除。  
采矿权转让的，由受让人履行矿区生态修复义务，国家另有规定或者矿业权出让、转让合同另有约定的除外。  
历史遗留的废弃矿区，矿区生态修复责任人灭失或者无法确认的，由所在地县级以上地方人民政府组织开展矿区生态修复。  
国家鼓励社会资本参与矿区生态修复。

**Article 45** Where ecological damage to a mining area is caused by the mining of mineral resources, the mining right holder shall perform the ecological restoration obligation in accordance with law. The ecological restoration obligation of a mining right holder shall not be exempted due to the extinguishment of the mining right.  
Where a mining right is transferred, the transferee shall perform the ecological restoration obligation of the mining area, unless otherwise provided by the State or otherwise agreed in the mineral rights grant or transfer contract.  
For abandoned mining areas left over from history, where the person responsible for ecological restoration of the mining area is missing or cannot be identified, the local people's government at or above the county level where the mining area is located shall organize the ecological restoration of the mining area.  
The State shall encourage social capital to participate in the ecological restoration of mining areas.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　开采矿产资源前，采矿权人应当依照法律、法规和国务院自然资源主管部门的规定以及矿业权出让合同编制矿区生态修复方案，随开采方案报原矿业权出让部门批准。矿区生态修复方案应当包括尾矿库生态修复的专门措施。  
编制矿区生态修复方案，应当在矿区涉及的有关范围内公示征求意见，并专门听取矿区涉及的居民委员会、村民委员会、农村集体经济组织和居民代表、村民代表的意见。

**Article 46** Before mining mineral resources, a mining right holder shall, in accordance with laws, regulations, the provisions of the competent department of natural resources under the State Council, and the mineral rights grant contract, prepare a mining area ecological restoration plan, which shall be submitted together with the mining plan to the original mineral rights granting department for approval. The mining area ecological restoration plan shall include specific measures for the ecological restoration of tailings ponds.  
When preparing the mining area ecological restoration plan, the plan shall be publicized within the relevant scope of the mining area to solicit opinions, and the opinions of the residents' committees, villagers' committees, rural collective economic organizations, and representatives of residents and villagers involved in the mining area shall be specially solicited.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　采矿权人应当按照经批准的矿区生态修复方案进行矿区生态修复。能够边开采、边修复的，应当边开采、边修复；能够分区、分期修复的，应当分区、分期修复；不能边开采、边修复或者分区、分期修复的，应当在矿山闭坑前或者闭坑后的合理期限内及时修复。

**Article 47** A mining right holder shall carry out ecological restoration of the mining area in accordance with the approved mining area ecological restoration plan. Where simultaneous mining and restoration is possible, simultaneous mining and restoration shall be carried out; where restoration by sub-area or by phases is possible, restoration shall be carried out by sub-area or by phases; where neither simultaneous mining and restoration nor restoration by sub-area or by phases is possible, restoration shall be carried out in a timely manner before the closure of the mine or within a reasonable period after the closure.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　矿区生态修复由县级以上地方人民政府自然资源主管部门会同生态环境主管部门等有关部门组织验收。验收应当邀请有关专家以及矿区涉及的居民委员会、村民委员会、农村集体经济组织和居民代表、村民代表参加，验收结果应当向社会公布。  
矿区生态修复分区、分期进行的，应当分区、分期验收。

**Article 48** The ecological restoration of a mining area shall be organized for acceptance inspection by the competent department of natural resources of the local people's government at or above the county level together with the competent department of ecological environment and other relevant departments. Relevant experts, as well as the residents' committees, villagers' committees, rural collective economic organizations, and representatives of residents and villagers involved in the mining area, shall be invited to participate in the acceptance inspection, and the acceptance inspection results shall be made public.  
Where ecological restoration of a mining area is carried out by sub-area or by phases, acceptance inspection shall be carried out by sub-area or by phases.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　采矿权人应当按照规定提取矿区生态修复费用，专门用于矿区生态修复。矿区生态修复费用计入成本。  
县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门应当会同财政等有关部门对矿区生态修复费用的提取、使用情况进行监督检查。  
矿区生态修复费用提取、使用和监督管理的具体办法由国务院财政部门会同国务院自然资源主管部门制定。

**Article 49** A mining right holder shall, in accordance with the provisions, set aside funds for ecological restoration of the mining area, which shall be used exclusively for the ecological restoration of the mining area. The costs of ecological restoration of the mining area shall be included in the cost.  
The competent departments of natural resources of people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with the competent departments of finance and other relevant departments, conduct supervision and inspection on the setting aside and use of funds for ecological restoration of the mining area.  
Specific measures for the setting aside, use, supervision and management of funds for ecological restoration of the mining area shall be formulated by the competent department of finance under the State Council together with the competent department of natural resources under the State Council.

### 第五章　矿产资源储备和应急

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家构建产品储备、产能储备和产地储备相结合的战略性矿产资源储备体系，科学合理确定储备结构、规模和布局并动态调整。

**Article 50** The State shall establish a strategic mineral resources reserve system combining product reserves, production capacity reserves and origin reserves, scientifically and rationally determine the reserve structure, scale and layout, and make dynamic adjustments.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国务院发展改革、财政、物资储备、能源等有关部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当按照国家有关规定加强战略性矿产资源储备设施建设，组织实施矿产品储备，建立灵活高效的收储、轮换、动用机制。

**Article 51** The relevant departments of the State Council for development and reform, finance, materials and reserves, energy, and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, strengthen the construction of strategic mineral resources reserve facilities, organize the implementation of mineral products reserves, and establish a flexible and efficient mechanism for acquisition, rotation, and deployment.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　开采战略性矿产资源的采矿权人应当按照国家有关规定，落实产能储备责任，合理规划生产能力，确保应急增产需要。

**Article 52** The holder of mining rights exploiting strategic mineral resources shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, fulfill the responsibility of production capacity reserve, rationally plan production capacity, and ensure the need for emergency production increase.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国务院自然资源主管部门会同有关部门，根据保障国家矿产资源安全需要，结合资源储量、分布情况及其稀缺和重要程度等因素，划定战略性矿产资源储备地。  
战略性矿产资源储备地管理办法由国务院自然资源主管部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 53** The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments, delimit strategic mineral resources reserve lands based on the need to ensure national mineral resources security, taking into account factors such as resource reserves, distribution, and their scarcity and importance.  
The administrative measures for strategic mineral resources reserve lands shall be formulated by the competent department of natural resources of the State Council together with the relevant departments.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家建立和完善矿产资源供应安全预测预警体系，提高预测预警能力和水平，及时对矿产品供求变化、价格波动以及安全风险状况等进行预测预警。

**Article 54** The State shall establish and improve the prediction and early warning system for the safety of mineral resources supply, enhance the capacity and level of prediction and early warning, and timely carry out prediction and early warning on changes in mineral product supply and demand, price fluctuations, and safety risk conditions.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　出现矿产品供需严重失衡、经济社会发展和人民生活受到重大影响等矿产资源应急状态的，省级以上人民政府应当按照职责权限及时启动应急响应，可以依法采取下列应急处置措施：  
（一）发布矿产品供求等相关信息；  
（二）紧急调度矿产资源开采以及矿产品运输、供应；  
（三）在战略性矿产资源储备地等区域组织实施矿产资源应急性开采；  
（四）动用矿产品储备；  
（五）实施价格干预措施、紧急措施；  
（六）其他必要措施。  
出现矿产资源应急状态时，有关单位和个人应当服从统一指挥和安排，承担相应的应急义务，配合采取应急处置措施，协助维护市场秩序。  
因执行应急处置措施给有关单位、个人造成损失的，应当按照有关规定给予补偿。  
矿产资源应急状态消除后，省级以上人民政府应当按照职责权限及时终止实施应急处置措施。

**Article 55** Where a mineral resources emergency state occurs, such as severe imbalance between supply and demand of mineral products or major impacts on economic and social development and people's livelihood, the people's government at or above the provincial level shall, in accordance with its functions and powers, promptly initiate emergency response and may take the following emergency response measures according to law:  
(1) publishing relevant information on mineral product supply and demand;  
(2) urgently dispatching the exploitation of mineral resources and the transportation and supply of mineral products;  
(3) organizing emergency exploitation of mineral resources in areas such as strategic mineral resources reserve lands;  
(4) deploying mineral product reserves;  
(5) implementing price intervention measures and emergency measures;  
(6) other necessary measures.  
Where a mineral resources emergency state occurs, the relevant entities and individuals shall obey unified command and arrangements, assume corresponding emergency obligations, cooperate in taking emergency response measures, and assist in maintaining market order.  
Where losses are caused to relevant entities or individuals due to the execution of emergency response measures, compensation shall be given in accordance with relevant provisions.  
After the elimination of the mineral resources emergency state, the people's government at or above the provincial level shall promptly terminate the implementation of emergency response measures in accordance with its functions and powers.

### 第六章　监督管理

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门和其他有关部门应当按照职责分工，加强对矿产资源勘查、开采和矿区生态修复等活动的监督检查，依法及时查处违法行为。  
上级人民政府自然资源主管部门和其他有关部门应当加强对下级人民政府自然资源主管部门和其他有关部门执法活动的监督。

**Article 56** The competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with the division of duties, strengthen supervision and inspection over activities such as mineral resources exploration, exploitation, and mine ecological restoration, and investigate and deal with illegal acts in a timely manner according to law.  
The competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at higher levels and other relevant departments shall strengthen supervision over the law enforcement activities of the competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at lower levels and other relevant departments.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门和其他有关部门实施监督检查，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）进入勘查、开采区域等实施现场查验、勘测；  
（二）询问与检查事项有关的人员，要求其对有关事项作出说明；  
（三）查阅、复制与检查事项有关的文件、资料；  
（四）查封、扣押直接用于违法勘查、开采的工具、设备、设施、场所以及违法采出的矿产品；  
（五）法律、法规规定的其他措施。  
自然资源主管部门和其他有关部门依法实施监督检查，被检查单位及其有关人员应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻碍。  
自然资源主管部门和其他有关部门及其工作人员对监督检查过程中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私和个人信息依法负有保密义务。

**Article 57** The competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments may take the following measures when conducting supervision and inspection:  
(1) entering the exploration and exploitation areas to conduct on-site inspection and survey;  
(2) questioning persons involved in the matters under inspection and requiring them to make explanations on relevant matters;  
(3) consulting and copying documents and materials related to the matters under inspection;  
(4) sealing up and seizing tools, equipment, facilities, and premises directly used for illegal exploration and exploitation, as well as illegally exploited mineral products;  
(5) other measures provided for by laws and regulations.  
Where the competent departments of natural resources and other relevant departments conduct supervision and inspection according to law, the inspected entities and relevant persons shall cooperate and shall not refuse or obstruct.  
The competent departments of natural resources and other relevant departments and their staff members shall, according to law, bear the obligation of confidentiality with respect to State secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, and personal information learned during the process of supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国家建立矿产资源开发利用水平调查评估制度。  
国务院自然资源主管部门建立矿产资源开发利用水平评估指标体系。县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门应当加强对矿产资源勘查、开采情况的汇总、分析，并定期进行评估，提出节约集约开发利用矿产资源等方面的改进措施。

**Article 58** The State shall establish a system for the investigation and assessment of the level of mineral resources development and utilization.  
The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall establish an evaluation index system for the level of mineral resources development and utilization. The competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the summary and analysis of mineral resources exploration and exploitation, conduct regular assessments, and propose improvement measures for the economical and intensive development and utilization of mineral resources.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国务院自然资源主管部门建立全国矿业权分布底图和动态数据库。  
国务院自然资源主管部门组织建立全国矿产资源监督管理信息系统，提升监管和服务效能，依法及时公开监管和服务信息，并做好信息共享工作。

**Article 59** The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall establish a national base map of mining rights distribution and a dynamic database.  
The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall organize the establishment of a national mineral resources supervision and management information system to enhance the efficiency of supervision and service, timely disclose supervision and service information according to law, and ensure information sharing.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门应当按照国家有关规定，将矿业权人和从事矿区生态修复等活动的其他单位和个人的信用信息记入信用记录。

**Article 60** The competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, record the credit information of mining rights holders and other entities and individuals engaged in activities such as mine ecological restoration into the credit record.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　任何单位和个人对违反矿产资源法律、法规的行为，有权向县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门和其他有关部门举报，接到举报的部门应当及时依法处理。

**Article 61** Any entity or individual has the right to report acts violating the laws and regulations on mineral resources to the competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments, and the departments receiving the report shall handle it according to law in a timely manner.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门和其他有关部门的工作人员在矿产资源勘查、开采和矿区生态修复等活动的监督管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 62** Where a staff member of the competent department of natural resources of a people's government at or above the county level or other relevant department abuses power, neglects duty, or engages in malpractices for personal gain in the supervision and management of activities such as mineral resources exploration, exploitation, and mine ecological restoration, disciplinary action shall be taken according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本法规定，未取得探矿权勘查矿产资源的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得以及直接用于违法勘查的工具、设备，并处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款；拒不停止违法行为的，可以责令停业整顿。  
超出探矿权登记的勘查区域勘查矿产资源的，依照前款规定处罚；拒不停止违法行为，情节严重的，原矿业权出让部门可以吊销其勘查许可证。

**Article 63** Whoever, in violation of this Law, explores mineral resources without obtaining exploration rights shall be ordered by the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level to cease the illegal act, confiscate the illegal gains and the tools and equipment directly used for illegal exploration, and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if the illegal act is not ceased, the entity may be ordered to suspend operations for rectification.  
Whoever explores mineral resources beyond the exploration area registered in the exploration rights shall be punished in accordance with the preceding paragraph; if the illegal act is not ceased and the circumstances are serious, the original mining rights granting department may revoke the exploration permit.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反本法规定，未取得采矿权开采矿产资源的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收直接用于违法开采的工具、设备以及违法采出的矿产品，并处违法采出的矿产品市场价值三倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有采出矿产品或者违法采出的矿产品市场价值不足十万元的，并处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款；拒不停止违法行为的，可以责令停业整顿。  
超出采矿权登记的开采区域开采矿产资源的，依照前款规定处罚；拒不停止违法行为，情节严重的，原矿业权出让部门可以吊销其采矿许可证。  
违反本法规定，从事石油、天然气等矿产资源勘查活动，未在国务院自然资源主管部门规定的期限内依法取得采矿权进行开采的，依照本条第一款规定处罚。

**Article 64** Whoever, in violation of this Law, exploits mineral resources without obtaining mining rights shall be ordered by the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level to cease the illegal act, confiscate the tools and equipment directly used for illegal exploitation and the illegally exploited mineral products, and impose a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the market value of the illegally exploited mineral products; if no mineral products have been exploited or the market value of the illegally exploited mineral products is less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the illegal act is not ceased, the entity may be ordered to suspend operations for rectification.  
Whoever exploits mineral resources beyond the exploitation area registered in the mining rights shall be punished in accordance with the preceding paragraph; if the illegal act is not ceased and the circumstances are serious, the original mining rights granting department may revoke the mining permit.  
Whoever, in violation of this Law, engages in exploration activities of mineral resources such as oil and natural gas without obtaining mining rights for exploitation within the time limit prescribed by the competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall be punished in accordance with the first paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本法规定，建设项目未经批准压覆战略性矿产资源的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令改正，处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款。

**Article 65** Whoever, in violation of this Law, buries strategic mineral resources without approval for a construction project shall be ordered by the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本法规定，探矿权人未取得勘查许可证进行矿产资源勘查作业的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，没收违法所得以及直接用于违法勘查的工具、设备，处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款，并可以责令停业整顿。

**Article 66** Whoever, in violation of this Law, as an exploration right holder, carries out mineral resources exploration operations without obtaining an exploration permit shall be ordered by the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level to make corrections; if no correction is made, the illegal gains and the tools and equipment directly used for illegal exploration shall be confiscated, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the entity may be ordered to suspend operations for rectification.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　违反本法规定，采矿权人未取得采矿许可证进行矿产资源开采作业的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，没收直接用于违法开采的工具、设备以及违法采出的矿产品，处违法采出的矿产品市场价值一倍以上三倍以下罚款，没有采出矿产品或者违法采出的矿产品市场价值不足十万元的，处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款，并可以责令停业整顿。  
违反本法规定，从事石油、天然气等矿产资源勘查活动，未在国务院自然资源主管部门规定的期限内依法取得采矿许可证进行开采的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 67** Whoever, in violation of this Law, as a mining right holder, carries out mineral resources exploitation operations without obtaining a mining permit shall be ordered by the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level to make corrections; if no correction is made, the tools and equipment directly used for illegal exploitation and the illegally exploited mineral products shall be confiscated, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the market value of the illegally exploited mineral products shall be imposed; if no mineral products have been exploited or the market value of the illegally exploited mineral products is less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the entity may be ordered to suspend operations for rectification.  
Whoever, in violation of this Law, engages in exploration activities of mineral resources such as oil and natural gas without obtaining a mining permit for exploitation within the time limit prescribed by the competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall be punished in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一，造成矿产资源破坏的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令改正，处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款；拒不改正的，可以责令停业整顿；情节严重的，原矿业权出让部门可以吊销其勘查许可证、采矿许可证：  
（一）未按照经批准的勘查方案、开采方案进行矿产资源勘查、开采作业；  
（二）采取不合理的开采顺序、开采方法开采矿产资源；  
（三）矿产资源开采回采率、选矿回收率和综合利用率未达到有关国家标准的要求。  
违反本法规定，未按照保护性开采要求开采特定战略性矿产资源的，依照前款规定处罚；法律、行政法规另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 68** Whoever, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts, causing damage to mineral resources, shall be ordered by the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if no correction is made, the entity may be ordered to suspend operations for rectification; if the circumstances are serious, the original mining rights granting department may revoke the exploration permit or mining permit:  
(1) conducting mineral resources exploration or exploitation operations not in accordance with the approved exploration plan or exploitation plan;  
(2) adopting unreasonable mining sequence or mining methods to exploit mineral resources;  
(3) failing to meet the requirements of the relevant national standards for the recovery rate, mineral processing recovery rate, and comprehensive utilization rate of mineral resources.  
Whoever, in violation of this Law, exploits specific strategic mineral resources not in accordance with the requirements of protective exploitation shall be punished in accordance with the preceding paragraph; if otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　违反本法规定，勘查活动结束后探矿权人未及时对勘查区域进行清理或者未及时恢复受到破坏的地表植被的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令改正，可以处五万元以下罚款；拒不改正的，处五万元以上十万元以下罚款，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门确定有关单位代为清理、恢复，所需费用由探矿权人承担。

**Article 69** Where, after the exploration activities are completed, the exploration right holder fails to timely clean up the exploration area or fails to timely restore the damaged surface vegetation, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall order correction and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if no correction is made, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall designate a relevant entity to carry out clean-up and restoration at the expense of the exploration right holder.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　未按照规定汇交地质资料，或者矿业权人未按照规定编制并报送矿产资源储量报告的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令改正，处二万元以上十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款。  
矿业权人故意报送虚假的矿产资源储量报告的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门没收违法所得，并处二十万元以上一百万元以下罚款；情节严重的，由原矿业权出让部门收回矿业权。

**Article 70** Where geological data are not submitted as required, or the mining right holder fails to compile and submit a mineral resources reserves report as required, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where the mining right holder intentionally submits a false mineral resources reserves report, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the original mining rights granting department shall recover the mining rights.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　违反本法规定，采矿权人不履行矿区生态修复义务或者未按照经批准的矿区生态修复方案进行矿区生态修复的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令改正，可以处矿区生态修复所需费用二倍以下罚款；拒不改正的，处矿区生态修复所需费用二倍以上五倍以下罚款，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门确定有关单位代为修复，所需费用由采矿权人承担。

**Article 71** Where, in violation of this Law, the mining right holder fails to perform the mine ecological restoration obligation or fails to carry out mine ecological restoration in accordance with the approved mine ecological restoration plan, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall order correction and may impose a fine of not more than two times the cost required for mine ecological restoration; if no correction is made, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the cost required for mine ecological restoration shall be imposed, and the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall designate a relevant entity to carry out restoration at the expense of the mining right holder.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　出现矿产资源应急状态时，有关单位和个人违反本法规定，不服从统一指挥和安排、不承担相应的应急义务或者不配合采取应急处置措施的，由省级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门或者其他有关部门责令改正，给予警告或者通报批评；拒不改正的，对单位处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款，根据情节轻重，可以责令停业整顿或者依法吊销相关许可证件，对个人处一万元以上五万元以下罚款。

**Article 72** Where, during a mineral resources emergency state, the relevant entity or individual, in violation of this Law, fails to obey unified command and arrangement, fails to assume corresponding emergency obligations, or fails to cooperate in taking emergency response measures, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the provincial level or other relevant department shall order correction and give a warning or circulate a notice of criticism; if no correction is made, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed on the entity, and depending on the severity of the circumstances, the entity may be ordered to suspend operations for rectification or relevant licenses may be revoked according to law, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　违反本法规定，矿业权人拒绝、阻碍监督检查，或者在接受监督检查时弄虚作假的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门或者其他有关部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处二万元以上十万元以下罚款。

**Article 73** Where, in violation of this Law, the mining right holder refuses or obstructs supervision and inspection, or practices fraud when undergoing supervision and inspection, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level or other relevant department shall order correction; if no correction is made, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　违反本法规定，破坏矿产资源或者污染环境、破坏生态，损害国家利益、社会公共利益的，人民检察院、法律规定的机关和有关组织可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 74** Where, in violation of this Law, mineral resources are damaged or the environment is polluted or ecology is destroyed, harming national interests or social public interests, the People's Procuratorates, the organs prescribed by law, and relevant organizations may file a lawsuit with the People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反本法规定，造成他人人身财产损害或者生态环境损害的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 75** Whoever, in violation of this Law, causes personal or property damage to others or damage to the ecological environment shall bear civil liability according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　勘查、开采矿产资源、开展矿区生态修复，违反有关生态环境保护、安全生产、职业病防治、土地管理、林业草原、文物保护等法律、行政法规的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定处理、处罚。

**Article 76** Where exploration, exploitation of mineral resources, or mine ecological restoration violates the laws and administrative regulations on ecological and environmental protection, work safety, prevention and control of occupational diseases, land management, forestry and grassland, cultural relics protection, etc., the matter shall be handled and penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　外商投资勘查、开采矿产资源，法律、行政法规另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 77** Where there are other provisions in laws or administrative regulations regarding foreign investment in the exploration and exploitation of mineral resources, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　中华人民共和国境外的组织和个人，实施危害中华人民共和国国家矿产资源安全行为的，依法追究其法律责任。

**Article 78** Where an organization or individual outside the territory of the People's Republic of China commits an act that endangers the national mineral resources security of the People's Republic of China, its legal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约与本法有不同规定的，适用国际条约的规定；但是，中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。

**Article 79** Where an international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China contains provisions differing from those of this Law, the provisions of the international treaty shall apply, except those clauses on which the People's Republic of China has declared reservations.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　本法自2025年7月1日起施行。

**Article 80** This Law shall come into force as of July 1, 2025.
