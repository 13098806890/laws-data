# 中华人民共和国农业机械化促进法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2018-10-26  
**生效日期**：2018-10-26

> （2004年6月25日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议通过　根据2018年10月26日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第六次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国野生动物保护法〉等十五部法律的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　科研开发 第三章　质量保障 第四章　推广使用 第五章　社会化服务 第六章　扶持措施 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了鼓励、扶持农民和农业生产经营组织使用先进适用的农业机械，促进农业机械化，建设现代农业，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称农业机械化，是指运用先进适用的农业机械装备农业，改善农业生产经营条件，不断提高农业的生产技术水平和经济效益、生态效益的过程。 本法所称农业机械，是指用于农业生产及其产品初加工等相关农事活动的机械、设备。 第三条　县级以上人民政府应当把推进农业机械化纳入国民经济和社会发展计划，采取财政支持和实施国家规定的税收优惠政策以及金融扶持等措施，逐步提高对农业机械化的资金投入，充分发挥市场机制的作用，按照因地制宜、经济有效、保障安全、保护环境的原则，促进农业机械化的发展。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了鼓励、扶持农民和农业生产经营组织使用先进适用的农业机械，促进农业机械化，建设现代农业，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of encouraging and supporting farmers and agricultural production and operation organizations to use advanced and suitable agricultural machinery, promoting agricultural mechanization, and building modern agriculture.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称农业机械化，是指运用先进适用的农业机械装备农业，改善农业生产经营条件，不断提高农业的生产技术水平和经济效益、生态效益的过程。  
本法所称农业机械，是指用于农业生产及其产品初加工等相关农事活动的机械、设备。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, agricultural mechanization means the process of equipping agriculture with advanced and suitable agricultural machinery, improving agricultural production and operation conditions, and continuously enhancing the production technical level and economic and ecological benefits of agriculture.  
For the purposes of this Law, agricultural machinery means the machinery and equipment used for agricultural production, primary processing of agricultural products, and related agricultural activities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　县级以上人民政府应当把推进农业机械化纳入国民经济和社会发展计划，采取财政支持和实施国家规定的税收优惠政策以及金融扶持等措施，逐步提高对农业机械化的资金投入，充分发挥市场机制的作用，按照因地制宜、经济有效、保障安全、保护环境的原则，促进农业机械化的发展。

**Article 3** The people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the promotion of agricultural mechanization into the national economic and social development plans, adopt measures such as financial support, implementation of tax preferential policies prescribed by the State, and financial support, gradually increase the capital investment in agricultural mechanization, fully leverage the role of market mechanisms, and promote the development of agricultural mechanization in accordance with the principles of adapting measures to local conditions, economic efficiency, safety assurance, and environmental protection.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家引导、支持农民和农业生产经营组织自主选择先进适用的农业机械。任何单位和个人不得强迫农民和农业生产经营组织购买其指定的农业机械产品。

**Article 4** The State shall guide and support farmers and agricultural production and operation organizations to independently select advanced and suitable agricultural machinery. No unit or individual shall compel farmers or agricultural production and operation organizations to purchase the agricultural machinery products designated by them.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家采取措施，开展农业机械化科技知识的宣传和教育，培养农业机械化专业人才，推进农业机械化信息服务，提高农业机械化水平。

**Article 5** The State shall take measures to carry out publicity and education on agricultural mechanization science and technology knowledge, train specialized talents in agricultural mechanization, promote agricultural mechanization information services, and raise the level of agricultural mechanization.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院农业行政主管部门和其他负责农业机械化有关工作的部门，按照各自的职责分工，密切配合，共同做好农业机械化促进工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府主管农业机械化工作的部门和其他有关部门，按照各自的职责分工，密切配合，共同做好本行政区域的农业机械化促进工作。

**Article 6** The competent agricultural administrative department of the State Council and other departments responsible for work related to agricultural mechanization shall, in accordance with their respective division of duties, closely cooperate and jointly do a good job in the promotion of agricultural mechanization.  
The departments in charge of agricultural mechanization work of the local people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective division of duties, closely cooperate and jointly do a good job in the promotion of agricultural mechanization in their respective administrative regions.

### 第二章　科研开发

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　省级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当组织有关单位采取技术攻关、试验、示范等措施，促进基础性、关键性、公益性农业机械科学研究和先进适用的农业机械的推广应用。

**Article 7** The people's governments at or above the provincial level and their relevant departments shall organize relevant units to take measures such as technical tackling, experiments, and demonstrations to promote basic, key, and public-interest agricultural machinery scientific research and the widespread application of advanced and suitable agricultural machinery.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家支持有关科研机构和院校加强农业机械化科学技术研究，根据不同的农业生产条件和农民需求，研究开发先进适用的农业机械；支持农业机械科研、教学与生产、推广相结合，促进农业机械与农业生产技术的发展要求相适应。

**Article 8** The State shall support relevant scientific research institutions and colleges and universities in strengthening scientific and technological research on agricultural mechanization, researching and developing advanced and suitable agricultural machinery based on different agricultural production conditions and farmers' needs; and support the combination of agricultural machinery research, teaching, production, and popularization, to promote the adaptation of agricultural machinery to the development requirements of agricultural production technology.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家支持农业机械生产者开发先进适用的农业机械，采用先进技术、先进工艺和先进材料，提高农业机械产品的质量和技术水平，降低生产成本，提供系列化、标准化、多功能和质量优良、节约能源、价格合理的农业机械产品。

**Article 9** The State shall support agricultural machinery producers in developing advanced and suitable agricultural machinery, adopting advanced technologies, advanced techniques, and advanced materials, improving the quality and technical level of agricultural machinery products, reducing production costs, and providing agricultural machinery products that are serialized, standardized, multi-functional, of excellent quality, energy-saving, and reasonably priced.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家支持引进、利用先进的农业机械、关键零配件和技术，鼓励引进外资从事农业机械的研究、开发、生产和经营。

**Article 10** The State shall support the introduction and utilization of advanced agricultural machinery, key parts and components, and technologies, and encourage the introduction of foreign capital to engage in the research, development, production, and operation of agricultural machinery.

### 第三章　质量保障

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家加强农业机械化标准体系建设，制定和完善农业机械产品质量、维修质量和作业质量等标准。对农业机械产品涉及人身安全、农产品质量安全和环境保护的技术要求，应当按照有关法律、行政法规的规定制定强制执行的技术规范。

**Article 11** The State shall strengthen the construction of agricultural mechanization standard system, formulate and improve standards for the quality of agricultural machinery products, maintenance quality, and operation quality. For technical requirements of agricultural machinery products involving personal safety, quality safety of agricultural products, and environmental protection, compulsory technical specifications shall be formulated in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　市场监督管理部门应当依法组织对农业机械产品质量的监督抽查，加强对农业机械产品市场的监督管理工作。  
国务院农业行政主管部门和省级人民政府主管农业机械化工作的部门根据农业机械使用者的投诉情况和农业生产的实际需要，可以组织对在用的特定种类农业机械产品的适用性、安全性、可靠性和售后服务状况进行调查，并公布调查结果。

**Article 12** The market supervision and administration department shall, according to law, organize supervision and random inspection of the quality of agricultural machinery products, and strengthen the supervision and management of the market for agricultural machinery products.  
The competent agricultural administrative department of the State Council and the departments in charge of agricultural mechanization work of the provincial people's governments may, based on the complaints of users of agricultural machinery and the actual needs of agricultural production, organize investigations into the applicability, safety, reliability, and after-sales service status of specific types of agricultural machinery products in use, and publish the investigation results.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　农业机械生产者、销售者应当对其生产、销售的农业机械产品质量负责，并按照国家有关规定承担零配件供应和培训等售后服务责任。  
农业机械生产者应当按照国家标准、行业标准和保障人身安全的要求，在其生产的农业机械产品上设置必要的安全防护装置、警示标志和中文警示说明。

**Article 13** Agricultural machinery producers and sellers shall be responsible for the quality of the agricultural machinery products they produce or sell, and shall undertake after-sales service responsibilities such as the supply of spare parts and training in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Agricultural machinery producers shall, in accordance with national standards, industry standards, and the requirements for ensuring personal safety, set up necessary safety protection devices, warning signs, and Chinese warning instructions on the agricultural machinery products they produce.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　农业机械产品不符合质量要求的，农业机械生产者、销售者应当负责修理、更换、退货；给农业机械使用者造成农业生产损失或者其他损失的，应当依法赔偿损失。农业机械使用者有权要求农业机械销售者先予赔偿。农业机械销售者赔偿后，属于农业机械生产者的责任的，农业机械销售者有权向农业机械生产者追偿。  
因农业机械存在缺陷造成人身伤害、财产损失的，农业机械生产者、销售者应当依法赔偿损失。

**Article 14** If an agricultural machinery product does not meet the quality requirements, the agricultural machinery producer or seller shall be responsible for repair, replacement, or return of the product; if it causes agricultural production losses or other losses to the user of the agricultural machinery, the producer or seller shall compensate for the losses according to law. The user of the agricultural machinery shall have the right to demand that the agricultural machinery seller provide compensation first. After the agricultural machinery seller compensates, if the liability belongs to the agricultural machinery producer, the agricultural machinery seller shall have the right to recover from the agricultural machinery producer.  
If personal injury or property loss is caused due to defects in agricultural machinery, the agricultural machinery producer or seller shall compensate for the losses according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　列入依法必须经过认证的产品目录的农业机械产品，未经认证并标注认证标志，禁止出厂、销售和进口。  
禁止生产、销售不符合国家技术规范强制性要求的农业机械产品。  
禁止利用残次零配件和报废机具的部件拼装农业机械产品。

**Article 15** No agricultural machinery product listed in the product catalog that must undergo certification according to law shall be delivered from the factory, sold, or imported without being certified and marked with certification marks.  
No agricultural machinery product that does not meet the compulsory requirements of national technical specifications shall be produced or sold.  
No agricultural machinery product shall be assembled using defective parts and components or parts of scrapped machinery.

### 第四章　推广使用

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家支持向农民和农业生产经营组织推广先进适用的农业机械产品。推广农业机械产品，应当适应当地农业发展的需要，并依照农业技术推广法的规定，在推广地区经过试验证明具有先进性和适用性。  
农业机械生产者或者销售者，可以委托农业机械试验鉴定机构，对其定型生产或者销售的农业机械产品进行适用性、安全性和可靠性检测，作出技术评价。农业机械试验鉴定机构应当公布具有适用性、安全性和可靠性的农业机械产品的检测结果，为农民和农业生产经营组织选购先进适用的农业机械提供信息。

**Article 16** The State shall support the promotion of advanced and suitable agricultural machinery products to farmers and agricultural production and operation organizations. The promotion of agricultural machinery products shall meet the needs of local agricultural development and, in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Popularization of Agricultural Technology, shall be proven to be advanced and applicable through experiments in the promotion areas.  
An agricultural machinery producer or seller may entrust an agricultural machinery test and appraisal institution to conduct tests on the applicability, safety, and reliability of its finalized or sold agricultural machinery products and make technical evaluations. The agricultural machinery test and appraisal institution shall publish the test results of agricultural machinery products that have applicability, safety, and reliability, so as to provide information for farmers and agricultural production and operation organizations to select and purchase advanced and suitable agricultural machinery.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上人民政府可以根据实际情况，在不同的农业区域建立农业机械化示范基地，并鼓励农业机械生产者、经营者等建立农业机械示范点，引导农民和农业生产经营组织使用先进适用的农业机械。

**Article 17** The people's governments at or above the county level may, based on actual conditions, establish agricultural mechanization demonstration bases in different agricultural regions, and encourage agricultural machinery producers, operators, and others to establish agricultural machinery demonstration sites, to guide farmers and agricultural production and operation organizations to use advanced and suitable agricultural machinery.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院农业行政主管部门会同国务院财政部门、经济综合宏观调控部门，根据促进农业结构调整、保护自然资源与生态环境、推广农业新技术与加快农机具更新的原则，确定、公布国家支持推广的先进适用的农业机械产品目录，并定期调整。省级人民政府主管农业机械化工作的部门会同同级财政部门、经济综合宏观调控部门根据上述原则，确定、公布省级人民政府支持推广的先进适用的农业机械产品目录，并定期调整。  
列入前款目录的产品，应当由农业机械生产者自愿提出申请，并通过农业机械试验鉴定机构进行的先进性、适用性、安全性和可靠性鉴定。

**Article 18** The competent agricultural administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the finance department of the State Council and the comprehensive economic macro-control department, determine and publish the catalog of advanced and suitable agricultural machinery products that the State supports for popularization in accordance with the principles of promoting agricultural structural adjustment, protecting natural resources and the ecological environment, popularizing new agricultural technologies, and accelerating the renewal of agricultural machinery and tools, and shall regularly adjust the catalog. The department in charge of agricultural mechanization work of the provincial people's government shall, together with the finance department at the same level and the comprehensive economic macro-control department, determine and publish the catalog of advanced and suitable agricultural machinery products that the provincial people's government supports for popularization in accordance with the above-mentioned principles, and shall regularly adjust the catalog.  
Products included in the catalog mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be subject to an application voluntarily made by the agricultural machinery producer, and shall pass the appraisal of advanced nature, applicability, safety, and reliability conducted by an agricultural machinery test and appraisal institution.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家鼓励和支持农民合作使用农业机械，提高农业机械利用率和作业效率，降低作业成本。  
国家支持和保护农民在坚持家庭承包经营的基础上，自愿组织区域化、标准化种植，提高农业机械的作业水平。任何单位和个人不得以区域化、标准化种植为借口，侵犯农民的土地承包经营权。

**Article 19** The State shall encourage and support farmers to cooperatively use agricultural machinery, so as to increase the utilization rate and operation efficiency of agricultural machinery and reduce operation costs.  
The State shall support and protect farmers, on the basis of adhering to the household contract management system, to voluntarily organize regionalized and standardized planting, so as to raise the operation level of agricultural machinery. No unit or individual shall, on the pretext of regionalized and standardized planting, infringe upon the farmers' right to land contractual management.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院农业行政主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府主管农业机械化工作的部门，应当按照安全生产、预防为主的方针，加强对农业机械安全使用的宣传、教育和管理。  
农业机械使用者作业时，应当按照安全操作规程操作农业机械，在有危险的部位和作业现场设置防护装置或者警示标志。

**Article 20** The competent agricultural administrative department of the State Council and the departments in charge of agricultural mechanization work of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the policy of putting safety first and focusing on prevention, strengthen publicity, education, and management on the safe use of agricultural machinery.  
When operating agricultural machinery, the user of agricultural machinery shall operate the agricultural machinery in accordance with safety operation procedures, and set up protective devices or warning signs at dangerous parts and at the operation site.

### 第五章　社会化服务

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　农民、农业机械作业组织可以按照双方自愿、平等协商的原则，为本地或者外地的农民和农业生产经营组织提供各项有偿农业机械作业服务。有偿农业机械作业应当符合国家或者地方规定的农业机械作业质量标准。  
国家鼓励跨行政区域开展农业机械作业服务。各级人民政府及其有关部门应当支持农业机械跨行政区域作业，维护作业秩序，提供便利和服务，并依法实施安全监督管理。

**Article 21** Farmers and agricultural machinery operation organizations may, in accordance with the principles of voluntariness and equal consultation between the two parties, provide various paid agricultural machinery operation services to local or non-local farmers and agricultural production and operation organizations. Paid agricultural machinery operation shall meet the national or local standards for the quality of agricultural machinery operation.  
The State shall encourage cross-administrative-region agricultural machinery operation services. The people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall support cross-administrative-region operation of agricultural machinery, maintain operation order, provide convenience and services, and carry out safety supervision and management according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　各级人民政府应当采取措施，鼓励和扶持发展多种形式的农业机械服务组织，推进农业机械化信息网络建设，完善农业机械化服务体系。农业机械服务组织应当根据农民、农业生产经营组织的需求，提供农业机械示范推广、实用技术培训、维修、信息、中介等社会化服务。

**Article 22** The people's governments at all levels shall take measures to encourage and support the development of various forms of agricultural machinery service organizations, promote the construction of agricultural mechanization information networks, and improve the agricultural mechanization service system. Agricultural machinery service organizations shall, based on the needs of farmers and agricultural production and operation organizations, provide social services such as agricultural machinery demonstration and popularization, practical technical training, maintenance, information, and intermediary services.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家设立的基层农业机械技术推广机构应当以试验示范基地为依托，为农民和农业生产经营组织无偿提供公益性农业机械技术的推广、培训等服务。

**Article 23** The grass-roots agricultural machinery technology popularization institutions established by the State shall, relying on experimental demonstration bases, provide farmers and agricultural production and operation organizations with public-interest agricultural machinery technology popularization, training, and other services free of charge.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　从事农业机械维修，应当具备与维修业务相适应的仪器、设备和具有农业机械维修职业技能的技术人员，保证维修质量。维修质量不合格的，维修者应当免费重新修理；造成人身伤害或者财产损失的，维修者应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 24** Those engaged in the maintenance of agricultural machinery shall have the instruments and equipment commensurate with the maintenance business and technical personnel with professional skills in agricultural machinery maintenance, and shall ensure the maintenance quality. If the maintenance quality is not up to standard, the maintainer shall carry out re-maintenance free of charge; if personal injury or property loss is caused, the maintainer shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　农业机械生产者、经营者、维修者可以依照法律、行政法规的规定，自愿成立行业协会，实行行业自律，为会员提供服务，维护会员的合法权益。

**Article 25** Agricultural machinery producers, operators, and maintainers may, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, voluntarily establish trade associations, exercise industry self-discipline, provide services for their members, and protect the legitimate rights and interests of their members.

### 第六章　扶持措施

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家采取措施，鼓励和支持农业机械生产者增加新产品、新技术、新工艺的研究开发投入，并对农业机械的科研开发和制造实施税收优惠政策。  
中央和地方财政预算安排的科技开发资金应当对农业机械工业的技术创新给予支持。

**Article 26** The State shall take measures to encourage and support agricultural machinery producers to increase investment in the research and development of new products, new technologies, and new techniques, and shall implement tax preferential policies for the scientific research, development, and manufacturing of agricultural machinery.  
The science and technology development funds arranged in the budgets of the central and local finances shall provide support for the technological innovation of the agricultural machinery industry.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　中央财政、省级财政应当分别安排专项资金，对农民和农业生产经营组织购买国家支持推广的先进适用的农业机械给予补贴。补贴资金的使用应当遵循公开、公正、及时、有效的原则，可以向农民和农业生产经营组织发放，也可以采用贴息方式支持金融机构向农民和农业生产经营组织购买先进适用的农业机械提供贷款。具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 27** The central finance and the provincial finance shall respectively arrange special funds to provide subsidies to farmers and agricultural production and operation organizations for the purchase of advanced and suitable agricultural machinery that the State supports for popularization. The use of subsidy funds shall follow the principles of openness, fairness, timeliness, and effectiveness. The subsidies may be distributed to farmers and agricultural production and operation organizations, or may be used in the form of interest discounts to support financial institutions in providing loans to farmers and agricultural production and operation organizations for the purchase of advanced and suitable agricultural machinery. The specific measures shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　从事农业机械生产作业服务的收入，按照国家规定给予税收优惠。  
国家根据农业和农村经济发展的需要，对农业机械的农业生产作业用燃油安排财政补贴。燃油补贴应当向直接从事农业机械作业的农民和农业生产经营组织发放。具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 28** The income from providing agricultural machinery production operation services shall be granted tax preferences in accordance with the provisions of the State.  
The State shall, based on the needs of agricultural and rural economic development, arrange financial subsidies for fuel used for agricultural production operations of agricultural machinery. The fuel subsidies shall be distributed to farmers and agricultural production and operation organizations that directly engage in agricultural machinery operations. The specific measures shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　地方各级人民政府应当采取措施加强农村机耕道路等农业机械化基础设施的建设和维护，为农业机械化创造条件。  
县级以上地方人民政府主管农业机械化工作的部门应当建立农业机械化信息搜集、整理、发布制度，为农民和农业生产经营组织免费提供信息服务。

**Article 29** The local people's governments at all levels shall take measures to strengthen the construction and maintenance of agricultural mechanization infrastructure such as rural tractor roads, so as to create conditions for agricultural mechanization.  
The departments in charge of agricultural mechanization work of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish systems for the collection, collation, and publication of agricultural mechanization information, and provide information services to farmers and agricultural production and operation organizations free of charge.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　违反[本法第十五条](#art-15)规定的，依照产品质量法的有关规定予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 30** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 15 of this Law shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Product Quality Law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　农业机械驾驶、操作人员违反国家规定的安全操作规程，违章作业的，责令改正，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 31** If an agricultural machinery driver or operator violates the safety operation procedures prescribed by the State and operates in violation of regulations, he or she shall be ordered to make corrections and shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　农业机械试验鉴定机构在鉴定工作中不按照规定为农业机械生产者、销售者进行鉴定，或者伪造鉴定结果、出具虚假证明，给农业机械使用者造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 32** If an agricultural machinery test and appraisal institution fails to conduct appraisal for agricultural machinery producers or sellers in accordance with the provisions in its appraisal work, or forges appraisal results or issues false certificates, thereby causing losses to users of agricultural machinery, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院农业行政主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府主管农业机械化工作的部门违反本法规定，强制或者变相强制农业机械生产者、销售者对其生产、销售的农业机械产品进行鉴定的，由上级主管机关或者监察机关责令限期改正，并对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予行政处分。

**Article 33** If the competent agricultural administrative department of the State Council or the departments in charge of agricultural mechanization work of the local people's governments at or above the county level violate the provisions of this Law by forcing or forcing in disguised form agricultural machinery producers or sellers to conduct appraisal of the agricultural machinery products they produce or sell, the competent authority at the higher level or the supervisory authority shall order them to make corrections within a time limit, and shall give administrative sanctions to the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反[本法第二十七条](#art-27)、第二十八条规定，截留、挪用有关补贴资金的，由上级主管机关责令限期归还被截留、挪用的资金，没收非法所得，并由上级主管机关、监察机关或者所在单位对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 34** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 27 or Article 28 of this Law by intercepting or misappropriating relevant subsidy funds shall be ordered by the competent authority at the higher level to return the intercepted or misappropriated funds within a time limit, and the illegal gains shall be confiscated; and the competent authority at the higher level, the supervisory authority, or the entity where the person works shall give administrative sanctions to the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本法自2004年11月1日起施行。

**Article 35** This Law shall come into force as of November 1, 2004.
