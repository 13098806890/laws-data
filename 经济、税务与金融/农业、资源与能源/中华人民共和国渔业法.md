### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强渔业资源的保护、开发和可持续利用，保障渔业生产者的合法权益，促进渔业高质量发展，适应社会主义现代化建设和人民生活需要，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of strengthening the protection, development and sustainable utilization of fishery resources, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of fishery producers, promoting high-quality development of fisheries, and meeting the needs of socialist modernization and the people's livelihood.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国内水、滩涂、领海、专属经济区以及中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事养殖和捕捞水生动物、水生植物等渔业生产活动，以及渔业资源保护等活动，适用本法。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to fishery production activities such as aquaculture and fishing of aquatic animals and aquatic plants, and activities such as protection of fishery resources, conducted in the inland waters, tidal flats, territorial seas, exclusive economic zones and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　渔业工作坚持中国共产党的领导，统筹发展和安全，统筹渔业资源保护与可持续利用，坚持量质并重、创新驱动、绿色发展。

**Article 3** Fishery work shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, coordinate development and security, coordinate the protection and sustainable utilization of fishery resources, and adhere to the principles of attaching equal importance to quantity and quality, innovation-driven development, and green development.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对渔业生产实行以养殖为主，养殖、捕捞、加工并举，因地制宜、各有侧重的方针，提高水产品供应保障能力。

**Article 4** The State shall implement a policy of focusing on aquaculture, with equal emphasis on aquaculture, fishing and processing, adapting measures to local conditions and giving priority to different aspects, so as to improve the capacity to ensure the supply of aquatic products.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府应当把渔业发展纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。

**Article 5** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate fishery development into the national economic and social development plans.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院渔业渔政主管部门主管全国的渔业工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府渔业渔政主管部门主管本行政区域内的渔业工作。

**Article 6** The competent department of fishery and fishery administration under the State Council shall be in charge of fishery work nationwide.  
The competent department of fishery and fishery administration under the local people's government at or above the county level shall be in charge of fishery work within its administrative region.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对渔业的监督管理，实行统一领导、分级管理。  
海洋渔业，除国务院划定由国务院渔业渔政主管部门监督管理的海域和特定渔业资源渔场外，由毗邻海域的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业渔政主管部门监督管理。  
江河、湖泊、水库等水域的渔业，按照行政区划由有关县级以上地方人民政府渔业渔政主管部门监督管理；跨行政区域的，由有关县级以上地方人民政府协商制定管理办法，或者由上一级人民政府渔业渔政主管部门监督管理。  
渔业执法工作由县级以上人民政府渔业渔政主管部门或者县级以上人民政府明确的执法机构（以下统称渔业执法机构）承担。

**Article 7** The State shall implement unified leadership and hierarchical management for the supervision and administration of fisheries.  
With respect to marine fisheries, except for the sea areas and specific fishery resource fishing grounds designated by the State Council to be supervised and administered by the competent department of fishery and fishery administration under the State Council, the supervision and administration shall be carried out by the competent department of fishery and fishery administration under the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government adjacent to the sea area.  
With respect to fisheries in rivers, lakes, reservoirs and other waters, the supervision and administration shall be carried out by the competent department of fishery and fishery administration under the relevant local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the administrative divisions; for those across administrative divisions, the relevant local people's governments at or above the county level shall consult and formulate management measures, or the supervision and administration shall be carried out by the competent department of fishery and fishery administration under the people's government at the next higher level.  
The fishery law enforcement work shall be undertaken by the competent department of fishery and fishery administration under the people's government at or above the county level or the law enforcement agency designated by the people's government at or above the county level (hereinafter collectively referred to as the fishery law enforcement agency).

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励、支持渔业科学技术研究开发和成果转化，加强渔业资源与环境监测评估，推广优良水产品种、先进技术和新型设施装备等，提高渔业科学技术水平。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/农业农村/中华人民共和国渔业法实施细则.md#art-37" title="被《中华人民共和国渔业法实施细则》第37条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 8** The State shall encourage and support the research and development of fishery science and technology and the transformation of achievements, strengthen the monitoring and assessment of fishery resources and environment, and promote high-quality aquatic varieties, advanced technologies, new facilities and equipment, etc., so as to raise the level of fishery science and technology.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上人民政府应当按照有关规定做好转产转业渔民的就业创业扶持、社会救助等工作，加强职业技能培训，依法维护其社会保障权益。

**Article 9** People's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, do a good job in employment and entrepreneurship support, social assistance, etc. for fishermen transferred from fisheries to other industries, strengthen vocational skill training, and safeguard their social security rights and interests according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　从事渔业生产的单位应当遵守法律、法规和国家有关规定，建立健全安全生产规章制度，加强安全生产教育、培训和管理，提高安全生产水平。

**Article 10** Entities engaged in fishery production shall comply with laws, regulations and relevant provisions of the State, establish and improve safety production rules and regulations, strengthen safety production education, training and management, and improve the level of safety production.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　渔业生产者依法自愿成立行业协会，为其成员提供生产、营销、信息、技术、培训、咨询等方面的服务，发挥协调和自律作用，维护成员和行业的合法权益。  
国家鼓励发展渔业互助保险等多种形式的渔业保险，增强渔业生产的抗风险能力。

**Article 11** Fishery producers may voluntarily establish industry associations according to law, providing their members with services in production, marketing, information, technology, training, consultation, etc., playing a role in coordination and self-discipline, and safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of members and the industry.  
The State shall encourage the development of various forms of fishery insurance such as fishery mutual insurance, so as to enhance the risk resistance capability of fishery production.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家鼓励渔业对外交流与合作，积极参与国际渔业治理，促进全球渔业资源科学养护和可持续利用。

**Article 12** The State shall encourage foreign exchanges and cooperation in fisheries, actively participate in international fisheries governance, and promote the scientific conservation and sustainable utilization of global fishery resources.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　外国人、外国籍渔业船舶进入中华人民共和国管辖水域，从事渔业生产或者渔业资源调查活动，应当经国务院有关主管部门批准，并遵守本法和中华人民共和国其他有关法律、法规的规定；中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的条约、协定有规定的，按照条约、协定办理。  
国务院渔业渔政主管部门对外行使渔政渔港监督管理权。

**Article 13** Aliens and foreign fishing vessels entering the waters under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China to engage in fishery production or fishery resource survey activities shall obtain approval from the relevant competent department under the State Council and comply with the provisions of this Law and other relevant laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China; if there are provisions in treaties or agreements concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, those provisions shall prevail.  
The competent department of fishery and fishery administration under the State Council shall exercise the power of supervision and administration of fishery administration and fishery ports externally.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对在保护渔业资源、发展渔业生产、进行渔业科学技术研究开发和成果转化，以及传承渔业文化等方面成绩显著的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 14** Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in protecting fishery resources, developing fishery production, conducting research and development and transformation of fishery science and technology achievements, and inheriting fishery culture, etc., shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　养殖业

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家鼓励单位和个人科学合理利用适于养殖的水域、滩涂发展养殖业，支持资源节约、环境友好、质量安全的养殖模式。  
国家鼓励在海洋、江河、湖泊、水库等水域依法开展绿色生态增殖养殖，发展深远海养殖，开展对外养殖合作。

**Article 15** The State shall encourage units and individuals to scientifically and rationally utilize waters and tidal flats suitable for aquaculture to develop aquaculture, and support resource-saving, environment-friendly, and quality-safe aquaculture models.  
The State shall encourage the development of green ecological proliferation aquaculture in waters such as oceans, rivers, lakes, and reservoirs according to law, develop deep-sea aquaculture, and carry out foreign aquaculture cooperation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家对水域、滩涂利用进行统一规划，确定可以用于养殖业的水域、滩涂，建立养殖水域、滩涂保护制度。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当根据需要组织编制并实施养殖水域滩涂规划，合理划定养殖区、限养区。养殖水域滩涂规划应当符合国土空间规划、流域综合规划，与防洪规划、海上交通资源规划、生态环境保护有关规划等相衔接。  
禁止在港口、航道、海上安全作业区等法律、行政法规规定的禁止养殖区域开展水产养殖。

**Article 16** The State shall make unified planning for the utilization of waters and tidal flats, determine the waters and tidal flats that can be used for aquaculture, and establish a protection system for aquaculture waters and tidal flats.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, as needed, organize the formulation and implementation of aquaculture waters and tidal flats plans, and reasonably designate aquaculture areas and restricted aquaculture areas. The aquaculture waters and tidal flats plans shall conform to the territorial space plan and the comprehensive river basin plan, and be consistent with flood control plans, maritime traffic resource plans, and plans related to ecological environmental protection.  
It shall be prohibited to carry out aquaculture in areas where aquaculture is prohibited under laws and administrative regulations, such as ports, waterways, and maritime safety operation areas.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　单位和个人使用国家所有的水域、滩涂从事养殖生产的，应当向县级以上地方人民政府渔业渔政主管部门提出申请，由本级人民政府核发养殖证，合理确定养殖期限，许可其使用该水域、滩涂从事养殖生产。核发养殖证的具体办法由国务院规定。  
县级以上地方人民政府在核发养殖证时，应当优先安排具有下列情形之一的当地渔业生产者从事养殖生产：  
（一）以水域、滩涂养殖生产为主要生活来源；  
（二）因渔业产业结构调整，由捕捞业转产从事养殖业；  
（三）因养殖水域滩涂规划调整，需要另行安排养殖水域、滩涂从事养殖生产；  
（四）法律、法规规定的其他情形。  
集体所有或者国家所有由农村集体经济组织使用的水域、滩涂，可以由个人或者单位依照有关法律、法规的规定承包，从事养殖生产。

**Article 17** Where units or individuals use State-owned waters or tidal flats to engage in aquaculture production, they shall apply to the competent department of fishery and fishery administration under the local people's government at or above the county level, and the people's government at the same level shall issue an aquaculture certificate, reasonably determine the aquaculture period, and permit them to use such waters or tidal flats for aquaculture production. The specific measures for issuing aquaculture certificates shall be formulated by the State Council.  
When issuing aquaculture certificates, the local people's government at or above the county level shall give priority to local fishery producers who meet any of the following circumstances for aquaculture production:  
(1) primarily relying on aquaculture production in waters or tidal flats as their main source of livelihood;  
(2) having transferred from fishing to aquaculture due to adjustment of fishery industrial structure;  
(3) needing to be assigned other waters or tidal flats for aquaculture production due to adjustment of the aquaculture waters and tidal flats plan;  
(4) other circumstances as provided by laws and regulations.  
Waters or tidal flats that are collectively owned or State-owned but used by rural collective economic organizations may be contracted by individuals or units for aquaculture production in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　单位和个人因使用水域、滩涂从事养殖生产发生争议的，依照有关法律规定处理；争议解决前，任何一方不得破坏养殖生产。

**Article 18** Where disputes arise between units or individuals over the use of waters or tidal flats for aquaculture production, they shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws; before the dispute is resolved, neither party shall damage the aquaculture production.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上地方人民政府因公共利益需要依法变更或者撤回养殖证，给单位或者个人造成财产损失的，应当给予公平、合理的补偿。  
征收集体所有的水域、滩涂的，依照有关土地管理的法律、行政法规的规定办理。

**Article 19** Where a local people's government at or above the county level alters or revokes an aquaculture certificate according to law due to the need of public interest, causing property losses to a unit or individual, it shall provide fair and reasonable compensation.  
Where collectively owned waters or tidal flats are expropriated, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on land management.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级以上地方人民政府应当采取措施，加强对水产品生产基地和重要养殖水域、滩涂的保护。

**Article 20** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to strengthen the protection of aquatic product production bases and important aquaculture waters and tidal flats.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　省级以上人民政府渔业渔政主管部门应当根据水产种质资源保护的需要，确定水产种质资源保种场，加强水产原种和优良品种的保护。

**Article 21** The competent departments of fisheries and fishery administration of the people's governments at or above the provincial level shall, according to the need for protection of aquatic germplasm resources, designate aquatic germplasm resource conservation farms and strengthen the protection of aquatic original species and fine varieties.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家鼓励和支持水产优良品种的选育、培育和推广。  
水产新品种应当经全国水产品种审定委员会审定，由国务院渔业渔政主管部门公告后推广。水产新品种审定办法由国务院渔业渔政主管部门制定。  
从事水产苗种生产活动，应当取得县级以上地方人民政府渔业渔政主管部门核发的水产苗种生产许可证。但是，渔业生产者自育、自用水产苗种的除外。

**Article 22** The State encourages and supports the selection, cultivation, and promotion of fine aquatic varieties.  
New aquatic varieties shall be examined and approved by the National Aquatic Variety Approval Committee, and shall be promoted after being announced by the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration of the State Council. The measures for the approval of new aquatic varieties shall be formulated by the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration of the State Council.  
Entities engaging in the production of aquatic fry and fingerlings shall obtain a production license for aquatic fry and fingerlings issued by the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration of the local people's government at or above the county level, except where fishery producers cultivate and use aquatic fry and fingerlings for their own purposes.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　销售或者运输水产苗种，应当依照有关动植物检疫的法律、行政法规的规定实施检疫。

**Article 23** The sale or transportation of aquatic fry and fingerlings shall undergo quarantine in accordance with the provisions of the laws and administrative regulations on animal and plant quarantine.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　水产苗种的进口、出口，应当依照有关进出境动植物检疫的法律、行政法规的规定实施检疫，防止病害传入境内和传出境外，加强生态安全风险防控。  
引进转基因水产苗种，应当按照国务院有关规定进行安全性评价。

**Article 24** The import and export of aquatic fry and fingerlings shall undergo quarantine in accordance with the provisions of the laws and administrative regulations on the quarantine of animals and plants entering and leaving the country, to prevent the introduction of diseases into and their spread out of the country, and to strengthen the prevention and control of ecological security risks.  
The introduction of genetically modified aquatic fry and fingerlings shall be subject to safety assessment in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上人民政府渔业渔政主管部门应当加强对养殖生产的技术指导和病害防治工作。

**Article 25** The competent departments of fisheries and fishery administration of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen technical guidance for aquaculture production and the prevention and control of diseases.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　从事养殖生产、运输、销售等活动，应当遵守本法和有关农产品质量安全、食品安全的法律、法规的规定，不得使用国家禁止使用的饵料、饲料、饲料添加剂、药物或者其他有毒有害物质，保障水产品质量安全。

**Article 26** Entities engaging in activities such as aquaculture production, transportation, and sale shall comply with the provisions of this Law and the laws and regulations on the quality and safety of agricultural products and food safety, and shall not use bait, feed, feed additives, drugs, or other toxic or harmful substances prohibited by the State, so as to ensure the quality and safety of aquatic products.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　从事养殖生产应当保护水域、滩涂生态环境，科学确定养殖密度、规模等，合理投饵、投饲、使用药物，养殖排放尾水应当符合有关污染物排放标准，不得造成水域、滩涂的环境污染、生态破坏。

**Article 27** Entities engaging in aquaculture production shall protect the ecological environment of waters and tidal flats, scientifically determine stocking density, scale, etc., reasonably apply bait, feed, and drugs, and the discharged tail water from aquaculture shall comply with the relevant pollutant discharge standards, and shall not cause environmental pollution or ecological damage to waters or tidal flats.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　水产苗种生产者、从事水产养殖的单位应当按照国务院渔业渔政主管部门的规定，如实记录水产苗种亲本引种和培育、苗种繁育生产、疫病防控、用药、投入品使用、产品销售等信息，并至少保存二年。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/农业农村/中华人民共和国渔业法实施细则.md#art-29" title="被《中华人民共和国渔业法实施细则》第29条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/农业农村/中华人民共和国渔业法实施细则.md#art-34" title="被《中华人民共和国渔业法实施细则》第34条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 28** Producers of aquatic fry and fingerlings and entities engaged in aquaculture shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration of the State Council, truthfully record information on the introduction and cultivation of parent stocks, fry and fingerling breeding production, disease prevention and control, drug use, input use, and product sales, and shall retain such records for at least two years.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　养殖水生外来种、杂交种，应当向县级以上地方人民政府渔业渔政主管部门备案，制定应急处置预案，采取必要的防护措施，防止物种逃逸造成生态安全风险。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/农业农村/中华人民共和国渔业法实施细则.md#art-30" title="被《中华人民共和国渔业法实施细则》第30条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 29** Entities that cultivate aquatic alien species or hybrid species shall file records with the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration of the local people's government at or above the county level, formulate emergency response plans, and take necessary protective measures to prevent species escape that may cause ecological security risks.

### 第三章　捕捞业

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家根据捕捞量低于渔业资源增长量的原则，确定渔业资源的总可捕捞量，实行捕捞限额制度。国务院渔业渔政主管部门依法组织渔业资源的调查和评估，为实行捕捞限额制度提供科学依据。  
中华人民共和国内海、领海、专属经济区和其他管辖海域的捕捞限额总量由国务院渔业渔政主管部门确定，报国务院批准后逐级分解下达；国家确定的重要江河、湖泊的捕捞限额总量由有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定或者协商确定，逐级分解下达，并向国务院渔业渔政主管部门报告。捕捞限额总量的分配应当体现公平、公正的原则，分配办法和分配结果应当向社会公开，并接受监督。  
省级以上人民政府渔业渔政主管部门应当加强对捕捞限额制度实施情况的监督检查，对超过上级下达的捕捞限额指标的，应当在其次年捕捞限额指标中予以核减。  
特殊种类渔业资源的捕捞限额管理，按照国务院渔业渔政主管部门的有关规定执行。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/农业农村/中华人民共和国渔业法实施细则.md#art-31" title="被《中华人民共和国渔业法实施细则》第31条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 30** The State shall, based on the principle that the catch volume shall be lower than the growth volume of fishery resources, determine the total allowable catch of fishery resources and implement a fishing quota system. The competent department of fisheries and fishery administration of the State Council shall, according to law, organize the survey and assessment of fishery resources to provide a scientific basis for the implementation of the fishing quota system.  
The total fishing quota for the internal waters, territorial sea, exclusive economic zone, and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China shall be determined by the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration of the State Council and, after being submitted to the State Council for approval, shall be decomposed and assigned level by level; the total fishing quota for major rivers and lakes designated by the State shall be determined or determined through consultation by the people's governments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, and shall be decomposed and assigned level by level, and reported to the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration of the State Council. The distribution of the total fishing quota shall reflect the principles of fairness and impartiality, and the distribution methods and results shall be disclosed to the public and subject to supervision.  
The competent departments of fisheries and fishery administration of the people's governments at or above the provincial level shall strengthen supervision and inspection of the implementation of the fishing quota system, and where the quota assigned by the higher authority is exceeded, the excess shall be deducted from the following year's fishing quota.  
The management of fishing quotas for special types of fishery resources shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration of the State Council.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家根据捕捞能力与渔业资源可捕捞量相适应的原则，确定渔业船网工具控制指标。  
县级以上人民政府渔业渔政主管部门在渔业船网工具控制指标范围内，按照规定的权限分级分类批准渔业船网工具指标。  
对应当取得渔业船网工具指标的渔业船舶，单位和个人取得渔业船网工具指标后方可制造、改造并申请检验、登记。  
禁止为应当取得而未取得渔业船网工具指标的单位和个人制造、改造渔业船舶，或者不按照渔业船网工具指标核定的内容制造、改造渔业船舶。  
渔业船网工具指标可以按照国家有关规定随船转让。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/农业农村/中华人民共和国渔业法实施细则.md#art-32" title="被《中华人民共和国渔业法实施细则》第32条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 31** The State shall, based on the principle that the fishing capacity shall be compatible with the allowable catch of fishery resources, determine the control targets for fishing vessel and gear quotas.  
The competent departments of fisheries and fishery administration of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, within the scope of the control targets for fishing vessel and gear quotas, approve the targets for fishing vessel and gear quotas by category and at different levels according to the prescribed authority.  
For fishing vessels that shall obtain fishing vessel and gear quota targets, units and individuals may only manufacture, modify, and apply for inspection and registration after obtaining such targets.  
It shall be prohibited to manufacture or modify fishing vessels for units or individuals that are required to obtain but have not obtained fishing vessel and gear quota targets, or to manufacture or modify fishing vessels not in accordance with the approved content of the fishing vessel and gear quota targets.  
Fishing vessel and gear quota targets may be transferred together with the vessel in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家对捕捞业实行捕捞许可证制度。捕捞许可证由县级以上人民政府渔业渔政主管部门批准发放。  
到国务院渔业渔政主管部门规定的渔业资源渔场从事捕捞作业的，应当取得省级以上人民政府渔业渔政主管部门批准发放的捕捞许可证。  
到公海从事捕捞作业的，应当取得国务院渔业渔政主管部门批准发放的捕捞许可证，遵守中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的有关条约、协定。  
到他国管辖海域从事捕捞作业的，应当经国务院渔业渔政主管部门批准，遵守中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的有关条约、协定以及有关国家的法律。  
捕捞许可证不得买卖、出租、出借或者以其他形式转让，不得涂改、伪造、变造。  
个人通过娱乐性垂钓或者手工采集的方式零星获得水产品的，不属于捕捞作业，不需要申请捕捞许可证；县级以上地方人民政府渔业渔政主管部门应当加强管理，防止破坏渔业资源。

**Article 32** The State shall implement a fishing license system for the fishing industry. Fishing licenses shall be approved and issued by the competent departments of fisheries and fishery administration of the people's governments at or above the county level.  
Those engaging in fishing operations in fishery grounds specified by the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration of the State Council shall obtain fishing licenses approved and issued by the competent departments of fisheries and fishery administration of the people's governments at or above the provincial level.  
Those engaging in fishing operations on the high seas shall obtain fishing licenses approved and issued by the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration of the State Council, and shall comply with the relevant treaties and agreements concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China.  
Those engaging in fishing operations in sea areas under the jurisdiction of other countries shall be subject to approval by the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration of the State Council, and shall comply with the relevant treaties and agreements concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China and the laws of the relevant countries.  
Fishing licenses shall not be bought, sold, rented, lent, or transferred in any other form, and shall not be altered, forged, or counterfeited.  
Individuals who sporadically obtain aquatic products through recreational angling or manual collection shall not be regarded as engaging in fishing operations and are not required to apply for fishing licenses; the competent departments of fisheries and fishery administration of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen management to prevent damage to fishery resources.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　取得捕捞许可证，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有渔业船舶所有权登记证书、国籍证书和检验证书，不使用船舶从事捕捞作业的除外；  
（二）渔具、捕捞方法符合有关规定；  
（三）国务院渔业渔政主管部门规定的其他条件。  
县级以上地方人民政府渔业渔政主管部门批准发放的捕捞许可证，应当与上级人民政府渔业渔政主管部门下达的捕捞限额指标相适应。

**Article 33** To obtain a fishing license, the following conditions shall be satisfied:  
(1) Having the certificate of ownership registration, nationality certificate, and inspection certificate for the fishing vessel, except where fishing operations are conducted without using a vessel;  
(2) The fishing gear and fishing methods comply with the relevant provisions; and  
(3) Other conditions prescribed by the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration of the State Council.  
The fishing licenses approved and issued by the competent departments of fisheries and fishery administration of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be compatible with the fishing quota targets assigned by the competent departments of fisheries and fishery administration of the higher-level people's governments.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　从事捕捞作业的单位和个人，应当按照捕捞许可证规定的作业类型、场所、时限、渔具数量和捕捞限额等进行作业。  
海洋大中型渔业船舶应当如实填写作业日志，记载捕捞作业、转载、购销等信息，并至少保存二年。  
辅助捕捞作业的船舶，不得从事捕捞作业；携带渔具航行、停泊的，应当进行捆扎、覆盖。

**Article 34** Units and individuals engaging in fishing operations shall conduct operations in accordance with the type of operation, location, time limit, quantity of fishing gear, and fishing quota specified in the fishing license.  
Large and medium-sized marine fishing vessels shall truthfully fill in operation logs, recording information on fishing operations, transshipment, purchase and sale, etc., and shall retain such logs for at least two years.  
Vessels auxiliary to fishing operations shall not engage in fishing operations; where they carry fishing gear while navigating or mooring, such gear shall be tied up and covered.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　渔业船舶应当符合适航要求，按照有关规定安装安全通信导航、船位监测、消防、救生、污染防治等设施设备并确保处于良好运行状态，足额配备符合条件的渔业船员，依法办理安全生产责任保险。  
渔业船舶不得超越船舶检验证书核定的航区航行和作业。任何单位和个人不得篡改、隐瞒、销毁渔业船舶安全通信导航信息数据。  
渔业船舶应当按照有关规定进行标识。任何单位和个人不得擅自涂改、遮盖、损毁、伪造、变造、冒用、借用渔业船舶标识。  
国家推广使用渔业船舶标准化船型，提高渔业船舶安全运行水平。

**Article 35** Fishing vessels shall meet seaworthiness requirements, install safety communication and navigation, vessel position monitoring, fire-fighting, life-saving, pollution prevention and control, and other facilities and equipment in accordance with the relevant provisions, and ensure that they are in good operating condition, fully equip with qualified fishery crew members, and handle work safety liability insurance according to law.  
Fishing vessels shall not navigate or operate beyond the navigation area approved in the vessel inspection certificate. No unit or individual may tamper with, conceal, or destroy the safety communication and navigation information data of fishing vessels.  
Fishing vessels shall be marked in accordance with the relevant provisions. No unit or individual may, without authorization, alter, cover, damage, forge, counterfeit, fraudulently use, or borrow the markings of fishing vessels.  
The State promotes the use of standardized fishing vessel designs to improve the safe operation level of fishing vessels.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　制造、改造、购置、进口的渔业船舶应当经船舶检验机构检验合格后，方可下水、作业。具体管理办法由国务院制定。

**Article 36** Fishing vessels that are manufactured, modified, purchased, or imported shall be subject to inspection by a vessel inspection authority and found to be qualified before they may be launched into water or put into operation. The specific management measures shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　禁止无船名船号、无渔业船舶证书、无船籍港的船舶从事捕捞作业。  
对前款规定的船舶，任何单位和个人不得为其提供供油、供水、供冰等服务，不得代冻、转载、运输、收购、加工、销售其非法捕捞的渔获物。

**Article 37** It shall be prohibited for vessels without a vessel name and number, without a fishing vessel certificate, or without a port of registry to engage in fishing operations.  
For the vessels specified in the preceding paragraph, no unit or individual may provide them with services such as fuel supply, water supply, or ice supply, nor may they freeze, transship, transport, purchase, process, or sell the illegally caught fish from such vessels.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　渔港建设应当符合国家统一规划，实行谁投资谁受益的原则。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当对位于本行政区域内的渔港加强监督管理，维护渔港的正常秩序。  
改变渔港性质、功能和范围的，应当征求有关渔业渔政主管部门的意见。  
渔港所有者、经营者应当加强渔港建设和维护，遵守渔港管理章程，做好安全生产、生态环境保护等工作，提供停泊补给、应急避险等渔港服务。

**Article 38** The construction of fishing ports shall conform to the unified national plan and implement the principle that the investor benefits.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and management of fishing ports located within their administrative areas and maintain the normal order of fishing ports.  
Any change in the nature, function, or scope of a fishing port shall be subject to consultation with the relevant competent department of fisheries and fishery administration.  
The owners and operators of fishing ports shall strengthen the construction and maintenance of fishing ports, comply with the fishing port management regulations, carry out work on safety production and ecological environment protection, and provide fishing port services such as berthing, supplies, and emergency shelter.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家采取措施鼓励、扶持远洋渔业的可持续发展，加强与周边邻国渔业协作，共同养护渔业资源。

**Article 39** The State shall adopt measures to encourage and support the sustainable development of distant-water fisheries, strengthen fishery cooperation with neighboring countries, and jointly conserve fishery resources.

### 第四章　渔业资源保护

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家加强重要渔业水域保护。海洋重要渔业水域由国务院渔业渔政主管部门征求国务院交通运输、自然资源、生态环境等部门意见后划定并公布；其他重要渔业水域由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业渔政主管部门征求同级水行政、交通运输、自然资源、生态环境等部门意见后划定并公布。  
编制规划涉及重要渔业水域的，在开展生态环境影响评价时，应当将对渔业资源的影响作为专题内容，并征求同级渔业渔政主管部门意见。

**Article 40** The State strengthens the protection of important fishery waters. Marine important fishery waters shall be delineated and announced by the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration of the State Council after consulting the departments of transport, natural resources, and ecological environment under the State Council; other important fishery waters shall be delineated and announced by the competent departments of fisheries and fishery administration of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government after consulting the departments of water administration, transport, natural resources, and ecological environment at the same level.  
Where planning involving important fishery waters is prepared, when conducting an ecological environment impact assessment, the impact on fishery resources shall be taken as a specific topic, and the opinions of the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration at the same level shall be sought.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家保护水产种质资源及其生存环境，并在具有较高经济价值和遗传育种价值的水产种质资源的主要生长繁育区域建立水产种质资源保护区。  
未经省级以上人民政府渔业渔政主管部门批准，任何单位和个人不得在水产种质资源保护区内从事捕捞活动。  
禁止非法占用或者破坏水产种质资源保护区。  
禁止在水产种质资源保护区内新建、扩建排污口。

**Article 41** The State shall protect aquatic germplasm resources and their living environment, and shall establish aquatic germplasm resources protection zones in the main growth and breeding areas of aquatic germplasm resources with high economic value and genetic breeding value.  
Without approval from the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration under the people's government at or above the provincial level, no unit or individual shall engage in fishing activities within an aquatic germplasm resources protection zone.  
It is prohibited to illegally occupy or destroy aquatic germplasm resources protection zones.  
It is prohibited to construct or expand sewage outlets within aquatic germplasm resources protection zones.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家加强对水产种质资源的调查、收集、整理、鉴定、登记、保存，建立水产种质资源库。  
国家对水产种质资源享有主权。利用我国水产种质资源开展国际科学研究合作，应当依法取得批准，保证中方单位及其研究人员全过程、实质性地参与研究，依法分享相关权益。  
水产种质资源的进口、出口由省级以上人民政府渔业渔政主管部门审批。对首次进口的水产种质资源，国务院渔业渔政主管部门应当组织进行生物安全风险评估，有效防范和化解生物安全风险。  
国务院渔业渔政主管部门制定并公布水产种质资源保护名录。

**Article 42** The State shall strengthen the investigation, collection, sorting, expert evaluation, registration, and preservation of aquatic germplasm resources, and shall establish an aquatic germplasm resources bank.  
The State enjoys sovereignty over aquatic germplasm resources. The use of China's aquatic germplasm resources for international scientific research cooperation shall be approved according to law, and shall ensure that Chinese entities and their researchers participate in the research throughout the entire process and substantively, and share relevant rights and interests according to law.  
The import and export of aquatic germplasm resources shall be subject to approval by the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration under the people's government at or above the provincial level. For aquatic germplasm resources imported for the first time, the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration under the State Council shall organize a biological safety risk assessment to effectively prevent and resolve biological safety risks.  
The competent department of fisheries and fishery administration under the State Council shall formulate and publish a list of protected aquatic germplasm resources.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　县级以上人民政府渔业渔政主管部门应当因地制宜采取增殖放流、建设海洋牧场、投放人工鱼礁、种植海藻场或者海草床等措施，增殖渔业资源，修复渔业水域生态环境。  
县级以上人民政府渔业渔政主管部门可以向受益的单位和个人征收渔业资源增殖保护费。渔业资源增殖保护费的征收、使用，按照国务院财政、发展改革主管部门有关规定执行。

**Article 43** The competent department of fisheries and fishery administration under the people's government at or above the county level shall, according to local conditions, adopt measures such as stocking and releasing, building marine ranches, deploying artificial reefs, planting seaweed fields or seagrass beds to increase fishery resources and restore the ecological environment of fishery waters.  
The competent department of fisheries and fishery administration under the people's government at or above the county level may levy a fishery resources增殖保护 fee from the benefiting units and individuals. The levy and use of the fishery resources增殖保护 fee shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the competent departments of finance and development and reform under the State Council.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　省级以上人民政府渔业渔政主管部门根据渔业资源状况、水生生物繁殖生长规律以及重点水生生物物种、水产种质资源保护等方面的需要，科学论证、设立并公布禁渔区、禁渔期，明确禁渔的区域范围、起止时间和禁止作业类型等，并加强宣传教育。  
禁止违反禁渔区、禁渔期的规定从事捕捞活动，禁止违规垂钓。禁止渔业船舶违反禁渔区、禁渔期的规定航行、停泊。  
对违反前款规定的渔业船舶，任何单位和个人不得为其提供供油、供水、供冰等服务，不得代冻、转载、运输、收购、加工、销售其非法捕捞的渔获物。

**Article 44** The competent department of fisheries and fishery administration under the people's government at or above the provincial level shall, based on the status of fishery resources, the reproduction and growth patterns of aquatic organisms, and the needs for the protection of key aquatic organism species and aquatic germplasm resources, scientifically demonstrate, establish and publish no-fishing zones and closed fishing seasons, specifying the scope of the zones, start and end dates, and prohibited types of operations, and shall strengthen publicity and education.  
It is prohibited to engage in fishing activities in violation of the provisions on no-fishing zones and closed fishing seasons, and it is prohibited to fish illegally. It is prohibited for fishing vessels to navigate or berth in violation of the provisions on no-fishing zones and closed fishing seasons.  
No unit or individual shall provide services such as refueling, water supply, or ice supply to any fishing vessel that violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph, and shall not freeze, transship, transport, purchase, process, or sell the illegally caught catches thereof.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　禁止使用炸鱼、毒鱼、电鱼等破坏渔业资源和渔业水域生态环境的方法进行捕捞。禁止携带炸鱼、毒鱼、电鱼等破坏渔业资源和渔业水域生态环境的装置、器具等进入渔业水域。  
禁止制造、销售、使用禁用的渔具。禁止使用小于最小网目尺寸的网具进行捕捞。国家鼓励、支持使用推荐渔具目录规定的渔具从事捕捞作业。  
禁止使用的捕捞方法、禁用渔具目录、最小网目尺寸和推荐渔具目录，由国务院渔业渔政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业渔政主管部门规定，并及时调整。  
捕捞的渔获物中幼鱼不得超过规定的比例。

**Article 45** It is prohibited to use methods such as explosive fishing, toxic fishing, or electric fishing that destroy fishery resources and the ecological environment of fishery waters. It is prohibited to carry devices or instruments for explosive fishing, toxic fishing, or electric fishing that destroy fishery resources and the ecological environment of fishery waters into fishery waters.  
It is prohibited to manufacture, sell, or use prohibited fishing gear. It is prohibited to use nets with a mesh size smaller than the minimum mesh size for fishing. The State encourages and supports the use of fishing gear specified in the list of recommended fishing gear for fishing operations.  
The prohibited fishing methods, list of prohibited fishing gear, minimum mesh sizes, and list of recommended fishing gear shall be prescribed by the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration under the State Council or the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall be adjusted in a timely manner.  
The proportion of juvenile fish in the catches shall not exceed the prescribed ratio.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　禁止捕捞有重要经济价值的水生动物苗种或者禁捕的怀卵亲本；捕捞作业时误捕的，应当及时放生；因科学研究、养殖、捕捞过坝、增殖放流或者其他特殊需要确需捕捞的，应当经省级以上人民政府渔业渔政主管部门批准，在指定的区域和时间内，按照限额捕捞。  
在水生动物苗种重点产区引水用水时，应当采取措施，保护苗种。

**Article 46** It is prohibited to catch fry of aquatic animals of significant economic value or prohibited gravid broodstock; if caught accidentally during fishing operations, they shall be released immediately. If it is truly necessary to catch them for scientific research, aquaculture, fish pass fishing, stock enhancement, or other special needs, approval shall be obtained from the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration under the people's government at or above the provincial level, and the catch shall be made within a designated area and time and in accordance with the catch limit.  
When diverting or using water in key production areas of aquatic animal fry, measures shall be taken to protect the fry.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　在重要渔业水域开展建闸、筑坝、航道建设、港口建设等工程建设，其生态环境影响评价文件应当包括对渔业资源和渔业水域生态环境影响的内容；涉及水产种质资源保护区的，应当就其对渔业资源和渔业水域生态环境的影响编制专题论证报告。生态环境主管部门审查相关生态环境影响评价文件时，应当征求同级渔业渔政主管部门意见。  
建设单位应当按照经批准的生态环境影响评价文件要求，建造过鱼设施或者采取其他渔业资源保护措施。相关渔业资源保护设施应当与工程建设项目的主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投入使用。

**Article 47** For construction projects such as building sluices, dams, waterways, and ports in important fishery waters, the ecological environment impact assessment documents shall include the content regarding the impact on fishery resources and the ecological environment of fishery waters; where aquatic germplasm resources protection zones are involved, a special argumentation report on the impact on fishery resources and the ecological environment of fishery waters shall be prepared. When the competent department of ecological environment reviews the relevant ecological environment impact assessment documents, it shall seek the opinions of the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration at the same level.  
The construction unit shall, in accordance with the requirements of the approved ecological environment impact assessment documents, construct fish passage facilities or adopt other fishery resources protection measures. The relevant fishery resources protection facilities shall be designed, constructed, and put into use simultaneously with the main project of the construction project.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　对用于渔业并兼有调蓄、灌溉等功能的水体，水行政、渔业渔政等部门应当确定渔业资源保护和渔业生产所需的最低水位线。  
水工程的规划、建设、运行、拆除等应当加强渔业资源保护。水工程管理单位应当合理制定调度规程，在确保防洪、供水安全的前提下，开展符合水生生物生长繁育需要的生态调度。

**Article 48** For water bodies used for fisheries and also having functions such as regulation, storage, and irrigation, the water administrative department and the fisheries and fishery administration department shall determine the minimum water level line required for fishery resources protection and fishery production.  
The planning, construction, operation, and demolition of water projects shall strengthen fishery resources protection. The water project management unit shall reasonably formulate scheduling rules, and on the premise of ensuring flood control and water supply safety, shall carry out ecological scheduling that meets the needs of aquatic organism growth and reproduction.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　禁止围湖造田。重要的水产苗种基地和养殖场所不得围垦。

**Article 49** It is prohibited to reclaim land from lakes. Important aquatic fry bases and aquaculture sites shall not be reclaimed.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　进行水下爆破、勘探、采砂、施工等作业，对渔业资源有严重影响的，作业单位应当事先同县级以上地方人民政府渔业渔政主管部门协商，采取措施，防止或者减少对渔业资源的损害；造成渔业资源损失的，依法给予赔偿。

**Article 50** Where operations such as underwater blasting, exploration, sand mining, or construction have a serious impact on fishery resources, the operating unit shall consult in advance with the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration under the local people's government at or above the county level, and shall take measures to prevent or reduce damage to fishery resources; if fishery resources losses are caused, compensation shall be made according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　各级人民政府应当采取措施，保护和改善渔业水域生态环境，防治污染。  
禁止向开放水域投放水生外来种、杂交种等不符合生态要求的水生生物。  
渔业水域生态环境的监督管理和渔业污染事故的调查处理，依照有关生态环境保护的法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 51** People's governments at all levels shall take measures to protect and improve the ecological environment of fishery waters and to prevent and control pollution.  
It is prohibited to release aquatic alien species, hybrid species, or other aquatic organisms that do not meet ecological requirements into open waters.  
The supervision and management of the ecological environment of fishery waters and the investigation and handling of fishery pollution accidents shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations on ecological environment protection.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家对珍贵、濒危水生野生动物实行重点保护，防止其灭绝。禁止捕杀、伤害国家重点保护的水生野生动物；因科学研究、种群调控、疫源疫病监测或者其他特殊需要确需捕捞的，依照有关野生动物保护等法律、行政法规的规定执行。  
捕捞活动中误捕珍贵、濒危水生野生动物的，应当及时采取救护、处置措施，并报告渔业渔政主管部门。

**Article 52** The State shall provide key protection for precious and endangered aquatic wild animals to prevent their extinction. It is prohibited to kill or harm aquatic wild animals under key State protection; if it is truly necessary to catch them for scientific research, population regulation, epidemic source and disease monitoring, or other special needs, the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations on wildlife protection shall be followed.  
If precious or endangered aquatic wild animals are accidentally caught during fishing activities, timely rescue and disposal measures shall be taken, and the competent department of fisheries and fishery administration shall be reported.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　渔业执法机构应当加强对渔业工作的监督检查，及时查处违法行为。  
渔业执法机构依法履行监督检查职责，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）进入渔业生产以及相关活动场所、设施，实施现场检查；  
（二）向有关单位和个人询问情况，查阅、复制有关证书、文件、档案、记录、电子数据等相关资料；  
（三）责令从事渔业生产以及相关活动的船舶停航，实施登临检查或者调查；  
（四）责令涉嫌违法从事渔业生产以及相关活动的船舶禁止离港或者开往指定地点；  
（五）查封涉嫌违法从事渔业生产以及相关活动的场所，查封、扣押涉嫌违法从事渔业生产以及相关活动的船舶、车辆和有关渔具、养殖设施、投入品、水生生物及其制品等。对查封、扣押的鲜活、易腐水生生物及其制品，先行依法拍卖、变卖或者采取其他合理方式处置。  
有关单位和个人应当配合渔业执法机构依法履行监督检查职责。

**Article 53** The fishery law enforcement agencies shall strengthen supervision and inspection of fishery work, and shall investigate and deal with illegal acts in a timely manner.  
When performing supervision and inspection duties according to law, the fishery law enforcement agencies may take the following measures:  
(1) Enter the sites and facilities of fishery production and related activities to conduct on-site inspections;  
(2) Inquire about the circumstances of relevant units and individuals, and examine and copy relevant certificates, documents, archives, records, electronic data, and other related materials;  
(3) Order vessels engaged in fishery production and related activities to stop navigation, and carry out boarding inspections or investigations;  
(4) Order vessels suspected of engaging in illegal fishery production and related activities to be prohibited from departing the port or to proceed to a designated location;  
(5) Seal up the sites suspected of engaging in illegal fishery production and related activities, seal up and detain vessels, vehicles, and relevant fishing gear, aquaculture facilities, inputs, aquatic organisms and products thereof suspected of being used in illegal fishery production and related activities. For live or perishable aquatic organisms and products thereof that have been sealed up or detained, they shall be auctioned, sold, or disposed of by other reasonable means according to law in advance.  
Relevant units and individuals shall cooperate with the fishery law enforcement agencies in performing their supervision and inspection duties according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　机动渔船底拖网禁渔区线外侧海域和特定渔业资源渔场的渔业生产作业等活动，由海警机构依照《中华人民共和国海警法》的规定实施监督检查。海警机构依法实施监督检查，可以采取[本法第五十三条](#art-53)规定的措施。

**Article 54** The fishery production operations and other activities in the waters outside the bottom trawl no-fishing zone line for motorized fishing vessels and in specific fishery resource fishing grounds shall be subject to supervision and inspection by the coast guard agencies in accordance with the provisions of the Coast Guard Law of the People's Republic of China. When performing supervision and inspection according to law, the coast guard agencies may take the measures provided for in Article 53 of this Law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　渔业执法人员实施监督检查，应当遵守执法规范，不得少于二人，并按照规定着制式服装，出示执法证件。  
渔业执法人员使用标示有专用标志的执法船舶的，即为表明身份。

**Article 55** Fishery law enforcement personnel conducting supervision and inspection shall comply with law enforcement norms, shall be no fewer than two persons, and shall wear prescribed uniforms and present law enforcement credentials.  
If fishery law enforcement personnel use law enforcement vessels marked with special signs, that shall constitute identification.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　船舶进出渔港，应当按照有关规定报告并服从调度。  
进港停泊的远洋渔业船舶、外国籍渔业船舶以及装载渔获物的相关船舶应当停靠在指定的泊位，并向港口所在地渔业执法机构报告。国家鼓励其他渔业船舶在指定港口靠泊卸载渔获物。  
国家鼓励实行渔获物可追溯管理。

**Article 56** Vessels entering or leaving a fishing port shall report and comply with scheduling in accordance with relevant regulations.  
Distant-water fishing vessels, foreign fishing vessels, and relevant vessels loaded with catches that berth in port shall moor at designated berths and report to the fishery law enforcement agency at the port location. The State encourages other fishing vessels to berth at designated ports to unload catches.  
The State encourages the implementation of traceability management of catches.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　远洋渔业船舶应当从国家对外开放口岸、国务院或者国务院授权的部门批准的口岸出境入境，并按照有关规定接受口岸检疫、检查。

**Article 57** Distant-water fishing vessels shall exit and enter the country through the State's open ports or ports approved by the State Council or a department authorized by the State Council, and shall undergo port quarantine and inspection in accordance with relevant regulations.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　渔业执法机构依照有关法律、行政法规以及中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的条约、协定的规定，对外国籍渔业船舶实施港口国、沿岸国监督检查。

**Article 58** Fishery law enforcement agencies shall, in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of treaties or agreements concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, conduct port State and coastal State supervision and inspection of foreign fishing vessels.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　外国籍渔业船舶以及装载渔获物的相关船舶到中华人民共和国港口装卸渔获物，应当经有关港口主管部门批准，并到国家对外开放口岸、国务院或者国务院授权的部门批准的港口停泊，按照有关规定接受口岸检疫、检查。  
依照中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的条约、协定的规定，被认定为从事非法捕捞活动的外国籍渔业船舶，不得进入中华人民共和国港口。

**Article 59** Foreign fishing vessels and relevant vessels loaded with catches that come to ports of the People's Republic of China to load or unload catches shall obtain approval from the relevant port authority, and shall moor at the State's open ports or ports approved by the State Council or a department authorized by the State Council, and shall undergo port quarantine and inspection in accordance with relevant regulations.  
Foreign fishing vessels that are identified as engaging in illegal fishing activities in accordance with the provisions of treaties or agreements concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China shall not enter the ports of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国务院渔业渔政主管部门应当会同有关部门加强渔业信用体系建设，按照国家有关规定建立信用记录制度。

**Article 60** The competent department of fisheries and fishery administration under the State Council shall, together with relevant departments, strengthen the construction of the fishery credit system, and shall establish a credit recording system in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　渔业执法机构及其工作人员不得参与渔业生产经营活动。

**Article 61** A fishery law enforcement agency and its staff shall not participate in fishery production or business operations.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　县级以上人民政府渔业渔政主管部门与交通运输主管部门应当按照职责分工，加强协作，共同防范渔业船舶和商船碰撞事故发生，保障相关人员生命财产安全。

**Article 62** The competent departments of fishery and fisheries administration of the people's governments at or above the county level and the competent departments of transportation shall, according to their respective functions and duties, strengthen cooperation, jointly prevent collisions between fishing vessels and merchant vessels, and ensure the safety of life and property of relevant persons.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　渔业执法机构、海警机构与有关部门、机构根据实际需要建立协同执法机制，加强信息共享和执法配合。

**Article 63** Fishery law enforcement agencies, maritime police agencies, and relevant departments and agencies shall establish a coordinated law enforcement mechanism based on actual needs, and strengthen information sharing and law enforcement cooperation.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　任何单位和个人对违反本法的行为，有权向渔业执法机构或者海警机构举报。接到举报的机构应当及时依法处理。

**Article 64** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report any violation of this Law to a fishery law enforcement agency or a maritime police agency. An agency that receives such a report shall handle it promptly according to law.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　未依法取得养殖证擅自在国家所有的水域、滩涂从事养殖生产的，责令限期改正，补办养殖证或者拆除养殖设施并恢复原状；逾期不改正的，依法强制拆除、代为恢复原状，所需费用由违法者承担。  
使用国家所有的水域、滩涂从事养殖生产，无正当理由使水域、滩涂荒芜满一年的，由发放养殖证的机关责令限期开发利用；逾期未开发利用的，吊销养殖证，可以并处五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 65** Where a person engages in aquaculture production in waters or tidal flats owned by the State without having obtained an aquaculture license according to law, such person shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit, and to obtain an aquaculture license retroactively or dismantle the aquaculture facilities and restore the original state; if the person fails to make corrections within the time limit, the facilities shall be forcibly dismantled or restored to the original state on the person's behalf according to law, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator.  
Where a person uses waters or tidal flats owned by the State for aquaculture production and, without justifiable reasons, leaves such waters or tidal flats uncultivated for a full year, the authority that issued the aquaculture license shall order such person to develop and utilize them within a specified time limit; if the person fails to develop and utilize them within the time limit, the aquaculture license shall be revoked, and a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may also be imposed.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　未依法取得水产苗种生产许可证从事水产苗种生产活动或者生产、经营未经审定的水产新品种苗种的，责令停止违法行为，没收水产苗种和违法所得，并处水产苗种价值一倍以上十倍以下的罚款。  
水产苗种生产者、从事水产养殖的单位未按照规定如实记录并保存相关信息的，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处二千元以上二万元以下的罚款。

**Article 66** Where a person engages in the production of aquatic fry or fingerlings without having obtained a production license for aquatic fry and fingerlings according to law, or produces or deals in fry or fingerlings of new aquatic species that have not been approved, such person shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, and the aquatic fry and fingerlings and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the value of the aquatic fry and fingerlings shall also be imposed.  
Where a producer of aquatic fry and fingerlings or an entity engaged in aquaculture fails to truthfully record and preserve relevant information as prescribed, such person or entity shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit; if the person or entity fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　养殖水生外来种、杂交种，未向县级以上地方人民政府渔业渔政主管部门备案或者未制定应急处置预案的，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处五万元以下的罚款。  
养殖水生外来种、杂交种，未采取必要的防护措施，致使物种逃逸造成生态安全风险的，责令限期采取补救措施，处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款；造成严重后果的，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。逾期未采取补救措施的，由渔业执法机构代为采取相关措施，所需费用由违法者承担。

**Article 67** Where a person cultivates alien aquatic species or hybrid aquatic species without filing a record with the competent department of fishery and fisheries administration of the local people's government at or above the county level or without formulating an emergency response plan, such person shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit; if the person fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where a person cultivates alien aquatic species or hybrid aquatic species and fails to take necessary protective measures, thereby causing the escape of such species and creating ecological safety risks, such person shall be ordered to take remedial measures within a specified time limit, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed. If the person fails to take remedial measures within the time limit, the fishery law enforcement agency shall take relevant measures on the person's behalf, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　制造、改造应当取得而未取得渔业船网工具指标的渔业船舶的，责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，没收制造、改造的渔业船舶及其设备、部件和材料；情节严重的，并处船舶价值二倍以下的罚款。对委托制造、改造者，处船舶价值一倍以下的罚款。  
未按照渔业船网工具指标核定的内容制造、改造渔业船舶的，责令停止违法行为，限期改正，没收违法所得；逾期不改正的，没收制造、改造的渔业船舶及其设备、部件和材料，并处船舶价值一倍以下的罚款。对委托制造、改造者，处船舶价值百分之五十以下的罚款。

**Article 68** Where a person manufactures or modifies a fishing vessel that should have been but has not obtained a fishing vessel and fishing gear quota, such person shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, and the illegal gains, as well as the manufactured or modified fishing vessel and its equipment, parts and materials, shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not more than two times the value of the vessel shall also be imposed. With respect to the person who commissions the manufacture or modification, a fine of not more than one time the value of the vessel shall be imposed.  
Where a person manufactures or modifies a fishing vessel not in accordance with the contents verified in the fishing vessel and fishing gear quota, such person shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, make corrections within a specified time limit, and the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the person fails to make corrections within the time limit, the manufactured or modified fishing vessel and its equipment, parts and materials shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than one time the value of the vessel shall also be imposed. With respect to the person who commissions the manufacture or modification, a fine of not more than 50% of the value of the vessel shall be imposed.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　未依法取得捕捞许可证擅自进行捕捞的，没收渔获物和违法所得，并处二十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款，可以没收渔具、船舶。  
违反捕捞许可证关于作业类型、场所、时限、渔具数量和捕捞限额等规定进行捕捞的，没收渔获物和违法所得，并处十万元以下的罚款，可以暂扣捕捞许可证六个月以下；情节严重的，并处十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款，可以没收渔具、吊销捕捞许可证。

**Article 69** Where a person engages in fishing without having obtained a fishing license according to law, the catch and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2 million yuan shall also be imposed, and the fishing gear and vessel may be confiscated.  
Where a person engages in fishing in violation of the provisions of the fishing license on the type of operation, location, time limit, number of fishing gears, and catch quota, the catch and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed, and the fishing license may be temporarily suspended for not more than six months; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1 million yuan shall also be imposed, and the fishing gear may be confiscated and the fishing license may be revoked.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　涂改、伪造、变造、买卖、出租、出借或者以其他形式转让捕捞许可证的，没收违法所得，吊销捕捞许可证，并处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 70** Where a person alters, forges, misrepresents, buys, sells, leases, lends, or transfers a fishing license in any other form, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, the fishing license shall be revoked, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　违反[本法第三十二条](#art-32)第四款规定，未经国务院渔业渔政主管部门批准从事捕捞作业的，予以批评教育，没收渔获物和违法所得；情节严重的，并处二百万元以下的罚款，可以没收渔具。在他国已经受过处罚的，可以减轻或者免除处罚。

**Article 71** Where a person engages in fishing operations without the approval of the competent department of fishery and fisheries administration of the State Council, in violation of the provisions of Article 32, paragraph 4 of this Law, such person shall be given criticism and education, and the catch and illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not more than 2 million yuan shall also be imposed, and the fishing gear may be confiscated. If the person has already been penalized in another country, the penalty may be mitigated or exempted.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　无渔业船舶证书、无船籍港且无船名船号的船舶从事捕捞作业的，没收渔获物、违法所得、渔具和船舶，可以并处船舶价值二倍以下的罚款。  
渔业船舶未经检验合格下水、作业的，责令限期检验，可以处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；逾期未检验的，可以没收船舶。

**Article 72** Where a vessel without a fishing vessel certificate, without a port of registry, and without a vessel name and number engages in fishing operations, the catch, illegal gains, fishing gear and vessel shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than two times the value of the vessel may also be imposed.  
Where a fishing vessel is launched or operates without having passed inspection, such vessel shall be ordered to undergo inspection within a specified time limit, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan may be imposed; if the vessel fails to undergo inspection within the time limit, the vessel may be confiscated.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，没收渔获物、违法所得和渔具，并处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）制造、销售禁用的渔具；  
（二）明知是无渔业船舶证书、无船籍港且无船名船号从事捕捞作业的船舶，仍向其提供供油、供水、供冰等服务，或者代冻、转载、运输、收购、加工、销售其非法捕捞的渔获物；  
（三）明知是违反禁渔区、禁渔期规定的渔业船舶，仍向其提供供油、供水、供冰等服务，或者代冻、转载、运输、收购、加工、销售其非法捕捞的渔获物。

**Article 73** Where a person, in violation of the provisions of this Law, falls under any of the following circumstances, the catch, illegal gains and fishing gear shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed:  
(1) manufacturing or selling prohibited fishing gear;  
(2) providing services such as oil supply, water supply, or ice supply, or freezing, transshipping, transporting, purchasing, processing, or selling the illegally caught catch of a vessel that such person knows is engaging in fishing operations without a fishing vessel certificate, without a port of registry, and without a vessel name and number;  
(3) providing services such as oil supply, water supply, or ice supply, or freezing, transshipping, transporting, purchasing, processing, or selling the illegally caught catch of a fishing vessel that such person knows is in violation of the provisions on closed fishing areas or closed fishing seasons.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，责令改正，处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）海洋大中型渔业船舶未按照规定如实填写、保存作业日志；  
（二）渔业船舶未按照规定进行标识或者擅自涂改、遮盖、损毁、伪造、变造、冒用、借用标识；  
（三）船舶进出渔港未按照规定报告或者拒不服从调度。

**Article 74** Where a person, in violation of the provisions of this Law, falls under any of the following circumstances, such person shall be ordered to make corrections, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) a large or medium-sized marine fishing vessel fails to truthfully fill in and keep operation logs as prescribed;  
(2) a fishing vessel fails to be marked as prescribed or, without authorization, alters, covers, damages, forges, misrepresents, fraudulently uses, or borrows marks;  
(3) a vessel fails to report when entering or leaving a fishing port as prescribed or refuses to comply with dispatching.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，责令改正，处五万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）渔业船舶未按照规定安装安全通信导航、船位监测、消防、救生、污染防治等设施设备，或者相关设施设备未处于良好运行状态；  
（二）未足额配备符合条件的渔业船员；  
（三）渔业船舶超越船舶检验证书核定的航区航行或者作业；  
（四）篡改、隐瞒、销毁渔业船舶安全通信导航信息数据。

**Article 75** Where a person, in violation of the provisions of this Law, falls under any of the following circumstances, such person shall be ordered to make corrections, and a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) a fishing vessel fails to install safety communication and navigation, vessel position monitoring, fire-fighting, life-saving, pollution prevention, and other facilities and equipment as prescribed, or such facilities and equipment are not in good operating condition;  
(2) failing to have a sufficient number of qualified fishery crew members;  
(3) a fishing vessel navigates or operates beyond the navigation area verified in the vessel inspection certificate;  
(4) tampering with, concealing, or destroying safety communication and navigation information data of a fishing vessel.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　未经批准，捕捞有重要经济价值的水生动物苗种或者禁捕的怀卵亲本，或者在水产种质资源保护区内从事捕捞活动的，没收渔获物和违法所得，并处一万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款，可以没收渔具、船舶。  
未经批准进口、出口水产种质资源的，责令停止违法行为，没收水产种质资源和违法所得，并处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 76** Where a person, without approval, catches fry or fingerlings of aquatic animals of significant economic value or prohibited gravid broodstock, or engages in fishing activities within a fishery germplasm resources protection zone, the catch and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2 million yuan shall also be imposed, and the fishing gear and vessel may be confiscated.  
Where a person imports or exports fishery germplasm resources without approval, such person shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, and the fishery germplasm resources and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　使用炸鱼、毒鱼、电鱼等破坏渔业资源和渔业水域生态环境的方法进行捕捞的，没收装置、器具、有毒物质、渔获物和违法所得，并处二十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款，吊销捕捞许可证，可以没收船舶。  
携带炸鱼、毒鱼、电鱼等破坏渔业资源和渔业水域生态环境的装置、器具等进入渔业水域的，没收装置、器具等，可以处十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 77** Where a person uses methods that destroy fishery resources and the ecological environment of fishing waters, such as blasting, poisoning, or electrocuting fish, to engage in fishing, the devices, implements, toxic substances, catch and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2 million yuan shall also be imposed, the fishing license shall be revoked, and the vessel may be confiscated.  
Where a person carries devices, implements, or the like that destroy fishery resources and the ecological environment of fishing waters, such as those for blasting, poisoning, or electrocuting fish, into fishing waters, the devices and implements shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan may be imposed.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，没收渔获物、违法所得和渔具，并处二十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款，吊销捕捞许可证，可以没收船舶：  
（一）违反禁渔区、禁渔期的规定从事捕捞活动；  
（二）使用禁用的渔具、小于最小网目尺寸的网具进行捕捞；  
（三）渔获物中幼鱼超过规定比例。  
渔业船舶违反禁渔区、禁渔期的规定航行、停泊的，处二十万元以下的罚款。  
在禁渔区、禁渔期违规垂钓的，没收渔获物、违法所得和渔具，可以并处五万元以下的罚款。  
辅助捕捞作业的船舶携带渔具航行、停泊未进行捆扎、覆盖的，处五万元以下的罚款，并可以没收渔具；情节严重的，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 78** Where a person, in violation of the provisions of this Law, falls under any of the following circumstances, the catch, illegal gains and fishing gear shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2 million yuan shall also be imposed, the fishing license shall be revoked, and the vessel may be confiscated:  
(1) engaging in fishing activities in violation of the provisions on closed fishing areas or closed fishing seasons;  
(2) using prohibited fishing gear or nets with a mesh size smaller than the minimum mesh size for fishing;  
(3) the proportion of juvenile fish in the catch exceeds the prescribed limit.  
Where a fishing vessel navigates or berths in violation of the provisions on closed fishing areas or closed fishing seasons, a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where a person illegally engages in angling in a closed fishing area or closed fishing season, the catch, illegal gains and fishing gear shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may also be imposed.  
Where a vessel assisting in fishing operations navigates or berths while carrying fishing gear that is not lashed or covered, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the fishing gear may also be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　在重要渔业水域开展建闸、筑坝、航道建设、港口建设等工程建设，未按照要求开展生态环境影响评价，擅自开工建设的，依照有关生态环境保护的法律、法规的规定处理、处罚。  
在重要渔业水域开展建闸、筑坝、航道建设、港口建设等工程建设，未按照生态环境影响评价文件要求落实渔业资源保护设施以及其他渔业资源保护措施的，由渔业执法机构、生态环境主管部门和海警机构按照职责分工责令限期改正，处五万元以上一百万元以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处一百万元以上二百万元以下的罚款，并可以报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停产停业。  
违法行为逾期未改正的，渔业执法机构、生态环境主管部门和海警机构可以委托有治理能力的单位代为治理，所需费用由违法者承担。

**Article 79** Where a person carries out construction projects such as building locks, dams, waterways, or ports in important fishing waters, and commences construction without conducting an ecological environment impact assessment as required, such person shall be handled and penalized in accordance with the provisions of the laws and regulations on ecological environmental protection.  
Where a person carries out construction projects such as building locks, dams, waterways, or ports in important fishing waters, and fails to implement fishery resource protection facilities and other fishery resource protection measures as required by the ecological environment impact assessment document, the fishery law enforcement agency, the competent department of ecological environment, and the maritime police agency shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order corrections within a specified time limit, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 1 million yuan, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan on the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons; if the person refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 1 million yuan but not more than 2 million yuan shall be imposed, and the person may be ordered to suspend production or business operations upon approval by the people's government with the authority to approve.  
If the illegal act is not corrected within the time limit, the fishery law enforcement agency, the competent department of ecological environment, and the maritime police agency may entrust a unit with the capacity to undertake the remediation to carry out the remediation on behalf of the violator, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　从事养殖生产、运输、销售等活动中使用国家禁止使用的饵料、饲料、饲料添加剂、药物或者其他有毒有害物质，在水产种质资源保护区内新建、扩建排污口或者造成渔业水域生态环境破坏、渔业污染事故的，依照有关农产品质量安全、食品安全、生态环境保护等法律、行政法规的规定处理、处罚。

**Article 80** Where a person, in activities such as aquaculture production, transportation, or sale, uses baits, feed, feed additives, drugs, or other toxic or hazardous substances prohibited by the State, or constructs or expands a sewage outlet in a fishery germplasm resources protection zone, or causes damage to the ecological environment of fishing waters or a fishery pollution accident, such person shall be handled and penalized in accordance with the provisions of the laws and administrative regulations on the quality and safety of agricultural products, food safety, ecological and environmental protection, and other relevant matters.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　向开放水域投放水生外来种、杂交种等不符合生态要求的水生生物的，责令停止投放、限期采取补救措施，没收用于投放的水生生物，对投放者可以并处五万元以下的罚款；逾期未采取补救措施的，由渔业执法机构代为采取补救措施，所需费用由违法者承担。

**Article 81** Where any aquatic alien species, hybrid species or other aquatic organisms not meeting the ecological requirements are released into open waters, the release shall be ordered to cease, remedial measures shall be taken within a prescribed time limit, the aquatic organisms used for release shall be confiscated, and the releaser may be concurrently fined not more than 50,000 yuan; where remedial measures are not taken within the prescribed time limit, the fishery law enforcement agency shall take remedial measures on behalf of the violator, and the costs incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　外国人、外国籍渔业船舶违反本法规定，擅自进入中华人民共和国管辖水域从事渔业生产或者渔业资源调查活动的，没收渔获物、违法所得、渔具、渔业资源调查资料和设施设备，责令其离开或者将其驱逐；情节严重的，并处二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款；情节特别严重的，并没收船舶。  
依照中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的条约、协定的规定，被认定为从事非法捕捞活动的外国籍渔业船舶进入中华人民共和国港口的，没收渔获物、违法所得和渔具，并处二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款，责令其离开或者将其驱逐。  
在中华人民共和国专属经济区内违反本法规定，被依法采取强制措施的外国籍渔业船舶及其船员，依照中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的条约、协定提供相应担保的，有关机关可以依法解除或者变更强制措施。

**Article 82** Where any foreigner or foreign fishing vessel, in violation of this Law, enters the waters under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China without authorization to engage in fishery production or fishery resource investigation activities, the catch, illegal gains, fishing gear, fishery resource investigation materials, facilities and equipment shall be confiscated, and the violator shall be ordered to leave or be expelled; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if the circumstances are especially serious, the vessel shall also be confiscated.  
Where, in accordance with the provisions of treaties or agreements concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, a foreign fishing vessel determined to have engaged in illegal fishing activities enters a port of the People's Republic of China, the catch, illegal gains and fishing gear shall be confiscated, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the vessel shall be ordered to leave or be expelled.  
Where a foreign fishing vessel and its crew that have been subject to compulsory measures according to law for violating this Law in the exclusive economic zone of the People's Republic of China provide appropriate security in accordance with the provisions of treaties or agreements concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, the relevant authorities may, according to law, release or modify the compulsory measures.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　除本法另有规定外，本法规定的行政处罚由渔业执法机构作出决定；海警机构依法进行渔业执法的行政处罚，由海警机构作出决定，需要吊销捕捞许可证的，提请发证机关吊销。  
违法事实确凿，并有下列情形之一的，渔业执法人员可以当场作出处罚决定：  
（一）对个人处五百元以下的罚款、对单位处五千元以下的罚款；  
（二）罚款处罚决定不在水上当场作出，事后难以处罚。  
当场作出的处罚决定，应当及时报所属渔业执法机构或者海警机构备案。  
对不适用当场处罚，但事实清楚，当事人自愿认错认罚，且对违法事实和法律适用没有异议的水上案件，经当事人书面同意后，可以通过简化取证方式和审核审批手续等措施快速办理。  
有证据证明当事人在水上实施将物品倒入水中等故意毁灭证据的行为，给渔业执法举证造成困难的，可以结合其他证据，推定有关违法事实成立，但是当事人有证据足以推翻的除外。

**Article 83** Unless otherwise provided in this Law, the administrative penalties provided in this Law shall be decided by the fishery law enforcement agency; where the Coast Guard agency conducts fishery law enforcement according to law, the administrative penalties shall be decided by the Coast Guard agency, and if the fishing license needs to be revoked, the licensing authority shall be requested to revoke it.  
Where the facts of the violation are well-established and any of the following circumstances exists, the fishery law enforcement officer may make a penalty decision on the spot:  
(1) imposing a fine of not more than 500 yuan on an individual or a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan on a unit;  
(2) a fine penalty decision that cannot be made on the spot on water, and it would be difficult to impose the penalty afterwards.  
A penalty decision made on the spot shall be promptly reported to the fishery law enforcement agency or the Coast Guard agency to which it belongs for record.  
For cases on water that are not suitable for on-the-spot penalties but where the facts are clear, the party voluntarily admits the fault and accepts the penalty, and has no objection to the facts of the violation and the application of law, after obtaining the written consent of the party, the case may be handled quickly through measures such as simplifying the evidence collection methods and review and approval procedures.  
Where there is evidence that a party has intentionally destroyed evidence, such as dumping objects into water on water, causing difficulties for the fishery law enforcement evidence collection, the relevant illegal facts may be presumed based on other evidence, unless the party has sufficient evidence to rebut the presumption.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　违反本法规定，具有《中华人民共和国行政处罚法》规定的从轻、减轻或者不予处罚情形的，依照其规定从轻、减轻或者不予处罚。

**Article 84** Where, in violation of this Law, there are circumstances for a lighter, mitigated or no penalty as provided in the Administrative Penalty Law of the People's Republic of China, the penalty shall be lighter, mitigated or exempted in accordance therewith.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　县级以上人民政府渔业渔政主管部门等有关部门及其工作人员违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员，依法给予处分：  
（一）不符合行政许可条件准予行政许可；  
（二）依法应当作出责令改正、行政处罚等决定而未作出；  
（三）发现违法行为或者接到举报不依法查处；  
（四）参与渔业生产经营活动；  
（五）其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的行为。

**Article 85** Where the fishery and fishery administration departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments and their staff violate the provisions of this Law, and have any of the following circumstances, the leading persons responsible and the directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) granting an administrative license when the conditions for the administrative license are not met;  
(2) failing to make decisions such as ordering corrections or imposing administrative penalties that should be made according to law;  
(3) failing to investigate and handle illegal acts according to law upon discovery or receipt of reports;  
(4) participating in fishery production and operation activities;  
(5) other acts of abusing power, neglecting duties, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　违反本法规定，造成他人损害的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 86** Where, in violation of this Law, damage is caused to others, civil liability shall be borne according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　本法下列用语的含义：  
（一）水产种质资源，是指具有较高经济价值、遗传育种价值、生态保护价值和科学研究价值，可为捕捞、水产养殖以及其他人类活动所开发利用和科学研究的水生生物资源，包括水产苗种。  
（二）水产苗种，是指可直接用于繁育、增养殖（栽培）生产和科研试验、观赏的水生生物的亲本、稚体、幼体、受精卵、孢子及其遗传育种材料。  
（三）亲本，是指已达性成熟年龄的水生生物个体。  
（四）渔业船舶证书，包括渔业船舶所有权登记证书、国籍证书、检验证书和捕捞许可证。  
（五）开放水域，是指水生生物通过水的自然流通能够到达的水域，包括天然水域和与天然水域连通的人工水域。  
（六）渔业水域，是指鱼虾蟹贝类的产卵场、索饵场、越冬场、洄游通道和鱼虾蟹贝藻类以及其他水生动植物的养殖场。  
（七）海洋牧场，是指在特定海域通过采取投放人工鱼礁等措施，构建的供水生生物繁殖、生长的良好场所。

**Article 87** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms have the meanings assigned to them:  
(1) "aquatic germplasm resources" means aquatic biological resources with high economic value, genetic breeding value, ecological protection value and scientific research value, which can be exploited and utilized for fishing, aquaculture and other human activities and scientific research, including aquatic fry and fingerlings.  
(2) "aquatic fry and fingerlings" means the broodstock, juveniles, larvae, fertilized eggs, spores and their genetic breeding materials of aquatic organisms that can be directly used for breeding, enhancement and cultivation (farming) production, scientific research experiments and ornamental purposes.  
(3) "broodstock" means individual aquatic organisms that have reached the age of sexual maturity.  
(4) "fishing vessel certificates" include the certificate of ownership registration of fishing vessel, the certificate of nationality, the inspection certificate and the fishing license.  
(5) "open waters" means waters that aquatic organisms can reach through natural water circulation, including natural waters and artificial waters connected with natural waters.  
(6) "fishery waters" means the spawning grounds, feeding grounds, wintering grounds, migration passages of fish, shrimp, crab and shellfish, and the culture grounds of fish, shrimp, crab, shellfish, algae and other aquatic animals and plants.  
(7) "marine ranching" means a good place constructed in a specific sea area by taking measures such as deploying artificial reefs to provide for the reproduction and growth of aquatic organisms.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　省、自治区、直辖市根据需要制定休闲渔业管理办法。

**Article 88** The provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, as necessary, formulate administrative measures for recreational fisheries.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　在水域、滩涂以外通过设施养殖等方式进行渔业养殖的，适用本法有关规定。

**Article 89** Where fishery aquaculture is carried out through facility culture or other means outside waters and tidal flats, the relevant provisions of this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　本法自2026年5月1日起施行。

**Article 90** This Law shall come into force as of May 1, 2026.
