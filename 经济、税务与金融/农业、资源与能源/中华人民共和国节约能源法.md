### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了推动全社会节约能源，提高能源利用效率，保护和改善环境，促进经济社会全面协调可持续发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purpose of promoting energy conservation throughout society, improving energy utilization efficiency, protecting and improving the environment, and promoting comprehensive, coordinated and sustainable economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称能源，是指煤炭、石油、天然气、生物质能和电力、热力以及其他直接或者通过加工、转换而取得有用能的各种资源。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, energy means coal, petroleum, natural gas, biomass energy, electric power, heat power, and other resources from which useful energy can be obtained directly or through processing or conversion.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本法所称节约能源（以下简称节能），是指加强用能管理，采取技术上可行、经济上合理以及环境和社会可以承受的措施，从能源生产到消费的各个环节，降低消耗、减少损失和污染物排放、制止浪费，有效、合理地利用能源。

**Article 3** For the purposes of this Law, energy conservation (hereinafter referred to as energy conservation) means strengthening the management of energy use, adopting measures that are technically feasible, economically rational, and acceptable to the environment and society, reducing consumption, losses and pollutant discharge at all stages from energy production to consumption, curbing waste, and using energy efficiently and rationally.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　节约资源是我国的基本国策。国家实施节约与开发并举、把节约放在首位的能源发展战略。

**Article 4** Conservation of resources is the basic national policy of the People's Republic of China. The state implements an energy development strategy that emphasizes both conservation and development, with conservation placed first.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院和县级以上地方各级人民政府应当将节能工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划、年度计划，并组织编制和实施节能中长期专项规划、年度节能计划。  
国务院和县级以上地方各级人民政府每年向本级人民代表大会或者其常务委员会报告节能工作。

**Article 5** The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate energy conservation work into the national economic and social development plans and annual plans, and shall organize the formulation and implementation of special medium- and long-term energy conservation plans and annual energy conservation plans.  
The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level shall report annually on energy conservation work to the people's congress at the corresponding level or its standing committee.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家实行节能目标责任制和节能考核评价制度，将节能目标完成情况作为对地方人民政府及其负责人考核评价的内容。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府每年向国务院报告节能目标责任的履行情况。

**Article 6** The state applies a target responsibility system and an evaluation and assessment system for energy conservation, and shall regard the fulfillment of energy conservation targets as part of the evaluation and assessment of local people's governments and their leading officials.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall report annually to the State Council on the performance of their responsibilities with respect to energy conservation targets.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家实行有利于节能和环境保护的产业政策，限制发展高耗能、高污染行业，发展节能环保型产业。  
国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当加强节能工作，合理调整产业结构、企业结构、产品结构和能源消费结构，推动企业降低单位产值能耗和单位产品能耗，淘汰落后的生产能力，改进能源的开发、加工、转换、输送、储存和供应，提高能源利用效率。  
国家鼓励、支持开发和利用新能源、可再生能源。

**Article 7** The state applies industrial policies favorable to energy conservation and environmental protection, restricts the development of industries with high energy consumption and high pollution, and develops energy-conserving and environmentally-friendly industries.  
The State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall strengthen energy conservation work, rationally adjust the structures of industries, enterprises, products and energy consumption, encourage enterprises to reduce energy consumption per unit of output value and per unit of product, eliminate outdated production capacity, improve the development, processing, conversion, transmission, storage and supply of energy, and increase the efficiency of energy utilization.  
The state encourages and supports the development and utilization of new energy and renewable energy.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励、支持节能科学技术的研究、开发、示范和推广，促进节能技术创新与进步。  
国家开展节能宣传和教育，将节能知识纳入国民教育和培训体系，普及节能科学知识，增强全民的节能意识，提倡节约型的消费方式。

**Article 8** The state encourages and supports research, development, demonstration and dissemination of energy conservation science and technology, and promotes innovation and progress in energy conservation technology.  
The state conducts publicity and education on energy conservation, incorporates knowledge of energy conservation into the national education and training system, disseminates scientific knowledge of energy conservation, raises the energy conservation awareness of the entire population, and advocates conservation-oriented consumption patterns.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何单位和个人都应当依法履行节能义务，有权检举浪费能源的行为。  
新闻媒体应当宣传节能法律、法规和政策，发挥舆论监督作用。

**Article 9** All entities and individuals shall fulfill their obligations to conserve energy in accordance with law, and have the right to report acts of wasting energy.  
The news media shall publicize energy conservation laws, regulations and policies, and play a role in public opinion supervision.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院管理节能工作的部门主管全国的节能监督管理工作。国务院有关部门在各自的职责范围内负责节能监督管理工作，并接受国务院管理节能工作的部门的指导。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府管理节能工作的部门负责本行政区域内的节能监督管理工作。县级以上地方各级人民政府有关部门在各自的职责范围内负责节能监督管理工作，并接受同级管理节能工作的部门的指导。

**Article 10** The department in charge of energy conservation work under the State Council shall take charge of the supervision and administration of energy conservation nationwide. The relevant departments under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of energy conservation within the scope of their respective duties, and shall accept the guidance of the department in charge of energy conservation work under the State Council.  
The departments in charge of energy conservation work under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of energy conservation within their respective administrative regions. The relevant departments under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of energy conservation within the scope of their respective duties, and shall accept the guidance of the department in charge of energy conservation work at the corresponding level.

### 第二章　节能管理

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院和县级以上地方各级人民政府应当加强对节能工作的领导，部署、协调、监督、检查、推动节能工作。

**Article 11** The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen leadership over energy conservation work, and shall deploy, coordinate, supervise, inspect and promote energy conservation work.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上人民政府管理节能工作的部门和有关部门应当在各自的职责范围内，加强对节能法律、法规和节能标准执行情况的监督检查，依法查处违法用能行为。  
履行节能监督管理职责不得向监督管理对象收取费用。

**Article 12** The departments in charge of energy conservation work and relevant departments under the people's governments at or above the county level shall, within the scope of their respective duties, strengthen supervision and inspection over the implementation of energy conservation laws, regulations and standards, and investigate and deal with illegal energy use in accordance with law.  
No fees shall be charged from the objects of supervision and administration when performing the duties of energy conservation supervision and administration.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院标准化主管部门和国务院有关部门依法组织制定并适时修订有关节能的国家标准、行业标准，建立健全节能标准体系。  
国务院标准化主管部门会同国务院管理节能工作的部门和国务院有关部门制定强制性的用能产品、设备能源效率标准和生产过程中耗能高的产品的单位产品能耗限额标准。  
国家鼓励企业制定严于国家标准、行业标准的企业节能标准。  
省、自治区、直辖市制定严于强制性国家标准、行业标准的地方节能标准，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府报经国务院批准；本法另有规定的除外。

**Article 13** The standardization administrative department under the State Council and the relevant departments under the State Council shall, in accordance with law, organize the formulation and timely revision of national standards and industrial standards relating to energy conservation, and establish a sound energy conservation standard system.  
The standardization administrative department under the State Council shall, together with the department in charge of energy conservation work under the State Council and the relevant departments under the State Council, formulate mandatory energy efficiency standards for energy-using products and equipment, and quota standards for energy consumption per unit product for products with high energy consumption in the production process.  
The state encourages enterprises to formulate enterprise energy conservation standards that are stricter than the national standards or industrial standards.  
If provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government formulate local energy conservation standards that are stricter than the mandatory national standards or industrial standards, the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall submit them to the State Council for approval, unless otherwise provided for in this Law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　建筑节能的国家标准、行业标准由国务院建设主管部门组织制定，并依照法定程序发布。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府建设主管部门可以根据本地实际情况，制定严于国家标准或者行业标准的地方建筑节能标准，并报国务院标准化主管部门和国务院建设主管部门备案。

**Article 14** The national standards and industrial standards for building energy conservation shall be organized by the construction administrative department under the State Council and promulgated in accordance with legal procedures.  
The construction administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on local conditions, formulate local building energy conservation standards that are stricter than the national standards or industrial standards, and shall submit them to the standardization administrative department under the State Council and the construction administrative department under the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家实行固定资产投资项目节能评估和审查制度。不符合强制性节能标准的项目，建设单位不得开工建设；已经建成的，不得投入生产、使用。政府投资项目不符合强制性节能标准的，依法负责项目审批的机关不得批准建设。具体办法由国务院管理节能工作的部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 15** The state applies an energy conservation assessment and review system for fixed asset investment projects. For projects that do not meet the mandatory energy conservation standards, the construction entity shall not commence construction; those already constructed shall not be put into production or use. For government-invested projects that do not meet the mandatory energy conservation standards, the authority responsible for project approval in accordance with law shall not approve the construction. Specific measures shall be formulated by the department in charge of energy conservation work under the State Council together with the relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家对落后的耗能过高的用能产品、设备和生产工艺实行淘汰制度。淘汰的用能产品、设备、生产工艺的目录和实施办法，由国务院管理节能工作的部门会同国务院有关部门制定并公布。  
生产过程中耗能高的产品的生产单位，应当执行单位产品能耗限额标准。对超过单位产品能耗限额标准用能的生产单位，由管理节能工作的部门按照国务院规定的权限责令限期治理。  
对高耗能的特种设备，按照国务院的规定实行节能审查和监管。

**Article 16** The state applies a phase-out system for backward energy-using products, equipment and production processes with excessively high energy consumption. The catalog and implementing measures for the phased-out energy-using products, equipment and production processes shall be formulated and published by the department in charge of energy conservation work under the State Council together with the relevant departments under the State Council.  
Production units of products with high energy consumption in the production process shall implement the quota standards for energy consumption per unit product. For production units that use energy exceeding the quota standards for energy consumption per unit product, the department in charge of energy conservation work shall order them to rectify within a prescribed time limit in accordance with the authority prescribed by the State Council.  
Special equipment with high energy consumption shall be subject to energy conservation review and supervision in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　禁止生产、进口、销售国家明令淘汰或者不符合强制性能源效率标准的用能产品、设备；禁止使用国家明令淘汰的用能设备、生产工艺。

**Article 17** It shall be prohibited to produce, import or sell energy-using products or equipment that are explicitly eliminated by the state or do not meet the mandatory energy efficiency standards; it shall be prohibited to use energy-using equipment or production processes explicitly eliminated by the state.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家对家用电器等使用面广、耗能量大的用能产品，实行能源效率标识管理。实行能源效率标识管理的产品目录和实施办法，由国务院管理节能工作的部门会同国务院市场监督管理部门制定并公布。

**Article 18** The state applies energy efficiency labeling management to energy-using products such as household appliances that are widely used and have high energy consumption. The catalog of products subject to energy efficiency labeling management and the implementing measures shall be formulated and published by the department in charge of energy conservation work under the State Council together with the market regulatory department under the State Council.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　生产者和进口商应当对列入国家能源效率标识管理产品目录的用能产品标注能源效率标识，在产品包装物上或者说明书中予以说明，并按照规定报国务院市场监督管理部门和国务院管理节能工作的部门共同授权的机构备案。  
生产者和进口商应当对其标注的能源效率标识及相关信息的准确性负责。禁止销售应当标注而未标注能源效率标识的产品。  
禁止伪造、冒用能源效率标识或者利用能源效率标识进行虚假宣传。

**Article 19** Producers and importers shall affix energy efficiency labels on energy-using products that are included in the catalog of products subject to national energy efficiency labeling management, indicate the labels on the product packaging or in the instructions, and file them for recordation with the institution jointly authorized by the market regulatory department under the State Council and the department in charge of energy conservation work under the State Council in accordance with the provisions.  
Producers and importers shall be responsible for the accuracy of the energy efficiency labels they affix and related information. It shall be prohibited to sell products that should have been labeled but are not labeled with energy efficiency labels.  
It shall be prohibited to forge or fraudulently use energy efficiency labels, or to use energy efficiency labels for false propaganda.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　用能产品的生产者、销售者，可以根据自愿原则，按照国家有关节能产品认证的规定，向经国务院认证认可监督管理部门认可的从事节能产品认证的机构提出节能产品认证申请；经认证合格后，取得节能产品认证证书，可以在用能产品或者其包装物上使用节能产品认证标志。  
禁止使用伪造的节能产品认证标志或者冒用节能产品认证标志。

**Article 20** Producers and sellers of energy-using products may, on a voluntary basis and in accordance with the provisions of the state on energy conservation product certification, apply for energy conservation product certification to an institution approved by the certification and accreditation administrative department under the State Council that engages in energy conservation product certification; upon passing the certification, they may obtain an energy conservation product certification certificate and may use the energy conservation product certification mark on the energy-using products or their packaging.  
It shall be prohibited to use forged energy conservation product certification marks or to fraudulently use energy conservation product certification marks.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上各级人民政府统计部门应当会同同级有关部门，建立健全能源统计制度，完善能源统计指标体系，改进和规范能源统计方法，确保能源统计数据真实、完整。  
国务院统计部门会同国务院管理节能工作的部门，定期向社会公布各省、自治区、直辖市以及主要耗能行业的能源消费和节能情况等信息。

**Article 21** The statistical departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments at the corresponding level, establish and improve the energy statistics system, perfect the energy statistical indicator system, improve and standardize energy statistical methods, and ensure that energy statistical data are truthful and complete.  
The statistical department under the State Council shall, together with the department in charge of energy conservation work under the State Council, regularly publish to the public information such as energy consumption and energy conservation status of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and major energy-consuming industries.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家鼓励节能服务机构的发展，支持节能服务机构开展节能咨询、设计、评估、检测、审计、认证等服务。  
国家支持节能服务机构开展节能知识宣传和节能技术培训，提供节能信息、节能示范和其他公益性节能服务。

**Article 22** The state encourages the development of energy conservation service institutions and supports them in providing services such as energy conservation consultancy, design, evaluation, testing, auditing and certification.  
The state supports energy conservation service institutions in conducting publicity on energy conservation knowledge and training on energy conservation technology, and providing energy conservation information, energy conservation demonstration and other public-benefit energy conservation services.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家鼓励行业协会在行业节能规划、节能标准的制定和实施、节能技术推广、能源消费统计、节能宣传培训和信息咨询等方面发挥作用。

**Article 23** The state encourages trade associations to play a role in such aspects as industry energy conservation planning, formulation and implementation of energy conservation standards, dissemination of energy conservation technology, energy consumption statistics, energy conservation publicity and training, and information consultation.

### 第三章　合理使用与节约能源

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　用能单位应当按照合理用能的原则，加强节能管理，制定并实施节能计划和节能技术措施，降低能源消耗。

**Article 24** Energy-using units shall, in accordance with the principle of rational use of energy, strengthen energy conservation management, formulate and implement energy conservation plans and energy conservation technical measures, and reduce energy consumption.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　用能单位应当建立节能目标责任制，对节能工作取得成绩的集体、个人给予奖励。

**Article 25** Energy-using units shall establish a target responsibility system for energy conservation and reward the collectives and individuals that have made achievements in energy conservation work.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　用能单位应当定期开展节能教育和岗位节能培训。

**Article 26** Energy-using units shall regularly carry out energy conservation education and job-specific energy conservation training.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　用能单位应当加强能源计量管理，按照规定配备和使用经依法检定合格的能源计量器具。  
用能单位应当建立能源消费统计和能源利用状况分析制度，对各类能源的消费实行分类计量和统计，并确保能源消费统计数据真实、完整。

**Article 27** Energy-using units shall strengthen energy metering management, and equip and use energy metering instruments that have been verified as qualified in accordance with law as prescribed.  
Energy-using units shall establish systems for energy consumption statistics and analysis of energy utilization status, conduct classified metering and statistics on consumption of various types of energy, and ensure that energy consumption statistical data are truthful and complete.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　能源生产经营单位不得向本单位职工无偿提供能源。任何单位不得对能源消费实行包费制。

**Article 28** Energy production and operation units shall not provide energy to their employees free of charge. No entity shall apply a flat-rate system for energy consumption.

#### 第二节　工业节能

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府推进能源资源优化开发利用和合理配置，推进有利于节能的行业结构调整，优化用能结构和企业布局。

**Article 29** The State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall promote the optimal development, utilization and rational allocation of energy resources, promote the adjustment of industrial structures favorable to energy conservation, and optimize the structure of energy use and the distribution of enterprises.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国务院管理节能工作的部门会同国务院有关部门制定电力、钢铁、有色金属、建材、石油加工、化工、煤炭等主要耗能行业的节能技术政策，推动企业节能技术改造。

**Article 30** The department in charge of energy conservation work under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments under the State Council, formulate energy conservation technology policies for major energy-consuming industries such as electric power, iron and steel, non-ferrous metals, building materials, petroleum processing, chemical industry and coal, and promote the technological transformation of enterprises for energy conservation.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家鼓励工业企业采用高效、节能的电动机、锅炉、窑炉、风机、泵类等设备，采用热电联产、余热余压利用、洁净煤以及先进的用能监测和控制等技术。

**Article 31** The state encourages industrial enterprises to adopt high-efficiency and energy-conserving equipment such as electric motors, boilers, kilns, fans and pumps, and to adopt technologies such as combined heat and power, utilization of waste heat and pressure, clean coal, and advanced energy use monitoring and control.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　电网企业应当按照国务院有关部门制定的节能发电调度管理的规定，安排清洁、高效和符合规定的热电联产、利用余热余压发电的机组以及其他符合资源综合利用规定的发电机组与电网并网运行，上网电价执行国家有关规定。

**Article 32** Grid enterprises shall, in accordance with the provisions on energy-saving power generation dispatching management formulated by the relevant departments under the State Council, arrange for clean, efficient and compliant combined heat and power units, units using waste heat and pressure for power generation, and other generator sets that comply with the provisions on comprehensive utilization of resources to connect to and operate with the power grid, and the on-grid electricity price shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　禁止新建不符合国家规定的燃煤发电机组、燃油发电机组和燃煤热电机组。

**Article 33** It shall be prohibited to construct new coal-fired generator sets, oil-fired generator sets, and coal-fired heating generator sets that do not comply with the state provisions.

#### 第三节　建筑节能

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院建设主管部门负责全国建筑节能的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府建设主管部门负责本行政区域内建筑节能的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府建设主管部门会同同级管理节能工作的部门编制本行政区域内的建筑节能规划。建筑节能规划应当包括既有建筑节能改造计划。

**Article 34** The construction administrative department under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of building energy conservation nationwide.  
The construction administrative departments under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of building energy conservation within their respective administrative regions.  
The construction administrative departments under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with the departments in charge of energy conservation work at the corresponding level, formulate building energy conservation plans within their respective administrative regions. The building energy conservation plans shall include plans for energy conservation retrofitting of existing buildings.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　建筑工程的建设、设计、施工和监理单位应当遵守建筑节能标准。  
不符合建筑节能标准的建筑工程，建设主管部门不得批准开工建设；已经开工建设的，应当责令停止施工、限期改正；已经建成的，不得销售或者使用。  
建设主管部门应当加强对在建建筑工程执行建筑节能标准情况的监督检查。

**Article 35** Construction, design, construction and supervision entities for construction projects shall comply with building energy conservation standards.  
For construction projects that do not meet the building energy conservation standards, the construction administrative department shall not approve the commencement of construction; those for which construction has already commenced shall be ordered to stop construction and rectify within a prescribed time limit; those already constructed shall not be sold or used.  
The construction administrative departments shall strengthen supervision and inspection over the implementation of building energy conservation standards in ongoing construction projects.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　房地产开发企业在销售房屋时，应当向购买人明示所售房屋的节能措施、保温工程保修期等信息，在房屋买卖合同、质量保证书和使用说明书中载明，并对其真实性、准确性负责。

**Article 36** Real estate development enterprises, when selling houses, shall clearly indicate to the purchasers information such as the energy conservation measures of the houses sold and the warranty period of insulation works, specify them in the house sale contract, quality guarantee certificate and instructions for use, and be responsible for the truthfulness and accuracy thereof.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　使用空调采暖、制冷的公共建筑应当实行室内温度控制制度。具体办法由国务院建设主管部门制定。

**Article 37** Public buildings using air conditioning for heating or cooling shall implement an indoor temperature control system. Specific measures shall be formulated by the construction administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家采取措施，对实行集中供热的建筑分步骤实行供热分户计量、按照用热量收费的制度。新建建筑或者对既有建筑进行节能改造，应当按照规定安装用热计量装置、室内温度调控装置和供热系统调控装置。具体办法由国务院建设主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 38** The state takes measures to gradually implement a system of household-based heat metering and heat-based charging for buildings with central heat supply. For new buildings or existing buildings undergoing energy conservation retrofitting, heat metering devices, indoor temperature control devices and heating system control devices shall be installed in accordance with the provisions. Specific measures shall be formulated by the construction administrative department under the State Council together with the relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上地方各级人民政府有关部门应当加强城市节约用电管理，严格控制公用设施和大型建筑物装饰性景观照明的能耗。

**Article 39** The relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the management of urban electricity conservation, and strictly control the energy consumption of decorative landscape lighting in public facilities and large buildings.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家鼓励在新建建筑和既有建筑节能改造中使用新型墙体材料等节能建筑材料和节能设备，安装和使用太阳能等可再生能源利用系统。

**Article 40** The state encourages the use of energy-saving building materials such as new wall materials and energy-saving equipment in new buildings and in energy conservation retrofitting of existing buildings, and the installation and use of renewable energy utilization systems such as solar energy.

#### 第四节　交通运输节能

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国务院有关交通运输主管部门按照各自的职责负责全国交通运输相关领域的节能监督管理工作。  
国务院有关交通运输主管部门会同国务院管理节能工作的部门分别制定相关领域的节能规划。

**Article 41** The relevant competent departments of transportation under the State Council shall, within the scope of their respective duties, be responsible for the supervision and administration of energy conservation in the relevant fields of transportation nationwide.  
The relevant competent departments of transportation under the State Council shall, together with the department in charge of energy conservation work under the State Council, formulate energy conservation plans for the relevant fields respectively.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国务院及其有关部门指导、促进各种交通运输方式协调发展和有效衔接，优化交通运输结构，建设节能型综合交通运输体系。

**Article 42** The State Council and its relevant departments shall guide and promote the coordinated development and effective integration of various modes of transportation, optimize the transportation structure, and build an energy-saving comprehensive transportation system.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当优先发展公共交通，加大对公共交通的投入，完善公共交通服务体系，鼓励利用公共交通工具出行；鼓励使用非机动交通工具出行。

**Article 43** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall give priority to the development of public transportation, increase investment in public transportation, improve the public transport service system, encourage the use of public transport for travel, and encourage the use of non-motorized vehicles for travel.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国务院有关交通运输主管部门应当加强交通运输组织管理，引导道路、水路、航空运输企业提高运输组织化程度和集约化水平，提高能源利用效率。

**Article 44** The relevant competent departments of transportation under the State Council shall strengthen the organization and management of transportation, guide road, waterway and air transport enterprises to improve the degree of organization and intensification of transport, and enhance energy utilization efficiency.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家鼓励开发、生产、使用节能环保型汽车、摩托车、铁路机车车辆、船舶和其他交通运输工具，实行老旧交通运输工具的报废、更新制度。  
国家鼓励开发和推广应用交通运输工具使用的清洁燃料、石油替代燃料。

**Article 45** The state encourages the development, production and use of energy-saving and environmentally-friendly automobiles, motorcycles, railway locomotives and rolling stock, ships and other means of transportation, and applies a scrapping and renewal system for old means of transportation.  
The state encourages the development and promotion of clean fuels and petroleum substitute fuels for use in means of transportation.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国务院有关部门制定交通运输营运车船的燃料消耗量限值标准；不符合标准的，不得用于营运。  
国务院有关交通运输主管部门应当加强对交通运输营运车船燃料消耗检测的监督管理。

**Article 46** The relevant departments under the State Council shall formulate fuel consumption limit standards for vehicles and vessels used in transportation operations; those that do not meet the standards shall not be used for operations.  
The relevant competent departments of transportation under the State Council shall strengthen the supervision and administration of fuel consumption testing for vehicles and vessels used in transportation operations.

#### 第五节　公共机构节能

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　公共机构应当厉行节约，杜绝浪费，带头使用节能产品、设备，提高能源利用效率。  
本法所称公共机构，是指全部或者部分使用财政性资金的国家机关、事业单位和团体组织。

**Article 47** Public institutions shall practice strict economy, eliminate waste, take the lead in using energy-saving products and equipment, and improve energy utilization efficiency.  
For the purposes of this Law, public institutions mean state organs, institutions and organizations that use all or part of fiscal funds.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国务院和县级以上地方各级人民政府管理机关事务工作的机构会同同级有关部门制定和组织实施本级公共机构节能规划。公共机构节能规划应当包括公共机构既有建筑节能改造计划。

**Article 48** The institutions in charge of government affairs administration under the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments at the corresponding level, formulate and organize the implementation of energy conservation plans for public institutions at the corresponding level. The energy conservation plans for public institutions shall include plans for energy conservation retrofitting of existing buildings of public institutions.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　公共机构应当制定年度节能目标和实施方案，加强能源消费计量和监测管理，向本级人民政府管理机关事务工作的机构报送上年度的能源消费状况报告。  
国务院和县级以上地方各级人民政府管理机关事务工作的机构会同同级有关部门按照管理权限，制定本级公共机构的能源消耗定额，财政部门根据该定额制定能源消耗支出标准。

**Article 49** Public institutions shall formulate annual energy conservation targets and implementation plans, strengthen the management of energy consumption metering and monitoring, and submit reports on the energy consumption status of the previous year to the institution in charge of government affairs administration at the corresponding level.  
The institutions in charge of government affairs administration under the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments at the corresponding level, formulate energy consumption quotas for public institutions at the corresponding level in accordance with their administrative authority, and the finance departments shall formulate energy consumption expenditure standards based on such quotas.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　公共机构应当加强本单位用能系统管理，保证用能系统的运行符合国家相关标准。  
公共机构应当按照规定进行能源审计，并根据能源审计结果采取提高能源利用效率的措施。

**Article 50** Public institutions shall strengthen the management of their energy use systems and ensure that the operation of the energy use systems complies with the relevant national standards.  
Public institutions shall conduct energy audits in accordance with the provisions and take measures to improve energy utilization efficiency based on the results of the energy audits.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　公共机构采购用能产品、设备，应当优先采购列入节能产品、设备政府采购名录中的产品、设备。禁止采购国家明令淘汰的用能产品、设备。  
节能产品、设备政府采购名录由省级以上人民政府的政府采购监督管理部门会同同级有关部门制定并公布。

**Article 51** When public institutions procure energy-using products and equipment, they shall give priority to the procurement of products and equipment included in the government procurement list of energy-saving products and equipment. It shall be prohibited to procure energy-using products and equipment explicitly eliminated by the state.  
The government procurement list of energy-saving products and equipment shall be formulated and published by the government procurement supervision and administration departments of the people's governments at or above the provincial level together with the relevant departments at the corresponding level.

#### 第六节　重点用能单位节能

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家加强对重点用能单位的节能管理。  
下列用能单位为重点用能单位：  
（一）年综合能源消费总量一万吨标准煤以上的用能单位；  
（二）国务院有关部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理节能工作的部门指定的年综合能源消费总量五千吨以上不满一万吨标准煤的用能单位。  
重点用能单位节能管理办法，由国务院管理节能工作的部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 52** The state strengthens energy conservation management for key energy-using units.  
The following energy-using units are key energy-using units:  
(1) energy-using units with an annual total comprehensive energy consumption of 10,000 tons of standard coal or more;  
(2) energy-using units designated by the relevant departments under the State Council or the departments in charge of energy conservation work under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government with an annual total comprehensive energy consumption of 5,000 tons or more but less than 10,000 tons of standard coal.  
The measures for the administration of energy conservation in key energy-using units shall be formulated by the department in charge of energy conservation work under the State Council together with the relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　重点用能单位应当每年向管理节能工作的部门报送上年度的能源利用状况报告。能源利用状况包括能源消费情况、能源利用效率、节能目标完成情况和节能效益分析、节能措施等内容。

**Article 53** Key energy-using units shall submit annually to the department in charge of energy conservation work a report on the energy utilization status of the previous year. The energy utilization status includes energy consumption, energy utilization efficiency, completion of energy conservation targets, analysis of energy conservation benefits, energy conservation measures, and other content.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　管理节能工作的部门应当对重点用能单位报送的能源利用状况报告进行审查。对节能管理制度不健全、节能措施不落实、能源利用效率低的重点用能单位，管理节能工作的部门应当开展现场调查，组织实施用能设备能源效率检测，责令实施能源审计，并提出书面整改要求，限期整改。

**Article 54** The department in charge of energy conservation work shall review the reports on energy utilization status submitted by key energy-using units. For key energy-using units with unsound energy conservation management systems, unimplemented energy conservation measures, and low energy utilization efficiency, the department in charge of energy conservation work shall conduct on-site investigations, organize the implementation of energy efficiency testing of energy-using equipment, order the implementation of energy audits, issue written rectification requirements, and order rectification within a prescribed time limit.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　重点用能单位应当设立能源管理岗位，在具有节能专业知识、实际经验以及中级以上技术职称的人员中聘任能源管理负责人，并报管理节能工作的部门和有关部门备案。  
能源管理负责人负责组织对本单位用能状况进行分析、评价，组织编写本单位能源利用状况报告，提出本单位节能工作的改进措施并组织实施。  
能源管理负责人应当接受节能培训。

**Article 55** Key energy-using units shall establish energy management positions, appoint energy management persons-in-charge from among personnel with professional knowledge of energy conservation, practical experience and intermediate or higher technical titles, and report them to the department in charge of energy conservation work and the relevant departments for recordation.  
The energy management person-in-charge shall be responsible for organizing the analysis and evaluation of the energy use status of the unit, organizing the compilation of the report on the energy utilization status of the unit, proposing improvement measures for energy conservation work of the unit and organizing their implementation.  
The energy management person-in-charge shall receive energy conservation training.

### 第四章　节能技术进步

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国务院管理节能工作的部门会同国务院科技主管部门发布节能技术政策大纲，指导节能技术研究、开发和推广应用。

**Article 56** The department in charge of energy conservation work under the State Council shall, together with the science and technology administrative department under the State Council, issue an outline of energy conservation technology policies to guide research, development and dissemination of energy conservation technology.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　县级以上各级人民政府应当把节能技术研究开发作为政府科技投入的重点领域，支持科研单位和企业开展节能技术应用研究，制定节能标准，开发节能共性和关键技术，促进节能技术创新与成果转化。

**Article 57** The people's governments at or above the county level shall take research and development of energy conservation technology as a key area of government investment in science and technology, support scientific research institutions and enterprises in conducting applied research on energy conservation technology, formulating energy conservation standards, developing generic and key energy conservation technologies, and promoting innovation and transformation of achievements in energy conservation technology.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国务院管理节能工作的部门会同国务院有关部门制定并公布节能技术、节能产品的推广目录，引导用能单位和个人使用先进的节能技术、节能产品。  
国务院管理节能工作的部门会同国务院有关部门组织实施重大节能科研项目、节能示范项目、重点节能工程。

**Article 58** The department in charge of energy conservation work under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments under the State Council, formulate and publish a promotion catalog of energy conservation technologies and products to guide energy-using entities and individuals in using advanced energy conservation technologies and products.  
The department in charge of energy conservation work under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments under the State Council, organize the implementation of major energy conservation scientific research projects, energy conservation demonstration projects, and key energy conservation projects.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　县级以上各级人民政府应当按照因地制宜、多能互补、综合利用、讲求效益的原则，加强农业和农村节能工作，增加对农业和农村节能技术、节能产品推广应用的资金投入。  
农业、科技等有关主管部门应当支持、推广在农业生产、农产品加工储运等方面应用节能技术和节能产品，鼓励更新和淘汰高耗能的农业机械和渔业船舶。  
国家鼓励、支持在农村大力发展沼气，推广生物质能、太阳能和风能等可再生能源利用技术，按照科学规划、有序开发的原则发展小型水力发电，推广节能型的农村住宅和炉灶等，鼓励利用非耕地种植能源植物，大力发展薪炭林等能源林。

**Article 59** The people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the principles of adapting to local conditions, complementing multiple energy sources, comprehensive utilization, and emphasizing benefits, strengthen energy conservation work in agriculture and rural areas, and increase funding for the promotion and application of energy conservation technologies and energy-saving products in agriculture and rural areas.  
The competent departments for agriculture, science and technology, and other relevant departments shall support and promote the application of energy conservation technologies and energy-saving products in agricultural production, agricultural product processing, storage and transportation, and encourage the renewal and phase-out of high-energy-consuming agricultural machinery and fishing vessels.  
The state encourages and supports the vigorous development of biogas in rural areas, promotes the utilization of renewable energy technologies such as biomass energy, solar energy and wind energy, develops small-scale hydropower in accordance with the principles of scientific planning and orderly development, promotes energy-saving rural houses and stoves, encourages the cultivation of energy plants on non-arable land, and vigorously develops energy forests such as fuelwood forests.

### 第五章　激励措施

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　中央财政和省级地方财政安排节能专项资金，支持节能技术研究开发、节能技术和产品的示范与推广、重点节能工程的实施、节能宣传培训、信息服务和表彰奖励等。

**Article 60** The central finance and provincial local finances shall allocate special funds for energy conservation to support research and development of energy conservation technology, demonstration and promotion of energy conservation technologies and products, implementation of key energy conservation projects, energy conservation publicity and training, information services, and commendations and rewards.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家对生产、使用列入[本法第五十八条](#art-58)规定的推广目录的需要支持的节能技术、节能产品，实行税收优惠等扶持政策。  
国家通过财政补贴支持节能照明器具等节能产品的推广和使用。

**Article 61** The state applies supporting policies such as tax incentives to the production and use of energy conservation technologies and products that are included in the promotion catalog provided for in Article 58 of this Law and need support.  
The state supports the promotion and use of energy-saving products such as energy-saving lighting devices through financial subsidies.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　国家实行有利于节约能源资源的税收政策，健全能源矿产资源有偿使用制度，促进能源资源的节约及其开采利用水平的提高。

**Article 62** The state applies tax policies favorable to the conservation of energy resources, improves the system of compensated use of energy mineral resources, and promotes the conservation of energy resources and the improvement of their exploitation and utilization.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国家运用税收等政策，鼓励先进节能技术、设备的进口，控制在生产过程中耗能高、污染重的产品的出口。

**Article 63** The state uses tax and other policies to encourage the import of advanced energy-saving technologies and equipment, and to control the export of products with high energy consumption and heavy pollution in the production process.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　政府采购监督管理部门会同有关部门制定节能产品、设备政府采购名录，应当优先列入取得节能产品认证证书的产品、设备。

**Article 64** The government procurement supervision and administration departments, together with the relevant departments, when formulating the government procurement list of energy-saving products and equipment, shall give priority to including products and equipment that have obtained energy conservation product certification certificates.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国家引导金融机构增加对节能项目的信贷支持，为符合条件的节能技术研究开发、节能产品生产以及节能技术改造等项目提供优惠贷款。  
国家推动和引导社会有关方面加大对节能的资金投入，加快节能技术改造。

**Article 65** The state guides financial institutions to increase credit support for energy conservation projects, and to provide preferential loans for projects such as research and development of energy conservation technology, production of energy-saving products, and energy conservation technological transformation that meet the conditions.  
The state promotes and guides relevant social sectors to increase capital input into energy conservation, and to accelerate energy conservation technological transformation.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家实行有利于节能的价格政策，引导用能单位和个人节能。  
国家运用财税、价格等政策，支持推广电力需求侧管理、合同能源管理、节能自愿协议等节能办法。  
国家实行峰谷分时电价、季节性电价、可中断负荷电价制度，鼓励电力用户合理调整用电负荷；对钢铁、有色金属、建材、化工和其他主要耗能行业的企业，分淘汰、限制、允许和鼓励类实行差别电价政策。

**Article 66** The state applies price policies favorable to energy conservation, guiding energy-using entities and individuals to conserve energy.  
The state uses fiscal, tax and price policies to support the promotion of energy conservation methods such as power demand side management, contract energy management and voluntary energy conservation agreements.  
The state applies systems of time-of-use pricing for peak and off-peak hours, seasonal pricing, and interruptible load pricing, encouraging power users to rationally adjust their electricity loads; for enterprises in the iron and steel, non-ferrous metals, building materials, chemical and other major energy-consuming industries, a differential electricity pricing policy is applied according to categories of elimination, restriction, permission and encouragement.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　各级人民政府对在节能管理、节能科学技术研究和推广应用中有显著成绩以及检举严重浪费能源行为的单位和个人，给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 67** The people's governments at all levels shall give commendations and rewards to entities and individuals that have made remarkable achievements in energy conservation management, scientific research and promotion of energy conservation, or have reported severe acts of wasting energy.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　负责审批政府投资项目的机关违反本法规定，对不符合强制性节能标准的项目予以批准建设的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。  
固定资产投资项目建设单位开工建设不符合强制性节能标准的项目或者将该项目投入生产、使用的，由管理节能工作的部门责令停止建设或者停止生产、使用，限期改造；不能改造或者逾期不改造的生产性项目，由管理节能工作的部门报请本级人民政府按照国务院规定的权限责令关闭。

**Article 68** If the authority responsible for approving government-invested projects, in violation of the provisions of this Law, approves the construction of a project that does not meet the mandatory energy conservation standards, the directly responsible person-in-charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions in accordance with law.  
If the construction entity of a fixed asset investment project commences construction of a project that does not meet the mandatory energy conservation standards, or puts such project into production or use, the department in charge of energy conservation work shall order the suspension of construction or the suspension of production or use, and order rectification within a prescribed time limit; if the productive project cannot be rectified or fails to be rectified after the expiration of the time limit, the department in charge of energy conservation work shall submit a report to the people's government at the corresponding level and request it to order closure in accordance with the authority prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　生产、进口、销售国家明令淘汰的用能产品、设备的，使用伪造的节能产品认证标志或者冒用节能产品认证标志的，依照《中华人民共和国产品质量法》的规定处罚。

**Article 69** Anyone who produces, imports or sells energy-using products or equipment explicitly eliminated by the state, or uses forged energy conservation product certification marks or fraudulently uses energy conservation product certification marks, shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Product Quality Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　生产、进口、销售不符合强制性能源效率标准的用能产品、设备的，由市场监督管理部门责令停止生产、进口、销售，没收违法生产、进口、销售的用能产品、设备和违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销营业执照。

**Article 70** Anyone who produces, imports or sells energy-using products or equipment that do not meet the mandatory energy efficiency standards shall be ordered by the market regulatory department to stop production, import or sale, and the illegally produced, imported or sold energy-using products and equipment and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　使用国家明令淘汰的用能设备或者生产工艺的，由管理节能工作的部门责令停止使用，没收国家明令淘汰的用能设备；情节严重的，可以由管理节能工作的部门提出意见，报请本级人民政府按照国务院规定的权限责令停业整顿或者关闭。

**Article 71** Anyone who uses energy-using equipment or production processes explicitly eliminated by the state shall be ordered by the department in charge of energy conservation work to stop using them, and the energy-using equipment explicitly eliminated by the state shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the department in charge of energy conservation work may propose an opinion and submit it to the people's government at the corresponding level to order suspension of operations for rectification or closure in accordance with the authority prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　生产单位超过单位产品能耗限额标准用能，情节严重，经限期治理逾期不治理或者没有达到治理要求的，可以由管理节能工作的部门提出意见，报请本级人民政府按照国务院规定的权限责令停业整顿或者关闭。

**Article 72** If a production unit uses energy exceeding the quota standards for energy consumption per unit product, and the circumstances are serious, and if it fails to rectify within the prescribed time limit after being ordered to rectify or fails to meet the rectification requirements, the department in charge of energy conservation work may propose an opinion and submit it to the people's government at the corresponding level to order suspension of operations for rectification or closure in accordance with the authority prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　违反本法规定，应当标注能源效率标识而未标注的，由市场监督管理部门责令改正，处三万元以上五万元以下罚款。  
违反本法规定，未办理能源效率标识备案，或者使用的能源效率标识不符合规定的，由市场监督管理部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处一万元以上三万元以下罚款。  
伪造、冒用能源效率标识或者利用能源效率标识进行虚假宣传的，由市场监督管理部门责令改正，处五万元以上十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销营业执照。

**Article 73** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, fails to affix an energy efficiency label that should have been affixed shall be ordered by the market regulatory department to make corrections, and a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, fails to file an energy efficiency label for recordation, or uses an energy efficiency label that does not comply with the provisions, shall be ordered by the market regulatory department to rectify within a prescribed time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Anyone who forges or fraudulently uses an energy efficiency label, or uses an energy efficiency label for false propaganda, shall be ordered by the market regulatory department to make corrections, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　用能单位未按照规定配备、使用能源计量器具的，由市场监督管理部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处一万元以上五万元以下罚款。

**Article 74** If an energy-using unit fails to equip or use energy metering instruments in accordance with the provisions, the market regulatory department shall order it to rectify within a prescribed time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　瞒报、伪造、篡改能源统计资料或者编造虚假能源统计数据的，依照《中华人民共和国统计法》的规定处罚。

**Article 75** Anyone who conceals, forges, or falsifies energy statistical data, or fabricates false energy statistical data, shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　从事节能咨询、设计、评估、检测、审计、认证等服务的机构提供虚假信息的，由管理节能工作的部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处五万元以上十万元以下罚款。

**Article 76** If an institution providing services such as energy conservation consultancy, design, evaluation, testing, auditing or certification provides false information, the department in charge of energy conservation work shall order it to make corrections, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　违反本法规定，无偿向本单位职工提供能源或者对能源消费实行包费制的，由管理节能工作的部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处五万元以上二十万元以下罚款。

**Article 77** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, provides energy free of charge to its employees or applies a flat-rate system for energy consumption shall be ordered by the department in charge of energy conservation work to rectify within a prescribed time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　电网企业未按照本法规定安排符合规定的热电联产和利用余热余压发电的机组与电网并网运行，或者未执行国家有关上网电价规定的，由国家电力监管机构责令改正；造成发电企业经济损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 78** If a grid enterprise fails, in accordance with the provisions of this Law, to arrange for compliant combined heat and power units and units using waste heat and pressure for power generation to connect to and operate with the power grid, or fails to implement the relevant state provisions on on-grid electricity prices, the national electric power regulatory institution shall order it to make corrections; if economic losses are caused to power generation enterprises, it shall bear compensation liability in accordance with law.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　建设单位违反建筑节能标准的，由建设主管部门责令改正，处二十万元以上五十万元以下罚款。  
设计单位、施工单位、监理单位违反建筑节能标准的，由建设主管部门责令改正，处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，由颁发资质证书的部门降低资质等级或者吊销资质证书；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 79** If a construction entity violates building energy conservation standards, the construction administrative department shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.  
If a design entity, construction entity or supervision entity violates building energy conservation standards, the construction administrative department shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the department that issued the qualification certificate shall lower the qualification grade or revoke the qualification certificate; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability in accordance with law.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　房地产开发企业违反本法规定，在销售房屋时未向购买人明示所售房屋的节能措施、保温工程保修期等信息的，由建设主管部门责令限期改正，逾期不改正的，处三万元以上五万元以下罚款；对以上信息作虚假宣传的，由建设主管部门责令改正，处五万元以上二十万元以下罚款。

**Article 80** If a real estate development enterprise, in violation of the provisions of this Law, fails to clearly indicate to the purchaser information such as the energy conservation measures of the houses sold and the warranty period of insulation works when selling houses, the construction administrative department shall order it to rectify within a prescribed time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if it makes false propaganda about the above information, the construction administrative department shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　公共机构采购用能产品、设备，未优先采购列入节能产品、设备政府采购名录中的产品、设备，或者采购国家明令淘汰的用能产品、设备的，由政府采购监督管理部门给予警告，可以并处罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分，并予通报。

**Article 81** If a public institution, when procuring energy-using products and equipment, fails to give priority to procuring products and equipment included in the government procurement list of energy-saving products and equipment, or procures energy-using products and equipment explicitly eliminated by the state, the government procurement supervision and administration department shall give a warning and may also impose a fine; the directly responsible person-in-charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions in accordance with law, and shall be circulated.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　重点用能单位未按照本法规定报送能源利用状况报告或者报告内容不实的，由管理节能工作的部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处一万元以上五万元以下罚款。

**Article 82** If a key energy-using unit fails to submit a report on energy utilization status in accordance with the provisions of this Law, or the content of the report is untrue, the department in charge of energy conservation work shall order it to rectify within a prescribed time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　重点用能单位无正当理由拒不落实[本法第五十四条](#art-54)规定的整改要求或者整改没有达到要求的，由管理节能工作的部门处十万元以上三十万元以下罚款。

**Article 83** If a key energy-using unit, without justifiable reasons, refuses to implement the rectification requirements provided for in Article 54 of this Law, or the rectification fails to meet the requirements, the department in charge of energy conservation work shall impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　重点用能单位未按照本法规定设立能源管理岗位，聘任能源管理负责人，并报管理节能工作的部门和有关部门备案的，由管理节能工作的部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处一万元以上三万元以下罚款。

**Article 84** If a key energy-using unit fails to establish an energy management position, appoint an energy management person-in-charge, and report to the department in charge of energy conservation work and the relevant departments for recordation in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the department in charge of energy conservation work shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 85** Anyone who violates the provisions of this Law, if a crime is constituted, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with law.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　国家工作人员在节能管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 86** If a state functionary abuses his power, neglects his duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain in energy conservation administration work, if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law; if no crime is constituted, sanctions shall be given in accordance with law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　本法自2008年4月1日起施行。

**Article 87** This Law shall come into force as of April 1, 2008.
