### 最高人民法院关于破坏生产单位正在使用的电动机是否构成破坏电力设备罪问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于破坏生产单位正在使用的电动机  
  
是否构成破坏电力设备罪问题的批复  
  
法明传（1993）241号  
  
湖北省高级人民法院：  
  
你院91035号传真《关于破坏生产单位正在使用的电动机是否可以构成破坏电力设备罪的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
破坏电力设备罪是危害公共安全的犯罪。该罪所侵犯的客体，是社会的公共安全。如果行为人的行为不具有危害社会公共安全的性质，不能构成该罪。  
  
对拆盗某些排灌站、加工厂等生产单位正在使用中的电机设备等，没有危及社会公共安全，但应当追究刑事责任的，可以根据案件的不同情况，按盗窃罪、破坏集体生产罪或者故意毁坏公私财物罪处理。  
  
1993年8月4日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply Concerning Whether the Destruction of Electric Motors Being Used by Production Units Constitutes the Crime of Sabotaging Electrical Equipment  
Fa Ming Chuan (1993) No. 241  
Higher People's Court of Hubei Province:  
We acknowledge receipt of your court's Fax No. 91035, the "Request for Instructions on Whether the Destruction of Electric Motors Being Used by Production Units May Constitute the Crime of Sabotaging Electrical Equipment." After study, we reply as follows:  
The crime of sabotaging electrical equipment is a crime endangering public security. The object infringed by this crime is the public security of society. If the actor's conduct does not have the nature of endangering the public security of society, the crime shall not be constituted.  
For dismantling and stealing of motor equipment being used by production units such as certain irrigation and drainage stations or processing plants, which does not endanger the public security of society but for which criminal liability shall be investigated, the matter may be handled in accordance with the circumstances of the case as the crime of theft, the crime of sabotaging collective production, or the crime of intentionally damaging public or private property.  
August 4, 1993
