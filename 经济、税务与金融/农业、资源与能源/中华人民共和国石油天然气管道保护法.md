# 中华人民共和国石油天然气管道保护法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2010-06-25  
**生效日期**：2010-10-01

> （2010年6月25日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十五次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　管道规划与建设 第三章　管道运行中的保护 第四章　管道建设工程与其他建设工程相遇关系的处理 第五章　法律责任 第六章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保护石油、天然气管道，保障石油、天然气输送安全，维护国家能源安全和公共安全，制定本法。 第二条　中华人民共和国境内输送石油、天然气的管道的保护，适用本法。 城镇燃气管道和炼油、化工等企业厂区内管道的保护，不适用本法。 第三条　本法所称石油包括原油和成品油，所称天然气包括天然气、煤层气和煤制气。 本法所称管道包括管道及管道附属设施。 第四条　国务院能源主管部门依照本法规定主管全国管道保护工作，负责组织编制并实施全国管道发展规划，统筹协调全国管道发展规划与其他专项规划的衔接，协调跨省、自治区、直辖市管道保护的重大问题。国务院其他有关部门依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，在各自职责范围内负责管道保护的相关工作。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护石油、天然气管道，保障石油、天然气输送安全，维护国家能源安全和公共安全，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of protecting oil and natural gas pipelines, ensuring the safety of oil and natural gas transportation, and safeguarding national energy security and public security.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国境内输送石油、天然气的管道的保护，适用本法。  
城镇燃气管道和炼油、化工等企业厂区内管道的保护，不适用本法。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the protection of pipelines for transporting oil and natural gas within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
This Law shall not apply to the protection of urban gas pipelines and pipelines within the factory premises of enterprises such as oil refineries and chemical plants.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本法所称石油包括原油和成品油，所称天然气包括天然气、煤层气和煤制气。  
本法所称管道包括管道及管道附属设施。

**Article 3** For the purposes of this Law, petroleum includes crude oil and refined oil products, and natural gas includes natural gas, coalbed methane, and coal-based synthetic gas.  
For the purposes of this Law, pipelines include pipelines and pipeline ancillary facilities.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院能源主管部门依照本法规定主管全国管道保护工作，负责组织编制并实施全国管道发展规划，统筹协调全国管道发展规划与其他专项规划的衔接，协调跨省、自治区、直辖市管道保护的重大问题。国务院其他有关部门依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，在各自职责范围内负责管道保护的相关工作。

**Article 4** The energy主管部门 of the State Council shall be responsible for the national pipeline protection work in accordance with the provisions of this Law, organize the formulation and implementation of the national pipeline development plan, coordinate the integration of the national pipeline development plan with other special plans, and coordinate major issues concerning pipeline protection across provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government. Other relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, be responsible for the relevant work of pipeline protection within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府能源主管部门和设区的市级、县级人民政府指定的部门，依照本法规定主管本行政区域的管道保护工作，协调处理本行政区域管道保护的重大问题，指导、监督有关单位履行管道保护义务，依法查处危害管道安全的违法行为。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，在各自职责范围内负责管道保护的相关工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府能源主管部门和设区的市级、县级人民政府指定的部门，统称县级以上地方人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门。

**Article 5** The energy authorities of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the departments designated by the people's governments of cities with districts and counties, shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law, be responsible for the pipeline protection work within their respective administrative regions, coordinate and handle major issues concerning pipeline protection within their administrative regions, guide and supervise relevant units in fulfilling pipeline protection obligations, and investigate and deal with illegal acts endangering pipeline safety according to law. Other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, be responsible for the relevant work of pipeline protection within their respective functions and duties.  
The energy authorities of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the departments designated by the people's governments of cities with districts and counties, are collectively referred to as the departments in charge of pipeline protection work of local people's governments at or above the county level.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对本行政区域管道保护工作的领导，督促、检查有关部门依法履行管道保护职责，组织排除管道的重大外部安全隐患。

**Article 6** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen their leadership over pipeline protection work within their respective administrative regions, supervise and inspect relevant departments in fulfilling their pipeline protection duties according to law, and organize the elimination of major external safety hazards to pipelines.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　管道企业应当遵守本法和有关规划、建设、安全生产、质量监督、环境保护等法律、行政法规，执行国家技术规范的强制性要求，建立、健全本企业有关管道保护的规章制度和操作规程并组织实施，宣传管道安全与保护知识，履行管道保护义务，接受人民政府及其有关部门依法实施的监督，保障管道安全运行。

**Article 7** Pipeline enterprises shall comply with this Law and relevant laws and administrative regulations on planning, construction, work safety, quality supervision, and environmental protection, implement the mandatory requirements of national technical specifications, establish and improve the rules, regulations, and operating procedures of the enterprise concerning pipeline protection and organize their implementation, publicize knowledge on pipeline safety and protection, fulfill pipeline protection obligations, accept supervision implemented by the people's government and its relevant departments according to law, and ensure the safe operation of pipelines.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　任何单位和个人不得实施危害管道安全的行为。  
对危害管道安全的行为，任何单位和个人有权向县级以上地方人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门或者其他有关部门举报。接到举报的部门应当在职责范围内及时处理。

**Article 8** No unit or individual may commit any act that endangers pipeline safety.  
Any unit or individual shall have the right to report acts endangering pipeline safety to the department in charge of pipeline protection work of the local people's government at or above the county level or other relevant departments. The department that receives the report shall handle the matter promptly within its functions and duties.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家鼓励和促进管道保护新技术的研究开发和推广应用。

**Article 9** The State encourages and promotes the research, development, and popularization and application of new technologies for pipeline protection.

### 第二章　管道规划与建设

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　管道的规划、建设应当符合管道保护的要求，遵循安全、环保、节约用地和经济合理的原则。

**Article 10** The planning and construction of pipelines shall comply with the requirements of pipeline protection and follow the principles of safety, environmental protection, land conservation, and economic reasonableness.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院能源主管部门根据国民经济和社会发展的需要组织编制全国管道发展规划。组织编制全国管道发展规划应当征求国务院有关部门以及有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的意见。  
全国管道发展规划应当符合国家能源规划，并与土地利用总体规划、城乡规划以及矿产资源、环境保护、水利、铁路、公路、航道、港口、电信等规划相协调。

**Article 11** The energy authority of the State Council shall organize the formulation of a national pipeline development plan in light of the needs of national economic and social development. The formulation of the national pipeline development plan shall solicit the opinions of the relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
The national pipeline development plan shall conform to the national energy plan and shall coordinate with the master plan for land use, urban and rural planning, and plans for mineral resources, environmental protection, water conservancy, railways, highways, waterways, ports, and telecommunications.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　管道企业应当根据全国管道发展规划编制管道建设规划，并将管道建设规划确定的管道建设选线方案报送拟建管道所在地县级以上地方人民政府城乡规划主管部门审核；经审核符合城乡规划的，应当依法纳入当地城乡规划。  
纳入城乡规划的管道建设用地，不得擅自改变用途。

**Article 12** Pipeline enterprises shall, in accordance with the national pipeline development plan, formulate pipeline construction plans and submit the pipeline construction route selection scheme determined in the pipeline construction plan to the competent department of urban and rural planning of the local people's government at or above the county level where the pipeline is to be constructed for examination and verification; if the scheme is verified to be in compliance with urban and rural planning, it shall be incorporated into the local urban and rural planning according to law.  
The land for pipeline construction incorporated into urban and rural planning shall not be changed in use without authorization.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　管道建设的选线应当避开地震活动断层和容易发生洪灾、地质灾害的区域，与建筑物、构筑物、铁路、公路、航道、港口、市政设施、军事设施、电缆、光缆等保持本法和有关法律、行政法规以及国家技术规范的强制性要求规定的保护距离。  
新建管道通过的区域受地理条件限制，不能满足前款规定的管道保护要求的，管道企业应当提出防护方案，经管道保护方面的专家评审论证，并经管道所在地县级以上地方人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门批准后，方可建设。  
管道建设项目应当依法进行环境影响评价。

**Article 13** The route selection for pipeline construction shall avoid active earthquake faults and areas prone to floods and geological disasters, and shall maintain the protection distances prescribed by this Law, relevant laws and administrative regulations, and the mandatory requirements of national technical specifications from buildings, structures, railways, highways, waterways, ports, municipal facilities, military facilities, cables, and optical cables.  
Where the area through which a newly constructed pipeline passes is subject to geographical constraints and cannot meet the pipeline protection requirements prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the pipeline enterprise shall propose a protection scheme, which shall be reviewed and demonstrated by experts in pipeline protection, and construction may only proceed after approval by the department in charge of pipeline protection work of the local people's government at or above the county level where the pipeline is located.  
Pipeline construction projects shall undergo environmental impact assessment according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　管道建设使用土地，依照《中华人民共和国土地管理法》等法律、行政法规的规定执行。  
依法建设的管道通过集体所有的土地或者他人取得使用权的国有土地，影响土地使用的，管道企业应当按照管道建设时土地的用途给予补偿。

**Article 14** The use of land for pipeline construction shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China and other laws and administrative regulations.  
Where a pipeline constructed according to law passes through collectively-owned land or State-owned land for which others have obtained the right to use, and the use of the land is affected, the pipeline enterprise shall pay compensation according to the purpose of the land at the time of pipeline construction.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　依照法律和国务院的规定，取得行政许可或者已报送备案并符合开工条件的管道项目的建设，任何单位和个人不得阻碍。

**Article 15** No unit or individual may obstruct the construction of a pipeline project that has obtained an administrative license or has been submitted for recordation and meets the conditions for commencement of construction in accordance with the provisions of laws and the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　管道建设应当遵守法律、行政法规有关建设工程质量管理的规定。  
管道企业应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，选择具备相应资质的勘察、设计、施工、工程监理单位进行管道建设。  
管道的安全保护设施应当与管道主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投入使用。  
管道建设使用的管道产品及其附件的质量，应当符合国家技术规范的强制性要求。

**Article 16** Pipeline construction shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on the quality management of construction projects.  
Pipeline enterprises shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, select survey, design, construction, and project supervision units with corresponding qualifications for pipeline construction.  
The safety protection facilities of a pipeline shall be designed, constructed, and put into use simultaneously with the main pipeline project.  
The quality of pipeline products and accessories used in pipeline construction shall comply with the mandatory requirements of national technical specifications.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　穿跨越水利工程、防洪设施、河道、航道、铁路、公路、港口、电力设施、通信设施、市政设施的管道的建设，应当遵守本法和有关法律、行政法规，执行国家技术规范的强制性要求。

**Article 17** The construction of pipelines that cross or traverse hydraulic engineering works, flood control facilities, rivers, waterways, railways, highways, ports, power facilities, communication facilities, and municipal facilities shall comply with this Law and relevant laws and administrative regulations, and implement the mandatory requirements of national technical specifications.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　管道企业应当按照国家技术规范的强制性要求在管道沿线设置管道标志。管道标志毁损或者安全警示不清的，管道企业应当及时修复或者更新。

**Article 18** Pipeline enterprises shall, in accordance with the mandatory requirements of national technical specifications, set up pipeline markers along the pipeline route. If pipeline markers are damaged or the safety warnings are unclear, the pipeline enterprise shall promptly repair or replace them.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　管道建成后应当按照国家有关规定进行竣工验收。竣工验收应当审查管道是否符合本法规定的管道保护要求，经验收合格方可正式交付使用。

**Article 19** After a pipeline is completed, it shall undergo completion acceptance in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. The completion acceptance shall examine whether the pipeline meets the pipeline protection requirements prescribed by this Law, and the pipeline may only be formally delivered for use after passing the acceptance inspection.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　管道企业应当自管道竣工验收合格之日起六十日内，将竣工测量图报管道所在地县级以上地方人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门备案；县级以上地方人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门应当将管道企业报送的管道竣工测量图分送本级人民政府规划、建设、国土资源、铁路、交通、水利、公安、安全生产监督管理等部门和有关军事机关。

**Article 20** Pipeline enterprises shall, within sixty days from the date on which the pipeline passes the completion acceptance, submit the as-built survey drawing to the department in charge of pipeline protection work of the local people's government at or above the county level where the pipeline is located for recordation; the department in charge of pipeline protection work of the local people's government at or above the county level shall distribute the as-built survey drawing submitted by the pipeline enterprise to the department of planning, construction, land and resources, railway, transport, water resources, public security, and work safety supervision and management of the people's government at the same level, and to relevant military authorities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　地方各级人民政府编制、调整土地利用总体规划和城乡规划，需要管道改建、搬迁或者增加防护设施的，应当与管道企业协商确定补偿方案。

**Article 21** Where local people's governments at all levels, in preparing or adjusting overall land use plans and urban and rural plans, need to alter or relocate pipelines or add protective facilities, they shall consult with pipeline enterprises to determine the compensation plan.

### 第三章　管道运行中的保护

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　管道企业应当建立、健全管道巡护制度，配备专门人员对管道线路进行日常巡护。管道巡护人员发现危害管道安全的情形或者隐患，应当按照规定及时处理和报告。

**Article 22** Pipeline enterprises shall establish and improve pipeline patrol systems, and assign specialized personnel to conduct daily patrols of pipeline routes. Where patrol personnel discover circumstances or hazards endangering pipeline safety, they shall promptly handle and report them in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　管道企业应当定期对管道进行检测、维修，确保其处于良好状态；对管道安全风险较大的区段和场所应当进行重点监测，采取有效措施防止管道事故的发生。  
对不符合安全使用条件的管道，管道企业应当及时更新、改造或者停止使用。

**Article 23** Pipeline enterprises shall regularly inspect and maintain pipelines to ensure they are in good condition; they shall conduct key monitoring of sections and locations where pipeline safety risks are relatively high, and adopt effective measures to prevent the occurrence of pipeline accidents.  
For pipelines that do not meet the conditions for safe use, pipeline enterprises shall promptly update, renovate, or discontinue use.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　管道企业应当配备管道保护所必需的人员和技术装备，研究开发和使用先进适用的管道保护技术，保证管道保护所必需的经费投入，并对在管道保护中做出突出贡献的单位和个人给予奖励。

**Article 24** Pipeline enterprises shall be equipped with the personnel and technical equipment necessary for pipeline protection, research, develop and use advanced and applicable pipeline protection technologies, ensure the necessary funding for pipeline protection, and give rewards to units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to pipeline protection.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　管道企业发现管道存在安全隐患，应当及时排除。对管道存在的外部安全隐患，管道企业自身排除确有困难的，应当向县级以上地方人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门报告。接到报告的主管管道保护工作的部门应当及时协调排除或者报请人民政府及时组织排除安全隐患。

**Article 25** Where a pipeline enterprise discovers a safety hazard in a pipeline, it shall promptly eliminate it. Where there is an external safety hazard to a pipeline that the pipeline enterprise itself has difficulty eliminating, it shall report to the department in charge of pipeline protection under the local people's government at or above the county level. The department in charge of pipeline protection that receives the report shall promptly coordinate the elimination or report to the people's government to promptly organize the elimination of the safety hazard.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　管道企业依法取得使用权的土地，任何单位和个人不得侵占。  
为合理利用土地，在保障管道安全的条件下，管道企业可以与有关单位、个人约定，同意有关单位、个人种植浅根农作物。但是，因管道巡护、检测、维修造成的农作物损失，除另有约定外，管道企业不予赔偿。

**Article 26** No unit or individual shall occupy land for which a pipeline enterprise has obtained the right to use according to law.  
For the rational use of land, under the condition of ensuring pipeline safety, a pipeline enterprise may agree with relevant units or individuals to permit them to plant shallow-root crops. However, except as otherwise agreed, the pipeline enterprise shall not compensate for crop losses caused by pipeline patrol, inspection, or maintenance.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　管道企业对管道进行巡护、检测、维修等作业，管道沿线的有关单位、个人应当给予必要的便利。  
因管道巡护、检测、维修等作业给土地使用权人或者其他单位、个人造成损失的，管道企业应当依法给予赔偿。

**Article 27** Where a pipeline enterprise conducts operations such as patrol, inspection, or maintenance on a pipeline, the relevant units and individuals along the pipeline route shall provide necessary convenience.  
If such operations cause losses to land use right holders or other units or individuals, the pipeline enterprise shall provide compensation according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　禁止下列危害管道安全的行为：  
（一）擅自开启、关闭管道阀门；  
（二）采用移动、切割、打孔、砸撬、拆卸等手段损坏管道；  
（三）移动、毁损、涂改管道标志；  
（四）在埋地管道上方巡查便道上行驶重型车辆；  
（五）在地面管道线路、架空管道线路和管桥上行走或者放置重物。

**Article 28** The following acts endangering pipeline safety are prohibited:  
(1) opening or closing pipeline valves without authorization;  
(2) damaging pipelines by means such as moving, cutting, drilling, smashing, prying, or disassembling;  
(3) moving, destroying, or altering pipeline markers;  
(4) driving heavy vehicles on the inspection walkways above buried pipelines;  
(5) walking or placing heavy objects on above-ground pipeline routes, overhead pipeline routes, and pipe bridges.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　禁止在[本法第五十八条](#art-58)第一项所列管道附属设施的上方架设电力线路、通信线路或者在储气库构造区域范围内进行工程挖掘、工程钻探、采矿。

**Article 29** It is prohibited to erect power lines, communication lines above the pipeline auxiliary facilities listed in item (1) of Article 58 of this Law, or to carry out engineering excavation, engineering drilling, or mining within the structural area of gas storage reservoirs.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　在管道线路中心线两侧各五米地域范围内，禁止下列危害管道安全的行为：  
（一）种植乔木、灌木、藤类、芦苇、竹子或者其他根系深达管道埋设部位可能损坏管道防腐层的深根植物；  
（二）取土、采石、用火、堆放重物、排放腐蚀性物质、使用机械工具进行挖掘施工；  
（三）挖塘、修渠、修晒场、修建水产养殖场、建温室、建家畜棚圈、建房以及修建其他建筑物、构筑物。

**Article 30** Within the area five meters on each side of the centerline of a pipeline route, the following acts endangering pipeline safety are prohibited:  
(1) planting trees, shrubs, vines, reeds, bamboo, or other deep-rooted plants whose roots reach the depth of the pipeline laying site and may damage the pipeline's anti-corrosion coating;  
(2) excavating earth, quarrying stone, using fire, stacking heavy objects, discharging corrosive substances, or using mechanical tools for excavation and construction;  
(3) digging ponds, building canals, building drying grounds, building aquaculture farms, building greenhouses, building livestock sheds, building houses, or constructing other buildings or structures.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　在管道线路中心线两侧和[本法第五十八条](#art-58)第一项所列管道附属设施周边修建下列建筑物、构筑物的，建筑物、构筑物与管道线路和管道附属设施的距离应当符合国家技术规范的强制性要求：  
（一）居民小区、学校、医院、娱乐场所、车站、商场等人口密集的建筑物；  
（二）变电站、加油站、加气站、储油罐、储气罐等易燃易爆物品的生产、经营、存储场所。  
前款规定的国家技术规范的强制性要求，应当按照保障管道及建筑物、构筑物安全和节约用地的原则确定。

**Article 31** Where the following buildings or structures are constructed on both sides of the centerline of a pipeline route or around the pipeline auxiliary facilities listed in item (1) of Article 58 of this Law, the distance between the buildings or structures and the pipeline route and pipeline auxiliary facilities shall comply with the mandatory requirements of national technical specifications:  
(1) densely populated buildings such as residential communities, schools, hospitals, entertainment venues, stations, and shopping malls;  
(2) production, operation, and storage sites for flammable and explosive articles such as substations, gas stations, filling stations, oil tanks, and gas tanks.  
The mandatory requirements of the national technical specifications specified in the preceding paragraph shall be determined in accordance with the principle of ensuring the safety of pipelines, buildings, and structures and saving land.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　在穿越河流的管道线路中心线两侧各五百米地域范围内，禁止抛锚、拖锚、挖砂、挖泥、采石、水下爆破。但是，在保障管道安全的条件下，为防洪和航道通畅而进行的养护疏浚作业除外。

**Article 32** Within the area five hundred meters on each side of the centerline of a pipeline route crossing a river, anchoring, dragging anchors, sand digging, mud digging, stone quarrying, and underwater blasting are prohibited. However, maintenance and dredging operations carried out for flood control and navigation clearance under the condition of ensuring pipeline safety are excepted.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　在管道专用隧道中心线两侧各一千米地域范围内，除本条第二款规定的情形外，禁止采石、采矿、爆破。  
在前款规定的地域范围内，因修建铁路、公路、水利工程等公共工程，确需实施采石、爆破作业的，应当经管道所在地县级人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门批准，并采取必要的安全防护措施，方可实施。

**Article 33** Within the area one thousand meters on each side of the centerline of a pipeline-specific tunnel, except as provided in paragraph 2 of this Article, stone quarrying, mining, and blasting are prohibited.  
Within the area specified in the preceding paragraph, where stone quarrying or blasting operations are truly necessary for the construction of public projects such as railways, highways, or water conservancy projects, they shall be subject to the approval of the department in charge of pipeline protection under the county people's government in the location of the pipeline, and necessary safety protection measures shall be taken before implementation.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　未经管道企业同意，其他单位不得使用管道专用伴行道路、管道水工防护设施、管道专用隧道等管道附属设施。

**Article 34** Without the consent of a pipeline enterprise, other units shall not use pipeline auxiliary facilities such as dedicated accompanying roads, hydraulic protection facilities, and dedicated tunnels.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　进行下列施工作业，施工单位应当向管道所在地县级人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门提出申请：  
（一）穿跨越管道的施工作业；  
（二）在管道线路中心线两侧各五米至五十米和[本法第五十八条](#art-58)第一项所列管道附属设施周边一百米地域范围内，新建、改建、扩建铁路、公路、河渠，架设电力线路，埋设地下电缆、光缆，设置安全接地体、避雷接地体；  
（三）在管道线路中心线两侧各二百米和[本法第五十八条](#art-58)第一项所列管道附属设施周边五百米地域范围内，进行爆破、地震法勘探或者工程挖掘、工程钻探、采矿。  
县级人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门接到申请后，应当组织施工单位与管道企业协商确定施工作业方案，并签订安全防护协议；协商不成的，主管管道保护工作的部门应当组织进行安全评审，作出是否批准作业的决定。

**Article 35** Where the following construction operations are carried out, the construction unit shall submit an application to the department in charge of pipeline protection under the county people's government in the location of the pipeline:  
(1) construction operations crossing or spanning a pipeline;  
(2) within the area from five to fifty meters on each side of the centerline of the pipeline route and within one hundred meters around the pipeline auxiliary facilities listed in item (1) of Article 58 of this Law, constructing, reconstructing, or expanding railways, highways, or canals, erecting power lines, burying underground cables or optical cables, or installing safety grounding bodies or lightning protection grounding bodies;  
(3) within the area of two hundred meters on each side of the centerline of the pipeline route and within five hundred meters around the pipeline auxiliary facilities listed in item (1) of Article 58 of this Law, carrying out blasting, seismic prospecting, or engineering excavation, engineering drilling, or mining.  
After receiving the application, the department in charge of pipeline protection under the county people's government shall organize the construction unit and the pipeline enterprise to negotiate and determine the construction operation plan and sign a safety protection agreement; if negotiation fails, the department in charge of pipeline protection shall organize a safety review and make a decision on whether to approve the operation.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　申请进行[本法第三十三条](#art-33)第二款、第三十五条规定的施工作业，应当符合下列条件：  
（一）具有符合管道安全和公共安全要求的施工作业方案；  
（二）已制定事故应急预案；  
（三）施工作业人员具备管道保护知识；  
（四）具有保障安全施工作业的设备、设施。

**Article 36** An application for construction operations as specified in Article 33, paragraph 2, and Article 35 of this Law shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having a construction operation plan that meets the requirements for pipeline safety and public safety;  
(2) having formulated an emergency response plan for accidents;  
(3) the construction operation personnel possess knowledge of pipeline protection;  
(4) having equipment and facilities to ensure safe construction operations.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　进行[本法第三十三条](#art-33)第二款、第三十五条规定的施工作业，应当在开工七日前书面通知管道企业。管道企业应当指派专门人员到现场进行管道保护安全指导。

**Article 37** For construction operations as specified in Article 33, paragraph 2, and Article 35 of this Law, the pipeline enterprise shall be notified in writing seven days before the start of construction. The pipeline enterprise shall assign specialized personnel to the site to provide safety guidance for pipeline protection.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　管道企业在紧急情况下进行管道抢修作业，可以先行使用他人土地或者设施，但应当及时告知土地或者设施的所有权人或者使用权人。给土地或者设施的所有权人或者使用权人造成损失的，管道企业应当依法给予赔偿。

**Article 38** Where a pipeline enterprise carries out emergency repair operations in an emergency, it may first use another person's land or facilities, but shall promptly notify the owner or right holder of the land or facilities. If losses are caused to the owner or right holder of the land or facilities, the pipeline enterprise shall provide compensation according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　管道企业应当制定本企业管道事故应急预案，并报管道所在地县级人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门备案；配备抢险救援人员和设备，并定期进行管道事故应急救援演练。  
发生管道事故，管道企业应当立即启动本企业管道事故应急预案，按照规定及时通报可能受到事故危害的单位和居民，采取有效措施消除或者减轻事故危害，并依照有关事故调查处理的法律、行政法规的规定，向事故发生地县级人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门、安全生产监督管理部门和其他有关部门报告。  
接到报告的主管管道保护工作的部门应当按照规定及时上报事故情况，并根据管道事故的实际情况组织采取事故处置措施或者报请人民政府及时启动本行政区域管道事故应急预案，组织进行事故应急处置与救援。

**Article 39** A pipeline enterprise shall formulate its own pipeline accident emergency plan and submit it to the department in charge of pipeline protection under the county people's government in the location of the pipeline for record; it shall be equipped with emergency rescue personnel and equipment, and regularly conduct emergency rescue drills for pipeline accidents.  
In the event of a pipeline accident, the pipeline enterprise shall immediately activate its own pipeline accident emergency plan, promptly notify units and residents that may be endangered by the accident in accordance with regulations, adopt effective measures to eliminate or mitigate the harm caused by the accident, and report to the department in charge of pipeline protection, work safety supervision and administration department, and other relevant departments under the county people's government in the location of the accident, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on accident investigation and handling.  
The department in charge of pipeline protection that receives the report shall promptly report the accident situation upward in accordance with regulations, and based on the actual circumstances of the pipeline accident, organize the adoption of accident handling measures or report to the people's government to promptly activate the administrative region's pipeline accident emergency plan, and organize emergency response and rescue for the accident.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　管道泄漏的石油和因管道抢修排放的石油造成环境污染的，管道企业应当及时治理。因第三人的行为致使管道泄漏造成环境污染的，管道企业有权向第三人追偿治理费用。  
环境污染损害的赔偿责任，适用《中华人民共和国侵权责任法》和防治环境污染的法律的有关规定。

**Article 40** Where oil leaked from a pipeline or oil discharged due to pipeline emergency repairs causes environmental pollution, the pipeline enterprise shall promptly remediate. Where a third party's act causes a pipeline leak resulting in environmental pollution, the pipeline enterprise has the right to recover remediation costs from the third party.  
The compensation liability for environmental pollution damage shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the Tort Liability Law of the People's Republic of China and the laws on prevention and control of environmental pollution.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　管道泄漏的石油和因管道抢修排放的石油，由管道企业回收、处理，任何单位和个人不得侵占、盗窃、哄抢。

**Article 41** Petroleum leaked from pipelines and petroleum discharged due to emergency pipeline repair shall be recovered and disposed of by pipeline enterprises. No unit or individual may encroach upon, steal, or plunder such petroleum.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　管道停止运行、封存、报废的，管道企业应当采取必要的安全防护措施，并报县级以上地方人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门备案。

**Article 42** Where pipelines are deactivated, sealed, or scrapped, the pipeline enterprise shall take necessary safety protection measures and report to the department in charge of pipeline protection work of the local people's government at or above the county level for record.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　管道重点保护部位，需要由中国人民武装警察部队负责守卫的，依照《中华人民共和国人民武装警察法》和国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 43** If the key protection parts of pipelines need to be guarded by the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, this shall be handled in accordance with the People's Armed Police Law of the People's Republic of China and the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

### 第四章　管道建设工程与其他建设工程相遇关系的处理

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　管道建设工程与其他建设工程的相遇关系，依照法律的规定处理；法律没有规定的，由建设工程双方按照下列原则协商处理，并为对方提供必要的便利：  
（一）后开工的建设工程服从先开工或者已建成的建设工程；  
（二）同时开工的建设工程，后批准的建设工程服从先批准的建设工程。  
依照前款规定，后开工或者后批准的建设工程，应当符合先开工、已建成或者先批准的建设工程的安全防护要求；需要先开工、已建成或者先批准的建设工程改建、搬迁或者增加防护设施的，后开工或者后批准的建设工程一方应当承担由此增加的费用。  
管道建设工程与其他建设工程相遇的，建设工程双方应当协商确定施工作业方案并签订安全防护协议，指派专门人员现场监督、指导对方施工。

**Article 44** The intersecting relationship between pipeline construction projects and other construction projects shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the law. If the law has no provisions, the parties of the two construction projects shall negotiate and handle it according to the following principles and provide necessary convenience for each other:  
(1) The later-commenced construction project shall be subordinate to the earlier-commenced or completed construction project;  
(2) For concurrently commenced construction projects, the later-approved construction project shall be subordinate to the earlier-approved construction project.  
In accordance with the preceding paragraph, the later-commenced or later-approved construction project shall meet the safety protection requirements of the earlier-commenced, completed, or earlier-approved construction project. If the earlier-commenced, completed, or earlier-approved construction project needs to be altered, relocated, or have additional protection facilities, the party of the later-commenced or later-approved construction project shall bear the additional costs incurred.  
Where a pipeline construction project intersects with another construction project, the parties of both construction projects shall negotiate and determine the construction operation plan, sign a safety protection agreement, and assign specialized personnel to supervise and guide each other's construction on site.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　经依法批准的管道建设工程，需要通过正在建设的其他建设工程的，其他工程建设单位应当按照管道建设工程的需要，预留管道通道或者预建管道通过设施，管道企业应当承担由此增加的费用。  
经依法批准的其他建设工程，需要通过正在建设的管道建设工程的，管道建设单位应当按照其他建设工程的需要，预留通道或者预建相关设施，其他工程建设单位应当承担由此增加的费用。

**Article 45** Where a pipeline construction project approved according to law needs to pass through another construction project under construction, the other construction unit shall reserve a pipeline passage or pre-build pipeline crossing facilities according to the needs of the pipeline construction project, and the pipeline enterprise shall bear the additional costs incurred.  
Where another construction project approved according to law needs to pass through a pipeline construction project under construction, the pipeline construction unit shall reserve a passage or pre-build relevant facilities according to the needs of the other construction project, and the other construction unit shall bear the additional costs incurred.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　管道建设工程通过矿产资源开采区域的，管道企业应当与矿产资源开采企业协商确定管道的安全防护方案，需要矿产资源开采企业按照管道安全防护要求预建防护设施或者采取其他防护措施的，管道企业应当承担由此增加的费用。  
矿产资源开采企业未按照约定预建防护设施或者采取其他防护措施，造成地面塌陷、裂缝、沉降等地质灾害，致使管道需要改建、搬迁或者采取其他防护措施的，矿产资源开采企业应当承担由此增加的费用。

**Article 46** Where a pipeline construction project passes through a mineral resource mining area, the pipeline enterprise shall negotiate with the mineral resource mining enterprise to determine a pipeline safety protection plan. If the mineral resource mining enterprise needs to pre-build protection facilities or take other protection measures according to pipeline safety protection requirements, the pipeline enterprise shall bear the additional costs incurred.  
If the mineral resource mining enterprise fails to pre-build protection facilities or take other protection measures as agreed, causing geological disasters such as ground subsidence, cracks, or settlement, thereby requiring the pipeline to be altered, relocated, or have other protection measures taken, the mineral resource mining enterprise shall bear the additional costs incurred.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　铁路、公路等建设工程修建防洪、分流等水工防护设施，可能影响管道保护的，应当事先通知管道企业并注意保护下游已建成的管道水工防护设施。  
建设工程修建防洪、分流等水工防护设施，使下游已建成的管道水工防护设施的功能受到影响，需要新建、改建、扩建管道水工防护设施的，工程建设单位应当承担由此增加的费用。

**Article 47** Where railway, highway, or other construction projects build hydraulic protection facilities such as flood control or diversion facilities that may affect pipeline protection, they shall notify the pipeline enterprise in advance and pay attention to protecting the downstream completed pipeline hydraulic protection facilities.  
Where the construction of hydraulic protection facilities such as flood control or diversion facilities affects the function of downstream completed pipeline hydraulic protection facilities, requiring new construction, alteration, or expansion of pipeline hydraulic protection facilities, the construction unit shall bear the additional costs incurred.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门制定防洪、泄洪方案应当兼顾管道的保护。  
需要在管道通过的区域泄洪的，县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门应当在泄洪方案确定后，及时将泄洪量和泄洪时间通知本级人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门和管道企业或者向社会公告。主管管道保护工作的部门和管道企业应当对管道采取防洪保护措施。

**Article 48** The water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, when formulating flood control and flood discharge plans, take into account the protection of pipelines.  
If flood discharge is required in areas where pipelines pass, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, after the flood discharge plan is determined, promptly notify the department in charge of pipeline protection work of the people's government at the same level and the pipeline enterprise of the flood discharge volume and time, or make a public announcement. The department in charge of pipeline protection work and the pipeline enterprise shall take flood control protection measures for the pipelines.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　管道与航道相遇，确需在航道中修建管道防护设施的，应当进行通航标准技术论证，并经航道主管部门批准。管道防护设施完工后，应经航道主管部门验收。  
进行前款规定的施工作业，应当在批准的施工区域内设置航标，航标的设置和维护费用由管道企业承担。

**Article 49** Where a pipeline intersects with a waterway and it is truly necessary to build pipeline protection facilities in the waterway, a technical demonstration of navigation standards shall be conducted and approval from the competent waterway authority shall be obtained. After the pipeline protection facilities are completed, they shall be inspected and accepted by the competent waterway authority.  
For construction operations under the preceding paragraph, navigation marks shall be set up within the approved construction area. The costs for setting up and maintaining the navigation marks shall be borne by the pipeline enterprise.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　管道企业有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予处分：  
（一）未依照本法规定对管道进行巡护、检测和维修的；  
（二）对不符合安全使用条件的管道未及时更新、改造或者停止使用的；  
（三）未依照本法规定设置、修复或者更新有关管道标志的；  
（四）未依照本法规定将管道竣工测量图报人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门备案的；  
（五）未制定本企业管道事故应急预案，或者未将本企业管道事故应急预案报人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门备案的；  
（六）发生管道事故，未采取有效措施消除或者减轻事故危害的；  
（七）未对停止运行、封存、报废的管道采取必要的安全防护措施的。  
管道企业违反本法规定的行为同时违反建设工程质量管理、安全生产、消防等其他法律的，依照其他法律的规定处罚。  
管道企业给他人合法权益造成损害的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 50** Where a pipeline enterprise commits any of the following acts, the department in charge of pipeline protection work of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; and the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions:  
(1) failing to patrol, inspect, and maintain pipelines in accordance with the provisions of this Law;  
(2) failing to timely update, alter, or deactivate pipelines that do not meet safe operation conditions;  
(3) failing to set up, repair, or update relevant pipeline signs in accordance with the provisions of this Law;  
(4) failing to report the as-built survey drawings of pipelines to the department in charge of pipeline protection work of the people's government for record in accordance with the provisions of this Law;  
(5) failing to formulate an emergency response plan for pipeline accidents of the enterprise, or failing to report such emergency response plan to the department in charge of pipeline protection work of the people's government for record;  
(6) failing to take effective measures to eliminate or mitigate the harm of a pipeline accident after it occurs;  
(7) failing to take necessary safety protection measures for deactivated, sealed, or scrapped pipelines.  
If the act of a pipeline enterprise that violates the provisions of this Law also violates other laws such as those on construction project quality management, work safety, and fire protection, it shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of such other laws.  
If a pipeline enterprise causes damage to the lawful rights and interests of others, it shall bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　采用移动、切割、打孔、砸撬、拆卸等手段损坏管道或者盗窃、哄抢管道输送、泄漏、排放的石油、天然气，尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 51** If a person uses means such as moving, cutting, drilling, smashing, prying, or disassembling to damage pipelines or steals or plunders petroleum or natural gas transported, leaked, or discharged through pipelines, which does not constitute a crime, he or she shall be given a public security penalty according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反[本法第二十九条](#art-29)、第三十条、第三十二条或者第三十三条第一款的规定，实施危害管道安全行为的，由县级以上地方人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门责令停止违法行为；情节较重的，对单位处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款，对个人处二百元以上二千元以下的罚款；对违法修建的建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施限期拆除；逾期未拆除的，由县级以上地方人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门组织拆除，所需费用由违法行为人承担。

**Article 52** Where a person violates the provisions of Article 29, Article 30, Article 32, or paragraph 1 of Article 33 of this Law and commits an act endangering pipeline safety, the department in charge of pipeline protection work of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the unit shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan, and the individual shall be fined not less than 200 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan; the illegally constructed buildings, structures, or other facilities shall be ordered to be removed within a time limit; if removal is not carried out within the time limit, the department in charge of pipeline protection work of the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize the removal, and the costs incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　未经依法批准，进行[本法第三十三条](#art-33)第二款或者第三十五条规定的施工作业的，由县级以上地方人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门责令停止违法行为；情节较重的，处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款；对违法修建的危害管道安全的建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施限期拆除；逾期未拆除的，由县级以上地方人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门组织拆除，所需费用由违法行为人承担。

**Article 53** Where construction operations under paragraph 2 of Article 33 or Article 35 of this Law are carried out without approval according to law, the department in charge of pipeline protection work of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; the illegally constructed buildings, structures, or other facilities that endanger pipeline safety shall be ordered to be removed within a time limit; if removal is not carried out within the time limit, the department in charge of pipeline protection work of the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize the removal, and the costs incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府主管管道保护工作的部门责令改正；情节严重的，处二百元以上一千元以下的罚款：  
（一）擅自开启、关闭管道阀门的；  
（二）移动、毁损、涂改管道标志的；  
（三）在埋地管道上方巡查便道上行驶重型车辆的；  
（四）在地面管道线路、架空管道线路和管桥上行走或者放置重物的；  
（五）阻碍依法进行的管道建设的。

**Article 54** Where a person violates the provisions of this Law and commits any of the following acts, the department in charge of pipeline protection work of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order him to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 200 yuan but not more than 1,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) opening or closing pipeline valves without authorization;  
(2) moving, damaging, or altering pipeline signs;  
(3) driving heavy vehicles on the inspection path above buried pipelines;  
(4) walking or placing heavy objects on above-ground pipeline lines, overhead pipeline lines, or pipe bridges;  
(5) obstructing pipeline construction carried out according to law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本法规定，实施危害管道安全的行为，给管道企业造成损害的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 55** Where a person violates the provisions of this Law, commits an act endangering pipeline safety, and causes damage to a pipeline enterprise, he shall bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　县级以上地方人民政府及其主管管道保护工作的部门或者其他有关部门，违反本法规定，对应当组织排除的管道外部安全隐患不及时组织排除，发现危害管道安全的行为或者接到对危害管道安全行为的举报后不依法予以查处，或者有其他不依照本法规定履行职责的行为的，由其上级机关责令改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 56** Where the local people's government at or above the county level, its department in charge of pipeline protection work, or other relevant departments violate the provisions of this Law by failing to timely organize the elimination of external safety hazards of pipelines that should be eliminated, failing to investigate and deal with acts endangering pipeline safety according to law after discovering such acts or receiving reports on such acts, or have other acts of not performing their duties in accordance with this Law, their superior organs shall order them to make corrections, and the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 57** Where a violation of this Law constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　本法所称管道附属设施包括：  
（一）管道的加压站、加热站、计量站、集油站、集气站、输油站、输气站、配气站、处理场、清管站、阀室、阀井、放空设施、油库、储气库、装卸栈桥、装卸场；  
（二）管道的水工防护设施、防风设施、防雷设施、抗震设施、通信设施、安全监控设施、电力设施、管堤、管桥以及管道专用涵洞、隧道等穿跨越设施；  
（三）管道的阴极保护站、阴极保护测试桩、阳极地床、杂散电流排流站等防腐设施；  
（四）管道穿越铁路、公路的检漏装置；  
（五）管道的其他附属设施。

**Article 58** For the purposes of this Law, pipeline ancillary facilities include:  
(1) booster stations, heating stations, metering stations, oil gathering stations, gas gathering stations, oil delivery stations, gas delivery stations, gas distribution stations, treatment yards, pigging stations, valve chambers, valve wells, venting facilities, oil depots, gas storage depots, loading/unloading trestles, and loading/unloading yards of pipelines;  
(2) hydraulic protection facilities, wind protection facilities, lightning protection facilities, seismic protection facilities, communication facilities, safety monitoring facilities, power facilities, pipe embankments, pipe bridges, and crossing facilities such as culverts and tunnels dedicated to pipelines;  
(3) anticorrosion facilities such as cathodic protection stations, cathodic protection test posts, anode beds, and stray current drainage stations of pipelines;  
(4) leak detection devices for pipelines crossing railways and highways;  
(5) other ancillary facilities of pipelines.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　本法施行前在管道保护距离内已建成的人口密集场所和易燃易爆物品的生产、经营、存储场所，应当由所在地人民政府根据当地的实际情况，有计划、分步骤地进行搬迁、清理或者采取必要的防护措施。需要已建成的管道改建、搬迁或者采取必要的防护措施的，应当与管道企业协商确定补偿方案。

**Article 59** For densely populated places and places for production, operation, and storage of inflammable and explosive articles that have been built within the pipeline protection distance before this Law comes into force, the local people's government shall, based on the actual local conditions, carry out relocation, clearance, or take necessary protection measures in a planned and step-by-step manner. If existing pipelines need to be altered, relocated, or have necessary protection measures taken, the compensation plan shall be negotiated and determined with the pipeline enterprise.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国务院可以根据海上石油、天然气管道的具体情况，制定海上石油、天然气管道保护的特别规定。

**Article 60** The State Council may, based on the specific circumstances of offshore oil and natural gas pipelines, formulate special provisions for the protection of offshore oil and natural gas pipelines.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　本法自2010年10月1日起施行。

**Article 61** This Law shall come into force as of October 1, 2010.
