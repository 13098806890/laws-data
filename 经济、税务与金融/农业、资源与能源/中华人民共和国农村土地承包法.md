### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了巩固和完善以家庭承包经营为基础、统分结合的双层经营体制，保持农村土地承包关系稳定并长久不变，维护农村土地承包经营当事人的合法权益，促进农业、农村经济发展和农村社会和谐稳定，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of consolidating and improving the two-tier management system that combines centralized and decentralized management on the basis of household contract management, maintaining the stability and permanence of rural land contractual relationships, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of the parties to rural land contract and management, and promoting the development of agriculture and the rural economy as well as the harmony and stability of rural society.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称农村土地，是指农民集体所有和国家所有依法由农民集体使用的耕地、林地、草地，以及其他依法用于农业的土地。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于国有土地开荒后用于农耕的土地使用权转让合同纠纷案件如何适用法律问题的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于国有土地开荒后用于农耕的土地使用权转让合同纠纷案件如何适用法律问题的批复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "rural land" means land owned by peasant collectives or owned by the state but used by peasant collectives according to law, including farmland, woodland, and grassland, as well as other land used for agriculture according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家实行农村土地承包经营制度。  
农村土地承包采取农村集体经济组织内部的家庭承包方式，不宜采取家庭承包方式的荒山、荒沟、荒丘、荒滩等农村土地，可以采取招标、拍卖、公开协商等方式承包。

**Article 3** The state shall practice the system of rural land contract and management.  
Rural land contract shall be in the form of household contract within a rural collective economic organization. For rural land such as barren mountains, barren gullies, barren hills, and barren beaches that are unsuitable for the household contract form, contract may be undertaken through bidding, auction, public consultation, or other means.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　农村土地承包后，土地的所有权性质不变。承包地不得买卖。

**Article 4** After rural land is contracted, the nature of land ownership shall remain unchanged. Contracted land shall not be bought or sold.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　农村集体经济组织成员有权依法承包由本集体经济组织发包的农村土地。  
任何组织和个人不得剥夺和非法限制农村集体经济组织成员承包土地的权利。

**Article 5** Members of a rural collective economic organization shall have the right to contract, according to law, the rural land offered for contract by the said collective economic organization.  
No organization or individual may deprive or illegally restrict the right of members of a rural collective economic organization to contract land.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　农村土地承包，妇女与男子享有平等的权利。承包中应当保护妇女的合法权益，任何组织和个人不得剥夺、侵害妇女应当享有的土地承包经营权。

**Article 6** Women shall enjoy equal rights with men in rural land contract. The legitimate rights and interests of women shall be protected in contracting. No organization or individual may deprive or infringe upon the right of women to contract and manage land that they shall enjoy.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　农村土地承包应当坚持公开、公平、公正的原则，正确处理国家、集体、个人三者的利益关系。

**Article 7** The principles of openness, fairness, and impartiality shall be adhered to in rural land contract, and the interests of the state, the collective, and the individual shall be properly handled.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家保护集体土地所有者的合法权益，保护承包方的土地承包经营权，任何组织和个人不得侵犯。

**Article 8** The state shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of the owners of collective land and protect the contractual right to land management of the contractor. No organization or individual may infringe upon them.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　承包方承包土地后，享有土地承包经营权，可以自己经营，也可以保留土地承包权，流转其承包地的土地经营权，由他人经营。

**Article 9** After contracting land, the contractor shall enjoy the contractual right to land management and may either manage it by himself or herself, or retain the right to land contract and transfer the right to land management of the contracted land for management by others.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家保护承包方依法、自愿、有偿流转土地经营权，保护土地经营权人的合法权益，任何组织和个人不得侵犯。

**Article 10** The state shall protect the contractor's right to transfer the right to land management voluntarily, according to law, and with compensation, and protect the legitimate rights and interests of the holder of the right to land management. No organization or individual may infringe upon them.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　农村土地承包经营应当遵守法律、法规，保护土地资源的合理开发和可持续利用。未经依法批准不得将承包地用于非农建设。  
国家鼓励增加对土地的投入，培肥地力，提高农业生产能力。

**Article 11** Rural land contract and management operations shall comply with laws and regulations, protect the rational development and sustainable utilization of land resources. Contracted land shall not be used for non-agricultural construction without approval according to law.  
The state encourages increased investment in land, improvement of soil fertility, and enhancement of agricultural production capacity.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院农业农村、林业和草原主管部门分别依照国务院规定的职责负责全国农村土地承包经营及承包经营合同管理的指导。  
县级以上地方人民政府农业农村、林业和草原等主管部门分别依照各自职责，负责本行政区域内农村土地承包经营及承包经营合同管理。  
乡（镇）人民政府负责本行政区域内农村土地承包经营及承包经营合同管理。

**Article 12** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland of the State Council shall, respectively in accordance with the responsibilities prescribed by the State Council, be responsible for the guidance of the nationwide management of rural land contract and management and contract management.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, respectively in accordance with their respective responsibilities, be responsible for the management of rural land contract and management and contract management within their respective administrative regions.  
The people's government of a township (town) shall be responsible for the management of rural land contract and management and contract management within its administrative region.

### 第二章　家庭承包

#### 第一节　发包方和承包方的权利和义务

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　农民集体所有的土地依法属于村农民集体所有的，由村集体经济组织或者村民委员会发包；已经分别属于村内两个以上农村集体经济组织的农民集体所有的，由村内各该农村集体经济组织或者村民小组发包。村集体经济组织或者村民委员会发包的，不得改变村内各集体经济组织农民集体所有的土地的所有权。  
国家所有依法由农民集体使用的农村土地，由使用该土地的农村集体经济组织、村民委员会或者村民小组发包。

**Article 13** Land owned by peasant collectives according to law and belonging to the collective ownership of the village peasants shall be contracted out by the village collective economic organization or the villagers committee; where such land has already been separately owned by the collective of peasants of two or more rural collective economic organizations within the village, it shall be contracted out by each such rural collective economic organization or villagers group within the village. Where the village collective economic organization or villagers committee contracts out the land, it shall not change the ownership of the land owned by the collectives of peasants of each collective economic organization within the village.  
Rural land owned by the state and used by peasant collectives according to law shall be contracted out by the rural collective economic organization, villagers committee or villagers group that uses such land.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　发包方享有下列权利：  
（一）发包本集体所有的或者国家所有依法由本集体使用的农村土地；  
（二）监督承包方依照承包合同约定的用途合理利用和保护土地；  
（三）制止承包方损害承包地和农业资源的行为；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他权利。

**Article 14** The party that contracts out the land shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) To contract out rural land owned by the collective or owned by the state and used by the collective according to law;  
(2) To supervise the contractor in reasonably using and protecting the land in accordance with the purposes stipulated in the contract;  
(3) To stop the contractor from damaging the contracted land and agricultural resources;  
(4) Other rights as provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　发包方承担下列义务：  
（一）维护承包方的土地承包经营权，不得非法变更、解除承包合同；  
（二）尊重承包方的生产经营自主权，不得干涉承包方依法进行正常的生产经营活动；  
（三）依照承包合同约定为承包方提供生产、技术、信息等服务；  
（四）执行县、乡（镇）土地利用总体规划，组织本集体经济组织内的农业基础设施建设；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他义务。

**Article 15** The party that contracts out the land shall assume the following obligations:  
(1) To maintain the contractor's right to land contractual management, and shall not unlawfully modify or rescind the contract;  
(2) To respect the contractor's autonomy in production and operation, and shall not interfere with the contractor's normal production and operation activities carried out according to law;  
(3) To provide services such as production, technology, and information to the contractor in accordance with the contract;  
(4) To implement the overall land use plan of the county, township (town), and organize the construction of agricultural infrastructure within the collective economic organization;  
(5) Other obligations as provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　家庭承包的承包方是本集体经济组织的农户。  
农户内家庭成员依法平等享有承包土地的各项权益。

**Article 16** The contractor under a household contract shall be the rural household of the collective economic organization.  
The family members within the household shall enjoy equally according to law all rights and interests in the contracted land.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　承包方享有下列权利：  
（一）依法享有承包地使用、收益的权利，有权自主组织生产经营和处置产品；  
（二）依法互换、转让土地承包经营权；  
（三）依法流转土地经营权；  
（四）承包地被依法征收、征用、占用的，有权依法获得相应的补偿；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他权利。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村土地承包纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释.md#art-8" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村土地承包纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释》第8条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 17** The contractor shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) To enjoy according to law the rights to use and benefit from the contracted land, and have the right to independently organize production and operation and dispose of products;  
(2) To exchange or transfer the right to land contractual management according to law;  
(3) To transfer the right to land management according to law;  
(4) Where the contracted land is lawfully expropriated, requisitioned, or occupied, to have the right to obtain corresponding compensation according to law;  
(5) Other rights as provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　承包方承担下列义务：  
（一）维持土地的农业用途，未经依法批准不得用于非农建设；  
（二）依法保护和合理利用土地，不得给土地造成永久性损害；  
（三）法律、行政法规规定的其他义务。

**Article 18** The contractor shall assume the following obligations:  
(1) To maintain the agricultural use of the land, and shall not use it for non-agricultural construction without approval according to law;  
(2) To protect and rationally use the land according to law, and shall not cause permanent damage to the land;  
(3) Other obligations as provided by laws and administrative regulations.

#### 第二节　承包的原则和程序

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　土地承包应当遵循以下原则：  
（一）按照规定统一组织承包时，本集体经济组织成员依法平等地行使承包土地的权利，也可以自愿放弃承包土地的权利；  
（二）民主协商，公平合理；  
（三）承包方案应当按照[本法第十三条](#art-13)的规定，依法经本集体经济组织成员的村民会议三分之二以上成员或者三分之二以上村民代表的同意；  
（四）承包程序合法。

**Article 19** Land contractual management shall follow the following principles:  
(1) When contracting is uniformly organized according to regulations, members of the collective economic organization shall exercise equally according to law the right to contract land, and may voluntarily waive the right to contract land;  
(2) Democratic consultation, fairness and reasonableness;  
(3) The contracting plan shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 13 of this Law, be subject to the consent of more than two-thirds of the members of the villagers meeting of the members of the collective economic organization or more than two-thirds of the villagers' representatives according to law;  
(4) The contracting procedure shall be lawful.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　土地承包应当按照以下程序进行：  
（一）本集体经济组织成员的村民会议选举产生承包工作小组；  
（二）承包工作小组依照法律、法规的规定拟订并公布承包方案；  
（三）依法召开本集体经济组织成员的村民会议，讨论通过承包方案；  
（四）公开组织实施承包方案；  
（五）签订承包合同。

**Article 20** Land contractual management shall be carried out in accordance with the following procedures:  
(1) The villagers meeting of the members of the collective economic organization shall elect a contracting working group;  
(2) The contracting working group shall draft and publish the contracting plan in accordance with laws and regulations;  
(3) The villagers meeting of the members of the collective economic organization shall be convened according to law to discuss and approve the contracting plan;  
(4) The contracting plan shall be implemented openly;  
(5) A contract shall be signed.

#### 第三节　承包期限和承包合同

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　耕地的承包期为三十年。草地的承包期为三十年至五十年。林地的承包期为三十年至七十年。  
前款规定的耕地承包期届满后再延长三十年，草地、林地承包期届满后依照前款规定相应延长。

**Article 21** The term of contract for cultivated land shall be thirty years. The term of contract for grassland shall be from thirty to fifty years. The term of contract for forestland shall be from thirty to seventy years.  
Upon expiration of the term for cultivated land as provided in the preceding paragraph, it shall be extended for another thirty years; upon expiration of the term for grassland and forestland, it shall be extended correspondingly according to the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　发包方应当与承包方签订书面承包合同。  
承包合同一般包括以下条款：  
（一）发包方、承包方的名称，发包方负责人和承包方代表的姓名、住所；  
（二）承包土地的名称、坐落、面积、质量等级；  
（三）承包期限和起止日期；  
（四）承包土地的用途；  
（五）发包方和承包方的权利和义务；  
（六）违约责任。

**Article 22** The party that contracts out the land shall conclude a written contract with the contractor.  
A contract generally includes the following clauses:  
(1) The names of the party contracting out and the contractor, the names and domiciles of the responsible person of the party contracting out and the representative of the contractor;  
(2) The name, location, area, and quality grade of the contracted land;  
(3) The term of contract and the commencement and expiration dates;  
(4) The purpose of the contracted land;  
(5) The rights and obligations of the party contracting out and the contractor;  
(6) Liability for breach of contract.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　承包合同自成立之日起生效。承包方自承包合同生效时取得土地承包经营权。

**Article 23** A contract shall take effect as of the date of its formation. The contractor shall obtain the right to land contractual management upon the contract taking effect.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家对耕地、林地和草地等实行统一登记，登记机构应当向承包方颁发土地承包经营权证或者林权证等证书，并登记造册，确认土地承包经营权。  
土地承包经营权证或者林权证等证书应当将具有土地承包经营权的全部家庭成员列入。  
登记机构除按规定收取证书工本费外，不得收取其他费用。

**Article 24** The State shall implement unified registration for cultivated land, forestland and grassland, and the registration institution shall issue certificates such as the certificate of right to land contractual management or forest ownership certificate to the contractor, and register them in a book to confirm the right to land contractual management.  
The certificate of right to land contractual management or forest ownership certificate shall list all family members who have the right to land contractual management.  
The registration institution shall not charge any fees other than the cost of the certificate as prescribed.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　承包合同生效后，发包方不得因承办人或者负责人的变动而变更或者解除，也不得因集体经济组织的分立或者合并而变更或者解除。

**Article 25** After a contract takes effect, the party that contracts out the land shall not modify or rescind the contract due to changes in the person handling the matter or the responsible person, nor shall it modify or rescind the contract due to the split or merger of the collective economic organization.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家机关及其工作人员不得利用职权干涉农村土地承包或者变更、解除承包合同。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村土地承包纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释.md#art-5" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村土地承包纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释》第5条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村土地承包纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释.md#art-9" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村土地承包纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释》第9条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 26** State organs and their staff shall not use their functions and powers to interfere with rural land contractual management or modify or rescind contracts.

#### 第四节　土地承包经营权的保护和互换、转让

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　承包期内，发包方不得收回承包地。  
国家保护进城农户的土地承包经营权。不得以退出土地承包经营权作为农户进城落户的条件。  
承包期内，承包农户进城落户的，引导支持其按照自愿有偿原则依法在本集体经济组织内转让土地承包经营权或者将承包地交回发包方，也可以鼓励其流转土地经营权。  
承包期内，承包方交回承包地或者发包方依法收回承包地时，承包方对其在承包地上投入而提高土地生产能力的，有权获得相应的补偿。

**Article 27** During the term of contract, the party that contracts out the land shall not take back the contracted land.  
The State protects the right to land contractual management of rural households that move into cities. The withdrawal of the right to land contractual management shall not be a condition for the household to settle in the city.  
During the term of contract, where a contracting rural household settles in a city, it shall be guided and supported to transfer the right to land contractual management within the collective economic organization according to law on a voluntary and compensatory basis, or to return the contracted land to the party contracting out, and it may also be encouraged to transfer the right to land management.  
During the term of contract, where the contractor returns the contracted land or the party contracting out lawfully takes back the contracted land, the contractor shall have the right to obtain corresponding compensation for the investment made on the contracted land that has increased the land productivity.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　承包期内，发包方不得调整承包地。  
承包期内，因自然灾害严重毁损承包地等特殊情形对个别农户之间承包的耕地和草地需要适当调整的，必须经本集体经济组织成员的村民会议三分之二以上成员或者三分之二以上村民代表的同意，并报乡（镇）人民政府和县级人民政府农业农村、林业和草原等主管部门批准。承包合同中约定不得调整的，按照其约定。

**Article 28** During the term of contract, the party that contracts out the land shall not adjust the contracted land.  
During the term of contract, where due to special circumstances such as severe damage to contracted land caused by natural disasters, proper adjustment of cultivated land and grassland contracted to individual households is necessary, it must be subject to the consent of more than two-thirds of the members of the villagers meeting of the members of the collective economic organization or more than two-thirds of the villagers' representatives, and reported to the people's government of township (town) and the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the people's government at the county level for approval. Where the contract stipulates that no adjustment shall be made, the contract shall prevail.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　下列土地应当用于调整承包土地或者承包给新增人口：  
（一）集体经济组织依法预留的机动地；  
（二）通过依法开垦等方式增加的；  
（三）发包方依法收回和承包方依法、自愿交回的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村土地承包纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释.md#art-10" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村土地承包纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释》第10条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 29** The following land shall be used for adjusting contracted land or for contracting to newly added population:  
(1) Reserve land lawfully reserved by the collective economic organization;  
(2) Land increased through lawful reclamation or other means;  
(3) Land lawfully taken back by the party contracting out and lawfully and voluntarily returned by the contractor.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　承包期内，承包方可以自愿将承包地交回发包方。承包方自愿交回承包地的，可以获得合理补偿，但是应当提前半年以书面形式通知发包方。承包方在承包期内交回承包地的，在承包期内不得再要求承包土地。

**Article 30** During the term of contract, the contractor may voluntarily return the contracted land to the party that contracts out the land. Where the contractor voluntarily returns the contracted land, it may obtain reasonable compensation, but shall notify the party contracting out in writing six months in advance. Where the contractor returns the contracted land during the term of contract, it shall not request to contract land again during the term of contract.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　承包期内，妇女结婚，在新居住地未取得承包地的，发包方不得收回其原承包地；妇女离婚或者丧偶，仍在原居住地生活或者不在原居住地生活但在新居住地未取得承包地的，发包方不得收回其原承包地。

**Article 31** During the term of contract, where a woman marries and has not obtained contracted land in her new place of residence, the party that contracts out the land shall not take back her original contracted land; where a woman divorces or becomes widowed and still lives in the original place of residence, or does not live in the original place of residence but has not obtained contracted land in the new place of residence, the party that contracts out the land shall not take back her original contracted land.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　承包人应得的承包收益，依照继承法的规定继承。  
林地承包的承包人死亡，其继承人可以在承包期内继续承包。

**Article 32** The contractual benefits due to the contractor shall be inherited in accordance with the provisions of the inheritance law.  
Where the contractor under a forestland contract dies, his heir may continue the contract within the term of contract.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　承包方之间为方便耕种或者各自需要，可以对属于同一集体经济组织的土地的土地承包经营权进行互换，并向发包方备案。

**Article 33** For the convenience of cultivation or respective needs, the contracting parties may exchange the land contract management rights of land belonging to the same collective economic organization, and shall report to the party that issued the contract for recordation.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　经发包方同意，承包方可以将全部或者部分的土地承包经营权转让给本集体经济组织的其他农户，由该农户同发包方确立新的承包关系，原承包方与发包方在该土地上的承包关系即行终止。

**Article 34** With the consent of the party that issued the contract, the contracting party may transfer all or part of the land contract management right to other farming households within the same collective economic organization. The new farming household shall establish a new contractual relationship with the party that issued the contract, and the original contractual relationship between the original contracting party and the party that issued the contract with respect to the land shall terminate immediately.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　土地承包经营权互换、转让的，当事人可以向登记机构申请登记。未经登记，不得对抗善意第三人。

**Article 35** Where land contract management rights are exchanged or transferred, the parties may apply to the registration authority for registration. Without registration, such rights shall not be valid against a bona fide third party.

#### 第五节　土地经营权

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　承包方可以自主决定依法采取出租（转包）、入股或者其他方式向他人流转土地经营权，并向发包方备案。

**Article 36** The contracting party may independently decide to transfer the land management right to others by means of lease (subcontracting), equity contribution, or other methods according to law, and shall report to the party that issued the contract for recordation.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　土地经营权人有权在合同约定的期限内占有农村土地，自主开展农业生产经营并取得收益。

**Article 37** The holder of the land management right shall have the right to possess the rural land within the term agreed upon in the contract, independently carry out agricultural production and operation, and obtain proceeds.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　土地经营权流转应当遵循以下原则：  
（一）依法、自愿、有偿，任何组织和个人不得强迫或者阻碍土地经营权流转；  
（二）不得改变土地所有权的性质和土地的农业用途，不得破坏农业综合生产能力和农业生态环境；  
（三）流转期限不得超过承包期的剩余期限；  
（四）受让方须有农业经营能力或者资质；  
（五）在同等条件下，本集体经济组织成员享有优先权。

**Article 38** The transfer of land management rights shall follow the following principles:  
(1) being in accordance with law, on a voluntary basis, and with compensation; no organization or individual may compel or hinder the transfer of land management rights;  
(2) the nature of land ownership and the agricultural use of the land shall not be changed, nor shall the comprehensive agricultural production capacity and the agricultural ecological environment be undermined;  
(3) the transfer term shall not exceed the remaining term of the contract period;  
(4) the transferee shall have agricultural operation capability or qualifications;  
(5) under equal conditions, members of the collective economic organization shall have priority.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　土地经营权流转的价款，应当由当事人双方协商确定。流转的收益归承包方所有，任何组织和个人不得擅自截留、扣缴。

**Article 39** The price for the transfer of land management rights shall be determined through negotiation between the parties. The proceeds from the transfer shall belong to the contracting party, and no organization or individual may unjustifiably withhold or deduct such proceeds.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　土地经营权流转，当事人双方应当签订书面流转合同。  
土地经营权流转合同一般包括以下条款：  
（一）双方当事人的姓名、住所；  
（二）流转土地的名称、坐落、面积、质量等级；  
（三）流转期限和起止日期；  
（四）流转土地的用途；  
（五）双方当事人的权利和义务；  
（六）流转价款及支付方式；  
（七）土地被依法征收、征用、占用时有关补偿费的归属；  
（八）违约责任。  
承包方将土地交由他人代耕不超过一年的，可以不签订书面合同。

**Article 40** For the transfer of land management rights, the parties shall conclude a written transfer contract.  
A land management right transfer contract shall generally include the following clauses:  
(1) the names and domiciles of the parties;  
(2) the name, location, area, and quality grade of the transferred land;  
(3) the transfer term and the commencement and termination dates;  
(4) the use of the transferred land;  
(5) the rights and obligations of the parties;  
(6) the transfer price and the method of payment;  
(7) the attribution of relevant compensation fees when the land is lawfully expropriated, requisitioned, or occupied;  
(8) liability for breach of contract.  
If the contracting party entrusts the land to another person for cultivation for a period not exceeding one year, a written contract may not be concluded.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　土地经营权流转期限为五年以上的，当事人可以向登记机构申请土地经营权登记。未经登记，不得对抗善意第三人。

**Article 41** Where the term of the transfer of land management rights is five years or more, the parties may apply to the registration authority for registration of the land management right. Without registration, such right shall not be valid against a bona fide third party.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　承包方不得单方解除土地经营权流转合同，但受让方有下列情形之一的除外：  
（一）擅自改变土地的农业用途；  
（二）弃耕抛荒连续两年以上；  
（三）给土地造成严重损害或者严重破坏土地生态环境；  
（四）其他严重违约行为。

**Article 42** The contracting party shall not unilaterally rescind the land management right transfer contract, except where the transferee is under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) having arbitrarily changed the agricultural use of the land;  
(2) having abandoned cultivation or left the land idle for two consecutive years or more;  
(3) having caused serious damage to the land or seriously undermined the land's ecological environment;  
(4) having committed other serious breaches of contract.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　经承包方同意，受让方可以依法投资改良土壤，建设农业生产附属、配套设施，并按照合同约定对其投资部分获得合理补偿。

**Article 43** With the consent of the contracting party, the transferee may, according to law, invest in improving the soil and building auxiliary and supporting facilities for agricultural production, and shall receive reasonable compensation for the portion of its investment in accordance with the contract.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　承包方流转土地经营权的，其与发包方的承包关系不变。

**Article 44** Where the contracting party transfers the land management right, its contractual relationship with the party that issued the contract shall remain unchanged.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上地方人民政府应当建立工商企业等社会资本通过流转取得土地经营权的资格审查、项目审核和风险防范制度。  
工商企业等社会资本通过流转取得土地经营权的，本集体经济组织可以收取适量管理费用。  
具体办法由国务院农业农村、林业和草原主管部门规定。

**Article 45** The local people's government at the county level or above shall establish a system for qualification review, project examination, and risk prevention for industrial and commercial enterprises and other social capital to obtain land management rights through transfer.  
Where industrial and commercial enterprises and other social capital obtain land management rights through transfer, the collective economic organization may collect an appropriate amount of management fees.  
The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry, and grassland under the State Council.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　经承包方书面同意，并向本集体经济组织备案，受让方可以再流转土地经营权。

**Article 46** With the written consent of the contracting party and upon reporting to the collective economic organization for recordation, the transferee may sub-transfer the land management right.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　承包方可以用承包地的土地经营权向金融机构融资担保，并向发包方备案。受让方通过流转取得的土地经营权，经承包方书面同意并向发包方备案，可以向金融机构融资担保。  
担保物权自融资担保合同生效时设立。当事人可以向登记机构申请登记；未经登记，不得对抗善意第三人。  
实现担保物权时，担保物权人有权就土地经营权优先受偿。  
土地经营权融资担保办法由国务院有关部门规定。

**Article 47** The contracting party may use the land management right of the contracted land as security for financing from a financial institution, and shall report to the party that issued the contract for recordation. Where the transferee obtains the land management right through transfer, with the written consent of the contracting party and upon reporting to the party that issued the contract for recordation, the transferee may use such right as security for financing from a financial institution.  
A security interest shall be established when the financing security contract takes effect. The parties may apply to the registration authority for registration; without registration, such security interest shall not be valid against a bona fide third party.  
When the security interest is enforced, the security interest holder shall have the right to be repaid in priority out of the land management right.  
The measures for financing security of land management rights shall be formulated by the relevant departments under the State Council.

### 第三章　其他方式的承包

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　不宜采取家庭承包方式的荒山、荒沟、荒丘、荒滩等农村土地，通过招标、拍卖、公开协商等方式承包的，适用本章规定。

**Article 48** Where rural land such as barren mountains, barren gullies, barren hills, and barren beaches that are unsuitable for the household contract form is contracted through bidding, auction, public consultation, or other means, the provisions of this Chapter shall apply.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　以其他方式承包农村土地的，应当签订承包合同，承包方取得土地经营权。当事人的权利和义务、承包期限等，由双方协商确定。以招标、拍卖方式承包的，承包费通过公开竞标、竞价确定；以公开协商等方式承包的，承包费由双方议定。

**Article 49** Where rural land is contracted by other means, a contract shall be concluded, and the contractor shall obtain the right to land management. The rights and obligations of the parties, the term of the contract, etc. shall be determined through consultation between the two parties. Where the contract is undertaken through bidding or auction, the contract fee shall be determined through open bidding or auction; where the contract is undertaken through public consultation or other means, the contract fee shall be agreed upon by the two parties.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　荒山、荒沟、荒丘、荒滩等可以直接通过招标、拍卖、公开协商等方式实行承包经营，也可以将土地经营权折股分给本集体经济组织成员后，再实行承包经营或者股份合作经营。  
承包荒山、荒沟、荒丘、荒滩的，应当遵守有关法律、行政法规的规定，防止水土流失，保护生态环境。

**Article 50** Barren mountains, barren gullies, barren hills, and barren beaches may be directly contracted through bidding, auction, public consultation, or other means, or may be operated under contract or shareholding cooperative operation after the right to land management is converted into shares and distributed to the members of the collective economic organization.  
Where barren mountains, barren gullies, barren hills, or barren beaches are contracted, the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations shall be complied with to prevent soil erosion and protect the ecological environment.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　以其他方式承包农村土地，在同等条件下，本集体经济组织成员有权优先承包。

**Article 51** Where rural land is contracted by other means, members of the collective economic organization shall have the priority right to contract under equal conditions.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　发包方将农村土地发包给本集体经济组织以外的单位或者个人承包，应当事先经本集体经济组织成员的村民会议三分之二以上成员或者三分之二以上村民代表的同意，并报乡（镇）人民政府批准。  
由本集体经济组织以外的单位或者个人承包的，应当对承包方的资信情况和经营能力进行审查后，再签订承包合同。

**Article 52** Where the contract-offering party offers rural land for contract to a unit or individual outside the collective economic organization, it shall obtain the consent of at least two-thirds of the members of the villagers' meeting of the members of the collective economic organization or at least two-thirds of the villagers' representatives in advance, and shall report to the people's government of the township (town) for approval.  
Where the contract is undertaken by a unit or individual outside the collective economic organization, the contract shall be concluded only after the credit standing and operational capacity of the contractor are examined.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　通过招标、拍卖、公开协商等方式承包农村土地，经依法登记取得权属证书的，可以依法采取出租、入股、抵押或者其他方式流转土地经营权。

**Article 53** Where rural land is contracted through bidding, auction, public consultation, or other means and the title certificate is obtained after registration according to law, the right to land management may be transferred according to law by means such as lease, shareholding, mortgage, or other means.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　依照本章规定通过招标、拍卖、公开协商等方式取得土地经营权的，该承包人死亡，其应得的承包收益，依照继承法的规定继承；在承包期内，其继承人可以继续承包。

**Article 54** Where a person who has obtained the right to land management through bidding, auction, public consultation, or other means in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter dies, his or her due proceeds from the contract shall be succeeded to in accordance with the provisions of the Law of Succession; within the term of the contract, his or her successor may continue to undertake the contract.

### 第四章　争议的解决和法律责任

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　因土地承包经营发生纠纷的，双方当事人可以通过协商解决，也可以请求村民委员会、乡（镇）人民政府等调解解决。  
当事人不愿协商、调解或者协商、调解不成的，可以向农村土地承包仲裁机构申请仲裁，也可以直接向人民法院起诉。

**Article 55** Where a dispute arises over land contract and management, the parties may resolve it through consultation, or may request mediation by the villagers' committee, the people's government of the township (town), or other entities.  
Where the parties are unwilling to consult or mediate, or consultation or mediation fails, they may apply to a rural land contract arbitration institution for arbitration, or may directly bring a prosecution to the People's Court.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　任何组织和个人侵害土地承包经营权、土地经营权的，应当承担民事责任。

**Article 56** Any organization or individual that infringes upon the right to land contractual management or the right to land management shall bear civil liability.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　发包方有下列行为之一的，应当承担停止侵害、排除妨碍、消除危险、返还财产、恢复原状、赔偿损失等民事责任：  
（一）干涉承包方依法享有的生产经营自主权；  
（二）违反本法规定收回、调整承包地；  
（三）强迫或者阻碍承包方进行土地承包经营权的互换、转让或者土地经营权流转；  
（四）假借少数服从多数强迫承包方放弃或者变更土地承包经营权；  
（五）以划分“口粮田”和“责任田”等为由收回承包地搞招标承包；  
（六）将承包地收回抵顶欠款；  
（七）剥夺、侵害妇女依法享有的土地承包经营权；  
（八）其他侵害土地承包经营权的行为。

**Article 57** Where the party awarding the contract commits any of the following acts, it shall bear civil liability such as ceasing the infringement, removing the obstruction, eliminating the danger, returning the property, restoring the original condition, and compensating for losses:  
(1) interfering with the contractor's right to independently conduct production and operations enjoyed by the contractor according to law;  
(2) recovering or adjusting the contracted land in violation of the provisions of this Law;  
(3) forcing or obstructing the contractor from conducting the exchange, transfer of the right to land contractual management or the circulation of the right to land management;  
(4) forcing the contractor to abandon or change the right to land contractual management under the pretext of the minority submitting to the majority;  
(5) recovering the contracted land for the purpose of bidding contracting on the pretext of dividing the land into "grain ration fields" and "responsibility fields", etc.;  
(6) recovering the contracted land to offset debts;  
(7) depriving or infringing upon the women's right to land contractual management enjoyed according to law;  
(8) other acts infringing upon the right to land contractual management.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　承包合同中违背承包方意愿或者违反法律、行政法规有关不得收回、调整承包地等强制性规定的约定无效。

**Article 58** Any stipulation in a contract for land contractual management that is contrary to the will of the contractor or violates the mandatory provisions of laws or administrative regulations on the prohibition of recovering or adjusting the contracted land shall be void.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　当事人一方不履行合同义务或者履行义务不符合约定的，应当依法承担违约责任。

**Article 59** Where one party fails to perform its contractual obligations or its performance does not conform to the agreement, it shall bear liability for breach of contract according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　任何组织和个人强迫进行土地承包经营权互换、转让或者土地经营权流转的，该互换、转让或者流转无效。

**Article 60** Where any organization or individual forces the exchange, transfer of the right to land contractual management or the circulation of the right to land management, such exchange, transfer, or circulation shall be void.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　任何组织和个人擅自截留、扣缴土地承包经营权互换、转让或者土地经营权流转收益的，应当退还。

**Article 61** Any organization or individual that, without authorization, withholds or deducts the proceeds from the exchange, transfer of the right to land contractual management or the circulation of the right to land management shall return such proceeds.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反土地管理法规，非法征收、征用、占用土地或者贪污、挪用土地征收、征用补偿费用，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；造成他人损害的，应当承担损害赔偿等责任。

**Article 62** Whoever, in violation of the laws and regulations on land administration, illegally expropriates, requisitions, or occupies land, or embezzles or misappropriates the compensation fees for land expropriation or requisition, which constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if causing damage to others, it shall bear liability for damages and other liability.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　承包方、土地经营权人违法将承包地用于非农建设的，由县级以上地方人民政府有关主管部门依法予以处罚。  
承包方给承包地造成永久性损害的，发包方有权制止，并有权要求赔偿由此造成的损失。

**Article 63** Where the contractor or the holder of the right to land management illegally uses the contracted land for non-agricultural construction, the relevant competent department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall impose a penalty according to law.  
Where the contractor causes permanent damage to the contracted land, the party awarding the contract shall have the right to stop such act and to claim compensation for the losses caused thereby.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　土地经营权人擅自改变土地的农业用途、弃耕抛荒连续两年以上、给土地造成严重损害或者严重破坏土地生态环境，承包方在合理期限内不解除土地经营权流转合同的，发包方有权要求终止土地经营权流转合同。土地经营权人对土地和土地生态环境造成的损害应当予以赔偿。

**Article 64** Where the holder of the right to land management, without authorization, changes the agricultural use of the land, abandons cultivation and leaves the land idle for two consecutive years or more, causes serious damage to the land, or seriously damages the ecological environment of the land, and the contractor fails to rescind the contract for the circulation of the right to land management within a reasonable period, the party awarding the contract shall have the right to demand termination of the contract for the circulation of the right to land management. The holder of the right to land management shall compensate for the damage caused to the land and the ecological environment of the land.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国家机关及其工作人员有利用职权干涉农村土地承包经营，变更、解除承包经营合同，干涉承包经营当事人依法享有的生产经营自主权，强迫、阻碍承包经营当事人进行土地承包经营权互换、转让或者土地经营权流转等侵害土地承包经营权、土地经营权的行为，给承包经营当事人造成损失的，应当承担损害赔偿等责任；情节严重的，由上级机关或者所在单位给予直接责任人员处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 65** Where a State organ or its staff member, by taking advantage of its/his functions and powers, interferes with the rural land contractual management, modifies or rescinds the contract for contractual management, interferes with the right of the parties to the contractual management to independently conduct production and operations enjoyed according to law, forces or obstructs the parties to the contractual management from conducting the exchange, transfer of the right to land contractual management or the circulation of the right to land management, or commits any other act infringing upon the right to land contractual management or the right to land management, and causes losses to the parties to the contractual management, it/he shall bear liability for damages and other liability; if the circumstances are serious, the person directly in charge shall be given sanctions by the superior organ or the entity where he works; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　本法实施前已经按照国家有关农村土地承包的规定承包，包括承包期限长于本法规定的，本法实施后继续有效，不得重新承包土地。未向承包方颁发土地承包经营权证或者林权证等证书的，应当补发证书。

**Article 66** Contracts concluded before the coming into force of this Law in accordance with the provisions of the State on rural land contracting, including those with a term of contract longer than that prescribed by this Law, shall remain valid after the coming into force of this Law, and no land shall be re-contracted. If no certificate of the right to land contractual management or certificate of forest right, etc. has been issued to the contractor, such certificate shall be issued retroactively.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　本法实施前已经预留机动地的，机动地面积不得超过本集体经济组织耕地总面积的百分之五。不足百分之五的，不得再增加机动地。  
本法实施前未留机动地的，本法实施后不得再留机动地。

**Article 67** Where reserve land has been set aside before the coming into force of this Law, the area of the reserve land shall not exceed five percent of the total arable land area of the collective economic organization. If it is less than five percent, no additional reserve land shall be set aside.  
Where no reserve land has been set aside before the coming into force of this Law, no reserve land may be set aside after the coming into force of this Law.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　各省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会常务委员会可以根据本法，结合本行政区域的实际情况，制定实施办法。

**Article 68** The standing committees of the people's congresses of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with this Law and in light of the actual circumstances of their respective administrative areas, formulate measures for implementation.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　确认农村集体经济组织成员身份的原则、程序等，由法律、法规规定。

**Article 69** The principles and procedures for confirming the membership status of the rural collective economic organizations shall be prescribed by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　本法自2003年3月1日起施行。

**Article 70** This Law shall come into force as of March 1, 2003.
