# 中华人民共和国农业技术推广法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2024-04-26  
**生效日期**：2024-04-26

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强农业技术推广工作，促使农业科研成果和实用技术尽快应用于农业生产，增强科技支撑保障能力，促进农业和农村经济可持续发展，实现农业现代化，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of strengthening agricultural technology extension, promoting the prompt application of agricultural scientific research achievements and practical technologies in agricultural production, enhancing scientific and technological support capabilities, facilitating the sustainable development of agriculture and the rural economy, and realizing agricultural modernization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称农业技术，是指应用于种植业、林业、畜牧业、渔业的科研成果和实用技术，包括：  
（一）良种繁育、栽培、肥料施用和养殖技术；  
（二）植物病虫害、动物疫病和其他有害生物防治技术；  
（三）农产品收获、加工、包装、贮藏、运输技术；  
（四）农业投入品安全使用、农产品质量安全技术；  
（五）农田水利、农村供排水、土壤改良与水土保持技术；  
（六）农业机械化、农用航空、农业气象和农业信息技术；  
（七）农业防灾减灾、农业资源与农业生态安全和农村能源开发利用技术；  
（八）其他农业技术。  
本法所称农业技术推广，是指通过试验、示范、培训、指导以及咨询服务等，把农业技术普及应用于农业产前、产中、产后全过程的活动。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "agricultural technology" means scientific research achievements and practical technologies applied to crop farming, forestry, animal husbandry, and fishery, including:  
(1) technologies for improved variety breeding, cultivation, fertilizer application, and aquaculture;  
(2) technologies for prevention and control of plant diseases and insect pests, animal epidemics, and other harmful organisms;  
(3) technologies for harvesting, processing, packaging, storage, and transportation of agricultural products;  
(4) technologies for safe use of agricultural inputs and quality safety of agricultural products;  
(5) technologies for farmland water conservancy, rural water supply and drainage, soil improvement, and water and soil conservation;  
(6) technologies for agricultural mechanization, agricultural aviation, agricultural meteorology, and agricultural information;  
(7) technologies for agricultural disaster prevention and mitigation, agricultural resources and agro-ecological safety, and rural energy development and utilization;  
(8) other agricultural technologies.  
For the purposes of this Law, "agricultural technology extension" means the activities of popularizing and applying agricultural technologies in the whole process of pre-production, in-production, and post-production of agriculture through experiments, demonstrations, training, guidance, and consulting services.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家扶持农业技术推广事业，加快农业技术的普及应用，发展高产、优质、高效、生态、安全农业。

**Article 3** The State shall support the cause of agricultural technology extension, accelerate the popularization and application of agricultural technologies, and develop a high-yield, high-quality, efficient, ecological, and safe agriculture.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　农业技术推广应当遵循下列原则：  
（一）有利于农业、农村经济可持续发展和增加农民收入；  
（二）尊重农业劳动者和农业生产经营组织的意愿；  
（三）因地制宜，经过试验、示范；  
（四）公益性推广与经营性推广分类管理；  
（五）兼顾经济效益、社会效益，注重生态效益。

**Article 4** Agricultural technology extension shall follow the following principles:  
(1) being conducive to the sustainable development of agriculture and the rural economy and increasing farmers' income;  
(2) respecting the will of agricultural workers and agricultural production and operation organizations;  
(3) adapting to local conditions and undergoing experiments and demonstrations;  
(4) practicing classified management of public-interest extension and commercial extension;  
(5) giving due consideration to economic and social benefits, and focusing on ecological benefits.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家鼓励和支持科技人员开发、推广应用先进的农业技术，鼓励和支持农业劳动者和农业生产经营组织应用先进的农业技术。  
国家鼓励运用现代信息技术等先进传播手段，普及农业科学技术知识，创新农业技术推广方式方法，提高推广效率。

**Article 5** The State shall encourage and support scientific and technical personnel to develop, extend, and apply advanced agricultural technologies, and encourage and support agricultural workers and agricultural production and operation organizations to apply advanced agricultural technologies.  
The State shall encourage the use of modern information technology and other advanced means of dissemination to popularize agricultural scientific and technical knowledge, innovate methods and approaches of agricultural technology extension, and improve extension efficiency.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励和支持引进国外先进的农业技术，促进农业技术推广的国际合作与交流。

**Article 6** The State shall encourage and support the introduction of advanced foreign agricultural technologies and promote international cooperation and exchanges in agricultural technology extension.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级人民政府应当加强对农业技术推广工作的领导，组织有关部门和单位采取措施，提高农业技术推广服务水平，促进农业技术推广事业的发展。

**Article 7** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen leadership over agricultural technology extension work, organize relevant departments and units to adopt measures, improve the service level of agricultural technology extension, and promote the development of the cause of agricultural technology extension.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对在农业技术推广工作中做出贡献的单位和个人，给予奖励。

**Article 8** Units and individuals that have made contributions in agricultural technology extension work shall be rewarded.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院农业农村、林业草原、水利等部门（以下统称农业技术推广部门）按照各自的职责，负责全国范围内有关的农业技术推广工作。县级以上地方各级人民政府农业技术推广部门在同级人民政府的领导下，按照各自的职责，负责本行政区域内有关的农业技术推广工作。同级人民政府其他有关部门按照各自的职责，负责农业技术推广的有关工作。

**Article 9** The agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland, water resources, and other departments under the State Council (hereinafter collectively referred to as the "agricultural technology extension departments") shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant agricultural technology extension work within the whole country. The agricultural technology extension departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, under the leadership of the people's government at the same level and in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant agricultural technology extension work within their respective administrative regions. Other relevant departments of the people's government at the same level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant work of agricultural technology extension.

### 第二章　农业技术推广体系

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　农业技术推广，实行国家农业技术推广机构与农业科研单位、有关学校、农民专业合作社、涉农企业、群众性科技组织、农民技术人员等相结合的推广体系。  
国家鼓励和支持供销合作社、其他企业事业单位、社会团体以及社会各界的科技人员，开展农业技术推广服务。

**Article 10** Agricultural technology extension shall implement an extension system combining State agricultural technology extension institutions with agricultural research institutes, relevant schools, farmers' specialized cooperatives, agriculture-related enterprises, mass scientific and technological organizations, and farmer technical personnel.  
The State shall encourage and support supply and marketing cooperatives, other enterprises and public institutions, social organizations, and scientific and technical personnel from all sectors of society to carry out agricultural technology extension services.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　各级国家农业技术推广机构属于公共服务机构，履行下列公益性职责：  
（一）各级人民政府确定的关键农业技术的引进、试验、示范；  
（二）植物病虫害、动物疫病及农业灾害的监测、预报和预防；  
（三）农产品生产过程中的检验、检测、监测咨询技术服务；  
（四）农业资源、森林资源、农业生态安全和农业投入品使用的监测服务；  
（五）水资源管理、防汛抗旱和农田水利建设技术服务；  
（六）农业公共信息和农业技术宣传教育、培训服务；  
（七）法律、法规规定的其他职责。

**Article 11** State agricultural technology extension institutions at all levels are public service institutions and shall perform the following public-interest duties:  
(1) introduction, experimentation, and demonstration of key agricultural technologies determined by people's governments at all levels;  
(2) monitoring, forecasting, and prevention of plant diseases and insect pests, animal epidemics, and agricultural disasters;  
(3) technical services for inspection, testing, monitoring, and consultation in the production process of agricultural products;  
(4) monitoring services for agricultural resources, forest resources, agro-ecological safety, and the use of agricultural inputs;  
(5) technical services for water resources management, flood control and drought relief, and farmland water conservancy construction;  
(6) services for agricultural public information and publicity, education, and training on agricultural technologies;  
(7) other functions and duties as provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　根据科学合理、集中力量的原则以及县域农业特色、森林资源、水系和水利设施分布等情况，因地制宜设置县、乡镇或者区域国家农业技术推广机构。  
乡镇国家农业技术推广机构，可以实行县级人民政府农业技术推广部门管理为主或者乡镇人民政府管理为主、县级人民政府农业技术推广部门业务指导的体制，具体由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定。

**Article 12** Based on the principles of scientific rationality and concentration of efforts, and taking into account the agricultural characteristics of counties, forest resources, water systems, and the distribution of water conservancy facilities, State agricultural technology extension institutions shall be established at the county, township, or regional level according to local conditions.  
Township State agricultural technology extension institutions may adopt a system where the agricultural technology extension department of the county people's government exercises primary management, or where the township people's government exercises primary management with the agricultural technology extension department of the county people's government providing professional guidance. The specific system shall be determined by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家农业技术推广机构的人员编制应当根据所服务区域的种养规模、服务范围和工作任务等合理确定，保证公益性职责的履行。  
国家农业技术推广机构的岗位设置应当以专业技术岗位为主。乡镇国家农业技术推广机构的岗位应当全部为专业技术岗位，县级国家农业技术推广机构的专业技术岗位不得低于机构岗位总量的百分之八十，其他国家农业技术推广机构的专业技术岗位不得低于机构岗位总量的百分之七十。

**Article 13** The staffing of State agricultural technology extension institutions shall be reasonably determined based on the scale of crop farming and animal husbandry, the scope of services, and the work tasks in the areas they serve, so as to ensure the performance of their public-interest duties.  
The post setting in State agricultural technology extension institutions shall mainly consist of professional and technical posts. All posts in township State agricultural technology extension institutions shall be professional and technical posts; the number of professional and technical posts in county State agricultural technology extension institutions shall not be less than eighty percent of the total posts; and the number of professional and technical posts in other State agricultural technology extension institutions shall not be less than seventy percent of the total posts.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家农业技术推广机构的专业技术人员应当具有相应的专业技术水平，符合岗位职责要求。  
国家农业技术推广机构聘用的新进专业技术人员，应当具有大专以上有关专业学历，并通过县级以上人民政府有关部门组织的专业技术水平考核。自治县、民族乡和国家确定的连片特困地区，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关部门批准，可以聘用具有中专有关专业学历的人员或者其他具有相应专业技术水平的人员。  
国家鼓励和支持高等学校毕业生和科技人员到基层从事农业技术推广工作。各级人民政府应当采取措施，吸引人才，充实和加强基层农业技术推广队伍。

**Article 14** Professional and technical personnel of State agricultural technology extension institutions shall have corresponding professional and technical competence and meet the requirements of their posts.  
Newly employed professional and technical personnel in State agricultural technology extension institutions shall have obtained a diploma from a college or university in a relevant specialty and pass the professional and technical competence assessment organized by the relevant department of the people's government at or above the county level. In autonomous counties, ethnic townships, and contiguous destitute areas designated by the State, with the approval of the relevant department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, persons having obtained a diploma from a specialized secondary school in a relevant specialty or other persons having corresponding professional and technical competence may be employed.  
The State shall encourage and support college and university graduates and scientific and technical personnel to engage in agricultural technology extension work at the grassroots level. People's governments at all levels shall adopt measures to attract talents, and to enrich and strengthen the grassroots agricultural technology extension teams.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家鼓励和支持村农业技术服务站点和农民技术人员开展农业技术推广。对农民技术人员协助开展公益性农业技术推广活动，按照规定给予补助。  
农民技术人员经考核符合条件的，可以按照有关规定授予相应的技术职称，并发给证书。  
国家农业技术推广机构应当加强对村农业技术服务站点和农民技术人员的指导。  
村民委员会和村集体经济组织，应当推动、帮助村农业技术服务站点和农民技术人员开展工作。

**Article 15** The State shall encourage and support village-level agricultural technology service stations and farmer technical personnel to carry out agricultural technology extension. Farmer technical personnel who assist in carrying out public-interest agricultural technology extension activities shall be subsidized in accordance with relevant provisions.  
Farmer technical personnel who meet the conditions upon assessment may be conferred corresponding technical titles in accordance with relevant provisions and issued certificates.  
State agricultural technology extension institutions shall strengthen guidance to village-level agricultural technology service stations and farmer technical personnel.  
Village committees and village collective economic organizations shall promote and assist village-level agricultural technology service stations and farmer technical personnel in their work.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　农业科研单位和有关学校应当适应农村经济建设发展的需要，开展农业技术开发和推广工作，加快先进技术在农业生产中的普及应用。  
农业科研单位和有关学校应当将其科技人员从事农业技术推广工作的实绩作为工作考核和职称评定的重要内容。

**Article 16** Agricultural research institutes and relevant schools shall adapt to the needs of rural economic construction and development, carry out agricultural technology development and extension work, and accelerate the popularization and application of advanced technologies in agricultural production.  
Agricultural research institutes and relevant schools shall take the actual performance of their scientific and technical personnel in agricultural technology extension work as an important part of their work assessment and professional title evaluation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家鼓励农场、林场、牧场、渔场、水利工程管理单位面向社会开展农业技术推广服务。

**Article 17** The State shall encourage farms, forest farms, pastures, fisheries, and water conservancy project management units to provide agricultural technology extension services to the public.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家鼓励和支持发展农村专业技术协会等群众性科技组织，发挥其在农业技术推广中的作用。

**Article 18** The State shall encourage and support the development of rural specialized technical associations and other mass scientific and technological organizations, and bring their role into play in agricultural technology extension.

### 第三章　农业技术的推广与应用

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　重大农业技术的推广应当列入国家和地方相关发展规划、计划，由农业技术推广部门会同相关部门按照各自的职责，相互配合，组织实施。

**Article 19** The extension of major agricultural technologies shall be incorporated into relevant State and local development plans and programs, and the agricultural technology extension departments shall, together with relevant departments, organize and implement such extension in accordance with their respective functions and duties and through mutual cooperation.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　农业科研单位和有关学校应当把农业生产中需要解决的技术问题列为研究课题，其科研成果可以通过有关农业技术推广单位进行推广或者直接向农业劳动者和农业生产经营组织推广。  
国家引导农业科研单位和有关学校开展公益性农业技术推广服务。

**Article 20** Agricultural research institutes and relevant schools shall list technical problems to be solved in agricultural production as research topics, and their scientific research achievements may be extended through relevant agricultural technology extension units or directly to agricultural workers and agricultural production and operation organizations.  
The State shall guide agricultural research institutes and relevant schools to carry out public-interest agricultural technology extension services.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　向农业劳动者和农业生产经营组织推广的农业技术，必须在推广地区经过试验证明具有先进性、适用性和安全性。

**Article 21** The agricultural technologies extended to agricultural workers and agricultural production and operation organizations must be proven to be advanced, applicable, and safe through experiments in the extension areas.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家鼓励和支持农业劳动者和农业生产经营组织参与农业技术推广。  
农业劳动者和农业生产经营组织在生产中应用先进的农业技术，有关部门和单位应当在技术培训、资金、物资和销售等方面给予扶持。  
农业劳动者和农业生产经营组织根据自愿的原则应用农业技术，任何单位或者个人不得强迫。  
推广农业技术，应当选择有条件的农户、区域或者工程项目，进行应用示范。

**Article 22** The State shall encourage and support agricultural workers and agricultural production and operation organizations to participate in agricultural technology extension.  
Where agricultural workers and agricultural production and operation organizations apply advanced agricultural technologies in production, relevant departments and units shall provide support in terms of technical training, funds, materials, and marketing.  
Agricultural workers and agricultural production and operation organizations shall apply agricultural technologies on a voluntary basis, and no unit or individual may compel them to do so.  
When extending agricultural technologies, demonstration of application shall be carried out by selecting qualified farmers, areas, or engineering projects.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　县、乡镇国家农业技术推广机构应当组织农业劳动者学习农业科学技术知识，提高其应用农业技术的能力。  
教育、人力资源和社会保障、农业农村、林业草原、水利、科学技术等部门应当支持农业科研单位、有关学校开展有关农业技术推广的职业技术教育和技术培训，提高农业技术推广人员和农业劳动者的技术素质。  
国家鼓励社会力量开展农业技术培训。

**Article 23** County and township State agricultural technology extension institutions shall organize agricultural workers to learn agricultural scientific and technical knowledge and improve their ability to apply agricultural technologies.  
The departments of education, human resources and social security, agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland, water resources, science and technology, and others shall support agricultural research institutes and relevant schools in carrying out vocational technical education and technical training related to agricultural technology extension, so as to improve the technical competence of agricultural technology extension personnel and agricultural workers.  
The State shall encourage social forces to carry out agricultural technology training.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　各级国家农业技术推广机构应当认真履行[本法第十一条](#art-11)规定的公益性职责，向农业劳动者和农业生产经营组织推广农业技术，实行无偿服务。  
国家农业技术推广机构以外的单位及科技人员以技术转让、技术服务、技术承包、技术咨询和技术入股等形式提供农业技术的，可以实行有偿服务，其合法收入和植物新品种、农业技术专利等知识产权受法律保护。进行农业技术转让、技术服务、技术承包、技术咨询和技术入股，当事人各方应当订立合同，约定各自的权利和义务。

**Article 24** State agricultural technology extension institutions at all levels shall diligently perform the public-interest duties as provided in Article 11 of this Law, and provide agricultural technology extension services to agricultural workers and agricultural production and operation organizations free of charge.  
Units and scientific and technical personnel other than State agricultural technology extension institutions may provide paid services when providing agricultural technologies in the forms of technology transfer, technical services, technical contracting, technical consulting, and technical contribution as shares. Their lawful incomes and intellectual property rights such as new plant varieties and agricultural technology patents shall be protected by law. When conducting technology transfer, technical services, technical contracting, technical consulting, or technology contribution as shares, the parties shall conclude a contract and agree on their respective rights and obligations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家鼓励和支持农民专业合作社、涉农企业，采取多种形式，为农民应用先进农业技术提供有关的技术服务。

**Article 25** The State shall encourage and support farmers' specialized cooperatives and agriculture-related enterprises to adopt various forms to provide relevant technical services for farmers to apply advanced agricultural technologies.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家鼓励和支持以大宗农产品和优势特色农产品生产为重点的农业示范区建设，发挥示范区对农业技术推广的引领作用，促进农业产业化发展和现代农业建设。

**Article 26** The State shall encourage and support the construction of agricultural demonstration zones focusing on the production of staple agricultural products and competitive specialty agricultural products, leverage the leading role of demonstration zones in agricultural technology extension, and promote the development of agricultural industrialization and the building of modern agriculture.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　各级人民政府可以采取购买服务等方式，引导社会力量参与公益性农业技术推广服务。

**Article 27** People's governments at all levels may adopt methods such as purchasing services to guide social forces to participate in public-interest agricultural technology extension services.

### 第四章　农业技术推广的保障措施

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家逐步提高对农业技术推广的投入。各级人民政府在财政预算内应当保障用于农业技术推广的资金，并按规定使该资金逐年增长。  
各级人民政府通过财政拨款以及从农业发展基金中提取一定比例的资金的渠道，筹集农业技术推广专项资金，用于实施农业技术推广项目。中央财政对重大农业技术推广给予补助。  
县、乡镇国家农业技术推广机构的工作经费根据当地服务规模和绩效确定，由各级财政共同承担。  
任何单位或者个人不得截留或者挪用用于农业技术推广的资金。

**Article 28** The State shall gradually increase investment in agricultural technology extension. People's governments at all levels shall ensure the funds for agricultural technology extension in their fiscal budgets, and make such funds increase year by year according to relevant provisions.  
People's governments at all levels shall raise special funds for agricultural technology extension through fiscal allocations and by extracting a certain proportion of funds from the agricultural development fund, for the implementation of agricultural technology extension projects. The central finance shall provide subsidies for major agricultural technology extension.  
The working funds of county and township State agricultural technology extension institutions shall be determined based on the scale of local services and performance, and shall be borne jointly by fiscal authorities at all levels.  
No unit or individual may withhold or misappropriate funds for agricultural technology extension.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　各级人民政府应当采取措施，保障和改善县、乡镇国家农业技术推广机构的专业技术人员的工作条件、生活条件和待遇，并按照国家规定给予补贴，保持国家农业技术推广队伍的稳定。  
对在县、乡镇、村从事农业技术推广工作的专业技术人员的职称评定，应当以考核其推广工作的业务技术水平和实绩为主。

**Article 29** People's governments at all levels shall adopt measures to ensure and improve the working conditions, living conditions, and treatment of professional and technical personnel in county and township State agricultural technology extension institutions, and grant them subsidies in accordance with State regulations, so as to maintain the stability of the State agricultural technology extension teams.  
The professional title evaluation of professional and technical personnel engaged in agricultural technology extension work in counties, townships, and villages shall mainly be based on the assessment of their professional and technical competence and actual performance in extension work.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　各级人民政府应当采取措施，保障国家农业技术推广机构获得必需的试验示范场所、办公场所、推广和培训设施设备等工作条件。  
地方各级人民政府应当保障国家农业技术推广机构的试验示范场所、生产资料和其他财产不受侵害。

**Article 30** People's governments at all levels shall adopt measures to ensure that State agricultural technology extension institutions obtain necessary working conditions, including experimental and demonstration sites, office premises, and extension and training facilities and equipment.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall ensure that the experimental and demonstration sites, means of production, and other property of State agricultural technology extension institutions are not infringed upon.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　农业技术推广部门和县级以上国家农业技术推广机构，应当有计划地对农业技术推广人员进行技术培训，组织专业进修，使其不断更新知识、提高业务水平。

**Article 31** The agricultural technology extension departments and State agricultural technology extension institutions at or above the county level shall, on a planned basis, provide technical training to agricultural technology extension personnel and organize professional refresher courses, so that they may continuously update their knowledge and improve their professional competence.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级以上农业技术推广部门、乡镇人民政府应当对其管理的国家农业技术推广机构履行公益性职责的情况进行监督、考评。  
各级农业技术推广部门和国家农业技术推广机构，应当建立国家农业技术推广机构的专业技术人员工作责任制度和考评制度。  
县级人民政府农业技术推广部门管理为主的乡镇国家农业技术推广机构的人员，其业务考核、岗位聘用以及晋升，应当充分听取所服务区域的乡镇人民政府和服务对象的意见。  
乡镇人民政府管理为主、县级人民政府农业技术推广部门业务指导的乡镇国家农业技术推广机构的人员，其业务考核、岗位聘用以及晋升，应当充分听取所在地的县级人民政府农业技术推广部门和服务对象的意见。

**Article 32** The agricultural technology extension departments at or above the county level and the township people's governments shall supervise and assess the performance of public-interest duties by the State agricultural technology extension institutions under their management.  
The agricultural technology extension departments at all levels and the State agricultural technology extension institutions shall establish a work responsibility system and an assessment system for the professional and technical personnel of State agricultural technology extension institutions.  
For personnel in township State agricultural technology extension institutions under the primary management of the agricultural technology extension department of the county people's government, their performance appraisal, post appointment, and promotion shall fully solicit the opinions of the township people's government and the service recipients in the areas they serve.  
For personnel in township State agricultural technology extension institutions under the primary management of the township people's government with professional guidance from the agricultural technology extension department of the county people's government, their performance appraisal, post appointment, and promotion shall fully solicit the opinions of the agricultural technology extension department of the county people's government at the locality and the service recipients.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　从事农业技术推广服务的，可以享受国家规定的税收、信贷等方面的优惠。

**Article 33** Those engaged in agricultural technology extension services may enjoy preferences in taxation, credit, and other aspects as provided by the State.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　各级人民政府有关部门及其工作人员未依照本法规定履行职责的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 34** Where the relevant departments of people's governments at all levels and their staff members fail to perform their duties in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家农业技术推广机构及其工作人员未依照本法规定履行职责的，由主管机关责令限期改正，通报批评；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 35** Where State agricultural technology extension institutions and their staff members fail to perform their duties in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the competent authority shall order them to make corrections within a time limit and circulate a notice of criticism; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本法规定，向农业劳动者、农业生产经营组织推广未经试验证明具有先进性、适用性或者安全性的农业技术，造成损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 36** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, agricultural technologies that have not been proven to be advanced, applicable, or safe through experiments are extended to agricultural workers or agricultural production and operation organizations, causing losses, the person responsible shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　违反本法规定，强迫农业劳动者、农业生产经营组织应用农业技术，造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 37** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, agricultural workers or agricultural production and operation organizations are compelled to apply agricultural technologies, causing losses, the person responsible shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反本法规定，截留或者挪用用于农业技术推广的资金的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 38** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, funds for agricultural technology extension are withheld or misappropriated, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本法自公布之日起施行。

**Article 39** This Law shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
