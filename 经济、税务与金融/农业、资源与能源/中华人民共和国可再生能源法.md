# 中华人民共和国可再生能源法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2009-12-26  
**生效日期**：2010-04-01

> （2005年2月28日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十四次会议通过　根据2009年12月26日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十二次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国可再生能源法〉的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　资源调查与发展规划 第三章　产业指导与技术支持 第四章　推广与应用 第五章　价格管理与费用补偿 第六章　经济激励与监督措施 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了促进可再生能源的开发利用，增加能源供应，改善能源结构，保障能源安全，保护环境，实现经济社会的可持续发展，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称可再生能源，是指风能、太阳能、水能、生物质能、地热能、海洋能等非化石能源。 水力发电对本法的适用，由国务院能源主管部门规定，报国务院批准。 通过低效率炉灶直接燃烧方式利用秸秆、薪柴、粪便等，不适用本法。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了促进可再生能源的开发利用，增加能源供应，改善能源结构，保障能源安全，保护环境，实现经济社会的可持续发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purposes of promoting the development and utilization of renewable energy, increasing energy supply, improving the energy structure, ensuring energy security, protecting the environment, and achieving sustainable economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称可再生能源，是指风能、太阳能、水能、生物质能、地热能、海洋能等非化石能源。  
水力发电对本法的适用，由国务院能源主管部门规定，报国务院批准。  
通过低效率炉灶直接燃烧方式利用秸秆、薪柴、粪便等，不适用本法。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, renewable energy means non-fossil energy sources such as wind energy, solar energy, hydro energy, biomass energy, geothermal energy, and ocean energy.  
The application of this Law to hydroelectric power shall be prescribed by the energy administrative department of the State Council and submitted to the State Council for approval.  
The direct combustion of straw, firewood, manure, etc., through low-efficiency stoves shall not be subject to this Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本法适用于中华人民共和国领域和管辖的其他海域。

**Article 3** This Law shall apply to the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家将可再生能源的开发利用列为能源发展的优先领域，通过制定可再生能源开发利用总量目标和采取相应措施，推动可再生能源市场的建立和发展。  
国家鼓励各种所有制经济主体参与可再生能源的开发利用，依法保护可再生能源开发利用者的合法权益。

**Article 4** The State shall list the development and utilization of renewable energy as a priority area for energy development, establish total volume targets for the development and utilization of renewable energy and adopt corresponding measures to promote the establishment and development of the renewable energy market.  
The State shall encourage economic entities under all forms of ownership to participate in the development and utilization of renewable energy, and shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of those who develop and utilize renewable energy according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院能源主管部门对全国可再生能源的开发利用实施统一管理。国务院有关部门在各自的职责范围内负责有关的可再生能源开发利用管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府管理能源工作的部门负责本行政区域内可再生能源开发利用的管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府有关部门在各自的职责范围内负责有关的可再生能源开发利用管理工作。

**Article 5** The energy administrative department of the State Council shall exercise unified management of the development and utilization of renewable energy nationwide. The relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for the management of the development and utilization of renewable energy within their respective scopes of duties.  
The departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level that are in charge of energy work shall be responsible for the management of the development and utilization of renewable energy within their respective administrative regions. The relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the management of the development and utilization of renewable energy within their respective scopes of duties.

### 第二章　资源调查与发展规划

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院能源主管部门负责组织和协调全国可再生能源资源的调查，并会同国务院有关部门组织制定资源调查的技术规范。  
国务院有关部门在各自的职责范围内负责相关可再生能源资源的调查，调查结果报国务院能源主管部门汇总。  
可再生能源资源的调查结果应当公布；但是，国家规定需要保密的内容除外。

**Article 6** The energy administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for organizing and coordinating the survey of renewable energy resources nationwide, and shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, organize the formulation of technical specifications for resource surveys.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for surveys of relevant renewable energy resources within their respective scopes of duties, and the survey results shall be submitted to the energy administrative department of the State Council for compilation.  
The survey results of renewable energy resources shall be made public; provided that the contents which are required to be kept confidential as prescribed by the State are excluded.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院能源主管部门根据全国能源需求与可再生能源资源实际状况，制定全国可再生能源开发利用中长期总量目标，报国务院批准后执行，并予公布。  
国务院能源主管部门根据前款规定的总量目标和省、自治区、直辖市经济发展与可再生能源资源实际状况，会同省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定各行政区域可再生能源开发利用中长期目标，并予公布。

**Article 7** The energy administrative department of the State Council shall, based on the national energy demand and the actual conditions of renewable energy resources, formulate medium- and long-term total volume targets for the development and utilization of renewable energy nationwide, which shall be implemented after being submitted to the State Council for approval, and shall be made public.  
The energy administrative department of the State Council shall, based on the total volume targets prescribed in the preceding paragraph and the economic development and actual conditions of renewable energy resources of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, together with the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, determine the medium- and long-term targets for the development and utilization of renewable energy in each administrative region, and shall make them public.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院能源主管部门会同国务院有关部门，根据全国可再生能源开发利用中长期总量目标和可再生能源技术发展状况，编制全国可再生能源开发利用规划，报国务院批准后实施。  
国务院有关部门应当制定有利于促进全国可再生能源开发利用中长期总量目标实现的相关规划。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理能源工作的部门会同本级人民政府有关部门，依据全国可再生能源开发利用规划和本行政区域可再生能源开发利用中长期目标，编制本行政区域可再生能源开发利用规划，经本级人民政府批准后，报国务院能源主管部门和国家电力监管机构备案，并组织实施。  
经批准的规划应当公布；但是，国家规定需要保密的内容除外。  
经批准的规划需要修改的，须经原批准机关批准。

**Article 8** The energy administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, prepare the national plan for the development and utilization of renewable energy based on the medium- and long-term total volume targets for the development and utilization of renewable energy nationwide and the status of renewable energy technology development, which shall be implemented after being submitted to the State Council for approval.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall formulate relevant plans that are conducive to promoting the achievement of the medium- and long-term total volume targets for the development and utilization of renewable energy nationwide.  
The departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government that are in charge of energy work shall, together with the relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level, prepare plans for the development and utilization of renewable energy in their respective administrative regions, based on the national plan for the development and utilization of renewable energy and the medium- and long-term targets for the development and utilization of renewable energy in their respective administrative regions. Such plans shall, after being approved by the people's governments at the same level, be submitted to the energy administrative department of the State Council and the State Electricity Regulatory Commission for record, and shall be organized and implemented.  
The approved plans shall be made public; provided that the contents which are required to be kept confidential as prescribed by the State are excluded.  
If an approved plan needs to be revised, the approval of the original approving authority must be obtained.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　编制可再生能源开发利用规划，应当遵循因地制宜、统筹兼顾、合理布局、有序发展的原则，对风能、太阳能、水能、生物质能、地热能、海洋能等可再生能源的开发利用作出统筹安排。规划内容应当包括发展目标、主要任务、区域布局、重点项目、实施进度、配套电网建设、服务体系和保障措施等。  
组织编制机关应当征求有关单位、专家和公众的意见，进行科学论证。

**Article 9** The preparation of plans for the development and utilization of renewable energy shall follow the principles of adapting measures to local conditions, making overall plans and taking all factors into consideration, rational layout, and orderly development, and shall make overall arrangements for the development and utilization of renewable energy such as wind energy, solar energy, hydro energy, biomass energy, geothermal energy, and ocean energy. The contents of the plan shall include development targets, main tasks, regional layout, key projects, implementation schedule, supporting power grid construction, service system, and safeguard measures, etc.  
The authority that organizes the preparation shall solicit opinions from relevant entities, experts, and the public, and conduct scientific demonstration.

### 第三章　产业指导与技术支持

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院能源主管部门根据全国可再生能源开发利用规划，制定、公布可再生能源产业发展指导目录。

**Article 10** The energy administrative department of the State Council shall, based on the national plan for the development and utilization of renewable energy, formulate and publish a guiding catalog for the development of the renewable energy industry.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院标准化行政主管部门应当制定、公布国家可再生能源电力的并网技术标准和其他需要在全国范围内统一技术要求的有关可再生能源技术和产品的国家标准。  
对前款规定的国家标准中未作规定的技术要求，国务院有关部门可以制定相关的行业标准，并报国务院标准化行政主管部门备案。

**Article 11** The administrative department for standardization under the State Council shall formulate and publish national technical standards for grid connection of renewable energy electricity and other national standards for renewable energy technologies and products for which unified technical requirements are needed nationwide.  
For technical requirements not provided for in the national standards prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the relevant departments of the State Council may formulate relevant industry standards and submit them to the administrative department for standardization under the State Council for record.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家将可再生能源开发利用的科学技术研究和产业化发展列为科技发展与高技术产业发展的优先领域，纳入国家科技发展规划和高技术产业发展规划，并安排资金支持可再生能源开发利用的科学技术研究、应用示范和产业化发展，促进可再生能源开发利用的技术进步，降低可再生能源产品的生产成本，提高产品质量。  
国务院教育行政部门应当将可再生能源知识和技术纳入普通教育、职业教育课程。

**Article 12** The State shall list the scientific and technological research and industrial development of the development and utilization of renewable energy as a priority area for scientific and technological development and high-tech industry development, incorporate it into the national scientific and technological development plan and the high-tech industry development plan, and arrange funds to support the scientific and technological research, application demonstration, and industrial development of the development and utilization of renewable energy, so as to promote technological progress in the development and utilization of renewable energy, reduce the production cost of renewable energy products, and improve product quality.  
The administrative department for education under the State Council shall incorporate knowledge and technology of renewable energy into the curricula of general education and vocational education.

### 第四章　推广与应用

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家鼓励和支持可再生能源并网发电。  
建设可再生能源并网发电项目，应当依照法律和国务院的规定取得行政许可或者报送备案。  
建设应当取得行政许可的可再生能源并网发电项目，有多人申请同一项目许可的，应当依法通过招标确定被许可人。

**Article 13** The State shall encourage and support grid-connected power generation from renewable energy.  
The construction of grid-connected power generation projects using renewable energy shall obtain administrative licenses or be submitted for record in accordance with the provisions of laws and the State Council.  
If multiple persons apply for the license of the same project for the construction of a grid-connected power generation project using renewable energy that requires an administrative license, the licensee shall be determined through bidding according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家实行可再生能源发电全额保障性收购制度。  
国务院能源主管部门会同国家电力监管机构和国务院财政部门，按照全国可再生能源开发利用规划，确定在规划期内应当达到的可再生能源发电量占全部发电量的比重，制定电网企业优先调度和全额收购可再生能源发电的具体办法，并由国务院能源主管部门会同国家电力监管机构在年度中督促落实。  
电网企业应当与按照可再生能源开发利用规划建设，依法取得行政许可或者报送备案的可再生能源发电企业签订并网协议，全额收购其电网覆盖范围内符合并网技术标准的可再生能源并网发电项目的上网电量。发电企业有义务配合电网企业保障电网安全。  
电网企业应当加强电网建设，扩大可再生能源电力配置范围，发展和应用智能电网、储能等技术，完善电网运行管理，提高吸纳可再生能源电力的能力，为可再生能源发电提供上网服务。

**Article 14** The State shall implement a system of full guaranteed purchase of electricity generated from renewable energy.  
The energy administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the State Electricity Regulatory Commission and the financial department of the State Council, and in accordance with the national plan for the development and utilization of renewable energy, determine the proportion of electricity generated from renewable energy in the total electricity generation that shall be achieved during the planning period, formulate specific measures for grid enterprises to dispatch renewable energy electricity on a priority basis and purchase it in full, and the energy administrative department of the State Council together with the State Electricity Regulatory Commission shall supervise the implementation during the year.  
Grid enterprises shall sign grid connection agreements with renewable energy power generation enterprises that are constructed in accordance with the plan for the development and utilization of renewable energy and have obtained administrative licenses or been submitted for record according to law, and shall purchase in full the on-grid electricity of the grid-connected renewable energy power generation projects that meet the grid connection technical standards within their grid coverage. Power generation enterprises shall have the obligation to cooperate with grid enterprises to ensure the safety of the grid.  
Grid enterprises shall strengthen grid construction, expand the scope of allocation of renewable energy electricity, develop and apply technologies such as smart grids and energy storage, improve grid operation management, enhance the capacity to absorb renewable energy electricity, and provide grid connection services for renewable energy power generation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家扶持在电网未覆盖的地区建设可再生能源独立电力系统，为当地生产和生活提供电力服务。

**Article 15** The State shall support the construction of independent renewable energy power systems in areas not covered by the grid, to provide electricity services for local production and daily life.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家鼓励清洁、高效地开发利用生物质燃料，鼓励发展能源作物。  
利用生物质资源生产的燃气和热力，符合城市燃气管网、热力管网的入网技术标准的，经营燃气管网、热力管网的企业应当接收其入网。  
国家鼓励生产和利用生物液体燃料。石油销售企业应当按照国务院能源主管部门或者省级人民政府的规定，将符合国家标准的生物液体燃料纳入其燃料销售体系。

**Article 16** The State shall encourage the clean and efficient development and utilization of biomass fuels, and encourage the development of energy crops.  
If the gas and heat produced from biomass resources meet the technical standards for connection to the urban gas pipeline network or heat pipeline network, the enterprises operating the gas pipeline network or heat pipeline network shall accept their connection to the network.  
The State shall encourage the production and utilization of bio-liquid fuels. Petroleum sales enterprises shall, in accordance with the provisions of the energy administrative department of the State Council or the provincial people's governments, incorporate bio-liquid fuels that meet national standards into their fuel sales system.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家鼓励单位和个人安装和使用太阳能热水系统、太阳能供热采暖和制冷系统、太阳能光伏发电系统等太阳能利用系统。  
国务院建设行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定太阳能利用系统与建筑结合的技术经济政策和技术规范。  
房地产开发企业应当根据前款规定的技术规范，在建筑物的设计和施工中，为太阳能利用提供必备条件。  
对已建成的建筑物，住户可以在不影响其质量与安全的前提下安装符合技术规范和产品标准的太阳能利用系统；但是，当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 17** The State shall encourage entities and individuals to install and use solar energy utilization systems such as solar water heating systems, solar heating and cooling systems, and solar photovoltaic power generation systems.  
The administrative department for construction under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate technical and economic policies and technical specifications for the integration of solar energy utilization systems with buildings.  
Real estate development enterprises shall, in accordance with the technical specifications prescribed in the preceding paragraph, provide necessary conditions for solar energy utilization in the design and construction of buildings.  
For existing buildings, the occupants may install solar energy utilization systems that meet the technical specifications and product standards, provided that the quality and safety of the buildings are not affected; unless otherwise agreed by the parties.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家鼓励和支持农村地区的可再生能源开发利用。  
县级以上地方人民政府管理能源工作的部门会同有关部门，根据当地经济社会发展、生态保护和卫生综合治理需要等实际情况，制定农村地区可再生能源发展规划，因地制宜地推广应用沼气等生物质资源转化、户用太阳能、小型风能、小型水能等技术。  
县级以上人民政府应当对农村地区的可再生能源利用项目提供财政支持。

**Article 18** The State shall encourage and support the development and utilization of renewable energy in rural areas.  
The departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level that are in charge of energy work shall, together with the relevant departments, formulate plans for the development and utilization of renewable energy in rural areas based on actual conditions such as local economic and social development, ecological protection, and comprehensive health management needs, and shall promote the application of technologies such as the conversion of biomass resources like biogas, household solar energy, small-scale wind energy, and small-scale hydro energy according to local conditions.  
The people's governments at or above the county level shall provide financial support for projects utilizing renewable energy in rural areas.

### 第五章　价格管理与费用补偿

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　可再生能源发电项目的上网电价，由国务院价格主管部门根据不同类型可再生能源发电的特点和不同地区的情况，按照有利于促进可再生能源开发利用和经济合理的原则确定，并根据可再生能源开发利用技术的发展适时调整。上网电价应当公布。  
依照[本法第十三条](#art-13)第三款规定实行招标的可再生能源发电项目的上网电价，按照中标确定的价格执行；但是，不得高于依照前款规定确定的同类可再生能源发电项目的上网电价水平。

**Article 19** The on-grid price of electricity generated from renewable energy power generation projects shall be determined by the price administrative department of the State Council based on the characteristics of different types of renewable energy power generation and the conditions of different regions, in accordance with the principles of being conducive to promoting the development and utilization of renewable energy and being economically reasonable, and shall be adjusted in a timely manner according to the development of renewable energy development and utilization technologies. The on-grid price shall be made public.  
The on-grid price of electricity generated from renewable energy power generation projects that are subject to bidding in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 13 of this Law shall be implemented at the price determined by the winning bid; provided that it shall not be higher than the level of on-grid price for similar renewable energy power generation projects determined in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　电网企业依照[本法第十九条](#art-19)规定确定的上网电价收购可再生能源电量所发生的费用，高于按照常规能源发电平均上网电价计算所发生费用之间的差额，由在全国范围对销售电量征收可再生能源电价附加补偿。

**Article 20** The difference between the expenses incurred by grid enterprises in purchasing renewable energy electricity at the on-grid price determined pursuant to Article 19 of this Law and the expenses calculated based on the average on-grid price of conventional energy power generation shall be compensated by a surcharge on the price of electricity levied on electricity sales nationwide for renewable energy.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　电网企业为收购可再生能源电量而支付的合理的接网费用以及其他合理的相关费用，可以计入电网企业输电成本，并从销售电价中回收。

**Article 21** The reasonable grid connection fees and other reasonable related expenses paid by a power grid enterprise for purchasing renewable energy electricity may be included in the transmission cost of the power grid enterprise and recovered from the electricity sales price.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家投资或者补贴建设的公共可再生能源独立电力系统的销售电价，执行同一地区分类销售电价，其合理的运行和管理费用超出销售电价的部分，依照[本法第二十条](#art-20)的规定补偿。

**Article 22** For public stand-alone renewable energy power systems constructed with state investment or subsidies, the electricity sales price shall be the classified sales price for the same region, and the portion of reasonable operation and management expenses exceeding the sales price shall be compensated in accordance with Article 20 of this Law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　进入城市管网的可再生能源热力和燃气的价格，按照有利于促进可再生能源开发利用和经济合理的原则，根据价格管理权限确定。

**Article 23** The prices of renewable energy heat and gas entering the urban pipeline network shall be determined based on the principles of promoting the development and utilization of renewable energy and economic reasonableness, and in accordance with the price management authority.

### 第六章　经济激励与监督措施

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家财政设立可再生能源发展基金，资金来源包括国家财政年度安排的专项资金和依法征收的可再生能源电价附加收入等。  
可再生能源发展基金用于补偿[本法第二十条](#art-20)、第二十二条规定的差额费用，并用于支持以下事项：  
（一）可再生能源开发利用的科学技术研究、标准制定和示范工程；  
（二）农村、牧区的可再生能源利用项目；  
（三）偏远地区和海岛可再生能源独立电力系统建设；  
（四）可再生能源的资源勘查、评价和相关信息系统建设；  
（五）促进可再生能源开发利用设备的本地化生产。  
[本法第二十一条](#art-21)规定的接网费用以及其他相关费用，电网企业不能通过销售电价回收的，可以申请可再生能源发展基金补助。  
可再生能源发展基金征收使用管理的具体办法，由国务院财政部门会同国务院能源、价格主管部门制定。

**Article 24** The State shall establish a renewable energy development fund from the State finance, with funding sources including special funds arranged in the annual State budget and the additional income from renewable energy electricity prices collected according to law.  
The renewable energy development fund shall be used to compensate for the difference expenses specified in Articles 20 and 22 of this Law, and to support the following:  
(1) Scientific and technological research on the development and utilization of renewable energy, standard setting, and demonstration projects;  
(2) Renewable energy utilization projects in rural and pastoral areas;  
(3) Construction of stand-alone renewable energy power systems in remote areas and islands;  
(4) Survey, evaluation of renewable energy resources and construction of related information systems;  
(5) Promotion of localized production of equipment for the development and utilization of renewable energy.  
Where the grid connection fees and other related expenses specified in Article 21 of this Law cannot be recovered by the power grid enterprise through electricity sales prices, it may apply for subsidies from the renewable energy development fund.  
The specific measures for the collection, use and management of the renewable energy development fund shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council jointly with the energy and price departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　对列入国家可再生能源产业发展指导目录、符合信贷条件的可再生能源开发利用项目，金融机构可以提供有财政贴息的优惠贷款。

**Article 25** For renewable energy development and utilization projects included in the national guidance catalogue for the development of the renewable energy industry and meeting credit conditions, financial institutions may provide preferential loans with financial interest subsidies.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家对列入可再生能源产业发展指导目录的项目给予税收优惠。具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 26** The State shall grant tax preferences to projects included in the guidance catalogue for the development of the renewable energy industry. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　电力企业应当真实、完整地记载和保存可再生能源发电的有关资料，并接受电力监管机构的检查和监督。  
电力监管机构进行检查时，应当依照规定的程序进行，并为被检查单位保守商业秘密和其他秘密。

**Article 27** Power enterprises shall truthfully and completely record and keep relevant materials concerning the generation of electricity from renewable energy, and shall accept the inspection and supervision of the electricity regulatory authority.  
When conducting inspections, the electricity regulatory authority shall do so in accordance with the prescribed procedures and shall keep the trade secrets and other secrets of the inspected entity confidential.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院能源主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府管理能源工作的部门和其他有关部门在可再生能源开发利用监督管理工作中，违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府有关部门责令改正，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）不依法作出行政许可决定的；  
（二）发现违法行为不予查处的；  
（三）有不依法履行监督管理职责的其他行为的。

**Article 28** Where the energy department of the State Council, the departments in charge of energy administration of the local people's governments at or above the county level, or other relevant departments, in their supervision and administration of the development and utilization of renewable energy, violate the provisions of this Law and commit any of the following acts, they shall be ordered by the people's government at the same level or by the relevant department of the people's government at a higher level to make corrections, and the persons in charge who bear responsibility and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Failing to make an administrative license decision according to law;  
(2) Discovering illegal acts but failing to investigate and deal with them;  
(3) Having other acts of failing to perform supervision and administration duties according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　违反[本法第十四条](#art-14)规定，电网企业未按照规定完成收购可再生能源电量，造成可再生能源发电企业经济损失的，应当承担赔偿责任，并由国家电力监管机构责令限期改正；拒不改正的，处以可再生能源发电企业经济损失额一倍以下的罚款。

**Article 29** Where, in violation of Article 14 of this Law, a power grid enterprise fails to complete the purchase of renewable energy electricity as prescribed, thereby causing economic losses to a renewable energy power generation enterprise, it shall bear compensation liability and shall be ordered by the national electricity regulatory authority to make corrections within a time limit; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be fined not more than the amount of the economic losses of the renewable energy power generation enterprise.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　违反[本法第十六条](#art-16)第二款规定，经营燃气管网、热力管网的企业不准许符合入网技术标准的燃气、热力入网，造成燃气、热力生产企业经济损失的，应当承担赔偿责任，并由省级人民政府管理能源工作的部门责令限期改正；拒不改正的，处以燃气、热力生产企业经济损失额一倍以下的罚款。

**Article 30** Where, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 16 of this Law, an enterprise operating a gas pipeline network or a heat pipeline network does not permit gas or heat that meets the technical standards for network access to enter the network, thereby causing economic losses to a gas or heat production enterprise, it shall bear compensation liability and shall be ordered by the department in charge of energy administration of the provincial people's government to make corrections within a time limit; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be fined not more than the amount of the economic losses of the gas or heat production enterprise.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　违反[本法第十六条](#art-16)第三款规定，石油销售企业未按照规定将符合国家标准的生物液体燃料纳入其燃料销售体系，造成生物液体燃料生产企业经济损失的，应当承担赔偿责任，并由国务院能源主管部门或者省级人民政府管理能源工作的部门责令限期改正；拒不改正的，处以生物液体燃料生产企业经济损失额一倍以下的罚款。

**Article 31** Where, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 16 of this Law, a petroleum sales enterprise fails to incorporate bio-liquid fuels that meet the national standards into its fuel sales system as prescribed, thereby causing economic losses to a bio-liquid fuel production enterprise, it shall bear compensation liability and shall be ordered by the energy department of the State Council or the department in charge of energy administration of the provincial people's government to make corrections within a time limit; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be fined not more than the amount of the economic losses of the bio-liquid fuel production enterprise.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本法中下列用语的含义：  
（一）生物质能，是指利用自然界的植物、粪便以及城乡有机废物转化成的能源。  
（二）可再生能源独立电力系统，是指不与电网连接的单独运行的可再生能源电力系统。  
（三）能源作物，是指经专门种植，用以提供能源原料的草本和木本植物。  
（四）生物液体燃料，是指利用生物质资源生产的甲醇、乙醇和生物柴油等液体燃料。

**Article 32** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms shall have the meanings assigned to them:  
(1) "Biomass energy" means energy converted from plants in nature, excrement, and urban and rural organic waste.  
(2) "Stand-alone renewable energy power system" means a separately operated renewable energy power system that is not connected to the power grid.  
(3) "Energy crops" means herbaceous and woody plants specially cultivated to provide energy raw materials.  
(4) "Bio-liquid fuels" means liquid fuels such as methanol, ethanol, and biodiesel produced from biomass resources.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　本法自2006年1月1日起施行。

**Article 33** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2006.
