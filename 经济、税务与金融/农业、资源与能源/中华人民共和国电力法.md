# 中华人民共和国电力法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2018-12-29  
**生效日期**：2018-12-29

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障和促进电力事业的发展，维护电力投资者、经营者和使用者的合法权益，保障电力安全运行，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of ensuring and promoting the development of the electric power industry, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of electric power investors, operators and users, and ensuring the safe operation of electric power.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法适用于中华人民共和国境内的电力建设、生产、供应和使用活动。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to electric power construction, production, supply and utilization activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　电力事业应当适应国民经济和社会发展的需要，适当超前发展。国家鼓励、引导国内外的经济组织和个人依法投资开发电源，兴办电力生产企业。  
电力事业投资，实行谁投资、谁收益的原则。

**Article 3** The electric power industry shall meet the needs of national economic and social development and shall develop appropriately in advance. The State encourages and guides domestic and foreign economic organizations and individuals to invest in the development of power sources and to establish electric power production enterprises according to law.  
Investment in the electric power industry shall be governed by the principle of "whoever invests shall reap the benefits".

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　电力设施受国家保护。  
禁止任何单位和个人危害电力设施安全或者非法侵占、使用电能。

**Article 4** Electric power facilities shall be protected by the State.  
No unit or individual may endanger the safety of electric power facilities or illegally occupy or use electric energy.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　电力建设、生产、供应和使用应当依法保护环境，采用新技术，减少有害物质排放，防治污染和其他公害。  
国家鼓励和支持利用可再生能源和清洁能源发电。

**Article 5** Electric power construction, production, supply and utilization shall protect the environment according to law, adopt new technologies, reduce the discharge of harmful substances, and prevent and control pollution and other public hazards.  
The State encourages and supports the use of renewable energy and clean energy for power generation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院电力管理部门负责全国电力事业的监督管理。国务院有关部门在各自的职责范围内负责电力事业的监督管理。  
县级以上地方人民政府经济综合主管部门是本行政区域内的电力管理部门，负责电力事业的监督管理。县级以上地方人民政府有关部门在各自的职责范围内负责电力事业的监督管理。

**Article 6** The electric power administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the electric power industry nationwide. The relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the electric power industry within their respective functions and duties.  
The comprehensive economic administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be the electric power administrative department within its administrative region, responsible for the supervision and administration of the electric power industry. The relevant departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the electric power industry within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　电力建设企业、电力生产企业、电网经营企业依法实行自主经营、自负盈亏，并接受电力管理部门的监督。

**Article 7** Electric power construction enterprises, electric power production enterprises and power grid operation enterprises shall, according to law, operate independently, assume sole responsibility for their profits and losses, and accept supervision by the electric power administrative departments.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家帮助和扶持少数民族地区、边远地区和贫困地区发展电力事业。

**Article 8** The State shall assist and support the development of the electric power industry in minority nationality areas, remote areas and poverty-stricken areas.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家鼓励在电力建设、生产、供应和使用过程中，采用先进的科学技术和管理方法，对在研究、开发、采用先进的科学技术和管理方法等方面作出显著成绩的单位和个人给予奖励。

**Article 9** The State encourages the adoption of advanced science and technology and management methods in the process of electric power construction, production, supply and utilization, and shall give awards to units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in the research, development and adoption of advanced science and technology and management methods.

### 第二章　电力建设

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　电力发展规划应当根据国民经济和社会发展的需要制定，并纳入国民经济和社会发展计划。  
电力发展规划，应当体现合理利用能源、电源与电网配套发展、提高经济效益和有利于环境保护的原则。

**Article 10** Electric power development plans shall be formulated according to the needs of national economic and social development and shall be incorporated into the national economic and social development plans.  
Electric power development plans shall reflect the principles of rational utilization of energy sources, coordinated development of power sources and power grids, improvement of economic benefits, and being conducive to environmental protection.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　城市电网的建设与改造规划，应当纳入城市总体规划。城市人民政府应当按照规划，安排变电设施用地、输电线路走廊和电缆通道。  
任何单位和个人不得非法占用变电设施用地、输电线路走廊和电缆通道。

**Article 11** The construction and renovation plans of urban power grids shall be incorporated into the overall urban plans. The people's governments of cities shall, in accordance with the plans, make arrangements for the land for substation facilities, corridors for transmission lines and channels for cables.  
No unit or individual may illegally occupy land for substation facilities, corridors for transmission lines or channels for cables.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家通过制定有关政策，支持、促进电力建设。  
地方人民政府应当根据电力发展规划，因地制宜，采取多种措施开发电源，发展电力建设。

**Article 12** The State shall support and promote electric power construction through the formulation of relevant policies.  
Local people's governments shall, in accordance with electric power development plans, adopt various measures suitable to local conditions to develop power sources and promote electric power construction.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　电力投资者对其投资形成的电力，享有法定权益。并网运行的，电力投资者有优先使用权；未并网的自备电厂，电力投资者自行支配使用。

**Article 13** Electric power investors shall enjoy statutory rights and interests in the electric power formed by their investment. For those connected to the grid for operation, the electric power investors shall have the priority right to use; for self-provided power plants not connected to the grid, the electric power investors shall have the right to dispose and use the electric power on their own.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　电力建设项目应当符合电力发展规划，符合国家电力产业政策。  
电力建设项目不得使用国家明令淘汰的电力设备和技术。

**Article 14** Electric power construction projects shall conform to electric power development plans and to the national electric power industrial policies.  
Electric power construction projects may not use electric power equipment and technologies that are explicitly eliminated by the State.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　输变电工程、调度通信自动化工程等电网配套工程和环境保护工程，应当与发电工程项目同时设计、同时建设、同时验收、同时投入使用。

**Article 15** Power transmission and transformation projects, dispatching communication automation projects and other supporting projects of the power grid as well as environmental protection projects shall be designed, constructed, accepted and put into use simultaneously with the power generation projects.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　电力建设项目使用土地，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定办理；依法征收土地的，应当依法支付土地补偿费和安置补偿费，做好迁移居民的安置工作。  
电力建设应当贯彻切实保护耕地、节约利用土地的原则。  
地方人民政府对电力事业依法使用土地和迁移居民，应当予以支持和协助。

**Article 16** The use of land for electric power construction projects shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; where land is lawfully expropriated, land compensation fees and resettlement compensation fees shall be paid according to law, and the work of resettling the relocated residents shall be done properly.  
Electric power construction shall implement the principles of earnestly protecting farmland and economically utilizing land.  
Local people's governments shall provide support and assistance for the lawful use of land and relocation of residents for the electric power industry.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　地方人民政府应当支持电力企业为发电工程建设勘探水源和依法取水、用水。电力企业应当节约用水。

**Article 17** Local people's governments shall support electric power enterprises in surveying water sources for power generation projects and in lawfully drawing and using water. Electric power enterprises shall economize on water use.

### 第三章　电力生产与电网管理

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　电力生产与电网运行应当遵循安全、优质、经济的原则。  
电网运行应当连续、稳定，保证供电可靠性。

**Article 18** Electric power production and grid operation shall follow the principles of safety, high quality and economy.  
Grid operation shall be continuous and stable to ensure the reliability of power supply.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　电力企业应当加强安全生产管理，坚持安全第一、预防为主的方针，建立、健全安全生产责任制度。  
电力企业应当对电力设施定期进行检修和维护，保证其正常运行。

**Article 19** Electric power enterprises shall strengthen safety production management, adhere to the policy of safety first and prevention foremost, and establish and improve the safety production responsibility system.  
Electric power enterprises shall regularly inspect and maintain electric power facilities to ensure their normal operation.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　发电燃料供应企业、运输企业和电力生产企业应当依照国务院有关规定或者合同约定供应、运输和接卸燃料。

**Article 20** Enterprises that supply fuel for power generation, transport enterprises and electric power production enterprises shall supply, transport and unload fuel in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council or contractual agreements.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　电网运行实行统一调度、分级管理。任何单位和个人不得非法干预电网调度。

**Article 21** The operation of the power grid shall be subject to unified dispatch and hierarchical management. No unit or individual shall illegally interfere with power grid dispatch.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家提倡电力生产企业与电网、电网与电网并网运行。具有独立法人资格的电力生产企业要求将生产的电力并网运行的，电网经营企业应当接受。  
并网运行必须符合国家标准或者电力行业标准。  
并网双方应当按照统一调度、分级管理和平等互利、协商一致的原则，签订并网协议，确定双方的权利和义务；并网双方达不成协议的，由省级以上电力管理部门协调决定。

**Article 22** The State encourages power production enterprises to connect with the power grid, and power grids to connect with each other for operation. Where a power production enterprise with independent legal person status requests to connect the power it produces to the grid for operation, the power grid operation enterprise shall accept such request.  
Grid connection operation must comply with national standards or electric power industry standards.  
The parties to the grid connection shall, in accordance with the principles of unified dispatch, hierarchical management, equality and mutual benefit, and consultation on an equal footing, enter into a grid connection agreement to determine their rights and obligations; where the parties fail to reach an agreement, the power regulatory department at or above the provincial level shall coordinate and make a decision.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　电网调度管理办法，由国务院依照本法的规定制定。

**Article 23** The measures for the administration of power grid dispatch shall be formulated by the State Council in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

### 第四章　电力供应与使用

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家对电力供应和使用，实行安全用电、节约用电、计划用电的管理原则。  
电力供应与使用办法由国务院依照本法的规定制定。

**Article 24** The State shall implement the management principles of safe electricity use, electricity conservation, and planned electricity use for power supply and consumption.  
The measures for power supply and consumption shall be formulated by the State Council in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　供电企业在批准的供电营业区内向用户供电。  
供电营业区的划分，应当考虑电网的结构和供电合理性等因素。一个供电营业区内只设立一个供电营业机构。  
供电营业区的设立、变更，由供电企业提出申请，电力管理部门依据职责和管理权限，会同同级有关部门审查批准后，发给《电力业务许可证》。供电营业区设立、变更的具体办法，由国务院电力管理部门制定。

**Article 25** A power supply enterprise shall supply electricity to users within the approved power supply business area.  
The division of power supply business areas shall take into account factors such as the structure of the power grid and the reasonableness of power supply. Only one power supply business entity shall be established within a single power supply business area.  
The establishment or modification of a power supply business area shall be subject to an application by the power supply enterprise. After examination and approval by the power regulatory department in conjunction with the relevant department at the same level based on its functions and authority, the "Electric Power Business License" shall be issued. The specific measures for the establishment or modification of power supply business areas shall be formulated by the power regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　供电营业区内的供电营业机构，对本营业区内的用户有按照国家规定供电的义务；不得违反国家规定对其营业区内申请用电的单位和个人拒绝供电。  
申请新装用电、临时用电、增加用电容量、变更用电和终止用电，应当依照规定的程序办理手续。  
供电企业应当在其营业场所公告用电的程序、制度和收费标准，并提供用户须知资料。

**Article 26** A power supply business entity within a power supply business area shall have the obligation to supply electricity to users within its business area in accordance with state regulations; it shall not refuse power supply to units or individuals that apply for electricity use within its business area in violation of state regulations.  
Applications for new installation of electricity use, temporary electricity use, increase of electricity capacity, change of electricity use, and termination of electricity use shall go through formalities in accordance with the prescribed procedures.  
A power supply enterprise shall publicly announce the procedures, systems, and fee standards for electricity use at its business premises, and provide information materials for users.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　电力供应与使用双方应当根据平等自愿、协商一致的原则，按照国务院制定的电力供应与使用办法签订供用电合同，确定双方的权利和义务。

**Article 27** Both parties to power supply and consumption shall, in accordance with the principles of equality, voluntariness, and consultation on an equal footing, sign a power supply and consumption contract pursuant to the measures for power supply and consumption formulated by the State Council to determine their rights and obligations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　供电企业应当保证供给用户的供电质量符合国家标准。对公用供电设施引起的供电质量问题，应当及时处理。  
用户对供电质量有特殊要求的，供电企业应当根据其必要性和电网的可能，提供相应的电力。

**Article 28** A power supply enterprise shall ensure that the quality of power supplied to users meets national standards. For power quality problems caused by public power supply facilities, it shall promptly address them.  
Where a user has special requirements for power quality, the power supply enterprise shall provide corresponding electric power based on the necessity thereof and the capability of the power grid.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　供电企业在发电、供电系统正常的情况下，应当连续向用户供电，不得中断。因供电设施检修、依法限电或者用户违法用电等原因，需要中断供电时，供电企业应当按照国家有关规定事先通知用户。  
用户对供电企业中断供电有异议的，可以向电力管理部门投诉；受理投诉的电力管理部门应当依法处理。

**Article 29** Under normal conditions of power generation and supply systems, a power supply enterprise shall continuously supply electricity to users and shall not interrupt the supply. Where interruption of power supply is necessary due to reasons such as maintenance of power supply facilities, lawful limitation of power consumption, or illegal use of electricity by users, the power supply enterprise shall notify users in advance in accordance with the relevant state regulations.  
Where a user has objections to the interruption of power supply by the power supply enterprise, it may file a complaint with the power regulatory department; the power regulatory department that accepts the complaint shall handle it according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　因抢险救灾需要紧急供电时，供电企业必须尽速安排供电，所需供电工程费用和应付电费依照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 30** Where emergency power supply is needed for emergency rescue and disaster relief, the power supply enterprise must arrange for power supply as quickly as possible. The expenses for the required power supply projects and the electricity fees payable shall be handled in accordance with the relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　用户应当安装用电计量装置。用户使用的电力电量，以计量检定机构依法认可的用电计量装置的记录为准。  
用户受电装置的设计、施工安装和运行管理，应当符合国家标准或者电力行业标准。

**Article 31** A user shall install a metering device for electricity consumption. The electric energy consumed by a user shall be determined based on the records of the metering device recognized according to law by a metrological verification institution.  
The design, construction, installation, and operation management of a user's power receiving facilities shall comply with national standards or electric power industry standards.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　用户用电不得危害供电、用电安全和扰乱供电、用电秩序。  
对危害供电、用电安全和扰乱供电、用电秩序的，供电企业有权制止。

**Article 32** Users shall not endanger the safety of power supply and consumption or disrupt the order of power supply and consumption in their use of electricity.  
For acts that endanger the safety of power supply and consumption or disrupt the order of power supply and consumption, the power supply enterprise shall have the right to stop them.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　供电企业应当按照国家核准的电价和用电计量装置的记录，向用户计收电费。  
供电企业查电人员和抄表收费人员进入用户，进行用电安全检查或者抄表收费时，应当出示有关证件。  
用户应当按照国家核准的电价和用电计量装置的记录，按时交纳电费；对供电企业查电人员和抄表收费人员依法履行职责，应当提供方便。

**Article 33** A power supply enterprise shall calculate and collect electricity fees from users in accordance with the electricity price approved by the State and the records of the metering device for electricity consumption.  
When personnel of a power supply enterprise for electricity inspection and meter reading and fee collection enter a user's premises to conduct safety inspections of electricity use or to read meters and collect fees, they shall present relevant identification documents.  
Users shall pay electricity fees on time in accordance with the electricity price approved by the State and the records of the metering device for electricity consumption; they shall provide convenience for the personnel of the power supply enterprise to perform their duties according to law during electricity inspection and meter reading and fee collection.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　供电企业和用户应当遵守国家有关规定，采取有效措施，做好安全用电、节约用电和计划用电工作。

**Article 34** Power supply enterprises and users shall comply with the relevant state regulations and take effective measures to properly carry out the work of safe electricity use, electricity conservation, and planned electricity use.

### 第五章　电价与电费

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本法所称电价，是指电力生产企业的上网电价、电网间的互供电价、电网销售电价。  
电价实行统一政策，统一定价原则，分级管理。

**Article 35** For the purpose of this Law, electricity price refers to the on-grid price of power production enterprises, the inter-grid supply price between power grids, and the power grid's sales price.  
Electricity prices shall implement a unified policy, unified pricing principles, and hierarchical management.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　制定电价，应当合理补偿成本，合理确定收益，依法计入税金，坚持公平负担，促进电力建设。

**Article 36** In setting electricity prices, the cost shall be reasonably compensated, profits shall be reasonably determined, taxes shall be included according to law, the principle of fair sharing of burdens shall be upheld, and the construction of the electric power industry shall be promoted.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　上网电价实行同网同质同价。具体办法和实施步骤由国务院规定。  
电力生产企业有特殊情况需另行制定上网电价的，具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 37** On-grid electricity prices shall implement the same price for the same quality on the same grid. The specific measures and implementation steps shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
Where a power production enterprise has special circumstances requiring separate formulation of on-grid electricity prices, the specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　跨省、自治区、直辖市电网和省级电网内的上网电价，由电力生产企业和电网经营企业协商提出方案，报国务院物价行政主管部门核准。  
独立电网内的上网电价，由电力生产企业和电网经营企业协商提出方案，报有管理权的物价行政主管部门核准。  
地方投资的电力生产企业所生产的电力，属于在省内各地区形成独立电网的或者自发自用的，其电价可以由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理。

**Article 38** On-grid electricity prices within cross-provincial, autonomous regional, or municipal grids and provincial grids shall be proposed through negotiation between power production enterprises and power grid operation enterprises, and reported to the price administrative department of the State Council for approval.  
On-grid electricity prices within independent power grids shall be proposed through negotiation between power production enterprises and power grid operation enterprises, and reported to the competent price administrative department with authority for approval.  
For electric power produced by locally-invested power production enterprises that forms independent power grids within a province or is self-generated and self-consumed, the electricity prices may be managed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　跨省、自治区、直辖市电网和独立电网之间、省级电网和独立电网之间的互供电价，由双方协商提出方案，报国务院物价行政主管部门或者其授权的部门核准。  
独立电网与独立电网之间的互供电价，由双方协商提出方案，报有管理权的物价行政主管部门核准。

**Article 39** Inter-grid supply prices between cross-provincial, autonomous regional, or municipal grids and independent power grids, or between provincial grids and independent power grids, shall be proposed through negotiation between the parties and reported to the price administrative department of the State Council or its authorized department for approval.  
Inter-grid supply prices between independent power grids shall be proposed through negotiation between the parties and reported to the competent price administrative department with authority for approval.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　跨省、自治区、直辖市电网和省级电网的销售电价，由电网经营企业提出方案，报国务院物价行政主管部门或者其授权的部门核准。  
独立电网的销售电价，由电网经营企业提出方案，报有管理权的物价行政主管部门核准。

**Article 40** Sales prices of cross-provincial, autonomous regional, or municipal grids and provincial grids shall be proposed by power grid operation enterprises and reported to the price administrative department of the State Council or its authorized department for approval.  
Sales prices of independent power grids shall be proposed by power grid operation enterprises and reported to the competent price administrative department with authority for approval.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家实行分类电价和分时电价。分类标准和分时办法由国务院确定。  
对同一电网内的同一电压等级、同一用电类别的用户，执行相同的电价标准。

**Article 41** The state shall implement classified electricity prices and time-of-use electricity prices. The classification standards and time-of-use methods shall be determined by the State Council.  
For users of the same voltage level and the same electricity consumption category within the same power grid, the same electricity price standard shall be applied.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　用户用电增容收费标准，由国务院物价行政主管部门会同国务院电力管理部门制定。

**Article 42** The charging standards for user electricity capacity increase shall be formulated by the price administrative department of the State Council jointly with the power management department of the State Council.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　任何单位不得超越电价管理权限制定电价。供电企业不得擅自变更电价。

**Article 43** No entity may set electricity prices beyond the authority of electricity price management. Power supply enterprises may not alter electricity prices without authorization.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　禁止任何单位和个人在电费中加收其他费用；但是，法律、行政法规另有规定的，按照规定执行。  
地方集资办电在电费中加收费用的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府依照国务院有关规定制定办法。  
禁止供电企业在收取电费时，代收其他费用。

**Article 44** Any unit or individual is prohibited from adding other charges to the electricity fee; however, if otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations, the provisions shall be followed.  
Where local fund-raising for power generation adds charges to the electricity fee, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall formulate measures in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State Council.  
Power supply enterprises are prohibited from collecting other fees on behalf of others when collecting electricity fees.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　电价的管理办法，由国务院依照本法的规定制定。

**Article 45** The measures for the administration of electricity prices shall be formulated by the State Council in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

### 第六章　农村电力建设和农业用电

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当制定农村电气化发展规划，并将其纳入当地电力发展规划及国民经济和社会发展计划。

**Article 46** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate rural electrification development plans and incorporate them into the local electric power development plans and the national economic and social development plans.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家对农村电气化实行优惠政策，对少数民族地区、边远地区和贫困地区的农村电力建设给予重点扶持。

**Article 47** The state shall implement preferential policies for rural electrification, and provide key support for rural electric power construction in ethnic minority areas, remote areas, and poverty-stricken areas.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家提倡农村开发水能资源，建设中、小型水电站，促进农村电气化。  
国家鼓励和支持农村利用太阳能、风能、地热能、生物质能和其他能源进行农村电源建设，增加农村电力供应。

**Article 48** The state advocates the development of water energy resources in rural areas, the construction of medium and small-sized hydropower stations, and the promotion of rural electrification.  
The state encourages and supports the use of solar energy, wind energy, geothermal energy, biomass energy, and other energy sources in rural areas for the construction of rural power sources to increase the supply of rural electric power.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上地方人民政府及其经济综合主管部门在安排用电指标时，应当保证农业和农村用电的适当比例，优先保证农村排涝、抗旱和农业季节性生产用电。  
电力企业应当执行前款的用电安排，不得减少农业和农村用电指标。

**Article 49** When arranging electricity consumption targets, the local people's governments at or above the county level and their comprehensive economic departments shall ensure an appropriate proportion for agricultural and rural electricity use, and give priority to ensuring electricity for rural drainage, drought relief, and seasonal agricultural production.  
Power enterprises shall implement the electricity consumption arrangements mentioned in the preceding paragraph and shall not reduce the electricity consumption targets for agriculture and rural areas.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　农业用电价格按照保本、微利的原则确定。  
农民生活用电与当地城镇居民生活用电应当逐步实行相同的电价。

**Article 50** The price of electricity for agricultural use shall be determined on the principle of covering costs and making a small profit.  
The electricity price for rural residents' living use and that for local urban residents' living use shall gradually be the same.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　农业和农村用电管理办法，由国务院依照本法的规定制定。

**Article 51** The measures for the administration of agricultural and rural electricity use shall be formulated by the State Council in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

### 第七章　电力设施保护

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　任何单位和个人不得危害发电设施、变电设施和电力线路设施及其有关辅助设施。  
在电力设施周围进行爆破及其他可能危及电力设施安全的作业的，应当按照国务院有关电力设施保护的规定，经批准并采取确保电力设施安全的措施后，方可进行作业。

**Article 52** No unit or individual may damage power generation facilities, power transformation facilities, power line facilities, and their related auxiliary facilities.  
Where blasting or other operations that may endanger the safety of electric power facilities are carried out around the facilities, such operations shall only be carried out after approval and adoption of measures to ensure the safety of the electric power facilities in accordance with the regulations of the State Council on the protection of electric power facilities.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　电力管理部门应当按照国务院有关电力设施保护的规定，对电力设施保护区设立标志。  
任何单位和个人不得在依法划定的电力设施保护区内修建可能危及电力设施安全的建筑物、构筑物，不得种植可能危及电力设施安全的植物，不得堆放可能危及电力设施安全的物品。  
在依法划定电力设施保护区前已经种植的植物妨碍电力设施安全的，应当修剪或者砍伐。

**Article 53** The power management department shall, in accordance with the regulations of the State Council on the protection of electric power facilities, set up signs in the protection areas of electric power facilities.  
No unit or individual may construct buildings or structures that may endanger the safety of electric power facilities, plant plants that may endanger the safety of electric power facilities, or stack items that may endanger the safety of electric power facilities within the legally designated protection areas of electric power facilities.  
Where plants planted before the legal designation of the protection areas of electric power facilities obstruct the safety of such facilities, they shall be pruned or cut down.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　任何单位和个人需要在依法划定的电力设施保护区内进行可能危及电力设施安全的作业时，应当经电力管理部门批准并采取安全措施后，方可进行作业。

**Article 54** Where any unit or individual needs to carry out operations that may endanger the safety of electric power facilities within the legally designated protection areas of electric power facilities, such operations shall only be carried out after approval by the power management department and adoption of safety measures.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　电力设施与公用工程、绿化工程和其他工程在新建、改建或者扩建中相互妨碍时，有关单位应当按照国家有关规定协商，达成协议后方可施工。

**Article 55** Where electric power facilities and public works, greening projects, or other projects obstruct each other during new construction, reconstruction, or expansion, the relevant units shall negotiate in accordance with the relevant state regulations and may only commence construction after reaching an agreement.

### 第八章　监督检查

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　电力管理部门依法对电力企业和用户执行电力法律、行政法规的情况进行监督检查。

**Article 56** The power management department shall, according to law, supervise and inspect the implementation of electric power laws and administrative regulations by power enterprises and users.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　电力管理部门根据工作需要，可以配备电力监督检查人员。  
电力监督检查人员应当公正廉洁，秉公执法，熟悉电力法律、法规，掌握有关电力专业技术。

**Article 57** The power management department may, based on work needs, assign electric power supervision and inspection personnel.  
Electric power supervision and inspection personnel shall be fair and incorruptible, enforce the law impartially, be familiar with electric power laws and regulations, and have mastered relevant electric power professional technology.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　电力监督检查人员进行监督检查时，有权向电力企业或者用户了解有关执行电力法律、行政法规的情况，查阅有关资料，并有权进入现场进行检查。  
电力企业和用户对执行监督检查任务的电力监督检查人员应当提供方便。  
电力监督检查人员进行监督检查时，应当出示证件。

**Article 58** When conducting supervision and inspection, electric power supervision and inspection personnel shall have the right to inquire with power enterprises or users about the implementation of electric power laws and administrative regulations, to review relevant materials, and to enter the site for inspection.  
Power enterprises and users shall provide convenience to electric power supervision and inspection personnel performing supervision and inspection tasks.  
When conducting supervision and inspection, electric power supervision and inspection personnel shall present their credentials.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　电力企业或者用户违反供用电合同，给对方造成损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。  
电力企业违反[本法第二十八条](#art-28)、第二十九条第一款的规定，未保证供电质量或者未事先通知用户中断供电，给用户造成损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 59** Where a power enterprise or user violates the power supply and consumption contract and causes losses to the other party, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.  
Where a power enterprise violates the provisions of Article 28 and paragraph 1 of Article 29 of this Law by failing to ensure the quality of power supply or failing to notify the user in advance of a power interruption, thereby causing losses to the user, the power enterprise shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　因电力运行事故给用户或者第三人造成损害的，电力企业应当依法承担赔偿责任。  
电力运行事故由下列原因之一造成的，电力企业不承担赔偿责任：  
（一）不可抗力；  
（二）用户自身的过错。  
因用户或者第三人的过错给电力企业或者其他用户造成损害的，该用户或者第三人应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 60** Where a power operation accident causes damage to a user or a third party, the power enterprise shall bear compensation liability according to law.  
Where a power operation accident is caused by any of the following reasons, the power enterprise shall not bear compensation liability:  
(1) Force majeure;  
(2) The fault of the user itself.  
Where damage is caused to a power enterprise or other users due to the fault of a user or a third party, such user or third party shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反[本法第十一条](#art-11)第二款的规定，非法占用变电设施用地、输电线路走廊或者电缆通道的，由县级以上地方人民政府责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，强制清除障碍。

**Article 61** Where any person, in violation of the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 11 of this Law, illegally occupies land for substation facilities, transmission line corridors or cable passages, the local people's government at or above the county level shall order him to make corrections within a specified time limit; if he fails to make corrections within the time limit, the obstacles shall be forcibly removed.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反[本法第十四条](#art-14)规定，电力建设项目不符合电力发展规划、产业政策的，由电力管理部门责令停止建设。  
违反[本法第十四条](#art-14)规定，电力建设项目使用国家明令淘汰的电力设备和技术的，由电力管理部门责令停止使用，没收国家明令淘汰的电力设备，并处五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 62** Where any electric power construction project, in violation of the provisions of Article 14 of this Law, does not conform to the electric power development plan or industrial policy, the electric power administrative department shall order it to suspend construction.  
Where any electric power construction project, in violation of the provisions of Article 14 of this Law, uses electric power equipment and technology that the State has expressly eliminated, the electric power administrative department shall order it to cease the use, confiscate such eliminated electric power equipment, and impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反[本法第二十五条](#art-25)规定，未经许可，从事供电或者变更供电营业区的，由电力管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得，可以并处违法所得五倍以下的罚款。

**Article 63** Where any person, in violation of the provisions of Article 25 of this Law, engages in power supply or alters the power supply business area without a license, the electric power administrative department shall order him to make corrections, confiscate his illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not more than five times the amount of the illegal gains.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反[本法第二十六条](#art-26)、第二十九条规定，拒绝供电或者中断供电的，由电力管理部门责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，对有关主管人员和直接责任人员给予行政处分。

**Article 64** Where any person, in violation of the provisions of Article 26 or Article 29 of this Law, refuses to supply power or interrupts power supply, the electric power administrative department shall order him to make corrections and give him a warning; if the circumstances are serious, the person in charge and the directly responsible personnel shall be given administrative sanctions.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反[本法第三十二条](#art-32)规定，危害供电、用电安全或者扰乱供电、用电秩序的，由电力管理部门责令改正，给予警告；情节严重或者拒绝改正的，可以中止供电，可以并处五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 65** Where any person, in violation of the provisions of Article 32 of this Law, endangers the safety of power supply or power consumption, or disrupts the order of power supply or power consumption, the electric power administrative department shall order him to make corrections and give him a warning; if the circumstances are serious or he refuses to make corrections, the department may suspend the power supply and may also impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反[本法第三十三条](#art-33)、第四十三条、第四十四条规定，未按照国家核准的电价和用电计量装置的记录向用户计收电费、超越权限制定电价或者在电费中加收其他费用的，由物价行政主管部门给予警告，责令返还违法收取的费用，可以并处违法收取费用五倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，对有关主管人员和直接责任人员给予行政处分。

**Article 66** Where any person, in violation of the provisions of Article 33, Article 43 or Article 44 of this Law, fails to calculate and collect electricity charges from users in accordance with the electricity price approved by the State and the records of electricity metering devices, fixes electricity prices beyond his authority, or adds other charges to the electricity charges, the competent administrative department for prices shall give him a warning, order him to refund the illegally collected charges, and may also impose a fine of not more than five times the amount of the illegally collected charges; if the circumstances are serious, the person in charge and the directly responsible personnel shall be given administrative sanctions.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　违反[本法第四十九条](#art-49)第二款规定，减少农业和农村用电指标的，由电力管理部门责令改正；情节严重的，对有关主管人员和直接责任人员给予行政处分；造成损失的，责令赔偿损失。

**Article 67** Where any person, in violation of the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 49 of this Law, reduces the electricity consumption quotas for agriculture and rural areas, the electric power administrative department shall order him to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, the person in charge and the directly responsible personnel shall be given administrative sanctions; if losses are caused, he shall be ordered to compensate for the losses.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　违反[本法第五十二条](#art-52)第二款和第五十四条规定，未经批准或者未采取安全措施在电力设施周围或者在依法划定的电力设施保护区内进行作业，危及电力设施安全的，由电力管理部门责令停止作业、恢复原状并赔偿损失。

**Article 68** Where any person, in violation of the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 52 and Article 54 of this Law, conducts operations around electric power facilities or within the lawfully delimited protection areas for electric power facilities without approval or without taking safety measures, thereby endangering the safety of electric power facilities, the electric power administrative department shall order him to cease operations, restore the original condition and compensate for the losses.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　违反[本法第五十三条](#art-53)规定，在依法划定的电力设施保护区内修建建筑物、构筑物或者种植植物、堆放物品，危及电力设施安全的，由当地人民政府责令强制拆除、砍伐或者清除。

**Article 69** Where any person, in violation of the provisions of Article 53 of this Law, builds structures or buildings, plants plants or stacks articles within the lawfully delimited protection areas for electric power facilities, thereby endangering the safety of electric power facilities, the local people's government shall order him to have them forcibly dismantled, cut down or removed.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　有下列行为之一，应当给予治安管理处罚的，由公安机关依照治安管理处罚法的有关规定予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）阻碍电力建设或者电力设施抢修，致使电力建设或者电力设施抢修不能正常进行的；  
（二）扰乱电力生产企业、变电所、电力调度机构和供电企业的秩序，致使生产、工作和营业不能正常进行的；  
（三）殴打、公然侮辱履行职务的查电人员或者抄表收费人员的；  
（四）拒绝、阻碍电力监督检查人员依法执行职务的。

**Article 70** Whoever commits any of the following acts, if it shall be subject to public security administration punishment, shall be punished by the public security organ in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law on Public Security Administration Punishments; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) obstructing electric power construction or emergency repair of electric power facilities, thereby causing the electric power construction or emergency repair to be unable to proceed normally;  
(2) disrupting the order of electric power production enterprises, substations, electric power dispatching institutions or power supply enterprises, thereby causing production, work or business to be unable to proceed normally;  
(3) beating or publicly insulting electricity inspectors or meter readers and fee collectors who are performing their duties; or  
(4) refusing or obstructing electric power supervision and inspection personnel from performing their duties according to law.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　盗窃电能的，由电力管理部门责令停止违法行为，追缴电费并处应交电费五倍以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 71** Any person who steals electric energy shall be ordered by the electric power administrative department to stop the illegal act, shall be required to make up the electricity charges and shall be fined not more than five times the amount of the electricity charges payable; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　盗窃电力设施或者以其他方法破坏电力设施，危害公共安全的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 72** Any person who steals electric power facilities or destroys electric power facilities by other means, thereby endangering public security, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　电力管理部门的工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 73** Any staff member of the electric power administrative department who abuses his power, neglects his duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, if constituting a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if not constituting a crime, shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　电力企业职工违反规章制度、违章调度或者不服从调度指令，造成重大事故的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。  
电力企业职工故意延误电力设施抢修或者抢险救灾供电，造成严重后果的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。  
电力企业的管理人员和查电人员、抄表收费人员勒索用户、以电谋私，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 74** Where any employee of an electric power enterprise violates the rules and regulations, dispatches in violation of the rules or fails to comply with dispatch instructions, thereby causing a major accident, he shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
Where any employee of an electric power enterprise intentionally delays the emergency repair of electric power facilities or the power supply for emergency rescue and disaster relief, thereby causing serious consequences, he shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
Where any managerial personnel, electricity inspector, or meter reading and fee collection personnel of an electric power enterprise extorts users or seeks personal gain through electricity, if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if not constituting a crime, administrative sanctions shall be given according to law.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　本法自1996年4月1日起施行。

**Article 75** This Law shall come into force as of April 1, 1996.
