# 中华人民共和国煤炭法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2016-11-07  
**生效日期**：2016-11-07

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了合理开发利用和保护煤炭资源，规范煤炭生产、经营活动，促进和保障煤炭行业的发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of rationally exploiting, utilizing and protecting coal resources, regulating coal production and business operations, and promoting and ensuring the development of the coal industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事煤炭生产、经营活动，适用本法。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to coal production and business operations conducted within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　煤炭资源属于国家所有。地表或者地下的煤炭资源的国家所有权，不因其依附的土地的所有权或者使用权的不同而改变。

**Article 3** Coal resources are owned by the State. The State ownership of coal resources on or under the ground shall not change due to the different ownership or use rights of the land on which they are attached.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对煤炭开发实行统一规划、合理布局、综合利用的方针。

**Article 4** The State shall implement the policy of unified planning, rational distribution and comprehensive utilization for coal development.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家依法保护煤炭资源，禁止任何乱采、滥挖破坏煤炭资源的行为。

**Article 5** The State shall protect coal resources according to law, and prohibit any acts of disorderly mining and indiscriminate excavation that damage coal resources.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家保护依法投资开发煤炭资源的投资者的合法权益。  
国家保障国有煤矿的健康发展。  
国家对乡镇煤矿采取扶持、改造、整顿、联合、提高的方针，实行正规合理开发和有序发展。

**Article 6** The State shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of investors who invest in the development of coal resources according to law.  
The State shall guarantee the healthy development of state-owned coal mines.  
The State shall adopt the policy of supporting, transforming, rectifying, merging and upgrading for township coal mines, and shall implement regular, rational development and orderly development.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　煤矿企业必须坚持安全第一、预防为主的安全生产方针，建立健全安全生产的责任制度和群防群治制度。

**Article 7** Coal mining enterprises must adhere to the production safety policy of safety first and prevention foremost, and shall establish and improve the responsibility system for production safety and the system of mass prevention and mass control.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　各级人民政府及其有关部门和煤矿企业必须采取措施加强劳动保护，保障煤矿职工的安全和健康。  
国家对煤矿井下作业的职工采取特殊保护措施。

**Article 8** The people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments and coal mining enterprises must take measures to strengthen labor protection and ensure the safety and health of coal mining employees.  
The State shall adopt special protection measures for employees working underground in coal mines.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家鼓励和支持在开发利用煤炭资源过程中采用先进的科学技术和管理方法。  
煤矿企业应当加强和改善经营管理，提高劳动生产率和经济效益。

**Article 9** The State shall encourage and support the adoption of advanced science, technology and management methods in the process of exploiting and utilizing coal resources.  
Coal mining enterprises shall strengthen and improve management, and increase labor productivity and economic benefits.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家维护煤矿矿区的生产秩序、工作秩序，保护煤矿企业设施。

**Article 10** The State shall maintain the production order and work order in coal mining areas, and protect the facilities of coal mining enterprises.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　开发利用煤炭资源，应当遵守有关环境保护的法律、法规，防治污染和其他公害，保护生态环境。

**Article 11** In exploiting and utilizing coal resources, the provisions of relevant laws and regulations on environmental protection shall be complied with, pollution and other public hazards shall be prevented and controlled, and the ecological environment shall be protected.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院煤炭管理部门依法负责全国煤炭行业的监督管理。国务院有关部门在各自的职责范围内负责煤炭行业的监督管理。  
县级以上地方人民政府煤炭管理部门和有关部门依法负责本行政区域内煤炭行业的监督管理。

**Article 12** The coal administrative department of the State Council shall, according to law, be responsible for the supervision and administration of the coal industry nationwide. The relevant departments of the State Council shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the supervision and administration of the coal industry.  
The coal administrative departments and relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, be responsible for the supervision and administration of the coal industry within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　煤炭矿务局是国有煤矿企业，具有独立法人资格。  
矿务局和其他具有独立法人资格的煤矿企业、煤炭经营企业依法实行自主经营、自负盈亏、自我约束、自我发展。

**Article 13** A coal mining bureau is a state-owned coal mining enterprise with independent legal person status.  
Coal mining bureaus and other coal mining enterprises and coal trading enterprises with independent legal person status shall, according to law, operate independently, assume sole responsibility for their profits and losses, exercise self-restraint and pursue self-development.

### 第二章　煤炭生产开发规划与煤矿建设

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院煤炭管理部门根据全国矿产资源勘查规划编制全国煤炭资源勘查规划。

**Article 14** The coal administrative department of the State Council shall, on the basis of the national mineral resources exploration plan, formulate a national coal resources exploration plan.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院煤炭管理部门根据全国矿产资源规划规定的煤炭资源，组织编制和实施煤炭生产开发规划。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府煤炭管理部门根据全国矿产资源规划规定的煤炭资源，组织编制和实施本地区煤炭生产开发规划，并报国务院煤炭管理部门备案。

**Article 15** The coal administrative department of the State Council shall, on the basis of the coal resources specified in the national mineral resources plan, organize the formulation and implementation of a coal production and development plan.  
The coal administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, on the basis of the coal resources specified in the national mineral resources plan, organize the formulation and implementation of their local coal production and development plans, and shall submit them to the coal administrative department of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　煤炭生产开发规划应当根据国民经济和社会发展的需要制定，并纳入国民经济和社会发展计划。

**Article 16** Coal production and development plans shall be formulated in light of the needs of national economic and social development, and shall be incorporated into national economic and social development plans.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家制定优惠政策，支持煤炭工业发展，促进煤矿建设。  
煤矿建设项目应当符合煤炭生产开发规划和煤炭产业政策。

**Article 17** The State shall formulate preferential policies to support the development of the coal industry and promote coal mine construction.  
Coal mine construction projects shall conform to coal production and development plans and coal industry policies.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　煤矿建设使用土地，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定办理。征收土地的，应当依法支付土地补偿费和安置补偿费，做好迁移居民的安置工作。  
煤矿建设应当贯彻保护耕地、合理利用土地的原则。  
地方人民政府对煤矿建设依法使用土地和迁移居民，应当给予支持和协助。

**Article 18** The use of land for coal mine construction shall proceed in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations. Where land is expropriated, land compensation fees and resettlement subsidies shall be paid according to law, and the resettlement of relocated residents shall be properly carried out.  
Coal mine construction shall implement the principle of protecting cultivated land and rationally utilizing land.  
Local people's governments shall provide support and assistance for the lawful use of land and relocation of residents in coal mine construction.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　煤矿建设应当坚持煤炭开发与环境治理同步进行。煤矿建设项目的环境保护设施必须与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时验收、同时投入使用。

**Article 19** Coal mine construction shall adhere to the synchronous progress of coal development and environmental improvement. The environmental protection facilities of a coal mine construction project must be designed, constructed, accepted and put into use simultaneously with the main project.

### 第三章　煤炭生产与煤矿安全

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　煤矿投入生产前，煤矿企业应当依照有关安全生产的法律、行政法规的规定取得安全生产许可证。未取得安全生产许可证的，不得从事煤炭生产。

**Article 20** Before a coal mine is put into production, the coal mining enterprise shall obtain a safety production license in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations on production safety. Without obtaining the safety production license, no coal production shall be engaged in.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对国民经济具有重要价值的特殊煤种或者稀缺煤种，国家实行保护性开采。

**Article 21** The State shall implement protective mining for special or scarce coal types that are of important value to the national economy.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　开采煤炭资源必须符合煤矿开采规程，遵守合理的开采顺序，达到规定的煤炭资源回采率。  
煤炭资源回采率由国务院煤炭管理部门根据不同的资源和开采条件确定。  
国家鼓励煤矿企业进行复采或者开采边角残煤和极薄煤。

**Article 22** The mining of coal resources must comply with the coal mine mining regulations, observe a reasonable mining sequence, and achieve the prescribed coal resource recovery rate.  
The coal resource recovery rate shall be determined by the coal administration department of the State Council based on different resources and mining conditions.  
The State encourages coal mining enterprises to carry out re-mining or to mine residual coal at edges and extremely thin coal seams.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　煤矿企业应当加强煤炭产品质量的监督检查和管理。煤炭产品质量应当按照国家标准或者行业标准分等论级。

**Article 23** Coal mining enterprises shall strengthen the supervision, inspection, and management of coal product quality. Coal product quality shall be graded according to national standards or industry standards.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　煤炭生产应当依法在批准的开采范围内进行，不得超越批准的开采范围越界、越层开采。  
采矿作业不得擅自开采保安煤柱，不得采用可能危及相邻煤矿生产安全的决水、爆破、贯通巷道等危险方法。

**Article 24** Coal production shall be carried out within the approved mining scope according to law, and shall not exceed the approved mining scope for cross-boundary or cross-stratum mining.  
Mining operations shall not extract protective coal pillars without authorization, and shall not use dangerous methods such as flooding, blasting, or connecting roadways that may endanger the production safety of adjacent coal mines.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　因开采煤炭压占土地或者造成地表土地塌陷、挖损，由采矿者负责进行复垦，恢复到可供利用的状态；造成他人损失的，应当依法给予补偿。

**Article 25** Where land is occupied or surface land subsidence or excavation damage is caused by coal mining, the miner shall be responsible for reclamation to restore the land to a usable state; if losses are caused to others, compensation shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　关闭煤矿和报废矿井，应当依照有关法律、法规和国务院煤炭管理部门的规定办理。

**Article 26** The closure of coal mines and the decommissioning of mine shafts shall be handled in accordance with relevant laws, regulations, and the provisions of the coal administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家建立煤矿企业积累煤矿衰老期转产资金的制度。  
国家鼓励和扶持煤矿企业发展多种经营。

**Article 27** The State shall establish a system for coal mining enterprises to accumulate funds for production conversion during the aging period of coal mines.  
The State encourages and supports coal mining enterprises to develop diversified operations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家提倡和支持煤矿企业和其他企业发展煤电联产、炼焦、煤化工、煤建材等，进行煤炭的深加工和精加工。  
国家鼓励煤矿企业发展煤炭洗选加工，综合开发利用煤层气、煤矸石、煤泥、石煤和泥炭。

**Article 28** The State advocates and supports coal mining enterprises and other enterprises to develop coal-electricity co-production, coking, coal chemical industry, coal building materials, etc., and to carry out deep processing and fine processing of coal.  
The State encourages coal mining enterprises to develop coal washing and processing, and to comprehensively develop and utilize coalbed methane, coal gangue, coal slime, stone coal, and peat.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家发展和推广洁净煤技术。  
国家采取措施取缔土法炼焦。禁止新建土法炼焦窑炉；现有的土法炼焦限期改造。

**Article 29** The State develops and promotes clean coal technology.  
The State takes measures to ban indigenous coking. The construction of new indigenous coking kilns is prohibited; existing indigenous coking operations shall be transformed within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上各级人民政府及其煤炭管理部门和其他有关部门，应当加强对煤矿安全生产工作的监督管理。

**Article 30** People's governments at or above the county level, their coal administration departments, and other relevant departments shall strengthen the supervision and management of coal mine safety production.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　煤矿企业的安全生产管理，实行矿务局长、矿长负责制。

**Article 31** The safety production management of coal mining enterprises shall implement a responsibility system of the mining bureau director and the mine manager.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　矿务局长、矿长及煤矿企业的其他主要负责人必须遵守有关矿山安全的法律、法规和煤炭行业安全规章、规程，加强对煤矿安全生产工作的管理，执行安全生产责任制度，采取有效措施，防止伤亡和其他安全生产事故的发生。

**Article 32** The mining bureau director, the mine manager, and other principal responsible persons of coal mining enterprises must comply with laws and regulations on mine safety and with coal industry safety rules and procedures, strengthen the management of coal mine safety production, implement the safety production responsibility system, and take effective measures to prevent the occurrence of casualties and other safety production accidents.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　煤矿企业应当对职工进行安全生产教育、培训；未经安全生产教育、培训的，不得上岗作业。  
煤矿企业职工必须遵守有关安全生产的法律、法规、煤炭行业规章、规程和企业规章制度。

**Article 33** Coal mining enterprises shall provide safety production education and training to employees; those who have not received safety production education and training shall not be allowed to work on the job.  
Employees of coal mining enterprises must comply with laws, regulations, coal industry rules, procedures, and enterprise rules and regulations on safety production.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　在煤矿井下作业中，出现危及职工生命安全并无法排除的紧急情况时，作业现场负责人或者安全管理人员应当立即组织职工撤离危险现场，并及时报告有关方面负责人。

**Article 34** In underground coal mine operations, when an emergency situation that endangers the life safety of employees and cannot be eliminated occurs, the person in charge of the operation site or the safety management personnel shall immediately organize the employees to evacuate the dangerous site and promptly report to the relevant responsible persons.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　煤矿企业工会发现企业行政方面违章指挥、强令职工冒险作业或者生产过程中发现明显重大事故隐患，可能危及职工生命安全的情况，有权提出解决问题的建议，煤矿企业行政方面必须及时作出处理决定。企业行政方面拒不处理的，工会有权提出批评、检举和控告。

**Article 35** Where the trade union of a coal mining enterprise discovers that the enterprise's administrative side is directing operations in violation of regulations, forcing employees to work under risk, or discovers obvious major hidden dangers in the production process that may endanger the life safety of employees, the trade union shall have the right to make suggestions for solving the problem, and the administrative side of the coal mining enterprise must promptly make a decision on handling the matter. If the administrative side refuses to handle it, the trade union shall have the right to criticize, report, and accuse.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　煤矿企业必须为职工提供保障安全生产所需的劳动保护用品。

**Article 36** Coal mining enterprises must provide employees with labor protection articles necessary to ensure safety production.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　煤矿企业应当依法为职工参加工伤保险缴纳工伤保险费。鼓励企业为井下作业职工办理意外伤害保险，支付保险费。

**Article 37** Coal mining enterprises shall, according to law, pay work-related injury insurance premiums for employees to participate in work-related injury insurance. Enterprises are encouraged to handle accident insurance for employees working underground and pay the insurance premiums.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　煤矿企业使用的设备、器材、火工产品和安全仪器，必须符合国家标准或者行业标准。

**Article 38** Equipment, apparatus, explosive products, and safety instruments used by coal mining enterprises must comply with national standards or industry standards.

### 第四章　煤炭经营

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　煤炭经营企业从事煤炭经营，应当遵守有关法律、法规的规定，改善服务，保障供应。禁止一切非法经营活动。

**Article 39** Coal trading enterprises engaged in coal trading shall comply with relevant laws and regulations, improve services, and ensure supply. All illegal business activities are prohibited.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　煤炭经营应当减少中间环节和取消不合理的中间环节，提倡有条件的煤矿企业直销。  
煤炭用户和煤炭销区的煤炭经营企业有权直接从煤矿企业购进煤炭。在煤炭产区可以组成煤炭销售、运输服务机构，为中小煤矿办理经销、运输业务。  
禁止行政机关违反国家规定擅自设立煤炭供应的中间环节和额外加收费用。

**Article 40** Coal trading shall reduce intermediate links and eliminate unreasonable intermediate links, and coal mining enterprises with suitable conditions are encouraged to engage in direct sales.  
Coal users and coal trading enterprises in coal sales areas shall have the right to purchase coal directly from coal mining enterprises. In coal-producing areas, coal sales and transportation service organizations may be formed to handle distribution and transportation business for small and medium-sized coal mines.  
Administrative organs are prohibited from illegally establishing intermediate links in coal supply or charging additional fees in violation of State regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　从事煤炭运输的车站、港口及其他运输企业不得利用其掌握的运力作为参与煤炭经营、谋取不正当利益的手段。

**Article 41** Stations, ports and other transportation enterprises engaged in coal transportation shall not use the transport capacity under their control as a means to participate in coal operations or seek illegitimate interests.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国务院物价行政主管部门会同国务院煤炭管理部门和有关部门对煤炭的销售价格进行监督管理。

**Article 42** The competent department of price administration under the State Council, together with the department in charge of the coal industry under the State Council and other relevant departments, shall supervise and administer the selling prices of coal.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　煤矿企业和煤炭经营企业供应用户的煤炭质量应当符合国家标准或者行业标准，质级相符，质价相符。用户对煤炭质量有特殊要求的，由供需双方在煤炭购销合同中约定。  
煤矿企业和煤炭经营企业不得在煤炭中掺杂、掺假，以次充好。

**Article 43** The quality of coal supplied by coal mining enterprises and coal trading enterprises to users shall conform to national standards or industry standards, with consistent quality grades and a fair price-quality ratio. Where users have special requirements for coal quality, the supplier and the demander shall specify them in the coal purchase and sale contract.  
Coal mining enterprises and coal trading enterprises shall not adulterate or falsify coal, or pass off inferior coal as quality coal.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　煤矿企业和煤炭经营企业供应用户的煤炭质量不符合国家标准或者行业标准，或者不符合合同约定，或者质级不符、质价不符，给用户造成损失的，应当依法给予赔偿。

**Article 44** Where the quality of coal supplied by coal mining enterprises or coal trading enterprises to users fails to conform to national standards or industry standards, or fails to conform to contractual stipulations, or where the quality grade is inconsistent or the price-quality ratio is unfair, thereby causing losses to users, compensation shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　煤矿企业、煤炭经营企业、运输企业和煤炭用户应当依照法律、国务院有关规定或者合同约定供应、运输和接卸煤炭。  
运输企业应当将承运的不同质量的煤炭分装、分堆。

**Article 45** Coal mining enterprises, coal trading enterprises, transportation enterprises and coal users shall supply, transport and receive coal in accordance with the law, the relevant provisions of the State Council, or contractual agreements.  
Transportation enterprises shall separately load and stockpile coal of different qualities that they have undertaken to transport.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　煤炭的进出口依照国务院的规定，实行统一管理。  
具备条件的大型煤矿企业经国务院对外经济贸易主管部门依法许可，有权从事煤炭出口经营。

**Article 46** The import and export of coal shall be subject to unified management in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.  
Large-scale coal mining enterprises that meet the conditions may, upon obtaining permission from the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council according to law, have the right to engage in coal export operations.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　煤炭经营管理办法，由国务院依照本法制定。

**Article 47** The measures for the administration of coal trading shall be formulated by the State Council in accordance with this Law.

### 第五章　煤矿矿区保护

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　任何单位或者个人不得危害煤矿矿区的电力、通讯、水源、交通及其他生产设施。  
禁止任何单位和个人扰乱煤矿矿区的生产秩序和工作秩序。

**Article 48** No unit or individual may endanger the electric power, communications, water sources, transportation and other production facilities in a coal mine area.  
No unit or individual may disrupt the production order and work order of a coal mine area.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　对盗窃或者破坏煤矿矿区设施、器材及其他危及煤矿矿区安全的行为，一切单位和个人都有权检举、控告。

**Article 49** With respect to acts of stealing or destroying facilities and equipment in a coal mine area, or other acts endangering the safety of a coal mine area, all units and individuals shall have the right to report and accuse such acts.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　未经煤矿企业同意，任何单位或者个人不得在煤矿企业依法取得土地使用权的有效期间内在该土地上种植、养殖、取土或者修建建筑物、构筑物。

**Article 50** Without the consent of the coal mining enterprise, no unit or individual may plant crops, engage in aquaculture, take earth, or construct buildings or structures on the land for which the coal mining enterprise has lawfully obtained the right to use the land during the valid period of such right.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　未经煤矿企业同意，任何单位或者个人不得占用煤矿企业的铁路专用线、专用道路、专用航道、专用码头、电力专用线、专用供水管路。

**Article 51** Without the consent of the coal mining enterprise, no unit or individual may occupy the coal mining enterprise's dedicated railway lines, dedicated roads, dedicated waterways, dedicated berths, dedicated power lines, or dedicated water supply pipelines.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　任何单位或者个人需要在煤矿采区范围内进行可能危及煤矿安全的作业时，应当经煤矿企业同意，报煤炭管理部门批准，并采取安全措施后，方可进行作业。  
在煤矿矿区范围内需要建设公用工程或者其他工程的，有关单位应当事先与煤矿企业协商并达成协议后，方可施工。

**Article 52** Where any unit or individual needs to carry out operations within the scope of a coal mining area that may endanger the safety of the coal mine, such operations may be carried out only after obtaining the consent of the coal mining enterprise, submitting to the department in charge of the coal industry for approval, and taking safety measures.  
Where a public engineering project or other project needs to be constructed within the area of a coal mine, the relevant unit may proceed with construction only after consulting with the coal mining enterprise in advance and reaching an agreement.

### 第六章　监督检查

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　煤炭管理部门和有关部门依法对煤矿企业和煤炭经营企业执行煤炭法律、法规的情况进行监督检查。

**Article 53** The department in charge of the coal industry and other relevant departments shall, according to law, supervise and inspect the compliance of coal mining enterprises and coal trading enterprises with the coal laws and regulations.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　煤炭管理部门和有关部门的监督检查人员应当熟悉煤炭法律、法规，掌握有关煤炭专业技术，公正廉洁，秉公执法。

**Article 54** Supervisory and inspection personnel of the department in charge of the coal industry and other relevant departments shall be familiar with coal laws and regulations, master relevant coal professional technology, be impartial and honest, and enforce the law fairly.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　煤炭管理部门和有关部门的监督检查人员进行监督检查时，有权向煤矿企业、煤炭经营企业或者用户了解有关执行煤炭法律、法规的情况，查阅有关资料，并有权进入现场进行检查。  
煤矿企业、煤炭经营企业和用户对依法执行监督检查任务的煤炭管理部门和有关部门的监督检查人员应当提供方便。

**Article 55** When conducting supervisory inspections, the supervisory and inspection personnel of the department in charge of the coal industry and other relevant departments shall have the right to inquire with coal mining enterprises, coal trading enterprises or users about the implementation of coal laws and regulations, to examine relevant materials, and to enter the site for inspection.  
Coal mining enterprises, coal trading enterprises and users shall provide convenience to the supervisory and inspection personnel of the department in charge of the coal industry and other relevant departments who are lawfully performing supervisory inspection duties.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　煤炭管理部门和有关部门的监督检查人员对煤矿企业和煤炭经营企业违反煤炭法律、法规的行为，有权要求其依法改正。  
煤炭管理部门和有关部门的监督检查人员进行监督检查时，应当出示证件。

**Article 56** The supervisory and inspection personnel of the department in charge of the coal industry and other relevant departments shall have the right to require coal mining enterprises and coal trading enterprises to rectify their violations of coal laws and regulations according to law.  
When conducting supervisory inspections, the supervisory and inspection personnel of the department in charge of the coal industry and other relevant departments shall present their credentials.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反[本法第二十二条](#art-22)的规定，开采煤炭资源未达到国务院煤炭管理部门规定的煤炭资源回采率的，由煤炭管理部门责令限期改正；逾期仍达不到规定的回采率的，责令停止生产。

**Article 57** Whoever, in violation of Article 22 of this Law, exploits coal resources without achieving the coal resource recovery rate prescribed by the department in charge of the coal industry under the State Council shall be ordered by the department in charge of the coal industry to make corrections within a specified time limit; if the prescribed recovery rate is still not achieved upon the expiration of the time limit, the production shall be ordered to cease.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反[本法第二十四条](#art-24)的规定，擅自开采保安煤柱或者采用危及相邻煤矿生产安全的危险方法进行采矿作业的，由劳动行政主管部门会同煤炭管理部门责令停止作业；由煤炭管理部门没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 58** Whoever, in violation of Article 24 of this Law, mines protective coal pillars without authorization or adopts dangerous methods that endanger the production safety of adjacent coal mines in mining operations shall be ordered by the competent administrative department of labor jointly with the department in charge of the coal industry to cease operations; the department in charge of the coal industry shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the amount of the illegal gains; if a crime is constituted, the judicial authority shall investigate criminal liability according to law; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反[本法第四十三条](#art-43)的规定，在煤炭产品中掺杂、掺假，以次充好的，责令停止销售，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 59** Whoever, in violation of Article 43 of this Law, adulterates or falsifies coal products, or passes off inferior coal as quality coal, shall be ordered to cease sales, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the amount of the illegal gains shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, the judicial authority shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反[本法第五十条](#art-50)的规定，未经煤矿企业同意，在煤矿企业依法取得土地使用权的有效期间内在该土地上修建建筑物、构筑物的，由当地人民政府动员拆除；拒不拆除的，责令拆除。

**Article 60** Whoever, in violation of Article 50 of this Law, constructs buildings or structures on the land for which the coal mining enterprise has lawfully obtained the right to use the land during the valid period of such right, without the consent of the coal mining enterprise, shall be urged by the local people's government to demolish such buildings or structures; if the party refuses to demolish, the demolition shall be ordered.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反[本法第五十一条](#art-51)的规定，未经煤矿企业同意，占用煤矿企业的铁路专用线、专用道路、专用航道、专用码头、电力专用线、专用供水管路的，由县级以上地方人民政府责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，强制清除，可以并处五万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 61** Where any entity, in violation of Article 51 of this Law, occupies, without the consent of the coal mining enterprise, the railway special line, special road, special waterway, special wharf, special power line, or special water supply pipeline of the coal mining enterprise, the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the said time limit, it shall be forcibly cleared and may also be fined not more than 50,000 yuan; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反[本法第五十二条](#art-52)的规定，未经批准或者未采取安全措施，在煤矿采区范围内进行危及煤矿安全作业的，由煤炭管理部门责令停止作业，可以并处五万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 62** Where any entity, in violation of Article 52 of this Law, conducts operations that endanger the safety of a coal mine within the mining area without approval or without taking safety measures, the coal administration department shall order it to stop the operations, and may also impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　有下列行为之一的，由公安机关依照治安管理处罚法的有关规定处罚；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）阻碍煤矿建设，致使煤矿建设不能正常进行的；  
（二）故意损坏煤矿矿区的电力、通讯、水源、交通及其他生产设施的；  
（三）扰乱煤矿矿区秩序，致使生产、工作不能正常进行的；  
（四）拒绝、阻碍监督检查人员依法执行职务的。

**Article 63** Whoever commits one of the following acts shall be punished by the public security organ in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law; if the act constitutes a crime, the judicial organ shall investigate criminal liability according to law:  
(1) obstructing coal mine construction, causing the coal mine construction to be unable to proceed normally;  
(2) intentionally damaging the power supply, communications, water source, transportation, and other production facilities in the coal mine area;  
(3) disturbing the order of the coal mine area, causing production and work to be unable to proceed normally;  
(4) refusing or obstructing supervision and inspection personnel from performing their duties according to law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　煤矿企业的管理人员违章指挥、强令职工冒险作业，发生重大伤亡事故的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 64** Where managerial personnel of a coal mining enterprise issue commands in violation of regulations or force workers to work under hazardous conditions, causing a major accident resulting in casualties, the judicial organ shall investigate criminal liability according to the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　煤矿企业的管理人员对煤矿事故隐患不采取措施予以消除，发生重大伤亡事故的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 65** Where managerial personnel of a coal mining enterprise fail to take measures to eliminate potential accidents in the coal mine, causing a major accident resulting in casualties, the judicial organ shall investigate criminal liability according to the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　煤炭管理部门和有关部门的工作人员玩忽职守、徇私舞弊、滥用职权的，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 66** Where any staff member of the coal administration department or the relevant department neglects their duty, engages in malpractice for personal gain, or abuses their power, they shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, the judicial organ shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　本法自1996年12月1日起施行。

**Article 67** This Law shall come into force as of December 1, 1996.
