# 中华人民共和国海关关衔条例

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2003-02-28  
**生效日期**：2003-02-28

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强海关队伍建设，增强海关工作人员的责任感、荣誉感和组织纪律性，有利于海关工作人员依法履行职责，根据宪法，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of strengthening the construction of the Customs team, enhancing the sense of responsibility, honor and organizational discipline of Customs staff, and facilitating Customs staff to perform their duties according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　海关实行关衔制度。海关总署、分署、特派员办公室、各直属海关、隶属海关和办事处的国家公务员可以授予海关关衔。  
海关缉私警察实行人民警察警衔制度。

**Article 2** The Customs shall implement a rank system. State civil servants of the General Administration of Customs, its branches, offices of commissioners, all directly subordinate customs, subordinate customs and offices may be conferred Customs ranks.  
Customs anti-smuggling police shall implement the police rank system of the People's Police.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　关衔是区分海关工作人员等级、表明海关工作人员身份的称号、标志和国家给予海关工作人员的荣誉。

**Article 3** The Customs rank is a title and symbol for distinguishing the rank of Customs staff and indicating their identity, and is an honor granted by the State to Customs staff.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　海关工作人员实行职务等级编制关衔。

**Article 4** Customs staff shall have their Customs ranks organized by post grade.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　关衔高的海关工作人员对关衔低的海关工作人员，关衔高的为上级。当关衔高的海关工作人员在职务上隶属于关衔低的海关工作人员时，职务高的为上级。

**Article 5** A Customs staff member with a higher rank shall be the superior of a Customs staff member with a lower rank. Where a Customs staff member with a higher rank is subordinate in post to a Customs staff member with a lower rank, the one with the higher post shall be the superior.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　海关总署主管关衔工作。

**Article 6** The General Administration of Customs shall be in charge of the work on Customs ranks.

### 第二章　关衔等级的设置

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　海关关衔设下列五等十三级：  
（一）海关总监、海关副总监；  
（二）关务监督：一级、二级、三级；  
（三）关务督察：一级、二级、三级；  
（四）关务督办：一级、二级、三级；  
（五）关务员：一级、二级。

**Article 7** Customs ranks shall have the following five grades and thirteen levels:  
(1) Customs Director-General, Deputy Customs Director-General;  
(2) Customs Supervisor: Grade 1, Grade 2, Grade 3;  
(3) Customs Inspector: Grade 1, Grade 2, Grade 3;  
(4) Customs Superintendent: Grade 1, Grade 2, Grade 3;  
(5) Customs Clerk: Grade 1, Grade 2.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　海关工作人员实行下列职务等级编制关衔：  
（一）署级正职：海关总监；  
（二）署级副职：海关副总监；  
（三）局级正职：一级关务监督至二级关务监督；  
（四）局级副职：二级关务监督至三级关务监督；  
（五）处级正职：三级关务监督至二级关务督察；  
（六）处级副职：一级关务督察至三级关务督察；  
（七）科级正职：二级关务督察至二级关务督办；  
（八）科级副职：三级关务督察至三级关务督办；  
（九）科员职：一级关务督办至一级关务员；  
（十）办事员职：二级关务督办至二级关务员。

**Article 8** Customs staff shall have the following post-grade organization of Customs ranks:  
(1) Department-level Principal: Customs Director-General;  
(2) Department-level Deputy: Deputy Customs Director-General;  
(3) Bureau-level Principal: Grade 1 Customs Supervisor to Grade 2 Customs Supervisor;  
(4) Bureau-level Deputy: Grade 2 Customs Supervisor to Grade 3 Customs Supervisor;  
(5) Division-level Principal: Grade 3 Customs Supervisor to Grade 2 Customs Inspector;  
(6) Division-level Deputy: Grade 1 Customs Inspector to Grade 3 Customs Inspector;  
(7) Section-level Principal: Grade 2 Customs Inspector to Grade 2 Customs Superintendent;  
(8) Section-level Deputy: Grade 3 Customs Inspector to Grade 3 Customs Superintendent;  
(9) Clerk-level: Grade 1 Customs Superintendent to Grade 1 Customs Clerk;  
(10) Junior Staff-level: Grade 2 Customs Superintendent to Grade 2 Customs Clerk.

### 第三章　关衔的授予

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　关衔的授予以海关工作人员现任职务、德才表现、任职时间和工作年限为依据。

**Article 9** The conferment of Customs ranks shall be based on the Customs staff's current post, moral character and professional competence, length of tenure, and years of service.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　海关招考录用海关工作人员或者从其他部门调任海关工作人员，在确定职务后授予相应的关衔。

**Article 10** When Customs recruits or transfers in Customs staff from other departments, the corresponding Customs rank shall be conferred after the post is determined.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　关衔按照下列规定权限批准授予：  
（一）海关总监、海关副总监、一级关务监督、二级关务监督由国务院总理批准授予；  
（二）三级关务监督至三级关务督察，由海关总署署长批准授予；  
（三）海关总署机关及海关总署派出机构的一级关务督办以下的关衔由海关总署政治部主任批准授予；  
（四）各直属海关、隶属海关的一级关务督办以下的关衔由各直属海关关长批准授予。

**Article 11** The approval and conferment of Customs ranks shall be authorized in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) Customs Director-General, Deputy Customs Director-General, Grade 1 Customs Supervisor, and Grade 2 Customs Supervisor shall be approved and conferred by the Premier of the State Council;  
(2) Customs ranks from Grade 3 Customs Supervisor to Grade 3 Customs Inspector shall be approved and conferred by the Director-General of the General Administration of Customs;  
(3) Customs ranks below Grade 1 Customs Superintendent in the General Administration of Customs and its dispatched agencies shall be approved and conferred by the Director of Political Affairs of the General Administration of Customs;  
(4) Customs ranks below Grade 1 Customs Superintendent in each directly subordinate customs and subordinate customs shall be approved and conferred by the Commissioner of the respective directly subordinate customs.

### 第四章　关衔的晋级

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　二级关务督察以下关衔的海关工作人员，在其职务等级编制关衔幅度内，按照下列期限晋级：  
二级关务员至一级关务督办，每三年晋升一级；  
一级关务督办至一级关务督察，每四年晋升一级。

**Article 12** Customs staff with Customs ranks below Grade 2 Customs Inspector shall be promoted within the range of their post-grade organization of Customs ranks in accordance with the following periods:  
From Grade 2 Customs Clerk to Grade 1 Customs Superintendent, one level shall be promoted every three years;  
From Grade 1 Customs Superintendent to Grade 1 Customs Inspector, one level shall be promoted every four years.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　二级关务督察以下关衔的海关工作人员，晋级期限届满，经考核具备晋级条件的，应当逐级晋升；不具备晋级条件的，应当延期晋升。在工作中有突出功绩的，经批准可以提前晋升。

**Article 13** Customs staff with Customs ranks below Grade 2 Customs Inspector, upon expiry of the promotion period and having passed the assessment with the qualifications for promotion, shall be promoted level by level; those without the qualifications shall have their promotion deferred. Those with outstanding achievements in work may, with approval, be promoted ahead of schedule.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　一级关务督察以上关衔的海关工作人员晋级，在职务等级编制关衔幅度内，根据其德才表现和工作实绩实行选升。

**Article 14** Promotion of Customs staff with Customs ranks above Grade 1 Customs Inspector shall be conducted by selective promotion within the range of their post-grade organization of Customs ranks, based on their moral character, professional competence, and work performance.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　海关工作人员提升职务，其关衔低于新任职务等级编制关衔的最低关衔的，应当晋升至新任职务等级编制关衔的最低关衔。

**Article 15** Where a Customs staff member's post is promoted and his/her Customs rank is lower than the minimum rank of the post-grade organization of the new post, it shall be promoted to the minimum rank of the post-grade organization of the new post.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　关务员晋升关务督办，关务督办晋升关务督察，关务督察晋升关务监督，经培训合格后，方可晋升。

**Article 16** Customs Clerk promoted to Customs Superintendent, Customs Superintendent promoted to Customs Inspector, and Customs Inspector promoted to Customs Supervisor may only be promoted after passing the required training.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　关衔晋级的批准权限适用[本条例第十一条](#art-11)的规定。

**Article 17** The authority for approval of promotion of Customs ranks shall be governed by the provisions of Article 11 of these Regulations.

### 第五章　关衔的保留、降级、取消

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　海关工作人员退休后，其关衔予以保留，但不得佩带标志。  
海关工作人员调离、辞职或者被辞退的，其关衔不予保留。

**Article 18** After a Customs staff member retires, his/her Customs rank shall be retained, but no insignia shall be worn.  
If a Customs staff member is transferred out, resigns, or is dismissed, his/her Customs rank shall not be retained.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　海关工作人员因不胜任现任职务被调任下级职务的，其关衔高于新任职务等级编制关衔的最高关衔的，应当降级至新任职务等级编制关衔的最高关衔。关衔降级的批准权限与原关衔的批准权限相同。

**Article 19** Where a Customs staff member is transferred to a lower post due to incompetence in the current post, and his/her Customs rank is higher than the maximum rank of the post-grade organization of the new post, it shall be downgraded to the maximum rank of the post-grade organization of the new post. The authority for approval of the downgrade of the Customs rank shall be the same as the authority for the original rank.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　海关工作人员受到降级、撤职行政处分的，应当相应降低关衔。关衔降级的批准权限与原关衔的批准权限相同。关衔降级后，其关衔晋级的期限按照降级后的关衔等级重新计算。  
关衔降级不适用于二级关务员。

**Article 20** Where a Customs staff member is subject to the administrative sanction of demotion or removal from office, his/her Customs rank shall be correspondingly reduced. The authority for approval of the reduction of the Customs rank shall be the same as the authority for the original rank. After the reduction, the period for promotion of the Customs rank shall be recalculated based on the reduced rank.  
The reduction of Customs rank shall not apply to Grade 2 Customs Clerk.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　海关工作人员受到开除行政处分的，因犯罪被依法判处剥夺政治权利或者有期徒刑以上刑罚的，其关衔相应取消，并且不再履行批准手续。  
退休的海关工作人员犯罪的，适用前款规定。

**Article 21** Where a Customs staff member is subject to the administrative sanction of dismissal, or is sentenced according to law to deprivation of political rights or to fixed-term imprisonment or heavier punishment for a crime, his/her Customs rank shall be correspondingly rescinded, and no approval procedures shall be gone through.  
Where a retired Customs staff member commits a crime, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　海关关衔标志式样和佩带办法，由国务院规定。

**Article 22** The designs and wearing methods of Customs rank insignia shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 23** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
