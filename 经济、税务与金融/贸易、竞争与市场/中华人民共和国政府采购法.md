# 中华人民共和国政府采购法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2014-08-31  
**生效日期**：2014-08-31

> （2002年6月29日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十八次会议通过　根据2014年8月31日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国保险法〉等五部法律的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　政府采购当事人 第三章　政府采购方式 第四章　政府采购程序 第五章　政府采购合同 第六章　质疑与投诉 第七章　监督检查 第八章　法律责任 第九章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范政府采购行为，提高政府采购资金的使用效益，维护国家利益和社会公共利益，保护政府采购当事人的合法权益，促进廉政建设，制定本法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内进行的政府采购适用本法。 本法所称政府采购，是指各级国家机关、事业单位和团体组织，使用财政性资金采购依法制定的集中采购目录以内的或者采购限额标准以上的货物、工程和服务的行为。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范政府采购行为，提高政府采购资金的使用效益，维护国家利益和社会公共利益，保护政府采购当事人的合法权益，促进廉政建设，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of regulating government procurement activities, improving the efficiency of the use of government procurement funds, safeguarding national interests and public interests, protecting the lawful rights and interests of parties to government procurement, and promoting integrity.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内进行的政府采购适用本法。  
本法所称政府采购，是指各级国家机关、事业单位和团体组织，使用财政性资金采购依法制定的集中采购目录以内的或者采购限额标准以上的货物、工程和服务的行为。  
政府集中采购目录和采购限额标准依照本法规定的权限制定。  
本法所称采购，是指以合同方式有偿取得货物、工程和服务的行为，包括购买、租赁、委托、雇用等。  
本法所称货物，是指各种形态和种类的物品，包括原材料、燃料、设备、产品等。  
本法所称工程，是指建设工程，包括建筑物和构筑物的新建、改建、扩建、装修、拆除、修缮等。  
本法所称服务，是指除货物和工程以外的其他政府采购对象。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-2" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 2** Any government procurement conducted within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be governed by this Law.  
For the purposes of this Law, 'government procurement' means the conduct of purchasing goods, works and services by state organs, institutions and organizations at all levels using fiscal funds within the centralized procurement catalogue formulated according to law or above the procurement quota standards.  
The centralized procurement catalogue and procurement quota standards shall be formulated in accordance with the authority prescribed by this Law.  
For the purposes of this Law, 'procurement' means the conduct of obtaining goods, works and services for compensation through contracts, including purchase, lease, mandate and employment, etc.  
For the purposes of this Law, 'goods' refer to articles in various forms and types, including raw materials, fuel, equipment, products, etc.  
For the purposes of this Law, 'works' refer to construction projects, including the construction, renovation, extension, decoration, demolition, repair of buildings and structures, etc.  
For the purposes of this Law, 'services' refer to government procurement items other than goods and works.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　政府采购应当遵循公开透明原则、公平竞争原则、公正原则和诚实信用原则。

**Article 3** Government procurement shall follow the principles of openness and transparency, fair competition, impartiality and good faith.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　政府采购工程进行招标投标的，适用招标投标法。

**Article 4** Where the procurement of works by government is subject to tender and bidding, the Tender and Bidding Law shall apply.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　任何单位和个人不得采用任何方式，阻挠和限制供应商自由进入本地区和本行业的政府采购市场。

**Article 5** No unit or individual may, by any means, obstruct or restrict suppliers from freely entering the government procurement market of their own regions or industries.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　政府采购应当严格按照批准的预算执行。

**Article 6** Government procurement shall be strictly implemented in accordance with the approved budget.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　政府采购实行集中采购和分散采购相结合。集中采购的范围由省级以上人民政府公布的集中采购目录确定。  
属于中央预算的政府采购项目，其集中采购目录由国务院确定并公布；属于地方预算的政府采购项目，其集中采购目录由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者其授权的机构确定并公布。  
纳入集中采购目录的政府采购项目，应当实行集中采购。

**Article 7** Government procurement shall combine centralized procurement and decentralized procurement. The scope of centralized procurement shall be determined by the centralized procurement catalogue published by the people's governments at or above the provincial level.  
For government procurement items under the central budget, the centralized procurement catalogue shall be determined and published by the State Council; for government procurement items under local budgets, the centralized procurement catalogue shall be determined and published by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government or their authorized agencies.  
Government procurement items included in the centralized procurement catalogue shall be subject to centralized procurement.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　政府采购限额标准，属于中央预算的政府采购项目，由国务院确定并公布；属于地方预算的政府采购项目，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者其授权的机构确定并公布。

**Article 8** The procurement quota standards for government procurement items under the central budget shall be determined and published by the State Council; those for government procurement items under local budgets shall be determined and published by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government or their authorized agencies.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　政府采购应当有助于实现国家的经济和社会发展政策目标，包括保护环境，扶持不发达地区和少数民族地区，促进中小企业发展等。

**Article 9** Government procurement shall contribute to achieving the national economic and social development policy objectives, including protection of the environment, support for underdeveloped areas and minority nationality regions, facilitation of the development of small and medium-sized enterprises, etc.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　政府采购应当采购本国货物、工程和服务。但有下列情形之一的除外：  
（一）需要采购的货物、工程或者服务在中国境内无法获取或者无法以合理的商业条件获取的；  
（二）为在中国境外使用而进行采购的；  
（三）其他法律、行政法规另有规定的。  
前款所称本国货物、工程和服务的界定，依照国务院有关规定执行。

**Article 10** Government procurement shall procure domestic goods, works and services, except under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the goods, works or services needed cannot be obtained within the territory of China or cannot be obtained on reasonable commercial terms;  
(2) the procurement is made for use outside the territory of China; or  
(3) otherwise provided for by other laws and administrative regulations.  
The definition of domestic goods, works and services referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　政府采购的信息应当在政府采购监督管理部门指定的媒体上及时向社会公开发布，但涉及商业秘密的除外。

**Article 11** Information on government procurement shall be published timely to the public on the media designated by the government procurement supervision and administration department, except for trade secrets.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在政府采购活动中，采购人员及相关人员与供应商有利害关系的，必须回避。供应商认为采购人员及相关人员与其他供应商有利害关系的，可以申请其回避。  
前款所称相关人员，包括招标采购中评标委员会的组成人员，竞争性谈判采购中谈判小组的组成人员，询价采购中询价小组的组成人员等。

**Article 12** In government procurement activities, if a purchaser and relevant persons have an interest in a supplier, they must withdraw. If a supplier believes that the purchaser and relevant persons have an interest in other suppliers, it may apply for their withdrawal.  
The term 'relevant persons' as referred to in the preceding paragraph include the members of the bid evaluation committee in tender procurement, the members of the negotiation team in competitive negotiation procurement, the members of the inquiry team in inquiry procurement, etc.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　各级人民政府财政部门是负责政府采购监督管理的部门，依法履行对政府采购活动的监督管理职责。  
各级人民政府其他有关部门依法履行与政府采购活动有关的监督管理职责。

**Article 13** The finance departments of the people's governments at all levels shall be the departments responsible for the supervision and administration of government procurement, and shall perform the duties of supervision and administration over government procurement activities according to law.  
Other relevant departments of the people's governments at all levels shall perform the duties of supervision and administration related to government procurement activities according to law.

### 第二章　政府采购当事人

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　政府采购当事人是指在政府采购活动中享有权利和承担义务的各类主体，包括采购人、供应商和采购代理机构等。

**Article 14** Parties to government procurement refer to various entities that enjoy rights and assume obligations in government procurement activities, including procuring entities, suppliers, and procurement agencies, etc.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　采购人是指依法进行政府采购的国家机关、事业单位、团体组织。

**Article 15** A procuring entity refers to a state organ, institution, or organization that conducts government procurement according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　集中采购机构为采购代理机构。设区的市、自治州以上人民政府根据本级政府采购项目组织集中采购的需要设立集中采购机构。  
集中采购机构是非营利事业法人，根据采购人的委托办理采购事宜。

**Article 16** Centralized procurement agencies shall be procurement agencies. The people's governments of cities divided into districts or autonomous prefectures and above shall establish centralized procurement agencies in light of the need for organizing centralized procurement of government procurement items at the corresponding level.  
A centralized procurement agency shall be a non-profit institutional legal person and shall handle procurement matters upon the entrustment of the procuring entity.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　集中采购机构进行政府采购活动，应当符合采购价格低于市场平均价格、采购效率更高、采购质量优良和服务良好的要求。

**Article 17** The government procurement activities conducted by a centralized procurement agency shall meet the requirements that the procurement price is lower than the average market price, the procurement efficiency is higher, the procurement quality is fine, and the service is good.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　采购人采购纳入集中采购目录的政府采购项目，必须委托集中采购机构代理采购；采购未纳入集中采购目录的政府采购项目，可以自行采购，也可以委托集中采购机构在委托的范围内代理采购。  
纳入集中采购目录属于通用的政府采购项目的，应当委托集中采购机构代理采购；属于本部门、本系统有特殊要求的项目，应当实行部门集中采购；属于本单位有特殊要求的项目，经省级以上人民政府批准，可以自行采购。

**Article 18** Where a procuring entity procures government procurement items that are included in the centralized procurement catalogue, it must entrust the centralized procurement agency to conduct the procurement; where it procures government procurement items that are not included in the centralized procurement catalogue, it may conduct the procurement on its own, or entrust the centralized procurement agency to conduct the procurement within the scope of entrustment.  
Where the government procurement items included in the centralized procurement catalogue are generic, the procuring entity shall entrust the centralized procurement agency to conduct the procurement; where the items have special requirements of the department or system, the procurement shall be conducted by departmental centralized procurement; where the items have special requirements of the unit, upon approval of the people's government at or above the provincial level, the procuring entity may conduct the procurement on its own.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　采购人可以委托集中采购机构以外的采购代理机构，在委托的范围内办理政府采购事宜。  
采购人有权自行选择采购代理机构，任何单位和个人不得以任何方式为采购人指定采购代理机构。

**Article 19** A procuring entity may entrust a procurement agency other than the centralized procurement agency to handle government procurement matters within the scope of entrustment.  
A procuring entity shall have the right to independently select a procurement agency, and no unit or individual may, in any way, designate a procurement agency for the procuring entity.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　采购人依法委托采购代理机构办理采购事宜的，应当由采购人与采购代理机构签订委托代理协议，依法确定委托代理的事项，约定双方的权利义务。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-16" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第16条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 20** Where a procuring entity entrusts a procurement agency to handle procurement matters according to law, the procuring entity and the procurement agency shall enter into an agency agreement, determine the matters of entrustment according to law, and agree on the rights and obligations of both parties.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　供应商是指向采购人提供货物、工程或者服务的法人、其他组织或者自然人。

**Article 21** Supplier means a legal person, other organization or natural person that supplies goods, engineering or services to the procuring entity.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　供应商参加政府采购活动应当具备下列条件：  
（一）具有独立承担民事责任的能力；  
（二）具有良好的商业信誉和健全的财务会计制度；  
（三）具有履行合同所必需的设备和专业技术能力；  
（四）有依法缴纳税收和社会保障资金的良好记录；  
（五）参加政府采购活动前三年内，在经营活动中没有重大违法记录；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。  
采购人可以根据采购项目的特殊要求，规定供应商的特定条件，但不得以不合理的条件对供应商实行差别待遇或者歧视待遇。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-17" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第17条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-19" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第19条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 22** A supplier shall meet the following conditions to participate in government procurement activities:  
(1) having the capacity to independently bear civil liability;  
(2) having a good business reputation and a sound financial accounting system;  
(3) having the equipment and professional technical capabilities necessary for performing the contract;  
(4) having a good record of paying taxes and social security funds according to law;  
(5) having no serious illegal record in business operations within the three years before participating in government procurement activities;  
(6) other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
The procuring entity may, based on the special requirements of the procurement project, prescribe specific conditions for suppliers, but shall not impose differential treatment or discriminatory treatment on suppliers with unreasonable conditions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　采购人可以要求参加政府采购的供应商提供有关资质证明文件和业绩情况，并根据本法规定的供应商条件和采购项目对供应商的特定要求，对供应商的资格进行审查。

**Article 23** The procuring entity may require suppliers participating in government procurement to provide relevant qualification certification documents and performance records, and shall examine the qualifications of suppliers in accordance with the conditions for suppliers prescribed by this Law and the specific requirements for suppliers of the procurement project.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　两个以上的自然人、法人或者其他组织可以组成一个联合体，以一个供应商的身份共同参加政府采购。  
以联合体形式进行政府采购的，参加联合体的供应商均应当具备[本法第二十二条](#art-22)规定的条件，并应当向采购人提交联合协议，载明联合体各方承担的工作和义务。联合体各方应当共同与采购人签订采购合同，就采购合同约定的事项对采购人承担连带责任。

**Article 24** Two or more natural persons, legal persons or other organizations may form a consortium to jointly participate in government procurement as one supplier.  
Where government procurement is conducted in the form of a consortium, all suppliers participating in the consortium shall meet the conditions prescribed in Article 22 of this Law, and shall submit a consortium agreement to the procuring entity, specifying the work and obligations to be undertaken by each party to the consortium. All parties to the consortium shall jointly sign the procurement contract with the procuring entity and bear joint and several liability to the procuring entity for matters agreed upon in the procurement contract.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　政府采购当事人不得相互串通损害国家利益、社会公共利益和其他当事人的合法权益；不得以任何手段排斥其他供应商参与竞争。  
供应商不得以向采购人、采购代理机构、评标委员会的组成人员、竞争性谈判小组的组成人员、询价小组的组成人员行贿或者采取其他不正当手段谋取中标或者成交。  
采购代理机构不得以向采购人行贿或者采取其他不正当手段谋取非法利益。

**Article 25** Parties to government procurement shall not collude with each other to harm the national interest, the public interest and the lawful rights and interests of other parties; and shall not use any means to exclude other suppliers from participating in competition.  
Suppliers shall not seek winning the bid or the deal by bribing the procuring entity, procurement agency, members of the bid evaluation committee, members of the competitive negotiation panel, members of the inquiry panel or by adopting other illegitimate means.  
Procurement agencies shall not seek illegal benefits by bribing the procuring entity or by adopting other illegitimate means.

### 第三章　政府采购方式

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　政府采购采用以下方式：  
（一）公开招标；  
（二）邀请招标；  
（三）竞争性谈判；  
（四）单一来源采购；  
（五）询价；  
（六）国务院政府采购监督管理部门认定的其他采购方式。  
公开招标应作为政府采购的主要采购方式。

**Article 26** Government procurement shall adopt the following methods:  
(1) open tender;  
(2) invited tender;  
(3) competitive negotiation;  
(4) single-source procurement;  
(5) inquiry;  
(6) other procurement methods recognized by the government procurement supervision and administration department of the State Council.  
Open tender shall be the main procurement method for government procurement.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　采购人采购货物或者服务应当采用公开招标方式的，其具体数额标准，属于中央预算的政府采购项目，由国务院规定；属于地方预算的政府采购项目，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定；因特殊情况需要采用公开招标以外的采购方式的，应当在采购活动开始前获得设区的市、自治州以上人民政府采购监督管理部门的批准。

**Article 27** Where the procuring entity shall adopt the open tender method for the procurement of goods or services, the specific amount thresholds for government procurement projects under the central budget shall be prescribed by the State Council; for those under the local budgets, shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government; where special circumstances require the adoption of procurement methods other than open tender, approval shall be obtained from the government procurement supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the level of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture before the commencement of procurement activities.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　采购人不得将应当以公开招标方式采购的货物或者服务化整为零或者以其他任何方式规避公开招标采购。

**Article 28** The procuring entity shall not break up the goods or services that shall be procured by open tender into smaller parts or avoid open tender procurement by any other means.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　符合下列情形之一的货物或者服务，可以依照本法采用邀请招标方式采购：  
（一）具有特殊性，只能从有限范围的供应商处采购的；  
（二）采用公开招标方式的费用占政府采购项目总价值的比例过大的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-23" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第23条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 29** Goods or services that meet any of the following circumstances may be procured by invited tender in accordance with this Law:  
(1) being of a special nature and can only be procured from suppliers within a limited scope;  
(2) the proportion of costs of adopting the open tender method to the total value of the government procurement project is excessively large.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　符合下列情形之一的货物或者服务，可以依照本法采用竞争性谈判方式采购：  
（一）招标后没有供应商投标或者没有合格标的或者重新招标未能成立的；  
（二）技术复杂或者性质特殊，不能确定详细规格或者具体要求的；  
（三）采用招标所需时间不能满足用户紧急需要的；  
（四）不能事先计算出价格总额的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-26" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第26条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 30** Goods or services that meet any of the following circumstances may be procured by competitive negotiation in accordance with this Law:  
(1) after tendering, no supplier bids or there is no qualified bid, or re-tendering fails;  
(2) the technology is complex or the nature is special, making it impossible to determine detailed specifications or specific requirements;  
(3) the time required for adopting the tender method cannot meet the urgent needs of the user;  
(4) the total price cannot be calculated in advance.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　符合下列情形之一的货物或者服务，可以依照本法采用单一来源方式采购：  
（一）只能从唯一供应商处采购的；  
（二）发生了不可预见的紧急情况不能从其他供应商处采购的；  
（三）必须保证原有采购项目一致性或者服务配套的要求，需要继续从原供应商处添购，且添购资金总额不超过原合同采购金额百分之十的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-27" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第27条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-38" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第38条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 31** Goods or services that meet any of the following circumstances may be procured by single-source method in accordance with this Law:  
(1) can only be procured from a single supplier;  
(2) an unforeseeable emergency occurs and procurement cannot be made from other suppliers;  
(3) it is necessary to ensure consistency with the original procurement project or requirements for supporting services, and additional procurement from the original supplier is needed, and the total amount of additional procurement funds does not exceed ten percent of the original contract procurement amount.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　采购的货物规格、标准统一、现货货源充足且价格变化幅度小的政府采购项目，可以依照本法采用询价方式采购。

**Article 32** For government procurement projects where the specifications and standards of the goods to be procured are uniform, sufficient spot goods are available and the price fluctuation is small, the inquiry method may be adopted in accordance with this Law.

### 第四章　政府采购程序

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　负有编制部门预算职责的部门在编制下一财政年度部门预算时，应当将该财政年度政府采购的项目及资金预算列出，报本级财政部门汇总。部门预算的审批，按预算管理权限和程序进行。

**Article 33** Departments responsible for preparing departmental budgets shall, when preparing the departmental budget for the next fiscal year, list the government procurement projects and fund budgets for that fiscal year and submit them to the finance department at the same level for consolidation. The approval of departmental budgets shall be carried out in accordance with the budget management authority and procedures.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　货物或者服务项目采取邀请招标方式采购的，采购人应当从符合相应资格条件的供应商中，通过随机方式选择三家以上的供应商，并向其发出投标邀请书。

**Article 34** Where goods or services projects are procured by invited tender, the procuring entity shall select no fewer than three suppliers from those meeting the corresponding qualification conditions by random method and issue invitation for bid documents to them.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　货物和服务项目实行招标方式采购的，自招标文件开始发出之日起至投标人提交投标文件截止之日止，不得少于二十日。

**Article 35** For goods and services projects procured by tender method, the period from the date of issuance of the tender documents to the deadline for submission of bid documents by bidders shall not be less than twenty days.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　在招标采购中，出现下列情形之一的，应予废标：  
（一）符合专业条件的供应商或者对招标文件作实质响应的供应商不足三家的；  
（二）出现影响采购公正的违法、违规行为的；  
（三）投标人的报价均超过了采购预算，采购人不能支付的；  
（四）因重大变故，采购任务取消的。  
废标后，采购人应当将废标理由通知所有投标人。

**Article 36** In tender procurement, any of the following circumstances shall lead to bid rejection:  
(1) the number of suppliers meeting the professional conditions or making substantive responses to the tender documents is less than three;  
(2) illegal or non-compliant acts affecting the fairness of procurement occur;  
(3) all bidders' quotations exceed the procurement budget and the procuring entity cannot pay;  
(4) the procurement task is cancelled due to a major change.  
After bid rejection, the procuring entity shall notify all bidders of the reasons for rejection.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　废标后，除采购任务取消情形外，应当重新组织招标；需要采取其他方式采购的，应当在采购活动开始前获得设区的市、自治州以上人民政府采购监督管理部门或者政府有关部门批准。

**Article 37** After bid rejection, except where the procurement task is cancelled, a new tender shall be organized; if other procurement methods need to be adopted, approval shall be obtained from the government procurement supervision and administration department or the relevant department of the people's government at or above the level of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture before the commencement of procurement activities.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　采用竞争性谈判方式采购的，应当遵循下列程序：  
（一）成立谈判小组。谈判小组由采购人的代表和有关专家共三人以上的单数组成，其中专家的人数不得少于成员总数的三分之二。  
（二）制定谈判文件。谈判文件应当明确谈判程序、谈判内容、合同草案的条款以及评定成交的标准等事项。  
（三）确定邀请参加谈判的供应商名单。谈判小组从符合相应资格条件的供应商名单中确定不少于三家的供应商参加谈判，并向其提供谈判文件。  
（四）谈判。谈判小组所有成员集中与单一供应商分别进行谈判。在谈判中，谈判的任何一方不得透露与谈判有关的其他供应商的技术资料、价格和其他信息。谈判文件有实质性变动的，谈判小组应当以书面形式通知所有参加谈判的供应商。  
（五）确定成交供应商。谈判结束后，谈判小组应当要求所有参加谈判的供应商在规定时间内进行最后报价，采购人从谈判小组提出的成交候选人中根据符合采购需求、质量和服务相等且报价最低的原则确定成交供应商，并将结果通知所有参加谈判的未成交的供应商。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-37" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第37条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 38** Procurement by competitive negotiation shall follow the following procedures:  
(1) Establishment of a negotiation panel. The negotiation panel shall be composed of representatives of the procuring entity and relevant experts, with an odd number of no fewer than three members, and the number of experts shall not be less than two-thirds of the total members.  
(2) Formulation of negotiation documents. The negotiation documents shall specify the negotiation procedures, negotiation content, terms of the draft contract and the standards for evaluating and determining the successful supplier, among other matters.  
(3) Determination of the list of suppliers invited to participate in the negotiation. The negotiation panel shall determine no fewer than three suppliers from the list of suppliers meeting the corresponding qualification conditions to participate in the negotiation and provide them with the negotiation documents.  
(4) Negotiation. All members of the negotiation panel shall conduct negotiations with each single supplier separately. During the negotiation, neither party to the negotiation shall disclose the technical data, price or other information of other suppliers related to the negotiation. If the negotiation documents undergo substantive changes, the negotiation panel shall notify all suppliers participating in the negotiation in writing.  
(5) Determination of the successful supplier. After the negotiation ends, the negotiation panel shall require all suppliers participating in the negotiation to make their final quotations within a specified time. The procuring entity shall determine the successful supplier from the candidates for successful transaction proposed by the negotiation panel based on the principle of meeting the procurement requirements with equal quality and service and the lowest quotation, and notify the result to all unsuccessful suppliers that participated in the negotiation.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　采取单一来源方式采购的，采购人与供应商应当遵循本法规定的原则，在保证采购项目质量和双方商定合理价格的基础上进行采购。

**Article 39** For procurement by single-source method, the procuring entity and the supplier shall conduct procurement in accordance with the principles prescribed in this Law, on the basis of ensuring the quality of the procurement project and negotiating a reasonable price between the two parties.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　采取询价方式采购的，应当遵循下列程序：  
（一）成立询价小组。询价小组由采购人的代表和有关专家共三人以上的单数组成，其中专家的人数不得少于成员总数的三分之二。询价小组应当对采购项目的价格构成和评定成交的标准等事项作出规定。  
（二）确定被询价的供应商名单。询价小组根据采购需求，从符合相应资格条件的供应商名单中确定不少于三家的供应商，并向其发出询价通知书让其报价。  
（三）询价。询价小组要求被询价的供应商一次报出不得更改的价格。  
（四）确定成交供应商。采购人根据符合采购需求、质量和服务相等且报价最低的原则确定成交供应商，并将结果通知所有被询价的未成交的供应商。

**Article 40** Procurement by inquiry method shall follow the following procedures:  
(1) Establishment of an inquiry panel. The inquiry panel shall be composed of representatives of the procuring entity and relevant experts, with an odd number of no fewer than three members, and the number of experts shall not be less than two-thirds of the total members. The inquiry panel shall prescribe the price composition of the procurement project and the standards for evaluating and determining the successful supplier, among other matters.  
(2) Determination of the list of suppliers to be inquired. The inquiry panel shall, based on the procurement needs, determine no fewer than three suppliers from the list of suppliers meeting the corresponding qualification conditions and issue inquiry notices to them for quotations.  
(3) Inquiry. The inquiry panel shall require the suppliers being inquired to submit a quotation that shall not be changed once made.  
(4) Determination of the successful supplier. The procuring entity shall determine the successful supplier based on the principle of meeting the procurement requirements with equal quality and service and the lowest quotation, and notify the result to all unsuccessful suppliers that were inquired.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　采购人或者其委托的采购代理机构应当组织对供应商履约的验收。大型或者复杂的政府采购项目，应当邀请国家认可的质量检测机构参加验收工作。验收方成员应当在验收书上签字，并承担相应的法律责任。

**Article 41** The procuring entity or its entrusted procurement agency shall organize the acceptance inspection of the supplier's performance. For large or complex government procurement projects, a nationally recognized quality inspection institution shall be invited to participate in the acceptance inspection. Members of the acceptance inspection party shall sign the acceptance certificate and bear corresponding legal liability.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　采购人、采购代理机构对政府采购项目每项采购活动的采购文件应当妥善保存，不得伪造、变造、隐匿或者销毁。采购文件的保存期限为从采购结束之日起至少保存十五年。  
采购文件包括采购活动记录、采购预算、招标文件、投标文件、评标标准、评估报告、定标文件、合同文本、验收证明、质疑答复、投诉处理决定及其他有关文件、资料。  
采购活动记录至少应当包括下列内容：  
（一）采购项目类别、名称；  
（二）采购项目预算、资金构成和合同价格；  
（三）采购方式，采用公开招标以外的采购方式的，应当载明原因；  
（四）邀请和选择供应商的条件及原因；  
（五）评标标准及确定中标人的原因；  
（六）废标的原因；  
（七）采用招标以外采购方式的相应记载。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-46" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第46条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 42** The procuring entity and the procurement agency shall properly keep the procurement documents for each procurement activity of a government procurement project, and shall not forge, alter, conceal or destroy them. The retention period for procurement documents shall be at least fifteen years from the date of conclusion of the procurement.  
  
Procurement documents include records of procurement activities, procurement budgets, bidding documents, bid documents, bid evaluation criteria, evaluation reports, bid award documents, contract texts, acceptance certificates, replies to inquiries, decisions on complaint handling, and other relevant documents and materials.  
  
Records of procurement activities shall at least include the following:  
(1) Category and name of the procurement project;  
(2) Budget, composition of funds, and contract price of the procurement project;  
(3) Procurement method; if a method other than public tendering is adopted, the reasons shall be stated;  
(4) Conditions and reasons for inviting and selecting suppliers;  
(5) Bid evaluation criteria and reasons for determining the winning bidder;  
(6) Reasons for bid rejection;  
(7) Corresponding records when a method other than tendering is adopted.

### 第五章　政府采购合同

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　政府采购合同适用合同法。采购人和供应商之间的权利和义务，应当按照平等、自愿的原则以合同方式约定。  
采购人可以委托采购代理机构代表其与供应商签订政府采购合同。由采购代理机构以采购人名义签订合同的，应当提交采购人的授权委托书，作为合同附件。

**Article 43** A government procurement contract shall be governed by the Contract Law. The rights and obligations between the procuring entity and the supplier shall be agreed upon in a contract in accordance with the principles of equality and voluntariness.  
  
The procuring entity may entrust a procurement agency to sign a government procurement contract with the supplier on its behalf. If the procurement agency signs the contract in the name of the procuring entity, it shall submit a power of attorney from the procuring entity as an appendix to the contract.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　政府采购合同应当采用书面形式。

**Article 44** A government procurement contract shall be in written form.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国务院政府采购监督管理部门应当会同国务院有关部门，规定政府采购合同必须具备的条款。

**Article 45** The government procurement supervision and administration department of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, stipulate the necessary clauses of a government procurement contract.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　采购人与中标、成交供应商应当在中标、成交通知书发出之日起三十日内，按照采购文件确定的事项签订政府采购合同。  
中标、成交通知书对采购人和中标、成交供应商均具有法律效力。中标、成交通知书发出后，采购人改变中标、成交结果的，或者中标、成交供应商放弃中标、成交项目的，应当依法承担法律责任。

**Article 46** The procuring entity and the winning supplier or the successful supplier shall sign a government procurement contract within thirty days from the date of issuance of the notice of award or success, in accordance with the matters determined in the procurement documents.  
  
The notice of award or success shall have legal effect on both the procuring entity and the winning supplier or the successful supplier. If, after the issuance of the notice of award or success, the procuring entity alters the result of the award or success, or the winning supplier or the successful supplier abandons the awarded or successful project, they shall bear legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　政府采购项目的采购合同自签订之日起七个工作日内，采购人应当将合同副本报同级政府采购监督管理部门和有关部门备案。

**Article 47** Within seven working days from the date of signing a procurement contract for a government procurement project, the procuring entity shall submit a copy of the contract to the government procurement supervision and administration department at the same level and the relevant departments for record.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　经采购人同意，中标、成交供应商可以依法采取分包方式履行合同。  
政府采购合同分包履行的，中标、成交供应商就采购项目和分包项目向采购人负责，分包供应商就分包项目承担责任。

**Article 48** With the consent of the procuring entity, the winning supplier or the successful supplier may perform the contract by subcontracting in accordance with law.  
  
If a government procurement contract is performed by subcontracting, the winning supplier or the successful supplier shall be liable to the procuring entity for the procurement project and the subcontracted project, and the subcontractor shall be liable for the subcontracted project.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　政府采购合同履行中，采购人需追加与合同标的相同的货物、工程或者服务的，在不改变合同其他条款的前提下，可以与供应商协商签订补充合同，但所有补充合同的采购金额不得超过原合同采购金额的百分之十。

**Article 49** During the performance of a government procurement contract, if the procuring entity needs to add additional goods, works or services of the same type as the subject matter of the contract, it may negotiate with the supplier to sign a supplementary contract on the premise that no other terms of the contract are modified, provided that the total procurement amount of all supplementary contracts shall not exceed ten percent of the procurement amount of the original contract.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　政府采购合同的双方当事人不得擅自变更、中止或者终止合同。  
政府采购合同继续履行将损害国家利益和社会公共利益的，双方当事人应当变更、中止或者终止合同。有过错的一方应当承担赔偿责任，双方都有过错的，各自承担相应的责任。

**Article 50** The parties to a government procurement contract shall not alter, suspend or terminate the contract without authorization.  
  
If continuous performance of the government procurement contract would harm the national interest or the public interest, the parties shall alter, suspend or terminate the contract. The party at fault shall bear compensation liability; if both parties are at fault, each shall bear its corresponding liability.

### 第六章　质疑与投诉

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　供应商对政府采购活动事项有疑问的，可以向采购人提出询问，采购人应当及时作出答复，但答复的内容不得涉及商业秘密。

**Article 51** If a supplier has questions regarding matters of government procurement activities, it may raise inquiries with the procuring entity, and the procuring entity shall make a timely reply, provided that the content of the reply shall not involve trade secrets.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　供应商认为采购文件、采购过程和中标、成交结果使自己的权益受到损害的，可以在知道或者应知其权益受到损害之日起七个工作日内，以书面形式向采购人提出质疑。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-53" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第53条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 52** If a supplier believes that the procurement documents, the procurement process, or the results of award or success have harmed its legitimate rights and interests, it may, within seven working days from the date of knowing or should have known that its rights and interests are harmed, raise a challenge in writing to the procuring entity.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　采购人应当在收到供应商的书面质疑后七个工作日内作出答复，并以书面形式通知质疑供应商和其他有关供应商，但答复的内容不得涉及商业秘密。

**Article 53** The procuring entity shall, within seven working days after receiving the written challenge from the supplier, make a reply and notify the challenging supplier and other relevant suppliers in writing, provided that the content of the reply shall not involve trade secrets.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　采购人委托采购代理机构采购的，供应商可以向采购代理机构提出询问或者质疑，采购代理机构应当依照[本法第五十一条](#art-51)、第五十三条的规定就采购人委托授权范围内的事项作出答复。

**Article 54** If the procuring entity entrusts a procurement agency with the procurement, the supplier may raise inquiries or challenges to the procurement agency, and the procurement agency shall make a reply in accordance with the provisions of Articles 51 and 53 of this Law on matters within the scope of authorization entrusted by the procuring entity.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　质疑供应商对采购人、采购代理机构的答复不满意或者采购人、采购代理机构未在规定的时间内作出答复的，可以在答复期满后十五个工作日内向同级政府采购监督管理部门投诉。

**Article 55** If the challenging supplier is dissatisfied with the reply of the procuring entity or the procurement agency, or if the procuring entity or the procurement agency fails to make a reply within the prescribed time limit, the supplier may, within fifteen working days after the expiration of the reply period, lodge a complaint with the government procurement supervision and administration department at the same level.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　政府采购监督管理部门应当在收到投诉后三十个工作日内，对投诉事项作出处理决定，并以书面形式通知投诉人和与投诉事项有关的当事人。

**Article 56** The government procurement supervision and administration department shall, within thirty working days after receiving the complaint, make a decision on handling the complaint and notify the complainant and the parties concerned with the complaint in writing.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　政府采购监督管理部门在处理投诉事项期间，可以视具体情况书面通知采购人暂停采购活动，但暂停时间最长不得超过三十日。

**Article 57** During the period of handling the complaint, the government procurement supervision and administration department may, depending on the specific circumstances, notify the procuring entity in writing to suspend the procurement activities, provided that the suspension period shall not exceed thirty days at most.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　投诉人对政府采购监督管理部门的投诉处理决定不服或者政府采购监督管理部门逾期未作处理的，可以依法申请行政复议或者向人民法院提起行政诉讼。

**Article 58** If the complainant is dissatisfied with the complaint handling decision of the government procurement supervision and administration department, or if the department fails to handle the matter within the prescribed time limit, the complainant may apply for administrative reconsideration or file an administrative action with the People's Court according to law.

### 第七章　监督检查

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　政府采购监督管理部门应当加强对政府采购活动及集中采购机构的监督检查。  
监督检查的主要内容是：  
（一）有关政府采购的法律、行政法规和规章的执行情况；  
（二）采购范围、采购方式和采购程序的执行情况；  
（三）政府采购人员的职业素质和专业技能。

**Article 59** The government procurement supervision and administration department shall strengthen supervision and inspection of government procurement activities and centralized procurement agencies.  
  
The main contents of supervision and inspection are:  
(1) The implementation of laws, administrative regulations and rules related to government procurement;  
(2) The implementation of procurement scope, procurement methods and procurement procedures;  
(3) The professional quality and professional skills of government procurement personnel.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　政府采购监督管理部门不得设置集中采购机构，不得参与政府采购项目的采购活动。  
采购代理机构与行政机关不得存在隶属关系或者其他利益关系。

**Article 60** The government procurement supervision and administration department shall not set up centralized procurement agencies, and shall not participate in procurement activities of government procurement projects.  
  
A procurement agency shall not have a subordinate relationship or other interest relationship with an administrative organ.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　集中采购机构应当建立健全内部监督管理制度。采购活动的决策和执行程序应当明确，并相互监督、相互制约。经办采购的人员与负责采购合同审核、验收人员的职责权限应当明确，并相互分离。

**Article 61** A centralized procurement agency shall establish and improve its internal supervision and management system. The decision-making and enforcement procedures for procurement activities shall be clear, and shall provide for mutual supervision and mutual checks. The duties and powers of personnel handling procurement and those responsible for review and acceptance of procurement contracts shall be clear, and shall be mutually separate.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　集中采购机构的采购人员应当具有相关职业素质和专业技能，符合政府采购监督管理部门规定的专业岗位任职要求。  
集中采购机构对其工作人员应当加强教育和培训；对采购人员的专业水平、工作实绩和职业道德状况定期进行考核。采购人员经考核不合格的，不得继续任职。

**Article 62** The procurement personnel of a centralized procurement agency shall possess relevant professional qualities and professional skills, and shall meet the requirements for professional positions as prescribed by the government procurement supervision and administration department.  
The centralized procurement agency shall strengthen education and training for its staff; and shall regularly assess the professional level, work performance, and professional ethics of procurement personnel. Procurement personnel who fail the assessment shall not continue to hold their positions.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　政府采购项目的采购标准应当公开。  
采用本法规定的采购方式的，采购人在采购活动完成后，应当将采购结果予以公布。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-59" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第59条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 63** The procurement standards for government procurement projects shall be made public.  
Where a procurement method prescribed by this Law is adopted, the procuring entity shall, after the completion of the procurement activities, announce the procurement results.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　采购人必须按照本法规定的采购方式和采购程序进行采购。  
任何单位和个人不得违反本法规定，要求采购人或者采购工作人员向其指定的供应商进行采购。

**Article 64** A procuring entity must conduct procurement in accordance with the procurement methods and procedures prescribed by this Law.  
No unit or individual may, in violation of the provisions of this Law, require the procuring entity or its procurement personnel to procure from a supplier designated by them.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　政府采购监督管理部门应当对政府采购项目的采购活动进行检查，政府采购当事人应当如实反映情况，提供有关材料。

**Article 65** The government procurement supervision and administration department shall inspect the procurement activities of government procurement projects, and the government procurement parties shall truthfully report the situation and provide relevant materials.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　政府采购监督管理部门应当对集中采购机构的采购价格、节约资金效果、服务质量、信誉状况、有无违法行为等事项进行考核，并定期如实公布考核结果。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-60" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第60条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 66** The government procurement supervision and administration department shall assess the centralized procurement agency in terms of procurement prices, effectiveness in saving funds, service quality, reputation status, whether there is any illegal act, and other matters, and shall periodically and truthfully publish the assessment results.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　依照法律、行政法规的规定对政府采购负有行政监督职责的政府有关部门，应当按照其职责分工，加强对政府采购活动的监督。

**Article 67** Relevant government departments that have administrative supervision duties over government procurement in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations shall, in accordance with their division of duties, strengthen supervision over government procurement activities.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　审计机关应当对政府采购进行审计监督。政府采购监督管理部门、政府采购各当事人有关政府采购活动，应当接受审计机关的审计监督。

**Article 68** Audit authorities shall exercise audit supervision over government procurement. The government procurement supervision and administration department and all government procurement parties shall accept audit supervision by audit authorities in relation to government procurement activities.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　监察机关应当加强对参与政府采购活动的国家机关、国家公务员和国家行政机关任命的其他人员实施监察。

**Article 69** Supervisory organs shall strengthen supervision over state organs, state civil servants, and other personnel appointed by state administrative organs that participate in government procurement activities.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　任何单位和个人对政府采购活动中的违法行为，有权控告和检举，有关部门、机关应当依照各自职责及时处理。

**Article 70** Any unit or individual has the right to accuse and report illegal acts in government procurement activities, and the relevant departments and organs shall handle them promptly in accordance with their respective duties.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　采购人、采购代理机构有下列情形之一的，责令限期改正，给予警告，可以并处罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，由其行政主管部门或者有关机关给予处分，并予通报：  
（一）应当采用公开招标方式而擅自采用其他方式采购的；  
（二）擅自提高采购标准的；  
（三）以不合理的条件对供应商实行差别待遇或者歧视待遇的；  
（四）在招标采购过程中与投标人进行协商谈判的；  
（五）中标、成交通知书发出后不与中标、成交供应商签订采购合同的；  
（六）拒绝有关部门依法实施监督检查的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-66" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第66条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-68" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第68条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-71" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第71条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 71** Where a procuring entity or procurement agency falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit, given a warning, and may also be fined; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions by their administrative department or relevant organ, and be notified:  
(1) Where an open tender method should have been used, but another method was used without authorization;  
(2) Where the procurement standards were raised without authorization;  
(3) Where differential or discriminatory treatment is applied to suppliers based on unreasonable conditions;  
(4) Where negotiations are conducted with bidders during the tender procurement process;  
(5) Where a procurement contract is not signed with the winning or successful supplier after issuance of the notice of award or transaction;  
(6) Where supervision and inspection carried out by relevant departments according to law is refused.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　采购人、采购代理机构及其工作人员有下列情形之一，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，处以罚款，有违法所得的，并处没收违法所得，属于国家机关工作人员的，依法给予行政处分：  
（一）与供应商或者采购代理机构恶意串通的；  
（二）在采购过程中接受贿赂或者获取其他不正当利益的；  
（三）在有关部门依法实施的监督检查中提供虚假情况的；  
（四）开标前泄露标底的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-66" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第66条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-74" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第74条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 72** Where a procuring entity, procurement agency, or their staff falls under any of the following circumstances, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law if a crime is constituted; if the act does not constitute a crime, a fine shall be imposed, and if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated concurrently; if the person is a state organ staff member, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law:  
(1) Malicious collusion with a supplier or procurement agency;  
(2) Acceptance of bribes or obtaining other illegitimate benefits during the procurement process;  
(3) Provision of false information during supervision and inspection carried out by relevant departments according to law;  
(4) Disclosure of the bid base price before bid opening.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　有前两条违法行为之一影响中标、成交结果或者可能影响中标、成交结果的，按下列情况分别处理：  
（一）未确定中标、成交供应商的，终止采购活动；  
（二）中标、成交供应商已经确定但采购合同尚未履行的，撤销合同，从合格的中标、成交候选人中另行确定中标、成交供应商；  
（三）采购合同已经履行的，给采购人、供应商造成损失的，由责任人承担赔偿责任。

**Article 73** Where any of the illegal acts in the preceding two Articles affects or may affect the result of award or transaction, the following measures shall be taken respectively:  
(1) If no winning or successful supplier has been determined, the procurement activities shall be terminated;  
(2) If a winning or successful supplier has been determined but the procurement contract has not yet been performed, the contract shall be revoked, and a new winning or successful supplier shall be determined from among the qualified winning or successful candidates;  
(3) If the procurement contract has been performed, and losses have been caused to the procuring entity or supplier, the liable person shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　采购人对应当实行集中采购的政府采购项目，不委托集中采购机构实行集中采购的，由政府采购监督管理部门责令改正；拒不改正的，停止按预算向其支付资金，由其上级行政主管部门或者有关机关依法给予其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处分。

**Article 74** Where a procuring entity, for a government procurement project that should undergo centralized procurement, fails to entrust a centralized procurement agency to carry out centralized procurement, the government procurement supervision and administration department shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, payment of funds according to the budget shall be stopped, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions by the higher administrative department or relevant organ according to law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　采购人未依法公布政府采购项目的采购标准和采购结果的，责令改正，对直接负责的主管人员依法给予处分。

**Article 75** Where a procuring entity fails to publish the procurement standards and procurement results of a government procurement project according to law, it shall be ordered to make corrections, and the directly responsible person in charge shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　采购人、采购代理机构违反本法规定隐匿、销毁应当保存的采购文件或者伪造、变造采购文件的，由政府采购监督管理部门处以二万元以上十万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 76** Where a procuring entity or procurement agency, in violation of the provisions of this Law, conceals or destroys procurement documents that should be preserved, or forges or alters procurement documents, the government procurement supervision and administration department shall impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan, and shall give sanctions to the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　供应商有下列情形之一的，处以采购金额千分之五以上千分之十以下的罚款，列入不良行为记录名单，在一至三年内禁止参加政府采购活动，有违法所得的，并处没收违法所得，情节严重的，由工商行政管理机关吊销营业执照；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）提供虚假材料谋取中标、成交的；  
（二）采取不正当手段诋毁、排挤其他供应商的；  
（三）与采购人、其他供应商或者采购代理机构恶意串通的；  
（四）向采购人、采购代理机构行贿或者提供其他不正当利益的；  
（五）在招标采购过程中与采购人进行协商谈判的；  
（六）拒绝有关部门监督检查或者提供虚假情况的。  
供应商有前款第（一）至（五）项情形之一的，中标、成交无效。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-72" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第72条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例.md#art-74" title="被《中华人民共和国政府采购法实施条例》第74条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 77** Where a supplier falls under any of the following circumstances, a fine of not less than five per thousand but not more than ten per thousand of the procurement amount shall be imposed, the supplier shall be entered into the record list of bad conduct, and shall be prohibited from participating in government procurement activities for one to three years; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated concurrently; if the circumstances are serious, the business license shall be revoked by the administrative authority for industry and commerce; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Providing false materials to seek award or transaction;  
(2) Using improper means to slander or exclude other suppliers;  
(3) Malicious collusion with the procuring entity, other suppliers, or the procurement agency;  
(4) Offering bribes to the procuring entity or procurement agency, or providing other illegitimate benefits;  
(5) Conducting negotiations with the procuring entity during the tender procurement process;  
(6) Refusing supervision and inspection by relevant departments or providing false information.  
Where a supplier falls under any of the circumstances under subparagraphs (1) to (5) of the preceding paragraph, the award or transaction shall be invalid.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　采购代理机构在代理政府采购业务中有违法行为的，按照有关法律规定处以罚款，可以在一至三年内禁止其代理政府采购业务，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 78** Where a procurement agency commits an illegal act in the course of acting as an agent for government procurement business, a fine shall be imposed in accordance with relevant legal provisions, and it may be prohibited from acting as an agent for government procurement business for one to three years; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　政府采购当事人有[本法第七十一条](#art-71)、第七十二条、第七十七条违法行为之一，给他人造成损失的，并应依照有关民事法律规定承担民事责任。

**Article 79** Where a government procurement party commits any of the illegal acts under Articles 71, 72, or 77 of this Law, and causes losses to others, it shall also bear civil liability in accordance with relevant civil legal provisions.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　政府采购监督管理部门的工作人员在实施监督检查中违反本法规定滥用职权，玩忽职守，徇私舞弊的，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 80** Where a staff member of the government procurement supervision and administration department, in the course of conducting supervision and inspection, violates the provisions of this Law by abusing powers, neglecting duties, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　政府采购监督管理部门对供应商的投诉逾期未作处理的，给予直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员行政处分。

**Article 81** Where the government procurement supervision and administration department fails to handle a supplier's complaint within the prescribed time limit, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given administrative sanctions.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　政府采购监督管理部门对集中采购机构业绩的考核，有虚假陈述，隐瞒真实情况的，或者不作定期考核和公布考核结果的，应当及时纠正，由其上级机关或者监察机关对其负责人进行通报，并对直接负责的人员依法给予行政处分。  
集中采购机构在政府采购监督管理部门考核中，虚报业绩，隐瞒真实情况的，处以二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款，并予以通报；情节严重的，取消其代理采购的资格。

**Article 82** Where the government procurement supervision and administration department, in assessing the performance of a centralized procurement agency, makes false statements, conceals the true situation, or fails to conduct regular assessments and publish the assessment results, it shall promptly make corrections, and the superior organ or the supervisory organ shall circulate a notice of criticism against the person in charge, and impose administrative sanctions on the directly responsible persons according to law.  
Where a centralized procurement agency, in the assessment by the government procurement supervision and administration department, falsely reports its performance or conceals the true situation, it shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, and a notice of criticism shall be circulated; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification for acting as a procurement agent shall be revoked.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　任何单位或者个人阻挠和限制供应商进入本地区或者本行业政府采购市场的，责令限期改正；拒不改正的，由该单位、个人的上级行政主管部门或者有关机关给予单位责任人或者个人处分。

**Article 83** Where any entity or individual obstructs or restricts a supplier from entering the government procurement market of its region or industry, it or he shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit; if it or he refuses to make corrections, the competent administrative department at a higher level of the entity or individual or the relevant organ shall impose a sanction on the person in charge of the entity or the individual.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　使用国际组织和外国政府贷款进行的政府采购，贷款方、资金提供方与中方达成的协议对采购的具体条件另有规定的，可以适用其规定，但不得损害国家利益和社会公共利益。

**Article 84** Where an agreement reached between the loan provider or fund provider and the Chinese side makes separate provisions on the specific terms of procurement in government procurement conducted with loans from international organizations or foreign governments, such provisions may apply, provided that national interests and social public interests shall not be harmed.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　对因严重自然灾害和其他不可抗力事件所实施的紧急采购和涉及国家安全和秘密的采购，不适用本法。

**Article 85** This Law shall not apply to emergency procurement carried out due to severe natural disasters and other force majeure events, and to procurement involving national security and secrets.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　军事采购法规由中央军事委员会另行制定。

**Article 86** The military procurement regulations shall be separately formulated by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　本法实施的具体步骤和办法由国务院规定。

**Article 87** The specific measures and steps for implementation of this Law shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　本法自2003年1月1日起施行。

**Article 88** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2003.
