# 中华人民共和国海南自由贸易港法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2021-06-10  
**生效日期**：2021-06-10

> （2021年6月10日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十九次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　贸易自由便利 第三章　投资自由便利 第四章　财政税收制度 第五章　生态环境保护 第六章　产业发展与人才支撑 第七章　综合措施 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了建设高水平的中国特色海南自由贸易港，推动形成更高层次改革开放新格局，建立开放型经济新体制，促进社会主义市场经济平稳健康可持续发展，制定本法。 第二条　国家在海南岛全岛设立海南自由贸易港，分步骤、分阶段建立自由贸易港政策和制度体系，实现贸易、投资、跨境资金流动、人员进出、运输来往自由便利和数据安全有序流动。 海南自由贸易港建设和管理活动适用本法。本法没有规定的，适用其他有关法律法规的规定。 第三条　海南自由贸易港建设，应当体现中国特色，借鉴国际经验，围绕海南战略定位，发挥海南优势，推进改革创新，加强风险防范，贯彻创新、协调、绿色、开放、共享的新发展理念，坚持高质量发展，坚持总体国家安全观，坚持以人民为中心，实现经济繁荣、社会文明、生态宜居、人民幸福。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了建设高水平的中国特色海南自由贸易港，推动形成更高层次改革开放新格局，建立开放型经济新体制，促进社会主义市场经济平稳健康可持续发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purposes of building a high-level Hainan Free Trade Port with Chinese characteristics, promoting the formation of a new pattern of reform and opening up at a higher level, establishing a new system of an open economy, and promoting the stable, healthy and sustainable development of the socialist market economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家在海南岛全岛设立海南自由贸易港，分步骤、分阶段建立自由贸易港政策和制度体系，实现贸易、投资、跨境资金流动、人员进出、运输来往自由便利和数据安全有序流动。  
海南自由贸易港建设和管理活动适用本法。本法没有规定的，适用其他有关法律法规的规定。

**Article 2** The State shall establish the Hainan Free Trade Port on the entire island of Hainan, and shall establish the policies and institutional systems of the free trade port step by step and in stages, to achieve the free and convenient flow of trade, investment, cross-border capital flow, personnel entry and exit, transportation, and orderly flow of data security.  
This Law shall apply to the construction and management activities of the Hainan Free Trade Port. Matters not provided for in this Law shall be governed by the provisions of other relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　海南自由贸易港建设，应当体现中国特色，借鉴国际经验，围绕海南战略定位，发挥海南优势，推进改革创新，加强风险防范，贯彻创新、协调、绿色、开放、共享的新发展理念，坚持高质量发展，坚持总体国家安全观，坚持以人民为中心，实现经济繁荣、社会文明、生态宜居、人民幸福。

**Article 3** The construction of the Hainan Free Trade Port shall reflect Chinese characteristics, draw on international experience, focus on Hainan's strategic positioning, leverage Hainan's advantages, promote reform and innovation, strengthen risk prevention, implement the new development concepts of innovation, coordination, green, openness and sharing, adhere to high-quality development, uphold a holistic approach to national security, adhere to a people-centered approach, and achieve economic prosperity, social civilization, ecological livability, and people's happiness.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　海南自由贸易港建设，以贸易投资自由化便利化为重点，以各类生产要素跨境自由有序安全便捷流动和现代产业体系为支撑，以特殊的税收制度安排、高效的社会治理体系和完备的法治体系为保障，持续优化法治化、国际化、便利化的营商环境和公平统一高效的市场环境。

**Article 4** The construction of the Hainan Free Trade Port shall focus on trade and investment liberalization and facilitation, be supported by the free, orderly, safe and convenient cross-border flow of various production factors and a modern industrial system, be guaranteed by special tax system arrangements, an efficient social governance system and a complete rule of law system, and continuously optimize a legalized, internationalized and convenient business environment and a fair, unified and efficient market environment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　海南自由贸易港实行最严格的生态环境保护制度，坚持生态优先、绿色发展，创新生态文明体制机制，建设国家生态文明试验区。

**Article 5** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall implement the strictest ecological environment protection system, adhere to ecological priority and green development, innovate the systems and mechanisms for ecological civilization, and build a national ecological civilization pilot zone.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家建立海南自由贸易港建设领导机制，统筹协调海南自由贸易港建设重大政策和重大事项。国务院发展改革、财政、商务、金融管理、海关、税务等部门按照职责分工，指导推动海南自由贸易港建设相关工作。  
国家建立与海南自由贸易港建设相适应的行政管理体制，创新监管模式。  
海南省应当切实履行责任，加强组织领导，全力推进海南自由贸易港建设各项工作。

**Article 6** The State shall establish a leadership mechanism for the construction of the Hainan Free Trade Port to coordinate major policies and major matters concerning the construction of the Hainan Free Trade Port. The development and reform, finance, commerce, financial management, customs, taxation and other departments of the State Council shall, according to their respective functions and duties, guide and promote the relevant work of the construction of the Hainan Free Trade Port.  
The State shall establish an administrative management system that is compatible with the construction of the Hainan Free Trade Port, and innovate the regulatory model.  
Hainan Province shall effectively fulfill its responsibilities, strengthen organizational leadership, and fully promote all work in the construction of the Hainan Free Trade Port.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家支持海南自由贸易港建设发展，支持海南省依照中央要求和法律规定行使改革自主权。国务院及其有关部门根据海南自由贸易港建设的实际需要，及时依法授权或者委托海南省人民政府及其有关部门行使相关管理职权。

**Article 7** The State shall support the construction and development of the Hainan Free Trade Port, and support Hainan Province in exercising its autonomy in reform in accordance with the requirements of the Central Government and legal provisions. The State Council and its relevant departments shall, according to the actual needs of the construction of the Hainan Free Trade Port, timely authorize or entrust the People's Government of Hainan Province and its relevant departments to exercise the relevant management functions and powers in accordance with law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　海南自由贸易港构建系统完备、科学规范、运行有效的海南自由贸易港治理体系，推动政府机构改革和职能转变，规范政府服务标准，加强预防和化解社会矛盾机制建设，提高社会治理智能化水平，完善共建共治共享的社会治理制度。  
国家推进海南自由贸易港行政区划改革创新，优化行政区划设置和行政区划结构体系。

**Article 8** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall establish a complete, scientific, standardized, and effective governance system for the Hainan Free Trade Port, promote government institutional reform and function transformation, standardize government service standards, strengthen the mechanism for preventing and resolving social conflicts, improve the intelligent level of social governance, and improve the social governance system of joint contribution, joint governance, and shared benefits.  
The State shall promote the reform and innovation of the administrative divisions of the Hainan Free Trade Port, and optimize the setting of administrative divisions and the structural system of administrative divisions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家支持海南自由贸易港主动适应国际经济贸易规则发展和全球经济治理体系改革新趋势，积极开展国际交流合作。

**Article 9** The State shall support the Hainan Free Trade Port in actively adapting to the development of international economic and trade rules and the new trends in the reform of the global economic governance system, and actively carrying out international exchanges and cooperation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　海南省人民代表大会及其常务委员会可以根据本法，结合海南自由贸易港建设的具体情况和实际需要，遵循宪法规定和法律、行政法规的基本原则，就贸易、投资及相关管理活动制定法规（以下称海南自由贸易港法规），在海南自由贸易港范围内实施。  
海南自由贸易港法规应当报送全国人民代表大会常务委员会和国务院备案；对法律或者行政法规的规定作变通规定的，应当说明变通的情况和理由。  
海南自由贸易港法规涉及依法应当由全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会制定法律或者由国务院制定行政法规事项的，应当分别报全国人民代表大会常务委员会或者国务院批准后生效。

**Article 10** The People's Congress of Hainan Province and its Standing Committee may, in accordance with this Law, in light of the specific conditions and actual needs of the construction of the Hainan Free Trade Port, and in compliance with the provisions of the Constitution and the basic principles of laws and administrative regulations, formulate regulations on trade, investment and related management activities (hereinafter referred to as the Hainan Free Trade Port regulations) to be implemented within the scope of the Hainan Free Trade Port.  
The Hainan Free Trade Port regulations shall be submitted to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress and the State Council for the record; where they make adaptive provisions to the provisions of a law or an administrative regulation, the circumstances and reasons for the adaption shall be explained.  
Where the Hainan Free Trade Port regulations involve matters that should, in accordance with law, be formulated as laws by the National People's Congress or its Standing Committee or as administrative regulations by the State Council, they shall be submitted respectively to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress or the State Council for approval before taking effect.

### 第二章　贸易自由便利

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家建立健全全岛封关运作的海南自由贸易港海关监管特殊区域制度。在依法有效监管基础上，建立自由进出、安全便利的货物贸易管理制度，优化服务贸易管理措施，实现贸易自由化便利化。

**Article 11** The State shall establish and improve the special customs supervision area system of the Hainan Free Trade Port for the closed island operation. On the basis of effective supervision in accordance with law, establish a goods trade management system with free entry and exit, safety and convenience, optimize the management measures for trade in services, and achieve trade liberalization and facilitation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　海南自由贸易港应当高标准建设口岸基础设施，加强口岸公共卫生安全、国门生物安全、食品安全、商品质量安全管控。

**Article 12** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall build port infrastructure with high standards, and strengthen the control of port public health security, national biosecurity, food safety, and commodity quality safety.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　在境外与海南自由贸易港之间，货物、物品可以自由进出，海关依法进行监管，列入海南自由贸易港禁止、限制进出口货物、物品清单的除外。  
前款规定的清单，由国务院商务主管部门会同国务院有关部门和海南省制定。

**Article 13** Goods and articles may freely enter and exit between the territory outside China and the Hainan Free Trade Port, subject to supervision by the customs in accordance with law, except for those goods and articles listed in the catalogue of prohibited and restricted import and export goods and articles of the Hainan Free Trade Port.  
The catalogue specified in the preceding paragraph shall be formulated by the competent department of commerce of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council and Hainan Province.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　货物由海南自由贸易港进入境内其他地区（以下简称内地），原则上按进口规定办理相关手续。物品由海南自由贸易港进入内地，按规定进行监管。对海南自由贸易港前往内地的运输工具，简化进口管理。  
货物、物品以及运输工具由内地进入海南自由贸易港，按国内流通规定管理。  
货物、物品以及运输工具在海南自由贸易港和内地之间进出的具体办法由国务院有关部门会同海南省制定。

**Article 14** Goods entering other regions within the territory of China (hereinafter referred to as the mainland) from the Hainan Free Trade Port shall, in principle, go through the relevant procedures according to the import regulations. Articles entering the mainland from the Hainan Free Trade Port shall be regulated according to the regulations. For means of transport traveling from the Hainan Free Trade Port to the mainland, the import management shall be simplified.  
Goods, articles and means of transport entering the Hainan Free Trade Port from the mainland shall be managed according to the regulations on domestic circulation.  
The specific measures for the entry and exit of goods, articles and means of transport between the Hainan Free Trade Port and the mainland shall be formulated by the relevant departments of the State Council jointly with Hainan Province.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　各类市场主体在海南自由贸易港内依法自由开展货物贸易以及相关活动，海关实施低干预、高效能的监管。  
在符合环境保护、安全生产等要求的前提下，海南自由贸易港对进出口货物不设存储期限，货物存放地点可以自由选择。

**Article 15** Various market entities may freely carry out goods trade and related activities within the Hainan Free Trade Port in accordance with law, and the customs shall implement low-intervention, high-efficiency supervision.  
On the condition that the requirements of environmental protection, safe production, etc. are met, the Hainan Free Trade Port shall not set a storage period for import and export goods, and the storage location of goods may be freely chosen.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　海南自由贸易港实行通关便利化政策，简化货物流转流程和手续。除依法需要检验检疫或者实行许可证件管理的货物外，货物进入海南自由贸易港，海关按照有关规定径予放行，为市场主体提供通关便利服务。

**Article 16** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall implement customs clearance facilitation policies and simplify the flow process and procedures of goods. Except for goods that are subject to inspection and quarantine or implement permit management in accordance with law, goods entering the Hainan Free Trade Port shall be directly released by the customs in accordance with the relevant regulations, providing customs clearance facilitation services for market entities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　海南自由贸易港对跨境服务贸易实行负面清单管理制度，并实施相配套的资金支付和转移制度。对清单之外的跨境服务贸易，按照内外一致的原则管理。  
海南自由贸易港跨境服务贸易负面清单由国务院商务主管部门会同国务院有关部门和海南省制定。

**Article 17** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall implement a negative list management system for cross-border trade in services, and implement a supporting system for fund payment and transfer. Cross-border trade in services not on the list shall be managed according to the principle of equal treatment for domestic and foreign entities.  
The negative list for cross-border trade in services of the Hainan Free Trade Port shall be formulated by the competent department of commerce of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council and Hainan Province.

### 第三章　投资自由便利

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　海南自由贸易港实行投资自由化便利化政策，全面推行极简审批投资制度，完善投资促进和投资保护制度，强化产权保护，保障公平竞争，营造公开、透明、可预期的投资环境。  
海南自由贸易港全面放开投资准入，涉及国家安全、社会稳定、生态保护红线、重大公共利益等国家实行准入管理的领域除外。

**Article 18** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall implement investment liberalization and facilitation policies, comprehensively promote a minimalist approval investment system, improve the investment promotion and investment protection systems, strengthen the protection of property rights, guarantee fair competition, and create an open, transparent and predictable investment environment.  
The Hainan Free Trade Port shall fully liberalize investment access, except for areas where the State implements access management involving national security, social stability, ecological protection red lines, major public interests, etc.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　海南自由贸易港对外商投资实行准入前国民待遇加负面清单管理制度。特别适用于海南自由贸易港的外商投资准入负面清单由国务院有关部门会同海南省制定，报国务院批准后发布。

**Article 19** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall implement a pre-establishment national treatment plus negative list management system for foreign investment. The negative list for foreign investment access specifically applicable to the Hainan Free Trade Port shall be formulated by the relevant departments of the State Council jointly with Hainan Province, and shall be published after being submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家放宽海南自由贸易港市场准入。海南自由贸易港放宽市场准入特别清单（特别措施）由国务院有关部门会同海南省制定。  
海南自由贸易港实行以过程监管为重点的投资便利措施，逐步实施市场准入承诺即入制。具体办法由海南省会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 20** The State shall relax market access in the Hainan Free Trade Port. The special list (special measures) for relaxing market access in the Hainan Free Trade Port shall be formulated by the relevant departments of the State Council jointly with Hainan Province.  
The Hainan Free Trade Port shall implement investment facilitation measures focusing on process supervision, and gradually implement the market access commitment system. The specific measures shall be formulated by Hainan Province jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　海南自由贸易港按照便利、高效、透明的原则，简化办事程序，提高办事效率，优化政务服务，建立市场主体设立便利、经营便利、注销便利等制度，优化破产程序。具体办法由海南省人民代表大会及其常务委员会制定。

**Article 21** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall, in accordance with the principles of convenience, efficiency and transparency, simplify procedures, improve efficiency, optimize government services, establish systems for facilitating the establishment, operation and termination of market entities, and optimize bankruptcy procedures. The specific measures shall be formulated by the People's Congress of Hainan Province or its Standing Committee.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家依法保护自然人、法人和非法人组织在海南自由贸易港内的投资、收益和其他合法权益，加强对中小投资者的保护。

**Article 22** The State shall protect, in accordance with law, the investment, income and other legitimate rights and interests of natural persons, legal persons and unincorporated organizations within the Hainan Free Trade Port, and strengthen the protection of small and medium investors.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家依法保护海南自由贸易港内自然人、法人和非法人组织的知识产权，促进知识产权创造、运用和管理服务能力提升，建立健全知识产权领域信用分类监管、失信惩戒等机制，对知识产权侵权行为，严格依法追究法律责任。

**Article 23** The State shall protect, in accordance with law, the intellectual property of natural persons, legal persons and unincorporated organizations within the Hainan Free Trade Port, promote the improvement of intellectual property creation, application and management service capabilities, establish and improve mechanisms such as credit classification supervision and punishment for breach of trust in the field of intellectual property, and strictly pursue legal liability for intellectual property infringement in accordance with law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　海南自由贸易港建立统一开放、竞争有序的市场体系，强化竞争政策的基础性地位，落实公平竞争审查制度，加强和改进反垄断和反不正当竞争执法，保护市场公平竞争。  
海南自由贸易港的各类市场主体，在准入许可、经营运营、要素获取、标准制定、优惠政策等方面依法享受平等待遇。具体办法由海南省人民代表大会及其常务委员会制定。

**Article 24** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall establish a unified, open, competitive and orderly market system, strengthen the fundamental position of competition policy, implement the fair competition review system, strengthen and improve the enforcement of anti-monopoly and anti-unfair competition laws, and protect fair competition in the market.  
Various market entities in the Hainan Free Trade Port shall enjoy equal treatment in accordance with law in aspects such as access licensing, operation, factor acquisition, standard setting, and preferential policies. The specific measures shall be formulated by the People's Congress of Hainan Province or its Standing Committee.

### 第四章　财政税收制度

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　在海南自由贸易港开发建设阶段，中央财政根据实际，结合税制变化情况，对海南自由贸易港给予适当财政支持。鼓励海南省在国务院批准的限额内发行地方政府债券支持海南自由贸易港项目建设。海南省设立政府引导、市场化方式运作的海南自由贸易港建设投资基金。

**Article 25** During the development and construction phase of the Hainan Free Trade Port, the central finance shall, based on actual circumstances and taking into account the changes in the tax system, provide appropriate financial support to the Hainan Free Trade Port. Hainan Province is encouraged to issue local government bonds within the limits approved by the State Council to support the construction projects of the Hainan Free Trade Port. Hainan Province shall establish a government-guided, market-oriented Hainan Free Trade Port construction investment fund.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　海南自由贸易港可以根据发展需要，自主减征、免征、缓征除具有生态补偿性质外的政府性基金。

**Article 26** The Hainan Free Trade Port may, based on development needs, independently reduce, exempt, or defer the collection of government funds other than those of an ecological compensation nature.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　按照税种结构简单科学、税制要素充分优化、税负水平明显降低、收入归属清晰、财政收支基本均衡的原则，结合国家税制改革方向，建立符合需要的海南自由贸易港税制体系。  
全岛封关运作时，将增值税、消费税、车辆购置税、城市维护建设税及教育费附加等税费进行简并，在货物和服务零售环节征收销售税；全岛封关运作后，进一步简化税制。  
国务院财政部门会同国务院有关部门和海南省及时提出简化税制的具体方案。

**Article 27** In accordance with the principles of a simple and scientific tax structure, full optimization of tax system elements, significant reduction of tax burden level, clear attribution of revenue, and basic balance of fiscal revenue and expenditure, and in combination with the direction of the national tax system reform, a tax system of the Hainan Free Trade Port that meets the needs shall be established.  
When the closed island operation begins, taxes and fees such as value-added tax, consumption tax, vehicle purchase tax, urban maintenance and construction tax, and education surcharge shall be simplified and merged, and a sales tax shall be levied on the retail of goods and services; after the closed island operation, the tax system shall be further simplified.  
The finance department of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council and Hainan Province, promptly put forward specific plans for simplifying the tax system.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　全岛封关运作、简并税制后，海南自由贸易港对进口征税商品实行目录管理，目录之外的货物进入海南自由贸易港，免征进口关税。进口征税商品目录由国务院财政部门会同国务院有关部门和海南省制定。  
全岛封关运作、简并税制前，对部分进口商品，免征进口关税、进口环节增值税和消费税。  
对由海南自由贸易港离境的出口应税商品，征收出口关税。

**Article 28** After the closed island operation and tax simplification and merger, the Hainan Free Trade Port shall implement a catalogue management for imported taxable goods. Goods not in the catalogue entering the Hainan Free Trade Port shall be exempted from import tariffs. The catalogue of imported taxable goods shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council and Hainan Province.  
Before the closed island operation and tax simplification and merger, certain imported goods shall be exempted from import tariffs, import value-added tax and consumption tax.  
Export tariffs shall be levied on export taxable goods departing from the Hainan Free Trade Port.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　货物由海南自由贸易港进入内地，原则上按照进口征税；但是，对鼓励类产业企业生产的不含进口料件或者含进口料件在海南自由贸易港加工增值达到一定比例的货物，免征关税。具体办法由国务院有关部门会同海南省制定。  
货物由内地进入海南自由贸易港，按照国务院有关规定退还已征收的增值税、消费税。  
全岛封关运作、简并税制前，对离岛旅客购买免税物品并提货离岛的，按照有关规定免征进口关税、进口环节增值税和消费税。全岛封关运作、简并税制后，物品在海南自由贸易港和内地之间进出的税收管理办法，由国务院有关部门会同海南省制定。

**Article 29** Goods entering the mainland from the Hainan Free Trade Port shall, in principle, be taxed as imports; however, goods produced by enterprises in encouraged industries that do not contain imported materials or contain imported materials that have undergone processing and value addition in the Hainan Free Trade Port reaching a certain proportion shall be exempted from tariffs. The specific measures shall be formulated by the relevant departments of the State Council jointly with Hainan Province.  
Goods entering the Hainan Free Trade Port from the mainland shall have the already collected value-added tax and consumption tax refunded in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State Council.  
Before the closed island operation and tax simplification and merger, for departing island passengers who purchase duty-free articles and take them away from the island, import tariffs, import value-added tax and consumption tax shall be exempted in accordance with the relevant regulations. After the closed island operation and tax simplification and merger, the tax administration measures for the entry and exit of articles between the Hainan Free Trade Port and the mainland shall be formulated by the relevant departments of the State Council jointly with Hainan Province.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对注册在海南自由贸易港符合条件的企业，实行企业所得税优惠；对海南自由贸易港内符合条件的个人，实行个人所得税优惠。

**Article 30** Enterprise income tax preferences shall be applied to eligible enterprises registered in the Hainan Free Trade Port; individual income tax preferences shall be applied to eligible individuals within the Hainan Free Trade Port.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　海南自由贸易港建立优化高效统一的税收征管服务体系，提高税收征管服务科学化、信息化、国际化、便民化水平，积极参与国际税收征管合作，提高税收征管服务质量和效率，保护纳税人的合法权益。

**Article 31** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall establish an optimized, efficient and unified tax collection and management service system, improve the scientific, informational, international and convenient level of tax collection and management services, actively participate in international tax collection and management cooperation, improve the quality and efficiency of tax collection and management services, and protect the legitimate rights and interests of taxpayers.

### 第五章　生态环境保护

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　海南自由贸易港健全生态环境评价和监测制度，制定生态环境准入清单，防止污染，保护生态环境；健全自然资源资产产权制度和有偿使用制度，促进资源节约高效利用。

**Article 32** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall improve the ecological environment evaluation and monitoring system, formulate an ecological environment access list, prevent pollution, and protect the ecological environment; improve the property rights system of natural resource assets and the paid use system, and promote resource conservation and efficient utilization.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　海南自由贸易港推进国土空间规划体系建设，实行差别化的自然生态空间用途管制，严守生态保护红线，构建以国家公园为主体的自然保护地体系，推进绿色城镇化、美丽乡村建设。  
海南自由贸易港严格保护海洋生态环境，建立健全陆海统筹的生态系统保护修复和污染防治区域联动机制。

**Article 33** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall promote the construction of a territorial spatial planning system, implement differentiated control of natural ecological space use, strictly observe the ecological protection red line, establish a natural protected area system with national parks as the main body, and promote green urbanization and beautiful countryside construction.  
The Hainan Free Trade Port shall strictly protect the marine ecological environment, and establish and improve a regional linkage mechanism for ecosystem protection and restoration and pollution prevention that coordinates land and sea.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　海南自由贸易港实行严格的进出境环境安全准入管理制度，加强检验检疫能力建设，防范外来物种入侵，禁止境外固体废物输入；提高医疗废物等危险废物处理处置能力，提升突发生态环境事件应急准备与响应能力，加强生态风险防控。

**Article 34** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall implement a strict environmental security access management system for entry and exit, strengthen the capacity building for inspection and quarantine, prevent the invasion of alien species, and prohibit the import of foreign solid waste; improve the treatment and disposal capacity of hazardous waste such as medical waste, enhance the emergency preparedness and response capacity for sudden ecological environmental incidents, and strengthen ecological risk prevention and control.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　海南自由贸易港推进建立政府主导、企业和社会参与、市场化运作、可持续的生态保护补偿机制，建立生态产品价值实现机制，鼓励利用市场机制推进生态环境保护，实现可持续发展。

**Article 35** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall promote the establishment of a government-led, enterprise and social participation, market-oriented operation, sustainable ecological protection compensation mechanism, establish a mechanism for realizing the value of ecological products, encourage the use of market mechanisms to promote ecological environmental protection, and achieve sustainable development.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　海南自由贸易港实行环境保护目标责任制和考核评价制度。县级以上地方人民政府对本级人民政府负有环境监督管理职责的部门及其负责人和下级人民政府及其负责人的年度考核，实行环境保护目标完成情况一票否决制。  
环境保护目标未完成的地区，一年内暂停审批该地区新增重点污染物排放总量的建设项目环境影响评价文件；对负有责任的地方人民政府及负有环境监督管理职责的部门的主要责任人，一年内不得提拔使用或者转任重要职务，并依法予以处分。

**Article 36** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall implement an environmental protection target responsibility system and an assessment and evaluation system. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in the annual assessment of the departments responsible for environmental supervision and management of the people's government at the same level and their responsible persons, and the people's governments at lower levels and their responsible persons, implement a one-vote veto system based on the completion of environmental protection targets.  
In areas where environmental protection targets have not been met, the approval of environmental impact assessment documents for construction projects that increase the total amount of key pollutant discharge in that area shall be suspended for one year; the main responsible persons of the local people's government responsible and the departments responsible for environmental supervision and management shall not be promoted, transferred to important positions within one year, and shall be punished in accordance with law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　海南自由贸易港实行生态环境损害责任终身追究制。对违背科学发展要求、造成生态环境严重破坏的地方人民政府及有关部门主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，应当严格追究责任。

**Article 37** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall implement a lifelong accountability system for ecological environment damage liability. The main responsible persons, directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons of local people's governments and relevant departments that violate the requirements of scientific development and cause serious damage to the ecological environment shall be strictly held accountable.

### 第六章　产业发展与人才支撑

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家支持海南自由贸易港建设开放型生态型服务型产业体系，积极发展旅游业、现代服务业、高新技术产业以及热带特色高效农业等重点产业。

**Article 38** The State shall support the Hainan Free Trade Port in building an open, ecological and service-oriented industrial system, and actively develop key industries such as tourism, modern service industry, high-tech industry, and tropical characteristic and efficient agriculture.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　海南自由贸易港推进国际旅游消费中心建设，推动旅游与文化体育、健康医疗、养老养生等深度融合，培育旅游新业态新模式。

**Article 39** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall promote the construction of an international tourism consumption center, promote the deep integration of tourism with culture, sports, health care, elderly care and wellness, and cultivate new forms and models of tourism.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　海南自由贸易港深化现代服务业对内对外开放，打造国际航运枢纽，推动港口、产业、城市融合发展，完善海洋服务基础设施，构建具有国际竞争力的海洋服务体系。  
境外高水平大学、职业院校可以在海南自由贸易港设立理工农医类学校。

**Article 40** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall deepen the opening up of the modern service industry to domestic and foreign parties, build an international shipping hub, promote the integrated development of ports, industries and cities, improve the infrastructure of marine services, and build a marine service system with international competitiveness.  
High-level overseas universities and vocational schools may establish schools in science, engineering, agriculture and medicine in the Hainan Free Trade Port.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家支持海南自由贸易港建设重大科研基础设施和条件平台，建立符合科研规律的科技创新管理制度和国际科技合作机制。

**Article 41** The State shall support the Hainan Free Trade Port in building major scientific research infrastructure and platform conditions, and establish a scientific and technological innovation management system and an international science and technology cooperation mechanism that conforms to the laws of scientific research.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　海南自由贸易港依法建立安全有序自由便利的数据流动管理制度，依法保护个人、组织与数据有关的权益，有序扩大通信资源和业务开放，扩大数据领域开放，促进以数据为关键要素的数字经济发展。  
国家支持海南自由贸易港探索实施区域性国际数据跨境流动制度安排。

**Article 42** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall, in accordance with law, establish a management system for safe, orderly, free and convenient data flow, protect the rights and interests of individuals and organizations related to data in accordance with law, orderly expand the opening of communication resources and services, expand the opening of the data field, and promote the development of the digital economy with data as a key factor.  
The State shall support the Hainan Free Trade Port in exploring the implementation of regional institutional arrangements for cross-border data flow.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　海南自由贸易港实施高度自由便利开放的运输政策，建立更加开放的航运制度和船舶管理制度，建设“中国洋浦港”船籍港，实行特殊的船舶登记制度；放宽空域管制和航路限制，优化航权资源配置，提升运输便利化和服务保障水平。

**Article 43** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall implement highly free, convenient and open transportation policies, establish a more open shipping system and ship management system, build the "China Yangpu Port" as a ship registry port, and implement a special ship registration system; relax airspace control and route restrictions, optimize the allocation of traffic rights resources, and improve the level of transportation facilitation and service guarantee.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　海南自由贸易港深化人才发展体制机制改革，创新人才培养支持机制，建立科学合理的人才引进、认定、使用和待遇保障机制。

**Article 44** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall deepen the reform of the systems and mechanisms for talent development, innovate the support mechanism for talent cultivation, and establish a scientific and reasonable mechanism for talent introduction, recognition, utilization and treatment guarantee.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　海南自由贸易港建立高效便利的出境入境管理制度，逐步实施更大范围适用免签入境政策，延长免签停留时间，优化出境入境检查管理，提供出境入境通关便利。

**Article 45** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall establish an efficient and convenient exit and entry management system, gradually implement a wider application of visa-free entry policies, extend the duration of visa-free stay, optimize exit and entry inspection management, and provide exit and entry clearance facilitation.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　海南自由贸易港实行更加开放的人才和停居留政策，实行更加宽松的人员临时出境入境政策、便利的工作签证政策，对外国人工作许可实行负面清单管理，进一步完善居留制度。

**Article 46** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall implement more open policies for talents and stay and residence, implement more relaxed policies for temporary exit and entry of personnel, convenient work visa policies, implement negative list management for work permits for foreigners, and further improve the residence system.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　海南自由贸易港放宽境外人员参加职业资格考试的限制，对符合条件的境外专业资格认定，实行单向认可清单制度。

**Article 47** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall relax restrictions on foreign personnel taking professional qualification examinations, and implement a system of unilateral recognition list for the recognition of foreign professional qualifications that meet the conditions.

### 第七章　综合措施

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国务院可以根据海南自由贸易港建设的需要，授权海南省人民政府审批由国务院审批的农用地转为建设用地和土地征收事项；授权海南省人民政府在不突破海南省国土空间规划明确的生态保护红线、永久基本农田面积、耕地和林地保有量、建设用地总规模等重要指标并确保质量不降低的前提下，按照国家规定的条件，对全省耕地、永久基本农田、林地、建设用地布局调整进行审批。  
海南自由贸易港积极推进城乡及垦区一体化协调发展和小城镇建设用地新模式，推进农垦土地资产化。  
依法保障海南自由贸易港国家重大项目用海需求。

**Article 48** The State Council may, based on the needs of the construction of the Hainan Free Trade Port, authorize the People's Government of Hainan Province to approve the conversion of agricultural land into construction land and land acquisition matters that are originally subject to the approval of the State Council; authorize the People's Government of Hainan Province, on the condition that it does not exceed the important indicators such as the ecological protection red line, the area of permanent basic farmland, the amount of cultivated land and forest land, and the total amount of construction land clearly specified in the territorial spatial planning of Hainan Province and ensures that the quality does not decrease, and in accordance with the conditions stipulated by the State, to approve the adjustment of the layout of cultivated land, permanent basic farmland, forest land, and construction land in the whole province.  
The Hainan Free Trade Port shall actively promote the integrated and coordinated development of urban and rural areas and reclamation areas, and a new model of small town construction land, and promote the capitalization of land owned by agricultural reclamation.  
The demand for sea use for major national projects in the Hainan Free Trade Port shall be guaranteed in accordance with law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　海南自由贸易港建设应当切实保护耕地，加强土地管理，建立集约节约用地制度、评价标准以及存量建设用地盘活处置制度。充分利用闲置土地，以出让方式取得土地使用权进行开发的土地，超过出让合同约定的竣工日期一年未竣工的，应当在竣工前每年征收出让土地现值一定比例的土地闲置费。具体办法由海南省制定。

**Article 49** The construction of the Hainan Free Trade Port shall effectively protect cultivated land, strengthen land management, and establish an intensive and economical land use system, evaluation standards, and a system for revitalizing and disposing of existing construction land. Idle land shall be fully utilized. For land acquired by the method of transfer for development, if the construction is not completed one year after the completion date stipulated in the transfer contract, a land idle fee at a certain percentage of the current value of the transferred land shall be collected each year until completion. The specific measures shall be formulated by Hainan Province.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　海南自由贸易港坚持金融服务实体经济，推进金融改革创新，率先落实金融业开放政策。

**Article 50** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall adhere to the principle of financial services serving the real economy, promote financial reform and innovation, and take the lead in implementing the opening policies of the financial industry.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　海南自由贸易港建立适应高水平贸易投资自由化便利化需要的跨境资金流动管理制度，分阶段开放资本项目，逐步推进非金融企业外债项下完全可兑换，推动跨境贸易结算便利化，有序推进海南自由贸易港与境外资金自由便利流动。

**Article 51** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall establish a cross-border capital flow management system that meets the needs of high-level trade and investment liberalization and facilitation, open capital accounts in phases, progressively promote full convertibility under non-financial enterprise foreign debts, promote the facilitation of cross-border trade settlement, and orderly advance the free and convenient flow of funds between the Hainan Free Trade Port and overseas.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　海南自由贸易港内经批准的金融机构可以通过指定账户或者在特定区域经营离岸金融业务。

**Article 52** Financial institutions approved within the Hainan Free Trade Port may conduct offshore financial business through designated accounts or in specific areas.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　海南自由贸易港加强社会信用体系建设和应用，构建守信激励和失信惩戒机制。

**Article 53** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall strengthen the construction and application of a social credit system, and establish mechanisms for incentivizing trustworthiness and penalizing dishonesty.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家支持探索与海南自由贸易港相适应的司法体制改革。海南自由贸易港建立多元化商事纠纷解决机制，完善国际商事纠纷案件集中审判机制，支持通过仲裁、调解等多种非诉讼方式解决纠纷。

**Article 54** The State supports the exploration of judicial system reforms compatible with the Hainan Free Trade Port. The Hainan Free Trade Port shall establish a diversified commercial dispute resolution mechanism, improve the centralized adjudication mechanism for international commercial dispute cases, and support the resolution of disputes through various non-litigation methods such as arbitration and mediation.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　海南自由贸易港建立风险预警和防控体系，防范和化解重大风险。  
海关负责口岸和其他海关监管区的常规监管，依法查缉走私和实施后续监管。海警机构负责查处海上走私违法行为。海南省人民政府负责全省反走私综合治理工作，加强对非设关地的管控，建立与其他地区的反走私联防联控机制。境外与海南自由贸易港之间、海南自由贸易港与内地之间，人员、货物、物品、运输工具等均需从口岸进出。  
在海南自由贸易港依法实施外商投资安全审查制度，对影响或者可能影响国家安全的外商投资进行安全审查。  
海南自由贸易港建立健全金融风险防控制度，实施网络安全等级保护制度，建立人员流动风险防控制度，建立传染病和突发公共卫生事件监测预警机制与防控救治机制，保障金融、网络与数据、人员流动和公共卫生等领域的秩序和安全。

**Article 55** The Hainan Free Trade Port shall establish a risk early warning and prevention and control system to prevent and mitigate major risks.  
The customs shall be responsible for routine supervision at ports and other customs surveillance zones, investigate and seize smuggling according to law, and conduct follow-up supervision. The maritime police agency shall be responsible for investigating and dealing with illegal acts of maritime smuggling. The people's government of Hainan Province shall be responsible for the comprehensive management of anti-smuggling throughout the province, strengthen control over non-designated ports, and establish a joint prevention and control mechanism for anti-smuggling with other regions. Personnel, goods, articles, means of transport, etc., between overseas and the Hainan Free Trade Port, and between the Hainan Free Trade Port and the mainland, shall all enter and exit through ports.  
In the Hainan Free Trade Port, a security review system for foreign investment shall be implemented according to law, and security reviews shall be conducted on foreign investments that affect or may affect national security.  
The Hainan Free Trade Port shall establish and improve a financial risk prevention and control system, implement a cybersecurity level protection system, establish a personnel flow risk prevention and control system, establish a monitoring and early warning mechanism for infectious diseases and public health emergencies, as well as prevention, control, and treatment mechanisms, and ensure order and security in the areas of finance, network and data, personnel flow, and public health.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　对本法规定的事项，在本法施行后，海南自由贸易港全岛封关运作前，国务院及其有关部门和海南省可以根据本法规定的原则，按照职责分工，制定过渡性的具体办法，推动海南自由贸易港建设。

**Article 56** For matters provided for in this Law, after this Law comes into force and before the Hainan Free Trade Port implements island-wide customs closure operations, the State Council and its relevant departments and Hainan Province may, in accordance with the principles set forth in this Law and based on their respective functions and duties, formulate transitional specific measures to promote the construction of the Hainan Free Trade Port.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　本法自公布之日起施行。

**Article 57** This Law shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
