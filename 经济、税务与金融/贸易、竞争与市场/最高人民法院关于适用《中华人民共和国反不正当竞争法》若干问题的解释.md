# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法》若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕9号  
**公布日期**：2022-01-29  
**生效日期**：2022-03-20

> 《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法〉若干问题的解释》已于2022年1月29日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1862次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年3月20日起施行。 2022年3月16日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法》 若干问题的解释 法释〔2022〕9号 为正确审理因不正当竞争行为引发的民事案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等有关法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条　经营者扰乱市场竞争秩序，损害其他经营者或者消费者合法权益，且属于违反反不正当竞争法第二章及专利法、商标法、著作权法等规定之外情形的，人民法院可以适用反不正当竞争法第二条予以认定。 第二条　与经营者在生产经营活动中存在可能的争夺交易机会、损害竞争优势等关系的市场主体，人民法院可以认定为反不正当竞争法第二条规定的“其他经营者”。 第三条　特定商业领域普遍遵循和认可的行为规范，人民法院可以认定为反不正当竞争法第二条规定的“商业道德”。 人民法院应当结合案件具体情况，综合考虑行业规则或者商业惯例、经营者的主观状态、交易相对人的选择意愿、对消费者权益、市场竞争秩序、社会公共利益的影响等因素，依法判断经营者是否违反商业道德。 人民法院认定经营者是否违反商业道德时，可以参考行业主管部门、行业协会或者自律组织制定的从业规范、技术规范、自律公约等。 第四条　具有一定的市场知名度并具有区别商品来源的显著特征的标识，人民法院可以认定为反不正当竞争法第六条规定的“有一定影响的”标识。 人民法院认定反不正当竞争法第六条规定的标识是否具有一定的市场知名度，应当综合考虑中国境内相关公众的知悉程度，商品销售的时间、区域、数额和对象，宣传的持续时间、程度和地域范围，标识受保护的情况等因素。 第五条　反不正当竞争法第六条规定的标识有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定其不具有区别商品来源的显著特征：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　经营者扰乱市场竞争秩序，损害其他经营者或者消费者合法权益，且属于违反反不正当竞争法第二章及专利法、商标法、著作权法等规定之外情形的，人民法院可以适用[反不正当竞争法第二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-2)予以认定。

**Article 1** Where a business operator disrupts the market competition order, damages the lawful rights and interests of other business operators or consumers, and the circumstances do not fall within the provisions of Chapter II of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law and the Patent Law, Trademark Law, Copyright Law or other laws, the People's Court may apply Article 2 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law for determination.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　与经营者在生产经营活动中存在可能的争夺交易机会、损害竞争优势等关系的市场主体，人民法院可以认定为[反不正当竞争法第二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-2)规定的“其他经营者”。

**Article 2** A market entity that has a relationship with a business operator in production or business activities, such as possible contention for transaction opportunities or impairment of competitive advantages, may be determined by the People's Court as an "other business operator" as prescribed in Article 2 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　特定商业领域普遍遵循和认可的行为规范，人民法院可以认定为[反不正当竞争法第二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-2)规定的“商业道德”。  
人民法院应当结合案件具体情况，综合考虑行业规则或者商业惯例、经营者的主观状态、交易相对人的选择意愿、对消费者权益、市场竞争秩序、社会公共利益的影响等因素，依法判断经营者是否违反商业道德。  
人民法院认定经营者是否违反商业道德时，可以参考行业主管部门、行业协会或者自律组织制定的从业规范、技术规范、自律公约等。

**Article 3** A code of conduct generally followed and recognized in a specific commercial field may be determined by the People's Court as "business ethics" as prescribed in Article 2 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law.  
The People's Court shall, in light of the specific circumstances of the case, comprehensively consider factors such as industry rules or commercial practices, the subjective status of the business operator, the willingness of the counterparty in the transaction, and the impact on consumers' rights and interests, market competition order, and social public interests, to determine according to law whether the business operator has violated business ethics.  
When determining whether a business operator has violated business ethics, the People's Court may refer to professional norms, technical specifications, self-discipline conventions, etc., formulated by competent industry authorities, industry associations, or self-regulatory organizations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　具有一定的市场知名度并具有区别商品来源的显著特征的标识，人民法院可以认定为[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)规定的“有一定影响的”标识。  
人民法院认定[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)规定的标识是否具有一定的市场知名度，应当综合考虑中国境内相关公众的知悉程度，商品销售的时间、区域、数额和对象，宣传的持续时间、程度和地域范围，标识受保护的情况等因素。

**Article 4** A mark that has a certain degree of market popularity and has distinctive characteristics for distinguishing the source of goods may be determined by the People's Court as a mark "with certain influence" as prescribed in Article 6 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law.  
When determining whether a mark as prescribed in Article 6 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law has a certain degree of market popularity, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider factors such as the degree of awareness among relevant domestic public, the time, region, amount, and target of commodity sales, the duration, extent, and geographical scope of publicity, and the protection status of the mark.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)规定的标识有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定其不具有区别商品来源的显著特征：  
（一）商品的通用名称、图形、型号；  
（二）仅直接表示商品的质量、主要原料、功能、用途、重量、数量及其他特点的标识；  
（三）仅由商品自身的性质产生的形状，为获得技术效果而需有的商品形状以及使商品具有实质性价值的形状；  
（四）其他缺乏显著特征的标识。  
前款第一项、第二项、第四项规定的标识经过使用取得显著特征，并具有一定的市场知名度，当事人请求依据[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)规定予以保护的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 5** Where a mark prescribed in Article 6 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall determine that it does not have distinctive characteristics for distinguishing the source of goods:  
(1) generic names, figures, or models of goods;  
(2) marks that merely directly indicate the quality, main raw materials, functions, uses, weight, quantity, or other characteristics of goods;  
(3) shapes that are solely derived from the nature of the goods themselves, shapes that are necessary to achieve technical effects, or shapes that give substantial value to the goods;  
(4) other marks lacking distinctive characteristics.  
Where a mark specified in items (1), (2), or (4) of the preceding paragraph has acquired distinctive characteristics through use and has a certain degree of market popularity, and a party requests protection in accordance with Article 6 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law, the People's Court shall uphold such a request.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　因客观描述、说明商品而正当使用下列标识，当事人主张属于[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)规定的情形的，人民法院不予支持：  
（一）含有本商品的通用名称、图形、型号；  
（二）直接表示商品的质量、主要原料、功能、用途、重量、数量以及其他特点；  
（三）含有地名。

**Article 6** Where a party claims that the fair use of the following marks for the objective description or explanation of goods falls within the circumstances prescribed in Article 6 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law, the People's Court shall not uphold such a claim:  
(1) marks containing generic names, figures, or models of the goods;  
(2) marks directly indicating the quality, main raw materials, functions, uses, weight, quantity, or other characteristics of the goods;  
(3) marks containing place names.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)规定的标识或者其显著识别部分属于[商标法第十条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-10)第一款规定的不得作为商标使用的标志，当事人请求依据[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)规定予以保护的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 7** Where a mark prescribed in Article 6 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law or its distinctive identifying part falls within the signs that may not be used as trademarks under Article 10, Paragraph 1 of the Trademark Law, and a party requests protection in accordance with Article 6 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law, the People's Court shall not uphold such a request.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　由经营者营业场所的装饰、营业用具的式样、营业人员的服饰等构成的具有独特风格的整体营业形象，人民法院可以认定为[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)第一项规定的“装潢”。

**Article 8** An overall business image that has a distinctive style and is composed of the decoration of the business premises, the style of business utensils, the attire of business personnel, etc., may be determined by the People's Court as "decoration" as prescribed in Article 6, Item 1 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　市场主体登记管理部门依法登记的企业名称，以及在中国境内进行商业使用的境外企业名称，人民法院可以认定为[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)第二项规定的“企业名称”。  
有一定影响的个体工商户、农民专业合作社（联合社）以及法律、行政法规规定的其他市场主体的名称（包括简称、字号等），人民法院可以依照[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)第二项予以认定。

**Article 9** An enterprise name registered by the market entity registration administration department according to law, and an overseas enterprise name used commercially within the territory of China, may be determined by the People's Court as an "enterprise name" as prescribed in Article 6, Item 2 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law.  
The name (including abbreviation, trade name, etc.) of an individual industrial or commercial household, a specialized farmer cooperative (union) with certain influence, or any other market entity prescribed by laws or administrative regulations may be determined by the People's Court in accordance with Article 6, Item 2 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在中国境内将有一定影响的标识用于商品、商品包装或者容器以及商品交易文书上，或者广告宣传、展览以及其他商业活动中，用于识别商品来源的行为，人民法院可以认定为[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)规定的“使用”。

**Article 10** The act of using a mark with certain influence on goods, goods packaging or containers, and transaction documents of goods, or in advertising, exhibitions, and other commercial activities within the territory of China for the purpose of identifying the source of goods may be determined by the People's Court as "use" as prescribed in Article 6 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　经营者擅自使用与他人有一定影响的企业名称（包括简称、字号等）、社会组织名称（包括简称等）、姓名（包括笔名、艺名、译名等）、域名主体部分、网站名称、网页等近似的标识，引人误认为是他人商品或者与他人存在特定联系，当事人主张属于[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)第二项、第三项规定的情形的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 11** Where a business operator, without authorization, uses a mark that is similar to another party's enterprise name (including abbreviation, trade name, etc.), social organization name (including abbreviation, etc.), personal name (including pseudonym, stage name, translated name, etc.), domain name main part, website name, webpage, etc., that has certain influence, and causes people to mistakenly believe that the goods are those of another party or that there is a specific connection with another party, and a party claims that such circumstances fall under Article 6, Items 2 and 3 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law, the People's Court shall uphold such a claim.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院认定与[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)规定的“有一定影响的”标识相同或者近似，可以参照商标相同或者近似的判断原则和方法。  
[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)规定的“引人误认为是他人商品或者与他人存在特定联系”，包括误认为与他人具有商业联合、许可使用、商业冠名、广告代言等特定联系。  
在相同商品上使用相同或者视觉上基本无差别的商品名称、包装、装潢等标识，应当视为足以造成与他人有一定影响的标识相混淆。

**Article 12** When determining whether a mark is identical or similar to a mark "with certain influence" as prescribed in Article 6 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law, the People's Court may refer to the principles and methods for determining identical or similar trademarks.  
The phrase "causing people to mistakenly believe that the goods are those of another party or that there is a specific connection with another party" as prescribed in Article 6 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law includes mistakenly believing that there is a specific connection such as commercial alliance, licensing, commercial nomination, or endorsement by another party.  
The use of a mark that is identical or visually substantially indistinguishable from the name, packaging, decoration, etc., of a good on the same good shall be deemed sufficient to cause confusion with a mark with certain influence of another party.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　经营者实施下列混淆行为之一，足以引人误认为是他人商品或者与他人存在特定联系的，人民法院可以依照[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)第四项予以认定：  
（一）擅自使用[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)第一项、第二项、第三项规定以外“有一定影响的”标识；  
（二）将他人注册商标、未注册的驰名商标作为企业名称中的字号使用，误导公众。

**Article 13** Where a business operator commits any of the following acts of confusion, which is sufficient to cause people to mistakenly believe that the goods are those of another party or that there is a specific connection with another party, the People's Court may make a determination in accordance with Article 6, Item 4 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law:  
(1) without authorization, using a mark "with certain influence" other than those prescribed in Article 6, Items 1, 2, and 3 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law;  
(2) using another party's registered trademark or unregistered well-known trademark as a trade name in an enterprise name, thereby misleading the public.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　经营者销售带有违反[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)规定的标识的商品，引人误认为是他人商品或者与他人存在特定联系，当事人主张构成[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)规定的情形的，人民法院应予支持。  
销售不知道是前款规定的侵权商品，能证明该商品是自己合法取得并说明提供者，经营者主张不承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 14** Where a business operator sells goods bearing a mark that violates the provisions of Article 6 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law, thereby causing people to mistakenly believe that the goods are those of another party or that there is a specific connection with another party, and a party claims that such conduct constitutes the circumstances prescribed in Article 6 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law, the People's Court shall uphold such a claim.  
Where a business operator who sells infringing goods as specified in the preceding paragraph without knowledge thereof, and is able to prove that the goods were lawfully obtained by it and can identify the provider, and claims that it shall not bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall uphold such a claim.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　故意为他人实施混淆行为提供仓储、运输、邮寄、印制、隐匿、经营场所等便利条件，当事人请求依据[民法典第一千一百六十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1169)第一款予以认定的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 15** Where a person intentionally provides convenience such as warehousing, transportation, mailing, printing, concealment, or business premises for another party to commit an act of confusion, and a party requests determination in accordance with Article 1169, Paragraph 1 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court shall uphold such a request.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　经营者在商业宣传过程中，提供不真实的商品相关信息，欺骗、误导相关公众的，人民法院应当认定为[反不正当竞争法第八条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-8)第一款规定的虚假的商业宣传。

**Article 16** Where a business operator, in the course of commercial publicity, provides untrue information related to goods, thereby deceiving or misleading the relevant public, the People's Court shall determine such conduct as false commercial publicity as prescribed in Article 8, Paragraph 1 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　经营者具有下列行为之一，欺骗、误导相关公众的，人民法院可以认定为[反不正当竞争法第八条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-8)第一款规定的“引人误解的商业宣传”：  
（一）对商品作片面的宣传或者对比；  
（二）将科学上未定论的观点、现象等当作定论的事实用于商品宣传；  
（三）使用歧义性语言进行商业宣传；  
（四）其他足以引人误解的商业宣传行为。  
人民法院应当根据日常生活经验、相关公众一般注意力、发生误解的事实和被宣传对象的实际情况等因素，对引人误解的商业宣传行为进行认定。

**Article 17** Where a business operator commits any of the following acts, thereby deceiving or misleading the relevant public, the People's Court may determine such conduct as "misleading commercial publicity" as prescribed in Article 8, Paragraph 1 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law:  
(1) making one-sided publicity or comparison of goods;  
(2) using scientifically unsettled views or phenomena as established facts in the publicity of goods;  
(3) using ambiguous language for commercial publicity;  
(4) other commercial publicity acts sufficient to cause misunderstanding.  
The People's Court shall, based on factors such as everyday life experience, the general attention of the relevant public, the fact of misunderstanding, and the actual circumstances of the advertised object, determine whether the act constitutes misleading commercial publicity.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　当事人主张经营者违反[反不正当竞争法第八条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-8)第一款的规定并请求赔偿损失的，应当举证证明其因虚假或者引人误解的商业宣传行为受到损失。

**Article 18** Where a party claims that a business operator has violated Article 8, Paragraph 1 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law and requests compensation for losses, it shall provide evidence to prove that it has suffered losses due to the false or misleading commercial publicity act.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　当事人主张经营者实施了[反不正当竞争法第十一条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-11)规定的商业诋毁行为的，应当举证证明其为该商业诋毁行为的特定损害对象。

**Article 19** Where a party claims that a business operator has committed an act of commercial defamation as prescribed in Article 11 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law, it shall provide evidence to prove that it is the specific object of damage of such commercial defamation act.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　经营者传播他人编造的虚假信息或者误导性信息，损害竞争对手的商业信誉、商品声誉的，人民法院应当依照[反不正当竞争法第十一条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-11)予以认定。

**Article 20** Where a business operator disseminates false information or misleading information fabricated by another party, thereby damaging the business reputation or commodity reputation of a competitor, the People's Court shall make a determination in accordance with Article 11 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　未经其他经营者和用户同意而直接发生的目标跳转，人民法院应当认定为[反不正当竞争法第十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-12)第二款第一项规定的“强制进行目标跳转”。  
仅插入链接，目标跳转由用户触发的，人民法院应当综合考虑插入链接的具体方式、是否具有合理理由以及对用户利益和其他经营者利益的影响等因素，认定该行为是否违反[反不正当竞争法第十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-12)第二款第一项的规定。

**Article 21** Where a target jump occurs directly without the consent of other business operators and users, the People's Court shall determine such conduct as "forced target jump" as prescribed in Article 12, Paragraph 2, Item 1 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law.  
Where a link is merely inserted and the target jump is triggered by the user, the People's Court shall, comprehensively considering factors such as the specific manner of inserting the link, whether there is a reasonable reason, and the impact on the interests of users and other business operators, determine whether such conduct violates the provisions of Article 12, Paragraph 2, Item 1 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　经营者事前未明确提示并经用户同意，以误导、欺骗、强迫用户修改、关闭、卸载等方式，恶意干扰或者破坏其他经营者合法提供的网络产品或者服务，人民法院应当依照[反不正当竞争法第十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-12)第二款第二项予以认定。

**Article 22** Where a business operator, without giving clear prior notice and obtaining user consent, maliciously interferes with or disrupts network products or services lawfully provided by other business operators by means such as misleading, deceiving, or forcing users to modify, close, or uninstall, the People's Court shall make a determination in accordance with Article 12, Paragraph 2, Item 2 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对于[反不正当竞争法第二条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-2)、第八条、第十一条、第十二条规定的不正当竞争行为，权利人因被侵权所受到的实际损失、侵权人因侵权所获得的利益难以确定，当事人主张依据[反不正当竞争法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-17)第四款确定赔偿数额的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 23** Where, for the unfair competition acts prescribed in Articles 2, 8, 11, and 12 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law, the actual losses suffered by the right holder due to the infringement or the profits gained by the infringer from the infringement are difficult to determine, and a party claims that the amount of compensation shall be determined in accordance with Article 17, Paragraph 4 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law, the People's Court shall uphold such a claim.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对于同一侵权人针对同一主体在同一时间和地域范围实施的侵权行为，人民法院已经认定侵害著作权、专利权或者注册商标专用权等并判令承担民事责任，当事人又以该行为构成不正当竞争为由请求同一侵权人承担民事责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 24** Where, for an infringement committed by the same infringer against the same subject within the same time and territorial scope, the People's Court has already determined that it constitutes infringement of copyright, patent right, or exclusive right to use a registered trademark, etc., and has ordered the infringer to bear civil liability, and a party subsequently requests the same infringer to bear civil liability on the ground that the act constitutes unfair competition, the People's Court shall not uphold such a request.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　依据[反不正当竞争法第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)的规定，当事人主张判令被告停止使用或者变更其企业名称的诉讼请求依法应予支持的，人民法院应当判令停止使用该企业名称。

**Article 25** Where, in accordance with Article 6 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law, a party claims that the request for an order to the defendant to cease using or to change its enterprise name shall be upheld according to law, the People's Court shall order the cessation of use of such enterprise name.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　因不正当竞争行为提起的民事诉讼，由侵权行为地或者被告住所地人民法院管辖。  
当事人主张仅以网络购买者可以任意选择的收货地作为侵权行为地的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 26** A civil action brought for an unfair competition act shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the infringing act is committed or where the defendant is domiciled.  
Where a party claims that the place of receipt arbitrarily selectable by an online purchaser alone shall be considered as the place where the infringing act is committed, the People's Court shall not uphold such a claim.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　被诉不正当竞争行为发生在中华人民共和国领域外，但侵权结果发生在中华人民共和国领域内，当事人主张由该侵权结果发生地人民法院管辖的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 27** Where the alleged unfair competition act occurs outside the territory of the People's Republic of China, but the result of the infringement occurs within the territory of the People's Republic of China, and a party claims that the place where the result of the infringement occurs shall have jurisdiction, the People's Court shall uphold such a claim.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　反不正当竞争法修改决定施行以后人民法院受理的不正当竞争民事案件，涉及该决定施行前发生的行为的，适用修改前的反不正当竞争法；涉及该决定施行前发生、持续到该决定施行以后的行为的，适用修改后的反不正当竞争法。

**Article 28** For civil unfair competition cases accepted by the People's Court after the Decision to Amend the Anti-Unfair Competition Law comes into force, if such cases involve acts that occurred before the Decision comes into force, the pre-amendment Anti-Unfair Competition Law shall apply; if such cases involve acts that occurred before the Decision comes into force and continue after the Decision comes into force, the amended Anti-Unfair Competition Law shall apply.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本解释自2022年3月20日起施行。《最高人民法院关于审理不正当竞争民事案件应用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2007〕2号）同时废止。  
本解释施行以后尚未终审的案件，适用本解释；施行以前已经终审的案件，不适用本解释再审。

**Article 29** This Interpretation shall come into force on March 20, 2022. The Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Civil Unfair Competition Cases (Fa Shi [2007] No. 2) shall be abolished simultaneously.  
Cases that have not yet been finally adjudicated after this Interpretation comes into force shall be governed by this Interpretation; cases that have been finally adjudicated before this Interpretation comes into force shall not be subject to retrial under this Interpretation.
