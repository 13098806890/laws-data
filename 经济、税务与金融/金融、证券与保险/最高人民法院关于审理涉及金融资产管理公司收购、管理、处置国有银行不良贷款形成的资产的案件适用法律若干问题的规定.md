# 最高人民法院关于审理涉及金融资产管理公司收购、管理、处置国有银行不良贷款形成的资产的案件适用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2001〕12号  
**公布日期**：2001-04-03  
**生效日期**：2001-04-23

> （2001年4月3日最高人民法院审判委员会第1167次会议通过，自2001年4月23日起施行。） 法释〔2001〕12号 为深化金融改革，规范金融秩序，根据有关法律规定，现对人民法院审理涉及金融资产管理公司收购、管理、处置国有银行不良贷款形成的资产的案件适用法律若干问题作如下规定： 第一条 金融资产管理公司办事处领取中国人民银行颁发的《金融机构营业许可证》，并向工商行政管理部门依法办理登记的，可以作为诉讼主体参加诉讼。 第二条 金融资产管理公司受让国有银行债权后，人民法院对于债权转让前原债权银行已经提起诉讼尚未审结的案件，可以根据原债权银行或者金融资产管理公司的申请将诉讼主体变更为受让债权的金融资产管理公司。 第三条 金融资产管理公司向债务人提起诉讼的，应当由被告人住所地人民法院管辖。原债权银行与债务人有协议管辖约定的，如不违反法律规定，该约定继续有效。 第四条 人民法院对金融资产管理公司申请支付令的，应当依法受理。债务人提出异议的，依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第十七章的规定处理。 第五条 人民法院对金融资产管理公司申请财产保全的，如金融资产管理公司与债务人之间债权债务关系明确，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第九十二条第二款的规定，可以不要求金融资产管理公司提供担保。 第六条 金融资产管理公司受让国有银行债权后，原债权银行在全国或者省级有影响的报纸上发布债权转让公告或通知的，人民法院可以认定债权人履行了《中华人民共和国合同法》第八十条第一款规定的通知义务。 在案件审理中，债务人以原债权银行转让债权未履行通知义务为由进行抗辩的，人民法院可以将原债权银行传唤到庭调查债权转让事实，并责令原债权银行告知债务人债权转让的事实。 第七条 债务人逾期归还贷款，原借款合同约定的利息计算方法不违反法律法规规定的，该约定有效。没有约定或者不明的，依照中国人民银行《人民币利率管理规定》计算利息和复息。 第八条 人民法院对最高额抵押所担保的不特定债权特定后，原债权银行转让主债权的，可以认定转让债权的行为有效。 第九条 金融资产管理公司受让有抵押担保的债权后，可以依法取得对债权的抵押权，原抵押权登记继续有效。 第十条 债务人在债权转让协议，债权转让通知上签章或者签收债务催收通知的，诉讼时效中断。原债权银行在全国或者省级有影响的报纸上发布的债权转让公告或通知中，有催收债务内容的，该公告或通知可以作为诉讼时效中断证据。 第十一条 本规定所称金融资产管理公司包括其依法设立在各地的办事处。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 金融资产管理公司办事处领取中国人民银行颁发的《金融机构营业许可证》，并向工商行政管理部门依法办理登记的，可以作为诉讼主体参加诉讼。

**Article 1** Where an office of an asset management company obtains the Financial Institution Business License issued by the People's Bank of China and completes registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce according to law, it may participate in litigation as a litigation subject.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 金融资产管理公司受让国有银行债权后，人民法院对于债权转让前原债权银行已经提起诉讼尚未审结的案件，可以根据原债权银行或者金融资产管理公司的申请将诉讼主体变更为受让债权的金融资产管理公司。

**Article 2** After an asset management company acquires the creditor's rights of a state-owned bank, the People's Court may, upon application by the original creditor bank or the asset management company, change the litigation subject to the asset management company that has acquired the creditor's rights for cases that have been prosecuted by the original creditor bank before the assignment of creditor's rights but have not yet been concluded.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 金融资产管理公司向债务人提起诉讼的，应当由被告人住所地人民法院管辖。原债权银行与债务人有协议管辖约定的，如不违反法律规定，该约定继续有效。

**Article 3** Where an asset management company initiates a lawsuit against a debtor, it shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the defendant's domicile. If the original creditor bank and the debtor have an agreement on jurisdiction, such agreement shall remain valid if it does not violate legal provisions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 人民法院对金融资产管理公司申请支付令的，应当依法受理。债务人提出异议的，依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第十七章的规定处理。

**Article 4** The People's Court shall accept the application for a payment order filed by an asset management company according to law. If the debtor raises an objection, it shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Chapter 17 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 人民法院对金融资产管理公司申请财产保全的，如金融资产管理公司与债务人之间债权债务关系明确，根据[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第九十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-92)第二款的规定，可以不要求金融资产管理公司提供担保。

**Article 5** Where the People's Court, in response to an application for property preservation by an asset management company, determines that the creditor-debtor relationship between the asset management company and the debtor is clear, the court may, in accordance with the provisions of Article 92, paragraph 2, of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, not require the asset management company to provide a guarantee.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 金融资产管理公司受让国有银行债权后，原债权银行在全国或者省级有影响的报纸上发布债权转让公告或通知的，人民法院可以认定债权人履行了《中华人民共和国合同法》第八十条第一款规定的通知义务。  
在案件审理中，债务人以原债权银行转让债权未履行通知义务为由进行抗辩的，人民法院可以将原债权银行传唤到庭调查债权转让事实，并责令原债权银行告知债务人债权转让的事实。

**Article 6** After an asset management company acquires the creditor's rights of a state-owned bank, if the original creditor bank publishes a public announcement or notice of assignment of creditor's rights in a nationally or provincially influential newspaper, the People's Court may find that the creditor has fulfilled the notification obligation as provided in Article 80, paragraph 1, of the Contract Law of the People's Republic of China.  
In the trial of a case, if the debtor argues that the original creditor bank failed to perform the notification obligation when assigning the creditor's rights, the People's Court may summon the original creditor bank to appear in court to investigate the facts of the assignment of creditor's rights and order the original creditor bank to inform the debtor of the fact of the assignment of creditor's rights.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 债务人逾期归还贷款，原借款合同约定的利息计算方法不违反法律法规规定的，该约定有效。没有约定或者不明的，依照中国人民银行《人民币利率管理规定》计算利息和复息。

**Article 7** Where a debtor fails to repay a loan on time, if the method for calculating interest agreed upon in the original loan contract does not violate laws and regulations, such agreement shall be valid. If there is no agreement or the agreement is unclear, interest and compound interest shall be calculated in accordance with the Provisions on the Administration of Renminbi Interest Rates of the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 人民法院对最高额抵押所担保的不特定债权特定后，原债权银行转让主债权的，可以认定转让债权的行为有效。

**Article 8** After the People's Court specifies the non-specific creditor's rights secured by a maximum amount mortgage, if the original creditor bank assigns the principal creditor's rights, it may be determined that the act of assigning the creditor's rights is valid.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 金融资产管理公司受让有抵押担保的债权后，可以依法取得对债权的抵押权，原抵押权登记继续有效。

**Article 9** After an asset management company acquires creditor's rights secured by a mortgage, it may lawfully obtain the mortgage on the creditor's rights, and the original registration of the mortgage shall continue to be valid.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 债务人在债权转让协议，债权转让通知上签章或者签收债务催收通知的，诉讼时效中断。原债权银行在全国或者省级有影响的报纸上发布的债权转让公告或通知中，有催收债务内容的，该公告或通知可以作为诉讼时效中断证据。

**Article 10** If the debtor signs or affixes its seal on the agreement for assignment of creditor's rights, or on the notice of assignment of creditor's rights, or signs to acknowledge receipt of a notice of debt collection, the limitation of action shall be interrupted. If a public announcement or notice of assignment of creditor's rights published by the original creditor bank in a nationally or provincially influential newspaper contains the content of debt collection, such announcement or notice may serve as evidence of the interruption of the limitation of action.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 本规定所称金融资产管理公司包括其依法设立在各地的办事处。

**Article 11** The term "asset management company" as used in these Provisions includes its offices lawfully established in various localities.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 本规定仅适用于审理涉及金融资产管理公司收购、管理、处置国有银行不良贷款形成的资产的有关案件。

**Article 12** These Provisions shall apply only to the trial of cases involving the assets formed from the acquisition, management, and disposal of non-performing loans of state-owned banks by asset management companies.
