# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部关于对冻结、扣划企业事业单位、机关团体在银行、非银行金融机构存款的执法活动加强监督的通知

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**发文字号**：法〔1996〕83号  
**公布日期**：1996-08-13  
**生效日期**：1996-08-13

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部发现地方各级人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关冻结、解冻、扣划有关单位在银行、非银行金融机构存款有错误时，上级人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关发现下级人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关冻结、解冻、扣划有关单位在银行、非银行金融机构存款有错误时，可依照法定程序作出决定或者裁定，送达本系统地方各级或下级有关法院、检察院、公安机关限期纠正。有关法院、检察院、公安机关应当立即执行。

**Article 1** Where the Supreme People's Court, the Supreme People's Procuratorate, and the Ministry of Public Security discover that a local people's court, people's procuratorate, or public security organ has made errors in freezing, unfreezing, or deducting and transferring deposits of a relevant entity in a bank or non-bank financial institution, or where a higher-level people's court, people's procuratorate, or public security organ discovers that a lower-level people's court, people's procuratorate, or public security organ has made errors in freezing, unfreezing, or deducting and transferring deposits of a relevant entity in a bank or non-bank financial institution, they may make a decision or ruling in accordance with legal procedures and serve it on the relevant local or lower-level courts, procuratorates, or public security organs within their own system, ordering correction within a specified period. The relevant courts, procuratorates, and public security organs shall immediately execute.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、有关法院、检察院、公安机关认为上级机关的决定或者裁定有错误的，可在收到该决定或者裁定之日起5日以内向作出决定或裁定的人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关请求复议。最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部或上级人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关经审查，认为请求复议的理由不能成立，依法有权直接向有关银行发出法律文书，纠正各自的下级机关所作的错误决定，并通知原作出决定的机关；有关银行、非银行金融机构接到此项法律文书后，应当立即办理，不得延误，不必征得原作出决定机关的同意。

**Article 2** Where a relevant court, procuratorate, or public security organ considers that a decision or ruling of a superior organ is erroneous, it may, within five days from the date of receipt of such decision or ruling, request reconsideration from the people's court, people's procuratorate, or public security organ that made the decision or ruling. The Supreme People's Court, the Supreme People's Procuratorate, the Ministry of Public Security, or the higher-level people's court, people's procuratorate, or public security organ, upon review, if it considers that the grounds for the request for reconsideration are untenable, shall have the authority according to law to directly issue legal documents to the relevant bank, correcting the erroneous decisions made by their respective lower-level organs, and notify the organ that originally made the decision; the relevant bank or non-bank financial institution, upon receiving such legal documents, shall handle the matter immediately without delay and need not obtain consent from the organ that originally made the decision.
