### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护商业银行、存款人和其他客户的合法权益，规范商业银行的行为，提高信贷资产质量，加强监督管理，保障商业银行的稳健运行，维护金融秩序，促进社会主义市场经济的发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purposes of protecting the legitimate rights and interests of commercial banks, depositors and other clients, regulating the conduct of commercial banks, improving the quality of credit assets, strengthening supervision and administration, ensuring the sound operation of commercial banks, maintaining financial order, and promoting the development of the socialist market economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称的商业银行是指依照本法和《中华人民共和国公司法》设立的吸收公众存款、发放贷款、办理结算等业务的企业法人。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, a commercial bank means an enterprise legal person that is established in accordance with this Law and the Company Law of the People's Republic of China to engage in such businesses as taking deposits from the general public, granting loans, and handling settlement.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　商业银行可以经营下列部分或者全部业务：  
（一）吸收公众存款；  
（二）发放短期、中期和长期贷款；  
（三）办理国内外结算；  
（四）办理票据承兑与贴现；  
（五）发行金融债券；  
（六）代理发行、代理兑付、承销政府债券；  
（七）买卖政府债券、金融债券；  
（八）从事同业拆借；  
（九）买卖、代理买卖外汇；  
（十）从事银行卡业务；  
（十一）提供信用证服务及担保；  
（十二）代理收付款项及代理保险业务；  
（十三）提供保管箱服务；  
（十四）经国务院银行业监督管理机构批准的其他业务。  
经营范围由商业银行章程规定，报国务院银行业监督管理机构批准。  
商业银行经中国人民银行批准，可以经营结汇、售汇业务。

**Article 3** A commercial bank may engage in part or all of the following businesses:  
(1) taking deposits from the general public;  
(2) granting short-term, medium-term and long-term loans;  
(3) handling domestic and foreign settlement;  
(4) handling acceptance and discounting of negotiable instruments;  
(5) issuing financial bonds;  
(6) acting as an agent for issuing, redeeming and underwriting government bonds;  
(7) buying and selling government bonds and financial bonds;  
(8) engaging in interbank lending;  
(9) buying and selling foreign exchange, and acting as an agent for the purchase and sale of foreign exchange;  
(10) engaging in bank card business;  
(11) providing letter of credit services and guarantees;  
(12) acting as an agent for receipt and payment of money and acting as an agent for insurance business;  
(13) providing safe deposit box services;  
(14) other businesses approved by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The business scope shall be specified in the articles of association of the commercial bank and shall be reported to the banking regulatory authority of the State Council for approval.  
A commercial bank may, upon approval by the People's Bank of China, engage in the business of settlement and sale of foreign exchange.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　商业银行以安全性、流动性、效益性为经营原则，实行自主经营，自担风险，自负盈亏，自我约束。  
商业银行依法开展业务，不受任何单位和个人的干涉。  
商业银行以其全部法人财产独立承担民事责任。

**Article 4** A commercial bank shall operate on the principles of safety, liquidity and efficiency, and shall exercise autonomous operation, assume its own risks, bear its own profits and losses, and exercise self-restraint.  
A commercial bank shall conduct its business in accordance with law and shall not be subject to interference by any unit or individual.  
A commercial bank shall independently bear civil liability with its entire legal person property.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　商业银行与客户的业务往来，应当遵循平等、自愿、公平和诚实信用的原则。

**Article 5** In business transactions with clients, commercial banks shall follow the principles of equality, voluntariness, fairness and good faith.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　商业银行应当保障存款人的合法权益不受任何单位和个人的侵犯。

**Article 6** A commercial bank shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of depositors from infringement by any unit or individual.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　商业银行开展信贷业务，应当严格审查借款人的资信，实行担保，保障按期收回贷款。  
商业银行依法向借款人收回到期贷款的本金和利息，受法律保护。

**Article 7** A commercial bank, when engaging in credit business, shall strictly examine the creditworthiness of a borrower, provide guarantee, and ensure the timely recovery of loans.  
The collection by a commercial bank of the principal and interest of matured loans from a borrower according to law shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　商业银行开展业务，应当遵守法律、行政法规的有关规定，不得损害国家利益、社会公共利益。

**Article 8** A commercial bank, when conducting business, shall comply with the relevant provisions of laws and administrative regulations and shall not harm the interests of the State or the public interest.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　商业银行开展业务，应当遵守公平竞争的原则，不得从事不正当竞争。

**Article 9** A commercial bank, when conducting business, shall follow the principle of fair competition and shall not engage in unfair competition.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　商业银行依法接受国务院银行业监督管理机构的监督管理，但法律规定其有关业务接受其他监督管理部门或者机构监督管理的，依照其规定。

**Article 10** A commercial bank shall, according to law, accept the supervision and administration of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council; provided that if a law provides that its relevant businesses shall be subject to the supervision and administration of another supervisory and administrative department or institution, the provisions of such law shall prevail.

### 第二章　商业银行的设立和组织机构

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　设立商业银行，应当经国务院银行业监督管理机构审查批准。  
未经国务院银行业监督管理机构批准，任何单位和个人不得从事吸收公众存款等商业银行业务，任何单位不得在名称中使用“银行”字样。

**Article 11** The establishment of a commercial bank shall be subject to examination and approval by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Without the approval of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, no unit or individual may engage in such commercial banking businesses as taking deposits from the general public, and no unit may use the word "bank" in its name.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　设立商业银行，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有符合本法和《中华人民共和国公司法》规定的章程；  
（二）有符合本法规定的注册资本最低限额；  
（三）有具备任职专业知识和业务工作经验的董事、高级管理人员；  
（四）有健全的组织机构和管理制度；  
（五）有符合要求的营业场所、安全防范措施和与业务有关的其他设施。  
设立商业银行，还应当符合其他审慎性条件。

**Article 12** The establishment of a commercial bank shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having an articles of association that complies with this Law and the Company Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) having the minimum amount of registered capital as prescribed by this Law;  
(3) having directors and senior officers with the professional knowledge and business experience required for their positions;  
(4) having a sound organizational structure and management system;  
(5) having business premises, safety precautions and other facilities related to the business that meet the requirements.  
The establishment of a commercial bank shall also meet other prudential conditions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　设立全国性商业银行的注册资本最低限额为十亿元人民币。设立城市商业银行的注册资本最低限额为一亿元人民币，设立农村商业银行的注册资本最低限额为五千万元人民币。注册资本应当是实缴资本。  
国务院银行业监督管理机构根据审慎监管的要求可以调整注册资本最低限额，但不得少于前款规定的限额。

**Article 13** The minimum amount of registered capital for the establishment of a national commercial bank shall be RMB 1 billion yuan. The minimum amount of registered capital for the establishment of a city commercial bank shall be RMB 100 million yuan, and the minimum amount of registered capital for the establishment of a rural commercial bank shall be RMB 50 million yuan. The registered capital shall be paid-in capital.  
The banking regulatory authority of the State Council may, based on the requirements of prudential regulation, adjust the minimum amount of registered capital, but such adjusted amount shall not be less than the minimum amount prescribed in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　设立商业银行，申请人应当向国务院银行业监督管理机构提交下列文件、资料：  
（一）申请书，申请书应当载明拟设立的商业银行的名称、所在地、注册资本、业务范围等；  
（二）可行性研究报告；  
（三）国务院银行业监督管理机构规定提交的其他文件、资料。

**Article 14** To establish a commercial bank, an applicant shall submit the following documents and materials to the banking regulatory authority of the State Council:  
(1) an application letter, which shall state the name, location, registered capital, business scope, etc. of the proposed commercial bank;  
(2) a feasibility study report;  
(3) other documents and materials as required by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　设立商业银行的申请经审查符合[本法第十四条](#art-14)规定的，申请人应当填写正式申请表，并提交下列文件、资料：  
（一）章程草案；  
（二）拟任职的董事、高级管理人员的资格证明；  
（三）法定验资机构出具的验资证明；  
（四）股东名册及其出资额、股份；  
（五）持有注册资本百分之五以上的股东的资信证明和有关资料；  
（六）经营方针和计划；  
（七）营业场所、安全防范措施和与业务有关的其他设施的资料；  
（八）国务院银行业监督管理机构规定的其他文件、资料。

**Article 15** Where an application for the establishment of a commercial bank is found, upon examination, to comply with the provisions of Article 14 of this Law, the applicant shall fill in a formal application form and submit the following documents and materials:  
(1) a draft of the articles of association;  
(2) qualifications certificates of the proposed directors and senior officers;  
(3) a capital verification certificate issued by a statutory capital verification institution;  
(4) a list of shareholders together with their capital contributions and shares;  
(5) creditworthiness certificates and relevant materials of shareholders holding five percent or more of the registered capital;  
(6) business policies and plans;  
(7) materials on the business premises, safety precautions and other facilities related to the business;  
(8) other documents and materials as prescribed by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　经批准设立的商业银行，由国务院银行业监督管理机构颁发经营许可证，并凭该许可证向工商行政管理部门办理登记，领取营业执照。

**Article 16** A commercial bank that has been established upon approval shall be issued a banking permit by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, and shall, on the strength of such permit, register with the administrative department for industry and commerce and obtain a business license.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　商业银行的组织形式、组织机构适用《中华人民共和国公司法》的规定。  
本法施行前设立的商业银行，其组织形式、组织机构不完全符合《中华人民共和国公司法》规定的，可以继续沿用原有的规定，适用前款规定的日期由国务院规定。

**Article 17** The organizational form and structure of a commercial bank shall be governed by the provisions of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where a commercial bank established before the entry into force of this Law does not fully comply with the provisions of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China in terms of its organizational form and structure, it may continue to follow the original provisions, and the date for the application of the preceding paragraph shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国有独资商业银行设立监事会。监事会的产生办法由国务院规定。  
监事会对国有独资商业银行的信贷资产质量、资产负债比例、国有资产保值增值等情况以及高级管理人员违反法律、行政法规或者章程的行为和损害银行利益的行为进行监督。

**Article 18** A wholly State-owned commercial bank shall establish a board of supervisors. The method for the formation of the board of supervisors shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
The board of supervisors shall supervise the credit asset quality, asset-liability ratio, preservation and appreciation of State-owned assets, and the acts of senior officers that violate laws, administrative regulations or the articles of association, as well as acts that harm the interests of the bank.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　商业银行根据业务需要可以在中华人民共和国境内外设立分支机构。设立分支机构必须经国务院银行业监督管理机构审查批准。在中华人民共和国境内的分支机构，不按行政区划设立。  
商业银行在中华人民共和国境内设立分支机构，应当按照规定拨付与其经营规模相适应的营运资金额。拨付各分支机构营运资金额的总和，不得超过总行资本金总额的百分之六十。

**Article 19** A commercial bank may, based on business needs, establish branches within or outside the territory of the People's Republic of China. The establishment of a branch must be subject to examination and approval by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council. Branches established within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall not be established according to administrative divisions.  
Where a commercial bank establishes a branch within the territory of the People's Republic of China, it shall allocate an amount of working capital commensurate with its scale of operations as prescribed. The total amount of working capital allocated to all branches shall not exceed sixty percent of the total capital of the head office.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　设立商业银行分支机构，申请人应当向国务院银行业监督管理机构提交下列文件、资料：  
（一）申请书，申请书应当载明拟设立的分支机构的名称、营运资金额、业务范围、总行及分支机构所在地等；  
（二）申请人最近二年的财务会计报告；  
（三）拟任职的高级管理人员的资格证明；  
（四）经营方针和计划；  
（五）营业场所、安全防范措施和与业务有关的其他设施的资料；  
（六）国务院银行业监督管理机构规定的其他文件、资料。

**Article 20** To establish a branch of a commercial bank, the applicant shall submit the following documents and materials to the banking regulatory authority of the State Council:  
(1) an application letter, which shall state the name, amount of working capital, business scope of the proposed branch, the locations of the head office and the branch, etc.;  
(2) the applicant's financial accounting reports for the latest two years;  
(3) qualifications certificates of the proposed senior officers;  
(4) business policies and plans;  
(5) materials on the business premises, safety precautions and other facilities related to the business;  
(6) other documents and materials as prescribed by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　经批准设立的商业银行分支机构，由国务院银行业监督管理机构颁发经营许可证，并凭该许可证向工商行政管理部门办理登记，领取营业执照。

**Article 21** A branch of a commercial bank that has been established upon approval shall be issued a banking permit by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, and shall, on the strength of such permit, register with the administrative department for industry and commerce and obtain a business license.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　商业银行对其分支机构实行全行统一核算，统一调度资金，分级管理的财务制度。  
商业银行分支机构不具有法人资格，在总行授权范围内依法开展业务，其民事责任由总行承担。

**Article 22** A commercial bank shall implement a financial system for its branches that features unified accounting, unified allocation of funds, and hierarchical management.  
A branch of a commercial bank does not have the status of a legal person, and shall conduct business according to law within the scope authorized by the head office, with the head office bearing its civil liability.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　经批准设立的商业银行及其分支机构，由国务院银行业监督管理机构予以公告。  
商业银行及其分支机构自取得营业执照之日起无正当理由超过六个月未开业的，或者开业后自行停业连续六个月以上的，由国务院银行业监督管理机构吊销其经营许可证，并予以公告。

**Article 23** The establishment of a commercial bank and its branches upon approval shall be announced by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Where a commercial bank or its branch fails to commence business within six months after obtaining its business license without a justifiable reason, or after commencing business suspends business for six consecutive months or more, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall revoke its banking permit and make an announcement.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　商业银行有下列变更事项之一的，应当经国务院银行业监督管理机构批准：  
（一）变更名称；  
（二）变更注册资本；  
（三）变更总行或者分支行所在地；  
（四）调整业务范围；  
（五）变更持有资本总额或者股份总额百分之五以上的股东；  
（六）修改章程；  
（七）国务院银行业监督管理机构规定的其他变更事项。  
更换董事、高级管理人员时，应当报经国务院银行业监督管理机构审查其任职资格。

**Article 24** A commercial bank shall obtain the approval of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council for any of the following changes:  
(1) change of name;  
(2) change of registered capital;  
(3) change of the location of the head office or a branch;  
(4) adjustment of the business scope;  
(5) change of shareholders holding five percent or more of the total capital or total shares;  
(6) amendment of the articles of association;  
(7) other changes as prescribed by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Where directors or senior officers are replaced, the qualifications of the proposed candidates shall be submitted to the banking regulatory authority of the State Council for examination.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　商业银行的分立、合并，适用《中华人民共和国公司法》的规定。  
商业银行的分立、合并，应当经国务院银行业监督管理机构审查批准。

**Article 25** The division or merger of a commercial bank shall be governed by the provisions of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The division or merger of a commercial bank shall be subject to examination and approval by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　商业银行应当依照法律、行政法规的规定使用经营许可证。禁止伪造、变造、转让、出租、出借经营许可证。

**Article 26** A commercial bank shall use its banking permit in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations. It is prohibited to forge, alter, transfer, lease or lend the banking permit.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　有下列情形之一的，不得担任商业银行的董事、高级管理人员：  
（一）因犯有贪污、贿赂、侵占财产、挪用财产罪或者破坏社会经济秩序罪，被判处刑罚，或者因犯罪被剥夺政治权利的；  
（二）担任因经营不善破产清算的公司、企业的董事或者厂长、经理，并对该公司、企业的破产负有个人责任的；  
（三）担任因违法被吊销营业执照的公司、企业的法定代表人，并负有个人责任的；  
（四）个人所负数额较大的债务到期未清偿的。

**Article 27** No person in any of the following circumstances may serve as a director or senior officer of a commercial bank:  
(1) having been sentenced to criminal punishment for committing the crime of embezzlement, bribery, misappropriation of property, or undermining the socio-economic order, or having been deprived of political rights for a crime;  
(2) having served as a director, factory director or manager of a company or enterprise that went bankrupt and was liquidated due to poor management, and bearing personal responsibility for the bankruptcy;  
(3) having served as the legal representative of a company or enterprise whose business license was revoked for illegal acts, and bearing personal responsibility;  
(4) having a relatively large amount of personal debts that are due but unpaid.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　任何单位和个人购买商业银行股份总额百分之五以上的，应当事先经国务院银行业监督管理机构批准。

**Article 28** Any unit or individual that purchases five percent or more of the total shares of a commercial bank shall obtain the prior approval of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

### 第三章　对存款人的保护

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　商业银行办理个人储蓄存款业务，应当遵循存款自愿、取款自由、存款有息、为存款人保密的原则。  
对个人储蓄存款，商业银行有权拒绝任何单位或者个人查询、冻结、扣划，但法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 29** A commercial bank, when handling personal savings deposit business, shall follow the principles of voluntary deposit, free withdrawal, interest payment on deposits, and confidentiality for depositors.  
With respect to personal savings deposits, a commercial bank shall have the right to refuse any unit or individual to inquire about, freeze or deduct such deposits, unless otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对单位存款，商业银行有权拒绝任何单位或者个人查询，但法律、行政法规另有规定的除外；有权拒绝任何单位或者个人冻结、扣划，但法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 30** With respect to deposits of units, a commercial bank shall have the right to refuse any unit or individual to inquire about such deposits, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations; and shall have the right to refuse any unit or individual to freeze or deduct such deposits, unless otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　商业银行应当按照中国人民银行规定的存款利率的上下限，确定存款利率，并予以公告。

**Article 31** A commercial bank shall determine its deposit interest rates within the upper and lower limits prescribed by the People's Bank of China, and shall announce such rates.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　商业银行应当按照中国人民银行的规定，向中国人民银行交存存款准备金，留足备付金。

**Article 32** A commercial bank shall, in accordance with the provisions of the People's Bank of China, deposit a reserve fund with the People's Bank of China and maintain sufficient provisions for payments.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　商业银行应当保证存款本金和利息的支付，不得拖延、拒绝支付存款本金和利息。

**Article 33** A commercial bank shall guarantee the payment of the principal and interest of deposits and shall not delay or refuse payment of the principal and interest of deposits.

### 第四章　贷款和其他业务的基本规则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　商业银行根据国民经济和社会发展的需要，在国家产业政策指导下开展贷款业务。

**Article 34** A commercial bank shall conduct its lending business under the guidance of the national industrial policies, as required by the development of the national economy and society.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　商业银行贷款，应当对借款人的借款用途、偿还能力、还款方式等情况进行严格审查。  
商业银行贷款，应当实行审贷分离、分级审批的制度。

**Article 35** A commercial bank, when granting a loan, shall strictly examine the borrower's intended use of the loan, repayment ability, repayment method, and other relevant matters.  
A commercial bank, when granting a loan, shall implement a system of separating loan examination from loan approval and graded approval.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　商业银行贷款，借款人应当提供担保。商业银行应当对保证人的偿还能力，抵押物、质物的权属和价值以及实现抵押权、质权的可行性进行严格审查。  
经商业银行审查、评估，确认借款人资信良好，确能偿还贷款的，可以不提供担保。

**Article 36** When a commercial bank grants a loan, the borrower shall provide a guarantee. The commercial bank shall strictly examine the repayment ability of the guarantor, the ownership and value of the collateral or pledge, and the feasibility of realizing the mortgage or pledge right.  
If, upon examination and assessment by the commercial bank, the borrower is found to have good credit standing and to be truly able to repay the loan, the borrower may not be required to provide a guarantee.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　商业银行贷款，应当与借款人订立书面合同。合同应当约定贷款种类、借款用途、金额、利率、还款期限、还款方式、违约责任和双方认为需要约定的其他事项。

**Article 37** When a commercial bank grants a loan, it shall enter into a written contract with the borrower. The contract shall stipulate the type of loan, the intended use of the loan, the amount, the interest rate, the repayment period, the repayment method, the liability for breach of contract, and other matters that the parties consider necessary.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　商业银行应当按照中国人民银行规定的贷款利率的上下限，确定贷款利率。

**Article 38** A commercial bank shall determine its loan interest rates within the upper and lower limits prescribed by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　商业银行贷款，应当遵守下列资产负债比例管理的规定：  
（一）资本充足率不得低于百分之八；  
（二）流动性资产余额与流动性负债余额的比例不得低于百分之二十五；  
（三）对同一借款人的贷款余额与商业银行资本余额的比例不得超过百分之十；  
（四）国务院银行业监督管理机构对资产负债比例管理的其他规定。  
本法施行前设立的商业银行，在本法施行后，其资产负债比例不符合前款规定的，应当在一定的期限内符合前款规定。具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 39** When granting loans, a commercial bank shall comply with the following provisions on the management of asset-liability ratios:  
(1) the capital adequacy ratio shall not be lower than eight percent;  
(2) the ratio of the balance of current assets to the balance of current liabilities shall not be lower than twenty-five percent;  
(3) the ratio of the loan balance to the capital balance of a commercial bank for the same borrower shall not exceed ten percent;  
(4) other provisions of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council on the management of asset-liability ratios.  
A commercial bank established before the entry into force of this Law, whose asset-liability ratios do not comply with the provisions of the preceding paragraph after the entry into force of this Law, shall bring them into compliance within a specified period. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　商业银行不得向关系人发放信用贷款；向关系人发放担保贷款的条件不得优于其他借款人同类贷款的条件。  
前款所称关系人是指：  
（一）商业银行的董事、监事、管理人员、信贷业务人员及其近亲属；  
（二）前项所列人员投资或者担任高级管理职务的公司、企业和其他经济组织。

**Article 40** A commercial bank shall not grant unsecured loans to its related parties; the conditions for granting secured loans to its related parties shall not be more favorable than those for granting similar loans to other borrowers.  
For the purposes of the preceding paragraph, "related parties" mean:  
(1) the directors, supervisors, management personnel, and credit business personnel of the commercial bank and their close relatives;  
(2) companies, enterprises and other economic organizations in which the persons listed in the preceding subparagraph invest or hold senior management positions.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　任何单位和个人不得强令商业银行发放贷款或者提供担保。商业银行有权拒绝任何单位和个人强令要求其发放贷款或者提供担保。

**Article 41** No unit or individual may compel a commercial bank to grant a loan or provide a guarantee. A commercial bank shall have the right to refuse any unit or individual that attempts to compel it to grant a loan or provide a guarantee.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　借款人应当按期归还贷款的本金和利息。  
借款人到期不归还担保贷款的，商业银行依法享有要求保证人归还贷款本金和利息或者就该担保物优先受偿的权利。商业银行因行使抵押权、质权而取得的不动产或者股权，应当自取得之日起二年内予以处分。  
借款人到期不归还信用贷款的，应当按照合同约定承担责任。

**Article 42** A borrower shall repay the principal and interest of a loan on schedule.  
If a borrower fails to repay a secured loan on the due date, the commercial bank shall, according to law, have the right to require the guarantor to repay the principal and interest of the loan or to obtain priority in compensation from the collateral. A commercial bank that acquires real estate or equity interests through the exercise of a mortgage or pledge right shall dispose of such assets within two years from the date of acquisition.  
If a borrower fails to repay an unsecured loan on the due date, it shall bear liability in accordance with the contract.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　商业银行在中华人民共和国境内不得从事信托投资和证券经营业务，不得向非自用不动产投资或者向非银行金融机构和企业投资，但国家另有规定的除外。

**Article 43** A commercial bank shall not, within the territory of the People's Republic of China, engage in trust investment and securities operations, invest in non-self-use real estate, or invest in non-bank financial institutions and enterprises, unless the State provides otherwise.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　商业银行办理票据承兑、汇兑、委托收款等结算业务，应当按照规定的期限兑现，收付入账，不得压单、压票或者违反规定退票。有关兑现、收付入账期限的规定应当公布。

**Article 44** A commercial bank, when handling settlement operations such as acceptance of negotiable instruments, remittance, and entrusted collection, shall effect payment or receipt and book the transactions within the prescribed time limits, and shall not hold up orders or bills or dishonor bills in violation of provisions. The provisions on the time limits for payment, receipt and booking shall be announced.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　商业银行发行金融债券或者到境外借款，应当依照法律、行政法规的规定报经批准。

**Article 45** If a commercial bank issues financial bonds or borrows money abroad, it shall report for approval in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　同业拆借，应当遵守中国人民银行的规定。禁止利用拆入资金发放固定资产贷款或者用于投资。  
拆出资金限于交足存款准备金、留足备付金和归还中国人民银行到期贷款之后的闲置资金。拆入资金用于弥补票据结算、联行汇差头寸的不足和解决临时性周转资金的需要。

**Article 46** Interbank lending shall comply with the provisions of the People's Bank of China. It is prohibited to use borrowed funds to grant loans for fixed assets or for investment.  
The funds that may be lent out in interbank lending shall be limited to the idle funds remaining after the deposit reserve has been fully paid, sufficient provisions for payments have been maintained, and the matured loans from the People's Bank of China have been repaid. The funds borrowed in interbank lending shall be used to make up the shortfall in the settlement of negotiable instruments and interbank clearing balances, and to meet temporary working capital needs.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　商业银行不得违反规定提高或者降低利率以及采用其他不正当手段，吸收存款，发放贷款。

**Article 47** A commercial bank shall not raise or lower interest rates in violation of regulations, or adopt other improper means to absorb deposits or grant loans.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　企业事业单位可以自主选择一家商业银行的营业场所开立一个办理日常转账结算和现金收付的基本账户，不得开立两个以上基本账户。  
任何单位和个人不得将单位的资金以个人名义开立账户存储。

**Article 48** Enterprises and institutions may, at their own discretion, choose a business place of a commercial bank to open one basic account for daily transfer settlement and cash receipts and payments, and shall not open more than one basic account.  
No unit or individual may deposit the funds of a unit in an account opened in the name of an individual.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　商业银行的营业时间应当方便客户，并予以公告。商业银行应当在公告的营业时间内营业，不得擅自停止营业或者缩短营业时间。

**Article 49** The business hours of a commercial bank shall be convenient for clients and shall be announced. A commercial bank shall operate during the announced business hours and shall not suspend business or shorten business hours without authorization.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　商业银行办理业务，提供服务，按照规定收取手续费。收费项目和标准由国务院银行业监督管理机构、中国人民银行根据职责分工，分别会同国务院价格主管部门制定。

**Article 50** A commercial bank shall charge fees for handling business and providing services in accordance with regulations. The fee items and standards shall be formulated by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council and the People's Bank of China, respectively, together with the pricing authority of the State Council, according to their respective functions.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　商业银行应当按照国家有关规定保存财务会计报表、业务合同以及其他资料。

**Article 51** A commercial bank shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, keep financial accounting statements, business contracts and other materials.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　商业银行的工作人员应当遵守法律、行政法规和其他各项业务管理的规定，不得有下列行为：  
（一）利用职务上的便利，索取、收受贿赂或者违反国家规定收受各种名义的回扣、手续费；  
（二）利用职务上的便利，贪污、挪用、侵占本行或者客户的资金；  
（三）违反规定徇私向亲属、朋友发放贷款或者提供担保；  
（四）在其他经济组织兼职；  
（五）违反法律、行政法规和业务管理规定的其他行为。

**Article 52** The staff members of a commercial bank shall comply with laws, administrative regulations and various provisions on business management, and shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) taking advantage of their positions to demand or accept bribes, or to accept kickbacks or handling fees of various forms in violation of State regulations;  
(2) taking advantage of their positions to embezzle, misappropriate or misappropriate the funds of the bank or its clients;  
(3) granting loans or providing guarantees to relatives or friends for personal gain in violation of regulations;  
(4) holding concurrent positions in other economic organizations;  
(5) other acts that violate laws, administrative regulations and business management provisions.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　商业银行的工作人员不得泄露其在任职期间知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密。

**Article 53** A staff member of a commercial bank shall not disclose any State secret or trade secret that he or she has come to know during his or her term of office.

### 第五章　财务会计

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　商业银行应当依照法律和国家统一的会计制度以及国务院银行业监督管理机构的有关规定，建立、健全本行的财务、会计制度。

**Article 54** A commercial bank shall, in accordance with the law, the uniform accounting system of the State and the relevant provisions of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, establish and improve its own financial and accounting systems.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　商业银行应当按照国家有关规定，真实记录并全面反映其业务活动和财务状况，编制年度财务会计报告，及时向国务院银行业监督管理机构、中国人民银行和国务院财政部门报送。商业银行不得在法定的会计账册外另立会计账册。

**Article 55** A commercial bank shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, faithfully record and fully reflect its business activities and financial condition, prepare annual financial accounting reports, and submit them promptly to the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, the People's Bank of China, and the finance department of the State Council. A commercial bank shall not set up any accounting books other than those legally required.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　商业银行应当于每一会计年度终了三个月内，按照国务院银行业监督管理机构的规定，公布其上一年度的经营业绩和审计报告。

**Article 56** A commercial bank shall, within three months after the end of each fiscal year, publish its operating results and audit report for the previous year in accordance with the provisions of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　商业银行应当按照国家有关规定，提取呆账准备金，冲销呆账。

**Article 57** A commercial bank shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, make provisions for non-performing loans and write off non-performing loans.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　商业银行的会计年度自公历l月1日起至12月31日止。

**Article 58** The fiscal year of a commercial bank shall begin on January 1 and end on December 31 of the Gregorian calendar.

### 第六章　监督管理

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　商业银行应当按照有关规定，制定本行的业务规则，建立、健全本行的风险管理和内部控制制度。

**Article 59** A commercial bank shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions, formulate its own business rules and establish and improve its own risk management and internal control systems.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　商业银行应当建立、健全本行对存款、贷款、结算、呆账等各项情况的稽核、检查制度。  
商业银行对分支机构应当进行经常性的稽核和检查监督。

**Article 60** A commercial bank shall establish and improve its own audit and inspection systems for deposits, loans, settlements, non-performing loans, and other matters.  
A commercial bank shall conduct regular audits, inspections and supervision on its branches.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　商业银行应当按照规定向国务院银行业监督管理机构、中国人民银行报送资产负债表、利润表以及其他财务会计、统计报表和资料。

**Article 61** A commercial bank shall, in accordance with regulations, submit balance sheets, income statements, and other financial accounting statements, statistical statements and materials to the banking regulatory authority of the State Council and the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　国务院银行业监督管理机构有权依照本法第三章、第四章、第五章的规定，随时对商业银行的存款、贷款、结算、呆账等情况进行检查监督。检查监督时，检查监督人员应当出示合法的证件。商业银行应当按照国务院银行业监督管理机构的要求，提供财务会计资料、业务合同和有关经营管理方面的其他信息。  
中国人民银行有权依照[《中华人民共和国中国人民银行法》第三十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/金融、证券与保险/中华人民共和国中国人民银行法.md#art-32)、第三十四条的规定对商业银行进行检查监督。

**Article 62** The banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall have the power to conduct inspection and supervision at any time on the deposits, loans, settlements, non-performing loans, etc. of commercial banks in accordance with the provisions of Chapters III, IV and V of this Law. When conducting inspection and supervision, the inspectors and supervisors shall produce valid identification. A commercial bank shall, as required by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, provide financial accounting materials, business contracts and other information concerning its operation and management.  
The People's Bank of China shall have the power to conduct inspection and supervision on commercial banks in accordance with the provisions of Articles 32 and 34 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　商业银行应当依法接受审计机关的审计监督。

**Article 63** A commercial bank shall, according to law, accept audit supervision by the audit authority.

### 第七章　接管和终止

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　商业银行已经或者可能发生信用危机，严重影响存款人的利益时，国务院银行业监督管理机构可以对该银行实行接管。  
接管的目的是对被接管的商业银行采取必要措施，以保护存款人的利益，恢复商业银行的正常经营能力。被接管的商业银行的债权债务关系不因接管而变化。

**Article 64** Where a commercial bank has suffered or may suffer a credit crisis that seriously affects the interests of depositors, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council may take over the bank.  
The purpose of the takeover is to take necessary measures with respect to the commercial bank under takeover to protect the interests of depositors and restore the normal operation capacity of the commercial bank. The creditor-debtor relationship of the commercial bank under takeover shall not change as a result of the takeover.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　接管由国务院银行业监督管理机构决定，并组织实施。国务院银行业监督管理机构的接管决定应当载明下列内容：  
（一）被接管的商业银行名称；  
（二）接管理由；  
（三）接管组织；  
（四）接管期限。  
接管决定由国务院银行业监督管理机构予以公告。

**Article 65** The takeover shall be decided upon and organized and implemented by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council. The takeover decision of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall state the following:  
(1) the name of the commercial bank under takeover;  
(2) the reason for the takeover;  
(3) the takeover organization;  
(4) the term of the takeover.  
The takeover decision shall be announced by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　接管自接管决定实施之日起开始。  
自接管开始之日起，由接管组织行使商业银行的经营管理权力。

**Article 66** The takeover shall commence from the date on which the takeover decision is implemented.  
From the date the takeover commences, the takeover organization shall exercise the operational and management power of the commercial bank.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　接管期限届满，国务院银行业监督管理机构可以决定延期，但接管期限最长不得超过二年。

**Article 67** Upon expiry of the term of takeover, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council may decide to extend the term; however, the maximum term of takeover shall not exceed two years.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　有下列情形之一的，接管终止：  
（一）接管决定规定的期限届满或者国务院银行业监督管理机构决定的接管延期届满；  
（二）接管期限届满前，该商业银行已恢复正常经营能力；  
（三）接管期限届满前，该商业银行被合并或者被依法宣告破产。

**Article 68** The takeover shall terminate in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the term specified in the takeover decision expires or the extended term decided by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council expires;  
(2) before the expiry of the term of takeover, the commercial bank has resumed its normal operation capacity;  
(3) before the expiry of the term of takeover, the commercial bank is merged or is declared bankrupt according to law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　商业银行因分立、合并或者出现公司章程规定的解散事由需要解散的，应当向国务院银行业监督管理机构提出申请，并附解散的理由和支付存款的本金和利息等债务清偿计划。经国务院银行业监督管理机构批准后解散。  
商业银行解散的，应当依法成立清算组，进行清算，按照清偿计划及时偿还存款本金和利息等债务。国务院银行业监督管理机构监督清算过程。

**Article 69** If a commercial bank needs to dissolve due to division, merger, or the occurrence of a cause for dissolution specified in its articles of association, it shall file an application with the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, together with the reasons for dissolution and a plan for the repayment of debts including the principal and interest of deposits. The dissolution shall be carried out after approval by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.  
If a commercial bank dissolves, it shall, according to law, establish a liquidation group to conduct liquidation and promptly repay debts including the principal and interest of deposits in accordance with the repayment plan. The banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall supervise the liquidation process.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　商业银行因吊销经营许可证被撤销的，国务院银行业监督管理机构应当依法及时组织成立清算组，进行清算，按照清偿计划及时偿还存款本金和利息等债务。

**Article 70** If a commercial bank is abolished due to the revocation of its banking permit, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall, according to law and in a timely manner, organize the establishment of a liquidation group to conduct liquidation and promptly repay debts including the principal and interest of deposits in accordance with the repayment plan.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　商业银行不能支付到期债务，经国务院银行业监督管理机构同意，由人民法院依法宣告其破产。商业银行被宣告破产的，由人民法院组织国务院银行业监督管理机构等有关部门和有关人员成立清算组，进行清算。  
商业银行破产清算时，在支付清算费用、所欠职工工资和劳动保险费用后，应当优先支付个人储蓄存款的本金和利息。

**Article 71** If a commercial bank is unable to pay its debts as they fall due, upon the consent of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, the People's Court shall declare it bankrupt according to law. Where a commercial bank is declared bankrupt, the People's Court shall organize the banking regulatory authority of the State Council and other relevant departments and persons to establish a liquidation group for liquidation.  
When a commercial bank is liquidated in bankruptcy, after the payment of liquidation expenses, wages owed to employees and labor insurance premiums, the principal and interest of personal savings deposits shall be paid in priority.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　商业银行因解散、被撤销和被宣告破产而终止。

**Article 72** A commercial bank shall terminate upon dissolution, abolition or declaration of bankruptcy.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　商业银行有下列情形之一，对存款人或者其他客户造成财产损害的，应当承担支付迟延履行的利息以及其他民事责任：  
（一）无故拖延、拒绝支付存款本金和利息的；  
（二）违反票据承兑等结算业务规定，不予兑现，不予收付入账，压单、压票或者违反规定退票的；  
（三）非法查询、冻结、扣划个人储蓄存款或者单位存款的；  
（四）违反本法规定对存款人或者其他客户造成损害的其他行为。  
有前款规定情形的，由国务院银行业监督管理机构责令改正，有违法所得的，没收违法所得，违法所得五万元以上的，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五万元的，处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款。

**Article 73** A commercial bank that, under any of the following circumstances, causes property damage to a depositor or other client shall be liable for paying interest for delayed performance and other civil liability:  
(1) delaying or refusing payment of the principal and interest of deposits without justification;  
(2) violating the provisions on settlement operations such as acceptance of negotiable instruments, failing to effect payment, failing to book receipts and payments, holding up orders or bills, or dishonoring bills in violation of regulations;  
(3) unlawfully inquiring about, freezing or deducting personal savings deposits or deposits of units;  
(4) other acts that cause damage to depositors or other clients in violation of the provisions of this Law.  
Where a commercial bank commits any of the acts specified in the preceding paragraph, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed if the illegal gains exceed RMB 50,000 yuan; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　商业银行有下列情形之一，由国务院银行业监督管理机构责令改正，有违法所得的，没收违法所得，违法所得五十万元以上的，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五十万元的，处五十万元以上二百万元以下罚款；情节特别严重或者逾期不改正的，可以责令停业整顿或者吊销其经营许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）未经批准设立分支机构的；  
（二）未经批准分立、合并或者违反规定对变更事项不报批的；  
（三）违反规定提高或者降低利率以及采用其他不正当手段，吸收存款，发放贷款的；  
（四）出租、出借经营许可证的；  
（五）未经批准买卖、代理买卖外汇的；  
（六）未经批准买卖政府债券或者发行、买卖金融债券的；  
（七）违反国家规定从事信托投资和证券经营业务、向非自用不动产投资或者向非银行金融机构和企业投资的；  
（八）向关系人发放信用贷款或者发放担保贷款的条件优于其他借款人同类贷款的条件的。

**Article 74** Where a commercial bank commits any of the following acts, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed if the illegal gains exceed RMB 500,000 yuan; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 500,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 yuan but not more than RMB 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are particularly serious or the commercial bank fails to make corrections within the time limit, it may be ordered to suspend business for rectification or its banking permit may be revoked; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) establishing a branch without approval;  
(2) dividing or merging without approval, or failing to report changes as required in violation of regulations;  
(3) raising or lowering interest rates in violation of regulations, or adopting other improper means to absorb deposits or grant loans;  
(4) leasing or lending the banking permit;  
(5) buying or selling foreign exchange, or acting as an agent for such purchase or sale, without approval;  
(6) buying or selling government bonds, or issuing or buying or selling financial bonds, without approval;  
(7) engaging in trust investment and securities operations, investing in non-self-use real estate, or investing in non-bank financial institutions and enterprises, in violation of State regulations;  
(8) granting unsecured loans to related parties, or granting secured loans to related parties on terms more favorable than those for similar loans to other borrowers.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　商业银行有下列情形之一，由国务院银行业监督管理机构责令改正，并处二十万元以上五十万元以下罚款；情节特别严重或者逾期不改正的，可以责令停业整顿或者吊销其经营许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）拒绝或者阻碍国务院银行业监督管理机构检查监督的；  
（二）提供虚假的或者隐瞒重要事实的财务会计报告、报表和统计报表的；  
（三）未遵守资本充足率、资产流动性比例、同一借款人贷款比例和国务院银行业监督管理机构有关资产负债比例管理的其他规定的。

**Article 75** Where a commercial bank commits any of the following acts, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are particularly serious or the commercial bank fails to make corrections within the time limit, it may be ordered to suspend business for rectification or its banking permit may be revoked; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) refusing or obstructing inspection and supervision by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council;  
(2) providing false financial accounting reports, statements or statistical statements, or concealing material facts;  
(3) failing to comply with the capital adequacy ratio, the asset liquidity ratio, the loan ratio for a single borrower, or other provisions of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council on the management of asset-liability ratios.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　商业银行有下列情形之一，由中国人民银行责令改正，有违法所得的，没收违法所得，违法所得五十万元以上的，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五十万元的，处五十万元以上二百万元以下罚款；情节特别严重或者逾期不改正的，中国人民银行可以建议国务院银行业监督管理机构责令停业整顿或者吊销其经营许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）未经批准办理结汇、售汇的；  
（二）未经批准在银行间债券市场发行、买卖金融债券或者到境外借款的；  
（三）违反规定同业拆借的。

**Article 76** Where a commercial bank commits any of the following acts, the People's Bank of China shall order it to make corrections; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed if the illegal gains exceed RMB 500,000 yuan; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 500,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 yuan but not more than RMB 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are particularly serious or the commercial bank fails to make corrections within the time limit, the People's Bank of China may recommend that the banking regulatory authority of the State Council order it to suspend business for rectification or revoke its banking permit; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) handling the settlement or sale of foreign exchange without approval;  
(2) issuing or buying or selling financial bonds on the interbank bond market, or borrowing abroad, without approval;  
(3) engaging in interbank lending in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　商业银行有下列情形之一，由中国人民银行责令改正，并处二十万元以上五十万元以下罚款；情节特别严重或者逾期不改正的，中国人民银行可以建议国务院银行业监督管理机构责令停业整顿或者吊销其经营许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）拒绝或者阻碍中国人民银行检查监督的；  
（二）提供虚假的或者隐瞒重要事实的财务会计报告、报表和统计报表的；  
（三）未按照中国人民银行规定的比例交存存款准备金的。

**Article 77** Where a commercial bank commits any of the following acts, the People's Bank of China shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are particularly serious or the commercial bank fails to make corrections within the time limit, the People's Bank of China may recommend that the banking regulatory authority of the State Council order it to suspend business for rectification or revoke its banking permit; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) refusing or obstructing inspection and supervision by the People's Bank of China;  
(2) providing false financial accounting reports, statements or statistical statements, or concealing material facts;  
(3) failing to deposit the reserve fund in accordance with the ratio prescribed by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　商业银行有[本法第七十三条](#art-73)至第七十七条规定情形的，对直接负责的董事、高级管理人员和其他直接责任人员，应当给予纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 78** Where a commercial bank falls under any of the circumstances specified in Articles 73 through 77 of this Law, the directors, senior officers and other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　有下列情形之一，由国务院银行业监督管理机构责令改正，有违法所得的，没收违法所得，违法所得五万元以上的，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五万元的，处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款：  
（一）未经批准在名称中使用“银行”字样的；  
（二）未经批准购买商业银行股份总额百分之五以上的；  
（三）将单位的资金以个人名义开立账户存储的。

**Article 79** Where any of the following acts is committed, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall order corrections; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed if the illegal gains exceed RMB 50,000 yuan; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) using the word "bank" in the name without approval;  
(2) purchasing five percent or more of the total shares of a commercial bank without approval;  
(3) depositing the funds of a unit in an account opened in the name of an individual.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　商业银行不按照规定向国务院银行业监督管理机构报送有关文件、资料的，由国务院银行业监督管理机构责令改正，逾期不改正的，处十万元以上三十万元以下罚款。  
商业银行不按照规定向中国人民银行报送有关文件、资料的，由中国人民银行责令改正，逾期不改正的，处十万元以上三十万元以下罚款。

**Article 80** If a commercial bank fails to submit relevant documents and materials to the banking regulatory authority of the State Council as required, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 300,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
If a commercial bank fails to submit relevant documents and materials to the People's Bank of China as required, the People's Bank of China shall order it to make corrections; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 300,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　未经国务院银行业监督管理机构批准，擅自设立商业银行，或者非法吸收公众存款、变相吸收公众存款，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；并由国务院银行业监督管理机构予以取缔。  
伪造、变造、转让商业银行经营许可证，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 81** Where, without the approval of the banking regulatory authority of the State Council, a commercial bank is established without authorization, or public deposits are illegally absorbed or absorbed in a disguised form, and such act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; and the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall ban such act.  
Where the banking permit of a commercial bank is forged, altered or transferred, and such act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　借款人采取欺诈手段骗取贷款，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 82** Where a borrower obtains a loan by fraudulent means, and such act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　有[本法第八十一条](#art-81)、第八十二条规定的行为，尚不构成犯罪的，由国务院银行业监督管理机构没收违法所得，违法所得五十万元以上的，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五十万元的，处五十万元以上二百万元以下罚款。

**Article 83** Where an act specified in Article 81 or 82 of this Law does not constitute a crime, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall confiscate the illegal gains; if the illegal gains exceed RMB 500,000 yuan, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed concurrently; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 500,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 yuan but not more than RMB 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　商业银行工作人员利用职务上的便利，索取、收受贿赂或者违反国家规定收受各种名义的回扣、手续费，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，应当给予纪律处分。  
有前款行为，发放贷款或者提供担保造成损失的，应当承担全部或者部分赔偿责任。

**Article 84** Where a staff member of a commercial bank, taking advantage of his or her position, demands or accepts bribes, or accepts kickbacks or handling fees of various forms in violation of State regulations, and such act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed.  
Where a person who commits the act specified in the preceding paragraph grants a loan or provides a guarantee and thereby causes losses, the person shall bear liability for full or partial compensation.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　商业银行工作人员利用职务上的便利，贪污、挪用、侵占本行或者客户资金，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，应当给予纪律处分。

**Article 85** Where a staff member of a commercial bank, taking advantage of his or her position, embezzles, misappropriates or misappropriates the funds of the bank or its clients, and such act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　商业银行工作人员违反本法规定玩忽职守造成损失的，应当给予纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反规定徇私向亲属、朋友发放贷款或者提供担保造成损失的，应当承担全部或者部分赔偿责任。

**Article 86** Where a staff member of a commercial bank, through dereliction of duty in violation of the provisions of this Law, causes losses, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a person, in violation of regulations, grants a loan or provides a guarantee to relatives or friends for personal gain and thereby causes losses, the person shall bear liability for full or partial compensation.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　商业银行工作人员泄露在任职期间知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密的，应当给予纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 87** Where a staff member of a commercial bank discloses a State secret or trade secret that he or she has come to know during his or her term of office, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　单位或者个人强令商业银行发放贷款或者提供担保的，应当对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员或者个人给予纪律处分；造成损失的，应当承担全部或者部分赔偿责任。  
商业银行的工作人员对单位或者个人强令其发放贷款或者提供担保未予拒绝的，应当给予纪律处分；造成损失的，应当承担相应的赔偿责任。

**Article 88** Where a unit or individual compels a commercial bank to grant a loan or provide a guarantee, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons or the individual shall be given disciplinary sanctions; if losses are caused, they shall bear liability for full or partial compensation.  
Where a staff member of a commercial bank fails to refuse the compulsion by a unit or individual to grant a loan or provide a guarantee, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed; if losses are caused, the staff member shall bear corresponding liability for compensation.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　商业银行违反本法规定的，国务院银行业监督管理机构可以区别不同情形，取消其直接负责的董事、高级管理人员一定期限直至终身的任职资格，禁止直接负责的董事、高级管理人员和其他直接责任人员一定期限直至终身从事银行业工作。  
商业银行的行为尚不构成犯罪的，对直接负责的董事、高级管理人员和其他直接责任人员，给予警告，处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款。

**Article 89** Where a commercial bank violates the provisions of this Law, the banking regulatory authority of the State Council may, depending on the specific circumstances, disqualify the directly responsible directors and senior officers for a certain period or for life, and prohibit the directly responsible directors, senior officers and other directly liable persons from engaging in banking work for a certain period or for life.  
If the act of the commercial bank does not constitute a crime, a warning shall be given to the directly responsible directors, senior officers and other directly liable persons, and a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　商业银行及其工作人员对国务院银行业监督管理机构、中国人民银行的处罚决定不服的，可以依照《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》的规定向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 90** Where a commercial bank or its staff member disagrees with a penalty decision made by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council or the People's Bank of China, it may file a suit with the People's Court in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　本法施行前，按照国务院的规定经批准设立的商业银行不再办理审批手续。

**Article 91** Commercial banks that were established upon approval in accordance with the provisions of the State Council before the entry into force of this Law shall not need to go through approval procedures again.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　外资商业银行、中外合资商业银行、外国商业银行分行适用本法规定，法律、行政法规另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 92** The provisions of this Law shall apply to wholly foreign-funded commercial banks, Chinese-foreign equity joint venture commercial banks and branches of foreign commercial banks; if laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise, the provisions of such laws or administrative regulations shall prevail.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　城市信用合作社、农村信用合作社办理存款、贷款和结算等业务，适用本法有关规定。

**Article 93** Urban credit cooperatives and rural credit cooperatives that handle deposit, loan, settlement and other businesses shall apply the relevant provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　邮政企业办理商业银行的有关业务，适用本法有关规定。

**Article 94** Postal enterprises that handle relevant commercial banking businesses shall apply the relevant provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　本法自2015年10月1日起施行。

**Article 95** This Law shall come into force as of October 1, 2015.
