### 最高人民法院关于经人民法院裁定冻结的当事人银行存款其他人民法院不应就同一笔款额重复冻结问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于经人民法院裁定冻结的当事人银行存款  
  
其他人民法院不应就同一笔款额重复冻结问题的批复  
  
法（经）复〔 1987〕49号  
  
河北省高级人民法院：  
  
你院 1986年10月29日（ 86）冀法经字第 13号函收悉。经研究答复如下：  
  
人民法院在审理经济纠纷案件中，根据民事诉讼法（试行）的有关规定，采取查封、冻结、扣押的保全措施，是为了保证判决或者调解所确认的权利义务得以实现。因此，经人民法院依法裁定冻结的当事人一定数额的银行存款，在冻结期间，任何单位不得动用，其他人民法院不得就该笔银行存款再行冻结，银行亦不得自行解冻或者回收用以清偿到期的贷款。  
  
根据你院和山东省高级人民法院的报告，山东省临邑县人民法院在 1985年 10月24日裁定冻结了山东省惠民县姜楼乡农民路培泉借用本乡冯王劳保厂农行帐户存入的 6万元沥青款，并委托惠民县人民法院代为执行。河北省秦皇岛市海港区人民法院虽在 1985年10月18日，裁定冻结路培泉借用该帐户存入的 6万元沥青款，但是委托惠民县人民法院代为实施冻结措施却在 10月28日，晚于临邑县人民法院。惠民县人民法院受临邑县人民法院的委托采取了冻结措施之后，又接受海港区人民法院委托，对被告在同一银行帐户上的同一笔款额，实行重复冻结是不当的。  
  
当事人的银行存款被人民法院冻结后，遇有其他人民法院要求执行该笔被冻结存款的，如果被告确无其他财产可供执行，先行冻结的人民法院应依照民事诉讼法（试行）第一百八十条规定的顺序清偿；不足清偿同一顺序债权人要求的，可按比例分配。在执行中如发生争议，由上级法院协调解决。  
  
人民法院查封、扣押当事人的财产发生上述情况时，亦应照此办理。  
  
此复  
  
1987年12月14日

**Article 1** Reply on the Issue That Other People's Courts Shall Not Impose Duplicate Freezes on the Same Amount of Bank Deposits of a Party Which Have Been Frozen by a People's Court's Ruling  
Fa (Jing) Fu [1987] No. 49  
Higher People's Court of Hebei Province:  
We have received your letter dated October 29, 1986 (No. [86] Ji Fa Jing Zi No. 13). Upon study, we reply as follows:  
When a People's Court tries an economic dispute case, it adopts preservative measures such as sealing up, freezing, or impounding in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation) for the purpose of ensuring the realization of the rights and obligations confirmed by the judgment or mediation. Therefore, for a certain amount of bank deposits of a party frozen by a People's Court's ruling according to law, no unit may use such deposits during the freezing period, no other People's Court may impose another freeze on such bank deposits, and the bank may not unfreeze or recover such deposits on its own to repay overdue loans.  
According to the reports from your court and the Higher People's Court of Shandong Province, the People's Court of Linyi County, Shandong Province, on October 24, 1985, ruled to freeze the 60,000 yuan asphalt fund deposited by Lu Peiquan, a farmer from Jianglou Township, Huimin County, Shandong Province, by using the agricultural bank account of Fengwang Laobao Factory in the same township, and entrusted the People's Court of Huimin County to execute it on its behalf. Although the People's Court of Haigang District, Qinhuangdao City, Hebei Province, on October 18, 1985, ruled to freeze the same 60,000 yuan asphalt fund deposited by Lu Peiquan using that account, it entrusted the People's Court of Huimin County to implement the freezing measures only on October 28, which was later than the People's Court of Linyi County. After the People's Court of Huimin County had taken the freezing measures upon entrustment by the People's Court of Linyi County, it subsequently accepted the entrustment from the People's Court of Haigang District and imposed a duplicate freeze on the same amount of money in the same bank account of the defendant, which was improper.  
After a party's bank deposits have been frozen by a People's Court, if another People's Court requests enforcement of such frozen deposits, and if the defendant truly has no other property available for enforcement, the People's Court that first imposed the freeze shall satisfy the claims in the order provided in Article 180 of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation); if the deposits are insufficient to satisfy the claims of creditors in the same order, distribution may be made on a pro rata basis. If a dispute arises during enforcement, the higher-level court shall coordinate and resolve it.  
When a People's Court seals up or impounds a party's property and the above situation occurs, the same shall apply.  
Hereby reply.  
December 14, 1987
