### 最高人民法院关于人民法院可以直接与银行系统的营业所、信用社联系查询、冻结或者扣划企事业等单位存款的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于人民法院可以直接  
  
与银行系统的营业所、信用社联系  
  
查询、冻结或者扣划企事业等单位存款的批复  
  
一九八五年一月十七日法（研）复〔1985〕4号  
  
广东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（84）粤法经行字第147号请示收悉。我院经与中国人民银行联系研究后，答复如下:  
  
我院与中国人民银行于一九八三年十二月二十八日联合发出的（83）法研字第30号《关于查询、冻结和扣划企业事业单位、机关、团体的银行存款的联合通知》第一项中所说的“（主任）”系指银行系统中营业所或者信用社的主任。人民法院需要查询、冻结或者扣划企业事业单位、机关、团体的银行存款，可以出具公函，直接同作为上述单位开户银行的营业所、信用社联系，或者向它们发出协助执行通知书（附判决书、裁定书或调解书副本），请它们协助执行，而无须先经银行县、市支行或市分行区办一级核对。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Official Reply on the People's Courts' Direct Contact with Business Offices and Credit Cooperatives of the Banking System to Inquire into, Freeze or Deduct Deposits of Enterprises and Institutions  
January 17, 1985, Fa (Yan) Fu [1985] No. 4  
Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
Your court's petition (84) Yue Fa Jing Xing Zi No. 147 has been received. After contacting and studying with the People's Bank of China, our court replies as follows:  
The term "(manager)" referred to in Item 1 of the Joint Notice on Inquiring into, Freezing and Deducting Bank Deposits of Enterprises, Institutions, Government Agencies and Organizations (Fa Yan Zi [1983] No. 30) jointly issued by our court and the People's Bank of China on December 28, 1983, means the manager of a business office or credit cooperative within the banking system. Where a People's Court needs to inquire into, freeze or deduct the bank deposits of enterprises, institutions, government agencies or organizations, it may issue an official letter and directly contact the business office or credit cooperative that serves as the bank for the account opening of the aforesaid entity, or serve on them a notice of assistance in enforcement (attaching copies of the judgment, ruling or mediation statement), requesting their assistance in enforcement, without the need to first undergo verification at the county or city sub-branch or district office level of the bank.  
This is a reply.
