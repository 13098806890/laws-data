# 全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于惩治骗购外汇、逃汇和非法买卖外汇犯罪的决定

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1998-12-29  
**生效日期**：1998-12-29

> （1998年12月29日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第六次会议通过） 为了惩治骗购外汇、逃汇和非法买卖外汇的犯罪行为，维护国家外汇管理秩序，对刑法作如下补充修改： 一、有下列情形之一，骗购外汇，数额较大的，处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处骗购外汇数额百分之五以上百分之三十以下罚金；数额巨大或者有其他严重情节的，处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处骗购外汇数额百分之五以上百分之三十以下罚金；数额特别巨大或者有其他特别严重情节的，处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑，并处骗购外汇数额百分之五以上百分之三十以下罚金或者没收财产： （一）使用伪造、变造的海关签发的报关单、进口证明、外汇管理部门核准件等凭证和单据的； （二）重复使用海关签发的报关单、进口证明、外汇管理部门核准件等凭证和单据的； （三）以其他方式骗购外汇的。 伪造、变造海关签发的报关单、进口证明、外汇管理部门核准件等凭证和单据，并用于骗购外汇的，依照前款的规定从重处罚。 明知用于骗购外汇而提供人民币资金的，以共犯论处。 单位犯前三款罪的，对单位依照第一款的规定判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役；数额巨大或者有其他严重情节的，处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑；数额特别巨大或者有其他特别严重情节的，处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑。 二、买卖伪造、变造的海关签发的报关单、进口证明、外汇管理部门核准件等凭证和单据或者国家机关的其他公文、证件、印章的，依照刑法第二百八十条的规定定罪处罚。 三、将刑法第一百九十条修改为：公司、企业或者其他单位，违反国家规定，擅自将外汇存放境外，或者将境内的外汇非法转移到境外，数额较大的，对单位判处逃汇数额百分之五以上百分之三十以下罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役；数额巨大或者有其他严重情节的，对单位判处逃汇数额百分之五以上百分之三十以下罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五年以上有期徒刑。 四、在国家规定的交易场所以外非法买卖外汇，扰乱市场秩序，情节严重的，依照刑法第二百二十五条的规定定罪处罚。 单位犯前款罪的，依照刑法第二百三十一条的规定处罚。 五、海关、外汇管理部门以及金融机构、从事对外贸易经营活动的公司、企业或者其他单位的工作人员与骗购外汇或者逃汇的行为人通谋，为其提供购买外汇的有关凭证或者其他便利的，或者明知是伪造、变造的凭证和单据而售汇、付汇的，以共犯论，依照本决定从重处罚。 六、海关、外汇管理部门的工作人员严重不负责任，造成大量外汇被骗购或者逃汇，致使国家利益遭受重大损失的，依照刑法第三百九十七条的规定定罪处罚。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、有下列情形之一，骗购外汇，数额较大的，处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处骗购外汇数额百分之五以上百分之三十以下罚金；数额巨大或者有其他严重情节的，处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处骗购外汇数额百分之五以上百分之三十以下罚金；数额特别巨大或者有其他特别严重情节的，处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑，并处骗购外汇数额百分之五以上百分之三十以下罚金或者没收财产：  
（一）使用伪造、变造的海关签发的报关单、进口证明、外汇管理部门核准件等凭证和单据的；  
（二）重复使用海关签发的报关单、进口证明、外汇管理部门核准件等凭证和单据的；  
（三）以其他方式骗购外汇的。  
伪造、变造海关签发的报关单、进口证明、外汇管理部门核准件等凭证和单据，并用于骗购外汇的，依照前款的规定从重处罚。  
明知用于骗购外汇而提供人民币资金的，以共犯论处。  
单位犯前三款罪的，对单位依照第一款的规定判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役；数额巨大或者有其他严重情节的，处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑；数额特别巨大或者有其他特别严重情节的，处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑。

**Article 1** . 1. Whoever commits any of the following acts to fraudulently purchase foreign exchange, where the amount is relatively large, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention, and shall also be fined not less than 5% but not more than 30% of the amount of foreign exchange fraudulently purchased; where the amount is huge or there are other serious circumstances, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years but not more than ten years, and shall also be fined not less than 5% but not more than 30% of the amount of foreign exchange fraudulently purchased; and where the amount is especially huge or there are other especially serious circumstances, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment, and shall also be fined not less than 5% but not more than 30% of the amount of the foreign exchange fraudulently purchased, or shall be subject to confiscation of property:  
(1) using forged or altered customs clearance declarations, import certificates, approvals of the foreign exchange administration department, or other such certificates or documents;  
(2) reusing customs clearance declarations, import certificates, approvals of the foreign exchange administration department, or other such certificates or documents  
(3) fraudulently purchasing foreign exchange by other means. 2. Whoever forges or alters customs clearance declarations, import certificates, approvals of the foreign exchange administration department, or other such certificates or documents, and uses them to fraudulently purchase foreign exchange, shall be given a heavier punishment within the limits of the provisions of the preceding paragraph. 3. Whoever provides Renminbi funds knowing that they are to be used for the fraudulent purchase of foreign exchange shall be punished as an accomplice. 4. Where a unit commits any of the crimes prescribed in the preceding three paragraphs, a fine shall be imposed on the unit in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph, and the person in charge directly in charge and other persons directly liable therefor shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention; where the amount is huge or there are other serious circumstances, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years but not more than ten years; and where the amount is especially huge or there are other especially serious circumstances, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、买卖伪造、变造的海关签发的报关单、进口证明、外汇管理部门核准件等凭证和单据或者国家机关的其他公文、证件、印章的，依照[刑法第二百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-280)的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 2** . Whoever buys or sells forged or altered customs clearance declarations, import certificates, approvals of the foreign exchange administration department, or other such certificates or documents, or other official documents, certificates, or seals of state organs, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 280 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、将[刑法第一百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-190)修改为：公司、企业或者其他单位，违反国家规定，擅自将外汇存放境外，或者将境内的外汇非法转移到境外，数额较大的，对单位判处逃汇数额百分之五以上百分之三十以下罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役；数额巨大或者有其他严重情节的，对单位判处逃汇数额百分之五以上百分之三十以下罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五年以上有期徒刑。

**Article 3** . Article 190 of the Criminal Law is amended as follows: Where any company, enterprise, or other unit, in violation of state regulations, without authorization deposits foreign exchange overseas, or illegally transfers foreign exchange from within the territory overseas, if the amount is relatively large, a fine of not less than 5% but not more than 30% of the amount of foreign exchange illegally transferred overseas shall be imposed on the unit, and the person in charge directly in charge and other persons directly liable therefor shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention; if the amount is huge or there are other serious circumstances, a fine of not less than 5% but not more than 30% of the amount of foreign exchange illegally transferred overseas shall be imposed on the unit, and the person in charge directly in charge and other persons directly liable therefor shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、在国家规定的交易场所以外非法买卖外汇，扰乱市场秩序，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)的规定定罪处罚。  
单位犯前款罪的，依照[刑法第二百三十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-231)的规定处罚。

**Article 4** . Whoever illegally buys or sells foreign exchange outside the trading places designated by the state, thereby disrupting market order, where the circumstances are serious, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 225 of the Criminal Law. 2. Where a unit commits the crime prescribed in the preceding paragraph, it shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 231 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、海关、外汇管理部门以及金融机构、从事对外贸易经营活动的公司、企业或者其他单位的工作人员与骗购外汇或者逃汇的行为人通谋，为其提供购买外汇的有关凭证或者其他便利的，或者明知是伪造、变造的凭证和单据而售汇、付汇的，以共犯论，依照本决定从重处罚。

**Article 5** . Where any staff member of the customs or the foreign exchange administration department, or of a financial institution, or of a company, enterprise, or other unit engaged in foreign trade operations, conspires with a person who fraudulently purchases foreign exchange or illegally transfers foreign exchange overseas, providing the latter with relevant certificates for purchasing foreign exchange or other facilities, or sells or pays foreign exchange while knowing that the certificates or documents are forged or altered, such staff member shall be deemed an accomplice and given a heavier punishment in accordance with this Decision.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、海关、外汇管理部门的工作人员严重不负责任，造成大量外汇被骗购或者逃汇，致使国家利益遭受重大损失的，依照[刑法第三百九十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-397)的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 6** . Where any staff member of the customs or the foreign exchange administration department grossly neglects their duties, resulting in the fraudulent purchase of a large amount of foreign exchange or the illegal transfer of a large amount of foreign exchange overseas, thereby causing heavy losses to state interests, such staff member shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 397 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、金融机构、从事对外贸易经营活动的公司、企业的工作人员严重不负责任，造成大量外汇被骗购或者逃汇，致使国家利益遭受重大损失的，依照[刑法第一百六十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-167)的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 7** . Where any staff member of a financial institution, or of a company or enterprise engaged in foreign trade operations, grossly neglects their duties, resulting in the fraudulent purchase of a large amount of foreign exchange or the illegal transfer of a large amount of foreign exchange overseas, thereby causing heavy losses to state interests, such staff member shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 167 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、犯本决定规定之罪，依法被追缴、没收的财物和罚金，一律上缴国库。

**Article 8** . All property and fines recovered or confiscated according to law for any crime stipulated in this Decision shall be turned over to the state treasury.

<a id="art-9"></a>九、本决定自公布之日起施行。

**Article 9** . This Decision shall come into force as of the date of its promulgation.
