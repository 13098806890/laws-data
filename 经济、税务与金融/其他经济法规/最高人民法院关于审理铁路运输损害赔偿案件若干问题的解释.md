# 最高人民法院关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：1994-10-27

> 最高人民法院审判委员会第1823次会议通过的《最高人民法院 关于修改〈最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用 《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释〉等二十七件 民事类司法解释的决定》修正）

### 正文

一、实际损失的赔偿范围  
铁路法第十七条中的“实际损失”，是指因灭失、短少、变质、污染、损坏导致货物、包裹、行李实际价值的损失。  
铁路运输企业按照实际损失赔偿时，对灭失、短少的货物、包裹、行李，按照其实际价值赔偿；对变质、污染、损坏降低原有价值的货物、包裹、行李，可按照其受损前后实际价值的差额或者加工、修复费用赔偿。  
货物、包裹、行李的赔偿价值按照托运时的实际价值计算。实际价值中未包含已支付的铁路运杂费、包装费、保险费、短途搬运费等费用的，按照损失部分的比例加算。

**Article None** I. Scope of Compensation for Actual Losses  
"Actual losses" within the meaning of Article 17 of the Railway Law refers to the loss of actual value of goods, parcels, and luggage caused by loss, shortage, deterioration, contamination, or damage.  
When a railway transport enterprise compensates for actual losses, it shall compensate for goods, parcels, and luggage that have been lost or are short according to their actual value; for goods, parcels, and luggage whose original value has been reduced due to deterioration, contamination, or damage, compensation may be made based on the difference in actual value before and after the damage or the costs of processing or repair.  
The compensation value of goods, parcels, and luggage shall be calculated based on their actual value at the time of consignment. If the actual value does not include paid railway freight and miscellaneous fees, packaging fees, insurance premiums, short-distance handling fees, and other such fees, such fees shall be added in proportion to the portion of the loss.

二、铁路运输企业的重大过失  
铁路法第十七条中的“重大过失”是指铁路运输企业或者其受雇人、代理人对承运的货物、包裹、行李明知可能造成损失而轻率地作为或者不作为。

**Article None** II. Gross Negligence of Railway Transport Enterprises  
"Gross negligence" within the meaning of Article 17 of the Railway Law refers to the railway transport enterprise or its employees or agents recklessly acting or failing to act with knowledge that such action or inaction may cause loss to the goods, parcels, or luggage under carriage.

三、保价货物损失的赔偿  
铁路法第十七条第一款（一）项中规定的“按照实际损失赔偿，但最高不超过保价额。”是指保价运输的货物、包裹、行李在运输中发生损失，无论托运人在办理保价运输时，保价额是否与货物、包裹、行李的实际价值相符，均应在保价额内按照损失部分的实际价值赔偿，实际损失超过保价额的部分不予赔偿。  
如果损失是因铁路运输企业的故意或者重大过失造成的，比照铁路法第十七条第一款（二）项的规定，不受保价额的限制，按照实际损失赔偿。

**Article None** III. Compensation for Loss of Insured-Value Goods  
"Compensation shall be made according to actual losses, but shall not exceed the insured value" as stipulated in Article 17, Paragraph 1, Item (1) of the Railway Law means that if goods, parcels, or luggage transported under an insured-value contract sustain loss during carriage, regardless of whether the insured value declared by the consignor at the time of handling the insured-value transport corresponds to the actual value of the goods, parcels, or luggage, compensation shall be made within the insured value according to the actual value of the lost portion; the portion of the actual loss exceeding the insured value shall not be compensated.  
If the loss is caused by the intention or gross negligence of the railway transport enterprise, in accordance with the provisions of Article 17, Paragraph 1, Item (2) of the Railway Law, the limitation of the insured value shall not apply, and compensation shall be made according to actual losses.

四、保险货物损失的赔偿  
投保货物运输险的货物在运输中发生损失，对不属于铁路运输企业免责范围的，适用铁路法第十七条第一款（二）项的规定，由铁路运输企业承担赔偿责任。  
保险公司按照保险合同的约定向托运人或收货人先行赔付后，对于铁路运输企业应按货物实际损失承担赔偿责任的，保险公司按照支付的保险金额向铁路运输企业追偿，因不足额保险产生的实际损失与保险金的差额部分，由铁路运输企业赔偿；对于铁路运输企业应按限额承担赔偿责任的，在足额保险的情况下，保险公司向铁路运输企业的追偿额为铁路运输企业的赔偿限额，在不足额保险的情况下，保险公司向铁路运输企业的追偿额在铁路运输企业的赔偿限额内按照投保金额与货物实际价值的比例计算，因不足额保险产生的铁路运输企业的赔偿限额与保险公司在限额内追偿额的差额部分，由铁路运输企业赔偿。

**Article None** IV. Compensation for Loss of Insured Goods  
If goods covered by cargo transport insurance sustain loss during carriage and such loss is not within the scope of exemption from liability of a railway transport enterprise, the provisions of Article 17, Paragraph 1, Item (2) of the Railway Law shall apply, and the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability.  
After an insurance company makes an advance payment to the consignor or consignee in accordance with the insurance contract, if the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability according to the actual loss of goods, the insurance company shall claim recourse from the railway transport enterprise up to the amount of insurance paid; the difference between the actual loss and the insurance payment arising from underinsurance shall be compensated by the railway transport enterprise. If the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability up to a limit, in the case of full insurance, the amount of recourse by the insurance company against the railway transport enterprise shall be the railway transport enterprise's compensation limit; in the case of underinsurance, the amount of recourse by the insurance company against the railway transport enterprise shall be calculated within the railway transport enterprise's compensation limit in proportion to the insured amount to the actual value of the goods; the difference between the railway transport enterprise's compensation limit arising from underinsurance and the amount of recourse by the insurance company within the limit shall be compensated by the railway transport enterprise.

五、保险保价货物损失的赔偿  
既保险又保价的货物在运输中发生损失，对不属于铁路运输企业免责范围的，适用铁路法第十七条第一款（一）项的规定由铁路运输企业承担赔偿责任。对于保险公司先行赔付的，比照本解释第四条对保险货物损失的赔偿处理。

**Article None** V. Compensation for Loss of Goods That Are Both Insured and Have Insured Value  
If goods that are both insured and have an insured value sustain loss during carriage and such loss is not within the scope of exemption from liability of a railway transport enterprise, the provisions of Article 17, Paragraph 1, Item (1) of the Railway Law shall apply, and the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability. For cases where an insurance company makes an advance payment, the compensation for loss of insured goods under Article 4 of this Interpretation shall apply mutatis mutandis.

六、保险补偿制度的适用  
《铁路货物运输实行保险与负责运输相结合的补偿制度的规定（试行）》（简称保险补偿制度），适用于1991年5月1日铁路法实施以前已投保货物运输险的案件。铁路法实施后投保货物运输险的案件，适用铁路法第十七条第一款的规定，保险补偿制度中有关保险补偿的规定不再适用。

**Article None** VI. Application of the Insurance Compensation System  
"Provisions on the Implementation of a Compensation System Combining Insurance and Responsible Transport for Railway Cargo Transport (Trial)" (hereinafter referred to as the Insurance Compensation System) shall apply to cases where cargo transport insurance was purchased before May 1, 1991, the effective date of the Railway Law. For cases where cargo transport insurance is purchased after the effective date of the Railway Law, the provisions of Article 17, Paragraph 1 of the Railway Law shall apply, and the relevant provisions on insurance compensation in the Insurance Compensation System shall no longer apply.

七、逾期交付的责任  
货物、包裹、行李逾期交付，如果是因铁路逾期运到造成的，由铁路运输企业支付逾期违约金；如果是因收货人或旅客逾期领取造成的，由收货人或旅客支付保管费；既因逾期运到又因收货人或旅客逾期领取造成的，由双方各自承担相应的责任。  
铁路逾期运到并且发生损失时，铁路运输企业除支付逾期违约金外，还应当赔偿损失。对收货人或者旅客逾期领取，铁路运输企业在代保管期间因保管不当造成损失的，由铁路运输企业赔偿。

**Article None** VII. Liability for Delayed Delivery  
If goods, parcels, or luggage are delivered late because of a delay in arrival caused by the railway, the railway transport enterprise shall pay liquidated damages for the delay; if the delay is caused by the consignee or passenger's late collection, the consignee or passenger shall pay storage fees; if the delay is caused by both the late arrival by the railway and the late collection by the consignee or passenger, each party shall bear corresponding liability.  
If the railway delays in arrival and a loss occurs, the railway transport enterprise shall, in addition to paying liquidated damages for the delay, also compensate for the loss. If the consignee or passenger delays collection and the railway transport enterprise causes loss due to improper storage during the period of custodianship, the railway transport enterprise shall compensate for the loss.

八、误交付的责任  
货物、包裹、行李误交付（包括被第三者冒领造成的误交付），铁路运输企业查找超过运到期限的，由铁路运输企业支付逾期违约金。不能交付的，或者交付时有损失的，由铁路运输企业赔偿。铁路运输企业赔付后，再向有责任的第三者追偿。

**Article None** VIII. Liability for Wrongful Delivery  
If goods, parcels, or luggage are wrongfully delivered (including wrongful delivery caused by a third party's fraudulent claim), and the railway transport enterprise's search exceeds the transit period, the railway transport enterprise shall pay liquidated damages for the delay. If delivery is impossible, or if a loss occurs at the time of delivery, the railway transport enterprise shall compensate for the loss. After making compensation, the railway transport enterprise shall then seek recourse from the responsible third party.

九、赔偿后又找回原物的处理  
铁路运输企业赔付后又找回丢失、被盗、冒领、逾期等按灭失处理的货物、包裹、行李的，在通知托运人、收货人或旅客退还赔款领回原物的期限届满后仍无人领取的，适用铁路法第二十二条按无主货物的规定处理。铁路运输企业未通知托运人、收货人或者旅客而自行处理找回的货物、包裹、行李的，由铁路运输企业赔偿实际损失与已付赔款差额。

**Article None** IX. Handling of Recovery of Original Items After Compensation  
If a railway transport enterprise, after paying compensation, subsequently recovers goods, parcels, or luggage that were lost, stolen, fraudulently claimed, or delayed and treated as lost, and if after the expiry of the time limit for notifying the consignor, consignee, or passenger to refund the compensation and reclaim the original items no one comes to claim them, Article 22 of the Railway Law regarding unclaimed goods shall apply. If the railway transport enterprise disposes of the recovered goods, parcels, or luggage without notifying the consignor, consignee, or passenger, the railway transport enterprise shall compensate for the difference between the actual loss and the compensation already paid.

十、代办运输货物损失的赔偿  
代办运输的货物在铁路运输中发生损失，对代办运输企业接受托运人的委托以自己的名义与铁路运输企业签订运输合同托运或领取货物的，如委托人依据委托合同要求代办运输企业向铁路运输企业索赔的，应予支持。对代办运输企业未及时索赔而超过运输合同索赔时效的，代办运输企业应当赔偿。

**Article None** X. Compensation for Loss of Goods Transported by Agent  
If goods transported by an agent sustain loss during railway carriage, and the agent transport enterprise, having accepted the consignor's commission, signs a transport contract with the railway transport enterprise in its own name to consign or collect the goods, if the principal, pursuant to the commission contract, requests the agent transport enterprise to claim compensation from the railway transport enterprise, such request shall be supported. If the agent transport enterprise fails to file a timely claim and the claim time limit under the transport contract expires, the agent transport enterprise shall compensate.

十一、铁路旅客运送责任期间  
铁路运输企业对旅客运送的责任期间自旅客持有效车票进站时起到旅客出站或者应当出站时止。不包括旅客在候车室内的期间。

**Article None** XI. Period of Liability for Railway Passenger Transport  
The period of liability of a railway transport enterprise for passenger transport shall commence when a passenger holding a valid ticket enters the station and shall end when the passenger exits the station or ought to exit the station. It shall not include the period during which the passenger is in the waiting room.

十二、第三者责任造成旅客伤亡的赔偿  
在铁路旅客运送期间因第三者责任造成旅客伤亡，旅客或者其继承人要求铁路运输企业先予赔偿的，应予支持。铁路运输企业赔付后，有权向有责任的第三者追偿。

**Article None** XII. Compensation for Passenger Casualties Caused by Third-party Liability  
If, during railway passenger transport, a passenger sustains personal injury or death due to the liability of a third party, and the passenger or his or her successor requests the railway transport enterprise to make advance compensation, such request shall be supported. After paying compensation, the railway transport enterprise shall have the right to seek recourse from the responsible third party.
