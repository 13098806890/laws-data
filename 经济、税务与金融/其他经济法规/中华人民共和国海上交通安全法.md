# 中华人民共和国海上交通安全法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2021-04-29  
**生效日期**：2021-09-01

> （1983年9月2日第六届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二次会议通过　根据2016年11月7日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十四次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国对外贸易法〉等十二部法律的决定》修正　2021年4月29日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十八次会议修订） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　船舶、海上设施和船员 第三章　海上交通条件和航行保障 第四章　航行、停泊、作业 第五章　海上客货运输安全 第六章　海上搜寻救助 第七章　海上交通事故调查处理 第八章　监督管理 第九章　法律责任 第十章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强海上交通管理，维护海上交通秩序，保障生命财产安全，维护国家权益，制定本法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国管辖海域内从事航行、停泊、作业以及其他与海上交通安全相关的活动，适用本法。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强海上交通管理，维护海上交通秩序，保障生命财产安全，维护国家权益，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of strengthening maritime traffic management, maintaining maritime traffic order, ensuring the safety of life and property, and safeguarding national rights and interests.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国管辖海域内从事航行、停泊、作业以及其他与海上交通安全相关的活动，适用本法。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to activities such as navigation, mooring, operations, and other activities related to maritime traffic safety within the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家依法保障交通用海。  
海上交通安全工作坚持安全第一、预防为主、便利通行、依法管理的原则，保障海上交通安全、有序、畅通。

**Article 3** The State shall ensure the use of sea areas for traffic according to law.  
Maritime traffic safety work shall adhere to the principles of safety first, prevention oriented, facilitating navigation, and management according to law, to ensure maritime traffic safety, order, and smoothness.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院交通运输主管部门主管全国海上交通安全工作。  
国家海事管理机构统一负责海上交通安全监督管理工作，其他各级海事管理机构按照职责具体负责辖区内的海上交通安全监督管理工作。

**Article 4** The competent transport department of the State Council shall be in charge of national maritime traffic safety work.  
The national maritime safety administration shall be uniformly responsible for the supervision and administration of maritime traffic safety, and other maritime safety administrations at various levels shall be specifically responsible for the supervision and administration of maritime traffic safety within their respective jurisdictions according to their functions and duties.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　各级人民政府及有关部门应当支持海上交通安全工作，加强海上交通安全的宣传教育，提高全社会的海上交通安全意识。

**Article 5** People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall support maritime traffic safety work, strengthen publicity and education on maritime traffic safety, and enhance the maritime traffic safety awareness of the whole society.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家依法保障船员的劳动安全和职业健康，维护船员的合法权益。

**Article 6** The State shall ensure the labor safety and occupational health of seafarers according to law, and safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of seafarers.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　从事船舶、海上设施航行、停泊、作业以及其他与海上交通相关活动的单位、个人，应当遵守有关海上交通安全的法律、行政法规、规章以及强制性标准和技术规范；依法享有获得航海保障和海上救助的权利，承担维护海上交通安全和保护海洋生态环境的义务。

**Article 7** Units and individuals engaged in navigation, mooring, operations of vessels and offshore installations, and other activities related to maritime traffic shall comply with the relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules, and mandatory standards and technical specifications on maritime traffic safety; they shall have the right to obtain navigational security and maritime search and rescue according to law, and shall bear the obligation to maintain maritime traffic safety and protect the marine ecological environment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励和支持先进科学技术在海上交通安全工作中的应用，促进海上交通安全现代化建设，提高海上交通安全科学技术水平。

**Article 8** The State shall encourage and support the application of advanced science and technology in maritime traffic safety work, promote the modernization of maritime traffic safety, and improve the level of science and technology in maritime traffic safety.

### 第二章　船舶、海上设施和船员

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　中国籍船舶、在中华人民共和国管辖海域设置的海上设施、船运集装箱，以及国家海事管理机构确定的关系海上交通安全的重要船用设备、部件和材料，应当符合有关法律、行政法规、规章以及强制性标准和技术规范的要求，经船舶检验机构检验合格，取得相应证书、文书。证书、文书的清单由国家海事管理机构制定并公布。  
设立船舶检验机构应当经国家海事管理机构许可。船舶检验机构设立条件、程序及其管理等依照有关船舶检验的法律、行政法规的规定执行。  
持有相关证书、文书的单位应当按照规定的用途使用船舶、海上设施、船运集装箱以及重要船用设备、部件和材料，并应当依法定期进行安全技术检验。

**Article 9** Vessels of Chinese nationality, offshore installations set up in the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, shipping containers, and important marine equipment, components, and materials related to maritime traffic safety as determined by the national maritime safety administration, shall meet the requirements of relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules, and mandatory standards and technical specifications, and shall undergo inspection by a vessel inspection authority and obtain corresponding certificates and documents. The list of certificates and documents shall be formulated and published by the national maritime safety administration.  
The establishment of a vessel inspection authority shall be subject to permission by the national maritime safety administration. The conditions, procedures, and management for the establishment of vessel inspection authorities shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on vessel inspection.  
Units holding relevant certificates and documents shall use vessels, offshore installations, shipping containers, and important marine equipment, components, and materials for the specified purposes, and shall undergo regular safety technical inspections according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　船舶依照有关船舶登记的法律、行政法规的规定向海事管理机构申请船舶国籍登记、取得国籍证书后，方可悬挂中华人民共和国国旗航行、停泊、作业。  
中国籍船舶灭失或者报废的，船舶所有人应当在国务院交通运输主管部门规定的期限内申请办理注销国籍登记；船舶所有人逾期不申请注销国籍登记的，海事管理机构可以发布关于拟强制注销船舶国籍登记的公告。船舶所有人自公告发布之日起六十日内未提出异议的，海事管理机构可以注销该船舶的国籍登记。

**Article 10** A vessel shall, after applying for and obtaining a certificate of registry of nationality from the maritime safety administration in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on vessel registration, be entitled to fly the national flag of the People's Republic of China for navigation, mooring, and operations.  
If a vessel of Chinese nationality is lost or scrapped, the owner of the vessel shall apply for cancellation of the nationality registration within the time limit prescribed by the competent transport department of the State Council; if the owner fails to apply for cancellation of nationality registration within the time limit, the maritime safety administration may issue an announcement on the intended compulsory cancellation of the vessel's nationality registration. If the owner does not raise any objection within sixty days from the date of issuance of the announcement, the maritime safety administration may cancel the nationality registration of the vessel.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　中国籍船舶所有人、经营人或者管理人应当建立并运行安全营运和防治船舶污染管理体系。  
海事管理机构经对前款规定的管理体系审核合格的，发给符合证明和相应的船舶安全管理证书。

**Article 11** The owner, operator, or manager of a vessel of Chinese nationality shall establish and operate a safety operation and prevention of pollution from vessels management system.  
If the maritime safety administration, after verifying that the management system specified in the preceding paragraph meets the requirements, shall issue a document of compliance and the corresponding safety management certificate for the vessel.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　中国籍国际航行船舶的所有人、经营人或者管理人应当依照国务院交通运输主管部门的规定建立船舶保安制度，制定船舶保安计划，并按照船舶保安计划配备船舶保安设备，定期开展演练。

**Article 12** The owner, operator, or manager of a vessel of Chinese nationality engaged in international voyages shall establish a vessel security system, formulate a vessel security plan in accordance with the provisions of the competent transport department of the State Council, equip the vessel with security equipment as per the security plan, and conduct regular drills.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　中国籍船员和海上设施上的工作人员应当接受海上交通安全以及相应岗位的专业教育、培训。  
中国籍船员应当依照有关船员管理的法律、行政法规的规定向海事管理机构申请取得船员适任证书，并取得健康证明。  
外国籍船员在中国籍船舶上工作的，按照有关船员管理的法律、行政法规的规定执行。  
船员在船舶上工作，应当符合船员适任证书载明的船舶、航区、职务的范围。

**Article 13** Seafarers of Chinese nationality and staff on offshore installations shall receive professional education and training in maritime traffic safety and in their respective positions.  
Seafarers of Chinese nationality shall apply to the maritime safety administration for a certificate of competency and obtain a health certificate in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on seafarer management.  
Foreign seafarers working on vessels of Chinese nationality shall be dealt with according to the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on seafarer management.  
Seafarers working on a vessel shall fall within the scope of the vessel, navigation area, and position indicated in their certificate of competency.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　中国籍船舶的所有人、经营人或者管理人应当为其国际航行船舶向海事管理机构申请取得海事劳工证书。船舶取得海事劳工证书应当符合下列条件：  
（一）所有人、经营人或者管理人依法招用船员，与其签订劳动合同或者就业协议，并为船舶配备符合要求的船员；  
（二）所有人、经营人或者管理人已保障船员在船舶上的工作环境、职业健康保障和安全防护、工作和休息时间、工资报酬、生活条件、医疗条件、社会保险等符合国家有关规定；  
（三）所有人、经营人或者管理人已建立符合要求的船员投诉和处理机制；  
（四）所有人、经营人或者管理人已就船员遣返费用以及在船就业期间发生伤害、疾病或者死亡依法应当支付的费用提供相应的财务担保或者投保相应的保险。  
海事管理机构商人力资源社会保障行政部门，按照各自职责对申请人及其船舶是否符合前款规定条件进行审核。经审核符合规定条件的，海事管理机构应当自受理申请之日起十个工作日内颁发海事劳工证书；不符合规定条件的，海事管理机构应当告知申请人并说明理由。  
海事劳工证书颁发及监督检查的具体办法由国务院交通运输主管部门会同国务院人力资源社会保障行政部门制定并公布。

**Article 14** The owner, operator, or manager of a vessel of Chinese nationality shall apply to the maritime safety administration for a maritime labor certificate for its vessel engaged in international voyages. A vessel shall meet the following conditions to obtain a maritime labor certificate:  
(1) The owner, operator, or manager has recruited seafarers according to law, concluded labor contracts or employment agreements with them, and equipped the vessel with seafarers meeting the requirements;  
(2) The owner, operator, or manager has ensured that the working environment, occupational health protection and safety, working and rest hours, wages and remuneration, living conditions, medical conditions, social insurance, etc., of seafarers on the vessel comply with relevant national provisions;  
(3) The owner, operator, or manager has established a compliant seafarer complaint and handling mechanism;  
(4) The owner, operator, or manager has provided corresponding financial guarantees or purchased corresponding insurance for seafarer repatriation costs and costs payable according to law for injuries, illnesses, or deaths occurring during employment on the vessel.  
The maritime safety administration shall, in consultation with the administrative department of human resources and social security, examine whether the applicant and its vessel meet the conditions specified in the preceding paragraph according to their respective functions and duties. If, after examination, the conditions are met, the maritime safety administration shall issue the maritime labor certificate within ten working days from the date of acceptance of the application; if the conditions are not met, the maritime safety administration shall inform the applicant and provide reasons.  
The specific measures for the issuance and supervision and inspection of maritime labor certificates shall be formulated and published by the competent transport department of the State Council jointly with the administrative department of human resources and social security of the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　海事管理机构依照有关船员管理的法律、行政法规的规定，对单位从事海船船员培训业务进行管理。

**Article 15** The maritime safety administration shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on seafarer management, manage the training business for seafarers of seagoing vessels conducted by units.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院交通运输主管部门和其他有关部门、有关县级以上地方人民政府应当建立健全船员境外突发事件预警和应急处置机制，制定船员境外突发事件应急预案。  
船员境外突发事件应急处置由船员派出单位所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责，船员户籍所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府予以配合。  
中华人民共和国驻外国使馆、领馆和相关海事管理机构应当协助处置船员境外突发事件。

**Article 16** The competent transport department of the State Council and other relevant departments, and relevant local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve early warning and emergency response mechanisms for overseas emergencies involving seafarers, and formulate emergency plans for overseas emergencies involving seafarers.  
The emergency response to overseas emergencies involving seafarers shall be the responsibility of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the unit dispatching the seafarers is located, and the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the seafarer's registered residence is located shall provide cooperation.  
The embassies and consulates of the People's Republic of China in foreign countries and relevant maritime safety administrations shall assist in handling overseas emergencies involving seafarers.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本章第九条至第十二条、第十四条规定适用的船舶范围由有关法律、行政法规具体规定，或者由国务院交通运输主管部门拟定并报国务院批准后公布。

**Article 17** The scope of vessels to which the provisions of Articles 9 to 12 and Article 14 of this Chapter apply shall be specifically prescribed by relevant laws and administrative regulations, or shall be formulated by the competent transport department of the State Council and published after approval by the State Council.

### 第三章　海上交通条件和航行保障

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院交通运输主管部门统筹规划和管理海上交通资源，促进海上交通资源的合理开发和有效利用。  
海上交通资源规划应当符合国土空间规划。

**Article 18** The competent transport department of the State Council shall coordinate the planning and management of maritime traffic resources, and promote the rational development and effective utilization of maritime traffic resources.  
The planning of maritime traffic resources shall conform to the territorial space planning.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　海事管理机构根据海域的自然状况、海上交通状况以及海上交通安全管理的需要，划定、调整并及时公布船舶定线区、船舶报告区、交通管制区、禁航区、安全作业区和港外锚地等海上交通功能区域。  
海事管理机构划定或者调整船舶定线区、港外锚地以及对其他海洋功能区域或者用海活动造成影响的安全作业区，应当征求渔业渔政、生态环境、自然资源等有关部门的意见。为了军事需要划定、调整禁航区的，由负责划定、调整禁航区的军事机关作出决定，海事管理机构予以公布。

**Article 19** The maritime safety administration shall, based on the natural conditions of the sea areas, maritime traffic conditions, and the needs of maritime traffic safety management, delimit, adjust, and promptly publish maritime traffic functional areas such as ship routing areas, ship reporting areas, traffic control areas, prohibited navigation areas, safety operation areas, and anchorages outside ports.  
When delimiting or adjusting ship routing areas, anchorages outside ports, or safety operation areas that may affect other marine functional areas or activities using the sea, the maritime safety administration shall seek opinions from the relevant departments of fishery administration, ecological environment, natural resources, etc. Where prohibited navigation areas are delimited or adjusted for military needs, the military authority responsible for delimiting or adjusting such areas shall make the decision, and the maritime safety administration shall publish it.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　建设海洋工程、海岸工程影响海上交通安全的，应当根据情况配备防止船舶碰撞的设施、设备并设置专用航标。

**Article 20** Where the construction of marine engineering or coastal engineering projects affects maritime traffic safety, facilities and equipment for preventing collisions between vessels shall be provided as appropriate, and special aids to navigation shall be established.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家建立完善船舶定位、导航、授时、通信和远程监测等海上交通支持服务系统，为船舶、海上设施提供信息服务。

**Article 21** The State shall establish and improve maritime traffic support and service systems such as vessel positioning, navigation, timing, communication, and remote monitoring, to provide information services to vessels and offshore installations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　任何单位、个人不得损坏海上交通支持服务系统或者妨碍其工作效能。建设建筑物、构筑物，使用设施设备可能影响海上交通支持服务系统正常使用的，建设单位、所有人或者使用人应当与相关海上交通支持服务系统的管理单位协商，作出妥善安排。

**Article 22** No unit or individual may damage a maritime traffic support and service system or impair its working efficiency. Where the construction of buildings or structures or the use of facilities and equipment may affect the normal use of a maritime traffic support and service system, the construction unit, owner, or user shall consult with the management unit of the relevant maritime traffic support and service system and make appropriate arrangements.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院交通运输主管部门应当采取必要的措施，保障海上交通安全无线电通信设施的合理布局和有效覆盖，规划本系统（行业）海上无线电台（站）的建设布局和台址，核发船舶制式无线电台执照及电台识别码。  
国务院交通运输主管部门组织本系统（行业）的海上无线电监测系统建设并对其无线电信号实施监测，会同国家无线电管理机构维护海上无线电波秩序。

**Article 23** The competent transport department of the State Council shall take necessary measures to ensure the reasonable layout and effective coverage of radio communication facilities for maritime traffic safety, plan the construction layout and station sites of maritime radio stations within its system (industry), and issue ship station licenses and station identity codes for standard ship radio stations.  
The competent transport department of the State Council shall organize the construction of a maritime radio monitoring system within its system (industry) and monitor its radio signals, and shall maintain the order of maritime radio waves jointly with the national radio regulatory authority.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　船舶在中华人民共和国管辖海域内通信需要使用岸基无线电台（站）转接的，应当通过依法设置的境内海岸无线电台（站）或者卫星关口站进行转接。  
承担无线电通信任务的船员和岸基无线电台（站）的工作人员应当遵守海上无线电通信规则，保持海上交通安全通信频道的值守和畅通，不得使用海上交通安全通信频率交流与海上交通安全无关的内容。  
任何单位、个人不得违反国家有关规定使用无线电台识别码，影响海上搜救的身份识别。

**Article 24** Where a vessel needs to use shore-based radio stations for relay in communications within the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, it shall relay through a coastal radio station or satellite gateway station legally established within the territory.  
Seafarers undertaking radio communication tasks and staff of shore-based radio stations shall comply with the maritime radio communication rules, maintain listening watch and ensure the smoothness of the maritime traffic safety communication channels, and shall not use maritime traffic safety communication frequencies to exchange content unrelated to maritime traffic safety.  
No unit or individual may, in violation of relevant state provisions, use a radio station identity code to affect the identification of maritime search and rescue.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　天文、气象、海洋等有关单位应当及时预报、播发和提供航海天文、世界时、海洋气象、海浪、海流、潮汐、冰情等信息。

**Article 25** Astronomy, meteorology, marine, and other relevant units shall forecast, broadcast, and provide information on nautical astronomy, universal time, marine meteorology, waves, currents, tides, ice conditions, etc., in a timely manner.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国务院交通运输主管部门统一布局、建设和管理公用航标。海洋工程、海岸工程的建设单位、所有人或者经营人需要设置、撤除专用航标，移动专用航标位置或者改变航标灯光、功率等的，应当报经海事管理机构同意。需要设置临时航标的，应当符合海事管理机构确定的航标设置点。  
自然资源主管部门依法保障航标设施和装置的用地、用海、用岛，并依法为其办理有关手续。  
航标的建设、维护、保养应当符合有关强制性标准和技术规范的要求。航标维护单位和专用航标的所有人应当对航标进行巡查和维护保养，保证航标处于良好适用状态。航标发生位移、损坏、灭失的，航标维护单位或者专用航标的所有人应当及时予以恢复。

**Article 26** The competent transport department of the State Council shall uniformly plan, construct, and manage public aids to navigation. Where the construction unit, owner, or operator of marine or coastal engineering projects needs to establish or remove special aids to navigation, move the location of special aids to navigation, or change the light, power, etc., of aids to navigation, it shall report to the maritime safety administration for approval. Where temporary aids to navigation need to be established, they shall comply with the aids-to-navigation placement points determined by the maritime safety administration.  
The competent department of natural resources shall ensure the use of land, sea areas, and islands for aids-to-navigation facilities and devices according to law, and handle relevant procedures according to law.  
The construction, maintenance, and upkeep of aids to navigation shall comply with the requirements of relevant mandatory standards and technical specifications. The maintenance unit of aids to navigation and the owner of special aids to navigation shall inspect and maintain the aids to navigation to ensure they are in good working condition. If an aid to navigation is displaced, damaged, or lost, the maintenance unit or the owner of the special aid to navigation shall restore it promptly.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　任何单位、个人发现下列情形之一的，应当立即向海事管理机构报告；涉及航道管理机构职责或者专用航标的，海事管理机构应当及时通报航道管理机构或者专用航标的所有人：  
（一）助航标志或者导航设施位移、损坏、灭失；  
（二）有妨碍海上交通安全的沉没物、漂浮物、搁浅物或者其他碍航物；  
（三）其他妨碍海上交通安全的异常情况。

**Article 27** Any unit or individual that discovers any of the following circumstances shall immediately report to the maritime safety administration; if the matter involves the functions and duties of the fairway administration or special aids to navigation, the maritime safety administration shall promptly notify the fairway administration or the owner of the special aids to navigation:  
(1) A navigational aid or navigation facility is displaced, damaged, or lost;  
(2) There are sunken objects, floating objects, grounded objects, or other obstructions to navigation that impede maritime traffic safety;  
(3) Other abnormal circumstances that impede maritime traffic safety.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　海事管理机构应当依据海上交通安全管理的需要，就具有紧迫性、危险性的情况发布航行警告，就其他影响海上交通安全的情况发布航行通告。  
海事管理机构应当将航行警告、航行通告，以及船舶定线区的划定、调整情况通报海军航海保证部门，并及时提供有关资料。

**Article 28** The maritime safety administration shall, based on the needs of maritime traffic safety management, issue navigational warnings for urgent and dangerous situations, and issue navigational notices for other situations affecting maritime traffic safety.  
The maritime safety administration shall notify the naval navigation assurance department of the navigational warnings, navigational notices, and the delimitation and adjustment of ship routing areas, and provide relevant materials in a timely manner.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　海事管理机构应当及时向船舶、海上设施播发海上交通安全信息。  
船舶、海上设施在定线区、交通管制区或者通航船舶密集的区域航行、停泊、作业时，海事管理机构应当根据其请求提供相应的安全信息服务。

**Article 29** The maritime safety administration shall promptly broadcast maritime traffic safety information to vessels and offshore installations.  
When vessels or offshore installations navigate, moor, or operate in routing areas, traffic control areas, or areas with dense navigation traffic, the maritime safety administration shall provide corresponding safety information services upon request.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　下列船舶在国务院交通运输主管部门划定的引航区内航行、停泊或者移泊的，应当向引航机构申请引航：  
（一）外国籍船舶，但国务院交通运输主管部门经报国务院批准后规定可以免除的除外；  
（二）核动力船舶、载运放射性物质的船舶、超大型油轮；  
（三）可能危及港口安全的散装液化气船、散装危险化学品船；  
（四）长、宽、高接近相应航道通航条件限值的船舶。  
前款第三项、第四项船舶的具体标准，由有关海事管理机构根据港口实际情况制定并公布。  
船舶自愿申请引航的，引航机构应当提供引航服务。

**Article 30** The following vessels, when navigating, mooring, or shifting berths within the pilotage areas delimited by the competent transport department of the State Council, shall apply to a pilotage organization for pilotage:  
(1) Vessels of foreign nationality, except those that may be exempted as prescribed by the competent transport department of the State Council upon approval by the State Council;  
(2) Nuclear-powered vessels, vessels carrying radioactive substances, and very large crude carriers;  
(3) Bulk liquefied gas carriers and bulk dangerous chemical carriers that may endanger port safety;  
(4) Vessels whose length, breadth, or height is close to the limits of the navigable conditions of the relevant fairway.  
The specific standards for the vessels referred to in items (3) and (4) of the preceding paragraph shall be formulated and published by the relevant maritime safety administration according to the actual port conditions.  
If a vessel voluntarily applies for pilotage, the pilotage organization shall provide pilotage services.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　引航机构应当及时派遣具有相应能力、经验的引航员为船舶提供引航服务。  
引航员应当根据引航机构的指派，在规定的水域登离被引领船舶，安全谨慎地执行船舶引航任务。被引领船舶应当配备符合规定的登离装置，并保障引航员在登离船舶及在船上引航期间的安全。  
引航员引领船舶时，不解除船长指挥和管理船舶的责任。

**Article 31** A pilotage organization shall promptly dispatch a pilot with appropriate competence and experience to provide pilotage services for a vessel.  
A pilot shall, according to the assignment of the pilotage organization, embark on and disembark from the vessel being piloted in the specified waters, and perform the pilotage task safely and with caution. The vessel being piloted shall be equipped with boarding and disembarkation facilities that comply with the provisions, and shall ensure the safety of the pilot during embarkation, disembarkation, and while piloting on board.  
When a pilot is piloting a vessel, the master's responsibility for commanding and managing the vessel shall not be relieved.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院交通运输主管部门根据船舶、海上设施和港口面临的保安威胁情形，确定并及时发布保安等级。船舶、海上设施和港口应当根据保安等级采取相应的保安措施。

**Article 32** The competent transport department of the State Council shall determine and promptly publish security levels based on the security threat situations faced by vessels, offshore installations, and ports. Vessels, offshore installations, and ports shall take corresponding security measures according to the security levels.

### 第四章　航行、停泊、作业

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　船舶航行、停泊、作业，应当持有有效的船舶国籍证书及其他法定证书、文书，配备依照有关规定出版的航海图书资料，悬挂相关国家、地区或者组织的旗帜，标明船名、船舶识别号、船籍港、载重线标志。  
船舶应当满足最低安全配员要求，配备持有合格有效证书的船员。  
海上设施停泊、作业，应当持有法定证书、文书，并按规定配备掌握避碰、信号、通信、消防、救生等专业技能的人员。

**Article 33** When navigating, mooring, or operating, a vessel shall hold valid certificates of vessel nationality and other statutory certificates and documents, be equipped with nautical charts and publications published in accordance with relevant provisions, fly the flags of relevant countries, regions, or organizations, and display the vessel's name, vessel identification number, port of registry, and load line marks.  
A vessel shall meet the minimum safe manning requirements and be equipped with seafarers holding valid and appropriate certificates.  
When mooring or operating, an offshore installation shall hold statutory certificates and documents, and be staffed with personnel possessing professional skills such as collision avoidance, signals, communication, firefighting, and lifesaving as required.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　船长应当在船舶开航前检查并在开航时确认船员适任、船舶适航、货物适载，并了解气象和海况信息以及海事管理机构发布的航行通告、航行警告及其他警示信息，落实相应的应急措施，不得冒险开航。  
船舶所有人、经营人或者管理人不得指使、强令船员违章冒险操作、作业。

**Article 34** The master shall, before the vessel's departure, inspect and, at the time of departure, confirm that the seafarers are competent, the vessel is seaworthy, and the cargo is stowed properly, and shall ascertain the meteorological and sea condition information as well as navigational notices, navigational warnings, and other warning information issued by the maritime safety administration, and implement corresponding emergency measures; the master shall not risk departure.  
The owner, operator, or manager of the vessel shall not direct or compel seafarers to operate or work in a risky manner in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　船舶应当在其船舶检验证书载明的航区内航行、停泊、作业。  
船舶航行、停泊、作业时，应当遵守相关航行规则，按照有关规定显示信号、悬挂标志，保持足够的富余水深。

**Article 35** A vessel shall navigate, moor, and operate within the navigation area indicated in its vessel inspection certificate.  
When navigating, mooring, or operating, a vessel shall comply with the relevant navigation rules, display signals and exhibit marks in accordance with relevant provisions, and maintain sufficient under-keel clearance.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　船舶在航行中应当按照有关规定开启船舶的自动识别、航行数据记录、远程识别和跟踪、通信等与航行安全、保安、防治污染相关的装置，并持续进行显示和记录。  
任何单位、个人不得拆封、拆解、初始化、再设置航行数据记录装置或者读取其记录的信息，但法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 36** When underway, a vessel shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, activate devices related to navigation safety, security, and pollution prevention, such as automatic identification system, voyage data recorder, long-range identification and tracking, and communication, and keep them continuously displaying and recording.  
No unit or individual may break the seal, disassemble, initialize, or reset the voyage data recording device, or read the information recorded by it, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　船舶应当配备航海日志、轮机日志、无线电记录簿等航行记录，按照有关规定全面、真实、及时记录涉及海上交通安全的船舶操作以及船舶航行、停泊、作业中的重要事件，并妥善保管相关记录簿。

**Article 37** A vessel shall be equipped with navigation records such as deck logbook, engine logbook, and radio logbook, and shall comprehensively, truthfully, and promptly record vessel operations related to maritime traffic safety and important events during navigation, mooring, and operations in accordance with relevant provisions, and shall properly keep the relevant logbooks.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　船长负责管理和指挥船舶。在保障海上生命安全、船舶保安和防治船舶污染方面，船长有权独立作出决定。  
船长应当采取必要的措施，保护船舶、在船人员、船舶航行文件、货物以及其他财产的安全。船长在其职权范围内发布的命令，船员、乘客及其他在船人员应当执行。

**Article 38** The master shall be responsible for the management and command of the vessel. The master shall have the right to make independent decisions regarding the safeguarding of maritime life safety, vessel security, and prevention of pollution from vessels.  
The master shall take necessary measures to protect the safety of the vessel, persons on board, vessel navigation documents, cargo, and other property. Orders issued by the master within his functions and powers shall be executed by seafarers, passengers, and other persons on board.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　为了保障船舶和在船人员的安全，船长有权在职责范围内对涉嫌在船上进行违法犯罪活动的人员采取禁闭或者其他必要的限制措施，并防止其隐匿、毁灭、伪造证据。  
船长采取前款措施，应当制作案情报告书，由其和两名以上在船人员签字。中国籍船舶抵达我国港口后，应当及时将相关人员移送有关主管部门。

**Article 39** For the purpose of ensuring the safety of the vessel and persons on board, the master shall have the right, within his functions and duties, to take confinement or other necessary restrictive measures against persons suspected of engaging in illegal or criminal activities on board, and to prevent them from concealing, destroying, or fabricating evidence.  
If the master takes the measures in the preceding paragraph, he shall prepare a case report signed by himself and two or more persons on board. After a vessel of Chinese nationality arrives at a port of China, the relevant persons shall be promptly transferred to the relevant competent authority.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　发现在船人员患有或者疑似患有严重威胁他人健康的传染病的，船长应当立即启动相应的应急预案，在职责范围内对相关人员采取必要的隔离措施，并及时报告有关主管部门。

**Article 40** If it is discovered that a person on board has or is suspected of having an infectious disease that seriously threatens the health of others, the master shall immediately activate the corresponding emergency plan, take necessary isolation measures against the relevant person within his functions and duties, and promptly report to the relevant competent authority.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　船长在航行中死亡或者因故不能履行职责的，应当由驾驶员中职务最高的人代理船长职务；船舶在下一个港口开航前，其所有人、经营人或者管理人应当指派新船长接任。

**Article 41** If the master dies during navigation or is unable to perform his duties for any reason, the officer with the highest rank among the deck officers shall act as master; before the vessel departs from the next port, its owner, operator, or manager shall assign a new master to take over.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　船员应当按照有关航行、值班的规章制度和操作规程以及船长的指令操纵、管理船舶，保持安全值班，不得擅离职守。船员履行在船值班职责前和值班期间，不得摄入可能影响安全值班的食品、药品或者其他物品。

**Article 42** Seafarers shall operate and manage the vessel in accordance with the relevant regulations and operating procedures on navigation and watchkeeping, and the master's orders, and shall maintain a safe watch and not leave their posts without authorization. Before and during the performance of watchkeeping duties on board, seafarers shall not consume any food, medicine, or other articles that may affect safe watchkeeping.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　船舶进出港口、锚地或者通过桥区水域、海峡、狭水道、重要渔业水域、通航船舶密集的区域、船舶定线区、交通管制区，应当加强瞭望、保持安全航速，并遵守前述区域的特殊航行规则。  
前款所称重要渔业水域由国务院渔业渔政主管部门征求国务院交通运输主管部门意见后划定并公布。  
船舶穿越航道不得妨碍航道内船舶的正常航行，不得抢越他船船艏。超过桥梁通航尺度的船舶禁止进入桥区水域。

**Article 43** When entering or leaving a port or anchorage, or passing through bridge waters, straits, narrow channels, important fishing waters, areas with dense navigation traffic, ship routing areas, or traffic control areas, a vessel shall maintain a proper lookout, proceed at a safe speed, and comply with the special navigation rules of the aforementioned areas.  
The important fishing waters referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be delimited and published by the competent fishery administration department of the State Council after soliciting the opinions of the competent transport department of the State Council.  
A vessel crossing a fairway shall not impede the normal navigation of vessels within the fairway, and shall not cross ahead of another vessel's bow. Vessels exceeding the navigable dimensions of a bridge are prohibited from entering the bridge waters.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　船舶不得违反规定进入或者穿越禁航区。  
船舶进出船舶报告区，应当向海事管理机构报告船位和动态信息。  
在安全作业区、港外锚地范围内，禁止从事养殖、种植、捕捞以及其他影响海上交通安全的作业或者活动。

**Article 44** A vessel shall not enter or cross a prohibited navigation area in violation of regulations.  
When entering or leaving a ship reporting area, a vessel shall report its position and movement information to the maritime safety administration.  
Within the scope of safety operation areas and anchorages outside ports, aquaculture, planting, fishing, and other operations or activities affecting maritime traffic safety are prohibited.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　船舶载运或者拖带超长、超高、超宽、半潜的船舶、海上设施或者其他物体航行，应当采取拖拽部位加强、护航等特殊的安全保障措施，在开航前向海事管理机构报告航行计划，并按有关规定显示信号、悬挂标志；拖带移动式平台、浮船坞等大型海上设施的，还应当依法交验船舶检验机构出具的拖航检验证书。

**Article 45** Where a vessel carries or tows vessels, offshore installations, or other objects that are over-length, over-height, over-width, or semi-submersible, it shall adopt special safety assurance measures such as reinforcing towing points and escorting, report the voyage plan to the maritime safety administration before departure, and display signals and exhibit marks in accordance with relevant provisions; if towing large offshore installations such as mobile platforms or floating docks, it shall also submit the towing survey certificate issued by the vessel inspection authority according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国际航行船舶进出口岸，应当依法向海事管理机构申请许可并接受海事管理机构及其他口岸查验机构的监督检查。海事管理机构应当自受理申请之日起五个工作日内作出许可或者不予许可的决定。  
外国籍船舶临时进入非对外开放水域，应当依照国务院关于船舶进出口岸的规定取得许可。  
国内航行船舶进出港口、港外装卸站，应当向海事管理机构报告船舶的航次计划、适航状态、船员配备和客货载运等情况。

**Article 46** Vessels engaged in international voyages entering or leaving a port shall apply to the maritime safety administration for permission according to law and accept supervision and inspection by the maritime safety administration and other port inspection authorities. The maritime safety administration shall make a decision whether to grant permission within five working days from the date of acceptance of the application.  
Where a vessel of foreign nationality temporarily enters a water area not open to foreign vessels, it shall obtain permission in accordance with the provisions of the State Council on vessels entering and leaving ports.  
Vessels engaged in domestic voyages entering or leaving a port or an offshore loading and unloading station shall report to the maritime safety administration the voyage plan, seaworthiness status, manning, and passenger and cargo carriage information.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　船舶应当在符合安全条件的码头、泊位、装卸站、锚地、安全作业区停泊。船舶停泊不得危及其他船舶、海上设施的安全。  
船舶进出港口、港外装卸站，应当符合靠泊条件和关于潮汐、气象、海况等航行条件的要求。  
超长、超高、超宽的船舶或者操纵能力受到限制的船舶进出港口、港外装卸站可能影响海上交通安全的，海事管理机构应当对船舶进出港安全条件进行核查，并可以要求船舶采取加配拖轮、乘潮进港等相应的安全措施。

**Article 47** Vessels shall moor at berths, berths, loading and unloading stations, anchorages, or safety operation areas that meet safety conditions. Mooring shall not endanger the safety of other vessels or offshore installations.  
Vessels entering or leaving a port or an offshore loading and unloading station shall meet the berthing conditions and the requirements for navigation conditions such as tides, weather, and sea conditions.  
If an over-length, over-height, or over-width vessel, or a vessel with restricted maneuvering capability, may affect maritime traffic safety when entering or leaving a port or an offshore loading and unloading station, the maritime safety administration shall verify the vessel's entry and exit conditions and may require the vessel to take corresponding safety measures such as additional tugs or entering port on the tide.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　在中华人民共和国管辖海域内进行施工作业，应当经海事管理机构许可，并核定相应安全作业区。取得海上施工作业许可，应当符合下列条件：  
（一）施工作业的单位、人员、船舶、设施符合安全航行、停泊、作业的要求；  
（二）有施工作业方案；  
（三）有符合海上交通安全和防治船舶污染海洋环境要求的保障措施、应急预案和责任制度。  
从事施工作业的船舶应当在核定的安全作业区内作业，并落实海上交通安全管理措施。其他无关船舶、海上设施不得进入安全作业区。  
在港口水域内进行采掘、爆破等可能危及港口安全的作业，适用港口管理的法律规定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/交通运输/中华人民共和国渔港水域交通安全管理条例.md#art-1" title="被《中华人民共和国渔港水域交通安全管理条例》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 48** Construction operations within the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China shall be subject to permission by the maritime safety administration, and the corresponding safety operation area shall be determined. To obtain a maritime construction operation permit, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) The units, personnel, vessels, and facilities engaged in the construction operations meet the requirements for safe navigation, mooring, and operations;  
(2) There is a construction operation plan;  
(3) There are safeguard measures, emergency plans, and responsibility systems meeting the requirements for maritime traffic safety and prevention of pollution of the marine environment from vessels.  
Vessels engaged in construction operations shall operate within the determined safety operation area and implement maritime traffic safety management measures. Other unrelated vessels and offshore installations shall not enter the safety operation area.  
For operations such as excavation or blasting that may endanger port safety within port waters, the legal provisions on port management shall apply.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　从事体育、娱乐、演练、试航、科学观测等水上水下活动，应当遵守海上交通安全管理规定；可能影响海上交通安全的，应当提前十个工作日将活动涉及的海域范围报告海事管理机构。

**Article 49** Activities on or under the water such as sports, recreation, drills, trial voyages, and scientific observation shall comply with the provisions on maritime traffic safety management; if they may affect maritime traffic safety, the scope of the sea areas involved in the activities shall be reported to the maritime safety administration ten working days in advance.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　海上施工作业或者水上水下活动结束后，有关单位、个人应当及时消除可能妨碍海上交通安全的隐患。

**Article 50** After the conclusion of maritime construction operations or activities on or under the water, the relevant units and individuals shall promptly eliminate any potential hazards that may impede maritime traffic safety.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　碍航物的所有人、经营人或者管理人应当按照有关强制性标准和技术规范的要求及时设置警示标志，向海事管理机构报告碍航物的名称、形状、尺寸、位置和深度，并在海事管理机构限定的期限内打捞清除。碍航物的所有人放弃所有权的，不免除其打捞清除义务。  
不能确定碍航物的所有人、经营人或者管理人的，海事管理机构应当组织设置标志、打捞或者采取相应措施，发生的费用纳入部门预算。

**Article 51** The owner, operator or manager of an obstruction to navigation shall, in accordance with the requirements of relevant compulsory standards and technical specifications, promptly set up warning signs, report to the maritime administration the name, shape, dimensions, position and depth of the obstruction, and salvage and remove it within the time limit prescribed by the maritime administration. Where the owner of the obstruction abandons the ownership, the obligation to salvage and remove shall not be exempted.  
If the owner, operator or manager of the obstruction cannot be determined, the maritime administration shall organize the setting up of signs, salvage or take corresponding measures, and the expenses incurred shall be included in the departmental budget.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　有下列情形之一，对海上交通安全有较大影响的，海事管理机构应当根据具体情况采取停航、限速或者划定交通管制区等相应交通管制措施并向社会公告：  
（一）天气、海况恶劣；  
（二）发生影响航行的海上险情或者海上交通事故；  
（三）进行军事训练、演习或者其他相关活动；  
（四）开展大型水上水下活动；  
（五）特定海域通航密度接近饱和；  
（六）其他对海上交通安全有较大影响的情形。

**Article 52** In any of the following circumstances that has a significant impact on maritime traffic safety, the maritime administration shall, according to the specific circumstances, take corresponding traffic control measures such as suspension of navigation, speed limitation or designation of a traffic control zone, and announce them to the public:  
(1) severe weather or sea conditions;  
(2) occurrence of a maritime peril or a maritime traffic accident affecting navigation;  
(3) military training, exercises or other related activities;  
(4) large-scale underwater or above-water activities;  
(5) the navigation density in a specific sea area is close to saturation;  
(6) other circumstances that have a significant impact on maritime traffic safety.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国务院交通运输主管部门为维护海上交通安全、保护海洋环境，可以会同有关主管部门采取必要措施，防止和制止外国籍船舶在领海的非无害通过。

**Article 53** The competent transport department of the State Council may, for the purpose of maintaining maritime traffic safety and protecting the marine environment, take necessary measures jointly with relevant competent departments to prevent and stop the non-innocent passage of foreign vessels in the territorial sea.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　下列外国籍船舶进出中华人民共和国领海，应当向海事管理机构报告：  
（一）潜水器；  
（二）核动力船舶；  
（三）载运放射性物质或者其他有毒有害物质的船舶；  
（四）法律、行政法规或者国务院规定的可能危及中华人民共和国海上交通安全的其他船舶。  
前款规定的船舶通过中华人民共和国领海，应当持有有关证书，采取符合中华人民共和国法律、行政法规和规章规定的特别预防措施，并接受海事管理机构的指令和监督。

**Article 54** The following foreign vessels shall report to the maritime administration when entering or leaving the territorial sea of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) submersibles;  
(2) nuclear-powered vessels;  
(3) vessels carrying radioactive substances or other toxic and hazardous substances;  
(4) other vessels that may endanger the maritime traffic safety of the People's Republic of China as specified by laws, administrative regulations or the State Council.  
When passing through the territorial sea of the People's Republic of China, the vessels specified in the preceding paragraph shall hold relevant certificates, take special preventive measures in compliance with the provisions of the laws, administrative regulations and rules of the People's Republic of China, and accept the instructions and supervision of the maritime administration.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　除依照本法规定获得进入口岸许可外，外国籍船舶不得进入中华人民共和国内水；但是，因人员病急、机件故障、遇难、避风等紧急情况未及获得许可的可以进入。  
外国籍船舶因前款规定的紧急情况进入中华人民共和国内水的，应当在进入的同时向海事管理机构紧急报告，接受海事管理机构的指令和监督。海事管理机构应当及时通报管辖海域的海警机构、就近的出入境边防检查机关和当地公安机关、海关等其他主管部门。

**Article 55** Unless having obtained a port entry permit in accordance with the provisions of this Law, a foreign vessel shall not enter the internal waters of the People's Republic of China; provided that, in emergency situations such as medical emergencies of personnel, mechanical failure, distress, or seeking shelter, which prevent obtaining a permit in time, it may enter.  
Where a foreign vessel enters the internal waters of the People's Republic of China due to the emergency situations specified in the preceding paragraph, it shall, at the same time of entry, make an urgent report to the maritime administration, and accept the instructions and supervision of the maritime administration. The maritime administration shall promptly notify the coast guard agency in the jurisdictional sea area, the nearest exit-entry border inspection authority, and other competent authorities such as the local public security organ and the customs.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　中华人民共和国军用船舶执行军事任务、公务船舶执行公务，遇有紧急情况，在保证海上交通安全的前提下，可以不受航行、停泊、作业有关规则的限制。

**Article 56** Vessels of the People's Republic of China for military use performing military missions and government vessels performing official duties may, in case of emergency, be exempt from the restrictions of the relevant rules on navigation, berthing and operations, provided that maritime traffic safety is ensured.

### 第五章　海上客货运输安全

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　除进行抢险或者生命救助外，客船应当按照船舶检验证书核定的载客定额载运乘客，货船载运货物应当符合船舶检验证书核定的载重线和载货种类，不得载运乘客。

**Article 57** Except for emergency rescue or life-saving, a passenger vessel shall carry passengers according to the passenger quota verified in the ship inspection certificate, and a cargo vessel shall carry cargo in compliance with the load line and cargo types verified in the ship inspection certificate, and shall not carry passengers.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　客船载运乘客不得同时载运危险货物。  
乘客不得随身携带或者在行李中夹带法律、行政法规或者国务院交通运输主管部门规定的危险物品。

**Article 58** A passenger vessel carrying passengers shall not simultaneously carry dangerous goods.  
Passengers shall not carry on their person or conceal in their luggage dangerous articles specified by laws, administrative regulations or the competent transport department of the State Council.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　客船应当在显著位置向乘客明示安全须知，设置安全标志和警示，并向乘客介绍救生用具的使用方法以及在紧急情况下应当采取的应急措施。乘客应当遵守安全乘船要求。

**Article 59** A passenger vessel shall, in a conspicuous position, display safety instructions to passengers, set up safety signs and warnings, and introduce to passengers the methods of using life-saving appliances and the emergency measures to be taken in case of emergency. Passengers shall comply with the safety requirements for boarding.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　海上渡口所在地的县级以上地方人民政府应当建立健全渡口安全管理责任制，制定海上渡口的安全管理办法，监督、指导海上渡口经营者落实安全主体责任，维护渡运秩序，保障渡运安全。  
海上渡口的渡运线路由渡口所在地的县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门会同海事管理机构划定。渡船应当按照划定的线路安全渡运。  
遇有恶劣天气、海况，县级以上地方人民政府或者其指定的部门应当发布停止渡运的公告。

**Article 60** The local people's government at or above the county level where the maritime ferry is located shall establish and improve a responsibility system for the safety management of the ferry, formulate safety management measures for the maritime ferry, supervise and guide the ferry operator to implement the primary responsibility for safety, maintain ferry order, and ensure ferry safety.  
The ferry route of the maritime ferry shall be designated by the competent transport department of the local people's government at or above the county level where the ferry is located jointly with the maritime administration. The ferry vessel shall safely operate along the designated route.  
In the event of severe weather or sea conditions, the local people's government at or above the county level or the department designated by it shall issue an announcement to suspend ferry operations.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　船舶载运货物，应当按照有关法律、行政法规、规章以及强制性标准和技术规范的要求安全装卸、积载、隔离、系固和管理。

**Article 61** A vessel carrying cargo shall, in accordance with the requirements of relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules, and compulsory standards and technical specifications, safely load, stow, segregate, secure and manage the cargo.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　船舶载运危险货物，应当持有有效的危险货物适装证书，并根据危险货物的特性和应急措施的要求，编制危险货物应急处置预案，配备相应的消防、应急设备和器材。

**Article 62** A vessel carrying dangerous goods shall hold a valid dangerous goods transport certificate, and shall, based on the characteristics of the dangerous goods and the requirements of emergency measures, formulate an emergency response plan for dangerous goods, and be equipped with corresponding fire-fighting, emergency equipment and appliances.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　托运人托运危险货物，应当将其正式名称、危险性质以及应当采取的防护措施通知承运人，并按照有关法律、行政法规、规章以及强制性标准和技术规范的要求妥善包装，设置明显的危险品标志和标签。  
托运人不得在托运的普通货物中夹带危险货物或者将危险货物谎报为普通货物托运。  
托运人托运的货物为国际海上危险货物运输规则和国家危险货物品名表上未列明但具有危险特性的货物的，托运人还应当提交有关专业机构出具的表明该货物危险特性以及应当采取的防护措施等情况的文件。  
货物危险特性的判断标准由国家海事管理机构制定并公布。

**Article 63** A shipper consigning dangerous goods shall notify the carrier of their proper shipping name, dangerous nature, and the protective measures to be taken, and shall properly pack them in accordance with the requirements of relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules, and compulsory standards and technical specifications, and set up obvious dangerous goods marks and labels.  
A shipper shall not conceal dangerous goods in the ordinary goods consigned or falsely declare dangerous goods as ordinary goods for consignment.  
Where the goods consigned by the shipper are not listed in the International Maritime Dangerous Goods Code or the National Dangerous Goods List but have dangerous characteristics, the shipper shall also submit documents issued by relevant professional institutions indicating the dangerous characteristics of such goods and the protective measures to be taken.  
The criteria for determining the dangerous characteristics of goods shall be formulated and published by the national maritime administration.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　船舶载运危险货物进出港口，应当符合下列条件，经海事管理机构许可，并向海事管理机构报告进出港口和停留的时间等事项：  
（一）所载运的危险货物符合海上安全运输要求；  
（二）船舶的装载符合所持有的证书、文书的要求；  
（三）拟靠泊或者进行危险货物装卸作业的港口、码头、泊位具备有关法律、行政法规规定的危险货物作业经营资质。  
海事管理机构应当自收到申请之时起二十四小时内作出许可或者不予许可的决定。  
定船舶、定航线并且定货种的船舶可以申请办理一定期限内多次进出港口许可，期限不超过三十日。海事管理机构应当自收到申请之日起五个工作日内作出许可或者不予许可的决定。  
海事管理机构予以许可的，应当通报港口行政管理部门。

**Article 64** A vessel carrying dangerous goods entering or leaving a port shall meet the following conditions, obtain permission from the maritime administration, and report to the maritime administration the time of entering and leaving the port and the duration of stay, among other matters:  
(1) the dangerous goods carried meet the requirements for safe maritime transport;  
(2) the loading of the vessel complies with the requirements of the certificates and documents held;  
(3) the port, terminal or berth intended for berthing or for loading or unloading dangerous goods has the operational qualifications for dangerous goods operations as required by relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
The maritime administration shall, within twenty-four hours from the receipt of the application, make a decision to grant or deny the permit.  
A vessel operating on a fixed vessel, fixed route and fixed cargo type may apply for a permit for multiple entries and exits within a certain period, not exceeding thirty days. The maritime administration shall, within five working days from the date of receipt of the application, make a decision to grant or deny the permit.  
Where the maritime administration grants the permit, it shall notify the port administrative department.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　船舶、海上设施从事危险货物运输或者装卸、过驳作业，应当编制作业方案，遵守有关强制性标准和安全作业操作规程，采取必要的预防措施，防止发生安全事故。  
在港口水域外从事散装液体危险货物过驳作业的，还应当符合下列条件，经海事管理机构许可并核定安全作业区：  
（一）拟进行过驳作业的船舶或者海上设施符合海上交通安全与防治船舶污染海洋环境的要求；  
（二）拟过驳的货物符合安全过驳要求；  
（三）参加过驳作业的人员具备法律、行政法规规定的过驳作业能力；  
（四）拟作业水域及其底质、周边环境适宜开展过驳作业；  
（五）过驳作业对海洋资源以及附近的军事目标、重要民用目标不构成威胁；  
（六）有符合安全要求的过驳作业方案、安全保障措施和应急预案。  
对单航次作业的船舶，海事管理机构应当自收到申请之时起二十四小时内作出许可或者不予许可的决定；对在特定水域多航次作业的船舶，海事管理机构应当自收到申请之日起五个工作日内作出许可或者不予许可的决定。

**Article 65** Vessels and maritime installations engaged in the transport, loading, unloading or lightering of dangerous goods shall formulate an operation plan, comply with relevant compulsory standards and safe operation procedures, and take necessary preventive measures to prevent safety accidents.  
Where lightering operations of bulk liquid dangerous goods are carried out outside port waters, the following conditions shall also be met, and permission from the maritime administration and the designation of a safety operation zone shall be obtained:  
(1) the vessel or maritime installation to perform the lightering operation meets the requirements for maritime traffic safety and prevention of vessel-source marine environment pollution;  
(2) the goods to be lightered meet the requirements for safe lightering;  
(3) the personnel participating in the lightering operation have the capability for lightering operations as provided by laws and administrative regulations;  
(4) the waters to be used for the operation, their bottom quality and surrounding environment are suitable for carrying out lightering operations;  
(5) the lightering operation does not pose a threat to marine resources and nearby military targets or important civilian targets;  
(6) there is a lightering operation plan, safety guarantee measures and emergency plan that meet the safety requirements.  
For a vessel operating on a single voyage, the maritime administration shall, within twenty-four hours from the receipt of the application, make a decision to grant or deny the permit; for a vessel operating on multiple voyages in a specific water area, the maritime administration shall, within five working days from the date of receipt of the application, make a decision to grant or deny the permit.

### 第六章　海上搜寻救助

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　海上遇险人员依法享有获得生命救助的权利。生命救助优先于环境和财产救助。

**Article 66** Persons in distress at sea have the right to obtain life-saving according to law. Life-saving shall take precedence over environmental and property salvage.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　海上搜救工作应当坚持政府领导、统一指挥、属地为主、专群结合、就近快速的原则。

**Article 67** Maritime search and rescue work shall adhere to the principles of government leadership, unified command, territorial primacy, combination of professional and civilian efforts, and proximity and speed.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　国家建立海上搜救协调机制，统筹全国海上搜救应急反应工作，研究解决海上搜救工作中的重大问题，组织协调重大海上搜救应急行动。协调机制由国务院有关部门、单位和有关军事机关组成。  
中国海上搜救中心和有关地方人民政府设立的海上搜救中心或者指定的机构（以下统称海上搜救中心）负责海上搜救的组织、协调、指挥工作。

**Article 68** The State shall establish a maritime search and rescue coordination mechanism to coordinate the national maritime search and rescue emergency response efforts, study and resolve major issues in maritime search and rescue work, and organize and coordinate major maritime search and rescue emergency operations. The coordination mechanism shall be composed of relevant departments, units of the State Council and relevant military organs.  
The China Maritime Search and Rescue Center and the maritime search and rescue centers or designated institutions established by relevant local people's governments (hereinafter collectively referred to as maritime search and rescue centers) shall be responsible for the organization, coordination and command of maritime search and rescue.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　沿海县级以上地方人民政府应当安排必要的海上搜救资金，保障搜救工作的正常开展。

**Article 69** The local people's governments at or above the county level along the coast shall allocate necessary funds for maritime search and rescue to ensure the normal conduct of search and rescue work.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　海上搜救中心各成员单位应当在海上搜救中心统一组织、协调、指挥下，根据各自职责，承担海上搜救应急、抢险救灾、支持保障、善后处理等工作。

**Article 70** Each member unit of a maritime search and rescue center shall, under the unified organization, coordination and command of the maritime search and rescue center, undertake the work of maritime search and rescue emergency response, emergency rescue and disaster relief, support and guarantee, and post-incident handling, in accordance with their respective responsibilities.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　国家设立专业海上搜救队伍，加强海上搜救力量建设。专业海上搜救队伍应当配备专业搜救装备，建立定期演练和日常培训制度，提升搜救水平。  
国家鼓励社会力量建立海上搜救队伍，参与海上搜救行动。

**Article 71** The State shall establish professional maritime search and rescue teams to strengthen the construction of maritime search and rescue capabilities. Professional maritime search and rescue teams shall be equipped with professional search and rescue equipment, and establish a system of regular drills and routine training to improve the level of search and rescue.  
The State encourages social forces to establish maritime search and rescue teams and participate in maritime search and rescue operations.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　船舶、海上设施、航空器及人员在海上遇险的，应当立即报告海上搜救中心，不得瞒报、谎报海上险情。  
船舶、海上设施、航空器及人员误发遇险报警信号的，除立即向海上搜救中心报告外，还应当采取必要措施消除影响。  
其他任何单位、个人发现或者获悉海上险情的，应当立即报告海上搜救中心。

**Article 72** Vessels, maritime installations, aircraft and persons in distress at sea shall immediately report to the maritime search and rescue center, and shall not conceal or falsely report a maritime peril.  
Where vessels, maritime installations, aircraft or persons send out a distress alert signal by mistake, they shall, in addition to immediately reporting to the maritime search and rescue center, take necessary measures to eliminate the effects.  
Any other unit or individual that discovers or learns of a maritime peril shall immediately report to the maritime search and rescue center.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　发生碰撞事故的船舶、海上设施，应当互通名称、国籍和登记港，在不严重危及自身安全的情况下尽力救助对方人员，不得擅自离开事故现场水域或者逃逸。

**Article 73** Vessels and maritime installations involved in a collision shall exchange names, nationalities and ports of registry, do their best to rescue persons from the other side without seriously endangering their own safety, and shall not leave the waters of the accident scene without authorization or flee.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　遇险的船舶、海上设施及其所有人、经营人或者管理人应当采取有效措施防止、减少生命财产损失和海洋环境污染。  
船舶遇险时，乘客应当服从船长指挥，配合采取相关应急措施。乘客有权获知必要的险情信息。  
船长决定弃船时，应当组织乘客、船员依次离船，并尽力抢救法定航行资料。船长应当最后离船。

**Article 74** A vessel or maritime installation in distress and its owner, operator or manager shall take effective measures to prevent and reduce loss of life and property and marine environmental pollution.  
When a vessel is in distress, passengers shall obey the orders of the master and cooperate in taking relevant emergency measures. Passengers have the right to be informed of necessary information about the peril.  
When the master decides to abandon the vessel, the master shall organize the passengers and crew to leave the vessel in proper order, and make every effort to salvage the statutory navigation documents. The master shall be the last to leave the vessel.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　船舶、海上设施、航空器收到求救信号或者发现有人遭遇生命危险的，在不严重危及自身安全的情况下，应当尽力救助遇险人员。

**Article 75** Where a vessel, maritime installation or aircraft receives a distress signal or discovers that a person is in danger of life, it shall, without seriously endangering its own safety, do its best to rescue the persons in distress.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　海上搜救中心接到险情报告后，应当立即进行核实，及时组织、协调、指挥政府有关部门、专业搜救队伍、社会有关单位等各方力量参加搜救，并指定现场指挥。参加搜救的船舶、海上设施、航空器及人员应当服从现场指挥，及时报告搜救动态和搜救结果。  
搜救行动的中止、恢复、终止决定由海上搜救中心作出。未经海上搜救中心同意，参加搜救的船舶、海上设施、航空器及人员不得擅自退出搜救行动。  
军队参加海上搜救，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 76** After receiving a report of a peril, the maritime search and rescue center shall immediately verify it, and promptly organize, coordinate and command the government departments concerned, professional search and rescue teams, relevant social units and other forces to participate in the search and rescue, and designate an on-site commander. Vessels, maritime installations, aircraft and personnel participating in the search and rescue shall obey the on-site commander and promptly report the search and rescue dynamics and results.  
Decisions on suspension, resumption or termination of search and rescue operations shall be made by the maritime search and rescue center. Without the consent of the maritime search and rescue center, the vessels, maritime installations, aircraft and personnel participating in the search and rescue shall not withdraw from the search and rescue operation without authorization.  
The participation of the armed forces in maritime search and rescue shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　遇险船舶、海上设施、航空器或者遇险人员应当服从海上搜救中心和现场指挥的指令，及时接受救助。  
遇险船舶、海上设施、航空器不配合救助的，现场指挥根据险情危急情况，可以采取相应救助措施。

**Article 77** A vessel, maritime installation, aircraft or person in distress shall obey the instructions of the maritime search and rescue center and the on-site commander, and timely accept rescue.  
Where a vessel, maritime installation or aircraft in distress fails to cooperate with the rescue, the on-site commander may, based on the urgency of the peril, take corresponding rescue measures.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　海上事故或者险情发生后，有关地方人民政府应当及时组织医疗机构为遇险人员提供紧急医疗救助，为获救人员提供必要的生活保障，并组织有关方面采取善后措施。

**Article 78** After the occurrence of a maritime accident or peril, the relevant local people's government shall promptly organize medical institutions to provide emergency medical assistance to the persons in distress, provide necessary livelihood support for the rescued persons, and organize relevant parties to take post-incident measures.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　在中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约规定由我国承担搜救义务的海域内开展搜救，依照本章规定执行。  
中国籍船舶在中华人民共和国管辖海域以及海上搜救责任区域以外的其他海域发生险情的，中国海上搜救中心接到信息后，应当依据中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约的规定开展国际协作。

**Article 79** Search and rescue carried out in sea areas where the People's Republic of China has assumed search and rescue obligations under international treaties concluded or acceded to by it shall be performed in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter.  
Where a Chinese vessel is in peril in sea areas outside the waters under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China and the maritime search and rescue responsibility zone, the China Maritime Search and Rescue Center shall, after receiving the information, carry out international cooperation in accordance with the provisions of international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China.

### 第七章　海上交通事故调查处理

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　船舶、海上设施发生海上交通事故，应当及时向海事管理机构报告，并接受调查。

**Article 80** Where a vessel or maritime installation is involved in a maritime traffic accident, it shall promptly report to the maritime administration and accept investigation.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　海上交通事故根据造成的损害后果分为特别重大事故、重大事故、较大事故和一般事故。事故等级划分的人身伤亡标准依照有关安全生产的法律、行政法规的规定确定；事故等级划分的直接经济损失标准，由国务院交通运输主管部门会同国务院有关部门根据海上交通事故中的特殊情况确定，报国务院批准后公布施行。

**Article 81** Maritime traffic accidents are classified into extraordinarily serious accidents, major accidents, relatively serious accidents and ordinary accidents according to the damage consequences caused. The criteria for personal injury or death in the classification of accident levels shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations on work safety; the criteria for direct economic losses in the classification of accident levels shall be determined by the competent transport department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council based on the special circumstances of maritime traffic accidents, and shall be published and implemented after approval by the State Council.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　特别重大海上交通事故由国务院或者国务院授权的部门组织事故调查组进行调查，海事管理机构应当参与或者配合开展调查工作。  
其他海上交通事故由海事管理机构组织事故调查组进行调查，有关部门予以配合。国务院认为有必要的，可以直接组织或者授权有关部门组织事故调查组进行调查。  
海事管理机构进行事故调查，事故涉及执行军事运输任务的，应当会同有关军事机关进行调查；涉及渔业船舶的，渔业渔政主管部门、海警机构应当参与调查。

**Article 82** An extraordinarily serious maritime traffic accident shall be investigated by an accident investigation team organized by the State Council or a department authorized by the State Council, and the maritime administration shall participate in or cooperate with the investigation.  
Other maritime traffic accidents shall be investigated by an accident investigation team organized by the maritime administration, with the cooperation of relevant departments. If the State Council deems it necessary, it may directly organize or authorize relevant departments to organize an accident investigation team to conduct the investigation.  
Where the maritime administration conducts an accident investigation involving the execution of military transport tasks, it shall conduct the investigation jointly with the relevant military organs; where the accident involves a fishing vessel, the fishery and fishery administration department and the coast guard agency shall participate in the investigation.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　调查海上交通事故，应当全面、客观、公正、及时，依法查明事故事实和原因，认定事故责任。

**Article 83** The investigation of a maritime traffic accident shall be comprehensive, objective, fair and timely, and shall, according to law, ascertain the facts and causes of the accident and determine the responsibility for the accident.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　海事管理机构可以根据事故调查处理需要拆封、拆解当事船舶的航行数据记录装置或者读取其记录的信息，要求船舶驶向指定地点或者禁止其离港，扣留船舶或者海上设施的证书、文书、物品、资料等并妥善保管。有关人员应当配合事故调查。

**Article 84** The maritime administration may, as necessary for accident investigation and handling, unseal or disassemble the voyage data recording device of the vessel involved or read the information recorded therein, require the vessel to proceed to a designated place or prohibit it from leaving port, and detain the certificates, documents, articles, materials, etc. of the vessel or maritime installation and keep them in safe custody. The persons concerned shall cooperate with the accident investigation.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　海上交通事故调查组应当自事故发生之日起九十日内提交海上交通事故调查报告；特殊情况下，经负责组织事故调查组的部门负责人批准，提交事故调查报告的期限可以适当延长，但延长期限最长不得超过九十日。事故技术鉴定所需时间不计入事故调查期限。  
海事管理机构应当自收到海上交通事故调查报告之日起十五个工作日内作出事故责任认定书，作为处理海上交通事故的证据。  
事故损失较小、事实清楚、责任明确的，可以依照国务院交通运输主管部门的规定适用简易调查程序。  
海上交通事故调查报告、事故责任认定书应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定向社会公开。

**Article 85** The maritime traffic accident investigation team shall submit a maritime traffic accident investigation report within ninety days from the date of the accident; under special circumstances, with the approval of the head of the department responsible for organizing the accident investigation team, the time limit for submitting the accident investigation report may be appropriately extended, but the extended period shall not exceed ninety days at most. The time required for technical appraisal of the accident shall not be counted in the accident investigation period.  
The maritime administration shall, within fifteen working days from the date of receipt of the maritime traffic accident investigation report, issue an accident responsibility determination letter as evidence for handling the maritime traffic accident.  
Where the accident loss is small, the facts are clear and the responsibility is definite, a simplified investigation procedure may be applied in accordance with the provisions of the competent transport department of the State Council.  
The maritime traffic accident investigation report and the accident responsibility determination letter shall be made public to the society in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　中国籍船舶在中华人民共和国管辖海域外发生海上交通事故的，应当及时向海事管理机构报告事故情况并接受调查。  
外国籍船舶在中华人民共和国管辖海域外发生事故，造成中国公民重伤或者死亡的，海事管理机构根据中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约的规定参与调查。

**Article 86** Where a Chinese vessel has a maritime traffic accident outside the waters under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, it shall promptly report the accident to the maritime administration and accept investigation.  
Where a foreign vessel has an accident outside the waters under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, causing serious injury or death to a Chinese citizen, the maritime administration shall participate in the investigation in accordance with the provisions of international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　船舶、海上设施在海上遭遇恶劣天气、海况以及意外事故，造成或者可能造成损害，需要说明并记录时间、海域以及所采取的应对措施等具体情况的，可以向海事管理机构申请办理海事声明签注。海事管理机构应当依照规定提供签注服务。

**Article 87** Where a vessel or maritime installation encounters severe weather, sea conditions or accidents at sea, causing or likely to cause damage, and it is necessary to explain and record specific circumstances such as the time, sea area and the response measures taken, it may apply to the maritime administration for endorsement of a marine note of protest. The maritime administration shall provide endorsement services in accordance with the relevant provisions.

### 第八章　监督管理

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　海事管理机构对在中华人民共和国管辖海域内从事航行、停泊、作业以及其他与海上交通安全相关的活动，依法实施监督检查。  
海事管理机构依照中华人民共和国法律、行政法规以及中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约对外国籍船舶实施港口国、沿岸国监督检查。  
海事管理机构工作人员执行公务时，应当按照规定着装，佩戴职衔标志，出示执法证件，并自觉接受监督。  
海事管理机构依法履行监督检查职责，有关单位、个人应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻碍依法实施的监督检查。

**Article 88** The maritime administration shall, according to law, exercise supervision and inspection over activities such as navigation, berthing, operations and other activities related to maritime traffic safety carried out within the waters under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.  
The maritime administration shall, in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China and the international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, carry out port State and coastal State supervision and inspection on foreign vessels.  
When performing official duties, the staff of the maritime administration shall wear uniforms, wear insignia of rank, produce law enforcement credentials, and voluntarily accept supervision.  
Where the maritime administration performs its supervision and inspection duties according to law, the relevant units and individuals shall cooperate and shall not refuse or obstruct the supervision and inspection carried out according to law.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　海事管理机构实施监督检查可以采取登船检查、查验证书、现场检查、询问有关人员、电子监控等方式。  
载运危险货物的船舶涉嫌存在瞒报、谎报危险货物等情况的，海事管理机构可以采取开箱查验等方式进行检查。海事管理机构应当将开箱查验情况通报有关部门。港口经营人和有关单位、个人应当予以协助。

**Article 89** The maritime administration may, when conducting supervision and inspection, adopt methods such as onboard inspection, verification of certificates, on-site inspection, inquiring of relevant persons, and electronic monitoring.  
Where a vessel carrying dangerous goods is suspected of concealing or falsely reporting the dangerous goods, the maritime administration may adopt methods such as container inspection to conduct inspection. The maritime administration shall notify the relevant departments of the container inspection situation. Port operators and relevant units and individuals shall provide assistance.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　海事管理机构对船舶、海上设施实施监督检查时，应当避免、减少对其正常作业的影响。  
除法律、行政法规另有规定或者不立即实施监督检查可能造成严重后果外，不得拦截正在航行中的船舶进行检查。

**Article 90** When conducting supervision and inspection on vessels and maritime installations, the maritime administration shall avoid or reduce the impact on their normal operations.  
Unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations, or unless failure to immediately conduct supervision and inspection may cause serious consequences, a vessel under way shall not be intercepted for inspection.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　船舶、海上设施对港口安全具有威胁的，海事管理机构应当责令立即或者限期改正、限制操作，责令驶往指定地点、禁止进港或者将其驱逐出港。  
船舶、海上设施处于不适航或者不适拖状态，船员、海上设施上的相关人员未持有有效的法定证书、文书，或者存在其他严重危害海上交通安全、污染海洋环境的隐患的，海事管理机构应当根据情况禁止有关船舶、海上设施进出港，暂扣有关证书、文书或者责令其停航、改航、驶往指定地点或者停止作业。船舶超载的，海事管理机构可以依法对船舶进行强制减载。因强制减载发生的费用由违法船舶所有人、经营人或者管理人承担。  
船舶、海上设施发生海上交通事故、污染事故，未结清国家规定的税费、滞纳金且未提供担保或者未履行其他法定义务的，海事管理机构应当责令改正，并可以禁止其离港。

**Article 91** Where a vessel or maritime installation poses a threat to port safety, the maritime administration shall order immediate or timely rectification, restrict operations, order it to proceed to a designated place, prohibit it from entering the port, or expel it from the port.  
Where a vessel or maritime installation is in an unseaworthy or untowable condition, the crew members or relevant personnel on the maritime installation do not hold valid statutory certificates or documents, or there are other serious hidden dangers endangering maritime traffic safety or causing marine environmental pollution, the maritime administration shall, according to the circumstances, prohibit the relevant vessel or maritime installation from entering or leaving the port, temporarily detain the relevant certificates or documents, or order it to stop navigation, change course, proceed to a designated place, or stop operations. Where a vessel is overloaded, the maritime administration may, according to law, carry out forced unloading of the vessel. The expenses incurred from forced unloading shall be borne by the owner, operator or manager of the illegal vessel.  
Where a vessel or maritime installation is involved in a maritime traffic accident or pollution accident and has not settled taxes, fees or late payment surcharges prescribed by the State, nor provided a guarantee or fulfilled other statutory obligations, the maritime administration shall order rectification and may prohibit it from leaving port.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　外国籍船舶可能威胁中华人民共和国内水、领海安全的，海事管理机构有权责令其离开。  
外国籍船舶违反中华人民共和国海上交通安全或者防治船舶污染的法律、行政法规的，海事管理机构可以依法行使紧追权。

**Article 92** Where a foreign vessel may threaten the safety of the internal waters or territorial sea of the People's Republic of China, the maritime administration has the right to order it to leave.  
Where a foreign vessel violates the laws or administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China on maritime traffic safety or prevention of vessel-source pollution, the maritime administration may, according to law, exercise the right of hot pursuit.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　任何单位、个人有权向海事管理机构举报妨碍海上交通安全的行为。海事管理机构接到举报后，应当及时进行核实、处理。

**Article 93** Any unit or individual has the right to report to the maritime administration any act that obstructs maritime traffic safety. Upon receipt of a report, the maritime administration shall promptly verify and handle it.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　海事管理机构在监督检查中，发现船舶、海上设施有违反其他法律、行政法规行为的，应当依法及时通报或者移送有关主管部门处理。

**Article 94** Where, during supervision and inspection, the maritime administration discovers that a vessel or maritime installation has violated other laws or administrative regulations, it shall, according to law, promptly notify or transfer the matter to the relevant competent department for handling.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　船舶、海上设施未持有有效的证书、文书的，由海事管理机构责令改正，对违法船舶或者海上设施的所有人、经营人或者管理人处三万元以上三十万元以下的罚款，对船长和有关责任人员处三千元以上三万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，暂扣船长、责任船员的船员适任证书十八个月至三十个月，直至吊销船员适任证书；对船舶持有的伪造、变造证书、文书，予以没收；对存在严重安全隐患的船舶，可以依法予以没收。

**Article 95** Where a vessel or maritime installation does not hold valid certificates or documents, the maritime administration shall order rectification, impose a fine of not less than RMB 30,000 but not more than RMB 300,000 on the owner, operator or manager of the illegal vessel or maritime installation, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 3,000 but not more than RMB 30,000 on the master and the responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, the seafarer's certificate of competency of the master and the responsible seafarers shall be temporarily detained for 18 to 30 months, or even revoked; the forged or altered certificates or documents held by the vessel shall be confiscated; where there are serious safety hazards on the vessel, it may be confiscated according to law.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　船舶或者海上设施有下列情形之一的，由海事管理机构责令改正，对违法船舶或者海上设施的所有人、经营人或者管理人处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款，对船长和有关责任人员处二千元以上二万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销违法船舶所有人、经营人或者管理人的有关证书、文书，暂扣船长、责任船员的船员适任证书十二个月至二十四个月，直至吊销船员适任证书：  
（一）船舶、海上设施的实际状况与持有的证书、文书不符；  
（二）船舶未依法悬挂国旗，或者违法悬挂其他国家、地区或者组织的旗帜；  
（三）船舶未按规定标明船名、船舶识别号、船籍港、载重线标志；  
（四）船舶、海上设施的配员不符合最低安全配员要求。

**Article 96** Where a vessel or maritime installation is in any of the following circumstances, the maritime administration shall order rectification, impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 200,000 on the owner, operator or manager of the illegal vessel or maritime installation, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 but not more than RMB 20,000 on the master and the responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant certificates and documents of the owner, operator or manager of the illegal vessel shall be revoked, and the seafarer's certificate of competency of the master and the responsible seafarers shall be temporarily detained for 12 to 24 months, or even revoked:  
(1) the actual condition of the vessel or maritime installation does not conform to the certificates or documents held;  
(2) the vessel fails to fly the national flag according to law, or illegally flies the flag of another country, region or organization;  
(3) the vessel fails to mark the ship's name, ship identification number, port of registry and load line marks in accordance with regulations;  
(4) the manning of the vessel or maritime installation does not meet the minimum safe manning requirements.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　在船舶上工作未持有船员适任证书、船员健康证明或者所持船员适任证书、健康证明不符合要求的，由海事管理机构对船舶的所有人、经营人或者管理人处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款，对责任船员处三千元以上三万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，对船舶的所有人、经营人或者管理人处三万元以上三十万元以下的罚款，暂扣责任船员的船员适任证书六个月至十二个月，直至吊销船员适任证书。

**Article 97** Where a person working on a vessel does not hold a seafarer's certificate of competency or a seafarer's health certificate, or the certificate of competency or health certificate held does not meet the requirements, the maritime administration shall impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 on the owner, operator or manager of the vessel, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 3,000 but not more than RMB 30,000 on the responsible seafarer; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 30,000 but not more than RMB 300,000 shall be imposed on the owner, operator or manager of the vessel, and the seafarer's certificate of competency of the responsible seafarer shall be temporarily detained for 6 to 12 months, or even revoked.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　以欺骗、贿赂等不正当手段为中国籍船舶取得相关证书、文书的，由海事管理机构撤销有关许可，没收相关证书、文书，对船舶所有人、经营人或者管理人处四万元以上四十万元以下的罚款。  
以欺骗、贿赂等不正当手段取得船员适任证书的，由海事管理机构撤销有关许可，没收船员适任证书，对责任人员处五千元以上五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 98** Where relevant certificates or documents are obtained for a Chinese vessel by fraudulent, bribery or other improper means, the maritime administration shall revoke the relevant permit, confiscate the relevant certificates or documents, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 40,000 but not more than RMB 400,000 on the owner, operator or manager of the vessel.  
Where a seafarer's certificate of competency is obtained by fraudulent, bribery or other improper means, the maritime administration shall revoke the relevant permit, confiscate the seafarer's certificate of competency, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 but not more than RMB 50,000 on the responsible person.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　船员未保持安全值班，违反规定摄入可能影响安全值班的食品、药品或者其他物品，或者有其他违反海上船员值班规则的行为的，由海事管理机构对船长、责任船员处一千元以上一万元以下的罚款，或者暂扣船员适任证书三个月至十二个月；情节严重的，吊销船长、责任船员的船员适任证书。

**Article 99** Where a seafarer fails to maintain safe watch, violates regulations by ingesting food, medicine or other substances that may affect safe watch, or commits other acts in violation of the maritime seafarer watchkeeping rules, the maritime administration shall impose a fine of not less than RMB 1,000 but not more than RMB 10,000 on the master or the responsible seafarer, or temporarily detain the seafarer's certificate of competency for 3 to 12 months; if the circumstances are serious, the seafarer's certificate of competency of the master or the responsible seafarer shall be revoked.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　有下列情形之一的，由海事管理机构责令改正；情节严重的，处三万元以上十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）建设海洋工程、海岸工程未按规定配备相应的防止船舶碰撞的设施、设备并设置专用航标；  
（二）损坏海上交通支持服务系统或者妨碍其工作效能；  
（三）未经海事管理机构同意设置、撤除专用航标，移动专用航标位置或者改变航标灯光、功率等其他状况，或者设置临时航标不符合海事管理机构确定的航标设置点；  
（四）在安全作业区、港外锚地范围内从事养殖、种植、捕捞以及其他影响海上交通安全的作业或者活动。

**Article 100** In any of the following circumstances, the maritime administration shall order rectification; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 30,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 shall be imposed:  
(1) the construction of marine engineering or coastal engineering fails to equip corresponding facilities and equipment for preventing vessel collisions and set up special navigation marks in accordance with regulations;  
(2) damaging a maritime traffic support service system or impairing its working efficiency;  
(3) without the consent of the maritime administration, setting up or removing special navigation marks, moving the position of special navigation marks or changing other conditions such as the light or power of navigation marks, or setting up temporary navigation marks not in accordance with the navigation mark setting points determined by the maritime administration;  
(4) engaging in aquaculture, planting, fishing or other operations or activities affecting maritime traffic safety within the scope of a safety operation zone or an outer-port anchorage.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　有下列情形之一的，由海事管理机构责令改正，对有关责任人员处三万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处三万元以上十万元以下的罚款，并暂扣责任船员的船员适任证书一个月至三个月：  
（一）承担无线电通信任务的船员和岸基无线电台（站）的工作人员未保持海上交通安全通信频道的值守和畅通，或者使用海上交通安全通信频率交流与海上交通安全无关的内容；  
（二）违反国家有关规定使用无线电台识别码，影响海上搜救的身份识别；  
（三）其他违反海上无线电通信规则的行为。

**Article 101** In any of the following circumstances, the maritime administrative agency shall order corrections, impose a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan on the responsible person; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the seafarer's certificate of competency of the responsible seafarer shall be suspended for not less than one month but not more than three months:  
(1) A seafarer performing radio communication duties or a staff member of a shore-based radio station (post) fails to maintain the watchkeeping and unobstructed operation of the maritime traffic safety communication channel, or uses the maritime traffic safety communication frequency to exchange content unrelated to maritime traffic safety;  
(2) Using a radio station identification code in violation of the relevant state provisions, affecting the identification of persons during maritime search and rescue;  
(3) Other acts in violation of the rules for maritime radio communication.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　船舶未依照本法规定申请引航的，由海事管理机构对违法船舶的所有人、经营人或者管理人处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，对船长处一千元以上一万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，暂扣有关船舶证书三个月至十二个月，暂扣船长的船员适任证书一个月至三个月。  
引航机构派遣引航员存在过失，造成船舶损失的，由海事管理机构对引航机构处三万元以上三十万元以下的罚款。  
未经引航机构指派擅自提供引航服务的，由海事管理机构对引领船舶的人员处三千元以上三万元以下的罚款。

**Article 102** If a ship fails to apply for pilotage in accordance with this Law, the maritime administrative agency shall impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan on the owner, operator, or manager of the illegal ship, and a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan on the master; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant ship certificates shall be suspended for not less than three months but not more than twelve months, and the seafarer's certificate of competency of the master shall be suspended for not less than one month but not more than three months.  
If a pilotage agency dispatches a pilot with negligence, causing loss to the ship, the maritime administrative agency shall impose a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan on the pilotage agency.  
If a person provides pilotage service without authorization from a pilotage agency, the maritime administrative agency shall impose a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan on the person piloting the ship.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　船舶在海上航行、停泊、作业，有下列情形之一的，由海事管理机构责令改正，对违法船舶的所有人、经营人或者管理人处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款，对船长、责任船员处二千元以上二万元以下的罚款，暂扣船员适任证书三个月至十二个月；情节严重的，吊销船长、责任船员的船员适任证书：  
（一）船舶进出港口、锚地或者通过桥区水域、海峡、狭水道、重要渔业水域、通航船舶密集的区域、船舶定线区、交通管制区时，未加强瞭望、保持安全航速并遵守前述区域的特殊航行规则；  
（二）未按照有关规定显示信号、悬挂标志或者保持足够的富余水深；  
（三）不符合安全开航条件冒险开航，违章冒险操作、作业，或者未按照船舶检验证书载明的航区航行、停泊、作业；  
（四）未按照有关规定开启船舶的自动识别、航行数据记录、远程识别和跟踪、通信等与航行安全、保安、防治污染相关的装置，并持续进行显示和记录；  
（五）擅自拆封、拆解、初始化、再设置航行数据记录装置或者读取其记录的信息；  
（六）船舶穿越航道妨碍航道内船舶的正常航行，抢越他船船艏或者超过桥梁通航尺度进入桥区水域；  
（七）船舶违反规定进入或者穿越禁航区；  
（八）船舶载运或者拖带超长、超高、超宽、半潜的船舶、海上设施或者其他物体航行，未采取特殊的安全保障措施，未在开航前向海事管理机构报告航行计划，未按规定显示信号、悬挂标志，或者拖带移动式平台、浮船坞等大型海上设施未依法交验船舶检验机构出具的拖航检验证书；  
（九）船舶在不符合安全条件的码头、泊位、装卸站、锚地、安全作业区停泊，或者停泊危及其他船舶、海上设施的安全；  
（十）船舶违反规定超过检验证书核定的载客定额、载重线、载货种类载运乘客、货物，或者客船载运乘客同时载运危险货物；  
（十一）客船未向乘客明示安全须知、设置安全标志和警示；  
（十二）未按照有关法律、行政法规、规章以及强制性标准和技术规范的要求安全装卸、积载、隔离、系固和管理货物；  
（十三）其他违反海上航行、停泊、作业规则的行为。

**Article 103** If a ship, while navigating, berthing, or operating at sea, is in any of the following circumstances, the maritime administrative agency shall order corrections, impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan on the owner, operator, or manager of the illegal ship, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan on the master and responsible seafarers, and suspend the seafarer's certificate of competency for not less than three months but not more than twelve months; if the circumstances are serious, the seafarer's certificate of competency of the master and responsible seafarers shall be revoked:  
(1) When entering or leaving a port or anchorage, or passing through a bridge area, strait, narrow channel, important fishing water, an area dense with navigating vessels, a ship routing system, or a traffic control area, the ship fails to enhance lookout, maintain a safe speed, and comply with the special navigation rules of such areas;  
(2) Failing to display signals, hoist marks, or maintain sufficient under-keel clearance in accordance with the relevant provisions;  
(3) Setting sail recklessly without meeting the safe departure conditions, operating or performing in violation of rules at risk, or navigating, berthing, or operating outside the navigation area specified in the ship's inspection certificate;  
(4) Failing to activate the ship's automatic identification, voyage data recording, remote identification and tracking, communication, and other devices related to navigation safety, security, and pollution prevention in accordance with the relevant provisions, and failing to continuously display and record;  
(5) Unauthorized unsealing, disassembling, initializing, resetting the voyage data recording device, or reading the recorded information;  
(6) The ship crossing a fairway, obstructing the normal navigation of other ships in the fairway, cutting across the bow of another ship, or entering a bridge area beyond the bridge navigation clearance;  
(7) The ship entering or crossing a prohibited navigation area in violation of regulations;  
(8) The ship carrying or towing an extraordinarily long, high, wide, or semi-submersible ship, maritime installation, or other object without taking special safety measures, failing to report the navigation plan to the maritime administrative agency before departure, failing to display signals or hoist marks as required, or towing a mobile platform, floating dry dock, or other large maritime installation without submitting the towage inspection certificate issued by a ship inspection institution for verification according to law;  
(9) The ship berthing at a wharf, berth, loading station, anchorage, or safety operation area that does not meet safety conditions, or berthing in a manner that endangers the safety of other ships or maritime installations;  
(10) The ship carrying passengers or cargo in violation of regulations beyond the passenger capacity, load line, or cargo type specified in the inspection certificate, or a passenger ship carrying passengers while also carrying dangerous cargo;  
(11) A passenger ship fails to indicate safety instructions to passengers or set up safety signs and warnings;  
(12) Failing to safely load, stow, segregate, secure, and manage cargo in accordance with the requirements of relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules, and mandatory standards and technical specifications;  
(13) Other acts in violation of the rules for maritime navigation, berthing, or operation.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　国际航行船舶未经许可进出口岸的，由海事管理机构对违法船舶的所有人、经营人或者管理人处三千元以上三万元以下的罚款，对船长、责任船员或者其他责任人员，处二千元以上二万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销船长、责任船员的船员适任证书。  
国内航行船舶进出港口、港外装卸站未依法向海事管理机构报告的，由海事管理机构对违法船舶的所有人、经营人或者管理人处三千元以上三万元以下的罚款，对船长、责任船员或者其他责任人员处五百元以上五千元以下的罚款。

**Article 104** If an internationally navigating ship enters or leaves a port without permission, the maritime administrative agency shall impose a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan on the owner, operator, or manager of the illegal ship, and a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan on the master, responsible seafarers, or other responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, the seafarer's certificate of competency of the master and responsible seafarers shall be revoked.  
If a domestically navigating ship enters or leaves a port or an offshore loading station without reporting to the maritime administrative agency according to law, the maritime administrative agency shall impose a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan on the owner, operator, or manager of the illegal ship, and a fine of not less than 500 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan on the master, responsible seafarers, or other responsible persons.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　船舶、海上设施未经许可从事海上施工作业，或者未按照许可要求、超出核定的安全作业区进行作业的，由海事管理机构责令改正，对违法船舶、海上设施的所有人、经营人或者管理人处三万元以上三十万元以下的罚款，对船长、责任船员处三千元以上三万元以下的罚款，或者暂扣船员适任证书六个月至十二个月；情节严重的，吊销船长、责任船员的船员适任证书。  
从事可能影响海上交通安全的水上水下活动，未按规定提前报告海事管理机构的，由海事管理机构对违法船舶、海上设施的所有人、经营人或者管理人处一万元以上三万元以下的罚款，对船长、责任船员处二千元以上二万元以下的罚款。

**Article 105** If a ship or maritime installation engages in maritime construction operations without permission, or fails to comply with the permission requirements or operates beyond the approved safety operation zone, the maritime administrative agency shall order corrections, impose a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan on the owner, operator, or manager of the illegal ship or maritime installation, and a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan on the master and responsible seafarers, or suspend the seafarer's certificate of competency for not less than six months but not more than twelve months; if the circumstances are serious, the seafarer's certificate of competency of the master and responsible seafarers shall be revoked.  
If a person engages in water surface or underwater activities that may affect maritime traffic safety without reporting to the maritime administrative agency in advance as required, the maritime administrative agency shall impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan on the owner, operator, or manager of the illegal ship or maritime installation, and a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan on the master and responsible seafarers.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　碍航物的所有人、经营人或者管理人有下列情形之一的，由海事管理机构责令改正，处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，海事管理机构有权依法实施代履行，代履行的费用由碍航物的所有人、经营人或者管理人承担：  
（一）未按照有关强制性标准和技术规范的要求及时设置警示标志；  
（二）未向海事管理机构报告碍航物的名称、形状、尺寸、位置和深度；  
（三）未在海事管理机构限定的期限内打捞清除碍航物。

**Article 106** If the owner, operator, or manager of an obstruction to navigation is in any of the following circumstances, the maritime administrative agency shall order corrections and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the correction is not made within the time limit, the maritime administrative agency shall have the right to carry out substitute performance according to law, and the cost of substitute performance shall be borne by the owner, operator, or manager of the obstruction to navigation:  
(1) Failing to set up warning marks in a timely manner in accordance with the requirements of the relevant mandatory standards and technical specifications;  
(2) Failing to report the name, shape, dimensions, position, and depth of the obstruction to navigation to the maritime administrative agency;  
(3) Failing to remove the obstruction to navigation by salvage within the time limit specified by the maritime administrative agency.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　外国籍船舶进出中华人民共和国内水、领海违反本法规定的，由海事管理机构对违法船舶的所有人、经营人或者管理人处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，对船长处一万元以上三万元以下的罚款。

**Article 107** If a foreign ship enters or leaves the internal waters or territorial sea of the People's Republic of China in violation of the provisions of this Law, the maritime administrative agency shall impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan on the owner, operator, or manager of the illegal ship, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan on the master.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　载运危险货物的船舶有下列情形之一的，海事管理机构应当责令改正，对违法船舶的所有人、经营人或者管理人处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，对船长、责任船员或者其他责任人员，处五千元以上五万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停止作业或者航行，暂扣船长、责任船员的船员适任证书六个月至十二个月，直至吊销船员适任证书：  
（一）未经许可进出港口或者从事散装液体危险货物过驳作业；  
（二）未按规定编制相应的应急处置预案，配备相应的消防、应急设备和器材；  
（三）违反有关强制性标准和安全作业操作规程的要求从事危险货物装卸、过驳作业。

**Article 108** If a ship carrying dangerous cargo is in any of the following circumstances, the maritime administrative agency shall order corrections, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan on the owner, operator, or manager of the illegal ship, and a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the master, responsible seafarers, or other responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, the operation or navigation shall be ordered to stop, the seafarer's certificate of competency of the master and responsible seafarers shall be suspended for not less than six months but not more than twelve months, up to revocation of the seafarer's certificate of competency:  
(1) Entering or leaving a port or engaging in the lightering of bulk liquid dangerous cargo without permission;  
(2) Failing to prepare the corresponding emergency response plan and equip the corresponding fire-fighting and emergency equipment and devices as required;  
(3) Engaging in the loading, unloading, or lightering of dangerous cargo in violation of the requirements of the relevant mandatory standards and safe operation procedures.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　托运人托运危险货物，有下列情形之一的，由海事管理机构责令改正，处五万元以上三十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未将托运的危险货物的正式名称、危险性质以及应当采取的防护措施通知承运人；  
（二）未按照有关法律、行政法规、规章以及强制性标准和技术规范的要求对危险货物妥善包装，设置明显的危险品标志和标签；  
（三）在托运的普通货物中夹带危险货物或者将危险货物谎报为普通货物托运；  
（四）未依法提交有关专业机构出具的表明该货物危险特性以及应当采取的防护措施等情况的文件。

**Article 109** If a consignor consigns dangerous cargo in any of the following circumstances, the maritime administrative agency shall order corrections and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan:  
(1) Failing to notify the carrier of the formal name, hazardous nature, and protective measures to be taken for the consigned dangerous cargo;  
(2) Failing to properly pack the dangerous cargo and set up clear dangerous goods marks and labels in accordance with the requirements of the relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules, and mandatory standards and technical specifications;  
(3) Mixing dangerous cargo with ordinary cargo consigned, or falsely declaring dangerous cargo as ordinary cargo for consignment;  
(4) Failing to submit documents issued by a relevant professional institution indicating the hazardous characteristics of the cargo and the protective measures to be taken, as required by law.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　船舶、海上设施遇险或者发生海上交通事故后未履行报告义务，或者存在瞒报、谎报情形的，由海事管理机构对违法船舶、海上设施的所有人、经营人或者管理人处三千元以上三万元以下的罚款，对船长、责任船员处二千元以上二万元以下的罚款，暂扣船员适任证书六个月至二十四个月；情节严重的，对违法船舶、海上设施的所有人、经营人或者管理人处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款，吊销船长、责任船员的船员适任证书。

**Article 110** If after a ship or maritime installation encounters a distress or a maritime traffic accident, it fails to fulfill the reporting obligation, or there is concealment or false reporting, the maritime administrative agency shall impose a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan on the owner, operator, or manager of the illegal ship or maritime installation, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan on the master and responsible seafarers, and suspend the seafarer's certificate of competency for not less than six months but not more than twenty-four months; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed on the owner, operator, or manager of the illegal ship or maritime installation, and the seafarer's certificate of competency of the master and responsible seafarers shall be revoked.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　船舶发生海上交通事故后逃逸的，由海事管理机构对违法船舶的所有人、经营人或者管理人处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，对船长、责任船员处五千元以上五万元以下的罚款并吊销船员适任证书，受处罚者终身不得重新申请。

**Article 111** If a ship escapes after a maritime traffic accident, the maritime administrative agency shall impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan on the owner, operator, or manager of the illegal ship, a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the master and responsible seafarers, and revoke their seafarer's certificate of competency, and the punished person shall not apply again for life.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　船舶、海上设施不依法履行海上救助义务，不服从海上搜救中心指挥的，由海事管理机构对船舶、海上设施的所有人、经营人或者管理人处三万元以上三十万元以下的罚款，暂扣船长、责任船员的船员适任证书六个月至十二个月，直至吊销船员适任证书。

**Article 112** If a ship or maritime installation fails to fulfill the maritime salvage obligation according to law or fails to comply with the command of the maritime search and rescue center, the maritime administrative agency shall impose a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan on the owner, operator, or manager of the ship or maritime installation, and suspend the seafarer's certificate of competency of the master and responsible seafarers for not less than six months but not more than twelve months, up to revocation of the seafarer's certificate of competency.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　有关单位、个人拒绝、阻碍海事管理机构监督检查，或者在接受监督检查时弄虚作假的，由海事管理机构处二千元以上二万元以下的罚款，暂扣船长、责任船员的船员适任证书六个月至二十四个月，直至吊销船员适任证书。

**Article 113** If a relevant entity or individual refuses or obstructs the supervision and inspection by the maritime administrative agency, or practices fraud during the supervision and inspection, the maritime administrative agency shall impose a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan, and suspend the seafarer's certificate of competency of the master and responsible seafarers for not less than six months but not more than twenty-four months, up to revocation of the seafarer's certificate of competency.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　交通运输主管部门、海事管理机构及其他有关部门的工作人员违反本法规定，滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 114** If a staff member of the competent department of transportation, the maritime administrative agency, or other relevant departments violates the provisions of this Law by abusing power, neglecting duty, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain, disciplinary action shall be taken according to law.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　因海上交通事故引发民事纠纷的，当事人可以依法申请仲裁或者向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 115** If a civil dispute arises from a maritime traffic accident, the parties may apply for arbitration or file a prosecution with a People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；造成人身、财产损害的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 116** If a violation of the provisions of this Law constitutes a public security violation, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law; if it causes personal or property damage, civil liability shall be borne according to law; if it constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　本法下列用语的含义是：  
船舶，是指各类排水或者非排水的船、艇、筏、水上飞行器、潜水器、移动式平台以及其他移动式装置。  
海上设施，是指水上水下各种固定或者浮动建筑、装置和固定平台，但是不包括码头、防波堤等港口设施。  
内水，是指中华人民共和国领海基线向陆地一侧至海岸线的海域。  
施工作业，是指勘探、采掘、爆破，构筑、维修、拆除水上水下构筑物或者设施，航道建设、疏浚（航道养护疏浚除外）作业，打捞沉船沉物。  
海上交通事故，是指船舶、海上设施在航行、停泊、作业过程中发生的，由于碰撞、搁浅、触礁、触碰、火灾、风灾、浪损、沉没等原因造成人员伤亡或者财产损失的事故。  
海上险情，是指对海上生命安全、水域环境构成威胁，需立即采取措施规避、控制、减轻和消除的各种情形。  
危险货物，是指国际海上危险货物运输规则和国家危险货物品名表上列明的，易燃、易爆、有毒、有腐蚀性、有放射性、有污染危害性等，在船舶载运过程中可能造成人身伤害、财产损失或者环境污染而需要采取特别防护措施的货物。  
海上渡口，是指海上岛屿之间、海上岛屿与大陆之间，以及隔海相望的大陆与大陆之间，专用于渡船渡运人员、行李、车辆的交通基础设施。

**Article 117** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:  
Ship means various types of vessels, boats, rafts, watercraft, submersibles, mobile platforms, and other mobile installations, whether displacing or non-displacing water.  
Maritime installation means various fixed or floating structures, installations, and fixed platforms above or underwater, excluding port facilities such as wharves and breakwaters.  
Internal waters means the sea areas on the landward side of the baseline of the territorial sea of the People's Republic of China up to the coastline.  
Construction operations means exploration, mining, blasting, construction, repair, demolition of structures or installations above or underwater, channel construction and dredging operations (excluding channel maintenance dredging), and salvage of sunken ships and objects.  
Maritime traffic accident means an accident occurring during the navigation, berthing, or operation of a ship or maritime installation, resulting in casualties or property damage due to collision, grounding, striking a reef, contact, fire, wind damage, wave damage, sinking, or other causes.  
Maritime distress means various situations that threaten life safety at sea or the marine environment and require immediate measures to avoid, control, mitigate, and eliminate them.  
Dangerous cargo means cargo listed in the International Maritime Dangerous Goods Code and the national list of dangerous goods that is flammable, explosive, toxic, corrosive, radioactive, pollution-hazardous, etc., and which, during carriage by ship, may cause personal injury, property damage, or environmental pollution and requires special protective measures.  
Maritime ferry means a transportation infrastructure dedicated to ferry ships for transporting persons, luggage, and vehicles between maritime islands, between maritime islands and the mainland, and between opposite mainland areas separated by sea.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　公务船舶检验、船员配备的具体办法由国务院交通运输主管部门会同有关主管部门另行制定。  
体育运动船舶的登记、检验办法由国务院体育主管部门另行制定。训练、比赛期间的体育运动船舶的海上交通安全监督管理由体育主管部门负责。  
渔业船员、渔业无线电、渔业航标的监督管理，渔业船舶的登记管理，渔港水域内的海上交通安全管理，渔业船舶（含外国籍渔业船舶）之间交通事故的调查处理，由县级以上人民政府渔业渔政主管部门负责。法律、行政法规或者国务院对渔业船舶之间交通事故的调查处理另有规定的，从其规定。  
除前款规定外，渔业船舶的海上交通安全管理由海事管理机构负责。渔业船舶的检验及其监督管理，由海事管理机构依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。  
浮式储油装置等海上石油、天然气生产设施的检验适用有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 118** The specific measures for the inspection of public service ships and the manning of seafarers shall be separately formulated by the competent department of transportation of the State Council jointly with the relevant competent departments.  
The measures for the registration and inspection of sports ships shall be separately formulated by the competent department of sports of the State Council. The supervision and administration of maritime traffic safety of sports ships during training and competition shall be the responsibility of the competent department of sports.  
The supervision and administration of fishery seafarers, fishery radio, and fishery aids to navigation, the registration administration of fishery ships, the maritime traffic safety management within fishing port waters, and the investigation and handling of traffic accidents between fishery ships (including foreign fishery ships) shall be the responsibility of the competent fishery and fishery administration department of the people's government at or above the county level. Where laws, administrative regulations, or the State Council provide otherwise for the investigation and handling of traffic accidents between fishery ships, those provisions shall prevail.  
Except as provided in the preceding paragraph, the maritime traffic safety management of fishery ships shall be the responsibility of the maritime administrative agency. The inspection of fishery ships and its supervision and administration shall be carried out by the maritime administrative agency in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
The inspection of floating oil storage units and other offshore oil and natural gas production facilities shall be subject to the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　海上军事管辖区和军用船舶、海上设施的内部海上交通安全管理，军用航标的设立和管理，以及为军事目的进行作业或者水上水下活动的管理，由中央军事委员会另行制定管理办法。  
划定、调整海上交通功能区或者领海内特定水域，划定海上渡口的渡运线路，许可海上施工作业，可能对军用船舶的战备、训练、执勤等行动造成影响的，海事管理机构应当事先征求有关军事机关的意见。  
执行军事运输任务有特殊需要的，有关军事机关应当及时向海事管理机构通报相关信息。海事管理机构应当给予必要的便利。  
海上交通安全管理涉及国防交通、军事设施保护的，依照有关法律的规定执行。

**Article 119** The internal maritime traffic safety management of maritime military control zones and military ships and maritime installations, the establishment and management of military aids to navigation, and the management of operations or water surface and underwater activities for military purposes shall be separately formulated by the Central Military Commission.  
Where the delimitation or adjustment of maritime traffic functional zones or specific waters within the territorial sea, the delimitation of ferry routes of maritime ferries, or the permission of maritime construction operations may affect the combat readiness, training, or duty performance of military ships, the maritime administrative agency shall seek the opinions of the relevant military authorities in advance.  
Where there are special needs for carrying out military transport tasks, the relevant military authorities shall promptly notify the maritime administrative agency of the relevant information. The maritime administrative agency shall provide necessary convenience.  
Where maritime traffic safety management involves national defense transportation or the protection of military facilities, the provisions of relevant laws shall be followed.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　外国籍公务船舶在中华人民共和国领海航行、停泊、作业，违反中华人民共和国法律、行政法规的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定处理。  
在中华人民共和国管辖海域内的外国籍军用船舶的管理，适用有关法律的规定。

**Article 120** If a foreign public service ship navigating, berthing, or operating in the territorial sea of the People's Republic of China violates the laws or administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China, it shall be dealt with in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
The management of foreign military ships within the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China shall be governed by the provisions of relevant laws.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约同本法有不同规定的，适用国际条约的规定，但中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。&thinsp;<sup>[1]</sup>

**Article 121** If an international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China contains provisions differing from those of this Law, the provisions of the international treaty shall prevail, except for clauses on which the People's Republic of China has declared reservations.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　本法自2021年9月1日起施行。

**Article 122** This Law shall come into force as of September 1, 2021.
