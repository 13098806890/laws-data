### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范和加强航道的规划、建设、养护、保护，保障航道畅通和通航安全，促进水路运输发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of regulating and strengthening the planning, construction, maintenance and protection of waterways, ensuring the unimpeded flow and navigation safety of waterways, and promoting the development of water transport.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称航道，是指中华人民共和国领域内的江河、湖泊等内陆水域中可以供船舶通航的通道，以及内海、领海中经建设、养护可以供船舶通航的通道。航道包括通航建筑物、航道整治建筑物和航标等航道设施。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "waterway" means the channels within inland waters such as rivers and lakes within the territory of the People's Republic of China that are navigable for ships, as well as the channels within internal seas and territorial seas that are navigable for ships after construction and maintenance. Waterways include navigation structures, waterway regulation structures, navigation aids and other waterway facilities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　规划、建设、养护、保护航道，应当根据经济社会发展和国防建设的需要，遵循综合利用和保护水资源、保护生态环境的原则，服从综合交通运输体系建设和防洪总体安排，统筹兼顾供水、灌溉、发电、渔业等需求，发挥水资源的综合效益。

**Article 3** The planning, construction, maintenance and protection of waterways shall be carried out in accordance with the needs of economic and social development and national defense construction, follow the principles of comprehensive utilization and protection of water resources and protection of the ecological environment, be subject to the overall arrangement of comprehensive transportation system construction and flood control, and give overall consideration to the water supply, irrigation, power generation, fisheries and other needs, so as to give full play to the comprehensive benefits of water resources.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院和有关县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对航道工作的领导，组织、协调、督促有关部门采取措施，保持和改善航道通航条件，保护航道安全，维护航道网络完整和畅通。  
国务院和有关县级以上地方人民政府应当根据经济社会发展水平和航道建设、养护的需要，在财政预算中合理安排航道建设和养护资金。

**Article 4** The State Council and the relevant local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the leadership over waterway work, organize, coordinate and urge the relevant departments to take measures to maintain and improve the navigation conditions of waterways, protect the safety of waterways, and maintain the integrity and unimpeded flow of the waterway network.  
The State Council and the relevant local people's governments at or above the county level shall, on the basis of the level of economic and social development and the needs of waterway construction and maintenance, make reasonable arrangements in the financial budget for funds for waterway construction and maintenance.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院交通运输主管部门主管全国航道管理工作，并按照国务院的规定直接管理跨省、自治区、直辖市的重要干线航道和国际、国境河流航道等重要航道。  
县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的规定主管所辖航道的管理工作。  
国务院交通运输主管部门按照国务院规定设置的负责航道管理的机构和县级以上地方人民政府负责航道管理的部门或者机构（以下统称负责航道管理的部门），承担本法规定的航道管理工作。

**Article 5** The competent department of transport under the State Council shall be in charge of the administration of waterways nationwide, and shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council, directly administer important trunk waterways crossing provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and important waterways such as international and boundary river waterways.  
The competent department of transport under the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, be in charge of the administration of waterways under its jurisdiction.  
The institutions responsible for waterway administration established by the competent department of transport under the State Council in accordance with the provisions of the State Council, and the departments or institutions responsible for waterway administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level (hereinafter collectively referred to as the "department responsible for waterway administration"), shall undertake the waterway administration work prescribed by this Law.

### 第二章　航道规划

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　航道规划分为全国航道规划、流域航道规划、区域航道规划和省、自治区、直辖市航道规划。  
航道规划应当包括航道的功能定位、规划目标、发展规划技术等级、规划实施步骤以及保障措施等内容。  
航道规划应当符合依法制定的流域、区域综合规划，符合水资源规划、防洪规划和海洋功能区划，并与涉及水资源综合利用的相关专业规划以及依法制定的城乡规划、环境保护规划等其他相关规划和军事设施保护区划相协调。

**Article 6** Waterway planning shall be divided into national waterway planning, basin waterway planning, regional waterway planning, and provincial waterway planning for provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
A waterway plan shall include the functional positioning of the waterway, the planning objectives, the technical grades for development planning, the implementation steps, and the safeguard measures.  
A waterway plan shall conform to the basin and regional comprehensive plans formulated according to law, be consistent with water resources planning, flood control planning, and marine functional zoning, and be coordinated with other relevant special plans involving comprehensive utilization of water resources, as well as other relevant plans such as urban and rural plans and environmental protection plans formulated according to law, and military facility protection zoning.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　航道应当划分技术等级。航道技术等级包括现状技术等级和发展规划技术等级。航道发展规划技术等级根据相关自然条件以及防洪、供水、水资源保护、生态环境保护要求和航运发展需求等因素评定。

**Article 7** Waterways shall be classified into technical grades. The technical grades of waterways include the current technical grade and the planned technical grade for development. The planned technical grade for development of a waterway shall be evaluated on the basis of factors such as relevant natural conditions, flood control, water supply, water resources protection, ecological and environmental protection requirements, and shipping development needs.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　全国航道规划由国务院交通运输主管部门会同国务院发展改革部门、国务院水行政主管部门等部门编制，报国务院批准公布。流域航道规划、区域航道规划由国务院交通运输主管部门编制并公布。  
省、自治区、直辖市航道规划由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通运输主管部门会同同级发展改革部门、水行政主管部门等部门编制，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府会同国务院交通运输主管部门批准公布。  
编制航道规划应当征求有关部门和有关军事机关的意见，并依法进行环境影响评价。涉及海域、重要渔业水域的，应当有同级海洋主管部门、渔业行政主管部门参加。编制全国航道规划和流域航道规划、区域航道规划应当征求相关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的意见。  
流域航道规划、区域航道规划和省、自治区、直辖市航道规划应当符合全国航道规划。

**Article 8** The national waterway plan shall be formulated by the competent department of transport under the State Council jointly with the development and reform department under the State Council, the water administrative department under the State Council, and other departments, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval and promulgation. Basin waterway plans and regional waterway plans shall be formulated and promulgated by the competent department of transport under the State Council.  
Provincial waterway plans for provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be formulated by the competent department of transport under the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the development and reform department and the water administrative department at the same level, and shall be submitted to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the competent department of transport under the State Council for approval and promulgation.  
In formulating a waterway plan, the opinions of the relevant departments and relevant military organs shall be solicited, and an environmental impact assessment shall be conducted according to law. Where sea areas or important fishing waters are involved, the marine administrative department and the fishery administrative department at the same level shall participate. In formulating the national waterway plan, basin waterway plans, and regional waterway plans, the opinions of the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be solicited.  
Basin waterway plans, regional waterway plans, and provincial waterway plans for provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall conform to the national waterway plan.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　依法制定并公布的航道规划应当依照执行；航道规划确需修改的，依照规划编制程序办理。

**Article 9** The waterway plan formulated and promulgated according to law shall be implemented accordingly; if a waterway plan indeed needs to be amended, it shall be handled in accordance with the planning formulation procedures.

### 第三章　航道建设

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　新建航道以及为改善航道通航条件而进行的航道工程建设，应当遵守法律、行政法规关于建设工程质量管理、安全管理和生态环境保护的规定，符合航道规划，执行有关的国家标准、行业标准和技术规范，依法办理相关手续。

**Article 10** The construction of new waterways and the construction of waterway projects for improving the navigation conditions of existing waterways shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on the quality management, safety management, and ecological and environmental protection of construction projects, conform to the waterway plan, implement the relevant national standards, industry standards, and technical specifications, and go through the relevant procedures according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　航道建设单位应当根据航道建设工程的技术要求，依法通过招标等方式选择具有相应资质的勘察、设计、施工和监理单位进行工程建设，对工程质量和安全进行监督检查，并对工程质量和安全负责。  
从事航道工程建设的勘察、设计、施工和监理单位，应当依照法律、行政法规的规定取得相应的资质，并在其资质等级许可的范围内从事航道工程建设活动，依法对勘察、设计、施工、监理的质量和安全负责。

**Article 11** The waterway construction entity shall, in accordance with the technical requirements of the waterway construction project, select, through bidding or other means according to law, survey, design, construction, and supervision units with corresponding qualifications to carry out the project construction, conduct supervision and inspection over the project quality and safety, and shall be responsible for the project quality and safety.  
The survey, design, construction, and supervision units engaged in waterway construction projects shall obtain corresponding qualifications in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, and engage in waterway construction activities within the scope permitted by their qualification grades, and shall be responsible for the quality and safety of the survey, design, construction, and supervision according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　有关县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门应当加强对航道建设工程质量和安全的监督检查，保障航道建设工程的质量和安全。

**Article 12** The relevant competent departments of transport under the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and inspection over the quality and safety of waterway construction projects to ensure the quality and safety of waterway construction projects.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　航道建设工程竣工后，应当按照国家有关规定组织竣工验收，经验收合格方可正式投入使用。  
航道建设单位应当自航道建设工程竣工验收合格之日起六十日内，将竣工测量图报送负责航道管理的部门。沿海航道的竣工测量图还应当报送海军航海保证部门。

**Article 13** Upon completion of a waterway construction project, a completion acceptance inspection shall be organized in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and the project may be officially put into use only after it passes the completion acceptance inspection.  
The waterway construction entity shall, within sixty days from the date of passing the completion acceptance inspection of the waterway construction project, submit the completion survey drawing to the department responsible for waterway administration. The completion survey drawing of a coastal waterway shall also be submitted to the naval navigation guarantee department.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　进行航道工程建设应当维护河势稳定，符合防洪要求，不得危及依法建设的其他工程或者设施的安全。因航道工程建设损坏依法建设的其他工程或者设施的，航道建设单位应当予以修复或者依法赔偿。

**Article 14** In carrying out waterway construction projects, the river regime shall be kept stable, the flood control requirements shall be met, and the safety of other projects or facilities constructed according to law shall not be endangered. Where other projects or facilities constructed according to law are damaged due to waterway construction, the waterway construction entity shall repair them or make compensation according to law.

### 第四章　航道养护

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院交通运输主管部门应当制定航道养护技术规范。  
负责航道管理的部门应当按照航道养护技术规范进行航道养护，保证航道处于良好通航技术状态。

**Article 15** The competent department of transport under the State Council shall formulate technical specifications for waterway maintenance.  
The department responsible for waterway administration shall carry out waterway maintenance in accordance with the technical specifications for waterway maintenance to ensure that the waterway is maintained in a good technical condition for navigation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　负责航道管理的部门应当根据航道现状技术等级或者航道自然条件确定并公布航道维护尺度和内河航道图。  
航道维护尺度是指航道在不同水位期应当保持的水深、宽度、弯曲半径等技术要求。

**Article 16** The department responsible for waterway administration shall, in accordance with the current technical grade of the waterway or the natural conditions of the waterway, determine and announce the waterway maintenance dimensions and the inland waterway chart.  
"Waterway maintenance dimensions" mean the technical requirements such as water depth, width, and radius of curvature that shall be maintained by the waterway during different water level periods.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　负责航道管理的部门应当按照国务院交通运输主管部门的规定对航道进行巡查，发现航道实际尺度达不到航道维护尺度或者有其他不符合保证船舶通航安全要求的情形，应当进行维护，及时发布航道通告并通报海事管理机构。

**Article 17** The department responsible for waterway administration shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of transport under the State Council, conduct inspections of the waterway. Where it discovers that the actual dimensions of the waterway fall below the waterway maintenance dimensions or that there are other circumstances that do not meet the requirements for ensuring the navigation safety of ships, it shall carry out maintenance, promptly issue a waterway notice, and notify the maritime administrative agency.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　海事管理机构发现航道损毁等危及通航安全的情形，应当及时通报负责航道管理的部门，并采取必要的安全保障措施。  
其他单位和人员发现航道损毁等危及通航安全的情形，应当及时报告负责航道管理的部门或者海事管理机构。

**Article 18** Where a maritime administrative agency discovers a situation such as damage to the waterway that endangers navigation safety, it shall promptly notify the department responsible for waterway administration and take necessary safety assurance measures.  
Where other entities or individuals discover a situation such as damage to the waterway that endangers navigation safety, they shall promptly report to the department responsible for waterway administration or the maritime administrative agency.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　负责航道管理的部门应当合理安排航道养护作业，避免限制通航的集中作业和在通航高峰期作业。  
负责航道管理的部门进行航道疏浚、清障等影响通航的航道养护活动，或者确需限制通航的养护作业的，应当设置明显的作业标志，采取必要的安全措施，并提前通报海事管理机构，保证过往船舶通行以及依法建设的工程设施的安全。养护作业结束后，应当及时清除影响航道通航条件的作业标志及其他残留物，恢复正常通航。

**Article 19** The department responsible for waterway administration shall reasonably arrange waterway maintenance operations, avoiding concentrated operations that restrict navigation and operations during peak navigation periods.  
Where the department responsible for waterway administration carries out waterway maintenance activities that affect navigation, such as dredging or obstruction removal, or maintenance operations that indeed require restricting navigation, it shall set up obvious operation markers, take necessary safety measures, and notify the maritime administrative agency in advance to ensure the passage of passing ships and the safety of engineering facilities constructed according to law. After the completion of maintenance operations, the operation markers and other residues that affect the navigation conditions of the waterway shall be promptly removed to restore normal navigation.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　进行航道养护作业可能造成航道堵塞的，有关负责航道管理的部门应当会同海事管理机构事先通报相关区域负责航道管理的部门和海事管理机构，共同制定船舶疏导方案，并向社会公告。

**Article 20** Where waterway maintenance operations may cause waterway blockage, the relevant department responsible for waterway administration shall, jointly with the maritime administrative agency, notify in advance the departments responsible for waterway administration and the maritime administrative agencies in the relevant areas, jointly formulate a ship diversion plan, and make a public announcement.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　因自然灾害、事故灾难等突发事件造成航道损坏、阻塞的，负责航道管理的部门应当按照突发事件应急预案尽快修复抢通；必要时由县级以上人民政府组织尽快修复抢通。  
船舶、设施或者其他物体在航道水域中沉没，影响航道畅通和通航安全的，其所有人或者经营人应当立即报告负责航道管理的部门和海事管理机构，按照规定自行或者委托负责航道管理的部门或者海事管理机构代为设置标志，并应当在海事管理机构限定的时间内打捞清除。

**Article 21** Where a waterway is damaged or blocked due to emergencies such as natural disasters or accidents, the department responsible for waterway administration shall, in accordance with the emergency response plan for emergencies, carry out repair and clearance as soon as possible; if necessary, the people's government at or above the county level shall organize repair and clearance as soon as possible.  
Where a ship, facility, or other object sinks in waterway waters, affecting the unimpeded flow and navigation safety of the waterway, the owner or operator shall immediately report to the department responsible for waterway administration and the maritime administrative agency, and shall, in accordance with regulations, set up markers by itself or entrust the department responsible for waterway administration or the maritime administrative agency to set up markers on its behalf, and shall salvage and remove the object within the time limit specified by the maritime administrative agency.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　航标的设置、养护、保护和管理，依照有关法律、行政法规和国家标准或者行业标准的规定执行。

**Article 22** The setting, maintenance, protection, and management of navigation aids shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws, administrative regulations, and national standards or industry standards.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　部队执行任务、战备训练需要使用航道的，负责航道管理的部门应当给予必要的支持和协助。

**Article 23** Where troops need to use waterways for performing tasks or combat readiness training, the department responsible for waterway administration shall provide necessary support and assistance.

### 第五章　航道保护

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　新建、改建、扩建（以下统称建设）跨越、穿越航道的桥梁、隧道、管道、缆线等建筑物、构筑物，应当符合该航道发展规划技术等级对通航净高、净宽、埋设深度等航道通航条件的要求。

**Article 24** The new construction, reconstruction, or expansion (hereinafter collectively referred to as "construction") of buildings or structures such as bridges, tunnels, pipelines, or cables that cross or span a waterway shall meet the requirements of the planned technical grade for the development of the waterway regarding the navigable clearance height, navigable clearance width, burial depth, and other navigation conditions of the waterway.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　在通航河流上建设永久性拦河闸坝，建设单位应当按照航道发展规划技术等级建设通航建筑物。通航建筑物应当与主体工程同步规划、同步设计、同步建设、同步验收、同步投入使用。  
闸坝建设期间难以维持航道原有通航能力的，建设单位应当采取修建临时航道、安排翻坝转运等补救措施，所需费用由建设单位承担。  
在不通航河流上建设闸坝后可以通航的，闸坝建设单位应当同步建设通航建筑物或者预留通航建筑物位置，通航建筑物建设费用除国家另有规定外，由交通运输主管部门承担。  
通航建筑物的运行应当适应船舶通行需要，运行方案应当经负责航道管理的部门同意并公布。通航建筑物的建设单位或者管理单位应当按照规定维护保养通航建筑物，保持其正常运行。

**Article 25** Where a permanent barrage lock is built on a navigable river, the construction entity shall construct navigation structures in accordance with the planned technical grade for the development of the waterway. The navigation structures shall be planned, designed, constructed, accepted, and put into use simultaneously with the main project.  
Where it is difficult to maintain the original navigable capacity of the waterway during the construction of the barrage lock, the construction entity shall take remedial measures such as constructing temporary waterways or arranging transshipment over the lock, and the costs incurred shall be borne by the construction entity.  
Where a barrage lock built on a non-navigable river can become navigable after construction, the construction entity of the barrage lock shall simultaneously construct navigation structures or reserve space for navigation structures; unless otherwise provided by the State, the construction costs of the navigation structures shall be borne by the competent department of transport.  
The operation of navigation structures shall meet the needs of ship passage, and the operation plan shall be approved by the department responsible for waterway administration and made public. The construction entity or management entity of the navigation structures shall, in accordance with regulations, maintain and service the navigation structures to keep them in normal operation.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　在航道保护范围内建设临河、临湖、临海建筑物或者构筑物，应当符合该航道通航条件的要求。  
航道保护范围由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门会同水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构、国土资源主管部门根据航道发展规划技术等级和航道保护实际需要划定，报本级人民政府批准公布。国务院交通运输主管部门直接管理的航道的航道保护范围，由国务院交通运输主管部门会同国务院水行政主管部门、国务院国土资源主管部门和有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府划定公布。航道保护范围涉及海域、重要渔业水域的，还应当分别会同同级海洋主管部门、渔业行政主管部门划定。

**Article 26** Where a building or structure adjacent to a river, lake, or sea is constructed within the waterway protection scope, it shall meet the requirements of the navigation conditions of the waterway.  
The waterway protection scope shall be delineated by the competent department of transport under the local people's government at or above the county level jointly with the water administrative department or the basin administration authority, and the land and resources department, on the basis of the planned technical grade for the development of the waterway and the actual needs for waterway protection, and shall be submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval and promulgation. The waterway protection scope for waterways directly administered by the competent department of transport under the State Council shall be delineated and promulgated by the competent department of transport under the State Council jointly with the water administrative department under the State Council, the land and resources department under the State Council, and the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government. Where the waterway protection scope involves sea areas or important fishing waters, it shall also be delineated jointly with the marine administrative department and the fishery administrative department at the same level respectively.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　建设[本法第二十四条](#art-24)、第二十五条第一款、第二十六条第一款规定的工程（以下统称与航道有关的工程），除依照法律、行政法规或者国务院规定进行的防洪、供水等特殊工程外，不得因工程建设降低航道通航条件。

**Article 27** Where the projects prescribed in Articles 24, 25(1), and 26(1) of this Law (hereinafter collectively referred to as "projects related to waterways") are constructed, except for special projects such as flood control and water supply carried out in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, or the provisions of the State Council, the navigation conditions of the waterway shall not be reduced due to the project construction.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　建设与航道有关的工程，建设单位应当在工程可行性研究阶段就建设项目对航道通航条件的影响作出评价，并报送有审核权的交通运输主管部门或者航道管理机构审核，但下列工程除外：  
（一）临河、临湖的中小河流治理工程；  
（二）不通航河流上建设的水工程；  
（三）现有水工程的水毁修复、除险加固、不涉及通航建筑物和不改变航道原通航条件的更新改造等不影响航道通航条件的工程。  
建设单位报送的航道通航条件影响评价材料不符合本法规定的，可以进行补充或者修改，重新报送审核部门审核。  
未进行航道通航条件影响评价或者经审核部门审核认为建设项目不符合本法规定的，建设单位不得建设。政府投资项目未进行航道通航条件影响评价或者经审核部门审核认为建设项目不符合本法规定的，负责建设项目审批的部门不予批准。

**Article 28** Where a project related to a waterway is constructed, the construction entity shall, at the stage of the feasibility study of the project, evaluate the impact of the construction project on the navigation conditions of the waterway, and submit the evaluation to the competent department of transport or the waterway administration authority with review authority for review, except for the following projects:  
(1) small and medium-sized river improvement projects adjacent to rivers or lakes;  
(2) hydraulic engineering projects constructed on non-navigable rivers;  
(3) projects such as repair of water-damaged existing hydraulic engineering, reinforcement and risk elimination, and renovation and upgrading that do not involve navigation structures and do not change the original navigation conditions of the waterway, and that do not affect the navigation conditions of the waterway.  
Where the materials for the evaluation of the impact on the navigation conditions of the waterway submitted by the construction entity do not comply with the provisions of this Law, they may be supplemented or revised, and resubmitted to the review authority for review.  
If no evaluation of the impact on the navigation conditions of the waterway has been conducted, or the review authority has reviewed and found that the construction project does not comply with the provisions of this Law, the construction entity shall not construct the project. For a government-invested project, if no evaluation of the impact on the navigation conditions of the waterway has been conducted, or the review authority has reviewed and found that the construction project does not comply with the provisions of this Law, the department responsible for approving the construction project shall not grant approval.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国务院或者国务院有关部门批准、核准的建设项目，以及与国务院交通运输主管部门直接管理的航道有关的建设项目的航道通航条件影响评价，由国务院交通运输主管部门审核；其他建设项目的航道通航条件影响评价，按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的规定由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门或者航道管理机构审核。

**Article 29** The evaluation of the impact on the navigation conditions of the waterway for construction projects approved or authorized by the State Council or the relevant departments under the State Council, and for construction projects related to waterways directly administered by the competent department of transport under the State Council, shall be reviewed by the competent department of transport under the State Council; the evaluation of the impact on the navigation conditions of the waterway for other construction projects shall be reviewed by the competent department of transport under the local people's government at or above the county level or the waterway administration authority, in accordance with the provisions of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　航道上相邻拦河闸坝之间的航道通航水位衔接，应当符合国家规定的通航标准和技术要求。位于航道及其上游支流上的水工程，应当在设计、施工和调度运行中统筹考虑下游航道设计最低通航水位所需的下泄流量，但水文条件超出实际标准的除外。  
保障下游航道通航所需的最小下泄流量以及满足航道通航条件允许的水位变化的确定，应当征求负责航道管理的部门的意见。  
水工程需大幅度减流或者大流量泄水的，应当提前通报负责航道管理的部门和海事管理机构，给船舶避让留出合理的时间。

**Article 30** The connection of navigation water levels between adjacent barrage locks on a waterway shall meet the national navigable standards and technical requirements. A hydraulic engineering project located on the waterway or its upstream tributaries shall, in the design, construction, and operation scheduling, take into overall consideration the downstream flow required for the design minimum navigation water level of the downstream waterway, except where the hydrological conditions exceed the actual standards.  
The determination of the minimum downstream flow required to guarantee downstream navigation and the water level changes allowed by the navigation conditions of the waterway shall be subject to the opinions of the department responsible for waterway administration.  
Where a hydraulic engineering project needs to substantially reduce flow or discharge a large volume of water, it shall notify the department responsible for waterway administration and the maritime administrative agency in advance to leave reasonable time for ships to avoid.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　与航道有关的工程施工影响航道正常功能的，负责航道管理的部门、海事管理机构应当根据需要对航标或者航道的位置、走向进行临时调整；影响消除后应当及时恢复。所需费用由建设单位承担，但因防洪抢险工程引起调整的除外。

**Article 31** Where the construction of a project related to a waterway affects the normal functions of the waterway, the department responsible for waterway administration and the maritime administrative agency shall, as needed, make temporary adjustments to the position or alignment of navigation aids or the waterway; after the impact is eliminated, they shall promptly restore them. The costs incurred shall be borne by the construction entity, except where the adjustment is caused by flood control and emergency rescue projects.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　与航道有关的工程竣工验收前，建设单位应当及时清除影响航道通航条件的临时设施及其残留物。

**Article 32** Before the completion acceptance inspection of a project related to a waterway, the construction entity shall promptly remove the temporary facilities and their residues that affect the navigation conditions of the waterway.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　与航道有关的工程建设活动不得危及航道安全。  
与航道有关的工程建设活动损坏航道的，建设单位应当予以修复或者依法赔偿。

**Article 33** The construction activities of projects related to waterways shall not endanger the safety of the waterway.  
Where the construction activities of a project related to a waterway damage the waterway, the construction entity shall repair it or make compensation according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　在通航水域上建设桥梁等建筑物，建设单位应当按照国家有关规定和技术要求设置航标等设施，并承担相应费用。  
桥区水上航标由负责航道管理的部门、海事管理机构负责管理维护。

**Article 34** Where a building such as a bridge is constructed on navigable waters, the construction entity shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and technical requirements, set up navigation aids and other facilities, and bear the corresponding costs.  
The waterway navigation aids in the bridge area shall be managed and maintained by the department responsible for waterway administration and the maritime administrative agency.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　禁止下列危害航道通航安全的行为：  
（一）在航道内设置渔具或者水产养殖设施的；  
（二）在航道和航道保护范围内倾倒砂石、泥土、垃圾以及其他废弃物的；  
（三）在通航建筑物及其引航道和船舶调度区内从事货物装卸、水上加油、船舶维修、捕鱼等，影响通航建筑物正常运行的；  
（四）危害航道设施安全的；  
（五）其他危害航道通航安全的行为。

**Article 35** The following acts endangering the navigation safety of waterways are prohibited:  
(1) placing fishing gear or aquaculture facilities in the waterway;  
(2) dumping gravel, soil, garbage, or other waste in the waterway or the waterway protection scope;  
(3) engaging in cargo loading and unloading, refueling on water, ship repair, fishing, or other activities in navigation structures, approach channels, and ship dispatch areas that affect the normal operation of navigation structures;  
(4) endangering the safety of waterway facilities;  
(5) other acts endangering the navigation safety of waterways.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　在河道内采砂，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定进行。禁止在河道内依法划定的砂石禁采区采砂、无证采砂、未按批准的范围和作业方式采砂等非法采砂行为。  
在航道和航道保护范围内采砂，不得损害航道通航条件。

**Article 36** Sand mining in river channels shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations. Illegal sand mining activities such as mining in sand mining prohibited areas lawfully designated in river channels, mining without a license, and mining beyond the approved scope and method of operation are prohibited.  
Sand mining in the waterway or the waterway protection scope shall not impair the navigation conditions of the waterway.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　本法施行前建设的拦河闸坝造成通航河流断航，需要恢复通航且具备建设通航建筑物条件的，由发展改革部门会同水行政主管部门、交通运输主管部门提出恢复通航方案，报本级人民政府决定。

**Article 37** Where a barrage lock constructed before the coming into force of this Law has caused the navigable river to become unnavigable, if the navigation needs to be restored and conditions exist for constructing navigation structures, the development and reform department shall, jointly with the water administrative department and the competent department of transport, propose a plan for restoring navigation, and submit it to the people's government at the same level for decision.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　航道建设、勘察、设计、施工、监理单位在航道建设活动中违反本法规定的，由县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门依照有关招标投标和工程建设管理的法律、行政法规的规定处罚。

**Article 38** Where a waterway construction, survey, design, construction, or supervision entity violates the provisions of this Law in waterway construction activities, the competent department of transport under the people's government at or above the county level shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on bidding and project construction management.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　建设单位未依法报送航道通航条件影响评价材料而开工建设的，由有审核权的交通运输主管部门或者航道管理机构责令停止建设，限期补办手续，处三万元以下的罚款；逾期不补办手续继续建设的，由有审核权的交通运输主管部门或者航道管理机构责令恢复原状，处二十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。  
报送的航道通航条件影响评价材料未通过审核，建设单位开工建设的，由有审核权的交通运输主管部门或者航道管理机构责令停止建设、恢复原状，处二十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。  
违反航道通航条件影响评价的规定建成的项目导致航道通航条件严重下降的，由前两款规定的交通运输主管部门或者航道管理机构责令限期采取补救措施或者拆除；逾期未采取补救措施或者拆除的，由交通运输主管部门或者航道管理机构代为采取补救措施或者依法组织拆除，所需费用由建设单位承担。

**Article 39** Where a construction entity commences construction without submitting the materials for the evaluation of the impact on the navigation conditions of the waterway according to law, the competent department of transport or the waterway administration authority with review authority shall order the suspension of construction, require the entity to go through the formalities within a specified time limit, and impose a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan; if the entity fails to go through the formalities within the specified time limit and continues construction, the competent department of transport or the waterway administration authority with review authority shall order restoration to the original state and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.  
Where the submitted materials for the evaluation of the impact on the navigation conditions of the waterway fail the review and the construction entity commences construction, the competent department of transport or the waterway administration authority with review authority shall order suspension of construction and restoration to the original state, and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.  
Where a project constructed in violation of the provisions on the evaluation of the impact on the navigation conditions of the waterway causes a serious reduction in the navigation conditions of the waterway, the competent department of transport or the waterway administration authority specified in the preceding two paragraphs shall order the entity to take remedial measures or demolish the project within a specified time limit; if the entity fails to take remedial measures or demolish the project within the specified time limit, the competent department of transport or the waterway administration authority shall take remedial measures or organize demolition on behalf of the entity according to law, and the costs incurred shall be borne by the construction entity.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　与航道有关的工程的建设单位违反本法规定，未及时清除影响航道通航条件的临时设施及其残留物的，由负责航道管理的部门责令限期清除，处二万元以下的罚款；逾期仍未清除的，处三万元以上二十万元以下的罚款，并由负责航道管理的部门依法组织清除，所需费用由建设单位承担。

**Article 40** Where the construction entity of a project related to a waterway violates the provisions of this Law by failing to promptly remove temporary facilities and their residues that affect the navigation conditions of the waterway, the department responsible for waterway administration shall order the entity to remove them within a specified time limit and impose a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan; if the entity fails to remove them within the specified time limit, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the department responsible for waterway administration shall organize the removal according to law, with the costs borne by the construction entity.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　在通航水域上建设桥梁等建筑物，建设单位未按照规定设置航标等设施的，由负责航道管理的部门或者海事管理机构责令改正，处五万元以下罚款。

**Article 41** Where a construction entity builds a structure such as a bridge on navigable waters without setting up navigation aids or other facilities in accordance with the regulations, the department responsible for waterway administration or the maritime administrative agency shall order correction and impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由负责航道管理的部门责令改正，对单位处五万元以下罚款，对个人处二千元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
（一）在航道内设置渔具或者水产养殖设施的；  
（二）在航道和航道保护范围内倾倒砂石、泥土、垃圾以及其他废弃物的；  
（三）在通航建筑物及其引航道和船舶调度区内从事货物装卸、水上加油、船舶维修、捕鱼等，影响通航建筑物正常运行的；  
（四）危害航道设施安全的；  
（五）其他危害航道通航安全的行为。

**Article 42** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the department responsible for waterway administration to correct, and a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on the entity, and a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual; if losses are caused, the person shall bear compensation liability according to law:  
(1) placing fishing gear or aquaculture facilities in the waterway;  
(2) dumping gravel, soil, garbage, or other waste in the waterway or the waterway protection scope;  
(3) engaging in cargo loading and unloading, refueling on water, ship repair, fishing, or other activities in navigation structures, approach channels, and ship dispatch areas that affect the normal operation of navigation structures;  
(4) endangering the safety of waterway facilities;  
(5) other acts endangering the navigation safety of waterways.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　在河道内依法划定的砂石禁采区采砂、无证采砂、未按批准的范围和作业方式采砂等非法采砂的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定处罚。  
违反本法规定，在航道和航道保护范围内采砂，损害航道通航条件的，由负责航道管理的部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，可以扣押或者没收非法采砂船舶，并处五万元以上三十万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 43** Illegal sand mining such as mining in sand mining prohibited areas lawfully designated in river channels, mining without a license, and mining beyond the approved scope and method of operation shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, mines sand in the waterway or the waterway protection scope, impairing the navigation conditions of the waterway, shall be ordered by the department responsible for waterway administration to cease the illegal act, confiscate the illegal gains, may have the illegal sand mining vessel seized or confiscated, and shall be imposed a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if losses are caused, the person shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反法律规定，污染环境、破坏生态或者有其他环境违法行为的，依照《中华人民共和国环境保护法》等法律的规定处罚。

**Article 44** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of the law, pollutes the environment, damages the ecology, or commits other environmental violations shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Environmental Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and other laws.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　交通运输主管部门以及其他有关部门不依法履行本法规定的职责的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。  
负责航道管理的机构不依法履行本法规定的职责的，由其上级主管部门责令改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 45** Where the competent department of transport or other relevant departments fail to perform their duties under this Law according to law, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.  
Where the institution responsible for waterway administration fails to perform its duties under this Law according to law, its superior competent department shall order correction, and the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 46** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, constitutes a violation of public security administration shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　进出军事港口、渔业港口的专用航道不适用本法。专用航道由专用部门管理。

**Article 47** This Law does not apply to specialized waterways entering military ports or fishing ports. Specialized waterways shall be managed by the specialized departments.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本法自2015年3月1日起施行。

**Article 48** This Law shall come into force as of March 1, 2015.
