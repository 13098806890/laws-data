# 中华人民共和国原子能法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2025-09-12  
**生效日期**：2026-01-15

> （2025年9月12日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十七次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　科学研究与技术开发 第三章　核燃料循环 第四章　利　　用 第一节　核反应堆应用 第二节　核技术应用 第五章　安全监督管理 第六章　进出口 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保障原子能研究、开发与和平利用，推进科技进步和产业提升，促进经济社会高质量发展，维护国家安全，增进人民福祉，制定本法。 第二条　本法适用于中华人民共和国领域及中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域内的原子能研究、开发和利用活动。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障原子能研究、开发与和平利用，推进科技进步和产业提升，促进经济社会高质量发展，维护国家安全，增进人民福祉，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted to ensure the research, development and peaceful use of atomic energy, promote scientific and technological progress and industrial upgrading, facilitate high-quality economic and social development, safeguard national security, and enhance the well-being of the people.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法适用于中华人民共和国领域及中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域内的原子能研究、开发和利用活动。  
本法所称原子能，也称核能，是指裂变、聚变、衰变等核反应释放的能量。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to activities of atomic energy research, development and utilization within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.  
Atomic energy as mentioned in this Law, also known as nuclear energy, means the energy released from nuclear reactions such as fission, fusion and decay.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　从事原子能研究、开发和利用活动，应当坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻总体国家安全观，坚持理性、协调、并进的核安全观，坚持底线思维与系统观念，统筹发展和安全，保护生态环境，保障人身健康和生命财产安全。

**Article 3** In conducting atomic energy research, development and utilization activities, the leadership of the Communist Party of China shall be adhered to, the holistic approach to national security shall be implemented, the rational, coordinated and balanced nuclear safety philosophy shall be upheld, bottom-line thinking and a systematic approach shall be maintained, development and security shall be coordinated, the ecological environment shall be protected, and human health and the safety of life and property shall be ensured.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　原子能科技与产业发展应当坚持创新驱动发展战略和绿色发展、可持续发展战略。  
国家加强原子能产业发展统筹规划，合理安排原子能产业整体布局。

**Article 4** The development of atomic energy science and technology and the atomic energy industry shall adhere to the innovation-driven development strategy and the strategies of green development and sustainable development.  
The State shall strengthen the overall planning for the development of the atomic energy industry and rationally arrange the overall layout of the atomic energy industry.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院核工业主管部门、能源主管部门、核安全监督管理部门按照职责分工负责原子能研究、开发和利用活动的管理和监督。  
国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关原子能研究、开发和利用活动的管理和监督工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门对本行政区域内的原子能研究、开发和利用活动予以支持，按照法律、行政法规的规定履行相应的管理和监督职责。

**Article 5** The nuclear industry competent authority, the energy competent authority, and the nuclear safety regulatory authority under the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the management and supervision of atomic energy research, development and utilization activities.  
Other relevant departments under the State Council shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the management and supervision of relevant atomic energy research, development and utilization activities.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall support atomic energy research, development and utilization activities within their respective administrative regions and perform corresponding management and supervision duties in accordance with laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院有关部门和有关地方人民政府对原子能研究、开发和利用活动，在科学技术奖励、产业发展、财政、税费等方面，按照国家规定给予政策支持。

**Article 6** The relevant departments under the State Council and relevant local people's governments shall, in respect of atomic energy research, development and utilization activities, provide policy support in such aspects as scientific and technological awards, industrial development, finance, taxes and fees, in accordance with state regulations.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　有关行政机关、企业事业单位应当依法做好原子能研究、开发和利用活动中安全监督管理、环境影响评价等方面的信息公开工作。  
对原子能研究、开发和利用活动中影响公众利益的重大事项，有关行政机关、企业事业单位应当加强与公众的沟通，征求利益相关方的意见，并以适当形式反馈。  
前两款规定的信息公开和公众参与涉及国家秘密、商业秘密、个人信息的，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 7** Relevant administrative organs, enterprises and institutions shall, according to law, properly carry out information disclosure regarding safety supervision and management, environmental impact assessment, etc. in atomic energy research, development and utilization activities.  
For major matters affecting the public interest in atomic energy research, development and utilization activities, relevant administrative organs, enterprises and institutions shall strengthen communication with the public, solicit opinions from interested parties, and provide feedback in an appropriate manner.  
Where the information disclosure and public participation provided in the preceding two paragraphs involve state secrets, trade secrets or personal information, the matter shall be handled in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家加强原子能科学知识和法律知识的宣传教育，鼓励有条件的地方人民政府、核设施营运单位以及其他有关单位建设和利用科普场馆、设施，开展原子能科学知识普及活动。

**Article 8** The State shall strengthen publicity and education on atomic energy scientific knowledge and legal knowledge, and encourage local people's governments, nuclear facility operating entities and other relevant entities with appropriate conditions to build and utilize science popularization venues and facilities to carry out activities for popularizing atomic energy scientific knowledge.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家支持和平利用原子能，鼓励和平利用原子能的国际交流与合作，促进共享和平利用原子能事业成果。  
中华人民共和国履行缔结或者参加的国际条约所规定的义务，反对和禁止一切形式的核扩散活动，防范和应对核恐怖主义威胁，推动构建公平、合作、共赢的国际核安全体系。

**Article 9** The State supports the peaceful use of atomic energy, encourages international exchange and cooperation in the peaceful use of atomic energy, and promotes the sharing of achievements in the peaceful use of atomic energy.  
The People's Republic of China shall fulfill the obligations provided for in international treaties concluded or acceded to by it, oppose and prohibit all forms of nuclear proliferation activities, prevent and respond to nuclear terrorism threats, and promote the building of a fair, cooperative and mutually beneficial international nuclear safety system.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家加强原子能领域标准体系建设，推动、参与原子能领域国际标准化活动。

**Article 10** The State shall strengthen the development of the standards system in the atomic energy field, and promote and participate in international standardization activities in the atomic energy field.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对在原子能研究、开发和利用活动中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 11** Entities and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to atomic energy research, development and utilization activities shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with relevant state regulations.

### 第二章　科学研究与技术开发

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家加强原子能科学研究与技术开发，强化基础研究，探索前沿技术，推进学科交叉融合，鼓励自主研发，加强知识产权保护，强化国家战略科技力量建设，促进原子能领域高素质专业人才队伍建设。  
原子能领域专业人才应当坚持立德为先、诚信为本，弘扬科学家精神，遵守学术和伦理规范，恪守职业道德。

**Article 12** The State shall strengthen atomic energy scientific research and technological development, reinforce basic research, explore frontier technologies, promote interdisciplinary integration, encourage independent research and development, strengthen intellectual property protection, enhance the development of national strategic scientific and technological capabilities, and promote the building of a contingent of high-quality professional personnel in the atomic energy field.  
Professional personnel in the atomic energy field shall give priority to moral integrity, take honesty as the foundation, promote the spirit of scientists, abide by academic and ethical norms, and adhere to professional ethics.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家建立原子能领域相关科技创新基地和平台，加强原子能科研设施和科研条件保障能力建设，推动科研设施、资源开放共享，促进科技成果转化。

**Article 13** The State shall establish relevant scientific and technological innovation bases and platforms in the atomic energy field, strengthen the capacity building for atomic energy research facilities and research conditions support, promote the opening and sharing of research facilities and resources, and facilitate the transformation of scientific and technological achievements.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家鼓励和支持受控热核聚变的科学研究与技术开发。

**Article 14** The State encourages and supports scientific research and technological development of controlled thermonuclear fusion.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家制定并实施原子能科学研究与技术开发专项规划，促进核燃料循环、核反应堆、核技术应用等领域先进技术的研究与开发，提升原子能科学技术水平。  
原子能科学研究与技术开发专项规划应当统筹基础理论、前沿领域、市场应用类研究项目，兼顾原子能行业相关发展需求，提高原子能产业的安全性、经济性和可持续性。

**Article 15** The State shall formulate and implement special plans for atomic energy scientific research and technological development, promote the research and development of advanced technologies in such fields as nuclear fuel cycle, nuclear reactors, and nuclear technology application, and enhance the level of atomic energy science and technology.  
The special plans for atomic energy scientific research and technological development shall coordinate research projects in basic theory, frontier fields and market application, take into account the relevant development needs of the atomic energy industry, and improve the safety, economy and sustainability of the atomic energy industry.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家公开征集原子能科学研究与技术开发需求建议，发布项目申报指南，鼓励科研院所、高等学校、企业等单位开展原子能科学研究与技术开发。  
国家发挥财政性资金投入的引导作用，鼓励、带动承担或者参与原子能科学研究与技术开发工作的单位加大资金投入，优化资金投入结构，提高资金投入效益。  
国家支持企业组织原子能科研项目开发，发挥企业在技术创新中的主体作用，支持企业自主研发的先进技术示范应用。

**Article 16** The State shall publicly solicit proposals for atomic energy scientific research and technological development needs, issue project application guidelines, and encourage research institutes, institutions of higher learning, enterprises and other entities to carry out atomic energy scientific research and technological development.  
The State shall play the guiding role of financial fund investment, encourage and drive entities undertaking or participating in atomic energy scientific research and technological development to increase fund investment, optimize the fund investment structure, and improve the efficiency of fund investment.  
The State supports enterprises in organizing the development of atomic energy research projects, giving play to the principal role of enterprises in technological innovation, and supporting the demonstration and application of advanced technologies independently developed by enterprises.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家加强原子能领域科学技术规划计划的衔接，完善科学技术资源、技术开发需求的交流机制，发挥市场配置资源的作用。

**Article 17** The State shall strengthen the coordination of scientific and technological plans and programs in the atomic energy field, improve the exchange mechanism for scientific and technological resources and technological development needs, and give play to the role of the market in allocating resources.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家推动建立和完善与原子能发展相适应的设备研制生产体系，鼓励和支持企业形成自主研发、设计、制造能力。

**Article 18** The State shall promote the establishment and improvement of an equipment research, development and production system commensurate with the development of atomic energy, and encourage and support enterprises in developing independent research, design and manufacturing capabilities.

### 第三章　核燃料循环

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家建立完整的核燃料循环体系，对乏燃料实行循环利用，妥善处理处置放射性废物。  
核燃料循环体系包括铀（钍）矿勘查、开采冶炼、纯化转化、同位素分离、燃料元件制造、乏燃料后处理等环节。

**Article 19** The State shall establish a complete nuclear fuel cycle system, implement the recycling of spent fuel, and properly treat and dispose of radioactive waste.  
The nuclear fuel cycle system includes such stages as uranium (thorium) prospecting, mining and smelting, purification and conversion, isotope separation, fuel element fabrication, and spent fuel reprocessing.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院核工业主管部门负责组织编制核燃料循环中长期发展专项规划，统筹核燃料生产能力和布局。

**Article 20** The nuclear industry competent authority under the State Council shall be responsible for organizing the formulation of special plans for the medium- and long-term development of the nuclear fuel cycle, and coordinate the production capacity and layout of nuclear fuel.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　核燃料循环设施建设项目由国务院核工业主管部门依法审批或者核准，重大项目报国务院审批或者核准。申请条件、审批程序和时限等按照国家规定执行。

**Article 21** Construction projects of nuclear fuel cycle facilities shall be examined and approved or verified by the nuclear industry competent authority under the State Council according to law; major projects shall be submitted to the State Council for examination and approval or verification. The application conditions, examination and approval procedures and time limits, etc. shall be implemented in accordance with state regulations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家依法加强铀（钍）矿勘查，合理确定铀（钍）矿与共生和伴生矿产资源的勘查和开发秩序，实行保护性开发。

**Article 22** The State shall strengthen the prospecting of uranium (thorium) mines according to law, reasonably determine the order of prospecting and development of uranium (thorium) mines and associated and accompanying mineral resources, and implement protective development.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　经国家批准的单位方可从事核燃料生产经营活动。

**Article 23** Only entities approved by the State may engage in the production and operation of nuclear fuel.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家建立健全乏燃料贮存、运输和后处理等管理制度，统筹规划乏燃料处理处置能力和布局，确保乏燃料的安全、高效和环保处理。  
国家设立核电站乏燃料处理处置基金，其征收使用按照国家规定执行。

**Article 24** The State shall establish and improve management systems for spent fuel storage, transportation and reprocessing, make overall plans for spent fuel treatment and disposal capacity and layout, and ensure the safe, efficient and environmentally friendly treatment of spent fuel.  
The State shall establish a fund for spent fuel treatment and disposal of nuclear power plants, and its levy and use shall be implemented in accordance with state regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　从事原子能研究、开发和利用活动的单位，应当尽可能减少放射性废物的产生量，严格按照有关法律、行政法规和标准的要求，对放射性废物实行分类管理和安全处理处置。  
放射性废物处置场所的建设应当与原子能发展的要求相适应。

**Article 25** Entities engaged in atomic energy research, development and utilization activities shall minimize the generation of radioactive waste, and strictly in accordance with the requirements of relevant laws, administrative regulations and standards, implement classified management and safe treatment and disposal of radioactive waste.  
The construction of radioactive waste disposal sites shall be commensurate with the requirements of atomic energy development.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　在确保国家安全的前提下，国家允许核燃料循环产业相关企业有效利用资本市场，逐步形成核燃料循环产业多元化投入机制。

**Article 26** On the premise of ensuring national security, the State permits enterprises related to the nuclear fuel cycle industry to effectively utilize the capital market, and gradually form a diversified investment mechanism for the nuclear fuel cycle industry.

### 第四章　利用

#### 第一节　核反应堆应用

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家积极安全有序发展核电，规范和促进核反应堆综合利用。  
国家鼓励和支持先进核反应堆的应用。

**Article 27** The State shall actively, safely and orderly develop nuclear power, and regulate and promote the comprehensive utilization of nuclear reactors.  
The State encourages and supports the application of advanced nuclear reactors.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院能源主管部门负责核电管理，组织编制国家核电发展规划。核电发展规划按照规定的程序报国务院批准。核电站建设项目由国务院核准。

**Article 28** The energy competent authority under the State Council shall be responsible for nuclear power management, and organize the formulation of the national nuclear power development plan. The nuclear power development plan shall be submitted to the State Council for approval in accordance with the prescribed procedures. Nuclear power plant construction projects shall be verified by the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家支持核反应堆在动力、供热、海水淡化、制氢、同位素生产以及科研等方面的应用。

**Article 29** The State supports the application of nuclear reactors in such aspects as power generation, heat supply, seawater desalination, hydrogen production, isotope production, and scientific research.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　核反应堆的选址、设计、建造、调试、运行和管理等应当遵守有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 30** The siting, design, construction, commissioning, operation and management of nuclear reactors shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　核设施营运单位应当按照国家规定预提核设施退役费用、放射性废物处置费用，列入投资概算、生产成本，专门用于核设施退役、放射性废物处置。  
承担非营利性任务的核设施退役费用，按照财政事权和支出责任划分原则，由中央和地方财政承担。

**Article 31** A nuclear facility operating entity shall, in accordance with state regulations, set aside in advance the costs for decommissioning of nuclear facilities and disposal of radioactive waste, include them in the investment estimate and production cost, and use them exclusively for decommissioning of nuclear facilities and disposal of radioactive waste.  
The costs for decommissioning of nuclear facilities undertaking non-profit tasks shall be borne by the central and local fiscal authorities in accordance with the principle of dividing fiscal powers and expenditure responsibilities.

#### 第二节　核技术应用

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家支持放射性同位素、射线装置等在工业、农业、生物、医疗卫生、生态环境保护等领域的应用。

**Article 32** The State supports the application of radioactive isotopes, radiation devices, etc. in such fields as industry, agriculture, biology, medical and health care, and ecological and environmental protection.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院核工业、生态环境、公安、卫生健康、疾病预防控制等主管部门按照职责分工加强对核技术应用活动的管理和监督。

**Article 33** The competent authorities under the State Council for nuclear industry, ecological environment, public security, health, disease prevention and control, etc. shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, strengthen the management and supervision of nuclear technology application activities.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院核工业主管部门会同有关部门制定核技术应用产业发展指导意见。  
县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当采取措施，鼓励核技术应用领域先进技术研发、知识产权保护、成果转化示范应用，引导核技术应用生产经营单位有序、公平参与市场竞争。

**Article 34** The nuclear industry competent authority under the State Council shall, jointly with relevant departments, formulate guiding opinions for the development of the nuclear technology application industry.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall take measures to encourage the research and development of advanced technologies, intellectual property protection, demonstration and application of achievements in the nuclear technology application field, and guide the production and operation units of nuclear technology application to participate in market competition in an orderly and fair manner.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家对放射源、射线装置实行分类管理，具体分类办法由国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 35** The State implements classified management of radiation sources and radiation devices, and the specific classification measures shall be formulated by the relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　从事核技术应用生产经营活动的单位，应当依法取得相应的资质，严格执行放射性同位素和射线装置在生产、运输、销售、使用、贮存、处置各环节的管理规定。

**Article 36** A unit engaged in the production and operation of nuclear technology applications shall obtain the corresponding qualifications according to law, and strictly implement the management provisions for radioactive isotopes and radiation devices in each link of production, transportation, sale, use, storage and disposal.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家建立核技术应用废旧放射源回收制度。放射源使用单位应当按照国家规定将废旧放射源交回生产单位、返回原出口方或者送交取得相应资质的单位贮存、处置。  
核技术应用生产经营单位使用放射源的场所和生产放射性同位素的场所，以及终结运行后产生放射性污染的射线装置，应当依法实施退役。

**Article 37** The State shall establish a recycling system for waste radiation sources in nuclear technology applications. A radiation source user entity shall, in accordance with state regulations, hand over waste radiation sources to the production entity, return them to the original exporter, or deliver them to an entity with corresponding qualifications for storage or disposal.  
The premises where a nuclear technology application production and operation entity uses radiation sources and produces radioactive isotopes, as well as radiation devices that generate radioactive contamination after termination of operation, shall undergo decommissioning according to law.

### 第五章　安全监督管理

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　从事原子能研究、开发和利用活动，必须遵循确保安全的方针，按照法律、行政法规的要求，严格落实核安全责任。  
核安全工作必须坚持安全第一、预防为主、责任明确、严格管理、纵深防御、独立监管、全面保障的原则。  
国务院核安全监督管理部门、核工业主管部门在各自职责范围内对原子能研究、开发和利用活动实施核安全监管。

**Article 38** In conducting atomic energy research, development and utilization activities, the policy of ensuring safety must be followed, and nuclear safety responsibilities must be strictly implemented in accordance with the requirements of laws and administrative regulations.  
Nuclear safety work must adhere to the principles of safety first, prevention foremost, clear responsibilities, strict management, defense in depth, independent regulation, and comprehensive assurance.  
The nuclear safety regulatory authority and the nuclear industry competent authority under the State Council shall, within their respective functions and duties, implement nuclear safety supervision over atomic energy research, development and utilization activities.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家建立符合受控热核聚变特点、促进核聚变应用的监督管理制度，对聚变燃料、聚变装置（设施）实行分级分类管理。

**Article 39** The State shall establish a supervision and management system that conforms to the characteristics of controlled thermonuclear fusion and promotes the application of nuclear fusion, and implement graded and classified management of fusion fuels and fusion devices (facilities).

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　从事原子能研究、开发和利用活动的单位，应当遵守国家有关辐射防护的规定，保护公众和从业人员的安全与健康，保护生态环境。

**Article 40** Entities engaged in atomic energy research, development and utilization activities shall comply with the state provisions on radiation protection, protect the safety and health of the public and workers, and protect the ecological environment.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家建立健全核安保制度，加强原子能研究、开发和利用活动的安全保卫工作。  
核材料、核设施、其他放射性物质及相关设施的持有或者营运单位应当依法开展安全保卫工作，防范相关盗窃、破坏、擅自接触、非法转移或者其他危害安全的行为，防范核恐怖主义行为。  
核设施营运单位应当采取必要措施，防范网络攻击、侵入、干扰和破坏，应对网络安全事件，保障核设施安全稳定运行。

**Article 41** The State shall establish and improve the nuclear security system, and strengthen the security protection of atomic energy research, development and utilization activities.  
Entities holding or operating nuclear materials, nuclear facilities, other radioactive substances and related facilities shall carry out security protection work according to law to prevent related theft, sabotage, unauthorized access, illegal transfer or other acts endangering security, and to prevent acts of nuclear terrorism.  
A nuclear facility operating entity shall take necessary measures to prevent cyber attacks, intrusions, interference and sabotage, respond to cyber security incidents, and ensure the safe and stable operation of the nuclear facility.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　从事涉及国家秘密的原子能研究、开发和利用活动的单位，应当依法建立健全保密管理制度，完善保密防护措施，开展保密宣传教育，加强保密监督检查。

**Article 42** Entities engaged in atomic energy research, development and utilization activities involving state secrets shall, according to law, establish and improve a confidentiality management system, improve confidentiality protection measures, carry out confidentiality publicity and education, and strengthen confidentiality supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家对核材料实行严格管制，建立和运行国家核材料衡算与控制系统。  
持有、使用、生产、储存、运输和处置核材料的单位，应当依法取得核材料许可证。

**Article 43** The State exercises strict control over nuclear materials, and establishes and operates a national nuclear material accounting and control system.  
Entities that hold, use, produce, store, transport and dispose of nuclear materials shall obtain a nuclear material license according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家加强运输通道及装备体系建设，保障放射性物品的公路、铁路、水路、航空等运输。  
国务院有关部门对放射性物品运输企业和运输过程实行严格监督和管理。

**Article 44** The State shall strengthen the construction of transport corridors and equipment systems to ensure the transport of radioactive articles by road, railway, waterway, air, etc.  
The relevant departments under the State Council shall exercise strict supervision and management over radioactive article transport enterprises and transport processes.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家设立核事故应急协调委员会，组织、协调全国的核事故应急管理工作，统筹制定国家核事故应急预案，对核事故应急实行分级管理。  
国家建立健全核事故应急准备金制度，保障核事故应急准备与响应工作所需经费。  
国务院有关部门、有关地方人民政府、核设施营运单位应当依法制定核事故应急预案，开展应急演练，做好核事故应急相关工作。  
中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队按照中央军事委员会的规定，做好核事故应急相关工作。

**Article 45** The State shall establish a nuclear accident emergency coordination committee to organize and coordinate the national nuclear accident emergency management, make overall plans for formulating the national nuclear accident emergency plan, and implement graded management of nuclear accident emergency response.  
The State shall establish and improve a nuclear accident emergency reserve fund system to ensure the funds needed for nuclear accident emergency preparedness and response work.  
The relevant departments under the State Council, relevant local people's governments, and nuclear facility operating entities shall, according to law, formulate nuclear accident emergency plans, conduct emergency drills, and properly carry out relevant work for nuclear accident emergencies.  
The People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Central Military Commission, properly carry out relevant work for nuclear accident emergencies.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　发生核事故时，有关单位和个人必须立即采取应急措施，并按照有关法律、行政法规的规定报告，禁止缓报、瞒报、谎报核事故。

**Article 46** When a nuclear accident occurs, the relevant entities and individuals must immediately take emergency measures and report in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; it is prohibited to delay reporting, conceal or make false reports of a nuclear accident.

### 第六章　进出口

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家加强原子能领域进出口管理工作，履行进出口国际义务和承诺，保证进出口物项的和平用途。

**Article 47** The State shall strengthen the import and export management in the atomic energy field, fulfill international obligations and commitments regarding import and export, and ensure the peaceful use of imported and exported items.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家依法对核以及核两用物项出口进行严格管制，实行许可制度。  
核以及核两用物项出口，应当遵守国家有关法律、行政法规的规定，不得损害国家安全或者社会公共利益。

**Article 48** The State exercises strict control over the export of nuclear and dual-use nuclear items according to law, and implements a licensing system.  
The export of nuclear and dual-use nuclear items shall comply with the provisions of relevant state laws and administrative regulations, and shall not harm national security or social public interests.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家严格限制铀浓缩设施、设备，乏燃料后处理设施、设备，重水生产设施、设备等物项及其相关技术等核扩散敏感物项，以及可以用于核爆炸装置的材料的出口。  
国家鼓励和支持企业积极有序参与国际市场开发，推动符合国家有关规定的核电、核燃料相关设备和技术服务出口。

**Article 49** The State strictly restricts the export of items such as uranium enrichment facilities and equipment, spent fuel reprocessing facilities and equipment, heavy water production facilities and equipment, and related technologies, which are sensitive to nuclear proliferation, as well as materials that can be used in nuclear explosive devices.  
The State encourages and supports enterprises to actively and orderly participate in the development of the international market, and promotes the export of nuclear power and nuclear fuel related equipment and technical services that comply with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　核进口单位应当严格执行国家关于核进口的法律、行政法规和其他有关规定，履行核进口承诺义务。  
国务院核工业主管部门负责审查、办理核进口政府承诺事务，管理核进口涉及的保障监督事项。重要的保障监督事项，由国务院核工业主管部门会同外交部门、商务主管部门提出处理意见，必要时应当报国务院审批。

**Article 50** A nuclear importing entity shall strictly implement the state laws, administrative regulations and other relevant provisions on nuclear import, and fulfill the commitment obligations for nuclear import.  
The nuclear industry competent authority under the State Council shall be responsible for examining and handling governmental commitment affairs for nuclear import, and managing safeguard matters involved in nuclear import. For important safeguard matters, the nuclear industry competent authority under the State Council shall, jointly with the foreign affairs department and the commerce competent authority, propose handling opinions, and shall, when necessary, submit them to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　进出口放射性同位素应当遵守法律、行政法规和其他有关规定确定的目录、条件和程序等要求。

**Article 51** : Import and export of radioactive isotopes shall comply with the requirements such as the catalogue, conditions, and procedures determined by laws, administrative regulations, and other relevant provisions.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　禁止将放射性废物和被放射性污染的物品输入中华人民共和国境内或者经中华人民共和国境内转移，法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 52** : It shall be prohibited to import radioactive waste and radioactive contaminated articles into the territory of the People's Republic of China or to transfer them through the territory of the People's Republic of China, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国务院有关部门、县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门，违反本法规定，有下列情形之一，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）未依法对许可申请进行审批或者核准的；  
（二）发现违法行为不予查处的；  
（三）未依法公开原子能安全监督管理、环境影响评价等方面信息的；  
（四）未就原子能研究、开发和利用活动中影响公众利益的重大事项依法征求利益相关方意见的；  
（五）有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为的。

**Article 53** : Where the relevant departments of the State Council, the local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commit any of the following acts, the responsible leaders and directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) Failing to examine or approve a permit application according to law;  
(2) Failing to investigate and punish illegal acts discovered;  
(3) Failing to disclose information on atomic energy safety supervision and management, environmental impact assessment, etc. according to law;  
(4) Failing to solicit opinions from interested parties according to law on major matters affecting the public interest in atomic energy research, development, and utilization activities;  
(5) Other acts of abusing power, neglecting duties, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本法规定，未经批准，擅自从事核燃料生产经营活动的，由国务院核工业主管部门责令停止生产经营活动，没收核燃料及违法所得，处违法生产经营的核燃料市场价值二倍以上十倍以下的罚款；对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，并依法给予处分。

**Article 54** : Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, engages in the production and operation of nuclear fuel without approval shall be ordered by the competent department of nuclear industry of the State Council to cease production and operation activities, confiscate the nuclear fuel and illegal gains, and be imposed a fine of not less than two times but not more than ten times the market value of the nuclear fuel illegally produced and operated; the responsible leaders and directly responsible personnel shall be imposed a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　核材料、核设施、其他放射性物质及相关设施的持有或者营运单位未按照有关法律、行政法规要求开展安全保卫工作的，由国务院核工业主管部门会同有关部门责令限期整改；拒不整改或者经限期整改仍不符合要求的，处一百万元以上五百万元以下的罚款，暂扣或者吊销相关许可证，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，并依法给予处分。

**Article 55** : Where an entity that holds or operates nuclear materials, nuclear facilities, other radioactive substances, and related facilities fails to carry out security protection work in accordance with the requirements of relevant laws and administrative regulations, the competent department of nuclear industry of the State Council together with relevant departments shall order it to rectify within a time limit; if it refuses to rectify or still fails to meet the requirements after rectification within the time limit, it shall be imposed a fine of not less than 1,000,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan, its relevant permit shall be suspended or revoked, and the responsible leaders and directly responsible personnel shall be imposed a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　核进口单位未按照有关规定履行核进口承诺义务的，由国务院核工业主管部门责令改正，处二百万元以上一千万元以下的罚款；对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，并依法给予处分。  
核出口单位违反本法规定出口核以及核两用物项的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定承担法律责任。

**Article 56** : Where a nuclear import entity fails to perform its nuclear import commitment obligations in accordance with relevant provisions, the competent department of nuclear industry of the State Council shall order it to make corrections, and impose a fine of not less than 2,000,000 yuan but not more than 10,000,000 yuan; the responsible leaders and directly responsible personnel shall be imposed a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and shall be given sanctions according to law.  
Where a nuclear export entity exports nuclear materials or dual-use nuclear items in violation of the provisions of this Law, it shall bear legal liability in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国家建立核损害赔偿责任制度，按照法律和国家有关规定处理核事故造成的损害。

**Article 57** : The State shall establish a nuclear damage liability system, and handle the damage caused by nuclear accidents in accordance with laws and relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本法规定的行为，本法未作行政处罚规定，其他有关法律、行政法规有规定的，依照其规定；造成财产损失或者其他损害的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 58** : For any act violating the provisions of this Law, if this Law does not provide for administrative penalties but other relevant laws or administrative regulations have provisions, those provisions shall prevail; if property losses or other damage are caused, civil liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　涉及军工、军事等国防领域的原子能研究、开发和利用活动的管理办法，由国务院、中央军事委员会依据本法规定的原则另行制定。

**Article 59** : Measures for the management of atomic energy research, development, and utilization activities involving national defense fields such as military industry and military affairs shall be separately formulated by the State Council and the Central Military Commission in accordance with the principles provided for in this Law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　任何国家或者地区在原子能领域对中华人民共和国采取歧视性的禁止、限制或者其他类似措施的，中华人民共和国可以根据实际情况对该国家或者该地区采取相应的措施。

**Article 60** : If any country or region adopts discriminatory prohibitions, restrictions, or other similar measures against the People's Republic of China in the field of atomic energy, the People's Republic of China may, based on the actual circumstances, adopt corresponding measures against that country or region.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　本法下列用语的含义：  
（一）核材料，是指需要管制的核材料，包括铀-235及含铀-235的材料和制品，铀-233及含铀-233的材料和制品，钚-239及含钚-239的材料和制品，法律、行政法规规定的其他需要管制的核材料，不包括铀（钍）矿石及其初级制品。  
（二）乏燃料，是指在反应堆堆芯内受过辐照并从堆芯永久卸出的核燃料。  
（三）后处理，是指对反应堆乏燃料进行处理，以分离其中的裂变产物，并回收可裂变物质的过程。  
（四）核反应堆，是指为了利用核反应产生的能量、中子、核素等建设的装置。  
（五）核技术应用，是指放射性同位素和射线装置等非动力核技术在国民经济和社会发展各领域的应用。  
（六）放射性同位素，是指化学元素中具有相同原子序数，但质量不同且能发生放射性衰变的核素。  
（七）射线装置，是指在接通电源后方能产生电离辐射的装置，如X射线机、加速器、中子发生器等。  
（八）放射源，是指除研究堆和动力堆核燃料循环范畴的材料以外，永久密封在容器中或者有严密包层的放射性材料。  
（九）放射性物品，是指放射性物质中放射性活度和比活度均高于国家规定的豁免值的物质。  
（十）放射性废物，是指含有放射性核素或者被放射性核素污染，其浓度或者比活度大于国家确定的清洁解控水平，预期不再使用的废弃物。

**Article 61** : For the purposes of this Law, the following terms have the meanings as set forth below:  
(1) "Nuclear materials" means controlled nuclear materials, including uranium-235 and materials and products containing uranium-235, uranium-233 and materials and products containing uranium-233, plutonium-239 and materials and products containing plutonium-239, and other controlled nuclear materials as provided for by laws and administrative regulations, excluding uranium (thorium) ores and their primary products.  
(2) "Spent fuel" means nuclear fuel that has been irradiated in the reactor core and permanently discharged from the core.  
(3) "Reprocessing" means the process of treating spent fuel from a reactor to separate fission products and recover fissile materials.  
(4) "Nuclear reactor" means an installation constructed to utilize the energy, neutrons, nuclides, etc. generated by nuclear reactions.  
(5) "Nuclear technology application" means the application of non-power nuclear technologies such as radioactive isotopes and radiation devices in various fields of national economic and social development.  
(6) "Radioactive isotope" means a nuclide of a chemical element that has the same atomic number but different masses and is capable of radioactive decay.  
(7) "Radiation device" means a device that can generate ionizing radiation only after being powered on, such as X-ray machines, accelerators, neutron generators, etc.  
(8) "Radioactive source" means radioactive material permanently sealed in a container or having a tight cladding, excluding materials falling within the category of research reactor and power reactor nuclear fuel cycles.  
(9) "Radioactive article" means a substance of radioactive material whose radioactivity and specific activity are both higher than the exemption values prescribed by the State.  
(10) "Radioactive waste" means waste material that contains or is contaminated by radioactive nuclides and whose concentration or specific activity is greater than the clearance level determined by the State and that is no longer intended to be used.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　本法自2026年1月15日起施行。

**Article 62** : This Law shall come into force on January 15, 2026.
