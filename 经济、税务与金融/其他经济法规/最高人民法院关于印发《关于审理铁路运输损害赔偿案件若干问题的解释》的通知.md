# 最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1994〕25号  
**公布日期**：1994-10-27  
**生效日期**：1994-10-27

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、实际损失的赔偿范围  
[铁路法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国铁路法.md#art-17)中的“实际损失”，是指因灭失、短少、变质、污染、损坏导致货物、包裹、行李实际价值的损失。  
铁路运输企业按照实际损失赔偿时，对灭失、短少的货物、包裹、行李，按照其实际价值赔偿；对变质、污染、损坏降低原有价值的货物、包裹、行李，可按照其受损前后实际价值的差额或者加工、修复费用赔偿。  
货物、包裹、行李的赔偿价格按照托运时的实际价值计算。实际价值中未包含已支付的铁路运杂费、包装费、保险费、短途搬运费等费用的，按照损失部分的比例加算。

**Article 1** I. Scope of Compensation for Actual Loss  
"Actual loss" in Article 17 of the Railway Law means the loss of the actual value of goods, parcels, or luggage caused by loss, shortage, deterioration, contamination, or damage.  
When a railway transport enterprise compensates for actual loss, it shall compensate for lost or short-delivered goods, parcels, or luggage based on their actual value; for goods, parcels, or luggage whose original value is reduced due to deterioration, contamination, or damage, it may compensate based on the difference between their actual value before and after the damage, or based on the cost of processing or repair.  
The compensation price of goods, parcels, or luggage shall be calculated based on the actual value at the time of consignment. If the actual value does not include the railway freight and miscellaneous charges, packaging fees, insurance premiums, short-distance handling fees, etc. that have been paid, such expenses shall be added in proportion to the portion of the loss.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、铁路运输企业的重大过失  
[铁路法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国铁路法.md#art-17)中的“重大过失”，是指铁路运输企业或者其受雇人、代理人对承运的货物、包裹、行李明知可能造成损失而轻率地作为或者不作为。

**Article 2** II. Gross Negligence of Railway Transport Enterprise  
"Gross negligence" in Article 17 of the Railway Law means that the railway transport enterprise or its employees or agents, knowing that loss may be caused to the consigned goods, parcels, or luggage, recklessly act or fail to act.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、保价货物损失的赔偿  
[铁路法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国铁路法.md#art-17)第一款（一）项中规定的“按照实际损失赔偿，但最高不超过保价额。”是指保价运输的货物、包裹、行李在运输中发生损失，无论托运人在办理保价运输时，保价额是否与货物、包裹、行李的实际价值相符，均应在保价额内按照损失部分的实际价值赔偿，实际损失超过保价额的部分不予赔偿。  
如果损失是因铁路运输企业的故意或者重大过失造成的，比照[铁路法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国铁路法.md#art-17)第一款（二）项的规定，不受保价额的限制，按照实际损失赔偿。

**Article 3** III. Compensation for Loss of Declared-value Goods  
The provision in Article 17, Paragraph 1, Item (1) of the Railway Law that "compensation shall be made according to actual loss, but shall not exceed the declared value" means that when goods, parcels, or luggage under declared-value transport suffer loss during carriage, regardless of whether the declared value matches the actual value of the goods, parcels, or luggage at the time of declaration, compensation shall be made within the declared value according to the actual value of the lost portion, and the portion of actual loss exceeding the declared value shall not be compensated.  
If the loss is caused by the willful misconduct or gross negligence of the railway transport enterprise, the provisions of Article 17, Paragraph 1, Item (2) of the Railway Law shall apply by analogy, and compensation shall be made according to actual loss without being limited by the declared value.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、保险货物损失的赔偿  
投保货物运输险的货物在运输中发生损失，对不属于铁路运输企业免责范围的，适用[铁路法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国铁路法.md#art-17)第一款（二）项的规定，由铁路运输企业承担赔偿责任。  
保险公司按照保险合同的约定向托运人或收货人先行赔付后，对于铁路运输企业应按货物实际损失承担赔偿责任的，保险公司按照支付的保险金额向铁路运输企业追偿，因不足额保险产生的实际损失与保险金的差额部分，由铁路运输企业赔偿；对于铁路运输企业应按限额承担赔偿责任的，在足额保险的情况下，保险公司向铁路运输企业的追偿额为铁路运输企业的赔偿限额，在不足额保险的情况下，保险公司向铁路运输企业的追偿额在铁路运输企业的赔偿限额内按照投保金额与货物实际价值的比例计算，因不足额保险产生的铁路运输企业的赔偿限额与保险公司在限额内追偿额的差额部分，由铁路运输企业赔偿。

**Article 4** IV. Compensation for Loss of Insured Goods  
Where goods covered by cargo transport insurance suffer loss during carriage and the loss does not fall within the scope of exemption of the railway transport enterprise, Article 17, Paragraph 1, Item (2) of the Railway Law shall apply, and the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability.  
After the insurance company pays compensation to the consignor or consignee in advance in accordance with the insurance contract, where the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability according to the actual loss of the goods, the insurance company shall claim recourse against the railway transport enterprise up to the amount of insurance paid; the difference between the actual loss and the insurance amount arising from under-insurance shall be compensated by the railway transport enterprise. Where the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability within a limit, in the case of full insurance, the amount of recourse of the insurance company against the railway transport enterprise shall be the compensation limit of the railway transport enterprise; in the case of under-insurance, the amount of recourse of the insurance company against the railway transport enterprise shall be calculated within the compensation limit of the railway transport enterprise in proportion to the insured amount and the actual value of the goods, and the difference between the compensation limit of the railway transport enterprise and the recourse amount within the limit arising from under-insurance shall be compensated by the railway transport enterprise.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、保险保价货物损失的赔偿  
既保险又保价的货物在运输中发生损失，对不属于铁路运输企业免责范围的，适用[铁路法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国铁路法.md#art-17)第一款（一）项的规定由铁路运输企业承担赔偿责任。对于保险公司先行赔付的，比照本解释第四条对保险货物损失的赔偿处理。

**Article 5** V. Compensation for Loss of Goods Both Insured and Declared-in-value  
Where goods that are both insured and declared in value suffer loss during carriage and the loss does not fall within the scope of exemption of the railway transport enterprise, Article 17, Paragraph 1, Item (1) of the Railway Law shall apply, and the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability. Where the insurance company pays compensation in advance, the provisions of Article 4 of this Interpretation concerning compensation for loss of insured goods shall apply by analogy.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、保险补偿制度的适用  
《铁路货物运输实行保险与负责运输相结合的补偿制度的规定（试行）》（简称保险补偿制度），适用于1991年5月1日铁路法实施以前已投保货物运输险的案件。铁路法实施后投保货物运输险的案件，适用[铁路法第十七条](../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国铁路法.md#art-17)第一款的规定，保险补偿制度中有关保险补偿的规定不再适用。

**Article 6** VI. Application of the Insurance Compensation System  
The Provisions on the Implementation of a Compensation System Combining Insurance and Responsible Transportation for Railway Freight Transport (Trial) (hereinafter referred to as the Insurance Compensation System) shall apply to cases where cargo transport insurance was taken out before the implementation of the Railway Law on May 1, 1991. For cases where cargo transport insurance is taken out after the implementation of the Railway Law, the provisions of Article 17, Paragraph 1 of the Railway Law shall apply, and the provisions on insurance compensation in the Insurance Compensation System shall no longer apply.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、逾期交付的责任  
货物、包裹、行李逾期交付，如果是因铁路逾期运到造成的，由铁路运输企业支付逾期违约金；如果是因收货人或旅客逾期领取造成的，由收货人或旅客支付保管费；既因逾期运到又因收货人或旅客逾期领取造成的，由双方各自承担相应的责任。  
铁路逾期运到并且发生损失时，铁路运输企业除支付逾期违约金外，还应当赔偿损失。对收货人或者旅客逾期领取，铁路运输企业在代保管期间因保管不当造成损失的，由铁路运输企业赔偿。

**Article 7** VII. Liability for Delayed Delivery  
If goods, parcels, or luggage are delivered late due to the railway's delayed arrival, the railway transport enterprise shall pay liquidated damages for delay; if due to the consignee's or passenger's delayed collection, the consignee or passenger shall pay storage fees; if caused by both delayed arrival and delayed collection, each party shall bear corresponding liability respectively.  
Where the railway delays arrival and loss occurs, the railway transport enterprise shall, in addition to paying liquidated damages for delay, also compensate for the loss. Where the consignee or passenger delays collection and the railway transport enterprise causes loss due to improper custody during the period of custodianship, the railway transport enterprise shall compensate for the loss.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、误交付的责任  
货物、包裹、行李误交付（包括被第三人冒领造成的误交付），铁路运输企业查找超过运到期限的，由铁路运输企业支付逾期违约金。不能交付的，或者交付时有损失的，由铁路运输企业赔偿。铁路运输企业赔付后，再向有责任的第三者追偿。

**Article 8** VIII. Liability for Wrongful Delivery  
Where goods, parcels, or luggage are wrongfully delivered (including wrongful delivery caused by a third party claiming by fraud), and the railway transport enterprise's search exceeds the time limit for arrival, the railway transport enterprise shall pay liquidated damages for delay. If the goods cannot be delivered, or if loss occurs at the time of delivery, the railway transport enterprise shall compensate. After the railway transport enterprise makes compensation, it shall then recover from the liable third party.

<a id="art-9"></a>九、赔偿后又找回原物的处理  
铁路运输企业赔付后又找回丢失、被盗、冒领、逾期等按灭失处理的货物、包裹、行李的，在通知托运人、收货人或旅客退还赔款领回原物的期限届满后仍无人领取的，适用[铁路法第二十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国铁路法.md#art-22)按无主货物的规定处理。铁路运输企业未通知托运人、收货人或者旅客而自行处理找回的货物、包裹、行李的，由铁路运输企业赔偿实际损失与已付赔款差额。

**Article 9** IX. Handling of Recovery of Original Goods After Compensation  
Where, after paying compensation, the railway transport enterprise recovers goods, parcels, or luggage that were treated as lost due to loss, theft, fraudulent claim, or delay, if no one claims them after the expiration of the period for notifying the consignor, consignee, or passenger to refund the compensation and retrieve the original goods, Article 22 of the Railway Law shall apply, and they shall be treated as unclaimed goods. If the railway transport enterprise disposes of the recovered goods, parcels, or luggage without notifying the consignor, consignee, or passenger, it shall compensate for the difference between the actual loss and the compensation already paid.

<a id="art-10"></a>十、代办运输货物损失的赔偿  
代办运输的货物在铁路运输中发生损失，对代办运输企业接受托运人的委托以自己的名义与铁路运输企业签订运输合同托运或领取货物的，如委托人依据委托合同要求代办运输企业向铁路运输企业索赔的，应予支持。对代办运输企业未及时索赔而超过运输合同索赔时效的，代办运输企业应当赔偿。

**Article 10** X. Compensation for Loss of Goods Transported by an Agent  
Where goods transported by an agent suffer loss during railway carriage, and the agency transport enterprise, upon the consignor's entrustment, signs a transport contract in its own name with the railway transport enterprise to consign or collect the goods, if the principal demands that the agency transport enterprise claim compensation from the railway transport enterprise pursuant to the agency contract, such demand shall be supported. If the agency transport enterprise fails to claim compensation in time and exceeds the time limit for claims under the transport contract, the agency transport enterprise shall compensate.

<a id="art-11"></a>十一、人身伤亡的赔偿范围  
[铁路法第五十八条](../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国铁路法.md#art-58)规定的因铁路行车事故及其他铁路运营事故造成的人身伤亡，包括旅客伤亡和路外伤亡。  
人身伤亡，除[铁路法第五十八条](../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国铁路法.md#art-58)第二款列举的免责情况外，如果铁路运输企业能够证明人身伤亡是由受害人自身原因造成的，不应再责令铁路运输企业承担赔偿责任。  
对人身伤亡的赔偿责任范围适用民法通则第一百一十九条的规定。1994年9月1日以后发生的旅客伤亡的赔偿责任范围适用国务院批准的《铁路旅客运输损害赔偿规定》。

**Article 11** XI. Scope of Compensation for Personal Injury or Death  
Personal injury or death caused by railway traffic accidents or other railway operation accidents as specified in Article 58 of the Railway Law includes passenger casualties and off-railway casualties.  
For personal injury or death, except for the exemption circumstances listed in Article 58, Paragraph 2 of the Railway Law, if the railway transport enterprise can prove that the personal injury or death was caused by the victim's own fault, the railway transport enterprise shall not be ordered to bear compensation liability.  
The scope of compensation liability for personal injury or death shall apply the provisions of Article 119 of the General Principles of the Civil Law. For passenger casualties occurring after September 1, 1994, the scope of compensation liability shall apply the Provisions on Compensation for Damage in Railway Passenger Transport approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>十二、铁路旅客运送责任期间  
铁路运输企业对旅客运送的责任期间自旅客持有效车票进站时起到旅客出站或者应当出站时止。不包括旅客在候车室内的期间。

**Article 12** XII. Period of Responsibility for Railway Passenger Transport  
The period of responsibility of the railway transport enterprise for passenger transport shall start from the time when the passenger enters the station with a valid ticket and shall end at the time when the passenger exits the station or should exit the station. It shall not include the period when the passenger is in the waiting room.

<a id="art-13"></a>十三、旅客伤亡的保险责任与运输责任  
在铁路旅客运送责任期间发生旅客伤亡，属于《铁路旅客意外伤害强制保险条例》规定的保险责任范围的，铁路运输企业支付保险金后，对旅客伤亡不属于铁路运输企业免责范围的，铁路运输企业还应当支付赔偿金。

**Article 13** XIII. Insurance Liability and Transport Liability for Passenger Casualties  
If passenger casualties occur during the period of responsibility for railway passenger transport and fall within the scope of insurance liability under the Regulations on Compulsory Insurance for Accidental Injury of Railway Passengers, after the railway transport enterprise pays the insurance benefit, if the passenger casualties do not fall within the scope of exemption of the railway transport enterprise, the railway transport enterprise shall also pay compensation.

<a id="art-14"></a>十四、第三者责任造成旅客伤亡的赔偿  
在铁路旅客运送期间因第三者责任造成旅客伤亡，旅客或者其继承人要求铁路运输企业先予赔偿的，应予支持。铁路运输企业赔付后，有权向有责任的第三者追偿。

**Article 14** XIV. Compensation for Passenger Casualties Caused by Third-Party Liability  
If passenger casualties are caused by the liability of a third party during railway passenger transport, and the passenger or his/her successor demands that the railway transport entity make advance compensation, such demand shall be supported. After the railway transport enterprise pays compensation, it shall have the right to recover from the liable third party.

<a id="art-15"></a>十五、索赔时效  
对承运中的货物、包裹、行李发生损失或逾期，向铁路运输企业要求赔偿的请求权，时效期间适用铁路运输规章180日的规定，自铁路运输企业交付的次日起计算；货物、包裹、行李全部灭失的，自运到期限届满后第30日的次日起计算。但对在此期间内或者运到期限内已经确认灭失的，自铁路运输企业交给货运记录的次日起计算。  
对旅客伤亡，向铁路企业要求赔偿的请求权，时效期间适用民法通则第一百三十六条第（一）项1年的规定。自到达旅行目的地的次日或者旅行中止的次日起计算。  
对路外伤亡，向铁路运输企业要求赔偿的请求权，时效期间适用民法通则第一百三十六条第（一）项1年的规定，自受害人受到伤害的次日起计算。

**Article 15** XV. Time Limit for Claims  
The time limit for the right to claim compensation from the railway transport enterprise for loss of or delay in goods, parcels, or luggage during carriage shall be governed by the 180-day provision in the railway transport regulations, and shall be calculated from the day following the delivery by the railway transport enterprise; if the goods, parcels, or luggage are totally lost, it shall be calculated from the day following the 30th day after the expiry of the time limit for arrival. However, if loss has been confirmed during such period or within the time limit for arrival, it shall be calculated from the day following the delivery of the cargo record by the railway transport enterprise.  
For passenger casualties, the time limit for the right to claim compensation from the railway enterprise shall be governed by the one-year provision of Article 136, Item (1) of the General Principles of the Civil Law, and shall be calculated from the day following arrival at the destination of the trip or the day following the suspension of the trip.  
For off-railway casualties, the time limit for the right to claim compensation from the railway transport enterprise shall be governed by the one-year provision of Article 136, Item (1) of the General Principles of the Civil Law, and shall be calculated from the day following the day on which the victim suffered the injury.
