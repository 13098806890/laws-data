# 中华人民共和国中小企业促进法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2017-09-01  
**生效日期**：2018-01-01

> （2002年6月29日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十八次会议通过　2017年9月1日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十九次会议修订） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　财税支持 第三章　融资促进 第四章　创业扶持 第五章　创新支持 第六章　市场开拓 第七章　服务措施 第八章　权益保护 第九章　监督检查 第十章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了改善中小企业经营环境，保障中小企业公平参与市场竞争，维护中小企业合法权益，支持中小企业创业创新，促进中小企业健康发展，扩大城乡就业，发挥中小企业在国民经济和社会发展中的重要作用，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称中小企业，是指在中华人民共和国境内依法设立的，人员规模、经营规模相对较小的企业，包括中型企业、小型企业和微型企业。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了改善中小企业经营环境，保障中小企业公平参与市场竞争，维护中小企业合法权益，支持中小企业创业创新，促进中小企业健康发展，扩大城乡就业，发挥中小企业在国民经济和社会发展中的重要作用，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of improving the business environment for small and medium-sized enterprises, ensuring their fair participation in market competition, safeguarding their legitimate rights and interests, supporting their entrepreneurship and innovation, promoting their healthy development, expanding urban and rural employment, and giving play to their important role in the national economy and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称中小企业，是指在中华人民共和国境内依法设立的，人员规模、经营规模相对较小的企业，包括中型企业、小型企业和微型企业。  
中型企业、小型企业和微型企业划分标准由国务院负责中小企业促进工作综合管理的部门会同国务院有关部门，根据企业从业人员、营业收入、资产总额等指标，结合行业特点制定，报国务院批准。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "small and medium-sized enterprises" (SMEs) refer to enterprises legally established within the territory of the People's Republic of China that are relatively small in terms of headcount and business scale, including medium-sized enterprises, small enterprises, and micro enterprises.  
The criteria for classifying medium-sized enterprises, small enterprises, and micro enterprises shall be formulated by the department of the State Council responsible for integrated management of SME promotion, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council, based on indicators such as the number of employees, operating revenue, total assets, etc., taking into account the characteristics of the industries, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家将促进中小企业发展作为长期发展战略，坚持各类企业权利平等、机会平等、规则平等，对中小企业特别是其中的小型微型企业实行积极扶持、加强引导、完善服务、依法规范、保障权益的方针，为中小企业创立和发展创造有利的环境。

**Article 3** The State shall regard the promotion of SME development as a long-term development strategy, adhere to the principles of equal rights, equal opportunities, and equal rules for all types of enterprises, and adopt the policy of actively supporting, strengthening guidance, improving services, regulating according to law, and protecting the rights and interests of SMEs, especially small and micro enterprises, so as to create a favorable environment for the establishment and development of SMEs.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　中小企业应当依法经营，遵守国家劳动用工、安全生产、职业卫生、社会保障、资源环境、质量标准、知识产权、财政税收等方面的法律、法规，遵循诚信原则，规范内部管理，提高经营管理水平；不得损害劳动者合法权益，不得损害社会公共利益。

**Article 4** SMEs shall operate according to law, comply with laws and regulations of the State on labor and employment, work safety, occupational health, social security, resources and environment, quality standards, intellectual property, fiscal and taxation, etc., adhere to the principle of good faith, standardize internal management, and improve their management and operation level; they shall not harm the legitimate rights and interests of workers, nor shall they harm the public interest.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院制定促进中小企业发展政策，建立中小企业促进工作协调机制，统筹全国中小企业促进工作。  
国务院负责中小企业促进工作综合管理的部门组织实施促进中小企业发展政策，对中小企业促进工作进行宏观指导、综合协调和监督检查。  
国务院有关部门根据国家促进中小企业发展政策，在各自职责范围内负责中小企业促进工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府根据实际情况建立中小企业促进工作协调机制，明确相应的负责中小企业促进工作综合管理的部门，负责本行政区域内的中小企业促进工作。

**Article 5** The State Council shall formulate policies for promoting the development of SMEs, establish a coordination mechanism for SME promotion work, and coordinate SME promotion work nationwide.  
The department of the State Council responsible for integrated management of SME promotion shall organize the implementation of policies for promoting the development of SMEs, and exercise macro guidance, comprehensive coordination, supervision, and inspection over SME promotion work.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for SME promotion work according to the national policies for promoting the development of SMEs.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on actual conditions, establish coordination mechanisms for SME promotion work, designate the corresponding departments responsible for integrated management of SME promotion, and be responsible for SME promotion work within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家建立中小企业统计监测制度。统计部门应当加强对中小企业的统计调查和监测分析，定期发布有关信息。

**Article 6** The State shall establish a statistical monitoring system for SMEs. The statistics department shall strengthen statistical surveys and monitoring analysis of SMEs, and regularly publish relevant information.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家推进中小企业信用制度建设，建立社会化的信用信息征集与评价体系，实现中小企业信用信息查询、交流和共享的社会化。

**Article 7** The State shall advance the establishment of a credit system for SMEs, establish a socialized credit information collection and evaluation system, and realize the socialization of inquiry, exchange, and sharing of credit information of SMEs.

### 第二章　财税支持

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　中央财政应当在本级预算中设立中小企业科目，安排中小企业发展专项资金。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府应当根据实际情况，在本级财政预算中安排中小企业发展专项资金。

**Article 8** The central finance shall establish an item for SMEs in its budget, and allocate special funds for the development of SMEs.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on actual conditions, allocate special funds for the development of SMEs in their respective fiscal budgets.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　中小企业发展专项资金通过资助、购买服务、奖励等方式，重点用于支持中小企业公共服务体系和融资服务体系建设。  
中小企业发展专项资金向小型微型企业倾斜，资金管理使用坚持公开、透明的原则，实行预算绩效管理。

**Article 9** Special funds for the development of SMEs shall, through means such as grants, purchase of services, and awards, be used primarily to support the construction of public service systems and financing service systems for SMEs.  
The special funds for the development of SMEs shall be tilted toward small and micro enterprises, and the management and use of the funds shall adhere to the principles of openness and transparency, and be subject to budget performance management.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家设立中小企业发展基金。国家中小企业发展基金应当遵循政策性导向和市场化运作原则，主要用于引导和带动社会资金支持初创期中小企业，促进创业创新。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府可以设立中小企业发展基金。  
中小企业发展基金的设立和使用管理办法由国务院规定。

**Article 10** The State shall establish a development fund for SMEs. The National SME Development Fund shall follow the principles of policy orientation and market-based operation, and be used primarily to guide and drive social funds to support start-up SMEs and promote entrepreneurship and innovation.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level may establish development funds for SMEs.  
The measures for the establishment, use, and management of the SME development funds shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家实行有利于小型微型企业发展的税收政策，对符合条件的小型微型企业按照规定实行缓征、减征、免征企业所得税、增值税等措施，简化税收征管程序，减轻小型微型企业税收负担。

**Article 11** The State shall implement tax policies favorable to the development of small and micro enterprises, adopt measures such as deferred collection, reduction, or exemption of corporate income tax and value-added tax for eligible small and micro enterprises in accordance with relevant provisions, simplify tax collection and management procedures, and reduce the tax burden of small and micro enterprises.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家对小型微型企业行政事业性收费实行减免等优惠政策，减轻小型微型企业负担。

**Article 12** The State shall implement preferential policies such as reduction and exemption of administrative and institutional fees for small and micro enterprises, so as to reduce the burden on small and micro enterprises.

### 第三章　融资促进

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　金融机构应当发挥服务实体经济的功能，高效、公平地服务中小企业。

**Article 13** Financial institutions shall give play to their function of serving the real economy, and serve SMEs efficiently and fairly.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　中国人民银行应当综合运用货币政策工具，鼓励和引导金融机构加大对小型微型企业的信贷支持，改善小型微型企业融资环境。

**Article 14** The People's Bank of China shall comprehensively use monetary policy tools to encourage and guide financial institutions to increase credit support for small and micro enterprises, and improve the financing environment for small and micro enterprises.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院银行业监督管理机构对金融机构开展小型微型企业金融服务应当制定差异化监管政策，采取合理提高小型微型企业不良贷款容忍度等措施，引导金融机构增加小型微型企业融资规模和比重，提高金融服务水平。

**Article 15** The banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall formulate differentiated regulatory policies for financial institutions providing financial services to small and micro enterprises, adopt measures such as reasonably raising the tolerance for non-performing loans of small and micro enterprises, and guide financial institutions to increase the scale and proportion of financing for small and micro enterprises and improve the level of financial services.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家鼓励各类金融机构开发和提供适合中小企业特点的金融产品和服务。  
国家政策性金融机构应当在其业务经营范围内，采取多种形式，为中小企业提供金融服务。

**Article 16** The State encourages various types of financial institutions to develop and provide financial products and services suitable for the characteristics of SMEs.  
State policy-oriented financial institutions shall, within their scope of business operations, adopt various forms to provide financial services for SMEs.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家推进和支持普惠金融体系建设，推动中小银行、非存款类放贷机构和互联网金融有序健康发展，引导银行业金融机构向县域和乡镇等小型微型企业金融服务薄弱地区延伸网点和业务。  
国有大型商业银行应当设立普惠金融机构，为小型微型企业提供金融服务。国家推动其他银行业金融机构设立小型微型企业金融服务专营机构。  
地区性中小银行应当积极为其所在地的小型微型企业提供金融服务，促进实体经济发展。

**Article 17** The State shall promote and support the construction of an inclusive financial system, promote the orderly and healthy development of small and medium-sized banks, non-deposit-taking lending institutions, and internet finance, and guide banking financial institutions to extend their outlets and businesses to counties, townships, and other areas where financial services for small and micro enterprises are weak.  
Large state-owned commercial banks shall set up inclusive financial institutions to provide financial services for small and micro enterprises. The State shall promote the establishment of specialized institutions for financial services for small and micro enterprises by other banking financial institutions.  
Regional small and medium-sized banks shall actively provide financial services for the small and micro enterprises in their localities, so as to promote the development of the real economy.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家健全多层次资本市场体系，多渠道推动股权融资，发展并规范债券市场，促进中小企业利用多种方式直接融资。

**Article 18** The State shall improve the multi-level capital market system, promote equity financing through multiple channels, develop and regulate the bond market, and encourage SMEs to use various means of direct financing.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家完善担保融资制度，支持金融机构为中小企业提供以应收账款、知识产权、存货、机器设备等为担保品的担保融资。

**Article 19** The State shall improve the secured financing system, and support financial institutions in providing secured financing for SMEs using accounts receivable, intellectual property, inventory, machinery and equipment, etc. as collateral.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　中小企业以应收账款申请担保融资时，其应收账款的付款方，应当及时确认债权债务关系，支持中小企业融资。  
国家鼓励中小企业及付款方通过应收账款融资服务平台确认债权债务关系，提高融资效率，降低融资成本。

**Article 20** When an SME applies for secured financing against accounts receivable, the payor of such accounts receivable shall promptly confirm the creditor-debtor relationship to support the SME's financing.  
The State encourages SMEs and payors to confirm creditor-debtor relationships through the accounts receivable financing service platform, so as to improve financing efficiency and reduce financing costs.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上人民政府应当建立中小企业政策性信用担保体系，鼓励各类担保机构为中小企业融资提供信用担保。

**Article 21** People's governments at or above the county level shall establish a policy-oriented credit guarantee system for SMEs, and encourage various types of guarantee institutions to provide credit guarantees for SME financing.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家推动保险机构开展中小企业贷款保证保险和信用保险业务，开发适应中小企业分散风险、补偿损失需求的保险产品。

**Article 22** The State shall promote insurance institutions to carry out loan guarantee insurance and credit insurance for SMEs, and develop insurance products that meet the needs of SMEs for risk diversification and loss compensation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家支持征信机构发展针对中小企业融资的征信产品和服务，依法向政府有关部门、公用事业单位和商业机构采集信息。  
国家鼓励第三方评级机构开展中小企业评级服务。

**Article 23** The State supports credit reference agencies in developing credit reference products and services for SME financing, and collecting information from relevant government departments, public institutions, and commercial organizations according to law.  
The State encourages third-party rating agencies to carry out rating services for SMEs.

### 第四章　创业扶持

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当通过政府网站、宣传资料等形式，为创业人员免费提供工商、财税、金融、环境保护、安全生产、劳动用工、社会保障等方面的法律政策咨询和公共信息服务。

**Article 24** People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, through government websites, promotional materials, and other forms, provide free legal and policy consultation and public information services to entrepreneurs in areas such as industry and commerce, finance and taxation, finance, environmental protection, work safety, labor and employment, and social security.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　高等学校毕业生、退役军人和失业人员、残疾人员等创办小型微型企业，按照国家规定享受税收优惠和收费减免。

**Article 25** College graduates, retired servicemen, unemployed persons, persons with disabilities, and other individuals who establish small and micro enterprises shall enjoy tax preferences and fee reductions or exemptions according to national provisions.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家采取措施支持社会资金参与投资中小企业。创业投资企业和个人投资者投资初创期科技创新企业的，按照国家规定享受税收优惠。

**Article 26** The State shall take measures to support social funds in participating in investment in SMEs. Venture capital enterprises and individual investors who invest in start-up technological innovation enterprises shall enjoy tax preferences according to national provisions.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家改善企业创业环境，优化审批流程，实现中小企业行政许可便捷，降低中小企业设立成本。

**Article 27** The State shall improve the entrepreneurial environment for enterprises, optimize the approval process, achieve convenience in administrative licensing for SMEs, and reduce the cost of establishing SMEs.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家鼓励建设和创办小型微型企业创业基地、孵化基地，为小型微型企业提供生产经营场地和服务。

**Article 28** The State encourages the construction and establishment of entrepreneurship bases and incubation bases for small and micro enterprises, so as to provide production and operation premises and services for small and micro enterprises.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　地方各级人民政府应当根据中小企业发展的需要，在城乡规划中安排必要的用地和设施，为中小企业获得生产经营场所提供便利。  
国家支持利用闲置的商业用房、工业厂房、企业库房和物流设施等，为创业者提供低成本生产经营场所。

**Article 29** Local people's governments at all levels shall, based on the needs of SME development, arrange necessary land and facilities in urban and rural planning to facilitate SMEs in obtaining production and operation premises.  
The State supports the use of idle commercial premises, industrial plants, enterprise warehouses, logistics facilities, etc., to provide low-cost production and operation premises for entrepreneurs.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家鼓励互联网平台向中小企业开放技术、开发、营销、推广等资源，加强资源共享与合作，为中小企业创业提供服务。

**Article 30** The State encourages internet platforms to open up resources such as technology, development, marketing, and promotion to SMEs, strengthen resource sharing and cooperation, and provide services for SME entrepreneurship.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家简化中小企业注销登记程序，实现中小企业市场退出便利化。

**Article 31** The State shall simplify the procedures for the deregistration of SMEs, so as to facilitate their market exit.

### 第五章　创新支持

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家鼓励中小企业按照市场需求，推进技术、产品、管理模式、商业模式等创新。  
中小企业的固定资产由于技术进步等原因，确需加速折旧的，可以依法缩短折旧年限或者采取加速折旧方法。  
国家完善中小企业研究开发费用加计扣除政策，支持中小企业技术创新。

**Article 32** The State encourages SMEs to promote innovation in technology, products, management models, business models, etc., in accordance with market demand.  
Where the fixed assets of an SME truly need accelerated depreciation due to technological advancement or other reasons, it may, according to law, shorten the depreciation period or adopt an accelerated depreciation method.  
The State shall improve the policy on additional deduction of research and development expenses for SMEs, and support technological innovation by SMEs.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家支持中小企业在研发设计、生产制造、运营管理等环节应用互联网、云计算、大数据、人工智能等现代技术手段，创新生产方式，提高生产经营效率。

**Article 33** The State supports SMEs in applying modern technological means such as the internet, cloud computing, big data, and artificial intelligence in the areas of research and development, design, production and manufacturing, operation and management, so as to innovate production methods and improve production and operation efficiency.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家鼓励中小企业参与产业关键共性技术研究开发和利用财政资金设立的科研项目实施。  
国家推动军民融合深度发展，支持中小企业参与国防科研和生产活动。  
国家支持中小企业及中小企业的有关行业组织参与标准的制定。

**Article 34** The State encourages SMEs to participate in the research and development of key generic technologies in industries and the implementation of scientific research projects funded by government funds.  
The State shall promote the in-depth development of military-civilian integration, and support SMEs in participating in national defense scientific research and production activities.  
The State supports SMEs and relevant industry organizations of SMEs in participating in the formulation of standards.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家鼓励中小企业研究开发拥有自主知识产权的技术和产品，规范内部知识产权管理，提升保护和运用知识产权的能力；鼓励中小企业投保知识产权保险；减轻中小企业申请和维持知识产权的费用等负担。

**Article 35** The State encourages SMEs to research and develop technologies and products with independent intellectual property rights, standardize internal intellectual property management, and enhance their ability to protect and utilize intellectual property; encourages SMEs to take out intellectual property insurance; and reduces the burden on SMEs in terms of costs for applying for and maintaining intellectual property.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　县级以上人民政府有关部门应当在规划、用地、财政等方面提供支持，推动建立和发展各类创新服务机构。  
国家鼓励各类创新服务机构为中小企业提供技术信息、研发设计与应用、质量标准、实验试验、检验检测、技术转让、技术培训等服务，促进科技成果转化，推动企业技术、产品升级。

**Article 36** The relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall provide support in terms of planning, land use, finance, etc., to promote the establishment and development of various types of innovation service institutions.  
The State encourages various types of innovation service institutions to provide SMEs with services such as technical information, research and development design and application, quality standards, experimentation and testing, inspection and testing, technology transfer, and technical training, so as to promote the transformation of scientific and technological achievements and facilitate the upgrading of enterprise technology and products.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　县级以上人民政府有关部门应当拓宽渠道，采取补贴、培训等措施，引导高等学校毕业生到中小企业就业，帮助中小企业引进创新人才。  
国家鼓励科研机构、高等学校和大型企业等创造条件向中小企业开放试验设施，开展技术研发与合作，帮助中小企业开发新产品，培养专业人才。  
国家鼓励科研机构、高等学校支持本单位的科技人员以兼职、挂职、参与项目合作等形式到中小企业从事产学研合作和科技成果转化活动，并按照国家有关规定取得相应报酬。

**Article 37** The relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall broaden channels, adopt measures such as subsidies and training, to guide college graduates to work in SMEs, and help SMEs introduce innovative talents.  
The State encourages research institutions, institutions of higher education, and large enterprises, among others, to create conditions to open up experimental facilities to SMEs, carry out technology research and development and cooperation, help SMEs develop new products, and train professional personnel.  
The State encourages research institutions and institutions of higher education to support their scientific and technical personnel in engaging in industry-university-research cooperation and transformation of scientific and technological achievements in SMEs through forms such as part-time work, temporary posts, and participation in project cooperation, and to obtain corresponding remuneration according to relevant national provisions.

### 第六章　市场开拓

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家完善市场体系，实行统一的市场准入和市场监管制度，反对垄断和不正当竞争，营造中小企业公平参与竞争的市场环境。

**Article 38** The State shall improve the market system, implement a unified market access and market supervision system, oppose monopoly and unfair competition, and create a market environment in which SMEs can participate in competition on a fair basis.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家支持大型企业与中小企业建立以市场配置资源为基础的、稳定的原材料供应、生产、销售、服务外包、技术开发和技术改造等方面的协作关系，带动和促进中小企业发展。

**Article 39** The State supports large enterprises in establishing stable collaborative relationships with SMEs based on market allocation of resources in areas such as raw material supply, production, sales, service outsourcing, technology development, and technological transformation, so as to drive and promote the development of SMEs.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国务院有关部门应当制定中小企业政府采购的相关优惠政策，通过制定采购需求标准、预留采购份额、价格评审优惠、优先采购等措施，提高中小企业在政府采购中的份额。  
向中小企业预留的采购份额应当占本部门年度政府采购项目预算总额的百分之三十以上；其中，预留给小型微型企业的比例不低于百分之六十。中小企业无法提供的商品和服务除外。  
政府采购不得在企业股权结构、经营年限、经营规模和财务指标等方面对中小企业实行差别待遇或者歧视待遇。  
政府采购部门应当在政府采购监督管理部门指定的媒体上及时向社会公开发布采购信息，为中小企业获得政府采购合同提供指导和服务。

**Article 40** The relevant departments of the State Council shall formulate relevant preferential policies for SME government procurement, and increase the share of SMEs in government procurement by adopting measures such as formulating procurement demand standards, reserving procurement shares, granting price evaluation preferences, and prioritizing procurement.  
The procurement shares reserved for SMEs shall account for not less than 30% of the total budget of the department's annual government procurement projects; among these, the proportion reserved for small and micro enterprises shall be not less than 60%, except for goods and services that SMEs are unable to provide.  
Government procurement shall not impose differential treatment or discriminatory treatment on SMEs in terms of equity structure, years of operation, business scale, financial indicators, etc.  
Government procurement departments shall promptly publish procurement information to the public on the media designated by the government procurement supervision and administration department, and provide guidance and services for SMEs to obtain government procurement contracts.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　县级以上人民政府有关部门应当在法律咨询、知识产权保护、技术性贸易措施、产品认证等方面为中小企业产品和服务出口提供指导和帮助，推动对外经济技术合作与交流。  
国家有关政策性金融机构应当通过开展进出口信贷、出口信用保险等业务，支持中小企业开拓境外市场。

**Article 41** The relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall provide guidance and assistance to SMEs in the export of their products and services in areas such as legal consultation, intellectual property protection, technical trade measures, and product certification, and promote foreign economic and technological cooperation and exchanges.  
The relevant policy-oriented financial institutions of the State shall support SMEs in exploring overseas markets by conducting businesses such as import and export credit and export credit insurance.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　县级以上人民政府有关部门应当为中小企业提供用汇、人员出入境等方面的便利，支持中小企业到境外投资，开拓国际市场。

**Article 42** The relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall provide convenience for SMEs in terms of foreign exchange use, entry and exit of personnel, etc., and support SMEs in making overseas investments and exploring international markets.

### 第七章　服务措施

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家建立健全社会化的中小企业公共服务体系，为中小企业提供服务。

**Article 43** The State shall establish and improve a socialized public service system for SMEs, and provide services for SMEs.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当根据实际需要建立和完善中小企业公共服务机构，为中小企业提供公益性服务。

**Article 44** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on actual needs, establish and improve public service institutions for SMEs, and provide public welfare services for SMEs.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上人民政府负责中小企业促进工作综合管理的部门应当建立跨部门的政策信息互联网发布平台，及时汇集涉及中小企业的法律法规、创业、创新、金融、市场、权益保护等各类政府服务信息，为中小企业提供便捷无偿服务。

**Article 45** The departments of people's governments at or above the county level responsible for integrated management of SME promotion shall establish a cross-departmental internet platform for the release of policy information, promptly collect various types of government service information concerning SMEs, such as laws and regulations, entrepreneurship, innovation, finance, markets, and rights and interests protection, and provide convenient and free services for SMEs.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国家鼓励各类服务机构为中小企业提供创业培训与辅导、知识产权保护、管理咨询、信息咨询、信用服务、市场营销、项目开发、投资融资、财会税务、产权交易、技术支持、人才引进、对外合作、展览展销、法律咨询等服务。

**Article 46** The State encourages various types of service institutions to provide SMEs with services such as entrepreneurship training and coaching, intellectual property protection, management consulting, information consulting, credit services, marketing, project development, investment and financing, accounting and taxation, property rights transactions, technical support, talent introduction, foreign cooperation, exhibitions and sales, and legal consultation.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上人民政府负责中小企业促进工作综合管理的部门应当安排资金，有计划地组织实施中小企业经营管理人员培训。

**Article 47** The departments of people's governments at or above the county level responsible for integrated management of SME promotion shall allocate funds and organize the implementation of training for SME operation and management personnel in a planned manner.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家支持有关机构、高等学校开展针对中小企业经营管理及生产技术等方面的人员培训，提高企业营销、管理和技术水平。  
国家支持高等学校、职业教育院校和各类职业技能培训机构与中小企业合作共建实习实践基地，支持职业教育院校教师和中小企业技术人才双向交流，创新中小企业人才培养模式。

**Article 48** The State supports relevant institutions and institutions of higher education in conducting personnel training for SMEs in areas such as operation and management and production technology, so as to improve the marketing, management, and technical levels of enterprises.  
The State supports institutions of higher education, vocational education schools, and various types of vocational skill training institutions in cooperating with SMEs to jointly build internship and practice bases, supports two-way exchanges between teachers of vocational education schools and technical personnel of SMEs, and innovates the talent training models for SMEs.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　中小企业的有关行业组织应当依法维护会员的合法权益，反映会员诉求，加强自律管理，为中小企业创业创新、开拓市场等提供服务。

**Article 49** The relevant industry organizations of SMEs shall, according to law, safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of their members, reflect the demands of members, strengthen self-discipline management, and provide services for SME entrepreneurship and innovation, market development, etc.

### 第八章　权益保护

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家保护中小企业及其出资人的财产权和其他合法权益。任何单位和个人不得侵犯中小企业财产及其合法收益。

**Article 50** The State shall protect the property rights and other legitimate rights and interests of SMEs and their contributors. No unit or individual may infringe upon the property or legitimate income of SMEs.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　县级以上人民政府负责中小企业促进工作综合管理的部门应当建立专门渠道，听取中小企业对政府相关管理工作的意见和建议，并及时向有关部门反馈，督促改进。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府有关部门和有关行业组织应当公布联系方式，受理中小企业的投诉、举报，并在规定的时间内予以调查、处理。

**Article 51** The department of the people's government at or above the county level that is responsible for comprehensive management of the promotion of small and medium-sized enterprises shall establish a dedicated channel to hear opinions and suggestions of small and medium-sized enterprises on relevant government administrative work, and shall promptly provide feedback to the relevant departments and urge improvements.  
Relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level and relevant industry organizations shall disclose contact information, accept complaints and reports from small and medium-sized enterprises, and shall investigate and handle them within the prescribed time.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　地方各级人民政府应当依法实施行政许可，依法开展管理工作，不得实施没有法律、法规依据的检查，不得强制或者变相强制中小企业参加考核、评比、表彰、培训等活动。

**Article 52** Local people's governments at all levels shall implement administrative licenses according to law and carry out administrative work according to law. They shall not conduct inspections without the basis of laws or regulations, nor shall they compel or compel in disguised form small and medium-sized enterprises to participate in activities such as assessments, appraisals, commendations, or training.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家机关、事业单位和大型企业不得违约拖欠中小企业的货物、工程、服务款项。  
中小企业有权要求拖欠方支付拖欠款并要求对拖欠造成的损失进行赔偿。

**Article 53** State organs, public institutions, and large enterprises shall not breach contracts by defaulting on payments for goods, projects, or services owed to small and medium-sized enterprises.  
Small and medium-sized enterprises have the right to demand that the defaulting party pay the overdue amounts and claim compensation for losses caused by the default.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　任何单位不得违反法律、法规向中小企业收取费用，不得实施没有法律、法规依据的罚款，不得向中小企业摊派财物。中小企业对违反上述规定的行为有权拒绝和举报、控告。

**Article 54** No entity shall collect fees from small and medium-sized enterprises in violation of laws or regulations, impose fines without the basis of laws or regulations, or apportion property to small and medium-sized enterprises. Small and medium-sized enterprises have the right to refuse, report, and accuse any act violating the above provisions.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家建立和实施涉企行政事业性收费目录清单制度，收费目录清单及其实施情况向社会公开，接受社会监督。  
任何单位不得对中小企业执行目录清单之外的行政事业性收费，不得对中小企业擅自提高收费标准、扩大收费范围；严禁以各种方式强制中小企业赞助捐赠、订购报刊、加入社团、接受指定服务；严禁行业组织依靠代行政府职能或者利用行政资源擅自设立收费项目、提高收费标准。

**Article 55** The State establishes and implements a system of catalogue lists of administrative and institutional charges involving enterprises. The catalogue lists of charges and their implementation shall be disclosed to the public and subject to social supervision.  
No entity shall impose on small and medium-sized enterprises administrative and institutional charges not included in the catalogue lists, nor shall it arbitrarily raise charging standards or expand the scope of charges on small and medium-sized enterprises. It is strictly prohibited to compel, in any form, small and medium-sized enterprises to sponsor donations, subscribe to newspapers and periodicals, join associations, or accept designated services. It is strictly prohibited for industry organizations to rely on exercising government functions or use administrative resources to arbitrarily establish charge items or raise charging standards.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　县级以上地方各级人民政府有关部门对中小企业实施监督检查应当依法进行，建立随机抽查机制。同一部门对中小企业实施的多项监督检查能够合并进行的，应当合并进行；不同部门对中小企业实施的多项监督检查能够合并完成的，由本级人民政府组织有关部门实施合并或者联合检查。

**Article 56** Relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall conduct supervision and inspection of small and medium-sized enterprises according to law and establish a random inspection mechanism. Where multiple supervision and inspections conducted by the same department on small and medium-sized enterprises can be consolidated, they shall be consolidated; where multiple supervision and inspections conducted by different departments on small and medium-sized enterprises can be completed in a consolidated manner, the people's government at the same level shall organize the relevant departments to carry out consolidated or joint inspections.

### 第九章　监督检查

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　县级以上人民政府定期组织对中小企业促进工作情况的监督检查；对违反本法的行为及时予以纠正，并对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 57** People's governments at or above the county level shall regularly organize supervision and inspection of the work on promoting small and medium-sized enterprises; they shall promptly correct any act violating this Law and shall impose sanctions on the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国务院负责中小企业促进工作综合管理的部门应当委托第三方机构定期开展中小企业发展环境评估，并向社会公布。  
地方各级人民政府可以根据实际情况委托第三方机构开展中小企业发展环境评估。

**Article 58** The department of the State Council responsible for comprehensive management of the promotion of small and medium-sized enterprises shall entrust a third-party institution to regularly conduct assessments of the development environment for small and medium-sized enterprises and shall disclose the results to the public.  
Local people's governments at all levels may, based on actual conditions, entrust third-party institutions to conduct assessments of the development environment for small and medium-sized enterprises.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　县级以上人民政府应当定期组织开展对中小企业发展专项资金、中小企业发展基金使用效果的企业评价、社会评价和资金使用动态评估，并将评价和评估情况及时向社会公布，接受社会监督。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门在各自职责范围内，对中小企业发展专项资金、中小企业发展基金的管理和使用情况进行监督，对截留、挤占、挪用、侵占、贪污中小企业发展专项资金、中小企业发展基金等行为依法进行查处，并对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 59** People's governments at or above the county level shall regularly organize enterprise evaluation, social evaluation, and dynamic assessment of the use effectiveness of special funds for the development of small and medium-sized enterprises and the development fund for small and medium-sized enterprises, and shall disclose the evaluation and assessment results to the public in a timely manner, subject to social supervision.  
Relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, supervise the management and use of special funds for the development of small and medium-sized enterprises and the development fund for small and medium-sized enterprises; investigate and punish acts such as interception, misappropriation, diversion, embezzlement, or corruption of special funds for the development of small and medium-sized enterprises and the development fund for small and medium-sized enterprises according to law; and impose sanctions on the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons according to law. If a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　县级以上地方各级人民政府有关部门在各自职责范围内，对强制或者变相强制中小企业参加考核、评比、表彰、培训等活动的行为，违法向中小企业收费、罚款、摊派财物的行为，以及其他侵犯中小企业合法权益的行为进行查处，并对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 60** Relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, investigate and punish acts of compelling or compelling in disguised form small and medium-sized enterprises to participate in activities such as assessments, appraisals, commendations, or training; acts of illegally charging fees, imposing fines, or apportioning property to small and medium-sized enterprises; and other acts infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of small and medium-sized enterprises. They shall also impose sanctions on the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons according to law.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　本法自2018年1月1日起施行。

**Article 61** This Law shall come into force on January 1, 2018.
