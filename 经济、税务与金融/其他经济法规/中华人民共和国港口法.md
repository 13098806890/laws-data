### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强港口管理，维护港口的安全与经营秩序，保护当事人的合法权益，促进港口的建设与发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of strengthening port administration, maintaining port safety and business order, protecting the lawful rights and interests of parties, and promoting port construction and development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　从事港口规划、建设、维护、经营、管理及其相关活动，适用本法。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to activities involving port planning, construction, maintenance, operation, administration, and related activities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本法所称港口，是指具有船舶进出、停泊、靠泊，旅客上下，货物装卸、驳运、储存等功能，具有相应的码头设施，由一定范围的水域和陆域组成的区域。  
港口可以由一个或者多个港区组成。

**Article 3** For the purposes of this Law, "port" means an area consisting of a certain scope of waters and land areas, which has functions such as entry, berthing, and mooring of ships, embarkation and disembarkation of passengers, and loading, unloading, lightering, and storage of cargo, and which is equipped with corresponding wharf facilities.  
A port may consist of one or more port areas.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院和有关县级以上地方人民政府应当在国民经济和社会发展计划中体现港口的发展和规划要求，并依法保护和合理利用港口资源。

**Article 4** The State Council and the relevant local people's governments at or above the county level shall reflect the requirements for port development and planning in the national economic and social development plans, and shall protect and rationally utilize port resources according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家鼓励国内外经济组织和个人依法投资建设、经营港口，保护投资者的合法权益。

**Article 5** The State encourages domestic and foreign economic organizations and individuals to invest in the construction and operation of ports according to law, and protects the lawful rights and interests of investors.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院交通主管部门主管全国的港口工作。  
地方人民政府对本行政区域内港口的管理，按照国务院关于港口管理体制的规定确定。  
依照前款确定的港口管理体制，由港口所在地的市、县人民政府管理的港口，由市、县人民政府确定一个部门具体实施对港口的行政管理；由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理的港口，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定一个部门具体实施对港口的行政管理。  
依照前款确定的对港口具体实施行政管理的部门，以下统称港口行政管理部门。

**Article 6** The competent department of transport under the State Council shall be in charge of port work nationwide.  
The administration of ports within their respective administrative regions by local people's governments shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of the State Council on the port administration system.  
Under the port administration system determined pursuant to the preceding paragraph, for ports administered by the people's government of a city or county where the port is located, such people's government shall designate a department to specifically carry out the port administration; for ports administered by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, such people's government shall designate a department to specifically carry out the port administration.  
The departments designated pursuant to the preceding paragraph to specifically carry out port administration are hereinafter collectively referred to as port administration departments.

### 第二章　港口规划与建设

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　港口规划应当根据国民经济和社会发展的要求以及国防建设的需要编制，体现合理利用岸线资源的原则，符合城镇体系规划，并与土地利用总体规划、城市总体规划、江河流域规划、防洪规划、海洋功能区划、水路运输发展规划和其他运输方式发展规划以及法律、行政法规规定的其他有关规划相衔接、协调。  
编制港口规划应当组织专家论证，并依法进行环境影响评价。

**Article 7** Port plans shall be formulated in light of the requirements of national economic and social development and the needs of national defense construction, reflect the principle of rational utilization of shoreline resources, conform to the urban system planning, and be coherent and coordinated with the overall land use planning, overall urban planning, river basin planning, flood control planning, marine functional zoning, waterway transport development planning, other transport mode development planning, and other relevant planning provided for by laws and administrative regulations.  
When formulating port plans, expert demonstration shall be organized, and environmental impact assessment shall be conducted according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　港口规划包括港口布局规划和港口总体规划。  
港口布局规划，是指港口的分布规划，包括全国港口布局规划和省、自治区、直辖市港口布局规划。  
港口总体规划，是指一个港口在一定时期的具体规划，包括港口的水域和陆域范围、港区划分、吞吐量和到港船型、港口的性质和功能、水域和陆域使用、港口设施建设岸线使用、建设用地配置以及分期建设序列等内容。  
港口总体规划应当符合港口布局规划。

**Article 8** Port plans include port layout plans and port master plans.  
Port layout plans refer to plans for the distribution of ports, including the national port layout plan and the port layout plans of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
Port master plans refer to specific plans for a particular port within a certain period, including the scope of waters and land areas of the port, division of port areas, throughput capacity and types of vessels calling at the port, the nature and functions of the port, use of waters and land areas, use of shoreline for the construction of port facilities, allocation of construction land, and sequence of phased construction, among other contents.  
Port master plans shall be consistent with port layout plans.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　全国港口布局规划，由国务院交通主管部门征求国务院有关部门和有关军事机关的意见编制，报国务院批准后公布实施。  
省、自治区、直辖市港口布局规划，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据全国港口布局规划组织编制，并送国务院交通主管部门征求意见。国务院交通主管部门自收到征求意见的材料之日起满三十日未提出修改意见的，该港口布局规划由有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公布实施；国务院交通主管部门认为不符合全国港口布局规划的，应当自收到征求意见的材料之日起三十日内提出修改意见；有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府对修改意见有异议的，报国务院决定。

**Article 9** The national port layout plan shall be formulated by the competent department of transport under the State Council after soliciting opinions from the relevant departments under the State Council and relevant military authorities, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval before being promulgated and implemented.  
The port layout plan of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be formulated by the people's government of that province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government on the basis of the national port layout plan, and shall be submitted to the competent department of transport under the State Council for comments. If, within thirty days from the date of receipt of the materials submitted for comments, the competent department of transport under the State Council does not propose any amendments, the port layout plan shall be promulgated and implemented by the relevant people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; if the competent department of transport under the State Council considers that the plan is not consistent with the national port layout plan, it shall, within thirty days from the date of receipt of the materials submitted for comments, propose amendments; if the relevant people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government objects to the amendments, the matter shall be submitted to the State Council for decision.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　港口总体规划由港口行政管理部门征求有关部门和有关军事机关的意见编制。

**Article 10** Port master plans shall be formulated by port administration departments after soliciting opinions from the relevant departments and relevant military authorities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　地理位置重要、吞吐量较大、对经济发展影响较广的主要港口的总体规划，由国务院交通主管部门征求国务院有关部门和有关军事机关的意见后，会同有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，并公布实施。主要港口名录由国务院交通主管部门征求国务院有关部门意见后确定并公布。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府征求国务院交通主管部门的意见后确定本地区的重要港口。重要港口的总体规划由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府征求国务院交通主管部门意见后批准，公布实施。  
前两款规定以外的港口的总体规划，由港口所在地的市、县人民政府批准后公布实施，并报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府备案。  
市、县人民政府港口行政管理部门编制的属于本条第一款、第二款规定范围的港口的总体规划，在报送审批前应当经本级人民政府审核同意。

**Article 11** The master plans of major ports that are geographically important, have a large throughput, and have a significant impact on economic development shall, after the competent department of transport under the State Council solicits opinions from the relevant departments under the State Council and relevant military authorities, be approved by such department jointly with the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, and shall be promulgated and implemented. The list of major ports shall be determined and published by the competent department of transport under the State Council after soliciting opinions from the relevant departments under the State Council.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, after soliciting opinions from the competent department of transport under the State Council, determine the important ports in their respective regions. The master plans of important ports shall be approved by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government after soliciting opinions from the competent department of transport under the State Council, and shall be promulgated and implemented.  
The master plans of ports other than those specified in the preceding two paragraphs shall be approved by the people's government of the city or county where the port is located, and shall be promulgated and implemented, and shall be submitted to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for recordation.  
Where the port administration department of the people's government of a city or county prepares a master plan for a port falling within the scope specified in paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article, such plan shall, before being submitted for approval, be examined and approved by the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　港口规划的修改，按照港口规划制定程序办理。

**Article 12** Amendments to port plans shall be handled in accordance with the procedures for formulating port plans.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　在港口总体规划区内建设港口设施，使用港口深水岸线的，由国务院交通主管部门会同国务院经济综合宏观调控部门批准；建设港口设施，使用非深水岸线的，由港口行政管理部门批准。但是，由国务院或者国务院经济综合宏观调控部门批准建设的项目使用港口岸线，不再另行办理使用港口岸线的审批手续。  
港口深水岸线的标准由国务院交通主管部门制定。

**Article 13** Where port facilities are constructed within a port master plan area and deep-water shoreline of the port is used, the matter shall be approved by the competent department of transport under the State Council jointly with the department of comprehensive macroeconomic regulation under the State Council; where port facilities are constructed and non-deep-water shoreline is used, the matter shall be approved by the port administration department. However, where a project approved for construction by the State Council or by the department of comprehensive macroeconomic regulation under the State Council uses port shoreline, no separate approval procedures for the use of port shoreline need be undergone.  
The standard for deep-water shoreline of ports shall be formulated by the competent department of transport under the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　港口建设应当符合港口规划。不得违反港口规划建设任何港口设施。

**Article 14** Port construction shall conform to port plans. No port facilities shall be constructed in violation of port plans.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　按照国家规定须经有关机关批准的港口建设项目，应当按照国家有关规定办理审批手续，并符合国家有关标准和技术规范。  
建设港口工程项目，应当依法进行环境影响评价。  
港口建设项目的安全设施和环境保护设施，必须与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投入使用。

**Article 15** Port construction projects that require approval from relevant authorities according to the State's provisions shall go through the approval procedures in accordance with the relevant State provisions, and shall comply with the relevant State standards and technical specifications.  
For the construction of port engineering projects, environmental impact assessment shall be conducted according to law.  
The safety facilities and environmental protection facilities of a port construction project must be designed, constructed, and put into use simultaneously with the main project.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　港口建设使用土地和水域，应当依照有关土地管理、海域使用管理、河道管理、航道管理、军事设施保护管理的法律、行政法规以及其他有关法律、行政法规的规定办理。

**Article 16** The use of land and waters for port construction shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the laws and administrative regulations on land administration, sea area use administration, river administration, waterway administration, military facility protection administration, and other relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　港口的危险货物作业场所、实施卫生除害处理的专用场所，应当符合港口总体规划和国家有关安全生产、消防、检验检疫和环境保护的要求，其与人口密集区和港口客运设施的距离应当符合国务院有关部门的规定；经依法办理有关手续后，方可建设。

**Article 17** The places for handling dangerous cargo and the special places for sanitary and decontamination treatment in a port shall conform to the port master plan and the State's requirements for work safety, fire protection, inspection and quarantine, and environmental protection, and the distances between such places and densely populated areas and passenger transport facilities of the port shall conform to the provisions of the relevant departments under the State Council; such places may be constructed only after going through the relevant procedures according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　航标设施以及其他辅助性设施，应当与港口同步建设，并保证按期投入使用。  
港口内有关行政管理机构办公设施的建设应当符合港口总体规划，建设费用不得向港口经营人摊派。

**Article 18** Navigation aid facilities and other auxiliary facilities shall be constructed simultaneously with the port, and shall be ensured to be put into use on schedule.  
The construction of office facilities of relevant administrative agencies within the port shall conform to the port master plan, and the construction costs shall not be apportioned to port operators.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　港口设施建设项目竣工后，应当按照国家有关规定经验收合格，方可投入使用。  
港口设施的所有权，依照有关法律规定确定。

**Article 19** Upon completion of a port facility construction project, it may be put into use only after it has passed acceptance inspection in accordance with the relevant State provisions.  
The ownership of port facilities shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级以上有关人民政府应当保证必要的资金投入，用于港口公用的航道、防波堤、锚地等基础设施的建设和维护。具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 20** The relevant people's governments at or above the county level shall ensure necessary funding for the construction and maintenance of public infrastructure such as fairways, breakwaters, and anchorages of ports. The specific measures shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上有关人民政府应当采取措施，组织建设与港口相配套的航道、铁路、公路、给排水、供电、通信等设施。

**Article 21** The relevant people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to organize the construction of supporting facilities such as waterways, railways, highways, water supply and drainage, power supply, and communication facilities for ports.

### 第三章　港口经营

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　从事港口经营，应当向港口行政管理部门书面申请取得港口经营许可，并依法办理工商登记。  
港口行政管理部门实施港口经营许可，应当遵循公开、公正、公平的原则。  
港口经营包括码头和其他港口设施的经营，港口旅客运输服务经营，在港区内从事货物的装卸、驳运、仓储的经营和港口拖轮经营等。

**Article 22** To engage in port operations, a party shall submit a written application to the port administration department for a port operation permit, and shall undergo industrial and commercial registration according to law.  
The port administration department shall, when implementing port operation permits, adhere to the principles of openness, fairness, and impartiality.  
Port operations include the operation of wharves and other port facilities, the operation of passenger transport services at ports, the operation of cargo loading, unloading, lightering, and storage within port areas, and the operation of port tugs, among others.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　取得港口经营许可，应当有固定的经营场所，有与经营业务相适应的设施、设备、专业技术人员和管理人员，并应当具备法律、法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 23** To obtain a port operation permit, a party shall have a fixed place of business, have facilities, equipment, professional and technical personnel, and management personnel commensurate with the business operations, and shall meet other conditions prescribed by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　港口行政管理部门应当自收到[本法第二十二条](#art-22)第一款规定的书面申请之日起三十日内依法作出许可或者不予许可的决定。予以许可的，颁发港口经营许可证；不予许可的，应当书面通知申请人并告知理由。

**Article 24** The port administration department shall, within thirty days from the date of receipt of a written application as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 22 of this Law, make a decision on granting or denying the permit according to law. If the permit is granted, it shall issue a port operation permit; if the permit is denied, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国务院交通主管部门应当制定港口理货服务标准和规范。  
经营港口理货业务，应当按照规定报港口行政管理部门备案。  
港口理货业务经营人应当公正、准确地办理理货业务；不得兼营[本法第二十二条](#art-22)第三款规定的货物装卸经营业务和仓储经营业务。

**Article 25** The competent department of transport under the State Council shall formulate standards and norms for port tally services.  
To engage in port tally business, the party shall, in accordance with the provisions, report to the port administration department for recordation.  
A port tally business operator shall handle tally business fairly and accurately; it shall not concurrently engage in the cargo loading and unloading operation business and the warehousing operation business stipulated in paragraph 3 of Article 22 of this Law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　港口经营人从事经营活动，必须遵守有关法律、法规，遵守国务院交通主管部门有关港口作业规则的规定，依法履行合同约定的义务，为客户提供公平、良好的服务。  
从事港口旅客运输服务的经营人，应当采取保证旅客安全的有效措施，向旅客提供快捷、便利的服务，保持良好的候船环境。  
港口经营人应当依照有关环境保护的法律、法规的规定，采取有效措施，防治对环境的污染和危害。

**Article 26** Port operators engaging in business activities must comply with relevant laws and regulations, abide by the provisions of the competent department of transport under the State Council on port operation rules, perform the obligations stipulated in the contracts according to law, and provide customers with fair and good services.  
Operators engaged in port passenger transport services shall take effective measures to ensure passenger safety, provide passengers with fast and convenient services, and maintain a good waiting environment.  
Port operators shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations on environmental protection, take effective measures to prevent and control environmental pollution and hazards.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　港口经营人应当优先安排抢险物资、救灾物资和国防建设急需物资的作业。

**Article 27** Port operators shall give priority to the handling of materials for emergency rescue, disaster relief, and materials urgently needed for national defense construction.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　港口经营人应当在其经营场所公布经营服务的收费项目和收费标准；未公布的，不得实施。  
港口经营性收费依法实行政府指导价或者政府定价的，港口经营人应当按照规定执行。

**Article 28** Port operators shall publish the items and standards of charges for their business services at their business premises; those not published shall not be charged.  
Where port operational charges are subject to government-guided prices or government-fixed prices according to law, port operators shall implement them in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家鼓励和保护港口经营活动的公平竞争。  
港口经营人不得实施垄断行为和不正当竞争行为，不得以任何手段强迫他人接受其提供的港口服务。

**Article 29** The State encourages and protects fair competition in port business activities.  
Port operators shall not engage in monopolistic practices or unfair competition, and shall not, by any means, compel others to accept the port services they provide.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　港口行政管理部门依照《中华人民共和国统计法》和有关行政法规的规定要求港口经营人提供的统计资料，港口经营人应当如实提供。  
港口行政管理部门应当按照国家有关规定将港口经营人报送的统计资料及时上报，并为港口经营人保守商业秘密。

**Article 30** Where the port administration department requires port operators to provide statistical data in accordance with the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China and the provisions of relevant administrative regulations, port operators shall provide such data truthfully.  
The port administration department shall, in accordance with the relevant State provisions, promptly report the statistical data submitted by port operators to higher authorities, and shall keep the trade secrets of port operators confidential.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　港口经营人的合法权益受法律保护。任何单位和个人不得向港口经营人摊派或者违法收取费用，不得违法干预港口经营人的经营自主权。

**Article 31** The lawful rights and interests of port operators shall be protected by law. No unit or individual may apportion fees or illegally collect fees from port operators, or illegally interfere with the operational autonomy of port operators.

### 第四章　港口安全与监督管理

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　港口经营人必须依照《中华人民共和国安全生产法》等有关法律、法规和国务院交通主管部门有关港口安全作业规则的规定，加强安全生产管理，建立健全安全生产责任制等规章制度，完善安全生产条件，采取保障安全生产的有效措施，确保安全生产。  
港口经营人应当依法制定本单位的危险货物事故应急预案、重大生产安全事故的旅客紧急疏散和救援预案以及预防自然灾害预案，保障组织实施。

**Article 32** Port operators must, in accordance with the Work Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws and regulations, and the provisions of the competent department of transport under the State Council on port safety operation rules, strengthen work safety management, establish and improve the work safety responsibility system and other rules and regulations, improve work safety conditions, and adopt effective measures to ensure work safety, thereby ensuring safe production.  
Port operators shall, according to law, formulate emergency response plans for dangerous cargo accidents, emergency evacuation and rescue plans for passengers in the event of major production safety accidents, and plans for preventing natural disasters, and shall ensure their implementation.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　港口行政管理部门应当依法制定可能危及社会公共利益的港口危险货物事故应急预案、重大生产安全事故的旅客紧急疏散和救援预案以及预防自然灾害预案，建立健全港口重大生产安全事故的应急救援体系。

**Article 33** Port administration departments shall, according to law, formulate emergency response plans for port dangerous cargo accidents that may endanger public interests, emergency evacuation and rescue plans for passengers in the event of major production safety accidents, and plans for preventing natural disasters, and shall establish and improve the emergency rescue system for major production safety accidents in ports.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　船舶进出港口，应当依照有关水上交通安全的法律、行政法规的规定向海事管理机构报告。海事管理机构接到报告后，应当及时通报港口行政管理部门。  
船舶载运危险货物进出港口，应当按照国务院交通主管部门的规定将危险货物的名称、特性、包装和进出港口的时间报告海事管理机构。海事管理机构接到报告后，应当在国务院交通主管部门规定的时间内作出是否同意的决定，通知报告人，并通报港口行政管理部门。但是，定船舶、定航线、定货种的船舶可以定期报告。

**Article 34** When a vessel enters or leaves a port, it shall report to the maritime administration agency in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on water traffic safety. Upon receiving the report, the maritime administration agency shall promptly notify the port administration department.  
Where a vessel carrying dangerous cargo enters or leaves a port, it shall report to the maritime administration agency the name, nature, packaging of the dangerous cargo and the time of entry or departure in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of transport under the State Council. Upon receiving the report, the maritime administration agency shall, within the time limit prescribed by the competent department of transport under the State Council, make a decision on whether to grant approval, notify the reporting party, and notify the port administration department. However, vessels operating on fixed routes, with fixed cargo types, or on a regular basis may report periodically.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　在港口内进行危险货物的装卸、过驳作业，应当按照国务院交通主管部门的规定将危险货物的名称、特性、包装和作业的时间、地点报告港口行政管理部门。港口行政管理部门接到报告后，应当在国务院交通主管部门规定的时间内作出是否同意的决定，通知报告人，并通报海事管理机构。

**Article 35** Where loading, unloading, or lightering of dangerous cargo is carried out within a port, the name, nature, packaging of the dangerous cargo and the time and place of the operation shall be reported to the port administration department in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of transport under the State Council. Upon receiving the report, the port administration department shall, within the time limit prescribed by the competent department of transport under the State Council, make a decision on whether to grant approval, notify the reporting party, and notify the maritime administration agency.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　港口行政管理部门应当依法对港口安全生产情况实施监督检查，对旅客上下集中、货物装卸量较大或者有特殊用途的码头进行重点巡查；检查中发现安全隐患的，应当责令被检查人立即排除或者限期排除。  
负责安全生产监督管理的部门和其他有关部门依照法律、法规的规定，在各自职责范围内对港口安全生产实施监督检查。

**Article 36** Port administration departments shall, according to law, supervise and inspect the work safety situation at ports, and shall conduct key inspections on wharves where passenger embarkation and disembarkation are concentrated, where cargo loading and unloading volumes are large, or which have special purposes; where safety hazards are found during inspections, they shall order the person being inspected to eliminate the hazards immediately or within a specified period.  
The department responsible for work safety supervision and administration and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations, supervise and inspect port work safety within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　禁止在港口水域内从事养殖、种植活动。  
不得在港口进行可能危及港口安全的采掘、爆破等活动；因工程建设等确需进行的，必须采取相应的安全保护措施，并报经港口行政管理部门批准。港口行政管理部门应当将审批情况及时通报海事管理机构，海事管理机构不再依照有关水上交通安全的法律、行政法规的规定进行审批。  
禁止向港口水域倾倒泥土、砂石以及违反有关环境保护的法律、法规的规定排放超过规定标准的有毒、有害物质。

**Article 37** It is prohibited to engage in aquaculture or planting activities within port waters.  
No activities such as quarrying or blasting that may endanger port safety shall be conducted in a port; where such activities are truly necessary for engineering construction or other reasons, corresponding safety protection measures must be taken, and approval from the port administration department must be obtained. The port administration department shall promptly notify the maritime administration agency of the approval status, and the maritime administration agency shall not conduct separate approval procedures in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on water traffic safety.  
It is prohibited to dump earth, sand, or gravel into port waters, or to discharge toxic or harmful substances exceeding the prescribed standards in violation of the provisions of relevant laws and regulations on environmental protection.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　建设桥梁、水底隧道、水电站等可能影响港口水文条件变化的工程项目，负责审批该项目的部门在审批前应当征求港口行政管理部门的意见。

**Article 38** For construction projects such as bridges, underwater tunnels, and hydroelectric power stations that may affect changes in the hydrological conditions of a port, the department responsible for approving the project shall, before approval, solicit the opinions of the port administration department.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　依照有关水上交通安全的法律、行政法规的规定，进出港口须经引航的船舶，应当向引航机构申请引航。引航的具体办法由国务院交通主管部门规定。

**Article 39** Where, according to the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on water traffic safety, a vessel entering or leaving a port is subject to pilotage, it shall apply to the pilotage organization for pilotage. The specific measures for pilotage shall be formulated by the competent department of transport under the State Council.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　遇有旅客滞留、货物积压阻塞港口的情况，港口行政管理部门应当及时采取有效措施，进行疏港；港口所在地的市、县人民政府认为必要时，可以直接采取措施，进行疏港。

**Article 40** In the event of passenger stranding or cargo backlog obstructing a port, the port administration department shall promptly take effective measures to clear the port; where the people's government of the city or county where the port is located deems it necessary, it may directly take measures to clear the port.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　港口行政管理部门应当组织制定所管理的港口的章程，并向社会公布。  
港口章程的内容应当包括对港口的地理位置、航道条件、港池水深、机械设施和装卸能力等情况的说明，以及本港口贯彻执行有关港口管理的法律、法规和国务院交通主管部门有关规定的具体措施。

**Article 41** A port administration department shall organize the formulation of the port charter for the port under its administration and shall make it public.  
The contents of a port charter shall include a description of the port's geographical location, waterway conditions, water depth of the harbor basin, mechanical facilities and handling capacity, and other relevant information, as well as the specific measures for implementing the laws, regulations, and the relevant provisions of the competent department of transport under the State Council concerning port administration.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　港口行政管理部门依据职责对本法执行情况实施监督检查。  
港口行政管理部门的监督检查人员依法实施监督检查时，有权向被检查单位和有关人员了解有关情况，并可查阅、复制有关资料。  
监督检查人员对检查中知悉的商业秘密，应当保密。  
监督检查人员实施监督检查时，应当出示执法证件。

**Article 42** Port administration departments shall, within their functions and duties, supervise and inspect the implementation of this Law.  
When carrying out supervision and inspection according to law, the supervision and inspection personnel of a port administration department shall have the right to inquire about relevant information from the unit being inspected and relevant persons, and may examine and copy relevant materials.  
Supervision and inspection personnel shall keep confidential any trade secrets they become aware of during inspections.  
When carrying out supervision and inspection, supervision and inspection personnel shall produce their law enforcement credentials.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　监督检查人员应当将监督检查的时间、地点、内容、发现的问题及处理情况作出书面记录，并由监督检查人员和被检查单位的负责人签字；被检查单位的负责人拒绝签字的，监督检查人员应当将情况记录在案，并向港口行政管理部门报告。

**Article 43** Supervision and inspection personnel shall make written records of the time, place, content, problems discovered, and handling of the supervision and inspection, and such records shall be signed by the supervision and inspection personnel and the person in charge of the unit being inspected; if the person in charge of the unit being inspected refuses to sign, the supervision and inspection personnel shall record the fact in the file and report it to the port administration department.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　被检查单位和有关人员应当接受港口行政管理部门依法实施的监督检查，如实提供有关情况和资料，不得拒绝检查或者隐匿、谎报有关情况和资料。

**Article 44** The unit being inspected and relevant persons shall accept the supervision and inspection carried out by the port administration department according to law, and shall truthfully provide relevant information and materials, and shall not refuse inspection or conceal or falsely report relevant information and materials.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　港口经营人、港口理货业务经营人有本法规定的违法行为的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定纳入信用记录，并予以公示。

**Article 45** Where a port operator or a port tally business operator commits an illegal act as provided for in this Law, it shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, be included in the credit record and be publicized.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府或者港口行政管理部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，由作出限期改正决定的机关申请人民法院强制拆除违法建设的设施；可以处五万元以下罚款：  
（一）违反港口规划建设港口、码头或者其他港口设施的；  
（二）未经依法批准，建设港口设施使用港口岸线的。  
建设项目的审批部门对违反港口规划的建设项目予以批准的，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 46** Where any of the following acts is committed, the local people's government at or above the county level or the port administration department shall order correction within a specified period; if correction is not made within the specified period, the authority that made the order for correction within a specified period shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory demolition of the illegally constructed facilities; and a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may also be imposed:  
(1) constructing a port, wharf, or other port facility in violation of the port plan;  
(2) constructing port facilities using port shoreline without approval according to law.  
Where the approval department of a construction project approves a construction project that violates the port plan, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　在港口建设的危险货物作业场所、实施卫生除害处理的专用场所与人口密集区或者港口客运设施的距离不符合国务院有关部门的规定的，由港口行政管理部门责令停止建设或者使用，限期改正，可以处五万元以下罚款。

**Article 47** Where the distance between a place for handling dangerous cargo constructed in a port or a special place for sanitary and decontamination treatment and a densely populated area or a port passenger transport facility does not conform to the provisions of the relevant departments under the State Council, the port administration department shall order suspension of construction or use and correction within a specified period, and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　码头或者港口装卸设施、客运设施未经验收合格，擅自投入使用的，由港口行政管理部门责令停止使用，限期改正，可以处五万元以下罚款。

**Article 48** Where a wharf or a port loading and unloading facility or passenger transport facility is put into use without authorization before passing acceptance inspection, the port administration department shall order the cessation of use and correction within a specified period, and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　未依法取得港口经营许可证从事港口经营，或者港口理货业务经营人兼营货物装卸经营业务、仓储经营业务的，由港口行政管理部门责令停止违法经营，没收违法所得；违法所得十万元以上的，并处违法所得二倍以上五倍以下罚款；违法所得不足十万元的，处五万元以上二十万元以下罚款。

**Article 49** Where a party engages in port operations without obtaining a port operation permit according to law, or a port tally business operator concurrently engages in cargo loading and unloading operation business or warehousing operation business, the port administration department shall order the cessation of illegal operations and confiscate the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are 100,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　港口经营人不优先安排抢险物资、救灾物资、国防建设急需物资的作业的，由港口行政管理部门责令改正；造成严重后果的，吊销港口经营许可证。

**Article 50** Where a port operator fails to give priority to the handling of materials for emergency rescue, disaster relief, or materials urgently needed for national defense construction, the port administration department shall order correction; if serious consequences are caused, the port operation permit shall be revoked.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　港口经营人违反有关法律、行政法规的规定，在经营活动中实施垄断行为或者不正当竞争行为的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定承担法律责任。

**Article 51** Where a port operator, in violation of the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, commits monopolistic acts or acts of unfair competition in its business activities, it shall bear legal liability in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　港口经营人违反[本法第三十二条](#art-32)关于安全生产的规定的，由港口行政管理部门或者其他依法负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门依法给予处罚；情节严重的，由港口行政管理部门吊销港口经营许可证，并对其主要负责人依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 52** Where a port operator violates the provisions of Article 32 of this Law on production safety, the port administrative department or other department legally responsible for the supervision and administration of production safety shall impose a penalty according to law; if the circumstances are serious, the port administrative department shall revoke the port operation license and shall impose a sanction on the principal responsible person according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　船舶进出港口，未依照[本法第三十四条](#art-34)的规定向海事管理机构报告的，由海事管理机构依照有关水上交通安全的法律、行政法规的规定处罚。

**Article 53** Where a vessel entering or leaving a port fails to report to the maritime administrative agency in accordance with the provisions of Article 34 of this Law, the maritime administrative agency shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations on water traffic safety.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　未依法向港口行政管理部门报告并经其同意，在港口内进行危险货物的装卸、过驳作业的，由港口行政管理部门责令停止作业，处五千元以上五万元以下罚款。

**Article 54** Where a party fails to report to and obtain approval from the port administrative department according to law before carrying out loading, unloading, or lightering operations of dangerous goods in a port, the port administrative department shall order the operations to be stopped and shall impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　在港口水域内从事养殖、种植活动的，由海事管理机构责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，强制拆除养殖、种植设施，拆除费用由违法行为人承担；可以处一万元以下罚款。

**Article 55** Where a party engages in aquaculture or planting activities within the port waters, the maritime administrative agency shall order correction within a specified time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, the aquaculture or planting facilities shall be forcibly removed, and the removal costs shall be borne by the violator; a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan may also be imposed.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　未经依法批准在港口进行可能危及港口安全的采掘、爆破等活动的，向港口水域倾倒泥土、砂石的，由港口行政管理部门责令停止违法行为，限期消除因此造成的安全隐患；逾期不消除的，强制消除，因此发生的费用由违法行为人承担；处五千元以上五万元以下罚款；依照有关水上交通安全的法律、行政法规的规定由海事管理机构处罚的，依照其规定；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 56** Where a party, without approval according to law, engages in excavation, blasting, or other activities that may endanger port safety in a port, or dumps earth or gravel into port waters, the port administrative department shall order the cessation of the illegal act, and order the elimination of the safety hazards thus caused within a specified time limit; if elimination is not made within the time limit, the department shall forcibly eliminate them, and the costs thus incurred shall be borne by the violator; a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; where a penalty is to be imposed by the maritime administrative agency in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations on water traffic safety, that shall prevail; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　交通主管部门、港口行政管理部门、海事管理机构等不依法履行职责，有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）违法批准建设港口设施使用港口岸线，或者违法批准船舶载运危险货物进出港口、违法批准在港口内进行危险货物的装卸、过驳作业的；  
（二）对不符合法定条件的申请人给予港口经营许可的；  
（三）发现取得经营许可的港口经营人不再具备法定许可条件而不及时吊销许可证的；  
（四）不依法履行监督检查职责，对违反港口规划建设港口、码头或者其他港口设施的行为，未经依法许可从事港口经营业务的行为，不遵守安全生产管理规定的行为，危及港口作业安全的行为，以及其他违反本法规定的行为，不依法予以查处的。

**Article 57** Where the competent department of transportation, the port administrative department, the maritime administrative agency, or other department fails to perform its duties according to law and commits any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and the other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) illegally approving the use of port shoreline for constructing port facilities, or illegally approving the entry or departure of vessels carrying dangerous goods, or illegally approving loading, unloading, or lightering operations of dangerous goods in a port;  
(2) granting port operation licenses to applicants who do not meet the statutory requirements;  
(3) failing to revoke the license in a timely manner upon discovering that a port operator who has obtained the operation license no longer meets the statutory licensing conditions;  
(4) failing to perform the duty of supervision and inspection according to law, and failing to investigate and deal with according to law acts of constructing ports, wharves or other port facilities in violation of port plans, acts of engaging in port operation without legal permission, acts of failing to comply with production safety management regulations, acts endangering port operation safety, and other acts violating the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　行政机关违法干预港口经营人的经营自主权的，由其上级行政机关或者监察机关责令改正；向港口经营人摊派财物或者违法收取费用的，责令退回；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分。

**Article 58** Where an administrative organ illegally intervenes in the operational autonomy of a port operator, its superior administrative organ or the supervisory organ shall order correction; where it apportions property or illegally collects fees from a port operator, it shall be ordered to return them; if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly in charge and the other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　对航行国际航线的船舶开放的港口，由有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府按照国家有关规定商国务院有关部门和有关军事机关同意后，报国务院批准。

**Article 59** Ports open to vessels on international voyages shall be subject to the approval of the State Council after the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government consult with the relevant departments of the State Council and relevant military organs with consent in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　渔业港口的管理工作由县级以上人民政府渔业行政主管部门负责。具体管理办法由国务院规定。  
前款所称渔业港口，是指专门为渔业生产服务、供渔业船舶停泊、避风、装卸渔获物、补充渔需物资的人工港口或者自然港湾，包括综合性港口中渔业专用的码头、渔业专用的水域和渔船专用的锚地。

**Article 60** The administrative work of fishing ports shall be the responsibility of the fishery administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level. Specific administrative measures shall be formulated by the State Council.  
The term 'fishing port' as mentioned in the preceding paragraph refers to an artificial port or natural harbor specially serving fishery production and used for mooring of fishing vessels, shelter from wind, loading and unloading of catches, and replenishment of fishery supplies, including fishery-specific wharves, fishery-specific waters, and fishing vessel-specific anchorages within a comprehensive port.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　军事港口的建设和管理办法由国务院、中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 61** Measures for the construction and administration of military ports shall be formulated by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　本法自2004年1月1日起施行。

**Article 62** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2004.
