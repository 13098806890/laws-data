# 中华人民共和国种子法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2021-12-24  
**生效日期**：2022-03-01

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护和合理利用种质资源，规范品种选育、种子生产经营和管理行为，加强种业科学技术研究，鼓励育种创新，保护植物新品种权，维护种子生产经营者、使用者的合法权益，提高种子质量，发展现代种业，保障国家粮食安全，促进农业和林业的发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of protecting and rationally utilizing germplasm resources, regulating variety breeding, seed production, operation and management, strengthening scientific and technological research in the seed industry, encouraging breeding innovation, protecting rights to new plant varieties, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of seed producers, operators and users, improving seed quality, developing the modern seed industry, ensuring national food security, and promoting the development of agriculture and forestry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事品种选育、种子生产经营和管理等活动，适用本法。  
本法所称种子，是指农作物和林木的种植材料或者繁殖材料，包括籽粒、果实、根、茎、苗、芽、叶、花等。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to activities such as variety breeding, seed production, operation and management within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
For the purposes of this Law, "seeds" refer to planting or propagation materials of crops and forest trees, including grains, fruits, roots, stems, seedlings, buds, leaves, flowers, etc.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门分别主管全国农作物种子和林木种子工作；县级以上地方人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门分别主管本行政区域内农作物种子和林木种子工作。  
各级人民政府及其有关部门应当采取措施，加强种子执法和监督，依法惩处侵害农民权益的种子违法行为。

**Article 3** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the State Council shall respectively be in charge of the work on crop seeds and forest tree seeds nationwide; the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall respectively be in charge of the work on crop seeds and forest tree seeds within their respective administrative regions.  
People's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall take measures to strengthen seed law enforcement and supervision, and shall punish, according to law, illegal seed acts that infringe upon the rights and interests of farmers.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家扶持种质资源保护工作和选育、生产、更新、推广使用良种，鼓励品种选育和种子生产经营相结合，奖励在种质资源保护工作和良种选育、推广等工作中成绩显著的单位和个人。

**Article 4** The State shall support the protection of germplasm resources and the breeding, production, renewal, popularization and use of improved varieties, encourage the combination of variety breeding with seed production and operation, and reward units and individuals that have achieved remarkable results in the protection of germplasm resources and the breeding and popularization of improved varieties.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　省级以上人民政府应当根据科教兴农方针和农业、林业发展的需要制定种业发展规划并组织实施。

**Article 5** People's governments at or above the provincial level shall formulate plans for the development of the seed industry in accordance with the policy of revitalizing agriculture through science and education and the needs of agricultural and forestry development, and organize their implementation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　省级以上人民政府建立种子储备制度，主要用于发生灾害时的生产需要及余缺调剂，保障农业和林业生产安全。对储备的种子应当定期检验和更新。种子储备的具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 6** People's governments at or above the provincial level shall establish a seed reserve system, mainly for production needs in the event of disasters and for regulating surplus and deficiency, so as to ensure the safety of agricultural and forestry production. Seeds in reserve shall be inspected and renewed on a regular basis. The specific measures for seed reserves shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　转基因植物品种的选育、试验、审定和推广应当进行安全性评价，并采取严格的安全控制措施。国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当加强跟踪监管并及时公告有关转基因植物品种审定和推广的信息。具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 7** The breeding, testing, approval and popularization of genetically modified plant varieties shall be subject to safety assessment and strict safety control measures shall be taken. The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the State Council shall strengthen follow-up supervision and promptly publicize information on the approval and popularization of genetically modified plant varieties. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.

### 第二章　种质资源保护

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家依法保护种质资源，任何单位和个人不得侵占和破坏种质资源。  
禁止采集或者采伐国家重点保护的天然种质资源。因科研等特殊情况需要采集或者采伐的，应当经国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的农业农村、林业草原主管部门批准。

**Article 8** The State shall protect germplasm resources according to law, and no unit or individual may occupy or destroy germplasm resources.  
It is prohibited to collect or fell natural germplasm resources under special State protection. Where collection or felling is necessary due to special circumstances such as scientific research, approval shall be obtained from the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, or forestry and grassland, under the State Council or the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家有计划地普查、收集、整理、鉴定、登记、保存、交流和利用种质资源，重点收集珍稀、濒危、特有资源和特色地方品种，定期公布可供利用的种质资源目录。具体办法由国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门规定。

**Article 9** The State shall, in a planned manner, survey, collect, sort, identify, register, preserve, exchange and utilize germplasm resources, with emphasis on collecting rare, endangered, endemic resources and distinctive local varieties, and shall regularly publish a catalogue of available germplasm resources. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当建立种质资源库、种质资源保护区或者种质资源保护地。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门可以根据需要建立种质资源库、种质资源保护区、种质资源保护地。种质资源库、种质资源保护区、种质资源保护地的种质资源属公共资源，依法开放利用。  
占用种质资源库、种质资源保护区或者种质资源保护地的，需经原设立机关同意。

**Article 10** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the State Council shall establish germplasm resource banks, germplasm resource protection zones or germplasm resource protection areas. The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government may establish germplasm resource banks, germplasm resource protection zones or germplasm resource protection areas as needed. The germplasm resources in such banks, zones or areas are public resources and shall be open to utilization according to law.  
Anyone who occupies a germplasm resource bank, germplasm resource protection zone or germplasm resource protection area shall obtain the consent of the original establishment authority.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家对种质资源享有主权。任何单位和个人向境外提供种质资源，或者与境外机构、个人开展合作研究利用种质资源的，应当报国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门批准，并同时提交国家共享惠益的方案。国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门可以委托省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门接收申请材料。国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当将批准情况通报国务院生态环境主管部门。  
从境外引进种质资源的，依照国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门的有关规定办理。

**Article 11** The State shall exercise sovereignty over germplasm resources. Any unit or individual that provides germplasm resources abroad or conducts cooperative research and utilization of germplasm resources with foreign institutions or individuals shall submit an application to the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the State Council for approval, and shall simultaneously submit a national benefit-sharing plan. The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the State Council may entrust the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government to receive application materials. The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the State Council shall notify the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council of the approval status.  
The introduction of germplasm resources from abroad shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the State Council.

### 第三章　品种选育、审定与登记

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家支持科研院所及高等院校重点开展育种的基础性、前沿性和应用技术研究以及生物育种技术研究，支持常规作物、主要造林树种育种和无性繁殖材料选育等公益性研究。  
国家鼓励种子企业充分利用公益性研究成果，培育具有自主知识产权的优良品种；鼓励种子企业与科研院所及高等院校构建技术研发平台，开展主要粮食作物、重要经济作物育种攻关，建立以市场为导向、利益共享、风险共担的产学研相结合的种业技术创新体系。  
国家加强种业科技创新能力建设，促进种业科技成果转化，维护种业科技人员的合法权益。

**Article 12** The State shall support research institutes and institutions of higher education in focusing on basic, frontier and applied technology research in breeding as well as research on bio-breeding technologies, and shall support public-interest research such as conventional crop breeding, major afforestation tree species breeding, and selection of asexual propagation materials.  
The State shall encourage seed enterprises to make full use of public-interest research achievements to cultivate excellent varieties with independent intellectual property rights; shall encourage seed enterprises to establish technology research and development platforms with research institutes and institutions of higher education, to carry out key breeding projects for major food crops and important cash crops, and to establish a market-oriented, benefit-sharing, risk-sharing industry-university-research integrated seed industry technology innovation system.  
The State shall strengthen the construction of seed industry scientific and technological innovation capacity, promote the transformation of seed industry scientific and technological achievements, and safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of seed industry scientific and technical personnel.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　由财政资金支持形成的育种发明专利权和植物新品种权，除涉及国家安全、国家利益和重大社会公共利益的外，授权项目承担者依法取得。  
由财政资金支持为主形成的育种成果的转让、许可等应当依法公开进行，禁止私自交易。

**Article 13** Patent rights for breeding inventions and rights to new plant varieties formed with financial support, except those involving national security, national interests or major public interests, shall be granted to the project undertakers to acquire according to law.  
The transfer, licensing, etc. of breeding achievements formed mainly with financial support shall be conducted publicly according to law, and private transactions shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　单位和个人因林业草原主管部门为选育林木良种建立测定林、试验林、优树收集区、基因库等而减少经济收入的，批准建立的林业草原主管部门应当按照国家有关规定给予经济补偿。

**Article 14** Where units or individuals suffer a reduction in economic income due to the establishment of measurement forests, test forests, plus tree collection areas, gene banks, etc. by the competent departments of forestry and grassland for the purpose of breeding improved forest tree varieties, the competent departments of forestry and grassland that approved the establishment shall provide economic compensation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家对主要农作物和主要林木实行品种审定制度。主要农作物品种和主要林木品种在推广前应当通过国家级或者省级审定。由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业草原主管部门确定的主要林木品种实行省级审定。  
申请审定的品种应当符合特异性、一致性、稳定性要求。  
主要农作物品种和主要林木品种的审定办法由国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门规定。审定办法应当体现公正、公开、科学、效率的原则，有利于产量、品质、抗性等的提高与协调，有利于适应市场和生活消费需要的品种的推广。在制定、修改审定办法时，应当充分听取育种者、种子使用者、生产经营者和相关行业代表意见。

**Article 15** The State shall apply a variety approval system to major crops and major forest trees. Varieties of major crops and major forest trees shall be subject to national or provincial approval before being popularized. Major forest tree varieties determined by the competent department of forestry and grassland of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be subject to provincial approval.  
Varieties applied for approval shall satisfy the requirements of distinctness, uniformity and stability.  
The measures for the approval of varieties of major crops and major forest trees shall be prescribed by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the State Council. The approval measures shall reflect the principles of fairness, openness, science and efficiency, facilitate the increase and coordination of yield, quality, resistance, etc., and facilitate the popularization of varieties that meet the needs of the market and consumers. When formulating or revising the approval measures, opinions of breeders, seed users, producers and operators, and representatives of relevant industries shall be fully solicited.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的农业农村、林业草原主管部门分别设立由专业人员组成的农作物品种和林木品种审定委员会。品种审定委员会承担主要农作物品种和主要林木品种的审定工作，建立包括申请文件、品种审定试验数据、种子样品、审定意见和审定结论等内容的审定档案，保证可追溯。在审定通过的品种依法公布的相关信息中应当包括审定意见情况，接受监督。  
品种审定实行回避制度。品种审定委员会委员、工作人员及相关测试、试验人员应当忠于职守，公正廉洁。对单位和个人举报或者监督检查发现的上述人员的违法行为，省级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门和有关机关应当及时依法处理。

**Article 16** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall respectively establish variety approval committees composed of professionals for crop varieties and forest tree varieties. The variety approval committees shall undertake the approval of varieties of major crops and major forest trees, and establish approval archives including application documents, variety approval test data, seed samples, approval opinions and approval conclusions, ensuring traceability. The relevant information published according to law on approved varieties shall include the approval opinions, subject to supervision.  
A conflict of interest avoidance system shall be implemented for variety approval. Members of variety approval committees, staff members and related testing and trial personnel shall be devoted to their duties, be impartial and honest. Where illegal acts of the above-mentioned personnel are reported by units or individuals or discovered through supervision and inspection, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the people's governments at or above the provincial level and relevant authorities shall promptly handle them according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　实行选育生产经营相结合，符合国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门规定条件的种子企业，对其自主研发的主要农作物品种、主要林木品种可以按照审定办法自行完成试验，达到审定标准的，品种审定委员会应当颁发审定证书。种子企业对试验数据的真实性负责，保证可追溯，接受省级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门和社会的监督。

**Article 17** A seed enterprise that integrates breeding, production and operation and meets the conditions prescribed by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the State Council may, for its self-developed major crop varieties and major forest tree varieties, complete tests on its own in accordance with the approval measures; if the approval standards are met, the variety approval committee shall issue an approval certificate. The seed enterprise shall be responsible for the authenticity of the test data, ensure traceability, and be subject to supervision by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the people's governments at or above the provincial level and society.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　审定未通过的农作物品种和林木品种，申请人有异议的，可以向原审定委员会或者国家级审定委员会申请复审。

**Article 18** Where an applicant disagrees with the disapproval of a crop variety or forest tree variety, the applicant may apply for a review to the original approval committee or the national approval committee.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　通过国家级审定的农作物品种和林木良种由国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门公告，可以在全国适宜的生态区域推广。通过省级审定的农作物品种和林木良种由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门公告，可以在本行政区域内适宜的生态区域推广；其他省、自治区、直辖市属于同一适宜生态区的地域引种农作物品种、林木良种的，引种者应当将引种的品种和区域报所在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门备案。  
引种本地区没有自然分布的林木品种，应当按照国家引种标准通过试验。

**Article 19** Crop varieties and improved forest tree varieties that have passed national approval shall be announced by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the State Council, and may be popularized in suitable ecological regions nationwide. Crop varieties and improved forest tree varieties that have passed provincial approval shall be announced by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and may be popularized in suitable ecological regions within their respective administrative regions; where a region in another province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government that belongs to the same suitable ecological zone introduces crop varieties or improved forest tree varieties, the introducer shall report the introduced varieties and the region to the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the introducer is located for recordation.  
Forest tree varieties that are not naturally distributed in the local area shall be introduced only after passing tests in accordance with the national introduction standards.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当完善品种选育、审定工作的区域协作机制，促进优良品种的选育和推广。

**Article 20** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall improve the regional coordination mechanism for variety breeding and approval, so as to promote the breeding and popularization of fine varieties.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　审定通过的农作物品种和林木良种出现不可克服的严重缺陷等情形不宜继续推广、销售的，经原审定委员会审核确认后，撤销审定，由原公告部门发布公告，停止推广、销售。

**Article 21** Where a crop variety or improved tree variety that has passed审定 is no longer suitable for continued promotion or sale due to insurmountable serious defects or other circumstances, upon review and confirmation by the original审定 committee, the审定 shall be revoked, and the original announcement department shall issue a public announcement to stop promotion and sale.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家对部分非主要农作物实行品种登记制度。列入非主要农作物登记目录的品种在推广前应当登记。  
实行品种登记的农作物范围应当严格控制，并根据保护生物多样性、保证消费安全和用种安全的原则确定。登记目录由国务院农业农村主管部门制定和调整。  
申请者申请品种登记应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门提交申请文件和种子样品，并对其真实性负责，保证可追溯，接受监督检查。申请文件包括品种的种类、名称、来源、特性、育种过程以及特异性、一致性、稳定性测试报告等。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门自受理品种登记申请之日起二十个工作日内，对申请者提交的申请文件进行书面审查，符合要求的，报国务院农业农村主管部门予以登记公告。  
对已登记品种存在申请文件、种子样品不实的，由国务院农业农村主管部门撤销该品种登记，并将该申请者的违法信息记入社会诚信档案，向社会公布；给种子使用者和其他种子生产经营者造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
对已登记品种出现不可克服的严重缺陷等情形的，由国务院农业农村主管部门撤销登记，并发布公告，停止推广。  
非主要农作物品种登记办法由国务院农业农村主管部门规定。

**Article 22** The State shall implement a variety registration system for certain non-major crops. Varieties listed in the registration catalogue of non-major crops shall be registered before promotion.  
The scope of crops subject to variety registration shall be strictly controlled and determined in accordance with the principles of protecting biodiversity, ensuring consumption safety, and ensuring seed use safety. The registration catalogue shall be formulated and adjusted by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.  
An applicant for variety registration shall submit application documents and seed samples to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall be responsible for their authenticity, ensure traceability, and accept supervision and inspection. The application documents shall include the type, name, source, characteristics, breeding process, and test reports on distinctness, uniformity, and stability of the variety.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within twenty working days from the date of accepting the variety registration application, conduct a written review of the application documents submitted by the applicant. If the requirements are met, it shall report to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council for registration and public announcement.  
If the application documents or seed samples of a registered variety are found to be false, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall revoke the registration of such variety and record the applicant's illegal information in the social credit file, and make it public; if losses are caused to seed users or other seed producers and operators, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.  
If a registered variety has insurmountable serious defects or other circumstances, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall revoke the registration and issue a public announcement to stop promotion.  
The measures for the registration of non-major crop varieties shall be prescribed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　应当审定的农作物品种未经审定的，不得发布广告、推广、销售。  
应当审定的林木品种未经审定通过的，不得作为良种推广、销售，但生产确需使用的，应当经林木品种审定委员会认定。  
应当登记的农作物品种未经登记的，不得发布广告、推广，不得以登记品种的名义销售。

**Article 23** No crop variety that is subject to审定 but has not been审定 may be advertised, promoted, or sold.  
No tree variety that is subject to审定 but has not passed审定 may be promoted or sold as an improved variety; however, if it is actually needed for production, it shall be recognized by the tree variety审定 committee.  
No crop variety that is subject to registration but has not been registered may be advertised, promoted, or sold under the name of a registered variety.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　在中国境内没有经常居所或者营业场所的境外机构、个人在境内申请品种审定或者登记的，应当委托具有法人资格的境内种子企业代理。

**Article 24** Where an overseas institution or individual without a habitual residence or business site within the territory of China applies for variety审定 or registration within the territory, it shall entrust a domestic seed enterprise with legal person status to act as an agent.

### 第四章　新品种保护

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家实行植物新品种保护制度。对国家植物品种保护名录内经过人工选育或者发现的野生植物加以改良，具备新颖性、特异性、一致性、稳定性和适当命名的植物品种，由国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门授予植物新品种权，保护植物新品种权所有人的合法权益。植物新品种权的内容和归属、授予条件、申请和受理、审查与批准，以及期限、终止和无效等依照本法、有关法律和行政法规规定执行。  
国家鼓励和支持种业科技创新、植物新品种培育及成果转化。取得植物新品种权的品种得到推广应用的，育种者依法获得相应的经济利益。

**Article 25** The State shall implement a plant variety protection system. For plant varieties within the national plant variety protection list that are artificially bred or improved from discovered wild plants, and that possess novelty, distinctness, uniformity, stability, and an appropriate denomination, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and of forestry and grassland of the State Council shall grant plant variety rights and protect the lawful rights and interests of the holders of such rights. The content and attribution of plant variety rights, conditions for grant, application and acceptance, examination and approval, as well as duration, termination, and invalidation, shall be executed in accordance with this Law, relevant laws, and administrative regulations.  
The State shall encourage and support scientific and technological innovation in the seed industry, the breeding of new plant varieties, and the transformation of achievements. Where a variety for which plant variety rights have been obtained is promoted and applied, the breeder shall obtain corresponding economic benefits according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　一个植物新品种只能授予一项植物新品种权。两个以上的申请人分别就同一个品种申请植物新品种权的，植物新品种权授予最先申请的人；同时申请的，植物新品种权授予最先完成该品种育种的人。  
对违反法律，危害社会公共利益、生态环境的植物新品种，不授予植物新品种权。

**Article 26** Only one plant variety right may be granted for a new plant variety. Where two or more applicants separately apply for a plant variety right for the same variety, the plant variety right shall be granted to the applicant who first files the application; if the applications are filed simultaneously, the plant variety right shall be granted to the person who first completed the breeding of the variety.  
No plant variety right shall be granted for a new plant variety that violates the law or endangers the public interest or the ecological environment.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　授予植物新品种权的植物新品种名称，应当与相同或者相近的植物属或者种中已知品种的名称相区别。该名称经授权后即为该植物新品种的通用名称。  
下列名称不得用于授权品种的命名：  
（一）仅以数字表示的；  
（二）违反社会公德的；  
（三）对植物新品种的特征、特性或者育种者身份等容易引起误解的。  
同一植物品种在申请新品种保护、品种审定、品种登记、推广、销售时只能使用同一个名称。生产推广、销售的种子应当与申请植物新品种保护、品种审定、品种登记时提供的样品相符。

**Article 27** The denomination of a new plant variety for which a plant variety right is granted shall be distinguishable from the denominations of known varieties of the same or closely related plant genus or species. Such denomination, after being granted, shall become the common name of the new plant variety.  
The following denominations shall not be used for naming a protected variety:  
(1) those consisting solely of numerals;  
(2) those contrary to social morality; or  
(3) those likely to cause confusion regarding the characteristics, traits, or identity of the breeder of the new plant variety.  
The same plant variety may only use the same denomination when applying for new variety protection, variety审定, variety registration, promotion, and sale. The seeds produced, promoted, and sold shall correspond to the samples provided when applying for new plant variety protection, variety审定, and variety registration.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　植物新品种权所有人对其授权品种享有排他的独占权。植物新品种权所有人可以将植物新品种权许可他人实施，并按照合同约定收取许可使用费；许可使用费可以采取固定价款、从推广收益中提成等方式收取。  
任何单位或者个人未经植物新品种权所有人许可，不得生产、繁殖和为繁殖而进行处理、许诺销售、销售、进口、出口以及为实施上述行为储存该授权品种的繁殖材料，不得为商业目的将该授权品种的繁殖材料重复使用于生产另一品种的繁殖材料。本法、有关法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。  
实施前款规定的行为，涉及由未经许可使用授权品种的繁殖材料而获得的收获材料的，应当得到植物新品种权所有人的许可；但是，植物新品种权所有人对繁殖材料已有合理机会行使其权利的除外。  
对实质性派生品种实施第二款、第三款规定行为的，应当征得原始品种的植物新品种权所有人的同意。  
实质性派生品种制度的实施步骤和办法由国务院规定。

**Article 28** The holder of a plant variety right shall have the exclusive right over its protected variety. The holder may license others to exploit the plant variety right and collect a license fee in accordance with the contract; the license fee may be collected in the form of a fixed price, a royalty based on promotion income, or other methods.  
No entity or individual may, without the authorization of the holder of a plant variety right, produce, reproduce, or process for reproduction, offer for sale, sell, import, export, or store the reproductive material of the protected variety for the purpose of carrying out the aforesaid acts, nor may it repeatedly use the reproductive material of the protected variety for the commercial production of the reproductive material of another variety. This shall not apply unless otherwise provided for by this Law, relevant laws, or administrative regulations.  
Where the acts specified in the preceding paragraph involve harvested material obtained through the unauthorized use of the reproductive material of a protected variety, the authorization of the holder of the plant variety right shall be obtained; except where the holder has had a reasonable opportunity to exercise its right over the reproductive material.  
Where the acts specified in paragraphs 2 and 3 are carried out with respect to an essentially derived variety, the consent of the holder of the plant variety right of the initial variety shall be obtained.  
The implementing steps and measures for the essentially derived variety system shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　在下列情况下使用授权品种的，可以不经植物新品种权所有人许可，不向其支付使用费，但不得侵犯植物新品种权所有人依照本法、有关法律、行政法规享有的其他权利：  
（一）利用授权品种进行育种及其他科研活动；  
（二）农民自繁自用授权品种的繁殖材料。

**Article 29** The use of a protected variety under the following circumstances may be done without the authorization of the holder of the plant variety right and without payment of royalties, provided that the other rights enjoyed by the holder under this Law, relevant laws, and administrative regulations are not infringed:  
(1) using the protected variety for breeding and other scientific research activities; and  
(2) farmers reproducing and using the reproductive material of the protected variety for their own use.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　为了国家利益或者社会公共利益，国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门可以作出实施植物新品种权强制许可的决定，并予以登记和公告。  
取得实施强制许可的单位或者个人不享有独占的实施权，并且无权允许他人实施。

**Article 30** For the sake of national interest or public interest, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and of forestry and grassland of the State Council may make a decision to grant a compulsory license to exploit a plant variety right, which shall be registered and publicly announced.  
The entity or individual that obtains a compulsory license shall not enjoy the exclusive right of exploitation and shall have no right to authorize others to exploit it.

### 第五章　种子生产经营

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　从事种子进出口业务的种子生产经营许可证，由国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门核发。国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门可以委托省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门接收申请材料。  
从事主要农作物杂交种子及其亲本种子、林木良种繁殖材料生产经营的，以及符合国务院农业农村主管部门规定条件的实行选育生产经营相结合的农作物种子企业的种子生产经营许可证，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门核发。  
前两款规定以外的其他种子的生产经营许可证，由生产经营者所在地县级以上地方人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门核发。  
只从事非主要农作物种子和非主要林木种子生产的，不需要办理种子生产经营许可证。

**Article 31** Seed production and operation licenses for seed import and export business shall be issued by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and of forestry and grassland of the State Council. The said departments may entrust the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and of forestry and grassland of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government to receive application materials.  
Where a party engages in the production and operation of hybrid seeds of major crops and their parent seeds, or of improved tree variety reproductive materials, or where a crop seed enterprise that combines breeding with production and operation meets the conditions prescribed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, the seed production and operation license shall be issued by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and of forestry and grassland of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
Seed production and operation licenses for seeds other than those specified in the preceding two paragraphs shall be issued by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and of forestry and grassland of the local people's government at or above the county level where the producer or operator is located.  
If a party only produces seeds of non-major crops and non-major tree species, it is not required to obtain a seed production and operation license.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　申请取得种子生产经营许可证的，应当具有与种子生产经营相适应的生产经营设施、设备及专业技术人员，以及法规和国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门规定的其他条件。  
从事种子生产的，还应当同时具有繁殖种子的隔离和培育条件，具有无检疫性有害生物的种子生产地点或者县级以上人民政府林业草原主管部门确定的采种林。  
申请领取具有植物新品种权的种子生产经营许可证的，应当征得植物新品种权所有人的书面同意。

**Article 32** An applicant for a seed production and operation license shall have production and operation facilities, equipment, and professional technical personnel commensurate with seed production and operation, as well as other conditions prescribed by laws, regulations, and the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and of forestry and grassland of the State Council.  
If the party engages in seed production, it shall also have the isolation and cultivation conditions for reproducing seeds, and have a seed production site free of quarantine pests or a seed collection forest designated by the competent department of forestry and grassland of the people's government at or above the county level.  
If an applicant applies for a seed production and operation license involving plant variety rights, it shall obtain the written consent of the holder of the plant variety right.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　种子生产经营许可证应当载明生产经营者名称、地址、法定代表人、生产种子的品种、地点和种子经营的范围、有效期限、有效区域等事项。  
前款事项发生变更的，应当自变更之日起三十日内，向原核发许可证机关申请变更登记。  
除本法另有规定外，禁止任何单位和个人无种子生产经营许可证或者违反种子生产经营许可证的规定生产、经营种子。禁止伪造、变造、买卖、租借种子生产经营许可证。

**Article 33** A seed production and operation license shall state the name, address, legal representative of the producer or operator, the variety and location of seed production, and the scope of seed operation, period of validity, area of validity, and other matters.  
If any of the matters specified in the preceding paragraph changes, an application for change of registration shall be made to the original license-issuing authority within thirty days from the date of change.  
Except as otherwise provided in this Law, no entity or individual may produce or operate seeds without a seed production and operation license or in violation of the provisions of such license. The forgery, alteration, sale, or lending of seed production and operation licenses is prohibited.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　种子生产应当执行种子生产技术规程和种子检验、检疫规程，保证种子符合净度、纯度、发芽率等质量要求和检疫要求。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当指导、支持种子生产经营者采用先进的种子生产技术，改进生产工艺，提高种子质量。

**Article 34** Seed production shall comply with the seed production technical regulations and seed testing and quarantine regulations to ensure that seeds meet the quality requirements such as purity, germination rate, and quarantine requirements.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and of forestry and grassland of the people's government at or above the county level shall guide and support seed producers and operators in adopting advanced seed production techniques, improving production processes, and enhancing seed quality.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　在林木种子生产基地内采集种子的，由种子生产基地的经营者组织进行，采集种子应当按照国家有关标准进行。  
禁止抢采掠青、损坏母树，禁止在劣质林内、劣质母树上采集种子。

**Article 35** Seed collection within a tree seed production base shall be organized by the operator of the base and conducted in accordance with relevant national standards.  
Premature collection, stripping of green seeds, and damaging of mother trees are prohibited. Collection of seeds from inferior forests or inferior mother trees is prohibited.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　种子生产经营者应当建立和保存包括种子来源、产地、数量、质量、销售去向、销售日期和有关责任人员等内容的生产经营档案，保证可追溯。种子生产经营档案的具体载明事项，种子生产经营档案及种子样品的保存期限由国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门规定。

**Article 36** Seed producers and operators shall establish and maintain production and operation records that include the source, place of origin, quantity, quality, sales destination, sales date, and relevant responsible persons of the seeds, to ensure traceability. The specific items to be included in the seed production and operation records, and the retention period of the records and seed samples shall be prescribed by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and of forestry and grassland of the State Council.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　农民个人自繁自用的常规种子有剩余的，可以在当地集贸市场上出售、串换，不需要办理种子生产经营许可证。

**Article 37** If a farmer has surplus conventional seeds reproduced and used for his own purposes, he may sell or exchange them at a local rural trade market, and is not required to obtain a seed production and operation license.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　种子生产经营许可证的有效区域由发证机关在其管辖范围内确定。种子生产经营者在种子生产经营许可证载明的有效区域设立分支机构的，专门经营不再分装的包装种子的，或者受具有种子生产经营许可证的种子生产经营者以书面委托生产、代销其种子的，不需要办理种子生产经营许可证，但应当向当地农业农村、林业草原主管部门备案。  
实行选育生产经营相结合，符合国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门规定条件的种子企业的生产经营许可证的有效区域为全国。

**Article 38** The area of validity of a seed production and operation license shall be determined by the issuing authority within its jurisdiction. If a seed producer or operator establishes a branch within the area of validity specified in the license, exclusively deals in packaged seeds that are not to be repackaged, or produces or distributes seeds of a holder of a seed production and operation license under written entrustment, it is not required to obtain a separate seed production and operation license, but shall file a record with the local competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or forestry and grassland.  
The area of validity of the production and operation license of a seed enterprise that combines breeding with production and operation and meets the conditions prescribed by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and of forestry and grassland of the State Council shall be the entire country.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　销售的种子应当加工、分级、包装。但是不能加工、包装的除外。  
大包装或者进口种子可以分装；实行分装的，应当标注分装单位，并对种子质量负责。

**Article 39** Seeds sold shall be processed, graded, and packaged, except those that cannot be processed or packaged.  
Seeds in bulk packaging or imported seeds may be repackaged; if repackaging is carried out, the repackaging entity shall be indicated and shall be responsible for the quality of the seeds.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　销售的种子应当符合国家或者行业标准，附有标签和使用说明。标签和使用说明标注的内容应当与销售的种子相符。种子生产经营者对标注内容的真实性和种子质量负责。  
标签应当标注种子类别、品种名称、品种审定或者登记编号、品种适宜种植区域及季节、生产经营者及注册地、质量指标、检疫证明编号、种子生产经营许可证编号和信息代码，以及国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门规定的其他事项。  
销售授权品种种子的，应当标注品种权号。  
销售进口种子的，应当附有进口审批文号和中文标签。  
销售转基因植物品种种子的，必须用明显的文字标注，并应当提示使用时的安全控制措施。  
种子生产经营者应当遵守有关法律、法规的规定，诚实守信，向种子使用者提供种子生产者信息、种子的主要性状、主要栽培措施、适应性等使用条件的说明、风险提示与有关咨询服务，不得作虚假或者引人误解的宣传。  
任何单位和个人不得非法干预种子生产经营者的生产经营自主权。

**Article 40** Seeds sold shall conform to national or industry standards and be accompanied by a label and instructions for use. The contents indicated on the label and instructions shall correspond to the seeds being sold. The seed producer or operator shall be responsible for the truthfulness of the indicated contents and the quality of the seeds.  
The label shall indicate the seed category, variety name, variety审定 or registration number, suitable planting regions and seasons, producer or operator and registered place, quality indicators, quarantine certificate number, seed production and operation license number, and information code, as well as other matters prescribed by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and of forestry and grassland of the State Council.  
Where seeds of a protected variety are sold, the variety right number shall be indicated.  
Where imported seeds are sold, they shall be accompanied by the import approval document number and a Chinese label.  
Where seeds of a genetically modified plant variety are sold, they must be clearly marked with text and shall include safety control measures for use.  
Seed producers and operators shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, act in good faith, provide seed users with information on the seed producer, the main traits of the seeds, main cultivation measures, adaptability, and other conditions of use, risk warnings, and related consulting services, and shall not make false or misleading propaganda.  
No entity or individual may unlawfully interfere with the autonomy of seed producers and operators in production and operation.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　种子广告的内容应当符合本法和有关广告的法律、法规的规定，主要性状描述等应当与审定、登记公告一致。

**Article 41** The contents of seed advertisements shall comply with the provisions of this Law and the laws and regulations on advertisements, and the description of main characteristics, among others, shall be consistent with the announcements of examination and approval or registration.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　运输或者邮寄种子应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定进行检疫。

**Article 42** Transportation or mailing of seeds shall be subject to quarantine in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　种子使用者有权按照自己的意愿购买种子，任何单位和个人不得非法干预。

**Article 43** Seed users have the right to purchase seeds according to their own wishes, and no unit or individual may illegally interfere.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家对推广使用林木良种造林给予扶持。国家投资或者国家投资为主的造林项目和国有林业单位造林，应当根据林业草原主管部门制定的计划使用林木良种。

**Article 44** The State provides support for the promotion and use of improved forest tree varieties for afforestation. Afforestation projects invested by the State or mainly invested by the State, and afforestation by state-owned forestry units, shall use improved forest tree varieties in accordance with the plans formulated by the competent departments of forestry and grassland.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　种子使用者因种子质量问题或者因种子的标签和使用说明标注的内容不真实，遭受损失的，种子使用者可以向出售种子的经营者要求赔偿，也可以向种子生产者或者其他经营者要求赔偿。赔偿额包括购种价款、可得利益损失和其他损失。属于种子生产者或者其他经营者责任的，出售种子的经营者赔偿后，有权向种子生产者或者其他经营者追偿；属于出售种子的经营者责任的，种子生产者或者其他经营者赔偿后，有权向出售种子的经营者追偿。

**Article 45** Where a seed user suffers losses due to quality problems of seeds or due to false contents marked on the labels and instructions for use of seeds, the seed user may claim compensation from the operator who sold the seeds, or may claim compensation from the seed producer or other operators. The amount of compensation includes the purchase price of the seeds, loss of obtainable profits, and other losses. If the liability lies with the seed producer or other operators, the operator who sold the seeds has the right to recover from the seed producer or other operators after paying compensation; if the liability lies with the operator who sold the seeds, the seed producer or other operators have the right to recover from the operator who sold the seeds after paying compensation.

### 第六章　种子监督管理

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当加强对种子质量的监督检查。种子质量管理办法、行业标准和检验方法，由国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门制定。  
农业农村、林业草原主管部门可以采用国家规定的快速检测方法对生产经营的种子品种进行检测，检测结果可以作为行政处罚依据。被检查人对检测结果有异议的，可以申请复检，复检不得采用同一检测方法。因检测结果错误给当事人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 46** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of seed quality. The measures for the management of seed quality, industry standards, and testing methods shall be formulated by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland of the State Council.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland may use the rapid testing methods prescribed by the State to test the seed varieties being produced and operated, and the test results may serve as the basis for administrative penalties. Where the party under inspection disagrees with the test results, it may apply for a re-test, and the same testing method shall not be used for the re-test. Where losses are caused to the parties due to erroneous test results, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　农业农村、林业草原主管部门可以委托种子质量检验机构对种子质量进行检验。  
承担种子质量检验的机构应当具备相应的检测条件、能力，并经省级以上人民政府有关主管部门考核合格。  
种子质量检验机构应当配备种子检验员。种子检验员应当具有中专以上有关专业学历，具备相应的种子检验技术能力和水平。

**Article 47** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland may entrust seed quality testing institutions to test seed quality.  
The institution undertaking seed quality testing shall possess corresponding testing conditions and capabilities and shall have passed the assessment of the relevant competent department of the people's government at or above the provincial level.  
A seed quality testing institution shall be equipped with seed inspectors. Seed inspectors shall possess a relevant professional education background of technical secondary school or above, and have the corresponding technical capability and level of seed inspection.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　禁止生产经营假、劣种子。农业农村、林业草原主管部门和有关部门依法打击生产经营假、劣种子的违法行为，保护农民合法权益，维护公平竞争的市场秩序。  
下列种子为假种子：  
（一）以非种子冒充种子或者以此种品种种子冒充其他品种种子的；  
（二）种子种类、品种与标签标注的内容不符或者没有标签的。  
下列种子为劣种子：  
（一）质量低于国家规定标准的；  
（二）质量低于标签标注指标的；  
（三）带有国家规定的检疫性有害生物的。

**Article 48** The production and operation of counterfeit and inferior seeds shall be prohibited. The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland and relevant departments shall, according to law, crack down on the illegal acts of producing and operating counterfeit and inferior seeds, protect the legitimate rights and interests of farmers, and maintain the market order of fair competition.  
The following seeds are counterfeit seeds:  
(1) seeds that use non-seeds to impersonate seeds, or use seeds of one variety to impersonate seeds of another variety;  
(2) seeds whose type or variety is inconsistent with the contents marked on the label or which have no label.  
The following seeds are inferior seeds:  
(1) seeds whose quality is lower than the standards prescribed by the State;  
(2) seeds whose quality is lower than the indicators marked on the label;  
(3) seeds that carry quarantine pests prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　农业农村、林业草原主管部门是种子行政执法机关。种子执法人员依法执行公务时应当出示行政执法证件。农业农村、林业草原主管部门依法履行种子监督检查职责时，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）进入生产经营场所进行现场检查；  
（二）对种子进行取样测试、试验或者检验；  
（三）查阅、复制有关合同、票据、账簿、生产经营档案及其他有关资料；  
（四）查封、扣押有证据证明违法生产经营的种子，以及用于违法生产经营的工具、设备及运输工具等；  
（五）查封违法从事种子生产经营活动的场所。  
农业农村、林业草原主管部门依照本法规定行使职权，当事人应当协助、配合，不得拒绝、阻挠。  
农业农村、林业草原主管部门所属的综合执法机构或者受其委托的种子管理机构，可以开展种子执法相关工作。

**Article 49** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland are the administrative law enforcement organs for seeds. When performing official duties according to law, seed law enforcement personnel shall present their administrative law enforcement credentials. When performing seed supervision and inspection duties according to law, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) enter the production and operation sites for on-site inspection;  
(2) take samples of seeds for testing, trial or inspection;  
(3) consult and copy relevant contracts, bills, account books, production and operation archives, and other relevant materials;  
(4) seal up or seize seeds that are proven to be illegally produced and operated, as well as tools, equipment, and means of transport used for illegal production and operation;  
(5) seal up the sites engaged in illegal seed production and operation activities.  
When the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland exercise their powers in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the parties shall assist and cooperate, and shall not refuse or obstruct.  
The comprehensive law enforcement agencies affiliated with the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland, or the seed management agencies entrusted by them, may carry out seed law enforcement related work.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　种子生产经营者依法自愿成立种子行业协会，加强行业自律管理，维护成员合法权益，为成员和行业发展提供信息交流、技术培训、信用建设、市场营销和咨询等服务。

**Article 50** Seed producers and operators may voluntarily establish seed industry associations according to law, strengthen industry self-discipline management, protect the legitimate rights and interests of members, and provide services such as information exchange, technical training, credit building, marketing and consulting for members and the development of the industry.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　种子生产经营者可自愿向具有资质的认证机构申请种子质量认证。经认证合格的，可以在包装上使用认证标识。

**Article 51** Seed producers and operators may voluntarily apply for seed quality certification to qualified certification bodies. Those that pass the certification may use the certification mark on their packaging.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　由于不可抗力原因，为生产需要必须使用低于国家或者地方规定标准的农作物种子的，应当经用种地县级以上地方人民政府批准。

**Article 52** Where, due to force majeure, it is necessary to use crop seeds that are below the standards prescribed by the State or local authorities for production needs, the matter shall be approved by the local people's government at or above the county level of the place where the seeds are used.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　从事品种选育和种子生产经营以及管理的单位和个人应当遵守有关植物检疫法律、行政法规的规定，防止植物危险性病、虫、杂草及其他有害生物的传播和蔓延。  
禁止任何单位和个人在种子生产基地从事检疫性有害生物接种试验。

**Article 53** Units and individuals engaged in variety breeding, seed production and operation, and management shall comply with the provisions of relevant plant quarantine laws and administrative regulations to prevent the spread and proliferation of dangerous plant diseases, pests, weeds and other harmful organisms.  
No unit or individual may conduct inoculation tests of quarantine pests on seed production bases.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　省级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当在统一的政府信息发布平台上发布品种审定、品种登记、新品种保护、种子生产经营许可、监督管理等信息。  
国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门建立植物品种标准样品库，为种子监督管理提供依据。

**Article 54** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland of the people's governments at or above the provincial level shall publish information on variety examination and approval, variety registration, new variety protection, seed production and operation permits, supervision and management, etc. on a unified government information release platform.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland of the State Council shall establish a standard sample library of plant varieties to provide a basis for seed supervision and management.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　农业农村、林业草原主管部门及其工作人员，不得参与和从事种子生产经营活动。

**Article 55** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland and their staff may not participate in or engage in seed production and operation activities.

### 第七章　种子进出口和对外合作

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　进口种子和出口种子必须实施检疫，防止植物危险性病、虫、杂草及其他有害生物传入境内和传出境外，具体检疫工作按照有关植物进出境检疫法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 56** Imported seeds and exported seeds must be quarantined to prevent the introduction into or spread out of the territory of dangerous plant diseases, pests, weeds and other harmful organisms. The specific quarantine work shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations on plant entry and exit quarantine.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　从事种子进出口业务的，应当具备种子生产经营许可证；其中，从事农作物种子进出口业务的，还应当按照国家有关规定取得种子进出口许可。  
从境外引进农作物、林木种子的审定权限，农作物种子的进口审批办法，引进转基因植物品种的管理办法，由国务院规定。

**Article 57** Those engaged in the import and export of seeds shall possess a seed production and operation license; among them, those engaged in the import and export of crop seeds shall also obtain a seed import and export permit in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The authority for the examination and approval of the introduction of crop and forest tree seeds from abroad, the measures for the examination and approval of the import of crop seeds, and the measures for the management of the introduction of genetically modified plant varieties shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　进口种子的质量，应当达到国家标准或者行业标准。没有国家标准或者行业标准的，可以按照合同约定的标准执行。

**Article 58** The quality of imported seeds shall meet the national standards or industry standards. Where there are no national standards or industry standards, the standards agreed upon in the contract may be followed.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　为境外制种进口种子的，可以不受[本法第五十七条](#art-57)第一款的限制，但应当具有对外制种合同，进口的种子只能用于制种，其产品不得在境内销售。  
从境外引进农作物或者林木试验用种，应当隔离栽培，收获物也不得作为种子销售。

**Article 59** Where seeds are imported for the purpose of producing seeds for foreign parties, the restriction of the first paragraph of Article 57 of this Law may be exempted, provided that there is a contract for seed production for foreign parties, and the imported seeds may only be used for seed production, and the products thereof shall not be sold within the territory.  
Seeds introduced from abroad for experimentation on crops or forest trees shall be cultivated in isolation, and the harvested products shall not be sold as seeds.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　禁止进出口假、劣种子以及属于国家规定不得进出口的种子。

**Article 60** The import or export of counterfeit or inferior seeds, as well as seeds that are not allowed to be imported or exported as prescribed by the State, shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家建立种业国家安全审查机制。境外机构、个人投资、并购境内种子企业，或者与境内科研院所、种子企业开展技术合作，从事品种研发、种子生产经营的审批管理依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 61** The State shall establish a national security review mechanism for the seed industry. The examination and approval management of overseas institutions or individuals investing in or acquiring domestic seed enterprises, or conducting technical cooperation with domestic research institutes or seed enterprises, engaging in variety research and development, and seed production and operation shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

### 第八章　扶持措施

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　国家加大对种业发展的支持。对品种选育、生产、示范推广、种质资源保护、种子储备以及制种大县给予扶持。  
国家鼓励推广使用高效、安全制种采种技术和先进适用的制种采种机械，将先进适用的制种采种机械纳入农机具购置补贴范围。  
国家积极引导社会资金投资种业。

**Article 62** The State shall increase support for the development of the seed industry. It shall provide support for variety selection and breeding, production, demonstration and promotion, germplasm resource protection, seed reserves, and major seed production counties.  
The State encourages the promotion and use of efficient and safe seed production and harvesting technologies as well as advanced and applicable seed production and harvesting machinery, and shall include such advanced and applicable machinery within the scope of subsidies for the purchase of agricultural machinery and tools.  
The State actively guides social capital to invest in the seed industry.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国家加强种业公益性基础设施建设，保障育种科研设施用地合理需求。  
对优势种子繁育基地内的耕地，划入永久基本农田。优势种子繁育基地由国务院农业农村主管部门商所在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定。

**Article 63** The State shall strengthen the construction of public welfare infrastructure for the seed industry and guarantee reasonable land use demands for breeding research facilities.  
The cultivated land within advantageous seed propagation bases shall be designated as permanent basic farmland. The advantageous seed propagation bases shall be determined by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs under the State Council in consultation with the people's governments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　对从事农作物和林木品种选育、生产的种子企业，按照国家有关规定给予扶持。

**Article 64** Seed enterprises engaged in the selection, breeding, and production of crop and forest tree varieties shall be provided with support in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国家鼓励和引导金融机构为种子生产经营和收储提供信贷支持。

**Article 65** The State encourages and guides financial institutions to provide credit support for seed production, operation, and purchase and storage.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家支持保险机构开展种子生产保险。省级以上人民政府可以采取保险费补贴等措施，支持发展种业生产保险。

**Article 66** The State supports insurance institutions in carrying out seed production insurance. The people's governments at or above the provincial level may take measures such as premium subsidies to support the development of seed production insurance.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　国家鼓励科研院所及高等院校与种子企业开展育种科技人员交流，支持本单位的科技人员到种子企业从事育种成果转化活动；鼓励育种科研人才创新创业。

**Article 67** The State encourages research institutes and institutions of higher education to carry out exchanges of breeding science and technology personnel with seed enterprises, and supports the scientific and technical personnel of their own units to engage in the transformation of breeding achievements in seed enterprises; and encourages breeding research talents to innovate and start businesses.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门和异地繁育种子所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当加强对异地繁育种子工作的管理和协调，交通运输部门应当优先保证种子的运输。

**Article 68** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and of forestry and grassland under the State Council, and the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government where seed breeding in different places occurs shall strengthen the management and coordination of seed breeding in different places, and the transportation departments shall give priority to ensuring the transport of seeds.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　农业农村、林业草原主管部门不依法作出行政许可决定，发现违法行为或者接到对违法行为的举报不予查处，或者有其他未依照本法规定履行职责的行为的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府有关部门责令改正，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。  
违反[本法第五十五条](#art-55)规定，农业农村、林业草原主管部门工作人员从事种子生产经营活动的，依法给予处分。

**Article 69** Where the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs or of forestry and grassland fail to make an administrative license decision according to law, fail to investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered or after receiving reports of illegal acts, or have other acts of failing to perform their duties in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the people's government at the same level or the relevant department of the people's government at a higher level shall order them to make corrections, and the persons in charge with responsibility and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 55 of this Law, the staff members of the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs or of forestry and grassland engage in seed production and operation activities, they shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　违反[本法第十六条](#art-16)规定，品种审定委员会委员和工作人员不依法履行职责，弄虚作假、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；自处分决定作出之日起五年内不得从事品种审定工作。

**Article 70** Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 16 of this Law, members of the variety approval committee or staff members fail to perform their duties according to law, engage in fraud, or practice favoritism or irregularities, they shall be given sanctions according to law; and they shall not engage in variety approval work within five years from the date on which the sanction decision is made.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　品种测试、试验和种子质量检验机构伪造测试、试验、检验数据或者出具虚假证明的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门责令改正，对单位处五万元以上十万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以上五万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，并处没收违法所得；给种子使用者和其他种子生产经营者造成损失的，与种子生产经营者承担连带责任；情节严重的，由省级以上人民政府有关主管部门取消种子质量检验资格。

**Article 71** Where a variety testing, experiment, or seed quality inspection institution falsifies testing, experiment, or inspection data or issues false certificates, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or of forestry and grassland under the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the institution, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated concurrently; if losses are caused to seed users or other seed producers and operators, they shall bear joint and several liability with the seed producers and operators; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant competent department under the people's government at or above the provincial level shall cancel the seed quality inspection qualification.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　违反[本法第二十八条](#art-28)规定，有侵犯植物新品种权行为的，由当事人协商解决，不愿协商或者协商不成的，植物新品种权所有人或者利害关系人可以请求县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门进行处理，也可以直接向人民法院提起诉讼。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门，根据当事人自愿的原则，对侵犯植物新品种权所造成的损害赔偿可以进行调解。调解达成协议的，当事人应当履行；当事人不履行协议或者调解未达成协议的，植物新品种权所有人或者利害关系人可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。  
侵犯植物新品种权的赔偿数额按照权利人因被侵权所受到的实际损失确定；实际损失难以确定的，可以按照侵权人因侵权所获得的利益确定。权利人的损失或者侵权人获得的利益难以确定的，可以参照该植物新品种权许可使用费的倍数合理确定。故意侵犯植物新品种权，情节严重的，可以在按照上述方法确定数额的一倍以上五倍以下确定赔偿数额。  
权利人的损失、侵权人获得的利益和植物新品种权许可使用费均难以确定的，人民法院可以根据植物新品种权的类型、侵权行为的性质和情节等因素，确定给予五百万元以下的赔偿。  
赔偿数额应当包括权利人为制止侵权行为所支付的合理开支。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门处理侵犯植物新品种权案件时，为了维护社会公共利益，责令侵权人停止侵权行为，没收违法所得和种子；货值金额不足五万元的，并处一万元以上二十五万元以下罚款；货值金额五万元以上的，并处货值金额五倍以上十倍以下罚款。  
假冒授权品种的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门责令停止假冒行为，没收违法所得和种子；货值金额不足五万元的，并处一万元以上二十五万元以下罚款；货值金额五万元以上的，并处货值金额五倍以上十倍以下罚款。

**Article 72** Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 28 of this Law, a plant new variety right is infringed, the matter shall be resolved through consultation between the parties; if consultation is not desired or consultation fails, the owner of the plant new variety right or an interested party may request the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or of forestry and grassland under the people's government at or above the county level to handle the matter, or may directly file a lawsuit with the People's Court.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or of forestry and grassland under the people's government at or above the county level, based on the principle of voluntary participation of the parties, may conduct mediation for damage compensation caused by infringement of the plant new variety right. If a mediation agreement is reached, the parties shall perform it; if the parties fail to perform the agreement or mediation fails to reach an agreement, the owner of the plant new variety right or an interested party may file a lawsuit with the People's Court according to law.  
The amount of compensation for infringement of a plant new variety right shall be determined based on the actual losses suffered by the right holder due to the infringement; if the actual losses are difficult to determine, it may be determined based on the gains obtained by the infringer due to the infringement. If the losses of the right holder or the gains of the infringer are difficult to determine, it may be reasonably determined by reference to a multiple of the licensing fee for the use of the plant new variety right. If the infringement of the plant new variety right is intentional and the circumstances are serious, the amount of compensation may be determined at not less than one time but not more than five times the amount determined in accordance with the above methods.  
If the losses of the right holder, the gains of the infringer, and the licensing fee for the use of the plant new variety right are all difficult to determine, the People's Court may, based on factors such as the type of the plant new variety right, the nature and circumstances of the infringement, determine compensation of not more than 5,000,000 yuan.  
The amount of compensation shall include the reasonable expenses paid by the right holder to stop the infringement.  
When handling cases of infringement of plant new variety rights, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or of forestry and grassland under the people's government at or above the county level, in order to safeguard social public interests, shall order the infringer to stop the infringing act, confiscate the illegal gains and seeds; if the value of the goods is less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 250,000 yuan shall be imposed concurrently; if the value of the goods is 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the value of the goods shall be imposed concurrently.  
Where a licensed variety is counterfeited, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or of forestry and grassland under the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the counterfeiting act, confiscate the illegal gains and seeds; if the value of the goods is less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 250,000 yuan shall be imposed concurrently; if the value of the goods is 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the value of the goods shall be imposed concurrently.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　当事人就植物新品种的申请权和植物新品种权的权属发生争议的，可以向人民法院提起诉讼。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于审理侵害植物新品种权纠纷案件具体应用法律问题的若干规定.md#art-6" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理侵害植物新品种权纠纷案件具体应用法律问题的若干规定》第6条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 73** Where a dispute arises between parties concerning the right to apply for a plant new variety or the ownership of a plant new variety right, they may file a lawsuit with the People's Court.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　违反[本法第四十八条](#art-48)规定，生产经营假种子的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门责令停止生产经营，没收违法所得和种子，吊销种子生产经营许可证；违法生产经营的货值金额不足二万元的，并处二万元以上二十万元以下罚款；货值金额二万元以上的，并处货值金额十倍以上二十倍以下罚款。  
因生产经营假种子犯罪被判处有期徒刑以上刑罚的，种子企业或者其他单位的法定代表人、直接负责的主管人员自刑罚执行完毕之日起五年内不得担任种子企业的法定代表人、高级管理人员。

**Article 74** Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 48 of this Law, counterfeit seeds are produced or operated, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or of forestry and grassland under the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of production and operation, confiscate the illegal gains and seeds, and revoke the seed production and operation license; if the value of the goods illegally produced and operated is less than 20,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed concurrently; if the value of the goods is 20,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than ten times but not more than twenty times the value of the goods shall be imposed concurrently.  
Where a person is sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment or a heavier penalty for a crime committed by producing or operating counterfeit seeds, the legal representative or directly responsible person in charge of the seed enterprise or other entity shall not serve as the legal representative or senior officer of any seed enterprise within five years from the date of completion of the execution of the penalty.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反[本法第四十八条](#art-48)规定，生产经营劣种子的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门责令停止生产经营，没收违法所得和种子；违法生产经营的货值金额不足二万元的，并处一万元以上十万元以下罚款；货值金额二万元以上的，并处货值金额五倍以上十倍以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销种子生产经营许可证。  
因生产经营劣种子犯罪被判处有期徒刑以上刑罚的，种子企业或者其他单位的法定代表人、直接负责的主管人员自刑罚执行完毕之日起五年内不得担任种子企业的法定代表人、高级管理人员。

**Article 75** Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 48 of this Law, inferior seeds are produced or operated, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or of forestry and grassland under the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of production and operation, confiscate the illegal gains and seeds; if the value of the goods illegally produced and operated is less than 20,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed concurrently; if the value of the goods is 20,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the value of the goods shall be imposed concurrently; if the circumstances are serious, the seed production and operation license shall be revoked.  
Where a person is sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment or a heavier penalty for a crime committed by producing or operating inferior seeds, the legal representative or directly responsible person in charge of the seed enterprise or other entity shall not serve as the legal representative or senior officer of any seed enterprise within five years from the date of completion of the execution of the penalty.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　违反[本法第三十二条](#art-32)、第三十三条、第三十四条规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得和种子；违法生产经营的货值金额不足一万元的，并处三千元以上三万元以下罚款；货值金额一万元以上的，并处货值金额三倍以上五倍以下罚款；可以吊销种子生产经营许可证：  
（一）未取得种子生产经营许可证生产经营种子的；  
（二）以欺骗、贿赂等不正当手段取得种子生产经营许可证的；  
（三）未按照种子生产经营许可证的规定生产经营种子的；  
（四）伪造、变造、买卖、租借种子生产经营许可证的；  
（五）不再具有繁殖种子的隔离和培育条件，或者不再具有无检疫性有害生物的种子生产地点或者县级以上人民政府林业草原主管部门确定的采种林，继续从事种子生产的；  
（六）未执行种子检验、检疫规程生产种子的。  
被吊销种子生产经营许可证的单位，其法定代表人、直接负责的主管人员自处罚决定作出之日起五年内不得担任种子企业的法定代表人、高级管理人员。

**Article 76** Where, in violation of the provisions of Articles 32, 33, and 34 of this Law, any of the following acts is committed, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or of forestry and grassland under the people's government at or above the county level shall order corrections, confiscate the illegal gains and seeds; if the value of the goods illegally produced and operated is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan shall be imposed concurrently; if the value of the goods is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the value of the goods shall be imposed concurrently; and the seed production and operation license may be revoked:  
(1) producing or operating seeds without obtaining a seed production and operation license;  
(2) obtaining a seed production and operation license by fraudulent, bribery, or other improper means;  
(3) producing or operating seeds not in accordance with the provisions of the seed production and operation license;  
(4) forging, altering, buying, selling, or lending the seed production and operation license;  
(5) continuing to engage in seed production when the isolation and cultivation conditions for propagating seeds no longer exist, or the seed production site free from quarantine pests or the seed-collecting forest designated by the competent department of forestry and grassland under the people's government at or above the county level no longer exists;  
(6) producing seeds without implementing the seed inspection and quarantine procedures.  
For an entity whose seed production and operation license has been revoked, its legal representative and directly responsible person in charge shall not serve as the legal representative or senior officer of any seed enterprise within five years from the date on which the penalty decision is made.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　违反[本法第二十一条](#art-21)、第二十二条、第二十三条规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得和种子，并处二万元以上二十万元以下罚款：  
（一）对应当审定未经审定的农作物品种进行推广、销售的；  
（二）作为良种推广、销售应当审定未经审定的林木品种的；  
（三）推广、销售应当停止推广、销售的农作物品种或者林木良种的；  
（四）对应当登记未经登记的农作物品种进行推广，或者以登记品种的名义进行销售的；  
（五）对已撤销登记的农作物品种进行推广，或者以登记品种的名义进行销售的。  
违反[本法第二十三条](#art-23)、第四十一条规定，对应当审定未经审定或者应当登记未经登记的农作物品种发布广告，或者广告中有关品种的主要性状描述的内容与审定、登记公告不一致的，依照《中华人民共和国广告法》的有关规定追究法律责任。

**Article 77** Where, in violation of the provisions of Articles 21, 22, and 23 of this Law, any of the following acts is committed, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or of forestry and grassland under the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegal gains and seeds, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan:  
(1) promoting or selling crop varieties that should have been approved but have not been approved;  
(2) promoting or selling forest tree varieties that should have been approved but have not been approved as improved varieties;  
(3) promoting or selling crop varieties or improved forest tree varieties that should have ceased to be promoted or sold;  
(4) promoting crop varieties that should have been registered but have not been registered, or selling them under the name of registered varieties;  
(5) promoting crop varieties whose registration has been revoked, or selling them under the name of registered varieties.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of Articles 23 and 41 of this Law, advertisements are published for crop varieties that should have been approved but have not been approved or should have been registered but have not been registered, or the descriptions of the main characteristics of the varieties in the advertisements are inconsistent with the approval or registration announcements, legal liability shall be pursued in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Advertising Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　违反[本法第五十七条](#art-57)、第五十九条、第六十条规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得和种子；违法生产经营的货值金额不足一万元的，并处三千元以上三万元以下罚款；货值金额一万元以上的，并处货值金额三倍以上五倍以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销种子生产经营许可证：  
（一）未经许可进出口种子的；  
（二）为境外制种的种子在境内销售的；  
（三）从境外引进农作物或者林木种子进行引种试验的收获物作为种子在境内销售的；  
（四）进出口假、劣种子或者属于国家规定不得进出口的种子的。

**Article 78** Where, in violation of the provisions of Articles 57, 59, and 60 of this Law, any of the following acts is committed, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or of forestry and grassland under the people's government at or above the county level shall order corrections, confiscate the illegal gains and seeds; if the value of the goods illegally produced and operated is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan shall be imposed concurrently; if the value of the goods is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the value of the goods shall be imposed concurrently; if the circumstances are serious, the seed production and operation license shall be revoked:  
(1) importing or exporting seeds without permission;  
(2) selling seeds produced for foreign entities within the territory of China;  
(3) selling the harvest of crop or forest tree seeds introduced from abroad for introduction trials as seeds within the territory of China;  
(4) importing or exporting counterfeit or inferior seeds, or seeds that are prohibited from being imported or exported by the State.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　违反[本法第三十六条](#art-36)、第三十八条、第三十九条、第四十条规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门责令改正，处二千元以上二万元以下罚款：  
（一）销售的种子应当包装而没有包装的；  
（二）销售的种子没有使用说明或者标签内容不符合规定的；  
（三）涂改标签的；  
（四）未按规定建立、保存种子生产经营档案的；  
（五）种子生产经营者在异地设立分支机构、专门经营不再分装的包装种子或者受委托生产、代销种子，未按规定备案的。

**Article 79** Where, in violation of the provisions of Articles 36, 38, 39, and 40 of this Law, any of the following acts is committed, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or of forestry and grassland under the people's government at or above the county level shall order corrections, and impose a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan:  
(1) selling seeds that should be packaged but are not packaged;  
(2) selling seeds without instructions for use or with label contents that do not conform to regulations;  
(3) altering labels;  
(4) failing to establish or maintain seed production and operation records in accordance with regulations;  
(5) a seed producer or operator establishing a branch in a different place, specializing in the operation of packaged seeds that are not to be repackaged, or producing or selling seeds on commission without filing in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　违反[本法第八条](#art-8)规定，侵占、破坏种质资源，私自采集或者采伐国家重点保护的天然种质资源的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收种质资源和违法所得，并处五千元以上五万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 80** Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 8 of this Law, germplasm resources are encroached upon or damaged, or natural germplasm resources under key State protection are collected or felled without authorization, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or of forestry and grassland under the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the germplasm resources and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　违反[本法第十一条](#art-11)规定，向境外提供或者从境外引进种质资源，或者与境外机构、个人开展合作研究利用种质资源的，由国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的农业农村、林业草原主管部门没收种质资源和违法所得，并处二万元以上二十万元以下罚款。  
未取得农业农村、林业草原主管部门的批准文件携带、运输种质资源出境的，海关应当将该种质资源扣留，并移送省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门处理。

**Article 81** Where any person, in violation of Article 11 of this Law, provides domestic germplasm resources to overseas entities or introduces germplasm resources from overseas, or carries out cooperative research and utilization of germplasm resources with overseas entities or individuals, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or the competent department of forestry and grassland under the State Council or the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall confiscate the germplasm resources and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.  
Where any person carries or transports germplasm resources out of the country without obtaining the approval document from the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or the competent department of forestry and grassland, the Customs shall detain such germplasm resources and transfer them to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or the competent department of forestry and grassland under the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for disposition.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　违反[本法第三十五条](#art-35)规定，抢采掠青、损坏母树或者在劣质林内、劣质母树上采种的，由县级以上人民政府林业草原主管部门责令停止采种行为，没收所采种子，并处所采种子货值金额二倍以上五倍以下罚款。

**Article 82** Where any person, in violation of Article 35 of this Law, prematurely harvests seeds, damages mother trees, or collects seeds from inferior forests or inferior mother trees, the competent department of forestry and grassland under the people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the seed collection act, confiscate the collected seeds, and impose a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the value of the collected seeds.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　违反[本法第十七条](#art-17)规定，种子企业有造假行为的，由省级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门处一百万元以上五百万元以下罚款；不得再依照[本法第十七条](#art-17)的规定申请品种审定；给种子使用者和其他种子生产经营者造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 83** Where any seed enterprise, in violation of Article 17 of this Law, commits fraudulent acts, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or the competent department of forestry and grassland under the people's government at or above the provincial level shall impose a fine of not less than 1,000,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan; the enterprise shall not apply for variety approval in accordance with Article 17 of this Law; if losses are caused to seed users or other seed producers and dealers, the enterprise shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　违反[本法第四十四条](#art-44)规定，未根据林业草原主管部门制定的计划使用林木良种的，由同级人民政府林业草原主管部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处三千元以上三万元以下罚款。

**Article 84** Where any person, in violation of Article 44 of this Law, fails to use improved forest tree varieties according to the plan formulated by the competent department of forestry and grassland, the competent department of forestry and grassland under the people's government at the same level shall order rectification within a specified period; if rectification is not made within the specified period, a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　违反[本法第五十三条](#art-53)规定，在种子生产基地进行检疫性有害生物接种试验的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门责令停止试验，处五千元以上五万元以下罚款。

**Article 85** Where any person, in violation of Article 53 of this Law, conducts inoculation tests of quarantine pests in seed production bases, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or the competent department of forestry and grassland under the people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the test and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　违反[本法第四十九条](#art-49)规定，拒绝、阻挠农业农村、林业草原主管部门依法实施监督检查的，处二千元以上五万元以下罚款，可以责令停产停业整顿；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 86** Where any person, in violation of Article 49 of this Law, refuses or obstructs the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or the competent department of forestry and grassland in lawfully conducting supervision and inspection, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the person may be ordered to suspend production or business for rectification; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　违反[本法第十三条](#art-13)规定，私自交易育种成果，给本单位造成经济损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 87** Where any person, in violation of Article 13 of this Law, privately trades breeding achievements and thereby causes economic losses to the entity to which the person belongs, the person shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　违反[本法第四十三条](#art-43)规定，强迫种子使用者违背自己的意愿购买、使用种子，给使用者造成损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 88** Where any person, in violation of Article 43 of this Law, compels seed users to purchase or use seeds against their will and thereby causes losses to the users, the person shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 89** Where any violation of this Law constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　本法下列用语的含义是：  
（一）种质资源是指选育植物新品种的基础材料，包括各种植物的栽培种、野生种的繁殖材料以及利用上述繁殖材料人工创造的各种植物的遗传材料。  
（二）品种是指经过人工选育或者发现并经过改良，形态特征和生物学特性一致，遗传性状相对稳定的植物群体。  
（三）主要农作物是指稻、小麦、玉米、棉花、大豆。  
（四）主要林木由国务院林业草原主管部门确定并公布；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业草原主管部门可以在国务院林业草原主管部门确定的主要林木之外确定其他八种以下的主要林木。  
（五）林木良种是指通过审定的主要林木品种，在一定的区域内，其产量、适应性、抗性等方面明显优于当前主栽材料的繁殖材料和种植材料。  
（六）新颖性是指申请植物新品种权的品种在申请日前，经申请权人自行或者同意销售、推广其种子，在中国境内未超过一年；在境外，木本或者藤本植物未超过六年，其他植物未超过四年。  
本法施行后新列入国家植物品种保护名录的植物的属或者种，从名录公布之日起一年内提出植物新品种权申请的，在境内销售、推广该品种种子未超过四年的，具备新颖性。  
除销售、推广行为丧失新颖性外，下列情形视为已丧失新颖性：  
1.品种经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门依据播种面积确认已经形成事实扩散的；  
2.农作物品种已审定或者登记两年以上未申请植物新品种权的。  
（七）特异性是指一个植物品种有一个以上性状明显区别于已知品种。  
（八）一致性是指一个植物品种的特性除可预期的自然变异外，群体内个体间相关的特征或者特性表现一致。  
（九）稳定性是指一个植物品种经过反复繁殖后或者在特定繁殖周期结束时，其主要性状保持不变。  
（十）实质性派生品种是指由原始品种实质性派生，或者由该原始品种的实质性派生品种派生出来的品种，与原始品种有明显区别，并且除派生引起的性状差异外，在表达由原始品种基因型或者基因型组合产生的基本性状方面与原始品种相同。  
（十一）已知品种是指已受理申请或者已通过品种审定、品种登记、新品种保护，或者已经销售、推广的植物品种。  
（十二）标签是指印制、粘贴、固定或者附着在种子、种子包装物表面的特定图案及文字说明。

**Article 90** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) "Germplasm resources" mean the fundamental materials for breeding new plant varieties, including the propagation materials of cultivated species and wild species of various plants, as well as the genetic materials of various plants artificially created from the aforesaid propagation materials.  
(2) "Variety" means a plant group that has been artificially selected or discovered and improved, with consistent morphological and biological characteristics and relatively stable hereditary traits.  
(3) "Major crops" mean rice, wheat, corn, cotton, and soybean.  
(4) "Major forest trees" shall be determined and announced by the competent department of forestry and grassland under the State Council; the competent department of forestry and grassland under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, in addition to the major forest trees determined by the competent department of forestry and grassland under the State Council, determine no more than eight other species of major forest trees.  
(5) "Improved forest tree varieties" mean reproduction materials and planting materials of approved major forest tree varieties which, within a certain region, are significantly superior to the currently dominant materials in terms of yield, adaptability, resistance, etc.  
(6) "Novelty" means that, before the date of filing an application for a new plant variety right, the seeds of the variety have been sold or promoted by or with the consent of the applicant in China for not more than one year; outside China, for woody or vine plants, not more than six years, and for other plants, not more than four years.  
For a genus or species newly included in the National Catalogue of Protected Plant Varieties after this Law comes into force, if an application for a new plant variety right is filed within one year from the date of announcement of the catalogue, and the seeds of the variety have not been sold or promoted in China for more than four years, the variety shall be deemed to possess novelty.  
Except for the loss of novelty due to sale or promotion, the following circumstances shall be deemed as loss of novelty:  
1. The variety has formed factual diffusion as confirmed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or the competent department of forestry and grassland under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government based on the sown area;  
2. The crop variety has been approved or registered for more than two years without an application for a new plant variety right.  
(7) "Distinctness" means that a plant variety has one or more traits that are clearly different from any known variety.  
(8) "Uniformity" means that, except for predictable natural variations, the relevant characteristics or traits among individuals within the population of a plant variety are consistent.  
(9) "Stability" means that the essential traits of a plant variety remain unchanged after repeated propagation or at the end of a specific propagation cycle.  
(10) "Essentially derived variety" means a variety that is essentially derived from an initial variety, or from an essentially derived variety of the initial variety, while being clearly distinguishable from the initial variety and, except for the differences derived from the derivation, conforms to the initial variety in the expression of the essential characteristics that result from the genotype or combination of genotypes of the initial variety.  
(11) "Known variety" means a plant variety for which an application has been accepted or which has passed variety approval, variety registration, new variety protection, or has been sold or promoted.  
(12) "Label" means specific patterns and textual descriptions printed, affixed, fixed, or attached to the surface of seeds or seed packaging.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　国家加强中药材种质资源保护，支持开展中药材育种科学技术研究。  
草种、烟草种、中药材种、食用菌菌种的种质资源管理和选育、生产经营、管理等活动，参照本法执行。

**Article 91** The State shall strengthen the protection of germplasm resources of traditional Chinese medicinal materials and support scientific and technological research on breeding of traditional Chinese medicinal materials.  
The management and selection, production and operation, and administration of germplasm resources of grass seeds, tobacco seeds, traditional Chinese medicinal material seeds, and edible fungus strains shall be carried out by reference to this Law.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　本法自2016年1月1日起施行。

**Article 92** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2016.
