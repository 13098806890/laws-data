# 中华人民共和国粮食安全保障法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2023-12-29  
**生效日期**：2024-06-01

> （2023年12月29日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第七次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　耕地保护 第三章　粮食生产 第四章　粮食储备 第五章　粮食流通 第六章　粮食加工 第七章　粮食应急 第八章　粮食节约 第九章　监督管理 第十章　法律责任 第十一章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保障粮食有效供给，确保国家粮食安全，提高防范和抵御粮食安全风险能力，维护经济社会稳定和国家安全，根据宪法，制定本法。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障粮食有效供给，确保国家粮食安全，提高防范和抵御粮食安全风险能力，维护经济社会稳定和国家安全，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of ensuring the effective supply of grain, safeguarding national food security, improving the capability to prevent and resist food security risks, and maintaining economic and social stability and national security.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家粮食安全工作坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻总体国家安全观，统筹发展和安全，实施以我为主、立足国内、确保产能、适度进口、科技支撑的国家粮食安全战略，坚持藏粮于地、藏粮于技，提高粮食生产、储备、流通、加工能力，确保谷物基本自给、口粮绝对安全。  
保障国家粮食安全应当树立大食物观，构建多元化食物供给体系，全方位、多途径开发食物资源，满足人民群众对食物品种丰富多样、品质营养健康的消费需求。

**Article 2** The work on national food security shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, implement the holistic approach to national security, coordinate development and security, implement the national food security strategy of self-reliance, basing on domestic resources, ensuring production capacity, moderate import, and scientific and technological support, insist on storing grain in the land and storing grain in technology, improve the capacity of grain production, storage, circulation, and processing, ensure basic self-sufficiency of grains and absolute safety of rations.  
To safeguard national food security, a big food concept shall be established, a diversified food supply system shall be constructed, food resources shall be developed in an all-round and multi-channel manner to meet the people's consumption needs for a rich variety of food and for quality, nutrition, and health.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家建立粮食安全责任制，实行粮食安全党政同责。县级以上地方人民政府应当承担保障本行政区域粮食安全的具体责任。  
县级以上人民政府发展改革、自然资源、农业农村、粮食和储备等主管部门依照本法和规定的职责，协同配合，做好粮食安全保障工作。

**Article 3** The State shall establish a responsibility system for food security, implementing the joint responsibility of the Party and the government for food security. The local people's governments at or above the county level shall assume the specific responsibility for ensuring food security within their respective administrative regions.  
The competent departments of development and reform, natural resources, agriculture and rural affairs, grain and reserve, and other competent departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with this Law and their prescribed duties, coordinate and cooperate to ensure the work on food security.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家加强粮食宏观调控，优化粮食品种结构和区域布局，统筹利用国内、国际的市场和资源，构建科学合理、安全高效的粮食供给保障体系，提升粮食供给能力和质量安全。  
国家加强国际粮食安全合作，发挥粮食国际贸易作用。

**Article 4** The State shall strengthen macro-control of grain, optimize grain variety structure and regional layout, coordinate the use of domestic and international markets and resources, construct a scientific, reasonable, safe and efficient grain supply guarantee system, and improve grain supply capacity and quality safety.  
The State shall strengthen international cooperation on food security and give play to the role of international grain trade.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府应当将粮食安全保障纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。县级以上人民政府有关部门应当根据粮食安全保障目标、任务等，编制粮食安全保障相关专项规划，按照程序批准后实施。

**Article 5** The people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate food security guarantee into the national economic and social development plans. The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the objectives and tasks of food security guarantee, prepare relevant special plans for food security guarantee, and implement them after approval in accordance with procedures.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家建立健全粮食安全保障投入机制，采取财政、金融等支持政策加强粮食安全保障，完善粮食生产、收购、储存、运输、加工、销售协同保障机制，建设国家粮食安全产业带，调动粮食生产者和地方人民政府保护耕地、种粮、做好粮食安全保障工作的积极性，全面推进乡村振兴，促进粮食产业高质量发展，增强国家粮食安全保障能力。  
国家引导社会资本投入粮食生产、储备、流通、加工等领域，并保障其合法权益。  
国家引导金融机构合理推出金融产品和服务，为粮食生产、储备、流通、加工等提供支持。国家完善政策性农业保险制度，鼓励开展商业性保险业务。

**Article 6** The State shall establish and improve the investment mechanism for food security guarantee, adopt fiscal, financial and other support policies to strengthen food security guarantee, improve the coordinated guarantee mechanism for grain production, purchase, storage, transportation, processing, and sales, build national food security industrial belts, mobilize the enthusiasm of grain producers and local people's governments in protecting farmland, growing grain, and doing a good job in food security guarantee, comprehensively promote rural revitalization, promote high-quality development of the grain industry, and enhance the national food security guarantee capability.  
The State shall guide the investment of social capital in grain production, storage, circulation, processing and other fields, and protect their legitimate rights and interests.  
The State shall guide financial institutions to reasonably launch financial products and services to provide support for grain production, storage, circulation, processing, etc. The State shall improve the policy-oriented agricultural insurance system and encourage the development of commercial insurance business.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家加强粮食安全科技创新能力和信息化建设，支持粮食领域基础研究、关键技术研发和标准化工作，完善科技人才培养、评价和激励等机制，促进科技创新成果转化和先进技术、设备的推广使用，提高粮食生产、储备、流通、加工的科技支撑能力和应用水平。

**Article 7** The State shall strengthen scientific and technological innovation capabilities and informationization construction for food security, support basic research, key technology research and development and standardization work in the grain field, improve mechanisms for training, evaluating and incentivizing scientific and technological talents, promote the transformation of scientific and technological innovation achievements and the promotion and use of advanced technologies and equipment, and improve the scientific and technological support capacity and application level of grain production, storage, circulation, and processing.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　各级人民政府及有关部门应当采取多种形式加强粮食安全宣传教育，提升全社会粮食安全意识，引导形成爱惜粮食、节约粮食的良好风尚。

**Article 8** People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall adopt various forms to strengthen food security publicity and education, enhance the awareness of food security in the whole society, and guide the formation of a good fashion of cherishing grain and saving grain.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对在国家粮食安全保障工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 9** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in the work of safeguarding national food security shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with relevant state regulations.

### 第二章　耕地保护

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家实施国土空间规划下的国土空间用途管制，统筹布局农业、生态、城镇等功能空间，划定落实耕地和永久基本农田保护红线、生态保护红线和城镇开发边界，严格保护耕地。  
国务院确定省、自治区、直辖市人民政府耕地和永久基本农田保护任务。县级以上地方人民政府应当确保本行政区域内耕地和永久基本农田总量不减少、质量有提高。  
国家建立耕地保护补偿制度，调动耕地保护责任主体保护耕地的积极性。

**Article 10** The State shall implement territorial space use control under the territorial space planning, coordinate the layout of functional spaces such as agriculture, ecology, and urban areas, designate and implement the red lines for the protection of cultivated land and permanent basic farmland, the red line for ecological protection and the boundary for urban development, and strictly protect cultivated land.  
The State Council shall determine the tasks for the protection of cultivated land and permanent basic farmland of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government. The local people's governments at or above the county level shall ensure that the total amount of cultivated land and permanent basic farmland within their administrative regions does not decrease and the quality improves.  
The State shall establish a compensation system for cultivated land protection to mobilize the enthusiasm of the subjects responsible for cultivated land protection to protect cultivated land.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家实行占用耕地补偿制度，严格控制各类占用耕地行为；确需占用耕地的，应当依法落实补充耕地责任，补充与所占用耕地数量相等、质量相当的耕地。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当组织本级人民政府自然资源主管部门、农业农村主管部门对补充耕地的数量进行认定、对补充耕地的质量进行验收，并加强耕地质量跟踪评价。

**Article 11** The State shall implement a system for the compensation of occupied cultivated land, strictly control all types of occupation of cultivated land; where cultivated land indeed needs to be occupied, the responsibility for supplementing cultivated land shall be fulfilled according to law, and cultivated land equal in quantity and comparable in quality to the occupied cultivated land shall be supplemented.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall organize the competent departments of natural resources and the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of their governments to verify the quantity of supplementary cultivated land, inspect the quality of supplementary cultivated land, and strengthen the follow-up evaluation of cultivated land quality.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家严格控制耕地转为林地、草地、园地等其他农用地。禁止违规占用耕地绿化造林、挖湖造景等行为。禁止在国家批准的退耕还林还草计划外擅自扩大退耕范围。

**Article 12** The State shall strictly control the conversion of cultivated land into other types of agricultural land such as forestland, grassland, and garden land. Acts such as planting trees for greening and digging lakes to create landscapes on cultivated land in violation of regulations are prohibited. It is prohibited to arbitrarily expand the scope of returning farmland to forest or grassland beyond the plan approved by the State.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　耕地应当主要用于粮食和棉、油、糖、蔬菜等农产品及饲草饲料生产。县级以上地方人民政府应当根据粮食和重要农产品保供目标任务，加强耕地种植用途管控，落实耕地利用优先序，调整优化种植结构。具体办法由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。  
县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门应当加强耕地种植用途管控日常监督。村民委员会、农村集体经济组织发现违反耕地种植用途管控要求行为的，应当及时向乡镇人民政府或者县级人民政府农业农村主管部门报告。

**Article 13** Cultivated land shall mainly be used for the production of grain and agricultural products such as cotton, oil, sugar, and vegetables, as well as forage and feed. The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to the objectives and tasks of ensuring the supply of grain and important agricultural products, strengthen the control of the use of cultivated land, implement the priority order of cultivated land use, and adjust and optimize the planting structure. Specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the daily supervision of the control of the use of cultivated land. Where villagers' committees or rural collective economic organizations discover acts that violate the control requirements for the use of cultivated land, they shall promptly report to the township people's government or the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the county people's government.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家建立严格的耕地质量保护制度，加强高标准农田建设，按照量质并重、系统推进、永续利用的要求，坚持政府主导与社会参与、统筹规划与分步实施、用养结合与建管并重的原则，健全完善多元投入保障机制，提高建设标准和质量。

**Article 14** The State shall establish a strict quality protection system for cultivated land, strengthen the construction of high-standard farmland, in accordance with the requirements of equal emphasis on quantity and quality, systematic promotion, and sustainable use, adhere to the principles of government leadership and social participation, overall planning and phased implementation, combination of use and nurturing, and equal emphasis on construction and management, improve and perfect the diversified input guarantee mechanism, and raise construction standards and quality.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级以上人民政府应当建立耕地质量和种植用途监测网络，开展耕地质量调查和监测评价，采取土壤改良、地力培肥、治理修复等措施，提高中低产田产能，治理退化耕地，加强大中型灌区建设与改造，提升耕地质量。  
国家建立黑土地保护制度，保护黑土地的优良生产能力。  
国家建立健全耕地轮作休耕制度，鼓励农作物秸秆科学还田，加强农田防护林建设；支持推广绿色、高效粮食生产技术，促进生态环境改善和资源永续利用。

**Article 15** The people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a monitoring network for cultivated land quality and planting use, conduct surveys and monitoring evaluation of cultivated land quality, adopt measures such as soil improvement, soil fertility enhancement, and treatment and restoration to increase the production capacity of medium and low-yield fields, treat degraded cultivated land, strengthen the construction and renovation of large and medium-sized irrigation areas, and improve the quality of cultivated land.  
The State shall establish a black soil protection system to protect the excellent production capacity of black soil.  
The State shall establish and improve a system of crop rotation and fallow for cultivated land, encourage the scientific return of crop straw to the field, strengthen the construction of farmland shelterbelts; support the promotion of green and efficient grain production technologies, and promote the improvement of the ecological environment and the sustainable use of resources.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　县级以上地方人民政府应当因地制宜、分类推进撂荒地治理，采取措施引导复耕。家庭承包的发包方可以依法通过组织代耕代种等形式将撂荒地用于农业生产。

**Article 16** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in light of local conditions, promote the treatment of abandoned land by category, and take measures to guide re-cultivation. The party that grants the right to contracted management of household land may, in accordance with law, organize forms such as substitute farming and substitute planting to use abandoned land for agricultural production.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家推动盐碱地综合利用，制定相关规划和支持政策，鼓励和引导社会资本投入，挖掘盐碱地开发利用潜力，分区分类开展盐碱耕地治理改良，加快选育耐盐碱特色品种，推广改良盐碱地有效做法，遏制耕地盐碱化趋势。

**Article 17** The State shall promote the comprehensive utilization of saline-alkali land, formulate relevant plans and support policies, encourage and guide the investment of social capital, tap the potential for the development and utilization of saline-alkali land, carry out the treatment and improvement of saline-alkali farmland in different regions and categories, accelerate the selection and breeding of characteristic varieties tolerant to saline-alkali, promote effective practices for improving saline-alkali land, and curb the trend of salinization of cultivated land.

### 第三章　粮食生产

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家推进种业振兴，维护种业安全，推动种业高质量发展。  
国家加强粮食作物种质资源保护开发利用，建设国家农业种质资源库，健全国家良种繁育体系，推进粮食作物种质资源保护与管理信息化建设，提升供种保障能力。  
国家加强植物新品种权保护，支持育种基础性、前沿性研究和应用技术研究，鼓励粮食作物种子科技创新和产业化应用，支持开展育种联合攻关，培育具有自主知识产权的优良品种。

**Article 18** The State shall promote the revitalization of the seed industry, maintain the security of the seed industry, and promote the high-quality development of the seed industry.  
The State shall strengthen the protection, development and utilization of germplasm resources of grain crops, construct a national agricultural germplasm resource bank, improve the national improved seed breeding system, promote the informatization of the protection and management of germplasm resources of grain crops, and enhance the capacity to ensure seed supply.  
The State shall strengthen the protection of new plant variety rights, support basic and frontier research and applied technology research in breeding, encourage scientific and technological innovation and industrial application of grain crop seeds, support joint breeding research, and cultivate superior varieties with independent intellectual property rights.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　省级以上人民政府应当建立种子储备制度，主要用于发生灾害时的粮食生产需要及余缺调剂。

**Article 19** The people's governments at or above the provincial level shall establish a seed reserve system, mainly for the needs of grain production in the event of disasters and for adjusting surplus and deficiency.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级以上人民政府应当统筹做好肥料、农药、农用薄膜等农业生产资料稳定供应工作，引导粮食生产者科学施用化肥、农药，合理使用农用薄膜，增施有机肥料。

**Article 20** The people's governments at or above the county level shall make overall arrangements for the stable supply of agricultural production materials such as fertilizers, pesticides, and agricultural plastic films, guide grain producers to apply chemical fertilizers and pesticides scientifically, use agricultural plastic films rationally, and increase the application of organic fertilizers.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家加强水资源管理和水利基础设施建设，优化水资源配置，保障粮食生产合理用水需求。各级人民政府应当组织做好农田水利建设和运行维护，保护和完善农田灌溉排水体系，因地制宜发展高效节水农业。  
县级以上人民政府应当组织开展水土流失综合治理、土壤污染防治和地下水超采治理。

**Article 21** The State shall strengthen water resource management and water conservancy infrastructure construction, optimize the allocation of water resources, and ensure the reasonable water demand for grain production. People's governments at all levels shall organize the construction, operation and maintenance of farmland water conservancy, protect and improve the farmland irrigation and drainage system, and develop water-efficient agriculture according to local conditions.  
The people's governments at or above the county level shall organize comprehensive control of soil erosion, prevention and control of soil pollution, and treatment of over-exploitation of groundwater.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家推进农业机械产业发展，加强农业机械化作业基础条件建设，推广普及粮食生产机械化技术，鼓励使用绿色、智能、高效的农业机械，促进粮食生产全程机械化，提高粮食生产效率。

**Article 22** The State shall promote the development of the agricultural machinery industry, strengthen the construction of basic conditions for agricultural mechanization operations, promote and popularize mechanized grain production technologies, encourage the use of green, intelligent, and efficient agricultural machinery, promote full mechanization of grain production, and improve the efficiency of grain production.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家加强农业技术推广体系建设，支持推广应用先进适用的粮食生产技术，因地制宜推广间作套种等种植方法，鼓励创新推广方式，提高粮食生产技术推广服务水平，促进提高粮食单产。  
国家鼓励农业信息化建设，提高粮食生产信息化、智能化水平，推进智慧农业发展。

**Article 23** The State shall strengthen the construction of the agricultural technology extension system, support the promotion and application of advanced and applicable grain production technologies, promote intercropping and relay cropping and other planting methods according to local conditions, encourage innovation in promotion methods, improve the level of grain production technology extension services, and promote the increase of grain yield per unit area.  
The State shall encourage the construction of agricultural informatization, improve the level of informatization and intellectualization of grain production, and promote the development of smart agriculture.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家加强粮食生产防灾减灾救灾能力建设。县级以上人民政府应当建立健全农业自然灾害和生物灾害监测预警体系、防灾减灾救灾工作机制，加强干旱、洪涝、低温、高温、风雹、台风等灾害防御防控技术研究应用和安全生产管理，落实灾害防治属地责任，加强粮食作物病虫害防治和植物检疫工作。  
国家鼓励和支持开展粮食作物病虫害绿色防控和统防统治。粮食生产者应当做好粮食作物病虫害防治工作，并对各级人民政府及有关部门组织开展的病虫害防治工作予以配合。

**Article 24** The State shall strengthen the capacity building for disaster prevention, mitigation and relief in grain production. The people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve the monitoring and early warning system for agricultural natural disasters and biological disasters, the working mechanism for disaster prevention, mitigation and relief, strengthen the research and application of prevention and control technologies for disasters such as drought, flood, low temperature, high temperature, wind and hail, typhoon, and safety production management, implement the territorial responsibility for disaster prevention and control, and strengthen the prevention and control of grain crop diseases and insect pests and plant quarantine.  
The State shall encourage and support the green prevention and control and unified control of grain crop diseases and insect pests. Grain producers shall do a good job in the prevention and control of grain crop diseases and insect pests, and cooperate with the prevention and control work organized by the people's governments at all levels and relevant departments.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家加强粮食生产功能区和重要农产品生产保护区建设，鼓励农业生产者种植优质农作物。县级以上人民政府应当按照规定组织划定粮食生产功能区和重要农产品生产保护区并加强建设和管理，引导农业生产者种植目标作物。

**Article 25** The State shall strengthen the construction of grain production functional zones and important agricultural product protection zones, and encourage agricultural producers to plant high-quality crops. The people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with regulations, organize the designation of grain production functional zones and important agricultural product protection zones and strengthen their construction and management, and guide agricultural producers to plant target crops.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家采取措施稳定粮食播种面积，合理布局粮食生产，粮食主产区、主销区、产销平衡区都应当保面积、保产量。  
粮食主产区应当不断提高粮食综合生产能力，粮食主销区应当稳定和提高粮食自给率，粮食产销平衡区应当确保粮食基本自给。  
国家健全粮食生产者收益保障机制，以健全市场机制为目标完善农业支持保护制度和粮食价格形成机制，促进农业增效、粮食生产者增收，保护粮食生产者的种粮积极性。  
省级以上人民政府应当通过预算安排资金，支持粮食生产。

**Article 26** The State shall take measures to stabilize the sown area of grain, rationally arrange grain production. The main grain-producing areas, main grain-marketing areas, and areas with balanced production and marketing shall all maintain area and output.  
The main grain-producing areas shall continuously improve their comprehensive grain production capacity, the main grain-marketing areas shall stabilize and increase their grain self-sufficiency rate, and the areas with balanced production and marketing shall ensure the basic self-sufficiency of grain.  
The State shall improve the mechanism for guaranteeing the income of grain producers, improve the agricultural support and protection system and the grain price formation mechanism with the goal of perfecting the market mechanism, promote agricultural efficiency and increase the income of grain producers, and protect the enthusiasm of grain producers to grow grain.  
The people's governments at or above the provincial level shall arrange funds through budgets to support grain production.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家扶持和培育家庭农场、农民专业合作社等新型农业经营主体从事粮食生产，鼓励其与农户建立利益联结机制，提高粮食生产能力和现代化水平。  
国家支持面向粮食生产者的产前、产中、产后社会化服务，提高社会化服务水平，鼓励和引导粮食适度规模经营，支持粮食生产集约化。

**Article 27** The State shall support and cultivate new agricultural business entities such as family farms and farmers' professional cooperatives to engage in grain production, encourage them to establish benefit linkage mechanisms with farmers, and improve grain production capacity and modernization level.  
The State shall support pre-production, in-production and post-production socialization services for grain producers, improve the level of socialization services, encourage and guide moderate-scale grain operations, and support the intensification of grain production.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家健全粮食主产区利益补偿机制，完善对粮食主产区和产粮大县的财政转移支付制度，调动粮食生产积极性。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本行政区域实际情况，建立健全对产粮大县的利益补偿机制，提高粮食安全保障相关指标在产粮大县经济社会发展综合考核中的比重。

**Article 28** The State shall improve the benefit compensation mechanism for major grain-producing areas, improve the fiscal transfer payment system for major grain-producing areas and large grain-producing counties, and mobilize the enthusiasm for grain production.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, according to the actual situation of their administrative regions, establish and improve the benefit compensation mechanism for large grain-producing counties, and increase the weight of food security guarantee-related indicators in the comprehensive assessment of economic and social development of large grain-producing counties.

### 第四章　粮食储备

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家建立政府粮食储备体系。政府粮食储备分为中央政府储备和地方政府储备。政府粮食储备用于调节粮食供求、稳定粮食市场、应对突发事件等。  
中央政府粮食储备规模和地方政府粮食储备总量规模由国务院确定并实行动态调整。政府粮食储备的品种结构、区域布局按照国务院有关规定确定。  
政府粮食储备的收购、销售、轮换、动用等应当严格按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 29** The State shall establish a government grain reserve system. Government grain reserves are divided into central government reserves and local government reserves. Government grain reserves are used to adjust grain supply and demand, stabilize the grain market, and respond to emergencies.  
The scale of central government grain reserves and the total scale of local government grain reserves shall be determined by the State Council and subject to dynamic adjustment. The variety structure and regional layout of government grain reserves shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.  
The purchase, sale, rotation, and use of government grain reserves shall be strictly carried out in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　承储政府粮食储备的企业或者其他组织应当遵守法律、法规和国家有关规定，实行储备与商业性经营业务分开，建立健全内部管理制度，落实安全生产责任和消防安全责任，对承储粮食数量、质量负责，实施粮食安全风险事项报告制度，确保政府粮食储备安全。  
承储中央政府粮食储备和省级地方政府粮食储备的企业应当剥离商业性经营业务。  
政府粮食储备的收购、销售、轮换、动用等应当进行全过程记录，实现政府粮食储备信息实时采集、处理、传输、共享，确保可查询、可追溯。

**Article 30** The enterprise or other organization that stores government grain reserves shall comply with the provisions of laws, regulations and relevant state regulations, separate reserve operations from commercial operations, establish and improve internal management systems, implement safety production responsibilities and fire safety responsibilities, be responsible for the quantity and quality of stored grain, implement the reporting system for food security risk matters, and ensure the safety of government grain reserves.  
Enterprises that store central government grain reserves and provincial local government grain reserves shall divest commercial operations.  
The purchase, sale, rotation, and use of government grain reserves shall be recorded throughout the process to achieve real-time collection, processing, transmission, and sharing of government grain reserve information, ensuring traceability and searchability.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　承储政府粮食储备的企业或者其他组织应当保证政府粮食储备账实相符、账账相符，实行专仓储存、专人保管、专账记载，不得虚报、瞒报政府粮食储备数量、质量、品种。  
承储政府粮食储备的企业或者其他组织应当执行储备粮食质量安全检验监测制度，保证政府粮食储备符合规定的质量安全标准、达到规定的质量等级。

**Article 31** The enterprise or other organization that stores government grain reserves shall ensure that the accounts of government grain reserves match the actual grain and that accounts match accounts, store them in dedicated warehouses, keep them by dedicated personnel, and record them in dedicated accounts, and shall not falsely report or conceal the quantity, quality, or variety of government grain reserves.  
The enterprise or other organization that stores government grain reserves shall implement the inspection and monitoring system for the quality and safety of stored grain, ensuring that government grain reserves meet the prescribed quality and safety standards and reach the prescribed quality grade.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据本行政区域实际情况，指导规模以上粮食加工企业建立企业社会责任储备，鼓励家庭农场、农民专业合作社、农业产业化龙头企业自主储粮，鼓励有条件的经营主体为农户提供粮食代储服务。

**Article 32** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to the actual situation of their administrative regions, guide grain processing enterprises above a designated scale to establish corporate social responsibility reserves, encourage family farms, farmers' professional cooperatives, and leading agricultural industrialization enterprises to store grain independently, and encourage qualified business entities to provide grain storage agency services for farmers.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级以上人民政府应当加强粮食储备基础设施及质量检验能力建设，推进仓储科技创新和推广应用，加强政府粮食储备管理信息化建设。

**Article 33** The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of grain reserve infrastructure and quality inspection capacity, promote scientific and technological innovation and application in storage, and strengthen the informationization of government grain reserve management.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上人民政府应当将政府粮食储备情况列为年度国有资产报告内容，向本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告。

**Article 34** The people's governments at or above the county level shall include the status of government grain reserves as part of the annual report on state-owned assets and report it to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level.

### 第五章　粮食流通

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家加强对粮食市场的管理，充分发挥市场作用，健全市场规则，维护市场秩序，依法保障粮食经营者公平参与市场竞争，维护粮食经营者合法权益。  
国家采取多种手段加强对粮食市场的调控，保持全国粮食供求总量基本平衡和市场基本稳定。县级以上地方人民政府应当采取措施确保国家粮食宏观调控政策的贯彻执行。

**Article 35** The State shall strengthen the management of the grain market, give full play to the role of the market, improve market rules, maintain market order, protect grain operators' fair participation in market competition according to law, and safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of grain operators.  
The State shall adopt various means to strengthen the regulation of the grain market, maintain the basic balance of total grain supply and demand nationwide and the basic stability of the market. The local people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to ensure the implementation of the national grain macro-control policies.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对粮食仓储、物流等粮食流通基础设施的建设和保护，组织建设与本行政区域粮食收储规模和保障供应要求相匹配，布局合理、功能齐全的粮食流通基础设施，并引导社会资本投入粮食流通基础设施建设。  
任何单位和个人不得侵占、损毁、擅自拆除或者迁移政府投资建设的粮食流通基础设施，不得擅自改变政府投资建设的粮食流通基础设施的用途。

**Article 36** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction and protection of grain circulation infrastructure such as grain storage and logistics, organize the construction of grain circulation infrastructure that matches the grain storage scale and supply guarantee requirements of their administrative regions, with rational layout and complete functions, and guide the investment of social capital in the construction of grain circulation infrastructure.  
No unit or individual may encroach on, damage, dismantle or relocate without authorization government-invested grain circulation infrastructure, or change the use of government-invested grain circulation infrastructure without authorization.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　从事粮食收购、储存、加工、销售的经营者以及饲料、工业用粮企业，应当按照规定建立粮食经营台账，并向所在地的县级人民政府粮食和储备主管部门报送粮食购进、储存、销售等基本数据和有关情况。

**Article 37** Operators engaged in grain purchase, storage, processing, and sales, as well as feed and industrial grain enterprises, shall establish grain business ledgers in accordance with regulations, and submit basic data and relevant information on grain purchase, storage, and sales to the competent department of grain and reserve of the local county people's government.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　为了保障市场供应、保护粮食生产者利益，必要时国务院可以根据粮食安全形势和财政状况，决定对重点粮食品种在粮食主产区实行政策性收储。

**Article 38** In order to ensure market supply and protect the interests of grain producers, when necessary, the State Council may, based on the food security situation and fiscal conditions, decide to implement policy-based procurement and storage for key grain varieties in the main grain-producing areas.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　从事粮食收购、加工、销售的规模以上经营者，应当按照所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的规定，执行特定情况下的粮食库存量。

**Article 39** Operators of grain purchase, processing and sales above a designated scale shall, in accordance with the provisions of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, implement the grain inventory level under specific circumstances.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　粮食供求关系和价格显著变化或者有可能显著变化时，县级以上人民政府及其有关部门可以按照权限采取下列措施调控粮食市场：  
（一）发布粮食市场信息；  
（二）实行政策性粮食收储和销售；  
（三）要求执行特定情况下的粮食库存量；  
（四）组织投放储备粮食；  
（五）引导粮食加工转化或者限制粮食深加工用粮数量；  
（六）其他必要措施。  
必要时，国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以依照《中华人民共和国价格法》的规定采取相应措施。

**Article 40** When grain supply and demand relations and prices change significantly or are likely to change significantly, the people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments may, within their authority, take the following measures to regulate the grain market:  
(1) publish grain market information;  
(2) implement policy-based grain procurement and storage and sales;  
(3) require the implementation of grain inventory levels under specific circumstances;  
(4) organize the injection of reserve grain;  
(5) guide grain processing conversion or limit the amount of grain used for deep processing;  
(6) other necessary measures.  
When necessary, the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may take corresponding measures in accordance with the provisions of the Price Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家建立健全粮食风险基金制度。粮食风险基金主要用于支持粮食储备、稳定粮食市场等。

**Article 41** The State shall establish and improve the grain risk fund system. The grain risk fund is mainly used to support grain reserves, stabilize the grain market, etc.

### 第六章　粮食加工

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家鼓励和引导粮食加工业发展，重点支持在粮食生产功能区和重要农产品生产保护区发展粮食加工业，协调推进粮食初加工、精深加工、综合利用加工，保障粮食加工产品有效供给和质量安全。  
粮食加工经营者应当执行国家有关标准，不得掺杂使假、以次充好，对其加工的粮食质量安全负责，接受监督。

**Article 42** The State shall encourage and guide the development of the grain processing industry, with priority given to supporting the development of the grain processing industry in grain production functional zones and important agricultural product protection zones, coordinating the development of primary processing, deep processing, and comprehensive utilization processing of grain, ensuring the effective supply and quality safety of grain processed products.  
Grain processing operators shall implement relevant national standards, shall not adulterate or pass off inferior products as quality products, and shall be responsible for the quality and safety of the grain they process and accept supervision.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家鼓励和引导粮食加工结构优化，增加优质、营养粮食加工产品供给，优先保障口粮加工，饲料用粮、工业用粮加工应当服从口粮保障。

**Article 43** The State shall encourage and guide the optimization of the grain processing structure, increase the supply of high-quality and nutritious grain processed products, give priority to ensuring the processing of rations, and the processing of feed grain and industrial grain shall be subject to the guarantee of rations.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据本行政区域人口和经济社会发展水平，科学布局粮食加工业，确保本行政区域的粮食加工能力特别是应急状态下的粮食加工能力。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当在粮食生产功能区和重要农产品生产保护区科学规划布局粮食加工能力，合理安排粮食就地就近转化。

**Article 44** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the population and economic and social development level of their administrative regions, scientifically arrange the grain processing industry to ensure the grain processing capacity of their administrative regions, especially the grain processing capacity under emergency conditions.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall scientifically plan and layout the grain processing capacity within the grain production functional zones and important agricultural product protection zones, and reasonably arrange the local conversion of grain.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家鼓励粮食主产区和主销区以多种形式建立稳定的产销关系，鼓励粮食主销区的企业在粮食主产区建立粮源基地、加工基地和仓储物流设施等，促进区域粮食供求平衡。

**Article 45** The State shall encourage the main grain-producing areas and main grain-marketing areas to establish stable production and marketing relations in various forms, encourage enterprises in the main grain-marketing areas to establish grain source bases, processing bases, and storage and logistics facilities in the main grain-producing areas, and promote the balance of regional grain supply and demand.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国家支持建设粮食加工原料基地、基础设施和物流体系，支持粮食加工新技术、新工艺、新设备的推广应用。

**Article 46** The State shall support the construction of raw material bases for grain processing, infrastructure, and logistics systems, and support the promotion and application of new technologies, new processes, and new equipment for grain processing.

### 第七章　粮食应急

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家建立统一领导、分级负责、属地管理为主的粮食应急管理体制。  
县级以上人民政府应当加强粮食应急体系建设，健全布局合理、运转高效协调的粮食应急储存、运输、加工、供应网络，必要时建立粮食紧急疏运机制，确保具备与应急需求相适应的粮食应急能力，定期开展应急演练和培训。

**Article 47** The State shall establish a grain emergency management system with unified leadership, hierarchical responsibility, and territorial management as the main principle.  
The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of the grain emergency system, improve the rationally distributed, efficient and coordinated grain emergency storage, transportation, processing, and supply network, establish a grain emergency evacuation mechanism when necessary, ensure the grain emergency capacity suitable for emergency needs, and conduct regular emergency drills and training.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国务院发展改革、粮食和储备主管部门会同有关部门制定全国的粮食应急预案，报请国务院批准。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当根据本行政区域的实际情况，制定本行政区域的粮食应急预案。  
设区的市级、县级人民政府粮食应急预案的制定，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府决定。

**Article 48** The competent departments of development and reform, grain and reserve of the State Council shall, together with other relevant departments, formulate the national grain emergency plan and submit it to the State Council for approval. The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate the grain emergency plans for their respective administrative regions based on the actual situation.  
The formulation of grain emergency plans of the people's governments of cities divided into districts and counties shall be decided by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家建立粮食市场异常波动报告制度。发生突发事件，引起粮食市场供求关系和价格异常波动时，县级以上地方人民政府发展改革、农业农村、粮食和储备、市场监督管理等主管部门应当及时将粮食市场有关情况向本级人民政府和上一级人民政府主管部门报告。

**Article 49** The State shall establish a reporting system for abnormal fluctuations in the grain market. When an emergency occurs, causing abnormal fluctuations in grain supply and demand relations and prices, the competent departments of development and reform, agriculture and rural affairs, grain and reserve, market supervision and administration, etc. of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall promptly report the relevant situation of the grain market to the people's government at the same level and the competent department of the people's government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　县级以上人民政府按照权限确认出现粮食应急状态的，应当及时启动应急响应，可以依法采取下列应急处置措施：  
（一）[本法第四十条](#art-40)规定的措施；  
（二）增设应急供应网点；  
（三）组织进行粮食加工、运输和供应；  
（四）征用粮食、仓储设施、场地、交通工具以及保障粮食供应的其他物资；  
（五）其他必要措施。  
必要时，国务院可以依照《中华人民共和国价格法》的规定采取相应措施。  
出现粮食应急状态时，有关单位和个人应当服从县级以上人民政府的统一指挥和调度，配合采取应急处置措施，协助维护粮食市场秩序。  
因执行粮食应急处置措施给他人造成损失的，县级以上人民政府应当按照规定予以公平、合理补偿。

**Article 50** Upon confirmation of the occurrence of a grain emergency situation within its authority, the people's government at or above the county level shall promptly initiate an emergency response and may take the following emergency response measures according to law:  
(1) the measures provided for in Article 40 of this Law;  
(2) add emergency supply outlets;  
(3) organize grain processing, transportation, and supply;  
(4) requisition grain, storage facilities, sites, means of transport, and other materials for ensuring grain supply;  
(5) other necessary measures.  
When necessary, the State Council may take corresponding measures in accordance with the provisions of the Price Law of the People's Republic of China.  
When a grain emergency occurs, the relevant units and individuals shall obey the unified command and dispatch of the people's governments at or above the county level, cooperate in taking emergency response measures, and assist in maintaining the order of the grain market.  
If losses are caused to others due to the implementation of grain emergency response measures, the people's government at or above the county level shall provide fair and reasonable compensation in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　粮食应急状态消除后，县级以上人民政府应当及时终止实施应急处置措施，并恢复应对粮食应急状态的能力。

**Article 51** After the food emergency situation is eliminated, the people's governments at or above the county level shall promptly terminate the implementation of emergency response measures and restore the capacity to respond to food emergency situations.

### 第八章　粮食节约

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家厉行节约，反对浪费。县级以上人民政府应当建立健全引导激励与惩戒教育相结合的机制，加强对粮食节约工作的领导和监督管理，推进粮食节约工作。  
县级以上人民政府发展改革、农业农村、粮食和储备、市场监督管理、商务、工业和信息化、交通运输等有关部门，应当依照职责做好粮食生产、储备、流通、加工、消费等环节的粮食节约工作。

**Article 52** The State shall practice strict economy and oppose waste. The people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve a mechanism integrating guidance, incentives, punishment and education, strengthen leadership and supervision and management over food conservation work, and promote food conservation.  
The relevant departments such as development and reform, agriculture and rural affairs, food and reserve, market supervision and administration, commerce, industry and information technology, and transport of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their duties, perform well the food conservation work in the links of food production, storage, circulation, processing and consumption.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　粮食生产者应当加强粮食作物生长期保护和生产作业管理，减少播种、田间管理、收获等环节的粮食损失和浪费。  
禁止故意毁坏在耕地上种植的粮食作物青苗。  
国家鼓励和支持推广适时农业机械收获和产地烘干等实用技术，引导和扶持粮食生产者科学收获、储存粮食，改善粮食收获、储存条件，保障粮食品质良好，减少产后损失。

**Article 53** Food producers shall strengthen protection during the growth period of food crops and production operation management to reduce food loss and waste in the links of sowing, field management and harvesting.  
It is prohibited to intentionally damage the seedlings of food crops planted on cultivated land.  
The State shall encourage and support the promotion of practical technologies such as timely agricultural machinery harvesting and on-site drying, guide and support food producers to harvest and store food scientifically, improve food harvesting and storage conditions, ensure good quality of food, and reduce post-harvest losses.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家鼓励粮食经营者运用先进、高效的粮食储存、运输、加工设施设备，减少粮食损失损耗。

**Article 54** The State shall encourage food business operators to use advanced and efficient food storage, transport and processing facilities and equipment to reduce food loss and waste.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家推广应用粮食适度加工技术，防止过度加工，提高成品粮出品率。  
国家优化工业用粮生产结构，调控粮食不合理加工转化。

**Article 55** The State shall promote and apply moderate food processing technologies, prevent excessive processing, and increase the yield rate of finished grain.  
The State shall optimize the production structure of grain for industrial use, and regulate and control unreasonable processing and conversion of grain.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　粮食食品生产经营者应当依照有关法律、法规的规定，建立健全生产、储存、运输、加工等管理制度，引导消费者合理消费，防止和减少粮食浪费。  
公民个人和家庭应当树立文明、健康、理性、绿色的消费理念，培养形成科学健康、物尽其用、杜绝浪费的良好习惯。

**Article 56** Producers and business operators of food and food products shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, establish and improve management systems for production, storage, transport, processing, etc., guide consumers to consume rationally, and prevent and reduce food waste.  
Individual citizens and families shall establish a civilized, healthy, rational and green consumption concept, and cultivate good habits of being scientific and healthy, making the best use of things, and eliminating waste.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　机关、人民团体、社会组织、学校、企业事业单位等应当加强本单位食堂的管理，定期开展节约粮食检查，纠正浪费行为。  
有关粮食食品学会、协会等应当依法制定和完善节约粮食、减少损失损耗的相关团体标准，开展节约粮食知识普及和宣传教育工作。

**Article 57** Organs, people's organizations, social organizations, schools, enterprises, public institutions, etc. shall strengthen the management of their own canteens, regularly conduct inspections on food conservation, and correct wasteful behaviors.  
Relevant societies and associations of food and food products shall, according to law, formulate and improve relevant group standards for conserving food and reducing loss and waste, and carry out popularization and publicity and education on food conservation knowledge.

### 第九章　监督管理

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　县级以上人民政府发展改革、农业农村、粮食和储备、自然资源、水行政、生态环境、市场监督管理、工业和信息化等有关部门应当依照职责对粮食生产、储备、流通、加工等实施监督检查，并建立粮食安全监管协调机制和信息共享机制，加强协作配合。

**Article 58** The relevant departments such as development and reform, agriculture and rural affairs, food and reserve, natural resources, water administration, ecological environment, market supervision and administration, and industry and information technology of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their duties, conduct supervision and inspection over food production, storage, circulation, processing, etc., and establish a coordination mechanism for food safety supervision and an information sharing mechanism, and strengthen collaboration and coordination.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国务院发展改革、农业农村、粮食和储备主管部门应当会同有关部门建立粮食安全监测预警体系，加强粮食安全风险评估，健全粮食安全信息发布机制。  
任何单位和个人不得编造、散布虚假的粮食安全信息。

**Article 59** The competent departments of development and reform, agriculture and rural affairs, and food and reserve of the State Council shall, together with relevant departments, establish a food safety monitoring and early warning system, strengthen food safety risk assessment, and improve the food safety information release mechanism.  
No unit or individual may fabricate or disseminate false food safety information.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国家完善粮食生产、储存、运输、加工标准体系。粮食生产经营者应当严格遵守有关法律、法规的规定，执行有关标准和技术规范，确保粮食质量安全。  
县级以上人民政府应当依法加强粮食生产、储备、流通、加工等环节的粮食质量安全监督管理工作，建立粮食质量安全追溯体系，完善粮食质量安全风险监测和检验制度。

**Article 60** The State shall improve the standard system for food production, storage, transport and processing. Food producers and business operators shall strictly abide by the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, implement relevant standards and technical specifications, and ensure food quality and safety.  
The people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, strengthen the supervision and management of food quality and safety in the links of food production, storage, circulation, processing, etc., establish a traceability system for food quality and safety, and improve the monitoring and inspection system for food quality and safety risks.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　县级以上人民政府有关部门依照职责开展粮食安全监督检查，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）进入粮食生产经营场所实施现场检查；  
（二）向有关单位和人员调查了解相关情况；  
（三）进入涉嫌违法活动的场所调查取证；  
（四）查阅、复制有关文件、资料、账簿、凭证，对可能被转移、隐匿或者损毁的文件、资料、账簿、凭证、电子设备等予以封存；  
（五）查封、扣押涉嫌违法活动的场所、设施或者财物；  
（六）对有关单位的法定代表人、负责人或者其他工作人员进行约谈、询问。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门履行监督检查职责，发现公职人员涉嫌职务违法或者职务犯罪的问题线索，应当及时移送监察机关，监察机关应当依法受理并进行调查处置。

**Article 61** When conducting food safety supervision and inspection in accordance with their duties, the relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level may take the following measures:  
(1) entering the premises of food production and business operations to conduct on-site inspections;  
(2) investigating and understanding relevant circumstances from relevant units and individuals;  
(3) entering the premises suspected of illegal activities to investigate and collect evidence;  
(4) consulting and copying relevant documents, materials, account books, and vouchers, and sealing up documents, materials, account books, vouchers, electronic equipment, etc. that may be transferred, concealed, or damaged;  
(5) sealing up or seizing premises, facilities, or property suspected of illegal activities;  
(6) conducting interviews and inquiries with the legal representative, person in charge, or other staff members of the relevant unit.  
When performing supervision and inspection duties, the relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, upon discovering clues of issues concerning public officials suspected of duty-related violations or duty-related crimes, shall promptly transfer such clues to the supervisory organs, and the supervisory organs shall accept and investigate and handle them according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　国务院发展改革、自然资源、农业农村、粮食和储备主管部门应当会同有关部门，按照规定具体实施对省、自治区、直辖市落实耕地保护和粮食安全责任制情况的考核。  
省、自治区、直辖市对本行政区域耕地保护和粮食安全负总责，其主要负责人是本行政区域耕地保护和粮食安全的第一责任人，对本行政区域内的耕地保护和粮食安全目标负责。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当定期对本行政区域耕地保护和粮食安全责任落实情况开展监督检查，将耕地保护和粮食安全责任落实情况纳入对本级人民政府有关部门负责人、下级人民政府及其负责人的考核评价内容。  
对耕地保护和粮食安全工作责任落实不力、问题突出的地方人民政府，上级人民政府可以对其主要负责人进行责任约谈。被责任约谈的地方人民政府应当立即采取措施进行整改。

**Article 62** The competent departments of development and reform, natural resources, agriculture and rural affairs, and food and reserve of the State Council shall, together with relevant departments, specifically implement the assessment of the implementation of the responsibility system for cultivated land protection and food safety by provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government in accordance with the provisions.  
Provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall bear overall responsibility for cultivated land protection and food safety within their respective administrative regions, and their principal leading persons shall be the first responsible persons for cultivated land protection and food safety in their administrative regions, and shall be responsible for the targets of cultivated land protection and food safety within their administrative regions.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall regularly conduct supervision and inspection on the implementation of responsibilities for cultivated land protection and food safety within their administrative regions, and incorporate the implementation of responsibilities for cultivated land protection and food safety into the assessment and evaluation content for the responsible persons of the relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level and for the people's governments at lower levels and their responsible persons.  
For local people's governments that have ineffective implementation of responsibilities for cultivated land protection and food safety and prominent problems, the people's government at a higher level may conduct a responsibility interview with their principal leading persons. The local people's government subjected to the responsibility interview shall immediately take measures to rectify.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　外商投资粮食生产经营，影响或者可能影响国家安全的，应当按照国家有关规定进行外商投资安全审查。

**Article 63** Where foreign investment in food production and business operations affects or may affect national security, a foreign investment security review shall be conducted in accordance with relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　县级以上人民政府发展改革、农业农村、粮食和储备等主管部门应当加强粮食安全信用体系建设，建立粮食生产经营者信用记录。  
单位、个人有权对粮食安全保障工作进行监督，对违反本法的行为向县级以上人民政府有关部门进行投诉、举报，接到投诉、举报的部门应当按照规定及时处理。

**Article 64** The competent departments of development and reform, agriculture and rural affairs, food and reserve, etc. of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of the food safety credit system and establish credit records of food producers and business operators.  
Units and individuals have the right to supervise food security assurance work, and to complain and report acts violating this Law to the relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, and the departments receiving such complaints and reports shall handle them in a timely manner in accordance with the provisions.

### 第十章　法律责任

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本法规定，地方人民政府和县级以上人民政府有关部门不履行粮食安全保障工作职责或者有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 65** Where, in violation of this Law, local people's governments and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level fail to perform their duties in food security assurance work or commit other acts of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain, the leading persons responsible and the directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本法规定，种植不符合耕地种植用途管控要求作物的，由县级人民政府农业农村主管部门或者乡镇人民政府给予批评教育；经批评教育仍不改正的，可以不予发放粮食生产相关补贴；对有关农业生产经营组织，可以依法处以罚款。

**Article 66** Where, in violation of this Law, crops that do not meet the control requirements for the use purpose of cultivated land are planted, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at the county level or the people's government of a township or town shall give criticism and education; if no correction is made after criticism and education, the relevant subsidies for food production may be withheld; for the relevant agricultural production and business operation organizations, a fine may be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　违反本法规定，承储政府粮食储备的企业或者其他组织有下列行为之一的，依照有关行政法规的规定处罚：  
（一）拒不执行或者违反政府粮食储备的收购、销售、轮换、动用等规定；  
（二）未对政府粮食储备的收购、销售、轮换、动用等进行全过程记录；  
（三）未按照规定保障政府粮食储备数量、质量安全。  
从事粮食收购、储存、加工、销售的经营者以及饲料、工业用粮企业未按照规定建立粮食经营台账，或者报送粮食基本数据和有关情况的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 67** Where, in violation of this Law, an enterprise or other organization that undertakes government food reserves commits any of the following acts, it shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of relevant administrative regulations:  
(1) refusing to implement or violating the provisions on the purchase, sale, rotation, use, etc. of government food reserves;  
(2) failing to conduct a full-process record of the purchase, sale, rotation, use, etc. of government food reserves;  
(3) failing to ensure the quantity and quality safety of government food reserves in accordance with the provisions.  
Where business operators engaged in food purchase, storage, processing, and sales, as well as feed and industrial grain enterprises, fail to establish food business ledgers or submit basic food data and relevant information in accordance with the provisions, they shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　违反本法规定，侵占、损毁、擅自拆除或者迁移政府投资建设的粮食流通基础设施，或者擅自改变其用途的，由县级以上地方人民政府有关部门依照职责责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施；逾期不恢复原状、不采取其他补救措施的，对单位处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款，对个人处五千元以上五万元以下罚款。

**Article 68** Where, in violation of this Law, anyone encroaches on, damages, dismantles or relocates without authorization, or changes the use of, the food circulation infrastructure constructed with government investment, the relevant departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their duties, order cessation of the illegal act and restoration to the original condition or adoption of other remedial measures within a time limit; if restoration or other remedial measures are not taken within the time limit, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed on the unit, and a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　违反本法规定，粮食应急状态发生时，不服从县级以上人民政府的统一指挥和调度，或者不配合采取应急处置措施的，由县级以上人民政府有关部门依照职责责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正的，对单位处二万元以上二十万元以下罚款，对个人处二千元以上二万元以下罚款；情节严重的，对单位处二十万元以上二百万元以下罚款，对个人处二万元以上二十万元以下罚款。

**Article 69** Where, in violation of this Law, during a food emergency situation, anyone fails to obey the unified command and dispatch of the people's government at or above the county level, or fails to cooperate in taking emergency response measures, the relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their duties, order correction and give a warning; if correction is refused, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed on the unit, and a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed on the unit, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　违反本法规定，故意毁坏在耕地上种植的粮食作物青苗的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令停止违法行为；情节严重的，可以处毁坏粮食作物青苗价值五倍以下罚款。

**Article 70** Where, in violation of this Law, anyone intentionally damages the seedlings of food crops planted on cultivated land, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not more than five times the value of the damaged seedlings of food crops may be imposed.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　违反有关土地管理、耕地保护、种子、农产品质量安全、食品安全、反食品浪费、安全生产等法律、行政法规的，依照相关法律、行政法规的规定处理、处罚。

**Article 71** Where any law or administrative regulation on land administration, cultivated land protection, seeds, quality and safety of agricultural products, food safety, anti-food waste, work safety, etc. is violated, the matter shall be handled and punished in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　违反本法规定，给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 72** Where, in violation of this Law, losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十一章　附则

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　本法所称粮食，是指小麦、稻谷、玉米、大豆、杂粮及其成品粮。杂粮包括谷子、高粱、大麦、荞麦、燕麦、青稞、绿豆、马铃薯、甘薯等。  
油料、食用植物油的安全保障工作参照适用本法。

**Article 73** For the purposes of this Law, "food" refers to wheat, rice, corn, soybeans, miscellaneous grains and their processed products. Miscellaneous grains include millet, sorghum, barley, buckwheat, oats, highland barley, mung beans, potatoes, sweet potatoes, etc.  
The security assurance work for oilseeds and edible vegetable oils shall apply mutatis mutandis to this Law.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　本法自2024年6月1日起施行。

**Article 74** This Law shall come into force as of June 1, 2024.
