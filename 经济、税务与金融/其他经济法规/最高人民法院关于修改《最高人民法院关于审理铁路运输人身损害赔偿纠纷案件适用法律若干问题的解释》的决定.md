# 最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于审理铁路运输人身损害赔偿纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》的决定

**法律部门**：经济法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2021〕19号  
**公布日期**：2021-11-24

> 《最高人民法院关于修改〈最高人民法院关于审理铁路运输人身损害赔偿纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释〉的决定》已于2021年11月24日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1853次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年1月1日起施行。 2021年12月8日 最高人民法院 关于修改《最高人民法院关于审理铁路运输人身损 害赔偿纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》的决定 法释〔2021〕19号 最高人民法院审判委员会第1853次会议决定，对《最高人民法院关于审理铁路运输人身损害赔偿纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》（以下简称《解释》）作如下修改： 一、第一条第二款开始部分增加规定：“铁路运输企业在客运合同履行过程中造成旅客人身损害的赔偿纠纷案件，不适用本解释”。 二、删去第二条中的“依法由受害人承担扶养义务的被扶养人”。 三、删去第三条第一款中的“赔偿权利人依照民法典第三编要求承运人承担违约责任予以人身损害赔偿的，由运输始发地、目的地或者被告住所地铁路运输法院管辖”。同时增加一款作为第二款规定：“前款规定的地区没有铁路运输法院的，由高级人民法院指定的其他人民法院管辖。” 四、第五条条首部分修改为：“铁路行车事故及其他铁路运营事故造成人身损害，有下列情形之一的，铁路运输企业不承担赔偿责任”；并增加一项作为第三项规定：“法律规定铁路运输企业不承担赔偿责任的其他情形造成的。” 五、将第六条和第七条合并修改为： “因受害人的过错行为造成人身损害，依照法律规定应当由铁路运输企业承担赔偿责任的，根据受害人的过错程度可以适当减轻铁路运输企业的赔偿责任，并按照以下情形分别处理： （一）铁路运输企业未充分履行安全防护、警示等义务，铁路运输企业承担事故主要责任的，应当在全部损害的百分之九十至百分之六十之间承担赔偿责任；铁路运输企业承担事故同等责任的，应当在全部损害的百分之六十至百分之五十之间承担赔偿责任；铁路运输企业承担事故次要责任的，应当在全部损害的百分之四十至百分之十之间承担赔偿责任； （二）铁路运输企业已充分履行安全防护、警示等义务，受害人仍施以过错行为的，铁路运输企业应当在全部损害的百分之十以内承担赔偿责任。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审理铁路行车事故及其他铁路运营事故造成的铁路运输人身损害赔偿纠纷案件，适用本解释。  
铁路运输企业在客运合同履行过程中造成旅客人身损害的赔偿纠纷案件，不适用本解释；与铁路运输企业建立劳动合同关系或者形成劳动关系的铁路职工在执行职务中发生的人身损害，依照有关调整劳动关系的法律规定及其他相关法律规定处理。

**Article 1** This Interpretation shall apply to cases of disputes over compensation for personal injury in railway transport caused by railway traffic accidents and other railway operation accidents.  
This Interpretation shall not apply to disputes over compensation for personal injury caused by a railway transport enterprise to passengers during the performance of a passenger transport contract; personal injury caused to railway employees who have established a labor contract or formed a labor relationship with a railway transport enterprise in the course of performing their duties shall be dealt with in accordance with the legal provisions regulating labor relationships and other relevant legal provisions.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　铁路运输人身损害的受害人以及死亡受害人的近亲属为赔偿权利人，有权请求赔偿。

**Article 2** The victim of personal injury in railway transport and the close relatives of the deceased victim shall be the persons entitled to compensation and have the right to claim compensation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　赔偿权利人要求对方当事人承担侵权责任的，由事故发生地、列车最先到达地或者被告住所地铁路运输法院管辖。  
前款规定的地区没有铁路运输法院的，由高级人民法院指定的其他人民法院管辖。

**Article 3** Where the person entitled to compensation requests the other party to bear tort liability, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the railway transport court of the place where the accident occurred, the first place the train arrived, or the domicile of the defendant.  
If there is no railway transport court in the areas specified in the preceding paragraph, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of another People's Court designated by the Higher People's Court.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　铁路运输造成人身损害的，铁路运输企业应当承担赔偿责任；法律另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 4** Where personal injury is caused by railway transport, the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability; if otherwise provided by law, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　铁路行车事故及其他铁路运营事故造成人身损害，有下列情形之一的，铁路运输企业不承担赔偿责任：  
（一）不可抗力造成的；  
（二）受害人故意以卧轨、碰撞等方式造成的；  
（三）法律规定铁路运输企业不承担赔偿责任的其他情形造成的。

**Article 5** Where personal injury is caused by railway traffic accidents and other railway operation accidents, the railway transport enterprise shall not bear compensation liability under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Caused by force majeure;  
(2) Caused intentionally by the victim through lying on the tracks, collision, or other means;  
(3) Caused by other circumstances under which the railway transport enterprise is not liable for compensation as provided by law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　因受害人的过错行为造成人身损害，依照法律规定应当由铁路运输企业承担赔偿责任的，根据受害人的过错程度可以适当减轻铁路运输企业的赔偿责任，并按照以下情形分别处理：  
（一）铁路运输企业未充分履行安全防护、警示等义务，铁路运输企业承担事故主要责任的，应当在全部损害的百分之九十至百分之六十之间承担赔偿责任；铁路运输企业承担事故同等责任的，应当在全部损害的百分之六十至百分之五十之间承担赔偿责任；铁路运输企业承担事故次要责任的，应当在全部损害的百分之四十至百分之十之间承担赔偿责任；  
（二）铁路运输企业已充分履行安全防护、警示等义务，受害人仍施以过错行为的，铁路运输企业应当在全部损害的百分之十以内承担赔偿责任。  
铁路运输企业已充分履行安全防护、警示等义务，受害人不听从值守人员劝阻强行通过铁路平交道口、人行过道，或者明知危险后果仍然无视警示规定沿铁路线路纵向行走、坐卧故意造成人身损害的，铁路运输企业不承担赔偿责任，但是有证据证明并非受害人故意造成损害的除外。

**Article 6** Where personal injury is caused by the victim's own fault and, according to law, the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability, the compensation liability of the railway transport enterprise may be appropriately reduced based on the degree of the victim's fault, and the matter shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) Where the railway transport enterprise has not fully performed its obligations of safety protection, warning, etc., and bears primary liability for the accident, it shall bear compensation liability for between 90% and 60% of the total injury; where it bears equal liability for the accident, it shall bear compensation liability for between 60% and 50% of the total injury; where it bears secondary liability for the accident, it shall bear compensation liability for between 40% and 10% of the total injury;  
(2) Where the railway transport enterprise has fully performed its obligations of safety protection, warning, etc., but the victim still commits a fault act, the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability for no more than 10% of the total injury.  
Where the railway transport enterprise has fully performed its obligations of safety protection, warning, etc., but the victim forcibly crosses a railway level crossing or pedestrian crossing despite the warning of the duty personnel, or intentionally causes personal injury by walking or sitting along the railway line longitudinally in disregard of warning regulations with knowledge of the dangerous consequences, the railway transport enterprise shall not bear compensation liability, except where there is evidence proving that the injury was not intentionally caused by the victim.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　铁路运输造成无民事行为能力人人身损害的，铁路运输企业应当承担赔偿责任；监护人有过错的，按照过错程度减轻铁路运输企业的赔偿责任。  
铁路运输造成限制民事行为能力人人身损害的，铁路运输企业应当承担赔偿责任；监护人或者受害人自身有过错的，按照过错程度减轻铁路运输企业的赔偿责任。

**Article 7** Where railway transport causes personal injury to a person without civil capacity, the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability; if the guardian is at fault, the compensation liability of the railway transport enterprise shall be reduced in proportion to the degree of fault.  
Where railway transport causes personal injury to a person with limited civil capacity, the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability; if the guardian or the victim himself is at fault, the compensation liability of the railway transport enterprise shall be reduced in proportion to the degree of fault.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　铁路机车车辆与机动车发生碰撞造成机动车驾驶人员以外的人人身损害的，由铁路运输企业与机动车一方对受害人承担连带赔偿责任。铁路运输企业与机动车一方之间的责任份额根据各自责任大小确定；难以确定责任大小的，平均承担责任。对受害人实际承担赔偿责任超出应当承担份额的一方，有权向另一方追偿。  
铁路机车车辆与机动车发生碰撞造成机动车驾驶人员人身损害的，按照[本解释第四条](#art-4)至第六条的规定处理。

**Article 8** Where a collision between a railway locomotive or rolling stock and a motor vehicle causes personal injury to a person other than the motor vehicle driver, the railway transport enterprise and the motor vehicle party shall bear joint and several compensation liability to the victim. The share of liability between the railway transport enterprise and the motor vehicle party shall be determined according to their respective degrees of liability; if the degrees of liability are difficult to determine, they shall bear liability equally. A party that has actually borne compensation liability to the victim exceeding its own share shall have the right to seek recourse from the other party.  
Where a collision between a railway locomotive or rolling stock and a motor vehicle causes personal injury to the motor vehicle driver, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Articles 4 to 6 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　在非铁路运输企业实行监护的铁路无人看守道口发生事故造成人身损害的，由铁路运输企业按照本解释的有关规定承担赔偿责任。道口管理单位有过错的，铁路运输企业对赔偿权利人承担赔偿责任后，有权向道口管理单位追偿。

**Article 9** Where an accident occurs at a railway crossing without guards under the supervision of a non-railway transport enterprise, causing personal injury, the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Interpretation. If the crossing management unit is at fault, after the railway transport enterprise bears compensation liability to the person entitled to compensation, it shall have the right to seek recourse from the crossing management unit.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对于铁路桥梁、涵洞等设施负有管理、维护等职责的单位，因未尽职责使该铁路桥梁、涵洞等设施不能正常使用，导致行人、车辆穿越铁路线路造成人身损害的，铁路运输企业按照本解释有关规定承担赔偿责任后，有权向该单位追偿。

**Article 10** Where a unit responsible for management, maintenance, etc., of facilities such as railway bridges and culverts fails to perform its duties, rendering such railway bridges and culverts unusable, causing pedestrians or vehicles to cross railway lines and resulting in personal injury, after the railway transport enterprise bears compensation liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Interpretation, it shall have the right to seek recourse from that unit.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　有权作出事故认定的组织依照《铁路交通事故应急救援和调查处理条例》等有关规定制作的事故认定书，经庭审质证，对于事故认定书所认定的事实，当事人没有相反证据和理由足以推翻的，人民法院应当作为认定事实的根据。

**Article 11** An accident determination report prepared by an organization authorized to make accident determinations in accordance with the Regulations on Emergency Rescue, Investigation, and Handling of Railway Traffic Accidents and other relevant provisions, after being examined and cross-examined in court, shall be taken as the basis for the People's Court to determine the facts stated therein, unless the parties have sufficient contrary evidence and reasons to rebut it.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在专用铁路及铁路专用线上因运输造成人身损害，依法应当由肇事工具或者设备的所有人、使用人或者管理人承担赔偿责任的，适用本解释。

**Article 12** This Interpretation shall apply to personal injury caused by transport on dedicated railways and railway sidings, where, according to law, the owner, user, or manager of the causing tool or equipment shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本院以前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 13** Where any previously issued judicial interpretation of this Court is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
