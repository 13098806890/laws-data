# 中华人民共和国深海海底区域资源勘探开发法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2016-02-26  
**生效日期**：2016-05-01

> （2016年2月26日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十九次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　勘探、开发 第三章　环境保护 第四章　科学技术研究与资源调查 第五章　监督检查 第六章　法律责任 第七章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范深海海底区域资源勘探、开发活动，推进深海科学技术研究、资源调查，保护海洋环境，促进深海海底区域资源可持续利用，维护人类共同利益，制定本法。 第二条　中华人民共和国的公民、法人或者其他组织从事深海海底区域资源勘探、开发和相关环境保护、科学技术研究、资源调查活动，适用本法。 本法所称深海海底区域，是指中华人民共和国和其他国家管辖范围以外的海床、洋底及其底土。 第三条　深海海底区域资源勘探、开发活动应当坚持和平利用、合作共享、保护环境、维护人类共同利益的原则。 国家保护从事深海海底区域资源勘探、开发和资源调查活动的中华人民共和国公民、法人或者其他组织的正当权益。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范深海海底区域资源勘探、开发活动，推进深海科学技术研究、资源调查，保护海洋环境，促进深海海底区域资源可持续利用，维护人类共同利益，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted to regulate the exploration and exploitation of resources in the deep seabed area, to promote scientific and technological research and resource surveys in the deep sea, to protect the marine environment, to facilitate the sustainable use of resources in the deep seabed area, and to safeguard the common interests of mankind.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国的公民、法人或者其他组织从事深海海底区域资源勘探、开发和相关环境保护、科学技术研究、资源调查活动，适用本法。  
本法所称深海海底区域，是指中华人民共和国和其他国家管辖范围以外的海床、洋底及其底土。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to citizens of the People's Republic of China, legal persons, or other organizations engaged in the exploration and exploitation of resources in the deep seabed area and related environmental protection, scientific and technological research, and resource survey activities.  
The term "deep seabed area" as used in this Law shall mean the seabed, ocean floor, and subsoil thereof beyond the limits of national jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China and other States.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　深海海底区域资源勘探、开发活动应当坚持和平利用、合作共享、保护环境、维护人类共同利益的原则。  
国家保护从事深海海底区域资源勘探、开发和资源调查活动的中华人民共和国公民、法人或者其他组织的正当权益。

**Article 3** The exploration and exploitation of resources in the deep seabed area shall adhere to the principles of peaceful use, cooperation and sharing, environmental protection, and safeguarding the common interests of mankind.  
The State shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of citizens of the People's Republic of China, legal persons, or other organizations engaged in the exploration, exploitation, and resource survey activities in the deep seabed area.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家制定有关深海海底区域资源勘探、开发规划，并采取经济、技术政策和措施，鼓励深海科学技术研究和资源调查，提升资源勘探、开发和海洋环境保护的能力。

**Article 4** The State shall formulate plans for the exploration and exploitation of resources in the deep seabed area and adopt economic and technological policies and measures to encourage scientific and technological research and resource surveys in the deep sea, and to enhance the capacity for resource exploration, exploitation, and marine environmental protection.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院海洋主管部门负责对深海海底区域资源勘探、开发和资源调查活动的监督管理。国务院其他有关部门按照国务院规定的职责负责相关管理工作。

**Article 5** The marine administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the exploration, exploitation, and resource survey activities in the deep seabed area. Other relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for relevant administrative work in accordance with the functions assigned by the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励和支持在深海海底区域资源勘探、开发和相关环境保护、资源调查、科学技术研究和教育培训等方面，开展国际合作。

**Article 6** The State shall encourage and support international cooperation in the exploration and exploitation of resources in the deep seabed area and related environmental protection, resource surveys, scientific and technological research, and education and training.

### 第二章　勘探、开发

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　中华人民共和国的公民、法人或者其他组织在向国际海底管理局申请从事深海海底区域资源勘探、开发活动前，应当向国务院海洋主管部门提出申请，并提交下列材料：  
（一）申请者基本情况；  
（二）拟勘探、开发区域位置、面积、矿产种类等说明；  
（三）财务状况、投资能力证明和技术能力说明；  
（四）勘探、开发工作计划，包括勘探、开发活动可能对海洋环境造成影响的相关资料，海洋环境严重损害等的应急预案；  
（五）国务院海洋主管部门规定的其他材料。

**Article 7** Before applying to the International Seabed Authority for engaging in the exploration and exploitation of resources in the deep seabed area, citizens of the People's Republic of China, legal persons, or other organizations shall submit an application to the marine administrative department of the State Council and provide the following materials:  
(1) Basic information of the applicant;  
(2) A description of the location, area, and types of minerals of the proposed exploration or exploitation area;  
(3) Proof of financial status, investment capacity, and a description of technical capacity;  
(4) A plan for the exploration or exploitation work, including relevant information on the possible impacts of exploration or exploitation activities on the marine environment, and contingency plans for serious damage to the marine environment;  
(5) Other materials as required by the marine administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院海洋主管部门应当对申请者提交的材料进行审查，对于符合国家利益并具备资金、技术、装备等能力条件的，应当在六十个工作日内予以许可，并出具相关文件。  
获得许可的申请者在与国际海底管理局签订勘探、开发合同成为承包者后，方可从事勘探、开发活动。  
承包者应当自勘探、开发合同签订之日起三十日内，将合同副本报国务院海洋主管部门备案。  
国务院海洋主管部门应当将承包者及其勘探、开发的区域位置、面积等信息通报有关机关。

**Article 8** The marine administrative department of the State Council shall review the materials submitted by the applicant. Where the application is in the national interest and the applicant has the requisite financial, technical, equipment, and other capabilities, the department shall grant approval within sixty working days and issue relevant documents.  
An applicant that has obtained approval may engage in exploration or exploitation activities only after signing an exploration or exploitation contract with the International Seabed Authority and becoming a contractor.  
The contractor shall, within thirty days from the date of signing the exploration or exploitation contract, submit a copy of the contract to the marine administrative department of the State Council for record.  
The marine administrative department of the State Council shall notify the relevant authorities of information such as the contractor and the location and area of its exploration or exploitation area.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　承包者对勘探、开发合同区域内特定资源享有相应的专属勘探、开发权。  
承包者应当履行勘探、开发合同义务，保障从事勘探、开发作业人员的人身安全，保护海洋环境。  
承包者从事勘探、开发作业应当保护作业区域内的文物、铺设物等。  
承包者从事勘探、开发作业还应当遵守中华人民共和国有关安全生产、劳动保护方面的法律、行政法规。

**Article 9** A contractor shall enjoy the corresponding exclusive right to explore and exploit specific resources within the contract area.  
The contractor shall fulfill the obligations under the exploration or exploitation contract, ensure the personal safety of personnel engaged in exploration or exploitation operations, and protect the marine environment.  
The contractor shall protect cultural relics, installations, and other objects within the operation area during exploration or exploitation operations.  
The contractor shall also comply with the laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China on production safety and labor protection when conducting exploration or exploitation operations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　承包者在转让勘探、开发合同的权利、义务前，或者在对勘探、开发合同作出重大变更前，应当报经国务院海洋主管部门同意。  
承包者应当自勘探、开发合同转让、变更或者终止之日起三十日内，报国务院海洋主管部门备案。  
国务院海洋主管部门应当及时将勘探、开发合同转让、变更或者终止的信息通报有关机关。

**Article 10** A contractor shall report to and obtain consent from the marine administrative department of the State Council before assigning the rights and obligations under the exploration or exploitation contract, or before making any significant modification to the contract.  
The contractor shall, within thirty days from the date of assignment, modification, or termination of the exploration or exploitation contract, report to the marine administrative department of the State Council for record.  
The marine administrative department of the State Council shall promptly notify the relevant authorities of the assignment, modification, or termination of the exploration or exploitation contract.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　发生或者可能发生严重损害海洋环境等事故，承包者应当立即启动应急预案，并采取下列措施：  
（一）立即发出警报；  
（二）立即报告国务院海洋主管部门，国务院海洋主管部门应当及时通报有关机关；  
（三）采取一切实际可行与合理的措施，防止、减少、控制对人身、财产、海洋环境的损害。

**Article 11** Where an accident that causes or may cause serious damage to the marine environment occurs, the contractor shall immediately activate the contingency plan and take the following measures:  
(1) Issue an immediate warning;  
(2) Immediately report to the marine administrative department of the State Council, which shall promptly notify the relevant authorities;  
(3) Take all practically feasible and reasonable measures to prevent, reduce, and control damage to persons, property, and the marine environment.

### 第三章　环境保护

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　承包者应当在合理、可行的范围内，利用可获得的先进技术，采取必要措施，防止、减少、控制勘探、开发区域内的活动对海洋环境造成的污染和其他危害。

**Article 12** A contractor shall, within the scope of what is reasonable and feasible, use available advanced technologies and take necessary measures to prevent, reduce, and control pollution and other hazards to the marine environment caused by activities in the exploration or exploitation area.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　承包者应当按照勘探、开发合同的约定和要求、国务院海洋主管部门规定，调查研究勘探、开发区域的海洋状况，确定环境基线，评估勘探、开发活动可能对海洋环境的影响；制定和执行环境监测方案，监测勘探、开发活动对勘探、开发区域海洋环境的影响，并保证监测设备正常运行，保存原始监测记录。

**Article 13** A contractor shall, in accordance with the terms and requirements of the exploration or exploitation contract and the provisions of the marine administrative department of the State Council, survey and study the marine conditions of the exploration or exploitation area, establish environmental baselines, and assess the possible impacts of exploration or exploitation activities on the marine environment; formulate and implement an environmental monitoring program to monitor the impacts of exploration or exploitation activities on the marine environment of the area, and ensure the normal operation of monitoring equipment and preserve original monitoring records.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　承包者从事勘探、开发活动应当采取必要措施，保护和保全稀有或者脆弱的生态系统，以及衰竭、受威胁或者有灭绝危险的物种和其他海洋生物的生存环境，保护海洋生物多样性，维护海洋资源的可持续利用。

**Article 14** A contractor engaging in exploration or exploitation activities shall take necessary measures to protect and preserve rare or fragile ecosystems, as well as the habitats of depleted, threatened, or endangered species and other marine organisms, protect marine biodiversity, and maintain the sustainable use of marine resources.

### 第四章　科学技术研究与资源调查

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家支持深海科学技术研究和专业人才培养，将深海科学技术列入科学技术发展的优先领域，鼓励与相关产业的合作研究。  
国家支持企业进行深海科学技术研究与技术装备研发。

**Article 15** The State shall support deep-sea scientific and technological research and the training of professionals, include deep-sea science and technology as a priority area in the development of science and technology, and encourage cooperative research with related industries.  
The State shall support enterprises in conducting deep-sea scientific and technological research and the research and development of technical equipment.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家支持深海公共平台的建设和运行，建立深海公共平台共享合作机制，为深海科学技术研究、资源调查活动提供专业服务，促进深海科学技术交流、合作及成果共享。

**Article 16** The State shall support the construction and operation of public deep-sea platforms, establish a mechanism for sharing and cooperation on public deep-sea platforms, provide professional services for deep-sea scientific and technological research and resource survey activities, and promote exchange, cooperation, and sharing of deep-sea scientific and technological achievements.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家鼓励单位和个人通过开放科学考察船舶、实验室、陈列室和其他场地、设施，举办讲座或者提供咨询等多种方式，开展深海科学普及活动。

**Article 17** The State shall encourage entities and individuals to carry out deep-sea science popularization activities by means such as opening scientific research vessels, laboratories, exhibition rooms, and other venues and facilities, holding lectures, or providing consultations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　从事深海海底区域资源调查活动的公民、法人或者其他组织，应当按照有关规定将有关资料副本、实物样本或者目录汇交国务院海洋主管部门和其他相关部门。负责接受汇交的部门应当对汇交的资料和实物样本进行登记、保管，并按照有关规定向社会提供利用。  
承包者从事深海海底区域资源勘探、开发活动取得的有关资料、实物样本等的汇交，适用前款规定。

**Article 18** Citizens of the People's Republic of China, legal persons, or other organizations engaged in resource survey activities in the deep seabed area shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, submit copies of relevant data, physical samples, or catalogs to the marine administrative department of the State Council and other relevant departments. The department responsible for receiving such submissions shall register and keep the submitted data and physical samples, and provide them for use by the public in accordance with relevant provisions.  
The submission of relevant data, physical samples, etc., obtained by a contractor from exploration or exploitation activities in the deep seabed area shall be governed by the preceding paragraph.

### 第五章　监督检查

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国务院海洋主管部门应当对承包者勘探、开发活动进行监督检查。

**Article 19** The marine administrative department of the State Council shall supervise and inspect the exploration and exploitation activities of contractors.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　承包者应当定期向国务院海洋主管部门报告下列履行勘探、开发合同的事项：  
（一）勘探、开发活动情况；  
（二）环境监测情况；  
（三）年度投资情况；  
（四）国务院海洋主管部门要求的其他事项。

**Article 20** A contractor shall periodically report to the marine administrative department of the State Council on the following matters concerning the performance of the exploration or exploitation contract:  
(1) The status of exploration or exploitation activities;  
(2) The status of environmental monitoring;  
(3) The annual investment situation;  
(4) Other matters as required by the marine administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国务院海洋主管部门可以检查承包者用于勘探、开发活动的船舶、设施、设备以及航海日志、记录、数据等。

**Article 21** The marine administrative department of the State Council may inspect the vessels, facilities, equipment, as well as logbooks, records, data, etc., used by a contractor for exploration or exploitation activities.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　承包者应当对国务院海洋主管部门的监督检查予以协助、配合。

**Article 22** A contractor shall assist and cooperate with the supervision and inspection conducted by the marine administrative department of the State Council.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　违反[本法第七条](#art-7)、第九条第二款、第十条第一款规定，有下列行为之一的，国务院海洋主管部门可以撤销许可并撤回相关文件：  
（一）提交虚假材料取得许可的；  
（二）不履行勘探、开发合同义务或者履行合同义务不符合约定的；  
（三）未经同意，转让勘探、开发合同的权利、义务或者对勘探、开发合同作出重大变更的。  
承包者有前款第二项行为的，还应当承担相应的赔偿责任。

**Article 23** Where any of the following acts is committed in violation of Article 7, paragraph 2 of Article 9, or paragraph 1 of Article 10 of this Law, the marine administrative department of the State Council may revoke the approval and withdraw the relevant documents:  
(1) Obtaining approval by submitting false materials;  
(2) Failing to perform the obligations under the exploration or exploitation contract, or performing them not in conformity with the contract;  
(3) Assigning the rights and obligations under the exploration or exploitation contract or making significant modifications to the contract without consent.  
Where a contractor commits the act specified in subparagraph (2) of the preceding paragraph, it shall also bear corresponding liability for compensation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　违反[本法第八条](#art-8)第三款、第十条第二款、第十八条、第二十条、第二十二条规定，有下列行为之一的，由国务院海洋主管部门责令改正，处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未按规定将勘探、开发合同副本报备案的；  
（二）转让、变更或者终止勘探、开发合同，未按规定报备案的；  
（三）未按规定汇交有关资料副本、实物样本或者目录的；  
（四）未按规定报告履行勘探、开发合同事项的；  
（五）不协助、配合监督检查的。

**Article 24** Where any of the following acts is committed in violation of paragraph 3 of Article 8, paragraph 2 of Article 10, Article 18, Article 20, or Article 22 of this Law, the marine administrative department of the State Council shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan:  
(1) Failing to submit a copy of the exploration or exploitation contract for record as required;  
(2) Failing to report the assignment, modification, or termination of the exploration or exploitation contract for record as required;  
(3) Failing to submit copies of relevant data, physical samples, or catalogs as required;  
(4) Failing to report on the performance of the exploration or exploitation contract as required;  
(5) Failing to assist or cooperate with supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　违反[本法第八条](#art-8)第二款规定，未经许可或者未签订勘探、开发合同从事深海海底区域资源勘探、开发活动的，由国务院海洋主管部门责令停止违法行为，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，并处没收违法所得。

**Article 25** Where any person, in violation of paragraph 2 of Article 8 of this Law, engages in exploration or exploitation activities in the deep seabed area without approval or without having signed an exploration or exploitation contract, the marine administrative department of the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal act, impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; and if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated concurrently.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反[本法第九条](#art-9)第三款、第十一条、第十二条规定，造成海洋环境污染损害或者作业区域内文物、铺设物等损害的，由国务院海洋主管部门责令停止违法行为，处五十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 26** Where any person, in violation of paragraph 3 of Article 9, Article 11, or Article 12 of this Law, causes damage to the marine environment through pollution, or damage to cultural relics, installations, or other objects within the operation area, the marine administrative department of the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal act, impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; and if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本法下列用语的含义：  
（一）勘探，是指在深海海底区域探寻资源，分析资源，使用和测试资源采集系统和设备、加工设施及运输系统，以及对开发时应当考虑的环境、技术、经济、商业和其他有关因素的研究。  
（二）开发，是指在深海海底区域为商业目的收回并选取资源，包括建造和操作为生产和销售资源服务的采集、加工和运输系统。  
（三）资源调查，是指在深海海底区域搜寻资源，包括估计资源成分、多少和分布情况及经济价值。

**Article 27** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) "Exploration" means searching for resources in the deep seabed area, analyzing resources, using and testing resource collection systems and equipment, processing facilities, and transportation systems, and studying environmental, technical, economic, commercial, and other relevant factors to be considered for exploitation.  
(2) "Exploitation" means recovering and extracting resources in the deep seabed area for commercial purposes, including the construction and operation of collection, processing, and transportation systems for the production and sale of resources.  
(3) "Resource survey" means searching for resources in the deep seabed area, including estimating the composition, quantity, distribution, and economic value of resources.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　深海海底区域资源开发活动涉税事项，依照中华人民共和国税收法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 28** Tax matters related to development activities of resources in the deep seabed area shall be handled in accordance with the tax laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本法自2016年5月1日起施行。

**Article 29** This Law shall come into force as of May 1, 2016.
