# 中华人民共和国价格法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：1997-12-29  
**生效日期**：1998-05-01

> （1997年12月29日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十九次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　经营者的价格行为 第三章　政府的定价行为 第四章　价格总水平调控 第五章　价格监督检查 第六章　法律责任 第七章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范价格行为，发挥价格合理配置资源的作用，稳定市场价格总水平，保护消费者和经营者的合法权益，促进社会主义市场经济健康发展，制定本法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内发生的价格行为，适用本法。 本法所称价格包括商品价格和服务价格。 商品价格是指各类有形产品和无形资产的价格。 服务价格是指各类有偿服务的收费。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范价格行为，发挥价格合理配置资源的作用，稳定市场价格总水平，保护消费者和经营者的合法权益，促进社会主义市场经济健康发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of regulating pricing activities, enabling prices to function in the rational allocation of resources, stabilizing the general level of market prices, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of consumers and business operators, and promoting the healthy development of the socialist market economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内发生的价格行为，适用本法。  
本法所称价格包括商品价格和服务价格。  
商品价格是指各类有形产品和无形资产的价格。  
服务价格是指各类有偿服务的收费。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to pricing activities occurring within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
For the purposes of this Law, prices include commodity prices and service prices.  
Commodity prices refer to the prices of various types of tangible products and intangible assets.  
Service prices refer to the fees charged for various types of paid services.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家实行并逐步完善宏观经济调控下主要由市场形成价格的机制。价格的制定应当符合价值规律，大多数商品和服务价格实行市场调节价，极少数商品和服务价格实行政府指导价或者政府定价。  
市场调节价，是指由经营者自主制定，通过市场竞争形成的价格。  
本法所称经营者是指从事生产、经营商品或者提供有偿服务的法人、其他组织和个人。  
政府指导价，是指依照本法规定，由政府价格主管部门或者其他有关部门，按照定价权限和范围规定基准价及其浮动幅度，指导经营者制定的价格。  
政府定价，是指依照本法规定，由政府价格主管部门或者其他有关部门，按照定价权限和范围制定的价格。

**Article 3** The State shall implement and progressively improve a mechanism whereby prices are formed primarily by the market under macroeconomic control. Prices shall be set in accordance with the law of value. Market-adjusted prices shall apply to the prices of most commodities and services, while government-guided prices or government-fixed prices shall apply to the prices of a very small number of commodities and services. Market-adjusted prices refer to prices set autonomously by business operators and formed through market competition. For the purposes of this Law, business operators refer to legal persons, other organizations, and individuals that engage in the production or operation of commodities or the provision of paid services. Government-guided prices refer to prices guided and set by business operators in accordance with the provisions of this Law, based on base prices and their floating ranges prescribed by the competent departments for price administration or other relevant departments within their pricing authority and scope. Government-fixed prices refer to prices set by the competent departments for price administration or other relevant departments in accordance with the provisions of this Law within their pricing authority and scope.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家支持和促进公平、公开、合法的市场竞争，维护正常的价格秩序，对价格活动实行管理、监督和必要的调控。

**Article 4** The State shall support and promote fair, open, and lawful market competition, maintain normal pricing order, and exercise administration, supervision, and necessary regulation over pricing activities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院价格主管部门统一负责全国的价格工作。国务院其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内，负责有关的价格工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府价格主管部门负责本行政区域内的价格工作。县级以上地方各级人民政府其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内，负责有关的价格工作。

**Article 5** The competent department for price administration under the State Council shall be responsible for the pricing work of the entire country. Other relevant departments under the State Council shall be responsible for the relevant pricing work within their respective scopes of duties. The competent departments for price administration of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the pricing work within their respective administrative regions. Other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the relevant pricing work within their respective scopes of duties.

### 第二章　经营者的价格行为

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　商品价格和服务价格，除依照[本法第十八条](#art-18)规定适用政府指导价或者政府定价外，实行市场调节价，由经营者依照本法自主制定。

**Article 6** For commodity prices and service prices, except where government-guided prices or government-fixed prices apply in accordance with Article 18 of this Law, market-adjusted prices shall be in effect and shall be set autonomously by business operators in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　经营者定价，应当遵循公平、合法和诚实信用的原则。

**Article 7** When setting prices, business operators shall follow the principles of fairness, legality, and good faith.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　经营者定价的基本依据是生产经营成本和市场供求状况。

**Article 8** The basic basis for business operators to set prices shall be the production and operation costs and the market supply and demand situation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　经营者应当努力改进生产经营管理，降低生产经营成本，为消费者提供价格合理的商品和服务，并在市场竞争中获取合法利润。

**Article 9** Business operators shall strive to improve their production and operation management, reduce production and operation costs, provide consumers with commodities and services at reasonable prices, and obtain lawful profits through market competition.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　经营者应当根据其经营条件建立、健全内部价格管理制度，准确记录与核定商品和服务的生产经营成本，不得弄虚作假。

**Article 10** Business operators shall, based on their business conditions, establish and improve internal price management systems, accurately record and verify the production and operation costs of commodities and services, and shall not engage in fraudulent practices.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　经营者进行价格活动，享有下列权利：  
（一）自主制定属于市场调节的价格；  
（二）在政府指导价规定的幅度内制定价格；  
（三）制定属于政府指导价、政府定价产品范围内的新产品的试销价格，特定产品除外；  
（四）检举、控告侵犯其依法自主定价权利的行为。

**Article 11** When engaging in pricing activities, business operators shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) autonomously setting prices that fall within the scope of market-adjusted prices;  
(2) setting prices within the range prescribed by government-guided prices;  
(3) setting trial sale prices for new products that fall within the scope of products subject to government-guided prices or government-fixed prices, except for specific products; and (4) reporting or accusing acts that infringe upon their lawful right to set prices autonomously.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　经营者进行价格活动，应当遵守法律、法规，执行依法制定的政府指导价、政府定价和法定的价格干预措施、紧急措施。

**Article 12** When engaging in pricing activities, business operators shall comply with laws and regulations, implement government-guided prices and government-fixed prices formulated in accordance with the law, as well as statutory price intervention measures and emergency measures.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　经营者销售、收购商品和提供服务，应当按照政府价格主管部门的规定明码标价，注明商品的品名、产地、规格、等级、计价单位、价格或者服务的项目、收费标准等有关情况。  
经营者不得在标价之外加价出售商品，不得收取任何未予标明的费用。

**Article 13** When selling or purchasing commodities or providing services, business operators shall clearly mark the prices as prescribed by the competent departments for price administration, indicating the names of commodities, places of origin, specifications, grades, pricing units, prices, or the items of services, charging standards, and other relevant information.  
Business operators shall not sell commodities at prices higher than those marked, nor shall they charge any fees not explicitly stated.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　经营者不得有下列不正当价格行为：  
（一）相互串通，操纵市场价格，损害其他经营者或者消费者的合法权益；  
（二）在依法降价处理鲜活商品、季节性商品、积压商品等商品外，为了排挤竞争对手或者独占市场，以低于成本的价格倾销，扰乱正常的生产经营秩序，损害国家利益或者其他经营者的合法权益；  
（三）捏造、散布涨价信息，哄抬价格，推动商品价格过高上涨的；  
（四）利用虚假的或者使人误解的价格手段，诱骗消费者或者其他经营者与其进行交易；  
（五）提供相同商品或者服务，对具有同等交易条件的其他经营者实行价格歧视；  
（六）采取抬高等级或者压低等级等手段收购、销售商品或者提供服务，变相提高或者压低价格；  
（七）违反法律、法规的规定牟取暴利；  
（八）法律、行政法规禁止的其他不正当价格行为。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/价格违法行为行政处罚规定.md#art-4" title="被《价格违法行为行政处罚规定》第4条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/价格违法行为行政处罚规定.md#art-5" title="被《价格违法行为行政处罚规定》第5条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/价格违法行为行政处罚规定.md#art-6" title="被《价格违法行为行政处罚规定》第6条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/价格违法行为行政处罚规定.md#art-7" title="被《价格违法行为行政处罚规定》第7条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/价格违法行为行政处罚规定.md#art-8" title="被《价格违法行为行政处罚规定》第8条引用">[5]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/价格违法行为行政处罚规定.md#art-23" title="被《价格违法行为行政处罚规定》第23条引用">[6]</a></sup>

**Article 14** Business operators shall not engage in any of the following unfair pricing practices:  
(1) colluding with others to manipulate market prices and harm the legitimate rights and interests of other business operators or consumers;  
(2) except where commodities such as fresh goods, seasonal goods, and overstocked goods are sold at reduced prices lawfully, dumping commodities at prices below cost to squeeze out competitors or monopolize the market, thus disrupting normal production and operation order and harming the interests of the State or the legitimate rights and interests of other business operators;  
(3) fabricating or spreading information about price increases, driving up prices, and causing excessive price increases of commodities;  
(4) using false or misleading pricing methods to induce consumers or other business operators into transactions;  
(5) applying price discrimination to other business operators with the same transaction conditions when providing identical commodities or services;  
(6) adopting means such as raising or lowering grades to purchase or sell commodities or provide services, thus effectively raising or lowering prices;  
(7) seeking exorbitant profits in violation of laws and regulations  
(8) other unfair pricing practices prohibited by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　各类中介机构提供有偿服务收取费用，应当遵守本法的规定。法律另有规定的，按照有关规定执行。

**Article 15** When providing paid services and collecting fees, various types of intermediary agencies shall comply with the provisions of this Law. Where laws provide otherwise, the relevant provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　经营者销售进口商品、收购出口商品，应当遵守本章的有关规定，维护国内市场秩序。

**Article 16** When selling imported commodities or purchasing export commodities, business operators shall comply with the relevant provisions of this Chapter to maintain the order of the domestic market.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　行业组织应当遵守价格法律、法规，加强价格自律，接受政府价格主管部门的工作指导。

**Article 17** Trade organizations shall comply with pricing laws and regulations, strengthen price self-discipline, and accept the work guidance of the competent departments for price administration.

### 第三章　政府的定价行为

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　下列商品和服务价格，政府在必要时可以实行政府指导价或者政府定价：  
（一）与国民经济发展和人民生活关系重大的极少数商品价格；  
（二）资源稀缺的少数商品价格；  
（三）自然垄断经营的商品价格；  
（四）重要的公用事业价格；  
（五）重要的公益性服务价格。

**Article 18** For the prices of the following commodities and services, the government may, when necessary, implement government-guided prices or government-fixed prices:  
(1) the prices of a very small number of commodities which are of vital importance to the development of the national economy and the livelihood of the people;  
(2) the prices of a small number of commodities with scarce resources;  
(3) the prices of commodities subject to natural monopoly operations;  
(4) the prices of important public utilities; and (5) the prices of important public welfare services.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　政府指导价、政府定价的定价权限和具体适用范围，以中央的和地方的定价目录为依据。  
中央定价目录由国务院价格主管部门制定、修订，报国务院批准后公布。  
地方定价目录由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府价格主管部门按照中央定价目录规定的定价权限和具体适用范围制定，经本级人民政府审核同意，报国务院价格主管部门审定后公布。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府以下各级地方人民政府不得制定定价目录。

**Article 19** The pricing authority and specific scope of application for government-guided prices and government-fixed prices shall be based on the central and local pricing catalogs.  
The central pricing catalog shall be formulated and revised by the competent department for price administration under the State Council, and shall be published after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.  
Local pricing catalogs shall be formulated by the competent departments for price administration of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government in accordance with the pricing authority and specific scope of application prescribed in the central pricing catalog, and shall be published after being reviewed and approved by the people's government at the same level and submitted to the competent department for price administration under the State Council for examination and approval.  
No local people's government below the level of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate a pricing catalog.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院价格主管部门和其他有关部门，按照中央定价目录规定的定价权限和具体适用范围制定政府指导价、政府定价；其中重要的商品和服务价格的政府指导价、政府定价，应当按照规定经国务院批准。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府价格主管部门和其他有关部门，应当按照地方定价目录规定的定价权限和具体适用范围制定在本地区执行的政府指导价、政府定价。  
市、县人民政府可以根据省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的授权，按照地方定价目录规定的定价权限和具体适用范围制定在本地区执行的政府指导价、政府定价。

**Article 20** The competent department for price administration under the State Council and other relevant departments shall formulate government-guided prices and government-fixed prices in accordance with the pricing authority and specific scope of application prescribed in the central pricing catalog; among these, the government-guided prices and government-fixed prices for prices of important commodities and services shall be submitted to the State Council for approval as prescribed.  
The competent departments for price administration of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and other relevant departments shall formulate government-guided prices and government-fixed prices to be implemented in their respective regions in accordance with the pricing authority and specific scope of application prescribed in the local pricing catalog.  
The people's governments of cities and counties may, subject to the authorization of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, formulate government-guided prices and government-fixed prices to be implemented in their respective regions in accordance with the pricing authority and specific scope of application prescribed in the local pricing catalog.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　制定政府指导价、政府定价，应当依据有关商品或者服务的社会平均成本和市场供求状况、国民经济与社会发展要求以及社会承受能力，实行合理的购销差价、批零差价、地区差价和季节差价。

**Article 21** When formulating government-guided prices or government-set prices, the relevant social average cost of the relevant commodity or service, the market supply and demand situation, the requirements of national economic and social development, and the social affordability shall be taken as the basis, and reasonable purchasing and selling price differentials, wholesale and retail price differentials, regional price differentials, and seasonal price differentials shall be implemented.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　政府价格主管部门和其他有关部门制定政府指导价、政府定价，应当开展价格、成本调查，听取消费者、经营者和有关方面的意见。  
政府价格主管部门开展对政府指导价、政府定价的价格、成本调查时，有关单位应当如实反映情况，提供必需的帐簿、文件以及其他资料。

**Article 22** The government price administrative department and other relevant departments, when formulating government-guided prices or government-set prices, shall conduct price and cost investigations and solicit opinions from consumers, business operators, and relevant parties.  
When the government price administrative department conducts price and cost investigations on government-guided prices or government-set prices, the relevant units shall truthfully reflect the situation and provide the necessary account books, documents, and other materials.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　制定关系群众切身利益的公用事业价格、公益性服务价格、自然垄断经营的商品价格等政府指导价、政府定价，应当建立听证会制度，由政府价格主管部门主持，征求消费者、经营者和有关方面的意见，论证其必要性、可行性。

**Article 23** When formulating government-guided prices or government-set prices for public utility prices, prices for public welfare services, and prices for commodities under natural monopoly operations that concern the immediate interests of the public, a hearing system shall be established, presided over by the government price administrative department, to solicit opinions from consumers, business operators, and relevant parties, and to demonstrate their necessity and feasibility.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　政府指导价、政府定价制定后，由制定价格的部门向消费者、经营者公布。

**Article 24** After government-guided prices or government-set prices are formulated, the department that formulates the prices shall announce them to consumers and business operators.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　政府指导价、政府定价的具体适用范围、价格水平，应当根据经济运行情况，按照规定的定价权限和程序适时调整。  
消费者、经营者可以对政府指导价、政府定价提出调整建议。

**Article 25** The specific scope of application and price levels of government-guided prices and government-set prices shall be adjusted in a timely manner based on the situation of economic operation, in accordance with the prescribed pricing authority and procedures.  
Consumers and business operators may propose adjustments to government-guided prices or government-set prices.

### 第四章　价格总水平调控

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　稳定市场价格总水平是国家重要的宏观经济政策目标。国家根据国民经济发展的需要和社会承受能力，确定市场价格总水平调控目标，列入国民经济和社会发展计划，并综合运用货币、财政、投资、进出口等方面的政策和措施，予以实现。

**Article 26** Stabilizing the general level of market prices is an important macroeconomic policy objective of the State. The State shall, based on the needs of national economic development and social affordability, determine the control targets for the general level of market prices, include them in the national economic and social development plan, and achieve them through the comprehensive use of policies and measures in the areas of currency, finance, investment, and imports and exports.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　政府可以建立重要商品储备制度，设立价格调节基金，调控价格，稳定市场。

**Article 27** The government may establish a reserve system for important commodities and set up price adjustment funds to regulate prices and stabilize the market.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　为适应价格调控和管理的需要，政府价格主管部门应当建立价格监测制度，对重要商品、服务价格的变动进行监测。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国药品管理法实施条例.md#art-47" title="被《中华人民共和国药品管理法实施条例》第47条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 28** To meet the needs of price regulation and administration, the government price administrative department shall establish a price monitoring system to monitor changes in the prices of important commodities and services.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　政府在粮食等重要农产品的市场购买价格过低时，可以在收购中实行保护价格，并采取相应的经济措施保证其实现。

**Article 29** When the market purchase price for grain or other important agricultural products is too low, the government may implement a protective price in procurement and adopt corresponding economic measures to ensure its realization.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　当重要商品和服务价格显著上涨或者有可能显著上涨，国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以对部分价格采取限定差价率或者利润率、规定限价、实行提价申报制度和调价备案制度等干预措施。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府采取前款规定的干预措施，应当报国务院备案。

**Article 30** When the prices of important commodities and services rise significantly or are likely to rise significantly, the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may take intervention measures for some prices, such as limiting the price differential ratio or profit margin, prescribing price ceilings, and implementing a system of filing for price increases and a system of reporting price adjustments for record.  
People's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government that take the intervention measures prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall report them to the State Council for the record.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　当市场价格总水平出现剧烈波动等异常状态时，国务院可以在全国范围内或者部分区域内采取临时集中定价权限、部分或者全面冻结价格的紧急措施。

**Article 31** When the general level of market prices is in an abnormal state such as violent fluctuations, the State Council may take emergency measures, such as temporarily centralizing pricing authority or partially or fully freezing prices, within the whole country or in certain regions.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　依照[本法第三十条](#art-30)、第三十一条的规定实行干预措施、紧急措施的情形消除后，应当及时解除干预措施、紧急措施。

**Article 32** After the circumstances justifying the implementation of intervention measures or emergency measures in accordance with the provisions of Articles 30 and 31 of this Law have been eliminated, such intervention measures or emergency measures shall be promptly lifted.

### 第五章　价格监督检查

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级以上各级人民政府价格主管部门，依法对价格活动进行监督检查，并依照本法的规定对价格违法行为实施行政处罚。

**Article 33** The government price administrative departments at or above the county level shall, according to law, supervise and inspect price activities, and shall impose administrative penalties for illegal pricing acts in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　政府价格主管部门进行价格监督检查时，可以行使下列职权；  
（一）询问当事人或者有关人员，并要求其提供证明材料和与价格违法行为有关的其他资料；  
（二）查询、复制与价格违法行为有关的帐簿、单据、凭证、文件及其他资料，核对与价格违法行为有关的银行资料；  
（三）检查与价格违法行为有关的财物，必要时可以责令当事人暂停相关营业；  
（四）在证据可能灭失或者以后难以取得的情况下，可以依法先行登记保存，当事人或者有关人员不得转移、隐匿或者销毁。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/价格违法行为行政处罚规定.md#art-15" title="被《价格违法行为行政处罚规定》第15条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 34** When conducting price supervision and inspection, the government price administrative department may exercise the following powers:  
(1) to question the parties or relevant personnel and require them to provide evidentiary materials and other information related to the illegal pricing act;  
(2) to inquire into and copy account books, bills, vouchers, documents, and other materials related to the illegal pricing act, and to verify bank materials related to the illegal pricing act;  
(3) to inspect property and goods related to the illegal pricing act, and when necessary, to order the parties to suspend the relevant business;  
(4) where evidence may be destroyed or may be difficult to obtain later, to register and preserve such evidence in advance according to law, and the parties or relevant personnel shall not transfer, conceal, or destroy it.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　经营者接受政府价格主管部门的监督检查时，应当如实提供价格监督检查所必需的帐簿、单据、凭证、文件以及其他资料。

**Article 35** When a business operator accepts supervision and inspection by the government price administrative department, it shall truthfully provide the account books, bills, vouchers, documents, and other materials necessary for price supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　政府部门价格工作人员不得将依法取得的资料或者了解的情况用于依法进行价格管理以外的任何其他目的，不得泄露当事人的商业秘密。

**Article 36** Price working personnel of government departments shall not use materials obtained or information learned according to law for any purpose other than the lawful administration of prices, and shall not disclose the business secrets of the parties.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　消费者组织、职工价格监督组织、居民委员会、村民委员会等组织以及消费者，有权对价格行为进行社会监督。政府价格主管部门应当充分发挥群众的价格监督作用。  
新闻单位有权进行价格舆论监督。

**Article 37** Consumer organizations, employee price supervision organizations, residents' committees, villagers' committees, and other organizations, as well as consumers, have the right to exercise social supervision over pricing conduct. The government price administrative department shall give full play to the role of the masses in price supervision. News media units have the right to exercise public opinion supervision over prices.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　政府价格主管部门应当建立对价格违法行为的举报制度。  
任何单位和个人均有权对价格违法行为进行举报。政府价格主管部门应当对举报者给予鼓励，并负责为举报者保密。

**Article 38** The government price administrative department shall establish a reporting system for illegal pricing acts. Any unit or individual has the right to report illegal pricing acts. The government price administrative department shall encourage the informants and be responsible for keeping their identities confidential.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　经营者不执行政府指导价、政府定价以及法定的价格干预措施、紧急措施的，责令改正，没收违法所得，可以并处违法所得五倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，可以处以罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿。

**Article 39** If a business operator fails to implement government-guided prices, government-set prices, or the statutory price intervention measures or emergency measures, it shall be ordered to make corrections, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it may be concurrently fined not more than five times the amount of the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, a fine may be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　经营者有[本法第十四条](#art-14)所列行为之一的，责令改正，没收违法所得，可以并处违法所得五倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，予以警告，可以并处罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，或者由工商行政管理机关吊销营业执照。有关法律对[本法第十四条](#art-14)所列行为的处罚及处罚机关另有规定的，可以依照有关法律的规定执行。  
有[本法第十四条](#art-14)第（一）项、第（二）项所列行为，属于是全国性的，由国务院价格主管部门认定；属于是省及省以下区域性的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府价格主管部门认定。

**Article 40** If a business operator commits any of the acts listed in Article 14 of this Law, it shall be ordered to make corrections, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it may be concurrently fined not more than five times the amount of the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, a warning shall be given, and a fine may be concurrently imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, or its business license shall be revoked by the administrative authority for industry and commerce. If another relevant law provides otherwise for the penalties and the organs imposing penalties for the acts listed in Article 14 of this Law, the provisions of that relevant law may be followed. If the acts listed in subparagraphs (1) and (2) of Article 14 of this Law are national in scope, they shall be determined by the price administrative department of the State Council; if they are regional at the provincial level or below, they shall be determined by the price administrative department of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　经营者因价格违法行为致使消费者或者其他经营者多付价款的，应当退还多付部分；造成损害的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/价格违法行为行政处罚规定.md#art-16" title="被《价格违法行为行政处罚规定》第16条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 41** : Where any business operator causes a consumer or another business operator to pay an excessive amount due to a price-related illegal act, the business operator shall refund the excessive portion; where damages are caused, the business operator shall bear compensation liability in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　经营者违反明码标价规定的，责令改正，没收违法所得，可以并处五千元以下的罚款。

**Article 42** : Where any business operator violates the provisions on clearly marked prices, the business operator shall be ordered to make corrections, and the illegal gains shall be confiscated; concurrently, a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan may be imposed.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　经营者被责令暂停相关营业而不停止的，或者转移、隐匿、销毁依法登记保存的财物的，处相关营业所得或者转移、隐匿、销毁的财物价值一倍以上三倍以下的罚款。

**Article 43** : Where any business operator ordered to suspend relevant business operations fails to do so, or transfers, conceals, or destroys property legally registered and preserved, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the income from the relevant business operations or the value of the property transferred, concealed, or destroyed shall be imposed.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　拒绝按照规定提供监督检查所需资料或者提供虚假资料的，责令改正，予以警告；逾期不改正的，可以处以罚款。

**Article 44** : Where any person refuses to provide materials required for supervision and inspection in accordance with the provisions or provides false materials, the person shall be ordered to make corrections and be given a warning; where the person fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine may be imposed.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　地方各级人民政府或者各级人民政府有关部门违反本法规定，超越定价权限和范围擅自制定、调整价格或者不执行法定的价格干预措施、紧急措施的，责令改正，并可以通报批评；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 45** : Where any local people's government or relevant department of a people's government at any level, in violation of the provisions of this Law, exceeds the pricing authority and scope to formulate or adjust prices without authorization, or fails to implement the statutory price intervention measures or emergency measures, the government or department shall be ordered to make corrections and may be criticized in a circular; the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　价格工作人员泄露国家秘密、商业秘密以及滥用职权、徇私舞弊、玩忽职守、索贿受贿，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 46** : Where any price officer divulges state secrets or trade secrets, or abuses power, engages in malpractices for personal gain, neglects duties, or solicits or accepts bribes, and such act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the law; if the act does not constitute a crime, sanctions shall be given in accordance with the law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家行政机关的收费，应当依法进行，严格控制收费项目，限定收费范围、标准。收费的具体管理办法由国务院另行制定。  
利率、汇率、保险费率、证券及期货价格，适用有关法律、行政法规的规定，不适用本法。

**Article 47** : Fees charged by state administrative organs shall be charged in accordance with the law, with charge items strictly controlled and the scope and standards of charges limited. The specific administrative measures for charges shall be separately formulated by the State Council. Interest rates, exchange rates, insurance premium rates, and securities and futures prices shall be governed by the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, and shall not be subject to this Law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本法自1998年5月1日起施行。

**Article 48** : This Law shall come into force on May 1, 1998.
