### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对建筑活动的监督管理，维护建筑市场秩序，保证建筑工程的质量和安全，促进建筑业健康发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of strengthening supervision and administration of construction activities, maintaining order in the construction market, ensuring the quality and safety of construction projects, and promoting the healthy development of the construction industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事建筑活动，实施对建筑活动的监督管理，应当遵守本法。  
本法所称建筑活动，是指各类房屋建筑及其附属设施的建造和与其配套的线路、管道、设备的安装活动。

**Article 2** Any person engaging in construction activities and exercising supervision and administration over construction activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall comply with this Law.  
For the purpose of this Law, "construction activities" mean the construction of various types of buildings and their auxiliary facilities, and the installation of lines, pipelines, and equipment supporting such buildings.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　建筑活动应当确保建筑工程质量和安全，符合国家的建筑工程安全标准。

**Article 3** Construction activities shall ensure the quality and safety of construction projects and comply with the national safety standards for construction projects.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家扶持建筑业的发展，支持建筑科学技术研究，提高房屋建筑设计水平，鼓励节约能源和保护环境，提倡采用先进技术、先进设备、先进工艺、新型建筑材料和现代管理方式。

**Article 4** The State supports the development of the construction industry, encourages scientific and technological research in construction, improves the design level of buildings, promotes energy conservation and environmental protection, and advocates the adoption of advanced technologies, advanced equipment, advanced techniques, new building materials, and modern management methods.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　从事建筑活动应当遵守法律、法规，不得损害社会公共利益和他人的合法权益。  
任何单位和个人都不得妨碍和阻挠依法进行的建筑活动。

**Article 5** Any person engaging in construction activities shall comply with laws and regulations and shall not harm the public interest or the lawful rights and interests of others.  
No entity or individual may obstruct or impede construction activities carried out according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院建设行政主管部门对全国的建筑活动实施统一监督管理。

**Article 6** The administrative department of construction under the State Council shall exercise unified supervision and administration over construction activities nationwide.

### 第二章　建筑许可

#### 第一节　建筑工程施工许可

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　建筑工程开工前，建设单位应当按照国家有关规定向工程所在地县级以上人民政府建设行政主管部门申请领取施工许可证；但是，国务院建设行政主管部门确定的限额以下的小型工程除外。  
按照国务院规定的权限和程序批准开工报告的建筑工程，不再领取施工许可证。

**Article 7** Before commencing a construction project, the project owner shall apply to the administrative department of construction of the people's government at or above the county level in the place where the project is located for a construction permit in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; except for small-scale projects below the threshold determined by the administrative department of construction under the State Council.  
For a construction project for which a construction commencement report has been approved in accordance with the limits of authority and procedures prescribed by the State Council, no construction permit shall be required.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请领取施工许可证，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）已经办理该建筑工程用地批准手续；  
（二）依法应当办理建设工程规划许可证的，已经取得建设工程规划许可证；  
（三）需要拆迁的，其拆迁进度符合施工要求；  
（四）已经确定建筑施工企业；  
（五）有满足施工需要的资金安排、施工图纸及技术资料；  
（六）有保证工程质量和安全的具体措施。  
建设行政主管部门应当自收到申请之日起七日内，对符合条件的申请颁发施工许可证。

**Article 8** To apply for a construction permit, the following conditions shall be satisfied:  
(1) The approval procedures for the use of the land for the construction project have been completed;  
(2) If a construction project planning permit is required according to law, such permit has been obtained;  
(3) If demolition is necessary, the demolition progress meets the construction requirements;  
(4) The construction enterprise has been determined;  
(5) There are financial arrangements, construction drawings, and technical data meeting the construction needs;  
(6) There are specific measures to ensure project quality and safety.  
The administrative department of construction shall, within seven days from the date of receipt of the application, issue a construction permit for applications that meet the conditions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　建设单位应当自领取施工许可证之日起三个月内开工。因故不能按期开工的，应当向发证机关申请延期；延期以两次为限，每次不超过三个月。既不开工又不申请延期或者超过延期时限的，施工许可证自行废止。

**Article 9** The project owner shall commence construction within three months from the date of obtaining the construction permit. If it is unable to commence on schedule due to reasons, it shall apply to the authority that issued the permit for an extension; an extension shall be limited to two times, each time not exceeding three months. If construction is not commenced and no extension is applied for, or if the extension period expires, the construction permit shall automatically lapse.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在建的建筑工程因故中止施工的，建设单位应当自中止施工之日起一个月内，向发证机关报告，并按照规定做好建筑工程的维护管理工作。  
建筑工程恢复施工时，应当向发证机关报告；中止施工满一年的工程恢复施工前，建设单位应当报发证机关核验施工许可证。

**Article 10** If a construction project under construction is suspended for any reason, the project owner shall, within one month from the date of suspension, report to the authority that issued the permit and properly maintain and manage the construction project in accordance with the relevant provisions.  
When the construction project is resumed, the project owner shall report to the authority that issued the permit; for a project that has been suspended for one year, the project owner shall, before resuming construction, report to the authority that issued the permit for verification of the construction permit.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　按照国务院有关规定批准开工报告的建筑工程，因故不能按期开工或者中止施工的，应当及时向批准机关报告情况。因故不能按期开工超过六个月的，应当重新办理开工报告的批准手续。

**Article 11** For a construction project for which a construction commencement report has been approved in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council, if it is unable to commence on schedule or is suspended for any reason, the project owner shall promptly report the circumstances to the approving authority. If it is unable to commence on schedule for more than six months, the approval procedures for the construction commencement report shall be carried out anew.

#### 第二节　从业资格

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　从事建筑活动的建筑施工企业、勘察单位、设计单位和工程监理单位，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有符合国家规定的注册资本；  
（二）有与其从事的建筑活动相适应的具有法定执业资格的专业技术人员；  
（三）有从事相关建筑活动所应有的技术装备；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 12** Construction enterprises, survey units, design units, and project supervision units engaged in construction activities shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) They shall have registered capital that meets the requirements of the State;  
(2) They shall have professional and technical personnel with statutory practice qualifications suitable for the construction activities they engage in;  
(3) They shall have the technical equipment necessary for engaging in the relevant construction activities;  
(4) Other conditions as provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　从事建筑活动的建筑施工企业、勘察单位、设计单位和工程监理单位，按照其拥有的注册资本、专业技术人员、技术装备和已完成的建筑工程业绩等资质条件，划分为不同的资质等级，经资质审查合格，取得相应等级的资质证书后，方可在其资质等级许可的范围内从事建筑活动。

**Article 13** Construction enterprises, survey units, design units, and project supervision units engaged in construction activities shall be classified into different qualification grades based on their registered capital, professional and technical personnel, technical equipment, and completed construction project performance. Only after passing qualification review and obtaining a qualification certificate of the corresponding grade may they engage in construction activities within the scope permitted by their qualification grade.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　从事建筑活动的专业技术人员，应当依法取得相应的执业资格证书，并在执业资格证书许可的范围内从事建筑活动。

**Article 14** Professional and technical personnel engaged in construction activities shall obtain corresponding practice qualification certificates according to law and shall engage in construction activities within the scope permitted by such certificates.

### 第三章　建筑工程发包与承包

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　建筑工程的发包单位与承包单位应当依法订立书面合同，明确双方的权利和义务。  
发包单位和承包单位应当全面履行合同约定的义务。不按照合同约定履行义务的，依法承担违约责任。

**Article 15** The awarding party and the contractor of a construction project shall enter into a written contract according to law, specifying the rights and obligations of both parties.  
The awarding party and the contractor shall fully perform the obligations stipulated in the contract. Any party that fails to perform its obligations as stipulated in the contract shall bear liability for breach of contract according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　建筑工程发包与承包的招标投标活动，应当遵循公开、公正、平等竞争的原则，择优选择承包单位。  
建筑工程的招标投标，本法没有规定的，适用有关招标投标法律的规定。

**Article 16** Tendering and bidding activities for the awarding and contracting of construction projects shall follow the principles of openness, fairness, and equal competition, and select the best contractor.  
For matters concerning tendering and bidding of construction projects not provided for in this Law, the provisions of the relevant laws on tendering and bidding shall apply.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　发包单位及其工作人员在建筑工程发包中不得收受贿赂、回扣或者索取其他好处。  
承包单位及其工作人员不得利用向发包单位及其工作人员行贿、提供回扣或者给予其他好处等不正当手段承揽工程。

**Article 17** The awarding party and its staff members shall not accept bribes, kickbacks, or seek other benefits in the awarding of construction projects.  
The contractor and its staff members shall not undertake projects by using improper means such as offering bribes, giving kickbacks, or providing other benefits to the awarding party or its staff members.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　建筑工程造价应当按照国家有关规定，由发包单位与承包单位在合同中约定。公开招标发包的，其造价的约定，须遵守招标投标法律的规定。  
发包单位应当按照合同的约定，及时拨付工程款项。

**Article 18** The cost of a construction project shall be agreed upon in the contract between the awarding party and the contractor in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. For projects awarded through open tender, the agreement on the cost shall comply with the provisions of the law on tendering and bidding.  
The awarding party shall timely disburse the project funds in accordance with the contract.

#### 第二节　发包

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　建筑工程依法实行招标发包，对不适于招标发包的可以直接发包。

**Article 19** Construction projects shall be awarded through tender according to law; for those not suitable for tender award, they may be awarded directly.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　建筑工程实行公开招标的，发包单位应当依照法定程序和方式，发布招标公告，提供载有招标工程的主要技术要求、主要的合同条款、评标的标准和方法以及开标、评标、定标的程序等内容的招标文件。  
开标应当在招标文件规定的时间、地点公开进行。开标后应当按照招标文件规定的评标标准和程序对标书进行评价、比较，在具备相应资质条件的投标者中，择优选定中标者。

**Article 20** Where a construction project is subject to open tender, the awarding party shall, in accordance with the statutory procedures and methods, publish a tender notice and provide tender documents containing the main technical requirements for the project, the main contract terms, the evaluation criteria and methods, and the procedures for bid opening, evaluation, and award.  
The bid opening shall be conducted publicly at the time and place specified in the tender documents. After bid opening, the bids shall be evaluated and compared in accordance with the evaluation criteria and procedures specified in the tender documents, and the successful bidder shall be selected from among the bidders with appropriate qualifications.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　建筑工程招标的开标、评标、定标由建设单位依法组织实施，并接受有关行政主管部门的监督。

**Article 21** The opening, evaluation, and award of bids for a construction project shall be organized and implemented by the project owner according to law, and shall be subject to supervision by the relevant administrative departments.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　建筑工程实行招标发包的，发包单位应当将建筑工程发包给依法中标的承包单位。建筑工程实行直接发包的，发包单位应当将建筑工程发包给具有相应资质条件的承包单位。

**Article 22** Where a construction project is awarded through tender, the awarding party shall award the construction project to the contractor that has legally won the tender. Where a construction project is awarded directly, the awarding party shall award the construction project to a contractor that meets the corresponding qualification conditions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　政府及其所属部门不得滥用行政权力，限定发包单位将招标发包的建筑工程发包给指定的承包单位。

**Article 23** The government and its subordinate departments shall not abuse their administrative powers to restrict the awarding party from awarding construction projects subject to tender to designated contractors.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　提倡对建筑工程实行总承包，禁止将建筑工程肢解发包。  
建筑工程的发包单位可以将建筑工程的勘察、设计、施工、设备采购一并发包给一个工程总承包单位，也可以将建筑工程勘察、设计、施工、设备采购的一项或者多项发包给一个工程总承包单位；但是，不得将应当由一个承包单位完成的建筑工程肢解成若干部分发包给几个承包单位。

**Article 24** Comprehensive contracting of construction projects is encouraged, and the segmentation of construction projects for separate awarding is prohibited.  
The awarding party of a construction project may award the survey, design, construction, and equipment procurement of the project all together to a single general contractor, or may award one or more of these items to a single general contractor; however, it shall not divide a construction project that should be completed by one contractor into several parts and award them to several contractors.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　按照合同约定，建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备由工程承包单位采购的，发包单位不得指定承包单位购入用于工程的建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备或者指定生产厂、供应商。

**Article 25** Where the contractor purchases building materials, building components, and equipment in accordance with the contract, the awarding party may not designate the contractor to purchase building materials, building components, and equipment for the project, nor may it designate manufacturers or suppliers.

#### 第三节　承包

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　承包建筑工程的单位应当持有依法取得的资质证书，并在其资质等级许可的业务范围内承揽工程。  
禁止建筑施工企业超越本企业资质等级许可的业务范围或者以任何形式用其他建筑施工企业的名义承揽工程。禁止建筑施工企业以任何形式允许其他单位或者个人使用本企业的资质证书、营业执照，以本企业的名义承揽工程。

**Article 26** A unit contracting a construction project shall hold a qualification certificate obtained according to law and undertake projects within the business scope permitted by its qualification grade.  
A construction enterprise is prohibited from undertaking projects beyond the business scope permitted by its qualification grade or from using the name of another construction enterprise in any form. A construction enterprise is also prohibited from allowing any other unit or individual to use its qualification certificate or business license in any form to undertake projects in its name.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　大型建筑工程或者结构复杂的建筑工程，可以由两个以上的承包单位联合共同承包。共同承包的各方对承包合同的履行承担连带责任。  
两个以上不同资质等级的单位实行联合共同承包的，应当按照资质等级低的单位的业务许可范围承揽工程。

**Article 27** Large-scale construction projects or construction projects with complex structures may be jointly contracted by two or more contractors. All parties to a joint contract shall bear joint and several liability for the performance of the contract.  
Where two or more units of different qualification grades engage in joint contracting, they shall undertake the project within the business scope permitted by the unit with the lower qualification grade.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　禁止承包单位将其承包的全部建筑工程转包给他人，禁止承包单位将其承包的全部建筑工程肢解以后以分包的名义分别转包给他人。

**Article 28** A contractor is prohibited from subcontracting the entire construction project it has contracted to others, and is prohibited from dividing the entire construction project it has contracted into parts and subcontracting them to others under the guise of partial subcontracting.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　建筑工程总承包单位可以将承包工程中的部分工程发包给具有相应资质条件的分包单位；但是，除总承包合同中约定的分包外，必须经建设单位认可。施工总承包的，建筑工程主体结构的施工必须由总承包单位自行完成。  
建筑工程总承包单位按照总承包合同的约定对建设单位负责；分包单位按照分包合同的约定对总承包单位负责。总承包单位和分包单位就分包工程对建设单位承担连带责任。  
禁止总承包单位将工程分包给不具备相应资质条件的单位。禁止分包单位将其承包的工程再分包。

**Article 29** A general contractor of a construction project may award part of the contracted project to subcontractors meeting the corresponding qualification conditions; however, except for subcontracting provided for in the general contract, it must obtain the consent of the project owner. Where a general construction contract is adopted, the construction of the main structure of the construction project must be completed by the general contractor itself.  
The general contractor of a construction project is responsible to the project owner in accordance with the general contract; the subcontractor is responsible to the general contractor in accordance with the subcontract. The general contractor and the subcontractor shall bear joint and several liability to the project owner for the subcontracted project.  
A general contractor is prohibited from subcontracting a project to a unit that does not meet the corresponding qualification conditions. A subcontractor is prohibited from further subcontracting the project it has contracted.

### 第四章　建筑工程监理

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家推行建筑工程监理制度。  
国务院可以规定实行强制监理的建筑工程的范围。

**Article 30** The State promotes the system of project supervision for construction projects.  
The State Council may prescribe the scope of construction projects for which compulsory supervision shall be implemented.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　实行监理的建筑工程，由建设单位委托具有相应资质条件的工程监理单位监理。建设单位与其委托的工程监理单位应当订立书面委托监理合同。

**Article 31** For a construction project subject to supervision, the project owner shall entrust a project supervision unit meeting the corresponding qualification conditions to carry out supervision. The project owner and the entrusted project supervision unit shall enter into a written entrustment contract for supervision.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　建筑工程监理应当依照法律、行政法规及有关的技术标准、设计文件和建筑工程承包合同，对承包单位在施工质量、建设工期和建设资金使用等方面，代表建设单位实施监督。  
工程监理人员认为工程施工不符合工程设计要求、施工技术标准和合同约定的，有权要求建筑施工企业改正。  
工程监理人员发现工程设计不符合建筑工程质量标准或者合同约定的质量要求的，应当报告建设单位要求设计单位改正。

**Article 32** Project supervision of a construction project shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, relevant technical standards, design documents, and the construction contract, exercise supervision on behalf of the project owner over the contractor's construction quality, construction period, and use of construction funds.  
If a project supervisor considers that the construction does not conform to the design requirements, construction technical standards, or the contract, he or she shall have the right to require the construction enterprise to make corrections.  
If a project supervisor discovers that the project design does not conform to the quality standards for construction projects or the quality requirements stipulated in the contract, he or she shall report to the project owner and require the design unit to make corrections.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　实施建筑工程监理前，建设单位应当将委托的工程监理单位、监理的内容及监理权限，书面通知被监理的建筑施工企业。

**Article 33** Before implementing project supervision for a construction project, the project owner shall notify the supervised construction enterprise in writing of the entrusted project supervision unit, the content of supervision, and the scope of supervisory authority.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　工程监理单位应当在其资质等级许可的监理范围内，承担工程监理业务。  
工程监理单位应当根据建设单位的委托，客观、公正地执行监理任务。  
工程监理单位与被监理工程的承包单位以及建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备供应单位不得有隶属关系或者其他利害关系。  
工程监理单位不得转让工程监理业务。

**Article 34** A project supervision unit shall undertake project supervision business within the scope of supervision permitted by its qualification grade.  
A project supervision unit shall perform its supervision duties objectively and impartially in accordance with the entrustment from the project owner.  
A project supervision unit may not have a subordinate relationship or any other interest relationship with the contractor of the supervised project or with the suppliers of building materials, building components, and equipment.  
A project supervision unit may not transfer its project supervision business.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　工程监理单位不按照委托监理合同的约定履行监理义务，对应当监督检查的项目不检查或者不按照规定检查，给建设单位造成损失的，应当承担相应的赔偿责任。  
工程监理单位与承包单位串通，为承包单位谋取非法利益，给建设单位造成损失的，应当与承包单位承担连带赔偿责任。

**Article 35** If a project supervision unit fails to perform its supervision duties in accordance with the entrustment contract for supervision, fails to inspect items that should be supervised or does not inspect in accordance with the provisions, thereby causing losses to the project owner, it shall bear corresponding compensation liability.  
If a project supervision unit colludes with a contractor to seek illegal benefits for the contractor, thereby causing losses to the project owner, it shall bear joint and several compensation liability with the contractor.

### 第五章　建筑安全生产管理

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　建筑工程安全生产管理必须坚持安全第一、预防为主的方针，建立健全安全生产的责任制度和群防群治制度。

**Article 36** The safety production management of construction projects must adhere to the policy of safety first and prevention primarily, and establish and improve the responsibility system for safety production and the system of mass prevention and mass control.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　建筑工程设计应当符合按照国家规定制定的建筑安全规程和技术规范，保证工程的安全性能。

**Article 37** The design of construction projects shall comply with the building safety procedures and technical specifications formulated in accordance with the provisions of the State, and shall ensure the safety performance of the project.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　建筑施工企业在编制施工组织设计时，应当根据建筑工程的特点制定相应的安全技术措施；对专业性较强的工程项目，应当编制专项安全施工组织设计，并采取安全技术措施。

**Article 38** When formulating a construction organization design, a construction enterprise shall, based on the characteristics of the construction project, formulate corresponding safety technical measures; for highly specialized engineering projects, a special safety construction organization design shall be formulated, and safety technical measures shall be adopted.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　建筑施工企业应当在施工现场采取维护安全、防范危险、预防火灾等措施；有条件的，应当对施工现场实行封闭管理。  
施工现场对毗邻的建筑物、构筑物和特殊作业环境可能造成损害的，建筑施工企业应当采取安全防护措施。

**Article 39** A construction enterprise shall take measures on the construction site to maintain safety, prevent dangers, and protect against fire; where conditions permit, it shall implement enclosed management of the construction site.  
If the construction site may cause damage to adjacent buildings, structures, or special working environments, the construction enterprise shall take safety protection measures.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　建设单位应当向建筑施工企业提供与施工现场相关的地下管线资料，建筑施工企业应当采取措施加以保护。

**Article 40** The project owner shall provide the construction enterprise with information on underground pipelines related to the construction site, and the construction enterprise shall take measures to protect them.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　建筑施工企业应当遵守有关环境保护和安全生产的法律、法规的规定，采取控制和处理施工现场的各种粉尘、废气、废水、固体废物以及噪声、振动对环境的污染和危害的措施。

**Article 41** A construction enterprise shall comply with the provisions of laws and regulations on environmental protection and safety production, and shall take measures to control and treat the pollution and hazards caused by various dusts, exhaust gases, waste water, solid wastes, as well as noise and vibration on the construction site to the environment.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　有下列情形之一的，建设单位应当按照国家有关规定办理申请批准手续：  
（一）需要临时占用规划批准范围以外场地的；  
（二）可能损坏道路、管线、电力、邮电通讯等公共设施的；  
（三）需要临时停水、停电、中断道路交通的；  
（四）需要进行爆破作业的；  
（五）法律、法规规定需要办理报批手续的其他情形。

**Article 42** Under any of the following circumstances, the project owner shall go through the application and approval procedures in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State:  
(1) Where it is necessary to temporarily occupy a site outside the scope approved by the plan;  
(2) Where roads, pipelines, power, postal and telecommunications, or other public facilities may be damaged;  
(3) Where temporary water supply cut-off, power cut-off, or interruption of road traffic is required;  
(4) Where blasting operations are required;  
(5) Other circumstances where application and approval procedures are required by laws or regulations.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　建设行政主管部门负责建筑安全生产的管理，并依法接受劳动行政主管部门对建筑安全生产的指导和监督。

**Article 43** The administrative department of construction shall be responsible for the management of construction safety production, and shall accept the guidance and supervision of the administrative department of labor over construction safety production according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　建筑施工企业必须依法加强对建筑安全生产的管理，执行安全生产责任制度，采取有效措施，防止伤亡和其他安全生产事故的发生。  
建筑施工企业的法定代表人对本企业的安全生产负责。

**Article 44** A construction enterprise must strengthen the management of construction safety production according to law, implement the safety production responsibility system, and take effective measures to prevent casualties and other safety production accidents.  
The legal representative of a construction enterprise shall be responsible for the safety production of the enterprise.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　施工现场安全由建筑施工企业负责。实行施工总承包的，由总承包单位负责。分包单位向总承包单位负责，服从总承包单位对施工现场的安全生产管理。

**Article 45** The safety of the construction site shall be the responsibility of the construction enterprise. Where a general construction contract is adopted, the general contractor shall be responsible. The subcontractor shall be responsible to the general contractor and shall comply with the general contractor's safety production management of the construction site.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　建筑施工企业应当建立健全劳动安全生产教育培训制度，加强对职工安全生产的教育培训；未经安全生产教育培训的人员，不得上岗作业。

**Article 46** A construction enterprise shall establish and improve a system for education and training on labor safety production, and strengthen education and training on safety production for its employees; no person who has not received education and training on safety production may work at a post.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　建筑施工企业和作业人员在施工过程中，应当遵守有关安全生产的法律、法规和建筑行业安全规章、规程，不得违章指挥或者违章作业。作业人员有权对影响人身健康的作业程序和作业条件提出改进意见，有权获得安全生产所需的防护用品。作业人员对危及生命安全和人身健康的行为有权提出批评、检举和控告。

**Article 47** Construction enterprises and workers shall, during the construction process, comply with the laws and regulations on safety production and the safety rules and procedures of the construction industry, and shall not give illegal commands or engage in illegal operations. Workers have the right to put forward suggestions for improvement regarding work procedures and conditions that affect their health, and have the right to obtain protective equipment necessary for safety production. Workers have the right to criticize, report, and accuse acts that endanger life safety and personal health.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　建筑施工企业应当依法为职工参加工伤保险缴纳工伤保险费。鼓励企业为从事危险作业的职工办理意外伤害保险，支付保险费。

**Article 48** A construction enterprise shall, according to law, pay work-related injury insurance premiums for its employees to participate in work-related injury insurance. Enterprises are encouraged to purchase accident insurance for workers engaged in dangerous operations and pay the insurance premiums.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　涉及建筑主体和承重结构变动的装修工程，建设单位应当在施工前委托原设计单位或者具有相应资质条件的设计单位提出设计方案；没有设计方案的，不得施工。

**Article 49** For a decoration project involving changes to the main structure or load-bearing structure of a building, the project owner shall, before construction, entrust the original design unit or a design unit with corresponding qualification conditions to propose a design plan; without a design plan, construction shall not proceed.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　房屋拆除应当由具备保证安全条件的建筑施工单位承担，由建筑施工单位负责人对安全负责。

**Article 50** The demolition of a building shall be undertaken by a construction unit that has the conditions to ensure safety, and the person in charge of the construction unit shall be responsible for safety.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　施工中发生事故时，建筑施工企业应当采取紧急措施减少人员伤亡和事故损失，并按照国家有关规定及时向有关部门报告。

**Article 51** In the event of an accident during construction, a construction enterprise shall take emergency measures to reduce casualties and losses from the accident, and shall promptly report it to the relevant department in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第六章　建筑工程质量管理

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　建筑工程勘察、设计、施工的质量必须符合国家有关建筑工程安全标准的要求，具体管理办法由国务院规定。  
有关建筑工程安全的国家标准不能适应确保建筑安全的要求时，应当及时修订。

**Article 52** The quality of survey, design and construction of a construction project must meet the requirements of the State's safety standards for construction projects. The specific administrative measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
Where the national standards on the safety of construction projects cannot meet the requirements for ensuring the safety of construction projects, they shall be revised in a timely manner.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家对从事建筑活动的单位推行质量体系认证制度。从事建筑活动的单位根据自愿原则可以向国务院产品质量监督管理部门或者国务院产品质量监督管理部门授权的部门认可的认证机构申请质量体系认证。经认证合格的，由认证机构颁发质量体系认证证书。

**Article 53** The State shall implement a quality system certification system for units engaged in construction activities. A unit engaged in construction activities may, on a voluntary basis, apply to the product quality supervision and administration department under the State Council or to a certification body recognized by a department authorized by the product quality supervision and administration department under the State Council for quality system certification. Where the certification is passed, the certification body shall issue a quality system certification certificate.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　建设单位不得以任何理由，要求建筑设计单位或者建筑施工企业在工程设计或者施工作业中，违反法律、行政法规和建筑工程质量、安全标准，降低工程质量。  
建筑设计单位和建筑施工企业对建设单位违反前款规定提出的降低工程质量的要求，应当予以拒绝。

**Article 54** No project owner shall, on any ground, require a design institute or a construction enterprise to, in engineering design or construction operations, violate laws, administrative regulations and quality and safety standards for construction projects, thereby lowering the engineering quality.  
Design institutes and construction enterprises shall refuse any requirement raised by the project owner in violation of the provisions of the preceding paragraph to lower the engineering quality.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　建筑工程实行总承包的，工程质量由工程总承包单位负责，总承包单位将建筑工程分包给其他单位的，应当对分包工程的质量与分包单位承担连带责任。分包单位应当接受总承包单位的质量管理。

**Article 55** Where a construction project is undertaken on a general contracting basis, the general contractor shall be responsible for the engineering quality. Where the general contractor subcontracts part of the construction project to another unit, it shall bear joint and several liability with the subcontractor for the quality of the subcontracted work. The subcontractor shall accept the quality management of the general contractor.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　建筑工程的勘察、设计单位必须对其勘察、设计的质量负责。勘察、设计文件应当符合有关法律、行政法规的规定和建筑工程质量、安全标准、建筑工程勘察、设计技术规范以及合同的约定。设计文件选用的建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备，应当注明其规格、型号、性能等技术指标，其质量要求必须符合国家规定的标准。

**Article 56** The survey and design institute of a construction project must be responsible for the quality of its survey and design. The survey and design documents shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws, administrative regulations, the quality and safety standards for construction projects, the technical specifications for survey and design of construction projects, and the contractual agreement. Building materials, building components and parts, and equipment selected in the design documents shall be indicated with technical indicators such as their specifications, models, and performance, and their quality requirements must meet the standards prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　建筑设计单位对设计文件选用的建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备，不得指定生产厂、供应商。

**Article 57** A design institute shall not designate the manufacturer or supplier for building materials, building components and parts, and equipment selected in the design documents.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　建筑施工企业对工程的施工质量负责。  
建筑施工企业必须按照工程设计图纸和施工技术标准施工，不得偷工减料。工程设计的修改由原设计单位负责，建筑施工企业不得擅自修改工程设计。

**Article 58** A construction enterprise shall be responsible for the construction quality of the project.  
A construction enterprise must carry out construction in accordance with the engineering design drawings and construction technical standards, and shall not cut corners on work and materials. Modification of the engineering design shall be the responsibility of the original design institute, and a construction enterprise shall not modify the engineering design without authorization.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　建筑施工企业必须按照工程设计要求、施工技术标准和合同的约定，对建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备进行检验，不合格的不得使用。

**Article 59** A construction enterprise must inspect building materials, building components and parts, and equipment in accordance with the engineering design requirements, construction technical standards, and the contractual agreement. Those that are not up to standard shall not be used.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　建筑物在合理使用寿命内，必须确保地基基础工程和主体结构的质量。  
建筑工程竣工时，屋顶、墙面不得留有渗漏、开裂等质量缺陷；对已发现的质量缺陷，建筑施工企业应当修复。

**Article 60** Within the reasonable service life of a building, the quality of the foundation works and the main structure must be ensured.  
Upon completion of a construction project, the roof and walls shall be free from quality defects such as leakage, cracking, etc.; for quality defects that have been discovered, the construction enterprise shall repair them.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　交付竣工验收的建筑工程，必须符合规定的建筑工程质量标准，有完整的工程技术经济资料和经签署的工程保修书，并具备国家规定的其他竣工条件。  
建筑工程竣工经验收合格后，方可交付使用；未经验收或者验收不合格的，不得交付使用。

**Article 61** A construction project submitted for completion acceptance must conform to the prescribed quality standards for construction projects, have complete technical and economic data of the engineering and a signed warranty certificate for the project, and meet other completion conditions prescribed by the State.  
A construction project may only be delivered for use after it has passed the completion acceptance; if it has not undergone acceptance or fails the acceptance, it shall not be delivered for use.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　建筑工程实行质量保修制度。  
建筑工程的保修范围应当包括地基基础工程、主体结构工程、屋面防水工程和其他土建工程，以及电气管线、上下水管线的安装工程，供热、供冷系统工程等项目；保修的期限应当按照保证建筑物合理寿命年限内正常使用，维护使用者合法权益的原则确定。具体的保修范围和最低保修期限由国务院规定。

**Article 62** A quality warranty system shall be implemented for construction projects.  
The scope of warranty for a construction project shall include foundation works, main structure works, roof waterproofing works and other civil engineering works, as well as installation works for electrical wiring, water supply and drainage pipelines, heating and cooling systems, and other items; the warranty period shall be determined in accordance with the principle of ensuring normal use within the reasonable service life of the building and protecting the lawful rights and interests of the user. The specific scope of warranty and the minimum warranty period shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　任何单位和个人对建筑工程的质量事故、质量缺陷都有权向建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门进行检举、控告、投诉。

**Article 63** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report, accuse, or complain to the administrative department of construction or other relevant departments about quality accidents or quality defects in construction projects.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反本法规定，未取得施工许可证或者开工报告未经批准擅自施工的，责令改正，对不符合开工条件的责令停止施工，可以处以罚款。

**Article 64** Where in violation of this Law, a project is constructed without obtaining a construction permit or without approval of the commencement report, the violator shall be ordered to make correction; if the conditions for commencement are not met, the violator shall be ordered to stop construction, and may be imposed with a fine.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　发包单位将工程发包给不具有相应资质条件的承包单位的，或者违反本法规定将建筑工程肢解发包的，责令改正，处以罚款。  
超越本单位资质等级承揽工程的，责令停止违法行为，处以罚款，可以责令停业整顿，降低资质等级；情节严重的，吊销资质证书；有违法所得的，予以没收。  
未取得资质证书承揽工程的，予以取缔，并处罚款；有违法所得的，予以没收。  
以欺骗手段取得资质证书的，吊销资质证书，处以罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 65** Where a contract-awarding unit awards a project to a contractor that does not have the corresponding qualification conditions, or in violation of this Law, breaks up a construction project into parts for awarding, it shall be ordered to make correction and be imposed with a fine.  
Where a unit undertakes a project beyond its own qualification grade, it shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, be imposed with a fine, and may be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and have its qualification grade lowered; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification certificate shall be revoked; if there are illegal gains, they shall be confiscated.  
Where a unit undertakes a project without obtaining a qualification certificate, it shall be banned and be imposed with a fine; if there are illegal gains, they shall be confiscated.  
Where a qualification certificate is obtained by fraudulent means, the qualification certificate shall be revoked, and a fine shall be imposed; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　建筑施工企业转让、出借资质证书或者以其他方式允许他人以本企业的名义承揽工程的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处罚款，可以责令停业整顿，降低资质等级；情节严重的，吊销资质证书。对因该项承揽工程不符合规定的质量标准造成的损失，建筑施工企业与使用本企业名义的单位或者个人承担连带赔偿责任。

**Article 66** Where a construction enterprise transfers or lends its qualification certificate, or allows others to undertake projects in the name of the enterprise by any other means, it shall be ordered to make correction, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall be imposed with a fine; it may be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and have its qualification grade lowered; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification certificate shall be revoked. For losses caused by the failure of the project so undertaken to meet the prescribed quality standards, the construction enterprise shall bear joint and several compensation liability with the unit or individual that used the name of the enterprise.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　承包单位将承包的工程转包的，或者违反本法规定进行分包的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处罚款，可以责令停业整顿，降低资质等级；情节严重的，吊销资质证书。  
承包单位有前款规定的违法行为的，对因转包工程或者违法分包的工程不符合规定的质量标准造成的损失，与接受转包或者分包的单位承担连带赔偿责任。

**Article 67** Where a contractor subcontracts the project it has contracted, or engages in subcontracting in violation of this Law, it shall be ordered to make correction, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall be imposed with a fine; it may be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and have its qualification grade lowered; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification certificate shall be revoked.  
Where a contractor commits the illegal act provided for in the preceding paragraph, it shall bear joint and several compensation liability with the unit that accepts the subcontract or sub-subcontract for losses caused by the failure of the subcontracted or illegally subcontracted project to meet the prescribed quality standards.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　在工程发包与承包中索贿、受贿、行贿，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；不构成犯罪的，分别处以罚款，没收贿赂的财物，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予处分。  
对在工程承包中行贿的承包单位，除依照前款规定处罚外，可以责令停业整顿，降低资质等级或者吊销资质证书。

**Article 68** Where in the awarding and contracting of projects, solicitation of bribes, acceptance of bribes, or offering of bribes is committed, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, a fine shall be imposed respectively, the bribery property shall be confiscated, and disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed upon the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons.  
Where a contractor that offers bribes in project contracting is subject to punishment in accordance with the preceding paragraph, it may be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and have its qualification grade lowered or its qualification certificate revoked.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　工程监理单位与建设单位或者建筑施工企业串通，弄虚作假、降低工程质量的，责令改正，处以罚款，降低资质等级或者吊销资质证书；有违法所得的，予以没收；造成损失的，承担连带赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
工程监理单位转让监理业务的，责令改正，没收违法所得，可以责令停业整顿，降低资质等级；情节严重的，吊销资质证书。

**Article 69** Where a project supervision unit colludes with the project owner or the construction enterprise, practices fraud, or lowers the engineering quality, it shall be ordered to make correction, be imposed with a fine, and have its qualification grade lowered or its qualification certificate revoked; if there are illegal gains, they shall be confiscated; if losses are caused, it shall bear joint and several compensation liability; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a project supervision unit transfers its supervision business, it shall be ordered to make correction, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it may be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and have its qualification grade lowered; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　违反本法规定，涉及建筑主体或者承重结构变动的装修工程擅自施工的，责令改正，处以罚款；造成损失的，承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 70** Where in violation of this Law, a decoration project involving changes to the main structure or the load-bearing structure of a building is constructed without authorization, the violator shall be ordered to make correction and be imposed with a fine; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　建筑施工企业违反本法规定，对建筑安全事故隐患不采取措施予以消除的，责令改正，可以处以罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，降低资质等级或者吊销资质证书；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
建筑施工企业的管理人员违章指挥、强令职工冒险作业，因而发生重大伤亡事故或者造成其他严重后果的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 71** Where a construction enterprise, in violation of this Law, fails to take measures to eliminate potential safety hazards for building construction accidents, it shall be ordered to make correction and may be imposed with a fine; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and have its qualification grade lowered or its qualification certificate revoked; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where management personnel of a construction enterprise direct operations in violation of rules or compel workers to work at risk, thus causing serious casualty accidents or other serious consequences, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　建设单位违反本法规定，要求建筑设计单位或者建筑施工企业违反建筑工程质量、安全标准，降低工程质量的，责令改正，可以处以罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 72** Where a project owner, in violation of this Law, requires a design institute or a construction enterprise to violate the quality and safety standards for construction projects, thereby lowering the engineering quality, it shall be ordered to make correction and may be imposed with a fine; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　建筑设计单位不按照建筑工程质量、安全标准进行设计的，责令改正，处以罚款；造成工程质量事故的，责令停业整顿，降低资质等级或者吊销资质证书，没收违法所得，并处罚款；造成损失的，承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 73** Where a design institute fails to carry out design in accordance with the quality and safety standards for construction projects, it shall be ordered to make correction and be imposed with a fine; if it causes a project quality accident, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and have its qualification grade lowered or its qualification certificate revoked, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine shall be imposed; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　建筑施工企业在施工中偷工减料的，使用不合格的建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备的，或者有其他不按照工程设计图纸或者施工技术标准施工的行为的，责令改正，处以罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，降低资质等级或者吊销资质证书；造成建筑工程质量不符合规定的质量标准的，负责返工、修理，并赔偿因此造成的损失；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 74** Where a construction enterprise cuts corners on work and materials during construction, uses substandard building materials, building components and parts, or equipment, or commits any other act of failing to carry out construction in accordance with the engineering design drawings or construction technical standards, it shall be ordered to make correction and be imposed with a fine; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and have its qualification grade lowered or its qualification certificate revoked; if it causes the quality of the construction project to fail to meet the prescribed quality standards, it shall be responsible for rework and repair, and shall compensate for the losses caused thereby; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　建筑施工企业违反本法规定，不履行保修义务或者拖延履行保修义务的，责令改正，可以处以罚款，并对在保修期内因屋顶、墙面渗漏、开裂等质量缺陷造成的损失，承担赔偿责任。

**Article 75** Where a construction enterprise, in violation of this Law, fails to perform its warranty obligation or delays the performance of its warranty obligation, it shall be ordered to make correction and may be imposed with a fine, and it shall bear compensation liability for losses caused by quality defects such as leakage and cracking of roofs and walls within the warranty period.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　本法规定的责令停业整顿、降低资质等级和吊销资质证书的行政处罚，由颁发资质证书的机关决定；其他行政处罚，由建设行政主管部门或者有关部门依照法律和国务院规定的职权范围决定。  
依照本法规定被吊销资质证书的，由工商行政管理部门吊销其营业执照。

**Article 76** The administrative penalties of ordering suspension of operations for rectification, lowering of qualification grade, and revocation of qualification certificate as provided for in this Law shall be decided by the authority that issued the qualification certificate; other administrative penalties shall be decided by the administrative department of construction or the relevant department in accordance with the law and the scope of functions and powers prescribed by the State Council.  
Where a qualification certificate is revoked in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the business license shall be revoked by the administrative department for industry and commerce.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　违反本法规定，对不具备相应资质等级条件的单位颁发该等级资质证书的，由其上级机关责令收回所发的资质证书，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 77** Where in violation of this Law, a qualification certificate of a certain grade is issued to a unit that does not have the corresponding grade conditions, the superior authority shall order the recall of the issued qualification certificate, and administrative sanctions shall be imposed upon the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　政府及其所属部门的工作人员违反本法规定，限定发包单位将招标发包的工程发包给指定的承包单位的，由上级机关责令改正；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 78** Where any staff member of the government or its subordinate departments, in violation of this Law, restricts the contract-awarding unit from awarding a project to be awarded through tender to a designated contractor, the superior authority shall order correction; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　负责颁发建筑工程施工许可证的部门及其工作人员对不符合施工条件的建筑工程颁发施工许可证的，负责工程质量监督检查或者竣工验收的部门及其工作人员对不合格的建筑工程出具质量合格文件或者按合格工程验收的，由上级机关责令改正，对责任人员给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；造成损失的，由该部门承担相应的赔偿责任。

**Article 79** Where the department responsible for issuing construction permits for construction projects and its staff members issue a construction permit for a construction project that does not meet the construction conditions, or the department responsible for quality supervision and inspection or completion acceptance and its staff members issue a quality certificate or accept as a qualified project a construction project that is not up to standard, the superior authority shall order correction and impose administrative sanctions upon the responsible persons; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if losses are caused, the department concerned shall bear the corresponding compensation liability.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　在建筑物的合理使用寿命内，因建筑工程质量不合格受到损害的，有权向责任者要求赔偿。

**Article 80** Any person who suffers damage due to substandard quality of a construction project within the reasonable service life of the building shall have the right to claim compensation from the person liable.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　本法关于施工许可、建筑施工企业资质审查和建筑工程发包、承包、禁止转包，以及建筑工程监理、建筑工程安全和质量管理的规定，适用于其他专业建筑工程的建筑活动，具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 81** The provisions of this Law concerning construction permits, qualification review of construction enterprises, awarding and contracting of construction projects, prohibition of subcontracting, as well as construction project supervision, safety and quality management of construction projects, shall apply to the construction activities of other specialized construction projects. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　建设行政主管部门和其他有关部门在对建筑活动实施监督管理中，除按照国务院有关规定收取费用外，不得收取其他费用。

**Article 82** In the exercise of supervision and administration over construction activities, the administrative department of construction and other relevant departments shall not collect any fees other than those collected in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的小型房屋建筑工程的建筑活动，参照本法执行。  
依法核定作为文物保护的纪念建筑物和古建筑等的修缮，依照文物保护的有关法律规定执行。  
抢险救灾及其他临时性房屋建筑和农民自建低层住宅的建筑活动，不适用本法。

**Article 83** The construction activities of small-scale housing construction projects determined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be carried out by reference to this Law.  
The repair of memorial buildings and ancient buildings verified as cultural relics protection objects according to law shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the law on cultural relics protection.  
The construction activities of emergency rescue and disaster relief, other temporary housing, and low-rise residential houses built by farmers themselves shall not be subject to this Law.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　军用房屋建筑工程建筑活动的具体管理办法，由国务院、中央军事委员会依据本法制定。

**Article 84** The specific administrative measures for the construction activities of military housing construction projects shall be formulated by the State Council and the Central Military Commission in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　本法自1998年3月1日起施行。

**Article 85** This Law shall come into force as of March 1, 1998.
