# 中华人民共和国乡村振兴促进法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2021-04-29  
**生效日期**：2021-06-01

> （2021年4月29日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十八次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　产业发展 第三章　人才支撑 第四章　文化繁荣 第五章　生态保护 第六章　组织建设 第七章　城乡融合 第八章　扶持措施 第九章　监督检查 第十章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了全面实施乡村振兴战略，促进农业全面升级、农村全面进步、农民全面发展，加快农业农村现代化，全面建设社会主义现代化国家，制定本法。 第二条　全面实施乡村振兴战略，开展促进乡村产业振兴、人才振兴、文化振兴、生态振兴、组织振兴，推进城乡融合发展等活动，适用本法。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了全面实施乡村振兴战略，促进农业全面升级、农村全面进步、农民全面发展，加快农业农村现代化，全面建设社会主义现代化国家，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purpose of comprehensively implementing the rural revitalization strategy, promoting the all-round upgrading of agriculture, all-round progress of rural areas, and all-round development of farmers, accelerating the modernization of agriculture and rural areas, and comprehensively building a modern socialist country.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　全面实施乡村振兴战略，开展促进乡村产业振兴、人才振兴、文化振兴、生态振兴、组织振兴，推进城乡融合发展等活动，适用本法。  
本法所称乡村，是指城市建成区以外具有自然、社会、经济特征和生产、生活、生态、文化等多重功能的地域综合体，包括乡镇和村庄等。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the comprehensive implementation of the rural revitalization strategy and to activities such as promoting the revitalization of rural industries, talents, culture, ecology, and organizations, and advancing the integrated development of urban and rural areas.  
For the purpose of this Law, "rural areas" means a territorial complex outside the urban built-up areas, which has natural, social and economic characteristics and multiple functions of production, living, ecology and culture, including townships, towns and villages.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　促进乡村振兴应当按照产业兴旺、生态宜居、乡风文明、治理有效、生活富裕的总要求，统筹推进农村经济建设、政治建设、文化建设、社会建设、生态文明建设和党的建设，充分发挥乡村在保障农产品供给和粮食安全、保护生态环境、传承发展中华民族优秀传统文化等方面的特有功能。

**Article 3** In promoting rural revitalization, the general requirements of thriving industries, livable ecosystems, civilized rural customs, effective governance, and affluent living shall be followed, and efforts shall be made to coordinate the advancement of rural economic, political, cultural, social, ecological civilization, and Party building, while fully leveraging the unique functions of rural areas in ensuring the supply of agricultural products and food security, protecting the ecological environment, and inheriting and developing the excellent traditional culture of the Chinese nation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　全面实施乡村振兴战略，应当坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻创新、协调、绿色、开放、共享的新发展理念，走中国特色社会主义乡村振兴道路，促进共同富裕，遵循以下原则：  
（一）坚持农业农村优先发展，在干部配备上优先考虑，在要素配置上优先满足，在资金投入上优先保障，在公共服务上优先安排；  
（二）坚持农民主体地位，充分尊重农民意愿，保障农民民主权利和其他合法权益，调动农民的积极性、主动性、创造性，维护农民根本利益；  
（三）坚持人与自然和谐共生，统筹山水林田湖草沙系统治理，推动绿色发展，推进生态文明建设；  
（四）坚持改革创新，充分发挥市场在资源配置中的决定性作用，更好发挥政府作用，推进农业供给侧结构性改革和高质量发展，不断解放和发展乡村社会生产力，激发农村发展活力；  
（五）坚持因地制宜、规划先行、循序渐进，顺应村庄发展规律，根据乡村的历史文化、发展现状、区位条件、资源禀赋、产业基础分类推进。

**Article 4** The comprehensive implementation of the rural revitalization strategy shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, implement the new development philosophy of innovation, coordination, green development, openness, and sharing, follow the path of socialist rural revitalization with Chinese characteristics, promote common prosperity, and observe the following principles:  
(1) adhere to the priority development of agriculture and rural areas, giving priority consideration to the allocation of cadres, priority satisfaction of factor allocation, priority guarantee of capital input, and priority arrangement of public services;  
(2) adhere to the principal role of farmers, fully respect the will of farmers, protect the democratic rights and other lawful rights and interests of farmers, mobilize the enthusiasm, initiative, and creativity of farmers, and safeguard the fundamental interests of farmers;  
(3) adhere to the harmonious coexistence between human and nature, coordinate the systematic governance of mountains, rivers, forests, farmlands, lakes, grasslands, and deserts, promote green development, and advance the building of an ecological civilization;  
(4) adhere to reform and innovation, give full play to the decisive role of the market in resource allocation and better play the role of the government, advance the supply-side structural reform of agriculture and high-quality development, continuously emancipate and develop the social productive forces in rural areas, and stimulate the vitality of rural development; and  
(5) adhere to taking measures adapted to local conditions, planning first, and proceeding step by step, follow the laws of village development, and advance the work by category based on the historical culture, current development status, location conditions, resource endowments, and industrial foundation of rural areas.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家巩固和完善以家庭承包经营为基础、统分结合的双层经营体制，发展壮大农村集体所有制经济。

**Article 5** The State shall consolidate and improve the two-tier management system that combines unified and separate management on the basis of household contract management, and develop and strengthen the rural collective sector of the economy.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家建立健全城乡融合发展的体制机制和政策体系，推动城乡要素有序流动、平等交换和公共资源均衡配置，坚持以工补农、以城带乡，推动形成工农互促、城乡互补、协调发展、共同繁荣的新型工农城乡关系。

**Article 6** The State shall establish and improve the systems, mechanisms and policy framework for integrated urban-rural development, promote the orderly flow and equal exchange of urban and rural factors and the balanced allocation of public resources, adhere to the principle of industry supporting agriculture and cities driving rural areas, and promote the formation of a new type of relationship between industry and agriculture, and between urban and rural areas that features mutual promotion, complementarity, coordinated development and common prosperity.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家坚持以社会主义核心价值观为引领，大力弘扬民族精神和时代精神，加强乡村优秀传统文化保护和公共文化服务体系建设，繁荣发展乡村文化。  
每年农历秋分日为中国农民丰收节。

**Article 7** The State shall, guided by the core socialist values, vigorously promote the national spirit and the spirit of the times, strengthen the protection of outstanding traditional rural culture and the construction of the public cultural service system, and flourish and develop rural culture.  
The Autumnal Equinox Day of the lunar calendar each year shall be the Chinese Farmers' Harvest Festival.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家实施以我为主、立足国内、确保产能、适度进口、科技支撑的粮食安全战略，坚持藏粮于地、藏粮于技，采取措施不断提高粮食综合生产能力，建设国家粮食安全产业带，完善粮食加工、流通、储备体系，确保谷物基本自给、口粮绝对安全，保障国家粮食安全。  
国家完善粮食加工、储存、运输标准，提高粮食加工出品率和利用率，推动节粮减损。

**Article 8** The State shall implement a food security strategy that prioritizes self-reliance, is based on domestic production, ensures production capacity, allows moderate import, and relies on scientific and technological support, adhere to storing food in the land and in technology, take measures to continuously improve the comprehensive grain production capacity, build a national food security industrial belt, improve the grain processing, circulation and reserve systems, ensure basic self-sufficiency of grains and absolute security of staple food, and safeguard national food security.  
The State shall improve the standards for grain processing, storage and transportation, enhance the yield rate and utilization rate of grain processing, and promote grain conservation and loss reduction.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家建立健全中央统筹、省负总责、市县乡抓落实的乡村振兴工作机制。  
各级人民政府应当将乡村振兴促进工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，并建立乡村振兴考核评价制度、工作年度报告制度和监督检查制度。

**Article 9** The State shall establish and improve the rural revitalization working mechanism featuring central coordination, provincial-level overall responsibility, and implementation by cities, counties and townships.  
People's governments at all levels shall incorporate the work of promoting rural revitalization into the national economic and social development plans, and establish a system of assessment and evaluation for rural revitalization, an annual work reporting system, and a supervision and inspection system.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院农业农村主管部门负责全国乡村振兴促进工作的统筹协调、宏观指导和监督检查；国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的乡村振兴促进工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门负责本行政区域内乡村振兴促进工作的统筹协调、指导和监督检查；县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的乡村振兴促进工作。

**Article 10** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs under the State Council shall be responsible for the overall coordination, macro guidance, and supervision and inspection of the work of promoting rural revitalization nationwide; other relevant departments under the State Council shall be responsible for the relevant work of promoting rural revitalization within their respective areas of responsibility.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the overall coordination, guidance, and supervision and inspection of the work of promoting rural revitalization within their respective administrative regions; other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the relevant work of promoting rural revitalization within their respective areas of responsibility.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　各级人民政府及其有关部门应当采取多种形式，广泛宣传乡村振兴促进相关法律法规和政策，鼓励、支持人民团体、社会组织、企事业单位等社会各方面参与乡村振兴促进相关活动。  
对在乡村振兴促进工作中作出显著成绩的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 11** People's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall, in various forms, extensively publicize the laws, regulations and policies related to the promotion of rural revitalization, and encourage and support people's organizations, social organizations, enterprises, public institutions and other sectors of society to participate in activities related to the promotion of rural revitalization.  
Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in the work of promoting rural revitalization shall be given commendations and awards in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　产业发展

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家完善农村集体产权制度，增强农村集体所有制经济发展活力，促进集体资产保值增值，确保农民受益。  
各级人民政府应当坚持以农民为主体，以乡村优势特色资源为依托，支持、促进农村一二三产业融合发展，推动建立现代农业产业体系、生产体系和经营体系，推进数字乡村建设，培育新产业、新业态、新模式和新型农业经营主体，促进小农户和现代农业发展有机衔接。

**Article 12** The State shall improve the system of collective property rights in rural areas, enhance the vitality of the development of the rural collective sector of the economy, promote the preservation and appreciation of collective assets, and ensure that farmers benefit.  
People's governments at all levels shall adhere to the principal role of farmers, rely on advantageous and characteristic rural resources, support and promote the integrated development of primary, secondary and tertiary industries in rural areas, promote the establishment of a modern agricultural industry system, production system and management system, advance the construction of digital villages, cultivate new industries, new business forms and new models as well as new agricultural business entities, and promote the organic connection between small farmers and modern agricultural development.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家采取措施优化农业生产力布局，推进农业结构调整，发展优势特色产业，保障粮食和重要农产品有效供给和质量安全，推动品种培优、品质提升、品牌打造和标准化生产，推动农业对外开放，提高农业质量、效益和竞争力。  
国家实行重要农产品保障战略，分品种明确保障目标，构建科学合理、安全高效的重要农产品供给保障体系。

**Article 13** The State shall take measures to optimize the layout of agricultural productive forces, promote the adjustment of agricultural structure, develop advantageous and characteristic industries, ensure the effective supply and quality safety of food and important agricultural products, promote variety improvement, quality enhancement, brand building and standardized production, advance the opening up of agriculture, and improve the quality, efficiency and competitiveness of agriculture.  
The State shall implement a strategy for ensuring the supply of important agricultural products, specify the guarantee objectives by product category, and build a scientific, rational, safe and efficient supply guarantee system for important agricultural products.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家建立农用地分类管理制度，严格保护耕地，严格控制农用地转为建设用地，严格控制耕地转为林地、园地等其他类型农用地。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当采取措施确保耕地总量不减少、质量有提高。  
国家实行永久基本农田保护制度，建设粮食生产功能区、重要农产品生产保护区，建设并保护高标准农田。  
地方各级人民政府应当推进农村土地整理和农用地科学安全利用，加强农田水利等基础设施建设，改善农业生产条件。

**Article 14** The State shall establish a classified management system for agricultural land, strictly protect arable land, strictly control the conversion of agricultural land into construction land, and strictly control the conversion of arable land into other types of agricultural land such as woodland and garden plots. People's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall take measures to ensure that the total area of arable land is not reduced and its quality is improved.  
The State shall implement a system for the protection of permanent basic farmland, build functional areas for grain production and protected areas for important agricultural products, and construct and protect high-standard farmland.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall promote land consolidation in rural areas and the scientific and safe use of agricultural land, strengthen the construction of farmland water conservancy and other infrastructure, and improve agricultural production conditions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家加强农业种质资源保护利用和种质资源库建设，支持育种基础性、前沿性和应用技术研究，实施农作物和畜禽等良种培育、育种关键技术攻关，鼓励种业科技成果转化和优良品种推广，建立并实施种业国家安全审查机制，促进种业高质量发展。

**Article 15** The State shall strengthen the protection and utilization of agricultural germplasm resources and the construction of germplasm resource banks, support basic, cutting-edge and applied technology research in breeding, implement the breeding of improved varieties of crops, livestock and poultry and key technical breakthroughs in breeding, encourage the transformation of scientific and technological achievements in the seed industry and the promotion of improved varieties, establish and implement a national security review mechanism for the seed industry, and promote the high-quality development of the seed industry.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家采取措施加强农业科技创新，培育创新主体，构建以企业为主体、产学研协同的创新机制，强化高等学校、科研机构、农业企业创新能力，建立创新平台，加强新品种、新技术、新装备、新产品研发，加强农业知识产权保护，推进生物种业、智慧农业、设施农业、农产品加工、绿色农业投入品等领域创新，建设现代农业产业技术体系，推动农业农村创新驱动发展。  
国家健全农业科研项目评审、人才评价、成果产权保护制度，保障对农业科技基础性、公益性研究的投入，激发农业科技人员创新积极性。

**Article 16** The State shall take measures to strengthen agricultural science and technology innovation, cultivate innovation entities, build an innovation mechanism with enterprises as the main body and collaboration between industry, academia and research, enhance the innovation capacity of institutions of higher education, scientific research institutions and agricultural enterprises, establish innovation platforms, strengthen the research and development of new varieties, new technologies, new equipment and new products, strengthen the protection of agricultural intellectual property rights, promote innovation in fields such as biological seed industry, smart agriculture, facility agriculture, agricultural product processing, and green agricultural inputs, build a modern agricultural industry technology system, and promote innovation-driven development in agriculture and rural areas.  
The State shall improve the systems for the review of agricultural research projects, the evaluation of talent, and the protection of intellectual property rights in achievements, ensure investment in basic and public welfare research in agricultural science and technology, and stimulate the innovation enthusiasm of agricultural science and technology personnel.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家加强农业技术推广体系建设，促进建立有利于农业科技成果转化推广的激励机制和利益分享机制，鼓励企业、高等学校、职业学校、科研机构、科学技术社会团体、农民专业合作社、农业专业化社会化服务组织、农业科技人员等创新推广方式，开展农业技术推广服务。

**Article 17** The State shall strengthen the construction of the agricultural technology extension system, promote the establishment of incentive mechanisms and benefit-sharing mechanisms conducive to the transformation and promotion of agricultural scientific and technological achievements, encourage enterprises, institutions of higher education, vocational schools, scientific research institutions, scientific and technological social organizations, farmers' specialized cooperatives, agricultural specialized socialized service organizations, and agricultural science and technology personnel to innovate promotion methods and carry out agricultural technology extension services.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家鼓励农业机械生产研发和推广应用，推进主要农作物生产全程机械化，提高设施农业、林草业、畜牧业、渔业和农产品初加工的装备水平，推动农机农艺融合、机械化信息化融合，促进机械化生产与农田建设相适应、服务模式与农业适度规模经营相适应。  
国家鼓励农业信息化建设，加强农业信息监测预警和综合服务，推进农业生产经营信息化。

**Article 18** The State shall encourage the research, development, promotion and application of agricultural machinery production, promote the full mechanization of major crop production, improve the equipment level of facility agriculture, forestry and grass industry, animal husbandry, fishery and primary processing of agricultural products, promote the integration of agricultural machinery and agronomy, and the integration of mechanization and informatization, and ensure that mechanized production is adapted to farmland construction and service models are adapted to moderate-scale agricultural operations.  
The State shall encourage the construction of agricultural informatization, strengthen agricultural information monitoring, early warning and comprehensive services, and promote the informatization of agricultural production and operation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　各级人民政府应当发挥农村资源和生态优势，支持特色农业、休闲农业、现代农产品加工业、乡村手工业、绿色建材、红色旅游、乡村旅游、康养和乡村物流、电子商务等乡村产业的发展；引导新型经营主体通过特色化、专业化经营，合理配置生产要素，促进乡村产业深度融合；支持特色农产品优势区、现代农业产业园、农业科技园、农村创业园、休闲农业和乡村旅游重点村镇等的建设；统筹农产品生产地、集散地、销售地市场建设，加强农产品流通骨干网络和冷链物流体系建设；鼓励企业获得国际通行的农产品认证，增强乡村产业竞争力。  
发展乡村产业应当符合国土空间规划和产业政策、环境保护的要求。

**Article 19** People's governments at all levels shall leverage rural resources and ecological advantages, support the development of rural industries such as specialty agriculture, leisure agriculture, modern agricultural product processing, rural handicraft industry, green building materials, red tourism, rural tourism, health and wellness, rural logistics, and e-commerce; guide new business entities to rationally allocate production factors through specialized and professional operations, and promote the deep integration of rural industries; support the construction of advantageous areas for specialty agricultural products, modern agricultural industrial parks, agricultural science and technology parks, rural entrepreneurship parks, key towns and villages for leisure agriculture and rural tourism, among others; coordinate the construction of markets at the production, distribution and sales places of agricultural products, strengthen the construction of backbone agricultural product circulation networks and cold chain logistics systems; encourage enterprises to obtain internationally accepted agricultural product certifications, and enhance the competitiveness of rural industries.  
The development of rural industries shall comply with the requirements of territorial spatial planning, industrial policies, and environmental protection.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　各级人民政府应当完善扶持政策，加强指导服务，支持农民、返乡入乡人员在乡村创业创新，促进乡村产业发展和农民就业。

**Article 20** People's governments at all levels shall improve support policies, strengthen guidance and services, support farmers and persons returning to or entering rural areas to start businesses and innovate in rural areas, and promote the development of rural industries and the employment of farmers.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　各级人民政府应当建立健全有利于农民收入稳定增长的机制，鼓励支持农民拓宽增收渠道，促进农民增加收入。  
国家采取措施支持农村集体经济组织发展，为本集体成员提供生产生活服务，保障成员从集体经营收入中获得收益分配的权利。  
国家支持农民专业合作社、家庭农场和涉农企业、电子商务企业、农业专业化社会化服务组织等以多种方式与农民建立紧密型利益联结机制，让农民共享全产业链增值收益。

**Article 21** People's governments at all levels shall establish and improve mechanisms conducive to the steady growth of farmers' income, encourage and support farmers to broaden income channels, and promote the increase of farmers' income.  
The State shall take measures to support the development of rural collective economic organizations, provide production and living services for the members of the collective, and guarantee the right of members to obtain income distribution from the collective operating income.  
The State shall support farmers' specialized cooperatives, family farms, agricultural enterprises, e-commerce enterprises, agricultural specialized socialized service organizations, among others, to establish close interest linkage mechanisms with farmers in various ways, enabling farmers to share the value-added benefits of the entire industrial chain.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　各级人民政府应当加强国有农（林、牧、渔）场规划建设，推进国有农（林、牧、渔）场现代农业发展，鼓励国有农（林、牧、渔）场在农业农村现代化建设中发挥示范引领作用。

**Article 22** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the planning and construction of state-owned farms (forest, animal husbandry, and fishery farms), promote the modern agricultural development of state-owned farms (forest, animal husbandry, and fishery farms), and encourage state-owned farms (forest, animal husbandry, and fishery farms) to play a demonstration and leading role in the modernization of agriculture and rural areas.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　各级人民政府应当深化供销合作社综合改革，鼓励供销合作社加强与农民利益联结，完善市场运作机制，强化为农服务功能，发挥其为农服务综合性合作经济组织的作用。

**Article 23** People's governments at all levels shall deepen the comprehensive reform of supply and marketing cooperatives, encourage supply and marketing cooperatives to strengthen their interest linkages with farmers, improve market operation mechanisms, strengthen their functions of serving agriculture, and give full play to their role as comprehensive cooperative economic organizations serving agriculture.

### 第三章　人才支撑

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家健全乡村人才工作体制机制，采取措施鼓励和支持社会各方面提供教育培训、技术支持、创业指导等服务，培养本土人才，引导城市人才下乡，推动专业人才服务乡村，促进农业农村人才队伍建设。

**Article 24** The State shall improve the systems and mechanisms for rural talent work, take measures to encourage and support all sectors of society to provide services such as education and training, technical support, and entrepreneurship guidance, cultivate local talents, guide urban talents to go to rural areas, promote professional talents to serve the countryside, and promote the building of a contingent of agricultural and rural talents.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　各级人民政府应当加强农村教育工作统筹，持续改善农村学校办学条件，支持开展网络远程教育，提高农村基础教育质量，加大乡村教师培养力度，采取公费师范教育等方式吸引高等学校毕业生到乡村任教，对长期在乡村任教的教师在职称评定等方面给予优待，保障和改善乡村教师待遇，提高乡村教师学历水平、整体素质和乡村教育现代化水平。  
各级人民政府应当采取措施加强乡村医疗卫生队伍建设，支持县乡村医疗卫生人员参加培训、进修，建立县乡村上下贯通的职业发展机制，对在乡村工作的医疗卫生人员实行优惠待遇，鼓励医学院校毕业生到乡村工作，支持医师到乡村医疗卫生机构执业、开办乡村诊所、普及医疗卫生知识，提高乡村医疗卫生服务能力。  
各级人民政府应当采取措施培育农业科技人才、经营管理人才、法律服务人才、社会工作人才，加强乡村文化人才队伍建设，培育乡村文化骨干力量。

**Article 25** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the coordination of rural education work, continuously improve school running conditions in rural areas, support the development of online distance education, improve the quality of basic education in rural areas, increase the training of rural teachers, adopt methods such as publicly-funded teacher education to attract graduates from institutions of higher education to teach in rural areas, give preferential treatment to teachers who have taught in rural areas for a long time in terms of professional title evaluation, ensure and improve the treatment of rural teachers, and improve the educational level, overall quality of rural teachers and the level of modernization of rural education.  
People's governments at all levels shall take measures to strengthen the construction of the rural medical and health team, support medical and health personnel at the county, township and village levels to participate in training and further education, establish a career development mechanism that connects the county, township and village levels, implement preferential treatment for medical and health personnel working in rural areas, encourage graduates of medical schools to work in rural areas, support physicians to practice in rural medical and health institutions, set up rural clinics, popularize medical and health knowledge, and improve the capacity of rural medical and health services.  
People's governments at all levels shall take measures to cultivate agricultural science and technology talents, management talents, legal service talents, and social work talents, strengthen the construction of rural cultural talent teams, and cultivate backbone forces for rural culture.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　各级人民政府应当采取措施，加强职业教育和继续教育，组织开展农业技能培训、返乡创业就业培训和职业技能培训，培养有文化、懂技术、善经营、会管理的高素质农民和农村实用人才、创新创业带头人。

**Article 26** People's governments at all levels shall take measures to strengthen vocational education and continuing education, organize and carry out agricultural skills training, training for returning home to start businesses and seek employment, and vocational skills training, and cultivate high-quality farmers with literacy, technical skills, good management, and capable of operation, rural practical talents, and leaders in innovation and entrepreneurship.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上人民政府及其教育行政部门应当指导、支持高等学校、职业学校设置涉农相关专业，加大农村专业人才培养力度，鼓励高等学校、职业学校毕业生到农村就业创业。

**Article 27** People's governments at or above the county level and their education administrative departments shall guide and support institutions of higher education and vocational schools to set up agriculture-related majors, strengthen the cultivation of rural professional talents, and encourage graduates of institutions of higher education and vocational schools to work and start businesses in rural areas.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家鼓励城市人才向乡村流动，建立健全城乡、区域、校地之间人才培养合作与交流机制。  
县级以上人民政府应当建立鼓励各类人才参与乡村建设的激励机制，搭建社会工作和乡村建设志愿服务平台，支持和引导各类人才通过多种方式服务乡村振兴。  
乡镇人民政府和村民委员会、农村集体经济组织应当为返乡入乡人员和各类人才提供必要的生产生活服务。农村集体经济组织可以根据实际情况提供相关的福利待遇。

**Article 28** The State shall encourage urban talents to flow to rural areas, and establish and improve mechanisms for cooperation and exchange in talent cultivation between urban and rural areas, between regions, and between schools and localities.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall establish incentive mechanisms to encourage all types of talents to participate in rural construction, build platforms for social work and voluntary services for rural construction, and support and guide all types of talents to serve rural revitalization through various means.  
Township people's governments, villagers' committees, and rural collective economic organizations shall provide necessary production and living services to persons returning to or entering rural areas and all types of talents. Rural collective economic organizations may provide relevant welfare benefits according to the actual situation.

### 第四章　文化繁荣

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　各级人民政府应当组织开展新时代文明实践活动，加强农村精神文明建设，不断提高乡村社会文明程度。

**Article 29** People's governments at all levels shall organize and carry out new-era civilization practice activities, strengthen the construction of spiritual civilization in rural areas, and continuously improve the level of social civilization in rural areas.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　各级人民政府应当采取措施丰富农民文化体育生活，倡导科学健康的生产生活方式，发挥村规民约积极作用，普及科学知识，推进移风易俗，破除大操大办、铺张浪费等陈规陋习，提倡孝老爱亲、勤俭节约、诚实守信，促进男女平等，创建文明村镇、文明家庭，培育文明乡风、良好家风、淳朴民风，建设文明乡村。

**Article 30** People's governments at all levels shall take measures to enrich the cultural and sports life of farmers, advocate a scientific and healthy production and lifestyle, give full play to the positive role of village rules and regulations, popularize scientific knowledge, promote the change of customs, break outdated practices such as extravagant ceremonies and waste, advocate filial piety, diligence, frugality, honesty and trustworthiness, promote gender equality, create civilized villages and towns and civilized families, cultivate civilized rural customs, good family traditions, and simple folk customs, and build a civilized countryside.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　各级人民政府应当健全完善乡村公共文化体育设施网络和服务运行机制，鼓励开展形式多样的农民群众性文化体育、节日民俗等活动，充分利用广播电视、视听网络和书籍报刊，拓展乡村文化服务渠道，提供便利可及的公共文化服务。  
各级人民政府应当支持农业农村农民题材文艺创作，鼓励制作反映农民生产生活和乡村振兴实践的优秀文艺作品。

**Article 31** People's governments at all levels shall improve the network of rural public cultural and sports facilities and the service operation mechanism, encourage the carrying out of various forms of farmer mass cultural and sports activities, festival folk activities, etc., make full use of radio, television, audio-visual networks, books and newspapers, expand rural cultural service channels, and provide convenient and accessible public cultural services.  
People's governments at all levels shall support literary and artistic creation on themes of agriculture, rural areas and farmers, and encourage the production of excellent literary and artistic works reflecting the production and life of farmers and the practice of rural revitalization.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　各级人民政府应当采取措施保护农业文化遗产和非物质文化遗产，挖掘优秀农业文化深厚内涵，弘扬红色文化，传承和发展优秀传统文化。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对历史文化名镇名村、传统村落和乡村风貌、少数民族特色村寨的保护，开展保护状况监测和评估，采取措施防御和减轻火灾、洪水、地震等灾害。

**Article 32** People's governments at all levels shall take measures to protect agricultural cultural heritage and intangible cultural heritage, explore the profound connotation of excellent agricultural culture, promote red culture, and inherit and develop excellent traditional culture.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the protection of famous historical and cultural towns and villages, traditional villages and rural landscapes, and ethnic minority characteristic villages, monitor and evaluate the state of protection, and take measures to prevent and mitigate disasters such as fire, flood, and earthquake.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级以上地方人民政府应当坚持规划引导、典型示范，有计划地建设特色鲜明、优势突出的农业文化展示区、文化产业特色村落，发展乡村特色文化体育产业，推动乡村地区传统工艺振兴，积极推动智慧广电乡村建设，活跃繁荣农村文化市场。

**Article 33** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall adhere to planning guidance and typical demonstration, systematically build agricultural culture demonstration areas and cultural industry characteristic villages with distinct characteristics and outstanding advantages, develop rural characteristic cultural and sports industries, promote the revitalization of traditional crafts in rural areas, actively promote the construction of smart radio and television in rural areas, and enliven and prosper the rural cultural market.

### 第五章　生态保护

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家健全重要生态系统保护制度和生态保护补偿机制，实施重要生态系统保护和修复工程，加强乡村生态保护和环境治理，绿化美化乡村环境，建设美丽乡村。

**Article 34** The State shall improve the protection system for important ecosystems and the mechanism for ecological protection compensation, implement projects for the protection and restoration of important ecosystems, strengthen the ecological protection and environmental governance in rural areas, green and beautify the rural environment, and build beautiful villages.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家鼓励和支持农业生产者采用节水、节肥、节药、节能等先进的种植养殖技术，推动种养结合、农业资源综合开发，优先发展生态循环农业。  
各级人民政府应当采取措施加强农业面源污染防治，推进农业投入品减量化、生产清洁化、废弃物资源化、产业模式生态化，引导全社会形成节约适度、绿色低碳、文明健康的生产生活和消费方式。

**Article 35** The State shall encourage and support agricultural producers to adopt advanced planting and breeding technologies such as water-saving, fertilizer-saving, pesticide-saving and energy-saving, promote the combination of planting and breeding and the comprehensive development of agricultural resources, and give priority to the development of ecological circular agriculture.  
People's governments at all levels shall take measures to strengthen the prevention and control of agricultural non-point source pollution, promote the reduction of agricultural inputs, cleaner production, resource utilization of waste, and ecological industrial models, and guide the whole society to form a production, living and consumption pattern that is economical and moderate, green, low-carbon, civilized and healthy.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　各级人民政府应当实施国土综合整治和生态修复，加强森林、草原、湿地等保护修复，开展荒漠化、石漠化、水土流失综合治理，改善乡村生态环境。

**Article 36** People's governments at all levels shall carry out comprehensive land consolidation and ecological restoration, strengthen the protection and restoration of forests, grasslands, wetlands, etc., carry out comprehensive management of desertification, rocky desertification, and soil erosion, and improve the rural ecological environment.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　各级人民政府应当建立政府、村级组织、企业、农民等各方面参与的共建共管共享机制，综合整治农村水系，因地制宜推广卫生厕所和简便易行的垃圾分类，治理农村垃圾和污水，加强乡村无障碍设施建设，鼓励和支持使用清洁能源、可再生能源，持续改善农村人居环境。

**Article 37** People's governments at all levels shall establish a mechanism of joint construction, joint management and joint sharing involving the government, village-level organizations, enterprises, farmers and other parties, comprehensively improve rural water systems, promote sanitary toilets and simple waste classification according to local conditions, treat rural garbage and sewage, strengthen the construction of barrier-free facilities in rural areas, encourage and support the use of clean energy and renewable energy, and continuously improve the living environment in rural areas.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家建立健全农村住房建设质量安全管理制度和相关技术标准体系，建立农村低收入群体安全住房保障机制。建设农村住房应当避让灾害易发区域，符合抗震、防洪等基本安全要求。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当加强农村住房建设管理和服务，强化新建农村住房规划管控，严格禁止违法占用耕地建房；鼓励农村住房设计体现地域、民族和乡土特色，鼓励农村住房建设采用新型建造技术和绿色建材，引导农民建设功能现代、结构安全、成本经济、绿色环保、与乡村环境相协调的宜居住房。

**Article 38** The State shall establish and improve the quality and safety management system for rural housing construction and the related technical standards system, and establish a safety housing guarantee mechanism for low-income groups in rural areas. The construction of rural housing shall avoid disaster-prone areas and meet basic safety requirements such as earthquake resistance and flood control.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the management and service of rural housing construction, strengthen the planning and control of new rural housing, and strictly prohibit the illegal occupation of arable land for housing construction; encourage rural housing design to reflect regional, ethnic and local characteristics, encourage the use of new construction technologies and green building materials in rural housing construction, and guide farmers to build livable housing with modern functions, structural safety, cost economy, green environmental protection, and coordination with the rural environment.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家对农业投入品实行严格管理，对剧毒、高毒、高残留的农药、兽药采取禁用限用措施。农产品生产经营者不得使用国家禁用的农药、兽药或者其他有毒有害物质，不得违反农产品质量安全标准和国家有关规定超剂量、超范围使用农药、兽药、肥料、饲料添加剂等农业投入品。

**Article 39** The State shall exercise strict management over agricultural inputs, and adopt measures to prohibit or restrict the use of highly toxic, highly poisonous and high-residue pesticides and veterinary drugs. Producers and operators of agricultural products shall not use pesticides, veterinary drugs or other toxic and harmful substances prohibited by the State, and shall not use agricultural inputs such as pesticides, veterinary drugs, fertilizers, feed additives in excess of the dosage or scope prescribed by the quality and safety standards for agricultural products and the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家实行耕地养护、修复、休耕和草原森林河流湖泊休养生息制度。县级以上人民政府及其有关部门依法划定江河湖海限捕、禁捕的时间和区域，并可以根据地下水超采情况，划定禁止、限制开采地下水区域。  
禁止违法将污染环境、破坏生态的产业、企业向农村转移。禁止违法将城镇垃圾、工业固体废物、未经达标处理的城镇污水等向农业农村转移。禁止向农用地排放重金属或者其他有毒有害物质含量超标的污水、污泥，以及可能造成土壤污染的清淤底泥、尾矿、矿渣等；禁止将有毒有害废物用作肥料或者用于造田和土地复垦。  
地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当采取措施，推进废旧农膜和农药等农业投入品包装废弃物回收处理，推进农作物秸秆、畜禽粪污的资源化利用，严格控制河流湖库、近岸海域投饵网箱养殖。

**Article 40** The State shall implement a system for the conservation, restoration and fallow of arable land, and for the recuperation of grasslands, forests, rivers and lakes. People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, according to law, designate the time and areas for restricted fishing and prohibited fishing in rivers, lakes and seas, and may designate areas where the extraction of groundwater is prohibited or restricted based on the situation of groundwater over-extraction.  
It is prohibited to illegally transfer industries and enterprises that pollute the environment or damage the ecology to rural areas. It is prohibited to illegally transfer urban garbage, industrial solid waste, and urban sewage that has not been treated up to standard to agricultural and rural areas. It is prohibited to discharge into agricultural land sewage or sludge containing heavy metals or other toxic and harmful substances exceeding the standards, as well as dredged silt, tailings, slag, etc. that may cause soil pollution; it is prohibited to use toxic and harmful waste as fertilizer or for land reclamation and land reclamation.  
Local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall take measures to promote the recycling of waste agricultural film and pesticide packaging waste, and the resource utilization of crop straw and livestock and poultry manure, and strictly control the use of bait and cage culture in rivers, lakes, reservoirs and offshore waters.

### 第六章　组织建设

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　建立健全党委领导、政府负责、民主协商、社会协同、公众参与、法治保障、科技支撑的现代乡村社会治理体制和自治、法治、德治相结合的乡村社会治理体系，建设充满活力、和谐有序的善治乡村。  
地方各级人民政府应当加强乡镇人民政府社会管理和服务能力建设，把乡镇建成乡村治理中心、农村服务中心、乡村经济中心。

**Article 41** The State shall establish and improve a modern rural social governance system featuring Party committee leadership, government responsibility, democratic consultation, social coordination, public participation, legal guarantee, and scientific and technological support, and a rural social governance system combining self-governance, rule of law, and rule of virtue, to build a well-governed countryside that is full of vitality, harmonious and orderly.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen the capacity building of township people's governments in social management and services, and build townships into rural governance centers, rural service centers, and rural economic centers.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　中国共产党农村基层组织，按照中国共产党章程和有关规定发挥全面领导作用。村民委员会、农村集体经济组织等应当在乡镇党委和村党组织的领导下，实行村民自治，发展集体所有制经济，维护农民合法权益，并应当接受村民监督。

**Article 42** The rural grassroots organizations of the Communist Party of China shall play an overall leading role in accordance with the Constitution of the Communist Party of China and relevant provisions. Villagers' committees, rural collective economic organizations, etc. shall, under the leadership of the township Party committee and the village Party organization, practice villagers' autonomy, develop the collective sector of the economy, safeguard the lawful rights and interests of farmers, and shall be subject to the supervision of the villagers.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家建立健全农业农村工作干部队伍的培养、配备、使用、管理机制，选拔优秀干部充实到农业农村工作干部队伍，采取措施提高农业农村工作干部队伍的能力和水平，落实农村基层干部相关待遇保障，建设懂农业、爱农村、爱农民的农业农村工作干部队伍。

**Article 43** The State shall establish and improve the mechanisms for the cultivation, allocation, use and management of the cadre contingent for agricultural and rural work, select outstanding cadres to strengthen the cadre contingent for agricultural and rural work, take measures to improve the capacity and level of the cadre contingent for agricultural and rural work, implement the relevant treatment guarantees for rural grassroots cadres, and build a cadre contingent for agricultural and rural work that understands agriculture, loves rural areas, and cares for farmers.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　地方各级人民政府应当构建简约高效的基层管理体制，科学设置乡镇机构，加强乡村干部培训，健全农村基层服务体系，夯实乡村治理基础。

**Article 44** Local people's governments at all levels shall build a simple and efficient grassroots management system, scientifically set up township institutions, strengthen the training of rural cadres, improve the rural grassroots service system, and consolidate the foundation of rural governance.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　乡镇人民政府应当指导和支持农村基层群众性自治组织规范化、制度化建设，健全村民委员会民主决策机制和村务公开制度，增强村民自我管理、自我教育、自我服务、自我监督能力。

**Article 45** Township people's governments shall guide and support the standardization and institutionalization of rural grassroots mass self-governing organizations, improve the democratic decision-making mechanism of villagers' committees and the system of village affairs disclosure, and enhance the capacity of villagers for self-management, self-education, self-service and self-supervision.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　各级人民政府应当引导和支持农村集体经济组织发挥依法管理集体资产、合理开发集体资源、服务集体成员等方面的作用，保障农村集体经济组织的独立运营。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当支持发展农民专业合作社、家庭农场、农业企业等多种经营主体，健全农业农村社会化服务体系。

**Article 46** People's governments at all levels shall guide and support rural collective economic organizations to play their roles in managing collective assets according to law, rationally developing collective resources, and serving collective members, and ensure the independent operation of rural collective economic organizations.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall support the development of various business entities such as farmers' specialized cooperatives, family farms, and agricultural enterprises, and improve the socialized service system for agriculture and rural areas.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上地方人民政府应当采取措施加强基层群团组织建设，支持、规范和引导农村社会组织发展，发挥基层群团组织、农村社会组织团结群众、联系群众、服务群众等方面的作用。

**Article 47** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to strengthen the construction of grassroots people's organizations and mass organizations, support, regulate and guide the development of rural social organizations, and bring into play the role of grassroots people's organizations and mass organizations and rural social organizations in uniting, contacting and serving the masses.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　地方各级人民政府应当加强基层执法队伍建设，鼓励乡镇人民政府根据需要设立法律顾问和公职律师，鼓励有条件的地方在村民委员会建立公共法律服务工作室，深入开展法治宣传教育和人民调解工作，健全乡村矛盾纠纷调处化解机制，推进法治乡村建设。

**Article 48** Local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen the construction of grassroots law enforcement teams, encourage township people's governments to set up legal advisors and public lawyers as needed, encourage places with conditions to establish public legal service studios in villagers' committees, carry out in-depth legal publicity and education and people's mediation work, improve the mechanism for handling and resolving rural conflicts and disputes, and promote the construction of a rule of law countryside.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　地方各级人民政府应当健全农村社会治安防控体系，加强农村警务工作，推动平安乡村建设；健全农村公共安全体系，强化农村公共卫生、安全生产、防灾减灾救灾、应急救援、应急广播、食品、药品、交通、消防等安全管理责任。

**Article 49** Local people's governments at all levels shall improve the rural public security prevention and control system, strengthen rural police work, and promote the construction of safe villages; improve the rural public safety system, and strengthen safety management responsibilities in rural public health, work safety, disaster prevention, mitigation and relief, emergency rescue, emergency broadcasting, food, drugs, transportation, fire protection, etc.

### 第七章　城乡融合

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　各级人民政府应当协同推进乡村振兴战略和新型城镇化战略的实施，整体筹划城镇和乡村发展，科学有序统筹安排生态、农业、城镇等功能空间，优化城乡产业发展、基础设施、公共服务设施等布局，逐步健全全民覆盖、普惠共享、城乡一体的基本公共服务体系，加快县域城乡融合发展，促进农业高质高效、乡村宜居宜业、农民富裕富足。

**Article 50** People's governments at all levels shall coordinate the implementation of the rural revitalization strategy and the new urbanization strategy, make overall plans for the development of urban and rural areas, scientifically and orderly coordinate the arrangement of functional spaces such as ecology, agriculture, and urban areas, optimize the layout of urban and rural industrial development, infrastructure, public service facilities, etc., gradually improve the basic public service system that covers the entire population, is universally beneficial and shared, and integrates urban and rural areas, accelerate the integrated urban-rural development within counties, and promote high-quality and efficient agriculture, livable and business-friendly rural areas, and affluent and prosperous farmers.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　县级人民政府和乡镇人民政府应当优化本行政区域内乡村发展布局，按照尊重农民意愿、方便群众生产生活、保持乡村功能和特色的原则，因地制宜安排村庄布局，依法编制村庄规划，分类有序推进村庄建设，严格规范村庄撤并，严禁违背农民意愿、违反法定程序撤并村庄。

**Article 51** The people's government at the county level and the people's government at the township level shall optimize the layout of rural development within their respective administrative regions, arrange village layouts according to local conditions in compliance with the principles of respecting farmers' wishes, facilitating the production and living of the masses, and preserving rural functions and characteristics, prepare village plans according to law, promote village construction in a classified and orderly manner, strictly regulate the merging and cancellation of villages, and strictly prohibit the merging and cancellation of villages in violation of farmers' wishes and legal procedures.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　县级以上地方人民政府应当统筹规划、建设、管护城乡道路以及垃圾污水处理、供水供电供气、物流、客运、信息通信、广播电视、消防、防灾减灾等公共基础设施和新型基础设施，推动城乡基础设施互联互通，保障乡村发展能源需求，保障农村饮用水安全，满足农民生产生活需要。

**Article 52** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall make overall plans for, build, manage, and protect urban and rural roads and public infrastructure and new infrastructure such as garbage and sewage treatment, water supply, power supply, gas supply, logistics, passenger transport, information and communication, radio and television, fire control, disaster prevention and reduction, promote the interconnection and interoperability of urban and rural infrastructure, guarantee the energy needs for rural development, guarantee the safety of drinking water in rural areas, and meet the production and living needs of farmers.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家发展农村社会事业，促进公共教育、医疗卫生、社会保障等资源向农村倾斜，提升乡村基本公共服务水平，推进城乡基本公共服务均等化。  
国家健全乡村便民服务体系，提升乡村公共服务数字化智能化水平，支持完善村级综合服务设施和综合信息平台，培育服务机构和服务类社会组织，完善服务运行机制，促进公共服务与自我服务有效衔接，增强生产生活服务功能。

**Article 53** The State shall develop rural social undertakings, promote the tilt of resources such as public education, medical and health care, and social insurance towards rural areas, raise the level of basic public services in villages, and promote the equalization of basic public services between urban and rural areas.  
The State shall improve the rural convenience service system, raise the level of digitalization and intellectualization of rural public services, support the improvement of village-level comprehensive service facilities and comprehensive information platforms, foster service institutions and service-type social organizations, improve the service operation mechanism, promote the effective connection between public services and self-services, and enhance the functions of production and living services.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家完善城乡统筹的社会保障制度，建立健全保障机制，支持乡村提高社会保障管理服务水平；建立健全城乡居民基本养老保险待遇确定和基础养老金标准正常调整机制，确保城乡居民基本养老保险待遇随经济社会发展逐步提高。  
国家支持农民按照规定参加城乡居民基本养老保险、基本医疗保险，鼓励具备条件的灵活就业人员和农业产业化从业人员参加职工基本养老保险、职工基本医疗保险等社会保险。  
国家推进城乡最低生活保障制度统筹发展，提高农村特困人员供养等社会救助水平，加强对农村留守儿童、妇女和老年人以及残疾人、困境儿童的关爱服务，支持发展农村普惠型养老服务和互助性养老。

**Article 54** The State shall improve the social insurance system with urban-rural coordination, establish and improve a guarantee mechanism, and support villages in raising the level of social insurance management and services; establish and improve the mechanism for the determination of basic old-age insurance treatment for urban and rural residents and the normal adjustment of the basic pension standard, to ensure that the basic old-age insurance treatment for urban and rural residents gradually increases with economic and social development.  
The State shall support farmers in participating in the basic old-age insurance and basic medical insurance for urban and rural residents in accordance with the regulations, and encourage flexible employment personnel and agricultural industrialization employees who meet the conditions to participate in social insurance such as basic old-age insurance for employees and basic medical insurance for employees.  
The State shall promote the coordinated development of the urban and rural minimum subsistence guarantee system, raise the level of social assistance such as support for rural destitute persons, strengthen care services for left-behind children, women, the elderly, disabled persons, and children in difficulty, and support the development of inclusive elderly care services and mutual-aid elderly care in rural areas.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家推动形成平等竞争、规范有序、城乡统一的人力资源市场，健全城乡均等的公共就业创业服务制度。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当采取措施促进在城镇稳定就业和生活的农民自愿有序进城落户，不得以退出土地承包经营权、宅基地使用权、集体收益分配权等作为农民进城落户的条件；推进取得居住证的农民及其随迁家属享受城镇基本公共服务。  
国家鼓励社会资本到乡村发展与农民利益联结型项目，鼓励城市居民到乡村旅游、休闲度假、养生养老等，但不得破坏乡村生态环境，不得损害农村集体经济组织及其成员的合法权益。

**Article 55** The State shall promote the formation of a human resources market that is equally competitive, standardized and orderly, and unified between urban and rural areas, and improve the public employment and entrepreneurship service system that is equal between urban and rural areas.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to promote the voluntary and orderly settlement of farmers who have stable employment and residence in cities and towns, and shall not take the relinquishment of land contractual management rights, the right to use homestead land, and the right to distribute collective income as conditions for farmers to settle in cities; promote farmers who have obtained residence permits and their accompanying family members to enjoy basic public services in cities and towns.  
The State shall encourage social capital to develop projects linked with farmers' interests in rural areas, and encourage urban residents to travel to rural areas for tourism, leisure, health preservation, and elderly care, provided that the rural ecological environment shall not be damaged, and the legitimate rights and interests of rural collective economic organizations and their members shall not be harmed.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　县级以上人民政府应当采取措施促进城乡产业协同发展，在保障农民主体地位的基础上健全联农带农激励机制，实现乡村经济多元化和农业全产业链发展。

**Article 56** People's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to promote the coordinated development of urban and rural industries, improve the incentive mechanism for linking and leading farmers on the basis of ensuring the principal status of farmers, and achieve the diversification of the rural economy and the development of the entire agricultural industry chain.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　各级人民政府及其有关部门应当采取措施鼓励农民进城务工，全面落实城乡劳动者平等就业、同工同酬，依法保障农民工工资支付和社会保障权益。

**Article 57** People's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall take measures to encourage farmers to work in cities and towns, fully implement equal employment and equal pay for equal work for urban and rural workers, and safeguard according to law the payment of wages and social insurance rights and interests of migrant workers.

### 第八章　扶持措施

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国家建立健全农业支持保护体系和实施乡村振兴战略财政投入保障制度。县级以上人民政府应当优先保障用于乡村振兴的财政投入，确保投入力度不断增强、总量持续增加、与乡村振兴目标任务相适应。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以依法发行政府债券，用于现代农业设施建设和乡村建设。  
各级人民政府应当完善涉农资金统筹整合长效机制，强化财政资金监督管理，全面实施预算绩效管理，提高财政资金使用效益。

**Article 58** The State shall establish and improve the agricultural support and protection system and the system for guaranteeing fiscal investment for implementing the rural revitalization strategy. People's governments at or above the county level shall give priority to ensuring fiscal investment for rural revitalization, and ensure that the intensity of investment is continuously enhanced, the total amount continues to increase, and it matches the target tasks of rural revitalization.  
People's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may issue government bonds according to law for the construction of modern agricultural facilities and rural construction.  
People's governments at all levels shall improve the long-term mechanism for the overall planning and integration of agricultural funds, strengthen the supervision and management of fiscal funds, fully implement budget performance management, and improve the efficiency of the use of fiscal funds.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　各级人民政府应当采取措施增强脱贫地区内生发展能力，建立农村低收入人口、欠发达地区帮扶长效机制，持续推进脱贫地区发展；建立健全易返贫致贫人口动态监测预警和帮扶机制，实现巩固拓展脱贫攻坚成果同乡村振兴有效衔接。  
国家加大对革命老区、民族地区、边疆地区实施乡村振兴战略的支持力度。

**Article 59** People's governments at all levels shall take measures to enhance the endogenous development capacity of areas that have been lifted out of poverty, establish a long-term assistance mechanism for rural low-income populations and underdeveloped areas, and continuously promote the development of areas that have been lifted out of poverty; establish and improve a dynamic monitoring, early warning, and assistance mechanism for populations likely to return to poverty or fall into poverty, and achieve the effective connection between consolidating and expanding the achievements of poverty alleviation and rural revitalization.  
The State shall increase support for the implementation of the rural revitalization strategy in old revolutionary base areas, ethnic minority areas, and border areas.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国家按照增加总量、优化存量、提高效能的原则，构建以高质量绿色发展为导向的新型农业补贴政策体系。

**Article 60** The State shall, in accordance with the principles of increasing total volume, optimizing stock, and improving efficiency, establish a new agricultural subsidy policy system oriented towards high-quality green development.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　各级人民政府应当坚持取之于农、主要用之于农的原则，按照国家有关规定调整完善土地使用权出让收入使用范围，提高农业农村投入比例，重点用于高标准农田建设、农田水利建设、现代种业提升、农村供水保障、农村人居环境整治、农村土地综合整治、耕地及永久基本农田保护、村庄公共设施建设和管护、农村教育、农村文化和精神文明建设支出，以及与农业农村直接相关的山水林田湖草沙生态保护修复、以工代赈工程建设等。

**Article 61** People's governments at all levels shall adhere to the principle of taking from agriculture and mainly using in agriculture, adjust and improve the scope of use of income from the transfer of land use rights in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, increase the proportion of investment in agriculture and rural areas, and focus on high-standard farmland construction, farmland water conservancy construction, modern seed industry improvement, rural water supply guarantee, rural living environment improvement, comprehensive rural land improvement, arable land and permanent basic farmland protection, village public facility construction and management and protection, rural education, rural culture and spiritual civilization construction expenditure, as well as ecological protection and restoration of mountains, waters, forests, fields, lakes, grasslands, and deserts directly related to agriculture and rural areas, and work-relief project construction, etc.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　县级以上人民政府设立的相关专项资金、基金应当按照规定加强对乡村振兴的支持。  
国家支持以市场化方式设立乡村振兴基金，重点支持乡村产业发展和公共基础设施建设。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当优化乡村营商环境，鼓励创新投融资方式，引导社会资本投向乡村。

**Article 62** Special funds and funds established by people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen support for rural revitalization in accordance with regulations.  
The State shall support the establishment of rural revitalization funds in a market-oriented manner, with a focus on supporting the development of rural industries and the construction of public infrastructure.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall optimize the rural business environment, encourage innovation in investment and financing methods, and guide social capital investment in rural areas.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国家综合运用财政、金融等政策措施，完善政府性融资担保机制，依法完善乡村资产抵押担保权能，改进、加强乡村振兴的金融支持和服务。  
财政出资设立的农业信贷担保机构应当主要为从事农业生产和与农业生产直接相关的经营主体服务。

**Article 63** The State shall comprehensively use fiscal, financial, and other policy measures, improve the government-backed financing guarantee mechanism, improve according to law the mortgage and pledge functions of rural assets, and improve and strengthen financial support and services for rural revitalization.  
Agricultural credit guarantee institutions funded by fiscal allocations shall primarily serve the entities engaged in agricultural production and directly related to agricultural production.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　国家健全多层次资本市场，多渠道推动涉农企业股权融资，发展并规范债券市场，促进涉农企业利用多种方式融资；丰富农产品期货品种，发挥期货市场价格发现和风险分散功能。

**Article 64** The State shall improve the multi-level capital market, promote equity financing for agriculture-related enterprises through multiple channels, develop and regulate the bond market, and promote agriculture-related enterprises to use multiple methods to raise funds; enrich the variety of agricultural product futures, and leverage the price discovery and risk dispersion functions of the futures market.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国家建立健全多层次、广覆盖、可持续的农村金融服务体系，完善金融支持乡村振兴考核评估机制，促进农村普惠金融发展，鼓励金融机构依法将更多资源配置到乡村发展的重点领域和薄弱环节。  
政策性金融机构应当在业务范围内为乡村振兴提供信贷支持和其他金融服务，加大对乡村振兴的支持力度。  
商业银行应当结合自身职能定位和业务优势，创新金融产品和服务模式，扩大基础金融服务覆盖面，增加对农民和农业经营主体的信贷规模，为乡村振兴提供金融服务。  
农村商业银行、农村合作银行、农村信用社等农村中小金融机构应当主要为本地农业农村农民服务，当年新增可贷资金主要用于当地农业农村发展。

**Article 65** The State shall establish and improve a multi-level, broad-coverage, and sustainable rural financial service system, improve the assessment and evaluation mechanism for financial support for rural revitalization, promote the development of inclusive rural finance, and encourage financial institutions to allocate more resources according to law to the key areas and weak links of rural development.  
Policy financial institutions shall, within their business scope, provide credit support and other financial services for rural revitalization, and increase their support for rural revitalization.  
Commercial banks shall, based on their own functional positioning and business advantages, innovate financial products and service models, expand the coverage of basic financial services, increase the scale of credit to farmers and agricultural business entities, and provide financial services for rural revitalization.  
Rural small and medium financial institutions such as rural commercial banks, rural cooperative banks, and rural credit cooperatives shall primarily serve the local agriculture, rural areas, and farmers, and the newly increased loanable funds in the current year shall be mainly used for local agricultural and rural development.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家建立健全多层次农业保险体系，完善政策性农业保险制度，鼓励商业性保险公司开展农业保险业务，支持农民和农业经营主体依法开展互助合作保险。  
县级以上人民政府应当采取保费补贴等措施，支持保险机构适当增加保险品种，扩大农业保险覆盖面，促进农业保险发展。

**Article 66** The State shall establish and improve a multi-level agricultural insurance system, improve the policy-based agricultural insurance system, encourage commercial insurance companies to conduct agricultural insurance business, and support farmers and agricultural business entities to carry out mutual aid and cooperative insurance according to law.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall take measures such as premium subsidies to support insurance institutions to appropriately increase insurance varieties, expand the coverage of agricultural insurance, and promote the development of agricultural insurance.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　县级以上地方人民政府应当推进节约集约用地，提高土地使用效率，依法采取措施盘活农村存量建设用地，激活农村土地资源，完善农村新增建设用地保障机制，满足乡村产业、公共服务设施和农民住宅用地合理需求。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当保障乡村产业用地，建设用地指标应当向乡村发展倾斜，县域内新增耕地指标应当优先用于折抵乡村产业发展所需建设用地指标，探索灵活多样的供地新方式。  
经国土空间规划确定为工业、商业等经营性用途并依法登记的集体经营性建设用地，土地所有权人可以依法通过出让、出租等方式交由单位或者个人使用，优先用于发展集体所有制经济和乡村产业。

**Article 67** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall promote economical and intensive land use, improve land use efficiency, take measures according to law to revitalize existing rural construction land, activate rural land resources, improve the guarantee mechanism for new rural construction land, and meet the reasonable land needs for rural industries, public service facilities, and farmers' housing.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall guarantee land for rural industries, construction land quotas shall be tilted towards rural development, newly increased arable land quotas within the county shall be given priority for offsetting construction land quotas needed for rural industrial development, and flexible and diverse new land supply methods shall be explored.  
Collective operational construction land that has been designated for industrial, commercial, and other operational purposes through national land spatial planning and registered according to law may be transferred by the land owner to units or individuals for use through sale, lease, or other methods according to law, and shall be preferentially used for the development of collective ownership economy and rural industries.

### 第九章　监督检查

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　国家实行乡村振兴战略实施目标责任制和考核评价制度。上级人民政府应当对下级人民政府实施乡村振兴战略的目标完成情况等进行考核，考核结果作为地方人民政府及其负责人综合考核评价的重要内容。

**Article 68** The State shall implement a target responsibility system and an assessment and evaluation system for the implementation of the rural revitalization strategy. The people's government at a higher level shall assess the completion of targets for implementing the rural revitalization strategy by the people's government at a lower level, and the assessment results shall serve as important content in the comprehensive assessment and evaluation of the local people's government and its responsible persons.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关部门建立客观反映乡村振兴进展的指标和统计体系。县级以上地方人民政府应当对本行政区域内乡村振兴战略实施情况进行评估。

**Article 69** The relevant departments of the State Council and of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall establish indicators and a statistical system that objectively reflect the progress of rural revitalization. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall conduct an assessment of the implementation of the rural revitalization strategy within their administrative regions.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　县级以上各级人民政府应当向本级人民代表大会或者其常务委员会报告乡村振兴促进工作情况。乡镇人民政府应当向本级人民代表大会报告乡村振兴促进工作情况。

**Article 70** People's governments at or above the county level shall report to the people's congress at the corresponding level or its standing committee on the work of promoting rural revitalization. Township people's governments shall report to the people's congress at the corresponding level on the work of promoting rural revitalization.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　地方各级人民政府应当每年向上一级人民政府报告乡村振兴促进工作情况。  
县级以上人民政府定期对下一级人民政府乡村振兴促进工作情况开展监督检查。

**Article 71** Local people's governments at all levels shall report annually to the people's government at the next higher level on the work of promoting rural revitalization.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall regularly carry out oversight and inspection of the work of promoting rural revitalization by the people's government at the next lower level.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　县级以上人民政府发展改革、财政、农业农村、审计等部门按照各自职责对农业农村投入优先保障机制落实情况、乡村振兴资金使用情况和绩效等实施监督。

**Article 72** The development and reform, finance, agriculture and rural affairs, audit, and other departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions, supervise the implementation of the priority guarantee mechanism for agricultural and rural investment, the use and performance of rural revitalization funds, and other matters.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　各级人民政府及其有关部门在乡村振兴促进工作中不履行或者不正确履行职责的，依照法律法规和国家有关规定追究责任，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。  
违反有关农产品质量安全、生态环境保护、土地管理等法律法规的，由有关主管部门依法予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 73** If the people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments fail to perform or do not correctly perform their duties in the work of promoting rural revitalization, they shall be held accountable in accordance with laws, regulations, and the relevant provisions of the State, and the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.  
Anyone who violates laws and regulations on agricultural product quality and safety, ecological environment protection, land management, etc. shall be punished by the relevant competent departments according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　本法自2021年6月1日起施行。

**Article 74** This Law shall come into force as of June 1, 2021.
