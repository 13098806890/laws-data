# 中华人民共和国统计法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2024-09-13  
**生效日期**：2024-09-13

> （1983年12月8日第六届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三次会议通过　根据1996年5月15日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十九次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国统计法〉的决定》第一次修正　2009年6月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第九次会议修订　根据2024年9月13日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十一次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国统计法〉的决定》第二次修正）

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了科学、有效地组织统计工作，保障统计资料的真实性、准确性、完整性和及时性，加强统计监督，发挥统计在了解国情国力、服务经济社会高质量发展中的重要作用，推动全面建设社会主义现代化国家，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of organizing statistical work in a scientific and effective manner, ensuring the truthfulness, accuracy, completeness and timeliness of statistical data, strengthening statistical supervision, giving full play to the important role of statistics in understanding the national conditions and national strength, serving high-quality economic and social development, and promoting the comprehensive building of a modern socialist country.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法适用于各级人民政府、县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门组织实施的统计活动。  
统计的基本任务是对经济社会发展情况进行统计调查、统计分析，提供统计资料和统计咨询意见，实行统计监督。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to statistical activities organized and implemented by the people's governments at all levels, the statistical institutions of the people's governments at or above the county level, and the relevant departments.  
The basic tasks of statistics are to conduct statistical surveys and statistical analyses of economic and social development, to provide statistical data and statistical consultancy opinions, and to exercise statistical supervision.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　统计工作坚持中国共产党的领导。  
国家建立集中统一的统计系统，实行统一领导、分级负责的统计管理体制。

**Article 3** Statistical work shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China.  
The state shall establish a centralized and unified statistical system, and implement a statistical management system featuring unified leadership and division of labor with responsibility at different levels.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院和地方各级人民政府、各有关部门应当加强对统计工作的组织领导，为统计工作提供必要的保障。

**Article 4** The State Council, local people's governments at all levels, and all relevant departments shall strengthen organizational leadership over statistical work and provide necessary guarantees for statistical work.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家加强统计科学研究，根据经济社会发展的新情况，健全科学合理的统计标准和统计指标体系，将新经济新领域纳入统计调查范围，并不断改进统计调查方法，提高统计的科学性。  
国家有计划地加强统计信息化建设，推动现代信息技术与统计工作深度融合，促进统计信息搜集、处理、传输、共享、存储技术和统计数据库体系的现代化。

**Article 5** The state shall strengthen statistical science research, improve scientific and reasonable statistical standards and statistical indicator systems based on new developments in economic and society, incorporate new economies and new fields into the scope of statistical surveys, and continuously improve statistical survey methods to enhance the scientific nature of statistics.  
The state shall strengthen the construction of statistical informatization in a planned manner, promote the deep integration of modern information technology with statistical work, and facilitate the modernization of statistical information collection, processing, transmission, sharing, storage technologies and statistical database systems.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家构建系统完整、协同高效、约束有力、权威可靠的统计监督体系。  
统计机构根据统计调查制度和经批准的计划安排，对各地区、各部门贯彻落实国家重大经济社会政策措施情况、履行统计法定职责情况等进行统计监督。

**Article 6** The state shall establish a statistical supervision system that is systematic and complete, collaborative and efficient, with strong constraints, authoritative and reliable.  
Statistical institutions shall, in accordance with the statistical survey system and approved plans and arrangements, exercise statistical supervision over the implementation of major national economic and social policies and measures by various regions and departments, as well as the performance of their statutory statistical duties.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　统计机构和统计人员依照本法规定独立行使统计调查、统计报告、统计监督的职权，不受侵犯。  
地方各级人民政府、县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门以及各单位的负责人，不得自行修改统计机构和统计人员依法搜集、整理的统计资料，不得以任何方式要求统计机构、统计人员及其他机构、人员伪造、篡改统计资料，不得明示、暗示下级单位及其人员或者统计调查对象填报虚假统计数据，不得对依法履行职责或者拒绝、抵制统计违法行为的单位和个人打击报复。

**Article 7** Statistical institutions and statisticians shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law, independently exercise the powers of statistical survey, statistical report, and statistical supervision, which shall not be infringed upon.  
The leading persons of local people's governments at all levels, statistical institutions of the people's governments at or above the county level, relevant departments, and various entities shall not modify the statistical data lawfully collected and compiled by statistical institutions and statisticians; shall not in any manner require statistical institutions, statisticians, or other institutions or persons to forge or tamper with statistical data; shall not explicitly or implicitly instruct subordinate entities and their personnel or respondents to fill in false statistical data; and shall not retaliate against entities or individuals that lawfully perform their duties or refuse or resist illegal statistical acts.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家机关、企业事业单位和其他组织以及个体工商户和个人等统计调查对象，必须依照本法和国家有关规定，真实、准确、完整、及时地提供统计调查所需的资料，不得提供不真实或者不完整的统计资料，不得迟报、拒报统计资料。

**Article 8** State organs, enterprises, public institutions, other organizations, and self-employed individuals and individuals, as respondents of statistical surveys, must provide the data required for statistical surveys truthfully, accurately, completely and promptly in accordance with this Law and the relevant provisions of the state, and shall not provide untruthful or incomplete statistical data, and shall not delay reporting or refuse to report statistical data.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　地方各级人民政府、县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门应当根据国家有关规定，将防范和惩治统计造假、弄虚作假纳入依法行政、依法履职责任范围，建立健全相关责任制，加强对领导干部统计工作的考核管理，依法对统计造假、弄虚作假行为追究法律责任。

**Article 9** Local people's governments at all levels, statistical institutions of the people's governments at or above the county level, and relevant departments shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, incorporate the prevention and punishment of statistical fraud and falsification into the scope of responsibilities for law-based administration and law-based performance of duties, establish and improve relevant responsibility systems, strengthen the assessment and management of leading cadres' statistical work, and investigate legal liability for statistical fraud and falsification according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　统计工作应当接受社会公众的监督。任何单位和个人有权检举统计中弄虚作假等违法行为。对检举有功的单位和个人应当给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 10** Statistical work shall be subject to the supervision of the public. Any entity or individual shall have the right to report illegal acts such as falsification in statistics. Entities and individuals that have rendered meritorious service in reporting shall be commended and rewarded.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　统计机构和统计人员对在统计工作中知悉的国家秘密、工作秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私和个人信息，应当予以保密，不得泄露或者向他人非法提供。

**Article 11** Statistical institutions and statisticians shall keep confidential any state secrets, work secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, and personal information known to them during statistical work, and shall not disclose or illegally provide them to others.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　任何单位和个人不得利用虚假统计资料骗取荣誉称号、物质利益或者职务职级等晋升。

**Article 12** No entity or individual shall use false statistical data to fraudulently obtain honorary titles, material benefits, or promotion in positions or ranks.

### 第二章　统计调查管理

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　统计调查项目包括国家统计调查项目、部门统计调查项目和地方统计调查项目。  
国家统计调查项目是指全国性基本情况的统计调查项目。部门统计调查项目是指国务院有关部门的专业性统计调查项目。地方统计调查项目是指县级以上地方人民政府及其部门的地方性统计调查项目。  
国家统计调查项目、部门统计调查项目、地方统计调查项目应当明确分工，互相衔接，不得重复。

**Article 13** Statistical survey projects include national statistical survey projects, departmental statistical survey projects, and local statistical survey projects.  
A national statistical survey project means a statistical survey project on national basic conditions. A departmental statistical survey project means a specialized statistical survey project of a relevant department of the State Council. A local statistical survey project means a local statistical survey project of a local people's government at or above the county level and its departments.  
National statistical survey projects, departmental statistical survey projects, and local statistical survey projects shall have a clear division of labor, be mutually coordinated, and shall not overlap.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家统计调查项目由国家统计局制定，或者由国家统计局和国务院有关部门共同制定，报国务院备案；重大的国家统计调查项目报国务院审批。  
部门统计调查项目由国务院有关部门制定。统计调查对象属于本部门管辖系统的，报国家统计局备案；统计调查对象超出本部门管辖系统的，报国家统计局审批。  
地方统计调查项目由县级以上地方人民政府统计机构和有关部门分别制定或者共同制定。其中，由省级人民政府统计机构单独制定或者和有关部门共同制定的，报国家统计局审批；由省级以下人民政府统计机构单独制定或者和有关部门共同制定的，报省级人民政府统计机构审批；由县级以上地方人民政府有关部门制定的，报本级人民政府统计机构审批。

**Article 14** National statistical survey projects shall be formulated by the National Bureau of Statistics, or jointly by the National Bureau of Statistics and the relevant departments of the State Council, and shall be submitted to the State Council for recordation; major national statistical survey projects shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.  
Departmental statistical survey projects shall be formulated by the relevant departments of the State Council. Where the respondents of a statistical survey are within the departmental system under the jurisdiction of the department, the project shall be submitted to the National Bureau of Statistics for recordation; where the respondents exceed the departmental system under the jurisdiction of the department, the project shall be submitted to the National Bureau of Statistics for approval.  
Local statistical survey projects shall be formulated separately or jointly by the statistical institutions and relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level. Among them, those formulated solely by the statistical institution of a provincial people's government or jointly with relevant departments shall be submitted to the National Bureau of Statistics for approval; those formulated solely by a statistical institution of a people's government below the provincial level or jointly with relevant departments shall be submitted to the statistical institution of the provincial people's government for approval; those formulated by the relevant departments of a local people's government at or above the county level shall be submitted to the statistical institution of the people's government at the same level for approval.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　统计调查项目的审批机关应当对调查项目的必要性、可行性、科学性进行审查，对符合法定条件的，作出予以批准的书面决定，并公布；对不符合法定条件的，作出不予批准的书面决定，并说明理由。

**Article 15** The approving authority for statistical survey projects shall examine the necessity, feasibility, and scientific nature of the survey project; for those meeting the statutory conditions, it shall make a written decision to approve and publish it; for those not meeting the statutory conditions, it shall make a written decision not to approve and state the reasons.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　制定统计调查项目，应当同时制定该项目的统计调查制度，并依照[本法第十四条](#art-14)的规定一并报经审批或者备案。  
统计调查制度应当对调查目的、调查内容、调查方法、调查对象、调查组织方式、调查表式、统计资料的报送和公布等作出规定。  
统计调查应当按照统计调查制度组织实施。变更统计调查制度的内容，应当报经原审批机关批准或者原备案机关备案。

**Article 16** When formulating a statistical survey project, the statistical survey system for that project shall also be formulated, and shall be submitted together for approval or recordation in accordance with Article 14 of this Law.  
The statistical survey system shall provide for the purpose, content, method, respondents, organization method, survey form, and the submission and publication of statistical data, among others.  
Statistical surveys shall be organized and implemented in accordance with the statistical survey system. Any change to the content of a statistical survey system shall be submitted to the original approving authority for approval or the original recordation authority for recordation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　统计调查表应当标明表号、制定机关、批准或者备案文号、有效期限等标志。  
对未标明前款规定的标志或者超过有效期限的统计调查表，统计调查对象有权拒绝填报；县级以上人民政府统计机构应当依法责令停止有关统计调查活动。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国统计法实施条例.md#art-15" title="被《中华人民共和国统计法实施条例》第15条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 17** A statistical survey form shall bear markings such as the form number, the formulating authority, the approval or recordation document number, and the validity period.  
For a statistical survey form that does not bear the markings specified in the preceding paragraph or exceeds the validity period, the respondent shall have the right to refuse to fill it in; the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level shall, according to law, order the cessation of the relevant statistical survey activities.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　搜集、整理统计资料，应当以周期性普查为基础，以经常性抽样调查为主体，综合运用全面调查、重点调查等方法，并充分利用行政记录、社会大数据等资料。  
重大国情国力普查由国务院统一领导，国务院和地方人民政府组织统计机构和有关部门共同实施。

**Article 18** The collection and compilation of statistical data shall be based on periodic censuses, with regular sample surveys as the main body, comprehensively using methods such as comprehensive surveys and key surveys, and making full use of administrative records, social big data, and other data.  
Major censuses of national conditions and national strength shall be uniformly led by the State Council, and the State Council and local people's governments shall organize statistical institutions and relevant departments to jointly implement them.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家制定统一的统计标准，保障统计调查采用的指标涵义、计算方法、分类目录、调查表式和统计编码等的标准化。  
国家统计标准由国家统计局制定，或者由国家统计局和国务院标准化主管部门共同制定。  
国务院有关部门可以制定补充性的部门统计标准，报国家统计局审批。部门统计标准不得与国家统计标准相抵触。

**Article 19** The state shall formulate unified statistical standards to ensure the standardization of the meanings of indicators, calculation methods, classification catalogs, survey forms, and statistical codes used in statistical surveys.  
National statistical standards shall be formulated by the National Bureau of Statistics, or jointly by the National Bureau of Statistics and the standardization administrative department of the State Council.  
Relevant departments of the State Council may formulate supplementary departmental statistical standards, which shall be submitted to the National Bureau of Statistics for approval. Departmental statistical standards shall not conflict with national statistical standards.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家实施统一的国民经济核算制度。  
国家统计局统一组织和实施地区生产总值核算工作。

**Article 20** The state shall implement a unified system of national economic accounting.  
The National Bureau of Statistics shall uniformly organize and implement the calculation of gross domestic product (GDP) by region.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上人民政府统计机构根据统计任务的需要，可以在统计调查对象中推广使用计算机网络报送统计资料。

**Article 21** The statistical agencies of the people's governments at or above the county level may, based on the needs of statistical tasks, promote the use of computer networks for submitting statistical data among the respondents of statistical surveys.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级以上人民政府应当将统计工作所需经费列入财政预算。  
重大国情国力普查所需经费，由国务院和地方人民政府共同负担，列入相应年度的财政预算，按时拨付，确保到位。

**Article 22** The people's governments at or above the county level shall include the funds required for statistical work in the fiscal budget.  
The funds required for major censuses on national conditions and national strength shall be borne jointly by the State Council and local people's governments, and shall be included in the fiscal budgets for the corresponding years, allocated on time, and ensured to be in place.

### 第三章　统计资料的管理和公布

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门以及乡、镇人民政府，应当按照国家有关规定建立统计资料的保存、管理制度。  
县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门建立健全统计信息共享机制，明确统计信息的共享范围、标准和程序。

**Article 23** The statistical agencies and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, as well as the people's governments of townships and towns, shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, establish a system for the preservation and management of statistical data.  
The statistical agencies and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve mechanisms for sharing statistical information, and shall define the scope, standards, and procedures for sharing statistical information.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家机关、企业事业单位和其他组织等统计调查对象，应当按照国家有关规定设置原始记录、统计台账，推动统计台账电子化、数字化、标准化，建立健全统计资料的审核、签署、报送、归档等管理制度。  
统计资料的审核、签署人员应当对其审核、签署的统计资料的真实性、准确性和完整性负责。

**Article 24** Respondents of statistical surveys such as State organs, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, set up original records and statistical ledgers, promote the electronic, digital, and standardized management of statistical ledgers, and establish and improve a management system for the review, signing, submission, and archiving of statistical data.  
The personnel responsible for reviewing and signing statistical data shall bear responsibility for the authenticity, accuracy, and completeness of the statistical data they have reviewed and signed.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上人民政府有关部门应当及时向本级人民政府统计机构提供统计所需的行政记录资料和国民经济核算所需的财务资料、财政资料及其他资料，并按照统计调查制度的规定及时向本级人民政府统计机构报送其组织实施统计调查取得的有关资料。  
县级以上人民政府统计机构应当及时向本级人民政府有关部门提供有关统计资料。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国统计法实施条例.md#art-29" title="被《中华人民共和国统计法实施条例》第29条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 25** The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in a timely manner, provide the statistical agencies of the people's governments at the same level with administrative records data required for statistics and financial data, fiscal data, and other data required for national economic accounting, and shall, in accordance with the provisions of the statistical survey system, submit in a timely manner to the statistical agencies of the people's governments at the same level the relevant data obtained from the statistical surveys they organize and implement.  
The statistical agencies of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in a timely manner, provide the relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level with relevant statistical data.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上人民政府统计机构按照国家有关规定，定期公布统计资料。  
国家统计数据以国家统计局公布的数据为准。

**Article 26** The statistical agencies of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, regularly publish statistical data.  
National statistical data shall be subject to the data published by the National Bureau of Statistics.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上人民政府有关部门统计调查取得的统计资料，由本部门按照国家有关规定公布。

**Article 27** The statistical data obtained from statistical surveys conducted by the relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be published by such departments in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　统计调查中获得的能够识别或者推断单个统计调查对象身份的资料，任何单位和个人不得对外提供、泄露，不得用于统计以外的目的。

**Article 28** No unit or individual may provide or disclose to the outside any data obtained in statistical surveys that can identify or infer the identity of an individual statistical survey respondent, and may not use such data for purposes other than statistics.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门统计调查取得的统计资料，除依法应当保密的外，应当及时公开，供社会公众查询。

**Article 29** The statistical data obtained from statistical surveys conducted by the statistical agencies and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, except those that shall be kept confidential according to law, be disclosed in a timely manner for public inquiry.

### 第四章　统计机构和统计人员

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国务院设立国家统计局，依法组织领导和协调全国的统计工作。  
国家统计局根据工作需要设立的派出调查机构，承担国家统计局布置的统计调查等任务。  
县级以上地方人民政府设立独立的统计机构，乡、镇人民政府设置统计工作岗位，配备专职或者兼职统计人员，依法管理、开展统计工作，实施统计调查。

**Article 30** The State Council shall establish the National Bureau of Statistics, which shall, according to law, organize, lead, and coordinate statistical work nationwide.  
The dispatched survey agencies established by the National Bureau of Statistics as needed for work shall undertake tasks such as statistical surveys assigned by the National Bureau of Statistics.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish independent statistical agencies, and the people's governments of townships and towns shall set up statistical work positions and assign full-time or part-time statistical personnel to manage and carry out statistical work according to law and implement statistical surveys.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　县级以上人民政府有关部门根据统计任务的需要设立统计机构，或者在有关机构中设置统计人员，并指定统计负责人，依法组织、管理本部门职责范围内的统计工作，实施统计调查，在统计业务上受本级人民政府统计机构的指导。

**Article 31** The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the needs of statistical tasks, establish statistical agencies or assign statistical personnel within relevant agencies, and designate statistical responsible persons, to organize and manage statistical work within their respective responsibilities according to law, implement statistical surveys, and shall be subject to the guidance of the statistical agencies of the people's governments at the same level in terms of statistical operations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　统计机构、统计人员应当依法履行职责，如实搜集、报送统计资料，不得伪造、篡改统计资料，不得以任何方式要求任何单位和个人提供不真实的统计资料，不得有其他违反本法规定的行为。  
统计人员应当坚持实事求是，恪守职业道德，对其负责搜集、审核、录入的统计资料与统计调查对象报送的统计资料的一致性负责。

**Article 32** Statistical agencies and statistical personnel shall perform their duties according to law, collect and submit statistical data truthfully, and shall not forge or tamper with statistical data, shall not in any way require any unit or individual to provide untrue statistical data, and shall not have any other acts that violate the provisions of this Law.  
Statistical personnel shall adhere to the principle of seeking truth from facts, abide by professional ethics, and shall be responsible for the consistency between the statistical data they are responsible for collecting, reviewing, and entering and the statistical data submitted by the respondents of statistical surveys.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　统计人员进行统计调查时，有权就与统计有关的问题询问有关人员，要求其如实提供有关情况、资料并改正不真实、不准确的资料。  
统计人员进行统计调查时，应当出示县级以上人民政府统计机构或者有关部门颁发的工作证件；未出示的，统计调查对象有权拒绝调查。

**Article 33** When conducting statistical surveys, statistical personnel shall have the right to inquire with relevant persons about issues related to statistics, and to require them to truthfully provide relevant information and data and to correct untrue or inaccurate data.  
When conducting statistical surveys, statistical personnel shall present their work credentials issued by the statistical agency or relevant department of the people's government at or above the county level; if not presented, the respondents of statistical surveys shall have the right to refuse the survey.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家实行统计专业技术职务资格考试、评聘制度，提高统计人员的专业素质，保障统计队伍的稳定性。  
统计人员应当具备与其从事的统计工作相适应的专业知识和业务能力。  
县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门应当加强对统计人员的专业培训和职业道德教育。

**Article 34** The State shall implement a system for examinations, evaluation, and appointment for professional statistical technical positions, so as to improve the professional quality of statistical personnel and ensure the stability of the statistical workforce.  
Statistical personnel shall possess the professional knowledge and operational capabilities commensurate with the statistical work they engage in.  
The statistical agencies and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the professional training and professional ethics education of statistical personnel.

### 第五章　监督检查

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上人民政府对下级人民政府、本级人民政府统计机构和有关部门执行本法的情况，实施监督。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国统计法实施条例.md#art-53" title="被《中华人民共和国统计法实施条例》第53条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 35** The people's governments at or above the county level shall supervise the implementation of this Law by the people's governments at lower levels, the statistical agencies of the people's governments at the same level, and the relevant departments.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家统计局组织管理全国统计工作的监督检查，查处重大统计违法行为。  
县级以上地方人民政府统计机构依法查处本行政区域内发生的统计违法行为。但是，国家统计局派出的调查机构组织实施的统计调查活动中发生的统计违法行为，由组织实施该项统计调查的调查机构负责查处。  
法律、行政法规对有关部门查处统计违法行为另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 36** The National Bureau of Statistics shall organize and manage the supervision and inspection of statistical work nationwide, and investigate and punish major illegal statistical acts.  
The statistical agencies of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, investigate and punish illegal statistical acts occurring within their respective administrative regions. However, if an illegal statistical act occurs in a statistical survey activity organized and implemented by a survey agency dispatched by the National Bureau of Statistics, the survey agency that organized and implemented the statistical survey shall be responsible for investigating and punishing it.  
Where laws and administrative regulations provide otherwise for the investigation and punishment of illegal statistical acts by relevant departments, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　县级以上人民政府有关部门应当积极协助本级人民政府统计机构查处统计违法行为，及时向本级人民政府统计机构移送有关统计违法案件材料。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国统计法实施条例.md#art-40" title="被《中华人民共和国统计法实施条例》第40条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 37** The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall actively assist the statistical agencies of the people's governments at the same level in investigating and punishing illegal statistical acts, and shall timely transfer relevant case materials concerning illegal statistical acts to the statistical agencies of the people's governments at the same level.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　县级以上人民政府统计机构在调查统计违法行为或者核查统计数据时，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）发出统计检查查询书，向检查对象查询有关事项；  
（二）要求检查对象提供有关原始记录和凭证、统计台账、统计调查表、会计资料及其他相关证明和资料；  
（三）就与检查有关的事项询问有关人员；  
（四）进入检查对象的业务场所和统计数据处理信息系统进行检查、核对；  
（五）经本机构负责人批准，登记保存检查对象的有关原始记录和凭证、统计台账、统计调查表、会计资料及其他相关证明和资料；  
（六）对与检查事项有关的情况和资料进行记录、录音、录像、照相和复制。  
县级以上人民政府统计机构进行监督检查时，监督检查人员不得少于二人，并应当出示执法证件；未出示的，有关单位和个人有权拒绝检查。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国统计法实施条例.md#art-49" title="被《中华人民共和国统计法实施条例》第49条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 38** When investigating illegal statistical acts or verifying statistical data, the statistical agencies of the people's governments at or above the county level shall have the right to take the following measures:  
(1) issue a statistical inspection inquiry letter to inquire about relevant matters from the inspection target;  
(2) require the inspection target to provide relevant original records and vouchers, statistical ledgers, statistical survey forms, accounting materials, and other relevant certifications and materials;  
(3) ask relevant persons questions concerning matters related to the inspection;  
(4) enter the business premises and statistical data processing information systems of the inspection target to conduct inspections and verifications;  
(5) with the approval of the head of the agency, register and preserve the relevant original records and vouchers, statistical ledgers, statistical survey forms, accounting materials, and other relevant certifications and materials of the inspection target;  
(6) record, audio-record, video-record, photograph, and copy the circumstances and materials related to the inspection matters.  
When the statistical agencies of the people's governments at or above the county level conduct supervision and inspection, the number of supervision and inspection personnel shall not be less than two, and they shall present their law enforcement credentials; if not presented, the relevant units and individuals shall have the right to refuse the inspection.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上人民政府统计机构履行监督检查职责时，有关单位和个人应当如实反映情况，提供相关证明和资料，不得拒绝、阻碍检查，不得转移、隐匿、篡改、毁弃原始记录和凭证、统计台账、统计调查表、会计资料及其他相关证明和资料。

**Article 39** When the statistical agencies of the people's governments at or above the county level perform their duties of supervision and inspection, the relevant units and individuals shall truthfully reflect the situations, provide relevant certifications and materials, and shall not refuse or obstruct the inspection, and shall not transfer, conceal, tamper with, or destroy original records and vouchers, statistical ledgers, statistical survey forms, accounting materials, and other relevant certifications and materials.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　地方各级人民政府、县级以上人民政府统计机构或者有关部门、单位的负责人有下列行为之一的，由任免机关、单位或者监察机关依法给予处分，并由县级以上人民政府统计机构予以通报：  
（一）自行修改统计资料、编造虚假统计数据的；  
（二）要求统计机构、统计人员或者其他机构、人员伪造、篡改统计资料的；  
（三）明示、暗示下级单位及其人员或者统计调查对象填报虚假统计数据的；  
（四）对本地方、本部门、本单位发生的统计数据严重失实情况和严重统计违法行为失察的；  
（五）有其他统计造假、弄虚作假行为的。  
对依法履行职责或者拒绝、抵制统计违法行为的单位和个人打击报复的，依照前款规定给予处分和予以通报。

**Article 40** Where a person in charge of a local people's government at any level, a statistical agency of a people's government at or above the county level, or a relevant department or unit commits any of the following acts, the appointing authority, unit, or supervisory authority shall impose a sanction according to law, and the statistical agency of the people's government at or above the county level shall circulate a notice thereof:  
(1) modifying statistical data without authorization or fabricating false statistical data;  
(2) requiring a statistical agency, statistical personnel, or other agency or personnel to forge or tamper with statistical data;  
(3) explicitly or implicitly suggesting that a subordinate unit or its personnel, or a respondent of a statistical survey, fill in false statistical data;  
(4) failing to detect serious inaccuracy in statistical data or serious illegal statistical acts occurring in the locality, department, or unit;  
(5) committing any other act of statistical fraud or falsification.  
If a unit or individual that has performed their duties according to law or has refused or resisted illegal statistical acts is retaliated against, the sanctions and notice as provided in the preceding paragraph shall be imposed.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　县级以上人民政府统计机构或者有关部门有下列行为之一的，由本级人民政府、上级人民政府统计机构或者本级人民政府统计机构责令改正，予以通报；对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员，由任免机关或者监察机关依法给予处分：  
（一）未经批准或者备案擅自组织实施统计调查的；  
（二）未经批准或者备案擅自变更统计调查制度的内容的；  
（三）伪造、篡改统计资料的；  
（四）要求统计调查对象或者其他机构、人员提供不真实的统计资料的；  
（五）未按照统计调查制度的规定报送有关资料的。  
统计人员有前款第三项至第五项所列行为之一的，责令改正，依法给予处分。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/中华人民共和国统计法实施条例.md#art-50" title="被《中华人民共和国统计法实施条例》第50条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 41** Where a statistical institution of a people's government at or above the county level or a relevant department has any of the following acts, the people's government at the same level, the statistical institution of the people's government at a higher level, or the statistical institution of the people's government at the same level shall order it to make corrections and circulate a notice of criticism; the leading person liable and the directly liable person shall be given a sanction according to law by the appointing and removing organ or the supervisory organ:  
(1) organizing a statistical investigation without approval or filing;  
(2) changing the contents of a statistical investigation system without approval or filing;  
(3) forging or tampering with statistical data;  
(4) requiring a respondent to a statistical investigation or any other institution or person to provide untrue statistical data; or  
(5) failing to submit relevant data as provided for in a statistical investigation system.  
Where a statistician has any of the acts listed in items (3) to (5) of the preceding paragraph, the statistician shall be ordered to make corrections and shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　县级以上人民政府统计机构或者有关部门有下列行为之一的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员由任免机关或者监察机关依法给予处分：  
（一）违法公布统计资料的；  
（二）泄露或者向他人非法提供统计调查对象的商业秘密、个人隐私、个人信息的；  
（三）对外提供、泄露在统计调查中获得的能够识别或者推断单个统计调查对象身份的资料的；  
（四）违反国家有关规定，造成统计资料毁损、灭失的。  
统计人员有前款所列行为之一的，依法给予处分。

**Article 42** Where a statistical institution of a people's government at or above the county level or a relevant department has any of the following acts, the leading person liable and the directly liable person shall be given a sanction according to law by the appointing and removing organ or the supervisory organ:  
(1) illegally making public statistical data;  
(2) divulging or illegally providing to others the trade secrets, personal privacy, or personal information of a respondent to a statistical investigation;  
(3) providing or divulging to an outsider any data obtained in a statistical investigation that can identify or infer the identity of an individual respondent to the statistical investigation; or  
(4) causing damage to or loss of statistical data in violation of the relevant provisions of the State.  
Where a statistician has any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, the statistician shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　统计机构、统计人员泄露国家秘密、工作秘密的，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 43** Where a statistical institution or statistician divulges State secrets or work secrets, the offender shall be investigated for legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　作为统计调查对象的国家机关、企业事业单位或者其他组织有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府统计机构责令改正，给予警告，可以予以通报；其负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员属于公职人员的，由任免机关、单位或者监察机关依法给予处分：  
（一）拒绝提供统计资料或者经催报后仍未按时提供统计资料的；  
（二）提供不真实或者不完整的统计资料的；  
（三）拒绝答复或者不如实答复统计检查查询书的；  
（四）拒绝、阻碍统计调查、统计检查的；  
（五）转移、隐匿、篡改、毁弃或者拒绝提供原始记录和凭证、统计台账、统计调查表及其他相关证明和资料的。  
企业事业单位或者其他组织有前款所列行为之一的，可以并处十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。  
个体工商户有本条第一款所列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府统计机构责令改正，给予警告，可以并处一万元以下的罚款。

**Article 44** Where a State organ, enterprise, public institution or any other organization that is a respondent to a statistical investigation has any of the following acts, the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections, give it a warning, and may circulate a notice of criticism; if the leading person liable and the directly liable person are public servants, they shall be given a sanction according to law by the appointing and removing organ, entity, or the supervisory organ:  
(1) refusing to provide statistical data or failing to provide statistical data on time after being urged;  
(2) providing untrue or incomplete statistical data;  
(3) refusing to respond to or failing to truthfully respond to a statistical inspection inquiry letter;  
(4) refusing or obstructing a statistical investigation or statistical inspection; or  
(5) transferring, concealing, tampering with, destroying, or refusing to provide original records and vouchers, statistical ledgers, statistical investigation forms, and other relevant certificates and data.  
Where an enterprise, public institution or any other organization has any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan may also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall also be imposed.  
Where an individual industrial or commercial household that is a respondent to a statistical investigation has any of the acts listed in paragraph 1 of this Article, the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections, give it a warning, and may also impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　作为统计调查对象的国家机关、企业事业单位或者其他组织迟报统计资料，或者未按照国家有关规定设置原始记录、统计台账的，由县级以上人民政府统计机构责令改正，给予警告，可以予以通报；其负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员属于公职人员的，由任免机关、单位或者监察机关依法给予处分。  
企业事业单位或者其他组织有前款所列行为之一的，可以并处五万元以下的罚款。  
个体工商户迟报统计资料的，由县级以上人民政府统计机构责令改正，给予警告，可以并处一千元以下的罚款。

**Article 45** Where a State organ, enterprise, public institution or any other organization that is a respondent to a statistical investigation delays in submitting statistical data, or fails to set up original records or statistical ledgers in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections, give it a warning, and may circulate a notice of criticism; if the leading person liable and the directly liable person are public servants, they shall be given a sanction according to law by the appointing and removing organ, entity, or the supervisory organ.  
Where an enterprise, public institution or any other organization has any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may also be imposed.  
Where an individual industrial or commercial household that is a respondent to a statistical investigation delays in submitting statistical data, the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections, give it a warning, and may also impose a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上人民政府统计机构查处统计违法行为时，认为对有关公职人员依法应当给予处分的，应当向该公职人员的任免机关、单位提出给予处分的建议，该公职人员的任免机关、单位应当依法及时作出决定，并将结果书面通知县级以上人民政府统计机构；向监察机关移送的，由监察机关按照有关规定办理。

**Article 46** When investigating and handling illegal acts in statistics, if the statistical institution of a people's government at or above the county level considers that a relevant public servant shall be given a sanction according to law, it shall propose giving a sanction to the appointing and removing organ or entity of the public servant; the appointing and removing organ or entity of the public servant shall make a decision promptly according to law and notify the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level of the result in writing; if the case is transferred to the supervisory organ, the supervisory organ shall handle it in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　作为统计调查对象的个人在重大国情国力普查活动中拒绝、阻碍统计调查，或者提供不真实或者不完整的普查资料的，由县级以上人民政府统计机构责令改正，予以批评教育。

**Article 47** Where an individual who is a respondent to a statistical investigation in a major national census refuses or obstructs the statistical investigation, or provides untrue or incomplete census data, the statistical institution of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the individual to make corrections and give the individual criticism and education.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本法规定，利用虚假统计资料骗取荣誉称号、物质利益或者职务职级等晋升的，除对其编造虚假统计资料或者要求他人编造虚假统计资料的行为依法追究法律责任外，由作出有关决定的单位或者其上级单位、监察机关取消其荣誉称号，追缴获得的物质利益，撤销晋升的职务职级等。

**Article 48** Where a person, in violation of the provisions of this Law, uses false statistical data to fraudulently obtain honorary titles, material benefits, or promotions in positions or ranks, in addition to investigating the legal liability according to law for the act of fabricating false statistical data or requiring others to fabricate false statistical data, the entity that made the relevant decision, its superior entity, or the supervisory organ shall cancel the honorary titles, recover the material benefits obtained, and revoke the promotions in positions or ranks.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　当事人对县级以上人民政府统计机构作出的行政处罚决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。对国家统计局派出的调查机构作出的行政处罚决定不服的，向国家统计局申请行政复议。

**Article 49** If a party disagrees with the administrative penalty decision made by the statistical institution of a people's government at or above the county level, the party may apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate an administrative action according to law. If a party disagrees with the administrative penalty decision made by an investigation agency dispatched by the National Bureau of Statistics, the party shall apply for administrative reconsideration to the National Bureau of Statistics.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本法规定，造成人身损害、财产损失的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 50** Where a person, in violation of the provisions of this Law, causes personal injury or property damage, the person shall bear civil liability according to law; if a crime is constituted, the person shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　本法所称县级以上人民政府统计机构，是指国家统计局及其派出的调查机构、县级以上地方人民政府统计机构。

**Article 51** For the purposes of this Law, the term statistical institution of a people's government at or above the county level means the National Bureau of Statistics and its dispatched investigation agencies, as well as the statistical institutions of local people's governments at or above the county level.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　民间统计调查活动的管理办法，由国务院制定。  
中华人民共和国境外的组织、个人需要在中华人民共和国境内进行统计调查活动的，应当按照国务院的规定报请审批。  
利用统计调查危害国家安全、损害社会公共利益或者进行欺诈活动的，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 52** The measures for the administration of non-governmental statistical investigation activities shall be formulated by the State Council.  
Where an organization or individual outside the territory of the People's Republic of China needs to carry out a statistical investigation activity within the territory of the People's Republic of China, the organization or individual shall submit an application for examination and approval in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.  
Where a person uses a statistical investigation to endanger national security, harm the public interest, or conduct fraudulent activities, the person shall be investigated for legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　本法自2010年1月1日起施行。

**Article 53** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2010.
