# 最高人民法院关于审理道路交通事故损害赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2012〕19号  
**公布日期**：2012-11-27  
**生效日期**：2012-12-21

> 最高人民法院《关于审理道路交通事故损害赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2012年9月17日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1556次会议通过，现予公布，自2012年12月21日起施行。 2012年11月27日 最高人民法院 关于审理道路交通事故损害赔偿案件适用 法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2012〕19号 （2012年9月17日最高人民法院审判委员会第1556次会议通过　2012年11月27日最高人民法院公告公布　自2012年12月21日起施行）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　机动车发生交通事故造成损害，机动车所有人或者管理人有下列情形之一，人民法院应当认定其对损害的发生有过错，并适用侵权责任法第四十九条的规定确定其相应的赔偿责任：  
（一）知道或者应当知道机动车存在缺陷，且该缺陷是交通事故发生原因之一的；  
（二）知道或者应当知道驾驶人无驾驶资格或者未取得相应驾驶资格的；  
（三）知道或者应当知道驾驶人因饮酒、服用国家管制的精神药品或者麻醉药品，或者患有妨碍安全驾驶机动车的疾病等依法不能驾驶机动车的；  
（四）其它应当认定机动车所有人或者管理人有过错的。

**Article 1** Where a motor vehicle causes damage in a traffic accident, and the owner or manager of the motor vehicle falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall find that it is at fault for the occurrence of the damage and determine its corresponding compensation liability in accordance with Article 49 of the Tort Law:  
(1) It knew or should have known that the motor vehicle has a defect, and such defect is one of the causes of the traffic accident;  
(2) It knew or should have known that the driver had no driving qualification or had not obtained the corresponding driving qualification;  
(3) It knew or should have known that the driver could not legally drive the motor vehicle due to alcohol consumption, use of psychotropic substances or narcotic drugs controlled by the State, or suffering from a disease that impedes safe driving of the motor vehicle;  
(4) Other circumstances where the owner or manager of the motor vehicle shall be found to be at fault.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　未经允许驾驶他人机动车发生交通事故造成损害，当事人依照侵权责任法第四十九条的规定请求由机动车驾驶人承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。机动车所有人或者管理人有过错的，承担相应的赔偿责任，但具有侵权责任法第五十二条规定情形的除外。

**Article 2** Where a person driving another's motor vehicle without permission causes damage in a traffic accident, and the party requests that the motor vehicle driver bear compensation liability in accordance with Article 49 of the Tort Law, the People's Court shall support such request. If the motor vehicle owner or manager is at fault, it shall bear corresponding compensation liability, except in the circumstances set forth in Article 52 of the Tort Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　以挂靠形式从事道路运输经营活动的机动车发生交通事故造成损害，属于该机动车一方责任，当事人请求由挂靠人和被挂靠人承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 3** Where a motor vehicle engaged in road transport business operations under a affiliation arrangement causes damage in a traffic accident, and the liability belongs to the motor vehicle party, if the party requests that the affiliated operator and the entity with which it is affiliated bear joint and several liability, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　被多次转让但未办理转移登记的机动车发生交通事故造成损害，属于该机动车一方责任，当事人请求由最后一次转让并交付的受让人承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 4** Where a motor vehicle that has been transferred multiple times without undergoing transfer registration causes damage in a traffic accident, and the liability belongs to the motor vehicle party, if the party requests that the transferee of the last transfer and delivery bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　套牌机动车发生交通事故造成损害，属于该机动车一方责任，当事人请求由套牌机动车的所有人或者管理人承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持；被套牌机动车所有人或者管理人同意套牌的，应当与套牌机动车的所有人或者管理人承担连带责任。

**Article 5** Where a motor vehicle with a counterfeit license plate causes damage in a traffic accident, and the liability belongs to such motor vehicle party, if the party requests that the owner or manager of the motor vehicle with the counterfeit license plate bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall support such request; if the owner or manager of the motor vehicle whose license plate is counterfeited consents to the use of such counterfeit plate, it shall bear joint and several liability with the owner or manager of the motor vehicle with the counterfeit license plate.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　拼装车、已达到报废标准的机动车或者依法禁止行驶的其他机动车被多次转让，并发生交通事故造成损害，当事人请求由所有的转让人和受让人承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 6** Where a modified vehicle, a motor vehicle that has reached the scrapping standard, or any other motor vehicle prohibited from traveling according to law is transferred multiple times and causes damage in a traffic accident, if the party requests that all transferors and transferees bear joint and several liability, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　接受机动车驾驶培训的人员，在培训活动中驾驶机动车发生交通事故造成损害，属于该机动车一方责任，当事人请求驾驶培训单位承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 7** Where a person receiving motor vehicle driving training causes damage in a traffic accident while driving a motor vehicle during a training activity, and the liability belongs to such motor vehicle party, if the party requests that the driving training institution bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　机动车试乘过程中发生交通事故造成试乘人损害，当事人请求提供试乘服务者承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。试乘人有过错的，应当减轻提供试乘服务者的赔偿责任。

**Article 8** Where a traffic accident occurs during a test ride of a motor vehicle, causing damage to the test rider, if the party requests that the provider of the test ride service bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall support such request. If the test rider is at fault, the compensation liability of the provider of the test ride service shall be mitigated.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　因道路管理维护缺陷导致机动车发生交通事故造成损害，当事人请求道路管理者承担相应赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持，但道路管理者能够证明已按照法律、法规、规章、国家标准、行业标准或者地方标准尽到安全防护、警示等管理维护义务的除外。  
依法不得进入高速公路的车辆、行人，进入高速公路发生交通事故造成自身损害，当事人请求高速公路管理者承担赔偿责任的，适用侵权责任法第七十六条的规定。

**Article 9** Where damage is caused by a traffic accident involving a motor vehicle due to defects in road management and maintenance, and the party requests that the road manager bear corresponding compensation liability, the People's Court shall support such request, unless the road manager can prove that it has fulfilled its management and maintenance obligations such as safety protection and warning in accordance with laws, regulations, rules, national standards, industry standards or local standards.  
For vehicles or pedestrians that are legally prohibited from entering expressways, if they cause damage to themselves in a traffic accident after entering the expressway, and the party requests that the expressway manager bear compensation liability, the provisions of Article 76 of the Tort Law shall apply.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　因在道路上堆放、倾倒、遗撒物品等妨碍通行的行为，导致交通事故造成损害，当事人请求行为人承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。道路管理者不能证明已按照法律、法规、规章、国家标准、行业标准或者地方标准尽到清理、防护、警示等义务的，应当承担相应的赔偿责任。

**Article 10** Where damage is caused by a traffic accident due to acts such as piling, dumping, or spilling articles on the road that obstruct traffic, and the party requests that the actor bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall support such request. If the road manager cannot prove that it has fulfilled its obligations such as cleaning, protection, and warning in accordance with laws, regulations, rules, national standards, industry standards or local standards, it shall bear corresponding compensation liability.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　未按照法律、法规、规章或者国家标准、行业标准、地方标准的强制性规定设计、施工，致使道路存在缺陷并造成交通事故，当事人请求建设单位与施工单位承担相应赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 11** Where a road has defects due to design or construction that does not comply with the mandatory provisions of laws, regulations, rules, or national standards, industry standards, or local standards, and such defects cause a traffic accident, if the party requests that the construction developer and the construction unit bear corresponding compensation liability, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　机动车存在产品缺陷导致交通事故造成损害，当事人请求生产者或者销售者依照侵权责任法第五章的规定承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 12** Where a motor vehicle has a product defect that causes a traffic accident resulting in damage, if the party requests that the producer or seller bear compensation liability in accordance with the provisions of Chapter V of the Tort Law, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　多辆机动车发生交通事故造成第三人损害，当事人请求多个侵权人承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应当区分不同情况，依照侵权责任法第十条、第十一条或者第十二条的规定，确定侵权人承担连带责任或者按份责任。  
二、关于赔偿范围的认定

**Article 13** Where multiple motor vehicles cause damage to a third party in a traffic accident, and the party requests that multiple tortfeasors bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall distinguish different circumstances and determine, in accordance with Article 10, Article 11 or Article 12 of the Tort Law, whether the tortfeasors bear joint and several liability or proportionate liability.  
II. Determination of the Scope of Compensation

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　[道路交通安全法第七十六条](../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国道路交通安全法.md#art-76)规定的“人身伤亡”，是指机动车发生交通事故侵害被侵权人的生命权、健康权等人身权益所造成的损害，包括侵权责任法第十六条和第二十二条规定的各项损害。  
[道路交通安全法第七十六条](../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国道路交通安全法.md#art-76)规定的“财产损失”，是指因机动车发生交通事故侵害被侵权人的财产权益所造成的损失。

**Article 14** The term "personal injury or death" as provided in Article 76 of the Road Traffic Safety Law means damage caused by a motor vehicle traffic accident infringing upon the victim's personal rights and interests such as the right to life and the right to health, including various damages as provided in Articles 16 and 22 of the Tort Law.  
The term "property damage" as provided in Article 76 of the Road Traffic Safety Law means losses caused by a motor vehicle traffic accident infringing upon the victim's property rights and interests.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　因道路交通事故造成下列财产损失，当事人请求侵权人赔偿的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）维修被损坏车辆所支出的费用、车辆所载物品的损失、车辆施救费用；  
（二）因车辆灭失或者无法修复，为购买交通事故发生时与被损坏车辆价值相当的车辆重置费用；  
（三）依法从事货物运输、旅客运输等经营性活动的车辆，因无法从事相应经营活动所产生的合理停运损失；  
（四）非经营性车辆因无法继续使用，所产生的通常替代性交通工具的合理费用。  
三、关于责任承担的认定

**Article 15** For the following property losses caused by a road traffic accident, if the party requests compensation from the tortfeasor, the People's Court shall support such request:  
(1) Expenses incurred for repairing the damaged vehicle, losses of articles carried in the vehicle, and vehicle rescue costs;  
(2) Costs for replacement of a vehicle with a value equivalent to that of the damaged vehicle at the time of the traffic accident, due to the vehicle's destruction or irreparable condition;  
(3) Reasonable loss of operating income for vehicles legally engaged in commercial activities such as cargo transportation or passenger transport, due to inability to conduct corresponding operations;  
(4) Reasonable expenses for usual alternative transportation means, incurred due to the inability to continue using a non-commercial vehicle.  
III. Determination of Liability Assumption

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　同时投保机动车第三者责任强制保险（以下简称“交强险”）和第三者责任商业保险（以下简称“商业三者险”）的机动车发生交通事故造成损害，当事人同时起诉侵权人和保险公司的，人民法院应当按照下列规则确定赔偿责任：  
（一）先由承保交强险的保险公司在责任限额范围内予以赔偿；  
（二）不足部分，由承保商业三者险的保险公司根据保险合同予以赔偿；  
（三）仍有不足的，依照道路交通安全法和侵权责任法的相关规定由侵权人予以赔偿。  
被侵权人或者其近亲属请求承保交强险的保险公司优先赔偿精神损害的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 16** Where a motor vehicle that is simultaneously insured with compulsory motor vehicle third-party liability insurance (hereinafter referred to as "compulsory insurance") and commercial third-party liability insurance (hereinafter referred to as "commercial third-party insurance") causes damage in a traffic accident, and the party simultaneously sues the tortfeasor and the insurance company, the People's Court shall determine compensation liability in accordance with the following rules:  
(1) First, the insurance company underwriting the compulsory insurance shall provide compensation within the liability limit;  
(2) For the portion not covered, the insurance company underwriting the commercial third-party insurance shall provide compensation in accordance with the insurance contract;  
(3) For any remaining insufficiency, the tortfeasor shall provide compensation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Road Traffic Safety Law and the Tort Law.  
If the injured party or his close relatives request that the insurance company underwriting the compulsory insurance preferentially compensate for mental damage, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　投保人允许的驾驶人驾驶机动车致使投保人遭受损害，当事人请求承保交强险的保险公司在责任限额范围内予以赔偿的，人民法院应予支持，但投保人为本车上人员的除外。

**Article 17** Where a driver permitted by the policyholder drives the motor vehicle and causes damage to the policyholder, if the party requests that the insurance company underwriting the compulsory insurance provide compensation within the liability limit, the People's Court shall support such request, except where the policyholder is a person on board the vehicle.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　有下列情形之一导致第三人人身损害，当事人请求保险公司在交强险责任限额范围内予以赔偿，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）驾驶人未取得驾驶资格或者未取得相应驾驶资格的；  
（二）醉酒、服用国家管制的精神药品或者麻醉药品后驾驶机动车发生交通事故的；  
（三）驾驶人故意制造交通事故的。  
保险公司在赔偿范围内向侵权人主张追偿权的，人民法院应予支持。追偿权的诉讼时效期间自保险公司实际赔偿之日起计算。

**Article 18** Where any of the following circumstances causes personal injury to a third party, and the party requests that the insurance company provide compensation within the liability limit of the compulsory insurance, the People's Court shall support such request:  
(1) The driver has not obtained a driving qualification or has not obtained the corresponding driving qualification;  
(2) A traffic accident occurs due to driving after intoxication, or after using psychotropic substances or narcotic drugs controlled by the State;  
(3) The driver intentionally causes the traffic accident.  
If the insurance company claims recourse against the tortfeasor within the scope of compensation, the People's Court shall support such claim. The limitation of action for the right of recourse shall be calculated from the date when the insurance company actually pays compensation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　未依法投保交强险的机动车发生交通事故造成损害，当事人请求投保义务人在交强险责任限额范围内予以赔偿的，人民法院应予支持。  
投保义务人和侵权人不是同一人，当事人请求投保义务人和侵权人在交强险责任限额范围内承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 19** Where a motor vehicle that has not been insured with compulsory insurance according to law causes damage in a traffic accident, if the party requests that the person obligated to take out insurance provide compensation within the liability limit of the compulsory insurance, the People's Court shall support such request.  
If the person obligated to take out insurance and the tortfeasor are not the same person, and the party requests that the person obligated to take out insurance and the tortfeasor bear joint and several liability within the liability limit of the compulsory insurance, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　具有从事交强险业务资格的保险公司违法拒绝承保、拖延承保或者违法解除交强险合同，投保义务人在向第三人承担赔偿责任后，请求该保险公司在交强险责任限额范围内承担相应赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 20** Where an insurance company qualified to engage in compulsory insurance business illegally refuses to insure, delays in insuring, or illegally rescinds the compulsory insurance contract, and after the person obligated to take out insurance bears compensation liability to a third party, requests that such insurance company bear corresponding compensation liability within the liability limit of the compulsory insurance, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　多辆机动车发生交通事故造成第三人损害，损失超出各机动车交强险责任限额之和的，由各保险公司在各自责任限额范围内承担赔偿责任；损失未超出各机动车交强险责任限额之和，当事人请求由各保险公司按照其责任限额与责任限额之和的比例承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
依法分别投保交强险的牵引车和挂车连接使用时发生交通事故造成第三人损害，当事人请求由各保险公司在各自的责任限额范围内平均赔偿的，人民法院应予支持。  
多辆机动车发生交通事故造成第三人损害，其中部分机动车未投保交强险，当事人请求先由已承保交强险的保险公司在责任限额范围内予以赔偿的，人民法院应予支持。保险公司就超出其应承担的部分向未投保交强险的投保义务人或者侵权人行使追偿权的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 21** Where multiple motor vehicles cause damage to a third party in a traffic accident, if the losses exceed the sum of the liability limits of the compulsory insurance of each motor vehicle, each insurance company shall bear compensation liability within its respective liability limit; if the losses do not exceed the sum of the liability limits of the compulsory insurance of each motor vehicle, and the party requests that each insurance company bear compensation liability in proportion to its liability limit relative to the sum of the liability limits, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where a tractor and a trailer that are separately insured with compulsory insurance according to law are used together and cause damage to a third party in a traffic accident, if the party requests that each insurance company provide compensation in equal amount within its respective liability limit, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where multiple motor vehicles cause damage to a third party in a traffic accident, and some of the motor vehicles are not insured with compulsory insurance, if the party requests that the insurance companies that have underwritten compulsory insurance provide compensation within their liability limits first, the People's Court shall support such request. If an insurance company exercises a right of recourse against the person obligated to take out insurance or the tortfeasor of the uninsured motor vehicle for the portion exceeding its share, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　同一交通事故的多个被侵权人同时起诉的，人民法院应当按照各被侵权人的损失比例确定交强险的赔偿数额。

**Article 22** Where multiple injured parties in the same traffic accident sue simultaneously, the People's Court shall determine the compensation amount of compulsory insurance in proportion to the losses of each injured party.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　机动车所有权在交强险合同有效期内发生变动，保险公司在交通事故发生后，以该机动车未办理交强险合同变更手续为由主张免除赔偿责任的，人民法院不予支持。  
机动车在交强险合同有效期内发生改装、使用性质改变等导致危险程度增加的情形，发生交通事故后，当事人请求保险公司在责任限额范围内予以赔偿的，人民法院应予支持。  
前款情形下，保险公司另行起诉请求投保义务人按照重新核定后的保险费标准补足当期保险费的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 23** Where the ownership of a motor vehicle changes during the validity period of the compulsory insurance contract, if the insurance company, after the occurrence of a traffic accident, claims exemption from compensation liability on the ground that the change procedures for the compulsory insurance contract have not been completed, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
Where a motor vehicle undergoes modifications, changes in use nature, or other circumstances that increase the risk level during the validity period of the compulsory insurance contract, if after the traffic accident the party requests that the insurance company provide compensation within the liability limit, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Under the circumstances of the preceding paragraph, if the insurance company separately sues to request that the person obligated to take out insurance pay the deficiency of the current insurance premium according to the re-determined premium rate, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　当事人主张交强险人身伤亡保险金请求权转让或者设定担保的行为无效的，人民法院应予支持。  
四、关于诉讼程序的规定

**Article 24** Where a party claims that the act of transferring or creating a security interest in the compulsory insurance claim for personal injury or death is invalid, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
IV. Provisions on Litigation Procedures

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　人民法院审理道路交通事故损害赔偿案件，应当将承保交强险的保险公司列为共同被告。但该保险公司已经在交强险责任限额范围内予以赔偿且当事人无异议的除外。  
人民法院审理道路交通事故损害赔偿案件，当事人请求将承保商业三者险的保险公司列为共同被告的，人民法院应予准许。

**Article 25** When a People's Court hears a case concerning compensation for damage from a road traffic accident, it shall list the insurance company underwriting the compulsory insurance as a co-defendant, except where such insurance company has already provided compensation within the liability limit of the compulsory insurance and the parties have no objection.  
When a People's Court hears a case concerning compensation for damage from a road traffic accident, if a party requests that the insurance company underwriting the commercial third-party insurance be listed as a co-defendant, the People's Court shall grant such request.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　被侵权人因道路交通事故死亡，无近亲属或者近亲属不明，未经法律授权的机关或者有关组织向人民法院起诉主张死亡赔偿金的，人民法院不予受理。  
侵权人以已向未经法律授权的机关或者有关组织支付死亡赔偿金为理由，请求保险公司在交强险责任限额范围内予以赔偿的，人民法院不予支持。  
被侵权人因道路交通事故死亡，无近亲属或者近亲属不明，支付被侵权人医疗费、丧葬费等合理费用的单位或者个人，请求保险公司在交强险责任限额范围内予以赔偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 26** Where the injured party dies due to a road traffic accident, and has no close relatives or the close relatives are unknown, if an authority or an organization not authorized by law files a lawsuit with the People's Court claiming compensation for death, the People's Court shall not accept the case.  
If the tortfeasor requests the insurance company to provide compensation within the liability limit of the compulsory insurance on the ground that it has already paid compensation for death to an authority or organization not authorized by law, the People's Court shall not support such request.  
Where the injured party dies due to a road traffic accident, and has no close relatives or the close relatives are unknown, if a unit or individual that paid reasonable expenses such as medical expenses and funeral expenses for the injured party requests that the insurance company provide compensation within the liability limit of the compulsory insurance, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　公安机关交通管理部门制作的交通事故认定书，人民法院应依法审查并确认其相应的证明力，但有相反证据推翻的除外。  
五、关于适用范围的规定

**Article 27** For the traffic accident confirmation document produced by the traffic management department of the public security organ, the People's Court shall review and confirm its corresponding probative force according to law, unless contrary evidence overturns it.  
V. Provisions on Scope of Application

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　机动车在道路以外的地方通行时引发的损害赔偿案件，可以参照适用本解释的规定。

**Article 28** For a case concerning compensation for damage arising from a motor vehicle traveling in a place other than a road, the provisions of this Interpretation may be applied by reference.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本解释施行后尚未终审的案件，适用本解释；本解释施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本解释。

**Article 29** For cases for which a final judgment has not been rendered after this Interpretation comes into force, this Interpretation shall apply; for cases for which a final judgment has been rendered before this Interpretation comes into force, and for which a party applies for retrial or a retrial is decided in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, this Interpretation shall not apply.
