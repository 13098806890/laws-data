# 中华人民共和国公路法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2017-11-04  
**生效日期**：2017-11-05

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强公路的建设和管理，促进公路事业的发展，适应社会主义现代化建设和人民生活的需要，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of strengthening the construction and management of highways, promoting the development of highway undertakings, and meeting the needs of socialist modernization and the people's livelihood.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事公路的规划、建设、养护、经营、使用和管理，适用本法。  
本法所称公路，包括公路桥梁、公路隧道和公路渡口。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the planning, construction, maintenance, operation, use, and management of highways within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
For the purposes of this Law, 'highways' include highway bridges, highway tunnels, and highway ferries.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　公路的发展应当遵循全面规划、合理布局、确保质量、保障畅通、保护环境、建设改造与养护并重的原则。

**Article 3** The development of highways shall follow the principles of comprehensive planning, rational layout, ensuring quality, ensuring smooth traffic, protecting the environment, and placing equal emphasis on construction, reconstruction, and maintenance.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级人民政府应当采取有力措施，扶持、促进公路建设。公路建设应当纳入国民经济和社会发展计划。  
国家鼓励、引导国内外经济组织依法投资建设、经营公路。

**Article 4** People's governments at all levels shall take effective measures to support and promote highway construction. Highway construction shall be incorporated into the national economic and social development plan.  
The State encourages and guides domestic and foreign economic organizations to invest in the construction and operation of highways according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家帮助和扶持少数民族地区、边远地区和贫困地区发展公路建设。

**Article 5** The State shall assist and support minority nationality areas, remote areas, and poverty-stricken areas in developing highway construction.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　公路按其在公路路网中的地位分为国道、省道、县道和乡道，并按技术等级分为高速公路、一级公路、二级公路、三级公路和四级公路。具体划分标准由国务院交通主管部门规定。  
新建公路应当符合技术等级的要求。原有不符合最低技术等级要求的等外公路，应当采取措施，逐步改造为符合技术等级要求的公路。

**Article 6** Highways are classified, according to their position in the highway network, into national highways, provincial highways, county highways, and township highways, and are classified, according to technical grades, into expressways, Class I highways, Class II highways, Class III highways, and Class IV highways. The specific classification criteria shall be prescribed by the competent department of transportation under the State Council.  
Newly constructed highways shall meet the requirements of the technical grades. For existing substandard highways that do not meet the minimum technical grade requirements, measures shall be taken to gradually upgrade them to highways that meet the technical grade requirements.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　公路受国家保护，任何单位和个人不得破坏、损坏或者非法占用公路、公路用地及公路附属设施。  
任何单位和个人都有爱护公路、公路用地及公路附属设施的义务，有权检举和控告破坏、损坏公路、公路用地、公路附属设施和影响公路安全的行为。

**Article 7** Highways shall be protected by the State. No unit or individual may damage, destroy, or illegally occupy highways, highway land, or highway auxiliary facilities.  
Every unit and individual has the duty to protect highways, highway land, and highway auxiliary facilities, and has the right to report and accuse any act that damages or destroys highways, highway land, highway auxiliary facilities, or affects highway safety.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院交通主管部门主管全国公路工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府交通主管部门主管本行政区域内的公路工作；但是，县级以上地方人民政府交通主管部门对国道、省道的管理、监督职责，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定。  
乡、民族乡、镇人民政府负责本行政区域内的乡道的建设和养护工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府交通主管部门可以决定由公路管理机构依照本法规定行使公路行政管理职责。

**Article 8** The competent department of transportation under the State Council shall be responsible for the nationwide highway work.  
The competent departments of transportation of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the highway work within their respective administrative regions; however, the management and supervision duties of the competent departments of transportation of local people's governments at or above the county level with respect to national highways and provincial highways shall be determined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
The people's governments of townships, nationality townships, and towns shall be responsible for the construction and maintenance of township highways within their respective administrative regions.  
The competent departments of transportation of local people's governments at or above the county level may decide that the highway administration agency shall exercise the highway administrative management functions according to law in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　禁止任何单位和个人在公路上非法设卡、收费、罚款和拦截车辆。

**Article 9** No unit or individual may illegally set up checkpoints, collect tolls, impose fines, or intercept vehicles on highways.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家鼓励公路工作方面的科学技术研究，对在公路科学技术研究和应用方面作出显著成绩的单位和个人给予奖励。

**Article 10** The State encourages scientific and technological research in the field of highway work, and shall give awards to units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in the research and application of highway science and technology.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本法对专用公路有规定的，适用于专用公路。  
专用公路是指由企业或者其他单位建设、养护、管理，专为或者主要为本企业或者本单位提供运输服务的道路。

**Article 11** Where this Law has provisions on special-purpose highways, such provisions shall apply to special-purpose highways.  
'Special-purpose highways' refer to roads constructed, maintained, and managed by enterprises or other units, which are exclusively or mainly used to provide transport services for such enterprises or units.

### 第二章　公路规划

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　公路规划应当根据国民经济和社会发展以及国防建设的需要编制，与城市建设发展规划和其他方式的交通运输发展规划相协调。

**Article 12** Highway plans shall be formulated in accordance with the needs of national economic and social development and national defense construction, and shall be coordinated with urban construction development plans and transport development plans of other modes.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　公路建设用地规划应当符合土地利用总体规划，当年建设用地应当纳入年度建设用地计划。

**Article 13** The highway construction land plan shall conform to the overall plan for land use, and the annual construction land shall be incorporated into the annual plan for construction land.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国道规划由国务院交通主管部门会同国务院有关部门并商国道沿线省、自治区、直辖市人民政府编制，报国务院批准。  
省道规划由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门会同同级有关部门并商省道沿线下一级人民政府编制，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，并报国务院交通主管部门备案。  
县道规划由县级人民政府交通主管部门会同同级有关部门编制，经本级人民政府审定后，报上一级人民政府批准。  
乡道规划由县级人民政府交通主管部门协助乡、民族乡、镇人民政府编制，报县级人民政府批准。  
依照第三款、第四款规定批准的县道、乡道规划，应当报批准机关的上一级人民政府交通主管部门备案。  
省道规划应当与国道规划相协调。县道规划应当与省道规划相协调。乡道规划应当与县道规划相协调。

**Article 14** The plan for national highways shall be formulated by the competent department of transportation under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council and after consulting the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government along the national highways, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.  
The plan for provincial highways shall be formulated by the competent department of transportation of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the relevant departments at the same level and after consulting the next lower level people's governments along the provincial highways, and shall be submitted to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval, and shall be reported to the competent department of transportation under the State Council for record.  
The plan for county highways shall be formulated by the competent department of transportation of a county people's government jointly with the relevant departments at the same level, and after examination and approval by the people's government at the same level, shall be submitted to the next higher level people's government for approval.  
The plan for township highways shall be formulated by the competent department of transportation of a county people's government with the assistance of the people's governments of townships, nationality townships, and towns, and shall be submitted to the county people's government for approval.  
The approved plans for county highways and township highways pursuant to the provisions of paragraphs 3 and 4 shall be reported to the competent department of transportation of the people's government at the next higher level of the approving authority for record.  
The plan for provincial highways shall be coordinated with the plan for national highways. The plan for county highways shall be coordinated with the plan for provincial highways. The plan for township highways shall be coordinated with the plan for county highways.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　专用公路规划由专用公路的主管单位编制，经其上级主管部门审定后，报县级以上人民政府交通主管部门审核。  
专用公路规划应当与公路规划相协调。县级以上人民政府交通主管部门发现专用公路规划与国道、省道、县道、乡道规划有不协调的地方，应当提出修改意见，专用公路主管部门和单位应当作出相应的修改。

**Article 15** The plan for a special-purpose highway shall be formulated by the competent unit of the special-purpose highway, and after examination by its competent authority at a higher level, shall be submitted to the competent department of transportation of a people's government at or above the county level for review.  
The plan for a special-purpose highway shall be coordinated with the highway plan. Where the competent department of transportation of a people's government at or above the county level finds that the plan for a special-purpose highway is not coordinated with the plans for national highways, provincial highways, county highways, or township highways, it shall propose amendments, and the competent department and unit of the special-purpose highway shall make corresponding amendments.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国道规划的局部调整由原编制机关决定。国道规划需要作重大修改的，由原编制机关提出修改方案，报国务院批准。  
经批准的省道、县道、乡道公路规划需要修改的，由原编制机关提出修改方案，报原批准机关批准。

**Article 16** Partial adjustments to the plan for national highways shall be decided by the original formulating authority. Where a major revision of the plan for national highways is needed, the original formulating authority shall propose a revision plan and submit it to the State Council for approval.  
Where an approved highway plan for provincial highways, county highways, or township highways needs revision, the original formulating authority shall propose a revision plan and submit it to the original approving authority for approval.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国道的命名和编号，由国务院交通主管部门确定；省道、县道、乡道的命名和编号，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门按照国务院交通主管部门的有关规定确定。

**Article 17** The naming and numbering of national highways shall be determined by the competent department of transportation under the State Council; the naming and numbering of provincial highways, county highways, and township highways shall be determined by the competent departments of transportation of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government in accordance with the relevant provisions of the competent department of transportation under the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　规划和新建村镇、开发区，应当与公路保持规定的距离并避免在公路两侧对应进行，防止造成公路街道化，影响公路的运行安全与畅通。

**Article 18** In planning and constructing new villages, towns, and development zones, the prescribed distance from highways shall be maintained, and construction shall be avoided on both sides of highways in a corresponding manner, so as to prevent highway urbanization and to avoid affecting the safe and smooth operation of highways.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家鼓励专用公路用于社会公共运输。专用公路主要用于社会公共运输时，由专用公路的主管单位申请，或者由有关方面申请，专用公路的主管单位同意，并经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门批准，可以改划为省道、县道或者乡道。

**Article 19** The State encourages the use of special-purpose highways for public transport. Where a special-purpose highway is mainly used for public transport, upon application by the competent unit of the special-purpose highway, or upon application by the relevant party with the consent of the competent unit of the special-purpose highway, and upon approval by the competent department of transportation of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, it may be reclassified as a provincial highway, county highway, or township highway.

### 第三章　公路建设

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级以上人民政府交通主管部门应当依据职责维护公路建设秩序，加强对公路建设的监督管理。

**Article 20** The competent departments of transportation of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their functions and duties, maintain the order of highway construction and strengthen the supervision and administration of highway construction.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　筹集公路建设资金，除各级人民政府的财政拨款，包括依法征税筹集的公路建设专项资金转为的财政拨款外，可以依法向国内外金融机构或者外国政府贷款。  
国家鼓励国内外经济组织对公路建设进行投资。开发、经营公路的公司可以依照法律、行政法规的规定发行股票、公司债券筹集资金。  
依照本法规定出让公路收费权的收入必须用于公路建设。  
向企业和个人集资建设公路，必须根据需要与可能，坚持自愿原则，不得强行摊派，并符合国务院的有关规定。  
公路建设资金还可以采取符合法律或者国务院规定的其他方式筹集。

**Article 21** Funds for highway construction may be raised, in addition to financial allocations from people's governments at all levels, including such allocations converted from special funds for highway construction raised through taxation according to law, through loans from domestic or foreign financial institutions or foreign governments according to law.  
The State encourages domestic and foreign economic organizations to invest in highway construction. Companies that develop and operate highways may raise funds by issuing shares and corporate bonds in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.  
Proceeds from the transfer of the right to collect highway tolls in accordance with the provisions of this Law must be used for highway construction.  
The collection of funds from enterprises and individuals for highway construction must be based on need and feasibility, adhere to the principle of voluntariness, shall not involve compulsory apportionment, and shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State Council.  
Funds for highway construction may also be raised through other means that comply with the provisions of laws or the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　公路建设应当按照国家规定的基本建设程序和有关规定进行。

**Article 22** Highway construction shall be carried out in accordance with the capital construction procedures and relevant provisions prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　公路建设项目应当按照国家有关规定实行法人负责制度、招标投标制度和工程监理制度。

**Article 23** Highway construction projects shall implement the legal person responsibility system, the bidding and tendering system, and the engineering supervision system in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　公路建设单位应当根据公路建设工程的特点和技术要求，选择具有相应资格的勘查设计单位、施工单位和工程监理单位，并依照有关法律、法规、规章的规定和公路工程技术标准的要求，分别签订合同，明确双方的权利义务。  
承担公路建设项目的可行性研究单位、勘查设计单位、施工单位和工程监理单位，必须持有国家规定的资质证书。

**Article 24** The highway construction entity shall, based on the characteristics and technical requirements of the highway construction project, select survey and design units, construction units, and engineering supervision units with corresponding qualifications, and shall enter into contracts separately in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws, regulations, and rules as well as the requirements of highway engineering technical standards, specifying the rights and obligations of both parties.  
Feasibility study units, survey and design units, construction units, and engineering supervision units undertaking highway construction projects must hold qualification certificates as prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　公路建设项目的施工，须按国务院交通主管部门的规定报请县级以上地方人民政府交通主管部门批准。

**Article 25** The construction of a highway construction project shall be submitted to the competent department of transportation of the local people's government at or above the county level for approval in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of transportation under the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　公路建设必须符合公路工程技术标准。  
承担公路建设项目的设计单位、施工单位和工程监理单位，应当按照国家有关规定建立健全质量保证体系，落实岗位责任制，并依照有关法律、法规、规章以及公路工程技术标准的要求和合同约定进行设计、施工和监理，保证公路工程质量。

**Article 26** Highway construction must conform to highway engineering technical standards.  
Design units, construction units, and engineering supervision units undertaking highway construction projects shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, establish and improve a quality assurance system, implement post responsibility systems, and carry out design, construction, and supervision in accordance with the requirements of relevant laws, regulations, and rules as well as highway engineering technical standards and the contract terms, ensuring the quality of the highway project.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　公路建设使用土地依照有关法律、行政法规的规定办理。  
公路建设应当贯彻切实保护耕地、节约用地的原则。

**Article 27** The use of land for highway construction shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
Highway construction shall implement the principles of effectively protecting cultivated land and economizing on land use.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　公路建设需要使用国有荒山、荒地或者需要在国有荒山、荒地、河滩、滩涂上挖砂、采石、取土的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定办理后，任何单位和个人不得阻挠或者非法收取费用。

**Article 28** Where highway construction requires the use of State-owned barren hills or wasteland, or requires the digging of sand, quarrying of stone, or extraction of soil on State-owned barren hills, wasteland, river beaches, or tidal flats, after handling the procedures in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, no unit or individual may obstruct such activities or illegally collect fees.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　地方各级人民政府对公路建设依法使用土地和搬迁居民，应当给予支持和协助。

**Article 29** Local people's governments at all levels shall provide support and assistance for the lawful use of land and relocation of residents for highway construction.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　公路建设项目的设计和施工，应当符合依法保护环境、保护文物古迹和防止水土流失的要求。  
公路规划中贯彻国防要求的公路建设项目，应当严格按照规划进行建设，以保证国防交通的需要。

**Article 30** The design and construction of highway construction projects shall meet the requirements for protecting the environment, protecting cultural relics and historic sites, and preventing soil erosion in accordance with law.  
Highway construction projects that incorporate national defense requirements in highway planning shall be built strictly in accordance with the plan to ensure the needs of national defense transportation.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　因建设公路影响铁路、水利、电力、邮电设施和其他设施正常使用时，公路建设单位应当事先征得有关部门的同意；因公路建设对有关设施造成损坏的，公路建设单位应当按照不低于该设施原有的技术标准予以修复，或者给予相应的经济补偿。

**Article 31** Where highway construction affects the normal use of railways, water conservancy, electric power, postal and telecommunications facilities, and other facilities, the highway construction entity shall obtain the consent of the relevant departments in advance; if damage is caused to relevant facilities due to highway construction, the highway construction entity shall restore them to a technical standard not lower than the original, or provide corresponding economic compensation.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　改建公路时，施工单位应当在施工路段两端设置明显的施工标志、安全标志。需要车辆绕行的，应当在绕行路口设置标志；不能绕行的，必须修建临时道路，保证车辆和行人通行。

**Article 32** When rebuilding a highway, the construction unit shall set up conspicuous construction signs and safety signs at both ends of the construction section. Where vehicles need to detour, signs shall be set up at the detour intersections; where detour is not possible, a temporary road must be built to ensure the passage of vehicles and pedestrians.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　公路建设项目和公路修复项目竣工后，应当按照国家有关规定进行验收；未经验收或者验收不合格的，不得交付使用。  
建成的公路，应当按照国务院交通主管部门的规定设置明显的标志、标线。

**Article 33** After the completion of a highway construction project or highway repair project, acceptance inspection shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; no project that has not undergone acceptance inspection or has failed acceptance inspection may be delivered for use.  
For a completed highway, conspicuous signs and markings shall be set up in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of transportation under the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上地方人民政府应当确定公路两侧边沟（截水沟、坡脚护坡道，下同）外缘起不少于一米的公路用地。

**Article 34** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall determine a highway land use area of not less than one meter from the outer edge of the side ditches (including intercepting ditches, slope toe berms, the same below) on both sides of the highway.

### 第四章　公路养护

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　公路管理机构应当按照国务院交通主管部门规定的技术规范和操作规程对公路进行养护，保证公路经常处于良好的技术状态。

**Article 35** The highway administration agency shall maintain highways in accordance with the technical specifications and operation procedures prescribed by the competent department of transportation under the State Council, ensuring that highways are constantly kept in good technical condition.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家采用依法征税的办法筹集公路养护资金，具体实施办法和步骤由国务院规定。  
依法征税筹集的公路养护资金，必须专项用于公路的养护和改建。

**Article 36** The State adopts the method of collecting taxes according to law to raise funds for highway maintenance, and the specific implementation measures and procedures shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
Funds raised for highway maintenance through tax collection according to law must be used exclusively for the maintenance and rebuilding of highways.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　县、乡级人民政府对公路养护需要的挖砂、采石、取土以及取水，应当给予支持和协助。

**Article 37** People's governments at the county and township levels shall provide support and assistance for the digging of sand, quarrying of stone, extraction of soil, and water intake required for highway maintenance.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　县、乡级人民政府应当在农村义务工的范围内，按照国家有关规定组织公路两侧的农村居民履行为公路建设和养护提供劳务的义务。

**Article 38** People's governments at the county and township levels shall, within the scope of rural voluntary labor, organize rural residents on both sides of highways to fulfill the obligation of providing labor services for highway construction and maintenance in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　为保障公路养护人员的人身安全，公路养护人员进行养护作业时，应当穿着统一的安全标志服；利用车辆进行养护作业时，应当在公路作业车辆上设置明显的作业标志。  
公路养护车辆进行作业时，在不影响过往车辆通行的前提下，其行驶路线和方向不受公路标志、标线限制；过往车辆对公路养护车辆和人员应当注意避让。  
公路养护工程施工影响车辆、行人通行时，施工单位应当依照[本法第三十二条](#art-32)的规定办理。

**Article 39** To ensure the personal safety of highway maintenance personnel, when performing maintenance operations, highway maintenance personnel shall wear uniform safety warning clothing; when using vehicles for maintenance operations, conspicuous operation signs shall be set up on the highway maintenance vehicles.  
When highway maintenance vehicles are performing operations, their travel routes and directions shall not be restricted by highway signs or markings, provided that they do not affect the passage of passing vehicles; passing vehicles shall take care to yield to highway maintenance vehicles and personnel.  
Where highway maintenance engineering construction affects the passage of vehicles and pedestrians, the construction unit shall handle the matter in accordance with Article 32 of this Law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　因严重自然灾害致使国道、省道交通中断，公路管理机构应当及时修复；公路管理机构难以及时修复时，县级以上地方人民政府应当及时组织当地机关、团体、企业事业单位、城乡居民进行抢修，并可以请求当地驻军支援，尽快恢复交通。

**Article 40** Where traffic on national highways or provincial highways is interrupted due to severe natural disasters, the highway administration agency shall promptly repair them; where the highway administration agency cannot repair them in a timely manner, the local people's government at or above the county level shall promptly organize local authorities, organizations, enterprises, institutions, and urban and rural residents to carry out emergency repairs, and may request support from local military forces to restore traffic as soon as possible.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　公路用地范围内的山坡、荒地，由公路管理机构负责水土保持。

**Article 41** The highway administrative agency shall be responsible for water and soil conservation on the slopes and wasteland within the land used for highways.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　公路绿化工作，由公路管理机构按照公路工程技术标准组织实施。  
公路用地上的树木，不得任意砍伐；需要更新砍伐的，应当经县级以上地方人民政府交通主管部门同意后，依照《中华人民共和国森林法》的规定办理审批手续，并完成更新补种任务。

**Article 42** The highway administrative agency shall organize the implementation of highway greening in accordance with the technical standards for highway engineering.  
Trees on land used for highways shall not be arbitrarily felled; where renewal felling is necessary, approval shall be obtained from the transport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level, and the examination and approval procedures shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Forest Law of the People's Republic of China, and the tasks of renewal and replanting shall be completed.

### 第五章　路政管理

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　各级地方人民政府应当采取措施，加强对公路的保护。  
县级以上地方人民政府交通主管部门应当认真履行职责，依法做好公路保护工作，并努力采用科学的管理方法和先进的技术手段，提高公路管理水平，逐步完善公路服务设施，保障公路的完好、安全和畅通。

**Article 43** Local people's governments at all levels shall take measures to strengthen the protection of highways.  
The transport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall earnestly perform its duties, do a good job in highway protection according to law, and strive to adopt scientific management methods and advanced technical means to improve the level of highway management, gradually improve highway service facilities, and ensure the completeness, safety and smoothness of highways.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　任何单位和个人不得擅自占用、挖掘公路。  
因修建铁路、机场、电站、通信设施、水利工程和进行其他建设工程需要占用、挖掘公路或者使公路改线的，建设单位应当事先征得有关交通主管部门的同意；影响交通安全的，还须征得有关公安机关的同意。占用、挖掘公路或者使公路改线的，建设单位应当按照不低于该段公路原有的技术标准予以修复、改建或者给予相应的经济补偿。

**Article 44** No unit or individual may occupy or excavate a highway without authorization.  
If it is necessary to occupy, excavate a highway or alter its alignment due to the construction of railways, airports, power stations, communication facilities, water conservancy projects or other construction projects, the construction unit shall obtain the consent of the relevant transport administrative department in advance; if it affects traffic safety, consent from the relevant public security organ shall also be obtained. For occupying, excavating a highway or altering its alignment, the construction unit shall repair, reconstruct or provide corresponding economic compensation at a standard no lower than the original technical standard of that section of the highway.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　跨越、穿越公路修建桥梁、渡槽或者架设、埋设管线等设施的，以及在公路用地范围内架设、埋设管线、电缆等设施的，应当事先经有关交通主管部门同意，影响交通安全的，还须征得有关公安机关的同意；所修建、架设或者埋设的设施应当符合公路工程技术标准的要求。对公路造成损坏的，应当按照损坏程度给予补偿。

**Article 45** Where any facility such as a bridge, aqueduct, or pipeline is constructed, erected or buried across or through a highway, or pipelines, cables or other facilities are erected or buried within the land used for the highway, consent shall be obtained in advance from the relevant transport administrative department; if it affects traffic safety, consent from the relevant public security organ shall also be obtained. The facilities constructed, erected or buried shall comply with the requirements of the technical standards for highway engineering. If damage is caused to the highway, compensation shall be made according to the extent of the damage.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　任何单位和个人不得在公路上及公路用地范围内摆摊设点、堆放物品、倾倒垃圾、设置障碍、挖沟引水、利用公路边沟排放污物或者进行其他损坏、污染公路和影响公路畅通的活动。

**Article 46** No unit or individual may set up stalls, pile up articles, dump garbage, set up obstacles, dig ditches for water diversion, use highway side ditches to discharge sewage or engage in other activities that damage, pollute the highway or affect its smoothness on the highway or within the land used for the highway.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　在大中型公路桥梁和渡口周围二百米、公路隧道上方和洞口外一百米范围内，以及在公路两侧一定距离内，不得挖砂、采石、取土、倾倒废弃物，不得进行爆破作业及其他危及公路、公路桥梁、公路隧道、公路渡口安全的活动。  
在前款范围内因抢险、防汛需要修筑堤坝、压缩或者拓宽河床的，应当事先报经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门会同水行政主管部门批准，并采取有效的保护有关的公路、公路桥梁、公路隧道、公路渡口安全的措施。

**Article 47** Within a range of 200 meters around large and medium-sized highway bridges and ferries, within 100 meters above highway tunnels and outside the tunnel openings, and within a certain distance on both sides of the highway, it shall not be permitted to excavate sand, quarry stone, extract earth, dump waste, or conduct blasting operations or other activities endangering the safety of highways, highway bridges, highway tunnels, or highway ferries.  
Where it is necessary, for emergency rescue or flood control purposes, to construct embankments, narrow or widen riverbeds within the area specified in the preceding paragraph, approval shall be obtained in advance from the transport administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the water administrative department, and effective measures shall be taken to protect the safety of the relevant highways, highway bridges, highway tunnels and highway ferries.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　铁轮车、履带车和其他可能损害公路路面的机具，不得在公路上行驶。  
农业机械因当地田间作业需要在公路上短距离行驶或者军用车辆执行任务需要在公路上行驶的，可以不受前款限制，但是应当采取安全保护措施。对公路造成损坏的，应当按照损坏程度给予补偿。

**Article 48** Iron-wheeled vehicles, tracked vehicles and other machinery that may damage the highway pavement shall not travel on the highway.  
Agricultural machinery that needs to travel a short distance on the highway for local field operations, or military vehicles that need to travel on the highway to perform tasks, may be exempted from the restriction in the preceding paragraph, but safety protection measures shall be taken. If damage is caused to the highway, compensation shall be made according to the extent of the damage.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　在公路上行驶的车辆的轴载质量应当符合公路工程技术标准要求。

**Article 49** The axle load mass of vehicles traveling on highways shall comply with the requirements of the technical standards for highway engineering.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　超过公路、公路桥梁、公路隧道或者汽车渡船的限载、限高、限宽、限长标准的车辆，不得在有限定标准的公路、公路桥梁上或者公路隧道内行驶，不得使用汽车渡船。超过公路或者公路桥梁限载标准确需行驶的，必须经县级以上地方人民政府交通主管部门批准，并按要求采取有效的防护措施；运载不可解体的超限物品的，应当按照指定的时间、路线、时速行驶，并悬挂明显标志。  
运输单位不能按照前款规定采取防护措施的，由交通主管部门帮助其采取防护措施，所需费用由运输单位承担。

**Article 50** Vehicles exceeding the standards for load limit, height limit, width limit or length limit of highways, highway bridges, highway tunnels or automobile ferries shall not travel on the highways or highway bridges or in the highway tunnels with such specified standards, and shall not use the automobile ferries. If it is truly necessary to travel on a highway or highway bridge exceeding the load limit standard, approval must be obtained from the transport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level, and effective protective measures shall be taken as required; if carrying indivisible super-limit articles, the vehicle shall travel at the designated time, along the designated route and at the designated speed, and shall display conspicuous signs.  
If the transport unit is unable to take protective measures in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the transport administrative department shall assist it in taking protective measures, and the expenses required shall be borne by the transport unit.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　机动车制造厂和其他单位不得将公路作为检验机动车制动性能的试车场地。

**Article 51** Motor vehicle manufacturers and other units shall not use highways as testing grounds for checking the braking performance of motor vehicles.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　任何单位和个人不得损坏、擅自移动、涂改公路附属设施。  
前款公路附属设施，是指为保护、养护公路和保障公路安全畅通所设置的公路防护、排水、养护、管理、服务、交通安全、渡运、监控、通信、收费等设施、设备以及专用建筑物、构筑物等。

**Article 52** No unit or individual may damage, arbitrarily move or alter highway ancillary facilities.  
For the purpose of the preceding paragraph, "highway ancillary facilities" refer to facilities and equipment for highway protection, drainage, maintenance, management, service, traffic safety, ferry transportation, monitoring, communication, toll collection, etc., as well as special buildings and structures, which are installed to protect and maintain the highway and to ensure its safe and smooth operation.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　造成公路损坏的，责任者应当及时报告公路管理机构，并接受公路管理机构的现场调查。

**Article 53** Any person who causes damage to a highway shall promptly report to the highway administrative agency and accept the on-site investigation conducted by the highway administrative agency.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　任何单位和个人未经县级以上地方人民政府交通主管部门批准，不得在公路用地范围内设置公路标志以外的其他标志。

**Article 54** No unit or individual may, without approval from the transport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level, set up signs other than highway signs within the land used for highways.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　在公路上增设平面交叉道口，必须按照国家有关规定经过批准，并按照国家规定的技术标准建设。

**Article 55** The addition of a level crossing on a highway must be approved in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and constructed in accordance with the technical standards prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　除公路防护、养护需要的以外，禁止在公路两侧的建筑控制区内修建建筑物和地面构筑物；需要在建筑控制区内埋设管线、电缆等设施的，应当事先经县级以上地方人民政府交通主管部门批准。  
前款规定的建筑控制区的范围，由县级以上地方人民政府按照保障公路运行安全和节约用地的原则，依照国务院的规定划定。  
建筑控制区范围经县级以上地方人民政府依照前款规定划定后，由县级以上地方人民政府交通主管部门设置标桩、界桩。任何单位和个人不得损坏、擅自挪动该标桩、界桩。

**Article 56** Except where necessary for highway protection and maintenance, it shall be prohibited to construct buildings or ground structures within the building control areas on both sides of the highway; where it is necessary to bury pipelines, cables or other facilities within the building control areas, approval shall be obtained in advance from the transport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level.  
The scope of the building control areas specified in the preceding paragraph shall be delimited by the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the provisions of the State Council, following the principles of ensuring highway operation safety and economizing on land use.  
After the scope of the building control areas has been delimited by the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the transport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall set up marking stakes and boundary stakes. No unit or individual may damage or arbitrarily move such marking stakes or boundary stakes.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　除[本法第四十七条](#art-47)第二款的规定外，本章规定由交通主管部门行使的路政管理职责，可以依照[本法第八条](#art-8)第四款的规定，由公路管理机构行使。

**Article 57** Except as provided in the second paragraph of Article 47 of this Law, the highway administrative agency may, in accordance with the provisions of the fourth paragraph of Article 8 of this Law, exercise the highway administration duties that this Chapter prescribes to be exercised by the transport administrative department.

### 第六章　收费公路

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国家允许依法设立收费公路，同时对收费公路的数量进行控制。  
除[本法第五十九条](#art-59)规定可以收取车辆通行费的公路外，禁止任何公路收取车辆通行费。

**Article 58** The State permits the establishment of toll highways according to law, while controlling the number of such toll highways.  
Except for highways which may collect tolls for vehicle passage as provided in Article 59 of this Law, no highway may collect tolls for vehicle passage.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　符合国务院交通主管部门规定的技术等级和规模的下列公路，可以依法收取车辆通行费：  
（一）由县级以上地方人民政府交通主管部门利用贷款或者向企业、个人集资建成的公路；  
（二）由国内外经济组织依法受让前项收费公路收费权的公路；  
（三）由国内外经济组织依法投资建成的公路。

**Article 59** The following highways which meet the technical grades and scales stipulated by the transport administrative department of the State Council may collect tolls for vehicle passage according to law:  
(1) Highways constructed with loans or funds raised from enterprises or individuals by the transport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level;  
(2) Highways for which the toll collection right of the toll highway mentioned in the preceding item has been legally transferred to domestic or foreign economic organizations;  
(3) Highways constructed with investment by domestic or foreign economic organizations according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　县级以上地方人民政府交通主管部门利用贷款或者集资建成的收费公路的收费期限，按照收费偿还贷款、集资款的原则，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府依照国务院交通主管部门的规定确定。  
有偿转让公路收费权的公路，收费权转让后，由受让方收费经营。收费权的转让期限由出让、受让双方约定，最长不得超过国务院规定的年限。  
国内外经济组织投资建设公路，必须按照国家有关规定办理审批手续；公路建成后，由投资者收费经营。收费经营期限按照收回投资并有合理回报的原则，由有关交通主管部门与投资者约定并按照国家有关规定办理审批手续，但最长不得超过国务院规定的年限。

**Article 60** The toll collection period for a toll highway constructed with loans or funds raised by the transport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be determined by the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the provisions of the transport administrative department of the State Council, based on the principle of repaying loans or principal and interest with toll revenues.  
For a highway where the toll collection right is transferred for value, after the transfer, the transferee shall collect tolls and operate the highway. The term of transfer of the toll collection right shall be agreed upon by the transferor and the transferee, and shall not exceed the number of years prescribed by the State Council.  
Where a domestic or foreign economic organization invests in the construction of a highway, it must go through the examination and approval procedures in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; after the highway is completed, the investor shall collect tolls and operate it. The toll operation period shall be agreed upon by the relevant transport administrative department and the investor based on the principle of recovering the investment with a reasonable return, and the examination and approval procedures shall be completed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; however, the period shall not exceed the number of years prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　[本法第五十九条](#art-59)第一款第一项规定的公路中的国道收费权的转让，应当在转让协议签订之日起三十个工作日内报国务院交通主管部门备案；国道以外的其他公路收费权的转让，应当在转让协议签订之日起三十个工作日内报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府备案。  
前款规定的公路收费权出让的最低成交价，以国有资产评估机构评估的价值为依据确定。

**Article 61** The transfer of the toll collection right for national highways among the highways specified in Item 1, Paragraph 1 of Article 59 of this Law shall be reported to the competent transport department of the State Council for recordation within thirty working days from the date of signing the transfer agreement; the transfer of toll collection rights for highways other than national highways shall be reported to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for recordation within thirty working days from the date of signing the transfer agreement.  
The minimum transaction price for the assignment of highway toll collection rights as specified in the preceding paragraph shall be determined based on the value assessed by a state-owned asset appraisal institution.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　受让公路收费权和投资建设公路的国内外经济组织应当依法成立开发、经营公路的企业（以下简称公路经营企业）。

**Article 62** Domestic and foreign economic organizations that have acquired highway toll collection rights or invested in the construction of highways shall establish enterprises for the development and operation of highways (hereinafter referred to as highway operating enterprises) according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　收费公路车辆通行费的收费标准，由公路收费单位提出方案，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门会同同级物价行政主管部门审查批准。

**Article 63** The standards for tolls on toll highways shall be proposed by the highway toll collection entity and submitted to the competent transport department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, which shall, jointly with the competent price administration department at the same level, review and approve them.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　收费公路设置车辆通行费的收费站，应当报经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审查批准。跨省、自治区、直辖市的收费公路设置车辆通行费的收费站，由有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府协商确定；协商不成的，由国务院交通主管部门决定。同一收费公路由不同的交通主管部门组织建设或者由不同的公路经营企业经营的，应当按照“统一收费、按比例分成”的原则，统筹规划，合理设置收费站。  
两个收费站之间的距离，不得小于国务院交通主管部门规定的标准。

**Article 64** The establishment of toll stations on toll highways shall be submitted to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for review and approval. For toll highways that cross provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the establishment of toll stations shall be determined through consultation among the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government; if consultation fails, the decision shall be made by the competent transport department of the State Council. Where the same toll highway is constructed by different competent transport departments or operated by different highway operating enterprises, toll stations shall be planned as a whole and set up reasonably in accordance with the principle of "unified toll collection and proportional distribution."  
The distance between two toll stations shall not be less than the standard prescribed by the competent transport department of the State Council.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　有偿转让公路收费权的公路，转让收费权合同约定的期限届满，收费权由出让方收回。  
由国内外经济组织依照本法规定投资建成并经营的收费公路，约定的经营期限届满，该公路由国家无偿收回，由有关交通主管部门管理。

**Article 65** For a highway whose toll collection right has been transferred for value, upon expiration of the period stipulated in the contract for the transfer of the toll collection right, the toll collection right shall be taken back by the assignor.  
For a toll highway invested in and constructed and operated by domestic or foreign economic organizations in accordance with the provisions of this Law, upon expiration of the agreed operating period, the highway shall be taken back by the State without compensation and shall be managed by the relevant competent transport department.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　依照[本法第五十九条](#art-59)规定受让收费权或者由国内外经济组织投资建成经营的公路的养护工作，由各该公路经营企业负责。各该公路经营企业在经营期间应当按照国务院交通主管部门规定的技术规范和操作规程做好对公路的养护工作。在受让收费权的期限届满，或者经营期限届满时，公路应当处于良好的技术状态。  
前款规定的公路的绿化和公路用地范围内的水土保持工作，由各该公路经营企业负责。  
第一款规定的公路的路政管理，适用本法第五章的规定。该公路路政管理的职责由县级以上地方人民政府交通主管部门或者公路管理机构的派出机构、人员行使。

**Article 66** The maintenance of a highway the toll collection right of which has been acquired in accordance with Article 59 of this Law or which has been invested in, constructed and operated by domestic or foreign economic organizations shall be undertaken by the respective highway operating enterprise. Each such highway operating enterprise shall, during the operating period, perform the highway maintenance work properly in accordance with the technical specifications and operating procedures prescribed by the competent transport department of the State Council. Upon expiration of the period for which the toll collection right was acquired, or upon expiration of the operating period, the highway shall be in a good technical condition.  
The greening of the highway specified in the preceding paragraph and the water and soil conservation work within the highway land area shall be undertaken by the respective highway operating enterprise.  
The highway administration of the highway specified in Paragraph 1 shall be governed by the provisions of Chapter V of this Law. The duties of highway administration for such highway shall be exercised by the dispatched agencies or personnel of the competent transport department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the highway administration institution.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　在收费公路上从事[本法第四十四条](#art-44)第二款、第四十五条、第四十八条、第五十条所列活动的，除依照各该条的规定办理外，给公路经营企业造成损失的，应当给予相应的补偿。

**Article 67** Any person who engages in the activities listed in Paragraph 2 of Article 44, Article 45, Article 48, and Article 50 of this Law on a toll highway shall, in addition to handling the matter in accordance with the provisions of the respective articles, provide corresponding compensation if losses are caused to the highway operating enterprise.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　收费公路的具体管理办法，由国务院依照本法制定。

**Article 68** The specific measures for the administration of toll highways shall be formulated by the State Council in accordance with this Law.

### 第七章　监督检查

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　交通主管部门、公路管理机构依法对有关公路的法律、法规执行情况进行监督检查。

**Article 69** The competent transport departments and highway administration institutions shall, according to law, supervise and inspect the implementation of laws and regulations related to highways.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　交通主管部门、公路管理机构负有管理和保护公路的责任，有权检查、制止各种侵占、损坏公路、公路用地、公路附属设施及其他违反本法规定的行为。

**Article 70** The competent transport departments and highway administration institutions shall bear the responsibility of managing and protecting highways, and shall have the right to inspect and stop any acts of encroaching upon, damaging highways, highway land, highway auxiliary facilities, or other acts violating the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　公路监督检查人员依法在公路、建筑控制区、车辆停放场所、车辆所属单位等进行监督检查时，任何单位和个人不得阻挠。  
公路经营者、使用者和其他有关单位、个人，应当接受公路监督检查人员依法实施的监督检查，并为其提供方便。  
公路监督检查人员执行公务，应当佩戴标志，持证上岗。

**Article 71** When highway supervision and inspection personnel conduct supervision and inspection according to law on highways, in building control areas, at vehicle parking places, at vehicle owner units, etc., no unit or individual may obstruct them.  
Highway operators, users, and other relevant units and individuals shall accept the supervision and inspection conducted by highway supervision and inspection personnel according to law, and shall provide convenience for them.  
Highway supervision and inspection personnel, when performing official duties, shall wear insignia and hold certificates.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　交通主管部门、公路管理机构应当加强对所属公路监督检查人员的管理和教育，要求公路监督检查人员熟悉国家有关法律和规定，公正廉洁，热情服务，秉公执法，对公路监督检查人员的执法行为应当加强监督检查，对其违法行为应当及时纠正，依法处理。

**Article 72** The competent transport departments and highway administration institutions shall strengthen the management and education of the highway supervision and inspection personnel under them, require such personnel to be familiar with relevant national laws and regulations, be fair and incorruptible, provide enthusiastic service, and enforce the law impartially, shall strengthen supervision and inspection over the law enforcement conduct of highway supervision and inspection personnel, and shall promptly correct and handle according to law any illegal acts committed by them.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　用于公路监督检查的专用车辆，应当设置统一的标志和示警灯。

**Article 73** Special vehicles used for highway supervision and inspection shall be equipped with uniform markings and warning lights.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　违反法律或者国务院有关规定，擅自在公路上设卡、收费的，由交通主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，可以处违法所得三倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，可以处二万元以下的罚款；对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 74** Any person who, in violation of laws or relevant regulations of the State Council, sets up checkpoints or collects tolls on highways without authorization shall be ordered by the competent transport department to cease the illegal act, confiscate the illegal gains, and may be imposed a fine of not more than three times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan may be imposed; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反[本法第二十五条](#art-25)规定，未经有关交通主管部门批准擅自施工的，交通主管部门可以责令停止施工，并可以处五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 75** Any person who, in violation of Article 25 of this Law, commences construction without the approval of the relevant competent transport department, the competent transport department may order the cessation of construction and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　有下列违法行为之一的，由交通主管部门责令停止违法行为，可以处三万元以下的罚款：  
（一）违反[本法第四十四条](#art-44)第一款规定，擅自占用、挖掘公路的；  
（二）违反[本法第四十五条](#art-45)规定，未经同意或者未按照公路工程技术标准的要求修建桥梁、渡槽或者架设、埋设管线、电缆等设施的；  
（三）违反[本法第四十七条](#art-47)规定，从事危及公路安全的作业的；  
（四）违反[本法第四十八条](#art-48)规定，铁轮车、履带车和其他可能损害路面的机具擅自在公路上行驶的；  
（五）违反[本法第五十条](#art-50)规定，车辆超限使用汽车渡船或者在公路上擅自超限行驶的；  
（六）违反[本法第五十二条](#art-52)、第五十六条规定，损坏、移动、涂改公路附属设施或者损坏、挪动建筑控制区的标桩、界桩，可能危及公路安全的。

**Article 76** Any person who commits any of the following illegal acts shall be ordered by the competent transport department to cease the illegal act, and may be imposed a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan:  
(1) Violating Paragraph 1 of Article 44 of this Law by occupying or excavating highways without authorization;  
(2) Violating Article 45 of this Law by constructing bridges, aqueducts, or installing or laying pipelines, cables, or other facilities without consent or without complying with the technical standards for highway engineering;  
(3) Violating Article 47 of this Law by engaging in operations that endanger highway safety;  
(4) Violating Article 48 of this Law by allowing iron-wheeled vehicles, tracked vehicles, or other machinery that may damage the road surface to travel on highways without authorization;  
(5) Violating Article 50 of this Law by overloading the use of automobile ferries or traveling on highways with excessive loads without authorization;  
(6) Violating Articles 52 and 56 of this Law by damaging, moving, or altering highway auxiliary facilities, or damaging or moving stakes or boundary markers in building control areas, which may endanger highway safety.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　违反[本法第四十六条](#art-46)的规定，造成公路路面损坏、污染或者影响公路畅通的，或者违反[本法第五十一条](#art-51)规定，将公路作为试车场地的，由交通主管部门责令停止违法行为，可以处五千元以下的罚款。

**Article 77** Any person who, in violation of Article 46 of this Law, causes damage to or pollution of the highway surface, or affects the unimpeded flow of traffic on the highway, or who, in violation of Article 51 of this Law, uses a highway as a testing ground for vehicles, shall be ordered by the competent transport department to cease the illegal act, and may be imposed a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　违反[本法第五十三条](#art-53)规定，造成公路损坏，未报告的，由交通主管部门处一千元以下的罚款。

**Article 78** Any person who, in violation of Article 53 of this Law, causes damage to a highway and fails to report it, shall be imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan by the competent transport department.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　违反[本法第五十四条](#art-54)规定，在公路用地范围内设置公路标志以外的其他标志的，由交通主管部门责令限期拆除，可以处二万元以下的罚款；逾期不拆除的，由交通主管部门拆除，有关费用由设置者负担。

**Article 79** Any person who, in violation of Article 54 of this Law, erects signs other than highway signs within the highway land area, shall be ordered by the competent transport department to dismantle them within a specified period, and may be imposed a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan; if the signs are not dismantled within the specified period, the competent transport department shall dismantle them, and the relevant expenses shall be borne by the person who erected them.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　违反[本法第五十五条](#art-55)规定，未经批准在公路上增设平面交叉道口的，由交通主管部门责令恢复原状，处五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 80** Any person who, in violation of Article 55 of this Law, adds an at-grade crossing on a highway without approval, shall be ordered by the competent transport department to restore the original condition, and shall be imposed a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　违反[本法第五十六条](#art-56)规定，在公路建筑控制区内修建建筑物、地面构筑物或者擅自埋设管线、电缆等设施的，由交通主管部门责令限期拆除，并可以处五万元以下的罚款。逾期不拆除的，由交通主管部门拆除，有关费用由建筑者、构筑者承担。

**Article 81** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of Article 56 of this Law, constructs buildings, ground structures or unauthorizedly lays pipelines, cables or other facilities within the highway building control zone, shall be ordered by the competent department of transportation to dismantle within a specified time limit and may also be imposed a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan. If the dismantling is not completed within the time limit, the competent department of transportation shall dismantle them, and the relevant expenses shall be borne by the constructor or builder.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　除[本法第七十四条](#art-74)、第七十五条的规定外，本章规定由交通主管部门行使的行政处罚权和行政措施，可以依照[本法第八条](#art-8)第四款的规定由公路管理机构行使。

**Article 82** Except as provided in Articles 74 and 75 of this Law, the power of administrative penalty and administrative measures to be exercised by the competent department of transportation under this Chapter may be exercised by the highway management agency in accordance with the provisions of Paragraph 4 of Article 8 of this Law.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　阻碍公路建设或者公路抢修，致使公路建设或者抢修不能正常进行，尚未造成严重损失的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚。  
损毁公路或者擅自移动公路标志，可能影响交通安全，尚不够刑事处罚的，适用[《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法》第九十九条](../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国道路交通安全法.md#art-99)的处罚规定。  
拒绝、阻碍公路监督检查人员依法执行职务未使用暴力、威胁方法的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚。

**Article 83** Whoever obstructs highway construction or emergency repair, causing the construction or repair to fail to proceed normally, without causing serious losses, shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Whoever damages highways or unauthorizedly moves highway signs, which may affect traffic safety, and which is not sufficient for criminal punishment, shall be subject to the punishment provisions of Article 99 of the Road Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Whoever refuses or obstructs a highway supervision and inspection personnel from performing their duties according to law, without using violence or threat, shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　违反本法有关规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 84** Whoever violates the relevant provisions of this Law, constituting a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　违反本法有关规定，对公路造成损害的，应当依法承担民事责任。  
对公路造成较大损害的车辆，必须立即停车，保护现场，报告公路管理机构，接受公路管理机构的调查、处理后方得驶离。

**Article 85** Whoever violates the relevant provisions of this Law and causes damage to a highway shall bear civil liability according to law.  
A vehicle that causes relatively major damage to a highway must immediately stop, protect the scene, report to the highway management agency, and may only leave after undergoing investigation and handling by the highway management agency.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　交通主管部门、公路管理机构的工作人员玩忽职守、徇私舞弊、滥用职权，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 86** Any staff member of the competent department of transportation or the highway management agency who neglects their duty, engages in malpractice for personal gain, or abuses their authority, if constituting a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if not constituting a crime, shall be given an administrative sanction according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　本法自1998年1月1日起施行。

**Article 87** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 1998.
