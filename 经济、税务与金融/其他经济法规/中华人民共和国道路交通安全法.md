### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护道路交通秩序，预防和减少交通事故，保护人身安全，保护公民、法人和其他组织的财产安全及其他合法权益，提高通行效率，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of maintaining road traffic order, preventing and reducing traffic accidents, protecting personal safety, protecting the property safety and other lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons and other organizations, and improving traffic efficiency.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国境内的车辆驾驶人、行人、乘车人以及与道路交通活动有关的单位和个人，都应当遵守本法。

**Article 2** Any vehicle driver, pedestrian, passenger, and any unit or individual related to road traffic activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall observe this Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　道路交通安全工作，应当遵循依法管理、方便群众的原则，保障道路交通有序、安全、畅通。

**Article 3** The work on road traffic safety shall follow the principles of administration according to law and convenience for the people, so as to ensure orderly, safe and smooth road traffic.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级人民政府应当保障道路交通安全管理工作与经济建设和社会发展相适应。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府应当适应道路交通发展的需要，依据道路交通安全法律、法规和国家有关政策，制定道路交通安全管理规划，并组织实施。

**Article 4** People's governments at all levels shall ensure that the road traffic safety administration is compatible with economic construction and social development.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in light of the needs of road traffic development, formulate plans for road traffic safety administration in accordance with the laws, regulations and relevant state policies on road traffic safety, and organize their implementation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院公安部门负责全国道路交通安全管理工作。县级以上地方各级人民政府公安机关交通管理部门负责本行政区域内的道路交通安全管理工作。  
县级以上各级人民政府交通、建设管理部门依据各自职责，负责有关的道路交通工作。

**Article 5** The public security department under the State Council shall be in charge of the road traffic safety administration nationwide. The traffic administrative departments of the public security organs of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of the road traffic safety administration within their respective administrative areas.  
The departments of communications and construction of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, pursuant to their respective functions and duties, be in charge of the relevant road traffic work.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各级人民政府应当经常进行道路交通安全教育，提高公民的道路交通安全意识。  
公安机关交通管理部门及其交通警察执行职务时，应当加强道路交通安全法律、法规的宣传，并模范遵守道路交通安全法律、法规。  
机关、部队、企业事业单位、社会团体以及其他组织，应当对本单位的人员进行道路交通安全教育。  
教育行政部门、学校应当将道路交通安全教育纳入法制教育的内容。  
新闻、出版、广播、电视等有关单位，有进行道路交通安全教育的义务。

**Article 6** People's governments at all levels shall constantly carry out road traffic safety education to enhance citizens' awareness of road traffic safety.  
When performing their duties, the traffic administrative departments of the public security organs and their traffic police shall strengthen the publicity of the laws and regulations on road traffic safety, and shall themselves set an example in observing such laws and regulations.  
State organs, army units, enterprises, institutions, social groups and other organizations shall provide road traffic safety education to their own personnel.  
The administrative departments of education and schools shall incorporate road traffic safety education into the content of legal education.  
Units such as news media, publishing houses, radio stations and television stations shall have the obligation to conduct road traffic safety education.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对道路交通安全管理工作，应当加强科学研究，推广、使用先进的管理方法、技术、设备。

**Article 7** In the work on road traffic safety administration, scientific research shall be strengthened, and advanced management methods, technologies and equipment shall be popularized and applied.

### 第二章　车辆和驾驶人

#### 第一节　机动车、非机动车

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家对机动车实行登记制度。机动车经公安机关交通管理部门登记后，方可上道路行驶。尚未登记的机动车，需要临时上道路行驶的，应当取得临时通行牌证。

**Article 8** The State shall implement a registration system for motor vehicles. A motor vehicle may only be driven on roads after being registered with the traffic management department of the public security organ. For a motor vehicle that has not yet been registered and needs to be driven temporarily on roads, a temporary driving permit shall be obtained.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请机动车登记，应当提交以下证明、凭证：  
（一）机动车所有人的身份证明；  
（二）机动车来历证明；  
（三）机动车整车出厂合格证明或者进口机动车进口凭证；  
（四）车辆购置税的完税证明或者免税凭证；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定应当在机动车登记时提交的其他证明、凭证。  
公安机关交通管理部门应当自受理申请之日起五个工作日内完成机动车登记审查工作，对符合前款规定条件的，应当发放机动车登记证书、号牌和行驶证；对不符合前款规定条件的，应当向申请人说明不予登记的理由。  
公安机关交通管理部门以外的任何单位或者个人不得发放机动车号牌或者要求机动车悬挂其他号牌，本法另有规定的除外。  
机动车登记证书、号牌、行驶证的式样由国务院公安部门规定并监制。

**Article 9** To apply for registration of a motor vehicle, the following certificates and vouchers shall be submitted:  
(1) Identity certificate of the owner of the motor vehicle;  
(2) Certificate of origin of the motor vehicle;  
(3) Certificate of conformity for the complete motor vehicle or import certificate for imported motor vehicles;  
(4) Tax payment certificate or tax exemption certificate for vehicle purchase tax;  
(5) Other certificates and vouchers that shall be submitted at the time of motor vehicle registration as provided by laws and administrative regulations.  
The traffic management department of the public security organ shall complete the examination of motor vehicle registration within five working days from the date of acceptance of the application, and shall issue the motor vehicle registration certificate, license plate, and driving license to those that meet the conditions provided in the preceding paragraph; for those that do not meet the conditions, the reasons for not registering shall be explained to the applicant.  
No unit or individual other than the traffic management department of the public security organ shall issue motor vehicle license plates or require motor vehicles to display other license plates, except as otherwise provided in this Law.  
The formats of the motor vehicle registration certificate, license plate, and driving license shall be prescribed and supervised by the public security department of the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　准予登记的机动车应当符合机动车国家安全技术标准。申请机动车登记时，应当接受对该机动车的安全技术检验。但是，经国家机动车产品主管部门依据机动车国家安全技术标准认定的企业生产的机动车型，该车型的新车在出厂时经检验符合机动车国家安全技术标准，获得检验合格证的，免予安全技术检验。

**Article 10** Motor vehicles permitted for registration shall meet the national safety technical standards for motor vehicles. When applying for motor vehicle registration, the motor vehicle shall undergo a safety technical inspection. However, for a model of motor vehicle produced by an enterprise recognized by the competent department for motor vehicle products in accordance with the national safety technical standards for motor vehicles, if the new vehicle of that model passes inspection at the factory meeting the national safety technical standards for motor vehicles and obtains a certificate of inspection, it shall be exempted from safety technical inspection.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　驾驶机动车上道路行驶，应当悬挂机动车号牌，放置检验合格标志、保险标志，并随车携带机动车行驶证。  
机动车号牌应当按照规定悬挂并保持清晰、完整，不得故意遮挡、污损。  
任何单位和个人不得收缴、扣留机动车号牌。

**Article 11** When driving a motor vehicle on a road, a motor vehicle license plate shall be displayed, an inspection qualification mark and an insurance mark shall be placed, and the motor vehicle driving license shall be carried with the vehicle.  
The motor vehicle license plate shall be displayed in accordance with regulations and shall be kept clear and complete; it shall not be intentionally obscured or defaced.  
No unit or individual shall seize or detain a motor vehicle license plate.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　有下列情形之一的，应当办理相应的登记：  
（一）机动车所有权发生转移的；  
（二）机动车登记内容变更的；  
（三）机动车用作抵押的；  
（四）机动车报废的。

**Article 12** In any of the following circumstances, the corresponding registration shall be handled:  
(1) Where the ownership of a motor vehicle is transferred;  
(2) Where the registered content of a motor vehicle is changed;  
(3) Where a motor vehicle is used as a mortgage;  
(4) Where a motor vehicle is scrapped.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对登记后上道路行驶的机动车，应当依照法律、行政法规的规定，根据车辆用途、载客载货数量、使用年限等不同情况，定期进行安全技术检验。对提供机动车行驶证和机动车第三者责任强制保险单的，机动车安全技术检验机构应当予以检验，任何单位不得附加其他条件。对符合机动车国家安全技术标准的，公安机关交通管理部门应当发给检验合格标志。  
对机动车的安全技术检验实行社会化。具体办法由国务院规定。  
机动车安全技术检验实行社会化的地方，任何单位不得要求机动车到指定的场所进行检验。  
公安机关交通管理部门、机动车安全技术检验机构不得要求机动车到指定的场所进行维修、保养。  
机动车安全技术检验机构对机动车检验收取费用，应当严格执行国务院价格主管部门核定的收费标准。

**Article 13** For motor vehicles that are driven on roads after registration, safety technical inspections shall be carried out periodically in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, based on different circumstances such as the vehicle's purpose, passenger and cargo capacity, and age. For those that provide a motor vehicle driving license and a compulsory insurance policy for third-party liability of motor vehicles, the motor vehicle safety technical inspection institution shall conduct the inspection, and no unit shall impose additional conditions. For those that meet the national safety technical standards for motor vehicles, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall issue an inspection qualification mark.  
The safety technical inspection of motor vehicles shall be socialized. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
In areas where the safety technical inspection of motor vehicles is socialized, no unit shall require a motor vehicle to be inspected at a designated place.  
The traffic management department of the public security organ and the motor vehicle safety technical inspection institution shall not require a motor vehicle to be repaired or maintained at a designated place.  
When collecting fees for motor vehicle inspection, the motor vehicle safety technical inspection institution shall strictly implement the charging standards approved by the competent price department of the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家实行机动车强制报废制度，根据机动车的安全技术状况和不同用途，规定不同的报废标准。  
应当报废的机动车必须及时办理注销登记。  
达到报废标准的机动车不得上道路行驶。报废的大型客、货车及其他营运车辆应当在公安机关交通管理部门的监督下解体。

**Article 14** The State implements a mandatory scrapping system for motor vehicles, prescribing different scrapping standards based on the safety technical condition and different purposes of motor vehicles.  
Motor vehicles that shall be scrapped must undergo cancellation registration in a timely manner.  
Motor vehicles that have reached the scrapping standard shall not be driven on roads. Scrapped large passenger vehicles, trucks, and other operating vehicles shall be dismantled under the supervision of the traffic management department of the public security organ.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　警车、消防车、救护车、工程救险车应当按照规定喷涂标志图案，安装警报器、标志灯具。其他机动车不得喷涂、安装、使用上述车辆专用的或者与其相类似的标志图案、警报器或者标志灯具。  
警车、消防车、救护车、工程救险车应当严格按照规定的用途和条件使用。  
公路监督检查的专用车辆，应当依照公路法的规定，设置统一的标志和示警灯。

**Article 15** Police cars, fire engines, ambulances, and engineering rescue vehicles shall, in accordance with regulations, spray emblem patterns, install sirens and signal lamps. Other motor vehicles shall not spray, install, or use emblem patterns, sirens, or signal lamps that are exclusive to or similar to those of the above-mentioned vehicles.  
Police cars, fire engines, ambulances, and engineering rescue vehicles shall be used strictly in accordance with the prescribed purposes and conditions.  
Special vehicles for highway supervision and inspection shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Highway Law, be equipped with uniform markings and warning lights.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　任何单位或者个人不得有下列行为：  
（一）拼装机动车或者擅自改变机动车已登记的结构、构造或者特征；  
（二）改变机动车型号、发动机号、车架号或者车辆识别代号；  
（三）伪造、变造或者使用伪造、变造的机动车登记证书、号牌、行驶证、检验合格标志、保险标志；  
（四）使用其他机动车的登记证书、号牌、行驶证、检验合格标志、保险标志。

**Article 16** No unit or individual shall engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) Assembling a motor vehicle or altering the registered structure, construction, or features of a motor vehicle without authorization;  
(2) Changing the model number, engine number, frame number, or vehicle identification number of a motor vehicle;  
(3) Forging, altering, or using forged or altered motor vehicle registration certificates, license plates, driving licenses, inspection qualification marks, or insurance marks;  
(4) Using the registration certificates, license plates, driving licenses, inspection qualification marks, or insurance marks of other motor vehicles.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家实行机动车第三者责任强制保险制度，设立道路交通事故社会救助基金。具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 17** The State implements a compulsory insurance system for third-party liability of motor vehicles and establishes a road traffic accident social relief fund. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　依法应当登记的非机动车，经公安机关交通管理部门登记后，方可上道路行驶。  
依法应当登记的非机动车的种类，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据当地实际情况规定。  
非机动车的外形尺寸、质量、制动器、车铃和夜间反光装置，应当符合非机动车安全技术标准。

**Article 18** Non-motor vehicles that shall be registered according to law may only be driven on roads after being registered with the traffic management department of the public security organ.  
The types of non-motor vehicles that shall be registered according to law shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government according to local actual conditions.  
The dimensions, mass, brakes, bells, and nighttime reflective devices of non-motor vehicles shall meet the safety technical standards for non-motor vehicles.

#### 第二节　机动车驾驶人

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　驾驶机动车，应当依法取得机动车驾驶证。  
申请机动车驾驶证，应当符合国务院公安部门规定的驾驶许可条件；经考试合格后，由公安机关交通管理部门发给相应类别的机动车驾驶证。  
持有境外机动车驾驶证的人，符合国务院公安部门规定的驾驶许可条件，经公安机关交通管理部门考核合格的，可以发给中国的机动车驾驶证。  
驾驶人应当按照驾驶证载明的准驾车型驾驶机动车；驾驶机动车时，应当随身携带机动车驾驶证。  
公安机关交通管理部门以外的任何单位或者个人，不得收缴、扣留机动车驾驶证。

**Article 19** To drive a motor vehicle, a motor vehicle driving license shall be obtained according to law.  
To apply for a motor vehicle driving license, the applicant shall meet the driving permit conditions prescribed by the public security department of the State Council; after passing the examination, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall issue a motor vehicle driving license of the corresponding category.  
A person holding an overseas motor vehicle driving license who meets the driving permit conditions prescribed by the public security department of the State Council and passes the assessment by the traffic management department of the public security organ may be issued a Chinese motor vehicle driving license.  
A driver shall drive a motor vehicle according to the permitted type of vehicle indicated on the driving license; when driving a motor vehicle, the motor vehicle driving license shall be carried with the driver.  
No unit or individual other than the traffic management department of the public security organ shall seize or detain a motor vehicle driving license.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　机动车的驾驶培训实行社会化，由交通运输主管部门对驾驶培训学校、驾驶培训班实行备案管理，并对驾驶培训活动加强监督，其中专门的拖拉机驾驶培训学校、驾驶培训班由农业（农业机械）主管部门实行监督管理。  
驾驶培训学校、驾驶培训班应当严格按照国家有关规定，对学员进行道路交通安全法律、法规、驾驶技能的培训，确保培训质量。  
任何国家机关以及驾驶培训和考试主管部门不得举办或者参与举办驾驶培训学校、驾驶培训班。

**Article 20** Driving training for motor vehicles shall be socialized, with the competent department of transport implementing record-filing management for driving training schools and driving training classes, and strengthening supervision over driving training activities. Among them, specialized tractor driving training schools and driving training classes shall be subject to the supervision and management of the competent department of agriculture (agricultural machinery).  
Driving training schools and driving training classes shall, in strict accordance with relevant state regulations, provide training on road traffic safety laws, regulations, and driving skills to trainees, ensuring the quality of training.  
No state organ, and no competent department for driving training and examination, shall establish or participate in the establishment of driving training schools or driving training classes.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　驾驶人驾驶机动车上道路行驶前，应当对机动车的安全技术性能进行认真检查；不得驾驶安全设施不全或者机件不符合技术标准等具有安全隐患的机动车。

**Article 21** Before driving a motor vehicle on a road, the driver shall carefully inspect the safety technical performance of the motor vehicle; the driver shall not drive a motor vehicle with safety hazards such as incomplete safety facilities or parts not meeting technical standards.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　机动车驾驶人应当遵守道路交通安全法律、法规的规定，按照操作规范安全驾驶、文明驾驶。  
饮酒、服用国家管制的精神药品或者麻醉药品，或者患有妨碍安全驾驶机动车的疾病，或者过度疲劳影响安全驾驶的，不得驾驶机动车。  
任何人不得强迫、指使、纵容驾驶人违反道路交通安全法律、法规和机动车安全驾驶要求驾驶机动车。

**Article 22** Motor vehicle drivers shall abide by the provisions of road traffic safety laws and regulations, and drive safely and civilly in accordance with operating norms.  
A person who has consumed alcohol, taken psychotropic substances or narcotic drugs under state control, or suffers from a disease that impairs safe driving of a motor vehicle, or is excessively fatigued affecting safe driving, shall not drive a motor vehicle.  
No person shall compel, instruct, or connive at a driver driving a motor vehicle in violation of road traffic safety laws, regulations, and safe driving requirements.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　公安机关交通管理部门依照法律、行政法规的规定，定期对机动车驾驶证实施审验。

**Article 23** The traffic management department of the public security organ shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, periodically inspect motor vehicle driving licenses.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　公安机关交通管理部门对机动车驾驶人违反道路交通安全法律、法规的行为，除依法给予行政处罚外，实行累积记分制度。公安机关交通管理部门对累积记分达到规定分值的机动车驾驶人，扣留机动车驾驶证，对其进行道路交通安全法律、法规教育，重新考试；考试合格的，发还其机动车驾驶证。  
对遵守道路交通安全法律、法规，在一年内无累积记分的机动车驾驶人，可以延长机动车驾驶证的审验期。具体办法由国务院公安部门规定。

**Article 24** The traffic management department of the public security organ shall, in addition to imposing administrative penalties according to law for acts of motor vehicle drivers violating road traffic safety laws and regulations, implement a cumulative points system. The traffic management department of the public security organ shall, for a motor vehicle driver whose cumulative points have reached the prescribed value, detain the motor vehicle driving license, provide education on road traffic safety laws and regulations, and require a re-examination; upon passing the examination, the motor vehicle driving license shall be returned.  
For a motor vehicle driver who complies with road traffic safety laws and regulations and has no cumulative points within one year, the inspection period of the motor vehicle driving license may be extended. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the public security department of the State Council.

### 第三章　道路通行条件

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　全国实行统一的道路交通信号。  
交通信号包括交通信号灯、交通标志、交通标线和交通警察的指挥。  
交通信号灯、交通标志、交通标线的设置应当符合道路交通安全、畅通的要求和国家标准，并保持清晰、醒目、准确、完好。  
根据通行需要，应当及时增设、调换、更新道路交通信号。增设、调换、更新限制性的道路交通信号，应当提前向社会公告，广泛进行宣传。

**Article 25** Unified road traffic signals shall be used throughout the country.  
Traffic signals include traffic lights, traffic signs, road markings and the directions of traffic police.  
The installation of traffic lights, traffic signs and road markings shall comply with the requirements of road traffic safety and smoothness and the national standards, and they shall be kept clear, conspicuous, accurate and in good condition.  
Road traffic signals shall, when necessary, be added, changed or renewed in a timely manner. Where restrictive road traffic signals are added, changed or renewed, a public notice shall be given in advance and extensive publicity shall be conducted.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　交通信号灯由红灯、绿灯、黄灯组成。红灯表示禁止通行，绿灯表示准许通行，黄灯表示警示。

**Article 26** Traffic lights are composed of red lights, green lights and yellow lights. A red light indicates prohibition of passage; a green light indicates permission for passage; and a yellow light indicates a warning.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　铁路与道路平面交叉的道口，应当设置警示灯、警示标志或者安全防护设施。无人看守的铁路道口，应当在距道口一定距离处设置警示标志。

**Article 27** At level crossings of railways and roads, warning lights, warning signs or safety protection facilities shall be installed. At unmanned railway crossings, warning signs shall be installed at a certain distance from the crossings.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　任何单位和个人不得擅自设置、移动、占用、损毁交通信号灯、交通标志、交通标线。  
道路两侧及隔离带上种植的树木或者其他植物，设置的广告牌、管线等，应当与交通设施保持必要的距离，不得遮挡路灯、交通信号灯、交通标志，不得妨碍安全视距，不得影响通行。

**Article 28** No unit or individual may, without authorization, install, move, occupy or damage traffic lights, traffic signs or road markings.  
Trees or other plants planted on both sides of roads or on the central reservations, and billboards, pipelines, etc. installed there shall maintain necessary distances from traffic facilities, shall not block street lamps, traffic lights or traffic signs, shall not obstruct safe sight distance, and shall not hinder traffic.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　道路、停车场和道路配套设施的规划、设计、建设，应当符合道路交通安全、畅通的要求，并根据交通需求及时调整。  
公安机关交通管理部门发现已经投入使用的道路存在交通事故频发路段，或者停车场、道路配套设施存在交通安全严重隐患的，应当及时向当地人民政府报告，并提出防范交通事故、消除隐患的建议，当地人民政府应当及时作出处理决定。

**Article 29** The planning, design and construction of roads, parking lots and road supporting facilities shall satisfy the requirements of road traffic safety and smoothness, and shall be adjusted timely in light of traffic demands.  
Where a traffic administrative department of a public security organ discovers that a road already in use has a section where traffic accidents frequently occur, or that a parking lot or a road supporting facility has a serious hidden danger of traffic safety, it shall promptly report to the local people's government and put forward suggestions for preventing traffic accidents and eliminating the hidden danger, and the local people's government shall make a decision on handling the matter without delay.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　道路出现坍塌、坑漕、水毁、隆起等损毁或者交通信号灯、交通标志、交通标线等交通设施损毁、灭失的，道路、交通设施的养护部门或者管理部门应当设置警示标志并及时修复。  
公安机关交通管理部门发现前款情形，危及交通安全，尚未设置警示标志的，应当及时采取安全措施，疏导交通，并通知道路、交通设施的养护部门或者管理部门。

**Article 30** Where a road is damaged by collapse, potholes, floods, upheaval, or the like, or where traffic facilities such as traffic lights, traffic signs or road markings are damaged or missing, the maintenance department or the administrative department of the road or traffic facilities shall set up warning signs and promptly repair them.  
Where a traffic administrative department of a public security organ discovers a circumstance as mentioned in the preceding paragraph which endangers traffic safety and for which warning signs have not yet been set up, it shall promptly take safety measures to direct traffic and notify the maintenance department or the administrative department of the road or traffic facilities.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　未经许可，任何单位和个人不得占用道路从事非交通活动。

**Article 31** No unit or individual may, without permission, occupy the road to engage in non-traffic activities.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　因工程建设需要占用、挖掘道路，或者跨越、穿越道路架设、增设管线设施，应当事先征得道路主管部门的同意；影响交通安全的，还应当征得公安机关交通管理部门的同意。  
施工作业单位应当在经批准的路段和时间内施工作业，并在距离施工作业地点来车方向安全距离处设置明显的安全警示标志，采取防护措施；施工作业完毕，应当迅速清除道路上的障碍物，消除安全隐患，经道路主管部门和公安机关交通管理部门验收合格，符合通行要求后，方可恢复通行。  
对未中断交通的施工作业道路，公安机关交通管理部门应当加强交通安全监督检查，维护道路交通秩序。

**Article 32** Where it is necessary to occupy or excavate a road, or erect or add pipeline facilities over or across the road for construction projects, consent shall be obtained from the competent department of roads in advance; and where traffic safety may be affected, the consent of the traffic administrative department of the public security organ shall also be obtained.  
The construction operation unit shall carry out the construction operations within the approved section and time period, and shall set up conspicuous safety warning signs at a safe distance from the direction of approaching vehicles to the construction site, and take protective measures; upon completion of the construction operations, it shall promptly remove the obstructions on the road and eliminate the potential safety hazards, and the road may be re-opened for traffic only after the inspection and acceptance by the competent department of roads and the traffic administrative department of the public security organ pass and the requirements for traffic are met.  
With respect to roads under construction where traffic is not closed, the traffic administrative department of the public security organ shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of traffic safety and maintain the order of road traffic.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　新建、改建、扩建的公共建筑、商业街区、居住区、大（中）型建筑等，应当配建、增建停车场；停车泊位不足的，应当及时改建或者扩建；投入使用的停车场不得擅自停止使用或者改作他用。  
在城市道路范围内，在不影响行人、车辆通行的情况下，政府有关部门可以施划停车泊位。

**Article 33** For newly constructed, reconstructed or expanded public buildings, commercial blocks, residential areas, large (medium-sized) buildings, etc., parking lots shall be built or additional ones shall be constructed; where parking berths are insufficient, reconstruction or expansion shall be carried out in a timely manner; parking lots that have been put into use may not be discontinued for use or converted to other uses without authorization.  
Within the scope of urban roads, the relevant government departments may demarcate parking berths, provided that the passage of pedestrians and vehicles shall not be affected.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　学校、幼儿园、医院、养老院门前的道路没有行人过街设施的，应当施划人行横道线，设置提示标志。  
城市主要道路的人行道，应当按照规划设置盲道。盲道的设置应当符合国家标准。

**Article 34** Where there are no pedestrian crossing facilities on the road in front of a school, kindergarten, hospital or nursing home, crosswalks shall be marked and warning signs shall be set up.  
On the sidewalks of main urban roads, blind paths shall be installed in accordance with the plan. The installation of blind paths shall comply with the national standards.

### 第四章　道路通行规定

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　机动车、非机动车实行右侧通行。

**Article 35** Motor vehicles and non-motor vehicles shall keep to the right.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　根据道路条件和通行需要，道路划分为机动车道、非机动车道和人行道的，机动车、非机动车、行人实行分道通行。没有划分机动车道、非机动车道和人行道的，机动车在道路中间通行，非机动车和行人在道路两侧通行。

**Article 36** Where, according to road conditions and traffic needs, the road is divided into motor vehicle lanes, non-motor vehicle lanes, and sidewalks, motor vehicles, non-motor vehicles, and pedestrians shall travel in separate lanes. Where there is no division into motor vehicle lanes, non-motor vehicle lanes, and sidewalks, motor vehicles shall travel in the middle of the road, and non-motor vehicles and pedestrians shall travel on both sides of the road.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　道路划设专用车道的，在专用车道内，只准许规定的车辆通行，其他车辆不得进入专用车道内行驶。

**Article 37** Where a road has dedicated lanes marked, only the prescribed vehicles are permitted to travel in the dedicated lanes, and other vehicles shall not enter the dedicated lanes.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　车辆、行人应当按照交通信号通行；遇有交通警察现场指挥时，应当按照交通警察的指挥通行；在没有交通信号的道路上，应当在确保安全、畅通的原则下通行。

**Article 38** Vehicles and pedestrians shall pass according to traffic signals; when there is a traffic police officer directing traffic on site, they shall pass according to the directions of the traffic police officer; on roads without traffic signals, they shall pass under the principle of ensuring safety and smooth traffic.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　公安机关交通管理部门根据道路和交通流量的具体情况，可以对机动车、非机动车、行人采取疏导、限制通行、禁止通行等措施。遇有大型群众性活动、大范围施工等情况，需要采取限制交通的措施，或者作出与公众的道路交通活动直接有关的决定，应当提前向社会公告。

**Article 39** The traffic management department of the public security organ may, based on the specific conditions of roads and traffic flow, take measures such as diversion, restriction of passage, or prohibition of passage with respect to motor vehicles, non-motor vehicles, and pedestrians. Where it is necessary to take traffic restriction measures due to large-scale public events, extensive construction, or other circumstances, or to make decisions directly related to public road traffic activities, it shall announce them to the public in advance.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　遇有自然灾害、恶劣气象条件或者重大交通事故等严重影响交通安全的情形，采取其他措施难以保证交通安全时，公安机关交通管理部门可以实行交通管制。

**Article 40** Where there are natural disasters, severe weather conditions, major traffic accidents, or other circumstances that seriously affect traffic safety, and where it is difficult to ensure traffic safety by taking other measures, the traffic management department of the public security organ may implement traffic control.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　有关道路通行的其他具体规定，由国务院规定。

**Article 41** Other specific provisions concerning road passage shall be prescribed by the State Council.

#### 第二节　机动车通行规定

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　机动车上道路行驶，不得超过限速标志标明的最高时速。在没有限速标志的路段，应当保持安全车速。  
夜间行驶或者在容易发生危险的路段行驶，以及遇有沙尘、冰雹、雨、雪、雾、结冰等气象条件时，应当降低行驶速度。

**Article 42** A motor vehicle driving on a road shall not exceed the maximum speed indicated by the speed limit sign. On a road section without a speed limit sign, it shall maintain a safe speed.  
When driving at night, on a road section prone to danger, or in meteorological conditions such as sandstorms, hail, rain, snow, fog, or icing, it shall reduce its driving speed.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　同车道行驶的机动车，后车应当与前车保持足以采取紧急制动措施的安全距离。有下列情形之一的，不得超车：  
（一）前车正在左转弯、掉头、超车的；  
（二）与对面来车有会车可能的；  
（三）前车为执行紧急任务的警车、消防车、救护车、工程救险车的；  
（四）行经铁路道口、交叉路口、窄桥、弯道、陡坡、隧道、人行横道、市区交通流量大的路段等没有超车条件的。

**Article 43** A motor vehicle driving in the same lane as another vehicle behind shall maintain a safe distance from the vehicle ahead sufficient to take emergency braking measures. Overtaking is prohibited in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The vehicle ahead is making a left turn, making a U-turn, or overtaking;  
(2) There is a possibility of meeting an oncoming vehicle;  
(3) The vehicle ahead is a police car, fire engine, ambulance, or emergency rescue vehicle performing emergency tasks;  
(4) When passing through a railway crossing, intersection, narrow bridge, curve, steep slope, tunnel, crosswalk, or a section of road with heavy urban traffic flow, where there are no conditions for overtaking.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　机动车通过交叉路口，应当按照交通信号灯、交通标志、交通标线或者交通警察的指挥通过；通过没有交通信号灯、交通标志、交通标线或者交通警察指挥的交叉路口时，应当减速慢行，并让行人和优先通行的车辆先行。

**Article 44** A motor vehicle passing through an intersection shall pass according to traffic signals, traffic signs, traffic markings, or the directions of a traffic police officer; when passing through an intersection without traffic signals, traffic signs, traffic markings, or directions from a traffic police officer, it shall slow down and give way to pedestrians and vehicles with the right of way.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　机动车遇有前方车辆停车排队等候或者缓慢行驶时，不得借道超车或者占用对面车道，不得穿插等候的车辆。  
在车道减少的路段、路口，或者在没有交通信号灯、交通标志、交通标线或者交通警察指挥的交叉路口遇到停车排队等候或者缓慢行驶时，机动车应当依次交替通行。

**Article 45** When a motor vehicle encounters a situation where vehicles ahead are queuing or moving slowly, it shall not overtake by using another lane or occupy the opposite lane, and it shall not cut into the queue.  
On road sections or at intersections where lanes are reduced, or when encountering queuing or slow movement at an intersection without traffic signals, traffic signs, traffic markings, or directions from a traffic police officer, motor vehicles shall take turns to pass in order.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　机动车通过铁路道口时，应当按照交通信号或者管理人员的指挥通行；没有交通信号或者管理人员的，应当减速或者停车，在确认安全后通过。

**Article 46** When passing a railway crossing, a motor vehicle shall pass according to the traffic signals or the directions of the person in charge; if there are no traffic signals or person in charge, it shall slow down or stop, and pass only after confirming that it is safe.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　机动车行经人行横道时，应当减速行驶；遇行人正在通过人行横道，应当停车让行。  
机动车行经没有交通信号的道路时，遇行人横过道路，应当避让。

**Article 47** When approaching a crosswalk, a motor vehicle shall slow down; if a pedestrian is crossing the crosswalk, it shall stop and yield.  
When a motor vehicle is driving on a road without traffic signals and encounters a pedestrian crossing the road, it shall yield.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　机动车载物应当符合核定的载质量，严禁超载；载物的长、宽、高不得违反装载要求，不得遗洒、飘散载运物。  
机动车运载超限的不可解体的物品，影响交通安全的，应当按照公安机关交通管理部门指定的时间、路线、速度行驶，悬挂明显标志。在公路上运载超限的不可解体的物品，并应当依照公路法的规定执行。  
机动车载运爆炸物品、易燃易爆化学物品以及剧毒、放射性等危险物品，应当经公安机关批准后，按指定的时间、路线、速度行驶，悬挂警示标志并采取必要的安全措施。

**Article 48** The cargo carried by a motor vehicle shall comply with the approved load mass; overloading is strictly prohibited; the length, width, and height of the cargo shall not violate the loading requirements, and no cargo shall be spilled or scattered.  
If a motor vehicle carries oversized and indivisible articles that affect traffic safety, it shall drive at the time, on the route, and at the speed designated by the traffic management department of the public security organ, and shall display conspicuous signs. If such oversized and indivisible articles are carried on a highway, the provisions of the Highway Law shall also be complied with.  
If a motor vehicle carries explosive articles, flammable and explosive chemicals, or dangerous articles such as highly toxic or radioactive materials, it shall, after obtaining approval from the public security organ, drive at the designated time, on the designated route, and at the designated speed, display warning signs, and take necessary safety measures.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　机动车载人不得超过核定的人数，客运机动车不得违反规定载货。

**Article 49** The number of passengers carried by a motor vehicle shall not exceed the approved number, and a passenger vehicle shall not carry cargo in violation of the regulations.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　禁止货运机动车载客。  
货运机动车需要附载作业人员的，应当设置保护作业人员的安全措施。

**Article 50** A freight motor vehicle is prohibited from carrying passengers.  
If a freight motor vehicle needs to carry accompanying workers, safety measures shall be installed to protect them.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　机动车行驶时，驾驶人、乘坐人员应当按规定使用安全带，摩托车驾驶人及乘坐人员应当按规定戴安全头盔。

**Article 51** When a motor vehicle is in motion, the driver and passengers shall use seat belts as required, and the driver and passengers of a motorcycle shall wear safety helmets as required.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　机动车在道路上发生故障，需要停车排除故障时，驾驶人应当立即开启危险报警闪光灯，将机动车移至不妨碍交通的地方停放；难以移动的，应当持续开启危险报警闪光灯，并在来车方向设置警告标志等措施扩大示警距离，必要时迅速报警。

**Article 52** When a motor vehicle breaks down on the road and needs to stop to troubleshoot, the driver shall immediately turn on the hazard warning lights and move the vehicle to a place that does not obstruct traffic; if it is difficult to move, the driver shall keep the hazard warning lights on and take measures such as placing warning signs in the direction of oncoming traffic to expand the warning distance, and shall promptly call the police if necessary.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　警车、消防车、救护车、工程救险车执行紧急任务时，可以使用警报器、标志灯具；在确保安全的前提下，不受行驶路线、行驶方向、行驶速度和信号灯的限制，其他车辆和行人应当让行。  
警车、消防车、救护车、工程救险车非执行紧急任务时，不得使用警报器、标志灯具，不享有前款规定的道路优先通行权。

**Article 53** When a police car, fire engine, ambulance, or emergency rescue vehicle is performing emergency tasks, it may use sirens and signal lights; subject to ensuring safety, it is not restricted by the driving route, driving direction, driving speed, or traffic signals, and other vehicles and pedestrians shall yield.  
When a police car, fire engine, ambulance, or emergency rescue vehicle is not performing emergency tasks, it shall not use sirens or signal lights, and shall not enjoy the right of way provided in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　道路养护车辆、工程作业车进行作业时，在不影响过往车辆通行的前提下，其行驶路线和方向不受交通标志、标线限制，过往车辆和人员应当注意避让。  
洒水车、清扫车等机动车应当按照安全作业标准作业；在不影响其他车辆通行的情况下，可以不受车辆分道行驶的限制，但是不得逆向行驶。

**Article 54** When road maintenance vehicles and engineering service vehicles are engaged in operations, their driving route and direction are not restricted by traffic signs and markings, provided that they do not affect the passage of passing vehicles; passing vehicles and persons shall pay attention and yield.  
Sprinkler trucks, street sweepers, and other such motor vehicles shall operate in accordance with safe operating standards; they may not be restricted by lane-specific driving rules provided that they do not affect the passage of other vehicles, but they shall not drive in the opposite direction.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　高速公路、大中城市中心城区内的道路，禁止拖拉机通行。其他禁止拖拉机通行的道路，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据当地实际情况规定。  
在允许拖拉机通行的道路上，拖拉机可以从事货运，但是不得用于载人。

**Article 55** Tractors are prohibited from driving on expressways and on roads within the central urban areas of large and medium-sized cities. Other roads where tractors are prohibited shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government in light of local conditions.  
On roads where tractors are permitted to drive, tractors may be used for freight transport, but shall not be used to carry passengers.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　机动车应当在规定地点停放。禁止在人行道上停放机动车；但是，依照[本法第三十三条](#art-33)规定施划的停车泊位除外。  
在道路上临时停车的，不得妨碍其他车辆和行人通行。

**Article 56** Motor vehicles shall be parked in designated places. Parking on sidewalks is prohibited, except for parking spaces marked in accordance with Article 33 of this Law.  
When temporarily parking on a road, a motor vehicle shall not obstruct the passage of other vehicles and pedestrians.

#### 第三节　非机动车通行规定

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　驾驶非机动车在道路上行驶应当遵守有关交通安全的规定。非机动车应当在非机动车道内行驶；在没有非机动车道的道路上，应当靠车行道的右侧行驶。

**Article 57** A person driving a non-motor vehicle on a road shall comply with relevant traffic safety regulations. Non-motor vehicles shall drive within non-motor vehicle lanes; on roads without non-motor vehicle lanes, they shall drive on the right side of the carriageway.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　残疾人机动轮椅车、电动自行车在非机动车道内行驶时，最高时速不得超过十五公里。

**Article 58** When a motorized wheelchair for the disabled or an electric bicycle runs in the non-motor vehicle lane, the maximum speed shall not exceed fifteen kilometers per hour.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　非机动车应当在规定地点停放。未设停放地点的，非机动车停放不得妨碍其他车辆和行人通行。

**Article 59** Non-motor vehicles shall be parked at designated places. Where no parking place is provided, the parking of non-motor vehicles shall not hinder the passage of other vehicles and pedestrians.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　驾驭畜力车，应当使用驯服的牲畜；驾驭畜力车横过道路时，驾驭人应当下车牵引牲畜；驾驭人离开车辆时，应当拴系牲畜。

**Article 60** When driving an animal-drawn cart, the driver shall use a tame animal; when crossing a road with an animal-drawn cart, the driver shall dismount and lead the animal; when the driver leaves the vehicle, the animal shall be tethered.

#### 第四节　行人和乘车人通行规定

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　行人应当在人行道内行走，没有人行道的靠路边行走。

**Article 61** Pedestrians shall walk on the sidewalk, and where there is no sidewalk, they shall walk close to the roadside.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　行人通过路口或者横过道路，应当走人行横道或者过街设施；通过有交通信号灯的人行横道，应当按照交通信号灯指示通行；通过没有交通信号灯、人行横道的路口，或者在没有过街设施的路段横过道路，应当在确认安全后通过。

**Article 62** When crossing an intersection or crossing a road, pedestrians shall use crosswalks or pedestrian crossing facilities; when crossing a crosswalk with traffic signals, they shall cross in accordance with the traffic signal indications; when crossing an intersection without traffic signals or crosswalks, or crossing a road on a section without pedestrian crossing facilities, they shall cross after confirming safety.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　行人不得跨越、倚坐道路隔离设施，不得扒车、强行拦车或者实施妨碍道路交通安全的其他行为。

**Article 63** Pedestrians shall not step over or sit on road isolation facilities, nor shall they cling to vehicles, forcibly stop vehicles, or commit other acts that impede road traffic safety.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　学龄前儿童以及不能辨认或者不能控制自己行为的精神疾病患者、智力障碍者在道路上通行，应当由其监护人、监护人委托的人或者对其负有管理、保护职责的人带领。  
盲人在道路上通行，应当使用盲杖或者采取其他导盲手段，车辆应当避让盲人。

**Article 64** Preschool children, as well as persons with mental illness or intellectual disability who are unable to recognize or control their own conduct, shall be led by their guardians, persons entrusted by their guardians, or persons responsible for their management and protection when traveling on roads.  
Blind persons traveling on roads shall use a white cane or adopt other guide means, and vehicles shall yield to blind persons.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　行人通过铁路道口时，应当按照交通信号或者管理人员的指挥通行；没有交通信号和管理人员的，应当在确认无火车驶临后，迅速通过。

**Article 65** When crossing a railway crossing, pedestrians shall cross in accordance with traffic signals or the directions of management personnel; where there are no traffic signals or management personnel, they shall cross quickly after confirming that no train is approaching.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　乘车人不得携带易燃易爆等危险物品，不得向车外抛洒物品，不得有影响驾驶人安全驾驶的行为。

**Article 66** Passengers shall not carry flammable, explosive, or other dangerous articles, shall not throw objects out of the vehicle, and shall not engage in acts that affect the driver’s safe driving.

#### 第五节　高速公路的特别规定

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　行人、非机动车、拖拉机、轮式专用机械车、铰接式客车、全挂拖斗车以及其他设计最高时速低于七十公里的机动车，不得进入高速公路。高速公路限速标志标明的最高时速不得超过一百二十公里。

**Article 67** Pedestrians, non-motor vehicles, tractors, special wheeled mechanical vehicles, articulated buses, full-trailer trucks, and other motor vehicles with a designed maximum speed of less than seventy kilometers per hour shall not enter expressways. The maximum speed indicated on expressway speed limit signs shall not exceed one hundred and twenty kilometers per hour.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　机动车在高速公路上发生故障时，应当依照[本法第五十二条](#art-52)的有关规定办理；但是，警告标志应当设置在故障车来车方向一百五十米以外，车上人员应当迅速转移到右侧路肩上或者应急车道内，并且迅速报警。  
机动车在高速公路上发生故障或者交通事故，无法正常行驶的，应当由救援车、清障车拖曳、牵引。

**Article 68** When a motor vehicle breaks down on an expressway, the provisions of Article 52 of this Law shall be followed; however, the warning sign shall be placed at a distance of at least one hundred and fifty meters from the broken-down vehicle in the direction of oncoming traffic, and the persons on board shall promptly move to the right shoulder or emergency lane, and promptly call for help.  
If a motor vehicle breaks down or is involved in a traffic accident on an expressway and cannot run normally, it shall be towed or pulled by a rescue vehicle or a breakdown removal vehicle.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　任何单位、个人不得在高速公路上拦截检查行驶的车辆，公安机关的人民警察依法执行紧急公务除外。

**Article 69** No unit or individual shall intercept or inspect a moving vehicle on an expressway, except when the people's police of a public security organ perform urgent official duties according to law.

### 第五章　交通事故处理

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　在道路上发生交通事故，车辆驾驶人应当立即停车，保护现场；造成人身伤亡的，车辆驾驶人应当立即抢救受伤人员，并迅速报告执勤的交通警察或者公安机关交通管理部门。因抢救受伤人员变动现场的，应当标明位置。乘车人、过往车辆驾驶人、过往行人应当予以协助。  
在道路上发生交通事故，未造成人身伤亡，当事人对事实及成因无争议的，可以即行撤离现场，恢复交通，自行协商处理损害赔偿事宜；不即行撤离现场的，应当迅速报告执勤的交通警察或者公安机关交通管理部门。  
在道路上发生交通事故，仅造成轻微财产损失，并且基本事实清楚的，当事人应当先撤离现场再进行协商处理。

**Article 70** Where a traffic accident occurs on a road, the vehicle driver shall immediately stop the vehicle and protect the scene; where personal injuries or deaths are caused, the vehicle driver shall immediately rescue the injured persons and promptly report to the traffic police on duty or the traffic administrative department of the public security organ. Where the scene is changed due to the rescue of the injured, the location shall be marked. The passengers, passing vehicle drivers and passing pedestrians shall provide assistance.  
Where a traffic accident occurs on a road, causing no personal injuries or deaths, and the parties have no dispute over the facts and causes thereof, they may immediately withdraw from the scene, restore traffic, and negotiate on their own regarding the compensation for damages; where they do not immediately withdraw from the scene, they shall promptly report to the traffic police on duty or the traffic administrative department of the public security organ.  
Where a traffic accident occurs on a road, causing only minor property losses and the basic facts are clear, the parties shall first withdraw from the scene and then negotiate the settlement.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　车辆发生交通事故后逃逸的，事故现场目击人员和其他知情人员应当向公安机关交通管理部门或者交通警察举报。举报属实的，公安机关交通管理部门应当给予奖励。

**Article 71** Where a vehicle runs away after causing a traffic accident, eyewitnesses at the scene of the accident and other persons who know the situation shall report to the traffic administrative department of the public security organ or the traffic police. Where such a report is verified, the traffic administrative department of the public security organ shall give a reward.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　公安机关交通管理部门接到交通事故报警后，应当立即派交通警察赶赴现场，先组织抢救受伤人员，并采取措施，尽快恢复交通。  
交通警察应当对交通事故现场进行勘验、检查，收集证据；因收集证据的需要，可以扣留事故车辆，但是应当妥善保管，以备核查。  
对当事人的生理、精神状况等专业性较强的检验，公安机关交通管理部门应当委托专门机构进行鉴定。鉴定结论应当由鉴定人签名。

**Article 72** Upon receiving a report of a traffic accident, the traffic administrative department of the public security organ shall immediately dispatch traffic police to the scene, first organize the rescue of the injured persons, and take measures to restore traffic as soon as possible.  
The traffic police shall inspect and examine the scene of the traffic accident and collect evidence; they may, for the purpose of collecting evidence, detain the vehicle involved in the accident, but they shall keep it properly for inspection.  
With respect to examinations of a highly professional nature such as the physiological or mental conditions of the parties involved, the traffic administrative department of the public security organ shall entrust a specialized agency to conduct an expert evaluation. The expert evaluation conclusion shall be signed by the expert evaluator.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　公安机关交通管理部门应当根据交通事故现场勘验、检查、调查情况和有关的检验、鉴定结论，及时制作交通事故认定书，作为处理交通事故的证据。交通事故认定书应当载明交通事故的基本事实、成因和当事人的责任，并送达当事人。

**Article 73** The traffic management department of the public security organ shall, based on the inspection, examination, investigation of the traffic accident scene and relevant test and expert evaluation conclusions, promptly prepare a traffic accident identification certificate as evidence for handling the traffic accident. The traffic accident identification certificate shall contain the basic facts, causes, and the liability of the parties involved in the traffic accident, and shall be served on the parties.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　对交通事故损害赔偿的争议，当事人可以请求公安机关交通管理部门调解，也可以直接向人民法院提起民事诉讼。  
经公安机关交通管理部门调解，当事人未达成协议或者调解书生效后不履行的，当事人可以向人民法院提起民事诉讼。

**Article 74** In the event of a dispute over compensation for damage caused by a traffic accident, the parties may request mediation by the traffic management department of the public security organ, or may directly initiate a civil action in the People's Court.  
If the parties fail to reach an agreement through mediation by the traffic management department of the public security organ, or if the mediation agreement becomes effective and is not performed, the parties may initiate a civil action in the People's Court.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　医疗机构对交通事故中的受伤人员应当及时抢救，不得因抢救费用未及时支付而拖延救治。肇事车辆参加机动车第三者责任强制保险的，由保险公司在责任限额范围内支付抢救费用；抢救费用超过责任限额的，未参加机动车第三者责任强制保险或者肇事后逃逸的，由道路交通事故社会救助基金先行垫付部分或者全部抢救费用，道路交通事故社会救助基金管理机构有权向交通事故责任人追偿。

**Article 75** Medical institutions shall promptly rescue the injured in a traffic accident and shall not delay treatment due to the failure to pay rescue costs in a timely manner. If the vehicle causing the accident is covered by compulsory motor vehicle third-party liability insurance, the insurance company shall pay the rescue costs within the liability limit; if the rescue costs exceed the liability limit, or if the vehicle is not covered by compulsory motor vehicle third-party liability insurance, or if the person causing the accident flees after the accident, the road traffic accident social assistance fund shall advance part or all of the rescue costs, and the management institution of the road traffic accident social assistance fund has the right to recover from the person liable for the traffic accident.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　机动车发生交通事故造成人身伤亡、财产损失的，由保险公司在机动车第三者责任强制保险责任限额范围内予以赔偿；不足的部分，按照下列规定承担赔偿责任：  
（一）机动车之间发生交通事故的，由有过错的一方承担赔偿责任；双方都有过错的，按照各自过错的比例分担责任。  
（二）机动车与非机动车驾驶人、行人之间发生交通事故，非机动车驾驶人、行人没有过错的，由机动车一方承担赔偿责任；有证据证明非机动车驾驶人、行人有过错的，根据过错程度适当减轻机动车一方的赔偿责任；机动车一方没有过错的，承担不超过百分之十的赔偿责任。  
交通事故的损失是由非机动车驾驶人、行人故意碰撞机动车造成的，机动车一方不承担赔偿责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/最高人民法院关于审理道路交通事故损害赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-14" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理道路交通事故损害赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第14条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的解释.md#art-192" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的解释》第192条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 76** If a motor vehicle traffic accident causes personal injury or death or property damage, the insurance company shall provide compensation within the liability limit of the compulsory motor vehicle third-party liability insurance; for the part that is insufficient, liability for compensation shall be borne in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) In the case of a traffic accident between motor vehicles, the party at fault shall bear the liability for compensation; if both parties are at fault, they shall share the liability in proportion to their respective faults.  
(2) In the case of a traffic accident between a motor vehicle and a non-motor vehicle driver or pedestrian, if the non-motor vehicle driver or pedestrian is not at fault, the motor vehicle party shall bear the liability for compensation; if there is evidence that the non-motor vehicle driver or pedestrian is at fault, the motor vehicle party's liability for compensation shall be appropriately reduced based on the degree of fault; if the motor vehicle party is not at fault, it shall bear no more than ten percent of the liability for compensation.  
If the loss from a traffic accident is caused by the non-motor vehicle driver or pedestrian intentionally colliding with the motor vehicle, the motor vehicle party shall not bear any liability for compensation.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　车辆在道路以外通行时发生的事故，公安机关交通管理部门接到报案的，参照本法有关规定办理。

**Article 77** For accidents occurring when vehicles are traveling outside roads, if the traffic management department of the public security organ receives a report, it shall handle the matter by reference to the relevant provisions of this Law.

### 第六章　执法监督

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　公安机关交通管理部门应当加强对交通警察的管理，提高交通警察的素质和管理道路交通的水平。  
公安机关交通管理部门应当对交通警察进行法制和交通安全管理业务培训、考核。交通警察经考核不合格的，不得上岗执行职务。

**Article 78** The traffic management department of the public security organ shall strengthen the management of traffic police, improve the quality of traffic police and the level of road traffic management.  
The traffic management department of the public security organ shall provide legal education and traffic safety management professional training and assessment for traffic police. Traffic police who fail the assessment shall not assume their posts to perform duties.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　公安机关交通管理部门及其交通警察实施道路交通安全管理，应当依据法定的职权和程序，简化办事手续，做到公正、严格、文明、高效。

**Article 79** The traffic management department of the public security organ and its traffic police shall implement road traffic safety management in accordance with statutory powers and procedures, simplify procedures, and achieve fairness, strictness, civility, and efficiency.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　交通警察执行职务时，应当按照规定着装，佩带人民警察标志，持有人民警察证件，保持警容严整，举止端庄，指挥规范。

**Article 80** When performing duties, traffic police shall wear uniforms as required, wear the insignia of the people's police, hold a people's police identification card, maintain a dignified appearance, behave properly, and give commands in a standardized manner.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　依照本法发放牌证等收取工本费，应当严格执行国务院价格主管部门核定的收费标准，并全部上缴国库。

**Article 81** The fees charged for the issuance of licenses and certificates in accordance with this Law shall strictly comply with the charging standards approved by the pricing authority of the State Council, and all such fees shall be turned over to the State Treasury.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　公安机关交通管理部门依法实施罚款的行政处罚，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，实施罚款决定与罚款收缴分离；收缴的罚款以及依法没收的违法所得，应当全部上缴国库。

**Article 82** When the traffic management department of the public security organ imposes the administrative penalty of a fine in accordance with law, it shall implement the separation of the fine decision and the fine collection in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; the fines collected and the illegal gains confiscated according to law shall all be turned over to the State Treasury.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　交通警察调查处理道路交通安全违法行为和交通事故，有下列情形之一的，应当回避：  
（一）是本案的当事人或者当事人的近亲属；  
（二）本人或者其近亲属与本案有利害关系；  
（三）与本案当事人有其他关系，可能影响案件的公正处理。

**Article 83** If a traffic police officer, when investigating and handling a road traffic safety violation and a traffic accident, falls under any of the following circumstances, he or she shall recuse himself or herself:  
(1) being a party to the case or a close relative of a party;  
(2) having a personal interest in the case; or  
(3) having another relationship with a party to the case that may affect the impartial handling of the case.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　公安机关交通管理部门及其交通警察的行政执法活动，应当接受行政监察机关依法实施的监督。  
公安机关督察部门应当对公安机关交通管理部门及其交通警察执行法律、法规和遵守纪律的情况依法进行监督。  
上级公安机关交通管理部门应当对下级公安机关交通管理部门的执法活动进行监督。

**Article 84** The administrative law enforcement activities of the traffic management department of the public security organ and its traffic police shall be subject to the supervision of the administrative supervision organs in accordance with law.  
The internal supervision department of the public security organ shall conduct supervision over the enforcement of laws and regulations and compliance with discipline by the traffic management department of the public security organ and its traffic police in accordance with law.  
The higher-level traffic management department of the public security organ shall supervise the law enforcement activities of the lower-level traffic management department of the public security organ.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　公安机关交通管理部门及其交通警察执行职务，应当自觉接受社会和公民的监督。  
任何单位和个人都有权对公安机关交通管理部门及其交通警察不严格执法以及违法违纪行为进行检举、控告。收到检举、控告的机关，应当依据职责及时查处。

**Article 85** The traffic management department of the public security organ and its traffic police, when performing their duties, shall consciously accept supervision from society and citizens.  
Any entity or individual has the right to report or accuse the traffic management department of the public security organ and its traffic police of lax enforcement of law or illegal or disciplinary violations. The organ that receives such reports or accusations shall promptly investigate and handle them in accordance with its duties.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　任何单位不得给公安机关交通管理部门下达或者变相下达罚款指标；公安机关交通管理部门不得以罚款数额作为考核交通警察的标准。  
公安机关交通管理部门及其交通警察对超越法律、法规规定的指令，有权拒绝执行，并同时向上级机关报告。

**Article 86** No entity shall assign or indirectly assign fine quotas to the traffic management department of the public security organ; the traffic management department of the public security organ shall not use the amount of fines as a criterion for assessing traffic police.  
The traffic management department of the public security organ and its traffic police have the right to refuse to execute any instruction that exceeds the provisions of laws and regulations, and shall simultaneously report to the superior organ.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　公安机关交通管理部门及其交通警察对道路交通安全违法行为，应当及时纠正。  
公安机关交通管理部门及其交通警察应当依据事实和本法的有关规定对道路交通安全违法行为予以处罚。对于情节轻微，未影响道路通行的，指出违法行为，给予口头警告后放行。

**Article 87** The traffic management department of the public security organ and its traffic police shall promptly correct road traffic safety violations.  
The traffic management department of the public security organ and its traffic police shall impose penalties on road traffic safety violations based on facts and the relevant provisions of this Law. For minor violations that do not affect road traffic, the illegal act shall be pointed out, an oral warning shall be given, and the person shall be released.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　对道路交通安全违法行为的处罚种类包括：警告、罚款、暂扣或者吊销机动车驾驶证、拘留。

**Article 88** The types of penalties for road traffic safety violations include: warning, fine, temporary suspension or revocation of the motor vehicle driving license, and detention.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　行人、乘车人、非机动车驾驶人违反道路交通安全法律、法规关于道路通行规定的，处警告或者五元以上五十元以下罚款；非机动车驾驶人拒绝接受罚款处罚的，可以扣留其非机动车。

**Article 89** If a pedestrian, passenger, or non-motor vehicle driver violates the provisions of road traffic safety laws and regulations on road passage, a warning or a fine of not less than five Yuan but not more than fifty Yuan shall be imposed; if a non-motor vehicle driver refuses to accept the fine penalty, his or her non-motor vehicle may be impounded.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　机动车驾驶人违反道路交通安全法律、法规关于道路通行规定的，处警告或者二十元以上二百元以下罚款。本法另有规定的，依照规定处罚。

**Article 90** If a motor vehicle driver violates the provisions of road traffic safety laws and regulations on road passage, a warning or a fine of not less than twenty Yuan but not more than two hundred Yuan shall be imposed. If this Law provides otherwise, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with those provisions.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　饮酒后驾驶机动车的，处暂扣六个月机动车驾驶证，并处一千元以上二千元以下罚款。因饮酒后驾驶机动车被处罚，再次饮酒后驾驶机动车的，处十日以下拘留，并处一千元以上二千元以下罚款，吊销机动车驾驶证。  
醉酒驾驶机动车的，由公安机关交通管理部门约束至酒醒，吊销机动车驾驶证，依法追究刑事责任；五年内不得重新取得机动车驾驶证。  
饮酒后驾驶营运机动车的，处十五日拘留，并处五千元罚款，吊销机动车驾驶证，五年内不得重新取得机动车驾驶证。  
醉酒驾驶营运机动车的，由公安机关交通管理部门约束至酒醒，吊销机动车驾驶证，依法追究刑事责任；十年内不得重新取得机动车驾驶证，重新取得机动车驾驶证后，不得驾驶营运机动车。  
饮酒后或者醉酒驾驶机动车发生重大交通事故，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任，并由公安机关交通管理部门吊销机动车驾驶证，终生不得重新取得机动车驾驶证。

**Article 91** If a person drives a motor vehicle after consuming alcohol, his or her motor vehicle driving license shall be suspended for six months, and a fine of not less than one thousand Yuan but not more than two thousand Yuan shall be imposed. If a person who has been penalized for driving a motor vehicle after consuming alcohol drives a motor vehicle after consuming alcohol again, he or she shall be detained for not more than ten days, and a fine of not less than one thousand Yuan but not more than two thousand Yuan shall be imposed, and his or her motor vehicle driving license shall be revoked.  
If a person drives a motor vehicle while intoxicated, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall restrain him or her until sober, revoke his or her motor vehicle driving license, and investigate criminal liability in accordance with law; he or she shall not re-obtain a motor vehicle driving license within five years.  
If a person drives a commercial motor vehicle after consuming alcohol, he or she shall be detained for fifteen days, and a fine of five thousand Yuan shall be imposed, and his or her motor vehicle driving license shall be revoked, and he or she shall not re-obtain a motor vehicle driving license within five years.  
If a person drives a commercial motor vehicle while intoxicated, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall restrain him or her until sober, revoke his or her motor vehicle driving license, and investigate criminal liability in accordance with law; he or she shall not re-obtain a motor vehicle driving license within ten years, and after re-obtaining a motor vehicle driving license, he or she shall not drive a commercial motor vehicle.  
If a person, after consuming alcohol or while intoxicated, drives a motor vehicle and causes a major traffic accident constituting a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law, and the traffic management department of the public security organ shall revoke his or her motor vehicle driving license, and he or she shall not re-obtain a motor vehicle driving license for life.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　公路客运车辆载客超过额定乘员的，处二百元以上五百元以下罚款；超过额定乘员百分之二十或者违反规定载货的，处五百元以上二千元以下罚款。  
货运机动车超过核定载质量的，处二百元以上五百元以下罚款；超过核定载质量百分之三十或者违反规定载客的，处五百元以上二千元以下罚款。  
有前两款行为的，由公安机关交通管理部门扣留机动车至违法状态消除。  
运输单位的车辆有本条第一款、第二款规定的情形，经处罚不改的，对直接负责的主管人员处二千元以上五千元以下罚款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/交通运输/中华人民共和国道路交通安全法实施条例.md#art-107" title="被《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法实施条例》第107条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 92** If a highway passenger vehicle carries passengers exceeding the rated number, a fine of not less than two hundred Yuan but not more than five hundred Yuan shall be imposed; if it exceeds the rated number by twenty percent or carries cargo in violation of regulations, a fine of not less than five hundred Yuan but not more than two thousand Yuan shall be imposed.  
If a freight motor vehicle exceeds the approved load mass, a fine of not less than two hundred Yuan but not more than five hundred Yuan shall be imposed; if it exceeds the approved load mass by thirty percent or carries passengers in violation of regulations, a fine of not less than five hundred Yuan but not more than two thousand Yuan shall be imposed.  
If a vehicle commits any of the acts specified in the preceding two paragraphs, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall impound the motor vehicle until the illegal state is eliminated.  
If the vehicle of a transport unit is found to be in the circumstances specified in paragraph 1 or paragraph 2 of this Article, and after being penalized, fails to rectify the situation, the person directly in charge shall be fined not less than two thousand Yuan but not more than five thousand Yuan.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　对违反道路交通安全法律、法规关于机动车停放、临时停车规定的，可以指出违法行为，并予以口头警告，令其立即驶离。  
机动车驾驶人不在现场或者虽在现场但拒绝立即驶离，妨碍其他车辆、行人通行的，处二十元以上二百元以下罚款，并可以将该机动车拖移至不妨碍交通的地点或者公安机关交通管理部门指定的地点停放。公安机关交通管理部门拖车不得向当事人收取费用，并应当及时告知当事人停放地点。  
因采取不正确的方法拖车造成机动车损坏的，应当依法承担补偿责任。

**Article 93** Where a person violates the provisions of road traffic safety laws and regulations on parking or temporary stopping of motor vehicles, the illegal act may be pointed out, and an oral warning shall be given, ordering the person to leave the spot immediately.  
Where the motor vehicle driver is not present at the scene, or is present but refuses to leave immediately, thereby obstructing the passage of other vehicles or pedestrians, a fine of not less than 20 yuan but not more than 200 yuan shall be imposed, and the motor vehicle may be towed to a place that does not obstruct traffic or to a place designated by the traffic control department of the public security organ for parking. The traffic control department of the public security organ shall not charge any fee for towing the vehicle and shall promptly inform the party of the parking location.  
Where damage is caused to the motor vehicle due to improper towing methods, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　机动车安全技术检验机构实施机动车安全技术检验超过国务院价格主管部门核定的收费标准收取费用的，退还多收取的费用，并由价格主管部门依照《中华人民共和国价格法》的有关规定给予处罚。  
机动车安全技术检验机构不按照机动车国家安全技术标准进行检验，出具虚假检验结果的，由公安机关交通管理部门处所收检验费用五倍以上十倍以下罚款，并依法撤销其检验资格；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 94** Where a motor vehicle safety technical inspection institution charges fees exceeding the charging standards approved by the pricing authority of the State Council for conducting motor vehicle safety technical inspections, the excess fees shall be refunded, and the pricing authority shall impose a penalty in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Price Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where a motor vehicle safety technical inspection institution fails to conduct inspections in accordance with the national safety technical standards for motor vehicles and issues false inspection results, the traffic control department of the public security organ shall impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the inspection fees collected, and shall revoke its inspection qualification according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　上道路行驶的机动车未悬挂机动车号牌，未放置检验合格标志、保险标志，或者未随车携带行驶证、驾驶证的，公安机关交通管理部门应当扣留机动车，通知当事人提供相应的牌证、标志或者补办相应手续，并可以依照[本法第九十条](#art-90)的规定予以处罚。当事人提供相应的牌证、标志或者补办相应手续的，应当及时退还机动车。  
故意遮挡、污损或者不按规定安装机动车号牌的，依照[本法第九十条](#art-90)的规定予以处罚。

**Article 95** Where a motor vehicle driven on the road does not have a motor vehicle license plate, does not have the inspection合格标志 or insurance标志 placed, or does not carry the vehicle travel permit or the driver's license, the traffic control department of the public security organ shall detain the motor vehicle, notify the party to provide the corresponding license plates and marks or go through the corresponding formalities, and may impose a penalty in accordance with Article 90 of this Law. Where the party provides the corresponding license plates and marks or completes the corresponding formalities, the motor vehicle shall be promptly returned.  
Where the motor vehicle license plate is intentionally covered, defaced, or not installed in accordance with the provisions, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with Article 90 of this Law.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　伪造、变造或者使用伪造、变造的机动车登记证书、号牌、行驶证、驾驶证的，由公安机关交通管理部门予以收缴，扣留该机动车，处十五日以下拘留，并处二千元以上五千元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
伪造、变造或者使用伪造、变造的检验合格标志、保险标志的，由公安机关交通管理部门予以收缴，扣留该机动车，处十日以下拘留，并处一千元以上三千元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
使用其他车辆的机动车登记证书、号牌、行驶证、检验合格标志、保险标志的，由公安机关交通管理部门予以收缴，扣留该机动车，处二千元以上五千元以下罚款。  
当事人提供相应的合法证明或者补办相应手续的，应当及时退还机动车。

**Article 96** Where a person forges, alters, or uses forged or altered motor vehicle registration certificates, license plates, travel permits, or driving licenses, the traffic control department of the public security organ shall confiscate them, detain the motor vehicle, impose a detention of not more than 15 days, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a person forges, alters, or uses forged or altered inspection合格标志 or insurance标志, the traffic control department of the public security organ shall confiscate them, detain the motor vehicle, impose a detention of not more than 10 days, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 3,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a person uses the motor vehicle registration certificates, license plates, travel permits, inspection合格标志, or insurance标志 of another vehicle, the traffic control department of the public security organ shall confiscate them, detain the motor vehicle, and impose a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan.  
Where the party provides corresponding legal evidence or goes through the corresponding formalities, the motor vehicle shall be promptly returned.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　非法安装警报器、标志灯具的，由公安机关交通管理部门强制拆除，予以收缴，并处二百元以上二千元以下罚款。

**Article 97** Where a person illegally installs an alarm or marking lights, the traffic control department of the public security organ shall forcibly remove and confiscate them, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 200 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　机动车所有人、管理人未按照国家规定投保机动车第三者责任强制保险的，由公安机关交通管理部门扣留车辆至依照规定投保后，并处依照规定投保最低责任限额应缴纳的保险费的二倍罚款。  
依照前款缴纳的罚款全部纳入道路交通事故社会救助基金。具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 98** Where the owner or manager of a motor vehicle fails to take out compulsory third-party liability insurance for the motor vehicle in accordance with State regulations, the traffic control department of the public security organ shall detain the vehicle until the insurance is taken out in accordance with the regulations, and concurrently impose a fine of twice the premium payable for the minimum liability coverage according to the regulations.  
All fines paid in accordance with the preceding paragraph shall be incorporated into the road traffic accident social assistance fund. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　有下列行为之一的，由公安机关交通管理部门处二百元以上二千元以下罚款：  
（一）未取得机动车驾驶证、机动车驾驶证被吊销或者机动车驾驶证被暂扣期间驾驶机动车的；  
（二）将机动车交由未取得机动车驾驶证或者机动车驾驶证被吊销、暂扣的人驾驶的；  
（三）造成交通事故后逃逸，尚不构成犯罪的；  
（四）机动车行驶超过规定时速百分之五十的；  
（五）强迫机动车驾驶人违反道路交通安全法律、法规和机动车安全驾驶要求驾驶机动车，造成交通事故，尚不构成犯罪的；  
（六）违反交通管制的规定强行通行，不听劝阻的；  
（七）故意损毁、移动、涂改交通设施，造成危害后果，尚不构成犯罪的；  
（八）非法拦截、扣留机动车辆，不听劝阻，造成交通严重阻塞或者较大财产损失的。  
行为人有前款第二项、第四项情形之一的，可以并处吊销机动车驾驶证；有第一项、第三项、第五项至第八项情形之一的，可以并处十五日以下拘留。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国公路法.md#art-83" title="被《中华人民共和国公路法》第83条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 99** Where a person commits any of the following acts, the traffic control department of the public security organ shall impose a fine of not less than 200 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan:  
(1) driving a motor vehicle without obtaining a motor vehicle driving license, or during the period when the driving license is revoked or temporarily impounded;  
(2) handing over a motor vehicle to be driven by a person who has not obtained a driving license or whose driving license is revoked or temporarily impounded;  
(3) fleeing after causing a traffic accident, where the act does not constitute a crime;  
(4) driving a motor vehicle exceeding the prescribed speed limit by 50%;  
(5) compelling a motor vehicle driver to drive the motor vehicle in violation of road traffic safety laws, regulations, and safe driving requirements, thereby causing a traffic accident, where the act does not constitute a crime;  
(6) forcibly passing through in violation of traffic control regulations and refusing to heed persuasion;  
(7) intentionally damaging, removing, or defacing traffic facilities, thereby causing harmful consequences, where the act does not constitute a crime;  
(8) illegally intercepting or detaining motor vehicles, refusing to heed persuasion, thereby causing serious traffic congestion or significant property loss.  
Where the actor has any of the circumstances in items (2) and (4) of the preceding paragraph, the driving license may be revoked concurrently; where the actor has any of the circumstances in items (1), (3), and (5) through (8) of the preceding paragraph, a detention of not more than 15 days may be imposed concurrently.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　驾驶拼装的机动车或者已达到报废标准的机动车上道路行驶的，公安机关交通管理部门应当予以收缴，强制报废。  
对驾驶前款所列机动车上道路行驶的驾驶人，处二百元以上二千元以下罚款，并吊销机动车驾驶证。  
出售已达到报废标准的机动车的，没收违法所得，处销售金额等额的罚款，对该机动车依照本条第一款的规定处理。

**Article 100** Where a person drives an assembled motor vehicle or a motor vehicle that has reached the scrapping standard on the road, the traffic control department of the public security organ shall confiscate the vehicle and scrap it compulsorily.  
The driver who drives any of the motor vehicles listed in the preceding paragraph on the road shall be fined not less than 200 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan, and his/her motor vehicle driving license shall be revoked.  
Where a person sells a motor vehicle that has reached the scrapping standard, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, a fine equal to the sales amount shall be imposed, and the motor vehicle shall be dealt with in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of this Article.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　违反道路交通安全法律、法规的规定，发生重大交通事故，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任，并由公安机关交通管理部门吊销机动车驾驶证。  
造成交通事故后逃逸的，由公安机关交通管理部门吊销机动车驾驶证，且终生不得重新取得机动车驾驶证。

**Article 101** Where a person violates the provisions of road traffic safety laws and regulations, causes a major traffic accident, and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law, and the traffic control department of the public security organ shall revoke the motor vehicle driving license.  
Where a person flees after causing a traffic accident, the traffic control department of the public security organ shall revoke the motor vehicle driving license, and the person shall not be permitted to obtain a new driving license for life.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　对六个月内发生二次以上特大交通事故负有主要责任或者全部责任的专业运输单位，由公安机关交通管理部门责令消除安全隐患，未消除安全隐患的机动车，禁止上道路行驶。

**Article 102** Where a specialized transport unit bears primary or full responsibility for having two or more extraordinarily serious traffic accidents within six months, the traffic control department of the public security organ shall order it to eliminate potential safety hazards; motor vehicles that have not eliminated such hazards shall be prohibited from being driven on the road.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　国家机动车产品主管部门未按照机动车国家安全技术标准严格审查，许可不合格机动车型投入生产的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予降级或者撤职的行政处分。  
机动车生产企业经国家机动车产品主管部门许可生产的机动车型，不执行机动车国家安全技术标准或者不严格进行机动车成品质量检验，致使质量不合格的机动车出厂销售的，由质量技术监督部门依照《中华人民共和国产品质量法》的有关规定给予处罚。  
擅自生产、销售未经国家机动车产品主管部门许可生产的机动车型的，没收非法生产、销售的机动车成品及配件，可以并处非法产品价值三倍以上五倍以下罚款；有营业执照的，由工商行政管理部门吊销营业执照，没有营业执照的，予以查封。  
生产、销售拼装的机动车或者生产、销售擅自改装的机动车的，依照本条第三款的规定处罚。  
有本条第二款、第三款、第四款所列违法行为，生产或者销售不符合机动车国家安全技术标准的机动车，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 103** Where the State competent authority for motor vehicle products fails to strictly examine in accordance with the national safety technical standards for motor vehicles and permits a non-conforming motor vehicle model to be put into production, the person in charge who bears responsibility and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions of demotion or removal from office.  
Where a motor vehicle manufacturer that produces a motor vehicle model permitted by the State competent authority for motor vehicle products fails to implement the national safety technical standards for motor vehicles or fails to strictly carry out quality inspection of finished motor vehicles, thereby allowing substandard motor vehicles to leave the factory for sale, the quality and technical supervision department shall impose a penalty in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Product Quality Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where a person produces or sells a motor vehicle model without the permission of the State competent authority for motor vehicle products, the illegally produced or sold motor vehicle finished products and parts shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the value of the illegal products may be concurrently imposed; if the person has a business license, the administrative authority for industry and commerce shall revoke the license; if there is no business license, the business shall be sealed up.  
Where a person produces or sells an assembled motor vehicle or produces or sells a motor vehicle that has been illegally modified, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 3 of this Article.  
Where a person commits any of the illegal acts listed in paragraphs 2, 3, and 4 of this Article, producing or selling motor vehicles that do not meet the national safety technical standards for motor vehicles, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　未经批准，擅自挖掘道路、占用道路施工或者从事其他影响道路交通安全活动的，由道路主管部门责令停止违法行为，并恢复原状，可以依法给予罚款；致使通行的人员、车辆及其他财产遭受损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
有前款行为，影响道路交通安全活动的，公安机关交通管理部门可以责令停止违法行为，迅速恢复交通。

**Article 104** Where a person, without approval, digs up a road, occupies a road for construction, or engages in other activities affecting road traffic safety, the road authority shall order the cessation of the illegal act and restoration to the original condition, and may impose a fine according to law; if such act causes loss to persons, vehicles or other property passing by, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.  
Where an act as described in the preceding paragraph occurs and affects road traffic safety, the traffic control department of the public security organ may order the cessation of the illegal act and promptly restore traffic.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　道路施工作业或者道路出现损毁，未及时设置警示标志、未采取防护措施，或者应当设置交通信号灯、交通标志、交通标线而没有设置或者应当及时变更交通信号灯、交通标志、交通标线而没有及时变更，致使通行的人员、车辆及其他财产遭受损失的，负有相关职责的单位应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 105** Where construction operations on a road or damage to a road occurs, and warning signs are not set up in a timely manner, protective measures are not taken, or traffic signals, traffic signs, or traffic markings that should be installed are not installed, or traffic signals, traffic signs, or traffic markings that should be promptly changed are not changed in a timely manner, thereby causing loss to persons, vehicles, or other property passing by, the unit with relevant responsibilities shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　在道路两侧及隔离带上种植树木、其他植物或者设置广告牌、管线等，遮挡路灯、交通信号灯、交通标志，妨碍安全视距的，由公安机关交通管理部门责令行为人排除妨碍；拒不执行的，处二百元以上二千元以下罚款，并强制排除妨碍，所需费用由行为人负担。

**Article 106** Where trees or other plants are planted or advertising boards, pipelines, etc. are installed on both sides of a road or on the central divider, which obscure street lights, traffic signals, or traffic signs, or obstruct safe sight distance, the traffic control department of the public security organ shall order the actor to remove the obstruction; if the actor refuses to comply, a fine of not less than 200 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the obstruction shall be forcibly removed, with the costs borne by the actor.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　对道路交通违法行为人予以警告、二百元以下罚款，交通警察可以当场作出行政处罚决定，并出具行政处罚决定书。  
行政处罚决定书应当载明当事人的违法事实、行政处罚的依据、处罚内容、时间、地点以及处罚机关名称，并由执法人员签名或者盖章。

**Article 107** Where a road traffic violator is given a warning or a fine of not more than 200 yuan, the traffic police may make an administrative penalty decision on the spot and issue a written administrative penalty decision.  
The written administrative penalty decision shall state the illegal facts of the party, the basis for the administrative penalty, the penalty content, time, place, and the name of the penalty authority, and shall be signed or sealed by the law enforcement officer.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　当事人应当自收到罚款的行政处罚决定书之日起十五日内，到指定的银行缴纳罚款。  
对行人、乘车人和非机动车驾驶人的罚款，当事人无异议的，可以当场予以收缴罚款。  
罚款应当开具省、自治区、直辖市财政部门统一制发的罚款收据；不出具财政部门统一制发的罚款收据的，当事人有权拒绝缴纳罚款。

**Article 108** The party shall, within 15 days from the date of receiving the written administrative penalty decision on the fine, go to the designated bank to pay the fine.  
For fines imposed on pedestrians, passengers, and non-motor vehicle drivers, if the party has no objection, the fine may be collected on the spot.  
The fine shall be issued with a fine receipt uniformly printed by the finance department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; if the fine receipt uniformly printed by the finance department is not issued, the party shall have the right to refuse to pay the fine.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　当事人逾期不履行行政处罚决定的，作出行政处罚决定的行政机关可以采取下列措施：  
（一）到期不缴纳罚款的，每日按罚款数额的百分之三加处罚款；  
（二）申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 109** Where the party fails to comply with the administrative penalty decision within the prescribed time limit, the administrative organ that made the decision may take the following measures:  
(1) If the fine is not paid within the time limit, an additional fine of 3% of the fine amount shall be imposed for each day of delay;  
(2) Apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　执行职务的交通警察认为应当对道路交通违法行为人给予暂扣或者吊销机动车驾驶证处罚的，可以先予扣留机动车驾驶证，并在二十四小时内将案件移交公安机关交通管理部门处理。  
道路交通违法行为人应当在十五日内到公安机关交通管理部门接受处理。无正当理由逾期未接受处理的，吊销机动车驾驶证。  
公安机关交通管理部门暂扣或者吊销机动车驾驶证的，应当出具行政处罚决定书。

**Article 110** Where a traffic police officer on duty believes that a road traffic violator shall be given the penalty of temporarily impounding or revoking the motor vehicle driving license, the officer may first take custody of the driving license and shall transfer the case to the traffic control department of the public security organ for handling within 24 hours.  
The road traffic violator shall, within 15 days, go to the traffic control department of the public security organ to accept handling. If the violator fails to accept handling within the time limit without justifiable reasons, the driving license shall be revoked.  
Where the traffic control department of the public security organ temporarily impounds or revokes a motor vehicle driving license, it shall issue a written administrative penalty decision.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　对违反本法规定予以拘留的行政处罚，由县、市公安局、公安分局或者相当于县一级的公安机关裁决。

**Article 111** The administrative penalty of detention imposed for violating the provisions of this Law shall be decided by the public security bureau of a county or city, a public security sub-bureau, or a public security organ at the county level or equivalent.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　公安机关交通管理部门扣留机动车、非机动车，应当当场出具凭证，并告知当事人在规定期限内到公安机关交通管理部门接受处理。  
公安机关交通管理部门对被扣留的车辆应当妥善保管，不得使用。  
逾期不来接受处理，并且经公告三个月仍不来接受处理的，对扣留的车辆依法处理。

**Article 112** Where the traffic control department of the public security organ detains a motor vehicle or a non-motor vehicle, it shall issue a receipt on the spot and inform the party to go to the department to accept handling within the prescribed time limit.  
The traffic control department of the public security organ shall properly keep the detained vehicle and shall not use it.  
If the party fails to come to accept handling within the time limit, and after public announcement for three months still fails to do so, the detained vehicle shall be dealt with according to law.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　暂扣机动车驾驶证的期限从处罚决定生效之日起计算；处罚决定生效前先予扣留机动车驾驶证的，扣留一日折抵暂扣期限一日。  
吊销机动车驾驶证后重新申请领取机动车驾驶证的期限，按照机动车驾驶证管理规定办理。

**Article 113** The period of temporary seizure of a motor vehicle driving license shall be calculated from the date on which the penalty decision takes effect; if the motor vehicle driving license is detained before the penalty decision takes effect, one day of detention shall be offset against one day of the temporary seizure period.  
The period for re-applying for a motor vehicle driving license after its revocation shall be handled in accordance with the regulations on the administration of motor vehicle driving licenses.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　公安机关交通管理部门根据交通技术监控记录资料，可以对违法的机动车所有人或者管理人依法予以处罚。对能够确定驾驶人的，可以依照本法的规定依法予以处罚。

**Article 114** The public security organ's traffic management department may, on the basis of traffic technical surveillance records, impose penalties according to law on the owner or manager of a motor vehicle that has violated the law. Where the driver can be identified, penalties may be imposed according to law in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　交通警察有下列行为之一的，依法给予行政处分：  
（一）为不符合法定条件的机动车发放机动车登记证书、号牌、行驶证、检验合格标志的；  
（二）批准不符合法定条件的机动车安装、使用警车、消防车、救护车、工程救险车的警报器、标志灯具，喷涂标志图案的；  
（三）为不符合驾驶许可条件、未经考试或者考试不合格人员发放机动车驾驶证的；  
（四）不执行罚款决定与罚款收缴分离制度或者不按规定将依法收取的费用、收缴的罚款及没收的违法所得全部上缴国库的；  
（五）举办或者参与举办驾驶学校或者驾驶培训班、机动车修理厂或者收费停车场等经营活动的；  
（六）利用职务上的便利收受他人财物或者谋取其他利益的；  
（七）违法扣留车辆、机动车行驶证、驾驶证、车辆号牌的；  
（八）使用依法扣留的车辆的；  
（九）当场收取罚款不开具罚款收据或者不如实填写罚款额的；  
（十）徇私舞弊，不公正处理交通事故的；  
（十一）故意刁难，拖延办理机动车牌证的；  
（十二）非执行紧急任务时使用警报器、标志灯具的；  
（十三）违反规定拦截、检查正常行驶的车辆的；  
（十四）非执行紧急公务时拦截搭乘机动车的；  
（十五）不履行法定职责的。  
公安机关交通管理部门有前款所列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予相应的行政处分。

**Article 115** Where a traffic police officer commits any of the following acts, he shall be given an administrative sanction according to law:  
(1) issuing a motor vehicle registration certificate, license plate, driving permit, or inspection合格标志 to a motor vehicle that does not meet the statutory conditions;  
(2) approving the installation or use of alarms, signal lamps, or the painting of标志 patterns on police cars, fire engines, ambulances, or engineering rescue vehicles for motor vehicles that do not meet the statutory conditions;  
(3) issuing a motor vehicle driving license to a person who does not meet the driving permit conditions, has not taken the examination, or has failed the examination;  
(4) failing to implement the system of separating penalty decisions from fine collection, or failing to turn over to the state treasury in full all fees collected according to law, fines collected, and illegal gains confiscated;  
(5) establishing or participating in the establishment of driving schools or driving training classes, motor vehicle repair plants, or paid parking lots, or engaging in similar business activities;  
(6) taking advantage of his position to accept property from others or seek other benefits;  
(7) illegally detaining vehicles, motor vehicle driving permits, driving licenses, or vehicle license plates;  
(8) using vehicles detained according to law;  
(9) collecting fines on the spot without issuing a fine receipt or without truthfully filling in the amount of the fine;  
(10) engaging in malpractice for personal gain and failing to handle traffic accidents impartially;  
(11) deliberately creating difficulties or delaying the processing of motor vehicle licenses and plates;  
(12) using alarms or signal lamps when not on an emergency mission;  
(13) stopping or inspecting a normally moving vehicle in violation of regulations;  
(14) stopping and boarding a motor vehicle when not on an emergency official mission;  
(15) failing to perform statutory duties.  
Where a public security organ's traffic management department commits any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given corresponding administrative sanctions.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　依照[本法第一百一十五条](#art-115)的规定，给予交通警察行政处分的，在作出行政处分决定前，可以停止其执行职务；必要时，可以予以禁闭。  
依照[本法第一百一十五条](#art-115)的规定，交通警察受到降级或者撤职行政处分的，可以予以辞退。  
交通警察受到开除处分或者被辞退的，应当取消警衔；受到撤职以下行政处分的交通警察，应当降低警衔。

**Article 116** Where a traffic police officer is given an administrative sanction in accordance with the provisions of Article 115 of this Law, he may be suspended from duty before the decision on the administrative sanction is made; if necessary, he may be placed in confinement.  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 115 of this Law, a traffic police officer who receives an administrative sanction of demotion or dismissal from his post may be discharged.  
A traffic police officer who is expelled or discharged shall have his police rank cancelled; a traffic police officer who receives an administrative sanction below dismissal from his post shall have his police rank reduced.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　交通警察利用职权非法占有公共财物，索取、收受贿赂，或者滥用职权、玩忽职守，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 117** Where a traffic police officer, by taking advantage of his office, illegally takes public property, extorts or accepts bribes, or abuses his power or neglects his duty, and such act constitutes a crime, he shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　公安机关交通管理部门及其交通警察有[本法第一百一十五条](#art-115)所列行为之一，给当事人造成损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 118** Where a public security organ's traffic management department or any of its traffic police officers commits any act listed in Article 115 of this Law and causes losses to a party, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　本法中下列用语的含义：  
（一）“道路”，是指公路、城市道路和虽在单位管辖范围但允许社会机动车通行的地方，包括广场、公共停车场等用于公众通行的场所。  
（二）“车辆”，是指机动车和非机动车。  
（三）“机动车”，是指以动力装置驱动或者牵引，上道路行驶的供人员乘用或者用于运送物品以及进行工程专项作业的轮式车辆。  
（四）“非机动车”，是指以人力或者畜力驱动，上道路行驶的交通工具，以及虽有动力装置驱动但设计最高时速、空车质量、外形尺寸符合有关国家标准的残疾人机动轮椅车、电动自行车等交通工具。  
（五）“交通事故”，是指车辆在道路上因过错或者意外造成的人身伤亡或者财产损失的事件。

**Article 119** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them below:  
(1) “Road” means highways, urban roads, and places that are within the jurisdiction of a unit but are accessible to social motor vehicles, including squares, public parking lots, and other places used for public passage.  
(2) “Vehicle” means motor vehicles and non-motor vehicles.  
(3) “Motor vehicle” means a wheeled vehicle that is driven or towed by a power unit, used on roads for the conveyance of persons or for the transport of goods, or for special engineering operations.  
(4) “Non-motor vehicle” means a means of transport driven by human or animal power for use on roads, as well as a motorized wheelchair for the disabled, an electric bicycle, or other means of transport that, although driven by a power unit, have a design maximum speed, unladen mass, and dimensions that comply with the relevant national standards.  
(5) “Traffic accident” means an incident in which a vehicle on a road causes personal injury or death or property loss due to fault or accident.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队在编机动车牌证、在编机动车检验以及机动车驾驶人考核工作，由中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队有关部门负责。

**Article 120** The issuance of license plates and permits for motor vehicles on the active list of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, the inspection of such motor vehicles, and the examination of their drivers shall be the responsibility of the relevant departments of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　对上道路行驶的拖拉机，由农业（农业机械）主管部门行使[本法第八条](#art-8)、第九条、第十三条、第十九条、第二十三条规定的公安机关交通管理部门的管理职权。  
农业（农业机械）主管部门依照前款规定行使职权，应当遵守本法有关规定，并接受公安机关交通管理部门的监督；对违反规定的，依照本法有关规定追究法律责任。  
本法施行前由农业（农业机械）主管部门发放的机动车牌证，在本法施行后继续有效。

**Article 121** For tractors driven on roads, the competent department of agriculture (agricultural machinery) shall exercise the administrative functions and powers of the public security organ's traffic management department as provided for in Articles 8, 9, 13, 19, and 23 of this Law.  
Where the competent department of agriculture (agricultural machinery) exercises its functions and powers in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, it shall comply with the relevant provisions of this Law and shall accept supervision by the public security organ's traffic management department; anyone who violates the provisions shall be pursued for legal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Law.  
Motor vehicle licenses and plates issued by the competent department of agriculture (agricultural machinery) before this Law came into force shall remain valid after this Law comes into force.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　国家对入境的境外机动车的道路交通安全实施统一管理。

**Article 122** The State shall exercise unified management of road traffic safety for foreign motor vehicles entering the country.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会常务委员会可以根据本地区的实际情况，在本法规定的罚款幅度内，规定具体的执行标准。

**Article 123** The standing committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in light of the actual conditions of their respective regions and within the range of fines provided for in this Law, prescribe specific enforcement standards.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　本法自2004年5月1日起施行。

**Article 124** This Law shall come into force as of May 1, 2004.
