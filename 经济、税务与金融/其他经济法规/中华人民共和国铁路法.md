### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障铁路运输和铁路建设的顺利进行，适应社会主义现代化建设和人民生活的需要，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of ensuring the smooth progress of railway transport and railway construction, and meeting the needs of socialist modernization and the people's livelihood.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称铁路，包括国家铁路、地方铁路、专用铁路和铁路专用线。  
国家铁路是指由国务院铁路主管部门管理的铁路。  
地方铁路是指由地方人民政府管理的铁路。  
专用铁路是指由企业或者其他单位管理，专为本企业或者本单位内部提供运输服务的铁路。  
铁路专用线是指由企业或者其他单位管理的与国家铁路或者其他铁路线路接轨的岔线。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, railways include national railways, local railways, industrial railways and railway sidings.  
National railways refer to railways under the administration of the competent railway department under the State Council.  
Local railways refer to railways under the administration of local people's governments.  
Industrial railways refer to railways under the administration of enterprises or other entities, providing transport services exclusively for such enterprises or entities.  
Railway sidings refer to spur lines under the administration of enterprises or other entities which are connected with national railways or other railway lines.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院铁路主管部门主管全国铁路工作，对国家铁路实行高度集中、统一指挥的运输管理体制，对地方铁路、专用铁路和铁路专用线进行指导、协调、监督和帮助。  
国家铁路运输企业行使法律、行政法规授予的行政管理职能。

**Article 3** The competent railway department under the State Council shall be in charge of national railway work, implement a transport management system of highly centralized and unified command for national railways, and provide guidance, coordination, supervision and assistance for local railways, industrial railways and railway sidings.  
National railway transport enterprises shall exercise the administrative functions granted by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家重点发展国家铁路，大力扶持地方铁路的发展。

**Article 4** The State shall develop national railways as a priority, and vigorously support the development of local railways.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　铁路运输企业必须坚持社会主义经营方向和为人民服务的宗旨，改善经营管理，切实改进路风，提高运输服务质量。

**Article 5** Railway transport enterprises must adhere to the socialist orientation of operation and the principle of serving the people, improve management, earnestly rectify work style, and raise the quality of transport services.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　公民有爱护铁路设施的义务。禁止任何人破坏铁路设施，扰乱铁路运输的正常秩序。

**Article 6** Citizens shall have the duty to protect railway facilities. No person shall destroy railway facilities or disrupt the normal order of railway transport.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　铁路沿线各级地方人民政府应当协助铁路运输企业保证铁路运输安全畅通，车站、列车秩序良好，铁路设施完好和铁路建设顺利进行。

**Article 7** Local people's governments at all levels along the railway lines shall assist railway transport enterprises in ensuring the safe and smooth operation of railway transport, good order at stations and on trains, the integrity of railway facilities, and the smooth progress of railway construction.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家铁路的技术管理规程，由国务院铁路主管部门制定，地方铁路、专用铁路的技术管理办法，参照国家铁路的技术管理规程制定。

**Article 8** The technical management regulations for national railways shall be formulated by the competent railway department under the State Council. The technical management measures for local railways and industrial railways shall be formulated with reference to the technical management regulations for national railways.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家鼓励铁路科学技术研究，提高铁路科学技术水平。对在铁路科学技术研究中有显著成绩的单位和个人给予奖励。

**Article 9** The State encourages scientific and technological research in railways to raise the level of railway science and technology. Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in railway scientific and technological research shall be given awards.

### 第二章　铁路运输营业

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　铁路运输企业应当保证旅客和货物运输的安全，做到列车正点到达。

**Article 10** Railway transport enterprises shall ensure the safety of passenger and cargo transport and ensure that trains arrive on schedule.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　铁路运输合同是明确铁路运输企业与旅客、托运人之间权利义务关系的协议。  
旅客车票、行李票、包裹票和货物运单是合同或者合同的组成部分。

**Article 11** A railway transport contract is an agreement that defines the rights and obligations between a railway transport enterprise and passengers or consignors.  
Passenger tickets, luggage tickets, parcel tickets and consignment notes are the contract or constituent parts of the contract.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　铁路运输企业应当保证旅客按车票载明的日期、车次乘车，并到达目的站。因铁路运输企业的责任造成旅客不能按车票载明的日期、车次乘车的，铁路运输企业应当按照旅客的要求，退还全部票款或者安排改乘到达相同目的站的其他列车。

**Article 12** Railway transport enterprises shall ensure that passengers travel on the date and train specified on their tickets and arrive at the destination station. If a passenger cannot travel on the date and train specified on the ticket due to the fault of the railway transport enterprise, the railway transport enterprise shall, at the passenger's request, refund the full fare or arrange for the passenger to take another train to the same destination.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　铁路运输企业应当采取有效措施做好旅客运输服务工作，做到文明礼貌、热情周到，保持车站和车厢内的清洁卫生，提供饮用开水，做好列车上的饮食供应工作。  
铁路运输企业应当采取措施，防止对铁路沿线环境的污染。

**Article 13** Railway transport enterprises shall take effective measures to provide good passenger transport services, be courteous and considerate, maintain cleanliness and hygiene in stations and carriages, provide drinking water, and ensure food and beverage supply on trains.  
Railway transport enterprises shall take measures to prevent pollution of the environment along railway lines.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　旅客乘车应当持有效车票。对无票乘车或者持失效车票乘车的，应当补收票款，并按照规定加收票款；拒不交付的，铁路运输企业可以责令下车。

**Article 14** Passengers shall hold valid tickets when traveling by train. Those who travel without a ticket or with an invalid ticket shall pay the fare and an additional charge as prescribed; if they refuse to pay, the railway transport enterprise may order them to get off the train.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家铁路和地方铁路根据发展生产、搞活流通的原则，安排货物运输计划。  
对抢险救灾物资和国家规定需要优先运输的其他物资，应予优先运输。  
地方铁路运输的物资需要经由国家铁路运输的，其运输计划应当纳入国家铁路的运输计划。

**Article 15** National railways and local railways shall arrange cargo transport plans based on the principle of developing production and invigorating circulation.  
Priority shall be given to the transport of materials for emergency rescue and disaster relief, and other materials that require priority transport as prescribed by the State.  
Where materials transported by local railways need to be carried via national railways, the transport plan shall be incorporated into the transport plan of the national railways.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　铁路运输企业应当按照合同约定的期限或者国务院铁路主管部门规定的期限，将货物、包裹、行李运到目的站；逾期运到的，铁路运输企业应当支付违约金。  
铁路运输企业逾期三十日仍未将货物、包裹、行李交付收货人或者旅客的，托运人、收货人或者旅客有权按货物、包裹、行李灭失向铁路运输企业要求赔偿。

**Article 16** Railway transport enterprises shall transport cargo, parcels and luggage to the destination station within the time limit agreed in the contract or prescribed by the competent railway department under the State Council; if delivery is overdue, the railway transport enterprise shall pay liquidated damages.  
If a railway transport enterprise fails to deliver cargo, parcels or luggage to the consignee or passenger within 30 days after the expiry of the time limit, the consignor, consignee or passenger shall be entitled to claim compensation from the railway transport enterprise on the basis of loss of the cargo, parcels or luggage.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　铁路运输企业应当对承运的货物、包裹、行李自接受承运时起到交付时止发生的灭失、短少、变质、污染或者损坏，承担赔偿责任：  
（一）托运人或者旅客根据自愿申请办理保价运输的，按照实际损失赔偿，但最高不超过保价额。  
（二）未按保价运输承运的，按照实际损失赔偿，但最高不超过国务院铁路主管部门规定的赔偿限额；如果损失是由于铁路运输企业的故意或者重大过失造成的，不适用赔偿限额的规定，按照实际损失赔偿。  
托运人或者旅客根据自愿可以向保险公司办理货物运输保险，保险公司按照保险合同的约定承担赔偿责任。  
托运人或者旅客根据自愿，可以办理保价运输，也可以办理货物运输保险；还可以既不办理保价运输，也不办理货物运输保险。不得以任何方式强迫办理保价运输或者货物运输保险。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知》第2条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知.md#art-3" title="被《最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知》第3条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知.md#art-4" title="被《最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知》第4条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知.md#art-5" title="被《最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知》第5条引用">[5]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知.md#art-6" title="被《最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知》第6条引用">[6]</a></sup>

**Article 17** A railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability for loss, shortage, deterioration, contamination or damage to cargo, parcels or luggage occurring from the time of acceptance for carriage to the time of delivery:  
(1) If the consignor or passenger voluntarily applies for value-insured transport, compensation shall be based on the actual loss, but shall not exceed the declared value;  
(2) If carriage is not on a value-insured basis, compensation shall be based on the actual loss, but shall not exceed the compensation limit prescribed by the competent railway department under the State Council; if the loss is caused by the intentional act or gross negligence of the railway transport enterprise, the compensation limit shall not apply, and compensation shall be made according to the actual loss.  
A consignor or passenger may voluntarily take out cargo transport insurance with an insurance company, and the insurance company shall bear compensation liability in accordance with the insurance contract.  
A consignor or passenger may, at its own discretion, choose value-insured transport or cargo transport insurance, or may choose neither. No one shall compel value-insured transport or cargo transport insurance by any means.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　由于下列原因造成的货物、包裹、行李损失的，铁路运输企业不承担赔偿责任：  
（一）不可抗力。  
（二）货物或者包裹、行李中的物品本身的自然属性，或者合理损耗。  
（三）托运人、收货人或者旅客的过错。

**Article 18** A railway transport enterprise shall not bear compensation liability for loss of cargo, parcels or luggage caused by the following reasons:  
(1) Force majeure;  
(2) The inherent nature of the goods or items in parcels or luggage, or reasonable wastage;  
(3) Fault of the consignor, consignee or passenger.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　托运人应当如实填报托运单，铁路运输企业有权对填报的货物和包裹的品名、重量、数量进行检查。经检查，申报与实际不符的，检查费用由托运人承担；申报与实际相符的，检查费用由铁路运输企业承担，因检查对货物和包裹中的物品造成的损坏由铁路运输企业赔偿。  
托运人因申报不实而少交的运费和其他费用应当补交，铁路运输企业按照国务院铁路主管部门的规定加收运费和其他费用。

**Article 19** The consignor shall truthfully fill in the consignment note. The railway transport enterprise shall have the right to inspect the names, weights and quantities of the declared cargo and parcels. If after inspection the declaration is found to be inconsistent with the actual situation, the inspection expenses shall be borne by the consignor; if the declaration is consistent, the inspection expenses shall be borne by the railway transport enterprise, and any damage to the items in the cargo or parcels caused by the inspection shall be compensated by the railway transport enterprise.  
If the consignor underpays freight and other charges due to false declaration, the shortfall shall be made up, and the railway transport enterprise shall impose a surcharge on the freight and other charges in accordance with the provisions of the competent railway department under the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　托运货物需要包装的，托运人应当按照国家包装标准或者行业包装标准包装；没有国家包装标准或者行业包装标准的，应当妥善包装，使货物在运输途中不因包装原因而受损坏。  
铁路运输企业对承运的容易腐烂变质的货物和活动物，应当按照国务院铁路主管部门的规定和合同的约定，采取有效的保护措施。

**Article 20** Where cargo to be consigned requires packaging, the consignor shall pack it in accordance with the national packaging standards or trade packaging standards; in the absence of such standards, it shall be properly packed to prevent damage during transport due to packaging.  
For perishable goods and live animals accepted for carriage, the railway transport enterprise shall take effective protective measures in accordance with the provisions of the competent railway department under the State Council and the stipulations of the contract.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　货物、包裹、行李到站后，收货人或者旅客应当按照国务院铁路主管部门规定的期限及时领取，并支付托运人未付或者少付的运费和其他费用；逾期领取的，收货人或者旅客应当按照规定交付保管费。

**Article 21** After arrival of goods, parcels or luggage at the station, the consignee or passenger shall collect them within the period prescribed by the competent department of railways under the State Council and pay the freight or other expenses that have not been paid or have been underpaid by the consignor; for delayed collection, the consignee or passenger shall pay storage charges in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　自铁路运输企业发出领取货物通知之日起满三十日仍无人领取的货物，或者收货人书面通知铁路运输企业拒绝领取的货物，铁路运输企业应当通知托运人，托运人自接到通知之日起满三十日未作答复的，由铁路运输企业变卖；所得价款在扣除保管等费用后尚有余款的，应当退还托运人，无法退还、自变卖之日起一百八十日内托运人又未领回的，上缴国库。  
自铁路运输企业发出领取通知之日起满九十日仍无人领取的包裹或者到站后满九十日仍无人领取的行李，铁路运输企业应当公告，公告满九十日仍无人领取的，可以变卖；所得价款在扣除保管等费用后尚有余款的，托运人、收货人或者旅客可以自变卖之日起一百八十日内领回，逾期不领回的，上缴国库。  
对危险物品和规定限制运输的物品，应当移交公安机关或者有关部门处理，不得自行变卖。  
对不宜长期保存的物品，可以按照国务院铁路主管部门的规定缩短处理期限。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知.md#art-9" title="被《最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知》第9条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 22** If goods are not claimed by anyone within 30 days from the date of the notice of arrival issued by the railway transport enterprise, or if the consignee notifies the railway transport enterprise in writing of refusal to take delivery of the goods, the railway transport enterprise shall notify the consignor. If the consignor fails to reply within 30 days from receipt of the said notice, the railway transport enterprise may sell the goods by auction. The proceeds, after deduction of storage charges and other expenses, shall be refunded to the consignor; if such refund is impossible and the consignor fails to claim the proceeds within 180 days from the date of sale, the proceeds shall be turned over to the State Treasury.  
If a parcel is not claimed by anyone within 90 days from the date of the notice of arrival issued by the railway transport enterprise, or luggage is not claimed within 90 days after arrival at the station, the railway transport enterprise shall make an announcement. If still unclaimed after 90 days from the date of the announcement, the goods may be sold by auction. The proceeds, after deduction of storage charges and other expenses, may be claimed by the consignor, consignee or passenger within 180 days from the date of sale; if not claimed within the time limit, the proceeds shall be turned over to the State Treasury.  
Dangerous goods and articles the transportation of which is restricted by provisions shall be handed over to the public security organ or relevant department for disposal and may not be sold by auction without authorization.  
For articles not suitable for long-term storage, the time limit for disposal may be shortened in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of railways under the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　因旅客、托运人或者收货人的责任给铁路运输企业造成财产损失的，由旅客、托运人或者收货人承担赔偿责任。

**Article 23** Where property losses are caused to a railway transport enterprise due to the fault of a passenger, consignor or consignee, the passenger, consignor or consignee shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家鼓励专用铁路兼办公共旅客、货物运输营业；提倡铁路专用线与有关单位按照协议共用。  
专用铁路兼办公共旅客、货物运输营业的，应当报经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准。  
专用铁路兼办公共旅客、货物运输营业的，适用本法关于铁路运输企业的规定。

**Article 24** The State encourages special railways to additionally engage in public passenger and freight transport services; and advocates that railway sidings be used jointly by the relevant entities under agreements.  
If a special railway additionally engages in public passenger and freight transport services, it shall report to the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval.  
Where a special railway additionally engages in public passenger and freight transport services, the provisions of this Law concerning railway transport enterprises shall apply.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　铁路的旅客票价率和货物、行李的运价率实行政府指导价或者政府定价，竞争性领域实行市场调节价。政府指导价、政府定价的定价权限和具体适用范围以中央政府和地方政府的定价目录为依据。铁路旅客、货物运输杂费的收费项目和收费标准，以及铁路包裹运价率由铁路运输企业自主制定。

**Article 25** The passenger fare rates and freight rates for goods and luggage on railways shall be subject to government-guided prices or government-set prices, while market-regulated prices shall apply in competitive areas. The pricing authority and specific scope of application for government-guided prices and government-set prices shall be based on the pricing catalogues of the central government and local governments. The items and standards of incidental charges for railway passenger and freight transport, as well as parcel transport rates, shall be independently determined by railway transport enterprises.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　铁路的旅客票价，货物、包裹、行李的运价，旅客和货物运输杂费的收费项目和收费标准，必须公告；未公告的不得实施。

**Article 26** The passenger fares, freight rates for goods, parcels and luggage, and the items and standards of incidental charges for passenger and freight transport shall be announced; no such charges may be implemented without announcement.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家铁路、地方铁路和专用铁路印制使用的旅客、货物运输票证，禁止伪造和变造。  
禁止倒卖旅客车票和其他铁路运输票证。

**Article 27** The forgery or alteration of passenger and freight transport tickets and certificates printed and used by national railways, local railways and special railways is prohibited.  
The scalping of passenger tickets and other railway transport tickets and certificates is prohibited.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　托运、承运货物、包裹、行李，必须遵守国家关于禁止或者限制运输物品的规定。

**Article 28** In consigning or carrying goods, parcels or luggage, the provisions of the State concerning articles prohibited or restricted for transport must be complied with.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　铁路运输企业与公路、航空或者水上运输企业相互间实行国内旅客、货物联运，依照国家有关规定办理；国家没有规定的，依照有关各方的协议办理。

**Article 29** Domestic passenger and freight through-transport between railway transport enterprises and highway, air or water transport enterprises shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; where the State has no provisions, such through-transport shall be handled in accordance with agreements between the parties concerned.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家铁路、地方铁路参加国际联运，必须经国务院批准。

**Article 30** The participation of national railways or local railways in international through-transport must be approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　铁路军事运输依照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 31** Military transport by rail shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　发生铁路运输合同争议的，铁路运输企业和托运人、收货人或者旅客可以通过调解解决；不愿意调解解决或者调解不成的，可以依据合同中的仲裁条款或者事后达成的书面仲裁协议，向国家规定的仲裁机构申请仲裁。  
当事人一方在规定的期限内不履行仲裁机构的仲裁决定的，另一方可以申请人民法院强制执行。  
当事人没有在合同中订立仲裁条款，事后又没有达成书面仲裁协议的，可以向人民法院起诉。

**Article 32** Where a dispute arises over a railway transport contract, the railway transport enterprise and the consignor, consignee or passenger may settle the dispute through mediation; if mediation is not desired or fails, either party may apply for arbitration to the arbitration institution designated by the State according to the arbitration clause in the contract or a written arbitration agreement reached afterwards.  
If one party fails to comply with the arbitration award within the prescribed time limit, the other party may apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.  
If the parties have not included an arbitration clause in the contract and have not reached a written arbitration agreement afterwards, either party may institute a prosecution in the People's Court.

### 第三章　铁路建设

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　铁路发展规划应当依据国民经济和社会发展以及国防建设的需要制定，并与其他方式的交通运输发展规划相协调。

**Article 33** Railway development plans shall be formulated in accordance with the needs of national economic and social development and national defense construction, and shall be coordinated with the development plans of other modes of transport.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　地方铁路、专用铁路、铁路专用线的建设计划必须符合全国铁路发展规划，并征得国务院铁路主管部门或者国务院铁路主管部门授权的机构的同意。

**Article 34** The construction plans for local railways, special railways and railway sidings must conform to the national railway development plan and obtain the consent of the competent department of railways under the State Council or an institution authorized by it.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　在城市规划区范围内，铁路的线路、车站、枢纽以及其他有关设施的规划，应当纳入所在城市的总体规划。  
铁路建设用地规划，应当纳入土地利用总体规划。为远期扩建、新建铁路需要的土地，由县级以上人民政府在土地利用总体规划中安排。

**Article 35** Within the urban planning area, the planning of railway lines, stations, hubs and other related facilities shall be incorporated into the overall planning of the city concerned.  
The planning of land used for railway construction shall be incorporated into the overall planning of land utilization. Land needed for long-term expansion and new railway construction shall be arranged by the people's governments at or above the county level in the overall planning of land utilization.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　铁路建设用地，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定办理。  
有关地方人民政府应当支持铁路建设，协助铁路运输企业做好铁路建设征收土地工作和拆迁安置工作。

**Article 36** Land used for railway construction shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
The relevant local people's governments shall support railway construction and assist railway transport enterprises in properly carrying out land expropriation for railway construction and relocation and resettlement of residents.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　已经取得使用权的铁路建设用地，应当依照批准的用途使用，不得擅自改作他用；其他单位或者个人不得侵占。  
侵占铁路建设用地的，由县级以上地方人民政府土地管理部门责令停止侵占、赔偿损失。

**Article 37** Land used for railway construction that has already been obtained under the right of use shall be used for the approved purpose and shall not be diverted to other uses without authorization; no other unit or individual may encroach upon it.  
If anyone encroaches upon land used for railway construction, the land administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the encroachment to cease and compensate for the losses.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　铁路的标准轨距为1435毫米。新建国家铁路必须采用标准轨距。  
窄轨铁路的轨距为762毫米或者1000毫米。  
新建和改建铁路的其他技术要求应当符合国家标准或者行业标准。

**Article 38** The standard gauge of railways is 1435 millimeters. Newly-built national railways must adopt the standard gauge.  
The gauge of narrow-gauge railways is 762 millimeters or 1000 millimeters.  
Other technical requirements for new construction and reconstruction of railways shall comply with national standards or professional standards.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　铁路建成后，必须依照国家基本建设程序的规定，经验收合格，方能交付正式运行。

**Article 39** After a railway is completed, it must be checked and accepted as qualified in accordance with the provisions of the State on capital construction procedures before it may be formally put into operation.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　铁路与道路交叉处，应当优先考虑设置立体交叉；未设立体交叉的，可以根据国家有关规定设置平交道口或者人行过道。在城市规划区内设置平交道口或者人行过道，由铁路运输企业或者建有专用铁路、铁路专用线的企业或者其他单位和城市规划主管部门共同决定。  
拆除已经设置的平交道口或者人行过道，由铁路运输企业或者建有专用铁路、铁路专用线的企业或者其他单位和当地人民政府商定。

**Article 40** At crossings of railways and roads, priority shall be given to the construction of grade separations; where grade separations are not built, level crossings or pedestrian crossings may be established in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. The establishment of level crossings or pedestrian crossings within urban planning areas shall be decided jointly by the railway transport enterprise or the enterprise or other unit that has built the special railway or railway siding and the competent department of urban planning.  
The removal of existing level crossings or pedestrian crossings shall be decided through consultation between the railway transport enterprise or the enterprise or other unit that has built the special railway or railway siding and the local people's government.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　修建跨越河流的铁路桥梁，应当符合国家规定的防洪、通航和水流的要求。

**Article 41** The construction of railway bridges crossing rivers shall comply with the national requirements for flood control, navigation, and water flow.

### 第四章　铁路安全与保护

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　铁路运输企业必须加强对铁路的管理和保护，定期检查、维修铁路运输设施，保证铁路运输设施完好，保障旅客和货物运输安全。

**Article 42** Railway transport enterprises must strengthen the management and protection of railways, regularly inspect and maintain railway transport facilities, ensure the soundness of railway transport facilities, and guarantee the safety of passenger and cargo transport.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　铁路公安机关和地方公安机关分工负责共同维护铁路治安秩序。车站和列车内的治安秩序，由铁路公安机关负责维护；铁路沿线的治安秩序，由地方公安机关和铁路公安机关共同负责维护，以地方公安机关为主。

**Article 43** The railway public security organs and local public security organs shall, with a division of responsibilities, jointly maintain the public security order on railways. The public security order within stations and trains shall be maintained by the railway public security organs; the public security order along railway lines shall be jointly maintained by the local public security organs and the railway public security organs, with the local public security organs playing the primary role.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　电力主管部门应当保证铁路牵引用电以及铁路运营用电中重要负荷的电力供应。铁路运营用电中重要负荷的供应范围由国务院铁路主管部门和国务院电力主管部门商定。

**Article 44** The competent department of electric power shall ensure the power supply for railway traction and for important loads in railway operation electricity. The scope of supply for important loads in railway operation electricity shall be determined through consultation by the competent department of railways under the State Council and the competent department of electric power under the State Council.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　铁路线路两侧地界以外的山坡地由当地人民政府作为水土保持的重点进行整治。铁路隧道顶上的山坡地由铁路运输企业协助当地人民政府进行整治。铁路地界以内的山坡地由铁路运输企业进行整治。

**Article 45** The slope land outside the boundaries on both sides of railway lines shall be managed by the local people's government as a priority for soil and water conservation. The slope land above railway tunnels shall be managed by the railway transport enterprise with the assistance of the local people's government. The slope land within the railway boundaries shall be managed by the railway transport enterprise.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　在铁路线路和铁路桥梁、涵洞两侧一定距离内，修建山塘、水库、堤坝，开挖河道、干渠，采石挖砂，打井取水，影响铁路路基稳定或者危害铁路桥梁、涵洞安全的，由县级以上地方人民政府责令停止建设或者采挖、打井等活动，限期恢复原状或者责令采取必要的安全防护措施。  
在铁路线路上架设电力、通讯线路，埋置电缆、管道设施，穿凿通过铁路路基的地下坑道，必须经铁路运输企业同意，并采取安全防护措施。  
在铁路弯道内侧、平交道口和人行过道附近，不得修建妨碍行车瞭望的建筑物和种植妨碍行车瞭望的树木。修建妨碍行车瞭望的建筑物的，由县级以上地方人民政府责令限期拆除。种植妨碍行车瞭望的树木的，由县级以上地方人民政府责令有关单位或者个人限期迁移或者修剪、砍伐。  
违反前三款的规定，给铁路运输企业造成损失的单位或者个人，应当赔偿损失。

**Article 46** Where, within a certain distance on both sides of railway lines, railway bridges, or culverts, the construction of ponds, reservoirs, or dykes, the excavation of river channels or main canals, quarrying or sand excavation, or well drilling for water extraction affects the stability of railway subgrades or endangers the safety of railway bridges or culverts, the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of such construction, excavation, well drilling, or other activities, and order restoration to the original condition within a time limit or order the adoption of necessary safety protection measures.  
The erection of electric power or communication lines, the embedding of cables or pipeline facilities, or the excavation of underground tunnels through railway subgrades on railway lines must be approved by the railway transport enterprise and safety protection measures shall be taken.  
On the inner side of railway curves, near level crossings and pedestrian crossings, no buildings that obstruct the line of sight for train operation shall be constructed, and no trees that obstruct the line of sight for train operation shall be planted. Where buildings obstructing the line of sight for train operation are constructed, the local people's government at or above the county level shall order their demolition within a time limit. Where trees obstructing the line of sight for train operation are planted, the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the relevant unit or individual to relocate, prune, or fell them within a time limit.  
Any unit or individual that causes losses to the railway transport enterprise by violating the provisions of the preceding three paragraphs shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　禁止擅自在铁路线路上铺设平交道口和人行过道。  
平交道口和人行过道必须按照规定设置必要的标志和防护设施。  
行人和车辆通过铁路平交道口和人行过道时，必须遵守有关通行的规定。

**Article 47** It is prohibited to lay level crossings and pedestrian crossings on railway lines without authorization.  
Level crossings and pedestrian crossings must be provided with necessary signs and protective facilities in accordance with regulations.  
When pedestrians and vehicles pass through railway level crossings and pedestrian crossings, they must comply with the relevant passage regulations.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　运输危险品必须按照国务院铁路主管部门的规定办理，禁止以非危险品品名托运危险品。  
禁止旅客携带危险品进站上车。铁路公安人员和国务院铁路主管部门规定的铁路职工，有权对旅客携带的物品进行运输安全检查。实施运输安全检查的铁路职工应当佩戴执勤标志。  
危险品的品名由国务院铁路主管部门规定并公布。

**Article 48** The transport of dangerous goods must be handled in accordance with the regulations of the competent department of railways under the State Council. It is prohibited to consign dangerous goods under the name of non-dangerous goods.  
Passengers are prohibited from bringing dangerous goods into stations or onto trains. Railway public security personnel and railway employees designated by the competent department of railways under the State Council shall have the authority to conduct transport safety inspections on articles carried by passengers. Railway employees conducting transport safety inspections shall wear duty badges.  
The names of dangerous goods shall be prescribed and published by the competent department of railways under the State Council.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　对损毁、移动铁路信号装置及其他行车设施或者在铁路线路上放置障碍物的，铁路职工有权制止，可以扭送公安机关处理。

**Article 49** Where anyone damages or moves railway signal devices or other traffic facilities, or places obstacles on railway lines, railway employees shall have the right to stop such acts and may take the person to a public security organ for disposition.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　禁止偷乘货车、攀附行进中的列车或者击打列车。对偷乘货车、攀附行进中的列车或者击打列车的，铁路职工有权制止。

**Article 50** It is prohibited to secretly ride on freight trains, cling to moving trains, or strike trains. Where anyone secretly rides on freight trains, clings to moving trains, or strikes trains, railway employees shall have the right to stop such acts.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　禁止在铁路线路上行走、坐卧。对在铁路线路上行走、坐卧的，铁路职工有权制止。

**Article 51** It is prohibited to walk, sit, or lie down on railway lines. Where anyone walks, sits, or lies down on railway lines, railway employees shall have the right to stop such acts.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　禁止在铁路线路两侧二十米以内或者铁路防护林地内放牧。对在铁路线路两侧二十米以内或者铁路防护林地内放牧的，铁路职工有权制止。

**Article 52** It is prohibited to graze livestock within twenty meters on both sides of railway lines or in railway protective forest areas. Where anyone grazes livestock within twenty meters on both sides of railway lines or in railway protective forest areas, railway employees shall have the right to stop such acts.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　对聚众拦截列车或者聚众冲击铁路行车调度机构的，铁路职工有权制止；不听制止的，公安人员现场负责人有权命令解散；拒不解散的，公安人员现场负责人有权依照国家有关规定决定采取必要手段强行驱散，并对拒不服从的人员强行带离现场或者予以拘留。

**Article 53** Where a crowd intercepts a train or storms a railway traffic dispatching institution, railway employees shall have the right to stop such acts; if the crowd refuses to be stopped, the person in charge of the public security personnel on the scene shall have the right to order the crowd to disperse; if the crowd refuses to disperse, the person in charge of the public security personnel on the scene shall have the right, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, to decide on taking necessary means to forcibly disperse the crowd, and to forcibly take away from the scene or detain those who refuse to comply.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　对哄抢铁路运输物资的，铁路职工有权制止，可以扭送公安机关处理；现场公安人员可以予以拘留。

**Article 54** Where anyone plunders railway transport materials by looting, railway employees shall have the right to stop such acts and may take the person to a public security organ for disposition; public security personnel on the scene may detain such person.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　在列车内，寻衅滋事，扰乱公共秩序，危害旅客人身、财产安全的，铁路职工有权制止，铁路公安人员可以予以拘留。

**Article 55** Where anyone engages in provocative behavior, disrupts public order, or endangers the personal or property safety of passengers on a train, railway employees shall have the right to stop such acts, and railway public security personnel may detain such person.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　在车站和旅客列车内，发生法律规定需要检疫的传染病时，由铁路卫生检疫机构进行检疫；根据铁路卫生检疫机构的请求，地方卫生检疫机构应予协助。  
货物运输的检疫，依照国家规定办理。

**Article 56** When an infectious disease subject to quarantine as prescribed by law occurs in stations or passenger trains, quarantine shall be carried out by the railway health quarantine institution; upon request of the railway health quarantine institution, local health quarantine institutions shall provide assistance.  
Quarantine for cargo transport shall be handled in accordance with national regulations.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　发生铁路交通事故，铁路运输企业应当依照国务院和国务院有关主管部门关于事故调查处理的规定办理，并及时恢复正常行车，任何单位和个人不得阻碍铁路线路开通和列车运行。

**Article 57** In the event of a railway traffic accident, the railway transport enterprise shall handle it in accordance with the regulations of the State Council and the competent departments under the State Council on accident investigation and handling, and shall promptly resume normal train operation; no unit or individual shall obstruct the opening of railway lines or the operation of trains.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　因铁路行车事故及其他铁路运营事故造成人身伤亡的，铁路运输企业应当承担赔偿责任；如果人身伤亡是因不可抗力或者由于受害人自身的原因造成的，铁路运输企业不承担赔偿责任。  
违章通过平交道口或者人行过道，或者在铁路线路上行走、坐卧造成的人身伤亡，属于受害人自身的原因造成的人身伤亡。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知.md#art-11" title="被《最高人民法院关于印发《关于审理铁路运输损害赔偿案件若干问题的解释》的通知》第11条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 58** Where personal injury or death is caused by railway traffic accidents or other railway operation accidents, the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability; if the personal injury or death is caused by force majeure or due to the victim's own reasons, the railway transport enterprise shall not bear compensation liability.  
Personal injury or death caused by illegally passing through a level crossing or pedestrian crossing, or by walking, sitting, or lying down on railway lines, shall be deemed as personal injury or death caused by the victim's own reasons.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国家铁路的重要桥梁和隧道，由中国人民武装警察部队负责守卫。

**Article 59** Important bridges and tunnels of the national railways shall be guarded by the Chinese People's Armed Police Force.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本法规定，携带危险品进站上车或者以非危险品品名托运危险品，导致发生重大事故的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。企业事业单位、国家机关、社会团体犯本款罪的，处以罚金，对其主管人员和直接责任人员依法追究刑事责任。  
携带炸药、雷管或者非法携带枪支子弹、管制刀具进站上车的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 60** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, brings dangerous goods into a station or onto a train, or consigns dangerous goods under the name of non-dangerous goods, causing a major accident, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law. Where an enterprise, institution, State organ, or social organization commits the crime under this paragraph, a fine shall be imposed, and criminal liability shall be investigated against the person in charge and the person directly responsible in accordance with law.  
Where anyone brings explosives, detonators, or illegally brings firearms, bullets, or controlled knives into a station or onto a train, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　故意损毁、移动铁路行车信号装置或者在铁路线路上放置足以使列车倾覆的障碍物的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 61** Whoever intentionally damages or removes railway traffic signal devices or places on railway lines obstacles sufficient to cause train derailment shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　盗窃铁路线路上行车设施的零件、部件或者铁路线路上的器材，危及行车安全的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 62** Whoever steals parts or components of railway traffic facilities or equipment on railway lines, endangering traffic safety, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　聚众拦截列车、冲击铁路行车调度机构不听制止的，对首要分子和骨干分子依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 63** Where persons gather to intercept trains or assault railway traffic control organs and refuse to stop, the ringleaders and key members shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　聚众哄抢铁路运输物资的，对首要分子和骨干分子依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。  
铁路职工与其他人员勾结犯前款罪的，从重处罚。

**Article 64** Where persons gather to forcibly seize railway transport materials, the ringleaders and key members shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
If a railway employee colludes with other persons to commit the crime prescribed in the preceding paragraph, he shall be given a severe punishment.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　在列车内，抢劫旅客财物，伤害旅客的，依照刑法有关规定从重处罚。  
在列车内，寻衅滋事，侮辱妇女，情节恶劣的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任；敲诈勒索旅客财物的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 65** Whoever, inside a train, robs passengers of their property or injures passengers shall be given a severe punishment in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
Whoever, inside a train, engages in affrays, creates disturbances, or insults women, if the circumstances are flagrant, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; whoever extorts or blackmails passengers for property shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　倒卖旅客车票，构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。铁路职工倒卖旅客车票或者与其他人员勾结倒卖旅客车票的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 66** Whoever speculates in passenger tickets, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law. If a railway employee speculates in passenger tickets or colludes with other persons to speculate in passenger tickets, he shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　违反本法规定，尚不够刑事处罚，应当给予治安管理处罚的，依照治安管理处罚法的规定处罚。

**Article 67** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law, if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment but shall be given a public security administration punishment, shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　擅自在铁路线路上铺设平交道口、人行过道的，由铁路公安机关或者地方公安机关责令限期拆除，可以并处罚款。

**Article 68** Whoever, without authorization, constructs level crossings or pedestrian crossings on railway lines shall be ordered by the railway public security organ or the local public security organ to dismantle them within a time limit, and may also be fined.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　铁路运输企业违反本法规定，多收运费、票款或者旅客、货物运输杂费的，必须将多收的费用退还付款人，无法退还的上缴国库。将多收的费用据为己有或者侵吞私分的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 69** If a railway transport enterprise, in violation of the provisions of this Law, overcharges freight charges, ticket fares, or miscellaneous charges for passenger or cargo transport, it must refund the overcharged amount to the payer, and if the amount cannot be refunded, it shall be turned over to the State treasury. Whoever takes the overcharged amount as his own or embezzles and divides it up shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　铁路职工利用职务之便走私的，或者与其他人员勾结走私的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 70** If a railway employee takes advantage of his position to engage in smuggling, or colludes with other persons to engage in smuggling, he shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　铁路职工玩忽职守、违反规章制度造成铁路运营事故的，滥用职权、利用办理运输业务之便谋取私利的，给予行政处分；情节严重、构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 71** If a railway employee, through dereliction of duty or violation of rules and regulations, causes a railway operation accident, or abuses his power or takes advantage of his handling of transport operations to seek personal gain, he shall be given an administrative sanction; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, he shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　本法所称国家铁路运输企业是指铁路局和铁路分局。

**Article 72** For the purposes of this Law, the term 'State railway transport enterprises' means railway administrations and railway sub-administrations.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　国务院根据本法制定实施条例。

**Article 73** The State Council shall, in accordance with this Law, formulate implementing regulations.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　本法自1991年5月1日起施行。

**Article 74** This Law shall come into force as of May 1, 1991.
