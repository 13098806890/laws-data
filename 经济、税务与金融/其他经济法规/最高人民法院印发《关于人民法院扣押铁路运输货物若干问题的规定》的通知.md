# 最高人民法院印发《关于人民法院扣押铁路运输货物若干问题的规定》的通知

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1997〕8号  
**公布日期**：1997-04-22  
**生效日期**：1997-04-22

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、人民法院依法可以裁定扣押铁路运输货物。铁路运输企业依法应当予以协助。

**Article 1** 1. The People's Court may, according to law, rule to seize railway transport cargo. Railway transport enterprises shall, according to law, provide assistance.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、当事人申请人民法院扣押铁路运输货物，应当提供担保，申请人不提供担保的，驳回申请。申请人的申请应当写明：要求扣押货物的发货站、到货站，托运人、收货人的名称，货物的品名、数量、货票号码等。

**Article 2** 2. When a party applies to the People's Court for the seizure of railway transport cargo, the party shall provide security; if the applicant fails to provide security, the application shall be rejected. The applicant's application shall specify: the departure station and arrival station of the cargo to be seized, the names of the shipper and the consignee, the description, quantity, and waybill number of the cargo, etc.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、人民法院扣押铁路运输货物，应当制作裁定书并附协助执行通知书。协助执行通知书中应当载明：扣押货物的发货站、到货站，托运人、收货人的名称，货物的品名、数量和货票号码。在货物发送前扣押的，人民法院应当将裁定书副本和协助执行通知书送达始发地的铁路运输企业由其协助执行；在货物发送后扣押的，一般应当将裁定书副本和协助执行通知书送达目的地或最近中转编组站的铁路运输企业由其协助执行。  
人民法院一般不应在中途站、中转站扣押铁路运输货物。必要时，在不影响铁路正常运输秩序、不损害其他公民法人的合法权益的情况下，可在最近中转编组站或有条件的车站扣押。  
人民法院裁定扣押国际铁路联运货物，应当通知铁路运输企业、海关、边防、商检等有关部门协助执行。属于进口货物的，人民法院应当向我国进口国境、边境站、到货站或有关部门送达裁定书副本和协助执行通知书；属于出口货物的，在货物发送前应当向发货站或有关部门送达，在货物发送后，未出我国国境、边境前，应当向我国出境站或有关部门送达。

**Article 3** 3. When the People's Court seizes railway transport cargo, it shall issue a ruling and attach a notice of assistance in enforcement. The notice of assistance in enforcement shall specify: the departure station and arrival station of the cargo to be seized, the names of the shipper and the consignee, the description, quantity, and waybill number of the cargo. If the seizure is carried out before the cargo is dispatched, the People's Court shall serve a copy of the ruling and the notice of assistance in enforcement on the railway transport enterprise at the departure station for its assistance in enforcement; if the seizure is carried out after the cargo is dispatched, a copy of the ruling and the notice of assistance in enforcement shall generally be served on the railway transport enterprise at the destination or the nearest marshalling station for its assistance in enforcement.  
The People's Court shall generally not seize railway transport cargo at intermediate stations or transit stations. Where necessary, provided that the normal order of railway transport is not affected and the legitimate rights and interests of other citizens or legal persons are not impaired, the seizure may be carried out at the nearest marshalling station or a station with appropriate conditions.  
When the People's Court rules to seize international railway intermodal cargo, it shall notify the railway transport enterprise, customs, border defense, commodity inspection, and other relevant departments for assistance in enforcement. For imported cargo, the People's Court shall serve a copy of the ruling and the notice of assistance in enforcement on the entry port, border station, arrival station, or relevant departments of China; for exported cargo, before dispatch, it shall serve them on the departure station or relevant departments; after dispatch but before leaving China's borders, it shall serve them on the exit station or relevant departments of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、经人民法院裁定扣押的铁路运输货物，该铁路运输企业与托运人之间签订的铁路运输合同中涉及被扣押货物部分合同终止履行的，铁路运输企业不承担责任。因扣押货物造成的损失，由有关责任人承担。  
因申请人申请扣押错误所造成的损失，由申请人承担赔偿责任。

**Article 4** 4. Where, for railway transport cargo seized by a ruling of the People's Court, the part of the railway transport contract involving the seized cargo terminates in performance as signed between the railway transport enterprise and the shipper, the railway transport enterprise shall not bear liability. Losses caused by the seizure of cargo shall be borne by the relevant responsible person.  
Losses caused by an erroneous application for seizure by the applicant shall be borne by the applicant as compensation liability.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、铁路运输企业及有关部门因协助执行扣押货物而产生的装卸、保管、检验、监护等费用，由有关责任人承担，但应先由申请人垫付。申请人不是责任人的，可以再向责任人追偿。

**Article 5** 5. Expenses such as loading/unloading, storage, inspection, and custody arising from railway transport enterprises and relevant departments assisting in the enforcement of cargo seizure shall be borne by the relevant responsible person, but shall first be advanced by the applicant. If the applicant is not the responsible person, the applicant may recover the expenses from the responsible person.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、扣押后的进出口货物，因尚未办结海关手续，人民法院在对此类货物作出最终处理决定前，应当先责令有关当事人补交关税并办理海关其他手续。

**Article 6** 6. For imported or exported cargo after seizure, since customs procedures have not yet been completed, before the People's Court makes a final decision on the disposal of such cargo, it shall first order the relevant party to pay the customs duties retroactively and complete other customs formalities.
