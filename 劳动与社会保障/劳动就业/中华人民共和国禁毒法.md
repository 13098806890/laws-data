# 中华人民共和国禁毒法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2007-12-29  
**生效日期**：2008-06-01

> （2007年12月29日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十一次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　禁毒宣传教育 第三章　毒品管制 第四章　戒毒措施 第五章　禁毒国际合作 第六章　法律责任 第七章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了预防和惩治毒品违法犯罪行为，保护公民身心健康，维护社会秩序，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称毒品，是指鸦片、海洛因、甲基苯丙胺（冰毒）、吗啡、大麻、可卡因，以及国家规定管制的其他能够使人形成瘾癖的麻醉药品和精神药品。 根据医疗、教学、科研的需要，依法可以生产、经营、使用、储存、运输麻醉药品和精神药品。 第三条　禁毒是全社会的共同责任。国家机关、社会团体、企业事业单位以及其他组织和公民，应当依照本法和有关法律的规定，履行禁毒职责或者义务。 第四条　禁毒工作实行预防为主，综合治理，禁种、禁制、禁贩、禁吸并举的方针。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了预防和惩治毒品违法犯罪行为，保护公民身心健康，维护社会秩序，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of preventing and punishing drug-related illegal and criminal acts, protecting the physical and mental health of citizens, and maintaining social order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称毒品，是指鸦片、海洛因、甲基苯丙胺（冰毒）、吗啡、大麻、可卡因，以及国家规定管制的其他能够使人形成瘾癖的麻醉药品和精神药品。  
根据医疗、教学、科研的需要，依法可以生产、经营、使用、储存、运输麻醉药品和精神药品。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/毒品与走私/最高人民检察院关于《非药用类麻醉药品和精神药品管制品种增补目录》能否作为认定毒品依据的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民检察院关于《非药用类麻醉药品和精神药品管制品种增补目录》能否作为认定毒品依据的批复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "drugs" means opium, heroin, methamphetamine (ice), morphine, cannabis, cocaine, and other narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances that are controlled under state regulations and are capable of causing addiction.  
For medical, teaching, and scientific research needs, narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances may be produced, traded, used, stored, and transported according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　禁毒是全社会的共同责任。国家机关、社会团体、企业事业单位以及其他组织和公民，应当依照本法和有关法律的规定，履行禁毒职责或者义务。

**Article 3** Narcotics control is a common responsibility of the entire society. State organs, social organizations, enterprises, institutions, other organizations, and citizens shall, in accordance with this Law and relevant laws, fulfill their narcotics control duties or obligations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　禁毒工作实行预防为主，综合治理，禁种、禁制、禁贩、禁吸并举的方针。  
禁毒工作实行政府统一领导，有关部门各负其责，社会广泛参与的工作机制。

**Article 4** The work of narcotics control shall follow the policy of prevention first, comprehensive treatment, and simultaneous prohibition of planting, manufacturing, trafficking, and use.  
The work of narcotics control shall be carried out under a working mechanism of unified leadership by the government, respective responsibilities of relevant departments, and extensive participation by society.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院设立国家禁毒委员会，负责组织、协调、指导全国的禁毒工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府根据禁毒工作的需要，可以设立禁毒委员会，负责组织、协调、指导本行政区域内的禁毒工作。

**Article 5** The State Council shall establish a National Narcotics Control Committee, which shall be responsible for organizing, coordinating, and guiding the nationwide narcotics control work.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level may, based on the needs of narcotics control work, establish narcotics control committees, which shall be responsible for organizing, coordinating, and guiding the narcotics control work within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上各级人民政府应当将禁毒工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，并将禁毒经费列入本级财政预算。

**Article 6** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate narcotics control work into the national economic and social development plans, and include the funds for narcotics control in their respective fiscal budgets.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励对禁毒工作的社会捐赠，并依法给予税收优惠。

**Article 7** The State encourages social donations for narcotics control work and shall grant tax benefits according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励开展禁毒科学技术研究，推广先进的缉毒技术、装备和戒毒方法。

**Article 8** The State encourages the conduct of scientific and technological research on narcotics control, and the promotion of advanced drug enforcement techniques, equipment, and rehabilitation methods.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家鼓励公民举报毒品违法犯罪行为。各级人民政府和有关部门应当对举报人予以保护，对举报有功人员以及在禁毒工作中有突出贡献的单位和个人，给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 9** The State encourages citizens to report drug-related illegal and criminal acts. People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall protect whistleblowers, and shall commend and reward those who have rendered meritorious service in reporting and units or individuals that have made outstanding contributions to narcotics control work.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家鼓励志愿人员参与禁毒宣传教育和戒毒社会服务工作。地方各级人民政府应当对志愿人员进行指导、培训，并提供必要的工作条件。

**Article 10** The State encourages volunteers to participate in narcotics control publicity, education, and social services for drug rehabilitation. Local people's governments at all levels shall provide guidance, training, and necessary working conditions for volunteers.

### 第二章　禁毒宣传教育

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家采取各种形式开展全民禁毒宣传教育，普及毒品预防知识，增强公民的禁毒意识，提高公民自觉抵制毒品的能力。  
国家鼓励公民、组织开展公益性的禁毒宣传活动。

**Article 11** The State adopts various forms to carry out nationwide narcotics control publicity and education, disseminate knowledge on drug prevention, enhance citizens' awareness of narcotics control, and improve their ability to consciously resist drugs.  
The State encourages citizens and organizations to conduct public interest narcotics control publicity activities.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　各级人民政府应当经常组织开展多种形式的禁毒宣传教育。  
工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会应当结合各自工作对象的特点，组织开展禁毒宣传教育。

**Article 12** People's governments at all levels shall regularly organize and carry out diverse forms of narcotics control publicity and education.  
Trade unions, the Communist Youth League, and women's federations shall, in light of the characteristics of their respective target groups, organize and conduct narcotics control publicity and education.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　教育行政部门、学校应当将禁毒知识纳入教育、教学内容，对学生进行禁毒宣传教育。公安机关、司法行政部门和卫生行政部门应当予以协助。

**Article 13** Education administrative departments and schools shall incorporate knowledge on narcotics control into education and teaching content, and conduct narcotics control publicity and education for students. The public security organs, judicial administrative departments, and health administrative departments shall provide assistance.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　新闻、出版、文化、广播、电影、电视等有关单位，应当有针对性地面向社会进行禁毒宣传教育。

**Article 14** Relevant units such as news, publishing, culture, radio, film, and television shall conduct targeted narcotics control publicity and education for the public.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　飞机场、火车站、长途汽车站、码头以及旅店、娱乐场所等公共场所的经营者、管理者，负责本场所的禁毒宣传教育，落实禁毒防范措施，预防毒品违法犯罪行为在本场所内发生。

**Article 15** Operators and managers of public places such as airports, railway stations, long-distance bus stations, docks, hotels, and entertainment venues shall be responsible for narcotics control publicity and education in their premises, implement narcotics control preventive measures, and prevent drug-related illegal and criminal acts from occurring in their premises.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家机关、社会团体、企业事业单位以及其他组织，应当加强对本单位人员的禁毒宣传教育。

**Article 16** State organs, social organizations, enterprises, institutions, and other organizations shall strengthen narcotics control publicity and education for their personnel.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　居民委员会、村民委员会应当协助人民政府以及公安机关等部门，加强禁毒宣传教育，落实禁毒防范措施。

**Article 17** Residents' committees and villagers' committees shall assist the people's governments and public security organs and other departments in strengthening narcotics control publicity and education and implementing narcotics control preventive measures.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人应当对未成年人进行毒品危害的教育，防止其吸食、注射毒品或者进行其他毒品违法犯罪活动。

**Article 18** Parents or other guardians of minors shall educate minors on the dangers of drugs and prevent them from using or injecting drugs or engaging in other drug-related illegal and criminal activities.

### 第三章　毒品管制

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家对麻醉药品药用原植物种植实行管制。禁止非法种植罂粟、古柯植物、大麻植物以及国家规定管制的可以用于提炼加工毒品的其他原植物。禁止走私或者非法买卖、运输、携带、持有未经灭活的毒品原植物种子或者幼苗。  
地方各级人民政府发现非法种植毒品原植物的，应当立即采取措施予以制止、铲除。村民委员会、居民委员会发现非法种植毒品原植物的，应当及时予以制止、铲除，并向当地公安机关报告。

**Article 19** The State exercises control over the cultivation of narcotic drug raw plants. It is prohibited to illegally cultivate opium poppy, coca plants, cannabis plants, and other raw plants controlled under state regulations that can be used for refining and processing drugs. It is prohibited to smuggle or illegally trade, transport, carry, or possess seeds or seedlings of drug raw plants that have not been sterilized.  
Where local people's governments at all levels discover illegal cultivation of drug raw plants, they shall immediately take measures to stop and eradicate them. Where villagers' committees or residents' committees discover illegal cultivation of drug raw plants, they shall promptly stop and eradicate them and report to the local public security organs.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家确定的麻醉药品药用原植物种植企业，必须按照国家有关规定种植麻醉药品药用原植物。  
国家确定的麻醉药品药用原植物种植企业的提取加工场所，以及国家设立的麻醉药品储存仓库，列为国家重点警戒目标。  
未经许可，擅自进入国家确定的麻醉药品药用原植物种植企业的提取加工场所或者国家设立的麻醉药品储存仓库等警戒区域的，由警戒人员责令其立即离开；拒不离开的，强行带离现场。

**Article 20** The enterprises designated by the State for the cultivation of narcotic drug raw plants must cultivate such plants in accordance with the relevant state regulations.  
The extraction and processing sites of the enterprises designated by the State for the cultivation of narcotic drug raw plants, as well as the storage warehouses for narcotic drugs established by the State, shall be listed as key security targets of the State.  
Any person who, without permission, enters the extraction and processing sites of the enterprises designated by the State for the cultivation of narcotic drug raw plants or the security areas such as the storage warehouses for narcotic drugs established by the State shall be ordered to leave immediately by the security personnel; if the person refuses to leave, the security personnel shall forcibly remove the person from the scene.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家对麻醉药品和精神药品实行管制，对麻醉药品和精神药品的实验研究、生产、经营、使用、储存、运输实行许可和查验制度。  
国家对易制毒化学品的生产、经营、购买、运输实行许可制度。  
禁止非法生产、买卖、运输、储存、提供、持有、使用麻醉药品、精神药品和易制毒化学品。

**Article 21** The State exercises control over narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, and applies a licensing and inspection system for their experimental research, production, trade, use, storage, and transportation.  
The State applies a licensing system for the production, trade, purchase, and transportation of precursor chemicals.  
It is prohibited to illegally produce, trade, transport, store, supply, possess, or use narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, or precursor chemicals.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家对麻醉药品、精神药品和易制毒化学品的进口、出口实行许可制度。国务院有关部门应当按照规定的职责，对进口、出口麻醉药品、精神药品和易制毒化学品依法进行管理。禁止走私麻醉药品、精神药品和易制毒化学品。

**Article 22** The State applies a licensing system for the import and export of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, and precursor chemicals. The relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with their prescribed duties, manage the import and export of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, and precursor chemicals according to law. It is prohibited to smuggle narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, or precursor chemicals.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　发生麻醉药品、精神药品和易制毒化学品被盗、被抢、丢失或者其他流入非法渠道的情形，案发单位应当立即采取必要的控制措施，并立即向公安机关报告，同时依照规定向有关主管部门报告。  
公安机关接到报告后，或者有证据证明麻醉药品、精神药品和易制毒化学品可能流入非法渠道的，应当及时开展调查，并可以对相关单位采取必要的控制措施。药品监督管理部门、卫生行政部门以及其他有关部门应当配合公安机关开展工作。

**Article 23** In the event of theft, robbery, loss, or any other situation where narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, or precursor chemicals flow into illegal channels, the unit where the incident occurred shall immediately take necessary control measures and immediately report to the public security organ, and at the same time report to the relevant competent departments according to regulations.  
After receiving a report, or where there is evidence that narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, or precursor chemicals may have flowed into illegal channels, the public security organ shall promptly conduct an investigation and may take necessary control measures against the relevant units. The drug regulatory department, health administrative department, and other relevant departments shall cooperate with the public security organ in carrying out its work.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　禁止非法传授麻醉药品、精神药品和易制毒化学品的制造方法。公安机关接到举报或者发现非法传授麻醉药品、精神药品和易制毒化学品制造方法的，应当及时依法查处。

**Article 24** It is prohibited to illegally teach the manufacturing methods of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, or precursor chemicals. Where the public security organ receives a report or discovers illegal teaching of the manufacturing methods of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, or precursor chemicals, it shall promptly investigate and handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　麻醉药品、精神药品和易制毒化学品管理的具体办法，由国务院规定。

**Article 25** The specific measures for the management of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, and precursor chemicals shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　公安机关根据查缉毒品的需要，可以在边境地区、交通要道、口岸以及飞机场、火车站、长途汽车站、码头对来往人员、物品、货物以及交通工具进行毒品和易制毒化学品检查，民航、铁路、交通部门应当予以配合。  
海关应当依法加强对进出口岸的人员、物品、货物和运输工具的检查，防止走私毒品和易制毒化学品。  
邮政企业应当依法加强对邮件的检查，防止邮寄毒品和非法邮寄易制毒化学品。

**Article 26** Based on the needs of drug enforcement, public security organs may conduct inspections for drugs and precursor chemicals on persons, articles, goods, and means of transport in border areas, vital communication lines, ports, and airports, railway stations, long-distance bus stations, and docks. Civil aviation, railway, and transportation departments shall provide cooperation.  
The customs shall strengthen inspections of persons, articles, goods, and means of transport entering or leaving the country according to law to prevent the smuggling of drugs and precursor chemicals.  
Postal enterprises shall strengthen inspections of mail items according to law to prevent the mailing of drugs and the illegal mailing of precursor chemicals.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　娱乐场所应当建立巡查制度，发现娱乐场所内有毒品违法犯罪活动的，应当立即向公安机关报告。

**Article 27** Entertainment venues shall establish a patrol system. Where they discover drug-related illegal or criminal activities within the venue, they shall immediately report to the public security organ.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对依法查获的毒品，吸食、注射毒品的用具，毒品违法犯罪的非法所得及其收益，以及直接用于实施毒品违法犯罪行为的本人所有的工具、设备、资金，应当收缴，依照规定处理。

**Article 28** Drugs, instruments for using or injecting drugs, illegal gains and proceeds from drug-related illegal and criminal acts, and the tools, equipment, and funds owned by the perpetrator that are directly used for committing drug-related illegal and criminal acts, which are seized according to law, shall be confiscated and disposed of in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　反洗钱行政主管部门应当依法加强对可疑毒品犯罪资金的监测。反洗钱行政主管部门和其他依法负有反洗钱监督管理职责的部门、机构发现涉嫌毒品犯罪的资金流动情况，应当及时向侦查机关报告，并配合侦查机关做好侦查、调查工作。

**Article 29** The anti-money laundering administrative department shall strengthen the monitoring of funds suspected to be related to drug crimes according to law. Where the anti-money laundering administrative department and other departments or institutions legally responsible for anti-money laundering supervision and management discover any flow of funds suspected to be related to drug crimes, they shall promptly report to the investigation authorities and cooperate with them in carrying out investigation and inquiry work.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家建立健全毒品监测和禁毒信息系统，开展毒品监测和禁毒信息的收集、分析、使用、交流工作。

**Article 30** The State establishes and improves a drug monitoring and narcotics control information system, and carries out the collection, analysis, use, and exchange of drug monitoring and narcotics control information.

### 第四章　戒毒措施

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家采取各种措施帮助吸毒人员戒除毒瘾，教育和挽救吸毒人员。  
吸毒成瘾人员应当进行戒毒治疗。  
吸毒成瘾的认定办法，由国务院卫生行政部门、药品监督管理部门、公安部门规定。

**Article 31** The State adopts various measures to help drug users overcome their addiction, and to educate and rescue drug users.  
Persons addicted to drugs shall undergo drug rehabilitation treatment.  
The methods for determining drug addiction shall be prescribed by the health administrative department, the drug regulatory department, and the public security department of the State Council.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　公安机关可以对涉嫌吸毒的人员进行必要的检测，被检测人员应当予以配合；对拒绝接受检测的，经县级以上人民政府公安机关或者其派出机构负责人批准，可以强制检测。  
公安机关应当对吸毒人员进行登记。

**Article 32** Public security organs may conduct necessary testing on persons suspected of using drugs; the persons being tested shall cooperate. Where a person refuses to undergo testing, the public security organ may, with the approval of the responsible person of the public security organ at or above the county level or its dispatched agency, conduct compulsory testing.  
Public security organs shall register drug users.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　对吸毒成瘾人员，公安机关可以责令其接受社区戒毒，同时通知吸毒人员户籍所在地或者现居住地的城市街道办事处、乡镇人民政府。社区戒毒的期限为三年。  
戒毒人员应当在户籍所在地接受社区戒毒；在户籍所在地以外的现居住地有固定住所的，可以在现居住地接受社区戒毒。

**Article 33** For persons addicted to drugs, public security organs may order them to undergo community-based drug rehabilitation, and shall at the same time notify the sub-district office or township people's government of the place where the drug user's household registration is located or of the place of current residence. The period of community-based drug rehabilitation shall be three years.  
Drug rehabilitation recipients shall undergo community-based drug rehabilitation at the place where their household registration is located; if they have a fixed residence in a place of current residence different from their household registration, they may undergo community-based drug rehabilitation at the place of current residence.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　城市街道办事处、乡镇人民政府负责社区戒毒工作。城市街道办事处、乡镇人民政府可以指定有关基层组织，根据戒毒人员本人和家庭情况，与戒毒人员签订社区戒毒协议，落实有针对性的社区戒毒措施。公安机关和司法行政、卫生行政、民政等部门应当对社区戒毒工作提供指导和协助。  
城市街道办事处、乡镇人民政府，以及县级人民政府劳动行政部门对无职业且缺乏就业能力的戒毒人员，应当提供必要的职业技能培训、就业指导和就业援助。

**Article 34** Sub-district offices and township people's governments shall be responsible for community-based drug rehabilitation work. Sub-district offices and township people's governments may designate relevant grassroots organizations to sign community-based drug rehabilitation agreements with drug rehabilitation recipients based on their personal and family circumstances, and implement targeted community-based drug rehabilitation measures. The public security organs, judicial administrative departments, health administrative departments, civil affairs departments, and other departments shall provide guidance and assistance for community-based drug rehabilitation work.  
Sub-district offices, township people's governments, and the labor administrative departments of county people's governments shall provide necessary vocational skill training, employment guidance, and employment assistance to drug rehabilitation recipients who are unemployed and lack employability.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　接受社区戒毒的戒毒人员应当遵守法律、法规，自觉履行社区戒毒协议，并根据公安机关的要求，定期接受检测。  
对违反社区戒毒协议的戒毒人员，参与社区戒毒的工作人员应当进行批评、教育；对严重违反社区戒毒协议或者在社区戒毒期间又吸食、注射毒品的，应当及时向公安机关报告。

**Article 35** Drug rehabilitation recipients undergoing community-based drug rehabilitation shall abide by laws and regulations, conscientiously perform the community-based drug rehabilitation agreement, and undergo regular testing as required by the public security organs.  
For drug rehabilitation recipients who violate the community-based drug rehabilitation agreement, staff members involved in community-based drug rehabilitation shall criticize and educate them; for those who seriously violate the community-based drug rehabilitation agreement or use or inject drugs again during community-based drug rehabilitation, they shall promptly report to the public security organ.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　吸毒人员可以自行到具有戒毒治疗资质的医疗机构接受戒毒治疗。  
设置戒毒医疗机构或者医疗机构从事戒毒治疗业务的，应当符合国务院卫生行政部门规定的条件，报所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门批准，并报同级公安机关备案。戒毒治疗应当遵守国务院卫生行政部门制定的戒毒治疗规范，接受卫生行政部门的监督检查。  
戒毒治疗不得以营利为目的。戒毒治疗的药品、医疗器械和治疗方法不得做广告。戒毒治疗收取费用的，应当按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府价格主管部门会同卫生行政部门制定的收费标准执行。

**Article 36** Drug users may voluntarily go to medical institutions qualified for drug rehabilitation treatment to receive such treatment.  
The establishment of a drug rehabilitation medical institution or a medical institution engaging in drug rehabilitation treatment shall meet the conditions prescribed by the health administrative department of the State Council, be subject to approval by the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and file a record with the public security organ at the same level. Drug rehabilitation treatment shall comply with the norms for drug rehabilitation treatment formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council and accept supervision and inspection by the health administrative department.  
Drug rehabilitation treatment shall not be for profit. The pharmaceuticals, medical devices, and treatment methods for drug rehabilitation shall not be advertised. Where fees are charged for drug rehabilitation treatment, they shall be charged in accordance with the fee standards formulated by the competent price department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the health administrative department.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　医疗机构根据戒毒治疗的需要，可以对接受戒毒治疗的戒毒人员进行身体和所携带物品的检查；对在治疗期间有人身危险的，可以采取必要的临时保护性约束措施。  
发现接受戒毒治疗的戒毒人员在治疗期间吸食、注射毒品的，医疗机构应当及时向公安机关报告。

**Article 37** Medical institutions may, based on the needs of drug rehabilitation treatment, conduct physical examinations and inspections of articles carried by drug rehabilitation recipients undergoing treatment; for those who pose a personal danger during the treatment period, necessary temporary protective restraint measures may be taken.  
Where a medical institution discovers that a drug rehabilitation recipient undergoing treatment uses or injects drugs during the treatment period, it shall promptly report to the public security organ.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　吸毒成瘾人员有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府公安机关作出强制隔离戒毒的决定：  
（一）拒绝接受社区戒毒的；  
（二）在社区戒毒期间吸食、注射毒品的；  
（三）严重违反社区戒毒协议的；  
（四）经社区戒毒、强制隔离戒毒后再次吸食、注射毒品的。  
对于吸毒成瘾严重，通过社区戒毒难以戒除毒瘾的人员，公安机关可以直接作出强制隔离戒毒的决定。  
吸毒成瘾人员自愿接受强制隔离戒毒的，经公安机关同意，可以进入强制隔离戒毒场所戒毒。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/戒毒条例.md#art-25" title="被《戒毒条例》第25条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 38** Where a person addicted to drugs falls under any of the following circumstances, the public security organ of a people's government at or above the county level shall make a decision on compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation:  
(1) Refusing to undergo community-based drug rehabilitation;  
(2) Using or injecting drugs during community-based drug rehabilitation;  
(3) Seriously violating the community-based drug rehabilitation agreement;  
(4) Using or injecting drugs again after community-based drug rehabilitation or compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation.  
For persons with severe drug addiction for whom it is difficult to overcome the addiction through community-based drug rehabilitation, the public security organ may directly make a decision on compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation.  
Where a person addicted to drugs voluntarily accepts compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation, he or she may, with the consent of the public security organ, enter a compulsory isolation facility for drug rehabilitation.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　怀孕或者正在哺乳自己不满一周岁婴儿的妇女吸毒成瘾的，不适用强制隔离戒毒。不满十六周岁的未成年人吸毒成瘾的，可以不适用强制隔离戒毒。  
对依照前款规定不适用强制隔离戒毒的吸毒成瘾人员，依照本法规定进行社区戒毒，由负责社区戒毒工作的城市街道办事处、乡镇人民政府加强帮助、教育和监督，督促落实社区戒毒措施。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/戒毒条例.md#art-26" title="被《戒毒条例》第26条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 39** Compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation shall not apply to women addicted to drugs who are pregnant or breastfeeding their own infants under one year of age. For minors under the age of sixteen who are addicted to drugs, compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation may not apply.  
For persons addicted to drugs to whom compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation does not apply according to the preceding paragraph, community-based drug rehabilitation shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of this Law, and the sub-district office or township people's government responsible for community-based drug rehabilitation shall strengthen assistance, education, and supervision, and urge the implementation of community-based drug rehabilitation measures.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　公安机关对吸毒成瘾人员决定予以强制隔离戒毒的，应当制作强制隔离戒毒决定书，在执行强制隔离戒毒前送达被决定人，并在送达后二十四小时以内通知被决定人的家属、所在单位和户籍所在地公安派出所；被决定人不讲真实姓名、住址，身份不明的，公安机关应当自查清其身份后通知。  
被决定人对公安机关作出的强制隔离戒毒决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 40** Where the public security organ decides to subject a person addicted to drugs to compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation, it shall produce a written decision on compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation, serve it on the person subject to the decision before the enforcement of compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation, and notify the person's family members, work unit, and the local public security police station of the household registration place within twenty-four hours after service; where the person subject to the decision does not provide his or her true name or address, and his or her identity is unknown, the public security organ shall notify them after ascertaining the identity.  
Where a person subject to the decision refuses to accept the decision on compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation made by the public security organ, he or she may apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate administrative action according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　对被决定予以强制隔离戒毒的人员，由作出决定的公安机关送强制隔离戒毒场所执行。  
强制隔离戒毒场所的设置、管理体制和经费保障，由国务院规定。

**Article 41** Persons who have been decided to be subjected to compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation shall be sent by the public security organ that made the decision to a compulsory isolation facility for drug rehabilitation for enforcement.  
The establishment, management system, and funding guarantee of compulsory isolation facilities for drug rehabilitation shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　戒毒人员进入强制隔离戒毒场所戒毒时，应当接受对其身体和所携带物品的检查。

**Article 42** When entering a compulsory isolation facility for drug rehabilitation, a drug rehabilitation recipient shall undergo a physical examination and inspection of the articles carried.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　强制隔离戒毒场所应当根据戒毒人员吸食、注射毒品的种类及成瘾程度等，对戒毒人员进行有针对性的生理、心理治疗和身体康复训练。  
根据戒毒的需要，强制隔离戒毒场所可以组织戒毒人员参加必要的生产劳动，对戒毒人员进行职业技能培训。组织戒毒人员参加生产劳动的，应当支付劳动报酬。

**Article 43** A compulsory isolation facility for drug rehabilitation shall, based on the type of drugs used or injected by the drug rehabilitation recipient and the degree of addiction, provide targeted physiological and psychological treatment and physical rehabilitation training.  
Based on the needs of drug rehabilitation, a compulsory isolation facility for drug rehabilitation may organize drug rehabilitation recipients to participate in necessary productive labor and provide vocational skill training. Where drug rehabilitation recipients are organized to participate in productive labor, labor remuneration shall be paid.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　强制隔离戒毒场所应当根据戒毒人员的性别、年龄、患病等情况，对戒毒人员实行分别管理。  
强制隔离戒毒场所对有严重残疾或者疾病的戒毒人员，应当给予必要的看护和治疗；对患有传染病的戒毒人员，应当依法采取必要的隔离、治疗措施；对可能发生自伤、自残等情形的戒毒人员，可以采取相应的保护性约束措施。  
强制隔离戒毒场所管理人员不得体罚、虐待或者侮辱戒毒人员。

**Article 44** A compulsory isolation facility for drug rehabilitation shall implement separate management based on the gender, age, illness, and other conditions of drug rehabilitation recipients.  
A compulsory isolation facility for drug rehabilitation shall provide necessary care and treatment for drug rehabilitation recipients with severe disabilities or diseases; for those suffering from infectious diseases, necessary isolation and treatment measures shall be taken according to law; for those who may engage in self-harm or self-mutilation, corresponding protective restraint measures may be taken.  
Management personnel of a compulsory isolation facility for drug rehabilitation shall not physically punish, abuse, or insult drug rehabilitation recipients.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　强制隔离戒毒场所应当根据戒毒治疗的需要配备执业医师。强制隔离戒毒场所的执业医师具有麻醉药品和精神药品处方权的，可以按照有关技术规范对戒毒人员使用麻醉药品、精神药品。  
卫生行政部门应当加强对强制隔离戒毒场所执业医师的业务指导和监督管理。

**Article 45** A compulsory isolation facility for drug rehabilitation shall be staffed with licensed physicians based on the needs of drug rehabilitation treatment. Where a licensed physician in a compulsory isolation facility for drug rehabilitation has the authority to prescribe narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, he or she may use such drugs on drug rehabilitation recipients in accordance with relevant technical specifications.  
The health administrative department shall strengthen professional guidance and supervision over licensed physicians in compulsory isolation facilities for drug rehabilitation.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　戒毒人员的亲属和所在单位或者就读学校的工作人员，可以按照有关规定探访戒毒人员。戒毒人员经强制隔离戒毒场所批准，可以外出探视配偶、直系亲属。  
强制隔离戒毒场所管理人员应当对强制隔离戒毒场所以外的人员交给戒毒人员的物品和邮件进行检查，防止夹带毒品。在检查邮件时，应当依法保护戒毒人员的通信自由和通信秘密。

**Article 46** The family members of drug rehabilitation recipients and the staff of their work units or schools may visit them in accordance with relevant regulations. With the approval of the compulsory isolation facility for drug rehabilitation, a drug rehabilitation recipient may go out to visit his or her spouse or lineal relatives.  
Management personnel of a compulsory isolation facility for drug rehabilitation shall inspect the articles and mail delivered to drug rehabilitation recipients by persons outside the facility to prevent the concealment of drugs. When inspecting mail, the freedom and confidentiality of correspondence of drug rehabilitation recipients shall be protected according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　强制隔离戒毒的期限为二年。  
执行强制隔离戒毒一年后，经诊断评估，对于戒毒情况良好的戒毒人员，强制隔离戒毒场所可以提出提前解除强制隔离戒毒的意见，报强制隔离戒毒的决定机关批准。  
强制隔离戒毒期满前，经诊断评估，对于需要延长戒毒期限的戒毒人员，由强制隔离戒毒场所提出延长戒毒期限的意见，报强制隔离戒毒的决定机关批准。强制隔离戒毒的期限最长可以延长一年。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/戒毒条例.md#art-33" title="被《戒毒条例》第33条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 47** The period of compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation shall be two years.  
After one year of enforcement of compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation, upon diagnosis and evaluation, for a drug rehabilitation recipient who has a good rehabilitation status, the compulsory isolation facility may propose an opinion on early release from compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation, which shall be submitted to the decision-making authority for approval.  
Before the expiration of the compulsory isolation period, upon diagnosis and evaluation, for a drug rehabilitation recipient who needs an extension of the rehabilitation period, the compulsory isolation facility shall propose an opinion on extending the period, which shall be submitted to the decision-making authority for approval. The period of compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation may be extended by a maximum of one year.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　对于被解除强制隔离戒毒的人员，强制隔离戒毒的决定机关可以责令其接受不超过三年的社区康复。  
社区康复参照本法关于社区戒毒的规定实施。

**Article 48** For persons released from compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation, the decision-making authority may order them to undergo community-based rehabilitation for a period not exceeding three years.  
Community-based rehabilitation shall be implemented with reference to the provisions of this Law on community-based drug rehabilitation.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上地方各级人民政府根据戒毒工作的需要，可以开办戒毒康复场所；对社会力量依法开办的公益性戒毒康复场所应当给予扶持，提供必要的便利和帮助。  
戒毒人员可以自愿在戒毒康复场所生活、劳动。戒毒康复场所组织戒毒人员参加生产劳动的，应当参照国家劳动用工制度的规定支付劳动报酬。

**Article 49** Local people's governments at or above the county level may, based on the needs of drug rehabilitation work, establish drug rehabilitation facilities; for public interest drug rehabilitation facilities established by social forces according to law, they shall provide support and necessary conveniences and assistance.  
Drug rehabilitation recipients may voluntarily live and work in drug rehabilitation facilities. Where a drug rehabilitation facility organizes drug rehabilitation recipients to participate in productive labor, it shall pay labor remuneration with reference to the provisions of the state labor employment system.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　公安机关、司法行政部门对被依法拘留、逮捕、收监执行刑罚以及被依法采取强制性教育措施的吸毒人员，应当给予必要的戒毒治疗。

**Article 50** The public security organs and judicial administrative departments shall provide necessary drug rehabilitation treatment to drug users who are detained, arrested, imprisoned for execution of punishment, or subjected to compulsory educational measures according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门会同公安机关、药品监督管理部门依照国家有关规定，根据巩固戒毒成果的需要和本行政区域艾滋病流行情况，可以组织开展戒毒药物维持治疗工作。

**Article 51** The health administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in conjunction with the public security organs and drug regulatory departments and in accordance with relevant state regulations, based on the need to consolidate drug rehabilitation results and the prevalence of AIDS in their respective administrative regions, organize and carry out drug rehabilitation maintenance treatment work.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　戒毒人员在入学、就业、享受社会保障等方面不受歧视。有关部门、组织和人员应当在入学、就业、享受社会保障等方面对戒毒人员给予必要的指导和帮助。

**Article 52** Drug rehabilitation recipients shall not be discriminated against in terms of school enrollment, employment, enjoyment of social security, and other aspects. Relevant departments, organizations, and persons shall provide necessary guidance and assistance to drug rehabilitation recipients in terms of school enrollment, employment, enjoyment of social security, and other aspects.

### 第五章　禁毒国际合作

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　中华人民共和国根据缔结或者参加的国际条约或者按照对等原则，开展禁毒国际合作。

**Article 53** The People's Republic of China shall carry out international cooperation in narcotics control in accordance with the international treaties it has concluded or acceded to, or on the principle of reciprocity.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家禁毒委员会根据国务院授权，负责组织开展禁毒国际合作，履行国际禁毒公约义务。

**Article 54** The National Narcotics Control Committee shall, as authorized by the State Council, be responsible for organizing and carrying out international cooperation in narcotics control and fulfilling the obligations of international narcotics control conventions.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　涉及追究毒品犯罪的司法协助，由司法机关依照有关法律的规定办理。

**Article 55** Judicial assistance concerning the investigation of drug crimes shall be handled by the judicial organs in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国务院有关部门应当按照各自职责，加强与有关国家或者地区执法机关以及国际组织的禁毒情报信息交流，依法开展禁毒执法合作。  
经国务院公安部门批准，边境地区县级以上人民政府公安机关可以与有关国家或者地区的执法机关开展执法合作。

**Article 56** The relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, strengthen the exchange of narcotics control intelligence and information with law enforcement agencies of relevant countries or regions and international organizations, and carry out narcotics control law enforcement cooperation according to law.  
With the approval of the public security department of the State Council, the public security organs of the people's governments at or above the county level in border areas may carry out law enforcement cooperation with law enforcement agencies of relevant countries or regions.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　通过禁毒国际合作破获毒品犯罪案件的，中华人民共和国政府可以与有关国家分享查获的非法所得、由非法所得获得的收益以及供毒品犯罪使用的财物或者财物变卖所得的款项。

**Article 57** Where drug crime cases are solved through international cooperation in narcotics control, the government of the People's Republic of China may share with the relevant countries the illegal gains seized, the proceeds derived from illegal gains, and the property used for drug crimes or the proceeds from the sale of such property.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国务院有关部门根据国务院授权，可以通过对外援助等渠道，支持有关国家实施毒品原植物替代种植、发展替代产业。

**Article 58** The relevant departments of the State Council may, as authorized by the State Council, through channels such as foreign aid, support relevant countries in implementing alternative cultivation of drug raw plants and developing alternative industries.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　有下列行为之一，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予治安管理处罚：  
（一）走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品的；  
（二）非法持有毒品的；  
（三）非法种植毒品原植物的；  
（四）非法买卖、运输、携带、持有未经灭活的毒品原植物种子或者幼苗的；  
（五）非法传授麻醉药品、精神药品或者易制毒化学品制造方法的；  
（六）强迫、引诱、教唆、欺骗他人吸食、注射毒品的；  
（七）向他人提供毒品的。

**Article 59** Any person who commits any of the following acts, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, public security penalties shall be imposed according to law:  
(1) Smuggling, trafficking, transporting, or manufacturing drugs;  
(2) Illegally possessing drugs;  
(3) Illegally cultivating drug raw plants;  
(4) Illegally trading, transporting, carrying, or possessing seeds or seedlings of drug raw plants that have not been sterilized;  
(5) Illegally teaching the manufacturing methods of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, or precursor chemicals;  
(6) Compelling, luring, instigating, or deceiving others into using or injecting drugs;  
(7) Providing drugs to others.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　有下列行为之一，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予治安管理处罚：  
（一）包庇走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品的犯罪分子，以及为犯罪分子窝藏、转移、隐瞒毒品或者犯罪所得财物的；  
（二）在公安机关查处毒品违法犯罪活动时为违法犯罪行为人通风报信的；  
（三）阻碍依法进行毒品检查的；  
（四）隐藏、转移、变卖或者损毁司法机关、行政执法机关依法扣押、查封、冻结的涉及毒品违法犯罪活动的财物的。

**Article 60** Any person who commits any of the following acts, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, public security penalties shall be imposed according to law:  
(1) Harboring criminals who smuggle, traffic, transport, or manufacture drugs, or harboring, transferring, or concealing drugs or property derived from criminal proceeds for criminals;  
(2) Tipping off perpetrators of drug-related illegal or criminal acts when public security organs are investigating such activities;  
(3) Obstructing drug inspections conducted according to law;  
(4) Concealing, transferring, selling off, or destroying property seized, sealed, or frozen by judicial organs or administrative law enforcement organs in accordance with law that is involved in drug-related illegal or criminal activities.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　容留他人吸食、注射毒品或者介绍买卖毒品，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由公安机关处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处三千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以下拘留或者五百元以下罚款。

**Article 61** Any person who accommodates others to use or inject drugs or introduces drug trafficking, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the public security organ shall impose a detention of not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than 3,000 yuan; if the circumstances are minor, a detention of not more than five days or a fine of not more than 500 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　吸食、注射毒品的，依法给予治安管理处罚。吸毒人员主动到公安机关登记或者到有资质的医疗机构接受戒毒治疗的，不予处罚。

**Article 62** Any person who uses or injects drugs shall be given public security penalties according to law. Where a drug user voluntarily registers with the public security organ or undergoes drug rehabilitation treatment in a qualified medical institution, no penalty shall be imposed.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　在麻醉药品、精神药品的实验研究、生产、经营、使用、储存、运输、进口、出口以及麻醉药品药用原植物种植活动中，违反国家规定，致使麻醉药品、精神药品或者麻醉药品药用原植物流入非法渠道，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定给予处罚。

**Article 63** Any person who, in the activities of experimental research, production, trade, use, storage, transportation, import, export of narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances, or in the cultivation of narcotic drug raw plants, violates state regulations and causes narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, or narcotic drug raw plants to flow into illegal channels, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　在易制毒化学品的生产、经营、购买、运输或者进口、出口活动中，违反国家规定，致使易制毒化学品流入非法渠道，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定给予处罚。

**Article 64** Any person who, in the activities of production, trade, purchase, transportation, import, or export of precursor chemicals, violates state regulations and causes precursor chemicals to flow into illegal channels, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　娱乐场所及其从业人员实施毒品违法犯罪行为，或者为进入娱乐场所的人员实施毒品违法犯罪行为提供条件，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定给予处罚。  
娱乐场所经营管理人员明知场所内发生聚众吸食、注射毒品或者贩毒活动，不向公安机关报告的，依照前款的规定给予处罚。

**Article 65** Where an entertainment venue or its employees commit drug-related illegal or criminal acts, or provide conditions for persons entering the venue to commit such acts, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
Where a manager of an entertainment venue, knowing that group drug use or injection or drug trafficking activities are occurring in the venue, fails to report to the public security organ, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　未经批准，擅自从事戒毒治疗业务的，由卫生行政部门责令停止违法业务活动，没收违法所得和使用的药品、医疗器械等物品；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 66** Where any person engages in drug rehabilitation treatment business without approval, the health administrative department shall order the cessation of the illegal business activities, confiscate the illegal gains and the pharmaceuticals, medical devices, and other articles used; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　戒毒医疗机构发现接受戒毒治疗的戒毒人员在治疗期间吸食、注射毒品，不向公安机关报告的，由卫生行政部门责令改正；情节严重的，责令停业整顿。

**Article 67** Where a drug rehabilitation medical institution discovers that a drug rehabilitation recipient undergoing treatment uses or injects drugs during the treatment period and fails to report to the public security organ, the health administrative department shall order it to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　强制隔离戒毒场所、医疗机构、医师违反规定使用麻醉药品、精神药品，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定给予处罚。

**Article 68** Where a compulsory isolation facility for drug rehabilitation, a medical institution, or a physician uses narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances in violation of regulations, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　公安机关、司法行政部门或者其他有关主管部门的工作人员在禁毒工作中有下列行为之一，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分：  
（一）包庇、纵容毒品违法犯罪人员的；  
（二）对戒毒人员有体罚、虐待、侮辱等行为的；  
（三）挪用、截留、克扣禁毒经费的；  
（四）擅自处分查获的毒品和扣押、查封、冻结的涉及毒品违法犯罪活动的财物的。

**Article 69** Where any staff member of a public security organ, judicial administrative department, or other relevant competent authority commits any of the following acts in narcotics control work, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, sanctions shall be imposed according to law:  
(1) Harboring or condoning drug-related illegal or criminal persons;  
(2) Physically punishing, abusing, or insulting drug rehabilitation recipients;  
(3) Misappropriating, retaining, or embezzling narcotics control funds;  
(4) Unauthorized disposal of seized drugs and property seized, sealed, or frozen that is involved in drug-related illegal or criminal activities.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　有关单位及其工作人员在入学、就业、享受社会保障等方面歧视戒毒人员的，由教育行政部门、劳动行政部门责令改正；给当事人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 70** Where a relevant unit or its staff member discriminates against a drug rehabilitation recipient in terms of school enrollment, employment, enjoyment of social security, or other aspects, the education administrative department or the labor administrative department shall order it to make corrections; if losses are caused to the party, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　本法自2008年6月1日起施行。《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于禁毒的决定》同时废止。

**Article 71** This Law shall come into force as of June 1, 2008. The "Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Narcotics Control" shall be abolished simultaneously.
