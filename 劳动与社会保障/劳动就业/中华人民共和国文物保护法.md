### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对文物的保护，传承中华民族优秀历史文化遗产，促进科学研究工作，进行爱国主义和革命传统教育，增强历史自觉、坚定文化自信，建设社会主义精神文明和物质文明，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** . For the purpose of strengthening the protection of cultural relics, inheriting the excellent historical and cultural heritage of the Chinese nation, promoting scientific research, conducting education in patriotism and revolutionary tradition, enhancing historical consciousness, strengthening cultural confidence, and building socialist spiritual and material civilization, this Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　文物受国家保护。本法所称文物，是指人类创造的或者与人类活动有关的，具有历史、艺术、科学价值的下列物质遗存：  
（一）古文化遗址、古墓葬、古建筑、石窟寺和古石刻、古壁画；  
（二）与重大历史事件、革命运动或者著名人物有关的以及具有重要纪念意义、教育意义或者史料价值的近代现代重要史迹、实物、代表性建筑；  
（三）历史上各时代珍贵的艺术品、工艺美术品；  
（四）历史上各时代重要的文献资料、手稿和图书资料等；  
（五）反映历史上各时代、各民族社会制度、社会生产、社会生活的代表性实物。  
文物认定的主体、标准和程序，由国务院规定并公布。  
具有科学价值的古脊椎动物化石和古人类化石同文物一样受国家保护。

**Article 2** . Cultural relics are protected by the State. "Cultural relics" as used in this Law means the following material remains created by humans or related to human activities, which have historical, artistic, and scientific value:  
(1) Ancient cultural sites, ancient tombs, ancient buildings, cave temples, ancient stone carvings, and ancient murals;  
(2) Important modern and contemporary historical sites, objects, and representative buildings related to major historical events, revolutionary movements, or famous persons, and having important commemorative, educational, or historical value;  
(3) Precious works of art and arts and crafts from various historical periods;  
(4) Important documents, manuscripts, books, and materials from various historical periods;  
(5) Representative objects reflecting the social systems, social production, and social life of various nationalities and historical periods.  
The entity, standards, and procedures for the identification of cultural relics shall be prescribed and published by the State Council.  
Ancient vertebrate fossils and ancient human fossils of scientific value shall be protected by the State in the same manner as cultural relics.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　文物分为不可移动文物和可移动文物。  
古文化遗址、古墓葬、古建筑、石窟寺、古石刻、古壁画、近代现代重要史迹和代表性建筑等不可移动文物，分为文物保护单位和未核定公布为文物保护单位的不可移动文物（以下称未定级不可移动文物）；文物保护单位分为全国重点文物保护单位，省级文物保护单位，设区的市级、县级文物保护单位。  
历史上各时代重要实物、艺术品、工艺美术品、文献资料、手稿、图书资料、代表性实物等可移动文物，分为珍贵文物和一般文物；珍贵文物分为一级文物、二级文物、三级文物。

**Article 3** . Cultural relics are divided into immovable cultural relics and movable cultural relics.  
Immovable cultural relics such as ancient cultural sites, ancient tombs, ancient buildings, cave temples, ancient stone carvings, ancient murals, and important modern and contemporary historical sites and representative buildings are classified into cultural relic protection units and immovable cultural relics that have not been verified and announced as cultural relic protection units (hereinafter referred to as ungraded immovable cultural relics); cultural relic protection units are divided into national key cultural relic protection units, provincial cultural relic protection units, and cultural relic protection units at the city level (with districts) and county level.  
Movable cultural relics such as important objects, works of art, arts and crafts, documents, manuscripts, books, representative objects from various historical periods are classified into precious cultural relics and ordinary cultural relics; precious cultural relics are divided into Grade 1, Grade 2, and Grade 3.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　文物工作坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持以社会主义核心价值观为引领，贯彻保护为主、抢救第一、合理利用、加强管理的方针。

**Article 4** . Cultural relic work shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, take the core socialist values as guidance, and implement the policy of giving priority to protection, making rescue the first priority, making rational use, and strengthening management.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中华人民共和国境内地下、内水和领海中遗存的一切文物，以及中国管辖的其他海域内遗存的起源于中国的和起源国不明的文物，属于国家所有。  
古文化遗址、古墓葬、石窟寺属于国家所有。国家指定保护的纪念建筑物、古建筑、古石刻、古壁画、近代现代代表性建筑等不可移动文物，除国家另有规定的以外，属于国家所有。  
国有不可移动文物的所有权不因其所依附的土地的所有权或者使用权的改变而改变。

**Article 5** . All cultural relics remaining underground, in inland waters, and in territorial seas within the territory of the People's Republic of China, as well as cultural relics of Chinese origin or of unknown origin remaining in other sea areas under Chinese jurisdiction, belong to the State.  
Ancient cultural sites, ancient tombs, and cave temples belong to the State. Immovable cultural relics such as memorial buildings, ancient buildings, ancient stone carvings, ancient murals, and representative modern and contemporary buildings designated for protection by the State shall belong to the State, unless otherwise provided by the State.  
The ownership of State-owned immovable cultural relics shall not change due to changes in the ownership or right to use of the land to which they are attached.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　下列可移动文物，属于国家所有：  
（一）中国境内地下、内水和领海以及中国管辖的其他海域内出土、出水的文物，国家另有规定的除外；  
（二）国有文物收藏单位以及其他国家机关、部队和国有企业、事业单位等收藏、保管的文物；  
（三）国家征集、购买或者依法没收的文物；  
（四）公民、组织捐赠给国家的文物；  
（五）法律规定属于国家所有的其他文物。  
国有可移动文物的所有权不因其收藏、保管单位的终止或者变更而改变。

**Article 6** . The following movable cultural relics belong to the State:  
(1) Cultural relics unearthed or recovered from underground, inland waters, and territorial seas within the territory of China, as well as other sea areas under Chinese jurisdiction, unless otherwise provided by the State;  
(2) Cultural relics collected or kept by State-owned cultural relic collection entities as well as other State organs, armed forces, and State-owned enterprises, institutions, etc.;  
(3) Cultural relics collected, purchased, or confiscated according to law by the State;  
(4) Cultural relics donated to the State by citizens or organizations;  
(5) Other cultural relics that belong to the State as provided by law.  
The ownership of State-owned movable cultural relics shall not change due to the termination or change of the entity that collects or keeps them.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国有文物所有权受法律保护，不容侵犯。  
属于集体所有和私人所有的纪念建筑物、古建筑和祖传文物以及依法取得的其他文物，其所有权受法律保护。文物的所有者必须遵守国家有关文物保护的法律、法规的规定。

**Article 7** . The ownership of State-owned cultural relics is protected by law and shall not be violated.  
The ownership of memorial buildings, ancient buildings, and ancestral cultural relics belonging to collective ownership or private ownership, as well as other cultural relics obtained in accordance with law, shall be protected by law. The owners of cultural relics must comply with the provisions of the State's laws and regulations on the protection of cultural relics.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　一切机关、组织和个人都有依法保护文物的义务。

**Article 8** . All organs, organizations, and individuals have the obligation to protect cultural relics in accordance with law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院文物行政部门主管全国文物保护工作。  
地方各级人民政府负责本行政区域内的文物保护工作。县级以上地方人民政府文物行政部门对本行政区域内的文物保护实施监督管理。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门在各自的职责范围内，负责有关的文物保护工作。

**Article 9** . The administrative department for cultural relics of the State Council shall be in charge of the protection of cultural relics nationwide.  
The local people's governments at all levels shall be responsible for the protection of cultural relics within their respective administrative regions. The administrative departments for cultural relics of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall supervise and manage the protection of cultural relics within their respective administrative regions.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the protection of cultural relics within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家发展文物保护事业，贯彻落实保护第一、加强管理、挖掘价值、有效利用、让文物活起来的工作要求。

**Article 10** . The State shall develop the undertaking of cultural relic protection, and implement the work requirements of giving priority to protection, strengthening management, excavating value, making effective use, and bringing cultural relics to life.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　文物是不可再生的文化资源。各级人民政府应当重视文物保护，正确处理经济建设、社会发展与文物保护的关系，确保文物安全。  
基本建设、旅游发展必须把文物保护放在第一位，严格落实文物保护与安全管理规定，防止建设性破坏和过度商业化。

**Article 11** . Cultural relics are non-renewable cultural resources. The people's governments at all levels shall attach importance to the protection of cultural relics, correctly handle the relationship between economic construction, social development, and cultural relic protection, and ensure the safety of cultural relics.  
In capital construction and tourism development, cultural relic protection must be given top priority, and the provisions on cultural relic protection and safety management shall be strictly implemented to prevent destructive construction and excessive commercialization.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对与中国共产党各个历史时期重大事件、重要会议、重要人物和伟大建党精神等有关的文物，各级人民政府应当采取措施加强保护。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国文物保护法实施条例.md#art-6" title="被《中华人民共和国文物保护法实施条例》第6条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 12** . The people's governments at all levels shall take measures to strengthen the protection of cultural relics related to major events, important meetings, important figures, and the great founding spirit of the Communist Party of China in various historical periods.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上人民政府应当将文物保护事业纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，所需经费列入本级预算，确保文物保护事业发展与国民经济和社会发展水平相适应。  
国有博物馆、纪念馆、文物保护单位等的事业性收入，纳入预算管理，用于文物保护事业，任何单位或者个人不得侵占、挪用。  
国家鼓励通过捐赠等方式设立文物保护社会基金，专门用于文物保护，任何单位或者个人不得侵占、挪用。

**Article 13** . The people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the undertaking of cultural relic protection into their national economic and social development plans, and include the necessary funds in their budgets to ensure that the development of cultural relic protection is commensurate with the level of national economic and social development.  
The operating income of State-owned museums, memorial halls, and cultural relic protection units shall be included in budget management and used for the undertaking of cultural relic protection, and shall not be embezzled or misappropriated by any unit or individual.  
The State encourages the establishment of social funds for cultural relic protection through donations and other means, which shall be used exclusively for cultural relic protection, and shall not be embezzled or misappropriated by any unit or individual.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上人民政府及其文物行政部门应当加强文物普查和专项调查，全面掌握文物资源及保护情况。  
县级以上人民政府文物行政部门加强对国有文物资源资产的动态管理，按照国家有关规定，及时报送国有文物资源资产管理情况的报告。

**Article 14** . The people's governments at or above the county level and their administrative departments for cultural relics shall strengthen the general survey and special investigation of cultural relics to comprehensively grasp the situation of cultural relic resources and protection.  
The administrative departments for cultural relics of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the dynamic management of State-owned cultural relic resources and assets, and timely submit reports on the management of State-owned cultural relic resources and assets in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家支持和规范文物价值挖掘阐释，促进中华文明起源与发展研究，传承中华优秀传统文化，弘扬革命文化，发展社会主义先进文化，铸牢中华民族共同体意识，提升中华文化影响力。

**Article 15** . The State supports and standardizes the excavation and interpretation of the value of cultural relics, promotes research on the origin and development of Chinese civilization, inherits the excellent traditional Chinese culture, promotes revolutionary culture, develops advanced socialist culture, consolidates a strong sense of community for the Chinese nation, and enhances the influence of Chinese culture.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家加强文物保护的宣传教育，创新传播方式，增强全民文物保护的意识，营造自觉传承中华民族优秀历史文化遗产的社会氛围。  
新闻媒体应当开展文物保护法律法规和文物保护知识的宣传报道，并依法对危害文物安全、破坏文物的行为进行舆论监督。  
博物馆、纪念馆、文物保管所、考古遗址公园等有关单位应当结合参观游览内容有针对性地开展文物保护宣传教育活动。

**Article 16** . The State shall strengthen publicity and education on cultural relic protection, innovate methods of dissemination, enhance the awareness of cultural relic protection among the whole people, and create a social atmosphere of consciously inheriting the excellent historical and cultural heritage of the Chinese nation.  
News media shall carry out publicity and reports on laws, regulations, and knowledge of cultural relic protection, and exercise public opinion supervision over acts that endanger the safety of cultural relics or damage cultural relics in accordance with law.  
Museums, memorial halls, cultural relic depositories, archaeological site parks and other relevant entities shall, in combination with the content of visits, carry out targeted publicity and education activities on cultural relic protection.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家鼓励开展文物保护的科学研究，推广先进适用的文物保护技术，提高文物保护的科学技术水平。  
国家加强文物保护信息化建设，鼓励开展文物保护数字化工作，推进文物资源数字化采集和展示利用。  
国家加大考古、修缮、修复等文物保护专业人才培养力度，健全人才培养、使用、评价和激励机制。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/长城保护条例.md#art-12" title="被《长城保护条例》第12条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 17** . The State encourages scientific research on cultural relic protection, promotes advanced and applicable cultural relic protection technologies, and raises the scientific and technological level of cultural relic protection.  
The State shall strengthen the informationization construction of cultural relic protection, encourage the digital work of cultural relic protection, and promote the digital collection, display, and utilization of cultural relic resources.  
The State shall intensify the training of professional talents in archaeology, restoration, and conservation of cultural relics, and improve the mechanism for talent cultivation, use, evaluation, and incentive.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家鼓励开展文物利用研究，在确保文物安全的前提下，坚持社会效益优先，有效利用文物资源，提供多样化多层次的文化产品与服务。

**Article 18** . The State encourages research on the utilization of cultural relics, and on the premise of ensuring the safety of cultural relics, adheres to the priority of social benefits, effectively utilizes cultural relic resources, and provides diversified and multi-level cultural products and services.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家健全社会参与机制，调动社会力量参与文化遗产保护的积极性，鼓励引导社会力量投入文化遗产保护。

**Article 19** . The State shall improve the social participation mechanism, mobilize the enthusiasm of social forces to participate in cultural heritage protection, and encourage and guide social forces to invest in cultural heritage protection.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家支持开展考古、修缮、修复、展览、科学研究、执法、司法等文物保护国际交流与合作，促进人类文明交流互鉴。

**Article 20** . The State supports international exchanges and cooperation in cultural relic protection such as archaeology, restoration, conservation, exhibitions, scientific research, law enforcement, and judicial activities, and promotes mutual learning among human civilizations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上人民政府文物行政部门或者有关部门应当公开投诉、举报方式等信息，及时受理并处理涉及文物保护的投诉、举报。

**Article 21** : The administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government at or above the county level or the relevant department shall disclose information such as the means of complaints and reports, and shall promptly accept and handle complaints and reports concerning the protection of cultural relics.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　有下列事迹之一的单位或者个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励：  
（一）认真执行文物保护法律、法规，保护文物成绩显著的；  
（二）为保护文物与违法犯罪行为作坚决斗争的；  
（三）将收藏的重要文物捐献给国家或者向文物保护事业捐赠的；  
（四）发现文物及时上报或者上交，使文物得到保护的；  
（五）在考古发掘、文物价值挖掘阐释等工作中做出重大贡献的；  
（六）在文物保护科学技术方面有重要发明创造或者其他重要贡献的；  
（七）在文物面临破坏危险时，抢救文物有功的；  
（八）长期从事文物工作，做出显著成绩的；  
（九）组织、参与文物保护志愿服务，做出显著成绩的；  
（十）在文物保护国际交流与合作中做出重大贡献的。

**Article 22** : Any entity or individual that has one of the following deeds shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State:  
(1) Earnestly implementing the laws and regulations on the protection of cultural relics and achieving remarkable results in protecting cultural relics;  
(2) Resolutely fighting against illegal and criminal acts for the protection of cultural relics;  
(3) Donating important cultural relics in their collection to the State or making donations to the cause of cultural relic protection;  
(4) Timely reporting or handing over discovered cultural relics, thereby ensuring their protection;  
(5) Making significant contributions in archaeological excavation, exploration and interpretation of cultural relic values, and other related work;  
(6) Making important inventions and creations or other significant contributions in the scientific and technological aspects of cultural relic protection;  
(7) Rendering meritorious service in rescuing cultural relics when they are in danger of destruction;  
(8) Engaging in cultural relic work for a long time and achieving remarkable results;  
(9) Organizing or participating in volunteer services for cultural relic protection and achieving remarkable results;  
(10) Making significant contributions in international exchanges and cooperation on cultural relic protection.

### 第二章　不可移动文物

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　在文物普查、专项调查或者其他相关工作中发现的不可移动文物，应当及时核定公布为文物保护单位或者登记公布为未定级不可移动文物。公民、组织可以提出核定公布文物保护单位或者登记公布未定级不可移动文物的建议。  
国务院文物行政部门在省级和设区的市级、县级文物保护单位中，选择具有重大历史、艺术、科学价值的确定为全国重点文物保护单位，或者直接确定为全国重点文物保护单位，报国务院核定公布。  
省级文物保护单位，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府核定公布，并报国务院备案。  
设区的市级和县级文物保护单位，分别由设区的市、自治州人民政府和县级人民政府核定公布，并报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府备案。  
未定级不可移动文物，由县级人民政府文物行政部门登记，报本级人民政府和上一级人民政府文物行政部门备案，并向社会公布。

**Article 23** : Immovable cultural relics discovered during general surveys, special investigations, or other related work shall be verified and announced as protected cultural relic sites or registered and announced as ungraded immovable cultural relics in a timely manner. Citizens and organizations may propose suggestions for verifying and announcing protected cultural relic sites or registering and announcing ungraded immovable cultural relics.  
The administrative department for cultural relics under the State Council shall, among the protected cultural relic sites at the provincial level and at the city-with-districts and county levels, select those of major historical, artistic, or scientific value and designate them as major historical and cultural sites protected at the national level, or directly designate them as such, and submit them to the State Council for verification and announcement.  
Protected cultural relic sites at the provincial level shall be verified and announced by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall be reported to the State Council for record.  
Protected cultural relic sites at the city-with-districts level and county level shall be verified and announced respectively by the people's government of a city with districts or an autonomous prefecture and the people's government of a county, and shall be reported to the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for record.  
Ungraded immovable cultural relics shall be registered by the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government of a county, reported to the people's government at the same level and the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government at the next higher level for record, and announced to the public.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　在旧城区改建、土地成片开发中，县级以上人民政府应当事先组织进行相关区域内不可移动文物调查，及时开展核定、登记、公布工作，并依法采取保护措施。未经调查，任何单位不得开工建设，防止建设性破坏。

**Article 24** : In the renovation of old urban areas and the development of contiguous land, the people's government at or above the county level shall organize a survey of immovable cultural relics in the relevant areas in advance, promptly carry out verification, registration, and announcement, and take protection measures according to law. No entity may commence construction without a survey, so as to prevent destructive construction.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　保存文物特别丰富并且具有重大历史价值或者革命纪念意义的城市，由国务院核定公布为历史文化名城。  
保存文物特别丰富并且具有重大历史价值或者革命纪念意义的城镇、街道、村庄，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府核定公布为历史文化街区、村镇，并报国务院备案。  
历史文化名城和历史文化街区、村镇所在地县级以上地方人民政府应当组织编制专门的历史文化名城和历史文化街区、村镇保护规划，并纳入有关规划。  
历史文化名城和历史文化街区、村镇的保护办法，由国务院制定。

**Article 25** : Cities that are particularly rich in cultural relics and have major historical value or revolutionary commemorative significance shall be verified and announced by the State Council as famous historical and cultural cities.  
Towns, streets, and villages that are particularly rich in cultural relics and have major historical value or revolutionary commemorative significance shall be verified and announced by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government as historical and cultural blocks and villages and towns, and shall be reported to the State Council for record.  
The local people's government at or above the county level where a famous historical and cultural city or a historical and cultural block, village, or town is located shall organize the formulation of special protection plans for such city or block, village, or town, and incorporate them into relevant plans.  
The measures for the protection of famous historical and cultural cities and historical and cultural blocks, villages, and towns shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　各级文物保护单位，分别由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和设区的市级、县级人民政府划定公布必要的保护范围，作出标志说明，建立记录档案，并区别情况分别设置专门机构或者专人负责管理。全国重点文物保护单位的保护范围和记录档案，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门报国务院文物行政部门备案。  
未定级不可移动文物，由县级人民政府文物行政部门作出标志说明，建立记录档案，明确管理责任人。  
县级以上地方人民政府文物行政部门应当根据不同文物的保护需要，制定文物保护单位和未定级不可移动文物的具体保护措施，向本级人民政府报告，并公告施行。  
文物行政部门应当指导、鼓励基层群众性自治组织、志愿者等参与不可移动文物保护工作。

**Article 26** : For protected cultural relic sites at all levels, the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the people's governments of cities with districts and counties shall respectively delimit and announce the necessary protection areas, set up signs and explanations, establish record files, and, depending on circumstances, set up specialized agencies or assign personnel to manage them. The protection areas and record files of major historical and cultural sites protected at the national level shall be reported by the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to the administrative department for cultural relics under the State Council for record.  
For ungraded immovable cultural relics, the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government of a county shall set up signs and explanations, establish record files, and designate the person responsible for management.  
The administrative department for cultural relics of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the protection needs of different cultural relics, formulate specific protection measures for protected cultural relic sites and ungraded immovable cultural relics, report them to the people's government at the same level, and announce them for implementation.  
The administrative department for cultural relics shall guide and encourage grassroots self-governing mass organizations, volunteers, and others to participate in the protection of immovable cultural relics.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　各级人民政府制定有关规划，应当根据文物保护的需要，事先由有关部门会同文物行政部门商定本行政区域内不可移动文物的保护措施，并纳入规划。  
县级以上地方人民政府文物行政部门根据文物保护需要，组织编制本行政区域内不可移动文物的保护规划，经本级人民政府批准后公布实施，并报上一级人民政府文物行政部门备案；全国重点文物保护单位的保护规划由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准后公布实施，并报国务院文物行政部门备案。

**Article 27** : When the people's governments at all levels formulate relevant plans, they shall, in light of the needs of cultural relic protection, have the relevant departments consult with the administrative department for cultural relics in advance to agree on the protection measures for immovable cultural relics within their respective administrative areas and incorporate them into the plans.  
The administrative department for cultural relics of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the needs of cultural relic protection, organize the compilation of protection plans for immovable cultural relics within its administrative area, which shall be published and implemented after approval by the people's government at the same level, and reported to the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government at the next higher level for record; the protection plan for a major historical and cultural site protected at the national level shall be published and implemented after approval by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and reported to the administrative department for cultural relics under the State Council for record.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　在文物保护单位的保护范围内不得进行文物保护工程以外的其他建设工程或者爆破、钻探、挖掘等作业；因特殊情况需要进行的，必须保证文物保护单位的安全。  
因特殊情况需要在省级或者设区的市级、县级文物保护单位的保护范围内进行前款规定的建设工程或者作业的，必须经核定公布该文物保护单位的人民政府批准，在批准前应当征得上一级人民政府文物行政部门同意；在全国重点文物保护单位的保护范围内进行前款规定的建设工程或者作业的，必须经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，在批准前应当征得国务院文物行政部门同意。

**Article 28** : No construction projects other than cultural relic protection projects or operations such as blasting, drilling, or excavation shall be carried out within the protection areas of protected cultural relic sites; where such activities are necessary due to special circumstances, the safety of the protected cultural relic site must be ensured.  
Where, due to special circumstances, it is necessary to carry out the construction projects or operations specified in the preceding paragraph within the protection area of a protected cultural relic site at the provincial level or at the city-with-districts or county level, approval must be obtained from the people's government that verified and announced the protected cultural relic site, and prior to approval, consent shall be obtained from the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government at the next higher level; where it is necessary to carry out the construction projects or operations specified in the preceding paragraph within the protection area of a major historical and cultural site protected at the national level, approval must be obtained from the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and prior to approval, consent shall be obtained from the administrative department for cultural relics under the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　根据保护文物的实际需要，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，可以在文物保护单位的周围划出一定的建设控制地带，并予以公布。  
在文物保护单位的建设控制地带内进行建设工程，不得破坏文物保护单位的历史风貌；工程设计方案应当根据文物保护单位的级别和建设工程对文物保护单位历史风貌的影响程度，经国家规定的文物行政部门同意后，依法取得建设工程规划许可。

**Article 29** : Based on the actual needs for protecting cultural relics, with the approval of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, a construction control zone may be delineated around a protected cultural relic site and announced.  
Construction projects within the construction control zone of a protected cultural relic site shall not damage the historical appearance of the protected cultural relic site; the engineering design plan shall, depending on the level of the protected cultural relic site and the extent of the impact of the construction project on its historical appearance, obtain consent from the administrative department for cultural relics as prescribed by the State, and then obtain a construction project planning permit according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　在文物保护单位的保护范围和建设控制地带内，不得建设污染文物保护单位及其环境的设施，不得进行可能影响文物保护单位安全及其环境的活动。对已有的污染文物保护单位及其环境的设施，依照生态环境有关法律法规的规定处理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国文物保护法实施条例.md#art-24" title="被《中华人民共和国文物保护法实施条例》第24条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 30** : Within the protection areas and construction control zones of protected cultural relic sites, no facilities that pollute the protected cultural relic site and its environment shall be constructed, and no activities that may affect the safety of the protected cultural relic site and its environment shall be carried out. Existing facilities that pollute the protected cultural relic site and its environment shall be dealt with in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations on ecology and environment.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　建设工程选址，应当尽可能避开不可移动文物；因特殊情况不能避开的，应当尽可能实施原址保护。  
实施原址保护的，建设单位应当事先确定原址保护措施，根据文物保护单位的级别报相应的文物行政部门批准；未定级不可移动文物的原址保护措施，报县级人民政府文物行政部门批准；未经批准的，不得开工建设。  
无法实施原址保护，省级或者设区的市级、县级文物保护单位需要迁移异地保护或者拆除的，应当报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准；迁移或者拆除省级文物保护单位的，批准前必须征得国务院文物行政部门同意。全国重点文物保护单位不得拆除；需要迁移的，必须由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府报国务院批准。未定级不可移动文物需要迁移异地保护或者拆除的，应当报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门批准。  
依照前款规定拆除国有不可移动文物，由文物行政部门监督实施，对具有收藏价值的壁画、雕塑、建筑构件等，由文物行政部门指定的文物收藏单位收藏。  
本条规定的原址保护、迁移、拆除所需费用，由建设单位列入建设工程预算。

**Article 31** : The site selection for construction projects shall, as far as possible, avoid immovable cultural relics; where avoidance is not possible due to special circumstances, on-site protection shall, as far as possible, be implemented.  
Where on-site protection is implemented, the construction entity shall determine the on-site protection measures in advance and submit them for approval to the corresponding administrative department for cultural relics according to the level of the protected cultural relic site; the on-site protection measures for ungraded immovable cultural relics shall be submitted for approval to the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government of a county; without approval, no construction shall commence.  
Where on-site protection cannot be implemented, and a protected cultural relic site at the provincial level or at the city-with-districts or county level needs to be relocated for off-site protection or demolished, approval shall be obtained from the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; where a protected cultural relic site at the provincial level is to be relocated or demolished, consent must be obtained from the administrative department for cultural relics under the State Council prior to approval. Major historical and cultural sites protected at the national level shall not be demolished; where relocation is necessary, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government must submit a report to the State Council for approval. Where an ungraded immovable cultural relic needs to be relocated for off-site protection or demolished, approval shall be obtained from the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
Where a State-owned immovable cultural relic is demolished in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the administrative department for cultural relics shall supervise the implementation; murals, sculptures, architectural components, and other items of collection value shall be collected by the cultural relic collection unit designated by the administrative department for cultural relics.  
The expenses required for on-site protection, relocation, and demolition as provided for in this Article shall be included in the construction project budget by the construction entity.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国有不可移动文物由使用人负责修缮、保养；非国有不可移动文物由所有人或者使用人负责修缮、保养，县级以上人民政府可以予以补助。  
不可移动文物有损毁危险，所有人或者使用人不具备修缮能力的，县级以上人民政府应当给予帮助；所有人或者使用人具备修缮能力但拒不依法履行修缮义务的，县级以上人民政府可以给予抢救修缮，所需费用由所有人或者使用人承担。  
对文物保护单位进行修缮，应当根据文物保护单位的级别报相应的文物行政部门批准；对未定级不可移动文物进行修缮，应当报县级人民政府文物行政部门批准。  
文物保护单位的修缮、迁移、重建，由取得文物保护工程资质证书的单位承担。  
对不可移动文物进行修缮、保养、迁移，必须遵守不改变文物原状和最小干预的原则，确保文物的真实性和完整性。  
县级以上人民政府文物行政部门应当加强对不可移动文物保护的监督检查，及时发现问题隐患，防范安全风险，并督促指导不可移动文物所有人或者使用人履行保护职责。

**Article 32** : State-owned immovable cultural relics shall be repaired and maintained by the user; non-State-owned immovable cultural relics shall be repaired and maintained by the owner or user, and the people's government at or above the county level may provide subsidies.  
Where an immovable cultural relic is in danger of damage and the owner or user does not have the capacity to repair it, the people's government at or above the county level shall provide assistance; where the owner or user has the capacity to repair it but refuses to perform the repair obligation according to law, the people's government at or above the county level may carry out emergency repairs, and the expenses required shall be borne by the owner or user.  
Repairs to a protected cultural relic site shall be submitted for approval to the corresponding administrative department for cultural relics according to the level of the protected cultural relic site; repairs to an ungraded immovable cultural relic shall be submitted for approval to the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government of a county.  
Repair, relocation, and reconstruction of protected cultural relic sites shall be undertaken by entities that have obtained the qualification certificate for cultural relic protection projects.  
Repair, maintenance, and relocation of immovable cultural relics must comply with the principles of not altering the original state and minimal intervention, ensuring the authenticity and integrity of the cultural relics.  
The administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and inspection of the protection of immovable cultural relics, promptly identify potential problems and hazards, prevent safety risks, and urge and guide the owners or users of immovable cultural relics to fulfill their protection duties.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　不可移动文物已经全部毁坏的，应当严格实施遗址保护，不得在原址重建。因文物保护等特殊情况需要在原址重建的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准；全国重点文物保护单位需要在原址重建的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府征得国务院文物行政部门同意后报国务院批准。

**Article 33** : Where an immovable cultural relic has been completely destroyed, site protection shall be strictly implemented, and reconstruction on the original site shall not be allowed. Where reconstruction on the original site is necessary due to special circumstances such as cultural relic protection, the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall submit a report to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval; where reconstruction on the original site is necessary for a major historical and cultural site protected at the national level, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall obtain consent from the administrative department for cultural relics under the State Council and then submit a report to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国有文物保护单位中的纪念建筑物或者古建筑，除可以建立博物馆、文物保管所或者辟为参观游览场所外，改作其他用途的，设区的市级、县级文物保护单位应当经核定公布该文物保护单位的人民政府文物行政部门征得上一级人民政府文物行政部门同意后，报核定公布该文物保护单位的人民政府批准；省级文物保护单位应当经核定公布该文物保护单位的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门审核同意后，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准；全国重点文物保护单位应当由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府报国务院批准。国有未定级不可移动文物改作其他用途的，应当报告县级人民政府文物行政部门。

**Article 34** : Memorial buildings or ancient buildings within State-owned protected cultural relic sites, except where they may be used as museums, cultural relic repositories, or opened as tourist sites, if to be used for other purposes, for a protected cultural relic site at the city-with-districts or county level, the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government that verified and announced the protected cultural relic site shall obtain consent from the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government at the next higher level, and then submit a report to the people's government that verified and announced the protected cultural relic site for approval; for a protected cultural relic site at the provincial level, the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government that verified and announced the protected cultural relic site shall review and consent, and then submit a report to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval; for a major historical and cultural site protected at the national level, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall submit a report to the State Council for approval. Where a State-owned ungraded immovable cultural relic is to be used for other purposes, it shall be reported to the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government of a county.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国有不可移动文物不得转让、抵押，国家另有规定的，依照其规定。建立博物馆、文物保管所或者辟为参观游览场所的国有不可移动文物，不得改作企业资产经营；其管理机构不得改由企业管理。  
依托历史文化街区、村镇进行旅游等开发建设活动的，应当严格落实相关保护规划和保护措施，控制大规模搬迁，防止过度开发，加强整体保护和活态传承。

**Article 35** : State-owned immovable cultural relics shall not be transferred or mortgaged; where the State provides otherwise, such provisions shall prevail. State-owned immovable cultural relics used as museums, cultural relic repositories, or opened as tourist sites shall not be operated as enterprise assets; their management institutions shall not be changed to enterprise management.  
Where tourism or other development and construction activities are carried out relying on historical and cultural blocks, villages, and towns, the relevant protection plans and protection measures shall be strictly implemented, large-scale relocation shall be controlled, overdevelopment shall be prevented, and overall protection and living heritage transmission shall be strengthened.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　非国有不可移动文物不得转让、抵押给外国人、外国组织或者国际组织。  
非国有不可移动文物转让、抵押或者改变用途的，应当报相应的文物行政部门备案。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国文物保护法实施条例.md#art-31" title="被《中华人民共和国文物保护法实施条例》第31条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 36** : Non-State-owned immovable cultural relics shall not be transferred or mortgaged to foreigners, foreign organizations, or international organizations.  
Where non-State-owned immovable cultural relics are transferred, mortgaged, or have their use changed, they shall be reported to the corresponding administrative department for cultural relics for record.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当采取措施，在确保文物安全的前提下，因地制宜推动不可移动文物有效利用。  
文物保护单位应当尽可能向社会开放。文物保护单位向社会开放，应当合理确定开放时间和游客承载量，并向社会公布，积极为游客提供必要的便利。  
为保护不可移动文物建立的博物馆、纪念馆、文物保管所、考古遗址公园等单位，应当加强对不可移动文物价值的挖掘阐释，开展有针对性的宣传讲解。

**Article 37** : The people's government at or above the county level and its relevant departments shall take measures to promote the effective utilization of immovable cultural relics in accordance with local conditions, while ensuring the safety of the cultural relics.  
Protected cultural relic sites shall be open to the public as much as possible. When opening to the public, protected cultural relic sites shall reasonably determine the opening hours and visitor carrying capacity, announce them to the public, and actively provide necessary convenience for visitors.  
Units such as museums, memorial halls, cultural relic repositories, and archaeological site parks established for the protection of immovable cultural relics shall strengthen the exploration and interpretation of the value of immovable cultural relics, and carry out targeted promotional explanations.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　使用不可移动文物，必须遵守不改变文物原状和最小干预的原则，负责保护文物本体及其附属文物的安全，不得损毁、改建、添建或者拆除不可移动文物。  
对危害不可移动文物安全、破坏不可移动文物历史风貌的建筑物、构筑物，当地人民政府应当及时调查处理；必要时，对该建筑物、构筑物依法予以拆除、迁移。

**Article 38** : Anyone using immovable cultural relics must comply with the principles of not altering the original state and minimal intervention, be responsible for protecting the safety of the cultural relic itself and its attached cultural relics, and shall not damage, alter, add to, or demolish the immovable cultural relics.  
Where buildings or structures endanger the safety of immovable cultural relics or damage their historical appearance, the local people's government shall promptly investigate and deal with them; when necessary, such buildings or structures shall be demolished or relocated according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　不可移动文物的所有人或者使用人应当加强用火、用电、用气等的消防安全管理，根据不可移动文物的特点，采取有针对性的消防安全措施，提高火灾预防和应急处置能力，确保文物安全。

**Article 39** : The owner or user of an immovable cultural relic shall strengthen fire safety management concerning the use of fire, electricity, gas, etc., adopt targeted fire safety measures based on the characteristics of the immovable cultural relic, improve fire prevention and emergency response capabilities, and ensure the safety of the cultural relic.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以将地下埋藏、水下遗存的文物分布较为集中，需要整体保护的区域划定为地下文物埋藏区、水下文物保护区，制定具体保护措施，并公告施行。  
地下文物埋藏区、水下文物保护区涉及两个以上省、自治区、直辖市的，或者涉及中国领海以外由中国管辖的其他海域的，由国务院文物行政部门划定并制定具体保护措施，报国务院核定公布。

**Article 40** : The people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may delimit areas where underground buried or underwater remains of cultural relics are relatively concentrated and require overall protection as underground cultural relic burial zones or underwater cultural relic protection zones, formulate specific protection measures, and announce them for implementation.  
Where an underground cultural relic burial zone or underwater cultural relic protection zone involves two or more provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, or involves other sea areas under China's jurisdiction beyond the territorial sea, the administrative department for cultural relics under the State Council shall delimit such zones and formulate specific protection measures, and submit them to the State Council for verification and announcement.

### 第三章　考古发掘

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　一切考古发掘工作，必须履行报批手续；从事考古发掘的单位，应当取得国务院文物行政部门颁发的考古发掘资质证书。  
地下埋藏和水下遗存的文物，任何单位或者个人都不得私自发掘。

**Article 41** All archaeological excavation work must undergo the approval procedures; units engaged in archaeological excavation shall obtain a qualification certificate for archaeological excavation issued by the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council.  
No unit or individual may privately excavate any cultural relics buried underground or preserved underwater.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　从事考古发掘的单位，为了科学研究进行考古发掘，应当提出发掘计划，报国务院文物行政部门批准；对全国重点文物保护单位的考古发掘计划，应当经国务院文物行政部门审核后报国务院批准。国务院文物行政部门在批准或者审核前，应当征求社会科学研究机构及其他科研机构和有关专家的意见。

**Article 42** A unit engaged in archaeological excavation that conducts archaeological excavation for scientific research shall submit an excavation plan to the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council for approval; an archaeological excavation plan for a major historical and cultural site protected at the national level shall be submitted to the State Council for approval after being examined by the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council. Before approving or examining such a plan, the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council shall seek the opinions of social science research institutions, other scientific research institutions, and relevant experts.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　在可能存在地下文物的区域，县级以上地方人民政府进行土地出让或者划拨前，应当由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门组织从事考古发掘的单位进行考古调查、勘探。可能存在地下文物的区域，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门及时划定并动态调整。  
进行大型基本建设工程，或者在文物保护单位的保护范围、建设控制地带内进行建设工程，未依照前款规定进行考古调查、勘探的，建设单位应当事先报请省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门组织从事考古发掘的单位在工程范围内有可能埋藏文物的地方进行考古调查、勘探。  
考古调查、勘探中发现文物的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门根据文物保护的要求与建设单位共同商定保护措施；遇有重要发现的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门及时报国务院文物行政部门处理。由此导致停工或者工期延长，造成建设单位损失的，由县级以上地方人民政府文物行政部门会同有关部门听取建设单位意见后，提出处理意见，报本级人民政府批准。

**Article 43** In areas where underground cultural relics may exist, before the local people's government at or above the county level assigns or allocates land, the administrative department for cultural heritage under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize units engaged in archaeological excavation to conduct archaeological survey and exploration. The areas where underground cultural relics may exist shall be designated and dynamically adjusted in a timely manner by the administrative department for cultural heritage under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
Where a large-scale capital construction project is carried out, or a construction project is carried out within the protection area or construction control zone of a cultural relics protection unit, and the archaeological survey and exploration have not been conducted in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the construction unit shall, in advance, request the administrative department for cultural heritage under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to organize units engaged in archaeological excavation to conduct archaeological survey and exploration in the places within the project area where cultural relics may be buried.  
Where cultural relics are discovered during the archaeological survey and exploration, the administrative department for cultural heritage under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the requirements of cultural relics protection, agree with the construction unit on the protection measures; where important discoveries are made, the administrative department for cultural heritage under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall promptly report to the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council for handling. Where this leads to suspension of work or extension of the construction period, causing losses to the construction unit, the administrative department for cultural heritage under the local people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments, hear the opinions of the construction unit, propose handling opinions, and submit them to the people's government at the same level for approval.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　需要配合进行考古发掘工作的，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门应当在勘探工作的基础上提出发掘计划，报国务院文物行政部门批准。国务院文物行政部门在批准前，应当征求社会科学研究机构及其他科研机构和有关专家的意见。  
确因建设工期紧迫或者有自然破坏危险，对古文化遗址、古墓葬急需进行抢救发掘的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门组织发掘，并同时补办审批手续。

**Article 44** Where it is necessary to cooperate in carrying out archaeological excavation work, the administrative department for cultural heritage under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, on the basis of the exploration work, propose an excavation plan and submit it to the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council for approval. Before approving the plan, the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council shall seek the opinions of social science research institutions, other scientific research institutions, and relevant experts.  
Where, due to the urgency of the construction period or the danger of natural destruction, rescue excavation of ancient cultural sites or ancient tombs is urgently needed, the administrative department for cultural heritage under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the excavation and simultaneously complete the approval formalities retrospectively.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　凡因进行基本建设和生产建设需要的考古调查、勘探、发掘，所需费用由建设单位列入建设工程预算。  
县级以上人民政府可以通过适当方式对考古调查、勘探、发掘工作给予支持。

**Article 45** For any archaeological survey, exploration, or excavation required for capital construction or production construction, the necessary expenses shall be included in the construction project budget by the construction unit.  
The people's government at or above the county level may provide support for archaeological survey, exploration, and excavation work through appropriate means.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　在建设工程、农业生产等活动中，任何单位或者个人发现文物或者疑似文物的，应当保护现场，立即报告当地文物行政部门；文物行政部门应当在接到报告后二十四小时内赶赴现场，并在七日内提出处理意见。文物行政部门应当采取措施保护现场，必要时可以通知公安机关或者海上执法机关协助；发现重要文物的，应当立即上报国务院文物行政部门，国务院文物行政部门应当在接到报告后十五日内提出处理意见。  
依照前款规定发现的文物属于国家所有，任何单位或者个人不得哄抢、私分、藏匿。

**Article 46** Where any unit or individual discovers cultural relics or suspected cultural relics during construction projects, agricultural production, or other activities, it or he/she shall protect the site and immediately report to the local administrative department for cultural heritage; the administrative department for cultural heritage shall arrive at the site within twenty-four hours after receiving the report and propose handling opinions within seven days. The administrative department for cultural heritage shall take measures to protect the site, and may, when necessary, notify the public security organ or the maritime law enforcement organ to assist; where important cultural relics are discovered, it shall immediately report to the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council, and the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council shall propose handling opinions within fifteen days after receiving the report.  
Cultural relics discovered in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall be owned by the State, and no unit or individual may grab, privately distribute, or conceal them.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　未经国务院文物行政部门报国务院特别许可，任何外国人、外国组织或者国际组织不得在中国境内进行考古调查、勘探、发掘。

**Article 47** Without special permission from the State Council reported by the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council, no foreigner, foreign organization, or international organization may conduct archaeological survey, exploration, or excavation within the territory of China.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　考古调查、勘探、发掘的结果，应当如实报告国务院文物行政部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门。  
考古发掘的文物，应当登记造册，妥善保管，按照国家有关规定及时移交给由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门或者国务院文物行政部门指定的国有博物馆、图书馆或者其他国有收藏文物的单位收藏。经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门批准，从事考古发掘的单位可以保留少量出土、出水文物作为科研标本。  
考古发掘的文物和考古发掘资料，任何单位或者个人不得侵占。

**Article 48** The results of archaeological survey, exploration, and excavation shall be truthfully reported to the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council and the administrative department for cultural heritage under the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
Cultural relics excavated from archaeological sites shall be registered, properly preserved, and timely transferred, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, to state-owned museums, libraries, or other state-owned units collecting cultural relics designated by the administrative department for cultural heritage under the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or by the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council. With the approval of the administrative department for cultural heritage under the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, units engaged in archaeological excavation may retain a small number of unearthed or underwater cultural relics as scientific research specimens.  
No unit or individual may encroach upon cultural relics excavated from archaeological sites or archaeological excavation data.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　根据保证文物安全、进行科学研究和充分发挥文物作用的需要，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门经本级人民政府批准，可以调用本行政区域内的出土、出水文物；国务院文物行政部门经国务院批准，可以调用全国的重要出土、出水文物。

**Article 49** Where necessary to ensure the safety of cultural relics, conduct scientific research, and give full play to the role of cultural relics, the administrative department for cultural heritage under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, with the approval of the people's government at the same level, transfer unearthed or underwater cultural relics within its administrative region; the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council may, with the approval of the State Council, transfer important unearthed or underwater cultural relics nationwide.

### 第四章　馆藏文物

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家鼓励和支持文物收藏单位收藏、保护可移动文物，开展文物展览展示、宣传教育和科学研究等活动。  
有关部门应当在设立条件、社会服务要求、财税扶持政策等方面，公平对待国有文物收藏单位和非国有文物收藏单位。

**Article 50** The State encourages and supports cultural relics collection units to collect and protect movable cultural relics, and to carry out activities such as cultural relics exhibitions, publicity and education, and scientific research.  
Relevant departments shall treat state-owned cultural relics collection units and non-state-owned cultural relics collection units equally in terms of establishment conditions, social service requirements, fiscal and tax support policies, etc.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　博物馆、图书馆和其他文物收藏单位对其收藏的文物（以下称馆藏文物），必须按照国家有关文物定级标准区分文物等级，设置档案，建立严格的管理制度，并报主管的文物行政部门备案。  
县级以上地方人民政府文物行政部门应当建立本行政区域内的馆藏文物档案；国务院文物行政部门应当建立全国馆藏一级文物档案和其主管的国有文物收藏单位馆藏文物档案。

**Article 51** Museums, libraries, and other cultural relics collection units shall, for the cultural relics in their collections (hereinafter referred to as museum collections), classify the cultural relics according to the relevant state standards for grading cultural relics, set up archives, establish strict management systems, and report them to the competent administrative department for cultural heritage for recordation.  
The administrative department for cultural heritage under the local people's government at or above the county level shall establish archives of museum collections within its administrative region; the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council shall establish national archives of Grade One museum collections and archives of museum collections of the state-owned cultural relics collection units under its administration.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　文物收藏单位可以通过下列方式取得文物：  
（一）购买；  
（二）接受捐赠；  
（三）依法交换；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他方式。  
国有文物收藏单位还可以通过文物行政部门指定收藏或者调拨方式取得文物。  
文物收藏单位应当依法履行合理注意义务，对拟征集、购买文物来源的合法性进行了解、识别。

**Article 52** A cultural relics collection unit may obtain cultural relics by the following means:  
(1) purchase;  
(2) acceptance of donations;  
(3) exchange according to law;  
(4) other means provided for by laws and administrative regulations.  
A state-owned cultural relics collection unit may also obtain cultural relics by means of designation by the administrative department for cultural heritage or transfer.  
A cultural relics collection unit shall perform its reasonable duty of care according to law, and shall ascertain and identify the legality of the sources of cultural relics to be solicited or purchased.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　文物收藏单位应当根据馆藏文物的保护需要，按照国家有关规定建立、健全管理制度，并报主管的文物行政部门备案。未经批准，任何单位或者个人不得调取馆藏文物。  
文物收藏单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人对馆藏文物的安全负责。文物收藏单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人离任时，应当按照馆藏文物档案办理馆藏文物移交手续。

**Article 53** A cultural relics collection unit shall, in accordance with the protection needs of its museum collections and the relevant provisions of the State, establish and improve a management system, and report it to the competent administrative department for cultural heritage for recordation. Without approval, no unit or individual may retrieve museum collections.  
The legal representative or the principal responsible person of a cultural relics collection unit shall be responsible for the safety of the museum collections. When the legal representative or the principal responsible person of a cultural relics collection unit leaves his/her post, he/she shall handle the transfer procedures for museum collections in accordance with the archives of the museum collections.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国务院文物行政部门可以调拨全国的国有馆藏文物。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门可以调拨本行政区域内其主管的国有文物收藏单位馆藏文物；调拨国有馆藏一级文物，应当报国务院文物行政部门备案。  
国有文物收藏单位可以申请调拨国有馆藏文物。

**Article 54** The administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council may transfer state-owned museum collections nationwide. The administrative department for cultural heritage under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may transfer museum collections of state-owned cultural relics collection units under its administration within its administrative region; the transfer of state-owned Grade One museum collections shall be reported to the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council for recordation.  
State-owned cultural relics collection units may apply for the transfer of state-owned museum collections.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　文物收藏单位应当改善服务条件，提高服务水平，充分发挥馆藏文物的作用，通过举办展览、科学研究、文化创意等活动，加强对中华民族优秀的历史文化和革命传统的宣传教育；通过借用、交换、在线展览等方式，提高馆藏文物利用效率。  
文物收藏单位应当为学校、科研机构开展有关教育教学、科学研究等活动提供支持和帮助。  
博物馆应当按照国家有关规定向公众开放，合理确定开放时间和接待人数并向社会公布，采用多种形式提供科学、准确、生动的文字说明和讲解服务。

**Article 55** Cultural relics collection units shall improve their service conditions and raise their service standards, give full play to the role of museum collections, and strengthen the publicity and education of the excellent historical culture and revolutionary traditions of the Chinese nation by organizing exhibitions, scientific research, cultural and creative activities, and other means; they shall improve the utilization efficiency of museum collections by means of borrowing, exchange, online exhibitions, etc.  
Cultural relics collection units shall provide support and assistance to schools and scientific research institutions in carrying out relevant education, teaching, scientific research, and other activities.  
Museums shall open to the public in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, reasonably determine the opening hours and the number of visitors, announce them to the public, and provide scientific, accurate, and vivid written descriptions and interpretation services in multiple forms.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国有文物收藏单位之间因举办展览、科学研究等需借用馆藏文物的，应当报主管的文物行政部门备案；借用馆藏一级文物的，应当同时报国务院文物行政部门备案。  
非国有文物收藏单位和其他单位举办展览需借用国有馆藏文物的，应当报主管的文物行政部门批准；借用国有馆藏一级文物的，应当经国务院文物行政部门批准。  
文物收藏单位之间借用文物的，应当签订借用协议，协议约定的期限不得超过三年。

**Article 56** Where a state-owned cultural relics collection unit needs to borrow museum collections from another state-owned cultural relics collection unit for the purpose of holding exhibitions, scientific research, etc., it shall report to the competent administrative department for cultural heritage for recordation; where Grade One museum collections are borrowed, it shall also report to the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council for recordation.  
Where a non-state-owned cultural relics collection unit or another unit needs to borrow state-owned museum collections for holding exhibitions, it shall obtain the approval of the competent administrative department for cultural heritage; where state-owned Grade One museum collections are borrowed, it shall obtain the approval of the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council.  
Where cultural relics are borrowed between cultural relics collection units, a loan agreement shall be signed, and the term agreed upon in the agreement shall not exceed three years.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　已经依照本法规定建立馆藏文物档案、管理制度的国有文物收藏单位之间可以交换馆藏文物；交换馆藏文物的，应当经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门批准，并报国务院文物行政部门备案。

**Article 57** State-owned cultural relics collection units that have established archives and management systems for museum collections in accordance with the provisions of this Law may exchange museum collections among themselves; the exchange of museum collections shall be subject to the approval of the administrative department for cultural heritage under the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall be reported to the administrative department for cultural heritage under the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　未依照本法规定建立馆藏文物档案、管理制度的国有文物收藏单位，不得依照[本法第五十五条](#art-55)至第五十七条的规定借用、交换其馆藏文物。

**Article 58** A state-owned cultural relics collection unit that has not established archives and management systems for museum collections in accordance with the provisions of this Law may not borrow or exchange its museum collections in accordance with the provisions of Articles 55 to 57 of this Law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　依法调拨、交换、借用馆藏文物，取得文物的文物收藏单位可以对提供文物的文物收藏单位给予合理补偿。  
文物收藏单位调拨、交换、出借文物所得的补偿费用，必须用于改善文物的收藏条件和收集新的文物，不得挪作他用；任何单位或者个人不得侵占。  
调拨、交换、借用的文物必须严格保管，不得丢失、损毁。

**Article 59** Where museum collections are transferred, exchanged, or borrowed according to law, the cultural relics collection unit that obtains the cultural relics may provide reasonable compensation to the cultural relics collection unit that provides the cultural relics.  
Compensation fees received by a cultural relics collection unit from the transfer, exchange, or loan of cultural relics must be used for improving the conditions for collecting cultural relics and collecting new cultural relics, and shall not be appropriated for other purposes; no unit or individual may encroach upon such fees.  
Cultural relics that are transferred, exchanged, or borrowed must be strictly kept and shall not be lost or damaged.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　禁止国有文物收藏单位将馆藏文物赠与、出租、出售或者抵押、质押给其他单位、个人。

**Article 60** It is prohibited for a state-owned cultural relics collection unit to donate, lease, sell, or mortgage or pledge its museum collections to any other unit or individual.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国有文物收藏单位不再收藏的文物退出馆藏的办法，由国务院文物行政部门制定并公布。

**Article 61** The measures for the withdrawal of cultural relics from the collection of state-owned cultural relics collection institutions that are no longer collected shall be formulated and published by the cultural relics administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　修复馆藏文物，不得改变馆藏文物的原状；复制、拍摄、拓印馆藏文物，不得对馆藏文物造成损害。修复、复制、拓印馆藏二级文物和馆藏三级文物的，应当报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门批准；修复、复制、拓印馆藏一级文物的，应当报国务院文物行政部门批准。  
不可移动文物的单体文物的修复、复制、拍摄、拓印，适用前款规定。

**Article 62** In restoring cultural relics in collection, the original condition of the cultural relics in collection shall not be altered; in reproducing, photographing, or making rubbings of cultural relics in collection, damage shall not be caused to the cultural relics in collection. The restoration, reproduction, or making of rubbings of Grade II and Grade III cultural relics in collection shall be reported to the cultural relics administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval; the restoration, reproduction, or making of rubbings of Grade I cultural relics in collection shall be reported to the cultural relics administrative department of the State Council for approval.  
The restoration, reproduction, photographing, or making of rubbings of individual cultural relics of immovable cultural relics shall be governed by the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　博物馆、图书馆和其他收藏文物的单位应当按照国家有关规定配备防火、防盗、防自然损坏的设施，并采取相应措施，确保收藏文物的安全。

**Article 63** Museums, libraries, and other units that collect cultural relics shall be equipped with facilities for fire prevention, theft prevention, and prevention of natural damage in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and shall take corresponding measures to ensure the safety of the cultural relics in collection.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　馆藏一级文物损毁的，应当报国务院文物行政部门核查处理。其他馆藏文物损毁的，应当报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门核查处理；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门应当将核查处理结果报国务院文物行政部门备案。  
馆藏文物被盗、被抢或者丢失的，文物收藏单位应当立即向公安机关报案，并同时向主管的文物行政部门报告。

**Article 64** If Grade I cultural relics in collection are damaged, the matter shall be reported to the cultural relics administrative department of the State Council for verification and handling. If other cultural relics in collection are damaged, the matter shall be reported to the cultural relics administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for verification and handling; the cultural relics administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall report the verification and handling results to the cultural relics administrative department of the State Council for recordation.  
If cultural relics in collection are stolen, robbed, or lost, the cultural relics collection institution shall immediately report to the public security organ and at the same time report to the competent cultural relics administrative department.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　文物行政部门和国有文物收藏单位的工作人员不得借用国有文物，不得非法侵占国有文物。

**Article 65** The staff members of the cultural relics administrative department and state-owned cultural relics collection institutions may not borrow state-owned cultural relics, and may not illegally occupy state-owned cultural relics.

### 第五章　民间收藏文物

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家鼓励公民、组织合法收藏，加强对民间收藏活动的指导、管理和服务。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/长城保护条例.md#art-25" title="被《长城保护条例》第25条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国文物保护法实施条例.md#art-57" title="被《中华人民共和国文物保护法实施条例》第57条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国文物保护法实施条例.md#art-62" title="被《中华人民共和国文物保护法实施条例》第62条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 66** The State encourages citizens and organizations to collect lawfully, and strengthens the guidance, management, and service for folk collection activities.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　文物收藏单位以外的公民、组织可以收藏通过下列方式取得的文物：  
（一）依法继承或者接受赠与；  
（二）从文物销售单位购买；  
（三）通过经营文物拍卖的拍卖企业（以下称文物拍卖企业）购买；  
（四）公民个人合法所有的文物相互交换或者依法转让；  
（五）国家规定的其他合法方式。  
文物收藏单位以外的公民、组织收藏的前款文物可以依法流通。

**Article 67** Citizens and organizations other than cultural relics collection institutions may collect cultural relics obtained through the following methods:  
(1) Succession according to law or acceptance of gifts;  
(2) Purchase from cultural relics sales units;  
(3) Purchase through auction enterprises that engage in auction of cultural relics (hereinafter referred to as cultural relics auction enterprises);  
(4) Exchange of cultural relics lawfully owned by individual citizens or transfer according to law;  
(5) Other lawful methods prescribed by the State.  
The cultural relics in the preceding paragraph collected by citizens and organizations other than cultural relics collection institutions may circulate according to law.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　禁止买卖下列文物：  
（一）国有文物，但是国家允许的除外；  
（二）国有不可移动文物中的壁画、雕塑、建筑构件等，但是依法拆除的国有不可移动文物中的壁画、雕塑、建筑构件等不属于[本法第三十一条](#art-31)第四款规定的应由文物收藏单位收藏的除外；  
（三）非国有馆藏珍贵文物；  
（四）国务院有关部门通报或者公告的被盗文物以及其他来源不符合[本法第六十七条](#art-67)规定的文物；  
（五）外国政府、相关国际组织按照有关国际公约通报或者公告的流失文物。

**Article 68** The sale of the following cultural relics is prohibited:  
(1) State-owned cultural relics, except those permitted by the State;  
(2) Murals, sculptures, building components, etc. in state-owned immovable cultural relics, except those murals, sculptures, building components, etc. in state-owned immovable cultural relics removed according to law that are not required to be collected by cultural relics collection institutions as provided in paragraph 4 of Article 31 of this Law;  
(3) Non-state-owned precious cultural relics in collection;  
(4) Stolen cultural relics notified or announced by the relevant departments of the State Council, and other cultural relics whose sources do not comply with the provisions of Article 67 of this Law;  
(5) Lost cultural relics notified or announced by foreign governments or relevant international organizations in accordance with relevant international conventions.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　国家鼓励文物收藏单位以外的公民、组织将其收藏的文物捐赠给文物收藏单位或者出借给文物收藏单位展览和研究。  
文物收藏单位应当尊重并按照捐赠人的意愿，对受赠的文物妥善收藏、保管和展示。  
国家禁止出境的文物，不得转让、出租、抵押、质押给境外组织或者个人。

**Article 69** The State encourages citizens and organizations other than cultural relics collection institutions to donate their collected cultural relics to cultural relics collection institutions or lend them to cultural relics collection institutions for exhibition and research.  
Cultural relics collection institutions shall respect and, in accordance with the wishes of the donors, properly collect, preserve, and display the donated cultural relics.  
Cultural relics that are prohibited by the State from being taken out of the country may not be transferred, leased, mortgaged, or pledged to overseas organizations or individuals.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　文物销售单位应当取得省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门颁发的文物销售许可证。  
文物销售单位不得从事文物拍卖经营活动，不得设立文物拍卖企业。

**Article 70** A cultural relics sales unit shall obtain a cultural relics sales license issued by the cultural relics administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
A cultural relics sales unit may not engage in cultural relics auction business activities, and may not establish a cultural relics auction enterprise.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　依法设立的拍卖企业经营文物拍卖的，应当取得省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门颁发的文物拍卖许可证。  
文物拍卖企业不得从事文物销售经营活动，不得设立文物销售单位。

**Article 71** If an auction enterprise established according to law engages in auction of cultural relics, it shall obtain a cultural relics auction license issued by the cultural relics administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
A cultural relics auction enterprise may not engage in cultural relics sales business activities, and may not establish a cultural relics sales unit.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　文物行政部门的工作人员不得举办或者参与举办文物销售单位或者文物拍卖企业。  
文物收藏单位及其工作人员不得举办或者参与举办文物销售单位或者文物拍卖企业。  
禁止设立外商投资的文物销售单位或者文物拍卖企业。  
除文物销售单位、文物拍卖企业外，其他单位或者个人不得从事文物商业经营活动。

**Article 72** The staff members of the cultural relics administrative department may not establish or participate in the establishment of cultural relics sales units or cultural relics auction enterprises.  
Cultural relics collection institutions and their staff members may not establish or participate in the establishment of cultural relics sales units or cultural relics auction enterprises.  
The establishment of cultural relics sales units or cultural relics auction enterprises with foreign investment is prohibited.  
Except for cultural relics sales units and cultural relics auction enterprises, no other unit or individual may engage in commercial activities involving cultural relics.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　文物销售单位不得销售、文物拍卖企业不得拍卖[本法第六十八条](#art-68)规定的文物。  
文物拍卖企业拍卖的文物，在拍卖前应当经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门依照前款规定进行审核，并报国务院文物行政部门备案。  
文物销售单位销售文物、文物拍卖企业拍卖文物，应当如实表述文物的相关信息，不得进行虚假宣传。

**Article 73** Cultural relics sales units may not sell, and cultural relics auction enterprises may not auction, the cultural relics specified in Article 68 of this Law.  
Cultural relics to be auctioned by a cultural relics auction enterprise shall, before auction, be examined by the cultural relics administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the preceding paragraph, and reported to the cultural relics administrative department of the State Council for recordation.  
When selling cultural relics, a cultural relics sales unit, or when auctioning cultural relics, a cultural relics auction enterprise, shall truthfully describe the relevant information of the cultural relics, and may not engage in false propaganda.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门应当建立文物购销、拍卖信息与信用管理系统，推动文物流通领域诚信建设。文物销售单位购买、销售文物，文物拍卖企业拍卖文物，应当按照国家有关规定作出记录，并于销售、拍卖文物后三十日内报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政部门备案。  
拍卖文物时，委托人、买受人要求对其身份保密的，文物行政部门应当为其保密；法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 74** The cultural relics administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall establish a cultural relics purchase and sale and auction information and credit management system to promote integrity building in the field of cultural relics circulation. When purchasing or selling cultural relics, a cultural relics sales unit, or when auctioning cultural relics, a cultural relics auction enterprise, shall make records in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and report to the cultural relics administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for recordation within thirty days after the sale or auction of the cultural relics.  
When auctioning cultural relics, if the client or the buyer requests confidentiality of their identity, the cultural relics administrative department shall maintain confidentiality for them, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　文物行政部门在审核拟拍卖的文物时，可以指定国有文物收藏单位优先购买其中的珍贵文物。购买价格由国有文物收藏单位的代表与文物的委托人协商确定。

**Article 75** When examining cultural relics proposed for auction, the cultural relics administrative department may designate a state-owned cultural relics collection institution to have priority in purchasing the precious cultural relics among them. The purchase price shall be determined through negotiation between the representative of the state-owned cultural relics collection institution and the client of the cultural relics.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　银行、冶炼厂、造纸厂以及废旧物资回收单位，应当与当地文物行政部门共同负责拣选掺杂在金银器和废旧物资中的文物。拣选文物除供银行研究所必需的历史货币可以由中国人民银行留用外，应当移交当地文物行政部门。移交拣选文物，应当给予合理补偿。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/长城保护条例.md#art-29" title="被《长城保护条例》第29条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 76** Banks, smelters, paper mills, and waste materials recycling units shall, together with the local cultural relics administrative department, be responsible for sorting out cultural relics mixed in gold and silver articles and waste materials. Except for historical currencies necessary for the research of banks which may be retained by the People's Bank of China, the sorted-out cultural relics shall be handed over to the local cultural relics administrative department. Reasonable compensation shall be given for the handover of sorted-out cultural relics.

### 第六章　文物出境进境

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　国有文物、非国有文物中的珍贵文物和国家禁止出境的其他文物，不得出境；依照本法规定出境展览，或者因特殊需要经国务院批准出境的除外。  
国家禁止出境的文物的具体范围，由国务院文物行政部门规定并公布。

**Article 77** State-owned cultural relics, precious cultural relics among non-state-owned cultural relics, and other cultural relics prohibited by the State from being taken out of the country shall not be taken out of the country, except for exhibition abroad in accordance with the provisions of this Law or when taken out of the country with the approval of the State Council due to special needs.  
The specific scope of cultural relics prohibited by the State from being taken out of the country shall be prescribed and published by the cultural relics administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　文物出境，应当经国务院文物行政部门指定的文物进出境审核机构审核。经审核允许出境的文物，由国务院文物行政部门颁发文物出境许可证，从国务院文物行政部门指定的口岸出境。  
任何单位或者个人运送、邮寄、携带文物出境，应当向海关申报；海关凭文物出境许可证放行。

**Article 78** Cultural relics taken out of the country shall be examined by the cultural relics entry-exit examination institution designated by the cultural relics administrative department of the State Council. Cultural relics permitted to be taken out of the country after examination shall be issued a cultural relics exit permit by the cultural relics administrative department of the State Council, and shall exit the country from the port designated by the cultural relics administrative department of the State Council.  
Any unit or individual that transports, sends by post, or carries cultural relics out of the country shall declare to the Customs; the Customs shall release them upon presentation of the cultural relics exit permit.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　文物出境展览，应当报国务院文物行政部门批准；一级文物超过国务院规定数量的，应当报国务院批准。  
一级文物中的孤品和易损品，禁止出境展览。  
出境展览的文物出境，由文物进出境审核机构审核、登记。海关凭国务院文物行政部门或者国务院的批准文件放行。出境展览的文物复进境，由原审核、登记的文物进出境审核机构审核查验。

**Article 79** The exhibition of cultural relics abroad shall be reported to the cultural relics administrative department of the State Council for approval; if the number of Grade I cultural relics exceeds the quantity prescribed by the State Council, it shall be reported to the State Council for approval.  
Unique items and fragile items among Grade I cultural relics are prohibited from being exhibited abroad.  
Cultural relics exhibited abroad shall be examined and registered by the cultural relics entry-exit examination institution when taken out of the country. The Customs shall release them upon presentation of the approval document of the cultural relics administrative department of the State Council or the State Council. When cultural relics exhibited abroad are brought back into the country, they shall be examined and checked by the original cultural relics entry-exit examination institution that examined and registered them.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　文物临时进境，应当向海关申报，并报文物进出境审核机构审核、登记。文物进出境审核机构发现临时进境的文物属于[本法第六十八条](#art-68)规定的文物的，应当向国务院文物行政部门报告并通报海关。  
临时进境的文物复出境，必须经原审核、登记的文物进出境审核机构审核查验；经审核查验无误的，由国务院文物行政部门颁发文物出境许可证，海关凭文物出境许可证放行。

**Article 80** Cultural relics temporarily brought into the country shall be declared to the Customs and reported to the cultural relics entry-exit examination institution for examination and registration. If the cultural relics entry-exit examination institution finds that the temporarily brought-in cultural relics belong to the cultural relics specified in Article 68 of this Law, it shall report to the cultural relics administrative department of the State Council and notify the Customs.  
When temporarily brought-in cultural relics are taken out of the country again, they must be examined and checked by the original cultural relics entry-exit examination institution that examined and registered them; if they are found to be correct after examination, the cultural relics administrative department of the State Council shall issue a cultural relics exit permit, and the Customs shall release them upon presentation of the cultural relics exit permit.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　国家加强文物追索返还领域的国际合作。国务院文物行政部门依法会同有关部门对因被盗、非法出境等流失境外的文物开展追索；对非法流入中国境内的外国文物，根据有关条约、协定、协议或者对等原则与相关国家开展返还合作。  
国家对于因被盗、非法出境等流失境外的文物，保留收回的权利，且该权利不受时效限制。

**Article 81** The State shall strengthen international cooperation in the field of recovery and return of cultural relics. The cultural relic administrative department of the State Council shall, in accordance with law, together with the relevant departments, carry out recovery of cultural relics lost overseas due to theft, illegal exit, or other reasons; and shall, in accordance with relevant treaties, agreements, protocols, or the principle of reciprocity, cooperate with relevant countries in the return of foreign cultural relics illegally entering China.  
The State reserves the right to recover cultural relics lost overseas due to theft, illegal exit, or other reasons, and such right shall not be subject to any statute of limitations.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　违反本法规定，地方各级人民政府和县级以上人民政府有关部门及其工作人员，以及其他依法履行公职的人员，滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 82** Where, in violation of this Law, local people's governments at all levels, the relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level and their staff members, or other persons who perform public duties in accordance with law, abuse their powers, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractice for personal gain, the responsible leaders and the directly liable persons shall be given sanctions in accordance with law.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府文物行政部门责令改正，给予警告；造成文物损坏或者其他严重后果的，对单位处五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款，对个人处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，责令承担相关文物修缮和复原费用，由原发证机关降低资质等级；情节严重的，对单位可以处五百万元以上一千万元以下的罚款，由原发证机关吊销资质证书：  
（一）擅自在文物保护单位的保护范围内进行文物保护工程以外的其他建设工程或者爆破、钻探、挖掘等作业；  
（二）工程设计方案未经文物行政部门同意，擅自在文物保护单位的建设控制地带内进行建设工程；  
（三）未制定不可移动文物原址保护措施，或者不可移动文物原址保护措施未经文物行政部门批准，擅自开工建设；  
（四）擅自迁移、拆除不可移动文物；  
（五）擅自修缮不可移动文物，明显改变文物原状；  
（六）擅自在原址重建已经全部毁坏的不可移动文物；  
（七）未取得文物保护工程资质证书，擅自从事文物修缮、迁移、重建；  
（八）进行大型基本建设工程，或者在文物保护单位的保护范围、建设控制地带内进行建设工程，未依法进行考古调查、勘探。  
损毁依照本法规定设立的不可移动文物保护标志的，由县级以上人民政府文物行政部门给予警告，可以并处五百元以下的罚款。

**Article 83** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the cultural relic administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level to make corrections and shall be given a warning; if damage to cultural relics or other serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan shall be imposed on the entity, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual, the entity or individual shall be ordered to bear the costs of repairing and restoring the relevant cultural relics, and the original issuing authority shall downgrade the qualification grade; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 5,000,000 yuan but not more than 10,000,000 yuan may be imposed on the entity, and the original issuing authority shall revoke the qualification certificate:  
(1) carrying out, without authorization, any construction project other than a cultural relic protection project, or any operation such as blasting, drilling, or excavation, within the protection area of a cultural relic protection site;  
(2) carrying out a construction project, without authorization, within the construction control zone of a cultural relic protection site, without the consent of the cultural relic administrative department on the project design plan;  
(3) commencing construction without formulating measures for on-site protection of an immovable cultural relic, or without the approval of the cultural relic administrative department for the measures for on-site protection of an immovable cultural relic;  
(4) relocating or demolishing an immovable cultural relic without authorization;  
(5) repairing an immovable cultural relic without authorization, thereby significantly altering its original state;  
(6) rebuilding, without authorization, an immovable cultural relic that has been completely destroyed on its original site;  
(7) engaging in the repair, relocation, or reconstruction of cultural relics without obtaining a qualification certificate for cultural relic protection projects;  
(8) undertaking a large-scale capital construction project or a construction project within the protection area or construction control zone of a cultural relic protection site without conducting an archaeological survey or exploration in accordance with law.  
Whoever damages a protection sign for an immovable cultural relic established in accordance with this Law shall be given a warning by the cultural relic administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level, and may also be fined not more than 500 yuan.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　在文物保护单位的保护范围或者建设控制地带内建设污染文物保护单位及其环境的设施的，由生态环境主管部门依法给予处罚。

**Article 84** Where facilities that pollute a cultural relic protection site or its environment are constructed within the protection area or construction control zone of the cultural relic protection site, the ecological and environmental competent department shall impose a penalty in accordance with law.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府文物行政部门责令改正，给予警告或者通报批评，没收违法所得；违法所得五千元以上的，并处违法所得二倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五千元的，并处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款：  
（一）转让或者抵押国有不可移动文物；  
（二）将建立博物馆、文物保管所或者辟为参观游览场所的国有不可移动文物改作企业资产经营，或者将其管理机构改由企业管理；  
（三）将非国有不可移动文物转让或者抵押给外国人、外国组织或者国际组织；  
（四）擅自改变国有文物保护单位中的纪念建筑物或者古建筑的用途。

**Article 85** Whoever, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the cultural relic administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level to make corrections, shall be given a warning or a notice of criticism, and shall have the illegal gains confiscated; if the illegal gains are not less than 5,000 yuan, a fine of not less than two times but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 5,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall also be imposed:  
(1) transferring or mortgaging a State-owned immovable cultural relic;  
(2) converting a State-owned immovable cultural relic that has been established as a museum, cultural relic repository, or visiting site into an enterprise asset for operation, or converting its management institution into an enterprise-managed one;  
(3) transferring or mortgaging a non-State-owned immovable cultural relic to a foreigner, a foreign organization, or an international organization;  
(4) altering, without authorization, the use of a memorial building or an ancient building within a State-owned cultural relic protection site.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　历史文化名城的布局、环境、历史风貌等遭到严重破坏的，由国务院撤销其历史文化名城称号；历史文化街区、村镇的布局、环境、历史风貌等遭到严重破坏的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府撤销其历史文化街区、村镇称号；对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 86** Where the layout, environment, historical features, etc., of a famous historical and cultural city have been seriously damaged, the State Council shall revoke its title as a famous historical and cultural city; where the layout, environment, historical features, etc., of a famous historical and cultural district, town, or village have been seriously damaged, the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall revoke its title as a famous historical and cultural district, town, or village; and the responsible leaders and the directly liable persons shall be given sanctions in accordance with law.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府文物行政部门责令改正，给予警告或者通报批评，没收违法所得；违法所得五千元以上的，并处违法所得二倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五千元的，可以并处五万元以下的罚款：  
（一）文物收藏单位未按照国家有关规定配备防火、防盗、防自然损坏的设施；  
（二）文物收藏单位法定代表人或者主要负责人离任时未按照馆藏文物档案移交馆藏文物，或者所移交的馆藏文物与馆藏文物档案不符；  
（三）国有文物收藏单位将馆藏文物赠与、出租、出售或者抵押、质押给其他单位、个人；  
（四）违反本法规定借用、交换馆藏文物；  
（五）挪用或者侵占依法调拨、交换、出借文物所得的补偿费用。

**Article 87** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the cultural relic administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level to make corrections, shall be given a warning or a notice of criticism, and shall have the illegal gains confiscated; if the illegal gains are not less than 5,000 yuan, a fine of not less than two times but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 5,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may also be imposed:  
(1) a cultural relic collection entity fails to install facilities for fire prevention, theft prevention, and prevention of natural damage in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(2) the legal representative or principal responsible person of a cultural relic collection entity fails to hand over the cultural relics in the collection according to the records of the collection, or the handover does not match the records;  
(3) a State-owned cultural relic collection entity donates, leases, sells, mortgages, or pledges cultural relics in its collection to another entity or individual;  
(4) borrowing or exchanging cultural relics in a collection in violation of this Law;  
(5) misappropriating or encroaching upon the compensation fees obtained from the allocation, exchange, or lending of cultural relics in accordance with law.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　买卖国家禁止买卖的文物或者将国家禁止出境的文物转让、出租、抵押、质押给境外组织或者个人的，由县级以上人民政府文物行政部门责令改正，没收违法所得、非法经营的文物；违法经营额五千元以上的，并处违法经营额二倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法经营额或者违法经营额不足五千元的，并处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款。  
文物销售单位、文物拍卖企业有前款规定的违法行为的，由县级以上人民政府文物行政部门没收违法所得、非法经营的文物；违法经营额三万元以上的，并处违法经营额二倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法经营额或者违法经营额不足三万元的，并处五万元以上二十五万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原发证机关吊销许可证书。

**Article 88** Whoever buys or sells cultural relics the trading of which is prohibited by the State, or transfers, leases, mortgages, or pledges cultural relics the export of which is prohibited by the State to an overseas organization or individual, shall be ordered by the cultural relic administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level to make corrections, and the illegal gains and the cultural relics involved in the illegal transaction shall be confiscated; if the illegal turnover is not less than 5,000 yuan, a fine of not less than two times but not more than ten times the illegal turnover shall also be imposed; if there is no illegal turnover or the illegal turnover is less than 5,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall also be imposed.  
Where a cultural relic sales entity or a cultural relic auction enterprise commits the illegal act specified in the preceding paragraph, the cultural relic administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the illegal gains and the cultural relics involved in the illegal transaction; if the illegal turnover is not less than 30,000 yuan, a fine of not less than two times but not more than ten times the illegal turnover shall also be imposed; if there is no illegal turnover or the illegal turnover is less than 30,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 250,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the original issuing authority shall revoke the license certificate.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　未经许可擅自从事文物商业经营活动的，由县级以上人民政府文物行政部门责令改正，给予警告或者通报批评，没收违法所得、非法经营的文物；违法经营额三万元以上的，并处违法经营额二倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法经营额或者违法经营额不足三万元的，并处五万元以上二十五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 89** Whoever engages in commercial cultural relic business activities without a license shall be ordered by the cultural relic administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level to make corrections, shall be given a warning or a notice of criticism, and the illegal gains and the cultural relics involved in the illegal transaction shall be confiscated; if the illegal turnover is not less than 30,000 yuan, a fine of not less than two times but not more than ten times the illegal turnover shall also be imposed; if there is no illegal turnover or the illegal turnover is less than 30,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 250,000 yuan shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府文物行政部门责令改正，给予警告或者通报批评，没收违法所得、非法经营的文物；违法经营额三万元以上的，并处违法经营额二倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法经营额或者违法经营额不足三万元的，并处五万元以上二十五万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原发证机关吊销许可证书：  
（一）文物销售单位从事文物拍卖经营活动；  
（二）文物拍卖企业从事文物销售经营活动；  
（三）文物拍卖企业拍卖的文物，未经审核；  
（四）文物收藏单位从事文物商业经营活动；  
（五）文物销售单位、文物拍卖企业知假售假、知假拍假或者进行虚假宣传。

**Article 90** Under any of the following circumstances, the cultural relic administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level shall order corrections, give a warning or a notice of criticism, and confiscate the illegal gains and the cultural relics involved in the illegal transaction; if the illegal turnover is not less than 30,000 yuan, a fine of not less than two times but not more than ten times the illegal turnover shall also be imposed; if there is no illegal turnover or the illegal turnover is less than 30,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 250,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the original issuing authority shall revoke the license certificate:  
(1) a cultural relic sales entity engages in cultural relic auction business activities;  
(2) a cultural relic auction enterprise engages in cultural relic sales business activities;  
(3) the cultural relics auctioned by a cultural relic auction enterprise have not been examined;  
(4) a cultural relic collection entity engages in commercial cultural relic business activities;  
(5) a cultural relic sales entity or a cultural relic auction enterprise knowingly sells or auctions counterfeit cultural relics, or engages in false publicity.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府文物行政部门会同公安机关、海上执法机关追缴文物，给予警告；情节严重的，对单位处十万元以上三百万元以下的罚款，对个人处五千元以上五万元以下的罚款：  
（一）发现文物隐匿不报或者拒不上交；  
（二）未按照规定移交拣选文物。

**Article 91** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the cultural relic administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level, together with the public security organ and the maritime enforcement authority, to recover the cultural relics, and shall be given a warning; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 3,000,000 yuan shall be imposed on the entity, and a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual:  
(1) concealing the discovery of cultural relics without reporting or refusing to hand them over;  
(2) failing to hand over sorted cultural relics in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　文物进出境未依照本法规定申报的，由海关或者海上执法机关依法给予处罚。

**Article 92** Where the import or export of cultural relics is not declared in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the Customs or the maritime enforcement authority shall impose a penalty in accordance with law.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府文物行政部门责令改正；情节严重的，对单位处十万元以上三百万元以下的罚款，限制业务活动或者由原发证机关吊销许可证书，对个人处五千元以上五万元以下的罚款：  
（一）改变国有未定级不可移动文物的用途，未依照本法规定报告；  
（二）转让、抵押非国有不可移动文物或者改变其用途，未依照本法规定备案；  
（三）国有不可移动文物的使用人具备修缮能力但拒不依法履行修缮义务；  
（四）从事考古发掘的单位未经批准擅自进行考古发掘，或者不如实报告考古调查、勘探、发掘结果，或者未按照规定移交考古发掘的文物；  
（五）文物收藏单位未按照国家有关规定建立馆藏文物档案、管理制度，或者未将馆藏文物档案、管理制度备案；  
（六）未经批准擅自调取馆藏文物；  
（七）未经批准擅自修复、复制、拓印文物；  
（八）馆藏文物损毁未报文物行政部门核查处理，或者馆藏文物被盗、被抢或者丢失，文物收藏单位未及时向公安机关或者文物行政部门报告；  
（九）文物销售单位销售文物或者文物拍卖企业拍卖文物，未按照国家有关规定作出记录或者未将所作记录报文物行政部门备案。

**Article 93** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the cultural relic administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 3,000,000 yuan shall be imposed on the entity, its business activities shall be restricted, or the original issuing authority shall revoke its license certificate, and a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual:  
(1) altering the use of a State-owned immovable cultural relic that has not been graded, without reporting in accordance with this Law;  
(2) transferring or mortgaging a non-State-owned immovable cultural relic or altering its use, without filing for the record in accordance with this Law;  
(3) the user of a State-owned immovable cultural relic, being capable of making repairs, refuses to perform the repair obligation in accordance with law;  
(4) a unit engaged in archaeological excavations conducts archaeological excavations without approval, or fails to truthfully report the results of archaeological surveys, explorations, or excavations, or fails to hand over the cultural relics unearthed from archaeological excavations in accordance with the provisions;  
(5) a cultural relic collection entity fails to establish records and management systems for its collection in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, or fails to file the records and management systems for the record;  
(6) removing cultural relics from a collection without approval;  
(7) repairing, reproducing, or making rubbings of cultural relics without approval;  
(8) failing to report to the cultural relic administrative department for verification and disposal when cultural relics in a collection are damaged, or failing to promptly report to the public security organ or the cultural relic administrative department when cultural relics in a collection are stolen, robbed, or lost;  
(9) a cultural relic sales entity selling cultural relics or a cultural relic auction enterprise auctioning cultural relics fails to make records in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State or fails to report the records to the cultural relic administrative department for the record.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　文物行政部门、文物收藏单位、文物销售单位、文物拍卖企业的工作人员，有下列行为之一的，依法给予处分；情节严重的，依法开除公职或者吊销其从业资格证书：  
（一）文物行政部门和国有文物收藏单位的工作人员借用或者非法侵占国有文物；  
（二）文物行政部门、文物收藏单位的工作人员举办或者参与举办文物销售单位或者文物拍卖企业；  
（三）因不负责任造成文物保护单位、珍贵文物损毁或者流失；  
（四）贪污、挪用文物保护经费。  
前款被开除公职或者被吊销从业资格证书的人员，自被开除公职或者被吊销从业资格证书之日起十年内不得担任文物管理人员或者从事文物经营活动。

**Article 94** Any staff member of a cultural relic administrative department, a cultural relic collection entity, a cultural relic sales entity, or a cultural relic auction enterprise who commits any of the following acts shall be given a sanction in accordance with law; if the circumstances are serious, the person shall be discharged from public employment or have their professional qualification certificate revoked in accordance with law:  
(1) a staff member of a cultural relic administrative department or a State-owned cultural relic collection entity borrows or illegally takes possession of a State-owned cultural relic;  
(2) a staff member of a cultural relic administrative department or a cultural relic collection entity establishes or participates in the establishment of a cultural relic sales entity or a cultural relic auction enterprise;  
(3) causing damage to or loss of a cultural relic protection site or a valuable cultural relic due to irresponsibility;  
(4) embezzling or misappropriating funds for cultural relic protection.  
A person who has been discharged from public employment or whose professional qualification certificate has been revoked as specified in the preceding paragraph shall not serve as a cultural relic manager or engage in cultural relic business activities within ten years from the date of the discharge or revocation.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　单位违反本法规定受到行政处罚，情节严重的，对单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五千元以上五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 95** Where an entity violates the provisions of this Law and is subject to an administrative penalty, if the circumstances are serious, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons of the entity shall be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　违反本法规定，损害他人民事权益的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 96** Whoever, in violation of this Law, harms the civil rights and interests of another person, shall bear civil liability in accordance with law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty in accordance with law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　县级以上人民政府文物行政部门依法实施监督检查，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）进入现场进行检查；  
（二）查阅、复制有关文件资料，询问有关人员，对可能被转移、销毁或者篡改的文件资料予以封存；  
（三）查封、扣押涉嫌违法活动的场所、设施或者财物；  
（四）责令行为人停止侵害文物的行为。

**Article 97** When conducting supervision and inspection in accordance with law, the cultural relic administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level may take the following measures:  
(1) entering a site to conduct inspections;  
(2) consulting and copying relevant documents and materials, questioning relevant persons, and sealing up documents and materials that may be transferred, destroyed, or tampered with;  
(3) sealing up or seizing premises, facilities, or property suspected of being used in illegal activities;  
(4) ordering the actor to cease conduct that infringes upon cultural relics.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　监察委员会、人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关、海关、市场监督管理部门和海上执法机关依法没收的文物应当登记造册，妥善保管，结案后无偿移交文物行政部门，由文物行政部门指定的国有文物收藏单位收藏。

**Article 98** Cultural relics confiscated in accordance with law by a supervisory commission, a People's Court, a People's Procuratorate, a public security organ, the Customs, a market supervision and administration department, or a maritime enforcement authority shall be registered, inventoried, and properly kept, and shall be transferred without compensation to the cultural relic administrative department after the case is closed, for collection by a State-owned cultural relic collection entity designated by the cultural relic administrative department.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　因违反本法规定造成文物严重损害或者存在严重损害风险，致使社会公共利益受到侵害的，人民检察院可以依照有关诉讼法的规定提起公益诉讼。

**Article 99** Where a violation of this Law causes serious damage to cultural relics or creates a serious risk of such damage, resulting in harm to the public interest, a People's Procuratorate may initiate a public interest lawsuit in accordance with the provisions of the relevant procedural laws.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　文物保护有关行政许可的条件、期限等，本法未作规定的，适用《中华人民共和国行政许可法》和有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 100** Where conditions, time limits, or other matters concerning administrative licenses related to cultural relic protection are not provided for in this Law, the provisions of the Administrative License Law of the People's Republic of China and the relevant laws and administrative regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　本法自2025年3月1日起施行。

**Article 101** This Law shall come into force as of March 1, 2025.
