# 中华人民共和国反家庭暴力法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2015-12-27  
**生效日期**：2016-03-01

> （2015年12月27日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十八次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　家庭暴力的预防 第三章　家庭暴力的处置 第四章　人身安全保护令 第五章　法律责任 第六章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了预防和制止家庭暴力，保护家庭成员的合法权益，维护平等、和睦、文明的家庭关系，促进家庭和谐、社会稳定，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称家庭暴力，是指家庭成员之间以殴打、捆绑、残害、限制人身自由以及经常性谩骂、恐吓等方式实施的身体、精神等侵害行为。 第三条　家庭成员之间应当互相帮助，互相关爱，和睦相处，履行家庭义务。 反家庭暴力是国家、社会和每个家庭的共同责任。 国家禁止任何形式的家庭暴力。 第四条　县级以上人民政府负责妇女儿童工作的机构，负责组织、协调、指导、督促有关部门做好反家庭暴力工作。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了预防和制止家庭暴力，保护家庭成员的合法权益，维护平等、和睦、文明的家庭关系，促进家庭和谐、社会稳定，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of preventing and stopping domestic violence, protecting the lawful rights and interests of family members, maintaining equal, harmonious and civilized family relations, and promoting family harmony and social stability.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称家庭暴力，是指家庭成员之间以殴打、捆绑、残害、限制人身自由以及经常性谩骂、恐吓等方式实施的身体、精神等侵害行为。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于办理人身安全保护令案件适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-3" title="被《最高人民法院关于办理人身安全保护令案件适用法律若干问题的规定》第3条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 2** Domestic violence as mentioned in this Law means physical, psychological or other infringements committed among family members by means of beating, binding, mutilation, restricting personal freedom, as well as frequent verbal abuse and intimidation, etc.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　家庭成员之间应当互相帮助，互相关爱，和睦相处，履行家庭义务。  
反家庭暴力是国家、社会和每个家庭的共同责任。  
国家禁止任何形式的家庭暴力。

**Article 3** Family members shall help each other, care for each other, live in harmony, and fulfill family obligations.  
Combating domestic violence is a common responsibility of the State, society and every family.  
The State prohibits any form of domestic violence.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府负责妇女儿童工作的机构，负责组织、协调、指导、督促有关部门做好反家庭暴力工作。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门、司法机关、人民团体、社会组织、居民委员会、村民委员会、企业事业单位，应当依照本法和有关法律规定，做好反家庭暴力工作。  
各级人民政府应当对反家庭暴力工作给予必要的经费保障。

**Article 4** The organs of the people's governments at or above the county level responsible for women and children affairs shall be responsible for organizing, coordinating, guiding and supervising the relevant departments to properly carry out anti-domestic violence work.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, judicial organs, people's organizations, social organizations, residents' committees, villagers' committees, enterprises and institutions shall, in accordance with this Law and the provisions of relevant laws, properly carry out anti-domestic violence work.  
People's governments at all levels shall provide necessary funding guarantees for anti-domestic violence work.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　反家庭暴力工作遵循预防为主，教育、矫治与惩处相结合原则。  
反家庭暴力工作应当尊重受害人真实意愿，保护当事人隐私。  
未成年人、老年人、残疾人、孕期和哺乳期的妇女、重病患者遭受家庭暴力的，应当给予特殊保护。

**Article 5** Anti-domestic violence work shall follow the principle of prevention first, combining education, correction and punishment.  
Anti-domestic violence work shall respect the true wishes of victims and protect the privacy of the parties.  
Special protection shall be given to minors, the elderly, the disabled, pregnant and lactating women, and patients with serious diseases who suffer domestic violence.

### 第二章　家庭暴力的预防

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家开展家庭美德宣传教育，普及反家庭暴力知识，增强公民反家庭暴力意识。  
工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会、残疾人联合会应当在各自工作范围内，组织开展家庭美德和反家庭暴力宣传教育。  
广播、电视、报刊、网络等应当开展家庭美德和反家庭暴力宣传。  
学校、幼儿园应当开展家庭美德和反家庭暴力教育。

**Article 6** The State conducts publicity and education on family virtues, popularizes anti-domestic violence knowledge, and enhances citizens' awareness of anti-domestic violence.  
Trade unions, the Communist Youth League, women's federations, and disabled persons' federations shall, within their respective work scopes, organize and carry out publicity and education on family virtues and anti-domestic violence.  
Radio, television, newspapers, the internet, etc. shall carry out publicity on family virtues and anti-domestic violence.  
Schools and kindergartens shall carry out education on family virtues and anti-domestic violence.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上人民政府有关部门、司法机关、妇女联合会应当将预防和制止家庭暴力纳入业务培训和统计工作。  
医疗机构应当做好家庭暴力受害人的诊疗记录。

**Article 7** The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, judicial organs, and women's federations shall incorporate the prevention and stopping of domestic violence into professional training and statistical work.  
Medical institutions shall properly keep diagnosis and treatment records of domestic violence victims.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　乡镇人民政府、街道办事处应当组织开展家庭暴力预防工作，居民委员会、村民委员会、社会工作服务机构应当予以配合协助。

**Article 8** Township people's governments and sub-district offices shall organize and carry out domestic violence prevention work; residents' committees, villagers' committees, and social work service institutions shall cooperate and assist.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各级人民政府应当支持社会工作服务机构等社会组织开展心理健康咨询、家庭关系指导、家庭暴力预防知识教育等服务。

**Article 9** People's governments at all levels shall support social organizations such as social work service institutions in providing services including psychological health counseling, family relationship guidance, and education on domestic violence prevention knowledge.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民调解组织应当依法调解家庭纠纷，预防和减少家庭暴力的发生。

**Article 10** People's mediation organizations shall, according to law, mediate family disputes to prevent and reduce the occurrence of domestic violence.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　用人单位发现本单位人员有家庭暴力情况的，应当给予批评教育，并做好家庭矛盾的调解、化解工作。

**Article 11** Where an employer discovers that a person of its unit commits domestic violence, it shall give criticism and education, and properly mediate and resolve family conflicts.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　未成年人的监护人应当以文明的方式进行家庭教育，依法履行监护和教育职责，不得实施家庭暴力。

**Article 12** Guardians of minors shall conduct family education in a civilized manner, perform guardianship and education duties according to law, and shall not commit domestic violence.

### 第三章　家庭暴力的处置

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　家庭暴力受害人及其法定代理人、近亲属可以向加害人或者受害人所在单位、居民委员会、村民委员会、妇女联合会等单位投诉、反映或者求助。有关单位接到家庭暴力投诉、反映或者求助后，应当给予帮助、处理。  
家庭暴力受害人及其法定代理人、近亲属也可以向公安机关报案或者依法向人民法院起诉。  
单位、个人发现正在发生的家庭暴力行为，有权及时劝阻。

**Article 13** A victim of domestic violence and his or her legal representative or close relative may make a complaint, report or seek help from the unit where the perpetrator or victim works, the residents' committee, the villagers' committee, the women's federation, and other units. The relevant unit shall, after receiving a domestic violence complaint, report or request for help, provide assistance and handle the matter.  
A victim of domestic violence and his or her legal representative or close relative may also report the case to a public security organ or bring a prosecution in a People's Court according to law.  
Any unit or individual that discovers an ongoing act of domestic violence shall have the right to promptly dissuade it.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　学校、幼儿园、医疗机构、居民委员会、村民委员会、社会工作服务机构、救助管理机构、福利机构及其工作人员在工作中发现无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人遭受或者疑似遭受家庭暴力的，应当及时向公安机关报案。公安机关应当对报案人的信息予以保密。

**Article 14** Where schools, kindergartens, medical institutions, residents' committees, villagers' committees, social work service institutions, rescue management institutions, welfare institutions and their staff discover in their work that a person with no capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct is suffering or is suspected to be suffering domestic violence, they shall promptly report to the public security organ. The public security organ shall keep the reporter's information confidential.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　公安机关接到家庭暴力报案后应当及时出警，制止家庭暴力，按照有关规定调查取证，协助受害人就医、鉴定伤情。  
无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人因家庭暴力身体受到严重伤害、面临人身安全威胁或者处于无人照料等危险状态的，公安机关应当通知并协助民政部门将其安置到临时庇护场所、救助管理机构或者福利机构。

**Article 15** After receiving a domestic violence report, the public security organ shall promptly dispatch police to stop the domestic violence, investigate and collect evidence in accordance with relevant regulations, and assist the victim in seeking medical treatment and having the injury assessed.  
Where a person with no capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct suffers serious bodily injury due to domestic violence, faces a threat to personal safety, or is in a dangerous state of being left unattended, the public security organ shall notify and assist the civil affairs department in placing him or her in a temporary shelter, rescue management institution or welfare institution.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　家庭暴力情节较轻，依法不给予治安管理处罚的，由公安机关对加害人给予批评教育或者出具告诫书。  
告诫书应当包括加害人的身份信息、家庭暴力的事实陈述、禁止加害人实施家庭暴力等内容。

**Article 16** Where domestic violence is minor and no public security punishment is imposed according to law, the public security organ shall give criticism and education to the perpetrator or issue a warning letter.  
The warning letter shall contain the perpetrator's identity information, a factual statement of the domestic violence, and a prohibition on the perpetrator from committing domestic violence.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公安机关应当将告诫书送交加害人、受害人，并通知居民委员会、村民委员会。  
居民委员会、村民委员会、公安派出所应当对收到告诫书的加害人、受害人进行查访，监督加害人不再实施家庭暴力。

**Article 17** The public security organ shall deliver the warning letter to the perpetrator and the victim, and notify the residents' committee and the villagers' committee.  
The residents' committee, the villagers' committee, and the public security police station shall make follow-up visits to the perpetrator and the victim who have received the warning letter, and supervise the perpetrator to ensure that he or she no longer commits domestic violence.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级或者设区的市级人民政府可以单独或者依托救助管理机构设立临时庇护场所，为家庭暴力受害人提供临时生活帮助。

**Article 18** People's governments at the county level or at the level of a city divided into districts may, independently or relying on rescue management institutions, set up temporary shelter places to provide temporary living assistance to victims of domestic violence.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　法律援助机构应当依法为家庭暴力受害人提供法律援助。  
人民法院应当依法对家庭暴力受害人缓收、减收或者免收诉讼费用。

**Article 19** Legal aid institutions shall, according to law, provide legal aid to victims of domestic violence.  
People's Courts shall, according to law, defer, reduce or exempt litigation costs for victims of domestic violence.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　人民法院审理涉及家庭暴力的案件，可以根据公安机关出警记录、告诫书、伤情鉴定意见等证据，认定家庭暴力事实。

**Article 20** When trying a case involving domestic violence, a People's Court may, based on evidence such as the public security organ's dispatch records, warning letters, and injury assessment opinions, determine the facts of domestic violence.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　监护人实施家庭暴力严重侵害被监护人合法权益的，人民法院可以根据被监护人的近亲属、居民委员会、村民委员会、县级人民政府民政部门等有关人员或者单位的申请，依法撤销其监护人资格，另行指定监护人。  
被撤销监护人资格的加害人，应当继续负担相应的赡养、扶养、抚养费用。

**Article 21** Where a guardian commits domestic violence that seriously infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of the ward, the People's Court may, upon application by the ward's close relatives, residents' committee, villagers' committee, the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level, or other relevant persons or units, revoke the guardian's qualification according to law and appoint a new guardian.  
The perpetrator whose guardian qualification has been revoked shall continue to bear the corresponding expenses for support, maintenance and upbringing.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会、残疾人联合会、居民委员会、村民委员会等应当对实施家庭暴力的加害人进行法治教育，必要时可以对加害人、受害人进行心理辅导。

**Article 22** Trade unions, the Communist Youth League, women's federations, disabled persons' federations, residents' committees, villagers' committees, etc. shall conduct legal education for perpetrators of domestic violence, and may, when necessary, provide psychological counseling to both the perpetrator and the victim.

### 第四章　人身安全保护令

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　当事人因遭受家庭暴力或者面临家庭暴力的现实危险，向人民法院申请人身安全保护令的，人民法院应当受理。  
当事人是无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人，或者因受到强制、威吓等原因无法申请人身安全保护令的，其近亲属、公安机关、妇女联合会、居民委员会、村民委员会、救助管理机构可以代为申请。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于办理人身安全保护令案件适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院关于办理人身安全保护令案件适用法律若干问题的规定》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 23** Where a party applies to a People's Court for a personal safety protection order due to suffering domestic violence or facing a real threat of domestic violence, the People's Court shall accept the application.  
Where the party is a person with no capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct, or is unable to apply for a personal safety protection order due to coercion, intimidation or other reasons, his or her close relatives, the public security organ, the women's federation, the residents' committee, the villagers' committee, or the rescue management institution may apply on his or her behalf.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　申请人身安全保护令应当以书面方式提出；书面申请确有困难的，可以口头申请，由人民法院记入笔录。

**Article 24** An application for a personal safety protection order shall be made in writing; if it is truly difficult to make a written application, an oral application may be made, and the People's Court shall record it in the case file.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　人身安全保护令案件由申请人或者被申请人居住地、家庭暴力发生地的基层人民法院管辖。

**Article 25** A case for a personal safety protection order shall be under the jurisdiction of the Primary People's Court of the place where the applicant or the respondent resides or where the domestic violence occurs.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　人身安全保护令由人民法院以裁定形式作出。

**Article 26** A personal safety protection order shall be made by a People's Court in the form of a ruling.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　作出人身安全保护令，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有明确的被申请人；  
（二）有具体的请求；  
（三）有遭受家庭暴力或者面临家庭暴力现实危险的情形。

**Article 27** To issue a personal safety protection order, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) There is a definite respondent;  
(2) There are specific requests;  
(3) There are circumstances of having suffered domestic violence or facing a real threat of domestic violence.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　人民法院受理申请后，应当在七十二小时内作出人身安全保护令或者驳回申请；情况紧急的，应当在二十四小时内作出。

**Article 28** After accepting an application, the People's Court shall make a personal safety protection order or reject the application within seventy-two hours; in an emergency, it shall do so within twenty-four hours.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　人身安全保护令可以包括下列措施：  
（一）禁止被申请人实施家庭暴力；  
（二）禁止被申请人骚扰、跟踪、接触申请人及其相关近亲属；  
（三）责令被申请人迁出申请人住所；  
（四）保护申请人人身安全的其他措施。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于办理人身安全保护令案件适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-10" title="被《最高人民法院关于办理人身安全保护令案件适用法律若干问题的规定》第10条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 29** A personal safety protection order may include the following measures:  
(1) Prohibiting the respondent from committing domestic violence;  
(2) Prohibiting the respondent from harassing, stalking, or contacting the applicant and his or her close relatives;  
(3) Ordering the respondent to move out of the applicant's residence;  
(4) Other measures to protect the applicant's personal safety.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　人身安全保护令的有效期不超过六个月，自作出之日起生效。人身安全保护令失效前，人民法院可以根据申请人的申请撤销、变更或者延长。

**Article 30** The validity period of a personal safety protection order shall not exceed six months and shall take effect from the date it is made. Before the personal safety protection order ceases to have effect, the People's Court may, upon application by the applicant, revoke, modify or extend it.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　申请人对驳回申请不服或者被申请人对人身安全保护令不服的，可以自裁定生效之日起五日内向作出裁定的人民法院申请复议一次。人民法院依法作出人身安全保护令的，复议期间不停止人身安全保护令的执行。

**Article 31** Where an applicant disagrees with the rejection of the application or the respondent disagrees with the personal safety protection order, he or she may apply for a review once to the People's Court that made the ruling within five days from the date the ruling takes effect. If the People's Court makes a personal safety protection order according to law, the enforcement of the personal safety protection order shall not be suspended during the review period.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　人民法院作出人身安全保护令后，应当送达申请人、被申请人、公安机关以及居民委员会、村民委员会等有关组织。人身安全保护令由人民法院执行，公安机关以及居民委员会、村民委员会等应当协助执行。

**Article 32** After making a personal safety protection order, the People's Court shall serve it on the applicant, the respondent, the public security organ, and relevant organizations such as the residents' committee and the villagers' committee. The personal safety protection order shall be enforced by the People's Court, and the public security organ, the residents' committee, the villagers' committee, etc. shall assist in its enforcement.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　加害人实施家庭暴力，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 33** Where a perpetrator commits domestic violence, which constitutes a violation of public security administration, he or she shall be given a public security punishment according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　被申请人违反人身安全保护令，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，人民法院应当给予训诫，可以根据情节轻重处以一千元以下罚款、十五日以下拘留。

**Article 34** Where the respondent violates a personal safety protection order, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, the People's Court shall give a reprimand, and may impose a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan and a detention of not more than 15 days depending on the severity of the circumstances.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　学校、幼儿园、医疗机构、居民委员会、村民委员会、社会工作服务机构、救助管理机构、福利机构及其工作人员未依照[本法第十四条](#art-14)规定向公安机关报案，造成严重后果的，由上级主管部门或者本单位对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 35** Where schools, kindergartens, medical institutions, residents' committees, villagers' committees, social work service institutions, rescue management institutions, welfare institutions and their staff fail to report to the public security organ in accordance with Article 14 of this Law, causing serious consequences, the competent higher authority or the unit itself shall impose sanctions on the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　负有反家庭暴力职责的国家工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 36** Where State functionaries responsible for anti-domestic violence duties neglect their duties, abuse their power, or engage in malpractice for personal gain, they shall be given sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　家庭成员以外共同生活的人之间实施的暴力行为，参照本法规定执行。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于办理人身安全保护令案件适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-4" title="被《最高人民法院关于办理人身安全保护令案件适用法律若干问题的规定》第4条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 37** The provisions of this Law shall be applied mutatis mutandis to acts of violence committed between persons who live together but are not family members.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本法自2016年3月1日起施行。

**Article 38** This Law shall come into force on March 1, 2016.
