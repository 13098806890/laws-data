### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了预防火灾和减少火灾危害，加强应急救援工作，保护人身、财产安全，维护公共安全，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purpose of preventing fires and reducing fire hazards, strengthening emergency rescue work, protecting personal and property safety, and maintaining public security.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　消防工作贯彻预防为主、防消结合的方针，按照政府统一领导、部门依法监管、单位全面负责、公民积极参与的原则，实行消防安全责任制，建立健全社会化的消防工作网络。

**Article 2** Fire protection work shall implement the policy of putting prevention first and combining prevention with fire fighting, and in accordance with the principles of unified leadership by the government, supervision according to law by departments, full responsibility by entities, and active participation by citizens, a fire safety responsibility system shall be implemented and a socialized fire protection work network shall be established and improved.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院领导全国的消防工作。地方各级人民政府负责本行政区域内的消防工作。  
各级人民政府应当将消防工作纳入国民经济和社会发展计划，保障消防工作与经济社会发展相适应。

**Article 3** The State Council shall lead the national fire protection work. Local people's governments at all levels shall be responsible for the fire protection work within their respective administrative areas.  
People's governments at all levels shall incorporate fire protection work into the national economic and social development plan and ensure that fire protection work is compatible with economic and social development.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院应急管理部门对全国的消防工作实施监督管理。县级以上地方人民政府应急管理部门对本行政区域内的消防工作实施监督管理，并由本级人民政府消防救援机构负责实施。军事设施的消防工作，由其主管单位监督管理，消防救援机构协助；矿井地下部分、核电厂、海上石油天然气设施的消防工作，由其主管单位监督管理。  
县级以上人民政府其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内，依照本法和其他相关法律、法规的规定做好消防工作。  
法律、行政法规对森林、草原的消防工作另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 4** The emergency management department of the State Council shall exercise supervision and administration over the national fire protection work. The emergency management departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall exercise supervision and administration over the fire protection work within their respective administrative areas, and the fire rescue authorities of the people's governments at the same level shall be responsible for implementation. The fire protection work of military installations shall be supervised and administered by the competent units thereof, with the assistance of the fire rescue authorities; the fire protection work of underground parts of mines, nuclear power plants, and offshore oil and gas facilities shall be supervised and administered by the competent units thereof.  
Other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective scope of duties, carry out fire protection work well in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other relevant laws and regulations.  
Where laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise for the fire protection work of forests and grasslands, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　任何单位和个人都有维护消防安全、保护消防设施、预防火灾、报告火警的义务。任何单位和成年人都有参加有组织的灭火工作的义务。

**Article 5** All entities and individuals shall have the duty to maintain fire safety, protect fire protection facilities, prevent fires, and report fire alarms. All entities and adults shall have the duty to participate in organized fire fighting work.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各级人民政府应当组织开展经常性的消防宣传教育，提高公民的消防安全意识。  
机关、团体、企业、事业等单位，应当加强对本单位人员的消防宣传教育。  
应急管理部门及消防救援机构应当加强消防法律、法规的宣传，并督促、指导、协助有关单位做好消防宣传教育工作。  
教育、人力资源行政主管部门和学校、有关职业培训机构应当将消防知识纳入教育、教学、培训的内容。  
新闻、广播、电视等有关单位，应当有针对性地面向社会进行消防宣传教育。  
工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会等团体应当结合各自工作对象的特点，组织开展消防宣传教育。  
村民委员会、居民委员会应当协助人民政府以及公安机关、应急管理等部门，加强消防宣传教育。

**Article 6** People's governments at all levels shall organize regular fire publicity and education to enhance citizens' awareness of fire safety.  
State organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions and other entities shall strengthen fire publicity and education among their own personnel.  
The emergency management departments and the fire rescue authorities shall strengthen the publicity of fire laws and regulations, and shall urge, guide, and assist relevant entities to carry out fire publicity and education work well.  
The administrative departments of education and human resources, as well as schools and relevant vocational training institutions, shall incorporate fire knowledge into the content of education, teaching, and training.  
News, radio, television and other relevant entities shall conduct targeted fire publicity and education for the public.  
Trade unions, the Communist Youth League, women's federations and other organizations shall organize and carry out fire publicity and education in light of the characteristics of their respective work objects.  
Villagers' committees and residents' committees shall assist the people's governments and the departments of public security, emergency management, etc., in strengthening fire publicity and education.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励、支持消防科学研究和技术创新，推广使用先进的消防和应急救援技术、设备；鼓励、支持社会力量开展消防公益活动。  
对在消防工作中有突出贡献的单位和个人，应当按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 7** The State encourages and supports scientific research and technological innovation in fire protection, and promotes the use of advanced fire protection and emergency rescue technologies and equipment; encourages and supports social forces to carry out fire public welfare activities.  
Entities and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in fire protection work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　火灾预防

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　地方各级人民政府应当将包括消防安全布局、消防站、消防供水、消防通信、消防车通道、消防装备等内容的消防规划纳入城乡规划，并负责组织实施。  
城乡消防安全布局不符合消防安全要求的，应当调整、完善；公共消防设施、消防装备不足或者不适应实际需要的，应当增建、改建、配置或者进行技术改造。

**Article 8** Local people's governments at all levels shall incorporate fire protection plans, including fire safety layout, fire stations, fire water supply, fire communications, fire truck passages, fire equipment, etc., into urban and rural plans, and shall be responsible for organizing implementation.  
Where the urban and rural fire safety layout does not meet fire safety requirements, it shall be adjusted and improved; where public fire protection facilities and fire equipment are insufficient or unsuitable for actual needs, they shall be built, rebuilt, equipped, or technologically upgraded.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　建设工程的消防设计、施工必须符合国家工程建设消防技术标准。建设、设计、施工、工程监理等单位依法对建设工程的消防设计、施工质量负责。

**Article 9** The fire protection design and construction of construction projects must comply with the national engineering construction fire technical standards. The entities of construction, design, construction, and project supervision shall be responsible for the quality of the fire protection design and construction of construction projects according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对按照国家工程建设消防技术标准需要进行消防设计的建设工程，实行建设工程消防设计审查验收制度。

**Article 10** For construction projects that require fire protection design in accordance with the national engineering construction fire technical standards, a system of fire protection design review and acceptance inspection for construction projects shall be implemented.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院住房和城乡建设主管部门规定的特殊建设工程，建设单位应当将消防设计文件报送住房和城乡建设主管部门审查，住房和城乡建设主管部门依法对审查的结果负责。  
前款规定以外的其他建设工程，建设单位申请领取施工许可证或者申请批准开工报告时应当提供满足施工需要的消防设计图纸及技术资料。

**Article 11** For special construction projects as prescribed by the housing and urban-rural construction administrative department of the State Council, the construction entity shall submit the fire protection design documents to the housing and urban-rural construction administrative department for review, and the housing and urban-rural construction administrative department shall be responsible for the review results according to law.  
For other construction projects other than those specified in the preceding paragraph, the construction entity, when applying for a construction permit or applying for approval of a commencement report, shall provide fire protection design drawings and technical materials that meet the construction needs.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　特殊建设工程未经消防设计审查或者审查不合格的，建设单位、施工单位不得施工；其他建设工程，建设单位未提供满足施工需要的消防设计图纸及技术资料的，有关部门不得发放施工许可证或者批准开工报告。

**Article 12** Where a special construction project has not been reviewed for fire protection design or the review fails, the construction entity and the construction unit shall not commence construction; for other construction projects, if the construction entity fails to provide fire protection design drawings and technical materials that meet the construction needs, the relevant department shall not issue the construction permit or approve the commencement report.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院住房和城乡建设主管部门规定应当申请消防验收的建设工程竣工，建设单位应当向住房和城乡建设主管部门申请消防验收。  
前款规定以外的其他建设工程，建设单位在验收后应当报住房和城乡建设主管部门备案，住房和城乡建设主管部门应当进行抽查。  
依法应当进行消防验收的建设工程，未经消防验收或者消防验收不合格的，禁止投入使用；其他建设工程经依法抽查不合格的，应当停止使用。

**Article 13** Upon completion of a construction project for which fire protection acceptance inspection shall be applied as prescribed by the housing and urban-rural construction administrative department of the State Council, the construction entity shall apply to the housing and urban-rural construction administrative department for fire protection acceptance inspection.  
For other construction projects other than those specified in the preceding paragraph, the construction entity shall, after acceptance inspection, report to the housing and urban-rural construction administrative department for record, and the housing and urban-rural construction administrative department shall conduct spot checks.  
Construction projects that shall undergo fire protection acceptance inspection according to law shall be prohibited from being put into use if they have not undergone fire protection acceptance inspection or have failed such inspection; other construction projects that fail the spot check conducted according to law shall stop being used.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　建设工程消防设计审查、消防验收、备案和抽查的具体办法，由国务院住房和城乡建设主管部门规定。

**Article 14** The specific measures for fire protection design review, fire protection acceptance inspection, recording, and spot checks of construction projects shall be prescribed by the housing and urban-rural construction administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　公众聚集场所投入使用、营业前消防安全检查实行告知承诺管理。公众聚集场所在投入使用、营业前，建设单位或者使用单位应当向场所所在地的县级以上地方人民政府消防救援机构申请消防安全检查，作出场所符合消防技术标准和管理规定的承诺，提交规定的材料，并对其承诺和材料的真实性负责。  
消防救援机构对申请人提交的材料进行审查；申请材料齐全、符合法定形式的，应当予以许可。消防救援机构应当根据消防技术标准和管理规定，及时对作出承诺的公众聚集场所进行核查。  
申请人选择不采用告知承诺方式办理的，消防救援机构应当自受理申请之日起十个工作日内，根据消防技术标准和管理规定，对该场所进行检查。经检查符合消防安全要求的，应当予以许可。  
公众聚集场所未经消防救援机构许可的，不得投入使用、营业。消防安全检查的具体办法，由国务院应急管理部门制定。

**Article 15** The fire safety inspection before the use or operation of public gathering places shall be subject to the management of notification and commitment. Before the use or operation of a public gathering place, the construction entity or the using entity shall apply to the fire rescue authority of the local people's government at or above the county level where the place is located for fire safety inspection, make a commitment that the place complies with fire technical standards and management regulations, submit the prescribed materials, and be responsible for the authenticity of its commitment and materials.  
The fire rescue authority shall review the materials submitted by the applicant; if the application materials are complete and conform to the statutory form, it shall grant permission. The fire rescue authority shall, in accordance with the fire technical standards and management regulations, promptly verify the public gathering place that has made the commitment.  
Where the applicant chooses not to adopt the method of notification and commitment, the fire rescue authority shall, within ten working days from the date of acceptance of the application, inspect the place in accordance with the fire technical standards and management regulations. If the inspection shows that the place meets the fire safety requirements, permission shall be granted.  
No public gathering place shall be put into use or operated without permission from the fire rescue authority. The specific measures for fire safety inspection shall be formulated by the emergency management department of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　机关、团体、企业、事业等单位应当履行下列消防安全职责：  
（一）落实消防安全责任制，制定本单位的消防安全制度、消防安全操作规程，制定灭火和应急疏散预案；  
（二）按照国家标准、行业标准配置消防设施、器材，设置消防安全标志，并定期组织检验、维修，确保完好有效；  
（三）对建筑消防设施每年至少进行一次全面检测，确保完好有效，检测记录应当完整准确，存档备查；  
（四）保障疏散通道、安全出口、消防车通道畅通，保证防火防烟分区、防火间距符合消防技术标准；  
（五）组织防火检查，及时消除火灾隐患；  
（六）组织进行有针对性的消防演练；  
（七）法律、法规规定的其他消防安全职责。  
单位的主要负责人是本单位的消防安全责任人。

**Article 16** State organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions and other entities shall perform the following fire safety duties:  
(1) Implement the fire safety responsibility system, formulate the entity's fire safety system and fire safety operation procedures, and formulate fire fighting and emergency evacuation plans;  
(2) Equip fire protection facilities and equipment in accordance with national and industry standards, set up fire safety signs, and regularly organize inspection and maintenance to ensure they are in good condition and effective;  
(3) Conduct a comprehensive test of building fire protection facilities at least once a year to ensure they are in good condition and effective, and the test records shall be complete and accurate, and kept on file for reference;  
(4) Ensure that evacuation passages, safety exits, and fire truck passages are unobstructed, and ensure that fire and smoke compartments and fire separation distances comply with fire technical standards;  
(5) Organize fire prevention inspections and promptly eliminate fire hazards;  
(6) Organize targeted fire drills;  
(7) Other fire safety duties as prescribed by laws and regulations.  
The principal responsible person of an entity shall be the fire safety responsible person of the entity.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上地方人民政府消防救援机构应当将发生火灾可能性较大以及发生火灾可能造成重大的人身伤亡或者财产损失的单位，确定为本行政区域内的消防安全重点单位，并由应急管理部门报本级人民政府备案。  
消防安全重点单位除应当履行[本法第十六条](#art-16)规定的职责外，还应当履行下列消防安全职责：  
（一）确定消防安全管理人，组织实施本单位的消防安全管理工作；  
（二）建立消防档案，确定消防安全重点部位，设置防火标志，实行严格管理；  
（三）实行每日防火巡查，并建立巡查记录；  
（四）对职工进行岗前消防安全培训，定期组织消防安全培训和消防演练。

**Article 17** The fire rescue authorities of local people's governments at or above the county level shall determine entities with a higher likelihood of fire occurrence or that may cause significant personal injury or property loss in the event of a fire as key fire safety entities within their administrative areas, and the emergency management department shall report to the people's government at the same level for record.  
In addition to performing the duties prescribed in Article 16 of this Law, key fire safety entities shall also perform the following fire safety duties:  
(1) Designate a fire safety manager to organize and implement the entity's fire safety management work;  
(2) Establish fire protection files, designate key fire safety areas, set up fire prevention signs, and exercise strict management;  
(3) Conduct daily fire prevention patrols and establish patrol records;  
(4) Provide pre-job fire safety training to employees, and regularly organize fire safety training and fire drills.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　同一建筑物由两个以上单位管理或者使用的，应当明确各方的消防安全责任，并确定责任人对共用的疏散通道、安全出口、建筑消防设施和消防车通道进行统一管理。  
住宅区的物业服务企业应当对管理区域内的共用消防设施进行维护管理，提供消防安全防范服务。

**Article 18** Where a building is managed or used by two or more entities, the fire safety responsibilities of each party shall be clearly defined, and a responsible person shall be designated to uniformly manage the shared evacuation passages, safety exits, building fire protection facilities, and fire truck passages.  
A property service enterprise in a residential area shall carry out maintenance and management of the shared fire protection facilities within the management area and provide fire safety prevention services.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　生产、储存、经营易燃易爆危险品的场所不得与居住场所设置在同一建筑物内，并应当与居住场所保持安全距离。  
生产、储存、经营其他物品的场所与居住场所设置在同一建筑物内的，应当符合国家工程建设消防技术标准。

**Article 19** Places for producing, storing, or operating flammable and explosive dangerous goods shall not be located in the same building as residential premises, and shall maintain a safe distance from residential premises.  
Where places for producing, storing, or operating other articles are located in the same building as residential premises, they shall comply with the national engineering construction fire technical standards.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　举办大型群众性活动，承办人应当依法向公安机关申请安全许可，制定灭火和应急疏散预案并组织演练，明确消防安全责任分工，确定消防安全管理人员，保持消防设施和消防器材配置齐全、完好有效，保证疏散通道、安全出口、疏散指示标志、应急照明和消防车通道符合消防技术标准和管理规定。

**Article 20** For holding large-scale mass activities, the organizer shall apply to the public security organ for safety permission according to law, formulate fire fighting and emergency evacuation plans and organize drills, clearly define the division of fire safety responsibilities, designate fire safety management personnel, ensure that fire protection facilities and fire equipment are fully equipped and in good condition, and ensure that evacuation passages, safety exits, evacuation indication signs, emergency lighting, and fire truck passages comply with fire technical standards and management regulations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　禁止在具有火灾、爆炸危险的场所吸烟、使用明火。因施工等特殊情况需要使用明火作业的，应当按照规定事先办理审批手续，采取相应的消防安全措施；作业人员应当遵守消防安全规定。  
进行电焊、气焊等具有火灾危险作业的人员和自动消防系统的操作人员，必须持证上岗，并遵守消防安全操作规程。

**Article 21** Smoking and use of open flames are prohibited in places with fire or explosion hazards. Where open flame operations are necessary due to special circumstances such as construction, prior approval procedures shall be handled in accordance with the provisions, and corresponding fire safety measures shall be taken; the operators shall comply with fire safety regulations.  
Personnel performing fire hazardous operations such as electric welding and gas welding, and operators of automatic fire protection systems, must hold certificates to work and comply with fire safety operating procedures.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　生产、储存、装卸易燃易爆危险品的工厂、仓库和专用车站、码头的设置，应当符合消防技术标准。易燃易爆气体和液体的充装站、供应站、调压站，应当设置在符合消防安全要求的位置，并符合防火防爆要求。  
已经设置的生产、储存、装卸易燃易爆危险品的工厂、仓库和专用车站、码头，易燃易爆气体和液体的充装站、供应站、调压站，不再符合前款规定的，地方人民政府应当组织、协调有关部门、单位限期解决，消除安全隐患。

**Article 22** The establishment of factories, warehouses, and dedicated stations and docks for producing, storing, or loading and unloading flammable and explosive dangerous goods shall comply with fire technical standards. Filling stations, supply stations, and pressure regulating stations for flammable and explosive gases and liquids shall be located in positions that meet fire safety requirements and shall comply with fire and explosion prevention requirements.  
Where existing factories, warehouses, dedicated stations and docks for producing, storing, or loading and unloading flammable and explosive dangerous goods, or filling stations, supply stations, and pressure regulating stations for flammable and explosive gases and liquids no longer comply with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the local people's government shall organize and coordinate relevant departments and entities to solve the problem within a time limit and eliminate safety hazards.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　生产、储存、运输、销售、使用、销毁易燃易爆危险品，必须执行消防技术标准和管理规定。  
进入生产、储存易燃易爆危险品的场所，必须执行消防安全规定。禁止非法携带易燃易爆危险品进入公共场所或者乘坐公共交通工具。  
储存可燃物资仓库的管理，必须执行消防技术标准和管理规定。

**Article 23** The production, storage, transportation, sale, use, and destruction of flammable and explosive dangerous goods must comply with fire technical standards and management regulations.  
Entering places where flammable and explosive dangerous goods are produced or stored must comply with fire safety regulations. It is prohibited to illegally carry flammable and explosive dangerous goods into public places or take public transportation.  
The management of warehouses storing combustible materials must comply with fire technical standards and management regulations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　消防产品必须符合国家标准；没有国家标准的，必须符合行业标准。禁止生产、销售或者使用不合格的消防产品以及国家明令淘汰的消防产品。  
依法实行强制性产品认证的消防产品，由具有法定资质的认证机构按照国家标准、行业标准的强制性要求认证合格后，方可生产、销售、使用。实行强制性产品认证的消防产品目录，由国务院产品质量监督部门会同国务院应急管理部门制定并公布。  
新研制的尚未制定国家标准、行业标准的消防产品，应当按照国务院产品质量监督部门会同国务院应急管理部门规定的办法，经技术鉴定符合消防安全要求的，方可生产、销售、使用。  
依照本条规定经强制性产品认证合格或者技术鉴定合格的消防产品，国务院应急管理部门应当予以公布。

**Article 24** Fire protection products must comply with national standards; where there are no national standards, they must comply with industry standards. It is prohibited to produce, sell, or use unqualified fire protection products or fire protection products that have been expressly eliminated by the State.  
Fire protection products subject to compulsory product certification according to law may be produced, sold, or used only after they have been certified as conforming to the compulsory requirements of national and industry standards by a certification body with legal qualifications. The catalog of fire protection products subject to compulsory product certification shall be formulated and published by the product quality supervision department of the State Council jointly with the emergency management department of the State Council.  
Newly developed fire protection products for which national standards or industry standards have not yet been formulated may be produced, sold, or used only after they have passed technical appraisal in accordance with the measures prescribed by the product quality supervision department of the State Council jointly with the emergency management department of the State Council and are found to meet fire safety requirements.  
The emergency management department of the State Council shall publish fire protection products that have passed compulsory product certification or technical appraisal in accordance with this Article.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　产品质量监督部门、工商行政管理部门、消防救援机构应当按照各自职责加强对消防产品质量的监督检查。

**Article 25** The product quality supervision departments, administrative departments for industry and commerce, and fire rescue authorities shall, within their respective duties, strengthen supervision and inspection of the quality of fire protection products.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　建筑构件、建筑材料和室内装修、装饰材料的防火性能必须符合国家标准；没有国家标准的，必须符合行业标准。  
人员密集场所室内装修、装饰，应当按照消防技术标准的要求，使用不燃、难燃材料。

**Article 26** The fire resistance performance of building components, building materials, and indoor decoration and finishing materials must comply with national standards; where there are no national standards, they must comply with industry standards.  
For indoor decoration and finishing of crowded places, non-combustible or flame-retardant materials shall be used in accordance with the requirements of fire technical standards.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　电器产品、燃气用具的产品标准，应当符合消防安全的要求。  
电器产品、燃气用具的安装、使用及其线路、管路的设计、敷设、维护保养、检测，必须符合消防技术标准和管理规定。

**Article 27** Product standards for electrical appliances and gas appliances shall comply with fire safety requirements.  
The installation, use, design, laying, maintenance, and testing of electrical appliances, gas appliances, and their lines and pipelines must comply with fire technical standards and management regulations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　任何单位、个人不得损坏、挪用或者擅自拆除、停用消防设施、器材，不得埋压、圈占、遮挡消火栓或者占用防火间距，不得占用、堵塞、封闭疏散通道、安全出口、消防车通道。人员密集场所的门窗不得设置影响逃生和灭火救援的障碍物。

**Article 28** No entity or individual may damage, misappropriate, or dismantle or deactivate fire protection facilities or equipment without authorization, may not bury, enclose, or obscure fire hydrants or occupy fire separation distances, and may not occupy, block, or seal off evacuation passages, safety exits, or fire truck passages. Doors and windows of crowded places shall not be installed with obstacles that affect escape and fire fighting and rescue.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　负责公共消防设施维护管理的单位，应当保持消防供水、消防通信、消防车通道等公共消防设施的完好有效。在修建道路以及停电、停水、截断通信线路时有可能影响消防队灭火救援的，有关单位必须事先通知当地消防救援机构。

**Article 29** The entity responsible for the maintenance and management of public fire protection facilities shall keep public fire protection facilities such as fire water supply, fire communications, and fire truck passages in good condition and effective. Where road construction, power outages, water outages, or interruption of communication lines may affect fire fighting and rescue by fire brigades, the relevant entity must notify the local fire rescue authority in advance.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　地方各级人民政府应当加强对农村消防工作的领导，采取措施加强公共消防设施建设，组织建立和督促落实消防安全责任制。

**Article 30** Local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen leadership over rural fire protection work, take measures to strengthen the construction of public fire protection facilities, organize the establishment of and supervise the implementation of the fire safety responsibility system.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　在农业收获季节、森林和草原防火期间、重大节假日期间以及火灾多发季节，地方各级人民政府应当组织开展有针对性的消防宣传教育，采取防火措施，进行消防安全检查。

**Article 31** During the agricultural harvest season, forest and grassland fire prevention periods, major holidays, and seasons with high fire incidence, local people's governments at all levels shall organize targeted fire publicity and education, take fire prevention measures, and conduct fire safety inspections.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　乡镇人民政府、城市街道办事处应当指导、支持和帮助村民委员会、居民委员会开展群众性的消防工作。村民委员会、居民委员会应当确定消防安全管理人，组织制定防火安全公约，进行防火安全检查。

**Article 32** Town people's governments and sub-district offices of cities shall guide, support, and assist villagers' committees and residents' committees in carrying out mass fire protection work. Villagers' committees and residents' committees shall designate a fire safety manager, organize the formulation of fire safety conventions, and conduct fire prevention inspections.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家鼓励、引导公众聚集场所和生产、储存、运输、销售易燃易爆危险品的企业投保火灾公众责任保险；鼓励保险公司承保火灾公众责任保险。

**Article 33** The State encourages and guides public gathering places and enterprises that produce, store, transport, or sell flammable and explosive dangerous goods to take out fire public liability insurance; encourages insurance companies to underwrite fire public liability insurance.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　消防设施维护保养检测、消防安全评估等消防技术服务机构应当符合从业条件，执业人员应当依法获得相应的资格；依照法律、行政法规、国家标准、行业标准和执业准则，接受委托提供消防技术服务，并对服务质量负责。

**Article 34** Fire technical service institutions such as those for fire protection facility maintenance, testing, and fire safety assessment shall meet the practice conditions, and their practicing personnel shall obtain corresponding qualifications according to law; they shall accept entrustment to provide fire technical services in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, national standards, industry standards, and practice standards, and shall be responsible for the quality of services.

### 第三章　消防组织

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　各级人民政府应当加强消防组织建设，根据经济社会发展的需要，建立多种形式的消防组织，加强消防技术人才培养，增强火灾预防、扑救和应急救援的能力。

**Article 35** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the construction of fire protection organizations, establish various forms of fire protection organizations according to the needs of economic and social development, strengthen the training of fire technical personnel, and enhance the capability of fire prevention, fire fighting, and emergency rescue.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　县级以上地方人民政府应当按照国家规定建立国家综合性消防救援队、专职消防队，并按照国家标准配备消防装备，承担火灾扑救工作。  
乡镇人民政府应当根据当地经济发展和消防工作的需要，建立专职消防队、志愿消防队，承担火灾扑救工作。

**Article 36** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State, establish national comprehensive fire rescue teams and full-time fire brigades, equip them with fire equipment in accordance with national standards, and undertake fire fighting work.  
Town people's governments shall, according to the needs of local economic development and fire protection work, establish full-time fire brigades and volunteer fire brigades to undertake fire fighting work.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家综合性消防救援队、专职消防队按照国家规定承担重大灾害事故和其他以抢救人员生命为主的应急救援工作。

**Article 37** National comprehensive fire rescue teams and full-time fire brigades shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State, undertake emergency rescue work for major disasters and accidents and other rescues primarily focused on saving lives.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家综合性消防救援队、专职消防队应当充分发挥火灾扑救和应急救援专业力量的骨干作用；按照国家规定，组织实施专业技能训练，配备并维护保养装备器材，提高火灾扑救和应急救援的能力。

**Article 38** National comprehensive fire rescue teams and full-time fire brigades shall fully play the backbone role of professional forces in fire fighting and emergency rescue; in accordance with the provisions of the State, organize and implement professional skills training, equip and maintain equipment and apparatus, and improve the capability of fire fighting and emergency rescue.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　下列单位应当建立单位专职消防队，承担本单位的火灾扑救工作：  
（一）大型核设施单位、大型发电厂、民用机场、主要港口；  
（二）生产、储存易燃易爆危险品的大型企业；  
（三）储备可燃的重要物资的大型仓库、基地；  
（四）第一项、第二项、第三项规定以外的火灾危险性较大、距离国家综合性消防救援队较远的其他大型企业；  
（五）距离国家综合性消防救援队较远、被列为全国重点文物保护单位的古建筑群的管理单位。

**Article 39** The following entities shall establish full-time fire brigades for their own entities to undertake fire fighting work within their own entities:  
(1) Large nuclear facilities, large power plants, civil airports, and major ports;  
(2) Large enterprises producing or storing flammable and explosive dangerous goods;  
(3) Large warehouses or bases storing important combustible materials;  
(4) Other large enterprises not specified in items (1), (2), or (3) that have relatively high fire risks and are far from national comprehensive fire rescue teams;  
(5) Management units of ancient building complexes that are listed as national key cultural relics protection units and are far from national comprehensive fire rescue teams.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　专职消防队的建立，应当符合国家有关规定，并报当地消防救援机构验收。  
专职消防队的队员依法享受社会保险和福利待遇。

**Article 40** The establishment of full-time fire brigades shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State and shall be reported to the local fire rescue authority for acceptance inspection.  
Members of full-time fire brigades shall enjoy social insurance and welfare benefits according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　机关、团体、企业、事业等单位以及村民委员会、居民委员会根据需要，建立志愿消防队等多种形式的消防组织，开展群众性自防自救工作。

**Article 41** State organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions and other entities, as well as villagers' committees and residents' committees, shall, as needed, establish volunteer fire brigades and other forms of fire protection organizations to carry out mass self-prevention and self-rescue work.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　消防救援机构应当对专职消防队、志愿消防队等消防组织进行业务指导；根据扑救火灾的需要，可以调动指挥专职消防队参加火灾扑救工作。

**Article 42** Fire rescue authorities shall provide professional guidance to full-time fire brigades, volunteer fire brigades, and other fire protection organizations; they may, according to the needs of fire fighting, mobilize and command full-time fire brigades to participate in fire fighting work.

### 第四章　灭火救援

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　县级以上地方人民政府应当组织有关部门针对本行政区域内的火灾特点制定应急预案，建立应急反应和处置机制，为火灾扑救和应急救援工作提供人员、装备等保障。

**Article 43** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize relevant departments to formulate emergency plans based on the characteristics of fires within their administrative areas, establish emergency response and handling mechanisms, and provide personnel, equipment, and other support for fire fighting and emergency rescue work.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　任何人发现火灾都应当立即报警。任何单位、个人都应当无偿为报警提供便利，不得阻拦报警。严禁谎报火警。  
人员密集场所发生火灾，该场所的现场工作人员应当立即组织、引导在场人员疏散。  
任何单位发生火灾，必须立即组织力量扑救。邻近单位应当给予支援。  
消防队接到火警，必须立即赶赴火灾现场，救助遇险人员，排除险情，扑灭火灾。

**Article 44** Any person who discovers a fire shall immediately report it. Any entity or individual shall provide convenience for reporting without compensation and shall not obstruct the report. False reporting of fire alarms is strictly prohibited.  
When a fire occurs in a crowded place, the on-site staff of the place shall immediately organize and guide the evacuation of persons present.  
When a fire occurs in any entity, it must immediately organize forces to fight the fire. Neighboring entities shall provide support.  
Upon receiving a fire alarm, the fire brigade must immediately proceed to the fire scene to rescue persons in distress, eliminate dangers, and extinguish the fire.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　消防救援机构统一组织和指挥火灾现场扑救，应当优先保障遇险人员的生命安全。  
火灾现场总指挥根据扑救火灾的需要，有权决定下列事项：  
（一）使用各种水源；  
（二）截断电力、可燃气体和可燃液体的输送，限制用火用电；  
（三）划定警戒区，实行局部交通管制；  
（四）利用临近建筑物和有关设施；  
（五）为了抢救人员和重要物资，防止火势蔓延，拆除或者破损毗邻火灾现场的建筑物、构筑物或者设施等；  
（六）调动供水、供电、供气、通信、医疗救护、交通运输、环境保护等有关单位协助灭火救援。  
根据扑救火灾的紧急需要，有关地方人民政府应当组织人员、调集所需物资支援灭火。

**Article 45** The fire rescue authority shall organize and command the fire fighting at the fire scene in a unified manner, and shall prioritize ensuring the life safety of persons in distress.  
The general commander at the fire scene, according to the needs of fire fighting, shall have the authority to decide on the following matters:  
(1) Use of various water sources;  
(2) Cut off the supply of electricity, combustible gases, and combustible liquids, and restrict the use of fire and electricity;  
(3) Designate a warning zone and implement local traffic control;  
(4) Utilize adjacent buildings and relevant facilities;  
(5) For the purpose of rescuing persons and important materials and preventing the spread of fire, demolish or damage buildings, structures, or facilities adjacent to the fire scene, etc.;  
(6) Mobilize relevant units such as water supply, power supply, gas supply, communications, medical rescue, transportation, and environmental protection to assist in fire fighting and rescue.  
According to the urgent needs of fire fighting, the relevant local people's government shall organize personnel and mobilize the necessary materials to support fire fighting.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国家综合性消防救援队、专职消防队参加火灾以外的其他重大灾害事故的应急救援工作，由县级以上人民政府统一领导。

**Article 46** The participation of national comprehensive fire rescue teams and full-time fire brigades in emergency rescue work for major disasters and accidents other than fires shall be under the unified leadership of the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　消防车、消防艇前往执行火灾扑救或者应急救援任务，在确保安全的前提下，不受行驶速度、行驶路线、行驶方向和指挥信号的限制，其他车辆、船舶以及行人应当让行，不得穿插超越；收费公路、桥梁免收车辆通行费。交通管理指挥人员应当保证消防车、消防艇迅速通行。  
赶赴火灾现场或者应急救援现场的消防人员和调集的消防装备、物资，需要铁路、水路或者航空运输的，有关单位应当优先运输。

**Article 47** When fire trucks and fire boats are on missions to perform fire fighting or emergency rescue, they shall, on the premise of ensuring safety, not be subject to restrictions on speed, route, direction, or command signals; other vehicles, vessels, and pedestrians shall give way and shall not cut in or overtake; toll roads and bridges shall be exempted from vehicle tolls. Traffic control command personnel shall ensure the rapid passage of fire trucks and fire boats.  
Where fire personnel dispatched to the fire scene or emergency rescue scene and the mobilized fire equipment and materials require railway, waterway, or air transportation, the relevant units shall give priority to transportation.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　消防车、消防艇以及消防器材、装备和设施，不得用于与消防和应急救援工作无关的事项。

**Article 48** Fire trucks, fire boats, and fire protection equipment, apparatus, and facilities shall not be used for matters unrelated to fire fighting and emergency rescue work.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家综合性消防救援队、专职消防队扑救火灾、应急救援，不得收取任何费用。  
单位专职消防队、志愿消防队参加扑救外单位火灾所损耗的燃料、灭火剂和器材、装备等，由火灾发生地的人民政府给予补偿。

**Article 49** National comprehensive fire rescue teams and full-time fire brigades shall not charge any fees for fire fighting or emergency rescue.  
The fuel, extinguishing agents, equipment, and apparatus consumed by an entity's full-time fire brigade or volunteer fire brigade when participating in fire fighting for another entity shall be compensated by the people's government of the place where the fire occurred.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　对因参加扑救火灾或者应急救援受伤、致残或者死亡的人员，按照国家有关规定给予医疗、抚恤。

**Article 50** Persons who are injured, disabled, or die as a result of participating in fire fighting or emergency rescue shall be given medical treatment and pensions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　消防救援机构有权根据需要封闭火灾现场，负责调查火灾原因，统计火灾损失。  
火灾扑灭后，发生火灾的单位和相关人员应当按照消防救援机构的要求保护现场，接受事故调查，如实提供与火灾有关的情况。  
消防救援机构根据火灾现场勘验、调查情况和有关的检验、鉴定意见，及时制作火灾事故认定书，作为处理火灾事故的证据。

**Article 51** The fire rescue authority shall have the right to seal off the fire scene as necessary, be responsible for investigating the cause of the fire, and compiling fire loss statistics.  
After the fire is extinguished, the unit where the fire occurred and relevant persons shall, in accordance with the requirements of the fire rescue authority, protect the scene, accept the accident investigation, and truthfully provide information related to the fire.  
The fire rescue authority shall, based on the inspection of the fire scene, investigation findings, and relevant inspection and expert evaluation opinions, promptly prepare a fire accident identification certificate, which shall serve as evidence for handling the fire accident.

### 第五章　监督检查

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　地方各级人民政府应当落实消防工作责任制，对本级人民政府有关部门履行消防安全职责的情况进行监督检查。  
县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当根据本系统的特点，有针对性地开展消防安全检查，及时督促整改火灾隐患。

**Article 52** Local people's governments at all levels shall implement the fire protection work responsibility system, and supervise and inspect the performance of fire safety duties by the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level.  
The relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the characteristics of their respective systems, conduct targeted fire safety inspections and promptly urge the rectification of fire hazards.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　消防救援机构应当对机关、团体、企业、事业等单位遵守消防法律、法规的情况依法进行监督检查。公安派出所可以负责日常消防监督检查、开展消防宣传教育，具体办法由国务院公安部门规定。  
消防救援机构、公安派出所的工作人员进行消防监督检查，应当出示证件。

**Article 53** The fire rescue authority shall, according to law, conduct supervision and inspection on the compliance with fire protection laws and regulations by state organs, organizations, enterprises, institutions, and other entities. The public security police station may be responsible for daily fire supervision and inspection and carrying out fire protection publicity and education, with specific measures prescribed by the public security department of the State Council.  
Staff members of the fire rescue authority and the public security police station shall produce their credentials when conducting fire supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　消防救援机构在消防监督检查中发现火灾隐患的，应当通知有关单位或者个人立即采取措施消除隐患；不及时消除隐患可能严重威胁公共安全的，消防救援机构应当依照规定对危险部位或者场所采取临时查封措施。

**Article 54** Where a fire rescue authority discovers a fire hazard during fire supervision and inspection, it shall notify the relevant unit or individual to take immediate measures to eliminate the hazard; if failure to eliminate the hazard in a timely manner may seriously threaten public safety, the fire rescue authority shall, in accordance with regulations, take interim sealing measures on the dangerous parts or premises.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　消防救援机构在消防监督检查中发现城乡消防安全布局、公共消防设施不符合消防安全要求，或者发现本地区存在影响公共安全的重大火灾隐患的，应当由应急管理部门书面报告本级人民政府。  
接到报告的人民政府应当及时核实情况，组织或者责成有关部门、单位采取措施，予以整改。

**Article 55** Where a fire rescue authority, during fire supervision and inspection, discovers that the urban or rural fire safety layout or public fire protection facilities do not meet fire safety requirements, or discovers that there are major fire hazards affecting public safety in the local area, the emergency management department shall report in writing to the people's government at the same level.  
The people's government that receives the report shall promptly verify the situation, and organize or instruct the relevant departments and units to take measures for rectification.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　住房和城乡建设主管部门、消防救援机构及其工作人员应当按照法定的职权和程序进行消防设计审查、消防验收、备案抽查和消防安全检查，做到公正、严格、文明、高效。  
住房和城乡建设主管部门、消防救援机构及其工作人员进行消防设计审查、消防验收、备案抽查和消防安全检查等，不得收取费用，不得利用职务谋取利益；不得利用职务为用户、建设单位指定或者变相指定消防产品的品牌、销售单位或者消防技术服务机构、消防设施施工单位。

**Article 56** The competent department of housing and urban-rural development, the fire rescue authority, and their staff shall conduct fire protection design review, fire protection acceptance, filing spot checks, and fire safety inspections in accordance with statutory powers and procedures, and shall be fair, strict, civilized, and efficient.  
The competent department of housing and urban-rural development, the fire rescue authority, and their staff shall not charge fees for conducting fire protection design review, fire protection acceptance, filing spot checks, and fire safety inspections, nor shall they use their positions to seek benefits; they shall not use their positions to designate or indirectly designate brands, sales units of fire protection products, or fire protection technical service institutions, or fire protection facility construction units for users or construction entities.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　住房和城乡建设主管部门、消防救援机构及其工作人员执行职务，应当自觉接受社会和公民的监督。  
任何单位和个人都有权对住房和城乡建设主管部门、消防救援机构及其工作人员在执法中的违法行为进行检举、控告。收到检举、控告的机关，应当按照职责及时查处。

**Article 57** The competent department of housing and urban-rural development, the fire rescue authority, and their staff, when performing their duties, shall consciously accept supervision from society and citizens.  
Any unit or individual shall have the right to report or accuse any illegal acts committed by the competent department of housing and urban-rural development, the fire rescue authority, and their staff in law enforcement. The authority that receives such reports or accusations shall promptly investigate and handle them in accordance with its duties.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由住房和城乡建设主管部门、消防救援机构按照各自职权责令停止施工、停止使用或者停产停业，并处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款：  
（一）依法应当进行消防设计审查的建设工程，未经依法审查或者审查不合格，擅自施工的；  
（二）依法应当进行消防验收的建设工程，未经消防验收或者消防验收不合格，擅自投入使用的；  
（三）[本法第十三条](#art-13)规定的其他建设工程验收后经依法抽查不合格，不停止使用的；  
（四）公众聚集场所未经消防救援机构许可，擅自投入使用、营业的，或者经核查发现场所使用、营业情况与承诺内容不符的。  
核查发现公众聚集场所使用、营业情况与承诺内容不符，经责令限期改正，逾期不整改或者整改后仍达不到要求的，依法撤销相应许可。  
建设单位未依照本法规定在验收后报住房和城乡建设主管部门备案的，由住房和城乡建设主管部门责令改正，处五千元以下罚款。

**Article 58** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the competent department of housing and urban-rural development or the fire rescue authority within their respective powers to stop construction, stop use, or suspend production and business operations, and shall also be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan:  
(1) For a construction project that shall undergo fire protection design review according to law, commencing construction without undergoing such review or after failing the review;  
(2) For a construction project that shall undergo fire protection acceptance according to law, putting it into use without undergoing such acceptance or after failing acceptance;  
(3) For other construction projects as specified in Article 13 of this Law, which are found unqualified after spot checks conducted according to law following acceptance, and failing to cease use;  
(4) For a public gathering place, putting it into use or operation without permission from the fire rescue authority, or if verification reveals that the actual use or operation of the place is inconsistent with the promised content.  
If verification reveals that the actual use or operation of a public gathering place is inconsistent with the promised content, and upon being ordered to rectify within a time limit, the party fails to rectify or still fails to meet requirements after rectification, the corresponding permission shall be revoked according to law.  
If a construction entity fails to file with the competent department of housing and urban-rural development after acceptance in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the competent department of housing and urban-rural development shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由住房和城乡建设主管部门责令改正或者停止施工，并处一万元以上十万元以下罚款：  
（一）建设单位要求建筑设计单位或者建筑施工企业降低消防技术标准设计、施工的；  
（二）建筑设计单位不按照消防技术标准强制性要求进行消防设计的；  
（三）建筑施工企业不按照消防设计文件和消防技术标准施工，降低消防施工质量的；  
（四）工程监理单位与建设单位或者建筑施工企业串通，弄虚作假，降低消防施工质量的。

**Article 59** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the competent department of housing and urban-rural development to make corrections or stop construction, and shall also be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan:  
(1) A construction entity requiring a building design unit or a construction enterprise to lower the fire protection technical standards for design or construction;  
(2) A building design unit failing to conduct fire protection design in accordance with the mandatory requirements of fire protection technical standards;  
(3) A construction enterprise failing to construct in accordance with fire protection design documents and fire protection technical standards, thereby lowering the quality of fire protection construction;  
(4) A project supervision unit colluding with a construction entity or construction enterprise to practice fraud and lower the quality of fire protection construction.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　单位违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，责令改正，处五千元以上五万元以下罚款：  
（一）消防设施、器材或者消防安全标志的配置、设置不符合国家标准、行业标准，或者未保持完好有效的；  
（二）损坏、挪用或者擅自拆除、停用消防设施、器材的；  
（三）占用、堵塞、封闭疏散通道、安全出口或者有其他妨碍安全疏散行为的；  
（四）埋压、圈占、遮挡消火栓或者占用防火间距的；  
（五）占用、堵塞、封闭消防车通道，妨碍消防车通行的；  
（六）人员密集场所在门窗上设置影响逃生和灭火救援的障碍物的；  
（七）对火灾隐患经消防救援机构通知后不及时采取措施消除的。  
个人有前款第二项、第三项、第四项、第五项行为之一的，处警告或者五百元以下罚款。  
有本条第一款第三项、第四项、第五项、第六项行为，经责令改正拒不改正的，强制执行，所需费用由违法行为人承担。

**Article 60** Where a unit violates the provisions of this Law and commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections and be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan:  
(1) The configuration or installation of fire protection facilities, equipment, or fire safety signs fails to meet national standards or trade standards, or they are not maintained in good condition and effective;  
(2) Damaging, misappropriating, or unauthorized removal or deactivation of fire protection facilities or equipment;  
(3) Occupying, blocking, or sealing off evacuation routes, safety exits, or otherwise obstructing safe evacuation;  
(4) Burying, encircling, or covering fire hydrants, or occupying fire prevention distances;  
(5) Occupying, blocking, or sealing off fire truck access roads, obstructing the passage of fire trucks;  
(6) Installing obstacles on doors or windows in crowded places that hinder escape and fire fighting and rescue;  
(7) Failing to take timely measures to eliminate fire hazards after being notified by the fire rescue authority.  
If an individual commits any of the acts specified in subparagraphs (2), (3), (4), or (5) of the preceding paragraph, a warning or a fine of not more than 500 yuan shall be imposed.  
If a person commits any of the acts specified in subparagraphs (3), (4), (5), or (6) of the first paragraph of this Article and, after being ordered to make corrections, refuses to do so, compulsory execution shall be carried out, and the costs incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　生产、储存、经营易燃易爆危险品的场所与居住场所设置在同一建筑物内，或者未与居住场所保持安全距离的，责令停产停业，并处五千元以上五万元以下罚款。  
生产、储存、经营其他物品的场所与居住场所设置在同一建筑物内，不符合消防技术标准的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 61** If a place for producing, storing, or dealing in inflammable and explosive dangerous goods is located in the same building as a residential place, or is not kept at a safe distance from a residential place, the violator shall be ordered to suspend production and business operations and also be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.  
If a place for producing, storing, or dealing in other articles is located in the same building as a residential place and fails to meet fire protection technical standards, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　有下列行为之一的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚：  
（一）违反有关消防技术标准和管理规定生产、储存、运输、销售、使用、销毁易燃易爆危险品的；  
（二）非法携带易燃易爆危险品进入公共场所或者乘坐公共交通工具的；  
（三）谎报火警的；  
（四）阻碍消防车、消防艇执行任务的；  
（五）阻碍消防救援机构的工作人员依法执行职务的。

**Article 62** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments:  
(1) Producing, storing, transporting, selling, using, or disposing of inflammable and explosive dangerous goods in violation of relevant fire protection technical standards and management regulations;  
(2) Illegally carrying inflammable and explosive dangerous goods into public places or on public transport;  
(3) Making false fire alarms;  
(4) Obstructing fire trucks or fire boats from performing their duties;  
(5) Obstructing staff members of the fire rescue authority from performing their duties according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，处警告或者五百元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五日以下拘留：  
（一）违反消防安全规定进入生产、储存易燃易爆危险品场所的；  
（二）违反规定使用明火作业或者在具有火灾、爆炸危险的场所吸烟、使用明火的。

**Article 63** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and commits any of the following acts shall be given a warning or be fined not more than 500 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, he or she shall be detained for not more than five days:  
(1) Entering a place where inflammable and explosive dangerous goods are produced or stored in violation of fire safety regulations;  
(2) Using open flame operations in violation of regulations, or smoking or using open flame in a place with fire or explosion hazards.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，尚不构成犯罪的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处五百元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处警告或者五百元以下罚款：  
（一）指使或者强令他人违反消防安全规定，冒险作业的；  
（二）过失引起火灾的；  
（三）在火灾发生后阻拦报警，或者负有报告职责的人员不及时报警的；  
（四）扰乱火灾现场秩序，或者拒不执行火灾现场指挥员指挥，影响灭火救援的；  
（五）故意破坏或者伪造火灾现场的；  
（六）擅自拆封或者使用被消防救援机构查封的场所、部位的。

**Article 64** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and commits any of the following acts, if the act does not constitute a crime, shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days, and may also be fined not more than 500 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively minor, a warning or a fine of not more than 500 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) Instructing or compelling others to work at risk in violation of fire safety regulations;  
(2) Negligently causing a fire;  
(3) Blocking an alarm after a fire occurs, or a person responsible for reporting failing to report in a timely manner;  
(4) Disturbing the order of a fire scene, or refusing to obey the command of the fire scene commander, affecting fire fighting and rescue;  
(5) Intentionally destroying or falsifying a fire scene;  
(6) Unauthorized unsealing or using a place or part sealed by the fire rescue authority.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本法规定，生产、销售不合格的消防产品或者国家明令淘汰的消防产品的，由产品质量监督部门或者工商行政管理部门依照《中华人民共和国产品质量法》的规定从重处罚。  
人员密集场所使用不合格的消防产品或者国家明令淘汰的消防产品的，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处五千元以上五万元以下罚款，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五百元以上二千元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停产停业。  
消防救援机构对于本条第二款规定的情形，除依法对使用者予以处罚外，应当将发现不合格的消防产品和国家明令淘汰的消防产品的情况通报产品质量监督部门、工商行政管理部门。产品质量监督部门、工商行政管理部门应当对生产者、销售者依法及时查处。

**Article 65** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, produces or sells unqualified fire products or fire products that are explicitly eliminated by the state shall be given a severe punishment by the product quality supervision department or the administrative department for industry and commerce in accordance with the provisions of the Product Quality Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If a crowded place uses unqualified fire products or fire products explicitly eliminated by the state, the violator shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit; if the corrections are not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than 500 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the violator shall be ordered to suspend production and business operations.  
In the case specified in the second paragraph of this Article, the fire rescue authority shall, in addition to punishing the user according to law, notify the product quality supervision department and the administrative department for industry and commerce of the discovery of unqualified fire products and fire products explicitly eliminated by the state. The product quality supervision department and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall promptly investigate and handle the producers and sellers according to law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　电器产品、燃气用具的安装、使用及其线路、管路的设计、敷设、维护保养、检测不符合消防技术标准和管理规定的，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令停止使用，可以并处一千元以上五千元以下罚款。

**Article 66** If the installation or use of electrical products or gas appliances, or the design, laying, maintenance, or testing of their circuits or pipelines, fails to meet fire protection technical standards and management regulations, the violator shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit; if the corrections are not made within the time limit, the violator shall be ordered to cease use, and may also be fined not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　机关、团体、企业、事业等单位违反[本法第十六条](#art-16)、第十七条、第十八条、第二十一条第二款规定的，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分或者给予警告处罚。

**Article 67** If a state organ, organization, enterprise, institution, or other unit violates the provisions of Article 16, Article 17, Article 18, or the second paragraph of Article 21 of this Law, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit; if the corrections are not made within the time limit, the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons shall be given disciplinary sanctions according to law or be given a warning penalty.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　人员密集场所发生火灾，该场所的现场工作人员不履行组织、引导在场人员疏散的义务，情节严重，尚不构成犯罪的，处五日以上十日以下拘留。

**Article 68** Where a fire occurs in a crowded place, and the on-site staff of such place fail to fulfill their obligation to organize and guide the evacuation of persons present, if the circumstances are serious and the act does not constitute a crime, the violator shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　消防设施维护保养检测、消防安全评估等消防技术服务机构，不具备从业条件从事消防技术服务活动或者出具虚假文件的，由消防救援机构责令改正，处五万元以上十万元以下罚款，并对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以上五万元以下罚款；不按照国家标准、行业标准开展消防技术服务活动的，责令改正，处五万元以下罚款，并对直接负责的主管人员和其它直接责任人员处一万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，并处没收违法所得；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；情节严重的，依法责令停止执业或者吊销相应资格；造成重大损失的，由相关部门吊销营业执照，并对有关责任人员采取终身市场禁入措施。  
前款规定的机构出具失实文件，给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；造成重大损失的，由消防救援机构依法责令停止执业或者吊销相应资格，由相关部门吊销营业执照，并对有关责任人员采取终身市场禁入措施。

**Article 69** If a fire protection technical service institution such as one providing fire protection facility maintenance, testing, or fire safety assessment engages in fire protection technical service activities without meeting the business conditions or issues false documents, the fire rescue authority shall order it to make corrections, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons; if it fails to carry out fire protection technical service activities in accordance with national standards or trade standards, it shall be ordered to make corrections, imposed a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan, and a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to cease practice or have its corresponding qualifications revoked according to law; if major losses are caused, the relevant departments shall revoke its business license, and adopt lifelong market ban measures against the responsible persons.  
If the institution specified in the preceding paragraph issues inaccurate documents, causing losses to others, it shall bear compensation liability according to law; if major losses are caused, the fire rescue authority shall order it to cease practice or revoke the corresponding qualifications according to law, the relevant departments shall revoke its business license, and adopt lifelong market ban measures against the responsible persons.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　本法规定的行政处罚，除应当由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的有关规定决定的外，由住房和城乡建设主管部门、消防救援机构按照各自职权决定。  
被责令停止施工、停止使用、停产停业的，应当在整改后向作出决定的部门或者机构报告，经检查合格，方可恢复施工、使用、生产、经营。  
当事人逾期不执行停产停业、停止使用、停止施工决定的，由作出决定的部门或者机构强制执行。  
责令停产停业，对经济和社会生活影响较大的，由住房和城乡建设主管部门或者应急管理部门报请本级人民政府依法决定。

**Article 70** The administrative penalties provided for in this Law shall, except those that shall be decided by the public security organ in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments, be decided by the competent department of housing and urban-rural development or the fire rescue authority within their respective powers.  
A person who is ordered to stop construction, stop use, or suspend production and business operations shall, after rectification, report to the department or authority that made the decision, and may resume construction, use, production, or business operations only after passing inspection.  
If a party fails to execute the decision of suspending production and business operations, stopping use, or stopping construction within the time limit, the department or authority that made the decision shall carry out compulsory execution.  
If an order to suspend production and business operations has a relatively large impact on the economy and social life, the competent department of housing and urban-rural development or the emergency management department shall submit the matter to the people's government at the same level for a decision according to law.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　住房和城乡建设主管部门、消防救援机构的工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，有下列行为之一，尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分：  
（一）对不符合消防安全要求的消防设计文件、建设工程、场所准予审查合格、消防验收合格、消防安全检查合格的；  
（二）无故拖延消防设计审查、消防验收、消防安全检查，不在法定期限内履行职责的；  
（三）发现火灾隐患不及时通知有关单位或者个人整改的；  
（四）利用职务为用户、建设单位指定或者变相指定消防产品的品牌、销售单位或者消防技术服务机构、消防设施施工单位的；  
（五）将消防车、消防艇以及消防器材、装备和设施用于与消防和应急救援无关的事项的；  
（六）其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的行为。  
产品质量监督、工商行政管理等其他有关行政主管部门的工作人员在消防工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 71** Where a staff member of the competent department of housing and urban-rural development or the fire rescue authority abuses power, neglects duty, or engages in malpractices for personal gain, and commits any of the following acts, if the act does not constitute a crime, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed according to law:  
(1) Approving as qualified fire protection design documents, construction projects, or premises that do not meet fire safety requirements in review, acceptance, or inspection;  
(2) Unjustifiably delaying fire protection design review, fire protection acceptance, or fire safety inspection, or failing to perform duties within the statutory time limit;  
(3) Failing to promptly notify the relevant unit or individual to rectify a discovered fire hazard;  
(4) Using the position to designate or indirectly designate for users or construction entities the brands, sales units of fire protection products, or fire protection technical service institutions, or fire protection facility construction units;  
(5) Using fire trucks, fire boats, and fire equipment, appliances, and facilities for matters unrelated to fire fighting and emergency rescue;  
(6) Other acts of abusing power, neglecting duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain.  
Where a staff member of other relevant administrative departments such as product quality supervision or industry and commerce administration abuses power, neglects duty, or engages in malpractices for personal gain in fire protection work, if the act does not constitute a crime, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 72** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　本法下列用语的含义：  
（一）消防设施，是指火灾自动报警系统、自动灭火系统、消火栓系统、防烟排烟系统以及应急广播和应急照明、安全疏散设施等。  
（二）消防产品，是指专门用于火灾预防、灭火救援和火灾防护、避难、逃生的产品。  
（三）公众聚集场所，是指宾馆、饭店、商场、集贸市场、客运车站候车室、客运码头候船厅、民用机场航站楼、体育场馆、会堂以及公共娱乐场所等。  
（四）人员密集场所，是指公众聚集场所，医院的门诊楼、病房楼，学校的教学楼、图书馆、食堂和集体宿舍，养老院，福利院，托儿所，幼儿园，公共图书馆的阅览室，公共展览馆、博物馆的展示厅，劳动密集型企业的生产加工车间和员工集体宿舍，旅游、宗教活动场所等。

**Article 73** The meanings of the following terms used in this Law:  
(1) "Fire protection facilities" means automatic fire alarm systems, automatic fire extinguishing systems, fire hydrant systems, smoke control and exhaust systems, as well as emergency broadcasting and emergency lighting, and safety evacuation facilities, among others.  
(2) "Fire products" means products specially used for fire prevention, fire fighting and rescue, fire protection, shelter, and escape.  
(3) "Public gathering places" means hotels, restaurants, shopping malls, markets, waiting rooms of passenger bus stations, waiting halls of passenger terminals, terminal buildings of civil airports, stadiums, auditoriums, and public entertainment venues, among others.  
(4) "Crowded places" means public gathering places, outpatient buildings and ward buildings of hospitals, teaching buildings, libraries, canteens, and dormitories of schools, nursing homes, welfare institutions, nurseries, kindergartens, reading rooms of public libraries, exhibition halls of public exhibition halls and museums, production and processing workshops and employee dormitories of labor-intensive enterprises, tourist and religious activity venues, among others.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　本法自2009年5月1日起施行。

**Article 74** This Law shall come into force as of May 1, 2009.
