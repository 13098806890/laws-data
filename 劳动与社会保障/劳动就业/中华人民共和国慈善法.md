# 中华人民共和国慈善法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2023-12-29  
**生效日期**：2024-09-05

> （2016年3月16日第十二届全国人民代表大会第四次会议通过　根据2023年12月29日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第七次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国慈善法〉的决定》修正）

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了发展慈善事业，弘扬慈善文化，规范慈善活动，保护慈善组织、捐赠人、志愿者、受益人等慈善活动参与者的合法权益，促进社会进步，共享发展成果，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted to develop charitable undertakings, promote charitable culture, regulate charitable activities, protect the legitimate rights and interests of participants in charitable activities including charitable organizations, donors, volunteers, and beneficiaries, promote social progress, and share the fruits of development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　自然人、法人和非法人组织开展慈善活动以及与慈善有关的活动，适用本法。其他法律有特别规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to natural persons, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations carrying out charitable activities and activities related to charity. Where other laws provide otherwise, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本法所称慈善活动，是指自然人、法人和非法人组织以捐赠财产或者提供服务等方式，自愿开展的下列公益活动：  
（一）扶贫、济困；  
（二）扶老、救孤、恤病、助残、优抚；  
（三）救助自然灾害、事故灾难和公共卫生事件等突发事件造成的损害；  
（四）促进教育、科学、文化、卫生、体育等事业的发展；  
（五）防治污染和其他公害，保护和改善生态环境；  
（六）符合本法规定的其他公益活动。

**Article 3** For the purposes of this Law, "charitable activities" mean the following public benefit activities voluntarily carried out by natural persons, legal persons, or unincorporated organizations by means of donating property or providing services:  
(1) poverty alleviation and assistance;  
(2) support for the elderly, rescue of orphans, care for the sick, assistance for the disabled, and special care for entitled groups;  
(3) relief of damage caused by emergencies such as natural disasters, accident disasters, and public health incidents;  
(4) promotion of the development of education, science, culture, health, sports, and other undertakings;  
(5) prevention and control of pollution and other public hazards, and protection and improvement of the ecological environment;  
(6) other public benefit activities that comply with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　慈善工作坚持中国共产党的领导。  
开展慈善活动，应当遵循合法、自愿、诚信、非营利的原则，不得违背社会公德，不得危害国家安全、损害社会公共利益和他人合法权益。

**Article 4** Charitable work shall adhere to the leadership of the Chinese Communist Party.  
In carrying out charitable activities, the principles of legality, voluntariness, good faith, and non-profit shall be followed, and no violation of social morality shall be allowed, nor shall any act endanger national security, harm public interests, or infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of others.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家鼓励和支持自然人、法人和非法人组织践行社会主义核心价值观，弘扬中华民族传统美德，依法开展慈善活动。

**Article 5** The State shall encourage and support natural persons, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations to practice socialist core values, promote the traditional virtues of the Chinese nation, and carry out charitable activities according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上人民政府应当统筹、协调、督促和指导有关部门在各自职责范围内做好慈善事业的扶持发展和规范管理工作。  
国务院民政部门主管全国慈善工作，县级以上地方各级人民政府民政部门主管本行政区域内的慈善工作；县级以上人民政府有关部门依照本法和其他有关法律法规，在各自的职责范围内做好相关工作，加强对慈善活动的监督、管理和服务；慈善组织有业务主管单位的，业务主管单位应当对其进行指导、监督。

**Article 6** The people's governments at or above the county level shall coordinate, supervise, and guide the relevant departments to properly carry out the support, development, and regulation of charitable undertakings within their respective functions and duties.  
The civil affairs department of the State Council shall be in charge of charitable work nationwide; the civil affairs departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of charitable work within their respective administrative regions; the relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws and regulations, properly perform related work within their respective functions and duties, and strengthen the supervision, management, and services for charitable activities; where a charitable organization has a competent business authority, such authority shall provide guidance and supervision over the charitable organization.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　每年9月5日为“中华慈善日”。

**Article 7** September 5 of each year shall be the "China Charity Day".

### 第二章　慈善组织

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　本法所称慈善组织，是指依法成立、符合本法规定，以面向社会开展慈善活动为宗旨的非营利性组织。  
慈善组织可以采取基金会、社会团体、社会服务机构等组织形式。

**Article 8** For the purposes of this Law, "charitable organization" means a non-profit organization established according to law, complying with the provisions of this Law, and aiming to carry out charitable activities for the public.  
Charitable organizations may take forms such as foundations, social groups, and social service institutions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　慈善组织应当符合下列条件：  
（一）以开展慈善活动为宗旨；  
（二）不以营利为目的；  
（三）有自己的名称和住所；  
（四）有组织章程；  
（五）有必要的财产；  
（六）有符合条件的组织机构和负责人；  
（七）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 9** A charitable organization shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having the aim of carrying out charitable activities;  
(2) not aiming at profit-making;  
(3) having its own name and domicile;  
(4) having organizational articles of association;  
(5) having necessary property;  
(6) having organizational structure and responsible persons that meet the requirements;  
(7) other conditions provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　设立慈善组织，应当向县级以上人民政府民政部门申请登记，民政部门应当自受理申请之日起三十日内作出决定。符合本法规定条件的，准予登记并向社会公告；不符合本法规定条件的，不予登记并书面说明理由。  
已经设立的基金会、社会团体、社会服务机构等非营利性组织，可以向办理其登记的民政部门申请认定为慈善组织，民政部门应当自受理申请之日起二十日内作出决定。符合慈善组织条件的，予以认定并向社会公告；不符合慈善组织条件的，不予认定并书面说明理由。  
有特殊情况需要延长登记或者认定期限的，报经国务院民政部门批准，可以适当延长，但延长的期限不得超过六十日。

**Article 10** To establish a charitable organization, an application for registration shall be made to the civil affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level, and the civil affairs department shall make a decision within 30 days from the date of accepting the application. If the conditions provided by this Law are met, registration shall be granted and publicly announced; if not, registration shall be denied with a written explanation of reasons.  
An already established non-profit organization such as a foundation, social group, or social service institution may apply to the civil affairs department that handled its registration for recognition as a charitable organization, and the civil affairs department shall make a decision within 20 days from the date of accepting the application. If the conditions for a charitable organization are met, recognition shall be granted and publicly announced; if not, recognition shall be denied with a written explanation of reasons.  
Where special circumstances require an extension of the time limit for registration or recognition, an appropriate extension may be granted upon approval by the civil affairs department of the State Council, but the extended period shall not exceed 60 days.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　慈善组织的章程，应当符合法律法规的规定，并载明下列事项：  
（一）名称和住所；  
（二）组织形式；  
（三）宗旨和活动范围；  
（四）财产来源及构成；  
（五）决策、执行机构的组成及职责；  
（六）内部监督机制；  
（七）财产管理使用制度；  
（八）项目管理制度；  
（九）终止情形及终止后的清算办法；  
（十）其他重要事项。

**Article 11** The articles of association of a charitable organization shall comply with the provisions of laws and regulations and shall include the following matters:  
(1) name and domicile;  
(2) form of organization;  
(3) purpose and scope of activities;  
(4) source and composition of property;  
(5) composition and functions of decision-making and executive bodies;  
(6) internal supervision mechanism;  
(7) system for property management and use;  
(8) project management system;  
(9) circumstances of dissolution and liquidation method after dissolution;  
(10) other important matters.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　慈善组织应当根据法律法规以及章程的规定，建立健全内部治理结构，明确决策、执行、监督等方面的职责权限，开展慈善活动。  
慈善组织应当执行国家统一的会计制度，依法进行会计核算，建立健全会计监督制度，并接受政府有关部门的监督管理。

**Article 12** A charitable organization shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws, regulations, and its articles of association, establish and improve its internal governance structure, clarify the functions and powers in decision-making, execution, and supervision, and carry out charitable activities.  
A charitable organization shall implement the unified national accounting system, conduct accounting according to law, establish and improve the accounting supervision system, and accept supervision and management from relevant government departments.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　慈善组织应当每年向办理其登记的民政部门报送年度工作报告和财务会计报告。报告应当包括年度开展募捐和接受捐赠、慈善财产的管理使用、慈善项目实施、募捐成本、慈善组织工作人员工资福利以及与境外组织或者个人开展合作等情况。

**Article 13** A charitable organization shall submit an annual work report and financial accounting report each year to the civil affairs department that handled its registration. The report shall include information on fundraising and donation acceptance for the year, management and use of charitable property, implementation of charitable projects, fundraising costs, salary and welfare of staff of the charitable organization, and cooperation with foreign organizations or individuals.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　慈善组织的发起人、主要捐赠人以及管理人员，不得利用其关联关系损害慈善组织、受益人的利益和社会公共利益。  
慈善组织的发起人、主要捐赠人以及管理人员与慈善组织发生交易行为的，不得参与慈善组织有关该交易行为的决策，有关交易情况应当向社会公开。

**Article 14** Founders, major donors, and management personnel of a charitable organization shall not use their affiliated relationships to harm the interests of the charitable organization, beneficiaries, or the public interest.  
Where founders, major donors, or management personnel engage in transactions with a charitable organization, they shall not participate in the decision-making of the charitable organization concerning such transactions, and the relevant transaction information shall be disclosed to the public.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　慈善组织不得从事、资助危害国家安全和社会公共利益的活动，不得接受附加违反法律法规和违背社会公德条件的捐赠，不得对受益人附加违反法律法规和违背社会公德的条件。

**Article 15** A charitable organization shall not engage in or fund activities that endanger national security or the public interest, shall not accept donations with conditions that violate laws and regulations or contradict social morality, and shall not impose conditions on beneficiaries that violate laws and regulations or contradict social morality.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　有下列情形之一的，不得担任慈善组织的负责人：  
（一）无民事行为能力或者限制民事行为能力的；  
（二）因故意犯罪被判处刑罚，自刑罚执行完毕之日起未逾五年的；  
（三）在被吊销登记证书或者被取缔的组织担任负责人，自该组织被吊销登记证书或者被取缔之日起未逾五年的；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 16** A person shall not serve as a responsible person of a charitable organization under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) having no civil capacity or having limited civil capacity;  
(2) having been sentenced to a criminal penalty for an intentional crime, and less than five years have elapsed since the completion of the execution of the penalty;  
(3) having served as a responsible person of an organization whose registration certificate was revoked or which was banned, and less than five years have elapsed since the revocation or ban;  
(4) other circumstances provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　慈善组织有下列情形之一的，应当终止：  
（一）出现章程规定的终止情形的；  
（二）因分立、合并需要终止的；  
（三）连续二年未从事慈善活动的；  
（四）依法被撤销登记或者吊销登记证书的；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定应当终止的其他情形。

**Article 17** A charitable organization shall be terminated under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) circumstances of termination specified in its articles of association have occurred;  
(2) termination is necessary due to split or merger;  
(3) it has not engaged in charitable activities for two consecutive years;  
(4) its registration has been revoked or its registration certificate has been cancelled according to law;  
(5) other circumstances under which termination shall occur as provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　慈善组织终止，应当进行清算。  
慈善组织的决策机构应当在[本法第十七条](#art-17)规定的终止情形出现之日起三十日内成立清算组进行清算，并向社会公告。不成立清算组或者清算组不履行职责的，办理其登记的民政部门可以申请人民法院指定有关人员组成清算组进行清算。  
慈善组织清算后的剩余财产，应当按照慈善组织章程的规定转给宗旨相同或者相近的慈善组织；章程未规定的，由办理其登记的民政部门主持转给宗旨相同或者相近的慈善组织，并向社会公告。  
慈善组织清算结束后，应当向办理其登记的民政部门办理注销登记，并由民政部门向社会公告。

**Article 18** When a charitable organization is terminated, it shall carry out liquidation.  
The decision-making body of the charitable organization shall establish a liquidation group within 30 days from the occurrence of the termination circumstances provided in Article 17 of this Law to conduct liquidation and make a public announcement. If no liquidation group is established or if the liquidation group fails to perform its duties, the civil affairs department that handled the registration may apply to the People's Court to appoint relevant persons to form a liquidation group for liquidation.  
The remaining property after liquidation of a charitable organization shall be transferred to a charitable organization with the same or similar purpose in accordance with the provisions of its articles of association; if the articles of association do not provide for it, the civil affairs department that handled the registration shall arrange for the transfer to a charitable organization with the same or similar purpose and make a public announcement.  
After the liquidation is completed, the charitable organization shall go through cancellation of registration with the civil affairs department that handled its registration, and the civil affairs department shall make a public announcement.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　慈善组织依法成立行业组织。  
慈善行业组织应当反映行业诉求，推动行业交流，提高慈善行业公信力，促进慈善事业发展。

**Article 19** Charitable organizations may establish trade organizations according to law.  
Charitable trade organizations shall reflect industry demands, promote industry exchanges, enhance the credibility of the charitable industry, and promote the development of charitable undertakings.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　慈善组织的组织形式、登记管理的具体办法由国务院制定。

**Article 20** The specific measures for the organizational forms and registration management of charitable organizations shall be formulated by the State Council.

### 第三章　慈善募捐

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本法所称慈善募捐，是指慈善组织基于慈善宗旨募集财产的活动。  
慈善募捐，包括面向社会公众的公开募捐和面向特定对象的定向募捐。

**Article 21** For the purposes of this Law, charitable fundraising refers to the activities of charitable organizations to raise property based on charitable purposes.  
Charitable fundraising includes public fundraising targeting the general public and targeted fundraising targeting specific objects.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　慈善组织开展公开募捐，应当取得公开募捐资格。依法登记满一年的慈善组织，可以向办理其登记的民政部门申请公开募捐资格。民政部门应当自受理申请之日起二十日内作出决定。慈善组织符合内部治理结构健全、运作规范的条件的，发给公开募捐资格证书；不符合条件的，不发给公开募捐资格证书并书面说明理由。  
其他法律、行政法规规定可以公开募捐的非营利性组织，由县级以上人民政府民政部门直接发给公开募捐资格证书。

**Article 22** A charitable organization that conducts public fundraising shall obtain the qualification for public fundraising. A charitable organization that has been registered according to law for at least one year may apply to the civil affairs department that handled its registration for the qualification for public fundraising. The civil affairs department shall make a decision within twenty days from the date of accepting the application. If the charitable organization meets the conditions of having a sound internal governance structure and operating in a standardized manner, it shall be issued a certificate of qualification for public fundraising; if it does not meet the conditions, it shall not be issued such a certificate and the reasons shall be stated in writing.  
For non-profit organizations that may conduct public fundraising under other laws and administrative regulations, the civil affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level shall directly issue a certificate of qualification for public fundraising.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　开展公开募捐，可以采取下列方式：  
（一）在公共场所设置募捐箱；  
（二）举办面向社会公众的义演、义赛、义卖、义展、义拍、慈善晚会等；  
（三）通过广播、电视、报刊、互联网等媒体发布募捐信息；  
（四）其他公开募捐方式。  
慈善组织采取前款第一项、第二项规定的方式开展公开募捐的，应当在办理其登记的民政部门管辖区域内进行，确有必要在办理其登记的民政部门管辖区域外进行的，应当报其开展募捐活动所在地的县级以上人民政府民政部门备案。捐赠人的捐赠行为不受地域限制。

**Article 23** Public fundraising may be conducted in the following manners:  
(1) Setting up donation boxes in public places;  
(2) Holding charity performances, charity competitions, charity sales, charity exhibitions, charity auctions, charity galas, etc. targeted at the general public;  
(3) Publishing fundraising information through media such as radio, television, newspapers, and the Internet;  
(4) Other public fundraising methods.  
If a charitable organization conducts public fundraising by means specified in items (1) and (2) of the preceding paragraph, it shall do so within the area under the jurisdiction of the civil affairs department that handled its registration. If it is truly necessary to do so outside such area, it shall report to the civil affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level in the place where the fundraising activity is conducted for archival filing. The donation behavior of donors shall not be subject to geographical restrictions.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　开展公开募捐，应当制定募捐方案。募捐方案包括募捐目的、起止时间和地域、活动负责人姓名和办公地址、接受捐赠方式、银行账户、受益人、募得款物用途、募捐成本、剩余财产的处理等。  
募捐方案应当在开展募捐活动前报慈善组织登记的民政部门备案。

**Article 24** When conducting public fundraising, a fundraising plan shall be formulated. The fundraising plan shall include the purpose of the fundraising, the start and end times and regions, the name and office address of the person in charge of the activity, the method of accepting donations, the bank account, the beneficiaries, the use of the funds and goods raised, the fundraising costs, the disposal of remaining property, etc.  
The fundraising plan shall be submitted to the civil affairs department where the charitable organization is registered for archival filing before the fundraising activity is carried out.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　开展公开募捐，应当在募捐活动现场或者募捐活动载体的显著位置，公布募捐组织名称、公开募捐资格证书、募捐方案、联系方式、募捐信息查询方法等。

**Article 25** When conducting public fundraising, the name of the fundraising organization, the certificate of qualification for public fundraising, the fundraising plan, contact information, methods for inquiring about fundraising information, etc. shall be published at the fundraising activity site or in a prominent position on the carrier of the fundraising activity.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　不具有公开募捐资格的组织或者个人基于慈善目的，可以与具有公开募捐资格的慈善组织合作，由该慈善组织开展公开募捐，合作方不得以任何形式自行开展公开募捐。具有公开募捐资格的慈善组织应当对合作方进行评估，依法签订书面协议，在募捐方案中载明合作方的相关信息，并对合作方的相关行为进行指导和监督。  
具有公开募捐资格的慈善组织负责对合作募得的款物进行管理和会计核算，将全部收支纳入其账户。

**Article 26** An organization or individual that does not have the qualification for public fundraising may, based on charitable purposes, cooperate with a charitable organization that has the qualification for public fundraising, and the charitable organization shall conduct the public fundraising. The cooperating party shall not conduct public fundraising on its own in any form. The charitable organization with the qualification for public fundraising shall evaluate the cooperating party, sign a written agreement according to law, include the relevant information of the cooperating party in the fundraising plan, and provide guidance and supervision over the relevant acts of the cooperating party.  
The charitable organization with the qualification for public fundraising shall be responsible for managing and conducting accounting for the funds and goods raised through cooperation, and shall include all revenues and expenditures into its accounts.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　慈善组织通过互联网开展公开募捐的，应当在国务院民政部门指定的互联网公开募捐服务平台进行，并可以同时在其网站进行。  
国务院民政部门指定的互联网公开募捐服务平台，提供公开募捐信息展示、捐赠支付、捐赠财产使用情况查询等服务；无正当理由不得拒绝为具有公开募捐资格的慈善组织提供服务，不得向其收费，不得在公开募捐信息页面插入商业广告和商业活动链接。

**Article 27** If a charitable organization conducts public fundraising through the Internet, it shall do so on the Internet public fundraising service platform designated by the civil affairs department of the State Council, and may also do so on its own website at the same time.  
The Internet public fundraising service platform designated by the civil affairs department of the State Council shall provide services such as displaying public fundraising information, donation payment, and inquiry into the use of donated property; it shall not refuse to provide services to charitable organizations with the qualification for public fundraising without justifiable reasons, shall not charge fees to them, and shall not insert commercial advertisements or commercial activity links on the pages displaying public fundraising information.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　广播、电视、报刊以及网络服务提供者、电信运营商，应当对利用其平台开展公开募捐的慈善组织的登记证书、公开募捐资格证书进行验证。

**Article 28** Radio stations, television stations, newspapers, and Internet service providers as well as telecommunications operators shall verify the registration certificate and the certificate of qualification for public fundraising of a charitable organization that uses their platform to conduct public fundraising.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　慈善组织自登记之日起可以开展定向募捐。  
慈善组织开展定向募捐，应当在发起人、理事会成员和会员等特定对象的范围内进行，并向募捐对象说明募捐目的、募得款物用途等事项。

**Article 29** A charitable organization may conduct targeted fundraising from the date of its registration.  
When a charitable organization conducts targeted fundraising, it shall do so within the scope of specific objects such as initiators, board members, and members, and shall explain to the fundraising targets the purpose of the fundraising, the use of the funds and goods raised, and other matters.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　开展定向募捐，不得采取或者变相采取[本法第二十三条](#art-23)规定的方式。

**Article 30** When conducting targeted fundraising, the methods specified in Article 23 of this Law shall not be adopted, either directly or in a disguised form.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　开展募捐活动，应当尊重和维护募捐对象的合法权益，保障募捐对象的知情权，不得通过虚构事实等方式欺骗、诱导募捐对象实施捐赠。

**Article 31** When conducting fundraising activities, the legitimate rights and interests of the fundraising targets shall be respected and protected, and the right of the fundraising targets to be informed shall be ensured. No fraud or inducement of the fundraising targets to make donations through fabricating facts or other means shall be allowed.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　开展募捐活动，不得摊派或者变相摊派，不得妨碍公共秩序、企业生产经营和居民生活。

**Article 32** When conducting fundraising activities, no apportionment or disguised apportionment shall be imposed, and no obstruction shall be caused to public order, the production and operation of enterprises, or the life of residents.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　禁止任何组织或者个人假借慈善名义或者假冒慈善组织开展募捐活动，骗取财产。

**Article 33** Any organization or individual is prohibited from carrying out fundraising activities under the guise of charity or by impersonating a charitable organization to defraud property.

### 第四章　慈善捐赠

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本法所称慈善捐赠，是指自然人、法人和非法人组织基于慈善目的，自愿、无偿赠与财产的活动。

**Article 34** For the purposes of this Law, charitable donation refers to the activity in which a natural person, legal person, or unincorporated organization voluntarily and gratuitously gives property based on charitable purposes.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　捐赠人可以通过慈善组织捐赠，也可以直接向受益人捐赠。

**Article 35** A donor may make a donation through a charitable organization or directly to a beneficiary.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　捐赠人捐赠的财产应当是其有权处分的合法财产。捐赠财产包括货币、实物、房屋、有价证券、股权、知识产权等有形和无形财产。  
捐赠人捐赠的实物应当具有使用价值，符合安全、卫生、环保等标准。  
捐赠人捐赠本企业产品的，应当依法承担产品质量责任和义务。

**Article 36** The property donated by a donor shall be legal property over which the donor has the right of disposal. Donated property includes tangible and intangible property such as currency, physical goods, houses, negotiable securities, equity interests, and intellectual property.  
Physical goods donated by a donor shall have use value and comply with safety, hygiene, environmental protection and other standards.  
If a donor donates products of his or her own enterprise, the donor shall bear responsibility and obligations for product quality according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　自然人、法人和非法人组织开展演出、比赛、销售、拍卖等经营性活动，承诺将全部或者部分所得用于慈善目的的，应当在举办活动前与慈善组织或者其他接受捐赠的人签订捐赠协议，活动结束后按照捐赠协议履行捐赠义务，并将捐赠情况向社会公开。

**Article 37** If a natural person, legal person, or unincorporated organization conducts commercial activities such as performances, competitions, sales, or auctions and promises to use all or part of the proceeds for charitable purposes, it shall enter into a donation agreement with a charitable organization or other person accepting the donation before holding the activity, perform the donation obligation in accordance with the donation agreement after the activity concludes, and disclose the donation information to the public.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　慈善组织接受捐赠，应当向捐赠人开具由财政部门统一监（印）制的捐赠票据。捐赠票据应当载明捐赠人、捐赠财产的种类及数量、慈善组织名称和经办人姓名、票据日期等。捐赠人匿名或者放弃接受捐赠票据的，慈善组织应当做好相关记录。

**Article 38** When a charitable organization accepts a donation, it shall issue to the donor a donation receipt uniformly supervised (printed) by the finance department. The donation receipt shall state the donor, the type and quantity of the donated property, the name of the charitable organization and the name of the handler, the date of the receipt, etc. If the donor is anonymous or waives the right to receive the donation receipt, the charitable organization shall keep relevant records.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　慈善组织接受捐赠，捐赠人要求签订书面捐赠协议的，慈善组织应当与捐赠人签订书面捐赠协议。  
书面捐赠协议包括捐赠人和慈善组织名称，捐赠财产的种类、数量、质量、用途、交付时间等内容。

**Article 39** When a charitable organization accepts a donation, if the donor requests the conclusion of a written donation agreement, the charitable organization shall enter into a written donation agreement with the donor.  
The written donation agreement shall include the names of the donor and the charitable organization, the type, quantity, quality, use, and time of delivery of the donated property, and other contents.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　捐赠人与慈善组织约定捐赠财产的用途和受益人时，不得指定或者变相指定捐赠人的利害关系人作为受益人。  
任何组织和个人不得利用慈善捐赠违反法律规定宣传烟草制品，不得利用慈善捐赠以任何方式宣传法律禁止宣传的产品和事项。

**Article 40** When a donor agrees with a charitable organization on the use of the donated property and the beneficiary, the donor shall not designate, directly or in a disguised manner, a person with an interest in the donor as the beneficiary.  
No organization or individual may use charitable donations to advertise tobacco products in violation of legal provisions, or use charitable donations in any way to advertise products or matters that are prohibited from being advertised by law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　捐赠人应当按照捐赠协议履行捐赠义务。捐赠人违反捐赠协议逾期未交付捐赠财产，有下列情形之一的，慈善组织或者其他接受捐赠的人可以要求交付；捐赠人拒不交付的，慈善组织和其他接受捐赠的人可以依法向人民法院申请支付令或者提起诉讼：  
（一）捐赠人通过广播、电视、报刊、互联网等媒体公开承诺捐赠的；  
（二）捐赠财产用于[本法第三条](#art-3)第一项至第三项规定的慈善活动，并签订书面捐赠协议的。  
捐赠人公开承诺捐赠或者签订书面捐赠协议后经济状况显著恶化，严重影响其生产经营或者家庭生活的，经向公开承诺捐赠地或者书面捐赠协议签订地的县级以上人民政府民政部门报告并向社会公开说明情况后，可以不再履行捐赠义务。

**Article 41** A donor shall perform the donation obligation in accordance with the donation agreement. If a donor violates the donation agreement by failing to deliver the donated property upon expiration of the time limit, under any of the following circumstances, the charitable organization or other recipient of the donation may demand delivery; if the donor refuses to deliver, the charitable organization or other recipient of the donation may apply to the People's Court for a payment order or initiate a prosecution according to law:  
(1) the donor has publicly promised to donate through broadcast, television, press, the Internet, or other media; or  
(2) the donated property is used for charitable activities as specified in Items (1) to (3) of Article 3 of this Law, and a written donation agreement has been signed.  
If, after making a public promise to donate or signing a written donation agreement, the donor's economic condition significantly deteriorates, seriously affecting the donor's production, operation, or family life, the donor may no longer perform the donation obligation after reporting to the civil affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level at the place where the public promise to donate was made or the place where the written donation agreement was signed, and publicly explaining the circumstances.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　捐赠人有权查询、复制其捐赠财产管理使用的有关资料，慈善组织应当及时主动向捐赠人反馈有关情况。  
慈善组织违反捐赠协议约定的用途，滥用捐赠财产的，捐赠人有权要求其改正；拒不改正的，捐赠人可以向县级以上人民政府民政部门投诉、举报或者向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 42** A donor has the right to consult and duplicate relevant materials on the management and use of the donated property, and the charitable organization shall timely and proactively provide feedback to the donor on relevant circumstances.  
If a charitable organization violates the purposes agreed upon in the donation agreement and misuses the donated property, the donor has the right to demand correction; if the charitable organization refuses to correct, the donor may file a complaint or report to the civil affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level, or initiate a prosecution in the People's Court.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国有企业实施慈善捐赠应当遵守有关国有资产管理的规定，履行批准和备案程序。

**Article 43** A State-owned enterprise implementing charitable donations shall comply with the relevant provisions on the management of State-owned assets and undergo the approval and filing procedures.

### 第五章　慈善信托

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　本法所称慈善信托属于公益信托，是指委托人基于慈善目的，依法将其财产委托给受托人，由受托人按照委托人意愿以受托人名义进行管理和处分，开展慈善活动的行为。

**Article 44** For the purposes of this Law, a charitable trust belongs to a public interest trust, and refers to the act where the settlor, for charitable purposes, entrusts the settlor's property to a trustee according to law, and the trustee manages and disposes of the property in the trustee's own name in accordance with the settlor's wishes to carry out charitable activities.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　设立慈善信托、确定受托人和监察人，应当采取书面形式。受托人应当在慈善信托文件签订之日起七日内，将相关文件向受托人所在地县级以上人民政府民政部门备案。  
未按照前款规定将相关文件报民政部门备案的，不享受税收优惠。

**Article 45** The establishment of a charitable trust and the appointment of a trustee and a supervisor shall be in written form. The trustee shall, within seven days from the date of signing the charitable trust document, file the relevant documents with the civil affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level at the place where the trustee is located.  
If the relevant documents are not filed with the civil affairs department in accordance with the preceding paragraph, no tax benefits shall be enjoyed.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　慈善信托的委托人不得指定或者变相指定其利害关系人作为受益人。  
慈善信托的受托人确定受益人，应当坚持公开、公平、公正的原则，不得指定或者变相指定受托人及其工作人员的利害关系人作为受益人。

**Article 46** The settlor of a charitable trust shall not designate, directly or indirectly, any interested person of the settlor as a beneficiary.  
When determining beneficiaries, the trustee of a charitable trust shall adhere to the principles of openness, fairness, and impartiality, and shall not designate, directly or indirectly, any interested person of the trustee or its staff as a beneficiary.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　慈善信托的受托人，可以由委托人确定其信赖的慈善组织或者信托公司担任。

**Article 47** The trustee of a charitable trust may be a charitable organization or a trust company trusted by the settlor as determined by the settlor.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　慈善信托的受托人违反信托义务或者难以履行职责的，委托人可以变更受托人。变更后的受托人应当自变更之日起七日内，将变更情况报原备案的民政部门重新备案。

**Article 48** If the trustee of a charitable trust violates the trust obligation or is unable to perform the duties, the settlor may change the trustee. The changed trustee shall, within seven days from the date of change, report the change to the civil affairs department that originally filed the trust for re-filing.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　慈善信托的受托人管理和处分信托财产，应当按照信托目的，恪尽职守，履行诚信、谨慎管理的义务。  
慈善信托的受托人应当根据信托文件和委托人的要求，及时向委托人报告信托事务处理情况、信托财产管理使用情况。慈善信托的受托人应当每年至少一次将信托事务处理情况及财务状况向办理其备案的民政部门报告，并向社会公开。

**Article 49** When managing and disposing of trust property, the trustee of a charitable trust shall, in accordance with the trust purpose, perform the duties with due diligence and fulfill the obligation of honest and prudent management.  
The trustee of a charitable trust shall, in accordance with the trust document and the requirements of the settlor, timely report to the settlor on the handling of trust affairs and the management and use of trust property. The trustee of a charitable trust shall, at least once a year, report to the civil affairs department that handles the filing on the handling of trust affairs and the financial status, and disclose the information to the public.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　慈善信托的委托人根据需要，可以确定信托监察人。  
信托监察人对受托人的行为进行监督，依法维护委托人和受益人的权益。信托监察人发现受托人违反信托义务或者难以履行职责的，应当向委托人报告，并有权以自己的名义向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 50** The settlor of a charitable trust may, as needed, appoint a trust supervisor.  
The trust supervisor shall supervise the conduct of the trustee and safeguard the rights and interests of the settlor and the beneficiary according to law. If the trust supervisor discovers that the trustee violates the trust obligation or is unable to perform the duties, the trust supervisor shall report to the settlor and has the right to initiate a prosecution in the People's Court in the trust supervisor's own name.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　慈善信托的设立、信托财产的管理、信托当事人、信托的终止和清算等事项，本章未规定的，适用本法其他有关规定；本法未规定的，适用《中华人民共和国信托法》的有关规定。

**Article 51** Matters such as the establishment of a charitable trust, the management of trust property, the parties to the trust, and the termination and liquidation of the trust, which are not provided for in this Chapter, shall be governed by other relevant provisions of this Law; if not provided for in this Law, the relevant provisions of the Trust Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply.

### 第六章　慈善财产

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　慈善组织的财产包括：  
（一）发起人捐赠、资助的创始财产；  
（二）募集的财产；  
（三）其他合法财产。

**Article 52** The property of a charitable organization includes:  
(1) initial property donated or subsidized by the founder;  
(2) property raised;  
(3) other lawful property.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　慈善组织的财产应当根据章程和捐赠协议的规定全部用于慈善目的，不得在发起人、捐赠人以及慈善组织成员中分配。  
任何组织和个人不得私分、挪用、截留或者侵占慈善财产。

**Article 53** The property of a charitable organization shall be used entirely for charitable purposes in accordance with the provisions of the articles of association and the donation agreement, and shall not be distributed among the founders, donors, or members of the charitable organization.  
No organization or individual shall privately divide, misappropriate, intercept, or embezzle charitable property.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　慈善组织对募集的财产，应当登记造册，严格管理，专款专用。  
捐赠人捐赠的实物不易储存、运输或者难以直接用于慈善目的的，慈善组织可以依法拍卖或者变卖，所得收入扣除必要费用后，应当全部用于慈善目的。

**Article 54** A charitable organization shall register the raised property in a ledger, strictly manage it, and use it exclusively for designated purposes.  
If the donated property in kind is not easy to store or transport, or is difficult to use directly for charitable purposes, the charitable organization may auction or sell it according to law, and the proceeds, after deducting necessary expenses, shall be used entirely for charitable purposes.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　慈善组织为实现财产保值、增值进行投资的，应当遵循合法、安全、有效的原则，投资取得的收益应当全部用于慈善目的。慈善组织的重大投资方案应当经决策机构组成人员三分之二以上同意。政府资助的财产和捐赠协议约定不得投资的财产，不得用于投资。慈善组织的负责人和工作人员不得在慈善组织投资的企业兼职或者领取报酬。  
前款规定事项的具体办法，由国务院民政部门制定。

**Article 55** If a charitable organization invests for the purpose of preserving and increasing the value of property, it shall follow the principles of legality, safety, and effectiveness, and the income from investment shall be used entirely for charitable purposes. The major investment plan of a charitable organization shall be approved by more than two-thirds of the members of the decision-making body. Property subsidized by the government and property that is agreed in the donation agreement not to be invested shall not be used for investment. The responsible persons and staff of a charitable organization shall not hold a part-time position or receive remuneration in an enterprise invested by the charitable organization.  
The specific measures for the matters provided for in the preceding paragraph shall be formulated by the civil affairs department of the State Council.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　慈善组织开展慈善活动，应当依照法律法规和章程的规定，按照募捐方案或者捐赠协议使用捐赠财产。慈善组织确需变更募捐方案规定的捐赠财产用途的，应当报原备案的民政部门备案；确需变更捐赠协议约定的捐赠财产用途的，应当征得捐赠人同意。

**Article 56** When carrying out charitable activities, a charitable organization shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws, regulations, and its articles of association, use the donated property in accordance with the fundraising plan or donation agreement. If a charitable organization truly needs to change the use of donated property as stipulated in the fundraising plan, it shall report to the civil affairs department that originally filed the plan for filing; if it truly needs to change the use of donated property as agreed in the donation agreement, it shall obtain the consent of the donor.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　慈善组织应当合理设计慈善项目，优化实施流程，降低运行成本，提高慈善财产使用效益。  
慈善组织应当建立项目管理制度，对项目实施情况进行跟踪监督。

**Article 57** A charitable organization shall reasonably design charitable projects, optimize implementation procedures, reduce operating costs, and improve the efficiency of using charitable property.  
A charitable organization shall establish a project management system and track and supervise the implementation of projects.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　慈善项目终止后捐赠财产有剩余的，按照募捐方案或者捐赠协议处理；募捐方案未规定或者捐赠协议未约定的，慈善组织应当将剩余财产用于目的相同或者相近的其他慈善项目，并向社会公开。

**Article 58** If after the termination of a charitable project, there is surplus donated property, it shall be handled in accordance with the fundraising plan or donation agreement; if the fundraising plan does not provide for it or the donation agreement does not stipulate it, the charitable organization shall use the surplus property for other charitable projects with the same or similar purposes, and disclose it to the public.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　慈善组织确定慈善受益人，应当坚持公开、公平、公正的原则，不得指定或者变相指定慈善组织管理人员的利害关系人作为受益人。

**Article 59** When determining charitable beneficiaries, a charitable organization shall adhere to the principles of openness, fairness, and impartiality, and shall not designate, directly or indirectly, any interested person of the management personnel of the charitable organization as a beneficiary.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　慈善组织根据需要可以与受益人签订协议，明确双方权利义务，约定慈善财产的用途、数额和使用方式等内容。  
受益人应当珍惜慈善资助，按照协议使用慈善财产。受益人未按照协议使用慈善财产或者有其他严重违反协议情形的，慈善组织有权要求其改正；受益人拒不改正的，慈善组织有权解除协议并要求受益人返还财产。

**Article 60** A charitable organization may, as needed, sign an agreement with the beneficiary to specify the rights and obligations of both parties, and to stipulate the purpose, amount, and manner of use of the charitable property, among other matters.  
The beneficiary shall cherish the charitable assistance and use the charitable property in accordance with the agreement. If the beneficiary fails to use the charitable property in accordance with the agreement or there are other serious circumstances of violating the agreement, the charitable organization has the right to demand correction; if the beneficiary refuses to correct, the charitable organization has the right to terminate the agreement and demand the beneficiary to return the property.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　慈善组织应当积极开展慈善活动，遵循管理费用、募捐成本等最必要原则，厉行节约，减少不必要的开支，充分、高效运用慈善财产。具有公开募捐资格的基金会开展慈善活动的年度支出，不得低于上一年总收入的百分之七十或者前三年收入平均数额的百分之七十；年度管理费用不得超过当年总支出的百分之十；特殊情况下，年度支出和管理费用难以符合前述规定的，应当报告办理其登记的民政部门并向社会公开说明情况。  
慈善组织开展慈善活动的年度支出、管理费用和募捐成本的标准由国务院民政部门会同财政、税务等部门制定。  
捐赠协议对单项捐赠财产的慈善活动支出和管理费用有约定的，按照其约定。  
慈善信托的年度支出和管理费用标准，由国务院民政部门会同财政、税务和金融监督管理等部门制定。

**Article 61** A charitable organization shall actively carry out charitable activities, follow the most necessary principle of administrative expenses and fundraising costs, practice strict economy, reduce unnecessary expenses, and fully and efficiently use charitable property. For a foundation with public fundraising qualifications, its annual expenditures on charitable activities shall not be less than seventy percent of its total revenue of the previous year or seventy percent of the average revenue of the preceding three years; its annual administrative expenses shall not exceed ten percent of the total annual expenditures; under special circumstances, if it is difficult to comply with the aforesaid provisions on annual expenditures and administrative expenses, it shall report to the civil affairs department where it is registered and publicly explain the situation.  
  
The standards for annual expenditures, administrative expenses, and fundraising costs of charitable organizations in carrying out charitable activities shall be formulated by the civil affairs department of the State Council jointly with the finance, taxation, and other departments.  
  
Where a donation agreement stipulates the charitable activity expenditures and administrative expenses for a single donated property, such stipulations shall prevail.  
  
The standards for annual expenditures and administrative expenses of charitable trusts shall be formulated by the civil affairs department of the State Council jointly with the finance, taxation, financial supervision and management, and other departments.

### 第七章　慈善服务

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　本法所称慈善服务，是指慈善组织和其他组织以及个人基于慈善目的，向社会或者他人提供的志愿无偿服务以及其他非营利服务。  
慈善组织开展慈善服务，可以自己提供或者招募志愿者提供，也可以委托有服务专长的其他组织提供。

**Article 62** For the purposes of this Law, charitable services refer to voluntary and free services and other non-profit services provided to the public or other persons by charitable organizations, other organizations, and individuals based on charitable purposes.  
  
A charitable organization may provide charitable services by itself or by recruiting volunteers, or may entrust other organizations with specialized expertise to provide such services.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　开展慈善服务，应当尊重受益人、志愿者的人格尊严，不得侵害受益人、志愿者的隐私。

**Article 63** When carrying out charitable services, the personal dignity of beneficiaries and volunteers shall be respected, and the privacy of beneficiaries and volunteers shall not be infringed upon.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　开展医疗康复、教育培训等慈善服务，需要专门技能的，应当执行国家或者行业组织制定的标准和规程。  
慈善组织招募志愿者参与慈善服务，需要专门技能的，应当对志愿者开展相关培训。

**Article 64** Where charitable services such as medical rehabilitation, education, and training require specialized skills, the standards and procedures formulated by the state or industry organizations shall be complied with.  
  
Where a charitable organization recruits volunteers to participate in charitable services that require specialized skills, it shall provide relevant training to the volunteers.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　慈善组织招募志愿者参与慈善服务，应当公示与慈善服务有关的全部信息，告知服务过程中可能发生的风险。  
慈善组织根据需要可以与志愿者签订协议，明确双方权利义务，约定服务的内容、方式和时间等。

**Article 65** A charitable organization that recruits volunteers to participate in charitable services shall disclose all information related to the charitable services and inform them of the risks that may occur during the provision of the services.  
  
A charitable organization may, as needed, sign an agreement with volunteers to clarify the rights and obligations of both parties and stipulate the content, manner, and duration of the services, among other matters.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　慈善组织应当对志愿者实名登记，记录志愿者的服务时间、内容、评价等信息。根据志愿者的要求，慈善组织应当无偿、如实出具志愿服务记录证明。

**Article 66** A charitable organization shall register volunteers with their real names and record information such as the service time, content, and evaluation of volunteers. At the request of volunteers, the charitable organization shall provide, free of charge and truthfully, a certificate of volunteer service records.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　慈善组织安排志愿者参与慈善服务，应当与志愿者的年龄、文化程度、技能和身体状况相适应。

**Article 67** When a charitable organization arranges for volunteers to participate in charitable services, it shall be appropriate to the volunteers' age, educational level, skills, and physical condition.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　志愿者接受慈善组织安排参与慈善服务的，应当服从管理，接受必要的培训。

**Article 68** Volunteers who participate in charitable services arranged by a charitable organization shall comply with management and receive necessary training.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　慈善组织应当为志愿者参与慈善服务提供必要条件，保障志愿者的合法权益。  
慈善组织安排志愿者参与可能发生人身危险的慈善服务前，应当为志愿者购买相应的人身意外伤害保险。

**Article 69** A charitable organization shall provide necessary conditions for volunteers to participate in charitable services and protect the legitimate rights and interests of volunteers.  
  
Before arranging volunteers to participate in charitable services that may involve personal danger, the charitable organization shall purchase corresponding personal accident insurance for volunteers.

### 第八章　应急慈善

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　发生重大突发事件需要迅速开展救助时，履行统一领导职责或者组织处置突发事件的人民政府应当依法建立协调机制，明确专门机构、人员，提供需求信息，及时有序引导慈善组织、志愿者等社会力量开展募捐和救助活动。

**Article 70** When a major emergency occurs requiring rapid rescue operations, the people's government that performs the duty of unified leadership or organizes the handling of the emergency shall, in accordance with law, establish a coordination mechanism, designate special institutions and personnel, provide demand information, and promptly and orderly guide charitable organizations, volunteers, and other social forces to carry out fundraising and rescue activities.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　国家鼓励慈善组织、慈善行业组织建立应急机制，加强信息共享、协商合作，提高慈善组织运行和慈善资源使用的效率。  
在发生重大突发事件时，鼓励慈善组织、志愿者等在有关人民政府的协调引导下依法开展或者参与慈善活动。

**Article 71** The State encourages charitable organizations and charitable industry organizations to establish emergency response mechanisms, strengthen information sharing, consultation, and cooperation, and improve the efficiency of charitable organization operations and the use of charitable resources.  
  
When a major emergency occurs, charitable organizations, volunteers, and others are encouraged to carry out or participate in charitable activities in accordance with law under the coordination and guidance of the relevant people's government.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　为应对重大突发事件开展公开募捐的，应当及时分配或者使用募得款物，在应急处置与救援阶段至少每五日公开一次募得款物的接收情况，及时公开分配、使用情况。

**Article 72** Where public fundraising is conducted in response to a major emergency, the raised funds and materials shall be distributed or used promptly. During the emergency response and rescue phase, the receipt of the raised funds and materials shall be disclosed at least once every five days, and the distribution and use shall be disclosed in a timely manner.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　为应对重大突发事件开展公开募捐，无法在募捐活动前办理募捐方案备案的，应当在活动开始后十日内补办备案手续。

**Article 73** Where public fundraising is conducted in response to a major emergency and the filing of the fundraising plan cannot be completed before the fundraising activity, the filing procedures shall be completed within ten days after the start of the activity.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当为捐赠款物分配送达提供便利条件。乡级人民政府、街道办事处和村民委员会、居民委员会，应当为捐赠款物分配送达、信息统计等提供力所能及的帮助。

**Article 74** The people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall provide convenient conditions for the distribution and delivery of donated funds and materials. The people's governments at the township level, sub-district offices, villagers' committees, and residents' committees shall provide all possible assistance for the distribution and delivery of donated funds and materials, information statistics, and other matters.

### 第九章　信息公开

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　国家建立健全慈善信息统计和发布制度。  
国务院民政部门建立健全统一的慈善信息平台，免费提供慈善信息发布服务。  
县级以上人民政府民政部门应当在前款规定的平台及时向社会公开慈善信息。  
慈善组织和慈善信托的受托人应当在本条第二款规定的平台发布慈善信息，并对信息的真实性负责。

**Article 75** The State shall establish and improve the statistical and publication system for charitable information.  
  
The civil affairs department of the State Council shall establish and improve a unified charitable information platform and provide free charitable information publication services.  
  
The civil affairs departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall timely disclose charitable information to the public on the platform provided for in the preceding paragraph.  
  
Charitable organizations and trustees of charitable trusts shall publish charitable information on the platform provided for in the second paragraph of this Article, and shall be responsible for the authenticity of the information.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　县级以上人民政府民政部门和其他有关部门应当及时向社会公开下列慈善信息：  
（一）慈善组织登记事项；  
（二）慈善信托备案事项；  
（三）具有公开募捐资格的慈善组织名单；  
（四）具有出具公益性捐赠税前扣除票据资格的慈善组织名单；  
（五）对慈善活动的税收优惠、资助补贴等促进措施；  
（六）向慈善组织购买服务的信息；  
（七）对慈善组织、慈善信托开展检查、评估的结果；  
（八）对慈善组织和其他组织以及个人的表彰、处罚结果；  
（九）法律法规规定应当公开的其他信息。

**Article 76** The civil affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall timely disclose the following charitable information to the public:  
  
(1) Registration information of charitable organizations;  
(2) Filing information of charitable trusts;  
(3) List of charitable organizations with public fundraising qualifications;  
(4) List of charitable organizations with qualifications to issue tax-deductible receipts for public welfare donations;  
(5) Promotion measures such as tax preferences and subsidies for charitable activities;  
(6) Information on purchasing services from charitable organizations;  
(7) Results of inspections and evaluations of charitable organizations and charitable trusts;  
(8) Results of commendations and penalties for charitable organizations, other organizations, and individuals;  
(9) Other information that shall be disclosed in accordance with laws and regulations.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　慈善组织、慈善信托的受托人应当依法履行信息公开义务。信息公开应当真实、完整、及时。

**Article 77** Charitable organizations and trustees of charitable trusts shall perform the obligation of information disclosure in accordance with law. Information disclosure shall be truthful, complete, and timely.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　慈善组织应当向社会公开组织章程和决策、执行、监督机构成员信息以及国务院民政部门要求公开的其他信息。上述信息有重大变更的，慈善组织应当及时向社会公开。  
慈善组织应当每年向社会公开其年度工作报告和财务会计报告。具有公开募捐资格的慈善组织的财务会计报告须经审计。

**Article 78** A charitable organization shall disclose to the public its articles of association, information on members of its decision-making, execution, and supervision bodies, and other information required to be disclosed by the civil affairs department of the State Council. If there is any major change in the aforesaid information, the charitable organization shall timely disclose it to the public.  
  
A charitable organization shall annually disclose its annual work report and financial accounting report to the public. The financial accounting report of a charitable organization with public fundraising qualifications shall be audited.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　具有公开募捐资格的慈善组织应当定期向社会公开其募捐情况和慈善项目实施情况。  
公开募捐周期超过六个月的，至少每三个月公开一次募捐情况，公开募捐活动结束后三个月内应当全面、详细公开募捐情况。  
慈善项目实施周期超过六个月的，至少每三个月公开一次项目实施情况，项目结束后三个月内应当全面、详细公开项目实施情况和募得款物使用情况。

**Article 79** A charitable organization with public fundraising qualifications shall periodically disclose its fundraising status and the implementation status of charitable projects to the public.  
  
Where the public fundraising period exceeds six months, the fundraising status shall be disclosed at least once every three months, and within three months after the end of the public fundraising activity, the fundraising status shall be disclosed comprehensively and in detail.  
  
Where the implementation period of a charitable project exceeds six months, the project implementation status shall be disclosed at least once every three months, and within three months after the end of the project, the project implementation status and the use of the raised funds and materials shall be disclosed comprehensively and in detail.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　慈善组织开展定向募捐的，应当及时向捐赠人告知募捐情况、募得款物的管理使用情况。

**Article 80** Where a charitable organization conducts targeted fundraising, it shall promptly inform donors of the fundraising status and the management and use of the raised funds and materials.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　慈善组织、慈善信托的受托人应当向受益人告知其资助标准、工作流程和工作规范等信息。

**Article 81** The trustee of a charitable organization or a charitable trust shall inform the beneficiary of such information as the funding criteria, workflow, and work specifications.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　涉及国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私的信息以及捐赠人、慈善信托的委托人不同意公开的姓名、名称、住所、通讯方式等信息，不得公开。

**Article 82** Information involving State secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy, as well as information such as names, titles, addresses, and contact methods that a donor or the settlor of a charitable trust has not consented to disclose, shall not be disclosed.

### 第十章　促进措施

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　县级以上人民政府应当将慈善事业纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，制定促进慈善事业发展的政策和措施。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门应当在各自职责范围内，向慈善组织、慈善信托受托人等提供慈善需求信息，为慈善活动提供指导和帮助。

**Article 83** The people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate charitable undertakings into the national economic and social development plans and formulate policies and measures to promote the development of charitable undertakings.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, within the scope of their respective duties, provide information on charitable needs to charitable organizations and trustees of charitable trusts, and provide guidance and assistance for charitable activities.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　县级以上人民政府民政部门应当建立与其他部门之间的慈善信息共享机制。

**Article 84** The civil affairs departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a charitable information sharing mechanism with other departments.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　国家鼓励、引导、支持有意愿有能力的自然人、法人和非法人组织积极参与慈善事业。  
国家对慈善事业实施税收优惠政策，具体办法由国务院财政、税务部门会同民政部门依照税收法律、行政法规的规定制定。

**Article 85** The State encourages, guides, and supports natural persons, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations that are willing and capable to actively participate in charitable undertakings.  
The State implements preferential tax policies for charitable undertakings, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the finance and tax departments of the State Council together with the civil affairs departments in accordance with tax laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　慈善组织及其取得的收入依法享受税收优惠。

**Article 86** Charitable organizations and the income they obtain shall enjoy tax preferences according to law.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　自然人、法人和非法人组织捐赠财产用于慈善活动的，依法享受税收优惠。企业慈善捐赠支出超过法律规定的准予在计算企业所得税应纳税所得额时当年扣除的部分，允许结转以后三年内在计算应纳税所得额时扣除。  
境外捐赠用于慈善活动的物资，依法减征或者免征进口关税和进口环节增值税。

**Article 87** Natural persons, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations that donate property for charitable activities shall enjoy tax preferences according to law. Where the charitable donation expenditure of an enterprise exceeds the portion allowed by law to be deducted in the current year when calculating the taxable income for enterprise income tax, the excess may be carried over and deducted in the calculation of taxable income within the following three years.  
Materials donated from overseas for charitable activities shall be subject to reduced or exempted import customs duties and import value-added tax according to law.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　自然人、法人和非法人组织设立慈善信托开展慈善活动的，依法享受税收优惠。

**Article 88** Natural persons, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations that establish charitable trusts to carry out charitable activities shall enjoy tax preferences according to law.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　受益人接受慈善捐赠，依法享受税收优惠。

**Article 89** Beneficiaries who accept charitable donations shall enjoy tax preferences according to law.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　慈善组织、捐赠人、受益人依法享受税收优惠的，有关部门应当及时办理相关手续。

**Article 90** Where charitable organizations, donors, and beneficiaries enjoy tax preferences according to law, the relevant departments shall handle the relevant procedures in a timely manner.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　捐赠人向慈善组织捐赠实物、有价证券、股权和知识产权的，依法免征权利转让的相关行政事业性费用。

**Article 91** Where a donor donates physical goods, securities, equity, or intellectual property to a charitable organization, the relevant administrative and institutional fees for the transfer of rights shall be exempted according to law.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　国家对开展扶贫济困、参与重大突发事件应对、参与重大国家战略的慈善活动，实行特殊的优惠政策。

**Article 92** The State shall implement special preferential policies for charitable activities that involve poverty alleviation and assistance, participation in response to major emergencies, and participation in major national strategies.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　慈善组织开展[本法第三条](#art-3)第一项、第二项规定的慈善活动需要慈善服务设施用地的，可以依法申请使用国有划拨土地或者农村集体建设用地。慈善服务设施用地非经法定程序不得改变用途。

**Article 93** Where a charitable organization needs land for charitable service facilities to carry out charitable activities specified in Items (1) and (2) of Article 3 of this Law, it may apply according to law for the use of state-allocated land or rural collective construction land. The land for charitable service facilities shall not have its use changed without statutory procedures.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　国家为慈善事业提供金融政策支持，鼓励金融机构为慈善组织、慈善信托提供融资和结算等金融服务。

**Article 94** The State provides financial policy support for charitable undertakings and encourages financial institutions to provide financial services such as financing and settlement for charitable organizations and charitable trusts.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　各级人民政府及其有关部门可以依法通过购买服务等方式，支持符合条件的慈善组织向社会提供服务，并依照有关政府采购的法律法规向社会公开相关情况。  
国家鼓励在慈善领域应用现代信息技术；鼓励社会力量通过公益创投、孵化培育、人员培训、项目指导等方式，为慈善组织提供资金支持和能力建设服务。

**Article 95** The people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments may, according to law, support charitable organizations that meet the conditions to provide services to the public by means such as purchasing services, and shall disclose relevant information to the public in accordance with laws and regulations on government procurement.  
The State encourages the application of modern information technology in the charity field; encourages social forces to provide financial support and capacity-building services for charitable organizations through public venture capital, incubation, personnel training, project guidance, and other means.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　国家鼓励有条件的地方设立社区慈善组织，加强社区志愿者队伍建设，发展社区慈善事业。

**Article 96** The State encourages areas with conditions to establish community charitable organizations, strengthen the building of community volunteer teams, and develop community charitable undertakings.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　国家采取措施弘扬慈善文化，培育公民慈善意识。  
学校等教育机构应当将慈善文化纳入教育教学内容。国家鼓励高等学校培养慈善专业人才，支持高等学校和科研机构开展慈善理论研究。  
广播、电视、报刊、互联网等媒体应当积极开展慈善公益宣传活动，普及慈善知识，传播慈善文化。

**Article 97** The State takes measures to promote the culture of charity and cultivate citizens' awareness of charity.  
Schools and other educational institutions shall incorporate charitable culture into the content of education and teaching. The State encourages institutions of higher education to cultivate charitable professionals and supports institutions of higher education and scientific research institutions in conducting theoretical research on charity.  
Broadcasting, television, newspapers, the Internet, and other media shall actively carry out publicity activities for charitable public welfare, popularize charitable knowledge, and disseminate charitable culture.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　国家鼓励企业事业单位和其他组织为开展慈善活动提供场所和其他便利条件。

**Article 98** The State encourages enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations to provide venues and other conveniences for carrying out charitable activities.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　经受益人同意，捐赠人对其捐赠的慈善项目可以冠名纪念，法律法规规定需要批准的，从其规定。

**Article 99** With the consent of the beneficiary, a donor may name a charitable project that it has donated to for commemoration; if approval is required by laws or regulations, the provisions thereof shall prevail.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　国家建立慈善表彰制度，对在慈善事业发展中做出突出贡献的自然人、法人和非法人组织，由县级以上人民政府或者有关部门予以表彰。

**Article 100** The State establishes a charitable commendation system. Natural persons, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations that have made outstanding contributions to the development of charitable undertakings shall be commended by the people's governments at or above the county level or relevant departments.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　县级以上人民政府民政等有关部门将慈善捐赠、志愿服务记录等信息纳入相关主体信用记录，健全信用激励制度。

**Article 101** The civil affairs departments and other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate information such as charitable donation and volunteer service records into the credit records of relevant entities, and improve the credit incentive system.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　国家鼓励开展慈善国际交流与合作。  
慈善组织接受境外慈善捐赠、与境外组织或者个人合作开展慈善活动的，根据国家有关规定履行批准、备案程序。

**Article 102** The State encourages international exchanges and cooperation in charity.  
Where a charitable organization accepts charitable donations from abroad or cooperates with foreign organizations or individuals to carry out charitable activities, it shall go through the approval and filing procedures in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第十一章　监督管理

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　县级以上人民政府民政部门应当依法履行职责，对慈善活动进行监督检查，对慈善行业组织进行指导。

**Article 103** The civil affairs departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall perform their duties according to law, supervise and inspect charitable activities, and provide guidance to charitable industry organizations.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　县级以上人民政府民政部门对涉嫌违反本法规定的慈善组织、慈善信托的受托人，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）对慈善组织、慈善信托的受托人的住所和慈善活动发生地进行现场检查；  
（二）要求慈善组织、慈善信托的受托人作出说明，查阅、复制有关资料；  
（三）向与慈善活动有关的单位和个人调查与监督管理有关的情况；  
（四）经本级人民政府批准，可以查询慈善组织的金融账户；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他措施。  
慈善组织、慈善信托的受托人涉嫌违反本法规定的，县级以上人民政府民政部门可以对有关负责人进行约谈，要求其说明情况、提出改进措施。  
其他慈善活动参与者涉嫌违反本法规定的，县级以上人民政府民政部门可以会同有关部门调查和处理。

**Article 104** The civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level shall have the authority to take the following measures against a charitable organization or a trustee of a charitable trust suspected of violating the provisions of this Law:  
(1) Conduct on-site inspections of the domicile of the charitable organization or trustee of the charitable trust and the place where charitable activities occur;  
(2) Require the charitable organization or trustee of the charitable trust to make explanations, and inspect and copy relevant materials;  
(3) Investigate the relevant circumstances relating to supervision and administration from entities and individuals involved in charitable activities;  
(4) With the approval of the people's government at the same level, may inquire into the financial accounts of the charitable organization;  
(5) Other measures provided for by laws and administrative regulations.  
Where a charitable organization or trustee of a charitable trust is suspected of violating the provisions of this Law, the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level may interview the relevant responsible persons, requiring them to explain the situation and propose improvement measures.  
Where other participants in charitable activities are suspected of violating the provisions of this Law, the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level may, together with the relevant departments, investigate and handle the matter.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　县级以上人民政府民政部门对慈善组织、有关单位和个人进行检查或者调查时，检查人员或者调查人员不得少于二人，并应当出示合法证件和检查、调查通知书。

**Article 105** When the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level conducts an inspection or investigation of a charitable organization, relevant entities, or individuals, there shall be no fewer than two inspectors or investigators, and they shall present their lawful credentials and the inspection or investigation notice.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　县级以上人民政府民政部门应当建立慈善组织及其负责人、慈善信托的受托人信用记录制度，并向社会公布。  
县级以上人民政府民政部门应当建立慈善组织评估制度，鼓励和支持第三方机构对慈善组织的内部治理、财务状况、项目开展情况以及信息公开等进行评估，并向社会公布评估结果。

**Article 106** The civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level shall establish a credit record system for charitable organizations and their responsible persons, and trustees of charitable trusts, and shall make such records known to the public.  
The civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level shall establish an evaluation system for charitable organizations, encourage and support third-party institutions to evaluate the internal governance, financial status, project implementation, and information disclosure of charitable organizations, and shall make the evaluation results known to the public.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　慈善行业组织应当建立健全行业规范，加强行业自律。

**Article 107** Charitable industry organizations shall establish and improve industry norms and strengthen self-regulation of the industry.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　任何单位和个人发现慈善组织、慈善信托有违法行为的，可以向县级以上人民政府民政部门、其他有关部门或者慈善行业组织投诉、举报。民政部门、其他有关部门或者慈善行业组织接到投诉、举报后，应当及时调查处理。  
国家鼓励公众、媒体对慈善活动进行监督，对假借慈善名义或者假冒慈善组织骗取财产以及慈善组织、慈善信托的违法违规行为予以曝光，发挥舆论和社会监督作用。

**Article 108** Any entity or individual that discovers any illegal act by a charitable organization or charitable trust may file a complaint or report with the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level, other relevant departments, or a charitable industry organization. The civil affairs department, other relevant departments, or the charitable industry organization shall promptly investigate and handle the matter after receiving the complaint or report.  
The State encourages the public and the media to supervise charitable activities, expose the acts of using the name of charity or counterfeiting charitable organizations to defraud property, as well as the illegal or non-compliant acts of charitable organizations and charitable trusts, thereby exerting the supervisory role of public opinion and society.

### 第十二章　法律责任

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　慈善组织有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府民政部门责令限期改正，予以警告或者责令限期停止活动，并没收违法所得；情节严重的，吊销登记证书并予以公告：  
（一）未按照慈善宗旨开展活动的；  
（二）私分、挪用、截留或者侵占慈善财产的；  
（三）接受附加违反法律法规或者违背社会公德条件的捐赠，或者对受益人附加违反法律法规或者违背社会公德的条件的。

**Article 109** Where a charitable organization falls under any of the following circumstances, the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit, give it a warning or order it to suspend activities within a time limit, and confiscate its illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, its registration certificate shall be revoked and a public announcement shall be made:  
(1) It fails to carry out activities in accordance with the purposes of charity;  
(2) It privately divides, misappropriates, withholds, or encroaches upon charitable property;  
(3) It accepts donations that are subject to conditions that violate laws, regulations, or social morality, or imposes conditions on beneficiaries that violate laws, regulations, or social morality.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　慈善组织有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府民政部门责令限期改正，予以警告，并没收违法所得；逾期不改正的，责令限期停止活动并进行整改：  
（一）违反[本法第十四条](#art-14)规定造成慈善财产损失的；  
（二）指定或者变相指定捐赠人、慈善组织管理人员的利害关系人作为受益人的；  
（三）将不得用于投资的财产用于投资的；  
（四）擅自改变捐赠财产用途的；  
（五）因管理不善造成慈善财产重大损失的；  
（六）开展慈善活动的年度支出、管理费用或者募捐成本违反规定的；  
（七）未依法履行信息公开义务的；  
（八）未依法报送年度工作报告、财务会计报告或者报备募捐方案的；  
（九）泄露捐赠人、志愿者、受益人个人隐私以及捐赠人、慈善信托的委托人不同意公开的姓名、名称、住所、通讯方式等信息的。  
慈善组织违反本法规定泄露国家秘密、商业秘密的，依照有关法律的规定予以处罚。  
慈善组织有前两款规定的情形，经依法处理后一年内再出现前款规定的情形，或者有其他情节严重情形的，由县级以上人民政府民政部门吊销登记证书并予以公告。

**Article 110** Where a charitable organization falls under any of the following circumstances, the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit, give it a warning, and confiscate its illegal gains; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend activities within a time limit and undergo rectification:  
(1) It causes losses to charitable property by violating the provisions of Article 14 of this Law;  
(2) It designates or designates in disguised form interested parties of the donor or the managerial personnel of the charitable organization as beneficiaries;  
(3) It uses property that is not allowed to be invested for investment;  
(4) It changes the use of donated property without authorization;  
(5) It causes significant losses to charitable property due to poor management;  
(6) The annual expenditure, administrative expenses, or fundraising costs for carrying out charitable activities violate the provisions;  
(7) It fails to perform the obligation of information disclosure according to law;  
(8) It fails to submit the annual work report, financial accounting report, or file the fundraising plan according to law;  
(9) It discloses the personal privacy of donors, volunteers, or beneficiaries, or information such as names, titles, addresses, or contact methods of donors or settlors of charitable trusts who do not agree to disclosure.  
Where a charitable organization violates the provisions of this Law by disclosing State secrets or trade secrets, it shall be punished in accordance with the relevant laws.  
Where a charitable organization falls under the circumstances provided for in the preceding two paragraphs, and within one year after being dealt with according to law, it again commits any of the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, or where there are other serious circumstances, the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level shall revoke its registration certificate and make a public announcement.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　慈善组织开展募捐活动有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府民政部门予以警告，责令停止募捐活动；责令退还违法募集的财产，无法退还的，由民政部门予以收缴，转给其他慈善组织用于慈善目的；情节严重的，吊销公开募捐资格证书或者登记证书并予以公告，公开募捐资格证书被吊销的，五年内不得再次申请：  
（一）通过虚构事实等方式欺骗、诱导募捐对象实施捐赠的；  
（二）向单位或者个人摊派或者变相摊派的；  
（三）妨碍公共秩序、企业生产经营或者居民生活的；  
（四）与不具有公开募捐资格的组织或者个人合作，违反[本法第二十六条](#art-26)规定的；  
（五）通过互联网开展公开募捐，违反[本法第二十七条](#art-27)规定的；  
（六）为应对重大突发事件开展公开募捐，不及时分配、使用募得款物的。

**Article 111** Where a charitable organization carries out fundraising activities under any of the following circumstances, the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level shall give it a warning and order it to stop the fundraising activities; order it to return the illegally raised property, and if it cannot be returned, the civil affairs department shall confiscate it and transfer it to other charitable organizations for charitable purposes; if the circumstances are serious, the public fundraising qualification certificate or registration certificate shall be revoked and a public announcement shall be made, and if the public fundraising qualification certificate is revoked, no new application shall be made within five years:  
(1) It deceives or induces the fundraising targets to make donations by fabricating facts or other means;  
(2) It apportions or apportions in disguised form to entities or individuals;  
(3) It interferes with public order, the production and operation of enterprises, or the daily life of residents;  
(4) It cooperates with organizations or individuals that do not have public fundraising qualifications, violating the provisions of Article 26 of this Law;  
(5) It conducts public fundraising through the Internet, violating the provisions of Article 27 of this Law;  
(6) It conducts public fundraising in response to major emergencies but fails to distribute or use the raised funds or materials in a timely manner.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　慈善组织有[本法第一百零九条](#art-109)、第一百一十条、第一百一十一条规定情形的，由县级以上人民政府民政部门对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处二万元以上二十万元以下罚款，并没收违法所得；情节严重的，禁止其一年至五年内担任慈善组织的管理人员。

**Article 112** Where a charitable organization falls under the circumstances specified in Articles 109, 110, or 111 of this Law, the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level shall impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 200,000 on the directly responsible managers and other directly liable persons, and confiscate their illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be prohibited from serving as managers of charitable organizations for one to five years.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　不具有公开募捐资格的组织或者个人擅自开展公开募捐的，由县级以上人民政府民政部门予以警告，责令停止募捐活动；责令退还违法募集的财产，无法退还的，由民政部门予以收缴，转给慈善组织用于慈善目的；情节严重的，对有关组织或者个人处二万元以上二十万元以下罚款。  
自然人、法人或者非法人组织假借慈善名义或者假冒慈善组织骗取财产的，由公安机关依法查处。

**Article 113** Where an organization or individual that does not have public fundraising qualifications carries out public fundraising without authorization, the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level shall give it a warning and order it to stop the fundraising activities; order it to return the illegally raised property, and if it cannot be returned, the civil affairs department shall confiscate it and transfer it to charitable organizations for charitable purposes; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 200,000 shall be imposed on the relevant organization or individual.  
Where a natural person, legal person, or unincorporated organization uses the name of charity or counterfeits a charitable organization to defraud property, the public security organ shall investigate and deal with it according to law.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　互联网公开募捐服务平台违反[本法第二十七条](#art-27)规定的，由省级以上人民政府民政部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，由国务院民政部门取消指定。  
未经指定的互联网信息服务提供者擅自提供互联网公开募捐服务的，由县级以上人民政府民政部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，由县级以上人民政府民政部门会同网信、工业和信息化部门依法进行处理。  
广播、电视、报刊以及网络服务提供者、电信运营商未依法履行验证义务的，由其主管部门责令限期改正，予以警告；逾期不改正的，予以通报批评。

**Article 114** Where an Internet public fundraising service platform violates the provisions of Article 27 of this Law, the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the provincial level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the civil affairs department of the State Council shall cancel its designation.  
Where an Internet information service provider that has not been designated provides Internet public fundraising services without authorization, the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the cyberspace affairs and industry information technology departments, handle the matter according to law.  
Where a radio, television, or press and publication entity, or a network service provider or telecommunications operator fails to perform its verification obligation according to law, its competent department shall order it to make corrections within a time limit and give it a warning; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be criticized in a circular.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　慈善组织不依法向捐赠人开具捐赠票据、不依法向志愿者出具志愿服务记录证明或者不及时主动向捐赠人反馈有关情况的，由县级以上人民政府民政部门予以警告，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令限期停止活动。

**Article 115** Where a charitable organization fails to issue donation receipts to donors according to law, fails to issue volunteer service record certificates to volunteers according to law, or fails to timely and proactively provide feedback to donors on relevant circumstances, the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level shall give it a warning and order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend activities within a time limit.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　慈善组织弄虚作假骗取税收优惠的，由税务机关依法查处；情节严重的，由县级以上人民政府民政部门吊销登记证书并予以公告。

**Article 116** Where a charitable organization obtains tax benefits by fraud through fraudulent means, the tax authority shall investigate and deal with it according to law; if the circumstances are serious, the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level shall revoke its registration certificate and make a public announcement.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　慈善组织从事、资助危害国家安全或者社会公共利益活动的，由有关机关依法查处，由县级以上人民政府民政部门吊销登记证书并予以公告。

**Article 117** Where a charitable organization engages in or funds activities that endanger national security or public interests, the relevant authorities shall investigate and deal with it according to law, and the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level shall revoke its registration certificate and make a public announcement.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　慈善信托的委托人、受托人有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府民政部门责令限期改正，予以警告，并没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处二万元以上二十万元以下罚款：  
（一）将信托财产及其收益用于非慈善目的的；  
（二）指定或者变相指定委托人、受托人及其工作人员的利害关系人作为受益人的；  
（三）未按照规定将信托事务处理情况及财务状况向民政部门报告的；  
（四）违反慈善信托的年度支出或者管理费用标准的；  
（五）未依法履行信息公开义务的。

**Article 118** Where the settlor or trustee of a charitable trust falls under any of the following circumstances, the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit, give it a warning, and confiscate its illegal gains; and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 200,000 on the directly responsible managers and other directly liable persons:  
(1) It uses the trust property and its income for non-charitable purposes;  
(2) It designates or designates in disguised form interested parties of the settlor, trustee, or their staff as beneficiaries;  
(3) It fails to report the handling of trust affairs and financial status to the civil affairs department in accordance with the regulations;  
(4) It violates the annual expenditure or administrative expense standards of the charitable trust;  
(5) It fails to perform the obligation of information disclosure according to law.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　慈善服务过程中，因慈善组织或者志愿者过错造成受益人、第三人损害的，慈善组织依法承担赔偿责任；损害是由志愿者故意或者重大过失造成的，慈善组织可以向其追偿。  
志愿者在参与慈善服务过程中，因慈善组织过错受到损害的，慈善组织依法承担赔偿责任；损害是由不可抗力造成的，慈善组织应当给予适当补偿。

**Article 119** Where, in the course of charitable services, damage is caused to a beneficiary or a third party due to the fault of a charitable organization or a volunteer, the charitable organization shall bear compensation liability according to law; if the damage is caused by the volunteer's intentional act or gross negligence, the charitable organization may claim recourse against the volunteer.  
Where a volunteer suffers damage in the course of participating in charitable services due to the fault of a charitable organization, the charitable organization shall bear compensation liability according to law; if the damage is caused by force majeure, the charitable organization shall provide appropriate compensation.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　县级以上人民政府民政部门和其他有关部门及其工作人员有下列情形之一的，由上级机关或者监察机关责令改正；依法应当给予处分的，由任免机关或者监察机关对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予处分：  
（一）未依法履行信息公开义务的；  
（二）摊派或者变相摊派捐赠任务，强行指定志愿者、慈善组织提供服务的；  
（三）未依法履行监督管理职责的；  
（四）违法实施行政强制措施和行政处罚的；  
（五）私分、挪用、截留或者侵占慈善财产的；  
（六）其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的行为。

**Article 120** Where the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level, other relevant departments, or their staff members fall under any of the following circumstances, the higher-level authority or supervisory authority shall order them to make corrections; where disciplinary action shall be taken according to law, the appointing authority or supervisory authority shall impose disciplinary action on the directly responsible managers and other directly liable persons:  
(1) They fail to perform the obligation of information disclosure according to law;  
(2) They apportion or apportion in disguised form donation tasks, or forcibly designate volunteers or charitable organizations to provide services;  
(3) They fail to perform the duty of supervision and administration according to law;  
(4) They illegally implement administrative compulsory measures and administrative penalties;  
(5) They privately divide, misappropriate, withhold, or encroach upon charitable property;  
(6) Other acts of abusing power, neglecting duties, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 121** : Anyone who violates the provisions of this Law and constitutes an act violating public security administration shall be given a public security administration penalty by the public security organ according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十三章　附则

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　城乡社区组织、单位可以在本社区、单位内部开展群众性互助互济活动。

**Article 122** : Urban and rural community organizations and units may carry out mass mutual aid activities within their own communities or units.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　慈善组织以外的其他组织可以开展力所能及的慈善活动。

**Article 123** : Organizations other than charitable organizations may carry out charitable activities within their capabilities.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　个人因疾病等原因导致家庭经济困难，向社会发布求助信息的，求助人和信息发布人应当对信息真实性负责，不得通过虚构、隐瞒事实等方式骗取救助。  
从事个人求助网络服务的平台应当经国务院民政部门指定，对通过其发布的求助信息真实性进行查验，并及时、全面向社会公开相关信息。具体管理办法由国务院民政部门会同网信、工业和信息化等部门另行制定。

**Article 124** : Where an individual publishes a request for assistance to the public due to disease or other reasons causing family economic difficulties, the requester and the information publisher shall be responsible for the truthfulness of the information and shall not obtain assistance by fraudulent means such as fabricating or concealing facts.  
Platforms providing personal assistance network services shall be designated by the civil affairs department of the State Council, shall verify the truthfulness of the assistance requests published through them, and shall promptly and comprehensively disclose relevant information to the public. Specific management measures shall be separately formulated by the civil affairs department of the State Council in conjunction with the cyberspace administration, industry and information technology, and other departments.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　本法自2016年9月1日起施行。

**Article 125** : This Law shall come into force on September 1, 2016.
