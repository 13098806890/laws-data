# 最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕26号  
**公布日期**：2020-12-29  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> 第1825次会议通过，自2021年1月1日起施行） 为正确审理劳动争议案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国劳动法》《中华人民共和国劳动合同法》《中华人民共和国劳动争议调解仲裁法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等相关法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条　劳动者与用人单位之间发生的下列纠纷，属于劳动争议，当事人不服劳动争议仲裁机构作出的裁决，依法提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理： （一）劳动者与用人单位在履行劳动合同过程中发生的纠纷； （二）劳动者与用人单位之间没有订立书面劳动合同，但已形成劳动关系后发生的纠纷； （三）劳动者与用人单位因劳动关系是否已经解除或者终止，以及应否支付解除或者终止劳动关系经济补偿金发生的纠纷； （四）劳动者与用人单位解除或者终止劳动关系后，请求用人单位返还其收取的劳动合同定金、保证金、抵押金、抵押物发生的纠纷，或者办理劳动者的人事档案、社会保险关系等移转手续发生的纠纷； （五）劳动者以用人单位未为其办理社会保险手续，且社会保险经办机构不能补办导致其无法享受社会保险待遇为由，要求用人单位赔偿损失发生的纠纷； （六）劳动者退休后，与尚未参加社会保险统筹的原用人单位因追索养老金、医疗费、工伤保险待遇和其他社会保险待遇而发生的纠纷； （七）劳动者因为工伤、职业病，请求用人单位依法给予工伤保险待遇发生的纠纷； （八）劳动者依据劳动合同法第八十五条规定，要求用人单位支付加付赔偿金发生的纠纷； （九）因企业自主进行改制发生的纠纷。 第二条　下列纠纷不属于劳动争议： （一）劳动者请求社会保险经办机构发放社会保险金的纠纷； （二）劳动者与用人单位因住房制度改革产生的公有住房转让纠纷；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　劳动者与用人单位之间发生的下列纠纷，属于劳动争议，当事人不服劳动争议仲裁机构作出的裁决，依法提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理：  
（一）劳动者与用人单位在履行劳动合同过程中发生的纠纷；  
（二）劳动者与用人单位之间没有订立书面劳动合同，但已形成劳动关系后发生的纠纷；  
（三）劳动者与用人单位因劳动关系是否已经解除或者终止，以及应否支付解除或者终止劳动关系经济补偿金发生的纠纷；  
（四）劳动者与用人单位解除或者终止劳动关系后，请求用人单位返还其收取的劳动合同定金、保证金、抵押金、抵押物发生的纠纷，或者办理劳动者的人事档案、社会保险关系等移转手续发生的纠纷；  
（五）劳动者以用人单位未为其办理社会保险手续，且社会保险经办机构不能补办导致其无法享受社会保险待遇为由，要求用人单位赔偿损失发生的纠纷；  
（六）劳动者退休后，与尚未参加社会保险统筹的原用人单位因追索养老金、医疗费、工伤保险待遇和其他社会保险待遇而发生的纠纷；  
（七）劳动者因为工伤、职业病，请求用人单位依法给予工伤保险待遇发生的纠纷；  
（八）劳动者依据[劳动合同法第八十五条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-85)规定，要求用人单位支付加付赔偿金发生的纠纷；  
（九）因企业自主进行改制发生的纠纷。

**Article 1** The following disputes between a worker and an employer shall constitute labor disputes. Where a party is dissatisfied with an award made by a labor dispute arbitration institution and initiates a prosecution according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case:  
(1) disputes arising between a worker and an employer in the process of performing a labor contract;  
(2) disputes arising after a worker and an employer have formed a labor relationship without concluding a written labor contract;  
(3) disputes between a worker and an employer concerning whether the labor relationship has been rescinded or terminated, and whether economic compensation for rescission or termination of the labor relationship shall be paid;  
(4) disputes arising after the worker and employer rescind or terminate the labor relationship, where the worker requests the employer to return the collected labor contract deposit, guarantee money, mortgage money, or mortgaged property, or disputes arising from handling the transfer procedures such as the worker's personnel file or social insurance relationship;  
(5) disputes arising where a worker claims that the employer has not handled social insurance procedures for him, and the social insurance agency cannot make up the procedures, thereby causing the worker to be unable to enjoy social insurance benefits, and requests the employer to compensate for losses;  
(6) disputes arising after a worker retires, between the worker and the former employer that has not participated in social insurance pooling, over the recovery of pension, medical expenses, work-related injury insurance benefits, and other social insurance benefits;  
(7) disputes arising where a worker, due to work-related injury or occupational disease, requests the employer to grant work-related injury insurance benefits according to law;  
(8) disputes arising where a worker, pursuant to Article 85 of the Labor Contract Law, requests the employer to pay additional compensation; and  
(9) disputes arising from the enterprise's independent restructuring.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　下列纠纷不属于劳动争议：  
（一）劳动者请求社会保险经办机构发放社会保险金的纠纷；  
（二）劳动者与用人单位因住房制度改革产生的公有住房转让纠纷；  
（三）劳动者对劳动能力鉴定委员会的伤残等级鉴定结论或者对职业病诊断鉴定委员会的职业病诊断鉴定结论的异议纠纷；  
（四）家庭或者个人与家政服务人员之间的纠纷；  
（五）个体工匠与帮工、学徒之间的纠纷；  
（六）农村承包经营户与受雇人之间的纠纷。

**Article 2** The following disputes shall not constitute labor disputes:  
(1) disputes where a worker requests a social insurance agency to distribute social insurance funds;  
(2) disputes between a worker and an employer over the transfer of public housing arising from the housing system reform;  
(3) disputes arising from a worker's objection to the disability grade appraisal conclusion of the Labor Capacity Appraisal Committee or to the occupational disease diagnosis appraisal conclusion of the Occupational Disease Diagnosis Appraisal Committee;  
(4) disputes between a family or individual and a domestic service worker;  
(5) disputes between an individual craftsman and an assistant or apprentice;  
(6) disputes between a rural contractual household and an employee.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　劳动争议案件由用人单位所在地或者劳动合同履行地的基层人民法院管辖。  
劳动合同履行地不明确的，由用人单位所在地的基层人民法院管辖。  
法律另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 3** A labor dispute case shall be under the jurisdiction of the Primary People's Court of the place where the employer is located or where the labor contract is performed.  
If the place where the labor contract is performed is unclear, jurisdiction shall lie with the Primary People's Court of the place where the employer is located.  
Where laws provide otherwise, those provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　劳动者与用人单位均不服劳动争议仲裁机构的同一裁决，向同一人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当并案审理，双方当事人互为原告和被告，对双方的诉讼请求，人民法院应当一并作出裁决。在诉讼过程中，一方当事人撤诉的，人民法院应当根据另一方当事人的诉讼请求继续审理。双方当事人就同一仲裁裁决分别向有管辖权的人民法院起诉的，后受理的人民法院应当将案件移送给先受理的人民法院。

**Article 4** Where both the worker and the employer are dissatisfied with the same award of a labor dispute arbitration institution and initiate a prosecution in the same People's Court, the People's Court shall consolidate the cases for trial, and the two parties shall be each other's plaintiff and defendant. The People's Court shall render a ruling on the claims of both parties. In the course of the proceedings, if one party withdraws the prosecution, the People's Court shall continue the trial based on the claims of the other party. Where the two parties each initiate a prosecution in a People's Court with jurisdiction over the same arbitration award, the People's Court that later accepts the case shall transfer the case to the People's Court that first accepted it.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　劳动争议仲裁机构以无管辖权为由对劳动争议案件不予受理，当事人提起诉讼的，人民法院按照以下情形分别处理：  
（一）经审查认为该劳动争议仲裁机构对案件确无管辖权的，应当告知当事人向有管辖权的劳动争议仲裁机构申请仲裁；  
（二）经审查认为该劳动争议仲裁机构有管辖权的，应当告知当事人申请仲裁，并将审查意见书面通知该劳动争议仲裁机构；劳动争议仲裁机构仍不受理，当事人就该劳动争议事项提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 5** Where a labor dispute arbitration institution refuses to accept a labor dispute case on the ground that it has no jurisdiction, and a party initiates a prosecution, the People's Court shall handle the matter separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If upon examination it is determined that the labor dispute arbitration institution indeed has no jurisdiction over the case, the party shall be informed to apply for arbitration with the labor dispute arbitration institution that has jurisdiction;  
(2) If upon examination it is determined that the labor dispute arbitration institution has jurisdiction, the party shall be informed to apply for arbitration, and the examination opinion shall be notified in writing to the labor dispute arbitration institution; if the labor dispute arbitration institution still refuses to accept the case and the party initiates a prosecution regarding the labor dispute matter, the People's Court shall accept it.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　劳动争议仲裁机构以当事人申请仲裁的事项不属于劳动争议为由，作出不予受理的书面裁决、决定或者通知，当事人不服依法提起诉讼的，人民法院应当分别情况予以处理：  
（一）属于劳动争议案件的，应当受理；  
（二）虽不属于劳动争议案件，但属于人民法院主管的其他案件，应当依法受理。

**Article 6** Where a labor dispute arbitration institution makes a written ruling, decision or notice of non-acceptance on the ground that the matter for which a party applies for arbitration does not constitute a labor dispute, and the party refuses to accept it and initiates a prosecution according to law, the People's Court shall handle the matter separately according to the circumstances:  
(1) If it is a labor dispute case, it shall accept it;  
(2) If it does not constitute a labor dispute case but is another case under the jurisdiction of the People's Court, it shall accept it according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　劳动争议仲裁机构以申请仲裁的主体不适格为由，作出不予受理的书面裁决、决定或者通知，当事人不服依法提起诉讼，经审查确属主体不适格的，人民法院不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 7** Where a labor dispute arbitration institution makes a written ruling, decision or notice of non-acceptance on the ground that the applicant is not a proper subject for arbitration, and a party refuses to accept it and initiates a prosecution according to law, if upon examination it is confirmed that the subject is indeed not proper, the People's Court shall not accept the case; if it has already been accepted, it shall render a ruling to dismiss the prosecution.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　劳动争议仲裁机构为纠正原仲裁裁决错误重新作出裁决，当事人不服依法提起诉讼的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 8** Where a labor dispute arbitration institution makes a new ruling to correct an error in the original arbitration ruling, and a party refuses to accept it and initiates a prosecution according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　劳动争议仲裁机构仲裁的事项不属于人民法院受理的案件范围，当事人不服依法提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 9** Where a matter arbitrated by a labor dispute arbitration institution does not fall within the scope of cases accepted by the People's Court, and a party refuses to accept it and initiates a prosecution according to law, the People's Court shall not accept the case; if it has already been accepted, it shall render a ruling to dismiss the prosecution.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　当事人不服劳动争议仲裁机构作出的预先支付劳动者劳动报酬、工伤医疗费、经济补偿或者赔偿金的裁决，依法提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。  
用人单位不履行上述裁决中的给付义务，劳动者依法申请强制执行的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 10** Where a party is dissatisfied with an award made by a labor dispute arbitration institution for the advance payment of labor remuneration, work-related injury medical expenses, economic compensation, or compensation to a worker, and initiates a prosecution according to law, the People's Court shall not accept the case.  
Where the employer fails to perform the payment obligation under the aforesaid award and the worker applies for compulsory execution according to law, the People's Court shall accept the application.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　劳动争议仲裁机构作出的调解书已经发生法律效力，一方当事人反悔提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 11** Where a mediation document made by a labor dispute arbitration institution has already taken legal effect, and one party reneges and initiates a prosecution, the People's Court shall not accept the case; if it has already been accepted, it shall render a ruling to dismiss the prosecution.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　劳动争议仲裁机构逾期未作出受理决定或仲裁裁决，当事人直接提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理，但申请仲裁的案件存在下列事由的除外：  
（一）移送管辖的；  
（二）正在送达或者送达延误的；  
（三）等待另案诉讼结果、评残结论的；  
（四）正在等待劳动争议仲裁机构开庭的；  
（五）启动鉴定程序或者委托其他部门调查取证的；  
（六）其他正当事由。  
当事人以劳动争议仲裁机构逾期未作出仲裁裁决为由提起诉讼的，应当提交该仲裁机构出具的受理通知书或者其他已接受仲裁申请的凭证、证明。

**Article 12** Where a labor dispute arbitration institution fails to make a decision on acceptance or an arbitration ruling within the time limit, and a party directly initiates a prosecution, the People's Court shall accept the case, except where the case applied for arbitration has one of the following causes:  
(1) Transfer of jurisdiction;  
(2) Service is in progress or delayed;  
(3) Awaiting the outcome of another pending case or disability assessment conclusion;  
(4) Awaiting the hearing of the labor dispute arbitration institution;  
(5) Initiation of expert evaluation procedures or entrustment of other departments to investigate and collect evidence;  
(6) Other justifiable causes.  
Where a party initiates a prosecution on the ground that the labor dispute arbitration institution has failed to make an arbitration ruling within the time limit, it shall submit the notice of acceptance or other voucher or proof that the arbitration institution has accepted the arbitration application.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　劳动者依据[劳动合同法第三十条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-30)第二款和调解[仲裁法第十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-16)规定向人民法院申请支付令，符合民事诉讼法第十七章督促程序规定的，人民法院应予受理。  
依据[劳动合同法第三十条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-30)第二款规定申请支付令被人民法院裁定终结督促程序后，劳动者就劳动争议事项直接提起诉讼的，人民法院应当告知其先向劳动争议仲裁机构申请仲裁。  
依据调解[仲裁法第十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-16)规定申请支付令被人民法院裁定终结督促程序后，劳动者依据调解协议直接提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 13** Where a worker applies to the People's Court for a payment order pursuant to paragraph 2 of Article 30 of the Labor Contract Law and Article 16 of the Mediation and Arbitration Law, and the application meets the provisions of Chapter 17 of the Civil Procedure Law on the order for payment procedure, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
Where the payment order applied for pursuant to paragraph 2 of Article 30 of the Labor Contract Law is terminated by the People's Court's ruling and the worker directly initiates a prosecution on the labor dispute matter, the People's Court shall inform him to first apply for arbitration to a labor dispute arbitration institution.  
Where the payment order applied for pursuant to Article 16 of the Mediation and Arbitration Law is terminated by the People's Court's ruling and the worker directly initiates a prosecution on the basis of a mediation agreement, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院受理劳动争议案件后，当事人增加诉讼请求的，如该诉讼请求与讼争的劳动争议具有不可分性，应当合并审理；如属独立的劳动争议，应当告知当事人向劳动争议仲裁机构申请仲裁。

**Article 14** After the People's Court accepts a labor dispute case, if a party adds a claim, where such claim is inseparable from the labor dispute in dispute, it shall be heard jointly; if it is an independent labor dispute, the party shall be informed to apply for arbitration with the labor dispute arbitration institution.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　劳动者以用人单位的工资欠条为证据直接提起诉讼，诉讼请求不涉及劳动关系其他争议的，视为拖欠劳动报酬争议，人民法院按照普通民事纠纷受理。

**Article 15** Where a worker directly initiates a prosecution based on an IOU for wages issued by the employer as evidence, and the claims do not involve other disputes over the labor relationship, the dispute shall be deemed a dispute over arrears of labor remuneration, and the People's Court shall accept the case as an ordinary civil dispute.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　劳动争议仲裁机构作出仲裁裁决后，当事人对裁决中的部分事项不服，依法提起诉讼的，劳动争议仲裁裁决不发生法律效力。

**Article 16** After a labor dispute arbitration institution makes an arbitration ruling, if a party refuses to accept part of the matters in the ruling and initiates a prosecution according to law, the labor dispute arbitration ruling shall not take legal effect.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　劳动争议仲裁机构对多个劳动者的劳动争议作出仲裁裁决后，部分劳动者对仲裁裁决不服，依法提起诉讼的，仲裁裁决对提起诉讼的劳动者不发生法律效力；对未提起诉讼的部分劳动者，发生法律效力，如其申请执行的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 17** After a labor dispute arbitration institution has made an arbitration award on a labor dispute involving multiple workers, if some workers are dissatisfied with the arbitration award and initiate a prosecution according to law, the arbitration award shall not have legal force on the workers who have initiated the prosecution; it shall have legal force on those workers who have not initiated the prosecution. If they apply for enforcement, the People's Court shall accept the application.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　仲裁裁决的类型以仲裁裁决书确定为准。仲裁裁决书未载明该裁决为终局裁决或者非终局裁决，用人单位不服该仲裁裁决向基层人民法院提起诉讼的，应当按照以下情形分别处理：  
（一）经审查认为该仲裁裁决为非终局裁决的，基层人民法院应予受理；  
（二）经审查认为该仲裁裁决为终局裁决的，基层人民法院不予受理，但应告知用人单位可以自收到不予受理裁定书之日起三十日内向劳动争议仲裁机构所在地的中级人民法院申请撤销该仲裁裁决；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 18** The type of arbitration ruling shall be determined by the arbitration ruling document. If the arbitration ruling document does not indicate whether the ruling is a final ruling or a non-final ruling, and the employer refuses to accept the arbitration ruling and initiates a prosecution in the Primary People's Court, the matter shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If upon examination it is determined that the arbitration ruling is a non-final ruling, the Primary People's Court shall accept the case;  
(2) If upon examination it is determined that the arbitration ruling is a final ruling, the Primary People's Court shall not accept the case, but shall inform the employer that it may apply to the Intermediate People's Court of the place where the labor dispute arbitration institution is located for revocation of the arbitration ruling within 30 days from the date of receipt of the ruling of non-acceptance; if the case has already been accepted, the Court shall render a ruling to dismiss the prosecution.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　仲裁裁决书未载明该裁决为终局裁决或者非终局裁决，劳动者依据调解[仲裁法第四十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-47)第一项规定，追索劳动报酬、工伤医疗费、经济补偿或者赔偿金，如果仲裁裁决涉及数项，每项确定的数额均不超过当地月最低工资标准十二个月金额的，应当按照终局裁决处理。

**Article 19** Where an arbitration award does not specify whether it is a final award or a non-final award, and a worker, pursuant to Item (1) of Article 47 of the Mediation and Arbitration Law, seeks to recover labor remuneration, work-related injury medical expenses, economic compensation, or compensation, if the arbitration award involves several items and the amount determined for each item does not exceed the amount of twelve months of the local monthly minimum wage standard, the award shall be handled as a final award.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　劳动争议仲裁机构作出的同一仲裁裁决同时包含终局裁决事项和非终局裁决事项，当事人不服该仲裁裁决向人民法院提起诉讼的，应当按照非终局裁决处理。

**Article 20** If the same arbitration ruling made by a labor dispute arbitration institution contains both final ruling matters and non-final ruling matters, and a party refuses to accept the arbitration ruling and initiates a prosecution in the People's Court, the ruling shall be treated as a non-final ruling.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　劳动者依据调解[仲裁法第四十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-48)规定向基层人民法院提起诉讼，用人单位依据调解[仲裁法第四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-49)规定向劳动争议仲裁机构所在地的中级人民法院申请撤销仲裁裁决的，中级人民法院应当不予受理；已经受理的，应当裁定驳回申请。  
被人民法院驳回起诉或者劳动者撤诉的，用人单位可以自收到裁定书之日起三十日内，向劳动争议仲裁机构所在地的中级人民法院申请撤销仲裁裁决。

**Article 21** Where a worker initiates a prosecution in a Primary People's Court pursuant to Article 48 of the Mediation and Arbitration Law, and the employer applies to the Intermediate People's Court at the place where the labor dispute arbitration institution is located to set aside the arbitration award pursuant to Article 49 of the Mediation and Arbitration Law, the Intermediate People's Court shall not accept the application; if it has already accepted it, it shall rule to dismiss the application.  
Where the prosecution is dismissed by the People's Court or the worker withdraws the prosecution, the employer may apply to the Intermediate People's Court at the place where the labor dispute arbitration institution is located to set aside the arbitration award within thirty days from the date of receipt of the written ruling.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　用人单位依据调解[仲裁法第四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-49)规定向中级人民法院申请撤销仲裁裁决，中级人民法院作出的驳回申请或者撤销仲裁裁决的裁定为终审裁定。

**Article 22** Where an employer applies to the Intermediate People's Court for revocation of an arbitration ruling pursuant to Article 49 of the Mediation and Arbitration Law, the Intermediate People's Court's ruling to dismiss the application or revoke the arbitration ruling shall be a final ruling.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　中级人民法院审理用人单位申请撤销终局裁决的案件，应当组成合议庭开庭审理。经过阅卷、调查和询问当事人，对没有新的事实、证据或者理由，合议庭认为不需要开庭审理的，可以不开庭审理。  
中级人民法院可以组织双方当事人调解。达成调解协议的，可以制作调解书。一方当事人逾期不履行调解协议的，另一方可以申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 23** When the Intermediate People's Court hears a case where an employer applies for revocation of a final ruling, it shall form a collegial panel and hold a hearing. After reviewing the case files, conducting investigations, and questioning the parties, if the collegial panel finds that there are no new facts, evidence or reasons and deems that a hearing is unnecessary, it may proceed without a hearing.  
The Intermediate People's Court may organize mediation between the parties. If a mediation agreement is reached, a mediation document may be prepared. If one party fails to perform the mediation agreement within the time limit, the other party may apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　当事人申请人民法院执行劳动争议仲裁机构作出的发生法律效力的裁决书、调解书，被申请人提出证据证明劳动争议仲裁裁决书、调解书有下列情形之一，并经审查核实的，人民法院可以根据[民事诉讼法第二百三十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-237)规定，裁定不予执行：  
（一）裁决的事项不属于劳动争议仲裁范围，或者劳动争议仲裁机构无权仲裁的；  
（二）适用法律、法规确有错误的；  
（三）违反法定程序的；  
（四）裁决所根据的证据是伪造的；  
（五）对方当事人隐瞒了足以影响公正裁决的证据的；  
（六）仲裁员在仲裁该案时有索贿受贿、徇私舞弊、枉法裁决行为的；  
（七）人民法院认定执行该劳动争议仲裁裁决违背社会公共利益的。  
人民法院在不予执行的裁定书中，应当告知当事人在收到裁定书之次日起三十日内，可以就该劳动争议事项向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 24** Where a party applies to the People's Court for enforcement of a legally effective ruling or mediation document made by a labor dispute arbitration institution, and the respondent provides evidence that the ruling or mediation document has one of the following circumstances, and upon examination and verification, the People's Court may, pursuant to Article 237 of the Civil Procedure Law, render a ruling not to enforce:  
(1) The matters ruled upon do not fall within the scope of labor dispute arbitration, or the labor dispute arbitration institution has no jurisdiction to arbitrate;  
(2) There is indeed an error in the application of laws or regulations;  
(3) The statutory procedures were violated;  
(4) The evidence on which the ruling is based is forged;  
(5) The opposing party concealed evidence sufficient to affect a fair ruling;  
(6) The arbitrator engaged in acts of soliciting or accepting bribes, engaging in malpractice for personal gain, or perverting the law in arbitrating the case;  
(7) The People's Court determines that enforcement of the labor dispute arbitration ruling violates public interests.  
In the ruling of non-enforcement, the People's Court shall inform the parties that they may initiate a prosecution in the People's Court regarding the labor dispute matter within 30 days from the day following the date of receipt of the ruling.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　劳动争议仲裁机构作出终局裁决，劳动者向人民法院申请执行，用人单位向劳动争议仲裁机构所在地的中级人民法院申请撤销的，人民法院应当裁定中止执行。  
用人单位撤回撤销终局裁决申请或者其申请被驳回的，人民法院应当裁定恢复执行。仲裁裁决被撤销的，人民法院应当裁定终结执行。  
用人单位向人民法院申请撤销仲裁裁决被驳回后，又在执行程序中以相同理由提出不予执行抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 25** Where a labor dispute arbitration institution renders a final award, the worker applies to the People's Court for enforcement, and the employer applies to the Intermediate People's Court at the place where the labor dispute arbitration institution is located to set aside the award, the People's Court shall rule to suspend the enforcement.  
Where the employer withdraws the application to set aside the final award or the application is dismissed, the People's Court shall rule to resume the enforcement. If the arbitration award is set aside, the People's Court shall rule to terminate the enforcement.  
Where the employer's application to the People's Court to set aside the arbitration award is dismissed, and the employer subsequently raises a defense of non-enforcement on the same ground in the enforcement proceedings, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　用人单位与其它单位合并的，合并前发生的劳动争议，由合并后的单位为当事人；用人单位分立为若干单位的，其分立前发生的劳动争议，由分立后的实际用人单位为当事人。  
用人单位分立为若干单位后，具体承受劳动权利义务的单位不明确的，分立后的单位均为当事人。

**Article 26** Where an employer merges with another entity, the entity resulting from the merger shall be the party to labor disputes arising before the merger; where an employer is divided into several entities, the actual employer after the division shall be the party to labor disputes arising before the division.  
If, after the division of an employer into several entities, the entity that specifically assumes the labor rights and obligations is unclear, all entities after the division shall be parties.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　用人单位招用尚未解除劳动合同的劳动者，原用人单位与劳动者发生的劳动争议，可以列新的用人单位为第三人。  
原用人单位以新的用人单位侵权为由提起诉讼的，可以列劳动者为第三人。  
原用人单位以新的用人单位和劳动者共同侵权为由提起诉讼的，新的用人单位和劳动者列为共同被告。

**Article 27** Where an employer hires a worker who has not yet rescinded his labor contract, and a labor dispute arises between the original employer and the worker, the new employer may be listed as a third party.  
Where the original employer initiates a prosecution on the ground of infringement by the new employer, the worker may be listed as a third party.  
Where the original employer initiates a prosecution on the ground of joint infringement by the new employer and the worker, the new employer and the worker shall be listed as co-defendants.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　劳动者在用人单位与其他平等主体之间的承包经营期间，与发包方和承包方双方或者一方发生劳动争议，依法提起诉讼的，应当将承包方和发包方作为当事人。

**Article 28** Where, during the period of a contractual operation between an employer and another equal subject, a worker has a labor dispute with both the party granting the contract and the contractor, or with either of them, and initiates a prosecution according to law, the contractor and the party granting the contract shall be named as parties.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　劳动者与未办理营业执照、营业执照被吊销或者营业期限届满仍继续经营的用人单位发生争议的，应当将用人单位或者其出资人列为当事人。

**Article 29** Where a worker has a dispute with an employer that has not obtained a business license, whose business license has been revoked, or which continues to operate after the expiration of the term of operation, the employer or its investor shall be named as a party.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　未办理营业执照、营业执照被吊销或者营业期限届满仍继续经营的用人单位，以挂靠等方式借用他人营业执照经营的，应当将用人单位和营业执照出借方列为当事人。

**Article 30** Where an employer that has not obtained a business license, or whose business license has been revoked, or that continues to operate after the expiration of its business term, operates by borrowing another entity's business license through affiliation or other means, the employer and the entity that lent the business license shall be listed as parties.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　当事人不服劳动争议仲裁机构作出的仲裁裁决，依法提起诉讼，人民法院审查认为仲裁裁决遗漏了必须共同参加仲裁的当事人的，应当依法追加遗漏的人为诉讼当事人。  
被追加的当事人应当承担责任的，人民法院应当一并处理。

**Article 31** Where a party refuses to accept an arbitration ruling made by a labor dispute arbitration institution and initiates a prosecution according to law, and upon examination the People's Court finds that the arbitration ruling omitted a person who must participate in the arbitration jointly, the omitted person shall be added as a party to the action according to law.  
If the added party shall bear liability, the People's Court shall handle the matter together.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　用人单位与其招用的已经依法享受养老保险待遇或者领取退休金的人员发生用工争议而提起诉讼的，人民法院应当按劳务关系处理。  
企业停薪留职人员、未达到法定退休年龄的内退人员、下岗待岗人员以及企业经营性停产放长假人员，因与新的用人单位发生用工争议而提起诉讼的，人民法院应当按劳动关系处理。

**Article 32** Where an employer initiates a prosecution over an employment dispute with a person recruited who has already lawfully enjoyed pension insurance benefits or received a retirement pension, the People's Court shall handle the matter as a labor service relationship.  
Where a person who has been granted leave without pay, a person who has taken early retirement without reaching the statutory retirement age, a laid-off worker waiting for a post, or a person placed on an extended leave due to the enterprise's operational suspension initiates a prosecution because of an employment dispute with a new employer, the People's Court shall handle the matter as a labor relationship.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　外国人、无国籍人未依法取得就业证件即与中华人民共和国境内的用人单位签订劳动合同，当事人请求确认与用人单位存在劳动关系的，人民法院不予支持。  
持有《外国专家证》并取得《外国人来华工作许可证》的外国人，与中华人民共和国境内的用人单位建立用工关系的，可以认定为劳动关系。

**Article 33** Where a foreigner or stateless person who has not lawfully obtained an employment certificate concludes a labor contract with an employer within the territory of the People's Republic of China, and a party requests confirmation of a labor relationship with the employer, the People's Court shall not support the request.  
If a foreigner who holds a Foreign Expert Certificate and has obtained a Foreigner's Work Permit in China establishes an employment relationship with an employer within the territory of the People's Republic of China, it may be recognized as a labor relationship.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　劳动合同期满后，劳动者仍在原用人单位工作，原用人单位未表示异议的，视为双方同意以原条件继续履行劳动合同。一方提出终止劳动关系的，人民法院应予支持。  
根据[劳动合同法第十四条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-14)规定，用人单位应当与劳动者签订无固定期限劳动合同而未签订的，人民法院可以视为双方之间存在无固定期限劳动合同关系，并以原劳动合同确定双方的权利义务关系。

**Article 34** After the expiration of a labor contract, if the worker continues to work for the original employer and the original employer does not express any objection, the parties shall be deemed to have agreed to continue performing the labor contract under the original terms. Where one party proposes to terminate the labor relationship, the People's Court shall support it.  
Where, pursuant to Article 14 of the Labor Contract Law, an employer should have concluded a labor contract with no fixed term with a worker but fails to do so, the People's Court may deem that a labor contract relationship with no fixed term exists between the parties, and the rights and obligations of the parties shall be determined based on the original labor contract.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　劳动者与用人单位就解除或者终止劳动合同办理相关手续、支付工资报酬、加班费、经济补偿或者赔偿金等达成的协议，不违反法律、行政法规的强制性规定，且不存在欺诈、胁迫或者乘人之危情形的，应当认定有效。  
前款协议存在重大误解或者显失公平情形，当事人请求撤销的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 35** An agreement reached between a worker and an employer regarding the handling of relevant procedures for rescission or termination of a labor contract, payment of wages, overtime pay, economic compensation, or compensation, which does not violate mandatory provisions of laws or administrative regulations and is not tainted by fraud, coercion, or exploitation of another's dire straits, shall be deemed valid.  
Where the agreement described in the preceding paragraph contains a material misunderstanding or is obviously unfair, and a party requests its rescission, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　当事人在劳动合同或者保密协议中约定了竞业限制，但未约定解除或者终止劳动合同后给予劳动者经济补偿，劳动者履行了竞业限制义务，要求用人单位按照劳动者在劳动合同解除或者终止前十二个月平均工资的30%按月支付经济补偿的，人民法院应予支持。  
前款规定的月平均工资的30%低于劳动合同履行地最低工资标准的，按照劳动合同履行地最低工资标准支付。

**Article 36** Where the parties have stipulated a non-competition restriction in the labor contract or confidentiality agreement but have not stipulated the economic compensation to be given to the worker after the rescission or termination of the labor contract, and the worker has fulfilled the non-competition obligation, and requests the employer to pay monthly economic compensation at 30% of the worker's average monthly wage for the twelve months preceding the rescission or termination of the labor contract, the People's Court shall support it.  
Where 30% of the average monthly wage as specified in the preceding paragraph is lower than the minimum wage standard at the place where the labor contract is performed, the payment shall be made according to the minimum wage standard at the place where the labor contract is performed.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　当事人在劳动合同或者保密协议中约定了竞业限制和经济补偿，当事人解除劳动合同时，除另有约定外，用人单位要求劳动者履行竞业限制义务，或者劳动者履行了竞业限制义务后要求用人单位支付经济补偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 37** Where the parties have stipulated a non-competition restriction and economic compensation in the labor contract or confidentiality agreement, and when the parties rescind the labor contract, unless otherwise agreed, the employer requests the worker to perform the non-competition obligation, or the worker, after having performed the non-competition obligation, requests the employer to pay economic compensation, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　当事人在劳动合同或者保密协议中约定了竞业限制和经济补偿，劳动合同解除或者终止后，因用人单位的原因导致三个月未支付经济补偿，劳动者请求解除竞业限制约定的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 38** Where the parties have stipulated a non-competition restriction and economic compensation in the labor contract or confidentiality agreement, and after the rescission or termination of the labor contract, the employer fails to pay economic compensation for three months due to reasons attributable to the employer, and the worker requests to rescind the non-competition restriction agreement, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　在竞业限制期限内，用人单位请求解除竞业限制协议的，人民法院应予支持。  
在解除竞业限制协议时，劳动者请求用人单位额外支付劳动者三个月的竞业限制经济补偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 39** During the period of the non-competition restriction, where the employer requests to rescind the non-competition restriction agreement, the People's Court shall support it.  
At the time of rescinding the non-competition restriction agreement, where the worker requests the employer to pay an additional three months of economic compensation for the non-competition restriction, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　劳动者违反竞业限制约定，向用人单位支付违约金后，用人单位要求劳动者按照约定继续履行竞业限制义务的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 40** Where a worker violates the non-competition restriction agreement and pays liquidated damages to the employer, and the employer requests the worker to continue performing the non-competition obligation according to the agreement, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　劳动合同被确认为无效，劳动者已付出劳动的，用人单位应当按照[劳动合同法第二十八条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-28)、第四十六条、第四十七条的规定向劳动者支付劳动报酬和经济补偿。  
由于用人单位原因订立无效劳动合同，给劳动者造成损害的，用人单位应当赔偿劳动者因合同无效所造成的经济损失。

**Article 41** Where a labor contract is confirmed to be void and the worker has performed work, the employer shall pay the worker labor remuneration and economic compensation in accordance with Articles 28, 46, and 47 of the Labor Contract Law.  
Where the conclusion of the void labor contract is attributable to the employer and causes damage to the worker, the employer shall compensate the worker for the economic losses resulting from the invalidity of the contract.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　劳动者主张加班费的，应当就加班事实的存在承担举证责任。但劳动者有证据证明用人单位掌握加班事实存在的证据，用人单位不提供的，由用人单位承担不利后果。

**Article 42** Where a worker claims overtime pay, he shall bear the burden of adducing evidence as to the existence of overtime work. However, if the worker has evidence that the employer holds the evidence of the existence of overtime work, and the employer fails to provide it, the employer shall bear the adverse consequences.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　用人单位与劳动者协商一致变更劳动合同，虽未采用书面形式，但已经实际履行了口头变更的劳动合同超过一个月，变更后的劳动合同内容不违反法律、行政法规且不违背公序良俗，当事人以未采用书面形式为由主张劳动合同变更无效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 43** Where the employer and the worker, through consultation, agree to modify the labor contract, although the modification is not in written form, if the orally modified labor contract has been actually performed for more than one month, and the content of the modified labor contract does not violate laws or administrative regulations and does not contravene public order and good customs, and a party claims that the modification of the labor contract is void on the ground that it was not made in writing, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　因用人单位作出的开除、除名、辞退、解除劳动合同、减少劳动报酬、计算劳动者工作年限等决定而发生的劳动争议，用人单位负举证责任。

**Article 44** In a labor dispute arising from a decision made by the employer on dismissal, removal, discharge, rescission of labor contract, reduction of labor remuneration, or calculation of the worker's length of service, the employer shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　用人单位有下列情形之一，迫使劳动者提出解除劳动合同的，用人单位应当支付劳动者的劳动报酬和经济补偿，并可支付赔偿金：  
（一）以暴力、威胁或者非法限制人身自由的手段强迫劳动的；  
（二）未按照劳动合同约定支付劳动报酬或者提供劳动条件的；  
（三）克扣或者无故拖欠劳动者工资的；  
（四）拒不支付劳动者延长工作时间工资报酬的；  
（五）低于当地最低工资标准支付劳动者工资的。

**Article 45** Where an employer has any of the following circumstances, forcing a worker to propose rescission of the labor contract, the employer shall pay the worker's labor remuneration and economic compensation, and may also pay compensation:  
(1) forcing the worker to work by means of violence, threats, or illegal restriction of personal freedom;  
(2) failing to pay labor remuneration or provide labor conditions as agreed in the labor contract;  
(3) deducting or defaulting on the worker's wages without cause;  
(4) refusing to pay the worker remuneration for extended working hours; or  
(5) paying the worker's wages below the local minimum wage standard.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　劳动者非因本人原因从原用人单位被安排到新用人单位工作，原用人单位未支付经济补偿，劳动者依据[劳动合同法第三十八条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-38)规定与新用人单位解除劳动合同，或者新用人单位向劳动者提出解除、终止劳动合同，在计算支付经济补偿或赔偿金的工作年限时，劳动者请求把在原用人单位的工作年限合并计算为新用人单位工作年限的，人民法院应予支持。  
用人单位符合下列情形之一的，应当认定属于“劳动者非因本人原因从原用人单位被安排到新用人单位工作”：  
（一）劳动者仍在原工作场所、工作岗位工作，劳动合同主体由原用人单位变更为新用人单位；  
（二）用人单位以组织委派或任命形式对劳动者进行工作调动；  
（三）因用人单位合并、分立等原因导致劳动者工作调动；  
（四）用人单位及其关联企业与劳动者轮流订立劳动合同；  
（五）其他合理情形。

**Article 46** Where a worker is assigned from the original employer to work for a new employer for reasons not attributable to the worker, and the original employer has not paid economic compensation, and the worker rescinds the labor contract with the new employer pursuant to Article 38 of the Labor Contract Law, or the new employer proposes to rescind or terminate the labor contract, when calculating the length of service for the payment of economic compensation or compensation, if the worker requests that the length of service with the original employer be counted together as the length of service with the new employer, the People's Court shall support it.  
The circumstance shall be deemed as "the worker is assigned from the original employer to work for a new employer for reasons not attributable to the worker" where the employer meets any of the following conditions:  
(1) the worker continues to work at the same workplace and position, but the subject of the labor contract is changed from the original employer to the new employer;  
(2) the employer transfers the worker in the form of organizational assignment or appointment;  
(3) the worker is transferred due to merger or division of the employer, or other reasons;  
(4) the employer and its related enterprises alternately conclude labor contracts with the worker; or  
(5) other reasonable circumstances.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　建立了工会组织的用人单位解除劳动合同符合[劳动合同法第三十九条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-39)、第四十条规定，但未按照[劳动合同法第四十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-43)规定事先通知工会，劳动者以用人单位违法解除劳动合同为由请求用人单位支付赔偿金的，人民法院应予支持，但起诉前用人单位已经补正有关程序的除外。

**Article 47** Where an employer that has established a trade union organization rescinds a labor contract in compliance with Articles 39 and 40 of the Labor Contract Law but fails to notify the trade union in advance as required by Article 43 of the Labor Contract Law, and the worker requests the employer to pay compensation on the ground that the employer illegally rescinded the labor contract, the People's Court shall support it, unless the employer has already remedied the relevant procedures before the prosecution.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　劳动合同法施行后，因用人单位经营期限届满不再继续经营导致劳动合同不能继续履行，劳动者请求用人单位支付经济补偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 48** After the Labor Contract Law comes into force, where the employer ceases to operate due to the expiration of its term of operation, causing the labor contract to be unable to continue to be performed, and the worker requests the employer to pay economic compensation, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　在诉讼过程中，劳动者向人民法院申请采取财产保全措施，人民法院经审查认为申请人经济确有困难，或者有证据证明用人单位存在欠薪逃匿可能的，应当减轻或者免除劳动者提供担保的义务，及时采取保全措施。  
人民法院作出的财产保全裁定中，应当告知当事人在劳动争议仲裁机构的裁决书或者在人民法院的裁判文书生效后三个月内申请强制执行。逾期不申请的，人民法院应当裁定解除保全措施。

**Article 49** In the course of the proceedings, where a worker applies to the People's Court for taking property preservation measures, and upon examination the People's Court finds that the applicant is truly in financial difficulty, or there is evidence proving that the employer may abscond with arrears of wages, the People's Court shall reduce or exempt the worker from the obligation to provide a guarantee and timely take preservation measures.  
In the written ruling on property preservation made by the People's Court, the parties shall be informed that they shall apply for compulsory execution within three months after the award of the labor dispute arbitration institution or the judgment or ruling of the People's Court takes effect. If the application is not made within the time limit, the People's Court shall rule to lift the preservation measures.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　用人单位根据[劳动合同法第四条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-4)规定，通过民主程序制定的规章制度，不违反国家法律、行政法规及政策规定，并已向劳动者公示的，可以作为确定双方权利义务的依据。  
用人单位制定的内部规章制度与集体合同或者劳动合同约定的内容不一致，劳动者请求优先适用合同约定的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 50** Where an employer formulates rules and regulations through democratic procedures in accordance with Article 4 of the Labor Contract Law, which do not violate national laws, administrative regulations, or policy provisions, and have been made known to the workers, they may serve as the basis for determining the rights and obligations of the parties.  
Where the internal rules and regulations formulated by an employer are inconsistent with the content stipulated in a collective contract or labor contract, and the worker requests that the contract stipulations prevail, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　当事人在调解[仲裁法第十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-10)规定的调解组织主持下达成的具有劳动权利义务内容的调解协议，具有劳动合同的约束力，可以作为人民法院裁判的根据。  
当事人在调解[仲裁法第十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-10)规定的调解组织主持下仅就劳动报酬争议达成调解协议，用人单位不履行调解协议确定的给付义务，劳动者直接提起诉讼的，人民法院可以按照普通民事纠纷受理。

**Article 51** A mediation agreement that contains the content of labor rights and obligations reached by the parties under the auspices of a mediation organization as prescribed in Article 10 of the Mediation and Arbitration Law shall have the binding force of a labor contract and may serve as the basis for the judgment of a People's Court.  
Where the parties reach a mediation agreement only on a labor remuneration dispute under the auspices of a mediation organization as prescribed in Article 10 of the Mediation and Arbitration Law, and the employer fails to perform the payment obligations determined in the mediation agreement, and the worker directly initiates a prosecution, the People's Court may accept the case as an ordinary civil dispute.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　当事人在人民调解委员会主持下仅就给付义务达成的调解协议，双方认为有必要的，可以共同向人民调解委员会所在地的基层人民法院申请司法确认。

**Article 52** Where the parties reach a mediation agreement only on payment obligations under the auspices of a People's Mediation Committee, and both parties consider it necessary, they may jointly apply to the Primary People's Court in the locality of the People's Mediation Committee for judicial confirmation.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　用人单位对劳动者作出的开除、除名、辞退等处理，或者因其他原因解除劳动合同确有错误的，人民法院可以依法判决予以撤销。  
对于追索劳动报酬、养老金、医疗费以及工伤保险待遇、经济补偿金、培训费及其他相关费用等案件，给付数额不当的，人民法院可以予以变更。

**Article 53** Where the employer's decision to discharge, remove, dismiss, or otherwise terminate a labor contract for other reasons is indeed erroneous, the People's Court may, according to law, render a judgment to revoke such decision.  
For cases involving the recovery of labor remuneration, pension, medical expenses, work-related injury insurance benefits, economic compensation, training fees, and other related expenses, if the amount of payment is improper, the People's Court may modify it.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　本解释自2021年1月1日起施行。

**Article 54** This Interpretation shall come into force on January 1, 2021.
