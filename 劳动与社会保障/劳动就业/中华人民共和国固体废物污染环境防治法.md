# 中华人民共和国固体废物污染环境防治法

**公布日期**：2020-04-29

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护和改善生态环境，防治固体废物污染环境，保障公众健康，维护生态安全，推进生态文明建设，促进经济社会可持续发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of protecting and improving the ecological environment, preventing and controlling environmental pollution by solid waste, safeguarding public health, maintaining ecological security, advancing ecological civilization, and promoting sustainable economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　固体废物污染环境的防治适用本法。  
固体废物污染海洋环境的防治和放射性固体废物污染环境的防治不适用本法。

**Article 2** The prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste shall be governed by this Law.  
The prevention and control of environmental pollution of the marine environment by solid waste and the prevention and control of environmental pollution by radioactive solid waste shall not be governed by this Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家推行绿色发展方式，促进清洁生产和循环经济发展。  
国家倡导简约适度、绿色低碳的生活方式，引导公众积极参与固体废物污染环境防治。

**Article 3** The State shall promote green development methods, and facilitate cleaner production and the development of a circular economy.  
The State shall advocate a simple, moderate, green and low-carbon lifestyle, and guide the public to actively participate in the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　固体废物污染环境防治坚持减量化、资源化和无害化的原则。  
任何单位和个人都应当采取措施，减少固体废物的产生量，促进固体废物的综合利用，降低固体废物的危害性。

**Article 4** The prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste shall adhere to the principles of reduction, resource recovery and harmlessness.  
Any unit or individual shall take measures to reduce the generation of solid waste, promote the comprehensive utilization of solid waste, and reduce the harmfulness of solid waste.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　固体废物污染环境防治坚持污染担责的原则。  
产生、收集、贮存、运输、利用、处置固体废物的单位和个人，应当采取措施，防止或者减少固体废物对环境的污染，对所造成的环境污染依法承担责任。

**Article 5** The prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste shall adhere to the principle that the polluter bears responsibility.  
Units and individuals that generate, collect, store, transport, utilize or dispose of solid waste shall take measures to prevent or reduce environmental pollution by solid waste, and shall bear liability according to law for the environmental pollution caused.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家推行生活垃圾分类制度。  
生活垃圾分类坚持政府推动、全民参与、城乡统筹、因地制宜、简便易行的原则。

**Article 6** The State shall implement a system of household waste classification.  
Household waste classification shall adhere to the principles of government promotion, public participation, urban and rural overall planning, adaptation to local conditions, and simplicity and convenience.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　地方各级人民政府对本行政区域固体废物污染环境防治负责。  
国家实行固体废物污染环境防治目标责任制和考核评价制度，将固体废物污染环境防治目标完成情况纳入考核评价的内容。

**Article 7** Local people's governments at various levels shall be responsible for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste within their respective administrative regions.  
The State shall implement a target responsibility system and an assessment and evaluation system for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste, and incorporate the completion of the targets for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste into the assessment and evaluation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　各级人民政府应当加强对固体废物污染环境防治工作的领导，组织、协调、督促有关部门依法履行固体废物污染环境防治监督管理职责。  
省、自治区、直辖市之间可以协商建立跨行政区域固体废物污染环境的联防联控机制，统筹规划制定、设施建设、固体废物转移等工作。

**Article 8** People's governments at various levels shall strengthen leadership over the work of prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste, organize, coordinate and urge the relevant departments to perform their duties of supervision and administration of the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste according to law.  
Provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, through consultation, establish mechanisms for joint prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste across administrative regions, and coordinate the formulation of plans, facility construction, and solid waste transfer.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院生态环境主管部门对全国固体废物污染环境防治工作实施统一监督管理。国务院发展改革、工业和信息化、自然资源、住房城乡建设、交通运输、农业农村、商务、卫生健康、海关等主管部门在各自职责范围内负责固体废物污染环境防治的监督管理工作。  
地方人民政府生态环境主管部门对本行政区域固体废物污染环境防治工作实施统一监督管理。地方人民政府发展改革、工业和信息化、自然资源、住房城乡建设、交通运输、农业农村、商务、卫生健康等主管部门在各自职责范围内负责固体废物污染环境防治的监督管理工作。

**Article 9** The competent department of ecology and environment under the State Council shall exercise unified supervision and administration over the national work of prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste. The competent departments of development and reform, industry and information technology, natural resources, housing and urban-rural development, transport, agriculture and rural affairs, commerce, health, customs, etc. under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste within their respective functions.  
The competent department of ecology and environment of a local people's government shall exercise unified supervision and administration over the work of prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste within its administrative region. The competent departments of development and reform, industry and information technology, natural resources, housing and urban-rural development, transport, agriculture and rural affairs, commerce, health, etc. of a local people's government shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste within their respective functions.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家鼓励、支持固体废物污染环境防治的科学研究、技术开发、先进技术推广和科学普及，加强固体废物污染环境防治科技支撑。

**Article 10** The State shall encourage and support scientific research, technological development, promotion of advanced technologies and scientific popularization in the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste, and strengthen the scientific and technological support for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家机关、社会团体、企业事业单位、基层群众性自治组织和新闻媒体应当加强固体废物污染环境防治宣传教育和科学普及，增强公众固体废物污染环境防治意识。  
学校应当开展生活垃圾分类以及其他固体废物污染环境防治知识普及和教育。

**Article 11** State organs, social organizations, enterprises, institutions, grass-roots mass autonomous organizations and news media shall strengthen the publicity, education and scientific popularization on the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste, and enhance the public's awareness of the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste.  
Schools shall carry out education on the classification of household waste and other knowledge popularization and education on the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　各级人民政府对在固体废物污染环境防治工作以及相关的综合利用活动中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 12** People's governments at various levels shall commend and reward, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste and related comprehensive utilization activities.

### 第二章　监督管理

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上人民政府应当将固体废物污染环境防治工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划、生态环境保护规划，并采取有效措施减少固体废物的产生量、促进固体废物的综合利用、降低固体废物的危害性，最大限度降低固体废物填埋量。

**Article 13** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the work of prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste into the national economic and social development plans and ecological and environmental protection plans, and take effective measures to reduce the generation of solid waste, promote the comprehensive utilization of solid waste, reduce the harmfulness of solid waste, and minimize the amount of solid waste landfilled.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院生态环境主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门根据国家环境质量标准和国家经济、技术条件，制定固体废物鉴别标准、鉴别程序和国家固体废物污染环境防治技术标准。

**Article 14** The competent department of ecology and environment under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate standards for the identification of solid waste, identification procedures and national technical standards for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste, based on the national environmental quality standards and the national economic and technical conditions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院标准化主管部门应当会同国务院发展改革、工业和信息化、生态环境、农业农村等主管部门，制定固体废物综合利用标准。  
综合利用固体废物应当遵守生态环境法律法规，符合固体废物污染环境防治技术标准。使用固体废物综合利用产物应当符合国家规定的用途、标准。

**Article 15** The competent department of standardization under the State Council shall, together with the competent departments of development and reform, industry and information technology, ecology and environment, agriculture and rural affairs, etc. under the State Council, formulate standards for the comprehensive utilization of solid waste.  
The comprehensive utilization of solid waste shall comply with the laws and regulations on ecology and environment, and meet the technical standards for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste. The use of products from the comprehensive utilization of solid waste shall meet the purposes and standards prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院生态环境主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门建立全国危险废物等固体废物污染环境防治信息平台，推进固体废物收集、转移、处置等全过程监控和信息化追溯。

**Article 16** The competent department of ecology and environment under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, establish a national information platform for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by hazardous waste and other solid waste, and promote the whole-process monitoring and information traceability of the collection, transfer and disposal of solid waste.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　建设产生、贮存、利用、处置固体废物的项目，应当依法进行环境影响评价，并遵守国家有关建设项目环境保护管理的规定。

**Article 17** For projects that generate, store, utilize or dispose of solid waste, environmental impact assessment shall be conducted according to law, and the provisions of the State on environmental protection management of construction projects shall be complied with.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　建设项目的环境影响评价文件确定需要配套建设的固体废物污染环境防治设施，应当与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投入使用。建设项目的初步设计，应当按照环境保护设计规范的要求，将固体废物污染环境防治内容纳入环境影响评价文件，落实防治固体废物污染环境和破坏生态的措施以及固体废物污染环境防治设施投资概算。  
建设单位应当依照有关法律法规的规定，对配套建设的固体废物污染环境防治设施进行验收，编制验收报告，并向社会公开。

**Article 18** The facilities for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste that are determined to be built in support of a construction project in its environmental impact assessment document shall be designed, constructed and put into use simultaneously with the main project. The preliminary design of the construction project shall, in accordance with the requirements of the environmental protection design specifications, incorporate the content of prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste into the environmental impact assessment document, implement the measures to prevent environmental pollution and ecological damage by solid waste, and include the investment budget for the facilities for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste.  
The construction unit shall, in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and regulations, check and accept the supporting facilities for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste, prepare an acceptance report, and make it public to the society.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　收集、贮存、运输、利用、处置固体废物的单位和其他生产经营者，应当加强对相关设施、设备和场所的管理和维护，保证其正常运行和使用。

**Article 19** Units and other business operators that collect, store, transport, utilize or dispose of solid waste shall strengthen the management and maintenance of relevant facilities, equipment and sites, and ensure their normal operation and use.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　产生、收集、贮存、运输、利用、处置固体废物的单位和其他生产经营者，应当采取防扬散、防流失、防渗漏或者其他防止污染环境的措施，不得擅自倾倒、堆放、丢弃、遗撒固体废物。  
禁止任何单位或者个人向江河、湖泊、运河、渠道、水库及其最高水位线以下的滩地和岸坡以及法律法规规定的其他地点倾倒、堆放、贮存固体废物。

**Article 20** Units and other business operators that generate, collect, store, transport, utilize or dispose of solid waste shall take measures to prevent scattering, loss, leakage or other pollution, and shall not dump, pile up, discard or spill solid waste without authorization.  
No unit or individual may dump, pile up or store solid waste into rivers, lakes, canals, channels, reservoirs, and the beaches and bank slopes below the highest water level line thereof, or other places prescribed by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　在生态保护红线区域、永久基本农田集中区域和其他需要特别保护的区域内，禁止建设工业固体废物、危险废物集中贮存、利用、处置的设施、场所和生活垃圾填埋场。

**Article 21** In areas such as the red line areas for ecological protection, concentrated areas of permanent basic farmland and other areas requiring special protection, the construction of facilities and sites for centralized storage, utilization and disposal of industrial solid waste and hazardous waste, as well as landfills for household waste, shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　转移固体废物出省、自治区、直辖市行政区域贮存、处置的，应当向固体废物移出地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门提出申请。移出地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门应当及时商经接受地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门同意后，在规定期限内批准转移该固体废物出省、自治区、直辖市行政区域。未经批准的，不得转移。  
转移固体废物出省、自治区、直辖市行政区域利用的，应当报固体废物移出地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门备案。移出地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门应当将备案信息通报接受地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门。

**Article 22** If solid waste is to be transferred out of the administrative region of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for storage or disposal, an application shall be submitted to the competent department of ecology and environment of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government from which the solid waste is transferred. The competent department of ecology and environment of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government from which the solid waste is transferred shall, after promptly consulting with and obtaining the consent of the competent department of ecology and environment of the people's government of the receiving province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, approve the transfer of the solid waste out of the administrative region of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government within the prescribed time limit. Without such approval, no transfer may be made.  
If solid waste is transferred out of the administrative region of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for utilization, it shall be reported to the competent department of ecology and environment of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government from which the solid waste is transferred for record. The competent department of ecology and environment of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government from which the solid waste is transferred shall notify the competent department of ecology and environment of the people's government of the receiving province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government of the record information.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　禁止中华人民共和国境外的固体废物进境倾倒、堆放、处置。

**Article 23** It shall be prohibited to dump, pile up or dispose of solid waste from outside the territory of the People's Republic of China into the territory.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家逐步实现固体废物零进口，由国务院生态环境主管部门会同国务院商务、发展改革、海关等主管部门组织实施。

**Article 24** The State shall gradually achieve zero import of solid waste, and the competent department of ecology and environment under the State Council shall organize the implementation together with the competent departments of commerce, development and reform, customs, etc. under the State Council.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　海关发现进口货物疑似固体废物的，可以委托专业机构开展属性鉴别，并根据鉴别结论依法管理。

**Article 25** If the customs discovers that imported goods are suspected to be solid waste, it may entrust a professional institution to conduct attribute identification, and manage them according to law based on the identification conclusion.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　生态环境主管部门及其环境执法机构和其他负有固体废物污染环境防治监督管理职责的部门，在各自职责范围内有权对从事产生、收集、贮存、运输、利用、处置固体废物等活动的单位和其他生产经营者进行现场检查。被检查者应当如实反映情况，并提供必要的资料。  
实施现场检查，可以采取现场监测、采集样品、查阅或者复制与固体废物污染环境防治相关的资料等措施。检查人员进行现场检查，应当出示证件。对现场检查中知悉的商业秘密应当保密。

**Article 26** The competent departments of ecology and environment and their environmental law enforcement agencies, as well as other departments responsible for the supervision and administration of the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste, shall have the authority to conduct on-site inspections of units and other business operators engaged in activities such as generating, collecting, storing, transporting, utilizing or disposing of solid waste within their respective functions. The inspected party shall truthfully report the situation and provide necessary information.  
When conducting on-site inspections, measures such as on-site monitoring, sample collection, and reviewing or copying materials related to the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste may be taken. Inspection personnel shall present their credentials when conducting on-site inspections. Trade secrets learned during on-site inspections shall be kept confidential.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　有下列情形之一，生态环境主管部门和其他负有固体废物污染环境防治监督管理职责的部门，可以对违法收集、贮存、运输、利用、处置的固体废物及设施、设备、场所、工具、物品予以查封、扣押：  
（一）可能造成证据灭失、被隐匿或者非法转移的；  
（二）造成或者可能造成严重环境污染的。

**Article 27** If one of the following circumstances exists, the competent departments of ecology and environment and other departments responsible for the supervision and administration of the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste may seal up or seize solid waste and related facilities, equipment, sites, tools and articles that are illegally collected, stored, transported, utilized or disposed of:  
(1) It may cause the loss, concealment or illegal transfer of evidence;  
(2) It has caused or may cause serious environmental pollution.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　生态环境主管部门应当会同有关部门建立产生、收集、贮存、运输、利用、处置固体废物的单位和其他生产经营者信用记录制度，将相关信用记录纳入全国信用信息共享平台。

**Article 28** The competent departments of ecology and environment shall, together with the relevant departments, establish a credit record system for units and other business operators that generate, collect, store, transport, utilize or dispose of solid waste, and incorporate the relevant credit records into the national credit information sharing platform.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　设区的市级人民政府生态环境主管部门应当会同住房城乡建设、农业农村、卫生健康等主管部门，定期向社会发布固体废物的种类、产生量、处置能力、利用处置状况等信息。  
产生、收集、贮存、运输、利用、处置固体废物的单位，应当依法及时公开固体废物污染环境防治信息，主动接受社会监督。  
利用、处置固体废物的单位，应当依法向公众开放设施、场所，提高公众环境保护意识和参与程度。

**Article 29** The competent department of ecology and environment of the people's government of a city divided into districts shall, together with the competent departments of housing and urban-rural development, agriculture and rural affairs, health, etc., regularly publish information on the types, generation amounts, disposal capacity, utilization and disposal status of solid waste to the public.  
Units that generate, collect, store, transport, utilize or dispose of solid waste shall, according to law, promptly disclose information on the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste and actively accept public supervision.  
Units that utilize or dispose of solid waste shall, according to law, open their facilities and sites to the public to enhance public environmental awareness and participation.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上人民政府应当将工业固体废物、生活垃圾、危险废物等固体废物污染环境防治情况纳入环境状况和环境保护目标完成情况年度报告，向本级人民代表大会或者人民代表大会常务委员会报告。

**Article 30** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the status of prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste such as industrial solid waste, household waste and hazardous waste into the annual report on the environmental conditions and the completion of environmental protection targets, and report to the people's congress or its standing committee at the same level.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　任何单位和个人都有权对造成固体废物污染环境的单位和个人进行举报。  
生态环境主管部门和其他负有固体废物污染环境防治监督管理职责的部门应当将固体废物污染环境防治举报方式向社会公布，方便公众举报。  
接到举报的部门应当及时处理并对举报人的相关信息予以保密；对实名举报并查证属实的，给予奖励。  
举报人举报所在单位的，该单位不得以解除、变更劳动合同或者其他方式对举报人进行打击报复。

**Article 31** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report any unit or individual that causes environmental pollution by solid waste.  
The competent departments of ecology and environment and other departments responsible for the supervision and administration of the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste shall make public the reporting methods for environmental pollution by solid waste to facilitate public reporting.  
The department that receives the report shall promptly handle it and keep the relevant information of the whistleblower confidential; if the report is made with the real name and verified to be true, a reward shall be given.  
If the whistleblower reports the unit in which he works, the unit shall not retaliate against the whistleblower by means such as rescinding or modifying the labor contract or other means.

### 第三章　工业固体废物

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院生态环境主管部门应当会同国务院发展改革、工业和信息化等主管部门对工业固体废物对公众健康、生态环境的危害和影响程度等作出界定，制定防治工业固体废物污染环境的技术政策，组织推广先进的防治工业固体废物污染环境的生产工艺和设备。

**Article 32** The competent department of ecology and environment under the State Council shall, together with the competent departments of development and reform, industry and information technology, etc. under the State Council, define the extent of harm and impact of industrial solid waste on public health and the ecological environment, formulate technical policies for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by industrial solid waste, and organize the promotion of advanced production techniques and equipment for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by industrial solid waste.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院工业和信息化主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门组织研究开发、推广减少工业固体废物产生量和降低工业固体废物危害性的生产工艺和设备，公布限期淘汰产生严重污染环境的工业固体废物的落后生产工艺、设备的名录。  
生产者、销售者、进口者、使用者应当在国务院工业和信息化主管部门会同国务院有关部门规定的期限内分别停止生产、销售、进口或者使用列入前款规定名录中的设备。生产工艺的采用者应当在国务院工业和信息化主管部门会同国务院有关部门规定的期限内停止采用列入前款规定名录中的工艺。  
列入限期淘汰名录被淘汰的设备，不得转让给他人使用。

**Article 33** The competent department of industry and information technology under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, organize the research, development and promotion of production techniques and equipment that reduce the generation of industrial solid waste and reduce the harmfulness of industrial solid waste, and publish a list of outdated production techniques and equipment that generate industrial solid waste causing serious environmental pollution and that are to be phased out within a time limit.  
Producers, sellers, importers and users shall, within the time limit prescribed by the competent department of industry and information technology under the State Council together with the relevant departments of the State Council, respectively stop producing, selling, importing or using the equipment listed in the list specified in the preceding paragraph. Adopters of the production techniques shall, within the time limit prescribed by the competent department of industry and information technology under the State Council together with the relevant departments of the State Council, stop adopting the techniques listed in the list specified in the preceding paragraph.  
Equipment that is phased out as listed in the phase-out list shall not be transferred to others for use.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院工业和信息化主管部门应当会同国务院发展改革、生态环境等主管部门，定期发布工业固体废物综合利用技术、工艺、设备和产品导向目录，组织开展工业固体废物资源综合利用评价，推动工业固体废物综合利用。

**Article 34** The competent department of industry and information technology under the State Council shall, together with the competent departments of development and reform, ecology and environment, etc. under the State Council, regularly publish a guiding catalogue of technologies, processes, equipment and products for the comprehensive utilization of industrial solid waste, organize and carry out evaluation of the comprehensive utilization of industrial solid waste resources, and promote the comprehensive utilization of industrial solid waste.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上地方人民政府应当制定工业固体废物污染环境防治工作规划，组织建设工业固体废物集中处置等设施，推动工业固体废物污染环境防治工作。

**Article 35** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall formulate work plans for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by industrial solid waste, organize the construction of facilities such as centralized disposal facilities for industrial solid waste, and promote the work of prevention and control of environmental pollution by industrial solid waste.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　产生工业固体废物的单位应当建立健全工业固体废物产生、收集、贮存、运输、利用、处置全过程的污染环境防治责任制度，建立工业固体废物管理台账，如实记录产生工业固体废物的种类、数量、流向、贮存、利用、处置等信息，实现工业固体废物可追溯、可查询，并采取防治工业固体废物污染环境的措施。  
禁止向生活垃圾收集设施中投放工业固体废物。

**Article 36** Units that generate industrial solid waste shall establish and improve the responsibility system for the prevention and control of environmental pollution throughout the whole process of generation, collection, storage, transportation, utilization and disposal of industrial solid waste, establish a management ledger for industrial solid waste, truthfully record information on the types, quantities, flow directions, storage, utilization and disposal of industrial solid waste, achieve traceability and query ability for industrial solid waste, and take measures to prevent environmental pollution by industrial solid waste.  
It shall be prohibited to put industrial solid waste into household waste collection facilities.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　产生工业固体废物的单位委托他人运输、利用、处置工业固体废物的，应当对受托方的主体资格和技术能力进行核实，依法签订书面合同，在合同中约定污染防治要求。  
受托方运输、利用、处置工业固体废物，应当依照有关法律法规的规定和合同约定履行污染防治要求，并将运输、利用、处置情况告知产生工业固体废物的单位。  
产生工业固体废物的单位违反本条第一款规定的，除依照有关法律法规的规定予以处罚外，还应当与造成环境污染和生态破坏的受托方承担连带责任。

**Article 37** If a unit that generates industrial solid waste entrusts others to transport, utilize or dispose of industrial solid waste, it shall verify the principal qualifications and technical capacity of the trustee, sign a written contract according to law, and stipulate pollution prevention requirements in the contract.  
The trustee, when transporting, utilizing or disposing of industrial solid waste, shall fulfill the pollution prevention requirements in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations and the contract, and inform the unit that generates industrial solid waste of the transportation, utilization and disposal status.  
If a unit that generates industrial solid waste violates the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article, in addition to being punished in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, it shall also bear joint and several liability with the trustee that caused environmental pollution and ecological damage.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　产生工业固体废物的单位应当依法实施清洁生产审核，合理选择和利用原材料、能源和其他资源，采用先进的生产工艺和设备，减少工业固体废物的产生量，降低工业固体废物的危害性。

**Article 38** Units that generate industrial solid waste shall, according to law, implement cleaner production audits, reasonably select and utilize raw materials, energy and other resources, adopt advanced production techniques and equipment, reduce the generation of industrial solid waste, and reduce the harmfulness of industrial solid waste.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　产生工业固体废物的单位应当取得排污许可证。排污许可的具体办法和实施步骤由国务院规定。  
产生工业固体废物的单位应当向所在地生态环境主管部门提供工业固体废物的种类、数量、流向、贮存、利用、处置等有关资料，以及减少工业固体废物产生、促进综合利用的具体措施，并执行排污许可管理制度的相关规定。

**Article 39** Units that generate industrial solid waste shall obtain a pollutant discharge permit. The specific measures and implementation steps for pollutant discharge permits shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
Units that generate industrial solid waste shall provide the local competent department of ecology and environment with relevant information on the types, quantities, flow directions, storage, utilization and disposal of industrial solid waste, as well as specific measures to reduce the generation of industrial solid waste and promote comprehensive utilization, and implement the relevant provisions of the pollutant discharge permit management system.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　产生工业固体废物的单位应当根据经济、技术条件对工业固体废物加以利用；对暂时不利用或者不能利用的，应当按照国务院生态环境等主管部门的规定建设贮存设施、场所，安全分类存放，或者采取无害化处置措施。贮存工业固体废物应当采取符合国家环境保护标准的防护措施。  
建设工业固体废物贮存、处置的设施、场所，应当符合国家环境保护标准。

**Article 40** Units that generate industrial solid waste shall utilize the industrial solid waste based on economic and technical conditions; for those that are temporarily not utilized or cannot be utilized, they shall construct storage facilities and sites in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of ecology and environment under the State Council, safely store them by category, or take harmless disposal measures. The storage of industrial solid waste shall adopt protective measures that comply with national environmental protection standards.  
The construction of facilities and sites for the storage and disposal of industrial solid waste shall comply with national environmental protection standards.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　产生工业固体废物的单位终止的，应当在终止前对工业固体废物的贮存、处置的设施、场所采取污染防治措施，并对未处置的工业固体废物作出妥善处置，防止污染环境。  
产生工业固体废物的单位发生变更的，变更后的单位应当按照国家有关环境保护的规定对未处置的工业固体废物及其贮存、处置的设施、场所进行安全处置或者采取有效措施保证该设施、场所安全运行。变更前当事人对工业固体废物及其贮存、处置的设施、场所的污染防治责任另有约定的，从其约定；但是，不得免除当事人的污染防治义务。  
对2005年4月1日前已经终止的单位未处置的工业固体废物及其贮存、处置的设施、场所进行安全处置的费用，由有关人民政府承担；但是，该单位享有的土地使用权依法转让的，应当由土地使用权受让人承担处置费用。当事人另有约定的，从其约定；但是，不得免除当事人的污染防治义务。

**Article 41** If a unit that generates industrial solid waste terminates its existence, it shall, before termination, take pollution prevention measures for the storage and disposal facilities and sites of industrial solid waste, and properly dispose of the unutilized industrial solid waste to prevent environmental pollution.  
If a unit that generates industrial solid waste undergoes a change, the unit after the change shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State on environmental protection, carry out safe disposal of the unutilized industrial solid waste and its storage and disposal facilities and sites, or take effective measures to ensure the safe operation of such facilities and sites. If the parties before the change have otherwise agreed on the pollution prevention liability for the industrial solid waste and its storage and disposal facilities and sites, such agreement shall prevail; however, the pollution prevention obligations of the parties shall not be exempted.  
For the costs of safe disposal of the unutilized industrial solid waste and its storage and disposal facilities and sites of a unit that had terminated its existence before April 1, 2005, the relevant people's government shall bear them; however, if the land use right enjoyed by such unit is transferred according to law, the transferee of the land use right shall bear the disposal costs. If the parties have otherwise agreed, such agreement shall prevail; however, the pollution prevention obligations of the parties shall not be exempted.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　矿山企业应当采取科学的开采方法和选矿工艺，减少尾矿、煤矸石、废石等矿业固体废物的产生量和贮存量。  
国家鼓励采取先进工艺对尾矿、煤矸石、废石等矿业固体废物进行综合利用。  
尾矿、煤矸石、废石等矿业固体废物贮存设施停止使用后，矿山企业应当按照国家有关环境保护等规定进行封场，防止造成环境污染和生态破坏。

**Article 42** Mining enterprises shall adopt scientific mining methods and mineral processing techniques to reduce the generation and storage of mining solid wastes such as tailings, coal gangue and waste rock.  
The State shall encourage the adoption of advanced techniques for the comprehensive utilization of mining solid wastes such as tailings, coal gangue and waste rock.  
After the storage facilities for mining solid wastes such as tailings, coal gangue and waste rock are decommissioned, the mining enterprise shall close the site in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State on environmental protection, to prevent environmental pollution and ecological damage.

### 第四章　生活垃圾

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加快建立分类投放、分类收集、分类运输、分类处理的生活垃圾管理系统，实现生活垃圾分类制度有效覆盖。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当建立生活垃圾分类工作协调机制，加强和统筹生活垃圾分类管理能力建设。  
各级人民政府及其有关部门应当组织开展生活垃圾分类宣传，教育引导公众养成生活垃圾分类习惯，督促和指导生活垃圾分类工作。

**Article 43** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall accelerate the establishment of a household waste management system for classified delivery, classified collection, classified transportation and classified treatment, and achieve effective coverage of the household waste classification system.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a coordination mechanism for household waste classification work, and strengthen and coordinate the capacity building of household waste classification management.  
People's governments at various levels and their relevant departments shall organize publicity on household waste classification, educate and guide the public to develop household waste classification habits, and supervise and guide the household waste classification work.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　县级以上地方人民政府应当有计划地改进燃料结构，发展清洁能源，减少燃料废渣等固体废物的产生量。  
县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当加强产品生产和流通过程管理，避免过度包装，组织净菜上市，减少生活垃圾的产生量。

**Article 44** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall systematically improve the fuel structure, develop clean energy, and reduce the generation of solid wastes such as fuel residues.  
The relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the management of product production and circulation processes, avoid excessive packaging, organize the marketing of clean vegetables, and reduce the generation of household waste.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上人民政府应当统筹安排建设城乡生活垃圾收集、运输、处理设施，确定设施厂址，提高生活垃圾的综合利用和无害化处置水平，促进生活垃圾收集、处理的产业化发展，逐步建立和完善生活垃圾污染环境防治的社会服务体系。  
县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当统筹规划，合理安排回收、分拣、打包网点，促进生活垃圾的回收利用工作。

**Article 45** People's governments at or above the county level shall make overall arrangements for the construction of facilities for the collection, transportation and treatment of urban and rural household waste, determine the sites of such facilities, improve the level of comprehensive utilization and harmless disposal of household waste, promote the industrialization of household waste collection and treatment, and gradually establish and improve a social service system for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by household waste.  
The relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall make overall plans, reasonably arrange collection, sorting and baling outlets, and promote the recycling of household waste.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　地方各级人民政府应当加强农村生活垃圾污染环境的防治，保护和改善农村人居环境。  
国家鼓励农村生活垃圾源头减量。城乡结合部、人口密集的农村地区和其他有条件的地方，应当建立城乡一体的生活垃圾管理系统；其他农村地区应当积极探索生活垃圾管理模式，因地制宜，就近就地利用或者妥善处理生活垃圾。

**Article 46** Local people's governments at various levels shall strengthen the prevention and control of environmental pollution by rural household waste, and protect and improve the rural living environment.  
The State shall encourage source reduction of rural household waste. In urban-rural fringe areas, densely populated rural areas and other areas where conditions permit, an integrated urban and rural household waste management system shall be established; in other rural areas, household waste management models shall be actively explored, and household waste shall be utilized locally or nearby or properly disposed of according to local conditions.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　设区的市级以上人民政府环境卫生主管部门应当制定生活垃圾清扫、收集、贮存、运输和处理设施、场所建设运行规范，发布生活垃圾分类指导目录，加强监督管理。

**Article 47** The competent department of environmental sanitation of the people's government at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall formulate norms for the construction and operation of facilities and sites for household waste sweeping, collection, storage, transportation and treatment, publish a guidance catalogue for household waste classification, and strengthen supervision and administration.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上地方人民政府环境卫生等主管部门应当组织对城乡生活垃圾进行清扫、收集、运输和处理，可以通过招标等方式选择具备条件的单位从事生活垃圾的清扫、收集、运输和处理。

**Article 48** The competent departments of environmental sanitation and other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize the sweeping, collection, transportation and treatment of urban and rural household waste, and may select qualified units through bidding or other means to engage in the sweeping, collection, transportation and treatment of household waste.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　产生生活垃圾的单位、家庭和个人应当依法履行生活垃圾源头减量和分类投放义务，承担生活垃圾产生者责任。  
任何单位和个人都应当依法在指定的地点分类投放生活垃圾。禁止随意倾倒、抛撒、堆放或者焚烧生活垃圾。  
机关、事业单位等应当在生活垃圾分类工作中起示范带头作用。  
已经分类投放的生活垃圾，应当按照规定分类收集、分类运输、分类处理。

**Article 49** Units, households and individuals that generate household waste shall, according to law, fulfill the obligations of source reduction and classified delivery of household waste, and bear the responsibility as generators of household waste.  
Any unit or individual shall, according to law, deliver household waste in a classified manner at designated locations. It shall be prohibited to arbitrarily dump, scatter, pile up or burn household waste.  
State organs, public institutions, etc. shall play an exemplary and leading role in the work of household waste classification.  
Household waste that has been delivered in a classified manner shall be collected, transported and treated in a classified manner in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　清扫、收集、运输、处理城乡生活垃圾，应当遵守国家有关环境保护和环境卫生管理的规定，防止污染环境。  
从生活垃圾中分类并集中收集的有害垃圾，属于危险废物的，应当按照危险废物管理。

**Article 50** The sweeping, collection, transportation and treatment of urban and rural household waste shall comply with the provisions of the State on environmental protection and environmental sanitation management, and prevent environmental pollution.  
Hazardous waste that is classified and centrally collected from household waste, if it falls within the category of hazardous waste, shall be managed as hazardous waste.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　从事公共交通运输的经营单位，应当及时清扫、收集运输过程中产生的生活垃圾。

**Article 51** Operating units engaged in public transport shall promptly sweep and collect domestic waste generated during transportation.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　农贸市场、农产品批发市场等应当加强环境卫生管理，保持环境卫生清洁，对所产生的垃圾及时清扫、分类收集、妥善处理。

**Article 52** Farmers' markets, agricultural product wholesale markets, etc. shall strengthen environmental sanitation management, keep the environment clean, and promptly sweep, separately collect, and properly handle the waste generated.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　从事城市新区开发、旧区改建和住宅小区开发建设、村镇建设的单位，以及机场、码头、车站、公园、商场、体育场馆等公共设施、场所的经营管理单位，应当按照国家有关环境卫生的规定，配套建设生活垃圾收集设施。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当统筹生活垃圾公共转运、处理设施与前款规定的收集设施的有效衔接，并加强生活垃圾分类收运体系和再生资源回收体系在规划、建设、运营等方面的融合。

**Article 53** Units engaged in the development of new urban areas, renovation of old districts, development and construction of residential communities, and construction of villages and towns, as well as the operation and management units of public facilities and places such as airports, docks, stations, parks, shopping malls, stadiums and gymnasiums, shall, in accordance with the relevant state provisions on environmental sanitation, construct supporting domestic waste collection facilities.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall coordinate the effective connection between the public transfer and treatment facilities for domestic waste and the collection facilities specified in the preceding paragraph, and strengthen the integration of the classified collection and transportation system for domestic waste and the renewable resource recovery system in terms of planning, construction, and operation.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　从生活垃圾中回收的物质应当按照国家规定的用途、标准使用，不得用于生产可能危害人体健康的产品。

**Article 54** Substances recovered from domestic waste shall be used in accordance with the purposes and standards prescribed by the state, and shall not be used for the production of products that may endanger human health.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　建设生活垃圾处理设施、场所，应当符合国务院生态环境主管部门和国务院住房城乡建设主管部门规定的环境保护和环境卫生标准。  
鼓励相邻地区统筹生活垃圾处理设施建设，促进生活垃圾处理设施跨行政区域共建共享。  
禁止擅自关闭、闲置或者拆除生活垃圾处理设施、场所；确有必要关闭、闲置或者拆除的，应当经所在地的市、县级人民政府环境卫生主管部门商所在地生态环境主管部门同意后核准，并采取防止污染环境的措施。

**Article 55** The construction of domestic waste treatment facilities and sites shall comply with the environmental protection and environmental sanitation standards prescribed by the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council and the competent department of housing and urban-rural construction of the State Council.  
Adjacent regions are encouraged to coordinate the construction of domestic waste treatment facilities and to promote the cross-administrative-region joint construction and sharing of domestic waste treatment facilities.  
It is prohibited to close, leave idle, or dismantle domestic waste treatment facilities or sites without authorization; where it is truly necessary to close, leave idle, or dismantle them, approval shall be obtained after the local environmental sanitation department of the city or county people's government consults with the local competent department of ecological environment, and measures to prevent environmental pollution shall be taken.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　生活垃圾处理单位应当按照国家有关规定，安装使用监测设备，实时监测污染物的排放情况，将污染排放数据实时公开。监测设备应当与所在地生态环境主管部门的监控设备联网。

**Article 56** Domestic waste treatment units shall, in accordance with the relevant state provisions, install and use monitoring equipment to monitor the discharge of pollutants in real time and disclose pollutant discharge data in real time. The monitoring equipment shall be connected to the monitoring equipment of the local competent department of ecological environment.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　县级以上地方人民政府环境卫生主管部门负责组织开展厨余垃圾资源化、无害化处理工作。  
产生、收集厨余垃圾的单位和其他生产经营者，应当将厨余垃圾交由具备相应资质条件的单位进行无害化处理。  
禁止畜禽养殖场、养殖小区利用未经无害化处理的厨余垃圾饲喂畜禽。

**Article 57** The competent department of environmental sanitation of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing and carrying out the resource utilization and harmless treatment of kitchen waste.  
Units and other producers and business operators that generate or collect kitchen waste shall deliver the kitchen waste to units with corresponding qualifications and conditions for harmless treatment.  
Livestock and poultry farms and breeding communities are prohibited from using kitchen waste that has not been treated harmlessly to feed livestock and poultry.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　县级以上地方人民政府应当按照产生者付费原则，建立生活垃圾处理收费制度。  
县级以上地方人民政府制定生活垃圾处理收费标准，应当根据本地实际，结合生活垃圾分类情况，体现分类计价、计量收费等差别化管理，并充分征求公众意见。生活垃圾处理收费标准应当向社会公布。  
生活垃圾处理费应当专项用于生活垃圾的收集、运输和处理等，不得挪作他用。

**Article 58** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the principle of "producer pays", establish a charging system for domestic waste treatment.  
When formulating the charging standards for domestic waste treatment, local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on local actual conditions, take into account the classification of domestic waste, reflect differentiated management such as classified pricing and metered charging, and fully solicit public opinions. The charging standards for domestic waste treatment shall be disclosed to the public.  
Fees for domestic waste treatment shall be used exclusively for the collection, transportation, and treatment of domestic waste, and shall not be misappropriated for other purposes.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　省、自治区、直辖市和设区的市、自治州可以结合实际，制定本地方生活垃圾具体管理办法。

**Article 59** Provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, as well as cities divided into districts and autonomous prefectures, may, based on actual conditions, formulate specific management measures for domestic waste in their localities.

### 第五章　建筑垃圾、农业固体废物等

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强建筑垃圾污染环境的防治，建立建筑垃圾分类处理制度。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当制定包括源头减量、分类处理、消纳设施和场所布局及建设等在内的建筑垃圾污染环境防治工作规划。

**Article 60** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the prevention and control of environmental pollution by construction waste and establish a classified treatment system for construction waste.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall formulate work plans for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by construction waste, including source reduction, classified treatment, and the layout and construction of disposal facilities and sites.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家鼓励采用先进技术、工艺、设备和管理措施，推进建筑垃圾源头减量，建立建筑垃圾回收利用体系。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当推动建筑垃圾综合利用产品应用。

**Article 61** The state encourages the adoption of advanced technologies, processes, equipment, and management measures to promote source reduction of construction waste and to establish a system for the recovery and utilization of construction waste.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall promote the application of products derived from comprehensive utilization of construction waste.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　县级以上地方人民政府环境卫生主管部门负责建筑垃圾污染环境防治工作，建立建筑垃圾全过程管理制度，规范建筑垃圾产生、收集、贮存、运输、利用、处置行为，推进综合利用，加强建筑垃圾处置设施、场所建设，保障处置安全，防止污染环境。

**Article 62** The competent department of environmental sanitation of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by construction waste, establish a whole-process management system for construction waste, regulate the activities of generation, collection, storage, transportation, utilization, and disposal of construction waste, promote comprehensive utilization, strengthen the construction of construction waste disposal facilities and sites, ensure the safety of disposal, and prevent environmental pollution.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　工程施工单位应当编制建筑垃圾处理方案，采取污染防治措施，并报县级以上地方人民政府环境卫生主管部门备案。  
工程施工单位应当及时清运工程施工过程中产生的建筑垃圾等固体废物，并按照环境卫生主管部门的规定进行利用或者处置。  
工程施工单位不得擅自倾倒、抛撒或者堆放工程施工过程中产生的建筑垃圾。

**Article 63** Engineering construction units shall prepare a construction waste treatment plan, take pollution prevention and control measures, and report it to the competent department of environmental sanitation of the local people's government at or above the county level for recordation.  
Engineering construction units shall promptly remove and transport solid wastes such as construction waste generated during the construction process, and utilize or dispose of them in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of environmental sanitation.  
Engineering construction units shall not dump, scatter, or pile up construction waste generated during the construction process without authorization.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门负责指导农业固体废物回收利用体系建设，鼓励和引导有关单位和其他生产经营者依法收集、贮存、运输、利用、处置农业固体废物，加强监督管理，防止污染环境。

**Article 64** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for guiding the construction of a system for the recovery and utilization of agricultural solid waste, encouraging and guiding relevant units and other producers and business operators to collect, store, transport, utilize, and dispose of agricultural solid waste in accordance with law, strengthen supervision and management, and prevent environmental pollution.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　产生秸秆、废弃农用薄膜、农药包装废弃物等农业固体废物的单位和其他生产经营者，应当采取回收利用和其他防止污染环境的措施。  
从事畜禽规模养殖应当及时收集、贮存、利用或者处置养殖过程中产生的畜禽粪污等固体废物，避免造成环境污染。  
禁止在人口集中地区、机场周围、交通干线附近以及当地人民政府划定的其他区域露天焚烧秸秆。  
国家鼓励研究开发、生产、销售、使用在环境中可降解且无害的农用薄膜。

**Article 65** Units and other producers and business operators that generate agricultural solid wastes such as straw, discarded agricultural films, and pesticide packaging wastes shall take measures for recovery and utilization and other measures to prevent environmental pollution.  
Those engaged in large-scale livestock and poultry breeding shall promptly collect, store, utilize, or dispose of solid wastes such as livestock and poultry manure generated during the breeding process, avoiding environmental pollution.  
It is prohibited to openly burn straw in densely populated areas, around airports, near major traffic routes, and in other areas designated by the local people's government.  
The state encourages the research, development, production, sale, and use of agricultural films that are degradable and harmless in the environment.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家建立电器电子、铅蓄电池、车用动力电池等产品的生产者责任延伸制度。  
电器电子、铅蓄电池、车用动力电池等产品的生产者应当按照规定以自建或者委托等方式建立与产品销售量相匹配的废旧产品回收体系，并向社会公开，实现有效回收和利用。  
国家鼓励产品的生产者开展生态设计，促进资源回收利用。

**Article 66** The state establishes an extended producer responsibility system for products such as electrical and electronic products, lead-acid batteries, and vehicle power batteries.  
Producers of products such as electrical and electronic products, lead-acid batteries, and vehicle power batteries shall, in accordance with the provisions, establish a waste product recovery system that matches the sales volume of products by means of self-construction or entrustment, and disclose it to the public, achieving effective recovery and utilization.  
The state encourages producers of products to carry out ecological design and promote resource recovery and utilization.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　国家对废弃电器电子产品等实行多渠道回收和集中处理制度。  
禁止将废弃机动车船等交由不符合规定条件的企业或者个人回收、拆解。  
拆解、利用、处置废弃电器电子产品、废弃机动车船等，应当遵守有关法律法规的规定，采取防止污染环境的措施。

**Article 67** The state implements a system of multi-channel recovery and centralized treatment for waste electrical and electronic products.  
It is prohibited to entrust waste motor vehicles and vessels, etc., to enterprises or individuals that do not meet the prescribed conditions for recovery and dismantling.  
When dismantling, utilizing, or disposing of waste electrical and electronic products, waste motor vehicles and vessels, etc., the provisions of relevant laws and regulations shall be observed, and measures to prevent environmental pollution shall be taken.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　产品和包装物的设计、制造，应当遵守国家有关清洁生产的规定。国务院标准化主管部门应当根据国家经济和技术条件、固体废物污染环境防治状况以及产品的技术要求，组织制定有关标准，防止过度包装造成环境污染。  
生产经营者应当遵守限制商品过度包装的强制性标准，避免过度包装。县级以上地方人民政府市场监督管理部门和有关部门应当按照各自职责，加强对过度包装的监督管理。  
生产、销售、进口依法被列入强制回收目录的产品和包装物的企业，应当按照国家有关规定对该产品和包装物进行回收。  
电子商务、快递、外卖等行业应当优先采用可重复使用、易回收利用的包装物，优化物品包装，减少包装物的使用，并积极回收利用包装物。县级以上地方人民政府商务、邮政等主管部门应当加强监督管理。  
国家鼓励和引导消费者使用绿色包装和减量包装。

**Article 68** The design and manufacture of products and packaging shall comply with the relevant state provisions on cleaner production. The competent standardization department of the State Council shall, based on the national economic and technical conditions, the status of prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste, and the technical requirements of the products, organize the formulation of relevant standards to prevent excessive packaging from causing environmental pollution.  
Producers and business operators shall comply with the mandatory standards for restricting excessive packaging of commodities and avoid excessive packaging. The market supervision and management department of the local people's government at or above the county level and relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective duties, strengthen the supervision and management of excessive packaging.  
Enterprises that produce, sell, or import products and packaging that are legally included in the compulsory recovery catalog shall recover such products and packaging in accordance with the relevant state provisions.  
Industries such as e-commerce, express delivery, and food delivery shall prioritize the use of reusable and easily recyclable packaging, optimize the packaging of items, reduce the use of packaging, and actively recover and utilize packaging. The competent departments of commerce and postal services, etc., of the local people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and management.  
The state encourages and guides consumers to use green packaging and reduced packaging.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　国家依法禁止、限制生产、销售和使用不可降解塑料袋等一次性塑料制品。  
商品零售场所开办单位、电子商务平台企业和快递企业、外卖企业应当按照国家有关规定向商务、邮政等主管部门报告塑料袋等一次性塑料制品的使用、回收情况。  
国家鼓励和引导减少使用、积极回收塑料袋等一次性塑料制品，推广应用可循环、易回收、可降解的替代产品。

**Article 69** The state prohibits and restricts the production, sale, and use of non-degradable plastic bags and other disposable plastic products in accordance with law.  
Organizers of commodity retail premises, e-commerce platform enterprises, express delivery enterprises, and food delivery enterprises shall, in accordance with the relevant state provisions, report to the competent departments of commerce, postal services, etc., on the use and recovery of disposable plastic products such as plastic bags.  
The state encourages and guides the reduction of use and active recovery of disposable plastic products such as plastic bags, and promotes the application of recyclable, easily recoverable, and degradable alternative products.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　旅游、住宿等行业应当按照国家有关规定推行不主动提供一次性用品。  
机关、企业事业单位等的办公场所应当使用有利于保护环境的产品、设备和设施，减少使用一次性办公用品。

**Article 70** Industries such as tourism and accommodation shall, in accordance with the relevant state provisions, promote the practice of not actively providing disposable items.  
Office premises of state organs, enterprises, institutions, etc., shall use products, equipment, and facilities that are conducive to environmental protection, and reduce the use of disposable office supplies.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位或者污泥处理单位应当安全处理污泥，保证处理后的污泥符合国家有关标准，对污泥的流向、用途、用量等进行跟踪、记录，并报告城镇排水主管部门、生态环境主管部门。  
县级以上人民政府城镇排水主管部门应当将污泥处理设施纳入城镇排水与污水处理规划，推动同步建设污泥处理设施与污水处理设施，鼓励协同处理，污水处理费征收标准和补偿范围应当覆盖污泥处理成本和污水处理设施正常运营成本。

**Article 71** The maintenance and operation unit of urban sewage treatment facilities or the sludge treatment unit shall safely treat sludge, ensure that the treated sludge meets the relevant national standards, track and record the flow direction, use, quantity, etc. of the sludge, and report to the competent department of urban drainage and the competent department of ecological environment.  
The competent department of urban drainage of the people's government at or above the county level shall incorporate sludge treatment facilities into the urban drainage and sewage treatment plan, promote the simultaneous construction of sludge treatment facilities and sewage treatment facilities, encourage co-treatment, and the collection standards and compensation scope for sewage treatment fees shall cover the costs of sludge treatment and the normal operation costs of sewage treatment facilities.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　禁止擅自倾倒、堆放、丢弃、遗撒城镇污水处理设施产生的污泥和处理后的污泥。  
禁止重金属或者其他有毒有害物质含量超标的污泥进入农用地。  
从事水体清淤疏浚应当按照国家有关规定处理清淤疏浚过程中产生的底泥，防止污染环境。

**Article 72** It is prohibited to dump, pile up, discard, or scatter sludge generated by urban sewage treatment facilities and treated sludge without authorization.  
Sludge with excessive content of heavy metals or other toxic and hazardous substances is prohibited from entering agricultural land.  
Those engaged in water body dredging shall treat the sediment generated during the dredging process in accordance with the relevant state provisions to prevent environmental pollution.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　各级各类实验室及其设立单位应当加强对实验室产生的固体废物的管理，依法收集、贮存、运输、利用、处置实验室固体废物。实验室固体废物属于危险废物的，应当按照危险废物管理。

**Article 73** All types of laboratories at all levels and their establishing units shall strengthen the management of solid waste generated by laboratories, and collect, store, transport, utilize, and dispose of laboratory solid waste in accordance with law. Where laboratory solid waste falls under hazardous waste, it shall be managed as hazardous waste.

### 第六章　危险废物

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　危险废物污染环境的防治，适用本章规定；本章未作规定的，适用本法其他有关规定。

**Article 74** The prevention and control of environmental pollution by hazardous waste shall be governed by the provisions of this Chapter; where this Chapter does not provide, the other relevant provisions of this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　国务院生态环境主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门制定国家危险废物名录，规定统一的危险废物鉴别标准、鉴别方法、识别标志和鉴别单位管理要求。国家危险废物名录应当动态调整。  
国务院生态环境主管部门根据危险废物的危害特性和产生数量，科学评估其环境风险，实施分级分类管理，建立信息化监管体系，并通过信息化手段管理、共享危险废物转移数据和信息。

**Article 75** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate the national hazardous waste list, and prescribe unified identification standards, identification methods, identification marks, and management requirements for identification units. The national hazardous waste list shall be dynamically adjusted.  
The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall scientifically assess the environmental risks of hazardous waste based on their hazardous characteristics and generation quantity, implement tiered and classified management, establish an information-based supervision system, and manage and share the data and information of hazardous waste transfer through information-based means.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当组织有关部门编制危险废物集中处置设施、场所的建设规划，科学评估危险废物处置需求，合理布局危险废物集中处置设施、场所，确保本行政区域的危险废物得到妥善处置。  
编制危险废物集中处置设施、场所的建设规划，应当征求有关行业协会、企业事业单位、专家和公众等方面的意见。  
相邻省、自治区、直辖市之间可以开展区域合作，统筹建设区域性危险废物集中处置设施、场所。

**Article 76** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall organize relevant departments to formulate construction plans for centralized hazardous waste treatment facilities and sites, scientifically assess the demand for hazardous waste treatment, rationally lay out centralized hazardous waste treatment facilities and sites, and ensure that hazardous waste in their administrative regions is properly treated.  
When formulating construction plans for centralized hazardous waste treatment facilities and sites, opinions from relevant industry associations, enterprises, institutions, experts, and the public, among others, shall be solicited.  
Adjacent provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may carry out regional cooperation and coordinately construct regional centralized hazardous waste treatment facilities and sites.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　对危险废物的容器和包装物以及收集、贮存、运输、利用、处置危险废物的设施、场所，应当按照规定设置危险废物识别标志。

**Article 77** For containers and packaging of hazardous waste and facilities and sites for collecting, storing, transporting, utilizing, and disposing of hazardous waste, hazardous waste identification marks shall be set up in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　产生危险废物的单位，应当按照国家有关规定制定危险废物管理计划；建立危险废物管理台账，如实记录有关信息，并通过国家危险废物信息管理系统向所在地生态环境主管部门申报危险废物的种类、产生量、流向、贮存、处置等有关资料。  
前款所称危险废物管理计划应当包括减少危险废物产生量和降低危险废物危害性的措施以及危险废物贮存、利用、处置措施。危险废物管理计划应当报产生危险废物的单位所在地生态环境主管部门备案。  
产生危险废物的单位已经取得排污许可证的，执行排污许可管理制度的规定。

**Article 78** Units that generate hazardous waste shall, in accordance with the relevant state provisions, formulate a hazardous waste management plan; establish a hazardous waste management ledger, truthfully record relevant information, and declare to the local competent department of ecological environment through the national hazardous waste information management system the types, generation quantities, flow directions, storage, disposal, and other relevant data of hazardous waste.  
The hazardous waste management plan referred to in the preceding paragraph shall include measures to reduce the quantity of hazardous waste generated and mitigate the hazardousness of hazardous waste, as well as measures for the storage, utilization, and disposal of hazardous waste. The hazardous waste management plan shall be submitted to the competent department of ecological environment at the location of the unit generating the hazardous waste for recordation.  
Where a unit that generates hazardous waste has already obtained a pollutant discharge permit, it shall implement the provisions of the pollutant discharge permit management system.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　产生危险废物的单位，应当按照国家有关规定和环境保护标准要求贮存、利用、处置危险废物，不得擅自倾倒、堆放。

**Article 79** Units that generate hazardous waste shall store, utilize, and dispose of hazardous waste in accordance with the relevant state provisions and environmental protection standards, and shall not dump or pile up without authorization.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　从事收集、贮存、利用、处置危险废物经营活动的单位，应当按照国家有关规定申请取得许可证。许可证的具体管理办法由国务院制定。  
禁止无许可证或者未按照许可证规定从事危险废物收集、贮存、利用、处置的经营活动。  
禁止将危险废物提供或者委托给无许可证的单位或者其他生产经营者从事收集、贮存、利用、处置活动。

**Article 80** Units engaged in business activities of collecting, storing, utilizing, and disposing of hazardous waste shall apply for and obtain a permit in accordance with the relevant state provisions. The specific measures for the management of permits shall be formulated by the State Council.  
It is prohibited to engage in business activities of collecting, storing, utilizing, and disposing of hazardous waste without a permit or not in accordance with the provisions of the permit.  
It is prohibited to provide or entrust hazardous waste to units or other producers and business operators without a permit for collection, storage, utilization, and disposal activities.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　收集、贮存危险废物，应当按照危险废物特性分类进行。禁止混合收集、贮存、运输、处置性质不相容而未经安全性处置的危险废物。  
贮存危险废物应当采取符合国家环境保护标准的防护措施。禁止将危险废物混入非危险废物中贮存。  
从事收集、贮存、利用、处置危险废物经营活动的单位，贮存危险废物不得超过一年；确需延长期限的，应当报经颁发许可证的生态环境主管部门批准；法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 81** The collection and storage of hazardous waste shall be carried out by classification according to the characteristics of the hazardous waste. It is prohibited to mix, collect, store, transport, or dispose of hazardous waste that are incompatible in nature and have not undergone safety treatment.  
For the storage of hazardous waste, protective measures that meet the national environmental protection standards shall be taken. It is prohibited to mix hazardous waste into non-hazardous waste for storage.  
Units engaged in business activities of collecting, storing, utilizing, and disposing of hazardous waste shall not store hazardous waste for more than one year; where an extension is truly necessary, approval shall be obtained from the competent department of ecological environment that issued the permit; unless otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　转移危险废物的，应当按照国家有关规定填写、运行危险废物电子或者纸质转移联单。  
跨省、自治区、直辖市转移危险废物的，应当向危险废物移出地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门申请。移出地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门应当及时商经接受地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门同意后，在规定期限内批准转移该危险废物，并将批准信息通报相关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门和交通运输主管部门。未经批准的，不得转移。  
危险废物转移管理应当全程管控、提高效率，具体办法由国务院生态环境主管部门会同国务院交通运输主管部门和公安部门制定。

**Article 82** Where hazardous waste is transferred, the electronic or paper transfer manifest for hazardous waste shall be filled in and operated in accordance with the relevant state provisions.  
For the transfer of hazardous waste across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, an application shall be made to the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the hazardous waste is moved out. The competent department of ecological environment of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the hazardous waste is moved out shall, after promptly consulting with and obtaining the consent of the competent department of ecological environment of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the hazardous waste is received, approve the transfer of the hazardous waste within the prescribed time limit, and notify the relevant competent departments of ecological environment and the competent departments of transport of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government of the approval information. Without approval, no transfer shall be made.  
The management of hazardous waste transfer shall achieve whole-process control and improve efficiency, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council together with the competent department of transport of the State Council and the public security department.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　运输危险废物，应当采取防止污染环境的措施，并遵守国家有关危险货物运输管理的规定。  
禁止将危险废物与旅客在同一运输工具上载运。

**Article 83** When transporting hazardous waste, measures to prevent environmental pollution shall be taken, and the relevant state provisions on the management of dangerous goods transport shall be complied with.  
It is prohibited to carry hazardous waste together with passengers on the same means of transport.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　收集、贮存、运输、利用、处置危险废物的场所、设施、设备和容器、包装物及其他物品转作他用时，应当按照国家有关规定经过消除污染处理，方可使用。

**Article 84** When places, facilities, equipment, containers, packaging, and other items used for collecting, storing, transporting, utilizing, and disposing of hazardous waste are converted to other uses, they shall be decontaminated in accordance with the relevant state provisions before being used.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　产生、收集、贮存、运输、利用、处置危险废物的单位，应当依法制定意外事故的防范措施和应急预案，并向所在地生态环境主管部门和其他负有固体废物污染环境防治监督管理职责的部门备案；生态环境主管部门和其他负有固体废物污染环境防治监督管理职责的部门应当进行检查。

**Article 85** Units that generate, collect, store, transport, utilize, or dispose of hazardous waste shall formulate preventive measures and emergency plans for accidents in accordance with law, and report them to the local competent department of ecological environment and other departments responsible for the supervision and management of the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste for recordation; the competent department of ecological environment and other departments responsible for the supervision and management of the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste shall conduct inspections.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　因发生事故或者其他突发性事件，造成危险废物严重污染环境的单位，应当立即采取有效措施消除或者减轻对环境的污染危害，及时通报可能受到污染危害的单位和居民，并向所在地生态环境主管部门和有关部门报告，接受调查处理。

**Article 86** A unit that causes serious environmental pollution by hazardous waste due to an accident or other sudden incident shall immediately take effective measures to eliminate or mitigate the pollution hazards to the environment, promptly notify the units and residents that may be harmed by the pollution, and report to the local competent department of ecological environment and relevant departments, and accept investigation and treatment.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　在发生或者有证据证明可能发生危险废物严重污染环境、威胁居民生命财产安全时，生态环境主管部门或者其他负有固体废物污染环境防治监督管理职责的部门应当立即向本级人民政府和上一级人民政府有关部门报告，由人民政府采取防止或者减轻危害的有效措施。有关人民政府可以根据需要责令停止导致或者可能导致环境污染事故的作业。

**Article 87** When serious environmental pollution by hazardous waste occurs or there is evidence that it may occur, threatening the life and property safety of residents, the competent department of ecological environment or other departments responsible for the supervision and management of the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste shall immediately report to the people's government at the same level and the relevant departments of the people's government at the next higher level, and the people's government shall take effective measures to prevent or mitigate the hazards. The relevant people's government may, as necessary, order the cessation of operations that cause or may cause environmental pollution accidents.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　重点危险废物集中处置设施、场所退役前，运营单位应当按照国家有关规定对设施、场所采取污染防治措施。退役的费用应当预提，列入投资概算或者生产成本，专门用于重点危险废物集中处置设施、场所的退役。具体提取和管理办法，由国务院财政部门、价格主管部门会同国务院生态环境主管部门规定。

**Article 88** Before the decommissioning of key centralized hazardous waste treatment facilities and sites, the operating unit shall take pollution prevention and control measures for the facilities and sites in accordance with the relevant state provisions. The costs of decommissioning shall be set aside in advance, included in the investment estimate or production cost, and used exclusively for the decommissioning of key centralized hazardous waste treatment facilities and sites. The specific measures for setting aside and management shall be prescribed by the competent finance department and the competent pricing department of the State Council together with the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　禁止经中华人民共和国过境转移危险废物。

**Article 89** It is prohibited to transfer hazardous waste through the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　医疗废物按照国家危险废物名录管理。县级以上地方人民政府应当加强医疗废物集中处置能力建设。  
县级以上人民政府卫生健康、生态环境等主管部门应当在各自职责范围内加强对医疗废物收集、贮存、运输、处置的监督管理，防止危害公众健康、污染环境。  
医疗卫生机构应当依法分类收集本单位产生的医疗废物，交由医疗废物集中处置单位处置。医疗废物集中处置单位应当及时收集、运输和处置医疗废物。  
医疗卫生机构和医疗废物集中处置单位，应当采取有效措施，防止医疗废物流失、泄漏、渗漏、扩散。

**Article 90** Medical waste shall be managed in accordance with the national hazardous waste list. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the capacity for centralized treatment of medical waste.  
The competent departments of health, ecological environment, etc., of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within their respective duties, strengthen the supervision and management of the collection, storage, transportation, and disposal of medical waste to prevent harm to public health and environmental pollution.  
Medical and health institutions shall collect separately the medical waste generated by their own units in accordance with law, and deliver it to centralized medical waste treatment units for treatment. Centralized medical waste treatment units shall promptly collect, transport, and treat medical waste.  
Medical and health institutions and centralized medical waste treatment units shall take effective measures to prevent the loss, leakage, seepage, and spreading of medical waste.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　重大传染病疫情等突发事件发生时，县级以上人民政府应当统筹协调医疗废物等危险废物收集、贮存、运输、处置等工作，保障所需的车辆、场地、处置设施和防护物资。卫生健康、生态环境、环境卫生、交通运输等主管部门应当协同配合，依法履行应急处置职责。

**Article 91** When emergencies such as major infectious disease outbreaks occur, the people's government at or above the county level shall coordinate the work of collecting, storing, transporting, and disposing of hazardous waste such as medical waste, and ensure the necessary vehicles, sites, treatment facilities, and protective materials. The competent departments of health, ecological environment, environmental sanitation, and transport, among others, shall cooperate and coordinate, and perform their emergency response duties in accordance with law.

### 第七章　保障措施

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　国务院有关部门、县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门在编制国土空间规划和相关专项规划时，应当统筹生活垃圾、建筑垃圾、危险废物等固体废物转运、集中处置等设施建设需求，保障转运、集中处置等设施用地。

**Article 92** When formulating territorial spatial plans and relevant special plans, the relevant departments of the State Council, the local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall coordinate the construction needs for facilities for the transfer and centralized treatment of solid wastes such as domestic waste, construction waste, and hazardous waste, and ensure the land use for transfer and centralized treatment facilities.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　国家采取有利于固体废物污染环境防治的经济、技术政策和措施，鼓励、支持有关方面采取有利于固体废物污染环境防治的措施，加强对从事固体废物污染环境防治工作人员的培训和指导，促进固体废物污染环境防治产业专业化、规模化发展。

**Article 93** The state adopts economic and technical policies and measures conducive to the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste, encourages and supports relevant parties to take measures conducive to the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste, strengthens the training and guidance of personnel engaged in the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste, and promotes the specialized and scaled development of the industry for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　国家鼓励和支持科研单位、固体废物产生单位、固体废物利用单位、固体废物处置单位等联合攻关，研究开发固体废物综合利用、集中处置等的新技术，推动固体废物污染环境防治技术进步。

**Article 94** The state encourages and supports scientific research units, solid waste generation units, solid waste utilization units, solid waste disposal units, etc., to jointly tackle key problems, research and develop new technologies for comprehensive utilization and centralized treatment of solid waste, and promote the technological progress in the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　各级人民政府应当加强固体废物污染环境的防治，按照事权划分的原则安排必要的资金用于下列事项：  
（一）固体废物污染环境防治的科学研究、技术开发；  
（二）生活垃圾分类；  
（三）固体废物集中处置设施建设；  
（四）重大传染病疫情等突发事件产生的医疗废物等危险废物应急处置；  
（五）涉及固体废物污染环境防治的其他事项。  
使用资金应当加强绩效管理和审计监督，确保资金使用效益。

**Article 95** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste, and arrange necessary funds in accordance with the principle of division of powers for the following matters:  
(1) Scientific research and technological development for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste;  
(2) Classification of domestic waste;  
(3) Construction of centralized solid waste treatment facilities;  
(4) Emergency disposal of hazardous waste such as medical waste generated by emergencies such as major infectious disease outbreaks;  
(5) Other matters involving the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste.  
When using the funds, performance management and audit supervision shall be strengthened to ensure the efficiency of fund use.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　国家鼓励和支持社会力量参与固体废物污染环境防治工作，并按照国家有关规定给予政策扶持。

**Article 96** The state encourages and supports social forces to participate in the work of preventing and controlling environmental pollution by solid waste, and provides policy support in accordance with the relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　国家发展绿色金融，鼓励金融机构加大对固体废物污染环境防治项目的信贷投放。

**Article 97** The state develops green finance and encourages financial institutions to increase credit supply for projects of preventing and controlling environmental pollution by solid waste.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　从事固体废物综合利用等固体废物污染环境防治工作的，依照法律、行政法规的规定，享受税收优惠。  
国家鼓励并提倡社会各界为防治固体废物污染环境捐赠财产，并依照法律、行政法规的规定，给予税收优惠。

**Article 98** Those engaged in work such as comprehensive utilization of solid waste for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste shall enjoy tax preferences in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.  
The state encourages and advocates all sectors of society to donate property for the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid waste, and grants tax preferences in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　收集、贮存、运输、利用、处置危险废物的单位，应当按照国家有关规定，投保环境污染责任保险。

**Article 99** Units that collect, store, transport, utilize, and dispose of hazardous waste shall, in accordance with the relevant state provisions, take out environmental pollution liability insurance.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　国家鼓励单位和个人购买、使用综合利用产品和可重复使用产品。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门在政府采购过程中，应当优先采购综合利用产品和可重复使用产品。

**Article 100** The state encourages units and individuals to purchase and use comprehensive utilization products and reusable products.  
The people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, in the process of government procurement, give priority to the procurement of comprehensive utilization products and reusable products.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　生态环境主管部门或者其他负有固体废物污染环境防治监督管理职责的部门违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府有关部门责令改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）未依法作出行政许可或者办理批准文件的；  
（二）对违法行为进行包庇的；  
（三）未依法查封、扣押的；  
（四）发现违法行为或者接到对违法行为的举报后未予查处的；  
（五）有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊等违法行为的。  
依照本法规定应当作出行政处罚决定而未作出的，上级主管部门可以直接作出行政处罚决定。

**Article 101** Any competent department of ecological environment or other department responsible for the supervision and administration of the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid wastes that, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the people's government at the same level or the relevant department of the people's government at a higher level to make corrections, and the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to grant an administrative license or handle an approval document according to law;  
(2) covering up illegal acts;  
(3) failing to seal up or seize according to law;  
(4) failing to investigate and deal with an illegal act after discovering it or after receiving a report thereof; or  
(5) committing other illegal acts such as abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain.  
Where an administrative penalty decision should be made according to the provisions of this Law but is not made, the competent department at a higher level may directly make the administrative penalty decision.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，处以罚款，没收违法所得；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，可以责令停业或者关闭：  
（一）产生、收集、贮存、运输、利用、处置固体废物的单位未依法及时公开固体废物污染环境防治信息的；  
（二）生活垃圾处理单位未按照国家有关规定安装使用监测设备、实时监测污染物的排放情况并公开污染排放数据的；  
（三）将列入限期淘汰名录被淘汰的设备转让给他人使用的；  
（四）在生态保护红线区域、永久基本农田集中区域和其他需要特别保护的区域内，建设工业固体废物、危险废物集中贮存、利用、处置的设施、场所和生活垃圾填埋场的；  
（五）转移固体废物出省、自治区、直辖市行政区域贮存、处置未经批准的；  
（六）转移固体废物出省、自治区、直辖市行政区域利用未报备案的；  
（七）擅自倾倒、堆放、丢弃、遗撒工业固体废物，或者未采取相应防范措施，造成工业固体废物扬散、流失、渗漏或者其他环境污染的；  
（八）产生工业固体废物的单位未建立固体废物管理台账并如实记录的；  
（九）产生工业固体废物的单位违反本法规定委托他人运输、利用、处置工业固体废物的；  
（十）贮存工业固体废物未采取符合国家环境保护标准的防护措施的；  
（十一）单位和其他生产经营者违反固体废物管理其他要求，污染环境、破坏生态的。  
有前款第一项、第八项行为之一，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；有前款第二项、第三项、第四项、第五项、第六项、第九项、第十项、第十一项行为之一，处十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；有前款第七项行为，处所需处置费用一倍以上三倍以下的罚款，所需处置费用不足十万元的，按十万元计算。对前款第十一项行为的处罚，有关法律、行政法规另有规定的，适用其规定。

**Article 102** Any entity that, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the competent department of ecological environment to make corrections, be fined, and have its illegal gains confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the matter shall be reported to the people's government with approval authority for approval, and the entity may be ordered to suspend business or close down:  
(1) a unit that generates, collects, stores, transports, utilizes, or disposes of solid wastes fails to disclose information on the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid wastes in a timely manner according to law;  
(2) a household waste treatment unit fails to install and use monitoring equipment, monitor the discharge of pollutants in real time, and disclose pollutant discharge data in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(3) transferring equipment that has been eliminated as listed in the list of equipment to be eliminated within a prescribed time limit to another person;  
(4) constructing facilities and sites for centralized storage, utilization, and disposal of industrial solid wastes and hazardous wastes, and landfills for household waste within ecological protection red line areas, concentrated areas of permanent basic farmland, and other areas requiring special protection;  
(5) transferring solid wastes out of the administrative region of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for storage or disposal without approval;  
(6) transferring solid wastes out of the administrative region of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for utilization without filing for record;  
(7) dumping, piling up, discarding, or scattering industrial solid wastes without authorization, or failing to take corresponding preventive measures, causing the scattering, loss, seepage, or other environmental pollution of industrial solid wastes;  
(8) a unit that generates industrial solid wastes fails to establish a solid waste management ledger and keep truthful records;  
(9) a unit that generates industrial solid wastes, in violation of the provisions of this Law, entrusts another person to transport, utilize, or dispose of industrial solid wastes;  
(10) failing to take protective measures that meet the national environmental protection standards for storing industrial solid wastes; or  
(11) a unit or other business operator violates other requirements for solid waste management, polluting the environment or damaging the ecosystem.  
For an act specified in item (1) or (8) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; for an act specified in item (2), (3), (4), (5), (6), (9), (10), or (11) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; for an act specified in item (7) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the necessary disposal costs shall be imposed, and if the necessary disposal costs are less than 100,000 yuan, the calculation shall be based on 100,000 yuan. Where there are other provisions in relevant laws or administrative regulations on the penalty for an act specified in item (11) of the preceding paragraph, those provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　违反本法规定，以拖延、围堵、滞留执法人员等方式拒绝、阻挠监督检查，或者在接受监督检查时弄虚作假的，由生态环境主管部门或者其他负有固体废物污染环境防治监督管理职责的部门责令改正，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 103** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, refuses or obstructs supervision and inspection by means of delaying, surrounding, detaining law enforcement officers, or engages in fraud during supervision and inspection shall be ordered by the competent department of ecological environment or another department responsible for the supervision and administration of the prevention and control of environmental pollution by solid wastes to make corrections, and be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　违反本法规定，未依法取得排污许可证产生工业固体废物的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正或者限制生产、停产整治，处十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业或者关闭。

**Article 104** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, generates industrial solid wastes without obtaining a pollutant discharge permit according to law shall be ordered by the competent department of ecological environment to make corrections, or be subject to production restrictions or suspension of production for remediation, and be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the matter shall be reported to the people's government with approval authority for approval, and the person shall be ordered to suspend business or close down.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　违反本法规定，生产经营者未遵守限制商品过度包装的强制性标准的，由县级以上地方人民政府市场监督管理部门或者有关部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处二千元以上二万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 105** Any business operator who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, fails to comply with the mandatory standards restricting excessive packaging of commodities shall be ordered by the market supervision and administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the relevant department to make corrections; if the business operator refuses to make corrections, it shall be fined not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　违反本法规定，未遵守国家有关禁止、限制使用不可降解塑料袋等一次性塑料制品的规定，或者未按照国家有关规定报告塑料袋等一次性塑料制品的使用情况的，由县级以上地方人民政府商务、邮政等主管部门责令改正，处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 106** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, fails to comply with the State's provisions on prohibiting or restricting the use of non-degradable plastic bags and other disposable plastic products, or fails to report the use of plastic bags and other disposable plastic products in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, shall be ordered by the competent departments of commerce, postal services, etc. of the local people's government at or above the county level to make corrections, and be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　从事畜禽规模养殖未及时收集、贮存、利用或者处置养殖过程中产生的畜禽粪污等固体废物的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，可以处十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业或者关闭。

**Article 107** Any person engaged in large-scale livestock and poultry breeding who fails to collect, store, utilize, or dispose of solid wastes such as livestock and poultry manure generated during the breeding process in a timely manner shall be ordered by the competent department of ecological environment to make corrections, and may be fined not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the matter shall be reported to the people's government with approval authority for approval, and the person shall be ordered to suspend business or close down.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　违反本法规定，城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位或者污泥处理单位对污泥流向、用途、用量等未进行跟踪、记录，或者处理后的污泥不符合国家有关标准的，由城镇排水主管部门责令改正，给予警告；造成严重后果的，处十万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，城镇排水主管部门可以指定有治理能力的单位代为治理，所需费用由违法者承担。  
违反本法规定，擅自倾倒、堆放、丢弃、遗撒城镇污水处理设施产生的污泥和处理后的污泥的，由城镇排水主管部门责令改正，处二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款；造成严重后果的，处二百万元以上五百万元以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，城镇排水主管部门可以指定有治理能力的单位代为治理，所需费用由违法者承担。

**Article 108** Any maintenance and operation unit of an urban sewage treatment facility or sludge treatment unit that, in violation of the provisions of this Law, fails to track and record the flow direction, use, and quantity of sludge, or treats sludge that does not meet the relevant national standards, shall be ordered by the competent department of urban drainage to make corrections and be given a warning; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the unit refuses to make corrections, the competent department of urban drainage may designate a unit with remediation capability to carry out remediation on its behalf, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator.  
Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, dumps, piles up, discards, or scatters sludge generated from urban sewage treatment facilities or treated sludge without authorization shall be ordered by the competent department of urban drainage to make corrections, and be fined not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 2,000,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the person refuses to make corrections, the competent department of urban drainage may designate a unit with remediation capability to carry out remediation on its behalf, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　违反本法规定，生产、销售、进口或者使用淘汰的设备，或者采用淘汰的生产工艺的，由县级以上地方人民政府指定的部门责令改正，处十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款，没收违法所得；情节严重的，由县级以上地方人民政府指定的部门提出意见，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业或者关闭。

**Article 109** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, produces, sells, imports, or uses eliminated equipment, or adopts eliminated production processes shall be ordered by the department designated by the local people's government at or above the county level to make corrections, be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan, and have its illegal gains confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the department designated by the local people's government at or above the county level shall put forward its opinion, and after reporting to and obtaining approval from the people's government with approval authority, the person shall be ordered to suspend business or close down.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　尾矿、煤矸石、废石等矿业固体废物贮存设施停止使用后，未按照国家有关环境保护规定进行封场的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，处二十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 110** Where, after the cessation of use of storage facilities for mining solid wastes such as tailings, coal gangue, and waste rock, the facility is not closed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State on environmental protection, the competent department of ecological environment shall order corrections and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由县级以上地方人民政府环境卫生主管部门责令改正，处以罚款，没收违法所得：  
（一）随意倾倒、抛撒、堆放或者焚烧生活垃圾的；  
（二）擅自关闭、闲置或者拆除生活垃圾处理设施、场所的；  
（三）工程施工单位未编制建筑垃圾处理方案报备案，或者未及时清运施工过程中产生的固体废物的；  
（四）工程施工单位擅自倾倒、抛撒或者堆放工程施工过程中产生的建筑垃圾，或者未按照规定对施工过程中产生的固体废物进行利用或者处置的；  
（五）产生、收集厨余垃圾的单位和其他生产经营者未将厨余垃圾交由具备相应资质条件的单位进行无害化处理的；  
（六）畜禽养殖场、养殖小区利用未经无害化处理的厨余垃圾饲喂畜禽的；  
（七）在运输过程中沿途丢弃、遗撒生活垃圾的。  
单位有前款第一项、第七项行为之一，处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；单位有前款第二项、第三项、第四项、第五项、第六项行为之一，处十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；个人有前款第一项、第五项、第七项行为之一，处一百元以上五百元以下的罚款。  
违反本法规定，未在指定的地点分类投放生活垃圾的，由县级以上地方人民政府环境卫生主管部门责令改正；情节严重的，对单位处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，对个人依法处以罚款。

**Article 111** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the competent department of environmental sanitation of the local people's government at or above the county level to make corrections, be fined, and have its illegal gains confiscated:  
(1) dumping, scattering, piling up, or incinerating household waste at will;  
(2) closing, leaving idle, or dismantling household waste treatment facilities or sites without authorization;  
(3) a construction unit fails to prepare a construction waste treatment plan for filing, or fails to promptly remove solid wastes generated during the construction process;  
(4) a construction unit dumps, scatters, or piles up construction waste generated during the construction process without authorization, or fails to utilize or dispose of solid wastes generated during the construction process in accordance with the provisions;  
(5) a unit that generates or collects kitchen waste or other business operator fails to deliver kitchen waste to a unit with corresponding qualifications for harmless treatment;  
(6) a livestock and poultry farm or farming community uses kitchen waste that has not been harmlessly treated to feed livestock and poultry;  
(7) discarding or scattering household waste along the way during transportation.  
Where a unit commits an act specified in item (1) or (7) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; where a unit commits an act specified in item (2), (3), (4), (5), or (6) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; where an individual commits an act specified in item (1), (5), or (7) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than 100 yuan but not more than 500 yuan shall be imposed.  
Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, fails to sort and deposit household waste at designated locations shall be ordered by the competent department of environmental sanitation of the local people's government at or above the county level to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, a unit shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and an individual shall be fined according to law.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，处以罚款，没收违法所得；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，可以责令停业或者关闭：  
（一）未按照规定设置危险废物识别标志的；  
（二）未按照国家有关规定制定危险废物管理计划或者申报危险废物有关资料的；  
（三）擅自倾倒、堆放危险废物的；  
（四）将危险废物提供或者委托给无许可证的单位或者其他生产经营者从事经营活动的；  
（五）未按照国家有关规定填写、运行危险废物转移联单或者未经批准擅自转移危险废物的；  
（六）未按照国家环境保护标准贮存、利用、处置危险废物或者将危险废物混入非危险废物中贮存的；  
（七）未经安全性处置，混合收集、贮存、运输、处置具有不相容性质的危险废物的；  
（八）将危险废物与旅客在同一运输工具上载运的；  
（九）未经消除污染处理，将收集、贮存、运输、处置危险废物的场所、设施、设备和容器、包装物及其他物品转作他用的；  
（十）未采取相应防范措施，造成危险废物扬散、流失、渗漏或者其他环境污染的；  
（十一）在运输过程中沿途丢弃、遗撒危险废物的；  
（十二）未制定危险废物意外事故防范措施和应急预案的；  
（十三）未按照国家有关规定建立危险废物管理台账并如实记录的。  
有前款第一项、第二项、第五项、第六项、第七项、第八项、第九项、第十二项、第十三项行为之一，处十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；有前款第三项、第四项、第十项、第十一项行为之一，处所需处置费用三倍以上五倍以下的罚款，所需处置费用不足二十万元的，按二十万元计算。

**Article 112** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the competent department of ecological environment to make corrections, be fined, and have its illegal gains confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the matter shall be reported to the people's government with approval authority for approval, and the person may be ordered to suspend business or close down:  
(1) failing to set up identification signs for hazardous waste in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) failing to formulate a hazardous waste management plan or declare information on hazardous waste in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(3) dumping or piling up hazardous waste without authorization;  
(4) providing or entrusting hazardous waste to a unit without a permit or to another business operator to engage in business activities;  
(5) failing to fill in and operate a hazardous waste transfer manifest in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, or transferring hazardous waste without authorization or approval;  
(6) failing to store, utilize, or dispose of hazardous waste in accordance with national environmental protection standards, or mixing hazardous waste with non-hazardous waste for storage;  
(7) mixing, collecting, storing, transporting, or disposing of hazardous wastes with incompatible properties without safety treatment;  
(8) carrying hazardous waste together with passengers on the same means of transport;  
(9) converting places, facilities, equipment, containers, packaging, and other items used for collecting, storing, transporting, or disposing of hazardous waste to other uses without decontamination treatment;  
(10) failing to take corresponding preventive measures, causing the scattering, loss, seepage, or other environmental pollution of hazardous waste;  
(11) discarding or scattering hazardous waste along the way during transportation;  
(12) failing to formulate preventive measures and emergency plans for hazardous waste accidents; or  
(13) failing to establish a hazardous waste management ledger and keep truthful records in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
For an act specified in item (1), (2), (5), (6), (7), (8), (9), (12), or (13) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; for an act specified in item (3), (4), (10), or (11) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the necessary disposal costs shall be imposed, and if the necessary disposal costs are less than 200,000 yuan, the calculation shall be based on 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　违反本法规定，危险废物产生者未按照规定处置其产生的危险废物被责令改正后拒不改正的，由生态环境主管部门组织代为处置，处置费用由危险废物产生者承担；拒不承担代为处置费用的，处代为处置费用一倍以上三倍以下的罚款。

**Article 113** Where a generator of hazardous waste who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, fails to dispose of the hazardous waste generated by it in accordance with the provisions and, after being ordered to make corrections, refuses to do so, the competent department of ecological environment shall arrange for the disposal on its behalf, and the disposal costs shall be borne by the generator of the hazardous waste; if the generator refuses to bear the costs of the disposal on its behalf, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the costs of the disposal on its behalf shall be imposed.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　无许可证从事收集、贮存、利用、处置危险废物经营活动的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，处一百万元以上五百万元以下的罚款，并报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业或者关闭；对法定代表人、主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他责任人员，处十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款。  
未按照许可证规定从事收集、贮存、利用、处置危险废物经营活动的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，限制生产、停产整治，处五十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款；对法定代表人、主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他责任人员，处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业或者关闭，还可以由发证机关吊销许可证。

**Article 114** Any person who engages in the business activities of collecting, storing, utilizing, or disposing of hazardous waste without a license shall be ordered by the competent department of ecological environment to make corrections, be fined not less than 1,000,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan, and after reporting to and obtaining approval from the people's government with approval authority, shall be ordered to suspend business or close down; the legal representative, principal responsible person, directly responsible persons in charge, and other liable persons shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan.  
Any person who engages in the business activities of collecting, storing, utilizing, or disposing of hazardous waste not in accordance with the provisions of the license shall be ordered by the competent department of ecological environment to make corrections, be subject to production restrictions or suspension of production for remediation, and be fined not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan; the legal representative, principal responsible person, directly responsible persons in charge, and other liable persons shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, after reporting to and obtaining approval from the people's government with approval authority, the person shall be ordered to suspend business or close down, and the licensing authority may also revoke the license.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　违反本法规定，将中华人民共和国境外的固体废物输入境内的，由海关责令退运该固体废物，处五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款。  
承运人对前款规定的固体废物的退运、处置，与进口者承担连带责任。

**Article 115** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, imports solid wastes from outside the territory of the People's Republic of China into the territory shall be ordered by the Customs to return the solid wastes, and be fined not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan.  
The carrier shall bear joint and several liability with the importer for the return and disposal of the solid wastes specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　违反本法规定，经中华人民共和国过境转移危险废物的，由海关责令退运该危险废物，处五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 116** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, transfers hazardous waste through the territory of the People's Republic of China in transit shall be ordered by the Customs to return the hazardous waste, and be fined not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　对已经非法入境的固体废物，由省级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门依法向海关提出处理意见，海关应当依照[本法第一百一十五条](#art-115)的规定作出处罚决定；已经造成环境污染的，由省级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门责令进口者消除污染。

**Article 117** With respect to solid wastes that have already been illegally imported, the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall, according to law, submit its handling opinion to the Customs, and the Customs shall make a penalty decision in accordance with the provisions of Article 115 of this Law; if environmental pollution has already been caused, the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall order the importer to eliminate the pollution.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　违反本法规定，造成固体废物污染环境事故的，除依法承担赔偿责任外，由生态环境主管部门依照本条第二款的规定处以罚款，责令限期采取治理措施；造成重大或者特大固体废物污染环境事故的，还可以报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令关闭。  
造成一般或者较大固体废物污染环境事故的，按照事故造成的直接经济损失的一倍以上三倍以下计算罚款；造成重大或者特大固体废物污染环境事故的，按照事故造成的直接经济损失的三倍以上五倍以下计算罚款，并对法定代表人、主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他责任人员处上一年度从本单位取得的收入百分之五十以下的罚款。

**Article 118** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, causes an environmental pollution accident involving solid wastes shall, in addition to bearing compensation liability according to law, be fined by the competent department of ecological environment in accordance with the provisions of the second paragraph of this Article, and be ordered to take control measures within a specified time limit; if a major or very major environmental pollution accident involving solid wastes is caused, the matter may also be reported to the people's government with approval authority for approval, and the person may be ordered to close down.  
Where a general or relatively major environmental pollution accident involving solid wastes is caused, the fine shall be calculated at not less than one time but not more than three times the direct economic losses caused by the accident; where a major or very major environmental pollution accident involving solid wastes is caused, the fine shall be calculated at not less than three times but not more than five times the direct economic losses caused by the accident, and the legal representative, principal responsible person, directly responsible persons in charge, and other liable persons shall be fined not more than 50 percent of the income they received from the entity in the previous year.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　单位和其他生产经营者违反本法规定排放固体废物，受到罚款处罚，被责令改正的，依法作出处罚决定的行政机关应当组织复查，发现其继续实施该违法行为的，依照《中华人民共和国环境保护法》的规定按日连续处罚。

**Article 119** Where a unit or other business operator discharges solid wastes in violation of the provisions of this Law, is subject to a fine penalty, and is ordered to make corrections, the administrative organ that made the penalty decision according to law shall organize a re-inspection; if it finds that the unit or operator continues to commit the illegal act, a continuous fine on a daily basis shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Environmental Protection Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，尚不构成犯罪的，由公安机关对法定代表人、主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他责任人员处十日以上十五日以下的拘留；情节较轻的，处五日以上十日以下的拘留：  
（一）擅自倾倒、堆放、丢弃、遗撒固体废物，造成严重后果的；  
（二）在生态保护红线区域、永久基本农田集中区域和其他需要特别保护的区域内，建设工业固体废物、危险废物集中贮存、利用、处置的设施、场所和生活垃圾填埋场的；  
（三）将危险废物提供或者委托给无许可证的单位或者其他生产经营者堆放、利用、处置的；  
（四）无许可证或者未按照许可证规定从事收集、贮存、利用、处置危险废物经营活动的；  
（五）未经批准擅自转移危险废物的；  
（六）未采取防范措施，造成危险废物扬散、流失、渗漏或者其他严重后果的。

**Article 120** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts, if the act does not constitute a crime, shall be detained by the public security organ for not less than 10 days but not more than 15 days for the legal representative, principal responsible person, directly responsible persons in charge, and other liable persons; if the circumstances are minor, the detention shall be for not less than 5 days but not more than 10 days:  
(1) dumping, piling up, discarding, or scattering solid wastes without authorization, causing serious consequences;  
(2) constructing facilities and sites for centralized storage, utilization, and disposal of industrial solid wastes and hazardous wastes, and landfills for household waste within ecological protection red line areas, concentrated areas of permanent basic farmland, and other areas requiring special protection;  
(3) providing or entrusting hazardous waste to a unit without a license or to another business operator for piling up, utilization, or disposal;  
(4) engaging in the business activities of collecting, storing, utilizing, or disposing of hazardous waste without a license or not in accordance with the provisions of the license;  
(5) transferring hazardous waste without authorization or approval; or  
(6) failing to take preventive measures, causing scattering, loss, seepage, or other serious consequences of hazardous waste.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　固体废物污染环境、破坏生态，损害国家利益、社会公共利益的，有关机关和组织可以依照《中华人民共和国环境保护法》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》、《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等法律的规定向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 121** Where solid wastes pollute the environment or damage the ecosystem, harming the national interest or the public interest, the relevant organs and organizations may file a lawsuit with the People's Court in accordance with the provisions of the Environmental Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and other laws.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　固体废物污染环境、破坏生态给国家造成重大损失的，由设区的市级以上地方人民政府或者其指定的部门、机构组织与造成环境污染和生态破坏的单位和其他生产经营者进行磋商，要求其承担损害赔偿责任；磋商未达成一致的，可以向人民法院提起诉讼。  
对于执法过程中查获的无法确定责任人或者无法退运的固体废物，由所在地县级以上地方人民政府组织处理。

**Article 122** Where solid wastes pollute the environment or damage the ecosystem, causing significant losses to the State, the local people's government at or above the city with districts level or the department or institution designated by it shall organize consultations with the unit or other business operator that caused the environmental pollution and ecological damage, demanding that it bear compensation liability for the damage; if no agreement is reached through consultations, a lawsuit may be filed with the People's Court.  
For solid wastes seized during law enforcement that cannot be attributed to a responsible person or cannot be returned, the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize their disposal.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；造成人身、财产损害的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 123** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits an act that constitutes a violation of public security administration shall be given a public security administration penalty by the public security organ according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if personal injury or property damage is caused, civil liability shall be borne according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　本法下列用语的含义：  
（一）固体废物，是指在生产、生活和其他活动中产生的丧失原有利用价值或者虽未丧失利用价值但被抛弃或者放弃的固态、半固态和置于容器中的气态的物品、物质以及法律、行政法规规定纳入固体废物管理的物品、物质。经无害化加工处理，并且符合强制性国家产品质量标准，不会危害公众健康和生态安全，或者根据固体废物鉴别标准和鉴别程序认定为不属于固体废物的除外。  
（二）工业固体废物，是指在工业生产活动中产生的固体废物。  
（三）生活垃圾，是指在日常生活中或者为日常生活提供服务的活动中产生的固体废物，以及法律、行政法规规定视为生活垃圾的固体废物。  
（四）建筑垃圾，是指建设单位、施工单位新建、改建、扩建和拆除各类建筑物、构筑物、管网等，以及居民装饰装修房屋过程中产生的弃土、弃料和其他固体废物。  
（五）农业固体废物，是指在农业生产活动中产生的固体废物。  
（六）危险废物，是指列入国家危险废物名录或者根据国家规定的危险废物鉴别标准和鉴别方法认定的具有危险特性的固体废物。  
（七）贮存，是指将固体废物临时置于特定设施或者场所中的活动。  
（八）利用，是指从固体废物中提取物质作为原材料或者燃料的活动。  
（九）处置，是指将固体废物焚烧和用其他改变固体废物的物理、化学、生物特性的方法，达到减少已产生的固体废物数量、缩小固体废物体积、减少或者消除其危险成分的活动，或者将固体废物最终置于符合环境保护规定要求的填埋场的活动。

**Article 124** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms have the meanings ascribed to them below:  
(1) "Solid wastes" means articles and substances in solid, semi-solid, or gaseous form contained in containers that are generated in production, daily life, and other activities and that have lost their original use value or have been discarded or abandoned even though they have not lost their use value, as well as articles and substances that are included in the management of solid wastes under laws and administrative regulations. However, this term does not include articles and substances that, after harmless processing treatment and meeting mandatory national product quality standards, will not endanger public health or ecological security, or that are determined not to be solid wastes according to the solid waste identification standards and procedures.  
(2) "Industrial solid wastes" means solid wastes generated in industrial production activities.  
(3) "Household waste" means solid wastes generated in daily life or in activities providing services for daily life, as well as solid wastes that are considered household waste under laws and administrative regulations.  
(4) "Construction waste" means spoil, waste materials, and other solid wastes generated during the construction, renovation, expansion, or demolition of various buildings, structures, pipe networks, etc. by construction entities and construction units, as well as during the decoration of residential houses.  
(5) "Agricultural solid wastes" means solid wastes generated in agricultural production activities.  
(6) "Hazardous waste" means solid wastes that are included in the National Catalogue of Hazardous Wastes or that are identified as having hazardous characteristics according to the national hazardous waste identification standards and methods.  
(7) "Storage" means the activity of temporarily placing solid wastes in specific facilities or sites.  
(8) "Utilization" means the activity of extracting substances from solid wastes as raw materials or fuel.  
(9) "Disposal" means the activity of incinerating solid wastes or using other methods to change the physical, chemical, or biological characteristics of solid wastes so as to reduce the quantity of generated solid wastes, reduce the volume of solid wastes, or reduce or eliminate their hazardous components, or the activity of ultimately placing solid wastes in a landfill that meets the environmental protection requirements.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　液态废物的污染防治，适用本法；但是，排入水体的废水的污染防治适用有关法律，不适用本法。

**Article 125** This Law shall apply to the prevention and control of pollution from liquid wastes; however, the prevention and control of pollution from wastewater discharged into water bodies shall be governed by the relevant laws and not by this Law.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条　本法自2020年9月1日起施行。

**Article 126** This Law shall come into force as of September 1, 2020.
