# 最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律若干问题的解释（二）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2006〕6号  
**公布日期**：2006-07-10  
**生效日期**：2006-10-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律若干问题的解释（二）》已于2006年7月10日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1393次会议通过，现予公布，自2006年10月1日起施行。 2006年8月14日 最高人民法院 关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律 若干问题的解释（二）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  人民法院审理劳动争议案件，对下列情形，视为[劳动法第八十二条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动法.md#art-82)规定的“劳动争议发生之日”：  
（一）在劳动关系存续期间产生的支付工资争议，用人单位能够证明已经书面通知劳动者拒付工资的，书面通知送达之日为劳动争议发生之日。用人单位不能证明的，劳动者主张权利之日为劳动争议发生之日。  
（二）因解除或者终止劳动关系产生的争议，用人单位不能证明劳动者收到解除或者终止劳动关系书面通知时间的，劳动者主张权利之日为劳动争议发生之日。  
（三）劳动关系解除或者终止后产生的支付工资、经济补偿金、福利待遇等争议，劳动者能够证明用人单位承诺支付的时间为解除或者终止劳动关系后的具体日期的，用人单位承诺支付之日为劳动争议发生之日。劳动者不能证明的，解除或者终止劳动关系之日为劳动争议发生之日。

**Article 1** When a People's Court hears a labor dispute case, the following circumstances shall be deemed as the "date of occurrence of the labor dispute" as provided in Article 82 of the Labor Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Where a dispute arises over payment of wages during the existence of the labor relationship, if the employing unit can prove that it has notified the worker in writing of its refusal to pay wages, the date of service of such written notice shall be the date of occurrence of the labor dispute. If the employing unit cannot prove the same, the date on which the worker asserts his rights shall be the date of occurrence of the labor dispute.  
(2) Where a dispute arises due to dissolution or termination of the labor relationship, if the employing unit cannot prove the date on which the worker received the written notice of dissolution or termination of the labor relationship, the date on which the worker asserts his rights shall be the date of occurrence of the labor dispute.  
(3) Where a dispute arises after dissolution or termination of the labor relationship concerning payment of wages, economic compensation, welfare benefits, etc., if the worker can prove that the employing unit promised to pay on a specific date after the dissolution or termination of the labor relationship, the date on which the employing unit promised to pay shall be the date of occurrence of the labor dispute. If the worker cannot prove the same, the date of dissolution or termination of the labor relationship shall be the date of occurrence of the labor dispute.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  拖欠工资争议，劳动者申请仲裁时劳动关系仍然存续，用人单位以劳动者申请仲裁超过六十日为由主张不再支付的，人民法院不予支持。但用人单位能够证明劳动者已经收到拒付工资的书面通知的除外。

**Article 2** Where a dispute arises over overdue wages, and the labor relationship still exists at the time when the worker applies for arbitration, if the employing unit asserts that it shall no longer make payment on the ground that the worker's application for arbitration exceeds sixty days, the People's Court shall not uphold such assertion, except where the employing unit can prove that the worker has received a written notice of refusal to pay wages.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  劳动者以用人单位的工资欠条为证据直接向人民法院起诉，诉讼请求不涉及劳动关系其他争议的，视为拖欠劳动报酬争议，按照普通民事纠纷受理。

**Article 3** Where a worker, using the employing unit's wage IOU as evidence, directly prosecutes in the People's Court, and the claim does not involve other disputes concerning the labor relationship, it shall be deemed as a dispute over overdue labor remuneration, and shall be accepted as an ordinary civil dispute.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  用人单位和劳动者因劳动关系是否已经解除或者终止，以及应否支付解除或终止劳动关系经济补偿金产生的争议，经劳动争议仲裁委员会仲裁后，当事人依法起诉的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 4** Where a dispute arises between the employing unit and the worker concerning whether the labor relationship has been dissolved or terminated, and whether economic compensation for dissolution or termination of the labor relationship shall be paid, after arbitration by the labor dispute arbitration commission, if the party prosecutes according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  劳动者与用人单位解除或者终止劳动关系后，请求用人单位返还其收取的劳动合同定金、保证金、抵押金、抵押物产生的争议，或者办理劳动者的人事档案、社会保险关系等移转手续产生的争议，经劳动争议仲裁委员会仲裁后，当事人依法起诉的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 5** After dissolution or termination of the labor relationship between the worker and the employing unit, a dispute arising from the worker's request for the employing unit to return the deposit, guarantee deposit, mortgage money, or mortgaged property collected by the employing unit, or a dispute arising from the handling of transfer procedures for the worker's personnel file, social insurance relationship, etc., after arbitration by the labor dispute arbitration commission, if the party prosecutes according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  劳动者因为工伤、职业病，请求用人单位依法承担给予工伤保险待遇的争议，经劳动争议仲裁委员会仲裁后，当事人依法起诉的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 6** Where a dispute arises from a worker's request for the employing unit to bear the obligation of providing work-related injury insurance benefits according to law due to work-related injury or occupational disease, after arbitration by the labor dispute arbitration commission, if the party prosecutes according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  下列纠纷不属于劳动争议：  
（一）劳动者请求社会保险经办机构发放社会保险金的纠纷；  
（二）劳动者与用人单位因住房制度改革产生的公有住房转让纠纷；  
（三）劳动者对劳动能力鉴定委员会的伤残等级鉴定结论或者对职业病诊断鉴定委员会的职业病诊断鉴定结论的异议纠纷；  
（四）家庭或者个人与家政服务人员之间的纠纷；  
（五）个体工匠与帮工、学徒之间的纠纷；  
（六）农村承包经营户与受雇人之间的纠纷。

**Article 7** The following disputes shall not be labor disputes:  
(1) A dispute arising from a worker's request for a social insurance agency to disburse social insurance money;  
(2) A dispute over the transfer of public housing arising between a worker and the employing unit due to the housing system reform;  
(3) A dispute over a worker's objection to the disability grade evaluation conclusion made by the Labor Ability Appraisal Committee or the occupational disease diagnosis evaluation conclusion made by the Occupational Disease Diagnosis Appraisal Committee;  
(4) A dispute between a family or individual and a domestic service worker;  
(5) A dispute between an individual craftsman and an assistant or apprentice;  
(6) A dispute between a rural contractual household and an employee.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  当事人不服劳动争议仲裁委员会作出的预先支付劳动者部分工资或者医疗费用的裁决，向人民法院起诉的，人民法院不予受理。  
用人单位不履行上述裁决中的给付义务，劳动者依法向人民法院申请强制执行的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 8** Where a party, dissatisfied with the ruling made by the labor dispute arbitration commission for advance payment of part of the worker's wages or medical expenses, prosecutes in the People's Court, the People's Court shall not accept the case.  
Where the employing unit fails to perform the payment obligation under the aforesaid ruling, and the worker applies to the People's Court for compulsory execution according to law, the People's Court shall accept the application.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  劳动者与起有字号的个体工商户产生的劳动争议诉讼，人民法院应当以营业执照上登记的字号为当事人，但应同时注明该字号业主的自然情况。

**Article 9** In a labor dispute lawsuit between a worker and an individual industrial and commercial household with a trade name, the People's Court shall name the trade name registered on the business license as the party, but shall at the same time indicate the natural particulars of the owner of such trade name.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  劳动者因履行劳动力派遣合同产生劳动争议而起诉，以派遣单位为被告；争议内容涉及接受单位的，以派遣单位和接受单位为共同被告。

**Article 10** Where a worker prosecutes due to a labor dispute arising from the performance of a labor dispatch contract, the dispatch unit shall be named as the defendant; if the content of the dispute involves the receiving unit, the dispatch unit and the receiving unit shall be named as co-defendants.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  劳动者和用人单位均不服劳动争议仲裁委员会的同一裁决，向同一人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当并案审理，双方当事人互为原告和被告。在诉讼过程中，一方当事人撤诉的，人民法院应当根据另一方当事人的诉讼请求继续审理。

**Article 11** Where both the worker and the employing unit are dissatisfied with the same ruling of the labor dispute arbitration commission and prosecute in the same People's Court, the People's Court shall consolidate the cases for trial, and the two parties shall act as plaintiff and defendant against each other. During the litigation, if one party withdraws the prosecution, the People's Court shall continue to hear the case based on the claim of the other party.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  当事人能够证明在申请仲裁期间内因不可抗力或者其他客观原因无法申请仲裁的，人民法院应当认定申请仲裁期间中止，从中止的原因消灭之次日起，申请仲裁期间连续计算。

**Article 12** If a party can prove that it was unable to apply for arbitration within the limitation period for applying for arbitration due to force majeure or other objective reasons, the People's Court shall find that the limitation period for applying for arbitration is suspended. The limitation period shall continue to run from the day after the cause of suspension ceases to exist.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  当事人能够证明在申请仲裁期间内具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定申请仲裁期间中断：  
(一)向对方当事人主张权利；  
(二)向有关部门请求权利救济；  
(三)对方当事人同意履行义务。  
申请仲裁期间中断的，从对方当事人明确拒绝履行义务，或者有关部门作出处理决定或明确表示不予处理时起，申请仲裁期间重新计算。

**Article 13** If a party can prove that any of the following circumstances occurred during the limitation period for applying for arbitration, the People's Court shall find that the limitation period is interrupted:  
(1) The party asserts its rights against the other party;  
(2) The party requests rights relief from the relevant authority;  
(3) The other party agrees to perform its obligations.  
Where the limitation period for applying for arbitration is interrupted, it shall be recalculated from the time when the other party expressly refuses to perform its obligations, or when the relevant authority makes a decision or expressly indicates that it will not handle the matter.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  在诉讼过程中，劳动者向人民法院申请采取财产保全措施，人民法院经审查认为申请人经济确有困难，或有证据证明用人单位存在欠薪逃匿可能的，应当减轻或者免除劳动者提供担保的义务，及时采取保全措施。

**Article 14** During the litigation process, if a worker applies to the People's Court for adoption of property preservation measures, and upon examination the People's Court considers that the applicant is genuinely in financial difficulty, or there is evidence proving that the employing unit may evade by absconding due to wage arrears, it shall reduce or exempt the worker from the obligation to provide a guarantee, and shall promptly take preservation measures.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  人民法院作出的财产保全裁定中，应当告知当事人在劳动仲裁机构的裁决书或者在人民法院的裁判文书生效后三个月内申请强制执行。逾期不申请的，人民法院应当裁定解除保全措施。

**Article 15** In a property preservation ruling made by the People's Court, it shall inform the party to apply for compulsory execution within three months after the ruling of the labor arbitration institution or the judgment document of the People's Court takes effect. If the application is not made within the time limit, the People's Court shall rule on the cancellation of the preservation measures.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  用人单位制定的内部规章制度与集体合同或者劳动合同约定的内容不一致，劳动者请求优先适用合同约定的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 16** Where the internal rules and regulations formulated by the employing unit are inconsistent with the content stipulated in the collective contract or the labor contract, and the worker requests that the contract stipulations be applied preferentially, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  当事人在劳动争议调解委员会主持下达成的具有劳动权利义务内容的调解协议，具有劳动合同的约束力，可以作为人民法院裁判的根据。  
当事人在劳动争议调解委员会主持下仅就劳动报酬争议达成调解协议，用人单位不履行调解协议确定的给付义务，劳动者直接向人民法院起诉的，人民法院可以按照普通民事纠纷受理。

**Article 17** A mediation agreement with content on labor rights and obligations reached by the parties under the auspices of the labor dispute mediation committee shall have the binding force of a labor contract, and may serve as the basis for the People's Court's judgment.  
Where the parties, under the auspices of the labor dispute mediation committee, only reach a mediation agreement on a labor remuneration dispute, and the employing unit fails to perform the payment obligation determined in the mediation agreement, if the worker directly prosecutes in the People's Court, the People's Court may accept the case as an ordinary civil dispute.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  本解释自二〇〇六年十月一日起施行。本解释施行前本院颁布的有关司法解释与本解释规定不一致的，以本解释的规定为准。  
本解释施行后，人民法院尚未审结的一审、二审案件适用本解释。本解释施行前已经审结的案件，不得适用本解释的规定进行再审。

**Article 18** This Interpretation shall come into force as of October 1, 2006. Where relevant judicial interpretations promulgated by this Court before the coming into force of this Interpretation are inconsistent with the provisions of this Interpretation, the provisions of this Interpretation shall prevail.  
After the coming into force of this Interpretation, cases of first instance and second instance that have not yet been concluded by the People's Court shall be governed by this Interpretation. Cases that have already been concluded before the coming into force of this Interpretation shall not be retried by applying the provisions of this Interpretation.
