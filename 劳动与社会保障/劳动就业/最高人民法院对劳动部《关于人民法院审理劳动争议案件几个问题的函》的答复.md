# 最高人民法院对劳动部《关于人民法院审理劳动争议案件几个问题的函》的答复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：函〔1989〕53号  
**公布日期**：1989-08-10

---

### 正文

一、下列劳动争议案件，当事人一方或者双方对劳动争议仲裁委员会的仲裁决定不服，在收到仲裁决定书之日起15日内向人民法院起诉，符合《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法（试行）》第八十一条规定的起诉条件的，人民法院应予受理：  
1、国务院《国营企业劳动争议处理暂行规定》第二条、第三十条和第三十二条规定的劳动争议案件；  
2、国务院《私营企业暂行条例》第二十九条规定的劳动争议案件；  
3、其他法律、法规规定应由人民法院审理的劳动争议案件。

**Article None** 1. For the following labor dispute cases, where one or both parties refuse to accept the arbitration decision of the labor dispute arbitration commission and initiate a prosecution in the People's Court within 15 days from the date of receipt of the written arbitration decision, if the conditions for prosecution stipulated in Article 81 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (for Trial Implementation) are satisfied, the People's Court shall accept the case:  
1. Labor dispute cases as specified in Articles 2, 30 and 32 of the Interim Provisions of the State Council on the Handling of Labor Disputes in State-owned Enterprises;  
2. Labor dispute cases as specified in Article 29 of the Interim Regulations of the State Council on Private Enterprises;  
3. Labor dispute cases that shall be tried by the People's Court pursuant to other laws and regulations.

二、劳动争议当事人对仲裁决定不服，向人民法院起诉的，人民法院仍应以争议的双方为诉讼当事人，不应将劳动争议仲裁委员会列为被告或者第三人。在判决书、裁定书、调解书中也不应含有撤销或者维持仲裁决定的内容。

**Article None** 2. Where a party to a labor dispute refuses to accept the arbitration decision and initiates a prosecution in the People's Court, the People's Court shall still take the two parties to the dispute as the litigation parties and shall not list the labor dispute arbitration commission as a defendant or a third party. No judgment, ruling, or mediation document shall contain any content revoking or upholding the arbitration decision.

三、劳动争议当事人按规定申请执行人民法院的判决、裁定、调解协议或者仲裁机关的仲裁决定的，人民法院应当依照民事诉讼法（试行）规定的执行程序办理。在执行中，对于企业拒绝给职工安排工作并且不发工资或者不给福利待遇的，人民法院可按民事诉讼法（试行）第一百七十九条的规定，通知银行或者信用社扣划应付的工资和应享受的福利待遇，必要时可责令企业赔偿该职工的实际损失。  
1989年8月10日

**Article None** 3. Where a party to a labor dispute applies for enforcement of a judgment, ruling, mediation agreement of the People's Court, or an arbitration decision of an arbitration authority in accordance with the provisions, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with the enforcement procedures stipulated in the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation). During enforcement, if an enterprise refuses to assign work to an employee and neither pays wages nor provides welfare benefits, the People's Court may, in accordance with the provisions of Article 179 of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation), notify a bank or credit cooperative to deduct and transfer the payable wages and the welfare benefits that shall be enjoyed, and, if necessary, may order the enterprise to compensate the employee for the actual losses.  
August 10, 1989
