### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强国境卫生检疫工作，防止传染病跨境传播，保障公众生命安全和身体健康，防范和化解公共卫生风险，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of strengthening frontier health and quarantine, preventing cross-border transmission of infectious diseases, ensuring public life safety and health, and preventing and mitigating public health risks.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国境卫生检疫及相关活动，适用本法。  
在中华人民共和国对外开放的口岸（以下简称口岸），海关依照本法规定履行检疫查验、传染病监测、卫生监督和应急处置等国境卫生检疫职责。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to frontier health and quarantine and related activities.  
At the ports open to foreign countries of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as ports), the Customs shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law, perform the frontier health and quarantine duties such as quarantine inspection, infectious disease surveillance, health supervision, and emergency response.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本法所称传染病，包括检疫传染病、监测传染病和其他需要在口岸采取相应卫生检疫措施的新发传染病、突发原因不明的传染病。  
检疫传染病目录，由国务院疾病预防控制部门会同海关总署编制、调整，报国务院批准后公布。监测传染病目录，由国务院疾病预防控制部门会同海关总署编制、调整并公布。  
检疫传染病目录、监测传染病目录应当根据境内外传染病暴发、流行情况和危害程度及时调整。

**Article 3** Infectious diseases as referred to in this Law include quarantinable infectious diseases, surveillance infectious diseases, and other emerging infectious diseases or infectious diseases with unknown causes for which corresponding health and quarantine measures need to be taken at ports.  
The catalogue of quarantinable infectious diseases shall be compiled and adjusted by the disease prevention and control department of the State Council jointly with the General Administration of Customs, and submitted to the State Council for approval before publication. The catalogue of surveillance infectious diseases shall be compiled and adjusted by the disease prevention and control department of the State Council jointly with the General Administration of Customs, and published.  
The catalogues of quarantinable infectious diseases and surveillance infectious diseases shall be adjusted in a timely manner based on the outbreak, prevalence, and degree of harm of infectious diseases at home and abroad.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国境卫生检疫工作坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持风险管理、科学施策、高效处置的原则，健全常态和应急相结合的口岸传染病防控体系。

**Article 4** Frontier health and quarantine work shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, adhere to the principles of risk management, scientific measures, and efficient handling, and improve the port infectious disease prevention and control system combining routine and emergency.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　海关总署统一管理全国国境卫生检疫工作。国务院卫生健康主管部门、国务院疾病预防控制部门和其他有关部门依据各自职责做好国境卫生检疫相关工作。  
口岸所在地县级以上地方人民政府应当将国境卫生检疫工作纳入传染病防治规划，加大对国境卫生检疫工作的支持力度。  
海关、卫生健康、疾病预防控制和其他有关部门在国境卫生检疫工作中应当密切配合，建立部门协调机制，强化信息共享和协同联动。  
国家依法强化边境管控措施，严密防范非法入境行为导致的传染病输入风险。

**Article 5** The General Administration of Customs shall uniformly administer the frontier health and quarantine work nationwide. The health department of the State Council, the disease prevention and control department of the State Council, and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective duties, properly carry out the relevant work of frontier health and quarantine.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level where the ports are located shall incorporate frontier health and quarantine work into the infectious disease prevention and control plan, and increase support for frontier health and quarantine work.  
The Customs, health, disease prevention and control, and other relevant departments shall closely cooperate in frontier health and quarantine work, establish a departmental coordination mechanism, and strengthen information sharing and coordinated action.  
The State shall, according to law, strengthen border control measures and strictly prevent the risk of infectious disease importation caused by illegal entry.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　海关依法履行国境卫生检疫职责，有关单位和个人应当予以配合，不得拒绝或者阻碍。  
海关履行国境卫生检疫职责，应当依法保护商业秘密、个人隐私和个人信息，不得侵犯有关单位和个人的合法权益。

**Article 6** The Customs shall, according to law, perform the frontier health and quarantine duties. Relevant units and individuals shall cooperate and shall not refuse or obstruct.  
When performing frontier health and quarantine duties, the Customs shall, according to law, protect trade secrets, personal privacy, and personal information, and shall not infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of relevant units and individuals.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家采取多种措施，加强口岸公共卫生能力建设，不断提升国境卫生检疫工作水平。

**Article 7** The State shall adopt various measures to strengthen the capacity building of port public health and continuously improve the level of frontier health and quarantine work.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家加强与其他国家或者地区以及有关国际组织在国境卫生检疫领域的交流合作。

**Article 8** The State shall strengthen exchanges and cooperation with other countries or regions and relevant international organizations in the field of frontier health and quarantine.

### 第二章　检疫查验

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　进境出境的人员、交通运输工具，集装箱等运输设备、货物、行李、邮包等物品及外包装（以下统称货物、物品），应当依法接受检疫查验，经海关准许，方可进境出境。  
享有外交、领事特权与豁免等相关待遇的人员，以及享有外交、领事特权与豁免等相关待遇的机构和人员的物品进境出境，在不影响其依法享有特权与豁免的前提下，应当依法接受检疫查验。

**Article 9** Persons entering or leaving the country, means of transport, containers and other transport equipment, goods, luggage, postal parcels and other articles and their outer packaging (hereinafter collectively referred to as goods and articles) shall, according to law, undergo quarantine inspection, and may only enter or leave the country upon permission by the Customs.  
Persons enjoying diplomatic and consular privileges and immunities and other related treatment, and articles of institutions and persons enjoying diplomatic and consular privileges and immunities and other related treatment entering or leaving the country shall, according to law, undergo quarantine inspection, provided that this does not affect the privileges and immunities they enjoy according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　进境出境的人员、交通运输工具、货物、物品，应当分别在最先到达的口岸和最后离开的口岸接受检疫查验；货物、物品也可以在海关指定的其他地点接受检疫查验。  
来自境外的交通运输工具因不可抗力或者其他紧急原因停靠、降落在境内口岸以外地区的，交通运输工具负责人应当立即向就近的海关报告，接到报告的海关应当立即派员到场处理，必要时可以请求当地人民政府疾病预防控制部门予以协助；除避险等紧急情况外，未经海关准许，该交通运输工具不得装卸货物、物品，不得上下引航员以外的人员。

**Article 10** Persons, means of transport, goods, and articles entering or leaving the country shall undergo quarantine inspection at the port of first arrival and the port of last departure respectively; goods and articles may also undergo quarantine inspection at other places designated by the Customs.  
If a means of transport from abroad stops or lands at a place other than a port within the territory due to force majeure or other emergency reasons, the person in charge of the means of transport shall immediately report to the nearest Customs. The Customs that receives the report shall immediately dispatch personnel to the scene to handle the matter, and may, when necessary, request assistance from the disease prevention and control department of the local people's government. Except for emergency situations such as avoiding danger, without permission by the Customs, the means of transport shall not load or unload goods or articles, or embark or disembark persons other than the pilot.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对进境出境人员，海关可以要求如实申报健康状况及相关信息，进行体温检测、医学巡查，必要时可以查阅旅行证件。  
除前款规定的检疫查验措施外，海关还可以根据情况对有关进境出境人员实施下列检疫查验措施：  
（一）要求提供疫苗接种证明或者其他预防措施证明并进行核查；  
（二）进行流行病学调查、医学检查；  
（三）法律、行政法规规定的其他检疫查验措施。  
进境的外国人拒绝接受本条规定的检疫查验措施的，海关可以作出不准其进境的决定，并同时通知移民管理机构。

**Article 11** For persons entering or leaving the country, the Customs may require truthful declaration of health status and related information, carry out temperature measurement and medical inspection, and may, when necessary, examine travel documents.  
In addition to the quarantine inspection measures prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the Customs may also, depending on the circumstances, implement the following quarantine inspection measures on relevant persons entering or leaving the country:  
(1) requiring the provision of vaccination certificates or other preventive measure certificates and conducting verification;  
(2) carrying out epidemiological investigation and medical examination;  
(3) other quarantine inspection measures prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
If a foreigner entering the country refuses to accept the quarantine inspection measures prescribed in this Article, the Customs may decide to prohibit him/her from entering the country, and shall simultaneously notify the immigration administration authority.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　海关依据检疫医师提供的检疫查验结果，对判定为检疫传染病染疫人、疑似染疫人的，应当立即采取有效的现场防控措施，并及时通知口岸所在地县级以上地方人民政府疾病预防控制部门。接到通知的疾病预防控制部门应当及时组织将检疫传染病染疫人、疑似染疫人接送至县级以上地方人民政府指定的医疗机构或者其他场所实施隔离治疗或者医学观察。有关医疗机构和场所应当及时接收。  
对可能患有监测传染病的人员，海关应当发给就诊方便卡，并及时通知口岸所在地县级以上地方人民政府疾病预防控制部门。对持有就诊方便卡的人员，医疗机构应当优先诊治。

**Article 12** The Customs shall, based on the quarantine inspection results provided by quarantine physicians, immediately take effective on-site prevention and control measures for persons determined to be persons with quarantinable infectious diseases or suspected persons, and shall promptly notify the disease prevention and control department of the local people's government at or above the county level where the port is located. The disease prevention and control department that receives the notification shall promptly arrange for the transportation of the persons with quarantinable infectious diseases or suspected persons to medical institutions or other places designated by the local people's government at or above the county level for isolation treatment or medical observation. The relevant medical institutions and places shall promptly receive them.  
For persons who may have surveillance infectious diseases, the Customs shall issue a convenient card for medical treatment, and promptly notify the disease prevention and control department of the local people's government at or above the county level where the port is located. For persons holding a convenient card for medical treatment, medical institutions shall give priority to diagnosis and treatment.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　进境出境交通运输工具负责人应当按照规定向海关如实申报与检疫查验有关的事项。

**Article 13** The person in charge of a means of transport entering or leaving the country shall, in accordance with the provisions, truthfully declare to the Customs matters related to quarantine inspection.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　海关可以登临交通运输工具进行检疫查验，对符合规定条件的，可以采取电讯方式进行检疫查验。  
除避险等紧急情况外，进境的交通运输工具在检疫查验结束前、出境的交通运输工具在检疫查验结束后至出境前，未经海关准许，不得驶离指定的检疫查验地点，不得装卸货物、物品，不得上下引航员以外的人员。

**Article 14** The Customs may board the means of transport for quarantine inspection, and for those that meet the prescribed conditions, may conduct quarantine inspection by means of telecommunications.  
Except for emergency situations such as avoiding danger, before the quarantine inspection of an incoming means of transport is completed, or after the quarantine inspection of an outgoing means of transport is completed but before its departure, without permission by the Customs, the means of transport shall not leave the designated quarantine inspection place, load or unload goods or articles, or embark or disembark persons other than the pilot.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　进境出境交通运输工具有下列情形之一的，应当实施卫生处理，并接受海关监督；必要时，海关可以会同有关部门对交通运输工具实施隔离：  
（一）受到检疫传染病污染；  
（二）发现与人类健康有关的病媒生物；  
（三）存在传播检疫传染病风险的其他情形。  
外国交通运输工具的负责人拒绝实施卫生处理的，除特殊情况外，海关应当责令该交通运输工具在其监督下立即离境。

**Article 15** If a means of transport entering or leaving the country falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall undergo sanitation treatment and accept Customs supervision; when necessary, the Customs may, jointly with relevant departments, implement isolation of the means of transport:  
(1) being contaminated by quarantinable infectious diseases;  
(2) finding vectors related to human health;  
(3) other circumstances where there is a risk of spreading quarantinable infectious diseases.  
If the person in charge of a foreign means of transport refuses to implement sanitation treatment, except under special circumstances, the Customs shall order the means of transport to leave the country immediately under its supervision.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　海关依据检疫医师提供的检疫查验结果，对没有传播检疫传染病风险或者已经实施有效卫生处理的交通运输工具，签发进境检疫证或者出境检疫证。

**Article 16** The Customs shall, based on the quarantine inspection results provided by quarantine physicians, issue an entry quarantine certificate or an exit quarantine certificate for means of transport that have no risk of spreading quarantinable infectious diseases or have undergone effective sanitation treatment.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　已经实施检疫查验的交通运输工具在口岸停留期间，发现检疫传染病染疫人、疑似染疫人或者有人非因意外伤害死亡且死因不明的，交通运输工具负责人应当立即向海关报告，海关应当依照本法规定采取相应的措施。

**Article 17** If, during the stay at a port of a means of transport that has undergone quarantine inspection, a person with quarantinable infectious diseases or a suspected person is found, or someone dies not due to accidental injury and the cause of death is unknown, the person in charge of the means of transport shall immediately report to the Customs, and the Customs shall take corresponding measures in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　海关对过境的交通运输工具不实施检疫查验，但有证据表明该交通运输工具存在传播检疫传染病风险的除外。  
过境的交通运输工具在中国境内不得装卸货物、物品或者上下人员；添加燃料、饮用水、食品和供应品的，应当停靠在指定地点，在海关监督下进行。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国国境卫生检疫法实施细则.md#art-104" title="被《中华人民共和国国境卫生检疫法实施细则》第104条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 18** The Customs shall not conduct quarantine inspection on means of transport in transit, unless there is evidence indicating that the means of transport poses a risk of spreading quarantinable infectious diseases.  
Means of transport in transit shall not load or unload goods or articles, or embark or disembark persons within the territory of China; if they add fuel, drinking water, food, and supplies, they shall stop at designated places and do so under Customs supervision.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　进境出境货物、物品的收发货人、收寄件人、携运人（携带人）、承运人或者其代理人应当按照规定向海关如实申报与检疫查验有关的事项。

**Article 19** The consignor or consignee, sender or recipient, carrier (person carrying), transporter or its agent of goods or articles entering or leaving the country shall, in accordance with the provisions, truthfully declare to the Customs matters related to quarantine inspection.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对有[本法第十五条](#art-15)第一款规定情形的货物、物品，应当实施卫生处理，并接受海关监督；卫生处理完成前，相关货物、物品应当单独存放，未经海关准许不得移运或者提离。  
对有[本法第十五条](#art-15)第一款规定情形但无法实施有效卫生处理的货物、物品，海关可以决定不准其进境或者出境，或者予以退运、销毁；对境内公共卫生安全可能造成重大危害的，海关可以暂停相关货物的进口。

**Article 20** Goods and articles that fall under the circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of Article 15 of this Law shall undergo sanitation treatment and accept Customs supervision; before the sanitation treatment is completed, the relevant goods and articles shall be stored separately, and shall not be moved or removed without permission by the Customs.  
For goods and articles that fall under the circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of Article 15 of this Law but cannot undergo effective sanitation treatment, the Customs may decide to prohibit their entry or exit, or order their return or destruction; if they may cause significant harm to public health security within the territory, the Customs may suspend the import of relevant goods.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　托运尸体、骸骨进境出境的，托运人或者其代理人应当按照规定向海关如实申报，经检疫查验合格后，方可进境出境。  
因患检疫传染病死亡的，尸体应当就近火化。

**Article 21** Where corpses or human remains are consigned for entry or exit, the consignor or its agent shall truthfully declare to the Customs in accordance with the provisions, and may only enter or leave the country after passing quarantine inspection.  
Where a person dies of a quarantinable infectious disease, the corpse shall be cremated nearby.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　血液等人体组织、病原微生物、生物制品等关系公共卫生安全的货物、物品进境出境，除纳入药品、兽药、医疗器械管理的外，应当由海关事先实施卫生检疫审批，并经检疫查验合格后方可进境出境。

**Article 22** Where goods and articles related to public health security such as blood and other human tissues, pathogenic microorganisms, and biological products enter or leave the country, unless they are subject to the administration of drugs, veterinary drugs, and medical devices, the Customs shall carry out health quarantine approval in advance, and they may only enter or leave the country after passing quarantine inspection.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　海关根据检疫查验需要，可以请求有关部门和单位协助查询进境出境的人员、交通运输工具、货物、物品等的相关信息，有关部门和单位应当予以协助。海关对查询所获得的信息，不得用于卫生检疫以外的用途。

**Article 23** The Customs may, based on the needs of quarantine inspection, request relevant departments and units to assist in inquiring about relevant information on persons, means of transport, goods, and articles entering or leaving the country. The relevant departments and units shall provide assistance. The Customs shall not use the information obtained through inquiry for purposes other than health and quarantine.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　海关总署应当根据境内外传染病监测和风险评估情况，不断优化检疫查验流程。

**Article 24** The General Administration of Customs shall, based on domestic and foreign infectious disease surveillance and risk assessment, continuously optimize the quarantine inspection procedures.

### 第三章　传染病监测

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　海关总署会同国务院疾病预防控制部门，建立跨境传播传染病监测制度，制定口岸传染病监测规划和方案。  
海关总署在国际公共卫生合作框架下，完善传染病监测网络布局，加强对境外传染病疫情的监测。

**Article 25** The General Administration of Customs jointly with the disease prevention and control department of the State Council shall establish a surveillance system for cross-border transmission of infectious diseases, and formulate port infectious disease surveillance plans and programs.  
Under the framework of international public health cooperation, the General Administration of Customs shall improve the layout of infectious disease surveillance networks and strengthen surveillance of infectious disease epidemics abroad.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　各地海关应当按照口岸传染病监测规划和方案，结合对进境出境的人员、交通运输工具、货物、物品等实施检疫查验，系统持续地收集、核对和分析相关数据，对可能跨境传播的传染病的发生、流行及影响因素、发展趋势等进行评估。  
海关开展传染病监测，应当充分利用现代信息技术，拓宽监测渠道，提升监测效能。

**Article 26** Local Customs shall, in accordance with the port infectious disease surveillance plans and programs, and in combination with the implementation of quarantine inspection on persons, means of transport, goods, and articles entering or leaving the country, systematically and continuously collect, verify, and analyze relevant data, and assess the occurrence, prevalence, influencing factors, and development trends of infectious diseases that may spread across borders.  
When carrying out infectious disease surveillance, the Customs shall make full use of modern information technology, broaden surveillance channels, and enhance surveillance effectiveness.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　各地海关发现传染病，应当采取相应的控制措施，并及时向海关总署报告，同时向口岸所在地县级以上地方人民政府疾病预防控制部门以及移民管理机构通报。县级以上地方人民政府疾病预防控制部门发现传染病，应当及时向当地海关、移民管理机构通报。  
任何单位和个人发现口岸或者进境出境的人员、交通运输工具、货物、物品等存在传播传染病风险的，应当及时向就近的海关或者口岸所在地疾病预防控制机构报告。

**Article 27** Where local Customs discover infectious diseases, they shall take corresponding control measures, promptly report to the General Administration of Customs, and simultaneously notify the disease prevention and control department of the local people's government at or above the county level where the port is located and the immigration administration authority. Where the disease prevention and control department of the local people's government at or above the county level discovers infectious diseases, it shall promptly notify the local Customs and immigration administration authority.  
Any unit or individual that discovers a risk of infectious disease transmission at the port or from persons, means of transport, goods, and articles entering or leaving the country shall promptly report to the nearest Customs or the disease prevention and control institution where the port is located.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　海关总署、国务院卫生健康主管部门、国务院疾病预防控制部门应当依据职责及时互相通报传染病相关信息。  
国务院有关部门根据我国缔结或者参加的国境卫生检疫国际条约，依据职责与有关国家或者地区、国际组织互相通报传染病相关信息。

**Article 28** The General Administration of Customs, the health department of the State Council, and the disease prevention and control department of the State Council shall, in accordance with their duties, promptly exchange information on infectious diseases with each other.  
Relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with the international treaties on frontier health and quarantine concluded or acceded to by China, and in accordance with their duties, exchange information on infectious diseases with relevant countries or regions and international organizations.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　海关总署应当根据境外传染病监测情况，对境外传染病疫情风险进行评估，并及时发布相关风险提示信息。

**Article 29** The General Administration of Customs shall, based on the surveillance of infectious diseases abroad, assess the risk of infectious disease epidemics abroad, and promptly issue relevant risk alert information.

### 第四章　卫生监督

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　海关依照本法以及有关法律、行政法规和国家规定的卫生标准，对口岸和停留在口岸的进境出境交通运输工具的卫生状况实施卫生监督，履行下列职责：  
（一）开展病媒生物监测，监督和指导有关单位和人员对病媒生物的防除；  
（二）监督食品生产经营、饮用水供应、公共场所的卫生状况以及从业人员健康状况；  
（三）监督固体、液体废弃物和船舶压舱水的处理；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他卫生监督职责。

**Article 30** The Customs shall, in accordance with this Law and relevant laws, administrative regulations, and national health standards, carry out health supervision on the health conditions of ports and means of transport entering or leaving the country that stay at ports, and perform the following duties:  
(1) carrying out vector surveillance, supervising and guiding relevant units and personnel in the prevention and elimination of vectors;  
(2) supervising the health conditions of food production and operation, drinking water supply, public places, and the health status of employees;  
(3) supervising the treatment of solid and liquid waste and ship ballast water;  
(4) other health supervision duties prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　口岸运营单位应当建立健全并严格落实相关卫生制度，保证口岸卫生状况符合法律、行政法规和国家规定的卫生标准的要求。  
进境出境交通运输工具负责人应当采取有效措施，保持交通运输工具清洁卫生，保持无污染状态。

**Article 31** Port operation units shall establish, improve, and strictly implement relevant health systems to ensure that the health conditions of ports meet the requirements of laws, administrative regulations, and national health standards.  
The person in charge of a means of transport entering or leaving the country shall take effective measures to keep the means of transport clean and sanitary, and maintain a pollution-free state.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　在口岸内从事食品生产经营、饮用水供应服务、公共场所经营的，由海关依法实施卫生许可；食品生产经营者取得卫生许可的，无需另行取得食品生产经营许可。

**Article 32** Where food production and operation, drinking water supply services, or public place operation are carried out within ports, the Customs shall implement health licensing according to law; if a food producer or operator obtains a health license, it is not required to separately obtain a food production and operation license.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　海关实施卫生监督，发现口岸或者进境出境交通运输工具的卫生状况不符合法律、行政法规和国家规定的卫生标准要求的，有权要求有关单位和个人进行整改，必要时要求其实施卫生处理。

**Article 33** When implementing health supervision, if the Customs finds that the health conditions of a port or a means of transport entering or leaving the country do not meet the requirements of laws, administrative regulations, and national health standards, it shall have the authority to require the relevant units and individuals to make corrections, and when necessary, require them to carry out sanitation treatment.

### 第五章　应急处置

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　发生重大传染病疫情，需要在口岸采取应急处置措施的，适用本章规定。

**Article 34** Where a major infectious disease epidemic occurs and emergency response measures need to be taken at ports, the provisions of this Chapter shall apply.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　发生重大传染病疫情，需要在口岸采取应急处置措施的，海关总署、国务院卫生健康主管部门、国务院疾病预防控制部门应当提请国务院批准启动应急响应。海关总署、国务院卫生健康主管部门、国务院疾病预防控制部门和其他有关部门应当依据各自职责，密切配合开展相关的应急处置工作。  
口岸所在地县级以上地方人民政府应当为应急处置提供场所、设施、设备、物资以及人力和技术等支持。

**Article 35** Where a major infectious disease epidemic occurs and emergency response measures need to be taken at ports, the General Administration of Customs, the health department of the State Council, and the disease prevention and control department of the State Council shall request the State Council to approve the initiation of an emergency response. The General Administration of Customs, the health department of the State Council, the disease prevention and control department of the State Council, and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective duties, closely cooperate to carry out relevant emergency response work.  
The local people's government at or above the county level where the port is located shall provide venues, facilities, equipment, materials, as well as human and technical support for the emergency response.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　根据重大传染病疫情应急处置需要，经国务院决定，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）对来自特定国家或者地区的人员实施采样检验；  
（二）禁止特定货物、物品进境出境；  
（三）指定进境出境口岸；  
（四）暂时关闭有关口岸或者暂停有关口岸部分功能；  
（五）暂时封锁有关国境；  
（六）其他必要的应急处置措施。  
采取前款规定的应急处置措施，应当事先公布。

**Article 36** According to the needs of emergency response to major infectious disease epidemics, with the decision of the State Council, the following measures may be taken:  
(1) implementing sampling and testing on persons from specific countries or regions;  
(2) prohibiting the entry or exit of specific goods or articles;  
(3) designating ports for entry or exit;  
(4) temporarily closing relevant ports or suspending some functions of relevant ports;  
(5) temporarily sealing off relevant borders;  
(6) other necessary emergency response measures.  
The adoption of the emergency response measures prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be announced in advance.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　采取本章规定的应急处置措施，应当根据重大传染病疫情防控的实际情况，及时调整或者解除，并予以公布。

**Article 37** The emergency response measures adopted under this Chapter shall be adjusted or lifted in a timely manner based on the actual situation of the prevention and control of major infectious disease epidemics, and shall be announced.

### 第六章　保障措施

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　海关总署会同国务院有关部门制定并组织实施口岸公共卫生能力建设规划。  
国务院有关部门、口岸所在地县级以上地方人民政府、口岸运营单位以及其他有关单位应当积极支持口岸公共卫生能力建设。

**Article 38** The General Administration of Customs jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council shall formulate and organize the implementation of plans for port public health capacity building.  
Relevant departments of the State Council, local people's governments at or above the county level where ports are located, port operation units, and other relevant units shall actively support port public health capacity building.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家将国境卫生检疫工作纳入传染病防治体系。  
国境卫生检疫工作所需经费纳入预算，口岸重大传染病疫情应急处置所需物资纳入国家公共卫生应急物资保障体系。

**Article 39** The State shall incorporate frontier health and quarantine work into the infectious disease prevention and control system.  
The funds needed for frontier health and quarantine work shall be included in the budget, and the materials needed for emergency response to major infectious disease epidemics at ports shall be included in the national public health emergency material support system.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国境卫生检疫基础设施建设应当统筹兼顾国境卫生检疫日常工作和重大传染病疫情应急处置的需要。  
国境卫生检疫基础设施建设应当纳入口岸建设规划。新建、改建、扩建口岸应当统筹建设国境卫生检疫基础设施，有关建设方案应当经海关审核同意。  
国境卫生检疫基础设施应当符合规定的建设标准，不符合建设标准的，不得投入使用。国境卫生检疫基础设施建设标准和管理办法由海关总署会同国务院有关部门制定。海关对国境卫生检疫基础设施建设标准的执行实施监督。

**Article 40** The construction of frontier health and quarantine infrastructure shall take into account both the routine frontier health and quarantine work and the needs for emergency response to major infectious disease epidemics.  
The construction of frontier health and quarantine infrastructure shall be incorporated into port construction plans. When constructing, reconstructing, or expanding ports, the construction of frontier health and quarantine infrastructure shall be planned as a whole, and the relevant construction plans shall be reviewed and approved by the Customs.  
Frontier health and quarantine infrastructure shall meet the prescribed construction standards; those that do not meet the construction standards shall not be put into use. The construction standards and management measures for frontier health and quarantine infrastructure shall be formulated by the General Administration of Customs jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council. The Customs shall supervise the implementation of the construction standards for frontier health and quarantine infrastructure.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家鼓励、支持国境卫生检疫领域的科学研究、技术创新和信息化建设，推动新技术、新设备、新产品和信息化成果的应用，提高国境卫生检疫工作的技术和信息化水平。

**Article 41** The State encourages and supports scientific research, technological innovation, and informatization construction in the field of frontier health and quarantine, promotes the application of new technologies, new equipment, new products, and informatization achievements, and improves the technical and informatization level of frontier health and quarantine work.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　海关应当加强国境卫生检疫技术机构建设，为国境卫生检疫工作提供技术和服务支撑。

**Article 42** The Customs shall strengthen the construction of frontier health and quarantine technical institutions to provide technical and service support for frontier health and quarantine work.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国境卫生检疫工作人员应当具备与履行职责相适应的专业知识和业务技能。  
海关应当加强国境卫生检疫队伍建设，组织开展继续教育和职业培训，持续提升国境卫生检疫工作人员的专业知识和业务技能水平。

**Article 43** Frontier health and quarantine staff shall possess professional knowledge and business skills commensurate with the performance of their duties.  
The Customs shall strengthen the construction of the frontier health and quarantine team, organize and carry out continuing education and vocational training, and continuously improve the professional knowledge and business skill level of frontier health and quarantine staff.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反本法规定，进境出境人员不如实申报健康状况、相关信息或者拒绝接受检疫查验的，由海关责令改正，可以给予警告或者处一万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 44** Where a person entering or leaving the country, in violation of the provisions of this Law, fails to truthfully declare health status or relevant information, or refuses to accept quarantine inspection, the Customs shall order correction, and may give a warning or impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，对交通运输工具负责人，由海关责令改正，给予警告，可以并处五万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处五万元以上三十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未按照规定向海关申报与检疫查验有关的事项或者不如实申报有关事项；  
（二）拒绝接受对交通运输工具的检疫查验或者拒绝实施卫生处理；  
（三）未取得进境检疫证或者出境检疫证，交通运输工具擅自进境或者出境；  
（四）未经海关准许，交通运输工具驶离指定的检疫查验地点，装卸货物、物品或者上下人员；  
（五）已经实施检疫查验的交通运输工具在口岸停留期间，发现检疫传染病染疫人、疑似染疫人或者有人非因意外伤害死亡且死因不明的，未立即向海关报告；  
（六）过境的交通运输工具在中国境内装卸货物、物品或者上下人员，或者添加燃料、饮用水、食品和供应品不接受海关监督。  
有下列情形之一的，依照前款规定给予处罚：  
（一）进境出境货物、物品的收发货人、收寄件人、携运人（携带人）、承运人或者其代理人未按照规定向海关申报与检疫查验有关的事项或者不如实申报有关事项，或者拒绝接受检疫查验、拒绝实施卫生处理，或者未经海关准许移运或者提离货物、物品；  
（二）托运尸体、骸骨进境出境的托运人或者其代理人未按照规定向海关申报或者不如实申报，或者未经检疫查验合格擅自进境出境。

**Article 45** Where any of the following circumstances occurs in violation of the provisions of this Law, the Customs shall order the person in charge of the means of transport to make corrections, give a warning, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed:  
(1) failing to declare to the Customs matters related to quarantine inspection in accordance with the provisions or failing to truthfully declare relevant matters;  
(2) refusing to accept quarantine inspection of the means of transport or refusing to undergo sanitation treatment;  
(3) the means of transport entering or leaving the country without obtaining an entry quarantine certificate or an exit quarantine certificate;  
(4) without permission by the Customs, the means of transport leaving the designated quarantine inspection place, loading or unloading goods or articles, or embarking or disembarking persons;  
(5) during the stay at the port of a means of transport that has undergone quarantine inspection, finding a person with quarantinable infectious diseases or a suspected person, or someone dies not due to accidental injury and the cause of death is unknown, without immediately reporting to the Customs;  
(6) means of transport in transit loading or unloading goods or articles, or embarking or disembarking persons within the territory of China, or adding fuel, drinking water, food, and supplies without accepting Customs supervision.  
Where any of the following circumstances occurs, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph:  
(1) the consignor or consignee, sender or recipient, carrier (person carrying), transporter or its agent of goods or articles entering or leaving the country failing to declare to the Customs matters related to quarantine inspection in accordance with the provisions or failing to truthfully declare relevant matters, or refusing to accept quarantine inspection, refusing to undergo sanitation treatment, or moving or removing goods or articles without permission by the Customs;  
(2) the consignor or its agent of corpses or human remains consigned for entry or exit failing to declare to the Customs in accordance with the provisions or failing to truthfully declare, or entering or leaving the country without authorization without passing quarantine inspection.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本法规定，血液等人体组织、病原微生物、生物制品等关系公共卫生安全的货物、物品进境出境未经检疫审批或者未经检疫查验合格擅自进境出境的，由海关责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处一万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处五十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 46** Where goods and articles related to public health security such as blood and other human tissues, pathogenic microorganisms, and biological products enter or leave the country without health quarantine approval or without authorization without passing quarantine inspection, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the Customs shall order correction, give a warning, confiscate illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反本法规定，未经许可在口岸从事食品生产经营、饮用水供应服务、公共场所经营的，由海关依照《中华人民共和国食品安全法》等有关法律、行政法规的规定给予处罚。  
违反本法有关卫生监督的其他规定，或者拒绝接受卫生监督的，由海关责令改正，给予警告，可以并处十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处十万元以上三十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 47** Where a person, in violation of the provisions of this Law, engages in food production and operation, drinking water supply services, or public place operation at a port without a license, the Customs shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
Where a person violates other provisions of this Law concerning health supervision or refuses to accept health supervision, the Customs shall order correction, give a warning, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　使用买卖、出借或者伪造、变造的国境卫生检疫单证的，由海关责令改正，处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 48** Where a person uses bought, sold, lent, forged, or altered frontier health and quarantine documents, the Customs shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　海关等有关部门、地方人民政府及其工作人员在国境卫生检疫工作中玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，由上级机关或者所在单位责令改正，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 49** Where the Customs and other relevant departments, local people's governments, or their staff members neglect their duties, abuse their powers, or engage in malpractice for personal gain in frontier health and quarantine work, the superior authority or the unit to which they belong shall order correction, and shall impose sanctions on the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 50** Where a violation of the provisions of this Law constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　本法中下列用语的含义：  
（一）检疫查验，是指对进境出境的人员、交通运输工具、货物、物品、尸体、骸骨等采取检查措施、实施医学措施。  
（二）医学巡查，是指检疫医师在口岸进境出境旅客通道，观察进境出境人员是否有传染病临床症状，并对有临床症状的人员进行询问的活动。  
（三）医学检查，是指检疫医师对进境出境人员检查医学证明文件，实施必要的体格检查、采样检验的活动。  
（四）卫生处理，是指消毒、杀虫、灭鼠、除污等措施。

**Article 51** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:  
(1) Quarantine inspection means the taking of inspection measures and implementation of medical measures on persons, means of transport, goods, articles, corpses, and skeletons entering or leaving the country.  
(2) Medical patrol means the activity of quarantine doctors observing at the entry-exit passenger passageways of ports whether persons entering or leaving the country have clinical symptoms of infectious diseases, and inquiring about such persons with clinical symptoms.  
(3) Medical examination means the activity of quarantine doctors examining medical certificates of persons entering or leaving the country, and carrying out necessary physical examinations and sample testing.  
(4) Sanitary treatment means measures such as disinfection, disinsection, deratization, and decontamination.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的有关卫生检疫的国际条约同本法有不同规定的，适用该国际条约的规定，但中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。

**Article 52** Where an international treaty on health and quarantine concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China contains provisions different from those of this Law, the provisions of such international treaty shall apply, except for the clauses on which the People's Republic of China has declared reservations.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　从口岸以外经国务院或者国务院授权的部门批准的地点进境出境的人员、交通运输工具、货物、物品的卫生检疫，我国与有关国家或者地区有双边协议的，按照协议办理；没有协议的，按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 53** For the health and quarantine of persons, means of transport, goods, and articles entering or leaving the country from places other than ports that have been approved by the State Council or a department authorized by the State Council, if China has a bilateral agreement with the relevant country or region, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the agreement; if there is no such agreement, it shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　经国务院批准，海关总署可以根据境内外传染病监测和风险评估情况，对有关口岸的卫生检疫措施作出便利化安排。

**Article 54** With the approval of the State Council, the General Administration of Customs may, based on the monitoring of infectious diseases at home and abroad and risk assessment, make facilitation arrangements for health and quarantine measures at relevant ports.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国境卫生检疫及相关活动，本法未作规定的，适用《中华人民共和国传染病防治法》等有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 55** For frontier health and quarantine and related activities not provided for in this Law, the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases and other relevant laws and administrative regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队的人员、交通运输工具和装备物资进境出境的卫生检疫工作，依照本法和国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定办理。

**Article 56** The health and quarantine work for persons, means of transport, and equipment and materials of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force entering or leaving the country shall be handled in accordance with this Law and the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　本法自2025年1月1日起施行。

**Article 57** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2025.
