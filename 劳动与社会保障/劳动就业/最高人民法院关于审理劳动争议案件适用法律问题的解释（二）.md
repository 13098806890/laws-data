# 最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（二）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**发文字号**：法释〔2025〕12号  
**公布日期**：2025-07-31  
**生效日期**：2025-09-01

> （2025年2月17日最高人民法院审判委员会第1942次会议通过，自2025年9月1日起施行） 为正确审理劳动争议案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国劳动法》《中华人民共和国劳动合同法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国劳动争议调解仲裁法》等相关法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条　具备合法经营资格的承包人将承包业务转包或者分包给不具备合法经营资格的组织或者个人，该组织或者个人招用的劳动者请求确认承包人为承担用工主体责任单位，承担支付劳动报酬、认定工伤后的工伤保险待遇等责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。 第二条　不具备合法经营资格的组织或者个人挂靠具备合法经营资格的单位对外经营，该组织或者个人招用的劳动者请求确认被挂靠单位为承担用工主体责任单位，承担支付劳动报酬、认定工伤后的工伤保险待遇等责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。 第三条　劳动者被多个存在关联关系的单位交替或者同时用工，其请求确认劳动关系的，人民法院按照下列情形分别处理： （一）已订立书面劳动合同，劳动者请求按照劳动合同确认劳动关系的，人民法院依法予以支持； （二）未订立书面劳动合同的，根据用工管理行为，综合考虑工作时间、工作内容、劳动报酬支付、社会保险费缴纳等因素确认劳动关系。 劳动者请求符合前款第二项规定情形的关联单位共同承担支付劳动报酬、福利待遇等责任的，人民法院依法予以支持，但关联单位之间依法对劳动者的劳动报酬、福利待遇等作出约定且经劳动者同意的除外。 第四条　外国人与中华人民共和国境内的用人单位建立用工关系，有下列情形之一，外国人请求确认与用人单位存在劳动关系的，人民法院依法予以支持： （一）已取得永久居留资格的； （二）已取得工作许可且在中国境内合法停留居留的； （三）按照国家有关规定办理相关手续的。 第五条　依法设立的外国企业常驻代表机构可以作为劳动争议案件的当事人。当事人申请追加外国企业参加诉讼的，人民法院依法予以支持。 第六条　用人单位未依法与劳动者订立书面劳动合同，应当支付劳动者的二倍工资按月计算；不满一个月的，按该月实际工作日计算。 第七条　劳动者以用人单位未订立书面劳动合同为由，请求用人单位支付二倍工资的，人民法院依法予以支持，但用人单位举证证明存在下列情形之一的除外：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　具备合法经营资格的承包人将承包业务转包或者分包给不具备合法经营资格的组织或者个人，该组织或者个人招用的劳动者请求确认承包人为承担用工主体责任单位，承担支付劳动报酬、认定工伤后的工伤保险待遇等责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 1** Where a contractor with legal business qualifications subcontracts or assigns the contracted business to an organization or individual without legal business qualifications, and a worker recruited by such organization or individual requests confirmation that the contractor shall be the entity bearing the primary responsibility for employing the worker and shall bear liability for payment of labor remuneration, work-related injury insurance benefits after work-related injury determination, and other liabilities, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　不具备合法经营资格的组织或者个人挂靠具备合法经营资格的单位对外经营，该组织或者个人招用的劳动者请求确认被挂靠单位为承担用工主体责任单位，承担支付劳动报酬、认定工伤后的工伤保险待遇等责任的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 2** Where an organization or individual without legal business qualifications operates under the name of an entity with legal business qualifications, and a worker recruited by such organization or individual requests confirmation that the entity being used as the front shall be the entity bearing the primary responsibility for employing the worker and shall bear liability for payment of labor remuneration, work-related injury insurance benefits after work-related injury determination, and other liabilities, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　劳动者被多个存在关联关系的单位交替或者同时用工，其请求确认劳动关系的，人民法院按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）已订立书面劳动合同，劳动者请求按照劳动合同确认劳动关系的，人民法院依法予以支持；  
（二）未订立书面劳动合同的，根据用工管理行为，综合考虑工作时间、工作内容、劳动报酬支付、社会保险费缴纳等因素确认劳动关系。  
劳动者请求符合前款第二项规定情形的关联单位共同承担支付劳动报酬、福利待遇等责任的，人民法院依法予以支持，但关联单位之间依法对劳动者的劳动报酬、福利待遇等作出约定且经劳动者同意的除外。

**Article 3** Where a worker is employed alternately or simultaneously by multiple related entities, and the worker requests confirmation of an employment relationship, the People's Court shall handle the matter according to the following circumstances:  
(1) Where a written labor contract has been concluded, and the worker requests confirmation of the employment relationship in accordance with the labor contract, the People's Court shall support such request according to law;  
(2) Where no written labor contract has been concluded, the employment relationship shall be confirmed based on the employment management conduct, taking into comprehensive consideration factors such as working hours, job content, payment of labor remuneration, and payment of social insurance premiums.  
Where a worker requests that related entities falling under the circumstance specified in subparagraph (2) of the preceding paragraph jointly bear liability for payment of labor remuneration, welfare benefits, and other liabilities, the People's Court shall support such request according to law, unless the related entities have made an agreement according to law regarding the worker's labor remuneration, welfare benefits, etc., and the worker has consented thereto.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　外国人与中华人民共和国境内的用人单位建立用工关系，有下列情形之一，外国人请求确认与用人单位存在劳动关系的，人民法院依法予以支持：  
（一）已取得永久居留资格的；  
（二）已取得工作许可且在中国境内合法停留居留的；  
（三）按照国家有关规定办理相关手续的。

**Article 4** Where a foreign national establishes an employment relationship with an employer within the territory of the People's Republic of China, and under any of the following circumstances, the foreign national requests confirmation of an employment relationship with the employer, the People's Court shall support such request according to law:  
(1) The foreign national has obtained permanent residency status;  
(2) The foreign national has obtained a work permit and lawfully stays or resides within China;  
(3) The foreign national has handled relevant procedures in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　依法设立的外国企业常驻代表机构可以作为劳动争议案件的当事人。当事人申请追加外国企业参加诉讼的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 5** A resident representative office of a foreign enterprise established according to law may serve as a party to a labor dispute case. Where a party applies to add the foreign enterprise to participate in the litigation, the People's Court shall support such application according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　用人单位未依法与劳动者订立书面劳动合同，应当支付劳动者的二倍工资按月计算；不满一个月的，按该月实际工作日计算。

**Article 6** Where an employer fails to conclude a written labor contract with a worker according to law, the double wages payable to the worker shall be calculated on a monthly basis; if the period is less than one month, it shall be calculated based on the actual working days of that month.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　劳动者以用人单位未订立书面劳动合同为由，请求用人单位支付二倍工资的，人民法院依法予以支持，但用人单位举证证明存在下列情形之一的除外：  
（一）因不可抗力导致未订立的；  
（二）因劳动者本人故意或者重大过失未订立的；  
（三）法律、行政法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 7** Where a worker requests the employer to pay double wages on the ground that the employer has failed to conclude a written labor contract, the People's Court shall support such request according to law, unless the employer adduces evidence proving that any of the following circumstances exists:  
(1) The failure to conclude is caused by force majeure;  
(2) The failure to conclude is caused by the worker's own intentional act or gross negligence;  
(3) Other circumstances as provided by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　劳动合同期满，有下列情形之一的，人民法院认定劳动合同期限依法自动续延，不属于用人单位未订立书面劳动合同的情形：  
（一）[劳动合同法第四十二条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-42)规定的用人单位不得解除劳动合同的；  
（二）[劳动合同法实施条例第十七条](../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法实施条例.md#art-17)规定的服务期尚未到期的；  
（三）[工会法第十九条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国工会法.md#art-19)规定的任期未届满的。

**Article 8** Where a labor contract expires, and any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall determine that the term of the labor contract is automatically extended according to law, and this shall not be deemed as a situation where the employer has failed to conclude a written labor contract:  
(1) The circumstances under Article 42 of the Labor Contract Law where the employer shall not terminate the labor contract;  
(2) The service period under Article 17 of the Regulation on the Implementation of the Labor Contract Law has not yet expired;  
(3) The term of office under Article 19 of the Trade Union Law has not yet expired.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　有证据证明存在[劳动合同法第十四条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-14)第三款规定的“视为用人单位与劳动者已订立无固定期限劳动合同”情形，劳动者请求与用人单位订立书面劳动合同的，人民法院依法予以支持；劳动者以用人单位未及时补订书面劳动合同为由，请求用人单位支付视为已与劳动者订立无固定期限劳动合同期间二倍工资的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 9** Where there is evidence proving the existence of the circumstance specified in Article 14, paragraph 3 of the Labor Contract Law that 'the employer and the worker are deemed to have concluded an open-term labor contract', and the worker requests the employer to conclude a written labor contract, the People's Court shall support such request according to law; where the worker requests the employer to pay double wages for the period during which the employer is deemed to have concluded an open-term labor contract with the worker on the ground that the employer has failed to timely supplement the written labor contract, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　有下列情形之一的，人民法院应认定为符合[劳动合同法第十四条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-14)第二款第三项“连续订立二次固定期限劳动合同”的规定：  
（一）用人单位与劳动者协商延长劳动合同期限累计达到一年以上，延长期限届满的；  
（二）用人单位与劳动者约定劳动合同期满后自动续延，续延期限届满的；  
（三）劳动者非因本人原因仍在原工作场所、工作岗位工作，用人单位变换劳动合同订立主体，但继续对劳动者进行劳动管理，合同期限届满的；  
（四）以其他违反诚信原则的规避行为再次订立劳动合同，期限届满的。

**Article 10** Where any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall determine that it complies with the provision of Article 14, paragraph 2, subparagraph 3 of the Labor Contract Law regarding 'consecutive conclusion of two fixed-term labor contracts':  
(1) The employer and the worker have negotiated to extend the term of the labor contract cumulatively to one year or more, and the extended term has expired;  
(2) The employer and the worker have agreed that the labor contract shall be automatically renewed upon expiration, and the renewed term has expired;  
(3) The worker continues to work at the original workplace and position for reasons not attributable to the worker himself/herself, the employer changes the subject concluding the labor contract but continues to exercise labor management over the worker, and the contract term expires;  
(4) Other circumventing conduct violating the principle of good faith leads to the conclusion of another labor contract, and the term expires.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　劳动合同期满后，劳动者仍在用人单位工作，用人单位未表示异议超过一个月，劳动者请求用人单位以原条件续订劳动合同的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
符合订立无固定期限劳动合同情形，劳动者请求用人单位以原条件订立无固定期限劳动合同的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
用人单位解除劳动合同，劳动者请求用人单位依法承担解除劳动合同法律后果的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 11** After the expiration of a labor contract, if the worker continues to work for the employer and the employer does not raise any objection for more than one month, and the worker requests the employer to renew the labor contract on the original terms, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.  
Where the circumstances for concluding an open-term labor contract are met, and the worker requests the employer to conclude an open-term labor contract on the original terms, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.  
Where the employer terminates the labor contract, and the worker requests the employer to bear the legal consequences of the termination according to law, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　除向劳动者支付正常劳动报酬外，用人单位与劳动者约定服务期限并提供特殊待遇，劳动者违反约定提前解除劳动合同且不符合[劳动合同法第三十八条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-38)规定的单方解除劳动合同情形时，用人单位请求劳动者承担赔偿损失责任的，人民法院可以综合考虑实际损失、当事人的过错程度、已经履行的年限等因素确定劳动者应当承担的赔偿责任。

**Article 12** In addition to paying the worker normal labor remuneration, where the employer and the worker agree on a service period and provide special benefits, and the worker violates the agreement by prematurely terminating the labor contract and does not meet the circumstances for unilateral termination of the labor contract as provided in Article 38 of the Labor Contract Law, when the employer requests the worker to bear liability for compensation of losses, the People's Court may, taking into comprehensive consideration factors such as the actual losses, the degree of fault of the parties, and the number of years already performed, determine the compensation liability that the worker shall bear.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　劳动者未知悉、接触用人单位的商业秘密和与知识产权相关的保密事项，劳动者请求确认竞业限制条款不生效的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
竞业限制条款约定的竞业限制范围、地域、期限等内容与劳动者知悉、接触的商业秘密和与知识产权相关的保密事项不相适应，劳动者请求确认竞业限制条款超过合理比例部分无效的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 13** Where a worker has not learned of or had access to the employer's trade secrets and confidentiality matters related to intellectual property rights, and the worker requests confirmation that the non-competition clause is not effective, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.  
Where the scope, territory, term, and other contents of the non-competition restriction stipulated in the non-competition clause are not commensurate with the trade secrets and confidentiality matters related to intellectual property rights that the worker has learned of or had access to, and the worker requests confirmation that the part of the non-competition clause exceeding a reasonable proportion is invalid, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　用人单位与高级管理人员、高级技术人员和其他负有保密义务的人员约定在职期间竞业限制条款，劳动者以不得约定在职期间竞业限制、未支付经济补偿为由请求确认竞业限制条款无效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 14** Where an employer agrees with senior officers, senior technical personnel, and other persons with confidentiality obligations on a non-competition clause during the term of employment, and the worker requests confirmation that the non-competition clause is invalid on the grounds that non-competition during the term of employment cannot be agreed upon or that economic compensation has not been paid, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　劳动者违反有效的竞业限制约定，用人单位请求劳动者按照约定返还已经支付的经济补偿并支付违约金的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 15** Where a worker violates a valid non-competition agreement, and the employer requests the worker to return the economic compensation already paid and to pay liquidated damages in accordance with the agreement, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　用人单位违法解除或者终止劳动合同后，有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以认定为[劳动合同法第四十八条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-48)规定的“劳动合同已经不能继续履行”：  
（一）劳动合同在仲裁或者诉讼过程中期满且不存在应当依法续订、续延劳动合同情形的；  
（二）劳动者开始依法享受基本养老保险待遇的；  
（三）用人单位被宣告破产的；  
（四）用人单位解散的，但因合并或者分立需要解散的除外；  
（五）劳动者已经与其他用人单位建立劳动关系，对完成用人单位的工作任务造成严重影响，或者经用人单位提出，不与其他用人单位解除劳动合同的；  
（六）存在劳动合同客观不能履行的其他情形的。

**Article 16** After the employer illegally rescinds or terminates a labor contract, if any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court may determine that the 'labor contract can no longer be performed' as provided in Article 48 of the Labor Contract Law:  
(1) The labor contract expires during arbitration or litigation proceedings and there is no circumstance requiring the contract to be renewed or extended according to law;  
(2) The worker has begun to receive basic pension insurance benefits according to law;  
(3) The employer is declared bankrupt;  
(4) The employer is dissolved, except where dissolution is necessary due to merger or division;  
(5) The worker has already established an employment relationship with another employer, which materially affects the performance of the original employer's work tasks, or after being requested by the employer, the worker fails to terminate the labor contract with the other employer;  
(6) Other circumstances where the labor contract objectively cannot be performed.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　用人单位未按照国务院安全生产监督管理部门、卫生行政部门的规定组织从事接触职业病危害作业的劳动者进行离岗前的职业健康检查，劳动者在双方解除劳动合同后请求继续履行劳动合同的，人民法院依法予以支持，但有下列情形之一的除外：  
（一）一审法庭辩论终结前，用人单位已经组织劳动者进行职业健康检查且经检查劳动者未患职业病的；  
（二）一审法庭辩论终结前，用人单位组织劳动者进行职业健康检查，劳动者无正当理由拒绝检查的。

**Article 17** Where an employer fails to organize workers engaged in operations exposed to occupational disease hazards to undergo pre-departure occupational health checks in accordance with the provisions of the State Administration of Work Safety and the health administrative department, and after the parties have rescinded the labor contract, the worker requests the continued performance of the labor contract, the People's Court shall support such request according to law, unless any of the following circumstances exists:  
(1) Before the conclusion of the debate at the first instance trial, the employer has organized the worker to undergo an occupational health check and the check shows that the worker does not have an occupational disease;  
(2) Before the conclusion of the debate at the first instance trial, the employer organizes the worker to undergo an occupational health check, but the worker refuses the check without a justifiable reason.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　用人单位违法解除、终止可以继续履行的劳动合同，劳动者请求用人单位支付违法解除、终止决定作出后至劳动合同继续履行前一日工资的，用人单位应当按照劳动者提供正常劳动时的工资标准向劳动者支付上述期间的工资。  
用人单位、劳动者对于劳动合同解除、终止都有过错的，应当各自承担相应的责任。

**Article 18** Where an employer illegally rescinds or terminates a labor contract that can continue to be performed, and the worker requests the employer to pay wages from the date the illegal rescission or termination decision is made to the day before the labor contract is resumed, the employer shall pay the worker wages for the aforesaid period according to the wage standard applicable when the worker provides normal labor.  
Where both the employer and the worker are at fault for the rescission or termination of the labor contract, each shall bear corresponding liability.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　用人单位与劳动者约定或者劳动者向用人单位承诺无需缴纳社会保险费的，人民法院应当认定该约定或者承诺无效。用人单位未依法缴纳社会保险费，劳动者根据[劳动合同法第三十八条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-38)第一款第三项规定请求解除劳动合同、由用人单位支付经济补偿的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
有前款规定情形，用人单位依法补缴社会保险费后，请求劳动者返还已支付的社会保险费补偿的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 19** Where an employer and a worker agree, or the worker promises to the employer, that social insurance premiums need not be paid, the People's Court shall determine that such agreement or promise is invalid. Where the employer fails to pay social insurance premiums according to law, and the worker requests to rescind the labor contract and for the employer to pay economic compensation in accordance with Article 38, paragraph 1, subparagraph 3 of the Labor Contract Law, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.  
Where the circumstance in the preceding paragraph exists, and after the employer has paid the social insurance premiums retroactively according to law, the employer requests the worker to return the social insurance premium compensation already paid, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　当事人在仲裁期间因自身原因未提出仲裁时效抗辩，在一审或者二审诉讼期间提出仲裁时效抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。当事人基于新的证据能够证明对方当事人请求权的仲裁时效期间届满的，人民法院应予支持。  
当事人未按照前款规定提出仲裁时效抗辩，以仲裁时效期间届满为由申请再审或者提出再审抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 20** Where a party fails to raise a statute of limitations defense in arbitration due to its own reasons, and raises the defense during the first instance or second instance litigation, the People's Court shall not support it. Where a party is able to prove, based on new evidence, that the statute of limitations for the opposing party's claim has expired, the People's Court shall support it.  
Where a party fails to raise the statute of limitations defense in accordance with the preceding paragraph, and applies for retrial or raises a retrial defense on the ground that the statute of limitations has expired, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本解释自2025年9月1日起施行。《最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）》（法释〔2020〕26号）第三十二条第一款同时废止。最高人民法院此前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 21** This Interpretation shall come into force as of September 1, 2025. Paragraph 1 of Article 32 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Issues Concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Labor Dispute Cases (I) (Fa Shi [2020] No. 26) shall be abolished simultaneously. Where any judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
