### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护和改善海洋环境，保护海洋资源，防治污染损害，保障生态安全和公众健康，维护国家海洋权益，建设海洋强国，推进生态文明建设，促进经济社会可持续发展，实现人与自然和谐共生，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of protecting and improving the marine environment, conserving marine resources, preventing and controlling pollution damage, ensuring ecological safety and public health, safeguarding national marine rights and interests, building a maritime power, advancing ecological civilization, promoting sustainable economic and social development, and achieving harmony between human and nature.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法适用于中华人民共和国管辖海域。  
在中华人民共和国管辖海域内从事航行、勘探、开发、生产、旅游、科学研究及其他活动，或者在沿海陆域内从事影响海洋环境活动的任何单位和个人，应当遵守本法。  
在中华人民共和国管辖海域以外，造成中华人民共和国管辖海域环境污染、生态破坏的，适用本法相关规定。&thinsp;<sup>[1]</sup>

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.  
Any unit or individual that engages in navigation, exploration, development, production, tourism, scientific research or other activities within the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, or engages in activities affecting the marine environment in coastal land areas, shall comply with this Law.  
Where any act outside the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China causes environmental pollution or ecological damage to such sea areas, the relevant provisions of this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　海洋环境保护应当坚持保护优先、预防为主、源头防控、陆海统筹、综合治理、公众参与、损害担责的原则。

**Article 3** Marine environmental protection shall adhere to the principles of giving priority to protection, putting prevention first, source prevention and control, land-sea coordination, comprehensive management, public participation, and the assumption of liability for damage.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院生态环境主管部门负责全国海洋环境的监督管理，负责全国防治陆源污染物、海岸工程和海洋工程建设项目（以下称工程建设项目）、海洋倾倒废弃物对海洋环境污染损害的环境保护工作，指导、协调和监督全国海洋生态保护修复工作。  
国务院自然资源主管部门负责海洋保护和开发利用的监督管理，负责全国海洋生态、海域海岸线和海岛的修复工作。  
国务院交通运输主管部门负责所辖港区水域内非军事船舶和港区水域外非渔业、非军事船舶污染海洋环境的监督管理，组织、协调、指挥重大海上溢油应急处置。海事管理机构具体负责上述水域内相关船舶污染海洋环境的监督管理，并负责污染事故的调查处理；对在中华人民共和国管辖海域航行、停泊和作业的外国籍船舶造成的污染事故登轮检查处理。船舶污染事故给渔业造成损害的，应当吸收渔业主管部门参与调查处理。  
国务院渔业主管部门负责渔港水域内非军事船舶和渔港水域外渔业船舶污染海洋环境的监督管理，负责保护渔业水域生态环境工作，并调查处理前款规定的污染事故以外的渔业污染事故。  
国务院发展改革、水行政、住房和城乡建设、林业和草原等部门在各自职责范围内负责有关行业、领域涉及的海洋环境保护工作。  
海警机构在职责范围内对海洋工程建设项目、海洋倾倒废弃物对海洋环境污染损害、自然保护地海岸线向海一侧保护利用等活动进行监督检查，查处违法行为，按照规定权限参与海洋环境污染事故的应急处置和调查处理。  
军队生态环境保护部门负责军事船舶污染海洋环境的监督管理及污染事故的调查处理。

**Article 4** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the national marine environment, for the national environmental protection work concerning the prevention and control of pollution damage to the marine environment caused by land-based pollutants, coastal engineering and marine engineering construction projects (hereinafter referred to as engineering construction projects), and ocean dumping of waste, and shall guide, coordinate and supervise the national marine ecological protection and restoration work.  
The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of marine protection, development and utilization, and for the restoration of the national marine ecology, sea areas, coastlines and islands.  
The competent department of transport of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of pollution of the marine environment by non-military vessels within the waters under its port administration and by non-fishing, non-military vessels outside such waters, and shall organize, coordinate and direct emergency response to major marine oil spills. The maritime administrative agency shall be specifically responsible for the supervision and administration of pollution of the marine environment by relevant vessels within the aforementioned waters, and for the investigation and handling of pollution accidents; it shall board and inspect foreign vessels navigating, anchoring or operating within the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China to handle pollution accidents caused thereby. Where a vessel pollution accident causes damage to fisheries, the fishery authority shall be involved in the investigation and handling.  
The competent department of fisheries of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of pollution of the marine environment by non-military vessels within the waters of fishing ports and by fishing vessels outside such waters, for the protection of the ecological environment of fishing waters, and for the investigation and handling of fishery pollution accidents other than those specified in the preceding paragraph.  
The departments of development and reform, water administration, housing and urban-rural construction, forestry and grassland, and other relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for marine environmental protection work in their respective industries and fields within the scope of their respective functions and duties.  
The coast guard agency shall, within the scope of its functions and duties, conduct supervision and inspection of activities such as marine engineering construction projects, pollution damage to the marine environment caused by ocean dumping of waste, and the protection and utilization of the seaward side of the coastline of protected natural areas, investigate and punish illegal acts, and participate in emergency response and investigation and handling of marine environmental pollution accidents in accordance with the prescribed authority.  
The ecological environment protection department of the military shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of pollution of the marine environment by military vessels and the investigation and handling of pollution accidents.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　沿海县级以上地方人民政府对其管理海域的海洋环境质量负责。  
国家实行海洋环境保护目标责任制和考核评价制度，将海洋环境保护目标完成情况纳入考核评价的内容。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/防治船舶污染海洋环境管理条例.md#art-74" title="被《防治船舶污染海洋环境管理条例》第74条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup>[2]</sup>

**Article 5** The local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall be responsible for the quality of the marine environment within the sea areas under their administration.  
The State shall implement a target responsibility system and an assessment and evaluation system for marine environmental protection, and incorporate the fulfillment of marine environmental protection targets into the content of assessment and evaluation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　沿海县级以上地方人民政府可以建立海洋环境保护区域协作机制，组织协调其管理海域的环境保护工作。  
跨区域的海洋环境保护工作，由有关沿海地方人民政府协商解决，或者由上级人民政府协调解决。  
跨部门的重大海洋环境保护工作，由国务院生态环境主管部门协调；协调未能解决的，由国务院作出决定。

**Article 6** The local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas may establish regional cooperation mechanisms for marine environmental protection to organize and coordinate the environmental protection work in the sea areas under their administration.  
Cross-regional marine environmental protection work shall be resolved through consultation among the relevant local coastal people's governments, or through coordination by the people's government at a higher level.  
Major cross-departmental marine environmental protection work shall be coordinated by the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council; if coordination fails to resolve the matter, the State Council shall make a decision.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院和沿海县级以上地方人民政府应当将海洋环境保护工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，按照事权和支出责任划分原则，将海洋环境保护工作所需经费纳入本级政府预算。

**Article 7** The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall incorporate marine environmental protection work into national economic and social development plans, and, in accordance with the principle of division of powers and expenditure responsibilities, include the funds required for marine environmental protection work into the budgets of the governments at the corresponding levels.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　各级人民政府及其有关部门应当加强海洋环境保护的宣传教育和知识普及工作，增强公众海洋环境保护意识，引导公众依法参与海洋环境保护工作；鼓励基层群众性自治组织、社会组织、志愿者等开展海洋环境保护法律法规和知识的宣传活动；按照职责分工依法公开海洋环境相关信息。  
新闻媒体应当采取多种形式开展海洋环境保护的宣传报道，并对违法行为进行舆论监督。

**Article 8** The people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall strengthen publicity, education and knowledge popularization on marine environmental protection, enhance the public's awareness of marine environmental protection, and guide the public to participate in marine environmental protection work in accordance with the law; they shall encourage grassroots autonomous organizations, social organizations, volunteers, and others to carry out publicity activities on marine environmental protection laws, regulations and knowledge; and they shall, according to the division of their functions and duties, disclose relevant information on the marine environment in accordance with the law.  
News media shall adopt various forms to conduct publicity and reporting on marine environmental protection, and exercise public opinion supervision over illegal acts.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何单位和个人都有保护海洋环境的义务，并有权对污染海洋环境、破坏海洋生态的单位和个人，以及海洋环境监督管理人员的违法行为进行监督和检举。  
从事影响海洋环境活动的任何单位和个人，都应当采取有效措施，防止、减轻海洋环境污染、生态破坏。排污者应当依法公开排污信息。

**Article 9** All units and individuals shall have the obligation to protect the marine environment, and shall have the right to supervise and report any unit or individual that pollutes the marine environment or damages the marine ecology, as well as any illegal act committed by marine environmental supervision and administration personnel.  
All units and individuals engaged in activities affecting the marine environment shall take effective measures to prevent and mitigate marine environmental pollution and ecological damage. Polluters shall disclose their pollutant discharge information in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家鼓励、支持海洋环境保护科学技术研究、开发和应用，促进海洋环境保护信息化建设，加强海洋环境保护专业技术人才培养，提高海洋环境保护科学技术水平。  
国家鼓励、支持海洋环境保护国际交流与合作。

**Article 10** The State shall encourage and support scientific and technological research, development and application for marine environmental protection, promote the construction of informatization for marine environmental protection, strengthen the cultivation of professional and technical personnel for marine environmental protection, and raise the level of science and technology for marine environmental protection.  
The State shall encourage and support international exchanges and cooperation in marine environmental protection.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对在海洋环境保护工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 11** Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in marine environmental protection shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　海洋环境监督管理

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家实施陆海统筹、区域联动的海洋环境监督管理制度，加强规划、标准、监测等监督管理制度的衔接协调。  
各级人民政府及其有关部门应当加强海洋环境监督管理能力建设，提高海洋环境监督管理科技化、信息化水平。

**Article 12** The State shall implement a marine environmental supervision and administration system featuring land-sea coordination and regional linkage, and strengthen the integration and coordination of supervision and administration systems such as planning, standards, and monitoring.  
The people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall strengthen the capacity building for marine environmental supervision and administration, and enhance the technological and information-based level of marine environmental supervision and administration.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家优先将生态功能极重要、生态极敏感脆弱的海域划入生态保护红线，实行严格保护。  
开发利用海洋资源或者从事影响海洋环境的建设活动，应当根据国土空间规划科学合理布局，严格遵守国土空间用途管制要求，严守生态保护红线，不得造成海洋生态环境的损害。沿海地方各级人民政府应当根据国土空间规划，保护和科学合理地使用海域。沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当加强对生态保护红线内人为活动的监督管理，定期评估保护成效。  
国务院有关部门、沿海设区的市级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门，对其组织编制的国土空间规划和相关规划，应当依法进行包括海洋环境保护内容在内的环境影响评价。

**Article 13** The State shall give priority to delimiting sea areas with extremely important ecological functions and extremely sensitive and fragile ecology into the ecological protection red line, and implement strict protection.  
The development and utilization of marine resources or the construction activities affecting the marine environment shall be scientifically and rationally laid out in accordance with the territorial spatial plan, strictly comply with the requirements for use control of territorial space, strictly adhere to the ecological protection red line, and shall not cause damage to the marine ecological environment. The local people's governments at all levels in coastal areas shall protect and use sea areas scientifically and rationally in accordance with the territorial spatial plan. The people's governments of coastal provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall strengthen the supervision and administration of human activities within the ecological protection red line, and regularly assess the effectiveness of protection.  
The relevant departments of the State Council, the local people's governments at or above the districted city level in coastal areas and their relevant departments shall, for the territorial spatial plans and relevant plans organized by them, conduct environmental impact assessments including the content of marine environmental protection in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院生态环境主管部门会同有关部门、机构和沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定全国海洋生态环境保护规划，报国务院批准后实施。全国海洋生态环境保护规划应当与全国国土空间规划相衔接。  
沿海地方各级人民政府应当根据全国海洋生态环境保护规划，组织实施其管理海域的海洋环境保护工作。

**Article 14** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall, jointly with relevant departments, agencies and the people's governments of coastal provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, formulate a national marine ecological environment protection plan, which shall be implemented after being submitted to and approved by the State Council. The national marine ecological environment protection plan shall be aligned with the national territorial spatial plan.  
The local people's governments at all levels in coastal areas shall, in accordance with the national marine ecological environment protection plan, organize and implement marine environmental protection work in the sea areas under their administration.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当根据其管理海域的生态环境和资源利用状况，将其管理海域纳入生态环境分区管控方案和生态环境准入清单，报国务院生态环境主管部门备案后实施。生态环境分区管控方案和生态环境准入清单应当与国土空间规划相衔接。

**Article 15** The people's governments of coastal provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the ecological environment and resource utilization status of the sea areas under their administration, incorporate such sea areas into the ecological environment zoning control plan and the ecological environment access list, and implement them after filing with the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council. The ecological environment zoning control plan and the ecological environment access list shall be aligned with the territorial spatial plan.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院生态环境主管部门根据海洋环境质量状况和国家经济、技术条件，制定国家海洋环境质量标准。  
沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府对国家海洋环境质量标准中未作规定的项目，可以制定地方海洋环境质量标准；对国家海洋环境质量标准中已作规定的项目，可以制定严于国家海洋环境质量标准的地方海洋环境质量标准。地方海洋环境质量标准应当报国务院生态环境主管部门备案。  
国家鼓励开展海洋环境基准研究。

**Article 16** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall formulate national marine environmental quality standards based on the status of marine environmental quality and the national economic and technical conditions.  
The people's governments of coastal provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate local marine environmental quality standards for items not specified in the national marine environmental quality standards; and may formulate local marine environmental quality standards that are stricter than the national marine environmental quality standards for items already specified in the national standards. Local marine environmental quality standards shall be filed with the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council.  
The State shall encourage research on marine environmental baselines.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　制定海洋环境质量标准，应当征求有关部门、行业协会、企业事业单位、专家和公众等的意见，提高海洋环境质量标准的科学性。  
海洋环境质量标准应当定期评估，并根据评估结果适时修订。

**Article 17** When formulating marine environmental quality standards, opinions from relevant departments, industry associations, enterprises and public institutions, experts, and the public, among others, shall be solicited to enhance the scientific nature of such standards.  
Marine environmental quality standards shall be periodically assessed and revised timely based on the assessment results.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家和有关地方水污染物排放标准的制定，应当将海洋环境质量标准作为重要依据之一。  
对未完成海洋环境保护目标的海域，省级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门暂停审批新增相应种类污染物排放总量的建设项目环境影响报告书（表），会同有关部门约谈该地区人民政府及其有关部门的主要负责人，要求其采取有效措施及时整改，约谈和整改情况应当向社会公开。

**Article 18** The formulation of national and relevant local water pollutant discharge standards shall take the marine environmental quality standards as one of the important bases.  
For sea areas where the marine environmental protection targets have not been fulfilled, the competent departments of ecological environment of the people's governments at or above the provincial level shall suspend the review and approval of environmental impact reports (forms) for construction projects that would increase the total discharge of corresponding types of pollutants, and shall, jointly with relevant departments, hold talks with the principal persons in charge of the people's government of the region concerned and its relevant departments, requiring them to take effective measures for timely rectification. The situation of talks and rectification shall be disclosed to the public.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家加强海洋环境质量管控，推进海域综合治理，严格海域排污许可管理，提升重点海域海洋环境质量。  
需要直接向海洋排放工业废水、医疗污水的海岸工程和海洋工程单位，城镇污水集中处理设施的运营单位及其他企业事业单位和生产经营者，应当依法取得排污许可证。排污许可的管理按照国务院有关规定执行。  
实行排污许可管理的企业事业单位和其他生产经营者应当执行排污许可证关于排放污染物的种类、浓度、排放量、排放方式、排放去向和自行监测等要求。  
禁止通过私设暗管或者篡改、伪造监测数据，以及不正常运行污染防治设施等逃避监管的方式向海洋排放污染物。

**Article 19** The State shall strengthen the control of marine environmental quality, promote comprehensive management of sea areas, strictly manage pollution discharge permits for sea areas, and enhance the marine environmental quality of key sea areas.  
Units of coastal engineering and marine engineering that need to discharge industrial wastewater or medical sewage directly into the sea, as well as operators of centralized urban sewage treatment facilities and other enterprises, public institutions and producers and business operators, shall obtain pollution discharge permits in accordance with the law. The management of pollution discharge permits shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the State Council.  
Enterprises, public institutions and other producers and business operators subject to pollution discharge permit management shall comply with the requirements of the pollution discharge permit concerning the types, concentrations, quantities, methods, destination of pollutant discharge, and self-monitoring, among others.  
It shall be prohibited to discharge pollutants into the sea by means of evading supervision, such as secretly setting up pipes, tampering with or falsifying monitoring data, or operating pollution prevention facilities improperly.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院生态环境主管部门根据海洋环境状况和质量改善要求，会同国务院发展改革、自然资源、住房和城乡建设、交通运输、水行政、渔业等部门和海警机构，划定国家环境治理重点海域及其控制区域，制定综合治理行动方案，报国务院批准后实施。  
沿海设区的市级以上地方人民政府应当根据综合治理行动方案，制定其管理海域的实施方案，因地制宜采取特别管控措施，开展综合治理，协同推进重点海域治理与美丽海湾建设。

**Article 20** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall, in light of the marine environmental conditions and the requirements for quality improvement, and jointly with the departments of development and reform, natural resources, housing and urban-rural construction, transport, water administration, and fisheries of the State Council, and the coast guard agency, delimit the key sea areas for national environmental governance and their control areas, formulate a comprehensive governance action plan, which shall be implemented after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.  
The local people's governments at or above the districted city level in coastal areas shall, based on the comprehensive governance action plan, formulate implementation plans for the sea areas under their administration, adopt special control measures suited to local conditions, carry out comprehensive governance, and jointly promote the governance of key sea areas and the construction of beautiful bays.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　直接向海洋排放应税污染物的企业事业单位和其他生产经营者，应当依照法律规定缴纳环境保护税。  
向海洋倾倒废弃物，应当按照国家有关规定缴纳倾倒费。具体办法由国务院发展改革部门、国务院财政主管部门会同国务院生态环境主管部门制定。

**Article 21** Enterprises, institutions and other producers and operators that directly discharge taxable pollutants into the sea shall pay environmental protection tax in accordance with the provisions of laws.  
Those who dump waste into the sea shall pay dumping fees in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. The specific measures shall be formulated by the development and reform department of the State Council and the competent financial department of the State Council jointly with the competent ecological and environmental department of the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家加强防治海洋环境污染损害的科学技术的研究和开发，对严重污染海洋环境的落后生产工艺和落后设备，实行淘汰制度。  
企业事业单位和其他生产经营者应当优先使用清洁低碳能源，采用资源利用率高、污染物排放量少的清洁生产工艺，防止对海洋环境的污染。

**Article 22** The State shall strengthen scientific and technological research and development for the prevention and control of marine environmental pollution damage, and shall implement a phase-out system for outdated production processes and equipment that severely pollute the marine environment.  
Enterprises, institutions and other producers and operators shall give priority to the use of clean and low-carbon energy, adopt clean production processes with high resource utilization rates and low pollutant emissions, and prevent pollution of the marine environment.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院生态环境主管部门负责海洋生态环境监测工作，制定海洋生态环境监测规范和标准并监督实施，组织实施海洋生态环境质量监测，统一发布国家海洋生态环境状况公报，定期组织对海洋生态环境质量状况进行调查评价。  
国务院自然资源主管部门组织开展海洋资源调查和海洋生态预警监测，发布海洋生态预警监测警报和公报。  
其他依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门和机构应当按照职责分工开展监测、监视。

**Article 23** The competent ecological and environmental department of the State Council shall be responsible for marine ecological and environmental monitoring, formulate marine ecological and environmental monitoring norms and standards and supervise their implementation, organize the implementation of marine ecological and environmental quality monitoring, uniformly release the national bulletin on the state of the marine ecological environment, and periodically organize investigations and evaluations of the quality of the marine ecological environment.  
The competent natural resources department of the State Council shall organize the conduct of marine resource surveys and marine ecological early-warning monitoring, and release marine ecological early-warning monitoring alerts and bulletins.  
Other departments and agencies exercising marine environmental supervision and management powers in accordance with this Law shall carry out monitoring and surveillance in accordance with their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国务院有关部门和海警机构应当向国务院生态环境主管部门提供编制国家海洋生态环境状况公报所必需的入海河口和海洋环境监测、调查、监视等方面的资料。  
生态环境主管部门应当向有关部门和海警机构提供与海洋环境监督管理有关的资料。

**Article 24** Relevant departments of the State Council and the coast guard agency shall provide the competent ecological and environmental department of the State Council with materials on monitoring, investigation and surveillance of estuaries and the marine environment necessary for the preparation of the national bulletin on the state of the marine ecological environment.  
The competent ecological and environmental department shall provide relevant departments and the coast guard agency with materials related to marine environmental supervision and management.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国务院生态环境主管部门会同有关部门和机构通过智能化的综合信息系统，为海洋环境保护监督管理、信息共享提供服务。  
国务院有关部门、海警机构和沿海县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当按照规定，推进综合监测、协同监测和常态化监测，加强监测数据、执法信息等海洋环境管理信息共享，提高海洋环境保护综合管理水平。

**Article 25** The competent ecological and environmental department of the State Council shall, jointly with relevant departments and agencies, provide services for marine environmental protection supervision, management and information sharing through an intelligent comprehensive information system.  
Relevant departments of the State Council, the coast guard agency, and local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with regulations, promote comprehensive monitoring, coordinated monitoring and routine monitoring, strengthen sharing of monitoring data, law enforcement information and other marine environment management information, and improve the comprehensive management level of marine environmental protection.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家加强海洋辐射环境监测，国务院生态环境主管部门负责制定海洋辐射环境应急监测方案并组织实施。

**Article 26** The State shall strengthen marine radiation environmental monitoring. The competent ecological and environmental department of the State Council shall be responsible for formulating a marine radiation environmental emergency monitoring plan and organizing its implementation.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　因发生事故或者其他突发性事件，造成或者可能造成海洋环境污染、生态破坏事件的单位和个人，应当立即采取有效措施解除或者减轻危害，及时向可能受到危害者通报，并向依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门和机构报告，接受调查处理。  
沿海县级以上地方人民政府在本行政区域近岸海域的生态环境受到严重损害时，应当采取有效措施，解除或者减轻危害。

**Article 27** Where, due to an accident or other emergency, an event causing or likely to cause marine environmental pollution or ecological damage occurs, the unit or individual concerned shall immediately take effective measures to eliminate or mitigate the harm, promptly notify those who may be harmed, and report to the departments and agencies exercising marine environmental supervision and management powers in accordance with this Law, and shall accept investigation and handling.  
Where the ecological environment of the offshore sea areas within their respective administrative regions is severely damaged, the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall take effective measures to eliminate or mitigate the harm.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家根据防止海洋环境污染的需要，制定国家重大海上污染事件应急预案，建立健全海上溢油污染等应急机制，保障应对工作的必要经费。  
国家建立重大海上溢油应急处置部际联席会议制度。国务院交通运输主管部门牵头组织编制国家重大海上溢油应急处置预案并组织实施。  
国务院生态环境主管部门负责制定全国海洋石油勘探开发海上溢油污染事件应急预案并组织实施。  
国家海事管理机构负责制定全国船舶重大海上溢油污染事件应急预案，报国务院生态环境主管部门、国务院应急管理部门备案。  
沿海县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当制定有关应急预案，在发生海洋突发环境事件时，及时启动应急预案，采取有效措施，解除或者减轻危害。  
可能发生海洋突发环境事件的单位，应当按照有关规定，制定本单位的应急预案，配备应急设备和器材，定期组织开展应急演练；应急预案应当向依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门和机构备案。

**Article 28** The State shall, based on the needs to prevent marine environmental pollution, formulate a national emergency plan for major marine pollution incidents, establish and improve emergency mechanisms for offshore oil spill pollution, among others, and guarantee the necessary funds for response work.  
The State shall establish an inter-ministerial joint conference system for major offshore oil spill emergency response. The competent transportation department of the State Council shall take the lead in organizing the formulation of a national emergency response plan for major offshore oil spills and organize its implementation.  
The competent ecological and environmental department of the State Council shall be responsible for formulating a national emergency plan for offshore oil spill pollution incidents arising from marine petroleum exploration and development and organize its implementation.  
The national maritime administrative agency shall be responsible for formulating a national emergency plan for major offshore oil spill pollution incidents from ships, and shall file it with the competent ecological and environmental department of the State Council and the emergency management department of the State Council.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas and their relevant departments shall formulate relevant emergency plans, and in the event of a marine environmental emergency, shall promptly activate the emergency plan and take effective measures to eliminate or mitigate the harm.  
Units that may cause marine environmental emergencies shall, in accordance with relevant regulations, formulate their own emergency plans, equip emergency equipment and appliances, and regularly organize emergency drills; the emergency plans shall be filed with the departments and agencies exercising marine environmental supervision and management powers in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门和机构，有权对从事影响海洋环境活动的单位和个人进行现场检查；在巡航监视中发现违反本法规定的行为时，应当予以制止并调查取证，必要时有权采取有效措施，防止事态扩大，并报告有关部门或者机构处理。  
被检查者应当如实反映情况，提供必要的资料。检查者应当依法为被检查者保守商业秘密、个人隐私和个人信息。  
依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门和机构可以在海上实行联合执法。

**Article 29** Departments and agencies exercising marine environmental supervision and management powers in accordance with this Law shall have the right to conduct on-site inspections of units and individuals engaged in activities affecting the marine environment; when discovering acts violating the provisions of this Law during cruise surveillance, they shall stop such acts and investigate for evidence, and when necessary, shall have the right to take effective measures to prevent the expansion of the situation and report to relevant departments or agencies for handling.  
The party being inspected shall truthfully provide information and necessary materials. The inspector shall, in accordance with law, keep confidential the trade secrets, personal privacy and personal information of the inspected party.  
Departments and agencies exercising marine environmental supervision and management powers in accordance with this Law may conduct joint law enforcement at sea.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　造成或者可能造成严重海洋环境污染、生态破坏的，或者有关证据可能灭失或者被隐匿的，依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门和机构可以查封、扣押有关船舶、设施、设备、物品。

**Article 30** Where serious marine environmental pollution or ecological damage has been caused or may be caused, or where relevant evidence may be lost or concealed, the departments and agencies exercising marine environmental supervision and management powers in accordance with this Law may seal up or seize the relevant ships, facilities, equipment or articles.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　在中华人民共和国管辖海域以外，造成或者可能造成中华人民共和国管辖海域环境污染、生态破坏的，有关部门和机构有权采取必要的措施。

**Article 31** Where an act outside the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China causes or may cause environmental pollution or ecological damage to sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, the relevant departments and agencies shall have the right to take necessary measures.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院生态环境主管部门会同有关部门和机构建立向海洋排放污染物、从事废弃物海洋倾倒、从事海洋生态环境治理和服务的企业事业单位和其他生产经营者信用记录与评价应用制度，将相关信用记录纳入全国公共信用信息共享平台。

**Article 32** The competent ecological and environmental department of the State Council shall, jointly with relevant departments and agencies, establish a system for credit records and evaluation application of enterprises, institutions and other producers and operators that discharge pollutants into the sea, engage in marine dumping of waste, or engage in marine ecological environment governance and services, and incorporate relevant credit records into the national public credit information sharing platform.

### 第三章　海洋生态保护

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家加强海洋生态保护，提升海洋生态系统质量和多样性、稳定性、持续性。  
国务院和沿海地方各级人民政府应当采取有效措施，重点保护红树林、珊瑚礁、海藻场、海草床、滨海湿地、海岛、海湾、入海河口、重要渔业水域等具有典型性、代表性的海洋生态系统，珍稀濒危海洋生物的天然集中分布区，具有重要经济价值的海洋生物生存区域及有重大科学文化价值的海洋自然遗迹和自然景观。

**Article 33** The State shall strengthen marine ecological protection, and enhance the quality, diversity, stability and sustainability of marine ecosystems.  
The State Council and local people's governments at all levels in coastal areas shall take effective measures to focus on protecting marine ecosystems of typical and representative nature, such as mangroves, coral reefs, seaweed beds, seagrass beds, coastal wetlands, islands, bays, estuaries and important fishing waters, natural concentration areas of rare and endangered marine organisms, living areas of marine organisms with important economic value, and marine natural relics and natural landscapes with significant scientific and cultural value.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院和沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府及其有关部门根据保护海洋的需要，依法将重要的海洋生态系统、珍稀濒危海洋生物的天然集中分布区、海洋自然遗迹和自然景观集中分布区等区域纳入国家公园、自然保护区或者自然公园等自然保护地。

**Article 34** The State Council and the people's governments of coastal provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with the needs of marine protection and according to law, include areas such as important marine ecosystems, natural concentration areas of rare and endangered marine organisms, and concentrated distribution areas of marine natural relics and natural landscapes into natural protected areas, such as national parks, nature reserves or natural parks.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家建立健全海洋生态保护补偿制度。  
国务院和沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当通过转移支付、产业扶持等方式支持开展海洋生态保护补偿。  
沿海地方各级人民政府应当落实海洋生态保护补偿资金，确保其用于海洋生态保护补偿。

**Article 35** The State shall establish and improve a compensation system for marine ecological protection.  
The State Council and the people's governments of coastal provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall support the conduct of marine ecological protection compensation through transfer payments, industrial support and other means.  
Local people's governments at all levels in coastal areas shall implement the funds for marine ecological protection compensation and ensure that they are used for marine ecological protection compensation.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家加强海洋生物多样性保护，健全海洋生物多样性调查、监测、评估和保护体系，维护和修复重要海洋生态廊道，防止对海洋生物多样性的破坏。  
开发利用海洋和海岸带资源，应当对重要海洋生态系统、生物物种、生物遗传资源实施有效保护，维护海洋生物多样性。  
引进海洋动植物物种，应当进行科学论证，避免对海洋生态系统造成危害。

**Article 36** The State shall strengthen the protection of marine biodiversity, improve the system for investigation, monitoring, assessment and protection of marine biodiversity, maintain and restore important marine ecological corridors, and prevent the destruction of marine biodiversity.  
The development and utilization of marine and coastal resources shall provide effective protection for important marine ecosystems, biological species and biological genetic resources, and maintain marine biodiversity.  
The introduction of marine animal and plant species shall be scientifically justified to avoid causing harm to the marine ecosystem.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家鼓励科学开展水生生物增殖放流，支持科学规划，因地制宜采取投放人工鱼礁和种植海藻场、海草床、珊瑚等措施，恢复海洋生物多样性，修复改善海洋生态。

**Article 37** The State shall encourage the scientific conduct of aquatic organism enhancement and release, support scientific planning, and take measures such as deploying artificial reefs and planting seaweed beds, seagrass beds, and corals according to local conditions, to restore marine biodiversity and repair and improve the marine ecology.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　开发海岛及周围海域的资源，应当采取严格的生态保护措施，不得造成海岛地形、岸滩、植被和海岛周围海域生态环境的损害。

**Article 38** The development of resources on islands and surrounding sea areas shall adopt strict ecological protection measures, and shall not cause damage to the island terrain, shore beaches, vegetation, or the ecological environment of the surrounding sea areas.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家严格保护自然岸线，建立健全自然岸线控制制度。沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责划定严格保护岸线的范围并发布。  
沿海地方各级人民政府应当加强海岸线分类保护与利用，保护修复自然岸线，促进人工岸线生态化，维护岸线岸滩稳定平衡，因地制宜、科学合理划定海岸建筑退缩线。  
禁止违法占用、损害自然岸线。

**Article 39** The State shall strictly protect natural coastlines, and establish and improve a control system for natural coastlines. The people's governments of coastal provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for delineating and publishing the scope of strictly protected coastlines.  
Local people's governments at all levels in coastal areas shall strengthen the classified protection and utilization of coastlines, protect and restore natural coastlines, promote the ecologicalization of artificial coastlines, maintain the stability and balance of coastlines and beaches, and scientifically and reasonably delineate coastal building setback lines according to local conditions.  
It is prohibited to illegally occupy or damage natural coastlines.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国务院水行政主管部门确定重要入海河流的生态流量管控指标，应当征求并研究国务院生态环境、自然资源等部门的意见。确定生态流量管控指标，应当进行科学论证，综合考虑水资源条件、气候状况、生态环境保护要求、生活生产用水状况等因素。  
入海河口所在地县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门按照河海联动的要求，制定实施河口生态修复和其他保护措施方案，加强对水、沙、盐、潮滩、生物种群、河口形态的综合监测，采取有效措施防止海水入侵和倒灌，维护河口良好生态功能。

**Article 40** When determining the ecological flow control indicators for important rivers flowing into the sea, the competent water administration department of the State Council shall seek and study the opinions of the competent ecological and environmental, natural resources and other departments of the State Council. The determination of ecological flow control indicators shall be scientifically justified, taking into comprehensive consideration factors such as water resource conditions, climatic conditions, ecological and environmental protection requirements, and domestic and production water use conditions.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the areas where the estuaries are located and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with the requirements for river-sea coordination, formulate and implement plans for estuarine ecological restoration and other protection measures, strengthen comprehensive monitoring of water, sediment, salt, tidal flats, biological populations, and estuarine morphology, and take effective measures to prevent seawater intrusion and backflow, so as to maintain good estuarine ecological functions.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　沿海地方各级人民政府应当结合当地自然环境的特点，建设海岸防护设施、沿海防护林、沿海城镇园林和绿地，对海岸侵蚀和海水入侵地区进行综合治理。  
禁止毁坏海岸防护设施、沿海防护林、沿海城镇园林和绿地。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/生态环境/中华人民共和国海洋石油勘探开发环境保护管理条例.md#art-28" title="被《中华人民共和国海洋石油勘探开发环境保护管理条例》第28条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 41** The local people's governments at various levels in coastal areas shall, taking into account the characteristics of the local natural environment, construct coastal protection facilities, coastal shelterbelts, coastal urban parks and green spaces, and carry out comprehensive management in areas affected by coastal erosion and seawater intrusion.  
It shall be prohibited to destroy coastal protection facilities, coastal shelterbelts, coastal urban parks and green spaces.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　对遭到破坏的具有重要生态、经济、社会价值的海洋生态系统，应当进行修复。海洋生态修复应当以改善生境、恢复生物多样性和生态系统基本功能为重点，以自然恢复为主、人工修复为辅，并优先修复具有典型性、代表性的海洋生态系统。  
国务院自然资源主管部门负责统筹海洋生态修复，牵头组织编制海洋生态修复规划并实施有关海洋生态修复重大工程。编制海洋生态修复规划，应当进行科学论证评估。  
国务院自然资源、生态环境等部门应当按照职责分工开展修复成效监督评估。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/生态环境/中华人民共和国海洋石油勘探开发环境保护管理条例.md#art-22" title="被《中华人民共和国海洋石油勘探开发环境保护管理条例》第22条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/生态环境/中华人民共和国海洋石油勘探开发环境保护管理条例.md#art-25" title="被《中华人民共和国海洋石油勘探开发环境保护管理条例》第25条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 42** Marine ecosystems with significant ecological, economic and social values that have been damaged shall be restored. Marine ecological restoration shall focus on improving habitats, restoring biodiversity and basic functions of ecosystems, with natural recovery as the primary method and artificial restoration as supplementary, and priority shall be given to restoring typical and representative marine ecosystems.  
The competent department of natural resources under the State Council shall be responsible for coordinating marine ecological restoration, taking the lead in organizing the preparation of marine ecological restoration plans and implementing major projects related to marine ecological restoration. Scientific feasibility study and assessment shall be conducted in preparing marine ecological restoration plans.  
The competent departments of natural resources and ecological environment under the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, carry out supervision and assessment of the effectiveness of restoration.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国务院自然资源主管部门负责开展全国海洋生态灾害预防、风险评估和隐患排查治理。  
沿海县级以上地方人民政府负责其管理海域的海洋生态灾害应对工作，采取必要的灾害预防、处置和灾后恢复措施，防止和减轻灾害影响。  
企业事业单位和其他生产经营者应当采取必要应对措施，防止海洋生态灾害扩大。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/生态环境/中华人民共和国海洋石油勘探开发环境保护管理条例.md#art-24" title="被《中华人民共和国海洋石油勘探开发环境保护管理条例》第24条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 43** The competent department of natural resources under the State Council shall be responsible for carrying out national marine ecological disaster prevention, risk assessment, and investigation and management of potential hazards.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall be responsible for marine ecological disaster response within the sea areas under their administration, and shall take necessary measures for disaster prevention, response, and post-disaster recovery to prevent and mitigate the impact of disasters.  
Enterprises, public institutions, and other producers and business operators shall take necessary response measures to prevent the expansion of marine ecological disasters.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家鼓励发展生态渔业，推广多种生态渔业生产方式，改善海洋生态状况，保护海洋环境。  
沿海县级以上地方人民政府应当因地制宜编制并组织实施养殖水域滩涂规划，确定可以用于养殖业的水域和滩涂，科学划定海水养殖禁养区、限养区和养殖区，建立禁养区内海水养殖的清理和退出机制。

**Article 44** The State encourages the development of eco-friendly fisheries, promotes various eco-friendly fishery production methods, improves the marine ecological status, and protects the marine environment.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall, in light of local conditions, formulate and organize the implementation of plans for aquaculture waters and tidal flats, determine the waters and tidal flats that may be used for aquaculture, scientifically designate prohibited aquaculture zones, restricted aquaculture zones, and aquaculture zones for marine aquaculture, and establish mechanisms for the clearance and withdrawal of marine aquaculture in prohibited aquaculture zones.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　从事海水养殖活动应当保护海域环境，科学确定养殖规模和养殖密度，合理投饵、投肥，正确使用药物，及时规范收集处理固体废物，防止造成海洋生态环境的损害。  
禁止在氮磷浓度严重超标的近岸海域新增或者扩大投饵、投肥海水养殖规模。  
向海洋排放养殖尾水污染物等应当符合污染物排放标准。沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当制定海水养殖污染物排放相关地方标准，加强养殖尾水污染防治的监督管理。  
工厂化养殖和设置统一排污口的集中连片养殖的排污单位，应当按照有关规定对养殖尾水自行监测。

**Article 45** Engaged in marine aquaculture activities, one shall protect the marine environment, scientifically determine the scale and density of aquaculture, reasonably apply feed and fertilizer, properly use drugs, and timely collect and dispose of solid wastes in a regulated manner, so as to prevent damage to the marine ecological environment.  
It shall be prohibited to add or expand the scale of marine aquaculture involving feed and fertilizer application in coastal waters where the nitrogen and phosphorus concentrations seriously exceed the standards.  
The discharge of aquaculture tail water pollutants into the sea shall meet the pollutant discharge standards. The people's governments of coastal provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate relevant local standards for the discharge of pollutants from marine aquaculture and strengthen the supervision and management of the prevention and control of pollution from aquaculture tail water.  
Pollutant-discharging entities engaged in factory farming and concentrated contiguous farming with unified sewage outlets shall conduct self-monitoring of aquaculture tail water in accordance with relevant provisions.

### 第四章　陆源污染物污染防治

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　向海域排放陆源污染物，应当严格执行国家或者地方规定的标准和有关规定。

**Article 46** The discharge of land-based pollutants into the sea shall strictly comply with the standards and relevant provisions prescribed by the State or local authorities.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　入海排污口位置的选择，应当符合国土空间用途管制要求，根据海水动力条件和有关规定，经科学论证后，报设区的市级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门备案。排污口的责任主体应当加强排污口监测，按照规定开展监控和自动监测。  
生态环境主管部门应当在完成备案后十五个工作日内将入海排污口设置情况通报自然资源、渔业等部门和海事管理机构、海警机构、军队生态环境保护部门。  
沿海县级以上地方人民政府应当根据排污口类别、责任主体，组织有关部门对本行政区域内各类入海排污口进行排查整治和日常监督管理，建立健全近岸水体、入海排污口、排污管线、污染源全链条治理体系。  
国务院生态环境主管部门负责制定入海排污口设置和管理的具体办法，制定入海排污口技术规范，组织建设统一的入海排污口信息平台，加强动态更新、信息共享和公开。

**Article 47** The selection of locations for sea outfalls shall comply with the requirements of territorial space use control, and shall be subject to scientific feasibility study and then filed with the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the city with districts level, based on hydrodynamic conditions and relevant provisions. The responsible entities of outfalls shall strengthen the monitoring of outfalls and carry out monitoring and automatic monitoring in accordance with relevant provisions.  
The competent department of ecological environment shall, within fifteen working days after the completion of filing, notify the departments of natural resources, fisheries, and other relevant departments, as well as maritime administrative agencies, coast guard agencies, and the ecological environment protection department of the military, of the establishment of sea outfalls.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall, based on the categories and responsible entities of outfalls, organize relevant departments to carry out investigation, remediation, and routine supervision and management of various types of sea outfalls within their respective administrative areas, and establish and improve a full-chain governance system covering nearshore water bodies, sea outfalls, discharge pipelines, and pollution sources.  
The competent department of ecological environment under the State Council shall be responsible for formulating specific measures for the establishment and management of sea outfalls, formulating technical specifications for sea outfalls, organizing the construction of a unified information platform for sea outfalls, and strengthening dynamic updates, information sharing, and disclosure.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　禁止在自然保护地、重要渔业水域、海水浴场、生态保护红线区域及其他需要特别保护的区域，新设工业排污口和城镇污水处理厂排污口；法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。  
在有条件的地区，应当将排污口深水设置，实行离岸排放。

**Article 48** It shall be prohibited to newly establish industrial sewage outfalls and sewage outfalls of urban sewage treatment plants in nature reserves, important fishery waters, seawater bathing beaches, areas within the ecological protection red line, and other areas requiring special protection, unless otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations.  
In areas where conditions permit, outfalls shall be set in deep water and discharge shall be carried out offshore.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　经开放式沟（渠）向海洋排放污染物的，对开放式沟（渠）按照国家和地方的有关规定、标准实施水环境质量管理。

**Article 49** Where pollutants are discharged into the sea through open ditches (canals), water environmental quality management shall be implemented for such open ditches (canals) in accordance with relevant provisions and standards of the State and local authorities.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国务院有关部门和县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当依照水污染防治有关法律、行政法规的规定，加强入海河流管理，协同推进入海河流污染防治，使入海河口的水质符合入海河口环境质量相关要求。  
入海河流流域省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当按照国家有关规定，加强入海总氮、总磷排放的管控，制定控制方案并组织实施。

**Article 50** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on water pollution prevention and control, strengthen the management of rivers entering the sea, and jointly promote the prevention and control of pollution in rivers entering the sea, so that the water quality at the estuaries meets the relevant requirements of estuarine environmental quality.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government within the basins of rivers entering the sea shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, strengthen the control of total nitrogen and total phosphorus discharged into the sea, formulate control plans and organize their implementation.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　禁止向海域排放油类、酸液、碱液、剧毒废液。  
禁止向海域排放污染海洋环境、破坏海洋生态的放射性废水。  
严格控制向海域排放含有不易降解的有机物和重金属的废水。

**Article 51** It shall be prohibited to discharge oils, acid liquids, alkaline liquids, or highly toxic waste liquids into the sea.  
It shall be prohibited to discharge radioactive wastewater that pollutes the marine environment and damages the marine ecology into the sea.  
The discharge of wastewater containing non-degradable organic substances and heavy metals into the sea shall be strictly controlled.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　含病原体的医疗污水、生活污水和工业废水应当经过处理，符合国家和地方有关排放标准后，方可排入海域。

**Article 52** Medical wastewater, domestic sewage, and industrial wastewater containing pathogens shall be treated and may be discharged into the sea only after meeting the relevant discharge standards of the State and local authorities.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　含有机物和营养物质的工业废水、生活污水，应当严格控制向海湾、半封闭海及其他自净能力较差的海域排放。

**Article 53** The discharge of industrial wastewater and domestic sewage containing organic substances and nutrients into bays, semi-enclosed seas, and other sea areas with poor self-purification capacity shall be strictly controlled.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　向海域排放含热废水，应当采取有效措施，保证邻近自然保护地、渔业水域的水温符合国家和地方海洋环境质量标准，避免热污染对珍稀濒危海洋生物、海洋水产资源造成危害。

**Article 54** Where thermal wastewater is discharged into the sea, effective measures shall be taken to ensure that the water temperature in adjacent nature reserves and fishery waters meets the national and local marine environmental quality standards, and to prevent harm to rare and endangered marine organisms and marine aquatic resources caused by thermal pollution.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　沿海地方各级人民政府应当加强农业面源污染防治。沿海农田、林场施用化学农药，应当执行国家农药安全使用的规定和标准。沿海农田、林场应当合理使用化肥和植物生长调节剂。

**Article 55** Local people's governments at various levels in coastal areas shall strengthen the prevention and control of agricultural non-point source pollution. The application of chemical pesticides in coastal farmland and forest farms shall comply with the national regulations and standards on safe use of pesticides. Coastal farmland and forest farms shall rationally use chemical fertilizers and plant growth regulators.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　在沿海陆域弃置、堆放和处理尾矿、矿渣、煤灰渣、垃圾和其他固体废物的，依照《中华人民共和国固体废物污染环境防治法》的有关规定执行，并采取有效措施防止固体废物进入海洋。  
禁止在岸滩弃置、堆放和处理固体废物；法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 56** Where tailings, slag, coal ash, garbage, and other solid wastes are dumped, piled, or disposed of in coastal land areas, the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Control of Environmental Pollution by Solid Wastes shall be complied with, and effective measures shall be taken to prevent solid wastes from entering the sea.  
It shall be prohibited to dump, pile, or dispose of solid wastes on the shore or beach, unless otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　沿海县级以上地方人民政府负责其管理海域的海洋垃圾污染防治，建立海洋垃圾监测、清理制度，统筹规划建设陆域接收、转运、处理海洋垃圾的设施，明确有关部门、乡镇、街道、企业事业单位等的海洋垃圾管控区域，建立海洋垃圾监测、拦截、收集、打捞、运输、处理体系并组织实施，采取有效措施鼓励、支持公众参与上述活动。国务院生态环境、住房和城乡建设、发展改革等部门应当按照职责分工加强海洋垃圾污染防治的监督指导和保障。

**Article 57** Local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall be responsible for the prevention and control of marine litter pollution within the sea areas under their administration, establish a monitoring and cleaning system for marine litter, make overall plans for the construction of facilities on land for receiving, transferring, and treating marine litter, designate the marine litter control areas for relevant departments, townships, sub-districts, enterprises, public institutions, etc., establish and organize the implementation of a monitoring, interception, collection, salvage, transportation, and treatment system for marine litter, and take effective measures to encourage and support public participation in the above activities. The competent departments of ecological environment, housing and urban-rural construction, and development and reform under the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, strengthen supervision, guidance, and support for the prevention and control of marine litter pollution.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　禁止经中华人民共和国内水、领海过境转移危险废物。  
经中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域转移危险废物的，应当事先取得国务院生态环境主管部门的书面同意。

**Article 58** It shall be prohibited to transit hazardous wastes through the internal waters or territorial sea of the People's Republic of China.  
Where hazardous wastes are transferred through other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, the written consent of the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council shall be obtained in advance.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　沿海县级以上地方人民政府应当建设和完善排水管网，根据改善海洋环境质量的需要建设城镇污水处理厂和其他污水处理设施，加强城乡污水处理。  
建设污水海洋处置工程，应当符合国家有关规定。

**Article 59** Local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall construct and improve drainage pipe networks, and construct urban sewage treatment plants and other sewage treatment facilities based on the need to improve marine environmental quality, and strengthen urban and rural sewage treatment.  
The construction of marine sewage disposal projects shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国家采取必要措施，防止、减少和控制来自大气层或者通过大气层造成的海洋环境污染损害。

**Article 60** The State shall take necessary measures to prevent, reduce, and control pollution damage to the marine environment originating from or through the atmosphere.

### 第五章　工程建设项目污染防治

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　新建、改建、扩建工程建设项目，应当遵守国家有关建设项目环境保护管理的规定，并把污染防治和生态保护所需资金纳入建设项目投资计划。  
禁止在依法划定的自然保护地、重要渔业水域及其他需要特别保护的区域，违法建设污染环境、破坏生态的工程建设项目或者从事其他活动。

**Article 61** For the construction, reconstruction, or expansion of engineering construction projects, the provisions of the State concerning environmental protection management of construction projects shall be complied with, and the funds required for pollution prevention and ecological protection shall be included in the investment plan of the construction project.  
It is prohibited to illegally construct engineering construction projects that pollute the environment or damage the ecology, or engage in other activities, in legally designated nature reserves, important fishery waters, and other areas requiring special protection.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　工程建设项目应当按照国家有关建设项目环境影响评价的规定进行环境影响评价。未依法进行并通过环境影响评价的建设项目，不得开工建设。  
环境保护设施应当与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投产使用。环境保护设施应当符合经批准的环境影响评价报告书（表）的要求。建设单位应当依照有关法律法规的规定，对环境保护设施进行验收，编制验收报告，并向社会公开。环境保护设施未经验收或者经验收不合格的，建设项目不得投入生产或者使用。

**Article 62** Engineering construction projects shall undergo environmental impact assessment in accordance with the State's provisions concerning environmental impact assessment of construction projects. No construction project that has not undergone and passed the environmental impact assessment according to law may commence construction.  
Environmental protection facilities shall be designed, constructed, and put into operation simultaneously with the main project. Environmental protection facilities shall meet the requirements of the approved environmental impact report (form). The construction entity shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, carry out acceptance inspection of environmental protection facilities, prepare an acceptance report, and make it available to the public. Where the environmental protection facilities have not undergone acceptance inspection or have failed the acceptance inspection, the construction project may not be put into production or use.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　禁止在沿海陆域新建不符合国家产业政策的化学制浆造纸、化工、印染、制革、电镀、酿造、炼油、岸边冲滩拆船及其他严重污染海洋环境的生产项目。

**Article 63** It is prohibited to construct new production projects in coastal land areas that do not conform to the national industrial policy, such as chemical pulping and papermaking, chemical industry, printing and dyeing, tanning, electroplating, brewing, oil refining, beach beaching and shipbreaking, and other production projects that seriously pollute the marine environment.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　新建、改建、扩建工程建设项目，应当采取有效措施，保护国家和地方重点保护的野生动植物及其生存环境，保护海洋水产资源，避免或者减轻对海洋生物的影响。  
禁止在严格保护岸线范围内开采海砂。依法在其他区域开发利用海砂资源，应当采取严格措施，保护海洋环境。载运海砂资源应当持有合法来源证明；海砂开采者应当为载运海砂的船舶提供合法来源证明。  
从岸上打井开采海底矿产资源，应当采取有效措施，防止污染海洋环境。

**Article 64** For the construction, reconstruction, or expansion of engineering construction projects, effective measures shall be taken to protect wild animals and plants under State and local key protection and their habitats, protect marine fishery resources, and avoid or mitigate impacts on marine organisms.  
It is prohibited to exploit sea sand within strictly protected shorelines. Where sea sand resources are exploited and utilized in other areas according to law, strict measures shall be taken to protect the marine environment. The carriage of sea sand resources shall be accompanied by a certificate of lawful origin; the sea sand exploiter shall provide the vessel carrying the sea sand with a certificate of lawful origin.  
For the exploitation of seabed mineral resources by drilling wells from the shore, effective measures shall be taken to prevent pollution of the marine environment.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　工程建设项目不得使用含超标准放射性物质或者易溶出有毒有害物质的材料；不得造成领海基点及其周围环境的侵蚀、淤积和损害，不得危及领海基点的稳定。

**Article 65** Engineering construction projects may not use materials containing radioactive substances exceeding the standards or readily leachable toxic and hazardous substances; may not cause erosion, siltation, or damage to the baseline points of the territorial sea and their surrounding environment, and shall not endanger the stability of the baseline points of the territorial sea.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　工程建设项目需要爆破作业时，应当采取有效措施，保护海洋环境。  
海洋石油勘探开发及输油过程中，应当采取有效措施，避免溢油事故的发生。

**Article 66** Where blasting operations are required for engineering construction projects, effective measures shall be taken to protect the marine environment.  
During the exploration and development of offshore petroleum and the transportation of oil, effective measures shall be taken to avoid the occurrence of oil spill accidents.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　工程建设项目不得违法向海洋排放污染物、废弃物及其他有害物质。  
海洋油气钻井平台（船）、生产生活平台、生产储卸装置等海洋油气装备的含油污水和油性混合物，应当经过处理达标后排放；残油、废油应当予以回收，不得排放入海。  
钻井所使用的油基泥浆和其他有毒复合泥浆不得排放入海。水基泥浆和无毒复合泥浆及钻屑的排放，应当符合国家有关规定。

**Article 67** Engineering construction projects may not illegally discharge pollutants, wastes, or other harmful substances into the sea.  
Oily wastewater and oily mixtures from offshore oil and gas drilling platforms (vessels), production and living platforms, production storage and offloading units, and other offshore oil and gas equipment shall be discharged after treatment to meet the standards; residual oil and waste oil shall be recovered and may not be discharged into the sea.  
Oil-based muds and other toxic composite muds used in drilling may not be discharged into the sea. The discharge of water-based muds, non-toxic composite muds, and drill cuttings shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　海洋油气钻井平台（船）、生产生活平台、生产储卸装置等海洋油气装备及其有关海上设施，不得向海域处置含油的工业固体废物。处置其他固体废物，不得造成海洋环境污染。

**Article 68** Offshore oil and gas drilling platforms (vessels), production and living platforms, production storage and offloading units, and other offshore oil and gas equipment and related offshore facilities may not dispose of oily industrial solid waste into the sea area. The disposal of other solid waste shall not cause marine environmental pollution.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　海上试油时，应当确保油气充分燃烧，油和油性混合物不得排放入海。

**Article 69** During offshore well testing, it shall be ensured that the oil and gas are fully combusted, and oil and oily mixtures may not be discharged into the sea.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　勘探开发海洋油气资源，应当按照有关规定编制油气污染应急预案，报国务院生态环境主管部门海域派出机构备案。

**Article 70** For the exploration and development of offshore oil and gas resources, an oil and gas pollution emergency response plan shall be prepared in accordance with relevant provisions and submitted to the sea area agency dispatched by the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council for recordation.

### 第六章　废弃物倾倒污染防治

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　任何个人和未经批准的单位，不得向中华人民共和国管辖海域倾倒任何废弃物。  
需要倾倒废弃物的，产生废弃物的单位应当向国务院生态环境主管部门海域派出机构提出书面申请，并出具废弃物特性和成分检验报告，取得倾倒许可证后，方可倾倒。  
国家鼓励疏浚物等废弃物的综合利用，避免或者减少海洋倾倒。  
禁止中华人民共和国境外的废弃物在中华人民共和国管辖海域倾倒。

**Article 71** No individual or unit without approval may dump any waste into the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.  
Where the dumping of waste is necessary, the unit generating the waste shall submit a written application to the sea area agency dispatched by the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council, and provide a test report on the characteristics and composition of the waste. Only after obtaining a dumping permit may dumping be carried out.  
The State encourages the comprehensive utilization of wastes such as dredged materials to avoid or reduce ocean dumping.  
It is prohibited to dump wastes from outside the territory of the People's Republic of China into the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　国务院生态环境主管部门根据废弃物的毒性、有毒物质含量和对海洋环境影响程度，制定海洋倾倒废弃物评价程序和标准。  
可以向海洋倾倒的废弃物名录，由国务院生态环境主管部门制定。

**Article 72** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall formulate procedures and standards for evaluating wastes to be dumped into the sea based on the toxicity, content of toxic substances, and degree of impact on the marine environment.  
The list of wastes that may be dumped into the sea shall be formulated by the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　国务院生态环境主管部门会同国务院自然资源主管部门编制全国海洋倾倒区规划，并征求国务院交通运输、渔业等部门和海警机构的意见，报国务院批准。  
国务院生态环境主管部门根据全国海洋倾倒区规划，按照科学、合理、经济、安全的原则及时选划海洋倾倒区，征求国务院交通运输、渔业等部门和海警机构的意见，并向社会公告。

**Article 73** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall, jointly with the competent department of natural resources of the State Council, prepare a national plan for ocean dumping areas, and shall solicit opinions from the departments of transport, fisheries, and other relevant departments of the State Council and the coast guard agency, and submit it to the State Council for approval.  
The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall, in accordance with the national plan for ocean dumping areas, promptly designate ocean dumping areas on the principles of scientific, reasonable, economical, and safe, solicit opinions from the departments of transport, fisheries, and other relevant departments of the State Council and the coast guard agency, and make a public announcement.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　国务院生态环境主管部门组织开展海洋倾倒区使用状况评估，根据评估结果予以调整、暂停使用或者封闭海洋倾倒区。  
海洋倾倒区的调整、暂停使用和封闭情况，应当通报国务院有关部门、海警机构并向社会公布。

**Article 74** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall organize and carry out assessments of the use status of ocean dumping areas, and shall adjust, suspend use, or close the ocean dumping areas based on the assessment results.  
The adjustment, suspension of use, and closure of ocean dumping areas shall be notified to the relevant departments of the State Council and the coast guard agency, and made public.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　获准和实施倾倒废弃物的单位，应当按照许可证注明的期限及条件，到指定的区域进行倾倒。倾倒作业船舶等载运工具应当安装使用符合要求的海洋倾倒在线监控设备，并与国务院生态环境主管部门监管系统联网。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/中华人民共和国防治海岸工程建设项目污染损害海洋环境管理条例.md#art-26" title="被《中华人民共和国防治海岸工程建设项目污染损害海洋环境管理条例》第26条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 75** The units that have obtained approval for and carry out the dumping of waste shall conduct dumping in the designated area within the time limit and under the conditions specified in the permit. Vessels and other means of transport for dumping operations shall install and use online monitoring equipment for ocean dumping that meets the requirements, and be connected to the supervision system of the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　获准和实施倾倒废弃物的单位，应当按照规定向颁发许可证的国务院生态环境主管部门海域派出机构报告倾倒情况。倾倒废弃物的船舶应当向驶出港的海事管理机构、海警机构作出报告。

**Article 76** The units that have obtained approval for and carry out the dumping of waste shall report the dumping situation to the sea area agency of the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council that issued the permit, in accordance with the provisions. The vessel dumping waste shall report to the maritime administration agency and the coast guard agency of the port of departure.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　禁止在海上焚烧废弃物。  
禁止在海上处置污染海洋环境、破坏海洋生态的放射性废物或者其他放射性物质。

**Article 77** It is prohibited to incinerate waste at sea.  
It is prohibited to dispose of radioactive waste or other radioactive substances that pollute the marine environment or damage the marine ecology at sea.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　获准倾倒废弃物的单位委托实施废弃物海洋倾倒作业的，应当对受托单位的主体资格、技术能力和信用状况进行核实，依法签订书面合同，在合同中约定污染防治与生态保护要求，并监督实施。  
受托单位实施废弃物海洋倾倒作业，应当依照有关法律法规的规定和合同约定，履行污染防治和生态保护要求。  
获准倾倒废弃物的单位违反本条第一款规定的，除依照有关法律法规的规定予以处罚外，还应当与造成环境污染、生态破坏的受托单位承担连带责任。

**Article 78** Where a unit that has obtained approval for dumping waste entrusts another unit to carry out ocean dumping operations, it shall verify the entrusted unit's subject qualifications, technical capability, and credit standing, sign a written contract according to law, stipulate in the contract the requirements for pollution prevention and ecological protection, and supervise the implementation.  
The entrusted unit carrying out ocean dumping operations shall fulfill the requirements for pollution prevention and ecological protection in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations and the contract.  
Where a unit that has obtained approval for dumping waste violates the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article, in addition to being punished in accordance with relevant laws and regulations, it shall also bear joint and several liability with the entrusted unit that caused environmental pollution or ecological damage.

### 第七章　船舶及有关作业活动污染防治

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　在中华人民共和国管辖海域，任何船舶及相关作业不得违法向海洋排放船舶垃圾、生活污水、含油污水、含有毒有害物质污水、废气等污染物，废弃物，压载水和沉积物及其他有害物质。  
船舶应当按照国家有关规定采取有效措施，对压载水和沉积物进行处理处置，严格防控引入外来有害生物。  
从事船舶污染物、废弃物接收和船舶清舱、洗舱作业活动的，应当具备相应的接收处理能力。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/中华人民共和国防治海岸工程建设项目污染损害海洋环境管理条例.md#art-25" title="被《中华人民共和国防治海岸工程建设项目污染损害海洋环境管理条例》第25条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 79** Within the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, no vessel or related operation may illegally discharge into the sea pollutants such as ship garbage, domestic sewage, oily wastewater, wastewater containing toxic and hazardous substances, exhaust gas, wastes, ballast water and sediment, and other harmful substances.  
Vessels shall take effective measures in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State to treat and dispose of ballast water and sediment, and strictly prevent the introduction of harmful alien organisms.  
Those engaged in the reception of ship pollutants and wastes, and in ship tank cleaning and hold cleaning operations, shall possess the corresponding reception and treatment capacity.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　船舶应当配备相应的防污设备和器材。  
船舶的结构、配备的防污设备和器材应当符合国家防治船舶污染海洋环境的有关规定，并经检验合格。  
船舶应当取得并持有防治海洋环境污染的证书与文书，在进行涉及船舶污染物、压载水和沉积物排放及操作时，应当按照有关规定监测、监控，如实记录并保存。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/中华人民共和国防治海岸工程建设项目污染损害海洋环境管理条例.md#art-27" title="被《中华人民共和国防治海岸工程建设项目污染损害海洋环境管理条例》第27条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 80** Vessels shall be equipped with corresponding anti-pollution equipment and devices.  
The structure of the vessel and the anti-pollution equipment and devices with which it is equipped shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State on the prevention of pollution of the marine environment by vessels, and shall be inspected and found to be satisfactory.  
Vessels shall obtain and hold certificates and documents for the prevention of marine environmental pollution, and when conducting operations involving the discharge of ship pollutants, ballast water, and sediment, shall monitor and control in accordance with relevant provisions, and truthfully record and retain records.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　船舶应当遵守海上交通安全法律、法规的规定，防止因碰撞、触礁、搁浅、火灾或者爆炸等引起的海难事故，造成海洋环境的污染。

**Article 81** Ships shall comply with the provisions of laws and regulations on maritime traffic safety to prevent marine pollution caused by marine perils such as collision, striking a reef, grounding, fire or explosion.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　国家完善并实施船舶油污损害民事赔偿责任制度；按照船舶油污损害赔偿责任由船东和货主共同承担风险的原则，完善并实施船舶油污保险、油污损害赔偿基金制度，具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 82** The State shall improve and implement the civil liability system for compensation for damage caused by oil pollution from ships; in accordance with the principle that the shipowner and cargo owner shall jointly bear the risk of liability for compensation for oil pollution damage from ships, the State shall improve and implement the system of oil pollution insurance and oil pollution damage compensation fund. Specific measures shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　载运具有污染危害性货物进出港口的船舶，其承运人、货物所有人或者代理人，应当事先向海事管理机构申报。经批准后，方可进出港口或者装卸作业。

**Article 83** For a ship carrying pollutive hazardous cargo entering or leaving a port, the carrier, cargo owner or agent shall declare to the maritime administration in advance. Only after approval may the ship enter or leave the port or conduct loading and unloading operations.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　交付船舶载运污染危害性货物的，托运人应当将货物的正式名称、污染危害性以及应当采取的防护措施如实告知承运人。污染危害性货物的单证、包装、标志、数量限制等，应当符合对所交付货物的有关规定。  
需要船舶载运污染危害性不明的货物，应当按照有关规定事先进行评估。  
装卸油类及有毒有害货物的作业，船岸双方应当遵守安全防污操作规程。

**Article 84** Where a cargo of pollutive hazardous nature is delivered to be carried by ship, the shipper shall truthfully inform the carrier of the formal name of the cargo, its pollutive hazardous nature and the protective measures to be taken. The documents, packaging, marking, quantity restrictions, etc., of pollutive hazardous cargo shall comply with the relevant provisions for the delivered cargo.  
For cargo whose pollutive hazardous nature is unknown that needs to be carried by ship, an assessment shall be conducted in advance in accordance with relevant provisions.  
For operations involving the loading and unloading of oil and toxic and hazardous cargoes, both the ship and shore parties shall comply with the safe anti-pollution operating procedures.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　港口、码头、装卸站和船舶修造拆解单位所在地县级以上地方人民政府应当统筹规划建设船舶污染物等的接收、转运、处理处置设施，建立相应的接收、转运、处理处置多部门联合监管制度。  
沿海县级以上地方人民政府负责对其管理海域的渔港和渔业船舶停泊点及周边区域污染防治的监督管理，规范生产生活污水和渔业垃圾回收处置，推进污染防治设备建设和环境清理整治。  
港口、码头、装卸站和船舶修造拆解单位应当按照有关规定配备足够的用于处理船舶污染物、废弃物的接收设施，使该设施处于良好状态并有效运行。  
装卸油类等污染危害性货物的港口、码头、装卸站和船舶应当编制污染应急预案，并配备相应的污染应急设备和器材。

**Article 85** The local people's governments at or above the county level of the places where ports, wharves, loading and unloading stations, and ship building, repair and dismantling units are located shall make overall plans for the construction of facilities for receiving, transferring, treating and disposing of ship pollutants, etc., and establish corresponding multi-department joint supervision systems for receiving, transferring, treating and disposing thereof.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall be responsible for the supervision and management of pollution prevention and control in fishing ports, fishing vessel berthing points and surrounding areas within the sea areas under their jurisdiction, regulate the recovery and disposal of domestic and production sewage and fishing garbage, and promote the construction of pollution prevention and control facilities and environmental clean-up and remediation.  
Ports, wharves, loading and unloading stations, and ship building, repair and dismantling units shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, be equipped with sufficient reception facilities for treating ship pollutants and wastes, and shall keep such facilities in good condition and in effective operation.  
Ports, wharves, loading and unloading stations, and ships that handle pollutive hazardous cargo such as oil shall prepare pollution emergency response plans and be equipped with corresponding pollution emergency response equipment and devices.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　国家海事管理机构组织制定中国籍船舶禁止或者限制安装和使用的有害材料名录。  
船舶修造单位或者船舶所有人、经营人或者管理人应当在船上备有有害材料清单，在船舶建造、营运和维修过程中持续更新，并在船舶拆解前提供给从事船舶拆解的单位。

**Article 86** The national maritime administration shall organize the formulation of a list of harmful materials prohibited or restricted for installation and use on ships of Chinese nationality.  
Ship building and repair units, or ship owners, operators or managers shall keep on board an inventory of hazardous materials, continuously update it during the construction, operation and repair of the ship, and provide it to the ship dismantling unit before the ship is dismantled.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　从事船舶拆解的单位，应当采取有效的污染防治措施，在船舶拆解前将船舶污染物减至最小量，对拆解产生的船舶污染物、废弃物和其他有害物质进行安全与环境无害化处置。拆解的船舶部件不得进入水体。  
禁止采取冲滩方式进行船舶拆解作业。

**Article 87** Units engaged in ship dismantling shall take effective pollution prevention and control measures, reduce ship pollutants to a minimum before ship dismantling, and conduct safe and environmentally sound disposal of ship pollutants, wastes and other harmful substances generated from dismantling. Dismantled ship parts shall not be discharged into water bodies.  
Ship dismantling operations by beaching are prohibited.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　国家倡导绿色低碳智能航运，鼓励船舶使用新能源或者清洁能源，淘汰高耗能高排放老旧船舶，减少温室气体和大气污染物的排放。沿海县级以上地方人民政府应当制定港口岸电、船舶受电等设施建设和改造计划，并组织实施。港口岸电设施的供电能力应当与靠港船舶的用电需求相适应。  
船舶应当按照国家有关规定采取有效措施提高能效水平。具备岸电使用条件的船舶靠港应当按照国家有关规定使用岸电，但是使用清洁能源的除外。具备岸电供应能力的港口经营人、岸电供电企业应当按照国家有关规定为具备岸电使用条件的船舶提供岸电。  
国务院和沿海县级以上地方人民政府对港口岸电设施、船舶受电设施的改造和使用，清洁能源或者新能源动力船舶建造等按照规定给予支持。

**Article 88** The State advocates green, low-carbon and intelligent shipping, encourages ships to use new energy or clean energy, and phases out old ships with high energy consumption and high emissions to reduce emissions of greenhouse gases and air pollutants. The local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall formulate plans for the construction and renovation of port shore power and ship shore power reception facilities, and organize their implementation. The power supply capacity of port shore power facilities shall meet the power demand of ships calling at the port.  
Ships shall take effective measures in accordance with relevant State regulations to improve energy efficiency. Ships with shore power connection conditions that call at a port shall use shore power in accordance with relevant State regulations, except for ships using clean energy. Port operators and shore power supply enterprises with shore power supply capability shall provide shore power to ships with shore power connection conditions in accordance with relevant State regulations.  
The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall provide support for the renovation and use of port shore power facilities and ship shore power reception facilities, and the construction of ships powered by clean energy or new energy, in accordance with relevant regulations.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　船舶及有关作业活动应当遵守有关法律法规和标准，采取有效措施，防止造成海洋环境污染。海事管理机构等应当加强对船舶及有关作业活动的监督管理。  
船舶进行散装液体污染危害性货物的过驳作业，应当编制作业方案，采取有效的安全和污染防治措施，并事先按照有关规定报经批准。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/行政法规/中华人民共和国海洋倾废管理条例.md#art-15" title="被《中华人民共和国海洋倾废管理条例》第15条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup>[2]</sup>&thinsp;<sup>[3]</sup>

**Article 89** Ships and related operational activities shall comply with relevant laws, regulations and standards, and take effective measures to prevent marine environmental pollution. The maritime administration and other relevant authorities shall strengthen the supervision and administration of ships and related operational activities.  
Where a ship carries out lightering operations of bulk liquid pollutive hazardous cargo, it shall prepare an operation plan, take effective safety and pollution prevention measures, and report to and obtain approval from the relevant authority in advance in accordance with relevant regulations.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　船舶发生海难事故，造成或者可能造成海洋环境重大污染损害的，国家海事管理机构有权强制采取避免或者减少污染损害的措施。  
对在公海上因发生海难事故，造成中华人民共和国管辖海域重大污染损害后果或者具有污染威胁的船舶、海上设施，国家海事管理机构有权采取与实际的或者可能发生的损害相称的必要措施。

**Article 90** Where a ship experiences a marine peril that causes or is likely to cause major pollution damage to the marine environment, the national maritime administration shall have the power to compulsorily take measures to avoid or mitigate the pollution damage.  
With respect to ships or offshore installations on the high seas that, due to a marine peril, have caused major pollution damage consequences or pose a pollution threat to the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, the national maritime administration shall have the power to take necessary measures commensurate with the actual or possible damage.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　所有船舶均有监视海上污染的义务，在发现海上污染事件或者违反本法规定的行为时，应当立即向就近的依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门或者机构报告。  
民用航空器发现海上排污或者污染事件，应当及时向就近的民用航空空中交通管制单位报告。接到报告的单位，应当立即向依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门或者机构通报。

**Article 91** All ships have the obligation to monitor marine pollution. Upon discovering a marine pollution incident or any act violating the provisions of this Law, they shall immediately report to the nearest department or agency exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and administration in accordance with this Law.  
Where a civil aircraft discovers a marine discharge or pollution incident, it shall promptly report to the nearest civil aviation air traffic control unit. The unit that receives the report shall immediately notify the department or agency exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and administration in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　国务院交通运输主管部门可以划定船舶污染物排放控制区。进入控制区的船舶应当符合船舶污染物排放相关控制要求。

**Article 92** The competent transport department of the State Council may designate ship pollutant discharge control areas. Ships entering the control areas shall meet the relevant control requirements for ship pollutant discharge.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门或者机构责令改正或者责令采取限制生产、停产整治等措施，并处以罚款；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业、关闭：  
（一）向海域排放本法禁止排放的污染物或者其他物质的；  
（二）未依法取得排污许可证排放污染物的；  
（三）超过标准、总量控制指标排放污染物的；  
（四）通过私设暗管或者篡改、伪造监测数据，或者不正常运行污染防治设施等逃避监管的方式违法向海洋排放污染物的；  
（五）违反本法有关船舶压载水和沉积物排放和管理规定的；  
（六）其他未依照本法规定向海洋排放污染物、废弃物的。  
有前款第一项、第二项行为之一的，处二十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；有前款第三项行为的，处十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；有前款第四项行为的，处十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款，情节严重的，吊销排污许可证；有前款第五项、第六项行为之一的，处一万元以上二十万元以下的罚款。个人擅自在岸滩弃置、堆放和处理生活垃圾的，按次处一百元以上一千元以下的罚款。

**Article 93** Where any of the following acts is committed in violation of the provisions of this Law, the department or agency exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and administration in accordance with this Law shall order correction or order the adoption of measures such as restricting production or suspending production for remediation, and impose a fine; if the circumstances are serious, the matter shall be reported to the people's government with the approval authority for approval, and the violator shall be ordered to suspend business or close down:  
(1) discharging into the sea any pollutant or other substance the discharge of which is prohibited by this Law;  
(2) discharging pollutants without obtaining a pollutant discharge permit according to law;  
(3) discharging pollutants in excess of the standards or the total discharge control targets;  
(4) illegally discharging pollutants into the sea through evading supervision by means such as secretly setting up underground pipes, tampering with or falsifying monitoring data, or abnormal operation of pollution prevention and control facilities;  
(5) violating the provisions of this Law on the discharge and management of ship ballast water and sediment;  
(6) any other act of discharging pollutants or wastes into the sea not in accordance with the provisions of this Law.  
Where the act falls under Item (1) or (2) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 yuan but not more than RMB 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the act falls under Item (3) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the act falls under Item (4) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed, and if the circumstances are serious, the pollutant discharge permit shall be revoked; where the act falls under Item (5) or (6) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan shall be imposed. Where an individual arbitrarily dumps, piles or disposes of domestic waste on the shore or beach, a fine of not less than RMB 100 yuan but not more than RMB 1,000 yuan per occurrence shall be imposed.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门或者机构责令改正，处以罚款：  
（一）未依法公开排污信息或者弄虚作假的；  
（二）因发生事故或者其他突发性事件，造成或者可能造成海洋环境污染、生态破坏事件，未按照规定通报或者报告的；  
（三）未按照有关规定制定应急预案并备案，或者未按照有关规定配备应急设备、器材的；  
（四）因发生事故或者其他突发性事件，造成或者可能造成海洋环境污染、生态破坏事件，未立即采取有效措施或者逃逸的；  
（五）未采取必要应对措施，造成海洋生态灾害危害扩大的。  
有前款第一项行为的，处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款，拒不改正的，责令限制生产、停产整治；有前款第二项行为的，处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款，并可以暂扣或者吊销相关任职资格许可；有前款第三项行为的，处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；有前款第四项、第五项行为之一的，处二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 94** Where any of the following acts is committed in violation of the provisions of this Law, the department or agency exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and administration in accordance with this Law shall order correction and impose a fine:  
(1) failing to disclose pollutant discharge information according to law or practicing fraud;  
(2) in the event of an accident or other emergency that causes or is likely to cause marine environmental pollution or ecological damage, failing to notify or report in accordance with regulations;  
(3) failing to formulate and file an emergency response plan in accordance with relevant regulations, or failing to equip emergency response equipment and devices in accordance with relevant regulations;  
(4) in the event of an accident or other emergency that causes or is likely to cause marine environmental pollution or ecological damage, failing to immediately take effective measures or fleeing;  
(5) failing to take necessary response measures, resulting in the expansion of the harm of marine ecological disaster.  
Where the act falls under Item (1) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the party refuses to correct, an order shall be given to restrict production or suspend production for remediation. Where the act falls under Item (2) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons, and the relevant qualification permit may be temporarily seized or revoked. Where the act falls under Item (3) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan shall be imposed. Where the act falls under Item (4) or (5) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 yuan but not more than RMB 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　违反本法规定，拒绝、阻挠调查和现场检查，或者在被检查时弄虚作假的，由依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门或者机构责令改正，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 95** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a party refuses or obstructs investigation and on-site inspection, or practices fraud during the inspection, the department or agency exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and administration in accordance with this Law shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan; a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　违反本法规定，造成珊瑚礁等海洋生态系统或者自然保护地破坏的，由依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门或者机构责令改正、采取补救措施，处每平方米一千元以上一万元以下的罚款。

**Article 96** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, damage is caused to marine ecosystems such as coral reefs or to nature reserves, the department or agency exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and administration in accordance with this Law shall order correction and the adoption of remedial measures, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 1,000 yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 yuan per square meter.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门或者机构责令改正，处以罚款：  
（一）占用、损害自然岸线的；  
（二）在严格保护岸线范围内开采海砂的；  
（三）违反本法其他关于海砂、矿产资源规定的。  
有前款第一项行为的，处每米五百元以上一万元以下的罚款；有前款第二项行为的，处货值金额二倍以上二十倍以下的罚款，货值金额不足十万元的，处二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款；有前款第三项行为的，处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 97** Where any of the following acts is committed in violation of the provisions of this Law, the department or agency exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and administration in accordance with this Law shall order correction and impose a fine:  
(1) occupying or damaging natural shoreline;  
(2) mining sea sand within strictly protected shoreline areas;  
(3) violating other provisions of this Law concerning sea sand and mineral resources.  
Where the act falls under Item (1) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than RMB 500 yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 yuan per meter shall be imposed; where the act falls under Item (2) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than two times but not more than twenty times the value of the goods shall be imposed; if the value of the goods is less than RMB 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 yuan but not more than RMB 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the act falls under Item (3) of the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　违反本法规定，从事海水养殖活动有下列行为之一，由依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门或者机构责令改正，处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业、关闭：  
（一）违反禁养区、限养区规定的；  
（二）违反养殖规模、养殖密度规定的；  
（三）违反投饵、投肥、药物使用规定的；  
（四）未按照有关规定对养殖尾水自行监测的。

**Article 98** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, any of the following acts is committed in mariculture activities, the department or agency exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and administration in accordance with this Law shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the matter shall be reported to the people's government with the approval authority for approval, and the violator shall be ordered to suspend business or close down:  
(1) violating the provisions on prohibited culture areas or restricted culture areas;  
(2) violating the provisions on culture scale or culture density;  
(3) violating the provisions on baiting, fertilizing or the use of drugs;  
(4) failing to conduct self-monitoring of aquaculture tail water in accordance with relevant regulations.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　违反本法规定设置入海排污口的，由生态环境主管部门责令关闭或者拆除，处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款；拒不关闭或者拆除的，强制关闭、拆除，所需费用由违法者承担，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，可以责令停产整治。  
违反本法规定，设置入海排污口未备案的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款。  
违反本法规定，入海排污口的责任主体未按照规定开展监控、自动监测的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，可以责令停产整治。  
自然资源、渔业等部门和海事管理机构、海警机构、军队生态环境保护部门发现前三款违法行为之一的，应当通报生态环境主管部门。

**Article 99** Where a sewage outfall into the sea is installed in violation of the provisions of this Law, the competent ecological environment department shall order its closure or removal, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan; if the party refuses to close or remove it, compulsory closure or removal shall be carried out, the costs incurred shall be borne by the violator, and a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, an order may be given to suspend production for remediation.  
Where a sewage outfall into the sea is installed without filing in violation of the provisions of this Law, the competent ecological environment department shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan.  
Where the responsible party of a sewage outfall into the sea fails to carry out monitoring or automatic monitoring in accordance with relevant regulations in violation of the provisions of this Law, the competent ecological environment department shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan; if it refuses to correct, an order may be given to suspend production for remediation.  
Where the departments of natural resources, fisheries, and the maritime administration, coast guard agencies, and the military ecological and environmental protection departments discover any of the illegal acts as set forth in the preceding three paragraphs, they shall notify the competent ecological environment department.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　违反本法规定，经中华人民共和国管辖海域，转移危险废物的，由国家海事管理机构责令非法运输该危险废物的船舶退出中华人民共和国管辖海域，处五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 100** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, hazardous wastes are transferred through the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, the national maritime administration shall order the ship illegally transporting such hazardous wastes to withdraw from the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 yuan but not more than RMB 5,000,000 yuan.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　违反本法规定，建设单位未落实建设项目投资计划有关要求的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处二十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款。  
违反本法规定，建设单位未依法报批或者报请重新审核环境影响报告书（表），擅自开工建设的，由生态环境主管部门或者海警机构责令其停止建设，根据违法情节和危害后果，处建设项目总投资额百分之一以上百分之五以下的罚款，并可以责令恢复原状；对建设单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。建设单位未依法备案环境影响登记表的，由生态环境主管部门责令备案，处五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 101** Where a construction entity, in violation of this Law, fails to fulfill the relevant requirements of the construction project investment plan, the competent department of ecological and environmental protection shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if it refuses to rectify, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where a construction entity, in violation of this Law, commences construction without submitting a report or applying for re-examination of an environmental impact report (statement) according to law, the competent department of ecological and environmental protection or the maritime police agency shall order it to suspend construction, impose a fine of not less than one percent but not more than five percent of the total investment amount of the construction project based on the circumstances of the violation and the consequences of the hazard, and may order restoration to the original condition; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons of the construction entity shall be given sanctions according to law. Where a construction entity fails to record an environmental impact registration form according to law, the competent department of ecological and environmental protection shall order it to make the record and impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　违反本法规定，在依法划定的自然保护地、重要渔业水域及其他需要特别保护的区域建设污染环境、破坏生态的工程建设项目或者从事其他活动，或者在沿海陆域新建不符合国家产业政策的生产项目的，由县级以上人民政府按照管理权限责令关闭。  
违反生态环境准入清单进行生产建设活动的，由依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门或者机构责令停止违法行为，限期拆除并恢复原状，所需费用由违法者承担，处五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令关闭。

**Article 102** Where a person, in violation of this Law, constructs an engineering project that pollutes the environment or damages the ecology, or engages in other activities in legally designated nature reserves, important fishery waters, or other areas requiring special protection, or constructs new production projects not conforming to national industrial policies in coastal land areas, the people's government at or above the county level shall, according to its management authority, order closure.  
Where a person conducts production or construction activities in violation of the ecological and environmental access list, the department or agency exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and management according to this Law shall order cessation of the illegal act, dismantling within a time limit, and restoration to the original condition; the expenses thus incurred shall be borne by the violator; a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons; if the circumstances are serious, upon approval by the people's government with the authority to approve, closure shall be ordered.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　违反本法规定，环境保护设施未与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投产使用的，或者环境保护设施未建成、未达到规定要求、未经验收或者经验收不合格即投入生产、使用的，由生态环境主管部门或者海警机构责令改正，处二十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处一百万元以上二百万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他责任人员处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；造成重大环境污染、生态破坏的，责令其停止生产、使用，或者报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令关闭。

**Article 103** Where a person, in violation of this Law, fails to have environmental protection facilities designed, constructed, and put into operation simultaneously with the main project, or puts the project into production or use when the environmental protection facilities are not completed, do not meet the prescribed requirements, have not been accepted, or have been accepted as unqualified, the competent department of ecological and environmental protection or the maritime police agency shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if it refuses to rectify, a fine of not less than 1,000,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible persons in charge and other liable persons; if serious environmental pollution or ecological damage is caused, the production or use shall be ordered to stop, or upon approval by the people's government with the authority to approve, closure shall be ordered.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　违反本法规定，工程建设项目有下列行为之一，由依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门或者机构责令其停止违法行为、消除危害，处二十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业、关闭：  
（一）使用含超标准放射性物质或者易溶出有毒有害物质的材料的；  
（二）造成领海基点及其周围环境的侵蚀、淤积、损害，或者危及领海基点稳定的。

**Article 104** Where an engineering construction project, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the department or agency exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and management according to this Law shall order cessation of the illegal act and elimination of the hazard, and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, upon approval by the people's government with the authority to approve, suspension of business or closure shall be ordered:  
(1) Using materials containing radioactive substances exceeding the standard or readily leachable toxic or hazardous substances;  
(2) Causing erosion, silting, or damage to the baseline points of the territorial sea or their surrounding environment, or endangering the stability of such baseline points.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　违反本法规定进行海洋油气勘探开发活动，造成海洋环境污染的，由海警机构责令改正，给予警告，并处二十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 105** Where a person, in violation of this Law, engages in offshore oil and gas exploration and development activities, causing marine environmental pollution, the maritime police agency shall order rectification, issue a warning, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由国务院生态环境主管部门及其海域派出机构、海事管理机构或者海警机构责令改正，处以罚款，必要时可以扣押船舶；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业、关闭：  
（一）倾倒废弃物的船舶驶出港口未报告的；  
（二）未取得倾倒许可证，向海洋倾倒废弃物的；  
（三）在海上焚烧废弃物或者处置放射性废物及其他放射性物质的。  
有前款第一项行为的，对违法船舶的所有人、经营人或者管理人处三千元以上三万元以下的罚款，对船长、责任船员或者其他责任人员处五百元以上五千元以下的罚款；有前款第二项行为的，处二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款；有前款第三项行为的，处五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款。有前款第二项、第三项行为之一，两年内受到行政处罚三次以上的，三年内不得从事废弃物海洋倾倒活动。

**Article 106** Where a person, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the competent department of ecological and environmental protection of the State Council and its sea area offices, the maritime administration, or the maritime police agency shall order rectification, impose a fine, and may detain the vessel when necessary; if the circumstances are serious, upon approval by the people's government with the authority to approve, suspension of business or closure shall be ordered:  
(1) A vessel dumping waste sails out of port without reporting;  
(2) Dumping waste into the sea without obtaining a dumping permit;  
(3) Incinerating waste at sea or disposing of radioactive waste or other radioactive substances.  
Where the act under item (1) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan shall be imposed on the owner, operator, or manager of the illegal vessel, and a fine of not less than 500 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed on the master, responsible crew members, or other responsible persons; where the act under item (2) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the act under item (3) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan shall be imposed. Where a person has received an administrative penalty three times or more within two years for committing any act under item (2) or (3) of the preceding paragraph, that person shall not engage in marine dumping activities for three years.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由国务院生态环境主管部门及其海域派出机构、海事管理机构或者海警机构责令改正，处以罚款，暂扣或者吊销倾倒许可证，必要时可以扣押船舶；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业、关闭：  
（一）未按照国家规定报告倾倒情况的；  
（二）未按照国家规定安装使用海洋倾废在线监控设备的；  
（三）获准倾倒废弃物的单位未依照本法规定委托实施废弃物海洋倾倒作业或者未依照本法规定监督实施的；  
（四）未按照倾倒许可证的规定倾倒废弃物的。  
有前款第一项行为的，按次处五千元以上二万元以下的罚款；有前款第二项行为的，处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；有前款第三项行为的，处三万元以上三十万元以下的罚款；有前款第四项行为的，处二十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款，被吊销倾倒许可证的，三年内不得从事废弃物海洋倾倒活动。  
以提供虚假申请材料、欺骗、贿赂等不正当手段申请取得倾倒许可证的，由国务院生态环境主管部门及其海域派出机构依法撤销倾倒许可证，并处二十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；三年内不得再次申请倾倒许可证。

**Article 107** Where a person, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the competent department of ecological and environmental protection of the State Council and its sea area offices, the maritime administration, or the maritime police agency shall order rectification, impose a fine, suspend or revoke the dumping permit, and may detain the vessel when necessary; if the circumstances are serious, upon approval by the people's government with the authority to approve, suspension of business or closure shall be ordered:  
(1) Failing to report the dumping situation as required by the State;  
(2) Failing to install and use online monitoring equipment for marine dumping as required by the State;  
(3) A unit approved to dump waste failing to entrust the marine dumping operation or failing to supervise the implementation according to this Law;  
(4) Dumping waste not in accordance with the provisions of the dumping permit.  
Where the act under item (1) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed each time; where the act under item (2) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the act under item (3) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the act under item (4) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed, and if the dumping permit is revoked, the person shall not engage in marine dumping activities for three years.  
Where a person obtains a dumping permit by providing false application materials, fraud, bribery, or other improper means, the competent department of ecological and environmental protection of the State Council and its sea area offices shall revoke the dumping permit according to law and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; the person shall not apply for a dumping permit again within three years.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　违反本法规定，将中华人民共和国境外废弃物运进中华人民共和国管辖海域倾倒的，由海警机构责令改正，根据造成或者可能造成的危害后果，处五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 108** Where a person, in violation of this Law, transports waste from outside the People's Republic of China into the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China for dumping, the maritime police agency shall order rectification and, based on the hazardous consequences caused or likely to be caused, impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门或者机构责令改正，处以罚款：  
（一）港口、码头、装卸站、船舶修造拆解单位未按照规定配备或者有效运行船舶污染物、废弃物接收设施，或者船舶的结构、配备的防污设备和器材不符合国家防污规定或者未经检验合格的；  
（二）从事船舶污染物、废弃物接收和船舶清舱、洗舱作业活动，不具备相应接收处理能力的；  
（三）从事船舶拆解、旧船改装、打捞和其他水上、水下施工作业，造成海洋环境污染损害的；  
（四）采取冲滩方式进行船舶拆解作业的。  
有前款第一项、第二项行为之一的，处二万元以上三十万元以下的罚款；有前款第三项行为的，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；有前款第四项行为的，处十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 109** Where a person, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the department or agency exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and management according to this Law shall order rectification and impose a fine:  
(1) A port, dock, loading and unloading station, or shipbuilding, repair, or scrapping unit fails to be equipped with or effectively operate facilities for receiving pollutants and waste from ships as required, or the structure of a ship or its anti-pollution equipment and apparatus do not conform to national anti-pollution requirements or have not passed inspection;  
(2) A person engaged in the reception of ship pollutants and waste or in ship tank cleaning and hold washing operations does not have the corresponding reception and treatment capacity;  
(3) A person engaged in ship scrapping, old ship conversion, salvage, or other above-water or underwater construction operations causes marine environmental pollution damage;  
(4) A person uses the beaching method for ship scrapping operations.  
Where the act under item (1) or (2) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the act under item (3) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the act under item (4) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门或者机构责令改正，处以罚款：  
（一）未在船上备有有害材料清单，未在船舶建造、营运和维修过程中持续更新有害材料清单，或者未在船舶拆解前将有害材料清单提供给从事船舶拆解单位的；  
（二）船舶未持有防污证书、防污文书，或者不按照规定监测、监控，如实记载和保存船舶污染物、压载水和沉积物的排放及操作记录的；  
（三）船舶采取措施提高能效水平未达到有关规定的；  
（四）进入控制区的船舶不符合船舶污染物排放相关控制要求的；  
（五）具备岸电供应能力的港口经营人、岸电供电企业未按照国家规定为具备岸电使用条件的船舶提供岸电的；  
（六）具备岸电使用条件的船舶靠港，不按照国家规定使用岸电的。  
有前款第一项行为的，处二万元以下的罚款；有前款第二项行为的，处十万元以下的罚款；有前款第三项行为的，处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款；有前款第四项行为的，处三万元以上三十万元以下的罚款；有前款第五项、第六项行为之一的，处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款，情节严重的，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 110** Where a person, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the department or agency exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and management according to this Law shall order rectification and impose a fine:  
(1) Failing to have a hazardous materials inventory on board, failing to continuously update the hazardous materials inventory during ship construction, operation, and maintenance, or failing to provide the hazardous materials inventory to the ship scrapping unit before the ship is scrapped;  
(2) A ship failing to hold anti-pollution certificates or anti-pollution documents, or failing to monitor and control in accordance with regulations, or failing to truthfully record and keep records of the discharge and operation of ship pollutants, ballast water, and sediment;  
(3) A ship failing to meet the relevant requirements for measures taken to improve energy efficiency;  
(4) A ship entering a control area failing to comply with the relevant control requirements for ship pollutant discharge;  
(5) A port operator or shore power supply enterprise with shore power supply capacity failing to provide shore power in accordance with State regulations to ships with the conditions for using shore power;  
(6) A ship with the conditions for using shore power, when calling at a port, failing to use shore power in accordance with State regulations.  
Where the act under item (1) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the act under item (2) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the act under item (3) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the act under item (4) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the act under item (5) or (6) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed, and if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门或者机构责令改正，处以罚款：  
（一）拒报或者谎报船舶载运污染危害性货物申报事项的；  
（二）托运人未将托运的污染危害性货物的正式名称、污染危害性以及应当采取的防护措施如实告知承运人的；  
（三）托运人交付承运人的污染危害性货物的单证、包装、标志、数量限制不符合对所交付货物的有关规定的；  
（四）托运人在托运的普通货物中夹带污染危害性货物或者将污染危害性货物谎报为普通货物的；  
（五）需要船舶载运污染危害性不明的货物，未按照有关规定事先进行评估的。  
有前款第一项行为的，处五万元以下的罚款；有前款第二项行为的，处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款；有前款第三项、第五项行为之一的，处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款；有前款第四项行为的，处十万元以上二十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 111** Where a person, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the department or agency exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and management according to this Law shall order rectification and impose a fine:  
(1) Refusing to report or falsely reporting the declaration matters for ships carrying polluting or hazardous cargo;  
(2) The shipper failing to truthfully inform the carrier of the formal name, polluting or hazardous nature, and protective measures to be taken for the polluting or hazardous cargo shipped;  
(3) The documents, packaging, marks, and quantity restrictions of the polluting or hazardous cargo delivered by the shipper to the carrier not conforming to the relevant provisions concerning the cargo delivered;  
(4) The shipper mixing polluting or hazardous cargo into ordinary cargo shipped or falsely declaring polluting or hazardous cargo as ordinary cargo;  
(5) Failing to conduct a prior assessment in accordance with relevant provisions for cargo with unknown polluting or hazardous nature that needs to be carried by ship.  
Where the act under item (1) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the act under item (2) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the act under item (3) or (5) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the act under item (4) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门或者机构责令改正，处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款：  
（一）载运具有污染危害性货物的船舶未经许可进出港口或者装卸作业的；  
（二）装卸油类及有毒有害货物的作业，船岸双方未遵守安全防污操作规程的；  
（三）船舶进行散装液体污染危害性货物的过驳作业，未编制作业方案或者未按照有关规定报经批准的。

**Article 112** Where a person, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the department or agency exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and management according to this Law shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan:  
(1) A ship carrying polluting or hazardous cargo entering or leaving a port, or conducting loading and unloading operations, without permission;  
(2) During operations involving the loading and unloading of oil and toxic or hazardous goods, the ship and shore parties fail to comply with safety and anti-pollution operating procedures;  
(3) A ship conducting lightering operations of bulk liquid polluting or hazardous cargo fails to prepare an operation plan or fails to obtain approval in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　企业事业单位和其他生产经营者违反本法规定向海域排放、倾倒、处置污染物、废弃物或者其他物质，受到罚款处罚，被责令改正的，依法作出处罚决定的部门或者机构应当组织复查，发现其继续实施该违法行为或者拒绝、阻挠复查的，依照《中华人民共和国环境保护法》的规定按日连续处罚。

**Article 113** Where an enterprise, public institution, or other producer or business operator, in violation of this Law, discharges, dumps, or disposes of pollutants, waste, or other substances into the sea, and has been fined and ordered to rectify, the department or agency that made the penalty decision according to law shall organize a re-inspection, and if it is found that the illegal act continues or that the person refuses or obstructs the re-inspection, a daily cumulative penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Environmental Protection Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　对污染海洋环境、破坏海洋生态，造成他人损害的，依照《中华人民共和国民法典》等法律的规定承担民事责任。  
对污染海洋环境、破坏海洋生态，给国家造成重大损失的，由依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门代表国家对责任者提出损害赔偿要求。  
前款规定的部门不提起诉讼的，人民检察院可以向人民法院提起诉讼。前款规定的部门提起诉讼的，人民检察院可以支持起诉。

**Article 114** Where a person pollutes the marine environment or damages the marine ecology, causing harm to others, civil liability shall be borne in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and other laws.  
Where a person pollutes the marine environment or damages the marine ecology, causing significant losses to the State, the department exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and management according to this Law shall, on behalf of the State, claim compensation for damages from the responsible person.  
Where the department specified in the preceding paragraph does not initiate a lawsuit, the People's Procuratorate may initiate a lawsuit in the People's Court. Where the department specified in the preceding paragraph initiates a lawsuit, the People's Procuratorate may support the prosecution.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　对违反本法规定，造成海洋环境污染、生态破坏事故的单位，除依法承担赔偿责任外，由依照本法规定行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门或者机构处以罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员可以处上一年度从本单位取得收入百分之五十以下的罚款；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员属于公职人员的，依法给予处分。  
对造成一般或者较大海洋环境污染、生态破坏事故的，按照直接损失的百分之二十计算罚款；对造成重大或者特大海洋环境污染、生态破坏事故的，按照直接损失的百分之三十计算罚款。

**Article 115** Where a unit, in violation of this Law, causes an accident of marine environmental pollution or ecological damage, in addition to bearing compensation liability according to law, a fine shall be imposed by the department or agency exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and management according to this Law; a fine of not more than 50 percent of the income obtained from the unit in the previous year may be imposed on the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons; if the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons are public officials, sanctions shall be imposed according to law.  
For an accident causing ordinary or relatively major marine environmental pollution or ecological damage, a fine shall be calculated at 20 percent of the direct losses; for an accident causing major or extraordinarily major marine environmental pollution or ecological damage, a fine shall be calculated at 30 percent of the direct losses.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　完全属于下列情形之一，经过及时采取合理措施，仍然不能避免对海洋环境造成污染损害的，造成污染损害的有关责任者免予承担责任：  
（一）战争；  
（二）不可抗拒的自然灾害；  
（三）负责灯塔或者其他助航设备的主管部门，在执行职责时的疏忽，或者其他过失行为。

**Article 116** Where a pollution damage to the marine environment cannot be avoided despite timely adoption of reasonable measures, and is wholly attributable to any of the following circumstances, the responsible person causing the pollution damage shall be exempted from liability:  
(1) War;  
(2) Irresistible natural disasters;  
(3) Negligence or other wrongful acts of the competent authority responsible for lighthouses or other navigational aids in the performance of its duties.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　未依照本法规定缴纳倾倒费的，由国务院生态环境主管部门及其海域派出机构责令限期缴纳；逾期拒不缴纳的，处应缴纳倾倒费数额一倍以上三倍以下的罚款，并可以报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业、关闭。

**Article 117** Where a person fails to pay dumping fees in accordance with this Law, the competent department of ecological and environmental protection of the State Council and its sea area offices shall order payment within a time limit; if the person refuses to pay after the expiration of the time limit, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the amount of dumping fees payable shall be imposed, and upon approval by the people's government with the authority to approve, suspension of business or closure may be ordered.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　海洋环境监督管理人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，造成海洋环境污染损害、生态破坏的，依法给予处分。

**Article 118** Where a marine environmental supervision and management official abuses his or her power, neglects his or her duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, causing marine environmental pollution damage or ecological damage, sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 119** Where a violation of this Law constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　本法中下列用语的含义是：  
（一）海洋环境污染损害，是指直接或者间接地把物质或者能量引入海洋环境，产生损害海洋生物资源、危害人体健康、妨害渔业和海上其他合法活动、损害海水使用素质和减损环境质量等有害影响。  
（二）内水，是指我国领海基线向内陆一侧的所有海域。  
（三）沿海陆域，是指与海岸相连，或者通过管道、沟渠、设施，直接或者间接向海洋排放污染物及其相关活动的一带区域。  
（四）滨海湿地，是指低潮时水深不超过六米的水域及其沿岸浸湿地带，包括水深不超过六米的永久性水域、潮间带（或者洪泛地带）和沿海低地等，但是用于养殖的人工的水域和滩涂除外。  
（五）陆地污染源（简称陆源），是指从陆地向海域排放污染物，造成或者可能造成海洋环境污染的场所、设施等。  
（六）陆源污染物，是指由陆地污染源排放的污染物。  
（七）倾倒，是指通过船舶、航空器、平台或者其他载运工具，向海洋处置废弃物和其他有害物质的行为，包括弃置船舶、航空器、平台及其辅助设施和其他浮动工具的行为。  
（八）海岸线，是指多年大潮平均高潮位时海陆分界痕迹线，以国家组织开展的海岸线修测结果为准。  
（九）入海河口，是指河流终端与受水体（海）相结合的地段。  
（十）海洋生态灾害，是指受自然环境变化或者人为因素影响，导致一种或者多种海洋生物暴发性增殖或者高度聚集，对海洋生态系统结构和功能造成损害。  
（十一）渔业水域，是指鱼虾蟹贝类的产卵场、索饵场、越冬场、洄游通道和鱼虾蟹贝藻类及其他水生动植物的养殖场。  
（十二）排放，是指把污染物排入海洋的行为，包括泵出、溢出、泄出、喷出和倒出。  
（十三）油类，是指任何类型的油及其炼制品。  
（十四）入海排污口，是指直接或者通过管道、沟、渠等排污通道向海洋环境水体排放污水的口门，包括工业排污口、城镇污水处理厂排污口、农业排口及其他排口等类型。  
（十五）油性混合物，是指任何含有油份的混合物。  
（十六）海上焚烧，是指以热摧毁为目的，在海上焚烧设施上，故意焚烧废弃物或者其他物质的行为，但是船舶、平台或者其他人工构造物正常操作中所附带发生的行为除外。

**Article 120** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) "Marine environmental pollution damage" means the direct or indirect introduction of substances or energy into the marine environment, resulting in harmful effects such as damage to marine biological resources, hazards to human health, hindrance to fishing and other legitimate activities at sea, impairment of seawater quality for use, and degradation of environmental quality.  
(2) "Internal waters" means all sea areas on the landward side of the baseline of the territorial sea of China.  
(3) "Coastal land areas" means the area adjacent to the coast, or an area where pollutants are discharged directly or indirectly into the sea through pipelines, ditches, or facilities and related activities are conducted.  
(4) "Coastal wetlands" means waters with a depth of not more than six meters at low tide and the adjacent saturated areas along the coast, including permanent waters with a depth of not more than six meters, intertidal zones (or floodplains), and low-lying coastal areas, excluding artificial waters and tidal flats used for aquaculture.  
(5) "Land-based pollution sources" (hereinafter referred to as "land-based sources") means places, facilities, etc., from which pollutants are discharged from land to the sea, causing or likely to cause marine environmental pollution.  
(6) "Land-based pollutants" means pollutants discharged from land-based pollution sources.  
(7) "Dumping" means the disposal of waste and other harmful substances into the sea by means of vessels, aircraft, platforms, or other means of transport, including the abandonment of vessels, aircraft, platforms and their auxiliary facilities, and other floating devices.  
(8) "Coastline" means the trace line of the boundary between land and sea at the average high tide level of spring tides over many years, which shall be based on the results of coastline revision organized by the State.  
(9) "Estuary" means the area where a river terminates and joins the receiving water body (the sea).  
(10) "Marine ecological disaster" means a phenomenon where, due to changes in the natural environment or human factors, one or more marine organisms proliferate explosively or aggregate in high density, causing damage to the structure and function of the marine ecosystem.  
(11) "Fishery waters" means the spawning grounds, feeding grounds, wintering grounds, migration channels of fish, shrimp, crabs, and shellfish, and the breeding grounds of fish, shrimp, crabs, shellfish, algae, and other aquatic animals and plants.  
(12) "Discharge" means the act of discharging pollutants into the sea, including pumping, spilling, leaking, spraying, and pouring.  
(13) "Oil" means any type of oil and its refined products.  
(14) "Sea outfall" means an outfall that discharges sewage directly or through discharge channels such as pipelines, ditches, or canals into the marine environment water body, including types such as industrial outfalls, urban sewage treatment plant outfalls, agricultural outfalls, and other outfalls.  
(15) "Oily mixture" means any mixture containing oil.  
(16) "Incineration at sea" means the deliberate combustion of waste or other substances on an incineration facility at sea for the purpose of thermal destruction, excluding incidental activities arising from the normal operation of vessels, platforms, or other artificial structures.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　涉及海洋环境监督管理的有关部门的具体职权划分，本法未作规定的，由国务院规定。  
沿海县级以上地方人民政府行使海洋环境监督管理权的部门的职责，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据本法及国务院有关规定确定。

**Article 121** Where the specific division of functions and powers among relevant departments involved in marine environmental supervision and administration is not provided for in this Law, it shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
The duties of the departments exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and administration of the local people's governments at or above the county level along the coast shall be determined by the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government in accordance with this Law and the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　军事船舶和军事用海环境保护管理办法，由国务院、中央军事委员会依照本法制定。

**Article 122** The measures for the administration of environmental protection of military vessels and military sea-use areas shall be formulated by the State Council and the Central Military Commission in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的与海洋环境保护有关的国际条约与本法有不同规定的，适用国际条约的规定；但是，中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。

**Article 123** Where an international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China concerning marine environmental protection contains provisions differing from those of this Law, the provisions of the international treaty shall apply; however, the provisions on which the People's Republic of China has declared reservations shall be excluded.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　本法自2024年1月1日起施行。

**Article 124** This Law shall come into force on January 1, 2024.
