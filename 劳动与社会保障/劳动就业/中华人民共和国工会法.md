### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保障工会在国家政治、经济和社会生活中的地位，确定工会的权利与义务，发挥工会在社会主义现代化建设事业中的作用，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution with a view to safeguarding the status of trade unions in the political, economic and social life of the State, defining the rights and obligations of trade unions, and giving play to their role in the cause of socialist modernization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　工会是中国共产党领导的职工自愿结合的工人阶级群众组织，是中国共产党联系职工群众的桥梁和纽带。  
中华全国总工会及其各工会组织代表职工的利益，依法维护职工的合法权益。

**Article 2** Trade unions are mass organizations of the working class formed by the workers and staff members voluntarily under the leadership of the Communist Party of China, and serve as a bridge and bond linking the Communist Party of China with the masses of workers and staff members.  
The All-China Federation of Trade Unions and all trade union organizations shall represent the interests of the workers and staff members and safeguard the lawful rights and interests of the workers and staff members according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　在中国境内的企业、事业单位、机关、社会组织（以下统称用人单位）中以工资收入为主要生活来源的劳动者，不分民族、种族、性别、职业、宗教信仰、教育程度，都有依法参加和组织工会的权利。任何组织和个人不得阻挠和限制。  
工会适应企业组织形式、职工队伍结构、劳动关系、就业形态等方面的发展变化，依法维护劳动者参加和组织工会的权利。

**Article 3** All workers and staff members doing mental or physical labor in enterprises, institutions, state organs, or social organizations (hereinafter collectively referred to as "employing units") within the territory of China who rely on wages or salaries as their main source of income, regardless of nationality, race, sex, occupation, religious belief, or educational background, shall have the right to organize or join trade unions according to law. No organizations or individuals shall obstruct or restrict such right.  
Trade unions shall adapt to the development and changes in the forms of enterprise organization, the structure of the workforce, labor relations, employment patterns, etc., and shall protect the right of workers to join or organize trade unions according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　工会必须遵守和维护宪法，以宪法为根本的活动准则，以经济建设为中心，坚持社会主义道路，坚持人民民主专政，坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论、“三个代表”重要思想、科学发展观、习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想，坚持改革开放，保持和增强政治性、先进性、群众性，依照工会章程独立自主地开展工作。  
工会会员全国代表大会制定或者修改《中国工会章程》，章程不得与宪法和法律相抵触。  
国家保护工会的合法权益不受侵犯。

**Article 4** Trade unions must abide by and uphold the Constitution, take the Constitution as the fundamental guideline for their activities, take economic development as the central task, adhere to the socialist road, adhere to the people's democratic dictatorship, adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, adhere to Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the important thought of Three Represents, the Scientific Outlook on Development, and Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, adhere to reform and opening up, maintain and enhance their political, advanced, and mass character, and conduct their work independently and autonomously in accordance with the constitution of trade unions.  
The National Congress of trade union members shall formulate or amend the Constitution of Chinese Trade Unions, which shall not contravene the Constitution or laws.  
The State shall protect the lawful rights and interests of trade unions from infringement.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　工会组织和教育职工依照宪法和法律的规定行使民主权利，发挥国家主人翁的作用，通过各种途径和形式，参与管理国家事务、管理经济和文化事业、管理社会事务；协助人民政府开展工作，维护工人阶级领导的、以工农联盟为基础的人民民主专政的社会主义国家政权。

**Article 5** Trade unions shall organize and educate the workers and staff members to exercise their democratic rights in accordance with the provisions of the Constitution and laws, to give play to their role as masters of the country, and to participate in various ways and through various forms in the management of state affairs, economic and cultural undertakings, and social affairs; and shall assist the people's governments in their work and uphold the socialist state power under the people's democratic dictatorship led by the working class and based on the alliance of workers and peasants.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　维护职工合法权益、竭诚服务职工群众是工会的基本职责。工会在维护全国人民总体利益的同时，代表和维护职工的合法权益。  
工会通过平等协商和集体合同制度等，推动健全劳动关系协调机制，维护职工劳动权益，构建和谐劳动关系。  
工会依照法律规定通过职工代表大会或者其他形式，组织职工参与本单位的民主选举、民主协商、民主决策、民主管理和民主监督。  
工会建立联系广泛、服务职工的工会工作体系，密切联系职工，听取和反映职工的意见和要求，关心职工的生活，帮助职工解决困难，全心全意为职工服务。

**Article 6** It is the basic duty of trade unions to safeguard the lawful rights and interests of workers and staff members and serve the masses of workers and staff members wholeheartedly. While protecting the overall interests of the people throughout the country, trade unions shall represent and safeguard the lawful rights and interests of workers and staff members.  
Trade unions shall, through equal consultation and the collective contract system, etc., promote the improvement of the labor relations coordination mechanism, safeguard the labor rights and interests of workers and staff members, and build harmonious labor relations.  
Trade unions shall, in accordance with the law, organize workers and staff members to participate in the democratic election, democratic consultation, democratic decision-making, democratic management, and democratic supervision of their respective units through the congress of workers and staff members or other forms.  
Trade unions shall establish a trade union work system with broad connections and serving workers and staff members, closely link with them, listen to and reflect their opinions and demands, care for their lives, help them solve difficulties, and serve them wholeheartedly.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　工会动员和组织职工积极参加经济建设，努力完成生产任务和工作任务。教育职工不断提高思想道德、技术业务和科学文化素质，建设有理想、有道德、有文化、有纪律的职工队伍。

**Article 7** Trade unions shall mobilize and organize workers and staff members to actively participate in economic development and strive to fulfill production tasks and work tasks. They shall educate workers and staff members to continuously improve their ideological and moral qualities, technical and professional skills, and scientific and cultural attainments, and to build a contingent of workers and staff members with ideals, morality, cultural knowledge, and discipline.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　工会推动产业工人队伍建设改革，提高产业工人队伍整体素质，发挥产业工人骨干作用，维护产业工人合法权益，保障产业工人主人翁地位，造就一支有理想守信念、懂技术会创新、敢担当讲奉献的宏大产业工人队伍。

**Article 8** Trade unions shall promote the reform of the construction of the industrial workers' contingent, improve the overall quality of the industrial workers' contingent, give play to the backbone role of industrial workers, safeguard the lawful rights and interests of industrial workers, ensure their status as masters of the country, and cultivate a large contingent of industrial workers with ideals and convictions, technical skills and innovation capability, sense of responsibility and dedication.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　中华全国总工会根据独立、平等、互相尊重、互不干涉内部事务的原则，加强同各国工会组织的友好合作关系。

**Article 9** The All-China Federation of Trade Unions shall, on the principles of independence, equality, mutual respect, and non-interference in each other's internal affairs, strengthen friendly and cooperative relations with trade union organizations of various countries.

### 第二章　工会组织

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　工会各级组织按照民主集中制原则建立。  
各级工会委员会由会员大会或者会员代表大会民主选举产生。企业主要负责人的近亲属不得作为本企业基层工会委员会成员的人选。  
各级工会委员会向同级会员大会或者会员代表大会负责并报告工作，接受其监督。  
工会会员大会或者会员代表大会有权撤换或者罢免其所选举的代表或者工会委员会组成人员。  
上级工会组织领导下级工会组织。

**Article 10** Trade union organizations at all levels shall be established on the principle of democratic centralism.  
Trade union committees at all levels shall be democratically elected by the general assemblies of members or congresses of members. Close relatives of the principal responsible persons of an enterprise shall not be selected as candidates for members of the basic-level trade union committee of that enterprise.  
Trade union committees at all levels shall be responsible to and shall report on their work to the general assemblies of members or congresses of members at the corresponding levels and shall accept their supervision.  
The general assemblies of members or congresses of members of trade unions shall have the power to recall or remove the representatives or members of the trade union committees elected by them.  
Higher-level trade union organizations shall lead the lower-level trade union organizations.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　用人单位有会员二十五人以上的，应当建立基层工会委员会；不足二十五人的，可以单独建立基层工会委员会，也可以由两个以上单位的会员联合建立基层工会委员会，也可以选举组织员一人，组织会员开展活动。女职工人数较多的，可以建立工会女职工委员会，在同级工会领导下开展工作；女职工人数较少的，可以在工会委员会中设女职工委员。  
企业职工较多的乡镇、城市街道，可以建立基层工会的联合会。  
县级以上地方建立地方各级总工会。  
同一行业或者性质相近的几个行业，可以根据需要建立全国的或者地方的产业工会。  
全国建立统一的中华全国总工会。

**Article 11** In an employing unit with twenty-five or more members, a basic-level trade union committee shall be established; in an employing unit with less than twenty-five members, a basic-level trade union committee may be established separately, or a basic-level trade union committee may be established jointly by members from two or more units, or a single organizer may be elected to organize activities among the members. Where there is a relatively large number of female workers and staff members, a trade union women's committee may be established to carry out work under the leadership of the trade union at the same level; where there is a relatively small number of female workers and staff members, a women's member may be designated in the trade union committee.  
In townships, towns, and urban streets where there is a large number of workers and staff members in enterprises, a federation of basic-level trade unions may be established.  
General trade unions at all levels shall be established at the local level at or above the county level.  
Industrial trade unions at the national or local level may be established for the same industry or for several industries of similar nature as required.  
A unified All-China Federation of Trade Unions shall be established nationwide.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　基层工会、地方各级总工会、全国或者地方产业工会组织的建立，必须报上一级工会批准。  
上级工会可以派员帮助和指导企业职工组建工会，任何单位和个人不得阻挠。

**Article 12** The establishment of a basic-level trade union, a local general trade union at any level, or a national or local industrial trade union organization must be reported to the trade union at the next higher level for approval.  
The higher-level trade union may send personnel to assist and guide the workers and staff members of an enterprise in forming a trade union, and no unit or individual may obstruct such process.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　任何组织和个人不得随意撤销、合并工会组织。  
基层工会所在的用人单位终止或者被撤销，该工会组织相应撤销，并报告上一级工会。  
依前款规定被撤销的工会，其会员的会籍可以继续保留，具体管理办法由中华全国总工会制定。

**Article 13** No organization or individual may arbitrarily dissolve or merge a trade union organization.  
When the employing unit where a basic-level trade union is located is terminated or is abolished, the trade union organization shall be dissolved accordingly and the matter shall be reported to the trade union at the next higher level.  
For a trade union dissolved in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the membership of its members may be retained. The specific measures for the administration thereof shall be formulated by the All-China Federation of Trade Unions.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　职工二百人以上的企业、事业单位、社会组织的工会，可以设专职工会主席。工会专职工作人员的人数由工会与企业、事业单位、社会组织协商确定。

**Article 14** In enterprises, institutions, and social organizations with two hundred or more workers and staff members, a full-time chairman of the trade union may be appointed. The number of full-time functionaries of the trade union shall be determined through consultation between the trade union and the enterprise, institution, or social organization.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　中华全国总工会、地方总工会、产业工会具有社会团体法人资格。  
基层工会组织具备民法典规定的法人条件的，依法取得社会团体法人资格。

**Article 15** The All-China Federation of Trade Unions, local general trade unions, and industrial trade unions shall have the status of a legal person as a social organization.  
A basic-level trade union organization that meets the conditions for a legal person as prescribed in the Civil Code shall obtain the status of a legal person as a social organization according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　基层工会委员会每届任期三年或者五年。各级地方总工会委员会和产业工会委员会每届任期五年。

**Article 16** The term of office of each basic-level trade union committee shall be three or five years. The term of office of each local general trade union committee at any level and each industrial trade union committee shall be five years.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　基层工会委员会定期召开会员大会或者会员代表大会，讨论决定工会工作的重大问题。经基层工会委员会或者三分之一以上的工会会员提议，可以临时召开会员大会或者会员代表大会。

**Article 17** A basic-level trade union committee shall convene regular meetings of the general assembly of members or the congress of members to discuss and decide on major issues concerning the work of the trade union. Upon the proposal of the basic-level trade union committee or more than one third of the trade union members, an interim meeting of the general assembly of members or the congress of members may be convened.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　工会主席、副主席任期未满时，不得随意调动其工作。因工作需要调动时，应当征得本级工会委员会和上一级工会的同意。  
罢免工会主席、副主席必须召开会员大会或者会员代表大会讨论，非经会员大会全体会员或者会员代表大会全体代表过半数通过，不得罢免。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 18** When the term of office of the chairman or vice-chairman of a trade union has not expired, their work shall not be arbitrarily transferred. Where a transfer is necessary due to work requirements, the consent of the trade union committee at the same level and the trade union at the next higher level shall be obtained.  
The recall of the chairman or vice-chairman of a trade union must be discussed at the general assembly of members or the congress of members. No such recall may be effected without a resolution passed by a majority of all members of the general assembly or all representatives of the congress of members.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　基层工会专职主席、副主席或者委员自任职之日起，其劳动合同期限自动延长，延长期限相当于其任职期间；非专职主席、副主席或者委员自任职之日起，其尚未履行的劳动合同期限短于任期的，劳动合同期限自动延长至任期期满。但是，任职期间个人严重过失或者达到法定退休年龄的除外。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/司法解释/最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（二）.md#art-8" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（二）》第8条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 19** For full-time chairmen, vice-chairmen, or members of basic-level trade unions, their labor contract terms shall be automatically extended from the date of taking office by a period equal to their term of office. For part-time chairmen, vice-chairmen, or members, if the unexpired term of their labor contract is shorter than their term of office, the labor contract term shall be automatically extended to the expiration of their term of office. However, this shall not apply where the individual has committed a serious fault during the term of office or has reached the statutory retirement age.

### 第三章　工会的权利和义务

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　企业、事业单位、社会组织违反职工代表大会制度和其他民主管理制度，工会有权要求纠正，保障职工依法行使民主管理的权利。  
法律、法规规定应当提交职工大会或者职工代表大会审议、通过、决定的事项，企业、事业单位、社会组织应当依法办理。

**Article 20** Where an enterprise, institution, or social organization violates the system of the congress of workers and staff members or other democratic management systems, the trade union shall have the right to demand correction and ensure that the workers and staff members exercise their right to democratic management according to law.  
Matters that shall be submitted to the general meeting of workers and staff members or the congress of workers and staff members for consideration, adoption, or decision as provided by laws and regulations shall be handled by the enterprise, institution, or social organization in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　工会帮助、指导职工与企业、实行企业化管理的事业单位、社会组织签订劳动合同。  
工会代表职工与企业、实行企业化管理的事业单位、社会组织进行平等协商，依法签订集体合同。集体合同草案应当提交职工代表大会或者全体职工讨论通过。  
工会签订集体合同，上级工会应当给予支持和帮助。  
企业、事业单位、社会组织违反集体合同，侵犯职工劳动权益的，工会可以依法要求企业、事业单位、社会组织予以改正并承担责任；因履行集体合同发生争议，经协商解决不成的，工会可以向劳动争议仲裁机构提请仲裁，仲裁机构不予受理或者对仲裁裁决不服的，可以向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 21** Trade unions shall assist and guide employees in concluding labor contracts with enterprises, public institutions that implement enterprise management, and social organizations.  
Trade unions shall conduct equal consultation on behalf of employees with enterprises, public institutions that implement enterprise management, and social organizations, and conclude collective contracts in accordance with law. The draft collective contract shall be submitted to the employees' congress or all employees for discussion and adoption.  
When a trade union concludes a collective contract, the superior trade union shall provide support and assistance.  
If an enterprise, public institution, or social organization violates a collective contract and infringes upon the labor rights and interests of employees, the trade union may, in accordance with law, require the enterprise, public institution, or social organization to make corrections and assume liability; if a dispute arises over the performance of the collective contract and cannot be resolved through consultation, the trade union may apply to the labor dispute arbitration institution for arbitration. If the arbitration institution refuses to accept the case or the trade union is dissatisfied with the arbitration award, it may initiate litigation in the People's Court.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　企业、事业单位、社会组织处分职工，工会认为不适当的，有权提出意见。  
用人单位单方面解除职工劳动合同时，应当事先将理由通知工会，工会认为用人单位违反法律、法规和有关合同，要求重新研究处理时，用人单位应当研究工会的意见，并将处理结果书面通知工会。  
职工认为用人单位侵犯其劳动权益而申请劳动争议仲裁或者向人民法院提起诉讼的，工会应当给予支持和帮助。

**Article 22** If an enterprise, public institution, or social organization imposes disciplinary sanctions on an employee and the trade union considers it inappropriate, the trade union shall have the right to present opinions.  
When an employer unilaterally terminates an employee's labor contract, it shall notify the trade union of the reasons in advance. If the trade union considers that the employer has violated laws, regulations, or relevant contracts and requests reconsideration, the employer shall study the trade union's opinions and notify the trade union in writing of the handling results.  
If an employee, believing that the employer has infringed upon his/her labor rights and interests, applies for labor dispute arbitration or initiates litigation in the People's Court, the trade union shall provide support and assistance.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　企业、事业单位、社会组织违反劳动法律法规规定，有下列侵犯职工劳动权益情形，工会应当代表职工与企业、事业单位、社会组织交涉，要求企业、事业单位、社会组织采取措施予以改正；企业、事业单位、社会组织应当予以研究处理，并向工会作出答复；企业、事业单位、社会组织拒不改正的，工会可以提请当地人民政府依法作出处理：  
（一）克扣、拖欠职工工资的；  
（二）不提供劳动安全卫生条件的；  
（三）随意延长劳动时间的；  
（四）侵犯女职工和未成年工特殊权益的；  
（五）其他严重侵犯职工劳动权益的。

**Article 23** If an enterprise, public institution, or social organization violates labor laws and regulations and commits any of the following acts infringing upon employees' labor rights and interests, the trade union shall, on behalf of employees, negotiate with the enterprise, public institution, or social organization and require them to take measures to make corrections; the enterprise, public institution, or social organization shall study and handle the matter and respond to the trade union; if the enterprise, public institution, or social organization refuses to make corrections, the trade union may request the local people's government to handle the matter in accordance with law:  
(1) withholding or delaying payment of employees' wages;  
(2) failing to provide labor safety and health conditions;  
(3) arbitrarily extending working hours;  
(4) infringing upon the special rights and interests of female employees and minor employees;  
(5) other serious infringements upon employees' labor rights and interests.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　工会依照国家规定对新建、扩建企业和技术改造工程中的劳动条件和安全卫生设施与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投产使用进行监督。对工会提出的意见，企业或者主管部门应当认真处理，并将处理结果书面通知工会。

**Article 24** Trade unions shall, in accordance with state regulations, supervise the simultaneous design, simultaneous construction, and simultaneous commissioning of labor conditions and safety and health facilities with the main project in new construction, expansion, and technological renovation projects of enterprises. With respect to opinions raised by the trade union, the enterprise or the competent department shall seriously handle them and notify the trade union in writing of the handling results.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　工会发现企业违章指挥、强令工人冒险作业，或者生产过程中发现明显重大事故隐患和职业危害，有权提出解决的建议，企业应当及时研究答复；发现危及职工生命安全的情况时，工会有权向企业建议组织职工撤离危险现场，企业必须及时作出处理决定。

**Article 25** Where a trade union discovers that an enterprise is giving illegal directions and forcing workers to work under dangerous conditions, or discovers obvious major accident hazards and occupational hazards during the production process, it shall have the right to propose solutions, and the enterprise shall study and respond in a timely manner; when discovering situations that endanger the life safety of employees, the trade union has the right to propose to the enterprise to organize employees to evacuate the dangerous site, and the enterprise must make a handling decision in a timely manner.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　工会有权对企业、事业单位、社会组织侵犯职工合法权益的问题进行调查，有关单位应当予以协助。

**Article 26** Trade unions have the right to investigate issues of enterprises, public institutions, or social organizations infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of employees, and the relevant units shall provide assistance.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　职工因工伤亡事故和其他严重危害职工健康问题的调查处理，必须有工会参加。工会应当向有关部门提出处理意见，并有权要求追究直接负责的主管人员和有关责任人员的责任。对工会提出的意见，应当及时研究，给予答复。

**Article 27** Trade unions must participate in the investigation and handling of work-related injury and death accidents and other issues seriously endangering the health of employees. The trade union shall present handling opinions to the relevant departments and have the right to require the investigation of liability of the directly responsible persons in charge and relevant responsible persons. With respect to opinions raised by the trade union, the relevant departments shall study them in a timely manner and give a response.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　企业、事业单位、社会组织发生停工、怠工事件，工会应当代表职工同企业、事业单位、社会组织或者有关方面协商，反映职工的意见和要求并提出解决意见。对于职工的合理要求，企业、事业单位、社会组织应当予以解决。工会协助企业、事业单位、社会组织做好工作，尽快恢复生产、工作秩序。

**Article 28** In the event of a work stoppage or slowdown incident in an enterprise, public institution, or social organization, the trade union shall, on behalf of employees, negotiate with the enterprise, public institution, social organization, or relevant parties, reflect the opinions and demands of employees, and propose solutions. For the reasonable demands of employees, the enterprise, public institution, or social organization shall satisfy them. The trade union shall assist the enterprise, public institution, or social organization in carrying out work properly and promptly restoring production and work order.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　工会参加企业的劳动争议调解工作。  
地方劳动争议仲裁组织应当有同级工会代表参加。

**Article 29** Trade unions shall participate in the mediation of labor disputes in enterprises.  
Local labor dispute arbitration organizations shall include representatives of trade unions at the same level.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上各级总工会依法为所属工会和职工提供法律援助等法律服务。

**Article 30** Trade union federations at or above the county level shall, in accordance with law, provide legal aid and other legal services to their subordinate trade unions and employees.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　工会协助用人单位办好职工集体福利事业，做好工资、劳动安全卫生和社会保险工作。

**Article 31** Trade unions shall assist employers in properly managing employees' collective welfare affairs and in carrying out work related to wages, labor safety and health, and social insurance.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　工会会同用人单位加强对职工的思想政治引领，教育职工以国家主人翁态度对待劳动，爱护国家和单位的财产；组织职工开展群众性的合理化建议、技术革新、劳动和技能竞赛活动，进行业余文化技术学习和职工培训，参加职业教育和文化体育活动，推进职业安全健康教育和劳动保护工作。

**Article 32** Trade unions shall, jointly with employers, strengthen ideological and political guidance for employees, educate employees to treat labor with the attitude of masters of the country, and take good care of state and unit property; organize employees to carry out mass activities such as rationalization suggestions, technological innovation, labor and skills competitions, conduct spare-time cultural and technical studies and employee training, participate in vocational education and cultural and sports activities, and promote occupational safety and health education and labor protection work.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　根据政府委托，工会与有关部门共同做好劳动模范和先进生产（工作）者的评选、表彰、培养和管理工作。

**Article 33** In accordance with entrustment by the government, trade unions shall, jointly with relevant departments, properly carry out the selection, commendation, cultivation, and management of model workers and advanced producers (workers).

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家机关在组织起草或者修改直接涉及职工切身利益的法律、法规、规章时，应当听取工会意见。  
县级以上各级人民政府制定国民经济和社会发展计划，对涉及职工利益的重大问题，应当听取同级工会的意见。  
县级以上各级人民政府及其有关部门研究制定劳动就业、工资、劳动安全卫生、社会保险等涉及职工切身利益的政策、措施时，应当吸收同级工会参加研究，听取工会意见。

**Article 34** When state organs organize the drafting or revision of laws, regulations, and rules directly involving the vital interests of employees, they shall solicit opinions from trade unions.  
When people's governments at or above the county level formulate national economic and social development plans, they shall solicit opinions from trade unions at the same level on major issues involving the interests of employees.  
When people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments study and formulate policies and measures concerning labor employment, wages, labor safety and health, social insurance, and other matters involving the vital interests of employees, they shall invite trade unions at the same level to participate in the study and solicit opinions from trade unions.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上地方各级人民政府可以召开会议或者采取适当方式，向同级工会通报政府的重要的工作部署和与工会工作有关的行政措施，研究解决工会反映的职工群众的意见和要求。  
各级人民政府劳动行政部门应当会同同级工会和企业方面代表，建立劳动关系三方协商机制，共同研究解决劳动关系方面的重大问题。

**Article 35** Local people's governments at or above the county level may hold meetings or adopt appropriate methods to inform trade unions at the same level of the government's important work deployments and administrative measures related to trade union work, and study and resolve the opinions and demands of the employees reflected by trade unions.  
The labor administrative departments of people's governments at all levels shall, jointly with trade unions at the same level and representatives of enterprises, establish a tripartite consultation mechanism for labor relations to jointly study and resolve major issues concerning labor relations.

### 第四章　基层工会组织

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国有企业职工代表大会是企业实行民主管理的基本形式，是职工行使民主管理权力的机构，依照法律规定行使职权。  
国有企业的工会委员会是职工代表大会的工作机构，负责职工代表大会的日常工作，检查、督促职工代表大会决议的执行。

**Article 36** The employees' congress in a state-owned enterprise is the basic form for the enterprise to implement democratic management and the institution through which employees exercise their power of democratic management, and it shall exercise its functions and powers in accordance with the provisions of law.  
The trade union committee in a state-owned enterprise is the working body of the employees' congress, responsible for the daily work of the employees' congress, and inspects and supervises the implementation of the resolutions of the employees' congress.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　集体企业的工会委员会，应当支持和组织职工参加民主管理和民主监督，维护职工选举和罢免管理人员、决定经营管理的重大问题的权力。

**Article 37** The trade union committee in a collective enterprise shall support and organize employees to participate in democratic management and democratic supervision, and safeguard the employees' power to elect and recall management personnel and to decide major issues concerning operation and management.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　[本法第三十六条](#art-36)、第三十七条规定以外的其他企业、事业单位的工会委员会，依照法律规定组织职工采取与企业、事业单位相适应的形式，参与企业、事业单位民主管理。

**Article 38** Trade union committees in enterprises and public institutions other than those provided for in Articles 36 and 37 of this Law shall, in accordance with the provisions of law, organize employees to adopt forms appropriate to the enterprise or public institution to participate in the democratic management of the enterprise or public institution.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　企业、事业单位、社会组织研究经营管理和发展的重大问题应当听取工会的意见；召开会议讨论有关工资、福利、劳动安全卫生、工作时间、休息休假、女职工保护和社会保险等涉及职工切身利益的问题，必须有工会代表参加。  
企业、事业单位、社会组织应当支持工会依法开展工作，工会应当支持企业、事业单位、社会组织依法行使经营管理权。

**Article 39** When an enterprise, public institution, or social organization studies major issues concerning operation, management, and development, it shall solicit opinions from the trade union; when convening meetings to discuss issues concerning wages, welfare, labor safety and health, working hours, rest and leave, protection of female employees, social insurance, and other matters involving the vital interests of employees, representatives of the trade union must participate.  
The enterprise, public institution, or social organization shall support the trade union in carrying out its work in accordance with law, and the trade union shall support the enterprise, public institution, or social organization in exercising its operation and management rights in accordance with law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　公司的董事会、监事会中职工代表的产生，依照公司法有关规定执行。

**Article 40** The election of employee representatives in the board of directors and board of supervisors of a company shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Company Law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　基层工会委员会召开会议或者组织职工活动，应当在生产或者工作时间以外进行，需要占用生产或者工作时间的，应当事先征得企业、事业单位、社会组织的同意。  
基层工会的非专职委员占用生产或者工作时间参加会议或者从事工会工作，每月不超过三个工作日，其工资照发，其他待遇不受影响。

**Article 41** The primary trade union committee shall hold meetings or organize activities for workers outside production or working hours; if it is necessary to occupy production or working hours, it shall obtain the consent of the enterprise, public institution, or social organization in advance.  
Where a non-full-time member of the primary trade union spends production or working hours to attend meetings or engage in trade union work, such time shall not exceed three working days per month, and the member's salary shall be paid as usual and other benefits shall not be affected.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　用人单位工会委员会的专职工作人员的工资、奖励、补贴，由所在单位支付。社会保险和其他福利待遇等，享受本单位职工同等待遇。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释.md#art-4" title="被《最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释》第4条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 42** The salaries, bonuses, and subsidies of the full-time staff of the trade union committee of the employer shall be paid by the employer. Social insurance and other welfare benefits shall be enjoyed equally with other workers of the employer.

### 第五章　工会的经费和财产

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　工会经费的来源：  
（一）工会会员缴纳的会费；  
（二）建立工会组织的用人单位按每月全部职工工资总额的百分之二向工会拨缴的经费；  
（三）工会所属的企业、事业单位上缴的收入；  
（四）人民政府的补助；  
（五）其他收入。  
前款第二项规定的企业、事业单位、社会组织拨缴的经费在税前列支。  
工会经费主要用于为职工服务和工会活动。经费使用的具体办法由中华全国总工会制定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释.md#art-3" title="被《最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释》第3条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释.md#art-4" title="被《最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释》第4条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释.md#art-5" title="被《最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释》第5条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 43** The sources of trade union funds are:  
(1) membership dues paid by trade union members;  
(2) funds allocated by the employer that has established a trade union organization to the trade union at a rate of two percent of the total monthly wages of all workers;  
(3) income turned over by enterprises and public institutions under the trade union;  
(4) subsidies from the people's government;  
(5) other income.  
The funds allocated by enterprises, public institutions, and social organizations as specified in subparagraph (2) of the preceding paragraph shall be deducted before tax.  
Trade union funds shall be mainly used for serving workers and for trade union activities. Specific measures for the use of funds shall be formulated by the All-China Federation of Trade Unions.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　企业、事业单位、社会组织无正当理由拖延或者拒不拨缴工会经费，基层工会或者上级工会可以向当地人民法院申请支付令；拒不执行支付令的，工会可以依法申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 44** Where an enterprise, public institution, or social organization delays or refuses to allocate trade union funds without justifiable reasons, the primary trade union or the trade union at a higher level may apply to the local People's Court for a payment order; if the payment order is not complied with, the trade union may, according to law, apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　工会应当根据经费独立原则，建立预算、决算和经费审查监督制度。  
各级工会建立经费审查委员会。  
各级工会经费收支情况应当由同级工会经费审查委员会审查，并且定期向会员大会或者会员代表大会报告，接受监督。工会会员大会或者会员代表大会有权对经费使用情况提出意见。  
工会经费的使用应当依法接受国家的监督。

**Article 45** A trade union shall, in accordance with the principle of independent funds, establish a system of budgets, final accounts, and fund examination and supervision.  
Trade unions at all levels shall establish fund examination committees.  
The income and expenditure of trade union funds at all levels shall be examined by the fund examination committee of the same level, and shall be reported regularly to the general assembly of members or the congress of members for supervision. The general assembly of members or the congress of members shall have the right to raise opinions on the use of funds.  
The use of trade union funds shall be subject to state supervision according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　各级人民政府和用人单位应当为工会办公和开展活动，提供必要的设施和活动场所等物质条件。

**Article 46** People's governments at all levels and employers shall provide necessary material conditions such as facilities and activity venues for trade unions to conduct office work and carry out activities.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　工会的财产、经费和国家拨给工会使用的不动产，任何组织和个人不得侵占、挪用和任意调拨。

**Article 47** No organization or individual shall misappropriate, divert, or arbitrarily transfer the property and funds of a trade union or the immovable property allocated by the state for the use of the trade union.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　工会所属的为职工服务的企业、事业单位，其隶属关系不得随意改变。

**Article 48** The subordinate relationship of enterprises and public institutions under the trade union that serve workers shall not be arbitrarily altered.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上各级工会的离休、退休人员的待遇，与国家机关工作人员同等对待。

**Article 49** The treatment of retired and resigned personnel of trade unions at or above the county level shall be equal to that of state organ staff.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　工会对违反本法规定侵犯其合法权益的，有权提请人民政府或者有关部门予以处理，或者向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 50** Where a trade union's lawful rights and interests are infringed upon in violation of the provisions of this Law, it shall have the right to request the people's government or the relevant department to handle the matter, or to institute proceedings in the People's Court.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反[本法第三条](#art-3)、第十二条规定，阻挠职工依法参加和组织工会或者阻挠上级工会帮助、指导职工筹建工会的，由劳动行政部门责令其改正；拒不改正的，由劳动行政部门提请县级以上人民政府处理；以暴力、威胁等手段阻挠造成严重后果，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 51** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of Articles 3 and 12 of this Law, obstructs workers from joining or organizing a trade union according to law, or obstructs a trade union at a higher level from helping and guiding workers in the preparation for the establishment of a trade union, shall be ordered by the labor administrative department to make corrections; if the party refuses to make corrections, the labor administrative department shall refer the matter to the people's government at or above the county level for handling; if the obstruction by means of violence, threats, or other means causes serious consequences and constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本法规定，对依法履行职责的工会工作人员无正当理由调动工作岗位，进行打击报复的，由劳动行政部门责令改正、恢复原工作；造成损失的，给予赔偿。  
对依法履行职责的工会工作人员进行侮辱、诽谤或者进行人身伤害，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚未构成犯罪的，由公安机关依照治安管理处罚法的规定处罚。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释.md#art-6" title="被《最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释》第6条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 52** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a trade union staff member who performs duties according to law is transferred from his or her position without justifiable reasons or subjected to retaliation, the labor administrative department shall order the employer to make corrections and restore the original work; if losses are caused, compensation shall be paid.  
If a trade union staff member who performs duties according to law is subjected to insult, defamation, or personal injury, and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not yet constitute a crime, the public security organ shall impose punishment in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Public Security Administration Punishments.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由劳动行政部门责令恢复其工作，并补发被解除劳动合同期间应得的报酬，或者责令给予本人年收入二倍的赔偿：  
（一）职工因参加工会活动而被解除劳动合同的；  
（二）工会工作人员因履行本法规定的职责而被解除劳动合同的。

**Article 53** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, any of the following circumstances occurs, the labor administrative department shall order the employer to reinstate the worker and retroactively pay the remuneration that should have been received during the period of rescission of the labor contract, or order the employer to pay compensation equal to twice the worker's annual income:  
(1) a worker's labor contract is rescinded because he or she participated in trade union activities;  
(2) a trade union staff member's labor contract is rescinded because he or she performed the duties provided for in this Law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府责令改正，依法处理：  
（一）妨碍工会组织职工通过职工代表大会和其他形式依法行使民主权利的；  
（二）非法撤销、合并工会组织的；  
（三）妨碍工会参加职工因工伤亡事故以及其他侵犯职工合法权益问题的调查处理的；  
（四）无正当理由拒绝进行平等协商的。

**Article 54** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, any of the following circumstances occurs, the people's government at or above the county level shall order corrections and handle the matter according to law:  
(1) obstructing a trade union from organizing workers to exercise their democratic rights according to law through the workers' congress or other forms;  
(2) unlawfully dissolving or merging a trade union organization;  
(3) obstructing a trade union from participating in the investigation and handling of work-related injury or death accidents involving workers or other issues infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of workers;  
(4) refusing to conduct equal consultation without justifiable reasons.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反[本法第四十七条](#art-47)规定，侵占工会经费和财产拒不返还的，工会可以向人民法院提起诉讼，要求返还，并赔偿损失。

**Article 55** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of Article 47 of this Law, misappropriates trade union funds or property and refuses to return them, the trade union may bring a lawsuit in the People's Court to demand restitution and compensation for losses.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　工会工作人员违反本法规定，损害职工或者工会权益的，由同级工会或者上级工会责令改正，或者予以处分；情节严重的，依照《中国工会章程》予以罢免；造成损失的，应当承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 56** Where a trade union staff member, in violation of the provisions of this Law, damages the rights and interests of workers or the trade union, the trade union at the same level or at a higher level shall order corrections or impose a sanction; if the circumstances are serious, the staff member shall be removed from office in accordance with the Constitution of the Chinese Trade Unions; if losses are caused, the staff member shall bear compensation liability; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　中华全国总工会会同有关国家机关制定机关工会实施本法的具体办法。

**Article 57** The All-China Federation of Trade Unions shall, jointly with the relevant state organs, formulate specific measures for the implementation of this Law by the trade unions of state organs.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　本法自公布之日起施行。1950年6月29日中央人民政府颁布的《中华人民共和国工会法》同时废止。

**Article 58** This Law shall come into force as of the date of its promulgation. The Trade Union Law of the People's Republic of China promulgated by the Central People's Government on June 29, 1950 shall be abolished simultaneously.
