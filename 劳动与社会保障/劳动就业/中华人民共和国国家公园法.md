# 中华人民共和国国家公园法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2025-09-12  
**生效日期**：2026-01-01

> （2025年9月12日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十七次会议通过）

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了高质量推进国家公园建设，加强对国家公园的保护和管理，保持重要自然生态系统原真性和完整性，建设生态文明，全面推进美丽中国建设，加快推进人与自然和谐共生的现代化，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of advancing the development of national parks with high quality, strengthening the protection and management of national parks, preserving the authenticity and integrity of important natural ecosystems, building an ecological civilization, comprehensively promoting the construction of a Beautiful China, and accelerating the modernization of harmonious coexistence between humans and nature.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称国家公园，是指由国务院批准设立，以保护具有国家代表性的自然生态系统为主要目的，实现自然资源科学保护和合理利用的特定陆地和海洋区域。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, a "national park" means a specific land and marine area approved by the State Council for establishment, with the primary purpose of protecting natural ecosystems of national representativeness, and achieving scientific protection and rational utilization of natural resources.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家建立以国家公园为主体的自然保护地体系，逐步将自然生态系统最重要、自然景观最独特、自然遗产最精华、生物多样性最富集的自然生态空间纳入国家公园，实行严格保护。

**Article 3** The State shall establish a system of protected natural areas with national parks as the mainstay, and shall gradually incorporate natural ecological spaces that are the most important for natural ecosystems, the most unique in natural landscapes, the most essential in natural heritage, and the richest in biodiversity into national parks, and shall implement strict protection.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家公园建设应当坚持中国共产党的领导，践行绿水青山就是金山银山理念，坚持以人民为中心的发展思想，坚持生态保护第一、统筹保护和发展，坚持国家代表性和全民公益性，坚持因地制宜、突出特色，构建政府主导、多方参与、社会共享的机制，实现生态保护、绿色发展、民生改善相统一。

**Article 4** The development of national parks shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, practice the concept that lucid waters and lush mountains are invaluable assets, uphold the people-centered development philosophy, prioritize ecological protection while coordinating protection and development, uphold national representativeness and public welfare for all, proceed from local conditions and highlight distinctive features, and establish a mechanism featuring government leadership, multi-party participation, and social sharing, so as to achieve the alignment of ecological protection, green development, and improvement of people's livelihoods.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院和国家公园所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当加强对国家公园建设的统筹协调，完善支持国家公园建设的政策措施。  
国务院和国家公园所在地县级以上地方人民政府应当将国家公园建设纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。

**Article 5** The State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government where national parks are located shall strengthen overall coordination for the development of national parks and improve policies and measures in support of the development of national parks.  
The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level where national parks are located shall incorporate the development of national parks into their plans for national economic and social development.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家建立统一规范高效的国家公园管理体制。  
国务院国家公园主管部门依照本法和有关法律、行政法规，负责全国国家公园的监督管理工作。国务院自然资源、生态环境、水行政、农业农村等有关部门依照本法和有关法律、行政法规，在各自职责范围内负责国家公园的有关监督管理工作。  
按照规定设立的各国家公园管理机构依照本法和规定的职责，负责各该国家公园的保护和管理工作，履行自然资源、林业草原等领域的相关行政执法职责。  
国家公园所在地地方人民政府行使本行政区域内国家公园的经济社会发展综合协调、公共服务、社会管理、市场监管、防灾减灾等职责。  
国务院国家公园主管部门、国务院有关部门、国家公园管理机构、国家公园所在地地方人民政府应当建立健全相应的机制，加强工作协同，实现信息共享。  
国务院国家公园主管部门商国家公园所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，建立工作协调机制，组织协调跨省域国家公园建设重大事项。

**Article 6** The State shall establish a unified, standardized, and efficient management system for national parks.  
The national park authority of the State Council shall, in accordance with this Law and relevant laws and administrative regulations, be responsible for the supervision and administration of national parks nationwide. The competent departments of natural resources, ecology and environment, water administration, agriculture and rural affairs, and other relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with this Law and relevant laws and administrative regulations, be responsible for the relevant supervision and administration of national parks within their respective functions and duties.  
Each national park management institution established according to regulations shall, in accordance with this Law and its prescribed duties, be responsible for the protection and management of that national park and shall perform relevant administrative law enforcement duties in the fields of natural resources, forestry and grassland, among others.  
Local people's governments where national parks are located shall exercise duties in their respective administrative regions, including comprehensive coordination of economic and social development, public services, social management, market regulation, and disaster prevention and mitigation, with respect to national parks.  
The national park authority of the State Council, relevant departments of the State Council, national park management institutions, and local people's governments where national parks are located shall establish and improve corresponding mechanisms, strengthen work coordination, and achieve information sharing.  
The national park authority of the State Council shall, in consultation with the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government where national parks are located, establish a work coordination mechanism to organize and coordinate major matters concerning the development of cross-provincial national parks.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院国家公园主管部门和有关部门按照职责建立健全国家公园标准体系，依法组织制定并适时修订国家公园相关标准。

**Article 7** The national park authority of the State Council and relevant departments shall, in accordance with their functions and duties, establish and improve a standard system for national parks, and shall, according to law, organize the formulation and timely revision of relevant national park standards.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励和支持国家公园相关科学技术研究开发和应用推广，加强国家公园专业人才培养，强化科技创新对国家公园建设的支撑作用。

**Article 8** The State shall encourage and support research, development, application, and promotion of science and technology related to national parks, strengthen the cultivation of professionals for national parks, and enhance the supporting role of scientific and technological innovation in the development of national parks.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院国家公园主管部门、国务院有关部门、国家公园管理机构、国家公园所在地县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当组织开展多种形式的国家公园保护宣传教育活动，培育国家公园文化，传播国家公园理念。  
新闻媒体应当采取多种形式开展国家公园保护公益宣传，依法进行舆论监督。

**Article 9** The national park authority of the State Council, relevant departments of the State Council, national park management institutions, and local people's governments at or above the county level where national parks are located and their relevant departments shall organize and conduct publicity and education activities on national park protection in various forms, cultivate a culture of national parks, and disseminate the concept of national parks.  
News media shall, in various forms, conduct public interest publicity on national park protection and exercise oversight through public opinion according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对在国家公园建设中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 10** Entities and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the development of national parks shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家通过多种方式，支持和促进国家公园领域的国际交流合作。

**Article 11** The State shall support and promote international exchanges and cooperation in the field of national parks through various means.

### 第二章　布局和设立

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家科学规划国家公园总体发展布局，严格国家公园设立条件，合理确定国家公园数量和规模。  
国务院国家公园主管部门会同有关部门，根据自然生态空间分布和系统性保护需要，编制国家公园空间布局方案，遴选国家公园候选区，报国务院批准后实施。  
遴选国家公园候选区，应当就相关区域自然生态空间的国家代表性、生态重要性以及纳入国家公园保护和管理的可行性进行深入评估论证。

**Article 12** The State shall scientifically plan the overall development layout of national parks, strictly set the conditions for the establishment of national parks, and reasonably determine the quantity and scale of national parks.  
The national park authority of the State Council shall, in conjunction with relevant departments, formulate a spatial layout plan for national parks based on the distribution of natural ecological spaces and the need for systematic protection, select candidate areas for national parks, submit the plan to the State Council for approval, and then implement it.  
In selecting candidate areas for national parks, an in-depth assessment and demonstration shall be conducted regarding the national representativeness and ecological importance of the natural ecological spaces in the relevant areas, as well as the feasibility of incorporating them into the protection and management of national parks.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　拟设立国家公园的，国家公园候选区所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当按照国家公园空间布局方案有关要求做好前期工作，组织开展基础调查，在广泛听取有关方面意见、深入论证的基础上提出国家公园名称、区域范围、管控分区方案以及原有居民、企业生产生活影响评估和解决方案等，并向社会公示。对拟设国家公园区域内不符合管控要求的开发建设项目，依法依规做好分类处置、有序退出、合理补偿等工作。  
前期工作完成后，经国务院国家公园主管部门组织评估并征求有关部门意见，具备设立条件的，由相关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府向国务院提出国家公园设立申请，报送国家公园设立方案等材料，经国务院批准后设立国家公园。

**Article 13** Where a national park is to be established, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the candidate area for the national park is located shall, in accordance with the relevant requirements of the spatial layout plan for national parks, carry out preliminary work, organize basic surveys, and, on the basis of extensively soliciting opinions from relevant parties and conducting in-depth demonstration, propose the name, scope, and zoning plan for the national park, as well as an assessment of the impact on the production and livelihood of existing residents and enterprises and a solution thereto, and shall make the same publicly known. For development and construction projects within the proposed national park area that do not meet the control requirements, measures such as classified disposal, orderly withdrawal, and reasonable compensation shall be taken in accordance with laws and regulations.  
After the preliminary work is completed, if the conditions for establishment are met, the relevant people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, after evaluation by the national park authority of the State Council and consultation with relevant departments, submit an application to the State Council for the establishment of a national park, along with the establishment plan and other materials, and the national park shall be established upon approval by the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家公园名称应当符合国家公园命名规范和地名管理的有关规定。国家公园名称一般不使用行政区划名称。

**Article 14** The name of a national park shall comply with the national park naming standards and the relevant regulations on toponymy management. The name of a national park shall not normally use the name of an administrative division.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家公园区域范围的划定应当统筹考虑自然生态系统的原真性、完整性和周边经济社会发展的需要，坚持实事求是、科学合理，经过充分调查和科学论证。  
设立国家公园后，其他类型的自然保护地全部划入该国家公园区域范围的，不再保留；部分划入的，未划入部分经科学评估并按照规定程序批准，予以整合或者撤销。  
设立国家公园后，按照规定的权限和程序，可以将国家公园作为特定区域编制国土空间规划，并纳入国土空间规划实施监督信息系统，接受统一监管。

**Article 15** The delineation of the scope of a national park shall take into comprehensive consideration the authenticity and integrity of natural ecosystems and the needs of surrounding economic and social development, adhere to the principles of seeking truth from facts and being scientific and reasonable, and be based on sufficient investigation and scientific demonstration.  
After a national park is established, if other types of protected natural areas are entirely included within the scope of the national park, they shall no longer be retained; if they are partially included, the unincluded parts shall be integrated or revoked after scientific evaluation and approval in accordance with the prescribed procedures.  
After a national park is established, the national park may, in accordance with the prescribed authorities and procedures, be treated as a specific area in the preparation of territorial spatial planning, and shall be included in the territorial spatial planning implementation supervision information system to be subject to unified supervision.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　根据自然生态系统特性、功能定位和管理目标，国家公园划分为核心保护区和一般控制区。国家公园区域内自然生态系统保存完整、代表性强，核心资源集中分布，或者生态脆弱需要休养生息的区域划为核心保护区，核心保护区以外的区域划为一般控制区。

**Article 16** National parks shall be divided into core protection zones and general control zones based on the characteristics of natural ecosystems, functional orientation, and management objectives. Within the area of a national park, areas where natural ecosystems are well-preserved and highly representative, core resources are concentrated, or areas that are ecologically fragile and require restoration shall be designated as core protection zones, and areas other than the core protection zones shall be designated as general control zones.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　设立国家公园后，国家公园所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当及时组织完成国家公园勘界，国家公园管理机构应当根据保护和管理需要及时设置国家公园界碑界桩、电子标识或者电子围栏等界线标志。  
禁止损毁、涂改、遮挡或者擅自拆除、移动国家公园界线标志。

**Article 17** After a national park is established, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the national park is located shall promptly organize the completion of the delimitation of the national park boundaries, and the national park management institution shall, in accordance with the needs of protection and management, promptly set up boundary markers such as boundary monuments, boundary posts, electronic markers, or electronic fences for the national park.  
It is prohibited to damage, alter, obstruct, or dismantle or move the boundary markers of a national park without authorization.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家公园区域范围、管控分区等由国务院国家公园主管部门向社会公布。  
国务院国家公园主管部门应当与有关部门共享国家公园区域范围、管控分区坐标等信息。  
国家公园变更名称、调整区域范围或者管控分区应当经国务院批准。

**Article 18** The national park authority of the State Council shall make public the scope and management zoning of national parks to the public.  
The national park authority of the State Council shall share information such as the scope and management zoning coordinates of national parks with relevant departments.  
Any change of the name, adjustment of the scope, or modification of the management zoning of a national park shall be subject to the approval of the State Council.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国务院国家公园主管部门发布全国国家公园统一标志。国家公园管理机构可以确定其管理的国家公园的专属标志，报国务院国家公园主管部门同意后发布。  
国家公园标志受法律保护。未经国家公园标志发布机构同意，任何单位和个人不得为商业目的使用国家公园标志。国家公园标志保护的具体办法由国务院国家公园主管部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 19** The national park authority of the State Council shall release a unified symbol for national parks nationwide. A national park management institution may determine the exclusive symbol for the national park under its management and release it after reporting it to the national park authority of the State Council for approval.  
The symbol of a national park shall be protected by law. No entity or individual may use the symbol of a national park for commercial purposes without the consent of the institution that released the symbol. Specific measures for the protection of national park symbols shall be formulated by the national park authority of the State Council in conjunction with relevant departments.

### 第三章　保护和管理

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家坚持山水林田湖草沙一体化保护，按照自然生态系统特性和内在规律，对国家公园实行整体保护、系统修复、综合治理。

**Article 20** The State shall adhere to the integrated protection of mountains, waters, forests, farmlands, grasslands, and deserts, and shall, in accordance with the characteristics and inherent laws of natural ecosystems, implement holistic protection, systematic restoration, and comprehensive management of national parks.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家公园管理机构组织编制所管理国家公园的总体规划，明确保护和管理的目标任务、保护对象、保护措施等事项，并对拟开展的生态修复活动和原有居民生产生活活动等作出安排。组织编制国家公园总体规划，应当广泛听取有关方面意见、深入论证。  
国家公园总体规划经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审查同意后，报国务院国家公园主管部门批准实施。国务院国家公园主管部门审查批准国家公园总体规划，应当征求有关部门的意见。  
国家公园总体规划应当与国民经济和社会发展规划、国土空间总体规划等相衔接。国家公园总体规划应当向社会公开。

**Article 21** The national park management agency shall organize the preparation of the overall plan for the national park under its management, specifying the objectives, tasks, protection targets, protection measures, and other matters for protection and management, and make arrangements for proposed ecological restoration activities and the production and living activities of original residents. When organizing the preparation of the overall plan for a national park, opinions from relevant parties shall be extensively solicited and in-depth demonstration shall be conducted.  
After being reviewed and approved by the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the overall plan for a national park shall be submitted to the national park authority under the State Council for approval and implementation. When reviewing and approving the overall plan for a national park, the national park authority under the State Council shall solicit opinions from relevant departments.  
The overall plan for a national park shall be coordinated with the national economic and social development plan, the overall territorial space plan, and other relevant plans. The overall plan for a national park shall be made available to the public.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家公园管理机构应当依据法律、法规、国家有关规定和国家公园总体规划，健全完善国家公园保护和管理的规章制度，加强国家公园日常保护和管理工作。  
国家公园区域内的单位、个人以及进入国家公园的人员，应当遵守法律、法规、国家公园保护和管理的规章制度，接受国家公园管理机构的管理。

**Article 22** The national park management agency shall, in accordance with laws, regulations, relevant state provisions, and the overall plan for the national park, improve and perfect the rules and regulations for the protection and management of the national park, and strengthen the daily protection and management of the national park.  
Units and individuals within the national park area, as well as persons entering the national park, shall comply with laws, regulations, and the rules and regulations for the protection and management of the national park, and accept the management of the national park management agency.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对国家公园区域内的自然资源，按照国家自然资源确权登记有关规定统一进行确权登记，确权登记时应当将国家公园作为独立登记单元。

**Article 23** Natural resources within the national park area shall be uniformly registered for rights confirmation in accordance with the relevant provisions on the confirmation and registration of rights to natural resources of the State. When confirming and registering rights, the national park shall be taken as an independent registration unit.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家加强国家公园监测网络体系建设，充分发挥各类监测站点的作用，运用信息化手段加强监测数据集成分析、共享和综合应用，全面掌握自然生态系统构成、分布、动态变化和生物多样性状况，及时评估和预警生态风险。

**Article 24** The State shall strengthen the construction of a national park monitoring network system, fully leverage the role of various monitoring stations, and use information technology to strengthen the integrated analysis, sharing, and comprehensive application of monitoring data, to comprehensively grasp the composition, distribution, dynamic changes, and biodiversity status of natural ecosystems, and to promptly assess and warn of ecological risks.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家公园管理机构应当会同国家公园所在地地方人民政府有关部门对国家公园区域内各类自然生态系统和野生生物及其遗传资源、自然遗迹、自然景观等特定保护对象分类制定保护和管理目标，开展专项保护。  
国家公园所在地地方人民政府和国家公园管理机构应当建立协同保护制度，统筹国家公园区域内自然生态保护和文化遗产保护。

**Article 25** The national park management agency shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the local people's government where the national park is located, classify and formulate protection and management objectives for specific protection targets such as various natural ecosystems, wild organisms and their genetic resources, natural relics, and natural landscapes within the national park area, and carry out special protection.  
The local people's government where the national park is located and the national park management agency shall establish a coordinated protection system, and coordinate the protection of natural ecosystems and cultural heritage within the national park area.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家公园区域内受损自然生态系统修复、生态廊道连通、重要栖息地恢复等应当坚持自然恢复为主；确有必要开展种群调控、树种更新等人工修复活动的，应当充分听取有关方面意见，按照有关规定制定科学合理的修复方案。  
国家公园管理机构应当采取措施，加强对外来物种入侵的防范和应对，维护生态系统安全，提高生态系统质量。

**Article 26** The restoration of damaged natural ecosystems, the connection of ecological corridors, and the restoration of important habitats within the national park area shall adhere to the principle of natural restoration as the primary approach; where artificial restoration activities such as population regulation and tree species renewal are truly necessary, opinions from relevant parties shall be extensively solicited, and a scientific and reasonable restoration plan shall be formulated in accordance with relevant provisions.  
The national park management agency shall take measures to strengthen the prevention of and response to the invasion of alien species, maintain the security of the ecosystem, and improve the quality of the ecosystem.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　除下列活动外，国家公园核心保护区内禁止人为活动：  
（一）为保护国家公园开展的调查监测、生态修复、管护巡护等活动，科研观测、基础测绘、文物保护、防灾减灾、应急救援活动，以及国家机关依法履行执法职责确需开展的活动；  
（二）原有居民必要的生产生活活动，以及确需保留、无法避让的已有重要基础设施的运行、维护；  
（三）为维护国家安全、实施国家重大战略和重大基础设施建设确需开展的活动；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定或者国务院批准的其他活动。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于审理矿产资源纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-4" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理矿产资源纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第4条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 27** Except for the following activities, human activities are prohibited in the core protection zone of a national park:  
(1) Activities such as investigation and monitoring, ecological restoration, management and patrol carried out for the purpose of protecting the national park, scientific research and observation, basic surveying and mapping, cultural relic protection, disaster prevention and mitigation, emergency rescue activities, and activities truly necessary for state organs to perform their law enforcement duties according to law;  
(2) Necessary production and living activities of original residents, and the operation and maintenance of existing important infrastructure that truly needs to be retained and cannot be avoided;  
(3) Activities truly necessary for maintaining national security, implementing major national strategies, and constructing major infrastructure;  
(4) Other activities stipulated by laws, administrative regulations, or approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家公园一般控制区内仅允许开展下列人为活动：  
（一）核心保护区允许开展的活动；  
（二）符合国土空间规划且无法避让的重要基础设施的建设、运行和维护；  
（三）古生物化石调查发掘，基础地质调查，战略性矿产资源远景调查和规定范围内的战略性矿产资源勘查；  
（四）科普宣传、生态旅游、教育文化体育等公共服务活动；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定或者国务院批准的其他活动。

**Article 28** Only the following human activities are permitted in the general control zone of a national park:  
(1) Activities permitted in the core protection zone;  
(2) Construction, operation, and maintenance of important infrastructure that conforms to the overall territorial space plan and cannot be avoided;  
(3) Investigation and excavation of paleontological fossils, basic geological survey, prospective survey of strategic mineral resources, and exploration of strategic mineral resources within the prescribed scope;  
(4) Public service activities such as science popularization, ecological tourism, education, culture, and sports;  
(5) Other activities stipulated by laws, administrative regulations, or approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家公园区域内原有居民的生产生活活动，应当以不超出现有规模和利用强度、合理改善生产生活条件为前提。  
根据保护和管理需要，国家公园区域内原有居民确有必要迁出的，由有关地方人民政府妥善安置。

**Article 29** The production and living activities of original residents within the national park area shall be carried out on the premise of not exceeding the existing scale and utilization intensity and reasonably improving production and living conditions.  
Where, according to the needs of protection and management, it is truly necessary for original residents within the national park area to relocate, the relevant local people's government shall make proper arrangements.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　在国家公园区域内开展[本法第二十七条](#art-27)、第二十八条规定的活动，依法需要办理相关手续的，有关单位和个人应当依法办理；涉及建设活动的，有关主管部门还应当征求国务院国家公园主管部门或者国家公园管理机构的意见。  
在国家公园区域内开展[本法第二十七条](#art-27)、第二十八条规定的活动，应当采取必要措施，避免或者减少对自然生态系统、生态廊道、重要栖息地以及自然和人文景观的不利影响。国家公园管理机构应当根据实际情况和需要，明确开展相关活动的边界、强度以及应当采取的保护性措施等。

**Article 30** Where activities specified in Articles 27 and 28 of this Law are carried out within the national park area and relevant procedures are required according to law, the relevant units and individuals shall go through such procedures according to law; where construction activities are involved, the relevant competent department shall also solicit opinions from the national park authority under the State Council or the national park management agency.  
Where activities specified in Articles 27 and 28 of this Law are carried out within the national park area, necessary measures shall be taken to avoid or reduce adverse impacts on natural ecosystems, ecological corridors, important habitats, and natural and cultural landscapes. The national park management agency shall, according to the actual situation and needs, specify the boundaries, intensity, and protective measures to be taken for carrying out relevant activities.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对国家公园内自然生态过程、保护对象生息繁衍具有明显季节性变化规律的区域，经科学论证，在不损害生态功能的前提下，可以实行季节性差别管控措施。  
国家公园总体规划可以对国家公园一般控制区规定差别化管控措施。

**Article 31** For areas within a national park where natural ecological processes and the reproduction of protected objects have obvious seasonal variation patterns, after scientific demonstration, seasonal differential control measures may be implemented, provided that ecological functions are not impaired.  
The overall plan for a national park may provide for differential control measures for the general control zone of the national park.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院有关部门定期对国家公园内自然资源和生态环境状况等进行科学评估，并依法公布。

**Article 32** The relevant departments of the State Council shall periodically conduct scientific assessments of the status of natural resources and the ecological environment within the national park, and publish the results according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家公园管理机构应当建立日常巡护制度，合理配备巡护人员和巡护装备，加强巡护站点建设。巡护人员应当观察和记录主要保护对象生境状况及其变化情况，对违反国家公园保护和管理规定的行为予以劝阻、制止并及时报告。

**Article 33** The national park management agency shall establish a daily patrol system, reasonably allocate patrol personnel and equipment, and strengthen the construction of patrol stations. Patrol personnel shall observe and record the habitat status of major protected objects and changes thereof, and shall advise against, stop, and promptly report activities that violate the provisions on national park protection and management.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家公园管理机构应当制定国家公园突发事件应急预案，加强与国家公园所在地地方人民政府的协作配合，做好国家公园防灾减灾、安全管理和应急保障相关工作，提升应急保障能力。

**Article 34** The national park management agency shall formulate emergency response plans for national park emergencies, strengthen coordination and cooperation with the local people's government where the national park is located, and properly carry out relevant work on disaster prevention and mitigation, safety management, and emergency support for the national park, so as to enhance emergency support capabilities.

### 第四章　参与和共享

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家鼓励和支持公众参与国家公园保护，开展多种形式的自然和生态环境保护教育，培育全社会热爱自然、保护生态环境的意识。

**Article 35** The State encourages and supports public participation in the protection of national parks, conducts various forms of education on the protection of nature and the ecological environment, and cultivates an awareness in the whole society of loving nature and protecting the ecological environment.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家公园管理机构应当与国家公园所在地地方人民政府加强协作配合，与国家公园区域内原有居民、农村集体经济组织以及国家公园周边居民、企业等加强沟通协商，指导、支持其积极参与国家公园的保护，提供与国家公园保护目标相一致的生态产品和服务。

**Article 36** The national park management agency shall strengthen coordination and cooperation with the local people's government where the national park is located, strengthen communication and consultation with original residents, rural collective economic organizations within the national park area, and residents and enterprises around the national park, guide and support their active participation in the protection of the national park, and provide ecological products and services consistent with the protection objectives of the national park.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家公园所在地地方人民政府可以与国家公园管理机构协作，按照与国家公园保护目标相协调的要求，共同保护国家公园周边自然资源，依据国土空间规划合理建设周边社区。

**Article 37** The local people's government where the national park is located may cooperate with the national park management agency to jointly protect natural resources around the national park and rationally build surrounding communities in accordance with the overall territorial space plan, in a manner coordinated with the protection objectives of the national park.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家公园管理机构根据生态保护需要设立的生态管护岗位应当优先聘用当地居民。

**Article 38** Ecological management posts established by the national park management agency according to the needs of ecological protection shall give priority to employing local residents.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　对划入国家公园区域内的集体所有土地及其附属资源，应当依法维护权利人的权益，引导相关利益主体通过多元化方式参与国家公园建设和保护。

**Article 39** For collectively-owned land and its attached resources included within the national park area, the rights and interests of the right holders shall be protected according to law, and relevant stakeholders shall be guided to participate in the construction and protection of the national park through diversified means.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家公园管理机构应当在确保生态得到保护的前提下，完善国家公园公共服务体系，提升公共服务功能。  
国家公园一般控制区依据国土空间规划可以划定适当区域，设置必要的辅助设施设备，为开展相关科学研究、科普宣传、生态旅游、教育文化体育等公共服务活动提供支持。  
国家公园管理机构应当加强国家公园访客管理和服务，合理确定访客容量，明确访客行为规范，按照国家有关规定提供必要的无障碍服务，完善访客安全保障和紧急救助等相关机制。  
国家公园区域内不得开设与保护目标不一致的参观、旅游等项目。

**Article 40** The national park management agency shall, on the premise of ensuring ecological protection, improve the public service system of the national park and enhance public service functions.  
In the general control zone of a national park, appropriate areas may be delineated in accordance with the overall territorial space plan, and necessary auxiliary facilities and equipment may be installed to support the carrying out of public service activities such as relevant scientific research, science popularization, ecological tourism, education, culture, and sports.  
The national park management agency shall strengthen visitor management and services, reasonably determine visitor capacity, specify visitor behavior norms, provide necessary barrier-free services in accordance with relevant state provisions, and improve related mechanisms such as visitor safety guarantees and emergency rescue.  
No visits, tourism, or other projects inconsistent with the protection objectives shall be established within the national park area.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　在国家公园区域内开展经营性服务的，可以由国家公园管理机构以竞争性方式选择服务提供者。具体办法由国务院国家公园主管部门会同有关部门制定。  
鼓励国家公园区域内原有居民参与前款规定的服务。

**Article 41** If operational services are provided within a national park area, the national park management authority may select service providers through competitive means. The specific measures shall be formulated by the national park competent department of the State Council together with relevant departments.  
Original residents within the national park area shall be encouraged to participate in the services prescribed in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家公园区域内利用公共资源建设的景区的门票以及景区内的游览场所、交通工具等另行收费项目，实行政府定价或者政府指导价。  
前款规定的景区应当按照国家有关规定，对残疾人、老年人、未成年人、军人、消防救援人员等特殊群体实行免票或者优惠票价。鼓励面向公众设立国家公园免费开放日。

**Article 42** The ticket prices of scenic spots built with public resources within a national park area, as well as separately charged items such as visiting sites and means of transport within the scenic spots, shall be subject to government pricing or government-guided pricing.  
The scenic spots prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, provide free tickets or preferential ticket prices for special groups such as persons with disabilities, the elderly, minors, military personnel, and fire and rescue personnel. The establishment of free open days of national parks for the public shall be encouraged.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家鼓励和支持志愿者、志愿服务组织和其他组织依法开展与国家公园有关的志愿服务活动。  
国家公园管理机构可以依法招募志愿者，成立志愿服务队伍。  
国家公园管理机构应当做好志愿者培训、管理等工作，为志愿者提供必要的工作条件和安全保障措施，维护志愿者的合法权益。

**Article 43** The State encourages and supports volunteers, volunteer service organizations, and other organizations to carry out volunteer service activities related to national parks according to law.  
The national park management authority may recruit volunteers and establish volunteer service teams according to law.  
The national park management authority shall properly conduct volunteer training and management, provide necessary working conditions and safety protection measures for volunteers, and safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of volunteers.

### 第五章　保障和监督

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家建立以财政投入为主的多元化国家公园资金保障制度。国务院和有关地方人民政府应当按照财政事权与支出责任划分原则将所需经费列入本级预算。

**Article 44** The State shall establish a diversified national park fund guarantee system mainly based on financial input. The State Council and relevant local people's governments shall, in accordance with the principle of division of financial powers and expenditure responsibilities, include the required funds in their respective budgets.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家鼓励企业事业单位、社会组织和个人等社会力量通过设立基金、捐赠、资助等方式，为国家公园建设提供支持。

**Article 45** The State encourages enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and individuals and other social forces to provide support for the construction of national parks through means such as establishing funds, making donations, and providing financial assistance.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国家鼓励国家公园特色生态产品区域公用品牌创建，支持国家公园生态保护形成的生态资源权益依法参与市场交易，拓展国家公园生态产品价值实现机制。

**Article 46** The State encourages the creation of regional public brands for characteristic ecological products of national parks, supports the participation of ecological resource rights formed by ecological protection of national parks in market transactions according to law, and expands the value realization mechanism of ecological products of national parks.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家健全国家公园生态保护补偿机制，根据国家公园规模和管护成效等合理确定财政转移支付规模，并推进国家公园市场化生态保护补偿工作。

**Article 47** The State shall improve the ecological protection compensation mechanism for national parks, reasonably determine the scale of fiscal transfer payments based on the scale and management effectiveness of national parks, and promote the market-based ecological protection compensation work for national parks.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　因保护国家公园区域内受法律保护的野生动物和其他致害严重的陆生野生动物，造成人员伤亡、农作物或者其他财产损失的，依法由有关地方人民政府给予补偿，相关经费按照规定由中央财政予以补助。  
有关地方人民政府可以推动保险机构开展野生动物致害赔偿保险业务。

**Article 48** Where personal injury or death, or damage to crops or other property is caused due to the protection of wild animals protected by law and other terrestrial wild animals that cause serious harm within a national park area, compensation shall be given by the relevant local people's government according to law, and the relevant funds shall be subsidized by the central finance in accordance with regulations.  
The relevant local people's government may promote insurance institutions to carry out wildlife damage compensation insurance business.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家公园管理机构、其他有关部门应当依据职责加强对国家公园保护情况的监督检查，依法查处违法行为；对不属于本部门执法权限的案件线索，应当及时移交有关部门处理。  
国家公园管理机构、其他有关部门依法履行监督检查职责，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）进行现场检查；  
（二）对违法活动涉及的野生动植物等自然资源进行检验、检测、抽样取证；  
（三）查阅、复制有关文件、资料，对可能被转移、销毁、隐匿或者篡改的文件、资料予以封存；  
（四）查封、扣押涉嫌违法活动的场所、设施或者财物。  
监督检查人员开展监督检查，应当主动出示执法证件。被检查单位和个人应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻碍。  
对有关国家机关依法在国家公园履行法定职责，国家公园管理机构应当提供必要的便利。

**Article 49** The national park management authority and other relevant departments shall, based on their duties, strengthen supervision and inspection of the protection of national parks and investigate and deal with illegal acts according to law; for case clues that are not within their law enforcement authority, they shall promptly transfer them to the relevant departments for handling.  
When the national park management authority and other relevant departments perform their supervision and inspection duties according to law, they have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) Carry out on-site inspections;  
(2) Conduct inspection, testing, and sampling of natural resources such as wild animals and plants involved in illegal activities;  
(3) Consult and copy relevant documents and materials, and seal up documents and materials that may be transferred, destroyed, concealed, or altered;  
(4) Seal up or seize places, facilities, or property suspected of involvement in illegal activities.  
Supervisory and inspection personnel shall proactively show their law enforcement certificates when conducting supervision and inspection. The units and individuals being inspected shall cooperate and shall not refuse or obstruct.  
Where relevant state organs perform their statutory duties within a national park according to law, the national park management authority shall provide necessary facilitation.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　对违反国家公园保护和管理规定的行为，任何单位和个人有权向国家公园管理机构或者其他有关部门举报，接到举报的部门应当依法及时处理。

**Article 50** For acts violating the provisions on protection and management of national parks, any unit or individual has the right to report to the national park management authority or other relevant departments, and the department that receives the report shall handle it promptly according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国家建立国家公园保护和管理成效考核评价制度，考核评价结果纳入相关考核评价体系。  
对国家公园保护工作不力、问题突出的地方人民政府，上级人民政府可以对其负责人进行约谈，被约谈的地方人民政府应当采取措施及时整改。

**Article 51** The State shall establish an assessment and evaluation system for the effectiveness of national park protection and management, and the results of assessment and evaluation shall be included in the relevant assessment and evaluation systems.  
Where a local people's government performs poorly in national park protection work and has prominent problems, the superior people's government may conduct an interview with its responsible person, and the local people's government that is interviewed shall take measures to make timely rectification.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家公园管理机构应当依法及时公开国家公园保护和管理等工作情况，自觉接受社会监督和舆论监督。

**Article 52** The national park management authority shall, according to law, promptly disclose the work on national park protection and management, and voluntarily accept social supervision and public opinion supervision.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家公园管理机构、其他有关部门工作人员在国家公园保护和管理等工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 53** If staff members of the national park management authority or other relevant departments abuse their powers, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractices for personal gain in the protection and management of national parks, they shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　损毁、涂改、遮挡或者擅自拆除、移动国家公园界线标志的，由国家公园管理机构责令停止违法行为、采取补救措施，可以处二万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 54** Whoever damages, defaces, obscures, or without authorization removes or moves boundary markers of a national park shall be ordered by the national park management authority to cease the illegal act and take remedial measures, and may be fined not more than 20,000 yuan; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　在国家公园核心保护区内开展[本法第二十七条](#art-27)规定以外的活动的，责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，可以处十万元以下的罚款；属于违法修筑设施或者进行工程建设的，责令限期拆除、恢复原状，处十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；造成生态破坏的，责令限期修复或者采取其他补救措施，处一百万元以上五百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 55** Whoever carries out activities other than those prescribed in Article 27 of this Law within the core protection area of a national park shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, confiscate illegal gains, and may be fined not more than 100,000 yuan; if the act involves illegally constructing facilities or carrying out engineering construction, the person shall be ordered to demolish within a time limit and restore the original state, and be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if ecological damage is caused, the person shall be ordered to restore within a time limit or take other remedial measures, and be fined not less than 1,000,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　在国家公园一般控制区内开展[本法第二十八条](#art-28)规定以外的活动的，责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，可以处五万元以下的罚款；属于违法修筑设施或者进行工程建设的，责令限期拆除、恢复原状，处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；造成生态破坏的，责令限期修复或者采取其他补救措施，处五十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 56** Whoever carries out activities other than those prescribed in Article 28 of this Law within the general control area of a national park shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, confiscate illegal gains, and may be fined not more than 50,000 yuan; if the act involves illegally constructing facilities or carrying out engineering construction, the person shall be ordered to demolish within a time limit and restore the original state, and be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if ecological damage is caused, the person shall be ordered to restore within a time limit or take other remedial measures, and be fined not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国家公园区域内原有居民违反[本法第二十九条](#art-29)第一款规定开展生产生活活动的，责令改正，根据情节轻重可以处二千元以下的罚款。

**Article 57** If original residents within a national park area carry out production and living activities in violation of the provisions of Paragraph 1 of Article 29 of this Law, they shall be ordered to make corrections, and may be fined not more than 2,000 yuan depending on the severity of the circumstances.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　在国家公园区域内开展[本法第二十七条](#art-27)、第二十八条规定的活动，未采取必要措施避免或者减少对自然生态系统、生态廊道、重要栖息地以及自然和人文景观的不利影响的，责令改正，可以处十万元以下的罚款；造成生态破坏的，责令限期修复或者采取其他补救措施，相关活动在核心保护区开展的，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，在一般控制区开展的，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 58** If activities prescribed in Articles 27 and 28 of this Law are carried out within a national park area without taking necessary measures to avoid or reduce adverse impacts on natural ecosystems, ecological corridors, important habitats, and natural and cultural landscapes, the person shall be ordered to make corrections and may be fined not more than 100,000 yuan; if ecological damage is caused, the person shall be ordered to restore within a time limit or take other remedial measures, and if the relevant activities are carried out in the core protection area, the person shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and if carried out in the general control area, the person shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国家公园区域内的单位、个人以及进入国家公园的人员不服从国家公园管理机构管理的，国家公园管理机构有权予以劝阻、制止；构成扰乱公共秩序等违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 59** If units, individuals within a national park area, or persons entering the national park do not submit to the management of the national park management authority, the national park management authority has the authority to dissuade and stop them; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration such as disturbing public order, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　[本法第五十五条](#art-55)、第五十六条、第五十七条、第五十八条规定的违法行为，按照有关规定由国家公园管理机构处罚，并与国家公园所在地地方人民政府有关部门建立执法协作机制。

**Article 60** The illegal acts prescribed in Articles 55, 56, 57, and 58 of this Law shall be punished by the national park management authority in accordance with relevant provisions, and the authority shall establish a law enforcement coordination mechanism with the relevant departments of the local people's government where the national park is located.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反本法规定，破坏自然资源或者污染环境、破坏生态的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 61** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law by destroying natural resources, polluting the environment, or damaging the ecology shall bear civil liability according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　国家公园所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会及其常务委员会可以依据本法，结合本地实际，对国家公园生态环境保护和修复及相关活动管理等事项制定具体办法。  
国家公园所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会及其常务委员会可以就国家公园保护和管理开展区域协同立法。

**Article 62** The people's congresses and their standing committees of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government where the national parks are located may, pursuant to this Law and in light of local actual conditions, formulate specific measures for matters such as the ecological environmental protection and restoration of national parks and the management of related activities.  
The people's congresses and their standing committees of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government where the national parks are located may carry out regional collaborative legislation on the protection and management of national parks.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本法自2026年1月1日起施行。

**Article 63** This Law shall come into force on January 1, 2026.
