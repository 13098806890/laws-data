### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为实施科教兴国战略，促进民办教育事业的健康发展，维护民办学校和受教育者的合法权益，根据宪法和教育法制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution and the Education Law for the purpose of implementing the strategy of rejuvenating the country through science and education, promoting the healthy development of private education, and safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of private schools and the educated.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家机构以外的社会组织或者个人，利用非国家财政性经费，面向社会举办学校及其他教育机构的活动，适用本法。本法未作规定的，依照教育法和其他有关教育法律执行。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the activities of social organizations or individuals other than state organs that use non-state financial funds to establish schools or other educational institutions for the public. Matters not provided for in this Law shall be handled in accordance with the Education Law and other relevant education laws.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　民办教育事业属于公益性事业，是社会主义教育事业的组成部分。  
国家对民办教育实行积极鼓励、大力支持、正确引导、依法管理的方针。  
各级人民政府应当将民办教育事业纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。

**Article 3** Private education is a public welfare undertaking and is an integral part of the socialist education.  
The state shall adopt the policy of active encouragement, vigorous support, correct guidance, and lawful administration of private education.  
People's governments at all levels shall incorporate private education into the national economic and social development plan.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　民办学校应当遵守法律、法规，贯彻国家的教育方针，保证教育质量，致力于培养社会主义建设事业的各类人才。  
民办学校应当贯彻教育与宗教相分离的原则。任何组织和个人不得利用宗教进行妨碍国家教育制度的活动。

**Article 4** Private schools shall abide by laws and regulations, implement the state's educational policy, ensure the quality of education, and devote themselves to cultivating various types of talents for the socialist construction.  
Private schools shall implement the principle of separation of education from religion. No organization or individual may use religion to engage in activities that impede the state's educational system.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　民办学校与公办学校具有同等的法律地位，国家保障民办学校的办学自主权。  
国家保障民办学校举办者、校长、教职工和受教育者的合法权益。

**Article 5** Private schools shall have equal legal status with public schools, and the state shall ensure the autonomy of private schools in school operation.  
The state shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of the sponsors, principals, faculty, staff, and the educated of private schools.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励捐资办学。  
国家对为发展民办教育事业做出突出贡献的组织和个人，给予奖励和表彰。

**Article 6** The state encourages donation for the establishment of schools.  
The state shall give awards and commendations to organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the development of private education.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院教育行政部门负责全国民办教育工作的统筹规划、综合协调和宏观管理。  
国务院人力资源社会保障行政部门及其他有关部门在国务院规定的职责范围内分别负责有关的民办教育工作。

**Article 7** The administrative department of education under the State Council shall be responsible for the overall planning, comprehensive coordination, and macro-management of private education nationwide.  
The administrative department of human resources and social security under the State Council and other relevant departments shall, within the scope of their duties defined by the State Council, be respectively responsible for related private education work.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上地方各级人民政府教育行政部门主管本行政区域内的民办教育工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府人力资源社会保障行政部门及其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内，分别负责有关的民办教育工作。

**Article 8** The administrative department of education of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of private education within their respective administrative regions.  
The administrative department of human resources and social security of the local people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall, within their respective duties, be responsible for related private education work.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　民办学校中的中国共产党基层组织，按照中国共产党章程的规定开展党的活动，加强党的建设。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/公司与破产/最高人民法院关于对因资不抵债无法继续办学被终止的民办学校如何组织清算问题的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于对因资不抵债无法继续办学被终止的民办学校如何组织清算问题的批复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 9** The primary organizations of the Communist Party of China in private schools shall carry out party activities and strengthen party building in accordance with the provisions of the Constitution of the Communist Party of China.

### 第二章　设立

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　举办民办学校的社会组织，应当具有法人资格。  
举办民办学校的个人，应当具有政治权利和完全民事行为能力。  
民办学校应当具备法人条件。

**Article 10** A social organization that sponsors a private school shall have the status of a legal person.  
An individual who sponsors a private school shall have political rights and full civil capacity.  
A private school shall meet the conditions for being a legal person.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　设立民办学校应当符合当地教育发展的需求，具备教育法和其他有关法律、法规规定的条件。  
民办学校的设置标准参照同级同类公办学校的设置标准执行。

**Article 11** The establishment of a private school shall meet the needs of local educational development and satisfy the conditions stipulated in the Education Law and other relevant laws and regulations.  
The establishment standards of private schools shall be implemented with reference to the establishment standards of public schools of the same level and type.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　举办实施学历教育、学前教育、自学考试助学及其他文化教育的民办学校，由县级以上人民政府教育行政部门按照国家规定的权限审批；举办实施以职业技能为主的职业资格培训、职业技能培训的民办学校，由县级以上人民政府人力资源社会保障行政部门按照国家规定的权限审批，并抄送同级教育行政部门备案。

**Article 12** The establishment of a private school that provides education for academic qualifications, preschool education, self-study examination assistance, or other cultural education shall be examined and approved by the administrative department of education of the people's government at or above the county level within the limits of authority prescribed by the state; the establishment of a private school that provides vocational qualification training or vocational skill training mainly based on vocational skills shall be examined and approved by the administrative department of human resources and social security of the people's government at or above the county level within the limits of authority prescribed by the state, and shall be copied to the administrative department of education at the same level for record.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　申请筹设民办学校，举办者应当向审批机关提交下列材料：  
（一）申办报告，内容应当主要包括：举办者、培养目标、办学规模、办学层次、办学形式、办学条件、内部管理体制、经费筹措与管理使用等；  
（二）举办者的姓名、住址或者名称、地址；  
（三）资产来源、资金数额及有效证明文件，并载明产权；  
（四）属捐赠性质的校产须提交捐赠协议，载明捐赠人的姓名、所捐资产的数额、用途和管理方法及相关有效证明文件。

**Article 13** When applying for the preparatory establishment of a private school, the sponsor shall submit the following materials to the examination and approval authority:  
(1) A sponsorship report, the contents of which shall mainly include: the sponsor, training objectives, scale of the school, level of education, form of education, conditions for running the school, internal management system, and the raising, management, and use of funds;  
(2) The name and address of the sponsor or the name and address of the organization;  
(3) The source of assets, the amount of funds, and valid certification documents, specifying the property rights;  
(4) For school assets of a donative nature, a donation agreement shall be submitted, specifying the donor's name, the amount of donated assets, the purpose and management method, and relevant valid certification documents.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　审批机关应当自受理筹设民办学校的申请之日起三十日内以书面形式作出是否同意的决定。  
同意筹设的，发给筹设批准书。不同意筹设的，应当说明理由。  
筹设期不得超过三年。超过三年的，举办者应当重新申报。

**Article 14** The examination and approval authority shall, within thirty days from the date of accepting the application for the preparatory establishment of a private school, make a decision whether to agree in writing.  
If it agrees to the preparatory establishment, it shall issue a preparatory establishment approval certificate. If it does not agree, it shall state the reasons.  
The preparatory establishment period shall not exceed three years. If it exceeds three years, the sponsor shall re-apply.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　申请正式设立民办学校的，举办者应当向审批机关提交下列材料：  
（一）筹设批准书；  
（二）筹设情况报告；  
（三）学校章程、首届学校理事会、董事会或者其他决策机构组成人员名单；  
（四）学校资产的有效证明文件；  
（五）校长、教师、财会人员的资格证明文件。

**Article 15** When applying for the formal establishment of a private school, the sponsor shall submit the following materials to the examination and approval authority:  
(1) The preparatory establishment approval certificate;  
(2) A report on the preparatory establishment;  
(3) The school charter and the list of members of the first school council, board of directors, or other decision-making bodies;  
(4) Valid certification documents of the school's assets;  
(5) Qualification certification documents of the principal, teachers, and financial and accounting personnel.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　具备办学条件，达到设置标准的，可以直接申请正式设立，并应当提交[本法第十三条](#art-13)和第十五条（三）、（四）、（五）项规定的材料。

**Article 16** If the conditions for running a school are met and the establishment standards are reached, an application may be directly made for formal establishment, and the materials specified in Article 13 and subparagraphs (3), (4), and (5) of Article 15 of this Law shall be submitted.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　申请正式设立民办学校的，审批机关应当自受理之日起三个月内以书面形式作出是否批准的决定，并送达申请人；其中申请正式设立民办高等学校的，审批机关也可以自受理之日起六个月内以书面形式作出是否批准的决定，并送达申请人。

**Article 17** For an application for the formal establishment of a private school, the examination and approval authority shall, within three months from the date of acceptance, make a decision whether to approve in writing and deliver it to the applicant; for an application for the formal establishment of a private institution of higher learning, the examination and approval authority may, within six months from the date of acceptance, make a decision whether to approve in writing and deliver it to the applicant.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　审批机关对批准正式设立的民办学校发给办学许可证。  
审批机关对不批准正式设立的，应当说明理由。

**Article 18** The examination and approval authority shall issue a school-running license to a private school that is approved for formal establishment.  
If the examination and approval authority does not approve the formal establishment, it shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　民办学校的举办者可以自主选择设立非营利性或者营利性民办学校。但是，不得设立实施义务教育的营利性民办学校。  
非营利性民办学校的举办者不得取得办学收益，学校的办学结余全部用于办学。  
营利性民办学校的举办者可以取得办学收益，学校的办学结余依照公司法等有关法律、行政法规的规定处理。  
民办学校取得办学许可证后，进行法人登记，登记机关应当依法予以办理。

**Article 19** The sponsor of a private school may independently choose to establish a non-profit or for-profit private school. However, no for-profit private school may be established for compulsory education.  
The sponsor of a non-profit private school shall not obtain school-running profits, and all the school's surplus shall be used for school operation.  
The sponsor of a for-profit private school may obtain school-running profits, and the school's surplus shall be handled in accordance with the Company Law and other relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
After obtaining the school-running license, the private school shall undergo legal person registration, and the registration authority shall handle it according to law.

### 第三章　学校的组织与活动

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　民办学校应当设立学校理事会、董事会或者其他形式的决策机构并建立相应的监督机制。  
民办学校的举办者根据学校章程规定的权限和程序参与学校的办学和管理。

**Article 20** A private school shall establish a school council, board of directors, or other form of decision-making body and establish corresponding supervision mechanisms.  
The sponsor of a private school shall participate in the school's operation and management in accordance with the authority and procedures stipulated in the school's charter.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　学校理事会或者董事会由举办者或者其代表、校长、教职工代表等人员组成。其中三分之一以上的理事或者董事应当具有五年以上教育教学经验。  
学校理事会或者董事会由五人以上组成，设理事长或者董事长一人。理事长、理事或者董事长、董事名单报审批机关备案。

**Article 21** A school council or board of directors shall be composed of the sponsor or its representative, the principal, representatives of the faculty and staff, and other persons. At least one third of the council members or directors shall have five or more years of experience in education and teaching.  
A school council or board of directors shall consist of five or more members, and shall have one chairperson of the council or chairperson of the board. The list of the chairperson and other members of the council or the chairperson and other directors of the board shall be submitted to the approving authority for recordation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　学校理事会或者董事会行使下列职权：  
（一）聘任和解聘校长；  
（二）修改学校章程和制定学校的规章制度；  
（三）制定发展规划，批准年度工作计划；  
（四）筹集办学经费，审核预算、决算；  
（五）决定教职工的编制定额和工资标准；  
（六）决定学校的分立、合并、终止；  
（七）决定其他重大事项。  
其他形式决策机构的职权参照本条规定执行。

**Article 22** A school council or board of directors shall exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) appoint and dismiss the principal;  
(2) amend the school charter and formulate the school's rules and regulations;  
(3) formulate development plans and approve annual work plans;  
(4) raise funds for school operation, and examine and approve budgets and final accounts;  
(5) decide on the staffing quotas and salary standards for faculty and staff;  
(6) decide on the division, merger, or termination of the school;  
(7) decide on other major matters.  
The functions and powers of other forms of decision-making bodies shall be exercised by reference to the provisions of this Article.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　民办学校的法定代表人由理事长、董事长或者校长担任。

**Article 23** The legal representative of a private school shall be the chairperson of the council, the chairperson of the board, or the principal.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　民办学校参照同级同类公办学校校长任职的条件聘任校长，年龄可以适当放宽。

**Article 24** A private school shall appoint the principal by reference to the qualifications for principals of public schools of the same level and category, and the age requirement may be appropriately relaxed.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　民办学校校长负责学校的教育教学和行政管理工作，行使下列职权：  
（一）执行学校理事会、董事会或者其他形式决策机构的决定；  
（二）实施发展规划，拟订年度工作计划、财务预算和学校规章制度；  
（三）聘任和解聘学校工作人员，实施奖惩；  
（四）组织教育教学、科学研究活动，保证教育教学质量；  
（五）负责学校日常管理工作；  
（六）学校理事会、董事会或者其他形式决策机构的其他授权。

**Article 25** The principal of a private school shall be responsible for the education, teaching and administrative work of the school, and shall exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) implement the decisions of the school council, board of directors or other forms of decision-making body;  
(2) implement development plans, and draft annual work plans, financial budgets, and school rules and regulations;  
(3) appoint and dismiss school staff, and implement rewards and punishments;  
(4) organize education, teaching and scientific research activities, and ensure the quality of education and teaching;  
(5) be responsible for the daily management of the school;  
(6) other authorizations granted by the school council, board of directors or other forms of decision-making body.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　民办学校对招收的学生，根据其类别、修业年限、学业成绩，可以根据国家有关规定发给学历证书、结业证书或者培训合格证书。  
对接受职业技能培训的学生，经备案的职业技能鉴定机构鉴定合格的，可以发给国家职业资格证书。

**Article 26** A private school may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, issue diplomas, completion certificates or training certificates to the students it enrolls, based on their category, length of schooling and academic performance.  
For students receiving vocational skill training, who pass the evaluation conducted by a registered vocational skill evaluation institution, the school may issue national vocational qualification certificates.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　民办学校依法通过以教师为主体的教职工代表大会等形式，保障教职工参与民主管理和监督。  
民办学校的教师和其他工作人员，有权依照工会法，建立工会组织，维护其合法权益。

**Article 27** A private school shall, according to law, ensure the participation of faculty and staff in democratic management and supervision through forms such as the faculty and staff congress with teachers as the main body.  
Teachers and other staff of a private school shall have the right, in accordance with the Trade Union Law, to establish trade union organizations to protect their lawful rights and interests.

### 第四章　教师与受教育者

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　民办学校的教师、受教育者与公办学校的教师、受教育者具有同等的法律地位。

**Article 28** Teachers and educatees of private schools shall have the same legal status as teachers and educatees of public schools.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　民办学校聘任的教师，应当具有国家规定的任教资格。

**Article 29** Teachers employed by private schools shall have the teaching qualifications prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　民办学校应当对教师进行思想品德教育和业务培训。

**Article 30** A private school shall provide ideological and moral education and professional training for teachers.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　民办学校应当依法保障教职工的工资、福利待遇和其他合法权益，并为教职工缴纳社会保险费。  
国家鼓励民办学校按照国家规定为教职工办理补充养老保险。

**Article 31** A private school shall, according to law, guarantee the salaries, welfare benefits and other lawful rights and interests of its faculty and staff, and pay social insurance premiums for them.  
The State encourages private schools to arrange supplementary endowment insurance for faculty and staff in accordance with the provisions of the State.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　民办学校教职工在业务培训、职务聘任、教龄和工龄计算、表彰奖励、社会活动等方面依法享有与公办学校教职工同等权利。

**Article 32** Faculty and staff of private schools shall, according to law, enjoy the same rights as those of public schools in terms of professional training, post appointment, calculation of teaching years and length of service, commendation and reward, and social activities.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　民办学校依法保障受教育者的合法权益。  
民办学校按照国家规定建立学籍管理制度，对受教育者实施奖励或者处分。

**Article 33** A private school shall, according to law, protect the lawful rights and interests of educatees.  
A private school shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State, establish a student status management system, and impose rewards or sanctions on educatees.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　民办学校的受教育者在升学、就业、社会优待以及参加先进评选等方面享有与同级同类公办学校的受教育者同等权利。

**Article 34** Educatees of private schools shall enjoy the same rights as educatees of public schools of the same level and category in terms of school admission, employment, social preferences, and participation in advanced selection.

### 第五章　学校资产与财务管理

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　民办学校应当依法建立财务、会计制度和资产管理制度，并按照国家有关规定设置会计帐簿。

**Article 35** A private school shall, according to law, establish financial and accounting systems and asset management systems, and shall set up account books in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　民办学校对举办者投入民办学校的资产、国有资产、受赠的财产以及办学积累，享有法人财产权。

**Article 36** A private school shall enjoy the right of a legal person over the assets contributed by the sponsor, state-owned assets, donated property, and accumulated assets from school operation.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　民办学校存续期间，所有资产由民办学校依法管理和使用，任何组织和个人不得侵占。  
任何组织和个人都不得违反法律、法规向民办教育机构收取任何费用。

**Article 37** During the period of existence of a private school, all assets shall be managed and used by the private school according to law, and no organization or individual may encroach upon them.  
No organization or individual may, in violation of laws or regulations, charge any fees from a private educational institution.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　民办学校收取费用的项目和标准根据办学成本、市场需求等因素确定，向社会公示，并接受有关主管部门的监督。  
非营利性民办学校收费的具体办法，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定；营利性民办学校的收费标准，实行市场调节，由学校自主决定。  
民办学校收取的费用应当主要用于教育教学活动、改善办学条件和保障教职工待遇。

**Article 38** The items and standards of fees charged by a private school shall be determined in light of factors such as school operation costs and market demand, shall be publicly disclosed to the public, and shall be subject to the supervision of the competent authorities.  
The specific measures for fee collection by non-profit private schools shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government; the fee standards for for-profit private schools shall be subject to market regulation and independently decided by the school.  
The fees collected by a private school shall be mainly used for education and teaching activities, improving school operation conditions, and guaranteeing the treatment of faculty and staff.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　民办学校资产的使用和财务管理受审批机关和其他有关部门的监督。  
民办学校应当在每个会计年度结束时制作财务会计报告，委托会计师事务所依法进行审计，并公布审计结果。

**Article 39** The use of assets and financial management of a private school shall be subject to the supervision of the approving authority and other relevant departments.  
A private school shall, at the end of each fiscal year, prepare financial accounting reports, entrust an accounting firm to conduct an audit according to law, and publish the audit results.

### 第六章　管理与监督

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　教育行政部门及有关部门应当对民办学校的教育教学工作、教师培训工作进行指导。

**Article 40** The education administrative department and relevant departments shall provide guidance to private schools regarding their education, teaching and teacher training work.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　教育行政部门及有关部门依法对民办学校实行督导，建立民办学校信息公示和信用档案制度，促进提高办学质量；组织或者委托社会中介组织评估办学水平和教育质量，并将评估结果向社会公布。

**Article 41** The education administrative department and relevant departments shall, according to law, supervise and guide private schools, establish a system for information disclosure and credit archives of private schools, and promote the improvement of school-running quality; organize or entrust social intermediary organizations to evaluate the school-running level and educational quality, and publish the evaluation results to the public.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　民办学校的招生简章和广告，应当报审批机关备案。

**Article 42** The enrollment brochures and advertisements of a private school shall be submitted to the examination and approval authority for archival filing.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　民办学校侵犯受教育者的合法权益，受教育者及其亲属有权向教育行政部门和其他有关部门申诉，有关部门应当及时予以处理。

**Article 43** If a private school infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of the educatees, the educatees and their relatives shall have the right to petition to the education administrative department and other relevant departments, and the relevant departments shall handle the matter in a timely manner.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家支持和鼓励社会中介组织为民办学校提供服务。

**Article 44** The State supports and encourages social intermediary organizations to provide services for private schools.

### 第七章　扶持与奖励

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上各级人民政府可以设立专项资金，用于资助民办学校的发展，奖励和表彰有突出贡献的集体和个人。

**Article 45** People's governments at or above the county level may establish special funds to subsidize the development of private schools, and to reward and commend collectives and individuals that have made outstanding contributions.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上各级人民政府可以采取购买服务、助学贷款、奖助学金和出租、转让闲置的国有资产等措施对民办学校予以扶持；对非营利性民办学校还可以采取政府补贴、基金奖励、捐资激励等扶持措施。

**Article 46** People's governments at or above the county level may take measures such as purchasing services, student loans, grants and scholarships, and leasing or transferring idle state-owned assets to support private schools; for non-profit private schools, support measures such as government subsidies, fund rewards, and donation incentives may also be adopted.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　民办学校享受国家规定的税收优惠政策；其中，非营利性民办学校享受与公办学校同等的税收优惠政策。

**Article 47** Private schools shall enjoy tax preferential policies provided by the State; among them, non-profit private schools shall enjoy the same tax preferential policies as public schools.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　民办学校依照国家有关法律、法规，可以接受公民、法人或者其他组织的捐赠。  
国家对向民办学校捐赠财产的公民、法人或者其他组织按照有关规定给予税收优惠，并予以表彰。

**Article 48** A private school may, in accordance with relevant laws and regulations of the State, accept donations from citizens, legal persons, or other organizations.  
The State shall grant tax preferences to citizens, legal persons, or other organizations that donate property to private schools in accordance with relevant provisions, and shall give them commendations.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家鼓励金融机构运用信贷手段，支持民办教育事业的发展。

**Article 49** The State encourages financial institutions to use credit measures to support the development of private education.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　人民政府委托民办学校承担义务教育任务，应当按照委托协议拨付相应的教育经费。

**Article 50** Where a people's government entrusts a private school to undertake compulsory education tasks, it shall allocate corresponding education funds in accordance with the entrustment agreement.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　新建、扩建非营利性民办学校，人民政府应当按照与公办学校同等原则，以划拨等方式给予用地优惠。新建、扩建营利性民办学校，人民政府应当按照国家规定供给土地。  
教育用地不得用于其他用途。

**Article 51** When a non-profit private school is newly built or expanded, the people's government shall, in accordance with the same principle as for public schools, provide land use preferences through allocation or other means. When a for-profit private school is newly built or expanded, the people's government shall supply land in accordance with State provisions.  
Land used for education shall not be used for other purposes.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家采取措施，支持和鼓励社会组织和个人到少数民族地区、边远贫困地区举办民办学校，发展教育事业。

**Article 52** The State shall take measures to support and encourage social organizations and individuals to establish private schools in ethnic minority areas, remote and poverty-stricken areas, so as to develop education.

### 第八章　变更与终止

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　民办学校的分立、合并，在进行财务清算后，由学校理事会或者董事会报审批机关批准。  
申请分立、合并民办学校的，审批机关应当自受理之日起三个月内以书面形式答复；其中申请分立、合并民办高等学校的，审批机关也可以自受理之日起六个月内以书面形式答复。

**Article 53** The division or merger of a private school shall be submitted to the examination and approval authority for approval by the school’s council or board of directors after financial liquidation.  
Where an application is made for the division or merger of a private school, the examination and approval authority shall give a written reply within three months from the date of acceptance; where the application is for the division or merger of a private institution of higher education, the examination and approval authority may also give a written reply within six months from the date of acceptance.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　民办学校举办者的变更，须由举办者提出，在进行财务清算后，经学校理事会或者董事会同意，报审批机关核准。

**Article 54** Any change of the sponsor of a private school shall be proposed by the sponsor, and after financial liquidation, with the consent of the school’s council or board of directors, shall be submitted to the examination and approval authority for verification and approval.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　民办学校名称、层次、类别的变更，由学校理事会或者董事会报审批机关批准。  
申请变更为其他民办学校，审批机关应当自受理之日起三个月内以书面形式答复；其中申请变更为民办高等学校的，审批机关也可以自受理之日起六个月内以书面形式答复。

**Article 55** Any change in the name, level, or category of a private school shall be submitted to the examination and approval authority for approval by the school’s council or board of directors.  
Where an application is made to convert to another private school, the examination and approval authority shall give a written reply within three months from the date of acceptance; where the application is to convert to a private institution of higher education, the examination and approval authority may also give a written reply within six months from the date of acceptance.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　民办学校有下列情形之一的，应当终止：  
（一）根据学校章程规定要求终止，并经审批机关批准的；  
（二）被吊销办学许可证的；  
（三）因资不抵债无法继续办学的。

**Article 56** A private school shall be terminated under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) It requests termination in accordance with the school’s articles of association and such termination is approved by the examination and approval authority;  
(2) Its school-running license is revoked;  
(3) It is unable to continue running due to insolvency.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　民办学校终止时，应当妥善安置在校学生。实施义务教育的民办学校终止时，审批机关应当协助学校安排学生继续就学。

**Article 57** When a private school is terminated, students enrolled in the school shall be properly arranged. When a private school implementing compulsory education is terminated, the examination and approval authority shall assist the school in arranging for students to continue their studies.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　民办学校终止时，应当依法进行财务清算。  
民办学校自己要求终止的，由民办学校组织清算；被审批机关依法撤销的，由审批机关组织清算；因资不抵债无法继续办学而被终止的，由人民法院组织清算。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/公司与破产/最高人民法院关于对因资不抵债无法继续办学被终止的民办学校如何组织清算问题的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于对因资不抵债无法继续办学被终止的民办学校如何组织清算问题的批复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 58** When a private school is terminated, financial liquidation shall be conducted according to law.  
If a private school requests termination on its own, the private school shall organize the liquidation; if it is revoked according to law by the examination and approval authority, the examination and approval authority shall organize the liquidation; if it is terminated due to inability to continue operations due to insolvency, the People's Court shall organize the liquidation.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　对民办学校的财产按照下列顺序清偿：  
（一）应退受教育者学费、杂费和其他费用；  
（二）应发教职工的工资及应缴纳的社会保险费用；  
（三）偿还其他债务。  
非营利性民办学校清偿上述债务后的剩余财产继续用于其他非营利性学校办学；营利性民办学校清偿上述债务后的剩余财产，依照公司法的有关规定处理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/公司与破产/最高人民法院关于对因资不抵债无法继续办学被终止的民办学校如何组织清算问题的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于对因资不抵债无法继续办学被终止的民办学校如何组织清算问题的批复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 59** The property of a private school shall be liquidated in the following order:  
(1) Tuition, miscellaneous fees, and other fees that shall be refunded to educatees;  
(2) Wages that shall be paid to faculty and staff and social insurance premiums that shall be paid;  
(3) Other debts that shall be repaid.  
The remaining property of a non-profit private school after the liquidation of the above debts shall continue to be used for the operation of other non-profit schools; the remaining property of a for-profit private school after the liquidation of the above debts shall be dealt with in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Company Law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　终止的民办学校，由审批机关收回办学许可证和销毁印章，并注销登记。

**Article 60** A terminated private school shall have its school-running license withdrawn and seals destroyed by the examination and approval authority, and its registration shall be cancelled.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　民办学校在教育活动中违反教育法、教师法规定的，依照教育法、教师法的有关规定给予处罚。

**Article 61** Where a private school violates the provisions of the Education Law or the Teachers Law in its educational activities, it shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Education Law and the Teachers Law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　民办学校有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府教育行政部门、人力资源社会保障行政部门或者其他有关部门责令限期改正，并予以警告；有违法所得的，退还所收费用后没收违法所得；情节严重的，责令停止招生、吊销办学许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）擅自分立、合并民办学校的；  
（二）擅自改变民办学校名称、层次、类别和举办者的；  
（三）发布虚假招生简章或者广告，骗取钱财的；  
（四）非法颁发或者伪造学历证书、结业证书、培训证书、职业资格证书的；  
（五）管理混乱严重影响教育教学，产生恶劣社会影响的；  
（六）提交虚假证明文件或者采取其他欺诈手段隐瞒重要事实骗取办学许可证的；  
（七）伪造、变造、买卖、出租、出借办学许可证的；  
（八）恶意终止办学、抽逃资金或者挪用办学经费的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国民办教育促进法实施条例.md#art-63" title="被《中华人民共和国民办教育促进法实施条例》第63条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国民办教育促进法实施条例.md#art-64" title="被《中华人民共和国民办教育促进法实施条例》第64条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 62** Where a private school commits any of the following acts, the education administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level, the human resources and social security administrative department, or other relevant departments shall order it to make corrections within a time limit and give it a warning; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated after the fees collected are refunded; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend enrollment and its school-running license shall be revoked; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) splitting or merging a private school without authorization;  
(2) changing the name, level, category, or sponsor of a private school without authorization;  
(3) issuing false enrollment brochures or advertisements to defraud money;  
(4) illegally issuing or forging academic degree certificates, completion certificates, training certificates, or vocational qualification certificates;  
(5) poor management that seriously affects education and teaching, causing adverse social impact;  
(6) providing false certification documents or using other fraudulent means to conceal important facts to obtain a school-running license;  
(7) forging, altering, buying, selling, leasing, or lending a school-running license;  
(8) maliciously terminating school operation, withdrawing capital, or misappropriating school-running funds.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　县级以上人民政府教育行政部门、人力资源社会保障行政部门或者其他有关部门有下列行为之一的，由上级机关责令其改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；造成经济损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）已受理设立申请，逾期不予答复的；  
（二）批准不符合本法规定条件申请的；  
（三）疏于管理，造成严重后果的；  
（四）违反国家有关规定收取费用的；  
（五）侵犯民办学校合法权益的；  
（六）其他滥用职权、徇私舞弊的。

**Article 63** Where an education administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level, a human resources and social security administrative department, or other relevant department commits any of the following acts, the superior organ shall order it to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law; if economic losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to give a reply within the time limit after accepting an application for establishment;  
(2) approving an application that does not meet the conditions prescribed by this Law;  
(3) neglecting supervision, causing serious consequences;  
(4) collecting fees in violation of the relevant provisions of the State;  
(5) infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of a private school;  
(6) other acts of abusing power or engaging in malpractice for personal gain.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反国家有关规定擅自举办民办学校的，由所在地县级以上地方人民政府教育行政部门或者人力资源社会保障行政部门会同同级公安、民政或者市场监督管理等有关部门责令停止办学、退还所收费用，并对举办者处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国民办教育促进法实施条例.md#art-65" title="被《中华人民共和国民办教育促进法实施条例》第65条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 64** Where a private school is established without authorization in violation of the relevant provisions of the State, the education administrative department or the human resources and social security administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level, together with the public security, civil affairs, or market supervision and administration departments at the same level, shall order the cessation of school operation and the refund of fees collected, and shall impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains on the sponsor; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　本法所称的民办学校包括依法举办的其他民办教育机构。  
本法所称的校长包括其他民办教育机构的主要行政负责人。

**Article 65** For the purposes of this Law, private schools include other private educational institutions established according to law.  
For the purposes of this Law, the principal includes the main administrative head of other private educational institutions.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　境外的组织和个人在中国境内合作办学的办法，由国务院规定。

**Article 66** Measures for cooperation in running schools within the territory of China by overseas organizations and individuals shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　本法自2003年9月1日起施行。1997年7月31日国务院颁布的《社会力量办学条例》同时废止。

**Article 67** This Law shall come into force as of September 1, 2003. The Regulations on School Operation by Social Forces promulgated by the State Council on July 31, 1997 shall be abolished simultaneously.
