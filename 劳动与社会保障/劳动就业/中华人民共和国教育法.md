# 中华人民共和国教育法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2021-04-29  
**生效日期**：2021-04-30

> （1995年3月18日第八届全国人民代表大会第三次会议通过　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》第一次修正　根据2015年12月27日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十八次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国教育法〉的决定》第二次修正　根据2021年4月29日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十八次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国教育法〉的决定》第三次修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　教育基本制度 第三章　学校及其他教育机构 第四章　教师和其他教育工作者 第五章　受教育者 第六章　教育与社会 第七章　教育投入与条件保障 第八章　教育对外交流与合作 第九章　法律责任 第十章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了发展教育事业，提高全民族的素质，促进社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内的各级各类教育，适用本法。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了发展教育事业，提高全民族的素质，促进社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution, for the purpose of developing educational undertakings, improving the quality of the entire nation, and promoting the construction of socialist material civilization and spiritual civilization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内的各级各类教育，适用本法。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to education of all levels and categories conducted within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持以马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论、“三个代表”重要思想、科学发展观、习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，遵循宪法确定的基本原则，发展社会主义的教育事业。

**Article 3** The State adheres to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, upholds the guidance of Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the important thought of the Three Represents, the Scientific Outlook on Development, and Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, follows the basic principles established by the Constitution, and develops socialist educational undertakings.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　教育是社会主义现代化建设的基础，对提高人民综合素质、促进人的全面发展、增强中华民族创新创造活力、实现中华民族伟大复兴具有决定性意义，国家保障教育事业优先发展。  
全社会应当关心和支持教育事业的发展。  
全社会应当尊重教师。

**Article 4** Education is the foundation of socialist modernization, is of decisive significance in improving the comprehensive quality of the people, promoting the all-round development of human beings, enhancing the innovation and creativity of the Chinese nation, and realizing the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation. The State ensures the priority development of educational undertakings.  
The whole society shall care for and support the development of educational undertakings.  
The whole society shall respect teachers.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　教育必须为社会主义现代化建设服务、为人民服务，必须与生产劳动和社会实践相结合，培养德智体美劳全面发展的社会主义建设者和接班人。

**Article 5** Education must serve socialist modernization and serve the people, and must be combined with productive labor and social practice, in order to cultivate builders and successors for the socialist cause who are well developed morally, intellectually, physically, aesthetically, and in labor.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　教育应当坚持立德树人，对受教育者加强社会主义核心价值观教育，增强受教育者的社会责任感、创新精神和实践能力。  
国家在受教育者中进行爱国主义、集体主义、中国特色社会主义的教育，进行理想、道德、纪律、法治、国防和民族团结的教育。

**Article 6** Education shall adhere to fostering virtue through education, strengthen education in socialist core values for educatees, and enhance educatees' sense of social responsibility, spirit of innovation, and practical ability.  
The State shall conduct education in patriotism, collectivism, and socialism with Chinese characteristics among educatees, as well as education in ideals, morality, discipline, rule of law, national defense, and national unity.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　教育应当继承和弘扬中华优秀传统文化、革命文化、社会主义先进文化，吸收人类文明发展的一切优秀成果。

**Article 7** Education shall inherit and carry forward the excellent traditional Chinese culture, revolutionary culture, and advanced socialist culture, and absorb all outstanding achievements in the development of human civilization.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　教育活动必须符合国家和社会公共利益。  
国家实行教育与宗教相分离。任何组织和个人不得利用宗教进行妨碍国家教育制度的活动。

**Article 8** Educational activities must conform to the public interest of the State and society.  
The State implements the separation of education from religion. No organization or individual may use religion to engage in activities that obstruct the State's educational system.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　中华人民共和国公民有受教育的权利和义务。  
公民不分民族、种族、性别、职业、财产状况、宗教信仰等，依法享有平等的受教育机会。

**Article 9** Citizens of the People's Republic of China have the right and obligation to receive education.  
Citizens, regardless of ethnicity, race, gender, occupation, property status, or religious belief, shall enjoy equal opportunities for education according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家根据各少数民族的特点和需要，帮助各少数民族地区发展教育事业。  
国家扶持边远贫困地区发展教育事业。  
国家扶持和发展残疾人教育事业。

**Article 10** The State shall, in light of the characteristics and needs of each ethnic minority, assist the areas inhabited by ethnic minorities in developing educational undertakings.  
The State shall support the development of educational undertakings in remote and poverty-stricken areas.  
The State shall support and develop educational undertakings for persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家适应社会主义市场经济发展和社会进步的需要，推进教育改革，推动各级各类教育协调发展、衔接融通，完善现代国民教育体系，健全终身教育体系，提高教育现代化水平。  
国家采取措施促进教育公平，推动教育均衡发展。  
国家支持、鼓励和组织教育科学研究，推广教育科学研究成果，促进教育质量提高。

**Article 11** The State shall adapt to the needs of the development of the socialist market economy and social progress, promote educational reform, advance the coordinated development, connection and integration of education at all levels and categories, improve the modern national education system, perfect the lifelong education system, and raise the level of educational modernization.  
The State shall take measures to promote educational equity and facilitate balanced development of education.  
The State shall support, encourage and organize educational scientific research, disseminate the achievements of educational scientific research, and promote the improvement of educational quality.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家通用语言文字为学校及其他教育机构的基本教育教学语言文字，学校及其他教育机构应当使用国家通用语言文字进行教育教学。  
民族自治地方以少数民族学生为主的学校及其他教育机构，从实际出发，使用国家通用语言文字和本民族或者当地民族通用的语言文字实施双语教育。  
国家采取措施，为少数民族学生为主的学校及其他教育机构实施双语教育提供条件和支持。

**Article 12** The spoken and written language commonly used throughout the State shall be the basic language for education and teaching in schools and other educational institutions. Schools and other educational institutions shall use the spoken and written language commonly used throughout the State for education and teaching.  
Schools and other educational institutions in ethnic autonomous areas where the students are mainly from ethnic minorities may, based on the actual conditions, use both the spoken and written language commonly used throughout the State and the spoken and written language commonly used by the local ethnic group or groups to implement bilingual education.  
The State shall take measures to provide conditions and support for schools and other educational institutions where the students are mainly from ethnic minorities to implement bilingual education.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家对发展教育事业做出突出贡献的组织和个人，给予奖励。

**Article 13** The State shall give awards to organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the development of educational undertakings.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院和地方各级人民政府根据分级管理、分工负责的原则，领导和管理教育工作。  
中等及中等以下教育在国务院领导下，由地方人民政府管理。  
高等教育由国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理。

**Article 14** The State Council and local people's governments at all levels shall lead and administer educational work in accordance with the principles of tiered management and division of responsibilities.  
Education at the secondary level and below shall be managed by local people's governments under the leadership of the State Council.  
Higher education shall be managed by the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院教育行政部门主管全国教育工作，统筹规划、协调管理全国的教育事业。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府教育行政部门主管本行政区域内的教育工作。  
县级以上各级人民政府其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内，负责有关的教育工作。

**Article 15** The administrative department for education under the State Council shall be responsible for the educational work of the whole country, and shall make overall plans and coordinate the management of educational undertakings nationwide.  
The administrative departments for education of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for educational work within their respective administrative areas.  
Other relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for relevant educational work within the scope of their respective duties.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院和县级以上地方各级人民政府应当向本级人民代表大会或者其常务委员会报告教育工作和教育经费预算、决算情况，接受监督。

**Article 16** The State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall report on educational work and on the budget and final accounts of educational funds to the people's congress at the corresponding level or its standing committee, and shall accept supervision.

### 第二章　教育基本制度

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家实行学前教育、初等教育、中等教育、高等教育的学校教育制度。  
国家建立科学的学制系统。学制系统内的学校和其他教育机构的设置、教育形式、修业年限、招生对象、培养目标等，由国务院或者由国务院授权教育行政部门规定。

**Article 17** The State implements a school education system consisting of preschool education, primary education, secondary education, and higher education.  
The State shall establish a scientific school system. The establishment, educational forms, duration of schooling, enrollment targets, and training objectives of schools and other educational institutions within the school system shall be prescribed by the State Council or by the administrative department for education authorized by the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家制定学前教育标准，加快普及学前教育，构建覆盖城乡，特别是农村的学前教育公共服务体系。  
各级人民政府应当采取措施，为适龄儿童接受学前教育提供条件和支持。

**Article 18** The State shall formulate standards for preschool education, accelerate the universalization of preschool education, and build a public service system for preschool education covering both urban and rural areas, especially rural areas.  
People's governments at all levels shall take measures to provide conditions and support for school-age children to receive preschool education.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家实行九年制义务教育制度。  
各级人民政府采取各种措施保障适龄儿童、少年就学。  
适龄儿童、少年的父母或者其他监护人以及有关社会组织和个人有义务使适龄儿童、少年接受并完成规定年限的义务教育。

**Article 19** The State implements a system of nine-year compulsory education.  
People's governments at all levels shall take various measures to ensure that school-age children and adolescents are enrolled in school.  
Parents or other guardians of school-age children and adolescents, as well as relevant social organizations and individuals, have the obligation to ensure that school-age children and adolescents receive and complete compulsory education for the prescribed number of years.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家实行职业教育制度和继续教育制度。  
各级人民政府、有关行政部门和行业组织以及企业事业组织应当采取措施，发展并保障公民接受职业学校教育或者各种形式的职业培训。  
国家鼓励发展多种形式的继续教育，使公民接受适当形式的政治、经济、文化、科学、技术、业务等方面的教育，促进不同类型学习成果的互认和衔接，推动全民终身学习。

**Article 20** The State implements a system of vocational education and a system of continuing education.  
People's governments at all levels, relevant administrative departments and industry organizations, as well as enterprises and institutions, shall take measures to develop and ensure that citizens receive vocational school education or various forms of vocational training.  
The State encourages the development of various forms of continuing education, enabling citizens to receive appropriate forms of education in politics, economics, culture, science, technology, and professional skills, promoting the mutual recognition and connection of different types of learning outcomes, and advancing lifelong learning for all.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家实行国家教育考试制度。  
国家教育考试由国务院教育行政部门确定种类，并由国家批准的实施教育考试的机构承办。

**Article 21** The State implements a system of national education examinations.  
The types of national education examinations shall be determined by the administrative department for education under the State Council, and such examinations shall be administered by institutions approved by the State to conduct education examinations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家实行学业证书制度。  
经国家批准设立或者认可的学校及其他教育机构按照国家有关规定，颁发学历证书或者其他学业证书。

**Article 22** The State implements a system of academic certificates.  
Schools and other educational institutions established or recognized with the approval of the State shall, in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, issue diplomas or other academic certificates.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家实行学位制度。  
学位授予单位依法对达到一定学术水平或者专业技术水平的人员授予相应的学位，颁发学位证书。

**Article 23** The State implements a system of academic degrees.  
Degree-conferring units shall, according to law, confer corresponding academic degrees on persons who have attained a certain level of academic or professional technical proficiency, and issue degree certificates.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　各级人民政府、基层群众性自治组织和企业事业组织应当采取各种措施，开展扫除文盲的教育工作。  
按照国家规定具有接受扫除文盲教育能力的公民，应当接受扫除文盲的教育。

**Article 24** People's governments at all levels, grassroots mass autonomous organizations, and enterprises and institutions shall take various measures to carry out educational work for the elimination of illiteracy.  
Citizens who, according to State regulations, have the ability to receive education for the elimination of illiteracy shall receive such education.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家实行教育督导制度和学校及其他教育机构教育评估制度。

**Article 25** The State implements a system of educational supervision and a system of educational evaluation for schools and other educational institutions.

### 第三章　学校及其他教育机构

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家制定教育发展规划，并举办学校及其他教育机构。  
国家鼓励企业事业组织、社会团体、其他社会组织及公民个人依法举办学校及其他教育机构。  
国家举办学校及其他教育机构，应当坚持勤俭节约的原则。  
以财政性经费、捐赠资产举办或者参与举办的学校及其他教育机构不得设立为营利性组织。

**Article 26** The State shall formulate plans for the development of education, and establish schools and other educational institutions.  
The State encourages enterprises and institutions, social organizations, other social groups, and individual citizens to establish schools and other educational institutions according to law.  
In establishing schools and other educational institutions, the State shall adhere to the principle of diligence and frugality.  
Schools and other educational institutions established or participated in with financial funds or donated assets shall not be established as for-profit organizations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　设立学校及其他教育机构，必须具备下列基本条件：  
（一）有组织机构和章程；  
（二）有合格的教师；  
（三）有符合规定标准的教学场所及设施、设备等；  
（四）有必备的办学资金和稳定的经费来源。

**Article 27** The establishment of a school or other educational institution must meet the following basic conditions:  
(1) having an organizational structure and articles of association;  
(2) having qualified teachers;  
(3) having teaching premises, facilities, and equipment that meet the prescribed standards;  
(4) having the necessary funds for running the school and a stable source of funding.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　学校及其他教育机构的设立、变更和终止，应当按照国家有关规定办理审核、批准、注册或者备案手续。

**Article 28** The establishment, modification, and termination of schools and other educational institutions shall undergo procedures of review, approval, registration, or filing in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　学校及其他教育机构行使下列权利：  
（一）按照章程自主管理；  
（二）组织实施教育教学活动；  
（三）招收学生或者其他受教育者；  
（四）对受教育者进行学籍管理，实施奖励或者处分；  
（五）对受教育者颁发相应的学业证书；  
（六）聘任教师及其他职工，实施奖励或者处分；  
（七）管理、使用本单位的设施和经费；  
（八）拒绝任何组织和个人对教育教学活动的非法干涉；  
（九）法律、法规规定的其他权利。  
国家保护学校及其他教育机构的合法权益不受侵犯。

**Article 29** Schools and other educational institutions shall exercise the following rights:  
(1) to conduct autonomous management in accordance with their articles of association;  
(2) to organize and implement educational and teaching activities;  
(3) to enroll students or other educatees;  
(4) to manage the enrollment records of educatees, and to grant rewards or impose sanctions;  
(5) to issue corresponding academic certificates to educatees;  
(6) to employ teachers and other staff members, and to grant rewards or impose sanctions;  
(7) to manage and use their facilities and funds;  
(8) to reject any illegal interference by any organization or individual in educational and teaching activities;  
(9) other rights provided for by laws and regulations.  
The State shall protect the lawful rights and interests of schools and other educational institutions from infringement.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　学校及其他教育机构应当履行下列义务：  
（一）遵守法律、法规；  
（二）贯彻国家的教育方针，执行国家教育教学标准，保证教育教学质量；  
（三）维护受教育者、教师及其他职工的合法权益；  
（四）以适当方式为受教育者及其监护人了解受教育者的学业成绩及其他有关情况提供便利；  
（五）遵照国家有关规定收取费用并公开收费项目；  
（六）依法接受监督。

**Article 30** Schools and other educational institutions shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) to abide by laws and regulations;  
(2) to implement the State's educational policy, execute the State's educational and teaching standards, and ensure the quality of education and teaching;  
(3) to protect the lawful rights and interests of educatees, teachers, and other staff members;  
(4) to provide convenience in an appropriate manner for educatees and their guardians to obtain information about the educatees' academic performance and other relevant circumstances;  
(5) to collect fees in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State and make public the items for which fees are charged;  
(6) to accept supervision according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　学校及其他教育机构的举办者按照国家有关规定，确定其所举办的学校或者其他教育机构的管理体制。  
学校及其他教育机构的校长或者主要行政负责人必须由具有中华人民共和国国籍、在中国境内定居、并具备国家规定任职条件的公民担任，其任免按照国家有关规定办理。学校的教学及其他行政管理，由校长负责。  
学校及其他教育机构应当按照国家有关规定，通过以教师为主体的教职工代表大会等组织形式，保障教职工参与民主管理和监督。

**Article 31** The sponsors of schools and other educational institutions shall, in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, determine the management system of the schools or other educational institutions they sponsor.  
The principal or chief administrative officer of a school or other educational institution must be a citizen who has the nationality of the People's Republic of China, resides within the territory of China, and meets the conditions prescribed by the State for holding such a position. Their appointment and removal shall be handled in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State. The principal shall be responsible for the teaching and other administrative management of the school.  
Schools and other educational institutions shall, in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, guarantee the participation of faculty and staff in democratic management and supervision through organizational forms such as the staff and workers' congress with teachers as the main body.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　学校及其他教育机构具备法人条件的，自批准设立或者登记注册之日起取得法人资格。  
学校及其他教育机构在民事活动中依法享有民事权利，承担民事责任。  
学校及其他教育机构中的国有资产属于国家所有。  
学校及其他教育机构兴办的校办产业独立承担民事责任。

**Article 32** Where a school or other educational institution meets the conditions for a legal person, it shall acquire the status of a legal person from the date of approval for establishment or registration.  
Schools and other educational institutions shall enjoy civil rights and bear civil liability according to law in civil activities.  
The State-owned assets in schools and other educational institutions shall be owned by the State.  
The school-run industries run by schools and other educational institutions shall independently bear civil liability.

### 第四章　教师和其他教育工作者

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　教师享有法律规定的权利，履行法律规定的义务，忠诚于人民的教育事业。

**Article 33** Teachers shall enjoy the rights provided by law, perform the obligations provided by law, and be loyal to the people's educational cause.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家保护教师的合法权益，改善教师的工作条件和生活条件，提高教师的社会地位。  
教师的工资报酬、福利待遇，依照法律、法规的规定办理。

**Article 34** The State shall protect the lawful rights and interests of teachers, improve their working and living conditions, and raise their social status.  
The salaries, remuneration, and welfare benefits of teachers shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家实行教师资格、职务、聘任制度，通过考核、奖励、培养和培训，提高教师素质，加强教师队伍建设。

**Article 35** The State implements a system of qualifications, posts, and appointment for teachers, and through assessment, rewards, cultivation, and training, improves the quality of teachers and strengthens the construction of the teaching force.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　学校及其他教育机构中的管理人员，实行教育职员制度。  
学校及其他教育机构中的教学辅助人员和其他专业技术人员，实行专业技术职务聘任制度。

**Article 36** Administrative staff in schools and other educational institutions shall be subject to a system of educational staff.  
Teaching assistants and other professional and technical personnel in schools and other educational institutions shall be subject to a system of appointment for professional and technical posts.

### 第五章　受教育者

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　受教育者在入学、升学、就业等方面依法享有平等权利。  
学校和有关行政部门应当按照国家有关规定，保障女子在入学、升学、就业、授予学位、派出留学等方面享有同男子平等的权利。

**Article 37** Educatees shall enjoy equal rights according to law in aspects such as admission to schools, advancement to higher levels of education, and employment.  
Schools and relevant administrative departments shall, in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, ensure that women enjoy equal rights with men in admission to schools, advancement to higher levels of education, employment, conferment of degrees, and dispatch for study abroad.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家、社会对符合入学条件、家庭经济困难的儿童、少年、青年，提供各种形式的资助。

**Article 38** The State and society shall provide various forms of financial assistance to children, adolescents, and young people who meet the admission requirements but come from families with financial difficulties.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家、社会、学校及其他教育机构应当根据残疾人身心特性和需要实施教育，并为其提供帮助和便利。

**Article 39** The State, society, schools, and other educational institutions shall provide education in light of the physical and mental characteristics and needs of persons with disabilities, and shall provide them with assistance and convenience.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家、社会、家庭、学校及其他教育机构应当为有违法犯罪行为的未成年人接受教育创造条件。

**Article 40** The State, society, families, schools, and other educational institutions shall create conditions for minors who have committed illegal or criminal acts to receive education.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　从业人员有依法接受职业培训和继续教育的权利和义务。  
国家机关、企业事业组织和其他社会组织，应当为本单位职工的学习和培训提供条件和便利。

**Article 41** Employees have the right and obligation to receive vocational training and continuing education according to law.  
State organs, enterprises and institutions, and other social organizations shall provide conditions and convenience for the learning and training of their own staff members.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家鼓励学校及其他教育机构、社会组织采取措施，为公民接受终身教育创造条件。

**Article 42** The State encourages schools, other educational institutions, and social organizations to take measures to create conditions for citizens to receive lifelong education.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　受教育者享有下列权利：  
（一）参加教育教学计划安排的各种活动，使用教育教学设施、设备、图书资料；  
（二）按照国家有关规定获得奖学金、贷学金、助学金；  
（三）在学业成绩和品行上获得公正评价，完成规定的学业后获得相应的学业证书、学位证书；  
（四）对学校给予的处分不服向有关部门提出申诉，对学校、教师侵犯其人身权、财产权等合法权益，提出申诉或者依法提起诉讼；  
（五）法律、法规规定的其他权利。

**Article 43** Educatees shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) to participate in various activities arranged in the educational and teaching plan, and to use educational and teaching facilities, equipment, and reference materials;  
(2) to obtain scholarships, student loans, and grants-in-aid in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State;  
(3) to receive a fair evaluation of academic performance and conduct, and to obtain corresponding academic certificates and degree certificates upon completion of the prescribed studies;  
(4) to lodge a petition with the relevant departments if dissatisfied with the sanctions imposed by the school, and to lodge a petition or initiate a lawsuit according to law against the school or teachers for infringement of their personal rights, property rights, or other lawful rights and interests;  
(5) other rights provided for by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　受教育者应当履行下列义务：  
（一）遵守法律、法规；  
（二）遵守学生行为规范，尊敬师长，养成良好的思想品德和行为习惯；  
（三）努力学习，完成规定的学习任务；  
（四）遵守所在学校或者其他教育机构的管理制度。

**Article 44** Educatees shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) to abide by laws and regulations;  
(2) to comply with student conduct standards, respect teachers, and cultivate good ideological and moral character and behavioral habits;  
(3) to study diligently and complete the prescribed learning tasks;  
(4) to comply with the management system of the school or other educational institution they attend.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　教育、体育、卫生行政部门和学校及其他教育机构应当完善体育、卫生保健设施，保护学生的身心健康。

**Article 45** The administrative departments for education, sports, and health, as well as schools and other educational institutions, shall improve sports and health care facilities and protect the physical and mental health of students.

### 第六章　教育与社会

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国家机关、军队、企业事业组织、社会团体及其他社会组织和个人，应当依法为儿童、少年、青年学生的身心健康成长创造良好的社会环境。

**Article 46** State organs, the armed forces, enterprises and institutions, social organizations, other social groups, and individuals shall, according to law, create a favorable social environment for the healthy physical and mental growth of children, adolescents, and young students.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家鼓励企业事业组织、社会团体及其他社会组织同高等学校、中等职业学校在教学、科研、技术开发和推广等方面进行多种形式的合作。  
企业事业组织、社会团体及其他社会组织和个人，可以通过适当形式，支持学校的建设，参与学校管理。

**Article 47** The State encourages enterprises and institutions, social organizations, and other social groups to cooperate in various forms with institutions of higher learning and secondary vocational schools in teaching, scientific research, technological development, and promotion.  
Enterprises and institutions, social organizations, other social groups, and individuals may, through appropriate forms, support the construction of schools and participate in school management.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家机关、军队、企业事业组织及其他社会组织应当为学校组织的学生实习、社会实践活动提供帮助和便利。

**Article 48** State organs, the armed forces, enterprises and institutions, and other social organizations shall provide assistance and convenience for the internships and social practice activities organized by schools.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　学校及其他教育机构在不影响正常教育教学活动的前提下，应当积极参加当地的社会公益活动。

**Article 49** Schools and other educational institutions shall, on the premise of not affecting normal educational and teaching activities, actively participate in local public welfare activities.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人应当为其未成年子女或者其他被监护人受教育提供必要条件。  
未成年人的父母或者其他监护人应当配合学校及其他教育机构，对其未成年子女或者其他被监护人进行教育。  
学校、教师可以对学生家长提供家庭教育指导。

**Article 50** Parents or other guardians of minors shall provide the necessary conditions for their minor children or other wards to receive education.  
Parents or other guardians of minors shall cooperate with schools and other educational institutions in educating their minor children or other wards.  
Schools and teachers may provide guidance on family education to the parents of students.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　图书馆、博物馆、科技馆、文化馆、美术馆、体育馆（场）等社会公共文化体育设施，以及历史文化古迹和革命纪念馆（地），应当对教师、学生实行优待，为受教育者接受教育提供便利。  
广播、电视台（站）应当开设教育节目，促进受教育者思想品德、文化和科学技术素质的提高。

**Article 51** Libraries, museums, science and technology museums, cultural centers, art galleries, stadiums (gymnasiums) and other social public cultural and sports facilities, as well as historical and cultural relics and revolutionary memorial halls (sites), shall grant preferential treatment to teachers and students and provide convenience for educatees to receive education.  
Radio and television stations (broadcasting stations) shall offer educational programs to promote the improvement of the ideological and moral character, cultural and scientific and technological qualities of educatees.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家、社会建立和发展对未成年人进行校外教育的设施。  
学校及其他教育机构应当同基层群众性自治组织、企业事业组织、社会团体相互配合，加强对未成年人的校外教育工作。

**Article 52** The State and society shall establish and develop facilities for providing off-campus education to minors.  
Schools and other educational institutions shall cooperate with grassroots self-governing mass organizations, enterprises, institutions, and social organizations to strengthen off-campus education for minors.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家鼓励社会团体、社会文化机构及其他社会组织和个人开展有益于受教育者身心健康的社会文化教育活动。

**Article 53** The State shall encourage social organizations, social cultural institutions, and other social organizations and individuals to carry out social and cultural educational activities that are beneficial to the physical and mental health of educatees.

### 第七章　教育投入与条件保障

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家建立以财政拨款为主、其他多种渠道筹措教育经费为辅的体制，逐步增加对教育的投入，保证国家举办的学校教育经费的稳定来源。  
企业事业组织、社会团体及其他社会组织和个人依法举办的学校及其他教育机构，办学经费由举办者负责筹措，各级人民政府可以给予适当支持。

**Article 54** The State shall establish a system with financial appropriations as the main source and multiple other channels for raising educational funds as supplementary, gradually increase investment in education, and ensure a stable source of funding for schools run by the State.  
For schools and other educational institutions established by enterprises, institutions, social organizations, other social organizations, and individuals in accordance with law, the sponsors shall be responsible for raising the funds for school operation, and the people's governments at all levels may provide appropriate support.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家财政性教育经费支出占国民生产总值的比例应当随着国民经济的发展和财政收入的增长逐步提高。具体比例和实施步骤由国务院规定。  
全国各级财政支出总额中教育经费所占比例应当随着国民经济的发展逐步提高。

**Article 55** The proportion of the State's financial educational expenditure to the gross national product shall gradually increase with the development of the national economy and the growth of fiscal revenue. The specific proportion and implementation steps shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
The proportion of educational funds in the total fiscal expenditure at all levels throughout the country shall gradually increase with the development of the national economy.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　各级人民政府的教育经费支出，按照事权和财权相统一的原则，在财政预算中单独列项。  
各级人民政府教育财政拨款的增长应当高于财政经常性收入的增长，并使按在校学生人数平均的教育费用逐步增长，保证教师工资和学生人均公用经费逐步增长。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国高等教育法.md#art-60" title="被《中华人民共和国高等教育法》第60条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 56** The expenditure on education by the people's governments at all levels shall be listed separately in the fiscal budget in accordance with the principle of unifying administrative responsibilities and financial powers.  
The increase in the financial appropriations for education by the people's governments at all levels shall be higher than the increase in regular fiscal revenue, and shall ensure a gradual increase in the average educational expenditure per student, and guarantee the gradual increase in teachers' salaries and per capita public funds for students.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国务院及县级以上地方各级人民政府应当设立教育专项资金，重点扶持边远贫困地区、少数民族地区实施义务教育。

**Article 57** The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish special education funds to focus on supporting the implementation of compulsory education in remote and poverty-stricken areas and ethnic minority areas.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　税务机关依法足额征收教育费附加，由教育行政部门统筹管理，主要用于实施义务教育。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据国务院的有关规定，可以决定开征用于教育的地方附加费，专款专用。

**Article 58** The tax authorities shall collect the education surcharge in full according to law, which shall be managed centrally by the education administrative department and mainly used for implementing compulsory education.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council, decide to levy local surcharges for education, which shall be used exclusively for that purpose.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国家采取优惠措施，鼓励和扶持学校在不影响正常教育教学的前提下开展勤工俭学和社会服务，兴办校办产业。

**Article 59** The State shall adopt preferential measures to encourage and support schools in carrying out work-study programs and social services, and in running school-run enterprises, provided that normal education and teaching are not affected.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国家鼓励境内、境外社会组织和个人捐资助学。

**Article 60** The State shall encourage domestic and overseas social organizations and individuals to make donations to support education.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家财政性教育经费、社会组织和个人对教育的捐赠，必须用于教育，不得挪用、克扣。

**Article 61** The State financial educational funds and donations for education from social organizations and individuals must be used for education, and shall not be misappropriated or withheld.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　国家鼓励运用金融、信贷手段，支持教育事业的发展。

**Article 62** The State shall encourage the use of financial and credit instruments to support the development of education.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　各级人民政府及其教育行政部门应当加强对学校及其他教育机构教育经费的监督管理，提高教育投资效益。

**Article 63** The people's governments at all levels and their education administrative departments shall strengthen the supervision and management of the educational funds of schools and other educational institutions, and improve the efficiency of educational investment.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　地方各级人民政府及其有关行政部门必须把学校的基本建设纳入城乡建设规划，统筹安排学校的基本建设用地及所需物资，按照国家有关规定实行优先、优惠政策。

**Article 64** The local people's governments at all levels and their relevant administrative departments must incorporate the capital construction of schools into urban and rural construction plans, make overall arrangements for land and materials required for school capital construction, and implement priority and preferential policies in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　各级人民政府对教科书及教学用图书资料的出版发行，对教学仪器、设备的生产和供应，对用于学校教育教学和科学研究的图书资料、教学仪器、设备的进口，按照国家有关规定实行优先、优惠政策。

**Article 65** The people's governments at all levels shall implement priority and preferential policies in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State for the publication and distribution of textbooks and teaching reference materials, for the production and supply of teaching instruments and equipment, and for the import of books, reference materials, teaching instruments, and equipment used for school education, teaching, and scientific research.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家推进教育信息化，加快教育信息基础设施建设，利用信息技术促进优质教育资源普及共享，提高教育教学水平和教育管理水平。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当发展教育信息技术和其他现代化教学方式，有关行政部门应当优先安排，给予扶持。  
国家鼓励学校及其他教育机构推广运用现代化教学方式。

**Article 66** The State shall promote the informatization of education, accelerate the construction of education information infrastructure, utilize information technology to promote the popularization and sharing of high-quality educational resources, and improve the level of education, teaching, and educational management.  
The people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall develop education information technology and other modern teaching methods, and the relevant administrative departments shall give priority and support.  
The State shall encourage schools and other educational institutions to promote and apply modern teaching methods.

### 第八章　教育对外交流与合作

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　国家鼓励开展教育对外交流与合作，支持学校及其他教育机构引进优质教育资源，依法开展中外合作办学，发展国际教育服务，培养国际化人才。  
教育对外交流与合作坚持独立自主、平等互利、相互尊重的原则，不得违反中国法律，不得损害国家主权、安全和社会公共利益。

**Article 67** The State shall encourage international exchange and cooperation in education, support schools and other educational institutions in introducing high-quality educational resources, conducting Sino-foreign cooperative education in accordance with law, developing international education services, and cultivating internationally-oriented talents.  
International exchange and cooperation in education shall adhere to the principles of independence, equality and mutual benefit, and mutual respect, and shall not violate Chinese laws or impair the national sovereignty, security, and social public interests.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　中国境内公民出国留学、研究、进行学术交流或者任教，依照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 68** Chinese citizens who go abroad for study, research, academic exchange, or teaching shall do so in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　中国境外个人符合国家规定的条件并办理有关手续后，可以进入中国境内学校及其他教育机构学习、研究、进行学术交流或者任教，其合法权益受国家保护。

**Article 69** Individuals from outside China who meet the conditions prescribed by the State and go through the relevant procedures may enter schools and other educational institutions in China for study, research, academic exchange, or teaching, and their lawful rights and interests shall be protected by the State.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　中国对境外教育机构颁发的学位证书、学历证书及其他学业证书的承认，依照中华人民共和国缔结或者加入的国际条约办理，或者按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 70** The recognition of degree certificates, academic certificates, and other academic certificates issued by overseas educational institutions by China shall be handled in accordance with the international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, or in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　违反国家有关规定，不按照预算核拨教育经费的，由同级人民政府限期核拨；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。  
违反国家财政制度、财务制度，挪用、克扣教育经费的，由上级机关责令限期归还被挪用、克扣的经费，并对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 71** If anyone, in violation of the relevant provisions of the State, fails to allocate educational funds according to the budget, the people's government at the same level shall order the allocation within a time limit; if the circumstances are serious, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.  
If anyone, in violation of the State's fiscal or financial systems, misappropriates or withholds educational funds, the competent authority at a higher level shall order the return of the misappropriated or withheld funds within a time limit, and shall give sanctions to the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　结伙斗殴、寻衅滋事，扰乱学校及其他教育机构教育教学秩序或者破坏校舍、场地及其他财产的，由公安机关给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
侵占学校及其他教育机构的校舍、场地及其他财产的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 72** If anyone, by engaging in gang fights, creating disturbances, or disrupting the order of education and teaching in schools or other educational institutions, or by destroying school buildings, premises, or other property, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If anyone encroaches on the school buildings, premises, or other property of a school or other educational institution, he or she shall bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　明知校舍或者教育教学设施有危险，而不采取措施，造成人员伤亡或者重大财产损失的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 73** If anyone, while clearly knowing that school buildings or educational and teaching facilities are dangerous, fails to take measures, causing casualties or heavy property losses, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　违反国家有关规定，向学校或者其他教育机构收取费用的，由政府责令退还所收费用；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 74** If anyone, in violation of the relevant provisions of the State, collects fees from a school or other educational institution, the government shall order the return of the fees collected; the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反国家有关规定，举办学校或者其他教育机构的，由教育行政部门或者其他有关行政部门予以撤销；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 75** If anyone, in violation of the relevant provisions of the State, establishes a school or other educational institution, the education administrative department or other relevant administrative department shall revoke it; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　学校或者其他教育机构违反国家有关规定招收学生的，由教育行政部门或者其他有关行政部门责令退回招收的学生，退还所收费用；对学校、其他教育机构给予警告，可以处违法所得五倍以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停止相关招生资格一年以上三年以下，直至撤销招生资格、吊销办学许可证；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 76** If a school or other educational institution enrolls students in violation of the relevant provisions of the State, the education administrative department or other relevant administrative department shall order the return of the enrolled students and the refund of the fees collected; a warning shall be given to the school or other educational institution, and a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains may be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant enrollment qualification shall be suspended for not less than one year but not more than three years, and the enrollment qualification may be revoked or the school-running permit may be cancelled; the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　在招收学生工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，由教育行政部门或者其他有关行政部门责令退回招收的不符合入学条件的人员；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
盗用、冒用他人身份，顶替他人取得的入学资格的，由教育行政部门或者其他有关行政部门责令撤销入学资格，并责令停止参加相关国家教育考试二年以上五年以下；已经取得学位证书、学历证书或者其他学业证书的，由颁发机构撤销相关证书；已经成为公职人员的，依法给予开除处分；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
与他人串通，允许他人冒用本人身份，顶替本人取得的入学资格的，由教育行政部门或者其他有关行政部门责令停止参加相关国家教育考试一年以上三年以下；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；已经成为公职人员的，依法给予处分；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
组织、指使盗用或者冒用他人身份，顶替他人取得的入学资格的，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；属于公职人员的，依法给予处分；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
入学资格被顶替权利受到侵害的，可以请求恢复其入学资格。

**Article 77** If anyone abuses his or her power, neglects his or her duty, or engages in malpractices for personal gain in the work of enrolling students, the education administrative department or other relevant administrative department shall order the return of the enrolled persons who do not meet the admission conditions; the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If anyone steals or fraudulently uses another person's identity to take the place of that person in obtaining enrollment qualification, the education administrative department or other relevant administrative department shall order the revocation of the enrollment qualification and order the suspension from taking the relevant national education examinations for not less than two years but not more than five years; if a degree certificate, academic certificate, or other academic certificate has already been obtained, the issuing institution shall revoke the relevant certificate; if the person has become a public official, he or she shall be given the sanction of expulsion according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If anyone, in collusion with another person, allows that person to fraudulently use his or her own identity to take the place of the person's own enrollment qualification, the education administrative department or other relevant administrative department shall order the suspension from taking the relevant national education examinations for not less than one year but not more than three years; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the person has become a public official, he or she shall be given a sanction according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If anyone organizes or instigates the theft or fraudulent use of another person's identity to take the place of that person's enrollment qualification, and if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the person is a public official, he or she shall be given a sanction according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Any person whose right to enrollment qualification has been infringed upon by substitution may request the restoration of his or her enrollment qualification.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　学校及其他教育机构违反国家有关规定向受教育者收取费用的，由教育行政部门或者其他有关行政部门责令退还所收费用；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 78** If a school or other educational institution, in violation of the relevant provisions of the State, collects fees from educatees, the education administrative department or other relevant administrative department shall order the refund of the fees collected; the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　考生在国家教育考试中有下列行为之一的，由组织考试的教育考试机构工作人员在考试现场采取必要措施予以制止并终止其继续参加考试；组织考试的教育考试机构可以取消其相关考试资格或者考试成绩；情节严重的，由教育行政部门责令停止参加相关国家教育考试一年以上三年以下；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）非法获取考试试题或者答案的；  
（二）携带或者使用考试作弊器材、资料的；  
（三）抄袭他人答案的；  
（四）让他人代替自己参加考试的；  
（五）其他以不正当手段获得考试成绩的作弊行为。

**Article 79** If an examinee commits any of the following acts in a national education examination, the staff of the educational examination institution organizing the examination shall take necessary measures on the spot to stop the act and terminate his or her continued participation in the examination; the educational examination institution organizing the examination may cancel his or her relevant examination qualification or test scores; if the circumstances are serious, the education administrative department shall order the suspension from taking the relevant national education examinations for not less than one year but not more than three years; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) illegally obtaining examination questions or answers;  
(2) carrying or using cheating equipment or materials for examinations;  
(3) copying others' answers;  
(4) having another person take the examination on his or her behalf;  
(5) other cheating acts of obtaining test scores by improper means.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　任何组织或者个人在国家教育考试中有下列行为之一，有违法所得的，由公安机关没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；情节严重的，处五日以上十五日以下拘留；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；属于国家机关工作人员的，还应当依法给予处分：  
（一）组织作弊的；  
（二）通过提供考试作弊器材等方式为作弊提供帮助或者便利的；  
（三）代替他人参加考试的；  
（四）在考试结束前泄露、传播考试试题或者答案的；  
（五）其他扰乱考试秩序的行为。

**Article 80** If any organization or individual commits any of the following acts in a national education examination, and if there are illegal gains, the public security organ shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, a detention of not less than five days but not more than fifteen days shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the person is a functionary of a State organ, a sanction shall also be given according to law:  
(1) organizing cheating;  
(2) providing assistance or convenience for cheating by providing cheating equipment for examinations or other means;  
(3) taking an examination on behalf of another person;  
(4) disclosing or disseminating examination questions or answers before the end of the examination;  
(5) other acts that disrupt the examination order.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　举办国家教育考试，教育行政部门、教育考试机构疏于管理，造成考场秩序混乱、作弊情况严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 81** If, in holding a national education examination, the education administrative department or educational examination institution neglects management, causing disorder in the examination room or serious cheating, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　学校或者其他教育机构违反本法规定，颁发学位证书、学历证书或者其他学业证书的，由教育行政部门或者其他有关行政部门宣布证书无效，责令收回或者予以没收；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，责令停止相关招生资格一年以上三年以下，直至撤销招生资格、颁发证书资格；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。  
前款规定以外的任何组织或者个人制造、销售、颁发假冒学位证书、学历证书或者其他学业证书，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
以作弊、剽窃、抄袭等欺诈行为或者其他不正当手段获得学位证书、学历证书或者其他学业证书的，由颁发机构撤销相关证书。购买、使用假冒学位证书、学历证书或者其他学业证书，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 82** If a school or other educational institution, in violation of the provisions of this Law, issues degree certificates, academic certificates, or other academic certificates, the education administrative department or other relevant administrative department shall declare the certificates invalid, and order the recovery or confiscation thereof; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant enrollment qualification shall be suspended for not less than one year but not more than three years, and the enrollment qualification or the qualification to issue certificates may be revoked; the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.  
If any organization or individual other than those specified in the preceding paragraph manufactures, sells, or issues counterfeit degree certificates, academic certificates, or other academic certificates, and the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If anyone obtains a degree certificate, academic certificate, or other academic certificate by cheating, plagiarism, copying, or other fraudulent acts or by other improper means, the issuing institution shall revoke the relevant certificate. If anyone purchases or uses a counterfeit degree certificate, academic certificate, or other academic certificate, and the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　违反本法规定，侵犯教师、受教育者、学校或者其他教育机构的合法权益，造成损失、损害的，应当依法承担民事责任。

**Article 83** If anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of a teacher, educatee, school, or other educational institution, causing losses or damages, civil liability shall be borne according to law.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　军事学校教育由中央军事委员会根据本法的原则规定。  
宗教学校教育由国务院另行规定。

**Article 84** Education in military schools shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission in accordance with the principles of this Law.  
Education in religious schools shall be separately prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　境外的组织和个人在中国境内办学和合作办学的办法，由国务院规定。

**Article 85** The measures for overseas organizations and individuals to run schools or cooperate in running schools within the territory of China shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　本法自1995年9月1日起施行。

**Article 86** This Law shall come into force as of September 1, 1995.
