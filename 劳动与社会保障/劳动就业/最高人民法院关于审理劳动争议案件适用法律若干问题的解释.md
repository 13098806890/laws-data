# 最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2001〕14号  
**公布日期**：2001-03-22  
**生效日期**：2001-04-30

> （2001年3月22日最高人民法院审判委员会第1165次会议通过，自2001年4月30日起施行。） 法释〔2001〕14号 为正确审理劳动争议案件，根据《中华人民共和国劳动法》（以下简称《劳动法》）和《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》（以下简称《民事诉讼法》）等相关法律之规定，就适用法律的若干问题，作如下解释。 第一条 劳动者与用人单位之间发生的下列纠纷，属于《劳动法》第二条规定的劳动争议，当事人不服劳动争议仲裁委员会作出的裁决，依法向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当受理： （一）劳动者与用人单位在履行劳动合同过程中发生的纠纷； （二）劳动者与用人单位之间没有订立书面劳动合同，但已形成劳动关系后发生的纠纷； （三）劳动者退休后，与尚未参加社会保险统筹的原用人单位因追索养老金、医疗费、工伤保险待遇和其他社会保险费而发生的纠纷。 第二条 劳动争议仲裁委员会以当事人申请仲裁的事项不属于劳动争议为由，作出不予受理的书面裁决、决定或者通知，当事人不服，依法向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当分别情况予以处理： （一）属于劳动争议案件的，应当受理； （二）虽不属于劳动争议案件，但属于人民法院主管的其他案件，应当依法受理。 第三条 劳动争议仲裁委员会根据《劳动法》第八十二条之规定，以当事人的仲裁申请超过六十日期限为由，作出不予受理的书面裁决、决定或者通知，当事人不服，依法向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当受理；对确已超过仲裁申请期限，又无不可抗力或者其他正当理由的，依法驳回其诉讼请求。 第四条 劳动争议仲裁委员会以申请仲裁的主体不适格为由，作出不予受理的书面裁决、决定或者通知，当事人不服，依法向人民法院起诉的，经审查，确属主体不适格的，裁定不予受理或者驳回起诉。 第五条 劳动争议仲裁委员会为纠正原仲裁裁决错误重新作出裁决，当事人不服，依法向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当受理。 第六条 人民法院受理劳动争议案件后，当事人增加诉讼请求的，如该诉讼请求与讼争的劳动争议具有不可分性，应当合并审理；如属独立的劳动争议，应当告知当事人向劳动争议仲裁委员会申请仲裁。 第七条 劳动争议仲裁委员会仲裁的事项不属于人民法院受理的案件范围，当事人不服，依法向人民法院起诉的，裁定不予受理或者驳回起诉。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 劳动者与用人单位之间发生的下列纠纷，属于[《劳动法》第二条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动法.md#art-2)规定的劳动争议，当事人不服劳动争议仲裁委员会作出的裁决，依法向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当受理：  
（一）劳动者与用人单位在履行劳动合同过程中发生的纠纷；  
（二）劳动者与用人单位之间没有订立书面劳动合同，但已形成劳动关系后发生的纠纷；  
（三）劳动者退休后，与尚未参加社会保险统筹的原用人单位因追索养老金、医疗费、工伤保险待遇和其他社会保险费而发生的纠纷。

**Article 1** The following disputes between a worker and an employer shall be labor disputes as defined in Article 2 of the Labor Law of the People's Republic of China. Where a party is dissatisfied with the award rendered by the labor dispute arbitration commission and brings a lawsuit in a People's Court according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case:  
(1) disputes arising between the worker and the employer during the performance of the labor contract;  
(2) disputes arising after a de facto labor relationship has been formed between the worker and the employer without a written labor contract having been concluded; and  
(3) disputes arising after the worker's retirement, between the worker and the original employer that has not participated in overall social insurance, concerning claims for pensions, medical expenses, work-related injury insurance benefits, and other social insurance premiums.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 劳动争议仲裁委员会以当事人申请仲裁的事项不属于劳动争议为由，作出不予受理的书面裁决、决定或者通知，当事人不服，依法向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当分别情况予以处理：  
（一）属于劳动争议案件的，应当受理；  
（二）虽不属于劳动争议案件，但属于人民法院主管的其他案件，应当依法受理。

**Article 2** Where the labor dispute arbitration commission, on the ground that the matter submitted for arbitration by the party does not constitute a labor dispute, renders a written award, decision, or notice of non-acceptance, and the party, being dissatisfied, brings a lawsuit in a People's Court according to law, the People's Court shall handle the case according to the following circumstances:  
(1) if the case falls within the scope of labor disputes, it shall accept the case; and  
(2) if the case does not fall within the scope of labor disputes but falls within the jurisdiction of the People's Court as another type of case, it shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 劳动争议仲裁委员会根据[《劳动法》第八十二条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动法.md#art-82)之规定，以当事人的仲裁申请超过六十日期限为由，作出不予受理的书面裁决、决定或者通知，当事人不服，依法向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当受理；对确已超过仲裁申请期限，又无不可抗力或者其他正当理由的，依法驳回其诉讼请求。

**Article 3** Where the labor dispute arbitration commission, in accordance with Article 82 of the Labor Law of the People's Republic of China, renders a written award, decision, or notice of non-acceptance on the ground that the party's arbitration application has exceeded the 60-day time limit, and the party, being dissatisfied, brings a lawsuit in a People's Court according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case; if the arbitration application time limit has indeed expired and there is no force majeure or other justifiable reason, the court shall dismiss the claim according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 劳动争议仲裁委员会以申请仲裁的主体不适格为由，作出不予受理的书面裁决、决定或者通知，当事人不服，依法向人民法院起诉的，经审查，确属主体不适格的，裁定不予受理或者驳回起诉。

**Article 4** Where the labor dispute arbitration commission renders a written award, decision, or notice of non-acceptance on the ground that the subject applying for arbitration is not a proper party, and the party, being dissatisfied, brings a lawsuit in a People's Court according to law, if upon examination the subject is indeed not a proper party, the People's Court shall rule to non-accept the case or dismiss the lawsuit.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 劳动争议仲裁委员会为纠正原仲裁裁决错误重新作出裁决，当事人不服，依法向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 5** Where the labor dispute arbitration commission makes a new award to correct an error in the original arbitration award, and a party, being dissatisfied, brings a lawsuit in a People's Court according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 人民法院受理劳动争议案件后，当事人增加诉讼请求的，如该诉讼请求与讼争的劳动争议具有不可分性，应当合并审理；如属独立的劳动争议，应当告知当事人向劳动争议仲裁委员会申请仲裁。

**Article 6** After a People's Court has accepted a labor dispute case, if a party adds a new claim, if such claim is inseparable from the labor dispute in question, it shall be heard together with the original dispute; if it is an independent labor dispute, the party shall be informed to apply for arbitration with the labor dispute arbitration commission.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 劳动争议仲裁委员会仲裁的事项不属于人民法院受理的案件范围，当事人不服，依法向人民法院起诉的，裁定不予受理或者驳回起诉。

**Article 7** Where a matter arbitrated by the labor dispute arbitration commission does not fall within the scope of cases accepted by the People's Court, and a party, being dissatisfied, brings a lawsuit in a People's Court according to law, the court shall rule to non-accept the case or dismiss the lawsuit.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 劳动争议案件由用人单位所在地或者劳动合同履行地的基层人民法院管辖。劳动合同履行地不明确的，由用人单位所在地的基层人民法院管辖。

**Article 8** A labor dispute case shall be under the jurisdiction of the Primary People's Court in the place where the employer is located or where the labor contract is performed. If the place of performance of the labor contract is unclear, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the Primary People's Court in the place where the employer is located.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 当事人双方不服劳动争议仲裁委员会作出的同一仲裁裁决，均向同一人民法院起诉的，先起诉的一方当事人为原告，但对双方的诉讼请求，人民法院应当一并作出裁决。  
当事人双方就同一仲裁裁决分别向有管辖权的人民法院起诉的，后受理的人民法院应当将案件移送给先受理的人民法院。

**Article 9** Where both parties are dissatisfied with the same arbitration award rendered by the labor dispute arbitration commission and both bring lawsuits in the same People's Court, the party that brings the lawsuit first shall be the plaintiff, but the People's Court shall render a ruling on the claims of both parties together.  
Where the two parties bring lawsuits regarding the same arbitration award in different People's Courts that have jurisdiction, the People's Court that accepts the case later shall transfer the case to the People's Court that accepted the case first.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 用人单位与其它单位合并的，合并前发生的劳动争议，由合并后的单位为当事人；用人单位分立为若干单位的，其分立前发生的劳动争议，由分立后的实际用人单位为当事人。  
用人单位分立为若干单位后，对承受劳动权利义务的单位不明确的，分立后的单位均为当事人。

**Article 10** Where an employer merges with another entity, the entity resulting from the merger shall be the party to any labor dispute that arose before the merger. Where an employer is divided into several entities, the actual employing entity after the division shall be the party to any labor dispute that arose before the division.  
Where after the employer is divided into several entities, the entity that assumes the labor rights and obligations is unclear, all entities resulting from the division shall be parties.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 用人单位招用尚未解除劳动合同的劳动者，原用人单位与劳动者发生的劳动争议，可以列新的用人单位为第三人。  
原用人单位以新的用人单位侵权为由向人民法院起诉的，可以列劳动者为第三人。  
原用人单位以新的用人单位和劳动者共同侵权为由向人民法院起诉的，新的用人单位和劳动者列为共同被告。

**Article 11** Where an employer employs a worker who has not yet terminated his or her labor contract with the original employer, and a labor dispute arises between the original employer and the worker, the new employer may be named as a third party.  
Where the original employer brings a lawsuit in a People's Court against the new employer on the ground of tort, the worker may be named as a third party.  
Where the original employer brings a lawsuit in a People's Court against the new employer and the worker on the ground of joint tort, the new employer and the worker shall be listed as co-defendants.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 劳动者在用人单位与其他平等主体之间的承包经营期间，与发包方和承包方双方或者一方发生劳动争议，依法向人民法院起诉的，应当将承包方和发包方作为当事人。

**Article 12** Where a worker, during a period of contracted operation between the employer and another equal subject, has a labor dispute with the party letting the contract, the contractor, or both, and brings a lawsuit in a People's Court according to law, the contractor and the party letting the contract shall be named as parties.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 因用人单位作出的开除、除名、辞退、解除劳动合同、减少劳动报酬、计算劳动者工作年限等决定而发生的劳动争议，用人单位负举证责任。

**Article 13** In a labor dispute arising from decisions made by the employer, such as expulsion, removal, dismissal, termination of the labor contract, reduction of labor remuneration, or calculation of the worker's length of service, the employer shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 劳动合同被确认为无效后，用人单位对劳动者付出的劳动，一般可参照本单位同期、同工种、同岗位的工资标准支付劳动报酬。  
根据[《劳动法》第九十七条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动法.md#art-97)之规定，由于用人单位的原因订立的无效合同，给劳动者造成损害的，应当比照违反和解除劳动合同经济补偿金的支付标准，赔偿劳动者因合同无效所造成的经济损失。

**Article 14** After a labor contract has been confirmed as void, the employer may generally pay labor remuneration to the worker for his or her work by reference to the wage standards for the same period, the same type of work, and the same position in that entity.  
In accordance with Article 97 of the Labor Law of the People's Republic of China, where a void contract concluded due to the employer's fault causes damage to the worker, the employer shall compensate the worker for the economic losses caused by the void contract by reference to the payment standards for economic compensation for violation and termination of labor contracts.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 用人单位有下列情形之一，迫使劳动者提出解除劳动合同的，用人单位应当支付劳动者的劳动报酬和经济补偿，并可支付赔偿金：  
（一）以暴力、威胁或者非法限制人身自由的手段强迫劳动的；  
（二）未按照劳动合同约定支付劳动报酬或者提供劳动条件的；  
（三）克扣或者无故拖欠劳动者工资的；  
（四）拒不支付劳动者延长工作时间工资报酬的；  
（五）低于当地最低工资标准支付劳动者工资的。

**Article 15** Where the employer commits any of the following acts, thereby compelling the worker to propose termination of the labor contract, the employer shall pay the worker's labor remuneration and economic compensation, and may also pay damages:  
(1) forcing the worker to work by means of violence, threats, or illegal restriction of personal freedom;  
(2) failing to pay labor remuneration or provide labor conditions as agreed in the labor contract;  
(3) deducting or delaying the worker's wages without justification;  
(4) refusing to pay the worker the remuneration for overtime work; or  
(5) paying the worker wages below the local minimum wage standard.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 劳动合同期满后，劳动者仍在原用人单位工作，原用人单位未表示异议的，视为双方同意以原条件继续履行劳动合同。一方提出终止劳动关系的，人民法院应当支持。  
根据[《劳动法》第二十条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动法.md#art-20)之规定，用人单位应当与劳动者签订无固定期限劳动合同而未签订的，人民法院可以视为双方之间存在无固定期限劳动合同关系，并以原劳动合同确定双方的权利义务关系。

**Article 16** Where after the expiration of a labor contract, the worker continues to work for the original employer and the original employer does not express any objection, it shall be deemed that both parties agree to continue performing the labor contract under the original terms. If either party proposes to terminate the labor relationship, the People's Court shall uphold such termination.  
In accordance with Article 20 of the Labor Law of the People's Republic of China, where the employer should have concluded a labor contract without a fixed term with the worker but failed to do so, the People's Court may deem that a labor contract without a fixed term relationship exists between the parties, and the rights and obligations of the parties shall be determined by reference to the original labor contract.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 劳动争议仲裁委员会作出仲裁裁决后，当事人对裁决中的部分事项不服，依法向人民法院起诉的，劳动争议仲裁裁决不发生法律效力。

**Article 17** After the labor dispute arbitration commission has rendered an arbitration award, if a party is dissatisfied with part of the award and brings a lawsuit in a People's Court according to law, the labor dispute arbitration award shall not become legally effective.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 劳动争议仲裁委员会对多个劳动者的劳动争议作出仲裁裁决后，部分劳动者对仲裁裁决不服，依法向人民法院起诉的，仲裁裁决对提出起诉的劳动者不发生法律效力；对未提出起诉的部分劳动者，发生法律效力，如其申请执行的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 18** After the labor dispute arbitration commission has rendered an arbitration award concerning a labor dispute involving multiple workers, if some of the workers are dissatisfied with the arbitration award and bring a lawsuit in a People's Court according to law, the arbitration award shall not become legally effective with respect to the workers who have brought the lawsuit; it shall become legally effective with respect to the workers who have not brought the lawsuit. If the latter apply for enforcement, the People's Court shall accept the application.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 用人单位根据[《劳动法》第四条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动法.md#art-4)之规定，通过民主程序制定的规章制度，不违反国家法律、行政法规及政策规定，并已向劳动者公示的，可以作为人民法院审理劳动争议案件的依据。

**Article 19** Rules and regulations formulated by the employer through democratic procedures in accordance with Article 4 of the Labor Law of the People's Republic of China, which do not violate the provisions of state laws, administrative regulations, and policies, and have been made known to the workers, may serve as the basis for the People's Court in hearing labor dispute cases.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 用人单位对劳动者作出的开除、除名、辞退等处理，或者因其他原因解除劳动合同确有错误的，人民法院可以依法判决予以撤销。  
对于追索劳动报酬、养老金、医疗费以及工伤保险待遇、经济补偿金、培训费及其他相关费用等案件，给付数额不当的，人民法院可以予以变更。

**Article 20** Where an employer's decision to expel, remove, dismiss, or otherwise terminate the labor contract with a worker is indeed erroneous, the People's Court may render a judgment to revoke such decision according to law.  
For cases involving claims for labor remuneration, pensions, medical expenses, work-related injury insurance benefits, economic compensation, training fees, and other relevant expenses, if the amount awarded is inappropriate, the People's Court may modify the amount.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条 当事人申请人民法院执行劳动争议仲裁机构作出的发生法律效力的裁决书、调解书，被申请人提出证据证明劳动争议仲裁裁决书、调解书有下列情形之一，并经审查核实的，人民法院可以根据[《民事诉讼法》第二百一十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-217)之规定，裁定不予执行：  
（一）裁决的事项不属于劳动争议仲裁范围，或者劳动争议仲裁机构无权仲裁的；  
（二）适用法律确有错误的；  
（三）仲裁员仲裁该案时，有徇私舞弊、枉法裁决行为的；  
（四）人民法院认定执行该劳动争议仲裁裁决违背社会公共利益的。  
人民法院在不予执行的裁定书中，应当告知当事人在收到裁定书之次日起三十日内，可以就该劳动争议事项向人民法院起诉。

**Article 21** Where a party applies to a People's Court for enforcement of a legally effective award or mediation statement issued by a labor dispute arbitration institution, and the respondent produces evidence proving that the labor dispute arbitration award or mediation statement falls under any of the following circumstances, and upon examination and verification, the People's Court may, in accordance with Article 217 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, rule to refuse enforcement:  
(1) the matter ruled upon is not within the scope of labor dispute arbitration, or the labor dispute arbitration institution has no authority to arbitrate;  
(2) the application of the law is indeed erroneous;  
(3) the arbitrator engaged in favoritism, fraud, or perversion of the law in arbitrating the case; or  
(4) the People's Court finds that enforcement of the labor dispute arbitration award would violate the public interest.  
In the ruling refusing enforcement, the People's Court shall inform the party that it may bring a lawsuit in the People's Court regarding the labor dispute matter within thirty days from the day following the receipt of the ruling.
