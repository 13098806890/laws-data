# 中华人民共和国公共文化服务保障法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2016-12-25  
**生效日期**：2017-03-01

> （2016年12月25日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十五次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　公共文化设施建设与管理 第三章　公共文化服务提供 第四章　保障措施 第五章　法律责任 第六章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强公共文化服务体系建设，丰富人民群众精神文化生活，传承中华优秀传统文化，弘扬社会主义核心价值观，增强文化自信，促进中国特色社会主义文化繁荣发展，提高全民族文明素质，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称公共文化服务，是指由政府主导、社会力量参与，以满足公民基本文化需求为主要目的而提供的公共文化设施、文化产品、文化活动以及其他相关服务。 第三条　公共文化服务应当坚持社会主义先进文化前进方向，坚持以人民为中心，坚持以社会主义核心价值观为引领；应当按照“百花齐放、百家争鸣”的方针，支持优秀公共文化产品的创作生产，丰富公共文化服务内容。 第四条　县级以上人民政府应当将公共文化服务纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，按照公益性、基本性、均等性、便利性的要求，加强公共文化设施建设，完善公共文化服务体系，提高公共文化服务效能。 第五条　国务院根据公民基本文化需求和经济社会发展水平，制定并调整国家基本公共文化服务指导标准。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据国家基本公共文化服务指导标准，结合当地实际需求、财政能力和文化特色，制定并调整本行政区域的基本公共文化服务实施标准。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强公共文化服务体系建设，丰富人民群众精神文化生活，传承中华优秀传统文化，弘扬社会主义核心价值观，增强文化自信，促进中国特色社会主义文化繁荣发展，提高全民族文明素质，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purposes of strengthening the public cultural service system, enriching the spiritual and cultural life of the people, carrying forward the fine traditional Chinese culture, promoting the core socialist values, enhancing cultural confidence, facilitating the prosperity and development of socialist culture with Chinese characteristics, and improving the civilization quality of the whole nation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称公共文化服务，是指由政府主导、社会力量参与，以满足公民基本文化需求为主要目的而提供的公共文化设施、文化产品、文化活动以及其他相关服务。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "public cultural services" means the public cultural facilities, cultural products, cultural activities and other related services which are provided, under the leadership of the government and with the participation of social forces, with the main purpose of meeting the basic cultural needs of citizens.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　公共文化服务应当坚持社会主义先进文化前进方向，坚持以人民为中心，坚持以社会主义核心价值观为引领；应当按照“百花齐放、百家争鸣”的方针，支持优秀公共文化产品的创作生产，丰富公共文化服务内容。

**Article 3** Public cultural services shall adhere to the direction of advanced socialist culture, adhere to a people-centered approach, and adhere to the guidance of the core socialist values; and shall, in accordance with the policy of "letting a hundred flowers bloom and a hundred schools of thought contend," support the creation and production of outstanding public cultural products and enrich the content of public cultural services.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府应当将公共文化服务纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，按照公益性、基本性、均等性、便利性的要求，加强公共文化设施建设，完善公共文化服务体系，提高公共文化服务效能。

**Article 4** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate public cultural services into their respective plans for national economic and social development, strengthen the construction of public cultural facilities, improve the public cultural service system, and enhance the efficiency of public cultural services in accordance with the requirements of public welfare, basic nature, equalization, and convenience.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院根据公民基本文化需求和经济社会发展水平，制定并调整国家基本公共文化服务指导标准。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据国家基本公共文化服务指导标准，结合当地实际需求、财政能力和文化特色，制定并调整本行政区域的基本公共文化服务实施标准。

**Article 5** The State Council shall, based on the basic cultural needs of citizens and the level of economic and social development, formulate and adjust the national guiding standards for basic public cultural services.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the national guiding standards for basic public cultural services and in light of local actual needs, financial capacity, and cultural characteristics, formulate and adjust the implementing standards for basic public cultural services within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院建立公共文化服务综合协调机制，指导、协调、推动全国公共文化服务工作。国务院文化主管部门承担综合协调具体职责。  
地方各级人民政府应当加强对公共文化服务的统筹协调，推动实现共建共享。

**Article 6** The State Council shall establish a comprehensive coordination mechanism for public cultural services to guide, coordinate, and promote public cultural services nationwide. The competent cultural department of the State Council shall undertake the specific duties of comprehensive coordination.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen the overall coordination of public cultural services and promote the achievement of joint development and sharing.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院文化主管部门、新闻出版广电主管部门依照本法和国务院规定的职责负责全国的公共文化服务工作；国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责相关公共文化服务工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府文化、新闻出版广电主管部门根据其职责负责本行政区域内的公共文化服务工作；县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责相关公共文化服务工作。

**Article 7** The competent cultural department of the State Council and the competent department of press, publication, radio, film and television under the State Council shall, in accordance with this Law and the duties prescribed by the State Council, be responsible for public cultural services nationwide; other relevant departments under the State Council shall be responsible for related public cultural services within their respective functions and duties.  
The competent cultural departments and the competent departments of press, publication, radio, film and television of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their functions and duties, be responsible for public cultural services within their respective administrative regions; other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for related public cultural services within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家扶助革命老区、民族地区、边疆地区、贫困地区的公共文化服务，促进公共文化服务均衡协调发展。

**Article 8** The state shall support public cultural services in old revolutionary base areas, ethnic minority areas, border areas, and poverty-stricken areas, and promote the balanced and coordinated development of public cultural services.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各级人民政府应当根据未成年人、老年人、残疾人和流动人口等群体的特点与需求，提供相应的公共文化服务。

**Article 9** People's governments at all levels shall, in light of the characteristics and needs of minors, the elderly, the disabled, and the floating population, provide corresponding public cultural services.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家鼓励和支持公共文化服务与学校教育相结合，充分发挥公共文化服务的社会教育功能，提高青少年思想道德和科学文化素质。

**Article 10** The state encourages and supports the integration of public cultural services with school education, gives full play to the social education function of public cultural services, and improves the ideological and moral quality as well as the scientific and cultural quality of young people.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家鼓励和支持发挥科技在公共文化服务中的作用，推动运用现代信息技术和传播技术，提高公众的科学素养和公共文化服务水平。

**Article 11** The state encourages and supports the role of science and technology in public cultural services, promotes the application of modern information technology and communication technology, and improves the scientific literacy of the public and the level of public cultural services.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家鼓励和支持在公共文化服务领域开展国际合作与交流。

**Article 12** The state encourages and supports international cooperation and exchange in the field of public cultural services.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家鼓励和支持公民、法人和其他组织参与公共文化服务。  
对在公共文化服务中作出突出贡献的公民、法人和其他组织，依法给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 13** The state encourages and supports citizens, legal persons, and other organizations to participate in public cultural services.  
Citizens, legal persons, and other organizations that have made outstanding contributions to public cultural services shall be commended and rewarded according to law.

### 第二章　公共文化设施建设与管理

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本法所称公共文化设施是指用于提供公共文化服务的建筑物、场地和设备，主要包括图书馆、博物馆、文化馆（站）、美术馆、科技馆、纪念馆、体育场馆、工人文化宫、青少年宫、妇女儿童活动中心、老年人活动中心、乡镇（街道）和村（社区）基层综合性文化服务中心、农家（职工）书屋、公共阅报栏（屏）、广播电视播出传输覆盖设施、公共数字文化服务点等。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当将本行政区域内的公共文化设施目录及有关信息予以公布。

**Article 14** For the purposes of this Law, "public cultural facilities" means the buildings, venues, and equipment used for providing public cultural services, mainly including libraries, museums, cultural centers (stations), art galleries, science and technology museums, memorial halls, sports venues, workers' cultural palaces, youth palaces, women and children's activity centers, seniors' activity centers, township (subdistrict) and village (community) grassroots comprehensive cultural service centers, farmers' (staff) bookstores, public newspaper reading boards (screens), radio and television broadcasting, transmission, and coverage facilities, public digital cultural service points, and the like.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall publish the catalog of public cultural facilities and relevant information within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级以上地方人民政府应当将公共文化设施建设纳入本级城乡规划，根据国家基本公共文化服务指导标准、省级基本公共文化服务实施标准，结合当地经济社会发展水平、人口状况、环境条件、文化特色，合理确定公共文化设施的种类、数量、规模以及布局，形成场馆服务、流动服务和数字服务相结合的公共文化设施网络。  
公共文化设施的选址，应当征求公众意见，符合公共文化设施的功能和特点，有利于发挥其作用。

**Article 15** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the construction of public cultural facilities into their respective urban and rural plans, rationally determine the types, quantities, sizes, and layout of public cultural facilities in accordance with the national guiding standards for basic public cultural services and the provincial implementing standards for basic public cultural services, and in light of the local level of economic and social development, population conditions, environmental conditions, and cultural characteristics, thereby forming a network of public cultural facilities that combines venue services, mobile services, and digital services.  
The location selection of public cultural facilities shall solicit public opinions, comply with the functions and features of public cultural facilities, and be conducive to their functioning.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　公共文化设施的建设用地，应当符合土地利用总体规划和城乡规划，并依照法定程序审批。  
任何单位和个人不得侵占公共文化设施建设用地或者擅自改变其用途。因特殊情况需要调整公共文化设施建设用地的，应当重新确定建设用地。调整后的公共文化设施建设用地不得少于原有面积。  
新建、改建、扩建居民住宅区，应当按照有关规定、标准，规划和建设配套的公共文化设施。

**Article 16** Land used for the construction of public cultural facilities shall comply with the overall plan for land use and the urban and rural plans, and shall be examined and approved in accordance with statutory procedures.  
No unit or individual may encroach upon the land designated for public cultural facilities construction or alter its use without authorization. Where the land for public cultural facilities construction needs to be adjusted due to special circumstances, the land for construction shall be re-determined. The adjusted land for public cultural facilities construction shall not be less than the original area.  
When building new residential areas or rebuilding or expanding existing ones, the supporting public cultural facilities shall be planned and constructed in accordance with relevant regulations and standards.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公共文化设施的设计和建设，应当符合实用、安全、科学、美观、环保、节约的要求和国家规定的标准，并配置无障碍设施设备。

**Article 17** The design and construction of public cultural facilities shall meet the requirements of practicality, safety, scientificity, aesthetics, environmental protection, and economy, as well as the standards prescribed by the state, and shall be equipped with accessible facilities and equipment.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　地方各级人民政府可以采取新建、改建、扩建、合建、租赁、利用现有公共设施等多种方式，加强乡镇（街道）、村（社区）基层综合性文化服务中心建设，推动基层有关公共设施的统一管理、综合利用，并保障其正常运行。

**Article 18** Local people's governments at all levels may adopt various methods, such as new construction, reconstruction, expansion, co-construction, leasing, and utilization of existing public facilities, to strengthen the construction of grassroots comprehensive cultural service centers in townships (subdistricts) and villages (communities), promote the unified management and comprehensive utilization of relevant public facilities at the grassroots level, and ensure their normal operation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　任何单位和个人不得擅自拆除公共文化设施，不得擅自改变公共文化设施的功能、用途或者妨碍其正常运行，不得侵占、挪用公共文化设施，不得将公共文化设施用于与公共文化服务无关的商业经营活动。  
因城乡建设确需拆除公共文化设施，或者改变其功能、用途的，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定重建、改建，并坚持先建设后拆除或者建设拆除同时进行的原则。重建、改建的公共文化设施的设施配置标准、建筑面积等不得降低。

**Article 19** No unit or individual may dismantle public cultural facilities without authorization, alter their functions or purposes without authorization, or hinder their normal operation; may not occupy or misappropriate public cultural facilities; or may not use public cultural facilities for commercial business activities unrelated to public cultural services.  
Where the dismantling of public cultural facilities or the alteration of their functions or purposes is truly necessary due to urban or rural construction, the facilities shall be rebuilt or renovated in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations, and the principle of building first and then demolishing, or carrying out construction and demolition simultaneously, shall be adhered to. The facility configuration standards and building area of the rebuilt or renovated public cultural facilities shall not be reduced.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　公共文化设施管理单位应当按照国家规定的标准，配置和更新必需的服务内容和设备，加强公共文化设施经常性维护管理工作，保障公共文化设施的正常使用和运转。

**Article 20** The administrative units of public cultural facilities shall, in accordance with the standards prescribed by the state, equip and update the necessary service contents and equipment, strengthen the routine maintenance and management of public cultural facilities, and ensure the normal use and operation of public cultural facilities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　公共文化设施管理单位应当建立健全管理制度和服务规范，建立公共文化设施资产统计报告制度和公共文化服务开展情况的年报制度。

**Article 21** The administrative units of public cultural facilities shall establish and improve management systems and service norms, establish an asset statistics and reporting system for public cultural facilities, and an annual report system on the provision of public cultural services.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　公共文化设施管理单位应当建立健全安全管理制度，开展公共文化设施及公众活动的安全评价，依法配备安全保护设备和人员，保障公共文化设施和公众活动安全。

**Article 22** The administrative units of public cultural facilities shall establish and improve safety management systems, conduct safety assessments of public cultural facilities and public activities, equip safety protection equipment and personnel according to law, and ensure the safety of public cultural facilities and public activities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　各级人民政府应当建立有公众参与的公共文化设施使用效能考核评价制度，公共文化设施管理单位应当根据评价结果改进工作，提高服务质量。

**Article 23** People's governments at all levels shall establish an assessment and evaluation system for the use efficiency of public cultural facilities with public participation, and the administrative units of public cultural facilities shall improve their work and enhance the quality of services based on the evaluation results.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家推动公共图书馆、博物馆、文化馆等公共文化设施管理单位根据其功能定位建立健全法人治理结构，吸收有关方面代表、专业人士和公众参与管理。

**Article 24** The state shall promote public libraries, museums, cultural centers, and other administrative units of public cultural facilities to establish and improve corporate governance structures in accordance with their functional positioning, and to absorb representatives from relevant sectors, professionals, and the public to participate in management.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家鼓励和支持公民、法人和其他组织兴建、捐建或者与政府部门合作建设公共文化设施，鼓励公民、法人和其他组织依法参与公共文化设施的运营和管理。

**Article 25** The state encourages and supports citizens, legal persons, and other organizations to construct, donate for the construction of, or cooperatively construct public cultural facilities with government departments, and encourages citizens, legal persons, and other organizations to participate in the operation and management of public cultural facilities according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　公众在使用公共文化设施时，应当遵守公共秩序，爱护公共设施，不得损坏公共设施设备和物品。

**Article 26** When using public cultural facilities, the public shall observe public order, take good care of public facilities, and shall not damage the equipment and articles of public facilities.

### 第三章　公共文化服务提供

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　各级人民政府应当充分利用公共文化设施，促进优秀公共文化产品的提供和传播，支持开展全民阅读、全民普法、全民健身、全民科普和艺术普及、优秀传统文化传承活动。

**Article 27** People's governments at all levels shall make full use of public cultural facilities to promote the provision and dissemination of outstanding public cultural products, and support the activities of nationwide reading, nationwide legal education, nationwide fitness, nationwide science popularization, art popularization, and the inheritance of fine traditional culture.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　设区的市级、县级地方人民政府应当根据国家基本公共文化服务指导标准和省、自治区、直辖市基本公共文化服务实施标准，结合当地实际，制定公布本行政区域公共文化服务目录并组织实施。

**Article 28** Local people's governments at the city level with districts and the county level shall, in accordance with the national guiding standards for basic public cultural services and the provincial, autonomous regional, and municipal implementing standards for basic public cultural services, and in light of local realities, formulate and publish the catalog of public cultural services within their respective administrative regions and organize their implementation.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　公益性文化单位应当完善服务项目、丰富服务内容，创造条件向公众提供免费或者优惠的文艺演出、陈列展览、电影放映、广播电视节目收听收看、阅读服务、艺术培训等，并为公众开展文化活动提供支持和帮助。  
国家鼓励经营性文化单位提供免费或者优惠的公共文化产品和文化活动。

**Article 29** Non-profit cultural units shall improve service items, enrich service content, create conditions to provide the public with free or discounted artistic performances, exhibitions, film screenings, radio and television program reception, reading services, art training, and the like, and provide support and assistance for the public to carry out cultural activities.  
The state encourages for-profit cultural units to provide free or discounted public cultural products and cultural activities.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　基层综合性文化服务中心应当加强资源整合，建立完善公共文化服务网络，充分发挥统筹服务功能，为公众提供书报阅读、影视观赏、戏曲表演、普法教育、艺术普及、科学普及、广播播送、互联网上网和群众性文化体育活动等公共文化服务，并根据其功能特点，因地制宜提供其他公共服务。

**Article 30** Grassroots comprehensive cultural service centers shall strengthen resource integration, establish and improve the public cultural service network, give full play to the overall service function, and provide the public with such public cultural services as book and newspaper reading, film and television viewing, opera performances, legal education, art popularization, science popularization, radio broadcasting, Internet access, and mass cultural and sports activities, and shall, based on their functional characteristics, provide other public services in light of local conditions.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　公共文化设施应当根据其功能、特点，按照国家有关规定，向公众免费或者优惠开放。  
公共文化设施开放收取费用的，应当每月定期向中小学生免费开放。  
公共文化设施开放或者提供培训服务等收取费用的，应当报经县级以上人民政府有关部门批准；收取的费用，应当用于公共文化设施的维护、管理和事业发展，不得挪作他用。  
公共文化设施管理单位应当公示服务项目和开放时间；临时停止开放的，应当及时公告。

**Article 31** Public cultural facilities shall, in accordance with their functions and features and with the relevant regulations of the state, be open to the public free of charge or at a discount.  
Where a fee is charged for opening a public cultural facility, it shall be open to primary and secondary school students free of charge on a regular monthly basis.  
Where a fee is charged for opening a public cultural facility or for providing training services, etc., approval from the relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall be obtained; the fees collected shall be used for the maintenance, management, and career development of public cultural facilities and shall not be misappropriated for any other purpose.  
The administrative units of public cultural facilities shall publicize service items and opening hours; where they are temporarily closed, a notice shall be released in a timely manner.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家鼓励和支持机关、学校、企业事业单位的文化体育设施向公众开放。

**Article 32** The state encourages and supports the opening of cultural and sports facilities of government organs, schools, enterprises, and public institutions to the public.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家统筹规划公共数字文化建设，构建标准统一、互联互通的公共数字文化服务网络，建设公共文化信息资源库，实现基层网络服务共建共享。  
国家支持开发数字文化产品，推动利用宽带互联网、移动互联网、广播电视网和卫星网络提供公共文化服务。  
地方各级人民政府应当加强基层公共文化设施的数字化和网络建设，提高数字化和网络服务能力。

**Article 33** The state shall make overall plans for the construction of public digital culture, build a public digital cultural service network with unified standards and interconnection, construct a public cultural information resource database, and realize the joint construction and sharing of grassroots network services.  
The state supports the development of digital cultural products and promotes the provision of public cultural services using broadband Internet, mobile Internet, radio and television networks, and satellite networks.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen the digitalization and network construction of grassroots public cultural facilities and improve their digital and network service capabilities.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　地方各级人民政府应当采取多种方式，因地制宜提供流动文化服务。

**Article 34** Local people's governments at all levels shall adopt various methods to provide mobile cultural services in light of local conditions.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家重点增加农村地区图书、报刊、戏曲、电影、广播电视节目、网络信息内容、节庆活动、体育健身活动等公共文化产品供给，促进城乡公共文化服务均等化。  
面向农村提供的图书、报刊、电影等公共文化产品应当符合农村特点和需求，提高针对性和时效性。

**Article 35** The state shall focus on increasing the supply of public cultural products such as books, newspapers and periodicals, traditional opera, films, radio and television programs, online information content, festival activities, and sports and fitness activities in rural areas, so as to promote the equalization of urban and rural public cultural services.  
Public cultural products provided for rural areas, such as books, newspapers and periodicals, and films, shall conform to rural characteristics and needs, and their pertinence and timeliness shall be enhanced.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　地方各级人民政府应当根据当地实际情况，在人员流动量较大的公共场所、务工人员较为集中的区域以及留守妇女儿童较为集中的农村地区，配备必要的设施，采取多种形式，提供便利可及的公共文化服务。

**Article 36** Local people's governments at all levels shall, in light of local actual conditions, equip necessary facilities and adopt various forms in public places with large flows of people, areas where migrant workers are relatively concentrated, and rural areas where left-behind women and children are relatively concentrated, to provide convenient and accessible public cultural services.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家鼓励公民主动参与公共文化服务，自主开展健康文明的群众性文化体育活动；地方各级人民政府应当给予必要的指导、支持和帮助。  
居民委员会、村民委员会应当根据居民的需求开展群众性文化体育活动，并协助当地人民政府有关部门开展公共文化服务相关工作。  
国家机关、社会组织、企业事业单位应当结合自身特点和需要，组织开展群众性文化体育活动，丰富职工文化生活。

**Article 37** The state encourages citizens to actively participate in public cultural services and to independently carry out healthy and civilized mass cultural and sports activities; local people's governments at all levels shall provide necessary guidance, support, and assistance.  
Residents' committees and villagers' committees shall, based on the needs of residents, carry out mass cultural and sports activities and assist the relevant departments of local people's governments in carrying out work related to public cultural services.  
State organs, social organizations, enterprises, and public institutions shall, in light of their own characteristics and needs, organize and carry out mass cultural and sports activities to enrich the cultural life of employees.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　地方各级人民政府应当加强面向在校学生的公共文化服务，支持学校开展适合在校学生特点的文化体育活动，促进德智体美教育。

**Article 38** Local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen public cultural services for enrolled students, support schools in carrying out cultural and sports activities suitable for the characteristics of enrolled students, and promote education in morality, intelligence, physical fitness, and aesthetics.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　地方各级人民政府应当支持军队基层文化建设，丰富军营文化体育活动，加强军民文化融合。

**Article 39** Local people's governments at all levels shall support the cultural construction at the grassroots level in the military, enrich cultural and sports activities in military camps, and strengthen military-civilian cultural integration.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家加强民族语言文字文化产品的供给，加强优秀公共文化产品的民族语言文字译制及其在民族地区的传播，鼓励和扶助民族文化产品的创作生产，支持开展具有民族特色的群众性文化体育活动。

**Article 40** The state shall strengthen the supply of cultural products in ethnic languages, strengthen the dubbing or subtitling of outstanding public cultural products into ethnic languages and their dissemination in ethnic minority areas, encourage and assist the creation and production of ethnic cultural products, and support the carrying out of mass cultural and sports activities with ethnic characteristics.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定政府购买公共文化服务的指导性意见和目录。国务院有关部门和县级以上地方人民政府应当根据指导性意见和目录，结合实际情况，确定购买的具体项目和内容，及时向社会公布。

**Article 41** The State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate guiding opinions and catalogs for government procurement of public cultural services. Relevant departments of the State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the guiding opinions and catalogs and in light of actual conditions, determine the specific items and contents to be procured and publish them to the public in a timely manner.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家鼓励和支持公民、法人和其他组织通过兴办实体、资助项目、赞助活动、提供设施、捐赠产品等方式，参与提供公共文化服务。

**Article 42** The state encourages and supports citizens, legal persons, and other organizations to participate in providing public cultural services by establishing entities, funding projects, sponsoring activities, providing facilities, donating products, and other means.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家倡导和鼓励公民、法人和其他组织参与文化志愿服务。  
公共文化设施管理单位应当建立文化志愿服务机制，组织开展文化志愿服务活动。  
县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当对文化志愿活动给予必要的指导和支持，并建立管理评价、教育培训和激励保障机制。

**Article 43** The state advocates and encourages citizens, legal persons, and other organizations to participate in cultural volunteer services.  
The administrative units of public cultural facilities shall establish a cultural volunteer service mechanism and organize cultural volunteer service activities.  
Relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall provide necessary guidance and support for cultural volunteer activities, and establish mechanisms for management evaluation, education and training, and incentive and guarantee.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　任何组织和个人不得利用公共文化设施、文化产品、文化活动以及其他相关服务，从事危害国家安全、损害社会公共利益和其他违反法律法规的活动。

**Article 44** No organization or individual may use public cultural facilities, cultural products, cultural activities, or other related services to engage in activities that endanger national security, harm public interests, or otherwise violate laws and regulations.

### 第四章　保障措施

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国务院和地方各级人民政府应当根据公共文化服务的事权和支出责任，将公共文化服务经费纳入本级预算，安排公共文化服务所需资金。

**Article 45** The State Council and local people's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the division of powers and expenditure responsibilities for public cultural services, incorporate the funds for public cultural services into their respective budgets and allocate the funds needed for public cultural services.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当增加投入，通过转移支付等方式，重点扶助革命老区、民族地区、边疆地区、贫困地区开展公共文化服务。  
国家鼓励和支持经济发达地区对革命老区、民族地区、边疆地区、贫困地区的公共文化服务提供援助。

**Article 46** The State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall increase investment and, through transfer payments and other means, provide key support to old revolutionary base areas, ethnic minority areas, border areas, and poverty-stricken areas in carrying out public cultural services.  
The state encourages and supports economically developed areas to provide assistance to old revolutionary base areas, ethnic minority areas, border areas, and poverty-stricken areas in public cultural services.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　免费或者优惠开放的公共文化设施，按照国家规定享受补助。

**Article 47** Public cultural facilities that are open free of charge or at a discount shall enjoy subsidies in accordance with state regulations.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家鼓励社会资本依法投入公共文化服务，拓宽公共文化服务资金来源渠道。

**Article 48** The state encourages the investment of social capital in public cultural services according to law, and expands the channels of fund sources for public cultural services.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家采取政府购买服务等措施，支持公民、法人和其他组织参与提供公共文化服务。

**Article 49** The state adopts measures such as government procurement of services to support citizens, legal persons, and other organizations in participating in the provision of public cultural services.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　公民、法人和其他组织通过公益性社会团体或者县级以上人民政府及其部门，捐赠财产用于公共文化服务的，依法享受税收优惠。  
国家鼓励通过捐赠等方式设立公共文化服务基金，专门用于公共文化服务。

**Article 50** Where citizens, legal persons, and other organizations donate property for public cultural services through public welfare social organizations or people's governments at or above the county level and their departments, they shall enjoy tax preferences according to law.  
The state encourages the establishment of public cultural service funds through donations and other means, which shall be used exclusively for public cultural services.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　地方各级人民政府应当按照公共文化设施的功能、任务和服务人口规模，合理设置公共文化服务岗位，配备相应专业人员。

**Article 51** Local people's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the functions, tasks and served population size of public cultural facilities, reasonably set up public cultural service posts and allocate corresponding professional personnel.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家鼓励和支持文化专业人员、高校毕业生和志愿者到基层从事公共文化服务工作。

**Article 52** The State shall encourage and support cultural professionals, college graduates and volunteers to engage in public cultural service work at the grassroots level.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家鼓励和支持公民、法人和其他组织依法成立公共文化服务领域的社会组织，推动公共文化服务社会化、专业化发展。

**Article 53** The State shall encourage and support citizens, legal persons and other organizations to lawfully establish social organizations in the field of public cultural services, and promote the socialized and professional development of public cultural services.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家支持公共文化服务理论研究，加强多层次专业人才教育和培训。

**Article 54** The State shall support theoretical research on public cultural services, and strengthen multi-level professional talent education and training.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　县级以上人民政府应当建立健全公共文化服务资金使用的监督和统计公告制度，加强绩效考评，确保资金用于公共文化服务。任何单位和个人不得侵占、挪用公共文化服务资金。  
审计机关应当依法加强对公共文化服务资金的审计监督。

**Article 55** People's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve the supervision and statistical announcement system for the use of public cultural service funds, strengthen performance appraisal, and ensure that funds are used for public cultural services. No entity or individual may embezzle or misappropriate public cultural service funds.  
Audit authorities shall, according to law, strengthen audit supervision over public cultural service funds.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　各级人民政府应当加强对公共文化服务工作的监督检查，建立反映公众文化需求的征询反馈制度和有公众参与的公共文化服务考核评价制度，并将考核评价结果作为确定补贴或者奖励的依据。

**Article 56** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen supervision and inspection of public cultural service work, establish a consultation and feedback system reflecting the cultural needs of the public and a public cultural service assessment and evaluation system with public participation, and shall take the assessment and evaluation results as the basis for determining subsidies or rewards.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　各级人民政府及有关部门应当及时公开公共文化服务信息，主动接受社会监督。  
新闻媒体应当积极开展公共文化服务的宣传报道，并加强舆论监督。

**Article 57** People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall timely disclose public cultural service information and proactively accept social supervision.  
News media shall actively carry out publicity and reporting on public cultural services and strengthen public opinion supervision.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本法规定，地方各级人民政府和县级以上人民政府有关部门未履行公共文化服务保障职责的，由其上级机关或者监察机关责令限期改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 58** Where, in violation of this Law, local people's governments at all levels and relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level fail to perform their duties of guaranteeing public cultural services, the superior authority or supervisory authority shall order them to make corrections within a time limit; if the circumstances are serious, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable personnel shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本法规定，地方各级人民政府和县级以上人民政府有关部门，有下列行为之一的，由其上级机关或者监察机关责令限期改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）侵占、挪用公共文化服务资金的；  
（二）擅自拆除、侵占、挪用公共文化设施，或者改变其功能、用途，或者妨碍其正常运行的；  
（三）未依照本法规定重建公共文化设施的；  
（四）滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的。

**Article 59** Where, in violation of this Law, local people's governments at all levels and relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level commit any of the following acts, the superior authority or supervisory authority shall order them to make corrections within a time limit; if the circumstances are serious, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable personnel shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) embezzling or misappropriating public cultural service funds;  
(2) demolishing, embezzling or misappropriating public cultural facilities without authorization, or changing their functions or purposes, or hindering their normal operation;  
(3) failing to rebuild public cultural facilities in accordance with the provisions of this Law;  
(4) abusing power, neglecting duties, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本法规定，侵占公共文化设施的建设用地或者擅自改变其用途的，由县级以上地方人民政府土地主管部门、城乡规划主管部门依据各自职责责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，由作出决定的机关依法强制执行，或者依法申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 60** Where, in violation of this Law, any person encroaches on the construction land for public cultural facilities or changes its use without authorization, the land administrative department or the urban and rural planning administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, order corrections within a time limit; if corrections are not made within the time limit, the organ that made the decision shall enforce the decision according to law, or apply to a People's Court for compulsory execution according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反本法规定，公共文化设施管理单位有下列情形之一的，由其主管部门责令限期改正；造成严重后果的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分：  
（一）未按照规定对公众开放的；  
（二）未公示服务项目、开放时间等事项的；  
（三）未建立安全管理制度的；  
（四）因管理不善造成损失的。

**Article 61** Where, in violation of this Law, a public cultural facility management unit falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent authority shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if serious consequences are caused, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable personnel shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to open to the public in accordance with regulations;  
(2) failing to publicize service items, opening hours and other matters;  
(3) failing to establish a safety management system;  
(4) causing losses due to poor management.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反本法规定，公共文化设施管理单位有下列行为之一的，由其主管部门或者价格主管部门责令限期改正，没收违法所得，违法所得五千元以上的，并处违法所得两倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得五千元以下的，可以处一万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分：  
（一）开展与公共文化设施功能、用途不符的服务活动的；  
（二）对应当免费开放的公共文化设施收费或者变相收费的；  
（三）收取费用未用于公共文化设施的维护、管理和事业发展，挪作他用的。

**Article 62** Where, in violation of this Law, a public cultural facility management unit commits any of the following acts, the competent authority or the price authority shall order it to make corrections within a time limit, confiscate illegal gains, and if the illegal gains are 5,000 yuan or more, concurrently impose a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 5,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan may be imposed; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable personnel shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) carrying out service activities inconsistent with the functions and purposes of the public cultural facility;  
(2) charging fees or imposing disguised charges for public cultural facilities that should be open to the public free of charge;  
(3) failing to use the fees collected for the maintenance, management and career development of the public cultural facility, and diverting them to other uses.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本法规定，损害他人民事权益的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 63** Where, in violation of this Law, any person causes damage to the civil rights and interests of others, he shall bear civil liability according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　境外自然人、法人和其他组织在中国境内从事公共文化服务的，应当符合相关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 64** Where any overseas natural person, legal person or other organization engages in public cultural services within the territory of China, they shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　本法自2017年3月1日起施行。

**Article 65** This Law shall come into force as of March 1, 2017.
