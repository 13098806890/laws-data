# 最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律若干问题的解释（三）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2010〕12号  
**公布日期**：2010-07-12  
**生效日期**：2010-09-14

> 最高人民法院《关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律若干问题的解释（三）》已于2010年7月12日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1489次会议通过，现予公布，自2010年9月14日起施行。 2010年9月13日 最高人民法院 关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律 若干问题的解释（三）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  劳动者以用人单位未为其办理社会保险手续，且社会保险经办机构不能补办导致其无法享受社会保险待遇为由，要求用人单位赔偿损失而发生争议的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 1** Where a dispute arises because a worker claims that an employer fails to undergo social insurance formalities for the worker, and the social insurance administrative body cannot remedy the situation so that the worker is unable to enjoy social insurance benefits, and the worker requests the employer to compensate for the loss, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  因企业自主进行改制引发的争议，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 2** A dispute arising from the restructuring initiated by an enterprise on its own shall be accepted by the People's Court.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  劳动者依据[劳动合同法第八十五条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-85)规定，向人民法院提起诉讼，要求用人单位支付加付赔偿金的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 3** Where, in accordance with Article 85 of the Labor Contract Law, a worker files a prosecution in the People's Court demanding additional compensation from the employer, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  劳动者与未办理营业执照、营业执照被吊销或者营业期限届满仍继续经营的用人单位发生争议的，应当将用人单位或者其出资人列为当事人。

**Article 4** Where a dispute arises between a worker and an employer that has not obtained a business license, whose business license has been revoked, or that continues operation after the expiration of its term of operation, the employer or its investor shall be listed as a party.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  未办理营业执照、营业执照被吊销或者营业期限届满仍继续经营的用人单位，以挂靠等方式借用他人营业执照经营的，应当将用人单位和营业执照出借方列为当事人。

**Article 5** Where an employer that has not obtained a business license, whose business license has been revoked, or that continues operation after the expiration of its term of operation, operates by borrowing another person's business license through affiliation or similar means, the employer and the lender of the business license shall be listed as parties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  当事人不服劳动人事争议仲裁委员会作出的仲裁裁决，依法向人民法院提起诉讼，人民法院审查认为仲裁裁决遗漏了必须共同参加仲裁的当事人的，应当依法追加遗漏的人为诉讼当事人。  
被追加的当事人应当承担责任的，人民法院应当一并处理。

**Article 6** Where a party, not satisfied with the arbitral award made by the labor dispute arbitration commission, files a lawsuit in the People's Court according to law, and the People's Court, after review, finds that the arbitral award has omitted a party that must jointly participate in the arbitration, the omitted person shall be added as a party to the proceedings according to law.  
If the added party shall bear liability, the People's Court shall handle the matter together.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  用人单位与其招用的已经依法享受养老保险待遇或领取退休金的人员发生用工争议，向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应当按劳务关系处理。

**Article 7** Where an employer, in a dispute over employment with a person it has hired who has already enjoyed pension insurance benefits or receives a pension according to law, files a lawsuit in the People's Court, the People's Court shall handle the case as a labor service relationship.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  企业停薪留职人员、未达到法定退休年龄的内退人员、下岗待岗人员以及企业经营性停产放长假人员，因与新的用人单位发生用工争议，依法向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应当按劳动关系处理。

**Article 8** Where a person who has suspended payment of wages and retained personnel status in an enterprise, an internal retiree who has not reached the statutory retirement age, a laid-off and waiting-for-job person, or a person who has been placed on long-term leave due to the enterprise's operational suspension, files a lawsuit in the People's Court according to law due to a dispute over employment with a new employer, the People's Court shall handle the case as a labor relationship.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  劳动者主张加班费的，应当就加班事实的存在承担举证责任。但劳动者有证据证明用人单位掌握加班事实存在的证据，用人单位不提供的，由用人单位承担不利后果。

**Article 9** Where a worker claims overtime pay, the worker shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the existence of overtime work. However, if the worker has evidence proving that the employer possesses evidence of the existence of overtime work, and the employer fails to provide such evidence, the employer shall bear the adverse consequences.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  劳动者与用人单位就解除或者终止劳动合同办理相关手续、支付工资报酬、加班费、经济补偿或者赔偿金等达成的协议，不违反法律、行政法规的强制性规定，且不存在欺诈、胁迫或者乘人之危情形的，应当认定有效。  
前款协议存在重大误解或者显失公平情形，当事人请求撤销的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 10** Where a worker and an employer reach an agreement on matters such as going through relevant formalities for dissolution or termination of a labor contract, payment of wages, overtime pay, economic compensation, or damages, and such agreement does not violate the mandatory provisions of laws or administrative regulations, and there is no fraud, coercion, or exploitation of another's distress, the agreement shall be deemed valid.  
If the agreement referred to in the preceding paragraph contains a material misunderstanding or is obviously unfair, and a party requests its revocation, the People's Court shall support the request.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  劳动人事争议仲裁委员会作出的调解书已经发生法律效力，一方当事人反悔提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 11** Where a mediation document issued by the labor dispute arbitration commission has taken legal effect, and one party files a lawsuit after reneging on the agreement, the People's Court shall not accept the case; if the case has been accepted, a ruling shall be made to dismiss the prosecution.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  劳动人事争议仲裁委员会逾期未作出受理决定或仲裁裁决，当事人直接提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理，但申请仲裁的案件存在下列事由的除外：  
（一）移送管辖的；  
（二）正在送达或送达延误的；  
（三）等待另案诉讼结果、评残结论的；  
（四）正在等待劳动人事争议仲裁委员会开庭的；  
（五）启动鉴定程序或者委托其他部门调查取证的；  
（六）其他正当事由。  
当事人以劳动人事争议仲裁委员会逾期未作出仲裁裁决为由提起诉讼的，应当提交劳动人事争议仲裁委员会出具的受理通知书或者其他已接受仲裁申请的凭证或证明。

**Article 12** Where the labor dispute arbitration commission fails to make a decision on acceptance or an arbitral award within the time limit, and the party directly files a lawsuit, the People's Court shall accept the case, except where the arbitration application involves any of the following matters:  
(1) transfer of jurisdiction;  
(2) ongoing service or delay in service;  
(3) awaiting the outcome of another case or the result of disability assessment;  
(4) awaiting a hearing before the labor dispute arbitration commission;  
(5) initiation of an expert evaluation procedure or entrusting other departments to investigate and collect evidence;  
(6) other justifiable reasons.  
Where a party files a lawsuit on the ground that the labor dispute arbitration commission has failed to make an arbitral award within the time limit, the party shall submit the acceptance notice or other certificate or proof that the arbitration application has been accepted, issued by the labor dispute arbitration commission.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  劳动者依据调解[仲裁法第四十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-47)第（一）项规定，追索劳动报酬、工伤医疗费、经济补偿或者赔偿金，如果仲裁裁决涉及数项，每项确定的数额均不超过当地月最低工资标准十二个月金额的，应当按照终局裁决处理。

**Article 13** Where, in accordance with Article 47(1) of the Mediation and Arbitration Law, a worker seeks recovery of labor remuneration, medical expenses for work-related injury, economic compensation, or damages, and the arbitral award involves several items, each of which does not exceed the amount equivalent to twelve months of the local monthly minimum wage, the award shall be treated as a final award.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  劳动人事争议仲裁委员会作出的同一仲裁裁决同时包含终局裁决事项和非终局裁决事项，当事人不服该仲裁裁决向人民法院提起诉讼的，应当按照非终局裁决处理。

**Article 14** Where the same arbitral award made by the labor dispute arbitration commission contains both final and non-final matters, and a party, unsatisfied with the award, files a lawsuit in the People's Court, the award shall be treated as a non-final award.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  劳动者依据调解[仲裁法第四十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-48)规定向基层人民法院提起诉讼，用人单位依据调解[仲裁法第四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-49)规定向劳动人事争议仲裁委员会所在地的中级人民法院申请撤销仲裁裁决的，中级人民法院应不予受理；已经受理的，应当裁定驳回申请。  
被人民法院驳回起诉或者劳动者撤诉的，用人单位可以自收到裁定书之日起三十日内，向劳动人事争议仲裁委员会所在地的中级人民法院申请撤销仲裁裁决。

**Article 15** Where a worker files a lawsuit in a Primary People's Court in accordance with Article 48 of the Mediation and Arbitration Law, and the employer applies to the Intermediate People's Court in the place where the labor dispute arbitration commission is located for revocation of the arbitral award in accordance with Article 49 of the Mediation and Arbitration Law, the Intermediate People's Court shall not accept the application; if the application has been accepted, a ruling shall be made to dismiss the application.  
If the prosecution is dismissed by the People's Court or the worker withdraws the lawsuit, the employer may, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the written ruling, apply to the Intermediate People's Court in the place where the labor dispute arbitration commission is located for revocation of the arbitral award.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  用人单位依照调解[仲裁法第四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-49)规定向中级人民法院申请撤销仲裁裁决，中级人民法院作出的驳回申请或者撤销仲裁裁决的裁定为终审裁定。

**Article 16** Where an employer applies to an Intermediate People's Court for revocation of an arbitral award in accordance with Article 49 of the Mediation and Arbitration Law, the ruling of the Intermediate People's Court dismissing the application or revoking the arbitral award shall be a final ruling.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  劳动者依据[劳动合同法第三十条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-30)第二款和调解[仲裁法第十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-16)规定向人民法院申请支付令，符合民事诉讼法第十七章督促程序规定的，人民法院应予受理。  
依据[劳动合同法第三十条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-30)第二款规定申请支付令被人民法院裁定终结督促程序后，劳动者就劳动争议事项直接向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当告知其先向劳动人事争议仲裁委员会申请仲裁。  
依据调解[仲裁法第十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-16)规定申请支付令被人民法院裁定终结督促程序后，劳动者依据调解协议直接向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 17** Where a worker, in accordance with Article 30(2) of the Labor Contract Law and Article 16 of the Mediation and Arbitration Law, applies to the People's Court for an order for payment, and such application complies with the provisions of Chapter 17 (Procedure for Urging Debt Recovery) of the Civil Procedure Law, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
After the People's Court rules to terminate the procedure for urging debt recovery in respect of an application for an order for payment made under Article 30(2) of the Labor Contract Law, if the worker directly files a lawsuit in the People's Court regarding the labor dispute, the People's Court shall inform the worker to first apply for arbitration to the labor dispute arbitration commission.  
After the People's Court rules to terminate the procedure for urging debt recovery in respect of an application for an order for payment made under Article 16 of the Mediation and Arbitration Law, if the worker directly files a lawsuit in the People's Court based on a mediation agreement, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  劳动人事争议仲裁委员会作出终局裁决，劳动者向人民法院申请执行，用人单位向劳动人事争议仲裁委员会所在地的中级人民法院申请撤销的，人民法院应当裁定中止执行。  
用人单位撤回撤销终局裁决申请或者其申请被驳回的，人民法院应当裁定恢复执行。仲裁裁决被撤销的，人民法院应当裁定终结执行。  
用人单位向人民法院申请撤销仲裁裁决被驳回后，又在执行程序中以相同理由提出不予执行抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 18** Where the labor dispute arbitration commission makes a final award, and the worker applies to the People's Court for enforcement while the employer applies to the Intermediate People's Court in the place where the labor dispute arbitration commission is located for revocation, the People's Court shall rule to suspend enforcement.  
If the employer withdraws the application for revocation of the final award or the application is dismissed, the People's Court shall rule to resume enforcement. If the arbitral award is revoked, the People's Court shall rule to terminate enforcement.  
Where an employer's application to the People's Court for revocation of an arbitral award is dismissed, and the employer subsequently raises a defense of non-enforcement on the same ground in the enforcement proceedings, the People's Court shall not support such defense.
