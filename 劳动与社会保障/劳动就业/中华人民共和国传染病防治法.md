# 中华人民共和国传染病防治法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2025-04-30  
**生效日期**：2025-09-01

> （1989年2月21日第七届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第六次会议通过　2004年8月28日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十一次会议第一次修订　根据2013年6月29日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国文物保护法〉等十二部法律的决定》修正　2025年4月30日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十五次会议第二次修订）

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了预防、控制和消除传染病的发生与流行，保障公众生命安全和身体健康，防范和化解公共卫生风险，维护国家安全和社会稳定，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of preventing, controlling and eliminating the occurrence and spread of infectious diseases, safeguarding the lives and health of the public, preventing and mitigating public health risks, and maintaining national security and social stability.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　传染病防治工作坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持人民至上、生命至上，坚持预防为主、防治结合的方针，坚持依法防控、科学防控的原则。

**Article 2** The work of infectious disease prevention and control shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, uphold the principle of putting the people and lives first, follow the policy of prevention first and combining prevention with control, and adhere to the principles of law-based and science-based prevention and control.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本法所称传染病，分为甲类传染病、乙类传染病、丙类传染病，以及突发原因不明的传染病等其他传染病。  
甲类传染病，是指对人体健康和生命安全危害特别严重，可能造成重大经济损失和社会影响，需要特别严格管理、控制疫情蔓延的传染病，包括鼠疫、霍乱。  
乙类传染病，是指对人体健康和生命安全危害严重，可能造成较大经济损失和社会影响，需要严格管理、降低发病率、减少危害的传染病，包括新型冠状病毒感染、传染性非典型肺炎、艾滋病、病毒性肝炎、脊髓灰质炎、人感染新亚型流感、麻疹、流行性出血热、狂犬病、流行性乙型脑炎、登革热、猴痘、炭疽、细菌性和阿米巴性痢疾、肺结核、伤寒和副伤寒、流行性脑脊髓膜炎、百日咳、白喉、新生儿破伤风、猩红热、布鲁氏菌病、淋病、梅毒、钩端螺旋体病、血吸虫病、疟疾。  
丙类传染病，是指常见多发，对人体健康和生命安全造成危害，可能造成一定程度的经济损失和社会影响，需要关注流行趋势、控制暴发和流行的传染病，包括流行性感冒、流行性腮腺炎、风疹、急性出血性结膜炎、麻风病、流行性和地方性斑疹伤寒、黑热病、包虫病、丝虫病、手足口病，除霍乱、细菌性和阿米巴性痢疾、伤寒和副伤寒以外的感染性腹泻病。  
国务院疾病预防控制部门根据传染病暴发、流行情况和危害程度，及时提出调整各类传染病目录的建议。调整甲类传染病目录，由国务院卫生健康主管部门报经国务院批准后予以公布；调整乙类、丙类传染病目录，由国务院卫生健康主管部门批准、公布。

**Article 3** For the purposes of this Law, infectious diseases are classified into Category A infectious diseases, Category B infectious diseases, Category C infectious diseases, and other infectious diseases such as infectious diseases of unknown cause that emerge suddenly.  
Category A infectious diseases refer to those that cause particularly serious harm to human health and life safety, may result in major economic losses and social impact, and require particularly strict management and containment of epidemic spread, including plague and cholera.  
Category B infectious diseases refer to those that cause serious harm to human health and life safety, may result in significant economic losses and social impact, and require strict management to reduce incidence and harm, including infection with novel coronavirus, severe acute respiratory syndrome (SARS), AIDS, viral hepatitis, poliomyelitis, human infection with new subtype influenza, measles, epidemic hemorrhagic fever, rabies, Japanese encephalitis, dengue fever, monkeypox, anthrax, bacillary and amebic dysentery, pulmonary tuberculosis, typhoid and paratyphoid fever, epidemic cerebrospinal meningitis, pertussis, diphtheria, neonatal tetanus, scarlet fever, brucellosis, gonorrhea, syphilis, leptospirosis, schistosomiasis, and malaria.  
Category C infectious diseases refer to those that are common and frequently occurring, cause harm to human health and life safety, may lead to a certain degree of economic losses and social impact, and require attention to epidemic trends and control of outbreaks and epidemics, including influenza, mumps, rubella, acute hemorrhagic conjunctivitis, leprosy, epidemic and endemic typhus, kala-azar, hydatid disease, filariasis, hand-foot-mouth disease, and infectious diarrheal diseases other than cholera, bacillary and amebic dysentery, typhoid and paratyphoid fever.  
The disease prevention and control department of the State Council shall, in light of the outbreak, prevalence, and degree of harm of infectious diseases, timely put forward proposals for adjusting the list of various categories of infectious diseases. Adjustments to the list of Category A infectious diseases shall be published by the competent health department of the State Council after being reported to and approved by the State Council; adjustments to the lists of Category B and Category C infectious diseases shall be approved and published by the competent health department of the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　突发原因不明的传染病需要采取本法规定的甲类传染病预防、控制措施的，国务院疾病预防控制部门及时提出建议，由国务院卫生健康主管部门报经国务院批准后予以公布。  
对乙类传染病中的传染性非典型肺炎、炭疽中的肺炭疽，采取本法规定的甲类传染病预防、控制措施。其他乙类传染病需要采取本法规定的甲类传染病预防、控制措施的，依照前款规定的程序批准、公布。  
需要解除依照本条规定采取的甲类传染病预防、控制措施的，国务院疾病预防控制部门及时提出建议，由国务院卫生健康主管部门报经国务院批准后予以公布。  
依照本法规定采取甲类传染病预防、控制措施的传染病，适用本法有关甲类传染病的规定。

**Article 4** Where an infectious disease with unknown cause emerging suddenly requires the adoption of the prevention and control measures prescribed in this Law for Category A infectious diseases, the disease prevention and control department of the State Council shall timely put forward suggestions, and the competent health department of the State Council shall report to the State Council for approval and then publish the measures.  
With respect to severe acute respiratory syndrome (SARS) among Category B infectious diseases and pulmonary anthrax among anthrax, the prevention and control measures prescribed in this Law for Category A infectious diseases shall be adopted. Where other Category B infectious diseases require the adoption of the prevention and control measures prescribed in this Law for Category A infectious diseases, the procedures as set forth in the preceding paragraph shall be followed for approval and publication.  
Where it is necessary to discontinue the adoption of the prevention and control measures for Category A infectious diseases taken in accordance with this Article, the disease prevention and control department of the State Council shall timely put forward suggestions, and the competent health department of the State Council shall report to the State Council for approval and then publish the decision.  
Infectious diseases for which the prevention and control measures for Category A infectious diseases are adopted in accordance with this Law shall be subject to the provisions of this Law concerning Category A infectious diseases.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　省级人民政府对本行政区域常见多发的其他传染病，可以根据情况决定按照乙类或者丙类传染病管理并予以公布，报国务院疾病预防控制部门备案。

**Article 5** The people's government at the provincial level may, in light of circumstances, decide to manage other infectious diseases that are common and frequently occurring in its administrative region in accordance with Category B or Category C infectious diseases and publish such decision, and shall report it to the disease prevention and control department of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家建立健全传染病防治体制机制，明确属地、部门、单位和个人责任，实行联防联控、群防群控。

**Article 6** The State shall establish and improve the institutional mechanism for infectious disease prevention and control, clarify the responsibilities of localities, departments, entities, and individuals, and implement joint prevention and control as well as mass prevention and control.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级人民政府加强对传染病防治工作的领导。  
县级以上人民政府建立健全传染病防治的疾病预防控制、医疗救治、应急处置、物资保障和监督管理体系，加强传染病防治能力建设。

**Article 7** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen their leadership over the work of infectious disease prevention and control.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve the systems for disease prevention and control, medical treatment, emergency response, material support, and supervision and management for infectious disease prevention and control, and strengthen capacity building for infectious disease prevention and control.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院卫生健康主管部门牵头组织协调全国传染病疫情应对工作，负责全国传染病医疗救治的组织指导工作。国务院疾病预防控制部门负责全国传染病预防、控制的组织指导工作，负责全国传染病疫情应对相关工作。国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责传染病防治有关工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府卫生健康主管部门牵头组织协调本行政区域传染病疫情应对工作，负责本行政区域传染病医疗救治的组织指导工作。县级以上地方人民政府疾病预防控制部门负责本行政区域传染病预防、控制的组织指导工作，负责本行政区域传染病疫情应对相关工作。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责传染病防治有关工作。  
中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队的传染病防治工作，依照本法和中央军事委员会的有关规定办理，由中央军事委员会负责卫生工作的部门实施监督管理。

**Article 8** The competent health department of the State Council shall take the lead in organizing and coordinating the national response to infectious disease epidemics, and shall be responsible for organizing and guiding nationwide medical treatment for infectious diseases. The disease prevention and control department of the State Council shall be responsible for organizing and guiding national infectious disease prevention and control, and for the relevant work related to national infectious disease epidemic response. Other relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for the work related to infectious disease prevention and control within their respective functions.  
The competent health department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall take the lead in organizing and coordinating the response to infectious disease epidemics within its administrative region, and shall be responsible for organizing and guiding medical treatment for infectious diseases within its administrative region. The disease prevention and control department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing and guiding infectious disease prevention and control within its administrative region, and for the relevant work related to infectious disease epidemic response within its administrative region. Other relevant departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the work related to infectious disease prevention and control within their respective functions.  
The prevention and control of infectious diseases within the People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be handled in accordance with this Law and the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission, and the department in charge of health work under the Central Military Commission shall exercise supervision and administration.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院和县级以上地方人民政府的重大传染病疫情联防联控机制开展疫情会商研判，组织协调、督促推进疫情防控工作。  
发生重大传染病疫情，构成突发公共卫生事件的，国务院和县级以上地方人民政府依照有关突发公共卫生事件应对的法律、行政法规规定设立应急指挥机构、启动应急响应。

**Article 9** The joint prevention and control mechanism for major infectious disease epidemics of the State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall conduct consultation and assessment of the epidemic situation, organize coordination, and supervise and promote epidemic prevention and control work.  
Where a major infectious disease epidemic occurs and constitutes a public health emergency, the State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on the response to public health emergencies, establish emergency command bodies and activate emergency response.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家建立健全城乡一体、上下联动、功能完备的疾病预防控制网络。  
国务院疾病预防控制部门领导各级疾病预防控制机构业务工作，建立上下联动的分工协作机制。  
国家、省级疾病预防控制机构成立疾病预防控制专家委员会，为传染病防治提供咨询、评估、论证等专业技术支持。

**Article 10** The State shall establish and improve a disease prevention and control network that integrates urban and rural areas, links upper and lower levels, and has complete functions.  
The disease prevention and control department of the State Council shall lead the professional work of disease prevention and control institutions at all levels and establish a division-of-labor and collaboration mechanism linking upper and lower levels.  
National and provincial disease prevention and control institutions shall establish an expert committee on disease prevention and control to provide professional technical support such as consultation, assessment, and review for infectious disease prevention and control.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家坚持中西医并重，加强中西医结合，充分发挥中医药在传染病防治中的作用。

**Article 11** The State shall adhere to equal emphasis on traditional Chinese medicine and Western medicine, strengthen their integration, and give full play to the role of traditional Chinese medicine in infectious disease prevention and control.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家支持和鼓励开展传染病防治的科学研究，组织开展传染病防治和公共卫生研究工作以及多学科联合攻关，提高传染病防治的科学技术水平。

**Article 12** The State shall support and encourage scientific research on infectious disease prevention and control, organize and carry out research on infectious disease prevention and control and public health, as well as multidisciplinary joint efforts, to improve the scientific and technological level of infectious disease prevention and control.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家支持和鼓励在传染病防治中运用现代信息技术。  
传染病防治中开展个人信息收集、存储、使用、加工、传输、提供、公开、删除等个人信息处理活动，应当遵守《中华人民共和国民法典》、《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》等法律、行政法规的规定，采取措施确保个人信息安全，保护个人隐私，不得过度收集个人信息；相关信息不得用于传染病防治以外的目的。

**Article 13** The State shall support and encourage the application of modern information technology in infectious disease prevention and control.  
In infectious disease prevention and control, activities for processing personal information, such as collection, storage, use, processing, transmission, provision, disclosure, and deletion of personal information, shall comply with the provisions of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, and other laws and administrative regulations. Measures shall be taken to ensure the security of personal information, protect personal privacy, and personal information shall not be excessively collected; relevant information shall not be used for purposes other than infectious disease prevention and control.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　中华人民共和国领域内的一切单位和个人应当支持传染病防治工作，接受和配合为预防、控制、消除传染病危害依法采取的调查、采集样本、检验检测、隔离治疗、医学观察等措施，根据传染病预防、控制需要采取必要的防护措施。  
国家支持和鼓励单位和个人参与传染病防治工作。各级人民政府应当完善有关制度，提供便利措施，引导单位和个人参与传染病防治的宣传教育、疫情报告、志愿服务和捐赠等活动。

**Article 14** All entities and individuals within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall support the work of infectious disease prevention and control, accept and cooperate with the measures taken according to law to prevent, control, and eliminate the harm of infectious diseases, such as investigation, sample collection, inspection and testing, isolation treatment, and medical observation, and adopt necessary protective measures as required by infectious disease prevention and control.  
The State shall support and encourage entities and individuals to participate in the work of infectious disease prevention and control. People's governments at all levels shall improve relevant systems, provide convenient measures, and guide entities and individuals to participate in activities such as publicity and education on infectious disease prevention and control, epidemic reporting, voluntary services, and donations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　疾病预防控制部门、街道办事处和乡镇人民政府应当开展群防群控工作，指导居民委员会、村民委员会协助做好城乡社区的传染病预防、控制工作。  
居民委员会、村民委员会应当协助县级以上人民政府及其有关部门、街道办事处和乡镇人民政府做好城乡社区传染病预防、控制的宣传教育、健康提示以及疫情防控工作，组织城乡居民参与城乡社区的传染病预防、控制活动。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门、街道办事处和乡镇人民政府应当为居民委员会、村民委员会开展传染病预防、控制工作提供必要的支持和保障。

**Article 15** Disease prevention and control departments, subdistrict offices, and township people's governments shall carry out mass prevention and control work, and guide residents' committees and villagers' committees to assist in the infectious disease prevention and control work in urban and rural communities.  
Residents' committees and villagers' committees shall assist the people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments, subdistrict offices, and township people's governments in carrying out publicity and education on infectious disease prevention and control, health tips, and epidemic prevention and control work in urban and rural communities, and organize urban and rural residents to participate in infectious disease prevention and control activities in urban and rural communities.  
People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments, subdistrict offices, and township people's governments shall provide necessary support and guarantees for residents' committees and villagers' committees to carry out infectious disease prevention and control work.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家和社会应当关心、帮助传染病患者、病原携带者和疑似患者，使其得到及时救治。  
任何单位或者个人不得歧视传染病患者、病原携带者和疑似患者，不得泄露个人隐私、个人信息。

**Article 16** The State and society shall care for and assist infectious disease patients, pathogen carriers, and suspected patients, so that they may receive timely treatment.  
No entity or individual shall discriminate against infectious disease patients, pathogen carriers, or suspected patients, or disclose their personal privacy or personal information.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　采取传染病预防、控制措施，应当依照法定权限和程序，与传染病暴发、流行和可能造成危害的程度、范围等相适应；有多种措施可供选择的，应当选择有利于最大程度保护单位和个人合法权益，且对他人权益损害和生产生活影响较小的措施，并根据情况变化及时调整。  
单位和个人认为有关地方人民政府、卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门和其他有关部门，以及疾病预防控制机构、医疗机构等实施的相关行政行为或者传染病预防、控制措施，侵犯其合法权益的，可以依法申请行政复议、提起诉讼。

**Article 17** Infectious disease prevention and control measures shall be taken in accordance with statutory authority and procedures, and shall be commensurate with the extent and scope of the outbreak, prevalence, and possible harm of the infectious disease; where multiple measures are available, the measures that are conducive to maximizing the protection of the lawful rights and interests of entities and individuals while causing less harm to others' rights and less impact on production and daily life shall be selected, and shall be adjusted in a timely manner as circumstances change.  
Where an entity or individual believes that the relevant administrative acts or infectious disease prevention and control measures implemented by the relevant local people's government, competent health department, disease prevention and control department, other relevant departments, or disease prevention and control institutions, medical institutions, etc., have infringed upon its or his lawful rights and interests, it or he may, according to law, apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate an action in court.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家开展传染病防治健康教育工作，加强传染病防治法治宣传，提高公众传染病防治健康素养和法治意识。  
学校、托育机构应当结合年龄特点对学生和幼儿进行健康知识和传染病防治知识的教育。  
新闻媒体应当开展传染病防治和公共卫生知识的公益宣传。  
个人应当学习传染病防治知识，养成良好的卫生习惯，培养健康的生活方式。

**Article 18** The State shall carry out health education on infectious disease prevention and control, strengthen publicity on the rule of law for infectious disease prevention and control, and improve the public's health literacy and awareness of the rule of law regarding infectious disease prevention and control.  
Schools and childcare institutions shall, in light of age characteristics, educate students and young children on health knowledge and knowledge of infectious disease prevention and control.  
News media shall carry out public-interest publicity on infectious disease prevention and control and public health knowledge.  
Individuals shall learn knowledge of infectious disease prevention and control, develop good hygiene habits, and cultivate a healthy lifestyle.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家支持和鼓励开展传染病防治的国际交流与合作。

**Article 19** The State shall support and encourage international exchanges and cooperation on infectious disease prevention and control.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对在传染病防治工作中做出显著成绩和贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。  
对因参与传染病防治工作致病、致残、死亡的人员，按照有关规定给予补助、抚恤和优待。

**Article 20** Entities and individuals that have made outstanding achievements and contributions in the work of infectious disease prevention and control shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Persons who become ill, disabled, or die due to participation in infectious disease prevention and control work shall be given subsidies, pensions, and preferential treatment in accordance with relevant provisions.

### 第二章　预防

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　各级人民政府组织开展爱国卫生运动，完善公共卫生设施，改善人居环境状况，加强社会健康管理，提升全民健康水平。

**Article 21** People's governments at all levels shall organize and carry out patriotic public health campaigns, improve public health facilities, enhance the living environment, strengthen social health management, and raise the health level of the entire population.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　地方各级人民政府应当有计划地建设和改造城乡公共卫生设施，改善饮用水卫生条件，对污水、污物、粪便进行无害化处置。城市应当按照国家和地方有关标准修建公共厕所、垃圾和粪便无害化处置场以及排水和污水处理系统等公共卫生设施。农村应当逐步改造厕所，建立必要的卫生管理制度。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当加强医疗废物收集处置能力建设。设区的市级人民政府应当确定医疗废物协同应急处置设施，提高重大传染病疫情医疗废物应急处置能力。

**Article 22** Local people's governments at all levels shall, in a planned manner, construct and renovate public health facilities in urban and rural areas, improve the sanitary conditions of drinking water, and carry out harmless disposal of sewage, waste, and excrement. Cities shall construct public toilets, harmless disposal sites for garbage and excrement, and drainage and sewage treatment systems and other public health facilities in accordance with relevant national and local standards. Rural areas shall gradually renovate toilets and establish necessary sanitary management systems.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the capacity building for the collection and disposal of medical waste. The people's government of a city divided into districts shall determine collaborative emergency disposal facilities for medical waste to enhance the emergency medical waste disposal capacity during major infectious disease outbreaks.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　县级以上人民政府农业农村、水利、林业草原等部门依据职责指导、组织控制和消除农田、湖区、河流、牧场、林区、草原地区的鼠害与血吸虫危害，以及其他传播传染病的动物和病媒生物的危害。  
交通运输、铁路、民用航空等部门依据职责指导、监督交通运输经营单位以及车站、港口、机场等相关场所的运营单位消除鼠害和蚊、蝇等病媒生物的危害。

**Article 23** The departments of agriculture and rural affairs, water resources, forestry and grassland, and other relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their duties, direct, organize, control, and eliminate the hazards of rodents and schistosomiasis in farmland, lake areas, rivers, pastures, forest areas, and grassland areas, as well as the hazards of other animals and disease vectors that transmit infectious diseases.  
The departments of transportation, railway, civil aviation, and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with their duties, direct and supervise transportation operating entities and the operating entities of stations, ports, airports, and other relevant places to eliminate the hazards of rodents, mosquitoes, flies, and other disease vectors.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家实行免疫规划制度。政府免费向居民提供免疫规划疫苗。  
国务院疾病预防控制部门制定国家免疫规划。省级人民政府在执行国家免疫规划时，可以根据本行政区域疾病预防、控制需要，增加免疫规划疫苗种类，加强重点地区、重点人群的预防接种，报国务院疾病预防控制部门备案并公布。  
国家对儿童实行预防接种证制度。医疗机构、疾病预防控制机构与儿童的监护人、所在学校和托育机构应当相互配合，保证儿童及时接种免疫规划疫苗。  
出现特别重大突发公共卫生事件或者其他严重威胁公众健康的紧急事件，可以依照《中华人民共和国疫苗管理法》的规定在一定范围和期限内紧急使用疫苗。

**Article 24** The State shall implement an immunization planning system. The government shall provide immunization planning vaccines to residents free of charge.  
The disease prevention and control department of the State Council shall formulate the national immunization plan. People's governments at the provincial level, when implementing the national immunization plan, may, based on the needs of disease prevention and control in their administrative regions, add categories of immunization planning vaccines, strengthen vaccination for key areas and key populations, and report to the disease prevention and control department of the State Council for record and publication.  
The State shall implement a vaccination certificate system for children. Medical institutions, disease prevention and control institutions shall cooperate with guardians of children, the schools and childcare institutions where the children are enrolled, to ensure timely vaccination of children with immunization planning vaccines.  
In the event of a particularly major public health emergency or other emergency that seriously threatens public health, vaccines may be used urgently within a certain scope and period in accordance with the provisions of the Vaccine Administration Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　各级疾病预防控制机构在传染病预防、控制中履行下列职责：  
（一）实施传染病预防控制规划，制定传染病预防控制技术方案并组织实施；  
（二）组织开展传染病监测，收集、分析和报告传染病监测信息，预测传染病的发生、流行趋势；  
（三）开展对传染病疫情和突发公共卫生事件的流行病学调查、风险评估、现场处理及其效果评价；  
（四）开展传染病实验室检验检测、诊断、病原学鉴定；  
（五）实施免疫规划，负责预防性生物制品的使用管理；  
（六）开展健康教育、咨询，普及传染病防治知识；  
（七）指导、培训下级疾病预防控制机构及其工作人员开展传染病预防、控制工作；  
（八）指导医疗机构和学校、托育机构、养老机构、康复机构、福利机构、未成年人救助保护机构、救助管理机构、体育场馆、监管场所、车站、港口、机场等重点场所开展传染病预防、控制工作；  
（九）开展传染病防治基础性研究、应用性研究和卫生评价，提供技术咨询。  
国家、省级疾病预防控制机构主要负责对传染病发生、流行以及分布进行监测，对重点传染病流行趋势进行预测，提出预防、控制对策，参与并指导对暴发的传染病疫情进行调查处理，开展传染病病原学鉴定，建立检验检测质量控制体系，开展基础性研究、应用性研究、卫生评价以及标准规范制定。  
设区的市级、县级疾病预防控制机构主要负责传染病预防控制规划、预防控制技术方案的落实，组织实施免疫、消毒，指导病媒生物危害控制，普及传染病防治知识，负责本地区传染病和突发公共卫生事件监测、报告，开展流行病学调查和常见病原微生物检测，开展应用性研究和卫生评价。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国传染病防治法实施办法.md#art-53" title="被《中华人民共和国传染病防治法实施办法》第53条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国传染病防治法实施办法.md#art-54" title="被《中华人民共和国传染病防治法实施办法》第54条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国传染病防治法实施办法.md#art-73" title="被《中华人民共和国传染病防治法实施办法》第73条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 25** Disease prevention and control institutions at all levels shall perform the following duties in the prevention and control of infectious diseases:  
(1) Implement infectious disease prevention and control plans, formulate technical schemes for infectious disease prevention and control and organize their implementation;  
(2) Organize and carry out infectious disease surveillance, collect, analyze and report infectious disease surveillance information, and predict the occurrence and epidemic trends of infectious diseases;  
(3) Conduct epidemiological investigations, risk assessments, on-site response, and effectiveness evaluations of infectious disease outbreaks and public health emergencies;  
(4) Conduct laboratory testing, diagnosis, and etiological identification of infectious diseases;  
(5) Implement immunization plans and be responsible for the use and management of preventive biological products;  
(6) Carry out health education and counseling, and disseminate knowledge on infectious disease prevention and treatment;  
(7) Direct and train lower-level disease prevention and control institutions and their staff in carrying out infectious disease prevention and control work;  
(8) Direct medical institutions and key places such as schools, childcare institutions, elderly care institutions, rehabilitation institutions, welfare institutions, juvenile assistance and protection institutions, rescue management institutions, sports venues, supervision venues, stations, ports, and airports to carry out infectious disease prevention and control work;  
(9) Conduct basic research, applied research, and health evaluation on infectious disease prevention and treatment, and provide technical consultation.  
National and provincial disease prevention and control institutions shall be primarily responsible for monitoring the occurrence, prevalence, and distribution of infectious diseases, predicting the epidemic trends of key infectious diseases, proposing prevention and control countermeasures, participating in and directing the investigation and handling of outbreaks of infectious diseases, conducting etiological identification of infectious diseases, establishing quality control systems for testing, and conducting basic research, applied research, health evaluation, and the formulation of standards and norms.  
Disease prevention and control institutions at the level of cities divided into districts and counties shall be primarily responsible for the implementation of infectious disease prevention and control plans and technical schemes, organizing the implementation of immunization and disinfection, directing the control of hazards of disease vectors, disseminating knowledge on infectious disease prevention and treatment, being responsible for the surveillance and reporting of infectious diseases and public health emergencies in their respective regions, conducting epidemiological investigations and testing of common pathogenic microorganisms, and conducting applied research and health evaluation.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　二级以上医疗机构应当有专门的科室并指定专门的人员，承担本机构的传染病预防、控制和传染病疫情报告以及责任区域内的传染病预防工作。  
基层医疗卫生机构应当有专门的科室或者指定人员负责传染病预防、控制管理工作，在疾病预防控制机构指导下，承担本机构的传染病预防、控制和责任区域内的传染病防治健康教育、预防接种、传染病疫情报告、传染病患者健康监测以及城乡社区传染病疫情防控指导等工作。

**Article 26** Medical institutions at or above the second level shall have specialized departments and designate specialized personnel to undertake the prevention and control of infectious diseases and the reporting of infectious disease outbreaks within the institution, as well as the prevention of infectious diseases in their responsible areas.  
Primary-level medical and health institutions shall have specialized departments or designate personnel to be responsible for the management of infectious disease prevention and control. Under the guidance of disease prevention and control institutions, they shall undertake the prevention and control of infectious diseases within the institution, health education on infectious disease prevention and treatment in their responsible areas, vaccination, reporting of infectious disease outbreaks, health monitoring of infectious disease patients, and guidance on infectious disease prevention and control in urban and rural communities.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　医疗机构的基本标准、建筑设计和服务流程应当符合预防医疗机构感染的要求，降低传染病在医疗机构内传播的风险。  
医疗机构应当严格执行国家规定的管理制度、操作规范，加强与医疗机构感染有关的危险因素监测、安全防护、消毒、隔离和医疗废物、医疗污水处置工作，防止传染病在医疗机构内的传播。  
医疗机构应当按照规定对使用的医疗器械进行消毒或者灭菌；对按照规定一次性使用的医疗器械，应当在使用后予以销毁。

**Article 27** The basic standards, architectural design, and service procedures of medical institutions shall meet the requirements for preventing healthcare-associated infections, reducing the risk of transmission of infectious diseases within medical institutions.  
Medical institutions shall strictly implement the management systems and operating standards prescribed by the State, strengthen the monitoring of risk factors related to healthcare-associated infections, safety protection, disinfection, isolation, and the disposal of medical waste and medical sewage, so as to prevent the transmission of infectious diseases within medical institutions.  
Medical institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions, disinfect or sterilize the medical devices used; medical devices that are for single use as prescribed shall be destroyed after use.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院疾病预防控制部门拟订国家重点传染病和突发原因不明的传染病预防控制应急预案，由国务院卫生健康主管部门批准、公布。  
县级以上地方人民政府制定本行政区域重点传染病和突发原因不明的传染病预防控制应急预案，报上一级人民政府备案并予以公布。鼓励毗邻、相近地区的地方人民政府制定应对区域性传染病的联合预防控制应急预案。  
传染病预防控制应急预案应当根据本法和其他有关法律、法规的规定，针对传染病暴发、流行情况和危害程度，具体规定传染病预防、控制工作的组织指挥体系和职责，传染病预防、监测、疫情报告和通报、疫情风险评估、预警、应急工作方案、人员调集以及物资和技术储备与调用等内容。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国传染病防治法实施办法.md#art-55" title="被《中华人民共和国传染病防治法实施办法》第55条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 28** The disease prevention and control department of the State Council shall draft the emergency response plan for the prevention and control of nationally key infectious diseases and infectious diseases with unknown causes of outbreak, which shall be approved and published by the competent health department of the State Council.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall formulate emergency response plans for the prevention and control of key infectious diseases and infectious diseases with unknown causes of outbreak in their respective administrative regions, which shall be submitted to the people's government at the next higher level for record and be published. Local people's governments in adjacent or proximate areas are encouraged to formulate joint prevention and control emergency response plans for regional infectious diseases.  
The emergency response plan for the prevention and control of infectious diseases shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other relevant laws and regulations, and based on the outbreak and prevalence of infectious diseases and the degree of harm, specify in detail the organizational command system and responsibilities for infectious disease prevention and control work, infectious disease prevention, surveillance, reporting and notification of outbreaks, risk assessment of outbreaks, early warning, emergency work plans, personnel mobilization, and material and technical reserves and deployment.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　医疗卫生机构和学校、托育机构、养老机构、康复机构、福利机构、未成年人救助保护机构、救助管理机构、体育场馆、监管场所、车站、港口、机场等重点场所，应当制定本单位传染病预防控制应急预案。

**Article 29** Medical and health institutions, and key places such as schools, childcare institutions, elderly care institutions, rehabilitation institutions, welfare institutions, juvenile assistance and protection institutions, rescue management institutions, sports venues, supervision venues, stations, ports, and airports, shall formulate emergency response plans for the prevention and control of infectious diseases within their respective units.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　传染病预防控制应急预案应当增强科学性、针对性和可操作性，并根据实际需要和形势变化及时修订。  
县级以上人民政府疾病预防控制部门应当根据有关传染病预防控制应急预案定期组织开展演练。医疗卫生机构和学校、托育机构、养老机构、康复机构、福利机构、未成年人救助保护机构、救助管理机构、体育场馆、监管场所、车站、港口、机场等重点场所应当根据本单位传染病预防控制应急预案开展演练。

**Article 30** Emergency response plans for the prevention and control of infectious diseases shall enhance their scientific nature, pertinence, and operability, and shall be revised promptly in light of actual needs and changes in circumstances.  
The disease prevention and control departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall regularly organize drills based on the relevant emergency response plans for the prevention and control of infectious diseases. Medical and health institutions, and key places such as schools, childcare institutions, elderly care institutions, rehabilitation institutions, welfare institutions, juvenile assistance and protection institutions, rescue management institutions, sports venues, supervision venues, stations, ports, and airports, shall conduct drills based on their own emergency response plans for the prevention and control of infectious diseases.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　疾病预防控制机构、医疗机构的实验室和从事病原微生物实验的单位，应当遵守有关病原微生物实验室生物安全的法律、行政法规规定，符合国家规定的条件和技术标准，建立严格的管理制度，对传染病病原体和样本按照规定的措施实行严格管理，严防传染病病原体的实验室感染和扩散。

**Article 31** Laboratories of disease prevention and control institutions and medical institutions, and units engaged in experiments with pathogenic microorganisms, shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on biosafety in pathogenic microbiology laboratories, meet the conditions and technical standards prescribed by the State, establish strict management systems, and strictly manage infectious disease pathogens and samples in accordance with prescribed measures, so as to strictly prevent laboratory infection and spread of infectious disease pathogens.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　采供血机构、生物制品生产单位应当严格执行国家有关规定，保证血液、血液制品的质量和安全。  
禁止非法采集血液或者组织他人出卖血液。  
疾病预防控制机构、医疗机构使用血液和血液制品，应当遵守国家有关规定，防止因输入血液、使用血液制品引起经血液传播疾病的发生。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国传染病防治法实施办法.md#art-3" title="被《中华人民共和国传染病防治法实施办法》第3条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 32** Blood collection and supply institutions and biological product production units shall strictly implement the relevant provisions of the State to ensure the quality and safety of blood and blood products.  
It is prohibited to illegally collect blood or organize others to sell blood.  
Disease prevention and control institutions and medical institutions, when using blood and blood products, shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State to prevent the occurrence of blood-borne diseases caused by blood transfusion or use of blood products.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　各级人民政府应当加强艾滋病的防治工作，采取预防、控制措施，防止艾滋病的传播。具体办法由国务院制定。

**Article 33** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the prevention and treatment of AIDS, adopt prevention and control measures to prevent the spread of AIDS. Specific measures shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家建立健全人畜共患传染病防治的协作机制，统筹规划、协同推进预防、控制工作，做好重点人群健康教育、传染病监测、疫情调查处置和信息通报等工作。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原、卫生健康、疾病预防控制等部门依据职责负责与人畜共患传染病有关的动物传染病的防治管理工作，重点加强鼠疫、狂犬病、人感染新亚型流感、布鲁氏菌病、炭疽、血吸虫病、包虫病等人畜共患传染病的防治工作。

**Article 34** The State shall establish and improve a coordination mechanism for the prevention and treatment of zoonotic infectious diseases, make overall plans and promote prevention and control work in a coordinated manner, and carry out health education for key populations, infectious disease surveillance, outbreak investigation and response, and information notification.  
The departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland, health, disease prevention and control, and other relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their duties, be responsible for the prevention and control management of animal infectious diseases related to zoonotic infectious diseases, with a focus on strengthening the prevention and treatment of plague, rabies, human infection with new subtypes of influenza, brucellosis, anthrax, schistosomiasis, echinococcosis, and other zoonotic infectious diseases.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家建立病原微生物菌（毒）种保藏库。  
对病原微生物菌（毒）种和传染病检测样本的采集、保藏、提供、携带、运输、使用实行分类管理，建立健全严格的管理制度。从事相关活动应当遵守有关病原微生物实验室生物安全的法律、行政法规规定；依法需要经过批准或者进行备案的，应当取得批准或者进行备案。

**Article 35** The State shall establish a repository for the preservation of pathogenic microorganism strains (poison strains).  
The collection, preservation, provision, carrying, transportation, and use of pathogenic microorganism strains (poison strains) and infectious disease testing samples shall be subject to classified management, and strict management systems shall be established and improved. Activities related thereto shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on biosafety in pathogenic microbiology laboratories; where approval or recordation is required according to law, approval shall be obtained or recordation shall be made.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　对被传染病病原体污染的水、物品和场所，有关单位和个人应当在疾病预防控制机构的指导下或者按照其提出的卫生要求，进行科学严格消毒处理；拒绝消毒处理的，由当地疾病预防控制部门组织进行强制消毒处理。

**Article 36** For water, articles, and places contaminated by infectious disease pathogens, the relevant units and individuals shall, under the guidance of the disease prevention and control institution or in accordance with the hygiene requirements proposed by it, carry out scientific and strict disinfection treatment; if disinfection treatment is refused, the local disease prevention and control department shall organize compulsory disinfection treatment.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　在国家确认的自然疫源地计划兴建水利、交通、旅游、能源等大型建设项目的，应当事先由省级以上疾病预防控制机构对施工环境进行卫生调查。建设单位应当根据疾病预防控制机构的意见，采取必要的传染病预防、控制措施。施工期间，建设单位应当设专人负责工地上的卫生防疫工作。施工期间和工程竣工后，疾病预防控制机构应当对可能发生的传染病进行监测。

**Article 37** Where a large-scale construction project such as a water conservancy, transportation, tourism, or energy project is planned to be constructed in a natural focus area identified by the State, a disease prevention and control institution at or above the provincial level shall conduct a hygiene survey of the construction environment in advance. The construction unit shall, based on the opinions of the disease prevention and control institution, take necessary infectious disease prevention and control measures. During the construction period, the construction unit shall assign dedicated personnel to be responsible for health and epidemic prevention work on the construction site. During the construction period and after the completion of the project, the disease prevention and control institution shall monitor possible infectious diseases.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　用于传染病防治的消毒产品、饮用水供水单位供应的饮用水和涉及饮用水卫生安全的产品，应当符合国家卫生标准和卫生规范。  
用于传染病防治的消毒产品的生产企业，应当经省级人民政府疾病预防控制部门批准，取得卫生许可。利用新材料、新工艺技术和新杀菌原理生产的消毒剂和消毒器械，应当经国务院疾病预防控制部门批准，取得卫生许可；其他消毒剂、消毒器械以及抗（抑）菌剂，应当报省级人民政府疾病预防控制部门备案。  
饮用水供水单位应当经设区的市级或者县级人民政府疾病预防控制部门批准，取得卫生许可。涉及饮用水卫生安全的产品应当经省级以上人民政府疾病预防控制部门批准，取得卫生许可。

**Article 38** Disinfection products used for the prevention and control of infectious diseases, drinking water supplied by drinking water supply units, and products involving the hygiene safety of drinking water shall meet national hygiene standards and hygiene specifications.  
Production enterprises of disinfection products used for the prevention and control of infectious diseases shall be approved by the disease prevention and control department of the provincial people's government and obtain a hygiene license. Disinfectants and disinfection devices produced using new materials, new technology, and new sterilization principles shall be approved by the disease prevention and control department of the State Council and obtain a hygiene license; other disinfectants, disinfection devices, and antibacterial (inhibitory) agents shall be reported to the disease prevention and control department of the provincial people's government for record.  
Drinking water supply units shall be approved by the disease prevention and control department of the people's government at the level of a city divided into districts or a county and obtain a hygiene license. Products involving the hygiene safety of drinking water shall be approved by the disease prevention and control department of the people's government at or above the provincial level and obtain a hygiene license.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　传染病患者、病原携带者和疑似患者应当如实提供相关信息，在治愈前或者在排除传染病嫌疑前，不得从事法律、行政法规和国务院疾病预防控制部门规定禁止从事的易使该传染病扩散的工作。  
传染病患者、病原携带者、疑似患者以及上述人员的密切接触者应当采取必要的防护措施。  
任何单位或者个人不得以任何方式故意传播传染病。

**Article 39** Infectious disease patients, pathogen carriers, and suspected patients shall truthfully provide relevant information, and before being cured or before being ruled out as having an infectious disease, they shall not engage in work that is prohibited by laws, administrative regulations, and the disease prevention and control department of the State Council and that is likely to facilitate the spread of the infectious disease.  
Infectious disease patients, pathogen carriers, suspected patients, and close contacts of the aforementioned persons shall take necessary protective measures.  
No unit or individual may intentionally spread infectious diseases in any manner.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　学校、托育机构、养老机构、康复机构、福利机构、未成年人救助保护机构、救助管理机构、体育场馆、监管场所、车站、港口、机场等重点场所应当落实主体责任，加强传染病预防、控制能力建设，在疾病预防控制机构指导下开展传染病预防、控制工作。

**Article 40** Key places such as schools, childcare institutions, elderly care institutions, rehabilitation institutions, welfare institutions, juvenile assistance and protection institutions, rescue management institutions, sports venues, supervision venues, stations, ports, and airports shall fulfill their primary responsibilities, strengthen capacity building for infectious disease prevention and control, and carry out infectious disease prevention and control work under the guidance of disease prevention and control institutions.

### 第三章　监测、报告和预警

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家加强传染病监测预警工作，建设多点触发、反应快速、权威高效的传染病监测预警体系。

**Article 41** The State shall strengthen the work of surveillance and early warning of infectious diseases, and build a multi-point trigger, rapid response, authoritative and efficient surveillance and early warning system for infectious diseases.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家建立健全传染病监测制度。  
国务院疾病预防控制部门会同国务院有关部门制定国家传染病监测规划和方案。省级人民政府疾病预防控制部门会同同级人民政府有关部门，根据国家传染病监测规划和方案，制定本行政区域传染病监测计划和工作方案，报国务院疾病预防控制部门审核后实施。  
国家加强传染病监测，依托传染病监测系统实行传染病疫情和突发公共卫生事件网络直报，建立重点传染病以及原因不明的传染病监测哨点，拓展传染病症状监测范围，收集传染病症候群、群体性不明原因疾病等信息，建立传染病病原学监测网络，多途径、多渠道开展多病原监测，建立智慧化多点触发机制，增强监测的敏感性和准确性，提高实时分析、集中研判能力，及时发现传染病疫情和突发公共卫生事件。

**Article 42** The State shall establish and improve the infectious disease surveillance system.  
The disease prevention and control department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate the national infectious disease surveillance plan and program. The disease prevention and control department of the provincial people's government shall, together with the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level, formulate the infectious disease surveillance plan and work plan for its administrative region based on the national infectious disease surveillance plan and program, and submit them to the disease prevention and control department of the State Council for review before implementation.  
The State shall strengthen infectious disease surveillance, implement direct network reporting of infectious disease epidemics and public health emergencies relying on the infectious disease surveillance system, establish surveillance sentinel sites for key infectious diseases and infectious diseases of unknown cause, expand the scope of infectious disease symptom surveillance, collect information on infectious disease syndromes and mass diseases of unknown cause, establish an infectious disease etiological surveillance network, conduct multi-pathogen surveillance through multiple approaches and channels, establish a smart multi-point trigger mechanism, enhance the sensitivity and accuracy of surveillance, improve real-time analysis and centralized assessment capabilities, and timely detect infectious disease epidemics and public health emergencies.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　疾病预防控制机构对传染病的发生、流行以及影响其发生、流行的因素进行监测，及时掌握重点传染病流行强度、危害程度以及病原体变异情况。  
疾病预防控制机构应当加强原因不明的传染病监测，提高快速发现和及时甄别能力；对新发传染病、境内已消除的传染病以及境外发生、境内尚未发生的传染病进行监测。

**Article 43** Disease prevention and control institutions shall monitor the occurrence, prevalence, and factors affecting the occurrence and prevalence of infectious diseases, and timely grasp the prevalence intensity, harm degree, and pathogen variation of key infectious diseases.  
Disease prevention and control institutions shall strengthen surveillance of infectious diseases of unknown cause, improve the ability to rapidly detect and timely identify them; and conduct surveillance of emerging infectious diseases, infectious diseases that have been eliminated within the territory, and infectious diseases occurring abroad but not yet occurring within the territory.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家建立跨部门、跨地域的传染病监测信息共享机制，加强卫生健康、疾病预防控制、生态环境、农业农村、海关、市场监督管理、移民管理、林业草原等部门的联动监测和信息共享。  
国家建立临床医疗、疾病预防控制信息的互通共享制度，加强医防协同，推动医疗机构等的信息系统与传染病监测系统互联互通，建立健全传染病诊断、病原体检测数据等的自动获取机制，规范信息共享流程，确保个人信息安全。

**Article 44** The State shall establish a cross-departmental and cross-regional information sharing mechanism for infectious disease surveillance, strengthen joint surveillance and information sharing among departments such as health, disease prevention and control, ecology and environment, agriculture and rural affairs, customs, market supervision and administration, immigration administration, forestry and grassland.  
The State shall establish a system for mutual access and sharing of clinical medical and disease prevention and control information, strengthen medical-prevention coordination, promote the interconnection and interoperability of the information systems of medical institutions and the infectious disease surveillance system, establish and improve the automatic acquisition mechanism for data such as infectious disease diagnosis and pathogen testing, standardize the information sharing process, and ensure the security of personal information.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家建立健全传染病疫情报告制度。  
疾病预防控制机构、医疗机构和采供血机构及其执行职务的人员发现甲类传染病患者、病原携带者、疑似患者或者新发传染病、突发原因不明的传染病，以及其他传染病暴发、流行时，应当于两小时内进行网络直报；发现乙类传染病患者、疑似患者或者国务院疾病预防控制部门规定需要报告的乙类传染病病原携带者时，应当于二十四小时内进行网络直报；发现丙类传染病患者时，应当于二十四小时内进行网络直报。  
中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队的医疗机构向社会公众提供医疗服务的，应当依照前款规定报告传染病疫情。  
传染病疫情报告遵循属地管理原则，具体办法由国务院疾病预防控制部门制定。

**Article 45** The State shall establish and improve the infectious disease epidemic reporting system.  
When disease prevention and control institutions, medical institutions, and blood collection and supply institutions and their personnel on duty discover Category A infectious disease patients, pathogen carriers, suspected patients, or emerging infectious diseases, infectious diseases of sudden unknown cause, as well as outbreaks or epidemics of other infectious diseases, they shall make direct network reports within two hours; when discovering Category B infectious disease patients, suspected patients, or Category B infectious disease pathogen carriers that need to be reported as stipulated by the disease prevention and control department of the State Council, they shall make direct network reports within twenty-four hours; when discovering Category C infectious disease patients, they shall make direct network reports within twenty-four hours.  
Where medical institutions of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force provide medical services to the public, they shall report infectious disease epidemics in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
The reporting of infectious disease epidemics shall follow the principle of territorial management, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the disease prevention and control department of the State Council.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　疾病预防控制机构、医疗机构和采供血机构应当建立健全传染病疫情报告管理制度，加强传染病疫情和相关信息报告的培训、日常管理和质量控制，定期对本机构报告的传染病疫情和相关信息以及报告质量进行分析、汇总和通报。

**Article 46** Disease prevention and control institutions, medical institutions, and blood collection and supply institutions shall establish and improve the management system for infectious disease epidemic reporting, strengthen the training, routine management, and quality control of infectious disease epidemic and related information reporting, and regularly analyze, summarize, and report on the infectious disease epidemics and related information reported by their own institutions as well as the reporting quality.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　学校、托育机构、养老机构、康复机构、福利机构、未成年人救助保护机构、救助管理机构、体育场馆、监管场所、车站、港口、机场等重点场所发现传染病患者、疑似患者时，应当按照国务院疾病预防控制部门的规定，向所在地疾病预防控制机构报告有关信息。  
检验检测机构等应当按照国务院疾病预防控制部门的规定，向所在地疾病预防控制机构报告与传染病防治有关的信息。

**Article 47** When key venues such as schools, childcare institutions, elderly care institutions, rehabilitation institutions, welfare institutions, minor assistance and protection institutions, relief management institutions, stadiums, detention places, stations, ports, and airports discover infectious disease patients or suspected patients, they shall report relevant information to the local disease prevention and control institution in accordance with the provisions of the disease prevention and control department of the State Council.  
Inspection and testing institutions, etc. shall report information related to infectious disease prevention and control to the local disease prevention and control institution in accordance with the provisions of the disease prevention and control department of the State Council.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　任何单位和个人发现传染病患者、疑似患者时，应当及时向附近的疾病预防控制机构、医疗机构或者疾病预防控制部门报告。  
疾病预防控制部门应当公布热线电话等，畅通报告途径，确保及时接收、调查和处理相关报告信息。

**Article 48** When any unit or individual discovers an infectious disease patient or suspected patient, they shall promptly report to the nearby disease prevention and control institution, medical institution, or disease prevention and control department.  
The disease prevention and control department shall publish hotline numbers, etc., smooth reporting channels, and ensure timely receipt, investigation, and handling of related report information.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　疾病预防控制机构应当设立或者指定专门的部门、人员负责传染病疫情信息管理工作，主动收集、分析、调查、核实传染病疫情信息。  
疾病预防控制机构接到甲类传染病、新发传染病、突发原因不明的传染病报告或者发现传染病暴发、流行时，应当于两小时内完成传染病疫情信息核实以及向同级卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门和上级疾病预防控制机构报告的工作。疾病预防控制部门接到报告后应当立即报告同级人民政府，同时报告上一级人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门和国务院卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门。

**Article 49** Disease prevention and control institutions shall establish or designate specialized departments and personnel responsible for the management of infectious disease epidemic information, and actively collect, analyze, investigate, and verify infectious disease epidemic information.  
When a disease prevention and control institution receives a report of a Category A infectious disease, emerging infectious disease, or infectious disease of sudden unknown cause, or discovers an outbreak or epidemic of an infectious disease, it shall complete the verification of the infectious disease epidemic information and the reporting to the health department and the disease prevention and control department at the same level and to the superior disease prevention and control institution within two hours. After receiving the report, the disease prevention and control department shall immediately report to the people's government at the same level, and at the same time report to the health department and the disease prevention and control department of the people's government at the next higher level and to the health department and the disease prevention and control department of the State Council.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　任何单位或者个人不得干预传染病疫情报告。  
依照本法规定负有传染病疫情报告职责的人民政府有关部门、疾病预防控制机构、医疗机构、采供血机构及其工作人员，不得隐瞒、谎报、缓报、漏报传染病疫情。

**Article 50** No unit or individual shall interfere with the reporting of infectious disease epidemics.  
The relevant departments of the people's government, disease prevention and control institutions, medical institutions, blood collection and supply institutions, and their staff who are responsible for reporting infectious disease epidemics in accordance with the provisions of this Law shall not conceal, falsely report, delay reporting, or omit reporting of infectious disease epidemics.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　对及时发现并报告新发传染病、突发原因不明的传染病的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予奖励。  
对经调查排除传染病疫情的，报告的单位和个人不承担法律责任。

**Article 51** Units and individuals that promptly discover and report emerging infectious diseases or infectious diseases of sudden unknown cause shall be rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Where an infectious disease epidemic is ruled out after investigation, the reporting unit or individual shall not bear legal liability.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家建立健全传染病疫情风险评估制度。  
疾病预防控制机构应当及时分析传染病和健康危害因素相关信息，评估发生传染病疫情的风险、可能造成的影响以及疫情发展态势。

**Article 52** The State shall establish and improve the infectious disease epidemic risk assessment system.  
Disease prevention and control institutions shall timely analyze information related to infectious diseases and health risk factors, and assess the risk of an infectious disease epidemic, the possible impact, and the development trend of the epidemic.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家建立健全传染病预警制度。  
疾病预防控制机构根据传染病监测信息和传染病疫情风险评估结果，向社会发布健康风险提示；发现可能发生突发公共卫生事件，经评估认为需要发布预警的，向同级疾病预防控制部门提出发布预警的建议。疾病预防控制部门收到建议后应当及时组织专家进行分析研判，需要发布预警的，由卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门立即向同级人民政府报告。  
县级以上人民政府依照有关突发公共卫生事件应对的法律、行政法规和国务院规定的权限和程序，决定向社会发布预警。

**Article 53** The State shall establish and improve the infectious disease early warning system.  
Disease prevention and control institutions shall, based on infectious disease surveillance information and the results of infectious disease epidemic risk assessment, issue health risk tips to the public; where they discover that a public health emergency may occur and, after assessment, consider it necessary to issue an early warning, they shall submit a proposal to the disease prevention and control department at the same level for issuing an early warning. After receiving the proposal, the disease prevention and control department shall promptly organize experts to conduct analysis and assessment; if an early warning is deemed necessary, the health department and the disease prevention and control department shall immediately report to the people's government at the same level.  
The people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations on responding to public health emergencies and the authority and procedures prescribed by the State Council, decide to issue an early warning to the public.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　县级以上地方人民政府疾病预防控制部门应当及时向本行政区域的疾病预防控制机构和医疗机构通报传染病疫情以及监测、预警的相关信息。接到通报的疾病预防控制机构和医疗机构应当及时报告本机构的主要负责人，并告知本机构的有关人员。

**Article 54** The disease prevention and control department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall promptly notify the disease prevention and control institutions and medical institutions in its administrative region of the infectious disease epidemic and related information on surveillance and early warning. The disease prevention and control institutions and medical institutions that receive the notification shall promptly report to the principal responsible persons of their own institutions and inform the relevant personnel of their own institutions.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国务院疾病预防控制部门应当及时向省级人民政府疾病预防控制部门和中央军事委员会负责卫生工作的部门通报全国传染病疫情以及监测、预警的相关信息。中央军事委员会负责卫生工作的部门发现传染病疫情时，应当向国务院疾病预防控制部门通报。  
毗邻或者相关地区的地方人民政府疾病预防控制部门，应当及时相互通报本行政区域的传染病疫情以及监测、预警的相关信息。

**Article 55** The disease prevention and control department of the State Council shall promptly notify the disease prevention and control departments of the provincial people's governments and the department responsible for health work of the Central Military Commission of the national infectious disease epidemic and related information on surveillance and early warning. When the department responsible for health work of the Central Military Commission discovers an infectious disease epidemic, it shall notify the disease prevention and control department of the State Council.  
The disease prevention and control departments of the local people's governments in adjacent or related areas shall promptly notify each other of the infectious disease epidemic in their respective administrative regions and related information on surveillance and early warning.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　县级以上人民政府疾病预防控制部门与同级人民政府教育、公安、民政、司法行政、生态环境、农业农村、市场监督管理、林业草原、中医药等部门建立传染病疫情通报机制，及时共享传染病疫情信息。  
传染病暴发、流行时，国务院卫生健康、疾病预防控制、外交、工业和信息化、公安、交通运输、铁路、民用航空、海关、移民管理等部门以及中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队的有关单位和部门等建立工作机制，及时共享传染病疫情信息。

**Article 56** The disease prevention and control department of the people's government at or above the county level shall establish an infectious disease epidemic notification mechanism with the departments of education, public security, civil affairs, judicial administration, ecology and environment, agriculture and rural affairs, market supervision and administration, forestry and grassland, traditional Chinese medicine, etc. of the people's government at the same level, and timely share infectious disease epidemic information.  
When an infectious disease outbreak or epidemic occurs, the departments of health, disease prevention and control, foreign affairs, industry and information technology, public security, transport, railway, civil aviation, customs, immigration administration, etc. of the State Council, as well as relevant units and departments of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, shall establish a working mechanism to timely share infectious disease epidemic information.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国家建立健全传染病疫情信息公布制度。  
国务院疾病预防控制部门定期向社会公布全国传染病疫情信息。县级以上地方人民政府疾病预防控制部门定期向社会公布本行政区域的传染病疫情信息。  
传染病暴发、流行时，县级以上地方人民政府疾病预防控制部门应当及时、准确地向社会公布本行政区域传染病名称、流行传播范围以及确诊病例、疑似病例、死亡病例数量等传染病疫情信息。传染病跨省级行政区域暴发、流行时，国务院疾病预防控制部门应当及时、准确地向社会公布上述信息。  
县级以上人民政府疾病预防控制部门发现虚假或者不完整传染病疫情信息的，应当及时发布准确的信息予以澄清。  
传染病疫情信息公布的具体办法由国务院疾病预防控制部门制定。

**Article 57** The State shall establish and improve the system for publication of infectious disease epidemic information.  
The disease prevention and control department of the State Council shall regularly publish to the public the national infectious disease epidemic information. The disease prevention and control department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall regularly publish to the public the infectious disease epidemic information of its administrative region.  
When an infectious disease outbreak or epidemic occurs, the disease prevention and control department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall timely and accurately publish to the public the infectious disease epidemic information of its administrative region, including the name of the infectious disease, the scope of prevalence and transmission, and the number of confirmed cases, suspected cases, and deaths. When an infectious disease outbreak or epidemic crosses provincial-level administrative regions, the disease prevention and control department of the State Council shall timely and accurately publish the above information to the public.  
Where the disease prevention and control department of the people's government at or above the county level discovers false or incomplete infectious disease epidemic information, it shall promptly publish accurate information to clarify.  
The specific measures for publication of infectious disease epidemic information shall be formulated by the disease prevention and control department of the State Council.

### 第四章　疫情控制

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　医疗机构、疾病预防控制机构发现甲类传染病时，应当立即采取下列措施，并向县级以上地方人民政府疾病预防控制部门报告：  
（一）对甲类传染病患者、病原携带者，予以隔离治疗、医学观察；  
（二）对甲类传染病疑似患者，确诊前单独隔离治疗；  
（三）对甲类传染病患者、病原携带者、疑似患者的密切接触者，予以医学观察，并采取其他必要的预防措施。  
医疗机构、疾病预防控制机构对甲类传染病患者、病原携带者、疑似患者以及上述人员的密切接触者采取隔离治疗、医学观察措施，应当根据国家有关规定和医学检查结果科学合理确定具体人员范围和期限，并根据情况变化及时调整。采取隔离治疗、医学观察措施，不得超出规定的范围和期限。  
医疗机构、疾病预防控制机构应当向甲类传染病患者、病原携带者、疑似患者以及上述人员的密切接触者书面告知诊断或者判定结果和依法应当采取的措施。  
甲类传染病患者、病原携带者、疑似患者以及上述人员的密切接触者应当主动接受和配合医学检查、隔离治疗、医学观察等措施。  
拒绝隔离治疗、医学观察或者隔离治疗、医学观察的期限未满擅自脱离的，由公安机关协助医疗机构、疾病预防控制机构采取强制隔离治疗、医学观察措施。

**Article 58** When medical institutions and disease prevention and control institutions discover Category A infectious diseases, they shall immediately take the following measures and report to the disease prevention and control department of the local people's government at or above the county level:  
(1) For Category A infectious disease patients and pathogen carriers, provide isolation treatment and medical observation;  
(2) For suspected Category A infectious disease patients, provide separate isolation treatment before diagnosis;  
(3) For close contacts of Category A infectious disease patients, pathogen carriers, and suspected patients, provide medical observation and take other necessary preventive measures.  
When medical institutions and disease prevention and control institutions take isolation treatment and medical observation measures for Category A infectious disease patients, pathogen carriers, suspected patients, and their close contacts, they shall scientifically and reasonably determine the specific scope of persons and duration in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the results of medical examination, and timely adjust according to changes in circumstances. The isolation treatment and medical observation measures shall not exceed the prescribed scope and duration.  
Medical institutions and disease prevention and control institutions shall inform the Category A infectious disease patients, pathogen carriers, suspected patients, and their close contacts in writing of the diagnosis or determination results and the measures to be taken according to law.  
Category A infectious disease patients, pathogen carriers, suspected patients, and their close contacts shall voluntarily accept and cooperate with measures such as medical examination, isolation treatment, and medical observation.  
If they refuse isolation treatment or medical observation, or leave without authorization before the expiration of the isolation treatment or medical observation period, the public security organ shall assist medical institutions and disease prevention and control institutions in taking compulsory isolation treatment and medical observation measures.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　医疗机构、疾病预防控制机构接到其他单位和个人报告甲类传染病的，有关甲类传染病患者、疑似患者的移交按照国务院疾病预防控制部门的规定执行。

**Article 59** Where medical institutions and disease prevention and control institutions receive reports of Category A infectious diseases from other units or individuals, the transfer of the relevant Category A infectious disease patients and suspected patients shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the disease prevention and control department of the State Council.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　医疗机构发现乙类或者丙类传染病患者时，应当根据病情采取必要的治疗和控制传播措施。  
县级以上地方人民政府疾病预防控制部门指定的医疗机构对肺结核患者进行治疗；对具有传染性的肺结核患者进行耐药检查和规范隔离治疗，对其密切接触者进行筛查。基层医疗卫生机构对肺结核患者进行健康管理。具体办法由国务院疾病预防控制部门拟订，报国务院卫生健康主管部门审核、发布。

**Article 60** When medical institutions discover Category B or Category C infectious disease patients, they shall take necessary treatment and transmission control measures based on the condition.  
Medical institutions designated by the disease prevention and control department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall provide treatment for tuberculosis patients; conduct drug resistance testing and standardized isolation treatment for communicable tuberculosis patients, and conduct screening for their close contacts. Primary-level medical and health institutions shall conduct health management for tuberculosis patients. The specific measures shall be formulated by the disease prevention and control department of the State Council, and submitted to the health department of the State Council for review and publication.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　医疗机构对本机构内被传染病病原体污染的场所、物品以及医疗废物、医疗污水，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定实施消毒和无害化处置。

**Article 61** A medical institution shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, carry out disinfection and harmless disposal of the premises, articles, medical waste, and medical sewage contaminated by infectious disease pathogens within its institution.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　疾病预防控制机构发现传染病疫情或者接到传染病疫情报告时，应当及时采取下列措施：  
（一）对传染病疫情进行流行病学调查，根据调查情况提出对受影响的相关区域的防控建议，对被污染的场所进行卫生处理，判定密切接触者，指导做好对密切接触者的管理，并向疾病预防控制部门提出传染病疫情防控方案；  
（二）传染病暴发、流行时，对受影响的相关区域进行卫生处理，向疾病预防控制部门提出传染病疫情防控方案，并按照传染病疫情防控相关要求采取措施；  
（三）指导下级疾病预防控制机构、医疗机构实施传染病预防、控制措施，组织、指导有关单位对传染病疫情的处理。  
有关单位和个人应当接受和配合疾病预防控制机构开展流行病学调查，如实提供信息。疾病预防控制机构开展流行病学调查，需要有关部门和单位协助的，有关部门和单位应当予以协助。  
发生传染病疫情时，疾病预防控制机构和省级以上人民政府疾病预防控制部门指派的其他与传染病有关的专业技术机构，可以进入受影响的相关区域进行调查、采集样本、技术分析和检验检测。被调查单位和个人应当如实提供信息；任何单位或者个人不得隐瞒信息、阻碍调查。

**Article 62** When a disease prevention and control institution discovers an infectious disease epidemic or receives a report of an infectious disease epidemic, it shall promptly take the following measures:  
(1) Conduct an epidemiological investigation of the infectious disease epidemic, propose prevention and control recommendations for the affected relevant areas based on the investigation, carry out sanitary treatment of contaminated premises, determine close contacts, guide the management of close contacts, and submit an infectious disease epidemic prevention and control plan to the disease prevention and control department;  
(2) When an infectious disease outbreak or epidemic occurs, carry out sanitary treatment of the affected relevant areas, submit an infectious disease epidemic prevention and control plan to the disease prevention and control department, and take measures in accordance with the relevant requirements for infectious disease epidemic prevention and control;  
(3) Guide lower-level disease prevention and control institutions and medical institutions in implementing infectious disease prevention and control measures, and organize and guide relevant entities in handling the infectious disease epidemic.  
Relevant entities and individuals shall accept and cooperate with disease prevention and control institutions in conducting epidemiological investigations and truthfully provide information. Where a disease prevention and control institution needs assistance from relevant departments and entities in conducting an epidemiological investigation, the relevant departments and entities shall provide assistance.  
When an infectious disease epidemic occurs, disease prevention and control institutions and other professional technical institutions related to infectious diseases assigned by the disease prevention and control department of the people's government at or above the provincial level may enter the affected relevant areas to conduct investigations, collect samples, perform technical analysis, and carry out inspection and testing. The investigated entities and individuals shall truthfully provide information; no entity or individual shall conceal information or obstruct the investigation.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　传染病暴发、流行时，县级以上地方人民政府应当立即组织力量，按照传染病预防控制应急预案进行防治，控制传染源，切断传染病的传播途径；发生重大传染病疫情，经评估必要时，可以采取下列紧急措施：  
（一）限制或者停止集市、影剧院演出或者其他人群聚集的活动；  
（二）停工、停业、停课；  
（三）封闭或者封存被传染病病原体污染的公共饮用水源、食品以及相关物品；  
（四）控制或者扑杀、无害化处理染疫动物；  
（五）封闭可能造成传染病扩散的场所；  
（六）防止传染病传播的其他必要措施。  
县级以上地方人民政府采取前款规定的紧急措施，应当同时向上一级人民政府报告。接到报告的上级人民政府认为采取的紧急措施不适当的，应当立即调整或者撤销。  
必要时，国务院或者国务院授权的部门可以决定在全国或者部分区域采取本条第一款规定的紧急措施。

**Article 63** When an infectious disease outbreak or epidemic occurs, the local people's government at or above the county level shall immediately organize forces to carry out prevention and treatment in accordance with the infectious disease prevention and control emergency plan, control the source of infection, and cut off the transmission routes of the infectious disease; where a major infectious disease epidemic occurs, upon assessment and if necessary, it may take the following emergency measures:  
(1) Restrict or stop activities such as markets, theater performances, or other crowd gatherings;  
(2) Suspend work, business, and classes;  
(3) Seal off or seal up public drinking water sources, food, and related articles contaminated by infectious disease pathogens;  
(4) Control or cull and harmlessly dispose of animals infected with epidemic diseases;  
(5) Seal off premises that may cause the spread of infectious diseases;  
(6) Other necessary measures to prevent the spread of infectious diseases.  
When taking the emergency measures prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the local people's government at or above the county level shall simultaneously report to the people's government at the next higher level. Where the higher-level people's government that receives the report considers the emergency measures taken to be inappropriate, it shall immediately adjust or revoke them.  
If necessary, the State Council or a department authorized by the State Council may decide to take the emergency measures prescribed in the first paragraph of this Article in the whole country or in some areas.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　对已经发生甲类传染病病例的场所或者该场所内的特定区域的人员，所在地县级以上地方人民政府可以实施隔离措施，同时向上一级人民政府报告。接到报告的上级人民政府认为实施的隔离措施不适当的，应当立即调整或者撤销。  
被实施隔离措施的人员应当予以配合；拒绝执行隔离措施的，由公安机关协助疾病预防控制机构采取强制隔离措施。

**Article 64** With respect to premises where cases of Class A infectious diseases have occurred or personnel in specific areas within such premises, the local people's government at or above the county level may implement isolation measures and simultaneously report to the people's government at the next higher level. Where the higher-level people's government that receives the report considers the implemented isolation measures to be inappropriate, it shall immediately adjust or revoke them.  
Personnel subject to isolation measures shall cooperate; where they refuse to comply with isolation measures, the public security organ shall assist the disease prevention and control institution in taking compulsory isolation measures.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　发生新发传染病、突发原因不明的传染病，县级以上地方人民政府经评估认为确有必要的，可以预先采取本法规定的甲类传染病预防、控制措施，同时向上一级人民政府报告。接到报告的上级人民政府认为预先采取的预防、控制措施不适当的，应当立即调整或者撤销。

**Article 65** When a new infectious disease or a sudden infectious disease of unknown cause occurs, the local people's government at or above the county level, upon assessment and deeming it truly necessary, may preemptively take the prevention and control measures for Class A infectious diseases prescribed in this Law, and simultaneously report to the people's government at the next higher level. Where the higher-level people's government that receives the report considers the preemptively taken prevention and control measures to be inappropriate, it shall immediately adjust or revoke them.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　因甲类、乙类传染病发生重大传染病疫情时，县级以上地方人民政府报经上一级人民政府决定，可以对进入或者离开本行政区域受影响的相关区域的人员、物资和交通工具实施卫生检疫。  
因甲类传染病发生重大传染病疫情时，省级人民政府可以决定对本行政区域受影响的相关区域实施封锁；封锁大、中城市或者跨省级行政区域的受影响的相关区域，以及因封锁导致中断干线交通或者封锁国境的，由国务院决定。

**Article 66** When a major infectious disease epidemic occurs due to Class A or Class B infectious diseases, the local people's government at or above the county level, upon reporting to and obtaining the decision of the people's government at the next higher level, may implement sanitary quarantine on personnel, materials, and means of transportation entering or leaving the affected relevant areas within its administrative region.  
When a major infectious disease epidemic occurs due to Class A infectious diseases, the people's government at the provincial level may decide to impose a lockdown on the affected relevant areas within its administrative region; the lockdown of large or medium-sized cities or affected relevant areas across provincial administrative regions, as well as lockdown resulting in interruption of trunk transportation or border closure, shall be decided by the State Council.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　依照[本法第六十三条](#art-63)至第六十六条规定采取传染病疫情防控措施时，决定采取措施的机关应当向社会发布公告，明确措施的具体内容、实施范围和实施期限，并进行必要的解释说明。相关疫情防控措施的解除，由原决定机关决定并宣布。  
采取前款规定的措施期间，当地人民政府应当保障食品、饮用水等基本生活必需品的供应，提供基本医疗服务，维护社会稳定；对未成年人、老年人、残疾人、孕产期和哺乳期的妇女以及需要及时救治的伤病人员等群体给予特殊照顾和安排，并确保相关人员获得医疗救治。当地人民政府应当公布求助电话等，畅通求助途径，及时向有需求的人员提供帮助。  
因采取[本法第五十八条](#art-58)、第六十三条至第六十六条规定的措施导致劳动者不能工作的，用人单位应当保留其工作，按照规定支付其在此期间的工资、发放生活费。用人单位可以按照规定享受有关帮扶政策。

**Article 67** When taking infectious disease epidemic prevention and control measures in accordance with Articles 63 to 66 of this Law, the authority that decides on the measures shall issue a public announcement to society, specifying the specific content, scope of implementation, and duration of the measures, and provide necessary explanations. The lifting of the relevant epidemic prevention and control measures shall be decided and announced by the original deciding authority.  
During the period when the measures prescribed in the preceding paragraph are taken, the local people's government shall ensure the supply of basic necessities such as food and drinking water, provide basic medical services, and maintain social stability; it shall give special care and arrangements to groups such as minors, the elderly, the disabled, women during pregnancy and lactation, and injured or sick persons requiring timely medical treatment, and ensure that relevant personnel receive medical treatment. The local people's government shall publish contact numbers for assistance, keep the channels for assistance open, and promptly provide help to persons in need.  
Where, due to the measures taken in accordance with Articles 58, 63 to 66 of this Law, a worker is unable to work, the employer shall retain the worker's job, pay wages and provide living allowances during the period in accordance with the provisions. The employer may enjoy relevant support policies in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　发生甲类传染病时，为了防止该传染病通过交通工具及其乘运的人员、物资传播，省级人民政府可以决定实施交通卫生检疫。具体办法由国务院制定。

**Article 68** When a Class A infectious disease occurs, in order to prevent the spread of the infectious disease through means of transportation and the persons and materials transported thereby, the people's government at the provincial level may decide to implement traffic sanitary quarantine. The specific measures shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　发生重大传染病疫情时，根据传染病疫情防控的需要，国务院及其有关部门有权在全国或者跨省级行政区域范围内，县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门有权在本行政区域内，紧急调集人员或者调用储备物资，临时征用房屋、交通工具以及相关设施、设备、场地和其他物资，要求有关单位和个人提供技术支持。  
紧急调集人员的，应当按照规定给予合理报酬。临时征用房屋、交通工具以及相关设施、设备、场地和其他物资，要求有关单位和个人提供技术支持的，应当依法给予公平、合理的补偿；能返还的，应当及时返还。

**Article 69** When a major infectious disease epidemic occurs, according to the needs of infectious disease epidemic prevention and control, the State Council and its relevant departments have the power to, within the whole country or across provincial administrative regions, and the local people's government at or above the county level and its relevant departments have the power to, within their respective administrative regions, urgently assemble personnel or call up reserve materials, temporarily requisition houses, means of transportation, and related facilities, equipment, premises, and other materials, and request relevant entities and individuals to provide technical support.  
Where personnel are urgently assembled, reasonable remuneration shall be paid in accordance with the provisions. Where houses, means of transportation, and related facilities, equipment, premises, and other materials are temporarily requisitioned, and relevant entities and individuals are requested to provide technical support, fair and reasonable compensation shall be given according to law; where they can be returned, they shall be promptly returned.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　医疗机构、疾病预防控制机构、检验检测机构应当按照传染病检验检测技术规范和标准开展检验检测活动，加强检验检测质量控制。

**Article 70** Medical institutions, disease prevention and control institutions, and inspection and testing institutions shall carry out inspection and testing activities in accordance with the technical specifications and standards for infectious disease inspection and testing, and strengthen quality control of inspection and testing.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　患甲类传染病、炭疽死亡的，应当将其尸体立即进行卫生处理，就近火化；患其他传染病死亡的，必要时应当将其尸体进行卫生处理后火化或者按照规定深埋。对尸体进行火化或者深埋应当及时告知死者家属。  
为了查找传染病病因，医疗机构在必要时可以按照国务院卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门的规定，对传染病患者尸体或者疑似传染病患者尸体进行解剖查验，并应当及时告知死者家属。对尸体进行解剖查验应当在符合生物安全条件的场所进行。

**Article 71** Where a person dies of a Class A infectious disease or anthrax, the corpse shall be promptly subjected to sanitary treatment and cremated nearby; where a person dies of another infectious disease, if necessary, the corpse shall be subjected to sanitary treatment and then cremated or buried deep in accordance with the provisions. The cremation or deep burial of the corpse shall be promptly notified to the family members of the deceased.  
For the purpose of finding the cause of an infectious disease, a medical institution may, when necessary, in accordance with the provisions of the competent health department and the disease prevention and control department of the State Council, perform an autopsy and examination on the corpse of an infectious disease patient or a suspected infectious disease patient, and shall promptly notify the family members of the deceased. The autopsy and examination of the corpse shall be conducted at a place that meets biosafety conditions.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　[本法第六十六条](#art-66)规定的受影响的相关区域中被传染病病原体污染或者可能被传染病病原体污染的物品，经消毒可以使用的，应当在疾病预防控制机构的指导下，进行消毒处理后，方可使用、出售和运输。

**Article 72** Articles contaminated or likely contaminated by infectious disease pathogens in the affected relevant areas as prescribed in Article 66 of this Law, if they can be used after disinfection, shall, under the guidance of a disease prevention and control institution, be subjected to disinfection treatment before they may be used, sold, or transported.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　传染病暴发、流行时，有关生产、供应单位应当及时生产、供应传染病疫情防控所需的药品、医疗器械和其他应急物资。交通运输、邮政、快递经营单位应当优先运送参与传染病疫情防控的人员以及传染病疫情防控所需的药品、医疗器械和其他应急物资。县级以上人民政府有关部门应当做好组织协调工作。

**Article 73** When an infectious disease outbreak or epidemic occurs, the relevant production and supply entities shall promptly produce and supply the drugs, medical devices, and other emergency materials needed for infectious disease epidemic prevention and control. Transportation, postal, and courier operation entities shall give priority to transporting personnel participating in infectious disease epidemic prevention and control as well as the drugs, medical devices, and other emergency materials needed for infectious disease epidemic prevention and control. The relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall perform well the work of organization and coordination.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　单位和个人认为采取[本法第五十八条](#art-58)、第六十三条至第六十六条规定的传染病疫情防控措施侵犯其合法权益的，可以向县级以上地方人民政府或者其指定的部门提出申诉，申诉期间相关措施不停止执行。县级以上地方人民政府应当畅通申诉渠道，完善处理程序，确保有关申诉及时处理。

**Article 74** Where an entity or individual considers that the infectious disease epidemic prevention and control measures taken in accordance with Articles 58 and 63 to 66 of this Law infringe upon its or his/her lawful rights and interests, it or he/she may file a petition with the local people's government at or above the county level or a department designated thereby. During the period of petition, the relevant measures shall not be suspended. The local people's government at or above the county level shall keep the petition channels open, improve the handling procedures, and ensure that relevant petitions are handled promptly.

### 第五章　医疗救治

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　县级以上人民政府应当加强和完善常态与应急相结合的传染病医疗救治服务网络建设，指定具备传染病救治条件和能力的医疗机构承担传染病救治任务，根据传染病救治需要设置传染病专科医院。

**Article 75** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen and improve the construction of a service network for infectious disease medical treatment combining normal conditions and emergencies, designate medical institutions with the conditions and capabilities for infectious disease treatment to undertake infectious disease treatment tasks, and establish specialized infectious disease hospitals according to the needs of infectious disease treatment.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　国家建立健全重大传染病疫情医疗救治体系，建立由传染病专科医院、综合医院、中医医院、院前急救机构、临时性救治场所、基层医疗卫生机构、血站等构成的综合医疗救治体系，对传染病患者进行分类救治，加强重症患者医疗救治，提高重大传染病疫情医疗救治能力。

**Article 76** The State shall establish and improve a medical treatment system for major infectious disease epidemics, establish a comprehensive medical treatment system composed of specialized infectious disease hospitals, general hospitals, traditional Chinese medicine hospitals, pre-hospital emergency institutions, temporary treatment sites, primary medical and health institutions, blood stations, etc., conduct classified treatment of infectious disease patients, strengthen medical treatment of severe patients, and improve the medical treatment capability for major infectious disease epidemics.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　医疗机构应当对传染病患者、疑似患者提供医疗救护、现场救援和接诊治疗，按照规定填写并妥善保管病历记录以及其他有关资料。  
医疗机构应当按照国务院卫生健康主管部门的规定设置发热门诊，加强发热门诊标准化建设，优化服务流程，提高服务能力。  
医疗机构应当实行传染病预检、分诊制度；对传染病患者、疑似患者，应当引导至相对隔离的分诊点进行初诊。医疗机构不具备相应救治能力的，应当将传染病患者、疑似患者及其病历记录一并转至具备相应救治能力的医疗机构。转诊过程中，对传染病患者、疑似患者应当采取必要的防护措施。

**Article 77** Medical institutions shall provide medical rescue, on-site rescue, and consultation and treatment to infectious disease patients and suspected patients, and fill in and properly keep medical records and other relevant materials in accordance with the provisions.  
Medical institutions shall set up fever clinics in accordance with the provisions of the competent health department of the State Council, strengthen the standardized construction of fever clinics, optimize service procedures, and improve service capabilities.  
Medical institutions shall implement a pre-examination and triage system for infectious diseases; for infectious disease patients and suspected patients, they shall guide them to relatively isolated triage points for initial diagnosis. Where a medical institution does not have the corresponding treatment capability, it shall transfer the infectious disease patient or suspected patient and their medical records to a medical institution with the corresponding treatment capability. During the transfer, necessary protective measures shall be taken for infectious disease patients and suspected patients.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　医疗机构应当按照传染病诊断标准和治疗要求采取相应措施，充分发挥中西医各自优势，加强中西医结合，提高传染病诊断和救治能力。  
国家支持和鼓励医疗机构结合自身特色，加强传染病诊断和救治研究。

**Article 78** Medical institutions shall take corresponding measures in accordance with infectious disease diagnostic standards and treatment requirements, fully leverage the respective advantages of traditional Chinese medicine and Western medicine, strengthen the integration of traditional Chinese medicine and Western medicine, and improve the capability of diagnosing and treating infectious diseases.  
The State supports and encourages medical institutions to, in light of their own characteristics, strengthen research on the diagnosis and treatment of infectious diseases.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　国家鼓励传染病防治用药品、医疗器械的研制和创新，对防治传染病急需的药品、医疗器械予以优先审评审批。  
因重大传染病疫情医疗救治紧急需要，医师可以按照国家统一制定的诊疗方案，在一定范围和期限内采用药品说明书中未明确的药品用法进行救治。  
发生重大传染病疫情，构成特别重大突发公共卫生事件的，国务院卫生健康主管部门根据传染病预防、控制和医疗救治需要提出紧急使用药物的建议，经国务院药品监督管理部门组织论证同意后可以在一定范围和期限内紧急使用。

**Article 79** The State encourages the research, development, and innovation of drugs and medical devices for the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases, and gives priority review and approval to drugs and medical devices urgently needed for the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases.  
Due to the urgent needs of medical treatment for a major infectious disease epidemic, a physician may, in accordance with the nationally unified diagnosis and treatment protocol, adopt the usage of drugs that are not specified in the drug instructions within a certain scope and period for treatment.  
Where a major infectious disease epidemic occurs and constitutes a particularly major public health emergency, the competent health department of the State Council may, based on the needs of infectious disease prevention, control, and medical treatment, propose recommendations for the emergency use of drugs, and after being organized for argumentation and approved by the drug regulatory department of the State Council, such drugs may be used on an emergency basis within a certain scope and period.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　国家建立重大传染病疫情心理援助制度。县级以上地方人民政府应当组织专业力量，定期开展培训和演练；发生重大传染病疫情时，对传染病患者、接受医学观察的人员、病亡者家属、相关工作人员等重点人群以及社会公众及时提供心理疏导和心理干预等服务。

**Article 80** The State establishes a psychological assistance system for major infectious disease epidemics. The local people's government at or above the county level shall organize professional forces, regularly carry out training and drills; when a major infectious disease epidemic occurs, it shall promptly provide psychological counseling and psychological intervention services to key groups such as infectious disease patients, persons under medical observation, family members of the deceased, relevant staff, and the general public.

### 第六章　保障措施

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　国家将传染病防治工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，县级以上地方人民政府将传染病防治工作纳入本行政区域的国民经济和社会发展规划。

**Article 81** The State shall incorporate the work of prevention and treatment of infectious diseases into the national economic and social development plan, and the local people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the work of prevention and treatment of infectious diseases into the national economic and social development plan of their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　县级以上地方人民政府按照本级政府职责，负责本行政区域传染病预防、控制工作经费。  
国务院卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门会同国务院有关部门，根据传染病流行趋势，确定全国传染病预防、监测、预测、预警、控制、救治、监督检查等项目。各级财政按照事权划分做好经费保障。  
省级人民政府根据本行政区域传染病流行趋势，在国务院卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门确定的项目基础上，确定传染病预防、监测、检测、风险评估、预测、预警、控制、救治、监督检查等项目，并保障项目的实施经费。

**Article 82** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the funds for the prevention and control of infectious diseases in their respective administrative regions.  
The health administrative department of the State Council and the disease prevention and control department shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council, determine the items for the prevention, surveillance, prediction, early warning, control, treatment, supervision and inspection of infectious diseases nationwide based on the epidemic trends of infectious diseases. The finance at all levels shall ensure adequate funding in accordance with the division of powers.  
The people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, based on the epidemic trends of infectious diseases in its administrative region and on the basis of the items determined by the health administrative department of the State Council and the disease prevention and control department, determine the items for the prevention, surveillance, testing, risk assessment, prediction, early warning, control, treatment, supervision and inspection of infectious diseases, and shall ensure the funding for the implementation of such items.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　县级以上人民政府应当按照规定落实疾病预防控制机构基本建设、设备购置、学科建设、人才培养等相关经费；对其他医疗卫生机构承担疾病预防控制任务所需经费按照规定予以保障。

**Article 83** The people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions, allocate relevant funds for the basic construction, equipment purchase, discipline development, and personnel training of disease prevention and control institutions; and shall ensure, in accordance with the provisions, the funds required by other medical and health institutions for undertaking disease prevention and control tasks.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　国家加强基层传染病防治体系建设，扶持欠发达地区、民族地区和边境地区的传染病防治工作。  
地方各级人民政府应当保障基层传染病预防、控制工作的必要经费。

**Article 84** The State shall strengthen the construction of a grass-roots system for the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases, and support the work of prevention and treatment of infectious diseases in underdeveloped areas, ethnic areas, and border areas.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall ensure the necessary funds for the grass-roots work of prevention and control of infectious diseases.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　国家加强医疗机构疾病预防控制能力建设，持续提升传染病专科医院、综合医院的传染病监测、检验检测、诊断和救治、科学研究等能力和水平。  
国家创新医防协同、医防融合机制，推进医疗机构与疾病预防控制机构深度协作。

**Article 85** The State shall strengthen the capacity building of medical institutions for disease prevention and control, and continuously enhance the capabilities and levels of infectious disease surveillance, testing, diagnosis and treatment, and scientific research in specialized infectious disease hospitals and general hospitals.  
The State shall innovate the mechanism of medical-prevention coordination and integration, and promote deep collaboration between medical institutions and disease prevention and control institutions.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　国家加强传染病防治人才队伍建设，推动传染病防治相关学科建设。  
开设医学专业的院校应当加强预防医学教育和科学研究，对在校医学专业学生以及其他与传染病防治相关的人员进行预防医学教育和培训，为传染病防治工作提供专业技术支持。  
疾病预防控制机构、医疗机构等应当定期对其工作人员进行传染病防治知识、技能的培训。

**Article 86** The State shall strengthen the building of a talent team for the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases, and promote the development of disciplines related to the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases.  
Institutions of higher education that offer medical programs shall strengthen preventive medicine education and scientific research, provide preventive medicine education and training to medical students and other personnel related to the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases, and provide professional technical support for the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases.  
Disease prevention and control institutions, medical institutions, and the like shall regularly conduct training on knowledge and skills of infectious disease prevention and treatment for their staff.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　县级以上人民政府应当加强疾病预防控制信息化建设，将其纳入全民健康信息化建设。  
县级以上人民政府应当建立传染病预防控制信息共享机制，利用全民健康信息平台、政务数据共享平台、应急管理信息系统等，共享并综合应用相关数据。  
国家加强传染病防治相关网络安全和数据安全管理工作，提高技术防范水平。

**Article 87** The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the informatization construction of disease prevention and control, and incorporate it into the informatization construction of national health.  
The people's governments at or above the county level shall establish an information sharing mechanism for the prevention and control of infectious diseases, and use the national health information platform, government data sharing platform, emergency management information system, etc., to share and comprehensively apply relevant data.  
The State shall strengthen the management of network security and data security related to the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases, and improve the level of technical prevention.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　对符合国家规定的传染病医疗费用，基本医疗保险按照规定予以支付。  
对患者、疑似患者治疗甲类传染病以及依照本法规定采取甲类传染病预防、控制措施的传染病的医疗费用，基本医疗保险、大病保险、医疗救助等按照规定支付后，其个人负担部分，政府按照规定予以补助。  
国家对患有特定传染病的困难人群实行医疗救助，减免医疗费用。  
国家鼓励商业保险机构开发传染病防治相关保险产品。

**Article 88** For the medical expenses of infectious diseases that meet the provisions of the State, the basic medical insurance shall make payments in accordance with the provisions.  
For the medical expenses of patients and suspected patients for the treatment of Class A infectious diseases and infectious diseases for which preventive and control measures applicable to Class A infectious diseases are taken in accordance with this Law, after payments by basic medical insurance, major illness insurance, medical assistance, etc., are made in accordance with the provisions, the part borne by the individual shall be subsidized by the government in accordance with the provisions.  
The State shall provide medical assistance to the needy population suffering from specific infectious diseases, and reduce or exempt medical expenses.  
The State shall encourage commercial insurance institutions to develop insurance products related to the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　国家建立健全公共卫生应急物资保障体系，提高传染病疫情防控应急物资保障水平，县级以上人民政府发展改革部门统筹防控应急物资保障工作。  
国家加强医药储备，将传染病防治相关药品、医疗器械、卫生防护用品等物资纳入公共卫生应急物资保障体系，实行中央和地方两级储备。  
国务院工业和信息化部门会同国务院有关部门，根据传染病预防、控制和公共卫生应急准备的需要，加强医药实物储备、产能储备、技术储备，指导地方开展医药储备工作，完善储备调整、调用和轮换机制。

**Article 89** The State shall establish and improve the system for guaranteeing public health emergency supplies, and raise the level of guarantee for emergency supplies for the prevention and control of infectious disease epidemics. The development and reform departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall coordinate the work of guaranteeing emergency supplies for prevention and control.  
The State shall strengthen the pharmaceutical stockpiles, incorporating drugs, medical devices, hygiene protective articles, and other materials related to the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases into the system for guaranteeing public health emergency supplies, and implement a two-level stockpile at the central and local levels.  
The industrial and information technology department of the State Council, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council, shall, based on the needs of prevention, control, and public health emergency preparedness for infectious diseases, strengthen the physical stockpile, production capacity stockpile, and technology stockpile of pharmaceuticals, guide local governments in carrying out pharmaceutical stockpile work, and improve the mechanisms for adjustment, deployment, and rotation of stockpiles.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　国家建立少见罕见传染病和境内已消除的传染病防治能力储备机制，支持相关疾病预防控制机构、医疗机构、科研机构持续开展相关培训、基础性和应用性研究、现场防治等工作，支持相关专家参与国际防控工作，持续保持对上述传染病进行识别、检验检测、诊断和救治的能力。

**Article 90** The State shall establish a mechanism for reserving the capacity for the prevention and treatment of rare infectious diseases and infectious diseases that have been eliminated within the territory, support relevant disease prevention and control institutions, medical institutions, and scientific research institutions in continuously carrying out relevant training, basic and applied research, on-site prevention and treatment, and other work, support relevant experts in participating in international prevention and control work, and continuously maintain the capacity to identify, test, diagnose, and treat the aforesaid infectious diseases.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　对从事传染病预防、医疗、科研、教学和现场处理疫情的人员，以及在生产、工作中接触传染病病原体的其他人员，按照国家规定采取有效的卫生防护措施和医疗保健措施，并给予适当的津贴。

**Article 91** Effective health protection measures and medical care measures shall be taken in accordance with State provisions for personnel engaged in the prevention, medical treatment, scientific research, teaching, and on-site handling of epidemic situations of infectious diseases, as well as other personnel who come into contact with infectious disease pathogens in production or work, and appropriate allowances shall be granted to them.

### 第七章　监督管理

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　县级以上人民政府应当定期研究部署重大传染病疫情防控等疾病预防控制工作，定期向社会发布传染病防治工作报告，向本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告传染病防治工作，依法接受监督。  
县级以上人民政府对下级人民政府履行传染病防治职责进行监督。地方人民政府未履行传染病防治职责的，上级人民政府可以对其主要负责人进行约谈。被约谈的地方人民政府应当立即采取措施进行整改，约谈和整改情况应当纳入地方人民政府工作评议、考核记录。履行传染病防治职责不力、失职失责，造成严重后果或者恶劣影响的，依法进行问责。

**Article 92** The people's governments at or above the county level shall regularly study and deploy the work of disease prevention and control such as the prevention and control of major infectious disease epidemics, regularly issue reports on the work of prevention and treatment of infectious diseases to the public, report the work of prevention and treatment of infectious diseases to the standing committees of the people's congresses at the corresponding level, and accept supervision in accordance with law.  
The people's governments at or above the county level shall supervise the performance of the duties of prevention and treatment of infectious diseases by the people's governments at lower levels. If a local people's government fails to perform its duties of prevention and treatment of infectious diseases, the people's government at a higher level may conduct an interview with its principal responsible person. The local people's government that has been interviewed shall immediately take measures to make rectification, and the interview and rectification shall be included in the work appraisal and assessment records of the local people's government. If the performance of duties of prevention and treatment of infectious diseases is ineffective, dereliction of duty or neglect of duty results in serious consequences or adverse effects, accountability shall be carried out in accordance with law.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　县级以上人民政府疾病预防控制部门对传染病防治工作履行下列监督检查职责：  
（一）对下级人民政府疾病预防控制部门履行本法规定的职责进行监督检查；  
（二）对疾病预防控制机构、医疗机构、采供血机构的传染病预防、控制工作进行监督检查；  
（三）对用于传染病防治的消毒产品及其生产企业、饮用水供水单位以及涉及饮用水卫生安全的产品进行监督检查；  
（四）对公共场所、学校、托育机构的卫生条件和传染病预防、控制措施进行监督检查。  
县级以上人民政府卫生健康、疾病预防控制等部门依据职责对病原微生物菌（毒）种和传染病检测样本的采集、保藏、提供、携带、运输、使用进行监督检查。

**Article 93** The disease prevention and control departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall perform the following supervisory and inspection duties over the work of prevention and treatment of infectious diseases:  
(1) Supervising and inspecting the performance of duties under this Law by the disease prevention and control departments of the people's governments at lower levels;  
(2) Supervising and inspecting the work of prevention and control of infectious diseases by disease prevention and control institutions, medical institutions, and blood collection and supply institutions;  
(3) Supervising and inspecting the disinfection products used for the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases and their production enterprises, drinking water supply units, and products concerning the safety of drinking water;  
(4) Supervising and inspecting the sanitary conditions and measures for prevention and control of infectious diseases in public places, schools, and childcare institutions.  
The health, disease prevention and control, and other departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their functions and duties, supervise and inspect the collection, storage, provision, carrying, transportation, and use of pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) and infectious disease test samples.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门在履行监督检查职责时，有权进入传染病疫情发生现场及相关单位，开展查阅或者复制有关资料、采集样本、制作现场笔录等调查取证工作。被检查单位应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻挠。

**Article 94** When performing the duty of supervision and inspection, the health administrative departments and disease prevention and control departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall have the right to enter the scene of an infectious disease epidemic and relevant units, and carry out investigation and evidence collection work such as consulting or copying relevant materials, collecting samples, and making on-site records. The unit being inspected shall cooperate and shall not refuse or obstruct.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　县级以上地方人民政府疾病预防控制部门在履行监督检查职责时，发现可能被传染病病原体污染的公共饮用水源、食品以及相关物品，如不及时采取控制措施可能导致传染病传播、暴发、流行的，应当采取封闭公共饮用水源、封存食品以及相关物品或者暂停销售的临时控制措施，并予以检验或者进行消毒处理。经检验，对被污染的食品，应当予以销毁；对未被污染的食品或者经消毒处理后可以使用的物品，应当及时解除控制措施。  
根据县级以上地方人民政府采取的传染病预防、控制措施，市场监督管理部门可以采取封存或者暂停销售可能导致传染病传播、暴发、流行的食品以及相关物品等措施。

**Article 95** When performing the duty of supervision and inspection, if the disease prevention and control department of a local people's government at or above the county level discovers that a public drinking water source, food, or related articles may be contaminated by infectious disease pathogens, and if control measures are not taken in time, the spread, outbreak, or prevalence of an infectious disease may result, it shall take temporary control measures such as sealing off the public drinking water source, sealing up the food and related articles, or suspending their sale, and shall conduct testing or carry out disinfection treatment. After testing, if the food is contaminated, it shall be destroyed; if the food is not contaminated or if the articles can be used after disinfection treatment, the control measures shall be promptly lifted.  
In accordance with the measures for prevention and control of infectious diseases taken by the local people's government at or above the county level, the market regulatory department may take measures such as sealing up or suspending the sale of food and related articles that may cause the spread, outbreak, or prevalence of infectious diseases.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门工作人员依法执行职务时，应当不少于两人，并出示执法证件，填写执法文书。  
执法文书经核对无误后，应当由执法人员和当事人签名。当事人拒绝签名的，执法人员应当注明情况。

**Article 96** When performing their duties in accordance with law, the staff members of the health administrative departments and disease prevention and control departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be not less than two persons, and shall present their law enforcement credentials and fill out law enforcement documents.  
After the law enforcement documents have been checked and found to be correct, they shall be signed by the law enforcement personnel and the party. If the party refuses to sign, the law enforcement personnel shall indicate such circumstances.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门应当依法建立健全内部监督制度，对其工作人员依据法定职权和程序履行职责的情况进行监督。  
上级人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门发现下级人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门不及时处理职责范围内的事项或者不履行职责的，应当责令纠正或者直接予以处理。

**Article 97** The health administrative departments and disease prevention and control departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with law, establish and improve internal supervision systems, and supervise the performance of duties by their staff members in accordance with statutory functions and procedures.  
If the health administrative department or disease prevention and control department of a people's government at a higher level discovers that the health administrative department or disease prevention and control department of a people's government at a lower level fails to promptly handle matters within its scope of functions or fails to perform its duties, it shall order rectification or directly handle the matter.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门和其他有关部门应当依法履行职责，自觉接受社会监督。  
任何单位和个人对违反本法规定的行为，有权向县级以上人民政府及其卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门和有关机关举报。接到举报的机关应当及时调查、处理。对查证属实的举报，按照规定给予举报人奖励。县级以上人民政府及其卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门和有关机关应当对举报人的信息予以保密，保护举报人的合法权益。

**Article 98** The health administrative departments, disease prevention and control departments, and other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall perform their duties in accordance with law and consciously accept public supervision.  
Any unit or individual shall have the right to report to the people's government at or above the county level and its health administrative department, disease prevention and control department, and relevant authorities any act that violates the provisions of this Law. The authority that receives the report shall promptly investigate and handle it. For a report verified to be true, a reward shall be given to the reporter in accordance with the provisions. The people's government at or above the county level and its health administrative department, disease prevention and control department, and relevant authorities shall keep confidential the information of the reporter and protect his or her lawful rights and interests.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　卫生健康、疾病预防控制等部门发现涉嫌传染病防治相关犯罪的，应当按照有关规定及时将案件移送公安机关。对移送的案件，公安机关应当及时审查处理。  
对依法不需要追究刑事责任或者免予刑事处罚，但依法应当追究行政责任的，公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院应当及时将案件移送卫生健康、疾病预防控制等部门，有关部门应当依法处理。  
公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院商请卫生健康、疾病预防控制等部门提供检验检测结论、认定意见以及对涉案物品进行无害化处置等协助的，有关部门应当及时予以协助。

**Article 99** Where the health, disease prevention and control, and other departments discover crimes suspected of being related to the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases, they shall promptly transfer the case to the public security organ in accordance with the relevant provisions. For a transferred case, the public security organ shall promptly review and handle it.  
Where the case does not need to be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with law or the person is exempted from criminal punishment, but administrative liability should be pursued in accordance with law, the public security organ, the people's procuratorate, and the people's court shall promptly transfer the case to the health, disease prevention and control, and other departments, and the relevant departments shall handle it in accordance with law.  
Where the public security organ, the people's procuratorate, or the people's court requests the health, disease prevention and control, and other departments to provide assistance such as testing conclusions, identification opinions, and harmless disposal of the items involved in the case, the relevant departments shall promptly provide such assistance.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　违反本法规定，地方各级人民政府未依法履行报告职责，隐瞒、谎报、缓报、漏报传染病疫情，干预传染病疫情报告，或者在传染病暴发、流行时未依法组织救治、采取控制措施的，由上级人民政府责令改正，通报批评；情节严重的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 100** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a local people's government at any level fails to perform the duty of reporting in accordance with law, conceals, falsely reports, delays reporting, or omits reporting an infectious disease epidemic, interferes with the reporting of an infectious disease epidemic, or fails to organize treatment or take control measures in accordance with law during an outbreak or prevalence of an infectious disease, the people's government at a higher level shall order it to make corrections and circulate a notice of criticism; if the circumstances are serious, the leading personnel in charge and the directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions in accordance with law.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　违反本法规定，县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门有下列情形之一的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门责令改正，通报批评；情节严重的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）未依法履行传染病疫情通报、报告或者公布职责，隐瞒、谎报、缓报、漏报传染病疫情，或者干预传染病疫情报告；  
（二）发生或者可能发生传染病传播时未依法采取预防、控制措施；  
（三）未依法履行监督检查职责，或者发现违法行为不及时查处；  
（四）未及时调查、处理对下级人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门不履行传染病防治职责的举报；  
（五）违反本法规定的其他失职、渎职行为。

**Article 101** In violation of this Law, where the health administrative department, disease prevention and control department of a people's government at or above the county level commits any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered to correct and notified in criticism by the people's government at the same level or the health administrative department, disease prevention and control department of the people's government at a higher level; if the circumstances are serious, the persons in charge and the directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) Failing to perform the duties of notification, reporting or publication of infectious disease epidemic situations according to law, concealing, falsely reporting, delaying reporting or underreporting infectious disease epidemic situations, or interfering with the reporting of infectious disease epidemic situations;  
(2) Failing to take prevention and control measures according to law when transmission of infectious diseases occurs or may occur;  
(3) Failing to perform supervision and inspection duties according to law, or failing to investigate and deal with illegal acts in a timely manner;  
(4) Failing to investigate and handle in a timely manner reports about lower-level people's government health administrative departments or disease prevention and control departments failing to perform their duties of infectious disease prevention and control;  
(5) Other acts of dereliction or malfeasance in violation of this Law.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　违反本法规定，县级以上人民政府有关部门未依法履行传染病防治、疫情通报和保障职责的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府有关部门责令改正，通报批评；情节严重的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 102** In violation of this Law, where the relevant department of a people's government at or above the county level fails to perform its duties of infectious disease prevention and control, epidemic notification and guarantee according to law, it shall be ordered to correct and notified in criticism by the people's government at the same level or the relevant department of the people's government at a higher level; if the circumstances are serious, the persons in charge and the directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　违反本法规定，疾病预防控制机构有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府疾病预防控制部门责令改正，给予警告或者通报批评，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分，并可以由原发证部门依法吊销有关责任人员的执业证书：  
（一）未依法履行传染病监测、疫情风险评估职责；  
（二）未依法履行传染病疫情报告职责，隐瞒、谎报、缓报、漏报传染病疫情，或者干预传染病疫情报告；  
（三）未主动收集传染病疫情信息，或者对传染病疫情信息和疫情报告未及时进行分析、调查、核实；  
（四）发现传染病疫情或者接到传染病疫情报告时，未依据职责及时采取本法规定的措施；  
（五）未遵守国家有关规定，导致因使用血液制品引起经血液传播疾病的发生。

**Article 103** In violation of this Law, where a disease prevention and control institution commits any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered to correct, given a warning or notified in criticism by the disease prevention and control department of a people's government at or above the county level, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law, and the original licensing department may revoke the practice certificate of the responsible person concerned according to law:  
(1) Failing to perform the duties of infectious disease monitoring and epidemic risk assessment according to law;  
(2) Failing to perform the duty of reporting infectious disease epidemic situations according to law, concealing, falsely reporting, delaying reporting or underreporting infectious disease epidemic situations, or interfering with the reporting of infectious disease epidemic situations;  
(3) Failing to proactively collect information on infectious disease epidemic situations, or failing to promptly analyze, investigate and verify such information and epidemic reports;  
(4) When discovering an infectious disease epidemic or receiving a report thereof, failing to promptly take the measures provided for in this Law according to its duties;  
(5) Failing to comply with relevant state regulations, resulting in the occurrence of blood-borne diseases due to the use of blood products.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　违反本法规定，医疗机构有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府疾病预防控制部门责令改正，给予警告或者通报批评，可以并处十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，可以由原发证部门或者原备案部门依法吊销医疗机构执业许可证或者责令停止执业活动，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分，并可以由原发证部门责令有关责任人员暂停六个月以上一年以下执业活动直至依法吊销执业证书：  
（一）未按照规定承担本机构的传染病预防、控制工作，医疗机构感染控制任务或者责任区域内的传染病预防工作；  
（二）未按照规定报告传染病疫情，隐瞒、谎报、缓报、漏报传染病疫情，或者干预传染病疫情报告；  
（三）未按照规定对本机构内被传染病病原体污染的场所、物品以及医疗废物、医疗污水实施消毒或者无害化处置。  
违反本法规定，医疗机构有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门依照前款规定给予行政处罚，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）发现传染病疫情时，未按照规定对传染病患者、疑似患者提供医疗救护、现场救援、接诊治疗、转诊，或者拒绝接受转诊；  
（二）未遵守国家有关规定，导致因输入血液、使用血液制品引起经血液传播疾病的发生。  
医疗机构未按照规定对使用的医疗器械进行消毒或者灭菌，或者对按照规定一次性使用的医疗器械使用后未予以销毁、再次使用的，依照有关医疗器械管理的法律、行政法规规定追究法律责任。

**Article 104** In violation of this Law, where a medical institution commits any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered to correct, given a warning or notified in criticism by the disease prevention and control department of a people's government at or above the county level, and may be concurrently imposed a fine of not more than RMB 100,000; if the circumstances are serious, the original licensing department or the original filing department may revoke the medical institution's practice license according to law or order it to cease practice activities, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law, and the original licensing department may order the responsible persons concerned to suspend practice activities for not less than six months but not more than one year until the revocation of practice certificates according to law:  
(1) Failing to undertake the institution's infectious disease prevention and control work, medical institution infection control tasks or infectious disease prevention work within the responsible area as required;  
(2) Failing to report infectious disease epidemic situations as required, concealing, falsely reporting, delaying reporting or underreporting infectious disease epidemic situations, or interfering with the reporting of infectious disease epidemic situations;  
(3) Failing to disinfect or carry out harmless treatment on places, articles contaminated by infectious disease pathogens, medical waste and medical sewage within the institution as required.  
In violation of this Law, where a medical institution commits any of the following circumstances, the health administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level shall impose administrative penalties in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) When discovering an infectious disease epidemic, failing to provide medical rescue, on-site rescue, consultation and treatment, or referral to infectious disease patients or suspected patients as required, or refusing to accept referrals;  
(2) Failing to comply with relevant state regulations, resulting in the occurrence of blood-borne diseases due to blood transfusion or use of blood products.  
Where a medical institution fails to disinfect or sterilize the medical devices used as required, or fails to destroy single-use medical devices after use as required and reuses them, legal liability shall be investigated according to the laws and administrative regulations on medical device management.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　违反本法规定，采供血机构未按照规定报告传染病疫情，隐瞒、谎报、缓报、漏报传染病疫情，或者干预传染病疫情报告的，由县级以上人民政府疾病预防控制部门责令改正，给予警告或者通报批评，可以并处十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，可以由原发证部门依法吊销采供血机构的执业许可证，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分，并可以由原发证部门责令有关责任人员暂停六个月以上一年以下执业活动直至依法吊销执业证书。  
采供血机构未执行国家有关规定，导致因输入血液引起经血液传播疾病发生的，由县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门依照前款规定给予行政处罚，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。  
非法采集血液或者组织他人出卖血液的，由县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，并处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款。

**Article 105** In violation of this Law, where a blood collection and supply institution fails to report infectious disease epidemic situations as required, conceals, falsely reports, delays reporting or underreports infectious disease epidemic situations, or interferes with the reporting of infectious disease epidemic situations, it shall be ordered to correct, given a warning or notified in criticism by the disease prevention and control department of a people's government at or above the county level, and may be concurrently imposed a fine of not more than RMB 100,000; if the circumstances are serious, the original licensing department may revoke the practice license of the blood collection and supply institution according to law, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law, and the original licensing department may order the responsible persons concerned to suspend practice activities for not less than six months but not more than one year until the revocation of practice certificates according to law.  
Where a blood collection and supply institution fails to implement relevant state regulations, resulting in the occurrence of blood-borne diseases due to blood transfusion, the health administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level shall impose administrative penalties in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.  
Where blood is illegally collected or others are organized to sell blood, the health administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 500,000.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　违反本法规定，交通运输、邮政、快递经营单位未优先运送参与传染病疫情防控的人员以及传染病疫情防控所需的药品、医疗器械和其他应急物资的，由交通运输、铁路、民用航空、邮政管理部门依据职责责令改正，给予警告；造成严重后果的，并处一万元以上十万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 106** In violation of this Law, where a transportation, postal or express delivery operating entity fails to give priority to the transportation of personnel participating in infectious disease epidemic prevention and control, as well as drugs, medical devices and other emergency materials needed for infectious disease epidemic prevention and control, the transportation, railway, civil aviation or postal administration department shall, according to its functions, order it to correct and give a warning; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 shall also be imposed, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府疾病预防控制部门责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，可以并处二十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，可以由原发证部门依法吊销相关许可证，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员可以禁止其五年内从事相应生产经营活动：  
（一）饮用水供水单位未取得卫生许可擅自供水，或者供应的饮用水不符合国家卫生标准和卫生规范造成或者可能造成传染病传播、暴发、流行；  
（二）生产、销售未取得卫生许可的涉及饮用水卫生安全的产品，或者生产、销售的涉及饮用水卫生安全的产品不符合国家卫生标准和卫生规范；  
（三）未取得卫生许可生产用于传染病防治的消毒产品，或者生产、销售的用于传染病防治的消毒产品不符合国家卫生标准和卫生规范；  
（四）生产、销售未取得卫生许可的利用新材料、新工艺技术和新杀菌原理生产的消毒剂和消毒器械；  
（五）出售、运输[本法第六十六条](#art-66)规定的受影响的相关区域中被传染病病原体污染或者可能被传染病病原体污染的物品，未进行消毒处理。

**Article 107** In violation of this Law, under any of the following circumstances, the disease prevention and control department of a people's government at or above the county level shall order correction, give a warning, confiscate illegal gains, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than RMB 200,000; if the circumstances are serious, the original licensing department may revoke the relevant license according to law, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons may be prohibited from engaging in corresponding production and operation activities for five years:  
(1) A drinking water supply unit supplies water without obtaining a health permit, or the drinking water supplied does not meet national health standards and health norms, causing or potentially causing the transmission, outbreak or epidemic of infectious diseases;  
(2) Producing or selling products related to drinking water hygiene and safety without obtaining a health permit, or the products produced or sold do not meet national health standards and health norms;  
(3) Producing disinfection products for infectious disease prevention and control without obtaining a health permit, or the disinfection products produced or sold do not meet national health standards and health norms;  
(4) Producing or selling disinfectants and disinfection devices produced using new materials, new process technologies and new sterilization principles without obtaining a health permit;  
(5) Selling or transporting articles contaminated or likely contaminated by infectious disease pathogens in the affected relevant areas specified in Article 66 of this Law without carrying out disinfection treatment.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府卫生健康、疾病预防控制等部门依据职责责令改正，给予警告或者通报批评，没收违法所得，可以并处十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，可以由原发证部门依法吊销相关许可证，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分，并可以由原发证部门责令有关责任人员暂停六个月以上一年以下执业活动直至依法吊销执业证书：  
（一）疾病预防控制机构、医疗机构的实验室和从事病原微生物实验的单位，不符合国家规定的条件和技术标准，对传染病病原体和样本未按照规定的措施实行严格管理；  
（二）违反国家有关规定，采集、保藏、提供、携带、运输、使用病原微生物菌（毒）种和传染病检测样本；  
（三）医疗机构、疾病预防控制机构、检验检测机构未按照传染病检验检测技术规范和标准开展检验检测活动，或者出具虚假检验检测报告；  
（四）生产、销售应当备案而未备案的消毒剂、消毒器械以及抗（抑）菌剂；  
（五）公共场所、学校、托育机构的卫生条件和传染病预防、控制措施不符合国家卫生标准和卫生规范。

**Article 108** In violation of this Law, under any of the following circumstances, the health, disease prevention and control and other departments of a people's government at or above the county level shall, according to their functions, order correction, give a warning or notify in criticism, confiscate illegal gains, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than RMB 100,000; if the circumstances are serious, the original licensing department may revoke the relevant license according to law, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law, and the original licensing department may order the responsible persons concerned to suspend practice activities for not less than six months but not more than one year until the revocation of practice certificates according to law:  
(1) Laboratories of disease prevention and control institutions or medical institutions, and units engaged in pathogenic microorganism experiments, do not meet the conditions and technical standards prescribed by the state, and fail to strictly manage infectious disease pathogens and samples according to the prescribed measures;  
(2) In violation of relevant state regulations, collecting, preserving, providing, carrying, transporting or using pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) and infectious disease testing samples;  
(3) Medical institutions, disease prevention and control institutions, or inspection and testing institutions fail to carry out inspection and testing activities in accordance with the technical specifications and standards for infectious disease inspection and testing, or issue false inspection and testing reports;  
(4) Producing or selling disinfectants, disinfection devices, and antibacterial (inhibitory) agents that should have been filed but have not been filed;  
(5) The sanitary conditions and infectious disease prevention and control measures of public places, schools, and childcare institutions do not meet national health standards and health norms.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　违反本法规定，在国家确认的自然疫源地兴建水利、交通、旅游、能源等大型建设项目，未经卫生调查进行施工，或者未按照疾病预防控制机构的意见采取必要的传染病预防、控制措施的，由县级以上人民政府疾病预防控制部门责令限期改正，给予警告，并处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款；逾期不改正的，处五十万元以上一百万元以下罚款，提请有关人民政府依据职责权限责令停建、拆除，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 109** In violation of this Law, where a large-scale construction project such as water conservancy, transportation, tourism or energy is built in a natural epidemic focus confirmed by the state, construction is carried out without a health survey, or the necessary infectious disease prevention and control measures are not taken according to the opinions of the disease prevention and control institution, the disease prevention and control department of a people's government at or above the county level shall order correction within a time limit, give a warning, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000; if correction is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000 shall be imposed, and the relevant people's government shall be requested to order cessation of construction or demolition according to its functions and powers, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　违反本法规定，县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门或者其他有关部门未依法履行个人信息保护义务的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府有关部门责令改正，通报批评；情节严重的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。  
医疗机构、疾病预防控制机构泄露传染病患者、病原携带者、疑似患者或者上述人员的密切接触者的个人隐私或者个人信息的，由县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门依据职责责令改正，给予警告或者通报批评，可以并处五万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分，对有关责任人员依照有关医师、护士管理等法律、行政法规规定追究法律责任。  
传染病防治中其他未依法履行个人信息保护义务的，依照有关个人信息保护的法律、行政法规规定追究法律责任。

**Article 110** In violation of this Law, where the health administrative department, disease prevention and control department or other relevant department of a people's government at or above the county level fails to perform its obligation to protect personal information according to law, it shall be ordered to correct and notified in criticism by the people's government at the same level or the relevant department of the people's government at a higher level; if the circumstances are serious, the persons in charge and the directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.  
Where a medical institution or disease prevention and control institution discloses the personal privacy or personal information of an infectious disease patient, pathogen carrier, suspected patient or a close contact of the above persons, the health administrative department or disease prevention and control department of a people's government at or above the county level shall, according to its functions, order correction, give a warning or notify in criticism, may concurrently impose a fine of not more than RMB 50,000, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law, and the responsible persons concerned shall be investigated for legal liability in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations on physician and nurse management.  
Where other obligations to protect personal information are not performed according to law in infectious disease prevention and control, legal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations on personal information protection.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府疾病预防控制部门责令改正，给予警告，对违法的单位可以并处二万元以下罚款，对违法的个人可以并处一千元以下罚款；情节严重的，由原发证部门依法吊销相关许可证或者营业执照：  
（一）拒不执行人民政府及其有关部门依法采取的传染病疫情防控措施；  
（二）拒不接受和配合疾病预防控制机构依法采取的传染病疫情防控措施；  
（三）拒不接受和配合疾病预防控制机构开展的流行病学调查，或者在流行病学调查中故意隐瞒传染病病情、传染病接触史或者传染病暴发、流行地区旅行史；  
（四）甲类传染病患者、病原携带者、疑似患者或者上述人员的密切接触者拒绝接受和配合依法采取的隔离治疗、医学观察措施，或者隔离治疗、医学观察的期限未满擅自脱离；  
（五）故意传播传染病；  
（六）故意编造、散布虚假传染病疫情信息；  
（七）其他妨害依法采取的传染病疫情防控措施的行为。  
安排传染病患者、病原携带者、疑似患者从事法律、行政法规和国务院疾病预防控制部门规定禁止从事的易使该传染病扩散的工作的，由县级以上人民政府疾病预防控制部门责令改正，给予警告，可以并处二万元以下罚款；法律、行政法规另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 111** In violation of this Law, under any of the following circumstances, the disease prevention and control department of a people's government at or above the county level shall order correction, give a warning, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than RMB 20,000 on the violating unit and a fine of not more than RMB 1,000 on the violating individual; if the circumstances are serious, the original licensing department shall revoke the relevant license or business license according to law:  
(1) Refusing to implement the infectious disease epidemic prevention and control measures taken according to law by the people's government and its relevant departments;  
(2) Refusing to accept and cooperate with the infectious disease epidemic prevention and control measures taken according to law by the disease prevention and control institution;  
(3) Refusing to accept and cooperate with the epidemiological investigation carried out by the disease prevention and control institution, or intentionally concealing the infectious disease condition, history of contact with infectious diseases, or travel history to areas with infectious disease outbreaks or epidemics during the epidemiological investigation;  
(4) A patient with a Class A infectious disease, pathogen carrier, suspected patient or a close contact of the above persons refuses to accept and cooperate with the isolation treatment or medical observation measures taken according to law, or leaves without authorization before the expiration of the isolation treatment or medical observation period;  
(5) Intentionally spreading infectious diseases;  
(6) Intentionally fabricating or disseminating false information about infectious disease epidemic situations;  
(7) Other acts hindering the infectious disease epidemic prevention and control measures taken according to law.  
Where a person arranges for an infectious disease patient, pathogen carrier or suspected patient to engage in work that is prohibited by laws, administrative regulations and the provisions of the disease prevention and control department of the State Council and is likely to spread the infectious disease, the disease prevention and control department of a people's government at or above the county level shall order correction, give a warning, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than RMB 20,000; if otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　违反本法规定，造成人身、财产损害的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 112** In violation of this Law, where personal or property damage is caused, civil liability shall be borne according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　本法中下列用语的含义：  
（一）重大传染病疫情，是指造成或者可能造成公众生命安全和身体健康严重损害的传染病疫情。  
（二）传染病患者、疑似患者，是指根据国务院卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门发布的传染病诊断标准，符合传染病患者、疑似患者诊断标准的人。  
（三）病原携带者，是指感染传染病病原体无临床症状但能排出病原体的人。  
（四）流行病学调查，是指对人群中疾病或者健康状况的分布及其决定因素进行调查研究，提出疾病预防、控制措施及保健对策。  
（五）人畜共患传染病，是指人与脊椎动物共同罹患的传染病，如鼠疫、狂犬病、血吸虫病、包虫病等。  
（六）自然疫源地，是指某些可引起人类传染病的病原体在自然界的野生动物中长期存在和循环的地区。  
（七）病媒生物，是指能够将传染病病原体从人或者其他动物传播给人的生物，如鼠、蚊、蝇、蚤类等。  
（八）医疗机构感染，是指在医疗机构内获得的感染，包括在医疗机构内发生的感染和在医疗机构内获得、离开医疗机构后发生的感染，但不包括进入医疗机构前已开始或者已处于潜伏期的感染。医疗机构工作人员在医疗机构内获得的感染也属医疗机构感染。  
（九）实验室感染，是指从事实验室工作时，因接触传染病病原体所致的感染。  
（十）消毒，是指用化学、物理、生物的方法杀灭或者消除环境中的病原微生物。  
（十一）疾病预防控制机构，是指从事疾病预防控制活动的疾病预防控制中心以及铁路疾病预防控制机构等与上述机构业务活动相同的单位。  
（十二）医疗机构，是指依法取得医疗机构执业许可证或者进行备案，从事疾病诊断、治疗活动的机构。  
（十三）暴发，是指在局部地区或者集体单位短时间内突然出现很多症状相同的患者。这些患者多有相同的传染源或者传播途径，大多数患者常同时出现在该病的最短和最长潜伏期之间。  
（十四）流行，是指在某地区某病的发病率显著超过该病历年发病率水平。

**Article 113** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) "Major infectious disease epidemic" means an infectious disease epidemic that causes or may cause serious damage to the life safety and health of the public.  
(2) "Infectious disease patient" or "suspected patient" means a person who meets the diagnostic criteria for an infectious disease patient or suspected patient according to the diagnostic standards for infectious diseases issued by the health administrative department and the disease prevention and control department of the State Council.  
(3) "Pathogen carrier" means a person who is infected with an infectious disease pathogen without clinical symptoms but can shed the pathogen.  
(4) "Epidemiological investigation" means the investigation and study of the distribution and determinants of disease or health conditions in a population, and the proposal of disease prevention and control measures and health care strategies.  
(5) "Zoonotic infectious disease" means an infectious disease shared by humans and vertebrates, such as plague, rabies, schistosomiasis, hydatid disease, etc.  
(6) "Natural epidemic focus" means an area where pathogens that can cause human infectious diseases persist and circulate in wild animals in nature.  
(7) "Vector" means an organism that can transmit infectious disease pathogens from humans or other animals to humans, such as rats, mosquitoes, flies, fleas, etc.  
(8) "Healthcare-associated infection" means an infection acquired within a medical institution, including infections that occur in the medical institution and infections that are acquired in the medical institution but occur after leaving the medical institution, but excluding infections that have already started or are in the incubation period before entering the medical institution. Infections acquired by medical institution staff within the medical institution are also healthcare-associated infections.  
(9) "Laboratory infection" means an infection caused by exposure to infectious disease pathogens during laboratory work.  
(10) "Disinfection" means the use of chemical, physical or biological methods to kill or eliminate pathogenic microorganisms in the environment.  
(11) "Disease prevention and control institution" means a center for disease prevention and control engaged in disease prevention and control activities, as well as units such as railway disease prevention and control institutions that have the same business activities as the above institutions.  
(12) "Medical institution" means an institution that has obtained a medical institution practice license or has been filed in accordance with law and engages in disease diagnosis and treatment activities.  
(13) "Outbreak" means the sudden appearance of many patients with the same symptoms in a local area or collective unit within a short period of time. These patients mostly have the same source of infection or route of transmission, and most patients often appear simultaneously between the shortest and longest incubation period of the disease.  
(14) "Epidemic" means that the incidence rate of a certain disease in a certain area significantly exceeds its annual incidence rate level in previous years.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　传染病防治中有关突发公共卫生事件的应对，本法未作规定的，适用有关突发公共卫生事件应对的法律、行政法规规定。

**Article 114** Where this Law does not provide for matters concerning the response to public health emergencies in the prevention and control of infectious diseases, the provisions of the laws and administrative regulations on the response to public health emergencies shall apply.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　本法自2025年9月1日起施行。

**Article 115** This Law shall come into force as of September 1, 2025.
