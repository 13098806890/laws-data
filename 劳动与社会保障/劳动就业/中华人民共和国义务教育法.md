# 中华人民共和国义务教育法

**法律部门**：社会法

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障适龄儿童、少年接受义务教育的权利，保证义务教育的实施，提高全民族素质，根据宪法和教育法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution and the Education Law for the purpose of ensuring the right of school-age children and adolescents to receive compulsory education, guaranteeing the implementation of compulsory education, and improving the quality of the entire nation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家实行九年义务教育制度。  
义务教育是国家统一实施的所有适龄儿童、少年必须接受的教育，是国家必须予以保障的公益性事业。  
实施义务教育，不收学费、杂费。  
国家建立义务教育经费保障机制，保证义务教育制度实施。

**Article 2** The State shall implement a system of nine-year compulsory education.  
Compulsory education is education which all school-age children and adolescents shall receive and which is uniformly implemented by the State, and is a public welfare undertaking which the State shall guarantee.  
No tuition or miscellaneous fees shall be charged for the implementation of compulsory education.  
The State shall establish a mechanism for guaranteeing funding for compulsory education to ensure the implementation of the compulsory education system.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　义务教育必须贯彻国家的教育方针，实施素质教育，提高教育质量，使适龄儿童、少年在品德、智力、体质等方面全面发展，为培养有理想、有道德、有文化、有纪律的社会主义建设者和接班人奠定基础。

**Article 3** Compulsory education must implement the State's educational policy, carry out quality education, improve the quality of education, enable school-age children and adolescents to develop comprehensively in morality, intelligence, physical fitness, etc., and lay the foundation for cultivating socialist builders and successors who are well-developed morally, intellectually, physically, and aesthetically, and who have ideals, morality, culture, and discipline.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　凡具有中华人民共和国国籍的适龄儿童、少年，不分性别、民族、种族、家庭财产状况、宗教信仰等，依法享有平等接受义务教育的权利，并履行接受义务教育的义务。

**Article 4** All school-age children and adolescents with nationality of the People's Republic of China shall, regardless of gender, ethnicity, race, family property status, religious belief, etc., enjoy the right to receive compulsory education equally according to law, and shall fulfill the obligation to receive compulsory education.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　各级人民政府及其有关部门应当履行本法规定的各项职责，保障适龄儿童、少年接受义务教育的权利。  
适龄儿童、少年的父母或者其他法定监护人应当依法保证其按时入学接受并完成义务教育。  
依法实施义务教育的学校应当按照规定标准完成教育教学任务，保证教育教学质量。  
社会组织和个人应当为适龄儿童、少年接受义务教育创造良好的环境。

**Article 5** People's governments at all levels and the relevant departments thereof shall perform the various duties prescribed by this Law and guarantee the right of school-age children and adolescents to receive compulsory education.  
Parents or other statutory guardians of school-age children and adolescents shall, according to law, ensure that they enter school on time to receive and complete compulsory education.  
Schools implementing compulsory education according to law shall complete the tasks of education and teaching in accordance with prescribed standards and guarantee the quality of education and teaching.  
Social organizations and individuals shall create a favorable environment for school-age children and adolescents to receive compulsory education.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院和县级以上地方人民政府应当合理配置教育资源，促进义务教育均衡发展，改善薄弱学校的办学条件，并采取措施，保障农村地区、民族地区实施义务教育，保障家庭经济困难的和残疾的适龄儿童、少年接受义务教育。  
国家组织和鼓励经济发达地区支援经济欠发达地区实施义务教育。

**Article 6** The State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall rationally allocate educational resources, promote balanced development of compulsory education, improve the conditions of weak schools, and take measures to guarantee the implementation of compulsory education in rural areas and ethnic minority areas, and guarantee that school-age children and adolescents from families with financial difficulties and those with disabilities receive compulsory education.  
The State shall organize and encourage economically developed areas to support economically underdeveloped areas in implementing compulsory education.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　义务教育实行国务院领导，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府统筹规划实施，县级人民政府为主管理的体制。  
县级以上人民政府教育行政部门具体负责义务教育实施工作；县级以上人民政府其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内负责义务教育实施工作。

**Article 7** Compulsory education shall implement a system under which the State Council provides leadership, the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government make overall plans for implementation, and the people's governments at the county level are primarily responsible for administration.  
The administrative departments for education of people's governments at or above the county level shall be specifically responsible for the implementation of compulsory education; other relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the implementation of compulsory education within the scope of their respective duties.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民政府教育督导机构对义务教育工作执行法律法规情况、教育教学质量以及义务教育均衡发展状况等进行督导，督导报告向社会公布。

**Article 8** The educational supervision institutions of the people's governments shall supervise the implementation of laws and regulations in compulsory education work, the quality of education and teaching, and the status of balanced development of compulsory education, and the supervision reports shall be published to the public.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何社会组织或者个人有权对违反本法的行为向有关国家机关提出检举或者控告。  
发生违反本法的重大事件，妨碍义务教育实施，造成重大社会影响的，负有领导责任的人民政府或者人民政府教育行政部门负责人应当引咎辞职。

**Article 9** Any social organization or individual shall have the right to report or accuse to the relevant State organs any conduct that violates this Law.  
If a major incident violating this Law occurs, hindering the implementation of compulsory education and causing a major social impact, the responsible persons of the people's government or the administrative department for education of the people's government bearing leadership responsibility shall resign to take the blame.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对在义务教育实施工作中做出突出贡献的社会组织和个人，各级人民政府及其有关部门按照有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 10** People's governments at all levels and the relevant departments thereof shall give commendations and rewards to social organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the implementation of compulsory education in accordance with relevant provisions.

### 第二章　学生

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　凡年满六周岁的儿童，其父母或者其他法定监护人应当送其入学接受并完成义务教育；条件不具备的地区的儿童，可以推迟到七周岁。  
适龄儿童、少年因身体状况需要延缓入学或者休学的，其父母或者其他法定监护人应当提出申请，由当地乡镇人民政府或者县级人民政府教育行政部门批准。

**Article 11** Parents or other statutory guardians of children who have reached the age of six shall send them to school to receive and complete compulsory education; children in areas where conditions are not available may be postponed to the age of seven.  
If school-age children or adolescents need to postpone enrollment or suspend schooling due to physical conditions, their parents or other statutory guardians shall submit an application, and the local township people's government or the administrative department for education of the people's government at the county level shall grant approval.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　适龄儿童、少年免试入学。地方各级人民政府应当保障适龄儿童、少年在户籍所在地学校就近入学。  
父母或者其他法定监护人在非户籍所在地工作或者居住的适龄儿童、少年，在其父母或者其他法定监护人工作或者居住地接受义务教育的，当地人民政府应当为其提供平等接受义务教育的条件。具体办法由省、自治区、直辖市规定。  
县级人民政府教育行政部门对本行政区域内的军人子女接受义务教育予以保障。

**Article 12** School-age children and adolescents shall enter school without examinations. Local people's governments at all levels shall guarantee that school-age children and adolescents attend schools near their registered residence.  
If school-age children or adolescents whose parents or other statutory guardians work or live in a place other than their registered residence receive compulsory education at the place where their parents or other statutory guardians work or live, the local people's government shall provide them with conditions for receiving compulsory education on an equal basis. Specific measures shall be prescribed by provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
The administrative department for education of the people's government at the county level shall guarantee that children of military personnel within its administrative region receive compulsory education.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级人民政府教育行政部门和乡镇人民政府组织和督促适龄儿童、少年入学，帮助解决适龄儿童、少年接受义务教育的困难，采取措施防止适龄儿童、少年辍学。  
居民委员会和村民委员会协助政府做好工作，督促适龄儿童、少年入学。

**Article 13** The administrative department for education of the people's government at the county level and the township people's governments shall organize and urge school-age children and adolescents to enter school, help solve difficulties of school-age children and adolescents in receiving compulsory education, and take measures to prevent school-age children and adolescents from dropping out.  
Residents' committees and villagers' committees shall assist the government in doing a good job and urge school-age children and adolescents to enter school.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　禁止用人单位招用应当接受义务教育的适龄儿童、少年。  
根据国家有关规定经批准招收适龄儿童、少年进行文艺、体育等专业训练的社会组织，应当保证所招收的适龄儿童、少年接受义务教育；自行实施义务教育的，应当经县级人民政府教育行政部门批准。

**Article 14** Employers shall be prohibited from recruiting school-age children or adolescents who shall receive compulsory education.  
Social organizations that recruit school-age children or adolescents for professional training in fields such as literature, art, or sports with approval in accordance with relevant State regulations shall guarantee that the recruited school-age children or adolescents receive compulsory education; if they implement compulsory education on their own, they shall obtain approval from the administrative department for education of the people's government at the county level.

### 第三章　学校

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级以上地方人民政府根据本行政区域内居住的适龄儿童、少年的数量和分布状况等因素，按照国家有关规定，制定、调整学校设置规划。新建居民区需要设置学校的，应当与居民区的建设同步进行。

**Article 15** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with relevant State regulations, formulate and adjust school establishment plans based on factors such as the number and distribution of school-age children and adolescents residing within their administrative regions. If a newly built residential area requires the establishment of a school, it shall be carried out simultaneously with the construction of the residential area.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　学校建设，应当符合国家规定的办学标准，适应教育教学需要；应当符合国家规定的选址要求和建设标准，确保学生和教职工安全。

**Article 16** The construction of schools shall comply with the school-running standards prescribed by the State and meet the needs of education and teaching; it shall comply with the site selection requirements and construction standards prescribed by the State to ensure the safety of students and teaching staff.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级人民政府根据需要设置寄宿制学校，保障居住分散的适龄儿童、少年入学接受义务教育。

**Article 17** People's governments at the county level shall, as needed, establish boarding schools to guarantee that school-age children and adolescents living in scattered areas enter school to receive compulsory education.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院教育行政部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据需要，在经济发达地区设置接收少数民族适龄儿童、少年的学校（班）。

**Article 18** The administrative department for education of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, as needed, establish schools (classes) in economically developed areas to receive school-age children and adolescents of ethnic minorities.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上地方人民政府根据需要设置相应的实施特殊教育的学校（班），对视力残疾、听力语言残疾和智力残疾的适龄儿童、少年实施义务教育。特殊教育学校（班）应当具备适应残疾儿童、少年学习、康复、生活特点的场所和设施。  
普通学校应当接收具有接受普通教育能力的残疾适龄儿童、少年随班就读，并为其学习、康复提供帮助。

**Article 19** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, as needed, establish corresponding special education schools (classes) to implement compulsory education for school-age children and adolescents with visual disabilities, hearing and speech disabilities, and intellectual disabilities. Special education schools (classes) shall have venues and facilities suitable for the learning, rehabilitation, and living characteristics of children and adolescents with disabilities.  
Ordinary schools shall admit school-age children and adolescents with disabilities who have the ability to receive ordinary education to study in regular classes, and shall provide assistance for their learning and rehabilitation.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级以上地方人民政府根据需要，为具有预防未成年人犯罪法规定的严重不良行为的适龄少年设置专门的学校实施义务教育。

**Article 20** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, as needed, establish specialized schools to implement compulsory education for school-age adolescents who have serious misbehavior as prescribed by the Law on the Prevention of Juvenile Delinquency.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对未完成义务教育的未成年犯和被采取强制性教育措施的未成年人应当进行义务教育，所需经费由人民政府予以保障。

**Article 21** Compulsory education shall be provided to juvenile offenders who have not completed compulsory education and minors subject to compulsory education measures, and the necessary funds shall be guaranteed by the people's government.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级以上人民政府及其教育行政部门应当促进学校均衡发展，缩小学校之间办学条件的差距，不得将学校分为重点学校和非重点学校。学校不得分设重点班和非重点班。  
县级以上人民政府及其教育行政部门不得以任何名义改变或者变相改变公办学校的性质。

**Article 22** People's governments at or above the county level and their administrative departments for education shall promote the balanced development of schools, narrow the gaps in school-running conditions between schools, and shall not divide schools into key schools and non-key schools. Schools shall not set up key classes and non-key classes.  
People's governments at or above the county level and their administrative departments for education shall not, in any name, change or covertly change the nature of public schools.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　各级人民政府及其有关部门依法维护学校周边秩序，保护学生、教师、学校的合法权益，为学校提供安全保障。

**Article 23** People's governments at all levels and the relevant departments thereof shall, according to law, maintain order around schools, protect the legitimate rights and interests of students, teachers, and schools, and provide security for schools.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　学校应当建立、健全安全制度和应急机制，对学生进行安全教育，加强管理，及时消除隐患，预防发生事故。  
县级以上地方人民政府定期对学校校舍安全进行检查；对需要维修、改造的，及时予以维修、改造。  
学校不得聘用曾经因故意犯罪被依法剥夺政治权利或者其他不适合从事义务教育工作的人担任工作人员。

**Article 24** Schools shall establish and improve safety systems and emergency mechanisms, conduct safety education for students, strengthen management, promptly eliminate hidden dangers, and prevent accidents.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall regularly inspect the safety of school buildings; for those requiring repair or renovation, they shall promptly carry out repair or renovation.  
Schools shall not employ persons who have been deprived of political rights according to law due to intentional crimes or who are otherwise unsuitable for working in compulsory education as staff members.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　学校不得违反国家规定收取费用，不得以向学生推销或者变相推销商品、服务等方式谋取利益。

**Article 25** Schools shall not charge fees in violation of State regulations, and shall not seek benefits by selling or covertly selling goods or services to students.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　学校实行校长负责制。校长应当符合国家规定的任职条件。校长由县级人民政府教育行政部门依法聘任。

**Article 26** Schools shall implement a system of principal responsibility. Principals shall meet the qualifications prescribed by the State. Principals shall be appointed according to law by the administrative department for education of the people's government at the county level.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对违反学校管理制度的学生，学校应当予以批评教育，不得开除。

**Article 27** Schools shall criticize and educate students who violate school management rules, and shall not expel them.

### 第四章　教师

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　教师享有法律规定的权利，履行法律规定的义务，应当为人师表，忠诚于人民的教育事业。  
全社会应当尊重教师。

**Article 28** Teachers shall enjoy the rights prescribed by law, perform the obligations prescribed by law, shall be exemplary in conduct and loyal to the people's educational cause.  
The whole society shall respect teachers.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　教师在教育教学中应当平等对待学生，关注学生的个体差异，因材施教，促进学生的充分发展。  
教师应当尊重学生的人格，不得歧视学生，不得对学生实施体罚、变相体罚或者其他侮辱人格尊严的行为，不得侵犯学生合法权益。

**Article 29** Teachers shall treat students equally in education and teaching, pay attention to individual differences among students, teach students in accordance with their aptitudes, and promote the full development of students.  
Teachers shall respect the personality of students, shall not discriminate against students, shall not impose corporal punishment, disguised corporal punishment, or other acts that insult human dignity on students, and shall not infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of students.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　教师应当取得国家规定的教师资格。  
国家建立统一的义务教育教师职务制度。教师职务分为初级职务、中级职务和高级职务。

**Article 30** Teachers shall obtain the teaching qualifications prescribed by the State.  
The State shall establish a unified system of professional titles for compulsory education teachers. Professional titles for teachers are divided into junior titles, intermediate titles, and senior titles.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　各级人民政府保障教师工资福利和社会保险待遇，改善教师工作和生活条件；完善农村教师工资经费保障机制。  
教师的平均工资水平应当不低于当地公务员的平均工资水平。  
特殊教育教师享有特殊岗位补助津贴。在民族地区和边远贫困地区工作的教师享有艰苦贫困地区补助津贴。

**Article 31** People's governments at all levels shall guarantee the wages, welfare, and social insurance benefits of teachers, improve the working and living conditions of teachers; and perfect the mechanism for guaranteeing salary funds for rural teachers.  
The average salary level of teachers shall not be lower than the average salary level of local civil servants.  
Teachers of special education shall enjoy subsidies for special posts. Teachers working in ethnic minority areas and remote poverty-stricken areas shall enjoy subsidies for hardship and poverty-stricken areas.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级以上人民政府应当加强教师培养工作，采取措施发展教师教育。  
县级人民政府教育行政部门应当均衡配置本行政区域内学校师资力量，组织校长、教师的培训和流动，加强对薄弱学校的建设。

**Article 32** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the training of teachers and take measures to develop teacher education.  
The administrative department for education of the people's government at the county level shall allocate the teaching staff of schools within its administrative region in a balanced manner, organize the training and rotation of principals and teachers, and strengthen the construction of weak schools.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院和地方各级人民政府鼓励和支持城市学校教师和高等学校毕业生到农村地区、民族地区从事义务教育工作。  
国家鼓励高等学校毕业生以志愿者的方式到农村地区、民族地区缺乏教师的学校任教。县级人民政府教育行政部门依法认定其教师资格，其任教时间计入工龄。

**Article 33** The State Council and local people's governments at all levels shall encourage and support teachers from urban schools and graduates of institutions of higher education to engage in compulsory education work in rural areas and ethnic minority areas.  
The State encourages graduates of institutions of higher education to teach in schools lacking teachers in rural areas and ethnic minority areas as volunteers. The administrative department for education of the people's government at the county level shall recognize their teaching qualifications according to law, and their teaching period shall be counted into the length of service.

### 第五章　教育教学

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　教育教学工作应当符合教育规律和学生身心发展特点，面向全体学生，教书育人，将德育、智育、体育、美育等有机统一在教育教学活动中，注重培养学生独立思考能力、创新能力和实践能力，促进学生全面发展。

**Article 34** Education and teaching work shall conform to the laws of education and the characteristics of students' physical and mental development, be oriented to all students, impart knowledge and educate people, organically integrate moral, intellectual, physical, and aesthetic education in education and teaching activities, focus on cultivating students' ability to think independently, innovation ability, and practical ability, and promote the comprehensive development of students.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国务院教育行政部门根据适龄儿童、少年身心发展的状况和实际情况，确定教学制度、教育教学内容和课程设置，改革考试制度，并改进高级中等学校招生办法，推进实施素质教育。  
学校和教师按照确定的教育教学内容和课程设置开展教育教学活动，保证达到国家规定的基本质量要求。  
国家鼓励学校和教师采用启发式教育等教育教学方法，提高教育教学质量。

**Article 35** The administrative department for education of the State Council shall, based on the physical and mental development status and actual conditions of school-age children and adolescents, determine the teaching system, education and teaching content, and curriculum setup, reform the examination system, and improve the enrollment methods of senior secondary schools, and promote the implementation of quality education.  
Schools and teachers shall carry out education and teaching activities in accordance with the determined education and teaching content and curriculum setup, and ensure that the basic quality requirements prescribed by the State are met.  
The State encourages schools and teachers to adopt teaching methods such as heuristic education to improve the quality of education and teaching.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　学校应当把德育放在首位，寓德育于教育教学之中，开展与学生年龄相适应的社会实践活动，形成学校、家庭、社会相互配合的思想道德教育体系，促进学生养成良好的思想品德和行为习惯。

**Article 36** Schools shall give priority to moral education, integrate moral education into education and teaching, carry out social practice activities appropriate to the age of students, form an ideological and moral education system with mutual cooperation among schools, families, and society, and promote students to develop good ideological character and behavioral habits.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　学校应当保证学生的课外活动时间，组织开展文化娱乐等课外活动。社会公共文化体育设施应当为学校开展课外活动提供便利。

**Article 37** Schools shall guarantee the time for students' extracurricular activities, and organize and carry out extracurricular activities such as cultural and recreational activities. Social public cultural and sports facilities shall provide convenience for schools to carry out extracurricular activities.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　教科书根据国家教育方针和课程标准编写，内容力求精简，精选必备的基础知识、基本技能，经济实用，保证质量。  
国家机关工作人员和教科书审查人员，不得参与或者变相参与教科书的编写工作。

**Article 38** Textbooks shall be compiled in accordance with the State's educational policy and curriculum standards, with content striving to be concise, selecting essential basic knowledge and basic skills, being economical and practical, and ensuring quality.  
State organ staff and textbook review personnel shall not participate or covertly participate in the compilation of textbooks.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家实行教科书审定制度。教科书的审定办法由国务院教育行政部门规定。  
未经审定的教科书，不得出版、选用。

**Article 39** The State shall implement a textbook approval system. The measures for textbook approval shall be prescribed by the administrative department for education of the State Council.  
Textbooks that have not been approved shall not be published or selected.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　教科书价格由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府价格行政部门会同同级出版主管部门按照微利原则确定。

**Article 40** The prices of textbooks shall be determined by the price administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government jointly with the publishing authorities at the same level in accordance with the principle of minimal profit.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家鼓励教科书循环使用。

**Article 41** The State encourages the recycling of textbooks.

### 第六章　经费保障

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家将义务教育全面纳入财政保障范围，义务教育经费由国务院和地方各级人民政府依照本法规定予以保障。  
国务院和地方各级人民政府将义务教育经费纳入财政预算，按照教职工编制标准、工资标准和学校建设标准、学生人均公用经费标准等，及时足额拨付义务教育经费，确保学校的正常运转和校舍安全，确保教职工工资按照规定发放。  
国务院和地方各级人民政府用于实施义务教育财政拨款的增长比例应当高于财政经常性收入的增长比例，保证按照在校学生人数平均的义务教育费用逐步增长，保证教职工工资和学生人均公用经费逐步增长。

**Article 42** The State shall comprehensively incorporate compulsory education into the scope of financial guarantee, and the funding for compulsory education shall be guaranteed by the State Council and local people's governments at all levels in accordance with the provisions of this Law.  
The State Council and local people's governments at all levels shall incorporate compulsory education funding into the fiscal budget, and allocate compulsory education funding in full and on time in accordance with the standards for the establishment of teaching staff, salary standards, school construction standards, and per-student public funding standards, so as to ensure the normal operation of schools and the safety of school buildings, and ensure that the salaries of teaching staff are paid in accordance with regulations.  
The growth rate of fiscal appropriations for compulsory education by the State Council and local people's governments at all levels shall be higher than the growth rate of regular fiscal revenue, ensure that the average compulsory education cost per enrolled student gradually increases, and ensure that the salaries of teaching staff and per-student public funding gradually increase.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　学校的学生人均公用经费基本标准由国务院财政部门会同教育行政部门制定，并根据经济和社会发展状况适时调整。制定、调整学生人均公用经费基本标准，应当满足教育教学基本需要。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本行政区域的实际情况，制定不低于国家标准的学校学生人均公用经费标准。  
特殊教育学校（班）学生人均公用经费标准应当高于普通学校学生人均公用经费标准。

**Article 43** The basic standards for per-student public funding for schools shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council jointly with the administrative department for education, and be adjusted in a timely manner based on the economic and social development situation. The formulation and adjustment of the basic standards for per-student public funding shall meet the basic needs of education and teaching.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on the actual conditions of their administrative regions, formulate per-student public funding standards for schools that are not lower than the national standards.  
The per-student public funding standards for special education schools (classes) shall be higher than the per-student public funding standards for ordinary schools.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　义务教育经费投入实行国务院和地方各级人民政府根据职责共同负担，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责统筹落实的体制。农村义务教育所需经费，由各级人民政府根据国务院的规定分项目、按比例分担。  
各级人民政府对家庭经济困难的适龄儿童、少年免费提供教科书并补助寄宿生生活费。  
义务教育经费保障的具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 44** The investment in compulsory education funding shall implement a system under which the State Council and local people's governments at all levels jointly bear the burden based on their respective duties, and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government are responsible for overall planning and implementation. The funding required for compulsory education in rural areas shall be shared by people's governments at all levels by item and proportion in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.  
People's governments at all levels shall provide free textbooks to school-age children and adolescents from families with financial difficulties and subsidize the living expenses of boarders.  
Specific measures for guaranteeing compulsory education funding shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　地方各级人民政府在财政预算中将义务教育经费单列。  
县级人民政府编制预算，除向农村地区学校和薄弱学校倾斜外，应当均衡安排义务教育经费。

**Article 45** Local people's governments at all levels shall list compulsory education funding separately in the fiscal budget.  
When preparing the budget, the people's government at the county level shall arrange compulsory education funding in a balanced manner, except for providing preferential treatment to schools in rural areas and weak schools.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规范财政转移支付制度，加大一般性转移支付规模和规范义务教育专项转移支付，支持和引导地方各级人民政府增加对义务教育的投入。地方各级人民政府确保将上级人民政府的义务教育转移支付资金按照规定用于义务教育。

**Article 46** The State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall regulate the fiscal transfer payment system, increase the scale of general transfer payments, standardize special transfer payments for compulsory education, and support and guide local people's governments at all levels to increase investment in compulsory education. Local people's governments at all levels shall ensure that the compulsory education transfer payment funds from higher-level people's governments are used for compulsory education in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国务院和县级以上地方人民政府根据实际需要，设立专项资金，扶持农村地区、民族地区实施义务教育。

**Article 47** The State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on actual needs, establish special funds to support the implementation of compulsory education in rural areas and ethnic minority areas.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家鼓励社会组织和个人向义务教育捐赠，鼓励按照国家有关基金会管理的规定设立义务教育基金。

**Article 48** The State encourages social organizations and individuals to donate to compulsory education, and encourages the establishment of compulsory education funds in accordance with the relevant State regulations on the management of foundations.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　义务教育经费严格按照预算规定用于义务教育；任何组织和个人不得侵占、挪用义务教育经费，不得向学校非法收取或者摊派费用。

**Article 49** Compulsory education funding shall be strictly used for compulsory education in accordance with budget provisions; no organization or individual may embezzle or misappropriate compulsory education funding, or illegally collect or apportion fees to schools.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　县级以上人民政府建立健全义务教育经费的审计监督和统计公告制度。

**Article 50** People's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve the audit supervision and statistical announcement system for compulsory education funding.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国务院有关部门和地方各级人民政府违反本法第六章的规定，未履行对义务教育经费保障职责的，由国务院或者上级地方人民政府责令限期改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分。

**Article 51** Where the relevant departments of the State Council and local people's governments at various levels violate the provisions of Chapter VI of this Law and fail to perform their duties of guaranteeing funds for compulsory education, the State Council or the local people's government at a higher level shall order them to make corrections within a specified time limit; if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　县级以上地方人民政府有下列情形之一的，由上级人民政府责令限期改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分：  
（一）未按照国家有关规定制定、调整学校的设置规划的；  
（二）学校建设不符合国家规定的办学标准、选址要求和建设标准的；  
（三）未定期对学校校舍安全进行检查，并及时维修、改造的；  
（四）未依照本法规定均衡安排义务教育经费的。

**Article 52** Where a local people's government at or above the county level is under any of the following circumstances, the people's government at a higher level shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to formulate or adjust the plan for the distribution of schools in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(2) constructing schools that do not meet the school-running standards, site selection requirements and construction standards prescribed by the State;  
(3) failing to regularly inspect the safety of school buildings and timely carry out repairs and renovations; or  
(4) failing to equitably allocate funds for compulsory education in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　县级以上人民政府或者其教育行政部门有下列情形之一的，由上级人民政府或者其教育行政部门责令限期改正、通报批评；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分：  
（一）将学校分为重点学校和非重点学校的；  
（二）改变或者变相改变公办学校性质的。  
县级人民政府教育行政部门或者乡镇人民政府未采取措施组织适龄儿童、少年入学或者防止辍学的，依照前款规定追究法律责任。

**Article 53** Where a people's government at or above the county level or its administrative department for education is under any of the following circumstances, the people's government at a higher level or its administrative department for education shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit and circulate a notice of criticism; if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law:  
(1) dividing schools into key schools and non-key schools; or  
(2) changing or altering in a disguised manner the nature of public schools.  
Where the administrative department for education of a people's government at the county level or the people's government of a township or town fails to take measures to organize school-age children and adolescents to attend school or prevent dropouts, legal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　有下列情形之一的，由上级人民政府或者上级人民政府教育行政部门、财政部门、价格行政部门和审计机关根据职责分工责令限期改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）侵占、挪用义务教育经费的；  
（二）向学校非法收取或者摊派费用的。

**Article 54** Where a person is under any of the following circumstances, the people's government at a higher level or the administrative department for education, finance department, price administrative department and audit authority of the people's government at a higher level shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order him/ her to make corrections within a specified time limit; if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) misappropriating or diverting funds for compulsory education; or  
(2) illegally collecting or apportioning fees to schools.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　学校或者教师在义务教育工作中违反教育法、教师法规定的，依照教育法、教师法的有关规定处罚。

**Article 55** Where a school or a teacher violates the provisions of the Education Law or the Teachers Law in the work of compulsory education, he/she shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Education Law and the Teachers Law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　学校违反国家规定收取费用的，由县级人民政府教育行政部门责令退还所收费用；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。  
学校以向学生推销或者变相推销商品、服务等方式谋取利益的，由县级人民政府教育行政部门给予通报批评；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。  
国家机关工作人员和教科书审查人员参与或者变相参与教科书编写的，由县级以上人民政府或者其教育行政部门根据职责权限责令限期改正，依法给予行政处分；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 56** Where a school collects fees in violation of the provisions of the State, the administrative department for education of the people's government at the county level shall order it to return the fees collected; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.  
Where a school seeks profits by promoting or promoting in a disguised manner goods or services to students, the administrative department for education of the people's government at the county level shall circulate a notice of criticism; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.  
Where a functionary of a State organ or a textbook reviewer participates or participates in a disguised manner in the compilation of textbooks, the people's government at or above the county level or its administrative department for education shall, according to its functions and powers, order him/her to make corrections within a specified time limit, and shall give administrative sanctions according to law; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　学校有下列情形之一的，由县级人民政府教育行政部门责令限期改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）拒绝接收具有接受普通教育能力的残疾适龄儿童、少年随班就读的；  
（二）分设重点班和非重点班的；  
（三）违反本法规定开除学生的；  
（四）选用未经审定的教科书的。

**Article 57** Where a school is under any of the following circumstances, the administrative department for education of the people's government at the county level shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) refusing to admit disabled school-age children and adolescents with the ability to receive regular education to study in regular classes;  
(2) setting up key classes and non-key classes separately;  
(3) expelling students in violation of the provisions of this Law; or  
(4) selecting textbooks that have not been examined and approved.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　适龄儿童、少年的父母或者其他法定监护人无正当理由未依照本法规定送适龄儿童、少年入学接受义务教育的，由当地乡镇人民政府或者县级人民政府教育行政部门给予批评教育，责令限期改正。

**Article 58** Where the parents or other statutory guardians of a school-age child or adolescent, without justifiable reasons, fail to send the school-age child or adolescent to school to receive compulsory education in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the local people's government of the township or town or the administrative department for education of the people's government at the county level shall give him/her criticism and education, and order him/her to make corrections within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　有下列情形之一的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚：  
（一）胁迫或者诱骗应当接受义务教育的适龄儿童、少年失学、辍学的；  
（二）非法招用应当接受义务教育的适龄儿童、少年的；  
（三）出版未经依法审定的教科书的。

**Article 59** Where a person is under any of the following circumstances, he/she shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations:  
(1) coercing or inducing school-age children or adolescents who shall receive compulsory education to drop out of school or become truants;  
(2) illegally employing school-age children or adolescents who shall receive compulsory education; or  
(3) publishing textbooks that have not been examined and approved according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 60** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　对接受义务教育的适龄儿童、少年不收杂费的实施步骤，由国务院规定。

**Article 61** The implementation steps for not charging miscellaneous fees for school-age children and adolescents receiving compulsory education shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　社会组织或者个人依法举办的民办学校实施义务教育的，依照民办教育促进法有关规定执行；民办教育促进法未作规定的，适用本法。

**Article 62** Where a private school established by a social organization or an individual in accordance with law provides compulsory education, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law on the Promotion of Private Education; matters not provided for in the Law on the Promotion of Private Education shall be governed by this Law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本法自2006年9月1日起施行。

**Article 63** This Law shall come into force as of September 1, 2006.
