### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强安全生产工作，防止和减少生产安全事故，保障人民群众生命和财产安全，促进经济社会持续健康发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purposes of strengthening work safety, preventing and reducing work safety accidents, ensuring the safety of life and property of the people, and promoting sustained and healthy economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域内从事生产经营活动的单位（以下统称生产经营单位）的安全生产，适用本法；有关法律、行政法规对消防安全和道路交通安全、铁路交通安全、水上交通安全、民用航空安全以及核与辐射安全、特种设备安全另有规定的，适用其规定。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the work safety of entities engaged in production and business activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter collectively referred to as production and business entities); where other laws and administrative regulations provide otherwise on fire safety, road traffic safety, railway traffic safety, water traffic safety, civil aviation safety, nuclear and radiation safety, and special equipment safety, those provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　安全生产工作坚持中国共产党的领导。  
安全生产工作应当以人为本，坚持人民至上、生命至上，把保护人民生命安全摆在首位，树牢安全发展理念，坚持安全第一、预防为主、综合治理的方针，从源头上防范化解重大安全风险。  
安全生产工作实行管行业必须管安全、管业务必须管安全、管生产经营必须管安全，强化和落实生产经营单位主体责任与政府监管责任，建立生产经营单位负责、职工参与、政府监管、行业自律和社会监督的机制。

**Article 3** Work safety shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China.  
Work safety shall be people-oriented, uphold the supremacy of the people and life, prioritize the protection of people's life safety, firmly establish the concept of safe development, adhere to the policy of safety first, prevention foremost, and comprehensive management, and prevent and defuse major safety risks at the source.  
Work safety shall implement the principle that those in charge of an industry must be responsible for safety, those in charge of business operations must be responsible for safety, and those in charge of production and business must be responsible for safety, strengthen and implement the primary responsibility of production and business entities and the government supervision responsibility, and establish a mechanism of production and business entities' accountability, employee participation, government supervision, industry self-discipline, and social oversight.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　生产经营单位必须遵守本法和其他有关安全生产的法律、法规，加强安全生产管理，建立健全全员安全生产责任制和安全生产规章制度，加大对安全生产资金、物资、技术、人员的投入保障力度，改善安全生产条件，加强安全生产标准化、信息化建设，构建安全风险分级管控和隐患排查治理双重预防机制，健全风险防范化解机制，提高安全生产水平，确保安全生产。  
平台经济等新兴行业、领域的生产经营单位应当根据本行业、领域的特点，建立健全并落实全员安全生产责任制，加强从业人员安全生产教育和培训，履行本法和其他法律、法规规定的有关安全生产义务。

**Article 4** Production and business entities must comply with this Law and other laws and regulations on work safety, strengthen work safety management, establish and improve the work safety responsibility system for all employees and the work safety rules and regulations, increase the input and guarantee of funds, materials, technology and personnel for work safety, improve work safety conditions, strengthen the standardization and informatization of work safety, establish a dual prevention mechanism of safety risk classification management and hazard investigation and control, improve the risk prevention and resolution mechanism, enhance work safety level, and ensure work safety.  
Production and business entities in emerging industries and fields such as the platform economy shall, according to the characteristics of their industries and fields, establish, improve and implement the work safety responsibility system for all employees, strengthen work safety education and training for employees, and fulfill the work safety obligations stipulated in this Law and other laws and regulations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　生产经营单位的主要负责人是本单位安全生产第一责任人，对本单位的安全生产工作全面负责。其他负责人对职责范围内的安全生产工作负责。

**Article 5** The principal person in charge of a production and business entity shall be the primary person responsible for work safety of the entity and shall assume full responsibility for the work safety of the entity. Other persons in charge shall be responsible for work safety within the scope of their duties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　生产经营单位的从业人员有依法获得安全生产保障的权利，并应当依法履行安全生产方面的义务。

**Article 6** Employees of production and business entities shall have the right to obtain work safety protection according to law, and shall perform their work safety obligations according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　工会依法对安全生产工作进行监督。  
生产经营单位的工会依法组织职工参加本单位安全生产工作的民主管理和民主监督，维护职工在安全生产方面的合法权益。生产经营单位制定或者修改有关安全生产的规章制度，应当听取工会的意见。

**Article 7** Trade unions shall supervise work safety according to law.  
Trade unions of production and business entities shall, according to law, organize employees to participate in democratic management and democratic supervision of the entity's work safety and protect the legitimate rights and interests of employees in work safety. When a production and business entity formulates or revises rules and regulations on work safety, it shall solicit the opinions of the trade union.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院和县级以上地方各级人民政府应当根据国民经济和社会发展规划制定安全生产规划，并组织实施。安全生产规划应当与国土空间规划等相关规划相衔接。  
各级人民政府应当加强安全生产基础设施建设和安全生产监管能力建设，所需经费列入本级预算。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府应当组织有关部门建立完善安全风险评估与论证机制，按照安全风险管控要求，进行产业规划和空间布局，并对位置相邻、行业相近、业态相似的生产经营单位实施重大安全风险联防联控。

**Article 8** The State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall formulate work safety plans based on the national economic and social development plans and organize their implementation. Work safety plans shall be coordinated with relevant plans such as territorial space plans.  
People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the construction of work safety infrastructure and work safety supervision capacity, and the required expenses shall be included in the budgets of the respective levels.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize relevant departments to establish and improve a safety risk assessment and demonstration mechanism, carry out industrial planning and spatial layout in accordance with safety risk control requirements, and implement joint prevention and control of major safety risks for production and business entities that are adjacent in location, similar in industry, or similar in business type.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院和县级以上地方各级人民政府应当加强对安全生产工作的领导，建立健全安全生产工作协调机制，支持、督促各有关部门依法履行安全生产监督管理职责，及时协调、解决安全生产监督管理中存在的重大问题。  
乡镇人民政府和街道办事处，以及开发区、工业园区、港区、风景区等应当明确负责安全生产监督管理的有关工作机构及其职责，加强安全生产监管力量建设，按照职责对本行政区域或者管理区域内生产经营单位安全生产状况进行监督检查，协助人民政府有关部门或者按照授权依法履行安全生产监督管理职责。

**Article 9** The State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen leadership over work safety, establish and improve a coordination mechanism for work safety, support and supervise relevant departments in performing their work safety supervision and management duties according to law, and promptly coordinate and resolve major problems in work safety supervision and management.  
Township people's governments and sub-district offices, as well as development zones, industrial parks, port areas, scenic areas, etc., shall designate the work institutions responsible for work safety supervision and management and their duties, strengthen the construction of work safety supervision forces, conduct supervision and inspection of the work safety conditions of production and business entities within their administrative or management areas according to their duties, and assist the relevant departments of the people's governments or perform work safety supervision and management duties according to law under authorization.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院应急管理部门依照本法，对全国安全生产工作实施综合监督管理；县级以上地方各级人民政府应急管理部门依照本法，对本行政区域内安全生产工作实施综合监督管理。  
国务院交通运输、住房和城乡建设、水利、民航等有关部门依照本法和其他有关法律、行政法规的规定，在各自的职责范围内对有关行业、领域的安全生产工作实施监督管理；县级以上地方各级人民政府有关部门依照本法和其他有关法律、法规的规定，在各自的职责范围内对有关行业、领域的安全生产工作实施监督管理。对新兴行业、领域的安全生产监督管理职责不明确的，由县级以上地方各级人民政府按照业务相近的原则确定监督管理部门。  
应急管理部门和对有关行业、领域的安全生产工作实施监督管理的部门，统称负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门。负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当相互配合、齐抓共管、信息共享、资源共用，依法加强安全生产监督管理工作。

**Article 10** The emergency management department of the State Council shall, in accordance with this Law, exercise comprehensive supervision and management over work safety nationwide; the emergency management departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with this Law, exercise comprehensive supervision and management over work safety within their respective administrative areas.  
The relevant departments of the State Council, such as those for transport, housing and urban-rural development, water resources, and civil aviation, shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other relevant laws and administrative regulations, exercise supervision and management over work safety in relevant industries and fields within their respective duties; the relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other relevant laws and regulations, exercise supervision and management over work safety in relevant industries and fields within their respective duties. Where the duties of work safety supervision and management for emerging industries and fields are not clearly defined, the local people's governments at or above the county level shall determine the supervision and management department based on the principle of business similarity.  
The emergency management departments and the departments that exercise supervision and management over work safety in relevant industries and fields are collectively referred to as the departments with work safety supervision and management duties. The departments with work safety supervision and management duties shall cooperate with each other, jointly manage, share information, and share resources, and strengthen work safety supervision and management according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院有关部门应当按照保障安全生产的要求，依法及时制定有关的国家标准或者行业标准，并根据科技进步和经济发展适时修订。  
生产经营单位必须执行依法制定的保障安全生产的国家标准或者行业标准。

**Article 11** The relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with the requirements for ensuring work safety, formulate relevant national standards or industrial standards in a timely manner according to law, and revise them as appropriate based on scientific and technological progress and economic development.  
Production and business entities must implement the national standards or industrial standards formulated according to law to ensure work safety.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院有关部门按照职责分工负责安全生产强制性国家标准的项目提出、组织起草、征求意见、技术审查。国务院应急管理部门统筹提出安全生产强制性国家标准的立项计划。国务院标准化行政主管部门负责安全生产强制性国家标准的立项、编号、对外通报和授权批准发布工作。国务院标准化行政主管部门、有关部门依据法定职责对安全生产强制性国家标准的实施进行监督检查。

**Article 12** The relevant departments of the State Council shall, according to their division of duties, be responsible for the proposal, drafting, solicitation of comments, and technical review of compulsory national standards for work safety. The emergency management department of the State Council shall coordinate and propose the project plan for compulsory national standards for work safety. The standardization administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the project approval, numbering, external notification, and authorized approval and publication of compulsory national standards for work safety. The standardization administrative department of the State Council and relevant departments shall conduct supervision and inspection over the implementation of compulsory national standards for work safety according to their statutory duties.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　各级人民政府及其有关部门应当采取多种形式，加强对有关安全生产的法律、法规和安全生产知识的宣传，增强全社会的安全生产意识。

**Article 13** People's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall adopt various forms to strengthen publicity of laws and regulations related to work safety and work safety knowledge, so as to enhance the work safety awareness of the whole society.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　有关协会组织依照法律、行政法规和章程，为生产经营单位提供安全生产方面的信息、培训等服务，发挥自律作用，促进生产经营单位加强安全生产管理。

**Article 14** Relevant trade associations and organizations shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and their articles of association, provide services such as information and training on work safety to production and business entities, play a self-disciplinary role, and promote production and business entities to strengthen work safety management.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　依法设立的为安全生产提供技术、管理服务的机构，依照法律、行政法规和执业准则，接受生产经营单位的委托为其安全生产工作提供技术、管理服务。  
生产经营单位委托前款规定的机构提供安全生产技术、管理服务的，保证安全生产的责任仍由本单位负责。

**Article 15** Institutions established according to law to provide technical and management services for work safety shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and professional standards, accept entrustment from production and business entities to provide technical and management services for their work safety.  
Where a production and business entity entrusts an institution as specified in the preceding paragraph to provide work safety technical and management services, the responsibility for ensuring work safety shall remain with the entity itself.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家实行生产安全事故责任追究制度，依照本法和有关法律、法规的规定，追究生产安全事故责任单位和责任人员的法律责任。

**Article 16** The State shall implement a system of accountability for work safety accidents, and shall investigate the legal liability of the units and persons responsible for work safety accidents in accordance with the provisions of this Law and relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上各级人民政府应当组织负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门依法编制安全生产权力和责任清单，公开并接受社会监督。

**Article 17** People's governments at or above the county level shall organize the departments with work safety supervision and management duties to compile a list of work safety powers and responsibilities according to law, make it public, and subject it to social supervision.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家鼓励和支持安全生产科学技术研究和安全生产先进技术的推广应用，提高安全生产水平。

**Article 18** The State encourages and supports scientific and technological research on work safety and the promotion and application of advanced work safety technologies to improve work safety standards.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家对在改善安全生产条件、防止生产安全事故、参加抢险救护等方面取得显著成绩的单位和个人，给予奖励。

**Article 19** The State shall give awards to units and individuals that have achieved notable successes in improving work safety conditions, preventing work safety accidents, or participating in emergency rescue and relief.

### 第二章　生产经营单位的安全生产保障

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　生产经营单位应当具备本法和有关法律、行政法规和国家标准或者行业标准规定的安全生产条件；不具备安全生产条件的，不得从事生产经营活动。

**Article 20** A production and business entity shall meet the work safety conditions stipulated in this Law, relevant laws, administrative regulations, and national or industrial standards; if it does not meet the work safety conditions, it shall not engage in production and business activities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　生产经营单位的主要负责人对本单位安全生产工作负有下列职责：  
（一）建立健全并落实本单位全员安全生产责任制，加强安全生产标准化建设；  
（二）组织制定并实施本单位安全生产规章制度和操作规程；  
（三）组织制定并实施本单位安全生产教育和培训计划；  
（四）保证本单位安全生产投入的有效实施；  
（五）组织建立并落实安全风险分级管控和隐患排查治理双重预防工作机制，督促、检查本单位的安全生产工作，及时消除生产安全事故隐患；  
（六）组织制定并实施本单位的生产安全事故应急救援预案；  
（七）及时、如实报告生产安全事故。

**Article 21** The principal person in charge of a production and business entity shall have the following duties with respect to the work safety of the entity:  
(1) establishing, improving and implementing the work safety responsibility system for all employees of the entity, and strengthening the standardization of work safety;  
(2) organizing the formulation and implementation of the entity's work safety rules and regulations and operating procedures;  
(3) organizing the formulation and implementation of the entity's work safety education and training plans;  
(4) ensuring the effective implementation of the entity's work safety investment;  
(5) organizing the establishment and implementation of a dual prevention mechanism for safety risk classification management and hazard investigation and control, supervising and inspecting the entity's work safety, and promptly eliminating potential work safety accident hazards;  
(6) organizing the formulation and implementation of the entity's emergency rescue plan for work safety accidents; and  
(7) promptly and truthfully reporting work safety accidents.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　生产经营单位的全员安全生产责任制应当明确各岗位的责任人员、责任范围和考核标准等内容。  
生产经营单位应当建立相应的机制，加强对全员安全生产责任制落实情况的监督考核，保证全员安全生产责任制的落实。

**Article 22** The work safety responsibility system for all employees of a production and business entity shall specify the persons responsible for each post, the scope of responsibility, and the assessment criteria, among other matters.  
A production and business entity shall establish a corresponding mechanism to strengthen the supervision and assessment of the implementation of the work safety responsibility system for all employees, so as to ensure the implementation of the work safety responsibility system for all employees.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　生产经营单位应当具备的安全生产条件所必需的资金投入，由生产经营单位的决策机构、主要负责人或者个人经营的投资人予以保证，并对由于安全生产所必需的资金投入不足导致的后果承担责任。  
有关生产经营单位应当按照规定提取和使用安全生产费用，专门用于改善安全生产条件。安全生产费用在成本中据实列支。安全生产费用提取、使用和监督管理的具体办法由国务院财政部门会同国务院应急管理部门征求国务院有关部门意见后制定。

**Article 23** The funds necessary for the work safety conditions that a production and business entity shall meet shall be guaranteed by the decision-making body, the principal person in charge, or the individual business investor of the entity, who shall bear liability for the consequences resulting from insufficient investment in the funds necessary for work safety.  
Relevant production and business entities shall, in accordance with regulations, set aside and use work safety expenses specifically for improving work safety conditions. Work safety expenses shall be itemized as actual expenditures in costs. The specific measures for the setting aside, use, supervision and management of work safety expenses shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council jointly with the emergency management department of the State Council after soliciting opinions from the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　矿山、金属冶炼、建筑施工、运输单位和危险物品的生产、经营、储存、装卸单位，应当设置安全生产管理机构或者配备专职安全生产管理人员。  
前款规定以外的其他生产经营单位，从业人员超过一百人的，应当设置安全生产管理机构或者配备专职安全生产管理人员；从业人员在一百人以下的，应当配备专职或者兼职的安全生产管理人员。

**Article 24** Entities engaged in mining, metal smelting, building construction, transport, and the production, operation, storage, and loading and unloading of hazardous substances shall set up work safety management organizations or be equipped with full-time work safety management personnel.  
For other production and business entities not specified in the preceding paragraph, if they have more than one hundred employees, they shall set up work safety management organizations or be equipped with full-time work safety management personnel; if they have one hundred or fewer employees, they shall be equipped with full-time or part-time work safety management personnel.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　生产经营单位的安全生产管理机构以及安全生产管理人员履行下列职责：  
（一）组织或者参与拟订本单位安全生产规章制度、操作规程和生产安全事故应急救援预案；  
（二）组织或者参与本单位安全生产教育和培训，如实记录安全生产教育和培训情况；  
（三）组织开展危险源辨识和评估，督促落实本单位重大危险源的安全管理措施；  
（四）组织或者参与本单位应急救援演练；  
（五）检查本单位的安全生产状况，及时排查生产安全事故隐患，提出改进安全生产管理的建议；  
（六）制止和纠正违章指挥、强令冒险作业、违反操作规程的行为；  
（七）督促落实本单位安全生产整改措施。  
生产经营单位可以设置专职安全生产分管负责人，协助本单位主要负责人履行安全生产管理职责。

**Article 25** The work safety management organization and work safety management personnel of a production and business entity shall perform the following duties:  
(1) organizing or participating in the drafting of the entity's work safety rules and regulations, operating procedures, and emergency rescue plans for work safety accidents;  
(2) organizing or participating in the entity's work safety education and training, and truthfully recording the education and training;  
(3) organizing the identification and assessment of hazard sources, and supervising the implementation of safety management measures for major hazard sources of the entity;  
(4) organizing or participating in the entity's emergency rescue drills;  
(5) inspecting the entity's work safety conditions, promptly investigating potential work safety accident hazards, and proposing suggestions for improving work safety management;  
(6) stopping and correcting acts of giving commands in violation of regulations, ordering risky operations, or violating operating procedures; and  
(7) supervising the implementation of the entity's work safety rectification measures.  
A production and business entity may designate a full-time deputy person in charge of work safety to assist the principal person in charge in performing the work safety management duties.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　生产经营单位的安全生产管理机构以及安全生产管理人员应当恪尽职守，依法履行职责。  
生产经营单位作出涉及安全生产的经营决策，应当听取安全生产管理机构以及安全生产管理人员的意见。  
生产经营单位不得因安全生产管理人员依法履行职责而降低其工资、福利等待遇或者解除与其订立的劳动合同。  
危险物品的生产、储存单位以及矿山、金属冶炼单位的安全生产管理人员的任免，应当告知主管的负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门。

**Article 26** The work safety management organization and work safety management personnel of a production and business entity shall perform their duties diligently and in accordance with law.  
When a production and business entity makes business decisions involving work safety, it shall solicit opinions from its work safety management organization and work safety management personnel.  
A production and business entity shall not lower the wages, benefits or other treatment of its work safety management personnel or terminate the labor contract concluded with them because they have performed their duties according to law.  
The appointment and removal of work safety management personnel in entities producing and storing hazardous substances, as well as in mining and metal smelting entities, shall be reported to the competent department with work safety supervision and management duties.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　生产经营单位的主要负责人和安全生产管理人员必须具备与本单位所从事的生产经营活动相应的安全生产知识和管理能力。  
危险物品的生产、经营、储存、装卸单位以及矿山、金属冶炼、建筑施工、运输单位的主要负责人和安全生产管理人员，应当由主管的负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门对其安全生产知识和管理能力考核合格。考核不得收费。  
危险物品的生产、储存、装卸单位以及矿山、金属冶炼单位应当有注册安全工程师从事安全生产管理工作。鼓励其他生产经营单位聘用注册安全工程师从事安全生产管理工作。注册安全工程师按专业分类管理，具体办法由国务院人力资源和社会保障部门、国务院应急管理部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 27** The principal person in charge and the work safety management personnel of a production and business entity shall possess the work safety knowledge and management capabilities corresponding to the production and business activities in which the entity is engaged.  
The principal person in charge and the work safety management personnel of entities producing, operating, storing, loading and unloading hazardous substances, as well as entities in mining, metal smelting, building construction, and transport, shall be assessed as qualified in work safety knowledge and management capabilities by the competent department with work safety supervision and management duties. No fees shall be charged for the assessment.  
Entities producing, storing, loading and unloading hazardous substances, as well as mining and metal smelting entities, shall have certified safety engineers engaged in work safety management. Other production and business entities are encouraged to employ certified safety engineers to engage in work safety management. Certified safety engineers shall be managed by professional classification, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the human resources and social security department of the State Council and the emergency management department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　生产经营单位应当对从业人员进行安全生产教育和培训，保证从业人员具备必要的安全生产知识，熟悉有关的安全生产规章制度和安全操作规程，掌握本岗位的安全操作技能，了解事故应急处理措施，知悉自身在安全生产方面的权利和义务。未经安全生产教育和培训合格的从业人员，不得上岗作业。  
生产经营单位使用被派遣劳动者的，应当将被派遣劳动者纳入本单位从业人员统一管理，对被派遣劳动者进行岗位安全操作规程和安全操作技能的教育和培训。劳务派遣单位应当对被派遣劳动者进行必要的安全生产教育和培训。  
生产经营单位接收中等职业学校、高等学校学生实习的，应当对实习学生进行相应的安全生产教育和培训，提供必要的劳动防护用品。学校应当协助生产经营单位对实习学生进行安全生产教育和培训。  
生产经营单位应当建立安全生产教育和培训档案，如实记录安全生产教育和培训的时间、内容、参加人员以及考核结果等情况。

**Article 28** A production and business entity shall provide work safety education and training to its employees, ensure that employees possess the necessary work safety knowledge, are familiar with the relevant work safety rules and regulations and safe operating procedures, master the safe operation skills for their posts, understand the emergency response measures, and are aware of their rights and obligations in work safety. No employee who has not passed the work safety education and training shall be assigned to work.  
Where a production and business entity uses dispatched workers, it shall incorporate the dispatched workers into the unified management of its employees and provide them with education and training on post safety operating procedures and safe operation skills. The labor dispatch unit shall provide the dispatched workers with necessary work safety education and training.  
Where a production and business entity accepts students from secondary vocational schools or institutions of higher education for internships, it shall provide the intern students with corresponding work safety education and training and provide necessary labor protection articles. The school shall assist the production and business entity in providing work safety education and training to the intern students.  
A production and business entity shall establish archives of work safety education and training, and truthfully record the time, content, participants, and assessment results of the work safety education and training.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　生产经营单位采用新工艺、新技术、新材料或者使用新设备，必须了解、掌握其安全技术特性，采取有效的安全防护措施，并对从业人员进行专门的安全生产教育和培训。

**Article 29** Where a production and business entity adopts new processes, new technologies, new materials, or uses new equipment, it must understand and master their safety technical characteristics, adopt effective safety protection measures, and provide specialized work safety education and training to its employees.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　生产经营单位的特种作业人员必须按照国家有关规定经专门的安全作业培训，取得相应资格，方可上岗作业。  
特种作业人员的范围由国务院应急管理部门会同国务院有关部门确定。

**Article 30** Special operations personnel of a production and business entity must, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, undergo special safety operation training and obtain the corresponding qualifications before they may be assigned to work.  
The scope of special operations personnel shall be determined by the emergency management department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　生产经营单位新建、改建、扩建工程项目（以下统称建设项目）的安全设施，必须与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投入生产和使用。安全设施投资应当纳入建设项目概算。

**Article 31** The safety facilities of new construction, renovation, and expansion projects of a production and business entity (hereinafter collectively referred to as construction projects) must be designed, constructed, and put into production and use simultaneously with the main project. The investment in safety facilities shall be included in the budget estimate of the construction project.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　矿山、金属冶炼建设项目和用于生产、储存、装卸危险物品的建设项目，应当按照国家有关规定进行安全评价。

**Article 32** Construction projects for mining, metal smelting, and construction projects for the production, storage, and loading and unloading of hazardous substances shall undergo safety assessment in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　建设项目安全设施的设计人、设计单位应当对安全设施设计负责。  
矿山、金属冶炼建设项目和用于生产、储存、装卸危险物品的建设项目的安全设施设计应当按照国家有关规定报经有关部门审查，审查部门及其负责审查的人员对审查结果负责。

**Article 33** The designer and design unit of the safety facilities of a construction project shall be responsible for the design of the safety facilities.  
The design of safety facilities for construction projects for mining, metal smelting, and construction projects for the production, storage, and loading and unloading of hazardous substances shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, be submitted to the relevant departments for review, and the reviewing department and the persons responsible for the review shall be responsible for the review results.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　矿山、金属冶炼建设项目和用于生产、储存、装卸危险物品的建设项目的施工单位必须按照批准的安全设施设计施工，并对安全设施的工程质量负责。  
矿山、金属冶炼建设项目和用于生产、储存、装卸危险物品的建设项目竣工投入生产或者使用前，应当由建设单位负责组织对安全设施进行验收；验收合格后，方可投入生产和使用。负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当加强对建设单位验收活动和验收结果的监督核查。

**Article 34** The construction unit of construction projects for mining, metal smelting, and construction projects for the production, storage, and loading and unloading of hazardous substances must construct in accordance with the approved safety facility design and be responsible for the engineering quality of the safety facilities.  
Before a construction project for mining, metal smelting, or for the production, storage, and loading and unloading of hazardous substances is completed and put into production or use, the construction entity shall be responsible for organizing the acceptance of the safety facilities; only after the acceptance is passed may it be put into production or use. The departments with work safety supervision and management duties shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of the acceptance activities and results of the construction entities.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　生产经营单位应当在有较大危险因素的生产经营场所和有关设施、设备上，设置明显的安全警示标志。

**Article 35** A production and business entity shall set up conspicuous safety warning signs in production and business sites and on relevant facilities and equipment where there are major hazardous factors.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　安全设备的设计、制造、安装、使用、检测、维修、改造和报废，应当符合国家标准或者行业标准。  
生产经营单位必须对安全设备进行经常性维护、保养，并定期检测，保证正常运转。维护、保养、检测应当作好记录，并由有关人员签字。  
生产经营单位不得关闭、破坏直接关系生产安全的监控、报警、防护、救生设备、设施，或者篡改、隐瞒、销毁其相关数据、信息。  
餐饮等行业的生产经营单位使用燃气的，应当安装可燃气体报警装置，并保障其正常使用。

**Article 36** The design, manufacture, installation, use, testing, maintenance, modification, and scrapping of safety equipment shall comply with national standards or industrial standards.  
A production and business entity must carry out routine maintenance and servicing of safety equipment and conduct periodic testing to ensure normal operation. Records of maintenance, servicing, and testing shall be made and signed by the relevant personnel.  
A production and business entity shall not turn off, damage, or destroy monitoring, alarm, protection, and lifesaving equipment and facilities that directly affect production safety, nor shall it tamper with, conceal, or destroy the related data or information.  
Where a production and business entity in the catering or other industries uses gas, it shall install a combustible gas alarm device and ensure its normal use.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　生产经营单位使用的危险物品的容器、运输工具，以及涉及人身安全、危险性较大的海洋石油开采特种设备和矿山井下特种设备，必须按照国家有关规定，由专业生产单位生产，并经具有专业资质的检测、检验机构检测、检验合格，取得安全使用证或者安全标志，方可投入使用。检测、检验机构对检测、检验结果负责。

**Article 37** Containers and means of transport for hazardous substances used by production and business entities, as well as special equipment for offshore oil exploitation and underground mining equipment that involve personal safety and have high risks, must, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, be manufactured by specialized production units, and after passing the inspection and testing by an inspection and testing institution with specialized qualifications, obtain a safety use certificate or safety mark before they may be put into use. The inspection and testing institution shall be responsible for the inspection and testing results.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家对严重危及生产安全的工艺、设备实行淘汰制度，具体目录由国务院应急管理部门会同国务院有关部门制定并公布。法律、行政法规对目录的制定另有规定的，适用其规定。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本地区实际情况制定并公布具体目录，对前款规定以外的危及生产安全的工艺、设备予以淘汰。  
生产经营单位不得使用应当淘汰的危及生产安全的工艺、设备。

**Article 38** The State implements a phasing-out system for processes and equipment that seriously endanger production safety. The specific catalogue shall be formulated and published by the emergency management department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council. Where laws or administrative regulations otherwise provide for the formulation of the catalogue, those provisions shall apply.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on local conditions, formulate and publish specific catalogues to phase out processes and equipment that endanger production safety but are not covered by the preceding paragraph.  
A production and business entity shall not use processes and equipment that endanger production safety and should be phased out.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　生产、经营、运输、储存、使用危险物品或者处置废弃危险物品的，由有关主管部门依照有关法律、法规的规定和国家标准或者行业标准审批并实施监督管理。  
生产经营单位生产、经营、运输、储存、使用危险物品或者处置废弃危险物品，必须执行有关法律、法规和国家标准或者行业标准，建立专门的安全管理制度，采取可靠的安全措施，接受有关主管部门依法实施的监督管理。

**Article 39** The production, operation, transport, storage, and use of hazardous substances or the disposal of waste hazardous substances shall be subject to approval and supervision by the relevant competent departments in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations and national or industrial standards.  
A production and business entity that produces, operates, transports, stores, uses hazardous substances or disposes of waste hazardous substances must implement the relevant laws, regulations, and national or industrial standards, establish a specialized safety management system, adopt reliable safety measures, and accept the supervision and management carried out by the relevant competent departments according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　生产经营单位对重大危险源应当登记建档，进行定期检测、评估、监控，并制定应急预案，告知从业人员和相关人员在紧急情况下应当采取的应急措施。  
生产经营单位应当按照国家有关规定将本单位重大危险源及有关安全措施、应急措施报有关地方人民政府应急管理部门和有关部门备案。有关地方人民政府应急管理部门和有关部门应当通过相关信息系统实现信息共享。

**Article 40** A production and business entity shall register and file major hazard sources, conduct periodic testing, assessment, and monitoring, formulate emergency response plans, and inform employees and relevant persons of the emergency measures to be taken in an emergency.  
A production and business entity shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, report its major hazard sources and related safety measures and emergency measures to the emergency management department of the relevant local people's government and relevant departments for record. The emergency management department of the relevant local people's government and the relevant departments shall achieve information sharing through relevant information systems.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　生产经营单位应当建立安全风险分级管控制度，按照安全风险分级采取相应的管控措施。  
生产经营单位应当建立健全并落实生产安全事故隐患排查治理制度，采取技术、管理措施，及时发现并消除事故隐患。事故隐患排查治理情况应当如实记录，并通过职工大会或者职工代表大会、信息公示栏等方式向从业人员通报。其中，重大事故隐患排查治理情况应当及时向负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门和职工大会或者职工代表大会报告。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当将重大事故隐患纳入相关信息系统，建立健全重大事故隐患治理督办制度，督促生产经营单位消除重大事故隐患。

**Article 41** A production and business entity shall establish a safety risk classification management system and adopt corresponding control measures according to the safety risk classification.  
A production and business entity shall establish, improve and implement a system for the investigation and control of potential work safety accident hazards, adopt technical and management measures to promptly discover and eliminate accident hazards. The investigation and control of potential accident hazards shall be truthfully recorded and communicated to employees through staff meetings or staff representative meetings, information bulletin boards, or other means. Among these, the investigation and control of major accident hazards shall be promptly reported to the departments with work safety supervision and management duties and to the staff meeting or staff representative meeting.  
The departments with work safety supervision and management duties of local people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate major accident hazards into relevant information systems, establish and improve a supervisory system for the control of major accident hazards, and supervise production and business entities in eliminating major accident hazards.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　生产、经营、储存、使用危险物品的车间、商店、仓库不得与员工宿舍在同一座建筑物内，并应当与员工宿舍保持安全距离。  
生产经营场所和员工宿舍应当设有符合紧急疏散要求、标志明显、保持畅通的出口、疏散通道。禁止占用、锁闭、封堵生产经营场所或者员工宿舍的出口、疏散通道。

**Article 42** Workshops, stores, and warehouses that produce, operate, store, or use hazardous substances shall not be located in the same building as employee dormitories and shall maintain a safe distance from employee dormitories.  
Production and business sites and employee dormitories shall be equipped with exits and evacuation routes that meet the requirements for emergency evacuation, are clearly marked, and remain unobstructed. It is prohibited to occupy, lock, or block exits and evacuation routes of production and business sites or employee dormitories.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　生产经营单位进行爆破、吊装、动火、临时用电以及国务院应急管理部门会同国务院有关部门规定的其他危险作业，应当安排专门人员进行现场安全管理，确保操作规程的遵守和安全措施的落实。

**Article 43** Where a production and business entity carries out blasting, hoisting, hot work, temporary electricity use, or other hazardous operations specified by the emergency management department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, it shall arrange special personnel to conduct on-site safety management to ensure compliance with operating procedures and implementation of safety measures.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　生产经营单位应当教育和督促从业人员严格执行本单位的安全生产规章制度和安全操作规程；并向从业人员如实告知作业场所和工作岗位存在的危险因素、防范措施以及事故应急措施。  
生产经营单位应当关注从业人员的身体、心理状况和行为习惯，加强对从业人员的心理疏导、精神慰藉，严格落实岗位安全生产责任，防范从业人员行为异常导致事故发生。

**Article 44** A production and business entity shall educate and urge its employees to strictly implement the entity's work safety rules and regulations and safe operating procedures; and shall truthfully inform employees of the hazardous factors, preventive measures, and emergency response measures existing in the workplace and work positions.  
A production and business entity shall pay attention to the physical and psychological conditions and behavioral habits of its employees, strengthen psychological counseling and mental comfort for employees, strictly implement post work safety responsibilities, and prevent accidents caused by abnormal behavior of employees.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　生产经营单位必须为从业人员提供符合国家标准或者行业标准的劳动防护用品，并监督、教育从业人员按照使用规则佩戴、使用。

**Article 45** A production and business entity must provide employees with labor protection articles that conform to national standards or industrial standards, and supervise and educate employees in wearing and using them in accordance with the usage rules.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　生产经营单位的安全生产管理人员应当根据本单位的生产经营特点，对安全生产状况进行经常性检查；对检查中发现的安全问题，应当立即处理；不能处理的，应当及时报告本单位有关负责人，有关负责人应当及时处理。检查及处理情况应当如实记录在案。  
生产经营单位的安全生产管理人员在检查中发现重大事故隐患，依照前款规定向本单位有关负责人报告，有关负责人不及时处理的，安全生产管理人员可以向主管的负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门报告，接到报告的部门应当依法及时处理。

**Article 46** The work safety management personnel of a production and business entity shall, based on the entity's production and business characteristics, conduct regular inspections of the work safety status; safety problems discovered during inspections shall be immediately addressed; if they cannot be addressed, they shall be promptly reported to the relevant person in charge of the entity, who shall promptly address them. The inspections and handling shall be truthfully recorded.  
Where a work safety management person of a production and business entity discovers a major accident hazard during an inspection and reports it to the relevant person in charge of the entity in accordance with the preceding paragraph, and the relevant person in charge fails to address it in a timely manner, the work safety management person may report it to the competent department with work safety supervision and management duties, and the department that receives the report shall handle it promptly according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　生产经营单位应当安排用于配备劳动防护用品、进行安全生产培训的经费。

**Article 47** A production and business entity shall allocate funds for the procurement of labor protection articles and for conducting work safety training.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　两个以上生产经营单位在同一作业区域内进行生产经营活动，可能危及对方生产安全的，应当签订安全生产管理协议，明确各自的安全生产管理职责和应当采取的安全措施，并指定专职安全生产管理人员进行安全检查与协调。

**Article 48** Where two or more production and business entities conduct production and business activities in the same operation area that may endanger each other's production safety, they shall sign a work safety management agreement specifying their respective work safety management duties and the safety measures to be taken, and designate full-time work safety management personnel to conduct safety inspections and coordination.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　生产经营单位不得将生产经营项目、场所、设备发包或者出租给不具备安全生产条件或者相应资质的单位或者个人。  
生产经营项目、场所发包或者出租给其他单位的，生产经营单位应当与承包单位、承租单位签订专门的安全生产管理协议，或者在承包合同、租赁合同中约定各自的安全生产管理职责；生产经营单位对承包单位、承租单位的安全生产工作统一协调、管理，定期进行安全检查，发现安全问题的，应当及时督促整改。  
矿山、金属冶炼建设项目和用于生产、储存、装卸危险物品的建设项目的施工单位应当加强对施工项目的安全管理，不得倒卖、出租、出借、挂靠或者以其他形式非法转让施工资质，不得将其承包的全部建设工程转包给第三人或者将其承包的全部建设工程支解以后以分包的名义分别转包给第三人，不得将工程分包给不具备相应资质条件的单位。

**Article 49** A production and business entity shall not contract out or lease its production and business projects, sites, or equipment to units or individuals that do not have the work safety conditions or corresponding qualifications.  
Where production and business projects or sites are contracted out or leased to other entities, the production and business entity shall enter into a special work safety management agreement with the contractor or lessee, or stipulate their respective work safety management duties in the contract or lease agreement; the production and business entity shall coordinate and manage the work safety of the contractor and lessee in a unified manner, conduct regular safety inspections, and promptly supervise the rectification of safety problems found.  
The construction unit of construction projects for mining, metal smelting, and for the production, storage, and loading and unloading of hazardous substances shall strengthen the safety management of the construction project, and shall not resell, lease, lend, affiliate, or illegally transfer construction qualifications in any other form, shall not subcontract the entire contracted construction project to a third party or split the entire contracted construction project and subcontract it in parts to third parties in the name of subcontracting, and shall not subcontract the project to units that do not have the corresponding qualifications.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　生产经营单位发生生产安全事故时，单位的主要负责人应当立即组织抢救，并不得在事故调查处理期间擅离职守。

**Article 50** When a work safety accident occurs in a production and business entity, the principal person in charge of the entity shall immediately organize rescue and shall not leave his or her post without authorization during the investigation and handling of the accident.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　生产经营单位必须依法参加工伤保险，为从业人员缴纳保险费。  
国家鼓励生产经营单位投保安全生产责任保险；属于国家规定的高危行业、领域的生产经营单位，应当投保安全生产责任保险。具体范围和实施办法由国务院应急管理部门会同国务院财政部门、国务院保险监督管理机构和相关行业主管部门制定。

**Article 51** A production and business operation entity must participate in work-related injury insurance according to law and pay insurance premiums for its employees.  
The State encourages production and business operation entities to purchase work safety liability insurance; production and business operation entities in high-risk industries or sectors as prescribed by the State shall purchase work safety liability insurance. The specific scope and implementation measures shall be formulated by the emergency management department of the State Council jointly with the finance department of the State Council, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, and the relevant competent industry departments.

### 第三章　从业人员的安全生产权利义务

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　生产经营单位与从业人员订立的劳动合同，应当载明有关保障从业人员劳动安全、防止职业危害的事项，以及依法为从业人员办理工伤保险的事项。  
生产经营单位不得以任何形式与从业人员订立协议，免除或者减轻其对从业人员因生产安全事故伤亡依法应承担的责任。

**Article 52** A labor contract concluded between a production and business operation entity and an employee shall include matters concerning ensuring the employee's occupational safety, preventing occupational hazards, and handling work-related injury insurance for the employee according to law.  
A production and business operation entity shall not conclude any agreement with an employee in any form to waive or reduce its liability that it shall bear according to law for casualties of the employee caused by a production safety accident.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　生产经营单位的从业人员有权了解其作业场所和工作岗位存在的危险因素、防范措施及事故应急措施，有权对本单位的安全生产工作提出建议。

**Article 53** An employee of a production and business operation entity shall have the right to be informed of the hazardous factors, preventive measures, and emergency response measures existing at his or her workplace and work post, and shall have the right to make suggestions regarding the work safety of the entity.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　从业人员有权对本单位安全生产工作中存在的问题提出批评、检举、控告；有权拒绝违章指挥和强令冒险作业。  
生产经营单位不得因从业人员对本单位安全生产工作提出批评、检举、控告或者拒绝违章指挥、强令冒险作业而降低其工资、福利等待遇或者解除与其订立的劳动合同。

**Article 54** An employee shall have the right to criticize, report, and accuse problems existing in the work safety of his or her entity; and shall have the right to refuse to comply with orders that violate regulations or to be forced to work under hazardous conditions.  
A production and business operation entity shall not reduce the employee's wages, benefits or other treatments, or terminate the labor contract concluded with the employee on the grounds that the employee has criticized, reported, or accused problems regarding the entity's work safety, or refused to comply with orders that violate regulations or to be forced to work under hazardous conditions.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　从业人员发现直接危及人身安全的紧急情况时，有权停止作业或者在采取可能的应急措施后撤离作业场所。  
生产经营单位不得因从业人员在前款紧急情况下停止作业或者采取紧急撤离措施而降低其工资、福利等待遇或者解除与其订立的劳动合同。

**Article 55** When an employee discovers an emergency situation that directly endangers personal safety, he or she shall have the right to stop work or evacuate the workplace after taking possible emergency measures.  
A production and business operation entity shall not reduce the employee's wages, benefits or other treatments, or terminate the labor contract concluded with the employee on the grounds that the employee has stopped work or taken emergency evacuation measures under the emergency situation specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　生产经营单位发生生产安全事故后，应当及时采取措施救治有关人员。  
因生产安全事故受到损害的从业人员，除依法享有工伤保险外，依照有关民事法律尚有获得赔偿的权利的，有权提出赔偿要求。

**Article 56** After a production safety accident occurs in a production and business operation entity, it shall promptly take measures to provide medical treatment to the relevant personnel.  
An employee who suffers damage due to a production safety accident shall, in addition to enjoying work-related injury insurance according to law, have the right to claim compensation if he or she still has the right to obtain compensation under relevant civil laws.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　从业人员在作业过程中，应当严格落实岗位安全责任，遵守本单位的安全生产规章制度和操作规程，服从管理，正确佩戴和使用劳动防护用品。

**Article 57** An employee shall, in the course of performing work, strictly implement post safety responsibilities, comply with the work safety rules, regulations and operating procedures of the entity, obey management, and correctly wear and use labor protection supplies.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　从业人员应当接受安全生产教育和培训，掌握本职工作所需的安全生产知识，提高安全生产技能，增强事故预防和应急处理能力。

**Article 58** An employee shall receive work safety education and training, master the work safety knowledge necessary for his or her job, improve work safety skills, and enhance the capability of accident prevention and emergency response.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　从业人员发现事故隐患或者其他不安全因素，应当立即向现场安全生产管理人员或者本单位负责人报告；接到报告的人员应当及时予以处理。

**Article 59** When an employee discovers a potential accident hazard or any other unsafe factor, he or she shall immediately report it to the on-site work safety manager or the person in charge of the entity; the person receiving the report shall promptly handle it.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　工会有权对建设项目的安全设施与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投入生产和使用进行监督，提出意见。  
工会对生产经营单位违反安全生产法律、法规，侵犯从业人员合法权益的行为，有权要求纠正；发现生产经营单位违章指挥、强令冒险作业或者发现事故隐患时，有权提出解决的建议，生产经营单位应当及时研究答复；发现危及从业人员生命安全的情况时，有权向生产经营单位建议组织从业人员撤离危险场所，生产经营单位必须立即作出处理。  
工会有权依法参加事故调查，向有关部门提出处理意见，并要求追究有关人员的责任。

**Article 60** A trade union shall have the right to supervise and provide opinions on the simultaneous design, construction, and commissioning for production and use of safety facilities with the main project of a construction project.  
A trade union shall have the right to demand correction of any act of a production and business operation entity that violates work safety laws or regulations and infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of employees; when discovering that the production and business operation entity issues orders that violate regulations, forces employees to work under hazardous conditions, or discovers potential accident hazards, it shall have the right to propose solutions, and the production and business operation entity shall promptly study and respond; when discovering a situation that endangers the life safety of employees, it shall have the right to suggest to the production and business operation entity that the employees be evacuated from the dangerous place, and the production and business operation entity shall immediately take action.  
A trade union shall have the right to participate in accident investigations according to law, to provide opinions regarding the handling to the relevant departments, and to demand that the responsible persons be held accountable.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　生产经营单位使用被派遣劳动者的，被派遣劳动者享有本法规定的从业人员的权利，并应当履行本法规定的从业人员的义务。

**Article 61** Where a production and business operation entity uses dispatched workers, such dispatched workers shall enjoy the rights of employees provided for in this Law and shall perform the obligations of employees provided for in this Law.

### 第四章　安全生产的监督管理

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当根据本行政区域内的安全生产状况，组织有关部门按照职责分工，对本行政区域内容易发生重大生产安全事故的生产经营单位进行严格检查。  
应急管理部门应当按照分类分级监督管理的要求，制定安全生产年度监督检查计划，并按照年度监督检查计划进行监督检查，发现事故隐患，应当及时处理。

**Article 62** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the work safety situation within their respective administrative regions, organize relevant departments to conduct strict inspections of production and business operation entities that are prone to major production safety accidents within their administrative regions, in accordance with their respective responsibilities.  
The emergency management department shall, in accordance with the requirements of categorized and tiered supervision and administration, formulate an annual work safety supervision and inspection plan, and conduct supervision and inspection in accordance with the annual plan; if a potential accident hazard is discovered, it shall be promptly dealt with.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门依照有关法律、法规的规定，对涉及安全生产的事项需要审查批准（包括批准、核准、许可、注册、认证、颁发证照等，下同）或者验收的，必须严格依照有关法律、法规和国家标准或者行业标准规定的安全生产条件和程序进行审查；不符合有关法律、法规和国家标准或者行业标准规定的安全生产条件的，不得批准或者验收通过。对未依法取得批准或者验收合格的单位擅自从事有关活动的，负责行政审批的部门发现或者接到举报后应当立即予以取缔，并依法予以处理。对已经依法取得批准的单位，负责行政审批的部门发现其不再具备安全生产条件的，应当撤销原批准。

**Article 63** A department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, strictly review items related to work safety that require examination and approval (including approval, verification, licensing, registration, certification, issuance of licenses, etc., hereinafter the same) or acceptance, in accordance with the work safety conditions and procedures prescribed by relevant laws, regulations and national standards or industry standards; those failing to meet the work safety conditions prescribed by relevant laws, regulations and national standards or industry standards shall not be approved or accepted. Where an entity that has not obtained approval or passed acceptance according to law engages in relevant activities without authorization, the department responsible for administrative examination and approval, upon discovery or receipt of a report, shall immediately ban it and deal with it according to law. Where an entity that has obtained approval according to law is found no longer to meet the work safety conditions, the department responsible for administrative examination and approval shall revoke the original approval.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门对涉及安全生产的事项进行审查、验收，不得收取费用；不得要求接受审查、验收的单位购买其指定品牌或者指定生产、销售单位的安全设备、器材或者其他产品。

**Article 64** A department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration shall not charge any fees when conducting examination or acceptance of items related to work safety; it shall not require the entity undergoing examination or acceptance to purchase safety equipment, apparatus or other products of a specified brand or from a specified manufacturer or seller.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　应急管理部门和其他负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门依法开展安全生产行政执法工作，对生产经营单位执行有关安全生产的法律、法规和国家标准或者行业标准的情况进行监督检查，行使以下职权：  
（一）进入生产经营单位进行检查，调阅有关资料，向有关单位和人员了解情况；  
（二）对检查中发现的安全生产违法行为，当场予以纠正或者要求限期改正；对依法应当给予行政处罚的行为，依照本法和其他有关法律、行政法规的规定作出行政处罚决定；  
（三）对检查中发现的事故隐患，应当责令立即排除；重大事故隐患排除前或者排除过程中无法保证安全的，应当责令从危险区域内撤出作业人员，责令暂时停产停业或者停止使用相关设施、设备；重大事故隐患排除后，经审查同意，方可恢复生产经营和使用；  
（四）对有根据认为不符合保障安全生产的国家标准或者行业标准的设施、设备、器材以及违法生产、储存、使用、经营、运输的危险物品予以查封或者扣押，对违法生产、储存、使用、经营危险物品的作业场所予以查封，并依法作出处理决定。  
监督检查不得影响被检查单位的正常生产经营活动。

**Article 65** The emergency management department and other departments with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration shall, according to law, carry out work safety administrative law enforcement, supervise and inspect the implementation by production and business operation entities of relevant work safety laws, regulations and national standards or industry standards, and exercise the following powers:  
(1) Enter a production and business operation entity for inspection, access relevant materials, and inquire of relevant entities and persons about the situation;  
(2) Terminate on the spot or order correction within a time limit any work safety violation discovered during an inspection; make a decision on administrative penalty for acts that shall be subject to administrative penalty according to law, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other relevant laws and administrative regulations;  
(3) Order immediate elimination of any potential accident hazard discovered during an inspection; where a major potential accident hazard cannot be eliminated or safety cannot be ensured during the elimination process, order the evacuation of workers from the hazardous area, order temporary suspension of production and business operations or suspension of use of relevant facilities or equipment; after the major potential accident hazard is eliminated and upon examination and approval, the entity may resume production, business operations, and use;  
(4) Seal up or seize facilities, equipment, or apparatus that are found to be not in conformity with national standards or industry standards for ensuring work safety, as well as dangerous articles that are illegally produced, stored, used, handled, or transported; seal up workplaces where dangerous articles are illegally produced, stored, used, or handled, and make a decision on handling according to law.  
Supervision and inspection shall not affect the normal production and business operations of the entity under inspection.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　生产经营单位对负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门的监督检查人员（以下统称安全生产监督检查人员）依法履行监督检查职责，应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻挠。

**Article 66** A production and business operation entity shall cooperate with the inspectors of the department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration (hereinafter collectively referred to as work safety supervision and inspection personnel) when they lawfully perform their supervision and inspection duties, and shall not refuse or obstruct them.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　安全生产监督检查人员应当忠于职守，坚持原则，秉公执法。  
安全生产监督检查人员执行监督检查任务时，必须出示有效的行政执法证件；对涉及被检查单位的技术秘密和业务秘密，应当为其保密。

**Article 67** Work safety supervision and inspection personnel shall be faithful to their duties, adhere to principles, and enforce the law impartially.  
When performing supervision and inspection tasks, work safety supervision and inspection personnel must present valid administrative law enforcement credentials; they shall keep confidential any technical secrets and business secrets of the entity under inspection.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　安全生产监督检查人员应当将检查的时间、地点、内容、发现的问题及其处理情况，作出书面记录，并由检查人员和被检查单位的负责人签字；被检查单位的负责人拒绝签字的，检查人员应当将情况记录在案，并向负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门报告。

**Article 68** Work safety supervision and inspection personnel shall make written records of the time, place, content, problems discovered, and their handling during an inspection, which shall be signed by the inspectors and the person in charge of the entity under inspection; if the person in charge of the entity under inspection refuses to sign, the inspectors shall record the situation and report it to the department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门在监督检查中，应当互相配合，实行联合检查；确需分别进行检查的，应当互通情况，发现存在的安全问题应当由其他有关部门进行处理的，应当及时移送其他有关部门并形成记录备查，接受移送的部门应当及时进行处理。

**Article 69** Departments with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration shall cooperate with each other in supervision and inspection and conduct joint inspections; where separate inspections are truly necessary, they shall exchange information, and where a safety problem is discovered that should be handled by another relevant department, they shall promptly transfer the matter to that other department and keep a record for future reference; the department receiving the transfer shall promptly handle it.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门依法对存在重大事故隐患的生产经营单位作出停产停业、停止施工、停止使用相关设施或者设备的决定，生产经营单位应当依法执行，及时消除事故隐患。生产经营单位拒不执行，有发生生产安全事故的现实危险的，在保证安全的前提下，经本部门主要负责人批准，负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门可以采取通知有关单位停止供电、停止供应民用爆炸物品等措施，强制生产经营单位履行决定。通知应当采用书面形式，有关单位应当予以配合。  
负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门依照前款规定采取停止供电措施，除有危及生产安全的紧急情形外，应当提前二十四小时通知生产经营单位。生产经营单位依法履行行政决定、采取相应措施消除事故隐患的，负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当及时解除前款规定的措施。

**Article 70** Where a department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration, according to law, makes a decision to suspend production and business operations, suspend construction, or suspend the use of relevant facilities or equipment of a production and business operation entity with a major potential accident hazard, the entity shall comply with the decision according to law and promptly eliminate the potential accident hazard. If the production and business operation entity refuses to comply and there is a real risk of a production safety accident, the department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration may, with the approval of its principal responsible person and on the premise of ensuring safety, take measures such as notifying the relevant entity to cut off the power supply or stop the supply of civil explosives, to compel the production and business operation entity to perform the decision. The notice shall be in writing, and the relevant entity shall cooperate.  
Where a department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration takes measures to cut off the power supply in accordance with the preceding paragraph, it shall give the production and business operation entity 24 hours' notice in advance, except in emergency situations endangering production safety. Where the production and business operation entity performs the administrative decision according to law and takes corresponding measures to eliminate the potential accident hazard, the department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration shall promptly terminate the measures specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　监察机关依照监察法的规定，对负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门及其工作人员履行安全生产监督管理职责实施监察。

**Article 71** The supervisory organs shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Supervision Law, supervise the departments with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration and their staff members in the performance of their duties of work safety supervision and administration.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　承担安全评价、认证、检测、检验职责的机构应当具备国家规定的资质条件，并对其作出的安全评价、认证、检测、检验结果的合法性、真实性负责。资质条件由国务院应急管理部门会同国务院有关部门制定。  
承担安全评价、认证、检测、检验职责的机构应当建立并实施服务公开和报告公开制度，不得租借资质、挂靠、出具虚假报告。

**Article 72** An institution responsible for work safety assessment, certification, testing, or inspection shall meet the qualification conditions prescribed by the State and shall be responsible for the legality and authenticity of the results of its work safety assessments, certifications, tests, or inspections. The qualification conditions shall be formulated by the emergency management department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.  
An institution responsible for work safety assessment, certification, testing, or inspection shall establish and implement a system of openness regarding its services and reports, and shall not rent out or lend its qualifications, engage in affiliation, or issue false reports.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当建立举报制度，公开举报电话、信箱或者电子邮件地址等网络举报平台，受理有关安全生产的举报；受理的举报事项经调查核实后，应当形成书面材料；需要落实整改措施的，报经有关负责人签字并督促落实。对不属于本部门职责，需要由其他有关部门进行调查处理的，转交其他有关部门处理。  
涉及人员死亡的举报事项，应当由县级以上人民政府组织核查处理。

**Article 73** A department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration shall establish a reporting system, make public the reporting telephone number, mailbox, or email address, and other online reporting platforms, and accept reports on work safety; after investigation and verification of the matters reported, it shall form written materials; where corrective measures need to be implemented, it shall submit them to the relevant responsible person for signature and supervise their implementation. Where a reported matter is not within the department's functions and needs to be investigated and handled by another relevant department, it shall be transferred to that department for handling.  
Where a report involves the death of a person, the people's government at or above the county level shall organize verification and handling.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　任何单位或者个人对事故隐患或者安全生产违法行为，均有权向负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门报告或者举报。  
因安全生产违法行为造成重大事故隐患或者导致重大事故，致使国家利益或者社会公共利益受到侵害的，人民检察院可以根据民事诉讼法、行政诉讼法的相关规定提起公益诉讼。

**Article 74** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report or tip off potential accident hazards or work safety violations to the department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration.  
Where a work safety violation causes a major potential accident hazard or leads to a major accident, thereby harming the national interest or the public interest, the People's Procuratorate may initiate a public interest lawsuit in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law and the Administrative Procedure Law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　居民委员会、村民委员会发现其所在区域内的生产经营单位存在事故隐患或者安全生产违法行为时，应当向当地人民政府或者有关部门报告。

**Article 75** Where a residents' committee or a villagers' committee discovers that a production and business operation entity within its area has a potential accident hazard or a work safety violation, it shall report to the local people's government or the relevant department.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　县级以上各级人民政府及其有关部门对报告重大事故隐患或者举报安全生产违法行为的有功人员，给予奖励。具体奖励办法由国务院应急管理部门会同国务院财政部门制定。

**Article 76** People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall reward persons who have rendered meritorious service in reporting major potential accident hazards or tipping off work safety violations. The specific reward measures shall be formulated by the emergency management department of the State Council jointly with the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　新闻、出版、广播、电影、电视等单位有进行安全生产公益宣传教育的义务，有对违反安全生产法律、法规的行为进行舆论监督的权利。

**Article 77** Entities such as news, publishing, broadcasting, film, and television shall have the obligation to conduct public benefit publicity and education on work safety, and shall have the right to exercise public opinion supervision over acts violating work safety laws and regulations.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当建立安全生产违法行为信息库，如实记录生产经营单位及其有关从业人员的安全生产违法行为信息；对违法行为情节严重的生产经营单位及其有关从业人员，应当及时向社会公告，并通报行业主管部门、投资主管部门、自然资源主管部门、生态环境主管部门、证券监督管理机构以及有关金融机构。有关部门和机构应当对存在失信行为的生产经营单位及其有关从业人员采取加大执法检查频次、暂停项目审批、上调有关保险费率、行业或者职业禁入等联合惩戒措施，并向社会公示。  
负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当加强对生产经营单位行政处罚信息的及时归集、共享、应用和公开，对生产经营单位作出处罚决定后七个工作日内在监督管理部门公示系统予以公开曝光，强化对违法失信生产经营单位及其有关从业人员的社会监督，提高全社会安全生产诚信水平。

**Article 78** A department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration shall establish a database of work safety violation information, truthfully record the work safety violation information of production and business operation entities and their relevant employees; where a production and business operation entity or its relevant employee commits a serious violation, the department shall promptly announce it to the public and notify the competent industry department, the investment department, the natural resources department, the ecological environment department, the securities regulatory authority, and the relevant financial institutions. The relevant departments and institutions shall take joint disciplinary measures against the dishonest production and business operation entities and their relevant employees, such as increasing the frequency of law enforcement inspections, suspending project approval, raising relevant insurance premium rates, and imposing industry or occupational bans, and shall make such information public.  
A department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration shall strengthen the timely collection, sharing, application, and disclosure of administrative penalty information on production and business operation entities, and shall publicly expose such information on the supervision and administration department's disclosure system within seven working days after making a penalty decision against a production and business operation entity, so as to strengthen social supervision over dishonest production and business operation entities and their relevant employees and enhance the level of work safety integrity in the whole society.

### 第五章　生产安全事故的应急救援与调查处理

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　国家加强生产安全事故应急能力建设，在重点行业、领域建立应急救援基地和应急救援队伍，并由国家安全生产应急救援机构统一协调指挥；鼓励生产经营单位和其他社会力量建立应急救援队伍，配备相应的应急救援装备和物资，提高应急救援的专业化水平。  
国务院应急管理部门牵头建立全国统一的生产安全事故应急救援信息系统，国务院交通运输、住房和城乡建设、水利、民航等有关部门和县级以上地方人民政府建立健全相关行业、领域、地区的生产安全事故应急救援信息系统，实现互联互通、信息共享，通过推行网上安全信息采集、安全监管和监测预警，提升监管的精准化、智能化水平。

**Article 79** The State shall strengthen the construction of emergency response capacity for production safety accidents, establish emergency rescue bases and emergency rescue teams in key industries and sectors, which shall be uniformly coordinated and directed by the national work safety emergency rescue agency; it shall encourage production and business operation entities and other social forces to establish emergency rescue teams and equip them with corresponding emergency rescue equipment and materials, so as to enhance the professional level of emergency rescue.  
The emergency management department of the State Council shall take the lead in establishing a unified national production safety accident emergency rescue information system; the relevant departments of the State Council, such as those for transport, housing and urban-rural development, water resources, and civil aviation, and local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve production safety accident emergency rescue information systems for relevant industries, sectors, and regions, so as to achieve interconnection and information sharing; by promoting online collection of safety information, safety supervision, and monitoring and early warning, the precision and intelligence of supervision shall be improved.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当组织有关部门制定本行政区域内生产安全事故应急救援预案，建立应急救援体系。  
乡镇人民政府和街道办事处，以及开发区、工业园区、港区、风景区等应当制定相应的生产安全事故应急救援预案，协助人民政府有关部门或者按照授权依法履行生产安全事故应急救援工作职责。

**Article 80** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize relevant departments to formulate emergency rescue plans for production safety accidents within their respective administrative regions and establish an emergency rescue system.  
Township people's governments and subdistrict offices, as well as development zones, industrial parks, port areas, scenic spots, etc., shall formulate corresponding emergency rescue plans for production safety accidents, and assist the relevant departments of the people's government or perform the duties of emergency rescue for production safety accidents according to law under authorization.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　生产经营单位应当制定本单位生产安全事故应急救援预案，与所在地县级以上地方人民政府组织制定的生产安全事故应急救援预案相衔接，并定期组织演练。

**Article 81** A production and business operation entity shall formulate its own emergency rescue plan for production safety accidents, ensure its compatibility with the emergency rescue plan for production safety accidents formulated by the local people's government at or above the county level, and regularly organize drills.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　危险物品的生产、经营、储存单位以及矿山、金属冶炼、城市轨道交通运营、建筑施工单位应当建立应急救援组织；生产经营规模较小的，可以不建立应急救援组织，但应当指定兼职的应急救援人员。  
危险物品的生产、经营、储存、运输单位以及矿山、金属冶炼、城市轨道交通运营、建筑施工单位应当配备必要的应急救援器材、设备和物资，并进行经常性维护、保养，保证正常运转。

**Article 82** An entity that produces, handles, or stores hazardous articles, as well as a mining, metal smelting, urban rail transit operation, or construction unit, shall establish an emergency rescue organization; if the scale of production and business operations is small, it may not establish an emergency rescue organization but shall designate part-time emergency rescue personnel.  
An entity that produces, handles, stores, or transports hazardous articles, as well as a mining, metal smelting, urban rail transit operation, or construction unit, shall be equipped with necessary emergency rescue equipment, devices, and materials, and shall regularly maintain and service them to ensure their normal operation.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　生产经营单位发生生产安全事故后，事故现场有关人员应当立即报告本单位负责人。  
单位负责人接到事故报告后，应当迅速采取有效措施，组织抢救，防止事故扩大，减少人员伤亡和财产损失，并按照国家有关规定立即如实报告当地负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门，不得隐瞒不报、谎报或者迟报，不得故意破坏事故现场、毁灭有关证据。

**Article 83** After a production safety accident occurs in a production and business operation entity, the relevant persons at the accident scene shall immediately report to the person in charge of the entity.  
Upon receiving the accident report, the person in charge of the entity shall promptly take effective measures, organize rescue, prevent the accident from escalating, reduce casualties and property losses, and immediately report truthfully to the local department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; he or she shall not conceal, make a false report, or delay the report, nor shall he or she deliberately damage the accident scene or destroy relevant evidence.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门接到事故报告后，应当立即按照国家有关规定上报事故情况。负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门和有关地方人民政府对事故情况不得隐瞒不报、谎报或者迟报。

**Article 84** Upon receiving an accident report, a department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration shall immediately report the accident situation to the higher authorities in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. The departments with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration and the relevant local people's governments shall not conceal, make a false report, or delay the report of the accident situation.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　有关地方人民政府和负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门的负责人接到生产安全事故报告后，应当按照生产安全事故应急救援预案的要求立即赶到事故现场，组织事故抢救。  
参与事故抢救的部门和单位应当服从统一指挥，加强协同联动，采取有效的应急救援措施，并根据事故救援的需要采取警戒、疏散等措施，防止事故扩大和次生灾害的发生，减少人员伤亡和财产损失。  
事故抢救过程中应当采取必要措施，避免或者减少对环境造成的危害。  
任何单位和个人都应当支持、配合事故抢救，并提供一切便利条件。

**Article 85** After receiving a report of a production safety accident, the relevant local people's governments and the heads of departments with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration shall immediately rush to the accident scene in accordance with the requirements of the emergency rescue plan for production safety accidents, and organize accident rescue.  
The departments and entities participating in accident rescue shall obey unified command, strengthen coordination and joint action, take effective emergency rescue measures, and take measures such as cordoning off and evacuation as needed for accident rescue, to prevent the accident from escalating and the occurrence of secondary disasters, and reduce casualties and property losses.  
During the accident rescue process, necessary measures shall be taken to avoid or reduce harm to the environment.  
Any entity or individual shall support and cooperate with accident rescue and provide all convenient conditions.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　事故调查处理应当按照科学严谨、依法依规、实事求是、注重实效的原则，及时、准确地查清事故原因，查明事故性质和责任，评估应急处置工作，总结事故教训，提出整改措施，并对事故责任单位和人员提出处理建议。事故调查报告应当依法及时向社会公布。事故调查和处理的具体办法由国务院制定。  
事故发生单位应当及时全面落实整改措施，负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当加强监督检查。  
负责事故调查处理的国务院有关部门和地方人民政府应当在批复事故调查报告后一年内，组织有关部门对事故整改和防范措施落实情况进行评估，并及时向社会公开评估结果；对不履行职责导致事故整改和防范措施没有落实的有关单位和人员，应当按照有关规定追究责任。

**Article 86** Accident investigation and handling shall be conducted in accordance with the principles of being scientific and rigorous, law-based and regulation-based, seeking truth from facts, and focusing on practical results; the cause of the accident shall be identified promptly and accurately, the nature of the accident and responsibilities shall be ascertained, the emergency response work shall be evaluated, lessons shall be drawn from the accident, corrective measures shall be proposed, and recommendations for handling the responsible entities and persons shall be made. The accident investigation report shall be made public in a timely manner according to law. The specific measures for accident investigation and handling shall be formulated by the State Council.  
The entity where the accident occurred shall fully implement the corrective measures in a timely manner, and the department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration shall strengthen supervision and inspection.  
The relevant department of the State Council or local people's government responsible for accident investigation and handling shall, within one year after approving the accident investigation report, organize relevant departments to evaluate the implementation of accident rectification and preventive measures, and disclose the evaluation results to the public in a timely manner; where any entity or person fails to perform its/his/her duties, resulting in the failure to implement accident rectification and preventive measures, liability shall be pursued in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　生产经营单位发生生产安全事故，经调查确定为责任事故的，除了应当查明事故单位的责任并依法予以追究外，还应当查明对安全生产的有关事项负有审查批准和监督职责的行政部门的责任，对有失职、渎职行为的，依照[本法第九十条](#art-90)的规定追究法律责任。

**Article 87** Where a production safety accident occurs in a production and business operation entity and is determined to be an accident attributable to fault after investigation, in addition to ascertaining the liability of the entity where the accident occurred and pursuing liability according to law, the liability of the administrative department responsible for examination, approval, and supervision of work safety matters shall also be ascertained; where there is dereliction of duty or malfeasance, legal liability shall be pursued in accordance with Article 90 of this Law.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　任何单位和个人不得阻挠和干涉对事故的依法调查处理。

**Article 88** No entity or individual may obstruct or interfere with the lawful investigation and handling of an accident.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应急管理部门应当定期统计分析本行政区域内发生生产安全事故的情况，并定期向社会公布。

**Article 89** The emergency management department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall regularly conduct statistical analysis of the production safety accidents occurring in its administrative region and regularly make the information public.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门的工作人员，有下列行为之一的，给予降级或者撤职的处分；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任：  
（一）对不符合法定安全生产条件的涉及安全生产的事项予以批准或者验收通过的；  
（二）发现未依法取得批准、验收的单位擅自从事有关活动或者接到举报后不予取缔或者不依法予以处理的；  
（三）对已经依法取得批准的单位不履行监督管理职责，发现其不再具备安全生产条件而不撤销原批准或者发现安全生产违法行为不予查处的；  
（四）在监督检查中发现重大事故隐患，不依法及时处理的。  
负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门的工作人员有前款规定以外的滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 90** Any staff member of a department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration who commits any of the following acts shall be given a sanction of demotion or removal from office; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Approving or accepting items related to work safety that do not meet the statutory work safety conditions;  
(2) Failing to ban an entity that engages in relevant activities without lawfully obtaining approval or passing acceptance, or failing to handle it according to law upon discovery or receipt of a report;  
(3) Failing to perform supervision and administration duties over an entity that has lawfully obtained approval, failing to revoke the original approval when finding that the entity no longer meets the work safety conditions, or failing to investigate and deal with work safety violations discovered;  
(4) Failing to promptly handle a major potential accident hazard discovered during supervision and inspection according to law.  
Any staff member of a department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration who abuses his or her power, neglects his or her duties, or engages in malpractices for personal gain, other than those specified in the preceding paragraph, shall be given a sanction according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门，要求被审查、验收的单位购买其指定的安全设备、器材或者其他产品的，在对安全生产事项的审查、验收中收取费用的，由其上级机关或者监察机关责令改正，责令退还收取的费用；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 91** Where a department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration requires an entity undergoing examination or acceptance to purchase safety equipment, apparatus, or other products designated by it, or charges fees for examining or accepting work safety items, the higher authority or the supervisory organ shall order it to make corrections and order the return of the fees charged; if the circumstances are serious, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　承担安全评价、认证、检测、检验职责的机构出具失实报告的，责令停业整顿，并处三万元以上十万元以下的罚款；给他人造成损害的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
承担安全评价、认证、检测、检验职责的机构租借资质、挂靠、出具虚假报告的，没收违法所得；违法所得在十万元以上的，并处违法所得二倍以上五倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足十万元的，单处或者并处十万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款；给他人造成损害的，与生产经营单位承担连带赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。  
对有前款违法行为的机构及其直接责任人员，吊销其相应资质和资格，五年内不得从事安全评价、认证、检测、检验等工作；情节严重的，实行终身行业和职业禁入。

**Article 92** Where an institution responsible for work safety assessment, certification, testing, or inspection issues a false report, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and shall be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if it causes harm to others, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.  
Where an institution responsible for work safety assessment, certification, testing, or inspection rents out or lends its qualifications, engages in affiliation, or issues a false report, its illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are 100,000 yuan or more, it shall also be fined not less than two times but not more than five times the amount of illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, it shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, either alone or in combination; the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if it causes harm to others, it shall bear joint and several compensation liability with the production and business operation entity; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
The corresponding qualifications and eligibility of the institution and its directly responsible persons who have committed the illegal acts specified in the preceding paragraph shall be revoked, and they shall be prohibited from engaging in work safety assessment, certification, testing, inspection, etc., for five years; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be subject to a lifelong ban from the industry and occupation.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　生产经营单位的决策机构、主要负责人或者个人经营的投资人不依照本法规定保证安全生产所必需的资金投入，致使生产经营单位不具备安全生产条件的，责令限期改正，提供必需的资金；逾期未改正的，责令生产经营单位停产停业整顿。  
有前款违法行为，导致发生生产安全事故的，对生产经营单位的主要负责人给予撤职处分，对个人经营的投资人处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 93** Where the decision-making body, principal responsible person, or individual investor of a production and business operation entity fails to ensure the capital investment necessary for work safety in accordance with the provisions of this Law, resulting in the entity not meeting the work safety conditions, it/he/she shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit and provide the necessary capital; if it/he/she fails to make corrections within the time limit, the production and business operation entity shall be ordered to suspend production and business operations for rectification.  
Where the illegal act specified in the preceding paragraph causes a production safety accident, the principal responsible person of the production and business operation entity shall be given a sanction of removal from office, and the individual investor shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　生产经营单位的主要负责人未履行本法规定的安全生产管理职责的，责令限期改正，处二万元以上五万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款，责令生产经营单位停产停业整顿。  
生产经营单位的主要负责人有前款违法行为，导致发生生产安全事故的，给予撤职处分；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。  
生产经营单位的主要负责人依照前款规定受刑事处罚或者撤职处分的，自刑罚执行完毕或者受处分之日起，五年内不得担任任何生产经营单位的主要负责人；对重大、特别重大生产安全事故负有责任的，终身不得担任本行业生产经营单位的主要负责人。

**Article 94** Where the principal responsible person of a production and business operation entity fails to perform the work safety management duties specified in this Law, he or she shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit and be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if he or she fails to make corrections within the time limit, he or she shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan, and the production and business operation entity shall be ordered to suspend production and business operations for rectification.  
Where the principal responsible person of a production and business operation entity commits the illegal act specified in the preceding paragraph, causing a production safety accident, he or she shall be given a sanction of removal from office; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
Where the principal responsible person of a production and business operation entity is subject to criminal punishment or a sanction of removal from office in accordance with the preceding paragraph, he or she shall not serve as the principal responsible person of any production and business operation entity within five years from the date of completion of the execution of the punishment or the date of the sanction; if he or she bears responsibility for a major or particularly major production safety accident, he or she shall be permanently prohibited from serving as the principal responsible person of a production and business operation entity in the same industry.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　生产经营单位的主要负责人未履行本法规定的安全生产管理职责，导致发生生产安全事故的，由应急管理部门依照下列规定处以罚款：  
（一）发生一般事故的，处上一年年收入百分之四十的罚款；  
（二）发生较大事故的，处上一年年收入百分之六十的罚款；  
（三）发生重大事故的，处上一年年收入百分之八十的罚款；  
（四）发生特别重大事故的，处上一年年收入百分之一百的罚款。

**Article 95** Where the principal responsible person of a production and business operation entity fails to perform the work safety management duties specified in this Law, thereby causing a production safety accident, the emergency management department shall impose a fine in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) For an ordinary accident, a fine of 40% of his or her annual income of the previous year;  
(2) For a relatively serious accident, a fine of 60% of his or her annual income of the previous year;  
(3) For a serious accident, a fine of 80% of his or her annual income of the previous year;  
(4) For a particularly serious accident, a fine of 100% of his or her annual income of the previous year.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　生产经营单位的其他负责人和安全生产管理人员未履行本法规定的安全生产管理职责的，责令限期改正，处一万元以上三万元以下的罚款；导致发生生产安全事故的，暂停或者吊销其与安全生产有关的资格，并处上一年年收入百分之二十以上百分之五十以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 96** Where any other responsible person or work safety manager of a production and business operation entity fails to perform the work safety management duties specified in this Law, he or she shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit and be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan; if such failure causes a production safety accident, his or her qualification related to work safety shall be suspended or revoked, and he or she shall also be fined not less than 20% but not more than 50% of his or her annual income of the previous year; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　生产经营单位有下列行为之一的，责令限期改正，处十万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，责令停产停业整顿，并处十万元以上二十万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处二万元以上五万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未按照规定设置安全生产管理机构或者配备安全生产管理人员、注册安全工程师的；  
（二）危险物品的生产、经营、储存、装卸单位以及矿山、金属冶炼、建筑施工、运输单位的主要负责人和安全生产管理人员未按照规定经考核合格的；  
（三）未按照规定对从业人员、被派遣劳动者、实习学生进行安全生产教育和培训，或者未按照规定如实告知有关的安全生产事项的；  
（四）未如实记录安全生产教育和培训情况的；  
（五）未将事故隐患排查治理情况如实记录或者未向从业人员通报的；  
（六）未按照规定制定生产安全事故应急救援预案或者未定期组织演练的；  
（七）特种作业人员未按照规定经专门的安全作业培训并取得相应资格，上岗作业的。

**Article 97** Where a production and business operation entity commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit and be fined not more than 100,000 yuan; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend production and business operations for rectification and be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, and the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan:  
(1) Failing to establish a work safety management institution or to assign work safety managers or certified safety engineers as required;  
(2) The principal responsible persons and work safety managers of entities that produce, handle, store, or load/unload hazardous articles, as well as mining, metal smelting, construction, and transport entities, fail to pass the prescribed assessment as required;  
(3) Failing to provide work safety education and training to employees, dispatched workers, or intern students as required, or failing to truthfully inform them of relevant work safety matters as required;  
(4) Failing to truthfully record work safety education and training;  
(5) Failing to truthfully record the investigation and management of potential accident hazards or to inform employees of such hazards;  
(6) Failing to formulate an emergency rescue plan for production safety accidents as required or failing to organize regular drills;  
(7) Assigning special operations personnel to work without their having received special safety operation training and obtained corresponding qualifications as required.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　生产经营单位有下列行为之一的，责令停止建设或者停产停业整顿，限期改正，并处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处二万元以上五万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，处五十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任：  
（一）未按照规定对矿山、金属冶炼建设项目或者用于生产、储存、装卸危险物品的建设项目进行安全评价的；  
（二）矿山、金属冶炼建设项目或者用于生产、储存、装卸危险物品的建设项目没有安全设施设计或者安全设施设计未按照规定报经有关部门审查同意的；  
（三）矿山、金属冶炼建设项目或者用于生产、储存、装卸危险物品的建设项目的施工单位未按照批准的安全设施设计施工的；  
（四）矿山、金属冶炼建设项目或者用于生产、储存、装卸危险物品的建设项目竣工投入生产或者使用前，安全设施未经验收合格的。

**Article 98** Where a production and business operation entity commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to stop construction or suspend production and business operations for rectification, make corrections within a time limit, and be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be fined not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan, and the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Failing to conduct a work safety assessment for a mining or metal smelting construction project or a construction project used for producing, storing, or loading/unloading hazardous articles as required;  
(2) A mining or metal smelting construction project or a construction project used for producing, storing, or loading/unloading hazardous articles has no design of safety facilities, or the design of safety facilities has not been submitted to the relevant department for examination and approval as required;  
(3) The construction unit of a mining or metal smelting construction project or a construction project used for producing, storing, or loading/unloading hazardous articles fails to construct in accordance with the approved design of safety facilities;  
(4) Before a mining or metal smelting construction project or a construction project used for producing, storing, or loading/unloading hazardous articles is completed and put into production or use, the safety facilities have not passed acceptance inspection.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　生产经营单位有下列行为之一的，责令限期改正，处五万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以上二万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停产停业整顿；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任：  
（一）未在有较大危险因素的生产经营场所和有关设施、设备上设置明显的安全警示标志的；  
（二）安全设备的安装、使用、检测、改造和报废不符合国家标准或者行业标准的；  
（三）未对安全设备进行经常性维护、保养和定期检测的；  
（四）关闭、破坏直接关系生产安全的监控、报警、防护、救生设备、设施，或者篡改、隐瞒、销毁其相关数据、信息的；  
（五）未为从业人员提供符合国家标准或者行业标准的劳动防护用品的；  
（六）危险物品的容器、运输工具，以及涉及人身安全、危险性较大的海洋石油开采特种设备和矿山井下特种设备未经具有专业资质的机构检测、检验合格，取得安全使用证或者安全标志，投入使用的；  
（七）使用应当淘汰的危及生产安全的工艺、设备的；  
（八）餐饮等行业的生产经营单位使用燃气未安装可燃气体报警装置的。

**Article 99** Where a production and business operation entity commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit and be fined not more than 50,000 yuan; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, and the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend production and business operations for rectification; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Failing to set up conspicuous work safety warning signs at production and business operation premises with relatively high hazardous factors and on relevant facilities and equipment;  
(2) The installation, use, testing, modification, or scrapping of safety equipment does not conform to national standards or industry standards;  
(3) Failing to regularly maintain, service, and periodically test safety equipment;  
(4) Shutting down or destroying monitoring, alarm, protection, or life-saving equipment or facilities directly related to production safety, or tampering with, concealing, or destroying their relevant data or information;  
(5) Failing to provide employees with labor protection supplies that conform to national standards or industry standards;  
(6) Vessels, transport vehicles for hazardous articles, as well as special equipment for offshore oil extraction and special equipment for underground mines that involve personal safety and are relatively dangerous, are put into use without having been tested and inspected qualified by an institution with professional qualifications and without obtaining a safety certificate or safety mark;  
(7) Using processes or equipment that should be eliminated and that endanger production safety;  
(8) Production and business operation entities in industries such as catering use gas without installing combustible gas alarm devices.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　未经依法批准，擅自生产、经营、运输、储存、使用危险物品或者处置废弃危险物品的，依照有关危险物品安全管理的法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 100** Any person who, without lawful approval, produces, handles, transports, stores, uses hazardous articles, or disposes of waste hazardous articles without authorization shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the laws and administrative regulations on the safety management of hazardous articles; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　生产经营单位有下列行为之一的，责令限期改正，处十万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，责令停产停业整顿，并处十万元以上二十万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处二万元以上五万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任：  
（一）生产、经营、运输、储存、使用危险物品或者处置废弃危险物品，未建立专门安全管理制度、未采取可靠的安全措施的；  
（二）对重大危险源未登记建档，未进行定期检测、评估、监控，未制定应急预案，或者未告知应急措施的；  
（三）进行爆破、吊装、动火、临时用电以及国务院应急管理部门会同国务院有关部门规定的其他危险作业，未安排专门人员进行现场安全管理的；  
（四）未建立安全风险分级管控制度或者未按照安全风险分级采取相应管控措施的；  
（五）未建立事故隐患排查治理制度，或者重大事故隐患排查治理情况未按照规定报告的。

**Article 101** Where a work safety unit commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified period and shall be fined not more than 100,000 yuan; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, it shall be ordered to suspend production or business for rectification and shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, and the directly responsible主管人员 and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law:  
(1) failing to establish a special safety management system or to adopt reliable safety measures in the production, operation, transportation, storage, use of dangerous articles or the disposal of waste dangerous articles;  
(2) failing to register and document major hazard sources, failing to conduct regular testing, assessment, monitoring, failing to formulate emergency response plans, or failing to inform of emergency measures;  
(3) failing to arrange specially assigned persons for on-site safety management when conducting blasting, hoisting, fire operations, temporary electricity use, or other dangerous operations specified by the emergency management department of the State Council in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council;  
(4) failing to establish a graded safety risk control system or failing to adopt corresponding control measures according to the safety risk classification;  
(5) failing to establish a system for investigation and management of hidden dangers of accidents, or failing to report the investigation and management of major hidden dangers of accidents in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　生产经营单位未采取措施消除事故隐患的，责令立即消除或者限期消除，处五万元以下的罚款；生产经营单位拒不执行的，责令停产停业整顿，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 102** Where a work safety unit fails to take measures to eliminate hidden dangers of accidents, it shall be ordered to eliminate them immediately or within a specified period, and shall be fined not more than 50,000 yuan; if the work safety unit refuses to implement the order, it shall be ordered to suspend production or business for rectification, and the directly responsible主管人员 and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　生产经营单位将生产经营项目、场所、设备发包或者出租给不具备安全生产条件或者相应资质的单位或者个人的，责令限期改正，没收违法所得；违法所得十万元以上的，并处违法所得二倍以上五倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足十万元的，单处或者并处十万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以上二万元以下的罚款；导致发生生产安全事故给他人造成损害的，与承包方、承租方承担连带赔偿责任。  
生产经营单位未与承包单位、承租单位签订专门的安全生产管理协议或者未在承包合同、租赁合同中明确各自的安全生产管理职责，或者未对承包单位、承租单位的安全生产统一协调、管理的，责令限期改正，处五万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，责令停产停业整顿。  
矿山、金属冶炼建设项目和用于生产、储存、装卸危险物品的建设项目的施工单位未按照规定对施工项目进行安全管理的，责令限期改正，处十万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处二万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，责令停产停业整顿。以上施工单位倒卖、出租、出借、挂靠或者以其他形式非法转让施工资质的，责令停产停业整顿，吊销资质证书，没收违法所得；违法所得十万元以上的，并处违法所得二倍以上五倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足十万元的，单处或者并处十万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 103** Where a work safety unit contracts out or leases out its production and business operation projects, premises, or equipment to a unit or individual that does not have the conditions for work safety or the corresponding qualifications, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified period, and its illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are not less than 100,000 yuan, it shall also be fined not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, it shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, either solely or concurrently; the directly responsible主管人员 and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan; if a production safety accident is caused and damage is caused to others, it shall bear joint and several compensation liability with the contractor or lessee.  
Where a work safety unit fails to sign a special work safety management agreement with the contracting unit or the lessee unit, or fails to specify their respective work safety management duties in the contract or lease agreement, or fails to conduct unified coordination and management of the work safety of the contracting unit or lessee unit, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified period and shall be fined not more than 50,000 yuan, and the directly responsible主管人员 and other directly liable persons shall be fined not more than 10,000 yuan; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, it shall be ordered to suspend production or business for rectification.  
Where the construction unit of a mine, a metal smelting project, or a construction project for the production, storage, or loading and unloading of dangerous articles fails to conduct safety management of the construction project in accordance with the provisions, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified period and shall be fined not more than 100,000 yuan, and the directly responsible主管人员 and other directly liable persons shall be fined not more than 20,000 yuan; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, it shall be ordered to suspend production or business for rectification. Where the above-mentioned construction unit resells, leases, lends out, attaches, or otherwise illegally transfers its construction qualifications, it shall be ordered to suspend production or business for rectification, its qualification certificate shall be revoked, and its illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are not less than 100,000 yuan, it shall also be fined not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, it shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, either solely or concurrently; the directly responsible主管人员 and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　两个以上生产经营单位在同一作业区域内进行可能危及对方安全生产的生产经营活动，未签订安全生产管理协议或者未指定专职安全生产管理人员进行安全检查与协调的，责令限期改正，处五万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，责令停产停业。

**Article 104** Where two or more work safety units carry out production and business operations in the same working area that may endanger each other's work safety, and fail to sign a work safety management agreement or fail to designate full-time work safety management personnel for safety inspection and coordination, they shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified period and shall be fined not more than 50,000 yuan, and the directly responsible主管人员 and other directly liable persons of each unit shall be fined not more than 10,000 yuan; if they fail to make corrections within the specified period, they shall be ordered to suspend production or business.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　生产经营单位有下列行为之一的，责令限期改正，处五万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，责令停产停业整顿；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任：  
（一）生产、经营、储存、使用危险物品的车间、商店、仓库与员工宿舍在同一座建筑内，或者与员工宿舍的距离不符合安全要求的；  
（二）生产经营场所和员工宿舍未设有符合紧急疏散需要、标志明显、保持畅通的出口、疏散通道，或者占用、锁闭、封堵生产经营场所或者员工宿舍出口、疏散通道的。

**Article 105** Where a work safety unit commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified period and shall be fined not more than 50,000 yuan, and the directly responsible主管人员 and other directly liable persons shall be fined not more than 10,000 yuan; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, it shall be ordered to suspend production or business for rectification; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the workshops, stores, or warehouses where dangerous articles are produced, operated, stored, or used are located in the same building as the employee dormitories, or the distance from the employee dormitories does not meet safety requirements;  
(2) the production and business premises and employee dormitories are not provided with exits or evacuation passages that meet the needs of emergency evacuation, with clear signs and unobstructed conditions, or the exits or evacuation passages of production and business premises or employee dormitories are occupied, locked, or blocked.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　生产经营单位与从业人员订立协议，免除或者减轻其对从业人员因生产安全事故伤亡依法应承担的责任的，该协议无效；对生产经营单位的主要负责人、个人经营的投资人处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 106** Where a work safety unit concludes an agreement with an employee to exempt or reduce the liability it shall bear according to law for the employee's death or injury caused by a production safety accident, such agreement shall be void; the principal responsible person of the work safety unit and the individual business investor shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　生产经营单位的从业人员不落实岗位安全责任，不服从管理，违反安全生产规章制度或者操作规程的，由生产经营单位给予批评教育，依照有关规章制度给予处分；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 107** Where an employee of a work safety unit fails to implement the post safety responsibility, fails to obey management, or violates the work safety rules and regulations or operating procedures, the work safety unit shall give criticism and education and impose disciplinary action in accordance with the relevant rules and regulations; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　违反本法规定，生产经营单位拒绝、阻碍负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门依法实施监督检查的，责令改正；拒不改正的，处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以上二万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 108** Where, in violation of this Law, a work safety unit refuses or obstructs the department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration from carrying out supervision and inspection according to law, it shall be ordered to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, and the directly responsible主管人员 and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　高危行业、领域的生产经营单位未按照国家规定投保安全生产责任保险的，责令限期改正，处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，处十万元以上二十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 109** Where a work safety unit in a high-risk industry or sector fails to take out work safety liability insurance in accordance with the state regulations, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified period and shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, it shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　生产经营单位的主要负责人在本单位发生生产安全事故时，不立即组织抢救或者在事故调查处理期间擅离职守或者逃匿的，给予降级、撤职的处分，并由应急管理部门处上一年年收入百分之六十至百分之一百的罚款；对逃匿的处十五日以下拘留；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。  
生产经营单位的主要负责人对生产安全事故隐瞒不报、谎报或者迟报的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 110** Where the principal responsible person of a work safety unit, when a production safety accident occurs in the unit, fails to immediately organize rescue, or leaves his post without authorization or absconds during the investigation and handling of the accident, he shall be given the sanction of demotion or removal from office, and the emergency management department shall impose a fine of not less than 60 percent but not more than 100 percent of his annual income of the previous year; if he absconds, he shall be detained for not more than 15 days; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
Where the principal responsible person of a work safety unit conceals, makes false reports, or delays the reporting of a production safety accident, he shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　有关地方人民政府、负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门，对生产安全事故隐瞒不报、谎报或者迟报的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 111** Where a relevant local people's government or a department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration conceals, makes false reports, or delays the reporting of a production safety accident, the directly responsible主管人员 and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　生产经营单位违反本法规定，被责令改正且受到罚款处罚，拒不改正的，负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门可以自作出责令改正之日的次日起，按照原处罚数额按日连续处罚。

**Article 112** Where a work safety unit violates the provisions of this Law, is ordered to make corrections and is subject to a fine penalty, but refuses to make corrections, the department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration may, from the day following the date of the order for corrections, impose a daily continuous fine at the original penalty amount.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　生产经营单位存在下列情形之一的，负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当提请地方人民政府予以关闭，有关部门应当依法吊销其有关证照。生产经营单位主要负责人五年内不得担任任何生产经营单位的主要负责人；情节严重的，终身不得担任本行业生产经营单位的主要负责人：  
（一）存在重大事故隐患，一百八十日内三次或者一年内四次受到本法规定的行政处罚的；  
（二）经停产停业整顿，仍不具备法律、行政法规和国家标准或者行业标准规定的安全生产条件的；  
（三）不具备法律、行政法规和国家标准或者行业标准规定的安全生产条件，导致发生重大、特别重大生产安全事故的；  
（四）拒不执行负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门作出的停产停业整顿决定的。

**Article 113** Where a work safety unit is in any of the following circumstances, the department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration shall propose to the local people's government that the unit be closed, and the relevant departments shall revoke its relevant licenses and certificates according to law. The principal responsible person of the work safety unit shall not hold the position of principal responsible person of any work safety unit within five years; if the circumstances are serious, he shall be disqualified for life from holding the position of principal responsible person of a work safety unit in the industry:  
(1) there is a major hidden danger of an accident, and the unit has been subject to administrative penalties under this Law three times within 180 days or four times within one year;  
(2) after being ordered to suspend production or business for rectification, it still does not meet the work safety conditions prescribed by laws, administrative regulations, national standards or industry standards;  
(3) it does not meet the work safety conditions prescribed by laws, administrative regulations, national standards or industry standards, resulting in a major or particularly major production safety accident;  
(4) it refuses to comply with the decision on suspension of production or business for rectification made by the department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　发生生产安全事故，对负有责任的生产经营单位除要求其依法承担相应的赔偿等责任外，由应急管理部门依照下列规定处以罚款：  
（一）发生一般事故的，处三十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；  
（二）发生较大事故的，处一百万元以上二百万元以下的罚款；  
（三）发生重大事故的，处二百万元以上一千万元以下的罚款；  
（四）发生特别重大事故的，处一千万元以上二千万元以下的罚款。  
发生生产安全事故，情节特别严重、影响特别恶劣的，应急管理部门可以按照前款罚款数额的二倍以上五倍以下对负有责任的生产经营单位处以罚款。

**Article 114** Where a production safety accident occurs, the work safety unit that bears responsibility shall, in addition to being required to bear corresponding compensation liability according to law, be fined by the emergency management department in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) if a general accident occurs, a fine of not less than 300,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan;  
(2) if a relatively serious accident occurs, a fine of not less than 1,000,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan;  
(3) if a major accident occurs, a fine of not less than 2,000,000 yuan but not more than 10,000,000 yuan;  
(4) if a particularly major accident occurs, a fine of not less than 10,000,000 yuan but not more than 20,000,000 yuan.  
Where a production safety accident occurs with particularly serious circumstances and particularly adverse effects, the emergency management department may impose a fine on the responsible work safety unit of not less than two times but not more than five times the amount of the fine prescribed in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　本法规定的行政处罚，由应急管理部门和其他负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门按照职责分工决定；其中，根据[本法第九十五条](#art-95)、第一百一十条、第一百一十四条的规定应当给予民航、铁路、电力行业的生产经营单位及其主要负责人行政处罚的，也可以由主管的负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门进行处罚。予以关闭的行政处罚，由负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门报请县级以上人民政府按照国务院规定的权限决定；给予拘留的行政处罚，由公安机关依照治安管理处罚的规定决定。

**Article 115** The administrative penalties prescribed in this Law shall be decided by the emergency management department and other departments with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration in accordance with their respective functions and duties; where, according to the provisions of Articles 95, 110, and 114 of this Law, administrative penalties shall be imposed on work safety units in the civil aviation, railway, and electric power industries and their principal responsible persons, the competent department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration may also impose penalties. The administrative penalty of closure shall be reported by the department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration to the people's government at or above the county level for decision within the authority prescribed by the State Council; the administrative penalty of detention shall be decided by the public security organ in accordance with the provisions on public security administration punishments.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　生产经营单位发生生产安全事故造成人员伤亡、他人财产损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任；拒不承担或者其负责人逃匿的，由人民法院依法强制执行。  
生产安全事故的责任人未依法承担赔偿责任，经人民法院依法采取执行措施后，仍不能对受害人给予足额赔偿的，应当继续履行赔偿义务；受害人发现责任人有其他财产的，可以随时请求人民法院执行。

**Article 116** Where a production safety accident causes casualties or property losses to others, the work safety unit shall bear compensation liability according to law; if it refuses to bear liability or its responsible person absconds, the People's Court shall enforce the execution according to law.  
Where the person liable for a production safety accident fails to bear compensation liability according to law, and after the People's Court takes enforcement measures according to law, still cannot give full compensation to the victim, he shall continue to perform the compensation obligation; if the victim discovers that the liable person has other property, he may at any time request the People's Court to enforce execution.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　本法下列用语的含义：  
危险物品，是指易燃易爆物品、危险化学品、放射性物品等能够危及人身安全和财产安全的物品。  
重大危险源，是指长期地或者临时地生产、搬运、使用或者储存危险物品，且危险物品的数量等于或者超过临界量的单元（包括场所和设施）。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/检察院/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理危害生产安全刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（二）.md#art-4" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理危害生产安全刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（二）》第4条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 117** The meanings of the following terms used in this Law:  
Dangerous articles means articles such as flammable and explosive articles, dangerous chemicals, and radioactive articles that can endanger personal safety and property safety.  
Major hazard source means a unit (including premises and facilities) where dangerous articles are produced, handled, used or stored on a long-term or temporary basis, and the quantity of such dangerous articles equals or exceeds the critical quantity.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　本法规定的生产安全一般事故、较大事故、重大事故、特别重大事故的划分标准由国务院规定。  
国务院应急管理部门和其他负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当根据各自的职责分工，制定相关行业、领域重大危险源的辨识标准和重大事故隐患的判定标准。

**Article 118** The classification standards for general accidents, relatively serious accidents, major accidents, and particularly major accidents of production safety prescribed in this Law shall be stipulated by the State Council.  
The emergency management department of the State Council and other departments with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, formulate the identification standards for major hazard sources and the determination standards for major hidden dangers of accidents in the relevant industries and sectors.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　本法自2002年11月1日起施行。

**Article 119** This Law shall come into force as of November 1, 2002.
