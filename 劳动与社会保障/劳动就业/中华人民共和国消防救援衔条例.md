### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强国家综合性消防救援队伍正规化、专业化、职业化建设，增强消防救援人员的责任感、荣誉感和组织纪律性，有利于国家综合性消防救援队伍的指挥、管理和依法履行职责，根据宪法，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Constitution to strengthen the standardization, specialization, and professionalization of the national comprehensive fire and rescue teams, enhance the sense of responsibility, honor, and organizational discipline of fire and rescue personnel, and facilitate the command, management, and performance of duties according to law by the national comprehensive fire and rescue teams.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家综合性消防救援队伍实行消防救援衔制度。  
消防救援衔授予对象为纳入国家行政编制、由国务院应急管理部门统一领导管理的综合性消防救援队伍在职人员。

**Article 2** The national comprehensive fire and rescue teams shall implement a system of fire and rescue ranks.  
The recipients of fire and rescue ranks shall be in-service personnel of the comprehensive fire and rescue teams who are included in the state administrative staffing and are under the unified leadership and management of the emergency management department of the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　消防救援衔是表明消防救援人员身份、区分消防救援人员等级的称号和标志，是国家给予消防救援人员的荣誉和相应待遇的依据。

**Article 3** Fire and rescue ranks are titles and insignia indicating the identity of fire and rescue personnel and distinguishing their grades, and are the basis for the honor and corresponding treatment given by the state to fire and rescue personnel.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　消防救援衔高的人员对消防救援衔低的人员，消防救援衔高的为上级。消防救援衔高的人员在职务上隶属于消防救援衔低的人员时，担任领导职务或者领导职务高的为上级。

**Article 4** A person with a higher fire and rescue rank shall be the superior of a person with a lower fire and rescue rank. Where a person with a higher fire and rescue rank is subordinate in position to a person with a lower fire and rescue rank, the person holding a leadership position or the person with a higher leadership position shall be the superior.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院应急管理部门主管消防救援衔工作。

**Article 5** The emergency management department of the State Council shall be responsible for the work concerning fire and rescue ranks.

### 第二章　消防救援衔等级的设置

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　消防救援衔按照管理指挥人员、专业技术人员和消防员分别设置。

**Article 6** Fire and rescue ranks shall be established separately for management and command personnel, professional and technical personnel, and firefighters.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　管理指挥人员消防救援衔设下列三等十一级：  
（一）总监、副总监、助理总监；  
（二）指挥长：高级指挥长、一级指挥长、二级指挥长、三级指挥长；  
（三）指挥员：一级指挥员、二级指挥员、三级指挥员、四级指挥员。

**Article 7** The fire and rescue ranks for management and command personnel shall consist of the following three grades and eleven levels:  
(1) Commissioner General, Deputy Commissioner General, and Assistant Commissioner General;  
(2) Commanders: Senior Commander, First-level Commander, Second-level Commander, and Third-level Commander;  
(3) Command Officers: First-level Command Officer, Second-level Command Officer, Third-level Command Officer, and Fourth-level Command Officer.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　专业技术人员消防救援衔设下列二等八级，在消防救援衔前冠以“专业技术”：  
（一）指挥长：高级指挥长、一级指挥长、二级指挥长、三级指挥长；  
（二）指挥员：一级指挥员、二级指挥员、三级指挥员、四级指挥员。

**Article 8** The fire and rescue ranks for professional and technical personnel shall consist of the following two grades and eight levels, with the prefix "Professional and Technical" before the rank:  
(1) Commanders: Senior Commander, First-level Commander, Second-level Commander, and Third-level Commander;  
(2) Command Officers: First-level Command Officer, Second-level Command Officer, Third-level Command Officer, and Fourth-level Command Officer.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　消防员消防救援衔设下列三等八级：  
（一）高级消防员：一级消防长、二级消防长、三级消防长；  
（二）中级消防员：一级消防士、二级消防士；  
（三）初级消防员：三级消防士、四级消防士、预备消防士。

**Article 9** The fire and rescue ranks for firefighters shall consist of the following three grades and eight levels:  
(1) Senior Firefighters: First-level Fire Chief, Second-level Fire Chief, and Third-level Fire Chief;  
(2) Intermediate Firefighters: First-level Fire Sergeant, Second-level Fire Sergeant;  
(3) Junior Firefighters: Third-level Fire Sergeant, Fourth-level Fire Sergeant, and Probationary Fire Sergeant.

### 第三章　消防救援衔等级的编制

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　管理指挥人员按照下列职务等级编制消防救援衔：  
（一）国务院应急管理部门正职：总监；  
（二）国务院应急管理部门消防救援队伍领导指挥机构、森林消防队伍领导指挥机构正职：副总监；  
（三）国务院应急管理部门消防救援队伍领导指挥机构、森林消防队伍领导指挥机构副职：助理总监；  
（四）总队级正职：高级指挥长；  
（五）总队级副职：一级指挥长；  
（六）支队级正职：二级指挥长；  
（七）支队级副职：三级指挥长；  
（八）大队级正职：一级指挥员；  
（九）大队级副职：二级指挥员；  
（十）站（中队）级正职：三级指挥员；  
（十一）站（中队）级副职：四级指挥员。

**Article 10** The fire and rescue ranks for management and command personnel shall be established according to the following position grades:  
(1) The principal leader of the emergency management department of the State Council: Commissioner General;  
(2) The principal leader of the leading and command organization of fire and rescue teams and the leading and command organization of forest fire teams under the emergency management department of the State Council: Deputy Commissioner General;  
(3) The deputy leader of the leading and command organization of fire and rescue teams and the leading and command organization of forest fire teams under the emergency management department of the State Council: Assistant Commissioner General;  
(4) The principal leader at the corps level: Senior Commander;  
(5) The deputy leader at the corps level: First-level Commander;  
(6) The principal leader at the brigade level: Second-level Commander;  
(7) The deputy leader at the brigade level: Third-level Commander;  
(8) The principal leader at the battalion level: First-level Command Officer;  
(9) The deputy leader at the battalion level: Second-level Command Officer;  
(10) The principal leader at the station (company) level: Third-level Command Officer;  
(11) The deputy leader at the station (company) level: Fourth-level Command Officer.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　专业技术人员按照下列职务等级编制消防救援衔：  
（一）高级专业技术职务：高级指挥长至三级指挥长；  
（二）中级专业技术职务：一级指挥长至二级指挥员；  
（三）初级专业技术职务：三级指挥长至四级指挥员。

**Article 11** The fire and rescue ranks for professional and technical personnel shall be established according to the following position grades:  
(1) Senior professional and technical positions: Senior Commander to Third-level Commander;  
(2) Intermediate professional and technical positions: First-level Commander to Second-level Command Officer;  
(3) Junior professional and technical positions: Third-level Commander to Fourth-level Command Officer.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　消防员按照下列工作年限编制消防救援衔：  
（一）工作满二十四年的：一级消防长；  
（二）工作满二十年的：二级消防长；  
（三）工作满十六年的：三级消防长；  
（四）工作满十二年的：一级消防士；  
（五）工作满八年的：二级消防士；  
（六）工作满五年的：三级消防士；  
（七）工作满二年的：四级消防士；  
（八）工作二年以下的：预备消防士。

**Article 12** The fire and rescue ranks for firefighters shall be established according to the following years of service:  
(1) Having served for twenty-four years or more: First-level Fire Chief;  
(2) Having served for twenty years or more: Second-level Fire Chief;  
(3) Having served for sixteen years or more: Third-level Fire Chief;  
(4) Having served for twelve years or more: First-level Fire Sergeant;  
(5) Having served for eight years or more: Second-level Fire Sergeant;  
(6) Having served for five years or more: Third-level Fire Sergeant;  
(7) Having served for two years or more: Fourth-level Fire Sergeant;  
(8) Having served for less than two years: Probationary Fire Sergeant.

### 第四章　消防救援衔的首次授予

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　授予消防救援衔，以消防救援人员现任职务、德才表现、学历学位、任职时间和工作年限为依据。

**Article 13** The conferment of a fire and rescue rank shall be based on the current position, moral character and professional competence, educational background and academic degrees, length of tenure in the position, and years of service of the fire and rescue personnel.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　初任管理指挥人员、专业技术人员，按照下列规定首次授予消防救援衔：  
（一）从普通高等学校毕业生中招录，取得大学专科、本科学历的，授予四级指挥员消防救援衔；取得硕士学位的研究生，授予三级指挥员消防救援衔；取得博士学位的研究生，授予一级指挥员消防救援衔；  
（二）从消防员选拔任命为管理指挥人员、专业技术人员的，按照所任命的职务等级授予相应的消防救援衔；  
（三）从国家机关或者其他救援队伍调入的，或者从符合条件的社会人员中招录的，按照所任命的职务等级授予相应的消防救援衔。

**Article 14** For newly appointed management and command personnel or professional and technical personnel, the fire and rescue rank shall be conferred for the first time according to the following provisions:  
(1) For those recruited from graduates of regular institutions of higher learning who have obtained a college diploma or bachelor's degree, the rank of Fourth-level Command Officer shall be conferred; for those who have obtained a master's degree, the rank of Third-level Command Officer shall be conferred; for those who have obtained a doctoral degree, the rank of First-level Command Officer shall be conferred;  
(2) For those selected and appointed from firefighters as management and command personnel or professional and technical personnel, the corresponding fire and rescue rank shall be conferred according to the position grade to which they are appointed;  
(3) For those transferred from state organs or other rescue teams, or recruited from qualified members of the public, the corresponding fire and rescue rank shall be conferred according to the position grade to which they are appointed.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　初任消防员，按照下列规定首次授予消防救援衔：  
（一）从高中毕业生、普通高等学校在校生或者毕业生中招录的，授予预备消防士；  
（二）从退役士兵中招录的，其服役年限计入工作时间，按照[本条例第十二条](#art-12)的规定，授予相应的消防救援衔；  
（三）从其他救援队伍或者具备专业技能的社会人员中招录的，根据其从事相关专业工作时间，比照国家综合性消防救援队伍中同等条件人员，授予相应的消防救援衔。

**Article 15** For newly appointed firefighters, the fire and rescue rank shall be conferred for the first time according to the following provisions:  
(1) For those recruited from high school graduates, current students or graduates of regular institutions of higher learning, the rank of Probationary Fire Sergeant shall be conferred;  
(2) For those recruited from retired soldiers, their years of military service shall be counted as working years, and the corresponding fire and rescue rank shall be conferred in accordance with the provisions of Article 12 of these Regulations;  
(3) For those recruited from other rescue teams or members of the public with professional skills, the corresponding fire and rescue rank shall be conferred by reference to personnel with equivalent conditions in the national comprehensive fire and rescue teams, based on their years of working in the relevant profession.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　首次授予管理指挥人员、专业技术人员消防救援衔，按照下列规定的权限予以批准：  
（一）授予总监、副总监、助理总监，由国务院总理批准；  
（二）授予高级指挥长、一级指挥长、二级指挥长，由国务院应急管理部门正职领导批准；  
（三）授予三级指挥长、一级指挥员，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应急管理部门同意后由总队级单位正职领导批准，其中森林消防队伍人员由国务院应急管理部门森林消防队伍领导指挥机构正职领导批准；  
（四）授予二级指挥员、三级指挥员、四级指挥员，由总队级单位正职领导批准。

**Article 16** The first conferment of fire and rescue ranks on management and command personnel or professional and technical personnel shall be approved with the following authority:  
(1) The conferment of Commissioner General, Deputy Commissioner General, and Assistant Commissioner General shall be approved by the Premier of the State Council;  
(2) The conferment of Senior Commander, First-level Commander, and Second-level Commander shall be approved by the principal leader of the emergency management department of the State Council;  
(3) The conferment of Third-level Commander and First-level Command Officer shall, after obtaining the consent of the emergency management department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, be approved by the principal leader of the unit at the corps level; among these, for personnel of the forest fire teams, the conferment shall be approved by the principal leader of the leading and command organization of forest fire teams under the emergency management department of the State Council;  
(4) The conferment of Second-level Command Officer, Third-level Command Officer, and Fourth-level Command Officer shall be approved by the principal leader of the unit at the corps level.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　首次授予消防员消防救援衔，按照下列规定的权限予以批准：  
（一）授予一级消防长、二级消防长、三级消防长，由国务院应急管理部门消防救援队伍领导指挥机构、森林消防队伍领导指挥机构正职领导批准；  
（二）授予一级消防士、二级消防士、三级消防士、四级消防士、预备消防士，由总队级单位正职领导批准。

**Article 17** The first conferment of fire and rescue ranks on firefighters shall be approved with the following authority:  
(1) The conferment of First-level Fire Chief, Second-level Fire Chief, and Third-level Fire Chief shall be approved by the principal leader of the leading and command organization of fire and rescue teams or the leading and command organization of forest fire teams under the emergency management department of the State Council;  
(2) The conferment of First-level Fire Sergeant, Second-level Fire Sergeant, Third-level Fire Sergeant, Fourth-level Fire Sergeant, and Probationary Fire Sergeant shall be approved by the principal leader of the unit at the corps level.

### 第五章　消防救援衔的晋级

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　消防救援衔一般根据职务等级调整情况或者工作年限逐级晋升。  
消防救援人员晋升上一级消防救援衔，应当胜任本职工作，遵纪守法，廉洁奉公，作风正派。  
消防救援人员经培训合格后，方可晋升上一级消防救援衔。

**Article 18** Fire and rescue ranks shall generally be promoted level by level based on adjustments in position grade or years of service.  
A fire and rescue personnel member who is to be promoted to the next higher fire and rescue rank shall be competent for the current duties, abide by laws and regulations, be honest and upright in performing official duties, and have a good style of work.  
A fire and rescue personnel member may be promoted to the next higher fire and rescue rank only after passing training.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　管理指挥人员、专业技术人员的消防救援衔晋升，一般与其职务等级晋升一致。  
消防员的消防救援衔晋升，按照[本条例第十二条](#art-12)的规定执行。通过全国普通高等学校招生统一考试、取得全日制大学专科以上学历的消防员晋升消防救援衔，其按照规定学制在普通高等学校学习的时间视同工作时间，但不计入工龄。

**Article 19** The promotion of fire and rescue ranks for management and command personnel and professional and technical personnel shall generally be consistent with the promotion of their position grades.  
The promotion of fire and rescue ranks for firefighters shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of Article 12 of these Regulations. For firefighters who have passed the national unified entrance examination for regular institutions of higher learning and obtained a full-time college diploma or higher degree, the time spent studying in regular institutions of higher learning according to the prescribed length of schooling shall be regarded as working time, but shall not be counted in the length of service.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　管理指挥人员、专业技术人员消防救援衔晋升，按照下列规定的权限予以批准：  
（一）晋升为总监、副总监、助理总监，由国务院总理批准；  
（二）晋升为高级指挥长、一级指挥长，由国务院应急管理部门正职领导批准；  
（三）晋升为二级指挥长，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应急管理部门同意后由总队级单位正职领导批准，其中森林消防队伍人员由国务院应急管理部门森林消防队伍领导指挥机构正职领导批准；  
（四）晋升为三级指挥长、一级指挥员，由总队级单位正职领导批准；  
（五）晋升为二级指挥员、三级指挥员，由支队级单位正职领导批准。

**Article 20** The promotion of fire and rescue ranks for management and command personnel and professional and technical personnel shall be approved with the following authority:  
(1) Promotion to Commissioner General, Deputy Commissioner General, or Assistant Commissioner General shall be approved by the Premier of the State Council;  
(2) Promotion to Senior Commander or First-level Commander shall be approved by the principal leader of the emergency management department of the State Council;  
(3) Promotion to Second-level Commander shall, after obtaining the consent of the emergency management department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, be approved by the principal leader of the unit at the corps level; among these, for personnel of the forest fire teams, the promotion shall be approved by the principal leader of the leading and command organization of forest fire teams under the emergency management department of the State Council;  
(4) Promotion to Third-level Commander or First-level Command Officer shall be approved by the principal leader of the unit at the corps level;  
(5) Promotion to Second-level Command Officer or Third-level Command Officer shall be approved by the principal leader of the unit at the brigade level.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　消防员消防救援衔晋升，按照下列规定的权限予以批准：  
（一）晋升为一级消防长、二级消防长、三级消防长，由国务院应急管理部门消防救援队伍领导指挥机构、森林消防队伍领导指挥机构正职领导批准；  
（二）晋升为一级消防士、二级消防士，由总队级单位正职领导批准；  
（三）晋升为三级消防士、四级消防士，由支队级单位正职领导批准。

**Article 21** The promotion of fire and rescue ranks for firefighters shall be approved with the following authority:  
(1) Promotion to First-level Fire Chief, Second-level Fire Chief, or Third-level Fire Chief shall be approved by the principal leader of the leading and command organization of fire and rescue teams or the leading and command organization of forest fire teams under the emergency management department of the State Council;  
(2) Promotion to First-level Fire Sergeant or Second-level Fire Sergeant shall be approved by the principal leader of the unit at the corps level;  
(3) Promotion to Third-level Fire Sergeant or Fourth-level Fire Sergeant shall be approved by the principal leader of the unit at the brigade level.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　消防救援人员在消防救援工作中做出重大贡献、德才表现突出的，其消防救援衔可以提前晋升。

**Article 22** Where a fire and rescue personnel member has made significant contributions in fire and rescue work and has demonstrated outstanding moral character and professional competence, the fire and rescue rank may be promoted ahead of schedule.

### 第六章　消防救援衔的保留、降级和取消

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　消防救援人员退休后，其消防救援衔予以保留。  
消防救援人员按照国家规定退出消防救援队伍，或者调离、辞职、被辞退的，其消防救援衔不予保留。

**Article 23** After a fire and rescue personnel member retires, the fire and rescue rank shall be retained.  
Where a fire and rescue personnel member withdraws from the fire and rescue teams in accordance with state regulations, or is transferred, resigns, or is dismissed, the fire and rescue rank shall not be retained.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　消防救援人员因不胜任现任职务被调任下级职务的，其消防救援衔应当调整至相应衔级，调整的批准权限与原衔级的批准权限相同。

**Article 24** Where a fire and rescue personnel member is transferred to a lower position due to incompetence in the current position, the fire and rescue rank shall be adjusted to the corresponding rank level, and the authority for approving the adjustment shall be the same as that for the original rank.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　消防救援人员受到降级、撤职处分的，应当相应降低消防救援衔，降级的批准权限与原衔级的批准权限相同。  
消防救援衔降级不适用于四级指挥员和预备消防士。

**Article 25** Where a fire and rescue personnel member is subjected to a sanction of demotion in rank or removal from position, the fire and rescue rank shall be correspondingly lowered, and the authority for approving the demotion shall be the same as that for the original rank.  
The demotion of a fire and rescue rank shall not apply to Fourth-level Command Officers and Probationary Fire Sergeants.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　消防救援人员受到开除处分的，以及因犯罪被依法判处剥夺政治权利或者有期徒刑以上刑罚的，其消防救援衔相应取消。  
消防救援人员退休后犯罪的，适用前款规定。

**Article 26** Where a fire and rescue personnel member is subjected to a sanction of expulsion, or is sentenced according to law to deprivation of political rights or to fixed-term imprisonment or a heavier punishment for a crime, the fire and rescue rank shall be correspondingly cancelled.  
Where a fire and rescue personnel member commits a crime after retirement, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　消防救援衔标志式样和佩带办法，由国务院制定。

**Article 27** The design of the insignia of fire and rescue ranks and the methods of wearing them shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本条例自2018年10月27日起施行。

**Article 28** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 27, 2018.
