# 最高人民法院研究室关于如何理解“审判的时候怀孕的妇女不适用死刑”问题的电话答复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：1991-03-18  
**生效日期**：1991-03-18

---

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院研究室  
关于如何理解“审判的时候怀孕的妇女  
不适用死刑”问题的电话答复  
（1991年3月18日）  
广东省高级人民法院：  
你院〔1990〕粤法刑一文字第16号《关于如何理解“审判的时候怀孕的妇女不适用死刑”问题的请示》已收悉。经研究，现答复如下：  
在羁押期间已是孕妇的被告人，无论其怀孕是否属于违反国家计划生育政策，也不论其是否自然流产或者经人工流产以及流产后移送起诉或审判期间的长短，仍应执行我院〔83〕法研字第18号《关于人民法院审判严重刑事犯罪案件中具体应用法律的若干问题的答复》中对第三个问题的答复：“对于这类案件，应当按照[刑法第四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-44)和[刑事诉讼法第一百五十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-154)的规定办理，即：人民法院对‘审判的时候怀孕的妇女，不适用死刑’。如果人民法院在审判时发现，在羁押受审时已是孕妇的，仍应依照上述法律规定，不适用死刑。”

**Article 1** Research Office of the Supreme People's Court  
Telephone Reply on How to Understand the Issue of "The Death Penalty Shall Not Apply to Women Who Are Pregnant at the Time of Adjudication"  
(March 18, 1991)  
Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
Your court's Document No. [1990] Yue Fa Xing Yi Wen Zi No. 16, titled "Request for Instructions on How to Understand the Issue of 'The Death Penalty Shall Not Apply to Women Who Are Pregnant at the Time of Adjudication'" has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
For a defendant who is already pregnant during the period of custody, regardless of whether her pregnancy violates the national family planning policy, and regardless of whether she has a natural miscarriage or induced abortion, and regardless of the length of time between the abortion and the transfer for prosecution or adjudication, the answer to the third question in the Reply of Our Court Document No. [1983] Fa Yan Zi No. 18, titled "Reply on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Adjudication of Serious Criminal Cases by People's Courts" shall still be implemented: "For such cases, the provisions of Article 44 of the Criminal Law and Article 154 of the Criminal Procedure Law shall be applied, i.e., the People's Court 'shall not apply the death penalty to a woman who is pregnant at the time of adjudication.' If the People's Court discovers during adjudication that the defendant was already pregnant at the time of custody for trial, it shall still, in accordance with the above legal provisions, not apply the death penalty."
