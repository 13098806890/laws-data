# 中华人民共和国未成年人保护法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2024-04-26  
**生效日期**：2024-04-26

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护未成年人身心健康，保障未成年人合法权益，促进未成年人德智体美劳全面发展，培养有理想、有道德、有文化、有纪律的社会主义建设者和接班人，培养担当民族复兴大任的时代新人，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution to protect the physical and mental health of minors, safeguard the lawful rights and interests of minors, promote the comprehensive development of minors in morality, intelligence, physical fitness, aesthetics, and labor, cultivate socialist builders and successors who have ideals, moral integrity, knowledge, and a sense of discipline, and train new generations to shoulder the great task of national rejuvenation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称未成年人是指未满十八周岁的公民。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, minors refer to citizens under the age of eighteen.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家保障未成年人的生存权、发展权、受保护权、参与权等权利。  
未成年人依法平等地享有各项权利，不因本人及其父母或者其他监护人的民族、种族、性别、户籍、职业、宗教信仰、教育程度、家庭状况、身心健康状况等受到歧视。

**Article 3** The State shall guarantee minors the rights to survival, development, protection, and participation, among others.  
Minors shall enjoy all rights equally according to law and shall not be discriminated against on account of their own or their parents' or other guardians' ethnicity, race, gender, household registration, occupation, religious belief, education level, family status, or physical or mental health condition.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　保护未成年人，应当坚持最有利于未成年人的原则。处理涉及未成年人事项，应当符合下列要求：  
（一）给予未成年人特殊、优先保护；  
（二）尊重未成年人人格尊严；  
（三）保护未成年人隐私权和个人信息；  
（四）适应未成年人身心健康发展的规律和特点；  
（五）听取未成年人的意见；  
（六）保护与教育相结合。

**Article 4** The protection of minors shall adhere to the principle of the best interests of the minor. Matters involving minors shall be handled in accordance with the following requirements:  
(1) granting special and priority protection to minors;  
(2) respecting the personal dignity of minors;  
(3) protecting the privacy rights and personal information of minors;  
(4) adapting to the laws and characteristics of minors' physical and mental development;  
(5) listening to the opinions of minors;  
(6) combining protection with education.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家、社会、学校和家庭应当对未成年人进行理想教育、道德教育、科学教育、文化教育、法治教育、国家安全教育、健康教育、劳动教育，加强爱国主义、集体主义和中国特色社会主义的教育，培养爱祖国、爱人民、爱劳动、爱科学、爱社会主义的公德，抵制资本主义、封建主义和其他腐朽思想的侵蚀，引导未成年人树立和践行社会主义核心价值观。

**Article 5** The State, society, schools, and families shall conduct education on ideals, morality, science, culture, rule of law, national security, health, and labor for minors, strengthen education on patriotism, collectivism, and socialism with Chinese characteristics, foster public virtues of loving the motherland, the people, labor, science, and socialism, resist the erosion of capitalist, feudal, and other decadent ideas, and guide minors to establish and practice the core socialist values.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　保护未成年人，是国家机关、武装力量、政党、人民团体、企业事业单位、社会组织、城乡基层群众性自治组织、未成年人的监护人以及其他成年人的共同责任。  
国家、社会、学校和家庭应当教育和帮助未成年人维护自身合法权益，增强自我保护的意识和能力。

**Article 6** The protection of minors is the common responsibility of state organs, armed forces, political parties, people's organizations, enterprises and institutions, social organizations, urban and rural grassroots self-governing mass organizations, guardians of minors, and other adults.  
The State, society, schools, and families shall educate and assist minors in safeguarding their own lawful rights and interests and enhance their awareness and ability of self-protection.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人依法对未成年人承担监护职责。  
国家采取措施指导、支持、帮助和监督未成年人的父母或者其他监护人履行监护职责。

**Article 7** The parents or other guardians of a minor shall undertake guardianship duties for the minor according to law.  
The State shall take measures to guide, support, assist, and supervise the parents or other guardians of minors in performing their guardianship duties.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上人民政府应当将未成年人保护工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，相关经费纳入本级政府预算。

**Article 8** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the work of protecting minors into the national economic and social development plans, and include the relevant expenditures in the budgets of the governments at the same level.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各级人民政府应当重视和加强未成年人保护工作。县级以上人民政府负责妇女儿童工作的机构，负责未成年人保护工作的组织、协调、指导、督促，有关部门在各自职责范围内做好相关工作。

**Article 9** People's governments at all levels shall attach importance to and strengthen the work of protecting minors. The institutions responsible for women and children affairs under the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing, coordinating, guiding, and supervising the work of protecting minors, and relevant departments shall perform their related work within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　共产主义青年团、妇女联合会、工会、残疾人联合会、关心下一代工作委员会、青年联合会、学生联合会、少年先锋队以及其他人民团体、有关社会组织，应当协助各级人民政府及其有关部门、人民检察院、人民法院做好未成年人保护工作，维护未成年人合法权益。

**Article 10** The Communist Youth League, Women's Federation, trade unions, Disabled Persons' Federation, Working Committee for Caring for the Next Generation, Youth Federation, Student Federation, Young Pioneers, and other people's organizations and relevant social organizations shall assist people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments, people's procuratorates, and people's courts in properly carrying out the work of protecting minors and safeguarding the lawful rights and interests of minors.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　任何组织或者个人发现不利于未成年人身心健康或者侵犯未成年人合法权益的情形，都有权劝阻、制止或者向公安、民政、教育等有关部门提出检举、控告。  
国家机关、居民委员会、村民委员会、密切接触未成年人的单位及其工作人员，在工作中发现未成年人身心健康受到侵害、疑似受到侵害或者面临其他危险情形的，应当立即向公安、民政、教育等有关部门报告。  
有关部门接到涉及未成年人的检举、控告或者报告，应当依法及时受理、处置，并以适当方式将处理结果告知相关单位和人员。

**Article 11** Any organization or individual that discovers circumstances detrimental to the physical or mental health of minors or infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of minors shall have the right to dissuade or stop such conduct, or to lodge a complaint or accusation with the public security, civil affairs, education, or other relevant departments.  
State organs, residents' committees, villagers' committees, entities that have close contact with minors, and their staff members, who discover in the course of their work that the physical or mental health of a minor has been infringed upon, is suspected of being infringed upon, or that the minor is facing other dangerous situations, shall immediately report to the public security, civil affairs, education, or other relevant departments.  
Upon receiving a complaint, accusation, or report concerning a minor, the relevant department shall accept and handle it in a timely manner according to law, and inform the relevant entities and individuals of the handling results in an appropriate manner.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家鼓励和支持未成年人保护方面的科学研究，建设相关学科、设置相关专业，加强人才培养。

**Article 12** The State shall encourage and support scientific research on the protection of minors, build relevant disciplines, establish relevant majors, and strengthen the training of personnel.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家建立健全未成年人统计调查制度，开展未成年人健康、受教育等状况的统计、调查和分析，发布未成年人保护的有关信息。

**Article 13** The State shall establish and improve the statistical survey system for minors, conduct statistics, surveys, and analyses of the health, education, and other conditions of minors, and publish relevant information on the protection of minors.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家对保护未成年人有显著成绩的组织和个人给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 14** The State shall commend and reward organizations and individuals that have achieved outstanding results in protecting minors.

### 第二章　家庭保护

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人应当学习家庭教育知识，接受家庭教育指导，创造良好、和睦、文明的家庭环境。  
共同生活的其他成年家庭成员应当协助未成年人的父母或者其他监护人抚养、教育和保护未成年人。

**Article 15** Parents or other guardians of minors shall learn knowledge of family education, receive guidance on family education, and create a good, harmonious, and civilized family environment.  
Other adult family members living together shall assist the parents or other guardians of minors in raising, educating, and protecting the minors.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人应当履行下列监护职责：  
（一）为未成年人提供生活、健康、安全等方面的保障；  
（二）关注未成年人的生理、心理状况和情感需求；  
（三）教育和引导未成年人遵纪守法、勤俭节约，养成良好的思想品德和行为习惯；  
（四）对未成年人进行安全教育，提高未成年人的自我保护意识和能力；  
（五）尊重未成年人受教育的权利，保障适龄未成年人依法接受并完成义务教育；  
（六）保障未成年人休息、娱乐和体育锻炼的时间，引导未成年人进行有益身心健康的活动；  
（七）妥善管理和保护未成年人的财产；  
（八）依法代理未成年人实施民事法律行为；  
（九）预防和制止未成年人的不良行为和违法犯罪行为，并进行合理管教；  
（十）其他应当履行的监护职责。

**Article 16** Parents or other guardians of minors shall perform the following guardianship duties:  
(1) providing the minor with guarantees in terms of living, health, safety, etc.;  
(2) paying attention to the minor's physical and psychological condition and emotional needs;  
(3) educating and guiding the minor to abide by laws and regulations, be diligent and thrifty, and develop good moral character and behavioral habits;  
(4) conducting safety education for the minor and enhancing the minor's awareness and ability of self-protection;  
(5) respecting the minor's right to education and ensuring that the minor of school age receives and completes compulsory education according to law;  
(6) ensuring the minor's time for rest, recreation, and physical exercise, and guiding the minor to engage in activities beneficial to physical and mental health;  
(7) properly managing and protecting the minor's property;  
(8) acting as agent for the minor in civil juristic acts according to law;  
(9) preventing and stopping the minor's misconduct and illegal or criminal acts, and exercising reasonable discipline;  
(10) other guardianship duties that shall be performed.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人不得实施下列行为：  
（一）虐待、遗弃、非法送养未成年人或者对未成年人实施家庭暴力；  
（二）放任、教唆或者利用未成年人实施违法犯罪行为；  
（三）放任、唆使未成年人参与邪教、迷信活动或者接受恐怖主义、分裂主义、极端主义等侵害；  
（四）放任、唆使未成年人吸烟（含电子烟，下同）、饮酒、赌博、流浪乞讨或者欺凌他人；  
（五）放任或者迫使应当接受义务教育的未成年人失学、辍学；  
（六）放任未成年人沉迷网络，接触危害或者可能影响其身心健康的图书、报刊、电影、广播电视节目、音像制品、电子出版物和网络信息等；  
（七）放任未成年人进入营业性娱乐场所、酒吧、互联网上网服务营业场所等不适宜未成年人活动的场所；  
（八）允许或者迫使未成年人从事国家规定以外的劳动；  
（九）允许、迫使未成年人结婚或者为未成年人订立婚约；  
（十）违法处分、侵吞未成年人的财产或者利用未成年人牟取不正当利益；  
（十一）其他侵犯未成年人身心健康、财产权益或者不依法履行未成年人保护义务的行为。

**Article 17** Parents or other guardians of minors shall not commit any of the following acts:  
(1) maltreating, abandoning, illegally placing out for adoption, or committing domestic violence against a minor;  
(2) conniving at, instigating, or using a minor to commit illegal or criminal acts;  
(3) conniving at or inducing a minor to participate in cult, superstitious activities, or to suffer from harm such as terrorism, separatism, or extremism;  
(4) conniving at or inducing a minor to smoke (including e-cigarettes, the same below), drink alcohol, gamble, wander around begging, or bully others;  
(5) conniving at or forcing a minor who should receive compulsory education to become truant or drop out of school;  
(6) conniving at a minor's addiction to the internet, exposure to books, newspapers, films, radio and television programs, audio-visual products, electronic publications, online information, etc. that are harmful or may affect his or her physical and mental health;  
(7) conniving at a minor entering places such as commercial entertainment venues, bars, internet service business places, etc. which are not suitable for minors' activities;  
(8) allowing or forcing a minor to engage in labor other than that prescribed by the State;  
(9) allowing or forcing a minor to marry or entering into a betrothal agreement for a minor;  
(10) illegally disposing of or embezzling a minor's property or using a minor to seek illegitimate interests;  
(11) other acts that infringe upon the physical and mental health or property rights and interests of a minor, or fail to perform the obligation of protecting minors according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人应当为未成年人提供安全的家庭生活环境，及时排除引发触电、烫伤、跌落等伤害的安全隐患；采取配备儿童安全座椅、教育未成年人遵守交通规则等措施，防止未成年人受到交通事故的伤害；提高户外安全保护意识，避免未成年人发生溺水、动物伤害等事故。

**Article 18** Parents or other guardians of minors shall provide a safe family living environment for the minor, promptly eliminate safety hazards that may cause injuries such as electric shock, scalding, and falling; take measures such as equipping child safety seats and educating the minor to obey traffic rules to prevent the minor from being injured in traffic accidents; enhance awareness of outdoor safety protection to prevent the minor from accidents such as drowning and animal attacks.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人应当根据未成年人的年龄和智力发展状况，在作出与未成年人权益有关的决定前，听取未成年人的意见，充分考虑其真实意愿。

**Article 19** Parents or other guardians of minors shall, based on the minor's age and intellectual development, listen to the minor's opinions and fully consider their true wishes before making decisions related to the rights and interests of the minor.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人发现未成年人身心健康受到侵害、疑似受到侵害或者其他合法权益受到侵犯的，应当及时了解情况并采取保护措施；情况严重的，应当立即向公安、民政、教育等部门报告。

**Article 20** Where parents or other guardians of a minor discover that the minor's physical or mental health is being or is suspected of being infringed upon, or that other lawful rights and interests of the minor are being violated, they shall promptly ascertain the situation and take protective measures; if the circumstances are serious, they shall immediately report to the public security, civil affairs, education, or other relevant departments.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人不得使未满八周岁或者由于身体、心理原因需要特别照顾的未成年人处于无人看护状态，或者将其交由无民事行为能力、限制民事行为能力、患有严重传染性疾病或者其他不适宜的人员临时照护。  
未成年人的父母或者其他监护人不得使未满十六周岁的未成年人脱离监护单独生活。

**Article 21** Parents or other guardians of minors shall not leave a minor under the age of eight or a minor who requires special care due to physical or psychological reasons unattended, or entrust temporary care of such a minor to a person who has no capacity for civil conduct, has limited capacity for civil conduct, suffers from a serious infectious disease, or is otherwise unsuitable.  
Parents or other guardians of minors shall not allow a minor under the age of sixteen to live apart from guardianship alone.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人因外出务工等原因在一定期限内不能完全履行监护职责的，应当委托具有照护能力的完全民事行为能力人代为照护；无正当理由的，不得委托他人代为照护。  
未成年人的父母或者其他监护人在确定被委托人时，应当综合考虑其道德品质、家庭状况、身心健康状况、与未成年人生活情感上的联系等情况，并听取有表达意愿能力未成年人的意见。  
具有下列情形之一的，不得作为被委托人：  
（一）曾实施性侵害、虐待、遗弃、拐卖、暴力伤害等违法犯罪行为；  
（二）有吸毒、酗酒、赌博等恶习；  
（三）曾拒不履行或者长期怠于履行监护、照护职责；  
（四）其他不适宜担任被委托人的情形。

**Article 22** Where parents or other guardians of a minor are unable to fully perform their guardianship duties for a certain period due to reasons such as working away from home, they shall entrust the care of the minor to a person with full capacity for civil conduct who has the ability to provide care; without justifiable reasons, they shall not entrust care to another person.  
When determining the entrusted person, the parents or other guardians of the minor shall comprehensively consider factors such as the entrusted person's moral character, family status, physical and mental health, and emotional connection with the minor, and shall solicit the opinions of the minor who has the ability to express their will.  
A person shall not be entrusted under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) having previously committed illegal or criminal acts such as sexual assault, abuse, abandonment, trafficking, or violent injury;  
(2) having bad habits such as drug use, alcohol abuse, or gambling;  
(3) having previously refused or long neglected to perform guardianship or care duties;  
(4) other circumstances that make the person unsuitable to be entrusted.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人应当及时将委托照护情况书面告知未成年人所在学校、幼儿园和实际居住地的居民委员会、村民委员会，加强和未成年人所在学校、幼儿园的沟通；与未成年人、被委托人至少每周联系和交流一次，了解未成年人的生活、学习、心理等情况，并给予未成年人亲情关爱。  
未成年人的父母或者其他监护人接到被委托人、居民委员会、村民委员会、学校、幼儿园等关于未成年人心理、行为异常的通知后，应当及时采取干预措施。

**Article 23** Parents or other guardians of a minor shall promptly inform in writing the school, kindergarten, and the residents' committee or villagers' committee of the minor's actual place of residence of the entrusted care arrangement, strengthen communication with the school and kindergarten attended by the minor; and shall contact and communicate with the minor and the entrusted person at least once a week to learn about the minor's life, study, and psychological condition, and provide the minor with family care and affection.  
Upon receiving a notice from the entrusted person, the residents' committee, villagers' committee, school, or kindergarten regarding abnormal psychological or behavioral conditions of the minor, the parents or other guardians of the minor shall promptly take intervention measures.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　未成年人的父母离婚时，应当妥善处理未成年子女的抚养、教育、探望、财产等事宜，听取有表达意愿能力未成年人的意见。不得以抢夺、藏匿未成年子女等方式争夺抚养权。  
未成年人的父母离婚后，不直接抚养未成年子女的一方应当依照协议、人民法院判决或者调解确定的时间和方式，在不影响未成年人学习、生活的情况下探望未成年子女，直接抚养的一方应当配合，但被人民法院依法中止探望权的除外。

**Article 24** When the parents of a minor divorce, they shall properly handle matters concerning the minor child's upbringing, education, visitation, and property, and shall solicit the opinions of the minor who has the ability to express their will. They shall not compete for custody by means such as snatching or hiding the minor child.  
After the parents of a minor divorce, the parent who does not directly raise the minor child shall visit the minor child in accordance with the time and manner determined by agreement, judgment of the People's Court, or mediation, without affecting the minor's study and life, and the parent who directly raises the child shall cooperate, except where the right of visitation has been suspended by the People's Court according to law.

### 第三章　学校保护

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　学校应当全面贯彻国家教育方针，坚持立德树人，实施素质教育，提高教育质量，注重培养未成年学生认知能力、合作能力、创新能力和实践能力，促进未成年学生全面发展。  
学校应当建立未成年学生保护工作制度，健全学生行为规范，培养未成年学生遵纪守法的良好行为习惯。

**Article 25** Schools shall fully implement the national education policy, uphold moral education and cultivate people, carry out quality education, improve the quality of education, and focus on fostering minor students' cognitive abilities, cooperative abilities, innovative abilities, and practical abilities, promoting the all-round development of minor students.  
Schools shall establish a system for the protection of minor students, improve student behavior standards, and cultivate good conduct habits of minor students in abiding by laws and disciplines.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　幼儿园应当做好保育、教育工作，遵循幼儿身心发展规律，实施启蒙教育，促进幼儿在体质、智力、品德等方面和谐发展。

**Article 26** Kindergartens shall properly carry out childcare and educational work, follow the laws of young children's physical and mental development, provide initiation education, and promote the harmonious development of young children in terms of physical fitness, intelligence, and moral character.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　学校、幼儿园的教职员工应当尊重未成年人人格尊严，不得对未成年人实施体罚、变相体罚或者其他侮辱人格尊严的行为。

**Article 27** Teaching and administrative staff of schools and kindergartens shall respect the dignity of minors and shall not subject minors to corporal punishment, disguised corporal punishment, or any other act that insults their personal dignity.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　学校应当保障未成年学生受教育的权利，不得违反国家规定开除、变相开除未成年学生。  
学校应当对尚未完成义务教育的辍学未成年学生进行登记并劝返复学；劝返无效的，应当及时向教育行政部门书面报告。

**Article 28** Schools shall protect the right of minor students to receive education, and shall not expel or disguisedly expel minor students in violation of state regulations.  
Schools shall register minor students who have dropped out before completing compulsory education and shall persuade them to return to school; if persuasion is ineffective, the school shall promptly report in writing to the education administrative department.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　学校应当关心、爱护未成年学生，不得因家庭、身体、心理、学习能力等情况歧视学生。对家庭困难、身心有障碍的学生，应当提供关爱；对行为异常、学习有困难的学生，应当耐心帮助。  
学校应当配合政府有关部门建立留守未成年学生、困境未成年学生的信息档案，开展关爱帮扶工作。

**Article 29** Schools shall care for and show affection to minor students, and shall not discriminate against students due to their family background, physical or mental conditions, learning abilities, or other circumstances. Schools shall provide care to students from families with financial difficulties or with physical or mental disabilities; schools shall patiently help students with behavioral abnormalities or learning difficulties.  
Schools shall cooperate with relevant government departments to establish information files for left-behind minor students and minor students in difficult circumstances, and carry out care and assistance work.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　学校应当根据未成年学生身心发展特点，进行社会生活指导、心理健康辅导、青春期教育和生命教育。

**Article 30** Schools shall, based on the characteristics of minor students' physical and mental development, provide guidance on social life, psychological health counseling, puberty education, and life education.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　学校应当组织未成年学生参加与其年龄相适应的日常生活劳动、生产劳动和服务性劳动，帮助未成年学生掌握必要的劳动知识和技能，养成良好的劳动习惯。

**Article 31** Schools shall organize minor students to participate in daily life labor, productive labor, and service labor that are appropriate to their age, help minor students acquire necessary labor knowledge and skills, and cultivate good labor habits.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　学校、幼儿园应当开展勤俭节约、反对浪费、珍惜粮食、文明饮食等宣传教育活动，帮助未成年人树立浪费可耻、节约为荣的意识，养成文明健康、绿色环保的生活习惯。

**Article 32** Schools and kindergartens shall carry out publicity and education activities on diligence and thrift, opposing waste, cherishing food, and civilized eating, to help minors establish an awareness that waste is shameful and thrift is honorable, and to cultivate civilized, healthy, green, and environmentally friendly living habits.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　学校应当与未成年学生的父母或者其他监护人互相配合，合理安排未成年学生的学习时间，保障其休息、娱乐和体育锻炼的时间。  
学校不得占用国家法定节假日、休息日及寒暑假期，组织义务教育阶段的未成年学生集体补课，加重其学习负担。  
幼儿园、校外培训机构不得对学龄前未成年人进行小学课程教育。

**Article 33** Schools shall cooperate with the parents or other guardians of minor students to reasonably arrange the study time of minor students and ensure their time for rest, recreation, and physical exercise.  
Schools shall not occupy national statutory holidays, rest days, or winter and summer vacations to organize collective make-up classes for minor students in compulsory education, thereby increasing their study burden.  
Kindergartens and after-school training institutions shall not provide primary school curriculum education to preschool minors.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　学校、幼儿园应当提供必要的卫生保健条件，协助卫生健康部门做好在校、在园未成年人的卫生保健工作。

**Article 34** Schools and kindergartens shall provide necessary health care conditions and assist the health departments in carrying out health care work for minors in schools and kindergartens.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　学校、幼儿园应当建立安全管理制度，对未成年人进行安全教育，完善安保设施、配备安保人员，保障未成年人在校、在园期间的人身和财产安全。  
学校、幼儿园不得在危及未成年人人身安全、身心健康的校舍和其他设施、场所中进行教育教学活动。  
学校、幼儿园安排未成年人参加文化娱乐、社会实践等集体活动，应当保护未成年人的身心健康，防止发生人身伤害事故。

**Article 35** Schools and kindergartens shall establish a safety management system, provide safety education to minors, improve security facilities, and deploy security personnel to ensure the personal and property safety of minors during their stay in schools and kindergartens.  
Schools and kindergartens shall not conduct educational and teaching activities in school buildings or other facilities or places that endanger the personal safety and physical and mental health of minors.  
When arranging collective activities such as cultural entertainment and social practice for minors, schools and kindergartens shall protect the physical and mental health of minors and prevent personal injury accidents.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　使用校车的学校、幼儿园应当建立健全校车安全管理制度，配备安全管理人员，定期对校车进行安全检查，对校车驾驶人进行安全教育，并向未成年人讲解校车安全乘坐知识，培养未成年人校车安全事故应急处理技能。

**Article 36** Schools and kindergartens that use school buses shall establish and improve a school bus safety management system, deploy safety management personnel, conduct regular safety inspections of school buses, provide safety education to school bus drivers, and explain to minors the knowledge of safe riding on school buses, and cultivate minors' emergency response skills for school bus safety accidents.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　学校、幼儿园应当根据需要，制定应对自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件等突发事件和意外伤害的预案，配备相应设施并定期进行必要的演练。  
未成年人在校内、园内或者本校、本园组织的校外、园外活动中发生人身伤害事故的，学校、幼儿园应当立即救护，妥善处理，及时通知未成年人的父母或者其他监护人，并向有关部门报告。

**Article 37** Schools and kindergartens shall, as needed, formulate plans for responding to emergencies such as natural disasters, accident disasters, public health incidents, and accidental injuries, equip corresponding facilities, and conduct necessary drills on a regular basis.  
If a personal injury accident occurs to a minor during activities on the school or kindergarten premises or during off-premises activities organized by the school or kindergarten, the school or kindergarten shall immediately provide first aid, handle the matter properly, promptly notify the minor's parents or other guardians, and report to the relevant authorities.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　学校、幼儿园不得安排未成年人参加商业性活动，不得向未成年人及其父母或者其他监护人推销或者要求其购买指定的商品和服务。  
学校、幼儿园不得与校外培训机构合作为未成年人提供有偿课程辅导。

**Article 38** Schools and kindergartens shall not arrange for minors to participate in commercial activities, nor shall they promote or require minors and their parents or other guardians to purchase designated goods or services.  
Schools and kindergartens shall not cooperate with after-school training institutions to provide paid course tutoring to minors.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　学校应当建立学生欺凌防控工作制度，对教职员工、学生等开展防治学生欺凌的教育和培训。  
学校对学生欺凌行为应当立即制止，通知实施欺凌和被欺凌未成年学生的父母或者其他监护人参与欺凌行为的认定和处理；对相关未成年学生及时给予心理辅导、教育和引导；对相关未成年学生的父母或者其他监护人给予必要的家庭教育指导。  
对实施欺凌的未成年学生，学校应当根据欺凌行为的性质和程度，依法加强管教。对严重的欺凌行为，学校不得隐瞒，应当及时向公安机关、教育行政部门报告，并配合相关部门依法处理。

**Article 39** Schools shall establish a work system for the prevention and control of student bullying, and carry out education and training on the prevention and handling of student bullying for teaching staff and students.  
Schools shall immediately stop bullying behavior, notify the parents or other guardians of the minor students who committed the bullying and those who were bullied to participate in the identification and handling of the bullying behavior; provide timely psychological counseling, education, and guidance to the relevant minor students; and provide necessary family education guidance to the parents or other guardians of the relevant minor students.  
For minor students who commit bullying, schools shall strengthen discipline in accordance with law based on the nature and severity of the bullying behavior. For serious bullying behavior, schools shall not conceal it, shall promptly report it to the public security organ and the education administrative department, and shall cooperate with the relevant departments in handling it according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　学校、幼儿园应当建立预防性侵害、性骚扰未成年人工作制度。对性侵害、性骚扰未成年人等违法犯罪行为，学校、幼儿园不得隐瞒，应当及时向公安机关、教育行政部门报告，并配合相关部门依法处理。  
学校、幼儿园应当对未成年人开展适合其年龄的性教育，提高未成年人防范性侵害、性骚扰的自我保护意识和能力。对遭受性侵害、性骚扰的未成年人，学校、幼儿园应当及时采取相关的保护措施。

**Article 40** Schools and kindergartens shall establish a work system for the prevention of sexual assault and sexual harassment of minors. Schools and kindergartens shall not conceal illegal or criminal acts such as sexual assault or sexual harassment of minors, shall promptly report them to the public security organ and the education administrative department, and shall cooperate with the relevant departments in handling them according to law.  
Schools and kindergartens shall provide age-appropriate sex education to minors, enhancing minors' self-protection awareness and ability to prevent sexual assault and sexual harassment. For minors who have suffered sexual assault or sexual harassment, schools and kindergartens shall promptly take relevant protective measures.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　婴幼儿照护服务机构、早期教育服务机构、校外培训机构、校外托管机构等应当参照本章有关规定，根据不同年龄阶段未成年人的成长特点和规律，做好未成年人保护工作。

**Article 41** Infant and toddler care service institutions, early education service institutions, after-school training institutions, after-school care institutions, and the like shall, by reference to the relevant provisions of this Chapter, and in light of the growth characteristics and patterns of minors at different ages, properly carry out the work of protecting minors.

### 第四章　社会保护

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　全社会应当树立关心、爱护未成年人的良好风尚。  
国家鼓励、支持和引导人民团体、企业事业单位、社会组织以及其他组织和个人，开展有利于未成年人健康成长的社会活动和服务。

**Article 42** The whole society shall foster a healthy atmosphere of caring for and protecting minors.  
The State encourages, supports and guides people's organizations, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations and other organizations and individuals to carry out social activities and services that are conducive to the healthy growth of minors.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　居民委员会、村民委员会应当设置专人专岗负责未成年人保护工作，协助政府有关部门宣传未成年人保护方面的法律法规，指导、帮助和监督未成年人的父母或者其他监护人依法履行监护职责，建立留守未成年人、困境未成年人的信息档案并给予关爱帮扶。  
居民委员会、村民委员会应当协助政府有关部门监督未成年人委托照护情况，发现被委托人缺乏照护能力、怠于履行照护职责等情况，应当及时向政府有关部门报告，并告知未成年人的父母或者其他监护人，帮助、督促被委托人履行照护职责。

**Article 43** Neighborhood committees and villagers committees shall establish dedicated personnel and posts to be responsible for the protection of minors, assist relevant government departments in publicizing laws and regulations on the protection of minors, guide, help and supervise the parents or other guardians of minors to perform their guardianship duties according to law, establish information files on minors left behind and minors in distress, and provide care and assistance.  
Neighborhood committees and villagers committees shall assist relevant government departments in supervising the entrusted care of minors. If they discover that the entrusted person lacks care capacity or is negligent in performing care duties, they shall promptly report to the relevant government departments, inform the parents or other guardians of the minor, and help and urge the entrusted person to perform the care duties.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　爱国主义教育基地、图书馆、青少年宫、儿童活动中心、儿童之家应当对未成年人免费开放；博物馆、纪念馆、科技馆、展览馆、美术馆、文化馆、社区公益性互联网上网服务场所以及影剧院、体育场馆、动物园、植物园、公园等场所，应当按照有关规定对未成年人免费或者优惠开放。  
国家鼓励爱国主义教育基地、博物馆、科技馆、美术馆等公共场馆开设未成年人专场，为未成年人提供有针对性的服务。  
国家鼓励国家机关、企业事业单位、部队等开发自身教育资源，设立未成年人开放日，为未成年人主题教育、社会实践、职业体验等提供支持。  
国家鼓励科研机构和科技类社会组织对未成年人开展科学普及活动。

**Article 44** Patriotic education bases, libraries, children's palaces, children's activity centers, and children's homes shall be open to minors free of charge; museums, memorials, science and technology museums, exhibition halls, art galleries, cultural centers, community-based public Internet access service venues, as well as cinemas, theaters, sports venues, zoos, botanical gardens, parks, and other such places shall be open to minors free of charge or at a discount in accordance with relevant regulations.  
The State encourages patriotic education bases, museums, science and technology museums, art galleries, and other public venues to hold special sessions for minors and provide targeted services to minors.  
The State encourages State organs, enterprises, public institutions, the armed forces, and others to develop their own educational resources, establish open days for minors, and provide support for thematic education, social practice, career experience, etc. for minors.  
The State encourages scientific research institutions and science-related social organizations to carry out science popularization activities for minors.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　城市公共交通以及公路、铁路、水路、航空客运等应当按照有关规定对未成年人实施免费或者优惠票价。

**Article 45** Urban public transport, as well as road, railway, waterway, and air passenger transport, shall, in accordance with relevant regulations, provide free or discounted fares for minors.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国家鼓励大型公共场所、公共交通工具、旅游景区景点等设置母婴室、婴儿护理台以及方便幼儿使用的坐便器、洗手台等卫生设施，为未成年人提供便利。

**Article 46** The State encourages large public places, public transport vehicles, tourist attractions and scenic spots, etc., to set up baby care rooms, baby changing tables, as well as sanitary facilities such as toilets and washbasins convenient for young children, so as to provide convenience for minors.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　任何组织或者个人不得违反有关规定，限制未成年人应当享有的照顾或者优惠。

**Article 47** No organization or individual may, in violation of relevant regulations, restrict the care or preferential treatment that minors are entitled to.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家鼓励创作、出版、制作和传播有利于未成年人健康成长的图书、报刊、电影、广播电视节目、舞台艺术作品、音像制品、电子出版物和网络信息等。

**Article 48** The State encourages the creation, publication, production and dissemination of books, newspapers, periodicals, films, radio and television programs, stage works, audio and video products, electronic publications, and online information that are conducive to the healthy growth of minors.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　新闻媒体应当加强未成年人保护方面的宣传，对侵犯未成年人合法权益的行为进行舆论监督。新闻媒体采访报道涉及未成年人事件应当客观、审慎和适度，不得侵犯未成年人的名誉、隐私和其他合法权益。

**Article 49** News media shall strengthen publicity on the protection of minors and exercise public opinion supervision over acts that infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of minors. When news media interview and report on incidents involving minors, they shall act objectively, prudently and appropriately, and shall not infringe upon the reputation, privacy or other lawful rights and interests of minors.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　禁止制作、复制、出版、发布、传播含有宣扬淫秽、色情、暴力、邪教、迷信、赌博、引诱自杀、恐怖主义、分裂主义、极端主义等危害未成年人身心健康内容的图书、报刊、电影、广播电视节目、舞台艺术作品、音像制品、电子出版物和网络信息等。

**Article 50** It is prohibited to produce, reproduce, publish, release or disseminate books, newspapers, periodicals, films, radio and television programs, stage works, audio and video products, electronic publications and online information that contain content advocating obscenity, pornography, violence, cults, superstition, gambling, inducing suicide, terrorism, separatism, extremism, or other content harmful to the physical and mental health of minors.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　任何组织或者个人出版、发布、传播的图书、报刊、电影、广播电视节目、舞台艺术作品、音像制品、电子出版物或者网络信息，包含可能影响未成年人身心健康内容的，应当以显著方式作出提示。

**Article 51** Any organization or individual that publishes, releases or disseminates books, newspapers, periodicals, films, radio and television programs, stage works, audio and video products, electronic publications or online information containing content that may affect the physical and mental health of minors shall give a prominent warning.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　禁止制作、复制、发布、传播或者持有有关未成年人的淫秽色情物品和网络信息。

**Article 52** It is prohibited to produce, reproduce, release, disseminate or possess pornographic and obscene articles and online information concerning minors.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　任何组织或者个人不得刊登、播放、张贴或者散发含有危害未成年人身心健康内容的广告；不得在学校、幼儿园播放、张贴或者散发商业广告；不得利用校服、教材等发布或者变相发布商业广告。

**Article 53** No organization or individual may publish, broadcast, display or distribute advertisements containing content harmful to the physical and mental health of minors; no commercial advertisements may be broadcast, displayed or distributed in schools or kindergartens; no commercial advertisements may be published or published in disguised form through school uniforms, teaching materials, etc.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　禁止拐卖、绑架、虐待、非法收养未成年人，禁止对未成年人实施性侵害、性骚扰。  
禁止胁迫、引诱、教唆未成年人参加黑社会性质组织或者从事违法犯罪活动。  
禁止胁迫、诱骗、利用未成年人乞讨。

**Article 54** It is prohibited to abduct, kidnap, abuse or illegally adopt minors, and to commit sexual assault or sexual harassment against minors.  
It is prohibited to coerce, entice or instigate minors to participate in organized crime groups or to engage in illegal or criminal activities.  
It is prohibited to coerce, deceive or use minors to beg.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　生产、销售用于未成年人的食品、药品、玩具、用具和游戏游艺设备、游乐设施等，应当符合国家或者行业标准，不得危害未成年人的人身安全和身心健康。上述产品的生产者应当在显著位置标明注意事项，未标明注意事项的不得销售。

**Article 55** The production and sale of food, drugs, toys, utensils, game and amusement equipment, playground facilities, etc. for minors shall comply with national or industry standards and shall not endanger the personal safety and physical and mental health of minors. The producers of the above-mentioned products shall indicate precautions in a prominent position; those that do not indicate precautions shall not be sold.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　未成年人集中活动的公共场所应当符合国家或者行业安全标准，并采取相应安全保护措施。对可能存在安全风险的设施，应当定期进行维护，在显著位置设置安全警示标志并标明适龄范围和注意事项；必要时应当安排专门人员看管。  
大型的商场、超市、医院、图书馆、博物馆、科技馆、游乐场、车站、码头、机场、旅游景区景点等场所运营单位应当设置搜寻走失未成年人的安全警报系统。场所运营单位接到求助后，应当立即启动安全警报系统，组织人员进行搜寻并向公安机关报告。  
公共场所发生突发事件时，应当优先救护未成年人。

**Article 56** Public places where minors gather shall meet national or industry safety standards and take corresponding safety protection measures. Facilities that may pose safety risks shall be regularly maintained, safety warning signs shall be set up in prominent positions, and the age-appropriate range and precautions shall be indicated; when necessary, dedicated personnel shall be arranged to supervise.  
Operators of large shopping malls, supermarkets, hospitals, libraries, museums, science and technology museums, playgrounds, railway stations, docks, airports, tourist attractions and scenic spots, and other such places shall set up safety alarm systems for searching for lost minors. After receiving a request for help, the operator of the place shall immediately activate the safety alarm system, organize personnel to conduct a search, and report to the public security organ.  
When an emergency occurs in a public place, minors shall be given priority in rescue.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　旅馆、宾馆、酒店等住宿经营者接待未成年人入住，或者接待未成年人和成年人共同入住时，应当询问父母或者其他监护人的联系方式、入住人员的身份关系等有关情况；发现有违法犯罪嫌疑的，应当立即向公安机关报告，并及时联系未成年人的父母或者其他监护人。

**Article 57** When accommodation operators such as inns, guesthouses, and hotels receive a minor for check-in, or receive a minor and an adult for joint check-in, they shall inquire about the contact information of the parent or other guardian, the identity relationship of the persons checking in, and other relevant information; if they suspect that an illegal or criminal act is involved, they shall immediately report to the public security organ and promptly contact the parent or other guardian of the minor.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　学校、幼儿园周边不得设置营业性娱乐场所、酒吧、互联网上网服务营业场所等不适宜未成年人活动的场所。营业性歌舞娱乐场所、酒吧、互联网上网服务营业场所等不适宜未成年人活动场所的经营者，不得允许未成年人进入；游艺娱乐场所设置的电子游戏设备，除国家法定节假日外，不得向未成年人提供。经营者应当在显著位置设置未成年人禁入、限入标志；对难以判明是否是未成年人的，应当要求其出示身份证件。

**Article 58** No commercial entertainment venues, bars, Internet access service business premises, or other places unsuitable for minors' activities may be established in the vicinity of schools and kindergartens. Operators of commercial song and dance entertainment venues, bars, Internet access service business premises, and other places unsuitable for minors' activities shall not allow minors to enter; electronic game devices set up in amusement entertainment venues shall not be provided to minors except on national statutory holidays. Operators shall set up signs in prominent positions indicating that minors are prohibited or restricted from entering; if it is difficult to determine whether a person is a minor, they shall require that person to produce identification documents.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　学校、幼儿园周边不得设置烟、酒、彩票销售网点。禁止向未成年人销售烟、酒、彩票或者兑付彩票奖金。烟、酒和彩票经营者应当在显著位置设置不向未成年人销售烟、酒或者彩票的标志；对难以判明是否是未成年人的，应当要求其出示身份证件。  
任何人不得在学校、幼儿园和其他未成年人集中活动的公共场所吸烟、饮酒。

**Article 59** No tobacco, alcohol or lottery sales outlets may be established in the vicinity of schools and kindergartens. It is prohibited to sell tobacco, alcohol or lottery tickets to minors, or to cash lottery winnings for minors. Operators of tobacco, alcohol and lottery tickets shall set up signs in prominent positions indicating that tobacco, alcohol or lottery tickets are not sold to minors; if it is difficult to determine whether a person is a minor, they shall require that person to produce identification documents.  
No person may smoke or drink alcohol in schools, kindergartens or other public places where minors gather.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　禁止向未成年人提供、销售管制刀具或者其他可能致人严重伤害的器具等物品。经营者难以判明购买者是否是未成年人的，应当要求其出示身份证件。

**Article 60** It is prohibited to provide or sell controlled knives or other instruments that may cause serious injury, and similar items to minors. If an operator finds it difficult to determine whether a purchaser is a minor, the operator shall require the purchaser to produce identification documents.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　任何组织或者个人不得招用未满十六周岁未成年人，国家另有规定的除外。  
营业性娱乐场所、酒吧、互联网上网服务营业场所等不适宜未成年人活动的场所不得招用已满十六周岁的未成年人。  
招用已满十六周岁未成年人的单位和个人应当执行国家在工种、劳动时间、劳动强度和保护措施等方面的规定，不得安排其从事过重、有毒、有害等危害未成年人身心健康的劳动或者危险作业。  
任何组织或者个人不得组织未成年人进行危害其身心健康的表演等活动。经未成年人的父母或者其他监护人同意，未成年人参与演出、节目制作等活动，活动组织方应当根据国家有关规定，保障未成年人合法权益。

**Article 61** No organization or individual may recruit minors under the age of 16, unless otherwise provided by the state.  
Commercial entertainment venues, bars, internet access service business premises, and other places unsuitable for minors' activities may not recruit minors who have reached the age of 16.  
Units and individuals that recruit minors who have reached the age of 16 shall implement the state's regulations on work types, working hours, labor intensity, and protective measures, and shall not assign them to engage in heavy, toxic, harmful, or other labor or dangerous operations that endanger the physical and mental health of minors.  
No organization or individual may organize minors to engage in performances or other activities that endanger their physical and mental health. Where minors participate in performances, program production, or other activities with the consent of their parents or other guardians, the activity organizer shall, in accordance with relevant state regulations, protect the lawful rights and interests of the minors.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　密切接触未成年人的单位招聘工作人员时，应当向公安机关、人民检察院查询应聘者是否具有性侵害、虐待、拐卖、暴力伤害等违法犯罪记录；发现其具有前述行为记录的，不得录用。  
密切接触未成年人的单位应当每年定期对工作人员是否具有上述违法犯罪记录进行查询。通过查询或者其他方式发现其工作人员具有上述行为的，应当及时解聘。

**Article 62** When recruiting staff members, units that have close contact with minors shall inquire with the public security organs and the People's Procuratorates whether the applicants have any records of illegal or criminal acts such as sexual assault, abuse, trafficking, violent injury, etc.; where it is found that they have records of the aforementioned acts, they shall not be hired.  
Units that have close contact with minors shall conduct annual regular inquiries into whether their staff members have the aforesaid illegal or criminal records. Where it is discovered through inquiries or other means that a staff member has the aforesaid acts, the unit shall promptly dismiss such staff member.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　任何组织或者个人不得隐匿、毁弃、非法删除未成年人的信件、日记、电子邮件或者其他网络通讯内容。  
除下列情形外，任何组织或者个人不得开拆、查阅未成年人的信件、日记、电子邮件或者其他网络通讯内容：  
（一）无民事行为能力未成年人的父母或者其他监护人代未成年人开拆、查阅；  
（二）因国家安全或者追查刑事犯罪依法进行检查；  
（三）紧急情况下为了保护未成年人本人的人身安全。

**Article 63** No organization or individual may conceal, destroy, or illegally delete minors' letters, diaries, e-mails, or other online communication content.  
Except under the following circumstances, no organization or individual may open or inspect minors' letters, diaries, e-mails, or other online communication content:  
(1) Where the parents or other guardians of a minor who has no civil capacity open or inspect the content on behalf of the minor;  
(2) Where an inspection is conducted according to law for reasons of national security or for investigating a criminal offense;  
(3) In an emergency situation, for the purpose of protecting the personal safety of the minor.

### 第五章　网络保护

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　国家、社会、学校和家庭应当加强未成年人网络素养宣传教育，培养和提高未成年人的网络素养，增强未成年人科学、文明、安全、合理使用网络的意识和能力，保障未成年人在网络空间的合法权益。

**Article 64** The state, society, schools, and families shall strengthen the publicity and education on minors' online literacy, cultivate and improve minors' online literacy, enhance minors' awareness and ability to use the internet in a scientific, civilized, safe, and reasonable manner, and protect minors' lawful rights and interests in cyberspace.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国家鼓励和支持有利于未成年人健康成长的网络内容的创作与传播，鼓励和支持专门以未成年人为服务对象、适合未成年人身心健康特点的网络技术、产品、服务的研发、生产和使用。

**Article 65** The state encourages and supports the creation and dissemination of online content that is conducive to the healthy growth of minors, and encourages and supports the research, development, production, and use of network technologies, products, and services that are specifically targeted at minors and suitable for their physical and mental health characteristics.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　网信部门及其他有关部门应当加强对未成年人网络保护工作的监督检查，依法惩处利用网络从事危害未成年人身心健康的活动，为未成年人提供安全、健康的网络环境。

**Article 66** The cyberspace administration departments and other relevant departments shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of the protection of minors online, punish according to law any activities that use the internet to harm the physical and mental health of minors, and provide a safe and healthy online environment for minors.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　网信部门会同公安、文化和旅游、新闻出版、电影、广播电视等部门根据保护不同年龄阶段未成年人的需要，确定可能影响未成年人身心健康网络信息的种类、范围和判断标准。

**Article 67** The cyberspace administration departments shall, together with the public security, culture and tourism, press and publication, film, radio and television, and other departments, determine the types, scope, and criteria for judging online information that may affect the physical and mental health of minors, based on the needs of protecting minors of different age groups.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　新闻出版、教育、卫生健康、文化和旅游、网信等部门应当定期开展预防未成年人沉迷网络的宣传教育，监督网络产品和服务提供者履行预防未成年人沉迷网络的义务，指导家庭、学校、社会组织互相配合，采取科学、合理的方式对未成年人沉迷网络进行预防和干预。  
任何组织或者个人不得以侵害未成年人身心健康的方式对未成年人沉迷网络进行干预。

**Article 68** The press and publication, education, health, culture and tourism, cyberspace administration, and other departments shall regularly conduct publicity and education on preventing minors from becoming addicted to the internet, supervise network product and service providers in fulfilling their obligations to prevent minors from internet addiction, and guide families, schools, and social organizations to cooperate with each other and adopt scientific and reasonable methods for the prevention and intervention of minors' internet addiction.  
No organization or individual may intervene in minors' internet addiction in a manner that infringes upon the physical and mental health of minors.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　学校、社区、图书馆、文化馆、青少年宫等场所为未成年人提供的互联网上网服务设施，应当安装未成年人网络保护软件或者采取其他安全保护技术措施。  
智能终端产品的制造者、销售者应当在产品上安装未成年人网络保护软件，或者以显著方式告知用户未成年人网络保护软件的安装渠道和方法。

**Article 69** Internet access service facilities provided for minors in places such as schools, communities, libraries, cultural centers, and youth palaces shall be installed with minors' online protection software or other safety protection technical measures shall be taken.  
Manufacturers and sellers of smart terminal products shall install minors' online protection software on the products, or inform users in a conspicuous manner of the installation channels and methods for minors' online protection software.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　学校应当合理使用网络开展教学活动。未经学校允许，未成年学生不得将手机等智能终端产品带入课堂，带入学校的应当统一管理。  
学校发现未成年学生沉迷网络的，应当及时告知其父母或者其他监护人，共同对未成年学生进行教育和引导，帮助其恢复正常的学习生活。

**Article 70** Schools shall use the internet reasonably to carry out teaching activities. Without the permission of the school, minor students may not bring smart terminal products such as mobile phones into the classroom; those brought into the school shall be managed uniformly.  
Where a school discovers that a minor student is addicted to the internet, it shall promptly inform the student's parents or other guardians, and jointly educate and guide the minor student to help restore normal study and life.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人应当提高网络素养，规范自身使用网络的行为，加强对未成年人使用网络行为的引导和监督。  
未成年人的父母或者其他监护人应当通过在智能终端产品上安装未成年人网络保护软件、选择适合未成年人的服务模式和管理功能等方式，避免未成年人接触危害或者可能影响其身心健康的网络信息，合理安排未成年人使用网络的时间，有效预防未成年人沉迷网络。

**Article 71** Parents or other guardians of minors shall improve their online literacy, regulate their own behavior in using the internet, and strengthen the guidance and supervision over minors' internet use.  
Parents or other guardians of minors shall, by installing minors' online protection software on smart terminal products, selecting service modes and management functions suitable for minors, and other means, prevent minors from accessing harmful online information or information that may affect their physical and mental health, rationally arrange the time minors spend using the internet, and effectively prevent minors from becoming addicted to the internet.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　信息处理者通过网络处理未成年人个人信息的，应当遵循合法、正当和必要的原则。处理不满十四周岁未成年人个人信息的，应当征得未成年人的父母或者其他监护人同意，但法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。  
未成年人、父母或者其他监护人要求信息处理者更正、删除未成年人个人信息的，信息处理者应当及时采取措施予以更正、删除，但法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 72** Where an information processor processes minors' personal information through the internet, it shall follow the principles of legality, legitimacy, and necessity. Where the personal information of a minor under the age of 14 is processed, the consent of the minor's parents or other guardians shall be obtained, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.  
Where a minor, or a parent or other guardian, requests the information processor to correct or delete the minor's personal information, the information processor shall promptly take measures to correct or delete it, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　网络服务提供者发现未成年人通过网络发布私密信息的，应当及时提示，并采取必要的保护措施。

**Article 73** Where a network service provider discovers that a minor has published private information through the internet, it shall promptly provide a reminder and take necessary protective measures.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　网络产品和服务提供者不得向未成年人提供诱导其沉迷的产品和服务。  
网络游戏、网络直播、网络音视频、网络社交等网络服务提供者应当针对未成年人使用其服务设置相应的时间管理、权限管理、消费管理等功能。  
以未成年人为服务对象的在线教育网络产品和服务，不得插入网络游戏链接，不得推送广告等与教学无关的信息。

**Article 74** Network product and service providers may not provide products or services that induce minors to become addicted.  
Network service providers such as online games, live streaming, online audio and video, and social networking shall set up corresponding time management, permission management, consumption management, and other functions for minors' use of their services.  
Online education network products and services targeted at minors may not embed links to online games or push advertisements or other information unrelated to teaching.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　网络游戏经依法审批后方可运营。  
国家建立统一的未成年人网络游戏电子身份认证系统。网络游戏服务提供者应当要求未成年人以真实身份信息注册并登录网络游戏。  
网络游戏服务提供者应当按照国家有关规定和标准，对游戏产品进行分类，作出适龄提示，并采取技术措施，不得让未成年人接触不适宜的游戏或者游戏功能。  
网络游戏服务提供者不得在每日二十二时至次日八时向未成年人提供网络游戏服务。

**Article 75** Online games may be operated only after being approved according to law.  
The state establishes a unified electronic identity authentication system for minors' online games. Network game service providers shall require minors to register and log in to online games with real identity information.  
Network game service providers shall, in accordance with relevant state regulations and standards, classify game products, provide age-appropriate tips, and take technical measures to prevent minors from accessing unsuitable games or game functions.  
Network game service providers may not provide online game services to minors between 22:00 and 8:00 the next day.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　网络直播服务提供者不得为未满十六周岁的未成年人提供网络直播发布者账号注册服务；为年满十六周岁的未成年人提供网络直播发布者账号注册服务时，应当对其身份信息进行认证，并征得其父母或者其他监护人同意。

**Article 76** Network live streaming service providers may not provide account registration services for live streaming broadcasters to minors under the age of 16; where providing such services to minors who have reached the age of 16, they shall verify their identity information and obtain the consent of their parents or other guardians.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　任何组织或者个人不得通过网络以文字、图片、音视频等形式，对未成年人实施侮辱、诽谤、威胁或者恶意损害形象等网络欺凌行为。  
遭受网络欺凌的未成年人及其父母或者其他监护人有权通知网络服务提供者采取删除、屏蔽、断开链接等措施。网络服务提供者接到通知后，应当及时采取必要的措施制止网络欺凌行为，防止信息扩散。

**Article 77** No organization or individual may, through the internet, commit cyberbullying against minors in the form of text, pictures, audio, video, or other means, such as insult, defamation, threat, or malicious damage to their image.  
Minors who suffer from cyberbullying, and their parents or other guardians, have the right to notify network service providers to take measures such as deletion, blocking, or disconnecting links. Upon receiving such notification, the network service provider shall promptly take necessary measures to stop the cyberbullying and prevent the spread of information.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　网络产品和服务提供者应当建立便捷、合理、有效的投诉和举报渠道，公开投诉、举报方式等信息，及时受理并处理涉及未成年人的投诉、举报。

**Article 78** Network product and service providers shall establish convenient, reasonable, and effective complaint and reporting channels, publicly disclose information such as complaint and reporting methods, and promptly accept and handle complaints and reports involving minors.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　任何组织或者个人发现网络产品、服务含有危害未成年人身心健康的信息，有权向网络产品和服务提供者或者网信、公安等部门投诉、举报。

**Article 79** Any organization or individual that discovers that a network product or service contains information harmful to the physical and mental health of minors has the right to complain or report to the network product or service provider or to the cyberspace administration, public security, or other departments.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　网络服务提供者发现用户发布、传播可能影响未成年人身心健康的信息且未作显著提示的，应当作出提示或者通知用户予以提示；未作出提示的，不得传输相关信息。  
网络服务提供者发现用户发布、传播含有危害未成年人身心健康内容的信息的，应当立即停止传输相关信息，采取删除、屏蔽、断开链接等处置措施，保存有关记录，并向网信、公安等部门报告。  
网络服务提供者发现用户利用其网络服务对未成年人实施违法犯罪行为的，应当立即停止向该用户提供网络服务，保存有关记录，并向公安机关报告。

**Article 80** Where a network service provider discovers that a user has published or disseminated information that may affect the physical and mental health of minors and has not given a prominent tip, it shall provide a tip or notify the user to provide a tip; if no tip is provided, the relevant information shall not be transmitted.  
Where a network service provider discovers that a user has published or disseminated information containing content harmful to the physical and mental health of minors, it shall immediately stop transmitting the relevant information, take measures such as deletion, blocking, or disconnecting links, preserve relevant records, and report to the cyberspace administration, public security, and other departments.  
Where a network service provider discovers that a user uses its network service to commit illegal or criminal acts against a minor, it shall immediately stop providing network services to that user, preserve relevant records, and report to the public security organ.

### 第六章　政府保护

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　县级以上人民政府承担未成年人保护协调机制具体工作的职能部门应当明确相关内设机构或者专门人员，负责承担未成年人保护工作。  
乡镇人民政府和街道办事处应当设立未成年人保护工作站或者指定专门人员，及时办理未成年人相关事务；支持、指导居民委员会、村民委员会设立专人专岗，做好未成年人保护工作。

**Article 81** . The functional department of the people's government at or above the county level that undertakes the specific work of the coordination mechanism for the protection of minors shall clearly designate relevant internal institutions or special personnel responsible for the protection of minors.  
The township people's government and the sub-district office shall set up minor protection workstations or designate special personnel to handle minor-related affairs in a timely manner; support and guide residents' committees and villagers' committees to set up dedicated personnel and posts to do a good job in the protection of minors.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　各级人民政府应当将家庭教育指导服务纳入城乡公共服务体系，开展家庭教育知识宣传，鼓励和支持有关人民团体、企业事业单位、社会组织开展家庭教育指导服务。

**Article 82** . People's governments at all levels shall incorporate family education guidance services into the urban and rural public service system, carry out publicity of family education knowledge, and encourage and support relevant people's organizations, enterprises, institutions, and social organizations to provide family education guidance services.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　各级人民政府应当保障未成年人受教育的权利，并采取措施保障留守未成年人、困境未成年人、残疾未成年人接受义务教育。  
对尚未完成义务教育的辍学未成年学生，教育行政部门应当责令父母或者其他监护人将其送入学校接受义务教育。

**Article 83** . People's governments at all levels shall guarantee minors' right to education, and take measures to ensure that left-behind minors, minors in distress, and disabled minors receive compulsory education.  
For minor students who have dropped out of school and have not yet completed compulsory education, the education administrative department shall order the parents or other guardians to send them to school to receive compulsory education.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　各级人民政府应当发展托育、学前教育事业，办好婴幼儿照护服务机构、幼儿园，支持社会力量依法兴办母婴室、婴幼儿照护服务机构、幼儿园。  
县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当培养和培训婴幼儿照护服务机构、幼儿园的保教人员，提高其职业道德素质和业务能力。

**Article 84** . People's governments at all levels shall develop childcare and preschool education, run infant and toddler care service institutions and kindergartens well, and support social forces to lawfully establish mother-infant rooms, infant and toddler care service institutions, and kindergartens.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall cultivate and train the childcare and education personnel of infant and toddler care service institutions and kindergartens, and improve their professional ethics and professional competence.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　各级人民政府应当发展职业教育，保障未成年人接受职业教育或者职业技能培训，鼓励和支持人民团体、企业事业单位、社会组织为未成年人提供职业技能培训服务。

**Article 85** . People's governments at all levels shall develop vocational education, ensure that minors receive vocational education or vocational skills training, and encourage and support people's organizations, enterprises, institutions, and social organizations to provide vocational skills training services for minors.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　各级人民政府应当保障具有接受普通教育能力、能适应校园生活的残疾未成年人就近在普通学校、幼儿园接受教育；保障不具有接受普通教育能力的残疾未成年人在特殊教育学校、幼儿园接受学前教育、义务教育和职业教育。  
各级人民政府应当保障特殊教育学校、幼儿园的办学、办园条件，鼓励和支持社会力量举办特殊教育学校、幼儿园。

**Article 86** . People's governments at all levels shall ensure that disabled minors who have the ability to receive ordinary education and can adapt to campus life receive education in ordinary schools and kindergartens in their vicinity; and ensure that disabled minors without the ability to receive ordinary education receive preschool education, compulsory education, and vocational education in special education schools and kindergartens.  
People's governments at all levels shall guarantee the conditions for running special education schools and kindergartens, and encourage and support social forces to establish special education schools and kindergartens.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　地方人民政府及其有关部门应当保障校园安全，监督、指导学校、幼儿园等单位落实校园安全责任，建立突发事件的报告、处置和协调机制。

**Article 87** . Local people's governments and their relevant departments shall ensure campus safety, supervise and guide schools, kindergartens and other units to implement campus safety responsibilities, and establish mechanisms for reporting, handling and coordinating emergencies.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　公安机关和其他有关部门应当依法维护校园周边的治安和交通秩序，设置监控设备和交通安全设施，预防和制止侵害未成年人的违法犯罪行为。

**Article 88** . Public security organs and other relevant departments shall maintain public security and traffic order around campuses according to law, install surveillance equipment and traffic safety facilities, and prevent and stop illegal and criminal acts that infringe upon minors.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　地方人民政府应当建立和改善适合未成年人的活动场所和设施，支持公益性未成年人活动场所和设施的建设和运行，鼓励社会力量兴办适合未成年人的活动场所和设施，并加强管理。  
地方人民政府应当采取措施，鼓励和支持学校在国家法定节假日、休息日及寒暑假期将文化体育设施对未成年人免费或者优惠开放。  
地方人民政府应当采取措施，防止任何组织或者个人侵占、破坏学校、幼儿园、婴幼儿照护服务机构等未成年人活动场所的场地、房屋和设施。

**Article 89** . Local people's governments shall establish and improve activity venues and facilities suitable for minors, support the construction and operation of public welfare minor activity venues and facilities, encourage social forces to establish activity venues and facilities suitable for minors, and strengthen management.  
Local people's governments shall take measures to encourage and support schools to open cultural and sports facilities to minors free of charge or at a discount during statutory holidays, rest days, and winter and summer vacations.  
Local people's governments shall take measures to prevent any organization or individual from encroaching upon or damaging the sites, houses, and facilities of activity venues for minors such as schools, kindergartens, and infant and toddler care service institutions.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　各级人民政府及其有关部门应当对未成年人进行卫生保健和营养指导，提供卫生保健服务。  
卫生健康部门应当依法对未成年人的疫苗预防接种进行规范，防治未成年人常见病、多发病，加强传染病防治和监督管理，做好伤害预防和干预，指导和监督学校、幼儿园、婴幼儿照护服务机构开展卫生保健工作。  
教育行政部门应当加强未成年人的心理健康教育，建立未成年人心理问题的早期发现和及时干预机制。卫生健康部门应当做好未成年人心理治疗、心理危机干预以及精神障碍早期识别和诊断治疗等工作。

**Article 90** . People's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall provide health care and nutrition guidance to minors and provide health care services.  
The health department shall regulate the vaccination of minors according to law, prevent and treat common and frequently occurring diseases of minors, strengthen the prevention, control, and supervision of infectious diseases, do a good job in injury prevention and intervention, and guide and supervise schools, kindergartens, and infant and toddler care service institutions in carrying out health care work.  
The education administrative department shall strengthen the mental health education of minors and establish mechanisms for early detection and timely intervention of psychological problems of minors. The health department shall do a good job in psychological treatment, psychological crisis intervention, and early identification, diagnosis and treatment of mental disorders for minors.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　各级人民政府及其有关部门对困境未成年人实施分类保障，采取措施满足其生活、教育、安全、医疗康复、住房等方面的基本需要。

**Article 91** . People's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall implement classified protection for minors in distress, and take measures to meet their basic needs in terms of living, education, safety, medical rehabilitation, housing, etc.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　具有下列情形之一的，民政部门应当依法对未成年人进行临时监护：  
（一）未成年人流浪乞讨或者身份不明，暂时查找不到父母或者其他监护人；  
（二）监护人下落不明且无其他人可以担任监护人；  
（三）监护人因自身客观原因或者因发生自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件等突发事件不能履行监护职责，导致未成年人监护缺失；  
（四）监护人拒绝或者怠于履行监护职责，导致未成年人处于无人照料的状态；  
（五）监护人教唆、利用未成年人实施违法犯罪行为，未成年人需要被带离安置；  
（六）未成年人遭受监护人严重伤害或者面临人身安全威胁，需要被紧急安置；  
（七）法律规定的其他情形。

**Article 92** . Where any of the following circumstances exists, the civil affairs department shall provide temporary guardianship for the minor according to law:  
(1) The minor is wandering, begging, or has an unidentified status, and the parents or other guardians cannot be found temporarily;  
(2) The guardian is missing and no other person can serve as guardian;  
(3) The guardian is unable to perform guardianship duties due to objective reasons or due to emergencies such as natural disasters, accidents, or public health incidents, resulting in the minor's lack of guardianship;  
(4) The guardian refuses or neglects to perform guardianship duties, leaving the minor in a state without care;  
(5) The guardian instigates or uses the minor to commit illegal or criminal acts, and the minor needs to be removed and placed;  
(6) The minor suffers serious harm from or faces a threat to personal safety from the guardian, and needs emergency placement;  
(7) Other circumstances as provided by law.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　对临时监护的未成年人，民政部门可以采取委托亲属抚养、家庭寄养等方式进行安置，也可以交由未成年人救助保护机构或者儿童福利机构进行收留、抚养。  
临时监护期间，经民政部门评估，监护人重新具备履行监护职责条件的，民政部门可以将未成年人送回监护人抚养。

**Article 93** . For minors under temporary guardianship, the civil affairs department may arrange placement by means of entrusting relatives to raise them, foster care, etc., or may hand them over to minor rescue and protection institutions or children's welfare institutions for shelter and care.  
During temporary guardianship, if the guardian meets the conditions for performing guardianship duties again after assessment by the civil affairs department, the civil affairs department may return the minor to the guardian for care.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　具有下列情形之一的，民政部门应当依法对未成年人进行长期监护：  
（一）查找不到未成年人的父母或者其他监护人；  
（二）监护人死亡或者被宣告死亡且无其他人可以担任监护人；  
（三）监护人丧失监护能力且无其他人可以担任监护人；  
（四）人民法院判决撤销监护人资格并指定由民政部门担任监护人；  
（五）法律规定的其他情形。

**Article 94** . Where any of the following circumstances exists, the civil affairs department shall provide long-term guardianship for the minor according to law:  
(1) The parents or other guardians of the minor cannot be found;  
(2) The guardian has died or has been declared dead and no other person can serve as guardian;  
(3) The guardian has lost guardianship capacity and no other person can serve as guardian;  
(4) The People's Court has ruled to revoke the guardian's qualifications and designated the civil affairs department as guardian;  
(5) Other circumstances as provided by law.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　民政部门进行收养评估后，可以依法将其长期监护的未成年人交由符合条件的申请人收养。收养关系成立后，民政部门与未成年人的监护关系终止。

**Article 95** . After conducting an adoption assessment, the civil affairs department may, according to law, transfer the minor under its long-term guardianship to a qualified applicant for adoption. After the adoption relationship is established, the guardianship relationship between the civil affairs department and the minor shall terminate.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　民政部门承担临时监护或者长期监护职责的，财政、教育、卫生健康、公安等部门应当根据各自职责予以配合。  
县级以上人民政府及其民政部门应当根据需要设立未成年人救助保护机构、儿童福利机构，负责收留、抚养由民政部门监护的未成年人。

**Article 96** . Where the civil affairs department undertakes temporary or long-term guardianship duties, the finance, education, health, public security and other departments shall cooperate according to their respective duties.  
People's governments at or above the county level and their civil affairs departments shall establish minor rescue and protection institutions and children's welfare institutions as needed to take in and care for minors under the guardianship of the civil affairs department.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　县级以上人民政府应当开通全国统一的未成年人保护热线，及时受理、转介侵犯未成年人合法权益的投诉、举报；鼓励和支持人民团体、企业事业单位、社会组织参与建设未成年人保护服务平台、服务热线、服务站点，提供未成年人保护方面的咨询、帮助。

**Article 97** . People's governments at or above the county level shall open a nationally unified minor protection hotline to timely accept and refer complaints and reports of infringement upon the lawful rights and interests of minors; encourage and support people's organizations, enterprises, institutions, and social organizations to participate in building minor protection service platforms, hotlines, and service stations to provide consultation and assistance in minor protection.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　国家建立性侵害、虐待、拐卖、暴力伤害等违法犯罪人员信息查询系统，向密切接触未成年人的单位提供免费查询服务。

**Article 98** . The State shall establish an information inquiry system for persons who have committed illegal and criminal acts such as sexual assault, abuse, abduction, and violent injury, and provide free inquiry services to units that have close contact with minors.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　地方人民政府应当培育、引导和规范有关社会组织、社会工作者参与未成年人保护工作，开展家庭教育指导服务，为未成年人的心理辅导、康复救助、监护及收养评估等提供专业服务。

**Article 99** . Local people's governments shall cultivate, guide, and regulate relevant social organizations and social workers to participate in minor protection work, carry out family education guidance services, and provide professional services for psychological counseling, rehabilitation assistance, guardianship and adoption assessment, etc. for minors.

### 第七章　司法保护

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院和司法行政部门应当依法履行职责，保障未成年人合法权益。

**Article 100** . Public security organs, People's Procuratorates, People's Courts, and judicial administrative departments shall perform their duties according to law to protect the lawful rights and interests of minors.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院和司法行政部门应当确定专门机构或者指定专门人员，负责办理涉及未成年人案件。办理涉及未成年人案件的人员应当经过专门培训，熟悉未成年人身心特点。专门机构或者专门人员中，应当有女性工作人员。  
公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院和司法行政部门应当对上述机构和人员实行与未成年人保护工作相适应的评价考核标准。

**Article 101** Public security organs, People's Procuratorates, People's Courts, and judicial administrative departments shall designate specialized institutions or appoint specialized personnel responsible for handling cases involving minors. Personnel handling cases involving minors shall undergo specialized training and be familiar with the physical and psychological characteristics of minors. Among the specialized institutions or specialized personnel, there shall be female staff members.  
Public security organs, People's Procuratorates, People's Courts, and judicial administrative departments shall implement evaluation and assessment standards for the aforesaid institutions and personnel that are compatible with the work of protecting minors.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院和司法行政部门办理涉及未成年人案件，应当考虑未成年人身心特点和健康成长的需要，使用未成年人能够理解的语言和表达方式，听取未成年人的意见。

**Article 102** When handling cases involving minors, public security organs, People's Procuratorates, People's Courts, and judicial administrative departments shall take into account the physical and psychological characteristics of minors and the needs of their healthy growth, use language and expressions that minors can understand, and hear the opinions of minors.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院、司法行政部门以及其他组织和个人不得披露有关案件中未成年人的姓名、影像、住所、就读学校以及其他可能识别出其身份的信息，但查找失踪、被拐卖未成年人等情形除外。

**Article 103** Public security organs, People's Procuratorates, People's Courts, judicial administrative departments, and other organizations and individuals shall not disclose the names, images, residences, schools attended, or other information that may identify the minors involved in a case, except in cases where missing or abducted minors are being searched for.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　对需要法律援助或者司法救助的未成年人，法律援助机构或者公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院和司法行政部门应当给予帮助，依法为其提供法律援助或者司法救助。  
法律援助机构应当指派熟悉未成年人身心特点的律师为未成年人提供法律援助服务。  
法律援助机构和律师协会应当对办理未成年人法律援助案件的律师进行指导和培训。

**Article 104** For minors who need legal aid or judicial assistance, legal aid institutions or public security organs, People's Procuratorates, People's Courts, and judicial administrative departments shall provide assistance and, in accordance with law, provide them with legal aid or judicial assistance.  
Legal aid institutions shall appoint lawyers who are familiar with the physical and psychological characteristics of minors to provide legal aid services to minors.  
Legal aid institutions and lawyers associations shall provide guidance and training to lawyers handling legal aid cases involving minors.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　人民检察院通过行使检察权，对涉及未成年人的诉讼活动等依法进行监督。

**Article 105** The People's Procuratorate shall, through the exercise of its prosecutorial power, supervise litigation activities involving minors in accordance with law.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　未成年人合法权益受到侵犯，相关组织和个人未代为提起诉讼的，人民检察院可以督促、支持其提起诉讼；涉及公共利益的，人民检察院有权提起公益诉讼。

**Article 106** Where the legitimate rights and interests of a minor are infringed upon and the relevant organization or individual fails to initiate a lawsuit on behalf of the minor, the People's Procuratorate may urge or support the initiation of a lawsuit; where the public interest is involved, the People's Procuratorate has the right to initiate a public interest lawsuit.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　人民法院审理继承案件，应当依法保护未成年人的继承权和受遗赠权。  
人民法院审理离婚案件，涉及未成年子女抚养问题的，应当尊重已满八周岁未成年子女的真实意愿，根据双方具体情况，按照最有利于未成年子女的原则依法处理。

**Article 107** When hearing a succession case, the People's Court shall protect the minor's right of inheritance and right to receive a legacy in accordance with law.  
When hearing a divorce case involving issues of child support for a minor child, the People's Court shall respect the true wishes of the minor child who has reached the age of eight, and shall handle the matter in accordance with law based on the specific circumstances of both parties and in accordance with the principle of acting in the best interests of the minor child.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人不依法履行监护职责或者严重侵犯被监护的未成年人合法权益的，人民法院可以根据有关人员或者单位的申请，依法作出人身安全保护令或者撤销监护人资格。  
被撤销监护人资格的父母或者其他监护人应当依法继续负担抚养费用。

**Article 108** Where the parents or other guardians of a minor fail to perform their guardianship duties in accordance with law or seriously infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of the minor under their guardianship, the People's Court may, upon application by the relevant person or entity, issue a personal safety protection order or disqualify the guardian in accordance with law.  
Parents or other guardians whose guardianship has been disqualified shall continue to bear child support expenses in accordance with law.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　人民法院审理离婚、抚养、收养、监护、探望等案件涉及未成年人的，可以自行或者委托社会组织对未成年人的相关情况进行社会调查。

**Article 109** Where a case involving divorce, child support, adoption, guardianship, visitation, or other matters tried by the People's Court involves a minor, the court may, on its own or by entrusting a social organization, conduct a social investigation into the relevant circumstances of the minor.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院讯问未成年犯罪嫌疑人、被告人，询问未成年被害人、证人，应当依法通知其法定代理人或者其成年亲属、所在学校的代表等合适成年人到场，并采取适当方式，在适当场所进行，保障未成年人的名誉权、隐私权和其他合法权益。  
人民法院开庭审理涉及未成年人案件，未成年被害人、证人一般不出庭作证；必须出庭的，应当采取保护其隐私的技术手段和心理干预等保护措施。

**Article 110** When interrogating a minor criminal suspect or defendant, or questioning a minor victim or witness, public security organs, People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts shall, in accordance with law, notify the minor's legal representative, or their adult relatives, representatives of the school they attend, or other appropriate adults to be present, and shall conduct the interrogation or questioning in an appropriate manner and in an appropriate place to protect the minor's right to reputation, right to privacy, and other legitimate rights and interests.  
When a People's Court holds a hearing in a case involving a minor, the minor victim or witness generally shall not appear in court to testify; if they must appear in court, protective measures such as technical means to protect their privacy and psychological intervention shall be adopted.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院应当与其他有关政府部门、人民团体、社会组织互相配合，对遭受性侵害或者暴力伤害的未成年被害人及其家庭实施必要的心理干预、经济救助、法律援助、转学安置等保护措施。

**Article 111** Public security organs, People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts shall, in coordination with other relevant government departments, people's organizations, and social organizations, implement necessary protective measures such as psychological intervention, financial assistance, legal aid, and transfer to another school for minors who have been victims of sexual assault or violent injury and their families.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院办理未成年人遭受性侵害或者暴力伤害案件，在询问未成年被害人、证人时，应当采取同步录音录像等措施，尽量一次完成；未成年被害人、证人是女性的，应当由女性工作人员进行。

**Article 112** When handling cases involving minors who have been victims of sexual assault or violent injury, public security organs, People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts shall, when questioning the minor victim or witness, take measures such as simultaneous audio and video recording and shall complete the questioning in as few sessions as possible; if the minor victim or witness is female, the questioning shall be conducted by female staff members.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　对违法犯罪的未成年人，实行教育、感化、挽救的方针，坚持教育为主、惩罚为辅的原则。  
对违法犯罪的未成年人依法处罚后，在升学、就业等方面不得歧视。

**Article 113** For minors who have violated the law or committed crimes, the policy of education, persuasion, and rescue shall be implemented, and the principle of focusing on education and supplementing it with punishment shall be adhered to.  
After minors who have violated the law or committed crimes have been punished in accordance with law, they shall not be discriminated against in matters such as further education or employment.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院和司法行政部门发现有关单位未尽到未成年人教育、管理、救助、看护等保护职责的，应当向该单位提出建议。被建议单位应当在一个月内作出书面回复。

**Article 114** Where public security organs, People's Procuratorates, People's Courts, and judicial administrative departments discover that a relevant entity has failed to fulfill its protection duties such as education, management, assistance, and care for minors, they shall make suggestions to that entity. The entity receiving the suggestion shall provide a written reply within one month.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院和司法行政部门应当结合实际，根据涉及未成年人案件的特点，开展未成年人法治宣传教育工作。

**Article 115** Public security organs, People's Procuratorates, People's Courts, and judicial administrative departments shall, based on actual conditions and the characteristics of cases involving minors, carry out legal publicity and education work concerning minors.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　国家鼓励和支持社会组织、社会工作者参与涉及未成年人案件中未成年人的心理干预、法律援助、社会调查、社会观护、教育矫治、社区矫正等工作。

**Article 116** The State encourages and supports social organizations and social workers to participate in work such as psychological intervention, legal aid, social investigation, social observation, educational correction, and community correction for minors involved in cases.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　违反[本法第十一条](#art-11)第二款规定，未履行报告义务造成严重后果的，由上级主管部门或者所在单位对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 117** Where a violation of the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 11 of this Law results in serious consequences due to failure to fulfill the reporting obligation, the competent authority at a higher level or the entity to which the person belongs shall impose sanctions on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible personnel in accordance with law.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人不依法履行监护职责或者侵犯未成年人合法权益的，由其居住地的居民委员会、村民委员会予以劝诫、制止；情节严重的，居民委员会、村民委员会应当及时向公安机关报告。  
公安机关接到报告或者公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院在办理案件过程中发现未成年人的父母或者其他监护人存在上述情形的，应当予以训诫，并可以责令其接受家庭教育指导。

**Article 118** Where the parents or other guardians of a minor fail to perform their guardianship duties in accordance with law or infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of the minor, the residents' committee or villagers' committee of the place where the minor resides shall admonish and stop such conduct; if the circumstances are serious, the residents' committee or villagers' committee shall promptly report to the public security organ.  
Upon receiving a report, or if during the handling of a case the public security organ, People's Procuratorate, or People's Court discovers that the parents or other guardians of a minor are in the circumstances mentioned above, the public security organ shall admonish them and may order them to receive family education guidance.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　学校、幼儿园、婴幼儿照护服务等机构及其教职员工违反[本法第二十七条](#art-27)、第二十八条、第三十九条规定的，由公安、教育、卫生健康、市场监督管理等部门按照职责分工责令改正；拒不改正或者情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 119** Where schools, kindergartens, infant care service institutions, or their faculty and staff violate the provisions of Articles 27, 28, or 39 of this Law, the public security, education, health, market supervision and administration, and other departments shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order corrections; if they refuse to make corrections or the circumstances are serious, sanctions shall be imposed on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible personnel in accordance with law.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　违反[本法第四十四条](#art-44)、第四十五条、第四十七条规定，未给予未成年人免费或者优惠待遇的，由市场监督管理、文化和旅游、交通运输等部门按照职责分工责令限期改正，给予警告；拒不改正的，处一万元以上十万元以下罚款。

**Article 120** Where a violation of the provisions of Articles 44, 45, or 47 of this Law occurs by failing to provide free or preferential treatment to minors, the market supervision and administration, culture and tourism, transportation, and other departments shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order corrections within a specified time limit and give a warning; if corrections are refused, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　违反[本法第五十条](#art-50)、第五十一条规定的，由新闻出版、广播电视、电影、网信等部门按照职责分工责令限期改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，可以并处十万元以下罚款；拒不改正或者情节严重的，责令暂停相关业务、停产停业或者吊销营业执照、吊销相关许可证，违法所得一百万元以上的，并处违法所得一倍以上十倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足一百万元的，并处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款。

**Article 121** Anyone who violates the provisions of Articles 50 and 51 of this Law shall be ordered by the departments of press and publication, radio and television, film, and cyberspace administration, among others, to make corrections within a specified time limit, given a warning, have illegal gains confiscated, and may also be imposed a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the party refuses to make corrections or the circumstances are serious, the party shall be ordered to suspend relevant business, cease production or operations, or have its business license or relevant permit revoked; if the illegal gains exceed one million yuan, a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than one million yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than one million yuan shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　场所运营单位违反[本法第五十六条](#art-56)第二款规定、住宿经营者违反[本法第五十七条](#art-57)规定的，由市场监督管理、应急管理、公安等部门按照职责分工责令限期改正，给予警告；拒不改正或者造成严重后果的，责令停业整顿或者吊销营业执照、吊销相关许可证，并处一万元以上十万元以下罚款。

**Article 122** Where a venue operation unit violates the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 56 of this Law, or an accommodation operator violates the provisions of Article 57 of this Law, the departments of market regulation, emergency management, and public security, among others, shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order corrections within a specified time limit and give a warning; if the party refuses to make corrections or causes serious consequences, the party shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification or have its business license or relevant permit revoked, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　相关经营者违反[本法第五十八条](#art-58)、第五十九条第一款、第六十条规定的，由文化和旅游、市场监督管理、烟草专卖、公安等部门按照职责分工责令限期改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，可以并处五万元以下罚款；拒不改正或者情节严重的，责令停业整顿或者吊销营业执照、吊销相关许可证，可以并处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款。

**Article 123** Where a relevant operator violates the provisions of Article 58, paragraph 1 of Article 59, or Article 60 of this Law, the departments of culture and tourism, market regulation, tobacco monopoly, and public security, among others, shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order corrections within a specified time limit, give a warning, confiscate illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if the party refuses to make corrections or the circumstances are serious, the party shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification or have its business license or relevant permit revoked, and may also be imposed a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　违反[本法第五十九条](#art-59)第二款规定，在学校、幼儿园和其他未成年人集中活动的公共场所吸烟、饮酒的，由卫生健康、教育、市场监督管理等部门按照职责分工责令改正，给予警告，可以并处五百元以下罚款；场所管理者未及时制止的，由卫生健康、教育、市场监督管理等部门按照职责分工给予警告，并处一万元以下罚款。

**Article 124** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 59 of this Law, smokes or drinks alcohol in schools, kindergartens, or other public places where minors gather, shall be ordered by the departments of health, education, and market regulation, among others, to make corrections, given a warning, and may also be imposed a fine of not more than 500 yuan; if the venue manager fails to stop such conduct in a timely manner, the venue manager shall be given a warning by the departments of health, education, and market regulation, among others, according to their respective functions and duties, and a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　违反[本法第六十一条](#art-61)规定的，由文化和旅游、人力资源和社会保障、市场监督管理等部门按照职责分工责令限期改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，可以并处十万元以下罚款；拒不改正或者情节严重的，责令停产停业或者吊销营业执照、吊销相关许可证，并处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款。

**Article 125** Anyone who violates the provisions of Article 61 of this Law shall be ordered by the departments of culture and tourism, human resources and social security, and market regulation, among others, to make corrections within a specified time limit, given a warning, have illegal gains confiscated, and may also be imposed a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the party refuses to make corrections or the circumstances are serious, the party shall be ordered to cease production or business operations or have its business license or relevant permit revoked, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than one million yuan shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条　密切接触未成年人的单位违反[本法第六十二条](#art-62)规定，未履行查询义务，或者招用、继续聘用具有相关违法犯罪记录人员的，由教育、人力资源和社会保障、市场监督管理等部门按照职责分工责令限期改正，给予警告，并处五万元以下罚款；拒不改正或者造成严重后果的，责令停业整顿或者吊销营业执照、吊销相关许可证，并处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 126** Where a unit that has close contact with minors violates the provisions of Article 62 of this Law by failing to perform the inquiry obligation, or recruiting or continuing to employ a person with relevant criminal or illegal records, the departments of education, human resources and social security, and market regulation, among others, shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order corrections within a specified time limit, give a warning, and impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if the party refuses to make corrections or causes serious consequences, the party shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification or have its business license or relevant permit revoked, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall also be imposed, and the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-127"></a>第一百二十七条　信息处理者违反[本法第七十二条](#art-72)规定，或者网络产品和服务提供者违反[本法第七十三条](#art-73)、第七十四条、第七十五条、第七十六条、第七十七条、第八十条规定的，由公安、网信、电信、新闻出版、广播电视、文化和旅游等有关部门按照职责分工责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，违法所得一百万元以上的，并处违法所得一倍以上十倍以下罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足一百万元的，并处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他责任人员处一万元以上十万元以下罚款；拒不改正或者情节严重的，并可以责令暂停相关业务、停业整顿、关闭网站、吊销营业执照或者吊销相关许可证。

**Article 127** Where an information processor violates the provisions of Article 72 of this Law, or a network product or service provider violates the provisions of Articles 73, 74, 75, 76, 77, or 80 of this Law, the relevant departments such as public security, cyberspace administration, telecommunications, press and publication, radio and television, and culture and tourism shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order corrections, give a warning, confiscate illegal gains; if the illegal gains exceed one million yuan, a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than one million yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than one million yuan shall also be imposed; and the directly responsible persons in charge and other responsible persons shall be imposed a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the party refuses to make corrections or the circumstances are serious, the party may also be ordered to suspend relevant business, suspend business for rectification, close the website, or have its business license or relevant permit revoked.

<a id="art-128"></a>第一百二十八条　国家机关工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊，损害未成年人合法权益的，依法给予处分。

**Article 128** Where a state organ staff member neglects duties, abuses power, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, thereby harming the legitimate rights and interests of minors, the staff member shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-129"></a>第一百二十九条　违反本法规定，侵犯未成年人合法权益，造成人身、财产或者其他损害的，依法承担民事责任。  
违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 129** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of minors, causing personal injury, property loss, or other damage, shall bear civil liability according to law.  
Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, constitutes a violation of public security administration shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-130"></a>第一百三十条　本法中下列用语的含义：  
（一）密切接触未成年人的单位，是指学校、幼儿园等教育机构；校外培训机构；未成年人救助保护机构、儿童福利机构等未成年人安置、救助机构；婴幼儿照护服务机构、早期教育服务机构；校外托管、临时看护机构；家政服务机构；为未成年人提供医疗服务的医疗机构；其他对未成年人负有教育、培训、监护、救助、看护、医疗等职责的企业事业单位、社会组织等。  
（二）学校，是指普通中小学、特殊教育学校、中等职业学校、专门学校。  
（三）学生欺凌，是指发生在学生之间，一方蓄意或者恶意通过肢体、语言及网络等手段实施欺压、侮辱，造成另一方人身伤害、财产损失或者精神损害的行为。

**Article 130** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) 'Units that have close contact with minors' refers to educational institutions such as schools and kindergartens; off-campus training institutions; minor rescue and protection institutions, child welfare institutions, and other institutions for the placement and rescue of minors; infant and toddler care service institutions, early education service institutions; off-campus trusteeship and temporary care institutions; domestic service institutions; medical institutions that provide medical services to minors; and other enterprises, public institutions, and social organizations that bear the duties of education, training, guardianship, rescue, care, and medical care for minors.  
(2) 'Schools' refers to ordinary primary and secondary schools, special education schools, secondary vocational schools, and specialized schools.  
(3) 'Student bullying' refers to conduct that occurs between students, where one party intentionally or maliciously commits bullying or humiliation through physical, verbal, or cyber means, causing personal injury, property loss, or mental damage to the other party.

<a id="art-131"></a>第一百三十一条　对中国境内未满十八周岁的外国人、无国籍人，依照本法有关规定予以保护。

**Article 131** Foreigners and stateless persons under the age of 18 within the territory of China shall be protected in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-132"></a>第一百三十二条　本法自2021年6月1日起施行。

**Article 132** This Law shall come into force as of June 1, 2021.
