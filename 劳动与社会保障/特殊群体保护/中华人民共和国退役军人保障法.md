# 中华人民共和国退役军人保障法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2020-11-11  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> （2020年11月11日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十三次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　移交接收 第三章　退役安置 第四章　教育培训 第五章　就业创业 第六章　抚恤优待 第七章　褒扬激励 第八章　服务管理 第九章　法律责任 第十章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强退役军人保障工作，维护退役军人合法权益，让军人成为全社会尊崇的职业，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称退役军人，是指从中国人民解放军依法退出现役的军官、军士和义务兵等人员。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强退役军人保障工作，维护退役军人合法权益，让军人成为全社会尊崇的职业，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution to strengthen the protection of ex-service members, safeguard the lawful rights and interests of ex-service members, and make military service a profession respected by the whole society.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称退役军人，是指从中国人民解放军依法退出现役的军官、军士和义务兵等人员。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "ex-service members" means officers, non-commissioned officers, and conscripts who have legally retired from active service in the People's Liberation Army.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　退役军人为国防和军队建设做出了重要贡献，是社会主义现代化建设的重要力量。  
尊重、关爱退役军人是全社会的共同责任。国家关心、优待退役军人，加强退役军人保障体系建设，保障退役军人依法享有相应的权益。

**Article 3** Ex-service members have made significant contributions to national defense and military construction, and are an important force in the socialist modernization drive.  
Respecting and caring for ex-service members is the common responsibility of the whole society. The State cares for and provides preferential treatment to ex-service members, strengthens the system for protecting ex-service members, and ensures that ex-service members enjoy corresponding rights and interests according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　退役军人保障工作坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持为经济社会发展服务、为国防和军队建设服务的方针，遵循以人为本、分类保障、服务优先、依法管理的原则。

**Article 4** The protection of ex-service members shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, adhere to the policy of serving economic and social development as well as national defense and military construction, and follow the principles of putting people first, providing categorized protection, prioritizing services, and managing according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　退役军人保障应当与经济发展相协调，与社会进步相适应。  
退役军人安置工作应当公开、公平、公正。  
退役军人的政治、生活等待遇与其服现役期间所做贡献挂钩。  
国家建立参战退役军人特别优待机制。

**Article 5** Protection of ex-service members shall be coordinated with economic development and suited to social progress.  
The placement of ex-service members shall be open, fair, and just.  
The political and living benefits of ex-service members shall be linked to their contributions made during active service.  
The State shall establish a special preferential treatment mechanism for ex-service members who have participated in combat.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　退役军人应当继续发扬人民军队优良传统，模范遵守宪法和法律法规，保守军事秘密，践行社会主义核心价值观，积极参加社会主义现代化建设。

**Article 6** Ex-service members shall continue to carry forward the fine traditions of the people's army, faithfully observe the Constitution and laws and regulations, keep military secrets, practice the core socialist values, and actively participate in the socialist modernization drive.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院退役军人工作主管部门负责全国的退役军人保障工作。县级以上地方人民政府退役军人工作主管部门负责本行政区域的退役军人保障工作。  
中央和国家有关机关、中央军事委员会有关部门、地方各级有关机关应当在各自职责范围内做好退役军人保障工作。  
军队各级负责退役军人有关工作的部门与县级以上人民政府退役军人工作主管部门应当密切配合，做好退役军人保障工作。

**Article 7** The competent department of ex-service member affairs under the State Council shall be responsible for the protection of ex-service members nationwide. The competent departments of ex-service member affairs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the protection of ex-service members within their respective administrative regions.  
The relevant organs of the Central Committee and the State, the relevant departments of the Central Military Commission, and the relevant organs at all levels of local governments shall properly carry out the protection of ex-service members within their respective duties.  
The departments of the armed forces at all levels responsible for ex-service member affairs and the competent departments of ex-service member affairs of people's governments at or above the county level shall closely cooperate to properly carry out the protection of ex-service members.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家加强退役军人保障工作信息化建设，为退役军人建档立卡，实现有关部门之间信息共享，为提高退役军人保障能力提供支持。  
国务院退役军人工作主管部门应当与中央和国家有关机关、中央军事委员会有关部门密切配合，统筹做好信息数据系统的建设、维护、应用和信息安全管理等工作。

**Article 8** The State shall strengthen the informatization construction of the protection of ex-service members, establish files and records for ex-service members, achieve information sharing among relevant departments, and provide support for improving the capacity to protect ex-service members.  
The competent department of ex-service member affairs under the State Council shall closely cooperate with the relevant organs of the Central Committee and the State and the relevant departments of the Central Military Commission to coordinate the construction, maintenance, application, and information security management of the information data system.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　退役军人保障工作所需经费由中央和地方财政共同负担。退役安置、教育培训、抚恤优待资金主要由中央财政负担。

**Article 9** The funds required for the protection of ex-service members shall be jointly borne by the central and local finances. The funds for retirement placement, education and training, and pension and preferential treatment shall be mainly borne by the central finance.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家鼓励和引导企业、社会组织、个人等社会力量依法通过捐赠、设立基金、志愿服务等方式为退役军人提供支持和帮助。

**Article 10** The State shall encourage and guide enterprises, social organizations, individuals, and other social forces to provide support and assistance to ex-service members according to law through donations, establishment of funds, voluntary services, and other means.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对在退役军人保障工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 11** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the protection of ex-service members shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　移交接收

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院退役军人工作主管部门、中央军事委员会政治工作部门、中央和国家有关机关应当制定全国退役军人的年度移交接收计划。

**Article 12** The competent department of ex-service member affairs under the State Council, the political work department of the Central Military Commission, and the relevant organs of the Central Committee and the State shall formulate annual plans for the transfer and reception of ex-service members nationwide.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　退役军人原所在部队应当将退役军人移交安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门，安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门负责接收退役军人。  
退役军人的安置地，按照国家有关规定确定。

**Article 13** The original unit of an ex-service member shall transfer the ex-service member to the competent department of ex-service member affairs of the people's government of the place of placement, and the competent department of ex-service member affairs of the people's government of the place of placement shall be responsible for receiving the ex-service member.  
The place of placement of an ex-service member shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　退役军人应当在规定时间内，持军队出具的退役证明到安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门报到。

**Article 14** Ex-service members shall, within the prescribed time limit, report to the competent department of ex-service member affairs of the people's government of the place of placement with the certificate of retirement issued by the armed forces.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门在接收退役军人时，向退役军人发放退役军人优待证。  
退役军人优待证全国统一制发、统一编号，管理使用办法由国务院退役军人工作主管部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 15** The competent department of ex-service member affairs of the people's government of the place of placement shall, when receiving an ex-service member, issue an ex-service member preferential treatment certificate to the ex-service member.  
The ex-service member preferential treatment certificate shall be uniformly issued and numbered nationwide, and its management and use measures shall be formulated by the competent department of ex-service member affairs under the State Council in conjunction with the relevant departments.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　军人所在部队在军人退役时，应当及时将其人事档案移交安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门。  
安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门应当按照国家人事档案管理有关规定，接收、保管并向有关单位移交退役军人人事档案。

**Article 16** The unit where a military member serves shall, when the military member retires from active service, promptly transfer his/her personnel file to the competent department of ex-service member affairs of the people's government of the place of placement.  
The competent department of ex-service member affairs of the people's government of the place of placement shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State on personnel file management, receive, keep, and transfer the personnel file of the ex-service member to the relevant units.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　安置地人民政府公安机关应当按照国家有关规定，及时为退役军人办理户口登记，同级退役军人工作主管部门应当予以协助。

**Article 17** The public security organ of the people's government of the place of placement shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, promptly handle household registration for ex-service members, and the competent department of ex-service member affairs at the same level shall provide assistance.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　退役军人原所在部队应当按照有关法律法规规定，及时将退役军人及随军未就业配偶的养老、医疗等社会保险关系和相应资金，转入安置地社会保险经办机构。  
安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门应当与社会保险经办机构、军队有关部门密切配合，依法做好有关社会保险关系和相应资金转移接续工作。

**Article 18** The original unit of an ex-service member shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, promptly transfer the social insurance relations for old-age, medical care, etc., and the corresponding funds of the ex-service member and his/her unemployed spouse accompanying the military, to the social insurance agency of the place of placement.  
The competent department of ex-service member affairs of the people's government of the place of placement shall closely cooperate with the social insurance agency and the relevant departments of the armed forces to properly carry out the transfer and continuation of the relevant social insurance relations and corresponding funds according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　退役军人移交接收过程中，发生与其服现役有关的问题，由原所在部队负责处理；发生与其安置有关的问题，由安置地人民政府负责处理；发生其他移交接收方面问题的，由安置地人民政府负责处理，原所在部队予以配合。  
退役军人原所在部队撤销或者转隶、合并的，由原所在部队的上级单位或者转隶、合并后的单位按照前款规定处理。

**Article 19** During the transfer and reception of an ex-service member, problems related to his/her active service shall be handled by the original unit; problems related to his/her placement shall be handled by the people's government of the place of placement; other problems in respect of transfer and reception shall be handled by the people's government of the place of placement, and the original unit shall cooperate.  
If the original unit of an ex-service member is dissolved, transferred, or merged, the superior unit of the original unit or the unit after transfer or merger shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

### 第三章　退役安置

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　地方各级人民政府应当按照移交接收计划，做好退役军人安置工作，完成退役军人安置任务。  
机关、群团组织、企业事业单位和社会组织应当依法接收安置退役军人，退役军人应当接受安置。

**Article 20** Local people's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the transfer and reception plans, properly carry out the placement of ex-service members and fulfill the tasks of placing ex-service members.  
State organs, mass organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and social organizations shall receive and place ex-service members according to law, and ex-service members shall accept placement.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对退役的军官，国家采取退休、转业、逐月领取退役金、复员等方式妥善安置。  
以退休方式移交人民政府安置的，由安置地人民政府按照国家保障与社会化服务相结合的方式，做好服务管理工作，保障其待遇。  
以转业方式安置的，由安置地人民政府根据其德才条件以及服现役期间的职务、等级、所做贡献、专长等和工作需要安排工作岗位，确定相应的职务职级。  
服现役满规定年限，以逐月领取退役金方式安置的，按照国家有关规定逐月领取退役金。  
以复员方式安置的，按照国家有关规定领取复员费。

**Article 21** For retired officers, the State shall adopt such methods as retirement, transfer to civilian work, monthly pension, and demobilization to properly arrange their placement.  
For those placed by means of retirement and transferred to the people's government for placement, the people's government of the place of placement shall, by combining State guarantee with socialized services, properly carry out service management and guarantee their benefits.  
For those placed by means of transfer to civilian work, the people's government of the place of placement shall arrange job positions and determine corresponding posts and ranks based on their moral integrity and professional competence, as well as their positions, ranks, contributions, specialties, and other factors during active service, and the needs of the work.  
For those who have completed the prescribed years of active service and are placed by means of monthly pension, they shall receive a monthly pension in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
For those placed by means of demobilization, they shall receive demobilization allowances in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　对退役的军士，国家采取逐月领取退役金、自主就业、安排工作、退休、供养等方式妥善安置。  
服现役满规定年限，以逐月领取退役金方式安置的，按照国家有关规定逐月领取退役金。  
服现役不满规定年限，以自主就业方式安置的，领取一次性退役金。  
以安排工作方式安置的，由安置地人民政府根据其服现役期间所做贡献、专长等安排工作岗位。  
以退休方式安置的，由安置地人民政府按照国家保障与社会化服务相结合的方式，做好服务管理工作，保障其待遇。  
以供养方式安置的，由国家供养终身。

**Article 22** For retired non-commissioned officers, the State shall adopt such methods as monthly pension, self-employment, job placement, retirement, and lifelong support to properly arrange their placement.  
For those who have completed the prescribed years of active service and are placed by means of monthly pension, they shall receive a monthly pension in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
For those who have not completed the prescribed years of active service and are placed by means of self-employment, they shall receive a one-time severance payment.  
For those placed by means of job placement, the people's government of the place of placement shall arrange job positions based on their contributions and specialties during active service.  
For those placed by means of retirement, the people's government of the place of placement shall, by combining State guarantee with socialized services, properly carry out service management and guarantee their benefits.  
For those placed by means of lifelong support, the State shall support them for life.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对退役的义务兵，国家采取自主就业、安排工作、供养等方式妥善安置。  
以自主就业方式安置的，领取一次性退役金。  
以安排工作方式安置的，由安置地人民政府根据其服现役期间所做贡献、专长等安排工作岗位。  
以供养方式安置的，由国家供养终身。

**Article 23** For retired conscripts, the State shall adopt such methods as self-employment, job placement, and lifelong support to properly arrange their placement.  
For those placed by means of self-employment, they shall receive a one-time severance payment.  
For those placed by means of job placement, the people's government of the place of placement shall arrange job positions based on their contributions and specialties during active service.  
For those placed by means of lifelong support, the State shall support them for life.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　退休、转业、逐月领取退役金、复员、自主就业、安排工作、供养等安置方式的适用条件，按照相关法律法规执行。

**Article 24** The conditions for applying placement methods such as retirement, transfer to civilian work, monthly pension, demobilization, self-employment, job placement, and lifelong support shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　转业军官、安排工作的军士和义务兵，由机关、群团组织、事业单位和国有企业接收安置。对下列退役军人，优先安置：  
（一）参战退役军人；  
（二）担任作战部队师、旅、团、营级单位主官的转业军官；  
（三）属于烈士子女、功臣模范的退役军人；  
（四）长期在艰苦边远地区或者特殊岗位服现役的退役军人。

**Article 25** Officers transferred to civilian work, and non-commissioned officers and conscripts placed by means of job placement, shall be received and placed by State organs, mass organizations, public institutions, and State-owned enterprises. Priority in placement shall be given to the following ex-service members:  
(1) ex-service members who have participated in combat;  
(2) officers transferred to civilian work who have served as principal leading officers of division, brigade, regiment, or battalion units in combat forces;  
(3) ex-service members who are children of martyrs or are meritorious models; and  
(4) ex-service members who have served in arduous border areas or special posts for a long period.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　机关、群团组织、事业单位接收安置转业军官、安排工作的军士和义务兵的，应当按照国家有关规定给予编制保障。  
国有企业接收安置转业军官、安排工作的军士和义务兵的，应当按照国家规定与其签订劳动合同，保障相应待遇。  
前两款规定的用人单位依法裁减人员时，应当优先留用接收安置的转业和安排工作的退役军人。

**Article 26** State organs, mass organizations, and public institutions receiving and placing officers transferred to civilian work and non-commissioned officers and conscripts placed by means of job placement shall provide staffing guarantees in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
State-owned enterprises receiving and placing officers transferred to civilian work and non-commissioned officers and conscripts placed by means of job placement shall conclude labor contracts with them in accordance with State regulations and guarantee corresponding benefits.  
When lawfully reducing their workforce, the employing units specified in the preceding two paragraphs shall give priority to retaining the ex-service members who have been transferred or placed through job placement.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　以逐月领取退役金方式安置的退役军官和军士，被录用为公务员或者聘用为事业单位工作人员的，自被录用、聘用下月起停发退役金，其待遇按照公务员、事业单位工作人员管理相关法律法规执行。

**Article 27** For retired officers and non-commissioned officers placed by means of monthly pension, if they are recruited as public servants or employed as staff members of public institutions, the monthly pension shall cease to be paid from the month following the recruitment or employment, and their benefits shall be governed by the relevant laws and regulations on the management of public servants and staff members of public institutions.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家建立伤病残退役军人指令性移交安置、收治休养制度。军队有关部门应当及时将伤病残退役军人移交安置地人民政府安置。安置地人民政府应当妥善解决伤病残退役军人的住房、医疗、康复、护理和生活困难。

**Article 28** The State shall establish a system for mandatory transfer, placement, and admission for rest and recuperation of ex-service members with wounds, illnesses, or disabilities. The relevant departments of the armed forces shall promptly transfer ex-service members with wounds, illnesses, or disabilities to the people's government of the place of placement for placement. The people's government of the place of placement shall properly address the housing, medical care, rehabilitation, nursing care, and living difficulties of ex-service members with wounds, illnesses, or disabilities.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　各级人民政府加强拥军优属工作，为军人和家属排忧解难。  
符合条件的军官和军士退出现役时，其配偶和子女可以按照国家有关规定随调随迁。  
随调配偶在机关或者事业单位工作，符合有关法律法规规定的，安置地人民政府负责安排到相应的工作单位；随调配偶在其他单位工作或者无工作单位的，安置地人民政府应当提供就业指导，协助实现就业。  
随迁子女需要转学、入学的，安置地人民政府教育行政部门应当予以及时办理。对下列退役军人的随迁子女，优先保障：  
（一）参战退役军人；  
（二）属于烈士子女、功臣模范的退役军人；  
（三）长期在艰苦边远地区或者特殊岗位服现役的退役军人；  
（四）其他符合条件的退役军人。

**Article 29** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the work of supporting the military and giving preferential treatment to their families, and help resolve difficulties for military personnel and their families.  
When eligible officers and non-commissioned officers retire from active service, their spouses and children may be transferred or relocated accordingly in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
If the spouse to be transferred works in a State organ or a public institution and meets the requirements of relevant laws and regulations, the people's government of the place of placement shall arrange for the spouse to be placed in a corresponding work unit; if the spouse to be transferred works in another unit or is unemployed, the people's government of the place of placement shall provide employment guidance and assist in achieving employment.  
If the children to be relocated need to transfer schools or enroll in schools, the education administrative department of the people's government of the place of placement shall promptly handle the matter. Priority shall be given to the following children to be relocated of ex-service members:  
(1) ex-service members who have participated in combat;  
(2) ex-service members who are children of martyrs or are meritorious models;  
(3) ex-service members who have served in arduous border areas or special posts for a long period; and  
(4) other eligible ex-service members.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　军人退役安置的具体办法由国务院、中央军事委员会制定。

**Article 30** The specific measures for the placement of ex-service members shall be formulated by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

### 第四章　教育培训

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　退役军人的教育培训应当以提高就业质量为导向，紧密围绕社会需求，为退役军人提供有特色、精细化、针对性强的培训服务。  
国家采取措施加强对退役军人的教育培训，帮助退役军人完善知识结构，提高思想政治水平、职业技能水平和综合职业素养，提升就业创业能力。

**Article 31** Education and training for ex-service members shall be guided by the goal of improving employment quality, closely focus on social needs, and provide ex-service members with distinctive, refined, and highly targeted training services.  
The State shall take measures to strengthen education and training for ex-service members, help them improve their knowledge structure, enhance their political and ideological awareness, vocational skill levels, and comprehensive professional competence, and increase their capacity for employment and entrepreneurship.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家建立学历教育和职业技能培训并行并举的退役军人教育培训体系，建立退役军人教育培训协调机制，统筹规划退役军人教育培训工作。

**Article 32** The State shall establish an education and training system for ex-service members that combines academic education and vocational skill training, establish a coordination mechanism for education and training of ex-service members, and make overall plans for the education and training of ex-service members.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　军人退役前，所在部队在保证完成军事任务的前提下，可以根据部队特点和条件提供职业技能储备培训，组织参加高等教育自学考试和各类高等学校举办的高等学历继续教育，以及知识拓展、技能培训等非学历继续教育。  
部队所在地县级以上地方人民政府退役军人工作主管部门应当为现役军人所在部队开展教育培训提供支持和协助。

**Article 33** Before military personnel retire from active service, their units may, on the premise of ensuring the completion of military tasks, provide vocational skill reserve training based on the characteristics and conditions of the unit, organize participation in higher education self-study examinations and higher academic continuing education organized by various institutions of higher learning, as well as non-degree continuing education such as knowledge expansion and skill training.  
The competent department of ex-service member affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level where the unit is stationed shall provide support and assistance for the unit of active-duty military personnel to carry out education and training.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　退役军人在接受学历教育时，按照国家有关规定享受学费和助学金资助等国家教育资助政策。  
高等学校根据国家统筹安排，可以通过单列计划、单独招生等方式招考退役军人。

**Article 34** When receiving academic education, ex-service members shall enjoy State education assistance policies such as tuition fees and grant assistance in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Institutions of higher learning may, based on the overall arrangements of the State, recruit ex-service members through separate plans, separate enrollment, and other means.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　现役军人入伍前已被普通高等学校录取或者是正在普通高等学校就学的学生，服现役期间保留入学资格或者学籍，退役后两年内允许入学或者复学，可以按照国家有关规定转入本校其他专业学习。达到报考研究生条件的，按照国家有关规定享受优惠政策。

**Article 35** For active-duty military personnel who have been admitted to ordinary institutions of higher learning before enlistment or are currently studying in ordinary institutions of higher learning, their admission qualification or student status shall be retained during active service, and they shall be allowed to enter school or resume studies within two years after retirement, and may transfer to other majors in the same institution in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. If they meet the conditions for applying for graduate studies, they shall enjoy preferential policies in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家依托和支持普通高等学校、职业院校（含技工院校）、专业培训机构等教育资源，为退役军人提供职业技能培训。退役军人未达到法定退休年龄需要就业创业的，可以享受职业技能培训补贴等相应扶持政策。  
军人退出现役，安置地人民政府应当根据就业需求组织其免费参加职业教育、技能培训，经考试考核合格的，发给相应的学历证书、职业资格证书或者职业技能等级证书并推荐就业。

**Article 36** The State shall rely on and support educational resources such as ordinary institutions of higher learning, vocational colleges (including technical colleges), and specialized training institutions to provide vocational skill training for ex-service members. Ex-service members who need employment or entrepreneurship before reaching the statutory retirement age may enjoy corresponding support policies such as vocational skill training subsidies.  
When military personnel retire from active service, the people's government of the place of placement shall organize them to participate in vocational education and skill training free of charge based on employment needs. Those who pass the examinations and assessments shall be issued corresponding academic certificates, vocational qualification certificates, or vocational skill level certificates, and be recommended for employment.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　省级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门会同有关部门加强动态管理，定期对为退役军人提供职业技能培训的普通高等学校、职业院校（含技工院校）、专业培训机构的培训质量进行检查和考核，提高职业技能培训质量和水平。

**Article 37** The competent department of ex-service member affairs of the provincial people's government shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments, strengthen dynamic management, regularly inspect and assess the training quality of ordinary institutions of higher learning, vocational colleges (including technical colleges), and specialized training institutions that provide vocational skill training for ex-service members, so as to improve the quality and level of vocational skill training.

### 第五章　就业创业

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家采取政府推动、市场引导、社会支持相结合的方式，鼓励和扶持退役军人就业创业。

**Article 38** The State shall, by combining government promotion, market guidance, and social support, encourage and support the employment and entrepreneurship of ex-service members.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　各级人民政府应当加强对退役军人就业创业的指导和服务。  
县级以上地方人民政府退役军人工作主管部门应当加强对退役军人就业创业的宣传、组织、协调等工作，会同有关部门采取退役军人专场招聘会等形式，开展就业推荐、职业指导，帮助退役军人就业。

**Article 39** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen guidance and services for the employment and entrepreneurship of ex-service members.  
The competent departments of ex-service member affairs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen publicity, organization, and coordination for the employment and entrepreneurship of ex-service members, and, in conjunction with the relevant departments, adopt forms such as special job fairs for ex-service members to carry out job recommendation and career guidance, and help ex-service members obtain employment.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　服现役期间因战、因公、因病致残被评定残疾等级和退役后补评或者重新评定残疾等级的残疾退役军人，有劳动能力和就业意愿的，优先享受国家规定的残疾人就业优惠政策。

**Article 40** Disabled ex-service members who were rated with a disability level due to combat, duty, or illness during active service, or whose disability level is supplemented or reassessed after retirement, and who have the ability to work and the willingness to be employed, shall enjoy priority in the preferential policies for employment of persons with disabilities as prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　公共人力资源服务机构应当免费为退役军人提供职业介绍、创业指导等服务。  
国家鼓励经营性人力资源服务机构和社会组织为退役军人就业创业提供免费或者优惠服务。  
退役军人未能及时就业的，在人力资源和社会保障部门办理求职登记后，可以按照规定享受失业保险待遇。

**Article 41** Public human resources service institutions shall provide ex-service members with free job placement, entrepreneurship guidance, and other services.  
The State shall encourage for-profit human resources service institutions and social organizations to provide free or preferential services for the employment and entrepreneurship of ex-service members.  
Ex-service members who are unable to obtain employment in a timely manner may, after registering as job seekers with the human resources and social security department, enjoy unemployment insurance benefits in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　机关、群团组织、事业单位和国有企业在招录或者招聘人员时，对退役军人的年龄和学历条件可以适当放宽，同等条件下优先招录、招聘退役军人。退役的军士和义务兵服现役经历视为基层工作经历。  
退役的军士和义务兵入伍前是机关、群团组织、事业单位或者国有企业人员的，退役后可以选择复职复工。

**Article 42** When recruiting or hiring personnel, State organs, mass organizations, public institutions, and State-owned enterprises may appropriately relax the age and academic requirements for ex-service members, and shall give priority to recruiting or hiring ex-service members under equal conditions. The active service experience of retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts shall be regarded as grassroots work experience.  
If retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts were staff members of State organs, mass organizations, public institutions, or State-owned enterprises before enlistment, they may choose to resume their positions and work after retirement.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　各地应当设置一定数量的基层公务员职位，面向服现役满五年的高校毕业生退役军人招考。  
服现役满五年的高校毕业生退役军人可以报考面向服务基层项目人员定向考录的职位，同服务基层项目人员共享公务员定向考录计划。  
各地应当注重从优秀退役军人中选聘党的基层组织、社区和村专职工作人员。  
军队文职人员岗位、国防教育机构岗位等，应当优先选用符合条件的退役军人。  
国家鼓励退役军人参加稳边固边等边疆建设工作。

**Article 43** All localities shall set up a certain number of grassroots civil servant positions for the recruitment of ex-service members who are college graduates with at least five years of active service.  
Ex-service members who are college graduates with at least five years of active service may apply for positions specially directed at personnel serving in grassroots projects, and share the targeted civil servant recruitment plan with such personnel.  
All localities shall pay attention to selecting and employing full-time staff for grassroots Party organizations, communities, and villages from among outstanding ex-service members.  
Positions of civilian personnel in the armed forces and positions in national defense education institutions shall give priority to employing eligible ex-service members.  
The State shall encourage ex-service members to participate in work such as stabilizing and consolidating border areas.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　退役军人服现役年限计算为工龄，退役后与所在单位工作年限累计计算。

**Article 44** The years of active service of ex-service members shall be counted as years of service, and after retirement, they shall be cumulatively counted together with the years of work in the employing unit.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上地方人民政府投资建设或者与社会共建的创业孵化基地和创业园区，应当优先为退役军人创业提供服务。有条件的地区可以建立退役军人创业孵化基地和创业园区，为退役军人提供经营场地、投资融资等方面的优惠服务。

**Article 45** Entrepreneurship incubation bases and entrepreneurship parks invested in or jointly built with society by local people's governments at or above the county level shall give priority to providing services for ex-service members to start businesses. Regions with conditions may establish entrepreneurship incubation bases and entrepreneurship parks for ex-service members, providing them with preferential services in terms of business premises, investment and financing, and other aspects.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　退役军人创办小微企业，可以按照国家有关规定申请创业担保贷款，并享受贷款贴息等融资优惠政策。  
退役军人从事个体经营，依法享受税收优惠政策。

**Article 46** Ex-service members who establish small and micro enterprises may apply for startup guaranteed loans in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and enjoy financing preferential policies such as loan interest subsidies.  
Ex-service members who engage in individual businesses shall enjoy tax preferential policies according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　用人单位招用退役军人符合国家规定的，依法享受税收优惠等政策。

**Article 47** Employers that recruit ex-service members in accordance with State regulations shall enjoy tax preferential policies and other policies according to law.

### 第六章　抚恤优待

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　各级人民政府应当坚持普惠与优待叠加的原则，在保障退役军人享受普惠性政策和公共服务基础上，结合服现役期间所做贡献和各地实际情况给予优待。  
对参战退役军人，应当提高优待标准。

**Article 48** People's governments at all levels shall adhere to the principle of combining universal benefits with preferential treatment, and on the basis of ensuring that ex-service members enjoy universal policies and public services, provide preferential treatment in light of the contributions made during active service and the actual conditions of each locality.  
For ex-service members who have participated in combat, the standards of preferential treatment shall be raised.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家逐步消除退役军人抚恤优待制度城乡差异、缩小地区差异，建立统筹平衡的抚恤优待量化标准体系。

**Article 49** The State shall gradually eliminate the differences between urban and rural areas in the pension and preferential treatment system for ex-service members, narrow regional differences, and establish a coordinated and balanced quantitative standard system for pension and preferential treatment.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　退役军人依法参加养老、医疗、工伤、失业、生育等社会保险，并享受相应待遇。  
退役军人服现役年限与入伍前、退役后参加职工基本养老保险、职工基本医疗保险、失业保险的缴费年限依法合并计算。

**Article 50** Ex-service members shall participate in social insurance such as old-age, medical, work-related injury, unemployment, and maternity insurance according to law, and enjoy corresponding benefits.  
The years of active service of ex-service members shall be combined with the contribution years for basic old-age insurance for employees, basic medical insurance for employees, and unemployment insurance before enlistment and after retirement, and be calculated cumulatively according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　退役军人符合安置住房优待条件的，实行市场购买与军地集中统建相结合，由安置地人民政府统筹规划、科学实施。

**Article 51** Where a veteran meets the conditions for preferential treatment in resettlement housing, a combination of market purchase and central construction by the military and local authorities shall be adopted, and the people's government of the resettlement place shall make overall planning and scientific implementation.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　军队医疗机构、公立医疗机构应当为退役军人就医提供优待服务，并对参战退役军人、残疾退役军人给予优惠。

**Article 52** Military medical institutions and public medical institutions shall provide preferential services for veterans in medical treatment, and shall grant preferential treatment to combat veterans and disabled veterans.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　退役军人凭退役军人优待证等有效证件享受公共交通、文化和旅游等优待，具体办法由省级人民政府制定。

**Article 53** Veterans shall enjoy preferential treatment in public transportation, culture, tourism, etc., with valid documents such as the veteran preferential treatment certificate. The specific measures shall be formulated by the provincial people's governments.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　县级以上人民政府加强优抚医院、光荣院建设，充分利用现有医疗和养老服务资源，收治或者集中供养孤老、生活不能自理的退役军人。  
各类社会福利机构应当优先接收老年退役军人和残疾退役军人。

**Article 54** The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of preferential treatment hospitals and glory homes, make full use of existing medical and elderly care service resources, and admit or provide centralized support for veterans who are orphaned, elderly, or unable to take care of themselves.  
All types of social welfare institutions shall give priority to receiving elderly veterans and disabled veterans.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家建立退役军人帮扶援助机制，在养老、医疗、住房等方面，对生活困难的退役军人按照国家有关规定给予帮扶援助。

**Article 55** The State shall establish a mechanism for assistance and support for veterans, and provide assistance and support to veterans in financial difficulties in aspects such as elderly care, medical treatment, and housing in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　残疾退役军人依法享受抚恤。  
残疾退役军人按照残疾等级享受残疾抚恤金，标准由国务院退役军人工作主管部门会同国务院财政部门综合考虑国家经济社会发展水平、消费物价水平、全国城镇单位就业人员工资水平、国家财力情况等因素确定。残疾抚恤金由县级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门发放。

**Article 56** Disabled veterans shall enjoy pensions according to law.  
Disabled veterans shall enjoy disability pensions according to their disability grade, and the standards shall be determined by the competent department of veterans affairs of the State Council jointly with the finance department of the State Council, taking comprehensive consideration of factors such as the national economic and social development level, consumer price level, wage level of employed persons in urban units nationwide, and national financial conditions. The disability pensions shall be distributed by the competent department of veterans affairs of the people's government at the county level.

### 第七章　褒扬激励

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国家建立退役军人荣誉激励机制，对在社会主义现代化建设中做出突出贡献的退役军人予以表彰、奖励。退役军人服现役期间获得表彰、奖励的，退役后按照国家有关规定享受相应待遇。

**Article 57** The State shall establish a mechanism of honor and incentives for veterans, and commend and reward veterans who have made outstanding contributions to the socialist modernization drive. Veterans who have obtained commendations and rewards during their active service shall, after retirement, enjoy corresponding benefits in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　退役军人安置地人民政府在接收退役军人时，应当举行迎接仪式。迎接仪式由安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门负责实施。

**Article 58** The people's government of the resettlement place for veterans shall hold a welcoming ceremony when receiving veterans. The ceremony shall be implemented by the competent department of veterans affairs of the people's government of the resettlement place.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　地方人民政府应当为退役军人家庭悬挂光荣牌，定期开展走访慰问活动。

**Article 59** Local people's governments shall hang honorary plaques for veteran families and regularly carry out visits and condolence activities.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国家、地方和军队举行重大庆典活动时，应当邀请退役军人代表参加。  
被邀请的退役军人参加重大庆典活动时，可以穿着退役时的制式服装，佩戴服现役期间和退役后荣获的勋章、奖章、纪念章等徽章。

**Article 60** When the State, local governments, and the military hold major celebration activities, representatives of veterans shall be invited to participate.  
When invited veterans participate in major celebration activities, they may wear the uniform of their service at the time of retirement and wear medals, decorations, and commemorative badges awarded during and after their active service.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家注重发挥退役军人在爱国主义教育和国防教育活动中的积极作用。机关、群团组织、企业事业单位和社会组织可以邀请退役军人协助开展爱国主义教育和国防教育。县级以上人民政府教育行政部门可以邀请退役军人参加学校国防教育培训，学校可以聘请退役军人参与学生军事训练。

**Article 61** The State shall emphasize playing the active role of veterans in patriotic education and national defense education activities. State organs, mass organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and social organizations may invite veterans to assist in carrying out patriotic education and national defense education. The education administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level may invite veterans to participate in school national defense education training, and schools may employ veterans to participate in student military training.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　县级以上人民政府退役军人工作主管部门应当加强对退役军人先进事迹的宣传，通过制作公益广告、创作主题文艺作品等方式，弘扬爱国主义精神、革命英雄主义精神和退役军人敬业奉献精神。

**Article 62** The competent departments of veterans affairs of people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the promotion of the advanced deeds of veterans, and promote the spirit of patriotism, revolutionary heroism, and the spirit of dedication to duty of veterans by means such as producing public service advertisements and creating themed literary and artistic works.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　县级以上地方人民政府负责地方志工作的机构应当将本行政区域内下列退役军人的名录和事迹，编辑录入地方志：  
（一）参战退役军人；  
（二）荣获二等功以上奖励的退役军人；  
（三）获得省部级或者战区级以上表彰的退役军人；  
（四）其他符合条件的退役军人。

**Article 63** The local history compilations institutions of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall compile and record the names and deeds of the following veterans in their respective administrative regions into the local records:  
(1) Combat veterans;  
(2) Veterans who have been awarded second-class merit or above;  
(3) Veterans who have been commended at the provincial or ministerial level or at the level of a theater command or above;  
(4) Other veterans who meet the conditions.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　国家统筹规划烈士纪念设施建设，通过组织开展英雄烈士祭扫纪念活动等多种形式，弘扬英雄烈士精神。退役军人工作主管部门负责烈士纪念设施的修缮、保护和管理。  
国家推进军人公墓建设。符合条件的退役军人去世后，可以安葬在军人公墓。

**Article 64** The State shall make overall plans for the construction of martyrs' memorial facilities, and promote the spirit of heroic martyrs through various forms such as organizing memorial activities for heroes and martyrs. The competent department of veterans affairs shall be responsible for the repair, protection, and management of martyrs' memorial facilities.  
The State shall promote the construction of military cemeteries. After their death, veterans who meet the conditions may be buried in military cemeteries.

### 第八章　服务管理

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国家加强退役军人服务机构建设，建立健全退役军人服务体系。县级以上人民政府设立退役军人服务中心，乡镇、街道、农村和城市社区设立退役军人服务站点，提升退役军人服务保障能力。

**Article 65** The State shall strengthen the construction of service institutions for veterans, and establish and improve the service system for veterans. The people's governments at or above the county level shall set up veteran service centers, and townships, subdistricts, rural and urban communities shall set up veteran service stations, so as to enhance the capacity of service and support for veterans.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　退役军人服务中心、服务站点等退役军人服务机构应当加强与退役军人联系沟通，做好退役军人就业创业扶持、优抚帮扶、走访慰问、权益维护等服务保障工作。

**Article 66** Veteran service institutions such as veteran service centers and service stations shall strengthen contact and communication with veterans, and do a good job in service and support work such as employment and entrepreneurship support, preferential treatment and assistance, visits and condolence, and protection of rights and interests.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　县级以上人民政府退役军人工作主管部门应当加强退役军人思想政治教育工作，及时掌握退役军人的思想情况和工作生活状况，指导接收安置单位和其他组织做好退役军人的思想政治工作和有关保障工作。  
接收安置单位和其他组织应当结合退役军人工作和生活状况，做好退役军人思想政治工作和有关保障工作。

**Article 67** The competent departments of veterans affairs of people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen ideological and political education for veterans, timely grasp the ideological situation and work and living conditions of veterans, and guide the receiving and resettlement units and other organizations to do a good job in ideological and political work and related security work for veterans.  
Receiving and resettlement units and other organizations shall, based on the work and living conditions of veterans, do a good job in ideological and political work and related security work for veterans.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　县级以上人民政府退役军人工作主管部门、接收安置单位和其他组织应当加强对退役军人的保密教育和管理。

**Article 68** The competent departments of veterans affairs of people's governments at or above the county level, receiving and resettlement units and other organizations shall strengthen confidentiality education and management for veterans.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　县级以上人民政府退役军人工作主管部门应当通过广播、电视、报刊、网络等多种渠道宣传与退役军人相关的法律法规和政策制度。

**Article 69** The competent departments of veterans affairs of people's governments at or above the county level shall publicize laws, regulations, and policies related to veterans through various channels such as radio, television, newspapers and magazines, and the Internet.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　县级以上人民政府退役军人工作主管部门应当建立健全退役军人权益保障机制，畅通诉求表达渠道，为退役军人维护其合法权益提供支持和帮助。退役军人的合法权益受到侵害，应当依法解决。公共法律服务有关机构应当依法为退役军人提供法律援助等必要的帮助。

**Article 70** The competent departments of veterans affairs of people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve the mechanism for the protection of the rights and interests of veterans, unblock the channels for expressing demands, and provide support and assistance to veterans in safeguarding their legitimate rights and interests. If the legitimate rights and interests of veterans are infringed upon, the matter shall be resolved according to law. Public legal service institutions shall provide necessary assistance such as legal aid to veterans according to law.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　县级以上人民政府退役军人工作主管部门应当依法指导、督促有关部门和单位做好退役安置、教育培训、就业创业、抚恤优待、褒扬激励、拥军优属等工作，监督检查退役军人保障相关法律法规和政策措施落实情况，推进解决退役军人保障工作中存在的问题。

**Article 71** The competent departments of veterans affairs of people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, guide and supervise relevant departments and units to do a good job in matters such as demobilization and resettlement, education and training, employment and entrepreneurship, pension and preferential treatment, commendation and incentives, and support for the military and preferential treatment for their families, supervise and inspect the implementation of laws, regulations, and policies related to veteran support, and promote the resolution of problems existing in the work of veteran support.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　国家实行退役军人保障工作责任制和考核评价制度。县级以上人民政府应当将退役军人保障工作完成情况，纳入对本级人民政府负责退役军人有关工作的部门及其负责人、下级人民政府及其负责人的考核评价内容。  
对退役军人保障政策落实不到位、工作推进不力的地区和单位，由省级以上人民政府退役军人工作主管部门会同有关部门约谈该地区人民政府主要负责人或者该单位主要负责人。

**Article 72** The State shall implement a work responsibility system and an assessment and evaluation system for veteran support work. The people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the completion of veteran support work into the assessment and evaluation content of the departments responsible for veteran-related work under the people's government at the same level and their persons in charge, and of the people's governments at lower levels and their persons in charge.  
For regions and units that fail to implement veteran support policies in place and have poor work progress, the competent departments of veterans affairs of the people's governments at or above the provincial level shall, jointly with relevant departments, interview the principal person in charge of the people's government of that region or the principal person in charge of that unit.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　退役军人工作主管部门及其工作人员履行职责，应当自觉接受社会监督。

**Article 73** The competent departments of veterans affairs and their staff members shall, when performing their duties, consciously accept social supervision.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　对退役军人保障工作中违反本法行为的检举、控告，有关机关和部门应当依法及时处理，并将处理结果告知检举人、控告人。

**Article 74** For reports and accusations of violations of this Law in the work of veteran support, the relevant organs and departments shall handle them promptly according to law and inform the informants and accusers of the handling results.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　退役军人工作主管部门及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，由其上级主管部门责令改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）未按照规定确定退役军人安置待遇的；  
（二）在退役军人安置工作中出具虚假文件的；  
（三）为不符合条件的人员发放退役军人优待证的；  
（四）挪用、截留、私分退役军人保障工作经费的；  
（五）违反规定确定抚恤优待对象、标准、数额或者给予退役军人相关待遇的；  
（六）在退役军人保障工作中利用职务之便为自己或者他人谋取私利的；  
（七）在退役军人保障工作中失职渎职的；  
（八）有其他违反法律法规行为的。

**Article 75** Where the competent departments of veterans affairs and their staff members commit any of the following acts, their superior competent departments shall order correction and impose sanctions according to law on the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons:  
(1) Failing to determine the resettlement treatment for veterans according to regulations;  
(2) Issuing false documents in the work of veteran resettlement;  
(3) Issuing the veteran preferential treatment certificate to persons who do not meet the conditions;  
(4) Misappropriating, intercepting, or privately distributing funds for veteran support work;  
(5) Determining the objects, standards, or amounts of pension and preferential treatment, or granting relevant treatment to veterans in violation of regulations;  
(6) Using the convenience of their positions to seek personal gain for themselves or others in veteran support work;  
(7) Dereliction of duty in veteran support work;  
(8) Other violations of laws and regulations.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　其他负责退役军人有关工作的部门及其工作人员违反本法有关规定的，由其上级主管部门责令改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 76** Where other departments responsible for veteran-related work and their staff members violate the relevant provisions of this Law, their superior competent departments shall order correction and impose sanctions according to law on the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　违反本法规定，拒绝或者无故拖延执行退役军人安置任务的，由安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，予以通报批评。对该单位主要负责人和直接责任人员，由有关部门依法给予处分。

**Article 77** Where, in violation of this Law, a unit refuses or unreasonably delays the performance of veteran resettlement tasks, the competent department of veterans affairs of the people's government of the resettlement place shall order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, circulate a notice of criticism. The principal person in charge and the directly responsible persons of the unit shall be given sanctions by the relevant departments according to law.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　退役军人弄虚作假骗取退役相关待遇的，由县级以上地方人民政府退役军人工作主管部门取消相关待遇，追缴非法所得，并由其所在单位或者有关部门依法给予处分。

**Article 78** Where a veteran obtains retirement-related benefits by fraud or deception, the competent department of veterans affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall cancel the relevant benefits, recover the illegal gains, and the unit to which he belongs or the relevant department shall impose sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　退役军人违法犯罪的，由省级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门按照国家有关规定中止、降低或者取消其退役相关待遇，报国务院退役军人工作主管部门备案。  
退役军人对省级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门作出的中止、降低或者取消其退役相关待遇的决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 79** Where a veteran commits an illegal or criminal act, the competent department of veterans affairs of the provincial people's government shall suspend, reduce or cancel his retirement-related benefits in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, and report to the competent department of veterans affairs of the State Council for record.  
If a veteran is dissatisfied with the decision of the competent department of veterans affairs of the provincial people's government to suspend, reduce or cancel his retirement-related benefits, he may apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate an administrative action according to law.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 80** Where a violation of this Law constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administrative penalty shall be given according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　中国人民武装警察部队依法退出现役的警官、警士和义务兵等人员，适用本法。

**Article 81** This Law shall apply to officers, sergeants, and conscripts of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force who have lawfully retired from active service.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　本法有关军官的规定适用于文职干部。  
军队院校学员依法退出现役的，参照本法有关规定执行。

**Article 82** The provisions of this Law concerning officers shall apply to civilian cadres.  
For cadets of military academies who lawfully retire from active service, the relevant provisions of this Law shall be applied by reference.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　参试退役军人参照本法有关参战退役军人的规定执行。  
参战退役军人、参试退役军人的范围和认定标准、认定程序，由中央军事委员会有关部门会同国务院退役军人工作主管部门等部门规定。

**Article 83** Veterans who have participated in nuclear tests shall be governed by reference to the provisions of this Law concerning combat veterans.  
The scope, recognition standards, and recognition procedures for combat veterans and veterans who have participated in nuclear tests shall be prescribed by the relevant departments of the Central Military Commission jointly with the competent department of veterans affairs of the State Council and other departments.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　军官离职休养和军级以上职务军官退休后，按照国务院和中央军事委员会的有关规定安置管理。  
本法施行前已经按照自主择业方式安置的退役军人的待遇保障，按照国务院和中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 84** Officers who leave their posts for rest and recuperation and officers at the level of a military command or above who retire after service shall be resettled and managed in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.  
The treatment guarantee for veterans who have been resettled in the manner of independent job selection before the coming into force of this Law shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　本法自2021年1月1日起施行。

**Article 85** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2021.
