# 中华人民共和国老年人权益保障法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2018-12-29  
**生效日期**：2018-12-29

> （1996年8月29日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十一次会议通过　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》第一次修正　2012年12月28日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十次会议修订　根据2015年4月24日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十四次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国电力法〉等六部法律的决定》第二次修正　根据2018年12月29日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第七次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国劳动法〉等七部法律的决定》第三次修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　家庭赡养与扶养 第三章　社会保障 第四章　社会服务 第五章　社会优待 第六章　宜居环境 第七章　参与社会发展 第八章　法律责任 第九章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保障老年人合法权益，发展老龄事业，弘扬中华民族敬老、养老、助老的美德，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称老年人是指六十周岁以上的公民。 第三条　国家保障老年人依法享有的权益。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障老年人合法权益，发展老龄事业，弘扬中华民族敬老、养老、助老的美德，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution with a view to protecting the lawful rights and interests of the elderly, developing undertakings for the elderly, and promoting the virtues of respecting, providing for, and assisting the elderly of the Chinese nation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称老年人是指六十周岁以上的公民。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, the elderly shall mean citizens over the age of sixty.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家保障老年人依法享有的权益。  
老年人有从国家和社会获得物质帮助的权利，有享受社会服务和社会优待的权利，有参与社会发展和共享发展成果的权利。  
禁止歧视、侮辱、虐待或者遗弃老年人。

**Article 3** The State shall protect the rights and interests that the elderly enjoy according to law.  
The elderly shall have the right to receive material assistance from the State and society, the right to enjoy social services and social preferential treatment, and the right to participate in social development and share the fruits of development.  
Discrimination, insult, abuse, or abandonment of the elderly shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　积极应对人口老龄化是国家的一项长期战略任务。  
国家和社会应当采取措施，健全保障老年人权益的各项制度，逐步改善保障老年人生活、健康、安全以及参与社会发展的条件，实现老有所养、老有所医、老有所为、老有所学、老有所乐。

**Article 4** Actively responding to the aging of the population shall be a long-term strategic task of the State.  
The State and society shall take measures to improve the systems for protecting the rights and interests of the elderly, gradually improve the conditions for ensuring the life, health, safety and participation in social development of the elderly, and ensure that the elderly are provided for, receive medical care, have opportunities to contribute to society, can continue to learn, and enjoy their later years.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家建立多层次的社会保障体系，逐步提高对老年人的保障水平。  
国家建立和完善以居家为基础、社区为依托、机构为支撑的社会养老服务体系。  
倡导全社会优待老年人。

**Article 5** The State shall establish a multi-tiered social security system and gradually improve the level of protection for the elderly.  
The State shall establish and improve a social elderly service system based on home care, supported by community services, and underpinned by institutions.  
The whole society shall be encouraged to give preferential treatment to the elderly.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各级人民政府应当将老龄事业纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，将老龄事业经费列入财政预算，建立稳定的经费保障机制，并鼓励社会各方面投入，使老龄事业与经济、社会协调发展。  
国务院制定国家老龄事业发展规划。县级以上地方人民政府根据国家老龄事业发展规划，制定本行政区域的老龄事业发展规划和年度计划。  
县级以上人民政府负责老龄工作的机构，负责组织、协调、指导、督促有关部门做好老年人权益保障工作。

**Article 6** People's governments at all levels shall incorporate undertakings for the elderly into the national economic and social development plans, include the funds for undertakings for the elderly in the fiscal budgets, establish a stable mechanism for fund guarantee, and encourage investment from all sectors of society, so as to ensure the coordinated development of undertakings for the elderly with the economy and society.  
The State Council shall formulate a national development plan for undertakings for the elderly. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the national development plan for undertakings for the elderly, formulate development plans and annual plans for undertakings for the elderly within their respective administrative regions.  
The institutions in charge of work on the elderly under the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing, coordinating, guiding, and supervising the relevant departments to properly protect the rights and interests of the elderly.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　保障老年人合法权益是全社会的共同责任。  
国家机关、社会团体、企业事业单位和其他组织应当按照各自职责，做好老年人权益保障工作。  
基层群众性自治组织和依法设立的老年人组织应当反映老年人的要求，维护老年人合法权益，为老年人服务。  
提倡、鼓励义务为老年人服务。

**Article 7** Protecting the lawful rights and interests of the elderly shall be the common responsibility of the whole society.  
State organs, social organizations, enterprises, institutions and other organizations shall, in accordance with their respective duties, properly carry out the work of protecting the rights and interests of the elderly.  
Grass-roots self-governing mass organizations and legally established organizations for the elderly shall reflect the demands of the elderly, safeguard the lawful rights and interests of the elderly, and serve the elderly.  
Voluntary service for the elderly shall be advocated and encouraged.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家进行人口老龄化国情教育，增强全社会积极应对人口老龄化意识。  
全社会应当广泛开展敬老、养老、助老宣传教育活动，树立尊重、关心、帮助老年人的社会风尚。  
青少年组织、学校和幼儿园应当对青少年和儿童进行敬老、养老、助老的道德教育和维护老年人合法权益的法制教育。  
广播、电影、电视、报刊、网络等应当反映老年人的生活，开展维护老年人合法权益的宣传，为老年人服务。

**Article 8** The State shall conduct national education on population aging to enhance the awareness of the whole society in actively responding to the aging of the population.  
The whole society shall extensively carry out publicity and education activities on respecting, providing for, and assisting the elderly, and cultivate a social atmosphere of respecting, caring for, and helping the elderly.  
Youth organizations, schools, and kindergartens shall conduct moral education on respecting, providing for, and assisting the elderly and legal education on safeguarding the lawful rights and interests of the elderly for the young people and children.  
Radio, film, television, newspapers, periodicals, the internet, and other media shall reflect the life of the elderly, conduct publicity on safeguarding the lawful rights and interests of the elderly, and serve the elderly.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家支持老龄科学研究，建立老年人状况统计调查和发布制度。

**Article 9** The State shall support scientific research on the elderly, and establish a system for statistical investigation and publication of the status of the elderly.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　各级人民政府和有关部门对维护老年人合法权益和敬老、养老、助老成绩显著的组织、家庭或者个人，对参与社会发展做出突出贡献的老年人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰或者奖励。

**Article 10** People's governments at all levels and the relevant departments shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, give commendation or awards to organizations, families, or individuals that have achieved outstanding results in protecting the lawful rights and interests of the elderly and in respecting, providing for, and assisting the elderly, and to the elderly who have made outstanding contributions to participating in social development.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　老年人应当遵纪守法，履行法律规定的义务。

**Article 11** The elderly shall abide by laws and disciplines and perform the obligations prescribed by law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　每年农历九月初九为老年节。

**Article 12** The ninth day of the ninth lunar month each year shall be the Day of the Elderly.

### 第二章　家庭赡养与扶养

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　老年人养老以居家为基础，家庭成员应当尊重、关心和照料老年人。

**Article 13** The provision for the elderly shall be based on home care, and family members shall respect, care for, and attend to the elderly.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　赡养人应当履行对老年人经济上供养、生活上照料和精神上慰藉的义务，照顾老年人的特殊需要。  
赡养人是指老年人的子女以及其他依法负有赡养义务的人。  
赡养人的配偶应当协助赡养人履行赡养义务。

**Article 14** The supporter shall perform the obligations of providing financial support, daily care, and spiritual comfort to the elderly, and attend to the special needs of the elderly.  
The supporter shall mean the children of the elderly and other persons who are legally obligated to provide support.  
The spouse of the supporter shall assist the supporter in performing the support obligations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　赡养人应当使患病的老年人及时得到治疗和护理；对经济困难的老年人，应当提供医疗费用。  
对生活不能自理的老年人，赡养人应当承担照料责任；不能亲自照料的，可以按照老年人的意愿委托他人或者养老机构等照料。

**Article 15** The supporter shall ensure that the sick elderly receive timely medical treatment and nursing care; for the elderly in financial difficulties, the supporter shall provide medical expenses.  
For the elderly who are unable to take care of themselves, the supporter shall bear the responsibility of care; if the supporter cannot personally provide care, the supporter may, in accordance with the wishes of the elderly, entrust the care to another person or an elderly care institution, among others.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　赡养人应当妥善安排老年人的住房，不得强迫老年人居住或者迁居条件低劣的房屋。  
老年人自有的或者承租的住房，子女或者其他亲属不得侵占，不得擅自改变产权关系或者租赁关系。  
老年人自有的住房，赡养人有维修的义务。

**Article 16** The supporter shall properly arrange the housing of the elderly, and shall not force the elderly to live in or move to a house with inferior conditions.  
The children or other relatives of the elderly shall not occupy the housing owned or rented by the elderly, and shall not alter the property rights relationship or lease relationship without authorization.  
For housing owned by the elderly, the supporter shall have the obligation to maintain it.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　赡养人有义务耕种或者委托他人耕种老年人承包的田地，照管或者委托他人照管老年人的林木和牲畜等，收益归老年人所有。

**Article 17** The supporter shall have the obligation to cultivate the contracted land of the elderly, or entrust it to another person, and to take care of the trees and livestock of the elderly, or entrust them to another person, and the proceeds shall belong to the elderly.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　家庭成员应当关心老年人的精神需求，不得忽视、冷落老年人。  
与老年人分开居住的家庭成员，应当经常看望或者问候老年人。  
用人单位应当按照国家有关规定保障赡养人探亲休假的权利。

**Article 18** Family members shall care about the spiritual needs of the elderly, and shall not neglect or cold-shoulder the elderly.  
Family members living separately from the elderly shall frequently visit or greet the elderly.  
Employing units shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, guarantee the right of supporters to take leave for visiting family members.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　赡养人不得以放弃继承权或者其他理由，拒绝履行赡养义务。  
赡养人不履行赡养义务，老年人有要求赡养人付给赡养费等权利。  
赡养人不得要求老年人承担力不能及的劳动。

**Article 19** The supporter shall not refuse to perform the support obligations on the grounds of waiving the right of inheritance or any other reasons.  
If the supporter fails to perform the support obligations, the elderly shall have the right to demand that the supporter pay alimony, among other rights.  
The supporter shall not require the elderly to undertake labor beyond their capability.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　经老年人同意，赡养人之间可以就履行赡养义务签订协议。赡养协议的内容不得违反法律的规定和老年人的意愿。  
基层群众性自治组织、老年人组织或者赡养人所在单位监督协议的履行。

**Article 20** With the consent of the elderly, the supporters may enter into an agreement on the performance of support obligations. The content of the support agreement shall not violate the provisions of law or the wishes of the elderly.  
The grass-roots self-governing mass organizations, the organizations for the elderly, or the units of the supporters shall supervise the performance of the agreement.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　老年人的婚姻自由受法律保护。子女或者其他亲属不得干涉老年人离婚、再婚及婚后的生活。  
赡养人的赡养义务不因老年人的婚姻关系变化而消除。

**Article 21** The freedom of marriage of the elderly shall be protected by law. The children or other relatives of the elderly shall not interfere with the elderly's divorce, remarriage, or post-marital life.  
The support obligations of the supporter shall not be eliminated due to changes in the marital relationship of the elderly.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　老年人对个人的财产，依法享有占有、使用、收益和处分的权利，子女或者其他亲属不得干涉，不得以窃取、骗取、强行索取等方式侵犯老年人的财产权益。  
老年人有依法继承父母、配偶、子女或者其他亲属遗产的权利，有接受赠与的权利。子女或者其他亲属不得侵占、抢夺、转移、隐匿或者损毁应当由老年人继承或者接受赠与的财产。  
老年人以遗嘱处分财产，应当依法为老年配偶保留必要的份额。

**Article 22** The elderly shall have the right to possess, use, benefit from, and dispose of their personal property according to law, and their children or other relatives shall not interfere, and shall not infringe upon the property rights and interests of the elderly by means of theft, fraud, forcible exaction, or other means.  
The elderly shall have the right to inherit the estate of their parents, spouses, children, or other relatives according to law, and the right to accept gifts. Children or other relatives shall not seize, snatch, transfer, conceal, or destroy the property that the elderly should inherit or accept as gifts.  
When the elderly dispose of property by will, they shall, according to law, reserve a necessary share for their elderly spouse.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　老年人与配偶有相互扶养的义务。  
由兄、姐扶养的弟、妹成年后，有负担能力的，对年老无赡养人的兄、姐有扶养的义务。

**Article 23** The elderly and their spouses shall have the mutual obligation to support each other.  
Where younger siblings are raised by elder siblings, after they reach adulthood, if they have the ability to bear the burden, they shall have the obligation to support the elder siblings who have no supporter in their old age.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　赡养人、扶养人不履行赡养、扶养义务的，基层群众性自治组织、老年人组织或者赡养人、扶养人所在单位应当督促其履行。

**Article 24** Where the supporter or the person with the support obligation fails to perform the support or maintenance obligations, the grass-roots self-governing mass organizations, the organizations for the elderly, or the units of the supporter or the person with the support obligation shall urge them to perform.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　禁止对老年人实施家庭暴力。

**Article 25** Domestic violence against the elderly shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　具备完全民事行为能力的老年人，可以在近亲属或者其他与自己关系密切、愿意承担监护责任的个人、组织中协商确定自己的监护人。监护人在老年人丧失或者部分丧失民事行为能力时，依法承担监护责任。  
老年人未事先确定监护人的，其丧失或者部分丧失民事行为能力时，依照有关法律的规定确定监护人。

**Article 26** An elderly person with full civil capacity may negotiate with close relatives or other individuals or organizations that have a close relationship with them and are willing to assume guardianship responsibilities to designate their own guardian. The guardian shall, in accordance with law, assume guardianship responsibilities when the elderly person loses or partially loses civil capacity.  
Where the elderly person has not designated a guardian in advance, when they lose or partially lose civil capacity, the guardian shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家建立健全家庭养老支持政策，鼓励家庭成员与老年人共同生活或者就近居住，为老年人随配偶或者赡养人迁徙提供条件，为家庭成员照料老年人提供帮助。

**Article 27** The State shall establish and improve family support policies for the elderly, encourage family members to live together with the elderly or reside nearby, provide conditions for the elderly to migrate with their spouses or supporters, and provide assistance to family members in caring for the elderly.

### 第三章　社会保障

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家通过基本养老保险制度，保障老年人的基本生活。

**Article 28** The State shall ensure the basic living of the elderly through the basic endowment insurance system.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家通过基本医疗保险制度，保障老年人的基本医疗需要。享受最低生活保障的老年人和符合条件的低收入家庭中的老年人参加新型农村合作医疗和城镇居民基本医疗保险所需个人缴费部分，由政府给予补贴。  
有关部门制定医疗保险办法，应当对老年人给予照顾。

**Article 29** The State shall ensure the basic medical needs of the elderly through the basic medical insurance system. For the elderly who enjoy the minimum subsistence allowance and the elderly from low-income families meeting the conditions, the government shall provide subsidies for the portion of personal contributions required for participating in the new rural cooperative medical care and the basic medical insurance for urban residents.  
The relevant departments shall, when formulating medical insurance measures, provide preferential treatment to the elderly.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家逐步开展长期护理保障工作，保障老年人的护理需求。  
对生活长期不能自理、经济困难的老年人，地方各级人民政府应当根据其失能程度等情况给予护理补贴。

**Article 30** The State shall gradually carry out long-term care security work to meet the nursing needs of the elderly.  
For the elderly who are long-term unable to take care of themselves and are in financial difficulties, local people's governments at all levels shall, based on their degree of disability and other circumstances, provide nursing subsidies.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家对经济困难的老年人给予基本生活、医疗、居住或者其他救助。  
老年人无劳动能力、无生活来源、无赡养人和扶养人，或者其赡养人和扶养人确无赡养能力或者扶养能力的，由地方各级人民政府依照有关规定给予供养或者救助。  
对流浪乞讨、遭受遗弃等生活无着的老年人，由地方各级人民政府依照有关规定给予救助。

**Article 31** The State shall provide basic living, medical, housing, or other assistance to the elderly in financial difficulties.  
Where the elderly have no capacity to work, no source of income, and no supporter or person with the support obligation, or their supporter or person with the support obligation truly lacks the ability to support, the local people's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions, provide support or assistance.  
For the elderly who are vagrants, beggars, or abandoned and have no means of livelihood, the local people's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions, provide assistance.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　地方各级人民政府在实施廉租住房、公共租赁住房等住房保障制度或者进行危旧房屋改造时，应当优先照顾符合条件的老年人。

**Article 32** When implementing housing security systems such as low-rent housing and public rental housing, or carrying out renovation of dilapidated and old houses, local people's governments at all levels shall give priority to the elderly who meet the conditions.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家建立和完善老年人福利制度，根据经济社会发展水平和老年人的实际需要，增加老年人的社会福利。  
国家鼓励地方建立八十周岁以上低收入老年人高龄津贴制度。  
国家建立和完善计划生育家庭老年人扶助制度。  
农村可以将未承包的集体所有的部分土地、山林、水面、滩涂等作为养老基地，收益供老年人养老。

**Article 33** The State shall establish and improve a welfare system for the elderly, and increase social welfare for the elderly based on the level of economic and social development and the actual needs of the elderly.  
The State shall encourage local governments to establish a high-age allowance system for low-income elderly persons over the age of eighty.  
The State shall establish and improve a support system for the elderly from families that have complied with family planning policies.  
In rural areas, part of the un-contracted collectively-owned land, hills, water surfaces, beaches, and other resources may be used as bases for providing for the elderly, with the proceeds used for the support of the elderly.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　老年人依法享有的养老金、医疗待遇和其他待遇应当得到保障，有关机构必须按时足额支付，不得克扣、拖欠或者挪用。  
国家根据经济发展以及职工平均工资增长、物价上涨等情况，适时提高养老保障水平。

**Article 34** The pensions, medical benefits, and other benefits enjoyed by the elderly according to law shall be guaranteed, and the relevant institutions must pay them in full and on time, and shall not embezzle, delay, or misappropriate them.  
The State shall, based on economic development, the increase in the average wages of employees, the rise in prices, and other circumstances, promptly raise the level of old-age security.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家鼓励慈善组织以及其他组织和个人为老年人提供物质帮助。

**Article 35** The State shall encourage charitable organizations and other organizations and individuals to provide material assistance to the elderly.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　老年人可以与集体经济组织、基层群众性自治组织、养老机构等组织或者个人签订遗赠扶养协议或者其他扶助协议。  
负有扶养义务的组织或者个人按照遗赠扶养协议，承担该老年人生养死葬的义务，享有受遗赠的权利。

**Article 36** The elderly may enter into a legacy-support agreement or other support agreement with collective economic organizations, grass-roots self-governing mass organizations, elderly care institutions, or other organizations or individuals.  
The organization or individual with the obligation to support, in accordance with the legacy-support agreement, shall undertake the obligations of caring for the elderly during their lifetime and arranging for their funeral after death, and shall have the right to receive the legacy.

### 第四章　社会服务

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　地方各级人民政府和有关部门应当采取措施，发展城乡社区养老服务，鼓励、扶持专业服务机构及其他组织和个人，为居家的老年人提供生活照料、紧急救援、医疗护理、精神慰藉、心理咨询等多种形式的服务。  
对经济困难的老年人，地方各级人民政府应当逐步给予养老服务补贴。

**Article 37** Local people's governments at all levels and the relevant departments shall take measures to develop urban and rural community elderly care services, encourage and support professional service institutions and other organizations and individuals to provide various forms of services for the elderly living at home, such as daily care, emergency rescue, medical care, spiritual comfort, and psychological counseling.  
For the elderly in financial difficulties, local people's governments at all levels shall gradually provide subsidies for elderly care services.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　地方各级人民政府和有关部门、基层群众性自治组织，应当将养老服务设施纳入城乡社区配套设施建设规划，建立适应老年人需要的生活服务、文化体育活动、日间照料、疾病护理与康复等服务设施和网点，就近为老年人提供服务。  
发扬邻里互助的传统，提倡邻里间关心、帮助有困难的老年人。  
鼓励慈善组织、志愿者为老年人服务。倡导老年人互助服务。

**Article 38** Local people's governments at all levels, the relevant departments, and grass-roots self-governing mass organizations shall incorporate elderly care service facilities into the construction plans of supporting facilities in urban and rural communities, establish service facilities and outlets that meet the needs of the elderly for life services, cultural and sports activities, day care, disease care and rehabilitation, and provide services to the elderly nearby.  
The tradition of neighborhood mutual assistance shall be promoted, and neighbors shall be encouraged to care for and help the elderly in need.  
Charitable organizations and volunteers shall be encouraged to serve the elderly. Mutual assistance services among the elderly shall be advocated.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　各级人民政府应当根据经济发展水平和老年人服务需求，逐步增加对养老服务的投入。  
各级人民政府和有关部门在财政、税费、土地、融资等方面采取措施，鼓励、扶持企业事业单位、社会组织或者个人兴办、运营养老、老年人日间照料、老年文化体育活动等设施。

**Article 39** People's governments at all levels shall, based on the level of economic development and the service needs of the elderly, gradually increase investment in elderly care services.  
People's governments at all levels and the relevant departments shall take measures in terms of finance, taxes and fees, land, and financing to encourage and support enterprises, institutions, social organizations, or individuals to establish and operate facilities such as elderly care, day care for the elderly, and cultural and sports activities for the elderly.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　地方各级人民政府和有关部门应当按照老年人口比例及分布情况，将养老服务设施建设纳入城乡规划和土地利用总体规划，统筹安排养老服务设施建设用地及所需物资。  
公益性养老服务设施用地，可以依法使用国有划拨土地或者农民集体所有的土地。  
养老服务设施用地，非经法定程序不得改变用途。

**Article 40** Local people's governments at all levels and the relevant departments shall, in accordance with the proportion and distribution of the elderly population, incorporate the construction of elderly care service facilities into urban and rural planning and the overall plan for land use, and make overall arrangements for the land and necessary materials for elderly care service facilities.  
Land for public welfare elderly care service facilities may, in accordance with law, use state-allocated land or land owned by farmers' collectives.  
The use of land for elderly care service facilities shall not be changed without statutory procedures.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　政府投资兴办的养老机构，应当优先保障经济困难的孤寡、失能、高龄等老年人的服务需求。

**Article 41** Elderly care institutions established with government investment shall give priority to meeting the service needs of the elderly in financial difficulties, such as those who are childless, disabled, or of advanced age.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国务院有关部门制定养老服务设施建设、养老服务质量和养老服务职业等标准，建立健全养老机构分类管理和养老服务评估制度。  
各级人民政府应当规范养老服务收费项目和标准，加强监督和管理。

**Article 42** The relevant departments of the State Council shall formulate standards for the construction of elderly care service facilities, the quality of elderly care services, and the professions of elderly care services, and establish and improve a system for the classified management of elderly care institutions and the evaluation of elderly care services.  
People's governments at all levels shall regulate the items and standards of charges for elderly care services, and strengthen supervision and management.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　设立公益性养老机构，应当依法办理相应的登记。  
设立经营性养老机构，应当在市场监督管理部门办理登记。  
养老机构登记后即可开展服务活动，并向县级以上人民政府民政部门备案。

**Article 43** To establish a public welfare elderly care institution, the corresponding registration shall be completed in accordance with law.  
To establish a for-profit elderly care institution, registration shall be completed with the market supervision and management department.  
An elderly care institution may carry out service activities after registration and shall file it with the civil affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　地方各级人民政府加强对本行政区域养老机构管理工作的领导，建立养老机构综合监管制度。  
县级以上人民政府民政部门负责养老机构的指导、监督和管理，其他有关部门依照职责分工对养老机构实施监督。

**Article 44** Local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen leadership over the management of elderly care institutions within their respective administrative regions, and establish a comprehensive supervision system for elderly care institutions.  
The civil affairs departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the guidance, supervision, and management of elderly care institutions, and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective duties, supervise elderly care institutions.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上人民政府民政部门依法履行监督检查职责，可以采取以下措施：  
（一）向养老机构和个人了解情况；  
（二）进入涉嫌违法的养老机构进行现场检查；  
（三）查阅或者复制有关合同、票据、账簿及其他有关资料；  
（四）发现养老机构存在可能危及人身健康和生命财产安全风险的，责令限期改正，逾期不改正的，责令停业整顿。  
县级以上人民政府民政部门调查养老机构涉嫌违法的行为，应当遵守《中华人民共和国行政强制法》和其他有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 45** The civil affairs departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall perform their supervision and inspection duties in accordance with law, and may take the following measures:  
(1) Inquire about the situation from elderly care institutions and individuals;  
(2) Enter an elderly care institution suspected of illegal activities to conduct on-site inspection;  
(3) Review or make copies of relevant contracts, bills, account books, and other relevant materials;  
(4) Where an elderly care institution is found to have risks that may endanger personal health or the safety of life and property, order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, order it to suspend business for rectification.  
When investigating the suspected illegal acts of an elderly care institution, the civil affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level shall comply with the Administrative Compulsion Law of the People's Republic of China and the provisions of other relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　养老机构变更或者终止的，应当妥善安置收住的老年人，并依照规定到有关部门办理手续。有关部门应当为养老机构妥善安置老年人提供帮助。

**Article 46** Where an elderly care institution changes or terminates, it shall properly resettle the elderly residents and handle the formalities with the relevant departments in accordance with the provisions. The relevant departments shall provide assistance for the elderly care institution to properly resettle the elderly.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家建立健全养老服务人才培养、使用、评价和激励制度，依法规范用工，促进从业人员劳动报酬合理增长，发展专职、兼职和志愿者相结合的养老服务队伍。  
国家鼓励高等学校、中等职业学校和职业培训机构设置相关专业或者培训项目，培养养老服务专业人才。

**Article 47** The State shall establish and improve the system for the training, utilization, evaluation, and incentive of elderly care service personnel, regulate employment in accordance with law, promote the reasonable growth of labor remuneration for practitioners, and develop an elderly care service team combining full-time, part-time, and volunteer workers.  
The State shall encourage institutions of higher learning, secondary vocational schools, and vocational training institutions to set up relevant specialties or training programs to cultivate professional talents for elderly care services.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　养老机构应当与接受服务的老年人或者其代理人签订服务协议，明确双方的权利、义务。  
养老机构及其工作人员不得以任何方式侵害老年人的权益。

**Article 48** An elderly care institution shall enter into a service agreement with the elderly person receiving services or their agent, specifying the rights and obligations of both parties.  
An elderly care institution and its staff shall not, in any manner, infringe upon the rights and interests of the elderly.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家鼓励养老机构投保责任保险，鼓励保险公司承保责任保险。

**Article 49** The State shall encourage elderly care institutions to take out liability insurance, and encourage insurance companies to underwrite liability insurance.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　各级人民政府和有关部门应当将老年医疗卫生服务纳入城乡医疗卫生服务规划，将老年人健康管理和常见病预防等纳入国家基本公共卫生服务项目。鼓励为老年人提供保健、护理、临终关怀等服务。  
国家鼓励医疗机构开设针对老年病的专科或者门诊。  
医疗卫生机构应当开展老年人的健康服务和疾病防治工作。

**Article 50** People's governments at all levels and the relevant departments shall incorporate medical and health services for the elderly into urban and rural medical and health service plans, and include the health management of the elderly and the prevention of common diseases in the national basic public health service items. The provision of such services as health care, nursing, and hospice care for the elderly shall be encouraged.  
The State shall encourage medical institutions to set up specialized departments or clinics for geriatric diseases.  
Medical and health institutions shall carry out health services and disease prevention and treatment for the elderly.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国家采取措施，加强老年医学的研究和人才培养，提高老年病的预防、治疗、科研水平，促进老年病的早期发现、诊断和治疗。  
国家和社会采取措施，开展各种形式的健康教育，普及老年保健知识，增强老年人自我保健意识。

**Article 51** The State shall take measures to strengthen research and personnel training in geriatrics, improve the level of prevention, treatment and scientific research of geriatric diseases, and promote early detection, diagnosis and treatment of geriatric diseases.  
The State and society shall take measures to carry out various forms of health education, popularize knowledge of geriatric health care, and enhance the self-health care awareness of the elderly.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家采取措施，发展老龄产业，将老龄产业列入国家扶持行业目录。扶持和引导企业开发、生产、经营适应老年人需要的用品和提供相关的服务。

**Article 52** The State shall take measures to develop the aging industry and include it in the catalogue of industries supported by the State. It shall support and guide enterprises to develop, produce and market products that meet the needs of the elderly and provide related services.

### 第五章　社会优待

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门根据经济社会发展情况和老年人的特殊需要，制定优待老年人的办法，逐步提高优待水平。  
对常住在本行政区域内的外埠老年人给予同等优待。

**Article 53** People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, in light of the economic and social development and the special needs of the elderly, formulate measures for preferential treatment of the elderly and gradually raise the level of such treatment.  
The same preferential treatment shall be given to elderly persons from other places who are permanent residents within the administrative region.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　各级人民政府和有关部门应当为老年人及时、便利地领取养老金、结算医疗费和享受其他物质帮助提供条件。

**Article 54** People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall provide conditions for the elderly to receive pensions, settle medical expenses and enjoy other material assistance in a timely and convenient manner.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　各级人民政府和有关部门办理房屋权属关系变更、户口迁移等涉及老年人权益的重大事项时，应当就办理事项是否为老年人的真实意思表示进行询问，并依法优先办理。

**Article 55** When handling major matters involving the rights and interests of the elderly, such as changes in house ownership or household registration transfer, people's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall inquire whether the matter reflects the true intention of the elderly person and shall give priority to handling it according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　老年人因其合法权益受侵害提起诉讼交纳诉讼费确有困难的，可以缓交、减交或者免交；需要获得律师帮助，但无力支付律师费用的，可以获得法律援助。  
鼓励律师事务所、公证处、基层法律服务所和其他法律服务机构为经济困难的老年人提供免费或者优惠服务。

**Article 56** Where an elderly person has true difficulty in paying litigation fees when filing a lawsuit because his or her lawful rights and interests have been infringed upon, he or she may apply for deferment, reduction or exemption of such fees; where he or she needs legal assistance but cannot afford to pay lawyer's fees, he or she may obtain legal aid.  
Law firms, notarization offices, grassroots legal service offices and other legal service institutions are encouraged to provide free or preferential services to elderly persons with economic difficulties.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　医疗机构应当为老年人就医提供方便，对老年人就医予以优先。有条件的地方，可以为老年人设立家庭病床，开展巡回医疗、护理、康复、免费体检等服务。  
提倡为老年人义诊。

**Article 57** Medical institutions shall provide convenience for the elderly to seek medical treatment and give priority to the elderly. Where conditions permit, family sickbeds may be set up for the elderly, and services such as circuit medical treatment, nursing, rehabilitation and free physical examination may be provided.  
Free medical consultation for the elderly shall be encouraged.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　提倡与老年人日常生活密切相关的服务行业为老年人提供优先、优惠服务。  
城市公共交通、公路、铁路、水路和航空客运，应当为老年人提供优待和照顾。

**Article 58** Service industries closely related to the daily life of the elderly shall be encouraged to provide priority and preferential services to the elderly.  
Urban public transport, road, railway, waterway and air passenger transport shall provide preferential treatment and care for the elderly.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　博物馆、美术馆、科技馆、纪念馆、公共图书馆、文化馆、影剧院、体育场馆、公园、旅游景点等场所，应当对老年人免费或者优惠开放。

**Article 59** Museums, art galleries, science and technology museums, memorial halls, public libraries, cultural centers, cinemas and theaters, sports venues, parks, tourist attractions and other such places shall be open to the elderly free of charge or at a preferential price.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　农村老年人不承担兴办公益事业的筹劳义务。

**Article 60** Rural elderly persons shall not bear the obligation of contributing labor for the establishment of public welfare undertakings.

### 第六章　宜居环境

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家采取措施，推进宜居环境建设，为老年人提供安全、便利和舒适的环境。

**Article 61** The State shall take measures to promote the construction of a livable environment and provide a safe, convenient and comfortable environment for the elderly.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　各级人民政府在制定城乡规划时，应当根据人口老龄化发展趋势、老年人口分布和老年人的特点，统筹考虑适合老年人的公共基础设施、生活服务设施、医疗卫生设施和文化体育设施建设。

**Article 62** When formulating urban and rural plans, people's governments at all levels shall, based on the development trend of population aging, the distribution of the elderly population and the characteristics of the elderly, make overall plans for the construction of public infrastructure, living service facilities, medical and health facilities, and cultural and sports facilities suitable for the elderly.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国家制定和完善涉及老年人的工程建设标准体系，在规划、设计、施工、监理、验收、运行、维护、管理等环节加强相关标准的实施与监督。

**Article 63** The State shall formulate and improve the engineering construction standard system involving the elderly, and strengthen the implementation and supervision of relevant standards in the stages of planning, design, construction, supervision, acceptance, operation, maintenance and management.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　国家制定无障碍设施工程建设标准。新建、改建和扩建道路、公共交通设施、建筑物、居住区等，应当符合国家无障碍设施工程建设标准。  
各级人民政府和有关部门应当按照国家无障碍设施工程建设标准，优先推进与老年人日常生活密切相关的公共服务设施的改造。  
无障碍设施的所有人和管理人应当保障无障碍设施正常使用。

**Article 64** The State shall formulate engineering construction standards for barrier-free facilities. New construction, reconstruction and extension of roads, public transport facilities, buildings, residential areas, etc. shall conform to the national engineering construction standards for barrier-free facilities.  
People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall, in accordance with the national engineering construction standards for barrier-free facilities, give priority to the renovation of public service facilities closely related to the daily life of the elderly.  
The owner and manager of barrier-free facilities shall ensure the normal use of such facilities.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国家推动老年宜居社区建设，引导、支持老年宜居住宅的开发，推动和扶持老年人家庭无障碍设施的改造，为老年人创造无障碍居住环境。

**Article 65** The State shall promote the construction of age-friendly communities, guide and support the development of age-friendly housing, promote and support the renovation of barrier-free facilities in the homes of the elderly, and create a barrier-free living environment for the elderly.

### 第七章　参与社会发展

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家和社会应当重视、珍惜老年人的知识、技能、经验和优良品德，发挥老年人的专长和作用，保障老年人参与经济、政治、文化和社会生活。

**Article 66** The State and society shall value and cherish the knowledge, skills, experience and fine moral character of the elderly, give play to their expertise and roles, and guarantee the participation of the elderly in economic, political, cultural and social life.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　老年人可以通过老年人组织，开展有益身心健康的活动。

**Article 67** The elderly may, through organizations of the elderly, carry out activities beneficial to physical and mental health.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　制定法律、法规、规章和公共政策，涉及老年人权益重大问题的，应当听取老年人和老年人组织的意见。  
老年人和老年人组织有权向国家机关提出老年人权益保障、老龄事业发展等方面的意见和建议。

**Article 68** When formulating laws, regulations, rules and public policies involving major issues concerning the rights and interests of the elderly, opinions of the elderly and organizations of the elderly shall be solicited.  
The elderly and organizations of the elderly shall have the right to submit opinions and suggestions to State organs on the protection of the rights and interests of the elderly and the development of the aging industry.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　国家为老年人参与社会发展创造条件。根据社会需要和可能，鼓励老年人在自愿和量力的情况下，从事下列活动：  
（一）对青少年和儿童进行社会主义、爱国主义、集体主义和艰苦奋斗等优良传统教育；  
（二）传授文化和科技知识；  
（三）提供咨询服务；  
（四）依法参与科技开发和应用；  
（五）依法从事经营和生产活动；  
（六）参加志愿服务、兴办社会公益事业；  
（七）参与维护社会治安、协助调解民间纠纷；  
（八）参加其他社会活动。

**Article 69** The State shall create conditions for the elderly to participate in social development. In light of social needs and possibilities, the elderly shall be encouraged, on a voluntary basis and within their capabilities, to engage in the following activities:  
(1) to educate young people and children in the fine traditions of socialism, patriotism, collectivism and hard work;  
(2) to impart cultural, scientific and technological knowledge;  
(3) to provide consultation services;  
(4) to participate in scientific and technological development and application according to law;  
(5) to engage in business and production activities according to law;  
(6) to participate in voluntary services and establish public welfare undertakings;  
(7) to participate in maintaining public security and assist in mediating civil disputes;  
(8) to participate in other social activities.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　老年人参加劳动的合法收入受法律保护。  
任何单位和个人不得安排老年人从事危害其身心健康的劳动或者危险作业。

**Article 70** The lawful income of the elderly from participation in labor shall be protected by law.  
No unit or individual may assign the elderly to perform labor that endangers their physical and mental health or to engage in dangerous operations.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　老年人有继续受教育的权利。  
国家发展老年教育，把老年教育纳入终身教育体系，鼓励社会办好各类老年学校。  
各级人民政府对老年教育应当加强领导，统一规划，加大投入。

**Article 71** The elderly shall have the right to continue education.  
The State shall develop education for the elderly, incorporate it into the lifelong education system, and encourage society to run various types of schools for the elderly.  
People's governments at all levels shall strengthen leadership, make unified plans and increase investment in education for the elderly.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　国家和社会采取措施，开展适合老年人的群众性文化、体育、娱乐活动，丰富老年人的精神文化生活。

**Article 72** The State and society shall take measures to carry out cultural, sports and recreational activities suitable for the elderly on a mass scale, and enrich the spiritual and cultural life of the elderly.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　老年人合法权益受到侵害的，被侵害人或者其代理人有权要求有关部门处理，或者依法向人民法院提起诉讼。  
人民法院和有关部门，对侵犯老年人合法权益的申诉、控告和检举，应当依法及时受理，不得推诿、拖延。

**Article 73** Where the lawful rights and interests of the elderly are infringed upon, the infringed elderly person or his or her agent shall have the right to request the relevant department to deal with the matter, or to file a lawsuit with a People's Court according to law.  
The People's Court and relevant departments shall, according to law, promptly accept complaints, accusations and reports concerning infringement upon the lawful rights and interests of the elderly, and shall not refuse or delay.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　不履行保护老年人合法权益职责的部门或者组织，其上级主管部门应当给予批评教育，责令改正。  
国家工作人员违法失职，致使老年人合法权益受到损害的，由其所在单位或者上级机关责令改正，或者依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 74** Where a department or organization fails to perform its duty of protecting the lawful rights and interests of the elderly, the competent authority at a higher level shall criticize and educate it and order it to make corrections.  
Where a State functionary, through illegal acts or dereliction of duty, causes damage to the lawful rights and interests of the elderly, his or her unit or the organ at a higher level shall order corrections or impose sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　老年人与家庭成员因赡养、扶养或者住房、财产等发生纠纷，可以申请人民调解委员会或者其他有关组织进行调解，也可以直接向人民法院提起诉讼。  
人民调解委员会或者其他有关组织调解前款纠纷时，应当通过说服、疏导等方式化解矛盾和纠纷；对有过错的家庭成员，应当给予批评教育。  
人民法院对老年人追索赡养费或者扶养费的申请，可以依法裁定先予执行。

**Article 75** Where disputes arise between the elderly and family members over alimony, support, housing, property or other matters, the elderly may apply to the people's mediation committee or other relevant organizations for mediation, or may directly file a lawsuit with a People's Court.  
When mediating the disputes mentioned in the preceding paragraph, the people's mediation committee or other relevant organizations shall resolve conflicts and disputes through persuasion and guidance; family members at fault shall be criticized and educated.  
For an application by an elderly person for recovery of alimony or support, the People's Court may, according to law, rule on advance execution.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　干涉老年人婚姻自由，对老年人负有赡养义务、扶养义务而拒绝赡养、扶养，虐待老年人或者对老年人实施家庭暴力的，由有关单位给予批评教育；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 76** Where a person interferes with the freedom of marriage of the elderly, refuses to perform the duty of support or maintenance owed to the elderly, maltreats the elderly, or commits domestic violence against the elderly, the relevant unit shall criticize and educate the person; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the person shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　家庭成员盗窃、诈骗、抢夺、侵占、勒索、故意损毁老年人财物，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 77** Where a family member steals, defrauds, forcibly takes, embezzles, extorts or intentionally damages the property of an elderly person, if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　侮辱、诽谤老年人，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 78** Where a person insults or defames an elderly person, if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　养老机构及其工作人员侵害老年人人身和财产权益，或者未按照约定提供服务的，依法承担民事责任；有关主管部门依法给予行政处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 79** Where a nursing home for the elderly or its staff infringes upon the personal or property rights and interests of the elderly, or fails to provide services as agreed, it/he/she shall bear civil liability according to law; the relevant competent department shall impose an administrative penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　对养老机构负有管理和监督职责的部门及其工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 80** Where a department responsible for the management and supervision of nursing homes for the elderly or its staff abuses power, neglects duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, the person directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　不按规定履行优待老年人义务的，由有关主管部门责令改正。

**Article 81** Where a person fails to perform the duty of providing preferential treatment to the elderly as prescribed, the relevant competent department shall order him or her to make corrections.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　涉及老年人的工程不符合国家规定的标准或者无障碍设施所有人、管理人未尽到维护和管理职责的，由有关主管部门责令改正；造成损害的，依法承担民事责任；对有关单位、个人依法给予行政处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 82** Where an engineering project involving the elderly does not conform to the standards prescribed by the State, or the owner or manager of barrier-free facilities fails to perform its/his/her maintenance and management duties, the relevant competent department shall order corrections; if damage is caused, civil liability shall be borne according to law; the relevant unit or individual shall be given an administrative penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　民族自治地方的人民代表大会，可以根据本法的原则，结合当地民族风俗习惯的具体情况，依照法定程序制定变通的或者补充的规定。

**Article 83** The people's congress of a national autonomous area may, in accordance with the principles of this Law and the specific conditions of local ethnic customs and habits, and following statutory procedures, formulate flexible or supplementary provisions.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　本法施行前设立的养老机构不符合本法规定条件的，应当限期整改。具体办法由国务院民政部门制定。

**Article 84** Nursing homes for the elderly established before the coming into force of this Law that do not meet the conditions prescribed by this Law shall make rectification within a time limit. The specific measures shall be formulated by the civil affairs department of the State Council.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　本法自2013年7月1日起施行。

**Article 85** This Law shall come into force as of July 1, 2013.
