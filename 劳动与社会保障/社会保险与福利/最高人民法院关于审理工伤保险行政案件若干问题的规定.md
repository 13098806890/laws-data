# 最高人民法院关于审理工伤保险行政案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2014〕9号  
**公布日期**：2014-04-21  
**生效日期**：2014-09-01

> 最高人民法院《关于审理工伤保险行政案件若干问题的规定》已于2014年4月21日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1613次会议通过，现予公布，自2014年9月1日起施行。 2014年6月18日 最高人民法院 关于审理工伤保险行政案件若干问题的规定 法释〔2014〕9号 为正确审理工伤保险行政案件，根据《中华人民共和国社会保险法》、《中华人民共和国劳动法》、《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》、《工伤保险条例》及其他有关法律、行政法规规定，结合行政审判实际，制定本规定。 第一条   人民法院审理工伤认定行政案件，在认定是否存在《工伤保险条例》第十四条第（六）项“本人主要责任”、第十六条第（二）项“醉酒或者吸毒”和第十六条第（三）项“自残或者自杀”等情形时，应当以有权机构出具的事故责任认定书、结论性意见和人民法院生效裁判等法律文书为依据，但有相反证据足以推翻事故责任认定书和结论性意见的除外。 前述法律文书不存在或者内容不明确，社会保险行政部门就前款事实作出认定的，人民法院应当结合其提供的相关证据依法进行审查。 《工伤保险条例》第十六条第（一）项“故意犯罪”的认定，应当以刑事侦查机关、检察机关和审判机关的生效法律文书或者结论性意见为依据。 第二条   人民法院受理工伤认定行政案件后，发现原告或者第三人在提起行政诉讼前已经就是否存在劳动关系申请劳动仲裁或者提起民事诉讼的，应当中止行政案件的审理。 第三条   社会保险行政部门认定下列单位为承担工伤保险责任单位的，人民法院应予支持： （一）职工与两个或两个以上单位建立劳动关系，工伤事故发生时，职工为之工作的单位为承担工伤保险责任的单位； （二）劳务派遣单位派遣的职工在用工单位工作期间因工伤亡的，派遣单位为承担工伤保险责任的单位； （三）单位指派到其他单位工作的职工因工伤亡的，指派单位为承担工伤保险责任的单位； （四）用工单位违反法律、法规规定将承包业务转包给不具备用工主体资格的组织或者自然人，该组织或者自然人聘用的职工从事承包业务时因工伤亡的，用工单位为承担工伤保险责任的单位；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   人民法院审理工伤认定行政案件，在认定是否存在[《工伤保险条例》第十四条](../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/金融证券/工伤保险条例.md#art-14)第（六）项“本人主要责任”、第十六条第（二）项“醉酒或者吸毒”和第十六条第（三）项“自残或者自杀”等情形时，应当以有权机构出具的事故责任认定书、结论性意见和人民法院生效裁判等法律文书为依据，但有相反证据足以推翻事故责任认定书和结论性意见的除外。  
前述法律文书不存在或者内容不明确，社会保险行政部门就前款事实作出认定的，人民法院应当结合其提供的相关证据依法进行审查。  
[《工伤保险条例》第十六条](../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/金融证券/工伤保险条例.md#art-16)第（一）项“故意犯罪”的认定，应当以刑事侦查机关、检察机关和审判机关的生效法律文书或者结论性意见为依据。

**Article 1** When a People's Court adjudicates an administrative case involving the determination of work-related injury, in determining whether the circumstances provided for in Article 14(6) "the employee's primary responsibility", Article 16(2) "intoxication or drug use", and Article 16(3) "self-inflicted injury or suicide" of the Regulations on Work-Related Injury Insurance exist, it shall take as the basis legal documents such as the accident responsibility determination, conclusive opinions issued by competent authorities, and effective judgments of the People's Court, unless there is sufficient evidence to the contrary to overturn the accident responsibility determination or conclusive opinions.  
If the aforementioned legal documents do not exist or their content is unclear, and the social insurance administrative department has made a determination on the facts in the preceding paragraph, the People's Court shall, in conjunction with the relevant evidence provided by it, conduct a review according to law.  
The determination of "intentional crime" under Article 16(1) of the Regulations on Work-Related Injury Insurance shall be based on the effective legal documents or conclusive opinions of the criminal investigation authorities, the procuratorial authorities, and the adjudicative authorities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   人民法院受理工伤认定行政案件后，发现原告或者第三人在提起行政诉讼前已经就是否存在劳动关系申请劳动仲裁或者提起民事诉讼的，应当中止行政案件的审理。

**Article 2** After accepting an administrative case on the determination of work-related injury, if the People's Court discovers that the plaintiff or a third party has already applied for labor arbitration or initiated a civil action regarding the existence of a labor relationship prior to the filing of the administrative action, the court shall suspend the trial of the administrative case.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   社会保险行政部门认定下列单位为承担工伤保险责任单位的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）职工与两个或两个以上单位建立劳动关系，工伤事故发生时，职工为之工作的单位为承担工伤保险责任的单位；  
（二）劳务派遣单位派遣的职工在用工单位工作期间因工伤亡的，派遣单位为承担工伤保险责任的单位；  
（三）单位指派到其他单位工作的职工因工伤亡的，指派单位为承担工伤保险责任的单位；  
（四）用工单位违反法律、法规规定将承包业务转包给不具备用工主体资格的组织或者自然人，该组织或者自然人聘用的职工从事承包业务时因工伤亡的，用工单位为承担工伤保险责任的单位；  
（五）个人挂靠其他单位对外经营，其聘用的人员因工伤亡的，被挂靠单位为承担工伤保险责任的单位。  
前款第（四）、（五）项明确的承担工伤保险责任的单位承担赔偿责任或者社会保险经办机构从工伤保险基金支付工伤保险待遇后，有权向相关组织、单位和个人追偿。

**Article 3** Where the social insurance administrative department determines the following entities as the entities liable for work-related injury insurance, the People's Court shall uphold such determination:  
(1) Where an employee has established labor relations with two or more entities, the entity for which the employee was working at the time of the work-related injury accident shall be the entity liable for work-related injury insurance;  
(2) Where an employee dispatched by a labor dispatch entity suffers work-related injury or death while working for the user entity, the dispatching entity shall be the entity liable for work-related injury insurance;  
(3) Where an employee assigned by an entity to work for another entity suffers work-related injury or death, the assigning entity shall be the entity liable for work-related injury insurance;  
(4) Where the user entity, in violation of laws or regulations, subcontracts contracted business to an organization or natural person that does not have the qualifications for employing workers, and an employee hired by such organization or natural person suffers work-related injury or death while engaging in the subcontracted business, the user entity shall be the entity liable for work-related injury insurance;  
(5) Where an individual affiliates with another entity for external operations, and the personnel employed by such individual suffer work-related injury or death, the affiliated entity shall be the entity liable for work-related injury insurance.  
After the entity liable for work-related injury insurance specified in items (4) and (5) of the preceding paragraph bears compensation liability, or the social insurance agency pays work-related injury insurance benefits from the work-related injury insurance fund, such entity or agency shall have the right to seek recourse from the relevant organizations, entities, and individuals.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   社会保险行政部门认定下列情形为工伤的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）职工在工作时间和工作场所内受到伤害，用人单位或者社会保险行政部门没有证据证明是非工作原因导致的；  
（二）职工参加用人单位组织或者受用人单位指派参加其他单位组织的活动受到伤害的；  
（三）在工作时间内，职工来往于多个与其工作职责相关的工作场所之间的合理区域因工受到伤害的；  
（四）其他与履行工作职责相关，在工作时间及合理区域内受到伤害的。

**Article 4** Where the social insurance administrative department determines the following circumstances as work-related injuries, the People's Court shall uphold such determination:  
(1) Where an employee is injured within working hours and at the workplace, and the employer or the social insurance administrative department has no evidence proving that the injury was caused by non-work-related reasons;  
(2) Where an employee is injured while participating in activities organized by the employer or assigned by the employer to participate in activities organized by another entity;  
(3) Where an employee is injured due to work while moving within a reasonable area between multiple workplaces related to his or her work duties during working hours;  
(4) Other injuries occurring during working hours and within a reasonable area that are related to the performance of work duties.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   社会保险行政部门认定下列情形为“因工外出期间”的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）职工受用人单位指派或者因工作需要在工作场所以外从事与工作职责有关的活动期间；  
（二）职工受用人单位指派外出学习或者开会期间；  
（三）职工因工作需要的其他外出活动期间。  
职工因工外出期间从事与工作或者受用人单位指派外出学习、开会无关的个人活动受到伤害，社会保险行政部门不认定为工伤的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 5** Where the social insurance administrative department determines the following circumstances as "during the period of being away on business", the People's Court shall uphold such determination:  
(1) The period during which an employee, assigned by the employer or due to work needs, engages in activities related to work duties outside the workplace;  
(2) The period during which an employee is assigned by the employer to attend training or meetings away from the workplace;  
(3) Other periods during which an employee is away on business due to work needs.  
Where an employee is injured while engaging in personal activities unrelated to work or to the training or meetings assigned by the employer during the period of being away on business, and the social insurance administrative department does not determine it as a work-related injury, the People's Court shall uphold such determination.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   对社会保险行政部门认定下列情形为“上下班途中”的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）在合理时间内往返于工作地与住所地、经常居住地、单位宿舍的合理路线的上下班途中；  
（二）在合理时间内往返于工作地与配偶、父母、子女居住地的合理路线的上下班途中；  
（三）从事属于日常工作生活所需要的活动，且在合理时间和合理路线的上下班途中；  
（四）在合理时间内其他合理路线的上下班途中。

**Article 6** Where the social insurance administrative department determines the following circumstances as "on the way to and from work", the People's Court shall uphold such determination:  
(1) On the way to and from work within a reasonable time, traveling a reasonable route between the workplace and the place of domicile, habitual residence, or employer-provided dormitory;  
(2) On the way to and from work within a reasonable time, traveling a reasonable route between the workplace and the place of residence of the spouse, parents, or children;  
(3) On the way to and from work while engaging in activities necessary for daily work and life, within a reasonable time and along a reasonable route;  
(4) On the way to and from work along other reasonable routes within a reasonable time.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   由于不属于职工或者其近亲属自身原因超过工伤认定申请期限的，被耽误的时间不计算在工伤认定申请期限内。  
有下列情形之一耽误申请时间的，应当认定为不属于职工或者其近亲属自身原因：  
（一）不可抗力；  
（二）人身自由受到限制；  
（三）属于用人单位原因；  
（四）社会保险行政部门登记制度不完善；  
（五）当事人对是否存在劳动关系申请仲裁、提起民事诉讼。

**Article 7** Where the time limit for applying for work-related injury determination is exceeded due to reasons not attributable to the employee or his or her close relatives, the delayed time shall not be counted within the time limit for applying for work-related injury determination.  
Where the application time is delayed due to any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as not attributable to the employee or his or her close relatives:  
(1) Force majeure;  
(2) Restriction of personal freedom;  
(3) Reasons attributable to the employer;  
(4) Imperfect registration system of the social insurance administrative department;  
(5) The party applies for arbitration or initiates a civil action regarding the existence of a labor relationship.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条   职工因第三人的原因受到伤害，社会保险行政部门以职工或者其近亲属已经对第三人提起民事诉讼或者获得民事赔偿为由，作出不予受理工伤认定申请或者不予认定工伤决定的，人民法院不予支持。  
职工因第三人的原因受到伤害，社会保险行政部门已经作出工伤认定，职工或者其近亲属未对第三人提起民事诉讼或者尚未获得民事赔偿，起诉要求社会保险经办机构支付工伤保险待遇的，人民法院应予支持。  
职工因第三人的原因导致工伤，社会保险经办机构以职工或者其近亲属已经对第三人提起民事诉讼为由，拒绝支付工伤保险待遇的，人民法院不予支持，但第三人已经支付的医疗费用除外。

**Article 8** Where an employee is injured due to the act of a third party, and the social insurance administrative department, on the grounds that the employee or his or her close relatives have already initiated a civil action against the third party or obtained civil compensation, makes a decision not to accept the application for work-related injury determination or not to determine it as a work-related injury, the People's Court shall not uphold such decision.  
Where an employee is injured due to the act of a third party, and the social insurance administrative department has already determined it as a work-related injury, but the employee or his or her close relatives have not initiated a civil action against the third party or have not obtained civil compensation, and they file a lawsuit requesting the social insurance agency to pay work-related injury insurance benefits, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.  
Where an employee suffers a work-related injury due to the act of a third party, and the social insurance agency refuses to pay work-related injury insurance benefits on the grounds that the employee or his or her close relatives have already initiated a civil action against the third party, the People's Court shall not uphold such refusal, except for medical expenses already paid by the third party.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条   因工伤认定申请人或者用人单位隐瞒有关情况或者提供虚假材料，导致工伤认定错误的，社会保险行政部门可以在诉讼中依法予以更正。  
工伤认定依法更正后，原告不申请撤诉，社会保险行政部门在作出原工伤认定时有过错的，人民法院应当判决确认违法；社会保险行政部门无过错的，人民法院可以驳回原告诉讼请求。

**Article 9** Where the applicant for work-related injury determination or the employer conceals relevant information or provides false materials, resulting in an erroneous work-related injury determination, the social insurance administrative department may correct it according to law during the litigation.  
After the work-related injury determination is corrected according to law, if the plaintiff does not apply for withdrawal of the lawsuit and the social insurance administrative department was at fault when making the original work-related injury determination, the People's Court shall render a judgment affirming the illegal nature; if the social insurance administrative department was not at fault, the People's Court may dismiss the plaintiff's claims.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 最高人民法院以前颁布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 10** Where any previous judicial interpretation promulgated by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
