# 高等教育自学考试暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2014-07-29

> (1988年3月3日国务院发布　根据2014年7月29日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为建立高等教育自学考试制度，完善高等教育体系，根据宪法第十九条“鼓励自学成才”的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称高等教育自学考试，是对自学者进行以学历考试为主的高等教育国家考试，是个人自学、社会助学和国家考试相结合的高等教育形式。 高等教育自学考试的任务，是通过国家考试促进广泛的个人自学和社会助学活动，推进在职专业教育和大学后继续教育，造就和选拔德才兼备的专门人才，提高全民族的思想道德、科学文化素质，适应社会主义现代化建设的需要。 第三条　中华人民共和国公民，不受性别、年龄、民族、种族和已受教育程度的限制，均可依照本条例的规定参加高等教育自学考试。 第四条　高等教育自学考试，应以教育为社会主义建设服务为根本方向，讲求社会效益，保证人才质量。根据经济建设和社会发展的需要，人才需求的科学预测和开考条件的实际可能，设置考试专业。 第五条　高等教育自学考试的专科(基础科)、本科等学历层次，与普通高等学校的学历层次水平的要求应相一致。 第二章　考试机构 第六条　全国高等教育自学考试指导委员会(以下简称“全国考委”)在国家教育委员会领导下，负责全国高等教育自学考试工作。 全国考委由国务院教育、计划、财政、劳动人事部门的负责人，军队和有关人民团体的负责人，以及部分高等学校的校(院)长、专家、学者组成。 全国考委的职责是： (一)根据国家的教育方针和有关政策、法规，制定高等教育自学考试的具体政策和业务规范； (二)指导和协调各省、自治区、直辖市的高等教育自学考试工作； (三)制定高等教育自学考试开考专业的规划，审批开考本科专业；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为建立高等教育自学考试制度，完善高等教育体系，根据宪法第十九条“鼓励自学成才”的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provision of Article 19 of the Constitution that "encourage self-study to become talented persons" for the purpose of establishing a system of higher education self-study examinations and improving the higher education system.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称高等教育自学考试，是对自学者进行以学历考试为主的高等教育国家考试，是个人自学、社会助学和国家考试相结合的高等教育形式。  
高等教育自学考试的任务，是通过国家考试促进广泛的个人自学和社会助学活动，推进在职专业教育和大学后继续教育，造就和选拔德才兼备的专门人才，提高全民族的思想道德、科学文化素质，适应社会主义现代化建设的需要。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Regulations, higher education self-study examinations refer to national higher education examinations for self-learners, primarily focusing on academic qualification examinations, and constitute a form of higher education that combines individual self-study, social assistance in learning, and national examinations.  
The task of higher education self-study examinations is to promote extensive individual self-study and social assistance in learning activities through national examinations, advance on-the-job professional education and post-university continuing education, cultivate and select specialized talents with both moral integrity and professional competence, improve the ideological, moral, scientific and cultural qualities of the whole nation, and meet the needs of socialist modernization.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国公民，不受性别、年龄、民族、种族和已受教育程度的限制，均可依照本条例的规定参加高等教育自学考试。

**Article 3** Citizens of the People's Republic of China, regardless of gender, age, ethnicity, race, or educational background, may participate in higher education self-study examinations in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　高等教育自学考试，应以教育为社会主义建设服务为根本方向，讲求社会效益，保证人才质量。根据经济建设和社会发展的需要，人才需求的科学预测和开考条件的实际可能，设置考试专业。

**Article 4** Higher education self-study examinations shall take serving socialist construction through education as the fundamental direction, emphasize social benefits, and ensure the quality of talents. Examination specialties shall be established based on the needs of economic construction and social development, scientific forecasts of talent demand, and the actual feasibility of examination conditions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　高等教育自学考试的专科(基础科)、本科等学历层次，与普通高等学校的学历层次水平的要求应相一致。

**Article 5** The academic qualification levels, such as associate degree (basic level) and bachelor's degree, of higher education self-study examinations shall be consistent with the requirements for the corresponding academic qualification levels of regular higher education institutions.

### 第二章　考试机构

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　全国高等教育自学考试指导委员会(以下简称“全国考委”)在国家教育委员会领导下，负责全国高等教育自学考试工作。  
全国考委由国务院教育、计划、财政、劳动人事部门的负责人，军队和有关人民团体的负责人，以及部分高等学校的校(院)长、专家、学者组成。  
全国考委的职责是：  
(一)根据国家的教育方针和有关政策、法规，制定高等教育自学考试的具体政策和业务规范；  
(二)指导和协调各省、自治区、直辖市的高等教育自学考试工作；  
(三)制定高等教育自学考试开考专业的规划，审批开考本科专业；  
(四)制定和审定高等教育自学考试专业考试计划、课程自学考试大纲；  
(五)根据本条例，对高等教育自学考试的有效性进行审查；  
(六)组织高等教育自学考试的研究工作。  
国家教育委员会设立高等教育自学考试工作管理机构，该机构同时作为全国考委的日常办事机构。

**Article 6** The National Steering Committee for Higher Education Self-study Examinations (hereinafter referred to as the "National Committee") shall, under the leadership of the State Education Commission, be responsible for the work of higher education self-study examinations nationwide.  
The National Committee shall be composed of the persons in charge of the education, planning, finance, and labor and personnel departments under the State Council, persons in charge of the military and relevant people's organizations, as well as presidents (chancellors) of some higher education institutions, experts, and scholars.  
The responsibilities of the National Committee are:  
(1) to formulate specific policies and operational norms for higher education self-study examinations in accordance with the national education policies and relevant policies and regulations;  
(2) to guide and coordinate the work of higher education self-study examinations in provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(3) to formulate plans for specialties to be offered in higher education self-study examinations and approve the offering of bachelor's degree specialties;  
(4) to formulate and examine examination plans for specialties and course self-study examination syllabuses;  
(5) to examine the validity of higher education self-study examinations in accordance with these Regulations;  
(6) to organize research work on higher education self-study examinations.  
The State Education Commission shall establish an administrative body for higher education self-study examination work, which shall also serve as the daily office of the National Committee.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　全国考委根据工作需要设立若干专业委员会，负责拟订专业考试计划和课程自学考试大纲，组织编写和推荐适合自学的高等教育教材，对本专业考试工作进行业务指导和质量评估。

**Article 7** The National Committee shall establish several specialized committees as needed, which shall be responsible for drafting examination plans for specialties and course self-study examination syllabuses, organizing the compilation and recommendation of higher education textbooks suitable for self-study, and providing professional guidance and quality evaluation for the examination work of their respective specialties.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　省、自治区、直辖市高等教育自学考试委员会(以下简称“省考委”)在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府领导和全国考委指导下进行工作。省考委的组成，参照全国考委的组成确定。  
省考委的职责是：  
(一)贯彻执行高等教育自学考试的方针、政策、法规和业务规范；  
(二)在全国考委关于开考专业的规划和原则的指导下，结合本地实际拟定开考专业，指定主考学校；  
(三)组织本地区开考专业的考试工作；  
(四)负责本地区应考者的考籍管理，颁发单科合格证书和毕业证书；  
(五)指导本地区的社会助学活动；  
(六)根据国家教育委员会的委托，对已经批准建校招生的成人高等学校的教学质量，通过考试的方法进行检查。  
省、自治区、直辖市教育行政部门设立高等教育自学考试工作管理机构，该机构同时作为省考委的日常办事机构。

**Article 8** The Provincial Higher Education Self-study Examination Committees (hereinafter referred to as "Provincial Committees") in provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall work under the leadership of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and under the guidance of the National Committee. The composition of the Provincial Committees shall be determined by reference to the composition of the National Committee.  
The responsibilities of the Provincial Committees are:  
(1) to implement the policies, regulations, and operational norms for higher education self-study examinations;  
(2) to draft the specialties to be offered in light of local actual conditions and designate the main examination schools under the guidance of the National Committee's plans and principles for offering specialties;  
(3) to organize the examination work for the specialties offered in their respective regions;  
(4) to be responsible for the examination registration management of examinees in their respective regions and issue single-subject certificate of completion and graduation certificates;  
(5) to guide social assistance in learning activities in their respective regions;  
(6) to inspect, by means of examinations, the teaching quality of adult higher education institutions that have been approved for establishment and enrollment upon the entrustment of the State Education Commission.  
The education administrative departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall establish administrative bodies for higher education self-study examination work, which shall also serve as the daily office of the respective Provincial Committees.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　省、自治区人民政府的派出机关所辖地区(以下简称“地区”)、市、直辖市的市辖区高等教育自学考试工作委员会(以下简称“地市考委”)在地区行署或市(区)人民政府领导和省考委的指导下进行工作。  
地市考委的职责是：  
(一)负责本地区高等教育自学考试的组织工作；  
(二)指导本地区的社会助学活动；  
(三)负责组织本地区高等教育自学考试毕业人员的思想品德鉴定工作。  
地市考委的日常工作由当地教育行政部门负责。

**Article 9** The Higher Education Self-study Examination Work Committees in the areas under the jurisdiction of agencies dispatched by the people's governments of provinces and autonomous regions (hereinafter referred to as "prefectures"), cities, and districts under the jurisdiction of municipalities directly under the Central Government (hereinafter referred to as "Prefectural/City Committees") shall work under the leadership of the administrative offices of the prefectures or the people's governments of cities (districts) and under the guidance of the Provincial Committees.  
The responsibilities of the Prefectural/City Committees are:  
(1) to be responsible for organizing higher education self-study examinations in their respective areas;  
(2) to guide social assistance in learning activities in their respective areas;  
(3) to be responsible for organizing the assessment of ideological and moral character of graduates from higher education self-study examinations in their respective areas.  
The daily work of the Prefectural/City Committees shall be handled by the local education administrative departments.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　主考学校由省考委遴选专业师资力量较强的全日制普通高等学校担任。主考学校在高等教育自学考试工作上接受省考委的领导，参与命题和评卷，负责有关实践性学习环节的考核，在毕业证书上副署，办理省考委交办的其他有关工作。  
主考学校应设立高等教育自学考试办事机构，根据任务配备专职工作人员，所需编制列入学校总编制数内，由学校主管部门解决。

**Article 10** The main examination schools shall be selected by the Provincial Committees from full-time regular higher education institutions with strong professional teaching staff. The main examination schools shall accept the leadership of the Provincial Committees in the work of higher education self-study examinations, participate in setting examination questions and grading papers, be responsible for the assessment of relevant practical learning stages, co-sign the graduation certificates, and handle other relevant tasks assigned by the Provincial Committees.  
The main examination schools shall establish offices for higher education self-study examinations, staff them with full-time personnel according to the tasks, and the required staffing positions shall be included in the total staffing of the school and shall be resolved by the competent department of the school.

### 第三章　开考专业

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　高等教育自学考试开考专科新专业，由省考委确定；开考本科新专业，由省考委组织有关部门和专家进行论证，并提出申请，报全国考委审批。

**Article 11** The offering of new associate degree specialties in higher education self-study examinations shall be determined by the Provincial Committees; the offering of new bachelor's degree specialties shall be subject to demonstration by relevant departments and experts organized by the Provincial Committees, and an application shall be submitted to the National Committee for approval.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　可以实行省际协作开考新专业。

**Article 12** Inter-provincial cooperation may be implemented to offer new specialties.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　开考新专业必须具备下列条件：  
(一)有健全的工作机构、必要的专职人员和经费；  
(二)有符合[本条例第十条](#art-10)规定的主考学校；  
(三)有专业考试计划；  
(四)有保证实践性环节考核的必要条件。

**Article 13** The following conditions must be met for offering a new specialty:  
(1) have a sound working body, necessary full-time personnel, and funding;  
(2) have a main examination school that meets the requirements of Article 10 of these Regulations;  
(3) have an examination plan for the specialty;  
(4) have the necessary conditions to ensure the assessment of practical stages.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　开考承认学历的新专业，一般应在普通高等学校已有专业目录中选择确定。

**Article 14** New specialties that grant recognized academic qualifications shall generally be selected and determined from the existing catalog of specialties in regular higher education institutions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院各部委、各直属机构和军队系统要求开考本系统所需专业的，可以委托省考委组织办理，或由全国考委协调办理。

**Article 15** Where the ministries and commissions under the State Council, their directly affiliated institutions, and the military system request to offer specialties needed by their respective systems, they may entrust the Provincial Committees to handle the matter, or the National Committee shall coordinate the handling.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　全国考委每年一次集中进行专业审批。省考委应于每年6月底前将申报材料报送全国考委，逾期者延至下一年度重新申报办理。审批结果由全国考委于当年第三季度内下达。凡批准开考的专业均可于次年接受报考，并于首次开考前半年向社会公布开考专业名称和专业考试计划。

**Article 16** The National Committee shall conduct centralized specialty approval once a year. The Provincial Committees shall submit application materials to the National Committee before the end of June each year; those who fail to meet the deadline shall defer to the next year for re-application. The approval results shall be issued by the National Committee within the third quarter of that year. All specialties approved for offering may accept candidates in the following year, and the names of the specialties and examination plans shall be announced to the public six months before the first examination.

### 第四章　考试办法

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　高等教育自学考试的命题由全国考委统筹安排，分别采取全国统一命题、区域命题、省级命题三种办法。逐步建立题库，实现必要的命题标准化。  
试题(包括副题)及参考答案、评分标准启用前属绝密材料。

**Article 17** The setting of examination questions for higher education self-study examinations shall be arranged by the National Committee in a unified manner, adopting three methods: nationwide unified question-setting, regional question-setting, and provincial question-setting. A question bank shall be gradually established to achieve necessary standardization of question-setting.  
The examination questions (including backup questions), reference answers, and scoring standards shall be classified as top secret materials before they are used.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　各专业考试计划的安排，专科(基础科)一般为3至4年，本科一般为4至5年。

**Article 18** The arrangement of examination plans for each specialty shall generally be 3 to 4 years for associate degree (basic level) and 4 to 5 years for bachelor's degree.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　按照专业考试计划的要求，每门课程进行一次性考试。课程考试合格者，发给单科合格证书，并按规定计算学分。不及格者，可参加下一次该门课程的考试。

**Article 19** In accordance with the requirements of the specialty examination plan, each course shall be examined once. Those who pass the course examination shall be issued a single-subject certificate of completion, and credits shall be calculated according to regulations. Those who fail may take the examination for that course again on the next occasion.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　报考人员可在本地区的开考专业范围内，自愿选择考试专业，但根据专业要求对报考对象作职业上必要限制的专业除外。  
提倡在职人员按照学用一致的原则选择考试专业。  
各级各类全日制学校的在校生不得报考。

**Article 20** Applicants may voluntarily choose their examination specialties within the scope of specialties offered in their respective regions, except for specialties that are subject to necessary occupational restrictions on applicants based on professional requirements.  
On-the-job personnel are encouraged to choose examination specialties in accordance with the principle of matching study with application.  
Students enrolled in full-time schools of all levels and types shall not apply for the examinations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　报考人员应按本地区的有关规定，到省考委或地市考委指定的单位办理报名手续。

**Article 21** Applicants shall go through the registration procedures at the units designated by the Provincial Committees or Prefectural/City Committees in accordance with the relevant provisions of their respective regions.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　已经取得高等学校研究生、本科生或专科生学历的人员参加高等教育自学考试的，可以按照有关规定免考部分课程。

**Article 22** Persons who have already obtained graduate, bachelor's, or associate degree qualifications from higher education institutions and participate in higher education self-study examinations may be exempted from certain courses in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　高等教育自学考试以地区、市、直辖市的市辖区为单位设考场。有条件的，地市考委经省考委批准可在县设考场，由地市考委直接领导。

**Article 23** Examination venues for higher education self-study examinations shall be established on the basis of prefectures, cities, and districts under the jurisdiction of municipalities directly under the Central Government. Where conditions permit, Prefectural/City Committees may, with the approval of the Provincial Committees, set up examination venues in counties, which shall be directly under the leadership of the Prefectural/City Committees.

### 第五章　考籍管理

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　高等教育自学考试应考者取得一门课程的单科合格证书后，省考委即应为其建立考籍管理档案。  
应考者因户口迁移或工作变动需要转地区或转专业参加考试的，按考籍管理办法办理有关手续。

**Article 24** After an examinee of higher education self-study examinations obtains a single-subject certificate of completion for one course, the Provincial Committee shall establish an examination registration management file for him/her.  
If an examinee needs to change regions or specialties for examination due to household registration transfer or job change, relevant procedures shall be handled in accordance with the examination registration management measures.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　高等教育自学考试应考者符合下列规定，可以取得毕业证书：  
(一)考完专业考试计划规定的全部课程，并取得合格成绩；  
(二)完成规定的毕业论文(设计)或其他教学实践任务；  
(三)思想品德鉴定合格。  
获得专科(基础科)或本科毕业证书者，国家承认其学历。

**Article 25** An examinee of higher education self-study examinations may obtain a graduation certificate if he/she complies with the following provisions:  
(1) complete all courses specified in the specialty examination plan and obtain passing grades;  
(2) complete the required graduation thesis (design) or other teaching practice tasks;  
(3) pass the ideological and moral character assessment.  
The state shall recognize the academic qualifications of those who obtain a graduation certificate for associate degree (basic level) or bachelor's degree.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　符合相应学位条件的高等教育自学考试本科毕业人员，由有学位授予权的主考学校依照《中华人民共和国学位条例》的规定，授予相应的学位。

**Article 26** Graduates of higher education self-study examinations who meet the corresponding degree requirements shall be conferred the corresponding degree by the main examination school with degree-conferring authority in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Academic Degrees.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　高等教育自学考试应考者毕业时间，为每年的6月和12月。

**Article 27** The graduation time for examinees of higher education self-study examinations shall be June and December of each year.

### 第六章　社会助学

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家鼓励企业、事业单位和其他社会力量，根据高等教育自学考试的专业考试计划和课程自学考试大纲的要求，通过电视、广播、函授、面授等多种形式开展助学活动。

**Article 28** The state encourages enterprises, institutions, and other social forces to carry out learning assistance activities through various forms such as television, radio, correspondence, and face-to-face instruction, in accordance with the requirements of the specialty examination plans and course self-study examination syllabuses of higher education self-study examinations.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　各种形式的社会助学活动，应当接受高等教育自学考试机构的指导和教育行政部门的管理。

**Article 29** Social learning assistance activities in various forms shall accept the guidance of higher education self-study examination institutions and the management of education administrative departments.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　高等教育自学考试辅导材料的出版、发行，应遵守国家的有关规定。

**Article 30** The publication and distribution of tutoring materials for higher education self-study examinations shall comply with the relevant provisions of the state.

### 第七章　毕业人员的使用与待遇

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　高等教育自学考试专科(基础科)或本科毕业证书获得者，在职人员由所在单位或其上级主管部门本着用其所学、发挥所长的原则，根据工作需要，调整他们的工作；非在职人员(包括农民)由省、自治区、直辖市劳动人事部门根据需要，在编制和增人指标范围内有计划地择优录用或聘用。

**Article 31** For those who have obtained a graduation certificate for associate degree (basic level) or bachelor's degree in higher education self-study examinations, if they are on-the-job personnel, their work shall be adjusted by their employing units or the competent departments at higher levels in accordance with the principle of utilizing their learning and giving full play to their strengths, based on work needs; if they are non-employed persons (including farmers), the labor and personnel departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, as needed, selectively recruit or employ them within the scope of staffing and increase targets in a planned manner.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　高等教育自学考试毕业证书获得者的工资待遇：非在职人员录用后，与普通高等学校同类毕业生相同；在职人员的工资待遇低于普通高等学校同类毕业生的，从获得毕业证书之日起，按普通高等学校同类毕业生工资标准执行。

**Article 32** The salary treatment of holders of graduation certificates from higher education self-study examinations: after non-employed persons are recruited, they shall be treated the same as graduates of the same category from regular higher education institutions; if the salary treatment of on-the-job personnel is lower than that of graduates of the same category from regular higher education institutions, starting from the date of obtaining the graduation certificate, the salary standard for graduates of the same category from regular higher education institutions shall apply.

### 第八章　考试经费

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县以上各级所需高等教育自学考试经费，按照现行财政管理体制，在教育事业费中列支。地方各级人民政府应妥善安排，予以保证。

**Article 33** The funds needed for higher education self-study examinations at all levels above the county level shall be listed and disbursed in the education expenditure in accordance with the current fiscal management system. Local people's governments at all levels shall make proper arrangements to ensure them.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　各业务部门和军队系统要求开考本部门、本系统所需专业的，须向高等教育自学考试机构提供考试补助费。

**Article 34** Where various business departments and the military system request to offer specialties needed by their respective departments or systems, they shall provide examination subsidies to the higher education self-study examination institutions.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　高等教育自学考试所收缴的报名费，应用于高等教育自学考试工作，不得挪作他用。

**Article 35** The registration fees collected for higher education self-study examinations shall be used for the work of higher education self-study examinations and shall not be misappropriated for other purposes.

### 第九章　奖励和处罚

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　有下列情形之一的个人或单位，可由全国考委或省考委给予奖励：  
(一)参加高等教育自学考试成绩特别优异或事迹突出的；  
(二)从事高等教育自学考试工作，作出重大贡献的；  
(三)从事高等教育自学考试的社会助学工作，取得显著成绩的。

**Article 36** Individuals or units that fall under any of the following circumstances may be rewarded by the National Committee or the Provincial Committees:  
(1) those who achieve particularly outstanding results or have remarkable deeds in participating in higher education self-study examinations;  
(2) those who have made significant contributions in engaging in the work of higher education self-study examinations;  
(3) those who have achieved notable results in engaging in social learning assistance work for higher education self-study examinations.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　高等教育自学考试应考者在考试中有夹带、传递、抄袭、换卷、代考等舞弊行为以及其他违反考试规则的行为，省考委视情节轻重，分别给予警告、取消考试成绩、停考1至3年的处罚。

**Article 37** Where an examinee of higher education self-study examinations commits fraudulent acts such as smuggling notes, passing information, plagiarizing, exchanging examination papers, taking the examination by proxy, or other acts violating examination rules, the Provincial Committees shall, depending on the severity of the circumstances, impose penalties of warning, cancellation of examination results, or suspension from taking examinations for one to three years.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　高等教育自学考试工作人员和考试组织工作参与人员有下列行为之一的，省考委或其所在单位取消其考试工作人员资格或给予行政处分：  
(一)涂改应考者试卷、考试分数及其他考籍档案材料的；  
(二)在应考者证明材料中弄虚作假的；  
(三)纵容他人实施本条(一)、(二)项舞弊行为的。

**Article 38** Where any staff member of higher education self-study examinations or participant in examination organization commits any of the following acts, the Provincial Committee or the unit to which he/she belongs shall disqualify him/her as an examination staff member or impose administrative sanctions:  
(1) altering examinees' examination papers, scores, or other examination registration file materials;  
(2) practicing fraud in examinees' supporting materials;  
(3) abetting others to commit the fraudulent acts specified in items (1) and (2) of this Article.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　有下列破坏高等教育自学考试工作行为之一的个人，由公安机关或司法机关依法追究法律责任：  
(一)盗窃或泄露试题及其他有关保密材料的；  
(二)扰乱考场秩序不听劝阻的；  
(三)利用职权徇私舞弊，情节严重的。

**Article 39** Any individual who commits any of the following acts that disrupt the work of higher education self-study examinations shall be investigated for legal liability by the public security organ or judicial organ according to law:  
(1) stealing or disclosing examination questions or other relevant confidential materials;  
(2) disrupting the order of examination venues and refusing to heed dissuasion;  
(3) abusing power for personal gain or engaging in malpractice, where the circumstances are serious.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家教育委员会根据本条例制定实施细则。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本条例和国家教育委员会的实施细则，制定具体实施办法。

**Article 40** The State Education Commission shall formulate implementing rules in accordance with these Regulations.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate specific implementation measures in accordance with these Regulations and the implementing rules of the State Education Commission.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　本条例由国家教育委员会负责解释。

**Article 41** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the State Education Commission.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　本条例自发布之日起施行。  
1981年1月13日《国务院批转教育部关于高等教育自学考试试行办法的报告》和1983年5月3日《国务院批转教育部等部门关于成立全国高等教育自学考试指导委员会的请示的通知》同时废止。

**Article 42** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.  
The "Report of the State Council Approving and Transmitting the Ministry of Education's Report on the Trial Measures for Higher Education Self-study Examinations" dated January 13, 1981, and the "Notice of the State Council Approving and Transmitting the Request of the Ministry of Education and Other Departments on the Establishment of the National Steering Committee for Higher Education Self-study Examinations" dated May 3, 1983 shall be abolished at the same time.
