# 残疾人专用品免征进口税收暂行规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：1997-04-10  
**生效日期**：1997-04-10

> (1997年1月22日国务院批准　1997年4月10日海关总署第61号令发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一条　为了支持残疾人康复工作，有利于残疾人专用品进口，制定本规定。 第二条　进口下列残疾人专用品，免征进口关税和进口环节增值税、消费税： (一)肢残者用的支辅具，假肢及其零部件，假眼，假鼻，内脏托带，矫形器，矫形鞋，非机动助行器，代步工具(不包括汽车、摩托车)，生活自助具，特殊卫生用品； (二)视力残疾者用的盲杖，导盲镜，助视器，盲人阅读器； (三)语言、听力残疾者用的语言训练器； (四)智力残疾者用的行为训练器，生活能力训练用品。 进口前款所列残疾人专用品，由纳税人直接在海关办理免税手续。 第三条　有关单位进口的国内不能生产的下列残疾人专用品，按隶属关系经民政部或者中国残疾人联合会批准，并报海关总署审核后，免征进口关税和进口环节增值税、消费税： (一)残疾人康复及专用设备，包括床房监护设备、中心监护设备、生化分析仪和超声诊断仪； (二)残疾人特殊教育设备和职业教育设备； (三)残疾人职业能力评估测试设备； (四)残疾人专用劳动设备和劳动保护设备； (五)残疾人文体活动专用设备； (六)假肢专用生产、装配、检测设备，包括假肢专用铣磨机、假肢专用真空成型机、假肢专用平板加热器和假肢综合检测仪；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了支持残疾人康复工作，有利于残疾人专用品进口，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated to support the rehabilitation work for persons with disabilities and to facilitate the import of special articles for persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　进口下列残疾人专用品，免征进口关税和进口环节增值税、消费税：  
(一)肢残者用的支辅具，假肢及其零部件，假眼，假鼻，内脏托带，矫形器，矫形鞋，非机动助行器，代步工具(不包括汽车、摩托车)，生活自助具，特殊卫生用品；  
(二)视力残疾者用的盲杖，导盲镜，助视器，盲人阅读器；  
(三)语言、听力残疾者用的语言训练器；  
(四)智力残疾者用的行为训练器，生活能力训练用品。  
进口前款所列残疾人专用品，由纳税人直接在海关办理免税手续。

**Article 2** Import of the following special articles for persons with disabilities shall be exempted from import customs duties and value-added tax and consumption tax levied at the import stage:  
(1) supporting aids and appliances for persons with physical disabilities, artificial limbs and their parts and components, artificial eyes, artificial noses, internal organ supports, orthoses, orthopaedic shoes, non-motorized walking aids, mobility aids (excluding automobiles and motorcycles), self-help devices for daily living, and special hygiene articles;  
(2) blind canes, guide mirrors, vision aids, and reading aids for the blind for persons with visual disabilities;  
(3) language training aids for persons with speech and hearing disabilities;  
(4) behavioral training aids and daily living skills training articles for persons with intellectual disabilities.  
Import of the special articles for persons with disabilities listed in the preceding paragraph shall be handled by the taxpayer directly at the customs office for duty exemption procedures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　有关单位进口的国内不能生产的下列残疾人专用品，按隶属关系经民政部或者中国残疾人联合会批准，并报海关总署审核后，免征进口关税和进口环节增值税、消费税：  
(一)残疾人康复及专用设备，包括床房监护设备、中心监护设备、生化分析仪和超声诊断仪；  
(二)残疾人特殊教育设备和职业教育设备；  
(三)残疾人职业能力评估测试设备；  
(四)残疾人专用劳动设备和劳动保护设备；  
(五)残疾人文体活动专用设备；  
(六)假肢专用生产、装配、检测设备，包括假肢专用铣磨机、假肢专用真空成型机、假肢专用平板加热器和假肢综合检测仪；  
(七)听力残疾者用的助听器。

**Article 3** Import of the following special articles for persons with disabilities that cannot be manufactured domestically, imported by relevant units, shall be exempted from import customs duties and value-added tax and consumption tax levied at the import stage, upon approval by the Ministry of Civil Affairs or the China Disabled Persons' Federation according to their subordination relationships, and after review by the General Administration of Customs:  
(1) rehabilitation and special equipment for persons with disabilities, including bedside monitoring equipment, central monitoring equipment, biochemical analyzers and ultrasonic diagnostic instruments;  
(2) special education equipment and vocational education equipment for persons with disabilities;  
(3) vocational ability assessment and testing equipment for persons with disabilities;  
(4) special labor equipment and labor protection equipment for persons with disabilities;  
(5) special equipment for cultural and sports activities for persons with disabilities;  
(6) special production, assembly and testing equipment for artificial limbs, including special milling and grinding machines for artificial limbs, special vacuum forming machines for artificial limbs, special flat panel heaters for artificial limbs and comprehensive testing instruments for artificial limbs;  
(7) hearing aids for persons with hearing disabilities.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　[本规定第三条](#art-3)规定的有关单位，是指：  
(一)民政部直属企事业单位和省、自治区、直辖市民政部门所属福利机构、假肢厂和荣誉军人康复医院(包括各类革命伤残军人休养院、荣军医院和荣军康复医院)；  
(二)中国残疾人联合会(中国残疾人福利基金会)直属事业单位和省、自治区、直辖市残疾人联合会(残疾人福利基金会)所属福利机构和康复机构。

**Article 4** The relevant units referred to in Article 3 of these Provisions mean:  
(1) enterprises and institutions directly under the Ministry of Civil Affairs and the welfare institutions, artificial limb factories and honorably discharged soldiers' rehabilitation hospitals affiliated to the civil affairs departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government (including various types of sanitariums for revolutionary disabled soldiers, veterans' hospitals and veterans' rehabilitation hospitals);  
(2) institutions directly under the China Disabled Persons' Federation (China Disabled Persons' Welfare Foundation) and the welfare institutions and rehabilitation institutions affiliated to the disabled persons' federations (disabled persons' welfare foundations) of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　依据本规定免税进口的残疾人专用品，不得擅自移作他用。  
违反前款规定，将免税进口的物品擅自移作他用，构成走私罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，按走私行为或者违反海关监管规定的行为论处。

**Article 5** Special articles for persons with disabilities imported duty-free pursuant to these Provisions shall not be diverted to other uses without authorization.  
Any violation of the provisions of the preceding paragraph by diverting duty-free imported articles to other uses without authorization, which constitutes the crime of smuggling, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, it shall be dealt with as an act of smuggling or an act violating the provisions on customs supervision.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　海关总署根据本规定制定实施办法。

**Article 6** The General Administration of Customs shall formulate implementing rules pursuant to these Provisions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 7** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
