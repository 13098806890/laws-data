# 中国人民解放军实施《中华人民共和国药品管理法》办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2025-04-05  
**生效日期**：2025-06-01

> （2004年12月9日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第425号公布　2025年4月5日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第805号修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强军队药品管理，根据《中华人民共和国药品管理法》和其他有关法律规定，制定本办法。 第二条　军队药品研制、生产（含配制，下同）、储备、供应、使用和监督管理活动，适用本办法。 第三条　军队药品管理工作坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻习近平强军思想，坚持风险管理、全程管控、军地协同的原则，科学严格监管，保障药品的安全、有效、可及。 第四条　在中央军事委员会领导下，中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责组织指导军队药品监督管理工作，具体工作由中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门承办；中央军事委员会机关其他部门按照职责分工做好与药品有关的监督管理工作。 军队团级以上单位后勤保障部门负责本系统、本单位药品监督管理工作以及药品安全突发事件应对工作；同级机关其他部门按照职责分工做好与药品有关的监督管理工作。 第五条　中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门与国务院药品监督管理部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门建立注册审评、联合监管、专业培训、信息通报等工作协调机制，协同开展药品监督管理工作。 国务院药品监督管理部门和县级以上地方人民政府承担药品监督管理职责的部门，按照本办法规定履行相应的药品监督管理职责。 第六条　军队药品专业技术机构依法承担军队药品监督管理所需的审评、检验、核查、监测与评价等工作。 第七条　军队单位应当按照国家和军队药品追溯有关标准和规范加强管理，实现药品可追溯。 军队单位应当按照国家和军队药物警戒有关规定，对药品不良反应及其他与用药有关的有害反应进行监测、识别、评估和控制。 第八条　对在军队药品研制、生产、储备、供应、使用和监督管理工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家和军队有关规定给予表彰、奖励。 第二章　药品储备与供应 第九条　军队实行战备药品储备制度。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强军队药品管理，根据《中华人民共和国药品管理法》和其他有关法律规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in order to strengthen the management of military drugs, in accordance with the Drug Administration Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　军队药品研制、生产（含配制，下同）、储备、供应、使用和监督管理活动，适用本办法。

**Article 2** These Measures shall apply to the research and development, production (including preparation, hereinafter the same), storage, supply, use, and supervision and management activities of military drugs.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　军队药品管理工作坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻习近平强军思想，坚持风险管理、全程管控、军地协同的原则，科学严格监管，保障药品的安全、有效、可及。

**Article 3** The management of military drugs shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, implement Xi Jinping's thought on strengthening the military, adhere to the principles of risk management, full-process control, and military-local coordination, and conduct scientific and strict supervision to ensure the safety, efficacy, and accessibility of drugs.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　在中央军事委员会领导下，中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责组织指导军队药品监督管理工作，具体工作由中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门承办；中央军事委员会机关其他部门按照职责分工做好与药品有关的监督管理工作。  
军队团级以上单位后勤保障部门负责本系统、本单位药品监督管理工作以及药品安全突发事件应对工作；同级机关其他部门按照职责分工做好与药品有关的监督管理工作。

**Article 4** Under the leadership of the Central Military Commission, the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission shall be responsible for organizing and guiding the supervision and management of military drugs, and the specific work shall be undertaken by the business department of the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission responsible for health work; other departments of the organs of the Central Military Commission shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, properly perform the supervision and management work related to drugs.  
The logistics support departments of units at the regiment level and above of the military shall be responsible for the supervision and management of drugs and the response to drug safety emergencies within their own systems and units; other departments of the organs at the same level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, properly perform the supervision and management work related to drugs.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门与国务院药品监督管理部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门建立注册审评、联合监管、专业培训、信息通报等工作协调机制，协同开展药品监督管理工作。  
国务院药品监督管理部门和县级以上地方人民政府承担药品监督管理职责的部门，按照本办法规定履行相应的药品监督管理职责。

**Article 5** The business department of the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission responsible for health work shall establish work coordination mechanisms with the drug regulatory department of the State Council and the drug regulatory departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government for registration review, joint supervision, professional training, information notification, etc., and shall jointly carry out drug supervision and management.  
The drug regulatory department of the State Council and the departments undertaking drug supervision and management responsibilities of the local people's governments at the county level and above shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Measures, perform the corresponding drug supervision and management duties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　军队药品专业技术机构依法承担军队药品监督管理所需的审评、检验、核查、监测与评价等工作。

**Article 6** The military drug professional and technical institutions shall, according to law, undertake the review, inspection, verification, monitoring and evaluation work required for the supervision and management of military drugs.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　军队单位应当按照国家和军队药品追溯有关标准和规范加强管理，实现药品可追溯。  
军队单位应当按照国家和军队药物警戒有关规定，对药品不良反应及其他与用药有关的有害反应进行监测、识别、评估和控制。

**Article 7** Military units shall, in accordance with the relevant standards and specifications of the State and the military for drug traceability, strengthen management to achieve drug traceability.  
Military units shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the military on pharmacovigilance, monitor, identify, assess and control adverse drug reactions and other harmful reactions related to drug use.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对在军队药品研制、生产、储备、供应、使用和监督管理工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家和军队有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 8** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the research and development, production, storage, supply, use, and supervision and management of military drugs shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the military.

### 第二章　药品储备与供应

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　军队实行战备药品储备制度。  
中央军事委员会后勤保障部与国务院有关部门建立军队战备药品和国家储备药品调用协作机制，保障发生战争以及重大灾情、疫情或者其他突发事件时药品应急使用需要。

**Article 9** The military shall implement a system of reserve drugs for combat readiness.  
The Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission and the relevant departments of the State Council shall establish a coordination mechanism for the deployment of military combat readiness drugs and national reserve drugs to ensure the emergency use of drugs in the event of war, major disasters, epidemics or other emergencies.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　军队建立军队基本药物目录，提高基本药物的储备和供给能力，满足军队人员伤病防治基本用药需求。

**Article 10** The military shall establish a military essential drugs list to improve the storage and supply capacity of essential drugs and meet the basic medication needs for the prevention and treatment of injuries and diseases of military personnel.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　军队医疗机构、药材供应保障机构应当从药品上市许可持有人或者具有药品生产、经营资格的企业购进药品；但是，购进未实施审批管理的中药材除外。

**Article 11** Military medical institutions and pharmaceutical supply guarantee institutions shall purchase drugs from drug marketing authorization holders or enterprises with drug manufacturing or operating qualifications; however, this does not apply to the purchase of Chinese medicinal materials that are not subject to approval administration.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　军队医疗机构、药材供应保障机构购进药品，应当建立并执行进货检查验收制度，验明药品合格证明和其他标识，有真实、完整、可追溯的药品购进记录。  
药品购进记录应当注明药品的通用名称、剂型、规格、产品批号、有效期、上市许可持有人、生产企业、供货单位、购进数量、购进价格、购进日期以及国务院药品监督管理部门规定的其他相关内容。  
军队医疗机构、药材供应保障机构应当制定和执行药品保管制度，采取必要的冷藏、防冻、防潮、防虫、防鼠等措施，保证药品质量。

**Article 12** When purchasing drugs, military medical institutions and pharmaceutical supply guarantee institutions shall establish and implement a system for inspection and acceptance upon purchase, verify drug qualification certificates and other marks, and maintain true, complete, and traceable drug purchase records.  
The drug purchase records shall indicate the generic name, dosage form, specification, product batch number, expiration date, marketing authorization holder, manufacturer, supplier, purchase quantity, purchase price, purchase date, and other relevant content as prescribed by the drug regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Military medical institutions and pharmaceutical supply guarantee institutions shall formulate and implement a drug storage system, and take necessary measures such as refrigeration, freeze protection, moisture protection, insect control, and rodent control to ensure drug quality.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　军队药材供应保障机构应当按照划定区域和指定任务，负责军队单位所需药品的供应保障。  
军队药材供应保障机构不得向地方单位和人员供应药品；因发生重大灾情、疫情或者其他突发事件等原因，确需向地方单位和人员供应的，应当经中央军事委员会后勤保障部批准。

**Article 13** Military pharmaceutical supply guarantee institutions shall be responsible for the supply guarantee of drugs needed by military units in accordance with designated areas and assigned tasks.  
Military pharmaceutical supply guarantee institutions shall not supply drugs to local units and personnel; if it is truly necessary to supply to local units and personnel due to major disasters, epidemics, or other emergencies, approval from the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission shall be obtained.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　军队药材供应保障机构从事药品供应保障活动，应当取得中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门核发的军队药材供应许可证；无军队药材供应许可证的，不得供应药品。

**Article 14** Military pharmaceutical supply guarantee institutions engaged in drug supply guarantee activities shall obtain a military pharmaceutical supply permit issued by the business department in charge of health work under the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission; those without a military pharmaceutical supply permit shall not supply drugs.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　军队人员需要接种的疫苗，由军队疾病预防控制机构按照国家规定的疫苗采购渠道向疫苗上市许可持有人采购；对未纳入国家组织采购的疫苗，可以按照军队采购有关规定向疫苗上市许可持有人采购。疫苗上市许可持有人应当按照采购合同约定，向军队疾病预防控制机构或者其指定的军队单位供应疫苗。  
军队疾病预防控制机构和军队其他相关单位从事疫苗储存、运输活动的，应当严格遵守国家有关规定，保证疫苗质量。

**Article 15** Vaccines needed for inoculation of military personnel shall be purchased by military disease prevention and control institutions from vaccine marketing authorization holders through the vaccine procurement channels prescribed by the State; for vaccines not included in the procurement organized by the State, they may be purchased from vaccine marketing authorization holders in accordance with relevant military procurement provisions. Vaccine marketing authorization holders shall supply vaccines to military disease prevention and control institutions or the military units designated by them in accordance with the procurement contract.  
Military disease prevention and control institutions and other relevant military units engaged in vaccine storage and transportation activities shall strictly comply with the relevant regulations of the State to ensure vaccine quality.

### 第三章　军队医疗机构药事管理

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　军队医疗机构应当配备经过国家或者军队资格认定的药师或者其他药学技术人员，负责本单位的药品管理、处方审核和调配、合理用药指导等工作。

**Article 16** Military medical institutions shall be equipped with pharmacists or other pharmaceutical technical personnel who have obtained national or military qualification certification, and such personnel shall be responsible for the drug management, prescription review and dispensing, rational drug use guidance, and other work of the institution.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　军队医疗机构应当坚持安全有效、经济合理的用药原则。

**Article 17** Military medical institutions shall adhere to the principles of safe, effective, economical and rational drug use.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　军队医疗机构配制制剂，应当取得中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门核发的军队医疗机构制剂许可证。  
军队医疗机构配制的制剂，应当是本单位临床需要而市场上没有供应或者任务急需但市场供应不足的品种，并应当取得中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门或者军兵种负责卫生工作的业务部门核发的制剂批准证明文件；但是，法律对配制中药制剂另有规定的除外。

**Article 18** Military medical institutions that prepare pharmaceutical preparations shall obtain a military medical institution preparation permit issued by the business department in charge of health work under the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission.  
The preparations prepared by military medical institutions shall be those that are clinically needed by the institution but are not available on the market, or are urgently needed for missions but insufficiently supplied on the market, and shall obtain a preparation approval certificate issued by the business department in charge of health work under the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission or the business department in charge of health work under the services and arms; except where the law provides otherwise for the preparation of Chinese herbal medicine preparations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　军队医疗机构配制的制剂应当在本单位使用。经中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门或者军兵种负责卫生工作的业务部门批准，可以在指定的军队医疗机构之间调剂使用。  
军队医疗机构配制的制剂，不得在市场上销售。

**Article 19** The preparations prepared by military medical institutions shall be used within the institution. With the approval of the business department in charge of health work under the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission or the business department in charge of health work under the services and arms, they may be transferred for use among designated military medical institutions.  
The preparations prepared by military medical institutions shall not be sold on the market.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　发生重大灾情、疫情以及其他突发事件或者临床急需而市场上没有供应时，经中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门和国务院药品监督管理部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准，医疗机构配制的制剂可以在指定的军队医疗机构和地方医疗机构之间调剂使用。

**Article 20** When major disasters, epidemics, or other emergencies occur, or when there is a clinical urgent need but no supply on the market, with the approval of the business department in charge of health work under the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission and the drug regulatory authority of the State Council or the drug regulatory authority of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the preparations prepared by medical institutions may be transferred for use between designated military medical institutions and local medical institutions.

### 第四章　军队特需药品管理

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　军队特需药品，是指军队用于防治战伤和军事特殊环境引发疾病，以及满足军事行动特定作业人员需求的药品。

**Article 21** Military special-needs drugs refer to drugs used by the military for the prevention and treatment of war injuries and diseases caused by special military environments, as well as to meet the needs of specific operational personnel in military operations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　投入生产的军队特需药品应当经过注册，取得中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门核发的批准证明文件。

**Article 22** Military special-needs drugs put into production shall be registered and obtain the approval certificate issued by the business department responsible for health work under the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　从事军队特需药品生产活动的单位，应当取得中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门核发的军队特需药品生产与配制许可证；其中，地方生产单位取得军队特需药品生产与配制许可证后，应当向所在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门备案。

**Article 23** A unit engaged in the production of military special-needs drugs shall obtain a military special-needs drug production and preparation license issued by the business department responsible for health work under the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission; among them, a local production unit, after obtaining the military special-needs drug production and preparation license, shall file with the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　军队特需药品研制单位委托生产军队特需药品的，应当与符合条件的生产单位签订委托协议和质量协议，并对生产活动进行监督，确保军队特需药品质量安全。

**Article 24** Where a military special-needs drug research and development unit commissions the production of military special-needs drugs, it shall enter into a commission agreement and a quality agreement with a qualified production unit, and shall supervise the production activities to ensure the quality safety of the military special-needs drugs.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　军队特需药品应当在军队内部使用；地方单位因特殊情况需要购进军队特需药品的，应当经国务院药品监督管理部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门审核，并经中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门同意。  
军队特需药品需要转化为民用药品的，应当经中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门审核，由国务院药品监督管理部门按照药品上市注册有关规定办理。

**Article 25** Military special-needs drugs shall be used within the military; where a local unit needs to purchase military special-needs drugs due to special circumstances, it shall be reviewed by the drug regulatory department of the State Council or the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and be approved by the business department responsible for health work under the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission.  
Where a military special-needs drug needs to be converted into a civilian drug, it shall be reviewed by the business department responsible for health work under the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission, and the drug regulatory department of the State Council shall handle the matter in accordance with relevant provisions on drug marketing registration.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门与国务院药品监督管理部门建立技术支持协作机制，为军队特需药品的审评、检验、核查、监测与评价等工作提供支撑。

**Article 26** The business department responsible for health work under the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission and the drug regulatory department of the State Council shall establish a technical support cooperation mechanism to provide support for the review, testing, inspection, monitoring, and evaluation of military special-needs drugs.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　军队后勤保障部门应当对军队药品研制、生产、储备、供应、使用等活动进行监督检查，并对军队特需药品、医疗机构制剂以及国家有特殊管理规定的药品实施重点监督检查。

**Article 27** The military logistics support department shall supervise and inspect the research and development, production, storage, supply, and use of military drugs, and shall carry out key supervision and inspection on military special-needs drugs, pharmaceutical preparations of medical institutions, and drugs subject to special management regulations of the State.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门制定军队药品质量抽查检验计划，组织对军队单位生产、储备、供应、使用的药品以及委托生产的军队特需药品进行抽查检验。药品质量抽查检验结果按照规定向军队有关单位和地方有关药品监督管理部门通报。  
军队医疗机构、科研机构、药材供应保障机构发现药品有疑似质量问题的，应当按照规定送交军队药品专业技术机构进行质量检验。

**Article 28** The business department responsible for health work under the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission shall formulate a plan for the random inspection and testing of military drug quality, and organize random inspection and testing of drugs produced, stored, supplied, and used by military units, as well as military special-needs drugs produced under commission. The results of drug quality random inspection and testing shall be notified to relevant military units and relevant local drug regulatory departments in accordance with regulations.  
Where a military medical institution, scientific research institution, or pharmaceutical supply support institution discovers a drug with a suspected quality problem, it shall submit the drug to a military drug professional technical institution for quality testing in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　军队药品专业技术机构应当根据抽查检验任务和军队有关单位提出的药品质量检验需求，对相关药品进行质量检验，及时出具检验结论。  
军队药品专业技术机构不得收取药品质量检验费用，抽查检验所需样品由其自行购买。

**Article 29** A military drug professional technical institution shall, in accordance with the random inspection and testing tasks and the drug quality testing requirements proposed by relevant military units, conduct quality testing on relevant drugs and issue testing conclusions in a timely manner.  
A military drug professional technical institution shall not charge fees for drug quality testing, and the samples required for random inspection and testing shall be purchased by itself.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对药品质量检验结论有异议的，可以自收到药品质量检验结论之日起7日内向原军队药品专业技术机构或者其上一级药品专业技术机构申请复验。受理复验的军队药品专业技术机构应当在规定的时间内作出复验结论。

**Article 30** Where a party disagrees with the conclusion of drug quality testing, it may, within 7 days from the date of receipt of the drug quality testing conclusion, apply for a re-testing to the original military drug professional technical institution or the drug professional technical institution at the next higher level. The military drug professional technical institution that accepts the re-testing shall issue a re-testing conclusion within the prescribed time.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对在军队医疗机构使用且有证据证明可能危害人体健康的药品及其有关材料，军队医疗机构应当立即停用，采取封存管控等措施，并逐级上报至中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门处理；其中，从地方单位购进的药品，由中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门协调国务院药品监督管理部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门按照国家有关规定处理。

**Article 31** For drugs and related materials used in military medical institutions that have evidence of potentially harming human health, the military medical institution shall immediately discontinue use, take measures such as sealing and controlling, and report level by level to the business department responsible for health work of the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission for handling; among them, for drugs purchased from local units, the business department responsible for health work of the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission shall coordinate with the drug regulatory department of the State Council or the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to handle in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　军队医疗机构、药材供应保障机构、特需药品研制单位和生产单位，应当经常考察本单位所生产、供应、使用的药品质量、疗效和不良反应。发现疑似不良反应的，应当按照国家和军队有关规定报告并采取风险控制措施。

**Article 32** Military medical institutions, drug supply support units, special-needs drug research and development units, and production units shall regularly investigate the quality, efficacy, and adverse reactions of the drugs produced, supplied, and used by their own units. If suspected adverse reactions are discovered, they shall report in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state and the military and take risk control measures.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门和军兵种负责卫生工作的业务部门应当对军队医疗机构配制的制剂和军队特需药品组织评估，发现疗效不确定、不良反应大或者因其他原因危害人体健康的，应当注销其批准证明文件。  
已被注销批准证明文件的军队医疗机构配制的制剂和军队特需药品，不得生产、供应、使用；已经生产的，由批准部门组织回收并监督销毁。

**Article 33** The business department responsible for health work of the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission and the business departments responsible for health work of the branches of the armed forces shall organize evaluation of preparations formulated by military medical institutions and military special-needs drugs. If it is discovered that the efficacy is uncertain, adverse reactions are significant, or human health is harmed for other reasons, they shall revoke their approval certificates.  
Preparations formulated by military medical institutions and military special-needs drugs whose approval certificates have been revoked shall not be produced, supplied, or used; those already produced shall be recalled and destroyed under supervision by the approving department.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　任何单位和个人不得以军队单位或者军队人员的名义、形象或者利用军队装备、设施等从事药品商业宣传。  
禁止对军队医疗机构配制的制剂、军队特需药品进行商业宣传。

**Article 34** No unit or individual shall engage in commercial drug publicity in the name or image of a military unit or military personnel, or by using military equipment, facilities, etc.  
Commercial publicity for preparations formulated by military medical institutions and military special-needs drugs is prohibited.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　军队药品监督员应当参加国家或者地方药品检查员培训并取得相应资格，按照规定的职责从事军队药品监督工作。

**Article 35** Military drug supervisors shall participate in national or local drug inspector training and obtain corresponding qualifications, and engage in military drug supervision work in accordance with the prescribed duties.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　地方单位或者人员认为军队单位和人员违反本办法规定的，可以向县级以上地方人民政府承担药品监督管理职责的部门检举、控告，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门通报中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门，中央军事委员会后勤保障部负责卫生工作的业务部门依法组织查处和通报。  
军队单位或者人员认为地方单位和人员违反本办法规定的，可以向县级以上地方人民政府承担药品监督管理职责的部门检举、控告，由县级以上地方人民政府承担药品监督管理职责的部门依法组织查处和通报。

**Article 36** If a local unit or individual believes that a military unit or individual has violated the provisions of these Measures, they may report or accuse to the department responsible for drug regulatory duties of the local people's government at or above the county level. The drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall notify the business department responsible for health work of the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission, and the business department responsible for health work of the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission shall organize investigation and handling and notification according to law.  
If a military unit or individual believes that a local unit or individual has violated the provisions of these Measures, they may report or accuse to the department responsible for drug regulatory duties of the local people's government at or above the county level. The department responsible for drug regulatory duties of the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize investigation and handling and notification according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家和军队对麻醉药品、精神药品、医疗用毒性药品、放射性药品、药品类易制毒化学品、疫苗和血液制品等有特殊管理规定的，依照其规定执行。

**Article 37** Where the state and the military have special management provisions for narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, medicinal toxic drugs, radioactive drugs, pharmaceutical precursor chemicals, vaccines, and blood products, the provisions shall prevail.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　未取得军队特需药品生产与配制许可证、军队药材供应许可证或者军队医疗机构制剂许可证生产、供应药品的，责令停止有关活动，没收违法生产、供应的药品和违法所得。

**Article 38** Where a unit produces or supplies drugs without obtaining a military special-needs drug production and preparation license, a military drug supply license, or a military medical institution preparation license, it shall be ordered to cease the relevant activities, and the illegally produced and supplied drugs and illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　军队医疗机构使用假药、劣药的，没收其使用的假药、劣药和违法所得，责令改正；情节严重的，有关责任人员有医疗卫生人员执业证书的，还应当吊销其执业证书。

**Article 39** Where a military medical institution uses counterfeit or substandard drugs, the counterfeit or substandard drugs used and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall be ordered to make corrections. If the circumstances are serious, if the responsible person holds a practice certificate for medical and health personnel, the practice certificate shall also be revoked.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　军队单位和人员知道或者应当知道属于假药、劣药，而利用军队设施、运输工具为其提供储存、运输等便利条件的，扣押储存、运输的药品，按照有关规定处理，并没收全部储存、运输收入。

**Article 40** Where a military unit or individual knows or should know that drugs are counterfeit or substandard, and uses military facilities or means of transport to provide storage, transport, or other convenience for them, the stored and transported drugs shall be seized and handled in accordance with the relevant provisions, and all storage and transport income shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　伪造、变造、出租、出借、买卖军队特需药品生产与配制许可证、军队药材供应许可证、军队医疗机构制剂许可证、军队医疗机构制剂批准证明文件、军队特需药品批准证明文件的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，吊销相关许可证或者批准证明文件。

**Article 41** Forging, altering, leasing, lending, buying, or selling the license for the production and preparation of military special-needs drugs, the license for the supply of military medicinal materials, the license for the preparation of pharmaceutical preparations by military medical institutions, the certificate of approval for the preparation of pharmaceutical preparations by military medical institutions, or the certificate of approval for military special-needs drugs shall result in the confiscation of illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant license or certificate of approval shall be revoked.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　提供虚假的证明、数据、资料、样品或者采取其他不正当手段骗取军队特需药品生产与配制许可、军队药材供应许可、军队医疗机构制剂许可或者军队特需药品注册、军队医疗机构制剂注册等许可的，撤销相关许可，10年内不受理其相应申请。

**Article 42** Providing false certificates, data, materials, samples, or taking other improper means to fraudulently obtain the license for the production and preparation of military special-needs drugs, the license for the supply of military medicinal materials, the license for the preparation of pharmaceutical preparations by military medical institutions, or the registration of military special-needs drugs, the registration of pharmaceutical preparations by military medical institutions, or other licenses, the relevant license shall be revoked, and its corresponding application shall not be accepted within 10 years.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　军队医疗机构、药材供应保障机构、疾病预防控制机构从药品上市许可持有人或者具有药品生产、经营资格的企业以外的地方单位和个人购进药品的，责令改正，没收违法购进的药品和违法所得。

**Article 43** If military medical institutions, institutions for the supply and guarantee of medicinal materials, or disease prevention and control institutions purchase drugs from local entities or individuals other than the drug marketing authorization holders or enterprises with drug production or operation qualifications, they shall be ordered to make corrections, and the illegally purchased drugs and illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　军队医疗机构、药材供应保障机构未按照规定建立并执行药品进货检查验收制度、药品保管制度的，责令改正，给予警告。

**Article 44** If military medical institutions or institutions for the supply and guarantee of medicinal materials fail to establish and implement the system for checking, accepting, and storing drugs in accordance with the provisions, they shall be ordered to make corrections and given a warning.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　军队特需药品研制单位和生产单位、药材供应保障机构，未经批准向地方单位或者个人提供军队特需药品的，责令停止有关活动，限期追回药品，没收违法所得。

**Article 45** If the military special-needs drug research and development units, production units, or institutions for the supply and guarantee of medicinal materials provide military special-needs drugs to local units or individuals without approval, they shall be ordered to stop the relevant activities, recover the drugs within a specified time limit, and confiscate the illegal gains.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　军队单位和人员，收受药品上市许可持有人、药品生产企业、药品经营企业或者其代理人给予的财物或者其他不正当利益的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，有关责任人员有医疗卫生人员执业证书的，还应当吊销其执业证书。

**Article 46** If military units and personnel accept property or other illegal benefits given by drug marketing authorization holders, drug manufacturers, drug distributors, or their agents, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the responsible persons who hold practice certificates for medical and health personnel shall also have their practice certificates revoked.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　军队医疗机构配制假劣制剂的，责令停止配制，没收违法配制的制剂和违法所得，吊销相关制剂批准证明文件；情节严重的，吊销军队医疗机构制剂许可证，10年内不受理其相应申请。

**Article 47** If a military medical institution prepares counterfeit or substandard pharmaceutical preparations, it shall be ordered to stop preparation, the illegally prepared pharmaceutical preparations and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and the relevant pharmaceutical preparation approval certificates shall be revoked; if the circumstances are serious, the license for the preparation of pharmaceutical preparations by military medical institutions shall be revoked, and its corresponding application shall not be accepted within 10 years.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　军队医疗机构将其配制的制剂在市场上销售或者未经批准调剂使用制剂的，责令改正，没收违法销售、使用的制剂和违法所得；情节严重的，吊销军队医疗机构制剂许可证。

**Article 48** If a military medical institution sells its prepared pharmaceutical preparations on the market or uses the preparations through adjustment without approval, it shall be ordered to make corrections, the illegally sold or used pharmaceutical preparations and illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the license for the preparation of pharmaceutical preparations by military medical institutions shall be revoked.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　军队医疗机构、药材供应保障机构、特需药品研制单位和生产单位，未按照规定开展药品不良反应监测或者报告疑似药品不良反应的，责令改正，给予警告。

**Article 49** If military medical institutions, institutions for the supply and guarantee of medicinal materials, special-needs drug research and development units, and production units fail to carry out adverse drug reaction monitoring or report suspected adverse drug reactions in accordance with the provisions, they shall be ordered to make corrections and given a warning.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　军队单位和人员违反本办法规定从事药品商业宣传的，责令改正，没收违法所得。

**Article 50** If military units and personnel engage in drug commercial propaganda in violation of the provisions of these Measures, they shall be ordered to make corrections and the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本办法规定，有下列情形之一的，应当撤销相关许可：  
（一）不符合条件而批准进行军队特需药品、医疗机构制剂临床试验；  
（二）不符合条件而颁发军队特需药品批准证明文件、医疗机构制剂批准证明文件；  
（三）不符合条件而颁发军队特需药品生产与配制许可证、军队药材供应许可证、军队医疗机构制剂许可证。

**Article 51** Where these Measures are violated and any of the following circumstances exists, the relevant license shall be revoked:  
(1) approving clinical trials of special military drugs or medicinal preparations of medical institutions without meeting the conditions;  
(2) issuing approval certificates for special military drugs or approval certificates for medicinal preparations of medical institutions without meeting the conditions; or  
(3) issuing the production and preparation license for special military drugs, the military medicinal materials supply license, or the license for medical institutions' medicinal preparations without meeting the conditions.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　军队药品专业技术机构违反本办法规定，有下列行为之一的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得；情节严重的，撤销其检验资格：  
（一）违规收取检验费用；  
（二）出具虚假检验报告；  
（三）其他弄虚作假行为。

**Article 52** Where a military drug professional technical institution violates these Measures and commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, and its illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, its inspection qualification shall be revoked:  
(1) charging inspection fees in violation of regulations;  
(2) issuing false inspection reports; or  
(3) other fraudulent acts.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　对军队单位和人员违反本办法规定的处罚，由军队有关部门按照职责权限作出决定；撤销许可、吊销许可证或者批准证明文件的，由原批准、发证的部门作出决定。  
地方单位和人员违反本办法规定的，依照《中华人民共和国药品管理法》等法律、行政法规的有关规定给予处罚。

**Article 53** The punishments for military units and personnel who violate these Measures shall be decided by the relevant military departments according to their functions and powers; the revocation of licenses or approval certificates shall be decided by the original approving and issuing departments.  
For local units and personnel who violate these Measures, punishments shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Pharmaceutical Administration Law of the People's Republic of China and other laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　军队单位和人员、国家机关及其工作人员在军队药品管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，或者有其他违反本办法规定行为的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员，依法依规给予处分。

**Article 54** Where military units and personnel, State organs and their functionaries abuse their powers, neglect their duties, engage in malpractices for personal gain, or commit other acts violating these Measures in the military drug administration work, sanctions shall be imposed on the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons according to law and regulations.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本办法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 55** Where these Measures are violated and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　本办法所称军队医疗机构，包括军队医院、部队卫生机构、派驻门诊医疗机构和干休所门诊部等单位。

**Article 56** For the purposes of these Measures, military medical institutions include military hospitals, troops' health institutions, dispatched outpatient medical institutions, and outpatient departments of rest homes for retired cadres, among others.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　本办法所称军队药品专业技术机构，是指军队设置的药品仪器监督检验总站、药品仪器监督检验站以及承担药品监测和评价任务的军队医疗机构、科研机构等单位。

**Article 57** For the purposes of these Measures, military drug professional technical institutions refer to the general station for drug and instrument supervision and inspection, drug and instrument supervision and inspection stations set up by the military, as well as military medical institutions, scientific research institutions, and other units that undertake drug monitoring and evaluation tasks.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　军队战备药品、医疗机构制剂、特需药品的具体管理办法，由中央军事委员会后勤保障部制定。

**Article 58** The specific management measures for military combat readiness drugs, medical institution preparations, and special-needs drugs shall be formulated by the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　中国人民武装警察部队的药品管理工作，适用本办法。

**Article 59** These Measures shall apply to the drug administration work of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　本办法自2025年6月1日起施行。

**Article 60** These Measures shall come into force as of June 1, 2025.
