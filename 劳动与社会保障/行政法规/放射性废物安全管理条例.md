**法律部门**：社会法

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对放射性废物的安全管理，保护环境，保障人体健康，根据《中华人民共和国放射性污染防治法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law on Prevention and Control of Radioactive Pollution of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of strengthening the safe management of radioactive waste, protecting the environment, and safeguarding human health.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称放射性废物，是指含有放射性核素或者被放射性核素污染，其放射性核素浓度或者比活度大于国家确定的清洁解控水平，预期不再使用的废弃物。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "radioactive waste" means waste that contains radionuclides or is contaminated by radionuclides, of which the concentration or specific activity of radionuclides is greater than the clearance level determined by the State, and which is not expected to be used any longer.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　放射性废物的处理、贮存和处置及其监督管理等活动，适用本条例。  
本条例所称处理，是指为了能够安全和经济地运输、贮存、处置放射性废物，通过净化、浓缩、固化、压缩和包装等手段，改变放射性废物的属性、形态和体积的活动。  
本条例所称贮存，是指将废旧放射源和其他放射性固体废物临时放置于专门建造的设施内进行保管的活动。  
本条例所称处置，是指将废旧放射源和其他放射性固体废物最终放置于专门建造的设施内并不再回取的活动。

**Article 3** These Regulations shall apply to the treatment, storage and disposal of radioactive waste, as well as the supervision and administration thereof.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "treatment" means the activity of changing the properties, form and volume of radioactive waste by means of purification, concentration, solidification, compaction and packaging, for the purpose of enabling the radioactive waste to be transported, stored and disposed of safely and economically.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "storage" means the activity of temporarily placing disused sealed sources and other radioactive solid waste in specially constructed facilities for safekeeping.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "disposal" means the activity of finally placing disused sealed sources and other radioactive solid waste in specially constructed facilities without any intention of retrieval.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　放射性废物的安全管理，应当坚持减量化、无害化和妥善处置、永久安全的原则。

**Article 4** The safe management of radioactive waste shall adhere to the principles of volume reduction, harmlessness, proper disposal and permanent safety.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院环境保护主管部门统一负责全国放射性废物的安全监督管理工作。  
国务院核工业行业主管部门和其他有关部门，依照本条例的规定和各自的职责负责放射性废物的有关管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门和其他有关部门依照本条例的规定和各自的职责负责本行政区域放射性废物的有关管理工作。

**Article 5** The competent department of environmental protection under the State Council shall be responsible for the safety supervision and administration of radioactive waste nationwide.  
The competent department of the nuclear industry under the State Council and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant administration of radioactive waste.  
The competent departments of environmental protection and other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant administration of radioactive waste within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家对放射性废物实行分类管理。  
根据放射性废物的特性及其对人体健康和环境的潜在危害程度，将放射性废物分为高水平放射性废物、中水平放射性废物和低水平放射性废物。

**Article 6** The State shall implement classified management of radioactive waste.  
According to the characteristics of radioactive waste and the degree of potential harm to human health and the environment, radioactive waste shall be classified into high-level radioactive waste, intermediate-level radioactive waste and low-level radioactive waste.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　放射性废物的处理、贮存和处置活动，应当遵守国家有关放射性污染防治标准和国务院环境保护主管部门的规定。

**Article 7** The treatment, storage and disposal of radioactive waste shall comply with the State's relevant standards for prevention and control of radioactive pollution and the provisions of the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院环境保护主管部门会同国务院核工业行业主管部门和其他有关部门建立全国放射性废物管理信息系统，实现信息共享。  
国家鼓励、支持放射性废物安全管理的科学研究和技术开发利用，推广先进的放射性废物安全管理技术。

**Article 8** The competent department of environmental protection under the State Council shall, together with the competent department of the nuclear industry under the State Council and other relevant departments, establish a national radioactive waste management information system to achieve information sharing.  
The State shall encourage and support scientific research and technological development and utilization for the safe management of radioactive waste, and promote advanced technologies for the safe management of radioactive waste.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何单位和个人对违反本条例规定的行为，有权向县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门或者其他有关部门举报。接到举报的部门应当及时调查处理，并为举报人保密；经调查情况属实的，对举报人给予奖励。

**Article 9** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report any act violating the provisions of these Regulations to the competent department of environmental protection or other relevant department of a local people's government at or above the county level. The department that receives the report shall promptly investigate and handle the matter, and maintain confidentiality for the informant; if the information is verified upon investigation, the informant shall be rewarded.

### 第二章　放射性废物的处理和贮存

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　核设施营运单位应当将其产生的不能回收利用并不能返回原生产单位或者出口方的废旧放射源(以下简称废旧放射源)，送交取得相应许可证的放射性固体废物贮存单位集中贮存，或者直接送交取得相应许可证的放射性固体废物处置单位处置。  
核设施营运单位应当对其产生的除废旧放射源以外的放射性固体废物和不能经净化排放的放射性废液进行处理，使其转变为稳定的、标准化的固体废物后自行贮存，并及时送交取得相应许可证的放射性固体废物处置单位处置。

**Article 10** A nuclear facility operating unit shall deliver the disused sealed sources generated by it that cannot be recycled or returned to the original production unit or the exporter (hereinafter referred to as disused sealed sources) to a radioactive solid waste storage unit that has obtained the corresponding permit for centralized storage, or directly deliver them to a radioactive solid waste disposal unit that has obtained the corresponding permit for disposal.  
A nuclear facility operating unit shall treat the radioactive solid waste other than disused sealed sources and the radioactive liquid waste that cannot be discharged after purification, generated by it, to convert them into stable, standardized solid waste, then store them by itself, and timely deliver them to a radioactive solid waste disposal unit that has obtained the corresponding permit for disposal.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　核技术利用单位应当对其产生的不能经净化排放的放射性废液进行处理，转变为放射性固体废物。  
核技术利用单位应当及时将其产生的废旧放射源和其他放射性固体废物，送交取得相应许可证的放射性固体废物贮存单位集中贮存，或者直接送交取得相应许可证的放射性固体废物处置单位处置。

**Article 11** A nuclear technology utilization unit shall treat the radioactive liquid waste generated by it that cannot be discharged after purification, to convert it into radioactive solid waste.  
A nuclear technology utilization unit shall timely deliver the disused sealed sources and other radioactive solid waste generated by it to a radioactive solid waste storage unit that has obtained the corresponding permit for centralized storage, or directly deliver them to a radioactive solid waste disposal unit that has obtained the corresponding permit for disposal.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　专门从事放射性固体废物贮存活动的单位，应当符合下列条件，并依照本条例的规定申请领取放射性固体废物贮存许可证：  
(一)有法人资格；  
(二)有能保证贮存设施安全运行的组织机构和3名以上放射性废物管理、辐射防护、环境监测方面的专业技术人员，其中至少有1名注册核安全工程师；  
(三)有符合国家有关放射性污染防治标准和国务院环境保护主管部门规定的放射性固体废物接收、贮存设施和场所，以及放射性检测、辐射防护与环境监测设备；  
(四)有健全的管理制度以及符合核安全监督管理要求的质量保证体系，包括质量保证大纲、贮存设施运行监测计划、辐射环境监测计划和应急方案等。  
核设施营运单位利用与核设施配套建设的贮存设施，贮存本单位产生的放射性固体废物的，不需要申请领取贮存许可证；贮存其他单位产生的放射性固体废物的，应当依照本条例的规定申请领取贮存许可证。

**Article 12** A unit that is specialized in engaging in radioactive solid waste storage activities shall meet the following conditions and shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, apply for and obtain a radioactive solid waste storage permit:  
(1) having the status of a legal person;  
(2) having an organizational structure capable of ensuring the safe operation of the storage facility, and at least three professional and technical personnel in the fields of radioactive waste management, radiation protection, and environmental monitoring, among whom at least one shall be a registered nuclear safety engineer;  
(3) having radioactive solid waste receiving and storage facilities and sites, as well as radioactive detection, radiation protection and environmental monitoring equipment, that conform to the State's relevant standards for prevention and control of radioactive pollution and the provisions of the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council;  
(4) having a sound management system and a quality assurance system that meets the requirements of nuclear safety supervision and administration, including a quality assurance program, a storage facility operation monitoring plan, a radiation environmental monitoring plan, and an emergency plan, among others.  
A nuclear facility operating unit that uses a storage facility constructed in support of the nuclear facility to store the radioactive solid waste generated by itself shall not be required to apply for a storage permit; if it stores radioactive solid waste generated by other units, it shall apply for a storage permit in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　申请领取放射性固体废物贮存许可证的单位，应当向国务院环境保护主管部门提出书面申请，并提交其符合[本条例第十二条](#art-12)规定条件的证明材料。  
国务院环境保护主管部门应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内完成审查，对符合条件的颁发许可证，予以公告；对不符合条件的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。  
国务院环境保护主管部门在审查过程中，应当组织专家进行技术评审，并征求国务院其他有关部门的意见。技术评审所需时间应当书面告知申请单位。

**Article 13** A unit applying for a radioactive solid waste storage permit shall submit a written application to the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council, and shall submit evidentiary materials demonstrating that it meets the conditions specified in Article 12 of these Regulations.  
The competent department of environmental protection under the State Council shall complete the review within 20 working days from the date of accepting the application; for those that meet the conditions, it shall issue a permit and make an announcement; for those that do not meet the conditions, it shall notify the applying unit in writing and state the reasons.  
During the review process, the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council shall organize expert technical review and seek opinions from other relevant departments under the State Council. The time required for the technical review shall be notified to the applying unit in writing.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　放射性固体废物贮存许可证应当载明下列内容：  
(一)单位的名称、地址和法定代表人；  
(二)准予从事的活动种类、范围和规模；  
(三)有效期限；  
(四)发证机关、发证日期和证书编号。

**Article 14** A radioactive solid waste storage permit shall specify the following:  
(1) the name, address and legal representative of the unit;  
(2) the type, scope and scale of activities permitted to be engaged in;  
(3) the validity period;  
(4) the issuing authority, date of issuance and certificate number.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　放射性固体废物贮存单位变更单位名称、地址、法定代表人的，应当自变更登记之日起20日内，向国务院环境保护主管部门申请办理许可证变更手续。  
放射性固体废物贮存单位需要变更许可证规定的活动种类、范围和规模的，应当按照原申请程序向国务院环境保护主管部门重新申请领取许可证。

**Article 15** Where a radioactive solid waste storage unit changes its name, address or legal representative, it shall, within 20 days from the date of change registration, apply to the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council for the procedures of modifying the permit.  
Where a radioactive solid waste storage unit needs to change the type, scope or scale of activities specified in the permit, it shall re-apply for a permit from the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council in accordance with the original application procedures.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　放射性固体废物贮存许可证的有效期为10年。  
许可证有效期届满，放射性固体废物贮存单位需要继续从事贮存活动的，应当于许可证有效期届满90日前，向国务院环境保护主管部门提出延续申请。  
国务院环境保护主管部门应当在许可证有效期届满前完成审查，对符合条件的准予延续；对不符合条件的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。

**Article 16** The validity period of a radioactive solid waste storage permit shall be 10 years.  
Where a radioactive solid waste storage unit needs to continue engaging in storage activities upon expiry of the validity period of the permit, it shall submit a renewal application to the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council at least 90 days before the expiry of the validity period of the permit.  
The competent department of environmental protection under the State Council shall complete the review before the expiry of the validity period of the permit; for those that meet the conditions, it shall approve the renewal; for those that do not meet the conditions, it shall notify the applying unit in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　放射性固体废物贮存单位应当按照国家有关放射性污染防治标准和国务院环境保护主管部门的规定，对其接收的废旧放射源和其他放射性固体废物进行分类存放和清理，及时予以清洁解控或者送交取得相应许可证的放射性固体废物处置单位处置。  
放射性固体废物贮存单位应当建立放射性固体废物贮存情况记录档案，如实完整地记录贮存的放射性固体废物的来源、数量、特征、贮存位置、清洁解控、送交处置等与贮存活动有关的事项。  
放射性固体废物贮存单位应当根据贮存设施的自然环境和放射性固体废物特性采取必要的防护措施，保证在规定的贮存期限内贮存设施、容器的完好和放射性固体废物的安全，并确保放射性固体废物能够安全回取。

**Article 17** A radioactive solid waste storage unit shall, in accordance with the State's relevant standards for prevention and control of radioactive pollution and the provisions of the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council, sort and clean the disused sealed sources and other radioactive solid waste it receives, and timely carry out clearance or deliver them to a radioactive solid waste disposal unit that has obtained the corresponding permit for disposal.  
A radioactive solid waste storage unit shall establish a record archive of radioactive solid waste storage, truthfully and completely recording matters related to the storage activities such as the source, quantity, characteristics, storage location, clearance, and delivery for disposal of the stored radioactive solid waste.  
A radioactive solid waste storage unit shall take necessary protective measures based on the natural environment of the storage facility and the characteristics of the radioactive solid waste, to ensure the integrity of the storage facility and containers and the safety of the radioactive solid waste within the specified storage period, and to ensure that the radioactive solid waste can be safely retrieved.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　放射性固体废物贮存单位应当根据贮存设施运行监测计划和辐射环境监测计划，对贮存设施进行安全性检查，并对贮存设施周围的地下水、地表水、土壤和空气进行放射性监测。  
放射性固体废物贮存单位应当如实记录监测数据，发现安全隐患或者周围环境中放射性核素超过国家规定的标准的，应当立即查找原因，采取相应的防范措施，并向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护主管部门报告。构成辐射事故的，应当立即启动本单位的应急方案，并依照《中华人民共和国放射性污染防治法》、《放射性同位素与射线装置安全和防护条例》的规定进行报告，开展有关事故应急工作。

**Article 18** A radioactive solid waste storage unit shall, in accordance with the storage facility operation monitoring plan and the radiation environmental monitoring plan, conduct safety inspections of the storage facility and carry out radioactive monitoring of the groundwater, surface water, soil and air around the storage facility.  
A radioactive solid waste storage unit shall truthfully record the monitoring data. If it discovers safety hazards or radionuclide levels in the surrounding environment exceeding the standards prescribed by the State, it shall immediately identify the causes, take corresponding preventive measures, and report to the competent department of environmental protection of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located. If a radiation accident is constituted, it shall immediately activate its own emergency plan, and report and carry out relevant accident emergency work in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Prevention and Control of Radioactive Pollution of the People's Republic of China and the Regulations on the Safety and Protection of Radioactive Isotopes and Radiation Devices.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　将废旧放射源和其他放射性固体废物送交放射性固体废物贮存、处置单位贮存、处置时，送交方应当一并提供放射性固体废物的种类、数量、活度等资料和废旧放射源的原始档案，并按照规定承担贮存、处置的费用。

**Article 19** When delivering disused sealed sources and other radioactive solid waste to a radioactive solid waste storage or disposal unit for storage or disposal, the delivering party shall also provide information on the type, quantity and activity of the radioactive solid waste, as well as the original archives of the disused sealed sources, and shall bear the costs of storage and disposal in accordance with the regulations.

### 第三章　放射性废物的处置

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院核工业行业主管部门会同国务院环境保护主管部门根据地质、环境、社会经济条件和放射性固体废物处置的需要，在征求国务院有关部门意见并进行环境影响评价的基础上编制放射性固体废物处置场所选址规划，报国务院批准后实施。  
有关地方人民政府应当根据放射性固体废物处置场所选址规划，提供放射性固体废物处置场所的建设用地，并采取有效措施支持放射性固体废物的处置。

**Article 20** The competent department of the nuclear industry under the State Council shall, together with the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council, formulate a siting plan for radioactive solid waste disposal sites based on geological, environmental and socio-economic conditions and the needs for radioactive solid waste disposal, after soliciting opinions from relevant departments under the State Council and conducting an environmental impact assessment, and submit it to the State Council for approval before implementation.  
The relevant local people's governments shall, in accordance with the siting plan for radioactive solid waste disposal sites, provide land for the construction of radioactive solid waste disposal sites, and take effective measures to support the disposal of radioactive solid waste.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　建造放射性固体废物处置设施，应当按照放射性固体废物处置场所选址技术导则和标准的要求，与居住区、水源保护区、交通干道、工厂和企业等场所保持严格的安全防护距离，并对场址的地质构造、水文地质等自然条件以及社会经济条件进行充分研究论证。

**Article 21** The construction of a radioactive solid waste disposal facility shall, in accordance with the requirements of the technical guidelines and standards for siting of radioactive solid waste disposal sites, maintain a strict safety protection distance from residential areas, water source protection zones, major traffic arteries, factories and enterprises, and other sites, and shall conduct adequate research and demonstration of the natural conditions such as geological structure and hydrogeology, as well as socio-economic conditions of the site.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　建造放射性固体废物处置设施，应当符合放射性固体废物处置场所选址规划，并依法办理选址批准手续和建造许可证。不符合选址规划或者选址技术导则、标准的，不得批准选址或者建造。  
高水平放射性固体废物和α放射性固体废物深地质处置设施的工程和安全技术研究、地下实验、选址和建造，由国务院核工业行业主管部门组织实施。

**Article 22** The construction of a radioactive solid waste disposal facility shall conform to the siting plan for radioactive solid waste disposal sites, and the approval procedures for site selection and the construction permit shall be handled according to law. If the siting does not conform to the siting plan or the technical guidelines or standards for site selection, the site selection or construction shall not be approved.  
The engineering and safety technology research, underground experiments, site selection and construction of deep geological disposal facilities for high-level radioactive solid waste and alpha radioactive solid waste shall be organized and implemented by the competent department of the nuclear industry under the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　专门从事放射性固体废物处置活动的单位，应当符合下列条件，并依照本条例的规定申请领取放射性固体废物处置许可证：  
(一)有国有或者国有控股的企业法人资格。  
(二)有能保证处置设施安全运行的组织机构和专业技术人员。低、中水平放射性固体废物处置单位应当具有10名以上放射性废物管理、辐射防护、环境监测方面的专业技术人员，其中至少有3名注册核安全工程师；高水平放射性固体废物和α放射性固体废物处置单位应当具有20名以上放射性废物管理、辐射防护、环境监测方面的专业技术人员，其中至少有5名注册核安全工程师。  
(三)有符合国家有关放射性污染防治标准和国务院环境保护主管部门规定的放射性固体废物接收、处置设施和场所，以及放射性检测、辐射防护与环境监测设备。低、中水平放射性固体废物处置设施关闭后应满足300年以上的安全隔离要求；高水平放射性固体废物和α放射性固体废物深地质处置设施关闭后应满足1万年以上的安全隔离要求。  
(四)有相应数额的注册资金。低、中水平放射性固体废物处置单位的注册资金应不少于3000万元；高水平放射性固体废物和α放射性固体废物处置单位的注册资金应不少于1亿元。  
(五)有能保证其处置活动持续进行直至安全监护期满的财务担保。  
(六)有健全的管理制度以及符合核安全监督管理要求的质量保证体系，包括质量保证大纲、处置设施运行监测计划、辐射环境监测计划和应急方案等。

**Article 23** A unit that is specialized in engaging in radioactive solid waste disposal activities shall meet the following conditions and shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, apply for and obtain a radioactive solid waste disposal permit:  
(1) having the status of a state-owned or state-controlled enterprise legal person;  
(2) having an organizational structure and professional and technical personnel capable of ensuring the safe operation of the disposal facility. A low- and intermediate-level radioactive solid waste disposal unit shall have at least 10 professional and technical personnel in the fields of radioactive waste management, radiation protection, and environmental monitoring, among whom at least three shall be registered nuclear safety engineers; a high-level radioactive solid waste and alpha radioactive solid waste disposal unit shall have at least 20 professional and technical personnel in the fields of radioactive waste management, radiation protection, and environmental monitoring, among whom at least five shall be registered nuclear safety engineers;  
(3) having radioactive solid waste receiving and disposal facilities and sites, as well as radioactive detection, radiation protection and environmental monitoring equipment, that conform to the State's relevant standards for prevention and control of radioactive pollution and the provisions of the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council. The closed low- and intermediate-level radioactive solid waste disposal facility shall meet the safety isolation requirement of more than 300 years; the closed high-level radioactive solid waste and alpha radioactive solid waste deep geological disposal facility shall meet the safety isolation requirement of more than 10,000 years;  
(4) having a corresponding amount of registered capital. The registered capital of a low- and intermediate-level radioactive solid waste disposal unit shall be not less than RMB 30 million; the registered capital of a high-level radioactive solid waste and alpha radioactive solid waste disposal unit shall be not less than RMB 100 million;  
(5) having financial guarantees capable of ensuring the continuous conduct of its disposal activities until the expiry of the safety supervision period;  
(6) having a sound management system and a quality assurance system that meets the requirements of nuclear safety supervision and administration, including a quality assurance program, a disposal facility operation monitoring plan, a radiation environmental monitoring plan, and an emergency plan, among others.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　放射性固体废物处置许可证的申请、变更、延续的审批权限和程序，以及许可证的内容、有效期限，依照[本条例第十三条](#art-13)至第十六条的规定执行。

**Article 24** The examination and approval authority and procedures for the application, modification and renewal of a radioactive solid waste disposal permit, as well as the content and validity period of the permit, shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of Articles 13 to 16 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　放射性固体废物处置单位应当按照国家有关放射性污染防治标准和国务院环境保护主管部门的规定，对其接收的放射性固体废物进行处置。  
放射性固体废物处置单位应当建立放射性固体废物处置情况记录档案，如实记录处置的放射性固体废物的来源、数量、特征、存放位置等与处置活动有关的事项。放射性固体废物处置情况记录档案应当永久保存。

**Article 25** A radioactive solid waste disposal unit shall, in accordance with the State's relevant standards for prevention and control of radioactive pollution and the provisions of the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council, dispose of the radioactive solid waste it receives.  
A radioactive solid waste disposal unit shall establish a record archive of radioactive solid waste disposal, truthfully recording matters related to the disposal activities such as the source, quantity, characteristics and storage location of the disposed radioactive solid waste. The record archive of radioactive solid waste disposal shall be permanently kept.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　放射性固体废物处置单位应当根据处置设施运行监测计划和辐射环境监测计划，对处置设施进行安全性检查，并对处置设施周围的地下水、地表水、土壤和空气进行放射性监测。  
放射性固体废物处置单位应当如实记录监测数据，发现安全隐患或者周围环境中放射性核素超过国家规定的标准的，应当立即查找原因，采取相应的防范措施，并向国务院环境保护主管部门和核工业行业主管部门报告。构成辐射事故的，应当立即启动本单位的应急方案，并依照《中华人民共和国放射性污染防治法》、《放射性同位素与射线装置安全和防护条例》的规定进行报告，开展有关事故应急工作。

**Article 26** A radioactive solid waste disposal unit shall, in accordance with the disposal facility operation monitoring plan and the radiation environmental monitoring plan, conduct safety inspections of the disposal facility and carry out radioactive monitoring of the groundwater, surface water, soil and air around the disposal facility.  
A radioactive solid waste disposal unit shall truthfully record the monitoring data. If it discovers safety hazards or radionuclide levels in the surrounding environment exceeding the standards prescribed by the State, it shall immediately identify the causes, take corresponding preventive measures, and report to the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council and the competent department of the nuclear industry. If a radiation accident is constituted, it shall immediately activate its own emergency plan, and report and carry out relevant accident emergency work in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Prevention and Control of Radioactive Pollution of the People's Republic of China and the Regulations on the Safety and Protection of Radioactive Isotopes and Radiation Devices.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　放射性固体废物处置设施设计服役期届满，或者处置的放射性固体废物已达到该设施的设计容量，或者所在地区的地质构造或者水文地质等条件发生重大变化导致处置设施不适宜继续处置放射性固体废物的，应当依法办理关闭手续，并在划定的区域设置永久性标记。  
关闭放射性固体废物处置设施的，处置单位应当编制处置设施安全监护计划，报国务院环境保护主管部门批准。  
放射性固体废物处置设施依法关闭后，处置单位应当按照经批准的安全监护计划，对关闭后的处置设施进行安全监护。放射性固体废物处置单位因破产、吊销许可证等原因终止的，处置设施关闭和安全监护所需费用由提供财务担保的单位承担。

**Article 27** If the design service life of a radioactive solid waste disposal facility expires, or the amount of radioactive solid waste disposed of has reached the design capacity of the facility, or significant changes in the geological structure or hydrogeological conditions of the area where the facility is located render the disposal facility unsuitable for continued disposal of radioactive solid waste, the closure procedures shall be handled according to law, and permanent markers shall be set up in the designated area.  
Where a radioactive solid waste disposal facility is to be closed, the disposal unit shall formulate a safety supervision plan for the disposal facility and submit it to the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council for approval.  
After a radioactive solid waste disposal facility is closed according to law, the disposal unit shall conduct safety supervision of the closed disposal facility in accordance with the approved safety supervision plan. If a radioactive solid waste disposal unit terminates due to bankruptcy, revocation of the permit or other reasons, the expenses required for the closure and safety supervision of the disposal facility shall be borne by the unit that provided the financial guarantee.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门和其他有关部门，依照《中华人民共和国放射性污染防治法》和本条例的规定，对放射性废物处理、贮存和处置等活动的安全性进行监督检查。

**Article 28** The competent departments of environmental protection and other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Prevention and Control of Radioactive Pollution of the People's Republic of China and these Regulations, conduct supervision and inspection over the safety of activities such as treatment, storage and disposal of radioactive waste.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门和其他有关部门进行监督检查时，有权采取下列措施：  
(一)向被检查单位的法定代表人和其他有关人员调查、了解情况；  
(二)进入被检查单位进行现场监测、检查或者核查；  
(三)查阅、复制相关文件、记录以及其他有关资料；  
(四)要求被检查单位提交有关情况说明或者后续处理报告。  
被检查单位应当予以配合，如实反映情况，提供必要的资料，不得拒绝和阻碍。  
县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门和其他有关部门的监督检查人员依法进行监督检查时，应当出示证件，并为被检查单位保守技术秘密和业务秘密。

**Article 29** When conducting supervision and inspection, the competent departments of environmental protection and other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall have the right to take the following measures:  
(1) investigating and learning about the situation from the legal representative and other relevant persons of the unit being inspected;  
(2) entering the unit being inspected to conduct on-site monitoring, inspection or verification;  
(3) consulting and copying relevant documents, records and other relevant materials;  
(4) requesting the unit being inspected to submit a description of the relevant situation or a follow-up treatment report.  
The unit being inspected shall cooperate, truthfully reflect the situation, provide necessary information, and shall not refuse or obstruct.  
When conducting supervision and inspection according to law, the inspectors of the competent departments of environmental protection and other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall produce their credentials, and keep confidential the technical secrets and business secrets of the unit being inspected.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　核设施营运单位、核技术利用单位和放射性固体废物贮存、处置单位，应当按照放射性废物危害的大小，建立健全相应级别的安全保卫制度，采取相应的技术防范措施和人员防范措施，并适时开展放射性废物污染事故应急演练。

**Article 30** Nuclear facility operating units, nuclear technology utilization units and radioactive solid waste storage and disposal units shall, according to the degree of hazard of the radioactive waste, establish and improve a corresponding level of security system, take corresponding technical prevention measures and personnel prevention measures, and timely conduct emergency drills for radioactive waste pollution accidents.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　核设施营运单位、核技术利用单位和放射性固体废物贮存、处置单位，应当对其直接从事放射性废物处理、贮存和处置活动的工作人员进行核与辐射安全知识以及专业操作技术的培训，并进行考核；考核合格的，方可从事该项工作。

**Article 31** Nuclear facility operating units, nuclear technology utilization units and radioactive solid waste storage and disposal units shall provide training on nuclear and radiation safety knowledge as well as professional operation techniques to the workers directly engaged in the treatment, storage and disposal of radioactive waste, and shall conduct examinations; only those who pass the examinations may engage in such work.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　核设施营运单位、核技术利用单位和放射性固体废物贮存单位应当按照国务院环境保护主管部门的规定定期如实报告放射性废物产生、排放、处理、贮存、清洁解控和送交处置等情况。  
放射性固体废物处置单位应当于每年3月31日前，向国务院环境保护主管部门和核工业行业主管部门如实报告上一年度放射性固体废物接收、处置和设施运行等情况。

**Article 32** Nuclear facility operating units, nuclear technology utilization units and radioactive solid waste storage units shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council, regularly and truthfully report on the generation, discharge, treatment, storage, clearance and delivery for disposal of radioactive waste.  
A radioactive solid waste disposal unit shall, before March 31 each year, truthfully report to the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council and the competent department of the nuclear industry on the previous year's receipt, disposal and facility operation of radioactive solid waste.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　禁止将废旧放射源和其他放射性固体废物送交无相应许可证的单位贮存、处置或者擅自处置。  
禁止无许可证或者不按照许可证规定的活动种类、范围、规模和期限从事放射性固体废物贮存、处置活动。

**Article 33** It shall be prohibited to deliver disused sealed sources and other radioactive solid waste to a unit without the corresponding permit for storage or disposal, or to dispose of them without authorization.  
It shall be prohibited to engage in radioactive solid waste storage or disposal activities without a permit, or not in accordance with the type, scope, scale and period of activities specified in the permit.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　禁止将放射性废物和被放射性污染的物品输入中华人民共和国境内或者经中华人民共和国境内转移。具体办法由国务院环境保护主管部门会同国务院商务主管部门、海关总署、国家出入境检验检疫主管部门制定。

**Article 34** It shall be prohibited to import radioactive waste or articles contaminated by radioactivity into the territory of the People's Republic of China or to transfer them through the territory of the People's Republic of China. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council together with the competent department of commerce under the State Council, the General Administration of Customs, and the competent department of entry-exit inspection and quarantine under the State Council.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　负有放射性废物安全监督管理职责的部门及其工作人员违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)违反本条例规定核发放射性固体废物贮存、处置许可证的；  
(二)违反本条例规定批准不符合选址规划或者选址技术导则、标准的处置设施选址或者建造的；  
(三)对发现的违反本条例的行为不依法查处的；  
(四)在办理放射性固体废物贮存、处置许可证以及实施监督检查过程中，索取、收受他人财物或者谋取其他利益的；  
(五)其他徇私舞弊、滥用职权、玩忽职守行为。

**Article 35** Where a department responsible for the safety supervision and administration of radioactive waste and its staff members violate the provisions of these Regulations, and commit any of the following acts, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) issuing a radioactive solid waste storage or disposal permit in violation of the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) approving the siting or construction of a disposal facility that does not conform to the siting plan or the technical guidelines or standards for site selection in violation of the provisions of these Regulations;  
(3) failing to investigate and deal with discovered violations of these Regulations according to law;  
(4) demanding, accepting property from others or seeking other benefits during the process of handling radioactive solid waste storage or disposal permits or conducting supervision and inspection;  
(5) other acts of engaging in fraud for personal gain, abusing power, or neglecting duties.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本条例规定，核设施营运单位、核技术利用单位有下列行为之一的，由审批该单位立项环境影响评价文件的环境保护主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正；逾期不改正的，指定有相应许可证的单位代为贮存或者处置，所需费用由核设施营运单位、核技术利用单位承担，可以处20万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)核设施营运单位未按照规定，将其产生的废旧放射源送交贮存、处置，或者将其产生的其他放射性固体废物送交处置的；  
(二)核技术利用单位未按照规定，将其产生的废旧放射源或者其他放射性固体废物送交贮存、处置的。

**Article 36** Where a nuclear facility operating unit or a nuclear technology utilization unit, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the competent department of environmental protection that approved the environmental impact assessment document for the project establishment of the unit shall order the cessation of the illegal act and prescribe a time limit for correction; if no correction is made within the time limit, a unit with the corresponding permit shall be designated to store or dispose of the waste on behalf of the violator, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the nuclear facility operating unit or the nuclear technology utilization unit, and a fine of not more than RMB 200,000 may be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) a nuclear facility operating unit fails to deliver, in accordance with regulations, the disused sealed sources generated by it for storage or disposal, or fails to deliver the other radioactive solid waste generated by it for disposal;  
(2) a nuclear technology utilization unit fails to deliver, in accordance with regulations, the disused sealed sources or other radioactive solid waste generated by it for storage or disposal.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款；造成环境污染的，责令限期采取治理措施消除污染，逾期不采取治理措施，经催告仍不治理的，可以指定有治理能力的单位代为治理，所需费用由违法者承担；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)核设施营运单位将废旧放射源送交无相应许可证的单位贮存、处置，或者将其他放射性固体废物送交无相应许可证的单位处置，或者擅自处置的；  
(二)核技术利用单位将废旧放射源或者其他放射性固体废物送交无相应许可证的单位贮存、处置，或者擅自处置的；  
(三)放射性固体废物贮存单位将废旧放射源或者其他放射性固体废物送交无相应许可证的单位处置，或者擅自处置的。

**Article 37** Where any of the following acts is committed in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the competent department of environmental protection of a local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act and prescribe a time limit for correction, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 200,000; if environmental pollution is caused, the violator shall be ordered to take remediation measures within a prescribed time limit to eliminate the pollution, and if no remediation measures are taken within the time limit and still not taken after a reminder, a unit with remediation capability may be designated to carry out remediation on behalf of the violator, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) a nuclear facility operating unit delivers disused sealed sources to a unit without the corresponding permit for storage or disposal, or delivers other radioactive solid waste to a unit without the corresponding permit for disposal, or disposes of them without authorization;  
(2) a nuclear technology utilization unit delivers disused sealed sources or other radioactive solid waste to a unit without the corresponding permit for storage or disposal, or disposes of them without authorization;  
(3) a radioactive solid waste storage unit delivers disused sealed sources or other radioactive solid waste to a unit without the corresponding permit for disposal, or disposes of them without authorization.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由省级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门责令停产停业或者吊销许可证；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；违法所得10万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足10万元的，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；造成环境污染的，责令限期采取治理措施消除污染，逾期不采取治理措施，经催告仍不治理的，可以指定有治理能力的单位代为治理，所需费用由违法者承担；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未经许可，擅自从事废旧放射源或者其他放射性固体废物的贮存、处置活动的；  
(二)放射性固体废物贮存、处置单位未按照许可证规定的活动种类、范围、规模、期限从事废旧放射源或者其他放射性固体废物的贮存、处置活动的；  
(三)放射性固体废物贮存、处置单位未按照国家有关放射性污染防治标准和国务院环境保护主管部门的规定贮存、处置废旧放射源或者其他放射性固体废物的。

**Article 38** Where any of the following acts is committed in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the competent department of environmental protection of a people's government at or above the provincial level shall order the suspension of production or business or revoke the permit; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are more than RMB 100,000, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be concurrently imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 100,000, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 shall be concurrently imposed; if environmental pollution is caused, the violator shall be ordered to take remediation measures within a prescribed time limit to eliminate the pollution, and if no remediation measures are taken within the time limit and still not taken after a reminder, a unit with remediation capability may be designated to carry out remediation on behalf of the violator, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) engaging in the storage or disposal of disused sealed sources or other radioactive solid waste without authorization and without a permit;  
(2) a radioactive solid waste storage or disposal unit engages in the storage or disposal of disused sealed sources or other radioactive solid waste not in accordance with the type, scope, scale or period of activities specified in the permit;  
(3) a radioactive solid waste storage or disposal unit stores or disposes of disused sealed sources or other radioactive solid waste not in accordance with the State's relevant standards for prevention and control of radioactive pollution and the provisions of the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　放射性固体废物贮存、处置单位未按照规定建立情况记录档案，或者未按照规定进行如实记录的，由省级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门责令限期改正，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 39** Where a radioactive solid waste storage or disposal unit fails to establish a record archive as required, or fails to make truthful records as required, the competent department of environmental protection of a people's government at or above the provincial level shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit and impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 50,000; if no correction is made within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 shall be imposed.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　核设施营运单位、核技术利用单位或者放射性固体废物贮存、处置单位未按照[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)的规定如实报告有关情况的，由县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门责令限期改正，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 40** Where a nuclear facility operating unit, a nuclear technology utilization unit or a radioactive solid waste storage or disposal unit fails to truthfully report the relevant information in accordance with Article 32 of these Regulations, the competent department of environmental protection of a local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit and impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 50,000; if no correction is made within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 shall be imposed.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本条例规定，拒绝、阻碍环境保护主管部门或者其他有关部门的监督检查，或者在接受监督检查时弄虚作假的，由监督检查部门责令改正，处2万元以下的罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 41** Where any person, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, refuses or obstructs the supervision and inspection conducted by the competent department of environmental protection or other relevant department, or practices fraud during the supervision and inspection, the supervision and inspection department shall order correction and impose a fine of not more than RMB 20,000; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　核设施营运单位、核技术利用单位或者放射性固体废物贮存、处置单位未按照规定对有关工作人员进行技术培训和考核的，由县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门责令限期改正，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 42** Where a nuclear facility operating unit, a nuclear technology utilization unit or a radioactive solid waste storage or disposal unit fails to provide technical training and examination for the relevant workers as required, the competent department of environmental protection of a local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit and impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 50,000; if no correction is made within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 shall be imposed.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反本条例规定，向中华人民共和国境内输入放射性废物或者被放射性污染的物品，或者经中华人民共和国境内转移放射性废物或者被放射性污染的物品的，由海关责令退运该放射性废物或者被放射性污染的物品，并处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** Where any person, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, imports radioactive waste or articles contaminated by radioactivity into the territory of the People's Republic of China, or transfers radioactive waste or articles contaminated by radioactivity through the territory of the People's Republic of China, the Customs shall order the return of the radioactive waste or articles contaminated by radioactivity, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　军用设施、装备所产生的放射性废物的安全管理，依照[《中华人民共和国放射性污染防治法》第六十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国放射性污染防治法.md#art-60)的规定执行。

**Article 44** The safe management of radioactive waste generated by military facilities and equipment shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of Article 60 of the Law on Prevention and Control of Radioactive Pollution of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　放射性废物运输的安全管理、放射性废物造成污染事故的应急处理，以及劳动者在职业活动中接触放射性废物造成的职业病防治，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 45** The safe management of the transport of radioactive waste, the emergency response to pollution accidents caused by radioactive waste, and the prevention and treatment of occupational diseases caused by workers' exposure to radioactive waste in occupational activities shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本条例自2012年3月1日起施行。

**Article 46** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 2012.
