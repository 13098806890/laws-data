# 社会保险费征缴暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-24

> (1999年1月22日中华人民共和国国务院令第259号发布　根据2019年3月24日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强和规范社会保险费征缴工作，保障社会保险金的发放，制定本条例。 第二条　基本养老保险费、基本医疗保险费、失业保险费(以下统称社会保险费)的征收、缴纳，适用本条例。 本条例所称缴费单位、缴费个人，是指依照有关法律、行政法规和国务院的规定，应当缴纳社会保险费的单位和个人。 第三条　基本养老保险费的征缴范围：国有企业、城镇集体企业、外商投资企业、城镇私营企业和其他城镇企业及其职工，实行企业化管理的事业单位及其职工。 基本医疗保险费的征缴范围：国有企业、城镇集体企业、外商投资企业、城镇私营企业和其他城镇企业及其职工，国家机关及其工作人员，事业单位及其职工，民办非企业单位及其职工，社会团体及其专职人员。 失业保险费的征缴范围：国有企业、城镇集体企业、外商投资企业、城镇私营企业和其他城镇企业及其职工，事业单位及其职工。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据当地实际情况，可以规定将城镇个体工商户纳入基本养老保险、基本医疗保险的范围，并可以规定将社会团体及其专职人员、民办非企业单位及其职工以及有雇工的城镇个体工商户及其雇工纳入失业保险的范围。 社会保险费的费基、费率依照有关法律、行政法规和国务院的规定执行。 第四条　缴费单位、缴费个人应当按时足额缴纳社会保险费。 征缴的社会保险费纳入社会保险基金，专款专用，任何单位和个人不得挪用。 第五条　国务院劳动保障行政部门负责全国的社会保险费征缴管理和监督检查工作。县级以上地方各级人民政府劳动保障行政部门负责本行政区域内的社会保险费征缴管理和监督检查工作。 第六条　社会保险费实行三项社会保险费集中、统一征收。社会保险费的征收机构由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定，可以由税务机关征收，也可以由劳动保障行政部门按照国务院规定设立的社会保险经办机构(以下简称社会保险经办机构)征收。 第二章　征缴管理

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强和规范社会保险费征缴工作，保障社会保险金的发放，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen and regulate the collection and payment of social insurance premiums and to ensure the disbursement of social insurance benefits.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　基本养老保险费、基本医疗保险费、失业保险费(以下统称社会保险费)的征收、缴纳，适用本条例。  
本条例所称缴费单位、缴费个人，是指依照有关法律、行政法规和国务院的规定，应当缴纳社会保险费的单位和个人。

**Article 2** The collection and payment of basic old-age insurance premiums, basic medical insurance premiums, and unemployment insurance premiums (hereinafter collectively referred to as social insurance premiums) shall be governed by these Regulations.  
The term "contributing unit" and "contributing individual" as used in these Regulations refer to the units and individuals that shall pay social insurance premiums in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　基本养老保险费的征缴范围：国有企业、城镇集体企业、外商投资企业、城镇私营企业和其他城镇企业及其职工，实行企业化管理的事业单位及其职工。  
基本医疗保险费的征缴范围：国有企业、城镇集体企业、外商投资企业、城镇私营企业和其他城镇企业及其职工，国家机关及其工作人员，事业单位及其职工，民办非企业单位及其职工，社会团体及其专职人员。  
失业保险费的征缴范围：国有企业、城镇集体企业、外商投资企业、城镇私营企业和其他城镇企业及其职工，事业单位及其职工。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据当地实际情况，可以规定将城镇个体工商户纳入基本养老保险、基本医疗保险的范围，并可以规定将社会团体及其专职人员、民办非企业单位及其职工以及有雇工的城镇个体工商户及其雇工纳入失业保险的范围。  
社会保险费的费基、费率依照有关法律、行政法规和国务院的规定执行。

**Article 3** The scope of collection of basic old-age insurance premiums: state-owned enterprises, urban collectively-owned enterprises, enterprises with foreign investment, urban private enterprises, and other urban enterprises and their employees, as well as enterprises managed as enterprises and their employees.  
The scope of collection of basic medical insurance premiums: state-owned enterprises, urban collectively-owned enterprises, enterprises with foreign investment, urban private enterprises, and other urban enterprises and their employees, state organs and their staff members, public institutions and their employees, private non-enterprise units and their employees, and social organizations and their full-time personnel.  
The scope of collection of unemployment insurance premiums: state-owned enterprises, urban collectively-owned enterprises, enterprises with foreign investment, urban private enterprises, and other urban enterprises and their employees, as well as public institutions and their employees.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on local actual conditions, provide for the inclusion of urban self-employed individuals within the scope of basic old-age insurance and basic medical insurance, and may also provide for the inclusion of social organizations and their full-time personnel, private non-enterprise units and their employees, and urban self-employed individuals with employees and their employees within the scope of unemployment insurance.  
The base and rate of social insurance premiums shall be executed in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　缴费单位、缴费个人应当按时足额缴纳社会保险费。  
征缴的社会保险费纳入社会保险基金，专款专用，任何单位和个人不得挪用。

**Article 4** Contributing units and contributing individuals shall pay social insurance premiums in full and on time.  
The social insurance premiums collected shall be incorporated into the social insurance fund, used for designated purposes only, and no unit or individual may misappropriate them.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院劳动保障行政部门负责全国的社会保险费征缴管理和监督检查工作。县级以上地方各级人民政府劳动保障行政部门负责本行政区域内的社会保险费征缴管理和监督检查工作。

**Article 5** The labor and social security administrative department under the State Council shall be responsible for the management, supervision, and inspection of the collection and payment of social insurance premiums nationwide. The labor and social security administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the management, supervision, and inspection of the collection and payment of social insurance premiums within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　社会保险费实行三项社会保险费集中、统一征收。社会保险费的征收机构由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定，可以由税务机关征收，也可以由劳动保障行政部门按照国务院规定设立的社会保险经办机构(以下简称社会保险经办机构)征收。

**Article 6** Social insurance premiums shall be collected in a centralized and unified manner for the three types of social insurance premiums. The collecting agency for social insurance premiums shall be designated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and may be the tax authority or a social insurance agency established by the labor and social security administrative department in accordance with the provisions of the State Council (hereinafter referred to as the social insurance agency).

### 第二章　征缴管理

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　缴费单位必须向当地社会保险经办机构办理社会保险登记，参加社会保险。  
登记事项包括：单位名称、住所、经营地点、单位类型、法定代表人或者负责人、开户银行账号以及国务院劳动保障行政部门规定的其他事项。

**Article 7** A contributing unit must register for social insurance with the local social insurance agency and participate in social insurance.  
The registration items shall include: the unit's name, domicile, place of business, type of unit, legal representative or responsible person, bank account number, and other items prescribed by the labor and social security administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　企业在办理登记注册时，同步办理社会保险登记。  
前款规定以外的缴费单位应当自成立之日起30日内，向当地社会保险经办机构申请办理社会保险登记。

**Article 8** When an enterprise undergoes registration, it shall simultaneously undergo social insurance registration.  
A contributing unit other than those specified in the preceding paragraph shall, within 30 days from the date of its establishment, apply to the local social insurance agency for social insurance registration.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　缴费单位的社会保险登记事项发生变更或者缴费单位依法终止的，应当自变更或者终止之日起30日内，到社会保险经办机构办理变更或者注销社会保险登记手续。

**Article 9** Where the social insurance registration items of a contributing unit change or the contributing unit terminates according to law, it shall, within 30 days from the date of the change or termination, go through the formalities of modification or cancellation of the social insurance registration with the social insurance agency.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　缴费单位必须按月向社会保险经办机构申报应缴纳的社会保险费数额，经社会保险经办机构核定后，在规定的期限内缴纳社会保险费。  
缴费单位不按规定申报应缴纳的社会保险费数额的，由社会保险经办机构暂按该单位上月缴费数额的110%确定应缴数额；没有上月缴费数额的，由社会保险经办机构暂按该单位的经营状况、职工人数等有关情况确定应缴数额。缴费单位补办申报手续并按核定数额缴纳社会保险费后，由社会保险经办机构按照规定结算。

**Article 10** A contributing unit must declare the amount of social insurance premiums payable to the social insurance agency on a monthly basis and, after verification by the social insurance agency, pay the social insurance premiums within the prescribed time limit.  
Where a contributing unit fails to declare the amount of social insurance premiums payable in accordance with regulations, the social insurance agency shall tentatively determine the payable amount at 110% of the unit's previous month's payment; where there is no previous month's payment, the social insurance agency shall tentatively determine the payable amount based on the unit's operating conditions, number of employees, and other relevant circumstances. After the contributing unit makes up the declaration procedures and pays the social insurance premiums in the verified amount, the social insurance agency shall settle accounts according to regulations.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定由税务机关征收社会保险费的，社会保险经办机构应当及时向税务机关提供缴费单位社会保险登记、变更登记、注销登记以及缴费申报的情况。

**Article 11** Where the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government provides that social insurance premiums shall be collected by the tax authority, the social insurance agency shall promptly provide the tax authority with information on the social insurance registration, modification registration, cancellation of registration, and payment declaration of the contributing units.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　缴费单位和缴费个人应当以货币形式全额缴纳社会保险费。  
缴费个人应当缴纳的社会保险费，由所在单位从其本人工资中代扣代缴。  
社会保险费不得减免。

**Article 12** Contributing units and contributing individuals shall pay social insurance premiums in full in monetary form.  
The social insurance premiums payable by a contributing individual shall be withheld and paid on his behalf by his employer from his wages.  
Social insurance premiums shall not be reduced or exempted.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　缴费单位未按规定缴纳和代扣代缴社会保险费的，由劳动保障行政部门或者税务机关责令限期缴纳；逾期仍不缴纳的，除补缴欠缴数额外，从欠缴之日起，按日加收2‰的滞纳金。滞纳金并入社会保险基金。

**Article 13** Where a contributing unit fails to pay or withhold and pay social insurance premiums in accordance with regulations, the labor and social security administrative department or the tax authority shall order it to pay within a specified time limit; if it still fails to pay upon expiration of the time limit, in addition to making up the amount in arrears, a late fee of 2‰ per day shall be imposed from the date the payment became overdue. The late fee shall be incorporated into the social insurance fund.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　征收的社会保险费存入财政部门在国有商业银行开设的社会保障基金财政专户。  
社会保险基金按照不同险种的统筹范围，分别建立基本养老保险基金、基本医疗保险基金、失业保险基金。各项社会保险基金分别单独核算。  
社会保险基金不计征税、费。

**Article 14** The social insurance premiums collected shall be deposited into the fiscal special account for social security funds opened by the finance department in a state-owned commercial bank.  
The social insurance fund shall establish separate basic old-age insurance fund, basic medical insurance fund, and unemployment insurance fund according to the pooling scope of each type of insurance. Each social insurance fund shall be separately accounted for.  
No taxes or fees shall be levied on the social insurance fund.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定由税务机关征收社会保险费的，税务机关应当及时向社会保险经办机构提供缴费单位和缴费个人的缴费情况；社会保险经办机构应当将有关情况汇总，报劳动保障行政部门。

**Article 15** Where the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government provides that social insurance premiums shall be collected by the tax authority, the tax authority shall promptly provide the social insurance agency with the payment information of contributing units and contributing individuals; the social insurance agency shall summarize the relevant information and report it to the labor and social security administrative department.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　社会保险经办机构应当建立缴费记录，其中基本养老保险、基本医疗保险并应当按照规定记录个人账户。社会保险经办机构负责保存缴费记录，并保证其完整、安全。社会保险经办机构应当至少每年向缴费个人发送一次基本养老保险、基本医疗保险个人账户通知单。  
缴费单位、缴费个人有权按照规定查询缴费记录。

**Article 16** A social insurance agency shall establish payment records, and for basic old-age insurance and basic medical insurance, it shall record individual accounts in accordance with regulations. The social insurance agency shall be responsible for preserving the payment records and ensuring their completeness and security. The social insurance agency shall send a notice of individual accounts for basic old-age insurance and basic medical insurance to contributing individuals at least once a year.  
Contributing units and contributing individuals shall have the right to inquire about payment records in accordance with regulations.

### 第三章　监督检查

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　缴费单位应当每年向本单位职工公布本单位全年社会保险费缴纳情况，接受职工监督。  
社会保险经办机构应当定期向社会公告社会保险费征收情况，接受社会监督。

**Article 17** A contributing unit shall annually publicize to its employees the unit's annual social insurance premium payment situation and accept employee supervision.  
A social insurance agency shall regularly announce to the public the collection situation of social insurance premiums and accept public supervision.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府关于社会保险费征缴机构的规定，劳动保障行政部门或者税务机关依法对单位缴费情况进行检查时，被检查的单位应当提供与缴纳社会保险费有关的用人情况、工资表、财务报表等资料，如实反映情况，不得拒绝检查，不得谎报、瞒报。劳动保障行政部门或者税务机关可以记录、录音、录像、照相和复制有关资料；但是，应当为缴费单位保密。  
劳动保障行政部门、税务机关的工作人员在行使前款所列职权时，应当出示执行公务证件。

**Article 18** When the labor and social security administrative department or the tax authority, in accordance with the provisions of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government on the social insurance premium collection agency, conducts an inspection of a unit's payment situation according to law, the unit being inspected shall provide materials such as employment information, payroll, and financial statements related to the payment of social insurance premiums, truthfully reflect the situation, and shall not refuse the inspection, nor make false reports or conceal information. The labor and social security administrative department or the tax authority may make records, audio recordings, video recordings, photographs, and copies of relevant materials; however, it shall maintain confidentiality for the contributing unit.  
When exercising the powers listed in the preceding paragraph, the staff members of the labor and social security administrative department or the tax authority shall produce their official documents.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　劳动保障行政部门或者税务机关调查社会保险费征缴违法案件时，有关部门、单位应当给予支持、协助。

**Article 19** When the labor and social security administrative department or the tax authority investigates illegal cases concerning the collection and payment of social insurance premiums, relevant departments and units shall provide support and assistance.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　社会保险经办机构受劳动保障行政部门的委托，可以进行与社会保险费征缴有关的检查、调查工作。

**Article 20** A social insurance agency, entrusted by the labor and social security administrative department, may carry out inspection and investigation work related to the collection and payment of social insurance premiums.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　任何组织和个人对有关社会保险费征缴的违法行为，有权举报。劳动保障行政部门或者税务机关对举报应当及时调查，按照规定处理，并为举报人保密。

**Article 21** Any organization or individual shall have the right to report any illegal act concerning the collection and payment of social insurance premiums. The labor and social security administrative department or the tax authority shall promptly investigate the report, handle it in accordance with regulations, and maintain confidentiality for the informant.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　社会保险基金实行收支两条线管理，由财政部门依法进行监督。  
审计部门依法对社会保险基金的收支情况进行监督。

**Article 22** The social insurance fund shall be managed under the system of separation of revenue and expenditure, and the finance department shall supervise it according to law.  
The audit department shall supervise the revenue and expenditure of the social insurance fund according to law.

### 第四章　罚则

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　缴费单位未按照规定办理社会保险登记、变更登记或者注销登记，或者未按照规定申报应缴纳的社会保险费数额的，由劳动保障行政部门责令限期改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员可以处1000元以上5000元以下的罚款；情节特别严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员可以处5000元以上10000元以下的罚款。

**Article 23** Where a contributing unit fails to undergo social insurance registration, modification registration, or cancellation of registration in accordance with regulations, or fails to declare the amount of social insurance premiums payable in accordance with regulations, the labor and social security administrative department shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan may be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons; if the circumstances are especially serious, a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan may be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　缴费单位违反有关财务、会计、统计的法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，伪造、变造、故意毁灭有关账册、材料，或者不设账册，致使社会保险费缴费基数无法确定的，除依照有关法律、行政法规的规定给予行政处罚、纪律处分、刑事处罚外，依照[本条例第十条](#art-10)的规定征缴；迟延缴纳的，由劳动保障行政部门或者税务机关依照[本条例第十三条](#art-13)的规定决定加收滞纳金，并对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处5000元以上20000元以下的罚款。

**Article 24** Where a contributing unit violates the laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions on finance, accounting, and statistics, forges, alters, intentionally destroys relevant account books or materials, or fails to set up account books, thereby making it impossible to determine the base for social insurance premium payment, in addition to being subject to administrative penalties, disciplinary sanctions, or criminal punishments in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations, the premiums shall be collected in accordance with Article 10 of these Regulations; if the payment is delayed, the labor and social security administrative department or the tax authority shall decide to impose a late fee in accordance with Article 13 of these Regulations, and shall also impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　缴费单位和缴费个人对劳动保障行政部门或者税务机关的处罚决定不服的，可以依法申请复议；对复议决定不服的，可以依法提起诉讼。

**Article 25** Where a contributing unit or contributing individual disagrees with a penalty decision made by the labor and social security administrative department or the tax authority, it/he may apply for reconsideration according to law; where it/he disagrees with the reconsideration decision, it/he may institute legal proceedings according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　缴费单位逾期拒不缴纳社会保险费、滞纳金的，由劳动保障行政部门或者税务机关申请人民法院依法强制征缴。

**Article 26** Where a contributing unit, after the expiration of the time limit, refuses to pay social insurance premiums or late fees, the labor and social security administrative department or the tax authority shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory collection according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　劳动保障行政部门、社会保险经办机构或者税务机关的工作人员滥用职权、徇私舞弊、玩忽职守，致使社会保险费流失的，由劳动保障行政部门或者税务机关追回流失的社会保险费；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 27** Where a staff member of the labor and social security administrative department, a social insurance agency, or the tax authority abuses his power, engages in graft, or neglects his duty, causing loss of social insurance premiums, the labor and social security administrative department or the tax authority shall recover the lost social insurance premiums; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if no crime is constituted, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　任何单位、个人挪用社会保险基金的，追回被挪用的社会保险基金；有违法所得的，没收违法所得，并入社会保险基金；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分。

**Article 28** Where any unit or individual misappropriates the social insurance fund, the misappropriated social insurance fund shall be recovered; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated and incorporated into the social insurance fund; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if no crime is constituted, an administrative sanction shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据本地实际情况，可以决定本条例适用于本行政区域内工伤保险费和生育保险费的征收、缴纳。

**Article 29** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on local actual conditions, decide that these Regulations shall apply to the collection and payment of work-related injury insurance premiums and maternity insurance premiums within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　税务机关、社会保险经办机构征收社会保险费，不得从社会保险基金中提取任何费用，所需经费列入预算，由财政拨付。

**Article 30** The tax authority and the social insurance agency, when collecting social insurance premiums, shall not deduct any expenses from the social insurance fund; the necessary expenses shall be included in the budget and appropriated by the finance department.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 31** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
