# 历史文化名城名镇名村保护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2017-10-07  
**生效日期**：2008-07-01

> (2008年4月22日中华人民共和国国务院令第524号公布　根据2017年10月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强历史文化名城、名镇、名村的保护与管理，继承中华民族优秀历史文化遗产，制定本条例。 第二条　历史文化名城、名镇、名村的申报、批准、规划、保护，适用本条例。 第三条　历史文化名城、名镇、名村的保护应当遵循科学规划、严格保护的原则，保持和延续其传统格局和历史风貌，维护历史文化遗产的真实性和完整性，继承和弘扬中华民族优秀传统文化，正确处理经济社会发展和历史文化遗产保护的关系。 第四条　国家对历史文化名城、名镇、名村的保护给予必要的资金支持。 历史文化名城、名镇、名村所在地的县级以上地方人民政府，根据本地实际情况安排保护资金，列入本级财政预算。 国家鼓励企业、事业单位、社会团体和个人参与历史文化名城、名镇、名村的保护。 第五条　国务院建设主管部门会同国务院文物主管部门负责全国历史文化名城、名镇、名村的保护和监督管理工作。 地方各级人民政府负责本行政区域历史文化名城、名镇、名村的保护和监督管理工作。 第六条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门对在历史文化名城、名镇、名村保护工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。 第二章　申报与批准 第七条　具备下列条件的城市、镇、村庄，可以申报历史文化名城、名镇、名村： (一)保存文物特别丰富； (二)历史建筑集中成片；

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强历史文化名城、名镇、名村的保护与管理，继承中华民族优秀历史文化遗产，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the protection and administration of famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages, and inheriting the outstanding historical and cultural heritage of the Chinese nation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　历史文化名城、名镇、名村的申报、批准、规划、保护，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the declaration, approval, planning and protection of famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　历史文化名城、名镇、名村的保护应当遵循科学规划、严格保护的原则，保持和延续其传统格局和历史风貌，维护历史文化遗产的真实性和完整性，继承和弘扬中华民族优秀传统文化，正确处理经济社会发展和历史文化遗产保护的关系。

**Article 3** The protection of famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages shall follow the principles of scientific planning and strict protection, maintain and carry forward their traditional layout and historical features, safeguard the authenticity and integrity of historical and cultural heritage, inherit and promote the fine traditional culture of the Chinese nation, and properly handle the relationship between economic and social development and the protection of historical and cultural heritage.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对历史文化名城、名镇、名村的保护给予必要的资金支持。  
历史文化名城、名镇、名村所在地的县级以上地方人民政府，根据本地实际情况安排保护资金，列入本级财政预算。  
国家鼓励企业、事业单位、社会团体和个人参与历史文化名城、名镇、名村的保护。

**Article 4** The State shall provide necessary financial support for the protection of famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages.  
The local people's government at or above the county level of the locality of a famous historical and cultural city, town or village shall, in light of the actual local conditions, arrange for protection funds and include them in the fiscal budget at the corresponding level.  
The State encourages enterprises, institutions, social organizations and individuals to participate in the protection of famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院建设主管部门会同国务院文物主管部门负责全国历史文化名城、名镇、名村的保护和监督管理工作。  
地方各级人民政府负责本行政区域历史文化名城、名镇、名村的保护和监督管理工作。

**Article 5** The competent department of construction under the State Council shall, together with the competent department of cultural relics under the State Council, be responsible for the protection, supervision and administration of famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages throughout the country.  
The local people's governments at various levels shall be responsible for the protection, supervision and administration of famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages within their respective administrative areas.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门对在历史文化名城、名镇、名村保护工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 6** The people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, give commendations and awards to units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the protection of famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages.

### 第二章　申报与批准

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　具备下列条件的城市、镇、村庄，可以申报历史文化名城、名镇、名村：  
(一)保存文物特别丰富；  
(二)历史建筑集中成片；  
(三)保留着传统格局和历史风貌；  
(四)历史上曾经作为政治、经济、文化、交通中心或者军事要地，或者发生过重要历史事件，或者其传统产业、历史上建设的重大工程对本地区的发展产生过重要影响，或者能够集中反映本地区建筑的文化特色、民族特色。  
申报历史文化名城的，在所申报的历史文化名城保护范围内还应当有2个以上的历史文化街区。

**Article 7** A city, town or village meeting the following conditions may be declared as a famous historical and cultural city, town or village:  
(1) it is particularly rich in preserved cultural relics;  
(2) its historical buildings are concentrated in an integrated area;  
(3) it retains its traditional layout and historical features;  
(4) it once served as a political, economic, cultural, transportation center or a strategic military location in history, or witnessed important historical events, or its traditional industries or major projects constructed in history have exerted significant influence on the development of the region, or it can reflect in a concentrated manner the cultural and ethnic characteristics of the local architecture.  
Where a city is declared as a famous historical and cultural city, there shall be two or more historical and cultural blocks within the protection area of the said declared famous historical and cultural city.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申报历史文化名城、名镇、名村，应当提交所申报的历史文化名城、名镇、名村的下列材料：  
(一)历史沿革、地方特色和历史文化价值的说明；  
(二)传统格局和历史风貌的现状；  
(三)保护范围；  
(四)不可移动文物、历史建筑、历史文化街区的清单；  
(五)保护工作情况、保护目标和保护要求。

**Article 8** For the declaration of a famous historical and cultural city, town or village, the following materials on the famous historical and cultural city, town or village being declared shall be submitted:  
(1) a description of the historical evolution, local characteristics and historical and cultural value;  
(2) the current status of the traditional layout and historical features;  
(3) the protection area;  
(4) a list of immovable cultural relics, historical buildings and historical and cultural blocks;  
(5) the status of protection work, protection objectives and protection requirements.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申报历史文化名城，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府提出申请，经国务院建设主管部门会同国务院文物主管部门组织有关部门、专家进行论证，提出审查意见，报国务院批准公布。  
申报历史文化名镇、名村，由所在地县级人民政府提出申请，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的保护主管部门会同同级文物主管部门组织有关部门、专家进行论证，提出审查意见，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准公布。

**Article 9** For the declaration of a famous historical and cultural city, the application shall be submitted by the people's government of a province, an autonomous region or a municipality directly under the Central Government. The competent department of construction under the State Council shall, together with the competent department of cultural relics under the State Council, organize relevant departments and experts to conduct review and produce review opinions, and report to the State Council for approval and public announcement.  
For the declaration of a famous historical and cultural town or village, the application shall be submitted by the local people's government at the county level. The competent department of protection designated by the people's government of a province, an autonomous region or a municipality directly under the Central Government shall, together with the competent department of cultural relics at the same level, organize relevant departments and experts to conduct review and produce review opinions, and report to the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval and public announcement.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对符合[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定的条件而没有申报历史文化名城的城市，国务院建设主管部门会同国务院文物主管部门可以向该城市所在地的省、自治区人民政府提出申报建议；仍不申报的，可以直接向国务院提出确定该城市为历史文化名城的建议。  
对符合[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定的条件而没有申报历史文化名镇、名村的镇、村庄，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的保护主管部门会同同级文物主管部门可以向该镇、村庄所在地的县级人民政府提出申报建议；仍不申报的，可以直接向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府提出确定该镇、村庄为历史文化名镇、名村的建议。

**Article 10** Where a city meets the conditions prescribed in Article 7 of these Regulations but has not been declared as a famous historical and cultural city, the competent department of construction under the State Council may, together with the competent department of cultural relics under the State Council, put forward a declaration suggestion to the people's government of the province or autonomous region of the locality of the said city; if the said city still fails to make a declaration, they may directly put forward a suggestion to the State Council for determining the said city as a famous historical and cultural city.  
Where a town or village meets the conditions prescribed in Article 7 of these Regulations but has not been declared as a famous historical and cultural town or village, the competent department of protection designated by the people's government of a province, an autonomous region or a municipality directly under the Central Government may, together with the competent department of cultural relics at the same level, put forward a declaration suggestion to the local people's government at the county level of the locality of the said town or village; if the said town or village still fails to make a declaration, they may directly put forward a suggestion to the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for determining the said town or village as a famous historical and cultural town or village.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院建设主管部门会同国务院文物主管部门可以在已批准公布的历史文化名镇、名村中，严格按照国家有关评价标准，选择具有重大历史、艺术、科学价值的历史文化名镇、名村，经专家论证，确定为中国历史文化名镇、名村。

**Article 11** The competent department of construction under the State Council may, together with the competent department of cultural relics under the State Council, strictly in accordance with the relevant national evaluation standards, select from among the already approved and publicly announced famous historical and cultural towns and villages those with significant historical, artistic and scientific value, and, after expert review, determine them as Chinese famous historical and cultural towns or villages.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　已批准公布的历史文化名城、名镇、名村，因保护不力使其历史文化价值受到严重影响的，批准机关应当将其列入濒危名单，予以公布，并责成所在地城市、县人民政府限期采取补救措施，防止情况继续恶化，并完善保护制度，加强保护工作。

**Article 12** Where the historical and cultural value of an already approved and publicly announced famous historical and cultural city, town or village has been seriously affected due to inadequate protection, the approving authority shall include it in the list of endangered items, publicly announce it, and instruct the local city or county people's government to take remedial measures within a time limit to prevent the situation from deteriorating, improve the protection system and strengthen the protection work.

### 第三章　保护规划

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　历史文化名城批准公布后，历史文化名城人民政府应当组织编制历史文化名城保护规划。  
历史文化名镇、名村批准公布后，所在地县级人民政府应当组织编制历史文化名镇、名村保护规划。  
保护规划应当自历史文化名城、名镇、名村批准公布之日起1年内编制完成。

**Article 13** After a famous historical and cultural city is approved and publicly announced, the people's government of the said famous historical and cultural city shall organize the formulation of a protection plan for the famous historical and cultural city.  
After a famous historical and cultural town or village is approved and publicly announced, the local people's government at the county level shall organize the formulation of a protection plan for the famous historical and cultural town or village.  
The protection plan shall be completed within one year as of the date of approval and public announcement of the famous historical and cultural city, town or village.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　保护规划应当包括下列内容：  
(一)保护原则、保护内容和保护范围；  
(二)保护措施、开发强度和建设控制要求；  
(三)传统格局和历史风貌保护要求；  
(四)历史文化街区、名镇、名村的核心保护范围和建设控制地带；  
(五)保护规划分期实施方案。

**Article 14** A protection plan shall include the following contents:  
(1) protection principles, protection contents and protection area;  
(2) protection measures, development intensity and construction control requirements;  
(3) requirements for the protection of traditional layout and historical features;  
(4) the core protection areas and construction control belts of historical and cultural blocks, famous towns and villages;  
(5) phased implementation plans for the protection plan.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　历史文化名城、名镇保护规划的规划期限应当与城市、镇总体规划的规划期限相一致；历史文化名村保护规划的规划期限应当与村庄规划的规划期限相一致。

**Article 15** The planning period of the protection plan for a famous historical and cultural city or town shall be consistent with the planning period of the overall plan for the city or town; the planning period of the protection plan for a famous historical and cultural village shall be consistent with the planning period of the village plan.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　保护规划报送审批前，保护规划的组织编制机关应当广泛征求有关部门、专家和公众的意见；必要时，可以举行听证。  
保护规划报送审批文件中应当附具意见采纳情况及理由；经听证的，还应当附具听证笔录。

**Article 16** Before a protection plan is submitted for examination and approval, the organ organizing the formulation of the protection plan shall extensively solicit opinions from relevant departments, experts and the public; where necessary, a hearing may be held.  
The documents submitted for examination and approval of the protection plan shall be accompanied by the status of adoption of opinions and the reasons therefor; where a hearing has been held, the hearing record shall also be attached.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　保护规划由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审批。  
保护规划的组织编制机关应当将经依法批准的历史文化名城保护规划和中国历史文化名镇、名村保护规划，报国务院建设主管部门和国务院文物主管部门备案。

**Article 17** A protection plan shall be examined and approved by the people's government of a province, an autonomous region or a municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The organ organizing the formulation of the protection plan shall report the protection plan for a famous historical and cultural city and the protection plan for a Chinese famous historical and cultural town or village that have been approved in accordance with law to the competent department of construction under the State Council and the competent department of cultural relics under the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　保护规划的组织编制机关应当及时公布经依法批准的保护规划。

**Article 18** The organ organizing the formulation of the protection plan shall promptly make public the protection plan that has been approved in accordance with law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　经依法批准的保护规划，不得擅自修改；确需修改的，保护规划的组织编制机关应当向原审批机关提出专题报告，经同意后，方可编制修改方案。修改后的保护规划，应当按照原审批程序报送审批。

**Article 19** A protection plan that has been approved in accordance with law shall not be modified without authorization; where modification is truly necessary, the organ that organized the formulation of the protection plan shall submit a special report to the original examining and approving authority, and may only formulate a modification plan after obtaining consent. The modified protection plan shall be submitted for examination and approval in accordance with the original examination and approval procedures.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院建设主管部门会同国务院文物主管部门应当加强对保护规划实施情况的监督检查。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对本行政区域保护规划实施情况的监督检查，并对历史文化名城、名镇、名村保护状况进行评估；对发现的问题，应当及时纠正、处理。

**Article 20** The competent department of construction under the State Council shall, together with the competent department of cultural relics under the State Council, strengthen supervision and inspection over the implementation of protection plans.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and inspection over the implementation of protection plans within their respective administrative areas, and evaluate the protection status of famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages; problems discovered shall be promptly corrected and dealt with.

### 第四章　保护措施

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　历史文化名城、名镇、名村应当整体保护，保持传统格局、历史风貌和空间尺度，不得改变与其相互依存的自然景观和环境。

**Article 21** Famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages shall be protected as a whole, maintaining their traditional layout, historical features and spatial scale, and the interdependent natural landscape and environment shall not be altered.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　历史文化名城、名镇、名村所在地县级以上地方人民政府应当根据当地经济社会发展水平，按照保护规划，控制历史文化名城、名镇、名村的人口数量，改善历史文化名城、名镇、名村的基础设施、公共服务设施和居住环境。

**Article 22** The local people's governments at or above the county level of the locality of famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages shall, in light of the local economic and social development level and in accordance with the protection plan, control the population size of the famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages, and improve the infrastructure, public service facilities and living environment of the famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　在历史文化名城、名镇、名村保护范围内从事建设活动，应当符合保护规划的要求，不得损害历史文化遗产的真实性和完整性，不得对其传统格局和历史风貌构成破坏性影响。

**Article 23** Construction activities within the protection areas of famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages shall comply with the requirements of the protection plan, shall not impair the authenticity and integrity of historical and cultural heritage, and shall not cause destructive impact on their traditional layout and historical features.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　在历史文化名城、名镇、名村保护范围内禁止进行下列活动：  
(一)开山、采石、开矿等破坏传统格局和历史风貌的活动；  
(二)占用保护规划确定保留的园林绿地、河湖水系、道路等；  
(三)修建生产、储存爆炸性、易燃性、放射性、毒害性、腐蚀性物品的工厂、仓库等；  
(四)在历史建筑上刻划、涂污。

**Article 24** The following activities shall be prohibited within the protection areas of famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages:  
(1) activities such as mountain excavation, quarrying and mining that damage the traditional layout and historical features;  
(2) occupying the gardens and green spaces, river and lake systems, roads, etc. that are designated for preservation in the protection plan;  
(3) constructing factories, warehouses, etc. for producing or storing explosive, flammable, radioactive, toxic or corrosive substances;  
(4) carving or defacing historical buildings.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　在历史文化名城、名镇、名村保护范围内进行下列活动，应当保护其传统格局、历史风貌和历史建筑；制订保护方案，并依照有关法律、法规的规定办理相关手续：  
(一)改变园林绿地、河湖水系等自然状态的活动；  
(二)在核心保护范围内进行影视摄制、举办大型群众性活动；  
(三)其他影响传统格局、历史风貌或者历史建筑的活动。

**Article 25** Where the following activities are carried out within the protection areas of famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages, the traditional layout, historical features and historical buildings shall be protected; a protection plan shall be formulated, and relevant procedures shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations:  
(1) activities that alter the natural state of gardens and green spaces, river and lake systems, etc.;  
(2) film and television filming or holding large-scale public activities within the core protection areas;  
(3) other activities that affect the traditional layout, historical features or historical buildings.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　历史文化街区、名镇、名村建设控制地带内的新建建筑物、构筑物，应当符合保护规划确定的建设控制要求。

**Article 26** New buildings and structures within the construction control belts of historical and cultural blocks, famous towns and villages shall comply with the construction control requirements specified in the protection plan.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对历史文化街区、名镇、名村核心保护范围内的建筑物、构筑物，应当区分不同情况，采取相应措施，实行分类保护。  
历史文化街区、名镇、名村核心保护范围内的历史建筑，应当保持原有的高度、体量、外观形象及色彩等。

**Article 27** For buildings and structures within the core protection areas of historical and cultural blocks, famous towns and villages, corresponding measures shall be taken based on different circumstances to implement classified protection.  
Historical buildings within the core protection areas of historical and cultural blocks, famous towns and villages shall retain their original height, volume, appearance, color, etc.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　在历史文化街区、名镇、名村核心保护范围内，不得进行新建、扩建活动。但是，新建、扩建必要的基础设施和公共服务设施除外。  
在历史文化街区、名镇、名村核心保护范围内，新建、扩建必要的基础设施和公共服务设施的，城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门核发建设工程规划许可证、乡村建设规划许可证前，应当征求同级文物主管部门的意见。  
在历史文化街区、名镇、名村核心保护范围内，拆除历史建筑以外的建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施的，应当经城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门会同同级文物主管部门批准。

**Article 28** Within the core protection areas of historical and cultural blocks, famous towns and villages, new construction or extension activities shall not be carried out, except for the construction or extension of necessary infrastructure and public service facilities.  
Where new construction or extension of necessary infrastructure and public service facilities is carried out within the core protection areas of historical and cultural blocks, famous towns and villages, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government shall, before issuing the construction project planning permit or the rural construction planning permit, solicit the opinions of the competent department of cultural relics at the same level.  
Where buildings, structures or other facilities other than historical buildings are demolished within the core protection areas of historical and cultural blocks, famous towns and villages, approval shall be obtained from the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government together with the competent department of cultural relics at the same level.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　审批[本条例第二十八条](#art-28)规定的建设活动，审批机关应当组织专家论证，并将审批事项予以公示，征求公众意见，告知利害关系人有要求举行听证的权利。公示时间不得少于20日。  
利害关系人要求听证的，应当在公示期间提出，审批机关应当在公示期满后及时举行听证。

**Article 29** When examining and approving the construction activities prescribed in Article 28 of these Regulations, the examining and approving authority shall organize expert review, and shall publicize the matters to be examined and approved, solicit public opinions, and inform interested parties of their right to request a hearing. The publicity period shall not be less than 20 days.  
Where an interested party requests a hearing, it shall make such request during the publicity period, and the examining and approving authority shall promptly hold a hearing after the publicity period expires.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　城市、县人民政府应当在历史文化街区、名镇、名村核心保护范围的主要出入口设置标志牌。  
任何单位和个人不得擅自设置、移动、涂改或者损毁标志牌。

**Article 30** The city or county people's government shall set up signage at the main entrances and exits of the core protection areas of historical and cultural blocks, famous towns and villages.  
No unit or individual may, without authorization, set up, move, alter or damage such signage.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　历史文化街区、名镇、名村核心保护范围内的消防设施、消防通道，应当按照有关的消防技术标准和规范设置。确因历史文化街区、名镇、名村的保护需要，无法按照标准和规范设置的，由城市、县人民政府公安机关消防机构会同同级城乡规划主管部门制订相应的防火安全保障方案。

**Article 31** Fire-fighting facilities and fire lanes within the core protection areas of historical and cultural blocks, famous towns and villages shall be installed in accordance with the relevant fire-fighting technical standards and specifications. Where, due to the protection needs of the historical and cultural block, famous town or village, it is truly impossible to install them in accordance with the standards and specifications, the fire control institution of the public security organ of the city or county people's government shall, together with the competent department of urban and rural planning at the same level, formulate a corresponding fire safety guarantee plan.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　城市、县人民政府应当对历史建筑设置保护标志，建立历史建筑档案。  
历史建筑档案应当包括下列内容：  
(一)建筑艺术特征、历史特征、建设年代及稀有程度；  
(二)建筑的有关技术资料；  
(三)建筑的使用现状和权属变化情况；  
(四)建筑的修缮、装饰装修过程中形成的文字、图纸、图片、影像等资料；  
(五)建筑的测绘信息记录和相关资料。

**Article 32** The city or county people's government shall set up protection signs for historical buildings and establish archives for historical buildings.  
The archives for a historical building shall include the following contents:  
(1) the architectural artistic features, historical features, construction period and degree of rarity;  
(2) relevant technical data of the building;  
(3) the current usage status and changes in ownership of the building;  
(4) materials such as texts, drawings, pictures and videos formed during the repair, decoration or renovation of the building;  
(5) surveying and mapping information records and related materials of the building.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　历史建筑的所有权人应当按照保护规划的要求，负责历史建筑的维护和修缮。  
县级以上地方人民政府可以从保护资金中对历史建筑的维护和修缮给予补助。  
历史建筑有损毁危险，所有权人不具备维护和修缮能力的，当地人民政府应当采取措施进行保护。  
任何单位或者个人不得损坏或者擅自迁移、拆除历史建筑。

**Article 33** The owner of a historical building shall, in accordance with the requirements of the protection plan, be responsible for the maintenance and repair of the historical building.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level may grant subsidies from the protection funds for the maintenance and repair of historical buildings.  
Where a historical building is in danger of damage and the owner does not have the capability to maintain or repair it, the local people's government shall take measures to protect it.  
No unit or individual may damage or, without authorization, relocate or demolish a historical building.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　建设工程选址，应当尽可能避开历史建筑；因特殊情况不能避开的，应当尽可能实施原址保护。  
对历史建筑实施原址保护的，建设单位应当事先确定保护措施，报城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门会同同级文物主管部门批准。  
因公共利益需要进行建设活动，对历史建筑无法实施原址保护、必须迁移异地保护或者拆除的，应当由城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门会同同级文物主管部门，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的保护主管部门会同同级文物主管部门批准。  
本条规定的历史建筑原址保护、迁移、拆除所需费用，由建设单位列入建设工程预算。

**Article 34** The site selection for a construction project shall, as far as possible, avoid historical buildings; where, due to special circumstances, avoidance is impossible, on-site protection shall be implemented as far as possible.  
Where on-site protection of a historical building is implemented, the construction unit shall determine protection measures in advance and report them to the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government, together with the competent department of cultural relics at the same level, for approval.  
Where a construction activity is necessary for public interest and it is impossible to implement on-site protection of a historical building, and it is necessary to relocate it to another site for protection or to demolish it, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government shall, together with the competent department of cultural relics at the same level, report to the competent department of protection designated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, together with the competent department of cultural relics at the same level, for approval.  
The expenses necessary for the on-site protection, relocation or demolition of a historical building as prescribed in this Article shall be included in the construction project budget by the construction unit.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　对历史建筑进行外部修缮装饰、添加设施以及改变历史建筑的结构或者使用性质的，应当经城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门会同同级文物主管部门批准，并依照有关法律、法规的规定办理相关手续。

**Article 35** External repair, decoration, addition of facilities, or alteration of the structure or use nature of a historical building shall be approved by the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government together with the competent department of cultural relics at the same level, and relevant procedures shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　在历史文化名城、名镇、名村保护范围内涉及文物保护的，应当执行文物保护法律、法规的规定。

**Article 36** Where the protection of cultural relics is involved within the protection areas of famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages, the provisions of the laws and regulations on the protection of cultural relics shall be implemented.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　违反本条例规定，国务院建设主管部门、国务院文物主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府及其有关主管部门的工作人员，不履行监督管理职责，发现违法行为不予查处或者有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 37** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any staff member of the competent department of construction under the State Council, the competent department of cultural relics under the State Council, or the local people's government at or above the county level or its relevant competent department fails to perform the duty of supervision and administration, fails to investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered, or commits other acts of abuse of power, dereliction of duty or engaging in malpractice for personal gain, which constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law; if the act does not constitute a crime, a sanction shall be given in accordance with law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反本条例规定，地方人民政府有下列行为之一的，由上级人民政府责令改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分：  
(一)未组织编制保护规划的；  
(二)未按照法定程序组织编制保护规划的；  
(三)擅自修改保护规划的；  
(四)未将批准的保护规划予以公布的。

**Article 38** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a local people's government commits any of the following acts, the people's government at a higher level shall order it to make corrections, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions in accordance with law:  
(1) failing to organize the formulation of a protection plan;  
(2) failing to organize the formulation of a protection plan in accordance with statutory procedures;  
(3) modifying the protection plan without authorization;  
(4) failing to make public the approved protection plan.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反本条例规定，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的保护主管部门或者城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门，未按照保护规划的要求或者未按照法定程序履行[本条例第二十八条](#art-28)、第三十四条、第三十五条规定的审批职责的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府有关部门责令改正，通报批评；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 39** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the competent department of protection designated by the people's government of a province, an autonomous region or a municipality directly under the Central Government, or the competent department of urban and rural planning of a city or county people's government, fails to perform the examination and approval duties prescribed in Articles 28, 34 and 35 of these Regulations in accordance with the requirements of the protection plan or in accordance with statutory procedures, the people's government at the same level or the relevant department of the people's government at a higher level shall order it to make corrections and circulate a notice of criticism; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions in accordance with law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本条例规定，城市、县人民政府因保护不力，导致已批准公布的历史文化名城、名镇、名村被列入濒危名单的，由上级人民政府通报批评；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 40** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a city or county people's government, due to inadequate protection, causes an already approved and publicly announced famous historical and cultural city, town or village to be included in the list of endangered items, the people's government at a higher level shall circulate a notice of criticism; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions in accordance with law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本条例规定，在历史文化名城、名镇、名村保护范围内有下列行为之一的，由城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门责令停止违法行为、限期恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；逾期不恢复原状或者不采取其他补救措施的，城乡规划主管部门可以指定有能力的单位代为恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，所需费用由违法者承担；造成严重后果的，对单位并处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款，对个人并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
(一)开山、采石、开矿等破坏传统格局和历史风貌的；  
(二)占用保护规划确定保留的园林绿地、河湖水系、道路等的；  
(三)修建生产、储存爆炸性、易燃性、放射性、毒害性、腐蚀性物品的工厂、仓库等的。

**Article 41** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any of the following acts is committed within the protection areas of famous historical and cultural cities, towns and villages, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government shall order the cessation of the illegal act, restoration to the original condition within a time limit or the taking of other remedial measures; any illegal gains shall be confiscated; if restoration to the original condition is not made or other remedial measures are not taken within the time limit, the competent department of urban and rural planning may designate a capable unit to carry out restoration to the original condition or take other remedial measures, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall additionally be imposed on the unit, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall additionally be imposed on the individual; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne in accordance with law:  
(1) engaging in activities such as mountain excavation, quarrying and mining that damage the traditional layout and historical features;  
(2) occupying the gardens and green spaces, river and lake systems, roads, etc. that are designated for preservation in the protection plan;  
(3) constructing factories, warehouses, etc. for producing or storing explosive, flammable, radioactive, toxic or corrosive substances.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本条例规定，在历史建筑上刻划、涂污的，由城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门责令恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，处50元的罚款。

**Article 42** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a historical building is carved or defaced, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government shall order restoration to the original condition or the taking of other remedial measures, and impose a fine of 50 yuan.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反本条例规定，未经城乡规划主管部门会同同级文物主管部门批准，有下列行为之一的，由城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门责令停止违法行为、限期恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；逾期不恢复原状或者不采取其他补救措施的，城乡规划主管部门可以指定有能力的单位代为恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，所需费用由违法者承担；造成严重后果的，对单位并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款，对个人并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
(一)拆除历史建筑以外的建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施的；  
(二)对历史建筑进行外部修缮装饰、添加设施以及改变历史建筑的结构或者使用性质的。  
有关单位或者个人进行[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)规定的活动，或者经批准进行本条第一款规定的活动，但是在活动过程中对传统格局、历史风貌或者历史建筑构成破坏性影响的，依照本条第一款规定予以处罚。

**Article 43** Where, in violation of these Regulations, without the approval of the competent department of urban and rural planning together with the competent department of cultural relics at the same level, any of the following acts is committed, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government shall order the cessation of the illegal act, restoration to the original condition within a time limit or the taking of other remedial measures; any illegal gains shall be confiscated; if restoration to the original condition is not made or other remedial measures are not taken within the time limit, the competent department of urban and rural planning may designate a capable unit to carry out restoration to the original condition or take other remedial measures, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall additionally be imposed on the unit, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall additionally be imposed on the individual; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne in accordance with law:  
(1) demolishing buildings, structures or other facilities other than historical buildings;  
(2) carrying out external repair, decoration, addition of facilities, or alteration of the structure or use nature of a historical building.  
Where a relevant unit or individual carries out an activity prescribed in Article 25 of these Regulations, or carries out an activity prescribed in the first paragraph of this Article upon approval, but causes destructive impact on the traditional layout, historical features or historical buildings during the activity, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反本条例规定，损坏或者擅自迁移、拆除历史建筑的，由城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门责令停止违法行为、限期恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；逾期不恢复原状或者不采取其他补救措施的，城乡规划主管部门可以指定有能力的单位代为恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，所需费用由违法者承担；造成严重后果的，对单位并处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款，对个人并处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 44** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a historical building is damaged or, without authorization, relocated or demolished, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government shall order the cessation of the illegal act, restoration to the original condition within a time limit or the taking of other remedial measures; any illegal gains shall be confiscated; if restoration to the original condition is not made or other remedial measures are not taken within the time limit, the competent department of urban and rural planning may designate a capable unit to carry out restoration to the original condition or take other remedial measures, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall additionally be imposed on the unit, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall additionally be imposed on the individual; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne in accordance with law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反本条例规定，擅自设置、移动、涂改或者损毁历史文化街区、名镇、名村标志牌的，由城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，对单位处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款，对个人处1000元以上1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 45** Where, in violation of these Regulations, signage of a historical and cultural block, famous town or village is set up, moved, altered or damaged without authorization, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government shall order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on the unit, and a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本条例规定，对历史文化名城、名镇、名村中的文物造成损毁的，依照文物保护法律、法规的规定给予处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 46** Where, in violation of these Regulations, damage is caused to cultural relics in a famous historical and cultural city, town or village, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the laws and regulations on the protection of cultural relics; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
(一)历史建筑，是指经城市、县人民政府确定公布的具有一定保护价值，能够反映历史风貌和地方特色，未公布为文物保护单位，也未登记为不可移动文物的建筑物、构筑物。  
(二)历史文化街区，是指经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府核定公布的保存文物特别丰富、历史建筑集中成片、能够较完整和真实地体现传统格局和历史风貌，并具有一定规模的区域。  
历史文化街区保护的具体实施办法，由国务院建设主管部门会同国务院文物主管部门制定。

**Article 47** For the purposes of these Regulations, the meanings of the following terms are as follows:  
(1) "Historical building" means a building or structure that is determined and publicly announced by the city or county people's government, has certain preservation value, reflects historical features and local characteristics, and has not been announced as a protected cultural relic unit or registered as an immovable cultural relic.  
(2) "Historical and cultural block" means an area that is verified and publicly announced by the people's government of a province, an autonomous region or a municipality directly under the Central Government, is particularly rich in preserved cultural relics, has historical buildings concentrated in an integrated area, can reflect the traditional layout and historical features in a relatively complete and authentic manner, and has a certain scale.  
The specific implementation measures for the protection of historical and cultural blocks shall be formulated by the competent department of construction under the State Council together with the competent department of cultural relics under the State Council.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本条例自2008年7月1日起施行。

**Article 48** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 2008.
