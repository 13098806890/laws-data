# 劳动就业服务企业管理规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：1990-11-22  
**生效日期**：1990-11-22

> (1990年11月22日中华人民共和国国务院令第66号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为巩固和发展劳动就业服务企业，保障其合法权益，加强管理，促进城镇劳动就业工作的开展，制定本规定。 第二条　劳动就业服务企业是承担安置城镇待业人员任务、由国家和社会扶持、进行生产经营自救的集体所有制经济组织。 前款所称承担安置城镇待业人员任务，是指： (一)劳动就业服务企业开办时，从业人员中百分之六十以上(含百分之六十)为城镇待业人员； (二)劳动就业服务企业存续期间，根据当地就业安置任务和企业常年生产经营情况按一定比例安置城镇待业人员。 本规定所称城镇待业人员，是指城镇居民中持有待业证明的未就过业的人员和曾就过业又失业的人员。 第三条　国家对劳动就业服务企业实行扶持政策，鼓励社会各方面依法扶持兴办各种形式的劳动就业服务企业。 各级人民政府及其行业主管部门应当重视和加强对劳动就业服务企业的领导，把巩固和发展劳动就业服务企业作为解决城镇就业问题的重要途径，将其纳入国民经济和社会发展计划，促进城镇劳动就业工作的开展。 第四条　国家对劳动就业服务企业给予下列税收优惠： (一)新开办的劳动就业服务企业免征所得税二至三年； (二)免税期满后，继续承担安置城镇待业人员任务并达到一定比例的，享受相应的减免税优惠； (三)适当调低劳动就业服务企业所得税的税率。 上述税收优惠的具体实施办法，由国家税务局商劳动部等有关部门制定。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为巩固和发展劳动就业服务企业，保障其合法权益，加强管理，促进城镇劳动就业工作的开展，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of consolidating and developing labour employment service enterprises, protecting their legitimate rights and interests, strengthening management, and promoting the development of urban labour employment work.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　劳动就业服务企业是承担安置城镇待业人员任务、由国家和社会扶持、进行生产经营自救的集体所有制经济组织。  
前款所称承担安置城镇待业人员任务，是指：  
(一)劳动就业服务企业开办时，从业人员中百分之六十以上(含百分之六十)为城镇待业人员；  
(二)劳动就业服务企业存续期间，根据当地就业安置任务和企业常年生产经营情况按一定比例安置城镇待业人员。  
本规定所称城镇待业人员，是指城镇居民中持有待业证明的未就过业的人员和曾就过业又失业的人员。

**Article 2** A labour employment service enterprise is a collectively-owned economic organization that undertakes the task of placing urban unemployed persons, is supported by the State and society, and engages in self-rescue through production and operation.  
The undertaking of the task of placing urban unemployed persons referred to in the preceding paragraph means:  
(1) when a labour employment service enterprise is established, not less than 60% (including 60%) of its employees are urban unemployed persons; and  
(2) during the existence of a labour employment service enterprise, it places urban unemployed persons in a certain proportion according to the local employment placement tasks and the enterprise's regular production and operation conditions.  
For the purposes of these Provisions, "urban unemployed persons" means persons among urban residents who hold unemployment certificates and have never been employed, or persons who have been employed but are now unemployed.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对劳动就业服务企业实行扶持政策，鼓励社会各方面依法扶持兴办各种形式的劳动就业服务企业。  
各级人民政府及其行业主管部门应当重视和加强对劳动就业服务企业的领导，把巩固和发展劳动就业服务企业作为解决城镇就业问题的重要途径，将其纳入国民经济和社会发展计划，促进城镇劳动就业工作的开展。

**Article 3** The State adopts a support policy for labour employment service enterprises and encourages all sectors of society to support and establish various forms of labour employment service enterprises according to law.  
People's governments at all levels and their competent industry departments shall attach importance to and strengthen leadership over labour employment service enterprises, regard consolidating and developing labour employment service enterprises as an important means of solving urban employment problems, incorporate them into the national economic and social development plans, and promote the development of urban labour employment work.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对劳动就业服务企业给予下列税收优惠：  
(一)新开办的劳动就业服务企业免征所得税二至三年；  
(二)免税期满后，继续承担安置城镇待业人员任务并达到一定比例的，享受相应的减免税优惠；  
(三)适当调低劳动就业服务企业所得税的税率。  
上述税收优惠的具体实施办法，由国家税务局商劳动部等有关部门制定。

**Article 4** The State grants the following tax preferences to labour employment service enterprises:  
(1) newly established labour employment service enterprises shall be exempt from income tax for two to three years;  
(2) after the expiry of the tax exemption period, those that continue to undertake the task of placing urban unemployed persons and meet a certain proportion shall enjoy corresponding tax reduction or exemption benefits; and  
(3) the income tax rate for labour employment service enterprises shall be appropriately reduced.  
The specific implementing measures for the above tax preferences shall be formulated by the State Administration of Taxation in consultation with the Ministry of Labour and other relevant departments.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家在开办条件、物资供应、固定资产和流动资金贷款等方面对劳动就业服务企业予以支持和照顾。

**Article 5** The State shall provide support and preferential treatment to labour employment service enterprises in respect of establishment conditions, material supply, fixed asset and working capital loans, and other aspects.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家保护劳动就业服务企业的合法权益。禁止任何机关和单位非法改变劳动就业服务企业的集体所有制性质、干预企业自主权和向企业平调或者摊派人力、物力和财力。

**Article 6** The State protects the legitimate rights and interests of labour employment service enterprises. No organ or unit may illegally change the collective ownership nature of labour employment service enterprises, interfere with their decision-making power, or transfer or apportion manpower, materials, or financial resources to them.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　劳动就业服务企业必须贯彻执行国家的方针、政策和法律、法规，坚持社会主义方向，坚持以安置待业人员为主、安置效益和经济效益相结合的原则。

**Article 7** Labour employment service enterprises must implement the State's guidelines, policies, laws, and regulations, adhere to the socialist orientation, and adhere to the principle of giving priority to placing unemployed persons and combining placement benefits with economic benefits.

### 第二章　政府对劳动就业服务企业的管理

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　开办劳动就业服务企业，须经审批机关批准，并经同级工商行政管理机关核准登记，领取《企业法人营业执照》或者《营业执照》后始得经营。  
前款所称审批机关批准是指：  
(一)有主办或者扶持单位的劳动就业服务企业，经主办或者扶持单位的主管部门审查同意，由同级劳动部门认定其劳动就业服务企业的性质；  
(二)待业人员自筹资金开办的劳动就业服务企业，由当地县(区)以上劳动部门批准。  
劳动就业服务企业应当在核准登记的经营范围内从事生产经营活动。

**Article 8** To establish a labour employment service enterprise, it must be approved by the approving authority and registered with the administrative department for industry and commerce at the same level, and shall not commence operations until it has obtained the "Business License for Enterprise Legal Person" or the "Business License".  
The approval by the approving authority referred to in the preceding paragraph means:  
(1) for a labour employment service enterprise that has a sponsoring or supporting unit, after examination and consent by the competent department of the sponsoring or supporting unit, the nature of the labour employment service enterprise shall be determined by the labour department at the same level; and  
(2) for a labour employment service enterprise established with funds raised by unemployed persons themselves, it shall be approved by the labour department at or above the county (district) level in the locality.  
A labour employment service enterprise shall engage in production and operation activities within the approved and registered scope of business.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各级人民政府的劳动部门对本地区劳动就业服务企业的职责是：  
(一)指导和监督劳动就业服务企业贯彻执行国家有关方针、政策和法律、法规；  
(二)制定劳动就业服务企业的地区发展规划；  
(三)根据国家有关规定，运用就业经费和生产扶持基金，推动劳动就业服务企业的发展，扩大其安置待业人员的能力；  
(四)开展技术培训，开辟物资渠道，组织技术咨询和信息交流，为劳动就业服务企业提供服务；  
(五)指导劳动部门所属的劳动就业服务企业的管理活动及其干部的管理和培养工作，开展评选先进集体和个人的活动；  
(六)省、自治区、直辖市(含计划单列市，下同)人民政府的劳动部门组织本地区的劳动就业服务企业开展产品评优、企业升级的工作。  
各级劳动部门的就业服务机构，按照国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的规定，可以承担上款各项的有关具体工作。

**Article 9** The responsibilities of the labour departments of people's governments at all levels with respect to labour employment service enterprises in their respective regions are:  
(1) to guide and supervise labour employment service enterprises in implementing the relevant State guidelines, policies, laws, and regulations;  
(2) to formulate regional development plans for labour employment service enterprises;  
(3) to use employment funds and production support funds, in accordance with the relevant State provisions, to promote the development of labour employment service enterprises and expand their capacity to place unemployed persons;  
(4) to carry out technical training, open up material supply channels, organize technical consultation and information exchanges, and provide services to labour employment service enterprises;  
(5) to guide the management activities of labour employment service enterprises under the labour department and the management and training of their cadres, and to carry out activities to select advanced collectives and individuals; and  
(6) the labour departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government (including cities with separate planning status, the same below) shall organize labour employment service enterprises in their respective regions to carry out product evaluation and enterprise upgrading.  
The employment service institutions of labour departments at all levels may, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, undertake the relevant specific work concerning the items in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　各行业主管部门对本部门劳动就业服务企业的职责是：  
(一)指导和监督劳动就业服务企业贯彻执行国家有关方针、政策和法律、法规；  
(二)制定劳动就业服务企业的部门发展规划，协助企业筹措发展资金；  
(三)协调劳动就业服务企业与部门内各有关方面的关系；  
(四)开展技术培训，为劳动就业服务企业提供咨询，组织物资、生产、技术等信息交流；  
(五)帮助劳动就业服务企业进行新产品鉴定和科研成果鉴定；  
(六)指导本部门所属的劳动就业服务企业的干部管理和培养工作，开展评选先进集体和个人的活动。

**Article 10** The responsibilities of the competent industry departments with respect to labour employment service enterprises under their respective departments are:  
(1) to guide and supervise labour employment service enterprises in implementing the relevant State guidelines, policies, laws, and regulations;  
(2) to formulate departmental development plans for labour employment service enterprises and assist enterprises in raising development funds;  
(3) to coordinate the relationships between labour employment service enterprises and the various relevant parties within the department;  
(4) to carry out technical training, provide consultation to labour employment service enterprises, and organize the exchange of information on materials, production, and technology;  
(5) to assist labour employment service enterprises in conducting appraisals of new products and scientific research achievements; and  
(6) to guide the management and training of cadres of labour employment service enterprises under the department, and to carry out activities to select advanced collectives and individuals.

### 第三章　主办或者扶持单位与劳动就业服务企业的关系

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　企业、事业单位、机关、团体、部队等主办或者扶持单位(简称主办或者扶持单位，下同)对所主办或者扶持开办的劳动就业服务企业的职责是：  
(一)劳动就业服务企业开办时，为企业筹措开办资金，帮助企业办理审批和工商登记手续；  
(二)为劳动就业服务企业安置待业人员提供一定的生产经营条件；  
(三)协调劳动就业服务企业与各方面的关系；  
(四)在劳动就业服务企业兴办初期，指导企业制定管理制度，任用、招聘或者组织民主选举企业的厂长(经理)；  
(五)尊重并维护劳动就业服务企业在人财物、产供销等方面的管理自主权；  
(六)在平等互利、等价交换的原则基础上，同劳动就业服务企业开展生产经营和服务等方面的合作活动。

**Article 11** The responsibilities of the sponsoring or supporting units (hereinafter referred to as sponsoring or supporting units), such as enterprises, institutions, government organs, organizations, and military units, with respect to the labour employment service enterprises they sponsor or support and establish are:  
(1) at the time of establishment of a labour employment service enterprise, to raise the initial capital for the enterprise and assist the enterprise in handling the approval and industrial and commercial registration procedures;  
(2) to provide certain production and operation conditions for the labour employment service enterprise to place unemployed persons;  
(3) to coordinate the relationships between the labour employment service enterprise and various parties;  
(4) in the early stage of the establishment of the labour employment service enterprise, to guide the enterprise in formulating management systems, and to appoint, recruit, or organize democratic elections for the enterprise's factory director (manager);  
(5) to respect and safeguard the labour employment service enterprise's decision-making power in respect of personnel, financial resources, materials, production, supply, and marketing; and  
(6) to carry out cooperative activities in production, operation, and services with the labour employment service enterprise on the basis of the principles of equality, mutual benefit, and exchange of equal values.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　主办或者扶持单位应当按照国家有关规定积极支持本单位职工到劳动就业服务企业担任生产经营和技术等方面的管理职务。  
主办或者扶持单位的职工到劳动就业服务企业任职，应当逐步实行聘任制，由主办或者扶持单位、任职人员和劳动就业服务企业三方签订聘用合同。聘用合同应当以书面形式订立，其主要内容应当包括：  
(一)聘用人员的职责；  
(二)聘用人员的待遇；  
(三)聘用期限；  
(四)违约责任及其处理办法；  
(五)三方认为应当规定的其他内容。  
聘用合同一经依法订立即具法律约束力，三方均应当认真履行，不得擅自改变。  
聘任期满后可以续聘。  
聘用合同书应当报劳动就业服务企业主管部门和劳动部门备案。

**Article 12** A sponsoring or supporting unit shall, in accordance with the relevant State provisions, actively support its own employees in taking up management positions in production, operation, technology, and other aspects in labour employment service enterprises.  
The appointment of employees of sponsoring or supporting units to positions in labour employment service enterprises shall gradually implement a contract system, and an employment contract shall be signed by the three parties, namely, the sponsoring or supporting unit, the appointed person, and the labour employment service enterprise. The employment contract shall be concluded in writing, and its main contents shall include:  
(1) the duties of the employed person;  
(2) the treatment of the employed person;  
(3) the term of employment;  
(4) the liability for breach of contract and the methods for handling such breach; and  
(5) other contents that the three parties deem necessary to stipulate.  
Once an employment contract is concluded according to law, it shall have legal binding force, and the three parties shall all perform it diligently and shall not alter it without authorization.  
After the expiry of the employment term, the contract may be renewed.  
The employment contract shall be reported to the competent department of the labour employment service enterprise and the labour department for the record.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　全民所有制的主办或者扶持单位的职工被劳动就业服务企业聘用后，仍保留其在原单位的全民所有制职工的身份和待遇。  
聘用人员退休后回原单位领取退休金并享受退休人员的一切待遇。

**Article 13** If an employee of a sponsoring or supporting unit under ownership by the whole people is employed by a labour employment service enterprise, he or she shall retain the status and treatment as an employee under ownership by the whole people in the original unit.  
An employed person who retires shall return to the original unit to receive a pension and enjoy all the benefits of retirees.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　主办或者扶持单位对支持劳动就业服务企业的资金、设备等，应当坚持有偿使用原则：  
(一)扶持资金(限于主办或者扶持单位的自有资金)可以作为借用款由劳动就业服务企业按双方约定分期归还，也可以依法作为投资参与劳动就业服务企业的利润分配；  
(二)设备、工具等生产资料和厂房可以在合理作价的基础上由劳动就业服务企业一次或分期付清；主办或者扶持单位也可以采用出租形式，收取相当于折旧费的租金。

**Article 14** With respect to funds, equipment, and other resources provided to support labour employment service enterprises, sponsoring or supporting units shall adhere to the principle of compensatory use:  
(1) support funds (limited to the self-owned funds of the sponsoring or supporting unit) may be used as loans and shall be repaid by the labour employment service enterprise in installments as agreed between the parties, or may be invested according to law and participate in the profit distribution of the labour employment service enterprise; and  
(2) means of production such as equipment, tools, and factory buildings may be paid for by the labour employment service enterprise in a lump sum or in installments on the basis of a reasonable valuation; the sponsoring or supporting unit may also adopt a leasing form and charge rent equivalent to the depreciation fee.

### 第四章　劳动就业服务企业的内部管理

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　劳动就业服务企业实行民主管理。除下列情况外，劳动就业服务企业的内部管理按国家有关城镇集体所有制企业的法律、法规的规定执行：  
(一)[本规定第十一条](#art-11)(四)所规定的情况；  
(二)以全民所有制企业为主办单位的劳动就业服务企业，其厂长(经理)人选可以由主办单位提出，由主办单位和劳动就业服务企业共同确定。厂长(经理)实行任期制。在厂长(经理)任期内，无法定理由，主办单位和劳动就业服务企业均不得擅自对厂长(经理)予以罢免或调动。

**Article 15** Labour employment service enterprises shall practise democratic management. Except for the following circumstances, the internal management of labour employment service enterprises shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the State's laws and regulations on urban collectively-owned enterprises:  
(1) the circumstances specified in Article 11(4) of these Provisions; and  
(2) for a labour employment service enterprise whose sponsoring unit is an enterprise under ownership by the whole people, the candidate for factory director (manager) may be proposed by the sponsoring unit and determined jointly by the sponsoring unit and the labour employment service enterprise. The factory director (manager) shall serve a term of office. During the term of office of the factory director (manager), unless there are legal grounds, neither the sponsoring unit nor the labour employment service enterprise may dismiss or transfer the factory director (manager) without authorization.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　劳动就业服务企业可以实行多种形式的生产经营责任制，但任何一种生产经营责任制均应当以安置待业人员作为责任制的一项重要内容。

**Article 16** Labour employment service enterprises may adopt various forms of production and operation responsibility systems, but any such responsibility system shall take the placement of unemployed persons as an important element of the responsibility system.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　劳动就业服务企业应当按照灵活方便、合同管理、骨干稳定、合理流动的原则，自主选择用工形式。  
从业人员在劳动就业服务企业工作期间应当计算工龄。

**Article 17** Labour employment service enterprises shall, in accordance with the principles of flexibility and convenience, contract management, stable backbone force, and reasonable mobility, independently choose the form of employment.  
The length of service of employees during their work in a labour employment service enterprise shall be counted towards their length of service.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　劳动就业服务企业根据自身情况可以有条件地适当安排全民所有制主办单位的富余人员在本企业就业。安置富余人员应当由劳动就业服务企业同全民所有制主办单位双方签订安置合同，合同内容由双方商定。

**Article 18** A labour employment service enterprise may, in light of its own circumstances and subject to conditions, appropriately arrange for the employment of surplus personnel from the sponsoring unit under ownership by the whole people in its enterprise. The placement of surplus personnel shall be carried out by concluding a placement contract between the labour employment service enterprise and the sponsoring unit under ownership by the whole people, the contents of which shall be agreed upon by both parties.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　劳动就业服务企业可以根据国家有关规定和企业经济效益，自主地确定适合本企业具体情况的工资和奖金的分配形式和办法。

**Article 19** A labour employment service enterprise may, in accordance with the relevant State provisions and its own economic benefits, independently determine the forms and methods of distributing wages and bonuses suitable to its specific conditions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　劳动就业服务企业对职工个人出资可以实行付息或者分红的办法。企业盈利，按一定比例付息或者分红；企业亏损，在弥补亏损之前，不得付息或者分红。付息或者分红的比例不得超过国家规定的最高限额。

**Article 20** A labour employment service enterprise may adopt the method of paying interest or distributing dividends on capital contributed by employees. If the enterprise makes a profit, it shall pay interest or distribute dividends at a certain proportion; if the enterprise suffers a loss, it shall not pay interest or distribute dividends until the loss is made up. The proportion of interest or dividends shall not exceed the maximum limit prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　由待业人员自筹资金开办的劳动就业服务企业，在企业具备偿还能力时，可以逐步偿还个人出资。

**Article 21** For a labour employment service enterprise established with funds raised by unemployed persons themselves, when the enterprise has the ability to repay, it may gradually repay the capital contributed by individuals.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　劳动就业服务企业应当建立养老保险制度并逐步建立待业保险制度。保险基金提取办法和保险项目按国家有关规定执行。

**Article 22** Labour employment service enterprises shall establish an old-age insurance system and gradually establish an unemployment insurance system. The methods for withdrawing insurance funds and the insurance items shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant State provisions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　劳动就业服务企业应当执行国家有关财务制度和财经纪律，健全财务管理，接受国家有关主管部门的指导和监督。

**Article 23** Labour employment service enterprises shall implement the relevant State financial systems and financial discipline, improve their financial management, and accept the guidance and supervision of the relevant competent State departments.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　违反[本规定第八条](#art-8)的规定，以劳动就业服务企业名义进行活动的，由工商行政管理机关根据国家有关规定给予行政处罚。

**Article 24** Whoever, in violation of Article 8 of these Provisions, conducts activities in the name of a labour employment service enterprise shall be given an administrative penalty by the administrative department for industry and commerce in accordance with the relevant State provisions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　任何机关和单位违反[本规定第六条](#art-6)的规定，非法改变劳动就业服务企业的集体所有制性质，干预企业自主权的，其上级主管部门应当予以纠正；向劳动就业服务企业平调或者摊派人力、物力、财力的，必须予以赔偿。对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，由其主管部门根据情节轻重，给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 25** If any organ or unit, in violation of Article 6 of these Provisions, illegally changes the collective ownership nature of a labour employment service enterprise or interferes with its decision-making power, the superior competent department shall order correction; if it transfers or apportions manpower, materials, or financial resources to a labour employment service enterprise, it must make compensation. The competent department shall, depending on the severity of the circumstances, impose administrative sanctions on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons; if the conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　劳动就业服务企业违反本规定有关企业领导人员的产生、罢免程序规定的，其主管部门应当予以纠正，并追究直接责任人员的行政责任。  
劳动就业服务企业的主管部门或者主办、扶持单位违反本规定有关劳动就业服务企业领导人员产生、罢免程序规定的，其上一级主管部门或者主办、扶持单位的主管部门应当予以纠正，并追究直接责任人员的行政责任。

**Article 26** If a labour employment service enterprise violates the provisions of these Provisions concerning the procedures for the election and removal of enterprise leading personnel, its competent department shall order correction and hold the directly responsible persons administratively liable.  
If the competent department of a labour employment service enterprise or the sponsoring or supporting unit violates the provisions of these Provisions concerning the procedures for the election and removal of leading personnel of labour employment service enterprises, the competent department at the next higher level or the competent department of the sponsoring or supporting unit shall order correction and hold the directly responsible persons administratively liable.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　除本规定有明文规定者外，劳动就业服务企业均应当执行国家有关城镇集体所有制企业的政策和法规。

**Article 27** Except as expressly provided in these Provisions, labour employment service enterprises shall all implement the policies, laws, and regulations of the State concerning urban collectively-owned enterprises.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和国务院各行业主管部门可以根据本规定并结合本地区、本部门的具体情况制定实施办法。

**Article 28** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and the competent industry departments of the State Council may, in accordance with these Provisions and in light of the specific conditions of their respective regions and departments, formulate implementing measures.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本规定由劳动部负责解释。

**Article 29** The Ministry of Labour shall be responsible for the interpretation of these Provisions.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本规定从发布之日起施行。

**Article 30** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
