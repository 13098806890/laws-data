# 使用有毒物品作业场所劳动保护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2024-12-06  
**生效日期**：2025-01-20

> （2002年5月12日中华人民共和国国务院令第352号公布　根据2024年12月6日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保证作业场所安全使用有毒物品，预防、控制和消除职业中毒危害，保护劳动者的生命安全、身体健康及其相关权益，根据职业病防治法和其他有关法律、行政法规的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　作业场所使用有毒物品可能产生职业中毒危害的劳动保护，适用本条例。 第三条　按照有毒物品产生的职业中毒危害程度，有毒物品分为一般有毒物品和高毒物品。国家对作业场所使用高毒物品实行特殊管理。 一般有毒物品目录、高毒物品目录由国务院卫生行政部门会同有关部门依据国家标准制定、调整并公布。 第四条　从事使用有毒物品作业的用人单位（以下简称用人单位）应当使用符合国家标准的有毒物品，不得在作业场所使用国家明令禁止使用的有毒物品或者使用不符合国家标准的有毒物品。 用人单位应当尽可能使用无毒物品；需要使用有毒物品的，应当优先选择使用低毒物品。 第五条　用人单位应当依照本条例和其他有关法律、行政法规的规定，采取有效的防护措施，预防职业中毒事故的发生，依法参加工伤保险，保障劳动者的生命安全和身体健康。 第六条　国家鼓励研制、开发、推广、应用有利于预防、控制、消除职业中毒危害和保护劳动者健康的新技术、新工艺、新材料；限制使用或者淘汰有关职业中毒危害严重的技术、工艺、材料；加强对有关职业病的机理和发生规律的基础研究，提高有关职业病防治科学技术水平。 第七条　禁止使用童工。 用人单位不得安排未成年人和孕期、哺乳期的女职工从事使用有毒物品的作业。 第八条　工会组织应当督促并协助用人单位开展职业卫生宣传教育和培训，对用人单位的职业卫生工作提出意见和建议，与用人单位就劳动者反映的职业病防治问题进行协调并督促解决。 工会组织对用人单位违反法律、法规，侵犯劳动者合法权益的行为，有权要求纠正；产生严重职业中毒危害时，有权要求用人单位采取防护措施，或者向政府有关部门建议采取强制性措施；发生职业中毒事故时，有权参与事故调查处理；发现危及劳动者生命、健康的情形时，有权建议用人单位组织劳动者撤离危险现场，用人单位应当立即作出处理。 第九条　县级以上人民政府卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门及其他有关行政部门应当依据各自的职责，监督用人单位严格遵守本条例和其他有关法律、法规的规定，加强作业场所使用有毒物品的劳动保护，防止职业中毒事故发生，确保劳动者依法享有的权利。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保证作业场所安全使用有毒物品，预防、控制和消除职业中毒危害，保护劳动者的生命安全、身体健康及其相关权益，根据职业病防治法和其他有关法律、行政法规的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law on the Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases and the provisions of other relevant laws and administrative regulations, for the purposes of ensuring the safe use of toxic substances at workplaces, preventing, controlling and eliminating the hazards of occupational poisoning, and protecting the safety, health and related rights and interests of workers.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　作业场所使用有毒物品可能产生职业中毒危害的劳动保护，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the labor protection against occupational poisoning hazards that may arise from the use of toxic substances at workplaces.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　按照有毒物品产生的职业中毒危害程度，有毒物品分为一般有毒物品和高毒物品。国家对作业场所使用高毒物品实行特殊管理。  
一般有毒物品目录、高毒物品目录由国务院卫生行政部门会同有关部门依据国家标准制定、调整并公布。

**Article 3** Toxic substances are divided into general toxic substances and highly toxic substances according to the degree of occupational poisoning hazards they may cause. The State shall exercise special management over the use of highly toxic substances at workplaces.  
The catalogue of general toxic substances and the catalogue of highly toxic substances shall be formulated, adjusted and published by the health administrative department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments in accordance with the national standards.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　从事使用有毒物品作业的用人单位（以下简称用人单位）应当使用符合国家标准的有毒物品，不得在作业场所使用国家明令禁止使用的有毒物品或者使用不符合国家标准的有毒物品。  
用人单位应当尽可能使用无毒物品；需要使用有毒物品的，应当优先选择使用低毒物品。

**Article 4** The employer that engages in operations involving the use of toxic substances (hereinafter referred to as the employer) shall use toxic substances that conform to the national standards, and shall not use toxic substances that are explicitly prohibited by the State or that do not conform to the national standards at workplaces.  
The employer shall, to the extent possible, use non-toxic substances; if it is necessary to use toxic substances, it shall, as a priority, choose to use low-toxicity substances.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　用人单位应当依照本条例和其他有关法律、行政法规的规定，采取有效的防护措施，预防职业中毒事故的发生，依法参加工伤保险，保障劳动者的生命安全和身体健康。

**Article 5** The employer shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and other relevant laws and administrative regulations, adopt effective protective measures to prevent the occurrence of occupational poisoning accidents, participate in work-related injury insurance according to law, and guarantee the safety and health of workers.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励研制、开发、推广、应用有利于预防、控制、消除职业中毒危害和保护劳动者健康的新技术、新工艺、新材料；限制使用或者淘汰有关职业中毒危害严重的技术、工艺、材料；加强对有关职业病的机理和发生规律的基础研究，提高有关职业病防治科学技术水平。

**Article 6** The State encourages the research, development, promotion and application of new technologies, techniques and materials that are conducive to preventing, controlling and eliminating the hazards of occupational poisoning and protecting the health of workers; restricts the use of or eliminates technologies, techniques and materials that cause serious occupational poisoning hazards; and strengthens basic research on the mechanism and occurrence pattern of relevant occupational diseases to raise the scientific and technological level of prevention and treatment of relevant occupational diseases.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　禁止使用童工。  
用人单位不得安排未成年人和孕期、哺乳期的女职工从事使用有毒物品的作业。

**Article 7** The employment of child labor is prohibited.  
The employer shall not assign minors or female workers during pregnancy or lactation to operations involving the use of toxic substances.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　工会组织应当督促并协助用人单位开展职业卫生宣传教育和培训，对用人单位的职业卫生工作提出意见和建议，与用人单位就劳动者反映的职业病防治问题进行协调并督促解决。  
工会组织对用人单位违反法律、法规，侵犯劳动者合法权益的行为，有权要求纠正；产生严重职业中毒危害时，有权要求用人单位采取防护措施，或者向政府有关部门建议采取强制性措施；发生职业中毒事故时，有权参与事故调查处理；发现危及劳动者生命、健康的情形时，有权建议用人单位组织劳动者撤离危险现场，用人单位应当立即作出处理。

**Article 8** Trade union organizations shall urge and assist the employer in carrying out propaganda, education and training on occupational health, present opinions and suggestions on the employer's occupational health work, and coordinate with the employer on and urge the settlement of issues concerning the prevention and control of occupational diseases raised by workers.  
Where the employer violates laws or regulations and infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of workers, the trade union organization shall have the right to request correction; where serious occupational poisoning hazards occur, the trade union organization shall have the right to request the employer to adopt protective measures, or to suggest that the relevant government department take compulsory measures; where an occupational poisoning accident occurs, the trade union organization shall have the right to participate in the investigation and handling of the accident; where situations endangering the life or health of workers are discovered, the trade union organization shall have the right to suggest that the employer organize the evacuation of workers from the dangerous site, and the employer shall deal with the matter immediately.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上人民政府卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门及其他有关行政部门应当依据各自的职责，监督用人单位严格遵守本条例和其他有关法律、法规的规定，加强作业场所使用有毒物品的劳动保护，防止职业中毒事故发生，确保劳动者依法享有的权利。

**Article 9** The health administrative departments, disease prevention and control departments and other relevant administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, supervise the employer in strictly observing the provisions of these Regulations and other relevant laws and regulations, strengthen labor protection in the use of toxic substances at workplaces, prevent the occurrence of occupational poisoning accidents, and ensure the rights enjoyed by workers according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　各级人民政府应当加强对使用有毒物品作业场所职业卫生安全及相关劳动保护工作的领导，督促、支持卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门及其他有关行政部门依法履行监督检查职责，及时协调、解决有关重大问题；在发生职业中毒事故时，应当采取有效措施，控制事故危害的蔓延并消除事故危害，并妥善处理有关善后工作。

**Article 10** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen leadership over the occupational health and safety and related labor protection work at workplaces where toxic substances are used, urge and support the health administrative departments, disease prevention and control departments and other relevant administrative departments to perform their duties of supervision and inspection according to law, and promptly coordinate and settle major issues; in the event of an occupational poisoning accident, they shall take effective measures to control the spread of and eliminate the hazards of the accident, and properly handle the related aftermath.

### 第二章　作业场所的预防措施

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　用人单位的设立，应当符合有关法律、行政法规规定的设立条件，并依法办理有关手续，取得营业执照。  
用人单位的使用有毒物品作业场所，除应当符合职业病防治法规定的职业卫生要求外，还必须符合下列要求：  
（一）作业场所与生活场所分开，作业场所不得住人；  
（二）有害作业与无害作业分开，高毒作业场所与其他作业场所隔离；  
（三）设置有效的通风装置；可能突然泄漏大量有毒物品或者易造成急性中毒的作业场所，设置自动报警装置和事故通风设施；  
（四）高毒作业场所设置应急撤离通道和必要的泄险区。

**Article 11** The establishment of an employer shall meet the establishment conditions prescribed by relevant laws and administrative regulations, and shall go through relevant procedures according to law to obtain a business license.  
The workplaces where toxic substances are used by the employer, in addition to complying with the occupational health requirements stipulated in the Law on Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases, shall also meet the following requirements:  
(1) The workplace shall be separated from the living area, and no person shall reside in the workplace;  
(2) Hazardous operations shall be separated from non-hazardous operations, and workplaces with high toxicity operations shall be isolated from other workplaces;  
(3) Effective ventilation devices shall be installed; workplaces where a large amount of toxic substances may be suddenly leaked or acute poisoning may easily occur shall be equipped with automatic alarm devices and emergency ventilation facilities;  
(4) Emergency evacuation passages and necessary decontamination areas shall be set up in workplaces with high toxicity operations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　使用有毒物品作业场所应当设置黄色区域警示线、警示标识和中文警示说明。警示说明应当载明产生职业中毒危害的种类、后果、预防以及应急救治措施等内容。  
高毒作业场所应当设置红色区域警示线、警示标识和中文警示说明，并设置通讯报警设备。

**Article 12** Workplaces where toxic substances are used shall be equipped with yellow area warning lines, warning signs and Chinese warning notices. The warning notices shall specify the types, consequences, prevention and emergency treatment measures of occupational poisoning hazards.  
Workplaces with high toxicity operations shall be equipped with red area warning lines, warning signs and Chinese warning notices, and shall be provided with communication and alarm equipment.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　新建、扩建、改建的建设项目和技术改造、技术引进项目（以下统称建设项目），可能产生职业中毒危害的，应当依照职业病防治法的规定进行职业中毒危害预评价；可能产生职业中毒危害的建设项目的职业中毒危害防护设施应当与主体工程同时设计，同时施工，同时投入生产和使用；建设项目竣工验收前，应当进行职业中毒危害控制效果评价；建设项目的职业中毒危害防护设施经依法组织验收合格后，方可投入生产和使用。  
可能产生职业中毒危害的建设项目的职业中毒危害防护设施设计应当符合国家职业卫生标准和卫生要求。

**Article 13** For new construction, expansion, reconstruction projects and technological transformation, technology introduction projects (hereinafter collectively referred to as construction projects) that may cause occupational poisoning hazards, a pre-assessment of occupational poisoning hazards shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases; the protective facilities against occupational poisoning hazards of such construction projects shall be designed, constructed, and put into production and use simultaneously with the main project; before the completion acceptance of the construction project, an evaluation of the control effect of occupational poisoning hazards shall be conducted; the protective facilities against occupational poisoning hazards of the construction project may be put into production and use only after they have passed the acceptance inspection organized according to law.  
The design of protective facilities against occupational poisoning hazards for construction projects that may cause occupational poisoning hazards shall comply with national occupational health standards and health requirements.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　用人单位应当按照国务院卫生行政部门的规定，向卫生行政部门及时、如实申报存在职业中毒危害项目。  
从事使用高毒物品作业的用人单位，在申报使用高毒物品作业项目时，应当向卫生行政部门提交下列有关资料：  
（一）职业中毒危害控制效果评价报告；  
（二）职业卫生管理制度和操作规程等材料；  
（三）职业中毒事故应急救援预案。  
从事使用高毒物品作业的用人单位变更所使用的高毒物品品种的，应当依照前款规定向原受理申报的卫生行政部门重新申报。

**Article 14** An employer shall, in accordance with the provisions of the health administrative department of the State Council, promptly and truthfully report to the health administrative department the projects involving occupational poisoning hazards.  
An employer engaged in operations using highly toxic substances shall, when reporting the projects using highly toxic substances, submit the following relevant materials to the health administrative department:  
(1) The evaluation report on the control effect of occupational poisoning hazards;  
(2) Materials such as the occupational health management system and operating procedures;  
(3) The emergency rescue plan for occupational poisoning accidents.  
If an employer engaged in operations using highly toxic substances changes the types of highly toxic substances used, it shall re-report to the health administrative department that originally accepted the report in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　用人单位变更名称、法定代表人或者负责人的，应当向原受理申报的卫生行政部门备案。

**Article 15** If an employer changes its name, legal representative or person in charge, it shall file with the health administrative department that originally accepted the report.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　从事使用高毒物品作业的用人单位，应当配备应急救援人员和必要的应急救援器材、设备，制定事故应急救援预案，并根据实际情况变化对应急救援预案适时进行修订，定期组织演练。事故应急救援预案和演练记录应当报当地卫生行政部门、应急管理部门和公安部门备案。

**Article 16** An employer engaged in operations using highly toxic substances shall be equipped with emergency rescue personnel and necessary emergency rescue equipment and devices, formulate emergency rescue plans for accidents, and timely revise the emergency rescue plans according to changes in actual conditions, and regularly organize drills. The emergency rescue plans and drill records shall be filed with the local health administrative department, emergency management department and public security department.

### 第三章　劳动过程的防护

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　用人单位应当依照职业病防治法的有关规定，采取有效的职业卫生防护管理措施，加强劳动过程中的防护与管理。  
从事使用高毒物品作业的用人单位，应当配备专职的或者兼职的职业卫生医师和护士；不具备配备专职的或者兼职的职业卫生医师和护士条件的，应当与依法取得资质认证的职业卫生技术服务机构签订合同，由其提供职业卫生服务。

**Article 17** An employer shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law on Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases, adopt effective occupational health protection management measures to strengthen protection and management during the labor process.  
An employer engaged in operations using highly toxic substances shall be equipped with full-time or part-time occupational health physicians and nurses; if it does not meet the conditions for having full-time or part-time occupational health physicians and nurses, it shall sign a contract with a legally qualified occupational health technical service institution to provide occupational health services.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　用人单位应当与劳动者订立劳动合同，将工作过程中可能产生的职业中毒危害及其后果、职业中毒危害防护措施和待遇等如实告知劳动者，并在劳动合同中写明，不得隐瞒或者欺骗。  
劳动者在已订立劳动合同期间因工作岗位或者工作内容变更，从事劳动合同中未告知的存在职业中毒危害的作业时，用人单位应当依照前款规定，如实告知劳动者，并协商变更原劳动合同有关条款。  
用人单位违反前两款规定的，劳动者有权拒绝从事存在职业中毒危害的作业，用人单位不得因此单方面解除或者终止与劳动者所订立的劳动合同。

**Article 18** An employer shall conclude a labor contract with a worker, truthfully inform the worker of the occupational poisoning hazards that may arise during the work process and their consequences, protective measures against occupational poisoning hazards and benefits, and specify them in the labor contract, and shall not conceal or deceive.  
If a worker, during the term of the labor contract, is assigned to a job involving occupational poisoning hazards that were not disclosed in the labor contract due to a change in the job position or work content, the employer shall truthfully inform the worker in accordance with the preceding paragraph and negotiate to modify the relevant terms of the original labor contract.  
If the employer violates the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs, the worker shall have the right to refuse to perform the work involving occupational poisoning hazards, and the employer shall not unilaterally rescind or terminate the labor contract concluded with the worker on this ground.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　用人单位有关管理人员应当熟悉有关职业病防治的法律、法规以及确保劳动者安全使用有毒物品作业的知识。  
用人单位应当对劳动者进行上岗前的职业卫生培训和在岗期间的定期职业卫生培训，普及有关职业卫生知识，督促劳动者遵守有关法律、法规和操作规程，指导劳动者正确使用职业中毒危害防护设备和个人使用的职业中毒危害防护用品。  
劳动者经培训考核合格，方可上岗作业。

**Article 19** The relevant management personnel of an employer shall be familiar with the laws and regulations on occupational disease prevention and control and the knowledge of ensuring the safe use of toxic substances in operations by workers.  
An employer shall provide pre-job occupational health training and regular on-the-job occupational health training to workers, disseminate relevant occupational health knowledge, urge workers to abide by relevant laws, regulations and operating procedures, and guide workers to correctly use protective equipment against occupational poisoning hazards and personal protective articles against occupational poisoning hazards.  
A worker may start working only after passing the training assessment.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　用人单位应当确保职业中毒危害防护设备、应急救援设施、通讯报警装置处于正常适用状态，不得擅自拆除或者停止运行。  
用人单位应当对前款所列设施进行经常性的维护、检修，定期检测其性能和效果，确保其处于良好运行状态。  
职业中毒危害防护设备、应急救援设施和通讯报警装置处于不正常状态时，用人单位应当立即停止使用有毒物品作业；恢复正常状态后，方可重新作业。

**Article 20** An employer shall ensure that protective equipment against occupational poisoning hazards, emergency rescue facilities, and communication alarm devices are in normal working condition, and shall not dismantle or stop their operation without authorization.  
An employer shall perform regular maintenance and repair on the facilities listed in the preceding paragraph, and regularly test their performance and effectiveness to ensure they are in good working condition.  
When protective equipment against occupational poisoning hazards, emergency rescue facilities, and communication alarm devices are in abnormal condition, the employer shall immediately stop operations using toxic substances; operations may resume only after normal condition is restored.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　用人单位应当为从事使用有毒物品作业的劳动者提供符合国家职业卫生标准的防护用品，并确保劳动者正确使用。

**Article 21** The employing unit shall provide protective equipment conforming to the national occupational health standards for workers who are engaged in operations involving the use of toxic substances, and shall ensure that workers use such equipment correctly.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　有毒物品必须附具说明书，如实载明产品特性、主要成分、存在的职业中毒危害因素、可能产生的危害后果、安全使用注意事项、职业中毒危害防护以及应急救治措施等内容；没有说明书或者说明书不符合要求的，不得向用人单位销售。  
用人单位有权向生产、经营有毒物品的单位索取说明书。

**Article 22** Toxic substances must be accompanied by instructions that truthfully state the product characteristics, main ingredients, existing occupational poisoning hazard factors, possible harmful consequences, safety precautions for use, occupational poisoning hazard prevention and emergency treatment measures, and other contents. If there are no instructions or if the instructions do not meet the requirements, such substances shall not be sold to employing units.  
The employing unit shall have the right to demand instructions from the units that produce or deal in toxic substances.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　有毒物品的包装应当符合国家标准，并以易于劳动者理解的方式加贴或者拴挂有毒物品安全标签。有毒物品的包装必须有醒目的警示标识和中文警示说明。  
经营、使用有毒物品的单位，不得经营、使用没有安全标签、警示标识和中文警示说明的有毒物品。

**Article 23** The packaging of toxic substances shall conform to the national standards, and safety labels for toxic substances shall be affixed or attached in a manner easy for workers to understand. The packaging of toxic substances must have conspicuous warning signs and warning statements in Chinese.  
Units that deal in or use toxic substances shall not deal in or use toxic substances without safety labels, warning signs, and warning statements in Chinese.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　用人单位维护、检修存在高毒物品的生产装置，必须事先制订维护、检修方案，明确职业中毒危害防护措施，确保维护、检修人员的生命安全和身体健康。  
维护、检修存在高毒物品的生产装置，必须严格按照维护、检修方案和操作规程进行。维护、检修现场应当有专人监护，并设置警示标志。

**Article 24** When an employing unit maintains or overhauls production equipment containing highly toxic substances, it must formulate in advance a maintenance and overhaul plan specifying the protective measures against occupational poisoning hazards, to ensure the life safety and health of maintenance and overhaul personnel.  
Maintenance and overhaul of production equipment containing highly toxic substances must be carried out strictly in accordance with the maintenance and overhaul plan and the operating procedures. The maintenance and overhaul site shall be under the supervision of designated persons, and warning signs shall be set up.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　需要进入存在高毒物品的设备、容器或者狭窄封闭场所作业时，用人单位应当事先采取下列措施：  
（一）保持作业场所良好的通风状态，确保作业场所职业中毒危害因素浓度符合国家职业卫生标准；  
（二）为劳动者配备符合国家职业卫生标准的防护用品；  
（三）设置现场监护人员和现场救援设备。  
未采取前款规定措施或者采取的措施不符合要求的，用人单位不得安排劳动者进入存在高毒物品的设备、容器或者狭窄封闭场所作业。

**Article 25** When it is necessary to enter equipment, containers, or narrow enclosed spaces containing highly toxic substances for work, the employing unit shall take the following measures in advance:  
(1) Maintain a good ventilation condition at the workplace to ensure that the concentration of occupational poisoning hazard factors at the workplace conforms to the national occupational health standards;  
(2) Provide workers with protective equipment conforming to the national occupational health standards;  
(3) Arrange on-site supervisors and on-site rescue equipment.  
If the measures specified in the preceding paragraph are not taken or if the measures taken do not meet the requirements, the employing unit shall not arrange for workers to enter equipment, containers, or narrow enclosed spaces containing highly toxic substances for work.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　用人单位应当按照国务院卫生行政部门的规定，定期对使用有毒物品作业场所职业中毒危害因素进行检测、评价。检测、评价结果存入用人单位职业卫生档案，定期向所在地卫生行政部门报告并向劳动者公布。  
从事使用高毒物品作业的用人单位应当至少每一个月对高毒作业场所进行一次职业中毒危害因素检测；至少每半年进行一次职业中毒危害控制效果评价。  
高毒作业场所职业中毒危害因素不符合国家职业卫生标准和卫生要求时，用人单位必须立即停止高毒作业，并采取相应的治理措施；经治理，职业中毒危害因素符合国家职业卫生标准和卫生要求的，方可重新作业。

**Article 26** The employing unit shall, in accordance with the provisions of the health administration department of the State Council, regularly conduct detection and evaluation of occupational poisoning hazard factors at workplaces where toxic substances are used. The results of such detection and evaluation shall be kept in the occupational health records of the employing unit, and shall be reported periodically to the local health administration department and made known to the workers.  
An employing unit engaged in operations involving the use of highly toxic substances shall conduct detection of occupational poisoning hazard factors at the highly toxic workplaces at least once every month; and shall conduct an evaluation of the effectiveness of occupational poisoning hazard control at least once every six months.  
When the occupational poisoning hazard factors at a highly toxic workplace do not conform to the national occupational health standards and hygienic requirements, the employing unit must immediately stop the highly toxic operations and take corresponding control measures. Only after the occupational poisoning hazard factors have been brought into conformity with the national occupational health standards and hygienic requirements through such control measures may the operations be resumed.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　从事使用高毒物品作业的用人单位应当设置淋浴间和更衣室，并设置清洗、存放或者处理从事使用高毒物品作业劳动者的工作服、工作鞋帽等物品的专用间。  
劳动者结束作业时，其使用的工作服、工作鞋帽等物品必须存放在高毒作业区域内，不得穿戴到非高毒作业区域。

**Article 27** An employing unit engaged in operations involving the use of highly toxic substances shall set up shower rooms and changing rooms, and shall set up special rooms for washing, storing, or handling the work clothes, work shoes, caps, and other articles used by workers engaged in such operations.  
When workers finish their work, the work clothes, work shoes, caps, and other articles used by them must be kept within the highly toxic work area, and shall not be worn or taken into non-highly-toxic work areas.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　用人单位应当按照规定对从事使用高毒物品作业的劳动者进行岗位轮换。  
用人单位应当为从事使用高毒物品作业的劳动者提供岗位津贴。

**Article 28** The employing unit shall, according to the provisions, carry out job rotation for workers engaged in operations involving the use of highly toxic substances.  
The employing unit shall provide post allowances for workers engaged in operations involving the use of highly toxic substances.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　用人单位转产、停产、停业或者解散、破产的，应当采取有效措施，妥善处理留存或者残留有毒物品的设备、包装物和容器。

**Article 29** When an employing unit changes its line of production, suspends production, ceases business, or is dissolved, or becomes bankrupt, it shall take effective measures to properly handle the equipment, packaging materials, and containers that retain or contain residual toxic substances.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　用人单位应当对本单位执行本条例规定的情况进行经常性的监督检查；发现问题，应当及时依照本条例规定的要求进行处理。

**Article 30** The employing unit shall regularly supervise and inspect the implementation of the provisions of these Regulations within its own unit; if any problem is found, it shall promptly handle it in accordance with the requirements of these Regulations.

### 第四章　职业健康监护

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　用人单位应当组织从事使用有毒物品作业的劳动者进行上岗前职业健康检查。  
用人单位不得安排未经上岗前职业健康检查的劳动者从事使用有毒物品的作业，不得安排有职业禁忌的劳动者从事其所禁忌的作业。

**Article 31** The employing unit shall organize workers who are to engage in operations involving the use of toxic substances to undergo pre-employment occupational health examinations.  
The employing unit shall not assign any worker who has not undergone a pre-employment occupational health examination to engage in operations involving the use of toxic substances, nor shall it assign any worker with occupational contraindications to engage in the operations to which they are contraindicated.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　用人单位应当对从事使用有毒物品作业的劳动者进行定期职业健康检查。  
用人单位发现有职业禁忌或者有与所从事职业相关的健康损害的劳动者，应当将其及时调离原工作岗位，并妥善安置。  
用人单位对需要复查和医学观察的劳动者，应当按照体检机构的要求安排其复查和医学观察。

**Article 32** The employing unit shall conduct periodic occupational health examinations for workers engaged in operations involving the use of toxic substances.  
Where the employing unit discovers any worker with occupational contraindications or health damage related to the occupation in which he or she is engaged, it shall promptly transfer such worker from his or her original work post and properly arrange for him or her.  
For workers who require re-examination or medical observation, the employing unit shall arrange for such re-examination or medical observation in accordance with the requirements of the medical examination institution.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　用人单位应当对从事使用有毒物品作业的劳动者进行离岗时的职业健康检查；对离岗时未进行职业健康检查的劳动者，不得解除或者终止与其订立的劳动合同。  
用人单位发生分立、合并、解散、破产等情形的，应当对从事使用有毒物品作业的劳动者进行健康检查，并按照国家有关规定妥善安置职业病病人。

**Article 33** The employing unit shall conduct occupational health examinations for workers engaged in operations involving the use of toxic substances at the time of leaving their posts; for workers who have not undergone an occupational health examination at the time of leaving their posts, the employing unit shall not dissolve or terminate the labor contract concluded with them.  
Where the employing unit undergoes division, merger, dissolution, bankruptcy, or other circumstances, it shall conduct health examinations for workers engaged in operations involving the use of toxic substances and properly arrange for occupational disease patients in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　用人单位对受到或者可能受到急性职业中毒危害的劳动者，应当及时组织进行健康检查和医学观察。

**Article 34** The employing unit shall promptly organize health examinations and medical observation for workers who have been or may have been exposed to acute occupational poisoning hazards.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　劳动者职业健康检查和医学观察的费用，由用人单位承担。

**Article 35** The expenses for occupational health examinations and medical observation of workers shall be borne by the employing unit.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　用人单位应当建立职业健康监护档案。  
职业健康监护档案应当包括下列内容：  
（一）劳动者的职业史和职业中毒危害接触史；  
（二）相应作业场所职业中毒危害因素监测结果；  
（三）职业健康检查结果及处理情况；  
（四）职业病诊疗等劳动者健康资料。

**Article 36** The employing unit shall establish occupational health surveillance files.  
The occupational health surveillance files shall include the following:  
(1) the occupational history of the workers and their history of exposure to occupational poisoning hazards;  
(2) the monitoring results of occupational poisoning hazard factors in the corresponding workplaces;  
(3) the results of occupational health examinations and the handling thereof; and  
(4) the health records of workers, such as diagnosis and treatment of occupational diseases.

### 第五章　劳动者的权利与义务

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　从事使用有毒物品作业的劳动者在存在威胁生命安全或者身体健康危险的情况下，有权通知用人单位并从使用有毒物品造成的危险现场撤离。  
用人单位不得因劳动者依据前款规定行使权利，而取消或者减少劳动者在正常工作时享有的工资、福利待遇。

**Article 37** Workers engaged in operations involving the use of toxic substances shall have the right to notify the employing unit and to evacuate from the dangerous site caused by the use of toxic substances under circumstances that threaten their life safety or physical health.  
The employing unit shall not cancel or reduce the wages and welfare benefits that workers enjoy during normal work on the grounds that the workers exercise their rights in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　劳动者享有下列职业卫生保护权利：  
（一）获得职业卫生教育、培训；  
（二）获得职业健康检查、职业病诊疗、康复等职业病防治服务；  
（三）了解工作场所产生或者可能产生的职业中毒危害因素、危害后果和应当采取的职业中毒危害防护措施；  
（四）要求用人单位提供符合防治职业病要求的职业中毒危害防护设施和个人使用的职业中毒危害防护用品，改善工作条件；  
（五）对违反职业病防治法律、法规，危及生命、健康的行为提出批评、检举和控告；  
（六）拒绝违章指挥和强令进行没有职业中毒危害防护措施的作业；  
（七）参与用人单位职业卫生工作的民主管理，对职业病防治工作提出意见和建议。  
用人单位应当保障劳动者行使前款所列权利。禁止因劳动者依法行使正当权利而降低其工资、福利等待遇或者解除、终止与其订立的劳动合同。

**Article 38** Workers shall enjoy the following rights to occupational health protection:  
(1) to receive occupational health education and training;  
(2) to obtain occupational health examinations, diagnosis and treatment of occupational diseases, rehabilitation, and other services for the prevention and treatment of occupational diseases;  
(3) to be informed of the occupational poisoning hazard factors that are or may be generated in the workplace, the consequences of such hazards, and the protective measures to be taken against occupational poisoning hazards;  
(4) to request that the employing unit provide protective facilities against occupational poisoning hazards that meet the requirements for the prevention and treatment of occupational diseases and personal protective equipment against occupational poisoning hazards, and to improve working conditions;  
(5) to criticize, report, and accuse acts that violate laws and regulations on the prevention and treatment of occupational diseases and endanger life or health;  
(6) to refuse to follow illegal commands and to refuse to be compelled to perform operations without protective measures against occupational poisoning hazards; and  
(7) to participate in the democratic management of the employing unit's occupational health work and to put forward opinions and suggestions on the prevention and treatment of occupational diseases.  
The employing unit shall guarantee workers' exercise of the rights set forth in the preceding paragraph. It is prohibited to reduce the wages, welfare, or other benefits of workers, or to dissolve or terminate the labor contract concluded with them, on the grounds that they lawfully exercise their legitimate rights.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　劳动者有权在正式上岗前从用人单位获得下列资料：  
（一）作业场所使用的有毒物品的特性、有害成分、预防措施、教育和培训资料；  
（二）有毒物品的标签、标识及有关资料；  
（三）有毒物品安全使用说明书；  
（四）可能影响安全使用有毒物品的其他有关资料。

**Article 39** Workers shall have the right to obtain the following materials from the employing unit before officially starting work:  
(1) materials on the characteristics, harmful components, preventive measures, education, and training regarding the toxic substances used in the workplace;  
(2) labels, markings, and related materials of toxic substances;  
(3) safety instructions for the use of toxic substances; and  
(4) other relevant materials that may affect the safe use of toxic substances.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　劳动者有权查阅、复印其本人职业健康监护档案。  
劳动者离开用人单位时，有权索取本人健康监护档案复印件；用人单位应当如实、无偿提供，并在所提供的复印件上签章。

**Article 40** Workers shall have the right to access and copy their own occupational health surveillance files.  
When a worker leaves the employing unit, he or she shall have the right to request a copy of his or her own health surveillance files; the employing unit shall provide them truthfully and free of charge, and shall affix its seal on the copies provided.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　用人单位按照国家规定参加工伤保险的，患职业病的劳动者有权按照国家有关工伤保险的规定，享受下列工伤保险待遇：  
（一）医疗费：因患职业病进行诊疗所需费用，由工伤保险基金按照规定标准支付；  
（二）住院伙食补助费：由用人单位按照当地因公出差伙食标准的一定比例支付；  
（三）康复费：由工伤保险基金按照规定标准支付；  
（四）残疾用具费：因残疾需要配置辅助器具的，所需费用由工伤保险基金按照普及型辅助器具标准支付；  
（五）停工留薪期待遇：原工资、福利待遇不变，由用人单位支付；  
（六）生活护理补助费：经评残并确认需要生活护理的，生活护理补助费由工伤保险基金按照规定标准支付；  
（七）一次性伤残补助金：经鉴定为十级至一级伤残的，按照伤残等级享受相当于6个月至24个月的本人工资的一次性伤残补助金，由工伤保险基金支付；  
（八）伤残津贴：经鉴定为四级至一级伤残的，按照规定享受相当于本人工资75%至90%的伤残津贴，由工伤保险基金支付；  
（九）死亡补助金：因职业中毒死亡的，由工伤保险基金按照不低于48个月的统筹地区上年度职工月平均工资的标准一次支付；  
（十）丧葬补助金：因职业中毒死亡的，由工伤保险基金按照6个月的统筹地区上年度职工月平均工资的标准一次支付；  
（十一）供养亲属抚恤金：因职业中毒死亡的，对由死者生前提供主要生活来源的亲属由工伤保险基金支付抚恤金：对其配偶每月按照统筹地区上年度职工月平均工资的40%发给，对其生前供养的直系亲属每人每月按照统筹地区上年度职工月平均工资的30%发给；  
（十二）国家规定的其他工伤保险待遇。  
本条例施行后，国家对工伤保险待遇的项目和标准作出调整时，从其规定。

**Article 41** Where the employing unit participates in work-related injury insurance in accordance with State regulations, workers suffering from occupational diseases shall have the right to enjoy the following work-related injury insurance benefits in accordance with the relevant State regulations on work-related injury insurance:  
(1) Medical expenses: The expenses required for diagnosis and treatment due to occupational diseases shall be paid from the work-related injury insurance fund at the rates prescribed by the regulations;  
(2) Hospital meal allowance: The employing unit shall pay this in accordance with a certain percentage of the local meal standard for official travel;  
(3) Rehabilitation expenses: The work-related injury insurance fund shall pay these at the rates prescribed by the regulations;  
(4) Expenses for disability aids: Where assistive devices are necessary due to disability, the required expenses shall be paid by the work-related injury insurance fund according to the standard for popular-type assistive devices;  
(5) Treatment during the period of suspension of work with retained pay: The original wages and benefits shall remain unchanged and shall be paid by the employing unit;  
(6) Living care subsidy: After assessment and confirmation of the need for living care, the living care subsidy shall be paid by the work-related injury insurance fund at the rates prescribed by the regulations;  
(7) Lump-sum disability grant: Where the disability is assessed as Grade 10 to Grade 1, a lump-sum disability grant equivalent to 6 to 24 months of the worker's personal wages shall be enjoyed according to the disability grade and paid by the work-related injury insurance fund;  
(8) Disability allowance: Where the disability is assessed as Grade 4 to Grade 1, a disability allowance equivalent to 75% to 90% of the worker's personal wages shall be enjoyed according to the regulations and paid by the work-related injury insurance fund;  
(9) Death allowance: In the case of death due to occupational poisoning, the work-related injury insurance fund shall make a lump-sum payment in an amount not less than 48 times the average monthly wage of the employees in the pooled area in the previous year;  
(10) Funeral subsidy: In the case of death due to occupational poisoning, the work-related injury insurance fund shall make a lump-sum payment at the standard of 6 times the average monthly wage of the employees in the pooled area in the previous year;  
(11) Survivor's pension for dependents: In the case of death due to occupational poisoning, the work-related injury insurance fund shall pay a pension to the relatives who were primarily supported by the deceased during his/her lifetime: 40% of the average monthly wage of the employees in the pooled area in the previous year shall be paid monthly to the spouse, and 30% of such average monthly wage shall be paid monthly to each direct relative who was supported by the deceased during his/her lifetime;  
(12) Other work-related injury insurance benefits as prescribed by the State.  
After these Regulations come into force, where the State makes adjustments to the items and standards of work-related injury insurance benefits, such adjustments shall prevail.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　用人单位未参加工伤保险的，其劳动者从事有毒物品作业患职业病的，用人单位应当按照国家有关工伤保险规定的项目和标准，保证劳动者享受工伤待遇。

**Article 42** Where the employing unit has not participated in work-related injury insurance, and a worker engaged in operations involving toxic substances suffers from an occupational disease, the employing unit shall, in accordance with the items and standards prescribed by the State regulations on work-related injury insurance, ensure that the worker enjoys work-related injury treatment.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　用人单位无营业执照以及被依法吊销营业执照，其劳动者从事使用有毒物品作业患职业病的，应当按照国家有关工伤保险规定的项目和标准，给予劳动者一次性赔偿。

**Article 43** Where the employing unit has no business license or its business license has been lawfully revoked, and a worker engaged in operations using toxic substances suffers from an occupational disease, the employing unit shall, in accordance with the items and standards prescribed by the State regulations on work-related injury insurance, make a lump-sum compensation to the worker.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　用人单位分立、合并的，承继单位应当承担由原用人单位对患职业病的劳动者承担的补偿责任。  
用人单位解散、破产的，应当依法从其清算财产中优先支付患职业病的劳动者的补偿费用。

**Article 44** Where the employing unit is split up or merged, the succeeding unit shall assume the compensation liability that the original employing unit had towards the worker suffering from an occupational disease.  
Where the employing unit is dissolved or declared bankrupt, it shall, according to law, first pay the compensation expenses for the worker suffering from an occupational disease from its liquidation property.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　劳动者除依法享有工伤保险外，依照有关民事法律的规定，尚有获得赔偿的权利的，有权向用人单位提出赔偿要求。

**Article 45** Where a worker, in addition to enjoying work-related injury insurance according to law, still has the right to obtain compensation under the provisions of relevant civil laws, the worker shall have the right to make a claim for compensation against the employing unit.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　劳动者应当学习和掌握相关职业卫生知识，遵守有关劳动保护的法律、法规和操作规程，正确使用和维护职业中毒危害防护设施及其用品；发现职业中毒事故隐患时，应当及时报告。  
作业场所出现使用有毒物品产生的危险时，劳动者应当采取必要措施，按照规定正确使用防护设施，将危险加以消除或者减少到最低限度。

**Article 46** Workers shall study and master relevant occupational hygiene knowledge, comply with the laws, regulations and operating procedures on labor protection, and correctly use and maintain facilities and articles for protection against occupational poisoning hazards; when discovering potential hazards of occupational poisoning accidents, they shall report promptly.  
When danger arising from the use of toxic substances occurs in the workplace, workers shall take necessary measures, correctly use protective facilities in accordance with the regulations, and eliminate or minimize the danger.

### 第六章　监督管理

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上人民政府卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门应当依照本条例的规定和国家有关职业卫生要求，依据职责划分，对作业场所使用有毒物品作业及职业中毒危害检测、评价活动进行监督检查。  
卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门实施监督检查，不得收取费用，不得接受用人单位的财物或者其他利益。

**Article 47** The health administrative departments and disease prevention and control departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and the relevant State requirements on occupational hygiene, and based on their division of duties, supervise and inspect operations involving toxic substances in workplaces and the activities of monitoring and assessing occupational poisoning hazards.  
When carrying out supervision and inspection, the health administrative departments and disease prevention and control departments shall not charge any fees, nor shall they accept property or other benefits from the employing unit.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门应当建立、健全监督制度，核查反映用人单位有关劳动保护的材料，履行监督责任。  
用人单位应当向卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门如实、具体提供反映有关劳动保护的材料；必要时，卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门可以查阅或者要求用人单位报送有关材料。

**Article 48** The health administrative departments and disease prevention and control departments shall establish and improve the supervision system, verify the materials reflecting the employing unit's labor protection, and fulfill their supervisory responsibilities.  
The employing unit shall truthfully and specifically provide the materials reflecting the relevant labor protection to the health administrative departments and disease prevention and control departments; where necessary, the health administrative departments and disease prevention and control departments may consult or request the employing unit to submit relevant materials.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门应当监督用人单位严格执行有关职业卫生规范。  
卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门应当依照本条例的规定对使用有毒物品作业场所的职业卫生防护设备、设施的防护性能进行定期检验和不定期的抽查；发现职业卫生防护设备、设施存在隐患时，应当责令用人单位立即消除隐患；消除隐患期间，应当责令其停止作业。

**Article 49** The health administrative departments and disease prevention and control departments shall supervise the employing unit to strictly implement the relevant occupational hygiene standards.  
The health administrative departments and disease prevention and control departments shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, conduct regular inspections and irregular spot checks on the protective performance of occupational hygiene protection equipment and facilities in workplaces where toxic substances are used; when discovering that occupational hygiene protection equipment or facilities have hidden dangers, they shall order the employing unit to immediately eliminate the hidden dangers; during the period of eliminating the hidden dangers, they shall order the employing unit to suspend operations.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门应当采取措施，鼓励对用人单位的违法行为进行举报、投诉、检举和控告。  
卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门对举报、投诉、检举和控告应当及时核实，依法作出处理，并将处理结果予以公布。  
卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门对举报人、投诉人、检举人和控告人负有保密的义务。

**Article 50** The health administrative departments and disease prevention and control departments shall take measures to encourage reporting, complaining, informing and accusing of the employing unit's illegal acts.  
The health administrative departments and disease prevention and control departments shall promptly verify reports, complaints, information and accusations, handle them according to law, and publish the results of the handling.  
The health administrative departments and disease prevention and control departments shall have the obligation to keep the identity of the reporter, complainant, informant and accuser confidential.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　职业卫生监督执法人员依法执行职务时，应当出示执法证件。  
职业卫生监督执法人员应当忠于职守，秉公执法；涉及用人单位秘密的，应当为其保密。

**Article 51** : When performing their duties according to law, occupational health supervision and law enforcement personnel shall present their law enforcement credentials.  
Occupational health supervision and law enforcement personnel shall be loyal to their duties and enforce the law impartially; where secrets of the employing unit are involved, they shall keep them confidential.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　疾病预防控制部门依法实施罚款的行政处罚，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，实施罚款决定与罚款收缴分离；收缴的罚款以及依法没收的经营所得，必须全部上缴国库。

**Article 52** : When the disease prevention and control department imposes the administrative penalty of a fine according to law, it shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, implement the separation of the fine decision and fine collection; all fines collected and business income confiscated according to law shall be turned over to the State Treasury in full.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门履行监督检查职责时，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）进入用人单位和使用有毒物品作业场所现场，了解情况，调查取证，进行抽样检查、检测、检验，进行实地检查；  
（二）查阅或者复制与违反本条例行为有关的资料，采集样品；  
（三）责令违反本条例规定的单位和个人停止违法行为。

**Article 53** : When performing their duties of supervision and inspection, the health administration and disease prevention and control departments shall have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) Enter the employing unit and the workplace where toxic substances are used to understand the situation, investigate and collect evidence, conduct sampling inspection, testing, examination, and conduct on-site inspection;  
(2) Consult or copy materials related to acts violating these Regulations, and collect samples;  
(3) Order the entities and individuals that violate the provisions of these Regulations to cease the illegal acts.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　发生职业中毒事故或者有证据证明职业中毒危害状态可能导致事故发生时，卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门有权采取下列临时控制措施：  
（一）责令暂停导致职业中毒事故的作业；  
（二）封存造成职业中毒事故或者可能导致事故发生的物品；  
（三）组织控制职业中毒事故现场。  
在职业中毒事故或者危害状态得到有效控制后，卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门应当及时解除控制措施。

**Article 54** : When an occupational poisoning accident occurs or there is evidence proving that the hazardous state of occupational poisoning may lead to an accident, the health administration and disease prevention and control departments shall have the authority to take the following temporary control measures:  
(1) Order the suspension of operations that cause the occupational poisoning accident;  
(2) Seal up articles that have caused the occupational poisoning accident or may lead to an accident;  
(3) Organize control of the scene of the occupational poisoning accident.  
After the occupational poisoning accident or hazardous state has been effectively controlled, the health administration and disease prevention and control departments shall promptly lift the control measures.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　职业卫生监督执法人员依法执行职务时，被检查单位应当接受检查并予以支持、配合，不得拒绝和阻碍。

**Article 55** : When occupational health supervision and law enforcement personnel perform their duties according to law, the unit being inspected shall accept the inspection, provide support and cooperation, and shall not refuse or obstruct.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　疾病预防控制部门应当加强队伍建设，提高职业卫生监督执法人员的政治、业务素质，依照本条例的规定，建立、健全内部监督制度，对职业卫生监督执法人员执行法律、法规和遵守纪律的情况进行监督检查。

**Article 56** : The disease prevention and control department shall strengthen team building, improve the political and professional quality of occupational health supervision and law enforcement personnel, establish and improve internal supervision systems in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, and conduct supervision and inspection on the implementation of laws and regulations and compliance with discipline by occupational health supervision and law enforcement personnel.

### 第七章　罚则

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　卫生行政、疾病预防控制部门的工作人员有下列行为之一，导致职业中毒事故发生的，依照刑法关于滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；造成职业中毒危害但尚未导致职业中毒事故发生，不够刑事处罚的，根据不同情节，依法给予降级、撤职或者开除的处分：  
（一）对用人单位不履行监督检查职责，或者发现用人单位存在违反本条例的行为不予查处的；  
（二）发现用人单位存在职业中毒危害，可能造成职业中毒事故，不及时依法采取控制措施的。

**Article 57** : Where any staff member of the health administration or disease prevention and control department commits any of the following acts, causing an occupational poisoning accident, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty, or other crimes; where occupational poisoning hazard is caused but has not yet led to an occupational poisoning accident, which is not enough for criminal punishment, a sanction of demotion, removal from office, or expulsion shall be given according to law based on the circumstances:  
(1) Failing to perform the duty of supervision and inspection over the employing unit, or failing to investigate and punish the employing unit upon discovering its acts violating these Regulations;  
(2) Discovering that the employing unit has occupational poisoning hazards that may cause an occupational poisoning accident and failing to take control measures in a timely manner according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　用人单位违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由疾病预防控制部门给予警告，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，提请有关人民政府按照国务院规定的权限责令停建、予以关闭；造成严重职业中毒危害或者导致职业中毒事故发生的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于重大劳动安全事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）可能产生职业中毒危害的建设项目，未依照职业病防治法的规定进行职业中毒危害预评价的；  
（二）职业中毒危害防护设施未与主体工程同时设计，同时施工，同时投入生产和使用的；  
（三）建设项目竣工验收前，未进行职业中毒危害控制效果评价，或者职业中毒危害防护设施未经依法组织验收合格，擅自投入生产和使用的；  
（四）可能产生职业中毒危害的建设项目，其职业中毒危害防护设施设计不符合国家职业卫生标准和卫生要求的。

**Article 58** : Where an employing unit violates the provisions of these Regulations and falls under any of the following circumstances, the disease prevention and control department shall give a warning and order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant people's government shall be requested to order suspension of construction or closure in accordance with the authority prescribed by the State Council; if serious occupational poisoning hazard is caused or an occupational poisoning accident occurs, for the person in charge and other directly responsible persons who are liable, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of major labor safety accidents or other crimes:  
(1) For a construction project that may produce occupational poisoning hazards, the pre-evaluation of occupational poisoning hazards has not been conducted in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases;  
(2) The protective facilities against occupational poisoning hazards have not been designed, constructed, and put into production and use simultaneously with the main project;  
(3) Before the completion acceptance of the construction project, the evaluation of the control effect of occupational poisoning hazards has not been conducted, or the protective facilities against occupational poisoning hazards have not been organized for acceptance as qualified according to law, and the project has been put into production and use without authorization;  
(4) For a construction project that may produce occupational poisoning hazards, the design of the protective facilities against occupational poisoning hazards does not meet the national occupational health standards and hygiene requirements.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　用人单位违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由疾病预防控制部门给予警告，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，提请有关人民政府按照国务院规定的权限予以关闭；造成严重职业中毒危害或者导致职业中毒事故发生的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于重大劳动安全事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）使用有毒物品作业场所未按照规定设置警示标识和中文警示说明的；  
（二）未对职业卫生防护设备、应急救援设施、通讯报警装置进行维护、检修和定期检测，导致上述设施处于不正常状态的；  
（三）未依照本条例的规定进行职业中毒危害因素检测和职业中毒危害控制效果评价的；  
（四）未向从事使用有毒物品作业的劳动者提供符合国家职业卫生标准的防护用品，或者未保证劳动者正确使用的。  
用人单位违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由疾病预防控制部门给予警告，责令限期改正，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，提请有关人民政府按照国务院规定的权限予以关闭；造成严重职业中毒危害或者导致职业中毒事故发生的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于重大劳动安全事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）高毒作业场所未按照规定设置撤离通道和泄险区的；  
（二）高毒作业场所未按照规定设置警示线的。

**Article 59** : Where an employing unit violates the provisions of these Regulations and falls under any of the following circumstances, the disease prevention and control department shall give a warning and order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant people's government shall be requested to close it down in accordance with the authority prescribed by the State Council; if serious occupational poisoning hazard is caused or an occupational poisoning accident occurs, for the person in charge and other directly responsible persons who are liable, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of major labor safety accidents or other crimes:  
(1) The workplace where toxic substances are used has not been equipped with warning signs and Chinese warning instructions as required;  
(2) The occupational health protective equipment, emergency rescue facilities, and communication and alarm devices have not been maintained, repaired, and regularly tested, causing the above facilities to be in an abnormal state;  
(3) The detection of occupational poisoning hazard factors and the evaluation of the control effect of occupational poisoning hazards have not been conducted in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(4) The employing unit has not provided protective articles that meet the national occupational health standards to workers engaged in the use of toxic substances, or has not ensured that workers use them correctly.  
Where an employing unit violates the provisions of these Regulations and falls under any of the following circumstances, the disease prevention and control department shall give a warning, order correction within a time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if correction is not made within the time limit, the relevant people's government shall be requested to close it down in accordance with the authority prescribed by the State Council; if serious occupational poisoning hazard is caused or an occupational poisoning accident occurs, for the person in charge and other directly responsible persons who are liable, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of major labor safety accidents or other crimes:  
(1) The high-toxicity workplace has not been equipped with evacuation passages and hazard relief areas as required;  
(2) The high-toxicity workplace has not been equipped with warning lines as required.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　用人单位违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由疾病预防控制部门给予警告，责令限期改正，处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，提请有关人民政府按照国务院规定的权限予以关闭；造成严重职业中毒危害或者导致职业中毒事故发生的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于重大责任事故罪、重大劳动安全事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）使用有毒物品作业场所未设置有效通风装置的，或者可能突然泄漏大量有毒物品或者易造成急性中毒的作业场所未设置自动报警装置或者事故通风设施的；  
（二）职业卫生防护设备、应急救援设施、通讯报警装置处于不正常状态而不停止作业，或者擅自拆除或者停止运行职业卫生防护设备、应急救援设施、通讯报警装置的。

**Article 60** : Where an employing unit violates the provisions of these Regulations and falls under any of the following circumstances, the disease prevention and control department shall give a warning, order correction within a time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if correction is not made within the time limit, the relevant people's government shall be requested to close it down in accordance with the authority prescribed by the State Council; if serious occupational poisoning hazard is caused or an occupational poisoning accident occurs, for the person in charge and other directly responsible persons who are liable, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of major liability accidents, the crime of major labor safety accidents, or other crimes:  
(1) The workplace where toxic substances are used has not been equipped with effective ventilation devices, or the workplace where a large amount of toxic substances may suddenly leak or acute poisoning is likely to occur has not been equipped with automatic alarm devices or emergency ventilation facilities;  
(2) The occupational health protective equipment, emergency rescue facilities, and communication and alarm devices are in an abnormal state without stopping operations, or the said equipment, facilities, and devices have been dismantled or stopped operating without authorization.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　从事使用高毒物品作业的用人单位违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由疾病预防控制部门给予警告，责令限期改正，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，提请有关人民政府按照国务院规定的权限予以关闭；造成严重职业中毒危害或者导致职业中毒事故发生的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于重大责任事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）作业场所职业中毒危害因素不符合国家职业卫生标准和卫生要求而不立即停止高毒作业并采取相应的治理措施的，或者职业中毒危害因素治理不符合国家职业卫生标准和卫生要求重新作业的；  
（二）未依照本条例的规定维护、检修存在高毒物品的生产装置的；  
（三）未采取本条例规定的措施，安排劳动者进入存在高毒物品的设备、容器或者狭窄封闭场所作业的。

**Article 61** Where an employer engaged in operations using highly toxic substances violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the disease prevention and control department shall issue a warning, order rectification within a time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, the matter shall be submitted to the relevant people's government for order of closure within the limits of authority prescribed by the State Council; if serious occupational poisoning hazards are caused or occupational poisoning accidents occur, the person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of major liability accident or other crimes, be investigated for criminal liability according to law:  
(1) failing to immediately stop the highly toxic operations and take corresponding control measures when the occupational poisoning hazard factors in the workplace do not conform to the national occupational health standards and hygiene requirements, or resuming operations after controlling the occupational poisoning hazard factors but such control still does not conform to the national occupational health standards and hygiene requirements;  
(2) failing to maintain or inspect production devices containing highly toxic substances in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(3) arranging workers to enter equipment, containers, or narrow enclosed spaces containing highly toxic substances without taking the measures prescribed in these Regulations.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　在作业场所使用国家明令禁止使用的有毒物品或者使用不符合国家标准的有毒物品的，由疾病预防控制部门责令立即停止使用，处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停止使用有毒物品作业，或者提请有关人民政府按照国务院规定的权限予以关闭；造成严重职业中毒危害或者导致职业中毒事故发生的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于危险物品肇事罪、重大责任事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 62** Where toxic substances explicitly prohibited by the State or toxic substances not conforming to national standards are used in the workplace, the disease prevention and control department shall order the immediate cessation of such use and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order cessation of operations using toxic substances, or submit the matter to the relevant people's government for order of closure within the limits of authority prescribed by the State Council; if serious occupational poisoning hazards are caused or occupational poisoning accidents occur, the person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of causing a dangerous substance accident, the crime of major liability accident, or other crimes, be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　用人单位违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由疾病预防控制部门责令限期改正，处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停止使用有毒物品作业，或者提请有关人民政府按照国务院规定的权限予以关闭；造成严重职业中毒危害或者导致职业中毒事故发生的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于重大责任事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）未组织从事使用有毒物品作业的劳动者进行上岗前职业健康检查，安排未经上岗前职业健康检查的劳动者从事使用有毒物品作业的；  
（二）使用未经培训考核合格的劳动者从事高毒作业的；  
（三）安排有职业禁忌的劳动者从事所禁忌的作业的；  
（四）发现有职业禁忌或者有与所从事职业相关的健康损害的劳动者，未及时调离原工作岗位，并妥善安置的；  
（五）安排未成年人或者孕期、哺乳期的女职工从事使用有毒物品作业的；  
（六）使用童工的。

**Article 63** Where an employer violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the disease prevention and control department shall order rectification within a time limit and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order cessation of operations using toxic substances, or submit the matter to the relevant people's government for order of closure within the limits of authority prescribed by the State Council; if serious occupational poisoning hazards are caused or occupational poisoning accidents occur, the person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of major liability accident or other crimes, be investigated for criminal liability according to law:  
(1) failing to organize pre-job occupational health examinations for workers engaged in operations using toxic substances, and arranging workers who have not undergone pre-job occupational health examinations to engage in operations using toxic substances;  
(2) using workers who have not passed training and assessment to engage in highly toxic operations;  
(3) arranging workers with occupational contraindications to engage in the contraindicated operations;  
(4) failing to promptly transfer workers found to have occupational contraindications or health damage related to their current occupation from their original work positions and properly reassign them;  
(5) arranging minors or female workers during pregnancy or lactation to engage in operations using toxic substances;  
(6) using child labor.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　从事使用有毒物品作业的用人单位违反本条例的规定，在转产、停产、停业或者解散、破产时未采取有效措施，妥善处理留存或者残留高毒物品的设备、包装物和容器的，由疾病预防控制部门责令改正，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；触犯刑律的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于污染环境罪、危险物品肇事罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 64** Where an employer engaged in operations using toxic substances violates the provisions of these Regulations and, when changing its product line, suspending production, ceasing business, or being dissolved or declared bankrupt, fails to take effective measures to properly handle equipment, packaging materials, and containers retaining or containing residues of highly toxic substances, the disease prevention and control department shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if a criminal law is violated, the person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of environmental pollution, the crime of causing a dangerous substance accident, or other crimes, be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　用人单位违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由疾病预防控制部门给予警告，责令限期改正，处5000元以上2万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停止使用有毒物品作业，或者提请有关人民政府按照国务院规定的权限予以关闭；造成严重职业中毒危害或者导致职业中毒事故发生的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于重大劳动安全事故罪、危险物品肇事罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）使用有毒物品作业场所未与生活场所分开或者在作业场所住人的；  
（二）未将有害作业与无害作业分开的；  
（三）高毒作业场所未与其他作业场所有效隔离的；  
（四）从事高毒作业未按照规定配备应急救援设施或者制定事故应急救援预案的。

**Article 65** Where an employer violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the disease prevention and control department shall issue a warning, order rectification within a time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, it shall order cessation of operations using toxic substances, or submit the matter to the relevant people's government for order of closure within the limits of authority prescribed by the State Council; if serious occupational poisoning hazards are caused or occupational poisoning accidents occur, the person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of major labor safety accident, the crime of causing a dangerous substance accident, or other crimes, be investigated for criminal liability according to law:  
(1) failing to separate the workplace where toxic substances are used from living areas, or allowing people to live in the workplace;  
(2) failing to separate hazardous operations from non-hazardous operations;  
(3) failing to effectively isolate the highly toxic operation workplace from other operation workplaces;  
(4) failing, when engaging in highly toxic operations, to equip emergency rescue facilities or formulate accident emergency rescue plans as required.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　用人单位违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由疾病预防控制部门给予警告，责令限期改正，处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，提请有关人民政府按照国务院规定的权限予以关闭：  
（一）未按照规定向卫生行政部门申报高毒作业项目的；  
（二）变更使用高毒物品品种，未按照规定向原受理申报的卫生行政部门重新申报，或者申报不及时、有虚假的。

**Article 66** Where an employer violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the disease prevention and control department shall issue a warning, order rectification within a time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, the matter shall be submitted to the relevant people's government for order of closure within the limits of authority prescribed by the State Council:  
(1) failing to declare highly toxic operation projects to the health administrative department as required;  
(2) changing the type of highly toxic substances used without re-declaring to the original health administrative department that accepted the declaration as required, or making a delayed or false declaration.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　用人单位违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由疾病预防控制部门给予警告，责令限期改正，可以并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停止使用有毒物品作业，或者提请有关人民政府按照国务院规定的权限予以关闭：  
（一）未组织从事使用有毒物品作业的劳动者进行定期职业健康检查的；  
（二）未组织从事使用有毒物品作业的劳动者进行离岗职业健康检查的；  
（三）对未进行离岗职业健康检查的劳动者，解除或者终止与其订立的劳动合同的；  
（四）发生分立、合并、解散、破产情形，未对从事使用有毒物品作业的劳动者进行健康检查，并按照国家有关规定妥善安置职业病病人的；  
（五）对受到或者可能受到急性职业中毒危害的劳动者，未及时组织进行健康检查和医学观察的；  
（六）未建立职业健康监护档案的；  
（七）劳动者离开用人单位时，用人单位未如实、无偿提供职业健康监护档案的；  
（八）未依照职业病防治法和本条例的规定将工作过程中可能产生的职业中毒危害及其后果、有关职业卫生防护措施和待遇等如实告知劳动者并在劳动合同中写明的；  
（九）劳动者在存在威胁生命、健康危险的情况下，从危险现场中撤离，而被取消或者减少应当享有的待遇的。

**Article 67** Where an employer violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the disease prevention and control department shall issue a warning, order rectification within a time limit, and may also impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, it shall order cessation of operations using toxic substances, or submit the matter to the relevant people's government for order of closure within the limits of authority prescribed by the State Council:  
(1) failing to organize periodic occupational health examinations for workers engaged in operations using toxic substances;  
(2) failing to organize exit occupational health examinations for workers engaged in operations using toxic substances;  
(3) rescinding or terminating the labor contract concluded with a worker who has not undergone an exit occupational health examination;  
(4) failing, in cases of division, merger, dissolution, or bankruptcy, to conduct health examinations for workers engaged in operations using toxic substances and to properly arrange for occupational disease patients in accordance with relevant state provisions;  
(5) failing to promptly organize health examinations and medical observation for workers who have suffered or may suffer from acute occupational poisoning hazards;  
(6) failing to establish occupational health surveillance records;  
(7) failing to truthfully and gratuitously provide occupational health surveillance records when a worker leaves the employer;  
(8) failing to truthfully inform workers of the occupational poisoning hazards that may arise in the course of work and their consequences, relevant occupational health protection measures and benefits, and to specify them in the labor contract, in accordance with the Law on the Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases and these Regulations;  
(9) canceling or reducing the benefits that a worker shall enjoy when the worker evacuates from a dangerous scene under circumstances where life or health is threatened.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　用人单位违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由疾病预防控制部门给予警告，责令限期改正，处5000元以上2万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停止使用有毒物品作业，或者提请有关人民政府按照国务院规定的权限予以关闭：  
（一）未按照规定配备或者聘请职业卫生医师和护士的；  
（二）未为从事使用高毒物品作业的劳动者设置淋浴间、更衣室或者未设置清洗、存放和处理工作服、工作鞋帽等物品的专用间，或者不能正常使用的；  
（三）未安排从事使用高毒物品作业一定年限的劳动者进行岗位轮换的。

**Article 68** Where an employer violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the disease prevention and control department shall issue a warning, order rectification within a time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, it shall order cessation of operations using toxic substances, or submit the matter to the relevant people's government for order of closure within the limits of authority prescribed by the State Council:  
(1) failing to provide or engage occupational health physicians and nurses as required;  
(2) failing to equip workers engaged in operations using highly toxic substances with shower rooms, changing rooms, or special rooms for cleaning, storing, and handling work clothes, work shoes and caps, and other items, or such facilities are not in normal use;  
(3) failing to arrange job rotation for workers who have been engaged in operations using highly toxic substances for a certain number of years.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　涉及作业场所使用有毒物品可能产生职业中毒危害的劳动保护的有关事项，本条例未作规定的，依照职业病防治法和其他有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。  
有毒物品的生产、经营、储存、运输、使用和废弃处置的安全管理，依照危险化学品安全管理条例执行。

**Article 69** Where matters concerning labor protection in relation to possible occupational poisoning hazards arising from the use of toxic substances in the workplace are not covered by these Regulations, they shall be carried out in accordance with the Law on the Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases and other relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
The safety management of the production, operation, storage, transportation, use, and disposal of toxic substances shall be carried out in accordance with the Regulations on the Safety Management of Hazardous Chemicals.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 70** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
