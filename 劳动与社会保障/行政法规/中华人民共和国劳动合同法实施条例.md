# 中华人民共和国劳动合同法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2008-09-18  
**生效日期**：2008-09-18

> (2008年9月3日国务院第25次常务会议通过　2008年9月18日中华人民共和国国务院令第535号公布　自公布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了贯彻实施《中华人民共和国劳动合同法》(以下简称劳动合同法)，制定本条例。 第二条　各级人民政府和县级以上人民政府劳动行政等有关部门以及工会等组织，应当采取措施，推动劳动合同法的贯彻实施，促进劳动关系的和谐。 第三条　依法成立的会计师事务所、律师事务所等合伙组织和基金会，属于劳动合同法规定的用人单位。 第二章　劳动合同的订立 第四条　劳动合同法规定的用人单位设立的分支机构，依法取得营业执照或者登记证书的，可以作为用人单位与劳动者订立劳动合同；未依法取得营业执照或者登记证书的，受用人单位委托可以与劳动者订立劳动合同。 第五条　自用工之日起一个月内，经用人单位书面通知后，劳动者不与用人单位订立书面劳动合同的，用人单位应当书面通知劳动者终止劳动关系，无需向劳动者支付经济补偿，但是应当依法向劳动者支付其实际工作时间的劳动报酬。 第六条　用人单位自用工之日起超过一个月不满一年未与劳动者订立书面劳动合同的，应当依照劳动合同法第八十二条的规定向劳动者每月支付两倍的工资，并与劳动者补订书面劳动合同；劳动者不与用人单位订立书面劳动合同的，用人单位应当书面通知劳动者终止劳动关系，并依照劳动合同法第四十七条的规定支付经济补偿。 前款规定的用人单位向劳动者每月支付两倍工资的起算时间为用工之日起满一个月的次日，截止时间为补订书面劳动合同的前一日。 第七条　用人单位自用工之日起满一年未与劳动者订立书面劳动合同的，自用工之日起满一个月的次日至满一年的前一日应当依照劳动合同法第八十二条的规定向劳动者每月支付两倍的工资，并视为自用工之日起满一年的当日已经与劳动者订立无固定期限劳动合同，应当立即与劳动者补订书面劳动合同。 第八条　劳动合同法第七条规定的职工名册，应当包括劳动者姓名、性别、公民身份号码、户籍地址及现住址、联系方式、用工形式、用工起始时间、劳动合同期限等内容。 第九条　劳动合同法第十四条第二款规定的连续工作满10年的起始时间，应当自用人单位用工之日起计算，包括劳动合同法施行前的工作年限。 第十条　劳动者非因本人原因从原用人单位被安排到新用人单位工作的，劳动者在原用人单位的工作年限合并计算为新用人单位的工作年限。原用人单位已经向劳动者支付经济补偿的，新用人单位在依法解除、终止劳动合同计算支付经济补偿的工作年限时，不再计算劳动者在原用人单位的工作年限。 第十一条　除劳动者与用人单位协商一致的情形外，劳动者依照劳动合同法第十四条第二款的规定，提出订立无固定期限劳动合同的，用人单位应当与其订立无固定期限劳动合同。对劳动合同的内容，双方应当按照合法、公平、平等自愿、协商一致、诚实信用的原则协商确定；对协商不一致的内容，依照劳动合同法第十八条的规定执行。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了贯彻实施《中华人民共和国劳动合同法》(以下简称劳动合同法)，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to implement the Labor Contract Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Labor Contract Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　各级人民政府和县级以上人民政府劳动行政等有关部门以及工会等组织，应当采取措施，推动劳动合同法的贯彻实施，促进劳动关系的和谐。

**Article 2** The people's governments at all levels, the relevant departments such as the labor administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, and organizations such as trade unions shall take measures to promote the implementation of the Labor Contract Law and foster harmonious labor relations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　依法成立的会计师事务所、律师事务所等合伙组织和基金会，属于劳动合同法规定的用人单位。

**Article 3** Partnerships such as accounting firms and law firms, as well as foundations, established according to law shall be considered as employing units as stipulated in the Labor Contract Law.

### 第二章　劳动合同的订立

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　劳动合同法规定的用人单位设立的分支机构，依法取得营业执照或者登记证书的，可以作为用人单位与劳动者订立劳动合同；未依法取得营业执照或者登记证书的，受用人单位委托可以与劳动者订立劳动合同。

**Article 4** A branch established by an employing unit as stipulated in the Labor Contract Law, which has obtained a business license or registration certificate according to law, may conclude a labor contract with a worker as an employing unit; if it has not obtained a business license or registration certificate according to law, it may conclude a labor contract with a worker on the entrustment of the employing unit.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　自用工之日起一个月内，经用人单位书面通知后，劳动者不与用人单位订立书面劳动合同的，用人单位应当书面通知劳动者终止劳动关系，无需向劳动者支付经济补偿，但是应当依法向劳动者支付其实际工作时间的劳动报酬。

**Article 5** Where, within one month from the date of commencement of employment, after being notified in writing by the employing unit, a worker fails to conclude a written labor contract with the employing unit, the employing unit shall notify the worker in writing of the termination of the labor relationship, and shall not be required to pay economic compensation to the worker, but shall pay the worker labor remuneration for the period actually worked according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　用人单位自用工之日起超过一个月不满一年未与劳动者订立书面劳动合同的，应当依照[劳动合同法第八十二条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-82)的规定向劳动者每月支付两倍的工资，并与劳动者补订书面劳动合同；劳动者不与用人单位订立书面劳动合同的，用人单位应当书面通知劳动者终止劳动关系，并依照[劳动合同法第四十七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-47)的规定支付经济补偿。  
前款规定的用人单位向劳动者每月支付两倍工资的起算时间为用工之日起满一个月的次日，截止时间为补订书面劳动合同的前一日。

**Article 6** Where an employing unit fails to conclude a written labor contract with a worker for more than one month but less than one year from the date of commencement of employment, it shall pay the worker twice the monthly wage in accordance with Article 82 of the Labor Contract Law and shall subsequently conclude a written labor contract with the worker; if the worker fails to conclude a written labor contract with the employing unit, the employing unit shall notify the worker in writing of the termination of the labor relationship and pay economic compensation in accordance with Article 47 of the Labor Contract Law.  
The starting date for the calculation of the employing unit paying the worker double wages each month as stipulated in the preceding paragraph shall be the day following the date one month from the commencement of employment, and the ending date shall be the day preceding the date of subsequently concluding the written labor contract.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　用人单位自用工之日起满一年未与劳动者订立书面劳动合同的，自用工之日起满一个月的次日至满一年的前一日应当依照[劳动合同法第八十二条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-82)的规定向劳动者每月支付两倍的工资，并视为自用工之日起满一年的当日已经与劳动者订立无固定期限劳动合同，应当立即与劳动者补订书面劳动合同。

**Article 7** Where an employing unit fails to conclude a written labor contract with a worker for one full year from the date of commencement of employment, it shall pay the worker twice the monthly wage each month from the day following the date one month from the commencement of employment to the day preceding the date of one full year in accordance with Article 82 of the Labor Contract Law, and it shall be deemed that an open-ended labor contract has been concluded with the worker on the date of one full year from the commencement of employment, and the employing unit shall immediately subsequently conclude a written labor contract with the worker.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　[劳动合同法第七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-7)规定的职工名册，应当包括劳动者姓名、性别、公民身份号码、户籍地址及现住址、联系方式、用工形式、用工起始时间、劳动合同期限等内容。

**Article 8** The employee roster as stipulated in Article 7 of the Labor Contract Law shall include the worker's name, gender, citizen identity number, registered domicile address and current address, contact information, form of employment, date of commencement of employment, term of the labor contract, and other such information.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　[劳动合同法第十四条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-14)第二款规定的连续工作满10年的起始时间，应当自用人单位用工之日起计算，包括劳动合同法施行前的工作年限。

**Article 9** The starting date for the calculation of continuous employment for a full period of 10 years as stipulated in paragraph 2 of Article 14 of the Labor Contract Law shall be calculated from the date of commencement of employment by the employing unit, and shall include the length of service prior to the implementation of the Labor Contract Law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　劳动者非因本人原因从原用人单位被安排到新用人单位工作的，劳动者在原用人单位的工作年限合并计算为新用人单位的工作年限。原用人单位已经向劳动者支付经济补偿的，新用人单位在依法解除、终止劳动合同计算支付经济补偿的工作年限时，不再计算劳动者在原用人单位的工作年限。

**Article 10** Where a worker is assigned to work for a new employing unit from the original employing unit not due to reasons attributable to the worker himself, the length of service of the worker with the original employing unit shall be aggregated into the length of service with the new employing unit. If the original employing unit has already paid economic compensation to the worker, when the new employing unit calculates the length of service for the payment of economic compensation upon lawfully dissolving or terminating the labor contract, the length of service of the worker with the original employing unit shall no longer be calculated.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　除劳动者与用人单位协商一致的情形外，劳动者依照[劳动合同法第十四条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-14)第二款的规定，提出订立无固定期限劳动合同的，用人单位应当与其订立无固定期限劳动合同。对劳动合同的内容，双方应当按照合法、公平、平等自愿、协商一致、诚实信用的原则协商确定；对协商不一致的内容，依照[劳动合同法第十八条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-18)的规定执行。

**Article 11** Except where the worker and the employing unit have reached a consensus, if a worker proposes to conclude an open-ended labor contract in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 14 of the Labor Contract Law, the employing unit shall conclude an open-ended labor contract with him. The content of the labor contract shall be negotiated and determined by both parties in accordance with the principles of legality, fairness, equality, voluntariness, consensus, honesty, and good faith; for matters on which no consensus is reached through negotiation, the provisions of Article 18 of the Labor Contract Law shall apply.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　地方各级人民政府及县级以上地方人民政府有关部门为安置就业困难人员提供的给予岗位补贴和社会保险补贴的公益性岗位，其劳动合同不适用劳动合同法有关无固定期限劳动合同的规定以及支付经济补偿的规定。

**Article 12** For public welfare positions provided by local people's governments at all levels and relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level to place persons with employment difficulties, which offer job subsidies and social insurance subsidies, the labor contracts therefor shall not be subject to the provisions of the Labor Contract Law concerning open-ended labor contracts and the provisions concerning the payment of economic compensation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　用人单位与劳动者不得在[劳动合同法第四十四条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-44)规定的劳动合同终止情形之外约定其他的劳动合同终止条件。

**Article 13** An employing unit and a worker shall not agree on any other conditions for the termination of a labor contract beyond the circumstances for the termination of a labor contract as stipulated in Article 44 of the Labor Contract Law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　劳动合同履行地与用人单位注册地不一致的，有关劳动者的最低工资标准、劳动保护、劳动条件、职业危害防护和本地区上年度职工月平均工资标准等事项，按照劳动合同履行地的有关规定执行；用人单位注册地的有关标准高于劳动合同履行地的有关标准，且用人单位与劳动者约定按照用人单位注册地的有关规定执行的，从其约定。

**Article 14** Where the place of performance of the labor contract differs from the place of registration of the employing unit, matters such as the minimum wage standard, labor protection, working conditions, occupational hazard protection, and the local average monthly wage of employees for the previous year shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant regulations of the place of performance of the labor contract; if the relevant standards of the place of registration of the employing unit are higher than those of the place of performance of the labor contract, and the employing unit and the worker have agreed to implement the relevant regulations of the place of registration of the employing unit, such agreement shall prevail.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　劳动者在试用期的工资不得低于本单位相同岗位最低档工资的80%或者不得低于劳动合同约定工资的80%，并不得低于用人单位所在地的最低工资标准。

**Article 15** The wage of a worker during the probation period shall not be less than 80% of the lowest-level wage for the same position in the unit, or shall not be less than 80% of the wage agreed in the labor contract, and shall not be less than the minimum wage standard of the locality of the employing unit.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　[劳动合同法第二十二条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-22)第二款规定的培训费用，包括用人单位为了对劳动者进行专业技术培训而支付的有凭证的培训费用、培训期间的差旅费用以及因培训产生的用于该劳动者的其他直接费用。

**Article 16** The training expenses as stipulated in paragraph 2 of Article 22 of the Labor Contract Law include the documented training fees paid by the employing unit for the professional technical training of the worker, the travel expenses during the training period, and other direct expenses incurred for the worker as a result of the training.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　劳动合同期满，但是用人单位与劳动者依照[劳动合同法第二十二条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-22)的规定约定的服务期尚未到期的，劳动合同应当续延至服务期满；双方另有约定的，从其约定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/司法解释/最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（二）.md#art-8" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（二）》第8条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 17** Where a labor contract expires but the service period agreed upon by the employing unit and the worker in accordance with Article 22 of the Labor Contract Law has not yet expired, the labor contract shall be extended until the expiration of the service period; if the parties have otherwise agreed, such agreement shall prevail.

### 第三章　劳动合同的解除和终止

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　有下列情形之一的，依照劳动合同法规定的条件、程序，劳动者可以与用人单位解除固定期限劳动合同、无固定期限劳动合同或者以完成一定工作任务为期限的劳动合同：  
(一)劳动者与用人单位协商一致的；  
(二)劳动者提前30日以书面形式通知用人单位的；  
(三)劳动者在试用期内提前3日通知用人单位的；  
(四)用人单位未按照劳动合同约定提供劳动保护或者劳动条件的；  
(五)用人单位未及时足额支付劳动报酬的；  
(六)用人单位未依法为劳动者缴纳社会保险费的；  
(七)用人单位的规章制度违反法律、法规的规定，损害劳动者权益的；  
(八)用人单位以欺诈、胁迫的手段或者乘人之危，使劳动者在违背真实意思的情况下订立或者变更劳动合同的；  
(九)用人单位在劳动合同中免除自己的法定责任、排除劳动者权利的；  
(十)用人单位违反法律、行政法规强制性规定的；  
(十一)用人单位以暴力、威胁或者非法限制人身自由的手段强迫劳动者劳动的；  
(十二)用人单位违章指挥、强令冒险作业危及劳动者人身安全的；  
(十三)法律、行政法规规定劳动者可以解除劳动合同的其他情形。

**Article 18** Under any of the following circumstances, in accordance with the conditions and procedures stipulated in the Labor Contract Law, a worker may dissolve a fixed-term labor contract, an open-ended labor contract, or a labor contract that concludes upon the completion of a specific work task:  
(1) The worker and the employing unit have reached a consensus through negotiation;  
(2) The worker has notified the employing unit in writing 30 days in advance;  
(3) The worker has notified the employing unit 3 days in advance during the probation period;  
(4) The employing unit has failed to provide labor protection or working conditions as agreed in the labor contract;  
(5) The employing unit has failed to pay labor remuneration in full and on time;  
(6) The employing unit has failed to pay social insurance premiums for the worker according to law;  
(7) The internal rules and regulations of the employing unit violate the provisions of laws or regulations, thereby harming the worker's rights and interests;  
(8) The employing unit has used fraud, coercion, or taking advantage of the worker's difficulties to cause the worker to conclude or modify the labor contract contrary to his true intention;  
(9) The employing unit has exempted itself from legal liabilities or excluded the worker's rights in the labor contract;  
(10) The employing unit has violated mandatory provisions of laws or administrative regulations;  
(11) The employing unit has forced the worker to work through violence, threats, or illegal restriction of personal freedom;  
(12) The employing unit has given commands in violation of regulations or forced risky operations endangering the worker's personal safety;  
(13) Other circumstances stipulated by laws or administrative regulations under which a worker may dissolve the labor contract.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　有下列情形之一的，依照劳动合同法规定的条件、程序，用人单位可以与劳动者解除固定期限劳动合同、无固定期限劳动合同或者以完成一定工作任务为期限的劳动合同：  
(一)用人单位与劳动者协商一致的；  
(二)劳动者在试用期间被证明不符合录用条件的；  
(三)劳动者严重违反用人单位的规章制度的；  
(四)劳动者严重失职，营私舞弊，给用人单位造成重大损害的；  
(五)劳动者同时与其他用人单位建立劳动关系，对完成本单位的工作任务造成严重影响，或者经用人单位提出，拒不改正的；  
(六)劳动者以欺诈、胁迫的手段或者乘人之危，使用人单位在违背真实意思的情况下订立或者变更劳动合同的；  
(七)劳动者被依法追究刑事责任的；  
(八)劳动者患病或者非因工负伤，在规定的医疗期满后不能从事原工作，也不能从事由用人单位另行安排的工作的；  
(九)劳动者不能胜任工作，经过培训或者调整工作岗位，仍不能胜任工作的；  
(十)劳动合同订立时所依据的客观情况发生重大变化，致使劳动合同无法履行，经用人单位与劳动者协商，未能就变更劳动合同内容达成协议的；  
(十一)用人单位依照企业破产法规定进行重整的；  
(十二)用人单位生产经营发生严重困难的；  
(十三)企业转产、重大技术革新或者经营方式调整，经变更劳动合同后，仍需裁减人员的；  
(十四)其他因劳动合同订立时所依据的客观经济情况发生重大变化，致使劳动合同无法履行的。

**Article 19** Under any of the following circumstances, in accordance with the conditions and procedures stipulated in the Labor Contract Law, an employing unit may dissolve a fixed-term labor contract, an open-ended labor contract, or a labor contract that concludes upon the completion of a specific work task:  
(1) The employing unit and the worker have reached a consensus through negotiation;  
(2) The worker is proven during the probation period not to meet the recruitment conditions;  
(3) The worker has seriously violated the internal rules and regulations of the employing unit;  
(4) The worker has been seriously derelict in duty or engaged in embezzlement, causing substantial damage to the employing unit;  
(5) The worker has concurrently established a labor relationship with another employing unit, which has a serious impact on the completion of the work tasks of the employing unit, or the worker, having been called upon by the employing unit, refuses to correct such conduct;  
(6) The worker has used fraud, coercion, or taking advantage of the employing unit's difficulties to cause the employing unit to conclude or modify the labor contract contrary to its true intention;  
(7) The worker has been investigated for criminal liability according to law;  
(8) The worker has fallen ill or sustained a non-work-related injury, and after the prescribed medical treatment period has expired, is unable to engage in the original work or any other work arranged by the employing unit;  
(9) The worker is incompetent for the job and remains incompetent after training or adjustment of the job position;  
(10) The objective circumstances based on which the labor contract was concluded have undergone a major change, rendering the labor contract unenforceable, and the employing unit and the worker have failed to reach an agreement on modifying the contract content through negotiation;  
(11) The employing unit is undergoing reorganization in accordance with the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law;  
(12) The employing unit is encountering serious difficulties in its production and operations;  
(13) The enterprise is undergoing a change of production, major technological innovation, or adjustment of its business model, and after modifying the labor contract, still needs to reduce its workforce;  
(14) Other situations where the objective economic circumstances based on which the labor contract was concluded have undergone a major change, rendering the labor contract unenforceable.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　用人单位依照[劳动合同法第四十条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-40)的规定，选择额外支付劳动者一个月工资解除劳动合同的，其额外支付的工资应当按照该劳动者上一个月的工资标准确定。

**Article 20** Where an employing unit, in accordance with Article 40 of the Labor Contract Law, chooses to dissolve the labor contract by paying the worker an additional month's wage, the amount of the additional wage paid shall be determined according to the wage standard of the worker for the preceding month.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　劳动者达到法定退休年龄的，劳动合同终止。

**Article 21** Where a worker has reached the statutory retirement age, the labor contract shall terminate.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　以完成一定工作任务为期限的劳动合同因任务完成而终止的，用人单位应当依照[劳动合同法第四十七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-47)的规定向劳动者支付经济补偿。

**Article 22** Where a labor contract that concludes upon the completion of a specific work task terminates due to the completion of the task, the employing unit shall pay economic compensation to the worker in accordance with Article 47 of the Labor Contract Law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　用人单位依法终止工伤职工的劳动合同的，除依照[劳动合同法第四十七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-47)的规定支付经济补偿外，还应当依照国家有关工伤保险的规定支付一次性工伤医疗补助金和伤残就业补助金。

**Article 23** Where an employing unit lawfully terminates the labor contract of a worker suffering from a work-related injury, in addition to paying economic compensation in accordance with Article 47 of the Labor Contract Law, it shall also pay a one-time medical subsidy for work-related injury and a one-time disability employment subsidy in accordance with the relevant state provisions on work-related injury insurance.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　用人单位出具的解除、终止劳动合同的证明，应当写明劳动合同期限、解除或者终止劳动合同的日期、工作岗位、在本单位的工作年限。

**Article 24** A certificate of dissolution or termination of a labor contract issued by an employing unit shall specify the term of the labor contract, the date of the dissolution or termination of the labor contract, the job position, and the length of service within the unit.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　用人单位违反劳动合同法的规定解除或者终止劳动合同，依照[劳动合同法第八十七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-87)的规定支付了赔偿金的，不再支付经济补偿。赔偿金的计算年限自用工之日起计算。

**Article 25** Where an employing unit dissolves or terminates a labor contract in violation of the Labor Contract Law and has paid compensation in accordance with Article 87 of the Labor Contract Law, economic compensation shall no longer be paid. The period for calculating the compensation shall be calculated from the date of commencement of employment.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　用人单位与劳动者约定了服务期，劳动者依照[劳动合同法第三十八条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-38)的规定解除劳动合同的，不属于违反服务期的约定，用人单位不得要求劳动者支付违约金。  
有下列情形之一，用人单位与劳动者解除约定服务期的劳动合同的，劳动者应当按照劳动合同的约定向用人单位支付违约金：  
(一)劳动者严重违反用人单位的规章制度的；  
(二)劳动者严重失职，营私舞弊，给用人单位造成重大损害的；  
(三)劳动者同时与其他用人单位建立劳动关系，对完成本单位的工作任务造成严重影响，或者经用人单位提出，拒不改正的；  
(四)劳动者以欺诈、胁迫的手段或者乘人之危，使用人单位在违背真实意思的情况下订立或者变更劳动合同的；  
(五)劳动者被依法追究刑事责任的。

**Article 26** Where an employing unit and a worker have agreed on a service period, and the worker dissolves the labor contract in accordance with Article 38 of the Labor Contract Law, this shall not constitute a breach of the agreement on the service period, and the employing unit may not demand payment of liquidated damages from the worker.  
Under any of the following circumstances, where the employing unit dissolves the labor contract containing an agreed service period with the worker, the worker shall pay liquidated damages to the employing unit in accordance with the provisions of the labor contract:  
(1) The worker has seriously violated the internal rules and regulations of the employing unit;  
(2) The worker has been seriously derelict in duty or engaged in embezzlement, causing substantial damage to the employing unit;  
(3) The worker has concurrently established a labor relationship with another employing unit, which has a serious impact on the completion of the work tasks of the employing unit, or the worker, having been called upon by the employing unit, refuses to correct such conduct;  
(4) The worker has used fraud, coercion, or taking advantage of the employing unit's difficulties to cause the employing unit to conclude or modify the labor contract contrary to its true intention;  
(5) The worker has been investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　[劳动合同法第四十七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-47)规定的经济补偿的月工资按照劳动者应得工资计算，包括计时工资或者计件工资以及奖金、津贴和补贴等货币性收入。劳动者在劳动合同解除或者终止前12个月的平均工资低于当地最低工资标准的，按照当地最低工资标准计算。劳动者工作不满12个月的，按照实际工作的月数计算平均工资。

**Article 27** The monthly wage for calculating economic compensation as stipulated in Article 47 of the Labor Contract Law shall be calculated based on the wages the worker is entitled to receive, including time-rate wages or piece-rate wages, as well as monetary income such as bonuses, allowances, and subsidies. If the worker's average wage in the 12 months prior to the dissolution or termination of the labor contract is lower than the local minimum wage standard, it shall be calculated based on the local minimum wage standard. If the worker has worked for less than 12 months, the average wage shall be calculated based on the actual number of months worked.

### 第四章　劳务派遣特别规定

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　用人单位或者其所属单位出资或者合伙设立的劳务派遣单位，向本单位或者所属单位派遣劳动者的，属于[劳动合同法第六十七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-67)规定的不得设立的劳务派遣单位。

**Article 28** Where a labor dispatch unit is established through investment or partnership formed by an employing unit or its subordinate unit, and dispatches workers to that employing unit or its subordinate unit, such a labor dispatch unit shall be considered one that may not be established as stipulated in Article 67 of the Labor Contract Law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　用工单位应当履行[劳动合同法第六十二条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-62)规定的义务，维护被派遣劳动者的合法权益。

**Article 29** The user unit shall fulfill the obligations stipulated in Article 62 of the Labor Contract Law and protect the legitimate rights and interests of the dispatched workers.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　劳务派遣单位不得以非全日制用工形式招用被派遣劳动者。

**Article 30** A labor dispatch unit may not recruit dispatched workers in the form of part-time employment.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　劳务派遣单位或者被派遣劳动者依法解除、终止劳动合同的经济补偿，依照[劳动合同法第四十六条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-46)、第四十七条的规定执行。

**Article 31** Economic compensation for the lawful dissolution or termination of a labor contract by a labor dispatch unit or a dispatched worker shall be implemented in accordance with Articles 46 and 47 of the Labor Contract Law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　劳务派遣单位违法解除或者终止被派遣劳动者的劳动合同的，依照[劳动合同法第四十八条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-48)的规定执行。

**Article 32** Where a labor dispatch unit unlawfully dissolves or terminates the labor contract of a dispatched worker, the provisions of Article 48 of the Labor Contract Law shall apply.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　用人单位违反劳动合同法有关建立职工名册规定的，由劳动行政部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，由劳动行政部门处2000元以上2万元以下的罚款。

**Article 33** Where an employing unit violates the provisions of the Labor Contract Law on establishing an employee roster, the labor administrative department shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the labor administrative department shall impose a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　用人单位依照劳动合同法的规定应当向劳动者每月支付两倍的工资或者应当向劳动者支付赔偿金而未支付的，劳动行政部门应当责令用人单位支付。

**Article 34** Where an employing unit should pay a worker double wages each month or pay compensation to a worker in accordance with the provisions of the Labor Contract Law but fails to do so, the labor administrative department shall order the employing unit to make such payment.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　用工单位违反劳动合同法和本条例有关劳务派遣规定的，由劳动行政部门和其他有关主管部门责令改正；情节严重的，以每位被派遣劳动者1000元以上5000元以下的标准处以罚款；给被派遣劳动者造成损害的，劳务派遣单位和用工单位承担连带赔偿责任。

**Article 35** Where a user unit violates the provisions of the Labor Contract Law and these Regulations on labor dispatch, the labor administrative department and other relevant competent authorities shall order it to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, a fine shall be imposed at a standard of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan for each dispatched worker; if damage is caused to the dispatched workers, the labor dispatch unit and the user unit shall bear joint and several liability for compensation.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　对违反劳动合同法和本条例的行为的投诉、举报，县级以上地方人民政府劳动行政部门依照《劳动保障监察条例》的规定处理。

**Article 36** Complaints and reports of acts violating the Labor Contract Law and these Regulations shall be handled by the labor administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Labor Security Supervision.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　劳动者与用人单位因订立、履行、变更、解除或者终止劳动合同发生争议的，依照《中华人民共和国劳动争议调解仲裁法》的规定处理。

**Article 37** Disputes arising between a worker and an employing unit over the conclusion, performance, modification, dissolution or termination of a labor contract shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Mediation and Arbitration of Labor Disputes.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 38** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
