# 森林和野生动物类型自然保护区管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：1985-07-06  
**生效日期**：1985-07-06

> (1985年6月21日国务院批准　1985年7月6日林业部公布　自公布之日起施行) 第一条　自然保护区是保护自然环境和自然资源、拯救濒于灭绝的生物物种、进行科学研究的重要基地；对促进科学技术、生产建设、文化教育、卫生保健等事业的发展，具有重要意义。根据《中华人民共和国森林法》和有关规定，制定本办法。 第二条　森林和野生动物类型自然保护区(以下简称自然保护区)，按照本办法进行管理。 第三条　自然保护区管理机构的主要任务：贯彻执行国家有关自然保护区的方针、政策和规定，加强管理，开展宣传教育，保护和发展珍贵稀有野生动植物资源，进行科学研究，探索自然演变规律和合理利用森林和动植物资源的途径，为社会主义建设服务。 第四条　自然保护区分为国家自然保护区和地方自然保护区。国家自然保护区，由林业部或所在省、自治区、直辖市林业主管部门管理；地方自然保护区，由县级以上林业主管部门管理。 第五条　具有下列条件之一者，可以建立自然保护区： (一)不同自然地带的典型森林生态系统的地区。 (二)珍贵稀有或者有特殊保护价值的动植物种的主要生存繁殖地区，包括： 国家重点保护动物的主要栖息、繁殖地区； 候鸟的主要繁殖地、越冬地和停歇地； 珍贵树种和有特殊价值的植物原生地； 野生生物模式标本的集中产地。 (三)其他有特殊保护价值的林区。 第六条　根据本办法第五条规定建立自然保护区，在科研上有重要价值，或者在国际上有一定影响的，报国务院批准，列为国家自然保护区；其他自然保护区，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，列为地方自然保护区。 第七条　建立自然保护区要注意保护对象的完整性和最适宜的范围，考虑当地经济建设和群众生产生活的需要，尽可能避开群众的土地、山林；确实不能避开的，应当严格控制范围，并根据国家有关规定，合理解决群众的生产生活问题。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　自然保护区是保护自然环境和自然资源、拯救濒于灭绝的生物物种、进行科学研究的重要基地；对促进科学技术、生产建设、文化教育、卫生保健等事业的发展，具有重要意义。根据《中华人民共和国森林法》和有关规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** Nature reserves are important bases for protecting the natural environment and natural resources, rescuing endangered biological species, and conducting scientific research; they are of great significance for promoting the development of science and technology, production and construction, cultural education, health care, and other undertakings. These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Forest Law of the People's Republic of China and relevant provisions.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　森林和野生动物类型自然保护区(以下简称自然保护区)，按照本办法进行管理。

**Article 2** Forest and wildlife type nature reserves (hereinafter referred to as nature reserves) shall be administered in accordance with these Measures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　自然保护区管理机构的主要任务：贯彻执行国家有关自然保护区的方针、政策和规定，加强管理，开展宣传教育，保护和发展珍贵稀有野生动植物资源，进行科学研究，探索自然演变规律和合理利用森林和动植物资源的途径，为社会主义建设服务。

**Article 3** The main tasks of the administrative organs of nature reserves are: to implement the national principles, policies, and regulations concerning nature reserves; to strengthen administration; to carry out publicity and education; to protect and develop precious and rare wild animals and plants; to conduct scientific research; to explore the laws of natural evolution and the ways of rationally utilizing forest and animal and plant resources; and to serve socialist construction.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　自然保护区分为国家自然保护区和地方自然保护区。国家自然保护区，由林业部或所在省、自治区、直辖市林业主管部门管理；地方自然保护区，由县级以上林业主管部门管理。

**Article 4** Nature reserves are divided into national nature reserves and local nature reserves. National nature reserves shall be administered by the Ministry of Forestry or the competent forestry departments of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government; local nature reserves shall be administered by the competent forestry departments at or above the county level.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　具有下列条件之一者，可以建立自然保护区：  
(一)不同自然地带的典型森林生态系统的地区。  
(二)珍贵稀有或者有特殊保护价值的动植物种的主要生存繁殖地区，包括：  
国家重点保护动物的主要栖息、繁殖地区；  
候鸟的主要繁殖地、越冬地和停歇地；  
珍贵树种和有特殊价值的植物原生地；  
野生生物模式标本的集中产地。  
(三)其他有特殊保护价值的林区。

**Article 5** Nature reserves may be established in any of the following areas:  
(1) Areas with typical forest ecosystems in different natural zones.  
(2) Main survival and reproduction areas of precious, rare, or specially protected animal and plant species, including:  
Main habitats and breeding areas of animals under state key protection;  
Main breeding, wintering, and stopover sites of migratory birds;  
Native places of precious tree species and plants of special value;  
Concentrated production areas of wild biological type specimens.  
(3) Other forest areas with special protection value.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　根据[本办法第五条](#art-5)规定建立自然保护区，在科研上有重要价值，或者在国际上有一定影响的，报国务院批准，列为国家自然保护区；其他自然保护区，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，列为地方自然保护区。

**Article 6** Nature reserves established according to the provisions of Article 5 of these Measures that have important scientific research value or a certain international influence shall be submitted to the State Council for approval and designated as national nature reserves; other nature reserves shall be submitted to the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government for approval and designated as local nature reserves.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　建立自然保护区要注意保护对象的完整性和最适宜的范围，考虑当地经济建设和群众生产生活的需要，尽可能避开群众的土地、山林；确实不能避开的，应当严格控制范围，并根据国家有关规定，合理解决群众的生产生活问题。

**Article 7** When establishing a nature reserve, attention shall be paid to the integrity and most suitable scope of the protected objects, consideration shall be given to the needs of local economic construction and the production and daily life of the masses, and the land and forests of the masses shall be avoided as far as possible; if it is truly unavoidable, the scope shall be strictly controlled, and the production and daily life problems of the masses shall be reasonably solved according to the relevant national provisions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　自然保护区的解除和范围的调整，必须经原审批机关批准；未经批准不得改变自然保护区的性质和范围。

**Article 8** The dissolution or adjustment of the scope of a nature reserve must be approved by the original approving authority; without approval, the nature and scope of the nature reserve shall not be changed.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　自然保护区的管理机构属于事业单位。机构的设置和人员的配备，要注意精干。国家或地方自然保护区管理机构的人员编制、基建投资、事业经费等，经主管部门批准后，分别纳入国家和省、自治区、直辖市的计划，由林业部门统一安排。

**Article 9** The administrative organs of nature reserves are public institutions. The establishment of organs and the allocation of personnel shall pay attention to being compact and efficient. The staffing, capital construction investment, and operating expenses of the administrative organs of national or local nature reserves shall, after being approved by the competent department, be incorporated respectively into the plans of the state and the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, and shall be uniformly arranged by the forestry departments.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　自然保护区管理机构，可以根据自然资源情况，将自然保护区分为核心区、实验区。核心区只供进行观测研究。实验区可以进行科学实验、教学实习、参观考察和驯化培育珍稀动植物等活动。

**Article 10** The administrative organ of a nature reserve may, according to the conditions of natural resources, divide the nature reserve into core zones and experimental zones. The core zones shall be used only for observation and research. In the experimental zones, activities such as scientific experiments, teaching internships, visits and inspections, and domestication and cultivation of rare animals and plants may be conducted.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　自然保护区的自然环境和自然资源，由自然保护区管理机构统一管理。未经林业部或省、自治区、直辖市林业主管部门批准，任何单位和个人不得进入自然保护区建立机构和修筑设施。

**Article 11** The natural environment and natural resources of a nature reserve shall be uniformly managed by the administrative organ of the nature reserve. Without the approval of the Ministry of Forestry or the competent forestry department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, no unit or individual may enter the nature reserve to establish institutions or construct facilities.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　有条件的自然保护区，经林业部或省、自治区、直辖市林业主管部门批准，可以在指定的范围内开展旅游活动。  
在自然保护区开展旅游必须遵守以下规定：  
(一)旅游业务由自然保护区管理机构统一管理，所得收入用于自然保护区的建设和保护事业；  
(二)有关部门投资或与自然保护区联合兴办的旅游建筑和设施，产权归自然保护区，所得收益在一定时期内按比例分成，但不得改变自然保护区隶属关系；  
(三)对旅游区必须进行规划设计，确定合适的旅游点和旅游路线；  
(四)旅游点的建筑和设施要体现民族风格，同自然景观和谐一致；  
(五)根据旅游需要和接待条件制订年度接待计划，按隶属关系报林业主管部门批准，有组织地开展旅游；  
(六)设置防火、卫生等设施，实行严格的巡护检查，防止造成环境污染和自然资源的破坏。

**Article 12** Qualified nature reserves may, with the approval of the Ministry of Forestry or the competent forestry department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, conduct tourism activities within designated areas.  
The following provisions must be observed when conducting tourism in a nature reserve:  
(1) Tourism business shall be uniformly managed by the administrative organ of the nature reserve, and the income derived shall be used for the construction and protection of the nature reserve;  
(2) The property rights of tourism buildings and facilities invested in by relevant departments or jointly established with the nature reserve shall belong to the nature reserve, and the income derived shall be divided in proportion within a certain period, but the subordination relationship of the nature reserve shall not be changed;  
(3) The tourism area must be planned and designed, and appropriate tourist spots and routes shall be determined;  
(4) The buildings and facilities at tourist spots shall reflect national style and be in harmony with the natural landscape;  
(5) Annual reception plans shall be formulated according to tourism needs and reception conditions, submitted to the competent forestry department for approval according to the subordination relationship, and tourism shall be conducted in an organized manner;  
(6) Fire prevention, sanitation, and other facilities shall be installed, strict patrol and inspection shall be implemented to prevent environmental pollution and damage to natural resources.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　进入自然保护区从事科学研究、教学实习、参观考察、拍摄影片、登山等活动的单位和个人，必须经省、自治区、直辖市以上林业主管部门的同意。  
任何部门、团体、单位与国外签署涉及国家自然保护区的协议，接待外国人到国家自然保护区从事有关活动，必须征得林业部的同意；涉及地方自然保护区的，必须征得省、自治区、直辖市林业主管部门的同意。  
经批准进入自然保护区从事上述活动的，必须遵守本办法和有关规定，并交纳保护管理费。

**Article 13** Units and individuals that enter a nature reserve to engage in activities such as scientific research, teaching internships, visits and inspections, film shooting, and mountaineering must obtain the consent of the competent forestry department at or above the provincial level.  
Any department, organization, or unit that signs an agreement with a foreign party involving a national nature reserve, or receives foreigners to engage in relevant activities in a national nature reserve, must obtain the consent of the Ministry of Forestry; if a local nature reserve is involved, the consent of the competent forestry department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government must be obtained.  
Those that have been approved to enter a nature reserve to engage in the above activities must comply with these Measures and relevant provisions, and pay protection and management fees.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　自然保护区内的居民，应当遵守自然保护区的有关规定，固定生产生活活动范围，在不破坏自然资源的前提下，从事种植、养殖业，也可以承包自然保护区组织的劳务或保护管理任务，以增加经济收入。

**Article 14** Residents within a nature reserve shall comply with the relevant provisions of the nature reserve, fix the scope of their production and daily life activities, engage in planting and breeding industries on the premise of not destroying natural resources, and may also contract for labor or protection and management tasks organized by the nature reserve to increase their economic income.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　自然保护区管理机构会同所在和毗邻的县、乡人民政府及有关单位，组成自然保护区联合保护委员会，制订保护公约，共同做好保护管理工作。

**Article 15** The administrative organ of a nature reserve shall, together with the people's governments of the county and township where it is located and the adjacent ones, and relevant units, form a joint protection committee for the nature reserve, formulate a protection pact, and jointly do a good job in protection and management.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　根据国家有关规定和需要，可以在自然保护区设立公安机构或者配备公安特派员，行政上受自然保护区管理机构领导，业务上受上级公安机关领导。  
自然保护区公安机构的主要任务：保护自然保护区的自然资源和国家财产，维护当地社会治安，依法查处破坏自然保护区的案件。

**Article 16** According to relevant national provisions and needs, a public security organ may be established or public security commissioners may be assigned in a nature reserve, administratively under the leadership of the administrative organ of the nature reserve, and professionally under the leadership of the superior public security organ.  
The main tasks of the public security organ of a nature reserve are: to protect the natural resources and state property of the nature reserve, to maintain local public security, and to investigate and deal with cases of damage to the nature reserve according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本办法自发布之日起施行。

**Article 17** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
