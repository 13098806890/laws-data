# 职工带薪年休假条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2007-12-14  
**生效日期**：2008-01-01

> (2007年12月7日国务院第198次常务会议通过　2007年12月14日中华人民共和国国务院令第514号公布　自2008年1月1日起施行) 第一条　为了维护职工休息休假权利，调动职工工作积极性，根据劳动法和公务员法，制定本条例。 第二条　机关、团体、企业、事业单位、民办非企业单位、有雇工的个体工商户等单位的职工连续工作1年以上的，享受带薪年休假(以下简称年休假)。单位应当保证职工享受年休假。 职工在年休假期间享受与正常工作期间相同的工资收入。 第三条　职工累计工作已满1年不满10年的，年休假5天；已满10年不满20年的，年休假10天；已满20年的，年休假15天。 国家法定休假日、休息日不计入年休假的假期。 第四条　职工有下列情形之一的，不享受当年的年休假： (一)职工依法享受寒暑假，其休假天数多于年休假天数的； (二)职工请事假累计20天以上且单位按照规定不扣工资的； (三)累计工作满1年不满10年的职工，请病假累计2个月以上的； (四)累计工作满10年不满20年的职工，请病假累计3个月以上的； (五)累计工作满20年以上的职工，请病假累计4个月以上的。 第五条　单位根据生产、工作的具体情况，并考虑职工本人意愿，统筹安排职工年休假。 年休假在1个年度内可以集中安排，也可以分段安排，一般不跨年度安排。单位因生产、工作特点确有必要跨年度安排职工年休假的，可以跨1个年度安排。 单位确因工作需要不能安排职工休年休假的，经职工本人同意，可以不安排职工休年休假。对职工应休未休的年休假天数，单位应当按照该职工日工资收入的300%支付年休假工资报酬。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护职工休息休假权利，调动职工工作积极性，根据劳动法和公务员法，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Labor Law and the Civil Servants Law for the purpose of safeguarding the right of employees to rest and leave, and mobilizing their work enthusiasm.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　机关、团体、企业、事业单位、民办非企业单位、有雇工的个体工商户等单位的职工连续工作1年以上的，享受带薪年休假(以下简称年休假)。单位应当保证职工享受年休假。  
职工在年休假期间享受与正常工作期间相同的工资收入。

**Article 2** Employees of state organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, private non-enterprise units, individual businesses with employees and other entities who have been continuously working for more than one year shall be entitled to paid annual leave (hereinafter referred to as annual leave). The entity shall ensure that employees enjoy annual leave.  
During annual leave, employees shall receive the same wage income as during normal working periods.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　职工累计工作已满1年不满10年的，年休假5天；已满10年不满20年的，年休假10天；已满20年的，年休假15天。  
国家法定休假日、休息日不计入年休假的假期。

**Article 3** Employees who have accumulated working years of not less than one year but less than ten years shall be entitled to five days of annual leave; those who have accumulated working years of not less than ten years but less than twenty years shall be entitled to ten days of annual leave; and those who have accumulated working years of not less than twenty years shall be entitled to fifteen days of annual leave.  
Statutory holidays and rest days shall not be counted as part of the annual leave period.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　职工有下列情形之一的，不享受当年的年休假：  
(一)职工依法享受寒暑假，其休假天数多于年休假天数的；  
(二)职工请事假累计20天以上且单位按照规定不扣工资的；  
(三)累计工作满1年不满10年的职工，请病假累计2个月以上的；  
(四)累计工作满10年不满20年的职工，请病假累计3个月以上的；  
(五)累计工作满20年以上的职工，请病假累计4个月以上的。

**Article 4** An employee shall not be entitled to annual leave for the current year under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the employee enjoys winter or summer vacation according to law and the number of vacation days exceeds the number of annual leave days;  
(2) the employee has accumulated more than twenty days of personal leave, and the entity does not deduct wages in accordance with regulations;  
(3) for employees with accumulated working years of not less than one year but less than ten years, the accumulated sick leave exceeds two months;  
(4) for employees with accumulated working years of not less than ten years but less than twenty years, the accumulated sick leave exceeds three months;  
(5) for employees with accumulated working years of not less than twenty years, the accumulated sick leave exceeds four months.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　单位根据生产、工作的具体情况，并考虑职工本人意愿，统筹安排职工年休假。  
年休假在1个年度内可以集中安排，也可以分段安排，一般不跨年度安排。单位因生产、工作特点确有必要跨年度安排职工年休假的，可以跨1个年度安排。  
单位确因工作需要不能安排职工休年休假的，经职工本人同意，可以不安排职工休年休假。对职工应休未休的年休假天数，单位应当按照该职工日工资收入的300%支付年休假工资报酬。

**Article 5** An entity shall arrange annual leave for its employees in a comprehensive manner based on the specific conditions of production and work and taking into account the employees' wishes.  
Annual leave may be arranged consecutively or in separate periods within one year, and shall generally not be carried over to the following year. If it is truly necessary for the entity to carry over annual leave to the following year due to the characteristics of production or work, it may be carried over to one year.  
If it is truly necessary for the entity to be unable to arrange annual leave for an employee due to work needs, with the employee's consent, the entity may not arrange annual leave for the employee. For the annual leave days that the employee is entitled to but has not taken, the entity shall pay the employee annual leave wage compensation at 300% of the employee's daily wage income.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上地方人民政府人事部门、劳动保障部门应当依据职权对单位执行本条例的情况主动进行监督检查。  
工会组织依法维护职工的年休假权利。

**Article 6** The personnel departments and labor and social security departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and powers, proactively conduct supervision and inspection of the implementation of these Regulations by entities.  
Trade union organizations shall protect employees' right to annual leave according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　单位不安排职工休年休假又不依照本条例规定给予年休假工资报酬的，由县级以上地方人民政府人事部门或者劳动保障部门依据职权责令限期改正；对逾期不改正的，除责令该单位支付年休假工资报酬外，单位还应当按照年休假工资报酬的数额向职工加付赔偿金；对拒不支付年休假工资报酬、赔偿金的，属于公务员和参照公务员法管理的人员所在单位的，对直接负责的主管人员以及其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；属于其他单位的，由劳动保障部门、人事部门或者职工申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 7** If an entity fails to arrange annual leave for employees and fails to pay annual leave wage compensation in accordance with these Regulations, the personnel department or the labor and social security department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within its functions and powers, order the entity to make corrections within a specified time limit; if the entity fails to make corrections within the time limit, in addition to ordering the entity to pay the annual leave wage compensation, the entity shall also pay compensation to the employees in an amount equal to the annual leave wage compensation; if the entity refuses to pay the annual leave wage compensation and the compensation, and the entity is an entity where the personnel are civil servants or personnel managed by reference to the Civil Servants Law, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given disciplinary sanctions according to law; if it is another type of entity, the labor and social security department, the personnel department, or the employees shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　职工与单位因年休假发生的争议，依照国家有关法律、行政法规的规定处理。

**Article 8** Disputes between employees and entities arising from annual leave shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant national laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院人事部门、国务院劳动保障部门依据职权，分别制定本条例的实施办法。

**Article 9** The personnel department of the State Council and the labor and social security department of the State Council shall, within their respective functions and powers, formulate the implementing rules of these Regulations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　本条例自2008年1月1日起施行。

**Article 10** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2008.
