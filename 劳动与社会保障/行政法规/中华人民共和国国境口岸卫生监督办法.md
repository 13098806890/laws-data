# 中华人民共和国国境口岸卫生监督办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02

> (1981年12月30日国务院批准　1982年2月4日卫生部、交通部、中国民用航空总局、铁道部发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强国境口岸和国际航行交通工具的卫生监督工作，改善国境口岸和交通工具的卫生面貌，控制和消灭传染源，切断传播途径，防止传染病由国外传入和由国内传出，保障人民身体健康，制定本办法。 第二条　本办法适用于对外开放的港口、机场、车站、关口(下称国境口岸)和停留在这些处所的国际航行的船舶、飞机和车辆(下称交通工具)。 第二章　国境口岸的卫生要求 第三条　国境口岸应当建立卫生清扫制度，消灭蚊蝇孳生场所，设置污物箱，定期进行清理，保持环境整洁。 第四条　国境口岸的生活垃圾应当日产日清，设置的固定垃圾场，应当定期清除；生活污水不得任意排放，应当做到无害化处理，以防止污染环境和水源。 第五条　对国境口岸的建筑物，有关部门应当采取切实可行的措施，控制病媒昆虫、啮齿动物，使其数量降低到不足为害的程度。 第六条　候船室、候机室、候车室、候检室应当做到地面整洁、墙壁无尘土、窗明几净、通风良好，并备有必要的卫生设施。 第七条　国境口岸的餐厅、食堂、厨房、小卖部应当建立和健全卫生制度，经常保持整洁，做到墙壁、天花板、桌椅清洁无尘土；应当有防蚊、防蝇、防鼠和冷藏设备，做到室内无蚊、无蝇、无鼠、无蟑螂。 第八条　国境口岸的厕所和浴室应当有专人管理，及时打扫，保持整洁，做到无蝇、无臭味。 第九条　国境口岸的仓库、货场应当保持清洁、整齐；发现鼠类有反常死亡时，应当及时向卫生检疫机关或地方卫生防疫部门报告。 第十条　做好国境口岸水源保护，在水源周围直径30米内，不得修建厕所、渗井等污染水源设施。 第三章　交通工具的卫生要求 第十一条　交通工具上必须备有急救药物、急救设备及消毒、杀虫、灭鼠药物。必要时，船舶上还需安排临时隔离室。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强国境口岸和国际航行交通工具的卫生监督工作，改善国境口岸和交通工具的卫生面貌，控制和消灭传染源，切断传播途径，防止传染病由国外传入和由国内传出，保障人民身体健康，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the sanitary supervision over frontier ports and means of transport engaged in international navigation, improving the sanitary conditions of frontier ports and means of transport, controlling and eliminating sources of infection, cutting off the routes of transmission, preventing infectious diseases from being introduced from abroad and spread from within the country, and protecting the health of the people.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本办法适用于对外开放的港口、机场、车站、关口(下称国境口岸)和停留在这些处所的国际航行的船舶、飞机和车辆(下称交通工具)。

**Article 2** These Measures shall apply to ports, airports, railway stations, and border crossings open to foreign traffic (hereinafter referred to as frontier ports) and ships, aircraft and vehicles engaged in international navigation which stay at such places (hereinafter referred to as means of transport).

### 第二章　国境口岸的卫生要求

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国境口岸应当建立卫生清扫制度，消灭蚊蝇孳生场所，设置污物箱，定期进行清理，保持环境整洁。

**Article 3** Frontier ports shall establish a sanitation and cleaning system, eliminate the breeding grounds of mosquitoes and flies, set up refuse bins, clean them regularly, and keep the environment clean and tidy.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国境口岸的生活垃圾应当日产日清，设置的固定垃圾场，应当定期清除；生活污水不得任意排放，应当做到无害化处理，以防止污染环境和水源。

**Article 4** Domestic refuse at frontier ports shall be removed daily, and the fixed refuse dumps set up shall be cleared regularly; domestic sewage shall not be discharged at will, and shall be rendered harmless so as to prevent pollution of the environment and water sources.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对国境口岸的建筑物，有关部门应当采取切实可行的措施，控制病媒昆虫、啮齿动物，使其数量降低到不足为害的程度。

**Article 5** With respect to the buildings at frontier ports, the relevant departments shall take feasible measures to control vector insects and rodents so as to reduce their numbers to a level of no harm.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　候船室、候机室、候车室、候检室应当做到地面整洁、墙壁无尘土、窗明几净、通风良好，并备有必要的卫生设施。

**Article 6** Waiting rooms for ships, aircraft, trains, and inspection shall be kept with clean floors, dust-free walls, bright windows and clean surroundings, good ventilation, and shall be equipped with necessary sanitary facilities.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国境口岸的餐厅、食堂、厨房、小卖部应当建立和健全卫生制度，经常保持整洁，做到墙壁、天花板、桌椅清洁无尘土；应当有防蚊、防蝇、防鼠和冷藏设备，做到室内无蚊、无蝇、无鼠、无蟑螂。

**Article 7** Restaurants, canteens, kitchens and retail shops at frontier ports shall establish and improve sanitation systems, keep them clean at all times, ensure that walls, ceilings, tables and chairs are clean and free from dust; and shall be provided with mosquito-proof, fly-proof, rodent-proof and refrigerating equipment, so that the rooms are free from mosquitoes, flies, rats and cockroaches.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国境口岸的厕所和浴室应当有专人管理，及时打扫，保持整洁，做到无蝇、无臭味。

**Article 8** Toilets and bathrooms at frontier ports shall be managed by designated persons, cleaned promptly, and kept tidy, so that they are free from flies and foul smells.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国境口岸的仓库、货场应当保持清洁、整齐；发现鼠类有反常死亡时，应当及时向卫生检疫机关或地方卫生防疫部门报告。

**Article 9** Warehouses and freight yards at frontier ports shall be kept clean and tidy; where abnormal death of rodents is discovered, a report shall be made in a timely manner to the health quarantine office or the local health and epidemic prevention department.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　做好国境口岸水源保护，在水源周围直径30米内，不得修建厕所、渗井等污染水源设施。

**Article 10** Water source protection at frontier ports shall be properly carried out. No toilet, seepage pit or other facility that may pollute the water source shall be constructed within a radius of 30 meters from the water source.

### 第三章　交通工具的卫生要求

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　交通工具上必须备有急救药物、急救设备及消毒、杀虫、灭鼠药物。必要时，船舶上还需安排临时隔离室。

**Article 11** Means of transport must be equipped with first-aid medicines, first-aid equipment, and disinfectants, insecticides, and rodenticides. When necessary, ships shall also be provided with temporary isolation rooms.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　交通工具上的病媒昆虫和啮齿动物的防除：  
(一)船舶、飞机、列车上，应当备有足够数量有效的防鼠装置；保持无鼠或鼠类数量保持不足为害的程度。  
(二)应当保持无蚊、无蝇、无其他有害昆虫，一旦发现应当采取杀灭措施。

**Article 12** Prevention and elimination of disease-transmitting insects and rodents on means of transport:  
(1) Ships, aircraft, and trains shall be equipped with a sufficient number of effective anti-rodent devices; maintain rodent-free status or keep the rodent population below a level that causes harm.  
(2) They shall remain free of mosquitoes, flies, and other harmful insects; once discovered, extermination measures shall be taken.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　交通工具上的厕所、浴室必须保持整洁，无臭味。

**Article 13** Toilets and bathrooms on means of transport must be kept clean and odorless.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　交通工具上的粪便、垃圾、污水处理的卫生要求：  
(一)生活垃圾应当集中放在带盖的容器内，禁止向港区、机场、站区随意倾倒，应当由污物专用车(船)集中送往指定地点进行无害化处理。必要时，粪便、污水须经过卫生处理后方能排放；  
(二)来自鼠疫疫区交通工具上的固体垃圾必须进行焚化处理，来自霍乱疫区交通工具上的粪便、压舱水、污水，必要时实施消毒。

**Article 14** Sanitary requirements for the disposal of feces, garbage, and sewage on means of transport:  
(1) Household garbage shall be collected in covered containers; it is prohibited to dump it indiscriminately in port areas, airport areas, or station areas; it shall be collected by special garbage vehicles (ships) and transported to designated locations for harmless treatment. When necessary, feces and sewage shall undergo sanitary treatment before discharge;  
(2) Solid garbage from means of transport coming from plague-affected areas must be incinerated; feces, ballast water, and sewage from means of transport coming from cholera-affected areas shall, when necessary, be disinfected.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　交通工具的货舱、行李车、邮政车和货车的卫生要求：  
(一)货舱、行李车、邮政车、货车应当消灭蚊、蝇、蟑螂、鼠等病媒昆虫和有害动物及其孳生条件；在装货前或卸货后应当进行彻底清扫，做到无粪便、垃圾；  
(二)凡装载有毒物品和食品的货车，应当分开按指定地点存放，防止污染，货物卸空后应当进行彻底洗刷；  
(三)来自疫区的行李、货物，要严格检查，防止带有病媒昆虫和啮齿动物。

**Article 15** Sanitary requirements for cargo holds, baggage cars, mail cars, and freight cars of means of transport:  
(1) Cargo holds, baggage cars, mail cars, and freight cars shall eliminate mosquitoes, flies, cockroaches, rats, and other disease-transmitting insects and harmful animals, as well as their breeding conditions; before loading or after unloading, they shall be thoroughly cleaned to be free of feces and garbage;  
(2) All freight cars carrying toxic substances and foodstuffs shall be loaded separately and stored at designated locations to prevent contamination; after unloading, they shall be thoroughly washed and scrubbed;  
(3) Baggage and cargo from epidemic areas shall be strictly inspected to prevent the presence of disease-transmitting insects and rodents.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　交通工具上的客舱、宿舱、客车的卫生要求：  
(一)客舱、宿舱和客车应当随时擦洗，保持无垃圾尘土，通风良好；  
(二)卧具每次使用后必须换洗。卧具上不得有虱子、跳蚤、臭虫等病媒昆虫。

**Article 16** Sanitary requirements for passenger cabins, berthing cabins, and passenger cars of means of transport:  
(1) Passenger cabins, berthing cabins, and passenger cars shall be wiped and cleaned at all times to remain free of garbage and dust, and well-ventilated;  
(2) Bedding must be changed and washed after each use. Bedding shall not have lice, fleas, bedbugs, or other disease-transmitting insects.

### 第四章　食品、饮用水及从业人员的卫生要求

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　供应国境口岸和交通工具上的食品必须符合《中华人民共和国食品安全法》的规定和食品安全标准。

**Article 17** Food supplied at frontier ports and on means of transport must comply with the provisions of the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and food safety standards.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　凡供应国境口岸和交通工具上的饮用水必须符合我国规定的“生活饮用水卫生标准”。供应饮用水的运输工具、储存容器及输水管道等设备都应当经常冲洗干净，保持清洁。

**Article 18** All drinking water supplied at frontier ports and on means of transport must comply with the 'Hygienic Standard for Drinking Water' prescribed by China. Conveyances, storage containers, and water pipelines and other equipment used to supply drinking water shall be frequently rinsed and kept clean.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　供应食品、饮用水的从业人员的卫生要求：  
(一)患有肠道传染病的患者或带菌者，以及活动性结核病、化脓性渗出性皮肤病患者，不得从事食品和饮用水供应工作；  
(二)从事食品、饮用水供应工作的人员，应当每年进行一次健康检查，新参加工作的人员，应当首先进行健康检查，经检查合格者，发给健康证；  
(三)从事食品、饮用水供应工作的人员，要养成良好卫生习惯，工作时要着装整洁，严格遵守卫生操作制度。

**Article 19** Sanitary requirements for personnel engaged in supplying food and drinking water:  
(1) Persons suffering from intestinal infectious diseases or carriers, as well as those with active tuberculosis or suppurative exudative skin diseases, shall not engage in the work of supplying food and drinking water;  
(2) Personnel engaged in the work of supplying food and drinking water shall undergo a health examination once a year; newly employed personnel shall first undergo a health examination; those who pass the examination shall be issued a health certificate;  
(3) Personnel engaged in the work of supplying food and drinking water shall cultivate good hygiene habits, dress neatly while working, and strictly observe the sanitary operation system.

### 第五章　国境口岸和交通工具的负责人的责任

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国境口岸和交通工具的负责人在卫生工作方面的责任是：  
(一)应当经常抓好卫生工作，接受卫生监督人员的监督和检查，并为其开展工作提供方便条件；  
(二)应当模范地遵守本办法和其他卫生法令、条例和规定；  
(三)应当按照卫生监督人员的建议，对国境口岸和交通工具的不卫生状况，及时采取措施，加以改进；  
(四)在发现检疫传染病和监测传染病时，应当向国境卫生检疫机关或地方防疫部门报告，并立即采取防疫措施。

**Article 20** Responsibilities of persons in charge of frontier ports and means of transport in sanitary work:  
(1) They shall constantly and diligently carry out sanitary work, accept supervision and inspection by health supervisors, and provide convenient conditions for their work;  
(2) They shall set an example in complying with these Measures and other health laws, regulations, and rules;  
(3) They shall, in accordance with the recommendations of health supervisors, take timely measures to improve unsanitary conditions at frontier ports and on means of transport;  
(4) Upon discovering quarantinable diseases or diseases under surveillance, they shall report to the frontier health and quarantine authorities or local epidemic prevention departments and immediately take epidemic prevention measures.

### 第六章　卫生监督机关的职责

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国境口岸卫生检疫机关对国境口岸和交通工具进行卫生监督，其主要职责是：  
(一)监督和指导国境口岸有关部门和交通工具的负责人对病媒昆虫、啮齿动物进行防除；  
(二)对停留在国境口岸出入国境的交通工具上的食品、饮用水实施检验，并对运输、供应、贮存设施等系统进行卫生监督；  
(三)对国境口岸和交通工具上的所有非因意外伤害致死的尸体，实施检查、监督和卫生处理；  
(四)监督国境口岸有关部门和交通工具的负责人对粪便、垃圾、污水进行清除和无害化处理；  
(五)对与检疫传染病、监测传染病有流行病学意义的环境因素实施卫生监督；  
(六)监督国境口岸周围内采取防蚊措施的执行；  
(七)开展卫生宣传教育，普及卫生知识，提高国境口岸和交通工具上的人员遵守和执行本办法的自觉性。

**Article 21** The frontier health and quarantine authorities shall conduct health supervision over frontier ports and means of transport, with its main duties being:  
(1) supervise and guide the persons in charge of relevant departments at frontier ports and means of transport in the prevention and elimination of vector insects and rodents;  
(2) conduct inspection of food and drinking water on means of transport staying at frontier ports for entering or leaving the country, and conduct health supervision over the systems for transportation, supply, storage facilities, etc.;  
(3) conduct inspection, supervision and sanitary treatment of all corpses not resulting from accidental injuries at frontier ports and on means of transport;  
(4) supervise the persons in charge of relevant departments at frontier ports and means of transport in the removal and harmless treatment of excrement, garbage and sewage;  
(5) conduct health supervision over environmental factors of epidemiological significance related to quarantine infectious diseases and surveillance infectious diseases;  
(6) supervise the implementation of mosquito prevention measures in the vicinity of frontier ports;  
(7) carry out health publicity and education, popularize health knowledge, and enhance the consciousness of personnel at frontier ports and on means of transport to abide by and implement these Measures.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国境口岸卫生检疫机关设国境口岸卫生监督员1至5名，执行卫生监督任务，发给统一式样的执法证件。

**Article 22** The frontier health and quarantine authorities shall establish one to five frontier port health supervisors at frontier ports to perform health supervision duties, and issue them with uniform law enforcement credentials.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国境口岸卫生监督员持其证件，有权对国境口岸和交通工具的负责人，进行卫生监督、检查和技术指导；配合有关部门，对卫生工作情况不良或引起传染病传播的单位或个人，提出改进意见，协同有关部门采取必要措施，进行处理。

**Article 23** The frontier port health supervisors, holding their credentials, shall have the right to conduct health supervision, inspection and technical guidance over the persons in charge of frontier ports and means of transport; and in coordination with relevant departments, put forward improvement suggestions for units or individuals with poor health conditions or causing the spread of infectious diseases, and cooperate with relevant departments to take necessary measures for disposal.

### 第七章　奖励和惩罚

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国境口岸卫生检疫机关，对贯彻执行本办法和国家有关卫生法令、条例、规定，做出显著成绩的单位和个人，应当给予表扬和奖励。

**Article 24** The frontier health and quarantine authorities shall give commendation and awards to units and individuals that have made remarkable achievements in implementing these Measures and relevant national health decrees, regulations and provisions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国境口岸卫生检疫机关，对违犯本办法和有关卫生法令、条例、规定的单位和个人，应当根据不同情况，给予警告、罚款，直至提请司法机关依法惩处。

**Article 25** The frontier health and quarantine authorities shall, depending on the circumstances, give warnings, impose fines, or even refer the matter to judicial organs for punishment according to law against units and individuals that violate these Measures and relevant health decrees, regulations and provisions.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本办法自发布之日起施行。

**Article 26** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
