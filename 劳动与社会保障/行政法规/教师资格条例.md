# 教师资格条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：1995-12-12  
**生效日期**：1995-12-12

> (1995年12月12日中华人民共和国国务院令第188号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了提高教师素质，加强教师队伍建设，依据《中华人民共和国教师法》(以下简称教师法)，制定本条例。 第二条　中国公民在各级各类学校和其他教育机构中专门从事教育教学工作，应当依法取得教师资格。 第三条　国务院教育行政部门主管全国教师资格工作。 第二章　教师资格分类与适用 第四条　教师资格分为： (一)幼儿园教师资格； (二)小学教师资格； (三)初级中学教师和初级职业学校文化课、专业课教师资格(以下统称初级中学教师资格)； (四)高级中学教师资格； (五)中等专业学校、技工学校、职业高级中学文化课、专业课教师资格(以下统称中等职业学校教师资格)； (六)中等专业学校、技工学校、职业高级中学实习指导教师资格(以下统称中等职业学校实习指导教师资格)； (七)高等学校教师资格。 成人教育的教师资格，按照成人教育的层次，依照上款规定确定类别。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了提高教师素质，加强教师队伍建设，依据《中华人民共和国教师法》(以下简称教师法)，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Teachers Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Teachers Law) for the purpose of improving the quality of teachers and strengthening the building of the teaching staff.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中国公民在各级各类学校和其他教育机构中专门从事教育教学工作，应当依法取得教师资格。

**Article 2** Chinese citizens who are engaged in teaching and educational work in schools and other educational institutions of various types and levels shall obtain teacher qualifications according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院教育行政部门主管全国教师资格工作。

**Article 3** The administrative department of education under the State Council shall be in charge of the work on teacher qualifications nationwide.

### 第二章　教师资格分类与适用

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　教师资格分为：  
(一)幼儿园教师资格；  
(二)小学教师资格；  
(三)初级中学教师和初级职业学校文化课、专业课教师资格(以下统称初级中学教师资格)；  
(四)高级中学教师资格；  
(五)中等专业学校、技工学校、职业高级中学文化课、专业课教师资格(以下统称中等职业学校教师资格)；  
(六)中等专业学校、技工学校、职业高级中学实习指导教师资格(以下统称中等职业学校实习指导教师资格)；  
(七)高等学校教师资格。  
成人教育的教师资格，按照成人教育的层次，依照上款规定确定类别。

**Article 4** Teacher qualifications are divided into:  
(1) kindergarten teacher qualification;  
(2) primary school teacher qualification;  
(3) junior secondary school teacher qualification and cultural courses and specialized courses teacher qualification for junior vocational schools (hereinafter collectively referred to as junior secondary school teacher qualification);  
(4) senior secondary school teacher qualification;  
(5) cultural courses and specialized courses teacher qualification for specialized secondary schools, skilled worker schools, and senior vocational secondary schools (hereinafter collectively referred to as secondary vocational school teacher qualification);  
(6) practice instruction teacher qualification for specialized secondary schools, skilled worker schools, and senior vocational secondary schools (hereinafter collectively referred to as secondary vocational school practice instruction teacher qualification);  
(7) higher education institution teacher qualification.  
The teacher qualification for adult education shall be categorized according to the level of adult education in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　取得教师资格的公民，可以在本级及其以下等级的各类学校和其他教育机构担任教师；但是，取得中等职业学校实习指导教师资格的公民只能在中等专业学校、技工学校、职业高级中学或者初级职业学校担任实习指导教师。  
高级中学教师资格与中等职业学校教师资格相互通用。

**Article 5** A citizen who has obtained a teacher qualification may serve as a teacher in schools and other educational institutions of the same level and lower levels; however, a citizen who has obtained the secondary vocational school practice instruction teacher qualification may only serve as a practice instruction teacher in specialized secondary schools, skilled worker schools, senior vocational secondary schools, or junior vocational schools.  
The senior secondary school teacher qualification and the secondary vocational school teacher qualification are mutually applicable.

### 第三章　教师资格条件

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　教师资格条件依照[教师法第十条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国教师法.md#art-10)第二款的规定执行，其中“有教育教学能力”应当包括符合国家规定的从事教育教学工作的身体条件。

**Article 6** The conditions for teacher qualifications shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of Article 10, paragraph 2 of the Teachers Law, in which 'having teaching and educational ability' shall include meeting the physical conditions for engaging in teaching and educational work as prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　取得教师资格应当具备的相应学历，依照[教师法第十一条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国教师法.md#art-11)的规定执行。  
取得中等职业学校实习指导教师资格，应当具备国务院教育行政部门规定的学历，并应当具有相当助理工程师以上专业技术职务或者中级以上工人技术等级。

**Article 7** The corresponding academic qualifications required for obtaining a teacher qualification shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of Article 11 of the Teachers Law.  
To obtain the secondary vocational school practice instruction teacher qualification, the citizen shall possess the academic qualifications prescribed by the administrative department of education under the State Council and shall hold a professional technical position equivalent to assistant engineer or above, or a worker technical grade of intermediate or above.

### 第四章　教师资格考试

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　不具备教师法规定的教师资格学历的公民，申请获得教师资格，应当通过国家举办的或者认可的教师资格考试。

**Article 8** A citizen who does not possess the academic qualifications for teacher qualifications as prescribed by the Teachers Law and applies to obtain a teacher qualification shall pass the national teacher qualification examination held or recognized by the State.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　教师资格考试科目、标准和考试大纲由国务院教育行政部门审定。  
教师资格考试试卷的编制、考务工作和考试成绩证明的发放，属于幼儿园、小学、初级中学、高级中学、中等职业学校教师资格考试和中等职业学校实习指导教师资格考试的，由县级以上人民政府教育行政部门组织实施；属于高等学校教师资格考试的，由国务院教育行政部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门委托的高等学校组织实施。

**Article 9** The subjects, standards, and examination syllabuses for the teacher qualification examination shall be examined and approved by the administrative department of education under the State Council.  
The compilation of examination papers, examination affairs, and issuance of examination score certificates for the teacher qualification examinations for kindergarten, primary school, junior secondary school, senior secondary school, secondary vocational school teacher qualifications and secondary vocational school practice instruction teacher qualification shall be organized and implemented by the administrative departments of education of the people's governments at or above the county level; for the higher education institution teacher qualification examination, it shall be organized and implemented by the higher education institutions entrusted by the administrative department of education under the State Council or the administrative departments of education of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　幼儿园、小学、初级中学、高级中学、中等职业学校的教师资格考试和中等职业学校实习指导教师资格考试，每年进行一次。  
参加前款所列教师资格考试，考试科目全部及格的，发给教师资格考试合格证明；当年考试不及格的科目，可以在下一年度补考；经补考仍有一门或者一门以上科目不及格的，应当重新参加全部考试科目的考试。

**Article 10** The teacher qualification examinations for kindergarten, primary school, junior secondary school, senior secondary school, secondary vocational school and the secondary vocational school practice instruction teacher qualification examination shall be held once each year.  
For persons participating in the teacher qualification examinations listed in the preceding paragraph, if they pass all examination subjects, a certificate of passing the teacher qualification examination shall be issued; subjects not passed in the current year may be retaken in the following year; if after the retake, one or more subjects are still not passed, the person shall retake the examination for all subjects.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　高等学校教师资格考试根据需要举行。  
申请参加高等学校教师资格考试的，应当学有专长，并有两名相关专业的教授或者副教授推荐。

**Article 11** The higher education institution teacher qualification examination shall be held as needed.  
A person who applies to take the higher education institution teacher qualification examination shall have specialized knowledge and be recommended by two professors or associate professors in relevant disciplines.

### 第五章　教师资格认定

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　具备教师法规定的学历或者经教师资格考试合格的公民，可以依照本条例的规定申请认定其教师资格。

**Article 12** A citizen who possesses the academic qualifications prescribed by the Teachers Law or passes the teacher qualification examination may, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, apply for the recognition of his or her teacher qualification.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　幼儿园、小学和初级中学教师资格，由申请人户籍所在地或者申请人任教学校所在地的县级人民政府教育行政部门认定。高级中学教师资格，由申请人户籍所在地或者申请人任教学校所在地的县级人民政府教育行政部门审查后，报上一级教育行政部门认定。中等职业学校教师资格和中等职业学校实习指导教师资格，由申请人户籍所在地或者申请人任教学校所在地的县级人民政府教育行政部门审查后，报上一级教育行政部门认定或者组织有关部门认定。  
受国务院教育行政部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门委托的高等学校，负责认定在本校任职的人员和拟聘人员的高等学校教师资格。  
在未受国务院教育行政部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门委托的高等学校任职的人员和拟聘人员的高等学校教师资格，按照学校行政隶属关系，由国务院教育行政部门认定或者由学校所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门认定。

**Article 13** The teacher qualifications for kindergarten, primary school, and junior secondary school shall be recognized by the administrative department of education of the people's government at the county level where the applicant's registered residence or the school where the applicant teaches is located. The senior secondary school teacher qualification shall be examined by the administrative department of education of the people's government at the county level where the applicant's registered residence or the school where the applicant teaches is located, and then reported to the administrative department of education at the next higher level for recognition. The secondary vocational school teacher qualification and secondary vocational school practice instruction teacher qualification shall be examined by the administrative department of education of the people's government at the county level where the applicant's registered residence or the school where the applicant teaches is located, and then reported to the administrative department of education at the next higher level for recognition or for organizing relevant departments to recognize.  
Higher education institutions entrusted by the administrative department of education under the State Council or the administrative departments of education of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for recognizing the higher education institution teacher qualifications of persons working or to be employed in their own institutions.  
For persons working or to be employed in higher education institutions that are not entrusted by the administrative department of education under the State Council or the administrative departments of education of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, their higher education institution teacher qualifications shall be recognized by the administrative department of education under the State Council or by the administrative department of education of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the institution is located, in accordance with the administrative subordination of the institution.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　认定教师资格，应当由本人提出申请。  
教育行政部门和受委托的高等学校每年春季、秋季各受理一次教师资格认定申请。具体受理期限由教育行政部门或者受委托的高等学校规定，并以适当形式公布。申请人应当在规定的受理期限内提出申请。

**Article 14** For recognition of teacher qualifications, the application shall be filed by the person himself or herself.  
The administrative department of education and the entrusted higher education institutions shall accept applications for teacher qualification recognition once each in spring and autumn each year. The specific acceptance period shall be prescribed by the administrative department of education or the entrusted higher education institution, and shall be publicized in an appropriate manner. The applicant shall file the application within the prescribed acceptance period.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　申请认定教师资格，应当提交教师资格认定申请表和下列证明或者材料：  
(一)身份证明；  
(二)学历证书或者教师资格考试合格证明；  
(三)教育行政部门或者受委托的高等学校指定的医院出具的体格检查证明；  
(四)户籍所在地的街道办事处、乡人民政府或者工作单位、所毕业的学校对其思想品德、有无犯罪记录等方面情况的鉴定及证明材料。  
申请人提交的证明或者材料不全的，教育行政部门或者受委托的高等学校应当及时通知申请人于受理期限终止前补齐。  
教师资格认定申请表由国务院教育行政部门统一格式。

**Article 15** To apply for teacher qualification recognition, the applicant shall submit a teacher qualification recognition application form and the following certificates or materials:  
(1) identity certificate;  
(2) academic qualification certificate or certificate of passing the teacher qualification examination;  
(3) physical examination certificate issued by a hospital designated by the administrative department of education or the entrusted higher education institution;  
(4) evaluation and certification materials on the applicant's ideological and moral character, whether there is any criminal record, and other aspects, issued by the sub-district office or township people's government of the place of registered residence, or the work unit or school from which the applicant graduated.  
If the certificates or materials submitted by the applicant are incomplete, the administrative department of education or the entrusted higher education institution shall promptly notify the applicant to complete them before the end of the acceptance period.  
The teacher qualification recognition application form shall be of unified format by the administrative department of education under the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　教育行政部门或者受委托的高等学校在接到公民的教师资格认定申请后，应当对申请人的条件进行审查；对符合认定条件的，应当在受理期限终止之日起30日内颁发相应的教师资格证书；对不符合认定条件的，应当在受理期限终止之日起30日内将认定结论通知本人。  
非师范院校毕业或者教师资格考试合格的公民申请认定幼儿园、小学或者其他教师资格的，应当进行面试和试讲，考察其教育教学能力；根据实际情况和需要，教育行政部门或者受委托的高等学校可以要求申请人补修教育学、心理学等课程。  
教师资格证书在全国范围内适用。教师资格证书由国务院教育行政部门统一印制。

**Article 16** After receiving an application for teacher qualification recognition from a citizen, the administrative department of education or the entrusted higher education institution shall examine the applicant's qualifications; for those meeting the recognition conditions, the corresponding teacher qualification certificate shall be issued within 30 days from the date of expiration of the acceptance period; for those not meeting the recognition conditions, the recognition conclusion shall be notified to the person within 30 days from the date of expiration of the acceptance period.  
For a citizen who graduates from a non-teacher-training institution or passes the teacher qualification examination and applies for recognition of kindergarten, primary school, or other teacher qualifications, an interview and trial lecture shall be conducted to assess his or her teaching and educational abilities; according to the actual situation and needs, the administrative department of education or the entrusted higher education institution may require the applicant to make up courses such as pedagogy and psychology.  
The teacher qualification certificate shall be applicable nationwide. The teacher qualification certificate shall be uniformly printed by the administrative department of education under the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　已取得教师资格的公民拟取得更高等级学校或者其他教育机构教师资格的，应当通过相应的教师资格考试或者取得教师法规定的相应学历，并依照本章规定，经认定合格后，由教育行政部门或者受委托的高等学校颁发相应的教师资格证书。

**Article 17** A citizen who has already obtained a teacher qualification and intends to obtain a teacher qualification of a higher level school or other educational institution shall pass the corresponding teacher qualification examination or obtain the corresponding academic qualifications prescribed by the Teachers Law, and after being recognized as qualified in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter, the corresponding teacher qualification certificate shall be issued by the administrative department of education or the entrusted higher education institution.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　依照[教师法第十四条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国教师法.md#art-14)的规定丧失教师资格的，不能重新取得教师资格，其教师资格证书由县级以上人民政府教育行政部门收缴。

**Article 18** A person who loses teacher qualifications in accordance with the provisions of Article 14 of the Teachers Law may not obtain teacher qualifications anew, and his or her teacher qualification certificate shall be recovered by the administrative department of education of the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府教育行政部门撤销其教师资格：  
(一) 弄虚作假、骗取教师资格的；  
(二) 品行不良、侮辱学生，影响恶劣的。  
被撤销教师资格的，自撤销之日起5年内不得重新申请认定教师资格，其教师资格证书由县级以上人民政府教育行政部门收缴。

**Article 19** In any of the following circumstances, the administrative department of education of the people's government at or above the county level shall revoke the teacher qualification:  
(1) obtaining a teacher qualification by fraud or deception;  
(2) having bad conduct, insulting students, and causing adverse effects.  
A person whose teacher qualification is revoked may not apply for teacher qualification recognition anew within five years from the date of revocation, and his or her teacher qualification certificate shall be recovered by the administrative department of education of the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　参加教师资格考试有作弊行为的，其考试成绩作废，3年内不得再次参加教师资格考试。

**Article 20** A person who commits fraudulent acts in the teacher qualification examination shall have his or her examination scores invalidated and may not take the teacher qualification examination again within three years.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　教师资格考试命题人员和其他有关人员违反保密规定，造成试题、参考答案及评分标准泄露的，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 21** If persons who set examination questions for the teacher qualification examination and other relevant personnel violate the confidentiality provisions, causing the leakage of examination questions, reference answers, and grading standards, they shall be subject to legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　在教师资格认定工作中玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，对教师资格认定工作造成损失的，由教育行政部门依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
附则

**Article 22** Any person who neglects duty or engages in malpractice for personal gain in the work of teacher qualification recognition, thereby causing loss to the work of teacher qualification recognition, shall be given administrative sanctions according to law by the administrative department of education; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 23** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
