# 医疗机构管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2022-03-29  
**生效日期**：2022-05-01

> （1994年2月26日中华人民共和国国务院令第149号发布　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2022年3月29日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订）

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对医疗机构的管理，促进医疗卫生事业的发展，保障公民健康，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the management of medical institutions, promoting the development of medical and health undertakings, and protecting the health of citizens.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于从事疾病诊断、治疗活动的医院、卫生院、疗养院、门诊部、诊所、卫生所（室）以及急救站等医疗机构。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to medical institutions such as hospitals, health centers, sanatoriums, outpatient departments, clinics, health stations (rooms), and first-aid stations that engage in disease diagnosis and treatment activities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　医疗机构以救死扶伤，防病治病，为公民的健康服务为宗旨。

**Article 3** A medical institution shall take it as its purpose to heal the wounded and rescue the dying, prevent and treat diseases, and serve the health of citizens.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家扶持医疗机构的发展，鼓励多种形式兴办医疗机构。

**Article 4** The State shall support the development of medical institutions and encourage the establishment of medical institutions in various forms.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院卫生行政部门负责全国医疗机构的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门负责本行政区域内医疗机构的监督管理工作。  
中国人民解放军卫生主管部门依照本条例和国家有关规定，对军队的医疗机构实施监督管理。

**Article 5** The health administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of medical institutions nationwide.  
The health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of medical institutions within its administrative region.  
The health competent authority of the Chinese People's Liberation Army shall, in accordance with these Regulations and relevant State regulations, exercise supervision and administration over medical institutions of the armed forces.

### 第二章　规划布局和设置审批

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门应当根据本行政区域内的人口、医疗资源、医疗需求和现有医疗机构的分布状况，制定本行政区域医疗机构设置规划。  
机关、企业和事业单位可以根据需要设置医疗机构，并纳入当地医疗机构的设置规划。

**Article 6** The health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall formulate a plan for the establishment of medical institutions within its administrative region based on the population, medical resources, medical needs, and distribution of existing medical institutions within the said region.  
State organs, enterprises, and institutions may, as needed, establish medical institutions, which shall be included in the local plan for the establishment of medical institutions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上地方人民政府应当把医疗机构设置规划纳入当地的区域卫生发展规划和城乡建设发展总体规划。

**Article 7** The local people's government at or above the county level shall incorporate the plan for the establishment of medical institutions into the local regional health development plan and the overall plan for urban and rural construction development.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　设置医疗机构应当符合医疗机构设置规划和医疗机构基本标准。  
医疗机构基本标准由国务院卫生行政部门制定。

**Article 8** The establishment of a medical institution shall conform to the plan for the establishment of medical institutions and the basic standards for medical institutions.  
The basic standards for medical institutions shall be formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　单位或者个人设置医疗机构，按照国务院的规定应当办理设置医疗机构批准书的，应当经县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门审查批准，并取得设置医疗机构批准书。

**Article 9** Where, in accordance with the regulations of the State Council, an entity or individual that establishes a medical institution shall apply for and obtain an approval certificate for the establishment of a medical institution, it shall be subject to the examination and approval of the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level and shall obtain the approval certificate for the establishment of a medical institution.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　申请设置医疗机构，应当提交下列文件：  
（一）设置申请书；  
（二）设置可行性研究报告；  
（三）选址报告和建筑设计平面图。

**Article 10** An application for establishing a medical institution shall submit the following documents:  
(1) a written application for establishment;  
(2) a feasibility study report on the establishment; and  
(3) a location report and a layout plan of the building design.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　单位或者个人设置医疗机构，应当按照以下规定提出设置申请：  
（一）不设床位或者床位不满100张的医疗机构，向所在地的县级人民政府卫生行政部门申请；  
（二）床位在100张以上的医疗机构和专科医院按照省级人民政府卫生行政部门的规定申请。

**Article 11** An entity or individual that establishes a medical institution shall submit an application for establishment in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) for a medical institution without beds or with fewer than 100 beds, the application shall be submitted to the health administrative department of the county people's government at the locality; and  
(2) for a medical institution with 100 beds or more and a specialized hospital, the application shall be submitted in accordance with the provisions of the health administrative department of the provincial people's government.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门应当自受理设置申请之日起30日内，作出批准或者不批准的书面答复；批准设置的，发给设置医疗机构批准书。

**Article 12** The health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within 30 days from the date of accepting the application for establishment, make a written reply of approval or disapproval; if the establishment is approved, it shall issue an approval certificate for the establishment of a medical institution.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家统一规划的医疗机构的设置，由国务院卫生行政部门决定。

**Article 13** The establishment of medical institutions that are uniformly planned by the State shall be determined by the health administrative department of the State Council.

### 第三章　登记

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　医疗机构执业，必须进行登记，领取《医疗机构执业许可证》；诊所按照国务院卫生行政部门的规定向所在地的县级人民政府卫生行政部门备案后，可以执业。

**Article 14** A medical institution must undergo registration and obtain a Medical Institution Practice License before it may practice; after a clinic files a record with the health administrative department of the county people's government at the locality in accordance with the regulations of the health administrative department of the State Council, it may practice.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　申请医疗机构执业登记，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）按照规定应当办理设置医疗机构批准书的，已取得设置医疗机构批准书；  
（二）符合医疗机构的基本标准；  
（三）有适合的名称、组织机构和场所；  
（四）有与其开展的业务相适应的经费、设施、设备和专业卫生技术人员；  
（五）有相应的规章制度；  
（六）能够独立承担民事责任。

**Article 15** An applicant for the practice registration of a medical institution shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) if, in accordance with the regulations, an approval certificate for the establishment of a medical institution shall be obtained, such certificate has been obtained;  
(2) conforming to the basic standards for medical institutions;  
(3) having an appropriate name, organizational structure, and premises;  
(4) having funds, facilities, equipment, and professional health technical personnel commensurate with the business to be carried out;  
(5) having corresponding rules and regulations; and  
(6) being capable of independently bearing civil liability.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　医疗机构的执业登记，由批准其设置的人民政府卫生行政部门办理；不需要办理设置医疗机构批准书的医疗机构的执业登记，由所在地的县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门办理。  
按照[本条例第十三条](#art-13)规定设置的医疗机构的执业登记，由所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门办理。  
机关、企业和事业单位设置的为内部职工服务的门诊部、卫生所（室）、诊所的执业登记或者备案，由所在地的县级人民政府卫生行政部门办理。

**Article 16** The practice registration of a medical institution shall be handled by the health administrative department of the people's government that approved its establishment; the practice registration of a medical institution for which an approval certificate for establishment is not required shall be handled by the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level.  
The practice registration of a medical institution established in accordance with Article 13 of these Regulations shall be handled by the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government at the locality.  
The practice registration or filing of outpatient departments, health stations (rooms), and clinics set up by state organs, enterprises, and institutions for the service of their internal staff shall be handled by the health administrative department of the county people's government at the locality.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　医疗机构执业登记的主要事项：  
（一）名称、地址、主要负责人；  
（二）所有制形式；  
（三）诊疗科目、床位；  
（四）注册资金。

**Article 17** The main items of practice registration of a medical institution shall be:  
(1) name, address, and principal person in charge;  
(2) form of ownership;  
(3) diagnosis and treatment subjects and beds; and  
(4) registered capital.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门自受理执业登记申请之日起45日内，根据本条例和医疗机构基本标准进行审核。审核合格的，予以登记，发给《医疗机构执业许可证》；审核不合格的，将审核结果以书面形式通知申请人。

**Article 18** The health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within 45 days from the date of accepting the application for practice registration, conduct an examination in accordance with these Regulations and the basic standards for medical institutions. If the examination is passed, it shall register and issue a Medical Institution Practice License; if the examination is failed, it shall notify the applicant in writing of the examination result.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　医疗机构改变名称、场所、主要负责人、诊疗科目、床位，必须向原登记机关办理变更登记或者向原备案机关备案。

**Article 19** If a medical institution changes its name, premises, principal person in charge, diagnosis and treatment subjects, or number of beds, it must undergo modification registration with the original registration authority or file a record with the original filing authority.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　医疗机构歇业，必须向原登记机关办理注销登记或者向原备案机关备案。经登记机关核准后，收缴《医疗机构执业许可证》。  
医疗机构非因改建、扩建、迁建原因停业超过1年的，视为歇业。

**Article 20** If a medical institution suspends business, it must undergo cancellation of registration with the original registration authority or file a record with the original filing authority. After verification and approval by the registration authority, the Medical Institution Practice License shall be confiscated.  
If a medical institution ceases operation for more than one year for reasons other than reconstruction, expansion, or relocation, it shall be deemed to have suspended business.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　床位不满100张的医疗机构，其《医疗机构执业许可证》每年校验1次；床位在100张以上的医疗机构，其《医疗机构执业许可证》每3年校验1次。校验由原登记机关办理。

**Article 21** A medical institution with fewer than 100 beds shall have its Medical Institution Practice License verified once every year; a medical institution with 100 beds or more shall have its Medical Institution Practice License verified once every three years. The verification shall be handled by the original registration authority.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　《医疗机构执业许可证》不得伪造、涂改、出卖、转让、出借。  
《医疗机构执业许可证》遗失的，应当及时申明，并向原登记机关申请补发。

**Article 22** No Medical Institution Practice License shall be forged, altered, sold, transferred, or lent.  
If the Medical Institution Practice License is lost, the medical institution shall promptly declare it and apply to the original registration authority for reissuance.

### 第四章　执业

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　任何单位或者个人，未取得《医疗机构执业许可证》或者未经备案，不得开展诊疗活动。

**Article 23** No unit or individual that has not obtained a Medical Institution Practice License or has not been filed for record shall carry out diagnosis and treatment activities.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　医疗机构执业，必须遵守有关法律、法规和医疗技术规范。

**Article 24** A medical institution shall, in its practice, comply with relevant laws, regulations, and medical technical standards.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　医疗机构必须将《医疗机构执业许可证》、诊疗科目、诊疗时间和收费标准悬挂于明显处所。

**Article 25** A medical institution must display its Medical Institution Practice License, diagnosis and treatment subjects, consultation hours, and fee schedules in a conspicuous place.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　医疗机构必须按照核准登记或者备案的诊疗科目开展诊疗活动。

**Article 26** A medical institution must carry out diagnosis and treatment activities in accordance with the diagnosis and treatment subjects approved and registered or filed for record.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　医疗机构不得使用非卫生技术人员从事医疗卫生技术工作。

**Article 27** A medical institution shall not employ non-health technical personnel to engage in medical and health technical work.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　医疗机构应当加强对医务人员的医德教育。

**Article 28** A medical institution shall strengthen medical ethics education for its medical personnel.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　医疗机构工作人员上岗工作，必须佩带载有本人姓名、职务或者职称的标牌。

**Article 29** Staff members of a medical institution, when on duty, must wear nameplates bearing their own names, positions, or professional titles.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　医疗机构对危重病人应当立即抢救。对限于设备或者技术条件不能诊治的病人，应当及时转诊。

**Article 30** A medical institution shall immediately provide emergency treatment to critically ill patients. For patients who cannot be diagnosed or treated due to limited equipment or technical conditions, the institution shall transfer them in a timely manner.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　未经医师（士）亲自诊查病人，医疗机构不得出具疾病诊断书、健康证明书或者死亡证明书等证明文件；未经医师（士）、助产人员亲自接产，医疗机构不得出具出生证明书或者死产报告书。

**Article 31** Without a physician (or assistant physician) having personally examined a patient, a medical institution shall not issue certificates such as a disease diagnosis certificate, health certificate, or death certificate; without a physician (or assistant physician) or a midwife having personally delivered the baby, a medical institution shall not issue a birth certificate or a stillbirth report.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　医务人员在诊疗活动中应当向患者说明病情和医疗措施。需要实施手术、特殊检查、特殊治疗的，医务人员应当及时向患者具体说明医疗风险、替代医疗方案等情况，并取得其明确同意；不能或者不宜向患者说明的，应当向患者的近亲属说明，并取得其明确同意。因抢救生命垂危的患者等紧急情况，不能取得患者或者其近亲属意见的，经医疗机构负责人或者授权的负责人批准，可以立即实施相应的医疗措施。

**Article 32** Medical personnel shall, during diagnosis and treatment activities, explain the patient's condition and medical measures to the patient. Where surgery, special examination, or special treatment is required, medical personnel shall promptly explain to the patient in detail the medical risks, alternative medical plans, and other relevant information, and obtain the patient's explicit consent; if it is impossible or inappropriate to explain to the patient, the explanation shall be given to the patient's close relatives and their explicit consent shall be obtained. In emergency situations such as rescuing a patient in critical condition, where the consent of the patient or his or her close relatives cannot be obtained, the corresponding medical measures may be implemented immediately upon approval by the person in charge of the medical institution or an authorized person in charge.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　医疗机构发生医疗事故，按照国家有关规定处理。

**Article 33** If a medical accident occurs in a medical institution, it shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　医疗机构对传染病、精神病、职业病等患者的特殊诊治和处理，应当按照国家有关法律、法规的规定办理。

**Article 34** Special diagnosis, treatment, and handling of patients with infectious diseases, mental illnesses, occupational diseases, etc., by a medical institution shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions of relevant State laws and regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　医疗机构必须按照有关药品管理的法律、法规，加强药品管理。

**Article 35** A medical institution must strengthen drug management in accordance with laws and regulations on drug administration.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　医疗机构必须按照人民政府或者物价部门的有关规定收取医疗费用，详列细项，并出具收据。

**Article 36** A medical institution must charge medical fees in accordance with the relevant provisions of the people's government or the pricing department, list the items in detail, and issue receipts.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　医疗机构必须承担相应的预防保健工作，承担县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门委托的支援农村、指导基层医疗卫生工作等任务。

**Article 37** A medical institution must undertake corresponding prevention and health care work, and assume tasks such as supporting rural areas and guiding grassroots medical and health work as assigned by the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　发生重大灾害、事故、疾病流行或者其他意外情况时，医疗机构及其卫生技术人员必须服从县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门的调遣。

**Article 38** In the event of a major disaster, accident, epidemic, or other emergency, a medical institution and its health technical personnel must obey the deployment of the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门行使下列监督管理职权：  
（一）负责医疗机构的设置审批、执业登记、备案和校验；  
（二）对医疗机构的执业活动进行检查指导；  
（三）负责组织对医疗机构的评审；  
（四）对违反本条例的行为给予处罚。

**Article 39** The health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall exercise the following supervisory and administrative powers:  
(1) responsible for the approval of establishment, practice registration, filing for record, and verification of medical institutions;  
(2) inspecting and guiding the practice activities of medical institutions;  
(3) responsible for organizing the evaluation of medical institutions;  
(4) imposing penalties for violations of these Regulations.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家实行医疗机构评审制度，由专家组成的评审委员会按照医疗机构评审办法和评审标准，对医疗机构的执业活动、医疗服务质量等进行综合评价。  
医疗机构评审办法和评审标准由国务院卫生行政部门制定。

**Article 40** The State adopts a medical institution evaluation system, under which an evaluation committee composed of experts shall conduct a comprehensive assessment of the practice activities and medical service quality of medical institutions in accordance with the medical institution evaluation measures and evaluation standards.  
The medical institution evaluation measures and evaluation standards shall be formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门负责组织本行政区域医疗机构评审委员会。  
医疗机构评审委员会由医院管理、医学教育、医疗、医技、护理和财务等有关专家组成。评审委员会成员由县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门聘任。

**Article 41** The health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing the medical institution evaluation committee within its administrative region.  
The medical institution evaluation committee shall be composed of experts in hospital management, medical education, medical treatment, medical technology, nursing, finance, and other relevant fields. The members of the evaluation committee shall be appointed by the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门根据评审委员会的评审意见，对达到评审标准的医疗机构，发给评审合格证书；对未达到评审标准的医疗机构，提出处理意见。

**Article 42** The health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the evaluation opinions of the evaluation committee, issue a certificate of evaluation qualification to medical institutions that meet the evaluation standards; and put forward handling opinions for medical institutions that fail to meet the evaluation standards.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反[本条例第二十三条](#art-23)规定，未取得《医疗机构执业许可证》擅自执业的，依照《中华人民共和国基本医疗卫生与健康促进法》的规定予以处罚。  
违反[本条例第二十三条](#art-23)规定，诊所未经备案执业的，由县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门责令其改正，没收违法所得，并处3万元以下罚款；拒不改正的，责令其停止执业活动。

**Article 43** Where a person, in violation of Article 23 of these Regulations, engages in medical practice without obtaining the "Medical Institution Practice License", the person shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of the "Basic Medical and Health Promotion Law of the People's Republic of China".  
Where a clinic, in violation of Article 23 of these Regulations, engages in medical practice without filing for record, the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections, confiscate its illegal gains, and impose a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan; if it refuses to make corrections, shall order it to cease its practice activities.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反[本条例第二十一条](#art-21)规定，逾期不校验《医疗机构执业许可证》仍从事诊疗活动的，由县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门责令其限期补办校验手续；拒不校验的，吊销其《医疗机构执业许可证》。

**Article 44** Where a person, in violation of Article 21 of these Regulations, fails to renew the "Medical Institution Practice License" within the time limit and still engages in diagnosis and treatment activities, the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to go through the renewal procedures within a specified time limit; if it refuses to renew, shall revoke its "Medical Institution Practice License".

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反[本条例第二十二条](#art-22)规定，出卖、转让、出借《医疗机构执业许可证》的，依照《中华人民共和国基本医疗卫生与健康促进法》的规定予以处罚。

**Article 45** Where a person, in violation of Article 22 of these Regulations, sells, transfers, or lends the "Medical Institution Practice License", the person shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of the "Basic Medical and Health Promotion Law of the People's Republic of China".

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)规定，诊疗活动超出登记或者备案范围的，由县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门予以警告、责令其改正，没收违法所得，并可以根据情节处以1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销其《医疗机构执业许可证》或者责令其停止执业活动。

**Article 46** Where a person, in violation of Article 26 of these Regulations, engages in diagnosis and treatment activities beyond the registered or filed scope, the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall give a warning, order it to make corrections, confiscate its illegal gains, and may, depending on the circumstances, impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, shall revoke its "Medical Institution Practice License" or order it to cease its practice activities.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反[本条例第二十七条](#art-27)规定，使用非卫生技术人员从事医疗卫生技术工作的，由县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门责令其限期改正，并可以处以1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销其《医疗机构执业许可证》或者责令其停止执业活动。

**Article 47** Where a person, in violation of Article 27 of these Regulations, employs non-health technical personnel to engage in medical and health technical work, the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit, and may impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, shall revoke its "Medical Institution Practice License" or order it to cease its practice activities.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反[本条例第三十一条](#art-31)规定，出具虚假证明文件的，由县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门予以警告；对造成危害后果的，可以处以1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；对直接责任人员由所在单位或者上级机关给予行政处分。

**Article 48** Where a person, in violation of Article 31 of these Regulations, issues false certification documents, the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall give a warning; if harmful consequences are caused, the department may impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; and the directly responsible personnel shall be given administrative sanctions by the unit to which they belong or the competent authority at a higher level.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　没收的财物和罚款全部上交国库。

**Article 49** All confiscated property and fines shall be turned over to the State Treasury.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　当事人对行政处罚决定不服的，可以依照国家法律、法规的规定申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。当事人对罚款及没收药品、器械的处罚决定未在法定期限内申请复议或者提起诉讼又不履行的，县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门可以申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 50** If a party is dissatisfied with an administrative penalty decision, the party may apply for administrative reconsideration or institute an administrative action in accordance with the provisions of national laws and regulations. If a party neither applies for reconsideration nor institutes an action within the statutory time limit with respect to the penalty decision of fine and confiscation of drugs and instruments, and also fails to comply with the decision, the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level may apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　本条例实施前已经执业的医疗机构，应当在条例实施后的6个月内，按照本条例第三章的规定，补办登记手续，领取《医疗机构执业许可证》。

**Article 51** Medical institutions that were already practicing before the implementation of these Regulations shall, within six months after the implementation of these Regulations, go through the registration procedures retroactively in accordance with the provisions of Chapter III of these Regulations and obtain the "Medical Institution Practice License".

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　外国人在中华人民共和国境内开设医疗机构及香港、澳门、台湾居民在内地开设医疗机构的管理办法，由国务院卫生行政部门另行制定。

**Article 52** The administrative measures for foreigners to establish medical institutions within the territory of the People's Republic of China and for residents of Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan to establish medical institutions in the mainland shall be separately formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　本条例自1994年9月1日起施行。1951年政务院批准发布的《医院诊所管理暂行条例》同时废止。

**Article 53** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 1994. The "Provisional Regulations on the Administration of Hospitals and Clinics" approved and promulgated by the Government Administration Council in 1951 shall be abolished simultaneously.
