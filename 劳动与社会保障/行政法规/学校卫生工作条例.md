# 学校卫生工作条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：1990-06-04  
**生效日期**：1990-06-04

> (1990年4月25日国务院批准　1990年6月4日国家教育委员会令第10号、卫生部令第1号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为加强学校卫生工作，提高学生的健康水平，制定本条例。 第二条　学校卫生工作的主要任务是：监测学生健康状况；对学生进行健康教育，培养学生良好的卫生习惯；改善学校卫生环境和教学卫生条件；加强对传染病、学生常见病的预防和治疗。 第三条　本条例所称的学校，是指普通中小学、农业中学、职业中学、中等专业学校、技工学校、普通高等学校。 第四条　教育行政部门负责学校卫生工作的行政管理。卫生行政部门负责对学校卫生工作的监督指导。 第二章　学校卫生工作要求 第五条　学校应当合理安排学生的学习时间。学生每日学习时间(包括自习)，小学不超过六小时，中学不超过八小时，大学不超过十小时。 学校或者教师不得以任何理由和方式，增加授课时间和作业量，加重学生学习负担。 第六条　学校教学建筑、环境噪声、室内微小气候、采光、照明等环境质量以及黑板、课桌椅的设置应当符合国家有关标准。 新建、改建、扩建校舍，其选址、设计应当符合国家的卫生标准，并取得当地卫生行政部门的许可。竣工验收应当有当地卫生行政部门参加。 第七条　学校应当按照有关规定为学生设置厕所和洗手设施。寄宿制学校应当为学生提供相应的洗漱、洗澡等卫生设施。 学校应当为学生提供充足的符合卫生标准的饮用水。 第八条　学校应当建立卫生制度，加强对学生个人卫生、环境卫生以及教室、宿舍卫生的管理。 第九条　学校应当认真贯彻执行食品卫生法律、法规，加强饮食卫生管理，办好学生膳食，加强营养指导。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为加强学校卫生工作，提高学生的健康水平，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen school hygiene work and improve the health level of students.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　学校卫生工作的主要任务是：监测学生健康状况；对学生进行健康教育，培养学生良好的卫生习惯；改善学校卫生环境和教学卫生条件；加强对传染病、学生常见病的预防和治疗。

**Article 2** The main tasks of school hygiene work are: to monitor students' health status; to provide health education to students and cultivate their good hygiene habits; to improve the school hygiene environment and teaching hygiene conditions; to strengthen the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases and common student diseases.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称的学校，是指普通中小学、农业中学、职业中学、中等专业学校、技工学校、普通高等学校。

**Article 3** Schools as referred to in these Regulations mean ordinary primary and secondary schools, agricultural secondary schools, vocational secondary schools, specialized secondary schools, technical schools, and ordinary institutions of higher education.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　教育行政部门负责学校卫生工作的行政管理。卫生行政部门负责对学校卫生工作的监督指导。

**Article 4** The education administrative department shall be responsible for the administrative management of school hygiene work. The health administrative department shall be responsible for the supervision and guidance of school hygiene work.

### 第二章　学校卫生工作要求

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　学校应当合理安排学生的学习时间。学生每日学习时间(包括自习)，小学不超过六小时，中学不超过八小时，大学不超过十小时。  
学校或者教师不得以任何理由和方式，增加授课时间和作业量，加重学生学习负担。

**Article 5** A school shall reasonably arrange students' study time. The daily study time of students (including self-study) shall not exceed six hours for primary school, eight hours for secondary school, and ten hours for university.  
No school or teacher shall increase teaching time and homework load in any reason or manner, so as to aggravate students' study burden.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　学校教学建筑、环境噪声、室内微小气候、采光、照明等环境质量以及黑板、课桌椅的设置应当符合国家有关标准。  
新建、改建、扩建校舍，其选址、设计应当符合国家的卫生标准，并取得当地卫生行政部门的许可。竣工验收应当有当地卫生行政部门参加。

**Article 6** The environmental quality of school teaching buildings, ambient noise, indoor microclimate, daylighting, lighting, and the arrangement of blackboards and desks and chairs shall conform to the relevant national standards.  
For the construction, renovation, or expansion of school buildings, the site selection and design shall conform to the national hygiene standards and obtain permission from the local health administrative department. The local health administrative department shall participate in the completion acceptance.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　学校应当按照有关规定为学生设置厕所和洗手设施。寄宿制学校应当为学生提供相应的洗漱、洗澡等卫生设施。  
学校应当为学生提供充足的符合卫生标准的饮用水。

**Article 7** A school shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, install toilets and hand-washing facilities for students. Boarding schools shall provide students with corresponding sanitary facilities such as washing and bathing.  
A school shall provide students with sufficient drinking water that meets hygiene standards.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　学校应当建立卫生制度，加强对学生个人卫生、环境卫生以及教室、宿舍卫生的管理。

**Article 8** A school shall establish a hygiene system and strengthen the management of students' personal hygiene, environmental hygiene, and the hygiene of classrooms and dormitories.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　学校应当认真贯彻执行食品卫生法律、法规，加强饮食卫生管理，办好学生膳食，加强营养指导。

**Article 9** A school shall earnestly implement the food hygiene laws and regulations, strengthen the management of food hygiene, properly manage students' meals, and strengthen nutritional guidance.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　学校体育场地和器材应当符合卫生和安全要求。运动项目和运动强度应当适合学生的生理承受能力和体质健康状况，防止发生伤害事故。

**Article 10** A school's sports venues and equipment shall meet hygiene and safety requirements. Sports events and exercise intensity shall be suitable for students' physiological endurance capacity and physical health status, so as to prevent injury accidents.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　学校应当根据学生的年龄，组织学生参加适当的劳动，并对参加劳动的学生，进行安全教育，提供必要的安全和卫生防护措施。  
普通中小学校组织学生参加劳动，不得让学生接触有毒有害物质或者从事不安全工种的作业，不得让学生参加夜班劳动。  
普通高等学校、中等专业学校、技工学校、农业中学、职业中学组织学生参加生产劳动，接触有毒有害物质的，按照国家有关规定，提供保健待遇。学校应当定期对他们进行体格检查，加强卫生防护。

**Article 11** A school shall, based on the age of students, organize students to participate in appropriate labor, and provide safety education and necessary safety and hygiene protection measures for students participating in labor.  
When ordinary primary and secondary schools organize students to participate in labor, they shall not allow students to come into contact with toxic or harmful substances or engage in unsafe types of work, nor shall they allow students to participate in night shift labor.  
When ordinary institutions of higher education, specialized secondary schools, technical schools, agricultural secondary schools, and vocational secondary schools organize students to participate in production labor and come into contact with toxic or harmful substances, they shall provide health care benefits in accordance with relevant state provisions. The school shall conduct regular physical examinations for such students and strengthen hygiene protection.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　学校在安排体育课以及劳动等体力活动时，应当注意女学生的生理特点，给予必要的照顾。

**Article 12** When arranging physical education classes and physical activities such as labor, a school shall pay attention to the physiological characteristics of female students and provide necessary care.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　学校应当把健康教育纳入教学计划。普通中小学必须开设健康教育课，普通高等学校、中等专业学校、技工学校、农业中学、职业中学应当开设健康教育选修课或者讲座。  
学校应当开展学生健康咨询活动。

**Article 13** A school shall incorporate health education into the teaching plan. Ordinary primary and secondary schools must offer health education courses; ordinary institutions of higher education, specialized secondary schools, technical schools, agricultural secondary schools, and vocational secondary schools shall offer elective courses or lectures on health education.  
A school shall carry out student health consultation activities.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　学校应当建立学生健康管理制度。根据条件定期对学生进行体格检查，建立学生体质健康卡片，纳入学生档案。  
学校对体格检查中发现学生有器质性疾病的，应当配合学生家长做好转诊治疗。  
学校对残疾、体弱学生，应当加强医学照顾和心理卫生工作。

**Article 14** A school shall establish a student health management system. It shall conduct regular physical examinations of students according to conditions, establish student physical health cards, and include them in student files.  
If a school discovers that a student has an organic disease during the physical examination, it shall cooperate with the student's parents to arrange referral treatment.  
A school shall strengthen medical care and mental health work for disabled and frail students.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　学校应当配备可以处理一般伤病事故的医疗用品。

**Article 15** A school shall be equipped with medical supplies capable of handling general injury and illness incidents.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　学校应当积极做好近视眼、弱视、沙眼、龋齿、寄生虫、营养不良、贫血、脊柱弯曲、神经衰弱等学生常见疾病的群体预防和矫治工作。

**Article 16** A school shall actively carry out group prevention and correction work for common student diseases such as myopia, amblyopia, trachoma, dental caries, parasitic diseases, malnutrition, anemia, spinal curvature, and neurasthenia.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　学校应当认真贯彻执行传染病防治法律、法规，做好急、慢性传染病的预防和控制管理工作，同时做好地方病的预防和控制管理工作。

**Article 17** A school shall earnestly implement the laws and regulations on the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases, perform well in the prevention and control management of acute and chronic infectious diseases, and at the same time perform well in the prevention and control management of endemic diseases.

### 第三章　学校卫生工作管理

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　各级教育行政部门应当把学校卫生工作纳入学校工作计划，作为考评学校工作的一项内容。

**Article 18** Education administrative departments at all levels shall incorporate school hygiene work into school work plans and regard it as an element in the assessment of school work.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　普通高等学校、中等专业学校、技工学校和规模较大的农业中学、职业中学、普通中小学，可以设立卫生管理机构，管理学校的卫生工作。

**Article 19** Ordinary institutions of higher education, specialized secondary schools, technical schools, and larger agricultural secondary schools, vocational secondary schools, and ordinary primary and secondary schools may establish hygiene management institutions to manage the school's hygiene work.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　普通高等学校设校医院或者卫生科。校医院应当设保健科(室)，负责师生的卫生保健工作。  
城市普通中小学、农村中心小学和普通中学设卫生室，按学生人数六百比一的比例配备专职卫生技术人员。  
中等专业学校、技工学校、农业中学、职业中学，可以根据需要，配备专职卫生技术人员。  
学生人数不足六百人的学校，可以配备专职或者兼职保健教师，开展学校卫生工作。

**Article 20** Ordinary institutions of higher education shall establish a school hospital or a health section. The school hospital shall establish a health care section (office) responsible for the health care of teachers and students.  
Urban ordinary primary and secondary schools, rural central primary schools, and ordinary secondary schools shall set up health rooms and be staffed with full-time health technical personnel at a ratio of one per six hundred students.  
Specialized secondary schools, technical schools, agricultural secondary schools, and vocational secondary schools may, as needed, be staffed with full-time health technical personnel.  
Schools with less than six hundred students may be staffed with full-time or part-time health care teachers to carry out school hygiene work.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　经本地区卫生行政部门批准，可以成立区域性的中小学生卫生保健机构。  
区域性的中小学生卫生保健机构的主要任务是：  
(一)调查研究本地区中小学生体质健康状况；  
(二)开展中小学生常见疾病的预防与矫治；  
(三)开展中小学卫生技术人员的技术培训和业务指导。

**Article 21** With the approval of the local health administration department, regional health care institutions for primary and secondary school students may be established.  
The main tasks of regional health care institutions for primary and secondary school students are:  
(1) to investigate and study the physical health status of primary and secondary school students in the region;  
(2) to carry out prevention and treatment of common diseases among primary and secondary school students;  
(3) to provide technical training and professional guidance for health technicians in primary and secondary schools.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　学校卫生技术人员的专业技术职称考核、评定，按照卫生、教育行政部门制定的考核标准和办法，由教育行政部门组织实施。  
学校卫生技术人员按照国家有关规定，享受卫生保健津贴。

**Article 22** The assessment and evaluation of professional technical titles for school health technicians shall be organized and implemented by the education administration department in accordance with the assessment standards and methods formulated by the health and education administration departments.  
School health technicians shall enjoy health care subsidies in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　教育行政部门应当将培养学校卫生技术人员的工作列入招生计划，并通过各种教育形式为学校卫生技术人员和保健教师提供进修机会。

**Article 23** The education administration department shall include the training of school health technicians in its enrollment plan and provide opportunities for further study for school health technicians and health care teachers through various forms of education.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　各级教育行政部门和学校应当将学校卫生经费纳入核定的年度教育经费预算。

**Article 24** Education administration departments at all levels and schools shall include school health expenditures in the approved annual education budget.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　各级卫生行政部门应当组织医疗单位和专业防治机构对学生进行健康检查、传染病防治和常见病矫治，接受转诊治疗。

**Article 25** Health administration departments at all levels shall organize medical units and specialized prevention and treatment institutions to conduct health examinations, prevent and treat infectious diseases, and treat common diseases among students, and accept referrals for treatment.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　各级卫生防疫站，对学校卫生工作承担下列任务：  
(一)实施学校卫生监测，掌握本地区学生生长发育和健康状况，掌握学生常见病、传染病、地方病动态；  
(二)制定学生常见病、传染病、地方病的防治计划；  
(三)对本地区学校卫生工作进行技术指导；  
(四)开展学校卫生服务。

**Article 26** Health and epidemic prevention stations at all levels shall undertake the following tasks in respect of school health work:  
(1) to implement school health monitoring, keep abreast of the growth, development and health status of students in the region, and keep abreast of the trends of common diseases, infectious diseases and endemic diseases among students;  
(2) to formulate plans for the prevention and treatment of common diseases, infectious diseases and endemic diseases among students;  
(3) to provide technical guidance for school health work in the region;  
(4) to provide school health services.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　供学生使用的文具、娱乐器具、保健用品，必须符合国家有关卫生标准。

**Article 27** Stationery, recreational appliances and health care products for use by students must conform to the relevant national hygiene standards.

### 第四章　学校卫生工作监督

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县以上卫生行政部门对学校卫生工作行使监督职权。其职责是：  
(一)对新建、改建、扩建校舍的选址、设计实行卫生监督；  
(二)对学校内影响学生健康的学习、生活、劳动、环境、食品等方面的卫生和传染病防治工作实行卫生监督；  
(三)对学生使用的文具、娱乐器具、保健用品实行卫生监督。  
国务院卫生行政部门可以委托国务院其他有关部门的卫生主管机构，在本系统内对前款所列第(一)、(二)项职责行使学校卫生监督职权。

**Article 28** Health administration departments at or above the county level shall exercise supervisory authority over school health work. Their duties are:  
(1) to exercise health supervision over the site selection and design of new construction, reconstruction and expansion of school buildings;  
(2) to exercise health supervision over learning, living, labor, environment, food and other aspects affecting students' health within schools as well as the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases;  
(3) to exercise health supervision over stationery, recreational appliances and health care products for use by students.  
The health administration department of the State Council may entrust the health regulatory authorities of other relevant departments of the State Council to exercise, within their respective systems, the school health supervision duties specified in items (1) and (2) of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　行使学校卫生监督职权的机构设立学校卫生监督员，由省级以上卫生行政部门聘任并发给学校卫生监督员证书。  
学校卫生监督员执行卫生行政部门或者其他有关部门卫生主管机构交付的学校卫生监督任务。

**Article 29** Institutions exercising school health supervision authority shall establish school health supervisors, who shall be appointed by health administration departments at or above the provincial level and issued with school health supervisor certificates.  
School health supervisors shall perform the school health supervision tasks assigned by health administration departments or health regulatory authorities of other relevant departments.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　学校卫生监督员在执行任务时应出示证件。  
学校卫生监督员在进行卫生监督时，有权查阅与卫生监督有关的资料，搜集与卫生监督有关的情况，被监督的单位或者个人应当给予配合。学校卫生监督员对所掌握的资料、情况负有保密责任。

**Article 30** School health supervisors shall present their identification when performing tasks.  
When conducting health supervision, school health supervisors shall have the right to consult materials related to health supervision and collect information related to health supervision, and the unit or individual under supervision shall provide cooperation. School health supervisors shall bear the responsibility of maintaining confidentiality regarding the materials and information obtained.

### 第五章　奖励与处罚

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对在学校卫生工作中成绩显著的单位或者个人，各级教育、卫生行政部门和学校应当给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 31** Units or individuals that have made outstanding achievements in school health work shall be commended and rewarded by education and health administration departments at all levels and by schools.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　违反[本条例第六条](#art-6)第二款规定，未经卫生行政部门许可新建、改建、扩建校舍的，由卫生行政部门对直接责任单位或者个人给予警告、责令停止施工或者限期改建。

**Article 32** Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 6, paragraph 2 of these Regulations, school buildings are constructed, reconstructed or expanded without the permission of the health administration department, the health administration department shall give a warning to the unit or individual directly responsible and order the cessation of construction or reconstruction within a prescribed time limit.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违反[本条例第六条](#art-6)第一款、第七条和第十条规定的，由卫生行政部门对直接责任单位或者个人给予警告并责令限期改进。情节严重的，可以同时建议教育行政部门给予行政处分。

**Article 33** Where the provisions of Article 6, paragraph 1, Article 7 and Article 10 of these Regulations are violated, the health administration department shall give a warning to the unit or individual directly responsible and order correction within a prescribed time limit. If the circumstances are serious, it may also recommend that the education administration department impose administrative sanctions.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反[本条例第十一条](#art-11)规定，致使学生健康受到损害的，由卫生行政部门对直接责任单位或者个人给予警告，责令限期改进。

**Article 34** Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 11 of these Regulations, the health of students is harmed, the health administration department shall give a warning to the unit or individual directly responsible and order correction within a prescribed time limit.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反[本条例第二十七条](#art-27)规定的，由卫生行政部门对直接责任单位或者个人给予警告。情节严重的，可以会同工商行政部门没收其不符合国家有关卫生标准的物品，并处以非法所得两倍以下的罚款。

**Article 35** Where the provisions of Article 27 of these Regulations are violated, the health administration department shall give a warning to the unit or individual directly responsible. If the circumstances are serious, it may, together with the administration for industry and commerce, confiscate the articles that do not conform to the relevant national hygiene standards and impose a fine of not more than two times the illegal gains.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　拒绝或者妨碍学校卫生监督员依照本条例实施卫生监督的，由卫生行政部门对直接责任单位或者个人给予警告。情节严重的，可以建议教育行政部门给予行政处分或者处以二百元以下的罚款。

**Article 36** Where a school health supervisor is refused or obstructed in carrying out health supervision in accordance with these Regulations, the health administration department shall give a warning to the unit or individual directly responsible. If the circumstances are serious, it may recommend that the education administration department impose administrative sanctions or impose a fine of not more than 200 yuan.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　当事人对没收、罚款的行政处罚不服的，可以在接到处罚决定书之日起十五日内，向作出处罚决定机关的上一级机关申请复议，也可以直接向人民法院起诉。对复议决定不服的，可以在接到复议决定之日起十五日内，向人民法院起诉。对罚款决定不履行又逾期不起诉的，由作出处罚决定的机关申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 37** If a party disagrees with the administrative penalty of confiscation or fine, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the decision on penalty, apply for reconsideration to the organ at the next higher level of the organ that made the penalty decision, or may directly file a prosecution with the People's Court. If it disagrees with the reconsideration decision, it may file a prosecution with the People's Court within 15 days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration decision. If the decision on fine is not complied with and no prosecution is filed within the time limit, the organ that made the penalty decision shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　学校卫生监督办法、学校卫生标准由卫生部会同国家教育委员会制定。

**Article 38** Measures for school health supervision and school hygiene standards shall be formulated by the Ministry of Health in conjunction with the State Education Commission.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　贫困县不能全部适用[本条例第六条](#art-6)第一款和第七条规定的，可以由所在省、自治区的教育、卫生行政部门制定变通的规定。变通的规定，应当报送国家教育委员会、卫生部备案。

**Article 39** Where a poor county is unable to fully apply the provisions of Article 6, paragraph 1 and Article 7 of these Regulations, the education and health administration departments of the province or autonomous region concerned may formulate alternative provisions. Such alternative provisions shall be submitted to the State Education Commission and the Ministry of Health for the record.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　本条例由国家教育委员会、卫生部负责解释。

**Article 40** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the State Education Commission and the Ministry of Health.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　本条例自发布之日起施行。原教育部、卫生部一九七九年十二月六日颁布的《中、小学卫生工作暂行规定(草案)》和一九八○年八月二十六日颁布的《高等学校卫生工作暂行规定(草案)》同时废止。

**Article 41** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Interim Provisions on Sanitary Work in Primary and Secondary Schools (Draft) promulgated by the former Ministry of Education and the Ministry of Health on December 6, 1979 and the Interim Provisions on Sanitary Work in Institutions of Higher Learning (Draft) promulgated on August 26, 1980 shall be abolished simultaneously.
