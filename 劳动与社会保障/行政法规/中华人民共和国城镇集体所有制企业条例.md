# 中华人民共和国城镇集体所有制企业条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2016-02-06  
**生效日期**：1992-01-01

> (1991年9月9日中华人民共和国国务院令第88号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了保障城镇集体所有制经济的巩固和发展，明确城镇集体所有制企业的权利和义务，维护其合法权益，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于城镇的各种行业、各种组织形式的集体所有制企业，但乡村农民集体举办的企业除外。 第三条　城镇集体所有制经济是我国社会主义公有制经济的一个基本组成部分，国家鼓励和扶持城镇集体所有制经济的发展。 第四条　城镇集体所有制企业(以下简称集体企业)是财产属于劳动群众集体所有、实行共同劳动、在分配方式上以按劳分配为主体的社会主义经济组织。 前款所称劳动群众集体所有，应当符合下列中任一项的规定： (一)本集体企业的劳动群众集体所有； (二)集体企业的联合经济组织范围内的劳动群众集体所有； (三)投资主体为两个或者两个以上的集体企业，其中前(一)、(二)项劳动群众集体所有的财产应当占主导地位。本项所称主导地位，是指劳动群众集体所有的财产占企业全部财产的比例，一般情况下应不低于51%，特殊情况经过原审批部门批准，可以适当降低。 第五条　集体企业应当遵循的原则是：自愿组合、自筹资金，独立核算、自负盈亏，自主经营、民主管理，集体积累、自主支配，按劳分配、入股分红。 集体企业应当发扬艰苦奋斗、勤俭建国的精神，走互助合作、共同富裕的道路。 第六条　集体企业依法取得法人资格，以其全部财产独立承担民事责任。 集体企业的财产及其合法权益受国家法律保护，不受侵犯。 第七条　集体企业的任务是：根据市场和社会需求，在国家计划指导下，发展商品生产，扩大商品经营，开展社会服务，创造财富，增加积累，不断提高经济效益和社会效益，繁荣社会主义经济。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障城镇集体所有制经济的巩固和发展，明确城镇集体所有制企业的权利和义务，维护其合法权益，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to safeguard the consolidation and development of the urban collective ownership economy, clarify the rights and obligations of urban collective ownership enterprises, and protect their legitimate rights and interests.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于城镇的各种行业、各种组织形式的集体所有制企业，但乡村农民集体举办的企业除外。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to collective ownership enterprises of various industries and organizational forms in cities and towns, except for enterprises collectively run by rural farmers.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　城镇集体所有制经济是我国社会主义公有制经济的一个基本组成部分，国家鼓励和扶持城镇集体所有制经济的发展。

**Article 3** The urban collective ownership economy is a basic component of the socialist public ownership economy of our country. The State encourages and supports the development of the urban collective ownership economy.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　城镇集体所有制企业(以下简称集体企业)是财产属于劳动群众集体所有、实行共同劳动、在分配方式上以按劳分配为主体的社会主义经济组织。  
前款所称劳动群众集体所有，应当符合下列中任一项的规定：  
(一)本集体企业的劳动群众集体所有；  
(二)集体企业的联合经济组织范围内的劳动群众集体所有；  
(三)投资主体为两个或者两个以上的集体企业，其中前(一)、(二)项劳动群众集体所有的财产应当占主导地位。本项所称主导地位，是指劳动群众集体所有的财产占企业全部财产的比例，一般情况下应不低于51%，特殊情况经过原审批部门批准，可以适当降低。

**Article 4** An urban collective ownership enterprise (hereinafter referred to as a collective enterprise) is a socialist economic organization in which property is owned collectively by the working masses, joint labor is carried out, and distribution is mainly according to work.  
The "collective ownership by the working masses" mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall meet any one of the following provisions:  
(1) Collective ownership by the working masses of the collective enterprise itself;  
(2) Collective ownership by the working masses within the scope of the joint economic organization of collective enterprises;  
(3) Where the investors are two or more collective enterprises, the property collectively owned by the working masses as specified in items (1) and (2) shall occupy a dominant position. The term "dominant position" in this item means that the proportion of property collectively owned by the working masses in the total property of the enterprise shall generally not be less than 51%. Under special circumstances, upon approval by the original examination and approval department, it may be appropriately reduced.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　集体企业应当遵循的原则是：自愿组合、自筹资金，独立核算、自负盈亏，自主经营、民主管理，集体积累、自主支配，按劳分配、入股分红。  
集体企业应当发扬艰苦奋斗、勤俭建国的精神，走互助合作、共同富裕的道路。

**Article 5** A collective enterprise shall follow the principles of voluntary association, self-raised funds, independent accounting, self-bearing profits and losses, independent operation, democratic management, collective accumulation, autonomous allocation, distribution according to work, and shareholding dividends.  
A collective enterprise shall carry forward the spirit of hard work and frugality in building the country, and follow the path of mutual assistance and cooperation towards common prosperity.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　集体企业依法取得法人资格，以其全部财产独立承担民事责任。  
集体企业的财产及其合法权益受国家法律保护，不受侵犯。

**Article 6** A collective enterprise shall obtain the status of a legal person according to law and independently bear civil liability with all its property.  
The property and legitimate rights and interests of a collective enterprise shall be protected by State laws and shall not be infringed upon.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　集体企业的任务是：根据市场和社会需求，在国家计划指导下，发展商品生产，扩大商品经营，开展社会服务，创造财富，增加积累，不断提高经济效益和社会效益，繁荣社会主义经济。

**Article 7** The tasks of a collective enterprise are: to develop commodity production, expand commodity operation, carry out social services, create wealth, increase accumulation, continuously improve economic and social benefits, and prosper the socialist economy in accordance with market and social needs under the guidance of State plans.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　集体企业的职工是企业的主人，依照法律、法规和集体企业章程行使管理企业的权力。集体企业职工的合法权益受法律保护。

**Article 8** The staff and workers of a collective enterprise are the masters of the enterprise and shall exercise the power to manage the enterprise in accordance with laws, regulations and the articles of association of the collective enterprise. The legitimate rights and interests of the staff and workers of a collective enterprise shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　集体企业依照法律规定实行民主管理。职工(代表)大会是集体企业的权力机构，由其选举和罢免企业管理人员，决定经营管理的重大问题。  
集体企业实行厂长(经理)负责制。  
集体企业职工的民主管理权和厂长(经理)依法行使职权，均受法律保护。

**Article 9** A collective enterprise shall practice democratic management in accordance with the provisions of laws. The workers' (representatives) congress is the organ of power of the collective enterprise, which elects and removes the management personnel of the enterprise and decides on major issues of operation and management.  
The collective enterprise shall implement the system of factory director (manager) responsibility.  
The democratic management right of the staff and workers of a collective enterprise and the exercise of functions and powers by the factory director (manager) according to law shall both be protected by law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　中国共产党在集体企业的基层组织是集体企业的政治领导核心，领导企业的思想政治工作，保证监督党和国家的方针、政策在本企业的贯彻执行。

**Article 10** The grassroots organization of the Communist Party of China in a collective enterprise is the political leadership core of the collective enterprise, leading the ideological and political work of the enterprise, and ensuring and supervising the implementation of the Party's and the State's principles and policies in the enterprise.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　集体企业的工会维护职工的合法权益，依法独立自主地开展工作，组织职工参加民主管理和民主监督。

**Article 11** The trade union of a collective enterprise shall safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of the employees, carry out work independently and autonomously according to law, and organize the employees to participate in democratic management and democratic supervision.

### 第二章　集体企业的设立、变更和终止

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　集体企业的设立必须具备下列条件：  
(一)有企业名称、组织机构和企业章程；  
(二)有固定的生产经营场所、必要的设施并符合规定的安全卫生条件；  
(三)有符合国家规定并与其生产经营和服务规模相适应的资金数额和从业人员；  
(四)有明确的经营范围；  
(五)能够独立承担民事责任；  
(六)法律、法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 12** The establishment of a collective enterprise must meet the following conditions:  
(1) having an enterprise name, organizational structure, and articles of association;  
(2) having a fixed place of production and business operation, necessary facilities, and meeting the prescribed safety and health conditions;  
(3) having a capital amount and employees that conform to the provisions of the State and are commensurate with the scale of its production, business operation, and services;  
(4) having a clear business scope;  
(5) being able to independently bear civil liability;  
(6) other conditions as provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　集体企业章程必须载明下列事项：  
(一)企业名称和住所；  
(二)经营范围和经营方式；  
(三)注册资金；  
(四)资金来源和投资方式；  
(五)收入分配方式；  
(六)组织机构及其职权和议事规则；  
(七)职工加入和退出企业的条件和程序；  
(八)职工的权利和义务；  
(九)法定代表人的产生程序及其职权范围；  
(十)企业终止的条件和程序；  
(十一)章程的修订程序；  
(十二)章程订立日期；  
(十三)需要明确的其他事项。

**Article 13** The articles of association of a collective enterprise must specify the following matters:  
(1) name and domicile of the enterprise;  
(2) business scope and mode of business operation;  
(3) registered capital;  
(4) sources of funds and investment methods;  
(5) method of income distribution;  
(6) organizational structure and its functions, powers, and rules of procedure;  
(7) conditions and procedures for employees to join and withdraw from the enterprise;  
(8) rights and obligations of employees;  
(9) procedure for the selection of the legal representative and its scope of functions and powers;  
(10) conditions and procedures for termination of the enterprise;  
(11) procedures for amending the articles of association;  
(12) date of adoption of the articles of association;  
(13) other matters that need to be specified.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　设立集体企业应当经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定的审批部门批准。  
设立集体企业的审批部门，法律、法规有专门规定的，从其规定。  
集体企业应当在核准登记的经营范围内从事生产经营活动。

**Article 14** The establishment of a collective enterprise shall be subject to approval by the examination and approval department designated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
Where there are special provisions in laws and regulations regarding the examination and approval department for the establishment of a collective enterprise, such provisions shall prevail.  
A collective enterprise shall engage in production and business operations within its approved and registered business scope.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　集体企业的合并、分立、停业、迁移或者主要登记事项的变更，必须符合国家的有关规定，由企业提出申请，报经原审批部门批准。

**Article 15** The merger, division, suspension of business, relocation, or change of major registration items of a collective enterprise must comply with the relevant provisions of the State, and the enterprise shall submit an application and report to the original examination and approval department for approval.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　集体企业的合并和分立，应当遵照自愿平等的原则，由有关各方依法签订协议，处理好债权债务、其他财产关系和遗留问题，妥善安置企业人员。  
合并、分立前的集体企业的权利和义务，由合并、分立后的法人享有和承担。

**Article 16** The merger and division of a collective enterprise shall follow the principle of voluntariness and equality, and the relevant parties shall, according to law, sign an agreement to properly handle the creditor's rights, debts, other property relations and leftover problems, and properly arrange the enterprise personnel.  
The rights and obligations of the collective enterprise before merger or division shall be enjoyed and assumed by the legal person after merger or division.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　集体企业有下列原因之一的，应当予以终止：  
(一)企业无法继续经营而申请解散，经原审批部门批准；  
(二)依法被撤销；  
(三)依法宣告破产；  
(四)其他原因。

**Article 17** A collective enterprise shall be terminated for any of the following reasons:  
(1) the enterprise applies for dissolution due to inability to continue operations, and the application is approved by the original examination and approval department;  
(2) it is revoked according to law;  
(3) it is declared bankrupt according to law;  
(4) other reasons.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　集体企业终止，应当依照国家有关规定清算企业财产。企业财产按下列顺序清偿各种债务和费用：  
(一)清算工作所需各项费用；  
(二)所欠职工工资和劳动保险费用；  
(三)所欠税款；  
(四)所欠银行和信用合作社贷款以及其他债务。  
不足清偿同一顺序的清偿要求的，按照比例分配。

**Article 18** Upon termination, a collective enterprise shall liquidate its property in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. The enterprise property shall be used to satisfy various debts and expenses in the following order:  
(1) various expenses necessary for the liquidation work;  
(2) wages and labor insurance expenses owed to employees;  
(3) taxes owed;  
(4) loans owed to banks and credit cooperatives, and other debts.  
If the property is insufficient to satisfy the claims of the same priority, distribution shall be made on a pro rata basis.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　集体企业财产清算后的剩余财产，按照下列办法处理：  
(一)有国家、本企业外的单位和个人以及本企业职工个人投资入股的，应当依照其投资入股金额占企业总资产的比例，从企业剩余财产中按相同的比例偿还；  
(二)其余财产，由企业上级管理机构作为该企业职工待业和养老救济、就业安置和职业培训等费用，专款专用，不得挪作他用。

**Article 19** The residual property after liquidation of a collective enterprise shall be dealt with as follows:  
(1) where the State, entities and individuals outside the enterprise, and individual employees of the enterprise have made investments in shares, such investments shall be repaid from the residual property in the same proportion as the amount of their investment in shares bears to the total assets of the enterprise;  
(2) the remaining property shall be used by the enterprise's superior management agency as expenses for the employees' unemployment and old-age relief, employment placement, and vocational training, and shall be used only for such specific purposes and shall not be misappropriated for other purposes.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　集体企业终止，必须依照《中华人民共和国企业法人登记管理条例》的规定办理注销登记并公告。

**Article 20** Upon termination, a collective enterprise must undergo cancellation registration and make a public announcement in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Registration of Enterprise Legal Persons.

### 第三章　集体企业的权利和义务

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　集体企业在国家法律、法规的规定范围内享有下列权利：  
(一)对其全部财产享有占有、使用、收益和处分的权利，拒绝任何形式的平调；  
(二)自主安排生产、经营、服务活动；  
(三)除国家规定由物价部门和有关主管部门控制价格的以外，企业有权自行确定产品价格、劳务价格；  
(四)企业有权依照国家规定与外商谈判并签订合同，提取和使用分成的外汇收入；  
(五)依照国家信贷政策的规定向有关专业银行申请贷款；  
(六)依照国家规定确定适合本企业情况的经济责任制形式、工资形式和奖金、分红办法；  
(七)享受国家政策规定的各种优惠待遇；  
(八)吸收职工和其他企业、事业单位、个人集资入股，与其他企业、事业单位联营，向其他企业、事业单位投资，持有其他企业的股份；  
(九)按照国家规定决定本企业的机构设置、人员编制、劳动组织形式和用工办法，录用和辞退职工；  
(十)奖惩职工。

**Article 21** Collective enterprises shall, within the scope prescribed by national laws and regulations, enjoy the following rights:  
(1) the right to possess, use, benefit from, and dispose of all their property, and to refuse any form of unilateral transfer;  
(2) autonomously arrange production, operation, and service activities;  
(3) except for those prices controlled by the pricing authorities and relevant competent departments as stipulated by the State, enterprises shall have the right to determine product prices and service prices on their own;  
(4) enterprises shall have the right to negotiate and sign contracts with foreign businessmen in accordance with national regulations, and to withdraw and use the foreign exchange income retained;  
(5) apply for loans from relevant specialized banks in accordance with national credit policies;  
(6) determine, in accordance with national regulations, the form of economic responsibility system, wage form, bonus and profit distribution method suitable for their own circumstances;  
(7) enjoy various preferential treatments stipulated by national policies;  
(8) absorb funds raised by employees and other enterprises, institutions, and individuals for shareholding, jointly operate with other enterprises and institutions, invest in other enterprises and institutions, and hold shares of other enterprises;  
(9) decide on the organization setup, staffing, labor organization form, and employment methods of the enterprise in accordance with national regulations, and hire and dismiss employees;  
(10) reward and punish employees.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　集体企业应当承担下列义务：  
(一)遵守国家法律、法规，接受国家计划指导；  
(二)依法缴纳税金和交纳费用；  
(三)依法履行合同；  
(四)改善经营管理，推进技术进步，提高经济效益；  
(五)保证产品质量和服务质量，对用户和消费者负责；  
(六)贯彻安全生产制度，落实劳动保护和环境保护措施；  
(七)做好企业内部的安全保卫工作；  
(八)维护职工合法权益，尊重职工的民主管理权利，改善劳动条件，做好计划生育工作，提高职工物质文化生活水平；  
(九)加强对职工的思想政治教育、法制教育、国防教育、科学文化教育和技术业务培训，提高职工队伍素质。

**Article 22** Collective enterprises shall undertake the following obligations:  
(1) comply with national laws and regulations, and accept guidance from national plans;  
(2) pay taxes and fees according to law;  
(3) perform contracts according to law;  
(4) improve business management, promote technological progress, and enhance economic benefits;  
(5) ensure product quality and service quality, and be responsible to users and consumers;  
(6) implement safety production systems, and implement labor protection and environmental protection measures;  
(7) carry out internal security and protection work;  
(8) safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of employees, respect employees' democratic management rights, improve working conditions, carry out family planning work, and improve the material and cultural living standards of employees;  
(9) strengthen ideological and political education, legal education, national defense education, scientific and cultural education, and technical and business training for employees, and improve the quality of the workforce.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　集体企业有权按照国家规定自愿组建、参加和退出集体企业的联合经济组织，并依照该联合经济组织的章程规定，享受权利，承担义务。

**Article 23** Collective enterprises shall have the right to voluntarily form, join, and withdraw from joint economic organizations of collective enterprises in accordance with national regulations, and enjoy rights and assume obligations in accordance with the articles of association of such joint economic organizations.

### 第四章　职工和职工(代表)大会

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　凡本人提出申请，承认并遵守集体企业章程，被企业招收，即可成为该集体企业的职工。

**Article 24** Any person who submits an application, acknowledges and abides by the articles of association of a collective enterprise, and is recruited by the enterprise may become an employee of that collective enterprise.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　职工依照法律、法规的规定，在集体企业内享有下列权利：  
(一)企业各级管理职务的选举权和被选举权；  
(二)参加企业民主管理，监督企业各项活动和管理人员的工作；  
(三)参加劳动并享受劳动报酬、劳动保护、劳动保险、医疗保健和休息、休假的权利；  
(四)接受职业技术教育和培训，按照国家规定评定业务技术职称；  
(五)辞职；  
(六)享受退休养老待遇；  
(七)其他权利。

**Article 25** Employees shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations, enjoy the following rights in a collective enterprise:  
(1) the right to vote and stand for election for management positions at all levels of the enterprise;  
(2) participate in democratic management of the enterprise, and supervise various activities of the enterprise and the work of management personnel;  
(3) the right to participate in labor and enjoy labor remuneration, labor protection, labor insurance, medical care, and rest and leave;  
(4) receive vocational technical education and training, and assess professional technical titles in accordance with national regulations;  
(5) resign;  
(6) enjoy retirement and old-age benefits;  
(7) other rights.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　职工应当履行下列义务：  
(一)遵守国家的法律、法规和集体企业的规章制度、劳动纪律，以企业主人的态度从事劳动，做好本职工作；  
(二)执行职工(代表)大会决议，完成任务；  
(三)维护企业的集体利益；  
(四)努力学习政治、文化和科技知识，不断提高自身素质；  
(五)法律、法规和企业章程规定的其他义务。

**Article 26** Employees shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) comply with national laws, regulations, and the rules, regulations, and labor discipline of the collective enterprise, engage in labor with the attitude of a master of the enterprise, and perform their own duties well;  
(2) implement the resolutions of the employees' (representatives') congress, and complete tasks;  
(3) safeguard the collective interests of the enterprise;  
(4) diligently study political, cultural, and scientific knowledge, and continuously improve their own quality;  
(5) other obligations stipulated by laws, regulations, and the articles of association of the enterprise.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　集体企业必须建立、健全职工(代表)大会制度：  
(一)100人以下的集体企业，建立职工大会制度；  
(二)300人以上的集体企业建立职工代表大会制度；  
(三)100人以上300人以下的集体企业，建立职工大会或者职工代表大会制度，由企业自定。  
职工代表大会代表由职工选举产生。代表应当是思想进步、工作积极、联系群众、有参加民主管理能力的职工。

**Article 27** A collective enterprise must establish and improve the system of employees' (representatives') congress:  
(1) a collective enterprise with fewer than 100 persons shall establish a system of employees' congress;  
(2) a collective enterprise with 300 persons or more shall establish a system of employees' representatives' congress;  
(3) a collective enterprise with between 100 and 300 persons shall establish either a system of employees' congress or a system of employees' representatives' congress, as determined by the enterprise itself.  
Representatives to the employees' representatives' congress shall be elected by the employees. Representatives shall be employees who are progressive in thought, active in work, maintain close ties with the masses, and have the ability to participate in democratic management.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　集体企业的职工(代表)大会在国家法律、法规的规定范围内行使下列职权：  
(一)制定、修改集体企业章程；  
(二)按照国家规定选举、罢免、聘用、解聘厂长(经理)、副厂长(副经理)；  
(三)审议厂长(经理)提交的各项议案，决定企业经营管理的重大问题；  
(四)审议并决定企业职工工资形式、工资调整方案、奖金和分红方案、职工住宅分配方案和其他有关职工生活福利的重大事项；  
(五)审议并决定企业的职工奖惩办法和其他重要规章制度；  
(六)法律、法规和企业章程规定的其他职权。

**Article 28** The employees' (representatives') congress of a collective enterprise shall, within the scope prescribed by national laws and regulations, exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) formulate and amend the articles of association of the collective enterprise;  
(2) elect, recall, appoint, and dismiss the factory director (manager) and deputy factory director (deputy manager) in accordance with national regulations;  
(3) deliberate on various proposals submitted by the factory director (manager) and decide on major issues concerning enterprise operation and management;  
(4) deliberate and decide on the form of wage, wage adjustment plan, bonus and profit distribution plan, employee housing distribution plan, and other major matters concerning employees' living welfare;  
(5) deliberate and decide on the enterprise's employee reward and punishment measures and other important rules and regulations;  
(6) other functions and powers stipulated by laws, regulations, and the articles of association of the enterprise.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　职工(代表)大会依照企业章程规定定期召开，但每年不得少于两次。

**Article 29** The employees' (representatives') congress shall be held regularly in accordance with the provisions of the enterprise's articles of association, but shall be held no fewer than twice a year.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　集体企业的职工代表大会，可以设立常设机构，负责职工代表大会闭会期间的工作。  
常设机构的人员组成、产生方式、职权范围及名称，由集体企业职工代表大会规定，报上级管理机构备案。

**Article 30** The employees' representatives' congress of a collective enterprise may set up a permanent body to handle work during the recess of the employees' representatives' congress.  
The composition, method of formation, scope of functions and powers, and name of the permanent body shall be stipulated by the employees' representatives' congress of the collective enterprise and reported to the higher-level management organization for record.

### 第五章　厂长(经理)

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　集体企业实行厂长(经理)负责制。厂长(经理)对企业职工(代表)大会负责，是集体企业的法定代表人。

**Article 31** A collective enterprise shall implement a system under which the factory director (manager) assumes full responsibility. The factory director (manager) shall be responsible to the enterprise's workers' (representatives') congress and shall be the legal representative of the collective enterprise.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　厂长(经理)由企业职工代表大会选举或者招聘产生。选举和招聘的具体办法，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定。  
由集体企业联合经济组织投资开办的集体企业，其厂长(经理)可以由该联合经济组织任免。  
投资主体多元化的集体企业，其中国家投资达到一定比例的，其厂长(经理)可以由上级管理机构按照国家有关规定任免。

**Article 32** The factory director (manager) shall be elected or recruited by the enterprise's workers' congress. The specific measures for election and recruitment shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
For a collective enterprise invested and established by a collective enterprise joint economic organization, the factory director (manager) may be appointed or removed by such joint economic organization.  
For a collective enterprise with diversified investment entities, where state investment reaches a certain proportion, the factory director (manager) may be appointed or removed by the superior administrative agency in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　厂长(经理)应当具备下列条件：  
(一)懂得有关法律、法规和方针、政策，坚持企业的社会主义经营方向；  
(二)熟悉本行业业务，善于经营管理，有组织领导能力；  
(三)热爱集体、廉洁奉公，联系群众，有民主作风；  
(四)法律、法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 33** The factory director (manager) shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) be well versed in relevant laws, regulations, guidelines, and policies; adhere to the enterprise's socialist orientation in business operations;  
(2) be familiar with the business of the industry, be skilled in management, and possess organizational and leadership abilities;  
(3) be devoted to the collective, be honest and upright, maintain close ties with the masses, and practice democratic style;  
(4) other requirements as prescribed by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　厂长(经理)在法律、法规的规定范围内行使下列职权：  
(一)领导和组织企业日常生产经营和行政工作；  
(二)主持编制并向职工(代表)大会提出企业的中长期发展规划、年度生产经营计划、固定资产投资方案；  
(三)主持编制并向职工(代表)大会提出企业机构设置的方案，决定劳动组织的调整方案；  
(四)按照国家规定任免或者聘任、解聘企业中层行政领导干部，但法律、法规另有规定的，从其规定；  
(五)提出企业年度财务预算、决算方案和利润分配方案；  
(六)提出企业的经济责任制方案、工资调整方案、劳动保护措施方案、奖惩办法和其他重要的规章制度；  
(七)奖惩职工；  
(八)遇到特殊情况时，提出召开职工(代表)大会的建议；  
(九)企业章程规定的其他职权。

**Article 34** Within the scope prescribed by laws and regulations, the factory director (manager) shall exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) lead and organize the enterprise's daily production, operation, and administrative work;  
(2) preside over the preparation and submission to the workers' (representatives') congress of the enterprise's medium- and long-term development plan, annual production and operation plan, and plan for fixed asset investment;  
(3) preside over the preparation and submission to the workers' (representatives') congress of the plan for the enterprise's organizational structure, and decide on the plan for the adjustment of labor organization;  
(4) appoint or remove, or employ or dismiss, the enterprise's middle-level administrative leading cadres in accordance with state provisions, unless otherwise prescribed by laws and regulations, in which case such provisions shall prevail;  
(5) propose the enterprise's annual financial budget plan, final accounts plan, and profit distribution plan;  
(6) propose the enterprise's economic responsibility system plan, wage adjustment plan, labor protection measures plan, reward and punishment measures, and other important rules and regulations;  
(7) reward and punish the workers;  
(8) propose to convene a workers' (representatives') congress meeting under special circumstances;  
(9) other functions and powers as prescribed by the enterprise's articles of association.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　厂长(经理)有下列职责：  
(一)贯彻执行党和国家的方针、政策，遵守国家的法律、法规，执行职工(代表)大会的决议；  
(二)组织职工完成企业生产经营任务和各项经济技术指标，推进企业技术进步，提高经济效益，增强企业发展能力；  
(三)严格遵守财经纪律，坚持民主理财，定期向职工公布财务账目；  
(四)保护企业的合法权益和职工在企业内的正当权利；  
(五)办好职工生活福利和逐步开展职工养老、待业等保险；  
(六)组织落实安全卫生措施，实现安全文明生产；  
(七)定期向本企业职工(代表)大会报告工作，听取意见，并接受监督；  
(八)法律、法规和企业章程规定的其他职责。

**Article 35** The factory director (manager) shall have the following duties:  
(1) implement the guidelines and policies of the Party and the state, comply with the laws and regulations of the state, and execute the resolutions of the workers' (representatives') congress;  
(2) organize the workers to accomplish the enterprise's production and operation tasks and various economic and technical indicators, promote the enterprise's technological progress, improve economic benefits, and enhance the enterprise's development capacity;  
(3) strictly observe financial discipline, adhere to democratic financial management, and periodically make the financial accounts public to the workers;  
(4) protect the lawful rights and interests of the enterprise and the legitimate rights of the workers within the enterprise;  
(5) manage the workers' welfare well and gradually develop insurance such as pension and unemployment insurance for the workers;  
(6) organize the implementation of safety and health measures to achieve safe and civilized production;  
(7) report work to the enterprise's workers' (representatives') congress on a regular basis, solicit opinions, and accept supervision;  
(8) other duties as prescribed by laws, regulations, and the enterprise's articles of association.

### 第六章　财产管理和收益分配

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　集体企业应当按照本章规定进行清产核资，明确其财产所有权的归属。

**Article 36** A collective enterprise shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter, conduct an inventory of its assets and verify its capital to ascertain the ownership of its property.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　集体企业的公共积累，归本企业劳动群众集体所有。

**Article 37** The public accumulation of a collective enterprise shall belong to the collective of the working masses of the enterprise.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　集体企业的联合经济组织的投资，归该联合经济组织范围内的劳动群众集体所有。  
集体企业联合经济组织设立的互助合作基金，应当主要用于该组织范围内发展生产和推进共同富裕。

**Article 38** The investment of a collective enterprise joint economic organization shall belong to the collective of the working masses within the scope of such joint economic organization.  
The mutual aid and cooperation fund established by a collective enterprise joint economic organization shall be mainly used to develop production and promote common prosperity within the scope of such organization.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　在企业、事业单位、社会团体等扶持下设立的集体企业，其扶持资金可按下列办法之一处理：  
(一)作为企业向扶持单位的借用款，按双方约定的方法和期限由企业归还扶持单位；  
(二)作为扶持单位对企业的投资，按其投资占企业总资产的比例，参与企业的利润分配。  
企业、事业单位、社会团体等的扶持资金的来源，必须符合国家财政主管部门的有关规定。  
企业、事业单位、社会团体等与其扶持设立的集体企业，应当明确划清产权和财务关系。扶持单位不得干预集体企业的经营管理活动，集体企业也不得依赖扶持单位。

**Article 39** For a collective enterprise established with the support of enterprises, institutions, social organizations, etc., the supporting funds may be dealt with by one of the following methods:  
(1) as a loan from the enterprise to the supporting unit, the enterprise shall repay the supporting unit according to the method and term agreed by both parties;  
(2) as an investment by the supporting unit in the enterprise, the supporting unit shall participate in the profit distribution of the enterprise in proportion to its investment share in the total assets of the enterprise.  
The source of supporting funds from enterprises, institutions, social organizations, etc. must comply with the relevant provisions of the competent financial department of the state.  
Enterprises, institutions, social organizations, etc. and the collective enterprises established with their support shall clearly define their property rights and financial relationships. The supporting units shall not interfere in the business management activities of the collective enterprises, and the collective enterprises shall not rely on the supporting units.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　职工股金，归职工个人所有。

**Article 40** The shares contributed by workers shall be owned by the workers individually.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　集体企业外的单位和个人的投资，归投资者所有。

**Article 41** Investments by entities and individuals outside collective enterprises shall belong to the investors.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　职工股金和集体企业吸收的各种投资，投资者可以依法转让或者继承。

**Article 42** Investors may transfer or succeed to employee share capital and various investments absorbed by collective enterprises according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　集体企业必须保证财产的完整性，合理使用、有效经营企业的财产。

**Article 43** Collective enterprises must guarantee the integrity of their property, and use and operate the enterprise property reasonably and effectively.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　集体企业的收益分配，必须遵循兼顾国家、集体和个人三者利益的原则。

**Article 44** The distribution of income of collective enterprises must follow the principle of taking into account the interests of the state, the collective, and individuals.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　集体企业必须执行国家有关财务、会计制度，接受审计监督，加强企业内部的财务管理。

**Article 45** Collective enterprises must implement the relevant financial and accounting systems of the state, accept audit supervision, and strengthen internal financial management.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　集体企业的税后利润，由企业依法自主支配。企业应当按照国家规定确定公积金、公益金、劳动分红和股金分红的比例。

**Article 46** The after-tax profit of a collective enterprise shall be independently disposed of by the enterprise according to law. The enterprise shall determine the proportions of statutory reserve fund, public welfare fund, labor dividends and share dividends in accordance with state regulations.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　集体企业职工的劳动报酬必须坚持按劳分配的原则。具体分配形式和办法由企业自行确定。

**Article 47** The labor remuneration of employees of collective enterprises must adhere to the principle of distribution according to work. The specific form and method of distribution shall be determined by the enterprise itself.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　集体企业的股金分红要同企业盈亏相结合。企业盈利，按股分红；企业亏损，在未弥补亏损之前，不得分红。

**Article 48** Share dividends of collective enterprises shall be combined with the enterprise's profits and losses. When the enterprise makes a profit, dividends shall be distributed according to shares. When the enterprise suffers a loss, dividends shall not be distributed until the loss is made up.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　集体企业必须依照国家规定提取职工养老、待业等保险基金。职工养老、待业等保险基金按国家规定在征收所得税前提取，专项储存，专款专用。

**Article 49** Collective enterprises must, in accordance with state regulations, appropriate insurance funds for employee retirement and unemployment, etc. Insurance funds for employee retirement and unemployment, etc. shall be appropriated before income tax is levied according to state regulations, and shall be deposited separately and used for special purposes.

### 第七章　集体企业和政府的关系

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　各级人民政府应当把发展城镇集体经济纳入各级政府的国民经济和社会发展计划，从各方面给予扶持和指导，保障城镇集体经济的健康发展。

**Article 50** The people's governments at all levels shall incorporate the development of the urban collective economy into the national economic and social development plans of the governments at all levels, and provide support and guidance in all aspects to ensure the healthy development of the urban collective economy.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国务院城镇集体经济的主管机构，负责全国城镇集体经济的宏观指导和管理，其主要职责是：拟订城镇集体经济的发展政策和法律法规，协调全国城镇集体经济发展中的重大问题，组织有关方面监督、检查集体企业政策、法规的执行情况。

**Article 51** The competent authority of the State Council for urban collective economy shall be responsible for the macro-guidance and management of the national urban collective economy. Its main functions and duties are: to formulate development policies and laws and regulations for urban collective economy, to coordinate major issues in the development of the national urban collective economy, and to organize relevant parties to supervise and inspect the enforcement of policies, laws and regulations for collective enterprises.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　市(含县级市，下同)以上人民政府应当根据城镇集体经济发展的需要，确定城镇集体企业的指导部门，加强对集体企业的政策指导，协调当地城镇集体经济发展中的问题，组织有关方面监督、检查集体企业政策、法规的执行情况。

**Article 52** People's governments at or above the city level (including county-level cities, the same below) shall, according to the needs of the development of urban collective economy, designate the guidance department for urban collective enterprises, strengthen policy guidance for collective enterprises, coordinate issues in the development of the local urban collective economy, and organize relevant parties to supervise and inspect the enforcement of policies, laws and regulations for collective enterprises.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　政府有关行业管理部门，应当依照法律、法规的规定，在各自的职责范围内，负责本行业集体企业的行业指导和管理工作。

**Article 53** The relevant government industry management departments shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the industry guidance and management of collective enterprises in their industries.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　各级人民政府的其他有关部门，依法对集体企业进行监督和提供服务。

**Article 54** Other relevant departments of people's governments at all levels shall, according to law, supervise and provide services to collective enterprises.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家保护集体企业的合法权益。  
任何政府部门及其他单位和个人不得改变集体企业的集体所有制性质和损害集体企业的财产所有权，不得向集体企业摊派人力、物力、财力，不得干预集体企业的生产经营和民主管理。

**Article 55** The State shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of collective enterprises.  
No government department or other entity or individual may change the collective ownership nature of collective enterprises or damage the property ownership of collective enterprises, nor may they apportion manpower, material resources, or financial resources to collective enterprises, nor may they interfere with the production, operation, and democratic management of collective enterprises.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　集体企业有下列行为之一的，由工商行政管理机关依照国家有关法律、法规的规定给予行政处罚：  
(一)登记时弄虚作假或者不按规定申请变更登记的；  
(二)违反核准登记事项或者超越核准登记的经营范围从事经营活动的；  
(三)利用分立、合并、终止和清算等行为抽逃资金、隐匿和私分财产的；  
(四)其他违法行为。

**Article 56** If a collective enterprise commits any of the following acts, the administrative authority for industry and commerce shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant national laws and regulations, impose an administrative penalty:  
(1) engaging in fraudulent activities during registration or failing to apply for change of registration as required;  
(2) violating approved registration items or engaging in business activities beyond the approved business scope;  
(3) using acts such as division, merger, termination, and liquidation to withdraw capital, conceal, and illegally distribute property;  
(4) other illegal acts.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　集体企业因生产、销售伪劣商品，给用户和消费者造成财产损失和人身伤害的，应当承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，对负有直接责任的集体企业领导人员和其他直接责任人员依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 57** If a collective enterprise causes property losses or personal injury to users and consumers due to the production or sale of counterfeit or substandard goods, it shall bear compensation liability; if the act constitutes a crime, the leaders of the collective enterprise who bear direct responsibility and other persons directly responsible shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　任何单位或者个人违反本条例规定，向集体企业摊派或者侵吞、挪用集体企业财产的，必须赔偿。对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，由有关主管机关根据情节轻重，给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 58** Any entity or individual that, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, apportions to a collective enterprise or embezzles or misappropriates the property of a collective enterprise must compensate. The person in charge who bears direct responsibility and other persons directly responsible shall be given an administrative sanction by the relevant competent authority according to the severity of the circumstances; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　集体企业领导人员滥用职权，侵犯职工合法权益，情节严重的，由上级管理机构按照干部管理权限给予行政处分；滥用职权，假公济私，对职工进行报复陷害的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 59** If a leader of a collective enterprise abuses his or her powers, infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of employees, and the circumstances are serious, the superior administrative authority shall, in accordance with the authority for management of cadres, impose an administrative sanction; if he or she abuses powers, uses the public office for private gain, and retaliates against or frames employees, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　集体企业的领导人员或者政府有关部门的工作人员，因工作过失给企业造成损失的，由企业的上级管理机构或者政府有关部门按照干部的管理权限给予行政处分。  
集体企业的领导人员和政府有关部门的工作人员玩忽职守，致使集体企业财产、利益遭受重大损失，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 60** If a leader of a collective enterprise or a staff member of a relevant government department causes losses to the enterprise due to work negligence, the superior administrative authority of the enterprise or the relevant government department shall, in accordance with the authority for management of cadres, impose an administrative sanction.  
If a leader of a collective enterprise or a staff member of a relevant government department neglects his or her duties, causing significant losses to the property or interests of the collective enterprise, and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　集体企业违反本条例有关集体企业领导人员的产生、罢免条件和程序规定的，上级管理机构应当予以纠正，并追究直接责任人员的行政责任。  
集体企业上级管理机构违反本条例有关集体企业领导人员产生、罢免条件和程序规定的，其上一级主管部门应当予以纠正；情节严重的，应当追究直接责任人员的行政责任。

**Article 61** Art. 61 Where a collective enterprise violates the provisions of these Regulations concerning the conditions and procedures for the election and removal of leading personnel of collective enterprises, the superior management institution shall rectify the violation and investigate the administrative liability of the persons directly responsible.  
Where the superior management institution of a collective enterprise violates the provisions of these Regulations concerning the conditions and procedures for the election and removal of leading personnel of collective enterprises, its superior competent department shall rectify the violation; if the circumstances are serious, it shall investigate the administrative liability of the persons directly responsible.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　扰乱集体企业的秩序，致使生产、营业、工作不能正常进行或者无法进行的，由公安机关依据《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 62** Art. 62 Whoever disrupts the order of a collective enterprise, thereby causing production, business, or work to fail to proceed normally or to be impossible, shall be penalized by the public security organ in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　集体企业联合经济组织的组建和管理办法，另行制定。

**Article 63** Art. 63 Measures for the formation and administration of the joint economic organizations of collective enterprises shall be formulated separately.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　集体所有制的各类公司的管理，按照国家有关公司的法律、法规执行。

**Article 64** Art. 64 The administration of companies of various types under collective ownership shall be governed by the laws and regulations of the State concerning companies.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　城镇中的文教、卫生、科研等集体所有制事业单位参照本条例执行。  
供销合作社的管理办法，另行制定。

**Article 65** Art. 65 Institutions of collective ownership in the fields of culture, education, health, and scientific research in cities and towns shall implement these Regulations by reference.  
Measures for the administration of supply and marketing cooperatives shall be formulated separately.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　劳动就业服务性集体企业应当遵循本条例规定的原则，具体管理办法按国务院发布的《劳动就业服务企业管理规定》执行。  
集中安置残疾人员的福利性集体企业的管理办法，根据本条例的原则另行制定。

**Article 66** Art. 66 Collective enterprises providing labor and employment services shall follow the principles set forth in these Regulations, and the specific administrative measures shall be implemented in accordance with the Provisions on the Administration of Labor and Employment Service Enterprises promulgated by the State Council.  
Measures for the administration of welfare collective enterprises that intensively employ disabled persons shall be formulated separately based on the principles of these Regulations.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　军队扶持开办的集体企业的管理办法，由中国人民解放军总后勤部根据本条例规定的原则另行制定。

**Article 67** Art. 67 Measures for the administration of collective enterprises established with support from the armed forces shall be formulated separately by the General Logistics Department of the People's Liberation Army in accordance with the principles set forth in these Regulations.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和国务院各行业主管部门，可以根据本条例并结合本地区、本行业的具体情况，制定本条例的实施细则。

**Article 68** Art. 68 The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the competent departments of various industries under the State Council, may, in accordance with these Regulations and in light of the specific circumstances of their respective regions and industries, formulate detailed rules for the implementation of these Regulations.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　本条例自1992年1月1日起施行。

**Article 69** Art. 69 These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 1992.
