# 学校体育工作条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2017-03-01

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保证学校体育工作的正常开展，促进学生身心的健康成长，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to ensure the normal development of school physical education and to promote the healthy growth of students both physically and mentally.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　学校体育工作是指普通中小学校、农业中学、职业中学、中等专业学校、普通高等学校的体育课教学、课外体育活动、课余体育训练和体育竞赛。

**Article 2** School physical education means physical education teaching, extracurricular sports activities, after-school sports training and sports competitions in ordinary primary and secondary schools, agricultural middle schools, vocational middle schools, specialized secondary schools and ordinary institutions of higher learning.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　学校体育工作的基本任务是：增进学生身心健康、增强学生体质；使学生掌握体育基本知识，培养学生体育运动能力和习惯；提高学生运动技术水平，为国家培养体育后备人才；对学生进行品德教育，增强组织纪律性，培养学生的勇敢、顽强、进取精神。

**Article 3** The basic tasks of school physical education shall be: to improve students' physical and mental health and enhance their physique; to enable students to acquire basic knowledge of physical education and cultivate their sports abilities and habits; to raise students' sports skill level and cultivate reserve sports talents for the country; and to provide moral education to students, strengthen their organizational discipline, and cultivate their courage, tenacity and enterprising spirit.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　学校体育工作应当坚持普及与提高相结合、体育锻炼与安全卫生相结合的原则，积极开展多种形式的强身健体活动，重视继承和发扬民族传统体育，注意吸取国外学校体育的有益经验，积极开展体育科学研究工作。

**Article 4** School physical education shall adhere to the principles of combining popularization with improvement and combining physical exercise with safety and hygiene, actively carry out various forms of fitness activities, attach importance to inheriting and developing traditional national sports, pay attention to drawing on the beneficial experience of school physical education in foreign countries, and actively conduct scientific research on physical education.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　学校体育工作应当面向全体学生，积极推行国家体育锻炼标准。

**Article 5** School physical education shall be oriented to all students and actively implement the national physical exercise standards.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　学校体育工作在教育行政部门领导下，由学校组织实施，并接受体育行政部门的指导。

**Article 6** School physical education shall be organized and implemented by schools under the leadership of the administrative department of education and shall accept the guidance of the administrative department of sports.

### 第二章　体育课教学

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　学校应当根据教育行政部门的规定，组织实施体育课教学活动。  
普通中小学校、农业中学、职业中学、中等专业学校各年级和普通高等学校的一、二年级必须开设体育课。普通高等学校对三年级以上学生开设体育选修课。

**Article 7** Schools shall organize and implement physical education teaching activities in accordance with the provisions of the administrative department of education.  
Ordinary primary and secondary schools, agricultural middle schools, vocational middle schools, all grades of specialized secondary schools, and the first and second grades of ordinary institutions of higher learning must offer physical education courses. Ordinary institutions of higher learning shall offer elective physical education courses for students in grade three and above.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　体育课教学应当遵循学生身心发展的规律，教学内容应当符合教学大纲的要求，符合学生年龄、性别特点和所在地区地理、气候条件。  
体育课的教学形式应当灵活多样，不断改进教学方法，改善教学条件，提高教学质量。

**Article 8** Physical education teaching shall follow the laws of students' physical and mental development, and the teaching content shall conform to the requirements of the teaching syllabus, and be suited to the age and gender characteristics of students as well as the geographical and climatic conditions of the region.  
The teaching forms of physical education shall be flexible and diverse, with continuous improvement of teaching methods, teaching conditions and teaching quality.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　体育课是学生毕业、升学考试科目。学生因病、残免修体育课或者免除体育课考试的，必须持医院证明，经学校体育教研室(组)审核同意，并报学校教务部门备案，记入学生健康档案。

**Article 9** Physical education is a subject for students' graduation and entrance examinations. Students who are exempted from physical education courses or from the physical education examination due to illness or disability must hold a hospital certificate, obtain the examination and approval of the school's physical education teaching and research office (group), report to the school's academic affairs department for record, and have it recorded in the student's health file.

### 第三章　课外体育活动

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　开展课外体育活动应当从实际情况出发，因地制宜，生动活泼。  
普通中小学校、农业中学、职业中学每天应当安排课间操，每周安排3次以上课外体育活动，保证学生每天有1小时体育活动的时间(含体育课)。  
中等专业学校、普通高等学校除安排有体育课、劳动课的当天外，每天应当组织学生开展各种课外体育活动。

**Article 10** Extracurricular sports activities shall be carried out in a lively manner based on actual conditions and local circumstances.  
Ordinary primary and secondary schools, agricultural middle schools and vocational middle schools shall arrange daily recess exercises and arrange extracurricular sports activities no fewer than three times per week, ensuring that students have at least one hour of physical activity time (including physical education courses) each day.  
Except for days when physical education courses or labor courses are scheduled, specialized secondary schools and ordinary institutions of higher learning shall organize students to carry out various extracurricular sports activities every day.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　学校应当在学生中认真推行国家体育锻炼标准的达标活动和等级运动员制度。  
学校可根据条件有计划地组织学生远足、野营和举办夏(冬)令营等多种形式的体育活动。

**Article 11** Schools shall conscientiously promote the activities of meeting the National Physical Exercise Standards and the system of graded athletes among students.  
Schools may, based on conditions, organize in a planned manner various forms of sports activities such as hiking, camping, and summer (winter) camps for students.

### 第四章　课余体育训练与竞赛

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　学校应当在体育课教学和课外体育活动的基础上，开展多种形式的课余体育训练，提高学生的运动技术水平。有条件的普通中小学校、农业中学、职业中学、中等专业学校经省级教育行政部门批准，普通高等学校经国家教育委员会批准，可以开展培养优秀体育后备人才的训练。

**Article 12** Schools shall, on the basis of physical education teaching and extracurricular sports activities, conduct various forms of after-school sports training to improve students' sports skills and techniques. Ordinary primary and secondary schools, agricultural secondary schools, vocational secondary schools, and specialized secondary schools with appropriate conditions may, with the approval of the provincial education administrative department, and ordinary institutions of higher learning may, with the approval of the State Education Commission, conduct training for cultivating outstanding reserve sports talents.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　学校对参加课余体育训练的学生，应当安排好文化课学习，加强思想品德教育，并注意改善他们的营养。普通高等学校对运动水平较高、具有培养前途的学生，报国家教育委员会批准，可适当延长学习年限。

**Article 13** Schools shall arrange cultural course study for students participating in after-school sports training, strengthen ideological and moral education, and pay attention to improving their nutrition. Ordinary institutions of higher learning may, with the approval of the State Education Commission, appropriately extend the duration of study for students with relatively high sports levels and potential for training.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　学校体育竞赛贯彻小型多样、单项分散、基层为主、勤俭节约的原则。学校每学年至少举行一次以田径项目为主的全校性运动会。

**Article 14** School sports competitions shall adhere to the principles of being small-scale and diverse, individual events and decentralized, grassroots-oriented, and thrifty and economical. Schools shall hold at least one school-wide sports meeting focusing on track and field events each academic year.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　全国中学生运动会每3年举行一次，全国大学生运动会每4年举行一次。特殊情况下，经国家教育委员会批准可提前或者延期举行。  
国家教育委员会根据需要，可以安排学生参加国际学生体育竞赛。

**Article 15** The National Middle School Students' Games shall be held once every three years, and the National University Students' Games shall be held once every four years. Under special circumstances, with the approval of the State Education Commission, they may be held earlier or postponed.  
The State Education Commission may, as needed, arrange for students to participate in international student sports competitions.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　学校体育竞赛应当执行国家有关的体育竞赛制度和规定，树立良好的赛风。

**Article 16** School sports competitions shall implement the relevant national sports competition systems and regulations, and foster a good competition atmosphere.

### 第五章　体育教师

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　体育教师应当热爱学校体育工作，具有良好的思想品德、文化素养，掌握体育教育的理论和教学方法。

**Article 17** Physical education teachers shall be devoted to school sports work, possess good ideological and moral character and cultural literacy, and master the theory and teaching methods of physical education.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　学校应当在各级教育行政部门核定的教师总编制数内，按照教学计划中体育课授课时数所占的比例和开展课余体育活动的需要配备体育教师。除普通小学外，学校应当根据学校女生数量配备一定比例的女体育教师。承担培养优秀体育后备人才训练任务的学校，体育教师的配备应当相应增加。

**Article 18** Schools shall, within the total teacher establishment approved by the education administrative departments at all levels, allocate physical education teachers in accordance with the proportion of physical education class hours in the teaching plan and the needs for conducting extracurricular sports activities. Except for ordinary primary schools, schools shall allocate a certain proportion of female physical education teachers based on the number of female students in the school. For schools undertaking the task of training outstanding reserve sports talents, the allocation of physical education teachers shall be correspondingly increased.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　各级教育行政部门和学校应当有计划地安排体育教师进修培训。对体育教师的职务聘任、工资待遇应当与其他任课教师同等对待。按照国家有关规定，有关部门应当妥善解决体育教师的工作服装和粮食定量。  
体育教师组织课间操(早操)、课外体育活动和课余训练、体育竞赛应当计算工作量。  
学校对妊娠、产后的女体育教师，应当依照《女职工劳动保护规定》给予相应的照顾。

**Article 19** Education administrative departments at all levels and schools shall arrange in-service training for physical education teachers in a planned manner. The appointment and salary treatment of physical education teachers shall be treated equally with those of other teachers. In accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, the relevant departments shall properly solve the issues of work uniforms and grain rations for physical education teachers.  
The work of organizing recess exercises (morning exercises), extracurricular sports activities, after-school training, and sports competitions by physical education teachers shall be counted as workload.  
Schools shall provide corresponding care to female physical education teachers during pregnancy and after childbirth in accordance with the "Provisions on Labor Protection for Female Workers".

### 第六章　场地、器材、设备和经费

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　学校的上级主管部门和学校应当按照国家或者地方制订的各类学校体育场地、器材、设备标准，有计划地逐步配齐。学校体育器材应当纳入教学仪器供应计划。新建、改建学校必须按照有关场地、器材的规定进行规划、设计和建设。  
在学校比较密集的城镇地区，逐步建立中小学体育活动中心，并纳入城市建设规划。社会的体育场(馆)和体育设施应当安排一定时间免费向学生开放。

**Article 20** The competent departments in charge of schools and the schools themselves shall, in accordance with the standards for sports venues, equipment, and facilities for various types of schools formulated by the State or local authorities, complete the allocation in a planned and gradual manner. School sports equipment shall be included in the teaching instrument supply plan. Newly built or renovated schools must plan, design, and construct in accordance with the relevant regulations on venues and equipment.  
In urban areas with a high concentration of schools, sports activity centers for primary and secondary schools shall be gradually established and incorporated into the urban construction plan. Public sports stadiums (halls) and facilities shall arrange a certain amount of time to be open to students free of charge.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　学校应当制定体育场地、器材、设备的管理维修制度，并由专人负责管理。  
任何单位或者个人不得侵占、破坏学校体育场地或者破坏体育器材、设备。

**Article 21** Schools shall establish management and maintenance systems for sports venues, equipment, and facilities, and shall assign dedicated personnel to be responsible for management.  
No entity or individual may occupy or damage school sports venues, or damage sports equipment or facilities.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　各级教育行政部门和学校应当根据学校体育工作的实际需要，把学校体育经费纳入核定的年度教育经费预算内，予以妥善安排。  
地方各级人民政府在安排年度学校教育经费时，应当安排一定数额的体育经费，以保证学校体育工作的开展。  
国家和地方各级体育行政部门在经费上应当尽可能对学校体育工作给予支持。  
国家鼓励各种社会力量以及个人自愿捐资支援学校体育工作。

**Article 22** Education administrative departments at all levels and schools shall, based on the actual needs of school physical education work, incorporate school physical education funds into the approved annual educational expenditure budget and arrange them appropriately.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall, when arranging annual school education funds, allocate a certain amount of physical education funds to ensure the development of school physical education work.  
The national and local sports administrative departments at all levels shall provide support to school physical education work in terms of funds to the extent possible.  
The State encourages various social forces and individuals to voluntarily donate funds to support school physical education work.

### 第七章　组织机构和管理

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　各级教育行政部门应当健全学校体育管理机构，加强对学校体育工作的指导和检查。  
学校体育工作应当作为考核学校工作的一项基本内容。普通中小学校的体育工作应当列入督导计划。

**Article 23** Education administrative departments at all levels shall improve the management institutions for school physical education and strengthen the guidance and inspection of school physical education work.  
School physical education work shall be regarded as a basic element in assessing school performance. The physical education work of ordinary primary and secondary schools shall be included in the supervision plan.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　学校应当由一位副校(院)长主管体育工作，在制定计划、总结工作、评选先进时，应当把体育工作列为重要内容。

**Article 24** Schools shall have a vice-principal (vice-president) in charge of physical education work, and shall list physical education work as an important element when formulating plans, summarizing work, and selecting advanced individuals or units.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　普通高等学校、中等专业学校和规模较大的普通中学，可以建立相应的体育管理部门，配备专职干部和管理人员。  
班主任、辅导员应当把学校体育工作作为一项工作内容，教育和督促学生积极参加体育活动。学校的卫生部门应当与体育管理部门互相配合，搞好体育卫生工作。总务部门应当搞好学校体育工作的后勤保障。  
学校应当充分发挥共青团、少先队、学生会以及大、中学生体育协会等组织在学校体育工作中的作用。

**Article 25** Ordinary institutions of higher education, specialized secondary schools, and ordinary secondary schools of a larger scale may establish corresponding physical education management departments and assign full-time cadres and management personnel.  
Class teachers and counselors shall treat school physical education work as part of their duties, educating and urging students to actively participate in sports activities. The school's health department shall cooperate with the physical education management department to ensure good sports hygiene. The general affairs department shall provide good logistical support for school physical education work.  
Schools shall give full play to the role of organizations such as the Communist Youth League, the Young Pioneers, student unions, and sports associations for college and secondary school students in school physical education work.

### 第八章　奖励与处罚

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对在学校体育工作中成绩显著的单位和个人，各级教育、体育行政部门或者学校应当给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 26** Education and sports administrative departments at all levels or schools shall commend and reward entities and individuals that have achieved outstanding results in school physical education work.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对违反本条例，有下列行为之一的单位或者个人，由当地教育行政部门令其限期改正，并视情节轻重对直接责任人员给予批评教育或者行政处分：  
(一)不按规定开设或者随意停止体育课的；  
(二)未保证学生每天1小时体育活动时间(含体育课)的；  
(三)在体育竞赛中违反纪律、弄虚作假的；  
(四)不按国家规定解决体育教师工作服装、粮食定量的。

**Article 27** Any entity or individual that violates these Regulations and commits one of the following acts shall be ordered by the local education administrative department to rectify within a specified time limit, and the directly responsible personnel shall be given criticism education or administrative sanction depending on the severity of the circumstances:  
(1) Failing to offer physical education classes as required or arbitrarily suspending them;  
(2) Failing to ensure that students have one hour of physical activity time per day (including physical education classes);  
(3) Violating discipline or engaging in fraud in sports competitions;  
(4) Failing to resolve the issues of work clothing and grain rations for physical education teachers in accordance with state regulations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对违反本条例，侵占、破坏学校体育场地、器材、设备的单位或者个人，由当地人民政府或者教育行政部门令其限期清退和修复场地、赔偿或者修复器材、设备。

**Article 28** Any entity or individual that violates these Regulations and occupies or damages school sports venues, equipment, or facilities shall be ordered by the local people's government or education administrative department to vacate and restore the venues, and to compensate for or repair the equipment and facilities within a specified time limit.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　高等体育院校和普通高等学校的体育专业的体育工作不适用本条例。  
技工学校、工读学校、特殊教育学校、成人学校的学校体育工作参照本条例执行。

**Article 29** These Regulations shall not apply to the physical education work of higher physical education institutions and the physical education specialties of ordinary institutions of higher education.  
The school physical education work of vocational schools, correctional schools, special education schools, and adult schools shall be carried out with reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家教育委员会、国家体育运动委员会可根据本条例制定实施办法。

**Article 30** The State Education Commission and the State Sports Commission may formulate implementing rules in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本条例自发布之日起施行。原教育部、国家体育运动委员会1979年10月5日发布的《高等学校体育工作暂行规定(试行草案)》和《中、小学体育工作暂行规定(试行草案)》同时废止。

**Article 31** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Interim Provisions on Physical Education in Institutions of Higher Education (Trial Draft) and the Interim Provisions on Physical Education in Middle and Primary Schools (Trial Draft) promulgated by the former Ministry of Education and the State Sports Commission on October 5, 1979 shall be abolished simultaneously.
