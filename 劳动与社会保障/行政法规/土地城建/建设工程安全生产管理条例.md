# 建设工程安全生产管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2003-11-24  
**生效日期**：2004-02-01

> (2003年11月12日国务院第28次常务会议通过　2003年11月24日中华人民共和国国务院令第393号公布　自2004年2月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强建设工程安全生产监督管理，保障人民群众生命和财产安全，根据《中华人民共和国建筑法》、《中华人民共和国安全生产法》，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事建设工程的新建、扩建、改建和拆除等有关活动及实施对建设工程安全生产的监督管理，必须遵守本条例。 本条例所称建设工程，是指土木工程、建筑工程、线路管道和设备安装工程及装修工程。 第三条　建设工程安全生产管理，坚持安全第一、预防为主的方针。 第四条　建设单位、勘察单位、设计单位、施工单位、工程监理单位及其他与建设工程安全生产有关的单位，必须遵守安全生产法律、法规的规定，保证建设工程安全生产，依法承担建设工程安全生产责任。 第五条　国家鼓励建设工程安全生产的科学技术研究和先进技术的推广应用，推进建设工程安全生产的科学管理。 第二章　建设单位的安全责任 第六条　建设单位应当向施工单位提供施工现场及毗邻区域内供水、排水、供电、供气、供热、通信、广播电视等地下管线资料，气象和水文观测资料，相邻建筑物和构筑物、地下工程的有关资料，并保证资料的真实、准确、完整。 建设单位因建设工程需要，向有关部门或者单位查询前款规定的资料时，有关部门或者单位应当及时提供。 第七条　建设单位不得对勘察、设计、施工、工程监理等单位提出不符合建设工程安全生产法律、法规和强制性标准规定的要求，不得压缩合同约定的工期。 第八条　建设单位在编制工程概算时，应当确定建设工程安全作业环境及安全施工措施所需费用。 第九条　建设单位不得明示或者暗示施工单位购买、租赁、使用不符合安全施工要求的安全防护用具、机械设备、施工机具及配件、消防设施和器材。 第十条　建设单位在申请领取施工许可证时，应当提供建设工程有关安全施工措施的资料。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强建设工程安全生产监督管理，保障人民群众生命和财产安全，根据《中华人民共和国建筑法》、《中华人民共和国安全生产法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Construction Law of the People's Republic of China and the Production Safety Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of strengthening the supervision and administration of production safety in construction projects and protecting the safety of life and property of the people.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事建设工程的新建、扩建、改建和拆除等有关活动及实施对建设工程安全生产的监督管理，必须遵守本条例。  
本条例所称建设工程，是指土木工程、建筑工程、线路管道和设备安装工程及装修工程。

**Article 2** Any entity that engages in activities such as new construction, expansion, reconstruction and demolition of construction projects and implements supervision and administration over production safety of construction projects within the territory of the People's Republic of China must comply with these Regulations.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, construction projects refer to civil engineering, building engineering, line, pipeline and equipment installation engineering and decoration engineering.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　建设工程安全生产管理，坚持安全第一、预防为主的方针。

**Article 3** The management of production safety in construction projects shall adhere to the policy of giving priority to safety and focusing on prevention.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　建设单位、勘察单位、设计单位、施工单位、工程监理单位及其他与建设工程安全生产有关的单位，必须遵守安全生产法律、法规的规定，保证建设工程安全生产，依法承担建设工程安全生产责任。

**Article 4** The project owner, survey unit, design unit, construction unit, project supervision unit and other units related to production safety of construction projects must comply with the provisions of laws and regulations on production safety, ensure the production safety of construction projects, and bear the responsibility for production safety of construction projects according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家鼓励建设工程安全生产的科学技术研究和先进技术的推广应用，推进建设工程安全生产的科学管理。

**Article 5** The State encourages scientific and technological research on production safety of construction projects and the promotion and application of advanced technologies to advance the scientific management of production safety in construction projects.

### 第二章　建设单位的安全责任

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　建设单位应当向施工单位提供施工现场及毗邻区域内供水、排水、供电、供气、供热、通信、广播电视等地下管线资料，气象和水文观测资料，相邻建筑物和构筑物、地下工程的有关资料，并保证资料的真实、准确、完整。  
建设单位因建设工程需要，向有关部门或者单位查询前款规定的资料时，有关部门或者单位应当及时提供。

**Article 6** The project owner shall provide the construction unit with the data on underground pipelines such as water supply, drainage, power supply, gas supply, heat supply, communication and broadcasting television within the construction site and adjacent areas, meteorological and hydrological observation data, and relevant data on adjacent buildings and structures and underground works, and shall guarantee the authenticity, accuracy and completeness of such data.  
When the project owner consults the relevant departments or units for the data specified in the preceding paragraph due to the needs of the construction project, the relevant departments or units shall provide them in a timely manner.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　建设单位不得对勘察、设计、施工、工程监理等单位提出不符合建设工程安全生产法律、法规和强制性标准规定的要求，不得压缩合同约定的工期。

**Article 7** The project owner shall not put forward to the survey, design, construction and project supervision units any requirements that do not comply with the laws, regulations and mandatory standards on production safety of construction projects, and shall not shorten the contractually agreed construction period.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　建设单位在编制工程概算时，应当确定建设工程安全作业环境及安全施工措施所需费用。

**Article 8** When preparing the project budget estimate, the project owner shall determine the expenses required for the safe working environment and safety construction measures of the construction project.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　建设单位不得明示或者暗示施工单位购买、租赁、使用不符合安全施工要求的安全防护用具、机械设备、施工机具及配件、消防设施和器材。

**Article 9** The project owner shall not expressly or impliedly require the construction unit to purchase, lease or use safety protective equipment, machinery, construction tools and accessories, fire-fighting facilities and equipment that do not meet the requirements for safe construction.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　建设单位在申请领取施工许可证时，应当提供建设工程有关安全施工措施的资料。  
依法批准开工报告的建设工程，建设单位应当自开工报告批准之日起15日内，将保证安全施工的措施报送建设工程所在地的县级以上地方人民政府建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门备案。

**Article 10** When applying for a construction permit, the project owner shall provide the data on the safety construction measures of the construction project.  
For a construction project for which a commencement report is approved according to law, the project owner shall, within 15 days from the date of approval of the commencement report, submit the measures for ensuring safe construction to the construction administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level where the construction project is located or other relevant departments for recordation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　建设单位应当将拆除工程发包给具有相应资质等级的施工单位。  
建设单位应当在拆除工程施工15日前，将下列资料报送建设工程所在地的县级以上地方人民政府建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门备案：  
(一)施工单位资质等级证明；  
(二)拟拆除建筑物、构筑物及可能危及毗邻建筑的说明；  
(三)拆除施工组织方案；  
(四)堆放、清除废弃物的措施。  
实施爆破作业的，应当遵守国家有关民用爆炸物品管理的规定。

**Article 11** The project owner shall contract the demolition project to a construction unit with corresponding qualification grade.  
The project owner shall, 15 days prior to the commencement of demolition works, submit the following data to the construction administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level where the construction project is located or other relevant departments for recordation:  
(1) certificate of the qualification grade of the construction unit;  
(2) description of the buildings and structures to be demolished and those that may endanger adjacent buildings;  
(3) organization plan for demolition works;  
(4) measures for stacking and removing wastes.  
Where blasting operations are carried out, the provisions of the State on the management of civil explosives shall be complied with.

### 第三章　勘察、设计、工程监理及其他有关单位的安全责任

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　勘察单位应当按照法律、法规和工程建设强制性标准进行勘察，提供的勘察文件应当真实、准确，满足建设工程安全生产的需要。  
勘察单位在勘察作业时，应当严格执行操作规程，采取措施保证各类管线、设施和周边建筑物、构筑物的安全。

**Article 12** The survey unit shall carry out survey in accordance with laws, regulations and mandatory standards for engineering construction, and the survey documents provided shall be authentic and accurate and meet the needs of production safety of the construction project.  
When conducting survey operations, the survey unit shall strictly implement operating procedures and take measures to ensure the safety of various pipelines, facilities and surrounding buildings and structures.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　设计单位应当按照法律、法规和工程建设强制性标准进行设计，防止因设计不合理导致生产安全事故的发生。  
设计单位应当考虑施工安全操作和防护的需要，对涉及施工安全的重点部位和环节在设计文件中注明，并对防范生产安全事故提出指导意见。  
采用新结构、新材料、新工艺的建设工程和特殊结构的建设工程，设计单位应当在设计中提出保障施工作业人员安全和预防生产安全事故的措施建议。  
设计单位和注册建筑师等注册执业人员应当对其设计负责。

**Article 13** The design unit shall carry out design in accordance with laws, regulations and mandatory standards for engineering construction to prevent the occurrence of production safety accidents due to unreasonable design.  
The design unit shall take into account the needs of safe construction operations and protection, specify the key parts and aspects relating to construction safety in the design documents, and provide guiding opinions on preventing production safety accidents.  
For construction projects adopting new structures, new materials, new processes and construction projects with special structures, the design unit shall propose measures in the design to ensure the safety of construction workers and prevent production safety accidents.  
The design unit and registered professionals such as registered architects shall be responsible for their designs.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　工程监理单位应当审查施工组织设计中的安全技术措施或者专项施工方案是否符合工程建设强制性标准。  
工程监理单位在实施监理过程中，发现存在安全事故隐患的，应当要求施工单位整改；情况严重的，应当要求施工单位暂时停止施工，并及时报告建设单位。施工单位拒不整改或者不停止施工的，工程监理单位应当及时向有关主管部门报告。  
工程监理单位和监理工程师应当按照法律、法规和工程建设强制性标准实施监理，并对建设工程安全生产承担监理责任。

**Article 14** The project supervision unit shall examine whether the safety technical measures in the construction organization design or the special construction plan comply with the mandatory standards for engineering construction.  
Where the project supervision unit, during the process of supervision, discovers any potential production safety accident, it shall require the construction unit to rectify; where the circumstances are serious, it shall require the construction unit to suspend construction temporarily and promptly report to the project owner. Where the construction unit refuses to rectify or does not suspend construction, the project supervision unit shall promptly report to the relevant competent departments.  
The project supervision unit and the supervising engineer shall carry out supervision in accordance with laws, regulations and mandatory standards for engineering construction, and shall bear the supervision responsibility for the production safety of the construction project.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　为建设工程提供机械设备和配件的单位，应当按照安全施工的要求配备齐全有效的保险、限位等安全设施和装置。

**Article 15** The unit that provides machinery and accessories for construction projects shall, in accordance with the requirements for safe construction, equip complete and effective safety facilities and devices such as insurance and limiters.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　出租的机械设备和施工机具及配件，应当具有生产(制造)许可证、产品合格证。  
出租单位应当对出租的机械设备和施工机具及配件的安全性能进行检测，在签订租赁协议时，应当出具检测合格证明。  
禁止出租检测不合格的机械设备和施工机具及配件。

**Article 16** The machinery, construction tools and accessories for lease shall have a production (manufacturing) license and a product certificate.  
The lessor shall test the safety performance of the machinery, construction tools and accessories for lease, and shall issue a test certificate when signing the lease agreement.  
It is prohibited to lease machinery, construction tools and accessories that fail the test.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在施工现场安装、拆卸施工起重机械和整体提升脚手架、模板等自升式架设设施，必须由具有相应资质的单位承担。  
安装、拆卸施工起重机械和整体提升脚手架、模板等自升式架设设施，应当编制拆装方案、制定安全施工措施，并由专业技术人员现场监督。  
施工起重机械和整体提升脚手架、模板等自升式架设设施安装完毕后，安装单位应当自检，出具自检合格证明，并向施工单位进行安全使用说明，办理验收手续并签字。

**Article 17** The installation and dismantling of construction lifting machinery and self-erecting facilities such as integral lifting scaffolds and formwork at the construction site must be undertaken by a unit with corresponding qualifications.  
The installation and dismantling of construction lifting machinery and self-erecting facilities such as integral lifting scaffolds and formwork shall be carried out with a prepared dismantling plan and formulated safety construction measures, and shall be supervised on site by professional technical personnel.  
After the installation of construction lifting machinery and self-erecting facilities such as integral lifting scaffolds and formwork is completed, the installation unit shall conduct a self-inspection, issue a self-inspection certificate, provide safety use instructions to the construction unit, go through the acceptance procedures and sign.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　施工起重机械和整体提升脚手架、模板等自升式架设设施的使用达到国家规定的检验检测期限的，必须经具有专业资质的检验检测机构检测。经检测不合格的，不得继续使用。

**Article 18** Where the use of construction lifting machinery and self-erecting facilities such as integral lifting scaffolds and formwork reaches the inspection and testing period specified by the State, they must be inspected and tested by an inspection and testing institution with professional qualifications. Those that fail the inspection and testing may not be used continuously.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　检验检测机构对检测合格的施工起重机械和整体提升脚手架、模板等自升式架设设施，应当出具安全合格证明文件，并对检测结果负责。

**Article 19** The inspection and testing institution shall issue a safety certificate for the construction lifting machinery and self-erecting facilities such as integral lifting scaffolds and formwork that pass the inspection and testing, and shall be responsible for the test results.

### 第四章　施工单位的安全责任

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　施工单位从事建设工程的新建、扩建、改建和拆除等活动，应当具备国家规定的注册资本、专业技术人员、技术装备和安全生产等条件，依法取得相应等级的资质证书，并在其资质等级许可的范围内承揽工程。

**Article 20** A construction unit that engages in activities such as new construction, expansion, reconstruction and demolition of construction projects shall possess the conditions such as registered capital, professional technical personnel, technical equipment and production safety as prescribed by the State, obtain a qualification certificate of the corresponding grade according to law, and undertake projects within the scope permitted by its qualification grade.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　施工单位主要负责人依法对本单位的安全生产工作全面负责。施工单位应当建立健全安全生产责任制度和安全生产教育培训制度，制定安全生产规章制度和操作规程，保证本单位安全生产条件所需资金的投入，对所承担的建设工程进行定期和专项安全检查，并做好安全检查记录。  
施工单位的项目负责人应当由取得相应执业资格的人员担任，对建设工程项目的安全施工负责，落实安全生产责任制度、安全生产规章制度和操作规程，确保安全生产费用的有效使用，并根据工程的特点组织制定安全施工措施，消除安全事故隐患，及时、如实报告生产安全事故。

**Article 21** The principal responsible person of a construction unit shall be fully responsible for the production safety work of the unit according to law. The construction unit shall establish and improve the production safety responsibility system and the production safety education and training system, formulate production safety rules and regulations and operating procedures, ensure the input of funds required for the production safety conditions of the unit, conduct regular and special safety inspections on the construction projects it undertakes, and keep records of safety inspections.  
The project leader of a construction unit shall be a person who has obtained the corresponding practicing qualification, shall be responsible for the safe construction of the construction project, implement the production safety responsibility system, production safety rules and regulations and operating procedures, ensure the effective use of production safety expenses, and organize the formulation of safe construction measures according to the characteristics of the project, eliminate potential production safety accidents, and report production safety accidents in a timely and truthful manner.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　施工单位对列入建设工程概算的安全作业环境及安全施工措施所需费用，应当用于施工安全防护用具及设施的采购和更新、安全施工措施的落实、安全生产条件的改善，不得挪作他用。

**Article 22** The expenses required for the safe working environment and safety construction measures included in the budget estimate of a construction project by the construction unit shall be used for the procurement and renewal of construction safety protective equipment and facilities, the implementation of safety construction measures, and the improvement of production safety conditions, and shall not be misappropriated for other purposes.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　施工单位应当设立安全生产管理机构，配备专职安全生产管理人员。  
专职安全生产管理人员负责对安全生产进行现场监督检查。发现安全事故隐患，应当及时向项目负责人和安全生产管理机构报告；对于违章指挥、违章操作的，应当立即制止。  
专职安全生产管理人员的配备办法由国务院建设行政主管部门会同国务院其他有关部门制定。

**Article 23** A construction unit shall set up a production safety management organization and allocate full-time production safety management personnel.  
Full-time production safety management personnel shall be responsible for on-site supervision and inspection of production safety. Where any potential production safety accident is discovered, it shall be promptly reported to the project leader and the production safety management organization; in case of illegal command or illegal operation, it shall be immediately stopped.  
The measures for the allocation of full-time production safety management personnel shall be formulated by the competent administrative department of construction of the State Council jointly with other relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　建设工程实行施工总承包的，由总承包单位对施工现场的安全生产负总责。  
总承包单位应当自行完成建设工程主体结构的施工。  
总承包单位依法将建设工程分包给其他单位的，分包合同中应当明确各自的安全生产方面的权利、义务。总承包单位和分包单位对分包工程的安全生产承担连带责任。  
分包单位应当服从总承包单位的安全生产管理，分包单位不服从管理导致生产安全事故的，由分包单位承担主要责任。

**Article 24** Where a construction project is contracted under a general construction contract, the general contractor shall assume overall responsibility for the production safety of the construction site.  
The general contractor shall complete the construction of the main structure of the construction project on its own.  
Where the general contractor subcontracts the construction project to other units according to law, the subcontract agreement shall specify the respective rights and obligations in respect of production safety. The general contractor and the subcontractor shall bear joint and several liability for the production safety of the subcontracted project.  
The subcontractor shall comply with the production safety management of the general contractor; where the subcontractor, due to non-compliance with the management, causes a production safety accident, the subcontractor shall bear the primary responsibility.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　垂直运输机械作业人员、安装拆卸工、爆破作业人员、起重信号工、登高架设作业人员等特种作业人员，必须按照国家有关规定经过专门的安全作业培训，并取得特种作业操作资格证书后，方可上岗作业。

**Article 25** Special operators such as vertical transport machinery operators, installation and dismantling workers, blasting workers, crane signal workers, and high-altitude erectors must undergo special safety operation training in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and obtain a special operation qualification certificate before they can take up their posts.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　施工单位应当在施工组织设计中编制安全技术措施和施工现场临时用电方案，对下列达到一定规模的危险性较大的分部分项工程编制专项施工方案，并附具安全验算结果，经施工单位技术负责人、总监理工程师签字后实施，由专职安全生产管理人员进行现场监督：  
(一)基坑支护与降水工程；  
(二)土方开挖工程；  
(三)模板工程；  
(四)起重吊装工程；  
(五)脚手架工程；  
(六)拆除、爆破工程；  
(七)国务院建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门规定的其他危险性较大的工程。  
对前款所列工程中涉及深基坑、地下暗挖工程、高大模板工程的专项施工方案，施工单位还应当组织专家进行论证、审查。  
本条第一款规定的达到一定规模的危险性较大工程的标准，由国务院建设行政主管部门会同国务院其他有关部门制定。

**Article 26** A construction unit shall prepare safety technical measures and a temporary electricity plan for the construction site in the construction organization design, prepare special construction plans for the following sub-projects and part projects that are of a certain scale and involve greater risks, attach the safety verification results, and implement them after being signed by the technical leader of the construction unit and the chief supervising engineer, with on-site supervision by full-time production safety management personnel:  
(1) foundation pit support and dewatering engineering;  
(2) earth excavation engineering;  
(3) formwork engineering;  
(4) hoisting engineering;  
(5) scaffolding engineering;  
(6) demolition and blasting engineering;  
(7) other projects involving greater risks as specified by the competent administrative department of construction of the State Council or other relevant departments.  
For the special construction plans involving deep foundation pits, underground concealed excavation works, and high and large formwork works among the projects listed in the preceding paragraph, the construction unit shall also organize experts to conduct argumentation and review.  
The standards for projects involving greater risks of a certain scale as specified in the first paragraph of this Article shall be formulated by the competent administrative department of construction of the State Council jointly with other relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　建设工程施工前，施工单位负责项目管理的技术人员应当对有关安全施工的技术要求向施工作业班组、作业人员作出详细说明，并由双方签字确认。

**Article 27** Before the commencement of construction of a construction project, the technical personnel responsible for project management of the construction unit shall make detailed explanations on the technical requirements for safe construction to the construction work teams and workers, and both parties shall sign for confirmation.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　施工单位应当在施工现场入口处、施工起重机械、临时用电设施、脚手架、出入通道口、楼梯口、电梯井口、孔洞口、桥梁口、隧道口、基坑边沿、爆破物及有害危险气体和液体存放处等危险部位，设置明显的安全警示标志。安全警示标志必须符合国家标准。  
施工单位应当根据不同施工阶段和周围环境及季节、气候的变化，在施工现场采取相应的安全施工措施。施工现场暂时停止施工的，施工单位应当做好现场防护，所需费用由责任方承担，或者按照合同约定执行。

**Article 28** A construction unit shall set up conspicuous safety warning signs at hazardous parts such as the entrance of the construction site, construction lifting machinery, temporary power facilities, scaffolds, passageways, stair openings, elevator shaft openings, holes, bridge openings, tunnel openings, edge of foundation pits, storage places for explosives and harmful dangerous gases and liquids. Safety warning signs must comply with national standards.  
A construction unit shall adopt corresponding safety construction measures at the construction site according to different construction stages, surrounding environment, and changes in seasons and climate. Where construction at the construction site is temporarily suspended, the construction unit shall take protective measures on site, and the expenses required shall be borne by the responsible party or executed in accordance with the contract agreement.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　施工单位应当将施工现场的办公、生活区与作业区分开设置，并保持安全距离；办公、生活区的选址应当符合安全性要求。职工的膳食、饮水、休息场所等应当符合卫生标准。施工单位不得在尚未竣工的建筑物内设置员工集体宿舍。  
施工现场临时搭建的建筑物应当符合安全使用要求。施工现场使用的装配式活动房屋应当具有产品合格证。

**Article 29** A construction unit shall set up separate offices, living areas and work areas at the construction site and maintain a safe distance; the selection of sites for offices and living areas shall meet safety requirements. The dining, drinking water, rest places, etc. for employees shall meet hygiene standards. A construction unit shall not set up employee dormitories in buildings that have not yet been completed.  
Temporary buildings erected at the construction site shall meet safe use requirements. Prefabricated movable houses used at the construction site shall have product certificates.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　施工单位对因建设工程施工可能造成损害的毗邻建筑物、构筑物和地下管线等，应当采取专项防护措施。  
施工单位应当遵守有关环境保护法律、法规的规定，在施工现场采取措施，防止或者减少粉尘、废气、废水、固体废物、噪声、振动和施工照明对人和环境的危害和污染。  
在城市市区内的建设工程，施工单位应当对施工现场实行封闭围挡。

**Article 30** A construction unit shall take special protective measures for adjacent buildings, structures and underground pipelines that may be damaged due to construction of the construction project.  
A construction unit shall comply with the provisions of relevant environmental protection laws and regulations, and take measures at the construction site to prevent or reduce harm and pollution to people and the environment from dust, exhaust gas, wastewater, solid waste, noise, vibration and construction lighting.  
For construction projects within urban areas, the construction unit shall enclose the construction site with barriers.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　施工单位应当在施工现场建立消防安全责任制度，确定消防安全责任人，制定用火、用电、使用易燃易爆材料等各项消防安全管理制度和操作规程，设置消防通道、消防水源，配备消防设施和灭火器材，并在施工现场入口处设置明显标志。

**Article 31** A construction unit shall establish a fire safety responsibility system at the construction site, designate fire safety responsible persons, formulate various fire safety management systems and operating procedures for the use of fire, electricity, and flammable and explosive materials, set up fire passages and fire water sources, equip fire-fighting facilities and fire extinguishers, and set up conspicuous signs at the entrance of the construction site.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　施工单位应当向作业人员提供安全防护用具和安全防护服装，并书面告知危险岗位的操作规程和违章操作的危害。  
作业人员有权对施工现场的作业条件、作业程序和作业方式中存在的安全问题提出批评、检举和控告，有权拒绝违章指挥和强令冒险作业。  
在施工中发生危及人身安全的紧急情况时，作业人员有权立即停止作业或者在采取必要的应急措施后撤离危险区域。

**Article 32** A construction unit shall provide workers with safety protective equipment and safety protective clothing, and inform them in writing of the operating procedures for hazardous positions and the dangers of illegal operations.  
Workers have the right to criticize, report and complain about the safety problems existing in the working conditions, working procedures and working methods at the construction site, and have the right to refuse illegal command and forced risky operations.  
Where an emergency situation endangering personal safety occurs during construction, workers have the right to immediately stop work or evacuate the dangerous area after taking necessary emergency measures.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　作业人员应当遵守安全施工的强制性标准、规章制度和操作规程，正确使用安全防护用具、机械设备等。

**Article 33** Workers shall comply with the mandatory standards, rules and regulations and operating procedures for safe construction, and correctly use safety protective equipment, machinery, etc.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　施工单位采购、租赁的安全防护用具、机械设备、施工机具及配件，应当具有生产(制造)许可证、产品合格证，并在进入施工现场前进行查验。  
施工现场的安全防护用具、机械设备、施工机具及配件必须由专人管理，定期进行检查、维修和保养，建立相应的资料档案，并按照国家有关规定及时报废。

**Article 34** The safety protective equipment, machinery, construction tools and accessories purchased or leased by a construction unit shall have a production (manufacturing) license and a product certificate, and shall be inspected before entering the construction site.  
Safety protective equipment, machinery, construction tools and accessories at the construction site must be managed by designated persons, regularly inspected, repaired and maintained, with corresponding archives established, and shall be scrapped in a timely manner in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　施工单位在使用施工起重机械和整体提升脚手架、模板等自升式架设设施前，应当组织有关单位进行验收，也可以委托具有相应资质的检验检测机构进行验收；使用承租的机械设备和施工机具及配件的，由施工总承包单位、分包单位、出租单位和安装单位共同进行验收。验收合格的方可使用。  
《特种设备安全监察条例》规定的施工起重机械，在验收前应当经有相应资质的检验检测机构监督检验合格。  
施工单位应当自施工起重机械和整体提升脚手架、模板等自升式架设设施验收合格之日起30日内，向建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门登记。登记标志应当置于或者附着于该设备的显著位置。

**Article 35** Before using construction lifting machinery and self-erecting facilities such as integral lifting scaffolds and formwork, a construction unit shall organize relevant units for acceptance inspection, or may entrust an inspection and testing institution with corresponding qualifications for acceptance inspection; where machinery and construction tools and accessories are leased, the acceptance inspection shall be carried out jointly by the general contractor, subcontractor, lessor and installation unit. Only those that pass the acceptance inspection may be used.  
Construction lifting machinery specified in the Regulations on Safety Supervision of Special Equipment shall pass the supervision inspection by an inspection and testing institution with corresponding qualifications before acceptance inspection.  
A construction unit shall, within 30 days from the date of passing the acceptance inspection of construction lifting machinery and self-erecting facilities such as integral lifting scaffolds and formwork, register with the construction administrative department or other relevant departments. The registration mark shall be placed or attached on a conspicuous position of the equipment.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　施工单位的主要负责人、项目负责人、专职安全生产管理人员应当经建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门考核合格后方可任职。  
施工单位应当对管理人员和作业人员每年至少进行一次安全生产教育培训，其教育培训情况记入个人工作档案。安全生产教育培训考核不合格的人员，不得上岗。

**Article 36** The principal responsible person, project leader, and full-time production safety management personnel of a construction unit shall pass the assessment by the construction administrative department or other relevant departments before taking office.  
A construction unit shall conduct at least one production safety education and training session per year for management personnel and workers, and the education and training records shall be kept in their personal work files. Personnel who fail the production safety education and training assessment shall not take up their posts.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　作业人员进入新的岗位或者新的施工现场前，应当接受安全生产教育培训。未经教育培训或者教育培训考核不合格的人员，不得上岗作业。  
施工单位在采用新技术、新工艺、新设备、新材料时，应当对作业人员进行相应的安全生产教育培训。

**Article 37** Before workers enter a new post or new construction site, they shall receive production safety education and training. Personnel who have not received education and training or have failed the education and training assessment shall not take up posts.  
When a construction unit adopts new technologies, new processes, new equipment or new materials, it shall conduct corresponding production safety education and training for workers.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　施工单位应当为施工现场从事危险作业的人员办理意外伤害保险。  
意外伤害保险费由施工单位支付。实行施工总承包的，由总承包单位支付意外伤害保险费。意外伤害保险期限自建设工程开工之日起至竣工验收合格止。

**Article 38** A construction unit shall purchase accident injury insurance for personnel engaged in hazardous operations at the construction site.  
The accident injury insurance premium shall be paid by the construction unit. Where a general construction contract is adopted, the general contractor shall pay the accident injury insurance premium. The term of accident injury insurance shall run from the commencement date of the construction project to the date of acceptance of the completed project as qualified.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国务院负责安全生产监督管理的部门依照《中华人民共和国安全生产法》的规定，对全国建设工程安全生产工作实施综合监督管理。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责安全生产监督管理的部门依照《中华人民共和国安全生产法》的规定，对本行政区域内建设工程安全生产工作实施综合监督管理。

**Article 39** The department responsible for the supervision and administration of production safety under the State Council shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Production Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, carry out comprehensive supervision and administration over the production safety of construction projects nationwide.  
The departments responsible for the supervision and administration of production safety of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Production Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, carry out comprehensive supervision and administration over the production safety of construction projects within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国务院建设行政主管部门对全国的建设工程安全生产实施监督管理。国务院铁路、交通、水利等有关部门按照国务院规定的职责分工，负责有关专业建设工程安全生产的监督管理。  
县级以上地方人民政府建设行政主管部门对本行政区域内的建设工程安全生产实施监督管理。县级以上地方人民政府交通、水利等有关部门在各自的职责范围内，负责本行政区域内的专业建设工程安全生产的监督管理。

**Article 40** The competent administrative department of construction of the State Council shall carry out supervision and administration over the production safety of construction projects nationwide. The relevant departments of the State Council for railways, communications, water resources, etc., in accordance with the division of duties prescribed by the State Council, shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of production safety of relevant specialized construction projects.  
The competent administrative departments of construction of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall carry out supervision and administration over the production safety of construction projects within their respective administrative regions. The relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level for communications, water resources, etc., within their respective duties, shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of production safety of specialized construction projects within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　建设行政主管部门和其他有关部门应当将[本条例第十条](#art-10)、第十一条规定的有关资料的主要内容抄送同级负责安全生产监督管理的部门。

**Article 41** The construction administrative departments and other relevant departments shall send copies of the main contents of the relevant data specified in Articles 10 and 11 of these Regulations to the departments responsible for the supervision and administration of production safety at the same level.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　建设行政主管部门在审核发放施工许可证时，应当对建设工程是否有安全施工措施进行审查，对没有安全施工措施的，不得颁发施工许可证。  
建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门对建设工程是否有安全施工措施进行审查时，不得收取费用。

**Article 42** When examining and issuing a construction permit, the construction administrative department shall examine whether the construction project has safety construction measures, and shall not issue a construction permit if there are no safety construction measures.  
The construction administrative department or other relevant departments shall not charge fees when examining whether a construction project has safety construction measures.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　县级以上人民政府负有建设工程安全生产监督管理职责的部门在各自的职责范围内履行安全监督检查职责时，有权采取下列措施：  
(一)要求被检查单位提供有关建设工程安全生产的文件和资料；  
(二)进入被检查单位施工现场进行检查；  
(三)纠正施工中违反安全生产要求的行为；  
(四)对检查中发现的安全事故隐患，责令立即排除；重大安全事故隐患排除前或者排除过程中无法保证安全的，责令从危险区域内撤出作业人员或者暂时停止施工。

**Article 43** The departments with the responsibility for supervision and administration of production safety of construction projects of the people's governments at or above the county level, when performing their safety supervision and inspection duties within their respective duties, shall have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) require the unit under inspection to provide documents and data relating to production safety of the construction project;  
(2) enter the construction site of the unit under inspection for inspection;  
(3) correct acts violating production safety requirements during construction;  
(4) order the immediate elimination of potential production safety accidents discovered during inspection; where safety cannot be guaranteed before the elimination of major potential production safety accidents or during the elimination process, order the withdrawal of workers from the dangerous area or the temporary suspension of construction.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门可以将施工现场的监督检查委托给建设工程安全监督机构具体实施。

**Article 44** The construction administrative department or other relevant departments may entrust the supervision and inspection of construction sites to a construction project safety supervision organization for specific implementation.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家对严重危及施工安全的工艺、设备、材料实行淘汰制度。具体目录由国务院建设行政主管部门会同国务院其他有关部门制定并公布。

**Article 45** The State implements a phase-out system for technologies, equipment and materials that seriously endanger construction safety. The specific catalogue shall be formulated and published by the competent administrative department of construction of the State Council jointly with other relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上人民政府建设行政主管部门和其他有关部门应当及时受理对建设工程生产安全事故及安全事故隐患的检举、控告和投诉。

**Article 46** The construction administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall promptly accept reports, accusations and complaints concerning production safety accidents and potential production safety accidents in construction projects.

### 第六章　生产安全事故的应急救援和调查处理

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上地方人民政府建设行政主管部门应当根据本级人民政府的要求，制定本行政区域内建设工程特大生产安全事故应急救援预案。

**Article 47** The competent administrative departments of construction of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the requirements of the people's governments at the same level, formulate emergency rescue plans for extraordinarily serious production safety accidents in construction projects within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　施工单位应当制定本单位生产安全事故应急救援预案，建立应急救援组织或者配备应急救援人员，配备必要的应急救援器材、设备，并定期组织演练。

**Article 48** A construction unit shall formulate an emergency rescue plan for production safety accidents of the unit, establish an emergency rescue organization or allocate emergency rescue personnel, equip necessary emergency rescue equipment and devices, and organize regular drills.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　施工单位应当根据建设工程施工的特点、范围，对施工现场易发生重大事故的部位、环节进行监控，制定施工现场生产安全事故应急救援预案。实行施工总承包的，由总承包单位统一组织编制建设工程生产安全事故应急救援预案，工程总承包单位和分包单位按照应急救援预案，各自建立应急救援组织或者配备应急救援人员，配备救援器材、设备，并定期组织演练。

**Article 49** A construction unit shall, according to the characteristics and scope of construction of the construction project, monitor the parts and aspects of the construction site where major accidents are likely to occur, and formulate an emergency rescue plan for production safety accidents at the construction site. Where a general construction contract is adopted, the general contractor shall uniformly organize the formulation of the emergency rescue plan for production safety accidents of the construction project, and the general contractor and the subcontractor shall, in accordance with the emergency rescue plan, respectively establish emergency rescue organizations or allocate emergency rescue personnel, equip rescue equipment and devices, and organize regular drills.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　施工单位发生生产安全事故，应当按照国家有关伤亡事故报告和调查处理的规定，及时、如实地向负责安全生产监督管理的部门、建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门报告；特种设备发生事故的，还应当同时向特种设备安全监督管理部门报告。接到报告的部门应当按照国家有关规定，如实上报。  
实行施工总承包的建设工程，由总承包单位负责上报事故。

**Article 50** Where a production safety accident occurs in a construction unit, it shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State on reporting and investigation of casualty accidents, promptly and truthfully report to the department responsible for the supervision and administration of production safety, the construction administrative department or other relevant departments; where an accident involving special equipment occurs, it shall also report at the same time to the special equipment safety supervision and administration department. The department receiving the report shall truthfully report upwards in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
For a construction project under a general construction contract, the general contractor shall be responsible for reporting the accident.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　发生生产安全事故后，施工单位应当采取措施防止事故扩大，保护事故现场。需要移动现场物品时，应当做出标记和书面记录，妥善保管有关证物。

**Article 51** After a production safety accident occurs, the construction unit shall take measures to prevent the accident from escalating and protect the accident scene. Where it is necessary to move items at the scene, it shall make markings and written records, and properly preserve relevant evidentiary items.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　建设工程生产安全事故的调查、对事故责任单位和责任人的处罚与处理，按照有关法律、法规的规定执行。

**Article 52** The investigation of production safety accidents in construction projects, and the punishment and handling of the units and persons responsible for the accidents, shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反本条例的规定，县级以上人民政府建设行政主管部门或者其他有关行政管理部门的工作人员，有下列行为之一的，给予降级或者撤职的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任：  
(一)对不具备安全生产条件的施工单位颁发资质证书的；  
(二)对没有安全施工措施的建设工程颁发施工许可证的；  
(三)发现违法行为不予查处的；  
(四)不依法履行监督管理职责的其他行为。

**Article 53** Where any staff member of the competent administrative department of construction or other relevant administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, he or she shall be given an administrative sanction of demotion or removal from office; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Issuing a qualification certificate to a construction unit that does not meet the conditions for safe production;  
(2) Issuing a construction permit for a construction project that does not have safe construction measures;  
(3) Failing to investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered; or  
(4) Other acts of failing to perform supervision and management duties according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本条例的规定，建设单位未提供建设工程安全生产作业环境及安全施工措施所需费用的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，责令该建设工程停止施工。  
建设单位未将保证安全施工的措施或者拆除工程的有关资料报送有关部门备案的，责令限期改正，给予警告。

**Article 54** Where a project owner, in violation of these Regulations, fails to provide the funds required for the safe production working environment and safe construction measures for the construction project, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend the construction of the project.  
Where a project owner fails to submit the measures for ensuring safe construction or the relevant information on the demolition project to the relevant department for recordation, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit and shall be given a warning.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本条例的规定，建设单位有下列行为之一的，责令限期改正，处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款；造成重大安全事故，构成犯罪的，对直接责任人员，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
(一)对勘察、设计、施工、工程监理等单位提出不符合安全生产法律、法规和强制性标准规定的要求的；  
(二)要求施工单位压缩合同约定的工期的；  
(三)将拆除工程发包给不具有相应资质等级的施工单位的。

**Article 55** Where a project owner, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit and shall be fined not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if a major safety accident is caused and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated against the persons directly responsible in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law:  
(1) Putting forward requirements to the survey, design, construction, project supervision and other units that do not comply with the provisions of laws, regulations and mandatory standards on safe production;  
(2) Requiring the construction unit to shorten the contractually agreed construction period; or  
(3) Contracting out the demolition project to a construction unit that does not have the corresponding qualification grade.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本条例的规定，勘察单位、设计单位有下列行为之一的，责令限期改正，处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，降低资质等级，直至吊销资质证书；造成重大安全事故，构成犯罪的，对直接责任人员，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
(一)未按照法律、法规和工程建设强制性标准进行勘察、设计的；  
(二)采用新结构、新材料、新工艺的建设工程和特殊结构的建设工程，设计单位未在设计中提出保障施工作业人员安全和预防生产安全事故的措施建议的。

**Article 56** Where a survey unit or design unit, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit and shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification, its qualification grade shall be lowered, or its qualification certificate shall be revoked; if a major safety accident is caused and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated against the persons directly responsible in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law:  
(1) Failing to conduct survey or design in accordance with the laws, regulations and mandatory standards for construction projects; or  
(2) For construction projects adopting new structures, new materials or new processes, and construction projects with special structures, the design unit fails to propose in the design measures to ensure the safety of construction workers and prevent production safety accidents.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本条例的规定，工程监理单位有下列行为之一的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，责令停业整顿，并处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，降低资质等级，直至吊销资质证书；造成重大安全事故，构成犯罪的，对直接责任人员，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
(一)未对施工组织设计中的安全技术措施或者专项施工方案进行审查的；  
(二)发现安全事故隐患未及时要求施工单位整改或者暂时停止施工的；  
(三)施工单位拒不整改或者不停止施工，未及时向有关主管部门报告的；  
(四)未依照法律、法规和工程建设强制性标准实施监理的。

**Article 57** Where a project supervision unit, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification grade shall be lowered, or its qualification certificate shall be revoked; if a major safety accident is caused and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated against the persons directly responsible in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law:  
(1) Failing to review the safety technical measures in the construction organization design or the special construction plan;  
(2) Failing to promptly require the construction unit to rectify a discovered safety accident hazard or to temporarily suspend construction;  
(3) Where the construction unit refuses to rectify or does not suspend construction, failing to promptly report to the relevant competent department; or  
(4) Failing to carry out supervision in accordance with the laws, regulations and mandatory standards for construction projects.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　注册执业人员未执行法律、法规和工程建设强制性标准的，责令停止执业3个月以上1年以下；情节严重的，吊销执业资格证书，5年内不予注册；造成重大安全事故的，终身不予注册；构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 58** Where a registered practicing professional fails to implement the laws, regulations and mandatory standards for construction projects, he or she shall be ordered to suspend practice for not less than three months but not more than one year; if the circumstances are serious, his or her practicing qualification certificate shall be revoked, and he or she shall not be registered within five years; if a major safety accident is caused, he or she shall not be registered for life; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本条例的规定，为建设工程提供机械设备和配件的单位，未按照安全施工的要求配备齐全有效的保险、限位等安全设施和装置的，责令限期改正，处合同价款1倍以上3倍以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 59** Where a unit providing mechanical equipment and accessories for a construction project, in violation of these Regulations, fails to equip complete and effective safety facilities and devices such as insurance, limit and others in accordance with the requirements for safe construction, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit and shall be fined not less than one time but not more than three times the contract price; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本条例的规定，出租单位出租未经安全性能检测或者经检测不合格的机械设备和施工机具及配件的，责令停业整顿，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 60** Where a lessor, in violation of these Regulations, leases out mechanical equipment, construction machines and tools and accessories that have not undergone safety performance inspection or have been inspected and found unqualified, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反本条例的规定，施工起重机械和整体提升脚手架、模板等自升式架设设施安装、拆卸单位有下列行为之一的，责令限期改正，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，降低资质等级，直至吊销资质证书；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
(一)未编制拆装方案、制定安全施工措施的；  
(二)未由专业技术人员现场监督的；  
(三)未出具自检合格证明或者出具虚假证明的；  
(四)未向施工单位进行安全使用说明，办理移交手续的。  
施工起重机械和整体提升脚手架、模板等自升式架设设施安装、拆卸单位有前款规定的第(一)项、第(三)项行为，经有关部门或者单位职工提出后，对事故隐患仍不采取措施，因而发生重大伤亡事故或者造成其他严重后果，构成犯罪的，对直接责任人员，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 61** Where a unit installing or dismantling construction hoisting machinery and self-climbing scaffolding, formwork and other self-climbing erection facilities, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit and shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification, its qualification grade shall be lowered, or its qualification certificate shall be revoked; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law:  
(1) Failing to prepare a dismantling and erection plan or formulate safe construction measures;  
(2) Failing to have professional technical personnel supervise at the site;  
(3) Failing to issue a self-inspection certificate or issuing a false certificate;  
(4) Failing to provide safe-use instructions to the construction unit and complete the handover procedures.  
Where a unit installing or dismantling construction hoisting machinery and self-climbing scaffolding, formwork and other self-climbing erection facilities commits the acts specified in subparagraphs (1) and (3) of the preceding paragraph, and after being notified by the relevant department or the unit's employees, still fails to take measures against the accident hazards, thereby causing a major casualty accident or other serious consequences, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated against the persons directly responsible in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反本条例的规定，施工单位有下列行为之一的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，责令停业整顿，依照《中华人民共和国安全生产法》的有关规定处以罚款；造成重大安全事故，构成犯罪的，对直接责任人员，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任：  
(一)未设立安全生产管理机构、配备专职安全生产管理人员或者分部分项工程施工时无专职安全生产管理人员现场监督的；  
(二)施工单位的主要负责人、项目负责人、专职安全生产管理人员、作业人员或者特种作业人员，未经安全教育培训或者经考核不合格即从事相关工作的；  
(三)未在施工现场的危险部位设置明显的安全警示标志，或者未按照国家有关规定在施工现场设置消防通道、消防水源、配备消防设施和灭火器材的；  
(四)未向作业人员提供安全防护用具和安全防护服装的；  
(五)未按照规定在施工起重机械和整体提升脚手架、模板等自升式架设设施验收合格后登记的；  
(六)使用国家明令淘汰、禁止使用的危及施工安全的工艺、设备、材料的。

**Article 62** Where a construction unit, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and shall be fined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Work Safety; if a major safety accident is caused and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated against the persons directly responsible in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Failing to set up a work safety management institution, allocate full-time work safety management personnel, or having no full-time work safety management personnel to supervise at the site during the construction of sub-projects or sectional projects;  
(2) The main person in charge, project leader, full-time work safety management personnel, operators or special operators of the construction unit engage in relevant work without having received safety education and training or without passing the assessment;  
(3) Failing to set up obvious safety warning signs at dangerous parts of the construction site, or failing to set up fire-fighting access, fire water sources, equip with fire-fighting facilities and fire extinguishers at the construction site in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(4) Failing to provide safety protective appliances and safety protective clothing to operators;  
(5) Failing to register construction hoisting machinery and self-climbing scaffolding, formwork and other self-climbing erection facilities after they have been inspected and accepted as qualified in accordance with the regulations; or  
(6) Using technologies, equipment or materials that are explicitly eliminated or prohibited by the State and that endanger construction safety.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本条例的规定，施工单位挪用列入建设工程概算的安全生产作业环境及安全施工措施所需费用的，责令限期改正，处挪用费用20%以上50%以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 63** Where a construction unit, in violation of these Regulations, misappropriates the funds required for the safe production working environment and safe construction measures included in the budget estimate of the construction project, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit and shall be fined not less than 20% but not more than 50% of the misappropriated amount; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反本条例的规定，施工单位有下列行为之一的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，责令停业整顿，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；造成重大安全事故，构成犯罪的，对直接责任人员，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任：  
(一)施工前未对有关安全施工的技术要求作出详细说明的；  
(二)未根据不同施工阶段和周围环境及季节、气候的变化，在施工现场采取相应的安全施工措施，或者在城市市区内的建设工程的施工现场未实行封闭围挡的；  
(三)在尚未竣工的建筑物内设置员工集体宿舍的；  
(四)施工现场临时搭建的建筑物不符合安全使用要求的；  
(五)未对因建设工程施工可能造成损害的毗邻建筑物、构筑物和地下管线等采取专项防护措施的。  
施工单位有前款规定第(四)项、第(五)项行为，造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 64** Where a construction unit, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if a major safety accident is caused and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated against the persons directly responsible in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Failing to provide detailed explanations of the technical requirements for safe construction before construction begins;  
(2) Failing to adopt corresponding safe construction measures at the construction site according to different construction stages, surrounding environment and changes in seasons and climate, or failing to implement enclosed hoarding at the construction site of a construction project within urban areas;  
(3) Setting up collective dormitories for employees in a building that has not yet been completed;  
(4) The temporary buildings at the construction site do not meet the requirements for safe use; or  
(5) Failing to take special protective measures for adjacent buildings, structures and underground pipelines that may be damaged by the construction of the project.  
Where a construction unit commits the acts specified in subparagraphs (4) and (5) of the preceding paragraph and causes losses, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本条例的规定，施工单位有下列行为之一的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，责令停业整顿，并处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，降低资质等级，直至吊销资质证书；造成重大安全事故，构成犯罪的，对直接责任人员，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
(一)安全防护用具、机械设备、施工机具及配件在进入施工现场前未经查验或者查验不合格即投入使用的；  
(二)使用未经验收或者验收不合格的施工起重机械和整体提升脚手架、模板等自升式架设设施的；  
(三)委托不具有相应资质的单位承担施工现场安装、拆卸施工起重机械和整体提升脚手架、模板等自升式架设设施的；  
(四)在施工组织设计中未编制安全技术措施、施工现场临时用电方案或者专项施工方案的。

**Article 65** Where a construction unit, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification grade shall be lowered, or its qualification certificate shall be revoked; if a major safety accident is caused and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated against the persons directly responsible in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law:  
(1) Using safety protective appliances, mechanical equipment, construction machines and tools and accessories before they have been inspected before entering the construction site, or using them after being inspected and found unqualified;  
(2) Using construction hoisting machinery and self-climbing scaffolding, formwork and other self-climbing erection facilities that have not been inspected and accepted or have been inspected and accepted as unqualified;  
(3) Entrusting a unit without corresponding qualifications to install or dismantle construction hoisting machinery and self-climbing scaffolding, formwork and other self-climbing erection facilities at the construction site; or  
(4) Failing to prepare safety technical measures, temporary electricity use plan for the construction site or special construction plan in the construction organization design.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本条例的规定，施工单位的主要负责人、项目负责人未履行安全生产管理职责的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，责令施工单位停业整顿；造成重大安全事故、重大伤亡事故或者其他严重后果，构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。  
作业人员不服管理、违反规章制度和操作规程冒险作业造成重大伤亡事故或者其他严重后果，构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。  
施工单位的主要负责人、项目负责人有前款违法行为，尚不够刑事处罚的，处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款或者按照管理权限给予撤职处分；自刑罚执行完毕或者受处分之日起，5年内不得担任任何施工单位的主要负责人、项目负责人。

**Article 66** Where the main person in charge or the project leader of a construction unit, in violation of these Regulations, fails to perform the duties of work safety management, he or she shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit; if he or she fails to make corrections within the time limit, the construction unit shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification; if a major safety accident, major casualty accident or other serious consequences are caused, and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
Where an operator, defying management, violating rules and regulations and operating recklessly in violation of operating procedures, causes a major casualty accident or other serious consequences, and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
Where the main person in charge or the project leader of a construction unit commits the illegal act specified in the preceding paragraph, which is not serious enough for criminal punishment, he or she shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, or shall be given the sanction of removal from office in accordance with the management authority; from the date of completion of the execution of the criminal punishment or from the date of being subjected to the sanction, he or she shall not serve as the main person in charge or project leader of any construction unit within five years.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　施工单位取得资质证书后，降低安全生产条件的，责令限期改正；经整改仍未达到与其资质等级相适应的安全生产条件的，责令停业整顿，降低其资质等级直至吊销资质证书。

**Article 67** Where a construction unit, after obtaining its qualification certificate, reduces the conditions for safe production, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit; if after rectification it still fails to meet the safe production conditions corresponding to its qualification grade, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification, and its qualification grade shall be lowered until its qualification certificate is revoked.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　本条例规定的行政处罚，由建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门依照法定职权决定。  
违反消防安全管理规定的行为，由公安消防机构依法处罚。  
有关法律、行政法规对建设工程安全生产违法行为的行政处罚决定机关另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 68** The administrative penalties prescribed in these Regulations shall be decided by the competent administrative department of construction or other relevant departments in accordance with their statutory functions and powers.  
Acts violating the provisions on fire safety management shall be punished by the public security fire-fighting institution according to law.  
Where relevant laws or administrative regulations have other provisions on the organs deciding administrative penalties for illegal acts in work safety of construction projects, such provisions shall prevail.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　抢险救灾和农民自建低层住宅的安全生产管理，不适用本条例。

**Article 69** These Regulations shall not apply to the work safety management of emergency rescue and disaster relief and low-rise residential buildings self-built by farmers.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　军事建设工程的安全生产管理，按照中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 70** The work safety management of military construction projects shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　本条例自2004年2月1日起施行。

**Article 71** These Regulations shall come into force as of February 1, 2004.
