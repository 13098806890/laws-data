# 防治海洋工程建设项目污染损害海洋环境管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2018-03-19  
**生效日期**：2006-11-01

> (2006年9月19日中华人民共和国国务院令第475号公布　根据2017年3月1日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2018年3月19日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了防治和减轻海洋工程建设项目(以下简称海洋工程)污染损害海洋环境，维护海洋生态平衡，保护海洋资源，根据《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国管辖海域内从事海洋工程污染损害海洋环境防治活动，适用本条例。 第三条　本条例所称海洋工程，是指以开发、利用、保护、恢复海洋资源为目的，并且工程主体位于海岸线向海一侧的新建、改建、扩建工程。具体包括： (一)围填海、海上堤坝工程； (二)人工岛、海上和海底物资储藏设施、跨海桥梁、海底隧道工程； (三)海底管道、海底电(光)缆工程； (四)海洋矿产资源勘探开发及其附属工程； (五)海上潮汐电站、波浪电站、温差电站等海洋能源开发利用工程； (六)大型海水养殖场、人工鱼礁工程； (七)盐田、海水淡化等海水综合利用工程； (八)海上娱乐及运动、景观开发工程； (九)国家海洋主管部门会同国务院环境保护主管部门规定的其他海洋工程。 第四条　国家海洋主管部门负责全国海洋工程环境保护工作的监督管理，并接受国务院环境保护主管部门的指导、协调和监督。沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门负责本行政区域毗邻海域海洋工程环境保护工作的监督管理。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了防治和减轻海洋工程建设项目(以下简称海洋工程)污染损害海洋环境，维护海洋生态平衡，保护海洋资源，根据《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Marine Environment Protection Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of preventing and mitigating pollution damage to the marine environment from marine engineering construction projects (hereinafter referred to as marine engineering), maintaining marine ecological balance, and protecting marine resources.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国管辖海域内从事海洋工程污染损害海洋环境防治活动，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to activities for the prevention and control of pollution damage to the marine environment from marine engineering conducted within the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称海洋工程，是指以开发、利用、保护、恢复海洋资源为目的，并且工程主体位于海岸线向海一侧的新建、改建、扩建工程。具体包括：  
(一)围填海、海上堤坝工程；  
(二)人工岛、海上和海底物资储藏设施、跨海桥梁、海底隧道工程；  
(三)海底管道、海底电(光)缆工程；  
(四)海洋矿产资源勘探开发及其附属工程；  
(五)海上潮汐电站、波浪电站、温差电站等海洋能源开发利用工程；  
(六)大型海水养殖场、人工鱼礁工程；  
(七)盐田、海水淡化等海水综合利用工程；  
(八)海上娱乐及运动、景观开发工程；  
(九)国家海洋主管部门会同国务院环境保护主管部门规定的其他海洋工程。

**Article 3** For the purpose of these Regulations, marine engineering means new construction, reconstruction, or expansion projects aimed at developing, utilizing, protecting, or restoring marine resources, with the main part of the project located on the seaward side of the coastline. Specifically, such projects include:  
(1) reclamation of land from the sea and offshore dam projects;  
(2) artificial islands, offshore and seabed material storage facilities, sea-crossing bridges, and submarine tunnel projects;  
(3) submarine pipelines and submarine electric (optical) cable projects;  
(4) exploration and development of marine mineral resources and ancillary projects thereof;  
(5) marine energy development and utilization projects such as offshore tidal power stations, wave power stations, and temperature difference power stations;  
(6) large-scale mariculture farms and artificial reef projects;  
(7) comprehensive seawater utilization projects such as salt pans and seawater desalination;  
(8) offshore recreation, sports, and landscape development projects;  
(9) other marine engineering projects as prescribed by the State ocean administration in conjunction with the environmental protection administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家海洋主管部门负责全国海洋工程环境保护工作的监督管理，并接受国务院环境保护主管部门的指导、协调和监督。沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门负责本行政区域毗邻海域海洋工程环境保护工作的监督管理。

**Article 4** The State ocean administration shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of environmental protection for marine engineering nationwide, and shall accept the guidance, coordination, and supervision of the environmental protection administrative department under the State Council. The ocean administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level along the coast shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of environmental protection for marine engineering in the sea areas adjacent to their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　海洋工程的选址和建设应当符合海洋功能区划、海洋环境保护规划和国家有关环境保护标准，不得影响海洋功能区的环境质量或者损害相邻海域的功能。

**Article 5** The site selection and construction of marine engineering shall conform to the marine functional zoning, marine environmental protection plan, and relevant national environmental protection standards, and shall not affect the environmental quality of marine functional zones or impair the functions of adjacent sea areas.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家海洋主管部门根据国家重点海域污染物排海总量控制指标，分配重点海域海洋工程污染物排海控制数量。

**Article 6** The State ocean administration shall, based on the national total pollutant discharge control indicators for key sea areas, allocate the controlled amounts of pollutant discharge into the sea from marine engineering in key sea areas.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　任何单位和个人对海洋工程污染损害海洋环境、破坏海洋生态等违法行为，都有权向海洋主管部门进行举报。  
接到举报的海洋主管部门应当依法进行调查处理，并为举报人保密。

**Article 7** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report to the ocean administrative department any illegal act such as marine engineering causing pollution damage to the marine environment or damaging the marine ecology.  
The ocean administrative department that receives the report shall, according to law, investigate and handle the matter, and shall keep the reporter confidential.

### 第二章　环境影响评价

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家实行海洋工程环境影响评价制度。  
海洋工程的环境影响评价，应当以工程对海洋环境和海洋资源的影响为重点进行综合分析、预测和评估，并提出相应的生态保护措施，预防、控制或者减轻工程对海洋环境和海洋资源造成的影响和破坏。  
海洋工程环境影响报告书应当依据海洋工程环境影响评价技术标准及其他相关环境保护标准编制。编制环境影响报告书应当使用符合国家海洋主管部门要求的调查、监测资料。

**Article 8** The State shall implement an environmental impact assessment system for marine engineering.  
The environmental impact assessment for marine engineering shall focus on the impact of the project on the marine environment and marine resources, carry out comprehensive analysis, prediction, and evaluation, and propose corresponding ecological protection measures to prevent, control, or mitigate the impact and damage caused by the project to the marine environment and marine resources.  
The environmental impact statement for marine engineering shall be prepared in accordance with the technical standards for environmental impact assessment of marine engineering and other relevant environmental protection standards. The preparation of the environmental impact statement shall use investigation and monitoring data that meet the requirements of the State ocean administration.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　海洋工程环境影响报告书应当包括下列内容：  
(一)工程概况；  
(二)工程所在海域环境现状和相邻海域开发利用情况；  
(三)工程对海洋环境和海洋资源可能造成影响的分析、预测和评估；  
(四)工程对相邻海域功能和其他开发利用活动影响的分析及预测；  
(五)工程对海洋环境影响的经济损益分析和环境风险分析；  
(六)拟采取的环境保护措施及其经济、技术论证；  
(七)公众参与情况；  
(八)环境影响评价结论。  
海洋工程可能对海岸生态环境产生破坏的，其环境影响报告书中应当增加工程对近岸自然保护区等陆地生态系统影响的分析和评价。

**Article 9** The environmental impact statement for marine engineering shall include the following contents:  
(1) project overview;  
(2) current environmental status of the sea area where the project is located and the development and utilization situation of adjacent sea areas;  
(3) analysis, prediction, and evaluation of the possible impacts of the project on the marine environment and marine resources;  
(4) analysis and prediction of the impacts of the project on the functions of adjacent sea areas and other development and utilization activities;  
(5) economic cost-benefit analysis and environmental risk analysis of the project's impact on the marine environment;  
(6) proposed environmental protection measures and their economic and technical justification;  
(7) public participation;  
(8) conclusion of the environmental impact assessment.  
Where a marine engineering project may cause damage to the coastal ecological environment, its environmental impact statement shall include an analysis and evaluation of the project's impact on terrestrial ecosystems such as offshore nature reserves.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　新建、改建、扩建海洋工程的建设单位，应当编制环境影响报告书，报有核准权的海洋主管部门核准。  
海洋主管部门在核准海洋工程环境影响报告书前，应当征求海事、渔业主管部门和军队环境保护部门的意见；必要时，可以举行听证会。其中，围填海工程必须举行听证会。

**Article 10** The construction entity of a new, reconstruction, or expansion marine engineering project shall prepare an environmental impact statement and submit it to the ocean administrative department with the power of approval for approval.  
Before approving the environmental impact statement for the marine engineering project, the ocean administrative department shall seek opinions from the maritime and fisheries administrative departments and the environmental protection department of the military; when necessary, it may hold a hearing. Among these, a hearing must be held for reclamation of land from the sea projects.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　下列海洋工程的环境影响报告书，由国家海洋主管部门核准：  
(一)涉及国家海洋权益、国防安全等特殊性质的工程；  
(二)海洋矿产资源勘探开发及其附属工程；  
(三)50公顷以上的填海工程，100公顷以上的围海工程；  
(四)潮汐电站、波浪电站、温差电站等海洋能源开发利用工程；  
(五)由国务院或者国务院有关部门审批的海洋工程。  
前款规定以外的海洋工程的环境影响报告书，由沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门根据沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定的权限核准。  
海洋工程可能造成跨区域环境影响并且有关海洋主管部门对环境影响评价结论有争议的，该工程的环境影响报告书由其共同的上一级海洋主管部门核准。

**Article 11** The environmental impact statements for the following marine engineering projects shall be approved by the State ocean administration:  
(1) projects involving special natures such as national maritime rights and interests or national defense security;  
(2) exploration and development of marine mineral resources and ancillary projects thereof;  
(3) land reclamation projects of 50 hectares or more, and sea enclosure projects of 100 hectares or more;  
(4) marine energy development and utilization projects such as tidal power stations, wave power stations, and temperature difference power stations;  
(5) marine engineering projects approved by the State Council or relevant departments under the State Council.  
The environmental impact statements for marine engineering projects other than those specified in the preceding paragraph shall be approved by the ocean administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level along the coast according to the authority prescribed by the people's governments of the coastal provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
Where a marine engineering project may cause cross-regional environmental impact and the relevant ocean administrative departments have disputes over the environmental impact assessment conclusion, the environmental impact statement for that project shall be approved by their common superior ocean administrative department.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　海洋主管部门应当自收到海洋工程环境影响报告书之日起60个工作日内，作出是否核准的决定，书面通知建设单位。  
需要补充材料的，应当及时通知建设单位，核准期限从材料补齐之日起重新计算。

**Article 12** The ocean administrative department shall, within 60 working days from the date of receiving the environmental impact statement for the marine engineering project, make a decision on whether to approve it and notify the construction entity in writing.  
If supplementary materials are required, the construction entity shall be promptly notified, and the approval period shall be recalculated from the date the materials are supplemented.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　海洋工程环境影响报告书核准后，工程的性质、规模、地点、生产工艺或者拟采取的环境保护措施等发生重大改变的，建设单位应当重新编制环境影响报告书，报原核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门核准；海洋工程自环境影响报告书核准之日起超过5年方开工建设的，应当在工程开工建设前，将该工程的环境影响报告书报原核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门重新核准。

**Article 13** After the approval of the environmental impact statement for a marine engineering project, if there are major changes in the nature, scale, location, production process, or proposed environmental protection measures of the project, the construction entity shall prepare a new environmental impact statement and submit it to the ocean administrative department that originally approved the environmental impact statement for approval; if a marine engineering project commences construction more than five years after the date of approval of its environmental impact statement, before the project commences construction, the construction entity shall submit the environmental impact statement for that project to the ocean administrative department that originally approved the environmental impact statement for re-approval.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　建设单位可以采取招标方式确定海洋工程的环境影响评价单位。其他任何单位和个人不得为海洋工程指定环境影响评价单位。

**Article 14** The construction entity may determine the environmental impact assessment unit for the marine engineering project by means of bidding. No other entity or individual may designate an environmental impact assessment unit for the marine engineering project.

### 第三章　海洋工程的污染防治

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　海洋工程的环境保护设施应当与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投产使用。

**Article 15** The environmental protection facilities of a marine engineering project shall be designed, constructed, and put into operation simultaneously with the main project.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　海洋工程的初步设计，应当按照环境保护设计规范和经核准的环境影响报告书的要求，编制环境保护篇章，落实环境保护措施和环境保护投资概算。

**Article 16** The preliminary design of a marine engineering project shall, in accordance with the environmental protection design specifications and the requirements of the approved environmental impact statement, include a chapter on environmental protection, and shall implement environmental protection measures and the budgetary estimate for environmental protection investment.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　建设单位应当在海洋工程投入运行之日30个工作日前，向原核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门申请环境保护设施的验收；海洋工程投入试运行的，应当自该工程投入试运行之日起60个工作日内，向原核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门申请环境保护设施的验收。  
分期建设、分期投入运行的海洋工程，其相应的环境保护设施应当分期验收。

**Article 17** The construction entity shall, 30 working days before the date of putting the marine engineering project into operation, apply to the ocean administrative department that originally approved the environmental impact statement for the project for the acceptance check of the environmental protection facilities; if the marine engineering project is put into trial operation, the construction entity shall, within 60 working days from the date of putting the project into trial operation, apply to the ocean administrative department that originally approved the environmental impact statement for the acceptance check of the environmental protection facilities.  
For marine engineering projects constructed and put into operation in stages, the corresponding environmental protection facilities shall be subject to acceptance check in stages.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　海洋主管部门应当自收到环境保护设施验收申请之日起30个工作日内完成验收；验收不合格的，应当限期整改。  
海洋工程需要配套建设的环境保护设施未经海洋主管部门验收或者经验收不合格的，该工程不得投入运行。  
建设单位不得擅自拆除或者闲置海洋工程的环境保护设施。

**Article 18** The ocean administrative department shall complete the acceptance check within 30 working days from the date of receiving the application for acceptance check of the environmental protection facilities; if the acceptance check fails, it shall order rectification within a time limit.  
Where the environmental protection facilities required to be built in conjunction with a marine engineering project have not been accepted by the ocean administrative department or have been accepted but found unqualified, the project shall not be put into operation.  
The construction entity shall not remove or idle the environmental protection facilities of the marine engineering project without authorization.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　海洋工程在建设、运行过程中产生不符合经核准的环境影响报告书的情形的，建设单位应当自该情形出现之日起20个工作日内组织环境影响的后评价，根据后评价结论采取改进措施，并将后评价结论和采取的改进措施报原核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门备案；原核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门也可以责成建设单位进行环境影响的后评价，采取改进措施。

**Article 19** Where, during the construction or operation of a marine engineering project, there arise circumstances that do not conform to the approved environmental impact statement, the construction entity shall, within 20 working days from the date of occurrence of such circumstances, organize a post-environmental impact assessment, take improvement measures based on the conclusions of the post-assessment, and report the conclusions of the post-assessment and the improvement measures taken to the ocean administrative department that originally approved the environmental impact statement for record; the ocean administrative department that originally approved the environmental impact statement may also instruct the construction entity to carry out a post-environmental impact assessment and take improvement measures.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　严格控制围填海工程。禁止在经济生物的自然产卵场、繁殖场、索饵场和鸟类栖息地进行围填海活动。  
围填海工程使用的填充材料应当符合有关环境保护标准。

**Article 20** Reclamation of land from the sea projects shall be strictly controlled. Reclamation activities in the natural spawning grounds, breeding grounds, feeding grounds of economic organisms, and bird habitats shall be prohibited.  
The filling materials used in reclamation of land from the sea projects shall conform to relevant environmental protection standards.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　建设海洋工程，不得造成领海基点及其周围环境的侵蚀、淤积和损害，危及领海基点的稳定。  
进行海上堤坝、跨海桥梁、海上娱乐及运动、景观开发工程建设的，应当采取有效措施防止对海岸的侵蚀或者淤积。

**Article 21** The construction of a marine engineering project shall not cause erosion, siltation, or damage to the base points of the territorial sea and their surrounding environment, thereby endangering the stability of the base points of the territorial sea.  
Where offshore dam, sea-crossing bridge, offshore recreation, sports, or landscape development projects are constructed, effective measures shall be taken to prevent erosion or siltation of the coast.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　污水离岸排放工程排污口的设置应当符合海洋功能区划和海洋环境保护规划，不得损害相邻海域的功能。  
污水离岸排放不得超过国家或者地方规定的排放标准。在实行污染物排海总量控制的海域，不得超过污染物排海总量控制指标。

**Article 22** The setting of discharge outlets for offshore sewage discharge projects shall conform to the marine functional zoning and marine environmental protection plan, and shall not impair the functions of adjacent sea areas.  
Offshore sewage discharge shall not exceed the discharge standards prescribed by the State or local authorities. In sea areas where total pollutant discharge control is implemented, the total pollutant discharge control indicators into the sea shall not be exceeded.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　从事海水养殖的养殖者，应当采取科学的养殖方式，减少养殖饵料对海洋环境的污染。因养殖污染海域或者严重破坏海洋景观的，养殖者应当予以恢复和整治。

**Article 23** Cultivators engaged in mariculture shall adopt scientific cultivation methods to reduce pollution of the marine environment from aquaculture feeds. Where the sea area is polluted or the marine landscape is seriously damaged due to cultivation, the cultivators shall restore and remedy the situation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　建设单位在海洋固体矿产资源勘探开发工程的建设、运行过程中，应当采取有效措施，防止污染物大范围悬浮扩散，破坏海洋环境。

**Article 24** During the construction and operation of exploration and development projects for marine solid mineral resources, the construction entity shall take effective measures to prevent pollutants from being widely suspended and dispersed, thus damaging the marine environment.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　海洋油气矿产资源勘探开发作业中应当配备油水分离设施、含油污水处理设备、排油监控装置、残油和废油回收设施、垃圾粉碎设备。  
海洋油气矿产资源勘探开发作业中所使用的固定式平台、移动式平台、浮式储油装置、输油管线及其他辅助设施，应当符合防渗、防漏、防腐蚀的要求；作业单位应当经常检查，防止发生漏油事故。  
前款所称固定式平台和移动式平台，是指海洋油气矿产资源勘探开发作业中所使用的钻井船、钻井平台、采油平台和其他平台。

**Article 25** In exploration and development operations for offshore oil and gas mineral resources, oil-water separation facilities, oily wastewater treatment equipment, oil discharge monitoring devices, residual oil and waste oil recovery facilities, and waste crushing equipment shall be equipped.  
The fixed platforms, mobile platforms, floating oil storage devices, oil pipelines, and other auxiliary facilities used in exploration and development operations for offshore oil and gas mineral resources shall meet the requirements for anti-seepage, anti-leakage, and anti-corrosion; the operating entity shall conduct regular inspections to prevent oil leakage accidents.  
The fixed platforms and mobile platforms referred to in the preceding paragraph mean drilling vessels, drilling platforms, oil production platforms, and other platforms used in exploration and development operations for offshore oil and gas mineral resources.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　海洋油气矿产资源勘探开发单位应当办理有关污染损害民事责任保险。

**Article 26** Entities engaged in exploration and development of offshore oil and gas mineral resources shall take out insurance for civil liability for pollution damage.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　海洋工程建设过程中需要进行海上爆破作业的，建设单位应当在爆破作业前报告海洋主管部门，海洋主管部门应当及时通报海事、渔业等有关部门。  
进行海上爆破作业，应当设置明显的标志、信号，并采取有效措施保护海洋资源。在重要渔业水域进行炸药爆破作业或者进行其他可能对渔业资源造成损害的作业活动的，应当避开主要经济类鱼虾的产卵期。

**Article 27** Where offshore blasting operations are necessary during the construction of a marine engineering project, the construction entity shall report to the ocean administrative department before the blasting operations, and the ocean administrative department shall promptly notify the maritime, fisheries, and other relevant departments.  
When carrying out offshore blasting operations, clear signs and signals shall be set up, and effective measures shall be taken to protect marine resources. Where explosive blasting operations are carried out in important fishery waters or other operations that may cause damage to fishery resources are conducted, the spawning seasons of main economic fish and shrimp species shall be avoided.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　海洋工程需要拆除或者改作他用的，应当在作业前报原核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门备案。拆除或者改变用途后可能产生重大环境影响的，应当进行环境影响评价。  
海洋工程需要在海上弃置的，应当拆除可能造成海洋环境污染损害或者影响海洋资源开发利用的部分，并按照有关海洋倾倒废弃物管理的规定进行。  
海洋工程拆除时，施工单位应当编制拆除的环境保护方案，采取必要的措施，防止对海洋环境造成污染和损害。

**Article 28** Where a marine engineering project needs to be dismantled or converted to other uses, it shall be reported to the ocean administrative department that originally approved the environmental impact statement for the project for record before the operation. Where dismantling or change of use may cause significant environmental impact, an environmental impact assessment shall be conducted.  
Where a marine engineering project needs to be discarded at sea, the parts that may cause pollution damage to the marine environment or affect the development and utilization of marine resources shall be dismantled, and the operation shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions on the management of marine dumping of wastes.  
When dismantling a marine engineering project, the construction entity shall prepare an environmental protection plan for dismantling and take necessary measures to prevent pollution and damage to the marine environment.

### 第四章　污染物排放管理

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　海洋油气矿产资源勘探开发作业中产生的污染物的处置，应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)含油污水不得直接或者经稀释排放入海，应当经处理符合国家有关排放标准后再排放；  
(二)塑料制品、残油、废油、油基泥浆、含油垃圾和其他有毒有害残液残渣，不得直接排放或者弃置入海，应当集中储存在专门容器中，运回陆地处理。

**Article 29** The disposal of pollutants generated from exploration and development operations for offshore oil and gas mineral resources shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) Oily wastewater shall not be discharged directly or after dilution into the sea; it shall be discharged after treatment to meet relevant national discharge standards;  
(2) Plastic products, residual oil, waste oil, oil-based mud, oily garbage, and other toxic and hazardous residual liquids and residues shall not be directly discharged or discarded into the sea; they shall be centrally stored in special containers and transported back to land for treatment.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　严格控制向水基泥浆中添加油类，确需添加的，应当如实记录并向原核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门报告添加油的种类和数量。禁止向海域排放含油量超过国家规定标准的水基泥浆和钻屑。

**Article 30** The addition of oil to water-based mud shall be strictly controlled. Where addition is truly necessary, the types and quantities of oil added shall be truthfully recorded and reported to the ocean administrative department that originally approved the environmental impact statement for the project. The discharge of water-based mud and drill cuttings with oil content exceeding the national standards into the sea is prohibited.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　建设单位在海洋工程试运行或者正式投入运行后，应当如实记录污染物排放设施、处理设备的运转情况及其污染物的排放、处置情况，并按照国家海洋主管部门的规定，定期向原核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门报告。

**Article 31** After the trial operation or formal operation of a marine engineering project, the construction entity shall truthfully record the operation of pollutant discharge facilities and treatment equipment as well as the discharge and disposal of pollutants, and shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State ocean administration, periodically report to the ocean administrative department that originally approved the environmental impact statement for the project.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门，应当按照各自的权限核定海洋工程排放污染物的种类、数量，根据国务院价格主管部门和财政部门制定的收费标准确定排污者应当缴纳的排污费数额。  
排污者应当到指定的商业银行缴纳排污费。

**Article 32** The ocean administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to their respective authority, verify the types and quantities of pollutants discharged from marine engineering, and determine the amount of pollution discharge fees payable by the discharger according to the fee standards formulated by the competent pricing department and the financial department under the State Council.  
The discharger shall pay the pollution discharge fees at the designated commercial bank.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　海洋油气矿产资源勘探开发作业中应当安装污染物流量自动监控仪器，对生产污水、机舱污水和生活污水的排放进行计量。

**Article 33** In exploration and development operations for offshore oil and gas mineral resources, automatic pollutant flow monitoring instruments shall be installed to measure the discharge of production wastewater, engine room wastewater, and domestic sewage.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　禁止向海域排放油类、酸液、碱液、剧毒废液和高、中水平放射性废水；严格限制向海域排放低水平放射性废水，确需排放的，应当符合国家放射性污染防治标准。  
严格限制向大气排放含有毒物质的气体，确需排放的，应当经过净化处理，并不得超过国家或者地方规定的排放标准；向大气排放含放射性物质的气体，应当符合国家放射性污染防治标准。  
严格控制向海域排放含有不易降解的有机物和重金属的废水；其他污染物的排放应当符合国家或者地方标准。

**Article 34** The discharge of oil, acid solutions, alkali solutions, highly toxic waste liquids, and high- and intermediate-level radioactive wastewater into the sea is prohibited; the discharge of low-level radioactive wastewater into the sea shall be strictly restricted. Where discharge is truly necessary, it shall comply with national radioactive pollution prevention and control standards.  
The discharge of gases containing toxic substances into the atmosphere shall be strictly restricted. Where discharge is truly necessary, the gases shall be purified and shall not exceed the national or local discharge standards; the discharge of gases containing radioactive substances into the atmosphere shall comply with national radioactive pollution prevention and control standards.  
The discharge of wastewater containing organic substances that are not easily biodegradable and heavy metals into the sea shall be strictly controlled; the discharge of other pollutants shall comply with national or local standards.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　海洋工程排污费全额纳入财政预算，实行“收支两条线”管理，并全部专项用于海洋环境污染防治。具体办法由国务院财政部门会同国家海洋主管部门制定。

**Article 35** The pollution discharge fees for marine engineering shall be fully included in the fiscal budget, managed under the system of 'separate management of revenue and expenditure', and shall be used exclusively for the prevention and control of marine environmental pollution. The specific measures shall be formulated by the financial department under the State Council in conjunction with the State ocean administration.

### 第五章　污染事故的预防和处理

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　建设单位应当在海洋工程正式投入运行前制定防治海洋工程污染损害海洋环境的应急预案，报原核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门和有关主管部门备案。

**Article 36** The construction entity shall, before the formal operation of the marine engineering project, formulate an emergency plan for the prevention and control of pollution damage to the marine environment from the marine engineering project, and submit it to the ocean administrative department that originally approved the environmental impact statement and relevant competent departments for record.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　防治海洋工程污染损害海洋环境的应急预案应当包括以下内容：  
(一)工程及其相邻海域的环境、资源状况；  
(二)污染事故风险分析；  
(三)应急设施的配备；  
(四)污染事故的处理方案。

**Article 37** The emergency plan for the prevention and control of pollution damage to the marine environment from a marine engineering project shall include the following contents:  
(1) environmental and resource conditions of the project and its adjacent sea areas;  
(2) risk analysis of pollution accidents;  
(3) configuration of emergency facilities;  
(4) treatment plans for pollution accidents.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　海洋工程在建设、运行期间，由于发生事故或者其他突发性事件，造成或者可能造成海洋环境污染事故时，建设单位应当立即向可能受到污染的沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门或者其他有关主管部门报告，并采取有效措施，减轻或者消除污染，同时通报可能受到危害的单位和个人。  
沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门或者其他有关主管部门接到报告后，应当按照污染事故分级规定及时向县级以上人民政府和上级有关主管部门报告。县级以上人民政府和有关主管部门应当按照各自的职责，立即派人赶赴现场，采取有效措施，消除或者减轻危害，对污染事故进行调查处理。

**Article 38** Where, during the construction or operation of a marine engineering project, a pollution accident occurs or is likely to occur due to an accident or other emergency, the construction entity shall immediately report to the ocean administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level along the coast that may be affected, or other relevant competent departments, and shall take effective measures to mitigate or eliminate the pollution, and simultaneously notify the entities and individuals that may be harmed.  
Upon receiving the report, the ocean administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level along the coast or other relevant competent department shall, in accordance with the classification of pollution accidents, promptly report to the people's government at or above the county level and the superior relevant competent department. The people's government at or above the county level and the relevant competent departments shall, in accordance with their respective responsibilities, immediately send personnel to the scene, take effective measures to eliminate or mitigate the harm, and investigate and handle the pollution accident.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　在海洋自然保护区内进行海洋工程建设活动，应当按照国家有关海洋自然保护区的规定执行。

**Article 39** The carrying out of marine engineering construction activities within marine nature reserves shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant national provisions on marine nature reserves.

### 第六章　监督检查

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门负责海洋工程污染损害海洋环境防治的监督检查，对违反海洋污染防治法律、法规的行为进行查处。  
县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门的监督检查人员应当严格按照法律、法规规定的程序和权限进行监督检查。

**Article 40** The ocean administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and inspection of the prevention and control of pollution damage to the marine environment from marine engineering, and shall investigate and deal with acts violating marine pollution prevention and control laws and regulations.  
The supervision and inspection personnel of the ocean administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall conduct supervision and inspection strictly in accordance with the procedures and authority prescribed by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门依法对海洋工程进行现场检查时，有权采取下列措施：  
(一)要求被检查单位或者个人提供与环境保护有关的文件、证件、数据以及技术资料等，进行查阅或者复制；  
(二)要求被检查单位负责人或者相关人员就有关问题作出说明；  
(三)进入被检查单位的工作现场进行监测、勘查、取样检验、拍照、摄像；  
(四)检查各项环境保护设施、设备和器材的安装、运行情况；  
(五)责令违法者停止违法活动，接受调查处理；  
(六)要求违法者采取有效措施，防止污染事态扩大。

**Article 41** When conducting on-site inspections of marine engineering according to law, the ocean administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) requiring the entity or individual under inspection to provide documents, certificates, data, and technical materials related to environmental protection, and to consult or copy them;  
(2) requiring the person in charge of the entity under inspection or relevant personnel to make explanations on relevant issues;  
(3) entering the work site of the entity under inspection to conduct monitoring, survey, sample testing, photography, and video recording;  
(4) inspecting the installation and operation of various environmental protection facilities, equipment, and devices;  
(5) ordering the violator to cease illegal activities and accept investigation and handling;  
(6) requiring the violator to take effective measures to prevent the expansion of the pollution situation.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门的监督检查人员进行现场执法检查时，应当出示规定的执法证件。用于执法检查、巡航监视的公务飞机、船舶和车辆应当有明显的执法标志。

**Article 42** When conducting on-site law enforcement inspections, the supervision and inspection personnel of the ocean administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall present the prescribed law enforcement credentials. The official aircraft, vessels, and vehicles used for law enforcement inspections and cruise surveillance shall bear clear law enforcement marks.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　被检查单位和个人应当如实提供材料，不得拒绝或者阻碍监督检查人员依法执行公务。  
有关单位和个人对海洋主管部门的监督检查工作应当予以配合。

**Article 43** The entity or individual under inspection shall truthfully provide materials and shall not refuse or obstruct the supervision and inspection personnel from performing their official duties according to law.  
The relevant entities and individuals shall cooperate with the ocean administrative departments in their supervision and inspection work.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门对违反海洋污染防治法律、法规的行为，应当依法作出行政处理决定；有关海洋主管部门不依法作出行政处理决定的，上级海洋主管部门有权责令其依法作出行政处理决定或者直接作出行政处理决定。

**Article 44** The ocean administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, make administrative handling decisions on acts violating marine pollution prevention and control laws and regulations; where the relevant ocean administrative department fails to make an administrative handling decision according to law, the superior ocean administrative department shall have the power to order it to make an administrative handling decision according to law or directly make an administrative handling decision.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　建设单位违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由负责核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门责令停止建设、运行，限期补办手续，并处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款：  
(一)环境影响报告书未经核准，擅自开工建设的；  
(二)海洋工程环境保护设施未申请验收或者经验收不合格即投入运行的。

**Article 45** Where a construction entity violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the ocean administrative department responsible for approving the environmental impact statement for the project shall order the cessation of construction or operation, require the completion of the relevant procedures within a time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan:  
(1) commencing construction without the approval of the environmental impact statement;  
(2) putting the marine engineering project into operation without applying for acceptance check of the environmental protection facilities or after the acceptance check is found unqualified.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　建设单位违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由原核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门责令停止建设、运行，限期补办手续，并处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款：  
(一)海洋工程的性质、规模、地点、生产工艺或者拟采取的环境保护措施发生重大改变，未重新编制环境影响报告书报原核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门核准的；  
(二)自环境影响报告书核准之日起超过5年，海洋工程方开工建设，其环境影响报告书未重新报原核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门核准的；  
(三)海洋工程需要拆除或者改作他用时，未报原核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门备案或者未按要求进行环境影响评价的。

**Article 46** Where a construction entity violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the ocean administrative department that originally approved the environmental impact statement for the project shall order the cessation of construction or operation, require the completion of the relevant procedures within a time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan:  
(1) where major changes occur in the nature, scale, location, production process, or proposed environmental protection measures of the marine engineering project, and a new environmental impact statement is not prepared and submitted to the ocean administrative department that originally approved the environmental impact statement for approval;  
(2) where the marine engineering project commences construction more than five years after the date of approval of its environmental impact statement, and the environmental impact statement is not re-submitted to the ocean administrative department that originally approved it for approval;  
(3) where the marine engineering project needs to be dismantled or converted to other uses, and it is not reported to the ocean administrative department that originally approved the environmental impact statement for record or an environmental impact assessment is not conducted as required.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　建设单位违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由原核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令停止运行，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款：  
(一)擅自拆除或者闲置环境保护设施的；  
(二)未在规定时间内进行环境影响后评价或者未按要求采取整改措施的。

**Article 47** Where a construction entity violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the ocean administrative department that originally approved the environmental impact statement for the project shall order rectification within a time limit; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, it shall order the cessation of operation and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan:  
(1) removing or idling environmental protection facilities without authorization;  
(2) failing to conduct a post-environmental impact assessment within the prescribed time limit or failing to take corrective measures as required.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　建设单位违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门责令停止建设、运行，限期恢复原状；逾期未恢复原状的，海洋主管部门可以指定具有相应资质的单位代为恢复原状，所需费用由建设单位承担，并处恢复原状所需费用1倍以上2倍以下的罚款：  
(一)造成领海基点及其周围环境被侵蚀、淤积或者损害的；  
(二)违反规定在海洋自然保护区内进行海洋工程建设活动的。

**Article 48** Where a construction entity violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the ocean administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of construction or operation and require restoration to the original state within a time limit; if the restoration is not completed within the time limit, the ocean administrative department may designate a unit with corresponding qualifications to carry out the restoration on its behalf, with the costs borne by the construction entity, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the costs required for restoration:  
(1) causing erosion, siltation, or damage to the base points of the territorial sea and their surrounding environment;  
(2) carrying out marine engineering construction activities within marine nature reserves in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　建设单位违反本条例规定，在围填海工程中使用的填充材料不符合有关环境保护标准的，由县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令停止建设、运行，并处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；造成海洋环境污染事故，直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 49** Where a construction entity violates the provisions of these Regulations and uses filling materials that do not meet relevant environmental protection standards in a reclamation of land from the sea project, the ocean administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification within a time limit; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, it shall order the cessation of construction or operation and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if a marine environmental pollution accident is caused and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible personnel constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　建设单位违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由原核准该工程环境影响报告书的海洋主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款：  
(一)未按规定报告污染物排放设施、处理设备的运转情况或者污染物的排放、处置情况的；  
(二)未按规定报告其向水基泥浆中添加油的种类和数量的；  
(三)未按规定将防治海洋工程污染损害海洋环境的应急预案备案的；  
(四)在海上爆破作业前未按规定报告海洋主管部门的；  
(五)进行海上爆破作业时，未按规定设置明显标志、信号的。

**Article 50** Where a construction entity violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the ocean administrative department that originally approved the environmental impact statement for the project shall order rectification within a time limit; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, it shall impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan:  
(1) failing to report the operation of pollutant discharge facilities and treatment equipment or the discharge and disposal of pollutants as required;  
(2) failing to report the types and quantities of oil added to water-based mud as required;  
(3) failing to file the emergency plan for the prevention and control of pollution damage to the marine environment from the marine engineering project for record as required;  
(4) failing to report to the ocean administrative department before offshore blasting operations as required;  
(5) failing to set up clear signs and signals during offshore blasting operations as required.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　建设单位违反本条例规定，进行海上爆破作业时未采取有效措施保护海洋资源的，由县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款。  
建设单位违反本条例规定，在重要渔业水域进行炸药爆破或者进行其他可能对渔业资源造成损害的作业，未避开主要经济类鱼虾产卵期的，由县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门予以警告、责令停止作业，并处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 51** Where a construction entity violates these Regulations by failing to take effective measures to protect marine resources when conducting offshore blasting operations, the marine administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where a construction entity violates these Regulations by conducting explosive blasting or other operations that may cause damage to fishery resources in important fishing waters without avoiding the spawning period of major economic fish and shrimp species, the marine administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall give it a warning, order it to cease operations, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　海洋油气矿产资源勘探开发单位违反本条例规定向海洋排放含油污水，或者将塑料制品、残油、废油、油基泥浆、含油垃圾和其他有毒有害残液残渣直接排放或者弃置入海的，由国家海洋主管部门或者其派出机构责令限期清理，并处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；逾期未清理的，国家海洋主管部门或者其派出机构可以指定有相应资质的单位代为清理，所需费用由海洋油气矿产资源勘探开发单位承担；造成海洋环境污染事故，直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 52** Where a marine oil and gas mineral resource exploration and development unit violates these Regulations by discharging oily wastewater into the sea, or directly discharging or discarding into the sea plastic products, residual oil, waste oil, oil-based mud, oily garbage, or other toxic and hazardous residual liquids and solids, the State marine administrative department or its dispatched agency shall order it to clean up within a prescribed time limit and concurrently impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan; if it fails to clean up within the time limit, the State marine administrative department or its dispatched agency may designate a unit with corresponding qualifications to clean up on its behalf, and the costs shall be borne by the marine oil and gas mineral resource exploration and development unit; if a marine environment pollution accident is caused, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　海水养殖者未按规定采取科学的养殖方式，对海洋环境造成污染或者严重影响海洋景观的，由县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令停止养殖活动，并处清理污染或者恢复海洋景观所需费用1倍以上2倍以下的罚款。

**Article 53** Where a mariculture operator fails to adopt scientific cultivation methods as required, causing pollution to the marine environment or seriously affecting the marine landscape, the marine administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to cease mariculture activities and concurrently be fined not less than one time but not more than two times the costs required for cleaning up the pollution or restoring the marine landscape.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　建设单位未按本条例规定缴纳排污费的，由县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门责令限期缴纳；逾期拒不缴纳的，处应缴纳排污费数额2倍以上3倍以下的罚款。

**Article 54** Where a construction entity fails to pay the pollutant discharge fee pursuant to these Regulations, the marine administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to pay within a prescribed time limit; if it refuses to pay within the time limit, a fine of not less than two times but not more than three times the amount of the pollutant discharge fee payable shall be imposed.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本条例规定，造成海洋环境污染损害的，责任者应当排除危害，赔偿损失。完全由于第三者的故意或者过失造成海洋环境污染损害的，由第三者排除危害，承担赔偿责任。  
违反本条例规定，造成海洋环境污染事故，直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 55** Where a violation of these Regulations causes damage to the marine environment, the responsible party shall eliminate the hazard and compensate for the losses. If the damage to the marine environment is caused entirely by the intentional act or negligence of a third party, the third party shall eliminate the hazard and bear compensation liability.  
Where a violation of these Regulations causes a marine environment pollution accident, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　海洋主管部门的工作人员违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未按规定核准海洋工程环境影响报告书的；  
(二)未按规定验收环境保护设施的；  
(三)未按规定对海洋环境污染事故进行报告和调查处理的；  
(四)未按规定征收排污费的；  
(五)未按规定进行监督检查的。

**Article 56** Where any staff member of the marine administrative department violates these Regulations and falls under any of the following circumstances, he or she shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to review and approve the marine engineering environmental impact report as required;  
(2) failing to accept environmental protection facilities as required;  
(3) failing to report and investigate marine environment pollution accidents as required;  
(4) failing to levy pollutant discharge fees as required;  
(5) failing to conduct supervision and inspection as required.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　船舶污染的防治按照国家有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 57** The prevention and control of pollution from ships shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the relevant national laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　本条例自2006年11月1日起施行。

**Article 58** These Regulations shall come into force as of November 1, 2006.
