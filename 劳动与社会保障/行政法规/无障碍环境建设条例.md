# 无障碍环境建设条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2012-06-28  
**生效日期**：2012-08-01

> (2012年6月13日国务院第208次常务会议通过　2012年6月28日中华人民共和国国务院令第622号公布　自2012年8月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了创造无障碍环境，保障残疾人等社会成员平等参与社会生活，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称无障碍环境建设，是指为便于残疾人等社会成员自主安全地通行道路、出入相关建筑物、搭乘公共交通工具、交流信息、获得社区服务所进行的建设活动。 第三条　无障碍环境建设应当与经济和社会发展水平相适应，遵循实用、易行、广泛受益的原则。 第四条　县级以上人民政府负责组织编制无障碍环境建设发展规划并组织实施。 编制无障碍环境建设发展规划，应当征求残疾人组织等社会组织的意见。 无障碍环境建设发展规划应当纳入国民经济和社会发展规划以及城乡规划。 第五条　国务院住房和城乡建设主管部门负责全国无障碍设施工程建设活动的监督管理工作，会同国务院有关部门制定无障碍设施工程建设标准，并对无障碍设施工程建设的情况进行监督检查。 国务院工业和信息化主管部门等有关部门在各自职责范围内，做好无障碍环境建设工作。 第六条　国家鼓励、支持采用无障碍通用设计的技术和产品，推进残疾人专用的无障碍技术和产品的开发、应用和推广。 第七条　国家倡导无障碍环境建设理念，鼓励公民、法人和其他组织为无障碍环境建设提供捐助和志愿服务。 第八条　对在无障碍环境建设工作中作出显著成绩的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。 第二章　无障碍设施建设 第九条　城镇新建、改建、扩建道路、公共建筑、公共交通设施、居住建筑、居住区，应当符合无障碍设施工程建设标准。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了创造无障碍环境，保障残疾人等社会成员平等参与社会生活，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to create accessible environments and ensure that disabled persons and other members of society equally participate in social life.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称无障碍环境建设，是指为便于残疾人等社会成员自主安全地通行道路、出入相关建筑物、搭乘公共交通工具、交流信息、获得社区服务所进行的建设活动。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "construction of accessible environments" means construction activities carried out to facilitate the autonomous and safe movement of disabled persons and other members of society on roads, entering and exiting relevant buildings, taking public transportation, exchanging information, and obtaining community services.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　无障碍环境建设应当与经济和社会发展水平相适应，遵循实用、易行、广泛受益的原则。

**Article 3** The construction of accessible environments shall be compatible with the level of economic and social development, and shall follow the principles of practicality, ease of implementation, and wide benefit.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府负责组织编制无障碍环境建设发展规划并组织实施。  
编制无障碍环境建设发展规划，应当征求残疾人组织等社会组织的意见。  
无障碍环境建设发展规划应当纳入国民经济和社会发展规划以及城乡规划。

**Article 4** People's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing the formulation of development plans for the construction of accessible environments and for organizing their implementation.  
When formulating a development plan for the construction of accessible environments, opinions from disabled persons' organizations and other social organizations shall be solicited.  
The development plan for the construction of accessible environments shall be incorporated into the national economic and social development plan as well as into urban and rural plans.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院住房和城乡建设主管部门负责全国无障碍设施工程建设活动的监督管理工作，会同国务院有关部门制定无障碍设施工程建设标准，并对无障碍设施工程建设的情况进行监督检查。  
国务院工业和信息化主管部门等有关部门在各自职责范围内，做好无障碍环境建设工作。

**Article 5** The competent department of housing and urban-rural development under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and management of the construction activities of accessible facilities nationwide, shall formulate the construction standards for accessible facilities jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council, and shall conduct supervision and inspection on the construction of accessible facilities.  
The competent department of industry and information technology under the State Council and other relevant departments shall, within their respective functions and duties, properly carry out the work on the construction of accessible environments.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励、支持采用无障碍通用设计的技术和产品，推进残疾人专用的无障碍技术和产品的开发、应用和推广。

**Article 6** The State encourages and supports the adoption of technologies and products of universal design for accessibility, and promotes the development, application and popularization of accessible technologies and products specially for disabled persons.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家倡导无障碍环境建设理念，鼓励公民、法人和其他组织为无障碍环境建设提供捐助和志愿服务。

**Article 7** The State advocates the concept of constructing accessible environments, and encourages citizens, legal persons and other organizations to provide donations and volunteer services for the construction of accessible environments.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对在无障碍环境建设工作中作出显著成绩的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 8** Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in the work of constructing accessible environments shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　无障碍设施建设

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　城镇新建、改建、扩建道路、公共建筑、公共交通设施、居住建筑、居住区，应当符合无障碍设施工程建设标准。  
乡、村庄的建设和发展，应当逐步达到无障碍设施工程建设标准。

**Article 9** New construction, reconstruction, and expansion of roads, public buildings, public transportation facilities, residential buildings, and residential areas in cities and towns shall comply with the construction standards for accessible facilities.  
The construction and development of townships and villages shall gradually meet the construction standards for accessible facilities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　无障碍设施工程应当与主体工程同步设计、同步施工、同步验收投入使用。新建的无障碍设施应当与周边的无障碍设施相衔接。

**Article 10** The construction of accessible facilities shall be designed, constructed, and accepted for use simultaneously with the main project. Newly built accessible facilities shall be connected with the surrounding accessible facilities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对城镇已建成的不符合无障碍设施工程建设标准的道路、公共建筑、公共交通设施、居住建筑、居住区，县级以上人民政府应当制定无障碍设施改造计划并组织实施。  
无障碍设施改造由所有权人或者管理人负责。

**Article 11** For existing urban roads, public buildings, public transport facilities, residential buildings, and residential areas that do not meet the barrier-free facilities construction standards, the people's governments at or above the county level shall formulate a renovation plan for barrier-free facilities and organize its implementation.  
The renovation of barrier-free facilities shall be the responsibility of the owner or manager.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上人民政府应当优先推进下列机构、场所的无障碍设施改造：  
(一)特殊教育、康复、社会福利等机构；  
(二)国家机关的公共服务场所；  
(三)文化、体育、医疗卫生等单位的公共服务场所；  
(四)交通运输、金融、邮政、商业、旅游等公共服务场所。

**Article 12** The people's governments at or above the county level shall give priority to promoting the renovation of barrier-free facilities in the following institutions and places:  
(1) Special education, rehabilitation, social welfare and other institutions;  
(2) Public service venues of state organs;  
(3) Public service venues of cultural, sports, medical and health units;  
(4) Public service venues for transportation, finance, postal services, commerce, tourism, etc.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　城市的主要道路、主要商业区和大型居住区的人行天桥和人行地下通道，应当按照无障碍设施工程建设标准配备无障碍设施，人行道交通信号设施应当逐步完善无障碍服务功能，适应残疾人等社会成员通行的需要。

**Article 13** The pedestrian overpasses and pedestrian underpasses on main urban roads, main commercial areas, and large residential areas shall be equipped with barrier-free facilities in accordance with the barrier-free facilities construction standards, and the traffic signal facilities on pedestrian sidewalks shall gradually improve the barrier-free service functions to meet the needs of persons with disabilities and other members of society for passage.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　城市的大中型公共场所的公共停车场和大型居住区的停车场，应当按照无障碍设施工程建设标准设置并标明无障碍停车位。  
无障碍停车位为肢体残疾人驾驶或者乘坐的机动车专用。

**Article 14** The public parking lots of large and medium-sized public places in cities and the parking lots of large residential areas shall be equipped with and clearly marked barrier-free parking spaces in accordance with the barrier-free facilities construction standards.  
Barrier-free parking spaces shall be reserved exclusively for motor vehicles driven or used by persons with physical disabilities.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　民用航空器、客运列车、客运船舶、公共汽车、城市轨道交通车辆等公共交通工具应当逐步达到无障碍设施的要求。有关主管部门应当制定公共交通工具的无障碍技术标准并确定达标期限。

**Article 15** Public transport vehicles such as civil aircraft, passenger trains, passenger ships, buses, and urban rail transit vehicles shall gradually meet the requirements of barrier-free facilities. The relevant competent departments shall formulate barrier-free technical standards for public transport vehicles and determine the deadlines for compliance.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　视力残疾人携带导盲犬出入公共场所，应当遵守国家有关规定，公共场所的工作人员应当按照国家有关规定提供无障碍服务。

**Article 16** When a person with visual impairment enters or leaves a public place with a guide dog, they shall comply with the relevant state regulations, and the staff of the public place shall provide barrier-free services in accordance with the relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　无障碍设施的所有权人和管理人，应当对无障碍设施进行保护，有损毁或者故障及时进行维修，确保无障碍设施正常使用。

**Article 17** The owners and managers of barrier-free facilities shall protect the barrier-free facilities, and promptly repair them if they are damaged or malfunctioning, to ensure the normal use of the barrier-free facilities.

### 第三章　无障碍信息交流

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上人民政府应当将无障碍信息交流建设纳入信息化建设规划，并采取措施推进信息交流无障碍建设。

**Article 18** The people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the construction of barrier-free information exchange into the information construction plan, and take measures to promote the construction of barrier-free information exchange.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门发布重要政府信息和与残疾人相关的信息，应当创造条件为残疾人提供语音和文字提示等信息交流服务。

**Article 19** When the people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments release important government information and information related to persons with disabilities, they shall create conditions to provide information exchange services such as voice and text prompts for persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家举办的升学考试、职业资格考试和任职考试，有视力残疾人参加的，应当为视力残疾人提供盲文试卷、电子试卷，或者由工作人员予以协助。

**Article 20** In entrance examinations, professional qualification examinations, and appointment examinations organized by the state, where persons with visual impairment participate, braille test papers or electronic test papers shall be provided for persons with visual impairment, or assistance shall be provided by the staff.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　设区的市级以上人民政府设立的电视台应当创造条件，在播出电视节目时配备字幕，每周播放至少一次配播手语的新闻节目。  
公开出版发行的影视类录像制品应当配备字幕。

**Article 21** The television stations established by the people's governments at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall create conditions to provide subtitles when broadcasting television programs, and shall broadcast news programs with sign language at least once a week.  
Publicly released film and video recordings shall be provided with subtitles.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　设区的市级以上人民政府设立的公共图书馆应当开设视力残疾人阅览室，提供盲文读物、有声读物，其他图书馆应当逐步开设视力残疾人阅览室。

**Article 22** Public libraries established by the people's governments at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall set up reading rooms for persons with visual disabilities, providing braille books and audio books. Other libraries shall gradually set up reading rooms for persons with visual disabilities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　残疾人组织的网站应当达到无障碍网站设计标准，设区的市级以上人民政府网站、政府公益活动网站，应当逐步达到无障碍网站设计标准。

**Article 23** The websites of organizations for persons with disabilities shall meet the accessibility website design standards. The websites of the people's governments at or above the level of a city divided into districts and government public welfare activity websites shall gradually meet the accessibility website design standards.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　公共服务机构和公共场所应当创造条件为残疾人提供语音和文字提示、手语、盲文等信息交流服务，并对工作人员进行无障碍服务技能培训。

**Article 24** Public service institutions and public places shall create conditions to provide information exchange services such as voice and text prompts, sign language, and braille for persons with disabilities, and shall conduct accessibility service skills training for staff members.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　举办听力残疾人集中参加的公共活动，举办单位应当提供字幕或者手语服务。

**Article 25** When organizing public activities that involve concentrated participation of persons with hearing disabilities, the organizing unit shall provide subtitles or sign language services.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　电信业务经营者提供电信服务，应当创造条件为有需求的听力、言语残疾人提供文字信息服务，为有需求的视力残疾人提供语音信息服务。  
电信终端设备制造者应当提供能够与无障碍信息交流服务相衔接的技术、产品。

**Article 26** Telecommunications business operators providing telecommunications services shall create conditions to provide text information services to persons with hearing or speech disabilities who have such needs, and provide voice information services to persons with visual disabilities who have such needs.  
Manufacturers of telecommunications terminal equipment shall provide technologies and products that can be integrated with accessible information exchange services.

### 第四章　无障碍社区服务

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　社区公共服务设施应当逐步完善无障碍服务功能，为残疾人等社会成员参与社区生活提供便利。

**Article 27** Community public service facilities shall gradually improve their accessibility service functions to provide convenience for persons with disabilities and other members of society to participate in community life.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　地方各级人民政府应当逐步完善报警、医疗急救等紧急呼叫系统，方便残疾人等社会成员报警、呼救。

**Article 28** Local people's governments at all levels shall gradually improve emergency call systems such as alarm and medical first aid to facilitate persons with disabilities and other members of society to report incidents and call for help.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　对需要进行无障碍设施改造的贫困家庭，县级以上地方人民政府可以给予适当补助。

**Article 29** For impoverished families that need to carry out accessibility facility renovations, the local people's governments at or above the county level may grant appropriate subsidies.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　组织选举的部门应当为残疾人参加选举提供便利，为视力残疾人提供盲文选票。

**Article 30** The departments organizing elections shall provide convenience for persons with disabilities to participate in elections and provide braille ballots for persons with visual disabilities.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　城镇新建、改建、扩建道路、公共建筑、公共交通设施、居住建筑、居住区，不符合无障碍设施工程建设标准的，由住房和城乡建设主管部门责令改正，依法给予处罚。

**Article 31** . For urban new construction, reconstruction, or expansion of roads, public buildings, public transportation facilities, residential buildings, and residential areas that do not comply with the engineering construction standards for accessibility facilities, the competent department of housing and urban-rural development shall order corrections and impose penalties according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　肢体残疾人驾驶或者乘坐的机动车以外的机动车占用无障碍停车位，影响肢体残疾人使用的，由公安机关交通管理部门责令改正，依法给予处罚。

**Article 32** . Where motor vehicles other than those driven or ridden by persons with physical disabilities occupy accessible parking spaces, thereby affecting the use by persons with physical disabilities, the traffic management department of the public security organ shall order corrections and impose penalties according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　无障碍设施的所有权人或者管理人对无障碍设施未进行保护或者及时维修，导致无法正常使用的，由有关主管部门责令限期维修；造成使用人人身、财产损害的，无障碍设施的所有权人或者管理人应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 33** . Where the owner or manager of accessibility facilities fails to protect or timely repair them, resulting in the inability to use them normally, the relevant competent department shall order repairs within a specified time limit; if personal injury or property damage is caused to users, the owner or manager of the accessibility facilities shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　无障碍环境建设主管部门工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 34** . Where staff members of the competent department for accessibility environment construction abuse their powers, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractice for personal gain, they shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本条例自2012年8月1日起施行。

**Article 35** . These Regulations shall come into force as of August 1, 2012.
