# 畜禽规模养殖污染防治条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2013-11-11  
**生效日期**：2014-01-01

> (2013年10月8日国务院第26次常务会议通过　2013年11月11日中华人民共和国国务院令第643号公布　自2014年1月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了防治畜禽养殖污染，推进畜禽养殖废弃物的综合利用和无害化处理，保护和改善环境，保障公众身体健康，促进畜牧业持续健康发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于畜禽养殖场、养殖小区的养殖污染防治。 畜禽养殖场、养殖小区的规模标准根据畜牧业发展状况和畜禽养殖污染防治要求确定。 牧区放牧养殖污染防治，不适用本条例。 第三条　畜禽养殖污染防治，应当统筹考虑保护环境与促进畜牧业发展的需要，坚持预防为主、防治结合的原则，实行统筹规划、合理布局、综合利用、激励引导。 第四条　各级人民政府应当加强对畜禽养殖污染防治工作的组织领导，采取有效措施，加大资金投入，扶持畜禽养殖污染防治以及畜禽养殖废弃物综合利用。 第五条　县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门负责畜禽养殖污染防治的统一监督管理。 县级以上人民政府农牧主管部门负责畜禽养殖废弃物综合利用的指导和服务。 县级以上人民政府循环经济发展综合管理部门负责畜禽养殖循环经济工作的组织协调。 县级以上人民政府其他有关部门依照本条例规定和各自职责，负责畜禽养殖污染防治相关工作。 乡镇人民政府应当协助有关部门做好本行政区域的畜禽养殖污染防治工作。 第六条　从事畜禽养殖以及畜禽养殖废弃物综合利用和无害化处理活动，应当符合国家有关畜禽养殖污染防治的要求，并依法接受有关主管部门的监督检查。 第七条　国家鼓励和支持畜禽养殖污染防治以及畜禽养殖废弃物综合利用和无害化处理的科学技术研究和装备研发。各级人民政府应当支持先进适用技术的推广，促进畜禽养殖污染防治水平的提高。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了防治畜禽养殖污染，推进畜禽养殖废弃物的综合利用和无害化处理，保护和改善环境，保障公众身体健康，促进畜牧业持续健康发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** For the purposes of preventing and controlling pollution from livestock and poultry breeding, promoting the comprehensive utilization and harmless treatment of livestock and poultry breeding waste, protecting and improving the environment, safeguarding public health, and promoting the sustainable and healthy development of the livestock industry, these Regulations are formulated.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于畜禽养殖场、养殖小区的养殖污染防治。  
畜禽养殖场、养殖小区的规模标准根据畜牧业发展状况和畜禽养殖污染防治要求确定。  
牧区放牧养殖污染防治，不适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the prevention and control of pollution from livestock and poultry farms and breeding zones.  
The scale standards for livestock and poultry farms and breeding zones shall be determined based on the development status of the livestock industry and the requirements for pollution prevention and control from livestock and poultry breeding.  
The prevention and control of pollution from grazing livestock in pastoral areas shall not be subject to these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　畜禽养殖污染防治，应当统筹考虑保护环境与促进畜牧业发展的需要，坚持预防为主、防治结合的原则，实行统筹规划、合理布局、综合利用、激励引导。

**Article 3** The prevention and control of pollution from livestock and poultry breeding shall take into comprehensive consideration the need to protect the environment and promote the development of the livestock industry, adhere to the principles of prevention first and combining prevention with control, and implement comprehensive planning, rational layout, comprehensive utilization, and incentive guidance.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级人民政府应当加强对畜禽养殖污染防治工作的组织领导，采取有效措施，加大资金投入，扶持畜禽养殖污染防治以及畜禽养殖废弃物综合利用。

**Article 4** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the organizational leadership for the prevention and control of pollution from livestock and poultry breeding, take effective measures, increase capital investment, and support the prevention and control of pollution from livestock and poultry breeding as well as the comprehensive utilization of livestock and poultry breeding waste.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门负责畜禽养殖污染防治的统一监督管理。  
县级以上人民政府农牧主管部门负责畜禽养殖废弃物综合利用的指导和服务。  
县级以上人民政府循环经济发展综合管理部门负责畜禽养殖循环经济工作的组织协调。  
县级以上人民政府其他有关部门依照本条例规定和各自职责，负责畜禽养殖污染防治相关工作。  
乡镇人民政府应当协助有关部门做好本行政区域的畜禽养殖污染防治工作。

**Article 5** The environmental protection department of the people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the unified supervision and management of the prevention and control of pollution from livestock and poultry breeding.  
The agriculture and animal husbandry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the guidance and services for the comprehensive utilization of livestock and poultry breeding waste.  
The comprehensive department for circular economy development of the people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the organization and coordination of circular economy work in livestock and poultry breeding.  
Other relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and their respective duties, be responsible for the work related to the prevention and control of pollution from livestock and poultry breeding.  
Township people's governments shall assist the relevant departments in carrying out the work on the prevention and control of pollution from livestock and poultry breeding within their administrative areas.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　从事畜禽养殖以及畜禽养殖废弃物综合利用和无害化处理活动，应当符合国家有关畜禽养殖污染防治的要求，并依法接受有关主管部门的监督检查。

**Article 6** Entities engaged in livestock and poultry breeding and activities of comprehensive utilization and harmless treatment of livestock and poultry breeding waste shall comply with the relevant national requirements for the prevention and control of pollution from livestock and poultry breeding and shall accept supervision and inspection by the relevant competent departments according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励和支持畜禽养殖污染防治以及畜禽养殖废弃物综合利用和无害化处理的科学技术研究和装备研发。各级人民政府应当支持先进适用技术的推广，促进畜禽养殖污染防治水平的提高。

**Article 7** The State shall encourage and support scientific and technological research and equipment development for the prevention and control of pollution from livestock and poultry breeding and for the comprehensive utilization and harmless treatment of livestock and poultry breeding waste. People's governments at all levels shall support the promotion of advanced and applicable technologies to improve the level of pollution prevention and control from livestock and poultry breeding.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　任何单位和个人对违反本条例规定的行为，有权向县级以上人民政府环境保护等有关部门举报。接到举报的部门应当及时调查处理。  
对在畜禽养殖污染防治中作出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 8** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report any act that violates the provisions of these Regulations to the environmental protection department of the people's government at or above the county level or other relevant departments. The department that receives the report shall promptly investigate and handle it.  
Entities and individuals that make outstanding contributions to the prevention and control of pollution from livestock and poultry breeding shall, in accordance with relevant State regulations, be commended and rewarded.

### 第二章　预防

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上人民政府农牧主管部门编制畜牧业发展规划，报本级人民政府或者其授权的部门批准实施。畜牧业发展规划应当统筹考虑环境承载能力以及畜禽养殖污染防治要求，合理布局，科学确定畜禽养殖的品种、规模、总量。

**Article 9** The agriculture and animal husbandry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall formulate a livestock industry development plan and submit it to the people's government at the same level or its authorized department for approval and implementation. The livestock industry development plan shall take into comprehensive consideration the environmental carrying capacity and the requirements for the prevention and control of pollution from livestock and poultry breeding, rationally arrange the layout, and scientifically determine the breeds, scales, and total quantities of livestock and poultry breeding.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门会同农牧主管部门编制畜禽养殖污染防治规划，报本级人民政府或者其授权的部门批准实施。畜禽养殖污染防治规划应当与畜牧业发展规划相衔接，统筹考虑畜禽养殖生产布局，明确畜禽养殖污染防治目标、任务、重点区域，明确污染治理重点设施建设，以及废弃物综合利用等污染防治措施。

**Article 10** The environmental protection department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, jointly with the agriculture and animal husbandry department, formulate a plan for the prevention and control of pollution from livestock and poultry breeding and submit it to the people's government at the same level or its authorized department for approval and implementation. The plan for the prevention and control of pollution from livestock and poultry breeding shall be aligned with the livestock industry development plan, take into comprehensive consideration the production layout of livestock and poultry breeding, define the objectives, tasks, and key areas for the prevention and control of pollution from livestock and poultry breeding, and specify the construction of key pollution treatment facilities and pollution prevention measures such as comprehensive utilization of waste.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　禁止在下列区域内建设畜禽养殖场、养殖小区：  
(一)饮用水水源保护区，风景名胜区；  
(二)自然保护区的核心区和缓冲区；  
(三)城镇居民区、文化教育科学研究区等人口集中区域；  
(四)法律、法规规定的其他禁止养殖区域。

**Article 11** Construction of livestock and poultry farms or farming communities in the following areas is prohibited:  
(1) Drinking water source protection areas and scenic spots;  
(2) Core zones and buffer zones of nature reserves;  
(3) Urban residential areas, cultural, educational and scientific research areas, and other densely populated areas;  
(4) Other areas where farming is prohibited as provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　新建、改建、扩建畜禽养殖场、养殖小区，应当符合畜牧业发展规划、畜禽养殖污染防治规划，满足动物防疫条件，并进行环境影响评价。对环境可能造成重大影响的大型畜禽养殖场、养殖小区，应当编制环境影响报告书；其他畜禽养殖场、养殖小区应当填报环境影响登记表。大型畜禽养殖场、养殖小区的管理目录，由国务院环境保护主管部门商国务院农牧主管部门确定。  
环境影响评价的重点应当包括：畜禽养殖产生的废弃物种类和数量，废弃物综合利用和无害化处理方案和措施，废弃物的消纳和处理情况以及向环境直接排放的情况，最终可能对水体、土壤等环境和人体健康产生的影响以及控制和减少影响的方案和措施等。

**Article 12** The construction, renovation or expansion of livestock and poultry farms or farming communities shall comply with the animal husbandry development plan and the livestock and poultry breeding pollution prevention plan, satisfy the animal epidemic prevention conditions, and undergo an environmental impact assessment. Large-scale livestock and poultry farms or farming communities that may cause significant impacts on the environment shall prepare an environmental impact report; other livestock and poultry farms or farming communities shall fill in an environmental impact registration form. The management catalogue of large-scale livestock and poultry farms or farming communities shall be determined by the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council in consultation with the competent department of agriculture and animal husbandry under the State Council.  
The focus of the environmental impact assessment shall include: the types and quantities of waste generated by livestock and poultry breeding, the plans and measures for comprehensive utilization and harm-free treatment of waste, the situation of waste absorption and treatment as well as direct discharge into the environment, the potential impacts on the environment such as water bodies and soil and on human health, and the plans and measures to control and reduce such impacts.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　畜禽养殖场、养殖小区应当根据养殖规模和污染防治需要，建设相应的畜禽粪便、污水与雨水分流设施，畜禽粪便、污水的贮存设施，粪污厌氧消化和堆沤、有机肥加工、制取沼气、沼渣沼液分离和输送、污水处理、畜禽尸体处理等综合利用和无害化处理设施。已经委托他人对畜禽养殖废弃物代为综合利用和无害化处理的，可以不自行建设综合利用和无害化处理设施。  
未建设污染防治配套设施、自行建设的配套设施不合格，或者未委托他人对畜禽养殖废弃物进行综合利用和无害化处理的，畜禽养殖场、养殖小区不得投入生产或者使用。  
畜禽养殖场、养殖小区自行建设污染防治配套设施的，应当确保其正常运行。

**Article 13** Livestock and poultry farms or farming communities shall, based on the scale of farming and the needs of pollution prevention, construct corresponding facilities for the separation of livestock and poultry manure, sewage and rainwater, storage facilities for livestock and poultry manure and sewage, and facilities for comprehensive utilization and harm-free treatment such as anaerobic digestion and composting of manure, organic fertilizer processing, biogas production, separation and transportation of biogas residue and slurry, sewage treatment, and disposal of livestock and poultry carcasses. Where a livestock and poultry farm or farming community has commissioned another party to conduct comprehensive utilization and harm-free treatment of livestock and poultry breeding waste, it may not construct the comprehensive utilization and harm-free treatment facilities on its own.  
Where pollution prevention supporting facilities are not constructed, the self-constructed supporting facilities are not up to standard, or no other party has been commissioned to conduct comprehensive utilization and harm-free treatment of livestock and poultry breeding waste, the livestock and poultry farm or farming community shall not commence production or use.  
Where a livestock and poultry farm or farming community constructs pollution prevention supporting facilities on its own, it shall ensure their normal operation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　从事畜禽养殖活动，应当采取科学的饲养方式和废弃物处理工艺等有效措施，减少畜禽养殖废弃物的产生量和向环境的排放量。

**Article 14** Any person engaged in livestock and poultry breeding activities shall adopt effective measures such as scientific feeding methods and waste treatment processes to reduce the amount of livestock and poultry breeding waste generated and discharged into the environment.

### 第三章　综合利用与治理

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家鼓励和支持采取粪肥还田、制取沼气、制造有机肥等方法，对畜禽养殖废弃物进行综合利用。

**Article 15** The State encourages and supports the comprehensive utilization of livestock and poultry breeding waste through methods such as returning manure to the fields, producing biogas, and manufacturing organic fertilizers.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家鼓励和支持采取种植和养殖相结合的方式消纳利用畜禽养殖废弃物，促进畜禽粪便、污水等废弃物就地就近利用。

**Article 16** The State encourages and supports the combination of planting and breeding to absorb and utilize livestock and poultry breeding waste, and promotes the local and nearby utilization of waste such as livestock and poultry manure and sewage.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家鼓励和支持沼气制取、有机肥生产等废弃物综合利用以及沼渣沼液输送和施用、沼气发电等相关配套设施建设。

**Article 17** The State encourages and supports the construction of supporting facilities for comprehensive utilization of waste such as biogas production and organic fertilizer manufacturing, as well as related facilities for transportation and application of biogas residue and slurry, and biogas power generation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　将畜禽粪便、污水、沼渣、沼液等用作肥料的，应当与土地的消纳能力相适应，并采取有效措施，消除可能引起传染病的微生物，防止污染环境和传播疫病。

**Article 18** Any person who uses livestock and poultry manure, sewage, biogas residue, biogas slurry, etc. as fertilizers shall ensure that such use is compatible with the absorption capacity of the land, and shall adopt effective measures to eliminate microorganisms that may cause infectious diseases, so as to prevent environmental pollution and the spread of epidemics.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　从事畜禽养殖活动和畜禽养殖废弃物处理活动，应当及时对畜禽粪便、畜禽尸体、污水等进行收集、贮存、清运，防止恶臭和畜禽养殖废弃物渗出、泄漏。

**Article 19** Any person engaged in livestock and poultry breeding activities and activities for the treatment of livestock and poultry breeding waste shall promptly collect, store, and transport livestock and poultry manure, carcasses, sewage, etc., to prevent malodorous gases and the seepage or leakage of livestock and poultry breeding waste.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　向环境排放经过处理的畜禽养殖废弃物，应当符合国家和地方规定的污染物排放标准和总量控制指标。畜禽养殖废弃物未经处理，不得直接向环境排放。

**Article 20** The discharge of treated livestock and poultry breeding waste into the environment shall comply with the national and local pollutant discharge standards and total discharge control indicators. Livestock and poultry breeding waste that has not been treated shall not be directly discharged into the environment.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　染疫畜禽以及染疫畜禽排泄物、染疫畜禽产品、病死或者死因不明的畜禽尸体等病害畜禽养殖废弃物，应当按照有关法律、法规和国务院农牧主管部门的规定，进行深埋、化制、焚烧等无害化处理，不得随意处置。

**Article 21** Diseased livestock and poultry, as well as diseased excreta, diseased products, carcasses of livestock and poultry that died of diseases or unknown causes, and other diseased livestock and poultry breeding wastes, shall undergo harmless treatment such as deep burial, rendering, or incineration in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws, regulations, and the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs under the State Council, and shall not be disposed of randomly.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　畜禽养殖场、养殖小区应当定期将畜禽养殖品种、规模以及畜禽养殖废弃物的产生、排放和综合利用等情况，报县级人民政府环境保护主管部门备案。环境保护主管部门应当定期将备案情况抄送同级农牧主管部门。

**Article 22** Livestock and poultry breeding farms and breeding communities shall periodically report to the environmental protection administrative department of the people's government at the county level for record the species and scale of livestock and poultry breeding, as well as the generation, discharge, and comprehensive utilization of livestock and poultry breeding wastes. The environmental protection administrative department shall periodically send a copy of the record to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs at the same level.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门应当依据职责对畜禽养殖污染防治情况进行监督检查，并加强对畜禽养殖环境污染的监测。  
乡镇人民政府、基层群众自治组织发现畜禽养殖环境污染行为的，应当及时制止和报告。

**Article 23** The environmental protection administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, pursuant to its functions and duties, supervise and inspect the prevention and control of pollution from livestock and poultry breeding, and strengthen the monitoring of environmental pollution from livestock and poultry breeding.  
Where the people's government at the township level or a grassroots self-governing organization discovers any act of environmental pollution from livestock and poultry breeding, it shall promptly stop and report such act.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对污染严重的畜禽养殖密集区域，市、县人民政府应当制定综合整治方案，采取组织建设畜禽养殖废弃物综合利用和无害化处理设施、有计划搬迁或者关闭畜禽养殖场所等措施，对畜禽养殖污染进行治理。

**Article 24** For densely populated areas with serious pollution from livestock and poultry breeding, the people's government of a city or county shall formulate a comprehensive remediation plan and take measures such as organizing the construction of facilities for comprehensive utilization and harmless treatment of livestock and poultry breeding wastes, and planned relocation or closure of livestock and poultry breeding sites, to control the pollution from livestock and poultry breeding.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　因畜牧业发展规划、土地利用总体规划、城乡规划调整以及划定禁止养殖区域，或者因对污染严重的畜禽养殖密集区域进行综合整治，确需关闭或者搬迁现有畜禽养殖场所，致使畜禽养殖者遭受经济损失的，由县级以上地方人民政府依法予以补偿。

**Article 25** Where the closure or relocation of existing livestock and poultry breeding sites is truly necessary due to adjustments to the development plan for animal husbandry, the general land use plan, or urban and rural planning, or the designation of prohibited breeding areas, or due to comprehensive remediation of densely populated areas with serious pollution from livestock and poultry breeding, thereby causing economic losses to livestock and poultry breeders, the local people's government at or above the county level shall compensate them according to law.

### 第四章　激励措施

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上人民政府应当采取示范奖励等措施，扶持规模化、标准化畜禽养殖，支持畜禽养殖场、养殖小区进行标准化改造和污染防治设施建设与改造，鼓励分散饲养向集约饲养方式转变。

**Article 26** The people's government at or above the county level shall adopt measures such as demonstration rewards to support scaled and standardized livestock and poultry breeding, assist livestock and poultry breeding farms and breeding communities in carrying out standardized transformation and the construction and renovation of pollution prevention facilities, and encourage the transition from scattered raising to intensive raising methods.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上地方人民政府在组织编制土地利用总体规划过程中，应当统筹安排，将规模化畜禽养殖用地纳入规划，落实养殖用地。  
国家鼓励利用废弃地和荒山、荒沟、荒丘、荒滩等未利用地开展规模化、标准化畜禽养殖。  
畜禽养殖用地按农用地管理，并按照国家有关规定确定生产设施用地和必要的污染防治等附属设施用地。

**Article 27** In the process of organizing the compilation of the general land use plan, the local people's government at or above the county level shall make overall arrangements to incorporate land for scaled livestock and poultry breeding into the plan and implement the land for breeding.  
The State encourages the use of abandoned land and unused land such as barren hills, barren gullies, barren hills, and barren beaches to carry out scaled and standardized livestock and poultry breeding.  
Land for livestock and poultry breeding shall be managed as agricultural land, and the land for production facilities and necessary ancillary facilities such as pollution prevention shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　建设和改造畜禽养殖污染防治设施，可以按照国家规定申请包括污染治理贷款贴息补助在内的环境保护等相关资金支持。

**Article 28** For the construction and renovation of pollution prevention facilities for livestock and poultry breeding, financial support from environmental protection and other related funds, including subsidy of loan interest for pollution control, may be applied for in accordance with the provisions of the State.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　进行畜禽养殖污染防治，从事利用畜禽养殖废弃物进行有机肥产品生产经营等畜禽养殖废弃物综合利用活动的，享受国家规定的相关税收优惠政策。

**Article 29** Those who carry out pollution prevention and control of livestock and poultry breeding and engage in comprehensive utilization activities of livestock and poultry breeding wastes, such as using livestock and poultry breeding wastes to produce and operate organic fertilizer products, shall enjoy the relevant tax preferential policies stipulated by the State.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　利用畜禽养殖废弃物生产有机肥产品的，享受国家关于化肥运力安排等支持政策；购买使用有机肥产品的，享受不低于国家关于化肥的使用补贴等优惠政策。  
畜禽养殖场、养殖小区的畜禽养殖污染防治设施运行用电执行农业用电价格。

**Article 30** Those who use livestock and poultry breeding wastes to produce organic fertilizer products shall enjoy the State's support policies such as the arrangement of transportation capacity for chemical fertilizers; those who purchase and use organic fertilizer products shall enjoy preferential policies such as subsidies for the use of chemical fertilizers at a level not lower than those provided by the State.  
The electricity used for the operation of pollution prevention facilities for livestock and poultry breeding in livestock and poultry breeding farms and breeding communities shall be charged at the agricultural electricity price.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家鼓励和支持利用畜禽养殖废弃物进行沼气发电，自发自用、多余电量接入电网。电网企业应当依照法律和国家有关规定为沼气发电提供无歧视的电网接入服务，并全额收购其电网覆盖范围内符合并网技术标准的多余电量。  
利用畜禽养殖废弃物进行沼气发电的，依法享受国家规定的上网电价优惠政策。利用畜禽养殖废弃物制取沼气或进而制取天然气的，依法享受新能源优惠政策。

**Article 31** The State encourages and supports the use of livestock and poultry breeding waste for biogas power generation, for self-generation and self-consumption, with surplus electricity connected to the power grid. Power grid enterprises shall, in accordance with laws and relevant State regulations, provide non-discriminatory grid access services for biogas power generation, and purchase in full the surplus electricity within the coverage of their power grids that meets the grid connection technical standards.  
Where biogas power generation is carried out using livestock and poultry breeding waste, it shall enjoy the preferential on-grid pricing policies prescribed by the State according to law. Where biogas or further natural gas is produced from livestock and poultry breeding waste, the preferential policies for new energy shall be enjoyed according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　地方各级人民政府可以根据本地区实际，对畜禽养殖场、养殖小区支出的建设项目环境影响咨询费用给予补助。

**Article 32** Local people's governments at all levels may, based on the actual conditions of their regions, provide subsidies for the expenses of environmental impact consultation on construction projects incurred by livestock and poultry farms and farming communities.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家鼓励和支持对染疫畜禽、病死或者死因不明畜禽尸体进行集中无害化处理，并按照国家有关规定对处理费用、养殖损失给予适当补助。

**Article 33** The State encourages and supports centralized harmless treatment of diseased livestock and poultry, and carcasses of livestock and poultry that have died of disease or unknown causes, and shall, in accordance with relevant State regulations, provide appropriate subsidies for treatment costs and farming losses.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　畜禽养殖场、养殖小区排放污染物符合国家和地方规定的污染物排放标准和总量控制指标，自愿与环境保护主管部门签订进一步削减污染物排放量协议的，由县级人民政府按照国家有关规定给予奖励，并优先列入县级以上人民政府安排的环境保护和畜禽养殖发展相关财政资金扶持范围。

**Article 34** Where livestock and poultry farms or farming communities that discharge pollutants meeting the national and local standards for pollutant discharge and total volume control indicators voluntarily enter into an agreement with the competent department of environmental protection on further reducing pollutant emissions, the people's government at the county level shall, in accordance with relevant State regulations, give rewards and give priority to including them in the scope of financial support for environmental protection and livestock and poultry breeding development arranged by the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　畜禽养殖户自愿建设综合利用和无害化处理设施、采取措施减少污染物排放的，可以依照本条例规定享受相关激励和扶持政策。

**Article 35** Livestock and poultry farming households that voluntarily construct comprehensive utilization and harmless treatment facilities and take measures to reduce pollutant emissions may enjoy the relevant incentive and support policies in accordance with these Regulations.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　各级人民政府环境保护主管部门、农牧主管部门以及其他有关部门未依照本条例规定履行职责的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 36** Where the competent departments of environmental protection, agriculture and animal husbandry, and other relevant departments of the people's governments at all levels fail to perform their duties in accordance with these Regulations, the persons directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if the persons directly in charge and other directly liable persons commit a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　违反本条例规定，在禁止养殖区域内建设畜禽养殖场、养殖小区的，由县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门责令停止违法行为；拒不停止违法行为的，处3万元以上10万元以下的罚款，并报县级以上人民政府责令拆除或者关闭。在饮用水水源保护区建设畜禽养殖场、养殖小区的，由县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门责令停止违法行为，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款，并报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令拆除或者关闭。

**Article 37** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a livestock and poultry farm or farming community is built in a prohibited farming area, the competent department of environmental protection of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act; if the illegal act is not stopped, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the matter shall be reported to the people's government at or above the county level for an order to dismantle or close down. Where a livestock and poultry farm or farming community is built in a drinking water source protection area, the competent department of environmental protection of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act, impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and report to and obtain approval from the people's government with approval authority, and order dismantlement or closure.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反本条例规定，畜禽养殖场、养殖小区依法应当进行环境影响评价而未进行的，由有权审批该项目环境影响评价文件的环境保护主管部门责令停止建设，限期补办手续；逾期不补办手续的，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 38** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a livestock and poultry farm or farming community that shall conduct an environmental impact assessment according to law fails to do so, the competent department of environmental protection with the authority to approve the environmental impact assessment document for the project shall order cessation of construction and require completion of the formalities within a time limit; if the formalities are not completed within the time limit, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反本条例规定，未建设污染防治配套设施或者自行建设的配套设施不合格，也未委托他人对畜禽养殖废弃物进行综合利用和无害化处理，畜禽养殖场、养殖小区即投入生产、使用，或者建设的污染防治配套设施未正常运行的，由县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门责令停止生产或者使用，可以处10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 39** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a livestock and poultry farm or farming community is put into production or use without having constructed supporting facilities for pollution prevention and control, or where the supporting facilities constructed by itself are substandard, and it has not entrusted others to carry out comprehensive utilization and harmless treatment of livestock and poultry breeding waste, or where the constructed supporting facilities for pollution prevention and control are not operated normally, the competent department of environmental protection of the people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of production or use, and may impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期采取治理措施消除污染，依照《中华人民共和国水污染防治法》、《中华人民共和国固体废物污染环境防治法》的有关规定予以处罚：  
(一)将畜禽养殖废弃物用作肥料，超出土地消纳能力，造成环境污染的；  
(二)从事畜禽养殖活动或者畜禽养殖废弃物处理活动，未采取有效措施，导致畜禽养殖废弃物渗出、泄漏的。

**Article 40** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any of the following acts is committed, the competent department of environmental protection of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act, require adoption of remediation measures within a time limit to eliminate pollution, and impose penalties in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Water Pollution Prevention and Control Law of the People's Republic of China and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Control of Environmental Pollution by Solid Wastes:  
(1) Using livestock and poultry breeding waste as fertilizer, exceeding the land's carrying capacity, and causing environmental pollution;  
(2) Engaging in livestock and poultry breeding activities or livestock and poultry breeding waste treatment activities without taking effective measures, resulting in seepage or leakage of livestock and poultry breeding waste.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　排放畜禽养殖废弃物不符合国家或者地方规定的污染物排放标准或者总量控制指标，或者未经无害化处理直接向环境排放畜禽养殖废弃物的，由县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门责令限期治理，可以处5万元以下的罚款。县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门作出限期治理决定后，应当会同同级人民政府农牧等有关部门对整改措施的落实情况及时进行核查，并向社会公布核查结果。

**Article 41** Where the discharge of livestock and poultry breeding wastes fails to meet the national or local pollutant discharge standards or total pollutant discharge control indicators, or where livestock and poultry breeding wastes are directly discharged into the environment without undergoing harmless treatment, the competent department of environmental protection under the local people's government at or above the county level shall order treatment within a specified time limit, and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan. After making a decision on treatment within a specified time limit, the competent department of environmental protection under the local people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments such as the competent department of agriculture and animal husbandry under the same people's government, promptly verify the implementation of the corrective measures and publish the verification results to the public.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　未按照规定对染疫畜禽和病害畜禽养殖废弃物进行无害化处理的，由动物卫生监督机构责令无害化处理，所需处理费用由违法行为人承担，可以处3000元以下的罚款。

**Article 42** Where livestock and poultry infected with epidemic diseases or disease-causing livestock and poultry breeding wastes are not subjected to harmless treatment in accordance with the provisions, the animal health supervision institution shall order the harmless treatment to be carried out, the cost of which shall be borne by the violator, and may impose a fine of not more than 3,000 yuan.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　畜禽养殖场、养殖小区的具体规模标准由省级人民政府确定，并报国务院环境保护主管部门和国务院农牧主管部门备案。

**Article 43** The specific scale standards for livestock and poultry breeding farms and communities shall be determined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and shall be reported to the competent department of environmental protection under the State Council and the competent department of agriculture and animal husbandry under the State Council for the record.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　本条例自2014年1月1日起施行。

**Article 44** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2014.
