# 生态环境监测条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2025-10-31  
**生效日期**：2026-01-01

> 第十五条　国务院生态环境主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门按照有关规定统一发布生态环境质量监测信息，定期发布生态环境状况公报。 任何单位和个人不得违法发布生态环境质量监测信息。 第十六条　生态环境主管部门、其他有关部门应当按照职责分工，依法加强对各类污染源（包括移动污染源）等的监督监测，并根据监测结果向有关单位或者个人提出相关建议、要求或者作出处理决定。 鼓励在生态环境监督监测中使用遥感监测等非接触式技术手段，提升监测效率，减少对正常生产经营活动等的影响。 第十七条　生态环境主管部门、其他有关部门应当加强对生态环境风险的监测预警，提升生态环境风险预警能力；发现可能危害生态环境安全或者公众健康情形的，县级以上人民政府应当依法及时发布预警信息。 第十八条　县级以上人民政府应当建立健全突发生态环境事件应急监测管理体系，将突发生态环境事件应急监测纳入相应的应急预案，提高突发生态环境事件应急监测响应能力。 突发生态环境事件发生后，生态环境主管部门和其他有关部门应当根据应急处置工作统一部署开展应急监测。突发生态环境事件应急处置工作结束后，有关人民政府应当立即组织评估事件造成的环境影响和损失，并及时将评估结果向社会公布。 第十九条　地方各级人民政府、县级以上人民政府有关部门工作人员不得以任何方式对公共监测数据弄虚作假或者明示、暗示有关单位、个人对公共监测数据弄虚作假，不得对依法履行职责或者拒绝、抵制监测违法行为的单位、个人打击报复。 第三章　自行监测 第二十条　企事业单位对其生产经营等活动中污染物、温室气体等的排放情况以及建设项目、突发生态环境事件等对生态环境的影响，依照法律、法规规定开展自行监测。 第二十一条　开展自行监测应当按照生态环境监测有关规范和标准制定监测方案，明确监测点位设置、监测指标、监测频次、监测方式等。 自行监测的主要监测点位应当按照规定安装、使用可以获取监测活动过程和监测设备运行情况的视频监控设备，并与生态环境主管部门或者其他有关部门联网。 第二十二条　开展自行监测应当使用符合国家标准和规范的监测设备，并对监测设备进行经常性维护、保养和定期检定、校准，保证监测设备正常运行。 第二十三条　依照法律、法规规定应当安装、使用自动监测设备的企事业单位，其自动监测设备应当与生态环境主管部门联网。 企事业单位发现自动监测设备传输数据异常的，应当及时报告生态环境主管部门，并对自动监测设备进行检查、修复。

### 正文

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　生态环境主管部门、其他有关部门应当按照职责分工，依法加强对各类污染源（包括移动污染源）等的监督监测，并根据监测结果向有关单位或者个人提出相关建议、要求或者作出处理决定。  
鼓励在生态环境监督监测中使用遥感监测等非接触式技术手段，提升监测效率，减少对正常生产经营活动等的影响。

**Article 16** The competent departments of ecological environment and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective duties, according to law strengthen the supervision and monitoring of various pollution sources (including mobile pollution sources), and based on the monitoring results, put forward relevant suggestions or requirements to the relevant units or individuals, or make handling decisions.  
Encouragement is given to the use of non-contact technical means such as remote sensing monitoring in ecological environment supervision and monitoring, so as to improve monitoring efficiency and reduce the impact on normal production and business activities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　生态环境主管部门、其他有关部门应当加强对生态环境风险的监测预警，提升生态环境风险预警能力；发现可能危害生态环境安全或者公众健康情形的，县级以上人民政府应当依法及时发布预警信息。

**Article 17** The competent departments of ecological environment and other relevant departments shall strengthen the monitoring and early warning of ecological environment risks, and enhance the capability of early warning of ecological environment risks; where any circumstances that may endanger ecological environment safety or public health are discovered, the people's government at or above the county level shall, according to law, release early warning information in a timely manner.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上人民政府应当建立健全突发生态环境事件应急监测管理体系，将突发生态环境事件应急监测纳入相应的应急预案，提高突发生态环境事件应急监测响应能力。  
突发生态环境事件发生后，生态环境主管部门和其他有关部门应当根据应急处置工作统一部署开展应急监测。突发生态环境事件应急处置工作结束后，有关人民政府应当立即组织评估事件造成的环境影响和损失，并及时将评估结果向社会公布。

**Article 18** The people's government at or above the county level shall establish and improve the emergency monitoring management system for sudden ecological environment incidents, incorporate emergency monitoring of sudden ecological environment incidents into the corresponding emergency plans, and improve the emergency monitoring response capability for sudden ecological environment incidents.  
After a sudden ecological environment incident occurs, the competent departments of ecological environment and other relevant departments shall carry out emergency monitoring based on the unified deployment of emergency handling work. After the emergency handling work for a sudden ecological environment incident is completed, the relevant people's government shall immediately organize an assessment of the environmental impact and losses caused by the incident, and promptly make the assessment results public to the society.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　地方各级人民政府、县级以上人民政府有关部门工作人员不得以任何方式对公共监测数据弄虚作假或者明示、暗示有关单位、个人对公共监测数据弄虚作假，不得对依法履行职责或者拒绝、抵制监测违法行为的单位、个人打击报复。

**Article 19** The staff of local people's governments at various levels and relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall not, in any manner, falsify public monitoring data, or explicitly or implicitly instruct relevant units or individuals to falsify public monitoring data, nor shall they retaliate against units or individuals that perform their duties according to law or refuse or resist illegal monitoring activities.

### 第三章　自行监测

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　企事业单位对其生产经营等活动中污染物、温室气体等的排放情况以及建设项目、突发生态环境事件等对生态环境的影响，依照法律、法规规定开展自行监测。

**Article 20** Enterprises and public institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations, conduct self-monitoring on the discharge of pollutants, greenhouse gases, etc. during their production and business activities, as well as the impact of construction projects and sudden ecological environment incidents on the ecological environment.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　开展自行监测应当按照生态环境监测有关规范和标准制定监测方案，明确监测点位设置、监测指标、监测频次、监测方式等。  
自行监测的主要监测点位应当按照规定安装、使用可以获取监测活动过程和监测设备运行情况的视频监控设备，并与生态环境主管部门或者其他有关部门联网。

**Article 21** When conducting self-monitoring, a monitoring plan shall be formulated in accordance with the relevant specifications and standards for ecological environment monitoring, specifying the setting of monitoring points, monitoring indicators, monitoring frequency, monitoring methods, etc.  
The main monitoring points for self-monitoring shall be installed and used with video monitoring equipment capable of obtaining the monitoring activity process and the operation status of monitoring equipment as required, and shall be networked with the competent department of ecological environment or other relevant departments.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　开展自行监测应当使用符合国家标准和规范的监测设备，并对监测设备进行经常性维护、保养和定期检定、校准，保证监测设备正常运行。

**Article 22** When conducting self-monitoring, monitoring equipment that meets the national standards and specifications shall be used, and the monitoring equipment shall be regularly maintained, serviced, and periodically verified and calibrated to ensure the normal operation of the monitoring equipment.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　依照法律、法规规定应当安装、使用自动监测设备的企事业单位，其自动监测设备应当与生态环境主管部门联网。  
企事业单位发现自动监测设备传输数据异常的，应当及时报告生态环境主管部门，并对自动监测设备进行检查、修复。

**Article 23** For enterprises and public institutions required by laws and regulations to install and use automatic monitoring equipment, their automatic monitoring equipment shall be networked with the competent department of ecological environment.  
If an enterprise or public institution finds that the data transmitted by the automatic monitoring equipment is abnormal, it shall promptly report to the competent department of ecological environment, and inspect and repair the automatic monitoring equipment.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　采用手工监测方式开展自行监测的，应当如实记录手工监测期间的生产负荷、污染防治设施运行情况等工况，确保监测数据具有代表性。

**Article 24** Where manual monitoring is used for self-monitoring, the production load, operation of pollution prevention and control facilities and other working conditions during the manual monitoring period shall be truthfully recorded to ensure that the monitoring data is representative.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　企事业单位应当建立健全监测数据质量管理制度。企事业单位及其负责人对监测数据的真实性、准确性负责。  
企事业单位不得实施或者明示、暗示有关单位、个人实施下列对监测数据弄虚作假的行为：  
（一）未实际开展监测，直接出具监测报告；  
（二）篡改、伪造原始监测记录、监测数据；  
（三）故意漏检监测项目或者改变监测条件；  
（四）调换监测样品或者擅自改变采样点位、时间等，干扰采样环境或者采样活动；  
（五）通过不正常运行、破坏监测设备，擅自修改监测设备参数设置，虚假标记自动监测设备状况或者生产设施、污染防治设施工况，或者使用作弊工具等手段，使监测数据失真；  
（六）其他对监测数据弄虚作假的行为。

**Article 25** Enterprises and public institutions shall establish and improve a monitoring data quality management system. Enterprises and public institutions and their persons in charge shall be responsible for the authenticity and accuracy of the monitoring data.  
Enterprises and public institutions shall not carry out, or explicitly or implicitly instruct relevant units or individuals to carry out, the following acts of falsifying monitoring data:  
(1) Directly issuing a monitoring report without actually conducting monitoring;  
(2) Tampering with or fabricating original monitoring records or monitoring data;  
(3) Intentionally omitting monitoring items or altering monitoring conditions;  
(4) Substituting monitoring samples or arbitrarily changing sampling points or times, thereby interfering with the sampling environment or sampling activities;  
(5) Causing monitoring data to be distorted by means such as abnormal operation or destruction of monitoring equipment, unauthorized modification of monitoring equipment parameter settings, false marking of the status of automatic monitoring equipment or the working conditions of production facilities and pollution prevention and control facilities, or using cheating tools;  
(6) Other acts of falsifying monitoring data.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　企事业单位应当依照法律、法规规定的期限保存自行监测的原始监测记录；法律、法规没有规定保存期限的，原始监测记录至少保存5年。  
企事业单位应当依法公开自行监测相关信息，接受社会监督。

**Article 26** Enterprises and public institutions shall, in accordance with the time limit prescribed by laws and regulations, preserve the original monitoring records of self-monitoring; if laws or regulations do not prescribe a preservation period, the original monitoring records shall be preserved for at least 5 years.  
Enterprises and public institutions shall, according to law, make public the relevant information on self-monitoring and accept social supervision.

### 第四章　技术服务机构

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　政府及其有关部门、企事业单位可以委托依法设立的技术服务机构开展生态环境监测相关服务（以下简称监测服务）。

**Article 27** The government and its relevant departments, enterprises and public institutions may entrust legally established technical service institutions to carry out ecological environment monitoring-related services (hereinafter referred to as monitoring services).

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　开展监测服务的技术服务机构应当具备相应的设施设备、技术能力、技术人员和管理能力，并按照规定向生态环境主管部门备案。其中从事检验检测活动的，应当依法取得检验检测机构资质认定。  
技术服务机构办理备案，应当向生态环境主管部门提交书面承诺，书面承诺应当包括其设施设备、技术能力、技术人员、管理能力方面的信息，技术服务机构对信息的真实性负责。  
生态环境主管部门应当将备案的技术服务机构及其书面承诺、业务范围等向社会公布，并提供备案信息查询服务。  
技术服务机构具体管理办法由国务院生态环境主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 28** Technical service institutions that carry out monitoring services shall have the corresponding facilities and equipment, technical capabilities, technical personnel and management capabilities, and shall register with the competent department of ecological environment as required. Among them, those engaged in inspection and testing activities shall obtain the qualification accreditation of inspection and testing institutions according to law.  
When going through the registration procedures, a technical service institution shall submit a written commitment to the competent department of ecological environment. The written commitment shall include information on its facilities and equipment, technical capabilities, technical personnel, and management capabilities, and the technical service institution shall be responsible for the authenticity of the information.  
The competent department of ecological environment shall make public the registered technical service institutions and their written commitments, business scope, etc., and provide information inquiry services for registration.  
The specific management measures for technical service institutions shall be formulated by the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　技术服务机构不得超出其业务范围接受委托，不得违反约定将受托业务转委托。  
技术服务机构应当独立、客观、公正开展监测服务，不得同时接受可能存在利益冲突的委托。

**Article 29** A technical service institution shall not accept entrustment beyond its business scope, nor shall it sub-entrust the entrusted business in violation of the agreement.  
A technical service institution shall carry out monitoring services independently, objectively and impartially, and shall not accept entrustments that may have a conflict of interest at the same time.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　技术服务机构接受委托开展监测服务，应当遵守生态环境监测规范和标准。  
委托单位及其有关人员应当配合技术服务机构开展监测服务，并提供必要的便利和条件。  
委托单位应当按规定对技术服务机构监测服务活动加强监督。

**Article 30** A technical service institution that accepts entrustment to carry out monitoring services shall comply with the specifications and standards for ecological environment monitoring.  
The entrusting unit and its relevant personnel shall cooperate with the technical service institution in carrying out monitoring services and provide necessary convenience and conditions.  
The entrusting unit shall strengthen supervision over the monitoring service activities of the technical service institution as required.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　技术服务机构接受委托开展生态环境监测设备运行维护，应当建立运行维护记录制度；对发现的影响生态环境监测设备正常运行的问题，应当及时处理，不得隐瞒。

**Article 31** A technical service institution that accepts entrustment to carry out operation and maintenance of ecological environment monitoring equipment shall establish an operation and maintenance recording system; any problems found that affect the normal operation of ecological environment monitoring equipment shall be dealt with in a timely manner and shall not be concealed.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　接受委托开展监测服务的技术服务机构应当建立监测数据质量管理制度，不得以任何方式对监测数据弄虚作假。  
[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)的规定，适用于接受委托开展监测服务的技术服务机构。  
技术服务机构及其负责人对技术服务机构出具的监测数据的真实性、准确性负责。

**Article 32** A technical service institution that accepts entrustment to carry out monitoring services shall establish a monitoring data quality management system, and shall not falsify monitoring data in any manner.  
The provisions of Article 25 of these Regulations apply to technical service institutions that accept entrustment to carry out monitoring services.  
A technical service institution and its person in charge shall be responsible for the authenticity and accuracy of the monitoring data issued by the technical service institution.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　接受委托开展监测服务的技术服务机构应当按照规定保存其开展业务的相关数据、报告、记录以及委托合同等材料，保证业务活动全过程可追溯。

**Article 33** A technical service institution that accepts entrustment to carry out monitoring services shall, as required, preserve the relevant data, reports, records, and entrustment contracts generated during its business activities, so as to ensure the entire process of business activities can be traced.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院生态环境主管部门推动通过生态环境监测管理服务平台开展监测数据报送、技术服务机构备案、相关信息公布等管理与服务，并与国务院有关部门建立信息共享机制。

**Article 34** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council promotes the management and services such as reporting of monitoring data, registration of technical service institutions, and publication of relevant information through the ecological environment monitoring management service platform, and establishes an information sharing mechanism with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　生态环境主管部门、其他有关部门应当按照职责分工，加强对生态环境监测活动的监督检查。生态环境主管部门、其他有关部门应当加强协调配合，能够联合检查的实行联合检查，鼓励通过非现场检查、使用非接触式技术手段开展监督检查。  
生态环境主管部门、其他有关部门根据需要对企事业单位、技术服务机构及相关场所等进行现场检查的，可以要求有关单位、个人就相关事项作出说明，查阅、复制相关资料，查询、检查相关信息系统，查封涉嫌违法的相关设备、场所等，并可以开展现场监测。有关单位、个人应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻碍。  
检查人员对检查中知悉的国家秘密、工作秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私和个人信息，依法负有保密义务。

**Article 35** The competent departments of ecological environment and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective duties, strengthen the supervision and inspection of ecological environment monitoring activities. The competent departments of ecological environment and other relevant departments shall strengthen coordination and cooperation; where joint inspection is possible, joint inspection shall be carried out, and the use of off-site inspection and non-contact technical means for supervision and inspection is encouraged.  
Where the competent departments of ecological environment and other relevant departments conduct on-site inspections of enterprises, public institutions, technical service institutions and relevant premises as necessary, they may require the relevant units or individuals to make explanations on relevant matters, consult and copy relevant materials, inquire and inspect relevant information systems, seal up suspected illegal equipment and premises, etc., and may conduct on-site monitoring. The relevant units and individuals shall cooperate and shall not refuse or obstruct.  
Inspectors shall, according to law, have a duty of confidentiality regarding state secrets, work secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy and personal information known during inspections.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国务院生态环境主管部门会同国务院有关部门建立生态环境监测信用评价制度，依法依规将相关违法违规行为记入信用记录，纳入全国信用信息共享平台。

**Article 36** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council establishes an ecological environment monitoring credit evaluation system, records relevant illegal or non-compliant acts into the credit records in accordance with laws and regulations, and incorporates them into the national credit information sharing platform.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　生态环境主管部门根据技术服务机构的规模、技术能力、技术人员水平、管理能力、信用状况等，对技术服务机构实行分级分类监管，引导技术服务机构规模化发展，提升专业化水平和市场公信力。

**Article 37** The competent department of ecological environment implements graded and categorized supervision over technical service institutions based on their scale, technical capabilities, level of technical personnel, management capabilities, credit status, etc., guides the scaled development of technical service institutions, and improves the level of specialization and market credibility.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　生态环境监测相关行业组织应当建立健全行业规范，加强行业自律管理，促进行业健康发展。

**Article 38** Industry organizations related to ecological environment monitoring shall establish and improve industry norms, strengthen industry self-discipline management, and promote the healthy development of the industry.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　任何单位和个人对违反本条例规定的行为，有权向生态环境主管部门和其他有关部门举报。接到举报的部门应当依法及时处理。

**Article 39** Any unit or individual has the right to report acts that violate the provisions of these Regulations to the competent departments of ecological environment and other relevant departments. The department that receives the report shall, according to law, handle it in a timely manner.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　侵占、损毁生态环境质量监测站点及其设施、设备，或者干扰生态环境质量监测站点正常运行的，由生态环境主管部门或者其他有关部门依据职责责令改正，处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 40** Anyone who encroaches upon or damages ecological environment quality monitoring stations and their facilities or equipment, or interferes with the normal operation of ecological environment quality monitoring stations, shall be ordered by the competent department of ecological environment or other relevant departments to make corrections within a specified time limit, and shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　地方各级人民政府、县级以上人民政府有关部门工作人员有下列情形之一的，依法给予处分，并予以通报：  
（一）对公共监测数据弄虚作假或者明示、暗示有关单位、个人对公共监测数据弄虚作假；  
（二）对依法履行职责或者拒绝、抵制监测违法行为的单位、个人打击报复；  
（三）其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的情形。

**Article 41** Where the staff of local people's governments at various levels or relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level fall under any of the following circumstances, they shall be given sanctions according to law and be notified:  
(1) Falsifying public monitoring data, or explicitly or implicitly instructing relevant units or individuals to falsify public monitoring data;  
(2) Retaliating against units or individuals that perform their duties according to law or refuse or resist illegal monitoring activities;  
(3) Other circumstances of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　企事业单位在开展自行监测中有下列情形之一的，由生态环境主管部门或者其他有关部门依据职责责令改正，处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停产停业：  
（一）不遵守生态环境监测规范或者标准，导致监测数据失真；  
（二）使用不符合国家标准或者规范的监测设备，或者未对监测设备进行经常性维护、保养或者定期检定、校准；  
（三）未按照规定在主要监测点位安装、使用视频监控设备；  
（四）未按照规定安装、使用自动监测设备，或者发现自动监测设备传输数据异常未及时报告并对自动监测设备进行检查、修复；  
（五）主要监测点位视频监控设备或者自动监测设备未与生态环境主管部门或者其他有关部门联网；  
（六）未记录或者未如实记录手工监测期间的生产负荷、污染防治设施运行情况等工况；  
（七）未建立监测数据质量管理制度；  
（八）未保存或者未按照规定期限保存原始监测记录；  
（九）未依法公开或者不如实公开自行监测相关信息。  
生态环境主管部门或者其他有关部门发现企事业单位使用的监测设备不符合国家标准或者规范的，除依照前款规定对企事业单位予以处罚外，可以将该设备有关情况以及其生产者、销售者向社会公布，并通报市场监督管理部门，由市场监督管理部门对生产者、销售者依法处理。

**Article 42** Where an enterprise or public institution, in conducting self-monitoring, falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent department of ecological environment or other relevant departments shall, within their respective duties, order it to make corrections, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be ordered to suspend production or business:  
(1) Failing to comply with ecological environment monitoring specifications or standards, resulting in distortion of monitoring data;  
(2) Using monitoring equipment that does not meet national standards or specifications, or failing to regularly maintain, service, or periodically verify and calibrate the monitoring equipment;  
(3) Failing to install and use video monitoring equipment at main monitoring points as required;  
(4) Failing to install and use automatic monitoring equipment as required, or failing to report in a timely manner and inspect and repair the automatic monitoring equipment upon discovering abnormal data transmission;  
(5) Failure of video monitoring equipment at main monitoring points or automatic monitoring equipment to be networked with the competent department of ecological environment or other relevant departments;  
(6) Failing to record or truthfully record the production load, operation of pollution prevention and control facilities and other working conditions during manual monitoring;  
(7) Failing to establish a monitoring data quality management system;  
(8) Failing to preserve or preserve original monitoring records for the required period;  
(9) Failing to disclose or truthfully disclose information related to self-monitoring according to law.  
Where the competent department of ecological environment or other relevant department finds that the monitoring equipment used by an enterprise or public institution does not meet national standards or specifications, in addition to punishing the enterprise or institution in accordance with the preceding paragraph, it may make public the situation of the equipment and its producer and seller, and notify the market supervision and administration department; the market supervision and administration department shall deal with the producer and seller according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　技术服务机构有下列情形之一的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；拒不改正的，责令停业：  
（一）不具备相应的设施设备、技术能力、技术人员、管理能力；  
（二）未按照规定备案；  
（三）超出其业务范围接受委托，违反约定将受托业务转委托，或者同时接受可能存在利益冲突的委托；  
（四）开展监测服务不遵守生态环境监测规范或者标准，导致监测数据失真；  
（五）开展生态环境监测设备运行维护未建立运行维护记录制度，或者隐瞒、不及时处理发现的影响生态环境监测设备正常运行的问题；  
（六）未建立监测数据质量管理制度；  
（七）未按照规定保存其开展业务的相关数据、报告、记录、委托合同等材料。

**Article 43** Where a technical service institution falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent department of ecological environment shall order it to make corrections, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be ordered to cease business:  
(1) Lacking the corresponding facilities and equipment, technical capabilities, technical personnel, or management capabilities;  
(2) Failing to register as required;  
(3) Accepting entrustment beyond its business scope, sub-entrusting the entrusted business in violation of the agreement, or simultaneously accepting entrustments that may have a conflict of interest;  
(4) Carrying out monitoring services without complying with ecological environment monitoring specifications or standards, resulting in distortion of monitoring data;  
(5) Failing to establish an operation and maintenance recording system for the operation and maintenance of ecological environment monitoring equipment, or concealing or failing to promptly deal with problems found that affect the normal operation of ecological environment monitoring equipment;  
(6) Failing to establish a monitoring data quality management system;  
(7) Failing to preserve relevant data, reports, records, entrustment contracts and other materials generated from its business activities as required.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　企事业单位实施或者明示、暗示有关单位、个人对监测数据弄虚作假的，由生态环境主管部门或者其他有关部门依据职责责令改正，处10万元以上100万元以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停产停业。

**Article 44** Where an enterprise or public institution carries out, or explicitly or implicitly instructs relevant units or individuals to falsify monitoring data, the competent department of ecological environment or other relevant departments shall, within their respective duties, order it to make corrections, and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible personnel shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend production or business.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　接受委托开展监测服务的技术服务机构对监测数据弄虚作假的，由生态环境主管部门处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处50万元以上200万元以下的罚款，禁止从事监测服务，对其中取得监测服务相关资质的，由授予其资质的部门吊销其资质证书。  
技术服务机构因前款规定的违法行为受到处罚的，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款，5年内禁止从事监测服务；情节严重的，10年内禁止从事监测服务；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任，终身禁止从事监测服务。

**Article 45** Where a technical service institution that accepts entrustment to carry out monitoring services falsifies monitoring data, the competent department of ecological environment shall impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed, and it shall be prohibited from engaging in monitoring services; if it has obtained relevant qualifications for monitoring services, the department that granted the qualifications shall revoke its qualification certificate.  
Where a technical service institution is punished for the illegal act specified in the preceding paragraph, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible personnel shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan, and shall be prohibited from engaging in monitoring services for 5 years; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be prohibited from engaging in monitoring services for 10 years; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law, and they shall be permanently prohibited from engaging in monitoring services.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本条例规定，给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 46** Anyone who violates the provisions of these Regulations and causes losses to others shall bear compensation liability according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　军队的生态环境监测工作按照中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 47** The ecological environment monitoring work of the armed forces shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　生态环境监测数据安全保护，依照《中华人民共和国数据安全法》、《网络数据安全管理条例》的规定执行。

**Article 48** The security protection of ecological environment monitoring data shall be carried out in accordance with the Data Security Law of the People's Republic of China and the Regulations on Network Data Security Management.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　本条例自2026年1月1日起施行。

**Article 49** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2026.
