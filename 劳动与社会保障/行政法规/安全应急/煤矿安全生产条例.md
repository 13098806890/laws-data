### 正文

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　煤矿建设项目竣工投入生产或者使用前，应当由建设单位负责组织对安全设施进行验收，并对验收结果负责；经验收合格后，方可投入生产和使用。

**Article 16** Before a completed coal mine construction project is put into production or use, the construction entity shall be responsible for organizing the acceptance inspection of the safety facilities and shall be responsible for the acceptance results; the project may be put into production and use only after it has passed the acceptance inspection.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　煤矿企业进行生产，应当依照《安全生产许可证条例》的规定取得安全生产许可证。未取得安全生产许可证的，不得生产。

**Article 17** A coal mining enterprise that engages in production shall obtain a safety production license in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Safety Production Licenses. No production shall be carried out without obtaining a safety production license.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　煤矿企业主要负责人对本企业安全生产工作负有下列职责：  
（一）建立健全并落实全员安全生产责任制，加强安全生产标准化建设；  
（二）组织制定并实施安全生产规章制度和作业规程、操作规程；  
（三）组织制定并实施安全生产教育和培训计划；  
（四）保证安全生产投入的有效实施；  
（五）组织建立并落实安全风险分级管控和隐患排查治理双重预防工作机制，督促、检查安全生产工作，及时消除事故隐患；  
（六）组织制定并实施生产安全事故应急救援预案；  
（七）及时、如实报告煤矿生产安全事故。

**Article 18** The principal responsible person of a coal mining enterprise shall have the following duties regarding the work of safety production of the enterprise:  
(1) Establish, improve, and implement the safety production responsibility system covering all employees, and strengthen the standardization of safety production;  
(2) Organize the formulation and implementation of safety production rules and regulations, as well as operating procedures and work instructions;  
(3) Organize the formulation and implementation of safety production education and training plans;  
(4) Ensure the effective implementation of the investment in safety production;  
(5) Organize the establishment and implementation of the dual prevention mechanism of graded risk control and hidden danger investigation and management, supervise and inspect the work of safety production, and promptly eliminate accident hazards;  
(6) Organize the formulation and implementation of emergency rescue plans for production safety accidents;  
(7) Promptly and truthfully report coal mine production safety accidents.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　煤矿企业应当设置安全生产管理机构并配备专职安全生产管理人员。安全生产管理机构和安全生产管理人员负有下列安全生产职责：  
（一）组织或者参与拟订安全生产规章制度、作业规程、操作规程和生产安全事故应急救援预案；  
（二）组织或者参与安全生产教育和培训，如实记录安全生产教育和培训情况；  
（三）组织开展安全生产法律法规宣传教育；  
（四）组织开展安全风险辨识评估，督促落实重大安全风险管控措施；  
（五）制止和纠正违章指挥、强令冒险作业、违反规程的行为，发现威胁安全的紧急情况时，有权要求立即停止危险区域内的作业，撤出作业人员；  
（六）检查安全生产状况，及时排查事故隐患，对事故隐患排查治理情况进行统计分析，提出改进安全生产管理的建议；  
（七）组织或者参与应急救援演练；  
（八）督促落实安全生产整改措施。  
煤矿企业应当配备主要技术负责人，建立健全并落实技术管理体系。

**Article 19** A coal mining enterprise shall set up a safety production management organization and equip it with full-time safety production management personnel. The safety production management organization and safety production management personnel shall have the following safety production duties:  
(1) Organize or participate in the drafting of safety production rules and regulations, operating procedures, work instructions, and emergency rescue plans for production safety accidents;  
(2) Organize or participate in safety production education and training, and truthfully record the situation of safety production education and training;  
(3) Organize the publicity and education of safety production laws and regulations;  
(4) Organize the identification and assessment of safety risks, and supervise the implementation of control measures for major safety risks;  
(5) Stop and correct acts of illegal command, ordering dangerous operations, and violating procedures. When an emergency threatening safety is discovered, they shall have the right to immediately require the cessation of operations in dangerous areas and the withdrawal of operators;  
(6) Inspect the safety production situation, timely identify accident hazards, conduct statistical analysis on the investigation and management of accident hazards, and propose suggestions for improving safety production management;  
(7) Organize or participate in emergency rescue drills;  
(8) Supervise the implementation of corrective measures for safety production.  
A coal mining enterprise shall be equipped with a principal technical responsible person, and shall establish, improve, and implement a technical management system.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　煤矿企业从业人员负有下列安全生产职责：  
（一）遵守煤矿企业安全生产规章制度和作业规程、操作规程，严格落实岗位安全责任；  
（二）参加安全生产教育和培训，掌握本职工作所需的安全生产知识，提高安全生产技能，增强事故预防和应急处理能力；  
（三）及时报告发现的事故隐患或者其他不安全因素。  
对违章指挥和强令冒险作业的行为，煤矿企业从业人员有权拒绝并向县级以上地方人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门、所在地矿山安全监察机构报告。  
煤矿企业不得因从业人员拒绝违章指挥或者强令冒险作业而降低其工资、福利等待遇，无正当理由调整工作岗位，或者解除与其订立的劳动合同。

**Article 20** Employees of coal mining enterprises shall have the following safety production duties:  
(1) Comply with the safety production rules and regulations, operating procedures, and work instructions of the coal mining enterprise, and strictly implement post safety responsibilities;  
(2) Participate in safety production education and training, master the safety production knowledge necessary for their work, improve safety production skills, and enhance the ability to prevent accidents and handle emergencies;  
(3) Promptly report any discovered accident hazards or other unsafe factors.  
With regard to acts of illegal command and ordering dangerous operations, employees of coal mining enterprises shall have the right to refuse and report to the department of the local people's government at or above the county level that is responsible for the supervision and administration of coal mine safety production, or to the local mine safety supervision institution.  
A coal mining enterprise shall not, due to an employee's refusal to comply with an illegal command or an order for dangerous operations, reduce the employee's wages, benefits, or other treatment, change the employee's work position without justifiable reasons, or terminate the labor contract concluded with the employee.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　煤矿企业主要负责人和安全生产管理人员应当通过安全生产知识和管理能力考核，并持续保持相应水平和能力。  
煤矿企业从业人员经安全生产教育和培训合格，方可上岗作业。煤矿企业特种作业人员应当按照国家有关规定经专门的安全技术培训和考核合格，并取得相应资格。

**Article 21** The principal responsible persons and work safety management personnel of a coal mining enterprise shall pass the assessment of work safety knowledge and management capabilities, and shall continuously maintain the corresponding levels and capabilities.  
Employees of a coal mining enterprise may only work after passing work safety education and training. Special operations personnel of a coal mining enterprise shall, in accordance with relevant state regulations, undergo specialized safety technical training and examination, pass them, and obtain corresponding qualifications.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　煤矿企业应当为煤矿分别配备专职矿长、总工程师，分管安全、生产、机电的副矿长以及专业技术人员。  
对煤（岩）与瓦斯（二氧化碳）突出、高瓦斯、冲击地压、煤层容易自燃、水文地质类型复杂和极复杂的煤矿，还应当设立相应的专门防治机构，配备专职副总工程师。

**Article 22** A coal mining enterprise shall assign a full-time mine manager, a chief engineer, deputy mine managers in charge of safety, production, and mechanical & electrical affairs, as well as professional technical personnel for each coal mine.  
For coal mines with coal (rock) and gas (carbon dioxide) outburst, high gas, rock burst, coal seams prone to spontaneous combustion, and complex or extremely complex hydrogeological types, it shall also establish corresponding specialized prevention and control institutions and assign full-time deputy chief engineers.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　煤矿企业应当按照国家有关规定建立健全领导带班制度并严格考核。  
井工煤矿企业的负责人和生产经营管理人员应当轮流带班下井，并建立下井登记档案。

**Article 23** A coal mining enterprise shall establish and improve a leadership on-duty system in accordance with relevant state regulations and strictly assess it.  
The responsible persons and production and operation management personnel of an underground coal mining enterprise shall take turns leading shifts to go down the mine, and shall establish records of going down the mine.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　煤矿企业应当为从业人员提供符合国家标准或者行业标准的劳动防护用品，并监督、教育从业人员按照使用规则佩戴、使用。  
煤矿井下作业人员实行安全限员制度。煤矿企业应当依法制定井下工作时间管理制度。煤矿井下工作岗位不得使用劳务派遣用工。

**Article 24** A coal mining enterprise shall provide employees with labor protection articles that meet national or industry standards, and shall supervise and educate employees to wear and use them in accordance with the usage rules.  
A safety personnel quota system shall be implemented for workers in underground coal mines. A coal mining enterprise shall, according to law, formulate a management system for underground working hours. No labor dispatch employment shall be used for underground coal mine work positions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　煤矿企业使用的安全设备的设计、制造、安装、使用、检测、维修、改造和报废，应当符合国家标准或者行业标准。  
煤矿企业应当建立安全设备台账和追溯、管理制度，对安全设备进行经常性维护、保养并定期检测，保证正常运转，对安全设备购置、入库、使用、维护、保养、检测、维修、改造、报废等进行全流程记录并存档。  
煤矿企业不得使用应当淘汰的危及生产安全的设备、工艺，具体目录由国家矿山安全监察机构制定并公布。

**Article 25** The design, manufacture, installation, use, inspection, repair, modification and scrapping of safety equipment used by a coal mining enterprise shall comply with national or industry standards.  
A coal mining enterprise shall establish ledgers and traceability and management systems for safety equipment, conduct regular maintenance and servicing of safety equipment and conduct periodic inspections to ensure normal operation, and keep full-process records of purchase, warehousing, use, maintenance, servicing, inspection, repair, modification, scrapping, etc. of safety equipment, and archive such records.  
A coal mining enterprise shall not use equipment or processes that shall be eliminated and endanger production safety. The specific catalogue shall be formulated and published by the National Mine Safety Administration.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　煤矿的采煤、掘进、机电、运输、通风、排水、排土等主要生产系统和防瓦斯、防煤（岩）与瓦斯（二氧化碳）突出、防冲击地压、防火、防治水、防尘、防热害、防滑坡、监控与通讯等安全设施，应当符合煤矿安全规程和国家标准或者行业标准规定的管理和技术要求。  
煤矿企业及其有关人员不得关闭、破坏直接关系生产安全的监控、报警、防护、救生设备、设施，或者篡改、隐瞒、销毁其相关数据、信息，不得以任何方式影响其正常使用。

**Article 26** The main production systems of a coal mine, such as coal winning, tunneling, mechanical and electrical, transportation, ventilation, drainage, and waste disposal, as well as safety facilities such as gas prevention, coal (rock) and gas (carbon dioxide) outburst prevention, rock burst prevention, fire prevention, water prevention and control, dust prevention, heat damage prevention, landslide prevention, monitoring and communication, shall meet the management and technical requirements specified in the coal mine safety regulations and national or industry standards.  
A coal mining enterprise and its relevant personnel shall not close or damage monitoring, alarm, protection, and rescue equipment and facilities that are directly related to production safety, or tamper with, conceal, or destroy related data and information thereof, and shall not affect their normal use in any way.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　井工煤矿应当有符合煤矿安全规程和国家标准或者行业标准规定的安全出口、独立通风系统、安全监控系统、防尘供水系统、防灭火系统、供配电系统、运送人员装置和反映煤矿实际情况的图纸，并按照规定进行瓦斯等级、冲击地压、煤层自燃倾向性和煤尘爆炸性鉴定。  
井工煤矿应当按矿井瓦斯等级选用相应的煤矿许用炸药和电雷管，爆破工作由专职爆破工承担。

**Article 27** An underground coal mine shall have safety exits, an independent ventilation system, a safety monitoring system, a dust control water supply system, a fire prevention and extinguishing system, a power supply and distribution system, a personnel transport device, and drawings reflecting the actual conditions of the coal mine that comply with the coal mine safety regulations and national or industry standards, and shall conduct assessments of gas grade, rock burst, spontaneous combustion tendency of coal seams, and coal dust explosiveness in accordance with the regulations.  
An underground coal mine shall select corresponding coal mine permissible explosives and electric detonators according to the mine gas grade, and blasting work shall be undertaken by full-time blasters.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　露天煤矿的采场及排土场边坡与重要建筑物、构筑物之间应当留有足够的安全距离。  
煤矿企业应当定期对露天煤矿进行边坡稳定性评价，评价范围应当涵盖露天煤矿所有边坡。达不到边坡稳定要求时，应当修改采矿设计或者采取安全措施，同时加强边坡监测工作。

**Article 28** A sufficient safety distance shall be maintained between the slopes of the mining pit and waste dump of an open-pit coal mine and important buildings and structures.  
A coal mining enterprise shall conduct slope stability assessments of the open-pit coal mine periodically, and the assessment scope shall cover all slopes of the open-pit coal mine. Where the slope stability requirements are not met, the mining design shall be modified or safety measures shall be taken, and slope monitoring work shall be strengthened.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　煤矿企业应当依法制定生产安全事故应急救援预案，与所在地县级以上地方人民政府组织制定的生产安全事故应急救援预案相衔接，并定期组织演练。  
煤矿企业应当设立专职救护队；不具备设立专职救护队条件的，应当设立兼职救护队，并与邻近的专职救护队签订救护协议。发生事故时，专职救护队应当在规定时间内到达煤矿开展救援。

**Article 29** A coal mining enterprise shall, according to law, formulate an emergency rescue plan for production safety accidents, which shall be linked with the production safety accident emergency rescue plan formulated by the local people's government at or above the county level, and shall organize regular drills.  
A coal mining enterprise shall establish a full-time rescue team; if conditions for establishing a full-time rescue team are not available, it shall establish a part-time rescue team and sign a rescue agreement with a nearby full-time rescue team. When an accident occurs, the full-time rescue team shall arrive at the coal mine within the prescribed time to carry out rescue.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　煤矿企业应当在依法确定的开采范围内进行生产，不得超层、越界开采。  
采矿作业不得擅自开采保安煤柱，不得采用可能危及相邻煤矿生产安全的决水、爆破、贯通巷道等危险方法。

**Article 30** A coal mining enterprise shall conduct production within the mining area determined according to law, and shall not mine beyond the designated level or boundary.  
Mining operations shall not arbitrarily extract safety coal pillars, and shall not adopt dangerous methods such as water bursting, blasting, or connecting roadways that may endanger the production safety of adjacent coal mines.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　煤矿企业不得超能力、超强度或者超定员组织生产。正常生产煤矿因地质、生产技术条件、采煤方法或者工艺等发生变化导致生产能力发生较大变化的，应当依法重新核定其生产能力。  
县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门不得要求不具备安全生产条件的煤矿企业进行生产。

**Article 31** A coal mining enterprise shall not organize production beyond its capacity, intensity, or personnel quota. Where the production capacity of a normally operating coal mine changes significantly due to changes in geological conditions, production technology conditions, coal mining methods or processes, etc., its production capacity shall be re-determined according to law.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall not require a coal mining enterprise that does not meet the work safety conditions to carry out production.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　煤矿企业应当按照煤矿灾害程度和类型实施灾害治理，编制年度灾害预防和处理计划，并根据具体情况及时修改。

**Article 32** A coal mining enterprise shall implement disaster control according to the degree and type of coal mine hazards, compile annual disaster prevention and response plans, and revise them in a timely manner based on specific circumstances.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　煤矿开采有下列情形之一的，应当编制专项设计：  
（一）有煤（岩）与瓦斯（二氧化碳）突出的；  
（二）有冲击地压危险的；  
（三）开采需要保护的建筑物、水体、铁路下压煤或者主要井巷留设煤柱的；  
（四）水文地质类型复杂、极复杂或者周边有老窑采空区的；  
（五）开采容易自燃和自燃煤层的；  
（六）其他需要编制专项设计的。

**Article 33** Where any of the following circumstances exists in coal mining, a special design shall be compiled:  
(1) there is coal (rock) and gas (carbon dioxide) outburst;  
(2) there is a risk of rock burst;  
(3) mining coal under buildings, water bodies, or railways that need protection, or reserving coal pillars for main roadways;  
(4) the hydrogeological type is complex or extremely complex, or there are mined-out areas of old kilns nearby;  
(5) mining coal seams that are prone to spontaneous combustion and liable to spontaneous combustion;  
(6) other cases where a special design is required.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　在煤矿进行石门揭煤、探放水、巷道贯通、清理煤仓、强制放顶、火区密闭和启封、动火以及国家矿山安全监察机构规定的其他危险作业，应当采取专门安全技术措施，并安排专门人员进行现场安全管理。

**Article 34** When carrying out dangerous operations in a coal mine such as crosscut coal uncovering, water exploration and drainage, roadway connection, coal bunker cleaning, forced roof caving, fire area sealing and unsealing, hot work, and other dangerous operations specified by the National Mine Safety Administration, special safety technical measures shall be taken, and dedicated personnel shall be arranged for on-site safety management.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　煤矿企业应当建立安全风险分级管控制度，开展安全风险辨识评估，按照安全风险分级采取相应的管控措施。  
煤矿企业应当建立健全事故隐患排查治理制度，采取技术、管理措施，及时发现并消除事故隐患。事故隐患排查治理情况应当如实记录，并定期向从业人员通报。重大事故隐患排查治理情况的书面报告经煤矿企业负责人签字后，每季度报县级以上地方人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门和所在地矿山安全监察机构。  
煤矿企业应当加强对所属煤矿的安全管理，定期对所属煤矿进行安全检查。

**Article 35** A coal mining enterprise shall establish a graded safety risk control system, conduct safety risk identification and assessment, and adopt corresponding control measures according to the safety risk grades.  
A coal mining enterprise shall establish and improve an accident hazard investigation and control system, and adopt technical and management measures to promptly detect and eliminate accident hazards. The accident hazard investigation and control status shall be truthfully recorded and regularly notified to employees. A written report on the investigation and control of major accident hazards, after being signed by the person in charge of the coal mining enterprise, shall be submitted quarterly to the relevant department of the local people's government at or above the county level that is responsible for the supervision and management of coal mine work safety and to the local mine safety administration.  
A coal mining enterprise shall strengthen the safety management of its subordinate coal mines and conduct regular safety inspections of its subordinate coal mines.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　煤矿企业有下列情形之一的，属于重大事故隐患，应当立即停止受影响区域生产、建设，并及时消除事故隐患：  
（一）超能力、超强度或者超定员组织生产的；  
（二）瓦斯超限作业的；  
（三）煤（岩）与瓦斯（二氧化碳）突出矿井未按照规定实施防突措施的；  
（四）煤（岩）与瓦斯（二氧化碳）突出矿井、高瓦斯矿井未按照规定建立瓦斯抽采系统，或者系统不能正常运行的；  
（五）通风系统不完善、不可靠的；  
（六）超层、越界开采的；  
（七）有严重水患，未采取有效措施的；  
（八）有冲击地压危险，未采取有效措施的；  
（九）自然发火严重，未采取有效措施的；  
（十）使用应当淘汰的危及生产安全的设备、工艺的；  
（十一）未按照规定建立监控与通讯系统，或者系统不能正常运行的；  
（十二）露天煤矿边坡角大于设计最大值或者边坡发生严重变形，未采取有效措施的；  
（十三）未按照规定采用双回路供电系统的；  
（十四）新建煤矿边建设边生产，煤矿改扩建期间，在改扩建的区域生产，或者在其他区域的生产超出设计规定的范围和规模的；  
（十五）实行整体承包生产经营后，未重新取得或者及时变更安全生产许可证而从事生产，或者承包方再次转包，以及将井下采掘工作面和井巷维修作业外包的；  
（十六）改制、合并、分立期间，未明确安全生产责任人和安全生产管理机构，或者在完成改制、合并、分立后，未重新取得或者及时变更安全生产许可证等的；  
（十七）有其他重大事故隐患的。

**Article 36** Where a coal mining enterprise has any of the following circumstances, it constitutes a major accident hazard, and production and construction in the affected area shall be immediately stopped, and the accident hazard shall be eliminated in a timely manner:  
(1) organizing production beyond its capacity, intensity, or personnel quota;  
(2) performing operations with gas exceeding the limit;  
(3) a coal (rock) and gas (carbon dioxide) outburst mine fails to implement outburst prevention measures as required;  
(4) a coal (rock) and gas (carbon dioxide) outburst mine or a high-gas mine fails to establish a gas extraction system as required, or the system fails to operate normally;  
(5) the ventilation system is imperfect or unreliable;  
(6) mining beyond the designated level or boundary;  
(7) there is a serious water hazard and no effective measures are taken;  
(8) there is a risk of rock burst and no effective measures are taken;  
(9) spontaneous combustion is serious and no effective measures are taken;  
(10) using equipment or processes that shall be eliminated and endanger production safety;  
(11) failing to establish a monitoring and communication system as required, or the system fails to operate normally;  
(12) the slope angle of an open-pit coal mine exceeds the design maximum value or the slope has severe deformation, and no effective measures are taken;  
(13) failing to adopt a dual-circuit power supply system as required;  
(14) a new coal mine produces while being constructed; during the expansion or renovation of a coal mine, production is carried out in the expanded or renovated area, or production in other areas exceeds the scope and scale specified in the design;  
(15) after implementing an overall contract for production and operation, production is carried out without re-obtaining or timely updating the work safety permit, or the contractor subcontracts again, or the underground mining face and roadway maintenance operations are outsourced;  
(16) during restructuring, merger, or division, the work safety responsible person and work safety management institution are not specified, or after completing restructuring, merger, or division, the work safety permit is not re-obtained or timely updated, etc.;  
(17) there are other major accident hazards.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　煤矿企业及其有关人员对县级以上人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门、国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构依法履行职责，应当予以配合，按照要求如实提供有关情况，不得隐瞒或者拒绝、阻挠。  
对县级以上人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门、国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构查处的事故隐患，煤矿企业应当立即进行整改，并按照要求报告整改结果。

**Article 37** A coal mining enterprise and its relevant personnel shall cooperate with the department responsible for the supervision and management of coal mine work safety of the local people's government at or above the county level, the National Mine Safety Administration, and its local mine safety administration agencies in lawfully performing their duties, and shall truthfully provide relevant information as required, and shall not conceal, refuse, or obstruct.  
A coal mining enterprise shall immediately rectify the accident hazards investigated and dealt with by the department responsible for the supervision and management of coal mine work safety of the local people's government at or above the county level, the National Mine Safety Administration, and its local mine safety administration agencies, and shall report the rectification results as required.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　煤矿企业应当及时足额安排安全生产费用等资金，确保符合安全生产要求。煤矿企业的决策机构、主要负责人对由于安全生产所必需的资金投入不足导致的后果承担责任。

**Article 38** A coal mining enterprise shall allocate sufficient funds such as work safety expenses in a timely manner to ensure compliance with work safety requirements. The decision-making body and the principal responsible person of a coal mining enterprise shall bear responsibility for the consequences caused by insufficient investment in funds necessary for work safety.

### 第三章　煤矿安全生产监督管理

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　煤矿安全生产实行地方党政领导干部安全生产责任制，强化煤矿安全生产属地管理。

**Article 39** A work safety responsibility system for local Party and government leading cadres shall be implemented for coal mine work safety, and territorial management of coal mine work safety shall be strengthened.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当按照分级分类监管的原则，明确煤矿企业的安全生产监管主体。  
县级以上人民政府相关主管部门对未依法取得安全生产许可证等擅自进行煤矿生产的，应当依法查处。  
乡镇人民政府在所辖区域内发现未依法取得安全生产许可证等擅自进行煤矿生产的，应当采取有效措施制止，并向县级人民政府相关主管部门报告。

**Article 40** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the principle of hierarchical and classified supervision, specify the work safety supervision entities for coal mining enterprises.  
The relevant competent departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall investigate and punish, according to law, those who have not lawfully obtained a work safety permit or the like and engage in coal mine production without authorization.  
Where a township people's government discovers, within its jurisdiction, any coal mine production without authorization without lawfully obtaining a work safety permit or the like, it shall take effective measures to stop it and report to the relevant competent department of the people's government at the county level.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门审查煤矿建设项目安全设施设计，应当自受理之日起30日内审查完毕，签署同意或者不同意的意见，并书面答复。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当加强对建设单位安全设施验收活动和验收结果的监督核查。

**Article 41** The department responsible for coal mine safety production supervision and administration of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 30 days from the date of acceptance, complete the review of the safety facility design of a coal mine construction project, sign an opinion of approval or disapproval, and provide a written reply.  
The department responsible for coal mine safety production supervision and administration of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall strengthen the supervision and verification of the safety facility acceptance activities and acceptance results of the construction unit.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门负责煤矿企业安全生产许可证的颁发和管理，并接受国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构的监督。

**Article 42** The department responsible for coal mine safety production supervision and administration of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the issuance and management of the coal mine safety production permit, and shall accept the supervision of the National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　县级以上地方人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当编制煤矿安全生产年度监督检查计划，并按照计划进行监督检查。  
煤矿安全生产年度监督检查计划应当抄送所在地矿山安全监察机构。

**Article 43** The department responsible for coal mine safety production supervision and administration of the local people's government at or above the county level shall prepare an annual supervision and inspection plan for coal mine safety production, and shall conduct supervision and inspection in accordance with the plan.  
The annual supervision and inspection plan for coal mine safety production shall be copied to the local mine safety supervision agency.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　县级以上地方人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门依法对煤矿企业进行监督检查，并将煤矿现场安全生产状况作为监督检查重点内容。监督检查可以采取以下措施：  
（一）进入煤矿企业进行检查，重点检查一线生产作业场所，调阅有关资料，向有关单位和人员了解情况；  
（二）对检查中发现的安全生产违法行为，当场予以纠正或者要求限期改正；  
（三）对检查中发现的事故隐患，应当责令立即排除；重大事故隐患排除前或者排除过程中无法保证安全的，应当责令从危险区域内撤出作业人员，责令暂时停产或者停止使用相关设施、设备；  
（四）对有根据认为不符合保障安全生产的国家标准或者行业标准的设施、设备、器材予以查封或者扣押。  
监督检查不得影响煤矿企业的正常生产经营活动。

**Article 44** The department responsible for coal mine safety production supervision and administration of the local people's government at or above the county level shall supervise and inspect coal mine enterprises according to law, and shall take the on-site safety production status of the coal mine as the key content of supervision and inspection. The supervision and inspection may adopt the following measures:  
(1) Enter the coal mine enterprise for inspection, focusing on the front-line production operation sites, access relevant materials, and inquire about the situation from relevant entities and personnel;  
(2) Correct on the spot or order rectification within a time limit for the illegal acts of safety production discovered during the inspection;  
(3) Order the immediate elimination of any potential accident hazards discovered during the inspection; before the elimination of major accident hazards or if safety cannot be guaranteed during the elimination process, order the evacuation of operators from the dangerous area, order temporary suspension of production or suspension of use of related facilities and equipment;  
(4) Seal up or seize facilities, equipment, and materials that are found to be not in compliance with the national standards or industry standards for ensuring safety production.  
Supervision and inspection shall not affect the normal production and operation activities of coal mine enterprises.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上地方人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当将重大事故隐患纳入相关信息系统，建立健全重大事故隐患治理督办制度，督促煤矿企业消除重大事故隐患。

**Article 45** The department responsible for coal mine safety production supervision and administration of the local people's government at or above the county level shall incorporate major accident hazards into the relevant information system, establish and improve a supervision system for the governance of major accident hazards, and urge coal mine enterprises to eliminate major accident hazards.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上地方人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当加强对煤矿安全生产技术服务机构的监管。  
承担安全评价、认证、检测、检验等职责的煤矿安全生产技术服务机构应当依照有关法律法规和国家标准或者行业标准的规定开展安全生产技术服务活动，并对出具的报告负责，不得租借资质、挂靠、出具虚假报告。

**Article 46** The department responsible for coal mine safety production supervision and administration of the local people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen the supervision of coal mine safety production technical service institutions.  
Coal mine safety production technical service institutions that undertake duties such as safety assessment, certification, testing, and inspection shall carry out safety production technical service activities in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations and national standards or industry standards, and shall be responsible for the reports they issue. They shall not lend or lease qualifications, engage in affiliated operations, or issue false reports.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门对存在安全生产失信行为的煤矿企业、煤矿安全生产技术服务机构及有关从业人员，依法依规实施失信惩戒。

**Article 47** The people's government at or above the county level and its relevant departments shall, in accordance with laws and regulations, impose disciplinary punishments for dishonesty on coal mine enterprises, coal mine safety production technical service institutions, and relevant practitioners that have engaged in dishonest acts concerning safety production.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　对被责令停产整顿的煤矿企业，在停产整顿期间，有关地方人民政府应当采取有效措施进行监督检查。  
煤矿企业有安全生产违法行为或者重大事故隐患依法被责令停产整顿的，应当制定整改方案并进行整改。整改结束后要求恢复生产的，县级以上地方人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当组织验收，并在收到恢复生产申请之日起20日内组织验收完毕。验收合格的，经本部门主要负责人签字，并经所在地矿山安全监察机构审核同意，报本级人民政府主要负责人批准后，方可恢复生产。

**Article 48** For a coal mine enterprise that has been ordered to suspend production for rectification, during the period of suspension for rectification, the relevant local people's government shall take effective measures to conduct supervision and inspection.  
If a coal mine enterprise is ordered to suspend production for rectification according to law due to illegal acts in safety production or major accident hazards, it shall formulate a rectification plan and carry out rectification. If it requests to resume production after the rectification is completed, the department responsible for coal mine safety production supervision and administration of the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize an acceptance inspection, and shall complete the acceptance inspection within 20 days from the date of receiving the application for resumption of production. If the acceptance inspection is passed, it may resume production only after being signed by the principal responsible person of the department, reviewed and approved by the local mine safety supervision agency, and reported to the principal responsible person of the people's government at the same level for approval.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上地方人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门对被责令停产整顿或者关闭的煤矿企业，应当在5个工作日内向社会公告；对被责令停产整顿的煤矿企业经验收合格恢复生产的，应当自恢复生产之日起5个工作日内向社会公告。

**Article 49** The department responsible for coal mine safety production supervision and administration of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within 5 working days, make a public announcement to the society regarding a coal mine enterprise that has been ordered to suspend production for rectification or closed down; for a coal mine enterprise that has passed the acceptance inspection and resumed production after being ordered to suspend production for rectification, the department shall, within 5 working days from the date of resumption of production, make a public announcement to the society.

### 第四章　煤矿安全监察

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构应当依法履行煤矿安全监察职责，对县级以上地方人民政府煤矿安全生产监督管理工作加强监督检查，并及时向有关地方人民政府通报监督检查的情况，提出改善和加强煤矿安全生产工作的监察意见和建议，督促开展重大事故隐患整改和复查。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当配合和接受国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构的监督检查，及时落实监察意见和建议。

**Article 50** The National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies shall perform their coal mine safety supervision duties according to law, strengthen the supervision and inspection of the coal mine safety production supervision and administration work of the local people's governments at or above the county level, and promptly notify the relevant local people's governments of the situation of supervision and inspection, put forward supervision opinions and suggestions for improving and strengthening coal mine safety production work, and urge the implementation of rectification and review of major accident hazards.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall cooperate with and accept the supervision and inspection of the National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies, and promptly implement the supervision opinions and suggestions.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　设在地方的矿山安全监察机构应当对所辖区域内煤矿安全生产实施监察；对事故多发地区，应当实施重点监察。国家矿山安全监察机构根据实际情况，组织对全国煤矿安全生产的全面监察或者重点监察。

**Article 51** The local mine safety supervision agencies shall conduct supervision over coal mine safety production within their respective jurisdictions; for areas with frequent accidents, they shall conduct priority supervision. The National Mine Safety Supervision Administration shall, based on actual conditions, organize comprehensive or priority supervision over coal mine safety production nationwide.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构对县级以上地方人民政府煤矿安全生产监督管理工作进行监督检查，可以采取以下方式：  
（一）听取有关地方人民政府及其负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门工作汇报；  
（二）调阅、复制与煤矿安全生产有关的文件、档案、工作记录等资料；  
（三）要求有关地方人民政府及其负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门和有关人员就煤矿安全生产工作有关问题作出说明；  
（四）有必要采取的其他方式。

**Article 52** The National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies may adopt the following methods to conduct supervision and inspection over the coal mine safety production supervision and administration work of the local people's governments at or above the county level:  
(1) Hear work reports from the relevant local people's government and its departments responsible for coal mine safety production supervision and administration;  
(2) Access and copy documents, archives, work records, and other materials related to coal mine safety production;  
(3) Require the relevant local people's government and its departments responsible for coal mine safety production supervision and administration and relevant personnel to provide explanations on issues related to coal mine safety production;  
(4) Other methods deemed necessary.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构履行煤矿安全监察职责，有权进入煤矿作业场所进行检查，参加煤矿企业安全生产会议，向有关煤矿企业及人员了解情况。  
国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构发现煤矿现场存在事故隐患的，有权要求立即排除或者限期排除；发现有违章指挥、强令冒险作业、违章作业以及其他安全生产违法行为的，有权立即纠正或者要求立即停止作业；发现威胁安全的紧急情况时，有权要求立即停止危险区域内的作业并撤出作业人员。  
矿山安全监察人员履行煤矿安全监察职责，应当出示执法证件。

**Article 53** The National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies, in performing their coal mine safety supervision duties, shall have the authority to enter coal mine workplaces for inspection, attend coal mine enterprise safety production meetings, and inquire about the situation from relevant coal mine enterprises and personnel.  
If the National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies discover potential accident hazards at a coal mine site, they shall have the authority to order immediate elimination or elimination within a time limit; if they discover illegal commands, forced orders to take risks, illegal operations, or other illegal acts in safety production, they shall have the authority to immediately correct or order immediate cessation of operations; if an emergency situation threatens safety, they shall have the authority to order immediate cessation of operations in the dangerous area and evacuation of operators.  
Mine safety supervision personnel shall present their law enforcement credentials when performing coal mine safety supervision duties.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构发现煤矿企业存在重大事故隐患责令停产整顿的，应当及时移送县级以上地方人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门处理并进行督办。

**Article 54** If the National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies discover that a coal mine enterprise has major accident hazards and order it to suspend production for rectification, they shall promptly transfer the matter to the department responsible for coal mine safety production supervision and administration of the local people's government at or above the county level for handling and shall supervise the implementation.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构发现煤矿企业存在应当由其他部门处理的违法行为的，应当及时移送有关部门处理。

**Article 55** If the National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies discover that a coal mine enterprise has illegal acts that should be handled by other departments, they shall promptly transfer the matter to the relevant departments for handling.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构和县级以上人民政府有关部门应当建立信息共享、案件移送机制，加强协作配合。

**Article 56** The National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies and the relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish mechanisms for information sharing and case transfer, and strengthen coordination and cooperation.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构应当加强煤矿安全生产信息化建设，运用信息化手段提升执法水平。  
煤矿企业应当按照国家矿山安全监察机构制定的安全生产电子数据规范联网并实时上传电子数据，对上传电子数据的真实性、准确性和完整性负责。

**Article 57** The National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies shall strengthen the informatization construction of coal mine safety production, and use informatization means to improve law enforcement standards.  
Coal mine enterprises shall, in accordance with the safety production electronic data specifications formulated by the National Mine Safety Supervision Administration, connect to the network and upload electronic data in real time, and shall be responsible for the authenticity, accuracy, and completeness of the uploaded electronic data.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构依法对煤矿企业贯彻执行安全生产法律法规、煤矿安全规程以及保障安全生产的国家标准或者行业标准的情况进行监督检查，行使[本条例第四十四条](#art-44)规定的职权。

**Article 58** The National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies shall, according to law, supervise and inspect the implementation of safety production laws and regulations, coal mine safety regulations, and national standards or industry standards for ensuring safety production by coal mine enterprises, and exercise the functions and powers provided in Article 44 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　发生煤矿生产安全事故后，煤矿企业及其负责人应当迅速采取有效措施组织抢救，并依照《生产安全事故报告和调查处理条例》的规定立即如实向当地应急管理部门、负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门和所在地矿山安全监察机构报告。  
国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构应当根据事故等级和工作需要，派出工作组赶赴事故现场，指导配合事故发生地地方人民政府开展应急救援工作。

**Article 59** After a coal mine production safety accident occurs, the coal mine enterprise and its principal responsible person shall promptly take effective measures to organize rescue, and shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Reporting and Investigation of Production Safety Accidents, immediately and truthfully report to the local emergency management department, the department responsible for coal mine safety production supervision and administration, and the local mine safety supervision agency.  
The National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies shall, based on the accident grade and work requirements, dispatch working groups to rush to the accident scene, guide and cooperate with the local people's government where the accident occurred in carrying out emergency rescue work.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　煤矿生产安全事故按照事故等级实行分级调查处理。  
特别重大事故由国务院或者国务院授权有关部门依照《生产安全事故报告和调查处理条例》的规定组织调查处理。重大事故、较大事故、一般事故由国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构依照《生产安全事故报告和调查处理条例》的规定组织调查处理。

**Article 60** Coal mine production safety accidents shall be subject to graded investigation and handling according to the accident grade.  
Extraordinarily serious accidents shall be organized for investigation and handling by the State Council or departments authorized by the State Council in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Reporting and Investigation of Production Safety Accidents. Major accidents, relatively serious accidents, and ordinary accidents shall be organized for investigation and handling by the National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Reporting and Investigation of Production Safety Accidents.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　未依法取得安全生产许可证等擅自进行煤矿生产的，应当责令立即停止生产，没收违法所得和开采出的煤炭以及采掘设备；违法所得在10万元以上的，并处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足10万元的，并处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款。  
关闭的煤矿企业擅自恢复生产的，依照前款规定予以处罚。

**Article 61** Where a coal mine produces without having lawfully obtained a safety production license or the like, it shall be ordered to immediately cease production, and its illegal gains, the coal mined and the mining equipment shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are more than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall also be imposed.  
If a closed coal mine enterprise resumes production without authorization, it shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　煤矿企业有下列行为之一的，依照《中华人民共和国安全生产法》有关规定予以处罚：  
（一）未按照规定设置安全生产管理机构并配备安全生产管理人员的；  
（二）主要负责人和安全生产管理人员未按照规定经考核合格并持续保持相应水平和能力的；  
（三）未按照规定进行安全生产教育和培训，未按照规定如实告知有关的安全生产事项，或者未如实记录安全生产教育和培训情况的；  
（四）特种作业人员未按照规定经专门的安全作业培训并取得相应资格，上岗作业的；  
（五）进行危险作业，未采取专门安全技术措施并安排专门人员进行现场安全管理的；  
（六）未按照规定建立并落实安全风险分级管控制度和事故隐患排查治理制度的，或者重大事故隐患排查治理情况未按照规定报告的；  
（七）未按照规定制定生产安全事故应急救援预案或者未定期组织演练的。

**Article 62** Where a coal mine enterprise commits any of the following acts, it shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Work Safety:  
(1) failing to establish a work safety management organization and allocate work safety management personnel as required;  
(2) the principal responsible person and work safety management personnel failing to pass the assessment as required and continuously maintain corresponding levels and capabilities;  
(3) failing to conduct work safety education and training as required, failing to truthfully inform of relevant work safety matters as required, or failing to truthfully record work safety education and training information;  
(4) special operations personnel failing to undergo special safety operation training and obtain corresponding qualifications as required before starting work;  
(5) conducting dangerous operations without adopting special safety technical measures and arranging special personnel for on-site safety management;  
(6) failing to establish and implement a safety risk分级管控 system and an accident hazard investigation and control system as required, or failing to report the investigation and control of major accident hazards as required;  
(7) failing to formulate emergency rescue plans for work safety accidents as required or failing to organize drills periodically.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　煤矿企业有下列行为之一的，责令限期改正，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，责令停产整顿，并处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处3万元以上5万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未按照规定制定并落实全员安全生产责任制和领导带班等安全生产规章制度的；  
（二）未按照规定为煤矿配备矿长等人员和机构，或者未按照规定设立救护队的；  
（三）煤矿的主要生产系统、安全设施不符合煤矿安全规程和国家标准或者行业标准规定的；  
（四）未按照规定编制专项设计的；  
（五）井工煤矿未按照规定进行瓦斯等级、冲击地压、煤层自燃倾向性和煤尘爆炸性鉴定的；  
（六）露天煤矿的采场及排土场边坡与重要建筑物、构筑物之间安全距离不符合规定的，或者未按照规定保持露天煤矿边坡稳定的；  
（七）违章指挥或者强令冒险作业、违反规程的。

**Article 63** Where a coal mine enterprise commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit and shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend production for rectification and shall be fined not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan:  
(1) failing to formulate and implement a system of responsibility for work safety for all employees and safety production rules such as the leadership shift system as required;  
(2) failing to allocate personnel such as the mine director and organizations for the coal mine as required, or failing to establish a rescue team as required;  
(3) the main production systems and safety facilities of the coal mine not conforming to the provisions of the Coal Mine Safety Regulations and national standards or industry standards;  
(4) failing to prepare special designs as required;  
(5) for underground coal mines, failing to conduct expert evaluations of gas grade, rock burst, coal seam spontaneous combustion tendency, and coal dust explosiveness as required;  
(6) for open-pit coal mines, the safety distance between the slopes of the mining site and waste dump and important buildings or structures not conforming to the provisions, or failing to maintain the stability of the open-pit coal mine slopes as required;  
(7) giving illegal commands or coercing risky operations, or violating the regulations.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　对存在重大事故隐患仍然进行生产的煤矿企业，责令停产整顿，明确整顿的内容、时间等具体要求，并处50万元以上200万元以下的罚款；对煤矿企业主要负责人处3万元以上15万元以下的罚款。

**Article 64** Where a coal mine enterprise that still engages in production despite the existence of major accident hazards, it shall be ordered to suspend production for rectification, with specific requirements such as the content and time of rectification clarified, and shall be fined not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan; the principal responsible person of the coal mine enterprise shall be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 150,000 yuan.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　煤矿企业超越依法确定的开采范围采矿的，依照有关法律法规的规定予以处理。  
擅自开采保安煤柱或者采用可能危及相邻煤矿生产安全的决水、爆破、贯通巷道等危险方法进行采矿作业的，责令立即停止作业，没收违法所得；违法所得在10万元以上的，并处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足10万元的，并处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 65** Where a coal mine enterprise mines beyond the lawfully determined mining scope, it shall be dealt with in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.  
Where it mines safety coal pillars without authorization or adopts dangerous methods such as flooding, blasting, or connecting roadways that may endanger the production safety of adjacent coal mines, it shall be ordered to immediately cease operations, and its illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are more than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　煤矿企业有下列行为之一的，责令改正；拒不改正的，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款；对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处1万元以上2万元以下的罚款：  
（一）违反[本条例第三十七条](#art-37)第一款规定，隐瞒存在的事故隐患以及其他安全问题的；  
（二）违反[本条例第四十四条](#art-44)第一款规定，擅自启封或者使用被查封、扣押的设施、设备、器材的；  
（三）有其他拒绝、阻碍监督检查行为的。

**Article 66** Where a coal mine enterprise commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan:  
(1) violating the provisions of Article 37, Paragraph 1 of these Regulations by concealing existing accident hazards and other safety problems;  
(2) violating the provisions of Article 44, Paragraph 1 of these Regulations by unsealing or using sealed or seized facilities, equipment, or devices without authorization;  
(3) committing other acts of refusing or obstructing supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　发生煤矿生产安全事故，对负有责任的煤矿企业除要求其依法承担相应的赔偿等责任外，依照下列规定处以罚款：  
（一）发生一般事故的，处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款；  
（二）发生较大事故的，处150万元以上200万元以下的罚款；  
（三）发生重大事故的，处500万元以上1000万元以下的罚款；  
（四）发生特别重大事故的，处1000万元以上2000万元以下的罚款。  
发生煤矿生产安全事故，情节特别严重、影响特别恶劣的，可以按照前款罚款数额的2倍以上5倍以下对负有责任的煤矿企业处以罚款。

**Article 67** Where a coal mine production safety accident occurs, in addition to requiring the responsible coal mine enterprise to bear corresponding compensation and other liabilities according to law, a fine shall be imposed in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) for a general accident, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan;  
(2) for a relatively serious accident, a fine of not less than 1,500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan;  
(3) for a serious accident, a fine of not less than 5,000,000 yuan but not more than 10,000,000 yuan;  
(4) for a particularly serious accident, a fine of not less than 10,000,000 yuan but not more than 20,000,000 yuan.  
Where a coal mine production safety accident occurs with particularly serious circumstances and particularly adverse impact, the responsible coal mine enterprise may be fined not less than two times but not more than five times the amount of the fine specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　煤矿企业的决策机构、主要负责人、其他负责人和安全生产管理人员未依法履行安全生产管理职责的，依照《中华人民共和国安全生产法》有关规定处罚并承担相应责任。  
煤矿企业主要负责人未依法履行安全生产管理职责，导致发生煤矿生产安全事故的，依照下列规定处以罚款：  
（一）发生一般事故的，处上一年年收入40%的罚款；  
（二）发生较大事故的，处上一年年收入60%的罚款；  
（三）发生重大事故的，处上一年年收入80%的罚款；  
（四）发生特别重大事故的，处上一年年收入100%的罚款。

**Article 68** Where the decision-making body, principal responsible person, other responsible persons, and work safety management personnel of a coal mine enterprise fail to perform work safety management duties according to law, they shall be punished and bear corresponding liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Work Safety.  
Where the principal responsible person of a coal mine enterprise fails to perform work safety management duties according to law, resulting in a coal mine production safety accident, a fine shall be imposed in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) for a general accident, a fine of 40% of the previous year's annual income;  
(2) for a relatively serious accident, a fine of 60% of the previous year's annual income;  
(3) for a serious accident, a fine of 80% of the previous year's annual income;  
(4) for a particularly serious accident, a fine of 100% of the previous year's annual income.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　煤矿企业及其有关人员有瞒报、谎报事故等行为的，依照《中华人民共和国安全生产法》、《生产安全事故报告和调查处理条例》有关规定予以处罚。  
有关地方人民政府及其应急管理部门、负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门和设在地方的矿山安全监察机构有瞒报、谎报事故等行为的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 69** Where a coal mine enterprise and its relevant personnel commit acts such as concealing or falsely reporting accidents, they shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Work Safety and the Regulations on the Reporting, Investigation and Handling of Production Safety Accidents.  
Where the relevant local people's government and its emergency management department, the department responsible for coal mine work safety supervision and administration, and the local mine safety supervision agency commit acts such as concealing or falsely reporting accidents, the responsible leading personnel and directly liable personnel shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　煤矿企业存在下列情形之一的，应当提请县级以上地方人民政府予以关闭：  
（一）未依法取得安全生产许可证等擅自进行生产的；  
（二）3个月内2次或者2次以上发现有重大事故隐患仍然进行生产的；  
（三）经地方人民政府组织的专家论证在现有技术条件下难以有效防治重大灾害的；  
（四）有《中华人民共和国安全生产法》规定的应当提请关闭的其他情形。  
有关地方人民政府作出予以关闭的决定，应当立即组织实施。关闭煤矿应当达到下列要求：  
（一）依照法律法规有关规定吊销、注销相关证照；  
（二）停止供应并妥善处理民用爆炸物品；  
（三）停止供电，拆除矿井生产设备、供电、通信线路；  
（四）封闭、填实矿井井筒，平整井口场地，恢复地貌；  
（五）妥善处理劳动关系，依法依规支付经济补偿、工伤保险待遇，组织离岗时职业健康检查，偿还拖欠工资，补缴欠缴的社会保险费；  
（六）设立标识牌；  
（七）报送、移交相关报告、图纸和资料等；  
（八）有关法律法规规定的其他要求。

**Article 70** Where a coal mine enterprise has any of the following circumstances, the local people's government at or above the county level shall be requested to close it:  
(1) producing without having lawfully obtained a safety production license or the like;  
(2) within three months, twice or more times found to still engage in production despite having major accident hazards;  
(3) through expert demonstration organized by the local people's government, it is difficult to effectively prevent and control major disasters under existing technical conditions;  
(4) having other circumstances specified in the Law of the People's Republic of China on Work Safety that warrant a request for closure.  
The relevant local people's government shall organize the implementation immediately after making the decision to close. The closure of a coal mine shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) revoking or canceling relevant licenses and certificates in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;  
(2) ceasing the supply and properly handling civil explosives;  
(3) ceasing power supply, and dismantling the mine's production equipment, power supply and communication lines;  
(4) sealing and filling the mine shafts, leveling the shaft site, and restoring the landform;  
(5) properly handling labor relations, paying economic compensation and work-related injury insurance benefits according to laws and regulations, organizing occupational health examinations upon departure, repaying overdue wages, and making up for overdue social insurance premiums;  
(6) setting up identification signs;  
(7) submitting and handing over relevant reports, drawings, and materials;  
(8) other requirements specified in relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　有下列情形之一的，依照《中华人民共和国安全生产法》有关规定予以处罚：  
（一）煤矿建设项目没有安全设施设计或者安全设施设计未按照规定报经有关部门审查同意的；  
（二）煤矿建设项目的施工单位未按照批准的安全设施设计施工的；  
（三）煤矿建设项目竣工投入生产或者使用前，安全设施未经验收合格的；  
（四）煤矿企业违反[本条例第二十四条](#art-24)第一款、第二十五条第一款和第二款、第二十六条第二款规定的。

**Article 71** Where any of the following circumstances exists, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Work Safety:  
(1) a coal mine construction project has no safety facility design, or the safety facility design has not been submitted to the relevant department for examination and approval as required;  
(2) the construction unit of a coal mine construction project fails to construct in accordance with the approved safety facility design;  
(3) before a coal mine construction project is completed and put into production or use, the safety facilities have not passed the acceptance inspection;  
(4) a coal mine enterprise violates the provisions of Article 24, Paragraph 1, Article 25, Paragraphs 1 and 2, and Article 26, Paragraph 2 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　承担安全评价、认证、检测、检验等职责的煤矿安全生产技术服务机构有出具失实报告、租借资质、挂靠、出具虚假报告等情形的，对该机构及直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，应当依照《中华人民共和国安全生产法》有关规定予以处罚并追究相应责任。其主要负责人对重大、特别重大煤矿生产安全事故负有责任的，终身不得从事煤矿安全生产相关技术服务工作。

**Article 72** Where a coal mine work safety technical service institution undertaking duties such as safety evaluation, certification, testing, inspection, etc., has circumstances such as issuing untrue reports, renting or lending qualifications, attaching itself to other entities, issuing false reports, etc., the institution and the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be punished and held correspondingly liable in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Work Safety. If its principal responsible person bears responsibility for a serious or particularly serious coal mine production safety accident, he or she shall be prohibited from engaging in coal mine work safety related technical services for life.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　本条例规定的行政处罚，由县级以上人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门和其他有关部门、国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构按照职责分工决定，对同一违法行为不得给予两次以上罚款的行政处罚。对被责令停产整顿的煤矿企业，应当暂扣安全生产许可证等。对违反本条例规定的严重违法行为，应当依法从重处罚。

**Article 73** The administrative penalties prescribed in these Regulations shall be decided by the departments responsible for coal mine work safety supervision and administration of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments, the National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies according to their respective duties, and no administrative penalty of a fine shall be imposed twice or more for the same illegal act. For a coal mine enterprise that has been ordered to suspend production for rectification, its safety production license and the like shall be temporarily seized. For serious illegal acts violating the provisions of these Regulations, severe punishment shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　地方各级人民政府、县级以上人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门和其他有关部门、国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构有下列情形之一的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）县级以上人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门、国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构不依法履行职责，不及时查处所辖区域内重大事故隐患和安全生产违法行为的；县级以上人民政府其他有关部门未依法履行煤矿安全生产相关职责的；  
（二）乡镇人民政府在所辖区域内发现未依法取得安全生产许可证等擅自进行煤矿生产，没有采取有效措施制止或者没有向县级人民政府相关主管部门报告的；  
（三）对被责令停产整顿的煤矿企业，在停产整顿期间，因有关地方人民政府监督检查不力，煤矿企业在停产整顿期间继续生产的；  
（四）关闭煤矿未达到[本条例第七十条](#art-70)第二款规定要求的；  
（五）县级以上人民政府负有煤矿安全生产监督管理职责的部门、国家矿山安全监察机构及其设在地方的矿山安全监察机构接到举报后，不及时处理的；  
（六）县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门要求不具备安全生产条件的煤矿企业进行生产的；  
（七）有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊情形的。

**Article 74** Where local people's governments at all levels, the departments responsible for coal mine work safety supervision and administration of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments, the National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies have any of the following circumstances, the responsible leading personnel and directly liable personnel shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) the departments responsible for coal mine work safety supervision and administration of the people's governments at or above the county level, the National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies fail to perform their duties according to law and fail to timely investigate and deal with major accident hazards and work safety illegal acts within their jurisdiction; other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level fail to perform their duties related to coal mine work safety according to law;  
(2) the township people's government discovers within its jurisdiction that coal mine production is carried out without having lawfully obtained a safety production license or the like, but fails to take effective measures to stop it or fails to report to the relevant competent department of the people's government at the county level;  
(3) for a coal mine enterprise ordered to suspend production for rectification, during the period of suspension for rectification, due to ineffective supervision and inspection by the relevant local people's government, the coal mine enterprise continues production during the suspension period;  
(4) the closed coal mine fails to meet the requirements specified in Article 70, Paragraph 2 of these Regulations;  
(5) after receiving a report, the departments responsible for coal mine work safety supervision and administration of the people's governments at or above the county level, the National Mine Safety Supervision Administration and its local mine safety supervision agencies fail to handle it in a timely manner;  
(6) the local people's government at or above the county level and its relevant departments require a coal mine enterprise that does not meet the conditions for safe production to engage in production;  
(7) having other circumstances of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 75** Whoever violates the provisions of these Regulations and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　本条例自2024年5月1日起施行。《煤矿安全监察条例》和《国务院关于预防煤矿生产安全事故的特别规定》同时废止。

**Article 76** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2024. The Coal Mine Safety Supervision Regulations and the Special Provisions of the State Council on Preventing Coal Mine Production Safety Accidents shall be abolished simultaneously.
