# 中华人民共和国文物保护法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2017-10-07  
**生效日期**：2003-07-01

> (2003年5月18日中华人民共和国国务院令第377号公布　根据2013年12月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　根据2017年3月1日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订　根据2017年10月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第四次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国文物保护法》(以下简称文物保护法)，制定本实施条例。 第二条　国家重点文物保护专项补助经费和地方文物保护专项经费，由县级以上人民政府文物行政主管部门、投资主管部门、财政部门按照国家有关规定共同实施管理。任何单位或者个人不得侵占、挪用。 第三条　国有的博物馆、纪念馆、文物保护单位等的事业性收入，应当用于下列用途： (一)文物的保管、陈列、修复、征集； (二)国有的博物馆、纪念馆、文物保护单位的修缮和建设； (三)文物的安全防范； (四)考古调查、勘探、发掘； (五)文物保护的科学研究、宣传教育。 第四条　文物行政主管部门和教育、科技、新闻出版、广播电视行政主管部门，应当做好文物保护的宣传教育工作。 第五条　国务院文物行政主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门，应当制定文物保护的科学技术研究规划，采取有效措施，促进文物保护科技成果的推广和应用，提高文物保护的科学技术水平。 第六条　有文物保护法第十二条所列事迹之一的单位或者个人，由人民政府及其文物行政主管部门、有关部门给予精神鼓励或者物质奖励。 第二章　不可移动文物 第七条　历史文化名城，由国务院建设行政主管部门会同国务院文物行政主管部门报国务院核定公布。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国文物保护法》(以下简称文物保护法)，制定本实施条例。

**Article 1** These Implementing Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Cultural Relics (hereinafter referred to as the Cultural Relics Protection Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家重点文物保护专项补助经费和地方文物保护专项经费，由县级以上人民政府文物行政主管部门、投资主管部门、财政部门按照国家有关规定共同实施管理。任何单位或者个人不得侵占、挪用。

**Article 2** The special subsidy funds for national key cultural relics protection and the special funds for local cultural relics protection shall be jointly managed by the administrative department of cultural relics, the investment administrative department, and the financial department of the people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. No unit or individual may encroach upon or misappropriate them.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国有的博物馆、纪念馆、文物保护单位等的事业性收入，应当用于下列用途：  
(一)文物的保管、陈列、修复、征集；  
(二)国有的博物馆、纪念馆、文物保护单位的修缮和建设；  
(三)文物的安全防范；  
(四)考古调查、勘探、发掘；  
(五)文物保护的科学研究、宣传教育。

**Article 3** The operating revenues of state-owned museums, memorial halls, and cultural relics protection units shall be used for the following purposes:  
(1) storage, display, restoration, and collection of cultural relics;  
(2) repair and construction of state-owned museums, memorial halls, and cultural relics protection units;  
(3) security and prevention of cultural relics;  
(4) archaeological investigation, prospecting, and excavation;  
(5) scientific research and publicity and education on cultural relics protection.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　文物行政主管部门和教育、科技、新闻出版、广播电视行政主管部门，应当做好文物保护的宣传教育工作。

**Article 4** The administrative department of cultural relics and the administrative departments of education, science and technology, press and publication, and radio and television shall properly conduct the publicity and education work on cultural relics protection.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院文物行政主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门，应当制定文物保护的科学技术研究规划，采取有效措施，促进文物保护科技成果的推广和应用，提高文物保护的科学技术水平。

**Article 5** The administrative department of cultural relics of the State Council and the administrative departments of cultural relics of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate plans for scientific and technological research on cultural relics protection, adopt effective measures to promote the dissemination and application of scientific and technological achievements in cultural relics protection, and enhance the level of science and technology in cultural relics protection.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　有[文物保护法第十二条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国文物保护法.md#art-12)所列事迹之一的单位或者个人，由人民政府及其文物行政主管部门、有关部门给予精神鼓励或者物质奖励。

**Article 6** Units or individuals that have performed one of the deeds listed in Article 12 of the Cultural Relics Protection Law shall be given spiritual encouragement or material rewards by the people's government and its administrative department of cultural relics and relevant departments.

### 第二章　不可移动文物

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　历史文化名城，由国务院建设行政主管部门会同国务院文物行政主管部门报国务院核定公布。  
历史文化街区、村镇，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府城乡规划行政主管部门会同文物行政主管部门报本级人民政府核定公布。  
县级以上地方人民政府组织编制的历史文化名城和历史文化街区、村镇的保护规划，应当符合文物保护的要求。

**Article 7** A famous historical and cultural city shall be submitted by the administrative department of construction of the State Council jointly with the administrative department of cultural relics of the State Council to the State Council for verification and publication.  
A historical and cultural block or town/village shall be submitted by the administrative department of urban and rural planning of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the administrative department of cultural relics to the people's government at the same level for verification and publication.  
The protection plans for famous historical and cultural cities and historical and cultural blocks and towns/villages organized and compiled by the local people's government at or above the county level shall meet the requirements of cultural relics protection.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　全国重点文物保护单位和省级文物保护单位自核定公布之日起1年内，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府划定必要的保护范围，作出标志说明，建立记录档案，设置专门机构或者指定专人负责管理。  
设区的市、自治州级和县级文物保护单位自核定公布之日起1年内，由核定公布该文物保护单位的人民政府划定保护范围，作出标志说明，建立记录档案，设置专门机构或者指定专人负责管理。

**Article 8** For a national key cultural relic protection unit or a provincial cultural relic protection unit, within one year from the date of verification and publication, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall delimit the necessary protection area, make signs and explanations, establish record files, and set up a specialized agency or designate a specific person to be responsible for management.  
For a cultural relic protection unit at the level of a city divided into districts, autonomous prefecture, or county, within one year from the date of verification and publication, the people's government that verified and published the cultural relic protection unit shall delimit the protection area, make signs and explanations, establish record files, and set up a specialized agency or designate a specific person to be responsible for management.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　文物保护单位的保护范围，是指对文物保护单位本体及周围一定范围实施重点保护的区域。  
文物保护单位的保护范围，应当根据文物保护单位的类别、规模、内容以及周围环境的历史和现实情况合理划定，并在文物保护单位本体之外保持一定的安全距离，确保文物保护单位的真实性和完整性。

**Article 9** The protection area of a cultural relic protection unit refers to the area where key protection is implemented for the cultural relic protection unit itself and a certain surrounding area.  
The protection area of a cultural relic protection unit shall be reasonably delimited based on the category, scale, content, and the historical and current conditions of the surrounding environment of the cultural relic protection unit, and a certain safety distance shall be maintained outside the cultural relic protection unit itself to ensure the authenticity and integrity of the cultural relic protection unit.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　文物保护单位的标志说明，应当包括文物保护单位的级别、名称、公布机关、公布日期、立标机关、立标日期等内容。民族自治地区的文物保护单位的标志说明，应当同时用规范汉字和当地通用的少数民族文字书写。

**Article 10** The signs and explanations of a cultural relic protection unit shall include the level, name, publication authority, publication date, sign-setting authority, and sign-setting date of the cultural relic protection unit. The signs and explanations of a cultural relic protection unit in an ethnic autonomous area shall be written in both standard Chinese characters and the local commonly used minority language.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　文物保护单位的记录档案，应当包括文物保护单位本体记录等科学技术资料和有关文献记载、行政管理等内容。  
文物保护单位的记录档案，应当充分利用文字、音像制品、图画、拓片、摹本、电子文本等形式，有效表现其所载内容。

**Article 11** The archives of a protected cultural heritage site shall include scientific and technological materials such as records of the site’s physical features, as well as relevant documentary records and administrative management.  
The archives of a protected cultural heritage site shall make full use of forms such as text, audio and video products, drawings, rubbings, facsimiles, and electronic texts to effectively present the content contained therein.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　古文化遗址、古墓葬、石窟寺和属于国家所有的纪念建筑物、古建筑，被核定公布为文物保护单位的，由县级以上地方人民政府设置专门机构或者指定机构负责管理。其他文物保护单位，由县级以上地方人民政府设置专门机构或者指定机构、专人负责管理；指定专人负责管理的，可以采取聘请文物保护员的形式。  
文物保护单位有使用单位的，使用单位应当设立群众性文物保护组织；没有使用单位的，文物保护单位所在地的村民委员会或者居民委员会可以设立群众性文物保护组织。文物行政主管部门应当对群众性文物保护组织的活动给予指导和支持。  
负责管理文物保护单位的机构，应当建立健全规章制度，采取安全防范措施；其安全保卫人员，可以依法配备防卫器械。

**Article 12** Where ancient cultural sites, ancient tombs, cave temples, and state-owned memorial buildings or ancient structures are verified and announced as protected cultural heritage sites, the local people’s government at or above the county level shall establish a specialized agency or designate an agency to be responsible for their management. For other protected cultural heritage sites, the local people’s government at or above the county level shall establish a specialized agency or designate an agency or a specific person to be responsible for management; where a specific person is designated, the form of employing a cultural heritage guardian may be adopted.  
If a protected cultural heritage site has a user unit, the user unit shall establish a mass organization for the protection of cultural heritage; if there is no user unit, the villagers’ committee or residents’ committee of the locality where the protected cultural heritage site is located may establish a mass organization for the protection of cultural heritage. The administrative department for cultural heritage shall provide guidance and support to the activities of such mass organizations.  
The agency responsible for managing a protected cultural heritage site shall establish and improve rules and regulations, and adopt safety precautions; its security personnel may, according to law, be equipped with defensive instruments.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　文物保护单位的建设控制地带，是指在文物保护单位的保护范围外，为保护文物保护单位的安全、环境、历史风貌对建设项目加以限制的区域。  
文物保护单位的建设控制地带，应当根据文物保护单位的类别、规模、内容以及周围环境的历史和现实情况合理划定。

**Article 13** The construction control zone of a protected cultural heritage site refers to the area outside the protection area of the site where construction projects are restricted in order to protect the safety, environment, and historical features of the site.  
The construction control zone of a protected cultural heritage site shall be reasonably delineated based on the category, scale, and content of the site, as well as the historical and current conditions of the surrounding environment.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　全国重点文物保护单位的建设控制地带，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的文物行政主管部门会同城乡规划行政主管部门划定并公布。  
省级、设区的市、自治州级和县级文物保护单位的建设控制地带，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，由核定公布该文物保护单位的人民政府的文物行政主管部门会同城乡规划行政主管部门划定并公布。

**Article 14** The construction control zone of a nationally important protected cultural heritage site shall be delineated and announced by the administrative department for cultural heritage of the people’s government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, in conjunction with the administrative department for urban and rural planning, subject to approval by the people’s government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality.  
The construction control zone of a protected cultural heritage site at the provincial, city divided into districts, autonomous prefecture, or county level shall be delineated and announced by the administrative department for cultural heritage of the people’s government that verified and announced the site, in conjunction with the administrative department for urban and rural planning, subject to approval by the people’s government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　承担文物保护单位的修缮、迁移、重建工程的单位，应当同时取得文物行政主管部门发给的相应等级的文物保护工程资质证书和建设行政主管部门发给的相应等级的资质证书。其中，不涉及建筑活动的文物保护单位的修缮、迁移、重建，应当由取得文物行政主管部门发给的相应等级的文物保护工程资质证书的单位承担。

**Article 15** A unit undertaking the repair, relocation, or reconstruction of a protected cultural heritage site shall simultaneously obtain the qualification certificate for cultural heritage protection projects of the corresponding grade issued by the administrative department for cultural heritage and the qualification certificate of the corresponding grade issued by the administrative department for construction. Among these, the repair, relocation, or reconstruction of a protected cultural heritage site that does not involve construction activities shall be undertaken by a unit that has obtained the qualification certificate for cultural heritage protection projects of the corresponding grade issued by the administrative department for cultural heritage.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　申领文物保护工程资质证书，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有取得文物博物专业技术职务的人员；  
(二)有从事文物保护工程所需的技术设备；  
(三)法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 16** To apply for a qualification certificate for cultural heritage protection projects, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) having personnel who have obtained professional titles in cultural heritage and museology;  
(2) having the technical equipment required for engaging in cultural heritage protection projects;  
(3) other conditions stipulated by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　申领文物保护工程资质证书，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门或者国务院文物行政主管部门提出申请。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门或者国务院文物行政主管部门应当自收到申请之日起30个工作日内作出批准或者不批准的决定。决定批准的，发给相应等级的文物保护工程资质证书；决定不批准的，应当书面通知当事人并说明理由。文物保护工程资质等级的分级标准和审批办法，由国务院文物行政主管部门制定。

**Article 17** To apply for a qualification certificate for cultural heritage protection projects, an application shall be submitted to the administrative department for cultural heritage of the people’s government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or to the administrative department for cultural heritage of the State Council. The said department shall, within 30 working days from the date of receiving the application, make a decision to approve or disapprove. If the decision is to approve, the corresponding grade of qualification certificate for cultural heritage protection projects shall be issued; if the decision is to disapprove, the party shall be notified in writing and the reasons shall be stated. The grading standards and examination and approval procedures for the qualification grades of cultural heritage protection projects shall be formulated by the administrative department for cultural heritage of the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　文物行政主管部门在审批文物保护单位的修缮计划和工程设计方案前，应当征求上一级人民政府文物行政主管部门的意见。

**Article 18** Before approving the repair plan and engineering design proposal for a protected cultural heritage site, the administrative department for cultural heritage shall solicit the opinions of the administrative department for cultural heritage of the people’s government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　危害全国重点文物保护单位安全或者破坏其历史风貌的建筑物、构筑物，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责调查处理。  
危害省级、设区的市、自治州级、县级文物保护单位安全或者破坏其历史风貌的建筑物、构筑物，由核定公布该文物保护单位的人民政府负责调查处理。  
危害尚未核定公布为文物保护单位的不可移动文物安全的建筑物、构筑物，由县级人民政府负责调查处理。

**Article 19** Buildings or structures that endanger the safety of a nationally important protected cultural heritage site or damage its historical features shall be investigated and handled by the people’s government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
Buildings or structures that endanger the safety of a protected cultural heritage site at the provincial, city divided into districts, autonomous prefecture, or county level or damage its historical features shall be investigated and handled by the people’s government that verified and announced the site.  
Buildings or structures that endanger the safety of immovable cultural relics that have not yet been verified and announced as protected cultural heritage sites shall be investigated and handled by the people’s government at the county level.

### 第三章　考古发掘

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　申请从事考古发掘的单位，取得考古发掘资质证书，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有4名以上接受过考古专业训练且主持过考古发掘项目的人员；  
(二)有取得文物博物专业技术职务的人员；  
(三)有从事文物安全保卫的专业人员；  
(四)有从事考古发掘所需的技术设备；  
(五)有保障文物安全的设施和场所；  
(六)法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 20** A unit applying to engage in archaeological excavations and obtain a qualification certificate for archaeological excavations shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having at least four personnel who have received professional training in archaeology and have led archaeological excavation projects;  
(2) having personnel who have obtained professional titles in cultural heritage and museology;  
(3) having professional personnel engaged in cultural heritage security;  
(4) having the technical equipment required for engaging in archaeological excavations;  
(5) having facilities and premises to ensure the safety of cultural relics;  
(6) other conditions stipulated by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　申领考古发掘资质证书，应当向国务院文物行政主管部门提出申请。国务院文物行政主管部门应当自收到申请之日起30个工作日内作出批准或者不批准的决定。决定批准的，发给考古发掘资质证书；决定不批准的，应当书面通知当事人并说明理由。

**Article 21** To apply for an archaeological excavation qualification certificate, an application shall be submitted to the competent administrative department of cultural relics under the State Council. The competent administrative department of cultural relics under the State Council shall, within 30 working days from the date of receipt of the application, make a decision of approval or disapproval. If the decision is approval, an archaeological excavation qualification certificate shall be issued; if the decision is disapproval, the party shall be notified in writing and the reasons shall be stated.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　考古发掘项目实行项目负责人负责制度。

**Article 22** The archaeological excavation project shall be subject to the project leader responsibility system.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　配合建设工程进行的考古调查、勘探、发掘，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门组织实施。跨省、自治区、直辖市的建设工程范围内的考古调查、勘探、发掘，由建设工程所在地的有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门联合组织实施；其中，特别重要的建设工程范围内的考古调查、勘探、发掘，由国务院文物行政主管部门组织实施。  
建设单位对配合建设工程进行的考古调查、勘探、发掘，应当予以协助，不得妨碍考古调查、勘探、发掘。

**Article 23** Archaeological surveys, explorations, and excavations conducted in coordination with construction projects shall be organized and implemented by the competent administrative department of cultural relics of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. For archaeological surveys, explorations, and excavations within the scope of construction projects that cross provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, they shall be jointly organized and implemented by the competent administrative departments of cultural relics of the relevant people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government where the construction projects are located; among these, for archaeological surveys, explorations, and excavations within the scope of particularly important construction projects, they shall be organized and implemented by the competent administrative department of cultural relics under the State Council.  
The construction entity shall provide assistance for the archaeological surveys, explorations, and excavations conducted in coordination with the construction project, and shall not obstruct such archaeological surveys, explorations, and excavations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国务院文物行政主管部门应当自收到[文物保护法第三十条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国文物保护法.md#art-30)第一款规定的发掘计划之日起30个工作日内作出批准或者不批准决定。决定批准的，发给批准文件；决定不批准的，应当书面通知当事人并说明理由。  
[文物保护法第三十条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国文物保护法.md#art-30)第二款规定的抢救性发掘，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门应当自开工之日起10个工作日内向国务院文物行政主管部门补办审批手续。

**Article 24** The competent administrative department of cultural relics under the State Council shall, within 30 working days from the date of receipt of the excavation plan as prescribed in Article 30, Paragraph 1 of the Law on the Protection of Cultural Relics, make a decision of approval or disapproval. If the decision is approval, an approval document shall be issued; if the decision is disapproval, the party shall be notified in writing and the reasons shall be stated.  
For rescue excavations as prescribed in Article 30, Paragraph 2 of the Law on the Protection of Cultural Relics, the competent administrative department of cultural relics of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 10 working days from the date of commencement of the project, retroactively complete the approval formalities with the competent administrative department of cultural relics under the State Council.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　考古调查、勘探、发掘所需经费的范围和标准，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 25** The scope and standards of funds required for archaeological surveys, explorations, and excavations shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　从事考古发掘的单位应当在考古发掘完成之日起30个工作日内向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门和国务院文物行政主管部门提交结项报告，并于提交结项报告之日起3年内向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门和国务院文物行政主管部门提交考古发掘报告。

**Article 26** A unit engaged in archaeological excavations shall, within 30 working days from the date of completion of the archaeological excavation, submit a project completion report to the competent administrative department of cultural relics of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and to the competent administrative department of cultural relics under the State Council, and shall, within three years from the date of submission of the project completion report, submit an archaeological excavation report to the competent administrative department of cultural relics of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and to the competent administrative department of cultural relics under the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　从事考古发掘的单位提交考古发掘报告后，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门批准，可以保留少量出土文物作为科研标本，并应当于提交发掘报告之日起6个月内将其他出土文物移交给由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门指定的国有的博物馆、图书馆或者其他国有文物收藏单位收藏。

**Article 27** After submitting the archaeological excavation report, a unit engaged in archaeological excavations may, with the approval of the competent administrative department of cultural relics of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, retain a small quantity of unearthed cultural relics as scientific research specimens, and shall, within six months from the date of submission of the excavation report, transfer the other unearthed cultural relics to state-owned museums, libraries, or other state-owned cultural relic collection units designated by the competent administrative department of cultural relics of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for collection.

### 第四章　馆藏文物

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　文物收藏单位应当建立馆藏文物的接收、鉴定、登记、编目和档案制度，库房管理制度，出入库、注销和统计制度，保养、修复和复制制度。

**Article 28** A cultural relic collection unit shall establish systems for the receipt, expert evaluation, registration, cataloging, and archiving of its collected cultural relics, a warehouse management system, systems for inbound and outbound transfer, cancellation, and statistics, and systems for maintenance, restoration, and replication.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级人民政府文物行政主管部门应当将本行政区域内的馆藏文物档案，按照行政隶属关系报设区的市、自治州级人民政府文物行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门备案；设区的市、自治州级人民政府文物行政主管部门应当将本行政区域内的馆藏文物档案，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门备案；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门应当将本行政区域内的一级文物藏品档案，报国务院文物行政主管部门备案。

**Article 29** The competent administrative department of cultural relics of the people's government at the county level shall, in accordance with the administrative subordination relationship, submit the archives of collected cultural relics within its administrative region to the competent administrative department of cultural relics of the people's government of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture, or to the competent administrative department of cultural relics of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for archival filing; the competent administrative department of cultural relics of the people's government of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture shall submit the archives of collected cultural relics within its administrative region to the competent administrative department of cultural relics of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for archival filing; the competent administrative department of cultural relics of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall submit the archives of first-grade cultural relic collections within its administrative region to the competent administrative department of cultural relics under the State Council for archival filing.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　文物收藏单位之间借用馆藏文物，借用人应当对借用的馆藏文物采取必要的保护措施，确保文物的安全。  
借用的馆藏文物的灭失、损坏风险，除当事人另有约定外，由借用该馆藏文物的文物收藏单位承担。

**Article 30** Where collected cultural relics are borrowed between cultural relic collection units, the borrower shall take necessary protective measures for the borrowed collected cultural relics to ensure the safety of the cultural relics.  
The risk of loss or damage to the borrowed collected cultural relics shall, unless otherwise agreed by the parties, be borne by the cultural relic collection unit that borrows such collected cultural relics.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国有文物收藏单位未依照[文物保护法第三十六条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国文物保护法.md#art-36)的规定建立馆藏文物档案并将馆藏文物档案报主管的文物行政主管部门备案的，不得交换、借用馆藏文物。

**Article 31** Where a state-owned museum or other collection entity fails to establish archives of its collection of cultural relics and register such archives with the competent department of cultural relics administration as required by Article 36 of the Law on the Protection of Cultural Relics, it shall not exchange or borrow cultural relics from its collection.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　修复、复制、拓印馆藏二级文物和馆藏三级文物的，应当报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门批准；修复、复制、拓印馆藏一级文物的，应当报国务院文物行政主管部门批准。

**Article 32** Restoration, reproduction, or rubbing of Grade II and Grade III cultural relics in the collection of a museum or other collection entity shall be subject to approval by the competent department of cultural relics administration of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; restoration, reproduction, or rubbing of Grade I cultural relics in such collection shall be subject to approval by the competent department of cultural relics administration under the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　从事馆藏文物修复、复制、拓印的单位，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有取得中级以上文物博物专业技术职务的人员；  
(二)有从事馆藏文物修复、复制、拓印所需的场所和技术设备；  
(三)法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 33** A unit engaged in restoration, reproduction, or rubbing of cultural relics in the collection of a museum or other collection entity shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) it shall have personnel who have obtained professional technical positions at or above the intermediate level in cultural relics and museology;  
(2) it shall have the premises and technical equipment necessary for restoration, reproduction, or rubbing of such cultural relics; and  
(3) other conditions as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　从事馆藏文物修复、复制、拓印，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门提出申请。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门应当自收到申请之日起30个工作日内作出批准或者不批准的决定。决定批准的，发给相应等级的资质证书；决定不批准的，应当书面通知当事人并说明理由。

**Article 34** To engage in restoration, reproduction, or rubbing of cultural relics in the collection of a museum or other collection entity, an application shall be submitted to the competent department of cultural relics administration of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The said competent department shall make a decision of approval or disapproval within 30 working days from the date of receipt of the application. If it decides to approve, it shall issue a qualification certificate of the corresponding grade; if it decides not to approve, it shall notify the party in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　为制作出版物、音像制品等拍摄馆藏文物的，应当征得文物收藏单位同意，并签署拍摄协议，明确文物保护措施和责任。文物收藏单位应当自拍摄工作完成后10个工作日内，将拍摄情况向文物行政主管部门报告。

**Article 35** Where a person photographs cultural relics in a collection for the purpose of producing publications, audio-visual products, or the like, the person shall obtain the consent of the collection entity and sign a filming agreement specifying the measures for protection of the cultural relics and the responsibilities. The collection entity shall, within 10 working days after the completion of the filming, report the filming to the competent department of cultural relics administration.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　馆藏文物被盗、被抢或者丢失的，文物收藏单位应当立即向公安机关报案，并同时向主管的文物行政主管部门报告；主管的文物行政主管部门应当在接到文物收藏单位的报告后24小时内，将有关情况报告国务院文物行政主管部门。

**Article 36** Where cultural relics in a collection are stolen, robbed, or lost, the collection entity shall immediately report the case to the public security organ and simultaneously report to the competent department of cultural relics administration. The competent department of cultural relics administration shall, within 24 hours after receiving the report from the collection entity, report the relevant information to the competent department of cultural relics administration under the State Council.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家机关和国有的企业、事业组织等收藏、保管国有文物的，应当履行下列义务：  
(一)建立文物藏品档案制度，并将文物藏品档案报所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门备案；  
(二)建立、健全文物藏品的保养、修复等管理制度，确保文物安全；  
(三)文物藏品被盗、被抢或者丢失的，应当立即向公安机关报案，并同时向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门报告。

**Article 37** Where state organs, state-owned enterprises, institutions, and other entities collect and keep state-owned cultural relics, they shall fulfill the following obligations:  
(1) establish an archive system for cultural relics in their collection and register the archives with the competent department of cultural relics administration of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where they are located;  
(2) establish and improve management systems for the maintenance, restoration, etc. of the cultural relics in their collection to ensure the safety of the cultural relics; and  
(3) where the cultural relics in their collection are stolen, robbed, or lost, immediately report the case to the public security organ and simultaneously report to the competent department of cultural relics administration of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where they are located.

### 第五章　民间收藏文物

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　文物收藏单位以外的公民、法人和其他组织，可以依法收藏文物，其依法收藏的文物的所有权受法律保护。  
公民、法人和其他组织依法收藏文物的，可以要求文物行政主管部门对其收藏的文物提供鉴定、修复、保管等方面的咨询。

**Article 38** Citizens, legal persons, and other organizations other than museum or collection entities may collect cultural relics according to law, and the ownership of the cultural relics they collect according to law shall be protected by law.  
Where citizens, legal persons, and other organizations collect cultural relics according to law, they may request the competent department of cultural relics administration to provide consultation on expert evaluation, restoration, storage, and other aspects of the cultural relics they collect.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　设立文物商店，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有200万元人民币以上的注册资本；  
(二)有5名以上取得中级以上文物博物专业技术职务的人员；  
(三)有保管文物的场所、设施和技术条件；  
(四)法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 39** The establishment of a cultural relic shop shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) a registered capital of not less than 2 million RMB;  
(2) at least five persons who have obtained professional technical positions at or above the intermediate level in cultural relics and museology;  
(3) the premises, facilities, and technical conditions for the storage of cultural relics; and  
(4) other conditions as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　设立文物商店，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门提出申请。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门应当自收到申请之日起30个工作日内作出批准或者不批准的决定。决定批准的，发给批准文件；决定不批准的，应当书面通知当事人并说明理由。

**Article 40** The establishment of a cultural relic shop shall be subject to an application to the competent department of cultural relics administration of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The said competent department shall, within 30 working days from the date of receipt of the application, make a decision of approval or disapproval. If it decides to approve, it shall issue an approval document; if it decides not to approve, it shall notify the party in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　依法设立的拍卖企业，从事文物拍卖经营活动的，应当有5名以上取得高级文物博物专业技术职务的文物拍卖专业人员，并取得省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门发给的文物拍卖许可证。

**Article 41** An auction enterprise established according to law that engages in the business of cultural relics auction shall have at least five cultural relics auction professionals who have obtained senior professional and technical positions in cultural relics and museology, and shall obtain a cultural relics auction permit issued by the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　依法设立的拍卖企业申领文物拍卖许可证，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门提出申请。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门应当自收到申请之日起30个工作日内作出批准或者不批准的决定。决定批准的，发给文物拍卖许可证；决定不批准的，应当书面通知当事人并说明理由。

**Article 42** An auction enterprise established according to law that applies for a cultural relics auction permit shall submit an application to the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 30 working days from the date of receipt of the application, make a decision of approval or disapproval. If it decides to approve, it shall issue a cultural relics auction permit; if it decides not to approve, it shall notify the party in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　文物商店购买、销售文物，经营文物拍卖的拍卖企业拍卖文物，应当记录文物的名称、图录、来源、文物的出卖人、委托人和买受人的姓名或者名称、住所、有效身份证件号码或者有效证照号码以及成交价格，并报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门备案。接受备案的文物行政主管部门应当依法为其保密，并将该记录保存75年。  
文物行政主管部门应当加强对文物商店和经营文物拍卖的拍卖企业的监督检查。

**Article 43** A cultural relics store that purchases or sells cultural relics, and an auction enterprise that operates cultural relics auctions and auctions cultural relics, shall record the name, catalogue, source of the cultural relics, the name or title, domicile, and valid identity document number or valid license number of the seller, consignor, and buyer of the cultural relics, as well as the transaction price, and shall report them to the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for archival filing. The administrative department for cultural relics that accepts the filing shall keep them confidential according to law and shall preserve the records for 75 years.  
The administrative department for cultural relics shall strengthen supervision and inspection over cultural relics stores and auction enterprises operating cultural relics auctions.

### 第六章　文物出境进境

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国务院文物行政主管部门指定的文物进出境审核机构，应当有5名以上取得中级以上文物博物专业技术职务的文物进出境责任鉴定人员。

**Article 44** A cultural relics entry-exit examination institution designated by the administrative department for cultural relics of the State Council shall have at least five cultural relics entry-exit responsible appraisal personnel who have obtained intermediate or above professional and technical positions in cultural relics and museology.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　运送、邮寄、携带文物出境，应当在文物出境前依法报文物进出境审核机构审核。文物进出境审核机构应当自收到申请之日起15个工作日内作出是否允许出境的决定。  
文物进出境审核机构审核文物，应当有3名以上文物博物专业技术人员参加；其中，应当有2名以上文物进出境责任鉴定人员。  
文物出境审核意见，由文物进出境责任鉴定员共同签署；对经审核，文物进出境责任鉴定员一致同意允许出境的文物，文物进出境审核机构方可作出允许出境的决定。  
文物出境审核标准，由国务院文物行政主管部门制定。

**Article 45** Where cultural relics are transported, mailed, or carried out of the country, the party shall, before the cultural relics leave the country, report to the cultural relics entry-exit examination institution for examination according to law. The cultural relics entry-exit examination institution shall, within 15 working days from the date of receipt of the application, make a decision on whether to permit the exit.  
When the cultural relics entry-exit examination institution examines cultural relics, at least three cultural relics and museology professional and technical personnel shall participate; among them, at least two shall be cultural relics entry-exit responsible appraisal personnel.  
The examination opinions on the exit of cultural relics shall be jointly signed by the cultural relics entry-exit responsible appraisers; where, after examination, the cultural relics entry-exit responsible appraisers unanimously agree to permit the exit of the cultural relics, the cultural relics entry-exit examination institution may make a decision to permit the exit.  
The standards for examination of the exit of cultural relics shall be formulated by the administrative department for cultural relics of the State Council.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　文物进出境审核机构应当对所审核进出境文物的名称、质地、尺寸、级别，当事人的姓名或者名称、住所、有效身份证件号码或者有效证照号码，以及进出境口岸、文物去向和审核日期等内容进行登记。

**Article 46** A cultural relics entry-exit examination institution shall register the name, texture, dimensions, and grade of the cultural relics examined for entry or exit, the name or title, domicile, valid identity document number or valid license number of the party, as well as the port of entry or exit, the destination of the cultural relics, the date of examination, and other such contents.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　经审核允许出境的文物，由国务院文物行政主管部门发给文物出境许可证，并由文物进出境审核机构标明文物出境标识。经审核允许出境的文物，应当从国务院文物行政主管部门指定的口岸出境。海关查验文物出境标识后，凭文物出境许可证放行。  
经审核不允许出境的文物，由文物进出境审核机构发还当事人。

**Article 47** For cultural relics that are permitted to exit after examination, the administrative department for cultural relics of the State Council shall issue a cultural relics exit permit, and the cultural relics entry-exit examination institution shall affix a cultural relics exit mark. Cultural relics that are permitted to exit after examination shall exit the country through the port designated by the administrative department for cultural relics of the State Council. Customs, after inspecting the cultural relics exit mark, shall release the cultural relics upon presentation of the cultural relics exit permit.  
Cultural relics that are not permitted to exit after examination shall be returned to the party by the cultural relics entry-exit examination institution.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　文物出境展览的承办单位，应当在举办展览前6个月向国务院文物行政主管部门提出申请。国务院文物行政主管部门应当自收到申请之日起30个工作日内作出批准或者不批准的决定。决定批准的，发给批准文件；决定不批准的，应当书面通知当事人并说明理由。  
一级文物展品超过120件(套)的，或者一级文物展品超过展品总数的20%的，应当报国务院批准。

**Article 48** The organizer of an overseas exhibition of cultural relics shall submit an application to the administrative department for cultural relics of the State Council six months before holding the exhibition. The administrative department for cultural relics of the State Council shall, within 30 working days from the date of receipt of the application, make a decision of approval or disapproval. If it decides to approve, it shall issue an approval document; if it decides not to approve, it shall notify the party in writing and state the reasons.  
Where the number of first-grade cultural relic exhibits exceeds 120 pieces (sets), or where first-grade cultural relic exhibits exceed 20% of the total number of exhibits, the matter shall be reported to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　一级文物中的孤品和易损品，禁止出境展览。禁止出境展览文物的目录，由国务院文物行政主管部门定期公布。  
未曾在国内正式展出的文物，不得出境展览。

**Article 49** Unique and fragile items among first-grade cultural relics are prohibited from being exhibited abroad. The catalogue of cultural relics prohibited from being exhibited abroad shall be published periodically by the administrative department for cultural relics of the State Council.  
Cultural relics that have not been formally exhibited in China shall not be exhibited abroad.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　文物出境展览的期限不得超过1年。因特殊需要，经原审批机关批准可以延期；但是，延期最长不得超过1年。

**Article 50** The duration of an overseas exhibition of cultural relics shall not exceed one year. For special needs, an extension may be granted with the approval of the original examination and approval authority; however, the extension shall not exceed one year at most.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　文物出境展览期间，出现可能危及展览文物安全情形的，原审批机关可以决定中止或者撤销展览。

**Article 51** During the period of exhibition of cultural relics abroad, if a situation occurs that may jeopardize the safety of the exhibited cultural relics, the original approval authority may decide to suspend or revoke the exhibition.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　临时进境的文物，经海关将文物加封后，交由当事人报文物进出境审核机构审核、登记。文物进出境审核机构查验海关封志完好无损后，对每件临时进境文物标明文物临时进境标识，并登记拍照。  
临时进境文物复出境时，应当由原审核、登记的文物进出境审核机构核对入境登记拍照记录，查验文物临时进境标识无误后标明文物出境标识，并由国务院文物行政主管部门发给文物出境许可证。  
未履行本条第一款规定的手续临时进境的文物复出境的，依照本章关于文物出境的规定办理。

**Article 52** For temporarily imported cultural relics, after the customs has sealed them, they shall be delivered to the party for reporting to the cultural relics entry-exit审核机构 for examination and registration. The cultural relics entry-exit examination institution, after verifying that the customs seal is intact, shall affix a temporary entry mark to each temporarily imported cultural relic, and register and photograph it.  
When temporarily imported cultural relics are re-exported, the original examining and registering cultural relics entry-exit examination institution shall check the entry registration and photographic records, verify that the temporary entry mark is correct, affix an exit mark, and the administrative department for cultural relics under the State Council shall issue a cultural relics exit permit.  
Where temporarily imported cultural relics are re-exported without completing the procedures prescribed in the first paragraph of this Article, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter regarding the export of cultural relics.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　任何单位或者个人不得擅自剥除、更换、挪用或者损毁文物出境标识、文物临时进境标识。

**Article 53** No unit or individual may remove, replace, misappropriate or damage the cultural relics exit mark or cultural relics temporary entry mark without authorization.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　公安机关、工商行政管理、文物、海关、城乡规划、建设等有关部门及其工作人员，违反本条例规定，滥用审批权限、不履行职责或者发现违法行为不予查处的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 54** Where public security organs, administrations for industry and commerce, cultural relics authorities, customs, urban and rural planning, construction and other relevant departments and their staff, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, abuse their approval authority, fail to perform their duties, or fail to investigate illegal activities discovered, the responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本条例规定，未取得相应等级的文物保护工程资质证书，擅自承担文物保护单位的修缮、迁移、重建工程的，由文物行政主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正，或者造成严重后果的，处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本条例规定，未取得建设行政主管部门发给的相应等级的资质证书，擅自承担含有建筑活动的文物保护单位的修缮、迁移、重建工程的，由建设行政主管部门依照有关法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚。

**Article 55** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a unit or individual undertakes the repair, relocation or reconstruction of a cultural relic protection site without obtaining a qualification certificate for cultural relic protection projects of the corresponding grade, the administrative department for cultural relics shall order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, or if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, a unit or individual undertakes the repair, relocation or reconstruction of a cultural relic protection site involving construction activities without obtaining a qualification certificate of the corresponding grade issued by the administrative department for construction, the administrative department for construction shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本条例规定，未取得资质证书，擅自从事馆藏文物的修复、复制、拓印活动的，由文物行政主管部门责令停止违法活动；没收违法所得和从事违法活动的专用工具、设备；造成严重后果的，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 56** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a unit or individual engages in the restoration, duplication or rubbing of museum collection cultural relics without obtaining a qualification certificate, the administrative department for cultural relics shall order the cessation of the illegal activities; confiscate the illegal gains and the special tools and equipment used in the illegal activities; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　[文物保护法第六十六条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国文物保护法.md#art-66)第二款规定的罚款，数额为200元以下。

**Article 57** The fine prescribed in the second paragraph of Article 66 of the Law on the Protection of Cultural Relics shall be an amount not exceeding 200 yuan.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本条例规定，未经批准擅自修复、复制、拓印馆藏珍贵文物的，由文物行政主管部门给予警告；造成严重后果的，处2000元以上2万元以下的罚款；对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分。  
文物收藏单位违反本条例规定，未在规定期限内将文物拍摄情况向文物行政主管部门报告的，由文物行政主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分。

**Article 58** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a unit or individual restores, duplicates or rubs precious museum collection cultural relics without approval, the administrative department for cultural relics shall give a warning; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed; the responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.  
Where a cultural relic collection unit, in violation of these Regulations, fails to report the photographing of cultural relics to the administrative department for cultural relics within the prescribed time limit, the administrative department for cultural relics shall order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, the responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　考古发掘单位违反本条例规定，未在规定期限内提交结项报告或者考古发掘报告的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门或者国务院文物行政主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分。

**Article 59** Where an archaeological excavation unit, in violation of these Regulations, fails to submit a project completion report or an archaeological excavation report within the prescribed time limit, the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, or the administrative department for cultural relics under the State Council, shall order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, the responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　考古发掘单位违反本条例规定，未在规定期限内移交文物的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政主管部门或者国务院文物行政主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正，或者造成严重后果的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分。

**Article 60** Where an archaeological excavation unit, in violation of these Regulations, fails to hand over cultural relics within the prescribed time limit, the administrative department for cultural relics of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, or the administrative department for cultural relics under the State Council, shall order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, or if serious consequences are caused, the responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反本条例规定，文物出境展览超过展览期限的，由国务院文物行政主管部门责令限期改正；对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分。

**Article 61** Where a party violates these Regulations by exceeding the exhibition period for cultural relics exhibited overseas, the competent administrative department for cultural relics under the State Council shall order rectification within a specified time limit; the persons in charge bearing responsibility and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　依照[文物保护法第六十六条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国文物保护法.md#art-66)、第七十三条的规定，单位被处以吊销许可证行政处罚的，应当依法到工商行政管理部门办理变更登记或者注销登记；逾期未办理的，由工商行政管理部门吊销营业执照。

**Article 62** Where, in accordance with Article 66 and Article 73 of the Law on the Protection of Cultural Relics, a unit is subjected to the administrative penalty of revocation of its license, it shall, according to law, undergo the procedures for modification registration or cancellation registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce; if it fails to do so within the prescribed time limit, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke its business license.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本条例规定，改变国有的博物馆、纪念馆、文物保护单位等的事业性收入的用途的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 63** Where a party violates these Regulations by altering the use of the operating income of state-owned museums, memorial halls, cultural relic protection sites, etc., the persons in charge bearing responsibility and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　本条例自2003年7月1日起施行。

**Article 64** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 2003.
