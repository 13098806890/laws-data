# 农村五保供养工作条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2006-01-21  
**生效日期**：2006-03-01

> (2006年1月11日国务院第121次常务会议通过　2006年1月21日中华人民共和国国务院令第456号公布　自2006年3月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了做好农村五保供养工作，保障农村五保供养对象的正常生活，促进农村社会保障制度的发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称农村五保供养，是指依照本条例规定，在吃、穿、住、医、葬方面给予村民的生活照顾和物质帮助。 第三条　国务院民政部门主管全国的农村五保供养工作；县级以上地方各级人民政府民政部门主管本行政区域内的农村五保供养工作。 乡、民族乡、镇人民政府管理本行政区域内的农村五保供养工作。 村民委员会协助乡、民族乡、镇人民政府开展农村五保供养工作。 第四条　国家鼓励社会组织和个人为农村五保供养对象和农村五保供养工作提供捐助和服务。 第五条　国家对在农村五保供养工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，给予表彰和奖励。 第二章　供养对象 第六条　老年、残疾或者未满16周岁的村民，无劳动能力、无生活来源又无法定赡养、抚养、扶养义务人，或者其法定赡养、抚养、扶养义务人无赡养、抚养、扶养能力的，享受农村五保供养待遇。 第七条　享受农村五保供养待遇，应当由村民本人向村民委员会提出申请；因年幼或者智力残疾无法表达意愿的，由村民小组或者其他村民代为提出申请。经村民委员会民主评议，对符合本条例第六条规定条件的，在本村范围内公告；无重大异议的，由村民委员会将评议意见和有关材料报送乡、民族乡、镇人民政府审核。 乡、民族乡、镇人民政府应当自收到评议意见之日起20日内提出审核意见，并将审核意见和有关材料报送县级人民政府民政部门审批。县级人民政府民政部门应当自收到审核意见和有关材料之日起20日内作出审批决定。对批准给予农村五保供养待遇的，发给《农村五保供养证书》；对不符合条件不予批准的，应当书面说明理由。 乡、民族乡、镇人民政府应当对申请人的家庭状况和经济条件进行调查核实；必要时，县级人民政府民政部门可以进行复核。申请人、有关组织或者个人应当配合、接受调查，如实提供有关情况。 第八条　农村五保供养对象不再符合本条例第六条规定条件的，村民委员会或者敬老院等农村五保供养服务机构(以下简称农村五保供养服务机构)应当向乡、民族乡、镇人民政府报告，由乡、民族乡、镇人民政府审核并报县级人民政府民政部门核准后，核销其《农村五保供养证书》。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了做好农村五保供养工作，保障农村五保供养对象的正常生活，促进农村社会保障制度的发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of ensuring the proper work of the rural five guarantees support, guaranteeing the normal life of the recipients of rural five guarantees support, and promoting the development of the rural social security system.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称农村五保供养，是指依照本条例规定，在吃、穿、住、医、葬方面给予村民的生活照顾和物质帮助。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "rural five guarantees support" means the living care and material assistance provided to villagers in respect of food, clothing, housing, medical care, and funeral expenses in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院民政部门主管全国的农村五保供养工作；县级以上地方各级人民政府民政部门主管本行政区域内的农村五保供养工作。  
乡、民族乡、镇人民政府管理本行政区域内的农村五保供养工作。  
村民委员会协助乡、民族乡、镇人民政府开展农村五保供养工作。

**Article 3** The civil affairs department of the State Council shall be in charge of the rural five guarantees support work nationwide; the civil affairs departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of the rural five guarantees support work within their respective administrative areas.  
The people's governments of townships, ethnic townships, and towns shall administer the rural five guarantees support work within their respective administrative areas.  
Villagers committees shall assist the people's governments of townships, ethnic townships, and towns in carrying out the rural five guarantees support work.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家鼓励社会组织和个人为农村五保供养对象和农村五保供养工作提供捐助和服务。

**Article 4** The State shall encourage social organizations and individuals to make donations and provide services to the recipients of rural five guarantees support and the rural five guarantees support work.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家对在农村五保供养工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 5** The State shall commend and reward the entities and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in the rural five guarantees support work.

### 第二章　供养对象

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　老年、残疾或者未满16周岁的村民，无劳动能力、无生活来源又无法定赡养、抚养、扶养义务人，或者其法定赡养、抚养、扶养义务人无赡养、抚养、扶养能力的，享受农村五保供养待遇。

**Article 6** A villager who is elderly, disabled, or under the age of 16, and who has no ability to work, no source of livelihood, and no statutory supporter, fosterer, or maintainer, or whose statutory supporter, fosterer, or maintainer has no ability to support, foster, or maintain him or her, shall be entitled to the treatment of rural five guarantees support.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　享受农村五保供养待遇，应当由村民本人向村民委员会提出申请；因年幼或者智力残疾无法表达意愿的，由村民小组或者其他村民代为提出申请。经村民委员会民主评议，对符合[本条例第六条](#art-6)规定条件的，在本村范围内公告；无重大异议的，由村民委员会将评议意见和有关材料报送乡、民族乡、镇人民政府审核。  
乡、民族乡、镇人民政府应当自收到评议意见之日起20日内提出审核意见，并将审核意见和有关材料报送县级人民政府民政部门审批。县级人民政府民政部门应当自收到审核意见和有关材料之日起20日内作出审批决定。对批准给予农村五保供养待遇的，发给《农村五保供养证书》；对不符合条件不予批准的，应当书面说明理由。  
乡、民族乡、镇人民政府应当对申请人的家庭状况和经济条件进行调查核实；必要时，县级人民政府民政部门可以进行复核。申请人、有关组织或者个人应当配合、接受调查，如实提供有关情况。

**Article 7** To enjoy the treatment of rural five guarantees support, the villager shall file an application with the villagers committee; if the villager is unable to express his or her wishes due to young age or intellectual disability, the villagers group or other villagers shall file the application on his or her behalf. After democratic appraisal by the villagers committee, if the applicant meets the conditions provided in Article 6 of these Regulations, the villagers committee shall make a public announcement within the village; if there is no major objection, the villagers committee shall submit the appraisal opinions and relevant materials to the people's government of the township, ethnic township, or town for examination and verification.  
The people's government of the township, ethnic township, or town shall, within 20 days from receipt of the appraisal opinions, issue examination and verification opinions, and submit the opinions and relevant materials to the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level for approval. The civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level shall, within 20 days from receipt of the examination and verification opinions and relevant materials, make a decision on approval. If approval is granted to give the treatment of rural five guarantees support, the "Rural Five Guarantees Support Certificate" shall be issued; if the conditions are not met and approval is denied, the reasons shall be provided in writing.  
The people's government of the township, ethnic township, or town shall investigate and verify the family status and economic conditions of the applicant; if necessary, the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level may conduct a review. The applicant, relevant organizations, or individuals shall cooperate with and accept the investigation and truthfully provide relevant information.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　农村五保供养对象不再符合[本条例第六条](#art-6)规定条件的，村民委员会或者敬老院等农村五保供养服务机构(以下简称农村五保供养服务机构)应当向乡、民族乡、镇人民政府报告，由乡、民族乡、镇人民政府审核并报县级人民政府民政部门核准后，核销其《农村五保供养证书》。  
农村五保供养对象死亡，丧葬事宜办理完毕后，村民委员会或者农村五保供养服务机构应当向乡、民族乡、镇人民政府报告，由乡、民族乡、镇人民政府报县级人民政府民政部门核准后，核销其《农村五保供养证书》。

**Article 8** If a recipient of rural five guarantees support no longer meets the conditions provided in Article 6 of these Regulations, the villagers committee or the rural five guarantees support service institution such as a nursing home (hereinafter referred to as the rural five guarantees support service institution) shall report to the people's government of the township, ethnic township, or town, and after examination and verification by the people's government of the township, ethnic township, or town and submission to the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level for approval, the "Rural Five Guarantees Support Certificate" shall be cancelled.  
If a recipient of rural five guarantees support dies, after the funeral is handled, the villagers committee or the rural five guarantees support service institution shall report to the people's government of the township, ethnic township, or town, and after submission by the people's government of the township, ethnic township, or town to the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level for approval, the "Rural Five Guarantees Support Certificate" shall be cancelled.

### 第三章　供养内容

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　农村五保供养包括下列供养内容：  
(一)供给粮油、副食品和生活用燃料；  
(二)供给服装、被褥等生活用品和零用钱；  
(三)提供符合基本居住条件的住房；  
(四)提供疾病治疗，对生活不能自理的给予照料；  
(五)办理丧葬事宜。  
农村五保供养对象未满16周岁或者已满16周岁仍在接受义务教育的，应当保障他们依法接受义务教育所需费用。  
农村五保供养对象的疾病治疗，应当与当地农村合作医疗和农村医疗救助制度相衔接。

**Article 9** The rural five guarantees support includes the following support contents:  
(1) supply of grain, oil, non-staple food, and fuel for daily life;  
(2) supply of clothing, bedding and other daily necessities and pocket money;  
(3) provision of housing meeting the basic living conditions;  
(4) provision of medical treatment for diseases, and care for those who are unable to take care of themselves;  
(5) handling of funeral arrangements.  
If a recipient of rural five guarantees support is under the age of 16 or has reached the age of 16 but is still receiving compulsory education, the expenses necessary for him or her to receive compulsory education according to law shall be guaranteed.  
The medical treatment for diseases of recipients of rural five guarantees support shall be linked with the local rural cooperative medical care and rural medical assistance systems.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　农村五保供养标准不得低于当地村民的平均生活水平，并根据当地村民平均生活水平的提高适时调整。  
农村五保供养标准，可以由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定，在本行政区域内公布执行，也可以由设区的市级或者县级人民政府制定，报所在的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府备案后公布执行。  
国务院民政部门、国务院财政部门应当加强对农村五保供养标准制定工作的指导。

**Article 10** The standard for rural five guarantees support shall not be lower than the average living standard of local villagers, and shall be adjusted in a timely manner according to the improvement of the average living standard of local villagers.  
The standard for rural five guarantees support may be formulated by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall be published and implemented within its administrative area; or may be formulated by the people's government of a city divided into districts or a county, and shall be published and implemented after being submitted to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for recordation.  
The civil affairs department of the State Council and the finance department of the State Council shall strengthen guidance on the formulation of the standard for rural five guarantees support.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　农村五保供养资金，在地方人民政府财政预算中安排。有农村集体经营等收入的地方，可以从农村集体经营等收入中安排资金，用于补助和改善农村五保供养对象的生活。农村五保供养对象将承包土地交由他人代耕的，其收益归该农村五保供养对象所有。具体办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定。  
中央财政对财政困难地区的农村五保供养，在资金上给予适当补助。  
农村五保供养资金，应当专门用于农村五保供养对象的生活，任何组织或者个人不得贪污、挪用、截留或者私分。

**Article 11** The funds for rural five guarantees support shall be arranged in the fiscal budget of the local people's government. In areas where there is income from rural collective operations, funds may be arranged from such income for subsidizing and improving the lives of recipients of rural five guarantees support. If a recipient of rural five guarantees support entrusts his or her contracted land to others for cultivation, the proceeds thereof shall belong to the said recipient. The specific measures shall be formulated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The central finance shall provide appropriate subsidies in funds for rural five guarantees support in areas with financial difficulties.  
The funds for rural five guarantees support shall be used exclusively for the lives of recipients of rural five guarantees support, and no organization or individual may embezzle, misappropriate, withhold, or privately divide such funds.

### 第四章　供养形式

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　农村五保供养对象可以在当地的农村五保供养服务机构集中供养，也可以在家分散供养。农村五保供养对象可以自行选择供养形式。

**Article 12** A recipient of rural five guarantees support may be provided with centralized support in a local rural five guarantees support service institution, or may be provided with decentralized support at home. The recipient of rural five guarantees support may choose the form of support on his or her own.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　集中供养的农村五保供养对象，由农村五保供养服务机构提供供养服务；分散供养的农村五保供养对象，可以由村民委员会提供照料，也可以由农村五保供养服务机构提供有关供养服务。

**Article 13** For recipients of rural five guarantees support under centralized support, the rural five guarantees support service institution shall provide support services; for recipients of rural five guarantees support under decentralized support, the villagers committee may provide care, or the rural five guarantees support service institution may provide relevant support services.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　各级人民政府应当把农村五保供养服务机构建设纳入经济社会发展规划。  
县级人民政府和乡、民族乡、镇人民政府应当为农村五保供养服务机构提供必要的设备、管理资金，并配备必要的工作人员。

**Article 14** People's governments at all levels shall incorporate the construction of rural five guarantees support service institutions into the economic and social development plans.  
The people's governments at the county level and the people's governments of townships, ethnic townships, and towns shall provide necessary equipment and administrative funds for the rural five guarantees support service institutions and equip them with necessary staff.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　农村五保供养服务机构应当建立健全内部民主管理和服务管理制度。  
农村五保供养服务机构工作人员应当经过必要的培训。

**Article 15** Rural five guarantees support service institutions shall establish and improve internal democratic management and service management systems.  
The staff of rural five guarantees support service institutions shall undergo necessary training.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　农村五保供养服务机构可以开展以改善农村五保供养对象生活条件为目的的农副业生产。地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当对农村五保供养服务机构开展农副业生产给予必要的扶持。

**Article 16** Rural five guarantees support service institutions may engage in agricultural and sideline production for the purpose of improving the living conditions of recipients of rural five guarantees support. The local people's governments at all levels and the relevant departments shall provide necessary support to rural five guarantees support service institutions in carrying out agricultural and sideline production.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　乡、民族乡、镇人民政府应当与村民委员会或者农村五保供养服务机构签订供养服务协议，保证农村五保供养对象享受符合要求的供养。  
村民委员会可以委托村民对分散供养的农村五保供养对象提供照料。

**Article 17** The people's government of the township, ethnic township, or town shall conclude a support service agreement with the villagers committee or the rural five guarantees support service institution to ensure that the recipients of rural five guarantees support enjoy support that meets the requirements.  
The villagers committee may entrust villagers to provide care for recipients of rural five guarantees support under decentralized support.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上人民政府应当依法加强对农村五保供养工作的监督管理。县级以上地方各级人民政府民政部门和乡、民族乡、镇人民政府应当制定农村五保供养工作的管理制度，并负责督促实施。

**Article 18** The people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, strengthen supervision and management of the rural five guarantees support work. The civil affairs departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level and the people's governments of townships, ethnic townships, and towns shall formulate management systems for the rural five guarantees support work and be responsible for supervising their implementation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　财政部门应当按时足额拨付农村五保供养资金，确保资金到位，并加强对资金使用情况的监督管理。  
审计机关应当依法加强对农村五保供养资金使用情况的审计。

**Article 19** The finance departments shall allocate the funds for rural five guarantees support in full and on time, ensure that the funds are in place, and strengthen supervision and management over the use of the funds.  
The audit organs shall, according to law, strengthen auditing of the use of the funds for rural five guarantees support.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　农村五保供养待遇的申请条件、程序、民主评议情况以及农村五保供养的标准和资金使用情况等，应当向社会公告，接受社会监督。

**Article 20** The application conditions, procedures, and democratic appraisal results for the treatment of rural five guarantees support, as well as the standards for rural five guarantees support and the use of funds, shall be publicly announced to the society to accept social supervision.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　农村五保供养服务机构应当遵守治安、消防、卫生、财务会计等方面的法律、法规和国家有关规定，向农村五保供养对象提供符合要求的供养服务，并接受地方人民政府及其有关部门的监督管理。

**Article 21** Rural five guarantees support service institutions shall comply with the laws, regulations, and relevant state provisions on public security, fire protection, public health, financial accounting, etc., provide support services that meet the requirements to recipients of rural five guarantees support, and accept supervision and management by the local people's government and its relevant departments.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　违反本条例规定，有关行政机关及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员以及其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)对符合农村五保供养条件的村民不予批准享受农村五保供养待遇的，或者对不符合农村五保供养条件的村民批准其享受农村五保供养待遇的；  
(二)贪污、挪用、截留、私分农村五保供养款物的；  
(三)有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为的。

**Article 22** Where any relevant administrative organ or its staff member, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to approve a villager who meets the conditions for rural five guarantees support to enjoy the treatment of rural five guarantees support, or approving a villager who does not meet the conditions for rural five guarantees support to enjoy the treatment of rural five guarantees support;  
(2) embezzling, misappropriating, withholding, or privately dividing the funds and materials for rural five guarantees support;  
(3) committing other acts of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　违反本条例规定，村民委员会组成人员贪污、挪用、截留农村五保供养款物的，依法予以罢免；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本条例规定，农村五保供养服务机构工作人员私分、挪用、截留农村五保供养款物的，予以辞退；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 23** Where any member of a villagers committee, in violation of these Regulations, embezzles, misappropriates, or withholds the funds and materials for rural five guarantees support, he or she shall be removed from office according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where any staff member of a rural five guarantees support service institution, in violation of these Regulations, privately divides, misappropriates, or withholds the funds and materials for rural five guarantees support, he or she shall be dismissed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　违反本条例规定，村民委员会或者农村五保供养服务机构对农村五保供养对象提供的供养服务不符合要求的，由乡、民族乡、镇人民政府责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，乡、民族乡、镇人民政府有权终止供养服务协议；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 24** Where the villagers committee or the rural five guarantees support service institution, in violation of these Regulations, fails to provide support services that meet the requirements to recipients of rural five guarantees support, the people's government of the township, ethnic township, or town shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the people's government of the township, ethnic township, or town shall have the right to terminate the support service agreement; if losses are caused, the compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　《农村五保供养证书》由国务院民政部门规定式样，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门监制。

**Article 25** The form of the "Rural Five Guarantees Support Certificate" shall be prescribed by the civil affairs department of the State Council, and its production shall be supervised by the civil affairs departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例自2006年3月1日起施行。1994年1月23日国务院发布的《农村五保供养工作条例》同时废止。

**Article 26** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 2006. The Regulations on the Work of Rural Five Guarantees Support promulgated by the State Council on January 23, 1994 shall be abolished simultaneously.
