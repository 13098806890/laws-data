# 志愿服务条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2017-08-22  
**生效日期**：2017-12-01

> (2017年6月7日国务院第175次常务会议通过　2017年8月22日中华人民共和国国务院令第685号公布　自2017年12月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了保障志愿者、志愿服务组织、志愿服务对象的合法权益，鼓励和规范志愿服务，发展志愿服务事业，培育和践行社会主义核心价值观，促进社会文明进步，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于在中华人民共和国境内开展的志愿服务以及与志愿服务有关的活动。 本条例所称志愿服务，是指志愿者、志愿服务组织和其他组织自愿、无偿向社会或者他人提供的公益服务。 第三条　开展志愿服务，应当遵循自愿、无偿、平等、诚信、合法的原则，不得违背社会公德、损害社会公共利益和他人合法权益，不得危害国家安全。 第四条　县级以上人民政府应当将志愿服务事业纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，合理安排志愿服务所需资金，促进广覆盖、多层次、宽领域开展志愿服务。 第五条　国家和地方精神文明建设指导机构建立志愿服务工作协调机制，加强对志愿服务工作的统筹规划、协调指导、督促检查和经验推广。 国务院民政部门负责全国志愿服务行政管理工作；县级以上地方人民政府民政部门负责本行政区域内志愿服务行政管理工作。 县级以上人民政府有关部门按照各自职责，负责与志愿服务有关的工作。 工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会等有关人民团体和群众团体应当在各自的工作范围内做好相应的志愿服务工作。 第二章　志愿者和志愿服务组织 第六条　本条例所称志愿者，是指以自己的时间、知识、技能、体力等从事志愿服务的自然人。 本条例所称志愿服务组织，是指依法成立，以开展志愿服务为宗旨的非营利性组织。 第七条　志愿者可以将其身份信息、服务技能、服务时间、联系方式等个人基本信息，通过国务院民政部门指定的志愿服务信息系统自行注册，也可以通过志愿服务组织进行注册。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障志愿者、志愿服务组织、志愿服务对象的合法权益，鼓励和规范志愿服务，发展志愿服务事业，培育和践行社会主义核心价值观，促进社会文明进步，制定本条例。

**Article 1** For the purposes of safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of volunteers, volunteer service organizations, and recipients of volunteer services, encouraging and regulating volunteer services, developing the volunteer service industry, cultivating and practicing the core values of socialism, and promoting social civility and progress, these Regulations are formulated.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于在中华人民共和国境内开展的志愿服务以及与志愿服务有关的活动。  
本条例所称志愿服务，是指志愿者、志愿服务组织和其他组织自愿、无偿向社会或者他人提供的公益服务。

**Article 2** These Regulations apply to volunteer services carried out within the territory of the People's Republic of China and activities related to volunteer services.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "volunteer services" means public welfare services voluntarily and gratuitously provided to society or others by volunteers, volunteer service organizations, and other organizations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　开展志愿服务，应当遵循自愿、无偿、平等、诚信、合法的原则，不得违背社会公德、损害社会公共利益和他人合法权益，不得危害国家安全。

**Article 3** In carrying out volunteer services, the principles of voluntariness, gratuitousness, equality, integrity, and legality shall be followed, and social morality shall not be violated, nor shall social public interests and the legitimate rights and interests of others be harmed, nor shall national security be endangered.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府应当将志愿服务事业纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，合理安排志愿服务所需资金，促进广覆盖、多层次、宽领域开展志愿服务。

**Article 4** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the volunteer service industry into the national economic and social development plan, reasonably arrange funds needed for volunteer services, and promote the development of volunteer services with wide coverage, multiple levels, and broad fields.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家和地方精神文明建设指导机构建立志愿服务工作协调机制，加强对志愿服务工作的统筹规划、协调指导、督促检查和经验推广。  
国务院民政部门负责全国志愿服务行政管理工作；县级以上地方人民政府民政部门负责本行政区域内志愿服务行政管理工作。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门按照各自职责，负责与志愿服务有关的工作。  
工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会等有关人民团体和群众团体应当在各自的工作范围内做好相应的志愿服务工作。

**Article 5** State and local spiritual civilization guidance institutions shall establish a coordination mechanism for volunteer service work, strengthen overall planning, coordination guidance, supervision and inspection, and experience promotion for volunteer service work.  
The civil affairs department of the State Council shall be responsible for the administrative management of volunteer services nationwide; the civil affairs departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the administrative management of volunteer services within their respective administrative regions.  
The relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for work related to volunteer services according to their respective functions and duties.  
Trade unions, the Communist Youth League, women's federations, and other relevant people's organizations and mass organizations shall do a good job in corresponding volunteer service work within their respective work scopes.

### 第二章　志愿者和志愿服务组织

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　本条例所称志愿者，是指以自己的时间、知识、技能、体力等从事志愿服务的自然人。  
本条例所称志愿服务组织，是指依法成立，以开展志愿服务为宗旨的非营利性组织。

**Article 6** For the purposes of these Regulations, "volunteer" means a natural person who engages in volunteer services with his or her own time, knowledge, skills, physical strength, etc.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "volunteer service organization" means a non-profit organization established according to law with the purpose of carrying out volunteer services.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　志愿者可以将其身份信息、服务技能、服务时间、联系方式等个人基本信息，通过国务院民政部门指定的志愿服务信息系统自行注册，也可以通过志愿服务组织进行注册。  
志愿者提供的个人基本信息应当真实、准确、完整。

**Article 7** A volunteer may register his or her personal basic information such as identity information, service skills, service hours, and contact information by himself or herself through the volunteer service information system designated by the civil affairs department of the State Council, or may register through a volunteer service organization.  
The personal basic information provided by a volunteer shall be truthful, accurate, and complete.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　志愿服务组织可以采取社会团体、社会服务机构、基金会等组织形式。志愿服务组织的登记管理按照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 8** Volunteer service organizations may take the organizational forms of social groups, social service institutions, foundations, etc. The registration management of volunteer service organizations shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　志愿服务组织可以依法成立行业组织，反映行业诉求，推动行业交流，促进志愿服务事业发展。

**Article 9** Volunteer service organizations may establish industry organizations according to law to reflect industry demands, promote industry exchanges, and facilitate the development of the volunteer service industry.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在志愿服务组织中，根据中国共产党章程的规定，设立中国共产党的组织，开展党的活动。志愿服务组织应当为党组织的活动提供必要条件。

**Article 10** In a volunteer service organization, a Chinese Communist Party organization shall be established and Party activities shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Constitution of the Chinese Communist Party. Volunteer service organizations shall provide necessary conditions for the activities of Party organizations.

### 第三章　志愿服务活动

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　志愿者可以参与志愿服务组织开展的志愿服务活动，也可以自行依法开展志愿服务活动。

**Article 11** Volunteers may participate in volunteer service activities organized by volunteer service organizations, and may also carry out volunteer service activities on their own according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　志愿服务组织可以招募志愿者开展志愿服务活动；招募时，应当说明与志愿服务有关的真实、准确、完整的信息以及在志愿服务过程中可能发生的风险。

**Article 12** A volunteer service organization may recruit volunteers to carry out volunteer service activities. When recruiting, it shall provide true, accurate, and complete information relating to the volunteer service and the risks that may arise in the course of the volunteer service.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　需要志愿服务的组织或者个人可以向志愿服务组织提出申请，并提供与志愿服务有关的真实、准确、完整的信息，说明在志愿服务过程中可能发生的风险。志愿服务组织应当对有关信息进行核实，并及时予以答复。

**Article 13** An organization or individual in need of volunteer service may submit an application to a volunteer service organization, and provide true, accurate, and complete information relating to the volunteer service, specifying the risks that may arise in the course of the volunteer service. The volunteer service organization shall verify the relevant information and give a timely reply.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　志愿者、志愿服务组织、志愿服务对象可以根据需要签订协议，明确当事人的权利和义务，约定志愿服务的内容、方式、时间、地点、工作条件和安全保障措施等。

**Article 14** Volunteers, volunteer service organizations, and recipients of volunteer service may, as needed, enter into an agreement to specify the rights and obligations of the parties, and agree on the content, methods, time, place, working conditions, and safety guarantee measures of the volunteer service, among others.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　志愿服务组织安排志愿者参与志愿服务活动，应当与志愿者的年龄、知识、技能和身体状况相适应，不得要求志愿者提供超出其能力的志愿服务。

**Article 15** A volunteer service organization arranging volunteers to participate in volunteer service activities shall ensure that such activities are compatible with the volunteers’ age, knowledge, skills, and physical condition, and shall not require volunteers to provide volunteer service beyond their capabilities.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　志愿服务组织安排志愿者参与的志愿服务活动需要专门知识、技能的，应当对志愿者开展相关培训。  
开展专业志愿服务活动，应当执行国家或者行业组织制定的标准和规程。法律、行政法规对开展志愿服务活动有职业资格要求的，志愿者应当依法取得相应的资格。

**Article 16** Where a volunteer service activity arranged by a volunteer service organization for volunteers to participate in requires specialized knowledge or skills, it shall provide relevant training to the volunteers.  
For carrying out professional volunteer service activities, the standards and procedures formulated by the State or industry organizations shall be followed. Where laws or administrative regulations have professional qualification requirements for carrying out volunteer service activities, volunteers shall obtain the corresponding qualifications according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　志愿服务组织应当为志愿者参与志愿服务活动提供必要条件，解决志愿者在志愿服务过程中遇到的困难，维护志愿者的合法权益。  
志愿服务组织安排志愿者参与可能发生人身危险的志愿服务活动前，应当为志愿者购买相应的人身意外伤害保险。

**Article 17** A volunteer service organization shall provide necessary conditions for volunteers to participate in volunteer service activities, resolve difficulties encountered by volunteers in the course of volunteer service, and protect the legitimate rights and interests of volunteers.  
Before arranging volunteers to participate in volunteer service activities that may involve personal danger, the volunteer service organization shall purchase corresponding personal accident insurance for the volunteers.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　志愿服务组织开展志愿服务活动，可以使用志愿服务标志。

**Article 18** A volunteer service organization may use the volunteer service logo when carrying out volunteer service activities.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　志愿服务组织安排志愿者参与志愿服务活动，应当如实记录志愿者个人基本信息、志愿服务情况、培训情况、表彰奖励情况、评价情况等信息，按照统一的信息数据标准录入国务院民政部门指定的志愿服务信息系统，实现数据互联互通。  
志愿者需要志愿服务记录证明的，志愿服务组织应当依据志愿服务记录无偿、如实出具。  
记录志愿服务信息和出具志愿服务记录证明的办法，由国务院民政部门会同有关单位制定。

**Article 19** A volunteer service organization arranging volunteers to participate in volunteer service activities shall truthfully record information such as the volunteer’s basic personal information, volunteer service status, training status, commendation and reward status, and evaluation status, and enter it into the volunteer service information system designated by the civil affairs department of the State Council in accordance with uniform information data standards, so as to achieve data interconnection and interworking.  
Where a volunteer needs a certificate of volunteer service record, the volunteer service organization shall, based on the volunteer service record, issue it gratuitously and truthfully.  
The measures for recording volunteer service information and issuing certificates of volunteer service record shall be formulated by the civil affairs department of the State Council jointly with the relevant entities.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　志愿服务组织、志愿服务对象应当尊重志愿者的人格尊严；未经志愿者本人同意，不得公开或者泄露其有关信息。

**Article 20** A volunteer service organization and the recipient of volunteer service shall respect the personal dignity of volunteers; without the consent of the volunteer, they shall not disclose or divulge the volunteer’s relevant information.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　志愿服务组织、志愿者应当尊重志愿服务对象人格尊严，不得侵害志愿服务对象个人隐私，不得向志愿服务对象收取或者变相收取报酬。

**Article 21** A volunteer service organization or a volunteer shall respect the personal dignity of the recipients of volunteer services, shall not infringe upon the personal privacy of the recipients of volunteer services, and shall not collect or covertly collect remuneration from the recipients of volunteer services.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　志愿者接受志愿服务组织安排参与志愿服务活动的，应当服从管理，接受必要的培训。  
志愿者应当按照约定提供志愿服务。志愿者因故不能按照约定提供志愿服务的，应当及时告知志愿服务组织或者志愿服务对象。

**Article 22** A volunteer who accepts arrangements by a volunteer service organization to participate in volunteer service activities shall comply with management and receive necessary training.  
A volunteer shall provide volunteer services in accordance with the agreement. If a volunteer is unable to provide volunteer services in accordance with the agreement for any reason, he or she shall promptly inform the volunteer service organization or the recipient of volunteer services.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家鼓励和支持国家机关、企业事业单位、人民团体、社会组织等成立志愿服务队伍开展专业志愿服务活动，鼓励和支持具备专业知识、技能的志愿者提供专业志愿服务。  
国家鼓励和支持公共服务机构招募志愿者提供志愿服务。

**Article 23** The State encourages and supports state organs, enterprises and public institutions, people's organizations, social organizations, and other entities to establish volunteer service teams to carry out professional volunteer service activities, and encourages and supports volunteers with professional knowledge and skills to provide professional volunteer services.  
The State encourages and supports public service agencies to recruit volunteers to provide volunteer services.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　发生重大自然灾害、事故灾难和公共卫生事件等突发事件，需要迅速开展救助的，有关人民政府应当建立协调机制，提供需求信息，引导志愿服务组织和志愿者及时有序开展志愿服务活动。  
志愿服务组织、志愿者开展应对突发事件的志愿服务活动，应当接受有关人民政府设立的应急指挥机构的统一指挥、协调。

**Article 24** Where a major natural disaster, accident disaster, public health incident, or other emergency occurs and rapid assistance is required, the relevant people's government shall establish a coordination mechanism, provide demand information, and guide volunteer service organizations and volunteers to carry out volunteer service activities in a timely and orderly manner.  
When volunteer service organizations and volunteers carry out volunteer service activities in response to emergencies, they shall accept the unified command and coordination of the emergency command institution established by the relevant people's government.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　任何组织和个人不得强行指派志愿者、志愿服务组织提供服务，不得以志愿服务名义进行营利性活动。

**Article 25** No organization or individual shall forcibly assign a volunteer or a volunteer service organization to provide services, or conduct for-profit activities in the name of volunteer services.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　任何组织和个人发现志愿服务组织有违法行为，可以向民政部门、其他有关部门或者志愿服务行业组织投诉、举报。民政部门、其他有关部门或者志愿服务行业组织接到投诉、举报，应当及时调查处理；对无权处理的，应当告知投诉人、举报人向有权处理的部门或者行业组织投诉、举报。

**Article 26** Any organization or individual that discovers that a volunteer service organization has committed an illegal act may file a complaint or report with the civil affairs department, other relevant departments, or the volunteer service industry organization. After receiving a complaint or report, the civil affairs department, other relevant departments, or the volunteer service industry organization shall promptly investigate and handle it; if it has no authority to handle it, it shall inform the complainant or reporter to file a complaint or report with the department or industry organization that has the authority to handle it.

### 第四章　促进措施

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上人民政府应当根据经济社会发展情况，制定促进志愿服务事业发展的政策和措施。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当在各自职责范围内，为志愿服务提供指导和帮助。

**Article 27** People's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the economic and social development situation, formulate policies and measures to promote the development of volunteer services.  
People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, within their respective functions and duties, provide guidance and assistance for volunteer services.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家鼓励企业事业单位、基层群众性自治组织和其他组织为开展志愿服务提供场所和其他便利条件。

**Article 28** The State encourages enterprises and public institutions, grassroots autonomous organizations, and other organizations to provide venues and other convenient conditions for carrying out volunteer services.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　学校、家庭和社会应当培养青少年的志愿服务意识和能力。  
高等学校、中等职业学校可以将学生参与志愿服务活动纳入实践学分管理。

**Article 29** Schools, families, and society shall cultivate young people's awareness and ability in volunteer services.  
Institutions of higher education and secondary vocational schools may incorporate students' participation in volunteer service activities into the management of practical credits.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　各级人民政府及其有关部门可以依法通过购买服务等方式，支持志愿服务运营管理，并依照国家有关规定向社会公开购买服务的项目目录、服务标准、资金预算等相关情况。

**Article 30** People's governments at all levels and their relevant departments may, according to law, support the operation and management of volunteer services through purchasing services or other means, and shall disclose to the public the catalog of projects, service standards, fund budgets, and other relevant information regarding the purchase of services in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　自然人、法人和其他组织捐赠财产用于志愿服务的，依法享受税收优惠。

**Article 31** Natural persons, legal persons and other organizations that donate property for volunteer service shall enjoy tax incentives according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　对在志愿服务事业发展中做出突出贡献的志愿者、志愿服务组织，由县级以上人民政府或者有关部门按照法律、法规和国家有关规定予以表彰、奖励。  
国家鼓励企业和其他组织在同等条件下优先招用有良好志愿服务记录的志愿者。公务员考录、事业单位招聘可以将志愿服务情况纳入考察内容。

**Article 32** Volunteers and volunteer service organizations that have made outstanding contributions to the development of volunteer service shall be commended and rewarded by the people's governments at or above the county level or relevant departments in accordance with laws, regulations and the relevant provisions of the State.  
The State encourages enterprises and other organizations to give priority to employing volunteers with good volunteer service records under equal conditions. The recruitment of civil servants and the recruitment of public institutions may incorporate volunteer service into the assessment content.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级以上地方人民政府可以根据实际情况采取措施，鼓励公共服务机构等对有良好志愿服务记录的志愿者给予优待。

**Article 33** The local people's governments at or above the county level may take measures according to actual circumstances to encourage public service institutions and others to give preferential treatment to volunteers with good volunteer service records.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上人民政府应当建立健全志愿服务统计和发布制度。

**Article 34** The people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and perfect the system for statistics and publication of volunteer service.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　广播、电视、报刊、网络等媒体应当积极开展志愿服务宣传活动，传播志愿服务文化，弘扬志愿服务精神。

**Article 35** Media such as radio, television, newspapers, and the Internet shall actively conduct publicity activities on volunteer service, spread the culture of volunteer service, and promote the spirit of volunteer service.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　志愿服务组织泄露志愿者有关信息、侵害志愿服务对象个人隐私的，由民政部门予以警告，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令限期停止活动并进行整改；情节严重的，吊销登记证书并予以公告。

**Article 36** Where a volunteer service organization discloses relevant information of volunteers or infringes on the personal privacy of the recipient of volunteer service, the civil affairs department shall give a warning and order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend activities for a time limit and make rectification; if the circumstances are serious, its registration certificate shall be revoked and an announcement shall be made.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　志愿服务组织、志愿者向志愿服务对象收取或者变相收取报酬的，由民政部门予以警告，责令退还收取的报酬；情节严重的，对有关组织或者个人并处所收取报酬一倍以上五倍以下的罚款。

**Article 37** Where a volunteer service organization or volunteer collects or collects remuneration in disguised form from the recipient of volunteer service, the civil affairs department shall give a warning and order the return of the collected remuneration; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant organization or individual shall be concurrently imposed a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the collected remuneration.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　志愿服务组织不依法记录志愿服务信息或者出具志愿服务记录证明的，由民政部门予以警告，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令限期停止活动，并可以向社会和有关单位通报。

**Article 38** Where a volunteer service organization fails to record volunteer service information or issue a certificate of volunteer service record according to law, the civil affairs department shall give a warning and order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend activities for a time limit and may be reported to the public and relevant units.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　对以志愿服务名义进行营利性活动的组织和个人，由民政、工商等部门依法查处。

**Article 39** Any organization or individual that engages in profit-making activities under the guise of volunteer service shall be investigated and dealt with by the civil affairs, industry and commerce and other departments according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　县级以上人民政府民政部门和其他有关部门及其工作人员有下列情形之一的，由上级机关或者监察机关责令改正；依法应当给予处分的，由任免机关或者监察机关对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予处分：  
(一)强行指派志愿者、志愿服务组织提供服务；  
(二)未依法履行监督管理职责；  
(三)其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的行为。

**Article 40** Where the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level and other relevant departments and their staff members fall under any of the following circumstances, the superior authority or the supervisory organ shall order them to make corrections; if disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed according to law, the appointing authority or the supervisory organ shall impose sanctions on the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons:  
(1) forcibly assigning volunteers or volunteer service organizations to provide services;  
(2) failing to perform the duties of supervision and administration according to law;  
(3) other acts of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　基层群众性自治组织、公益活动举办单位和公共服务机构开展公益活动，需要志愿者提供志愿服务的，可以与志愿服务组织合作，由志愿服务组织招募志愿者，也可以自行招募志愿者。自行招募志愿者提供志愿服务的，参照本条例关于志愿服务组织开展志愿服务活动的规定执行。

**Article 41** When grassroots self-governing organizations, public welfare activity organizers, and public service institutions conduct public welfare activities and require volunteer services, they may cooperate with volunteer service organizations to recruit volunteers or may recruit volunteers themselves. If they recruit volunteers themselves to provide volunteer services, the provisions of these Regulations concerning volunteer service organizations conducting volunteer service activities shall apply.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　志愿服务组织以外的其他组织可以开展力所能及的志愿服务活动。  
城乡社区、单位内部经基层群众性自治组织或者本单位同意成立的团体，可以在本社区、本单位内部开展志愿服务活动。

**Article 42** Organizations other than volunteer service organizations may conduct volunteer service activities within their capabilities.  
Groups established within urban and rural communities or organizations with the consent of grassroots self-governing organizations or the organization itself may conduct volunteer service activities within their own community or organization.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　境外志愿服务组织和志愿者在境内开展志愿服务，应当遵守本条例和中华人民共和国有关法律、行政法规以及国家有关规定。  
组织境内志愿者到境外开展志愿服务，在境内的有关事宜，适用本条例和中华人民共和国有关法律、行政法规以及国家有关规定；在境外开展志愿服务，应当遵守所在国家或者地区的法律。

**Article 43** Overseas volunteer service organizations and volunteers conducting volunteer service activities within the territory of China shall comply with these Regulations and the relevant laws, administrative regulations, and national provisions of the People's Republic of China.  
For organizing domestic volunteers to conduct volunteer service activities abroad, the relevant matters within the territory of China shall be governed by these Regulations and the relevant laws, administrative regulations, and national provisions of the People's Republic of China; volunteer service activities conducted abroad shall comply with the laws of the host country or region.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　本条例自2017年12月1日起施行。

**Article 44** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 2017.
