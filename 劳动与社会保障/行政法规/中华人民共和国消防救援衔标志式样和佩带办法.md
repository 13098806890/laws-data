### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国消防救援衔条例》的规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** : These Measures are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Fire Rescue Ranks.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　消防救援人员佩带的消防救援衔标志必须与所授予的消防救援衔相符。

**Article 2** : The fire rescue rank insignia worn by fire rescue personnel shall be consistent with the fire rescue rank conferred upon them.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　消防救援人员的消防救援衔标志：总监、副总监、助理总监衔标志由金黄色橄榄枝环绕金黄色徽标组成，徽标由五角星、雄鹰翅膀、消防斧和消防水带构成；指挥长、指挥员衔标志由金黄色横杠和金黄色六角星花组成；高级消防员、中级消防员和初级消防员中的三级消防士、四级消防士衔标志由金黄色横杠和金黄色徽标组成，徽标由交叉斧头、水枪、紧握手腕和雄鹰翅膀构成，预备消防士衔标志为金黄色横杠。

**Article 3** : The fire rescue rank insignia for fire rescue personnel are as follows: the rank insignia for Chief, Deputy Chief, and Assistant Chief shall consist of a golden olive branch encircling a golden emblem, where the emblem is composed of a five-pointed star, eagle wings, a fire axe, and a fire hose; the rank insignia for commanders and officers shall consist of golden horizontal bars and golden six-pointed star flowers; the rank insignia for senior firefighters, intermediate firefighters, and junior firefighters at the level of third-class firefighter and fourth-class firefighter shall consist of golden horizontal bars and a golden emblem, where the emblem is composed of crossed axes, a water nozzle, a clasped wrist, and eagle wings; and the rank insignia for probationary firefighters shall be a golden horizontal bar.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　消防救援衔标志佩带在肩章和领章上，肩章分为硬肩章、软肩章和套式肩章，硬肩章、软肩章为剑形，套式肩章、领章为四边形；肩章、领章版面为深火焰蓝色。消防救援人员着春秋常服、冬常服和常服大衣时，佩带硬肩章；着夏常服、棉大衣和作训大衣时，管理指挥人员、专业技术人员佩带软肩章，消防员佩带套式肩章；着作训服时，佩带领章。

**Article 4** : Fire rescue rank insignia shall be worn on shoulder boards and collar insignia. Shoulder boards are divided into hard shoulder boards, soft shoulder boards, and slip-on shoulder boards. Hard and soft shoulder boards shall be sword-shaped, while slip-on shoulder boards and collar insignia shall be quadrilateral. The background of the shoulder boards and collar insignia shall be deep flame blue. When wearing spring-autumn service uniforms, winter service uniforms, and service overcoats, fire rescue personnel shall wear hard shoulder boards. When wearing summer service uniforms, cotton overcoats, and training overcoats, management and command personnel and professional technical personnel shall wear soft shoulder boards, while firefighters shall wear slip-on shoulder boards. When wearing training uniforms, they shall wear collar insignia.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　总监衔标志缀钉一枚橄榄枝环绕一周徽标，副总监衔标志缀钉一枚橄榄枝环绕多半周徽标，助理总监衔标志缀钉一枚橄榄枝环绕小半周徽标。  
指挥长衔标志缀钉二道粗横杠，高级指挥长衔标志缀钉四枚六角星花，一级指挥长衔标志缀钉三枚六角星花，二级指挥长衔标志缀钉二枚六角星花，三级指挥长衔标志缀钉一枚六角星花。  
指挥员衔标志缀钉一道粗横杠，一级指挥员衔标志缀钉四枚六角星花，二级指挥员衔标志缀钉三枚六角星花，三级指挥员衔标志缀钉二枚六角星花，四级指挥员衔标志缀钉一枚六角星花。  
高级消防员衔标志缀钉一枚徽标，一级消防长衔标志缀钉三粗一细四道横杠，二级消防长衔标志缀钉三道粗横杠，三级消防长衔标志缀钉二粗一细三道横杠。  
中级消防员衔标志缀钉一枚徽标，一级消防士衔标志缀钉二道粗横杠，二级消防士衔标志缀钉一粗一细二道横杠。  
初级消防员衔标志中，三级消防士衔标志缀钉一枚徽标和一道粗横杠，四级消防士衔标志缀钉一枚徽标和一道细横杠，预备消防士衔标志缀钉一道加粗横杠。

**Article 5** : The rank insignia for Chief shall bear one emblem encircled by an olive branch; the rank insignia for Deputy Chief shall bear one emblem encircled by an olive branch covering more than half; the rank insignia for Assistant Chief shall bear one emblem encircled by an olive branch covering less than half.  
The rank insignia for directors shall bear two thick horizontal bars; for senior directors, four six-pointed star flowers; for first-class directors, three six-pointed star flowers; for second-class directors, two six-pointed star flowers; for third-class directors, one six-pointed star flower.  
The rank insignia for officers shall bear one thick horizontal bar; for first-class officers, four six-pointed star flowers; for second-class officers, three six-pointed star flowers; for third-class officers, two six-pointed star flowers; for fourth-class officers, one six-pointed star flower.  
The rank insignia for senior firefighters shall bear one emblem; for first-class fire chiefs, four horizontal bars (three thick and one thin); for second-class fire chiefs, three thick horizontal bars; for third-class fire chiefs, three horizontal bars (two thick and one thin).  
The rank insignia for intermediate firefighters shall bear one emblem; for first-class fire sergeants, two thick horizontal bars; for second-class fire sergeants, two horizontal bars (one thick and one thin).  
Among the rank insignia for junior firefighters: for third-class firefighters, one emblem and one thick horizontal bar; for fourth-class firefighters, one emblem and one thin horizontal bar; for probationary firefighters, one thickened horizontal bar.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　消防救援人员晋升或者降低消防救援衔时，由批准机关更换其消防救援衔标志；取消消防救援衔的，由批准机关收回其消防救援衔标志。

**Article 6** : When a fire rescue personnel member is promoted or reduced in fire rescue rank, the approving authority shall replace their fire rescue rank insignia. If the fire rescue rank is revoked, the approving authority shall recover their fire rescue rank insignia.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　消防救援人员的消防救援衔标志由国务院应急管理部门负责制作和管理。其他单位和个人不得制作、仿造、伪造、变造和买卖、使用消防救援衔标志，也不得使用与消防救援衔标志相类似的标志。

**Article 7** : The fire rescue rank insignia for fire rescue personnel shall be manufactured and managed by the Emergency Management Department of the State Council. No other unit or individual may manufacture, imitate, counterfeit, alter, buy, sell, or use fire rescue rank insignia, nor may they use any insignia similar to fire rescue rank insignia.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　本办法自公布之日起施行。
