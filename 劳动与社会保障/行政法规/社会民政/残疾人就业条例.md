# 残疾人就业条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2007-02-25  
**生效日期**：2007-05-01

> (2007年2月14日国务院第169次常务会议通过　2007年2月25日中华人民共和国国务院令第488号公布　自2007年5月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了促进残疾人就业，保障残疾人的劳动权利，根据《中华人民共和国残疾人保障法》和其他有关法律，制定本条例。 第二条　国家对残疾人就业实行集中就业与分散就业相结合的方针，促进残疾人就业。 县级以上人民政府应当将残疾人就业纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，并制定优惠政策和具体扶持保护措施，为残疾人就业创造条件。 第三条　机关、团体、企业、事业单位和民办非企业单位(以下统称用人单位)应当依照有关法律、本条例和其他有关行政法规的规定，履行扶持残疾人就业的责任和义务。 第四条　国家鼓励社会组织和个人通过多种渠道、多种形式，帮助、支持残疾人就业，鼓励残疾人通过应聘等多种形式就业。禁止在就业中歧视残疾人。 残疾人应当提高自身素质，增强就业能力。 第五条　各级人民政府应当加强对残疾人就业工作的统筹规划，综合协调。县级以上人民政府负责残疾人工作的机构，负责组织、协调、指导、督促有关部门做好残疾人就业工作。 县级以上人民政府劳动保障、民政等有关部门在各自的职责范围内，做好残疾人就业工作。 第六条　中国残疾人联合会及其地方组织依照法律、法规或者接受政府委托，负责残疾人就业工作的具体组织实施与监督。 工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会，应当在各自的工作范围内，做好残疾人就业工作。 第七条　各级人民政府对在残疾人就业工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，给予表彰和奖励。 第二章　用人单位的责任 第八条　用人单位应当按照一定比例安排残疾人就业，并为其提供适当的工种、岗位。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了促进残疾人就业，保障残疾人的劳动权利，根据《中华人民共和国残疾人保障法》和其他有关法律，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Persons with Disabilities and other relevant laws, for the purpose of promoting employment of persons with disabilities and safeguarding their labor rights.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家对残疾人就业实行集中就业与分散就业相结合的方针，促进残疾人就业。  
县级以上人民政府应当将残疾人就业纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，并制定优惠政策和具体扶持保护措施，为残疾人就业创造条件。

**Article 2** The State adopts the policy of combining centralized employment with decentralized employment for the employment of persons with disabilities, so as to promote their employment.  
The people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the employment of persons with disabilities into the national economic and social development plans, and shall formulate preferential policies and specific supporting and protective measures to create conditions for the employment of persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　机关、团体、企业、事业单位和民办非企业单位(以下统称用人单位)应当依照有关法律、本条例和其他有关行政法规的规定，履行扶持残疾人就业的责任和义务。

**Article 3** State organs, organizations, enterprises, institutions and private non-enterprise units (hereinafter collectively referred to as employing units) shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws, these Regulations and other relevant administrative regulations, fulfill the responsibilities and obligations to support the employment of persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家鼓励社会组织和个人通过多种渠道、多种形式，帮助、支持残疾人就业，鼓励残疾人通过应聘等多种形式就业。禁止在就业中歧视残疾人。  
残疾人应当提高自身素质，增强就业能力。

**Article 4** The State encourages social organizations and individuals to help and support the employment of persons with disabilities through various channels and forms, and encourages persons with disabilities to seek employment through various forms such as job application. Discrimination against persons with disabilities in employment is prohibited.  
Persons with disabilities shall improve their own quality and enhance their employability.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　各级人民政府应当加强对残疾人就业工作的统筹规划，综合协调。县级以上人民政府负责残疾人工作的机构，负责组织、协调、指导、督促有关部门做好残疾人就业工作。  
县级以上人民政府劳动保障、民政等有关部门在各自的职责范围内，做好残疾人就业工作。

**Article 5** The people's governments at all levels shall strengthen overall planning and comprehensive coordination for the employment of persons with disabilities. The institutions responsible for the work concerning persons with disabilities under the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing, coordinating, guiding and supervising the relevant departments to perform well the employment work for persons with disabilities.  
The departments of labor security, civil affairs and other relevant departments under the people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, perform well the employment work for persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　中国残疾人联合会及其地方组织依照法律、法规或者接受政府委托，负责残疾人就业工作的具体组织实施与监督。  
工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会，应当在各自的工作范围内，做好残疾人就业工作。

**Article 6** The China Disabled Persons' Federation and its local organizations shall, in accordance with laws and regulations or upon entrustment by the government, be responsible for the specific organization, implementation and supervision of the employment work for persons with disabilities.  
The trade unions, the Communist Youth League and the Women's Federation shall, within their respective scopes of work, perform well the employment work for persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级人民政府对在残疾人就业工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 7** The people's governments at all levels shall commend and reward units and individuals that have made remarkable achievements in the employment work for persons with disabilities.

### 第二章　用人单位的责任

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　用人单位应当按照一定比例安排残疾人就业，并为其提供适当的工种、岗位。  
用人单位安排残疾人就业的比例不得低于本单位在职职工总数的1.5%。具体比例由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据本地区的实际情况规定。  
用人单位跨地区招用残疾人的，应当计入所安排的残疾人职工人数之内。

**Article 8** Employing units shall arrange employment for persons with disabilities in a certain proportion and provide them with appropriate types of work and positions.  
The proportion of persons with disabilities employed by an employing unit shall not be less than 1.5% of the total number of on-the-job employees of that unit. The specific proportion shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government according to the actual conditions of their respective regions.  
Where an employing unit recruits persons with disabilities across regions, such persons shall be counted in the number of disabled employees arranged by the unit.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　用人单位安排残疾人就业达不到其所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定比例的，应当缴纳残疾人就业保障金。

**Article 9** Where an employing unit fails to meet the proportion prescribed by the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, it shall pay a surcharge for guaranteeing the employment of persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　政府和社会依法兴办的残疾人福利企业、盲人按摩机构和其他福利性单位(以下统称集中使用残疾人的用人单位)，应当集中安排残疾人就业。  
集中使用残疾人的用人单位的资格认定，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 10** Welfare enterprises for persons with disabilities, massage institutions for the blind and other welfare units established by the government and society according to law (hereinafter collectively referred to as employing units that intensively employ persons with disabilities) shall intensively arrange employment for persons with disabilities.  
The qualification recognition of employing units that intensively employ persons with disabilities shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　集中使用残疾人的用人单位中从事全日制工作的残疾人职工，应当占本单位在职职工总数的25%以上。

**Article 11** The number of disabled employees engaged in full-time work in an employing unit that intensively employs persons with disabilities shall account for not less than 25% of the total number of on-the-job employees of that unit.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　用人单位招用残疾人职工，应当依法与其签订劳动合同或者服务协议。

**Article 12** Where an employing unit recruits disabled employees, it shall sign a labor contract or a service agreement with them according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　用人单位应当为残疾人职工提供适合其身体状况的劳动条件和劳动保护，不得在晋职、晋级、评定职称、报酬、社会保险、生活福利等方面歧视残疾人职工。

**Article 13** Employing units shall provide disabled employees with working conditions and labor protection suitable to their physical conditions, and shall not discriminate against disabled employees in terms of promotion, job advancement, professional title evaluation, remuneration, social insurance, welfare, etc.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　用人单位应当根据本单位残疾人职工的实际情况，对残疾人职工进行上岗、在岗、转岗等培训。

**Article 14** Employing units shall, based on the actual conditions of their disabled employees, provide training for disabled employees on job entry, on-the-job and job transfer, etc.

### 第三章　保障措施

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级以上人民政府应当采取措施，拓宽残疾人就业渠道，开发适合残疾人就业的公益性岗位，保障残疾人就业。  
县级以上地方人民政府发展社区服务事业，应当优先考虑残疾人就业。

**Article 15** The people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to broaden employment channels for persons with disabilities, develop public welfare positions suitable for employment of persons with disabilities, and ensure employment of persons with disabilities.  
Where the local people's governments at or above the county level develop community service undertakings, they shall give priority to the employment of persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　依法征收的残疾人就业保障金应当纳入财政预算，专项用于残疾人职业培训以及为残疾人提供就业服务和就业援助，任何组织或者个人不得贪污、挪用、截留或者私分。残疾人就业保障金征收、使用、管理的具体办法，由国务院财政部门会同国务院有关部门规定。  
财政部门和审计机关应当依法加强对残疾人就业保障金使用情况的监督检查。

**Article 16** The employment security fund for persons with disabilities collected according to law shall be incorporated into the financial budget and used exclusively for vocational training for persons with disabilities and for providing employment services and employment assistance for persons with disabilities. No organization or individual shall embezzle, misappropriate, intercept or privately distribute the fund. The specific measures for the collection, use and management of the employment security fund for persons with disabilities shall be prescribed by the finance department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.  
The finance departments and audit authorities shall, according to law, strengthen supervision and inspection over the use of the employment security fund for persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家对集中使用残疾人的用人单位依法给予税收优惠，并在生产、经营、技术、资金、物资、场地使用等方面给予扶持。

**Article 17** The State shall grant tax preferences according to law to employing units that intensively employ persons with disabilities, and shall provide support in production, operation, technology, funds, materials, site use, etc.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当确定适合残疾人生产、经营的产品、项目，优先安排集中使用残疾人的用人单位生产或者经营，并根据集中使用残疾人的用人单位的生产特点确定某些产品由其专产。  
政府采购，在同等条件下，应当优先购买集中使用残疾人的用人单位的产品或者服务。

**Article 18** The local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall determine products and projects suitable for production and operation by persons with disabilities, give priority to employing units that intensively employ persons with disabilities to produce or operate them, and determine certain products to be exclusively produced by such employing units according to their production characteristics.  
Government procurement shall, under the same conditions, give priority to purchasing products or services from employing units that intensively employ persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家鼓励扶持残疾人自主择业、自主创业。对残疾人从事个体经营的，应当依法给予税收优惠，有关部门应当在经营场地等方面给予照顾，并按照规定免收管理类、登记类和证照类的行政事业性收费。  
国家对自主择业、自主创业的残疾人在一定期限内给予小额信贷等扶持。

**Article 19** The State encourages and supports persons with disabilities to choose their own careers and start their own businesses. Where persons with disabilities engage in self-employment, tax preferences shall be granted according to law, and the relevant departments shall give considerations in terms of business premises, etc., and shall exempt administrative and institutional charges of management, registration and license categories according to regulations.  
The State shall grant support such as micro-credit to persons with disabilities who choose their own careers and start their own businesses within a certain period.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　地方各级人民政府应当多方面筹集资金，组织和扶持农村残疾人从事种植业、养殖业、手工业和其他形式的生产劳动。  
有关部门对从事农业生产劳动的农村残疾人，应当在生产服务、技术指导、农用物资供应、农副产品收购和信贷等方面给予帮助。

**Article 20** The local people's governments at all levels shall raise funds from various sources, organize and support rural persons with disabilities to engage in planting, breeding, handicrafts and other forms of productive labor.  
The relevant departments shall provide assistance to rural persons with disabilities engaged in agricultural production in terms of production services, technical guidance, supply of agricultural materials, purchase of agricultural and sideline products, credit, etc.

### 第四章　就业服务

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　各级人民政府和有关部门应当为就业困难的残疾人提供有针对性的就业援助服务，鼓励和扶持职业培训机构为残疾人提供职业培训，并组织残疾人定期开展职业技能竞赛。

**Article 21** The people's governments at all levels and the relevant departments shall provide targeted employment assistance services for persons with disabilities with employment difficulties, encourage and support vocational training institutions to provide vocational training for persons with disabilities, and organize persons with disabilities to regularly hold vocational skill competitions.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　中国残疾人联合会及其地方组织所属的残疾人就业服务机构应当免费为残疾人就业提供下列服务：  
(一)发布残疾人就业信息；  
(二)组织开展残疾人职业培训；  
(三)为残疾人提供职业心理咨询、职业适应评估、职业康复训练、求职定向指导、职业介绍等服务；  
(四)为残疾人自主择业提供必要的帮助；  
(五)为用人单位安排残疾人就业提供必要的支持。  
国家鼓励其他就业服务机构为残疾人就业提供免费服务。

**Article 22** The employment service institutions for persons with disabilities affiliated with the China Disabled Persons' Federation and its local organizations shall provide the following services free of charge for the employment of persons with disabilities:  
(1) Publish employment information for persons with disabilities;  
(2) Organize and carry out vocational training for persons with disabilities;  
(3) Provide services such as vocational psychological counseling, vocational adaptability assessment, vocational rehabilitation training, job-seeking orientation guidance, and job introduction for persons with disabilities;  
(4) Provide necessary assistance for persons with disabilities to choose their own careers;  
(5) Provide necessary support for employing units to arrange employment for persons with disabilities.  
The State encourages other employment service institutions to provide free services for the employment of persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　受劳动保障部门的委托，残疾人就业服务机构可以进行残疾人失业登记、残疾人就业与失业统计；经所在地劳动保障部门批准，残疾人就业服务机构还可以进行残疾人职业技能鉴定。

**Article 23** Upon entrustment by the labor security department, the employment service institutions for persons with disabilities may conduct unemployment registration for persons with disabilities and statistics on employment and unemployment of persons with disabilities; with the approval of the local labor security department, such institutions may also conduct vocational skill appraisal for persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　残疾人职工与用人单位发生争议的，当地法律援助机构应当依法为其提供法律援助，各级残疾人联合会应当给予支持和帮助。

**Article 24** Where a dispute arises between a disabled employee and an employing unit, the local legal aid institution shall provide legal aid to the disabled employee according to law, and the disabled persons' federations at all levels shall give support and assistance.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　违反本条例规定，有关行政主管部门及其工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 25** Where any relevant administrative department or its staff member, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, abuses power, neglects duty, or engages in malpractices for personal gain, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, a sanction shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反本条例规定，贪污、挪用、截留、私分残疾人就业保障金，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，对有关责任单位、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分或者处罚。

**Article 26** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, any person embezzles, misappropriates, intercepts or privately distributes the employment security fund for persons with disabilities, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, a sanction or penalty shall be imposed according to law on the relevant responsible units, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反本条例规定，用人单位未按照规定缴纳残疾人就业保障金的，由财政部门给予警告，责令限期缴纳；逾期仍不缴纳的，除补缴欠缴数额外，还应当自欠缴之日起，按日加收5‰的滞纳金。

**Article 27** Where an employing unit, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, fails to pay the employment security fund for persons with disabilities as required, the finance department shall give a warning and order it to make the payment within a time limit; if it still fails to pay upon expiry of the time limit, in addition to paying the overdue amount, it shall also pay a surcharge of 5‰ per day calculated from the date the payment became overdue.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　违反本条例规定，用人单位弄虚作假，虚报安排残疾人就业人数，骗取集中使用残疾人的用人单位享受的税收优惠待遇的，由税务机关依法处理。

**Article 28** Where an employing unit, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, practices fraud by falsely reporting the number of persons with disabilities it has employed, so as to fraudulently obtain the preferential tax treatment enjoyed by employing units that intensively employ persons with disabilities, the tax authorities shall handle the matter according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本条例所称残疾人就业，是指符合法定就业年龄有就业要求的残疾人从事有报酬的劳动。

**Article 29** For the purposes of these Regulations, employment of persons with disabilities means that persons with disabilities who meet the statutory working age and have employment requirements engage in remunerative labor.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本条例自2007年5月1日起施行。

**Article 30** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2007.
