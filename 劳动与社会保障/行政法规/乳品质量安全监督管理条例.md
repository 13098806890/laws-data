# 乳品质量安全监督管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2008-10-09  
**生效日期**：2008-10-09

> (2008年10月6日国务院第28次常务会议通过　2008年10月9日中华人民共和国国务院令第536号公布　自公布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强乳品质量安全监督管理，保证乳品质量安全，保障公众身体健康和生命安全，促进奶业健康发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称乳品，是指生鲜乳和乳制品。 乳品质量安全监督管理适用本条例；法律对乳品质量安全监督管理另有规定的，从其规定。 第三条　奶畜养殖者、生鲜乳收购者、乳制品生产企业和销售者对其生产、收购、运输、销售的乳品质量安全负责，是乳品质量安全的第一责任者。 第四条　县级以上地方人民政府对本行政区域内的乳品质量安全监督管理负总责。 县级以上人民政府畜牧兽医主管部门负责奶畜饲养以及生鲜乳生产环节、收购环节的监督管理。县级以上质量监督检验检疫部门负责乳制品生产环节和乳品进出口环节的监督管理。县级以上工商行政管理部门负责乳制品销售环节的监督管理。县级以上食品药品监督部门负责乳制品餐饮服务环节的监督管理。县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门依照职权负责乳品质量安全监督管理的综合协调、组织查处食品安全重大事故。县级以上人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责乳品质量安全监督管理的其他工作。 第五条　发生乳品质量安全事故，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定及时报告、处理；造成严重后果或者恶劣影响的，对有关人民政府、有关部门负有领导责任的负责人依法追究责任。 第六条　生鲜乳和乳制品应当符合乳品质量安全国家标准。乳品质量安全国家标准由国务院卫生主管部门组织制定，并根据风险监测和风险评估的结果及时组织修订。 乳品质量安全国家标准应当包括乳品中的致病性微生物、农药残留、兽药残留、重金属以及其他危害人体健康物质的限量规定，乳品生产经营过程的卫生要求，通用的乳品检验方法与规程，与乳品安全有关的质量要求，以及其他需要制定为乳品质量安全国家标准的内容。 制定婴幼儿奶粉的质量安全国家标准应当充分考虑婴幼儿身体特点和生长发育需要，保证婴幼儿生长发育所需的营养成分。 国务院卫生主管部门应当根据疾病信息和监督管理部门的监督管理信息等，对发现添加或者可能添加到乳品中的非食品用化学物质和其他可能危害人体健康的物质，立即组织进行风险评估，采取相应的监测、检测和监督措施。 第七条　禁止在生鲜乳生产、收购、贮存、运输、销售过程中添加任何物质。 禁止在乳制品生产过程中添加非食品用化学物质或者其他可能危害人体健康的物质。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强乳品质量安全监督管理，保证乳品质量安全，保障公众身体健康和生命安全，促进奶业健康发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the supervision and administration of dairy product quality and safety, ensuring dairy product quality and safety, safeguarding public health and life safety, and promoting the healthy development of the dairy industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称乳品，是指生鲜乳和乳制品。  
乳品质量安全监督管理适用本条例；法律对乳品质量安全监督管理另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, “dairy products” mean raw fresh milk and dairy products.  
The supervision and administration of dairy product quality and safety shall be governed by these Regulations; where other provisions are prescribed by law, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　奶畜养殖者、生鲜乳收购者、乳制品生产企业和销售者对其生产、收购、运输、销售的乳品质量安全负责，是乳品质量安全的第一责任者。

**Article 3** Dairy livestock breeders, raw fresh milk purchasers, dairy product production enterprises, and sellers shall be responsible for the quality and safety of the dairy products they produce, purchase, transport, or sell, and they shall be the primary persons liable for dairy product quality and safety.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上地方人民政府对本行政区域内的乳品质量安全监督管理负总责。  
县级以上人民政府畜牧兽医主管部门负责奶畜饲养以及生鲜乳生产环节、收购环节的监督管理。县级以上质量监督检验检疫部门负责乳制品生产环节和乳品进出口环节的监督管理。县级以上工商行政管理部门负责乳制品销售环节的监督管理。县级以上食品药品监督部门负责乳制品餐饮服务环节的监督管理。县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门依照职权负责乳品质量安全监督管理的综合协调、组织查处食品安全重大事故。县级以上人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责乳品质量安全监督管理的其他工作。

**Article 4** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall assume overall responsibility for the supervision and administration of dairy product quality and safety within their respective administrative regions.  
The competent administrative departments for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the feeding of dairy livestock and the production and purchase of raw fresh milk. The quality supervision, inspection and quarantine departments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the production of dairy products and the import and export of dairy products. The administrative departments for industry and commerce at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the sale of dairy products. The food and drug supervision departments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the catering service of dairy products. The competent health administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their functions and powers, be responsible for comprehensive coordination of the supervision and administration of dairy product quality and safety, and for organizing the investigation and handling of major food safety accidents. Other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for other work relating to the supervision and administration of dairy product quality and safety within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　发生乳品质量安全事故，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定及时报告、处理；造成严重后果或者恶劣影响的，对有关人民政府、有关部门负有领导责任的负责人依法追究责任。

**Article 5** Where a dairy product quality and safety accident occurs, it shall be reported and handled in a timely manner in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; if serious consequences or adverse effects are caused, the leaders of the relevant people's governments and relevant departments who bear the responsibility for leadership shall be investigated for liability according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　生鲜乳和乳制品应当符合乳品质量安全国家标准。乳品质量安全国家标准由国务院卫生主管部门组织制定，并根据风险监测和风险评估的结果及时组织修订。  
乳品质量安全国家标准应当包括乳品中的致病性微生物、农药残留、兽药残留、重金属以及其他危害人体健康物质的限量规定，乳品生产经营过程的卫生要求，通用的乳品检验方法与规程，与乳品安全有关的质量要求，以及其他需要制定为乳品质量安全国家标准的内容。  
制定婴幼儿奶粉的质量安全国家标准应当充分考虑婴幼儿身体特点和生长发育需要，保证婴幼儿生长发育所需的营养成分。  
国务院卫生主管部门应当根据疾病信息和监督管理部门的监督管理信息等，对发现添加或者可能添加到乳品中的非食品用化学物质和其他可能危害人体健康的物质，立即组织进行风险评估，采取相应的监测、检测和监督措施。

**Article 6** Raw fresh milk and dairy products shall comply with the national standards for dairy product quality and safety. The national standards for dairy product quality and safety shall be formulated by the competent health administrative department of the State Council in an organized manner, and shall be promptly revised based on the results of risk monitoring and risk assessment.  
The national standards for dairy product quality and safety shall include the limit provisions for pathogenic microorganisms, pesticide residues, veterinary drug residues, heavy metals, and other substances harmful to human health in dairy products; the hygiene requirements for the production and operation of dairy products; the general inspection methods and procedures for dairy products; the quality requirements relating to dairy product safety; and other contents that need to be formulated as national standards for dairy product quality and safety.  
When formulating the national quality and safety standards for infant milk powder, full consideration shall be given to the physical characteristics and growth and development needs of infants, so as to ensure the nutrients necessary for the growth and development of infants.  
The competent health administrative department of the State Council shall, based on disease information and the supervision and administration information of the regulatory departments, promptly organize a risk assessment of any non-food chemical substances and other substances that may be harmful to human health that have been found to be added or may be added to dairy products, and adopt corresponding monitoring, testing, and supervision measures.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　禁止在生鲜乳生产、收购、贮存、运输、销售过程中添加任何物质。  
禁止在乳制品生产过程中添加非食品用化学物质或者其他可能危害人体健康的物质。

**Article 7** It is prohibited to add any substance during the production, purchase, storage, transportation, or sale of raw fresh milk.  
It is prohibited to add non-food chemical substances or other substances that may be harmful to human health during the production of dairy products.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院畜牧兽医主管部门会同国务院发展改革部门、工业和信息化部门、商务部门，制定全国奶业发展规划，加强奶源基地建设，完善服务体系，促进奶业健康发展。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当根据全国奶业发展规划，合理确定本行政区域内奶畜养殖规模，科学安排生鲜乳的生产、收购布局。

**Article 8** The competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council shall, in conjunction with the development and reform department, the department of industry and information technology, and the commerce department of the State Council, formulate a national dairy industry development plan, strengthen the construction of milk source bases, improve the service system, and promote the healthy development of the dairy industry.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the national dairy industry development plan, rationally determine the scale of dairy livestock breeding within their respective administrative regions, and scientifically arrange the production and purchase layout of raw fresh milk.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　有关行业协会应当加强行业自律，推动行业诚信建设，引导、规范奶畜养殖者、生鲜乳收购者、乳制品生产企业和销售者依法生产经营。

**Article 9** Relevant trade associations shall strengthen industry self-discipline, promote the construction of industry integrity, and guide and regulate dairy livestock breeders, raw fresh milk purchasers, dairy product production enterprises, and sellers to produce and operate according to law.

### 第二章　奶畜养殖

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家采取有效措施，鼓励、引导、扶持奶畜养殖者提高生鲜乳质量安全水平。省级以上人民政府应当在本级财政预算内安排支持奶业发展资金，并鼓励对奶畜养殖者、奶农专业生产合作社等给予信贷支持。  
国家建立奶畜政策性保险制度，对参保奶畜养殖者给予保费补助。

**Article 10** The State shall take effective measures to encourage, guide, and support dairy livestock breeders in improving the quality and safety level of raw fresh milk. The people's governments at or above the provincial level shall allocate funds to support the development of the dairy industry within their respective fiscal budgets, and shall encourage the provision of credit support to dairy livestock breeders, specialized dairy farmer production cooperatives, and the like.  
The State shall establish a policy-based insurance system for dairy livestock, providing premium subsidies to dairy livestock breeders who participate in the insurance.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　畜牧兽医技术推广机构应当向奶畜养殖者提供养殖技术培训、良种推广、疫病防治等服务。  
国家鼓励乳制品生产企业和其他相关生产经营者为奶畜养殖者提供所需的服务。

**Article 11** Animal husbandry and veterinary technology extension institutions shall provide dairy livestock breeders with services such as breeding technical training, improved variety promotion, and epidemic disease prevention and control.  
The State encourages dairy product production enterprises and other relevant production and operation entities to provide necessary services to dairy livestock breeders.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　设立奶畜养殖场、养殖小区应当具备下列条件：  
(一)符合所在地人民政府确定的本行政区域奶畜养殖规模；  
(二)有与其养殖规模相适应的场所和配套设施；  
(三)有为其服务的畜牧兽医技术人员；  
(四)具备法律、行政法规和国务院畜牧兽医主管部门规定的防疫条件；  
(五)有对奶畜粪便、废水和其他固体废物进行综合利用的沼气池等设施或者其他无害化处理设施；  
(六)有生鲜乳生产、销售、运输管理制度；  
(七)法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。  
奶畜养殖场、养殖小区开办者应当将养殖场、养殖小区的名称、养殖地址、奶畜品种和养殖规模向养殖场、养殖小区所在地县级人民政府畜牧兽医主管部门备案。

**Article 12** The establishment of a dairy livestock farm or breeding zone shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Conforming to the scale of dairy livestock breeding within the administrative region as determined by the local people's government;  
(2) Having premises and supporting facilities appropriate to the breeding scale;  
(3) Having animal husbandry and veterinary technicians to serve it;  
(4) Meeting the epidemic prevention conditions prescribed by laws, administrative regulations, and the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council;  
(5) Having facilities such as biogas digesters or other harmless treatment facilities for the comprehensive utilization of dairy livestock manure, wastewater, and other solid waste;  
(6) Having management systems for the production, sale, and transportation of raw fresh milk;  
(7) Other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
The initiator of a dairy livestock farm or breeding zone shall file a record with the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the people's government at the county level where the farm or breeding zone is located, providing the name of the farm or breeding zone, the breeding address, the breeds of dairy livestock, and the breeding scale.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　奶畜养殖场应当建立养殖档案，载明以下内容：  
(一)奶畜的品种、数量、繁殖记录、标识情况、来源和进出场日期；  
(二)饲料、饲料添加剂、兽药等投入品的来源、名称、使用对象、时间和用量；  
(三)检疫、免疫、消毒情况；  
(四)奶畜发病、死亡和无害化处理情况；  
(五)生鲜乳生产、检测、销售情况；  
(六)国务院畜牧兽医主管部门规定的其他内容。  
奶畜养殖小区开办者应当逐步建立养殖档案。

**Article 13** A dairy livestock farm shall establish breeding files, recording the following:  
(1) The breeds, quantity, breeding records, identification status, sources, and dates of entry and exit of dairy livestock;  
(2) The sources, names, objects of use, time, and dosage of input items such as feed, feed additives, and veterinary drugs;  
(3) The status of quarantine, immunization, and disinfection;  
(4) The incidence of disease, death, and harmless treatment of dairy livestock;  
(5) The production, testing, and sale of raw fresh milk;  
(6) Other contents prescribed by the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council.  
The initiator of a dairy livestock breeding zone shall gradually establish breeding files.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　从事奶畜养殖，不得使用国家禁用的饲料、饲料添加剂、兽药以及其他对动物和人体具有直接或者潜在危害的物质。  
禁止销售在规定用药期和休药期内的奶畜产的生鲜乳。

**Article 14** In engaging in dairy livestock breeding, it is prohibited to use feed, feed additives, veterinary drugs, or other substances that are banned by the State or that have direct or potential harm to animals and humans.  
It shall be prohibited to sell raw fresh milk produced by dairy livestock that are within the prescribed period of medication or withdrawal period.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　奶畜养殖者应当确保奶畜符合国务院畜牧兽医主管部门规定的健康标准，并确保奶畜接受强制免疫。  
动物疫病预防控制机构应当对奶畜的健康情况进行定期检测；经检测不符合健康标准的，应当立即隔离、治疗或者做无害化处理。

**Article 15** Dairy livestock breeders shall ensure that dairy livestock comply with the health standards prescribed by the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council, and shall ensure that dairy livestock receive compulsory immunization.  
Animal epidemic disease prevention and control institutions shall conduct regular testing of the health condition of dairy livestock; if any dairy livestock are found through testing not to comply with the health standards, they shall be immediately isolated, treated, or subjected to harmless treatment.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　奶畜养殖者应当做好奶畜和养殖场所的动物防疫工作，发现奶畜染疫或者疑似染疫的，应当立即报告，停止生鲜乳生产，并采取隔离等控制措施，防止疫病扩散。  
奶畜养殖者对奶畜养殖过程中的排泄物、废弃物应当及时清运、处理。

**Article 16** Dairy livestock breeders shall properly carry out animal epidemic prevention work for dairy livestock and breeding premises. If any dairy livestock are found to be infected or suspected of being infected with an epidemic, the breeder shall immediately report the matter, stop the production of raw fresh milk, and take control measures such as isolation to prevent the spread of the epidemic.  
Dairy livestock breeders shall promptly remove and dispose of excreta and waste generated during the breeding process.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　奶畜养殖者应当遵守国务院畜牧兽医主管部门制定的生鲜乳生产技术规程。直接从事挤奶工作的人员应当持有有效的健康证明。  
奶畜养殖者对挤奶设施、生鲜乳贮存设施等应当及时清洗、消毒，避免对生鲜乳造成污染。

**Article 17** Dairy livestock breeders shall comply with the technical procedures for raw fresh milk production formulated by the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council. Persons directly engaged in milking shall hold valid health certificates.  
Dairy livestock breeders shall promptly clean and disinfect milking facilities, raw fresh milk storage facilities, and the like, so as to avoid contaminating the raw fresh milk.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　生鲜乳应当冷藏。超过2小时未冷藏的生鲜乳，不得销售。

**Article 18** Raw fresh milk shall be refrigerated. Raw fresh milk that has not been refrigerated for more than two hours shall not be sold.

### 第三章　生鲜乳收购

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府畜牧兽医主管部门应当根据当地奶源分布情况，按照方便奶畜养殖者、促进规模化养殖的原则，对生鲜乳收购站的建设进行科学规划和合理布局。必要时，可以实行生鲜乳集中定点收购。  
国家鼓励乳制品生产企业按照规划布局，自行建设生鲜乳收购站或者收购原有生鲜乳收购站。

**Article 19** The competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, based on the distribution of local milk sources and in accordance with the principle of facilitating dairy livestock breeders and promoting scaled breeding, carry out scientific planning and rational layout for the construction of raw fresh milk purchasing stations. Where necessary, centralized fixed-point purchase of raw fresh milk may be implemented.  
The State encourages dairy product production enterprises to, in accordance with the planning layout, construct raw fresh milk purchasing stations themselves or acquire existing raw fresh milk purchasing stations.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　生鲜乳收购站应当由取得工商登记的乳制品生产企业、奶畜养殖场、奶农专业生产合作社开办，并具备下列条件，取得所在地县级人民政府畜牧兽医主管部门颁发的生鲜乳收购许可证：  
(一)符合生鲜乳收购站建设规划布局；  
(二)有符合环保和卫生要求的收购场所；  
(三)有与收奶量相适应的冷却、冷藏、保鲜设施和低温运输设备；  
(四)有与检测项目相适应的化验、计量、检测仪器设备；  
(五)有经培训合格并持有有效健康证明的从业人员；  
(六)有卫生管理和质量安全保障制度。  
生鲜乳收购许可证有效期2年；生鲜乳收购站不再办理工商登记。  
禁止其他单位或者个人开办生鲜乳收购站。禁止其他单位或者个人收购生鲜乳。  
国家对生鲜乳收购站给予扶持和补贴，提高其机械化挤奶和生鲜乳冷藏运输能力。

**Article 20** A raw fresh milk purchasing station shall be established by a dairy product production enterprise, a dairy livestock farm, or a specialized dairy farmer production cooperative that has obtained an industrial and commercial registration, and shall meet the following conditions, and shall obtain a raw fresh milk purchasing permit issued by the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the people's government at the county level where it is located:  
(1) Complying with the planned layout for the construction of raw fresh milk purchasing stations;  
(2) Having a purchasing site that meets environmental protection and hygiene requirements;  
(3) Having cooling, refrigeration, freshness-keeping facilities and low-temperature transportation equipment appropriate to the quantity of milk purchased;  
(4) Having testing, measuring, and inspection instruments and equipment appropriate to the items to be tested;  
(5) Having personnel who have passed training and hold valid health certificates;  
(6) Having a hygiene management and quality safety guarantee system.  
The raw fresh milk purchasing permit shall be valid for two years; a raw fresh milk purchasing station shall not undergo separate industrial and commercial registration.  
No other unit or individual may establish a raw fresh milk purchasing station. No other unit or individual may purchase raw fresh milk.  
The State shall provide support and subsidies to raw fresh milk purchasing stations to improve their capacity for mechanized milking and refrigerated transportation of raw fresh milk.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　生鲜乳收购站应当及时对挤奶设施、生鲜乳贮存运输设施等进行清洗、消毒，避免对生鲜乳造成污染。  
生鲜乳收购站应当按照乳品质量安全国家标准对收购的生鲜乳进行常规检测。检测费用不得向奶畜养殖者收取。  
生鲜乳收购站应当保持生鲜乳的质量。

**Article 21** A raw fresh milk purchasing station shall promptly clean and disinfect milking facilities, raw fresh milk storage and transportation facilities, and the like, so as to avoid contaminating the raw fresh milk.  
A raw fresh milk purchasing station shall conduct routine testing on the raw fresh milk it purchases in accordance with the national standards for dairy product quality and safety. No testing fee shall be charged to the dairy livestock breeder.  
A raw fresh milk purchasing station shall maintain the quality of the raw fresh milk.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　生鲜乳收购站应当建立生鲜乳收购、销售和检测记录。生鲜乳收购、销售和检测记录应当包括畜主姓名、单次收购量、生鲜乳检测结果、销售去向等内容，并保存2年。

**Article 22** A raw fresh milk purchasing station shall establish records of the purchase, sale, and testing of raw fresh milk. The records of purchase, sale, and testing of raw fresh milk shall include the name of the livestock owner, the quantity purchased each time, the test results of the raw fresh milk, the sales destination, and the like, and shall be kept for two years.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　县级以上地方人民政府价格主管部门应当加强对生鲜乳价格的监控和通报，及时发布市场供求信息和价格信息。必要时，县级以上地方人民政府建立由价格、畜牧兽医等部门以及行业协会、乳制品生产企业、生鲜乳收购者、奶畜养殖者代表组成的生鲜乳价格协调委员会，确定生鲜乳交易参考价格，供购销双方签订合同时参考。  
生鲜乳购销双方应当签订书面合同。生鲜乳购销合同示范文本由国务院畜牧兽医主管部门会同国务院工商行政管理部门制定并公布。

**Article 23** The competent price departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the monitoring and reporting of the price of raw fresh milk, and shall promptly publish market supply and demand information and price information. Where necessary, the local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a raw fresh milk price coordination committee composed of representatives from the price, animal husbandry and veterinary medicine departments, as well as trade associations, dairy product production enterprises, raw fresh milk purchasers, and dairy livestock breeders, to determine the reference price for raw fresh milk transactions, for reference by the purchasing and selling parties when signing contracts.  
The purchasing and selling parties of raw fresh milk shall sign a written contract. The model text for the raw fresh milk purchase and sale contract shall be formulated and published by the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council in conjunction with the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　禁止收购下列生鲜乳：  
(一)经检测不符合健康标准或者未经检疫合格的奶畜产的；  
(二)奶畜产犊7日内的初乳，但以初乳为原料从事乳制品生产的除外；  
(三)在规定用药期和休药期内的奶畜产的；  
(四)其他不符合乳品质量安全国家标准的。  
对前款规定的生鲜乳，经检测无误后，应当予以销毁或者采取其他无害化处理措施。

**Article 24** It is prohibited to purchase the following raw fresh milk:  
(1) Produced by dairy livestock that, after testing, do not comply with health standards or have not passed quarantine;  
(2) Colostrum produced within seven days after a dairy livestock gives birth, except where colostrum is used as a raw material for dairy product production;  
(3) Produced by dairy livestock that are within the prescribed period of medication or withdrawal period;  
(4) Other raw fresh milk that does not comply with the national standards for dairy product quality and safety.  
The raw fresh milk specified in the preceding paragraph, after being confirmed through testing, shall be destroyed or subjected to other harmless treatment measures.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　贮存生鲜乳的容器，应当符合国家有关卫生标准，在挤奶后2小时内应当降温至0－4℃。  
生鲜乳运输车辆应当取得所在地县级人民政府畜牧兽医主管部门核发的生鲜乳准运证明，并随车携带生鲜乳交接单。交接单应当载明生鲜乳收购站的名称、生鲜乳数量、交接时间，并由生鲜乳收购站经手人、押运员、司机、收奶员签字。  
生鲜乳交接单一式两份，分别由生鲜乳收购站和乳品生产者保存，保存时间2年。准运证明和交接单式样由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府畜牧兽医主管部门制定。

**Article 25** The containers for storing raw fresh milk shall comply with the relevant hygiene standards of the State, and the temperature shall be reduced to 0-4°C within two hours after milking.  
Vehicles transporting raw fresh milk shall obtain a raw fresh milk transportation permit issued by the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the people's government at the county level where the vehicle is located, and shall carry a raw fresh milk delivery receipt with the vehicle. The delivery receipt shall indicate the name of the raw fresh milk purchasing station, the quantity of raw fresh milk, and the time of delivery, and shall be signed by the handler of the raw fresh milk purchasing station, the escort, the driver, and the milk receiver.  
The raw fresh milk delivery receipt shall be made in duplicate, which shall be kept by the raw fresh milk purchasing station and the dairy product producer respectively, for a period of two years. The formats of the transportation permit and the delivery receipt shall be formulated by the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上人民政府应当加强生鲜乳质量安全监测体系建设，配备相应的人员和设备，确保监测能力与监测任务相适应。

**Article 26** The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of the raw fresh milk quality and safety monitoring system, allocate corresponding personnel and equipment, and ensure that the monitoring capacity is commensurate with the monitoring tasks.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上人民政府畜牧兽医主管部门应当加强生鲜乳质量安全监测工作，制定并组织实施生鲜乳质量安全监测计划，对生鲜乳进行监督抽查，并按照法定权限及时公布监督抽查结果。  
监测抽查不得向被抽查人收取任何费用，所需费用由同级财政列支。

**Article 27** The competent administrative departments for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the monitoring of raw fresh milk quality and safety, formulate and organize the implementation of raw fresh milk quality and safety monitoring plans, conduct supervisory spot checks on raw fresh milk, and promptly publish the results of such supervisory spot checks in accordance with the statutory authority.  
No fees shall be charged to the persons subject to such monitoring spot checks, and the necessary expenses shall be covered by the fiscal budget at the same level.

### 第四章　乳制品生产

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　从事乳制品生产活动，应当具备下列条件，取得所在地质量监督部门颁发的食品生产许可证：  
(一)符合国家奶业产业政策；  
(二)厂房的选址和设计符合国家有关规定；  
(三)有与所生产的乳制品品种和数量相适应的生产、包装和检测设备；  
(四)有相应的专业技术人员和质量检验人员；  
(五)有符合环保要求的废水、废气、垃圾等污染物的处理设施；  
(六)有经培训合格并持有有效健康证明的从业人员；  
(七)法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。  
质量监督部门对乳制品生产企业颁发食品生产许可证，应当征求所在地工业行业管理部门的意见。  
未取得食品生产许可证的任何单位和个人，不得从事乳制品生产。

**Article 28** To engage in dairy product production activities, the following conditions shall be met, and a food production permit issued by the local quality supervision department shall be obtained:  
(1) Complying with the national dairy industry policy;  
(2) The site selection and design of the factory premises comply with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(3) Having production, packaging, and testing equipment appropriate to the variety and quantity of dairy products produced;  
(4) Having corresponding professional technicians and quality inspectors;  
(5) Having treatment facilities for pollutants such as wastewater, waste gas, and garbage that meet environmental protection requirements;  
(6) Having personnel who have passed training and hold valid health certificates;  
(7) Other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
When issuing a food production permit to a dairy product production enterprise, the quality supervision department shall solicit the opinions of the local industrial sector management department.  
No unit or individual that has not obtained a food production permit may engage in the production of dairy products.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　乳制品生产企业应当建立质量管理制度，采取质量安全管理措施，对乳制品生产实施从原料进厂到成品出厂的全过程质量控制，保证产品质量安全。

**Article 29** A dairy product production enterprise shall establish a quality management system, adopt quality and safety management measures, and implement whole-process quality control over dairy product production from the entry of raw materials into the factory to the exit of finished products, so as to ensure product quality and safety.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　乳制品生产企业应当符合良好生产规范要求。国家鼓励乳制品生产企业实施危害分析与关键控制点体系，提高乳制品安全管理水平。生产婴幼儿奶粉的企业应当实施危害分析与关键控制点体系。  
对通过良好生产规范、危害分析与关键控制点体系认证的乳制品生产企业，认证机构应当依法实施跟踪调查；对不再符合认证要求的企业，应当依法撤销认证，并及时向有关主管部门报告。

**Article 30** A dairy product production enterprise shall comply with the requirements of good manufacturing practice. The State encourages dairy product production enterprises to implement a hazard analysis and critical control point system to improve the level of dairy product safety management. Enterprises producing infant milk powder shall implement a hazard analysis and critical control point system.  
For a dairy product production enterprise that has passed certification for good manufacturing practice and the hazard analysis and critical control point system, the certification body shall conduct follow-up investigations according to law; for an enterprise that no longer meets the certification requirements, the certification shall be revoked according to law, and the relevant competent departments shall be promptly notified.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　乳制品生产企业应当建立生鲜乳进货查验制度，逐批检测收购的生鲜乳，如实记录质量检测情况、供货者的名称以及联系方式、进货日期等内容，并查验运输车辆生鲜乳交接单。查验记录和生鲜乳交接单应当保存2年。乳制品生产企业不得向未取得生鲜乳收购许可证的单位和个人购进生鲜乳。  
乳制品生产企业不得购进兽药等化学物质残留超标，或者含有重金属等有毒有害物质、致病性的寄生虫和微生物、生物毒素以及其他不符合乳品质量安全国家标准的生鲜乳。

**Article 31** A dairy product production enterprise shall establish a raw fresh milk incoming inspection system, test each batch of purchased raw fresh milk, faithfully record the quality test results, the name and contact information of the supplier, the date of purchase, and other information, and inspect the raw fresh milk delivery receipt of the transport vehicle. The inspection records and raw fresh milk delivery receipts shall be kept for two years. A dairy product production enterprise shall not purchase raw fresh milk from any unit or individual that has not obtained a raw fresh milk purchasing permit.  
A dairy product production enterprise shall not purchase raw fresh milk that has excessive residues of chemical substances such as veterinary drugs, or contains toxic or harmful substances such as heavy metals, pathogenic parasites and microorganisms, biotoxins, or other raw fresh milk that does not comply with the national standards for dairy product quality and safety.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　生产乳制品使用的生鲜乳、辅料、添加剂等，应当符合法律、行政法规的规定和乳品质量安全国家标准。  
生产的乳制品应当经过巴氏杀菌、高温杀菌、超高温杀菌或者其他有效方式杀菌。  
生产发酵乳制品的菌种应当纯良、无害，定期鉴定，防止杂菌污染。  
生产婴幼儿奶粉应当保证婴幼儿生长发育所需的营养成分，不得添加任何可能危害婴幼儿身体健康和生长发育的物质。

**Article 32** The raw fresh milk, auxiliary materials, additives, and other substances used in the production of dairy products shall comply with the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, and the national standards for dairy product quality and safety.  
The dairy products produced shall be pasteurized, sterilized at high temperature, sterilized at ultra-high temperature, or sterilized by other effective means.  
The bacterial cultures used in the production of fermented dairy products shall be pure and harmless, and shall be regularly identified to prevent contamination by miscellaneous bacteria.  
The production of infant milk powder shall ensure the nutrients necessary for the growth and development of infants, and no substances that may harm the physical health and growth and development of infants may be added.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　乳制品的包装应当有标签。标签应当如实标明产品名称、规格、净含量、生产日期，成分或者配料表，生产企业的名称、地址、联系方式，保质期，产品标准代号，贮存条件，所使用的食品添加剂的化学通用名称，食品生产许可证编号，法律、行政法规或者乳品质量安全国家标准规定必须标明的其他事项。  
使用奶粉、黄油、乳清粉等原料加工的液态奶，应当在包装上注明；使用复原乳作为原料生产液态奶的，应当标明“复原乳”字样，并在产品配料中如实标明复原乳所含原料及比例。  
婴幼儿奶粉标签还应当标明主要营养成分及其含量，详细说明使用方法和注意事项。

**Article 33** The packaging of dairy products shall bear a label. The label shall truthfully indicate the product name, specifications, net content, date of production, list of ingredients or components, name, address and contact information of the production enterprise, shelf life, product standard code, storage conditions, the common chemical name of any food additives used, the food production permit number, and other matters that must be indicated as prescribed by laws, administrative regulations, or the national standards for dairy product quality and safety.  
Liquid milk processed with raw materials such as milk powder, butter, or whey powder shall be indicated on the packaging; where reconstituted milk is used as a raw material for the production of liquid milk, the characters “reconstituted milk” shall be marked, and the raw materials contained in the reconstituted milk and their proportions shall be truthfully indicated in the product ingredients.  
The label of infant milk powder shall also indicate the main nutritional components and their contents, and provide detailed instructions on usage methods and precautions.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　出厂的乳制品应当符合乳品质量安全国家标准。  
乳制品生产企业应当对出厂的乳制品逐批检验，并保存检验报告，留取样品。检验内容应当包括乳制品的感官指标、理化指标、卫生指标和乳制品中使用的添加剂、稳定剂以及酸奶中使用的菌种等；婴幼儿奶粉在出厂前还应当检测营养成分。对检验合格的乳制品应当标识检验合格证号；检验不合格的不得出厂。检验报告应当保存2年。

**Article 34** Dairy products leaving the factory shall comply with the national standards for dairy product quality and safety.  
A dairy product production enterprise shall test each batch of dairy products leaving the factory, keep the test reports, and retain samples. The test contents shall include the sensory indicators, physicochemical indicators, hygiene indicators, and the additives and stabilizers used in the dairy products, as well as the bacterial cultures used in yogurt, etc.; for infant milk powder, nutritional components shall also be tested before leaving the factory. Dairy products that pass the test shall be marked with a test certificate number; those that fail the test shall not leave the factory. The test reports shall be kept for two years.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　乳制品生产企业应当如实记录销售的乳制品名称、数量、生产日期、生产批号、检验合格证号、购货者名称及其联系方式、销售日期等。

**Article 35** A dairy product production enterprise shall faithfully record the name, quantity, date of production, batch number, test certificate number, name and contact information of the purchaser, and date of sale of the dairy products it sells.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　乳制品生产企业发现其生产的乳制品不符合乳品质量安全国家标准、存在危害人体健康和生命安全危险或者可能危害婴幼儿身体健康或者生长发育的，应当立即停止生产，报告有关主管部门，告知销售者、消费者，召回已经出厂、上市销售的乳制品，并记录召回情况。  
乳制品生产企业对召回的乳制品应当采取销毁、无害化处理等措施，防止其再次流入市场。

**Article 36** Where a dairy product production enterprise discovers that the dairy products it produces do not comply with the national standards for dairy product quality and safety, pose a danger to human health and life safety, or may harm the physical health or growth and development of infants, it shall immediately stop production, report to the relevant competent departments, notify sellers and consumers, recall the dairy products that have left the factory and been placed on the market for sale, and record the recall situation.  
A dairy product production enterprise shall take measures such as destruction and harmless treatment for the recalled dairy products to prevent them from re-entering the market.

### 第五章　乳制品销售

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　从事乳制品销售应当按照食品安全监督管理的有关规定，依法向工商行政管理部门申请领取有关证照。

**Article 37** To engage in the sale of dairy products, the relevant licenses and certificates shall be applied for from the administrative department for industry and commerce according to law, in accordance with the relevant provisions on food safety supervision and administration.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　乳制品销售者应当建立并执行进货查验制度，审验供货商的经营资格，验明乳制品合格证明和产品标识，并建立乳制品进货台账，如实记录乳制品的名称、规格、数量、供货商及其联系方式、进货时间等内容。从事乳制品批发业务的销售企业应当建立乳制品销售台账，如实记录批发的乳制品的品种、规格、数量、流向等内容。进货台账和销售台账保存期限不得少于2年。

**Article 38** A dairy product seller shall establish and implement an incoming goods inspection system, verify the business qualifications of the supplier, check the dairy product conformity certificate and product identification, and establish an incoming goods ledger for dairy products, faithfully recording the name, specifications, quantity, supplier and its contact information, time of purchase, and other information. A sales enterprise engaged in the wholesale of dairy products shall establish a dairy product sales ledger, faithfully recording the variety, specifications, quantity, destination, and other information of the dairy products wholesaled. The incoming goods ledger and the sales ledger shall be kept for a period of not less than two years.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　乳制品销售者应当采取措施，保持所销售乳制品的质量。  
销售需要低温保存的乳制品的，应当配备冷藏设备或者采取冷藏措施。

**Article 39** A dairy product seller shall take measures to maintain the quality of the dairy products it sells.  
Where dairy products that require low-temperature storage are sold, refrigeration equipment shall be provided or refrigeration measures shall be taken.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　禁止购进、销售无质量合格证明、无标签或者标签残缺不清的乳制品。  
禁止购进、销售过期、变质或者不符合乳品质量安全国家标准的乳制品。

**Article 40** It is prohibited to purchase or sell dairy products without a quality conformity certificate, without a label, or with a label that is incomplete or unclear.  
It is prohibited to purchase or sell dairy products that are expired, deteriorated, or that do not comply with the national standards for dairy product quality and safety.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　乳制品销售者不得伪造产地，不得伪造或者冒用他人的厂名、厂址，不得伪造或者冒用认证标志等质量标志。

**Article 41** A dairy product seller shall not forge the place of origin, forge or fraudulently use another person's factory name or address, or forge or fraudulently use quality marks such as certification marks.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　对不符合乳品质量安全国家标准、存在危害人体健康和生命安全或者可能危害婴幼儿身体健康和生长发育的乳制品，销售者应当立即停止销售，追回已经售出的乳制品，并记录追回情况。  
乳制品销售者自行发现其销售的乳制品有前款规定情况的，还应当立即报告所在地工商行政管理等有关部门，通知乳制品生产企业。

**Article 42** With respect to dairy products that do not comply with the national standards for dairy product quality and safety, pose a danger to human health and life safety, or may harm the physical health or growth and development of infants, the seller shall immediately stop selling, recover the dairy products already sold, and record the recovery situation.  
Where a dairy product seller itself discovers that the dairy products it sells fall under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, it shall also immediately report to the local administrative department for industry and commerce and other relevant departments, and notify the dairy product production enterprise.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　乳制品销售者应当向消费者提供购货凭证，履行不合格乳制品的更换、退货等义务。  
乳制品销售者依照前款规定履行更换、退货等义务后，属于乳制品生产企业或者供货商的责任的，销售者可以向乳制品生产企业或者供货商追偿。

**Article 43** A dairy product seller shall provide a purchase voucher to the consumer and fulfill the obligations of replacement and return of non-conforming dairy products.  
After a dairy product seller fulfills the obligations of replacement, return, and the like in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, if the responsibility lies with the dairy product production enterprise or the supplier, the seller may seek recourse from the dairy product production enterprise or the supplier.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　进口的乳品应当按照乳品质量安全国家标准进行检验；尚未制定乳品质量安全国家标准的，可以参照国家有关部门指定的国外有关标准进行检验。

**Article 44** Imported dairy products shall be inspected in accordance with the national standards for dairy product quality and safety; where no national standards for dairy product quality and safety have been formulated, the inspection may be conducted with reference to the relevant foreign standards designated by the relevant departments of the State.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　出口乳品的生产者、销售者应当保证其出口乳品符合乳品质量安全国家标准的同时还符合进口国家(地区)的标准或者合同要求。

**Article 45** The producers and sellers of exported dairy products shall ensure that their exported dairy products comply with the national standards for dairy product quality and safety and also meet the standards of the importing country (region) or the contractual requirements.

### 第六章　监督检查

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上人民政府畜牧兽医主管部门应当加强对奶畜饲养以及生鲜乳生产环节、收购环节的监督检查。县级以上质量监督检验检疫部门应当加强对乳制品生产环节和乳品进出口环节的监督检查。县级以上工商行政管理部门应当加强对乳制品销售环节的监督检查。县级以上食品药品监督部门应当加强对乳制品餐饮服务环节的监督管理。监督检查部门之间，监督检查部门与其他有关部门之间，应当及时通报乳品质量安全监督管理信息。  
畜牧兽医、质量监督、工商行政管理等部门应当定期开展监督抽查，并记录监督抽查的情况和处理结果。需要对乳品进行抽样检查的，不得收取任何费用，所需费用由同级财政列支。

**Article 46** The competent administrative departments for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and inspection over the feeding of dairy livestock and the production and purchase of raw fresh milk. The quality supervision, inspection and quarantine departments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and inspection over the production of dairy products and the import and export of dairy products. The administrative departments for industry and commerce at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and inspection over the sale of dairy products. The food and drug supervision departments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and administration over the catering service of dairy products. The supervision and inspection departments and other relevant departments shall promptly exchange information on the supervision and administration of dairy product quality and safety.  
The competent administrative departments for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine, the quality supervision departments, the administrative departments for industry and commerce, and other departments shall regularly conduct supervisory spot checks, and record the circumstances and results of such spot checks. Where it is necessary to conduct sampling inspection of dairy products, no fees shall be charged, and the necessary expenses shall be covered by the fiscal budget at the same level.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　畜牧兽医、质量监督、工商行政管理等部门在依据各自职责进行监督检查时，行使下列职权：  
(一)实施现场检查；  
(二)向有关人员调查、了解有关情况；  
(三)查阅、复制有关合同、票据、账簿、检验报告等资料；  
(四)查封、扣押有证据证明不符合乳品质量安全国家标准的乳品以及违法使用的生鲜乳、辅料、添加剂；  
(五)查封涉嫌违法从事乳品生产经营活动的场所，扣押用于违法生产经营的工具、设备；  
(六)法律、行政法规规定的其他职权。

**Article 47** When conducting supervision and inspection in accordance with their respective functions and duties, the competent administrative departments for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine, the quality supervision departments, the administrative departments for industry and commerce, and other departments shall exercise the following powers:  
(1) Carrying out on-site inspections;  
(2) Investigating and understanding relevant circumstances from relevant personnel;  
(3) Consulting and copying relevant contracts, invoices, account books, inspection reports, and other materials;  
(4) Sealing up or detaining dairy products that evidence proves do not comply with the national standards for dairy product quality and safety, as well as illegally used raw fresh milk, auxiliary materials, and additives;  
(5) Sealing up premises suspected of being used for illegal dairy product production and operation activities, and detaining tools and equipment used for illegal production and operation;  
(6) Other powers prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上质量监督部门、工商行政管理部门在监督检查中，对不符合乳品质量安全国家标准、存在危害人体健康和生命安全危险或者可能危害婴幼儿身体健康和生长发育的乳制品，责令并监督生产企业召回、销售者停止销售。

**Article 48** Where, during supervision and inspection, the quality supervision departments and the administrative departments for industry and commerce at or above the county level discover dairy products that do not comply with the national standards for dairy product quality and safety, pose a danger to human health and life safety, or may harm the physical health or growth and development of infants, they shall order and supervise the production enterprise to recall and the seller to stop selling such products.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上人民政府价格主管部门应当加强对生鲜乳购销过程中压级压价、价格欺诈、价格串通等不正当价格行为的监督检查。

**Article 49** The competent price departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and inspection over improper pricing behaviors such as price suppression based on grade, price fraud, and price collusion in the purchase and sale of raw fresh milk.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　畜牧兽医主管部门、质量监督部门、工商行政管理部门应当建立乳品生产经营者违法行为记录，及时提供给中国人民银行，由中国人民银行纳入企业信用信息基础数据库。

**Article 50** The competent administrative departments for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine, the quality supervision departments, and the administrative departments for industry and commerce shall establish records of illegal acts by dairy product producers and operators, and promptly provide such records to the People's Bank of China, which shall incorporate them into the enterprise credit information basic database.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　省级以上人民政府畜牧兽医主管部门、质量监督部门、工商行政管理部门依据各自职责，公布乳品质量安全监督管理信息。有关监督管理部门应当及时向同级卫生主管部门通报乳品质量安全事故信息；乳品质量安全重大事故信息由省级以上人民政府卫生主管部门公布。

**Article 51** The competent administrative departments for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine, the quality supervision departments, and the administrative departments for industry and commerce of the people's governments at or above the provincial level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, publish information on the supervision and administration of dairy product quality and safety. The relevant supervision and administration departments shall promptly report information on dairy product quality and safety accidents to the competent health department at the same level; information on major dairy product quality and safety accidents shall be published by the competent health department of the people's government at or above the provincial level.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　有关监督管理部门发现奶畜养殖者、生鲜乳收购者、乳制品生产企业和销售者涉嫌犯罪的，应当及时移送公安机关立案侦查。

**Article 52** Where the relevant supervision and administration departments discover that a dairy livestock breeder, raw fresh milk purchaser, dairy product production enterprise, or seller is suspected of committing a crime, they shall promptly transfer the case to the public security organ for filing and investigation.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　任何单位和个人有权向畜牧兽医、卫生、质量监督、工商行政管理、食品药品监督等部门举报乳品生产经营中的违法行为。畜牧兽医、卫生、质量监督、工商行政管理、食品药品监督等部门应当公布本单位的电子邮件地址和举报电话；对接到的举报，应当完整地记录、保存。  
接到举报的部门对属于本部门职责范围内的事项，应当及时依法处理，对于实名举报，应当及时答复；对不属于本部门职责范围内的事项，应当及时移交有权处理的部门，有权处理的部门应当立即处理，不得推诿。

**Article 53** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report illegal acts in the production and operation of dairy products to the competent administrative departments for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine, the health departments, the quality supervision departments, the administrative departments for industry and commerce, the food and drug supervision departments, and other departments. The competent administrative departments for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine, the health departments, the quality supervision departments, the administrative departments for industry and commerce, the food and drug supervision departments, and other departments shall publish their email addresses and reporting telephone numbers; they shall completely record and keep the reports received.  
The department that receives a report shall, with respect to matters within its own functions and duties, handle them promptly according to law; for a report made with real name, it shall respond promptly. With respect to matters not within its own functions and duties, it shall promptly transfer them to the department with the authority to handle them, and the department with such authority shall handle them immediately and shall not shirk its responsibility.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　生鲜乳收购者、乳制品生产企业在生鲜乳收购、乳制品生产过程中，加入非食品用化学物质或者其他可能危害人体健康的物质，依照[刑法第一百四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-144)的规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任，并由发证机关吊销许可证照；尚不构成犯罪的，由畜牧兽医主管部门、质量监督部门依据各自职责没收违法所得和违法生产的乳品，以及相关的工具、设备等物品，并处违法乳品货值金额15倍以上30倍以下罚款，由发证机关吊销许可证照。

**Article 54** Where a raw fresh milk purchaser or a dairy product production enterprise, in the process of purchasing raw fresh milk or producing dairy products, adds non-food chemical substances or other substances that may be harmful to human health, if the act constitutes a crime under Article 144 of the Criminal Law, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law, and the licensing authority shall revoke the licenses and permits; if the act does not constitute a crime, the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine or the quality supervision department shall, in accordance with its respective functions and duties, confiscate the illegal gains and the dairy products illegally produced, as well as the relevant tools, equipment, and other articles, and impose a fine of not less than 15 times but not more than 30 times the value of the illegal dairy products, and the licensing authority shall revoke the licenses and permits.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　生产、销售不符合乳品质量安全国家标准的乳品，依照[刑法第一百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-143)的规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任，并由发证机关吊销许可证照；尚不构成犯罪的，由畜牧兽医主管部门、质量监督部门、工商行政管理部门依据各自职责没收违法所得、违法乳品和相关的工具、设备等物品，并处违法乳品货值金额10倍以上20倍以下罚款，由发证机关吊销许可证照。

**Article 55** Where dairy products that do not comply with the national standards for dairy product quality and safety are produced or sold, if the act constitutes a crime under Article 143 of the Criminal Law, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law, and the licensing authority shall revoke the licenses and permits; if the act does not constitute a crime, the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine, the quality supervision department, or the administrative department for industry and commerce shall, in accordance with its respective functions and duties, confiscate the illegal gains, the illegal dairy products, and the relevant tools, equipment, and other articles, and impose a fine of not less than 10 times but not more than 20 times the value of the illegal dairy products, and the licensing authority shall revoke the licenses and permits.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　乳制品生产企业违反[本条例第三十六条](#art-36)的规定，对不符合乳品质量安全国家标准、存在危害人体健康和生命安全或者可能危害婴幼儿身体健康和生长发育的乳制品，不停止生产、不召回的，由质量监督部门责令停止生产、召回；拒不停止生产、拒不召回的，没收其违法所得、违法乳制品和相关的工具、设备等物品，并处违法乳制品货值金额15倍以上30倍以下罚款，由发证机关吊销许可证照。

**Article 56** Where a dairy product production enterprise, in violation of Article 36 of these Regulations, fails to stop production or to recall dairy products that do not comply with the national standards for dairy product quality and safety, pose a danger to human health and life safety, or may harm the physical health or growth and development of infants, the quality supervision department shall order it to stop production and to recall; if it refuses to stop production and to recall, its illegal gains, illegal dairy products, and the relevant tools, equipment, and other articles shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 15 times but not more than 30 times the value of the illegal dairy products shall be imposed, and the licensing authority shall revoke the licenses and permits.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　乳制品销售者违反[本条例第四十二条](#art-42)的规定，对不符合乳品质量安全国家标准、存在危害人体健康和生命安全或者可能危害婴幼儿身体健康和生长发育的乳制品，不停止销售、不追回的，由工商行政管理部门责令停止销售、追回；拒不停止销售、拒不追回的，没收其违法所得、违法乳制品和相关的工具、设备等物品，并处违法乳制品货值金额15倍以上30倍以下罚款，由发证机关吊销许可证照。

**Article 57** Where a dairy product seller, in violation of Article 42 of these Regulations, fails to stop selling or to recover dairy products that do not comply with the national standards for dairy product quality and safety, pose a danger to human health and life safety, or may harm the physical health or growth and development of infants, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order it to stop selling and to recover; if it refuses to stop selling and to recover, its illegal gains, illegal dairy products, and the relevant tools, equipment, and other articles shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 15 times but not more than 30 times the value of the illegal dairy products shall be imposed, and the licensing authority shall revoke the licenses and permits.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本条例规定，在婴幼儿奶粉生产过程中，加入非食品用化学物质或其他可能危害人体健康的物质的，或者生产、销售的婴幼儿奶粉营养成分不足、不符合乳品质量安全国家标准的，依照本条例规定，从重处罚。

**Article 58** Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, adds non-food chemical substances or other substances that may be harmful to human health during the production of infant milk powder, or produces or sells infant milk powder with insufficient nutritional components or that does not comply with the national standards for dairy product quality and safety, a severe punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　奶畜养殖者、生鲜乳收购者、乳制品生产企业和销售者在发生乳品质量安全事故后未报告、处置的，由畜牧兽医、质量监督、工商行政管理、食品药品监督等部门依据各自职责，责令改正，给予警告；毁灭有关证据的，责令停产停业，并处10万元以上20万元以下罚款；造成严重后果的，由发证机关吊销许可证照；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 59** Where a dairy livestock breeder, raw fresh milk purchaser, dairy product production enterprise, or seller fails to report or handle a dairy product quality and safety accident after its occurrence, the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine, the quality supervision department, the administrative department for industry and commerce, the food and drug supervision department, or other department shall, in accordance with its respective functions and duties, order rectification and give a warning; if relevant evidence is destroyed, the department shall order suspension of production or business and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if serious consequences are caused, the licensing authority shall revoke the licenses and permits; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　有下列情形之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府畜牧兽医主管部门没收违法所得、违法收购的生鲜乳和相关的设备、设施等物品，并处违法乳品货值金额5倍以上10倍以下罚款；有许可证照的，由发证机关吊销许可证照：  
(一)未取得生鲜乳收购许可证收购生鲜乳的；  
(二)生鲜乳收购站取得生鲜乳收购许可证后，不再符合许可条件继续从事生鲜乳收购的；  
(三)生鲜乳收购站收购[本条例第二十四条](#art-24)规定禁止收购的生鲜乳的。

**Article 60** Under any of the following circumstances, the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the local people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the illegal gains, the illegally purchased raw fresh milk, and the relevant equipment, facilities, and other articles, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 5 times but not more than 10 times the value of the illegal dairy products; if licenses and permits are held, the licensing authority shall revoke such licenses and permits:  
(1) Purchasing raw fresh milk without obtaining a raw fresh milk purchasing permit;  
(2) A raw fresh milk purchasing station, after obtaining a raw fresh milk purchasing permit, no longer meets the licensing conditions but continues to engage in the purchase of raw fresh milk;  
(3) A raw fresh milk purchasing station purchases raw fresh milk the purchase of which is prohibited under Article 24 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　乳制品生产企业和销售者未取得许可证，或者取得许可证后不按照法定条件、法定要求从事生产销售活动的，由县级以上地方质量监督部门、工商行政管理部门依照《国务院关于加强食品等产品安全监督管理的特别规定》等法律、行政法规的规定处罚。

**Article 61** Where a dairy product production enterprise or seller engages in production or sales activities without obtaining a permit, or after obtaining a permit, fails to comply with the statutory conditions and statutory requirements, the local quality supervision department or the administrative department for industry and commerce at or above the county level shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the Special Provisions of the State Council on Strengthening the Supervision and Administration of the Safety of Food and Other Products and other laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　畜牧兽医、卫生、质量监督、工商行政管理等部门，不履行本条例规定职责、造成后果的，或者滥用职权、有其他渎职行为的，由监察机关或者任免机关对其主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记大过或者降级的处分；造成严重后果的，给予撤职或者开除的处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 62** Where the competent administrative departments for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine, the health departments, the quality supervision departments, the administrative departments for industry and commerce, or other departments fail to perform their duties as prescribed in these Regulations, causing consequences, or abuse their powers or engage in other dereliction of duty, the supervisory authority or the appointing authority shall impose a sanction of recording a major demerit or demotion on the principal responsible person, the person directly in charge, and other persons directly responsible; if serious consequences are caused, a sanction of removal from office or expulsion shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　草原牧区放牧饲养的奶畜所产的生鲜乳收购办法，由所在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府参照本条例另行制定。

**Article 63** The measures for the purchase of raw fresh milk produced by dairy livestock grazed on pasture in pastoral areas shall be separately formulated by the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 64** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
