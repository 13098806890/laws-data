# 乡村医生从业管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2003-08-05  
**生效日期**：2004-01-01

> (2003年7月30日国务院第16次常务会议通过　2003年8月5日中华人民共和国国务院令第386号公布　自2004年1月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了提高乡村医生的职业道德和业务素质，加强乡村医生从业管理，保护乡村医生的合法权益，保障村民获得初级卫生保健服务，根据《中华人民共和国执业医师法》(以下称执业医师法)的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于尚未取得执业医师资格或者执业助理医师资格，经注册在村医疗卫生机构从事预防、保健和一般医疗服务的乡村医生。 村医疗卫生机构中的执业医师或者执业助理医师，依照执业医师法的规定管理，不适用本条例。 第三条　国务院卫生行政主管部门负责全国乡村医生的管理工作。 县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政主管部门负责本行政区域内乡村医生的管理工作。 第四条　国家对在农村预防、保健、医疗服务和突发事件应急处理工作中做出突出成绩的乡村医生，给予奖励。 第五条　地方各级人民政府应当加强乡村医生的培训工作，采取多种形式对乡村医生进行培训。 第六条　具有学历教育资格的医学教育机构，应当按照国家有关规定开展适应农村需要的医学学历教育，定向为农村培养适用的卫生人员。 国家鼓励乡村医生学习中医药基本知识，运用中医药技能防治疾病。 第七条　国家鼓励乡村医生通过医学教育取得医学专业学历；鼓励符合条件的乡村医生申请参加国家医师资格考试。 第八条　国家鼓励取得执业医师资格或者执业助理医师资格的人员，开办村医疗卫生机构，或者在村医疗卫生机构向村民提供预防、保健和医疗服务。 第二章　执业注册 第九条　国家实行乡村医生执业注册制度。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了提高乡村医生的职业道德和业务素质，加强乡村医生从业管理，保护乡村医生的合法权益，保障村民获得初级卫生保健服务，根据《中华人民共和国执业医师法》(以下称执业医师法)的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Practicing Physicians (hereinafter referred to as the Practicing Physicians Law) for the purposes of improving the professional ethics and professional competence of rural doctors, strengthening the administration of rural doctors' practice, protecting the lawful rights and interests of rural doctors, and ensuring that villagers obtain primary health care services.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于尚未取得执业医师资格或者执业助理医师资格，经注册在村医疗卫生机构从事预防、保健和一般医疗服务的乡村医生。  
村医疗卫生机构中的执业医师或者执业助理医师，依照执业医师法的规定管理，不适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to rural doctors who have not obtained the qualification of a practicing physician or a practicing assistant physician and are registered to engage in prevention, health care, and general medical services in village medical and health institutions.  
Practicing physicians or practicing assistant physicians in village medical and health institutions shall be managed in accordance with the provisions of the Practicing Physicians Law and shall not be subject to these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院卫生行政主管部门负责全国乡村医生的管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政主管部门负责本行政区域内乡村医生的管理工作。

**Article 3** The competent health administration department of the State Council shall be responsible for the administration of rural doctors nationwide.  
The competent health administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the administration of rural doctors within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对在农村预防、保健、医疗服务和突发事件应急处理工作中做出突出成绩的乡村医生，给予奖励。

**Article 4** The State shall grant awards to rural doctors who have made outstanding achievements in rural prevention, health care, medical services, and emergency response work.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　地方各级人民政府应当加强乡村医生的培训工作，采取多种形式对乡村医生进行培训。

**Article 5** Local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen training for rural doctors and conduct training in various forms.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　具有学历教育资格的医学教育机构，应当按照国家有关规定开展适应农村需要的医学学历教育，定向为农村培养适用的卫生人员。  
国家鼓励乡村医生学习中医药基本知识，运用中医药技能防治疾病。

**Article 6** Medical education institutions with the qualifications for academic education shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, carry out medical academic education that meets the needs of rural areas and train health personnel suitable for rural areas on a targeted basis.  
The State encourages rural doctors to learn basic knowledge of traditional Chinese medicine and to use traditional Chinese medicine skills for disease prevention and treatment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励乡村医生通过医学教育取得医学专业学历；鼓励符合条件的乡村医生申请参加国家医师资格考试。

**Article 7** The State encourages rural doctors to obtain medical professional academic qualifications through medical education and encourages eligible rural doctors to apply for the National Physician Qualification Examination.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励取得执业医师资格或者执业助理医师资格的人员，开办村医疗卫生机构，或者在村医疗卫生机构向村民提供预防、保健和医疗服务。

**Article 8** The State encourages persons who have obtained the qualification of a practicing physician or a practicing assistant physician to establish village medical and health institutions, or to provide prevention, health care, and medical services to villagers in village medical and health institutions.

### 第二章　执业注册

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家实行乡村医生执业注册制度。  
县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门负责乡村医生执业注册工作。

**Article 9** The State shall implement a practice registration system for rural doctors.  
The competent health administration department of the county people's government shall be responsible for the practice registration of rural doctors.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　本条例公布前的乡村医生，取得县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政主管部门颁发的乡村医生证书，并符合下列条件之一的，可以向县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门申请乡村医生执业注册，取得乡村医生执业证书后，继续在村医疗卫生机构执业：  
(一)已经取得中等以上医学专业学历的；  
(二)在村医疗卫生机构连续工作20年以上的；  
(三)按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政主管部门制定的培训规划，接受培训取得合格证书的。

**Article 10** Rural doctors before the promulgation of these Regulations who have obtained a rural doctor certificate issued by the competent health administration department of a local people's government at or above the county level and meet one of the following conditions may apply to the competent health administration department of the county people's government for practice registration as a rural doctor, and after obtaining a rural doctor practice certificate, may continue to practice in a village medical and health institution:  
(1) Having obtained a medical professional academic qualification at the secondary level or above;  
(2) Having worked continuously for 20 years or more in a village medical and health institution;  
(3) Having received training and obtained a certificate of competence in accordance with the training plan formulated by the competent health administration department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对具有县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政主管部门颁发的乡村医生证书，但不符合[本条例第十条](#art-10)规定条件的乡村医生，县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门应当进行有关预防、保健和一般医疗服务基本知识的培训，并根据省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政主管部门确定的考试内容、考试范围进行考试。  
前款所指的乡村医生经培训并考试合格的，可以申请乡村医生执业注册；经培训但考试不合格的，县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门应当组织对其再次培训和考试。不参加再次培训或者再次考试仍不合格的，不得申请乡村医生执业注册。  
本条所指的培训、考试，应当在本条例施行后6个月内完成。

**Article 11** With respect to rural doctors who possess a rural doctor certificate issued by the competent health administration department of a local people's government at or above the county level but do not meet the conditions specified in Article 10 of these Regulations, the competent health administration department of the county people's government shall conduct training on basic knowledge of prevention, health care, and general medical services, and shall conduct examinations based on the examination content and scope determined by the competent health administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
Rural doctors referred to in the preceding paragraph who have undergone training and passed the examinations may apply for practice registration as a rural doctor; those who have undergone training but failed the examinations shall be subject to further training and examinations organized by the competent health administration department of the county people's government. Those who do not participate in further training or still fail the further examination shall not apply for practice registration as a rural doctor.  
The training and examinations referred to in this Article shall be completed within six months after these Regulations come into force.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　本条例公布之日起进入村医疗卫生机构从事预防、保健和医疗服务的人员，应当具备执业医师资格或者执业助理医师资格。  
不具备前款规定条件的地区，根据实际需要，可以允许具有中等医学专业学历的人员，或者经培训达到中等医学专业水平的其他人员申请执业注册，进入村医疗卫生机构执业。具体办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。

**Article 12** Persons who, from the date of promulgation of these Regulations, enter village medical and health institutions to engage in prevention, health care, and medical services shall possess the qualification of a practicing physician or a practicing assistant physician.  
In areas that do not meet the conditions specified in the preceding paragraph, based on actual needs, persons with a medical professional academic qualification at the secondary level, or other persons who have reached the level of secondary medical profession through training, may be permitted to apply for practice registration and enter village medical and health institutions to practice. The specific measures shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　符合本条例规定申请在村医疗卫生机构执业的人员，应当持村医疗卫生机构出具的拟聘用证明和相关学历证明、证书，向村医疗卫生机构所在地的县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门申请执业注册。  
县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门应当自受理申请之日起15日内完成审核工作，对符合本条例规定条件的，准予执业注册，发给乡村医生执业证书；对不符合本条例规定条件的，不予注册，并书面说明理由。

**Article 13** Persons who apply to practice in a village medical and health institution in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations shall submit the proposed employment certificate issued by the village medical and health institution and relevant academic certificates and credentials, and apply for practice registration to the competent health administration department of the county people's government where the village medical and health institution is located.  
The competent health administration department of the county people's government shall complete the review within 15 days from the date of accepting the application; for those who meet the conditions specified in these Regulations, it shall grant practice registration and issue a rural doctor practice certificate; for those who do not meet the conditions, it shall not register and shall provide a written explanation of the reasons.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　乡村医生有下列情形之一的，不予注册：  
(一)不具有完全民事行为能力的；  
(二)受刑事处罚，自刑罚执行完毕之日起至申请执业注册之日止不满2年的；  
(三)受吊销乡村医生执业证书行政处罚，自处罚决定之日起至申请执业注册之日止不满2年的。

**Article 14** A rural doctor shall not be registered under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Having no capacity for civil conduct;  
(2) Having been subjected to criminal punishment, and less than two years have elapsed from the date of completion of the enforcement of the punishment to the date of applying for practice registration;  
(3) Having been subjected to the administrative penalty of revocation of the rural doctor practice certificate, and less than two years have elapsed from the date of the penalty decision to the date of applying for practice registration.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　乡村医生经注册取得执业证书后，方可在聘用其执业的村医疗卫生机构从事预防、保健和一般医疗服务。  
未经注册取得乡村医生执业证书的，不得执业。

**Article 15** A rural doctor, after being registered and obtaining a practice certificate, may engage in prevention, health care, and general medical services in the village medical and health institution that employs him or her.  
A person who is not registered and has not obtained a rural doctor practice certificate shall not practice.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　乡村医生执业证书有效期为5年。  
乡村医生执业证书有效期满需要继续执业的，应当在有效期满前3个月申请再注册。  
县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门应当自受理申请之日起15日内进行审核，对符合省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政主管部门规定条件的，准予再注册，换发乡村医生执业证书；对不符合条件的，不予再注册，由发证部门收回原乡村医生执业证书。

**Article 16** The validity of the rural doctor practice certificate shall be five years.  
If a rural doctor needs to continue practicing upon expiration of the validity of the practice certificate, he or she shall apply for re-registration three months before the expiration of the validity.  
The competent health administration department of the county people's government shall conduct a review within 15 days from the date of accepting the application; for those who meet the conditions specified by the competent health administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, it shall grant re-registration and issue a new rural doctor practice certificate; for those who do not meet the conditions, it shall not grant re-registration, and the issuing authority shall revoke the original rural doctor practice certificate.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　乡村医生应当在聘用其执业的村医疗卫生机构执业；变更执业的村医疗卫生机构的，应当依照[本条例第十三条](#art-13)规定的程序办理变更注册手续。

**Article 17** A rural doctor shall practice in the village medical and health institution that employs him or her; if he or she changes the village medical and health institution where he or she practices, he or she shall go through the change registration procedures in accordance with the procedures specified in Article 13 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　乡村医生有下列情形之一的，由原注册的卫生行政主管部门注销执业注册，收回乡村医生执业证书：  
(一)死亡或者被宣告失踪的；  
(二)受刑事处罚的；  
(三)中止执业活动满2年的；  
(四)考核不合格，逾期未提出再次考核申请或者经再次考核仍不合格的。

**Article 18** A rural doctor under any of the following circumstances shall have his or her practice registration cancelled by the original registration health administration department and the rural doctor practice certificate shall be revoked:  
(1) Death or being declared missing;  
(2) Having been subjected to criminal punishment;  
(3) Suspension of practice activities for a period of two years;  
(4) Failure in assessment, failure to apply for reassessment within the prescribed time limit, or still failing the reassessment.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门应当将准予执业注册、再注册和注销注册的人员名单向其执业的村医疗卫生机构所在地的村民公告，并由设区的市级人民政府卫生行政主管部门汇总，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政主管部门备案。

**Article 19** The competent health administration department of the county people's government shall announce to the villagers of the place where the village medical and health institution is located the list of persons who have been granted practice registration, re-registration, or cancellation of registration, and the competent health administration department of the city with districts shall compile the list and report it to the competent health administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for record.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门办理乡村医生执业注册、再注册、注销注册，应当依据法定权限、条件和程序，遵循便民原则，提高办事效率。

**Article 20** When handling practice registration, re-registration, and cancellation of registration for rural doctors, the competent health administration department of the county people's government shall act pursuant to statutory authority, conditions, and procedures, follow the principle of convenience for the people, and improve work efficiency.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　村民和乡村医生发现违法办理乡村医生执业注册、再注册、注销注册的，可以向有关人民政府卫生行政主管部门反映；有关人民政府卫生行政主管部门对反映的情况应当及时核实，调查处理，并将调查处理结果予以公布。

**Article 21** Where villagers or rural doctors discover illegal handling of practice registration, re-registration, or cancellation of registration for rural doctors, they may report to the relevant health administration department of the people's government; the relevant health administration department of the people's government shall promptly verify, investigate, and handle the reported situation, and shall publish the results of the investigation and handling.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　上级人民政府卫生行政主管部门应当加强对下级人民政府卫生行政主管部门办理乡村医生执业注册、再注册、注销注册的监督检查，及时纠正违法行为。

**Article 22** The competent health administration department of a higher-level people's government shall strengthen supervision and inspection over the practice registration, re-registration, and cancellation of registration for rural doctors handled by the competent health administration department of a lower-level people's government, and shall promptly correct illegal acts.

### 第三章　执业规则

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　乡村医生在执业活动中享有下列权利：  
(一)进行一般医学处置，出具相应的医学证明；  
(二)参与医学经验交流，参加专业学术团体；  
(三)参加业务培训和教育；  
(四)在执业活动中，人格尊严、人身安全不受侵犯；  
(五)获取报酬；  
(六)对当地的预防、保健、医疗工作和卫生行政主管部门的工作提出意见和建议。

**Article 23** A rural doctor shall enjoy the following rights in his or her practice activities:  
(1) To perform general medical treatment and issue corresponding medical certificates;  
(2) To participate in medical experience exchange and join professional academic groups;  
(3) To participate in professional training and education;  
(4) To have his or her personal dignity and personal safety not infringed upon during practice activities;  
(5) To receive remuneration;  
(6) To offer opinions and suggestions on local prevention, health care, and medical work, as well as the work of the health administration department.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　乡村医生在执业活动中应当履行下列义务：  
(一)遵守法律、法规、规章和诊疗护理技术规范、常规；  
(二)树立敬业精神，遵守职业道德，履行乡村医生职责，为村民健康服务；  
(三)关心、爱护、尊重患者，保护患者的隐私；  
(四)努力钻研业务，更新知识，提高专业技术水平；  
(五)向村民宣传卫生保健知识，对患者进行健康教育。

**Article 24** A rural doctor shall perform the following obligations in his or her practice activities:  
(1) To abide by laws, regulations, rules, and technical specifications and norms for diagnosis and treatment and nursing;  
(2) To foster a professional spirit, abide by professional ethics, fulfill the duties of a rural doctor, and serve the health of villagers;  
(3) To care for, protect, and respect patients, and protect patients' privacy;  
(4) To diligently study professional knowledge, update knowledge, and improve professional skill levels;  
(5) To disseminate health care knowledge to villagers and provide health education to patients.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　乡村医生应当协助有关部门做好初级卫生保健服务工作；按照规定及时报告传染病疫情和中毒事件，如实填写并上报有关卫生统计报表，妥善保管有关资料。

**Article 25** A rural doctor shall assist relevant departments in providing primary health care services; shall, in accordance with the provisions, promptly report epidemic situations of infectious diseases and poisoning incidents, truthfully fill out and submit relevant health statistics reports, and properly keep relevant materials.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　乡村医生在执业活动中，不得重复使用一次性医疗器械和卫生材料。对使用过的一次性医疗器械和卫生材料，应当按照规定处置。

**Article 26** In his or her practice activities, a rural doctor shall not reuse disposable medical instruments and health materials. Used disposable medical instruments and health materials shall be disposed of in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　乡村医生应当如实向患者或者其家属介绍病情，对超出一般医疗服务范围或者限于医疗条件和技术水平不能诊治的病人，应当及时转诊；情况紧急不能转诊的，应当先行抢救并及时向有抢救条件的医疗卫生机构求助。

**Article 27** A rural doctor shall truthfully explain the patient's condition to the patient or his or her family; for patients whose condition exceeds the scope of general medical services or cannot be diagnosed or treated due to limited medical conditions or technical levels, he or she shall promptly refer the patient; if the situation is urgent and referral is not possible, he or she shall provide first aid and promptly seek assistance from a medical and health institution with rescue capabilities.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　乡村医生不得出具与执业范围无关或者与执业范围不相符的医学证明，不得进行实验性临床医疗活动。

**Article 28** A rural doctor shall not issue medical certificates irrelevant to or inconsistent with his or her practice scope, and shall not conduct experimental clinical medical activities.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政主管部门应当按照乡村医生一般医疗服务范围，制定乡村医生基本用药目录。乡村医生应当在乡村医生基本用药目录规定的范围内用药。

**Article 29** The competent health administration department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the scope of general medical services for rural doctors, formulate a list of basic drugs for rural doctors. A rural doctor shall use drugs within the scope specified in the list of basic drugs for rural doctors.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级人民政府对乡村医生开展国家规定的预防、保健等公共卫生服务，应当按照有关规定予以补助。

**Article 30** The county people's government shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions, provide subsidies to rural doctors for carrying out public health services such as prevention and health care as prescribed by the State.

### 第四章　培训与考核

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府组织制定乡村医生培训规划，保证乡村医生至少每2年接受一次培训。县级人民政府根据培训规划制定本地区乡村医生培训计划。  
对承担国家规定的预防、保健等公共卫生服务的乡村医生，其培训所需经费列入县级财政预算。对边远贫困地区，设区的市级以上地方人民政府应当给予适当经费支持。  
国家鼓励社会组织和个人支持乡村医生培训工作。

**Article 31** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall organize the formulation of training plans for rural doctors to ensure that rural doctors receive training at least once every two years. The county people's governments shall formulate training plans for rural doctors in their regions based on the training plans.  
For rural doctors who undertake public health services such as prevention and health care as prescribed by the State, the funds required for their training shall be included in the county fiscal budget. For remote and poor areas, local people's governments at or above the city with districts shall provide appropriate financial support.  
The State encourages social organizations and individuals to support the training of rural doctors.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门根据乡村医生培训计划，负责组织乡村医生的培训工作。  
乡、镇人民政府以及村民委员会应当为乡村医生开展工作和学习提供条件，保证乡村医生接受培训和继续教育。

**Article 32** The competent health administration department of the county people's government shall, based on the rural doctor training plan, be responsible for organizing the training of rural doctors.  
The people's governments of townships and towns as well as villagers' committees shall provide conditions for rural doctors to work and study, and ensure that rural doctors receive training and continuing education.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　乡村医生应当按照培训规划的要求至少每2年接受一次培训，更新医学知识，提高业务水平。

**Article 33** A rural doctor shall receive training at least once every two years in accordance with the requirements of the training plan, update medical knowledge, and improve professional level.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门负责组织本地区乡村医生的考核工作；对乡村医生的考核，每2年组织一次。  
对乡村医生的考核应当客观、公正，充分听取乡村医生执业的村医疗卫生机构、乡村医生本人、所在村村民委员会和村民的意见。

**Article 34** The competent health administration department of the county people's government shall be responsible for organizing the assessment of rural doctors in its region; the assessment of rural doctors shall be organized once every two years.  
The assessment of rural doctors shall be objective and fair, and the opinions of the village medical and health institution where the rural doctor practices, the rural doctor himself or herself, the villagers' committee of the village where he or she is located, and the villagers shall be fully heard.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门负责检查乡村医生执业情况，收集村民对乡村医生业务水平、工作质量的评价和建议，接受村民对乡村医生的投诉，并进行汇总、分析。汇总、分析结果与乡村医生接受培训的情况作为对乡村医生进行考核的主要内容。

**Article 35** The competent health administration department of the county people's government shall be responsible for inspecting the practice of rural doctors, collecting villagers' evaluations and suggestions on the professional level and work quality of rural doctors, accepting villagers' complaints against rural doctors, and conducting summarization and analysis. The results of summarization and analysis, together with the training received by rural doctors, shall serve as the main content for assessing rural doctors.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　乡村医生经考核合格的，可以继续执业；经考核不合格的，在6个月之内可以申请进行再次考核。逾期未提出再次考核申请或者经再次考核仍不合格的乡村医生，原注册部门应当注销其执业注册，并收回乡村医生执业证书。

**Article 36** A rural doctor who passes the assessment may continue to practice; a rural doctor who fails the assessment may apply for reassessment within six months. For a rural doctor who fails to submit a reassessment application within the prescribed time limit or still fails the reassessment, the original registration department shall cancel his or her practice registration and revoke the rural doctor practice certificate.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　有关人民政府卫生行政主管部门对村民和乡村医生提出的意见、建议和投诉，应当及时调查处理，并将调查处理结果告知村民或者乡村医生。

**Article 37** The relevant health administration department of the people's government shall promptly investigate and handle the opinions, suggestions, and complaints raised by villagers and rural doctors, and shall inform the villagers or rural doctors of the results of the investigation and handling.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　乡村医生在执业活动中，违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告；逾期不改正的，责令暂停3个月以上6个月以下执业活动；情节严重的，由原发证部门暂扣乡村医生执业证书：  
(一)执业活动超出规定的执业范围，或者未按照规定进行转诊的；  
(二)违反规定使用乡村医生基本用药目录以外的处方药品的；  
(三)违反规定出具医学证明，或者伪造卫生统计资料的；  
(四)发现传染病疫情、中毒事件不按规定报告的。

**Article 38** Where a rural doctor, in his or her practice activities, violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the competent health administration department of the county people's government shall order correction within a prescribed time limit and give a warning; if correction is not made within the prescribed time limit, the rural doctor shall be ordered to suspend practice activities for a period of not less than three months and not more than six months; if the circumstances are serious, the original issuing department shall temporarily seize the rural doctor practice certificate:  
(1) The practice activities exceed the prescribed scope of practice, or referral is not made in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) The use of prescription drugs outside the list of basic drugs for rural doctors in violation of the provisions;  
(3) The issuance of medical certificates in violation of the provisions, or the falsification of health statistical data;  
(4) Failure to report epidemic situations of infectious diseases or poisoning incidents in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　乡村医生在执业活动中，违反规定进行实验性临床医疗活动，或者重复使用一次性医疗器械和卫生材料的，由县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，给予警告，可以并处1000元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原发证部门暂扣或者吊销乡村医生执业证书。

**Article 39** Where a rural doctor, in his or her practice activities, conducts experimental clinical medical activities in violation of the provisions, or reuses disposable medical instruments and health materials, the competent health administration department of the county people's government shall order cessation of the illegal act, give a warning, and may also impose a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the original issuing department shall temporarily seize or revoke the rural doctor practice certificate.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　乡村医生变更执业的村医疗卫生机构，未办理变更执业注册手续的，由县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门给予警告，责令限期办理变更注册手续。

**Article 40** Where a rural doctor changes the village medical and health institution where he or she practices and fails to go through the change registration procedures, the competent health administration department of the county people's government shall give a warning and order him or her to go through the change registration procedures within a prescribed time limit.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　以不正当手段取得乡村医生执业证书的，由发证部门收缴乡村医生执业证书；造成患者人身损害的，依法承担民事赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 41** Where a person obtains a rural doctor practice certificate by improper means, the issuing authority shall confiscate the certificate; if personal injury to a patient is caused, the person shall bear civil liability for compensation according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　未经注册在村医疗卫生机构从事医疗活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政主管部门予以取缔，没收其违法所得以及药品、医疗器械，违法所得5000元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5000元的，并处1000元以上3000元以下的罚款；造成患者人身损害的，依法承担民事赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 42** Where a person engages in medical activities in a village medical and health institution without registration, the competent health administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall close it down, confiscate its illegal income and drugs and medical instruments; if the illegal income is 5,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal income shall also be imposed; if there is no illegal income or the illegal income is less than 5,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 3,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if personal injury to a patient is caused, civil liability for compensation shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门未按照乡村医生培训规划、计划组织乡村医生培训的，由本级人民政府或者上一级人民政府卫生行政主管部门责令改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分。

**Article 43** Where the competent health administration department of the county people's government fails to organize training for rural doctors in accordance with the training plans and programs, the people's government at the same level or the competent health administration department of the people's government at the next higher level shall order correction; if the circumstances are serious, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门，对不符合本条例规定条件的人员发给乡村医生执业证书，或者对符合条件的人员不发给乡村医生执业证书的，由本级人民政府或者上一级人民政府卫生行政主管部门责令改正，收回或者补发乡村医生执业证书，并对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分。

**Article 44** Where the competent health administration department of the county people's government issues a rural doctor practice certificate to a person who does not meet the conditions specified in these Regulations, or fails to issue a rural doctor practice certificate to a person who meets the conditions, the people's government at the same level or the competent health administration department of the people's government at the next higher level shall order correction, revoke or reissue the rural doctor practice certificate, and shall give administrative sanctions to the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门对乡村医生执业注册或者再注册申请，未在规定时间内完成审核工作的，或者未按照规定将准予执业注册、再注册和注销注册的人员名单向村民予以公告的，由本级人民政府或者上一级人民政府卫生行政主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分。

**Article 45** Where the competent health administration department of the county people's government fails to complete the review of an application for practice registration or re-registration of a rural doctor within the prescribed time limit, or fails to publish the list of persons who have been granted practice registration, re-registration, or cancellation of registration to the villagers in accordance with the provisions, the people's government at the same level or the competent health administration department of the people's government at the next higher level shall order correction within a prescribed time limit; if correction is not made within the prescribed time limit, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　卫生行政主管部门对村民和乡村医生反映的办理乡村医生执业注册、再注册、注销注册的违法活动未及时核实、调查处理或者未公布调查处理结果的，由本级人民政府或者上一级人民政府卫生行政主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分。

**Article 46** Where the health administration department fails to promptly verify, investigate, and handle the illegal activities in handling practice registration, re-registration, or cancellation of registration for rural doctors reported by villagers or rural doctors, or fails to publish the results of the investigation and handling, the people's government at the same level or the competent health administration department of the people's government at the next higher level shall order correction within a prescribed time limit; if correction is not made within the prescribed time limit, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　寻衅滋事、阻碍乡村医生依法执业，侮辱、诽谤、威胁、殴打乡村医生，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 47** Where a person engages in affray, hinders a rural doctor from practicing according to law, or insults, slanders, threatens, or beats a rural doctor, constituting a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　乡村医生执业证书格式由国务院卫生行政主管部门规定。

**Article 48** The format of the rural doctor practice certificate shall be prescribed by the competent health administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　本条例自2004年1月1日起施行。

**Article 49** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2004.
