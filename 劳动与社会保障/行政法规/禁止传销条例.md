# 禁止传销条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2005-08-23  
**生效日期**：2005-11-01

> (2005年8月10日国务院第101次常务会议通过　2005年8月23日中华人民共和国国务院令第444号公布　自2005年11月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了防止欺诈，保护公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益，维护社会主义市场经济秩序，保持社会稳定，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称传销，是指组织者或者经营者发展人员，通过对被发展人员以其直接或者间接发展的人员数量或者销售业绩为依据计算和给付报酬，或者要求被发展人员以交纳一定费用为条件取得加入资格等方式牟取非法利益，扰乱经济秩序，影响社会稳定的行为。 第三条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对查处传销工作的领导，支持、督促各有关部门依法履行监督管理职责。 县级以上地方人民政府应当根据需要，建立查处传销工作的协调机制，对查处传销工作中的重大问题及时予以协调、解决。 第四条　工商行政管理部门、公安机关应当依照本条例的规定，在各自的职责范围内查处传销行为。 第五条　工商行政管理部门、公安机关依法查处传销行为，应当坚持教育与处罚相结合的原则，教育公民、法人或者其他组织自觉守法。 第六条　任何单位和个人有权向工商行政管理部门、公安机关举报传销行为。工商行政管理部门、公安机关接到举报后，应当立即调查核实，依法查处，并为举报人保密；经调查属实的，依照国家有关规定对举报人给予奖励。 第二章　传销行为的种类与查处机关 第七条　下列行为，属于传销行为： (一)组织者或者经营者通过发展人员，要求被发展人员发展其他人员加入，对发展的人员以其直接或者间接滚动发展的人员数量为依据计算和给付报酬(包括物质奖励和其他经济利益，下同)，牟取非法利益的； (二)组织者或者经营者通过发展人员，要求被发展人员交纳费用或者以认购商品等方式变相交纳费用，取得加入或者发展其他人员加入的资格，牟取非法利益的； (三)组织者或者经营者通过发展人员，要求被发展人员发展其他人员加入，形成上下线关系，并以下线的销售业绩为依据计算和给付上线报酬，牟取非法利益的。 第八条　工商行政管理部门依照本条例的规定，负责查处本条例第七条规定的传销行为。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了防止欺诈，保护公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益，维护社会主义市场经济秩序，保持社会稳定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** . These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of preventing fraud, protecting the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons and other organizations, maintaining the order of the socialist market economy, and preserving social stability.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称传销，是指组织者或者经营者发展人员，通过对被发展人员以其直接或者间接发展的人员数量或者销售业绩为依据计算和给付报酬，或者要求被发展人员以交纳一定费用为条件取得加入资格等方式牟取非法利益，扰乱经济秩序，影响社会稳定的行为。

**Article 2** . For the purposes of these Regulations, 'pyramid selling' refers to any act in which the organizer or operator develops persons and, by calculating and paying remuneration to the developed persons based on the number of persons they directly or indirectly develop or their sales performance, or requiring the developed persons to pay a certain fee as a condition for obtaining the qualification to join, or other means, seeks illegal profits, disrupts economic order, and affects social stability.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对查处传销工作的领导，支持、督促各有关部门依法履行监督管理职责。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当根据需要，建立查处传销工作的协调机制，对查处传销工作中的重大问题及时予以协调、解决。

**Article 3** . Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen their leadership over the work of investigating and punishing pyramid selling, and shall support and urge the relevant departments to perform their supervisory and administrative duties according to law.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, as needed, establish a coordination mechanism for the work of investigating and punishing pyramid selling, and shall timely coordinate and resolve major issues arising in the work of investigating and punishing pyramid selling.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　工商行政管理部门、公安机关应当依照本条例的规定，在各自的职责范围内查处传销行为。

**Article 4** . The administrative department for industry and commerce and the public security organ shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, investigate and punish pyramid selling activities within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　工商行政管理部门、公安机关依法查处传销行为，应当坚持教育与处罚相结合的原则，教育公民、法人或者其他组织自觉守法。

**Article 5** . When investigating and punishing pyramid selling activities according to law, the administrative department for industry and commerce and the public security organ shall adhere to the principle of combining education with punishment, and shall educate citizens, legal persons or other organizations to consciously abide by the law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何单位和个人有权向工商行政管理部门、公安机关举报传销行为。工商行政管理部门、公安机关接到举报后，应当立即调查核实，依法查处，并为举报人保密；经调查属实的，依照国家有关规定对举报人给予奖励。

**Article 6** . Any unit or individual shall have the right to report pyramid selling activities to the administrative department for industry and commerce or the public security organ. Upon receiving a report, the administrative department for industry and commerce or the public security organ shall promptly investigate and verify, investigate and punish according to law, and maintain confidentiality for the whistleblower; if the report is verified to be true, rewards shall be given to the whistleblower in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

### 第二章　传销行为的种类与查处机关

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　下列行为，属于传销行为：  
(一)组织者或者经营者通过发展人员，要求被发展人员发展其他人员加入，对发展的人员以其直接或者间接滚动发展的人员数量为依据计算和给付报酬(包括物质奖励和其他经济利益，下同)，牟取非法利益的；  
(二)组织者或者经营者通过发展人员，要求被发展人员交纳费用或者以认购商品等方式变相交纳费用，取得加入或者发展其他人员加入的资格，牟取非法利益的；  
(三)组织者或者经营者通过发展人员，要求被发展人员发展其他人员加入，形成上下线关系，并以下线的销售业绩为依据计算和给付上线报酬，牟取非法利益的。

**Article 7** . The following acts constitute pyramid selling:  
(1) The organizer or operator develops persons, requires the developed persons to develop other persons to join, calculates and pays remuneration (including material rewards and other economic benefits, the same below) to the persons developed based on the number of persons they directly or indirectly develop in a rolling manner, thereby seeking illegal profits;  
(2) The organizer or operator develops persons, requires the developed persons to pay fees or to make disguised payments of fees by subscribing for goods or other means, so as to obtain the qualification to join or to develop other persons to join, thereby seeking illegal profits;  
(3) The organizer or operator develops persons, requires the developed persons to develop other persons to join, forming an upline-downline relationship, and calculates and pays remuneration to the upline based on the sales performance of the downline, thereby seeking illegal profits.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　工商行政管理部门依照本条例的规定，负责查处[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定的传销行为。

**Article 8** . The administrative department for industry and commerce shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, be responsible for investigating and punishing the pyramid selling activities specified in Article 7 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　利用互联网等媒体发布含有[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定的传销信息的，由工商行政管理部门会同电信等有关部门依照本条例的规定查处。

**Article 9** . Where any person uses the Internet or other media to publish information containing pyramid selling content as specified in Article 7 of these Regulations, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall, together with the telecommunications and other relevant departments, investigate and punish in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在传销中以介绍工作、从事经营活动等名义欺骗他人离开居所地非法聚集并限制其人身自由的，由公安机关会同工商行政管理部门依法查处。

**Article 10** . Where any person, in the course of pyramid selling, deceives others by such means as introducing jobs or engaging in business activities to leave their places of residence and gather illegally and restricts their personal freedom, the public security organ shall, together with the administrative department for industry and commerce, investigate and punish according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　商务、教育、民政、财政、劳动保障、电信、税务等有关部门和单位，应当依照各自职责和有关法律、行政法规的规定配合工商行政管理部门、公安机关查处传销行为。

**Article 11** . The relevant departments and units such as commerce, education, civil affairs, finance, labor and social security, telecommunications, and taxation shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties and the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, cooperate with the administrative department for industry and commerce and the public security organ in investigating and punishing pyramid selling activities.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　农村村民委员会、城市居民委员会等基层组织，应当在当地人民政府指导下，协助有关部门查处传销行为。

**Article 12** . Basic-level organizations such as villagers' committees in rural areas and residents' committees in urban areas shall, under the guidance of the local people's government, assist the relevant departments in investigating and punishing pyramid selling activities.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　工商行政管理部门查处传销行为，对涉嫌犯罪的，应当依法移送公安机关立案侦查；公安机关立案侦查传销案件，对经侦查不构成犯罪的，应当依法移交工商行政管理部门查处。

**Article 13** . Where, in investigating and punishing pyramid selling activities, the administrative department for industry and commerce finds that a crime is suspected, it shall transfer the case to the public security organ according to law for filing a case for investigation; where the public security organ, after filing a case and investigating a pyramid selling case, finds that no crime is constituted after investigation, it shall transfer the case to the administrative department for industry and commerce according to law for investigation and punishment.

### 第三章　查处措施和程序

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上工商行政管理部门对涉嫌传销行为进行查处时，可以采取下列措施：  
(一)责令停止相关活动；  
(二)向涉嫌传销的组织者、经营者和个人调查、了解有关情况；  
(三)进入涉嫌传销的经营场所和培训、集会等活动场所，实施现场检查；  
(四)查阅、复制、查封、扣押涉嫌传销的有关合同、票据、账簿等资料；  
(五)查封、扣押涉嫌专门用于传销的产品(商品)、工具、设备、原材料等财物；  
(六)查封涉嫌传销的经营场所；  
(七)查询涉嫌传销的组织者或者经营者的账户及与存款有关的会计凭证、账簿、对账单等；  
(八)对有证据证明转移或者隐匿违法资金的，可以申请司法机关予以冻结。  
工商行政管理部门采取前款规定的措施，应当向县级以上工商行政管理部门主要负责人书面或者口头报告并经批准。遇有紧急情况需要当场采取前款规定措施的，应当在事后立即报告并补办相关手续；其中，实施前款规定的查封、扣押，以及第(七)项、第(八)项规定的措施，应当事先经县级以上工商行政管理部门主要负责人书面批准。

**Article 14** . When investigating and punishing suspected pyramid selling activities, the administrative department for industry and commerce at or above the county level may take the following measures:  
(1) Order cessation of the relevant activities;  
(2) Investigate and ascertain the relevant circumstances with the organizers, operators and individuals suspected of pyramid selling;  
(3) Enter the business premises and training, gathering and other activity sites suspected of being used for pyramid selling and conduct on-site inspections;  
(4) Peruse, copy, seal up or seize relevant contracts, receipts, account books and other materials suspected of being related to pyramid selling;  
(5) Seal up or seize properties such as products (goods), tools, equipment, raw materials and other materials suspected of being exclusively used for pyramid selling;  
(6) Seal up the business premises suspected of being used for pyramid selling;  
(7) Inquire into the accounts of the organizers or operators suspected of pyramid selling and the accounting vouchers, account books, statements of account and other documents related to deposits;  
(8) Where there is evidence proving that illegal funds are being transferred or concealed, apply to the judicial organ for freezing.  
Where the administrative department for industry and commerce takes the measures specified in the preceding paragraph, it shall report in writing or orally to and obtain approval from the principal responsible person of the administrative department for industry and commerce at or above the county level. In case of emergency where the measures specified in the preceding paragraph need to be taken on the spot, the department shall promptly report after the event and go through the relevant formalities retroactively; among them, the implementation of the sealing up and seizure specified in the preceding paragraph, as well as the measures specified in items (7) and (8), shall be approved in writing in advance by the principal responsible person of the administrative department for industry and commerce at or above the county level.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　工商行政管理部门对涉嫌传销行为进行查处时，执法人员不得少于2人。  
执法人员与当事人有直接利害关系的，应当回避。

**Article 15** . When the administrative department for industry and commerce investigates and punishes suspected pyramid selling activities, no fewer than two law enforcement officers shall be present.  
Where a law enforcement officer has a direct interest in a party, the officer shall recuse himself.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　工商行政管理部门的执法人员对涉嫌传销行为进行查处时，应当向当事人或者有关人员出示证件。

**Article 16** . When law enforcement officers of the administrative department for industry and commerce investigate and punish suspected pyramid selling activities, they shall present their credentials to the parties or relevant personnel.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　工商行政管理部门实施查封、扣押，应当向当事人当场交付查封、扣押决定书和查封、扣押财物及资料清单。  
在交通不便地区或者不及时实施查封、扣押可能影响案件查处的，可以先行实施查封、扣押，并应当在24小时内补办查封、扣押决定书，送达当事人。

**Article 17** . When the administrative department for industry and commerce implements sealing up or seizure, it shall deliver on the spot the decision on sealing up or seizure and the inventory of the sealed up or seized properties and materials to the party.  
In areas with inconvenient transportation, or where failure to implement sealing up or seizure in a timely manner may affect the investigation and punishment of the case, the sealing up or seizure may be implemented first, and the decision on sealing up or seizure shall be supplemented and delivered to the party within 24 hours.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　工商行政管理部门实施查封、扣押的期限不得超过30日；案件情况复杂的，经县级以上工商行政管理部门主要负责人批准，可以延长15日。  
对被查封、扣押的财物，工商行政管理部门应当妥善保管，不得使用或者损毁；造成损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。但是，因不可抗力造成的损失除外。

**Article 18** . The period of sealing up or seizure implemented by the administrative department for industry and commerce shall not exceed 30 days; where the case is complicated, with the approval of the principal responsible person of the administrative department for industry and commerce at or above the county level, the period may be extended by 15 days.  
The administrative department for industry and commerce shall properly keep the sealed up or seized properties and shall not use or damage them; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability. However, losses caused by force majeure are excepted.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　工商行政管理部门实施查封、扣押，应当及时查清事实，在查封、扣押期间作出处理决定。  
对于经调查核实属于传销行为的，应当依法没收被查封、扣押的非法财物；对于经调查核实没有传销行为或者不再需要查封、扣押的，应当在作出处理决定后立即解除查封，退还被扣押的财物。  
工商行政管理部门逾期未作出处理决定的，被查封的物品视为解除查封，被扣押的财物应当予以退还。拒不退还的，当事人可以向人民法院提起行政诉讼。

**Article 19** . When implementing sealing up or seizure, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall promptly ascertain the facts and make a handling decision within the period of sealing up or seizure.  
For those verified after investigation to constitute pyramid selling, the sealed up or seized illegal properties shall be confiscated according to law; for those verified after investigation not to constitute pyramid selling or where sealing up or seizure is no longer necessary, the sealing up shall be lifted immediately after the handling decision is made, and the seized properties shall be returned.  
If the administrative department for industry and commerce fails to make a handling decision within the time limit, the sealed up items shall be deemed as having their sealing up lifted, and the seized properties shall be returned. If the department refuses to return, the party may file an administrative action with the People's Court.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　工商行政管理部门及其工作人员违反本条例的规定使用或者损毁被查封、扣押的财物，造成当事人经济损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 20** . Where the administrative department for industry and commerce and its staff members, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, use or damage the sealed up or seized properties, causing economic losses to the party, they shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　工商行政管理部门对涉嫌传销行为进行查处时，当事人有权陈述和申辩。

**Article 21** When the administrative department for industry and commerce investigates and deals with suspected pyramid selling activities, the party shall have the right to make statements and defenses.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　工商行政管理部门对涉嫌传销行为进行查处时，应当制作现场笔录。  
现场笔录和查封、扣押清单由当事人、见证人和执法人员签名或者盖章，当事人不在现场或者当事人、见证人拒绝签名或者盖章的，执法人员应当在现场笔录中予以注明。

**Article 22** When the administrative department for industry and commerce investigates and deals with suspected pyramid selling activities, it shall make an on-site record.  
The on-site record and the list of sealed-up or seized items shall be signed or sealed by the party, witnesses, and law enforcement officers. If the party is not present on the site, or the party or witnesses refuse to sign or seal, the law enforcement officers shall note this in the on-site record.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对于经查证属于传销行为的，工商行政管理部门、公安机关可以向社会公开发布警示、提示。  
向社会公开发布警示、提示应当经县级以上工商行政管理部门主要负责人或者公安机关主要负责人批准。

**Article 23** For acts that have been verified as pyramid selling, the administrative department for industry and commerce and the public security organ may issue warnings and tips to the public.  
Issuing warnings and tips to the public shall be approved by the principal responsible person of the administrative department for industry and commerce at or above the county level or the principal responsible person of the public security organ.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　有[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定的行为，组织策划传销的，由工商行政管理部门没收非法财物，没收违法所得，处50万元以上200万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
有[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定的行为，介绍、诱骗、胁迫他人参加传销的，由工商行政管理部门责令停止违法行为，没收非法财物，没收违法所得，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
有[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定的行为，参加传销的，由工商行政管理部门责令停止违法行为，可以处2000元以下的罚款。

**Article 24** Anyone who commits an act specified in Article 7 of these Regulations by organizing or plotting pyramid selling shall be ordered by the administrative department for industry and commerce to confiscate illegal property and illegal gains, and shall be imposed a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2 million yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Anyone who commits an act specified in Article 7 of these Regulations by introducing, enticing, or coercing others to participate in pyramid selling shall be ordered by the administrative department for industry and commerce to cease the illegal act, confiscate illegal property and illegal gains, and shall be imposed a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Anyone who commits an act specified in Article 7 of these Regulations by participating in pyramid selling shall be ordered by the administrative department for industry and commerce to cease the illegal act, and may be imposed a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　工商行政管理部门依照[本条例第二十四条](#art-24)的规定进行处罚时，可以依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，责令停业整顿或者吊销营业执照。

**Article 25** When the administrative department for industry and commerce imposes penalties in accordance with Article 24 of these Regulations, it may, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, order a suspension of business for rectification or revoke the business license.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　为[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定的传销行为提供经营场所、培训场所、货源、保管、仓储等条件的，由工商行政管理部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款。  
为[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定的传销行为提供互联网信息服务的，由工商行政管理部门责令停止违法行为，并通知有关部门依照《互联网信息服务管理办法》予以处罚。

**Article 26** Anyone who provides business premises, training venues, sources of goods, custodial services, storage, or other conditions for the pyramid selling acts specified in Article 7 of these Regulations shall be ordered by the administrative department for industry and commerce to cease the illegal act, confiscate illegal gains, and shall be imposed a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.  
Anyone who provides Internet information services for the pyramid selling acts specified in Article 7 of these Regulations shall be ordered by the administrative department for industry and commerce to cease the illegal act, and the relevant department shall be notified to impose penalties in accordance with the Measures for the Administration of Internet Information Services.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　当事人擅自动用、调换、转移、损毁被查封、扣押财物的，由工商行政管理部门责令停止违法行为，处被动用、调换、转移、损毁财物价值5%以上20%以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处被动用、调换、转移、损毁财物价值1倍以上3倍以下的罚款。

**Article 27** If a party, without authorization, uses, exchanges, transfers, or damages the sealed-up or seized property, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order the cessation of the illegal act and impose a fine of not less than 5% but not more than 20% of the value of the property that was used, exchanged, transferred, or damaged; if the party refuses to rectify, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the value of the property that was used, exchanged, transferred, or damaged shall be imposed.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　有[本条例第十条](#art-10)规定的行为或者拒绝、阻碍工商行政管理部门的执法人员依法查处传销行为，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依照治安管理的法律、行政法规规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** Anyone who commits an act specified in Article 10 of these Regulations or refuses or obstructs law enforcement officers of the administrative department for industry and commerce from investigating pyramid selling activities according to law, if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, shall be punished by the public security organ in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations on public security administration; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　工商行政管理部门、公安机关及其工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，未依照本条例规定的职责和程序查处传销行为，或者发现传销行为不予查处，或者支持、包庇、纵容传销行为，构成犯罪的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 29** If the administrative department for industry and commerce, the public security organ, or their staff members abuse their powers, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractices for personal gain; fail to investigate pyramid selling acts in accordance with the duties and procedures prescribed in these Regulations; or fail to investigate pyramid selling acts upon discovery; or support, cover up, or connive at pyramid selling acts, if a crime is constituted, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本条例自2005年11月1日起施行。

**Article 30** These Regulations shall come into force as of November 1, 2005.
