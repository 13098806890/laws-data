# 残疾预防和残疾人康复条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2018-09-18  
**生效日期**：2017-07-01

> (2017年2月7日中华人民共和国国务院令第675号公布　根据2018年9月18日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修正) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了预防残疾的发生、减轻残疾程度，帮助残疾人恢复或者补偿功能，促进残疾人平等、充分地参与社会生活，发展残疾预防和残疾人康复事业，根据《中华人民共和国残疾人保障法》，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称残疾预防，是指针对各种致残因素，采取有效措施，避免个人心理、生理、人体结构上某种组织、功能的丧失或者异常，防止全部或者部分丧失正常参与社会活动的能力。 本条例所称残疾人康复，是指在残疾发生后综合运用医学、教育、职业、社会、心理和辅助器具等措施，帮助残疾人恢复或者补偿功能，减轻功能障碍，增强生活自理和社会参与能力。 第三条　残疾预防和残疾人康复工作应当坚持以人为本，从实际出发，实行预防为主、预防与康复相结合的方针。 国家采取措施为残疾人提供基本康复服务，支持和帮助其融入社会。禁止基于残疾的歧视。 第四条　县级以上人民政府领导残疾预防和残疾人康复工作，将残疾预防和残疾人康复工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，完善残疾预防和残疾人康复服务和保障体系，建立政府主导、部门协作、社会参与的工作机制，实行工作责任制，对有关部门承担的残疾预防和残疾人康复工作进行考核和监督。乡镇人民政府和街道办事处根据本地区的实际情况，组织开展残疾预防和残疾人康复工作。 县级以上人民政府负责残疾人工作的机构，负责残疾预防和残疾人康复工作的组织实施与监督。县级以上人民政府有关部门在各自的职责范围内做好残疾预防和残疾人康复有关工作。 第五条　中国残疾人联合会及其地方组织依照法律、法规、章程或者接受政府委托，开展残疾预防和残疾人康复工作。 工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会、红十字会等依法做好残疾预防和残疾人康复工作。 第六条　国家机关、社会组织、企业事业单位和城乡基层群众性自治组织应当做好所属范围内的残疾预防和残疾人康复工作。从事残疾预防和残疾人康复工作的人员应当依法履行职责。 第七条　社会各界应当关心、支持和参与残疾预防和残疾人康复事业。 新闻媒体应当积极开展残疾预防和残疾人康复的公益宣传。 国家鼓励和支持组织、个人提供残疾预防和残疾人康复服务，捐助残疾预防和残疾人康复事业，兴建相关公益设施。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了预防残疾的发生、减轻残疾程度，帮助残疾人恢复或者补偿功能，促进残疾人平等、充分地参与社会生活，发展残疾预防和残疾人康复事业，根据《中华人民共和国残疾人保障法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Persons with Disabilities, for the purposes of preventing the occurrence of disabilities, reducing the degree of disability, helping persons with disabilities to recover or compensate for functions, promoting their equal and full participation in social life, and developing the cause of disability prevention and rehabilitation of persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称残疾预防，是指针对各种致残因素，采取有效措施，避免个人心理、生理、人体结构上某种组织、功能的丧失或者异常，防止全部或者部分丧失正常参与社会活动的能力。  
本条例所称残疾人康复，是指在残疾发生后综合运用医学、教育、职业、社会、心理和辅助器具等措施，帮助残疾人恢复或者补偿功能，减轻功能障碍，增强生活自理和社会参与能力。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "disability prevention" refers to taking effective measures against various disability-causing factors to avoid the loss or abnormality of certain tissues or functions in an individual's psychology, physiology, or human body structure, and to prevent the total or partial loss of the ability to participate normally in social activities.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "rehabilitation of persons with disabilities" refers to, after the occurrence of a disability, comprehensively applying measures such as medicine, education, vocational training, social work, psychology, and assistive devices to help persons with disabilities recover or compensate for functions, reduce functional impairments, and enhance their abilities of self-care and social participation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　残疾预防和残疾人康复工作应当坚持以人为本，从实际出发，实行预防为主、预防与康复相结合的方针。  
国家采取措施为残疾人提供基本康复服务，支持和帮助其融入社会。禁止基于残疾的歧视。

**Article 3** Disability prevention and rehabilitation of persons with disabilities shall adhere to a people-oriented approach, proceed from reality, and implement a policy of prevention first combined with prevention and rehabilitation.  
The State shall take measures to provide basic rehabilitation services for persons with disabilities, support and help them integrate into society. Discrimination based on disability is prohibited.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府领导残疾预防和残疾人康复工作，将残疾预防和残疾人康复工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，完善残疾预防和残疾人康复服务和保障体系，建立政府主导、部门协作、社会参与的工作机制，实行工作责任制，对有关部门承担的残疾预防和残疾人康复工作进行考核和监督。乡镇人民政府和街道办事处根据本地区的实际情况，组织开展残疾预防和残疾人康复工作。  
县级以上人民政府负责残疾人工作的机构，负责残疾预防和残疾人康复工作的组织实施与监督。县级以上人民政府有关部门在各自的职责范围内做好残疾预防和残疾人康复有关工作。

**Article 4** People's governments at or above the county level shall lead the work of disability prevention and rehabilitation of persons with disabilities, incorporate such work into the national economic and social development plans, improve the service and guarantee systems for disability prevention and rehabilitation, establish a working mechanism featuring government leadership, departmental coordination, and social participation, implement a work responsibility system, and conduct assessment and supervision over the disability prevention and rehabilitation work undertaken by relevant departments. Township and township people's governments and sub-district offices shall, according to their actual local conditions, organize and carry out disability prevention and rehabilitation work.  
The institutions responsible for work related to persons with disabilities under the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the organization, implementation, and supervision of disability prevention and rehabilitation work. The relevant departments under the people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, properly carry out work related to disability prevention and rehabilitation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中国残疾人联合会及其地方组织依照法律、法规、章程或者接受政府委托，开展残疾预防和残疾人康复工作。  
工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会、红十字会等依法做好残疾预防和残疾人康复工作。

**Article 5** The China Disabled Persons' Federation and its local organizations shall, in accordance with laws, regulations, articles of association, or upon entrustment by the government, carry out disability prevention and rehabilitation work.  
Trade unions, the Communist Youth League, women's federations, the Red Cross Society, etc., shall, according to law, properly carry out disability prevention and rehabilitation work.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家机关、社会组织、企业事业单位和城乡基层群众性自治组织应当做好所属范围内的残疾预防和残疾人康复工作。从事残疾预防和残疾人康复工作的人员应当依法履行职责。

**Article 6** State organs, social organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and urban and rural grassroots self-governing organizations shall properly carry out disability prevention and rehabilitation work within their respective scopes. Personnel engaged in disability prevention and rehabilitation work shall perform their duties according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　社会各界应当关心、支持和参与残疾预防和残疾人康复事业。  
新闻媒体应当积极开展残疾预防和残疾人康复的公益宣传。  
国家鼓励和支持组织、个人提供残疾预防和残疾人康复服务，捐助残疾预防和残疾人康复事业，兴建相关公益设施。

**Article 7** All sectors of society shall care about, support, and participate in the cause of disability prevention and rehabilitation.  
The news media shall actively carry out public interest publicity on disability prevention and rehabilitation.  
The State encourages and supports organizations and individuals to provide disability prevention and rehabilitation services, donate to the cause of disability prevention and rehabilitation, and construct relevant public welfare facilities.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励开展残疾预防和残疾人康复的科学研究和应用，提高残疾预防和残疾人康复的科学技术水平。  
国家鼓励开展残疾预防和残疾人康复领域的国际交流与合作。

**Article 8** The State encourages scientific research and application in disability prevention and rehabilitation to improve the scientific and technological level of disability prevention and rehabilitation.  
The State encourages international exchanges and cooperation in the field of disability prevention and rehabilitation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对在残疾预防和残疾人康复工作中作出显著成绩的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 9** Organizations and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in disability prevention and rehabilitation work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　残疾预防

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　残疾预防工作应当覆盖全人群和全生命周期，以社区和家庭为基础，坚持普遍预防和重点防控相结合。

**Article 10** Disability prevention work shall cover the entire population and the whole life cycle, be based on communities and families, and adhere to the combination of universal prevention and focused prevention and control.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上人民政府组织有关部门、残疾人联合会等开展下列残疾预防工作：  
(一)实施残疾监测，定期调查残疾状况，分析致残原因，对遗传、疾病、药物、事故等主要致残因素实施动态监测；  
(二)制定并实施残疾预防工作计划，针对主要致残因素实施重点预防，对致残风险较高的地区、人群、行业、单位实施优先干预；  
(三)做好残疾预防宣传教育工作，普及残疾预防知识。

**Article 11** People's governments at or above the county level shall organize relevant departments, the Disabled Persons' Federation, etc., to carry out the following disability prevention work:  
(1) Implement disability monitoring, regularly investigate the status of disabilities, analyze the causes of disabilities, and implement dynamic monitoring of major disability-causing factors such as heredity, disease, drugs, and accidents;  
(2) Formulate and implement disability prevention work plans, implement focused prevention for major disability-causing factors, and implement priority intervention for regions, populations, industries, and units with higher risks of disability;  
(3) Carry out education and publicity on disability prevention, and popularize knowledge about disability prevention.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　卫生主管部门在开展孕前和孕产期保健、产前筛查、产前诊断以及新生儿疾病筛查，传染病、地方病、慢性病、精神疾病等防控，心理保健指导等工作时，应当做好残疾预防工作，针对遗传、疾病、药物等致残因素，采取相应措施消除或者降低致残风险，加强临床早期康复介入，减少残疾的发生。  
公安、安全生产监督管理、食品安全监督管理、药品监督管理、生态环境、防灾减灾救灾等部门在开展交通安全、生产安全、食品安全、药品安全、生态环境保护、防灾减灾救灾等工作时，应当针对事故、环境污染、灾害等致残因素，采取相应措施，减少残疾的发生。

**Article 12** When carrying out pre-pregnancy and perinatal health care, prenatal screening, prenatal diagnosis, and newborn disease screening, prevention and control of infectious diseases, endemic diseases, chronic diseases, mental illnesses, etc., and psychological health guidance, the competent health departments shall properly carry out disability prevention work, take corresponding measures to eliminate or reduce the risk of disability against genetic, disease, drug and other disability-causing factors, strengthen early clinical rehabilitation intervention, and reduce the occurrence of disabilities.  
When carrying out work on traffic safety, production safety, food safety, drug safety, ecological environment protection, disaster prevention, mitigation and relief, etc., the public security, work safety supervision and management, food and drug supervision and management, ecological environment, disaster prevention, mitigation and relief departments shall, in light of disability-causing factors such as accidents, environmental pollution, and disasters, take corresponding measures to reduce the occurrence of disabilities.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院卫生、教育、民政等有关部门和中国残疾人联合会在履行职责时应当收集、汇总残疾人信息，实现信息共享。

**Article 13** When performing their duties, the relevant departments of the State Council, including health, education, civil affairs, and the China Disabled Persons' Federation, shall collect and aggregate information on persons with disabilities and realize information sharing.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　承担新生儿疾病和未成年人残疾筛查、诊断的医疗卫生机构应当按照规定将残疾和患有致残性疾病的未成年人信息，向所在地县级人民政府卫生主管部门报告。接到报告的卫生主管部门应当按照规定及时将相关信息与残疾人联合会共享，并共同组织开展早期干预。

**Article 14** Medical and health institutions undertaking screening and diagnosis of newborn diseases and childhood disabilities shall report information on minors with disabilities or diseases that cause disabilities to the competent health department of the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the provisions. The competent health department that receives the report shall, in accordance with the provisions, promptly share the relevant information with the Disabled Persons' Federation and jointly organize early intervention.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　具有高度致残风险的用人单位应当对职工进行残疾预防相关知识培训，告知作业场所和工作岗位存在的致残风险，并采取防护措施，提供防护设施和防护用品。

**Article 15** Employers with a high risk of disability shall provide employees with training on knowledge related to disability prevention, inform them of the disability-causing risks existing in the workplace and positions, and take protective measures, providing protective facilities and protective equipment.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家鼓励公民学习残疾预防知识和技能，提高自我防护意识和能力。  
未成年人的监护人应当保证未成年人及时接受政府免费提供的疾病和残疾筛查，努力使有出生缺陷或者致残性疾病的未成年人及时接受治疗和康复服务。未成年人、老年人的监护人或者家庭成员应当增强残疾预防意识，采取有针对性的残疾预防措施。

**Article 16** The State encourages citizens to learn knowledge and skills about disability prevention and improve their awareness and ability of self-protection.  
Guardians of minors shall ensure that minors promptly receive free disease and disability screening provided by the government, and make efforts to ensure that minors with birth defects or disabilities-causing diseases promptly receive treatment and rehabilitation services. Guardians or family members of minors and elderly persons shall enhance their awareness of disability prevention and take targeted disability prevention measures.

### 第三章　康复服务

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上人民政府应当组织卫生、教育、民政等部门和残疾人联合会整合从事残疾人康复服务的机构(以下称康复机构)、设施和人员等资源，合理布局，建立和完善以社区康复为基础、康复机构为骨干、残疾人家庭为依托的残疾人康复服务体系，以实用、易行、受益广的康复内容为重点，为残疾人提供综合性的康复服务。  
县级以上人民政府应当优先开展残疾儿童康复工作，实行康复与教育相结合。

**Article 17** People's governments at or above the county level shall organize health, education, civil affairs and other departments and the Disabled Persons' Federation to integrate resources such as institutions providing rehabilitation services for persons with disabilities (hereinafter referred to as rehabilitation institutions), facilities, and personnel, make rational layouts, establish and improve the rehabilitation service system for persons with disabilities that is based on community rehabilitation, with rehabilitation institutions as the backbone and supported by families of persons with disabilities, focusing on practical, accessible, and widely beneficial rehabilitation content, and provide comprehensive rehabilitation services for persons with disabilities.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall give priority to carrying out rehabilitation work for children with disabilities, and implement the combination of rehabilitation and education.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上人民政府根据本行政区域残疾人数量、分布状况、康复需求等情况，制定康复机构设置规划，举办公益性康复机构，将康复机构设置纳入基本公共服务体系规划。  
县级以上人民政府支持社会力量投资康复机构建设，鼓励多种形式举办康复机构。  
社会力量举办的康复机构和政府举办的康复机构在准入、执业、专业技术人员职称评定、非营利组织的财税扶持、政府购买服务等方面执行相同的政策。

**Article 18** People's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the number, distribution, and rehabilitation needs of persons with disabilities in their respective administrative regions, formulate plans for the establishment of rehabilitation institutions, establish public welfare rehabilitation institutions, and incorporate the establishment of rehabilitation institutions into the planning of the basic public service system.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall support social forces to invest in the construction of rehabilitation institutions and encourage the establishment of rehabilitation institutions in various forms.  
Rehabilitation institutions established by social forces and those established by the government shall implement the same policies regarding access, practice, professional and technical personnel title evaluation, fiscal and tax support for non-profit organizations, and government procurement of services.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　康复机构应当具有符合无障碍环境建设要求的服务场所以及与所提供康复服务相适应的专业技术人员、设施设备等条件，建立完善的康复服务管理制度。  
康复机构应当依照有关法律、法规和标准、规范的规定，为残疾人提供安全、有效的康复服务。鼓励康复机构为所在区域的社区、学校、家庭提供康复业务指导和技术支持。  
康复机构的建设标准、服务规范、管理办法由国务院有关部门商中国残疾人联合会制定。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门应当依据各自职责，加强对康复机构的监督管理。残疾人联合会应当及时汇总、发布康复机构信息，为残疾人接受康复服务提供便利，各有关部门应当予以支持。残疾人联合会接受政府委托对康复机构及其服务质量进行监督。

**Article 19** Rehabilitation institutions shall have service sites that meet the requirements for barrier-free environment construction, professional and technical personnel and facilities and equipment commensurate with the rehabilitation services provided, and establish a sound rehabilitation service management system.  
Rehabilitation institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws, regulations, standards, and norms, provide safe and effective rehabilitation services for persons with disabilities. Rehabilitation institutions are encouraged to provide rehabilitation business guidance and technical support to communities, schools, and families in their areas.  
The construction standards, service specifications, and management measures for rehabilitation institutions shall be formulated by the relevant departments of the State Council in consultation with the China Disabled Persons' Federation.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on their respective responsibilities, strengthen the supervision and management of rehabilitation institutions. The Disabled Persons' Federation shall promptly collect and publish information on rehabilitation institutions to facilitate persons with disabilities to receive rehabilitation services, and all relevant departments shall provide support. The Disabled Persons' Federation, upon entrustment by the government, shall supervise rehabilitation institutions and the quality of their services.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　各级人民政府应当将残疾人社区康复纳入社区公共服务体系。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门、残疾人联合会应当利用社区资源，根据社区残疾人数量、类型和康复需求等设立康复场所，或者通过政府购买服务方式委托社会组织，组织开展康复指导、日常生活能力训练、康复护理、辅助器具配置、信息咨询、知识普及和转介等社区康复工作。  
城乡基层群众性自治组织应当鼓励和支持残疾人及其家庭成员参加社区康复活动，融入社区生活。

**Article 20** People's governments at all levels shall incorporate community rehabilitation for persons with disabilities into the community public service system.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and the Disabled Persons' Federation shall, by utilizing community resources, establish rehabilitation venues based on the number, type, and rehabilitation needs of persons with disabilities in the community, or entrust social organizations through government procurement of services, organize and carry out community rehabilitation work such as rehabilitation guidance, daily living ability training, rehabilitation nursing, assistive device configuration, information consultation, knowledge popularization, and referral.  
Urban and rural grassroots self-governing organizations shall encourage and support persons with disabilities and their family members to participate in community rehabilitation activities and integrate into community life.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　提供残疾人康复服务，应当针对残疾人的健康、日常活动、社会参与等需求进行评估，依据评估结果制定个性化康复方案，并根据实施情况对康复方案进行调整优化。制定、实施康复方案，应当充分听取、尊重残疾人及其家属的意见，告知康复措施的详细信息。  
提供残疾人康复服务，应当保护残疾人隐私，不得歧视、侮辱残疾人。

**Article 21** Providing rehabilitation services for persons with disabilities shall involve assessing the needs of persons with disabilities in terms of health, daily activities, social participation, etc., formulating individualized rehabilitation plans based on the assessment results, and adjusting and optimizing the rehabilitation plans according to the implementation situation. When formulating and implementing rehabilitation plans, the opinions of persons with disabilities and their families shall be fully heard and respected, and detailed information on rehabilitation measures shall be provided.  
Providing rehabilitation services for persons with disabilities shall protect the privacy of persons with disabilities and shall not discriminate against or insult persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　从事残疾人康复服务的人员应当具有人道主义精神，遵守职业道德，学习掌握必要的专业知识和技能并能够熟练运用；有关法律、行政法规规定需要取得相应资格的，还应当依法取得相应的资格。

**Article 22** Personnel engaged in providing rehabilitation services for persons with disabilities shall have humanitarian spirit, abide by professional ethics, and learn, master and be able to skillfully apply necessary professional knowledge and skills; where the relevant laws and administrative regulations require the acquisition of corresponding qualifications, they shall also obtain corresponding qualifications according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　康复机构应当对其工作人员开展在岗培训，组织学习康复专业知识和技能，提高业务水平和服务能力。

**Article 23** Rehabilitation institutions shall provide on-the-job training for their staff, organize learning of rehabilitation professional knowledge and skills, and improve their professional level and service capacity.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　各级人民政府和县级以上人民政府有关部门、残疾人联合会以及康复机构等应当为残疾人及其家庭成员学习掌握康复知识和技能提供便利条件，引导残疾人主动参与康复活动，残疾人的家庭成员应当予以支持和帮助。

**Article 24** People's governments at all levels, relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level, the Disabled Persons' Federation, and rehabilitation institutions shall provide facilitating conditions for persons with disabilities and their family members to learn and master rehabilitation knowledge and skills, guide persons with disabilities to actively participate in rehabilitation activities, and family members of persons with disabilities shall provide support and assistance.

### 第四章　保障措施

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　各级人民政府应当按照社会保险的有关规定将残疾人纳入基本医疗保险范围，对纳入基本医疗保险支付范围的医疗康复费用予以支付；按照医疗救助的有关规定，对家庭经济困难的残疾人参加基本医疗保险给予补贴，并对经基本医疗保险、大病保险和其他补充医疗保险支付医疗费用后仍有困难的给予医疗救助。

**Article 25** People's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of social insurance, include persons with disabilities in the scope of basic medical insurance, and pay for medical rehabilitation expenses within the scope of basic medical insurance payment; in accordance with the relevant provisions of medical assistance, provide subsidies for persons with disabilities from families with financial difficulties to participate in basic medical insurance, and provide medical assistance to those who still have difficulties after paying medical expenses through basic medical insurance, serious illness insurance and other supplementary medical insurance.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家建立残疾儿童康复救助制度，逐步实现0－6岁视力、听力、言语、肢体、智力等残疾儿童和孤独症儿童免费得到手术、辅助器具配置和康复训练等服务；完善重度残疾人护理补贴制度；通过实施重点康复项目为城乡贫困残疾人、重度残疾人提供基本康复服务，按照国家有关规定对基本型辅助器具配置给予补贴。具体办法由国务院有关部门商中国残疾人联合会根据经济社会发展水平和残疾人康复需求等情况制定。  
国家多渠道筹集残疾人康复资金，鼓励、引导社会力量通过慈善捐赠等方式帮助残疾人接受康复服务。工伤保险基金、残疾人就业保障金等按照国家有关规定用于残疾人康复。  
有条件的地区应当根据本地实际情况提高保障标准，扩大保障范围，实施高于国家规定水平的残疾人康复保障措施。

**Article 26** The State shall establish a rehabilitation assistance system for children with disabilities, gradually realize that children aged 0-6 with visual, hearing, speech, physical, intellectual disabilities and autism receive free surgeries, assistive device configuration, rehabilitation training and other services; improve the heavy disability nursing subsidy system; provide basic rehabilitation services for urban and rural poor persons with disabilities and severely disabled persons through implementing key rehabilitation projects, and provide subsidies for basic assistive device configuration in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. The specific measures shall be formulated by the relevant departments of the State Council in consultation with the China Disabled Persons' Federation based on the level of economic and social development and the rehabilitation needs of persons with disabilities.  
The State shall raise rehabilitation funds for persons with disabilities through multiple channels, and encourage and guide social forces to help persons with disabilities receive rehabilitation services through charitable donations and other means. The work injury insurance fund, the employment security fund for persons with disabilities, etc., shall be used for the rehabilitation of persons with disabilities in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Regions with the conditions shall, according to their actual local conditions, raise the guarantee standards, expand the scope of guarantee, and implement rehabilitation guarantee measures for persons with disabilities at a level higher than that stipulated by the State.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　各级人民政府应当根据残疾预防和残疾人康复工作需要，将残疾预防和残疾人康复工作经费列入本级政府预算。  
从事残疾预防和残疾人康复服务的机构依法享受有关税收优惠政策。县级以上人民政府有关部门对相关机构给予资金、设施设备、土地使用等方面的支持。

**Article 27** People's governments at all levels shall, based on the needs of disability prevention and rehabilitation of persons with disabilities, include the expenditure for disability prevention and rehabilitation work in their respective government budgets.  
Institutions providing disability prevention and rehabilitation services shall enjoy relevant tax preferential policies according to law. The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall provide support to relevant institutions in terms of funds, facilities and equipment, and land use.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家加强残疾预防和残疾人康复专业人才的培养；鼓励和支持高等学校、职业学校设置残疾预防和残疾人康复相关专业或者开设相关课程，培养专业技术人员。  
县级以上人民政府卫生、教育等有关部门应当将残疾预防和残疾人康复知识、技能纳入卫生、教育等相关专业技术人员的继续教育。

**Article 28** The State shall strengthen the cultivation of professionals in disability prevention and rehabilitation; encourage and support institutions of higher education and vocational schools to set up relevant majors or courses on disability prevention and rehabilitation, and train professional and technical personnel.  
The relevant departments of health, education, etc., of the people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate knowledge and skills of disability prevention and rehabilitation into the continuing education of relevant professional and technical personnel such as health and education personnel.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国务院人力资源社会保障部门应当会同国务院有关部门和中国残疾人联合会，根据残疾预防和残疾人康复工作需要，完善残疾预防和残疾人康复专业技术人员职业能力水平评价体系。

**Article 29** The human resources and social security department of the State Council shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council and the China Disabled Persons' Federation, improve the professional ability evaluation system for professional and technical personnel in disability prevention and rehabilitation according to the needs of disability prevention and rehabilitation work.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　省级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当积极支持辅助器具的研发、推广和应用。  
辅助器具研发、生产单位依法享受有关税收优惠政策。

**Article 30** People's governments at or above the provincial level and their relevant departments shall actively support the research and development, promotion and application of assistive devices.  
Units that research, develop, and produce assistive devices shall enjoy relevant tax preferential policies according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　各级人民政府和县级以上人民政府有关部门按照国家有关规定，保障残疾预防和残疾人康复工作人员的待遇。县级以上人民政府人力资源社会保障等部门应当在培训进修、表彰奖励等方面，对残疾预防和残疾人康复工作人员予以倾斜。

**Article 31** People's governments at all levels and the relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, guarantee the treatment of staff engaged in disability prevention and rehabilitation. The human resources and social security and other departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall give preferential treatment to staff engaged in disability prevention and rehabilitation in terms of training and further education, commendation and rewards, etc.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　地方各级人民政府和县级以上人民政府有关部门未依照本条例规定履行残疾预防和残疾人康复工作职责，或者滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员给予处分。  
各级残疾人联合会有违反本条例规定的情形的，依法对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员给予处分。

**Article 32** Where local people's governments at all levels and the relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level fail to perform their duties for disability prevention and rehabilitation in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, or abuse their powers, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractices for personal gain, the responsible leaders and directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions according to law.  
Where the Disabled Persons' Federation at various levels has circumstances in violation of these Regulations, the responsible leaders and directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　医疗卫生机构、康复机构及其工作人员未依照本条例规定开展残疾预防和残疾人康复工作的，由有关主管部门按照各自职责分工责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，责令暂停相关执业活动，依法对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员给予处分。

**Article 33** Where medical and health institutions, rehabilitation institutions, and their staff fail to carry out disability prevention and rehabilitation work in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the relevant competent departments shall, within their respective functions and duties, order them to make corrections and give warnings; if the circumstances are serious, they shall order the suspension of relevant practice activities, and impose sanctions on the responsible leaders and directly responsible personnel according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　具有高度致残风险的用人单位未履行[本条例第十五条](#art-15)规定的残疾预防义务，违反安全生产、职业病防治等法律、行政法规规定的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定给予处罚；有关法律、行政法规没有规定的，由有关主管部门按照各自职责分工责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正的，责令停产停业整顿。用人单位还应当依法承担救治、保障等义务。

**Article 34** Where an employer with a high risk of disability fails to fulfill the obligation of disability prevention as stipulated in Article 15 of these Regulations, violating the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on work safety and occupational disease prevention, the employer shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; where there are no provisions in relevant laws and administrative regulations, the relevant competent departments shall, within their respective functions and duties, order it to make corrections and give a warning; if it refuses to make corrections, order it to suspend production and business for rectification. The employer shall also bear the obligations of treatment and guarantee according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；造成人身、财产损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 35** Whoever violates the provisions of these Regulations, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if it causes personal or property losses, shall bear compensation liability according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本条例自2017年7月1日起施行。

**Article 36** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 2017.
