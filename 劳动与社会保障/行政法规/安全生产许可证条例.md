# 安全生产许可证条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2014-07-29  
**生效日期**：2014-07-29

> (2004年1月13日中华人民共和国国务院令第397号公布　根据2013年7月18日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2014年7月29日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一条　为了严格规范安全生产条件，进一步加强安全生产监督管理，防止和减少生产安全事故，根据《中华人民共和国安全生产法》的有关规定，制定本条例。 第二条　国家对矿山企业、建筑施工企业和危险化学品、烟花爆竹、民用爆炸物品生产企业(以下统称企业)实行安全生产许可制度。 企业未取得安全生产许可证的，不得从事生产活动。 第三条　国务院安全生产监督管理部门负责中央管理的非煤矿矿山企业和危险化学品、烟花爆竹生产企业安全生产许可证的颁发和管理。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府安全生产监督管理部门负责前款规定以外的非煤矿矿山企业和危险化学品、烟花爆竹生产企业安全生产许可证的颁发和管理，并接受国务院安全生产监督管理部门的指导和监督。 国家煤矿安全监察机构负责中央管理的煤矿企业安全生产许可证的颁发和管理。 在省、自治区、直辖市设立的煤矿安全监察机构负责前款规定以外的其他煤矿企业安全生产许可证的颁发和管理，并接受国家煤矿安全监察机构的指导和监督。 第四条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府建设主管部门负责建筑施工企业安全生产许可证的颁发和管理，并接受国务院建设主管部门的指导和监督。 第五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民用爆炸物品行业主管部门负责民用爆炸物品生产企业安全生产许可证的颁发和管理，并接受国务院民用爆炸物品行业主管部门的指导和监督。 第六条　企业取得安全生产许可证，应当具备下列安全生产条件： (一)建立、健全安全生产责任制，制定完备的安全生产规章制度和操作规程； (二)安全投入符合安全生产要求； (三)设置安全生产管理机构，配备专职安全生产管理人员； (四)主要负责人和安全生产管理人员经考核合格；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了严格规范安全生产条件，进一步加强安全生产监督管理，防止和减少生产安全事故，根据《中华人民共和国安全生产法》的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Work Safety Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of strictly regulating work safety conditions, further strengthening the supervision and management of work safety, and preventing and reducing work safety accidents.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家对矿山企业、建筑施工企业和危险化学品、烟花爆竹、民用爆炸物品生产企业(以下统称企业)实行安全生产许可制度。  
企业未取得安全生产许可证的，不得从事生产活动。

**Article 2** The State shall implement a work safety permit system for mining enterprises, construction enterprises, and enterprises producing hazardous chemicals, fireworks and firecrackers, and civil explosives (hereinafter collectively referred to as enterprises).  
An enterprise that has not obtained a work safety permit shall not engage in production activities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院安全生产监督管理部门负责中央管理的非煤矿矿山企业和危险化学品、烟花爆竹生产企业安全生产许可证的颁发和管理。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府安全生产监督管理部门负责前款规定以外的非煤矿矿山企业和危险化学品、烟花爆竹生产企业安全生产许可证的颁发和管理，并接受国务院安全生产监督管理部门的指导和监督。  
国家煤矿安全监察机构负责中央管理的煤矿企业安全生产许可证的颁发和管理。  
在省、自治区、直辖市设立的煤矿安全监察机构负责前款规定以外的其他煤矿企业安全生产许可证的颁发和管理，并接受国家煤矿安全监察机构的指导和监督。

**Article 3** The work safety supervision and administration department of the State Council shall be responsible for the issuance and administration of work safety permits for non-coal mining enterprises under the central administration and enterprises producing hazardous chemicals, fireworks and firecrackers.  
The work safety supervision and administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the issuance and administration of work safety permits for non-coal mining enterprises and enterprises producing hazardous chemicals, fireworks and firecrackers other than those specified in the preceding paragraph, and shall accept the guidance and supervision of the work safety supervision and administration department of the State Council.  
The National Coal Mine Safety Supervision Bureau shall be responsible for the issuance and administration of work safety permits for coal mining enterprises under the central administration.  
The coal mine safety supervision agencies established in provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the issuance and administration of work safety permits for other coal mining enterprises other than those specified in the preceding paragraph, and shall accept the guidance and supervision of the National Coal Mine Safety Supervision Bureau.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府建设主管部门负责建筑施工企业安全生产许可证的颁发和管理，并接受国务院建设主管部门的指导和监督。

**Article 4** The construction administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the issuance and administration of work safety permits for construction enterprises, and shall accept the guidance and supervision of the construction administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民用爆炸物品行业主管部门负责民用爆炸物品生产企业安全生产许可证的颁发和管理，并接受国务院民用爆炸物品行业主管部门的指导和监督。

**Article 5** The competent department of the civil explosives industry of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the issuance and administration of work safety permits for enterprises producing civil explosives, and shall accept the guidance and supervision of the competent department of the civil explosives industry of the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　企业取得安全生产许可证，应当具备下列安全生产条件：  
(一)建立、健全安全生产责任制，制定完备的安全生产规章制度和操作规程；  
(二)安全投入符合安全生产要求；  
(三)设置安全生产管理机构，配备专职安全生产管理人员；  
(四)主要负责人和安全生产管理人员经考核合格；  
(五)特种作业人员经有关业务主管部门考核合格，取得特种作业操作资格证书；  
(六)从业人员经安全生产教育和培训合格；  
(七)依法参加工伤保险，为从业人员缴纳保险费；  
(八)厂房、作业场所和安全设施、设备、工艺符合有关安全生产法律、法规、标准和规程的要求；  
(九)有职业危害防治措施，并为从业人员配备符合国家标准或者行业标准的劳动防护用品；  
(十)依法进行安全评价；  
(十一)有重大危险源检测、评估、监控措施和应急预案；  
(十二)有生产安全事故应急救援预案、应急救援组织或者应急救援人员，配备必要的应急救援器材、设备；  
(十三)法律、法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 6** To obtain a work safety permit, an enterprise shall meet the following work safety conditions:  
(1) establish and improve a work safety responsibility system, and formulate complete work safety rules and regulations and operating procedures;  
(2) safety investment meets the work safety requirements;  
(3) set up a work safety management organization and allocate full-time work safety management personnel;  
(4) the principal responsible person and the work safety management personnel have passed the assessment;  
(5) special operations personnel have passed the assessment by the relevant competent business departments and obtained the special operations operation qualification certificate;  
(6) employees have passed work safety education and training;  
(7) participate in work-related injury insurance according to law and pay insurance premiums for employees;  
(8) the workshops, workplaces, safety facilities, equipment, and processes comply with the requirements of relevant laws, regulations, standards, and procedures on work safety;  
(9) have measures for the prevention and control of occupational hazards, and provide employees with labor protection articles that meet national or industry standards;  
(10) conduct a safety assessment according to law;  
(11) have measures for the detection, assessment, monitoring, and emergency response plans for major hazard sources;  
(12) have emergency rescue plans for work safety accidents, emergency rescue organizations or emergency rescue personnel, and are equipped with necessary emergency rescue equipment and devices;  
(13) other conditions prescribed by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　企业进行生产前，应当依照本条例的规定向安全生产许可证颁发管理机关申请领取安全生产许可证，并提供[本条例第六条](#art-6)规定的相关文件、资料。安全生产许可证颁发管理机关应当自收到申请之日起45日内审查完毕，经审查符合本条例规定的安全生产条件的，颁发安全生产许可证；不符合本条例规定的安全生产条件的，不予颁发安全生产许可证，书面通知企业并说明理由。  
煤矿企业应当以矿(井)为单位，依照本条例的规定取得安全生产许可证。

**Article 7** Before commencing production, an enterprise shall, in accordance with these Regulations, apply to the authority issuing and administering work safety permits for a work safety permit, and provide the relevant documents and materials specified in Article 6 of these Regulations. The authority issuing and administering work safety permits shall complete the examination within 45 days from the date of receiving the application. If, upon examination, the enterprise meets the work safety conditions specified in these Regulations, it shall issue a work safety permit; if the enterprise does not meet the work safety conditions specified in these Regulations, it shall not issue a work safety permit, and shall notify the enterprise in writing and state the reasons.  
A coal mining enterprise shall, on a mine (shaft) basis, obtain a work safety permit in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　安全生产许可证由国务院安全生产监督管理部门规定统一的式样。

**Article 8** The work safety supervision and administration department of the State Council shall prescribe a unified format for the work safety permit.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　安全生产许可证的有效期为3年。安全生产许可证有效期满需要延期的，企业应当于期满前3个月向原安全生产许可证颁发管理机关办理延期手续。  
企业在安全生产许可证有效期内，严格遵守有关安全生产的法律法规，未发生死亡事故的，安全生产许可证有效期届满时，经原安全生产许可证颁发管理机关同意，不再审查，安全生产许可证有效期延期3年。

**Article 9** The validity period of a work safety permit shall be three years. If an enterprise needs to extend the validity period upon its expiration, it shall go through the extension formalities with the original authority that issued the work safety permit three months before the expiration.  
Where, during the validity period of the work safety permit, the enterprise has strictly observed the relevant laws and regulations on work safety and has not had any fatal accident, upon the expiration of the validity period of the work safety permit, with the consent of the original authority that issued the work safety permit, no re-examination shall be conducted, and the validity period of the work safety permit shall be extended for three years.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　安全生产许可证颁发管理机关应当建立、健全安全生产许可证档案管理制度，并定期向社会公布企业取得安全生产许可证的情况。

**Article 10** The authority issuing and administering work safety permits shall establish and improve a file management system for work safety permits, and shall regularly announce to the public the circumstances under which enterprises have obtained work safety permits.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　煤矿企业安全生产许可证颁发管理机关、建筑施工企业安全生产许可证颁发管理机关、民用爆炸物品生产企业安全生产许可证颁发管理机关，应当每年向同级安全生产监督管理部门通报其安全生产许可证颁发和管理情况。

**Article 11** The authority issuing and administering work safety permits for coal mining enterprises, the authority issuing and administering work safety permits for construction enterprises, and the authority issuing and administering work safety permits for enterprises producing civil explosives shall, each year, report to the work safety supervision and administration department at the same level on their issuance and administration of work safety permits.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院安全生产监督管理部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府安全生产监督管理部门对建筑施工企业、民用爆炸物品生产企业、煤矿企业取得安全生产许可证的情况进行监督。

**Article 12** The work safety supervision and administration department of the State Council and the work safety supervision and administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall supervise the circumstances under which construction enterprises, enterprises producing civil explosives, and coal mining enterprises have obtained work safety permits.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　企业不得转让、冒用安全生产许可证或者使用伪造的安全生产许可证。

**Article 13** An enterprise shall not transfer, fraudulently use, or use a forged work safety permit.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　企业取得安全生产许可证后，不得降低安全生产条件，并应当加强日常安全生产管理，接受安全生产许可证颁发管理机关的监督检查。  
安全生产许可证颁发管理机关应当加强对取得安全生产许可证的企业的监督检查，发现其不再具备本条例规定的安全生产条件的，应当暂扣或者吊销安全生产许可证。

**Article 14** After obtaining a work safety permit, an enterprise shall not lower its work safety conditions, and shall strengthen its daily work safety management and accept the supervision and inspection by the authority issuing and administering work safety permits.  
The authority issuing and administering work safety permits shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of enterprises that have obtained work safety permits. If it finds that an enterprise no longer meets the work safety conditions specified in these Regulations, it shall suspend or revoke the work safety permit.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　安全生产许可证颁发管理机关工作人员在安全生产许可证颁发、管理和监督检查工作中，不得索取或者接受企业的财物，不得谋取其他利益。

**Article 15** The staff members of the authority issuing and administering work safety permits shall not demand or accept property from enterprises, or seek other benefits, in the work of issuing, administering, and supervising and inspecting work safety permits.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　监察机关依照《中华人民共和国行政监察法》的规定，对安全生产许可证颁发管理机关及其工作人员履行本条例规定的职责实施监察。

**Article 16** The supervisory organs shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China, supervise the performance of duties specified in these Regulations by the authority issuing and administering work safety permits and its staff members.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　任何单位或者个人对违反本条例规定的行为，有权向安全生产许可证颁发管理机关或者监察机关等有关部门举报。

**Article 17** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report to the authority issuing and administering work safety permits, the supervisory organs, or other relevant departments any act violating the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　安全生产许可证颁发管理机关工作人员有下列行为之一的，给予降级或者撤职的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)向不符合本条例规定的安全生产条件的企业颁发安全生产许可证的；  
(二)发现企业未依法取得安全生产许可证擅自从事生产活动，不依法处理的；  
(三)发现取得安全生产许可证的企业不再具备本条例规定的安全生产条件，不依法处理的；  
(四)接到对违反本条例规定行为的举报后，不及时处理的；  
(五)在安全生产许可证颁发、管理和监督检查工作中，索取或者接受企业的财物，或者谋取其他利益的。

**Article 18** Where a staff member of the authority issuing and administering work safety permits commits any of the following acts, a disciplinary sanction of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) issuing a work safety permit to an enterprise that does not meet the work safety conditions specified in these Regulations;  
(2) discovering that an enterprise without lawfully obtaining a work safety permit is engaged in production activities without authorization, and fails to handle it according to law;  
(3) discovering that an enterprise that has obtained a work safety permit no longer meets the work safety conditions specified in these Regulations, and fails to handle it according to law;  
(4) upon receiving a report of an act violating the provisions of these Regulations, failing to handle it in a timely manner;  
(5) demanding or accepting property from an enterprise, or seeking other benefits, in the work of issuing, administering, and supervising and inspecting work safety permits.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　违反本条例规定，未取得安全生产许可证擅自进行生产的，责令停止生产，没收违法所得，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；造成重大事故或者其他严重后果，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 19** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, an enterprise engages in production without obtaining a work safety permit, it shall be ordered to stop production, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall be concurrently fined not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000; if a major accident or other serious consequences are caused and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　违反本条例规定，安全生产许可证有效期满未办理延期手续，继续进行生产的，责令停止生产，限期补办延期手续，没收违法所得，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；逾期仍不办理延期手续，继续进行生产的，依照[本条例第十九条](#art-19)的规定处罚。

**Article 20** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, an enterprise continues production without going through the extension formalities after the validity period of the work safety permit has expired, it shall be ordered to stop production, given a time limit to go through the extension formalities, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and it shall be concurrently fined not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 100,000; if it still fails to go through the extension formalities within the time limit and continues production, it shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 19 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　违反本条例规定，转让安全生产许可证的，没收违法所得，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款，并吊销其安全生产许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；接受转让的，依照[本条例第十九条](#art-19)的规定处罚。  
冒用安全生产许可证或者使用伪造的安全生产许可证的，依照[本条例第十九条](#art-19)的规定处罚。

**Article 21** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, an enterprise transfers its work safety permit, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, it shall be fined not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000, and its work safety permit shall be revoked; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; the party that accepts the transfer shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 19 of these Regulations.  
Whoever fraudulently uses a work safety permit or uses a forged work safety permit shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 19 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本条例施行前已经进行生产的企业，应当自本条例施行之日起1年内，依照本条例的规定向安全生产许可证颁发管理机关申请办理安全生产许可证；逾期不办理安全生产许可证，或者经审查不符合本条例规定的安全生产条件，未取得安全生产许可证，继续进行生产的，依照[本条例第十九条](#art-19)的规定处罚。

**Article 22** An enterprise that has already engaged in production before these Regulations come into force shall, within one year from the date on which these Regulations come into force, apply to the authority issuing and administering work safety permits for a work safety permit in accordance with these Regulations; if it fails to apply for a work safety permit within the time limit, or if, upon examination, it does not meet the work safety conditions specified in these Regulations and fails to obtain a work safety permit, and continues production, it shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 19 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本条例规定的行政处罚，由安全生产许可证颁发管理机关决定。

**Article 23** The administrative penalties prescribed in these Regulations shall be decided by the authority issuing and administering work safety permits.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 24** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
