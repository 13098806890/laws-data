# 中华人民共和国食品安全法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2019-10-11  
**生效日期**：2019-12-01

> (2009年7月20日中华人民共和国国务院令第557号公布　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订　2019年3月26日国务院第42次常务会议修订通过　2019年10月11日中华人民共和国国务院令第721号公布　自2019年12月1日起施行)

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国食品安全法》(以下简称食品安全法)，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Food Safety Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　食品生产经营者应当依照法律、法规和食品安全标准从事生产经营活动，建立健全食品安全管理制度，采取有效措施预防和控制食品安全风险，保证食品安全。

**Article 2** Food producers and traders shall engage in production and trading activities in accordance with laws, regulations, and food safety standards, establish and improve a food safety management system, adopt effective measures to prevent and control food safety risks, and ensure food safety.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院食品安全委员会负责分析食品安全形势，研究部署、统筹指导食品安全工作，提出食品安全监督管理的重大政策措施，督促落实食品安全监督管理责任。县级以上地方人民政府食品安全委员会按照本级人民政府规定的职责开展工作。

**Article 3** The Food Safety Committee of the State Council is responsible for analyzing the food safety situation, studying and deploying, coordinating and guiding food safety work, proposing major policies and measures for food safety supervision and administration, and supervising the implementation of food safety supervision and administration responsibilities. The food safety committees of local people's governments at or above the county level shall carry out work in accordance with the duties prescribed by the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府建立统一权威的食品安全监督管理体制，加强食品安全监督管理能力建设。  
县级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门和其他有关部门应当依法履行职责，加强协调配合，做好食品安全监督管理工作。  
乡镇人民政府和街道办事处应当支持、协助县级人民政府食品安全监督管理部门及其派出机构依法开展食品安全监督管理工作。

**Article 4** People's governments at or above the county level shall establish a unified and authoritative food safety supervision and administration system, and strengthen the capacity building for food safety supervision and administration.  
The food safety supervision and administration departments and other relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall perform their duties according to law, strengthen coordination and cooperation, and properly carry out food safety supervision and administration.  
Township people's governments and sub-district offices shall support and assist the food safety supervision and administration departments of county people's governments and their dispatched agencies in carrying out food safety supervision and administration according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家将食品安全知识纳入国民素质教育内容，普及食品安全科学常识和法律知识，提高全社会的食品安全意识。

**Article 5** The State shall incorporate food safety knowledge into the content of national quality education, popularize common scientific knowledge and legal knowledge on food safety, and enhance the food safety awareness of the whole society.

### 第二章　食品安全风险监测和评估

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门会同同级食品安全监督管理等部门建立食品安全风险监测会商机制，汇总、分析风险监测数据，研判食品安全风险，形成食品安全风险监测分析报告，报本级人民政府；县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门还应当将食品安全风险监测分析报告同时报上一级人民政府卫生行政部门。食品安全风险监测会商的具体办法由国务院卫生行政部门会同国务院食品安全监督管理等部门制定。

**Article 6** The health administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with the food safety supervision and administration departments and other departments at the same level, establish a food safety risk monitoring consultation mechanism, collect and analyze risk monitoring data, assess food safety risks, form a food safety risk monitoring analysis report, and submit it to the people's government at the same level; the health administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall also submit the food safety risk monitoring analysis report to the health administrative department of the people's government at the next higher level. The specific measures for food safety risk monitoring consultation shall be formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council together with the food safety supervision and administration department and other departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　食品安全风险监测结果表明存在食品安全隐患，食品安全监督管理等部门经进一步调查确认有必要通知相关食品生产经营者的，应当及时通知。  
接到通知的食品生产经营者应当立即进行自查，发现食品不符合食品安全标准或者有证据证明可能危害人体健康的，应当依照[食品安全法第六十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-63)的规定停止生产、经营，实施食品召回，并报告相关情况。

**Article 7** Where the results of food safety risk monitoring indicate the existence of potential food safety hazards, and the food safety supervision and administration departments and other departments, after further investigation, confirm that it is necessary to notify the relevant food producers and traders, they shall notify them in a timely manner.  
Upon receipt of the notification, the food producer or trader shall immediately conduct self-inspection; if it finds that the food does not comply with food safety standards or if there is evidence proving that it may endanger human health, it shall cease production and trading, implement a food recall, and report the relevant circumstances in accordance with Article 63 of the Food Safety Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院卫生行政、食品安全监督管理等部门发现需要对农药、肥料、兽药、饲料和饲料添加剂等进行安全性评估的，应当向国务院农业行政部门提出安全性评估建议。国务院农业行政部门应当及时组织评估，并向国务院有关部门通报评估结果。

**Article 8** Where the health administrative department, the food safety supervision and administration department, and other departments of the State Council find that safety assessment is needed for pesticides, fertilizers, veterinary drugs, feed, and feed additives, they shall propose safety assessment recommendations to the agricultural administrative department of the State Council. The agricultural administrative department of the State Council shall promptly organize the assessment and notify the relevant departments of the State Council of the assessment results.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院食品安全监督管理部门和其他有关部门建立食品安全风险信息交流机制，明确食品安全风险信息交流的内容、程序和要求。

**Article 9** The food safety supervision and administration department of the State Council and other relevant departments shall establish a food safety risk information exchange mechanism, and specify the content, procedures, and requirements for food safety risk information exchange.

### 第三章　食品安全标准

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院卫生行政部门会同国务院食品安全监督管理、农业行政等部门制定食品安全国家标准规划及其年度实施计划。国务院卫生行政部门应当在其网站上公布食品安全国家标准规划及其年度实施计划的草案，公开征求意见。

**Article 10** The health administrative department of the State Council, together with the food safety supervision and administration department, the agricultural administrative department, and other departments of the State Council, shall formulate national food safety standards plans and their annual implementation plans. The health administrative department of the State Council shall publish the drafts of the national food safety standards plans and their annual implementation plans on its website to solicit public comments.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门依照[食品安全法第二十九条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-29)的规定制定食品安全地方标准，应当公开征求意见。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门应当自食品安全地方标准公布之日起30个工作日内，将地方标准报国务院卫生行政部门备案。国务院卫生行政部门发现备案的食品安全地方标准违反法律、法规或者食品安全国家标准的，应当及时予以纠正。  
食品安全地方标准依法废止的，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门应当及时在其网站上公布废止情况。

**Article 11** The health administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate local food safety standards in accordance with Article 29 of the Food Safety Law, and shall solicit public comments. The health administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, within 30 working days from the date of publication of the local food safety standards, submit the local standards to the health administrative department of the State Council for recordation. Where the health administrative department of the State Council finds that a local food safety standard filed violates laws, regulations, or national food safety standards, it shall promptly correct it.  
Where a local food safety standard is abolished according to law, the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall promptly publish the abolition information on its website.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　保健食品、特殊医学用途配方食品、婴幼儿配方食品等特殊食品不属于地方特色食品，不得对其制定食品安全地方标准。

**Article 12** Special foods such as health foods, foods for special medical purposes, and infant formula foods are not local specialty foods, and local food safety standards shall not be formulated for them.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　食品安全标准公布后，食品生产经营者可以在食品安全标准规定的实施日期之前实施并公开提前实施情况。

**Article 13** After the publication of food safety standards, food producers and traders may implement them before the implementation date specified in the food safety standards and disclose the early implementation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　食品生产企业不得制定低于食品安全国家标准或者地方标准要求的企业标准。食品生产企业制定食品安全指标严于食品安全国家标准或者地方标准的企业标准的，应当报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门备案。  
食品生产企业制定企业标准的，应当公开，供公众免费查阅。

**Article 14** Food production enterprises shall not formulate enterprise standards that are lower than the requirements of national food safety standards or local standards. Where a food production enterprise formulates an enterprise standard with food safety indicators stricter than those of national food safety standards or local standards, it shall report to the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for recordation.  
Where a food production enterprise formulates an enterprise standard, it shall disclose it for public free inspection.

### 第四章　食品生产经营

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　食品生产经营许可的有效期为5年。  
食品生产经营者的生产经营条件发生变化，不再符合食品生产经营要求的，食品生产经营者应当立即采取整改措施；需要重新办理许可手续的，应当依法办理。

**Article 15** The validity period of a food production and trading license is five years.  
Where the production and trading conditions of a food producer or trader change and no longer meet the requirements for food production and trading, the food producer or trader shall immediately take corrective measures; if it is necessary to re-apply for a license, it shall do so according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院卫生行政部门应当及时公布新的食品原料、食品添加剂新品种和食品相关产品新品种目录以及所适用的食品安全国家标准。  
对按照传统既是食品又是中药材的物质目录，国务院卫生行政部门会同国务院食品安全监督管理部门应当及时更新。

**Article 16** The health administrative department of the State Council shall promptly publish the catalogues of new food raw materials, new varieties of food additives, and new varieties of food-related products, as well as the applicable national food safety standards.  
The health administrative department of the State Council, together with the food safety supervision and administration department of the State Council, shall promptly update the catalogue of substances that are traditionally both food and Chinese medicinal materials.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院食品安全监督管理部门会同国务院农业行政等有关部门明确食品安全全程追溯基本要求，指导食品生产经营者通过信息化手段建立、完善食品安全追溯体系。  
食品安全监督管理等部门应当将婴幼儿配方食品等针对特定人群的食品以及其他食品安全风险较高或者销售量大的食品的追溯体系建设作为监督检查的重点。

**Article 17** The food safety supervision and administration department of the State Council, together with the agricultural administrative department and other relevant departments of the State Council, shall specify the basic requirements for whole-process traceability of food safety, and guide food producers and traders to establish and improve a food safety traceability system through information technology means.  
The food safety supervision and administration departments and other departments shall take the construction of traceability systems for infant formula foods and other foods intended for specific populations, as well as other foods with high food safety risks or large sales volumes, as the focus of supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　食品生产经营者应当建立食品安全追溯体系，依照食品安全法的规定如实记录并保存进货查验、出厂检验、食品销售等信息，保证食品可追溯。

**Article 18** Food producers and traders shall establish a food safety traceability system, and truthfully record and preserve information such as incoming inspection, factory inspection, and food sales in accordance with the Food Safety Law, to ensure traceability of food.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　食品生产经营企业的主要负责人对本企业的食品安全工作全面负责，建立并落实本企业的食品安全责任制，加强供货者管理、进货查验和出厂检验、生产经营过程控制、食品安全自查等工作。食品生产经营企业的食品安全管理人员应当协助企业主要负责人做好食品安全管理工作。

**Article 19** The principal person in charge of a food production and trading enterprise shall assume overall responsibility for the food safety work of the enterprise, establish and implement the enterprise's food safety responsibility system, and strengthen the management of suppliers, incoming inspection and factory inspection, production and trading process control, and self-inspection of food safety. The food safety management personnel of a food production and trading enterprise shall assist the principal person in charge in properly carrying out food safety management.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　食品生产经营企业应当加强对食品安全管理人员的培训和考核。食品安全管理人员应当掌握与其岗位相适应的食品安全法律、法规、标准和专业知识，具备食品安全管理能力。食品安全监督管理部门应当对企业食品安全管理人员进行随机监督抽查考核。考核指南由国务院食品安全监督管理部门制定、公布。

**Article 20** Food production and trading enterprises shall strengthen the training and assessment of food safety management personnel. Food safety management personnel shall master the food safety laws, regulations, standards, and professional knowledge appropriate to their positions, and possess food safety management capabilities. The food safety supervision and administration departments shall conduct random supervision and spot-check assessments of enterprise food safety management personnel. The assessment guidelines shall be formulated and published by the food safety supervision and administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　食品、食品添加剂生产经营者委托生产食品、食品添加剂的，应当委托取得食品生产许可、食品添加剂生产许可的生产者生产，并对其生产行为进行监督，对委托生产的食品、食品添加剂的安全负责。受托方应当依照法律、法规、食品安全标准以及合同约定进行生产，对生产行为负责，并接受委托方的监督。

**Article 21** Where a food or food additive producer or trader commissions the production of food or food additives, it shall commission a producer that has obtained a food production license or food additive production license to produce, and shall supervise its production activities and be responsible for the safety of the commissioned food or food additives. The commissioned party shall produce in accordance with laws, regulations, food safety standards, and the contract, be responsible for the production activities, and accept supervision by the commissioning party.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　食品生产经营者不得在食品生产、加工场所贮存依照[本条例第六十三条](#art-63)规定制定的名录中的物质。

**Article 22** A food producer or trader shall not store in the food production or processing premises any substance listed in the catalog formulated in accordance with Article 63 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对食品进行辐照加工，应当遵守食品安全国家标准，并按照食品安全国家标准的要求对辐照加工食品进行检验和标注。

**Article 23** The irradiation processing of food shall comply with the national food safety standards, and the irradiated food shall be inspected and labeled in accordance with the requirements of the national food safety standards.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　贮存、运输对温度、湿度等有特殊要求的食品，应当具备保温、冷藏或者冷冻等设备设施，并保持有效运行。

**Article 24** For the storage and transportation of food with special requirements such as temperature and humidity, the equipment and facilities such as heat preservation, refrigeration, or freezing shall be provided and kept in effective operation.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　食品生产经营者委托贮存、运输食品的，应当对受托方的食品安全保障能力进行审核，并监督受托方按照保证食品安全的要求贮存、运输食品。受托方应当保证食品贮存、运输条件符合食品安全的要求，加强食品贮存、运输过程管理。  
接受食品生产经营者委托贮存、运输食品的，应当如实记录委托方和收货方的名称、地址、联系方式等内容。记录保存期限不得少于贮存、运输结束后2年。  
非食品生产经营者从事对温度、湿度等有特殊要求的食品贮存业务的，应当自取得营业执照之日起30个工作日内向所在地县级人民政府食品安全监督管理部门备案。

**Article 25** Where a food producer or trader commissions the storage or transportation of food, it shall examine the food safety assurance capability of the commissioned party and supervise the commissioned party to store and transport the food in accordance with the requirements for ensuring food safety. The commissioned party shall ensure that the food storage and transportation conditions meet the food safety requirements and strengthen the management of the food storage and transportation process.  
Where a party accepts the commission from a food producer or trader to store or transport food, it shall truthfully record the names, addresses, contact information, etc. of the commissioning party and the receiving party. The retention period of records shall not be less than two years after the end of storage or transportation.  
Where a non-food producer or trader engages in the storage of food with special requirements such as temperature and humidity, it shall, within 30 working days from the date of obtaining the business license, file a record with the food safety supervision and administration department of the local county people's government.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　餐饮服务提供者委托餐具饮具集中消毒服务单位提供清洗消毒服务的，应当查验、留存餐具饮具集中消毒服务单位的营业执照复印件和消毒合格证明。保存期限不得少于消毒餐具饮具使用期限到期后6个月。

**Article 26** Where a catering service provider commissions a centralized disinfection service unit for tableware and drinking utensils to provide cleaning and disinfection services, it shall verify and retain the copy of the business license and the disinfection qualification certificate of the centralized disinfection service unit for tableware and drinking utensils. The retention period shall not be less than six months after the expiration of the use period of the disinfected tableware and drinking utensils.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　餐具饮具集中消毒服务单位应当建立餐具饮具出厂检验记录制度，如实记录出厂餐具饮具的数量、消毒日期和批号、使用期限、出厂日期以及委托方名称、地址、联系方式等内容。出厂检验记录保存期限不得少于消毒餐具饮具使用期限到期后6个月。消毒后的餐具饮具应当在独立包装上标注单位名称、地址、联系方式、消毒日期和批号以及使用期限等内容。

**Article 27** A centralized disinfection service unit for tableware and drinking utensils shall establish a system for inspection records of tableware and drinking utensils leaving the factory, and truthfully record the quantity, disinfection date and batch number, use period, date of leaving the factory, and the name, address, contact information of the commissioning party, etc. The retention period of the inspection records of leaving the factory shall not be less than six months after the expiration of the use period of the disinfected tableware and drinking utensils. The disinfected tableware and drinking utensils shall be marked on the individual packaging with the unit's name, address, contact information, disinfection date and batch number, and use period.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　学校、托幼机构、养老机构、建筑工地等集中用餐单位的食堂应当执行原料控制、餐具饮具清洗消毒、食品留样等制度，并依照[食品安全法第四十七条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-47)的规定定期开展食堂食品安全自查。  
承包经营集中用餐单位食堂的，应当依法取得食品经营许可，并对食堂的食品安全负责。集中用餐单位应当督促承包方落实食品安全管理制度，承担管理责任。

**Article 28** The canteens of centralized dining units such as schools, preschool institutions, elderly care institutions, and construction sites shall implement systems for raw material control, cleaning and disinfection of tableware and drinking utensils, food sample retention, etc., and periodically conduct self-inspections of food safety in the canteens in accordance with Article 47 of the Food Safety Law.  
Where a party contracts to operate a canteen of a centralized dining unit, it shall obtain a food business license according to law and be responsible for the food safety of the canteen. The centralized dining unit shall urge the contractor to implement the food safety management system and bear management responsibility.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　食品生产经营者应当对变质、超过保质期或者回收的食品进行显著标示或者单独存放在有明确标志的场所，及时采取无害化处理、销毁等措施并如实记录。  
食品安全法所称回收食品，是指已经售出，因违反法律、法规、食品安全标准或者超过保质期等原因，被召回或者退回的食品，不包括依照[食品安全法第六十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-63)第三款的规定可以继续销售的食品。

**Article 29** A food producer or trader shall conspicuously mark or separately store in a place with clear signs the deteriorated, expired, or recalled food, and promptly take measures such as harmless treatment, destruction, and truthfully record them.  
The term 'recalled food' as used in the Food Safety Law means food that has been sold and is recalled or returned due to violation of laws, regulations, food safety standards, or expiration of shelf life, but does not include food that may continue to be sold in accordance with Article 63, paragraph 3 of the Food Safety Law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上地方人民政府根据需要建设必要的食品无害化处理和销毁设施。食品生产经营者可以按照规定使用政府建设的设施对食品进行无害化处理或者予以销毁。

**Article 30** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, as necessary, construct necessary facilities for harmless treatment and destruction of food. A food producer or trader may, in accordance with the provisions, use the facilities constructed by the government to carry out harmless treatment or destruction of food.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　食品集中交易市场的开办者、食品展销会的举办者应当在市场开业或者展销会举办前向所在地县级人民政府食品安全监督管理部门报告。

**Article 31** The sponsors of food centralized trading markets and the organizers of food exhibitions shall report to the food safety supervision and administration department of the local county people's government before the market opens or the exhibition is held.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　网络食品交易第三方平台提供者应当妥善保存入网食品经营者的登记信息和交易信息。县级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门开展食品安全监督检查、食品安全案件调查处理、食品安全事故处置确需了解有关信息的，经其负责人批准，可以要求网络食品交易第三方平台提供者提供，网络食品交易第三方平台提供者应当按照要求提供。县级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门及其工作人员对网络食品交易第三方平台提供者提供的信息依法负有保密义务。

**Article 32** A third-party platform provider for online food trading shall properly preserve the registration information and transaction information of food traders on the platform. Where the food safety supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level needs to obtain relevant information for the conduct of food safety supervision and inspection, investigation and handling of food safety cases, or handling of food safety incidents, upon approval by its responsible person, it may request the third-party platform provider for online food trading to provide such information, and the third-party platform provider shall provide it as requested. The food safety supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level and its staff shall have a confidentiality obligation regarding the information provided by the third-party platform provider for online food trading according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　生产经营转基因食品应当显著标示，标示办法由国务院食品安全监督管理部门会同国务院农业行政部门制定。

**Article 33** The production and trading of genetically modified food shall be conspicuously labeled. The labeling measures shall be formulated by the food safety supervision and administration department of the State Council jointly with the agriculture administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　禁止利用包括会议、讲座、健康咨询在内的任何方式对食品进行虚假宣传。食品安全监督管理部门发现虚假宣传行为的，应当依法及时处理。

**Article 34** No person shall use any means, including meetings, lectures, and health consultations, to make false propaganda about food. Where a food safety supervision and administration department discovers any act of false propaganda, it shall handle it promptly according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　保健食品生产工艺有原料提取、纯化等前处理工序的，生产企业应当具备相应的原料前处理能力。

**Article 35** Where the production process of health food includes pretreatment processes such as raw material extraction and purification, the production enterprise shall have the corresponding raw material pretreatment capability.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　特殊医学用途配方食品生产企业应当按照食品安全国家标准规定的检验项目对出厂产品实施逐批检验。  
特殊医学用途配方食品中的特定全营养配方食品应当通过医疗机构或者药品零售企业向消费者销售。医疗机构、药品零售企业销售特定全营养配方食品的，不需要取得食品经营许可，但是应当遵守食品安全法和本条例关于食品销售的规定。

**Article 36** A manufacturer of formula foods for special medical purposes shall implement batch-by-batch inspection of products leaving the factory in accordance with the inspection items specified in the national food safety standards.  
Specific full nutrition formula foods among formula foods for special medical purposes shall be sold to consumers through medical institutions or drug retail enterprises. Where a medical institution or drug retail enterprise sells specific full nutrition formula foods, it does not need to obtain a food business license, but shall comply with the provisions of the Food Safety Law and these Regulations regarding food sales.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　特殊医学用途配方食品中的特定全营养配方食品广告按照处方药广告管理，其他类别的特殊医学用途配方食品广告按照非处方药广告管理。

**Article 37** Advertisements for specific full nutrition formula foods among formula foods for special medical purposes shall be managed in accordance with the management of prescription drug advertisements; advertisements for other categories of formula foods for special medical purposes shall be managed in accordance with the management of non-prescription drug advertisements.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　对保健食品之外的其他食品，不得声称具有保健功能。  
对添加食品安全国家标准规定的选择性添加物质的婴幼儿配方食品，不得以选择性添加物质命名。

**Article 38** No food other than health food shall claim to have health functions.  
Infant formula food to which optional substances specified in the national food safety standards are added shall not be named after the optional substances.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　特殊食品的标签、说明书内容应当与注册或者备案的标签、说明书一致。销售特殊食品，应当核对食品标签、说明书内容是否与注册或者备案的标签、说明书一致，不一致的不得销售。省级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门应当在其网站上公布注册或者备案的特殊食品的标签、说明书。  
特殊食品不得与普通食品或者药品混放销售。

**Article 39** The content of labels and instructions of special food shall be consistent with the registered or recorded labels and instructions. When selling special food, it shall be checked whether the content of the food labels and instructions is consistent with the registered or recorded labels and instructions; inconsistent ones shall not be sold. The food safety supervision and administration departments of the people's governments at or above the provincial level shall publish the labels and instructions of registered or recorded special food on their websites.  
Special food shall not be mixed with ordinary food or drugs for sale.

### 第五章　食品检验

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　对食品进行抽样检验，应当按照食品安全标准、注册或者备案的特殊食品的产品技术要求以及国家有关规定确定的检验项目和检验方法进行。

**Article 40** Sampling inspection of food shall be conducted in accordance with the food safety standards, the product technical requirements of registered or recorded special food, and the inspection items and methods determined by the relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　对可能掺杂掺假的食品，按照现有食品安全标准规定的检验项目和检验方法以及依照[食品安全法第一百一十一条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-111)和[本条例第六十三条](#art-63)规定制定的检验项目和检验方法无法检验的，国务院食品安全监督管理部门可以制定补充检验项目和检验方法，用于对食品的抽样检验、食品安全案件调查处理和食品安全事故处置。

**Article 41** For foods that may be adulterated, if the inspection items and methods prescribed by the existing food safety standards and those formulated in accordance with Article 111 of the Food Safety Law and Article 63 of these Regulations cannot conduct inspection, the food safety supervision and administration department of the State Council may formulate supplementary inspection items and methods for use in sampling inspection of foods, investigation and handling of food safety cases, and disposal of food safety accidents.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　依照[食品安全法第八十八条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-88)的规定申请复检的，申请人应当向复检机构先行支付复检费用。复检结论表明食品不合格的，复检费用由复检申请人承担；复检结论表明食品合格的，复检费用由实施抽样检验的食品安全监督管理部门承担。  
复检机构无正当理由不得拒绝承担复检任务。

**Article 42** An applicant applying for a re-inspection in accordance with Article 88 of the Food Safety Law shall pay the re-inspection fees to the re-inspection institution in advance. If the re-inspection conclusion indicates that the food is unqualified, the re-inspection fees shall be borne by the re-inspection applicant; if the re-inspection conclusion indicates that the food is qualified, the re-inspection fees shall be borne by the food safety supervision and administration department that conducted the sampling inspection.  
The re-inspection institution shall not refuse to undertake the re-inspection task without justifiable reasons.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　任何单位和个人不得发布未依法取得资质认定的食品检验机构出具的食品检验信息，不得利用上述检验信息对食品、食品生产经营者进行等级评定，欺骗、误导消费者。

**Article 43** No unit or individual shall publish food inspection information issued by a food inspection institution that has not obtained qualification accreditation according to law, or use the said inspection information to grade foods or food producers and traders, thereby deceiving or misleading consumers.

### 第六章　食品进出口

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　进口商进口食品、食品添加剂，应当按照规定向出入境检验检疫机构报检，如实申报产品相关信息，并随附法律、行政法规规定的合格证明材料。

**Article 44** An importer importing foods or food additives shall, in accordance with the provisions, declare to the entry-exit inspection and quarantine authority, truthfully declare the relevant product information, and attach the certification documents of conformity as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　进口食品运达口岸后，应当存放在出入境检验检疫机构指定或者认可的场所；需要移动的，应当按照出入境检验检疫机构的要求采取必要的安全防护措施。大宗散装进口食品应当在卸货口岸进行检验。

**Article 45** After imported foods arrive at the port, they shall be stored in a place designated or recognized by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine authority; if movement is required, necessary safety protection measures shall be taken in accordance with the requirements of the entry-exit inspection and quarantine authority. Bulk imported foods in large quantities shall be inspected at the port of discharge.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国家出入境检验检疫部门根据风险管理需要，可以对部分食品实行指定口岸进口。

**Article 46** The national entry-exit inspection and quarantine department may, based on the need for risk management, implement import of certain foods through designated ports.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国务院卫生行政部门依照[食品安全法第九十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-93)的规定对境外出口商、境外生产企业或者其委托的进口商提交的相关国家(地区)标准或者国际标准进行审查，认为符合食品安全要求的，决定暂予适用并予以公布；暂予适用的标准公布前，不得进口尚无食品安全国家标准的食品。  
食品安全国家标准中通用标准已经涵盖的食品不属于[食品安全法第九十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-93)规定的尚无食品安全国家标准的食品。

**Article 47** The health administrative department of the State Council shall, in accordance with Article 93 of the Food Safety Law, review the relevant national (regional) standards or international standards submitted by overseas exporters, overseas production enterprises, or their entrusted importers; if it deems that the standards meet the food safety requirements, it shall decide to provisionally apply them and publish them. Before the provisionally applied standards are published, no food with no national food safety standard shall be imported.  
Foods covered by the general standards in the national food safety standards shall not be regarded as foods with no national food safety standard as prescribed in Article 93 of the Food Safety Law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　进口商应当建立境外出口商、境外生产企业审核制度，重点审核境外出口商、境外生产企业制定和执行食品安全风险控制措施的情况以及向我国出口的食品是否符合食品安全法、本条例和其他有关法律、行政法规的规定以及食品安全国家标准的要求。

**Article 48** An importer shall establish an audit system for overseas exporters and overseas production enterprises, focusing on auditing the formulation and implementation of food safety risk control measures by overseas exporters and overseas production enterprises, and whether the foods exported to China comply with the provisions of the Food Safety Law, these Regulations, and other relevant laws and administrative regulations, as well as the requirements of the national food safety standards.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　进口商依照[食品安全法第九十四条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-94)第三款的规定召回进口食品的，应当将食品召回和处理情况向所在地县级人民政府食品安全监督管理部门和所在地出入境检验检疫机构报告。

**Article 49** If an importer recalls imported foods in accordance with paragraph 3 of Article 94 of the Food Safety Law, it shall report the food recall and handling situation to the food safety supervision and administration department of the local people's government at the county level and the local entry-exit inspection and quarantine authority.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家出入境检验检疫部门发现已经注册的境外食品生产企业不再符合注册要求的，应当责令其在规定期限内整改，整改期间暂停进口其生产的食品；经整改仍不符合注册要求的，国家出入境检验检疫部门应当撤销境外食品生产企业注册并公告。

**Article 50** If the national entry-exit inspection and quarantine department finds that a registered overseas food production enterprise no longer meets the registration requirements, it shall order the enterprise to rectify within a specified period, and during the rectification period, the import of the food produced by the enterprise shall be suspended; if after rectification it still fails to meet the registration requirements, the national entry-exit inspection and quarantine department shall revoke the registration of the overseas food production enterprise and make an announcement.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　对通过我国良好生产规范、危害分析与关键控制点体系认证的境外生产企业，认证机构应当依法实施跟踪调查。对不再符合认证要求的企业，认证机构应当依法撤销认证并向社会公布。

**Article 51** For overseas production enterprises that have passed the certification of China's Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) and Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Point (HACCP) system, the certification body shall conduct follow-up investigations according to law. For enterprises that no longer meet the certification requirements, the certification body shall revoke the certification according to law and make a public announcement.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　境外发生的食品安全事件可能对我国境内造成影响，或者在进口食品、食品添加剂、食品相关产品中发现严重食品安全问题的，国家出入境检验检疫部门应当及时进行风险预警，并可以对相关的食品、食品添加剂、食品相关产品采取下列控制措施：  
(一)退货或者销毁处理；  
(二)有条件地限制进口；  
(三)暂停或者禁止进口。

**Article 52** If a food safety incident occurring abroad may affect the territory of China, or a serious food safety problem is found in imported foods, food additives, or food-related products, the national entry-exit inspection and quarantine department shall promptly issue a risk warning and may take the following control measures on the relevant foods, food additives, and food-related products:  
(1) return or destroy the products;  
(2) conditionally restrict import;  
(3) suspend or prohibit import.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　出口食品、食品添加剂的生产企业应当保证其出口食品、食品添加剂符合进口国家(地区)的标准或者合同要求；我国缔结或者参加的国际条约、协定有要求的，还应当符合国际条约、协定的要求。

**Article 53** A production enterprise exporting foods or food additives shall ensure that its exported foods or food additives comply with the standards of the importing country (region) or the contract requirements; if there are requirements in the international treaties or agreements that China has concluded or acceded to, it shall also comply with the requirements of such international treaties or agreements.

### 第七章　食品安全事故处置

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　食品安全事故按照国家食品安全事故应急预案实行分级管理。县级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门会同同级有关部门负责食品安全事故调查处理。  
县级以上人民政府应当根据实际情况及时修改、完善食品安全事故应急预案。

**Article 54** Food safety accidents shall be subject to graded management in accordance with the national emergency plan for food safety accidents. The food safety supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments at the same level, be responsible for the investigation and handling of food safety accidents.  
The people's government at or above the county level shall revise and improve the emergency plan for food safety accidents in a timely manner according to the actual situation.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　县级以上人民政府应当完善食品安全事故应急管理机制，改善应急装备，做好应急物资储备和应急队伍建设，加强应急培训、演练。

**Article 55** The people's government at or above the county level shall improve the emergency management mechanism for food safety accidents, upgrade emergency equipment, ensure the stockpile of emergency materials and the construction of emergency teams, and strengthen emergency training and drills.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　发生食品安全事故的单位应当对导致或者可能导致食品安全事故的食品及原料、工具、设备、设施等，立即采取封存等控制措施。

**Article 56** The unit where a food safety accident occurs shall immediately take control measures such as sealing up the food and raw materials, tools, equipment, and facilities that cause or may cause the food safety accident.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　县级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门接到食品安全事故报告后，应当立即会同同级卫生行政、农业行政等部门依照[食品安全法第一百零五条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-105)的规定进行调查处理。食品安全监督管理部门应当对事故单位封存的食品及原料、工具、设备、设施等予以保护，需要封存而事故单位尚未封存的应当直接封存或者责令事故单位立即封存，并通知疾病预防控制机构对与事故有关的因素开展流行病学调查。  
疾病预防控制机构应当在调查结束后向同级食品安全监督管理、卫生行政部门同时提交流行病学调查报告。  
任何单位和个人不得拒绝、阻挠疾病预防控制机构开展流行病学调查。有关部门应当对疾病预防控制机构开展流行病学调查予以协助。

**Article 57** After receiving a report on a food safety accident, the food safety supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the health administrative department, agricultural administrative department, and other relevant departments at the same level, investigate and handle the accident in accordance with Article 105 of the Food Safety Law. The food safety supervision and administration department shall protect the food and raw materials, tools, equipment, facilities, etc. that have been sealed up by the accident unit; if sealing up is necessary but the accident unit has not yet sealed them, it shall directly seal them up or order the accident unit to seal them up immediately, and notify the disease prevention and control institution to carry out an epidemiological investigation on the factors related to the accident.  
The disease prevention and control institution shall submit an epidemiological investigation report to the food safety supervision and administration department and the health administrative department at the same level simultaneously after the investigation is completed.  
No unit or individual shall refuse or obstruct the disease prevention and control institution in carrying out an epidemiological investigation. The relevant departments shall provide assistance to the disease prevention and control institution in carrying out the epidemiological investigation.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国务院食品安全监督管理部门会同国务院卫生行政、农业行政等部门定期对全国食品安全事故情况进行分析，完善食品安全监督管理措施，预防和减少事故的发生。

**Article 58** The food safety supervision and administration department of the State Council shall, together with the health administrative department, agricultural administrative department, and other relevant departments of the State Council, periodically analyze the situation of food safety accidents nationwide, improve food safety supervision and management measures, and prevent and reduce the occurrence of accidents.

### 第八章　监督管理

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　设区的市级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门根据监督管理工作需要，可以对由下级人民政府食品安全监督管理部门负责日常监督管理的食品生产经营者实施随机监督检查，也可以组织下级人民政府食品安全监督管理部门对食品生产经营者实施异地监督检查。  
设区的市级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门认为必要的，可以直接调查处理下级人民政府食品安全监督管理部门管辖的食品安全违法案件，也可以指定其他下级人民政府食品安全监督管理部门调查处理。

**Article 59** The food safety supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the level of a city divided into districts may, based on the needs of supervision and management, carry out random supervision and inspection on food producers and traders that are under the daily supervision and management of the food safety supervision and administration department of the lower-level people's government, and may also organize the food safety supervision and administration department of the lower-level people's government to carry out off-site supervision and inspection on food producers and traders.  
If the food safety supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the level of a city divided into districts deems it necessary, it may directly investigate and handle food safety illegal cases under the jurisdiction of the food safety supervision and administration department of the lower-level people's government, or may designate another lower-level food safety supervision and administration department to investigate and handle them.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国家建立食品安全检查员制度，依托现有资源加强职业化检查员队伍建设，强化考核培训，提高检查员专业化水平。

**Article 60** The State shall establish a food safety inspector system, strengthen the construction of a professional inspector team based on existing resources, intensify assessment and training, and improve the specialization level of inspectors.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　县级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门依照[食品安全法第一百一十条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-110)的规定实施查封、扣押措施，查封、扣押的期限不得超过30日；情况复杂的，经实施查封、扣押措施的食品安全监督管理部门负责人批准，可以延长，延长期限不得超过45日。

**Article 61** The food safety supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 110 of the Food Safety Law, implement measures of sealing and impoundment. The period of sealing and impoundment shall not exceed 30 days; under complex circumstances, upon approval of the person in charge of the food safety supervision and administration department that implemented the sealing and impoundment measures, it may be extended, and the extended period shall not exceed 45 days.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　网络食品交易第三方平台多次出现入网食品经营者违法经营或者入网食品经营者的违法经营行为造成严重后果的，县级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门可以对网络食品交易第三方平台提供者的法定代表人或者主要负责人进行责任约谈。

**Article 62** Where, on a third-party platform for online food transactions, food business operators on the platform repeatedly engage in illegal operations, or the illegal operations of the food business operators on the platform cause serious consequences, the food safety supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level may conduct a responsible talk with the legal representative or the principal responsible person of the third-party platform provider for online food transactions.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国务院食品安全监督管理部门会同国务院卫生行政等部门根据食源性疾病信息、食品安全风险监测信息和监督管理信息等，对发现的添加或者可能添加到食品中的非食品用化学物质和其他可能危害人体健康的物质，制定名录及检测方法并予以公布。

**Article 63** The food safety supervision and administration department of the State Council shall, together with the health administration department of the State Council and other relevant departments, based on information on foodborne diseases, food safety risk monitoring information, and supervision and management information, develop a list and testing methods for non-food chemical substances that are found to be added or may be added to food and other substances that may endanger human health, and publish them.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门应当对餐具饮具集中消毒服务单位进行监督检查，发现不符合法律、法规、国家相关标准以及相关卫生规范等要求的，应当及时调查处理。监督检查的结果应当向社会公布。

**Article 64** The health administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall supervise and inspect the centralized disinfection service units for tableware and drinking utensils. Where it finds that requirements of laws, regulations, relevant national standards, and relevant health specifications are not met, it shall promptly investigate and handle the matter. The results of supervision and inspection shall be published to the public.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国家实行食品安全违法行为举报奖励制度，对查证属实的举报，给予举报人奖励。举报人举报所在企业食品安全重大违法犯罪行为的，应当加大奖励力度。有关部门应当对举报人的信息予以保密，保护举报人的合法权益。食品安全违法行为举报奖励办法由国务院食品安全监督管理部门会同国务院财政等有关部门制定。  
食品安全违法行为举报奖励资金纳入各级人民政府预算。

**Article 65** The State implements a reward system for reporting illegal acts in food safety. Rewards shall be given to whistleblowers for reports that have been verified. Where a whistleblower reports a major illegal or criminal act in food safety by the enterprise where he or she works, the reward shall be increased. The relevant departments shall keep the information of the whistleblower confidential and protect the lawful rights and interests of the whistleblower. The measures for rewarding reports of illegal acts in food safety shall be formulated by the food safety supervision and administration department of the State Council together with the finance department of the State Council and other relevant departments.  
Funds for rewarding reports of illegal acts in food safety shall be included in the budgets of the people's governments at all levels.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国务院食品安全监督管理部门应当会同国务院有关部门建立守信联合激励和失信联合惩戒机制，结合食品生产经营者信用档案，建立严重违法生产经营者黑名单制度，将食品安全信用状况与准入、融资、信贷、征信等相衔接，及时向社会公布。

**Article 66** The food safety supervision and administration department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, establish a mechanism for joint incentives for trustworthiness and joint punishment for dishonesty, and, in conjunction with the credit files of food producers and traders, establish a blacklist system for seriously illegal producers and traders, linking the status of food safety credit with access, financing, credit, credit investigation, etc., and timely publish it to the public.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　有下列情形之一的，属于[食品安全法第一百二十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-123)至第一百二十六条、第一百三十二条以及[本条例第七十二条](#art-72)、第七十三条规定的情节严重情形：  
(一)违法行为涉及的产品货值金额2万元以上或者违法行为持续时间3个月以上；  
(二)造成食源性疾病并出现死亡病例，或者造成30人以上食源性疾病但未出现死亡病例；  
(三)故意提供虚假信息或者隐瞒真实情况；  
(四)拒绝、逃避监督检查；  
(五)因违反食品安全法律、法规受到行政处罚后1年内又实施同一性质的食品安全违法行为，或者因违反食品安全法律、法规受到刑事处罚后又实施食品安全违法行为；  
(六)其他情节严重的情形。  
对情节严重的违法行为处以罚款时，应当依法从重从严。

**Article 67** Any of the following circumstances shall constitute the circumstances of serious nature as provided for in Articles 123 to 126, Article 132 of the Food Safety Law and Articles 72 and 73 of these Regulations:  
(1) the value of the products involved in the illegal act is 20,000 yuan or more, or the duration of the illegal act is three months or more;  
(2) causing foodborne diseases with deaths, or causing foodborne diseases in 30 or more persons without deaths;  
(3) intentionally providing false information or concealing true facts;  
(4) refusing or evading supervision and inspection;  
(5) committing a food safety illegal act of the same nature within one year after receiving an administrative penalty for violating food safety laws and regulations, or committing a food safety illegal act after receiving a criminal penalty for violating food safety laws and regulations;  
(6) other circumstances of serious nature.  
Where a fine is imposed for an illegal act of serious nature, the punishment shall be heavier and stricter according to law.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　有下列情形之一的，依照[食品安全法第一百二十五条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-125)第一款、[本条例第七十五条](#art-75)的规定给予处罚：  
(一)在食品生产、加工场所贮存依照[本条例第六十三条](#art-63)规定制定的名录中的物质；  
(二)生产经营的保健食品之外的食品的标签、说明书声称具有保健功能；  
(三)以食品安全国家标准规定的选择性添加物质命名婴幼儿配方食品；  
(四)生产经营的特殊食品的标签、说明书内容与注册或者备案的标签、说明书不一致。

**Article 68** Any of the following circumstances shall be punished in accordance with Article 125, Paragraph 1 of the Food Safety Law and Article 75 of these Regulations:  
(1) storing substances listed in the list formulated in accordance with Article 63 of these Regulations in food production and processing premises;  
(2) labeling or instructions of food other than health food produced or traded claiming to have health functions;  
(3) naming infant formula food after selective added substances specified in the national food safety standards;  
(4) the content of the label or instructions of special food produced or traded is inconsistent with the registered or filed label or instructions.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　有下列情形之一的，依照[食品安全法第一百二十六条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-126)第一款、[本条例第七十五条](#art-75)的规定给予处罚：  
(一)接受食品生产经营者委托贮存、运输食品，未按照规定记录保存信息；  
(二)餐饮服务提供者未查验、留存餐具饮具集中消毒服务单位的营业执照复印件和消毒合格证明；  
(三)食品生产经营者未按照规定对变质、超过保质期或者回收的食品进行标示或者存放，或者未及时对上述食品采取无害化处理、销毁等措施并如实记录；  
(四)医疗机构和药品零售企业之外的单位或者个人向消费者销售特殊医学用途配方食品中的特定全营养配方食品；  
(五)将特殊食品与普通食品或者药品混放销售。

**Article 69** Any of the following circumstances shall be punished in accordance with Article 126, Paragraph 1 of the Food Safety Law and Article 75 of these Regulations:  
(1) accepting the entrustment of a food producer or trader to store or transport food and failing to record and retain information in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) a food service provider fails to examine and retain copies of the business license and disinfection qualification certificates of centralized disinfection service units for tableware and drinking utensils;  
(3) a food producer or trader fails to mark or store deteriorated, expired, or recalled food in accordance with the provisions, or fails to promptly take measures such as harmless treatment, destruction, etc., for such food and truthfully record them;  
(4) units or individuals other than medical institutions and pharmaceutical retail enterprises sell specific full nutrition formula food among formula food for special medical purposes to consumers;  
(5) mixing and selling special food with ordinary food or drugs.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　除[食品安全法第一百二十五条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-125)第一款、第一百二十六条规定的情形外，食品生产经营者的生产经营行为不符合[食品安全法第三十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-33)第一款第五项、第七项至第十项的规定，或者不符合有关食品生产经营过程要求的食品安全国家标准的，依照[食品安全法第一百二十六条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-126)第一款、[本条例第七十五条](#art-75)的规定给予处罚。

**Article 70** Except for the circumstances provided for in Article 125, Paragraph 1 and Article 126 of the Food Safety Law, where the production or trading activities of a food producer or trader do not comply with the provisions of Article 33, Paragraph 1, Items 5, 7 to 10 of the Food Safety Law, or do not comply with the national food safety standards concerning the requirements for the food production and trading process, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with Article 126, Paragraph 1 of the Food Safety Law and Article 75 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　餐具饮具集中消毒服务单位未按照规定建立并遵守出厂检验记录制度的，由县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门依照[食品安全法第一百二十六条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-126)第一款、[本条例第七十五条](#art-75)的规定给予处罚。

**Article 71** Where a centralized disinfection service unit for tableware and drinking utensils fails to establish and comply with the system of ex-factory inspection records in accordance with the provisions, the health administration department of the people's government at or above the county level shall impose punishment in accordance with Article 126, Paragraph 1 of the Food Safety Law and Article 75 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　从事对温度、湿度等有特殊要求的食品贮存业务的非食品生产经营者，食品集中交易市场的开办者、食品展销会的举办者，未按照规定备案或者报告的，由县级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停产停业，并处5万元以上20万元以下罚款。

**Article 72** Where a non-food producer or trader engaged in the storage business of food with special requirements for temperature, humidity, etc., or the organizer of a centralized food trading market or the organizer of a food exhibition or sales event, fails to file a record or report in accordance with the provisions, the food safety supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification and give a warning; if it refuses to rectify, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, production or business shall be ordered to be suspended, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　利用会议、讲座、健康咨询等方式对食品进行虚假宣传的，由县级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门责令消除影响，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，依照[食品安全法第一百四十条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-140)第五款的规定进行处罚；属于单位违法的，还应当依照[本条例第七十五条](#art-75)的规定对单位的法定代表人、主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予处罚。

**Article 73** Where false propaganda of food is made by means of meetings, lectures, health consultations, etc., the food safety supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the elimination of the effects, and if there are illegal gains, confiscate the illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with Article 140, Paragraph 5 of the Food Safety Law; if the violation is committed by a unit, punishment shall also be imposed on the legal representative, the principal responsible person, the directly responsible supervisor, and other directly responsible persons of the unit in accordance with Article 75 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　食品生产经营者生产经营的食品符合食品安全标准但不符合食品所标注的企业标准规定的食品安全指标的，由县级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门给予警告，并责令食品经营者停止经营该食品，责令食品生产企业改正；拒不停止经营或者改正的，没收不符合企业标准规定的食品安全指标的食品，货值金额不足1万元的，并处1万元以上5万元以下罚款，货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额5倍以上10倍以下罚款。

**Article 74** Where the food produced or traded by a food producer or trader complies with the food safety standards but does not comply with the food safety indicators specified in the enterprise standard marked on the food, the food safety supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level shall give a warning and order the food business operator to cease the operation of such food and order the food producer to make corrections; if it refuses to cease operation or make corrections, the food that does not comply with the food safety indicators specified in the enterprise standard shall be confiscated, and if the value of the goods is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if the value of the goods is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the value of the goods shall be concurrently imposed.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　食品生产经营企业等单位有食品安全法规定的违法情形，除依照食品安全法的规定给予处罚外，有下列情形之一的，对单位的法定代表人、主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处以其上一年度从本单位取得收入的1倍以上10倍以下罚款：  
(一)故意实施违法行为；  
(二)违法行为性质恶劣；  
(三)违法行为造成严重后果。  
属于[食品安全法第一百二十五条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-125)第二款规定情形的，不适用前款规定。

**Article 75** Where a food production or trading enterprise or other unit has an illegal act provided for in the Food Safety Law, in addition to punishment imposed in accordance with the Food Safety Law, if any of the following circumstances exists, the legal representative, the principal responsible person, the directly responsible supervisor, and other directly responsible persons of the unit shall be fined not less than one time but not more than ten times the income they obtained from the unit in the previous year:  
(1) intentionally committing the illegal act;  
(2) the illegal act is vicious in nature;  
(3) the illegal act causes serious consequences.  
In the case of the circumstances provided for in Article 125, Paragraph 2 of the Food Safety Law, the preceding paragraph shall not apply.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　食品生产经营者依照[食品安全法第六十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-63)第一款、第二款的规定停止生产、经营，实施食品召回，或者采取其他有效措施减轻或者消除食品安全风险，未造成危害后果的，可以从轻或者减轻处罚。

**Article 76** Where a food producer or trader, in accordance with Article 63, Paragraph 1 or 2 of the Food Safety Law, ceases production or operation, implements a food recall, or takes other effective measures to reduce or eliminate food safety risks, and no harmful consequences are caused, the punishment may be mitigated or reduced.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　县级以上地方人民政府食品安全监督管理等部门对有[食品安全法第一百二十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-123)规定的违法情形且情节严重，可能需要行政拘留的，应当及时将案件及有关材料移送同级公安机关。公安机关认为需要补充材料的，食品安全监督管理等部门应当及时提供。公安机关经审查认为不符合行政拘留条件的，应当及时将案件及有关材料退回移送的食品安全监督管理等部门。

**Article 77** Where the food safety supervision and administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level and other departments find an illegal act with serious circumstances as provided for in Article 123 of the Food Safety Law that may require administrative detention, they shall promptly transfer the case and relevant materials to the public security organ at the same level. If the public security organ considers that supplementary materials are needed, the food safety supervision and administration department and other departments shall promptly provide them. If the public security organ, after review, considers that the conditions for administrative detention are not met, it shall promptly return the case and relevant materials to the transferring food safety supervision and administration department or other departments.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　公安机关对发现的食品安全违法行为，经审查没有犯罪事实或者立案侦查后认为不需要追究刑事责任，但依法应当予以行政拘留的，应当及时作出行政拘留的处罚决定；不需要予以行政拘留但依法应当追究其他行政责任的，应当及时将案件及有关材料移送同级食品安全监督管理等部门。

**Article 78** Where a public security organ, after review, finds no criminal facts or after filing a case for investigation considers that no criminal liability shall be investigated, but, according to law, administrative detention shall be imposed on a discovered food safety illegal act, it shall promptly make a decision on administrative detention; if administrative detention is not required but other administrative liability shall be investigated according to law, it shall promptly transfer the case and relevant materials to the food safety supervision and administration department at the same level or other departments.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　复检机构无正当理由拒绝承担复检任务的，由县级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门给予警告，无正当理由1年内2次拒绝承担复检任务的，由国务院有关部门撤销其复检机构资质并向社会公布。

**Article 79** Where a re-inspection agency refuses to undertake the re-inspection task without justifiable reasons, the food safety supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level shall give a warning; if it refuses to undertake the re-inspection task twice within one year without justifiable reasons, the relevant department of the State Council shall revoke its re-inspection agency qualification and publish it to the public.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　发布未依法取得资质认定的食品检验机构出具的食品检验信息，或者利用上述检验信息对食品、食品生产经营者进行等级评定，欺骗、误导消费者的，由县级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门责令改正，有违法所得的，没收违法所得，并处10万元以上50万元以下罚款；拒不改正的，处50万元以上100万元以下罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 80** Where information on food inspection issued by a food inspection agency that has not obtained qualification accreditation according to law is published, or the above inspection information is used to grade food or food producers or traders, thereby deceiving or misleading consumers, the food safety supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order corrections, and if there are illegal gains, confiscate the illegal gains and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if corrections are refused, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if it constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　食品安全监督管理部门依照食品安全法、本条例对违法单位或者个人处以30万元以上罚款的，由设区的市级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门决定。罚款具体处罚权限由国务院食品安全监督管理部门规定。

**Article 81** Where a food safety supervision and administration department imposes a fine of more than 300,000 yuan on an illegal entity or individual in accordance with the Food Safety Law and these Regulations, the decision shall be made by the food safety supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the level of a city divided into districts. The specific penalty authority for fines shall be prescribed by the food safety supervision and administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　阻碍食品安全监督管理等部门工作人员依法执行职务，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 82** Any person who obstructs the staff members of the food safety supervision and administration department or other departments from performing their duties according to law and constitutes a violation of public security administration shall be given a public security administration penalty by the public security organ according to law.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　县级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理等部门发现单位或者个人违反[食品安全法第一百二十条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-120)第一款规定，编造、散布虚假食品安全信息，涉嫌构成违反治安管理行为的，应当将相关情况通报同级公安机关。

**Article 83** Where the food safety supervision and administration department or other departments of the people's government at or above the county level discover that an entity or individual, in violation of Article 120, paragraph 1 of the Food Safety Law, fabricates or disseminates false food safety information, and is suspected of constituting a violation of public security administration, they shall notify the public security organ at the same level of the relevant circumstances.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　县级以上人民政府食品安全监督管理部门及其工作人员违法向他人提供网络食品交易第三方平台提供者提供的信息的，依照[食品安全法第一百四十五条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-145)的规定给予处分。

**Article 84** Where the food safety supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level or its staff members illegally provide to others the information provided by a third-party platform provider for online food trading, they shall be given sanctions in accordance with Article 145 of the Food Safety Law.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 85** Any violation of these Regulations that constitutes a crime shall be subject to criminal liability according to law.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　本条例自2019年12月1日起施行。

**Article 86** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 2019.
