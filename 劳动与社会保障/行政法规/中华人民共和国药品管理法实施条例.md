# 中华人民共和国药品管理法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2024-12-06  
**生效日期**：2025-01-20

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国药品管理法》（以下简称《药品管理法》），制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Drug Administration Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Drug Administration Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国务院药品监督管理部门设置国家药品检验机构。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门可以在本行政区域内设置药品检验机构。地方药品检验机构的设置规划由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门提出，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准。  
国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的药品监督管理部门可以根据需要，确定符合药品检验条件的检验机构承担药品检验工作。

**Article 2** The drug regulatory department under the State Council shall establish a national drug testing institution.  
The drug regulatory department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may establish drug testing institutions within its administrative region. The plan for establishing local drug testing institutions shall be proposed by the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and submitted to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval.  
The drug regulatory departments under the State Council and under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, as needed, designate testing institutions that meet the conditions for drug testing to undertake drug testing work.

### 第二章　药品生产企业管理

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　开办药品生产企业，申办人应当向拟办企业所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门提出申请。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门应当自收到申请之日起30个工作日内，依据[《药品管理法》第八条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-8)规定的开办条件组织验收；验收合格的，发给《药品生产许可证》。

**Article 3** Where a drug manufacturing enterprise is to be established, the applicant shall submit an application to the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the proposed enterprise is located. The drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 30 working days from the date of receiving the application, organize an inspection in accordance with the establishment conditions specified in Article 8 of the Drug Administration Law; if the inspection is passed, it shall issue a Drug Manufacturing License.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　药品生产企业变更《药品生产许可证》许可事项的，应当在许可事项发生变更30日前，向原发证机关申请《药品生产许可证》变更登记；未经批准，不得变更许可事项。原发证机关应当自收到申请之日起15个工作日内作出决定。

**Article 4** Where a drug manufacturing enterprise changes the licensed matters in its Drug Manufacturing License, it shall apply to the original issuing authority for modification registration of the Drug Manufacturing License 30 days before the change of the licensed matters; without approval, no modification of the licensed matters shall be made. The original issuing authority shall make a decision within 15 working days from the date of receiving the application.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　省级以上人民政府药品监督管理部门应当按照《药品生产质量管理规范》和国务院药品监督管理部门规定的实施办法和实施步骤，组织对药品生产企业的认证工作；符合《药品生产质量管理规范》的，发给认证证书。其中，生产注射剂、放射性药品和国务院药品监督管理部门规定的生物制品的药品生产企业的认证工作，由国务院药品监督管理部门负责。  
《药品生产质量管理规范》认证证书的格式由国务院药品监督管理部门统一规定。

**Article 5** The drug regulatory department at or above the provincial level shall, in accordance with the Good Manufacturing Practice for Pharmaceutical Products and the implementation measures and implementation steps prescribed by the drug regulatory department under the State Council, organize the certification of drug manufacturing enterprises; those that meet the Good Manufacturing Practice for Pharmaceutical Products shall be issued a certification certificate. Among them, the certification work for drug manufacturing enterprises that produce injections, radioactive drugs, and biological products specified by the drug regulatory department under the State Council shall be the responsibility of the drug regulatory department under the State Council.  
The format of the certification certificate for the Good Manufacturing Practice for Pharmaceutical Products shall be uniformly prescribed by the drug regulatory department under the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　新开办药品生产企业、药品生产企业新建药品生产车间或者新增生产剂型的，应当自取得药品生产证明文件或者经批准正式生产之日起30日内，按照规定向药品监督管理部门申请《药品生产质量管理规范》认证。受理申请的药品监督管理部门应当自收到企业申请之日起6个月内，组织对申请企业是否符合《药品生产质量管理规范》进行认证；认证合格的，发给认证证书。

**Article 6** A newly established drug manufacturing enterprise, a drug manufacturing enterprise that builds a new drug production workshop or adds a new production dosage form shall, within 30 days from the date of obtaining the drug production approval document or from the date of formally commencing production upon approval, apply to the drug regulatory department for certification under the Good Manufacturing Practice for Pharmaceutical Products in accordance with the regulations. The drug regulatory department that accepts the application shall, within 6 months from the date of receiving the enterprise's application, organize a certification to verify whether the applicant enterprise meets the Good Manufacturing Practice for Pharmaceutical Products; if the certification is passed, it shall issue a certification certificate.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院药品监督管理部门应当设立《药品生产质量管理规范》认证检查员库。《药品生产质量管理规范》认证检查员必须符合国务院药品监督管理部门规定的条件。进行《药品生产质量管理规范》认证，必须按照国务院药品监督管理部门的规定，从《药品生产质量管理规范》认证检查员库中随机抽取认证检查员组成认证检查组进行认证检查。

**Article 7** The drug regulatory department under the State Council shall establish a pool of certification inspectors for the Good Manufacturing Practice for Pharmaceutical Products. Certification inspectors for the Good Manufacturing Practice for Pharmaceutical Products must meet the conditions prescribed by the drug regulatory department under the State Council. To conduct certification under the Good Manufacturing Practice for Pharmaceutical Products, certification inspectors must be randomly selected from the pool of certification inspectors for the Good Manufacturing Practice for Pharmaceutical Products in accordance with the regulations of the drug regulatory department under the State Council to form a certification inspection team for certification inspection.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　《药品生产许可证》有效期为5年。有效期届满，需要继续生产药品的，持证企业应当在许可证有效期届满前6个月，按照国务院药品监督管理部门的规定申请换发《药品生产许可证》。  
药品生产企业终止生产药品或者关闭的，《药品生产许可证》由原发证部门缴销。

**Article 8** The Drug Manufacturing License shall be valid for 5 years. If the license expires and the enterprise needs to continue manufacturing drugs, the licensed enterprise shall, within 6 months before the expiration of the license, apply for renewal of the Drug Manufacturing License in accordance with the regulations of the drug regulatory department under the State Council.  
If a drug manufacturing enterprise terminates drug manufacturing or is closed, the Drug Manufacturing License shall be cancelled by the original issuing authority.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　药品生产企业生产药品所使用的原料药，必须具有国务院药品监督管理部门核发的药品批准文号或者进口药品注册证书、医药产品注册证书；但是，未实施批准文号管理的中药材、中药饮片除外。

**Article 9** The active pharmaceutical ingredients used by a drug manufacturing enterprise in producing drugs must have a drug approval number issued by the drug regulatory department under the State Council or an import drug registration certificate or a pharmaceutical product registration certificate; however, this shall not apply to Chinese herbal medicines and prepared slices of Chinese crude drugs for which approval number management has not been implemented.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　依据[《药品管理法》第十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-13)规定，接受委托生产药品的，受托方必须是持有与其受托生产的药品相适应的《药品生产质量管理规范》认证证书的药品生产企业。  
疫苗、血液制品和国务院药品监督管理部门规定的其他药品，不得委托生产。

**Article 10** Where, in accordance with Article 13 of the Drug Administration Law, a drug manufacturing enterprise is entrusted to manufacture drugs, the entrusted party must be a drug manufacturing enterprise that holds a certification certificate under the Good Manufacturing Practice for Pharmaceutical Products corresponding to the drugs entrusted for manufacture.  
Vaccines, blood products, and other drugs specified by the drug regulatory department under the State Council shall not be manufactured on an entrusted basis.

### 第三章　药品经营企业管理

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　开办药品批发企业、药品零售企业，应当依据《药品管理法》的规定提出药品经营许可申请，并提交证明其符合《药品管理法》规定条件的资料。

**Article 11** To establish a drug wholesale enterprise or a drug retail enterprise, an application for a drug distribution license shall be submitted in accordance with the provisions of the Drug Administration Law, along with materials proving that the conditions specified in the Drug Administration Law are met.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对药品经营许可申请，应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内作出行政许可决定。对符合规定条件的，准予许可并发给《药品经营许可证》；对不符合规定条件的，不予许可并书面说明理由。

**Article 12** For an application for a drug distribution license, an administrative licensing decision shall be made within 20 working days from the date of accepting the application. If the conditions are met, the license shall be granted and a Drug Distribution License shall be issued; if the conditions are not met, the application shall be rejected with a written explanation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门和设区的市级药品监督管理机构负责组织药品经营企业的认证工作。药品经营企业应当按照国务院药品监督管理部门规定的实施办法和实施步骤，通过省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门或者设区的市级药品监督管理机构组织的《药品经营质量管理规范》的认证，取得认证证书。《药品经营质量管理规范》认证证书的格式由国务院药品监督管理部门统一规定。  
新开办药品批发企业和药品零售企业，应当自取得《药品经营许可证》之日起30日内，向发给其《药品经营许可证》的药品监督管理部门或者药品监督管理机构申请《药品经营质量管理规范》认证。受理申请的药品监督管理部门或者药品监督管理机构应当自收到申请之日起3个月内，按照国务院药品监督管理部门的规定，组织对申请认证的药品批发企业或者药品零售企业是否符合《药品经营质量管理规范》进行认证；认证合格的，发给认证证书。

**Article 13** The drug regulatory department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and the drug regulatory institution at the city level with districts shall be responsible for organizing the certification of drug distribution enterprises. A drug distribution enterprise shall, in accordance with the implementation measures and implementation steps prescribed by the drug regulatory department under the State Council, pass the certification under the Good Supply Practice for Pharmaceutical Products organized by the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or the drug regulatory institution at the city level with districts, and obtain a certification certificate. The format of the certification certificate for the Good Supply Practice for Pharmaceutical Products shall be uniformly prescribed by the drug regulatory department under the State Council.  
A newly established drug wholesale enterprise or drug retail enterprise shall, within 30 days from the date of obtaining the Drug Distribution License, apply for certification under the Good Supply Practice for Pharmaceutical Products to the drug regulatory department or drug regulatory institution that issued the Drug Distribution License. The drug regulatory department or drug regulatory institution that accepts the application shall, within 3 months from the date of receiving the application, in accordance with the regulations of the drug regulatory department under the State Council, organize a certification to verify whether the applicant drug wholesale enterprise or drug retail enterprise meets the Good Supply Practice for Pharmaceutical Products; if the certification is passed, it shall issue a certification certificate.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门应当设立《药品经营质量管理规范》认证检查员库。《药品经营质量管理规范》认证检查员必须符合国务院药品监督管理部门规定的条件。进行《药品经营质量管理规范》认证，必须按照国务院药品监督管理部门的规定，从《药品经营质量管理规范》认证检查员库中随机抽取认证检查员组成认证检查组进行认证检查。

**Article 14** The drug regulatory department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall establish a pool of certification inspectors for the Good Supply Practice for Pharmaceutical Products. Certification inspectors for the Good Supply Practice for Pharmaceutical Products must meet the conditions prescribed by the drug regulatory department under the State Council. To conduct certification under the Good Supply Practice for Pharmaceutical Products, certification inspectors must be randomly selected from the pool of certification inspectors for the Good Supply Practice for Pharmaceutical Products in accordance with the regulations of the drug regulatory department under the State Council to form a certification inspection team for certification inspection.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家实行处方药和非处方药分类管理制度。国家根据非处方药品的安全性，将非处方药分为甲类非处方药和乙类非处方药。  
经营处方药、甲类非处方药的药品零售企业，应当配备执业药师或者其他依法经资格认定的药学技术人员。经营乙类非处方药的药品零售企业，应当配备经设区的市级药品监督管理机构或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门直接设置的县级药品监督管理机构组织考核合格的业务人员。

**Article 15** The State implements a classification management system for prescription drugs and over-the-counter drugs. The State classifies over-the-counter drugs into Category A over-the-counter drugs and Category B over-the-counter drugs based on the safety of such drugs.  
Drug retail enterprises that distribute prescription drugs or Category A over-the-counter drugs shall be staffed with licensed pharmacists or other pharmaceutical technical personnel who have passed qualification accreditation according to law. Drug retail enterprises that distribute Category B over-the-counter drugs shall be staffed with business personnel who have passed the examination organized by the drug regulatory institution at the city level with districts or by the county-level drug regulatory institution directly established by the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　药品经营企业变更《药品经营许可证》许可事项的，应当在许可事项发生变更30日前，向原发证机关申请《药品经营许可证》变更登记；未经批准，不得变更许可事项。原发证机关应当自收到企业申请之日起15个工作日内作出决定。

**Article 16** Where a drug distribution enterprise changes the licensed matters in its Drug Distribution License, it shall apply to the original issuing authority for modification registration of the Drug Distribution License 30 days before the change of the licensed matters; without approval, no modification of the licensed matters shall be made. The original issuing authority shall make a decision within 15 working days from the date of receiving the enterprise's application.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　《药品经营许可证》有效期为5年。有效期届满，需要继续经营药品的，持证企业应当在许可证有效期届满前6个月，按照国务院药品监督管理部门的规定申请换发《药品经营许可证》。  
药品经营企业终止经营药品或者关闭的，《药品经营许可证》由原发证机关缴销。

**Article 17** The Drug Distribution License shall be valid for 5 years. If the license expires and the enterprise needs to continue distributing drugs, the licensed enterprise shall, within 6 months before the expiration of the license, apply for renewal of the Drug Distribution License in accordance with the regulations of the drug regulatory department under the State Council.  
If a drug distribution enterprise terminates drug distribution or is closed, the Drug Distribution License shall be cancelled by the original issuing authority.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　交通不便的边远地区城乡集市贸易市场没有药品零售企业的，当地药品零售企业经所在地县（市）药品监督管理机构批准并到工商行政管理部门办理登记注册后，可以在该城乡集市贸易市场内设点并在批准经营的药品范围内销售非处方药品。

**Article 18** Where there is no drug retail enterprise in an urban or rural market fair in a remote area with inconvenient transportation, a local drug retail enterprise may, upon approval by the county (city) drug regulatory institution at the location and after completing registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce, set up a sales point within such urban or rural market fair and sell non-prescription drugs within the scope of drugs approved for distribution.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　通过互联网进行药品交易的药品生产企业、药品经营企业、医疗机构及其交易的药品，必须符合《药品管理法》和本条例的规定。互联网药品交易服务的管理办法，由国务院药品监督管理部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 19** Drug manufacturing enterprises, drug distribution enterprises, medical institutions, and the drugs they trade through the Internet must comply with the provisions of the Drug Administration Law and these Regulations. The administrative measures for Internet drug trading services shall be formulated by the drug regulatory department under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council.

### 第四章　医疗机构的药剂管理

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　医疗机构设立制剂室，应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门提出申请，经审核同意后，报同级人民政府药品监督管理部门审批；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门验收合格的，予以批准，发给《医疗机构制剂许可证》。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门和药品监督管理部门应当在各自收到申请之日起30个工作日内，作出是否同意或者批准的决定。

**Article 20** To establish a preparation unit, a medical institution shall submit an application to the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government at its location; after examination and consent, it shall be reported to the drug regulatory department of the same level for approval. If the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government passes the inspection, it shall approve the establishment and issue a Medical Institution Preparation License.  
The health administrative department and the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 30 working days from the date of receiving the respective application, make a decision on whether to consent or approve.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　医疗机构变更《医疗机构制剂许可证》许可事项的，应当在许可事项发生变更30日前，依照[本条例第二十条](#art-20)的规定向原审核、批准机关申请《医疗机构制剂许可证》变更登记；未经批准，不得变更许可事项。原审核、批准机关应当在各自收到申请之日起15个工作日内作出决定。  
医疗机构新增配制剂型或者改变配制场所的，应当经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门验收合格后，依照前款规定办理《医疗机构制剂许可证》变更登记。

**Article 21** Where a medical institution changes the licensed matters in its Medical Institution Preparation License, it shall, 30 days before the change of the licensed matters, apply to the original examining and approving authorities for modification registration of the Medical Institution Preparation License in accordance with Article 20 of these Regulations; without approval, no modification of the licensed matters shall be made. The original examining and approving authorities shall each make a decision within 15 working days from the date of receiving the respective application.  
Where a medical institution adds a new preparation dosage form or changes the preparation site, it shall, after passing the inspection by the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government at its location, go through the modification registration of the Medical Institution Preparation License in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　《医疗机构制剂许可证》有效期为5年。有效期届满，需要继续配制制剂的，医疗机构应当在许可证有效期届满前6个月，按照国务院药品监督管理部门的规定申请换发《医疗机构制剂许可证》。  
医疗机构终止配制制剂或者关闭的，《医疗机构制剂许可证》由原发证机关缴销。

**Article 22** The Medical Institution Preparation License shall be valid for 5 years. If the license expires and the medical institution needs to continue preparing preparations, it shall, within 6 months before the expiration of the license, apply for renewal of the Medical Institution Preparation License in accordance with the regulations of the drug regulatory department under the State Council.  
If a medical institution terminates preparation of preparations or is closed, the Medical Institution Preparation License shall be cancelled by the original issuing authority.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　医疗机构配制制剂，必须按照国务院药品监督管理部门的规定报送有关资料和样品，经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准，并发给制剂批准文号后，方可配制。

**Article 23** To prepare preparations, a medical institution must submit relevant materials and samples in accordance with the regulations of the drug regulatory department under the State Council, and may prepare only after obtaining approval from the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government at its location and being issued a preparation approval number.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　医疗机构配制的制剂不得在市场上销售或者变相销售，不得发布医疗机构制剂广告。  
发生灾情、疫情、突发事件或者临床急需而市场没有供应时，经国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的药品监督管理部门批准，在规定期限内，医疗机构配制的制剂可以在指定的医疗机构之间调剂使用。  
国务院药品监督管理部门规定的特殊制剂的调剂使用以及省、自治区、直辖市之间医疗机构制剂的调剂使用，必须经国务院药品监督管理部门批准。

**Article 24** Preparations prepared by a medical institution shall not be marketed or marketed in a disguised form, and no advertisements for medical institution preparations shall be released.  
Where a disaster, epidemic, emergency, or urgent clinical need occurs and there is no supply on the market, preparations prepared by a medical institution may be transferred for use among designated medical institutions within a specified period upon approval by the drug regulatory department under the State Council or the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The transfer for use of special preparations specified by the drug regulatory department under the State Council and the transfer for use of preparations of medical institutions between different provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government must be approved by the drug regulatory department under the State Council.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　医疗机构审核和调配处方的药剂人员必须是依法经资格认定的药学技术人员。

**Article 25** The pharmaceutical personnel responsible for reviewing and dispensing prescriptions in a medical institution must be pharmaceutical technical personnel who have passed qualification accreditation according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　医疗机构购进药品，必须有真实、完整的药品购进记录。药品购进记录必须注明药品的通用名称、剂型、规格、批号、有效期、生产厂商、供货单位、购货数量、购进价格、购货日期以及国务院药品监督管理部门规定的其他内容。

**Article 26** When a medical institution purchases drugs, it must maintain true and complete records of drug purchases. The drug purchase records must specify the generic name, dosage form, specification, batch number, expiration date, manufacturer, supplier, quantity purchased, purchase price, purchase date, and other contents as prescribed by the drug regulatory department under the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　医疗机构向患者提供的药品应当与诊疗范围相适应，并凭执业医师或者执业助理医师的处方调配。  
计划生育技术服务机构采购和向患者提供药品，其范围应当与经批准的服务范围相一致，并凭执业医师或者执业助理医师的处方调配。  
个人设置的门诊部、诊所等医疗机构不得配备常用药品和急救药品以外的其他药品。常用药品和急救药品的范围和品种，由所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门会同同级人民政府药品监督管理部门规定。

**Article 27** Drugs provided by a medical institution to patients shall be compatible with the scope of diagnosis and treatment and shall be dispensed on the basis of prescriptions issued by licensed physicians or licensed assistant physicians.  
When a family planning technical service institution purchases and provides drugs to patients, the scope of such drugs shall be consistent with the approved scope of services, and they shall be dispensed on the basis of prescriptions issued by licensed physicians or licensed assistant physicians.  
Medical institutions such as outpatient departments and clinics established by individuals shall not stock drugs other than commonly used drugs and emergency drugs. The scope and varieties of commonly used drugs and emergency drugs shall be prescribed by the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government at the location jointly with the drug regulatory department of the same level.

### 第五章　药品管理

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　药物非临床安全性评价研究机构必须执行《药物非临床研究质量管理规范》，药物临床试验机构必须执行《药物临床试验质量管理规范》。《药物非临床研究质量管理规范》、《药物临床试验质量管理规范》由国务院药品监督管理部门分别商国务院科学技术行政部门和国务院卫生行政部门制定。

**Article 28** Institutions for non-clinical safety evaluation of drugs must comply with the Good Laboratory Practice for Non-clinical Studies of Drugs, and institutions for drug clinical trials must comply with the Good Clinical Practice for Drugs. The Good Laboratory Practice for Non-clinical Studies of Drugs and the Good Clinical Practice for Drugs shall be formulated by the drug regulatory department under the State Council in consultation with the science and technology administrative department under the State Council and the health administrative department under the State Council, respectively.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　药物临床试验、生产药品和进口药品，应当符合《药品管理法》及本条例的规定，经国务院药品监督管理部门审查批准；国务院药品监督管理部门可以委托省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门对申报药物的研制情况及条件进行审查，对申报资料进行形式审查，并对试制的样品进行检验。具体办法由国务院药品监督管理部门制定。

**Article 29** Drug clinical trials, manufacturing of drugs, and import of drugs shall comply with the provisions of the Drug Administration Law and these Regulations and shall be examined and approved by the drug regulatory department under the State Council. The drug regulatory department under the State Council may entrust the drug regulatory department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to examine the research status and conditions of the drug applied for, conduct a formal examination of the application materials, and test the trial samples. Specific measures shall be formulated by the drug regulatory department under the State Council.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　研制新药，需要进行临床试验的，应当依照[《药品管理法》第二十九条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-29)的规定，经国务院药品监督管理部门批准。  
药物临床试验申请经国务院药品监督管理部门批准后，申报人应当在经依法认定的具有药物临床试验资格的机构中选择承担药物临床试验的机构，并将该临床试验机构报国务院药品监督管理部门和国务院卫生行政部门备案。  
药物临床试验机构进行药物临床试验，应当事先告知受试者或者其监护人真实情况，并取得其书面同意。

**Article 30** If a new drug is developed and clinical trials are required, approval shall be obtained from the drug regulatory department under the State Council in accordance with Article 29 of the Drug Administration Law.  
After the application for a drug clinical trial is approved by the drug regulatory department under the State Council, the applicant shall select an institution that will undertake the drug clinical trial from among the institutions that have been recognized according to law as having the qualification for drug clinical trials, and shall report the clinical trial institution to the drug regulatory department under the State Council and the health administrative department under the State Council for record.  
Before conducting a drug clinical trial, the drug clinical trial institution shall inform the subject or his/her guardian of the true situation in advance and obtain their written consent.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　生产已有国家标准的药品，应当按照国务院药品监督管理部门的规定，向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门或者国务院药品监督管理部门提出申请，报送有关技术资料并提供相关证明文件。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门应当自受理申请之日起30个工作日内进行审查，提出意见后报送国务院药品监督管理部门审核，并同时将审查意见通知申报方。国务院药品监督管理部门经审核符合规定的，发给药品批准文号。

**Article 31** To manufacture a drug that already has a national standard, an application shall be made to the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or to the drug regulatory department under the State Council in accordance with the regulations of the drug regulatory department under the State Council, together with the submission of relevant technical materials and relevant supporting documents. The drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 30 working days from the date of accepting the application, conduct an examination and, after issuing an opinion, submit the materials to the drug regulatory department under the State Council for review, and shall at the same time notify the applicant of its examination opinion. If the drug regulatory department under the State Council, after review, finds that the requirements are met, it shall issue a drug approval number.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　变更研制新药、生产药品和进口药品已获批准证明文件及其附件中载明事项的，应当向国务院药品监督管理部门提出补充申请；国务院药品监督管理部门经审核符合规定的，应当予以批准。其中，不改变药品内在质量的，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门提出补充申请；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门经审核符合规定的，应当予以批准，并报国务院药品监督管理部门备案。不改变药品内在质量的补充申请事项由国务院药品监督管理部门制定。

**Article 32** To change matters specified in the approved supporting documents and their annexes for the development of a new drug, manufacture of a drug, or import of a drug, a supplementary application shall be submitted to the drug regulatory department under the State Council; if the drug regulatory department under the State Council, after examination, finds that the requirements are met, it shall approve the change. Among these, if the intrinsic quality of the drug is not changed, a supplementary application shall be submitted to the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; if the drug regulatory department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, after examination, finds that the requirements are met, it shall approve the change and report it to the drug regulatory department under the State Council for record. Matters of supplementary applications that do not change the intrinsic quality of drugs shall be specified by the drug regulatory department under the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院药品监督管理部门根据保护公众健康的要求，可以对药品生产企业生产的新药品种设立不超过5年的监测期；在监测期内，不得批准其他企业生产和进口。

**Article 33** The drug regulatory department under the State Council may, based on the need to protect public health, establish a monitoring period of not more than 5 years for new drug varieties manufactured by drug manufacturing enterprises; during the monitoring period, no other enterprise shall be approved to manufacture or import such drugs.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家对获得生产或者销售含有新型化学成份药品许可的生产者或者销售者提交的自行取得且未披露的试验数据和其他数据实施保护，任何人不得对该未披露的试验数据和其他数据进行不正当的商业利用。  
自药品生产者或者销售者获得生产、销售新型化学成份药品的许可证明文件之日起6年内，对其他申请人未经已获得许可的申请人同意，使用前款数据申请生产、销售新型化学成份药品许可的，药品监督管理部门不予许可；但是，其他申请人提交自行取得数据的除外。  
除下列情形外，药品监督管理部门不得披露本条第一款规定的数据：  
（一）公共利益需要；  
（二）已采取措施确保该类数据不会被不正当地进行商业利用。

**Article 34** The State protects the undisclosed test data and other data submitted by a manufacturer or distributor that has obtained a license to manufacture or distribute a drug containing a new chemical entity, which were obtained independently and have not been disclosed. No person shall improperly commercially exploit such undisclosed test data and other data.  
Within 6 years from the date on which a drug manufacturer or distributor obtains the license document for manufacturing or distributing a drug containing a new chemical entity, if another applicant, without the consent of the licensed applicant, uses the data mentioned in the preceding paragraph to apply for a license to manufacture or distribute a drug containing a new chemical entity, the drug regulatory department shall not grant the license; however, this shall not apply if the other applicant submits independently obtained data.  
Except under the following circumstances, the drug regulatory department shall not disclose the data specified in paragraph 1 of this Article:  
(1) Public interest requires it;  
(2) Measures have been taken to ensure that such data will not be improperly commercially exploited.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　申请进口的药品，应当是在生产国家或者地区获得上市许可的药品；未在生产国家或者地区获得上市许可的，经国务院药品监督管理部门确认该药品品种安全、有效而且临床需要的，可以依照《药品管理法》及本条例的规定批准进口。  
进口药品，应当按照国务院药品监督管理部门的规定申请注册。国外企业生产的药品取得《进口药品注册证》，中国香港、澳门和台湾地区企业生产的药品取得《医药产品注册证》后，方可进口。

**Article 35** A drug applied for import shall be a drug that has obtained marketing authorization in the country or region of manufacture; if it has not obtained marketing authorization in the country or region of manufacture, upon confirmation by the drug regulatory department under the State Council that the drug variety is safe, effective, and clinically needed, the import may be approved in accordance with the provisions of the Drug Administration Law and these Regulations.  
For imported drugs, registration shall be applied for in accordance with the regulations of the drug regulatory department under the State Council. Drugs manufactured by foreign enterprises shall be imported only after obtaining an Import Drug Registration Certificate, and drugs manufactured by enterprises in Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan regions shall be imported only after obtaining a Pharmaceutical Product Registration Certificate.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　医疗机构因临床急需进口少量药品的，应当持《医疗机构执业许可证》向国务院药品监督管理部门提出申请；经批准后，方可进口。进口的药品应当在指定医疗机构内用于特定医疗目的。

**Article 36** If a medical institution urgently needs to import a small quantity of drugs for clinical use, it shall submit an application to the drug regulatory department under the State Council with its Medical Institution Practice License; the import may be made only after approval. The imported drugs shall be used for specific medical purposes within the designated medical institution.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　进口药品到岸后，进口单位应当持《进口药品注册证》或者《医药产品注册证》以及产地证明原件、购货合同副本、装箱单、运单、货运发票、出厂检验报告书、说明书等材料，向口岸所在地药品监督管理部门备案。口岸所在地药品监督管理部门经审查，提交的材料符合要求的，发给《进口药品通关单》。进口单位凭《进口药品通关单》向海关办理报关验放手续。  
口岸所在地药品监督管理部门应当通知药品检验机构对进口药品逐批进行抽查检验；但是，有[《药品管理法》第四十一条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-41)规定情形的除外。

**Article 37** After an imported drug arrives at port, the importing entity shall file with the drug regulatory department at the port location by presenting the Import Drug Registration Certificate or Pharmaceutical Product Registration Certificate, together with the original certificate of origin, copy of the purchase contract, packing list, waybill, freight invoice, factory inspection report, package insert, and other materials. The drug regulatory department at the port location shall, after examination, issue a Customs Clearance Form for Imported Drugs if the submitted materials meet the requirements. The importing entity shall, with the Customs Clearance Form for Imported Drugs, handle customs declaration and release procedures with the customs.  
The drug regulatory department at the port location shall notify the drug testing institution to conduct batch-by-batch random testing of the imported drugs; however, this shall not apply if the circumstances specified in Article 41 of the Drug Administration Law exist.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　疫苗类制品、血液制品、用于血源筛查的体外诊断试剂以及国务院药品监督管理部门规定的其他生物制品在销售前或者进口时，应当按照国务院药品监督管理部门的规定进行检验或者审核批准；检验不合格或者未获批准的，不得销售或者进口。

**Article 38** Vaccine products, blood products, in vitro diagnostic reagents used for blood source screening, and other biological products specified by the drug regulatory department under the State Council shall, before sale or import, be tested or examined and approved in accordance with the regulations of the drug regulatory department under the State Council; those that fail the test or are not approved shall not be sold or imported.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家鼓励培育中药材。对集中规模化栽培养殖、质量可以控制并符合国务院药品监督管理部门规定条件的中药材品种，实行批准文号管理。

**Article 39** The State encourages the cultivation of Chinese medicinal materials. For Chinese medicinal material varieties that are cultivated or bred on a centralized scale, whose quality can be controlled and that meet the conditions prescribed by the drug regulatory department under the State Council, approval number management shall be implemented.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国务院药品监督管理部门对已批准生产、销售的药品进行再评价，根据药品再评价结果，可以采取责令修改药品说明书，暂停生产、销售和使用的措施；对不良反应大或者其他原因危害人体健康的药品，应当撤销该药品批准证明文件。

**Article 40** The drug regulatory department under the State Council shall conduct a re-evaluation of drugs that have been approved for manufacture and sale, and based on the results of the drug re-evaluation, may take measures such as ordering the revision of the drug package insert or suspending the manufacture, sale, and use of the drug; for drugs that have significant adverse reactions or otherwise endanger human health, the drug approval document shall be revoked.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国务院药品监督管理部门核发的药品批准文号、《进口药品注册证》、《医药产品注册证》的有效期为5年。有效期届满，需要继续生产或者进口的，应当在有效期届满前6个月申请再注册。药品再注册时，应当按照国务院药品监督管理部门的规定报送相关资料。有效期届满，未申请再注册或者经审查不符合国务院药品监督管理部门关于再注册的规定的，注销其药品批准文号、《进口药品注册证》或者《医药产品注册证》。  
药品批准文号的再注册由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门审批，并报国务院药品监督管理部门备案；《进口药品注册证》、《医药产品注册证》的再注册由国务院药品监督管理部门审批。

**Article 41** The drug approval number, Import Drug Registration Certificate, and Pharmaceutical Product Registration Certificate issued by the drug regulatory department under the State Council shall be valid for 5 years. If continued manufacture or import is needed upon expiration, an application for re-registration shall be filed within 6 months before the expiration of the validity period. For drug re-registration, relevant materials shall be submitted in accordance with the regulations of the drug regulatory department under the State Council. If no application for re-registration is filed upon expiration or if the examination finds that the requirements for re-registration prescribed by the drug regulatory department under the State Council are not met, the drug approval number, Import Drug Registration Certificate, or Pharmaceutical Product Registration Certificate shall be canceled.  
The re-registration of drug approval numbers shall be examined and approved by the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and reported to the drug regulatory department under the State Council for record; the re-registration of Import Drug Registration Certificates and Pharmaceutical Product Registration Certificates shall be examined and approved by the drug regulatory department under the State Council.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　非药品不得在其包装、标签、说明书及有关宣传资料上进行含有预防、治疗、诊断人体疾病等有关内容的宣传；但是，法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 42** Non-drug products shall not contain, in their packaging, labels, package inserts, or related promotional materials, any content that involves the prevention, treatment, or diagnosis of human diseases; however, this shall not apply where laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise.

### 第六章　药品包装的管理

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　药品生产企业使用的直接接触药品的包装材料和容器，必须符合药用要求和保障人体健康、安全的标准。  
直接接触药品的包装材料和容器的管理办法、产品目录和药用要求与标准，由国务院药品监督管理部门组织制定并公布。

**Article 43** The packaging materials and containers that directly contact drugs and are used by drug manufacturing enterprises must meet the requirements for medicinal use and the standards for ensuring human health and safety.  
The administrative measures, product catalog, and requirements and standards for medicinal use for packaging materials and containers that directly contact drugs shall be formulated and published by the drug regulatory department under the State Council.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　生产中药饮片，应当选用与药品性质相适应的包装材料和容器；包装不符合规定的中药饮片，不得销售。中药饮片包装必须印有或者贴有标签。  
中药饮片的标签必须注明品名、规格、产地、生产企业、产品批号、生产日期，实施批准文号管理的中药饮片还必须注明药品批准文号。

**Article 44** For the manufacture of prepared slices of Chinese crude drugs, packaging materials and containers that are compatible with the nature of the drugs shall be selected; prepared slices of Chinese crude drugs that are not packaged in compliance with the regulations shall not be sold. The packaging of prepared slices of Chinese crude drugs must bear or have affixed a label.  
The label of prepared slices of Chinese crude drugs must indicate the name, specification, place of origin, manufacturer, product batch number, date of manufacture, and, for prepared slices of Chinese crude drugs subject to approval number management, the drug approval number must also be indicated.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　药品包装、标签、说明书必须依照[《药品管理法》第五十四条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-54)和国务院药品监督管理部门的规定印制。  
药品商品名称应当符合国务院药品监督管理部门的规定。

**Article 45** The packaging, labels, and package inserts of drugs shall be printed in accordance with Article 54 of the Drug Administration Law and the regulations of the drug regulatory department under the State Council.  
The trade names of drugs shall comply with the regulations of the drug regulatory department under the State Council.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　医疗机构配制制剂所使用的直接接触药品的包装材料和容器、制剂的标签和说明书应当符合《药品管理法》第六章和本条例的有关规定，并经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准。

**Article 46** The packaging materials and containers that directly contact drugs used by a medical institution for preparing preparations, and the labels and package inserts of such preparations, shall comply with the provisions of Chapter 6 of the Drug Administration Law and the relevant provisions of these Regulations, and shall be approved by the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

### 第七章　药品价格和广告的管理

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　政府价格主管部门依照[《价格法》第二十八条](../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国价格法.md#art-28)的规定实行药品价格监测时，为掌握、分析药品价格变动和趋势，可以指定部分药品生产企业、药品经营企业和医疗机构作为价格监测定点单位；定点单位应当给予配合、支持，如实提供有关信息资料。

**Article 47** When the government price authority conducts drug price monitoring in accordance with Article 28 of the Price Law, it may designate certain drug manufacturing enterprises, drug distribution enterprises, and medical institutions as designated price monitoring units for the purpose of understanding and analyzing changes and trends in drug prices; the designated units shall cooperate and provide support and truthfully provide relevant information and materials.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　发布药品广告，应当向药品生产企业所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门报送有关材料。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门应当自收到有关材料之日起10个工作日内作出是否核发药品广告批准文号的决定；核发药品广告批准文号的，应当同时报国务院药品监督管理部门备案。具体办法由国务院药品监督管理部门制定。  
发布进口药品广告，应当依照前款规定向进口药品代理机构所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门申请药品广告批准文号。  
在药品生产企业所在地和进口药品代理机构所在地以外的省、自治区、直辖市发布药品广告的，发布广告的企业应当在发布前向发布地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门备案。接受备案的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门发现药品广告批准内容不符合药品广告管理规定的，应当交由原核发部门处理。

**Article 48** To publish a drug advertisement, relevant materials shall be submitted to the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the drug manufacturing enterprise is located. The drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 10 working days from the date of receiving the relevant materials, make a decision on whether to issue a drug advertisement approval number; if a drug advertisement approval number is issued, it shall also report it to the drug regulatory department under the State Council for record. Specific measures shall be formulated by the drug regulatory department under the State Council.  
To publish an advertisement for an imported drug, an application for a drug advertisement approval number shall be submitted to the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the importing agent is located, in accordance with the preceding paragraph.  
If a drug advertisement is to be published in a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government other than the location of the drug manufacturing enterprise or the importing agent, the enterprise publishing the advertisement shall, before publication, file it with the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the publication is to be made. If the drug regulatory department that accepts the filing finds that the approved content of the drug advertisement does not comply with the regulations on drug advertisement management, it shall refer the matter to the original approval authority for handling.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　经国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的药品监督管理部门决定，责令暂停生产、销售和使用的药品，在暂停期间不得发布该品种药品广告；已经发布广告的，必须立即停止。

**Article 49** If, by a decision of the drug regulatory department under the State Council or the drug regulatory department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the manufacture, sale, or use of a drug is ordered to be suspended, no advertisement for that drug variety shall be published during the suspension period; if an advertisement has already been published, it must be immediately stopped.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　未经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准的药品广告，使用伪造、冒用、失效的药品广告批准文号的广告，或者因其他广告违法活动被撤销药品广告批准文号的广告，发布广告的企业、广告经营者、广告发布者必须立即停止该药品广告的发布。  
对违法发布药品广告，情节严重的，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门可以予以公告。

**Article 50** For a drug advertisement that has not been approved by the drug regulatory department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or that uses a forged, falsely claimed, or expired drug advertisement approval number, or that has had its drug advertisement approval number revoked due to other illegal advertising activities, the enterprise that publishes the advertisement, the advertising operator, and the advertising publisher must immediately stop publishing the drug advertisement.  
Where a drug advertisement is published in violation of the law and the circumstances are serious, the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may make a public announcement.

### 第八章　药品监督

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　药品监督管理部门（含省级人民政府药品监督管理部门依法设立的药品监督管理机构，下同）依法对药品的研制、生产、经营、使用实施监督检查。

**Article 51** The drug regulatory department (including the drug regulatory institutions lawfully established by the drug regulatory department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the same below) shall, according to law, conduct supervision and inspection of the development, manufacture, distribution, and use of drugs.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　药品抽样必须由两名以上药品监督检查人员实施，并按照国务院药品监督管理部门的规定进行抽样；被抽检方应当提供抽检样品，不得拒绝。  
药品被抽检单位没有正当理由，拒绝抽查检验的，国务院药品监督管理部门和被抽检单位所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门可以宣布停止该单位拒绝抽检的药品上市销售和使用。

**Article 52** Drug sampling must be carried out by two or more drug inspectors in accordance with the regulations of the drug regulatory department under the State Council; the party from which the sample is taken shall provide the sample for testing and shall not refuse.  
If a unit from which a drug sample is taken refuses to undergo sampling inspection without justifiable reasons, the drug regulatory department under the State Council and the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the unit is located may declare that the drugs for which sampling is refused by that unit shall be prohibited from being marketed, sold, or used.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　对有掺杂、掺假嫌疑的药品，在国家药品标准规定的检验方法和检验项目不能检验时，药品检验机构可以补充检验方法和检验项目进行药品检验；经国务院药品监督管理部门批准后，使用补充检验方法和检验项目所得出的检验结果，可以作为药品监督管理部门认定药品质量的依据。

**Article 53** For drugs suspected of being adulterated or mixed with impurities, when the testing methods and testing items prescribed by the national drug standards cannot test them, the drug testing institution may supplement testing methods and testing items for drug testing; upon approval by the drug regulatory department under the State Council, the test results obtained using the supplementary testing methods and testing items may serve as the basis for the drug regulatory department to determine drug quality.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的药品监督管理部门应当根据药品质量抽查检验结果，定期发布药品质量公告。药品质量公告应当包括抽验药品的品名、检品来源、生产企业、生产批号、药品规格、检验机构、检验依据、检验结果、不合格项目等内容。药品质量公告不当的，发布部门应当自确认公告不当之日起5日内，在原公告范围内予以更正。  
当事人对药品检验机构的检验结果有异议，申请复验的，应当向负责复验的药品检验机构提交书面申请、原药品检验报告书。复验的样品从原药品检验机构留样中抽取。

**Article 54** The drug regulatory departments under the State Council and under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the results of drug quality random inspections, regularly publish drug quality bulletins. The drug quality bulletin shall include the name of the tested drug, source of the test sample, manufacturer, production batch number, drug specification, testing institution, testing basis, test results, non-conforming items, and other contents. If the drug quality bulletin is improper, the issuing authority shall make a correction within 5 days from the date of confirming the impropriety, within the original scope of the bulletin.  
If a party has objections to the test results of the drug testing institution and applies for re-testing, it shall submit a written application and the original drug test report to the drug testing institution responsible for re-testing. The sample for re-testing shall be taken from the sample retained by the original drug testing institution.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　药品监督管理部门依法对有证据证明可能危害人体健康的药品及其有关证据材料采取查封、扣押的行政强制措施的，应当自采取行政强制措施之日起7日内作出是否立案的决定；需要检验的，应当自检验报告书发出之日起15日内作出是否立案的决定；不符合立案条件的，应当解除行政强制措施；需要暂停销售和使用的，应当由国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的药品监督管理部门作出决定。

**Article 55** Where the drug regulatory department according to law takes administrative compulsory measures such as sealing up or seizing drugs that are proven to potentially harm human health and related evidentiary materials, it shall, within 7 days from the date of taking the administrative compulsory measures, decide whether to file a case; if testing is required, it shall decide whether to file a case within 15 days from the date of issuance of the test report; if the conditions for filing a case are not met, the administrative compulsory measures shall be lifted; if the suspension of sale and use is necessary, a decision shall be made by the drug regulatory department under the State Council or the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　药品抽查检验，不得收取任何费用。  
当事人对药品检验结果有异议，申请复验的，应当按照国务院有关部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关部门的规定，向复验机构预先支付药品检验费用。复验结论与原检验结论不一致的，复验检验费用由原药品检验机构承担。

**Article 56** No fees shall be charged for drug random inspections.  
If a party has objections to the drug test results and applies for re-testing, it shall prepay the drug testing fee to the re-testing institution in accordance with the regulations of the relevant department under the State Council or the relevant department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. If the conclusion of the re-testing is inconsistent with the original test conclusion, the re-testing fee shall be borne by the original drug testing institution.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　依据《药品管理法》和本条例的规定核发证书、进行药品注册、药品认证和实施药品审批检验及其强制性检验，可以收取费用。具体收费标准由国务院财政部门、国务院价格主管部门制定。

**Article 57** Fees may be charged for issuing certificates, conducting drug registration, drug certification, and implementing drug approval testing and compulsory testing in accordance with the provisions of the Drug Administration Law and these Regulations. The specific fee standards shall be formulated by the finance department under the State Council and the price authority under the State Council.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　药品生产企业、药品经营企业有下列情形之一的，由药品监督管理部门依照[《药品管理法》第七十九条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-79)的规定给予处罚：  
（一）开办药品生产企业、药品生产企业新建药品生产车间、新增生产剂型，在国务院药品监督管理部门规定的时间内未通过《药品生产质量管理规范》认证，仍进行药品生产的；  
（二）开办药品经营企业，在国务院药品监督管理部门规定的时间内未通过《药品经营质量管理规范》认证，仍进行药品经营的。

**Article 58** Where a drug manufacturing enterprise or drug distribution enterprise falls under any of the following circumstances, the drug regulatory department shall impose a penalty in accordance with Article 79 of the Drug Administration Law:  
(1) A drug manufacturing enterprise that is established, a drug manufacturing enterprise that builds a new drug production workshop or adds a new production dosage form fails to pass the certification under the Good Manufacturing Practice for Pharmaceutical Products within the time prescribed by the drug regulatory department under the State Council, and still manufactures drugs;  
(2) A drug distribution enterprise that is established fails to pass the certification under the Good Supply Practice for Pharmaceutical Products within the time prescribed by the drug regulatory department under the State Council, and still distributes drugs.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反[《药品管理法》第十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-13)的规定，擅自委托或者接受委托生产药品的，对委托方和受托方均依照[《药品管理法》第七十四条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-74)的规定给予处罚。

**Article 59** Where a party, in violation of Article 13 of the Drug Administration Law, without authorization entrusts or accepts the entrustment to manufacture drugs, both the entrusting party and the entrusted party shall be penalized in accordance with Article 74 of the Drug Administration Law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　未经批准，擅自在城乡集市贸易市场设点销售药品或者在城乡集市贸易市场设点销售的药品超出批准经营的药品范围的，依照[《药品管理法》第七十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-73)的规定给予处罚。

**Article 60** Where a party, without approval, without authorization sets up a sales point in an urban or rural market fair to sell drugs, or sells drugs at a sales point set up in an urban or rural market fair that exceed the scope of drugs approved for distribution, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with Article 73 of the Drug Administration Law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　未经批准，医疗机构擅自使用其他医疗机构配制的制剂的，依照[《药品管理法》第八十条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-80)的规定给予处罚。

**Article 61** Where a medical institution, without approval, without authorization uses preparations prepared by another medical institution, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with Article 80 of the Drug Administration Law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　个人设置的门诊部、诊所等医疗机构向患者提供的药品超出规定的范围和品种的，依照[《药品管理法》第七十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-73)的规定给予处罚。

**Article 62** Where a medical institution such as an outpatient department or clinic established by an individual provides patients with drugs that exceed the prescribed scope and varieties, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with Article 73 of the Drug Administration Law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　医疗机构使用假药、劣药的，依照[《药品管理法》第七十四条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-74)、第七十五条的规定给予处罚。

**Article 63** Where a medical institution uses counterfeit drugs or substandard drugs, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with Articles 74 and 75 of the Drug Administration Law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反[《药品管理法》第二十九条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-29)的规定，擅自进行临床试验的，对承担药物临床试验的机构，依照[《药品管理法》第七十九条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-79)的规定给予处罚。

**Article 64** Where a party, in violation of Article 29 of the Drug Administration Law, without authorization conducts clinical trials, the institution that undertakes the drug clinical trial shall be penalized in accordance with Article 79 of the Drug Administration Law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　药品申报者在申报临床试验时，报送虚假研制方法、质量标准、药理及毒理试验结果等有关资料和样品的，国务院药品监督管理部门对该申报药品的临床试验不予批准，对药品申报者给予警告；情节严重的，3年内不受理该药品申报者申报该品种的临床试验申请。

**Article 65** Where a drug applicant, when applying for a clinical trial, submits false research methods, quality standards, pharmacological and toxicological test results, and other relevant materials and samples, the drug regulatory department under the State Council shall not approve the clinical trial for the applied drug and shall give a warning to the drug applicant; if the circumstances are serious, the drug applicant's application for a clinical trial of that variety shall not be accepted for 3 years.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　生产没有国家药品标准的中药饮片，不符合省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门制定的炮制规范的；医疗机构不按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准的标准配制制剂的，依照[《药品管理法》第七十五条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-75)的规定给予处罚。

**Article 66** Where prepared slices of Chinese crude drugs that do not have a national drug standard are manufactured and do not meet the processing specifications formulated by the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; or where a medical institution prepares preparations that do not comply with the standards approved by the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with Article 75 of the Drug Administration Law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　药品监督管理部门及其工作人员违反规定，泄露生产者、销售者为获得生产、销售含有新型化学成份药品许可而提交的未披露试验数据或者其他数据，造成申请人损失的，由药品监督管理部门依法承担赔偿责任；药品监督管理部门赔偿损失后，应当责令故意或者有重大过失的工作人员承担部分或者全部赔偿费用，并对直接责任人员依法给予行政处分。

**Article 67** Where a drug regulatory department or its staff member, in violation of regulations, discloses undisclosed test data or other data submitted by a manufacturer or distributor to obtain a license for manufacturing or distributing a drug containing a new chemical entity, causing losses to the applicant, the drug regulatory department shall bear compensation liability according to law; after compensating the losses, the drug regulatory department shall order the staff member who acted intentionally or with gross negligence to bear part or all of the compensation expenses, and shall impose administrative sanctions on the person directly responsible according to law.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　药品生产企业、药品经营企业生产、经营的药品及医疗机构配制的制剂，其包装、标签、说明书违反《药品管理法》及本条例规定的，依照[《药品管理法》第八十六条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-86)的规定给予处罚。

**Article 68** Where the packaging, labels, or package inserts of drugs manufactured or distributed by a drug manufacturing enterprise or drug distribution enterprise, or of preparations prepared by a medical institution, violate the provisions of the Drug Administration Law or these Regulations, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with Article 86 of the Drug Administration Law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　药品生产企业、药品经营企业和医疗机构变更药品生产经营许可事项，应当办理变更登记手续而未办理的，由原发证部门给予警告，责令限期补办变更登记手续；逾期不补办的，宣布其《药品生产许可证》、《药品经营许可证》和《医疗机构制剂许可证》无效；仍从事药品生产经营活动的，依照[《药品管理法》第七十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-73)的规定给予处罚。

**Article 69** Where a drug manufacturing enterprise, drug distribution enterprise, or medical institution changes the licensed matters for drug manufacturing or distribution and fails to go through the modification registration procedures, the original issuing authority shall give a warning and order it to go through the modification registration procedures within a specified time limit; if it fails to do so within the time limit, its Drug Manufacturing License, Drug Distribution License, or Medical Institution Preparation License shall be declared invalid; if it still engages in drug manufacturing or distribution activities, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with Article 73 of the Drug Administration Law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　篡改经批准的药品广告内容的，由药品监督管理部门责令广告主立即停止该药品广告的发布，并由原审批的药品监督管理部门依照[《药品管理法》第九十二条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-92)的规定给予处罚。  
药品监督管理部门撤销药品广告批准文号后，应当自作出行政处理决定之日起5个工作日内通知广告监督管理机关。广告监督管理机关应当自收到药品监督管理部门通知之日起15个工作日内，依照《中华人民共和国广告法》的有关规定作出行政处理决定。

**Article 70** Where the approved content of a drug advertisement is altered, the drug regulatory department shall order the advertiser to immediately stop publishing the drug advertisement, and the originally approving drug regulatory department shall impose a penalty in accordance with Article 92 of the Drug Administration Law.  
After the drug regulatory department revokes a drug advertisement approval number, it shall notify the advertising regulatory authority within 5 working days from the date of making the administrative decision. The advertising regulatory authority shall, within 15 working days from the date of receiving the notification from the drug regulatory department, make an administrative decision in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Advertising Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　发布药品广告的企业在药品生产企业所在地或者进口药品代理机构所在地以外的省、自治区、直辖市发布药品广告，未按照规定向发布地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门备案的，由发布地的药品监督管理部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，停止该药品品种在发布地的广告发布活动。

**Article 71** Where an enterprise that publishes a drug advertisement publishes the drug advertisement in a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government other than the location of the drug manufacturing enterprise or the importing agent, and fails to file it with the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the publication is made, the drug regulatory department at the place of publication shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the advertising activities for that drug variety in the place of publication shall be stopped.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　未经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准，擅自发布药品广告的，药品监督管理部门发现后，应当通知广告监督管理部门依法查处。

**Article 72** Where a party publishes a drug advertisement without the approval of the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the drug regulatory department, upon discovering such act, shall notify the advertising regulatory authority to investigate and punish it according to law.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　违反《药品管理法》和本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由药品监督管理部门在《药品管理法》和本条例规定的处罚幅度内从重处罚：  
（一）以麻醉药品、精神药品、医疗用毒性药品、放射性药品冒充其他药品，或者以其他药品冒充上述药品的；  
（二）生产、销售以孕产妇、婴幼儿及儿童为主要使用对象的假药、劣药的；  
（三）生产、销售的生物制品、血液制品属于假药、劣药的；  
（四）生产、销售、使用假药、劣药，造成人员伤害后果的；  
（五）生产、销售、使用假药、劣药，经处理后重犯的；  
（六）拒绝、逃避监督检查，或者伪造、销毁、隐匿有关证据材料的，或者擅自动用查封、扣押物品的。

**Article 73** Where a party, in violation of the provisions of the Drug Administration Law and these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the drug regulatory department shall impose a severe punishment within the range of penalties prescribed by the Drug Administration Law and these Regulations:  
(1) Using narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, medicinal toxic drugs, or radioactive drugs to impersonate other drugs, or using other drugs to impersonate the aforementioned drugs;  
(2) Manufacturing or selling counterfeit or substandard drugs that are mainly used by pregnant women, infants, young children, or children;  
(3) Manufacturing or selling biological products or blood products that are counterfeit or substandard;  
(4) Manufacturing, selling, or using counterfeit or substandard drugs, resulting in personal injury;  
(5) Manufacturing, selling, or using counterfeit or substandard drugs, and repeating the offense after being penalized;  
(6) Refusing or evading supervision and inspection, or forging, destroying, or concealing relevant evidentiary materials, or without authorization using items that have been sealed up or seized.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　药品监督管理部门设置的派出机构，有权作出《药品管理法》和本条例规定的警告、罚款、没收违法生产、销售的药品和违法所得的行政处罚。

**Article 74** The dispatched institutions established by the drug regulatory department shall have the authority to impose administrative penalties such as warnings, fines, and confiscation of illegally manufactured or sold drugs and illegal income as prescribed by the Drug Administration Law and these Regulations.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　药品经营企业、医疗机构未违反《药品管理法》和本条例的有关规定，并有充分证据证明其不知道所销售或者使用的药品是假药、劣药的，应当没收其销售或者使用的假药、劣药和违法所得；但是，可以免除其他行政处罚。

**Article 75** Where a drug distribution enterprise or medical institution has not violated the relevant provisions of the Drug Administration Law and these Regulations, and has sufficient evidence to prove that it did not know that the drugs it sold or used were counterfeit or substandard, the counterfeit or substandard drugs sold or used and the illegal income shall be confiscated; however, other administrative penalties may be exempted.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　依照《药品管理法》和本条例的规定没收的物品，由药品监督管理部门按照规定监督处理。

**Article 76** Items confiscated in accordance with the provisions of the Drug Administration Law and these Regulations shall be supervised and disposed of by the drug regulatory department in accordance with the regulations.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
药品合格证明和其他标识，是指药品生产批准证明文件、药品检验报告书、药品的包装、标签和说明书。  
新药，是指未曾在中国境内上市销售的药品。  
处方药，是指凭执业医师和执业助理医师处方方可购买、调配和使用的药品。  
非处方药，是指由国务院药品监督管理部门公布的，不需要凭执业医师和执业助理医师处方，消费者可以自行判断、购买和使用的药品。  
医疗机构制剂，是指医疗机构根据本单位临床需要经批准而配制、自用的固定处方制剂。  
药品认证，是指药品监督管理部门对药品研制、生产、经营、使用单位实施相应质量管理规范进行检查、评价并决定是否发给相应认证证书的过程。  
药品经营方式，是指药品批发和药品零售。  
药品经营范围，是指经药品监督管理部门核准经营药品的品种类别。  
药品批发企业，是指将购进的药品销售给药品生产企业、药品经营企业、医疗机构的药品经营企业。  
药品零售企业，是指将购进的药品直接销售给消费者的药品经营企业。

**Article 77** The meanings of the following terms used in these Regulations:  
Drug qualification certificates and other identifiers refer to drug manufacturing approval documents, drug test reports, drug packaging, labels, and package inserts.  
New drugs refer to drugs that have never been marketed in China.  
Prescription drugs refer to drugs that can only be purchased, dispensed, and used with a prescription issued by a licensed physician or licensed assistant physician.  
Over-the-counter drugs refer to drugs that are announced by the drug regulatory department under the State Council, do not require a prescription from a licensed physician or licensed assistant physician, and can be self-assessed, purchased, and used by consumers.  
Medical institution preparations refer to preparations with fixed prescriptions that a medical institution prepares for its own use upon approval based on its own clinical needs.  
Drug certification refers to the process by which the drug regulatory department inspects and evaluates the implementation of corresponding quality management standards by units engaged in drug development, manufacturing, distribution, and use, and decides whether to issue corresponding certification certificates.  
Drug distribution methods refer to drug wholesale and drug retail.  
Drug distribution scope refers to the categories of drug varieties approved by the drug regulatory department for distribution.  
Drug wholesale enterprise refers to a drug distribution enterprise that sells purchased drugs to drug manufacturing enterprises, drug distribution enterprises, and medical institutions.  
Drug retail enterprise refers to a drug distribution enterprise that sells purchased drugs directly to consumers.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　[《药品管理法》第四十一条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-41)中“首次在中国销售的药品”，是指国内或者国外药品生产企业第一次在中国销售的药品，包括不同药品生产企业生产的相同品种。

**Article 78** "Drugs marketed in China for the first time" in Article 41 of the Drug Administration Law refers to drugs marketed in China for the first time by domestic or foreign drug manufacturing enterprises, including the same variety produced by different drug manufacturing enterprises.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　[《药品管理法》第五十九条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-59)第二款“禁止药品的生产企业、经营企业或者其代理人以任何名义给予使用其药品的医疗机构的负责人、药品采购人员、医师等有关人员以财物或者其他利益”中的“财物或者其他利益”，是指药品的生产企业、经营企业或者其代理人向医疗机构的负责人、药品采购人员、医师等有关人员提供的目的在于影响其药品采购或者药品处方行为的不正当利益。

**Article 79** The term "property or other benefits" in the second paragraph of Article 59 of the Drug Administration Law, which prohibits drug manufacturing enterprises, drug distribution enterprises, or their agents from giving property or other benefits in any name to responsible persons, drug purchasers, physicians, or other relevant personnel of medical institutions that use their drugs, refers to improper benefits provided by drug manufacturing enterprises, drug distribution enterprises, or their agents to responsible persons, drug purchasers, physicians, or other relevant personnel of medical institutions for the purpose of influencing their drug procurement or drug prescribing behavior.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　本条例自2002年9月15日起施行。

**Article 80** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 15, 2002.
