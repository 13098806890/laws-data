### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强放射性药品的管理，根据《中华人民共和国药品管理法》（以下称《药品管理法》）的规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Drug Administration Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Drug Administration Law) for the purpose of strengthening the administration of radiopharmaceuticals.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　放射性药品是指用于临床诊断或者治疗的放射性核素制剂或者其标记药物。

**Article 2** Radiopharmaceuticals refer to radionuclide preparations or their labeled drugs used for clinical diagnosis or treatment.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　凡在中华人民共和国领域内进行放射性药品的研究、生产、经营、运输、使用、检验、监督管理的单位和个人都必须遵守本办法。

**Article 3** All units and individuals that engage in research, production, operation, transportation, use, inspection, and supervision and administration of radiopharmaceuticals within the territory of the People's Republic of China must comply with these Measures.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院药品监督管理部门负责全国放射性药品监督管理工作。国务院国防科技工业主管部门依据职责负责与放射性药品有关的管理工作。国务院环境保护主管部门负责与放射性药品有关的辐射安全与防护的监督管理工作。

**Article 4** The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of radiopharmaceuticals nationwide. The competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense of the State Council shall, pursuant to its functions, be responsible for the administration of matters relating to radiopharmaceuticals. The competent department of environmental protection of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of radiation safety and protection relating to radiopharmaceuticals.

### 第二章　放射性新药的研制、临床研究和审批

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　放射性新药的研制内容，包括工艺路线、质量标准、临床前药理及临床研究。研制单位在制订新药工艺路线的同时，必须研究该药的理化性能、纯度（包括核素纯度）及检验方法、药理、毒理、动物药代动力学、放射性比活度、剂量、剂型、稳定性等。  
研制单位对放射免疫分析药盒必须进行可测限度、范围、特异性、准确度、精密度、稳定性等方法学的研究。  
放射性新药的分类，按国务院药品监督管理部门有关药品注册的规定办理。

**Article 5** The development of new radiopharmaceuticals includes the process route, quality standards, preclinical pharmacological and clinical studies. While establishing the process route for a new drug, the developer must study the physicochemical properties, purity (including radionuclidic purity) and testing methods, pharmacology, toxicology, animal pharmacokinetics, specific activity, dosage, dosage form, stability, etc. of the drug.  
The developer must conduct methodologic studies on the detection limit, range, specificity, accuracy, precision, stability, etc. of radioimmunoassay kits.  
The classification of new radiopharmaceuticals shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the drug regulatory department of the State Council on drug registration.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　研制单位研制的放射性新药，在进行临床试验或者验证前，应当向国务院药品监督管理部门提出申请，按规定报送资料及样品，经国务院药品监督管理部门审批同意后，在国务院药品监督管理部门指定的药物临床试验机构进行临床研究。

**Article 6** Before conducting clinical trials or validation of a new radiopharmaceutical developed, the developer shall submit an application to the drug regulatory department of the State Council, and submit materials and samples as required. Upon approval by the drug regulatory department of the State Council, clinical studies shall be conducted in the drug clinical trial institutions designated by the drug regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　研制单位在放射性新药临床研究结束后，向国务院药品监督管理部门提出申请，经国务院药品监督管理部门审核批准，发给新药证书。国务院药品监督管理部门在审核批准时，应当征求国务院国防科技工业主管部门的意见。

**Article 7** Upon completion of clinical studies of a new radiopharmaceutical, the developer shall submit an application to the drug regulatory department of the State Council. After examination and approval by the drug regulatory department of the State Council, a new drug certificate shall be issued. When examining and approving, the drug regulatory department of the State Council shall seek the opinions of the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense of the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　放射性新药投入生产，需由生产单位或者取得放射性药品生产许可证的研制单位，凭新药证书（副本）向国务院药品监督管理部门提出生产该药的申请，并提供样品，由国务院药品监督管理部门审核发给批准文号。

**Article 8** To put a new radiopharmaceutical into production, the manufacturer or the developer that has obtained a Radiopharmaceutical Manufacturing License shall file an application with the drug regulatory department of the State Council for the production of such drug by presenting the new drug certificate (duplicate), and provide samples. The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall, after examination, issue an approval document number.

### 第三章　放射性药品的生产、经营和进出口

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家根据需要，对放射性药品的生产企业实行合理布局。

**Article 9** The State shall, as needed, implement rational distribution for manufacturers of radiopharmaceuticals.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　开办放射性药品生产、经营企业，必须具备《药品管理法》规定的条件，符合国家有关放射性同位素安全和防护的规定与标准，并履行环境影响评价文件的审批手续；开办放射性药品生产企业，经所在省、自治区、直辖市国防科技工业主管部门审查同意，所在省、自治区、直辖市药品监督管理部门审核批准后，由所在省、自治区、直辖市药品监督管理部门发给《放射性药品生产企业许可证》；开办放射性药品经营企业，经所在省、自治区、直辖市药品监督管理部门审核并征求所在省、自治区、直辖市国防科技工业主管部门意见后批准的，由所在省、自治区、直辖市药品监督管理部门发给《放射性药品经营企业许可证》。无许可证的生产、经营企业，一律不准生产、销售放射性药品。

**Article 10** To establish an enterprise for the production or operation of radiopharmaceuticals, the conditions prescribed in the Drug Administration Law must be satisfied, the relevant state provisions and standards on the safety and protection of radioactive isotopes must be complied with, and the formalities for examination and approval of environmental impact assessment documents must be conducted. To establish an enterprise for the production of radiopharmaceuticals, after examination and consent by the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the enterprise is located, and after examination and approval by the drug regulatory department of the said province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, the drug regulatory department of the said province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall issue the Radiopharmaceutical Manufacturing License to the enterprise. To establish an enterprise for the operation of radiopharmaceuticals, after examination by the drug regulatory department of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the enterprise is located and after soliciting the opinions of the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense of the said province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government and obtaining approval, the drug regulatory department of the said province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall issue the Radiopharmaceutical Operating License to the enterprise. No enterprise without a license shall be allowed to produce or sell radiopharmaceuticals.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　《放射性药品生产企业许可证》、《放射性药品经营企业许可证》的有效期为5年，期满前6个月，放射性药品生产、经营企业应当分别向原发证的药品监督管理部门重新提出申请，按第十条审批程序批准后，换发新证。

**Article 11** The "License for Radioactive Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Enterprise" and the "License for Radioactive Pharmaceutical Distribution Enterprise" shall be valid for 5 years. Six months prior to the expiry, the radioactive pharmaceutical manufacturing and distribution enterprises shall respectively submit a new application to the original issuing drug regulatory department. After approval in accordance with the examination and approval procedures stipulated in Article 10, a new license shall be issued.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　放射性药品生产企业生产已有国家标准的放射性药品，必须经国务院药品监督管理部门征求国务院国防科技工业主管部门意见后审核批准，并发给批准文号。凡是改变国务院药品监督管理部门已批准的生产工艺路线和药品标准的，生产单位必须按原报批程序提出补充申请，经国务院药品监督管理部门批准后方能生产。

**Article 12** Where a radioactive pharmaceutical manufacturing enterprise produces radioactive pharmaceuticals that have existing national standards, it must be examined and approved by the drug regulatory department of the State Council after soliciting opinions from the competent department for science, technology and industry for national defense of the State Council, and an approval number shall be issued. If any production process route or pharmaceutical standard that has been approved by the drug regulatory department of the State Council is altered, the manufacturing unit must submit a supplementary application in accordance with the original approval procedures, and production may only commence after approval by the drug regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　放射性药品生产、经营企业，必须配备与生产、经营放射性药品相适应的专业技术人员，具有安全、防护和废气、废物、废水处理等设施，并建立严格的质量管理制度。

**Article 13** Radioactive pharmaceutical manufacturing and distribution enterprises must be equipped with professional and technical personnel appropriate to the production and distribution of radioactive pharmaceuticals, possess facilities for safety, protection, and treatment of waste gas, waste residue, and waste water, and establish a strict quality management system.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　放射性药品生产、经营企业，必须建立质量检验机构，严格实行生产全过程的质量控制和检验。产品出厂前，须经质量检验。符合国家药品标准的产品方可出厂，不符合标准的产品一律不准出厂。  
经国务院药品监督管理部门审核批准的含有短半衰期放射性核素的药品，可以边检验边出厂，但发现质量不符合国家药品标准时，该药品的生产企业应当立即停止生产、销售，并立即通知使用单位停止使用，同时报告国务院药品监督管理、卫生行政、国防科技工业主管部门。

**Article 14** Radioactive pharmaceutical manufacturing and distribution enterprises must establish a quality inspection department, strictly implement quality control and inspection throughout the entire production process. Before leaving the factory, products shall undergo quality inspection. Products that meet the national pharmaceutical standards may leave the factory; those that do not meet the standards shall not leave the factory.  
For pharmaceuticals containing short half-life radionuclides that have been examined and approved by the drug regulatory department of the State Council, they may be released for shipment while being inspected. However, if the quality is found not to meet the national pharmaceutical standards, the manufacturing enterprise of such pharmaceuticals shall immediately cease production and sale, and shall immediately notify the using units to stop use, and simultaneously report to the drug regulatory department, the health administrative department, and the competent department for science, technology and industry for national defense of the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　进口的放射性药品品种，必须符合我国的药品标准或者其他药用要求，并依照《药品管理法》的规定取得进口药品注册证书。  
进出口放射性药品，应当按照国家有关对外贸易、放射性同位素安全和防护的规定，办理进出口手续。

**Article 15** Imported varieties of radioactive pharmaceuticals must comply with the pharmaceutical standards of China or other medicinal requirements, and an import drug registration certificate shall be obtained in accordance with the provisions of the "Drug Administration Law of the People's Republic of China".  
For the import and export of radioactive pharmaceuticals, the import and export procedures shall be handled in accordance with the state provisions on foreign trade, and on the safety and protection of radioactive isotopes.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　进口放射性药品，必须经国务院药品监督管理部门指定的药品检验机构抽样检验；检验合格的，方准进口。  
对于经国务院药品监督管理部门审核批准的含有短半衰期放射性核素的药品，在保证安全使用的情况下，可以采取边进口检验，边投入使用的办法。进口检验单位发现药品质量不符合要求时，应当立即通知使用单位停止使用，并报告国务院药品监督管理、卫生行政、国防科技工业主管部门。

**Article 16** Imported radioactive pharmaceuticals must undergo sampling inspection by a drug testing institution designated by the drug regulatory department of the State Council; only those that pass the inspection may be imported.  
For pharmaceuticals containing short half-life radionuclides that have been examined and approved by the drug regulatory department of the State Council, under the condition that safe use is ensured, the method of conducting import inspection while placing them into use may be adopted. When the import inspection unit discovers that the quality of the pharmaceuticals does not meet the requirements, it shall immediately notify the using units to stop use, and shall report to the drug regulatory department, the health administrative department, and the competent department for science, technology and industry for national defense of the State Council.

### 第四章　放射性药品的包装和运输

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　放射性药品的包装必须安全实用，符合放射性药品质量要求，具有与放射性剂量相适应的防护装置。包装必须分内包装和外包装两部分，外包装必须贴有商标、标签、说明书和放射性药品标志，内包装必须贴有标签。  
标签必须注明药品品名、放射性比活度、装量。  
说明书除注明前款内容外，还须注明生产单位、批准文号、批号、主要成份、出厂日期、放射性核素半衰期、适应症、用法、用量、禁忌症、有效期和注意事项等。

**Article 17** The packaging of radioactive pharmaceuticals must be safe and practical, meet the quality requirements of radioactive pharmaceuticals, and be equipped with shielding devices appropriate to the radiation dose. The packaging shall be divided into inner packaging and outer packaging; the outer packaging shall be affixed with a trademark, label, instruction leaflet, and radioactive pharmaceutical symbol, and the inner packaging shall be affixed with a label.  
The label shall indicate the name of the pharmaceutical, radioactive specific activity, and quantity.  
In addition to the contents mentioned in the preceding paragraph, the instruction leaflet shall also indicate the manufacturing unit, approval number, batch number, main ingredients, date of manufacture, half-life of the radionuclide, indications, usage, dosage, contraindications, expiry date, and precautions, among others.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　放射性药品的运输，按国家运输、邮政等部门制订的有关规定执行。  
严禁任何单位和个人随身携带放射性药品乘坐公共交通运输工具。

**Article 18** The transportation of radioactive pharmaceuticals shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions formulated by the state departments of transport, postal services, and others.  
No unit or individual shall carry radioactive pharmaceuticals on public transport vehicles.

### 第五章　放射性药品的使用

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　医疗单位设置核医学科、室（同位素室），必须配备与其医疗任务相适应的并经核医学技术培训的技术人员。非核医学专业技术人员未经培训，不得从事放射性药品使用工作。

**Article 19** Where a medical unit establishes a nuclear medicine department or unit (isotope unit), it must be equipped with technical personnel who have received training in nuclear medicine technology and are appropriate for its medical tasks. Non-nuclear-medicine professional technical personnel who have not received training shall not engage in the use of radioactive pharmaceuticals.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　医疗单位使用放射性药品应当符合国家有关放射性同位素安全和防护的规定，具有与所使用放射性药品相适应的场所、设备、卫生环境和专用的仓储设施。

**Article 20** A medical unit using radioactive pharmaceuticals shall comply with the state provisions on the safety and protection of radioactive isotopes, and shall have premises, equipment, sanitary environment, and dedicated storage facilities appropriate to the radioactive pharmaceuticals being used.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　医疗单位配制放射性制剂，应当符合《药品管理法》及其实施条例的相关规定。  
医疗单位使用配制的放射性制剂，应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市药品监督管理部门申请核发相应等级的《放射性药品使用许可证》。  
《放射性药品使用许可证》有效期为5年，期满前6个月，医疗单位应当向原发证的行政部门重新提出申请，经审核批准后，换发新证。

**Article 21** A medical unit that prepares radiopharmaceuticals shall comply with the relevant provisions of the Drug Administration Law and its implementing regulations.  
A medical unit that uses self-prepared radiopharmaceuticals shall apply to the drug regulatory department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for the issuance of a Radiopharmaceutical Use License of the corresponding level.  
The Radiopharmaceutical Use License shall be valid for five years. Six months prior to the expiration, the medical unit shall re-apply to the original issuing administrative department, and upon review and approval, a new license shall be issued.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　医疗单位负责对使用的放射性药品进行临床质量检验、收集药品不良反应等项工作，并定期向所在地药品监督管理、卫生行政部门报告。由省、自治区、直辖市药品监督管理、卫生行政部门汇总后分别报国务院药品监督管理、卫生行政部门。

**Article 22** A medical unit shall be responsible for the clinical quality inspection of the radiopharmaceuticals it uses, the collection of adverse drug reactions, and other related tasks, and shall report to the local drug regulatory and health administrative departments on a regular basis. The drug regulatory and health administrative departments of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall summarize and report to the drug regulatory and health administrative departments of the State Council respectively.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　放射性药品使用后的废物（包括患者排出物），必须按国家有关规定妥善处置。

**Article 23** The waste generated after the use of radiopharmaceuticals (including patient excreta) must be properly disposed of in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第六章　放射性药品标准和检验

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　放射性药品的国家标准，由国务院药品监督管理部门药典委员会负责制定和修订，报国务院药品监督管理部门审批颁发。

**Article 24** The national standards for radiopharmaceuticals shall be formulated and revised by the Pharmacopoeia Committee of the drug regulatory department of the State Council, and submitted to the drug regulatory department of the State Council for approval and promulgation.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　放射性药品的检验由国务院药品监督管理部门公布的药品检验机构承担。

**Article 25** The inspection of radiopharmaceuticals shall be undertaken by drug inspection institutions published by the drug regulatory department of the State Council.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对违反本办法规定的单位或者个人，由县以上药品监督管理、卫生行政部门，按照《药品管理法》和有关法规的规定处罚。

**Article 26** Any unit or individual that violates the provisions of these Measures shall be penalized by the drug regulatory or health administrative departments at or above the county level in accordance with the Drug Administration Law and relevant regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本办法自发布之日起施行。

**Article 27** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
