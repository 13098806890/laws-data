# 优化营商环境条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2019-10-22  
**生效日期**：2020-01-01

> (2019年10月8日国务院第66次常务会议通过　2019年10月22日中华人民共和国国务院令第722号公布　自2020年1月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了持续优化营商环境，不断解放和发展社会生产力，加快建设现代化经济体系，推动高质量发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称营商环境，是指企业等市场主体在市场经济活动中所涉及的体制机制性因素和条件。 第三条　国家持续深化简政放权、放管结合、优化服务改革，最大限度减少政府对市场资源的直接配置，最大限度减少政府对市场活动的直接干预，加强和规范事中事后监管，着力提升政务服务能力和水平，切实降低制度性交易成本，更大激发市场活力和社会创造力，增强发展动力。 各级人民政府及其部门应当坚持政务公开透明，以公开为常态、不公开为例外，全面推进决策、执行、管理、服务、结果公开。 第四条　优化营商环境应当坚持市场化、法治化、国际化原则，以市场主体需求为导向，以深刻转变政府职能为核心，创新体制机制、强化协同联动、完善法治保障，对标国际先进水平，为各类市场主体投资兴业营造稳定、公平、透明、可预期的良好环境。 第五条　国家加快建立统一开放、竞争有序的现代市场体系，依法促进各类生产要素自由流动，保障各类市场主体公平参与市场竞争。 第六条　国家鼓励、支持、引导非公有制经济发展，激发非公有制经济活力和创造力。 国家进一步扩大对外开放，积极促进外商投资，平等对待内资企业、外商投资企业等各类市场主体。 第七条　各级人民政府应当加强对优化营商环境工作的组织领导，完善优化营商环境的政策措施，建立健全统筹推进、督促落实优化营商环境工作的相关机制，及时协调、解决优化营商环境工作中的重大问题。 县级以上人民政府有关部门应当按照职责分工，做好优化营商环境的相关工作。县级以上地方人民政府根据实际情况，可以明确优化营商环境工作的主管部门。 国家鼓励和支持各地区、各部门结合实际情况，在法治框架内积极探索原创性、差异化的优化营商环境具体措施；对探索中出现失误或者偏差，符合规定条件的，可以予以免责或者减轻责任。 第八条　国家建立和完善以市场主体和社会公众满意度为导向的营商环境评价体系，发挥营商环境评价对优化营商环境的引领和督促作用。 开展营商环境评价，不得影响各地区、各部门正常工作，不得影响市场主体正常生产经营活动或者增加市场主体负担。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了持续优化营商环境，不断解放和发展社会生产力，加快建设现代化经济体系，推动高质量发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of continuously optimizing the business environment, continuously liberating and developing social productive forces, accelerating the building of a modernized economy, and promoting high-quality development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称营商环境，是指企业等市场主体在市场经济活动中所涉及的体制机制性因素和条件。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "business environment" refers to the institutional mechanisms and conditions involved in the market economic activities of market entities such as enterprises.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家持续深化简政放权、放管结合、优化服务改革，最大限度减少政府对市场资源的直接配置，最大限度减少政府对市场活动的直接干预，加强和规范事中事后监管，着力提升政务服务能力和水平，切实降低制度性交易成本，更大激发市场活力和社会创造力，增强发展动力。  
各级人民政府及其部门应当坚持政务公开透明，以公开为常态、不公开为例外，全面推进决策、执行、管理、服务、结果公开。

**Article 3** The State shall continuously deepen the reforms of streamlining administration, delegating power, combining decentralization with effective regulation, and optimizing services, minimize the direct allocation of market resources by the government, minimize the direct intervention of the government in market activities, strengthen and standardize oversight during and after the process, enhance the capacity and level of government services, effectively reduce institutional transaction costs, and further stimulate market vitality and social creativity, and strengthen development momentum.  
People's governments at all levels and their departments shall adhere to openness and transparency in government affairs, taking openness as the norm and non-disclosure as the exception, and comprehensively promote the disclosure of decision-making, implementation, management, service, and results.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　优化营商环境应当坚持市场化、法治化、国际化原则，以市场主体需求为导向，以深刻转变政府职能为核心，创新体制机制、强化协同联动、完善法治保障，对标国际先进水平，为各类市场主体投资兴业营造稳定、公平、透明、可预期的良好环境。

**Article 4** Optimizing the business environment shall adhere to the principles of marketization, rule of law, and internationalization, be guided by the needs of market entities, take the profound transformation of government functions as the core, innovate institutional mechanisms, strengthen coordination and linkage, improve legal safeguards, and benchmark against international advanced levels, to create a stable, fair, transparent, and predictable good environment for various market entities to invest and do business.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家加快建立统一开放、竞争有序的现代市场体系，依法促进各类生产要素自由流动，保障各类市场主体公平参与市场竞争。

**Article 5** The State shall accelerate the establishment of a unified, open, competitive, and orderly modern market system, promote the free flow of various production factors according to law, and ensure that various market entities participate fairly in market competition.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励、支持、引导非公有制经济发展，激发非公有制经济活力和创造力。  
国家进一步扩大对外开放，积极促进外商投资，平等对待内资企业、外商投资企业等各类市场主体。

**Article 6** The State shall encourage, support, and guide the development of the non-public sector of the economy, and stimulate the vitality and creativity of the non-public sector of the economy.  
The State shall further expand opening up, actively promote foreign investment, and treat domestic-funded enterprises, foreign-invested enterprises, and other market entities equally.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级人民政府应当加强对优化营商环境工作的组织领导，完善优化营商环境的政策措施，建立健全统筹推进、督促落实优化营商环境工作的相关机制，及时协调、解决优化营商环境工作中的重大问题。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门应当按照职责分工，做好优化营商环境的相关工作。县级以上地方人民政府根据实际情况，可以明确优化营商环境工作的主管部门。  
国家鼓励和支持各地区、各部门结合实际情况，在法治框架内积极探索原创性、差异化的优化营商环境具体措施；对探索中出现失误或者偏差，符合规定条件的，可以予以免责或者减轻责任。

**Article 7** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the organization and leadership of the work on optimizing the business environment, improve policies and measures for optimizing the business environment, establish and improve the relevant mechanisms for overall planning, promotion, and supervision of the implementation of the work on optimizing the business environment, and timely coordinate and resolve major issues in the work of optimizing the business environment.  
The relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and division of responsibilities, perform the relevant work on optimizing the business environment. Local people's governments at or above the county level may, based on actual conditions, designate the competent departments for the work of optimizing the business environment.  
The State shall encourage and support all regions and departments, in light of actual conditions, to actively explore original and differentiated specific measures for optimizing the business environment within the legal framework; if errors or deviations occur during the exploration and meet the prescribed conditions, exemption from liability or reduction of liability may be granted.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家建立和完善以市场主体和社会公众满意度为导向的营商环境评价体系，发挥营商环境评价对优化营商环境的引领和督促作用。  
开展营商环境评价，不得影响各地区、各部门正常工作，不得影响市场主体正常生产经营活动或者增加市场主体负担。  
任何单位不得利用营商环境评价谋取利益。

**Article 8** The State shall establish and improve a business environment evaluation system oriented by the satisfaction of market entities and the public, and give full play to the leading and supervisory role of business environment evaluation in optimizing the business environment.  
When conducting business environment evaluations, it shall not affect the normal work of various regions and departments, nor affect the normal production and operation activities of market entities or increase the burden on market entities.  
No unit shall use business environment evaluations to seek benefits.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　市场主体应当遵守法律法规，恪守社会公德和商业道德，诚实守信、公平竞争，履行安全、质量、劳动者权益保护、消费者权益保护等方面的法定义务，在国际经贸活动中遵循国际通行规则。

**Article 9** Market entities shall abide by laws and regulations, observe social ethics and business ethics, act in good faith and engage in fair competition, fulfill statutory obligations in terms of safety, quality, protection of workers' rights and interests, protection of consumers' rights and interests, and follow internationally accepted rules in international economic and trade activities.

### 第二章　市场主体保护

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家坚持权利平等、机会平等、规则平等，保障各种所有制经济平等受到法律保护。

**Article 10** The State shall adhere to equality of rights, equality of opportunities, and equality of rules, and ensure that all forms of ownership economies are equally protected by law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　市场主体依法享有经营自主权。对依法应当由市场主体自主决策的各类事项，任何单位和个人不得干预。

**Article 11** Market entities shall, according to law, enjoy autonomy in business operations. No entity or individual may interfere with any matters that shall be independently decided by market entities according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家保障各类市场主体依法平等使用资金、技术、人力资源、土地使用权及其他自然资源等各类生产要素和公共服务资源。  
各类市场主体依法平等适用国家支持发展的政策。政府及其有关部门在政府资金安排、土地供应、税费减免、资质许可、标准制定、项目申报、职称评定、人力资源政策等方面，应当依法平等对待各类市场主体，不得制定或者实施歧视性政策措施。

**Article 12** The State shall ensure that all types of market entities have equal access, according to law, to various factors of production such as capital, technology, human resources, land use rights and other natural resources, and to public service resources.  
All types of market entities shall equally enjoy, according to law, the policies of the State supporting development. The government and its relevant departments shall, according to law, treat all types of market entities equally in terms of government fund arrangement, land supply, tax and fee reduction or exemption, qualification licensing, standard formulation, project application, professional title evaluation, human resources policies, etc., and shall not formulate or implement discriminatory policies or measures.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　招标投标和政府采购应当公开透明、公平公正，依法平等对待各类所有制和不同地区的市场主体，不得以不合理条件或者产品产地来源等进行限制或者排斥。  
政府有关部门应当加强招标投标和政府采购监管，依法纠正和查处违法违规行为。

**Article 13** Tender and bidding and government procurement shall be open, transparent, fair and just, and shall, according to law, equally treat market entities of all forms of ownership and from different regions, and shall not restrict or exclude them by imposing unreasonable conditions or on the basis of product origin, etc.  
The relevant government departments shall strengthen the supervision of tender and bidding and government procurement, and shall, according to law, rectify and investigate illegal or irregular acts.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家依法保护市场主体的财产权和其他合法权益，保护企业经营者人身和财产安全。  
严禁违反法定权限、条件、程序对市场主体的财产和企业经营者个人财产实施查封、冻结和扣押等行政强制措施；依法确需实施前述行政强制措施的，应当限定在所必需的范围内。  
禁止在法律、法规规定之外要求市场主体提供财力、物力或者人力的摊派行为。市场主体有权拒绝任何形式的摊派。

**Article 14** The State shall, according to law, protect the property rights and other lawful rights and interests of market entities, and protect the personal safety and property safety of business operators.  
It is strictly prohibited to take administrative compulsory measures such as sealing up, freezing or seizing the property of market entities and the personal property of business operators in violation of the statutory authority, conditions or procedures; if the aforesaid administrative compulsory measures are truly necessary according to law, they shall be limited to the necessary scope.  
It is prohibited to require market entities to provide financial, material or human resources in the form of apportionment beyond the provisions of laws and regulations. Market entities shall have the right to refuse any form of apportionment.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家建立知识产权侵权惩罚性赔偿制度，推动建立知识产权快速协同保护机制，健全知识产权纠纷多元化解决机制和知识产权维权援助机制，加大对知识产权的保护力度。  
国家持续深化商标注册、专利申请便利化改革，提高商标注册、专利申请审查效率。

**Article 15** The State shall establish a system of punitive damages for intellectual property infringement, promote the establishment of a fast and collaborative protection mechanism for intellectual property, improve the diversified dispute resolution mechanism for intellectual property and the aid mechanism for intellectual property rights protection, and strengthen the protection of intellectual property.  
The State shall continuously deepen the reform to facilitate trademark registration and patent application, and enhance the efficiency of examination of trademark registration and patent application.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家加大中小投资者权益保护力度，完善中小投资者权益保护机制，保障中小投资者的知情权、参与权，提升中小投资者维护合法权益的便利度。

**Article 16** The State shall increase the protection of the rights and interests of small and medium-sized investors, improve the mechanism for protecting the rights and interests of small and medium-sized investors, ensure the right to know and the right to participate of small and medium-sized investors, and enhance the convenience for small and medium-sized investors to safeguard their lawful rights and interests.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　除法律、法规另有规定外，市场主体有权自主决定加入或者退出行业协会商会等社会组织，任何单位和个人不得干预。  
除法律、法规另有规定外，任何单位和个人不得强制或者变相强制市场主体参加评比、达标、表彰、培训、考核、考试以及类似活动，不得借前述活动向市场主体收费或者变相收费。

**Article 17** Unless otherwise provided by laws and regulations, a market entity shall have the right to decide independently whether to join or withdraw from social organizations such as industry associations and chambers of commerce, and no entity or individual may interfere.  
Unless otherwise provided by laws and regulations, no entity or individual may compel or force a market entity in disguised form to participate in appraisals, standard-reaching activities, commendations, training, assessments, examinations or similar activities, and may not charge fees or disguised fees to a market entity in connection with the aforesaid activities.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家推动建立全国统一的市场主体维权服务平台，为市场主体提供高效、便捷的维权服务。

**Article 18** The State shall promote the establishment of a unified national rights protection service platform for market entities, providing efficient and convenient rights protection services for market entities.

### 第三章　市场环境

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家持续深化商事制度改革，统一企业登记业务规范，统一数据标准和平台服务接口，采用统一社会信用代码进行登记管理。  
国家推进“证照分离”改革，持续精简涉企经营许可事项，依法采取直接取消审批、审批改为备案、实行告知承诺、优化审批服务等方式，对所有涉企经营许可事项进行分类管理，为企业取得营业执照后开展相关经营活动提供便利。除法律、行政法规规定的特定领域外，涉企经营许可事项不得作为企业登记的前置条件。  
政府有关部门应当按照国家有关规定，简化企业从申请设立到具备一般性经营条件所需办理的手续。在国家规定的企业开办时限内，各地区应当确定并公开具体办理时间。  
企业申请办理住所等相关变更登记的，有关部门应当依法及时办理，不得限制。除法律、法规、规章另有规定外，企业迁移后其持有的有效许可证件不再重复办理。

**Article 19** The State shall continuously deepen the reform of the commercial system, unify the business rules for enterprise registration, unify data standards and platform service interfaces, and adopt unified social credit codes for registration management.  
The State shall promote the reform of "separation of permits and licenses", continuously streamline matters involving business operation permits, and adopt methods such as direct cancellation of approval, changing approval to filing, implementing notification-commitment, and optimizing approval services, to carry out classified management of all matters involving business operation permits, so as to facilitate enterprises in conducting relevant business activities after obtaining business licenses. Except for specific fields provided for by laws and administrative regulations, matters involving business operation permits shall not be a prerequisite for enterprise registration.  
The relevant government departments shall, in accordance with relevant state provisions, simplify the procedures required for enterprises from application for establishment to meeting general operational conditions. Within the time limit set by the State for enterprise establishment, each region shall determine and make public the specific processing time.  
Where an enterprise applies for registration of changes such as its domicile, the relevant department shall, according to law, handle it promptly and shall not restrict it. Unless otherwise provided by laws, regulations or rules, the valid licenses held by an enterprise after relocation shall not be re-processed.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家持续放宽市场准入，并实行全国统一的市场准入负面清单制度。市场准入负面清单以外的领域，各类市场主体均可以依法平等进入。  
各地区、各部门不得另行制定市场准入性质的负面清单。

**Article 20** The State shall continuously relax market access, and implement a unified national negative list system for market access. All types of market entities may, according to law, equally enter fields other than those on the negative list for market access.  
No region or department may separately formulate a negative list of a market access nature.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　政府有关部门应当加大反垄断和反不正当竞争执法力度，有效预防和制止市场经济活动中的垄断行为、不正当竞争行为以及滥用行政权力排除、限制竞争的行为，营造公平竞争的市场环境。

**Article 21** Government departments shall intensify law enforcement against monopolies and unfair competition, and effectively prevent and stop monopolistic conduct, unfair competition practices, and acts of abusing administrative power to exclude or restrict competition in market economic activities, so as to create a fair and competitive market environment.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家建立健全统一开放、竞争有序的人力资源市场体系，打破城乡、地区、行业分割和身份、性别等歧视，促进人力资源有序社会性流动和合理配置。

**Article 22** The State shall establish and improve a unified, open, competitive and orderly human resources market system, break down urban-rural, regional, and industry segmentation and discrimination based on status, gender, etc., and promote orderly social mobility and rational allocation of human resources.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　政府及其有关部门应当完善政策措施、强化创新服务，鼓励和支持市场主体拓展创新空间，持续推进产品、技术、商业模式、管理等创新，充分发挥市场主体在推动科技成果转化中的作用。

**Article 23** The government and its relevant departments shall improve policies and measures, strengthen innovation services, encourage and support market entities to expand innovation space, continuously promote innovation in products, technologies, business models, management, etc., and give full play to the role of market entities in promoting the transformation of scientific and technological achievements.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　政府及其有关部门应当严格落实国家各项减税降费政策，及时研究解决政策落实中的具体问题，确保减税降费政策全面、及时惠及市场主体。

**Article 24** The government and its relevant departments shall strictly implement the national policies on tax and fee reductions, promptly study and solve specific problems in the implementation of such policies, and ensure that tax and fee reduction policies benefit market entities comprehensively and in a timely manner.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　设立政府性基金、涉企行政事业性收费、涉企保证金，应当有法律、行政法规依据或者经国务院批准。对政府性基金、涉企行政事业性收费、涉企保证金以及实行政府定价的经营服务性收费，实行目录清单管理并向社会公开，目录清单之外的前述收费和保证金一律不得执行。推广以金融机构保函替代现金缴纳涉企保证金。

**Article 25** The establishment of government-managed funds, administrative and institutional fees involving enterprises, and deposits involving enterprises shall have a basis in laws or administrative regulations or be approved by the State Council. For government-managed funds, administrative and institutional fees involving enterprises, deposits involving enterprises, and operating service fees subject to government pricing, a catalog list management system shall be adopted and made public to society. No such fees or deposits outside the catalog list may be charged. The substitution of cash deposits involving enterprises with financial institution guarantees shall be promoted.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家鼓励和支持金融机构加大对民营企业、中小企业的支持力度，降低民营企业、中小企业综合融资成本。  
金融监督管理部门应当完善对商业银行等金融机构的监管考核和激励机制，鼓励、引导其增加对民营企业、中小企业的信贷投放，并合理增加中长期贷款和信用贷款支持，提高贷款审批效率。  
商业银行等金融机构在授信中不得设置不合理条件，不得对民营企业、中小企业设置歧视性要求。商业银行等金融机构应当按照国家有关规定规范收费行为，不得违规向服务对象收取不合理费用。商业银行应当向社会公开开设企业账户的服务标准、资费标准和办理时限。

**Article 26** The State encourages and supports financial institutions to increase support for private enterprises and small and medium-sized enterprises, and reduce the overall financing costs for private enterprises and small and medium-sized enterprises.  
  
The financial supervision and administration department shall improve the supervision, assessment and incentive mechanisms for commercial banks and other financial institutions, encourage and guide them to increase credit supply to private enterprises and small and medium-sized enterprises, and reasonably increase support for medium- and long-term loans and credit loans, and improve the efficiency of loan approval.  
  
Commercial banks and other financial institutions shall not set unreasonable conditions in credit granting, nor impose discriminatory requirements on private enterprises and small and medium-sized enterprises. Commercial banks and other financial institutions shall standardize their fee-charging practices in accordance with relevant state regulations, and shall not illegally charge unreasonable fees from service targets. Commercial banks shall publicly disclose the service standards, fee standards and processing time limits for opening corporate accounts.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家促进多层次资本市场规范健康发展，拓宽市场主体融资渠道，支持符合条件的民营企业、中小企业依法发行股票、债券以及其他融资工具，扩大直接融资规模。

**Article 27** The State promotes the standardized and healthy development of multi-tiered capital markets, expands financing channels for market entities, and supports eligible private enterprises and small and medium-sized enterprises to issue stocks, bonds, and other financing instruments according to law, so as to expand the scale of direct financing.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　供水、供电、供气、供热等公用企事业单位应当向社会公开服务标准、资费标准等信息，为市场主体提供安全、便捷、稳定和价格合理的服务，不得强迫市场主体接受不合理的服务条件，不得以任何名义收取不合理费用。各地区应当优化报装流程，在国家规定的报装办理时限内确定并公开具体办理时间。  
政府有关部门应当加强对公用企事业单位运营的监督管理。

**Article 28** Public utility enterprises and institutions such as water supply, power supply, gas supply, and heat supply shall publicly disclose information such as service standards and fee standards to the public, provide safe, convenient, stable, and reasonably priced services to market entities, shall not force market entities to accept unreasonable service conditions, and shall not charge unreasonable fees in any name. All regions shall optimize the application and installation procedures, and determine and publicly disclose the specific processing time within the time limit specified by the State for processing such applications.  
  
Government departments concerned shall strengthen the supervision and management of the operations of public utility enterprises and institutions.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　行业协会商会应当依照法律、法规和章程，加强行业自律，及时反映行业诉求，为市场主体提供信息咨询、宣传培训、市场拓展、权益保护、纠纷处理等方面的服务。  
国家依法严格规范行业协会商会的收费、评比、认证等行为。

**Article 29** Industry associations and chambers of commerce shall, in accordance with laws, regulations and their articles of association, strengthen industry self-discipline, reflect industry demands in a timely manner, and provide services such as information consulting, publicity and training, market expansion, rights protection, and dispute resolution for market entities.  
  
The State shall, according to law, strictly regulate the fee-charging, evaluation, certification and other activities of industry associations and chambers of commerce.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家加强社会信用体系建设，持续推进政务诚信、商务诚信、社会诚信和司法公信建设，提高全社会诚信意识和信用水平，维护信用信息安全，严格保护商业秘密和个人隐私。

**Article 30** The State strengthens the construction of a social credit system, continuously promotes the construction of government integrity, commercial integrity, social integrity, and judicial credibility, improves the integrity awareness and credit level of the whole society, safeguards the security of credit information, and strictly protects trade secrets and personal privacy.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当履行向市场主体依法作出的政策承诺以及依法订立的各类合同，不得以行政区划调整、政府换届、机构或者职能调整以及相关责任人更替等为由违约毁约。因国家利益、社会公共利益需要改变政策承诺、合同约定的，应当依照法定权限和程序进行，并依法对市场主体因此受到的损失予以补偿。

**Article 31** Local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall fulfill the policy commitments made to market players according to law and all kinds of contracts concluded according to law, and shall not breach contracts or renege on commitments on the grounds of adjustment of administrative divisions, change of government leadership, adjustment of institutions or functions, or replacement of relevant responsible persons. If it is necessary to change policy commitments or contractual terms due to the needs of national interests or public interests, such changes shall be made in accordance with the statutory authority and procedures, and the losses suffered by market players as a result shall be compensated according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家机关、事业单位不得违约拖欠市场主体的货物、工程、服务等账款，大型企业不得利用优势地位拖欠中小企业账款。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当加大对国家机关、事业单位拖欠市场主体账款的清理力度，并通过加强预算管理、严格责任追究等措施，建立防范和治理国家机关、事业单位拖欠市场主体账款的长效机制。

**Article 32** State organs and public institutions shall not default on payment of goods, construction projects, services, and other accounts payable to market players, and large enterprises shall not use their advantageous position to default on payment to small and medium-sized enterprises.  
People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall intensify the clearance of accounts owed by state organs and public institutions to market players, and establish a long-term mechanism for preventing and controlling such arrears through measures such as strengthening budget management and strictly pursuing accountability.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　政府有关部门应当优化市场主体注销办理流程，精简申请材料、压缩办理时间、降低注销成本。对设立后未开展生产经营活动或者无债权债务的市场主体，可以按照简易程序办理注销。对有债权债务的市场主体，在债权债务依法解决后及时办理注销。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当根据需要建立企业破产工作协调机制，协调解决企业破产过程中涉及的有关问题。

**Article 33** Relevant government departments shall optimize the deregistration procedures for market players, streamline application materials, shorten processing time, and reduce deregistration costs. For market players that have not engaged in production or business activities after establishment or have no claims or debts, deregistration may be handled through simplified procedures. For market players with claims or debts, deregistration shall be handled promptly after the claims and debts are resolved according to law.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, as needed, establish a coordination mechanism for enterprise bankruptcy work to coordinate and resolve issues involved in the bankruptcy process of enterprises.

### 第四章　政务服务

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　政府及其有关部门应当进一步增强服务意识，切实转变工作作风，为市场主体提供规范、便利、高效的政务服务。

**Article 34** The government and its relevant departments shall further enhance their service awareness, effectively transform their work style, and provide standardized, convenient, and efficient government services to market players.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　政府及其有关部门应当推进政务服务标准化，按照减环节、减材料、减时限的要求，编制并向社会公开政务服务事项(包括行政权力事项和公共服务事项，下同)标准化工作流程和办事指南，细化量化政务服务标准，压缩自由裁量权，推进同一事项实行无差别受理、同标准办理。没有法律、法规、规章依据，不得增设政务服务事项的办理条件和环节。

**Article 35** The government and its relevant departments shall promote the standardization of government services, compile and disclose to the public standardized workflows and service guides for government service items (including administrative power items and public service items, the same below) in accordance with the requirements of reducing steps, materials, and time limits, refine and quantify government service standards, reduce discretionary powers, and promote non-differentiated acceptance and same-standard processing of the same item. Without the basis of laws, regulations, or rules, no additional conditions or steps for processing government service items shall be added.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　政府及其有关部门办理政务服务事项，应当根据实际情况，推行当场办结、一次办结、限时办结等制度，实现集中办理、就近办理、网上办理、异地可办。需要市场主体补正有关材料、手续的，应当一次性告知需要补正的内容；需要进行现场踏勘、现场核查、技术审查、听证论证的，应当及时安排、限时办结。  
法律、法规、规章以及国家有关规定对政务服务事项办理时限有规定的，应当在规定的时限内尽快办结；没有规定的，应当按照合理、高效的原则确定办理时限并按时办结。各地区可以在国家规定的政务服务事项办理时限内进一步压减时间，并应当向社会公开；超过办理时间的，办理单位应当公开说明理由。  
地方各级人民政府已设立政务服务大厅的，本行政区域内各类政务服务事项一般应当进驻政务服务大厅统一办理。对政务服务大厅中部门分设的服务窗口，应当创造条件整合为综合窗口，提供一站式服务。

**Article 36** When handling government service items, the government and its relevant departments shall, based on actual circumstances, implement systems such as on-site completion, one-time completion, and time-limited completion, and achieve centralized processing, nearby processing, online processing, and remote processing. If market players need to supplement relevant materials or procedures, the required supplements shall be informed in one go; if on-site surveys, on-site inspections, technical reviews, or hearing arguments are required, they shall be arranged promptly and completed within a time limit.  
If laws, regulations, rules, or relevant state provisions have specified the time limit for handling government service items, the processing shall be completed as soon as possible within the specified time limit; if there is no such provision, the time limit shall be determined in accordance with the principles of reasonableness and efficiency, and the processing shall be completed on time. All regions may further shorten the time limit within the time limit specified by the state for handling government service items, and shall disclose such shortened time limit to the public; if the processing exceeds the time limit, the processing unit shall publicly explain the reasons.  
If a local people's government at any level has established a government service hall, all types of government service items within its administrative region shall generally be processed in the government service hall in a unified manner. For service windows set up separately by departments in the government service hall, conditions shall be created to integrate them into comprehensive windows to provide one-stop services.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家加快建设全国一体化在线政务服务平台(以下称一体化在线平台)，推动政务服务事项在全国范围内实现“一网通办”。除法律、法规另有规定或者涉及国家秘密等情形外，政务服务事项应当按照国务院确定的步骤，纳入一体化在线平台办理。  
国家依托一体化在线平台，推动政务信息系统整合，优化政务流程，促进政务服务跨地区、跨部门、跨层级数据共享和业务协同。政府及其有关部门应当按照国家有关规定，提供数据共享服务，及时将有关政务服务数据上传至一体化在线平台，加强共享数据使用全过程管理，确保共享数据安全。  
国家建立电子证照共享服务系统，实现电子证照跨地区、跨部门共享和全国范围内互信互认。各地区、各部门应当加强电子证照的推广应用。  
各地区、各部门应当推动政务服务大厅与政务服务平台全面对接融合。市场主体有权自主选择政务服务办理渠道，行政机关不得限定办理渠道。

**Article 37** The State shall accelerate the construction of a nationwide integrated online government service platform (hereinafter referred to as the integrated online platform), and promote the realization of "one-network processing" for government service items across the country. Unless otherwise provided by laws or regulations or involving state secrets, government service items shall be processed through the integrated online platform in accordance with the steps determined by the State Council.  
The State shall rely on the integrated online platform to promote the integration of government information systems, optimize government procedures, and facilitate cross-regional, cross-departmental, and cross-level data sharing and business collaboration in government services. The government and its relevant departments shall, in accordance with relevant state provisions, provide data sharing services, upload relevant government service data to the integrated online platform in a timely manner, strengthen the management of the entire process of shared data use, and ensure the security of shared data.  
The State shall establish an electronic certificate sharing service system to achieve cross-regional and cross-departmental sharing of electronic certificates and mutual trust and recognition nationwide. All regions and departments shall strengthen the promotion and application of electronic certificates.  
All regions and departments shall promote the full integration of government service halls with government service platforms. Market players have the right to independently choose the channel for processing government services, and administrative organs shall not restrict the processing channels.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　政府及其有关部门应当通过政府网站、一体化在线平台，集中公布涉及市场主体的法律、法规、规章、行政规范性文件和各类政策措施，并通过多种途径和方式加强宣传解读。

**Article 38** The government and its relevant departments shall, through government websites and the integrated online platform, centrally publish laws, regulations, rules, administrative normative documents, and various policies and measures relating to market players, and strengthen publicity and interpretation through multiple channels and methods.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家严格控制新设行政许可。新设行政许可应当按照行政许可法和国务院的规定严格设定标准，并进行合法性、必要性和合理性审查论证。对通过事中事后监管或者市场机制能够解决以及行政许可法和国务院规定不得设立行政许可的事项，一律不得设立行政许可，严禁以备案、登记、注册、目录、规划、年检、年报、监制、认定、认证、审定以及其他任何形式变相设定或者实施行政许可。  
法律、行政法规和国务院决定对相关管理事项已作出规定，但未采取行政许可管理方式的，地方不得就该事项设定行政许可。对相关管理事项尚未制定法律、行政法规的，地方可以依法就该事项设定行政许可。

**Article 39** The State strictly controls the establishment of new administrative licenses. The establishment of new administrative licenses shall strictly follow the standards prescribed by the Administrative License Law and the State Council, and undergo review and demonstration of legality, necessity, and rationality. For matters that can be resolved through interim and ex-post supervision or market mechanisms, or matters for which administrative licenses shall not be established under the Administrative License Law and the State Council's provisions, no administrative licenses shall be established. It is strictly prohibited to set up or implement administrative licenses in disguised forms such as filing, registration, recordation, catalog, planning, annual inspection, annual report, supervision, recognition, certification, examination and approval, or any other form.  
Where laws, administrative regulations, or decisions of the State Council have already made provisions on relevant management matters but have not adopted the form of administrative license management, local authorities shall not establish administrative licenses for such matters. Where no laws or administrative regulations have been enacted for relevant management matters, local authorities may, according to law, establish administrative licenses for such matters.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家实行行政许可清单管理制度，适时调整行政许可清单并向社会公布，清单之外不得违法实施行政许可。  
国家大力精简已有行政许可。对已取消的行政许可，行政机关不得继续实施或者变相实施，不得转由行业协会商会或者其他组织实施。  
对实行行政许可管理的事项，行政机关应当通过整合实施、下放审批层级等多种方式，优化审批服务，提高审批效率，减轻市场主体负担。符合相关条件和要求的，可以按照有关规定采取告知承诺的方式办理。

**Article 40** The State implements a list management system for administrative licenses, adjusts the list of administrative licenses at appropriate times and publishes it to the public, and no administrative license shall be implemented illegally outside the list.  
The State vigorously streamlines existing administrative licenses. For administrative licenses that have been abolished, administrative organs shall not continue to implement or implement them in disguised form, and shall not transfer them to industry associations, chambers of commerce, or other organizations for implementation.  
For matters subject to administrative license management, administrative organs shall optimize approval services, improve approval efficiency, and reduce the burden on market players through various methods such as integrated implementation and delegation of approval authority. Where conditions and requirements are met, the matter may be handled by adopting the commitment-based approach in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　县级以上地方人民政府应当深化投资审批制度改革，根据项目性质、投资规模等分类规范投资审批程序，精简审批要件，简化技术审查事项，强化项目决策与用地、规划等建设条件落实的协同，实行与相关审批在线并联办理。

**Article 41** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall deepen the reform of the investment approval system, classify and standardize investment approval procedures according to the nature of the project and the scale of investment, streamline approval requirements, simplify technical review items, strengthen coordination between project decision-making and the implementation of construction conditions such as land use and planning, and implement online parallel processing with relevant approvals.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　设区的市级以上地方人民政府应当按照国家有关规定，优化工程建设项目(不包括特殊工程和交通、水利、能源等领域的重大工程)审批流程，推行并联审批、多图联审、联合竣工验收等方式，简化审批手续，提高审批效能。  
在依法设立的开发区、新区和其他有条件的区域，按照国家有关规定推行区域评估，由设区的市级以上地方人民政府组织对一定区域内压覆重要矿产资源、地质灾害危险性等事项进行统一评估，不再对区域内的市场主体单独提出评估要求。区域评估的费用不得由市场主体承担。

**Article 42** Local people's governments at or above the city with districts level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, optimize the approval process for engineering construction projects (excluding special projects and major projects in the fields of transportation, water conservancy, energy, etc.), promote parallel approval, multi-drawing joint review, joint completion acceptance, etc., simplify approval procedures, and improve approval efficiency.  
In development zones, new areas and other qualified areas established according to law, regional assessment shall be promoted in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. The local people's government at or above the city with districts level shall organize a unified assessment of matters such as the pressure on important mineral resources and the risk of geological disasters in a certain area, and shall no longer put forward separate assessment requirements for market entities within the area. The cost of regional assessment shall not be borne by market entities.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　作为办理行政审批条件的中介服务事项(以下称法定行政审批中介服务)应当有法律、法规或者国务院决定依据；没有依据的，不得作为办理行政审批的条件。中介服务机构应当明确办理法定行政审批中介服务的条件、流程、时限、收费标准，并向社会公开。  
国家加快推进中介服务机构与行政机关脱钩。行政机关不得为市场主体指定或者变相指定中介服务机构；除法定行政审批中介服务外，不得强制或者变相强制市场主体接受中介服务。行政机关所属事业单位、主管的社会组织及其举办的企业不得开展与本机关所负责行政审批相关的中介服务，法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。  
行政机关在行政审批过程中需要委托中介服务机构开展技术性服务的，应当通过竞争性方式选择中介服务机构，并自行承担服务费用，不得转嫁给市场主体承担。

**Article 43** Intermediary service matters that serve as conditions for administrative examination and approval (hereinafter referred to as statutory administrative examination and approval intermediary services) shall be based on laws, regulations or decisions of the State Council; without such basis, they shall not be used as conditions for administrative examination and approval. Intermediary service institutions shall specify the conditions, procedures, time limits and charging standards for handling statutory administrative examination and approval intermediary services, and make them public.  
The State shall accelerate the decoupling of intermediary service institutions from administrative organs. Administrative organs shall not designate or implicitly designate intermediary service institutions for market entities; except for statutory administrative examination and approval intermediary services, they shall not force or implicitly force market entities to accept intermediary services. The institutions directly under administrative organs, social organizations under their supervision and the enterprises established by them shall not carry out intermediary services related to the administrative examination and approval for which the organ is responsible, unless otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations.  
Where administrative organs need to entrust intermediary service institutions to carry out technical services in the process of administrative examination and approval, they shall select the intermediary service institution by competitive means, and shall bear the service costs themselves, and shall not shift them to market entities.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　证明事项应当有法律、法规或者国务院决定依据。  
设定证明事项，应当坚持确有必要、从严控制的原则。对通过法定证照、法定文书、书面告知承诺、政府部门内部核查和部门间核查、网络核验、合同凭证等能够办理，能够被其他材料涵盖或者替代，以及开具单位无法调查核实的，不得设定证明事项。  
政府有关部门应当公布证明事项清单，逐项列明设定依据、索要单位、开具单位、办理指南等。清单之外，政府部门、公用企事业单位和服务机构不得索要证明。各地区、各部门之间应当加强证明的互认共享，避免重复索要证明。

**Article 44** Certification matters shall be based on laws, regulations or decisions of the State Council.  
When establishing certification matters, the principle of necessity and strict control shall be adhered to. Certification matters shall not be established for matters that can be handled through statutory certificates, statutory documents, written commitment notices, internal verification among government departments and inter-departmental verification, network verification, contract vouchers, etc., or that can be covered or replaced by other materials, or that cannot be investigated and verified by the issuing unit.  
Relevant government departments shall publish a list of certification matters, specifying the basis for establishment, the requesting unit, the issuing unit, the processing guide, etc. Matters outside the list, government departments, public utilities and service institutions shall not request certifications. All regions and departments shall strengthen mutual recognition and sharing of certifications to avoid repeated requests for certifications.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　政府及其有关部门应当按照国家促进跨境贸易便利化的有关要求，依法削减进出口环节审批事项，取消不必要的监管要求，优化简化通关流程，提高通关效率，清理规范口岸收费，降低通关成本，推动口岸和国际贸易领域相关业务统一通过国际贸易“单一窗口”办理。

**Article 45** The government and its relevant departments shall, in accordance with the relevant requirements of the State for promoting the facilitation of cross-border trade, reduce the examination and approval items in import and export links according to law, cancel unnecessary regulatory requirements, optimize and simplify customs clearance procedures, improve customs clearance efficiency, clean up and standardize port charges, reduce customs clearance costs, and promote the unified handling of port and international trade related businesses through the international trade "single window".

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　税务机关应当精简办税资料和流程，简并申报缴税次数，公开涉税事项办理时限，压减办税时间，加大推广使用电子发票的力度，逐步实现全程网上办税，持续优化纳税服务。

**Article 46** Tax authorities shall streamline tax handling materials and procedures, simplify the frequency of tax declaration and payment, disclose the time limit for handling tax-related matters, reduce tax handling time, increase the promotion and use of electronic invoices, gradually achieve full online tax handling, and continuously optimize tax payment services.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　不动产登记机构应当按照国家有关规定，加强部门协作，实行不动产登记、交易和缴税一窗受理、并行办理，压缩办理时间，降低办理成本。在国家规定的不动产登记时限内，各地区应当确定并公开具体办理时间。  
国家推动建立统一的动产和权利担保登记公示系统，逐步实现市场主体在一个平台上办理动产和权利担保登记。纳入统一登记公示系统的动产和权利范围另行规定。

**Article 47** The real estate registration authority shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, strengthen departmental coordination, implement one-window acceptance and parallel processing of real estate registration, transaction and tax payment, shorten processing time, and reduce processing costs. Within the time limit for real estate registration prescribed by the State, all regions shall determine and make public the specific processing time.  
The State shall promote the establishment of a unified registration and publicity system for chattel and rights guarantees, and gradually enable market entities to register chattel and rights guarantees on one platform. The scope of chattel and rights included in the unified registration and publicity system shall be separately provided.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　政府及其有关部门应当按照构建亲清新型政商关系的要求，建立畅通有效的政企沟通机制，采取多种方式及时听取市场主体的反映和诉求，了解市场主体生产经营中遇到的困难和问题，并依法帮助其解决。  
建立政企沟通机制，应当充分尊重市场主体意愿，增强针对性和有效性，不得干扰市场主体正常生产经营活动，不得增加市场主体负担。

**Article 48** The government and its relevant departments shall, in accordance with the requirements of building a new type of close and clean government-business relationship, establish a smooth and effective government-enterprise communication mechanism, adopt multiple methods to promptly listen to the reflections and demands of market entities, understand the difficulties and problems encountered by market entities in production and operation, and assist them in resolving them according to law.  
When establishing a government-enterprise communication mechanism, the wishes of market entities shall be fully respected, and the pertinence and effectiveness shall be enhanced. It shall not interfere with the normal production and operation activities of market entities, nor increase their burdens.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　政府及其有关部门应当建立便利、畅通的渠道，受理有关营商环境的投诉和举报。

**Article 49** The government and its relevant departments shall establish convenient and unimpeded channels to accept complaints and reports concerning the business environment.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　新闻媒体应当及时、准确宣传优化营商环境的措施和成效，为优化营商环境创造良好舆论氛围。  
国家鼓励对营商环境进行舆论监督，但禁止捏造虚假信息或者歪曲事实进行不实报道。

**Article 50** News media shall promptly and accurately publicize measures and achievements in optimizing the business environment, and create a good public opinion atmosphere for optimizing the business environment.  
The State encourages public opinion supervision over the business environment, but prohibits fabricating false information or distorting facts to make untrue reports.

### 第五章　监管执法

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　政府有关部门应当严格按照法律法规和职责，落实监管责任，明确监管对象和范围、厘清监管事权，依法对市场主体进行监管，实现监管全覆盖。

**Article 51** The relevant departments of the government shall strictly implement supervision responsibilities in accordance with laws, regulations and their functions, specify the objects and scope of supervision, clarify regulatory powers, and conduct supervision over market entities according to law to achieve full coverage of supervision.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家健全公开透明的监管规则和标准体系。国务院有关部门应当分领域制定全国统一、简明易行的监管规则和标准，并向社会公开。

**Article 52** The State shall improve the system of open and transparent regulatory rules and standards. The relevant departments of the State Council shall formulate nationally unified, concise and easy-to-implement regulatory rules and standards by sector, and make them public.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　政府及其有关部门应当按照国家关于加快构建以信用为基础的新型监管机制的要求，创新和完善信用监管，强化信用监管的支撑保障，加强信用监管的组织实施，不断提升信用监管效能。

**Article 53** The government and its relevant departments shall, in accordance with the requirements of the State on accelerating the construction of a new credit-based regulatory mechanism, innovate and improve credit supervision, strengthen the supporting guarantees for credit supervision, enhance the organization and implementation of credit supervision, and continuously improve the effectiveness of credit supervision.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家推行“双随机、一公开”监管，除直接涉及公共安全和人民群众生命健康等特殊行业、重点领域外，市场监管领域的行政检查应当通过随机抽取检查对象、随机选派执法检查人员、抽查事项及查处结果及时向社会公开的方式进行。针对同一检查对象的多个检查事项，应当尽可能合并或者纳入跨部门联合抽查范围。  
对直接涉及公共安全和人民群众生命健康等特殊行业、重点领域，依法依规实行全覆盖的重点监管，并严格规范重点监管的程序；对通过投诉举报、转办交办、数据监测等发现的问题，应当有针对性地进行检查并依法依规处理。

**Article 54** The State shall implement the "dual random, one disclosure" supervision. Except for special industries and key fields directly involving public safety and the people's life and health, administrative inspections in the field of market regulation shall be conducted by randomly selecting inspection targets, randomly assigning law enforcement inspection personnel, and promptly disclosing the inspection items and the results of investigation and handling to the public. For multiple inspection items targeting the same inspection object, they shall, as far as possible, be merged or included in the scope of cross-department joint spot checks.  
For special industries and key fields directly involving public safety and the people's life and health, full-coverage focused supervision shall be carried out in accordance with laws and regulations, and the procedures of focused supervision shall be strictly regulated; for problems discovered through complaints and reports, transferred assignments, data monitoring, etc., targeted inspections shall be conducted and handled in accordance with laws and regulations.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　政府及其有关部门应当按照鼓励创新的原则，对新技术、新产业、新业态、新模式等实行包容审慎监管，针对其性质、特点分类制定和实行相应的监管规则和标准，留足发展空间，同时确保质量和安全，不得简单化予以禁止或者不予监管。

**Article 55** The government and its relevant departments shall, in accordance with the principle of encouraging innovation, exercise inclusive and prudent supervision over new technologies, new industries, new business forms, and new models, formulate and implement corresponding regulatory rules and standards by category based on their nature and characteristics, leaving sufficient room for development, while ensuring quality and safety, and shall not simply prohibit or refrain from supervising them.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　政府及其有关部门应当充分运用互联网、大数据等技术手段，依托国家统一建立的在线监管系统，加强监管信息归集共享和关联整合，推行以远程监管、移动监管、预警防控为特征的非现场监管，提升监管的精准化、智能化水平。

**Article 56** The government and its relevant departments shall fully utilize technical means such as the Internet and big data, rely on the unified online supervision system established by the State, strengthen the collection, sharing and related integration of regulatory information, promote off-site supervision characterized by remote supervision, mobile supervision, and early warning and prevention, and enhance the precision and intelligence of supervision.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国家建立健全跨部门、跨区域行政执法联动响应和协作机制，实现违法线索互联、监管标准互通、处理结果互认。  
国家统筹配置行政执法职能和执法资源，在相关领域推行综合行政执法，整合精简执法队伍，减少执法主体和执法层级，提高基层执法能力。

**Article 57** The State shall establish and improve the cross-departmental and cross-regional joint response and coordination mechanism for administrative law enforcement, to achieve interconnection of illegal clues, interoperability of regulatory standards, and mutual recognition of handling results.  
The State shall coordinate the allocation of administrative law enforcement functions and law enforcement resources, promote comprehensive administrative law enforcement in relevant fields, integrate and streamline law enforcement teams, reduce the number of law enforcement entities and levels, and enhance law enforcement capacity at the grassroots level.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　行政执法机关应当按照国家有关规定，全面落实行政执法公示、行政执法全过程记录和重大行政执法决定法制审核制度，实现行政执法信息及时准确公示、行政执法全过程留痕和可回溯管理、重大行政执法决定法制审核全覆盖。

**Article 58** The administrative law enforcement organs shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, fully implement the systems of administrative law enforcement disclosure, full-process recording of administrative law enforcement, and legal review of major administrative law enforcement decisions, so as to achieve timely and accurate disclosure of administrative law enforcement information, full-process traceability and retrievable management of administrative law enforcement, and full coverage of legal review of major administrative law enforcement decisions.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　行政执法中应当推广运用说服教育、劝导示范、行政指导等非强制性手段，依法慎重实施行政强制。采用非强制性手段能够达到行政管理目的的，不得实施行政强制；违法行为情节轻微或者社会危害较小的，可以不实施行政强制；确需实施行政强制的，应当尽可能减少对市场主体正常生产经营活动的影响。  
开展清理整顿、专项整治等活动，应当严格依法进行，除涉及人民群众生命安全、发生重特大事故或者举办国家重大活动，并报经有权机关批准外，不得在相关区域采取要求相关行业、领域的市场主体普遍停产、停业的措施。  
禁止将罚没收入与行政执法机关利益挂钩。

**Article 59** In administrative law enforcement, non-compulsory means such as persuasion and education, persuasion and demonstration, and administrative guidance shall be promoted and applied, and administrative compulsion shall be implemented carefully in accordance with law. Where the purpose of administrative management can be achieved by non-compulsory means, administrative compulsion shall not be implemented; where the illegal act is minor or the social harm is small, administrative compulsion may not be implemented; where administrative compulsion is truly necessary, the impact on the normal production and operation activities of market entities shall be minimized as far as possible.  
Activities such as cleanup and rectification and special remediation shall be conducted strictly in accordance with law. Unless it involves the life safety of the people, the occurrence of a major or serious accident, or the holding of a major national event, and has been reported to and approved by the competent authority, measures requiring the general suspension of production or business operation of market entities in relevant industries or fields shall not be adopted in the relevant area.  
It is prohibited to link the confiscated income with the interests of the administrative law enforcement organs.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国家健全行政执法自由裁量基准制度，合理确定裁量范围、种类和幅度，规范行政执法自由裁量权的行使。

**Article 60** The State shall improve the benchmark system for administrative law enforcement discretion, reasonably determine the scope, types and range of discretion, and regulate the exercise of administrative law enforcement discretion.

### 第六章　法治保障

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家根据优化营商环境需要，依照法定权限和程序及时制定或者修改、废止有关法律、法规、规章、行政规范性文件。  
优化营商环境的改革措施涉及调整实施现行法律、行政法规等有关规定的，依照法定程序经有权机关授权后，可以先行先试。

**Article 61** The State shall, based on the needs of optimizing the business environment and in accordance with statutory authority and procedures, timely formulate, amend, or abolish relevant laws, regulations, rules, and administrative normative documents.  
Where reform measures for optimizing the business environment involve adjustments to the implementation of existing laws, administrative regulations, and other relevant provisions, they may, after being authorized by the competent authority in accordance with statutory procedures, be piloted on a trial basis.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　制定与市场主体生产经营活动密切相关的行政法规、规章、行政规范性文件，应当按照国务院的规定，充分听取市场主体、行业协会商会的意见。  
除依法需要保密外，制定与市场主体生产经营活动密切相关的行政法规、规章、行政规范性文件，应当通过报纸、网络等向社会公开征求意见，并建立健全意见采纳情况反馈机制。向社会公开征求意见的期限一般不少于30日。

**Article 62** When formulating administrative regulations, rules, and administrative normative documents that are closely related to the production and business activities of market entities, opinions of market entities, industry associations, and chambers of commerce shall be fully solicited in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.  
Except where confidentiality is required by law, when formulating administrative regulations, rules, and administrative normative documents that are closely related to the production and business activities of market entities, public comments shall be solicited through newspapers, the internet, etc., and a mechanism for providing feedback on the adoption of comments shall be established and improved. The period for soliciting public comments shall generally be no less than 30 days.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　制定与市场主体生产经营活动密切相关的行政法规、规章、行政规范性文件，应当按照国务院的规定进行公平竞争审查。  
制定涉及市场主体权利义务的行政规范性文件，应当按照国务院的规定进行合法性审核。  
市场主体认为地方性法规同行政法规相抵触，或者认为规章同法律、行政法规相抵触的，可以向国务院书面提出审查建议，由有关机关按照规定程序处理。

**Article 63** When formulating administrative regulations, rules, and administrative normative documents that are closely related to the production and business activities of market entities, a fair competition review shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.  
When formulating administrative normative documents that involve the rights and obligations of market entities, a legality review shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.  
Where a market entity considers that a local regulation contradicts an administrative regulation, or that a rule contradicts a law or administrative regulation, it may submit a written review proposal to the State Council, and the relevant authority shall handle it in accordance with the prescribed procedures.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　没有法律、法规或者国务院决定和命令依据的，行政规范性文件不得减损市场主体合法权益或者增加其义务，不得设置市场准入和退出条件，不得干预市场主体正常生产经营活动。  
涉及市场主体权利义务的行政规范性文件应当按照法定要求和程序予以公布，未经公布的不得作为行政管理依据。

**Article 64** Without the basis of laws, regulations, or decisions and orders of the State Council, administrative normative documents shall not impair the lawful rights and interests of market entities or increase their obligations, shall not set conditions for market access and exit, and shall not interfere with the normal production and business activities of market entities.  
Administrative normative documents involving the rights and obligations of market entities shall be published in accordance with statutory requirements and procedures, and those not published shall not be used as the basis for administrative management.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　制定与市场主体生产经营活动密切相关的行政法规、规章、行政规范性文件，应当结合实际，确定是否为市场主体留出必要的适应调整期。  
政府及其有关部门应当统筹协调、合理把握规章、行政规范性文件等的出台节奏，全面评估政策效果，避免因政策叠加或者相互不协调对市场主体正常生产经营活动造成不利影响。

**Article 65** When formulating administrative regulations, rules, and administrative normative documents that are closely related to the production and business activities of market entities, it shall, based on actual circumstances, be determined whether to leave necessary adaptation and adjustment periods for market entities.  
The government and its relevant departments shall coordinate and reasonably control the pace of promulgation of rules, administrative normative documents, etc., and comprehensively evaluate policy effects to avoid adverse impacts on the normal production and business activities of market entities due to policy accumulation or mutual incoordination.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家完善调解、仲裁、行政裁决、行政复议、诉讼等有机衔接、相互协调的多元化纠纷解决机制，为市场主体提供高效、便捷的纠纷解决途径。

**Article 66** The State shall improve the diversified dispute resolution mechanism that organically connects and coordinates mediation, arbitration, administrative adjudication, administrative reconsideration, litigation, etc., providing efficient and convenient dispute resolution channels for market entities.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　国家加强法治宣传教育，落实国家机关普法责任制，提高国家工作人员依法履职能力，引导市场主体合法经营、依法维护自身合法权益，不断增强全社会的法治意识，为营造法治化营商环境提供基础性支撑。

**Article 67** The State shall strengthen publicity and education on the rule of law, implement the responsibility system for legal popularization of state organs, improve the ability of state functionaries to perform their duties according to law, guide market entities to operate lawfully and safeguard their own lawful rights and interests according to law, continuously enhance the rule of law awareness of the whole society, and provide basic support for creating a law-based business environment.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　政府及其有关部门应当整合律师、公证、司法鉴定、调解、仲裁等公共法律服务资源，加快推进公共法律服务体系建设，全面提升公共法律服务能力和水平，为优化营商环境提供全方位法律服务。

**Article 68** The government and its relevant departments shall integrate public legal service resources such as lawyers, notarization, judicial expertise, mediation, and arbitration, accelerate the construction of the public legal service system, comprehensively improve the capacity and level of public legal services, and provide all-round legal services for optimizing the business environment.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　政府和有关部门及其工作人员有下列情形之一的，依法依规追究责任：  
(一)违法干预应当由市场主体自主决策的事项；  
(二)制定或者实施政策措施不依法平等对待各类市场主体；  
(三)违反法定权限、条件、程序对市场主体的财产和企业经营者个人财产实施查封、冻结和扣押等行政强制措施；  
(四)在法律、法规规定之外要求市场主体提供财力、物力或者人力；  
(五)没有法律、法规依据，强制或者变相强制市场主体参加评比、达标、表彰、培训、考核、考试以及类似活动，或者借前述活动向市场主体收费或者变相收费；  
(六)违法设立或者在目录清单之外执行政府性基金、涉企行政事业性收费、涉企保证金；  
(七)不履行向市场主体依法作出的政策承诺以及依法订立的各类合同，或者违约拖欠市场主体的货物、工程、服务等账款；  
(八)变相设定或者实施行政许可，继续实施或者变相实施已取消的行政许可，或者转由行业协会商会或者其他组织实施已取消的行政许可；  
(九)为市场主体指定或者变相指定中介服务机构，或者违法强制市场主体接受中介服务；  
(十)制定与市场主体生产经营活动密切相关的行政法规、规章、行政规范性文件时，不按照规定听取市场主体、行业协会商会的意见；  
(十一)其他不履行优化营商环境职责或者损害营商环境的情形。

**Article 69** Where the government and relevant departments and their functionaries have any of the following circumstances, they shall be held accountable in accordance with laws and regulations:  
(1) illegally intervening in matters that shall be independently decided by market entities;  
(2) formulating or implementing policies and measures that do not treat all types of market entities equally according to law;  
(3) violating statutory authority, conditions, or procedures to implement administrative compulsory measures such as sealing, freezing, and detaining the property of market entities and the personal property of enterprise operators;  
(4) requiring market entities to provide financial, material, or human resources beyond the provisions of laws and regulations;  
(5) without the basis of laws and regulations, forcing or forcing in disguised form market entities to participate in appraisals, assessments, commendations, training, examinations, tests, and similar activities, or charging fees or disguised fees to market entities through the aforesaid activities;  
(6) illegally establishing or implementing government funds, enterprise-related administrative charges, or enterprise-related deposits outside the catalog list;  
(7) failing to fulfill the policy commitments made to market entities according to law and the various contracts concluded according to law, or defaulting on debts such as payments for goods, projects, services, etc., owed to market entities;  
(8) setting up or implementing administrative licenses in disguised form, continuing to implement or implementing in disguised form administrative licenses that have been abolished, or transferring the abolished administrative licenses to industry associations, chambers of commerce, or other organizations for implementation;  
(9) designating or designating in disguised form intermediary service institutions for market entities, or illegally forcing market entities to accept intermediary services;  
(10) when formulating administrative regulations, rules, and administrative normative documents closely related to the production and business activities of market entities, failing to solicit opinions of market entities, industry associations, and chambers of commerce in accordance with regulations;  
(11) other circumstances where the duty of optimizing the business environment is not performed or the business environment is damaged.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　公用企事业单位有下列情形之一的，由有关部门责令改正，依法追究法律责任：  
(一)不向社会公开服务标准、资费标准、办理时限等信息；  
(二)强迫市场主体接受不合理的服务条件；  
(三)向市场主体收取不合理费用。

**Article 70** Where public enterprises and institutions have any of the following circumstances, the relevant departments shall order them to make corrections and pursue legal liability according to law:  
(1) failing to disclose to the public information such as service standards, fee standards, and processing time limits;  
(2) forcing market entities to accept unreasonable service conditions;  
(3) charging unreasonable fees to market entities.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　行业协会商会、中介服务机构有下列情形之一的，由有关部门责令改正，依法追究法律责任：  
(一)违法开展收费、评比、认证等行为；  
(二)违法干预市场主体加入或者退出行业协会商会等社会组织；  
(三)没有法律、法规依据，强制或者变相强制市场主体参加评比、达标、表彰、培训、考核、考试以及类似活动，或者借前述活动向市场主体收费或者变相收费；  
(四)不向社会公开办理法定行政审批中介服务的条件、流程、时限、收费标准；  
(五)违法强制或者变相强制市场主体接受中介服务。

**Article 71** Where trade associations, chambers of commerce, or intermediary service institutions fall under any of the following circumstances, the relevant departments shall order them to make corrections and pursue legal liability according to law:  
(1) illegally carrying out activities such as fee collection, appraisal, or certification;  
(2) illegally intervening in the entry or exit of market entities into or from social organizations such as trade associations or chambers of commerce;  
(3) without any basis in laws or regulations, compelling or compelling in disguised form market entities to participate in appraisals, assessments, commendations, training, examinations, tests, or similar activities, or using such activities to collect or collect in disguised form fees from market entities;  
(4) failing to make public to society the conditions, procedures, time limits, and fee standards for handling statutory administrative examination and approval intermediary services; or  
(5) illegally compelling or compelling in disguised form market entities to accept intermediary services.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　本条例自2020年1月1日起施行。

**Article 72** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2020.
