# 大型群众性活动安全管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2007-09-14  
**生效日期**：2007-10-01

> (2007年8月29日国务院第190次常务会议通过　2007年9月14日中华人民共和国国务院令第505号公布　自2007年10月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对大型群众性活动的安全管理，保护公民生命和财产安全，维护社会治安秩序和公共安全，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称大型群众性活动，是指法人或者其他组织面向社会公众举办的每场次预计参加人数达到1000人以上的下列活动： (一)体育比赛活动； (二)演唱会、音乐会等文艺演出活动； (三)展览、展销等活动； (四)游园、灯会、庙会、花会、焰火晚会等活动； (五)人才招聘会、现场开奖的彩票销售等活动。 影剧院、音乐厅、公园、娱乐场所等在其日常业务范围内举办的活动，不适用本条例的规定。 第三条　大型群众性活动的安全管理应当遵循安全第一、预防为主的方针，坚持承办者负责、政府监管的原则。 第四条　县级以上人民政府公安机关负责大型群众性活动的安全管理工作。 县级以上人民政府其他有关主管部门按照各自的职责，负责大型群众性活动的有关安全工作。 第二章　安全责任 第五条　大型群众性活动的承办者(以下简称承办者)对其承办活动的安全负责，承办者的主要负责人为大型群众性活动的安全责任人。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对大型群众性活动的安全管理，保护公民生命和财产安全，维护社会治安秩序和公共安全，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the safety management of large-scale mass activities, protect the life and property of citizens, and maintain public security order and public safety.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称大型群众性活动，是指法人或者其他组织面向社会公众举办的每场次预计参加人数达到1000人以上的下列活动：  
(一)体育比赛活动；  
(二)演唱会、音乐会等文艺演出活动；  
(三)展览、展销等活动；  
(四)游园、灯会、庙会、花会、焰火晚会等活动；  
(五)人才招聘会、现场开奖的彩票销售等活动。  
影剧院、音乐厅、公园、娱乐场所等在其日常业务范围内举办的活动，不适用本条例的规定。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "large-scale mass activities" means the following activities held by a legal person or any other organization for the general public, with an expected number of participants of 1,000 or more per event:  
(1) sports competitions;  
(2) performances such as concerts and concerts;  
(3) exhibitions, trade fairs, etc.;  
(4) garden parties, lantern festivals, temple fairs, flower festivals, fireworks parties, etc.;  
(5) job fairs, on-site lottery sales, etc.  
These Regulations shall not apply to activities held by cinemas, concert halls, parks, recreational venues, etc., within their daily business scope.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　大型群众性活动的安全管理应当遵循安全第一、预防为主的方针，坚持承办者负责、政府监管的原则。

**Article 3** The safety management of large-scale mass activities shall follow the principles of putting safety first and focusing on prevention, and adhere to the principles of the organizer being responsible and the government supervising.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府公安机关负责大型群众性活动的安全管理工作。  
县级以上人民政府其他有关主管部门按照各自的职责，负责大型群众性活动的有关安全工作。

**Article 4** The public security organs of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the safety management of large-scale mass activities.  
Other competent departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant safety work of large-scale mass activities.

### 第二章　安全责任

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　大型群众性活动的承办者(以下简称承办者)对其承办活动的安全负责，承办者的主要负责人为大型群众性活动的安全责任人。

**Article 5** The organizer of a large-scale mass activity (hereinafter referred to as the organizer) shall be responsible for the safety of the activity it organizes, and the principal person in charge of the organizer shall be the safety person responsible for the large-scale mass activity.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　举办大型群众性活动，承办者应当制订大型群众性活动安全工作方案。  
大型群众性活动安全工作方案包括下列内容：  
(一)活动的时间、地点、内容及组织方式；  
(二)安全工作人员的数量、任务分配和识别标志；  
(三)活动场所消防安全措施；  
(四)活动场所可容纳的人员数量以及活动预计参加人数；  
(五)治安缓冲区域的设定及其标识；  
(六)入场人员的票证查验和安全检查措施；  
(七)车辆停放、疏导措施；  
(八)现场秩序维护、人员疏导措施；  
(九)应急救援预案。

**Article 6** To hold a large-scale mass activity, the organizer shall formulate a safety work plan for the large-scale mass activity.  
The safety work plan for a large-scale mass activity shall include the following:  
(1) the time, place, content and organizational method of the activity;  
(2) the number, task assignment and identification marks of safety staff;  
(3) fire safety measures for the activity site;  
(4) the number of persons that the activity site can accommodate and the expected number of participants in the activity;  
(5) the establishment and marking of public security buffer zones;  
(6) ticket verification and security inspection measures for persons entering the site;  
(7) vehicle parking and guidance measures;  
(8) on-site order maintenance and personnel guidance measures;  
(9) emergency rescue plans.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　承办者具体负责下列安全事项：  
(一)落实大型群众性活动安全工作方案和安全责任制度，明确安全措施、安全工作人员岗位职责，开展大型群众性活动安全宣传教育；  
(二)保障临时搭建的设施、建筑物的安全，消除安全隐患；  
(三)按照负责许可的公安机关的要求，配备必要的安全检查设备，对参加大型群众性活动的人员进行安全检查，对拒不接受安全检查的，承办者有权拒绝其进入；  
(四)按照核准的活动场所容纳人员数量、划定的区域发放或者出售门票；  
(五)落实医疗救护、灭火、应急疏散等应急救援措施并组织演练；  
(六)对妨碍大型群众性活动安全的行为及时予以制止，发现违法犯罪行为及时向公安机关报告；  
(七)配备与大型群众性活动安全工作需要相适应的专业保安人员以及其他安全工作人员；  
(八)为大型群众性活动的安全工作提供必要的保障。

**Article 7** The organizer shall specifically be responsible for the following safety matters:  
(1) implementing the safety work plan and safety responsibility system for the large-scale mass activity, specifying safety measures and the job responsibilities of safety staff, and carrying out safety publicity and education for the large-scale mass activity;  
(2) ensuring the safety of temporarily set up facilities and structures, and eliminating potential safety hazards;  
(3) equipping necessary safety inspection equipment as required by the public security organ responsible for licensing, conducting security checks on persons participating in the large-scale mass activity, and refusing entry to those who refuse security checks;  
(4) distributing or selling tickets in accordance with the approved capacity of the activity site and designated areas;  
(5) implementing emergency rescue measures such as medical aid, fire suppression, emergency evacuation, and organizing drills;  
(6) promptly stopping acts that hinder the safety of the large-scale mass activity, and reporting illegal or criminal acts to the public security organ in a timely manner;  
(7) providing professional security personnel and other safety staff commensurate with the needs of the safety work of the large-scale mass activity;  
(8) providing necessary guarantees for the safety work of the large-scale mass activity.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　大型群众性活动的场所管理者具体负责下列安全事项：  
(一)保障活动场所、设施符合国家安全标准和安全规定；  
(二)保障疏散通道、安全出口、消防车通道、应急广播、应急照明、疏散指示标志符合法律、法规、技术标准的规定；  
(三)保障监控设备和消防设施、器材配置齐全、完好有效；  
(四)提供必要的停车场地，并维护安全秩序。

**Article 8** The site manager of a large-scale mass activity shall specifically be responsible for the following safety matters:  
(1) ensuring that the activity site and facilities comply with national safety standards and safety regulations;  
(2) ensuring that evacuation passages, safety exits, fire engine access, emergency broadcasting, emergency lighting, and evacuation direction signs comply with the provisions of laws, regulations and technical standards;  
(3) ensuring that monitoring equipment and fire-fighting facilities and equipment are fully equipped, in good condition and effective;  
(4) providing necessary parking spaces and maintaining safety order.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　参加大型群众性活动的人员应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)遵守法律、法规和社会公德，不得妨碍社会治安、影响社会秩序；  
(二)遵守大型群众性活动场所治安、消防等管理制度，接受安全检查，不得携带爆炸性、易燃性、放射性、毒害性、腐蚀性等危险物质或者非法携带枪支、弹药、管制器具；  
(三)服从安全管理，不得展示侮辱性标语、条幅等物品，不得围攻裁判员、运动员或者其他工作人员，不得投掷杂物。

**Article 9** Persons participating in a large-scale mass activity shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) abide by laws, regulations and social ethics, and shall not disturb public security or affect social order;  
(2) comply with the public security, fire safety and other management systems of the large-scale mass activity site, accept security checks, and shall not carry dangerous substances such as explosive, flammable, radioactive, toxic or corrosive substances, or illegally carry firearms, ammunition or controlled instruments;  
(3) obey safety management, shall not display insulting slogans, banners or other items, shall not surround referees, athletes or other staff, and shall not throw debris.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　公安机关应当履行下列职责：  
(一)审核承办者提交的大型群众性活动申请材料，实施安全许可；  
(二)制订大型群众性活动安全监督方案和突发事件处置预案；  
(三)指导对安全工作人员的教育培训；  
(四)在大型群众性活动举办前，对活动场所组织安全检查，发现安全隐患及时责令改正；  
(五)在大型群众性活动举办过程中，对安全工作的落实情况实施监督检查，发现安全隐患及时责令改正；  
(六)依法查处大型群众性活动中的违法犯罪行为，处置危害公共安全的突发事件。

**Article 10** The public security organ shall perform the following duties:  
(1) review the application materials for a large-scale mass activity submitted by the organizer, and issue a safety permit;  
(2) formulate a safety supervision plan and an emergency response plan for the large-scale mass activity;  
(3) guide the education and training of safety staff;  
(4) before the holding of a large-scale mass activity, organize safety inspections of the activity site, and promptly order rectification of any potential safety hazards discovered;  
(5) during the holding of a large-scale mass activity, supervise and inspect the implementation of safety work, and promptly order rectification of any potential safety hazards discovered;  
(6) investigate and punish illegal and criminal acts in large-scale mass activities according to law, and deal with emergencies that endanger public safety.

### 第三章　安全管理

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　公安机关对大型群众性活动实行安全许可制度。《营业性演出管理条例》对演出活动的安全管理另有规定的，从其规定。  
举办大型群众性活动应当符合下列条件：  
(一)承办者是依照法定程序成立的法人或者其他组织；  
(二)大型群众性活动的内容不得违反宪法、法律、法规的规定，不得违反社会公德；  
(三)具有符合本条例规定的安全工作方案，安全责任明确、措施有效；  
(四)活动场所、设施符合安全要求。

**Article 11** The public security organ shall implement a safety permit system for large-scale mass activities. Where the Regulations on the Administration of Commercial Performances have other provisions on the safety management of performances, those provisions shall prevail.  
The holding of a large-scale mass activity shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) the organizer is a legal person or other organization established in accordance with legal procedures;  
(2) the content of the large-scale mass activity shall not violate the provisions of the Constitution, laws or regulations, and shall not violate social morality;  
(3) there is a safety work plan that conforms to the provisions of these Regulations, with clear safety responsibilities and effective measures;  
(4) the activity site and facilities meet safety requirements.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　大型群众性活动的预计参加人数在1000人以上5000人以下的，由活动所在地县级人民政府公安机关实施安全许可；预计参加人数在5000人以上的，由活动所在地设区的市级人民政府公安机关或者直辖市人民政府公安机关实施安全许可；跨省、自治区、直辖市举办大型群众性活动的，由国务院公安部门实施安全许可。

**Article 12** Where the expected number of participants in a large-scale mass activity is 1,000 or more but less than 5,000, the public security organ of the people's government at the county level where the activity is located shall implement the safety permit; where the expected number of participants is 5,000 or more, the public security organ of the people's government of the city divided into districts or the public security organ of the people's government of the municipality directly under the Central Government where the activity is located shall implement the safety permit; where a large-scale mass activity is held across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the public security department of the State Council shall implement the safety permit.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　承办者应当在活动举办日的20日前提出安全许可申请，申请时，应当提交下列材料：  
(一)承办者合法成立的证明以及安全责任人的身份证明；  
(二)大型群众性活动方案及其说明，2个或者2个以上承办者共同承办大型群众性活动的，还应当提交联合承办的协议；  
(三)大型群众性活动安全工作方案；  
(四)活动场所管理者同意提供活动场所的证明。  
依照法律、行政法规的规定，有关主管部门对大型群众性活动的承办者有资质、资格要求的，还应当提交有关资质、资格证明。

**Article 13** The organizer shall submit an application for a safety permit 20 days before the date of the activity, and when applying, shall submit the following materials:  
(1) the certificate of lawful establishment of the organizer and the identity certificate of the safety person;  
(2) the plan for the large-scale mass activity and its description; where two or more organizers jointly organize a large-scale mass activity, the joint organization agreement shall also be submitted;  
(3) the safety work plan for the large-scale mass activity;  
(4) the certificate from the site manager agreeing to provide the activity site.  
Where, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, the competent authority has requirements on the qualifications of the organizer of a large-scale mass activity, the relevant qualification certificate shall also be submitted.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　公安机关收到申请材料应当依法做出受理或者不予受理的决定。对受理的申请，应当自受理之日起7日内进行审查，对活动场所进行查验，对符合安全条件的，做出许可的决定；对不符合安全条件的，做出不予许可的决定，并书面说明理由。

**Article 14** Upon receiving the application materials, the public security organ shall make a decision to accept or reject the application according to law. For an accepted application, it shall review the application within 7 days from the date of acceptance, inspect the activity site, and make a decision to grant the permit if the safety conditions are met; if the safety conditions are not met, it shall make a decision not to grant the permit, and state the reasons in writing.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　对经安全许可的大型群众性活动，承办者不得擅自变更活动的时间、地点、内容或者扩大大型群众性活动的举办规模。  
承办者变更大型群众性活动时间的，应当在原定举办活动时间之前向做出许可决定的公安机关申请变更，经公安机关同意方可变更。  
承办者变更大型群众性活动地点、内容以及扩大大型群众性活动举办规模的，应当依照本条例的规定重新申请安全许可。  
承办者取消举办大型群众性活动的，应当在原定举办活动时间之前书面告知做出安全许可决定的公安机关，并交回公安机关颁发的准予举办大型群众性活动的安全许可证件。

**Article 15** For a large-scale mass activity that has been granted a safety permit, the organizer shall not change the time, place or content of the activity without authorization, or expand the scale of the large-scale mass activity.  
If the organizer changes the time of the large-scale mass activity, it shall apply to the public security organ that made the permit decision for a change before the originally scheduled time of the activity, and the change may be made only with the consent of the public security organ.  
If the organizer changes the place or content of the large-scale mass activity or expands the scale of the large-scale mass activity, it shall reapply for a safety permit in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
If the organizer cancels the holding of a large-scale mass activity, it shall notify the public security organ that made the safety permit decision in writing before the originally scheduled time of the activity, and return the safety permit certificate issued by the public security organ for allowing the holding of the large-scale mass activity.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　对经安全许可的大型群众性活动，公安机关根据安全需要组织相应警力，维持活动现场周边的治安、交通秩序，预防和处置突发治安事件，查处违法犯罪活动。

**Article 16** For a large-scale mass activity that has been granted a safety permit, the public security organ shall, based on safety needs, deploy appropriate police forces to maintain public security and traffic order around the activity site, prevent and handle sudden public security incidents, and investigate and deal with illegal and criminal activities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在大型群众性活动现场负责执行安全管理任务的公安机关工作人员，凭值勤证件进入大型群众性活动现场，依法履行安全管理职责。  
公安机关和其他有关主管部门及其工作人员不得向承办者索取门票。

**Article 17** Staff of the public security organ responsible for carrying out safety management tasks at the site of a large-scale mass activity may enter the site of the large-scale mass activity with their duty certificates and perform safety management duties according to law.  
The public security organ and other relevant competent departments and their staff shall not demand tickets from the organizer.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　承办者发现进入活动场所的人员达到核准数量时，应当立即停止验票；发现持有划定区域以外的门票或者持假票的人员，应当拒绝其入场并向活动现场的公安机关工作人员报告。

**Article 18** When the organizer finds that the number of persons entering the activity site has reached the approved number, it shall immediately stop ticket checking; if it finds persons holding tickets for areas other than the designated areas or holding fake tickets, it shall refuse their entry and report to the public security organ staff at the activity site.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　在大型群众性活动举办过程中发生公共安全事故、治安案件的，安全责任人应当立即启动应急救援预案，并立即报告公安机关。

**Article 19** In the event of a public safety accident or public security incident during the holding of a large-scale mass activity, the safety person shall immediately initiate the emergency rescue plan and immediately report to the public security organ.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　承办者擅自变更大型群众性活动的时间、地点、内容或者擅自扩大大型群众性活动的举办规模的，由公安机关处1万元以上5万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。  
未经公安机关安全许可的大型群众性活动由公安机关予以取缔，对承办者处10万元以上30万元以下罚款。

**Article 20** If the organizer changes the time, place or content of a large-scale mass activity without authorization or expands the scale of the large-scale mass activity without authorization, the public security organ shall impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.  
A large-scale mass activity held without a safety permit from the public security organ shall be banned by the public security organ, and the organizer shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　承办者或者大型群众性活动场所管理者违反本条例规定致使发生重大伤亡事故、治安案件或者造成其他严重后果构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，对安全责任人和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分、治安管理处罚，对单位处1万元以上5万元以下罚款。

**Article 21** If an organizer or site manager of a large-scale mass activity violates the provisions of these Regulations, causing a major casualty accident, public security incident, or other serious consequences constituting a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, the safety person and other directly responsible persons shall be given disciplinary sanctions or public security penalties according to law, and the unit shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　在大型群众性活动举办过程中发生公共安全事故，安全责任人不立即启动应急救援预案或者不立即向公安机关报告的，由公安机关对安全责任人和其他直接责任人员处5000元以上5万元以下罚款。

**Article 22** If, during the holding of a large-scale mass activity, a public safety accident occurs and the safety person fails to immediately initiate the emergency rescue plan or fails to immediately report to the public security organ, the public security organ shall impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the safety person and other directly responsible persons.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　参加大型群众性活动的人员有违反[本条例第九条](#art-9)规定行为的，由公安机关给予批评教育；有危害社会治安秩序、威胁公共安全行为的，公安机关可以将其强行带离现场，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 23** If a person participating in a large-scale mass activity violates the provisions of Article 9 of these Regulations, the public security organ shall give criticism and education; if the person's act endangers public security order or threatens public safety, the public security organ may forcibly remove the person from the site and impose a public security penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　有关主管部门的工作人员和直接负责的主管人员在履行大型群众性活动安全管理职责中，有滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 24** If any staff member or directly responsible person in charge of the relevant competent department, in the performance of their duties in the safety management of large-scale mass activities, engages in abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or favoritism, they shall be given sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上各级人民政府、国务院部门直接举办的大型群众性活动的安全保卫工作，由举办活动的人民政府、国务院部门负责，不实行安全许可制度，但应当按照本条例的有关规定，责成或者会同有关公安机关制订更加严格的安全保卫工作方案，并组织实施。

**Article 25** The security work of large-scale mass activities directly organized by people's governments at or above the county level or departments of the State Council shall be the responsibility of the people's government or the department of the State Council organizing the activity, and the safety permit system shall not apply. However, in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations, the organizing entity shall instruct or jointly with the relevant public security organ to formulate a more stringent security work plan and organize its implementation.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例自2007年10月1日起施行。

**Article 26** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 1, 2007.
