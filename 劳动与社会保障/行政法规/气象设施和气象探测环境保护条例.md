# 气象设施和气象探测环境保护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2016-02-06  
**生效日期**：2012-12-01

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护气象设施和气象探测环境，确保气象探测信息的代表性、准确性、连续性和可比较性，根据《中华人民共和国气象法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Meteorology Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of protecting meteorological facilities and the meteorological detection environment, and ensuring the representativeness, accuracy, continuity and comparability of meteorological detection information.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称气象设施，是指气象探测设施、气象信息专用传输设施和大型气象专用技术装备等。  
本条例所称气象探测环境，是指为避开各种干扰，保证气象探测设施准确获得气象探测信息所必需的最小距离构成的环境空间。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "meteorological facilities" refer to meteorological detection facilities, dedicated meteorological information transmission facilities, large-scale specialized meteorological technical equipment, and the like.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "meteorological detection environment" refers to the environmental space formed by the minimum distance necessary to avoid various interferences and to ensure that meteorological detection facilities accurately obtain meteorological detection information.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　气象设施和气象探测环境保护实行分类保护、分级管理的原则。

**Article 3** The protection of meteorological facilities and the meteorological detection environment shall follow the principles of classified protection and graded management.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对气象设施和气象探测环境保护工作的组织领导和统筹协调，将气象设施和气象探测环境保护工作所需经费纳入财政预算。

**Article 4** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the organization, leadership and overall coordination of the protection of meteorological facilities and the meteorological detection environment, and shall incorporate the funds needed for such protection into the fiscal budget.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院气象主管机构负责全国气象设施和气象探测环境的保护工作。地方各级气象主管机构在上级气象主管机构和本级人民政府的领导下，负责本行政区域内气象设施和气象探测环境的保护工作。  
设有气象台站的国务院其他有关部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府其他有关部门应当做好本部门气象设施和气象探测环境的保护工作，并接受同级气象主管机构的指导和监督管理。  
发展改革、国土资源、城乡规划、无线电管理、环境保护等有关部门按照职责分工负责气象设施和气象探测环境保护的有关工作。

**Article 5** The meteorological competent authority of the State Council shall be responsible for the protection of meteorological facilities and the meteorological detection environment nationwide. The local meteorological competent authorities at various levels shall, under the leadership of the meteorological competent authorities at the higher level and the people's governments at the same level, be responsible for the protection of meteorological facilities and the meteorological detection environment within their respective administrative regions.  
Other relevant departments of the State Council and other relevant departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government that have established meteorological stations shall properly protect the meteorological facilities and the meteorological detection environment within their respective departments, and shall accept the guidance and supervision of the meteorological competent authority at the same level.  
The relevant departments such as development and reform, land and resources, urban and rural planning, radio regulation, and environmental protection shall be responsible for the relevant work of protecting meteorological facilities and the meteorological detection environment according to their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何单位和个人都有义务保护气象设施和气象探测环境，并有权对破坏气象设施和气象探测环境的行为进行举报。

**Article 6** Any unit or individual shall have the obligation to protect meteorological facilities and the meteorological detection environment, and shall have the right to report any act that damages meteorological facilities or the meteorological detection environment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　地方各级气象主管机构应当会同城乡规划、国土资源等部门制定气象设施和气象探测环境保护专项规划，报本级人民政府批准后依法纳入城乡规划。

**Article 7** The local meteorological competent authorities at various levels shall, in conjunction with the departments of urban and rural planning, land and resources, and other relevant departments, formulate special plans for the protection of meteorological facilities and the meteorological detection environment, and shall, after obtaining approval from the people's government at the same level, incorporate such plans into the urban and rural planning according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　气象设施是基础性公共服务设施。县级以上地方人民政府应当按照气象设施建设规划的要求，合理安排气象设施建设用地，保障气象设施建设顺利进行。

**Article 8** Meteorological facilities are basic public service facilities. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the requirements of the construction plan for meteorological facilities, reasonably arrange the land for the construction of meteorological facilities, and ensure the smooth construction of meteorological facilities.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各级气象主管机构应当按照相关质量标准和技术要求配备气象设施，设置必要的保护装置，建立健全安全管理制度。  
地方各级气象主管机构应当按照国务院气象主管机构的规定，在气象设施附近显著位置设立保护标志，标明保护要求。

**Article 9** The meteorological competent authorities at various levels shall equip meteorological facilities, install necessary protective devices, and establish and improve safety management systems in accordance with relevant quality standards and technical requirements.  
The local meteorological competent authorities at various levels shall, in accordance with the provisions of the meteorological competent authority of the State Council, set up protection signs in conspicuous locations near meteorological facilities, indicating the protection requirements.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　禁止实施下列危害气象设施的行为：  
(一)侵占、损毁、擅自移动气象设施或者侵占气象设施用地；  
(二)在气象设施周边进行危及气象设施安全的爆破、钻探、采石、挖砂、取土等活动；  
(三)挤占、干扰依法设立的气象无线电台(站)、频率；  
(四)设置影响大型气象专用技术装备使用功能的干扰源；  
(五)法律、行政法规和国务院气象主管机构规定的其他危害气象设施的行为。

**Article 10** The following acts that endanger meteorological facilities are prohibited:  
(1) occupying, damaging, or unauthorizedly moving meteorological facilities, or occupying the land for meteorological facilities;  
(2) carrying out blasting, drilling, quarrying, sand excavation, earth-taking, or other activities that endanger the safety of meteorological facilities in the vicinity of such facilities;  
(3) encroaching upon or interfering with lawfully established meteorological radio stations (or stations) and frequencies;  
(4) setting up interference sources that affect the normal operation of large-scale specialized meteorological technical equipment;  
(5) other acts endangering meteorological facilities as stipulated by laws, administrative regulations, and the meteorological competent authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　大气本底站、国家基准气候站、国家基本气象站、国家一般气象站、高空气象观测站、天气雷达站、气象卫星地面站、区域气象观测站等气象台站和单独设立的气象探测设施的探测环境，应当依法予以保护。

**Article 11** The detection environment of meteorological stations such as atmospheric background stations, national reference climate stations, national basic weather stations, national general weather stations, upper-air observation stations, weather radar stations, meteorological satellite ground stations, regional meteorological observation stations, and separately established meteorological detection facilities shall be protected according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　禁止实施下列危害大气本底站探测环境的行为：  
(一)在观测场周边3万米探测环境保护范围内新建、扩建城镇、工矿区，或者在探测环境保护范围上空设置固定航线；  
(二)在观测场周边1万米范围内设置垃圾场、排污口等干扰源；  
(三)在观测场周边1000米范围内修建建筑物、构筑物。

**Article 12** The following acts that harm the detection environment of atmospheric background stations are prohibited:  
(1) constructing or expanding towns, industrial and mining areas within the 30,000-meter detection environment protection perimeter around the observation field, or setting up fixed air routes above the detection environment protection perimeter;  
(2) setting up garbage dumps, sewage outlets, or other interference sources within the 10,000-meter perimeter around the observation field;  
(3) constructing buildings or structures within the 1,000-meter perimeter around the observation field.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　禁止实施下列危害国家基准气候站、国家基本气象站探测环境的行为：  
(一)在国家基准气候站观测场周边2000米探测环境保护范围内或者国家基本气象站观测场周边1000米探测环境保护范围内修建高度超过距观测场距离1/10的建筑物、构筑物；  
(二)在观测场周边500米范围内设置垃圾场、排污口等干扰源；  
(三)在观测场周边200米范围内修建铁路；  
(四)在观测场周边100米范围内挖筑水塘等；  
(五)在观测场周边50米范围内修建公路、种植高度超过1米的树木和作物等。

**Article 13** The following acts that harm the detection environment of national reference climate stations or national basic weather stations are prohibited:  
(1) constructing buildings or structures with a height exceeding 1/10 of the distance from the observation field within the 2,000-meter detection environment protection perimeter around the observation field of a national reference climate station or within the 1,000-meter detection environment protection perimeter around the observation field of a national basic weather station;  
(2) setting up garbage dumps, sewage outlets, or other interference sources within the 500-meter perimeter around the observation field;  
(3) constructing railways within the 200-meter perimeter around the observation field;  
(4) digging ponds, etc., within the 100-meter perimeter around the observation field;  
(5) constructing roads or planting trees or crops with a height exceeding 1 meter within the 50-meter perimeter around the observation field.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　禁止实施下列危害国家一般气象站探测环境的行为：  
(一)在观测场周边800米探测环境保护范围内修建高度超过距观测场距离1/8的建筑物、构筑物；  
(二)在观测场周边200米范围内设置垃圾场、排污口等干扰源；  
(三)在观测场周边100米范围内修建铁路；  
(四)在观测场周边50米范围内挖筑水塘等；  
(五)在观测场周边30米范围内修建公路、种植高度超过1米的树木和作物等。

**Article 14** The following acts that harm the detection environment of national general weather stations are prohibited:  
(1) constructing buildings or structures with a height exceeding 1/8 of the distance from the observation field within the 800-meter detection environment protection perimeter around the observation field;  
(2) setting up garbage dumps, sewage outlets, or other interference sources within the 200-meter perimeter around the observation field;  
(3) constructing railways within the 100-meter perimeter around the observation field;  
(4) digging ponds, etc., within the 50-meter perimeter around the observation field;  
(5) constructing roads or planting trees or crops with a height exceeding 1 meter within the 30-meter perimeter around the observation field.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　高空气象观测站、天气雷达站、气象卫星地面站、区域气象观测站和单独设立的气象探测设施探测环境的保护，应当严格执行国家规定的保护范围和要求。  
前款规定的保护范围和要求由国务院气象主管机构公布，涉及无线电频率管理的，国务院气象主管机构应当征得国务院无线电管理部门的同意。

**Article 15** The protection of the detection environment of upper-air observation stations, weather radar stations, meteorological satellite ground stations, regional meteorological observation stations, and separately established meteorological detection facilities shall strictly implement the protection scopes and requirements prescribed by the state.  
The protection scopes and requirements prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be published by the meteorological competent authority of the State Council; if radio frequency management is involved, the meteorological competent authority of the State Council shall obtain the consent of the radio regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　地方各级气象主管机构应当将本行政区域内气象探测环境保护要求报告本级人民政府和上一级气象主管机构，并抄送同级发展改革、国土资源、城乡规划、住房建设、无线电管理、环境保护等部门。  
对不符合气象探测环境保护要求的建筑物、构筑物、干扰源等，地方各级气象主管机构应当根据实际情况，商有关部门提出治理方案，报本级人民政府批准并组织实施。

**Article 16** Local meteorological competent authorities at all levels shall report the requirements for the protection of the meteorological detection environment within their respective administrative regions to the people's government at the same level and the meteorological competent authority at the next higher level, and send copies to the departments of development and reform, land and resources, urban and rural planning, housing construction, radio regulation, and environmental protection at the same level.  
For buildings, structures, interference sources, etc., that do not meet the requirements for the protection of the meteorological detection environment, local meteorological competent authorities at all levels shall, based on the actual circumstances, consult with the relevant departments to propose remediation plans, submit them to the people's government at the same level for approval, and organize their implementation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在气象台站探测环境保护范围内新建、改建、扩建建设工程，应当避免危害气象探测环境；确实无法避免的，建设单位应当向省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构报告并提出相应的补救措施，经省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构书面同意。未征得气象主管机构书面同意或者未落实补救措施的，有关部门不得批准其开工建设。  
在单独设立的气象探测设施探测环境保护范围内新建、改建、扩建建设工程的，建设单位应当事先报告当地气象主管机构，并按照要求采取必要的工程、技术措施。

**Article 17** For construction, renovation, or expansion of construction projects within the protection perimeter of the detection environment of a meteorological station, harm to the meteorological detection environment shall be avoided; if it is truly unavoidable, the construction unit shall report to the meteorological competent authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and propose corresponding remedial measures, subject to the written consent of said authority. If the written consent of the meteorological competent authority is not obtained or the remedial measures are not implemented, the relevant department shall not approve the commencement of construction.  
For construction, renovation, or expansion of construction projects within the protection perimeter of the detection environment of a separately established meteorological detection facility, the construction unit shall report in advance to the local meteorological competent authority and take necessary engineering and technical measures as required.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　气象台站站址应当保持长期稳定，任何单位或者个人不得擅自迁移气象台站。  
因国家重点工程建设或者城市(镇)总体规划变化，确需迁移气象台站的，建设单位或者当地人民政府应当向省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构提出申请，由省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构组织专家对拟迁新址的科学性、合理性进行评估，符合气象设施和气象探测环境保护要求的，在纳入城市(镇)控制性详细规划后，按照先建站后迁移的原则进行迁移。  
申请迁移大气本底站、国家基准气候站、国家基本气象站的，由受理申请的省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构签署意见并报送国务院气象主管机构审批；申请迁移其他气象台站的，由省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构审批，并报送国务院气象主管机构备案。  
气象台站迁移、建设费用由建设单位承担。

**Article 18** The site of a meteorological station shall remain stable for a long period, and no unit or individual may relocate a meteorological station without authorization.  
If relocation is truly necessary due to a state key construction project or a change in the overall urban (town) plan, the construction unit or the local people's government shall submit an application to the meteorological competent authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. Said authority shall organize experts to evaluate the scientific and rational nature of the proposed new site. If it meets the requirements for the protection of meteorological facilities and the detection environment, after incorporation into the regulatory detailed urban (town) plan, relocation shall be carried out in accordance with the principle of building the new station before demolishing the old one.  
For an application to relocate an atmospheric background station, national reference climate station, or national basic weather station, the meteorological competent authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government that receives the application shall sign an opinion and submit it to the meteorological competent authority of the State Council for approval; for an application to relocate other meteorological stations, the meteorological competent authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall approve it and report it to the meteorological competent authority of the State Council for record.  
The costs of relocating and constructing the meteorological station shall be borne by the construction unit.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　气象台站探测环境遭到严重破坏，失去治理和恢复可能的，国务院气象主管机构或者省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构可以按照职责权限和先建站后迁移的原则，决定迁移气象台站；该气象台站所在地地方人民政府应当保证气象台站迁移用地，并承担迁移、建设费用。地方人民政府承担迁移、建设费用后，可以向破坏探测环境的责任人追偿。

**Article 19** If the detection environment of a meteorological station is severely damaged and loses the possibility of remediation and restoration, the meteorological competent authority of the State Council or the meteorological competent authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with its functions and powers and the principle of building the new station before demolishing the old one, decide to relocate the meteorological station; the local people's government of the place where the meteorological station is located shall guarantee the land for relocation and bear the costs of relocation and construction. After bearing the relocation and construction costs, the local people's government may recover compensation from the person liable for the damage to the detection environment.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　迁移气象台站的，应当按照国务院气象主管机构的规定，在新址与旧址之间进行至少1年的对比观测。  
迁移的气象台站经批准、决定迁移的气象主管机构验收合格，正式投入使用后，方可改变旧址用途。

**Article 20** When relocating a meteorological station, comparative observations shall be conducted between the new site and the old site for at least one year in accordance with the provisions of the meteorological competent authority of the State Council.  
The use of the old site may be changed only after the relocated meteorological station passes the acceptance inspection by the meteorological competent authority that approved or decided on the relocation and is officially put into operation.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　因工程建设或者气象探测环境治理需要迁移单独设立的气象探测设施的，应当经设立该气象探测设施的单位同意，并按照国务院气象主管机构规定的技术要求进行复建。

**Article 21** Where it is necessary to relocate a separately established meteorological observation facility due to engineering construction or the improvement of the meteorological observation environment, consent shall be obtained from the unit that established the meteorological observation facility, and reconstruction shall be carried out in accordance with the technical requirements specified by the competent department of the State Council for meteorological affairs.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　各级气象主管机构应当加强对气象设施和气象探测环境保护的日常巡查和监督检查。各级气象主管机构可以采取下列措施：  
(一)要求被检查单位或者个人提供有关文件、证照、资料；  
(二)要求被检查单位或者个人就有关问题作出说明；  
(三)进入现场调查、取证。  
各级气象主管机构在监督检查中发现应当由其他部门查处的违法行为，应当通报有关部门进行查处。有关部门未及时查处的，各级气象主管机构可以直接通报、报告有关地方人民政府责成有关部门进行查处。

**Article 22** The competent meteorological authorities at all levels shall strengthen daily inspections and supervisory inspections of the protection of meteorological facilities and meteorological observation environments. The competent meteorological authorities at all levels may take the following measures:  
(1) Requiring the unit or individual under inspection to provide relevant documents, licenses, and materials;  
(2) Requiring the unit or individual under inspection to provide explanations on relevant issues;  
(3) Entering the site to conduct investigations and gather evidence.  
Where, during supervisory inspections, the competent meteorological authorities at all levels discover illegal acts that should be investigated and dealt with by other departments, they shall notify the relevant departments to investigate and deal with such acts. Where the relevant departments fail to investigate and deal with them in a timely manner, the competent meteorological authorities at all levels may directly notify or report to the relevant local people's government to instruct the relevant departments to investigate and deal with them.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　各级气象主管机构以及发展改革、国土资源、城乡规划、无线电管理、环境保护等有关部门及其工作人员违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由本级人民政府或者上级机关责令改正，通报批评；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)擅自迁移气象台站的；  
(二)擅自批准在气象探测环境保护范围内设置垃圾场、排污口、无线电台(站)等干扰源以及新建、改建、扩建建设工程危害气象探测环境的；  
(三)有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊等不履行气象设施和气象探测环境保护职责行为的。

**Article 23** Where the competent meteorological authorities at all levels, and the relevant departments such as development and reform, land and resources, urban and rural planning, radio management, and environmental protection, and their staff members, violate these Regulations and commit any of the following acts, the people's government at the same level or the competent authority at a higher level shall order correction and circulate a notice of criticism; the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Relocating a meteorological station without authorization;  
(2) Without authorization, approving the establishment of interference sources such as garbage dumps, sewage outlets, radio stations (stations) within the protection scope of meteorological observation environments, or approving the construction, reconstruction, or expansion of construction projects that endanger the meteorological observation environment;  
(3) Having other acts of abusing power, neglecting duties, or practicing favoritism and engaging in irregularities by failing to fulfill the responsibilities of protecting meteorological facilities and meteorological observation environments.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　违反本条例规定，危害气象设施的，由气象主管机构责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施；逾期拒不恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施的，由气象主管机构依法申请人民法院强制执行，并对违法单位处1万元以上5万元以下罚款，对违法个人处100元以上1000元以下罚款；造成损害的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
挤占、干扰依法设立的气象无线电台(站)、频率的，依照无线电管理相关法律法规的规定处罚。

**Article 24** Where anyone violates these Regulations and endangers meteorological facilities, the competent meteorological authority shall order cessation of the illegal act, restoration to the original condition or adoption of other remedial measures within a specified period; if the party refuses to restore to the original condition or adopt other remedial measures upon expiration of the period, the competent meteorological authority shall, according to law, apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the illegal unit, and a fine of not less than 100 yuan but not more than 1,000 yuan on the illegal individual; where damage is caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Encroaching upon or interfering with lawfully established meteorological radio stations (stations) or frequencies shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations on radio management.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　违反本条例规定，危害气象探测环境的，由气象主管机构责令停止违法行为，限期拆除或者恢复原状，情节严重的，对违法单位处2万元以上5万元以下罚款，对违法个人处200元以上5000元以下罚款；逾期拒不拆除或者恢复原状的，由气象主管机构依法申请人民法院强制执行；造成损害的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
在气象探测环境保护范围内，违法批准占用土地的，或者非法占用土地新建建筑物或者其他设施的，依照城乡规划、土地管理等相关法律法规的规定处罚。

**Article 25** Where anyone violates these Regulations and endangers the meteorological observation environment, the competent meteorological authority shall order cessation of the illegal act, and dismantling or restoration to the original condition within a specified period; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on the illegal unit, and a fine of not less than 200 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan on the illegal individual; if the party refuses to dismantle or restore to the original condition upon expiration of the period, the competent meteorological authority shall, according to law, apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement; where damage is caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.  
Approval of land occupation in violation of law, or illegal occupation of land for the construction of new buildings or other facilities, within the protection scope of the meteorological observation environment, shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations such as urban and rural planning and land management.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例自2012年12月1日起施行。

**Article 26** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 2012.
