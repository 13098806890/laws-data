# 规划环境影响评价条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：社会法  
**公布日期**：2009-08-17  
**生效日期**：2009-10-01

> (2009年8月12日国务院第76次常务会议通过　2009年8月17日中华人民共和国国务院令第559号公布　自2009年10月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对规划的环境影响评价工作，提高规划的科学性，从源头预防环境污染和生态破坏，促进经济、社会和环境的全面协调可持续发展，根据《中华人民共和国环境影响评价法》，制定本条例。 第二条　国务院有关部门、设区的市级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门，对其组织编制的土地利用的有关规划和区域、流域、海域的建设、开发利用规划(以下称综合性规划)，以及工业、农业、畜牧业、林业、能源、水利、交通、城市建设、旅游、自然资源开发的有关专项规划(以下称专项规划)，应当进行环境影响评价。 依照本条第一款规定应当进行环境影响评价的规划的具体范围，由国务院环境保护主管部门会同国务院有关部门拟订，报国务院批准后执行。 第三条　对规划进行环境影响评价，应当遵循客观、公开、公正的原则。 第四条　国家建立规划环境影响评价信息共享制度。 县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当对规划环境影响评价所需资料实行信息共享。 第五条　规划环境影响评价所需的费用应当按照预算管理的规定纳入财政预算，严格支出管理，接受审计监督。 第六条　任何单位和个人对违反本条例规定的行为或者对规划实施过程中产生的重大不良环境影响，有权向规划审批机关、规划编制机关或者环境保护主管部门举报。有关部门接到举报后，应当依法调查处理。 第二章　评价 第七条　规划编制机关应当在规划编制过程中对规划组织进行环境影响评价。 第八条　对规划进行环境影响评价，应当分析、预测和评估以下内容： (一)规划实施可能对相关区域、流域、海域生态系统产生的整体影响； (二)规划实施可能对环境和人群健康产生的长远影响；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对规划的环境影响评价工作，提高规划的科学性，从源头预防环境污染和生态破坏，促进经济、社会和环境的全面协调可持续发展，根据《中华人民共和国环境影响评价法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the environmental impact assessment of plans, improving the scientific nature of plans, preventing environmental pollution and ecological damage at the source, and promoting comprehensive, coordinated and sustainable development of the economy, society and the environment, in accordance with the Environmental Impact Assessment Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国务院有关部门、设区的市级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门，对其组织编制的土地利用的有关规划和区域、流域、海域的建设、开发利用规划(以下称综合性规划)，以及工业、农业、畜牧业、林业、能源、水利、交通、城市建设、旅游、自然资源开发的有关专项规划(以下称专项规划)，应当进行环境影响评价。  
依照本条第一款规定应当进行环境影响评价的规划的具体范围，由国务院环境保护主管部门会同国务院有关部门拟订，报国务院批准后执行。

**Article 2** The relevant departments of the State Council, local people's governments at or above the city level with districts and their relevant departments shall conduct environmental impact assessment of the relevant plans for land use and the plans for construction, development and utilization of regions, river basins and sea areas (hereinafter referred to as comprehensive plans) which they organize to prepare, and of the relevant special plans for industry, agriculture, animal husbandry, forestry, energy, water conservancy, transportation, urban construction, tourism and natural resource development (hereinafter referred to as special plans).  
The specific scope of plans that shall be subject to environmental impact assessment pursuant to the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article shall be drafted by the competent department of environmental protection of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, and shall come into implementation after being approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　对规划进行环境影响评价，应当遵循客观、公开、公正的原则。

**Article 3** Environmental impact assessment of a plan shall follow the principles of objectivity, openness and fairness.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家建立规划环境影响评价信息共享制度。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当对规划环境影响评价所需资料实行信息共享。

**Article 4** The State establishes an information sharing system for environmental impact assessment of plans.  
People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall implement information sharing of the data required for environmental impact assessment of plans.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　规划环境影响评价所需的费用应当按照预算管理的规定纳入财政预算，严格支出管理，接受审计监督。

**Article 5** The expenses required for environmental impact assessment of plans shall be included in the fiscal budget in accordance with the provisions on budget management, and strict management of expenditures shall be carried out, subject to audit supervision.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何单位和个人对违反本条例规定的行为或者对规划实施过程中产生的重大不良环境影响，有权向规划审批机关、规划编制机关或者环境保护主管部门举报。有关部门接到举报后，应当依法调查处理。

**Article 6** Any unit or individual that discovers any act violating the provisions of these Regulations or any major adverse environmental impact arising during the implementation of a plan shall have the right to report it to the plan approval authority, the plan compilation authority or the competent department of environmental protection. The relevant department, upon receiving such a report, shall investigate and handle it according to law.

### 第二章　评价

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　规划编制机关应当在规划编制过程中对规划组织进行环境影响评价。

**Article 7** The plan compilation authority shall, during the process of compiling a plan, organize the environmental impact assessment of the plan.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对规划进行环境影响评价，应当分析、预测和评估以下内容：  
(一)规划实施可能对相关区域、流域、海域生态系统产生的整体影响；  
(二)规划实施可能对环境和人群健康产生的长远影响；  
(三)规划实施的经济效益、社会效益与环境效益之间以及当前利益与长远利益之间的关系。

**Article 8** Environmental impact assessment of a plan shall analyze, predict and evaluate the following:  
(1) the overall impact that the implementation of the plan may have on the ecosystems of the relevant regions, river basins and sea areas;  
(2) the long-term impact that the implementation of the plan may have on the environment and human health; and  
(3) the relationship between the economic, social and environmental benefits of the implementation of the plan and between current and long-term interests.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对规划进行环境影响评价，应当遵守有关环境保护标准以及环境影响评价技术导则和技术规范。  
规划环境影响评价技术导则由国务院环境保护主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定；规划环境影响评价技术规范由国务院有关部门根据规划环境影响评价技术导则制定，并抄送国务院环境保护主管部门备案。

**Article 9** Environmental impact assessment of a plan shall comply with the relevant environmental protection standards, technical guidelines and technical specifications for environmental impact assessment.  
The technical guidelines for environmental impact assessment of plans shall be formulated by the competent department of environmental protection of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council; the technical specifications for environmental impact assessment of plans shall be formulated by the relevant departments of the State Council in accordance with the technical guidelines for environmental impact assessment of plans, and shall be submitted to the competent department of environmental protection of the State Council for archiving.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　编制综合性规划，应当根据规划实施后可能对环境造成的影响，编写环境影响篇章或者说明。  
编制专项规划，应当在规划草案报送审批前编制环境影响报告书。编制专项规划中的指导性规划，应当依照本条第一款规定编写环境影响篇章或者说明。  
本条第二款所称指导性规划是指以发展战略为主要内容的专项规划。

**Article 10** When compiling a comprehensive plan, the plan compilation authority shall, based on the impact that the plan may have on the environment after implementation, prepare a environmental impact chapter or statement.  
When compiling a special plan, the plan compilation authority shall prepare an environmental impact report before submitting the plan draft for approval. When compiling a guiding plan within a special plan, the plan compilation authority shall prepare a environmental impact chapter or statement in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article.  
The term "guiding plan" as mentioned in the second paragraph of this Article refers to a special plan with development strategy as its main content.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　环境影响篇章或者说明应当包括下列内容：  
(一)规划实施对环境可能造成影响的分析、预测和评估。主要包括资源环境承载能力分析、不良环境影响的分析和预测以及与相关规划的环境协调性分析。  
(二)预防或者减轻不良环境影响的对策和措施。主要包括预防或者减轻不良环境影响的政策、管理或者技术等措施。  
环境影响报告书除包括上述内容外，还应当包括环境影响评价结论。主要包括规划草案的环境合理性和可行性，预防或者减轻不良环境影响的对策和措施的合理性和有效性，以及规划草案的调整建议。

**Article 11** The environmental impact chapter or statement shall include the following:  
(1) analysis, prediction and assessment of the possible environmental impact of the implementation of the plan, mainly including analysis of the carrying capacity of resources and the environment, analysis and prediction of adverse environmental impact, and analysis of environmental compatibility with related plans; and  
(2) measures and countermeasures to prevent or mitigate adverse environmental impact, mainly including policy, management or technical measures to prevent or mitigate adverse environmental impact.  
In addition to the contents mentioned above, the environmental impact report shall also include the conclusions of the environmental impact assessment, mainly including the environmental reasonableness and feasibility of the plan draft, the reasonableness and effectiveness of the measures and countermeasures to prevent or mitigate adverse environmental impact, and suggestions for adjusting the plan draft.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　环境影响篇章或者说明、环境影响报告书(以下称环境影响评价文件)，由规划编制机关编制或者组织规划环境影响评价技术机构编制。规划编制机关应当对环境影响评价文件的质量负责。

**Article 12** The environmental impact chapter or statement and the environmental impact report (hereinafter referred to as environmental impact assessment documents) shall be prepared by the plan compilation authority or by a technical institution for environmental impact assessment of plans organized by the plan compilation authority. The plan compilation authority shall be responsible for the quality of the environmental impact assessment documents.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　规划编制机关对可能造成不良环境影响并直接涉及公众环境权益的专项规划，应当在规划草案报送审批前，采取调查问卷、座谈会、论证会、听证会等形式，公开征求有关单位、专家和公众对环境影响报告书的意见。但是，依法需要保密的除外。  
有关单位、专家和公众的意见与环境影响评价结论有重大分歧的，规划编制机关应当采取论证会、听证会等形式进一步论证。  
规划编制机关应当在报送审查的环境影响报告书中附具对公众意见采纳与不采纳情况及其理由的说明。

**Article 13** For a special plan that may cause adverse environmental impact and directly involve the environmental rights and interests of the public, the plan compilation authority shall, before submitting the plan draft for approval, publicly solicit comments from the relevant units, experts and the public on the environmental impact report through questionnaires, seminars, feasibility study meetings, public hearings and other forms, unless confidentiality is required by law.  
If the comments of the relevant units, experts and the public are significantly different from the conclusions of the environmental impact assessment, the plan compilation authority shall conduct further feasibility studies through feasibility study meetings, public hearings and other forms.  
The plan compilation authority shall attach to the environmental impact report submitted for review a statement explaining the adoption or non-adoption of public comments and the reasons therefor.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对已经批准的规划在实施范围、适用期限、规模、结构和布局等方面进行重大调整或者修订的，规划编制机关应当依照本条例的规定重新或者补充进行环境影响评价。

**Article 14** Where a plan that has been approved is substantially adjusted or revised in terms of implementation scope, applicable time limit, scale, structure, layout, etc., the plan compilation authority shall re-conduct or supplement the environmental impact assessment in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

### 第三章　审查

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　规划编制机关在报送审批综合性规划草案和专项规划中的指导性规划草案时，应当将环境影响篇章或者说明作为规划草案的组成部分一并报送规划审批机关。未编写环境影响篇章或者说明的，规划审批机关应当要求其补充；未补充的，规划审批机关不予审批。

**Article 15** When submitting a draft comprehensive plan or a draft guiding plan within a special plan for approval, the plan compilation authority shall submit the environmental impact chapter or statement as a component part of the plan draft to the plan approval authority. If no environmental impact chapter or statement is prepared, the plan approval authority shall require the plan compilation authority to supplement it; if it is not supplemented, the plan approval authority shall not approve the plan.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　规划编制机关在报送审批专项规划草案时，应当将环境影响报告书一并附送规划审批机关审查；未附送环境影响报告书的，规划审批机关应当要求其补充；未补充的，规划审批机关不予审批。

**Article 16** When submitting a draft special plan for approval, the plan compilation authority shall attach the environmental impact report to the plan approval authority for review; if no environmental impact report is attached, the plan approval authority shall require the plan compilation authority to supplement it; if it is not supplemented, the plan approval authority shall not approve the plan.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　设区的市级以上人民政府审批的专项规划，在审批前由其环境保护主管部门召集有关部门代表和专家组成审查小组，对环境影响报告书进行审查。审查小组应当提交书面审查意见。  
省级以上人民政府有关部门审批的专项规划，其环境影响报告书的审查办法，由国务院环境保护主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 17** For a special plan approved by a people's government at or above the city level with districts, the competent department of environmental protection of that government shall, before approval, convene representatives of the relevant departments and experts to form a review panel to review the environmental impact report. The review panel shall submit written review opinions.  
For a special plan approved by a relevant department of a people's government at or above the provincial level, the review method for its environmental impact report shall be formulated by the competent department of environmental protection of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　审查小组的专家应当从依法设立的专家库内相关专业的专家名单中随机抽取。但是，参与环境影响报告书编制的专家，不得作为该环境影响报告书审查小组的成员。  
审查小组中专家人数不得少于审查小组总人数的二分之一；少于二分之一的，审查小组的审查意见无效。

**Article 18** The experts of the review panel shall be randomly selected from the list of experts in relevant specialties within the expert database established according to law. However, an expert who participated in the preparation of the environmental impact report shall not serve as a member of the review panel for that environmental impact report.  
The number of experts in the review panel shall not be less than one-half of the total number of members of the review panel; if it is less than one-half, the review opinions of the review panel shall be null and void.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　审查小组的成员应当客观、公正、独立地对环境影响报告书提出书面审查意见，规划审批机关、规划编制机关、审查小组的召集部门不得干预。  
审查意见应当包括下列内容：  
(一)基础资料、数据的真实性；  
(二)评价方法的适当性；  
(三)环境影响分析、预测和评估的可靠性；  
(四)预防或者减轻不良环境影响的对策和措施的合理性和有效性；  
(五)公众意见采纳与不采纳情况及其理由的说明的合理性；  
(六)环境影响评价结论的科学性。  
审查意见应当经审查小组四分之三以上成员签字同意。审查小组成员有不同意见的，应当如实记录和反映。

**Article 19** Members of the review panel shall submit written review opinions on the environmental impact report objectively, fairly and independently, and the plan approval authority, the plan compilation authority and the department convening the review panel shall not interfere.  
The review opinions shall include the following:  
(1) the authenticity of the basic materials and data;  
(2) the appropriateness of the evaluation method;  
(3) the reliability of the environmental impact analysis, prediction and assessment;  
(4) the reasonableness and effectiveness of the measures and countermeasures to prevent or mitigate adverse environmental impact;  
(5) the reasonableness of the statement explaining the adoption or non-adoption of public comments and the reasons therefor; and  
(6) the scientific nature of the conclusions of the environmental impact assessment.  
The review opinions shall be approved by at least three-quarters of the members of the review panel by signing. If any member of the review panel has a dissenting opinion, it shall be truthfully recorded and reflected.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　有下列情形之一的，审查小组应当提出对环境影响报告书进行修改并重新审查的意见：  
(一)基础资料、数据失实的；  
(二)评价方法选择不当的；  
(三)对不良环境影响的分析、预测和评估不准确、不深入，需要进一步论证的；  
(四)预防或者减轻不良环境影响的对策和措施存在严重缺陷的；  
(五)环境影响评价结论不明确、不合理或者错误的；  
(六)未附具对公众意见采纳与不采纳情况及其理由的说明，或者不采纳公众意见的理由明显不合理的；  
(七)内容存在其他重大缺陷或者遗漏的。

**Article 20** Under any of the following circumstances, the review panel shall propose an opinion that the environmental impact report be modified and re-reviewed:  
(1) the basic materials and data are false;  
(2) the evaluation method is improperly selected;  
(3) the analysis, prediction and assessment of adverse environmental impact are inaccurate or insufficient, and further feasibility study is required;  
(4) the measures and countermeasures to prevent or mitigate adverse environmental impact have serious defects;  
(5) the conclusions of the environmental impact assessment are unclear, unreasonable or erroneous;  
(6) no statement explaining the adoption or non-adoption of public comments and the reasons therefor is attached, or the reasons for non-adoption of public comments are obviously unreasonable; or  
(7) there are other major defects or omissions in the content.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　有下列情形之一的，审查小组应当提出不予通过环境影响报告书的意见：  
(一)依据现有知识水平和技术条件，对规划实施可能产生的不良环境影响的程度或者范围不能作出科学判断的；  
(二)规划实施可能造成重大不良环境影响，并且无法提出切实可行的预防或者减轻对策和措施的。

**Article 21** Under any of the following circumstances, the review panel shall propose an opinion that the environmental impact report be disapproved:  
(1) given the existing level of knowledge and technical conditions, a scientific judgment cannot be made on the degree or scope of the possible adverse environmental impact of the implementation of the plan; or  
(2) the implementation of the plan may cause major adverse environmental impact, and no practical and feasible countermeasures or measures to prevent or mitigate such impact can be proposed.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　规划审批机关在审批专项规划草案时，应当将环境影响报告书结论以及审查意见作为决策的重要依据。  
规划审批机关对环境影响报告书结论以及审查意见不予采纳的，应当逐项就不予采纳的理由作出书面说明，并存档备查。有关单位、专家和公众可以申请查阅；但是，依法需要保密的除外。

**Article 22** When approving a draft special plan, the plan approval authority shall take the conclusions of the environmental impact report and the review opinions as important bases for decision-making.  
If the plan approval authority does not adopt the conclusions of the environmental impact report or the review opinions, it shall provide a written explanation item by item on the reasons for non-adoption, and keep it on file for future reference. The relevant units, experts and the public may apply for inspection, unless confidentiality is required by law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　已经进行环境影响评价的规划包含具体建设项目的，规划的环境影响评价结论应当作为建设项目环境影响评价的重要依据，建设项目环境影响评价的内容可以根据规划环境影响评价的分析论证情况予以简化。

**Article 23** Where a plan that has undergone environmental impact assessment contains specific construction projects, the conclusions of the environmental impact assessment of the plan shall serve as an important basis for the environmental impact assessment of the construction projects, and the content of the environmental impact assessment of the construction projects may be simplified based on the analysis and justification of the environmental impact assessment of the plan.

### 第四章　跟踪评价

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对环境有重大影响的规划实施后，规划编制机关应当及时组织规划环境影响的跟踪评价，将评价结果报告规划审批机关，并通报环境保护等有关部门。

**Article 24** After the implementation of a plan that has a major impact on the environment, the plan compilation authority shall promptly organize a follow-up evaluation of the plan's environmental impact, report the evaluation results to the plan approval authority, and notify the relevant departments such as the environmental protection department.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　规划环境影响的跟踪评价应当包括下列内容：  
(一)规划实施后实际产生的环境影响与环境影响评价文件预测可能产生的环境影响之间的比较分析和评估；  
(二)规划实施中所采取的预防或者减轻不良环境影响的对策和措施有效性的分析和评估；  
(三)公众对规划实施所产生的环境影响的意见；  
(四)跟踪评价的结论。

**Article 25** The follow-up evaluation of the plan's environmental impact shall include the following:  
(1) a comparative analysis and assessment between the actual environmental impact after implementation of the plan and the possible environmental impact predicted in the environmental impact assessment documents;  
(2) an analysis and assessment of the effectiveness of the countermeasures and measures taken during implementation of the plan to prevent or mitigate adverse environmental impact;  
(3) the opinions of the public on the environmental impact arising from the implementation of the plan; and  
(4) the conclusions of the follow-up evaluation.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　规划编制机关对规划环境影响进行跟踪评价，应当采取调查问卷、现场走访、座谈会等形式征求有关单位、专家和公众的意见。

**Article 26** When conducting a follow-up evaluation of the plan's environmental impact, the plan compilation authority shall solicit opinions from the relevant units, experts and the public by means such as questionnaires, on-site visits, and seminars.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　规划实施过程中产生重大不良环境影响的，规划编制机关应当及时提出改进措施，向规划审批机关报告，并通报环境保护等有关部门。

**Article 27** If a major adverse environmental impact arises during the implementation of a plan, the plan compilation authority shall promptly propose improvement measures, report to the plan approval authority, and notify the relevant departments such as the environmental protection department.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　环境保护主管部门发现规划实施过程中产生重大不良环境影响的，应当及时进行核查。经核查属实的，向规划审批机关提出采取改进措施或者修订规划的建议。

**Article 28** If the competent department of environmental protection discovers that a major adverse environmental impact arises during the implementation of a plan, it shall promptly conduct verification. If the matter is verified to be true, it shall propose to the plan approval authority suggestions for taking improvement measures or revising the plan.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　规划审批机关在接到规划编制机关的报告或者环境保护主管部门的建议后，应当及时组织论证，并根据论证结果采取改进措施或者对规划进行修订。

**Article 29** After receiving the report from the plan compilation authority or the suggestion from the competent department of environmental protection, the plan approval authority shall promptly organize a feasibility study and take improvement measures or revise the plan based on the results of the feasibility study.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　规划实施区域的重点污染物排放总量超过国家或者地方规定的总量控制指标的，应当暂停审批该规划实施区域内新增该重点污染物排放总量的建设项目的环境影响评价文件。

**Article 30** Where the total discharge amount of key pollutants in the area where a plan is implemented exceeds the total amount control target prescribed by the State or the locality, the environmental impact assessment documents for construction projects that would add to the total discharge amount of such key pollutants in that area shall be suspended from approval.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　规划编制机关在组织环境影响评价时弄虚作假或者有失职行为，造成环境影响评价严重失实的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 31** If the plan compilation authority engages in fraud or dereliction of duty when organizing the environmental impact assessment, causing the environmental impact assessment to be seriously inaccurate, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　规划审批机关有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分：  
(一)对依法应当编写而未编写环境影响篇章或者说明的综合性规划草案和专项规划中的指导性规划草案，予以批准的；  
(二)对依法应当附送而未附送环境影响报告书的专项规划草案，或者对环境影响报告书未经审查小组审查的专项规划草案，予以批准的。

**Article 32** If the plan approval authority commits any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) approving a draft comprehensive plan or a draft guiding plan within a special plan for which an environmental impact chapter or statement should have been prepared according to law but was not prepared; or  
(2) approving a draft special plan for which an environmental impact report should have been attached according to law but was not attached, or approving a draft special plan for which the environmental impact report has not been reviewed by the review panel.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　审查小组的召集部门在组织环境影响报告书审查时弄虚作假或者滥用职权，造成环境影响评价严重失实的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。  
审查小组的专家在环境影响报告书审查中弄虚作假或者有失职行为，造成环境影响评价严重失实的，由设立专家库的环境保护主管部门取消其入选专家库的资格并予以公告；审查小组的部门代表有上述行为的，依法给予处分。

**Article 33** If the department convening the review panel engages in fraud or abuses its powers when organizing the review of the environmental impact report, causing the environmental impact assessment to be seriously inaccurate, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.  
If an expert of the review panel engages in fraud or dereliction of duty during the review of the environmental impact report, causing the environmental impact assessment to be seriously inaccurate, the competent department of environmental protection that established the expert database shall disqualify such expert from the expert database and make a public announcement; if a department representative of the review panel commits the above-mentioned act, sanctions shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　规划环境影响评价技术机构弄虚作假或者有失职行为，造成环境影响评价文件严重失实的，由国务院环境保护主管部门予以通报，处所收费用1倍以上3倍以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 34** If a technical institution for environmental impact assessment of plans engages in fraud or dereliction of duty, causing the environmental impact assessment documents to be seriously inaccurate, the competent department of environmental protection of the State Council shall circulate a notice of criticism and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the fees collected; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本地的实际情况，要求本行政区域内的县级人民政府对其组织编制的规划进行环境影响评价。具体办法由省、自治区、直辖市参照《中华人民共和国环境影响评价法》和本条例的规定制定。

**Article 35** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in light of the actual local conditions, require the people's governments at the county level within their administrative regions to conduct environmental impact assessment of the plans they organize to compile. Specific measures shall be formulated by the provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government by reference to the Environmental Impact Assessment Law of the People's Republic of China and the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本条例自2009年10月1日起施行。

**Article 36** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 1, 2009.
