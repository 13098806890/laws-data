# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部关于办理刑事案件严格排除非法证据若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2017-06-20  
**生效日期**：2017-06-27

> （2017年6月20日发布 自2017年6月27日起施行 法发〔2017〕15号） 为准确惩罚犯罪，切实保障人权，规范司法行为，促进司法公正，根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》及有关司法解释等规定，结合司法实际，制定如下规定。 一、一般规定 第一条 严禁刑讯逼供和以威胁、引诱、欺骗以及其他非法方法收集证据，不得强迫任何人证实自己有罪。对一切案件的判处都要重证据，重调查研究，不轻信口供。 第二条 采取殴打、违法使用戒具等暴力方法或者变相肉刑的恶劣手段，使犯罪嫌疑人、被告人遭受难以忍受的痛苦而违背意愿作出的供述，应当予以排除。 第三条 采用以暴力或者严重损害本人及其近亲属合法权益等进行威胁的方法，使犯罪嫌疑人、被告人遭受难以忍受的痛苦而违背意愿作出的供述，应当予以排除。 第四条 采用非法拘禁等非法限制人身自由的方法收集的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人供述，应当予以排除。 第五条 采用刑讯逼供方法使犯罪嫌疑人、被告人作出供述，之后犯罪嫌疑人、被告人受该刑讯逼供行为影响而作出的与该供述相同的重复性供述，应当一并排除，但下列情形除外： （一）侦查期间，根据控告、举报或者自己发现等，侦查机关确认或者不能排除以非法方法收集证据而更换侦查人员，其他侦查人员再次讯问时告知诉讼权利和认罪的法律后果，犯罪嫌疑人自愿供述的； （二）审查逮捕、审查起诉和审判期间，检察人员、审判人员讯问时告知诉讼权利和认罪的法律后果，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人自愿供述的。 第六条 采用暴力、威胁以及非法限制人身自由等非法方法收集的证人证言、被害人陈述，应当予以排除。 第七条 收集物证、书证不符合法定程序，可能严重影响司法公正的，应当予以补正或者作出合理解释；不能补正或者作出合理解释的，对有关证据应当予以排除。 二、侦查 第八条 侦查机关应当依照法定程序开展侦查，收集、调取能够证实犯罪嫌疑人有罪或者无罪、罪轻或者罪重的证据材料。 第九条 拘留、逮捕犯罪嫌疑人后，应当按照法律规定送看守所羁押。犯罪嫌疑人被送交看守所羁押后，讯问应当在看守所讯问室进行。因客观原因侦查机关在看守所讯问室以外的场所进行讯问的，应当作出合理解释。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 严禁刑讯逼供和以威胁、引诱、欺骗以及其他非法方法收集证据，不得强迫任何人证实自己有罪。对一切案件的判处都要重证据，重调查研究，不轻信口供。

**Article 1** : It shall be strictly prohibited to collect evidence by torture, threat, inducement, deception, or other illegal methods. No one shall be compelled to prove himself guilty. In adjudicating any case, importance shall be attached to evidence, investigation and research, and confessions shall not be readily believed.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 采取殴打、违法使用戒具等暴力方法或者变相肉刑的恶劣手段，使犯罪嫌疑人、被告人遭受难以忍受的痛苦而违背意愿作出的供述，应当予以排除。

**Article 2** : A confession made by a criminal suspect or defendant against his will by means of beating, illegal use of restraint devices, or other violent means or cruel methods of disguised corporal punishment, which causes him unbearable pain, shall be excluded.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 采用以暴力或者严重损害本人及其近亲属合法权益等进行威胁的方法，使犯罪嫌疑人、被告人遭受难以忍受的痛苦而违背意愿作出的供述，应当予以排除。

**Article 3** : A confession made by a criminal suspect or defendant against his will by means of threat, such as violence or serious harm to the lawful rights and interests of himself and his close relatives, which causes him unbearable pain, shall be excluded.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 采用非法拘禁等非法限制人身自由的方法收集的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人供述，应当予以排除。

**Article 4** : A confession of a criminal suspect or defendant collected by illegal restriction of personal freedom, such as illegal detention, shall be excluded.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 采用刑讯逼供方法使犯罪嫌疑人、被告人作出供述，之后犯罪嫌疑人、被告人受该刑讯逼供行为影响而作出的与该供述相同的重复性供述，应当一并排除，但下列情形除外：  
（一）侦查期间，根据控告、举报或者自己发现等，侦查机关确认或者不能排除以非法方法收集证据而更换侦查人员，其他侦查人员再次讯问时告知诉讼权利和认罪的法律后果，犯罪嫌疑人自愿供述的；  
（二）审查逮捕、审查起诉和审判期间，检察人员、审判人员讯问时告知诉讼权利和认罪的法律后果，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人自愿供述的。

**Article 5** : If a criminal suspect or defendant makes a confession by torture, and thereafter makes repeated confessions identical to the tortured confession under the influence of such torture, such repeated confessions shall also be excluded, except under the following circumstances:  
(1) During the investigation period, upon reports, complaints or self-discovery, the investigative organ confirms or cannot rule out that evidence was collected illegally, and after changing the investigator, other investigators, upon re-interrogation, inform the criminal suspect of his litigation rights and the legal consequences of confession, and the criminal suspect voluntarily confesses;  
(2) During the period of review of arrest, review of prosecution and trial, when the prosecutor or judge informs the criminal suspect or defendant of his litigation rights and the legal consequences of confession during interrogation, and the criminal suspect or defendant voluntarily confesses.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 采用暴力、威胁以及非法限制人身自由等非法方法收集的证人证言、被害人陈述，应当予以排除。

**Article 6** : Witness testimony and victim statements collected by illegal methods such as violence, threat, and illegal restriction of personal freedom shall be excluded.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 收集物证、书证不符合法定程序，可能严重影响司法公正的，应当予以补正或者作出合理解释；不能补正或者作出合理解释的，对有关证据应当予以排除。  
二、侦查

**Article 7** : If the collection of physical evidence or documentary evidence does not conform to legal procedures and may seriously affect judicial fairness, it shall be corrected or a reasonable explanation shall be provided; if it cannot be corrected or a reasonable explanation cannot be provided, the relevant evidence shall be excluded.  
II. Investigation

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 侦查机关应当依照法定程序开展侦查，收集、调取能够证实犯罪嫌疑人有罪或者无罪、罪轻或者罪重的证据材料。

**Article 8** : An investigative organ shall carry out investigation in accordance with legal procedures, and collect and obtain evidence materials that can prove the criminal suspect's guilt or innocence, or whether the crime is serious or minor.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 拘留、逮捕犯罪嫌疑人后，应当按照法律规定送看守所羁押。犯罪嫌疑人被送交看守所羁押后，讯问应当在看守所讯问室进行。因客观原因侦查机关在看守所讯问室以外的场所进行讯问的，应当作出合理解释。

**Article 9** : After detaining or arresting a criminal suspect, he shall be sent to a detention center for custody pursuant to legal provisions. After a criminal suspect is sent to a detention center for custody, interrogation shall be conducted in the interrogation room of the detention center. If, due to objective reasons, the investigative organ conducts interrogation in a place other than the interrogation room of the detention center, a reasonable explanation shall be provided.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 侦查人员在讯问犯罪嫌疑人的时候，可以对讯问过程进行录音录像；对于可能判处无期徒刑、死刑的案件或者其他重大犯罪案件，应当对讯问过程进行录音录像。  
侦查人员应当告知犯罪嫌疑人对讯问过程录音录像，并在讯问笔录中写明。

**Article 10** : When interrogating a criminal suspect, an investigator may make audio and video recordings of the interrogation process; for cases that may be punished with life imprisonment, the death penalty, or other major criminal cases, the interrogation process shall be audio and video recorded.  
An investigator shall inform the criminal suspect that the interrogation process is being audio and video recorded, and record this in the interrogation record.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 对讯问过程录音录像，应当不间断进行，保持完整性，不得选择性地录制，不得剪接、删改。

**Article 11** : The audio and video recording of the interrogation process shall be conducted continuously, maintaining integrity, and shall not be selectively recorded, edited, or deleted.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 侦查人员讯问犯罪嫌疑人，应当依法制作讯问笔录。讯问笔录应当交犯罪嫌疑人核对，对于没有阅读能力的，应当向他宣读。对讯问笔录中有遗漏或者差错等情形，犯罪嫌疑人可以提出补充或者改正。

**Article 12** : When interrogating a criminal suspect, an investigator shall make an interrogation record according to law. The interrogation record shall be checked by the criminal suspect; if he is unable to read, it shall be read to him. If there are omissions or errors in the interrogation record, the criminal suspect may make supplements or corrections.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 看守所应当对提讯进行登记，写明提讯单位、人员、事由、起止时间以及犯罪嫌疑人姓名等情况。  
看守所收押犯罪嫌疑人，应当进行身体检查。检查时，人民检察院驻看守所检察人员可以在场。检查发现犯罪嫌疑人有伤或者身体异常的，看守所应当拍照或者录像，分别由送押人员、犯罪嫌疑人说明原因，并在体检记录中写明，由送押人员、收押人员和犯罪嫌疑人签字确认。

**Article 13** : The detention center shall register each interrogation, recording the unit, personnel, reason, start and end time of the interrogation, and the name of the criminal suspect.  
The detention center shall conduct a physical examination when taking a criminal suspect into custody. During the examination, a procurator stationed at the detention center by the People's Procuratorate may be present. If the examination reveals that the criminal suspect has injuries or abnormal physical conditions, the detention center shall take photos or make video recordings, obtain explanations from the escorting personnel and the criminal suspect respectively, and record them in the physical examination record, which shall be signed and confirmed by the escorting personnel, the admitting personnel, and the criminal suspect.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 犯罪嫌疑人及其辩护人在侦查期间可以向人民检察院申请排除非法证据。对犯罪嫌疑人及其辩护人提供相关线索或者材料的，人民检察院应当调查核实。调查结论应当书面告知犯罪嫌疑人及其辩护人。对确有以非法方法收集证据情形的，人民检察院应当向侦查机关提出纠正意见。  
侦查机关对审查认定的非法证据，应当予以排除，不得作为提请批准逮捕、移送审查起诉的根据。  
对重大案件，人民检察院驻看守所检察人员应当在侦查终结前询问犯罪嫌疑人，核查是否存在刑讯逼供、非法取证情形，并同步录音录像。经核查，确有刑讯逼供、非法取证情形的，侦查机关应当及时排除非法证据，不得作为提请批准逮捕、移送审查起诉的根据。

**Article 14** : During the investigation period, a criminal suspect and his defender may apply to the People's Procuratorate for the exclusion of illegal evidence. If the criminal suspect and his defender provide relevant clues or materials, the People's Procuratorate shall investigate and verify. The investigation conclusion shall be notified in writing to the criminal suspect and his defender. If there is indeed a situation of illegal evidence collection, the People's Procuratorate shall issue a corrective opinion to the investigative organ.  
The investigative organ shall exclude the illegal evidence confirmed upon review, and shall not use it as a basis for requesting approval of arrest or transferring for review of prosecution.  
For major cases, before the conclusion of the investigation, the procurator stationed at the detention center by the People's Procuratorate shall interview the criminal suspect and verify whether there is torture or illegal evidence collection, and make synchronous audio and video recordings. If, after verification, there is indeed torture or illegal evidence collection, the investigative organ shall promptly exclude the illegal evidence and shall not use it as a basis for requesting approval of arrest or transferring for review of prosecution.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 对侦查终结的案件，侦查机关应当全面审查证明证据收集合法性的证据材料，依法排除非法证据。排除非法证据后，证据不足的，不得移送审查起诉。  
侦查机关发现办案人员非法取证的，应当依法作出处理，并可另行指派侦查人员重新调查取证。  
三、审查逮捕、审查起诉

**Article 15** : For a case concluded after investigation, the investigative organ shall comprehensively review the evidence materials proving the legality of evidence collection and exclude illegal evidence according to law. If after excluding illegal evidence, the evidence is insufficient, the case shall not be transferred for review of prosecution.  
If the investigative organ discovers that the case-handling personnel have illegally collected evidence, it shall handle the matter according to law and may assign other investigators to conduct a new investigation to collect evidence.  
III. Review of Arrest, Review of Prosecution

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 审查逮捕、审查起诉期间讯问犯罪嫌疑人，应当告知其有权申请排除非法证据，并告知诉讼权利和认罪的法律后果。

**Article 16** : When interrogating a criminal suspect during the period of review of arrest and review of prosecution, he shall be informed of his right to apply for the exclusion of illegal evidence, and shall be informed of his litigation rights and the legal consequences of confession.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 审查逮捕、审查起诉期间，犯罪嫌疑人及其辩护人申请排除非法证据，并提供相关线索或者材料的，人民检察院应当调查核实。调查结论应当书面告知犯罪嫌疑人及其辩护人。  
人民检察院在审查起诉期间发现侦查人员以刑讯逼供等非法方法收集证据的，应当依法排除相关证据并提出纠正意见，必要时人民检察院可以自行调查取证。  
人民检察院对审查认定的非法证据，应当予以排除，不得作为批准或者决定逮捕、提起公诉的根据。被排除的非法证据应当随案移送，并写明为依法排除的非法证据。

**Article 17** : During the period of review of arrest and review of prosecution, if a criminal suspect and his defender apply for the exclusion of illegal evidence and provide relevant clues or materials, the People's Procuratorate shall investigate and verify. The investigation conclusion shall be notified in writing to the criminal suspect and his defender.  
If during the review of prosecution, the People's Procuratorate discovers that investigators have collected evidence by illegal methods such as torture, it shall exclude the relevant evidence according to law and issue a corrective opinion. If necessary, the People's Procuratorate may independently investigate and collect evidence.  
The People's Procuratorate shall exclude the illegal evidence confirmed upon review, and shall not use it as a basis for approving or deciding on arrest or initiating public prosecution. The excluded illegal evidence shall be transferred with the case and noted as illegal evidence excluded according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 人民检察院依法排除非法证据后，证据不足，不符合逮捕、起诉条件的，不得批准或者决定逮捕、提起公诉。  
对于人民检察院排除有关证据导致对涉嫌的重要犯罪事实未予认定，从而作出不批准逮捕、不起诉决定，或者对涉嫌的部分重要犯罪事实决定不起诉的，公安机关、国家安全机关可要求复议、提请复核。  
四、辩护

**Article 18** : After the People's Procuratorate excludes illegal evidence according to law, if the evidence is insufficient and does not meet the conditions for arrest or prosecution, it shall not approve or decide on arrest or initiate public prosecution.  
If the exclusion of relevant evidence by the People's Procuratorate results in the failure to identify the suspected major criminal facts, thereby making a decision not to approve arrest or not to prosecute, or deciding not to prosecute part of the suspected major criminal facts, the public security organ or the national security organ may request reconsideration or submit for review.  
IV. Defense

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 犯罪嫌疑人、被告人申请提供法律援助的，应当按照有关规定指派法律援助律师。  
法律援助值班律师可以为犯罪嫌疑人、被告人提供法律帮助，对刑讯逼供、非法取证情形代理申诉、控告。

**Article 19** : If a criminal suspect or defendant applies for legal aid, a legal aid lawyer shall be assigned in accordance with relevant provisions.  
The on-duty legal aid lawyer may provide legal assistance to the criminal suspect or defendant, and may represent him in petitions and complaints regarding torture and illegal evidence collection.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 犯罪嫌疑人、被告人及其辩护人申请排除非法证据，应当提供涉嫌非法取证的人员、时间、地点、方式、内容等相关线索或者材料。

**Article 20** : When a criminal suspect, defendant, or his defender applies for the exclusion of illegal evidence, he shall provide relevant clues or materials such as the personnel, time, location, method, and content suspected of illegal evidence collection.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条 辩护律师自人民检察院对案件审查起诉之日起，可以查阅、摘抄、复制讯问笔录、提讯登记、采取强制措施或者侦查措施的法律文书等证据材料。其他辩护人经人民法院、人民检察院许可，也可以查阅、摘抄、复制上述证据材料。

**Article 21** : From the date the People's Procuratorate reviews the case for prosecution, the defense lawyer may consult, extract, and copy evidence materials such as interrogation records, interrogation registration forms, legal documents concerning compulsory measures or investigative measures. Other defenders, with the permission of the People's Court or the People's Procuratorate, may also consult, extract, and copy the above evidence materials.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条 犯罪嫌疑人、被告人及其辩护人向人民法院、人民检察院申请调取公安机关、国家安全机关、人民检察院收集但未提交的讯问录音录像、体检记录等证据材料，人民法院、人民检察院经审查认为犯罪嫌疑人、被告人及其辩护人申请调取的证据材料与证明证据收集的合法性有联系的，应当予以调取；认为与证明证据收集的合法性没有联系的，应当决定不予调取并向犯罪嫌疑人、被告人及其辩护人说明理由。  
五、审判

**Article 22** : If a criminal suspect, defendant, or his defender applies to the People's Court or the People's Procuratorate for obtaining evidence materials such as interrogation audio and video recordings and physical examination records collected but not submitted by the public security organ, national security organ, or the People's Procuratorate, and upon review, the People's Court or the People's Procuratorate finds that the evidence materials applied for are related to proving the legality of evidence collection, it shall obtain them; if it finds that they are not related to proving the legality of evidence collection, it shall decide not to obtain them and explain the reasons to the criminal suspect, defendant, or his defender.  
V. Trial

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条 人民法院向被告人及其辩护人送达起诉书副本时，应当告知其有权申请排除非法证据。  
被告人及其辩护人申请排除非法证据，应当在开庭审理前提出，但在庭审期间发现相关线索或者材料等情形除外。人民法院应当在开庭审理前将申请书和相关线索或者材料的复制件送交人民检察院。

**Article 23** : When the People's Court serves a copy of the indictment on the defendant and his defender, it shall inform them of their right to apply for the exclusion of illegal evidence.  
The defendant and his defender shall apply for the exclusion of illegal evidence before the court session, except when relevant clues or materials are discovered during the trial. The People's Court shall, before the court session, send copies of the application and relevant clues or materials to the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条 被告人及其辩护人在开庭审理前申请排除非法证据，未提供相关线索或者材料，不符合法律规定的申请条件的，人民法院对申请不予受理。

**Article 24** : If the defendant and his defender apply for exclusion of illegal evidence before the court session but fail to provide relevant clues or materials and do not meet the legal conditions for application, the People's Court shall not accept the application.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条 被告人及其辩护人在开庭审理前申请排除非法证据，按照法律规定提供相关线索或者材料的，人民法院应当召开庭前会议。人民检察院应当通过出示有关证据材料等方式，有针对性地对证据收集的合法性作出说明。人民法院可以核实情况，听取意见。  
人民检察院可以决定撤回有关证据，撤回的证据，没有新的理由，不得在庭审中出示。  
被告人及其辩护人可以撤回排除非法证据的申请。撤回申请后，没有新的线索或者材料，不得再次对有关证据提出排除申请。

**Article 25** : If the defendant and his defender apply for exclusion of illegal evidence before the court session and provide relevant clues or materials in accordance with the law, the People's Court shall convene a pre-trial conference. The People's Procuratorate shall, by presenting relevant evidence materials, make a targeted explanation of the legality of evidence collection. The People's Court may verify the situation and hear opinions.  
The People's Procuratorate may decide to withdraw relevant evidence; the withdrawn evidence shall not be presented in court without new reasons.  
The defendant and his defender may withdraw the application for exclusion of illegal evidence. After withdrawal, without new clues or materials, they shall not apply again for exclusion of the relevant evidence.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条 公诉人、被告人及其辩护人在庭前会议中对证据收集是否合法未达成一致意见，人民法院对证据收集的合法性有疑问的，应当在庭审中进行调查；人民法院对证据收集的合法性没有疑问，且没有新的线索或者材料表明可能存在非法取证的，可以决定不再进行调查。

**Article 26** : If the public prosecutor, the defendant, and his defender fail to reach an agreement on the legality of evidence collection at the pre-trial conference, and the People's Court has doubts about the legality of evidence collection, it shall conduct an investigation during the trial; if the People's Court has no doubts about the legality of evidence collection and there are no new clues or materials indicating that illegal evidence collection may exist, it may decide not to conduct an investigation.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条 被告人及其辩护人申请人民法院通知侦查人员或者其他人员出庭，人民法院认为现有证据材料不能证明证据收集的合法性，确有必要通知上述人员出庭作证或者说明情况的，可以通知上述人员出庭。

**Article 27** : If the defendant and his defender apply to the People's Court to have investigators or other personnel appear in court, and the People's Court considers that the existing evidence materials cannot prove the legality of evidence collection and it is truly necessary to have the above-mentioned personnel testify or explain the situation in court, it may notify the above-mentioned personnel to appear in court.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条 公诉人宣读起诉书后，法庭应当宣布开庭审理前对证据收集合法性的审查及处理情况。

**Article 28** : After the public prosecutor reads the indictment, the court shall announce the review and handling of the legality of evidence collection before the court session.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条 被告人及其辩护人在开庭审理前未申请排除非法证据，在法庭审理过程中提出申请的，应当说明理由。  
对前述情形，法庭经审查，对证据收集的合法性有疑问的，应当进行调查；没有疑问的，应当驳回申请。  
法庭驳回排除非法证据申请后，被告人及其辩护人没有新的线索或者材料，以相同理由再次提出申请的，法庭不再审查。

**Article 29** : If the defendant and his defender did not apply for exclusion of illegal evidence before the court session but submit an application during the trial, they shall explain the reasons.  
For the aforementioned situation, if the court, upon review, has doubts about the legality of evidence collection, it shall conduct an investigation; if there are no doubts, it shall reject the application.  
After the court rejects the application for exclusion of illegal evidence, if the defendant and his defender, without new clues or materials, submit the application again on the same grounds, the court shall not review it again.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条 庭审期间，法庭决定对证据收集的合法性进行调查的，应当先行当庭调查。但为防止庭审过分迟延，也可以在法庭调查结束前进行调查。

**Article 30** : During the trial, if the court decides to investigate the legality of evidence collection, it shall first conduct the investigation in court. However, to prevent excessive delay of the trial, the investigation may also be conducted before the conclusion of the court investigation.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条 公诉人对证据收集的合法性加以证明，可以出示讯问笔录、提讯登记、体检记录、采取强制措施或者侦查措施的法律文书、侦查终结前对讯问合法性的核查材料等证据材料，有针对性地播放讯问录音录像，提请法庭通知侦查人员或者其他人员出庭说明情况。  
被告人及其辩护人可以出示相关线索或者材料，并申请法庭播放特定时段的讯问录音录像。  
侦查人员或者其他人员出庭，应当向法庭说明证据收集过程，并就相关情况接受发问。对发问方式不当或者内容与证据收集的合法性无关的，法庭应当制止。  
公诉人、被告人及其辩护人可以对证据收集的合法性进行质证、辩论。

**Article 31** : To prove the legality of evidence collection, the public prosecutor may present evidence materials such as interrogation records, interrogation registration forms, physical examination records, legal documents concerning compulsory measures or investigative measures, and verification materials of the legality of interrogations before the conclusion of investigation, play audio and video recordings of interrogations in a targeted manner, and request the court to notify investigators or other personnel to appear in court to explain the situation.  
The defendant and his defender may present relevant clues or materials and request the court to play audio and video recordings of interrogations for specific periods.  
When investigators or other personnel appear in court, they shall explain the process of evidence collection to the court and be subject to questioning on the relevant circumstances. If the manner of questioning is improper or the content is irrelevant to the legality of evidence collection, the court shall stop it.  
The public prosecutor, the defendant, and his defender may cross-examine and debate the legality of evidence collection.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条 法庭对控辩双方提供的证据有疑问的，可以宣布休庭，对证据进行调查核实。必要时，可以通知公诉人、辩护人到场。

**Article 32** : If the court has doubts about the evidence provided by the prosecution and defense, it may adjourn the court session to investigate and verify the evidence. If necessary, it may notify the public prosecutor and the defender to be present.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条 法庭对证据收集的合法性进行调查后，应当当庭作出是否排除有关证据的决定。必要时，可以宣布休庭，由合议庭评议或者提交审判委员会讨论，再次开庭时宣布决定。  
在法庭作出是否排除有关证据的决定前，不得对有关证据宣读、质证。

**Article 33** : After investigating the legality of evidence collection, the court shall make a decision in court on whether to exclude the relevant evidence. If necessary, it may adjourn the court session for the collegial panel to deliberate or submit to the judicial committee for discussion, and announce the decision when the court session resumes.  
Before the court makes a decision on whether to exclude the relevant evidence, the relevant evidence shall not be read out or cross-examined.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条 经法庭审理，确认存在本规定所规定的以非法方法收集证据情形的，对有关证据应当予以排除。法庭根据相关线索或者材料对证据收集的合法性有疑问，而人民检察院未提供证据或者提供的证据不能证明证据收集的合法性，不能排除存在本规定所规定的以非法方法收集证据情形的，对有关证据应当予以排除。  
对依法予以排除的证据，不得宣读、质证，不得作为判决的根据。

**Article 34** : If after the trial, the court confirms the existence of circumstances of illegally collecting evidence as stipulated in these Provisions, the relevant evidence shall be excluded. If the court has doubts about the legality of evidence collection based on relevant clues or materials, and the People's Procuratorate fails to provide evidence or the provided evidence cannot prove the legality of evidence collection, and it cannot be ruled out that there are circumstances of illegally collecting evidence as stipulated in these Provisions, the relevant evidence shall be excluded.  
The evidence excluded according to law shall not be read out, cross-examined, or used as a basis for judgment.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条 人民法院排除非法证据后，案件事实清楚，证据确实、充分，依据法律认定被告人有罪的，应当作出有罪判决；证据不足，不能认定被告人有罪的，应当作出证据不足、指控的犯罪不能成立的无罪判决；案件部分事实清楚，证据确实、充分的，依法认定该部分事实。

**Article 35** : After the People's Court excludes illegal evidence, if the facts of the case are clear and the evidence is credible and sufficient, and the defendant is found guilty according to law, a guilty verdict shall be rendered; if the evidence is insufficient to find the defendant guilty, a verdict of not guilty due to insufficient evidence and the charged crime not being established shall be rendered; if some facts of the case are clear and the evidence is credible and sufficient, those facts shall be determined according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条 人民法院对证据收集合法性的审查、调查结论，应当在裁判文书中写明，并说明理由。

**Article 36** : The People's Court's review and investigation conclusions on the legality of evidence collection shall be stated in the judgment document, with reasons explained.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条 人民法院对证人证言、被害人陈述等证据收集合法性的审查、调查，参照上述规定。

**Article 37** : The People's Court's review and investigation of the legality of collection of witness testimony, victim statements, and other evidence shall be conducted by reference to the above provisions.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条 人民检察院、被告人及其法定代理人提出抗诉、上诉，对第一审人民法院有关证据收集合法性的审查、调查结论提出异议的，第二审人民法院应当审查。  
被告人及其辩护人在第一审程序中未申请排除非法证据，在第二审程序中提出申请的，应当说明理由。第二审人民法院应当审查。  
人民检察院在第一审程序中未出示证据证明证据收集的合法性，第一审人民法院依法排除有关证据的，人民检察院在第二审程序中不得出示之前未出示的证据，但在第一审程序后发现的除外。

**Article 38** : Where the People's Procuratorate, the defendant, or his legal representative files a protest or appeal, raising objections to the review and investigation conclusions of the first-instance People's Court on the legality of evidence collection, the second-instance People's Court shall review the matter.  
If the defendant and his defender did not apply for exclusion of illegal evidence in the first-instance proceedings but submit an application in the second-instance proceedings, they shall explain the reasons. The second-instance People's Court shall review the matter.  
If the People's Procuratorate did not present evidence to prove the legality of evidence collection in the first-instance proceedings, and the first-instance People's Court excluded the relevant evidence according to law, the People's Procuratorate shall not present the previously unpresented evidence in the second-instance proceedings, except for evidence discovered after the first-instance proceedings.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条 第二审人民法院对证据收集合法性的调查，参照上述第一审程序的规定。

**Article 39** : The investigation by the second-instance People's Court into the legality of evidence collection shall be conducted by reference to the provisions on first-instance procedures above.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条 第一审人民法院对被告人及其辩护人排除非法证据的申请未予审查，并以有关证据作为定案根据，可能影响公正审判的，第二审人民法院可以裁定撤销原判，发回原审人民法院重新审判。  
一审人民法院对依法应当排除的非法证据未予排除的，第二审人民法院可以依法排除非法证据。排除非法证据后，原判决认定事实和适用法律正确、量刑适当的，应当裁定驳回上诉或者抗诉，维持原判；原判决认定事实没有错误，但适用法律有错误，或者量刑不当的，应当改判；原判决事实不清楚或者证据不足的，可以裁定撤销原判，发回原审人民法院重新审判。

**Article 40** : If the first-instance People's Court did not review the defendant and his defender's application for exclusion of illegal evidence and used the relevant evidence as the basis for the verdict, which may affect the fairness of the trial, the second-instance People's Court may rule to set aside the original judgment and remand the case to the original trial People's Court for retrial.  
If the first-instance People's Court failed to exclude illegal evidence that should have been excluded according to law, the second-instance People's Court may exclude the illegal evidence according to law. After excluding the illegal evidence, if the original judgment correctly determined the facts and applied the law, and the sentence was appropriate, the second-instance People's Court shall rule to dismiss the appeal or protest and affirm the original judgment; if the original judgment correctly determined the facts but applied the law incorrectly or the sentence was inappropriate, it shall amend the judgment; if the facts in the original judgment are unclear or the evidence is insufficient, it may rule to set aside the original judgment and remand the case to the original trial People's Court for retrial.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条 审判监督程序、死刑复核程序中对证据收集合法性的审查、调查，参照上述规定。

**Article 41** : The review and investigation of the legality of evidence collection in the procedures for trial supervision and death penalty review shall be conducted by reference to the above provisions.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条 本规定自2017年6月27日起施行。

**Article 42** : These Provisions shall come into force as of June 27, 2017.
