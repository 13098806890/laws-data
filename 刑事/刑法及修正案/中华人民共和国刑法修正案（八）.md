# 中华人民共和国刑法修正案（八）

**分类**：修正案  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2011-02-25  
**生效日期**：2011-05-01

> （2011年2月25日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十九次会议通过） 一、在刑法第十七条后增加一条，作为第十七条之一:“已满七十五周岁的人故意犯罪的，可以从轻或者减轻处罚；过失犯罪的，应当从轻或者减轻处罚。” 二、在刑法第三十八条中增加一款作为第二款:“判处管制，可以根据犯罪情况，同时禁止犯罪分子在执行期间从事特定活动，进入特定区域、场所，接触特定的人。” 原第二款作为第三款，修改为:“对判处管制的犯罪分子，依法实行社区矫正。” 增加一款作为第四款:“违反第二款规定的禁止令的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚。” 三、在刑法第四十九条中增加一款作为第二款:“审判的时候已满七十五周岁的人，不适用死刑，但以特别残忍手段致人死亡的除外。” 四、将刑法第五十条修改为:“判处死刑缓期执行的，在死刑缓期执行期间，如果没有故意犯罪，二年期满以后，减为无期徒刑；如果确有重大立功表现，二年期满以后，减为二十五年有期徒刑；如果故意犯罪，查证属实的，由最高人民法院核准，执行死刑。 “对被判处死刑缓期执行的累犯以及因故意杀人、强奸、抢劫、绑架、放火、爆炸、投放危险物质或者有组织的暴力性犯罪被判处死刑缓期执行的犯罪分子，人民法院根据犯罪情节等情况可以同时决定对其限制减刑。” 五、将刑法第六十三条第一款修改为:“犯罪分子具有本法规定的减轻处罚情节的，应当在法定刑以下判处刑罚；本法规定有数个量刑幅度的，应当在法定量刑幅度的下一个量刑幅度内判处刑罚。” 六、将刑法第六十五条第一款修改为:“被判处有期徒刑以上刑罚的犯罪分子，刑罚执行完毕或者赦免以后，在五年以内再犯应当判处有期徒刑以上刑罚之罪的，是累犯，应当从重处罚，但是过失犯罪和不满十八周岁的人犯罪的除外。” 七、将刑法第六十六条修改为:“危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪、黑社会性质的组织犯罪的犯罪分子，在刑罚执行完毕或者赦免以后，在任何时候再犯上述任一类罪的，都以累犯论处。” 八、在刑法第六十七条中增加一款作为第三款:“犯罪嫌疑人虽不具有前两款规定的自首情节，但是如实供述自己罪行的，可以从轻处罚；因其如实供述自己罪行，避免特别严重后果发生的，可以减轻处罚。” 九、删去刑法第六十八条第二款。 十、将刑法第六十九条修改为:“判决宣告以前一人犯数罪的，除判处死刑和无期徒刑的以外，应当在总和刑期以下、数刑中最高刑期以上，酌情决定执行的刑期，但是管制最高不能超过三年，拘役最高不能超过一年，有期徒刑总和刑期不满三十五年的，最高不能超过二十年，总和刑期在三十五年以上的，最高不能超过二十五年。 “数罪中有判处附加刑的，附加刑仍须执行，其中附加刑种类相同的，合并执行，种类不同的，分别执行。”

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、在[刑法第十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-17)后增加一条，作为第十七条之一:“已满七十五周岁的人故意犯罪的，可以从轻或者减轻处罚；过失犯罪的，应当从轻或者减轻处罚。”

**Article 1** 1. One article is added after Article 17 of the Criminal Law, as Article 17a: "A person who has attained the age of 75 and intentionally commits a crime may be given a lighter or mitigated punishment; a person who commits a crime negligently shall be given a lighter or mitigated punishment."

<a id="art-2"></a>二、在[刑法第三十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-38)中增加一款作为第二款:“判处管制，可以根据犯罪情况，同时禁止犯罪分子在执行期间从事特定活动，进入特定区域、场所，接触特定的人。”  
原第二款作为第三款，修改为:“对判处管制的犯罪分子，依法实行社区矫正。”  
增加一款作为第四款:“违反第二款规定的禁止令的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚。”

**Article 2** 2. One paragraph is added to Article 38 of the Criminal Law as paragraph 2: "Where public surveillance is imposed, the criminal may be prohibited, in light of the circumstances of the crime, from engaging in specified activities, entering specified areas or venues, or contacting specified persons during the period of enforcement."  
The original paragraph 2 is changed to paragraph 3 and amended to read: "A criminal who is sentenced to public surveillance shall be subjected to community correction according to law."  
One paragraph is added as paragraph 4: "A person who violates the restraining order provided in paragraph 2 shall be punished by the public security organ in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Penalties for Administration of Public Security."

<a id="art-3"></a>三、在[刑法第四十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-49)中增加一款作为第二款:“审判的时候已满七十五周岁的人，不适用死刑，但以特别残忍手段致人死亡的除外。”

**Article 3** 3. One paragraph is added to Article 49 of the Criminal Law as paragraph 2: "The death penalty shall not be applied to a person who has attained the age of 75 at the time of adjudication, except where death is caused by especially cruel means."

<a id="art-4"></a>四、将[刑法第五十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-50)修改为:“判处死刑缓期执行的，在死刑缓期执行期间，如果没有故意犯罪，二年期满以后，减为无期徒刑；如果确有重大立功表现，二年期满以后，减为二十五年有期徒刑；如果故意犯罪，查证属实的，由最高人民法院核准，执行死刑。  
“对被判处死刑缓期执行的累犯以及因故意杀人、强奸、抢劫、绑架、放火、爆炸、投放危险物质或者有组织的暴力性犯罪被判处死刑缓期执行的犯罪分子，人民法院根据犯罪情节等情况可以同时决定对其限制减刑。”

**Article 4** 4. Article 50 of the Criminal Law is amended to read: "Where a person is sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve, if no intentional crime is committed during the period of the reprieve, the sentence shall be commuted to life imprisonment upon expiration of the two-year period; if major meritorious service is actually performed, the sentence shall be commuted to fixed-term imprisonment of 25 years upon expiration of the two-year period; if an intentional crime is committed and it is verified, the death penalty shall be executed upon approval by the Supreme People's Court.  
"For a recidivist sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve or a criminal sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve for intentional homicide, rape, robbery, kidnapping, arson, explosion, dissemination of dangerous substances, or organized violent crimes, the People's Court may, in light of the circumstances of the crime, concurrently decide to restrict commutation of punishment."

<a id="art-5"></a>五、将[刑法第六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-63)第一款修改为:“犯罪分子具有本法规定的减轻处罚情节的，应当在法定刑以下判处刑罚；本法规定有数个量刑幅度的，应当在法定量刑幅度的下一个量刑幅度内判处刑罚。”

**Article 5** 5. Paragraph 1 of Article 63 of the Criminal Law is amended to read: "Where a criminal has a circumstance for mitigated punishment prescribed in this Law, the sentence shall be imposed below the statutory punishment; where this Law provides for several sentencing ranges, the sentence shall be imposed within the next sentencing range below the statutory sentencing range."

<a id="art-6"></a>六、将[刑法第六十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-65)第一款修改为:“被判处有期徒刑以上刑罚的犯罪分子，刑罚执行完毕或者赦免以后，在五年以内再犯应当判处有期徒刑以上刑罚之罪的，是累犯，应当从重处罚，但是过失犯罪和不满十八周岁的人犯罪的除外。”

**Article 6** 6. Paragraph 1 of Article 65 of the Criminal Law is amended to read: "A criminal who is sentenced to a punishment of fixed-term imprisonment or above, and within five years after completion of the enforcement or after pardon, commits another crime that shall be sentenced to a punishment of fixed-term imprisonment or above, is a recidivist and shall be given a severe punishment; however, this shall not apply to negligent crimes or crimes committed by a person under the age of 18."

<a id="art-7"></a>七、将[刑法第六十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-66)修改为:“危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪、黑社会性质的组织犯罪的犯罪分子，在刑罚执行完毕或者赦免以后，在任何时候再犯上述任一类罪的，都以累犯论处。”

**Article 7** 7. Article 66 of the Criminal Law is amended to read: "A criminal who commits a crime endangering national security, a terrorist activity crime, or an organized crime of a mafia-like nature who, after completion of the enforcement or after pardon, again commits any of the aforesaid categories of crimes at any time shall be treated as a recidivist."

<a id="art-8"></a>八、在[刑法第六十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-67)中增加一款作为第三款:“犯罪嫌疑人虽不具有前两款规定的自首情节，但是如实供述自己罪行的，可以从轻处罚；因其如实供述自己罪行，避免特别严重后果发生的，可以减轻处罚。”

**Article 8** 8. One paragraph is added to Article 67 of the Criminal Law as paragraph 3: "A criminal suspect who, although not having the circumstances of voluntary surrender as provided in the preceding two paragraphs, truthfully confesses his own crime may be given a lighter punishment; if, because of such truthful confession, especially serious consequences are avoided, a mitigated punishment may be given."

<a id="art-9"></a>九、删去[刑法第六十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-68)第二款。

**Article 9** 9. Paragraph 2 of Article 68 of the Criminal Law is deleted.

<a id="art-10"></a>十、将[刑法第六十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-69)修改为:“判决宣告以前一人犯数罪的，除判处死刑和无期徒刑的以外，应当在总和刑期以下、数刑中最高刑期以上，酌情决定执行的刑期，但是管制最高不能超过三年，拘役最高不能超过一年，有期徒刑总和刑期不满三十五年的，最高不能超过二十年，总和刑期在三十五年以上的，最高不能超过二十五年。  
“数罪中有判处附加刑的，附加刑仍须执行，其中附加刑种类相同的，合并执行，种类不同的，分别执行。”

**Article 10** 10. Article 69 of the Criminal Law is amended to read: "Where a person commits several crimes before a judgment is pronounced, unless he is sentenced to death or life imprisonment, the term of sentence to be executed shall be determined by discretion within the total term of the punishments and above the maximum term of the punishments imposed for the individual crimes, provided that the maximum term of public surveillance shall not exceed three years, the maximum term of criminal detention shall not exceed one year, and the maximum term of fixed-term imprisonment shall not exceed 20 years if the total term is less than 35 years, or shall not exceed 25 years if the total term is 35 years or more.  
"Where supplementary punishments are imposed for several crimes, the supplementary punishments shall still be executed; if the supplementary punishments are of the same type, they shall be executed concurrently; if they are of different types, they shall be executed separately."

<a id="art-11"></a>十一、将[刑法第七十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-72)修改为:“对于被判处拘役、三年以下有期徒刑的犯罪分子，同时符合下列条件的，可以宣告缓刑，对其中不满十八周岁的人、怀孕的妇女和已满七十五周岁的人，应当宣告缓刑:  
“（一）犯罪情节较轻；  
“（二）有悔罪表现；  
“（三）没有再犯罪的危险；  
“（四）宣告缓刑对所居住社区没有重大不良影响。  
“宣告缓刑，可以根据犯罪情况，同时禁止犯罪分子在缓刑考验期限内从事特定活动，进入特定区域、场所，接触特定的人。  
“被宣告缓刑的犯罪分子，如果被判处附加刑，附加刑仍须执行。”

**Article 11** 11. Article 72 of the Criminal Law is amended to read: "For a criminal who is sentenced to criminal detention or fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, probation may be declared if the following conditions are concurrently met; for a person under the age of 18, a pregnant woman, or a person who has attained the age of 75, probation shall be declared:  
"(1) the circumstances of the crime are minor;  
"(2) there is evidence of repentance;  
"(3) there is no danger of committing further crimes; and  
"(4) the declaration of probation will not have a significant adverse impact on the community in which the sentenced person resides.  
"Where probation is declared, the criminal may be prohibited, in light of the circumstances of the crime, from engaging in specified activities, entering specified areas or venues, or contacting specified persons during the probation period.  
"If a criminal declared with probation is sentenced to supplementary punishments, the supplementary punishments shall still be executed."

<a id="art-12"></a>十二、将[刑法第七十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-74)修改为:“对于累犯和犯罪集团的首要分子，不适用缓刑。”

**Article 12** 12. Article 74 of the Criminal Law is amended to read: "Probation shall not be applied to recidivists or ringleaders of criminal groups."

<a id="art-13"></a>十三、将[刑法第七十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-76)修改为:“对宣告缓刑的犯罪分子，在缓刑考验期限内，依法实行社区矫正，如果没有[本法第七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-77)规定的情形，缓刑考验期满，原判的刑罚就不再执行，并公开予以宣告。”

**Article 13** 13. Article 76 of the Criminal Law is amended to read: "For a criminal who is declared with probation, community correction shall be implemented according to law during the probation period. If there is no circumstance provided in Article 77 of this Law, upon expiration of the probation period, the original sentence shall no longer be executed, and shall be publicly announced."

<a id="art-14"></a>十四、将[刑法第七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-77)第二款修改为:“被宣告缓刑的犯罪分子，在缓刑考验期限内，违反法律、行政法规或者国务院有关部门关于缓刑的监督管理规定，或者违反人民法院判决中的禁止令，情节严重的，应当撤销缓刑，执行原判刑罚。”

**Article 14** 14. Paragraph 2 of Article 77 of the Criminal Law is amended to read: "Where a criminal who is declared with probation, during the probation period, violates the laws, administrative regulations, or the supervisory provisions on probation of the relevant departments under the State Council, or violates a restraining order in the judgment of the People's Court, if the circumstances are serious, the probation shall be revoked and the original sentence shall be executed."

<a id="art-15"></a>十五、将[刑法第七十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-78)第二款修改为:“减刑以后实际执行的刑期不能少于下列期限:  
“（一）判处管制、拘役、有期徒刑的，不能少于原判刑期的二分之一；  
“（二）判处无期徒刑的，不能少于十三年；  
“（三）人民法院依照[本法第五十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-50)第二款规定限制减刑的死刑缓期执行的犯罪分子，缓期执行期满后依法减为无期徒刑的，不能少于二十五年，缓期执行期满后依法减为二十五年有期徒刑的，不能少于二十年。”

**Article 15** 15. Paragraph 2 of Article 78 of the Criminal Law is amended to read: "The term of sentence actually executed after commutation of punishment shall not be less than the following periods:  
"(1) for a person sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention, or fixed-term imprisonment, not less than one-half of the original term of sentence;  
"(2) for a person sentenced to life imprisonment, not less than 13 years; and  
"(3) for a criminal sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve whose commutation of punishment is restricted by the People's Court in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 50 of this Law, if the sentence is commuted to life imprisonment after expiration of the reprieve period according to law, not less than 25 years; if the sentence is commuted to fixed-term imprisonment of 25 years after expiration of the reprieve period according to law, not less than 20 years."

<a id="art-16"></a>十六、将[刑法第八十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-81)修改为:“被判处有期徒刑的犯罪分子，执行原判刑期二分之一以上，被判处无期徒刑的犯罪分子，实际执行十三年以上，如果认真遵守监规，接受教育改造，确有悔改表现，没有再犯罪的危险的，可以假释。如果有特殊情况，经最高人民法院核准，可以不受上述执行刑期的限制。  
“对累犯以及因故意杀人、强奸、抢劫、绑架、放火、爆炸、投放危险物质或者有组织的暴力性犯罪被判处十年以上有期徒刑、无期徒刑的犯罪分子，不得假释。  
“对犯罪分子决定假释时，应当考虑其假释后对所居住社区的影响。”

**Article 16** 16. Article 81 of the Criminal Law is amended to read: "A criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment who has served more than one-half of the original term of sentence, or a criminal sentenced to life imprisonment who has actually served at least 13 years, may be granted parole if he earnestly observes prison regulations, accepts education and reform, shows true repentance, and has no danger of committing further crimes. Where there are special circumstances, the restriction on the above term of enforcement may be lifted upon approval by the Supreme People's Court.  
"Parole shall not be granted to recidivists or to criminals who are sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of 10 years or more or life imprisonment for intentional homicide, rape, robbery, kidnapping, arson, explosion, dissemination of dangerous substances, or organized violent crimes.  
"When deciding on parole for a criminal, the impact of the parole on the community in which the parolee resides shall be taken into consideration."

<a id="art-17"></a>十七、将[刑法第八十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-85)修改为:“对假释的犯罪分子，在假释考验期限内，依法实行社区矫正，如果没有[本法第八十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-86)规定的情形，假释考验期满，就认为原判刑罚已经执行完毕，并公开予以宣告。”

**Article 17** 17. Article 85 of the Criminal Law is amended to read: "For a criminal who is granted parole, community correction shall be implemented according to law during the parole period. If there is no circumstance provided in Article 86 of this Law, upon expiration of the parole period, the original sentence shall be deemed to have been fully executed, and shall be publicly announced."

<a id="art-18"></a>十八、将[刑法第八十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-86)第三款修改为:“被假释的犯罪分子，在假释考验期限内，有违反法律、行政法规或者国务院有关部门关于假释的监督管理规定的行为，尚未构成新的犯罪的，应当依照法定程序撤销假释，收监执行未执行完毕的刑罚。”

**Article 18** 18. Paragraph 3 of Article 86 of the Criminal Law is amended to read: "Where a criminal who is granted parole, during the parole period, commits acts that violate the laws, administrative regulations, or the supervisory provisions on parole of the relevant departments under the State Council, but which do not constitute a new crime, parole shall be revoked according to legal procedures, and the criminal shall be taken back into custody to serve the remainder of the sentence."

<a id="art-19"></a>十九、在[刑法第一百条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-100)中增加一款作为第二款:“犯罪的时候不满十八周岁被判处五年有期徒刑以下刑罚的人，免除前款规定的报告义务。”

**Article 19** 19. One paragraph is added to Article 100 of the Criminal Law as paragraph 2: "A person who was under the age of 18 when the crime was committed and was sentenced to a punishment of fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years shall be exempted from the reporting obligation provided in the preceding paragraph."

<a id="art-20"></a>二十、将[刑法第一百零七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-107)修改为:“境内外机构、组织或者个人资助实施本章第一百零二条、第一百零三条、第一百零四条、第一百零五条规定之罪的，对直接责任人员，处五年以下有期徒刑、拘役、管制或者剥夺政治权利；情节严重的，处五年以上有期徒刑。”

**Article 20** 20.
