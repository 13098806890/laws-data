## 第一编　总则

### 第一章　任务和基本原则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保证刑法的正确实施，惩罚犯罪，保护人民，保障国家安全和社会公共安全，维护社会主义社会秩序，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution to ensure the correct implementation of the Criminal Law, to punish crimes, to protect the people, to safeguard national security and public safety, and to maintain the socialist social order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法的任务，是保证准确、及时地查明犯罪事实，正确应用法律，惩罚犯罪分子，保障无罪的人不受刑事追究，教育公民自觉遵守法律，积极同犯罪行为作斗争，维护社会主义法制，尊重和保障人权，保护公民的人身权利、财产权利、民主权利和其他权利，保障社会主义建设事业的顺利进行。

**Article 2** The tasks of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China are to ensure accurate and timely investigation of criminal facts, correct application of law, punishment of criminals, protection of innocent persons from criminal prosecution, education of citizens to voluntarily abide by the law and actively combat criminal acts, safeguard the socialist legal system, respect and protect human rights, protect citizens' personal rights, property rights, democratic rights and other rights, and ensure the smooth progress of socialist construction.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　对刑事案件的侦查、拘留、执行逮捕、预审，由公安机关负责。检察、批准逮捕、检察机关直接受理的案件的侦查、提起公诉，由人民检察院负责。审判由人民法院负责。除法律特别规定的以外，其他任何机关、团体和个人都无权行使这些权力。  
人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关进行刑事诉讼，必须严格遵守本法和其他法律的有关规定。

**Article 3** The public security organs are responsible for investigation, detention, execution of arrest, and preliminary interrogation of criminal cases. The People's Procuratorates are responsible for procuratorial work, approval of arrest, investigation of cases directly accepted by the procuratorial organs, and initiation of public prosecution. The People's Courts are responsible for adjudication. No other organs, organizations, or individuals shall have the authority to exercise these powers, except as otherwise provided by law.  
In conducting criminal proceedings, the People's Courts, People's Procuratorates, and public security organs must strictly observe the relevant provisions of this Law and other laws.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家安全机关依照法律规定，办理危害国家安全的刑事案件，行使与公安机关相同的职权。

**Article 4** State security organs shall, in accordance with law, handle criminal cases endangering state security and exercise the same functions and powers as public security organs.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院依照法律规定独立行使审判权，人民检察院依照法律规定独立行使检察权，不受行政机关、社会团体和个人的干涉。

**Article 5** The People's Courts shall independently exercise the adjudicative power in accordance with law, and the People's Procuratorates shall independently exercise the procuratorial power in accordance with law, and shall not be subject to interference by administrative organs, social organizations, or individuals.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关进行刑事诉讼，必须依靠群众，必须以事实为根据，以法律为准绳。对于一切公民，在适用法律上一律平等，在法律面前，不允许有任何特权。

**Article 6** In conducting criminal proceedings, the People's Courts, People's Procuratorates, and public security organs must rely on the masses, must base themselves on facts and take law as the criterion. All citizens shall be equal in the application of law, and no privileges shall be allowed before the law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关进行刑事诉讼，应当分工负责，互相配合，互相制约，以保证准确有效地执行法律。

**Article 7** In conducting criminal proceedings, the People's Courts, People's Procuratorates, and public security organs shall divide their responsibilities, cooperate with each other, and check each other to ensure the accurate and effective enforcement of law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民检察院依法对刑事诉讼实行法律监督。

**Article 8** The People's Procuratorates shall exercise legal supervision over criminal proceedings according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各民族公民都有用本民族语言文字进行诉讼的权利。人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关对于不通晓当地通用的语言文字的诉讼参与人，应当为他们翻译。  
在少数民族聚居或者多民族杂居的地区，应当用当地通用的语言进行审讯，用当地通用的文字发布判决书、布告和其他文件。

**Article 9** Citizens of all nationalities shall have the right to use their own spoken and written languages in legal proceedings. The People's Courts, People's Procuratorates, and public security organs shall provide translation for any participant in the proceedings who is not familiar with the commonly used local spoken or written language.  
In areas where ethnic minorities are concentrated or where multiple nationalities live together, interrogations shall be conducted in the commonly used local language, and judgments, notices, and other documents shall be published in the commonly used local script.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院审判案件，实行两审终审制。

**Article 10** The People's Courts shall try cases under a system whereby the second instance is final.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院审判案件，除本法另有规定的以外，一律公开进行。被告人有权获得辩护，人民法院有义务保证被告人获得辩护。

**Article 11** The People's Courts shall try cases in public unless otherwise provided by this Law. A defendant shall have the right to obtain a defense, and the People's Court shall have the obligation to ensure that the defendant obtains a defense.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　未经人民法院依法判决，对任何人都不得确定有罪。

**Article 12** No person shall be found guilty without being adjudicated by a People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民法院审判案件，依照本法实行人民陪审员陪审的制度。

**Article 13** In adjudicating cases, the People's Courts shall adopt the system of people's assessors participating in adjudication in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关应当保障犯罪嫌疑人、被告人和其他诉讼参与人依法享有的辩护权和其他诉讼权利。  
诉讼参与人对于审判人员、检察人员和侦查人员侵犯公民诉讼权利和人身侮辱的行为，有权提出控告。

**Article 14** The People's Courts, People's Procuratorates, and public security organs shall safeguard the right to defense and other procedural rights enjoyed by criminal suspects, defendants, and other participants in proceedings according to law.  
Participants in proceedings shall have the right to file complaints against adjudicators, procurators, and investigators who infringe upon citizens' procedural rights or commit acts of personal insult.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　犯罪嫌疑人、被告人自愿如实供述自己的罪行，承认指控的犯罪事实，愿意接受处罚的，可以依法从宽处理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-19" title="被《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事程序解释/最高人民法院印发《关于人民法院执行〈中华人民共和国国家赔偿法〉几个问题的解释》的通知.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院印发《关于人民法院执行〈中华人民共和国国家赔偿法〉几个问题的解释》的通知》第1条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 15** Where a criminal suspect or defendant voluntarily and truthfully confesses his or her own crime, admits the alleged criminal facts, and is willing to accept punishment, he or she may be dealt with leniently according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　有下列情形之一的，不追究刑事责任，已经追究的，应当撤销案件，或者不起诉，或者终止审理，或者宣告无罪：  
（一）情节显著轻微、危害不大，不认为是犯罪的；  
（二）犯罪已过追诉时效期限的；  
（三）经特赦令免除刑罚的；  
（四）依照刑法告诉才处理的犯罪，没有告诉或者撤回告诉的；  
（五）犯罪嫌疑人、被告人死亡的；  
（六）其他法律规定免予追究刑事责任的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-139" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第139条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-171" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第171条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-242" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第242条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-278" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第278条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-285" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第285条引用">[5]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-287" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第287条引用">[6]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-365" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第365条引用">[7]</a></sup>

**Article 16** Criminal liability shall not be investigated under any of the following circumstances; if investigation has already been commenced, the case shall be dismissed, or a decision not to prosecute shall be made, or the trial shall be terminated, or a verdict of not guilty shall be pronounced:  
(1) If the facts are obviously minor and the harm is not great, and the act is not considered a crime;  
(2) If the statute of limitations for criminal prosecution has expired;  
(3) If an amnesty order exempts the person from punishment;  
(4) If a crime is to be handled only upon complaint under the Criminal Law, and no complaint has been made or the complaint has been withdrawn;  
(5) If the criminal suspect or defendant is deceased;  
(6) If other laws provide that criminal liability shall not be investigated.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对于外国人犯罪应当追究刑事责任的，适用本法的规定。  
对于享有外交特权和豁免权的外国人犯罪应当追究刑事责任的，通过外交途径解决。

**Article 17** Where a foreigner commits a crime for which criminal liability shall be investigated, the provisions of this Law shall apply.  
Where a foreigner enjoying diplomatic privileges and immunity commits a crime for which criminal liability shall be investigated, the matter shall be resolved through diplomatic channels.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　根据中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约，或者按照互惠原则，我国司法机关和外国司法机关可以相互请求刑事司法协助。

**Article 18** In accordance with international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, or under the principle of reciprocity, the judicial organs of China and those of foreign countries may request criminal judicial assistance from each other.

### 第二章　管辖

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　刑事案件的侦查由公安机关进行，法律另有规定的除外。  
人民检察院在对诉讼活动实行法律监督中发现的司法工作人员利用职权实施的非法拘禁、刑讯逼供、非法搜查等侵犯公民权利、损害司法公正的犯罪，可以由人民检察院立案侦查。对于公安机关管辖的国家机关工作人员利用职权实施的重大犯罪案件，需要由人民检察院直接受理的时候，经省级以上人民检察院决定，可以由人民检察院立案侦查。  
自诉案件，由人民法院直接受理。

**Article 19** Investigation of criminal cases shall be conducted by public security organs, except as otherwise provided by law.  
Where, in exercising legal supervision over litigation activities, a People's Procuratorate discovers crimes committed by judicial officers by taking advantage of their functions, such as illegal detention, extortion of confessions by torture, or illegal search, which infringe upon citizens' rights or impair judicial justice, the People's Procuratorate may file and investigate the case. Where a major crime case under the jurisdiction of public security organs, committed by state functionaries by taking advantage of their functions, needs to be directly accepted by the People's Procuratorate, the People's Procuratorate may file and investigate the case upon the decision of a People's Procuratorate at or above the provincial level.  
Cases of private prosecution shall be directly accepted by the People's Courts.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　基层人民法院管辖第一审普通刑事案件，但是依照本法由上级人民法院管辖的除外。

**Article 20** Primary People's Courts shall have jurisdiction over ordinary criminal cases of first instance, except those otherwise under the jurisdiction of People's Courts at higher levels according to this Law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　中级人民法院管辖下列第一审刑事案件：  
（一）危害国家安全、恐怖活动案件；  
（二）可能判处无期徒刑、死刑的案件。

**Article 21** Intermediate People's Courts shall have jurisdiction over the following criminal cases of first instance:  
(1) Cases involving endangering national security or terrorist activities;  
(2) Cases in which a sentence of life imprisonment or death penalty may be imposed.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　高级人民法院管辖的第一审刑事案件，是全省（自治区、直辖市）性的重大刑事案件。

**Article 22** Higher People's Courts shall have jurisdiction over major criminal cases of first instance that are of a province-wide (autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government) nature.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　最高人民法院管辖的第一审刑事案件，是全国性的重大刑事案件。

**Article 23** The Supreme People's Court shall have jurisdiction over major criminal cases of first instance that are of nationwide nature.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　上级人民法院在必要的时候，可以审判下级人民法院管辖的第一审刑事案件；下级人民法院认为案情重大、复杂需要由上级人民法院审判的第一审刑事案件，可以请求移送上一级人民法院审判。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 24** A People's Court at a higher level may, when necessary, adjudicate criminal cases of first instance that are under the jurisdiction of a People's Court at a lower level; where a People's Court at a lower level considers that the facts of a case are major or complex and need to be adjudicated by a People's Court at a higher level, it may request to transfer the case to the next higher People's Court for adjudication.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　刑事案件由犯罪地的人民法院管辖。如果由被告人居住地的人民法院审判更为适宜的，可以由被告人居住地的人民法院管辖。

**Article 25** Criminal cases shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the crime is committed. If it is more appropriate for the case to be tried by the People's Court of the place where the defendant resides, then it may be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the defendant's place of residence.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　几个同级人民法院都有权管辖的案件，由最初受理的人民法院审判。在必要的时候，可以移送主要犯罪地的人民法院审判。

**Article 26** Where a case falls within the jurisdiction of several People's Courts at the same level, it shall be tried by the People's Court that first accepted the case. When necessary, the case may be transferred to the People's Court of the principal place where the crime was committed for trial.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　上级人民法院可以指定下级人民法院审判管辖不明的案件，也可以指定下级人民法院将案件移送其他人民法院审判。

**Article 27** A People's Court at a higher level may designate a People's Court at a lower level to try a case with unclear jurisdiction, and may also designate a People's Court at a lower level to transfer the case to another People's Court for trial.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　专门人民法院案件的管辖另行规定。

**Article 28** Jurisdiction over cases of special People's Courts shall be separately provided.

### 第三章　回避

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　审判人员、检察人员、侦查人员有下列情形之一的，应当自行回避，当事人及其法定代理人也有权要求他们回避：  
（一）是本案的当事人或者是当事人的近亲属的；  
（二）本人或者他的近亲属和本案有利害关系的；  
（三）担任过本案的证人、鉴定人、辩护人、诉讼代理人的；  
（四）与本案当事人有其他关系，可能影响公正处理案件的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-24" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第24条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-28" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第28条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 29** An adjudicator, a procurator, or an investigator shall recuse himself or herself under any of the following circumstances, and the parties and their legal representatives shall have the right to request his or her recusal:  
(1) If the person is a party to the case or a close relative of a party;  
(2) If the person or a close relative of the person has an interest in the case;  
(3) If the person has served as a witness, expert evaluator, defender, or litigation agent in the case;  
(4) If the person has any other relationship with a party to the case that may affect the impartial handling of the case.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　审判人员、检察人员、侦查人员不得接受当事人及其委托的人的请客送礼，不得违反规定会见当事人及其委托的人。  
审判人员、检察人员、侦查人员违反前款规定的，应当依法追究法律责任。当事人及其法定代理人有权要求他们回避。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-27" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第27条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 30** Adjudicators, procurators, and investigators shall not accept the hospitality or gifts of parties and their entrusted persons, and shall not meet with parties and their entrusted persons in violation of regulations.  
Where an adjudicator, a procurator, or an investigator violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph, legal liability shall be pursued according to law. The parties and their legal representatives shall have the right to request their recusal.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　审判人员、检察人员、侦查人员的回避，应当分别由院长、检察长、公安机关负责人决定；院长的回避，由本院审判委员会决定；检察长和公安机关负责人的回避，由同级人民检察院检察委员会决定。  
对侦查人员的回避作出决定前，侦查人员不能停止对案件的侦查。  
对驳回申请回避的决定，当事人及其法定代理人可以申请复议一次。

**Article 31** Recusal of an adjudicator, a procurator, or an investigator shall be decided respectively by the president of the court, the chief procurator, or the head of the public security organ; the recusal of the president of the court shall be decided by the judicial committee of that court; and the recusal of the chief procurator and the head of the public security organ shall be decided by the procuratorial committee of the corresponding People's Procuratorate.  
Before a decision on the recusal of an investigator is made, the investigator shall not cease the investigation of the case.  
If a decision to reject a request for recusal is made, the parties or their legal representatives may apply for reconsideration once.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本章关于回避的规定适用于书记员、翻译人员和鉴定人。  
辩护人、诉讼代理人可以依照本章的规定要求回避、申请复议。

**Article 32** The provisions of this Chapter on recusal shall apply to court clerks, interpreters, and expert evaluators.  
Defenders and litigation agents may request recusal and apply for reconsideration in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter.

### 第四章　辩护与代理

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　犯罪嫌疑人、被告人除自己行使辩护权以外，还可以委托一至二人作为辩护人。下列的人可以被委托为辩护人：  
（一）律师；  
（二）人民团体或者犯罪嫌疑人、被告人所在单位推荐的人；  
（三）犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的监护人、亲友。  
正在被执行刑罚或者依法被剥夺、限制人身自由的人，不得担任辩护人。  
被开除公职和被吊销律师、公证员执业证书的人，不得担任辩护人，但系犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的监护人、近亲属的除外。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-55" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第55条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 33** In addition to exercising the right to defend themselves, criminal suspects and defendants may also entrust one to two persons as defenders. The following persons may be entrusted as defenders:  
(1) Lawyers;  
(2) Persons recommended by a people's organization or the unit of the criminal suspect or defendant;  
(3) Guardians or relatives or friends of the criminal suspect or defendant.  
Persons who are currently being subjected to criminal punishment or whose personal freedom is deprived or restricted according to law shall not serve as defenders.  
Persons who have been discharged from public office or whose lawyer or notary practice certificates have been revoked shall not serve as defenders, except where they are the guardians or close relatives of the criminal suspect or defendant.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　犯罪嫌疑人自被侦查机关第一次讯问或者采取强制措施之日起，有权委托辩护人；在侦查期间，只能委托律师作为辩护人。被告人有权随时委托辩护人。  
侦查机关在第一次讯问犯罪嫌疑人或者对犯罪嫌疑人采取强制措施的时候，应当告知犯罪嫌疑人有权委托辩护人。人民检察院自收到移送审查起诉的案件材料之日起三日以内，应当告知犯罪嫌疑人有权委托辩护人。人民法院自受理案件之日起三日以内，应当告知被告人有权委托辩护人。犯罪嫌疑人、被告人在押期间要求委托辩护人的，人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关应当及时转达其要求。  
犯罪嫌疑人、被告人在押的，也可以由其监护人、近亲属代为委托辩护人。  
辩护人接受犯罪嫌疑人、被告人委托后，应当及时告知办理案件的机关。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 34** A criminal suspect shall have the right to entrust a defender from the day he or she is first interrogated by an investigative organ or from the day a compulsory measure is taken against him or her; during the period of investigation, only a lawyer may be entrusted as a defender. A defendant shall have the right to entrust a defender at any time.  
When an investigative organ first interrogates a criminal suspect or takes a compulsory measure against him or her, it shall inform the criminal suspect of the right to entrust a defender. Within three days from the date of receiving the case materials transferred for examination before prosecution, the People's Procuratorate shall inform the criminal suspect of the right to entrust a defender. Within three days from the date of accepting a case, the People's Court shall inform the defendant of the right to entrust a defender. If a criminal suspect or defendant in custody requests to entrust a defender, the People's Court, People's Procuratorate, or public security organ shall convey the request in a timely manner.  
If a criminal suspect or defendant is in custody, his or her guardian or close relative may also entrust a defender on his or her behalf.  
After accepting the entrustment by a criminal suspect or defendant, the defender shall promptly inform the organ handling the case.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　犯罪嫌疑人、被告人因经济困难或者其他原因没有委托辩护人的，本人及其近亲属可以向法律援助机构提出申请。对符合法律援助条件的，法律援助机构应当指派律师为其提供辩护。  
犯罪嫌疑人、被告人是盲、聋、哑人，或者是尚未完全丧失辨认或者控制自己行为能力的精神病人，没有委托辩护人的，人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关应当通知法律援助机构指派律师为其提供辩护。  
犯罪嫌疑人、被告人可能被判处无期徒刑、死刑，没有委托辩护人的，人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关应当通知法律援助机构指派律师为其提供辩护。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-40" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第40条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 35** Where a criminal suspect or defendant has not entrusted a defender due to financial difficulties or other reasons, he or she or his or her close relatives may apply to a legal aid agency. If the conditions for legal aid are met, the legal aid agency shall assign a lawyer to provide defense.  
Where a criminal suspect or defendant is blind, deaf, or mute, or is a mentally ill person who has not completely lost the ability to recognize or control his or her conduct, and has not entrusted a defender, the People's Court, People's Procuratorate, or public security organ shall notify a legal aid agency to assign a lawyer to provide defense.  
Where a criminal suspect or defendant may be sentenced to life imprisonment or the death penalty and has not entrusted a defender, the People's Court, People's Procuratorate, or public security organ shall notify a legal aid agency to assign a lawyer to provide defense.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　法律援助机构可以在人民法院、看守所等场所派驻值班律师。犯罪嫌疑人、被告人没有委托辩护人，法律援助机构没有指派律师为其提供辩护的，由值班律师为犯罪嫌疑人、被告人提供法律咨询、程序选择建议、申请变更强制措施、对案件处理提出意见等法律帮助。  
人民法院、人民检察院、看守所应当告知犯罪嫌疑人、被告人有权约见值班律师，并为犯罪嫌疑人、被告人约见值班律师提供便利。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 36** Legal aid agencies may station duty lawyers at People's Courts, detention centers, and other places. Where a criminal suspect or defendant has not entrusted a defender and the legal aid agency has not assigned a lawyer to provide defense, the duty lawyer shall provide legal assistance to the criminal suspect or defendant, including legal consultation, advice on procedural choices, application for modification of compulsory measures, and opinions on case handling.  
The People's Court, People's Procuratorate, or detention center shall inform the criminal suspect or defendant of the right to meet with a duty lawyer and shall facilitate the meeting between the criminal suspect or defendant and the duty lawyer.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　辩护人的责任是根据事实和法律，提出犯罪嫌疑人、被告人无罪、罪轻或者减轻、免除其刑事责任的材料和意见，维护犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的诉讼权利和其他合法权益。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 37** The responsibility of a defender is to present, based on facts and law, materials and opinions indicating the innocence or lesser guilt of the criminal suspect or defendant, or the mitigation or exemption of his or her criminal liability, and to safeguard the procedural rights and other lawful rights and interests of the criminal suspect or defendant.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　辩护律师在侦查期间可以为犯罪嫌疑人提供法律帮助；代理申诉、控告；申请变更强制措施；向侦查机关了解犯罪嫌疑人涉嫌的罪名和案件有关情况，提出意见。

**Article 38** During the period of investigation, a defense lawyer may provide legal assistance to the criminal suspect; act as agent in filing petitions and complaints; apply for modification of compulsory measures; and inquire about the crime suspected of by the criminal suspect and other relevant circumstances of the case from the investigative organ, and present opinions.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　辩护律师可以同在押的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人会见和通信。其他辩护人经人民法院、人民检察院许可，也可以同在押的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人会见和通信。  
辩护律师持律师执业证书、律师事务所证明和委托书或者法律援助公函要求会见在押的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的，看守所应当及时安排会见，至迟不得超过四十八小时。  
危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪案件，在侦查期间辩护律师会见在押的犯罪嫌疑人，应当经侦查机关许可。上述案件，侦查机关应当事先通知看守所。  
辩护律师会见在押的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人，可以了解案件有关情况，提供法律咨询等；自案件移送审查起诉之日起，可以向犯罪嫌疑人、被告人核实有关证据。辩护律师会见犯罪嫌疑人、被告人时不被监听。  
辩护律师同被监视居住的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人会见、通信，适用第一款、第三款、第四款的规定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-8" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第8条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 39** A defense lawyer may meet and correspond with a criminal suspect or defendant in custody. Other defenders, with the permission of the People's Court or People's Procuratorate, may also meet and correspond with a criminal suspect or defendant in custody.  
Where a defense lawyer, holding a lawyer's practice certificate, a certificate from the law firm, and a letter of entrustment or a legal aid letter, requests to meet with a criminal suspect or defendant in custody, the detention center shall arrange the meeting in a timely manner, no later than forty-eight hours.  
In cases involving crimes endangering national security or terrorist activities, a defense lawyer meeting with a criminal suspect in custody during the investigation period shall be subject to permission from the investigative organ. The investigative organ shall notify the detention center in advance of such cases.  
When meeting with a criminal suspect or defendant in custody, a defense lawyer may learn about the relevant circumstances of the case and provide legal consultation, etc.; from the date the case is transferred for examination and prosecution, the defense lawyer may verify relevant evidence with the criminal suspect or defendant. The meeting between a defense lawyer and a criminal suspect or defendant shall not be monitored.  
The provisions of paragraphs 1, 3, and 4 shall apply to the meeting and correspondence between a defense lawyer and a criminal suspect or defendant under residential surveillance.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　辩护律师自人民检察院对案件审查起诉之日起，可以查阅、摘抄、复制本案的案卷材料。其他辩护人经人民法院、人民检察院许可，也可以查阅、摘抄、复制上述材料。

**Article 40** From the date the People's Procuratorate examines and prosecutes the case, a defense lawyer may consult, extract, and copy the case file materials of the case. Other defenders, with the permission of the People's Court or People's Procuratorate, may also consult, extract, and copy the above materials.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　辩护人认为在侦查、审查起诉期间公安机关、人民检察院收集的证明犯罪嫌疑人、被告人无罪或者罪轻的证据材料未提交的，有权申请人民检察院、人民法院调取。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 41** Where a defender believes that the evidence materials proving the innocence or minor guilt of a criminal suspect or defendant collected by a public security organ or a People's Procuratorate during investigation or examination for prosecution have not been submitted, the defender shall have the right to apply to the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court for their collection.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　辩护人收集的有关犯罪嫌疑人不在犯罪现场、未达到刑事责任年龄、属于依法不负刑事责任的精神病人的证据，应当及时告知公安机关、人民检察院。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 42** Where a defender collects evidence that a criminal suspect was not at the scene of the crime, had not attained the age of criminal responsibility, or was a mental patient who shall not bear criminal liability according to law, the defender shall promptly inform the public security organ and the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　辩护律师经证人或者其他有关单位和个人同意，可以向他们收集与本案有关的材料，也可以申请人民检察院、人民法院收集、调取证据，或者申请人民法院通知证人出庭作证。  
辩护律师经人民检察院或者人民法院许可，并且经被害人或者其近亲属、被害人提供的证人同意，可以向他们收集与本案有关的材料。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-52" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第52条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 43** A defense lawyer may, with the consent of a witness or any other relevant entity or individual, collect materials related to the case from them, and may also apply to the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court for the collection or procurement of evidence, or apply to the People's Court to notify a witness to appear in court to testify.  
A defense lawyer may, with the permission of the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court and with the consent of the victim, the victim's near relatives, or a witness produced by the victim, collect materials related to the case from them.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　辩护人或者其他任何人，不得帮助犯罪嫌疑人、被告人隐匿、毁灭、伪造证据或者串供，不得威胁、引诱证人作伪证以及进行其他干扰司法机关诉讼活动的行为。  
违反前款规定的，应当依法追究法律责任，辩护人涉嫌犯罪的，应当由办理辩护人所承办案件的侦查机关以外的侦查机关办理。辩护人是律师的，应当及时通知其所在的律师事务所或者所属的律师协会。

**Article 44** No defender or any other person may assist a criminal suspect or defendant in concealing, destroying, or falsifying evidence, or in colluding to make false statements, and may not threaten or induce a witness to give false testimony or engage in any other act that interferes with the litigation activities of the judicial authorities.  
For violation of the provisions in the preceding paragraph, legal liability shall be pursued according to law. Where a defender is suspected of committing a crime, the case shall be handled by a criminal investigation authority other than the one handling the case undertaken by the defender. Where the defender is a lawyer, the law firm or the lawyers' association to which the lawyer belongs shall be promptly notified.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　在审判过程中，被告人可以拒绝辩护人继续为他辩护，也可以另行委托辩护人辩护。

**Article 45** During the adjudication process, a defendant may refuse to have a defender continue to defend him or her, and may also appoint another defender.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　公诉案件的被害人及其法定代理人或者近亲属，附带民事诉讼的当事人及其法定代理人，自案件移送审查起诉之日起，有权委托诉讼代理人。自诉案件的自诉人及其法定代理人，附带民事诉讼的当事人及其法定代理人，有权随时委托诉讼代理人。  
人民检察院自收到移送审查起诉的案件材料之日起三日以内，应当告知被害人及其法定代理人或者其近亲属、附带民事诉讼的当事人及其法定代理人有权委托诉讼代理人。人民法院自受理自诉案件之日起三日以内，应当告知自诉人及其法定代理人、附带民事诉讼的当事人及其法定代理人有权委托诉讼代理人。

**Article 46** The victim of a public prosecution case and the victim's legal representative or near relatives, as well as the parties to an incidental civil action and their legal representatives, shall have the right to appoint a litigation agent from the date when the case is transferred for examination for prosecution. The private prosecutor of a case instituted upon a complaint and the private prosecutor's legal representative, as well as the parties to an incidental civil action and their legal representatives, shall have the right to appoint a litigation agent at any time.  
The People's Procuratorate shall, within three days of receiving the case materials transferred for examination for prosecution, inform the victim and the victim's legal representative or near relatives, as well as the parties to an incidental civil action and their legal representatives, of their right to appoint a litigation agent. The People's Court shall, within three days of accepting a case instituted upon a complaint, inform the private prosecutor and the private prosecutor's legal representative, as well as the parties to an incidental civil action and their legal representatives, of their right to appoint a litigation agent.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　委托诉讼代理人，参照[本法第三十三条](#art-33)的规定执行。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 47** The appointment of a litigation agent shall be executed by reference to the provisions of Article 33 of this Law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　辩护律师对在执业活动中知悉的委托人的有关情况和信息，有权予以保密。但是，辩护律师在执业活动中知悉委托人或者其他人，准备或者正在实施危害国家安全、公共安全以及严重危害他人人身安全的犯罪的，应当及时告知司法机关。

**Article 48** A defense lawyer shall have the right to keep confidential the relevant circumstances and information of the client learned in the practice of the profession. However, where a defense lawyer, in the practice of the profession, learns that the client or any other person is preparing or is committing a crime endangering national security, public security, or seriously endangering the personal safety of others, the defense lawyer shall promptly inform the judicial authorities.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　辩护人、诉讼代理人认为公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院及其工作人员阻碍其依法行使诉讼权利的，有权向同级或者上一级人民检察院申诉或者控告。人民检察院对申诉或者控告应当及时进行审查，情况属实的，通知有关机关予以纠正。

**Article 49** Where a defender or a litigation agent believes that a public security organ, a People's Procuratorate, a People's Court, or their functionaries have obstructed the defender's or litigation agent's exercise of litigation rights according to law, the defender or litigation agent shall have the right to file a complaint or accusation with the People's Procuratorate at the same level or at the next higher level. The People's Procuratorate shall promptly examine the complaint or accusation; where the circumstances are verified as true, the People's Procuratorate shall notify the relevant authority to make a correction.

### 第五章　证据

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　可以用于证明案件事实的材料，都是证据。  
证据包括：  
（一）物证；  
（二）书证；  
（三）证人证言；  
（四）被害人陈述；  
（五）犯罪嫌疑人、被告人供述和辩解；  
（六）鉴定意见；  
（七）勘验、检查、辨认、侦查实验等笔录；  
（八）视听资料、电子数据。  
证据必须经过查证属实，才能作为定案的根据。

**Article 50** Any material that may be used to prove the facts of a case shall be evidence.  
Evidence includes:  
(1) physical evidence;  
(2) documentary evidence;  
(3) testimony of witnesses;  
(4) statements of victims;  
(5) statements and exculpations of criminal suspects or defendants;  
(6) expert opinions;  
(7) records of inspection, examination, identification, investigative experiment, and the like;  
(8) audio-visual materials and electronic data.  
Evidence must be verified as true before it may be used as the basis for adjudication.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　公诉案件中被告人有罪的举证责任由人民检察院承担，自诉案件中被告人有罪的举证责任由自诉人承担。

**Article 51** In a public prosecution case, the burden of adducing evidence of the defendant's guilt shall be borne by the People's Procuratorate. In a case instituted upon a complaint, the burden of adducing evidence of the defendant's guilt shall be borne by the private prosecutor.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　审判人员、检察人员、侦查人员必须依照法定程序，收集能够证实犯罪嫌疑人、被告人有罪或者无罪、犯罪情节轻重的各种证据。严禁刑讯逼供和以威胁、引诱、欺骗以及其他非法方法收集证据，不得强迫任何人证实自己有罪。必须保证一切与案件有关或者了解案情的公民，有客观地充分地提供证据的条件，除特殊情况外，可以吸收他们协助调查。

**Article 52** Adjudicators, procurators, and investigators must, in accordance with the statutory procedures, collect various types of evidence that may prove the guilt or innocence of a criminal suspect or defendant, or the gravity of the crime. Extorting confessions by torture, and collecting evidence by threat, inducement, deception, or other illegal means are strictly prohibited. No one may be compelled to prove his or her own guilt. Conditions shall be ensured for all citizens who are involved in a case or who are aware of the circumstances of the case to objectively and fully provide evidence. Except in special circumstances, such persons may be allowed to assist in investigations.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　公安机关提请批准逮捕书、人民检察院起诉书、人民法院判决书，必须忠实于事实真象。故意隐瞒事实真象的，应当追究责任。

**Article 53** The written request for approval of arrest submitted by a public security organ, the written indictment of the People's Procuratorate, and the written judgment of the People's Court must be faithful to the facts. Where the facts are intentionally concealed, liability shall be investigated.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关有权向有关单位和个人收集、调取证据。有关单位和个人应当如实提供证据。  
行政机关在行政执法和查办案件过程中收集的物证、书证、视听资料、电子数据等证据材料，在刑事诉讼中可以作为证据使用。  
对涉及国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私的证据，应当保密。  
凡是伪造证据、隐匿证据或者毁灭证据的，无论属于何方，必须受法律追究。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-3" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第3条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部关于依法保障律师执业权利的规定.md#art-23" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部关于依法保障律师执业权利的规定》第23条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 54** The People's Court, the People's Procuratorate, and the public security organ shall have the authority to collect or procure evidence from relevant entities and individuals. Relevant entities and individuals shall truthfully provide evidence.  
Physical evidence, documentary evidence, audio-visual materials, electronic data, and other evidence materials collected by administrative authorities in the course of administrative law enforcement and case investigation may be used as evidence in criminal proceedings.  
Evidence involving state secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy shall be kept confidential.  
Whoever falsifies, conceals, or destroys evidence, regardless of which party is involved, shall be subject to legal recourse.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　对一切案件的判处都要重证据，重调查研究，不轻信口供。只有被告人供述，没有其他证据的，不能认定被告人有罪和处以刑罚；没有被告人供述，证据确实、充分的，可以认定被告人有罪和处以刑罚。  
证据确实、充分，应当符合以下条件：  
（一）定罪量刑的事实都有证据证明；  
（二）据以定案的证据均经法定程序查证属实；  
（三）综合全案证据，对所认定事实已排除合理怀疑。

**Article 55** In the adjudication of all cases, emphasis shall be placed on evidence, investigation, and study, and no reliance shall be placed on confessions. Where there is only a defendant's statement and no other evidence, the defendant shall not be found guilty and punished. Where there is no defendant's statement and the evidence is reliable and sufficient, the defendant may be found guilty and punished.  
Evidence being reliable and sufficient shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) the facts for conviction and sentencing are all supported by evidence;  
(2) the evidence upon which the adjudication is based has been verified as true through statutory procedures;  
(3) taking all the evidence in the case as a whole, the facts found have been excluded from reasonable doubt.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　采用刑讯逼供等非法方法收集的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人供述和采用暴力、威胁等非法方法收集的证人证言、被害人陈述，应当予以排除。收集物证、书证不符合法定程序，可能严重影响司法公正的，应当予以补正或者作出合理解释；不能补正或者作出合理解释的，对该证据应当予以排除。  
在侦查、审查起诉、审判时发现有应当排除的证据的，应当依法予以排除，不得作为起诉意见、起诉决定和判决的依据。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-410" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第410条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部关于取保候审若干问题的规定.md#art-9" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部关于取保候审若干问题的规定》第9条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部关于取保候审若干问题的规定.md#art-10" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部关于取保候审若干问题的规定》第10条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部关于取保候审若干问题的规定.md#art-11" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部关于取保候审若干问题的规定》第11条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部关于取保候审若干问题的规定.md#art-12" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部关于取保候审若干问题的规定》第12条引用">[5]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部关于取保候审若干问题的规定.md#art-16" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部关于取保候审若干问题的规定》第16条引用">[6]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部关于取保候审若干问题的规定.md#art-21" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部关于取保候审若干问题的规定》第21条引用">[7]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-3" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第3条引用">[8]</a></sup>

**Article 56** Statements of criminal suspects or defendants collected by torture or other illegal means, and testimony of witnesses and statements of victims collected by violence, threat, or other illegal means shall be excluded. Where physical evidence or documentary evidence is collected not in compliance with statutory procedures and may seriously affect judicial justice, the defects shall be corrected or a reasonable explanation shall be provided; where the defects cannot be corrected or a reasonable explanation cannot be provided, such evidence shall be excluded.  
Where evidence that should be excluded is discovered during investigation, examination for prosecution, or adjudication, it shall be excluded according to law and shall not be used as the basis for the opinion on prosecution, the decision to initiate a prosecution, or the judgment.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　人民检察院接到报案、控告、举报或者发现侦查人员以非法方法收集证据的，应当进行调查核实。对于确有以非法方法收集证据情形的，应当提出纠正意见；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 57** Where the People's Procuratorate receives a report, accusation, or tip, or discovers that an investigator has collected evidence by illegal means, it shall investigate and verify. Where there is indeed a situation of collecting evidence by illegal means, the People's Procuratorate shall issue an opinion for correction. Where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　法庭审理过程中，审判人员认为可能存在[本法第五十六条](#art-56)规定的以非法方法收集证据情形的，应当对证据收集的合法性进行法庭调查。  
当事人及其辩护人、诉讼代理人有权申请人民法院对以非法方法收集的证据依法予以排除。申请排除以非法方法收集的证据的，应当提供相关线索或者材料。

**Article 58** During the court trial, where an adjudicator believes that there may be a situation of collecting evidence by illegal means as provided in Article 56 of this Law, a court investigation into the legality of the evidence collection shall be conducted.  
A party, the party's defender, or litigation agent shall have the right to apply to the People's Court for the exclusion of evidence collected by illegal means according to law. Any application for the exclusion of evidence collected by illegal means shall provide relevant leads or materials.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　在对证据收集的合法性进行法庭调查的过程中，人民检察院应当对证据收集的合法性加以证明。  
现有证据材料不能证明证据收集的合法性的，人民检察院可以提请人民法院通知有关侦查人员或者其他人员出庭说明情况；人民法院可以通知有关侦查人员或者其他人员出庭说明情况。有关侦查人员或者其他人员也可以要求出庭说明情况。经人民法院通知，有关人员应当出庭。

**Article 59** During the court investigation into the legality of evidence collection, the People's Procuratorate shall prove the legality of the evidence collection.  
Where the existing evidence materials cannot prove the legality of the evidence collection, the People's Procuratorate may request the People's Court to notify the relevant investigator or other persons to appear in court to explain the circumstances; the People's Court may notify the relevant investigator or other persons to appear in court to explain the circumstances. The relevant investigator or other persons may also request to appear in court to explain the circumstances. Where notified by the People's Court, the relevant person shall appear in court.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　对于经过法庭审理，确认或者不能排除存在[本法第五十六条](#art-56)规定的以非法方法收集证据情形的，对有关证据应当予以排除。

**Article 60** Where, after court trial, it is confirmed or cannot be excluded that there exists a situation of collecting evidence by illegal means as provided in Article 56 of this Law, the relevant evidence shall be excluded.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　证人证言必须在法庭上经过公诉人、被害人和被告人、辩护人双方质证并且查实以后，才能作为定案的根据。法庭查明证人有意作伪证或者隐匿罪证的时候，应当依法处理。

**Article 61** Testimony of a witness may be used as the basis for deciding a case only after it has been examined and verified in court through cross-examination by both the public prosecutor, the victim, and the defendant and his defender. If the court ascertains that a witness has intentionally given false testimony or concealed criminal evidence, it shall handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　凡是知道案件情况的人，都有作证的义务。  
生理上、精神上有缺陷或者年幼，不能辨别是非、不能正确表达的人，不能作证人。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-3" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第3条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 62** All persons who have information about a case shall have the obligation to testify.  
Persons who, due to physical or mental disability or infancy, are unable to distinguish right from wrong or to correctly express themselves shall not be qualified as witnesses.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关应当保障证人及其近亲属的安全。  
对证人及其近亲属进行威胁、侮辱、殴打或者打击报复，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 63** The People's Courts, People's Procuratorates and public security organs shall ensure the safety of witnesses and their close relatives.  
Threatening, insulting, assaulting or retaliating against a witness or his close relatives, which constitutes a crime, shall be subject to criminal liability according to law; if such conduct does not constitute a crime, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　对于危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪、黑社会性质的组织犯罪、毒品犯罪等案件，证人、鉴定人、被害人因在诉讼中作证，本人或者其近亲属的人身安全面临危险的，人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关应当采取以下一项或者多项保护措施：  
（一）不公开真实姓名、住址和工作单位等个人信息；  
（二）采取不暴露外貌、真实声音等出庭作证措施；  
（三）禁止特定的人员接触证人、鉴定人、被害人及其近亲属；  
（四）对人身和住宅采取专门性保护措施；  
（五）其他必要的保护措施。  
证人、鉴定人、被害人认为因在诉讼中作证，本人或者其近亲属的人身安全面临危险的，可以向人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关请求予以保护。  
人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关依法采取保护措施，有关单位和个人应当配合。

**Article 64** In cases involving crimes of endangering national security, terrorist activities, organized crimes of a mafia-like nature, or drug crimes, if a witness, expert evaluator or victim is in danger to the personal safety of himself or his close relatives due to testifying in the proceedings, the People's Courts, People's Procuratorates and public security organs shall take one or more of the following protective measures:  
(1) Not disclosing personal information such as the true name, address and work unit;  
(2) Taking measures such as not revealing the appearance or true voice when testifying in court;  
(3) Prohibiting specific persons from contacting the witness, expert evaluator, victim and their close relatives;  
(4) Adopting special protective measures for the person and residence;  
(5) Other necessary protective measures.  
If a witness, expert evaluator or victim considers that he or his close relative is in danger to personal safety due to testifying in the proceedings, he may request protection from the People's Court, People's Procuratorate or public security organ.  
When the People's Court, People's Procuratorate or public security organ takes protective measures according to law, the relevant entities and individuals shall cooperate.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　证人因履行作证义务而支出的交通、住宿、就餐等费用，应当给予补助。证人作证的补助列入司法机关业务经费，由同级政府财政予以保障。  
有工作单位的证人作证，所在单位不得克扣或者变相克扣其工资、奖金及其他福利待遇。

**Article 65** Expenses such as transportation, accommodation and meals incurred by a witness in fulfilling his obligation to testify shall be subsidized. The subsidy for a witness's testimony shall be included in the operational funds of the judicial organs and guaranteed by the fiscal budget of the government at the same level.  
If a witness is employed, the employer shall not deduct or withhold in any form his salary, bonuses or other welfare benefits due to the witness's giving testimony.

### 第六章　强制措施

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关根据案件情况，对犯罪嫌疑人、被告人可以拘传、取保候审或者监视居住。

**Article 66** The People's Courts, People's Procuratorates and public security organs may, according to the circumstances of the case, summon a criminal suspect or defendant for interrogation by force, release him on bail pending investigation or trial, or place him under residential surveillance.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关对有下列情形之一的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人，可以取保候审：  
（一）可能判处管制、拘役或者独立适用附加刑的；  
（二）可能判处有期徒刑以上刑罚，采取取保候审不致发生社会危险性的；  
（三）患有严重疾病、生活不能自理，怀孕或者正在哺乳自己婴儿的妇女，采取取保候审不致发生社会危险性的；  
（四）羁押期限届满，案件尚未办结，需要采取取保候审的。  
取保候审由公安机关执行。

**Article 67** The People's Courts, People's Procuratorates and public security organs may release a criminal suspect or defendant on bail pending investigation or trial if he falls under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) He may be sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention or independently subject to supplementary punishment;  
(2) He may be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment or a heavier penalty, and releasing him on bail pending investigation or trial would not create a danger to society;  
(3) He has a serious illness, is unable to care for himself, or is a woman who is pregnant or is nursing her own infant, and releasing him on bail pending investigation or trial would not create a danger to society;  
(4) The period of custody has expired and the case has not been concluded, requiring a release on bail pending investigation or trial.  
Release on bail pending investigation or trial shall be executed by the public security organ.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关决定对犯罪嫌疑人、被告人取保候审，应当责令犯罪嫌疑人、被告人提出保证人或者交纳保证金。

**Article 68** When a People's Court, People's Procuratorate or public security organ decides to release a criminal suspect or defendant on bail pending investigation or trial, it shall order the criminal suspect or defendant to provide a guarantor or pay a deposit.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　保证人必须符合下列条件：  
（一）与本案无牵连；  
（二）有能力履行保证义务；  
（三）享有政治权利，人身自由未受到限制；  
（四）有固定的住处和收入。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-90" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第90条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 69** A guarantor must meet the following conditions:  
(1) Having no connection with the case;  
(2) Having the ability to fulfill the guarantee obligations;  
(3) Enjoying political rights and not being subject to restrictions on personal freedom;  
(4) Having a fixed domicile and income.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　保证人应当履行以下义务：  
（一）监督被保证人遵守[本法第七十一条](#art-71)的规定；  
（二）发现被保证人可能发生或者已经发生违反[本法第七十一条](#art-71)规定的行为的，应当及时向执行机关报告。  
被保证人有违反[本法第七十一条](#art-71)规定的行为，保证人未履行保证义务的，对保证人处以罚款，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-99" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第99条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 70** A guarantor shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) Supervising the person released on bail to comply with the provisions of Article 71 of this Law;  
(2) If discovering that the person released on bail may have committed or has committed a violation of Article 71 of this Law, promptly reporting to the executing authority.  
If the person released on bail commits an act violating Article 71 of this Law and the guarantor has failed to fulfill the guarantee obligations, the guarantor shall be subject to a fine; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　被取保候审的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人应当遵守以下规定：  
（一）未经执行机关批准不得离开所居住的市、县；  
（二）住址、工作单位和联系方式发生变动的，在二十四小时以内向执行机关报告；  
（三）在传讯的时候及时到案；  
（四）不得以任何形式干扰证人作证；  
（五）不得毁灭、伪造证据或者串供。  
人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关可以根据案件情况，责令被取保候审的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人遵守以下一项或者多项规定：  
（一）不得进入特定的场所；  
（二）不得与特定的人员会见或者通信；  
（三）不得从事特定的活动；  
（四）将护照等出入境证件、驾驶证件交执行机关保存。  
被取保候审的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人违反前两款规定，已交纳保证金的，没收部分或者全部保证金，并且区别情形，责令犯罪嫌疑人、被告人具结悔过，重新交纳保证金、提出保证人，或者监视居住、予以逮捕。  
对违反取保候审规定，需要予以逮捕的，可以对犯罪嫌疑人、被告人先行拘留。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-91" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第91条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-94" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第94条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-100" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第100条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-103" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第103条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-106" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第106条引用">[5]</a></sup>

**Article 71** A criminal suspect or defendant released on bail pending investigation or trial shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) Not leaving the city or county where he resides without the approval of the executing authority;  
(2) Reporting to the executing authority within 24 hours if his address, work unit or contact information changes;  
(3) Appearing before the authority in a timely manner when summoned;  
(4) Not interfering with witness testimony in any form;  
(5) Not destroying or forging evidence or colluding with others.  
The People's Court, People's Procuratorate or public security organ may, according to the circumstances of the case, order the criminal suspect or defendant released on bail to comply with one or more of the following provisions:  
(1) Not entering specific premises;  
(2) Not meeting or communicating with specific persons;  
(3) Not engaging in specific activities;  
(4) Surrendering his passport and other exit-entry documents and driver's license to the executing authority for safekeeping.  
If a criminal suspect or defendant released on bail violates the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs and has paid a deposit, the deposit shall be partially or wholly forfeited, and depending on the circumstances, the criminal suspect or defendant shall be ordered to make a statement of repentance, pay a new deposit, provide a new guarantor, or be placed under residential surveillance or arrested.  
If a criminal suspect or defendant who has violated the provisions on release on bail needs to be arrested, he may be detained in advance.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　取保候审的决定机关应当综合考虑保证诉讼活动正常进行的需要，被取保候审人的社会危险性，案件的性质、情节，可能判处刑罚的轻重，被取保候审人的经济状况等情况，确定保证金的数额。  
提供保证金的人应当将保证金存入执行机关指定银行的专门账户。

**Article 72** The authority that decides to release a person on bail pending investigation or trial shall determine the amount of the deposit by comprehensively considering the need to ensure the normal conduct of the proceedings, the danger to society posed by the person released on bail, the nature and circumstances of the case, the severity of the possible punishment, and the economic situation of the person released on bail.  
The person providing the deposit shall deposit it into a special account designated by the executing authority in a bank.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　犯罪嫌疑人、被告人在取保候审期间未违反[本法第七十一条](#art-71)规定的，取保候审结束的时候，凭解除取保候审的通知或者有关法律文书到银行领取退还的保证金。

**Article 73** If a criminal suspect or defendant has not violated the provisions of Article 71 of this Law during the period of release on bail pending investigation or trial, upon the conclusion of the release on bail, he may collect the returned deposit from the bank by presenting the notice of release from bail or relevant legal documents.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关对符合逮捕条件，有下列情形之一的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人，可以监视居住：  
（一）患有严重疾病、生活不能自理的；  
（二）怀孕或者正在哺乳自己婴儿的妇女；  
（三）系生活不能自理的人的唯一扶养人；  
（四）因为案件的特殊情况或者办理案件的需要，采取监视居住措施更为适宜的；  
（五）羁押期限届满，案件尚未办结，需要采取监视居住措施的。  
对符合取保候审条件，但犯罪嫌疑人、被告人不能提出保证人，也不交纳保证金的，可以监视居住。  
监视居住由公安机关执行。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-141" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第141条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 74** The People's Courts, People's Procuratorates and public security organs may place a criminal suspect or defendant under residential surveillance if he meets the conditions for arrest and falls under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) He has a serious illness and is unable to care for himself;  
(2) He is a woman who is pregnant or is nursing her own infant;  
(3) He is the sole supporter of a person who is unable to care for himself;  
(4) Due to the special circumstances of the case or the needs of handling the case, residential surveillance is more appropriate;  
(5) The period of custody has expired and the case has not been concluded, requiring residential surveillance.  
If a criminal suspect or defendant meets the conditions for release on bail pending investigation or trial but is unable to provide a guarantor or pay a deposit, he may be placed under residential surveillance.  
Residential surveillance shall be executed by the public security organ.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　监视居住应当在犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的住处执行；无固定住处的，可以在指定的居所执行。对于涉嫌危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪，在住处执行可能有碍侦查的，经上一级公安机关批准，也可以在指定的居所执行。但是，不得在羁押场所、专门的办案场所执行。  
指定居所监视居住的，除无法通知的以外，应当在执行监视居住后二十四小时以内，通知被监视居住人的家属。  
被监视居住的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人委托辩护人，适用[本法第三十四条](#art-34)的规定。  
人民检察院对指定居所监视居住的决定和执行是否合法实行监督。

**Article 75** Residential surveillance shall be executed at the domicile of the criminal suspect or defendant; if he has no fixed domicile, it may be executed at a designated residence. For those suspected of crimes of endangering national security or terrorist activities, if execution at the domicile may hinder the investigation, it may also be executed at a designated residence with the approval of the public security organ at the next higher level. However, it shall not be executed in custody facilities or specialized case-handling sites.  
If residential surveillance is executed at a designated residence, the family members of the person under residential surveillance shall be notified within 24 hours after the execution of the surveillance, unless it is impossible to do so.  
The provisions of Article 34 of this Law shall apply to the entrustment of defense counsel by a criminal suspect or defendant under residential surveillance.  
The People's Procuratorate shall supervise whether the decision and execution of designated residence surveillance are lawful.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　指定居所监视居住的期限应当折抵刑期。被判处管制的，监视居住一日折抵刑期一日；被判处拘役、有期徒刑的，监视居住二日折抵刑期一日。

**Article 76** The period of designated residence surveillance shall be deducted from the term of sentence. If sentenced to public surveillance, one day of residential surveillance shall be deducted from one day of the sentence; if sentenced to criminal detention or fixed-term imprisonment, two days of residential surveillance shall be deducted from one day of the sentence.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　被监视居住的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人应当遵守以下规定：  
（一）未经执行机关批准不得离开执行监视居住的处所；  
（二）未经执行机关批准不得会见他人或者通信；  
（三）在传讯的时候及时到案；  
（四）不得以任何形式干扰证人作证；  
（五）不得毁灭、伪造证据或者串供；  
（六）将护照等出入境证件、身份证件、驾驶证件交执行机关保存。  
被监视居住的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人违反前款规定，情节严重的，可以予以逮捕；需要予以逮捕的，可以对犯罪嫌疑人、被告人先行拘留。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院关于在审理经济纠纷案件中涉及经济犯罪嫌疑若干问题的规定.md#art-8" title="被《最高人民法院关于在审理经济纠纷案件中涉及经济犯罪嫌疑若干问题的规定》第8条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-108" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第108条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 77** A criminal suspect or defendant under residential surveillance shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) Not leaving the premises where he is under residential surveillance without the approval of the executing authority;  
(2) Not meeting or communicating with others without the approval of the executing authority;  
(3) Appearing before the authority in a timely manner when summoned;  
(4) Not interfering with witness testimony in any form;  
(5) Not destroying or forging evidence or colluding with others;  
(6) Surrendering his passport and other exit-entry documents, identity documents and driver's license to the executing authority for safekeeping.  
If a criminal suspect or defendant under residential surveillance violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph and the circumstances are serious, he may be arrested; if an arrest is necessary, he may be detained in advance.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　执行机关对被监视居住的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人，可以采取电子监控、不定期检查等监视方法对其遵守监视居住规定的情况进行监督；在侦查期间，可以对被监视居住的犯罪嫌疑人的通信进行监控。

**Article 78** The executing authority may use electronic monitoring, irregular inspections and other surveillance methods to monitor the compliance of a criminal suspect or defendant under residential surveillance with the provisions on residential surveillance; during the investigation period, it may monitor the communications of the criminal suspect under residential surveillance.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关对犯罪嫌疑人、被告人取保候审最长不得超过十二个月，监视居住最长不得超过六个月。  
在取保候审、监视居住期间，不得中断对案件的侦查、起诉和审理。对于发现不应当追究刑事责任或者取保候审、监视居住期限届满的，应当及时解除取保候审、监视居住。解除取保候审、监视居住，应当及时通知被取保候审、监视居住人和有关单位。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/法律解释/全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第七十九条第三款的解释.md#art-1" title="被《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第七十九条第三款的解释》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 79** The period of release on bail pending investigation or trial for a criminal suspect or defendant by the People's Court, People's Procuratorate or public security organ shall not exceed 12 months in total, and the period of residential surveillance shall not exceed 6 months in total.  
During the period of release on bail pending investigation or trial or residential surveillance, the investigation, prosecution and adjudication of the case shall not be interrupted. If it is discovered that criminal liability should not be investigated, or the period of release on bail or residential surveillance has expired, the release on bail or residential surveillance shall be promptly terminated. The person released on bail or under residential surveillance and the relevant entities shall be promptly notified of the termination.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　逮捕犯罪嫌疑人、被告人，必须经过人民检察院批准或者人民法院决定，由公安机关执行。

**Article 80** Arrest of a criminal suspect or defendant must be approved by the People's Procuratorate or decided by the People's Court, and shall be executed by the public security organ.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　对有证据证明有犯罪事实，可能判处徒刑以上刑罚的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人，采取取保候审尚不足以防止发生下列社会危险性的，应当予以逮捕：  
（一）可能实施新的犯罪的；  
（二）有危害国家安全、公共安全或者社会秩序的现实危险的；  
（三）可能毁灭、伪造证据，干扰证人作证或者串供的；  
（四）可能对被害人、举报人、控告人实施打击报复的；  
（五）企图自杀或者逃跑的。  
批准或者决定逮捕，应当将犯罪嫌疑人、被告人涉嫌犯罪的性质、情节，认罪认罚等情况，作为是否可能发生社会危险性的考虑因素。  
对有证据证明有犯罪事实，可能判处十年有期徒刑以上刑罚的，或者有证据证明有犯罪事实，可能判处徒刑以上刑罚，曾经故意犯罪或者身份不明的，应当予以逮捕。  
被取保候审、监视居住的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人违反取保候审、监视居住规定，情节严重的，可以予以逮捕。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-134" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第134条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部关于适用认罪认罚从宽制度的指导意见.md#art-6" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部关于适用认罪认罚从宽制度的指导意见》第6条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 81** If there is evidence of criminal facts, and a criminal suspect or defendant may be sentenced to a punishment of not less than fixed-term imprisonment, and if release on bail pending investigation or trial is insufficient to prevent the following social dangers, he shall be arrested:  
(a) he may commit a new crime;  
(b) there is a real danger of jeopardizing national security, public safety, or social order;  
(c) he may destroy or fabricate evidence, interfere with witness testimony, or collude with others to make false statements;  
(d) he may retaliate against the victim, the reporting person, or the accuser;  
(e) he may attempt to commit suicide or escape.  
When approving or deciding on an arrest, the nature and circumstances of the crime suspected of being committed by the criminal suspect or defendant, as well as his admission of guilt and acceptance of punishment, shall be considered as factors in assessing whether there is a social danger.  
If there is evidence of criminal facts and the criminal suspect or defendant may be sentenced to a punishment of not less than ten years of fixed-term imprisonment, or if there is evidence of criminal facts and the criminal suspect or defendant may be sentenced to a punishment of not less than fixed-term imprisonment and he has previously intentionally committed a crime or his identity is unknown, he shall be arrested.  
If a criminal suspect or defendant who is released on bail pending investigation or trial or placed under residential surveillance violates the provisions on release on bail pending investigation or trial or residential surveillance, and the circumstances are serious, he may be arrested.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　公安机关对于现行犯或者重大嫌疑分子，如果有下列情形之一的，可以先行拘留：  
（一）正在预备犯罪、实行犯罪或者在犯罪后即时被发觉的；  
（二）被害人或者在场亲眼看见的人指认他犯罪的；  
（三）在身边或者住处发现有犯罪证据的；  
（四）犯罪后企图自杀、逃跑或者在逃的；  
（五）有毁灭、伪造证据或者串供可能的；  
（六）不讲真实姓名、住址，身份不明的；  
（七）有流窜作案、多次作案、结伙作案重大嫌疑的。

**Article 82** The public security organ may detain a culprit caught in the act or a major suspect if any of the following circumstances exists:  
(a) he is preparing to commit a crime, in the process of committing a crime, or is discovered immediately after committing a crime;  
(b) the victim or a person who witnessed the crime on the spot identifies him as having committed the crime;  
(c) evidence of a crime is found on his person or at his residence;  
(d) after committing a crime, he attempts to commit suicide, escape, or is in flight;  
(e) there is a possibility of destroying or fabricating evidence or colluding with others to make false statements;  
(f) he does not tell his true name or address, and his identity is unknown;  
(g) there is a major suspicion that he commits crimes while moving from place to place, repeatedly commits crimes, or commits crimes in partnership.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　公安机关在异地执行拘留、逮捕的时候，应当通知被拘留、逮捕人所在地的公安机关，被拘留、逮捕人所在地的公安机关应当予以配合。

**Article 83** When a public security organ serves a detention or arrest warrant in a different locality, it shall notify the public security organ of the locality where the detained or arrested person is located, and that public security organ shall cooperate.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　对于有下列情形的人，任何公民都可以立即扭送公安机关、人民检察院或者人民法院处理：  
（一）正在实行犯罪或者在犯罪后即时被发觉的；  
（二）通缉在案的；  
（三）越狱逃跑的；  
（四）正在被追捕的。

**Article 84** Any citizen may immediately seize and deliver to a public security organ, a People's Procuratorate, or a People's Court for handling any person falling under any of the following circumstances:  
(a) he is in the process of committing a crime or is discovered immediately after committing a crime;  
(b) he is wanted;  
(c) he escapes from prison;  
(d) he is being pursued.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　公安机关拘留人的时候，必须出示拘留证。  
拘留后，应当立即将被拘留人送看守所羁押，至迟不得超过二十四小时。除无法通知或者涉嫌危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪通知可能有碍侦查的情形以外，应当在拘留后二十四小时以内，通知被拘留人的家属。有碍侦查的情形消失以后，应当立即通知被拘留人的家属。

**Article 85** When a public security organ detains a person, it must produce a detention warrant.  
After detention, the detained person shall be immediately delivered to a detention house for custody, and the time shall not exceed twenty-four hours. Unless it is impossible to notify or in cases where the crime involves national security or terrorist activities and notification may hinder the investigation, the family of the detained person shall be notified within twenty-four hours after detention. After the circumstances that hinder the investigation disappear, the family of the detained person shall be immediately notified.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　公安机关对被拘留的人，应当在拘留后的二十四小时以内进行讯问。在发现不应当拘留的时候，必须立即释放，发给释放证明。

**Article 86** The public security organ shall interrogate a detained person within twenty-four hours after detention. If it is discovered that the person should not have been detained, he must be immediately released and issued a release certificate.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　公安机关要求逮捕犯罪嫌疑人的时候，应当写出提请批准逮捕书，连同案卷材料、证据，一并移送同级人民检察院审查批准。必要的时候，人民检察院可以派人参加公安机关对于重大案件的讨论。

**Article 87** When a public security organ requests approval for the arrest of a criminal suspect, it shall submit a written request for approval of arrest together with the case files and evidence to the People's Procuratorate at the same level for examination and approval. If necessary, the People's Procuratorate may send personnel to participate in the public security organ's discussion of major cases.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　人民检察院审查批准逮捕，可以讯问犯罪嫌疑人；有下列情形之一的，应当讯问犯罪嫌疑人：  
（一）对是否符合逮捕条件有疑问的；  
（二）犯罪嫌疑人要求向检察人员当面陈述的；  
（三）侦查活动可能有重大违法行为的。  
人民检察院审查批准逮捕，可以询问证人等诉讼参与人，听取辩护律师的意见；辩护律师提出要求的，应当听取辩护律师的意见。

**Article 88** When a People's Procuratorate examines and approves an arrest, it may interrogate the criminal suspect; if any of the following circumstances exists, the criminal suspect shall be interrogated:  
(a) there are doubts about whether the conditions for arrest are met;  
(b) the criminal suspect requests to make a statement in person to the procuratorial personnel;  
(c) there may be major illegal activities in the investigation.  
When a People's Procuratorate examines and approves an arrest, it may question witnesses and other litigation participants and hear the opinions of defense counsel; if defense counsel makes a request, the opinions shall be heard.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　人民检察院审查批准逮捕犯罪嫌疑人由检察长决定。重大案件应当提交检察委员会讨论决定。

**Article 89** The decision to approve or disapprove the arrest of a criminal suspect by the People's Procuratorate shall be made by the chief procurator. Major cases shall be submitted to the procuratorial committee for discussion and decision.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　人民检察院对于公安机关提请批准逮捕的案件进行审查后，应当根据情况分别作出批准逮捕或者不批准逮捕的决定。对于批准逮捕的决定，公安机关应当立即执行，并且将执行情况及时通知人民检察院。对于不批准逮捕的，人民检察院应当说明理由，需要补充侦查的，应当同时通知公安机关。

**Article 90** After examining a case for which the public security organ requests approval of arrest, the People's Procuratorate shall, based on the circumstances, decide separately whether to approve or disapprove the arrest. For a decision to approve the arrest, the public security organ shall immediately execute it and notify the People's Procuratorate of the execution in a timely manner. If the arrest is disapproved, the People's Procuratorate shall state the reasons, and if supplementary investigation is needed, it shall simultaneously notify the public security organ.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　公安机关对被拘留的人，认为需要逮捕的，应当在拘留后的三日以内，提请人民检察院审查批准。在特殊情况下，提请审查批准的时间可以延长一日至四日。  
对于流窜作案、多次作案、结伙作案的重大嫌疑分子，提请审查批准的时间可以延长至三十日。  
人民检察院应当自接到公安机关提请批准逮捕书后的七日以内，作出批准逮捕或者不批准逮捕的决定。人民检察院不批准逮捕的，公安机关应当在接到通知后立即释放，并且将执行情况及时通知人民检察院。对于需要继续侦查，并且符合取保候审、监视居住条件的，依法取保候审或者监视居住。

**Article 91** If the public security organ considers it necessary to arrest a detained person, it shall, within three days after detention, submit a request to the People's Procuratorate for examination and approval. Under special circumstances, the time for submitting the request for examination and approval may be extended by one to four days.  
For major suspects who commit crimes while moving from place to place, repeatedly commit crimes, or commit crimes in partnership, the time for submitting the request for examination and approval may be extended to thirty days.  
The People's Procuratorate shall, within seven days after receiving the written request for approval of arrest from the public security organ, make a decision to approve or disapprove the arrest. If the People's Procuratorate disapproves the arrest, the public security organ shall immediately release the person after receiving the notice and notify the People's Procuratorate of the execution in a timely manner. If further investigation is needed and the conditions for release on bail pending investigation or trial or residential surveillance are met, release on bail pending investigation or trial or residential surveillance shall be granted according to law.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　公安机关对人民检察院不批准逮捕的决定，认为有错误的时候，可以要求复议，但是必须将被拘留的人立即释放。如果意见不被接受，可以向上一级人民检察院提请复核。上级人民检察院应当立即复核，作出是否变更的决定，通知下级人民检察院和公安机关执行。

**Article 92** If the public security organ considers the decision of the People's Procuratorate to disapprove the arrest to be erroneous, it may request reconsideration, but the detained person must be immediately released. If the public security organ's opinion is not accepted, it may submit a request for review to the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level. The higher-level People's Procuratorate shall immediately conduct a review and decide whether to change the decision, and notify the lower-level People's Procuratorate and public security organ to execute it.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　公安机关逮捕人的时候，必须出示逮捕证。  
逮捕后，应当立即将被逮捕人送看守所羁押。除无法通知的以外，应当在逮捕后二十四小时以内，通知被逮捕人的家属。

**Article 93** When a public security organ arrests a person, it must produce an arrest warrant.  
After arrest, the arrested person shall be immediately delivered to a detention house for custody. Unless it is impossible to notify, the family of the arrested person shall be notified within twenty-four hours after the arrest.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　人民法院、人民检察院对于各自决定逮捕的人，公安机关对于经人民检察院批准逮捕的人，都必须在逮捕后的二十四小时以内进行讯问。在发现不应当逮捕的时候，必须立即释放，发给释放证明。

**Article 94** For a person whose arrest is decided by a People's Court or a People's Procuratorate, or for a person whose arrest is approved by the People's Procuratorate, the public security organ must, in each case, interrogate the person within twenty-four hours after the arrest. If it is discovered that the person should not have been arrested, he must be immediately released and issued a release certificate.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　犯罪嫌疑人、被告人被逮捕后，人民检察院仍应当对羁押的必要性进行审查。对不需要继续羁押的，应当建议予以释放或者变更强制措施。有关机关应当在十日以内将处理情况通知人民检察院。

**Article 95** After a criminal suspect or defendant is arrested, the People's Procuratorate shall still review the necessity of custody. If continued custody is unnecessary, it shall recommend that the person be released or that the compulsory measures be changed. The relevant organ shall notify the People's Procuratorate of the handling within ten days.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关如果发现对犯罪嫌疑人、被告人采取强制措施不当的，应当及时撤销或者变更。公安机关释放被逮捕的人或者变更逮捕措施的，应当通知原批准的人民检察院。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民检察院关于审查起诉期间犯罪嫌疑人脱逃或者患有严重疾病的应当如何处理的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民检察院关于审查起诉期间犯罪嫌疑人脱逃或者患有严重疾病的应当如何处理的批复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 96** If a People's Court, a People's Procuratorate, or a public security organ discovers that the compulsory measures taken against a criminal suspect or defendant are improper, it shall promptly revoke or change them. If the public security organ releases an arrested person or changes the arrest measures, it shall notify the original People's Procuratorate that approved the arrest.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　犯罪嫌疑人、被告人及其法定代理人、近亲属或者辩护人有权申请变更强制措施。人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关收到申请后，应当在三日以内作出决定；不同意变更强制措施的，应当告知申请人，并说明不同意的理由。

**Article 97** A criminal suspect or defendant, his legal representative, close relative, or defense counsel has the right to apply for a change of compulsory measures. The People's Court, People's Procuratorate, or public security organ shall, after receiving the application, make a decision within three days; if the change of compulsory measures is not approved, the applicant shall be informed and the reasons for disapproval shall be stated.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　犯罪嫌疑人、被告人被羁押的案件，不能在本法规定的侦查羁押、审查起诉、一审、二审期限内办结的，对犯罪嫌疑人、被告人应当予以释放；需要继续查证、审理的，对犯罪嫌疑人、被告人可以取保候审或者监视居住。

**Article 98** If a case in which a criminal suspect or defendant is in custody cannot be concluded within the time limits for investigation and custody, examination and prosecution, first instance, and second instance provided by this Law, the criminal suspect or defendant shall be released; if further investigation or adjudication is necessary, the criminal suspect or defendant may be released on bail pending investigation or trial or placed under residential surveillance.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　人民法院、人民检察院或者公安机关对被采取强制措施法定期限届满的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人，应当予以释放、解除取保候审、监视居住或者依法变更强制措施。犯罪嫌疑人、被告人及其法定代理人、近亲属或者辩护人对于人民法院、人民检察院或者公安机关采取强制措施法定期限届满的，有权要求解除强制措施。

**Article 99** For a criminal suspect or defendant against whom compulsory measures have been taken and the statutory time limit expires, the People's Court, People's Procuratorate, or public security organ shall release the person, cancel the release on bail pending investigation or trial or residential surveillance, or change the compulsory measures according to law. A criminal suspect or defendant, his legal representative, close relative, or defense counsel has the right to request the cancellation of compulsory measures when the statutory time limit for compulsory measures taken by the People's Court, People's Procuratorate, or public security organ expires.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　人民检察院在审查批准逮捕工作中，如果发现公安机关的侦查活动有违法情况，应当通知公安机关予以纠正，公安机关应当将纠正情况通知人民检察院。

**Article 100** If, in the process of examining and approving an arrest, the People's Procuratorate discovers any illegal activities in the investigation carried out by the public security organ, it shall notify the public security organ to rectify them, and the public security organ shall notify the People's Procuratorate of the rectification.

### 第七章　附带民事诉讼

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　被害人由于被告人的犯罪行为而遭受物质损失的，在刑事诉讼过程中，有权提起附带民事诉讼。被害人死亡或者丧失行为能力的，被害人的法定代理人、近亲属有权提起附带民事诉讼。  
如果是国家财产、集体财产遭受损失的，人民检察院在提起公诉的时候，可以提起附带民事诉讼。

**Article 101** If a victim has suffered material losses as a result of a criminal act committed by a defendant, the victim has the right to file an incidental civil action during the criminal action. If the victim dies or loses capacity, his legal representative or close relative has the right to file an incidental civil action.  
If state property or collective property suffers losses, the People's Procuratorate may file an incidental civil action when initiating a public prosecution.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　人民法院在必要的时候，可以采取保全措施，查封、扣押或者冻结被告人的财产。附带民事诉讼原告人或者人民检察院可以申请人民法院采取保全措施。人民法院采取保全措施，适用民事诉讼法的有关规定。

**Article 102** When necessary, a People's Court may take preservation measures, such as sealing up, seizing, or freezing the property of the defendant. The plaintiff in the incidental civil action or the People's Procuratorate may apply to the People's Court for preservation measures. When a People's Court takes preservation measures, the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law shall apply.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　人民法院审理附带民事诉讼案件，可以进行调解，或者根据物质损失情况作出判决、裁定。

**Article 103** When hearing an incidental civil action case, a People's Court may conduct mediation or make a judgment or ruling based on the material losses.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　附带民事诉讼应当同刑事案件一并审判，只有为了防止刑事案件审判的过分迟延，才可以在刑事案件审判后，由同一审判组织继续审理附带民事诉讼。

**Article 104** An incidental civil action shall be adjudicated together with the criminal case. Only to prevent excessive delay in the adjudication of the criminal case may the same adjudication organization continue to hear the incidental civil action after the criminal case has been adjudicated.

### 第八章　期间、送达

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　期间以时、日、月计算。  
期间开始的时和日不算在期间以内。  
法定期间不包括路途上的时间。上诉状或者其他文件在期满前已经交邮的，不算过期。  
期间的最后一日为节假日的，以节假日后的第一日为期满日期，但犯罪嫌疑人、被告人或者罪犯在押期间，应当至期满之日为止，不得因节假日而延长。

**Article 105** Time periods shall be calculated in hours, days, and months.  
The hour or day on which a time period begins shall not be counted within the time period.  
The statutory time period shall not include travel time. If an appeal or other document is already posted before the expiration of the time period, it shall not be considered overdue.  
If the last day of a time period falls on a holiday, the first day after the holiday shall be considered the expiration date, except for a criminal suspect, defendant, or convict in custody, for whom the time period shall run until the expiration date and shall not be extended due to holidays.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　当事人由于不能抗拒的原因或者有其他正当理由而耽误期限的，在障碍消除后五日以内，可以申请继续进行应当在期满以前完成的诉讼活动。  
前款申请是否准许，由人民法院裁定。

**Article 106** If a party misses a time limit due to irresistible reasons or other legitimate grounds, he may, within five days after the removal of the obstacles, apply to continue the procedural activities that should have been completed before the time limit expired.  
Whether to grant the application mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be decided by the People's Court.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　送达传票、通知书和其他诉讼文件应当交给收件人本人；如果本人不在，可以交给他的成年家属或者所在单位的负责人员代收。  
收件人本人或者代收人拒绝接收或者拒绝签名、盖章的时候，送达人可以邀请他的邻居或者其他见证人到场，说明情况，把文件留在他的住处，在送达证上记明拒绝的事由、送达的日期，由送达人签名，即认为已经送达。

**Article 107** Summons, notices, and other procedural documents shall be served on the addressee in person; if the addressee is absent, they may be received on his behalf by an adult family member or a responsible person of the unit where he works.  
If the addressee or the person receiving the documents on his behalf refuses to accept or sign or seal the documents, the server may invite a neighbor or other witness to be present, explain the situation, leave the documents at the place of residence, note on the service certificate the reason for refusal and the date of service, and sign it, and the service shall be deemed to have been completed.

### 第九章　其他规定

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　本法下列用语的含意是：  
（一）“侦查”是指公安机关、人民检察院对于刑事案件，依照法律进行的收集证据、查明案情的工作和有关的强制性措施；  
（二）“当事人”是指被害人、自诉人、犯罪嫌疑人、被告人、附带民事诉讼的原告人和被告人；  
（三）“法定代理人”是指被代理人的父母、养父母、监护人和负有保护责任的机关、团体的代表；  
（四）“诉讼参与人”是指当事人、法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人、证人、鉴定人和翻译人员；  
（五）“诉讼代理人”是指公诉案件的被害人及其法定代理人或者近亲属、自诉案件的自诉人及其法定代理人委托代为参加诉讼的人和附带民事诉讼的当事人及其法定代理人委托代为参加诉讼的人；  
（六）“近亲属”是指夫、妻、父、母、子、女、同胞兄弟姊妹。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-55" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第55条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 108** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:  
(a) "Investigation" refers to the work of collecting evidence and ascertaining the facts of a criminal case as conducted according to law by a public security organ or People's Procuratorate, and the related compulsory measures;  
(b) "Party" refers to the victim, the private prosecutor, the criminal suspect, the defendant, the plaintiff in an incidental civil action, and the defendant in an incidental civil action;  
(c) "Legal representative" refers to the parent, adoptive parent, guardian of the represented person, and the representative of the organ or organization responsible for protection;  
(d) "Litigation participant" refers to the party, the legal representative, the litigation agent, the defense counsel, the witness, the expert evaluator, and the interpreter;  
(e) "Litigation agent" refers to a person entrusted to participate in the proceedings on behalf of the victim of a public prosecution case and his legal representative or close relative, on behalf of the private prosecutor of a private prosecution case and his legal representative, and on behalf of the parties to an incidental civil action and their legal representatives;  
(f) "Close relative" refers to husband, wife, father, mother, son, daughter, and full siblings.

## 第二编　立案、侦查和提起公诉

### 第一章　立案

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　公安机关或者人民检察院发现犯罪事实或者犯罪嫌疑人，应当按照管辖范围，立案侦查。

**Article 109** If a public security organ or People's Procuratorate discovers criminal facts or a criminal suspect, it shall open a case and conduct an investigation within its jurisdiction.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　任何单位和个人发现有犯罪事实或者犯罪嫌疑人，有权利也有义务向公安机关、人民检察院或者人民法院报案或者举报。  
被害人对侵犯其人身、财产权利的犯罪事实或者犯罪嫌疑人，有权向公安机关、人民检察院或者人民法院报案或者控告。  
公安机关、人民检察院或者人民法院对于报案、控告、举报，都应当接受。对于不属于自己管辖的，应当移送主管机关处理，并且通知报案人、控告人、举报人；对于不属于自己管辖而又必须采取紧急措施的，应当先采取紧急措施，然后移送主管机关。  
犯罪人向公安机关、人民检察院或者人民法院自首的，适用第三款规定。

**Article 110** Any unit or individual that discovers criminal facts or a criminal suspect has the right and the duty to report or provide information to a public security organ, People's Procuratorate, or People's Court.  
A victim has the right to report or file a complaint with a public security organ, People's Procuratorate, or People's Court regarding criminal facts or a criminal suspect that infringes upon his personal or property rights.  
A public security organ, People's Procuratorate, or People's Court shall accept all reports, complaints, and information. If the matter does not fall within its jurisdiction, it shall transfer the case to the competent authority for handling and inform the reporter, complainant, or informant; if the matter does not fall within its jurisdiction but emergency measures must be taken, it shall first take emergency measures and then transfer the case to the competent authority.  
If a criminal voluntarily surrenders to a public security organ, People's Procuratorate, or People's Court, the provisions of paragraph 3 shall apply.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　报案、控告、举报可以用书面或者口头提出。接受口头报案、控告、举报的工作人员，应当写成笔录，经宣读无误后，由报案人、控告人、举报人签名或者盖章。  
接受控告、举报的工作人员，应当向控告人、举报人说明诬告应负的法律责任。但是，只要不是捏造事实，伪造证据，即使控告、举报的事实有出入，甚至是错告的，也要和诬告严格加以区别。  
公安机关、人民检察院或者人民法院应当保障报案人、控告人、举报人及其近亲属的安全。报案人、控告人、举报人如果不愿公开自己的姓名和报案、控告、举报的行为，应当为他保守秘密。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-5" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第5条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 111** Reports, complaints, and information may be made in writing or orally. The personnel receiving oral reports, complaints, or information shall make a record, and after reading it to ensure accuracy, the reporter, complainant, or informant shall sign or seal it.  
The personnel receiving complaints or information shall inform the complainant or informant of the legal liability for false accusation. However, as long as facts are not fabricated or evidence not forged, even if there are discrepancies in the facts of the complaint or information, or even if it is a mistaken accusation, it shall be strictly distinguished from false accusation.  
A public security organ, People's Procuratorate, or People's Court shall ensure the safety of the reporter, complainant, informant, and their close relatives. If the reporter, complainant, or informant does not wish to make his name and the act of reporting, complaining, or providing information public, confidentiality shall be maintained.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　人民法院、人民检察院或者公安机关对于报案、控告、举报和自首的材料，应当按照管辖范围，迅速进行审查，认为有犯罪事实需要追究刑事责任的时候，应当立案；认为没有犯罪事实，或者犯罪事实显著轻微，不需要追究刑事责任的时候，不予立案，并且将不立案的原因通知控告人。控告人如果不服，可以申请复议。

**Article 112** A People's Court, People's Procuratorate, or public security organ shall, within its jurisdiction, promptly examine the materials of reports, complaints, information, and voluntary surrenders. If it believes that there are criminal facts and that criminal liability shall be investigated, it shall open a case; if it believes that there are no criminal facts, or that the criminal facts are obviously minor and do not require the investigation of criminal liability, it shall not open a case and shall inform the complainant of the reasons for not opening a case. If the complainant disagrees, he may apply for reconsideration.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　人民检察院认为公安机关对应当立案侦查的案件而不立案侦查的，或者被害人认为公安机关对应当立案侦查的案件而不立案侦查，向人民检察院提出的，人民检察院应当要求公安机关说明不立案的理由。人民检察院认为公安机关不立案理由不能成立的，应当通知公安机关立案，公安机关接到通知后应当立案。

**Article 113** If a People's Procuratorate considers that a public security organ should have opened a case for investigation but fails to do so, or if a victim submits to the People's Procuratorate that a public security organ should have opened a case for investigation but fails to do so, the People's Procuratorate shall request the public security organ to provide the reasons for not opening a case. If the People's Procuratorate finds the reasons given by the public security organ for not opening a case to be untenable, it shall notify the public security organ to open a case, and the public security organ shall open a case upon receiving the notice.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　对于自诉案件，被害人有权向人民法院直接起诉。被害人死亡或者丧失行为能力的，被害人的法定代理人、近亲属有权向人民法院起诉。人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 114** For a private prosecution case, the victim has the right to directly file a lawsuit with the People's Court. If the victim dies or loses capacity, his legal representative or close relative has the right to file a lawsuit with the People's Court. The People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

### 第二章　侦查

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　公安机关对已经立案的刑事案件，应当进行侦查，收集、调取犯罪嫌疑人有罪或者无罪、罪轻或者罪重的证据材料。对现行犯或者重大嫌疑分子可以依法先行拘留，对符合逮捕条件的犯罪嫌疑人，应当依法逮捕。

**Article 115** A public security organ shall conduct an investigation into a criminal case that has been opened, collect and gather evidence materials proving whether the criminal suspect is guilty or innocent, or whether the crime is light or serious. A culprit caught in the act or a major suspect may be detained in advance according to law, and a criminal suspect who meets the conditions for arrest shall be arrested according to law.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　公安机关经过侦查，对有证据证明有犯罪事实的案件，应当进行预审，对收集、调取的证据材料予以核实。

**Article 116** After conducting an investigation into a case where there is evidence of criminal facts, a public security organ shall conduct a preliminary hearing to verify the evidence materials collected and gathered.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　当事人和辩护人、诉讼代理人、利害关系人对于司法机关及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，有权向该机关申诉或者控告：  
（一）采取强制措施法定期限届满，不予以释放、解除或者变更的；  
（二）应当退还取保候审保证金不退还的；  
（三）对与案件无关的财物采取查封、扣押、冻结措施的；  
（四）应当解除查封、扣押、冻结不解除的；  
（五）贪污、挪用、私分、调换、违反规定使用查封、扣押、冻结的财物的。  
受理申诉或者控告的机关应当及时处理。对处理不服的，可以向同级人民检察院申诉；人民检察院直接受理的案件，可以向上一级人民检察院申诉。人民检察院对申诉应当及时进行审查，情况属实的，通知有关机关予以纠正。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-555" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第555条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-556" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第556条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 117** A party, defense counsel, litigation agent, or interested person has the right to file a complaint or accusation with the relevant authority against a judicial organ or its personnel for any of the following acts:  
(a) failing to release, cancel, or change compulsory measures after the statutory time limit for the compulsory measures has expired;  
(b) failing to return the release on bail pending investigation or trial deposit that should be returned;  
(c) taking measures to seal up, seize, or freeze property unrelated to the case;  
(d) failing to lift the sealing up, seizure, or freezing that should have been lifted;  
(e) embezzling, misappropriating, privately dividing, exchanging, or using property that has been sealed up, seized, or frozen in violation of regulations.  
The authority that accepts the complaint or accusation shall handle it promptly. If a party is dissatisfied with the handling, he may file a complaint with the People's Procuratorate at the same level; for cases directly accepted by the People's Procuratorate, a complaint may be filed with the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level. The People's Procuratorate shall promptly examine the complaint, and if the facts are verified, it shall notify the relevant authority to make corrections.

#### 第二节　讯问犯罪嫌疑人

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　讯问犯罪嫌疑人必须由人民检察院或者公安机关的侦查人员负责进行。讯问的时候，侦查人员不得少于二人。  
犯罪嫌疑人被送交看守所羁押以后，侦查人员对其进行讯问，应当在看守所内进行。

**Article 118** The interrogation of a criminal suspect must be conducted by investigators from the People's Procuratorate or the public security organ. During interrogation, there shall be no fewer than two investigators.  
After a criminal suspect is delivered to a detention house for custody, the interrogation by investigators shall be conducted in the detention house.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　对不需要逮捕、拘留的犯罪嫌疑人，可以传唤到犯罪嫌疑人所在市、县内的指定地点或者到他的住处进行讯问，但是应当出示人民检察院或者公安机关的证明文件。对在现场发现的犯罪嫌疑人，经出示工作证件，可以口头传唤，但应当在讯问笔录中注明。  
传唤、拘传持续的时间不得超过十二小时；案情特别重大、复杂，需要采取拘留、逮捕措施的，传唤、拘传持续的时间不得超过二十四小时。  
不得以连续传唤、拘传的形式变相拘禁犯罪嫌疑人。传唤、拘传犯罪嫌疑人，应当保证犯罪嫌疑人的饮食和必要的休息时间。

**Article 119** A criminal suspect who does not need to be arrested or detained may be summoned to a designated location within the city or county where he is located or to his residence for interrogation, but the identity certificate of the People's Procuratorate or public security organ shall be produced. For a criminal suspect discovered at the scene, an oral summons may be made after presenting a work certificate, but this shall be noted in the interrogation record.  
The duration of each summons or compulsory appearance shall not exceed twelve hours; if the case is exceptionally serious or complex and requires measures of detention or arrest, the duration of the summons or compulsory appearance shall not exceed twenty-four hours.  
A criminal suspect shall not be detained under duress by way of continuous summons or compulsory appearance. When summoning or compelling the appearance of a criminal suspect, the criminal suspect shall be given sufficient time for meals and necessary rest.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　侦查人员在讯问犯罪嫌疑人的时候，应当首先讯问犯罪嫌疑人是否有犯罪行为，让他陈述有罪的情节或者无罪的辩解，然后向他提出问题。犯罪嫌疑人对侦查人员的提问，应当如实回答。但是对与本案无关的问题，有拒绝回答的权利。  
侦查人员在讯问犯罪嫌疑人的时候，应当告知犯罪嫌疑人享有的诉讼权利，如实供述自己罪行可以从宽处理和认罪认罚的法律规定。

**Article 120** When interrogating a criminal suspect, the investigator shall first ask the criminal suspect whether he has committed any crime, allow him to state the circumstances of the crime or provide a defense of innocence, and then ask him questions. The criminal suspect shall truthfully answer the investigator's questions. However, he has the right to refuse to answer questions that are irrelevant to the case.  
When interrogating a criminal suspect, the investigator shall inform the criminal suspect of the litigation rights he enjoys and the legal provisions that lenient treatment may be granted if he truthfully confesses his crimes and admits guilt and accepts punishment.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　讯问聋、哑的犯罪嫌疑人，应当有通晓聋、哑手势的人参加，并且将这种情况记明笔录。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-6" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第6条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 121** When interrogating a criminal suspect who is deaf or mute, an interpreter proficient in sign language shall be present, and such circumstance shall be recorded in the written record.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　讯问笔录应当交犯罪嫌疑人核对，对于没有阅读能力的，应当向他宣读。如果记载有遗漏或者差错，犯罪嫌疑人可以提出补充或者改正。犯罪嫌疑人承认笔录没有错误后，应当签名或者盖章。侦查人员也应当在笔录上签名。犯罪嫌疑人请求自行书写供述的，应当准许。必要的时候，侦查人员也可以要犯罪嫌疑人亲笔书写供词。

**Article 122** The record of interrogation shall be verified by the criminal suspect. If the criminal suspect is unable to read, the record shall be read aloud to him. If there are omissions or errors in the record, the criminal suspect may request supplements or corrections. After the criminal suspect acknowledges that the record is free of errors, he shall sign or affix his seal. The investigatory personnel shall also sign the record. If the criminal suspect requests to provide a written confession himself, such request shall be permitted. When necessary, the investigatory personnel may also request the criminal suspect to write a confession in his own hand.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　侦查人员在讯问犯罪嫌疑人的时候，可以对讯问过程进行录音或者录像；对于可能判处无期徒刑、死刑的案件或者其他重大犯罪案件，应当对讯问过程进行录音或者录像。  
录音或者录像应当全程进行，保持完整性。

**Article 123** When interrogating a criminal suspect, the investigatory personnel may make audio or video recordings of the interrogation process; for cases where the criminal suspect may be sentenced to life imprisonment or the death penalty, or other major criminal cases, audio or video recordings of the interrogation process shall be made.  
The audio or video recording shall be made continuously and maintained in its entirety.

#### 第三节　询问证人

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　侦查人员询问证人，可以在现场进行，也可以到证人所在单位、住处或者证人提出的地点进行，在必要的时候，可以通知证人到人民检察院或者公安机关提供证言。在现场询问证人，应当出示工作证件，到证人所在单位、住处或者证人提出的地点询问证人，应当出示人民检察院或者公安机关的证明文件。  
询问证人应当个别进行。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-259" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第259条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 124** The investigatory personnel may question witnesses at the scene, or at the witness's entity, residence, or a location designated by the witness. When necessary, they may notify the witness to provide testimony at the People's Procuratorate or the public security organ. When questioning a witness at the scene, their work credentials shall be presented. When questioning a witness at the witness's entity, residence, or a designated location, the certification documents of the People's Procuratorate or the public security organ shall be presented.  
Witnesses shall be questioned individually.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　询问证人，应当告知他应当如实地提供证据、证言和有意作伪证或者隐匿罪证要负的法律责任。

**Article 125** When questioning a witness, he shall be informed that he shall truthfully provide evidence and testimony, and of the legal liability for knowingly giving false testimony or concealing incriminating evidence.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条　[本法第一百二十二条](#art-122)的规定，也适用于询问证人。

**Article 126** The provisions of Article 122 of this Law also apply to the questioning of witnesses.

<a id="art-127"></a>第一百二十七条　询问被害人，适用本节各条规定。

**Article 127** The provisions of this Section apply to the questioning of victims.

#### 第四节　勘验、检查

<a id="art-128"></a>第一百二十八条　侦查人员对于与犯罪有关的场所、物品、人身、尸体应当进行勘验或者检查。在必要的时候，可以指派或者聘请具有专门知识的人，在侦查人员的主持下进行勘验、检查。

**Article 128** The investigatory personnel shall conduct inspection or examination of the crime scene, articles, persons, and corpses related to a crime. When necessary, persons with specialized knowledge may be assigned or engaged to conduct the inspection or examination under the direction of the investigatory personnel.

<a id="art-129"></a>第一百二十九条　任何单位和个人，都有义务保护犯罪现场，并且立即通知公安机关派员勘验。

**Article 129** Any entity or individual shall have the obligation to protect the crime scene and immediately notify the public security organ to dispatch personnel to conduct an inspection.

<a id="art-130"></a>第一百三十条　侦查人员执行勘验、检查，必须持有人民检察院或者公安机关的证明文件。

**Article 130** When conducting an inspection or examination, the investigatory personnel must possess the certification documents of the People's Procuratorate or the public security organ.

<a id="art-131"></a>第一百三十一条　对于死因不明的尸体，公安机关有权决定解剖，并且通知死者家属到场。

**Article 131** For a corpse of unknown cause of death, the public security organ has the authority to decide on an autopsy and shall notify the family members of the deceased to be present.

<a id="art-132"></a>第一百三十二条　为了确定被害人、犯罪嫌疑人的某些特征、伤害情况或者生理状态，可以对人身进行检查，可以提取指纹信息，采集血液、尿液等生物样本。  
犯罪嫌疑人如果拒绝检查，侦查人员认为必要的时候，可以强制检查。  
检查妇女的身体，应当由女工作人员或者医师进行。

**Article 132** To ascertain certain characteristics, injuries, or physiological conditions of a victim or criminal suspect, a physical examination may be conducted, and fingerprint information may be collected, along with biological samples such as blood and urine.  
If the criminal suspect refuses the examination, the investigatory personnel may conduct a compulsory examination when they deem it necessary.  
An examination of a woman's body shall be conducted by a female staff member or a physician.

<a id="art-133"></a>第一百三十三条　勘验、检查的情况应当写成笔录，由参加勘验、检查的人和见证人签名或者盖章。

**Article 133** The details of an inspection or examination shall be recorded in a written record, which shall be signed or affixed with the seal of the persons who participated in the inspection or examination and the witnesses.

<a id="art-134"></a>第一百三十四条　人民检察院审查案件的时候，对公安机关的勘验、检查，认为需要复验、复查时，可以要求公安机关复验、复查，并且可以派检察人员参加。

**Article 134** When reviewing a case, if the People's Procuratorate considers that a re-inspection or re-examination of the public security organ's inspection or examination is necessary, it may request the public security organ to conduct a re-inspection or re-examination, and may send procuratorial personnel to participate.

<a id="art-135"></a>第一百三十五条　为了查明案情，在必要的时候，经公安机关负责人批准，可以进行侦查实验。  
侦查实验的情况应当写成笔录，由参加实验的人签名或者盖章。  
侦查实验，禁止一切足以造成危险、侮辱人格或者有伤风化的行为。

**Article 135** To clarify the circumstances of a case, an investigative experiment may be conducted when necessary, with the approval of the person in charge of the public security organ.  
The details of the investigative experiment shall be recorded in a written record, which shall be signed or affixed with the seal of the persons who participated in the experiment.  
Investigative experiments shall prohibit any act that may cause danger, insult human dignity, or be contrary to public decency.

#### 第五节　搜查

<a id="art-136"></a>第一百三十六条　为了收集犯罪证据、查获犯罪人，侦查人员可以对犯罪嫌疑人以及可能隐藏罪犯或者犯罪证据的人的身体、物品、住处和其他有关的地方进行搜查。

**Article 136** To collect criminal evidence and apprehend the criminal, the investigatory personnel may conduct searches of the persons, articles, residences, and other relevant places of criminal suspects and persons who may be hiding criminals or criminal evidence.

<a id="art-137"></a>第一百三十七条　任何单位和个人，有义务按照人民检察院和公安机关的要求，交出可以证明犯罪嫌疑人有罪或者无罪的物证、书证、视听资料等证据。

**Article 137** Any entity or individual shall have the obligation to, upon request of the People's Procuratorate or the public security organ, hand over material evidence, documentary evidence, audio-visual materials, and other evidence that may prove the guilt or innocence of the criminal suspect.

<a id="art-138"></a>第一百三十八条　进行搜查，必须向被搜查人出示搜查证。  
在执行逮捕、拘留的时候，遇有紧急情况，不另用搜查证也可以进行搜查。

**Article 138** A search warrant must be presented to the person to be searched when conducting a search.  
When executing an arrest or detention, in case of an emergency, a search may be conducted without a separate search warrant.

<a id="art-139"></a>第一百三十九条　在搜查的时候，应当有被搜查人或者他的家属，邻居或者其他见证人在场。  
搜查妇女的身体，应当由女工作人员进行。

**Article 139** During a search, the person to be searched or his family members, neighbors, or other witnesses shall be present.  
A search of a woman's body shall be conducted by female staff members.

<a id="art-140"></a>第一百四十条　搜查的情况应当写成笔录，由侦查人员和被搜查人或者他的家属，邻居或者其他见证人签名或者盖章。如果被搜查人或者他的家属在逃或者拒绝签名、盖章，应当在笔录上注明。

**Article 140** The details of a search shall be recorded in a written record, which shall be signed or affixed with the seal of the investigatory personnel, the person searched or his family members, and the neighbors or other witnesses. If the person searched or his family members are at large or refuse to sign or affix their seals, such fact shall be noted in the record.

#### 第六节　查封、扣押物证、书证

<a id="art-141"></a>第一百四十一条　在侦查活动中发现的可用以证明犯罪嫌疑人有罪或者无罪的各种财物、文件，应当查封、扣押；与案件无关的财物、文件，不得查封、扣押。  
对查封、扣押的财物、文件，要妥善保管或者封存，不得使用、调换或者损毁。

**Article 141** Any property or documents discovered during investigative activities that may be used to prove the guilt or innocence of the criminal suspect shall be sealed or seized; property or documents unrelated to the case shall not be sealed or seized.  
Sealed or seized property or documents shall be properly kept or stored under seal, and shall not be used, swapped, or damaged.

<a id="art-142"></a>第一百四十二条　对查封、扣押的财物、文件，应当会同在场见证人和被查封、扣押财物、文件持有人查点清楚，当场开列清单一式二份，由侦查人员、见证人和持有人签名或者盖章，一份交给持有人，另一份附卷备查。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-10" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第10条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 142** Sealed or seized property or documents shall be checked and counted in the presence of the witnesses present and the holder of the sealed or seized property or documents, and a list shall be made on the spot in duplicate, signed or affixed with the seals of the investigatory personnel, witnesses, and the holder. One copy shall be given to the holder, and the other shall be attached to the case file for reference.

<a id="art-143"></a>第一百四十三条　侦查人员认为需要扣押犯罪嫌疑人的邮件、电报的时候，经公安机关或者人民检察院批准，即可通知邮电机关将有关的邮件、电报检交扣押。  
不需要继续扣押的时候，应即通知邮电机关。

**Article 143** When investigatory personnel deem it necessary to seize the mail or telegrams of a criminal suspect, upon approval by the public security organ or the People's Procuratorate, they may notify the postal and telecommunications authorities to inspect and hand over the relevant mail or telegrams for seizure.  
When continued seizure is no longer necessary, the postal and telecommunications authorities shall be notified promptly.

<a id="art-144"></a>第一百四十四条　人民检察院、公安机关根据侦查犯罪的需要，可以依照规定查询、冻结犯罪嫌疑人的存款、汇款、债券、股票、基金份额等财产。有关单位和个人应当配合。  
犯罪嫌疑人的存款、汇款、债券、股票、基金份额等财产已被冻结的，不得重复冻结。

**Article 144** The People's Procuratorate or the public security organ may, according to the needs of criminal investigation, inquire into or freeze the property of a criminal suspect, including deposits, remittances, bonds, stocks, fund shares, etc., in accordance with regulations. Relevant entities and individuals shall cooperate.  
If the property of a criminal suspect, including deposits, remittances, bonds, stocks, fund shares, etc., has already been frozen, it shall not be frozen again.

<a id="art-145"></a>第一百四十五条　对查封、扣押的财物、文件、邮件、电报或者冻结的存款、汇款、债券、股票、基金份额等财产，经查明确实与案件无关的，应当在三日以内解除查封、扣押、冻结，予以退还。

**Article 145** If, upon investigation, sealed or seized property, documents, mail, or telegrams, or frozen property such as deposits, remittances, bonds, stocks, or fund shares, are confirmed to be unrelated to the case, the sealing, seizure, or freezing shall be lifted within three days and the items shall be returned.

#### 第七节　鉴定

<a id="art-146"></a>第一百四十六条　为了查明案情，需要解决案件中某些专门性问题的时候，应当指派、聘请有专门知识的人进行鉴定。

**Article 146** To clarify the circumstances of a case and resolve specific technical issues, persons with specialized knowledge shall be assigned or engaged to conduct expert evaluations.

<a id="art-147"></a>第一百四十七条　鉴定人进行鉴定后，应当写出鉴定意见，并且签名。  
鉴定人故意作虚假鉴定的，应当承担法律责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-11" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第11条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 147** After conducting an expert evaluation, the expert witness shall produce an expert opinion and sign it.  
If an expert witness intentionally makes a false evaluation, he shall bear legal liability.

<a id="art-148"></a>第一百四十八条　侦查机关应当将用作证据的鉴定意见告知犯罪嫌疑人、被害人。如果犯罪嫌疑人、被害人提出申请，可以补充鉴定或者重新鉴定。

**Article 148** The investigative organ shall notify the criminal suspect and the victim of the expert opinion to be used as evidence. If the criminal suspect or victim makes a request, a supplementary evaluation or a new evaluation may be conducted.

<a id="art-149"></a>第一百四十九条　对犯罪嫌疑人作精神病鉴定的期间不计入办案期限。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、劳动人事部研究室关于对无期徒刑犯减刑后原审法院发现原判决确有错误予以改判，原减刑裁定应如何适用法律条款予以撤销问题的答复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、劳动人事部研究室关于对无期徒刑犯减刑后原审法院发现原判决确有错误予以改判，原减刑裁定应如何适用法律条款予以撤销问题的答复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-6" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第6条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 149** The time period for conducting a psychiatric evaluation of a criminal suspect shall not be included in the case-handling time limit.

#### 第八节　技术侦查措施

<a id="art-150"></a>第一百五十条　公安机关在立案后，对于危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪、黑社会性质的组织犯罪、重大毒品犯罪或者其他严重危害社会的犯罪案件，根据侦查犯罪的需要，经过严格的批准手续，可以采取技术侦查措施。  
人民检察院在立案后，对于利用职权实施的严重侵犯公民人身权利的重大犯罪案件，根据侦查犯罪的需要，经过严格的批准手续，可以采取技术侦查措施，按照规定交有关机关执行。  
追捕被通缉或者批准、决定逮捕的在逃的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人，经过批准，可以采取追捕所必需的技术侦查措施。

**Article 150** After filing a case, the public security organ may, for crimes endangering national security, terrorist activities, organized crimes of a mafia nature, major drug crimes, or other crimes that seriously endanger society, and based on the needs of criminal investigation, adopt technical investigation measures after rigorous approval procedures.  
After filing a case, the People's Procuratorate may, for major crimes involving serious infringement of citizens' personal rights committed through the abuse of official position, and based on the needs of criminal investigation, adopt technical investigation measures after rigorous approval procedures, and shall hand them over to the relevant authorities for execution in accordance with regulations.  
For the pursuit of a wanted criminal suspect or defendant who has been approved or decided to be arrested and is at large, technical investigation measures necessary for the pursuit may be adopted after approval.

<a id="art-151"></a>第一百五十一条　批准决定应当根据侦查犯罪的需要，确定采取技术侦查措施的种类和适用对象。批准决定自签发之日起三个月以内有效。对于不需要继续采取技术侦查措施的，应当及时解除；对于复杂、疑难案件，期限届满仍有必要继续采取技术侦查措施的，经过批准，有效期可以延长，每次不得超过三个月。

**Article 151** The approval decision shall determine the type and the subjects of application of the technical investigation measures based on the needs of criminal investigation. The approval decision shall be valid for three months from the date of issuance. For cases where continued technical investigation measures are no longer necessary, they shall be promptly terminated. For complex or difficult cases where continued technical investigation measures remain necessary after the validity period expires, the validity period may be extended upon approval, with each extension not exceeding three months.

<a id="art-152"></a>第一百五十二条　采取技术侦查措施，必须严格按照批准的措施种类、适用对象和期限执行。  
侦查人员对采取技术侦查措施过程中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私，应当保密；对采取技术侦查措施获取的与案件无关的材料，必须及时销毁。  
采取技术侦查措施获取的材料，只能用于对犯罪的侦查、起诉和审判，不得用于其他用途。  
公安机关依法采取技术侦查措施，有关单位和个人应当配合，并对有关情况予以保密。

**Article 152** Technical investigation measures shall be executed strictly in accordance with the approved type of measures, subjects of application, and duration.  
Investigatory personnel shall keep confidential any state secrets, trade secrets, and personal privacy learned during the implementation of technical investigation measures; materials obtained through technical investigation measures that are unrelated to the case must be promptly destroyed.  
Materials obtained through technical investigation measures may only be used for the investigation, prosecution, and adjudication of crimes and shall not be used for other purposes.  
When the public security organ adopts technical investigation measures according to law, relevant entities and individuals shall cooperate and shall keep the relevant information confidential.

<a id="art-153"></a>第一百五十三条　为了查明案情，在必要的时候，经公安机关负责人决定，可以由有关人员隐匿其身份实施侦查。但是，不得诱使他人犯罪，不得采用可能危害公共安全或者发生重大人身危险的方法。  
对涉及给付毒品等违禁品或者财物的犯罪活动，公安机关根据侦查犯罪的需要，可以依照规定实施控制下交付。

**Article 153** To clarify the circumstances of a case, when necessary, upon decision by the person in charge of the public security organ, relevant personnel may conduct investigations by concealing their identities. However, they shall not induce others to commit crimes, nor shall they adopt methods that may endanger public safety or cause significant personal danger.  
For criminal activities involving the delivery of narcotics or other prohibited articles or property, the public security organ may, based on the needs of criminal investigation, carry out controlled delivery in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-154"></a>第一百五十四条　依照本节规定采取侦查措施收集的材料在刑事诉讼中可以作为证据使用。如果使用该证据可能危及有关人员的人身安全，或者可能产生其他严重后果的，应当采取不暴露有关人员身份、技术方法等保护措施，必要的时候，可以由审判人员在庭外对证据进行核实。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院研究室关于如何理解“审判的时候怀孕的妇女不适用死刑”问题的电话答复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院研究室关于如何理解“审判的时候怀孕的妇女不适用死刑”问题的电话答复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-407" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第407条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 154** Materials collected through investigative measures adopted in accordance with the provisions of this Section may be used as evidence in criminal proceedings. If using such evidence may endanger the personal safety of relevant personnel or may cause other serious consequences, protective measures such as not revealing the identities of relevant personnel or technical methods shall be taken. When necessary, judicial personnel may verify the evidence outside the courtroom.

#### 第九节　通缉

<a id="art-155"></a>第一百五十五条　应当逮捕的犯罪嫌疑人如果在逃，公安机关可以发布通缉令，采取有效措施，追捕归案。  
各级公安机关在自己管辖的地区以内，可以直接发布通缉令；超出自己管辖的地区，应当报请有权决定的上级机关发布。

**Article 155** If a criminal suspect who should be arrested is at large, the public security organ may issue a wanted notice and take effective measures to pursue and bring him to justice.  
Public security organs at all levels may directly issue wanted notices within their respective jurisdictions; where the wanted notice extends beyond their jurisdiction, approval shall be sought from the superior authority with decision-making power to issue it.

#### 第十节　侦查终结

<a id="art-156"></a>第一百五十六条　对犯罪嫌疑人逮捕后的侦查羁押期限不得超过二个月。案情复杂、期限届满不能终结的案件，可以经上一级人民检察院批准延长一个月。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-311" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第311条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 156** The period of investigative custody after the arrest of a criminal suspect shall not exceed two months. For cases that are complex and cannot be concluded upon expiry of the time limit, the period may be extended by one month upon approval by the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level.

<a id="art-157"></a>第一百五十七条　因为特殊原因，在较长时间内不宜交付审判的特别重大复杂的案件，由最高人民检察院报请全国人民代表大会常务委员会批准延期审理。

**Article 157** For particularly major and complex cases that, due to special reasons, are not suitable for trial within a relatively long period of time, the Supreme People's Procuratorate shall submit a request to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for approval of an extension of the trial proceedings.

<a id="art-158"></a>第一百五十八条　下列案件在[本法第一百五十六条](#art-156)规定的期限届满不能侦查终结的，经省、自治区、直辖市人民检察院批准或者决定，可以延长二个月：  
（一）交通十分不便的边远地区的重大复杂案件；  
（二）重大的犯罪集团案件；  
（三）流窜作案的重大复杂案件；  
（四）犯罪涉及面广，取证困难的重大复杂案件。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-306" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第306条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-6" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第6条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 158** If the following cases cannot be concluded upon expiry of the time limit specified in Article 156 of this Law, the period may be extended by two months upon approval or decision by the People's Procuratorate of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government:  
(1) major and complex cases in remote areas with extremely inconvenient transportation;  
(2) major cases involving criminal groups;  
(3) major and complex cases involving itinerant criminal activity; and  
(4) major and complex cases involving a wide scope of crime and difficulties in obtaining evidence.

<a id="art-159"></a>第一百五十九条　对犯罪嫌疑人可能判处十年有期徒刑以上刑罚，依照[本法第一百五十八条](#art-158)规定延长期限届满，仍不能侦查终结的，经省、自治区、直辖市人民检察院批准或者决定，可以再延长二个月。

**Article 159** If a criminal suspect may be sentenced to a fixed-term imprisonment of ten years or more, and the extended period in accordance with Article 158 of this Law expires but the investigation still cannot be concluded, the period may be further extended by two months upon approval or decision by the People's Procuratorate of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-160"></a>第一百六十条　在侦查期间，发现犯罪嫌疑人另有重要罪行的，自发现之日起依照[本法第一百五十六条](#art-156)的规定重新计算侦查羁押期限。  
犯罪嫌疑人不讲真实姓名、住址，身份不明的，应当对其身份进行调查，侦查羁押期限自查清其身份之日起计算，但是不得停止对其犯罪行为的侦查取证。对于犯罪事实清楚，证据确实、充分，确实无法查明其身份的，也可以按其自报的姓名起诉、审判。

**Article 160** If, during the investigation period, it is discovered that the criminal suspect has committed another major crime, the period of investigative custody shall be recalculated from the date of discovery in accordance with Article 156 of this Law.  
If a criminal suspect fails to provide his true name or address, or his identity is unknown, his identity shall be investigated, and the period of investigative custody shall be calculated from the date his identity is clarified; however, the investigation and evidence collection regarding his criminal conduct shall not cease. Where the criminal facts are clear, the evidence is true and sufficient, and the identity cannot be verified, he may also be prosecuted and adjudicated under the name he gives.

<a id="art-161"></a>第一百六十一条　在案件侦查终结前，辩护律师提出要求的，侦查机关应当听取辩护律师的意见，并记录在案。辩护律师提出书面意见的，应当附卷。

**Article 161** Where a defense lawyer makes a request before the conclusion of investigation of a case, the investigative authority shall hear the opinions of the defense lawyer and record them in the case file. If the defense lawyer submits written opinions, they shall be attached to the case file.

<a id="art-162"></a>第一百六十二条　公安机关侦查终结的案件，应当做到犯罪事实清楚，证据确实、充分，并且写出起诉意见书，连同案卷材料、证据一并移送同级人民检察院审查决定；同时将案件移送情况告知犯罪嫌疑人及其辩护律师。  
犯罪嫌疑人自愿认罪的，应当记录在案，随案移送，并在起诉意见书中写明有关情况。

**Article 162** Where a case investigated by a public security organ is concluded, the facts of the crime shall be clear, and the evidence shall be reliable and sufficient. The public security organ shall prepare a recommendation for prosecution and transfer it together with the case file materials and evidence to the People's Procuratorate at the same level for review and decision; at the same time, it shall notify the criminal suspect and his defense lawyer of the transfer.  
If the criminal suspect voluntarily admits guilt and accepts punishment, this shall be recorded in the case file, transferred with the case, and the relevant circumstances shall be stated in the recommendation for prosecution.

<a id="art-163"></a>第一百六十三条　在侦查过程中，发现不应对犯罪嫌疑人追究刑事责任的，应当撤销案件；犯罪嫌疑人已被逮捕的，应当立即释放，发给释放证明，并且通知原批准逮捕的人民检察院。

**Article 163** During the investigation, if it is discovered that the criminal liability of the criminal suspect should not be investigated, the case shall be dismissed; if the criminal suspect has been arrested, he shall be released immediately, a release certificate shall be issued, and the People's Procuratorate that approved the arrest shall be notified.

#### 第十一节　人民检察院对直接受理的案件的侦查

<a id="art-164"></a>第一百六十四条　人民检察院对直接受理的案件的侦查适用本章规定。

**Article 164** The provisions of this Chapter shall apply to the investigation of cases directly accepted by the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-165"></a>第一百六十五条　人民检察院直接受理的案件中符合[本法第八十一条](#art-81)、第八十二条第四项、第五项规定情形，需要逮捕、拘留犯罪嫌疑人的，由人民检察院作出决定，由公安机关执行。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部关于办理刑事案件排除非法证据若干问题的规定.md#art-7" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部关于办理刑事案件排除非法证据若干问题的规定》第7条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 165** Where a case directly accepted by the People's Procuratorate falls under the circumstances specified in Article 81 or Item (4) or (5) of Article 82 of this Law and the arrest or detention of the criminal suspect is necessary, the People's Procuratorate shall make a decision, and the public security organ shall execute it.

<a id="art-166"></a>第一百六十六条　人民检察院对直接受理的案件中被拘留的人，应当在拘留后的二十四小时以内进行讯问。在发现不应当拘留的时候，必须立即释放，发给释放证明。

**Article 166** The People's Procuratorate shall interrogate a person detained in a case directly accepted by it within twenty-four hours after the detention. If it is discovered that the person should not have been detained, he must be released immediately and a release certificate shall be issued.

<a id="art-167"></a>第一百六十七条　人民检察院对直接受理的案件中被拘留的人，认为需要逮捕的，应当在十四日以内作出决定。在特殊情况下，决定逮捕的时间可以延长一日至三日。对不需要逮捕的，应当立即释放；对需要继续侦查，并且符合取保候审、监视居住条件的，依法取保候审或者监视居住。

**Article 167** Where the People's Procuratorate considers that a person detained in a case directly accepted by it needs to be arrested, it shall make a decision within fourteen days. Under special circumstances, the time limit for deciding on arrest may be extended by one to three days. If arrest is not necessary, the person shall be released immediately; if further investigation is necessary and the conditions for release on bail pending investigation or trial or residential surveillance are met, the person shall be released on bail pending investigation or trial or placed under residential surveillance according to law.

<a id="art-168"></a>第一百六十八条　人民检察院侦查终结的案件，应当作出提起公诉、不起诉或者撤销案件的决定。

**Article 168** Where a case investigated by the People's Procuratorate is concluded, the People's Procuratorate shall make a decision to initiate a public prosecution, not to initiate a prosecution, or to dismiss the case.

### 第三章　提起公诉

<a id="art-169"></a>第一百六十九条　凡需要提起公诉的案件，一律由人民检察院审查决定。

**Article 169** All cases that require a public prosecution shall be reviewed and decided by the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-170"></a>第一百七十条　人民检察院对于监察机关移送起诉的案件，依照本法和监察法的有关规定进行审查。人民检察院经审查，认为需要补充核实的，应当退回监察机关补充调查，必要时可以自行补充侦查。  
对于监察机关移送起诉的已采取留置措施的案件，人民检察院应当对犯罪嫌疑人先行拘留，留置措施自动解除。人民检察院应当在拘留后的十日以内作出是否逮捕、取保候审或者监视居住的决定。在特殊情况下，决定的时间可以延长一日至四日。人民检察院决定采取强制措施的期间不计入审查起诉期限。

**Article 170** The People's Procuratorate shall review cases transferred by a supervisory authority for prosecution in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Law and the Supervision Law. If, after review, the People's Procuratorate considers that supplementary verification is necessary, it shall return the case to the supervisory authority for supplementary investigation; if necessary, it may conduct supplementary investigation on its own.  
For a case transferred by a supervisory authority for prosecution in which a custodial measure has been taken, the People's Procuratorate shall first detain the criminal suspect, and the custodial measure shall be automatically revoked. The People's Procuratorate shall, within ten days after the detention, make a decision on whether to arrest, release on bail pending investigation or trial, or place under residential surveillance. Under special circumstances, the time limit for making a decision may be extended by one to four days. The period during which the People's Procuratorate decides to take compulsory measures shall not be included in the time limit for review of prosecution.

<a id="art-171"></a>第一百七十一条　人民检察院审查案件的时候，必须查明：  
（一）犯罪事实、情节是否清楚，证据是否确实、充分，犯罪性质和罪名的认定是否正确；  
（二）有无遗漏罪行和其他应当追究刑事责任的人；  
（三）是否属于不应追究刑事责任的；  
（四）有无附带民事诉讼；  
（五）侦查活动是否合法。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民检察院关于审查起诉期间犯罪嫌疑人脱逃或者患有严重疾病的应当如何处理的批复.md#art-3" title="被《最高人民检察院关于审查起诉期间犯罪嫌疑人脱逃或者患有严重疾病的应当如何处理的批复》第3条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民检察院关于审查起诉期间犯罪嫌疑人脱逃或者患有严重疾病的应当如何处理的批复.md#art-4" title="被《最高人民检察院关于审查起诉期间犯罪嫌疑人脱逃或者患有严重疾病的应当如何处理的批复》第4条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 171** When reviewing a case, the People's Procuratorate must ascertain:  
(1) Whether the facts and circumstances of the crime are clear, whether the evidence is reliable and sufficient, and whether the determination of the nature of the crime and the charge is correct;  
(2) Whether there are any omitted crimes or other persons whose criminal liability should be investigated;  
(3) Whether the case falls under circumstances where criminal liability should not be investigated;  
(4) Whether there is any incidental civil action;  
(5) Whether the investigation activities are lawful.

<a id="art-172"></a>第一百七十二条　人民检察院对于监察机关、公安机关移送起诉的案件，应当在一个月以内作出决定，重大、复杂的案件，可以延长十五日；犯罪嫌疑人认罪认罚，符合速裁程序适用条件的，应当在十日以内作出决定，对可能判处的有期徒刑超过一年的，可以延长至十五日。  
人民检察院审查起诉的案件，改变管辖的，从改变后的人民检察院收到案件之日起计算审查起诉期限。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-391" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第391条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 172** The People's Procuratorate shall, within one month, make a decision on a case transferred by a supervisory authority or public security organ for prosecution; for a major or complex case, the time limit may be extended by fifteen days. Where the criminal suspect admits guilt and accepts punishment and meets the conditions for applying the quick adjudication procedure, a decision shall be made within ten days; if the possible fixed-term imprisonment imposed exceeds one year, the time limit may be extended to fifteen days.  
For a case under review for prosecution by the People's Procuratorate where the jurisdiction is changed, the time limit for review of prosecution shall be calculated from the date on which the case is received by the People's Procuratorate to which jurisdiction has been changed.

<a id="art-173"></a>第一百七十三条　人民检察院审查案件，应当讯问犯罪嫌疑人，听取辩护人或者值班律师、被害人及其诉讼代理人的意见，并记录在案。辩护人或者值班律师、被害人及其诉讼代理人提出书面意见的，应当附卷。  
犯罪嫌疑人认罪认罚的，人民检察院应当告知其享有的诉讼权利和认罪认罚的法律规定，听取犯罪嫌疑人、辩护人或者值班律师、被害人及其诉讼代理人对下列事项的意见，并记录在案：  
（一）涉嫌的犯罪事实、罪名及适用的法律规定；  
（二）从轻、减轻或者免除处罚等从宽处罚的建议；  
（三）认罪认罚后案件审理适用的程序；  
（四）其他需要听取意见的事项。  
人民检察院依照前两款规定听取值班律师意见的，应当提前为值班律师了解案件有关情况提供必要的便利。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-467" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第467条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/人身犯罪/人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件的规定.md#art-27" title="被《人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件的规定》第27条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/人身犯罪/人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件的规定.md#art-50" title="被《人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件的规定》第50条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 173** When reviewing a case, the People's Procuratorate shall interrogate the criminal suspect, hear the opinions of the defender or duty lawyer, the victim and his litigation agent, and record them in the case file. If the defender or duty lawyer, the victim or his litigation agent submits written opinions, they shall be attached to the case file.  
Where the criminal suspect admits guilt and accepts punishment, the People's Procuratorate shall inform him of the procedural rights he enjoys and the legal provisions on admitting guilt and accepting punishment, and shall hear the opinions of the criminal suspect, the defender or duty lawyer, the victim and his litigation agent on the following matters, and record them in the case file:  
(1) The alleged facts, charges and the applicable legal provisions;  
(2) Recommendations for lenient punishment, such as a lighter, mitigated or exempted punishment;  
(3) The procedure applicable to the trial after admitting guilt and accepting punishment;  
(4) Other matters on which opinions need to be heard.  
Where the People's Procuratorate hears the opinions of a duty lawyer in accordance with the preceding two paragraphs, it shall, in advance, provide the necessary convenience for the duty lawyer to understand the relevant circumstances of the case.

<a id="art-174"></a>第一百七十四条　犯罪嫌疑人自愿认罪，同意量刑建议和程序适用的，应当在辩护人或者值班律师在场的情况下签署认罪认罚具结书。  
犯罪嫌疑人认罪认罚，有下列情形之一的，不需要签署认罪认罚具结书：  
（一）犯罪嫌疑人是盲、聋、哑人，或者是尚未完全丧失辨认或者控制自己行为能力的精神病人的；  
（二）未成年犯罪嫌疑人的法定代理人、辩护人对未成年人认罪认罚有异议的；  
（三）其他不需要签署认罪认罚具结书的情形。

**Article 174** Where a criminal suspect voluntarily admits guilt and accepts the sentencing recommendation and the application of procedures, he shall sign a recognition of guilt and acceptance of punishment statement in the presence of a defender or duty lawyer.  
If a criminal suspect admits guilt and accepts punishment in any of the following circumstances, he is not required to sign a recognition of guilt and acceptance of punishment statement:  
(1) The criminal suspect is blind, deaf, or mute, or is a person with a mental illness who has not completely lost the ability to recognize or control his conduct;  
(2) The legal representative or defender of a juvenile criminal suspect objects to the juvenile's admission of guilt and acceptance of punishment;  
(3) Other circumstances where signing a recognition of guilt and acceptance of punishment statement is not required.

<a id="art-175"></a>第一百七十五条　人民检察院审查案件，可以要求公安机关提供法庭审判所必需的证据材料；认为可能存在[本法第五十六条](#art-56)规定的以非法方法收集证据情形的，可以要求其对证据收集的合法性作出说明。  
人民检察院审查案件，对于需要补充侦查的，可以退回公安机关补充侦查，也可以自行侦查。  
对于补充侦查的案件，应当在一个月以内补充侦查完毕。补充侦查以二次为限。补充侦查完毕移送人民检察院后，人民检察院重新计算审查起诉期限。  
对于二次补充侦查的案件，人民检察院仍然认为证据不足，不符合起诉条件的，应当作出不起诉的决定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-350" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第350条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-369" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第369条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 175** When reviewing a case, the People's Procuratorate may request the public security organ to provide the evidence materials necessary for court adjudication; if it considers that there may be circumstances specified in Article 56 of this Law involving the collection of evidence by illegal methods, it may request the public security organ to explain the legality of the evidence collection.  
When reviewing a case, if supplementary investigation is necessary, the People's Procuratorate may return the case to the public security organ for supplementary investigation, or may conduct the supplementary investigation on its own.  
For a case subject to supplementary investigation, the supplementary investigation shall be completed within one month. Supplementary investigation shall be limited to two times. After the supplementary investigation is completed and the case is transferred to the People's Procuratorate, the People's Procuratorate shall recalculate the time limit for review of prosecution.  
For a case that has undergone supplementary investigation twice, if the People's Procuratorate still considers the evidence insufficient and that the conditions for prosecution are not met, it shall make a decision not to initiate a prosecution.

<a id="art-176"></a>第一百七十六条　人民检察院认为犯罪嫌疑人的犯罪事实已经查清，证据确实、充分，依法应当追究刑事责任的，应当作出起诉决定，按照审判管辖的规定，向人民法院提起公诉，并将案卷材料、证据移送人民法院。  
犯罪嫌疑人认罪认罚的，人民检察院应当就主刑、附加刑、是否适用缓刑等提出量刑建议，并随案移送认罪认罚具结书等材料。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/法律解释/全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第二百七十一条第二款的解释.md#art-1" title="被《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第二百七十一条第二款的解释》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 176** Where the People's Procuratorate considers that the facts of the crime of the criminal suspect have been clarified, the evidence is reliable and sufficient, and criminal liability shall be investigated according to law, it shall make a decision to initiate a prosecution, initiate a public prosecution before the People's Court in accordance with the provisions on adjudication jurisdiction, and transfer the case file materials and evidence to the People's Court.  
Where the criminal suspect admits guilt and accepts punishment, the People's Procuratorate shall make a sentencing recommendation on matters such as the principal punishment, supplementary punishment, and whether probation shall apply, and shall transfer the recognition of guilt and acceptance of punishment statement and other materials together with the case.

<a id="art-177"></a>第一百七十七条　犯罪嫌疑人没有犯罪事实，或者有[本法第十六条](#art-16)规定的情形之一的，人民检察院应当作出不起诉决定。  
对于犯罪情节轻微，依照刑法规定不需要判处刑罚或者免除刑罚的，人民检察院可以作出不起诉决定。  
人民检察院决定不起诉的案件，应当同时对侦查中查封、扣押、冻结的财物解除查封、扣押、冻结。对被不起诉人需要给予行政处罚、处分或者需要没收其违法所得的，人民检察院应当提出检察意见，移送有关主管机关处理。有关主管机关应当将处理结果及时通知人民检察院。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-278" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第278条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-377" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第377条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-385" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第385条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 177** Where there is no criminal fact of the criminal suspect, or the circumstances specified in Article 16 of this Law exist, the People's Procuratorate shall make a decision not to initiate a prosecution.  
For a case where the criminal circumstances are minor and, according to the provisions of the Criminal Law, no punishment is required or punishment may be exempted, the People's Procuratorate may make a decision not to initiate a prosecution.  
Where the People's Procuratorate decides not to initiate a prosecution, it shall, at the same time, lift the sealing, seizure, or freezing of the property sealed, seized, or frozen during the investigation. If it is necessary to impose an administrative penalty or disciplinary sanction on the person against whom no prosecution is initiated, or to confiscate his illegal gains, the People's Procuratorate shall issue a procuratorial opinion and transfer it to the relevant competent authority for handling. The relevant competent authority shall promptly notify the People's Procuratorate of the result of the handling.

<a id="art-178"></a>第一百七十八条　不起诉的决定，应当公开宣布，并且将不起诉决定书送达被不起诉人和他的所在单位。如果被不起诉人在押，应当立即释放。

**Article 178** A decision not to initiate a prosecution shall be publicly announced, and the written decision not to initiate a prosecution shall be served on the person against whom no prosecution is initiated and his work unit. If the person against whom no prosecution is initiated is in custody, he shall be released immediately.

<a id="art-179"></a>第一百七十九条　对于公安机关移送起诉的案件，人民检察院决定不起诉的，应当将不起诉决定书送达公安机关。公安机关认为不起诉的决定有错误的时候，可以要求复议，如果意见不被接受，可以向上一级人民检察院提请复核。

**Article 179** Where the People's Procuratorate decides not to initiate a prosecution for a case transferred by a public security organ for prosecution, it shall serve the written decision not to initiate a prosecution on the public security organ. If the public security organ considers that the decision not to initiate a prosecution is erroneous, it may request a review; if its opinion is not accepted, it may petition the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level for a review.

<a id="art-180"></a>第一百八十条　对于有被害人的案件，决定不起诉的，人民检察院应当将不起诉决定书送达被害人。被害人如果不服，可以自收到决定书后七日以内向上一级人民检察院申诉，请求提起公诉。人民检察院应当将复查决定告知被害人。对人民检察院维持不起诉决定的，被害人可以向人民法院起诉。被害人也可以不经申诉，直接向人民法院起诉。人民法院受理案件后，人民检察院应当将有关案件材料移送人民法院。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-472" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第472条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 180** For a case involving a victim, where a decision not to initiate a prosecution is made, the People's Procuratorate shall serve the written decision not to initiate a prosecution on the victim. If the victim disagrees, he may, within seven days after receiving the written decision, petition the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level, requesting that a public prosecution be initiated. The People's Procuratorate shall inform the victim of the review decision. If the People's Procuratorate upholds the decision not to initiate a prosecution, the victim may file a lawsuit with the People's Court. The victim may also directly file a lawsuit with the People's Court without making a petition. After the People's Court accepts the case, the People's Procuratorate shall transfer the relevant case materials to the People's Court.

<a id="art-181"></a>第一百八十一条　对于人民检察院依照[本法第一百七十七条](#art-177)第二款规定作出的不起诉决定，被不起诉人如果不服，可以自收到决定书后七日以内向人民检察院申诉。人民检察院应当作出复查决定，通知被不起诉的人，同时抄送公安机关。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-8" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第8条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 181** Where the People's Procuratorate makes a decision not to initiate a prosecution in accordance with the provisions of Paragraph 2 of Article 177 of this Law, and the person against whom no prosecution is initiated disagrees, he may, within seven days after receiving the written decision, petition the People's Procuratorate. The People's Procuratorate shall make a review decision, notify the person against whom no prosecution is initiated, and simultaneously send a copy to the public security organ.

<a id="art-182"></a>第一百八十二条　犯罪嫌疑人自愿如实供述涉嫌犯罪的事实，有重大立功或者案件涉及国家重大利益的，经最高人民检察院核准，公安机关可以撤销案件，人民检察院可以作出不起诉决定，也可以对涉嫌数罪中的一项或者多项不起诉。  
根据前款规定不起诉或者撤销案件的，人民检察院、公安机关应当及时对查封、扣押、冻结的财物及其孳息作出处理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部关于依法保障律师执业权利的规定.md#art-23" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部关于依法保障律师执业权利的规定》第23条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 182** Where a criminal suspect voluntarily and truthfully confesses the facts of the suspected crime and has performed major meritorious service, or the case involves major national interests, with the approval of the Supreme People's Procuratorate, the public security organ may dismiss the case, and the People's Procuratorate may make a decision not to initiate a prosecution, or may decide not to initiate a prosecution for one or more of the several offenses charged.  
Where a decision not to initiate a prosecution or to dismiss a case is made in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the People's Procuratorate and the public security organ shall promptly deal with the sealed, seized, or frozen property and its fruits.

## 第三编　审判

### 第一章　审判组织

<a id="art-183"></a>第一百八十三条　基层人民法院、中级人民法院审判第一审案件，应当由审判员三人或者由审判员和人民陪审员共三人或者七人组成合议庭进行，但是基层人民法院适用简易程序、速裁程序的案件可以由审判员一人独任审判。  
高级人民法院审判第一审案件，应当由审判员三人至七人或者由审判员和人民陪审员共三人或者七人组成合议庭进行。  
最高人民法院审判第一审案件，应当由审判员三人至七人组成合议庭进行。  
人民法院审判上诉和抗诉案件，由审判员三人或者五人组成合议庭进行。  
合议庭的成员人数应当是单数。

**Article 183** For adjudication of a case at first instance, a Primary People's Court or an Intermediate People's Court shall form a collegial panel composed of three judges or three or seven judges and people's assessors, except that a case to which the summary procedure or the quick adjudication procedure applies in a Primary People's Court may be adjudicated by a single judge alone.  
For adjudication of a case at first instance, a Higher People's Court shall form a collegial panel composed of three to seven judges or three or seven judges and people's assessors.  
For adjudication of a case at first instance, the Supreme People's Court shall form a collegial panel composed of three to seven judges.  
For adjudication of an appeal case or a case of protest, a People's Court shall form a collegial panel composed of three or five judges.  
The number of members of a collegial panel shall be odd.

<a id="art-184"></a>第一百八十四条　合议庭进行评议的时候，如果意见分歧，应当按多数人的意见作出决定，但是少数人的意见应当写入笔录。评议笔录由合议庭的组成人员签名。

**Article 184** When a collegial panel deliberates a case, if there is a difference of opinion, a decision shall be made in accordance with the opinion of the majority, but the opinion of the minority shall be recorded in the minutes. The minutes of the deliberation shall be signed by the members of the collegial panel.

<a id="art-185"></a>第一百八十五条　合议庭开庭审理并且评议后，应当作出判决。对于疑难、复杂、重大的案件，合议庭认为难以作出决定的，由合议庭提请院长决定提交审判委员会讨论决定。审判委员会的决定，合议庭应当执行。

**Article 185** After a collegial panel opens a court session and deliberates, it shall render a judgment. For a difficult, complex, or major case, if the collegial panel considers it difficult to make a decision, the collegial panel shall submit it to the president to decide on referring it to the adjudication committee for discussion and decision. The collegial panel shall implement the decision of the adjudication committee.

### 第二章　第一审程序

#### 第一节　公诉案件

<a id="art-186"></a>第一百八十六条　人民法院对提起公诉的案件进行审查后，对于起诉书中有明确的指控犯罪事实的，应当决定开庭审判。

**Article 186** After reviewing a case submitted for public prosecution, the People's Court shall decide to open a court session for trial if the indictment explicitly states the facts of the alleged crime.

<a id="art-187"></a>第一百八十七条　人民法院决定开庭审判后，应当确定合议庭的组成人员，将人民检察院的起诉书副本至迟在开庭十日以前送达被告人及其辩护人。  
在开庭以前，审判人员可以召集公诉人、当事人和辩护人、诉讼代理人，对回避、出庭证人名单、非法证据排除等与审判相关的问题，了解情况，听取意见。  
人民法院确定开庭日期后，应当将开庭的时间、地点通知人民检察院，传唤当事人，通知辩护人、诉讼代理人、证人、鉴定人和翻译人员，传票和通知书至迟在开庭三日以前送达。公开审判的案件，应当在开庭三日以前先期公布案由、被告人姓名、开庭时间和地点。  
上述活动情形应当写入笔录，由审判人员和书记员签名。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-8" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第8条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 187** After deciding to open a court session for trial, the People's Court shall determine the composition of the collegial panel and serve a copy of the indictment of the People's Procuratorate on the defendant and his defender at the latest ten days before the court session.  
Before the court session, the adjudicators may convene the public prosecutor, the parties, the defender, and the litigation agent to inquire about and hear opinions on issues related to the adjudication, such as withdrawal, the list of witnesses to appear in court, and the exclusion of illegally obtained evidence.  
After the People's Court determines the date of the court session, it shall notify the People's Procuratorate of the time and place of the court session, summon the parties, and notify the defender, litigation agent, witnesses, expert evaluators, and interpreters. The summonses and notices shall be served at the latest three days before the court session. For cases to be tried in public, the cause of action, the name of the defendant, the time, and the place of the court session shall be announced in advance at the latest three days before the court session.  
The foregoing activities shall be recorded in the minutes, which shall be signed by the adjudicators and the court clerk.

<a id="art-188"></a>第一百八十八条　人民法院审判第一审案件应当公开进行。但是有关国家秘密或者个人隐私的案件，不公开审理；涉及商业秘密的案件，当事人申请不公开审理的，可以不公开审理。  
不公开审理的案件，应当当庭宣布不公开审理的理由。

**Article 188** A People's Court shall try a case at first instance in public. However, a case involving State secrets or personal privacy shall not be tried in public; a case involving trade secrets may not be tried in public if a party applies for it not to be tried in public.  
For a case not tried in public, the reasons for not trying it in public shall be announced in court.

<a id="art-189"></a>第一百八十九条　人民法院审判公诉案件，人民检察院应当派员出席法庭支持公诉。

**Article 189** When a People's Court tries a case of public prosecution, the People's Procuratorate shall send a representative to appear in court to support the public prosecution.

<a id="art-190"></a>第一百九十条　开庭的时候，审判长查明当事人是否到庭，宣布案由；宣布合议庭的组成人员、书记员、公诉人、辩护人、诉讼代理人、鉴定人和翻译人员的名单；告知当事人有权对合议庭组成人员、书记员、公诉人、鉴定人和翻译人员申请回避；告知被告人享有辩护权利。  
被告人认罪认罚的，审判长应当告知被告人享有的诉讼权利和认罪认罚的法律规定，审查认罪认罚的自愿性和认罪认罚具结书内容的真实性、合法性。

**Article 190** At the opening of a court session, the presiding judge shall ascertain whether the parties are present in court, announce the cause of action, announce the list of members of the collegial panel, the court clerk, the public prosecutor, the defender, the litigation agent, expert evaluators, and interpreters; inform the parties of their right to apply for the withdrawal of any member of the collegial panel, the court clerk, the public prosecutor, expert evaluators, or interpreters; and inform the defendant of his right to defense.  
Where the defendant admits guilt and accepts punishment, the presiding judge shall inform the defendant of his procedural rights and the legal provisions on admitting guilt and accepting punishment, and shall examine the voluntariness of his admission of guilt and acceptance of punishment and the truthfulness and legality of the contents of the recognition of guilt and acceptance of punishment statement.

<a id="art-191"></a>第一百九十一条　公诉人在法庭上宣读起诉书后，被告人、被害人可以就起诉书指控的犯罪进行陈述，公诉人可以讯问被告人。  
被害人、附带民事诉讼的原告人和辩护人、诉讼代理人，经审判长许可，可以向被告人发问。  
审判人员可以讯问被告人。

**Article 191** After the public prosecutor reads the indictment in court, the defendant and the victim may make statements on the crime alleged in the indictment, and the public prosecutor may interrogate the defendant.  
The victim, the plaintiff in an incidental civil action, the defender, and the litigation agent may, with the permission of the presiding judge, question the defendant.  
The adjudicators may interrogate the defendant.

<a id="art-192"></a>第一百九十二条　公诉人、当事人或者辩护人、诉讼代理人对证人证言有异议，且该证人证言对案件定罪量刑有重大影响，人民法院认为证人有必要出庭作证的，证人应当出庭作证。  
人民警察就其执行职务时目击的犯罪情况作为证人出庭作证，适用前款规定。  
公诉人、当事人或者辩护人、诉讼代理人对鉴定意见有异议，人民法院认为鉴定人有必要出庭的，鉴定人应当出庭作证。经人民法院通知，鉴定人拒不出庭作证的，鉴定意见不得作为定案的根据。

**Article 192** Where the public prosecutor, a party, the defender, or the litigation agent objects to the testimony of a witness, and such testimony has a significant impact on the conviction and sentencing of the case, and the People's Court considers it necessary for the witness to testify in court, the witness shall appear in court to testify.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply to a people's police officer appearing in court as a witness to testify regarding a crime witnessed in the performance of his duties.  
Where the public prosecutor, a party, the defender, or the litigation agent objects to an expert opinion, and the People's Court considers it necessary for the expert evaluator to appear in court, the expert evaluator shall appear in court to testify. If, after being notified by the People's Court, the expert evaluator refuses to appear in court to testify without justifiable reasons, the expert opinion shall not be used as the basis for a verdict.

<a id="art-193"></a>第一百九十三条　经人民法院通知，证人没有正当理由不出庭作证的，人民法院可以强制其到庭，但是被告人的配偶、父母、子女除外。  
证人没有正当理由拒绝出庭或者出庭后拒绝作证的，予以训诫，情节严重的，经院长批准，处以十日以下的拘留。被处罚人对拘留决定不服的，可以向上一级人民法院申请复议。复议期间不停止执行。

**Article 193** If, after being notified by the People's Court, a witness fails to appear in court to testify without justifiable reasons, the People's Court may compel his appearance, except for the spouse, parents, or children of the defendant.  
If a witness, without justifiable reasons, refuses to appear in court or, after appearing in court, refuses to testify, he shall be admonished; if the circumstances are serious, with the approval of the president, he may be detained for not more than ten days. If the punished person disagrees with the detention decision, he may apply to the People's Court at the next higher level for reconsideration. Execution shall not be suspended during the reconsideration period.

<a id="art-194"></a>第一百九十四条　证人作证，审判人员应当告知他要如实地提供证言和有意作伪证或者隐匿罪证要负的法律责任。公诉人、当事人和辩护人、诉讼代理人经审判长许可，可以对证人、鉴定人发问。审判长认为发问的内容与案件无关的时候，应当制止。  
审判人员可以询问证人、鉴定人。

**Article 194** When a witness gives testimony, the adjudicator shall inform him of his obligation to provide truthful testimony and the legal liability he shall bear for intentionally giving false testimony or concealing incriminating evidence. The public prosecutor, the parties, the defender, and the litigation agent may, with the permission of the presiding judge, question the witnesses and expert evaluators. If the presiding judge considers that the content of the questions is unrelated to the case, he shall stop them.  
The adjudicators may question the witnesses and expert evaluators.

<a id="art-195"></a>第一百九十五条　公诉人、辩护人应当向法庭出示物证，让当事人辨认，对未到庭的证人的证言笔录、鉴定人的鉴定意见、勘验笔录和其他作为证据的文书，应当当庭宣读。审判人员应当听取公诉人、当事人和辩护人、诉讼代理人的意见。

**Article 195** The public prosecutor and the defender shall present the physical evidence to the court for the parties to identify. The records of testimony of witnesses who are not present in court, the opinions of expert evaluators, records of inspection, and other documents used as evidence shall be read out in court. The adjudicators shall hear the opinions of the public prosecutor, the parties, the defender, and the litigation agent.

<a id="art-196"></a>第一百九十六条　法庭审理过程中，合议庭对证据有疑问的，可以宣布休庭，对证据进行调查核实。  
人民法院调查核实证据，可以进行勘验、检查、查封、扣押、鉴定和查询、冻结。

**Article 196** During court proceedings, if the collegial panel has doubts about the evidence, it may announce an adjournment of the court session to investigate and verify the evidence.  
When the People's Court investigates and verifies evidence, it may conduct inspections, examinations, sealing, seizure, expert evaluations, inquiries, and freezing.

<a id="art-197"></a>第一百九十七条　法庭审理过程中，当事人和辩护人、诉讼代理人有权申请通知新的证人到庭，调取新的物证，申请重新鉴定或者勘验。  
公诉人、当事人和辩护人、诉讼代理人可以申请法庭通知有专门知识的人出庭，就鉴定人作出的鉴定意见提出意见。  
法庭对于上述申请，应当作出是否同意的决定。  
第二款规定的有专门知识的人出庭，适用鉴定人的有关规定。

**Article 197** During court proceedings, the parties, the defender, and the litigation agent have the right to apply for the notification of new witnesses to appear in court, the procurement of new physical evidence, and the application for a new expert evaluation or inspection.  
The public prosecutor, the parties, the defender, and the litigation agent may apply to the court to notify a person with specialized knowledge to appear in court to give an opinion on the opinion made by the expert evaluator.  
The court shall decide whether to approve the foregoing applications.  
The provisions concerning expert evaluators shall apply to the appearance in court of a person with specialized knowledge as provided for in Paragraph 2.

<a id="art-198"></a>第一百九十八条　法庭审理过程中，对与定罪、量刑有关的事实、证据都应当进行调查、辩论。  
经审判长许可，公诉人、当事人和辩护人、诉讼代理人可以对证据和案件情况发表意见并且可以互相辩论。  
审判长在宣布辩论终结后，被告人有最后陈述的权利。

**Article 198** During court proceedings, all facts and evidence related to conviction and sentencing shall be investigated and debated.  
With the permission of the presiding judge, the public prosecutor, the parties, the defender, and the litigation agent may express opinions on the evidence and the circumstances of the case, and may debate with each other.  
After the presiding judge announces the conclusion of the debate, the defendant has the right to make a final statement.

<a id="art-199"></a>第一百九十九条　在法庭审判过程中，如果诉讼参与人或者旁听人员违反法庭秩序，审判长应当警告制止。对不听制止的，可以强行带出法庭；情节严重的，处以一千元以下的罚款或者十五日以下的拘留。罚款、拘留必须经院长批准。被处罚人对罚款、拘留的决定不服的，可以向上一级人民法院申请复议。复议期间不停止执行。  
对聚众哄闹、冲击法庭或者侮辱、诽谤、威胁、殴打司法工作人员或者诉讼参与人，严重扰乱法庭秩序，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 199** If, during the court proceedings, a participant in the proceedings or an observer violates the order of the court, the presiding judge shall warn him and order him to stop. If he does not heed the order, he may be forcibly removed from the court; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan or detention of not more than fifteen days may be imposed. The fine or detention must be approved by the president. If the punished person disagrees with the decision on the fine or detention, he may apply to the People's Court at the next higher level for reconsideration. Execution shall not be suspended during the reconsideration period.  
If a person assembles a crowd to create a disturbance, storm the court, or insult, slander, threaten, or beat judicial officers or participants in the proceedings, thereby seriously disrupting court order and constituting a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-200"></a>第二百条　在被告人最后陈述后，审判长宣布休庭，合议庭进行评议，根据已经查明的事实、证据和有关的法律规定，分别作出以下判决：  
（一）案件事实清楚，证据确实、充分，依据法律认定被告人有罪的，应当作出有罪判决；  
（二）依据法律认定被告人无罪的，应当作出无罪判决；  
（三）证据不足，不能认定被告人有罪的，应当作出证据不足、指控的犯罪不能成立的无罪判决。

**Article 200** After the defendant makes his final statement, the presiding judge shall announce the adjournment of the court session, and the collegial panel shall deliberate and, based on the facts, evidence, and relevant legal provisions that have been ascertained, render one of the following judgments:  
(1) If the facts of the case are clear, the evidence is reliable and sufficient, and the defendant is found guilty in accordance with the law, a conviction judgment shall be rendered;  
(2) If the defendant is found not guilty in accordance with the law, a judgment of acquittal shall be rendered;  
(3) If the evidence is insufficient and the defendant cannot be found guilty, a judgment of acquittal shall be rendered on the ground that the evidence is insufficient and the alleged crime cannot be established.

<a id="art-201"></a>第二百零一条　对于认罪认罚案件，人民法院依法作出判决时，一般应当采纳人民检察院指控的罪名和量刑建议，但有下列情形的除外：  
（一）被告人的行为不构成犯罪或者不应当追究其刑事责任的；  
（二）被告人违背意愿认罪认罚的；  
（三）被告人否认指控的犯罪事实的；  
（四）起诉指控的罪名与审理认定的罪名不一致的；  
（五）其他可能影响公正审判的情形。  
人民法院经审理认为量刑建议明显不当，或者被告人、辩护人对量刑建议提出异议的，人民检察院可以调整量刑建议。人民检察院不调整量刑建议或者调整量刑建议后仍然明显不当的，人民法院应当依法作出判决。

**Article 201** : In rendering a judgment according to law for a case in which a confession and acceptance of punishment are made, the People's Court shall generally adopt the crime charged and sentencing recommendation made by the People's Procuratorate, except under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The conduct of the defendant does not constitute a crime or shall not be subject to criminal liability;  
(2) The defendant confessed and accepted punishment against his will;  
(3) The defendant denies the alleged criminal facts;  
(4) The crime charged in the prosecution differs from the crime established through trial; or  
(5) Other circumstances that may affect a fair trial.  
Where upon trial the People's Court finds the sentencing recommendation clearly improper, or the defendant or defender raises an objection to the sentencing recommendation, the People's Procuratorate may adjust the sentencing recommendation. If the People's Procuratorate does not adjust the sentencing recommendation, or after adjustment the sentencing recommendation remains clearly improper, the People's Court shall render a judgment according to law.

<a id="art-202"></a>第二百零二条　宣告判决，一律公开进行。  
当庭宣告判决的，应当在五日以内将判决书送达当事人和提起公诉的人民检察院；定期宣告判决的，应当在宣告后立即将判决书送达当事人和提起公诉的人民检察院。判决书应当同时送达辩护人、诉讼代理人。

**Article 202** : Judgments shall be pronounced publicly in all cases.  
Where a judgment is pronounced in court, the written judgment shall be served on the parties and on the People's Procuratorate that initiated the public prosecution within five days; where a judgment is pronounced at a scheduled date, the written judgment shall be served on the parties and on the People's Procuratorate that initiated the public prosecution immediately after pronouncement. The written judgment shall also be served on the defender and litigation agent.

<a id="art-203"></a>第二百零三条　判决书应当由审判人员和书记员署名，并且写明上诉的期限和上诉的法院。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-8" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第8条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 203** : The written judgment shall be signed by the judicial personnel and court clerk, and shall specify the time limit for appeal and the court to which appeal may be lodged.

<a id="art-204"></a>第二百零四条　在法庭审判过程中，遇有下列情形之一，影响审判进行的，可以延期审理：  
（一）需要通知新的证人到庭，调取新的物证，重新鉴定或者勘验的；  
（二）检察人员发现提起公诉的案件需要补充侦查，提出建议的；  
（三）由于申请回避而不能进行审判的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院印发《关于规范人民法院再审立案的若干意见（试行）》的通知.md#art-15" title="被《最高人民法院印发《关于规范人民法院再审立案的若干意见（试行）》的通知》第15条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 204** : During court trial, where any of the following circumstances occurs and impedes the progress of the trial, the trial may be adjourned:  
(1) It is necessary to notify new witnesses to appear in court, to obtain new material evidence, or to conduct a new expert evaluation or inspection;  
(2) A prosecutor discovers that the case initiated for public prosecution requires supplementary investigation and makes a proposal to that effect; or  
(3) Trial cannot proceed due to a challenge filed for recusal.

<a id="art-205"></a>第二百零五条　依照[本法第二百零四条](#art-204)第二项的规定延期审理的案件，人民检察院应当在一个月以内补充侦查完毕。

**Article 205** : For a case in which the trial is adjourned pursuant to Item (2) of Article 204 of this Law, the People's Procuratorate shall complete the supplementary investigation within one month.

<a id="art-206"></a>第二百零六条　在审判过程中，有下列情形之一，致使案件在较长时间内无法继续审理的，可以中止审理：  
（一）被告人患有严重疾病，无法出庭的；  
（二）被告人脱逃的；  
（三）自诉人患有严重疾病，无法出庭，未委托诉讼代理人出庭的；  
（四）由于不能抗拒的原因。  
中止审理的原因消失后，应当恢复审理。中止审理的期间不计入审理期限。

**Article 206** : During trial, where any of the following circumstances occurs and makes it impossible to continue the trial for a considerable period of time, the trial may be suspended:  
(1) The defendant suffers from a serious illness and is unable to appear in court;  
(2) The defendant escapes;  
(3) The private prosecutor suffers from a serious illness and is unable to appear in court and has not entrusted a litigation agent to appear in court; or  
(4) Due to force majeure.  
After the cause for suspension is eliminated, the trial shall be resumed. The period of suspension shall not be counted in the trial time limit.

<a id="art-207"></a>第二百零七条　法庭审判的全部活动，应当由书记员写成笔录，经审判长审阅后，由审判长和书记员签名。  
法庭笔录中的证人证言部分，应当当庭宣读或者交给证人阅读。证人在承认没有错误后，应当签名或者盖章。  
法庭笔录应当交给当事人阅读或者向他宣读。当事人认为记载有遗漏或者差错的，可以请求补充或者改正。当事人承认没有错误后，应当签名或者盖章。

**Article 207** : All activities during the court trial shall be recorded in writing by the court clerk, and after review by the presiding judge, the record shall be signed by the presiding judge and the court clerk.  
The testimony of witnesses as recorded in the court record shall be read out in court or given to the witnesses for reading. After the witnesses confirm that the record is free of error, they shall sign or affix their seals.  
The court record shall be given to the parties for reading or be read out to them. If the parties believe that the record contains omissions or errors, they may request additions or corrections. After the parties confirm that the record is free of error, they shall sign or affix their seals.

<a id="art-208"></a>第二百零八条　人民法院审理公诉案件，应当在受理后二个月以内宣判，至迟不得超过三个月。对于可能判处死刑的案件或者附带民事诉讼的案件，以及有[本法第一百五十八条](#art-158)规定情形之一的，经上一级人民法院批准，可以延长三个月；因特殊情况还需要延长的，报请最高人民法院批准。  
人民法院改变管辖的案件，从改变后的人民法院收到案件之日起计算审理期限。  
人民检察院补充侦查的案件，补充侦查完毕移送人民法院后，人民法院重新计算审理期限。

**Article 208** : The People's Court shall pronounce judgment in a case of public prosecution within two months from the date of acceptance, but in no case shall this exceed three months. For a case in which a sentence of death penalty may be imposed or a case with an incidental civil action, or for a case under any of the circumstances provided for in Article 158 of this Law, the time limit may be extended by three months with the approval of the People's Court at the next higher level; if further extension is necessary due to special circumstances, an application shall be submitted to the Supreme People's Court for approval.  
For a case where jurisdiction is changed by the People's Court, the trial time limit shall be calculated from the date on which the case is received by the People's Court to which jurisdiction has been changed.  
For a case in which the People's Procuratorate conducts supplementary investigation, after the supplementary investigation is completed and the case is transferred to the People's Court, the People's Court shall recalculate the trial time limit.

<a id="art-209"></a>第二百零九条　人民检察院发现人民法院审理案件违反法律规定的诉讼程序，有权向人民法院提出纠正意见。

**Article 209** : Where the People's Procuratorate discovers that the People's Court has violated the legally prescribed procedure in trying a case, it has the authority to present corrective opinions to the People's Court.

#### 第二节　自诉案件

<a id="art-210"></a>第二百一十条　自诉案件包括下列案件：  
（一）告诉才处理的案件；  
（二）被害人有证据证明的轻微刑事案件；  
（三）被害人有证据证明对被告人侵犯自己人身、财产权利的行为应当依法追究刑事责任，而公安机关或者人民检察院不予追究被告人刑事责任的案件。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理拒不执行判决、裁定刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-14" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理拒不执行判决、裁定刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第14条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 210** : Cases of private prosecution include the following:  
(1) Cases to be handled only upon complaint;  
(2) Minor criminal cases for which the victim has evidence to prove the case;  
(3) Cases for which the victim has evidence to prove that the defendant has infringed upon the victim's personal or property rights and should be investigated for criminal liability according to law, but the public security organ or the People's Procuratorate does not investigate the defendant's criminal liability.

<a id="art-211"></a>第二百一十一条　人民法院对于自诉案件进行审查后，按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）犯罪事实清楚，有足够证据的案件，应当开庭审判；  
（二）缺乏罪证的自诉案件，如果自诉人提不出补充证据，应当说服自诉人撤回自诉，或者裁定驳回。  
自诉人经两次依法传唤，无正当理由拒不到庭的，或者未经法庭许可中途退庭的，按撤诉处理。  
法庭审理过程中，审判人员对证据有疑问，需要调查核实的，适用[本法第一百九十六条](#art-196)的规定。

**Article 211** : After reviewing a case of private prosecution, the People's Court shall handle it separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) Where the criminal facts are clear and there is sufficient evidence, the case shall be tried in open court;  
(2) For a case of private prosecution that lacks criminal evidence, if the private prosecutor fails to produce supplementary evidence, the People's Court shall persuade the private prosecutor to withdraw the private prosecution or shall render a ruling to dismiss the case.  
Where the private prosecutor, after being summoned twice according to law, refuses to appear in court without justifiable reasons, or withdraws from court without permission, the case shall be treated as withdrawn.  
Where during court trial the judicial personnel have doubts about the evidence and need to investigate and verify it, the provisions of Article 196 of this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-212"></a>第二百一十二条　人民法院对自诉案件，可以进行调解；自诉人在宣告判决前，可以同被告人自行和解或者撤回自诉。[本法第二百一十条](#art-210)第三项规定的案件不适用调解。  
人民法院审理自诉案件的期限，被告人被羁押的，适用[本法第二百零八条](#art-208)第一款、第二款的规定；未被羁押的，应当在受理后六个月以内宣判。

**Article 212** : The People's Court may conduct mediation in a case of private prosecution; before the judgment is pronounced, the private prosecutor may reach a settlement with the defendant or withdraw the private prosecution. Mediation shall not apply to cases specified in Item (3) of Article 210 of this Law.  
For the time limit for the People's Court to try a case of private prosecution, where the defendant is placed in custody, the provisions of Paragraphs 1 and 2 of Article 208 of this Law shall apply; where the defendant is not placed in custody, the judgment shall be pronounced within six months from the date of acceptance.

<a id="art-213"></a>第二百一十三条　自诉案件的被告人在诉讼过程中，可以对自诉人提起反诉。反诉适用自诉的规定。

**Article 213** : During the proceedings of a case of private prosecution, the defendant may file a counterclaim against the private prosecutor. The provisions on private prosecution shall apply to counterclaims.

#### 第三节　简易程序

<a id="art-214"></a>第二百一十四条　基层人民法院管辖的案件，符合下列条件的，可以适用简易程序审判：  
（一）案件事实清楚、证据充分的；  
（二）被告人承认自己所犯罪行，对指控的犯罪事实没有异议的；  
（三）被告人对适用简易程序没有异议的。  
人民检察院在提起公诉的时候，可以建议人民法院适用简易程序。

**Article 214** : For a case under the jurisdiction of a Primary People's Court, where the following conditions are met, the summary procedure may be applied for trial:  
(1) The facts of the case are clear and evidence is sufficient;  
(2) The defendant confesses to the crime committed and has no objection to the alleged criminal facts; and  
(3) The defendant has no objection to the application of the summary procedure.  
When initiating a public prosecution, the People's Procuratorate may recommend that the People's Court apply the summary procedure.

<a id="art-215"></a>第二百一十五条　有下列情形之一的，不适用简易程序：  
（一）被告人是盲、聋、哑人，或者是尚未完全丧失辨认或者控制自己行为能力的精神病人的；  
（二）有重大社会影响的；  
（三）共同犯罪案件中部分被告人不认罪或者对适用简易程序有异议的；  
（四）其他不宜适用简易程序审理的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-431" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第431条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 215** : The summary procedure shall not apply under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The defendant is blind, deaf, or mute, or is a person with a mental illness who has not completely lost the capacity to recognize or control his conduct;  
(2) The case has major social impact;  
(3) In a joint crime, some defendants do not confess or object to the application of the summary procedure; or  
(4) Other circumstances under which it is inappropriate to apply the summary procedure.

<a id="art-216"></a>第二百一十六条　适用简易程序审理案件，对可能判处三年有期徒刑以下刑罚的，可以组成合议庭进行审判，也可以由审判员一人独任审判；对可能判处的有期徒刑超过三年的，应当组成合议庭进行审判。  
适用简易程序审理公诉案件，人民检察院应当派员出席法庭。

**Article 216** : In trying a case with the summary procedure, for a case in which a sentence of less than three years of fixed-term imprisonment may be imposed, the trial may be conducted by a collegial panel or by a single judge; for a case in which a sentence of more than three years of fixed-term imprisonment may be imposed, the trial shall be conducted by a collegial panel.  
For a case of public prosecution tried with the summary procedure, the People's Procuratorate shall send a prosecutor to appear in court.

<a id="art-217"></a>第二百一十七条　适用简易程序审理案件，审判人员应当询问被告人对指控的犯罪事实的意见，告知被告人适用简易程序审理的法律规定，确认被告人是否同意适用简易程序审理。

**Article 217** : In trying a case with the summary procedure, the judicial personnel shall question the defendant about the alleged criminal facts, inform the defendant of the legal provisions on the application of the summary procedure, and confirm whether the defendant agrees to the application of the summary procedure.

<a id="art-218"></a>第二百一十八条　适用简易程序审理案件，经审判人员许可，被告人及其辩护人可以同公诉人、自诉人及其诉讼代理人互相辩论。

**Article 218** : In trying a case with the summary procedure, with the permission of the judicial personnel, the defendant and his defender may debate with the prosecutor, the private prosecutor, and their litigation agents.

<a id="art-219"></a>第二百一十九条　适用简易程序审理案件，不受本章第一节关于送达期限、讯问被告人、询问证人、鉴定人、出示证据、法庭辩论程序规定的限制。但在判决宣告前应当听取被告人的最后陈述意见。

**Article 219** : In trying a case with the summary procedure, it shall not be subject to the restrictions of the provisions in Section 1 of this Chapter concerning time limits for service, interrogation of the defendant, examination of witnesses and expert witnesses, presentation of evidence, and court debate procedures. However, before the judgment is pronounced, the defendant's final statement shall be heard.

<a id="art-220"></a>第二百二十条　适用简易程序审理案件，人民法院应当在受理后二十日以内审结；对可能判处的有期徒刑超过三年的，可以延长至一个半月。

**Article 220** : In trying a case with the summary procedure, the People's Court shall conclude the case within twenty days from the date of acceptance; for a case in which a sentence of more than three years of fixed-term imprisonment may be imposed, the time limit may be extended to one and a half months.

<a id="art-221"></a>第二百二十一条　人民法院在审理过程中，发现不宜适用简易程序的，应当按照本章第一节或者第二节的规定重新审理。

**Article 221** : Where during trial the People's Court finds that the summary procedure is not suitable, it shall retry the case in accordance with the provisions of Section 1 or Section 2 of this Chapter.

#### 第四节　速裁程序

<a id="art-222"></a>第二百二十二条　基层人民法院管辖的可能判处三年有期徒刑以下刑罚的案件，案件事实清楚，证据确实、充分，被告人认罪认罚并同意适用速裁程序的，可以适用速裁程序，由审判员一人独任审判。  
人民检察院在提起公诉的时候，可以建议人民法院适用速裁程序。

**Article 222** : For a case under the jurisdiction of a Primary People's Court where a sentence of less than three years of fixed-term imprisonment may be imposed, and where the facts of the case are clear, evidence is reliable and sufficient, and the defendant confesses and accepts punishment and agrees to the application of the expedited procedure, the expedited procedure may be applied, and the trial shall be conducted by a single judge.  
When initiating a public prosecution, the People's Procuratorate may recommend that the People's Court apply the expedited procedure.

<a id="art-223"></a>第二百二十三条　有下列情形之一的，不适用速裁程序：  
（一）被告人是盲、聋、哑人，或者是尚未完全丧失辨认或者控制自己行为能力的精神病人的；  
（二）被告人是未成年人的；  
（三）案件有重大社会影响的；  
（四）共同犯罪案件中部分被告人对指控的犯罪事实、罪名、量刑建议或者适用速裁程序有异议的；  
（五）被告人与被害人或者其法定代理人没有就附带民事诉讼赔偿等事项达成调解或者和解协议的；  
（六）其他不宜适用速裁程序审理的。

**Article 223** : The expedited procedure shall not apply under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The defendant is blind, deaf, or mute, or is a person with a mental illness who has not completely lost the capacity to recognize or control his conduct;  
(2) The defendant is a minor;  
(3) The case has major social impact;  
(4) In a joint crime, some defendants object to the alleged criminal facts, charges, sentencing recommendations, or the application of the expedited procedure;  
(5) The defendant and the victim or his legal representative have not reached a mediation or settlement agreement on matters such as compensation in the incidental civil action; or  
(6) Other circumstances under which it is inappropriate to try the case with the expedited procedure.

<a id="art-224"></a>第二百二十四条　适用速裁程序审理案件，不受本章第一节规定的送达期限的限制，一般不进行法庭调查、法庭辩论，但在判决宣告前应当听取辩护人的意见和被告人的最后陈述意见。  
适用速裁程序审理案件，应当当庭宣判。

**Article 224** : In trying a case with the expedited procedure, it shall not be subject to the restrictions of the time limits for service provided for in Section 1 of this Chapter. Generally, no court investigation or court debate shall be conducted, but before the judgment is pronounced, the opinions of the defender and the defendant's final statement shall be heard.  
A case tried with the expedited procedure shall have its judgment pronounced in court.

<a id="art-225"></a>第二百二十五条　适用速裁程序审理案件，人民法院应当在受理后十日以内审结；对可能判处的有期徒刑超过一年的，可以延长至十五日。

**Article 225** : In trying a case with the expedited procedure, the People's Court shall conclude the case within ten days from the date of acceptance; for a case in which a sentence of more than one year of fixed-term imprisonment may be imposed, the time limit may be extended to fifteen days.

<a id="art-226"></a>第二百二十六条　人民法院在审理过程中，发现有被告人的行为不构成犯罪或者不应当追究其刑事责任、被告人违背意愿认罪认罚、被告人否认指控的犯罪事实或者其他不宜适用速裁程序审理的情形的，应当按照本章第一节或者第三节的规定重新审理。

**Article 226** : Where during trial the People's Court discovers that the defendant's conduct does not constitute a crime or shall not be subject to criminal liability, the defendant confessed and accepted punishment against his will, the defendant denies the alleged criminal facts, or there are other circumstances under which it is inappropriate to try the case with the expedited procedure, it shall retry the case in accordance with the provisions of Section 1 or Section 3 of this Chapter.

### 第三章　第二审程序

<a id="art-227"></a>第二百二十七条　被告人、自诉人和他们的法定代理人，不服地方各级人民法院第一审的判决、裁定，有权用书状或者口头向上一级人民法院上诉。被告人的辩护人和近亲属，经被告人同意，可以提出上诉。  
附带民事诉讼的当事人和他们的法定代理人，可以对地方各级人民法院第一审的判决、裁定中的附带民事诉讼部分，提出上诉。  
对被告人的上诉权，不得以任何借口加以剥夺。

**Article 227** : The defendant, the private prosecutor, and their legal representatives who refuse to accept a judgment or ruling of first instance rendered by a local People's Court at any level shall have the right to appeal in writing or orally to the People's Court at the next higher level. The defender and close relative of the defendant may, with the defendant's consent, file an appeal.  
A party to an incidental civil action and his legal representative may appeal against the part concerning the incidental civil action in a judgment or ruling of first instance rendered by a local People's Court at any level.  
The defendant's right to appeal shall not be deprived on any pretext.

<a id="art-228"></a>第二百二十八条　地方各级人民检察院认为本级人民法院第一审的判决、裁定确有错误的时候，应当向上一级人民法院提出抗诉。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-78" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第78条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 228** : Where a local People's Procuratorate at any level considers that a judgment or ruling of first instance rendered by the People's Court at the corresponding level is indeed erroneous, it shall file a protest with the People's Court at the next higher level.

<a id="art-229"></a>第二百二十九条　被害人及其法定代理人不服地方各级人民法院第一审的判决的，自收到判决书后五日以内，有权请求人民检察院提出抗诉。人民检察院自收到被害人及其法定代理人的请求后五日以内，应当作出是否抗诉的决定并且答复请求人。

**Article 229** : Where the victim and his legal representative refuse to accept a judgment of first instance rendered by a local People's Court at any level, they shall have the right, within five days from receipt of the written judgment, to request the People's Procuratorate to file a protest. The People's Procuratorate shall, within five days from receipt of the request from the victim and his legal representative, make a decision on whether to file a protest and shall reply to the requester.

<a id="art-230"></a>第二百三十条　不服判决的上诉和抗诉的期限为十日，不服裁定的上诉和抗诉的期限为五日，从接到判决书、裁定书的第二日起算。

**Article 230** : The time limit for appeal or protest against a judgment shall be ten days, and the time limit for appeal or protest against a ruling shall be five days, calculated from the day after the receipt of the written judgment or ruling.

<a id="art-231"></a>第二百三十一条　被告人、自诉人、附带民事诉讼的原告人和被告人通过原审人民法院提出上诉的，原审人民法院应当在三日以内将上诉状连同案卷、证据移送上一级人民法院，同时将上诉状副本送交同级人民检察院和对方当事人。  
被告人、自诉人、附带民事诉讼的原告人和被告人直接向第二审人民法院提出上诉的，第二审人民法院应当在三日以内将上诉状交原审人民法院送交同级人民检察院和对方当事人。

**Article 231** : Where the defendant, the private prosecutor, or the plaintiff or defendant in an incidental civil action files an appeal through the original trial People's Court, the original trial People's Court shall, within three days, transfer the petition of appeal together with the case file and evidence to the People's Court at the next higher level, and shall also serve a copy of the petition of appeal on the People's Procuratorate at the corresponding level and on the other party.  
Where the defendant, the private prosecutor, or the plaintiff or defendant in an incidental civil action files an appeal directly with the second instance People's Court, the second instance People's Court shall, within three days, forward the petition of appeal to the original trial People's Court for service on the People's Procuratorate at the corresponding level and on the other party.

<a id="art-232"></a>第二百三十二条　地方各级人民检察院对同级人民法院第一审判决、裁定的抗诉，应当通过原审人民法院提出抗诉书，并且将抗诉书抄送上一级人民检察院。原审人民法院应当将抗诉书连同案卷、证据移送上一级人民法院，并且将抗诉书副本送交当事人。  
上级人民检察院如果认为抗诉不当，可以向同级人民法院撤回抗诉，并且通知下级人民检察院。

**Article 232** : Where a local People's Procuratorate at any level files a protest against a judgment or ruling of first instance rendered by the People's Court at the corresponding level, it shall submit a written protest through the original trial People's Court and shall send a copy of the written protest to the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level. The original trial People's Court shall transfer the written protest together with the case file and evidence to the People's Court at the next higher level and shall serve a copy of the written protest on the parties.  
If the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level considers the protest improper, it may withdraw the protest from the People's Court at the corresponding level and shall notify the People's Procuratorate at the lower level.

<a id="art-233"></a>第二百三十三条　第二审人民法院应当就第一审判决认定的事实和适用法律进行全面审查，不受上诉或者抗诉范围的限制。  
共同犯罪的案件只有部分被告人上诉的，应当对全案进行审查，一并处理。

**Article 233** : The second instance People's Court shall conduct a comprehensive review of the facts found and the law applied in the first instance judgment, without being limited to the scope of appeal or protest.  
For a joint crime case in which only some of the defendants have appealed, the entire case shall be reviewed and handled together.

<a id="art-234"></a>第二百三十四条　第二审人民法院对于下列案件，应当组成合议庭，开庭审理：  
（一）被告人、自诉人及其法定代理人对第一审认定的事实、证据提出异议，可能影响定罪量刑的上诉案件；  
（二）被告人被判处死刑的上诉案件；  
（三）人民检察院抗诉的案件；  
（四）其他应当开庭审理的案件。  
第二审人民法院决定不开庭审理的，应当讯问被告人，听取其他当事人、辩护人、诉讼代理人的意见。  
第二审人民法院开庭审理上诉、抗诉案件，可以到案件发生地或者原审人民法院所在地进行。

**Article 234** : The second instance People's Court shall form a collegial panel and hold a hearing for the following cases:  
(1) An appeal case in which the defendant, the private prosecutor, or their legal representatives raise objections to the facts and evidence found in the first instance, which may affect the conviction and sentencing;  
(2) An appeal case in which the defendant has been sentenced to the death penalty;  
(3) A case protested by the People's Procuratorate; or  
(4) Other cases that shall be heard in court.  
Where the second instance People's Court decides not to hold a hearing, it shall interrogate the defendant and hear the opinions of other parties, defenders, and litigation agents.  
The second instance People's Court may hear appeal and protest cases at the place where the case occurred or at the location of the original trial People's Court.

<a id="art-235"></a>第二百三十五条　人民检察院提出抗诉的案件或者第二审人民法院开庭审理的公诉案件，同级人民检察院都应当派员出席法庭。第二审人民法院应当在决定开庭审理后及时通知人民检察院查阅案卷。人民检察院应当在一个月以内查阅完毕。人民检察院查阅案卷的时间不计入审理期限。

**Article 235** : For a case protested by the People's Procuratorate or a case of public prosecution heard in court by the second instance People's Court, the People's Procuratorate at the corresponding level shall send a prosecutor to appear in court. The second instance People's Court shall promptly notify the People's Procuratorate to review the case file after deciding to hold a hearing. The People's Procuratorate shall complete the review within one month. The time spent by the People's Procuratorate in reviewing the case file shall not be counted in the trial time limit.

<a id="art-236"></a>第二百三十六条　第二审人民法院对不服第一审判决的上诉、抗诉案件，经过审理后，应当按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）原判决认定事实和适用法律正确、量刑适当的，应当裁定驳回上诉或者抗诉，维持原判；  
（二）原判决认定事实没有错误，但适用法律有错误，或者量刑不当的，应当改判；  
（三）原判决事实不清楚或者证据不足的，可以在查清事实后改判；也可以裁定撤销原判，发回原审人民法院重新审判。  
原审人民法院对于依照前款第三项规定发回重新审判的案件作出判决后，被告人提出上诉或者人民检察院提出抗诉的，第二审人民法院应当依法作出判决或者裁定，不得再发回原审人民法院重新审判。

**Article 236** : After hearing an appeal or protest case against a first instance judgment, the second instance People's Court shall handle it separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) Where the facts found and the law applied in the original judgment are correct and the sentence is appropriate, a ruling shall be made to dismiss the appeal or protest and affirm the original judgment;  
(2) Where there is no error in the facts found in the original judgment, but there is an error in the application of law or the sentence is inappropriate, the judgment shall be amended;  
(3) Where the facts in the original judgment are unclear or the evidence is insufficient, the judgment may be amended after the facts are clarified, or a ruling may be made to set aside the original judgment and remit the case to the original trial People's Court for retrial.  
After the original trial People's Court renders a judgment on a case remitted for retrial under the provisions of Item (3) of the preceding paragraph, if the defendant files an appeal or the People's Procuratorate files a protest, the second instance People's Court shall render a judgment or ruling according to law and shall not remit the case to the original trial People's Court for retrial again.

<a id="art-237"></a>第二百三十七条　第二审人民法院审理被告人或者他的法定代理人、辩护人、近亲属上诉的案件，不得加重被告人的刑罚。第二审人民法院发回原审人民法院重新审判的案件，除有新的犯罪事实，人民检察院补充起诉的以外，原审人民法院也不得加重被告人的刑罚。  
人民检察院提出抗诉或者自诉人提出上诉的，不受前款规定的限制。

**Article 237** : When the second instance People's Court tries an appeal case filed by the defendant or his legal representative, defender, or close relative, it shall not increase the defendant's punishment. For a case remitted by the second instance People's Court to the original trial People's Court for retrial, unless new criminal facts are discovered and the People's Procuratorate files a supplementary prosecution, the original trial People's Court shall not increase the defendant's punishment.  
The restriction in the preceding paragraph shall not apply where the People's Procuratorate files a protest or the private prosecutor files an appeal.

<a id="art-238"></a>第二百三十八条　第二审人民法院发现第一审人民法院的审理有下列违反法律规定的诉讼程序的情形之一的，应当裁定撤销原判，发回原审人民法院重新审判：  
（一）违反本法有关公开审判的规定的；  
（二）违反回避制度的；  
（三）剥夺或者限制了当事人的法定诉讼权利，可能影响公正审判的；  
（四）审判组织的组成不合法的；  
（五）其他违反法律规定的诉讼程序，可能影响公正审判的。

**Article 238** : Where the second instance People's Court discovers that the trial of the first instance People's Court is tainted by any of the following circumstances that violate the legally prescribed procedure, it shall render a ruling to set aside the original judgment and remit the case to the original trial People's Court for retrial:  
(1) Violation of the provisions of this Law regarding public trial;  
(2) Violation of the recusal system;  
(3) Deprivation or restriction of the parties' statutory litigation rights likely to affect a fair trial;  
(4) An unlawful composition of the trial organization; or  
(5) Other violations of the legally prescribed procedure likely to affect a fair trial.

<a id="art-239"></a>第二百三十九条　原审人民法院对于发回重新审判的案件，应当另行组成合议庭，依照第一审程序进行审判。对于重新审判后的判决，依照[本法第二百二十七条](#art-227)、第二百二十八条、第二百二十九条的规定可以上诉、抗诉。

**Article 239** : For a case remitted for retrial, the original trial People's Court shall form a new collegial panel and conduct the trial in accordance with the first instance procedure. The judgment rendered after retrial may be appealed or protested in accordance with the provisions of Articles 227, 228, and 229 of this Law.

<a id="art-240"></a>第二百四十条　第二审人民法院对不服第一审裁定的上诉或者抗诉，经过审查后，应当参照[本法第二百三十六条](#art-236)、第二百三十八条和第二百三十九条的规定，分别情形用裁定驳回上诉、抗诉，或者撤销、变更原裁定。

**Article 240** : After reviewing an appeal or protest against a first instance ruling, the second instance People's Court shall, by reference to the provisions of Article 236, Article 238, and Article 239 of this Law, render a ruling to dismiss the appeal or protest, or to set aside or modify the original ruling, as the case may be.

<a id="art-241"></a>第二百四十一条　第二审人民法院发回原审人民法院重新审判的案件，原审人民法院从收到发回的案件之日起，重新计算审理期限。

**Article 241** Where a case is remanded by the people's court of second instance to the original trial people's court for retrial, the original trial people's court shall recalculate the trial period from the date of receiving the remanded case.

<a id="art-242"></a>第二百四十二条　第二审人民法院审判上诉或者抗诉案件的程序，除本章已有规定的以外，参照第一审程序的规定进行。

**Article 242** The procedure for adjudicating appeals or protests by the people's court of second instance shall, unless otherwise provided in this Chapter, follow the provisions on the procedure of first instance.

<a id="art-243"></a>第二百四十三条　第二审人民法院受理上诉、抗诉案件，应当在二个月以内审结。对于可能判处死刑的案件或者附带民事诉讼的案件，以及有[本法第一百五十八条](#art-158)规定情形之一的，经省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院批准或者决定，可以延长二个月；因特殊情况还需要延长的，报请最高人民法院批准。  
最高人民法院受理上诉、抗诉案件的审理期限，由最高人民法院决定。

**Article 243** A people's court of second instance that accepts an appeal or protest case shall conclude the trial within two months. For a case in which a sentence of death penalty may be imposed or a case attached with a civil action, or for a case under any of the circumstances provided for in Article 158 of this Law, the time limit may be extended by two months upon the approval or decision of the Higher People's Court of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; if further extension is required due to special circumstances, the matter shall be submitted to the Supreme People's Court for approval.  
The trial period for an appeal or protest case accepted by the Supreme People's Court shall be determined by the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-244"></a>第二百四十四条　第二审的判决、裁定和最高人民法院的判决、裁定，都是终审的判决、裁定。

**Article 244** Judgments and orders of the second instance and judgments and orders of the Supreme People's Court shall be final judgments and orders.

<a id="art-245"></a>第二百四十五条　公安机关、人民检察院和人民法院对查封、扣押、冻结的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的财物及其孳息，应当妥善保管，以供核查，并制作清单，随案移送。任何单位和个人不得挪用或者自行处理。对被害人的合法财产，应当及时返还。对违禁品或者不宜长期保存的物品，应当依照国家有关规定处理。  
对作为证据使用的实物应当随案移送，对不宜移送的，应当将其清单、照片或者其他证明文件随案移送。  
人民法院作出的判决，应当对查封、扣押、冻结的财物及其孳息作出处理。  
人民法院作出的判决生效以后，有关机关应当根据判决对查封、扣押、冻结的财物及其孳息进行处理。对查封、扣押、冻结的赃款赃物及其孳息，除依法返还被害人的以外，一律上缴国库。  
司法工作人员贪污、挪用或者私自处理查封、扣押、冻结的财物及其孳息的，依法追究刑事责任；不构成犯罪的，给予处分。

**Article 245** The public security organ, the people's procuratorate, and the people's court shall properly preserve the sealed, seized, or frozen property and its fruits of a criminal suspect or defendant for verification, and shall prepare an inventory list to be transferred with the case. No entity or individual may misappropriate or dispose of them on its/his/her own. The victim's lawful property shall be returned in a timely manner. Contraband or articles unsuitable for long-term preservation shall be disposed of in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.  
Physical evidence shall be transferred with the case; if it is unsuitable for transfer, its inventory list, photographs, or other certification documents shall be transferred with the case.  
The judgment rendered by the people's court shall address the disposal of the sealed, seized, or frozen property and its fruits.  
After a judgment rendered by the people's court takes effect, the relevant organ shall dispose of the sealed, seized, or frozen property and its fruits in accordance with the judgment. All illegal gains and their fruits that have been sealed, seized, or frozen shall, except those returned to the victim according to law, be turned over to the state treasury.  
If a judicial officer embezzles, misappropriates, or privately disposes of the sealed, seized, or frozen property and its fruits, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, sanctions shall be imposed.

### 第四章　死刑复核程序

<a id="art-246"></a>第二百四十六条　死刑由最高人民法院核准。

**Article 246** A death penalty shall be subject to the approval of the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-247"></a>第二百四十七条　中级人民法院判处死刑的第一审案件，被告人不上诉的，应当由高级人民法院复核后，报请最高人民法院核准。高级人民法院不同意判处死刑的，可以提审或者发回重新审判。  
高级人民法院判处死刑的第一审案件被告人不上诉的，和判处死刑的第二审案件，都应当报请最高人民法院核准。

**Article 247** Where an Intermediate People's Court imposes the death penalty in a case of first instance and the defendant does not appeal, the case shall be submitted to the Supreme People's Court for approval after being reviewed by the Higher People's Court. If the Higher People's Court does not agree to the death sentence, it may bring the case up for trial or remand it for retrial.  
If a Higher People's Court imposes the death penalty in a case of first instance and the defendant does not appeal, and if it imposes the death penalty in a case of second instance, both shall be reported to the Supreme People's Court for approval.

<a id="art-248"></a>第二百四十八条　中级人民法院判处死刑缓期二年执行的案件，由高级人民法院核准。

**Article 248** A case in which an Intermediate People's Court imposes a death sentence with a two-year reprieve shall be subject to the approval of the Higher People's Court.

<a id="art-249"></a>第二百四十九条　最高人民法院复核死刑案件，高级人民法院复核死刑缓期执行的案件，应当由审判员三人组成合议庭进行。

**Article 249** The Supreme People's Court, when reviewing a death penalty case, and the Higher People's Court, when reviewing a case of death sentence with a two-year reprieve, shall form a collegial panel composed of three judges.

<a id="art-250"></a>第二百五十条　最高人民法院复核死刑案件，应当作出核准或者不核准死刑的裁定。对于不核准死刑的，最高人民法院可以发回重新审判或者予以改判。

**Article 250** When reviewing a death penalty case, the Supreme People's Court shall render an order either approving or not approving the death penalty. For a case where the death penalty is not approved, the Supreme People's Court may remand it for retrial or amend the judgment.

<a id="art-251"></a>第二百五十一条　最高人民法院复核死刑案件，应当讯问被告人，辩护律师提出要求的，应当听取辩护律师的意见。  
在复核死刑案件过程中，最高人民检察院可以向最高人民法院提出意见。最高人民法院应当将死刑复核结果通报最高人民检察院。

**Article 251** When reviewing a death penalty case, the Supreme People's Court shall interrogate the defendant; if the defense lawyer so requests, the defense lawyer's opinions shall be heard.  
During the process of reviewing a death penalty case, the Supreme People's Procuratorate may present opinions to the Supreme People's Court. The Supreme People's Court shall notify the Supreme People's Procuratorate of the result of the death penalty review.

### 第五章　审判监督程序

<a id="art-252"></a>第二百五十二条　当事人及其法定代理人、近亲属，对已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定，可以向人民法院或者人民检察院提出申诉，但是不能停止判决、裁定的执行。

**Article 252** A party or the party's legal representative or near relative may present a petition to a people's court or a people's procuratorate against a legally effective judgment or order, but the execution of the judgment or order shall not be stayed.

<a id="art-253"></a>第二百五十三条　当事人及其法定代理人、近亲属的申诉符合下列情形之一的，人民法院应当重新审判：  
（一）有新的证据证明原判决、裁定认定的事实确有错误，可能影响定罪量刑的；  
（二）据以定罪量刑的证据不确实、不充分、依法应当予以排除，或者证明案件事实的主要证据之间存在矛盾的；  
（三）原判决、裁定适用法律确有错误的；  
（四）违反法律规定的诉讼程序，可能影响公正审判的；  
（五）审判人员在审理该案件的时候，有贪污受贿，徇私舞弊，枉法裁判行为的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/司法解释/全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第二百五十四条第五款、第二百五十七条第二款的解释.md#art-1" title="被《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第二百五十四条第五款、第二百五十七条第二款的解释》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 253** If a petition presented by a party or the party's legal representative or near relative falls under any of the following circumstances, the people's court shall retry the case:  
(1) New evidence being sufficient to prove that the facts determined in the original judgment or order are indeed erroneous, which may affect the conviction and sentencing;  
(2) The evidence on which the conviction and sentencing were based being not credible or insufficient, and should be excluded according to law, or there being contradictions among the main evidence proving the facts of the case;  
(3) The application of law in the original judgment or order being indeed erroneous;  
(4) The litigation procedure violating the provisions of the law, which may affect the impartiality of adjudication; or  
(5) The judicial officer, during the trial of the case, having committed embezzlement, bribery, favoritism, or perversion of the law in rendering the judgment.

<a id="art-254"></a>第二百五十四条　各级人民法院院长对本院已经发生法律效力的判决和裁定，如果发现在认定事实上或者在适用法律上确有错误，必须提交审判委员会处理。  
最高人民法院对各级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决和裁定，上级人民法院对下级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决和裁定，如果发现确有错误，有权提审或者指令下级人民法院再审。  
最高人民检察院对各级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决和裁定，上级人民检察院对下级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决和裁定，如果发现确有错误，有权按照审判监督程序向同级人民法院提出抗诉。  
人民检察院抗诉的案件，接受抗诉的人民法院应当组成合议庭重新审理，对于原判决事实不清楚或者证据不足的，可以指令下级人民法院再审。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/司法解释/全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第二百五十四条第五款、第二百五十七条第二款的解释.md#art-1" title="被《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第二百五十四条第五款、第二百五十七条第二款的解释》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-9" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第9条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 254** If the president of a people's court at any level discovers any definite error in the determination of facts or in the application of law in a legally effective judgment or order of the same court, the matter must be submitted to the judicial committee for handling.  
If the Supreme People's Court discovers any definite error in a legally effective judgment or order of any people's court at a lower level, or if a people's court at a higher level discovers any definite error in a legally effective judgment or order of a people's court at a lower level, it/he/she shall have the power to bring the case up for trial or to direct the people's court at the lower level to conduct a retrial.  
If the Supreme People's Procuratorate discovers any definite error in a legally effective judgment or order of any people's court at any level, or if a people's procuratorate at a higher level discovers any definite error in a legally effective judgment or order of a people's court at a lower level, it/he/she shall have the power to lodge a protest in accordance with the trial supervision procedure with the corresponding people's court.  
For a case protested by a people's procuratorate, the people's court that accepts the protest shall form a collegial panel to retry the case; if the facts in the original judgment are unclear or the evidence is insufficient, it may direct the people's court at the lower level to conduct a retrial.

<a id="art-255"></a>第二百五十五条　上级人民法院指令下级人民法院再审的，应当指令原审人民法院以外的下级人民法院审理；由原审人民法院审理更为适宜的，也可以指令原审人民法院审理。

**Article 255** Where a people's court at a higher level directs a people's court at a lower level to conduct a retrial, it shall direct a lower-level people's court other than the court that originally tried the case to hear the case; if it is more appropriate for the original trial court to hear the case, it may also direct the original trial court to hear the case.

<a id="art-256"></a>第二百五十六条　人民法院按照审判监督程序重新审判的案件，由原审人民法院审理的，应当另行组成合议庭进行。如果原来是第一审案件，应当依照第一审程序进行审判，所作的判决、裁定，可以上诉、抗诉；如果原来是第二审案件，或者是上级人民法院提审的案件，应当依照第二审程序进行审判，所作的判决、裁定，是终审的判决、裁定。  
人民法院开庭审理的再审案件，同级人民检察院应当派员出席法庭。

**Article 256** Where a case retried by a people's court in accordance with the trial supervision procedure is heard by the original trial court, it shall form a new collegial panel. If the case was originally one of first instance, it shall be tried in accordance with the procedure of first instance, and an appeal or protest may be lodged against the resulting judgment or order; if the case was originally one of second instance, or if the case was brought up for trial by a people's court at a higher level, it shall be tried in accordance with the procedure of second instance, and the resulting judgment or order shall be final.  
For a retrial case heard in open court by a people's court, the corresponding people's procuratorate shall send personnel to attend the court session.

<a id="art-257"></a>第二百五十七条　人民法院决定再审的案件，需要对被告人采取强制措施的，由人民法院依法决定；人民检察院提出抗诉的再审案件，需要对被告人采取强制措施的，由人民检察院依法决定。  
人民法院按照审判监督程序审判的案件，可以决定中止原判决、裁定的执行。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/司法解释/全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第二百五十四条第五款、第二百五十七条第二款的解释.md#art-1" title="被《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第二百五十四条第五款、第二百五十七条第二款的解释》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定.md#art-9" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定》第9条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 257** Where a people's court decides to retry a case and compulsory measures need to be taken against the defendant, the people's court shall decide according to law; for a retrial case for which the people's procuratorate files a protest, if compulsory measures need to be taken against the defendant, the people's procuratorate shall decide according to law.  
A people's court, when trying a case in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, may decide to suspend the execution of the original judgment or order.

<a id="art-258"></a>第二百五十八条　人民法院按照审判监督程序重新审判的案件，应当在作出提审、再审决定之日起三个月以内审结，需要延长期限的，不得超过六个月。  
接受抗诉的人民法院按照审判监督程序审判抗诉的案件，审理期限适用前款规定；对需要指令下级人民法院再审的，应当自接受抗诉之日起一个月以内作出决定，下级人民法院审理案件的期限适用前款规定。

**Article 258** A people's court that retries a case in accordance with the trial supervision procedure shall conclude the trial within three months from the date of the decision to bring the case up for trial or to conduct a retrial; where an extension of time is necessary, the extension shall not exceed six months.  
For a protested case tried by the people's court that accepts the protest in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, the trial period shall be subject to the provisions of the preceding paragraph; where it is necessary to direct a people's court at a lower level to conduct a retrial, the decision shall be made within one month from the date of accepting the protest, and the trial period for the case tried by the lower-level people's court shall be subject to the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

## 第四编　执行

<a id="art-259"></a>第二百五十九条　判决和裁定在发生法律效力后执行。  
下列判决和裁定是发生法律效力的判决和裁定：  
（一）已过法定期限没有上诉、抗诉的判决和裁定；  
（二）终审的判决和裁定；  
（三）最高人民法院核准的死刑的判决和高级人民法院核准的死刑缓期二年执行的判决。

**Article 259** Judgments and orders shall be executed after they take legal effect.  
The following judgments and orders are legally effective judgments and orders:  
(1) Judgments and orders against which no appeal or protest has been filed within the legally prescribed time limit;  
(2) Final judgments and orders; and  
(3) Judgments imposing death penalty approved by the Supreme People's Court and judgments imposing death sentence with a two-year reprieve approved by the Higher People's Court.

<a id="art-260"></a>第二百六十条　第一审人民法院判决被告人无罪、免除刑事处罚的，如果被告人在押，在宣判后应当立即释放。

**Article 260** If a people's court of first instance renders a judgment declaring the defendant innocent or exempting the defendant from criminal punishment, and the defendant is in custody, the defendant shall be released immediately after the pronouncement of the judgment.

<a id="art-261"></a>第二百六十一条　最高人民法院判处和核准的死刑立即执行的判决，应当由最高人民法院院长签发执行死刑的命令。  
被判处死刑缓期二年执行的罪犯，在死刑缓期执行期间，如果没有故意犯罪，死刑缓期执行期满，应当予以减刑的，由执行机关提出书面意见，报请高级人民法院裁定；如果故意犯罪，情节恶劣，查证属实，应当执行死刑的，由高级人民法院报请最高人民法院核准；对于故意犯罪未执行死刑的，死刑缓期执行的期间重新计算，并报最高人民法院备案。

**Article 261** : A sentence of immediate execution of the death penalty imposed and approved by the Supreme People's Court shall be accompanied by an order for execution of the death penalty signed by the President of the Supreme People's Court.  
For a criminal sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve, if, during the period of the reprieve, he/she does not commit an intentional crime and should have his/her sentence commuted upon expiration of the reprieve, the executing organ shall submit a written opinion to the Higher People's Court for a ruling; if he/she commits an intentional crime with flagrant circumstances, which is verified, and should be executed, the Higher People's Court shall report to the Supreme People's Court for approval; if the death penalty is not executed for an intentional crime, the period of the death sentence with a two-year reprieve shall be recalculated, and the matter shall be reported to the Supreme People's Court for record.

<a id="art-262"></a>第二百六十二条　下级人民法院接到最高人民法院执行死刑的命令后，应当在七日以内交付执行。但是发现有下列情形之一的，应当停止执行，并且立即报告最高人民法院，由最高人民法院作出裁定：  
（一）在执行前发现判决可能有错误的；  
（二）在执行前罪犯揭发重大犯罪事实或者有其他重大立功表现，可能需要改判的；  
（三）罪犯正在怀孕。  
前款第一项、第二项停止执行的原因消失后，必须报请最高人民法院院长再签发执行死刑的命令才能执行；由于前款第三项原因停止执行的，应当报请最高人民法院依法改判。

**Article 262** : Upon receiving the order for execution of the death penalty from the Supreme People's Court, the lower people's court shall deliver the criminal for execution within seven days. However, if any of the following circumstances is discovered, execution shall be suspended, and the matter shall be immediately reported to the Supreme People's Court for a ruling:  
(1) The judgment may be erroneous before execution;  
(2) Before execution, the criminal exposes major criminal facts or has other significant meritorious performances, which may necessitate a change of sentence;  
(3) The criminal is pregnant.  
After the causes for suspension under items (1) and (2) of the preceding paragraph cease to exist, an order for execution of the death penalty must be reissued by the President of the Supreme People's Court before execution may proceed; if execution is suspended due to the cause under item (3) of the preceding paragraph, the matter shall be reported to the Supreme People's Court for a change of sentence according to law.

<a id="art-263"></a>第二百六十三条　人民法院在交付执行死刑前，应当通知同级人民检察院派员临场监督。  
死刑采用枪决或者注射等方法执行。  
死刑可以在刑场或者指定的羁押场所内执行。  
指挥执行的审判人员，对罪犯应当验明正身，讯问有无遗言、信札，然后交付执行人员执行死刑。在执行前，如果发现可能有错误，应当暂停执行，报请最高人民法院裁定。  
执行死刑应当公布，不应示众。  
执行死刑后，在场书记员应当写成笔录。交付执行的人民法院应当将执行死刑情况报告最高人民法院。  
执行死刑后，交付执行的人民法院应当通知罪犯家属。

**Article 263** : Before delivering a criminal for execution of the death penalty, the people's court shall notify the people's procuratorate at the same level to dispatch an officer to supervise the execution on-site.  
The death penalty shall be executed by methods such as shooting or injection.  
The death penalty may be executed at an execution ground or a designated custodial facility.  
The judicial officer directing the execution shall verify the identity of the criminal, inquire whether there are any last words or letters, and then deliver the criminal to the execution personnel for execution. If any possible error is discovered before execution, execution shall be suspended, and the matter shall be reported to the Supreme People's Court for a ruling.  
The execution of the death penalty shall be announced but shall not be conducted in public.  
After the execution of the death penalty, the court clerk present shall make a record. The people's court that delivered the execution shall report the circumstances of the execution to the Supreme People's Court.  
After the execution of the death penalty, the people's court that delivered the execution shall notify the family of the criminal.

<a id="art-264"></a>第二百六十四条　罪犯被交付执行刑罚的时候，应当由交付执行的人民法院在判决生效后十日以内将有关的法律文书送达公安机关、监狱或者其他执行机关。  
对被判处死刑缓期二年执行、无期徒刑、有期徒刑的罪犯，由公安机关依法将该罪犯送交监狱执行刑罚。对被判处有期徒刑的罪犯，在被交付执行刑罚前，剩余刑期在三个月以下的，由看守所代为执行。对被判处拘役的罪犯，由公安机关执行。  
对未成年犯应当在未成年犯管教所执行刑罚。  
执行机关应当将罪犯及时收押，并且通知罪犯家属。  
判处有期徒刑、拘役的罪犯，执行期满，应当由执行机关发给释放证明书。

**Article 264** : When a criminal is delivered for enforcement of a criminal punishment, the people's court that delivers the enforcement shall, within ten days after the judgment takes effect, serve the relevant legal documents to the public security organ, prison, or other executing organs.  
For a criminal sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve, life imprisonment, or fixed-term imprisonment, the public security organ shall, according to law, deliver the criminal to a prison for enforcement of the criminal punishment. For a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment, if the remaining term of the sentence is less than three months before delivery for enforcement, the detention center shall execute the sentence on behalf of the prison. For a criminal sentenced to criminal detention, the public security organ shall enforce the sentence.  
For a juvenile offender, the sentence shall be enforced in a juvenile correctional facility.  
The executing organ shall take the criminal into custody promptly and notify the criminal's family.  
For a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment or criminal detention, upon the expiration of the sentence, the executing organ shall issue a certificate of release.

<a id="art-265"></a>第二百六十五条　对被判处有期徒刑或者拘役的罪犯，有下列情形之一的，可以暂予监外执行：  
（一）有严重疾病需要保外就医的；  
（二）怀孕或者正在哺乳自己婴儿的妇女；  
（三）生活不能自理，适用暂予监外执行不致危害社会的。  
对被判处无期徒刑的罪犯，有前款第二项规定情形的，可以暂予监外执行。  
对适用保外就医可能有社会危险性的罪犯，或者自伤自残的罪犯，不得保外就医。  
对罪犯确有严重疾病，必须保外就医的，由省级人民政府指定的医院诊断并开具证明文件。  
在交付执行前，暂予监外执行由交付执行的人民法院决定；在交付执行后，暂予监外执行由监狱或者看守所提出书面意见，报省级以上监狱管理机关或者设区的市一级以上公安机关批准。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-631" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第631条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 265** : For a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment or criminal detention, if any of the following circumstances exists, the criminal may be allowed to serve the sentence temporarily outside prison:  
(1) Having a serious illness requiring medical treatment outside prison;  
(2) Being a pregnant woman or a woman nursing her own infant;  
(3) Being unable to take care of oneself, and serving the sentence temporarily outside prison would not endanger society.  
For a criminal sentenced to life imprisonment, if the circumstance under item (2) of the preceding paragraph exists, the criminal may be allowed to serve the sentence temporarily outside prison.  
For a criminal for whom medical treatment outside prison may pose a social danger, or a criminal who inflicts self-injury or self-harm, medical treatment outside prison shall not be granted.  
If a criminal truly has a serious illness and must receive medical treatment outside prison, a diagnosis and certificate shall be issued by a hospital designated by the provincial people's government.  
Before delivery for enforcement, the decision on temporary service outside prison shall be made by the people's court that delivers the enforcement; after delivery for enforcement, the prison or detention center shall submit a written opinion to the prison administration authority at or above the provincial level or the public security organ at or above the level of a city divided into districts for approval.

<a id="art-266"></a>第二百六十六条　监狱、看守所提出暂予监外执行的书面意见的，应当将书面意见的副本抄送人民检察院。人民检察院可以向决定或者批准机关提出书面意见。

**Article 266** : When a prison or detention center submits a written opinion on temporary service outside prison, it shall send a copy of the written opinion to the people's procuratorate. The people's procuratorate may submit a written opinion to the deciding or approving authority.

<a id="art-267"></a>第二百六十七条　决定或者批准暂予监外执行的机关应当将暂予监外执行决定抄送人民检察院。人民检察院认为暂予监外执行不当的，应当自接到通知之日起一个月以内将书面意见送交决定或者批准暂予监外执行的机关，决定或者批准暂予监外执行的机关接到人民检察院的书面意见后，应当立即对该决定进行重新核查。

**Article 267** : The authority that decides or approves temporary service outside prison shall send a copy of the decision on temporary service outside prison to the people's procuratorate. If the people's procuratorate considers the temporary service outside prison improper, it shall, within one month from the date of receiving the notice, send a written opinion to the authority that decided or approved the temporary service outside prison. Upon receiving the written opinion from the people's procuratorate, the authority that decided or approved the temporary service outside prison shall immediately conduct a re-examination of the decision.

<a id="art-268"></a>第二百六十八条　对暂予监外执行的罪犯，有下列情形之一的，应当及时收监：  
（一）发现不符合暂予监外执行条件的；  
（二）严重违反有关暂予监外执行监督管理规定的；  
（三）暂予监外执行的情形消失后，罪犯刑期未满的。  
对于人民法院决定暂予监外执行的罪犯应当予以收监的，由人民法院作出决定，将有关的法律文书送达公安机关、监狱或者其他执行机关。  
不符合暂予监外执行条件的罪犯通过贿赂等非法手段被暂予监外执行的，在监外执行的期间不计入执行刑期。罪犯在暂予监外执行期间脱逃的，脱逃的期间不计入执行刑期。  
罪犯在暂予监外执行期间死亡的，执行机关应当及时通知监狱或者看守所。

**Article 268** : For a criminal serving a sentence temporarily outside prison, if any of the following circumstances exists, the criminal shall be promptly taken back into custody:  
(1) It is discovered that the conditions for temporary service outside prison are not met;  
(2) Serious violation of the supervisory and administrative provisions on temporary service outside prison;  
(3) The circumstances for temporary service outside prison cease to exist, and the criminal's sentence has not been completed.  
If a criminal who was allowed temporary service outside prison by a people's court decision should be taken back into custody, the people's court shall make a decision and serve the relevant legal documents to the public security organ, prison, or other executing organs.  
If a criminal who does not meet the conditions for temporary service outside prison is allowed temporary service outside prison through illegal means such as bribery, the period served outside prison shall not be counted toward the term of the sentence. If a criminal escapes during the period of temporary service outside prison, the period of escape shall not be counted toward the term of the sentence.  
If a criminal dies during the period of temporary service outside prison, the executing organ shall promptly notify the prison or detention center.

<a id="art-269"></a>第二百六十九条　对被判处管制、宣告缓刑、假释或者暂予监外执行的罪犯，依法实行社区矫正，由社区矫正机构负责执行。

**Article 269** : For a criminal sentenced to public surveillance, granted probation, parole, or temporary service outside prison, community correction shall be implemented according to law, and the community correction institution shall be responsible for its enforcement.

<a id="art-270"></a>第二百七十条　对被判处剥夺政治权利的罪犯，由公安机关执行。执行期满，应当由执行机关书面通知本人及其所在单位、居住地基层组织。

**Article 270** : For a criminal sentenced to deprivation of political rights, the public security organ shall enforce the sentence. Upon the expiration of the sentence, the executing organ shall notify the criminal in writing, as well as the criminal's unit and the grassroots organization at the place of residence.

<a id="art-271"></a>第二百七十一条　被判处罚金的罪犯，期满不缴纳的，人民法院应当强制缴纳；如果由于遭遇不能抗拒的灾祸等原因缴纳确实有困难的，经人民法院裁定，可以延期缴纳、酌情减少或者免除。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/法律解释/全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第二百七十一条第二款的解释.md#art-1" title="被《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第二百七十一条第二款的解释》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 271** : If a criminal sentenced to a fine fails to pay it upon expiration of the period, the people's court shall enforce payment; if, due to reasons such as an irresistible disaster, payment is truly difficult, the people's court may, by ruling, grant an extension, reduce the amount appropriately, or remit the fine.

<a id="art-272"></a>第二百七十二条　没收财产的判决，无论附加适用或者独立适用，都由人民法院执行；在必要的时候，可以会同公安机关执行。

**Article 272** : A judgment of confiscation of property, whether applied as a supplementary punishment or independently, shall be enforced by the people's court; when necessary, it may be enforced jointly with the public security organ.

<a id="art-273"></a>第二百七十三条　罪犯在服刑期间又犯罪的，或者发现了判决的时候所没有发现的罪行，由执行机关移送人民检察院处理。  
被判处管制、拘役、有期徒刑或者无期徒刑的罪犯，在执行期间确有悔改或者立功表现，应当依法予以减刑、假释的时候，由执行机关提出建议书，报请人民法院审核裁定，并将建议书副本抄送人民检察院。人民检察院可以向人民法院提出书面意见。

**Article 273** : If a criminal commits another crime while serving a sentence, or if a crime not discovered at the time of judgment is discovered, the executing organ shall transfer the matter to the people's procuratorate for disposition.  
If a criminal sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention, fixed-term imprisonment, or life imprisonment demonstrates genuine repentance or meritorious performance during the period of enforcement, and should be granted commutation of punishment or parole according to law, the executing organ shall submit a proposal to the people's court for examination and ruling, and shall send a copy of the proposal to the people's procuratorate. The people's procuratorate may submit a written opinion to the people's court.

<a id="art-274"></a>第二百七十四条　人民检察院认为人民法院减刑、假释的裁定不当，应当在收到裁定书副本后二十日以内，向人民法院提出书面纠正意见。人民法院应当在收到纠正意见后一个月以内重新组成合议庭进行审理，作出最终裁定。

**Article 274** : If the people's procuratorate considers the ruling on commutation of punishment or parole by the people's court improper, it shall, within twenty days after receiving a copy of the ruling, submit a written opinion of correction to the people's court. The people's court shall, within one month after receiving the correction opinion, form a new collegial panel to conduct a trial and make a final ruling.

<a id="art-275"></a>第二百七十五条　监狱和其他执行机关在刑罚执行中，如果认为判决有错误或者罪犯提出申诉，应当转请人民检察院或者原判人民法院处理。

**Article 275** : If, during the enforcement of a criminal punishment, a prison or other executing organ considers the judgment erroneous, or if a criminal files a petition, the organ shall refer the matter to the people's procuratorate or the people's court that rendered the original judgment for disposition.

<a id="art-276"></a>第二百七十六条　人民检察院对执行机关执行刑罚的活动是否合法实行监督。如果发现有违法的情况，应当通知执行机关纠正。

**Article 276** : The people's procuratorate shall exercise supervision over the legality of the activities of the executing organs in enforcing criminal punishments. If any illegal circumstances are discovered, it shall notify the executing organ to correct them.

## 第五编　特别程序

### 第一章　未成年人刑事案件诉讼程序

<a id="art-277"></a>第二百七十七条　对犯罪的未成年人实行教育、感化、挽救的方针，坚持教育为主、惩罚为辅的原则。  
人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关办理未成年人刑事案件，应当保障未成年人行使其诉讼权利，保障未成年人得到法律帮助，并由熟悉未成年人身心特点的审判人员、检察人员、侦查人员承办。

**Article 277** : The policy of education, persuasion, and redemption shall be applied to minors who commit crimes, adhering to the principle of education as the mainstay and punishment as supplementary.  
When handling criminal cases involving minors, the people's courts, people's procuratorates, and public security organs shall ensure that minors exercise their procedural rights, ensure that minors receive legal assistance, and the cases shall be handled by judicial officers, procuratorial officers, and investigative officers who are familiar with the physical and psychological characteristics of minors.

<a id="art-278"></a>第二百七十八条　未成年犯罪嫌疑人、被告人没有委托辩护人的，人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关应当通知法律援助机构指派律师为其提供辩护。

**Article 278** : If a juvenile criminal suspect or defendant has not retained a defender, the people's court, people's procuratorate, and public security organ shall notify a legal aid institution to assign a lawyer to provide defense.

<a id="art-279"></a>第二百七十九条　公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院办理未成年人刑事案件，根据情况可以对未成年犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的成长经历、犯罪原因、监护教育等情况进行调查。

**Article 279** : When handling criminal cases involving minors, the public security organ, people's procuratorate, and people's court may, depending on the circumstances, conduct investigations into the growth experience, causes of the crime, guardianship and education, and other conditions of the juvenile criminal suspect or defendant.

<a id="art-280"></a>第二百八十条　对未成年犯罪嫌疑人、被告人应当严格限制适用逮捕措施。人民检察院审查批准逮捕和人民法院决定逮捕，应当讯问未成年犯罪嫌疑人、被告人，听取辩护律师的意见。  
对被拘留、逮捕和执行刑罚的未成年人与成年人应当分别关押、分别管理、分别教育。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民检察院关于审查起诉期间犯罪嫌疑人脱逃或者患有严重疾病的应当如何处理的批复.md#art-5" title="被《最高人民检察院关于审查起诉期间犯罪嫌疑人脱逃或者患有严重疾病的应当如何处理的批复》第5条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 280** : The application of arrest measures to juvenile criminal suspects and defendants shall be strictly limited. When the people's procuratorate examines and approves an arrest, and when the people's court decides on an arrest, it shall interrogate the juvenile criminal suspect or defendant and hear the opinions of the defense lawyer.  
Juveniles who are detained, arrested, or serving a sentence shall be held in separate custody, managed separately, and educated separately from adults.

<a id="art-281"></a>第二百八十一条　对于未成年人刑事案件，在讯问和审判的时候，应当通知未成年犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的法定代理人到场。无法通知、法定代理人不能到场或者法定代理人是共犯的，也可以通知未成年犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的其他成年亲属，所在学校、单位、居住地基层组织或者未成年人保护组织的代表到场，并将有关情况记录在案。到场的法定代理人可以代为行使未成年犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的诉讼权利。  
到场的法定代理人或者其他人员认为办案人员在讯问、审判中侵犯未成年人合法权益的，可以提出意见。讯问笔录、法庭笔录应当交给到场的法定代理人或者其他人员阅读或者向他宣读。  
讯问女性未成年犯罪嫌疑人，应当有女工作人员在场。  
审判未成年人刑事案件，未成年被告人最后陈述后，其法定代理人可以进行补充陈述。  
询问未成年被害人、证人，适用第一款、第二款、第三款的规定。

**Article 281** For criminal cases involving minors, when interrogating and adjudicating, the legal representative of the juvenile criminal suspect or defendant shall be notified to be present. If the legal representative cannot be notified, cannot be present, or is an accomplice, other adult relatives of the juvenile criminal suspect or defendant, or representatives from the school, unit, grassroots organization of the place of residence, or juvenile protection organization may also be notified to be present, and the relevant information shall be recorded in the case file. The legal representative present may exercise the litigation rights of the juvenile criminal suspect or defendant on their behalf.  
If the legal representative or other person present believes that the case handler has infringed the legitimate rights and interests of the juvenile during interrogation or adjudication, the person may raise an opinion. The interrogation record and court record shall be given to the legal representative or other person present for reading or shall be read aloud to them.  
When interrogating a female juvenile criminal suspect, a female staff member shall be present.  
When adjudicating a criminal case involving a minor, after the juvenile defendant makes a final statement, the legal representative may make a supplementary statement.  
When questioning a juvenile victim or witness, the provisions of paragraphs 1, 2, and 3 shall apply.

<a id="art-282"></a>第二百八十二条　对于未成年人涉嫌刑法分则第四章、第五章、第六章规定的犯罪，可能判处一年有期徒刑以下刑罚，符合起诉条件，但有悔罪表现的，人民检察院可以作出附条件不起诉的决定。人民检察院在作出附条件不起诉的决定以前，应当听取公安机关、被害人的意见。  
对附条件不起诉的决定，公安机关要求复议、提请复核或者被害人申诉的，适用[本法第一百七十九条](#art-179)、第一百八十条的规定。  
未成年犯罪嫌疑人及其法定代理人对人民检察院决定附条件不起诉有异议的，人民检察院应当作出起诉的决定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-469" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第469条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 282** For a juvenile suspected of committing a crime specified in Chapters IV, V, or VI of the Specific Provisions of the Criminal Law, with a possible punishment of not more than one year of fixed-term imprisonment, who meets the conditions for prosecution but shows signs of repentance, the People's Procuratorate may make a decision to impose a conditional non-prosecution. Before making a decision to impose a conditional non-prosecution, the People's Procuratorate shall hear the opinions of the public security organ and the victim.  
With respect to a decision on conditional non-prosecution, if the public security organ requests a reconsideration, applies for a review, or the victim petitions, the provisions of Articles 179 and 180 of this Law shall apply.  
If the juvenile criminal suspect and the legal representative have objections to the decision by the People's Procuratorate to impose a conditional non-prosecution, the People's Procuratorate shall make a decision to institute a prosecution.

<a id="art-283"></a>第二百八十三条　在附条件不起诉的考验期内，由人民检察院对被附条件不起诉的未成年犯罪嫌疑人进行监督考察。未成年犯罪嫌疑人的监护人，应当对未成年犯罪嫌疑人加强管教，配合人民检察院做好监督考察工作。  
附条件不起诉的考验期为六个月以上一年以下，从人民检察院作出附条件不起诉的决定之日起计算。  
被附条件不起诉的未成年犯罪嫌疑人，应当遵守下列规定：  
（一）遵守法律法规，服从监督；  
（二）按照考察机关的规定报告自己的活动情况；  
（三）离开所居住的市、县或者迁居，应当报经考察机关批准；  
（四）按照考察机关的要求接受矫治和教育。

**Article 283** During the probation period for conditional non-prosecution, the People's Procuratorate shall supervise and examine the juvenile criminal suspect subject to conditional non-prosecution. The guardian of the juvenile criminal suspect shall strengthen discipline and supervision over the juvenile criminal suspect and cooperate with the People's Procuratorate in the supervision and examination work.  
The probation period for conditional non-prosecution shall be not less than six months and not more than one year, calculated from the date the People's Procuratorate makes the decision to impose a conditional non-prosecution.  
A juvenile criminal suspect subject to conditional non-prosecution shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) Comply with laws and regulations and submit to supervision;  
(2) Report on the activities in accordance with the provisions of the examining authority;  
(3) Leaving the city or county of residence or changing residence shall be reported to and approved by the examining authority;  
(4) Undergo correction and education as required by the examining authority.

<a id="art-284"></a>第二百八十四条　被附条件不起诉的未成年犯罪嫌疑人，在考验期内有下列情形之一的，人民检察院应当撤销附条件不起诉的决定，提起公诉：  
（一）实施新的犯罪或者发现决定附条件不起诉以前还有其他犯罪需要追诉的；  
（二）违反治安管理规定或者考察机关有关附条件不起诉的监督管理规定，情节严重的。  
被附条件不起诉的未成年犯罪嫌疑人，在考验期内没有上述情形，考验期满的，人民检察院应当作出不起诉的决定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民检察院关于审查起诉期间犯罪嫌疑人脱逃或者患有严重疾病的应当如何处理的批复.md#art-4" title="被《最高人民检察院关于审查起诉期间犯罪嫌疑人脱逃或者患有严重疾病的应当如何处理的批复》第4条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 284** If a juvenile criminal suspect subject to conditional non-prosecution falls under any of the following circumstances during the probation period, the People's Procuratorate shall revoke the decision on conditional non-prosecution and initiate a public prosecution:  
(1) Commits a new crime or it is discovered that there is another crime that requires prosecution committed before the decision to impose conditional non-prosecution was made;  
(2) Violates provisions on public security administration or the supervisory and administrative provisions regarding conditional non-prosecution of the examining authority, where the circumstances are serious.  
If a juvenile criminal suspect subject to conditional non-prosecution does not fall under the abovementioned circumstances during the probation period and the probation period expires, the People's Procuratorate shall make a decision not to prosecute.

<a id="art-285"></a>第二百八十五条　审判的时候被告人不满十八周岁的案件，不公开审理。但是，经未成年被告人及其法定代理人同意，未成年被告人所在学校和未成年人保护组织可以派代表到场。

**Article 285** Cases in which the defendant is under the age of 18 at the time of adjudication shall not be heard in public. However, with the consent of the juvenile defendant and the legal representative, the school attended by the juvenile defendant and the juvenile protection organization may send representatives to be present.

<a id="art-286"></a>第二百八十六条　犯罪的时候不满十八周岁，被判处五年有期徒刑以下刑罚的，应当对相关犯罪记录予以封存。  
犯罪记录被封存的，不得向任何单位和个人提供，但司法机关为办案需要或者有关单位根据国家规定进行查询的除外。依法进行查询的单位，应当对被封存的犯罪记录的情况予以保密。

**Article 286** If a person is under the age of 18 at the time of committing a crime and is sentenced to a punishment of not more than five years of fixed-term imprisonment, the relevant criminal record shall be sealed.  
A sealed criminal record shall not be provided to any unit or individual, except where a judicial authority needs it for case handling or a relevant unit makes an inquiry in accordance with state regulations. A unit making an inquiry according to law shall keep confidential the information contained in the sealed criminal record.

<a id="art-287"></a>第二百八十七条　办理未成年人刑事案件，除本章已有规定的以外，按照本法的其他规定进行。

**Article 287** The handling of criminal cases involving minors, except where otherwise provided in this Chapter, shall be conducted in accordance with other provisions of this Law.

### 第二章　当事人和解的公诉案件诉讼程序

<a id="art-288"></a>第二百八十八条　下列公诉案件，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人真诚悔罪，通过向被害人赔偿损失、赔礼道歉等方式获得被害人谅解，被害人自愿和解的，双方当事人可以和解：  
（一）因民间纠纷引起，涉嫌刑法分则第四章、第五章规定的犯罪案件，可能判处三年有期徒刑以下刑罚的；  
（二）除渎职犯罪以外的可能判处七年有期徒刑以下刑罚的过失犯罪案件。  
犯罪嫌疑人、被告人在五年以内曾经故意犯罪的，不适用本章规定的程序。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-492" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第492条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 288** In the following cases of public prosecution, if the criminal suspect or defendant sincerely repents, obtains the victim's understanding by compensating the victim for losses, offering an apology, or other means, and the victim voluntarily reconciles, the parties may reach a settlement:  
(1) Cases arising from civil disputes involving crimes specified in Chapters IV or V of the Specific Provisions of the Criminal Law for which the possible punishment is not more than three years of fixed-term imprisonment;  
(2) Cases of negligent crimes, excluding crimes of dereliction of duty, for which the possible punishment is not more than seven years of fixed-term imprisonment.  
If the criminal suspect or defendant has previously intentionally committed a crime within five years, the procedures provided for in this Chapter shall not apply.

<a id="art-289"></a>第二百八十九条　双方当事人和解的，公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院应当听取当事人和其他有关人员的意见，对和解的自愿性、合法性进行审查，并主持制作和解协议书。

**Article 289** If the parties reach a settlement, the public security organ, the People's Procuratorate, and the People's Court shall hear the opinions of the parties and other relevant persons, examine the voluntariness and legality of the settlement, and preside over the creation of a settlement agreement.

<a id="art-290"></a>第二百九十条　对于达成和解协议的案件，公安机关可以向人民检察院提出从宽处理的建议。人民检察院可以向人民法院提出从宽处罚的建议；对于犯罪情节轻微，不需要判处刑罚的，可以作出不起诉的决定。人民法院可以依法对被告人从宽处罚。

**Article 290** For a case where a settlement agreement has been reached, the public security organ may recommend to the People's Procuratorate a lenient disposition. The People's Procuratorate may recommend to the People's Court a lenient punishment; where the circumstances of the crime are minor and a punishment is not necessary, the People's Procuratorate may make a decision not to prosecute. The People's Court may, according to law, impose a lenient punishment on the defendant.

### 第三章　缺席审判程序

<a id="art-291"></a>第二百九十一条　对于贪污贿赂犯罪案件，以及需要及时进行审判，经最高人民检察院核准的严重危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪案件，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人在境外，监察机关、公安机关移送起诉，人民检察院认为犯罪事实已经查清，证据确实、充分，依法应当追究刑事责任的，可以向人民法院提起公诉。人民法院进行审查后，对于起诉书中有明确的指控犯罪事实，符合缺席审判程序适用条件的，应当决定开庭审判。  
前款案件，由犯罪地、被告人离境前居住地或者最高人民法院指定的中级人民法院组成合议庭进行审理。

**Article 291** For cases of embezzlement or bribery, as well as cases of crimes seriously endangering national security or terrorist activities that require prompt adjudication and are approved by the Supreme People's Procuratorate, where the criminal suspect or defendant is outside the territory, and after the supervisory authority or public security organ has transferred the case for prosecution, if the People's Procuratorate finds that the criminal facts have been fully investigated, the evidence is reliable and sufficient, and criminal liability shall be pursued according to law, the People's Procuratorate may initiate a public prosecution with the People's Court. After review by the People's Court, if the indictment contains clear allegations of criminal facts and meets the conditions for applying the absentee adjudication procedure, the People's Court shall decide to hold a trial.  
Cases specified in the preceding paragraph shall be tried by a collegial panel formed by the Intermediate People's Court of the place of the crime, the place of residence of the defendant before departure, or designated by the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-292"></a>第二百九十二条　人民法院应当通过有关国际条约规定的或者外交途径提出的司法协助方式，或者被告人所在地法律允许的其他方式，将传票和人民检察院的起诉书副本送达被告人。传票和起诉书副本送达后，被告人未按要求到案的，人民法院应当开庭审理，依法作出判决，并对违法所得及其他涉案财产作出处理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/司法解释/全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第二百九十二条的解释.md#art-1" title="被《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第二百九十二条的解释》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 292** The People's Court shall serve the summons and a copy of the indictment from the People's Procuratorate on the defendant through judicial assistance methods provided for in relevant international treaties or through diplomatic channels, or by other methods allowed by the law of the defendant's location. After the summons and a copy of the indictment are served, if the defendant fails to appear as required, the People's Court shall hold a trial according to law, render a judgment, and dispose of the illegal gains and other property involved in the case.

<a id="art-293"></a>第二百九十三条　人民法院缺席审判案件，被告人有权委托辩护人，被告人的近亲属可以代为委托辩护人。被告人及其近亲属没有委托辩护人的，人民法院应当通知法律援助机构指派律师为其提供辩护。

**Article 293** In a case tried by absentee adjudication, the defendant shall have the right to entrust a defender, and the close relatives of the defendant may entrust a defender on the defendant's behalf. If the defendant or the close relatives fail to entrust a defender, the People's Court shall notify a legal aid institution to appoint a lawyer to provide defense for the defendant.

<a id="art-294"></a>第二百九十四条　人民法院应当将判决书送达被告人及其近亲属、辩护人。被告人或者其近亲属不服判决的，有权向上一级人民法院上诉。辩护人经被告人或者其近亲属同意，可以提出上诉。  
人民检察院认为人民法院的判决确有错误的，应当向上一级人民法院提出抗诉。

**Article 294** The People's Court shall serve the judgment on the defendant, the close relatives, and the defender. If the defendant or the close relatives are dissatisfied with the judgment, they shall have the right to appeal to the next higher People's Court. With the consent of the defendant or the close relatives, the defender may file an appeal.  
If the People's Procuratorate finds that the judgment of the People's Court is erroneous, it shall file a protest with the next higher People's Court.

<a id="art-295"></a>第二百九十五条　在审理过程中，被告人自动投案或者被抓获的，人民法院应当重新审理。  
罪犯在判决、裁定发生法律效力后到案的，人民法院应当将罪犯交付执行刑罚。交付执行刑罚前，人民法院应当告知罪犯有权对判决、裁定提出异议。罪犯对判决、裁定提出异议的，人民法院应当重新审理。  
依照生效判决、裁定对罪犯的财产进行的处理确有错误的，应当予以返还、赔偿。

**Article 295** During the trial process, if the defendant voluntarily surrenders or is captured, the People's Court shall retry the case.  
If the offender appears after the judgment or ruling has taken legal effect, the People's Court shall deliver the offender for execution of the punishment. Before delivering the offender for execution of the punishment, the People's Court shall inform the offender of the right to raise objections to the judgment or ruling. If the offender raises objections to the judgment or ruling, the People's Court shall retry the case.  
If the handling of the offender's property pursuant to an effective judgment or ruling is found to be erroneous, the property shall be returned or compensation shall be provided.

<a id="art-296"></a>第二百九十六条　因被告人患有严重疾病无法出庭，中止审理超过六个月，被告人仍无法出庭，被告人及其法定代理人、近亲属申请或者同意恢复审理的，人民法院可以在被告人不出庭的情况下缺席审理，依法作出判决。

**Article 296** If due to a serious illness the defendant cannot appear in court, and the suspension of trial lasts for more than six months while the defendant still cannot appear, and the defendant or the legal representative or close relatives apply for or agree to resume the trial, the People's Court may, in the absence of the defendant, conduct an absentee trial and render a judgment according to law.

<a id="art-297"></a>第二百九十七条　被告人死亡的，人民法院应当裁定终止审理，但有证据证明被告人无罪，人民法院经缺席审理确认无罪的，应当依法作出判决。  
人民法院按照审判监督程序重新审判的案件，被告人死亡的，人民法院可以缺席审理，依法作出判决。

**Article 297** If the defendant dies, the People's Court shall rule to terminate the trial, except where there is evidence proving the defendant's innocence and the People's Court, through absentee trial, confirms the innocence, in which case the People's Court shall render a judgment according to law.  
If the defendant dies in a case retried under the trial supervision procedure, the People's Court may conduct an absentee trial and render a judgment according to law.

### 第四章　犯罪嫌疑人、被告人逃匿、死亡案件违法所得的没收程序

<a id="art-298"></a>第二百九十八条　对于贪污贿赂犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪等重大犯罪案件，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人逃匿，在通缉一年后不能到案，或者犯罪嫌疑人、被告人死亡，依照刑法规定应当追缴其违法所得及其他涉案财产的，人民检察院可以向人民法院提出没收违法所得的申请。  
公安机关认为有前款规定情形的，应当写出没收违法所得意见书，移送人民检察院。  
没收违法所得的申请应当提供与犯罪事实、违法所得相关的证据材料，并列明财产的种类、数量、所在地及查封、扣押、冻结的情况。  
人民法院在必要的时候，可以查封、扣押、冻结申请没收的财产。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-522" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第522条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-523" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第523条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-527" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第527条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-533" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第533条引用">[4]</a></sup>

**Article 298** For major criminal cases such as embezzlement or bribery, or terrorist activities, where the criminal suspect or defendant has absconded and cannot appear after being wanted for one year, or where the criminal suspect or defendant has died, and the illegal gains and other property involved in the case shall be confiscated according to the Criminal Law, the People's Procuratorate may apply to the People's Court for the confiscation of illegal gains.  
If the public security organ believes that the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph exist, it shall prepare a written opinion on the confiscation of illegal gains and transfer it to the People's Procuratorate.  
An application for the confiscation of illegal gains shall provide evidence related to the criminal facts and illegal gains and shall specify the types, quantities, locations, and status of seizure, impoundment, or freezing of the property.  
The People's Court may, when necessary, seize, impound, or freeze the property that is the subject of the application for confiscation.

<a id="art-299"></a>第二百九十九条　没收违法所得的申请，由犯罪地或者犯罪嫌疑人、被告人居住地的中级人民法院组成合议庭进行审理。  
人民法院受理没收违法所得的申请后，应当发出公告。公告期间为六个月。犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的近亲属和其他利害关系人有权申请参加诉讼，也可以委托诉讼代理人参加诉讼。  
人民法院在公告期满后对没收违法所得的申请进行审理。利害关系人参加诉讼的，人民法院应当开庭审理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-517" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第517条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 299** An application for the confiscation of illegal gains shall be tried by a collegial panel formed by the Intermediate People's Court in the place of the crime or the place of residence of the criminal suspect or defendant.  
After accepting an application for the confiscation of illegal gains, the People's Court shall issue a public notice. The public notice period shall be six months. Close relatives of the criminal suspect or defendant and other interested parties shall have the right to apply to participate in the proceedings, and may also entrust litigation agents to participate in the proceedings.  
After the expiry of the public notice period, the People's Court shall conduct a trial on the application for the confiscation of illegal gains. If an interested party participates in the proceedings, the People's Court shall hold a hearing.

<a id="art-300"></a>第三百条　人民法院经审理，对经查证属于违法所得及其他涉案财产，除依法返还被害人的以外，应当裁定予以没收；对不属于应当追缴的财产的，应当裁定驳回申请，解除查封、扣押、冻结措施。  
对于人民法院依照前款规定作出的裁定，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的近亲属和其他利害关系人或者人民检察院可以提出上诉、抗诉。

**Article 300** After trial, if the property is verified to be illegal gains or other property involved in the case, the People's Court shall issue a ruling to confiscate it, unless it shall be returned to the victim according to law; if the property does not belong to what shall be recovered, the People's Court shall rule to reject the application and lift the seizure, impoundment, or freezing measures.  
For a ruling made by the People's Court in accordance with the preceding paragraph, close relatives of the criminal suspect or defendant, other interested parties, or the People's Procuratorate may file an appeal or protest.

<a id="art-301"></a>第三百零一条　在审理过程中，在逃的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人自动投案或者被抓获的，人民法院应当终止审理。  
没收犯罪嫌疑人、被告人财产确有错误的，应当予以返还、赔偿。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-532" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第532条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 301** During the trial process, if the absconded criminal suspect or defendant voluntarily surrenders or is captured, the People's Court shall terminate the trial.  
If the confiscation of the property of the criminal suspect or defendant is found to be erroneous, the property shall be returned or compensation shall be provided.

### 第五章　依法不负刑事责任的精神病人的强制医疗程序

<a id="art-302"></a>第三百零二条　实施暴力行为，危害公共安全或者严重危害公民人身安全，经法定程序鉴定依法不负刑事责任的精神病人，有继续危害社会可能的，可以予以强制医疗。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-539" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第539条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/人民检察院刑事诉讼规则.md#art-543" title="被《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》第543条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 302** If a mental patient who, through the commission of a violent act, endangers public security or seriously endangers the personal safety of others, and is identified through statutory procedures as not bearing criminal liability according to law, and there is a possibility of continuing to endanger society, they may be subjected to compulsory medical treatment.

<a id="art-303"></a>第三百零三条　根据本章规定对精神病人强制医疗的，由人民法院决定。  
公安机关发现精神病人符合强制医疗条件的，应当写出强制医疗意见书，移送人民检察院。对于公安机关移送的或者在审查起诉过程中发现的精神病人符合强制医疗条件的，人民检察院应当向人民法院提出强制医疗的申请。人民法院在审理案件过程中发现被告人符合强制医疗条件的，可以作出强制医疗的决定。  
对实施暴力行为的精神病人，在人民法院决定强制医疗前，公安机关可以采取临时的保护性约束措施。

**Article 303** The decision to subject a mental patient to compulsory medical treatment in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter shall be made by the People's Court.  
If the public security organ discovers that a mental patient meets the conditions for compulsory medical treatment, the public security organ shall prepare a written opinion on compulsory medical treatment and transfer it to the People's Procuratorate. If, during the examination of a case transferred by the public security organ or during the review of a prosecution, it is discovered that a mental patient meets the conditions for compulsory medical treatment, the People's Procuratorate shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory medical treatment. If, during the trial of a case, the People's Court discovers that the defendant meets the conditions for compulsory medical treatment, the People's Court may make a decision to impose compulsory medical treatment.  
For a mental patient who commits a violent act, before the People's Court decides on compulsory medical treatment, the public security organ may adopt temporary protective restraint measures.

<a id="art-304"></a>第三百零四条　人民法院受理强制医疗的申请后，应当组成合议庭进行审理。  
人民法院审理强制医疗案件，应当通知被申请人或者被告人的法定代理人到场。被申请人或者被告人没有委托诉讼代理人的，人民法院应当通知法律援助机构指派律师为其提供法律帮助。

**Article 304** After accepting an application for compulsory medical treatment, the People's Court shall form a collegial panel to conduct the trial.  
When trying a case of compulsory medical treatment, the People's Court shall notify the legal representative of the respondent or defendant to be present. If the respondent or defendant has not retained a litigation agent, the People's Court shall notify a legal aid institution to appoint a lawyer to provide legal assistance.

<a id="art-305"></a>第三百零五条　人民法院经审理，对于被申请人或者被告人符合强制医疗条件的，应当在一个月以内作出强制医疗的决定。  
被决定强制医疗的人、被害人及其法定代理人、近亲属对强制医疗决定不服的，可以向上一级人民法院申请复议。

**Article 305** After trial, if the respondent or defendant meets the conditions for compulsory medical treatment, the People's Court shall make a decision on compulsory medical treatment within one month.  
If the person subjected to compulsory medical treatment, the victim, and the legal representative or close relatives are dissatisfied with the decision on compulsory medical treatment, they may apply for reconsideration to the next higher People's Court.

<a id="art-306"></a>第三百零六条　强制医疗机构应当定期对被强制医疗的人进行诊断评估。对于已不具有人身危险性，不需要继续强制医疗的，应当及时提出解除意见，报决定强制医疗的人民法院批准。  
被强制医疗的人及其近亲属有权申请解除强制医疗。

**Article 306** A compulsory medical treatment institution shall regularly conduct diagnostic assessments of the persons subjected to compulsory medical treatment. If a person no longer poses a risk of personal danger and is no longer in need of further compulsory medical treatment, the institution shall promptly propose an opinion on discharge and report it to the People's Court that made the decision on compulsory medical treatment for approval.  
The person subjected to compulsory medical treatment and the close relatives shall have the right to apply for the lifting of the compulsory medical treatment.

<a id="art-307"></a>第三百零七条　人民检察院对强制医疗的决定和执行实行监督。  
附　　则

**Article 307** The People's Procuratorate shall exercise supervision over the decisions on and the enforcement of compulsory medical treatment.  
Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-308"></a>第三百零八条　军队保卫部门对军队内部发生的刑事案件行使侦查权。  
中国海警局履行海上维权执法职责，对海上发生的刑事案件行使侦查权。  
对罪犯在监狱内犯罪的案件由监狱进行侦查。  
军队保卫部门、中国海警局、监狱办理刑事案件，适用本法的有关规定。

**Article 308** The security departments of the military shall exercise the power of investigation over criminal cases occurring within the military.  
The China Coast Guard shall perform duties of safeguarding maritime rights and enforcing the law at sea, and exercise the power of investigation over criminal cases occurring at sea.  
Cases in which an offender commits a crime within a prison shall be investigated by the prison.  
When the security departments of the military, the China Coast Guard, or a prison handle criminal cases, the relevant provisions of this Law shall apply.
