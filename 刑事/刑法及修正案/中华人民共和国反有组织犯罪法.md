# 中华人民共和国反有组织犯罪法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2021-12-24  
**生效日期**：2022-05-01

> （2021年12月24日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十二次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　预防和治理 第三章　案件办理 第四章　涉案财产认定和处置 第五章　国家工作人员涉有组织犯罪的处理 第六章　国际合作 第七章　保障措施 第八章　法律责任 第九章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了预防和惩治有组织犯罪，加强和规范反有组织犯罪工作，维护国家安全、社会秩序、经济秩序，保护公民和组织的合法权益，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称有组织犯罪，是指《中华人民共和国刑法》第二百九十四条规定的组织、领导、参加黑社会性质组织犯罪，以及黑社会性质组织、恶势力组织实施的犯罪。 本法所称恶势力组织，是指经常纠集在一起，以暴力、威胁或者其他手段，在一定区域或者行业领域内多次实施违法犯罪活动，为非作恶，欺压群众，扰乱社会秩序、经济秩序，造成较为恶劣的社会影响，但尚未形成黑社会性质组织的犯罪组织。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了预防和惩治有组织犯罪，加强和规范反有组织犯罪工作，维护国家安全、社会秩序、经济秩序，保护公民和组织的合法权益，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of preventing and punishing organized crime, strengthening and regulating the work against organized crime, safeguarding national security, social order, and economic order, and protecting the lawful rights and interests of citizens and organizations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称有组织犯罪，是指[《中华人民共和国刑法》第二百九十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-294)规定的组织、领导、参加黑社会性质组织犯罪，以及黑社会性质组织、恶势力组织实施的犯罪。  
本法所称恶势力组织，是指经常纠集在一起，以暴力、威胁或者其他手段，在一定区域或者行业领域内多次实施违法犯罪活动，为非作恶，欺压群众，扰乱社会秩序、经济秩序，造成较为恶劣的社会影响，但尚未形成黑社会性质组织的犯罪组织。  
境外的黑社会组织到中华人民共和国境内发展组织成员、实施犯罪，以及在境外对中华人民共和国国家或者公民犯罪的，适用本法。

**Article 2** "Organized crime" as used in this Law refers to the crimes of organizing, leading, and participating in a mafia-type organization as prescribed in Article 294 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, as well as crimes committed by mafia-type organizations and evil force organizations.  
"Evil force organization" as used in this Law refers to a criminal organization that frequently gathers together, uses violence, threats, or other means to repeatedly commit illegal and criminal activities in a certain region or industrial sector, acting tyrannically, oppressing the masses, disrupting social order and economic order, causing relatively bad social impact, but has not yet formed a mafia-type organization.  
Where a mafia-type organization outside the territory develops its members or commits crimes within the territory of the People's Republic of China, or commits crimes against the state or citizens of the People's Republic of China outside the territory, this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　反有组织犯罪工作应当坚持总体国家安全观，综合运用法律、经济、科技、文化、教育等手段，建立健全反有组织犯罪工作机制和有组织犯罪预防治理体系。

**Article 3** The work against organized crime shall adhere to a holistic approach to national security, comprehensively employ legal, economic, scientific, technological, cultural, and educational means, and establish and improve the work mechanism against organized crime and the prevention and governance system for organized crime.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　反有组织犯罪工作应当坚持专门工作与群众路线相结合，坚持专项治理与系统治理相结合，坚持与反腐败相结合，坚持与加强基层组织建设相结合，惩防并举、标本兼治。

**Article 4** The work against organized crime shall adhere to the combination of specialized work with the mass line, the combination of special governance with systematic governance, the combination with anti-corruption efforts, and the combination with strengthening the construction of grassroots organizations, addressing both the symptoms and root causes by combining punishment and prevention.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　反有组织犯罪工作应当依法进行，尊重和保障人权，维护公民和组织的合法权益。

**Article 5** The work against organized crime shall be conducted in accordance with law, respecting and protecting human rights, and safeguarding the lawful rights and interests of citizens and organizations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　监察机关、人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关、司法行政机关以及其他有关国家机关，应当根据分工，互相配合，互相制约，依法做好反有组织犯罪工作。  
有关部门应当动员、依靠村民委员会、居民委员会、企业事业单位、社会组织，共同开展反有组织犯罪工作。

**Article 6** The supervisory organs, people's courts, people's procuratorates, public security organs, judicial administrative organs, and other relevant state organs shall, according to their division of duties, cooperate with and check each other, and conduct the work against organized crime in accordance with law.  
Relevant departments shall mobilize and rely on villagers' committees, residents' committees, enterprises, public institutions, and social organizations to jointly carry out the work against organized crime.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　任何单位和个人都有协助、配合有关部门开展反有组织犯罪工作的义务。  
国家依法对协助、配合反有组织犯罪工作的单位和个人给予保护。

**Article 7** All units and individuals have the obligation to assist and cooperate with relevant departments in carrying out the work against organized crime.  
The state shall protect, in accordance with law, units and individuals that assist and cooperate with the work against organized crime.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励单位和个人举报有组织犯罪。  
对举报有组织犯罪或者在反有组织犯罪工作中作出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 8** The state encourages units and individuals to report organized crime.  
Units and individuals that report organized crime or make outstanding contributions in the work against organized crime shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with relevant state regulations.

### 第二章　预防和治理

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各级人民政府和有关部门应当依法组织开展有组织犯罪预防和治理工作，将有组织犯罪预防和治理工作纳入考评体系。  
村民委员会、居民委员会应当协助人民政府以及有关部门开展有组织犯罪预防和治理工作。

**Article 9** People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall, in accordance with law, organize the prevention and governance of organized crime, and incorporate such work into the evaluation system.  
Villagers' committees and residents' committees shall assist people's governments and relevant departments in carrying out the prevention and governance of organized crime.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　承担有组织犯罪预防和治理职责的部门应当开展反有组织犯罪宣传教育，增强公民的反有组织犯罪意识和能力。  
监察机关、人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关、司法行政机关应当通过普法宣传、以案释法等方式，开展反有组织犯罪宣传教育。  
新闻、广播、电视、文化、互联网信息服务等单位，应当有针对性地面向社会开展反有组织犯罪宣传教育。

**Article 10** Departments undertaking the duty of preventing and governing organized crime shall conduct publicity and education against organized crime to enhance citizens' awareness and capability against organized crime.  
Supervisory organs, people's courts, people's procuratorates, public security organs, and judicial administrative organs shall conduct publicity and education against organized crime through legal publicity, case interpretation, and other means.  
Units engaged in news, broadcasting, television, culture, and internet information services shall, in a targeted manner, conduct publicity and education against organized crime for the public.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　教育行政部门、学校应当会同有关部门建立防范有组织犯罪侵害校园工作机制，加强反有组织犯罪宣传教育，增强学生防范有组织犯罪的意识，教育引导学生自觉抵制有组织犯罪，防范有组织犯罪的侵害。  
学校发现有组织犯罪侵害学生人身、财产安全，妨害校园及周边秩序的，有组织犯罪组织在学生中发展成员的，或者学生参加有组织犯罪活动的，应当及时制止，采取防范措施，并向公安机关和教育行政部门报告。

**Article 11** The administrative department for education and schools shall, in conjunction with relevant departments, establish a work mechanism to prevent organized crime from infringing on campuses, strengthen publicity and education against organized crime, enhance students' awareness of preventing organized crime, and educate and guide students to consciously resist organized crime and prevent infringement by organized crime.  
Where a school discovers that organized crime infringes on the personal or property safety of students, disrupts the order on campus or in its vicinity, or that an organized crime organization recruits members among students, or that students participate in organized criminal activities, the school shall promptly stop it, take preventive measures, and report to the public security organ and the administrative department for education.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　民政部门应当会同监察机关、公安机关等有关部门，对村民委员会、居民委员会成员候选人资格进行审查，发现因实施有组织犯罪受过刑事处罚的，应当依照有关规定及时作出处理；发现有组织犯罪线索的，应当及时向公安机关报告。

**Article 12** The civil affairs department shall, in conjunction with supervisory organs, public security organs, and other relevant departments, examine the qualifications of candidates for members of villagers' committees and residents' committees. Where it is discovered that a person has received criminal punishment for committing organized crime, the matter shall be dealt with promptly in accordance with relevant regulations; where clues of organized crime are discovered, they shall be reported to the public security organ in a timely manner.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　市场监管、金融监管、自然资源、交通运输等行业主管部门应当会同公安机关，建立健全行业有组织犯罪预防和治理长效机制，对相关行业领域内有组织犯罪情况进行监测分析，对有组织犯罪易发的行业领域加强监督管理。

**Article 13** The competent departments for market regulation, financial supervision, natural resources, transportation, and other industries shall, in conjunction with public security organs, establish a long-term mechanism for the prevention and governance of organized crime in their respective industries, monitor and analyze the situation of organized crime in relevant industries and sectors, and strengthen supervision and management over industries and sectors prone to organized crime.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　监察机关、人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关在办理案件中发现行业主管部门有组织犯罪预防和治理工作存在问题的，可以书面向相关行业主管部门提出意见建议。相关行业主管部门应当及时处理并书面反馈。

**Article 14** Where, in the course of handling cases, supervisory organs, people's courts, people's procuratorates, and public security organs discover problems in the prevention and governance of organized crime by competent industry departments, they may put forward opinions and suggestions in writing to the relevant competent industry departments. The relevant competent industry departments shall promptly deal with the matters and provide written feedback.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　公安机关可以会同有关部门根据本地有组织犯罪情况，确定预防和治理的重点区域、行业领域或者场所。  
重点区域、行业领域或者场所的管理单位应当采取有效措施，加强管理，并及时将工作情况向公安机关反馈。

**Article 15** Public security organs may, in conjunction with relevant departments, determine key areas, industrial sectors, or venues for prevention and governance based on the local situation of organized crime.  
The management units of key areas, industrial sectors, or venues shall take effective measures to strengthen management and promptly provide feedback on the work to the public security organs.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　电信业务经营者、互联网服务提供者应当依法履行网络信息安全管理义务，采取安全技术防范措施，防止含有宣扬、诱导有组织犯罪内容的信息传播；发现含有宣扬、诱导有组织犯罪内容的信息的，应当立即停止传输，采取消除等处置措施，保存相关记录，并向公安机关或者有关部门报告，依法为公安机关侦查有组织犯罪提供技术支持和协助。  
网信、电信、公安等主管部门对含有宣扬、诱导有组织犯罪内容的信息，应当按照职责分工，及时责令有关单位停止传输、采取消除等处置措施，或者下架相关应用、关闭相关网站、关停相关服务。有关单位应当立即执行，并保存相关记录，协助调查。对互联网上来源于境外的上述信息，电信主管部门应当采取技术措施，及时阻断传播。

**Article 16** Telecommunications business operators and internet service providers shall, in accordance with law, fulfill the obligations of network information security management, adopt security technical protection measures to prevent the dissemination of information containing content that promotes or induces organized crime; where information containing content that promotes or induces organized crime is discovered, they shall immediately cease transmission, take disposal measures such as removal, preserve relevant records, and report to the public security organ or relevant department, and provide technical support and assistance for the public security organ's investigation of organized crime in accordance with law.  
The competent departments for cyberspace affairs, telecommunications, and public security shall, according to their division of duties, promptly order relevant units to cease transmission, take disposal measures such as removal, or remove relevant applications, close relevant websites, or shut down relevant services with respect to information containing content that promotes or induces organized crime. The relevant units shall immediately execute such orders, preserve relevant records, and assist in the investigation. For the above-mentioned information originating from abroad on the internet, the competent telecommunications department shall adopt technical measures to promptly block its dissemination.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院反洗钱行政主管部门、国务院其他有关部门、机构应当督促金融机构和特定非金融机构履行反洗钱义务。发现与有组织犯罪有关的可疑交易活动的，有关主管部门可以依法进行调查，经调查不能排除洗钱嫌疑的，应当及时向公安机关报案。

**Article 17** The anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council and other relevant departments and institutions of the State Council shall urge financial institutions and specific non-financial institutions to fulfill their anti-money laundering obligations. Where suspicious transaction activities related to organized crime are discovered, the relevant competent departments may conduct investigations in accordance with law; if the suspicion of money laundering cannot be ruled out after investigation, they shall promptly report to the public security organ.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　监狱、看守所、社区矫正机构对有组织犯罪的罪犯，应当采取有针对性的监管、教育、矫正措施。  
有组织犯罪的罪犯刑满释放后，司法行政机关应当会同有关部门落实安置帮教等必要措施，促进其顺利融入社会。

**Article 18** Prisons, detention centers, and community correction institutions shall adopt targeted supervision, education, and correction measures for offenders of organized crime.  
After offenders of organized crime are released after serving their sentences, the judicial administrative organs shall, in conjunction with relevant departments, implement necessary measures such as resettlement and assistance to facilitate their smooth integration into society.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对因组织、领导黑社会性质组织被判处刑罚的人员，设区的市级以上公安机关可以决定其自刑罚执行完毕之日起，按照国家有关规定向公安机关报告个人财产及日常活动。报告期限不超过五年。

**Article 19** For persons sentenced to punishment for organizing or leading a mafia-type organization, the public security organ at or above the level of a city divided into districts may decide that such persons shall, from the date of completion of the execution of the punishment, report their personal property and daily activities to the public security organ in accordance with relevant state regulations. The reporting period shall not exceed five years.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　曾被判处刑罚的黑社会性质组织的组织者、领导者或者恶势力组织的首要分子开办企业或者在企业中担任高级管理人员的，相关行业主管部门应当依法审查，对其经营活动加强监督管理。

**Article 20** Where a person who has been sentenced to punishment as an organizer or leader of a mafia-type organization or a principal member of an evil force organization establishes an enterprise or serves as a senior officer in an enterprise, the relevant competent industry department shall examine it in accordance with law and strengthen supervision and management over its business activities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　移民管理、海关、海警等部门应当会同公安机关严密防范境外的黑社会组织入境渗透、发展、实施违法犯罪活动。  
出入境证件签发机关、移民管理机构对境外的黑社会组织的人员，有权决定不准其入境、不予签发入境证件或者宣布其入境证件作废。  
移民管理、海关、海警等部门发现境外的黑社会组织的人员入境的，应当及时通知公安机关。发现相关人员涉嫌违反我国法律或者发现涉嫌有组织犯罪物品的，应当依法扣留并及时处理。

**Article 21** The immigration administration, customs, coast guard, and other departments shall, jointly with the public security organs, rigorously prevent overseas underworld organizations from infiltrating, developing, and carrying out illegal and criminal activities within the country.  
The authorities issuing entry and exit documents and the immigration administration organs shall have the authority to decide not to permit the entry of personnel of overseas underworld organizations, to refuse to issue entry documents to them, or to declare their entry documents invalid.  
Where the immigration administration, customs, coast guard, and other departments discover that personnel of overseas underworld organizations have entered the country, they shall promptly notify the public security organs. Where they discover that such personnel are suspected of violating Chinese laws or that items suspected of being used in organized crime are involved, they shall detain such items according to law and handle them promptly.

### 第三章　案件办理

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　办理有组织犯罪案件，应当以事实为根据，以法律为准绳，坚持宽严相济。  
对有组织犯罪的组织者、领导者和骨干成员，应当严格掌握取保候审、不起诉、缓刑、减刑、假释和暂予监外执行的适用条件，充分适用剥夺政治权利、没收财产、罚金等刑罚。  
有组织犯罪的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人自愿如实供述自己的罪行，承认指控的犯罪事实，愿意接受处罚的，可以依法从宽处理。

**Article 22** In handling organized crime cases, facts shall be taken as the basis and the law as the criterion, and the principle of combining leniency with severity shall be adhered to.  
For the organizers, leaders, and key members of an organized crime group, the conditions for applying release on bail pending investigation or trial, non-prosecution, probation, commutation of punishment, parole, and temporary service of sentence outside prison shall be strictly controlled, and penalties such as deprivation of political rights, confiscation of property, and fines shall be fully applied.  
Where a criminal suspect or defendant in an organized crime case voluntarily and truthfully confesses his or her crimes, admits the criminal facts charged, and is willing to accept punishment, he or she may be dealt with leniently according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　利用网络实施的犯罪，符合[本法第二条](#art-2)规定的，应当认定为有组织犯罪。  
为谋取非法利益或者形成非法影响，有组织地进行滋扰、纠缠、哄闹、聚众造势等，对他人形成心理强制，足以限制人身自由、危及人身财产安全，影响正常社会秩序、经济秩序的，可以认定为有组织犯罪的犯罪手段。

**Article 23** Crimes committed by using the internet that meet the provisions of Article 2 of this Law shall be determined as organized crime.  
Where, for the purpose of obtaining illegal benefits or creating illegal influence, organized acts such as harassment, pestering, clamoring, or gathering people to create pressure are carried out, causing psychological coercion upon others, to the extent of restricting personal liberty, endangering personal safety and property, and affecting normal social order and economic order, such acts may be determined as criminal means of organized crime.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　公安机关应当依法运用现代信息技术，建立有组织犯罪线索收集和研判机制，分级分类进行处置。  
公安机关接到对有组织犯罪的报案、控告、举报后，应当及时开展统计、分析、研判工作，组织核查或者移送有关主管机关依法处理。

**Article 24** The public security organs shall, according to law, utilize modern information technology to establish a mechanism for collecting and analyzing clues on organized crime, and handle them by level and category.  
After receiving reports, complaints, or tip-offs regarding organized crime, the public security organs shall promptly carry out statistics, analysis, and evaluation, and organize verification or transfer the matter to the competent authority for handling according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　有关国家机关在履行职责时发现有组织犯罪线索，或者接到对有组织犯罪的举报的，应当及时移送公安机关等主管机关依法处理。

**Article 25** Where a relevant state organ discovers clues of organized crime while performing its duties, or receives a tip-off regarding organized crime, it shall promptly transfer the matter to the public security organs or other competent authorities for handling according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　公安机关核查有组织犯罪线索，可以按照国家有关规定采取调查措施。公安机关向有关单位和个人收集、调取相关信息和材料的，有关单位和个人应当如实提供。

**Article 26** When verifying clues of organized crime, the public security organs may adopt investigation measures in accordance with the relevant state provisions. Where the public security organs collect or obtain relevant information and materials from the relevant entities or individuals, such entities or individuals shall truthfully provide them.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　公安机关核查有组织犯罪线索，经县级以上公安机关负责人批准，可以查询嫌疑人员的存款、汇款、债券、股票、基金份额等财产信息。  
公安机关核查黑社会性质组织犯罪线索，发现涉案财产有灭失、转移的紧急风险的，经设区的市级以上公安机关负责人批准，可以对有关涉案财产采取紧急止付或者临时冻结、临时扣押的紧急措施，期限不得超过四十八小时。期限届满或者适用紧急措施的情形消失的，应当立即解除紧急措施。

**Article 27** When verifying clues of organized crime, with the approval of the person in charge of the public security organ at or above the county level, the public security organs may inquire about property information such as deposits, remittances, bonds, stocks, and fund shares of the suspected persons.  
Where, during the verification of clues of crimes committed by underworld organizations, the public security organs discover an imminent risk of loss or transfer of the property involved in the case, with the approval of the person in charge of the public security organ at or above the city with districts level, they may take emergency measures such as emergency payment suspension, temporary freezing, or temporary seizure of the property involved. The duration of such measures shall not exceed forty-eight hours. Upon the expiration of the period or when the circumstances justifying the emergency measures cease to exist, the emergency measures shall be immediately lifted.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　公安机关核查有组织犯罪线索，发现犯罪事实或者犯罪嫌疑人的，应当依照《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的规定立案侦查。

**Article 28** Where, during the verification of clues of organized crime, the public security organs discover criminal facts or criminal suspects, they shall initiate an investigation according to the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　公安机关办理有组织犯罪案件，可以依照《中华人民共和国出境入境管理法》的规定，决定对犯罪嫌疑人采取限制出境措施，通知移民管理机构执行。

**Article 29** When handling organized crime cases, the public security organs may, in accordance with the provisions of the Exit and Entry Administration Law of the People's Republic of China, decide to impose measures restricting the departure of criminal suspects and notify the immigration administration authorities to enforce such measures.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对有组织犯罪案件的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人，根据办理案件和维护监管秩序的需要，可以采取异地羁押、分别羁押或者单独羁押等措施。采取异地羁押措施的，应当依法通知犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的家属和辩护人。

**Article 30** For criminal suspects or defendants in organized crime cases, measures such as custodial detention at a different place, separate detention, or solitary detention may be adopted as needed for case handling and maintaining supervision order. Where custodial detention at a different place is adopted, the family members and defenders of the criminal suspect or defendant shall be notified according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　公安机关在立案后，根据侦查犯罪的需要，依照《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的规定，可以采取技术侦查措施、实施控制下交付或者由有关人员隐匿身份进行侦查。

**Article 31** After initiating a case, the public security organs, based on the needs of investigating the crime and in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, may adopt technical investigation measures, carry out controlled delivery, or conduct investigations with the identity of relevant personnel concealed.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　犯罪嫌疑人、被告人检举、揭发重大犯罪的其他共同犯罪人或者提供侦破重大案件的重要线索或者证据，同案处理可能导致其本人或者近亲属有人身危险的，可以分案处理。

**Article 32** Where a criminal suspect or defendant reports or exposes other joint offenders in a major crime, or provides important leads or evidence for the detection of a major case, and handling the case together would pose a personal safety risk to such person or his or her close relatives, the case may be handled separately.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　犯罪嫌疑人、被告人积极配合有组织犯罪案件的侦查、起诉、审判等工作，有下列情形之一的，可以依法从宽处罚，但对有组织犯罪的组织者、领导者应当严格适用：  
（一）为查明犯罪组织的组织结构及其组织者、领导者、首要分子的地位、作用提供重要线索或者证据的；  
（二）为查明犯罪组织实施的重大犯罪提供重要线索或者证据的；  
（三）为查处国家工作人员涉有组织犯罪提供重要线索或者证据的；  
（四）协助追缴、没收尚未掌握的赃款赃物的；  
（五）其他为查办有组织犯罪案件提供重要线索或者证据的情形。  
对参加有组织犯罪组织的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人不起诉或者免予刑事处罚的，可以根据案件的不同情况，依法予以训诫、责令具结悔过、赔礼道歉、赔偿损失，或者由主管部门予以行政处罚或者处分。

**Article 33** Where a criminal suspect or defendant actively cooperates with the investigation, prosecution, adjudication, or other work of an organized crime case, and falls under any of the following circumstances, he or she may be given a lenient punishment according to law, provided that strict application shall be made to the organizers and leaders of the organized crime group:  
(1) providing important leads or evidence for ascertaining the organizational structure of the criminal organization and the status and role of its organizers, leaders, and principal offenders;  
(2) providing important leads or evidence for ascertaining the major crimes committed by the criminal organization;  
(3) providing important leads or evidence for investigating state functionaries involved in organized crime;  
(4) assisting in the recovery and confiscation of stolen money or goods not yet seized; or  
(5) other circumstances where important leads or evidence are provided for handling organized crime cases.  
Where a criminal suspect or defendant who participated in an organized crime group is not prosecuted or is exempted from criminal punishment, depending on the circumstances of the case, he or she may be given a reprimand, ordered to make a statement of repentance, offer an apology, make compensation for losses, or be subject to administrative penalty or sanction by the competent authority according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　对黑社会性质组织的组织者、领导者，应当依法并处没收财产。对其他组织成员，根据其在犯罪组织中的地位、作用以及所参与违法犯罪活动的次数、性质、违法所得数额、造成的损失等，可以依法并处罚金或者没收财产。

**Article 34** For the organizers and leaders of an underworld organization, confiscation of property shall be imposed concurrently according to law. For other members of the organization, depending on their status and role in the criminal organization, as well as the frequency, nature, amount of illegal gains, and losses caused by the illegal and criminal activities they participated in, a fine or confiscation of property may be imposed concurrently according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　对有组织犯罪的罪犯，执行机关应当依法从严管理。  
黑社会性质组织的组织者、领导者或者恶势力组织的首要分子被判处十年以上有期徒刑、无期徒刑、死刑缓期二年执行的，应当跨省、自治区、直辖市异地执行刑罚。

**Article 35** For criminals convicted of organized crime, the enforcement authorities shall strictly manage them according to law.  
Where an organizer or leader of an underworld organization, or a principal offender of an evil force organization is sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years, life imprisonment, or death penalty with a two-year reprieve, the sentence shall be executed at a different place across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　对被判处十年以上有期徒刑、无期徒刑、死刑缓期二年执行的黑社会性质组织的组织者、领导者或者恶势力组织的首要分子减刑的，执行机关应当依法提出减刑建议，报经省、自治区、直辖市监狱管理机关复核后，提请人民法院裁定。  
对黑社会性质组织的组织者、领导者或者恶势力组织的首要分子假释的，适用前款规定的程序。

**Article 36** Where commutation of punishment is to be applied to an organizer or leader of an underworld organization, or a principal offender of an evil force organization sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years, life imprisonment, or death penalty with a two-year reprieve, the enforcement authority shall, according to law, submit a proposal for commutation, which shall be reviewed by the prison administration authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and then submitted to the People's Court for a ruling.  
Where parole is to be granted to an organizer or leader of an underworld organization, or a principal offender of an evil force organization, the procedure set forth in the preceding paragraph shall apply.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　人民法院审理黑社会性质组织犯罪罪犯的减刑、假释案件，应当通知人民检察院、执行机关参加审理，并通知被报请减刑、假释的罪犯参加，听取其意见。

**Article 37** When a People's Court hears a case concerning commutation of punishment or parole for a criminal convicted of an underworld organization crime, it shall notify the People's Procuratorate and the enforcement authority to participate in the hearing, and shall also notify the criminal for whom commutation or parole is sought to participate and hear his or her opinions.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　执行机关提出减刑、假释建议以及人民法院审理减刑、假释案件，应当充分考虑罪犯履行生效裁判中财产性判项、配合处置涉案财产等情况。

**Article 38** When an enforcement authority submits a proposal for commutation or parole, and when a People's Court hears a case concerning commutation or parole, full consideration shall be given to whether the criminal has performed the property-related judgments in the effective judgment and has cooperated in the disposal of the property involved in the case.

### 第四章　涉案财产认定和处置

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　办理有组织犯罪案件中发现的可用以证明犯罪嫌疑人、被告人有罪或者无罪的各种财物、文件，应当依法查封、扣押。  
公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院可以依照《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的规定查询、冻结犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的存款、汇款、债券、股票、基金份额等财产。有关单位和个人应当配合。

**Article 39** Various properties and documents discovered during the handling of organized crime cases that may be used to prove the guilt or innocence of a criminal suspect or defendant shall be sealed or seized according to law.  
The public security organs, People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts may, in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, inquire about and freeze properties such as deposits, remittances, bonds, stocks, and fund shares of criminal suspects or defendants. The relevant entities and individuals shall cooperate.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院根据办理有组织犯罪案件的需要，可以全面调查涉嫌有组织犯罪的组织及其成员的财产状况。

**Article 40** The public security organs, People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts may, based on the needs of handling organized crime cases, conduct a comprehensive investigation into the property status of the organization suspected of organized crime and its members.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　查封、扣押、冻结、处置涉案财物，应当严格依照法定条件和程序进行，依法保护公民和组织的合法财产权益，严格区分违法所得与合法财产、本人财产与其家属的财产，减少对企业正常经营活动的不利影响。不得查封、扣押、冻结与案件无关的财物。经查明确实与案件无关的财物，应当在三日以内解除查封、扣押、冻结，予以退还。对被害人的合法财产，应当及时返还。  
查封、扣押、冻结涉案财物，应当为犯罪嫌疑人、被告人及其扶养的家属保留必需的生活费用和物品。

**Article 41** : Sealing, seizing, freezing, and disposing of property involved in a case shall be conducted in strict accordance with legal conditions and procedures, protecting the lawful property rights and interests of citizens and organizations according to law, strictly distinguishing between illegal gains and lawful property, and between the property of the person and that of his or her family members, so as to reduce adverse impacts on the normal business activities of enterprises. Property unrelated to the case shall not be sealed, seized, or frozen. Where property is verified to be truly unrelated to the case, the sealing, seizure, or freezing shall be lifted within three days, and the property shall be returned. Lawful property of the victim shall be promptly returned.  
When sealing, seizing, or freezing property involved in a case, necessary living expenses and articles shall be reserved for the criminal suspect, defendant, and their dependents.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　公安机关可以向反洗钱行政主管部门查询与有组织犯罪相关的信息数据，提请协查与有组织犯罪相关的可疑交易活动，反洗钱行政主管部门应当予以配合并及时回复。

**Article 42** : Public security organs may inquire with the anti-money laundering administrative department about information and data related to organized crime, and may request assistance in investigating suspicious transaction activities related to organized crime; the anti-money laundering administrative department shall cooperate and provide a timely response.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　对下列财产，经县级以上公安机关、人民检察院或者人民法院主要负责人批准，可以依法先行出售、变现或者变卖、拍卖，所得价款由扣押、冻结机关保管，并及时告知犯罪嫌疑人、被告人或者其近亲属：  
（一）易损毁、灭失、变质等不宜长期保存的物品；  
（二）有效期即将届满的汇票、本票、支票等；  
（三）债券、股票、基金份额等财产，经权利人申请，出售不损害国家利益、被害人利益，不影响诉讼正常进行的。

**Article 43** : For the following property, with the approval of the principal responsible person of the public security organ, people's procuratorate, or people's court at or above the county level, it may be sold, realized, or disposed of by sale or auction in advance according to law, and the proceeds shall be kept by the organ that seized or froze the property, and the criminal suspect, defendant, or his or her close relative shall be promptly notified:  
(1) Articles that are perishable, easily damaged, liable to deteriorate, or otherwise unsuitable for long-term storage;  
(2) Drafts, promissory notes, checks, etc., whose validity is about to expire;  
(3) Bonds, stocks, fund shares, and other property, where the sale, upon application by the right holder, does not harm national interests or the interests of the victim, and does not affect the normal progress of the proceedings.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　公安机关、人民检察院应当对涉案财产审查甄别。在移送审查起诉、提起公诉时，应当对涉案财产提出处理意见。  
在审理有组织犯罪案件过程中，应当对与涉案财产的性质、权属有关的事实、证据进行法庭调查、辩论。人民法院应当依法作出判决，对涉案财产作出处理。

**Article 44** : Public security organs and people's procuratorates shall examine and screen the property involved in a case. When transferring the case for review and prosecution or initiating a public prosecution, they shall provide opinions on the handling of the property involved.  
During the adjudication of an organized crime case, facts and evidence relating to the nature and ownership of the property involved shall be subject to courtroom investigation and debate. The people's court shall render a judgment according to law and dispose of the property involved.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　有组织犯罪组织及其成员违法所得的一切财物及其孳息、收益，违禁品和供犯罪所用的本人财物，应当依法予以追缴、没收或者责令退赔。  
依法应当追缴、没收的涉案财产无法找到、灭失或者与其他合法财产混合且不可分割的，可以追缴、没收其他等值财产或者混合财产中的等值部分。  
被告人实施黑社会性质组织犯罪的定罪量刑事实已经查清，有证据证明其在犯罪期间获得的财产高度可能属于黑社会性质组织犯罪的违法所得及其孳息、收益，被告人不能说明财产合法来源的，应当依法予以追缴、没收。

**Article 45** : All property illegally obtained by an organized crime organization and its members, as well as its fruits and proceeds, contraband, and the offender's own property used for the crime, shall be recovered, confiscated, or ordered to be returned or compensated according to law.  
Where the property involved that shall be recovered or confiscated according to law cannot be found, has been destroyed, or is mixed with other lawful property and cannot be separated, other property of equivalent value or the equivalent portion of the mixed property may be recovered or confiscated.  
Where the facts concerning the conviction and sentencing of a defendant for committing a crime of a mafia-style organization have been established, and there is evidence showing that the property acquired by the defendant during the criminal period is highly likely to be the illegal gains and their fruits and proceeds from the mafia-style organization crime, if the defendant cannot explain the lawful source of the property, it shall be recovered or confiscated according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　涉案财产符合下列情形之一的，应当依法予以追缴、没收：  
（一）为支持或者资助有组织犯罪活动而提供给有组织犯罪组织及其成员的财产；  
（二）有组织犯罪组织成员的家庭财产中实际用于支持有组织犯罪活动的部分；  
（三）利用有组织犯罪组织及其成员的违法犯罪活动获得的财产及其孳息、收益。

**Article 46** : Property involved in a case that falls under any of the following circumstances shall be recovered or confiscated according to law:  
(1) Property provided to an organized crime organization or its members to support or finance organized criminal activities;  
(2) The portion of family property of a member of an organized crime organization that is actually used to support organized criminal activities;  
(3) Property and its fruits and proceeds obtained by using the illegal or criminal activities of an organized crime organization or its members.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　黑社会性质组织犯罪案件的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人逃匿，在通缉一年后不能到案，或者犯罪嫌疑人、被告人死亡，依照《中华人民共和国刑法》规定应当追缴其违法所得及其他涉案财产的，依照《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》有关犯罪嫌疑人、被告人逃匿、死亡案件违法所得的没收程序的规定办理。

**Article 47** : Where a criminal suspect or defendant in a mafia-style organization crime case absconds and cannot be brought to justice after being wanted for one year, or where the criminal suspect or defendant dies, and it is necessary to recover their illegal gains and other property involved in the case in accordance with the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China concerning the procedure for confiscating illegal gains in cases where the criminal suspect or defendant absconds or dies.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　监察机关、公安机关、人民检察院发现与有组织犯罪相关的洗钱以及掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益等犯罪的，应当依法查处。

**Article 48** : Where supervisory organs, public security organs, or people's procuratorates discover money laundering and crimes of concealing or disguising criminal proceeds or proceeds from criminal activities related to organized crime, they shall investigate and handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　利害关系人对查封、扣押、冻结、处置涉案财物提出异议的，公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院应当及时予以核实，听取其意见，依法作出处理。  
公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院对涉案财物作出处理后，利害关系人对处理不服的，可以提出申诉或者控告。

**Article 49** : Where an interested party raises an objection to the sealing, seizure, freezing, or disposal of property involved in a case, the public security organ, people's procuratorate, or people's court shall promptly verify the matter, hear the party's opinions, and make a decision according to law.  
After the public security organ, people's procuratorate, or people's court disposes of the property involved, if the interested party is dissatisfied with the decision, the party may file a petition or complaint.

### 第五章　国家工作人员涉有组织犯罪的处理

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家工作人员有下列行为的，应当全面调查，依法作出处理：  
（一）组织、领导、参加有组织犯罪活动的；  
（二）为有组织犯罪组织及其犯罪活动提供帮助的；  
（三）包庇有组织犯罪组织、纵容有组织犯罪活动的；  
（四）在查办有组织犯罪案件工作中失职渎职的；  
（五）利用职权或者职务上的影响干预反有组织犯罪工作的；  
（六）其他涉有组织犯罪的违法犯罪行为。  
国家工作人员组织、领导、参加有组织犯罪的，应当依法从重处罚。

**Article 50** : Where a state functionary commits any of the following acts, a comprehensive investigation shall be conducted, and a decision shall be made according to law:  
(1) Organizing, leading, or participating in organized criminal activities;  
(2) Providing assistance to an organized crime organization or its criminal activities;  
(3) Covering up an organized crime organization or conniving at organized criminal activities;  
(4) Dereliction of duty or malfeasance in the work of investigating and handling organized crime cases;  
(5) Using their authority or the influence of their position to interfere with anti-organized crime work;  
(6) Other illegal or criminal acts involving organized crime.  
Where a state functionary organizes, leads, or participates in organized crime, he or she shall be given a severe punishment according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　监察机关、人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关、司法行政机关应当加强协作配合，建立线索办理沟通机制，发现国家工作人员涉嫌[本法第五十条](#art-50)规定的违法犯罪的线索，应当依法处理或者及时移送主管机关处理。  
任何单位和个人发现国家工作人员与有组织犯罪有关的违法犯罪行为，有权向监察机关、人民检察院、公安机关等部门报案、控告、举报。有关部门接到报案、控告、举报后，应当及时处理。

**Article 51** : Supervisory organs, people's courts, people's procuratorates, public security organs, and judicial administrative organs shall strengthen collaboration and cooperation, establish a communication mechanism for handling leads, and where they discover leads concerning a state functionary suspected of committing illegal or criminal acts as specified in Article 50 of this Law, they shall handle the matter according to law or promptly transfer it to the competent authority for handling.  
Any organization or individual that discovers a state functionary committing illegal or criminal acts related to organized crime shall have the right to report, accuse, or inform the supervisory organ, people's procuratorate, public security organ, or other relevant departments. The relevant department, upon receiving such a report, accusation, or information, shall promptly handle the matter.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　依法查办有组织犯罪案件或者依照职责支持、协助查办有组织犯罪案件的国家工作人员，不得有下列行为：  
（一）接到报案、控告、举报不受理，发现犯罪信息、线索隐瞒不报、不如实报告，或者未经批准、授权擅自处置、不移送犯罪线索、涉案材料；  
（二）向违法犯罪人员通风报信，阻碍案件查处；  
（三）违背事实和法律处理案件；  
（四）违反规定查封、扣押、冻结、处置涉案财物；  
（五）其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的行为。

**Article 52** : State functionaries who investigate and handle organized crime cases according to law, or who support or assist in investigating and handling such cases according to their duties, shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) Failing to accept reports, accusations, or information; concealing or not truthfully reporting criminal information or leads; or without approval or authorization, disposing of or failing to transfer criminal leads or case-related materials;  
(2) Leaking information to illegal or criminal persons, impeding the investigation and handling of the case;  
(3) Handling the case contrary to facts and law;  
(4) Sealing, seizing, freezing, or disposing of property involved in the case in violation of regulations;  
(5) Other acts of abusing power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　有关机关接到对从事反有组织犯罪工作的执法、司法工作人员的举报后，应当依法处理，防止犯罪嫌疑人、被告人等利用举报干扰办案、打击报复。  
对利用举报等方式歪曲捏造事实，诬告陷害从事反有组织犯罪工作的执法、司法工作人员的，应当依法追究责任；造成不良影响的，应当按照规定及时澄清事实，恢复名誉，消除不良影响。

**Article 53** : After receiving a report concerning law enforcement or judicial personnel engaged in anti-organized crime work, the relevant authority shall handle it according to law, and prevent criminal suspects, defendants, or others from using such reports to interfere with case handling or retaliate.  
Where a person distorts or fabricates facts by means of reports or otherwise, or falsely accuses law enforcement or judicial personnel engaged in anti-organized crime work with the intent to frame them, legal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it causes adverse effects, the facts shall be promptly clarified in accordance with regulations, the reputation restored, and the adverse effects eliminated.

### 第六章　国际合作

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　中华人民共和国根据缔结或者参加的国际条约，或者按照平等互惠原则，与其他国家、地区、国际组织开展反有组织犯罪合作。

**Article 54** : The People's Republic of China shall, in accordance with international treaties concluded or acceded to, or on the principle of equality and mutual benefit, conduct anti-organized crime cooperation with other countries, regions, and international organizations.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国务院有关部门根据国务院授权，代表中国政府与外国政府和有关国际组织开展反有组织犯罪情报信息交流和执法合作。  
国务院公安部门应当加强跨境反有组织犯罪警务合作，推动与有关国家和地区建立警务合作机制。经国务院公安部门批准，边境地区公安机关可以与相邻国家或者地区执法机构建立跨境有组织犯罪情报信息交流和警务合作机制。

**Article 55** : The relevant departments of the State Council shall, as authorized by the State Council, on behalf of the Chinese government, conduct exchange of intelligence and information and law enforcement cooperation with foreign governments and relevant international organizations for anti-organized crime purposes.  
The public security department of the State Council shall strengthen cross-border police cooperation against organized crime and promote the establishment of police cooperation mechanisms with relevant countries and regions. With the approval of the public security department of the State Council, public security organs in border areas may establish cross-border exchange of intelligence and information on organized crime and police cooperation mechanisms with law enforcement agencies of neighboring countries or regions.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　涉及有组织犯罪的刑事司法协助、引渡，依照有关法律的规定办理。

**Article 56** : Criminal judicial assistance and extradition involving organized crime shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　通过反有组织犯罪国际合作取得的材料可以在行政处罚、刑事诉讼中作为证据使用，但依据条约规定或者我方承诺不作为证据使用的除外。

**Article 57** : Materials obtained through international cooperation against organized crime may be used as evidence in administrative penalties and criminal proceedings, except where, according to treaty provisions or commitments made by our side, they shall not be used as evidence.

### 第七章　保障措施

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国家为反有组织犯罪工作提供必要的组织保障、制度保障和物质保障。

**Article 58** : The State shall provide necessary organizational, institutional, and material guarantees for anti-organized crime work.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　公安机关和有关部门应当依照职责，建立健全反有组织犯罪专业力量，加强人才队伍建设和专业训练，提升反有组织犯罪工作能力。

**Article 59** : Public security organs and relevant departments shall, in accordance with their duties, establish and improve specialized anti-organized crime forces, strengthen talent development and professional training, and enhance the capability for anti-organized crime work.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国务院和县级以上地方各级人民政府应当按照事权划分，将反有组织犯罪工作经费列入本级财政预算。

**Article 60** : The State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the division of responsibilities, include the funds for anti-organized crime work in their respective fiscal budgets.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　因举报、控告和制止有组织犯罪活动，在有组织犯罪案件中作证，本人或者其近亲属的人身安全面临危险的，公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院应当按照有关规定，采取下列一项或者多项保护措施：  
（一）不公开真实姓名、住址和工作单位等个人信息；  
（二）采取不暴露外貌、真实声音等出庭作证措施；  
（三）禁止特定的人接触被保护人员；  
（四）对人身和住宅采取专门性保护措施；  
（五）变更被保护人员的身份，重新安排住所和工作单位；  
（六）其他必要的保护措施。

**Article 61** Where, due to reporting, accusing, and stopping organized crime activities, or testifying in organized crime cases, the personal safety of the person or his or her close relatives is endangered, the public security organ, the People's Procuratorate, and the People's Court shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, take one or more of the following protection measures:  
(1) not disclosing personal information such as real name, address, and work unit;  
(2) taking measures for testifying in court such as not revealing appearance or real voice;  
(3) prohibiting specific persons from contacting the protected persons;  
(4) taking special protection measures for personal safety and residence;  
(5) changing the identity of the protected person and reassigning residence and work unit;  
(6) other necessary protection measures.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　采取[本法第六十一条](#art-61)第三项、第四项规定的保护措施，由公安机关执行。根据[本法第六十一条](#art-61)第五项规定，变更被保护人员身份的，由国务院公安部门批准和组织实施。  
公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院依法采取保护措施，有关单位和个人应当配合。

**Article 62** The protection measures provided in Items (3) and (4) of Article 61 of this Law shall be executed by the public security organ. Where the identity of the protected person is changed pursuant to Item (5) of Article 61 of this Law, it shall be approved and organized by the public security department of the State Council.  
Where the public security organ, the People's Procuratorate, and the People's Court take protection measures according to law, relevant entities and individuals shall cooperate.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　实施有组织犯罪的人员配合侦查、起诉、审判等工作，对侦破案件或者查明案件事实起到重要作用的，可以参照证人保护的规定执行。

**Article 63** Where a person who commits organized crime cooperates with investigation, prosecution, adjudication, and other work and plays an important role in detecting the case or ascertaining the facts of the case, the provisions on witness protection may be applied by reference.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　对办理有组织犯罪案件的执法、司法工作人员及其近亲属，可以采取人身保护、禁止特定的人接触等保护措施。

**Article 64** For law enforcement and judicial personnel handling organized crime cases and their close relatives, protection measures such as personal safety protection and prohibition of specific persons from contacting may be taken.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　对因履行反有组织犯罪工作职责或者协助、配合有关部门开展反有组织犯罪工作导致伤残或者死亡的人员，按照国家有关规定给予相应的待遇。

**Article 65** Persons who are disabled or die due to performing duties in anti-organized crime work or assisting or cooperating with relevant departments in carrying out anti-organized crime work shall be given corresponding benefits in accordance with relevant state provisions.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　组织、领导、参加黑社会性质组织，国家机关工作人员包庇、纵容黑社会性质组织，以及黑社会性质组织、恶势力组织实施犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
境外的黑社会组织的人员到中华人民共和国境内发展组织成员、实施犯罪，以及在境外对中华人民共和国国家或者公民犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 66** Organizing, leading, or participating in mafia-type organizations; state functionaries harboring or conniving at mafia-type organizations; and mafia-type organizations or criminal groups of a villain nature committing crimes shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.  
Personnel of overseas mafia-type organizations who develop organization members or commit crimes within the territory of the People's Republic of China, or who commit crimes against the state or citizens of the People's Republic of China outside the territory, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　发展未成年人参加黑社会性质组织、境外的黑社会组织，教唆、诱骗未成年人实施有组织犯罪，或者实施有组织犯罪侵害未成年人合法权益的，依法从重追究刑事责任。

**Article 67** Whoever recruits minors to participate in mafia-type organizations or overseas mafia-type organizations, instigates or lures minors to commit organized crime, or commits organized crime that infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of minors, shall be given a severe punishment in investigation of criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　对有组织犯罪的罪犯，人民法院可以依照《中华人民共和国刑法》有关从业禁止的规定，禁止其从事相关职业，并通报相关行业主管部门。

**Article 68** For convicts of organized crime, the People's Court may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China on prohibition of employment, prohibit them from engaging in relevant professions and notify the relevant industry competent departments.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　有下列情形之一，尚不构成犯罪的，由公安机关处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处一万元以下罚款；情节较重的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，并处一万元以上三万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，除依法应当返还被害人的以外，应当予以没收：  
（一）参加境外的黑社会组织的；  
（二）积极参加恶势力组织的；  
（三）教唆、诱骗他人参加有组织犯罪组织，或者阻止他人退出有组织犯罪组织的；  
（四）为有组织犯罪活动提供资金、场所等支持、协助、便利的；  
（五）阻止他人检举揭发有组织犯罪、提供有组织犯罪证据，或者明知他人有有组织犯罪行为，在司法机关向其调查有关情况、收集有关证据时拒绝提供的。  
教唆、诱骗未成年人参加有组织犯罪组织或者阻止未成年人退出有组织犯罪组织，尚不构成犯罪的，依照前款规定从重处罚。

**Article 69** Where any of the following circumstances exists, which does not constitute a crime, the public security organ shall impose a detention of not less than five days but not more than ten days, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than RMB 10,000; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a detention of not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days shall be imposed, together with a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 30,000; if there is illegal income, except for which shall be returned to the victim according to law, it shall be confiscated:  
(1) participating in an overseas mafia-type organization;  
(2) actively participating in a criminal group of a villain nature;  
(3) instigating or luring another person to join an organized crime organization, or preventing another person from withdrawing from an organized crime organization;  
(4) providing support, assistance, or facilitation such as funds or premises for organized crime activities;  
(5) preventing another person from reporting or exposing organized crime or providing evidence of organized crime, or, while knowing that another person has committed organized crime, refusing to provide information when the judicial organ investigates relevant circumstances or collects relevant evidence.  
Where instigating or luring minors to join an organized crime organization or preventing minors from withdrawing from an organized crime organization does not constitute a crime, a severe punishment shall be given in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　违反[本法第十九条](#art-19)规定，不按照公安机关的决定如实报告个人财产及日常活动的，由公安机关给予警告，并责令改正；拒不改正的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，并处三万元以下罚款。

**Article 70** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 19 of this Law by failing to truthfully report personal property and daily activities in accordance with the decision of the public security organ shall be given a warning by the public security organ and be ordered to make corrections; if corrections are refused, a detention of not less than five days but not more than ten days shall be imposed, together with a fine of not more than RMB 30,000.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　金融机构等相关单位未依照[本法第二十七条](#art-27)规定协助公安机关采取紧急止付、临时冻结措施的，由公安机关责令改正；拒不改正的，由公安机关处五万元以上二十万元以下罚款，并对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五万元以下罚款；情节严重的，公安机关可以建议有关主管部门对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 71** Where a financial institution or other relevant entity fails to assist the public security organ in taking emergency payment suspension or temporary freezing measures in accordance with Article 27 of this Law, the public security organ shall order it to make corrections; if corrections are refused, the public security organ shall impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 200,000, and shall impose a fine of not more than RMB 50,000 on the directly responsible supervising officer and other directly responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, the public security organ may suggest that the relevant competent department give sanctions to the directly responsible supervising officer and other directly responsible persons according to law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　电信业务经营者、互联网服务提供者有下列情形之一的，由有关主管部门责令改正；拒不改正或者情节严重的，由有关主管部门依照《中华人民共和国网络安全法》的有关规定给予处罚：  
（一）拒不为侦查有组织犯罪提供技术支持和协助的；  
（二）不按照主管部门的要求对含有宣扬、诱导有组织犯罪内容的信息停止传输、采取消除等处置措施、保存相关记录的。

**Article 72** Where a telecommunications business operator or Internet service provider falls under any of the following circumstances, the relevant competent department shall order it to make corrections; if corrections are refused or the circumstances are serious, the relevant competent department shall impose a penalty in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) refusing to provide technical support and assistance for the investigation of organized crime;  
(2) failing to stop transmission of, take disposal measures such as removal, or preserve relevant records of information containing content promoting or inducing organized crime as required by the competent department.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　有关国家机关、行业主管部门拒不履行或者拖延履行反有组织犯罪法定职责，或者拒不配合反有组织犯罪调查取证，或者在其他工作中滥用反有组织犯罪工作有关措施的，由其上级机关责令改正；情节严重的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 73** Where a relevant state organ or industry competent department refuses to perform or delays the performance of statutory anti-organized crime duties, or refuses to cooperate with anti-organized crime investigation and evidence collection, or abuses measures related to anti-organized crime work in other work, its superior organ shall order it to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, the responsible leading personnel and directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　有关部门和单位、个人应当对在反有组织犯罪工作过程中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私予以保密。违反规定泄露国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私的，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 74** Relevant departments, entities, and individuals shall keep confidential the state secrets, trade secrets, and personal privacy learned during the course of anti-organized crime work. Whoever violates the provisions and discloses state secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy shall be investigated for legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　国家工作人员有[本法第五十条](#art-50)、第五十二条规定的行为，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 75** Where a state functionary commits an act specified in Article 50 or Article 52 of this Law, if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, a sanction shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　有关单位和个人对依照本法作出的行政处罚和行政强制措施决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 76** Where a relevant entity or individual is dissatisfied with a decision on administrative penalty or administrative compulsion made in accordance with this Law, it or he may apply for administrative reconsideration or file an administrative action according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　本法自2022年5月1日起施行。

**Article 77** This Law shall come into force as of May 1, 2022.
