# 中华人民共和国刑法修正案（九）

**分类**：修正案  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2015-08-29  
**生效日期**：2015-11-01

> （2015年8月29日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十六次会议通过） 一、在刑法第三十七条后增加一条，作为第三十七条之一:“因利用职业便利实施犯罪，或者实施违背职业要求的特定义务的犯罪被判处刑罚的，人民法院可以根据犯罪情况和预防再犯罪的需要，禁止其自刑罚执行完毕之日或者假释之日起从事相关职业，期限为三年至五年。 “被禁止从事相关职业的人违反人民法院依照前款规定作出的决定的，由公安机关依法给予处罚；情节严重的，依照本法第三百一十三条的规定定罪处罚。 “其他法律、行政法规对其从事相关职业另有禁止或者限制性规定的，从其规定。” 二、将刑法第五十条第一款修改为:“判处死刑缓期执行的，在死刑缓期执行期间，如果没有故意犯罪，二年期满以后，减为无期徒刑；如果确有重大立功表现，二年期满以后，减为二十五年有期徒刑；如果故意犯罪，情节恶劣的，报请最高人民法院核准后执行死刑；对于故意犯罪未执行死刑的，死刑缓期执行的期间重新计算，并报最高人民法院备案。” 三、将刑法第五十三条修改为:“罚金在判决指定的期限内一次或者分期缴纳。期满不缴纳的，强制缴纳。对于不能全部缴纳罚金的，人民法院在任何时候发现被执行人有可以执行的财产，应当随时追缴。 “由于遭遇不能抗拒的灾祸等原因缴纳确实有困难的，经人民法院裁定，可以延期缴纳、酌情减少或者免除。” 四、在刑法第六十九条中增加一款作为第二款:“数罪中有判处有期徒刑和拘役的，执行有期徒刑。数罪中有判处有期徒刑和管制，或者拘役和管制的，有期徒刑、拘役执行完毕后，管制仍须执行。” 原第二款作为第三款。 五、将刑法第一百二十条修改为:“组织、领导恐怖活动组织的，处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑，并处没收财产；积极参加的，处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；其他参加的，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役、管制或者剥夺政治权利，可以并处罚金。 “犯前款罪并实施杀人、爆炸、绑架等犯罪的，依照数罪并罚的规定处罚。” 六、将刑法第一百二十条之一修改为:“资助恐怖活动组织、实施恐怖活动的个人的，或者资助恐怖活动培训的，处五年以下有期徒刑、拘役、管制或者剥夺政治权利，并处罚金；情节严重的，处五年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产。 “为恐怖活动组织、实施恐怖活动或者恐怖活动培训招募、运送人员的，依照前款的规定处罚。 “单位犯前两款罪的，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照第一款的规定处罚。” 七、在刑法第一百二十条之一后增加五条，作为第一百二十条之二、第一百二十条之三、第一百二十条之四、第一百二十条之五、第一百二十条之六:

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、在[刑法第三十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-37)后增加一条，作为第三十七条之一:“因利用职业便利实施犯罪，或者实施违背职业要求的特定义务的犯罪被判处刑罚的，人民法院可以根据犯罪情况和预防再犯罪的需要，禁止其自刑罚执行完毕之日或者假释之日起从事相关职业，期限为三年至五年。  
“被禁止从事相关职业的人违反人民法院依照前款规定作出的决定的，由公安机关依法给予处罚；情节严重的，依照[本法第三百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-313)的规定定罪处罚。  
“其他法律、行政法规对其从事相关职业另有禁止或者限制性规定的，从其规定。”

**Article 1** . One article is added after Article 37 of the Criminal Law as Article 37a: "Where a person is sentenced to criminal punishment for committing a crime by taking advantage of occupational convenience, or for committing a crime that violates specific obligations required by an occupation, the People's Court may, based on the circumstances of the crime and the need to prevent recidivism, prohibit him or her from engaging in the relevant occupation from the date of completion of the execution of the criminal punishment or the date of parole, for a period of three to five years.  
"Where a person who is prohibited from engaging in the relevant occupation violates a decision made by the People's Court in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the public security organ shall impose a penalty according to law; if the circumstances are serious, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 313 of this Law.  
"Where other laws or administrative regulations have other prohibitive or restrictive provisions on engaging in the relevant occupation, such provisions shall prevail."

<a id="art-2"></a>二、将[刑法第五十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-50)第一款修改为:“判处死刑缓期执行的，在死刑缓期执行期间，如果没有故意犯罪，二年期满以后，减为无期徒刑；如果确有重大立功表现，二年期满以后，减为二十五年有期徒刑；如果故意犯罪，情节恶劣的，报请最高人民法院核准后执行死刑；对于故意犯罪未执行死刑的，死刑缓期执行的期间重新计算，并报最高人民法院备案。”

**Article 2** . Paragraph 1 of Article 50 of the Criminal Law is amended as: "Where a death sentence with a two-year reprieve is imposed, during the period of the reprieve, if the person does not commit any intentional crime, upon the expiration of the two-year period, the sentence shall be commuted to life imprisonment; if the person has truly performed major meritorious service, upon the expiration of the two-year period, the sentence shall be commuted to twenty-five years of fixed-term imprisonment; if the person commits an intentional crime with flagrant circumstances, the death penalty shall be executed after being reported to and approved by the Supreme People's Court; if the death penalty is not executed for the intentional crime, the period of the reprieve shall be recalculated and reported to the Supreme People's Court for record."

<a id="art-3"></a>三、将[刑法第五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-53)修改为:“罚金在判决指定的期限内一次或者分期缴纳。期满不缴纳的，强制缴纳。对于不能全部缴纳罚金的，人民法院在任何时候发现被执行人有可以执行的财产，应当随时追缴。  
“由于遭遇不能抗拒的灾祸等原因缴纳确实有困难的，经人民法院裁定，可以延期缴纳、酌情减少或者免除。”

**Article 3** . Article 53 of the Criminal Law is amended as: "A fine shall be paid in a lump sum or in installments within the period specified in the judgment. If the fine is not paid upon expiration of the period, enforcement shall be carried out. If a fine cannot be paid in full, the People's Court shall at any time discover that the person subject to enforcement has executable property, and shall pursue recovery promptly.  
"If it is truly difficult to pay due to circumstances such as an irresistible disaster, the People's Court may, by ruling, permit an extension of the time for payment, or reduce or waive the fine as appropriate."

<a id="art-4"></a>四、在[刑法第六十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-69)中增加一款作为第二款:“数罪中有判处有期徒刑和拘役的，执行有期徒刑。数罪中有判处有期徒刑和管制，或者拘役和管制的，有期徒刑、拘役执行完毕后，管制仍须执行。”  
原第二款作为第三款。

**Article 4** . A paragraph is added to Article 69 of the Criminal Law as paragraph 2: "Where among the several crimes, some are punishable by fixed-term imprisonment and some by criminal detention, the fixed-term imprisonment shall be executed. Where among the several crimes, some are punishable by fixed-term imprisonment and public surveillance, or by criminal detention and public surveillance, after the execution of the fixed-term imprisonment or criminal detention, the public surveillance shall still be executed."  
The original paragraph 2 shall be paragraph 3.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、将[刑法第一百二十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-120)修改为:“组织、领导恐怖活动组织的，处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑，并处没收财产；积极参加的，处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；其他参加的，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役、管制或者剥夺政治权利，可以并处罚金。  
“犯前款罪并实施杀人、爆炸、绑架等犯罪的，依照数罪并罚的规定处罚。”

**Article 5** . Article 120 of the Criminal Law is amended as: "Whoever organizes or leads a terrorist organization shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment, and shall also be sentenced to confiscation of property; whoever actively participates shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than ten years, and shall also be fined; other participants shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, public surveillance, or deprivation of political rights, and may also be fined.  
"Whoever commits the crime mentioned in the preceding paragraph and also commits crimes such as homicide, explosion, or kidnapping shall be punished in accordance with the provisions on combined punishment for several crimes."

<a id="art-6"></a>六、将[刑法第一百二十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-120)之一修改为:“资助恐怖活动组织、实施恐怖活动的个人的，或者资助恐怖活动培训的，处五年以下有期徒刑、拘役、管制或者剥夺政治权利，并处罚金；情节严重的，处五年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产。  
“为恐怖活动组织、实施恐怖活动或者恐怖活动培训招募、运送人员的，依照前款的规定处罚。  
“单位犯前两款罪的，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照第一款的规定处罚。”

**Article 6** . Article 120a of the Criminal Law is amended as: "Whoever provides financial support to a terrorist organization or to an individual who commits a terrorist activity, or provides financial support to terrorist training, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years, criminal detention, public surveillance, or deprivation of political rights, and shall also be fined; if the circumstances are serious, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years, and shall also be fined or have property confiscated.  
"Whoever recruits or transports personnel for a terrorist organization, for committing terrorist activities, or for terrorist training shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
"Where a unit commits the crimes described in the preceding two paragraphs, a fine shall be imposed on the unit, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible for the crime shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1."

<a id="art-7"></a>七、在[刑法第一百二十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-120)之一后增加五条，作为第一百二十条之二、第一百二十条之三、第一百二十条之四、第一百二十条之五、第一百二十条之六:  
“第一百二十条之二　有下列情形之一的，处五年以下有期徒刑、拘役、管制或者剥夺政治权利，并处罚金；情节严重的，处五年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产:  
“（一）为实施恐怖活动准备凶器、危险物品或者其他工具的；  
“（二）组织恐怖活动培训或者积极参加恐怖活动培训的；  
“（三）为实施恐怖活动与境外恐怖活动组织或者人员联络的；  
“（四）为实施恐怖活动进行策划或者其他准备的。  
“有前款行为，同时构成其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。  
“第一百二十条之三　以制作、散发宣扬恐怖主义、极端主义的图书、音频视频资料或者其他物品，或者通过讲授、发布信息等方式宣扬恐怖主义、极端主义的，或者煽动实施恐怖活动的，处五年以下有期徒刑、拘役、管制或者剥夺政治权利，并处罚金；情节严重的，处五年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产。  
“第一百二十条之四　利用极端主义煽动、胁迫群众破坏国家法律确立的婚姻、司法、教育、社会管理等制度实施的，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役或者管制，并处罚金；情节严重的，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；情节特别严重的，处七年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产。  
“第一百二十条之五　以暴力、胁迫等方式强制他人在公共场所穿着、佩戴宣扬恐怖主义、极端主义服饰、标志的，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役或者管制，并处罚金。  
“第一百二十条之六　明知是宣扬恐怖主义、极端主义的图书、音频视频资料或者其他物品而非法持有，情节严重的，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役或者管制，并处或者单处罚金。”

**Article 7** . Five articles are added after Article 120a of the Criminal Law as Articles 120b, 120c, 120d, 120e, and 120f:  
"Article 120b. Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years, criminal detention, public surveillance, or deprivation of political rights, and shall also be fined; if the circumstances are serious, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years, and shall also be fined or have property confiscated:  
"(1) preparing weapons, dangerous articles, or other tools for committing terrorist activities;  
"(2) organizing terrorist training or actively participating in terrorist training;  
"(3) contacting a foreign terrorist organization or persons for committing terrorist activities;  
"(4) making plans or other preparations for committing terrorist activities.  
"If the act described in the preceding paragraph also constitutes another crime, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions on the heavier punishment.  
"Article 120c. Whoever produces or disseminates books, audio or video materials, or other articles that advocate terrorism or extremism, or advocates terrorism or extremism through lectures or by publishing information, or incites the commission of terrorist activities, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years, criminal detention, public surveillance, or deprivation of political rights, and shall also be fined; if the circumstances are serious, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years, and shall also be fined or have property confiscated.  
"Article 120d. Whoever uses extremism to incite or coerce the masses to disrupt the implementation of the systems of marriage, judicature, education, and social management established by the laws of the State shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, or public surveillance, and shall also be fined; if the circumstances are serious, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years, and shall also be fined; if the circumstances are especially serious, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years, and shall also be fined or have property confiscated.  
"Article 120e. Whoever compels another person to wear or display clothing or symbols that advocate terrorism or extremism in a public place by means of violence, coercion, etc., shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, or public surveillance, and shall also be fined.  
"Article 120f. Whoever illegally holds books, audio or video materials, or other articles that advocate terrorism or extremism, knowing that such items advocate terrorism or extremism, if the circumstances are serious, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, or public surveillance, and shall be fined or shall be fined only."

<a id="art-8"></a>八、将[刑法第一百三十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-133)之一修改为:“在道路上驾驶机动车，有下列情形之一的，处拘役，并处罚金:  
“（一）追逐竞驶，情节恶劣的；  
“（二）醉酒驾驶机动车的；  
“（三）从事校车业务或者旅客运输，严重超过额定乘员载客，或者严重超过规定时速行驶的；  
“（四）违反危险化学品安全管理规定运输危险化学品，危及公共安全的。  
“机动车所有人、管理人对前款第三项、第四项行为负有直接责任的，依照前款的规定处罚。  
“有前两款行为，同时构成其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。”

**Article 8** . Article 133a of the Criminal Law is amended as: "Whoever drives a motor vehicle on the road under any of the following circumstances shall be sentenced to criminal detention and shall also be fined:  
"(1) engaging in racing with aggravated circumstances;  
"(2) driving a motor vehicle while intoxicated;  
"(3) engaging in school bus service or passenger transport, carrying passengers seriously in excess of the prescribed number, or driving at a speed seriously exceeding the prescribed speed limit;  
"(4) transporting dangerous chemicals in violation of the safety management regulations on dangerous chemicals, thereby endangering public safety.  
"Where the owner or manager of a motor vehicle bears direct responsibility for the conduct specified in items (3) or (4) of the preceding paragraph, the offender shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
"If the conduct described in the preceding two paragraphs also constitutes another crime, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions on the heavier punishment."

<a id="art-9"></a>九、将[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款修改为:“走私武器、弹药、核材料或者伪造的货币的，处七年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产；情节特别严重的，处无期徒刑，并处没收财产；情节较轻的，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。”

**Article 9** . Paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law is amended as: "Whoever smuggles weapons, ammunition, nuclear materials, or counterfeit currency shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years, and shall also be fined or have property confiscated; if the circumstances are especially serious, the offender shall be sentenced to life imprisonment and shall also have property confiscated; if the circumstances are relatively minor, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years, and shall also be fined."

<a id="art-10"></a>十、将[刑法第一百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-164)第一款修改为:“为谋取不正当利益，给予公司、企业或者其他单位的工作人员以财物，数额较大的，处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金；数额巨大的，处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。”

**Article 10** . Paragraph 1 of Article 164 of the Criminal Law is amended as: "Whoever gives property to an employee of a company, enterprise, or other unit for the purpose of seeking illegitimate benefits, if the amount is relatively large, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention, and shall also be fined; if the amount is huge, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than ten years, and shall also be fined."

<a id="art-11"></a>十一、将[刑法第一百七十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-170)修改为:“伪造货币的，处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；有下列情形之一的，处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产:  
“（一）伪造货币集团的首要分子；  
“（二）伪造货币数额特别巨大的；  
“（三）有其他特别严重情节的。”

**Article 11** . Article 170 of the Criminal Law is amended as: "Whoever counterfeits currency shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than ten years, and shall also be fined; if the counterfeiting falls under any of the following circumstances, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment, and shall also be fined or have property confiscated:  
"(1) the ringleader of a group engaged in counterfeiting currency;  
"(2) the amount counterfeited is especially huge;  
"(3) there are other especially serious circumstances."

<a id="art-12"></a>十二、删去[刑法第一百九十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-199)。

**Article 12** . Article 199 of the Criminal Law is deleted.

<a id="art-13"></a>十三、将[刑法第二百三十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-237)修改为:“以暴力、胁迫或者其他方法强制猥亵他人或者侮辱妇女的，处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役。  
“聚众或者在公共场所当众犯前款罪的，或者有其他恶劣情节的，处五年以上有期徒刑。  
“猥亵儿童的，依照前两款的规定从重处罚。”

**Article 13** . Article 237 of the Criminal Law is amended as: "Whoever forcibly commits an indecent act against another person or insults a woman by violence, coercion, or any other method shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention.  
"Whoever commits the crime in the preceding paragraph by assembling people or in a public place, or under other despicable circumstances, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years.  
"Whoever commits an indecent act against a child shall be given a heavier punishment in accordance with the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs."

**Article 47** . Paragraph 1 of Article 391 of the Criminal Law is amended as: "Whoever, for the purpose of seeking illegitimate benefits, gives property to a State organ, a State-owned company, enterprise, institution, or people's organization, or in economic exchanges, in violation of State regulations, gives kickbacks or service fees in various names, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention, and shall also be fined."

<a id="art-48"></a>四十八、将[刑法第三百九十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-392)第一款修改为:“向国家工作人员介绍贿赂，情节严重的，处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金。”

**Article 48** . Paragraph 1 of Article 392 of the Criminal Law is amended as: "Whoever introduces a bribe to a State functionary, if the circumstances are serious, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention, and shall also be fined."

<a id="art-49"></a>四十九、将[刑法第三百九十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-393)修改为:“单位为谋取不正当利益而行贿，或者违反国家规定，给予国家工作人员以回扣、手续费，情节严重的，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金。因行贿取得的违法所得归个人所有的，依照[本法第三百八十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-389)、第三百九十条的规定定罪处罚。”

**Article 49** . Article 393 of the Criminal Law is amended as: "Where a unit offers bribes for the purpose of seeking illegitimate benefits, or in violation of State regulations, gives kickbacks or service fees to a State functionary, if the circumstances are serious, a fine shall be imposed on the unit, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible for the crime shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention, and shall also be fined. If the illegal gains obtained through bribery are owned individually, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Articles 389 and 390 of this Law."

<a id="art-50"></a>五十、将[刑法第四百二十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-426)修改为:“以暴力、威胁方法，阻碍指挥人员或者值班、值勤人员执行职务的，处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役；情节严重的，处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑；情节特别严重的，处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑。战时从重处罚。”

**Article 50** . Article 426 of the Criminal Law is amended as: "Whoever, by means of violence or threat, obstructs a commander or a person on duty or on guard from performing his or her duties shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention; if the circumstances are serious, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years but not more than ten years; if the circumstances are especially serious, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment. During wartime, a heavier punishment shall be imposed."

<a id="art-51"></a>五十一、将[刑法第四百三十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-433)修改为:“战时造谣惑众，动摇军心的，处三年以下有期徒刑；情节严重的，处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑；情节特别严重的，处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑。”

**Article 51** 51. Article 433 of the Criminal Law is amended as: "Whoever, during wartime, spreads rumors to confuse the masses and shake the morale of the armed forces shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years; if the circumstances are serious, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than ten years; if the circumstances are especially serious, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment."

<a id="art-52"></a>五十二、本修正案自2015年11月1日起施行。

**Article 52** 52. This Amendment shall come into force on November 1, 2015.
