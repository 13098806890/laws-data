# 地方各级人民政府机构设置和编制管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2007-02-24  
**生效日期**：2007-05-01

> (2007年2月14日国务院第169次常务会议通过　2007年2月24日中华人民共和国国务院令第486号公布　自2007年5月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范地方各级人民政府机构设置，加强编制管理，提高行政效能，根据宪法、地方各级人民代表大会和地方各级人民政府组织法，制定本条例。 第二条　地方各级人民政府机构的设置、职责配置、编制核定以及对机构编制工作的监督管理，适用本条例。 第三条　地方各级人民政府机构设置和编制管理工作，应当按照经济社会全面协调可持续发展的要求，适应全面履行职能的需要，遵循精简、统一、效能的原则。 第四条　地方各级人民政府的机构编制工作，实行中央统一领导、地方分级管理的体制。 第五条　县级以上各级人民政府机构编制管理机关应当按照管理权限履行管理职责，并对下级机构编制工作进行业务指导和监督。 第六条　依照国家规定的程序设置的机构和核定的编制，是录用、聘用、调配工作人员、配备领导成员和核拨经费的依据。 县级以上各级人民政府应当建立机构编制、人员工资与财政预算相互制约的机制，在设置机构、核定编制时，应当充分考虑财政的供养能力。机构实有人员不得突破规定的编制。禁止擅自设置机构和增加编制。对擅自设置机构和增加编制的，不得核拨财政资金或者挪用其他资金安排其经费。 第七条　县级以上各级人民政府行政机构不得干预下级人民政府行政机构的设置和编制管理工作，不得要求下级人民政府设立与其业务对口的行政机构。 第二章　机构设置管理 第八条　地方各级人民政府行政机构应当以职责的科学配置为基础，综合设置，做到职责明确、分工合理、机构精简、权责一致，决策和执行相协调。 地方各级人民政府行政机构应当根据履行职责的需要，适时调整。但是，在一届政府任期内，地方各级人民政府的工作部门应当保持相对稳定。 第九条　地方各级人民政府行政机构的设立、撤销、合并或者变更规格、名称，由本级人民政府提出方案，经上一级人民政府机构编制管理机关审核后，报上一级人民政府批准；其中，县级以上地方各级人民政府行政机构的设立、撤销或者合并，还应当依法报本级人民代表大会常务委员会备案。 第十条　地方各级人民政府行政机构职责相同或者相近的，原则上由一个行政机构承担。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范地方各级人民政府机构设置，加强编制管理，提高行政效能，根据宪法、地方各级人民代表大会和地方各级人民政府组织法，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Constitution, the Organic Law of the Local People's Congresses and Local People's Governments at All Levels for the purpose of regulating the establishment of organs of local people's governments at all levels, strengthening organization management, and improving administrative efficiency.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　地方各级人民政府机构的设置、职责配置、编制核定以及对机构编制工作的监督管理，适用本条例。

**Article 2** The establishment of organs of local people's governments at all levels, allocation of functions, approval of organization, and supervision and management over organization work shall be governed by these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　地方各级人民政府机构设置和编制管理工作，应当按照经济社会全面协调可持续发展的要求，适应全面履行职能的需要，遵循精简、统一、效能的原则。

**Article 3** The establishment of organs and organization management of local people's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the requirements of comprehensive, coordinated and sustainable economic and social development, meet the needs of fully performing their functions and follow the principles of simplification, uniformity and efficiency.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　地方各级人民政府的机构编制工作，实行中央统一领导、地方分级管理的体制。

**Article 4** The organization work of local people's governments at all levels shall adopt the system of unified leadership by the central authorities and decentralized management by local authorities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上各级人民政府机构编制管理机关应当按照管理权限履行管理职责，并对下级机构编制工作进行业务指导和监督。

**Article 5** The organ for organization management of people's governments at or above the county level shall perform its management functions according to its authority, and shall provide professional guidance to and supervision over the organization work of lower-level governments.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　依照国家规定的程序设置的机构和核定的编制，是录用、聘用、调配工作人员、配备领导成员和核拨经费的依据。  
县级以上各级人民政府应当建立机构编制、人员工资与财政预算相互制约的机制，在设置机构、核定编制时，应当充分考虑财政的供养能力。机构实有人员不得突破规定的编制。禁止擅自设置机构和增加编制。对擅自设置机构和增加编制的，不得核拨财政资金或者挪用其他资金安排其经费。

**Article 6** The organs established and the organization approved under the procedures prescribed by the State shall serve as the basis for recruiting, hiring and transferring staff, appointing leading members and allocating funds.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall establish a mechanism of mutual restraint among organization, staff salaries and fiscal budgets, and shall give full consideration to the financial capacity for support when establishing organs and approving organization. The actual number of personnel shall not exceed the approved organization. It shall be prohibited to establish organs or increase organization without authorization. For those who establish organs or increase organization without authorization, no fiscal funds shall be allocated or other funds shall be misappropriated to arrange their expenses.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上各级人民政府行政机构不得干预下级人民政府行政机构的设置和编制管理工作，不得要求下级人民政府设立与其业务对口的行政机构。

**Article 7** The administrative organs of people's governments at or above the county level shall not interfere in the establishment and organization management of administrative organs of lower-level people's governments, and shall not require lower-level people's governments to set up administrative organs corresponding to their functions.

### 第二章　机构设置管理

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　地方各级人民政府行政机构应当以职责的科学配置为基础，综合设置，做到职责明确、分工合理、机构精简、权责一致，决策和执行相协调。  
地方各级人民政府行政机构应当根据履行职责的需要，适时调整。但是，在一届政府任期内，地方各级人民政府的工作部门应当保持相对稳定。

**Article 8** The administrative organs of local people's governments at all levels shall be established in a comprehensive manner on the basis of scientific allocation of functions so as to achieve clear definition of functions, rational division of work, simplification of organs, consistency of authority and responsibility, and coordination between decision-making and enforcement.  
The administrative organs of local people's governments at all levels shall be adjusted in a timely manner as required for performing their functions. However, during the term of office of a government, the working departments of local people's governments at all levels shall remain relatively stable.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　地方各级人民政府行政机构的设立、撤销、合并或者变更规格、名称，由本级人民政府提出方案，经上一级人民政府机构编制管理机关审核后，报上一级人民政府批准；其中，县级以上地方各级人民政府行政机构的设立、撤销或者合并，还应当依法报本级人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 9** The establishment, dissolution, merger or change of the level or name of an administrative organ of a local people's government at any level shall be subject to a proposal submitted by the people's government at the same level, which shall be examined by the organ for organization management of the people's government at the next higher level and submitted to the people's government at the next higher level for approval; for the establishment, dissolution or merger of an administrative organ of a local people's government at or above the county level, it shall also be reported to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level for recordation according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　地方各级人民政府行政机构职责相同或者相近的，原则上由一个行政机构承担。  
行政机构之间对职责划分有异议的，应当主动协商解决。协商一致的，报本级人民政府机构编制管理机关备案；协商不一致的，应当提请本级人民政府机构编制管理机关提出协调意见，由机构编制管理机关报本级人民政府决定。

**Article 10** If administrative organs of local people's governments at all levels have the same or similar functions, in principle, one administrative organ shall undertake the functions.  
If there is a dispute over the division of functions between administrative organs, they shall actively negotiate for a solution. If an agreement is reached through negotiation, it shall be reported to the organ for organization management of the people's government at the same level for recordation; if no agreement is reached, the matter shall be submitted to the organ for organization management of the people's government at the same level for coordination opinions, and the organ for organization management shall report it to the people's government at the same level for decision.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　地方各级人民政府设立议事协调机构，应当严格控制；可以交由现有机构承担职能的或者由现有机构进行协调可以解决问题的，不另设立议事协调机构。  
为办理一定时期内某项特定工作设立的议事协调机构，应当明确规定其撤销的条件和期限。

**Article 11** The establishment of deliberative and coordinating organs by local people's governments at all levels shall be strictly controlled; if the functions can be undertaken by existing organs or if problems can be solved through coordination by existing organs, no separate deliberative or coordinating organ shall be established.  
For a deliberative or coordinating organ established to handle a specific task for a certain period, the conditions and time limit for its dissolution shall be clearly defined.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上地方各级人民政府的议事协调机构不单独设立办事机构，具体工作由有关的行政机构承担。

**Article 12** A deliberative or coordinating organ of a local people's government at or above the county level shall not have a separate office; its specific work shall be undertaken by the relevant administrative organ.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　地方各级人民政府行政机构根据工作需要和精干的原则，设立必要的内设机构。县级以上地方各级人民政府行政机构的内设机构的设立、撤销、合并或者变更规格、名称，由该行政机构报本级人民政府机构编制管理机关审批。

**Article 13** An administrative organ of a local people's government at any level shall set up necessary internal organs in accordance with the need of work and the principle of efficiency. The establishment, dissolution, merger or change of the level or name of an internal organ of an administrative organ of a local people's government at or above the county level shall be reported by the administrative organ to the organ for organization management of the people's government at the same level for examination and approval.

### 第三章　编制管理

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　地方各级人民政府行政机构的编制，应当根据其所承担的职责，按照精简的原则核定。

**Article 14** The organization of administrative organs of local people's governments at all levels shall be approved in accordance with the functions they undertake and the principle of simplification.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　机构编制管理机关应当按照编制的不同类别和使用范围审批编制。地方各级人民政府行政机构应当使用行政编制，事业单位应当使用事业编制，不得混用、挤占、挪用或者自行设定其他类别的编制。

**Article 15** The organ for organization management shall examine and approve organization according to different types and scopes of use of organization. The administrative organs of local people's governments at all levels shall use administrative organization, and public institutions shall use public institution organization. They shall not mix, occupy, misappropriate or set up other categories of organization on their own.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　地方各级人民政府的行政编制总额，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府提出，经国务院机构编制管理机关审核后，报国务院批准。

**Article 16** The total amount of administrative organization of local people's governments at all levels shall be proposed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, examined by the organ for organization management of the State Council, and submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　根据工作需要，国务院机构编制管理机关报经国务院批准，可以在地方行政编制总额内对特定的行政机构的行政编制实行专项管理。

**Article 17** According to the need of work, the organ for organization management of the State Council may, subject to approval by the State Council, exercise special management over the administrative organization of specific administrative organs within the total amount of local administrative organization.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　地方各级人民政府根据调整职责的需要，可以在行政编制总额内调整本级人民政府有关部门的行政编制。但是，在同一个行政区域不同层级之间调配使用行政编制的，应当由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府机构编制管理机关报国务院机构编制管理机关审批。

**Article 18** Local people's governments at all levels may, according to the need to readjust functions, readjust the administrative organization of the relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level within the total amount of administrative organization. However, if administrative organization is allocated and used between different levels within the same administrative region, it shall be reported by the organ for organization management of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government to the organ for organization management of the State Council for examination and approval.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　地方各级人民政府议事协调机构不单独确定编制，所需要的编制由承担具体工作的行政机构解决。

**Article 19** The deliberative and coordinating organs of local people's governments at all levels shall not have separate organization; the required organization shall be provided by the administrative organ undertaking the specific work.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　地方各级人民政府行政机构的领导职数，按照地方各级人民代表大会和地方各级人民政府组织法的有关规定确定。

**Article 20** The number of leading posts of administrative organs of local people's governments at all levels shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Organic Law of the Local People's Congresses and Local People's Governments at All Levels.

### 第四章　监督检查

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上各级人民政府机构编制管理机关应当按照管理权限，对机构编制管理的执行情况进行监督检查；必要时，可以会同监察机关和其他有关部门对机构编制管理的执行情况进行监督检查。有关组织和个人应当予以配合。

**Article 21** The organ for organization management of people's governments at or above the county level shall, within its authority, exercise supervision and inspection over the implementation of organization management; when necessary, it may, together with the supervision organ and other relevant departments, exercise supervision and inspection over the implementation of organization management. The relevant organizations and individuals shall cooperate.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级以上各级人民政府机构编制管理机关实施监督检查时，应当严格执行规定的程序，发现违反本条例规定的行为，应当向本级人民政府提出处理意见和建议。

**Article 22** When conducting supervision and inspection, the organ for organization management of people's governments at or above the county level shall strictly follow the prescribed procedures. If it discovers any act in violation of these Regulations, it shall put forward opinions and suggestions for handling to the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　地方各级人民政府机构编制管理机关，应当如实向上级机构编制管理机关提交机构编制年度统计资料，不得虚报、瞒报、伪造。

**Article 23** The organ for organization management of a local people's government at any level shall truthfully submit annual statistics on organization to the organ for organization management at the next higher level, and shall not make false reports, conceal facts or forge data.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　县级以上各级人民政府机构编制管理机关应当定期评估机构和编制的执行情况，并将评估结果作为调整机构编制的参考依据。评估的具体办法，由国务院机构编制管理机关制定。

**Article 24** The organ for organization management of people's governments at or above the county level shall regularly evaluate the implementation of organs and organization, and shall use the evaluation results as reference for adjusting organs and organization. The specific measures for evaluation shall be formulated by the organ for organization management of the State Council.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　任何组织和个人对违反机构编制管理规定的行为，都有权向机构编制管理机关、监察机关等有关部门举报。  
县级以上各级人民政府机构编制管理机关应当接受社会监督。

**Article 25** Any organization or individual shall have the right to report to the organ for organization management, the supervision organ or other relevant departments any act in violation of the provisions on organization management.  
The organ for organization management of people's governments at or above the county level shall accept social supervision.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　有下列行为之一的，由机构编制管理机关给予通报批评，并责令限期改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分：  
(一)擅自设立、撤销、合并行政机构或者变更规格、名称的；  
(二)擅自改变行政机构职责的；  
(三)擅自增加编制或者改变编制使用范围的；  
(四)超出编制限额调配财政供养人员、为超编人员核拨财政资金或者挪用其他资金安排其经费、以虚报人员等方式占用编制并冒用财政资金的；  
(五)擅自超职数、超规格配备领导成员的；  
(六)违反规定干预下级人民政府行政机构的设置和编制管理工作的；  
(七)违反规定审批机构、编制的；  
(八)违反机构编制管理规定的其他行为。

**Article 26** Where any of the following acts is committed, the organ for organization management shall circulate a notice of criticism and order corrections within a time limit; if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) establishing, dissolving, merging administrative organs or changing the level or name without authorization;  
(2) changing the functions of administrative organs without authorization;  
(3) increasing organization or changing the scope of use of organization without authorization;  
(4) deploying personnel supported by fiscal funds beyond the organization limit, allocating fiscal funds for overstaffed personnel or misappropriating other funds for their expenses, occupying organization by falsely reporting personnel and fraudulently using fiscal funds;  
(5) equipping leading members beyond the authorized number or level without authorization;  
(6) interfering in the establishment and organization management of administrative organs of lower-level people's governments in violation of regulations;  
(7) examining and approving organs or organization in violation of regulations;  
(8) other acts in violation of the provisions on organization management.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　机构编制管理机关工作人员在机构编制管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 27** Where a staff member of the organ for organization management abuses his power, neglects his duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain in the work of organization management, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, he shall be given a sanction according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本条例所称编制，是指机构编制管理机关核定的行政机构和事业单位的人员数额和领导职数。

**Article 28** For the purposes of these Regulations, 'organization' means the number of personnel and the number of leading posts of administrative organs and public institutions approved by the organ for organization management.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　地方的事业单位机构和编制管理办法，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府机构编制管理机关拟定，报国务院机构编制管理机关审核后，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府发布。事业编制的全国性标准由国务院机构编制管理机关会同国务院财政部门和其他有关部门制定。

**Article 29** The measures for the management of organs and organization of local public institutions shall be drafted by the organ for organization management of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall be promulgated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government after being examined by the organ for organization management of the State Council. The national standards for public institution organization shall be formulated by the organ for organization management of the State Council jointly with the fiscal department of the State Council and other relevant departments.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本条例自2007年5月1日起施行。

**Article 30** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2007.
