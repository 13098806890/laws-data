<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
关于适用[刑法第六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-64)有关问题的批复  
2013年10月21日      法〔2013〕229号  
河南省高级人民法院：  
你院《关于[刑法第六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-64)法律适用问题的请示》收悉。经研究，批复如下：  
根据[刑法第六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-64)和《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法〉的解释》第一百三十八条、第一百三十九条的规定，被告人非法占有、处置被害人财产的，应当依法予以追缴或者责令退赔。据此，追缴或者责令退赔的具体内容，应当在判决主文中写明；其中，判决前已经发还被害人的财产，应当注明。被害人提起附带民事诉讼，或者另行提起民事诉讼请求返还被非法占有、处置的财产的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply concerning the Application of Article 64 of the Criminal Law  
October 21, 2013 Fa [2013] No. 229  
Higher People's Court of Henan Province:  
  
Your Request for Instructions on Issues concerning the Application of Article 64 of the Criminal Law has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
  
According to the provisions of Article 64 of the Criminal Law and Articles 138 and 139 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, where a defendant illegally takes possession of or disposes of the victim's property, the property shall be recovered or the defendant shall be ordered to make restitution according to law. Accordingly, the specific content of the recovery or the order for restitution shall be stated in the main text of the judgment; where property has already been returned to the victim before the judgment, this shall be indicated. Where the victim files an incidental civil action or separately files a civil action requesting the return of property illegally taken possession of or disposed of, the People's Court shall not accept such action.
