最高人民检察院关于涉嫌犯罪单位  
被撤销、注销、吊销营业执照或者宣告破产的  
应如何进行追诉问题的批复  
（2002年7月4日最高人民检察院第九届检察委员会  
第一百一十一次会议通过）  
高检发释字〔2002〕4号  
四川省人民检察院：  
你院《关于对已注销的单位原犯罪行为是否应当追诉的请示》（川检发研〔2001〕25号）收悉。经研究，批复如下：  
涉嫌犯罪的单位被撤销、注销、吊销营业执照或者宣告破产的，应当根据刑法关于单位犯罪的相关规定，对实施犯罪行为的该单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员追究刑事责任，对该单位不再追诉。  
此复。  
最高人民检察院  
2002年7月9日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Procuratorate  
July 9, 2002  
Reply of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Issues Concerning How to Pursue Legal Action When a Suspected Criminal Unit Has Been Dissolved, Cancelled, Had Its Business License Revoked, or Declared Bankrupt  
(Adopted at the 111th Session of the Ninth Procuratorial Committee of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on July 4, 2002)  
Gao Jian Fa Shi Zi [2002] No. 4  
The People's Procuratorate of Sichuan Province:  
Your Request for Instructions on Whether Criminal Acts Committed by a Unit That Has Been Cancelled Should Be Pursued (Chuan Jian Fa Yan [2001] No. 25) has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Where a unit suspected of committing a crime has been dissolved, cancelled, had its business license revoked, or declared bankrupt, criminal liability shall be investigated against the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons of the unit who committed the criminal act in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law on unit crimes, and the unit shall no longer be pursued.  
This is hereby replied.  
Supreme People's Procuratorate  
July 9, 2002
