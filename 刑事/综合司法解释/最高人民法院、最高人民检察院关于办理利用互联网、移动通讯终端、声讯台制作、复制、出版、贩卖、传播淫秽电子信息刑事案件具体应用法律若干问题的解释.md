# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理利用互联网、移动通讯终端、声讯台制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　以牟利为目的，利用互联网、移动通讯终端制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第一款的规定，以制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽物品牟利罪定罪处罚：  
（一）制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电影、表演、动画等视频文件二十个以上的；  
（二）制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽音频文件一百个以上的；  
（三）制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子刊物、图片、文章、短信息等二百件以上的；  
（四）制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播的淫秽电子信息，实际被点击数达到一万次以上的；  
（五）以会员制方式出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息，注册会员达二百人以上的；  
（六）利用淫秽电子信息收取广告费、会员注册费或者其他费用，违法所得一万元以上的；  
（七）数量或者数额虽未达到第（一）项至第（六）项规定标准，但分别达到其中两项以上标准一半以上的；  
（八）造成严重后果的。  
利用聊天室、论坛、即时通信软件、电子邮件等方式，实施第一款规定行为的，依照[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第一款的规定，以制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽物品牟利罪定罪处罚。

**Article 1** Whoever, for the purpose of profit, produces, reproduces, publishes, sells, or disseminates pornographic electronic information through the Internet or mobile communication terminals, under any of the following circumstances, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating pornographic materials for profit in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 363 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating twenty or more video files such as pornographic films, performances, or animations;  
(2) producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating one hundred or more pornographic audio files;  
(3) producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating two hundred or more pornographic electronic publications, pictures, articles, or short messages;  
(4) the pornographic electronic information produced, reproduced, published, sold, or disseminated has been actually clicked upon ten thousand or more times;  
(5) publishing, selling, or disseminating pornographic electronic information by means of a membership system with two hundred or more registered members;  
(6) collecting advertising fees, membership registration fees, or other fees by using pornographic electronic information, with illegal gains of 10,000 yuan or more;  
(7) although the quantity or amount does not meet the standards specified in items (1) to (6), the respective amounts reach more than half of two or more of those standards; or  
(8) causing serious consequences.  
Whoever, by means of chat rooms, forums, instant messaging software, e-mail, or the like, commits the act specified in the first paragraph shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating pornographic materials for profit in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 363 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　实施第一条规定的行为，数量或者数额达到第一条第一款第（一）项至第（六）项规定标准五倍以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第一款规定的“情节严重”；达到规定标准二十五倍以上的，应当认定为“情节特别严重”。

**Article 2** Where the quantity or amount of the act specified in Article 1 reaches five times or more the standards specified in items (1) to (6) of the first paragraph of Article 1, it shall be deemed as “serious circumstances” as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 363 of the Criminal Law; where it reaches twenty-five times or more the prescribed standards, it shall be deemed as “especially serious circumstances.”

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　不以牟利为目的，利用互联网或者移动通讯终端传播淫秽电子信息，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第三百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-364)第一款的规定，以传播淫秽物品罪定罪处罚；  
（一）数量达到第一条第一款第（一）项至第（五）项规定标准二倍以上的；  
（二）数量分别达到第一条第一款第（一）项至第（五）项两项以上标准的；  
（三）造成严重后果的。  
利用聊天室、论坛、即时通信软件、电子邮件等方式，实施第一款规定行为的，依照[刑法第三百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-364)第一款的规定，以传播淫秽物品罪定罪处罚。

**Article 3** Whoever disseminates pornographic electronic information through the Internet or mobile communication terminals, not for the purpose of profit, under any of the following circumstances, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of disseminating pornographic materials in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 364 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the quantity reaches two times or more the standards specified in items (1) to (5) of the first paragraph of Article 1;  
(2) the quantity respectively reaches two or more of the standards specified in items (1) to (5) of the first paragraph of Article 1; or  
(3) causing serious consequences.  
Whoever, by means of chat rooms, forums, instant messaging software, e-mail, or the like, commits the act specified in the first paragraph shall be convicted and punished for the crime of disseminating pornographic materials in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 364 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　明知是淫秽电子信息而在自己所有、管理或者使用的网站或者网页上提供直接链接的，其数量标准根据所链接的淫秽电子信息的种类计算。

**Article 4** Whoever knowingly provides direct links on websites or web pages that he or she owns, manages, or uses to pornographic electronic information shall have the quantity standards calculated on the basis of the types of pornographic electronic information to which the links are provided.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　以牟利为目的，通过声讯台传播淫秽语音信息，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第一款的规定，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员以传播淫秽物品牟利罪定罪处罚：  
（一）向一百人次以上传播的；  
（二）违法所得一万元以上的；  
（三）造成严重后果的。  
实施前款规定行为，数量或者数额达到前款第（一）项至第（二）项规定标准五倍以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第一款规定的“情节严重”；达到规定标准二十五倍以上的，应当认定为“情节特别严重”。

**Article 5** Whoever, for the purpose of profit, disseminates pornographic voice information through voice message platforms, under any of the following circumstances, shall have the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible convicted and punished for the crime of disseminating pornographic materials for profit in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 363 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) disseminating to one hundred or more person-times;  
(2) illegal gains of 10,000 yuan or more; or  
(3) causing serious consequences.  
Where the quantity or amount of the act specified in the preceding paragraph reaches five times or more the standards specified in items (1) to (2) of the preceding paragraph, it shall be deemed as “serious circumstances” as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 363 of the Criminal Law; where it reaches twenty-five times or more the prescribed standards, it shall be deemed as “especially serious circumstances.”

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　实施本解释前五条规定的犯罪，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第一款、第三百六十四条第一款的规定从重处罚：  
（一）制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播具体描绘不满十八周岁未成年人性行为的淫秽电子信息的；  
（二）明知是具体描绘不满十八周岁的未成年人性行为的淫秽电子信息而在自己所有、管理或者使用的网站或者网页上提供直接链接的；  
（三）向不满十八周岁的未成年人贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息和语音信息的；  
（四）通过使用破坏性程序、恶意代码修改用户计算机设置等方法，强制用户访问、下载淫秽电子信息的。

**Article 6** Where any crime specified in the preceding five articles of this Interpretation is committed under any of the following circumstances, a severe punishment shall be imposed in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 363 and paragraph 1 of Article 364 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating pornographic electronic information that specifically depicts sexual conduct of a minor under the age of 18;  
(2) knowingly providing direct links on a website or web page that he or she owns, manages, or uses to pornographic electronic information that specifically depicts sexual conduct of a minor under the age of 18;  
(3) selling or disseminating pornographic electronic information or voice information to a minor under the age of 18; or  
(4) forcibly causing users to access or download pornographic electronic information by means such as using destructive programs or malicious codes to modify users' computer settings.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　明知他人实施制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息犯罪，为其提供互联网接入、服务器托管、网络存储空间、通讯传输通道、费用结算等帮助的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，以共同犯罪论处。

**Article 7** Whoever knowingly provides assistance to another person in committing the crime of producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating pornographic electronic information, such as Internet access, server hosting, network storage space, communication transmission channels, or fee settlement, shall be punished as a joint offender with respect to the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　利用互联网、移动通讯终端、声讯台贩卖、传播淫秽书刊、影片、录像带、录音带等以实物为载体的淫秽物品的，依照《最高人民法院关于审理非法出版物刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》的有关规定定罪处罚。

**Article 8** Whoever sells or disseminates pornographic articles on physical media such as pornographic books, films, videotapes, or audiotapes through the Internet, mobile communication terminals, or voice message platforms shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Illegal Publications.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　[刑法第三百六十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-367)第一款规定的“其他淫秽物品”，包括具体描绘性行为或者露骨宣扬色情的诲淫性的视频文件、音频文件、电子刊物、图片、文章、短信息等互联网、移动通讯终端电子信息和声讯台语音信息。  
有关人体生理、医学知识的电子信息和声讯台语音信息不是淫秽物品。包含色情内容的有艺术价值的电子文学、艺术作品不视为淫秽物品。

**Article 9** The “other pornographic articles” as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 367 of the Criminal Law include pornographic video files, audio files, electronic publications, pictures, articles, short messages, and other electronic information on the Internet or mobile communication terminals, as well as voice information on voice message platforms, that specifically depict sexual conduct or blatantly propagate obscenity.  
Electronic information and voice information on voice message platforms concerning human physiology or medical knowledge shall not be deemed as pornographic articles. Electronic literary or artistic works containing pornographic content but of artistic value shall not be regarded as pornographic articles.
