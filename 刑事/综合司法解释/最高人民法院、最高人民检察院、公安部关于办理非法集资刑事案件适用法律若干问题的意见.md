# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部关于办理非法集资刑事案件适用法律若干问题的意见

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发文字号**：公通字[2014]16号  
**公布日期**：0000-00-00

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、关于行政认定的问题  
行政部门对于非法集资的性质认定，不是非法集资刑事案件进入刑事诉讼程序的必经程序。行政部门未对非法集资作出性质认定的，不影响非法集资刑事案件的侦查、起诉和审判。  
公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院应当依法认定案件事实的性质，对于案情复杂、性质认定疑难的案件，可参考有关部门的认定意见，根据案件事实和法律规定作出性质认定。

**Article 1** I. Issues concerning administrative determination  
Administrative departments' determination of the nature of illegal fundraising is not a prerequisite for illegal fundraising criminal cases to enter criminal proceedings. The failure of administrative departments to make a determination on the nature of illegal fundraising shall not affect the investigation, prosecution, and adjudication of illegal fundraising criminal cases.  
Public security organs, People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts shall determine the nature of the case facts according to law. For cases with complex circumstances or where the nature determination is difficult, they may refer to the determination opinions of relevant departments and make a nature determination based on the case facts and legal provisions.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、关于“向社会公开宣传”的认定问题  
[《最高人民法院关于审理非法集资刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》第一条](../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院关于审理非法集资刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-1)第一款第二项中的“向社会公开宣传”，包括以各种途径向社会公众传播吸收资金的信息，以及明知吸收资金的信息向社会公众扩散而予以放任等情形。

**Article 2** II. Issues concerning the determination of "public promotion to society"  
"Public promotion to society" as used in Article 1, paragraph 1, item (2) of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Adjudication of Illegal Fundraising Criminal Cases includes disseminating information on absorbing funds to the public through various channels, as well as knowingly allowing the information on absorbing funds to spread to the public and other similar circumstances.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、关于“社会公众”的认定问题  
下列情形不属于[《最高人民法院关于审理非法集资刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》第一条](../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院关于审理非法集资刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-1)第二款规定的“针对特定对象吸收资金”的行为，应当认定为向社会公众吸收资金：  
（一）在向亲友或者单位内部人员吸收资金的过程中，明知亲友或者单位内部人员向不特定对象吸收资金而予以放任的；  
（二）以吸收资金为目的，将社会人员吸收为单位内部人员，并向其吸收资金的。

**Article 3** III. Issues concerning the determination of "the public"  
The following circumstances shall not be deemed as "absorbing funds from specific targets" as provided in Article 1, paragraph 2 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Adjudication of Illegal Fundraising Criminal Cases, and shall be determined as absorbing funds from the public:  
(1) In the process of absorbing funds from relatives, friends, or internal personnel of a unit, knowingly allowing the relatives, friends, or internal personnel to absorb funds from unspecified targets;  
(2) For the purpose of absorbing funds, recruiting non-unit persons as internal personnel of the unit and absorbing funds from them.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、关于共同犯罪的处理问题  
为他人向社会公众非法吸收资金提供帮助，从中收取代理费、好处费、返点费、佣金、提成等费用，构成非法集资共同犯罪的，应当依法追究刑事责任。能够及时退缴上述费用的，可依法从轻处罚；其中情节轻微的，可以免除处罚；情节显著轻微、危害不大的，不作为犯罪处理。

**Article 4** IV. Issues concerning the handling of joint crimes  
Where a person provides assistance to another person in illegally absorbing funds from the public and collects agency fees, kickbacks, rebates, commissions, or other fees therefrom, thereby constituting a joint crime of illegal fundraising, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law. If such fees are timely returned, a lighter punishment may be imposed according to law; where the circumstances are minor, punishment may be exempted; where the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm is not significant, the conduct shall not be treated as a crime.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、关于涉案财物的追缴和处置问题  
向社会公众非法吸收的资金属于违法所得。以吸收的资金向集资参与人支付的利息、分红等回报，以及向帮助吸收资金人员支付的代理费、好处费、返点费、佣金、提成等费用，应当依法追缴。集资参与人本金尚未归还的，所支付的回报可予折抵本金。  
将非法吸收的资金及其转换财物用于清偿债务或者转让给他人，有下列情形之一的，应当依法追缴：  
（一）他人明知是上述资金及财物而收取的；  
（二）他人无偿取得上述资金及财物的；  
（三）他人以明显低于市场的价格取得上述资金及财物的；  
（四）他人取得上述资金及财物系源于非法债务或者违法犯罪活动的；  
（五）其他依法应当追缴的情形。  
查封、扣押、冻结的易贬值及保管、养护成本较高的涉案财物，可以在诉讼终结前依照有关规定变卖、拍卖。所得价款由查封、扣押、冻结机关予以保管，待诉讼终结后一并处置。  
查封、扣押、冻结的涉案财物，一般应在诉讼终结后，返还集资参与人。涉案财物不足全部返还的，按照集资参与人的集资额比例返还。

**Article 5** V. Issues concerning the recovery and disposal of involved property  
Funds illegally absorbed from the public constitute illegal gains. Interest, dividends, and other returns paid to fundraising participants with the absorbed funds, as well as agency fees, kickbacks, rebates, commissions, and other fees paid to persons who assisted in absorbing funds, shall be recovered according to law. If the principal of fundraising participants has not been returned, the returns paid may be used to offset the principal.  
Where the illegally absorbed funds and their converted property are used to repay debts or transferred to others under any of the following circumstances, they shall be recovered according to law:  
(1) The recipient knew the above-mentioned funds and property were such and accepted them;  
(2) The recipient obtained the above-mentioned funds and property gratuitously;  
(3) The recipient obtained the above-mentioned funds and property at a price obviously lower than the market price;  
(4) The recipient's acquisition of the above-mentioned funds and property originated from illegal debts or illegal and criminal activities;  
(5) Other circumstances where recovery shall be made according to law.  
For involved property that is subject to seizure, impoundment, or freezing and is prone to depreciation or has high storage and maintenance costs, it may be sold or auctioned according to relevant provisions before the conclusion of the proceedings. The proceeds shall be kept by the organ that carried out the seizure, impoundment, or freezing, and shall be disposed of together after the conclusion of the proceedings.  
Involved property that has been seized, impounded, or frozen shall generally be returned to the fundraising participants after the conclusion of the proceedings. If the involved property is insufficient for full return, it shall be returned in proportion to the amount contributed by each fundraising participant.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、关于证据的收集问题  
办理非法集资刑事案件中，确因客观条件的限制无法逐一收集集资参与人的言词证据的，可结合已收集的集资参与人的言词证据和依法收集并查证属实的书面合同、银行账户交易记录、会计凭证及会计账簿、资金收付凭证、审计报告、互联网电子数据等证据，综合认定非法集资对象人数和吸收资金数额等犯罪事实。

**Article 6** VI. Issues concerning the collection of evidence  
In handling illegal fundraising criminal cases, where due to objective limitations it is truly impossible to collect the testimonial evidence of each fundraising participant individually, the court may, in combination with the testimonial evidence already collected from fundraising participants and other evidence such as written contracts, bank account transaction records, accounting vouchers and ledgers, fund receipt and payment documents, audit reports, and internet electronic data that have been legally collected and verified, comprehensively determine the number of targets of illegal fundraising and the amount of funds absorbed, as well as other criminal facts.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、关于涉及民事案件的处理问题  
对于公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院正在侦查、起诉、审理的非法集资刑事案件，有关单位或者个人就同一事实向人民法院提起民事诉讼或者申请执行涉案财物的，人民法院应当不予受理，并将有关材料移送公安机关或者检察机关。  
人民法院在审理民事案件或者执行过程中，发现有非法集资犯罪嫌疑的，应当裁定驳回起诉或者中止执行，并及时将有关材料移送公安机关或者检察机关。  
公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院在侦查、起诉、审理非法集资刑事案件中，发现与人民法院正在审理的民事案件属同一事实，或者被申请执行的财物属于涉案财物的，应当及时通报相关人民法院。人民法院经审查认为确属涉嫌犯罪的，依照前款规定处理。

**Article 7** VII. Issues concerning the handling of related civil cases  
For illegal fundraising criminal cases that are under investigation, prosecution, or adjudication by public security organs, People's Procuratorates, or People's Courts, if a relevant unit or individual files a civil lawsuit with a People's Court regarding the same facts or applies for enforcement of involved property, the People's Court shall refuse to accept the case and shall transfer the relevant materials to the public security organ or procuratorial organ.  
If, during the trial of a civil case or during enforcement, a People's Court discovers that there is suspicion of illegal fundraising, it shall rule to dismiss the action or suspend the enforcement, and shall promptly transfer the relevant materials to the public security organ or procuratorial organ.  
Where, during the investigation, prosecution, or adjudication of an illegal fundraising criminal case, a public security organ, People's Procuratorate, or People's Court discovers that the facts are the same as those in a civil case being tried by a People's Court, or that the property subject to enforcement falls under involved property, it shall promptly notify the relevant People's Court. If, upon review, the People's Court determines that it is indeed suspected of a crime, it shall handle the matter in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、关于跨区域案件的处理问题  
跨区域非法集资刑事案件，在查清犯罪事实的基础上，可以由不同地区的公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院分别处理。  
对于分别处理的跨区域非法集资刑事案件，应当按照统一制定的方案处置涉案财物。  
国家机关工作人员违反规定处置涉案财物，构成渎职等犯罪的，应当依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 8** VIII. Issues concerning the handling of cross-regional cases  
For cross-regional illegal fundraising criminal cases, on the basis of clarifying the criminal facts, public security organs, People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts in different regions may handle the cases separately.  
For cross-regional illegal fundraising criminal cases handled separately, the involved property shall be disposed of in accordance with a uniformly formulated plan.  
Where state functionaries dispose of involved property in violation of regulations, constituting crimes of dereliction of duty or other crimes, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.
