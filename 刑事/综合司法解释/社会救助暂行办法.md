# 社会救助暂行办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2014-05-01

> (2014年2月21日中华人民共和国国务院令第649号公布　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强社会救助，保障公民的基本生活，促进社会公平，维护社会和谐稳定，根据宪法，制定本办法。 第二条　社会救助制度坚持托底线、救急难、可持续，与其他社会保障制度相衔接，社会救助水平与经济社会发展水平相适应。 社会救助工作应当遵循公开、公平、公正、及时的原则。 第三条　国务院民政部门统筹全国社会救助体系建设。国务院民政、应急管理、卫生健康、教育、住房城乡建设、人力资源社会保障、医疗保障等部门，按照各自职责负责相应的社会救助管理工作。 县级以上地方人民政府民政、应急管理、卫生健康、教育、住房城乡建设、人力资源社会保障、医疗保障等部门，按照各自职责负责本行政区域内相应的社会救助管理工作。 前两款所列行政部门统称社会救助管理部门。 第四条　乡镇人民政府、街道办事处负责有关社会救助的申请受理、调查审核，具体工作由社会救助经办机构或者经办人员承担。 村民委员会、居民委员会协助做好有关社会救助工作。 第五条　县级以上人民政府应当将社会救助纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，建立健全政府领导、民政部门牵头、有关部门配合、社会力量参与的社会救助工作协调机制，完善社会救助资金、物资保障机制，将政府安排的社会救助资金和社会救助工作经费纳入财政预算。 社会救助资金实行专项管理，分账核算，专款专用，任何单位或者个人不得挤占挪用。社会救助资金的支付，按照财政国库管理的有关规定执行。 第六条　县级以上人民政府应当按照国家统一规划建立社会救助管理信息系统，实现社会救助信息互联互通、资源共享。 第七条　国家鼓励、支持社会力量参与社会救助。 第八条　对在社会救助工作中作出显著成绩的单位、个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强社会救助，保障公民的基本生活，促进社会公平，维护社会和谐稳定，根据宪法，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated pursuant to the Constitution for the purposes of strengthening social assistance, safeguarding the basic livelihood of citizens, promoting social equity, and maintaining social harmony and stability.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　社会救助制度坚持托底线、救急难、可持续，与其他社会保障制度相衔接，社会救助水平与经济社会发展水平相适应。  
社会救助工作应当遵循公开、公平、公正、及时的原则。

**Article 2** The social assistance system shall adhere to the principles of ensuring a basic safety net, providing emergency relief, and being sustainable, and shall be linked with other social security systems, with the level of social assistance commensurate with the level of economic and social development.  
Social assistance work shall follow the principles of openness, fairness, impartiality, and timeliness.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院民政部门统筹全国社会救助体系建设。国务院民政、应急管理、卫生健康、教育、住房城乡建设、人力资源社会保障、医疗保障等部门，按照各自职责负责相应的社会救助管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府民政、应急管理、卫生健康、教育、住房城乡建设、人力资源社会保障、医疗保障等部门，按照各自职责负责本行政区域内相应的社会救助管理工作。  
前两款所列行政部门统称社会救助管理部门。

**Article 3** The civil affairs department of the State Council shall coordinate the national social assistance system. The departments of civil affairs, emergency management, health, education, housing and urban-rural development, human resources and social security, and medical security of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions, be responsible for the corresponding social assistance administration.  
The departments of civil affairs, emergency management, health, education, housing and urban-rural development, human resources and social security, and medical security of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions, be responsible for the corresponding social assistance administration within their respective administrative areas.  
The administrative departments listed in the preceding two paragraphs are collectively referred to as the social assistance administrative departments.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　乡镇人民政府、街道办事处负责有关社会救助的申请受理、调查审核，具体工作由社会救助经办机构或者经办人员承担。  
村民委员会、居民委员会协助做好有关社会救助工作。

**Article 4** The people's governments of townships and sub-district offices shall be responsible for receiving applications for relevant social assistance and conducting investigation and verification, and the specific work shall be undertaken by social assistance handling agencies or handling personnel.  
Villagers' committees and residents' committees shall assist in carrying out the relevant social assistance work.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府应当将社会救助纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，建立健全政府领导、民政部门牵头、有关部门配合、社会力量参与的社会救助工作协调机制，完善社会救助资金、物资保障机制，将政府安排的社会救助资金和社会救助工作经费纳入财政预算。  
社会救助资金实行专项管理，分账核算，专款专用，任何单位或者个人不得挤占挪用。社会救助资金的支付，按照财政国库管理的有关规定执行。

**Article 5** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate social assistance into the national economic and social development plan, establish and improve a coordination mechanism for social assistance work under the leadership of the government, with the civil affairs department taking the lead, relevant departments cooperating, and social forces participating, and improve the guarantee mechanism for social assistance funds and materials, and include the social assistance funds arranged by the government and the operating expenses for social assistance into the fiscal budget.  
Social assistance funds shall be subject to special management, separate accounting, and exclusive use for specified purposes, and no unit or individual may misappropriate or divert them. The payment of social assistance funds shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions on fiscal treasury management.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上人民政府应当按照国家统一规划建立社会救助管理信息系统，实现社会救助信息互联互通、资源共享。

**Article 6** People's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the unified national plan, establish a social assistance management information system to achieve interconnection and resource sharing of social assistance information.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励、支持社会力量参与社会救助。

**Article 7** The State encourages and supports social forces to participate in social assistance.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对在社会救助工作中作出显著成绩的单位、个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 8** Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in social assistance work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　最低生活保障

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家对共同生活的家庭成员人均收入低于当地最低生活保障标准，且符合当地最低生活保障家庭财产状况规定的家庭，给予最低生活保障。

**Article 9** The State shall grant subsistence allowances to families whose per capita income of family members living together is lower than the local subsistence allowance standard and that meet the provisions on the property status of families receiving subsistence allowances.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　最低生活保障标准，由省、自治区、直辖市或者设区的市级人民政府按照当地居民生活必需的费用确定、公布，并根据当地经济社会发展水平和物价变动情况适时调整。  
最低生活保障家庭收入状况、财产状况的认定办法，由省、自治区、直辖市或者设区的市级人民政府按照国家有关规定制定。

**Article 10** The subsistence allowance standard shall be determined and published by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or of a city divided into districts, based on the cost of basic necessities for local residents, and shall be adjusted in a timely manner based on the local economic and social development level and changes in prices.  
The measures for determining the income status and property status of families receiving subsistence allowances shall be formulated by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or of a city divided into districts, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请最低生活保障，按照下列程序办理：  
(一)由共同生活的家庭成员向户籍所在地的乡镇人民政府、街道办事处提出书面申请；家庭成员申请有困难的，可以委托村民委员会、居民委员会代为提出申请。  
(二)乡镇人民政府、街道办事处应当通过入户调查、邻里访问、信函索证、群众评议、信息核查等方式，对申请人的家庭收入状况、财产状况进行调查核实，提出初审意见，在申请人所在村、社区公示后报县级人民政府民政部门审批。  
(三)县级人民政府民政部门经审查，对符合条件的申请予以批准，并在申请人所在村、社区公布；对不符合条件的申请不予批准，并书面向申请人说明理由。

**Article 11** Applications for subsistence allowances shall be processed according to the following procedures:  
(1) A family member living together shall submit a written application to the people's government of the township or sub-district office where the household registration is located; if the family member has difficulty in applying, he or she may entrust the villagers' committee or residents' committee to submit the application on his or her behalf.  
(2) The people's government of the township or sub-district office shall investigate and verify the applicant's family income status and property status through methods such as door-to-door investigation, neighborhood visits, correspondence verification, public evaluation, and information verification, and shall put forward preliminary examination opinions, and after publicizing them in the village or community where the applicant resides, shall submit them to the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level for approval.  
(3) The civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level, upon review, shall approve applications that meet the conditions and publish them in the village or community where the applicant resides; and shall not approve applications that do not meet the conditions and shall explain the reasons to the applicant in writing.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对批准获得最低生活保障的家庭，县级人民政府民政部门按照共同生活的家庭成员人均收入低于当地最低生活保障标准的差额，按月发给最低生活保障金。  
对获得最低生活保障后生活仍有困难的老年人、未成年人、重度残疾人和重病患者，县级以上地方人民政府应当采取必要措施给予生活保障。

**Article 12** For families approved to receive subsistence allowances, the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level shall, on a monthly basis, pay the subsistence allowance in the amount of the difference between the per capita income of the family members living together and the local subsistence allowance standard.  
For elderly persons, minors, persons with severe disabilities, and persons with severe illnesses who still have difficulties in living after receiving subsistence allowances, the local people's government at or above the county level shall take necessary measures to provide living security.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　最低生活保障家庭的人口状况、收入状况、财产状况发生变化的，应当及时告知乡镇人民政府、街道办事处。  
县级人民政府民政部门以及乡镇人民政府、街道办事处应当对获得最低生活保障家庭的人口状况、收入状况、财产状况定期核查。  
最低生活保障家庭的人口状况、收入状况、财产状况发生变化的，县级人民政府民政部门应当及时决定增发、减发或者停发最低生活保障金；决定停发最低生活保障金的，应当书面说明理由。

**Article 13** If the demographic status, income status, or property status of a family receiving subsistence allowances changes, the family shall promptly inform the people's government of the township or the sub-district office.  
The civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level and the people's government of the township or the sub-district office shall periodically verify the demographic status, income status, and property status of families receiving subsistence allowances.  
If the demographic status, income status, or property status of a family receiving subsistence allowances changes, the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level shall promptly decide to increase, decrease, or stop the payment of the subsistence allowance; if it decides to stop the payment of the subsistence allowance, it shall explain the reasons in writing.

### 第三章　特困人员供养

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家对无劳动能力、无生活来源且无法定赡养、抚养、扶养义务人，或者其法定赡养、抚养、扶养义务人无赡养、抚养、扶养能力的老年人、残疾人以及未满16周岁的未成年人，给予特困人员供养。

**Article 14** The State shall provide support for persons in extreme difficulty, including elderly persons, disabled persons, and minors under the age of 16 who have no ability to work, no source of income, and no legal obligors for support, maintenance, or care, or whose legal obligors for support, maintenance, or care have no ability to provide such support, maintenance, or care.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　特困人员供养的内容包括：  
(一)提供基本生活条件；  
(二)对生活不能自理的给予照料；  
(三)提供疾病治疗；  
(四)办理丧葬事宜。  
特困人员供养标准，由省、自治区、直辖市或者设区的市级人民政府确定、公布。  
特困人员供养应当与城乡居民基本养老保险、基本医疗保障、最低生活保障、孤儿基本生活保障等制度相衔接。

**Article 15** The content of support for persons in extreme difficulty includes:  
(1) providing basic living conditions;  
(2) providing care for those who cannot take care of themselves;  
(3) providing medical treatment for diseases;  
(4) handling funeral matters.  
The standard for support for persons in extreme difficulty shall be determined and published by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or of a city divided into districts.  
Support for persons in extreme difficulty shall be linked with systems such as basic old-age insurance for urban and rural residents, basic medical security, subsistence allowances, and basic living security for orphans.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　申请特困人员供养，由本人向户籍所在地的乡镇人民政府、街道办事处提出书面申请；本人申请有困难的，可以委托村民委员会、居民委员会代为提出申请。  
特困人员供养的审批程序适用[本办法第十一条](#art-11)规定。

**Article 16** An application for support as a person in extreme difficulty shall be submitted in writing by the person himself or herself to the people's government of the township or sub-district office where the household registration is located; if the person has difficulty in applying, he or she may entrust the villagers' committee or residents' committee to submit the application on his or her behalf.  
The approval procedure for support for persons in extreme difficulty shall be governed by the provisions of Article 11 of these Measures.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　乡镇人民政府、街道办事处应当及时了解掌握居民的生活情况，发现符合特困供养条件的人员，应当主动为其依法办理供养。

**Article 17** The people's governments of townships and sub-district offices shall promptly understand and grasp the living conditions of residents, and if they discover persons who meet the conditions for support as persons in extreme difficulty, they shall proactively handle the support for them according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　特困供养人员不再符合供养条件的，村民委员会、居民委员会或者供养服务机构应当告知乡镇人民政府、街道办事处，由乡镇人民政府、街道办事处审核并报县级人民政府民政部门核准后，终止供养并予以公示。

**Article 18** If a person receiving support as a person in extreme difficulty no longer meets the conditions for support, the villagers' committee, residents' committee, or support service institution shall inform the people's government of the township or sub-district office, which shall review the matter and report it to the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level for approval, after which the support shall be terminated and publicly announced.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　特困供养人员可以在当地的供养服务机构集中供养，也可以在家分散供养。特困供养人员可以自行选择供养形式。

**Article 19** Persons in extreme difficulty may receive centralized support in local support service institutions or receive decentralized support at home. Persons in extreme difficulty may choose the form of support on their own.

### 第四章　受灾人员救助

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家建立健全自然灾害救助制度，对基本生活受到自然灾害严重影响的人员，提供生活救助。  
自然灾害救助实行属地管理，分级负责。

**Article 20** The State shall establish and improve the natural disaster relief system, and provide living assistance to persons whose basic livelihood is severely affected by natural disasters.  
Natural disaster relief shall be subject to territorial management and graded responsibility.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　设区的市级以上人民政府和自然灾害多发、易发地区的县级人民政府应当根据自然灾害特点、居民人口数量和分布等情况，设立自然灾害救助物资储备库，保障自然灾害发生后救助物资的紧急供应。

**Article 21** The people's governments at or above the level of a city divided into districts, and the people's governments at the county level in areas where natural disasters are frequent or prone to occur, shall, based on the characteristics of natural disasters, the number and distribution of residents, and other factors, establish storage warehouses for natural disaster relief materials to ensure the emergency supply of relief materials after natural disasters occur.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　自然灾害发生后，县级以上人民政府或者人民政府的自然灾害救助应急综合协调机构应当根据情况紧急疏散、转移、安置受灾人员，及时为受灾人员提供必要的食品、饮用水、衣被、取暖、临时住所、医疗防疫等应急救助。

**Article 22** After a natural disaster occurs, the people's government at or above the county level or the comprehensive coordination agency for natural disaster relief emergency response of the people's government shall, based on the circumstances, urgently evacuate, transfer, and relocate the affected persons, and promptly provide emergency assistance to the affected persons, including necessary food, drinking water, clothing and bedding, heating, temporary shelter, medical treatment and epidemic prevention.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　灾情稳定后，受灾地区县级以上人民政府应当评估、核定并发布自然灾害损失情况。

**Article 23** After the disaster situation stabilizes, the people's government at or above the county level in the affected area shall assess, verify, and publish the natural disaster loss situation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　受灾地区人民政府应当在确保安全的前提下，对住房损毁严重的受灾人员进行过渡性安置。

**Article 24** The people's government in the affected area shall, on the premise of ensuring safety, provide transitional resettlement for affected persons whose houses have been severely damaged.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　自然灾害危险消除后，受灾地区人民政府应急管理等部门应当及时核实本行政区域内居民住房恢复重建补助对象，并给予资金、物资等救助。

**Article 25** After the danger of a natural disaster is eliminated, the emergency management department and other departments of the people's government in the affected area shall promptly verify the recipients of subsidies for the restoration and reconstruction of residential housing within their administrative areas, and provide assistance in the form of funds, materials, etc.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　自然灾害发生后，受灾地区人民政府应当为因当年冬寒或者次年春荒遇到生活困难的受灾人员提供基本生活救助。

**Article 26** After a natural disaster occurs, the people's government in the affected area shall provide basic living assistance to affected persons who encounter living difficulties due to the cold winter of the current year or the spring famine of the following year.

### 第五章　医疗救助

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家建立健全医疗救助制度，保障医疗救助对象获得基本医疗卫生服务。

**Article 27** The State shall establish and improve the medical assistance system to ensure that recipients of medical assistance obtain basic medical and health services.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　下列人员可以申请相关医疗救助：  
(一)最低生活保障家庭成员；  
(二)特困供养人员；  
(三)县级以上人民政府规定的其他特殊困难人员。

**Article 28** The following persons may apply for relevant medical assistance:  
(1) family members of recipients of subsistence allowances;  
(2) persons receiving support as persons in extreme difficulty;  
(3) other persons in special difficulties as specified by the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　医疗救助采取下列方式：  
(一)对救助对象参加城镇居民基本医疗保险或者新型农村合作医疗的个人缴费部分，给予补贴；  
(二)对救助对象经基本医疗保险、大病保险和其他补充医疗保险支付后，个人及其家庭难以承担的符合规定的基本医疗自负费用，给予补助。  
医疗救助标准，由县级以上人民政府按照经济社会发展水平和医疗救助资金情况确定、公布。

**Article 29** Medical assistance shall be provided in the following ways:  
(1) subsidies shall be given for the portion of personal contributions made by assistance recipients to participate in the basic medical insurance for urban residents or the new rural cooperative medical care;  
(2) subsidies shall be given for the out-of-pocket expenses for basic medical care that are in accordance with regulations and that are difficult for the individual and his or her family to bear after payment by basic medical insurance, serious illness insurance, and other supplementary medical insurance.  
The standard for medical assistance shall be determined and published by the people's government at or above the county level based on the level of economic and social development and the situation of medical assistance funds.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　申请医疗救助的，应当向乡镇人民政府、街道办事处提出，经审核、公示后，由县级人民政府医疗保障部门审批。最低生活保障家庭成员和特困供养人员的医疗救助，由县级人民政府医疗保障部门直接办理。

**Article 30** An application for medical assistance shall be submitted to the people's government of the township or sub-district office, and after review and public announcement, shall be approved by the medical security department of the people's government at the county level. The medical assistance for family members of recipients of subsistence allowances and persons receiving support as persons in extreme difficulty shall be directly handled by the medical security department of the people's government at the county level.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　县级以上人民政府应当建立健全医疗救助与基本医疗保险、大病保险相衔接的医疗费用结算机制，为医疗救助对象提供便捷服务。

**Article 31** People's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve a medical expense settlement mechanism that links medical assistance with basic medical insurance and serious illness insurance, so as to provide convenient services to recipients of medical assistance.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家建立疾病应急救助制度，对需要急救但身份不明或者无力支付急救费用的急重危伤病患者给予救助。符合规定的急救费用由疾病应急救助基金支付。  
疾病应急救助制度应当与其他医疗保障制度相衔接。

**Article 32** The State shall establish a disease emergency rescue system to provide assistance to patients with acute, severe, and critical injuries or illnesses who need emergency treatment but whose identity is unknown or who are unable to pay for emergency treatment expenses. The emergency treatment expenses that meet the regulations shall be paid by the disease emergency rescue fund.  
The disease emergency rescue system shall be linked with other medical security systems.

### 第六章　教育救助

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家对在义务教育阶段就学的最低生活保障家庭成员、特困供养人员，给予教育救助。  
对在高中教育(含中等职业教育)、普通高等教育阶段就学的最低生活保障家庭成员、特困供养人员，以及不能入学接受义务教育的残疾儿童，根据实际情况给予适当教育救助。

**Article 33** The State shall provide educational assistance to family members of recipients of subsistence allowances and persons receiving support as persons in extreme difficulty who are studying at the stage of compulsory education.  
Appropriate educational assistance shall be provided, based on actual circumstances, to family members of recipients of subsistence allowances and persons receiving support as persons in extreme difficulty who are studying at the stage of high school education (including secondary vocational education) or ordinary higher education, as well as to disabled children who cannot attend school to receive compulsory education.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　教育救助根据不同教育阶段需求，采取减免相关费用、发放助学金、给予生活补助、安排勤工助学等方式实施，保障教育救助对象基本学习、生活需求。

**Article 34** Educational assistance shall be implemented according to the needs of different educational stages, by means of reducing or exempting relevant fees, granting scholarships, providing living subsidies, arranging work-study programs, etc., so as to meet the basic learning and living needs of recipients of educational assistance.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　教育救助标准，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据经济社会发展水平和教育救助对象的基本学习、生活需求确定、公布。

**Article 35** The standard for educational assistance shall be determined and published by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government based on the level of economic and social development and the basic learning and living needs of the recipients of educational assistance.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　申请教育救助，应当按照国家有关规定向就读学校提出，按规定程序审核、确认后，由学校按照国家有关规定实施。

**Article 36** An application for educational assistance shall be submitted to the school where the applicant is studying in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and after review and confirmation according to the prescribed procedures, the school shall implement it in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第七章　住房救助

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家对符合规定标准的住房困难的最低生活保障家庭、分散供养的特困人员，给予住房救助。

**Article 37** The State shall provide housing assistance to families receiving subsistence allowances and persons in extreme difficulty receiving decentralized support who have housing difficulties meeting the prescribed standards.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　住房救助通过配租公共租赁住房、发放住房租赁补贴、农村危房改造等方式实施。

**Article 38** Housing assistance shall be implemented through the allocation of public rental housing, the granting of housing rental subsidies, the renovation of dilapidated rural houses, and other methods.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　住房困难标准和救助标准，由县级以上地方人民政府根据本行政区域经济社会发展水平、住房价格水平等因素确定、公布。

**Article 39** The housing difficulty standard and the assistance standard shall be determined and published by the local people's government at or above the county level based on factors such as the level of economic and social development and the level of housing prices in its administrative area.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　城镇家庭申请住房救助的，应当经由乡镇人民政府、街道办事处或者直接向县级人民政府住房保障部门提出，经县级人民政府民政部门审核家庭收入、财产状况和县级人民政府住房保障部门审核家庭住房状况并公示后，对符合申请条件的申请人，由县级人民政府住房保障部门优先给予保障。  
农村家庭申请住房救助的，按照县级以上人民政府有关规定执行。

**Article 40** An urban family applying for housing assistance shall submit the application through the people's government of the township or sub-district office, or directly to the housing security department of the people's government at the county level. After the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level verifies the family's income and property status, and the housing security department of the people's government at the county level verifies the family's housing status and conducts a public announcement, the housing security department of the people's government at the county level shall give priority to providing assistance to applicants who meet the application conditions.  
A rural family applying for housing assistance shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　各级人民政府按照国家规定通过财政投入、用地供应等措施为实施住房救助提供保障。

**Article 41** People's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with State provisions, provide guarantees for the implementation of housing assistance through measures such as financial investment and land supply.

### 第八章　就业救助

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家对最低生活保障家庭中有劳动能力并处于失业状态的成员，通过贷款贴息、社会保险补贴、岗位补贴、培训补贴、费用减免、公益性岗位安置等办法，给予就业救助。

**Article 42** The State shall provide employment assistance to members of families receiving subsistence allowances who have the ability to work and are in a state of unemployment, through methods such as loan interest subsidies, social insurance subsidies, job subsidies, training subsidies, fee reductions or exemptions, and placement in public welfare positions.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　最低生活保障家庭有劳动能力的成员均处于失业状态的，县级以上地方人民政府应当采取有针对性的措施，确保该家庭至少有一人就业。

**Article 43** If all members of a family receiving subsistence allowances who have the ability to work are in a state of unemployment, the local people's government at or above the county level shall take targeted measures to ensure that at least one member of the family is employed.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　申请就业救助的，应当向住所地街道、社区公共就业服务机构提出，公共就业服务机构核实后予以登记，并免费提供就业岗位信息、职业介绍、职业指导等就业服务。

**Article 44** An application for employment assistance shall be submitted to the public employment service agency of the sub-district or community where the applicant resides. The public employment service agency, after verification, shall register the applicant and provide, free of charge, employment services such as job information, job referral, and vocational guidance.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　最低生活保障家庭中有劳动能力但未就业的成员，应当接受人力资源社会保障等有关部门介绍的工作；无正当理由，连续3次拒绝接受介绍的与其健康状况、劳动能力等相适应的工作的，县级人民政府民政部门应当决定减发或者停发其本人的最低生活保障金。

**Article 45** A member of a family receiving subsistence allowances who has the ability to work but is not employed shall accept the work recommended by the human resources and social security department or other relevant department; if, without justifiable reasons, he or she refuses to accept the recommended work that is suitable for his or her health condition and ability to work for three consecutive times, the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level shall decide to reduce or stop the payment of his or her subsistence allowance.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　吸纳就业救助对象的用人单位，按照国家有关规定享受社会保险补贴、税收优惠、小额担保贷款等就业扶持政策。

**Article 46** Employer units that absorb recipients of employment assistance shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, enjoy employment support policies such as social insurance subsidies, tax preferences, and small guaranteed loans.

### 第九章　临时救助

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家对因火灾、交通事故等意外事件，家庭成员突发重大疾病等原因，导致基本生活暂时出现严重困难的家庭，或者因生活必需支出突然增加超出家庭承受能力，导致基本生活暂时出现严重困难的最低生活保障家庭，以及遭遇其他特殊困难的家庭，给予临时救助。

**Article 47** The State shall provide temporary assistance to families that experience temporary severe difficulties in basic livelihood due to accidents such as fires or traffic accidents, sudden major illnesses of family members, or other reasons; to families receiving subsistence allowances that experience temporary severe difficulties in basic livelihood due to a sudden increase in necessary living expenses beyond the family's affordability; and to families encountering other special difficulties.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　申请临时救助的，应当向乡镇人民政府、街道办事处提出，经审核、公示后，由县级人民政府民政部门审批；救助金额较小的，县级人民政府民政部门可以委托乡镇人民政府、街道办事处审批。情况紧急的，可以按照规定简化审批手续。

**Article 48** An application for temporary assistance shall be submitted to the people's government of the township or sub-district office, and after review and public announcement, shall be approved by the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level; if the assistance amount is small, the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level may entrust the people's government of the township or sub-district office with the approval. In case of emergency, the approval procedures may be simplified in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　临时救助的具体事项、标准，由县级以上地方人民政府确定、公布。

**Article 49** The specific matters and standards for temporary assistance shall be determined and published by the local people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家对生活无着的流浪、乞讨人员提供临时食宿、急病救治、协助返回等救助。

**Article 50** The State shall provide assistance to vagrants and beggars without a means of living, including temporary food and accommodation, emergency medical treatment, and assistance in returning to their places of origin.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　公安机关和其他有关行政机关的工作人员在执行公务时发现流浪、乞讨人员的，应当告知其向救助管理机构求助。对其中的残疾人、未成年人、老年人和行动不便的其他人员，应当引导、护送到救助管理机构；对突发急病人员，应当立即通知急救机构进行救治。

**Article 51** When staff members of public security organs and other relevant administrative organs discover vagrants or beggars in the course of performing their official duties, they shall inform them to seek help from assistance management institutions. For disabled persons, minors, elderly persons, and other persons with mobility difficulties among them, they shall guide and escort them to assistance management institutions; for persons with sudden acute illnesses, they shall immediately notify emergency medical institutions to provide treatment.

### 第十章　社会力量参与

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家鼓励单位和个人等社会力量通过捐赠、设立帮扶项目、创办服务机构、提供志愿服务等方式，参与社会救助。

**Article 52** The State encourages social forces, including units and individuals, to participate in social assistance through methods such as donations, establishing assistance projects, establishing service institutions, and providing voluntary services.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　社会力量参与社会救助，按照国家有关规定享受财政补贴、税收优惠、费用减免等政策。

**Article 53** Social forces participating in social assistance shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, enjoy policies such as financial subsidies, tax preferences, and fee reductions or exemptions.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　县级以上地方人民政府可以将社会救助中的具体服务事项通过委托、承包、采购等方式，向社会力量购买服务。

**Article 54** Local people's governments at or above the county level may purchase specific service items in social assistance from social forces through means such as entrustment, contracting, and procurement.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　县级以上地方人民政府应当发挥社会工作服务机构和社会工作者作用，为社会救助对象提供社会融入、能力提升、心理疏导等专业服务。

**Article 55** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall give full play to the role of social work service institutions and social workers, and provide professional services such as social integration, capacity building, and psychological counseling to recipients of social assistance.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　社会救助管理部门及相关机构应当建立社会力量参与社会救助的机制和渠道，提供社会救助项目、需求信息，为社会力量参与社会救助创造条件、提供便利。

**Article 56** Social assistance administrative departments and relevant institutions shall establish mechanisms and channels for social forces to participate in social assistance, provide information on social assistance projects and needs, and create conditions and provide conveniences for social forces to participate in social assistance.

### 第十一章　监督管理

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　县级以上人民政府及其社会救助管理部门应当加强对社会救助工作的监督检查，完善相关监督管理制度。

**Article 57** People's governments at or above the county level and their social assistance administrative departments shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of social assistance work, and improve the relevant supervision and management systems.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　申请或者已获得社会救助的家庭，应当按照规定如实申报家庭收入状况、财产状况。  
县级以上人民政府民政部门根据申请或者已获得社会救助家庭的请求、委托，可以通过户籍管理、税务、社会保险、不动产登记、工商登记、住房公积金管理、车船管理等单位和银行、保险、证券等金融机构，代为查询、核对其家庭收入状况、财产状况；有关单位和金融机构应当予以配合。  
县级以上人民政府民政部门应当建立申请和已获得社会救助家庭经济状况信息核对平台，为审核认定社会救助对象提供依据。

**Article 58** Families applying for or already receiving social assistance shall truthfully declare their family income status and property status in accordance with the provisions.  
The civil affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level may, at the request or upon the entrustment of a family applying for or already receiving social assistance, inquire about and verify the family's income status and property status through units such as household registration management, taxation, social insurance, real estate registration, business registration, housing provident fund management, vehicle and vessel management, and financial institutions such as banks, insurance companies, and securities companies; the relevant units and financial institutions shall cooperate.  
The civil affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level shall establish an information verification platform for the economic status of families applying for and already receiving social assistance, so as to provide a basis for reviewing and determining recipients of social assistance.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　县级以上人民政府社会救助管理部门和乡镇人民政府、街道办事处在履行社会救助职责过程中，可以查阅、记录、复制与社会救助事项有关的资料，询问与社会救助事项有关的单位、个人，要求其对相关情况作出说明，提供相关证明材料。有关单位、个人应当如实提供。

**Article 59** In the course of performing their social assistance duties, the social assistance administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level and the people's governments of townships and sub-district offices may consult, record, and copy materials related to social assistance matters, inquire of units and individuals related to social assistance matters, and request them to explain the relevant circumstances and provide relevant evidentiary materials. The relevant units and individuals shall truthfully provide them.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　申请社会救助，应当按照本办法的规定提出；申请人难以确定社会救助管理部门的，可以先向社会救助经办机构或者县级人民政府民政部门求助。社会救助经办机构或者县级人民政府民政部门接到求助后，应当及时办理或者转交其他社会救助管理部门办理。  
乡镇人民政府、街道办事处应当建立统一受理社会救助申请的窗口，及时受理、转办申请事项。

**Article 60** An application for social assistance shall be submitted in accordance with the provisions of these Measures; if the applicant has difficulty in determining the social assistance administrative department, he or she may first seek help from the social assistance handling agency or the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level. After receiving the request for help, the social assistance handling agency or the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level shall promptly handle the matter or transfer it to another social assistance administrative department for handling.  
The people's governments of townships and sub-district offices shall establish a unified window for receiving social assistance applications, and promptly receive and transfer application matters.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　履行社会救助职责的工作人员对在社会救助工作中知悉的公民个人信息，除按照规定应当公示的信息外，应当予以保密。

**Article 61** Staff members performing social assistance duties shall keep confidential the personal information of citizens learned in the course of social assistance work, except for information that shall be publicly announced in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　县级以上人民政府及其社会救助管理部门应当通过报刊、广播、电视、互联网等媒体，宣传社会救助法律、法规和政策。  
县级人民政府及其社会救助管理部门应当通过公共查阅室、资料索取点、信息公告栏等便于公众知晓的途径，及时公开社会救助资金、物资的管理和使用等情况，接受社会监督。

**Article 62** People's governments at or above the county level and their social assistance administrative departments shall publicize social assistance laws, regulations, and policies through media such as newspapers, radio, television, and the Internet.  
People's governments at the county level and their social assistance administrative departments shall, through channels easily accessible to the public such as public reading rooms, material collection points, and information bulletin boards, promptly disclose the management and use of social assistance funds and materials, and accept social supervision.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　履行社会救助职责的工作人员行使职权，应当接受社会监督。  
任何单位、个人有权对履行社会救助职责的工作人员在社会救助工作中的违法行为进行举报、投诉。受理举报、投诉的机关应当及时核实、处理。

**Article 63** Staff members performing social assistance duties shall, when exercising their powers, accept social supervision.  
Any unit or individual shall have the right to report or complain about the illegal acts of staff members performing social assistance duties in the course of social assistance work. The organ that receives the report or complaint shall promptly verify and handle the matter.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　县级以上人民政府财政部门、审计机关依法对社会救助资金、物资的筹集、分配、管理和使用实施监督。

**Article 64** The financial departments and audit organs of people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, supervise the raising, allocation, management, and use of social assistance funds and materials.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　申请或者已获得社会救助的家庭或者人员，对社会救助管理部门作出的具体行政行为不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 65** If a family or person applying for or already receiving social assistance is dissatisfied with a specific administrative act taken by the social assistance administrative department, they may, according to law, apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate an administrative action.

### 第十二章　法律责任

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本办法规定，有下列情形之一的，由上级行政机关或者监察机关责令改正；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
(一)对符合申请条件的救助申请不予受理的；  
(二)对符合救助条件的救助申请不予批准的；  
(三)对不符合救助条件的救助申请予以批准的；  
(四)泄露在工作中知悉的公民个人信息，造成后果的；  
(五)丢失、篡改接受社会救助款物、服务记录等数据的；  
(六)不按照规定发放社会救助资金、物资或者提供相关服务的；  
(七)在履行社会救助职责过程中有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为的。

**Article 66** If any of the following circumstances occurs in violation of the provisions of these Measures, the superior administrative organ or the supervisory organ shall order the correction; and the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to accept an assistance application that meets the application conditions;  
(2) failing to approve an assistance application that meets the assistance conditions;  
(3) approving an assistance application that does not meet the assistance conditions;  
(4) disclosing personal information of citizens learned in the course of work, causing consequences;  
(5) losing or tampering with data such as records of receipt of social assistance funds, materials, and services;  
(6) failing to distribute social assistance funds or materials or provide relevant services in accordance with the provisions; or  
(7) engaging in other acts of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain in the course of performing social assistance duties.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　违反本办法规定，截留、挤占、挪用、私分社会救助资金、物资的，由有关部门责令追回；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 67** If, in violation of the provisions of these Measures, social assistance funds or materials are intercepted, misappropriated, diverted, or privately distributed, the relevant department shall order the recovery; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; and the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　采取虚报、隐瞒、伪造等手段，骗取社会救助资金、物资或者服务的，由有关部门决定停止社会救助，责令退回非法获取的救助资金、物资，可以处非法获取的救助款额或者物资价值1倍以上3倍以下的罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 68** If, by means of false reporting, concealment, forgery, or other means, a person fraudulently obtains social assistance funds, materials, or services, the relevant department shall decide to stop the social assistance, order the return of the illegally obtained assistance funds or materials, and may impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the amount of the illegally obtained assistance funds or the value of the materials; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration punishment shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　违反本办法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 69** If a violation of the provisions of these Measures constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十三章　附则

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　本办法自2014年5月1日起施行。

**Article 70** These Measures shall come into force as of May 1, 2014.
