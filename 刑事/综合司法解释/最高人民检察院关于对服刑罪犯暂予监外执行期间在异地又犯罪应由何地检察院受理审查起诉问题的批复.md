# 最高人民检察院关于对服刑罪犯暂予监外执行期间在异地又犯罪应由何地检察院受理审查起诉问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民检察院  
**发文字号**：高检发释字〔1998〕5号  
**公布日期**：1998-11-26  
**生效日期**：1998-11-26

> 第九次会议通过） 高检发释字〔1998〕5号 四川省人民检察院： 你院川检发研〔1998〕12号《关于服刑犯罪暂予监外执行期间在异地又犯罪应由何地检察院受理审查起诉的问题的请示》收悉。经研究，批复如下： 对罪犯在暂予监外执行期间在异地犯罪，如果罪行是在犯罪地被发现、罪犯是在犯罪地被捕获的，由犯罪地人民检察院审查起诉；如果案件由罪犯暂予监外执行地人民法院审判更为适宜的，也可以由犯罪暂予监外执行地的人民检察院审查起诉；如果罪行是在暂予监外执行的情形消失，罪犯被继续收监执行剩余刑期期间发现的，由罪犯服刑地的人民检察院审查起诉。 最高人民检察院 1998年11月26日

对罪犯在暂予监外执行期间在异地犯罪，如果罪行是在犯罪地被发现、罪犯是在犯罪地被捕获的，由犯罪地人民检察院审查起诉；如果案件由罪犯暂予监外执行地人民法院审判更为适宜的，也可以由犯罪暂予监外执行地的人民检察院审查起诉；如果罪行是在暂予监外执行的情形消失，罪犯被继续收监执行剩余刑期期间发现的，由罪犯服刑地的人民检察院审查起诉。  
最高人民检察院  
1998年11月26日

**Article 1** Interpretation No. 5 [1998] of the Supreme People's Procuratorate  
  
The People's Procuratorate of Sichuan Province:  
  
Your Request for Instructions on the Issue of Which Local Procuratorate Should Accept and Review for Prosecution the Crime Committed in a Different Place by a Prisoner Serving Sentence while under Temporary Execution of Sentence Outside Prison (Chuan Jian Fa Yan [1998] No. 12) has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
  
With respect to a prisoner who commits a crime in a different place while under temporary execution of sentence outside prison, if the crime is discovered at the place where it was committed and the prisoner is captured at the place where the crime was committed, the People's Procuratorate of the place where the crime was committed shall review the case for prosecution; if the case is more appropriately tried by the People's Court of the place where the prisoner was under temporary execution of sentence outside prison, the People's Procuratorate of the place where the crime was committed and where the prisoner was under temporary execution of sentence outside prison may also review the case for prosecution; if the crime is discovered after the circumstances for temporary execution of sentence outside prison have ceased to exist and the prisoner is recommitted to prison to serve the remaining sentence, the People's Procuratorate of the place where the prisoner serves the sentence shall review the case for prosecution.  
  
Supreme People's Procuratorate  
November 26, 1998
