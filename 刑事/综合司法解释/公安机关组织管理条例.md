# 公安机关组织管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2006-11-13  
**生效日期**：2007-01-01

> (2006年11月1日国务院第154次常务会议通过　2006年11月13日中华人民共和国国务院令第479号公布　自2007年1月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范公安机关组织管理，保障公安机关及其人民警察依法履行职责，根据《中华人民共和国公务员法》、《中华人民共和国人民警察法》，制定本条例。 第二条　公安机关是人民民主专政的重要工具，人民警察是武装性质的国家治安行政力量和刑事司法力量，承担依法预防、制止和惩治违法犯罪活动，保护人民，服务经济社会发展，维护国家安全，维护社会治安秩序的职责。 第三条　公安部在国务院领导下，主管全国的公安工作，是全国公安工作的领导、指挥机关。 县级以上地方人民政府公安机关在本级人民政府领导下，负责本行政区域的公安工作，是本行政区域公安工作的领导、指挥机关。 第四条　公安机关实行行政首长负责制。 第二章　公安机关的设置 第五条　县级以上人民政府公安机关依照法律、行政法规规定的权限和程序设置。 第六条　设区的市公安局根据工作需要设置公安分局。市、县、自治县公安局根据工作需要设置公安派出所。 公安分局和公安派出所的设立、撤销，按照规定的权限和程序审批。 第七条　县级以上地方人民政府公安机关和公安分局内设机构分为综合管理机构和执法勤务机构。 执法勤务机构实行队建制，称为总队、支队、大队、中队。 第八条　县级以上地方人民政府公安机关和公安分局内设机构的设立、撤销，按照国家规定的权限和程序审批。 第九条　看守所、拘留所、戒毒所、收容教育所依照法律、行政法规的规定设置。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范公安机关组织管理，保障公安机关及其人民警察依法履行职责，根据《中华人民共和国公务员法》、《中华人民共和国人民警察法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on Civil Servants and the People's Police Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of regulating the organization and management of public security organs and ensuring that the public security organs and their people's police perform their duties according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　公安机关是人民民主专政的重要工具，人民警察是武装性质的国家治安行政力量和刑事司法力量，承担依法预防、制止和惩治违法犯罪活动，保护人民，服务经济社会发展，维护国家安全，维护社会治安秩序的职责。

**Article 2** The public security organs are important tools of the people's democratic dictatorship, and the people's police are the armed public security administrative forces and criminal judicial forces of the state, assuming the responsibilities of preventing, stopping and punishing illegal and criminal activities according to law, protecting the people, serving economic and social development, maintaining national security, and maintaining public security order.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　公安部在国务院领导下，主管全国的公安工作，是全国公安工作的领导、指挥机关。  
县级以上地方人民政府公安机关在本级人民政府领导下，负责本行政区域的公安工作，是本行政区域公安工作的领导、指挥机关。

**Article 3** The Ministry of Public Security, under the leadership of the State Council, shall take charge of the public security work nationwide and shall be the leading and commanding organ for the national public security work.  
The public security organs of the local people's governments at or above the county level, under the leadership of the people's government at the same level, shall be responsible for the public security work within their respective administrative regions and shall be the leading and commanding organs for the public security work in those administrative regions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　公安机关实行行政首长负责制。

**Article 4** The public security organs shall practice a system of responsibility of the chief administrative officer.

### 第二章　公安机关的设置

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府公安机关依照法律、行政法规规定的权限和程序设置。

**Article 5** The public security organs of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be established in accordance with the limits of authority and procedures prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　设区的市公安局根据工作需要设置公安分局。市、县、自治县公安局根据工作需要设置公安派出所。  
公安分局和公安派出所的设立、撤销，按照规定的权限和程序审批。

**Article 6** The public security bureau of a city divided into districts may establish public security sub-bureaus as required by work. The public security bureaus of cities, counties and autonomous counties may establish police stations as required by work.  
The establishment or dissolution of a public security sub-bureau or a police station shall be subject to examination and approval in accordance with the prescribed limits of authority and procedures.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上地方人民政府公安机关和公安分局内设机构分为综合管理机构和执法勤务机构。  
执法勤务机构实行队建制，称为总队、支队、大队、中队。

**Article 7** The internal organs of the public security organs of the local people's governments at or above the county level and the public security sub-bureaus shall be divided into comprehensive management organs and law enforcement and service organs.  
The law enforcement and service organs shall practice a unit formation system, known as general unit, unit, brigade, and squadron.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上地方人民政府公安机关和公安分局内设机构的设立、撤销，按照国家规定的权限和程序审批。

**Article 8** The establishment or dissolution of the internal organs of the public security organs of the local people's governments at or above the county level and the public security sub-bureaus shall be subject to examination and approval in accordance with the limits of authority and procedures prescribed by the state.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　看守所、拘留所、戒毒所、收容教育所依照法律、行政法规的规定设置。

**Article 9** Detention houses, detention centers, drug rehabilitation centers, and shelter and education centers shall be established in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.

### 第三章　公安机关人民警察职务

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　公安机关人民警察职务分为警官职务、警员职务和警务技术职务。

**Article 10** The posts of the people's police in public security organs shall be divided into police officer posts, police staff posts, and police technical posts.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　公安机关履行警务指挥职责的人民警察实行警官职务序列。  
公安机关领导成员和内设综合管理机构警官职务由高至低为：省部级正职、省部级副职、厅局级正职、厅局级副职、县处级正职、县处级副职、乡科级正职、乡科级副职。  
公安机关内设执法勤务机构警官职务由高至低为：总队长、副总队长、支队长、副支队长、大队长、副大队长、中队长、副中队长。  
县级以上地方人民政府公安机关派出机构、内设执法勤务机构和不设区的市、县、自治县公安局根据工作需要，可以设置主管政治工作的政治委员、教导员、指导员等警官职务。

**Article 11** The people's police performing police command duties in public security organs shall practice a sequence of police officer posts.  
The police officer posts of the leading members of public security organs and the internal comprehensive management organs shall, from high to low, be: provincial-ministerial principal, provincial-ministerial deputy, departmental-bureau principal, departmental-bureau deputy, county-division principal, county-division deputy, township-section principal, and township-section deputy.  
The police officer posts of the internal law enforcement and service organs of public security organs shall, from high to low, be: chief of general unit, deputy chief of general unit, chief of unit, deputy chief of unit, chief of brigade, deputy chief of brigade, chief of squadron, and deputy chief of squadron.  
The dispatched organs of the public security organs of the local people's governments at or above the county level, the internal law enforcement and service organs, and the public security bureaus of cities not divided into districts, counties, and autonomous counties may, as required by work, set up police officer posts such as political commissars, political instructors, and political guides in charge of political work.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　公安机关履行警务执行职责的人民警察实行警员职务序列。  
公安机关及其内设综合管理机构警员职务由高至低为：巡视员、副巡视员、调研员、副调研员、主任科员、副主任科员、科员、办事员。  
公安机关内设执法勤务机构警员职务由高至低为：一级警长、二级警长、三级警长、四级警长、一级警员、二级警员、三级警员。

**Article 12** The people's police performing police enforcement duties in public security organs shall practice a sequence of police staff posts.  
The police staff posts of public security organs and their internal comprehensive management organs shall, from high to low, be: inspector, deputy inspector, researcher, deputy researcher, principal staff member, deputy principal staff member, staff member, and clerk.  
The police staff posts of the internal law enforcement and service organs of public security organs shall, from high to low, be: first-level police chief, second-level police chief, third-level police chief, fourth-level police chief, first-level police staff, second-level police staff, and third-level police staff.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　公安机关从事警务技术工作的人民警察实行警务技术职务序列。  
警务技术职务的设置，按照国家规定执行。

**Article 13** The people's police engaged in police technical work in public security organs shall practice a sequence of police technical posts.  
The establishment of police technical posts shall be implemented in accordance with the state provisions.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　公安机关人民警察的级别，根据所任职务及其德才表现、工作实绩和资历确定。

**Article 14** The ranks of the people's police of public security organs shall be determined on the basis of their posts and their moral integrity, ability, work performance, and seniority.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　公安机关人民警察职务与级别的对应关系，由国务院另行规定。

**Article 15** The corresponding relationship between the posts and ranks of the people's police of public security organs shall be separately prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　公安机关人民警察任职，应当符合国家规定的任职资格条件。

**Article 16** The appointment of the people's police of public security organs shall meet the qualifications and conditions prescribed by the state.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上地方人民政府公安机关正职领导职务的提名，应当事先征得上一级公安机关的同意。  
县级以上地方人民政府公安机关副职领导职务的任免，应当事先征求上一级公安机关的意见。

**Article 17** The nomination of the principal leading post of a public security organ of a local people's government at or above the county level shall obtain the consent of the public security organ at the next higher level in advance.  
The appointment or removal of the deputy leading post of a public security organ of a local people's government at or above the county level shall seek the opinion of the public security organ at the next higher level in advance.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　公安机关内设机构警官职务、警员职务的任免，由本公安机关按照干部管理权限决定或者报批。  
公安分局领导成员职务以及公安派出所警官职务、警员职务的任免，由派出公安分局、公安派出所的公安机关决定。

**Article 18** The appointment or removal of police officer posts and police staff posts of the internal organs of a public security organ shall be decided by the public security organ or submitted for approval in accordance with the limits of authority for cadre management.  
The appointment or removal of the posts of leading members of a public security sub-bureau and the police officer posts and police staff posts of a police station shall be decided by the public security organ that dispatched the public security sub-bureau or the police station.

### 第四章　公安机关的编制和经费

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　公安机关人民警察使用的国家行政编制，实行专项管理。

**Article 19** The state administrative establishment used by the people's police of public security organs shall be subject to special management.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　公安部根据工作需要，向国务院机构编制管理机关提出公安机关编制的规划和调整编制的意见，由国务院机构编制管理机关审核，按照规定的权限和程序审批。

**Article 20** The Ministry of Public Security shall, as required by work, submit to the state organ for the administration of organizational establishment under the State Council the plans for the organizational establishment of public security organs and opinions on adjusting the organizational establishment, which shall be examined by the state organ for the administration of organizational establishment under the State Council and submitted for examination and approval in accordance with the prescribed limits of authority and procedures.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据工作需要，可以向国务院机构编制管理机关提出调整公安机关编制的申请。  
国务院机构编制管理机关对省、自治区、直辖市人民政府调整公安机关编制的申请，征求公安部意见后进行审核，按照规定的权限和程序审批。

**Article 21** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, as required by work, submit to the state organ for the administration of organizational establishment under the State Council an application for adjusting the organizational establishment of public security organs.  
The state organ for the administration of organizational establishment under the State Council shall, after soliciting the opinions of the Ministry of Public Security, examine the application of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government for adjusting the organizational establishment of public security organs, and submit it for examination and approval in accordance with the prescribed limits of authority and procedures.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　公安机关根据工作需要，经中央公务员主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市公务员主管部门批准，可以对专业性较强的职位和辅助性职位实行聘任制。但是，对公安执法职位或者涉及国家秘密的职位，不实行聘任制。

**Article 22** Public security organs may, as required by work and upon approval of the central civil service competent department or the civil service competent departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, practice a contract system for posts requiring relatively high professional skills and auxiliary posts. However, the contract system shall not be practiced for public security law enforcement posts or posts involving state secrets.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　公安机关应当按照国家规定，将各项罚没收入和行政事业性收费收入全额上缴财政。  
县级以上人民政府按照国家规定的经费项目和标准，将公安机关经费列入财政预算，实行全额保障，并对经济困难地区的公安工作给予必要的经费支持。

**Article 23** Public security organs shall turn over all confiscated revenue and administrative and institutional fee revenue to the fiscal authorities in accordance with state provisions.  
The people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the state-prescribed items and standards for funds, include the funds of public security organs in the fiscal budgets, ensure full funding, and provide necessary financial support for public security work in economically difficult areas.

### 第五章　公安机关人民警察管理

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　公安机关录用人民警察实行考试录用制度。  
公安机关录用的人民警察应当符合国家规定的条件。

**Article 24** The recruitment of people's police by public security organs shall practice a system of examination and recruitment.  
The people's police recruited by public security organs shall meet the conditions prescribed by the state.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　公安部机关及其实行公务员制度的直属机构人民警察录用考试，由中央公务员主管部门负责组织。  
县级以上地方人民政府公安机关人民警察录用考试，由省、自治区、直辖市公务员主管部门负责组织。县级以上地方人民政府公安机关按照国家规定，承担相应的录用工作。

**Article 25** The recruitment examinations for the people's police of the Ministry of Public Security and its directly affiliated organs practicing the civil servant system shall be organized by the central civil service competent department.  
The recruitment examinations for the people's police of the public security organs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be organized by the civil service competent departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government. The public security organs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall undertake the corresponding recruitment work in accordance with state provisions.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　调任、转任到公安机关担任人民警察职务的，应当符合担任公安机关人民警察的条件和拟任职位所要求的资格条件。  
公安机关应当对调任、转任人选进行严格考察，并按照管理权限审批。必要时，可以对调任人选进行考试。

**Article 26** Persons transferred or rotated to public security organs to take up posts as people's police shall meet the conditions for serving as people's police of public security organs and the qualifications required for the proposed posts.  
Public security organs shall conduct strict examinations of persons to be transferred or rotated and shall submit them for approval in accordance with the limits of authority for management. When necessary, examinations may be conducted for persons to be transferred.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　公安机关人民警察实行警衔制度。公安机关授予警衔的人员应当是使用国家专项编制的在职人员。

**Article 27** The people's police of public security organs shall practice a system of police ranks. The persons to whom public security organs confer police ranks shall be active personnel using the state's special organizational establishment.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　公安机关按照管理权限对人民警察进行考核。  
考核结果作为调整公安机关人民警察职务、级别、工资以及辞退、奖励、培训的依据。

**Article 28** Public security organs shall appraise the people's police in accordance with the limits of authority for management.  
The appraisal results shall serve as the basis for adjusting the posts, ranks, and salaries of the people's police of public security organs, and for their discharge, reward, and training.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　公安机关人民警察应当经过公安院校等人民警察培训机构培训并考试、考核合格，方可任职、晋升职务、授予警衔、晋升警衔。  
公安机关应当组织人民警察接受国家规定的培训。

**Article 29** The people's police of public security organs shall undergo training at people's police training institutions such as public security colleges and pass examinations and appraisals before they may take office, be promoted to higher posts, be conferred police ranks, or be promoted in police ranks.  
Public security organs shall organize the people's police to receive training prescribed by the state.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　公安机关人民警察有下列情形之一的，应当予以辞退：  
(一)在年度考核中，连续两年被确定为不称职的；  
(二)不胜任现职工作，又不接受其他安排的；  
(三)因所在公安机关调整、撤销、合并或者缩减编制员额需要调整工作，本人拒绝合理安排的；  
(四)不履行人民警察义务，不遵守人民警察纪律，经教育仍无转变，不适合继续在公安机关工作，又不宜给予开除处分的；  
(五)旷工或者因公外出、请假期满无正当理由逾期不归连续超过15天，或者1年内累计超过30天的。

**Article 30** The people's police of public security organs shall be discharged under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) having been rated as incompetent in the annual appraisal for two consecutive years;  
(2) being incompetent for the current post and refusing other assignments;  
(3) being required to adjust work due to the readjustment, dissolution, merger or reduction of the establishment of the public security organ in which they work, and refusing a reasonable arrangement;  
(4) failing to fulfill the obligations of the people's police, failing to observe the discipline of the people's police, and showing no improvement after education, thus unsuitable to continue working in the public security organ, but not appropriate to be punished by dismissal;  
(5) being absent from work without leave or failing to return to work within the time limit after official travel or leave without legitimate reason for more than 15 consecutive days, or for a cumulative total of more than 30 days within a year.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　公安机关人民警察有下列情形之一的，不得辞退：  
(一)因公致残，被确认丧失或者部分丧失工作能力的；  
(二)患病或者负伤，在规定的医疗期内的；  
(三)女性人民警察在孕期、产假、哺乳期内的；  
(四)法律、行政法规规定的其他不得辞退的情形。

**Article 31** The people's police of public security organs shall not be discharged under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) having been disabled in the line of duty and confirmed to have lost or partially lost the ability to work;  
(2) being ill or injured and within the prescribed medical treatment period;  
(3) female people's police being during pregnancy, maternity leave, or nursing period;  
(4) other circumstances where discharge is prohibited by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　公安机关人民警察个人或者集体在工作中表现突出，有显著成绩和特殊贡献的，应当根据国家规定给予奖励。  
奖励分为：嘉奖、记三等功、记二等功、记一等功、授予荣誉称号。  
对受奖励的公安机关人民警察，可以根据国家规定提前晋升警衔，并给予一次性奖金或者其他待遇。  
拟以国务院名义授予荣誉称号的，由人事部审核后报国务院审批；拟授予全国公安系统一级英雄模范称号的，由人事部会同公安部审批；拟授予全国公安系统二级英雄模范称号的，由公安部依据国家有关规定审批。

**Article 32** Individual people's police or collectives of public security organs that have shown outstanding performance and made remarkable achievements and special contributions in work shall be given rewards in accordance with state provisions.  
The rewards shall be divided into: commendation, recording of third-class merit, recording of second-class merit, recording of first-class merit, and conferment of honorary title.  
For the rewarded people's police of public security organs, police ranks may be promoted ahead of schedule in accordance with state provisions, and a lump-sum bonus or other benefits may be granted.  
Where it is proposed to confer an honorary title in the name of the State Council, the Ministry of Personnel shall examine and submit it to the State Council for approval; where it is proposed to confer the title of first-class hero model of the national public security system, the Ministry of Personnel shall examine and approve it jointly with the Ministry of Public Security; where it is proposed to confer the title of second-class hero model of the national public security system, the Ministry of Public Security shall approve it in accordance with the relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　公安机关人民警察违法违纪的，应当根据国家规定给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
处分分为：警告、记过、记大过、降级、撤职、开除。  
对受处分的公安机关人民警察，根据国家规定降低警衔或者取消警衔。  
对公安机关人民警察的处分由任免机关或者监察机关决定。

**Article 33** Where a people's police of a public security organ violates laws or disciplines, a sanction shall be given in accordance with state provisions; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
The sanctions shall be divided into: warning, recording of a demerit, recording of a serious demerit, demotion, removal from post, and dismissal.  
For the people's police of public security organs who have been sanctioned, police ranks shall be demoted or cancelled in accordance with state provisions.  
The sanction on a people's police of a public security organ shall be decided by the appointing and removing organ or the supervisory organ.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　公安机关人民警察对涉及本人的人事处理决定不服，或者认为有关部门及其领导人员侵犯其合法权益的，可以依法申请复核，提出申诉或者控告。

**Article 34** Where a people's police of a public security organ disagrees with a personnel decision concerning him or her, or believes that a relevant department or its leading personnel has infringed upon his or her lawful rights and interests, he or she may, according to law, apply for review, lodge a complaint, or file an accusation.

### 第六章　公安机关人民警察待遇

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　公安机关人民警察享受国家规定的符合其职业特点的工资待遇。

**Article 35** The people's police of public security organs shall enjoy salary benefits prescribed by the state that are commensurate with the characteristics of their profession.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　公安机关人民警察实行国家规定的保险制度，保障其在退休、患病、工伤、生育、失业等情况下获得帮助和补偿。

**Article 36** The people's police of public security organs shall practice an insurance system prescribed by the state, ensuring that they receive assistance and compensation under circumstances such as retirement, illness, work-related injury, childbirth, and unemployment.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　公安机关人民警察因公致残的，应当享受必要的治疗、康复，其中被评定残疾的人员享受国家规定的抚恤和优待。  
公安机关人民警察因公牺牲或者病故的，其家属享受国家规定的抚恤和优待。

**Article 37** Where a people's police of a public security organ is disabled in the line of duty, he or she shall enjoy necessary treatment and rehabilitation, and those rated as disabled shall enjoy the pensions and preferential treatment prescribed by the state.  
Where a people's police of a public security organ dies in the line of duty or dies of illness, his or her family members shall enjoy the pensions and preferential treatment prescribed by the state.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　公安机关人民警察实行国家规定的工时制度和休假制度。  
公安机关人民警察在法定工作日之外工作的，应当补休；不能补休的，应当给予补助，具体办法由人事部会同财政部规定。

**Article 38** The people's police of public security organs shall practice a working hours system and a leave system prescribed by the state.  
Where the people's police of public security organs work beyond the statutory working days, they shall be granted compensatory leave; if compensatory leave cannot be granted, subsidies shall be given, and the specific measures shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Personnel jointly with the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　公安机关人民警察达到国家规定的退休年龄或者完全丧失工作能力的，应当退休。  
公安机关人民警察符合国家规定的提前退休条件的，本人自愿提出申请，经任免机关批准，可以提前退休。

**Article 39** Where a people's police of a public security organ reaches the retirement age prescribed by the state or completely loses the ability to work, he or she shall retire.  
Where a people's police of a public security organ meets the conditions for early retirement prescribed by the state, he or she may, upon voluntary application and approval by the appointing and removing organ, retire early.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　公安机关人民警察退休后，享受国家规定的退休金和其他福利待遇。

**Article 40** After retirement, the people's police of public security organs shall enjoy the retirement pension and other welfare benefits prescribed by the state.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　新疆生产建设兵团的公安机关和经批准参照《中华人民共和国公务员法》管理的公安机关所属事业单位的组织管理，适用本条例。

**Article 41** These Regulations shall apply to the organization and management of the public security organs of the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps and the institutions affiliated to public security organs that are approved to be managed by reference to the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　本条例自2007年1月1日起施行。

**Article 42** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2007.
