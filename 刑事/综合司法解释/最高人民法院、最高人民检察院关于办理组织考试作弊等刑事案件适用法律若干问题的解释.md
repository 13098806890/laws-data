# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理组织考试作弊等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2019-04-08  
**生效日期**：2019-09-04

> （2019年4月8日最高人民法院审判委员会第1765次会议、2019年6月28日最高人民检察院第十三届检察委员会第二十次会议通过，自2019年9月4日起施行 法释〔2019〕13号） 为依法惩治组织考试作弊、非法出售、提供试题、答案、代替考试等犯罪，维护考试公平与秩序，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　刑法第二百八十四条之一规定的“法律规定的国家考试”，仅限于全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会制定的法律所规定的考试。 根据有关法律规定，下列考试属于“法律规定的国家考试”： （一）普通高等学校招生考试、研究生招生考试、高等教育自学考试、成人高等学校招生考试等国家教育考试； （二）中央和地方公务员录用考试； （三）国家统一法律职业资格考试、国家教师资格考试、注册会计师全国统一考试、会计专业技术资格考试、资产评估师资格考试、医师资格考试、执业药师职业资格考试、注册建筑师考试、建造师执业资格考试等专业技术资格考试； （四）其他依照法律由中央或者地方主管部门以及行业组织的国家考试。 前款规定的考试涉及的特殊类型招生、特殊技能测试、面试等考试，属于“法律规定的国家考试”。 第二条　在法律规定的国家考试中，组织作弊，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百八十四条之一第一款规定的“情节严重”： （一）在普通高等学校招生考试、研究生招生考试、公务员录用考试中组织考试作弊的； （二）导致考试推迟、取消或者启用备用试题的； （三）考试工作人员组织考试作弊的； （四）组织考生跨省、自治区、直辖市作弊的； （五）多次组织考试作弊的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　[刑法第二百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-284)之一规定的“法律规定的国家考试”，仅限于全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会制定的法律所规定的考试。  
根据有关法律规定，下列考试属于“法律规定的国家考试”：  
（一）普通高等学校招生考试、研究生招生考试、高等教育自学考试、成人高等学校招生考试等国家教育考试；  
（二）中央和地方公务员录用考试；  
（三）国家统一法律职业资格考试、国家教师资格考试、注册会计师全国统一考试、会计专业技术资格考试、资产评估师资格考试、医师资格考试、执业药师职业资格考试、注册建筑师考试、建造师执业资格考试等专业技术资格考试；  
（四）其他依照法律由中央或者地方主管部门以及行业组织的国家考试。  
前款规定的考试涉及的特殊类型招生、特殊技能测试、面试等考试，属于“法律规定的国家考试”。

**Article 1** The "national examination prescribed by law" as provided for in Article 284a of the Criminal Law shall be limited to examinations prescribed by laws enacted by the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee.  
In accordance with relevant legal provisions, the following examinations fall within the scope of "national examinations prescribed by law":  
(1) National education examinations such as the ordinary higher education admission examination, postgraduate admission examination, higher education self-study examination, and adult higher education admission examination;  
(2) Central and local civil servant recruitment examinations;  
(3) Professional qualification examinations such as the national unified legal professional qualification examination, national teacher qualification examination, national unified certified public accountant examination, accounting professional technical qualification examination, asset appraiser qualification examination, physician qualification examination, licensed pharmacist professional qualification examination, registered architect examination, and constructor qualification examination;  
(4) Other national examinations conducted by central or local competent authorities or industries in accordance with law.  
Examinations involving special types of enrollment, special skills tests, interviews, etc., covered in the examinations specified in the preceding paragraph, shall fall within the scope of "national examinations prescribed by law."

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在法律规定的国家考试中，组织作弊，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-284)之一第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）在普通高等学校招生考试、研究生招生考试、公务员录用考试中组织考试作弊的；  
（二）导致考试推迟、取消或者启用备用试题的；  
（三）考试工作人员组织考试作弊的；  
（四）组织考生跨省、自治区、直辖市作弊的；  
（五）多次组织考试作弊的；  
（六）组织三十人次以上作弊的；  
（七）提供作弊器材五十件以上的；  
（八）违法所得三十万元以上的；  
（九）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 2** In a national examination prescribed by law, organizing cheating shall be deemed as falling under "serious circumstances" as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 284a of the Criminal Law if any of the following circumstances exists:  
(1) Organizing cheating in the ordinary higher education admission examination, postgraduate admission examination, or civil servant recruitment examination;  
(2) Causing the postponement or cancellation of the examination or the use of alternative examination questions;  
(3) An examination staff member organizing cheating;  
(4) Organizing examinees to cheat across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(5) Organizing cheating on multiple occasions;  
(6) Organizing cheating involving thirty or more person-times;  
(7) Providing fifty or more pieces of cheating equipment;  
(8) Illegal gains amounting to 300,000 yuan or more;  
(9) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　具有避开或者突破考场防范作弊的安全管理措施，获取、记录、传递、接收、存储考试试题、答案等功能的程序、工具，以及专门设计用于作弊的程序、工具，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-284)之一第二款规定的“作弊器材”。  
对于是否属于[刑法第二百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-284)之一第二款规定的“作弊器材”难以确定的，依据省级以上公安机关或者考试主管部门出具的报告，结合其他证据作出认定；涉及专用间谍器材、窃听、窃照专用器材、“伪基站”等器材的，依照相关规定作出认定。

**Article 3** Programs or tools that have the functions of evading or breaking through the security management measures for preventing cheating in examination venues, and of obtaining, recording, transmitting, receiving, or storing examination questions or answers, as well as programs or tools specially designed for cheating, shall be deemed as "cheating equipment" as provided for in paragraph 2 of Article 284a of the Criminal Law.  
Where it is difficult to determine whether an item constitutes "cheating equipment" as provided for in paragraph 2 of Article 284a of the Criminal Law, a determination shall be made based on a report issued by a public security organ at or above the provincial level or the competent examination authority, in conjunction with other evidence; where it involves specialized espionage equipment, eavesdropping and video recording equipment, "pseudo base stations", or other similar equipment, a determination shall be made in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　组织考试作弊，在考试开始之前被查获，但已经非法获取考试试题、答案或者具有其他严重扰乱考试秩序情形的，应当认定为组织考试作弊罪既遂。

**Article 4** Where organizing cheating in an examination is discovered before the start of the examination, but the examination questions or answers have already been illegally obtained, or other circumstances seriously disrupting the order of the examination exist, it shall be deemed as a completed offense of organizing cheating in an examination.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　为实施考试作弊行为，非法出售或者提供法律规定的国家考试的试题、答案，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-284)之一第三款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）非法出售或者提供普通高等学校招生考试、研究生招生考试、公务员录用考试的试题、答案的；  
（二）导致考试推迟、取消或者启用备用试题的；  
（三）考试工作人员非法出售或者提供试题、答案的；  
（四）多次非法出售或者提供试题、答案的；  
（五）向三十人次以上非法出售或者提供试题、答案的；  
（六）违法所得三十万元以上的；  
（七）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 5** For the purpose of implementing examination cheating, illegally selling or providing the questions or answers of a national examination prescribed by law shall be deemed as falling under "serious circumstances" as provided for in paragraph 3 of Article 284a of the Criminal Law if any of the following circumstances exists:  
(1) Illegally selling or providing the questions or answers of the ordinary higher education admission examination, postgraduate admission examination, or civil servant recruitment examination;  
(2) Causing the postponement or cancellation of the examination or the use of alternative examination questions;  
(3) An examination staff member illegally selling or providing the questions or answers;  
(4) Illegally selling or providing the questions or answers on multiple occasions;  
(5) Illegally selling or providing the questions or answers to thirty or more person-times;  
(6) Illegal gains amounting to 300,000 yuan or more;  
(7) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　为实施考试作弊行为，向他人非法出售或者提供法律规定的国家考试的试题、答案，试题不完整或者答案与标准答案不完全一致的，不影响非法出售、提供试题、答案罪的认定。

**Article 6** Where, for the purpose of implementing examination cheating, a person illegally sells or provides to others the questions or answers of a national examination prescribed by law, and the questions are incomplete or the answers are not entirely consistent with the standard answers, this shall not affect the determination of the crime of illegally selling or providing examination questions or answers.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　代替他人或者让他人代替自己参加法律规定的国家考试的，应当依照[刑法第二百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-284)之一第四款的规定，以代替考试罪定罪处罚。  
对于行为人犯罪情节较轻，确有悔罪表现，综合考虑行为人替考情况以及考试类型等因素，认为符合缓刑适用条件的，可以宣告缓刑；犯罪情节轻微的，可以不起诉或者免予刑事处罚；情节显著轻微危害不大的，不以犯罪论处。

**Article 7** Whoever takes a national examination prescribed by law on behalf of another person or causes another person to take the examination on his or her behalf shall, in accordance with paragraph 4 of Article 284a of the Criminal Law, be convicted and punished for the crime of taking an examination by proxy.  
Where the circumstances of the offender's crime are relatively minor, the offender shows true repentance, and after comprehensively considering factors such as the situation of taking the examination by proxy and the type of examination, it is deemed that the conditions for applying probation are met, probation may be pronounced; if the circumstances of the crime are minor, the case may be dismissed without prosecution or the offender may be granted exemption from criminal punishment; if the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm is not serious, the act shall not be deemed a crime.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　单位实施组织考试作弊、非法出售、提供试题、答案等行为的，依照本解释规定的相应定罪量刑标准，追究组织者、策划者、实施者的刑事责任。

**Article 8** Where a unit commits acts such as organizing cheating in an examination or illegally selling or providing examination questions or answers, the criminal liability of the organizers, planners, and implementers shall be investigated in accordance with the corresponding conviction and sentencing standards provided for in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　以窃取、刺探、收买方法非法获取法律规定的国家考试的试题、答案，又组织考试作弊或者非法出售、提供试题、答案，分别符合[刑法第二百八十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-282)和[刑法第二百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-284)之一规定的，以非法获取国家秘密罪和组织考试作弊罪或者非法出售、提供试题、答案罪数罪并罚。

**Article 9** Where a person illegally obtains the questions or answers of a national examination prescribed by law by means of theft, espionage, or bribery, and then organizes cheating in the examination or illegally sells or provides the questions or answers, if the acts respectively meet the provisions of Article 282 of the Criminal Law and Article 284a of the Criminal Law, the person shall be punished for multiple crimes, namely the crime of illegally obtaining state secrets and the crime of organizing cheating in an examination or the crime of illegally selling or providing examination questions or answers, in combination.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在法律规定的国家考试以外的其他考试中，组织作弊，为他人组织作弊提供作弊器材或者其他帮助，或者非法出售、提供试题、答案，符合非法获取国家秘密罪、非法生产、销售窃听、窃照专用器材罪、非法使用窃听、窃照专用器材罪、非法利用信息网络罪、扰乱无线电通讯管理秩序罪等犯罪构成要件的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 10** Where, in an examination other than a national examination prescribed by law, a person organizes cheating, provides cheating equipment or other assistance to others for organizing cheating, or illegally sells or provides examination questions or answers, if the act meets the constitutive elements of crimes such as the crime of illegally obtaining state secrets, the crime of illegally producing or selling specialized eavesdropping or video recording equipment, the crime of illegally using specialized eavesdropping or video recording equipment, the crime of illegally using information networks, or the crime of disrupting the order of radio communications management, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　设立用于实施考试作弊的网站、通讯群组或者发布有关考试作弊的信息，情节严重的，应当依照[刑法第二百八十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-287)之一的规定，以非法利用信息网络罪定罪处罚；同时构成组织考试作弊罪、非法出售、提供试题、答案罪、非法获取国家秘密罪等其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 11** Where a person establishes a website or a communication group for the purpose of implementing examination cheating, or publishes information about examination cheating, if the circumstances are serious, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegally using an information network in accordance with Article 287a of the Criminal Law; if the act also constitutes other crimes such as the crime of organizing cheating in an examination, the crime of illegally selling or providing examination questions or answers, or the crime of illegally obtaining state secrets, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision providing for the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对于实施本解释规定的犯罪被判处刑罚的，可以根据犯罪情况和预防再犯罪的需要，依法宣告职业禁止；被判处管制、宣告缓刑的，可以根据犯罪情况，依法宣告禁止令。

**Article 12** Where a person is sentenced to a criminal penalty for committing a crime provided for in this Interpretation, a prohibition from engaging in certain occupations may be pronounced according to law based on the circumstances of the crime and the need to prevent recidivism; where the person is sentenced to public surveillance or probation is pronounced, a restraining order may be pronounced according to law based on the circumstances of the crime.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对于实施本解释规定的行为构成犯罪的，应当综合考虑犯罪的危害程度、违法所得数额以及被告人的前科情况、认罪悔罪态度等，依法判处罚金。

**Article 13** Where an act provided for in this Interpretation constitutes a crime, the fine shall be imposed according to law after comprehensively considering the degree of harm of the crime, the amount of illegal gains, and the defendant's criminal record, attitude of admission of guilt and repentance, among other factors.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本解释自2019年9月4日起施行。

**Article 14** This Interpretation shall come into force as of September 4, 2019.
