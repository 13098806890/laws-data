# 最高人民法院关于认真学习宣传贯彻修订的《中华人民共和国刑法》的通知

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1997〕3号  
**公布日期**：1997-03-25  
**生效日期**：1997-03-25

> 修订的《中华人民共和国刑法》业经第八届全国人民代表大会第五次会议通过，1997年10月1日起施行。刑法是国家的基本法，是人民法院审理刑事案件的重要法律依据。修订刑法，是健全社会主义法制的一件大事，是完善我国刑事法律和司法制度的重要步骤，引起国内外的普遍关注。认真学习、宣传、贯彻好修订的刑法，对于人民法院进一步提高执法水平，更好地履行打击犯罪、保护人民的职责，具有十分重要的意义。 为正确贯彻执行修订的刑法，特作如下通知： 一、各级人民法院要认真组织广大审判人员学习修订的刑法。修订的刑法认真总结了17年实施刑法的实践经验，借鉴国内外有关刑法的立法经验，明确规定了刑法的基本原则；突出了对社会主义市场经济秩序的保护；适应与犯罪行为作斗争的现实需要，对人民群众反映强烈的一些常见、多发罪作了更加明确、详细的规定；对新出现的需要追究的犯罪行为规定了相应的刑事责任；对一些重要的刑法制度作了修改、完善，是一部更加科学、统一、完备的刑法典。各级人民法院要制定学习计划，采用各种形式，不断深化学习，真正学懂、弄通。 二、各级人民法院要结合当地实际情况，与有关部门配合，广泛、深入地开展修订的刑法的宣传活动。修订的刑法是司法机关打击和惩治犯罪的有力武器，并将对社会政治经济生活的诸多方面产生重大影响。人民法院要充分发挥审判工作的优势，通过新闻媒介等多种形式，突出重点，有针对性地做好修订的刑法的宣传工作。 三、修订的刑法实施后，各级人民法院必须坚决贯彻执行。对于修订的刑法实施前发生的行为，10月1日实施后尚未处理或者正在处理的案件，依照修订的刑法第十二条的规定办理；对于修订的刑法实施前，人民法院已审结的案件，实施后人民法院按照审判监督程序重新审理的，适用原审结时的有关法律规定。 四、修订的刑法实施前，人民法院审判刑事案件仍然应当依照现行刑法和人大常委会修改、补充刑法的有关决定、补充规定及最高人民法院的有关司法解释，并应遵守刑事诉讼法有关程序和期限的规定。 五、修订的刑法实施后，对已明令废止的全国人大常委会有关决定和补充规定，最高人民法院原作出的有关司法解释不再适用。但是如果修订的刑法有关条文实质内容没有变化的，人民法院在刑事审判工作中，在没有新的司法解释前，可参照执行。其他对于与修订的刑法规定相抵触的司法解释，不再适用。 六、各级人民法院在学习、宣传和贯彻修订的刑法中，要加强调查研究，对遇到的理解和适用法律的重大、疑难问题，要注意收集，及时上报。 特此通知。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、各级人民法院要认真组织广大审判人员学习修订的刑法。修订的刑法认真总结了17年实施刑法的实践经验，借鉴国内外有关刑法的立法经验，明确规定了刑法的基本原则；突出了对社会主义市场经济秩序的保护；适应与犯罪行为作斗争的现实需要，对人民群众反映强烈的一些常见、多发罪作了更加明确、详细的规定；对新出现的需要追究的犯罪行为规定了相应的刑事责任；对一些重要的刑法制度作了修改、完善，是一部更加科学、统一、完备的刑法典。各级人民法院要制定学习计划，采用各种形式，不断深化学习，真正学懂、弄通。

**Article 1** I. People's Courts at all levels shall earnestly organize the vast number of adjudicators to study the revised Criminal Law. The revised Criminal Law conscientiously summarizes the practical experience of implementing the Criminal Law over the past 17 years, draws on domestic and foreign legislative experience concerning criminal law, and clearly stipulates the basic principles of the Criminal Law; it highlights the protection of the socialist market economy order; it adapts to the practical needs of combating criminal acts, and provides more explicit and detailed provisions for some common and frequently occurring crimes that have drawn strong reactions from the people; it stipulates corresponding criminal liability for newly emerging criminal acts that require punishment; it modifies and improves some important criminal law systems, making it a more scientific, unified, and complete criminal code. People's Courts at all levels shall formulate study plans, adopt various forms, continuously deepen their study, and truly understand and master it.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、各级人民法院要结合当地实际情况，与有关部门配合，广泛、深入地开展修订的刑法的宣传活动。修订的刑法是司法机关打击和惩治犯罪的有力武器，并将对社会政治经济生活的诸多方面产生重大影响。人民法院要充分发挥审判工作的优势，通过新闻媒介等多种形式，突出重点，有针对性地做好修订的刑法的宣传工作。

**Article 2** II. People's Courts at all levels shall, in light of local actual conditions and in coordination with relevant departments, extensively and deeply conduct publicity activities on the revised Criminal Law. The revised Criminal Law is a powerful weapon for judicial organs to combat and punish crimes, and will have a significant impact on many aspects of social, political, and economic life. People's Courts shall give full play to the advantages of adjudication work, through various forms such as news media, highlight key points, and carry out targeted publicity work on the revised Criminal Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、修订的刑法实施后，各级人民法院必须坚决贯彻执行。对于修订的刑法实施前发生的行为，10月1日实施后尚未处理或者正在处理的案件，依照修订的[刑法第十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-12)的规定办理；对于修订的刑法实施前，人民法院已审结的案件，实施后人民法院按照审判监督程序重新审理的，适用原审结时的有关法律规定。

**Article 3** III. After the revised Criminal Law comes into effect, People's Courts at all levels must resolutely implement and enforce it. For acts occurring before the implementation of the revised Criminal Law, where cases have not yet been handled or are being handled after October 1, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 12 of the revised Criminal Law; for cases that have been finally adjudicated by the People's Court before the implementation of the revised Criminal Law, if after implementation the People's Court re-trials them under the trial supervision procedure, the relevant legal provisions at the time of the original final adjudication shall apply.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、修订的刑法实施前，人民法院审判刑事案件仍然应当依照现行刑法和人大常委会修改、补充刑法的有关决定、补充规定及最高人民法院的有关司法解释，并应遵守刑事诉讼法有关程序和期限的规定。

**Article 4** IV. Before the implementation of the revised Criminal Law, People's Courts shall still adjudicate criminal cases in accordance with the current Criminal Law and the relevant decisions and supplementary provisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on amending and supplementing the Criminal Law, as well as the relevant judicial interpretations of the Supreme People's Court, and shall comply with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law concerning procedures and time limits.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、修订的刑法实施后，对已明令废止的全国人大常委会有关决定和补充规定，最高人民法院原作出的有关司法解释不再适用。但是如果修订的刑法有关条文实质内容没有变化的，人民法院在刑事审判工作中，在没有新的司法解释前，可参照执行。其他对于与修订的刑法规定相抵触的司法解释，不再适用。

**Article 5** V. After the implementation of the revised Criminal Law, for the decisions and supplementary provisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress that have been explicitly abolished, the relevant judicial interpretations previously made by the Supreme People's Court shall no longer apply. However, if the substantive content of the relevant articles of the revised Criminal Law has not changed, the People's Court may, in criminal adjudication work, refer to them for enforcement before new judicial interpretations are issued. Other judicial interpretations that conflict with the provisions of the revised Criminal Law shall no longer apply.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、各级人民法院在学习、宣传和贯彻修订的刑法中，要加强调查研究，对遇到的理解和适用法律的重大、疑难问题，要注意收集，及时上报。  
特此通知。

**Article 6** VI. In the process of studying, publicizing, and implementing the revised Criminal Law, People's Courts at all levels shall strengthen investigation and research, pay attention to collecting major and difficult issues concerning the understanding and application of the law that are encountered, and promptly report them.  
This is hereby notified.
