# 最高人民法院关于审理非法制造、买卖、运输枪支、弹药、爆炸物等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2001〕15号  
**公布日期**：2001-05-15  
**生效日期**：2001-05-16

> （2001年5月10日最高人民法院审判委员会第1174次会议通过　2001年5月15日最高人民法院公告公布　自2001年5月16日起施行） 为依法严惩非法制造、买卖、运输枪支、弹药、爆炸物等犯罪活动，根据刑法有关规定，现就审理这类案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　个人或者单位非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存枪支、弹药、爆炸物，具有下列情形之一的，依照刑法第一百二十五条第一款的规定，以非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存枪支、弹药、爆炸物罪定罪处罚： （一）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存军用枪支1支以上的； （二）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存以火药为动力发射枪弹的非军用枪支1支以上或者以压缩气体等为动力的其他非军用枪支2支以上的； （三）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存军用子弹10发以上、气枪铅弹500发以上或者其他非军用子弹100发以上的； （四）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存手榴弹1枚以上的； （五）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存爆炸装置的； （六）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存炸药、发射药、黑火药1千克以上或者烟火药3千克以上、雷管30枚以上或者导火索、导爆索30米以上的； （七）具有生产爆炸物品资格的单位不按照规定的品种制造，或者具有销售、使用爆炸物品资格的单位超过限额买卖炸药、发射药、黑火药10千克以上或者烟火药30千克以上、雷管300枚以上或者导火索、导爆索300米以上的； （八）多次非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存弹药、爆炸物的； （九）虽未达到上述最低数量标准，但具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。 介绍买卖枪支、弹药、爆炸物的，以买卖枪支、弹药、爆炸物罪的共犯论处。 第二条　非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存枪支、弹药、爆炸物，具有下列情形之一的，属于刑法第一百二十五条第一款规定的“情节严重”： （一）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存枪支、弹药、爆炸物的数量达到本解释第一条第（一）、（二）、（三）、（六）、（七）项规定的最低数量标准5倍以上的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　个人或者单位非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存枪支、弹药、爆炸物，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第一百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-125)第一款的规定，以非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存枪支、弹药、爆炸物罪定罪处罚：  
（一）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存军用枪支1支以上的；  
（二）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存以火药为动力发射枪弹的非军用枪支1支以上或者以压缩气体等为动力的其他非军用枪支2支以上的；  
（三）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存军用子弹10发以上、气枪铅弹500发以上或者其他非军用子弹100发以上的；  
（四）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存手榴弹1枚以上的；  
（五）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存爆炸装置的；  
（六）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存炸药、发射药、黑火药1千克以上或者烟火药3千克以上、雷管30枚以上或者导火索、导爆索30米以上的；  
（七）具有生产爆炸物品资格的单位不按照规定的品种制造，或者具有销售、使用爆炸物品资格的单位超过限额买卖炸药、发射药、黑火药10千克以上或者烟火药30千克以上、雷管300枚以上或者导火索、导爆索300米以上的；  
（八）多次非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存弹药、爆炸物的；  
（九）虽未达到上述最低数量标准，但具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。  
介绍买卖枪支、弹药、爆炸物的，以买卖枪支、弹药、爆炸物罪的共犯论处。

**Article 1** : Where an individual or entity illegally manufactures, sells, transports, sends by mail, or stores firearms, ammunition, or explosives, and the circumstances fall under any of the following, they shall be convicted and punished in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 125 of the Criminal Law for the crime of illegally manufacturing, selling, transporting, sending by mail, or storing firearms, ammunition, or explosives:  
(1) illegally manufacturing, selling, transporting, sending by mail, or storing one or more military firearms;  
(2) illegally manufacturing, selling, transporting, sending by mail, or storing one or more non-military firearms that use gunpowder as a power source to fire bullets, or two or more other non-military firearms that use compressed gas or the like as a power source;  
(3) illegally manufacturing, selling, transporting, sending by mail, or storing 10 or more military bullets, 500 or more air gun pellets, or 100 or more other non-military bullets;  
(4) illegally manufacturing, selling, transporting, sending by mail, or storing one or more hand grenades;  
(5) illegally manufacturing, selling, transporting, sending by mail, or storing explosive devices;  
(6) illegally manufacturing, selling, transporting, sending by mail, or storing 1 kg or more of explosives, propellants, or black powder, or 3 kg or more of pyrotechnic powder, or 30 or more detonators, or 30 meters or more of fuse or detonating cord;  
(7) where an entity qualified to produce explosive articles manufactures them not in accordance with the prescribed types, or an entity qualified to sell or use explosive articles sells or purchases beyond the limits 10 kg or more of explosives, propellants, or black powder, or 30 kg or more of pyrotechnic powder, or 300 or more detonators, or 300 meters or more of fuse or detonating cord;  
(8) multiple instances of illegally manufacturing, selling, transporting, sending by mail, or storing ammunition or explosives;  
(9) although the above minimum quantity standards are not reached, there are other vile circumstances such as causing serious consequences.  
Any person who introduces the sale of firearms, ammunition, or explosives shall be punished as an accomplice to the crime of selling firearms, ammunition, or explosives.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存枪支、弹药、爆炸物，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-125)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存枪支、弹药、爆炸物的数量达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)第（一）、（二）、（三）、（六）、（七）项规定的最低数量标准5倍以上的；  
（二）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存手榴弹3枚以上的；  
（三）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存爆炸装置，危害严重的；  
（四）达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的最低数量标准，并具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。

**Article 2** : Where a person illegally manufactures, sells, transports, sends by mail, or stores firearms, ammunition, or explosives, and the circumstances fall under any of the following, it shall constitute “serious circumstances” as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 125 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the quantity of firearms, ammunition, or explosives illegally manufactured, sold, transported, sent by mail, or stored reaches five times or more the minimum quantity standards specified in subparagraphs (1), (2), (3), (6), and (7) of Article 1 of this Interpretation;  
(2) illegally manufacturing, selling, transporting, sending by mail, or storing three or more hand grenades;  
(3) illegally manufacturing, selling, transporting, sending by mail, or storing explosive devices causing serious harm;  
(4) reaching the minimum quantity standards specified in Article 1 of this Interpretation and having other vile circumstances such as causing serious consequences.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　依法被指定或者确定的枪支制造、销售企业，实施[刑法第一百二十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-126)规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，以违规制造、销售枪支罪定罪处罚：  
（一）违规制造枪支5支以上的；  
（二）违规销售枪支2支以上的；  
（三）虽未达到上述最低数量标准，但具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百二十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-126)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）违规制造枪支20支以上的；  
（二）违规销售枪支10支以上的；  
（三）达到本条第一款规定的最低数量标准，并具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百二十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-126)规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）违规制造枪支50支以上的；  
（二）违规销售枪支30支以上的；  
（三）达到本条第二款规定的最低数量标准，并具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。

**Article 3** : Where an enterprise that has been designated or determined according to law for the manufacture or sale of firearms commits any of the acts specified in Article 126 of the Criminal Law, and the circumstances fall under any of the following, it shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegally manufacturing or selling firearms:  
(1) illegally manufacturing five or more firearms;  
(2) illegally selling two or more firearms;  
(3) although the above minimum quantity standards are not reached, there are other vile circumstances such as causing serious consequences.  
Where the circumstances fall under any of the following, it shall constitute “serious circumstances” as provided for in Article 126 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) illegally manufacturing 20 or more firearms;  
(2) illegally selling 10 or more firearms;  
(3) reaching the minimum quantity standards specified in paragraph 1 of this Article and having other vile circumstances such as causing serious consequences.  
Where the circumstances fall under any of the following, it shall constitute “especially serious circumstances” as provided for in Article 126 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) illegally manufacturing 50 or more firearms;  
(2) illegally selling 30 or more firearms;  
(3) reaching the minimum quantity standards specified in paragraph 2 of this Article and having other vile circumstances such as causing serious consequences.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　盗窃、抢夺枪支、弹药、爆炸物，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第一百二十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-127)第一款的规定，以盗窃、抢夺枪支、弹药、爆炸物罪定罪处罚：  
（一）盗窃、抢夺以火药为动力的发射枪弹非军用枪支1支以上或者以压缩气体等为动力的其他非军用枪支2支以上的；  
（二）盗窃、抢夺军用子弹10发以上、气枪铅弹500发以上或者其他非军用子弹100发以上的；  
（三）盗窃、抢夺爆炸装置的；  
（四）盗窃、抢夺炸药、发射药、黑火药1千克以上或者烟火药3千克以上、雷管30枚以上或者导火索、导爆索30米以上的；  
（五）虽未达到上述最低数量标准，但具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百二十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-127)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）盗窃、抢夺枪支、弹药、爆炸物的数量达到本条第一款规定的最低数量标准5倍以上的；  
（二）盗窃、抢夺军用枪支的；  
（三）盗窃、抢夺手榴弹的；  
（四）盗窃、抢夺爆炸装置，危害严重的；  
（五）达到本条第一款规定的最低数量标准，并具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。

**Article 4** : Where a person steals or forcibly seizes firearms, ammunition, or explosives, and the circumstances fall under any of the following, they shall be convicted and punished in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 127 of the Criminal Law for the crime of stealing or forcibly seizing firearms, ammunition, or explosives:  
(1) stealing or forcibly seizing one or more non-military firearms that use gunpowder as a power source to fire bullets, or two or more other non-military firearms that use compressed gas or the like as a power source;  
(2) stealing or forcibly seizing 10 or more military bullets, 500 or more air gun pellets, or 100 or more other non-military bullets;  
(3) stealing or forcibly seizing explosive devices;  
(4) stealing or forcibly seizing 1 kg or more of explosives, propellants, or black powder, or 3 kg or more of pyrotechnic powder, or 30 or more detonators, or 30 meters or more of fuse or detonating cord;  
(5) although the above minimum quantity standards are not reached, there are other vile circumstances such as causing serious consequences.  
Where the circumstances fall under any of the following, it shall constitute “serious circumstances” as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 127 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the quantity of firearms, ammunition, or explosives stolen or forcibly seized reaches five times or more the minimum quantity standards specified in paragraph 1 of this Article;  
(2) stealing or forcibly seizing military firearms;  
(3) stealing or forcibly seizing hand grenades;  
(4) stealing or forcibly seizing explosive devices causing serious harm;  
(5) reaching the minimum quantity standards specified in paragraph 1 of this Article and having other vile circumstances such as causing serious consequences.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第一百二十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-128)第一款的规定，以非法持有、私藏枪支、弹药罪定罪处罚：  
（一）非法持有、私藏军用枪支1支的；  
（二）非法持有、私藏以火药为动力发射枪弹的非军用枪支1支或者以压缩气体等为动力的其他非军用枪支2支以上的；  
（三）非法持有、私藏军用子弹20发以上，气枪铅弹1000发以上或者其他非军用子弹200发以上的；  
（四）非法持有、私藏手榴弹1枚以上的；  
（五）非法持有、私藏的弹药造成人员伤亡、财产损失的。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百二十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-128)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）非法持有、私藏军用枪支2支以上的；  
（二）非法持有、私藏以火药为动力发射枪弹的非军用枪支2支以上或者以压缩气体等为动力的其他非军用枪支5支以上的；  
（三）非法持有、私藏军用子弹100发以上，气枪铅弹5000发以上或者其他非军用子弹1000发以上的；  
（四）非法持有、私藏手榴弹3枚以上的；  
（五）达到本条第一款规定的最低数量标准，并具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。

**Article 5** : Where the circumstances fall under any of the following, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 128 of the Criminal Law for the crime of illegally possessing or secretly storing firearms or ammunition:  
(1) illegally possessing or secretly storing one military firearm;  
(2) illegally possessing or secretly storing one non-military firearm that uses gunpowder as a power source to fire bullets, or two or more other non-military firearms that use compressed gas or the like as a power source;  
(3) illegally possessing or secretly storing 20 or more military bullets, 1,000 or more air gun pellets, or 200 or more other non-military bullets;  
(4) illegally possessing or secretly storing one or more hand grenades;  
(5) the ammunition illegally possessed or secretly stored causes casualties or property damage.  
Where the circumstances fall under any of the following, it shall constitute “serious circumstances” as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 128 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) illegally possessing or secretly storing two or more military firearms;  
(2) illegally possessing or secretly storing two or more non-military firearms that use gunpowder as a power source to fire bullets, or five or more other non-military firearms that use compressed gas or the like as a power source;  
(3) illegally possessing or secretly storing 100 or more military bullets, 5,000 or more air gun pellets, or 1,000 or more other non-military bullets;  
(4) illegally possessing or secretly storing three or more hand grenades;  
(5) reaching the minimum quantity standards specified in paragraph 1 of this Article and having other vile circumstances such as causing serious consequences.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　非法携带枪支、弹药、爆炸物进入公共场所或者公共交通工具，危及公共安全，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百三十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-130)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）携带枪支或者手榴弹的；  
（二）携带爆炸装置的；  
（三）携带炸药、发射药、黑火药500克以上或者烟火药1千克以上、雷管20枚以上或者导火索、导爆索20米以上的；  
（四）携带的弹药、爆炸物在公共场所或者公共交通工具上发生爆炸或者燃烧，尚未造成严重后果的；  
（五）具有其他严重情节的。  
行为人非法携带本条第一款第（三）项规定的爆炸物进入公共场所或者公共交通工具，虽未达到上述数量标准，但拒不交出的，依照[刑法第一百三十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-130)的规定定罪处罚；携带的数量达到最低数量标准，能够主动、全部交出的，可不以犯罪论处。

**Article 6** : Where a person illegally carries firearms, ammunition, or explosives into a public place or on a public means of transport, endangering public safety, and the circumstances fall under any of the following, it shall constitute “serious circumstances” as provided for in Article 130 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) carrying any firearm or hand grenade;  
(2) carrying any explosive device;  
(3) carrying 500 grams or more of explosives, propellants, or black powder, or 1 kg or more of pyrotechnic powder, or 20 or more detonators, or 20 meters or more of fuse or detonating cord;  
(4) the carried ammunition or explosives explode or ignite in a public place or on a public means of transport, without having caused serious consequences;  
(5) having other serious circumstances.  
Where the actor illegally carries explosives specified in subparagraph (3) of paragraph 1 of this Article into a public place or on a public means of transport, and although the above quantity standards are not reached, they refuse to surrender them, they shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 130 of the Criminal Law; where the quantity carried reaches the minimum quantity standard, and the person voluntarily and completely surrenders them, they may be deemed not to constitute a crime.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存、盗窃、抢夺、持有、私藏、携带成套枪支散件的，以相应数量的枪支计；非成套枪支散件以每30件为一成套枪支散件计。

**Article 7** : Where a person illegally manufactures, sells, transports, sends by mail, stores, steals, forcibly seizes, possesses, secretly stores, or carries complete sets of firearm parts, they shall be counted as the corresponding number of firearms; non-complete sets of firearm parts shall be counted as one complete set of firearm parts for every 30 parts.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　[刑法第一百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-125)第一款规定的“非法储存”，是指明知是他人非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄的枪支、弹药、爆炸物而为其存放的行为。  
[刑法第一百二十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-128)第一款规定的“非法持有”，是指不符合配备、配置枪支、弹药条件的人员，违反枪支管理法律、法规的规定，擅自持有枪支、弹药的行为。  
[刑法第一百二十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-128)第一款规定的“私藏”，是指依法配备、配置枪支、弹药的人员，在配备、配置枪支、弹药的条件消除后，违反枪支管理法律、法规的规定，私自藏匿所配备、配置的枪支、弹药且拒不交出的行为。

**Article 8** : “Illegal storage” as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 125 of the Criminal Law means the act of storing firearms, ammunition, or explosives on behalf of another person while knowing that they have been illegally manufactured, sold, transported, or sent by mail by that person.  
“Illegal possession” as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 128 of the Criminal Law means the act of a person who does not meet the conditions for being equipped or allocated with firearms or ammunition, in violation of the provisions of laws and regulations on the control of firearms, of holding firearms or ammunition without authorization.  
“Secret storage” as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 128 of the Criminal Law means the act of a person who has been equipped or allocated with firearms or ammunition according to law, after the conditions for such equipment or allocation have ceased to exist, in violation of the provisions of laws and regulations on the control of firearms, of secretly storing the firearms or ammunition with which they were equipped or allocated and refusing to surrender them.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　实施非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存、盗窃、抢夺、持有、私藏其他弹药、爆炸物品等行为，参照本解释有关条文规定的定罪量刑标准处罚。

**Article 9** : Any conduct of illegally manufacturing, selling, transporting, sending by mail, storing, stealing, forcibly seizing, possessing, or secretly storing other types of ammunition or explosive articles shall be punished by reference to the standards for conviction and sentencing provided for in the relevant articles of this Interpretation.
