# 最高人民检察院关于审查起诉期间犯罪嫌疑人脱逃或者患有严重疾病的应当如何处理的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民检察院  
**发文字号**：高检发释字〔2013〕4号  
**公布日期**：2013-12-27  
**生效日期**：2014-01-01

> 《最高人民检察院关于审查起诉期间犯罪嫌疑人脱逃或者患有严重疾病的应当如何处理的批复》已于2013年12月19日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第十四次会议通过，现予公布，自2014年1月1日起施行。 最高人民检察院 2013年12月27日 最高人民检察院关于审查起诉期间犯罪嫌疑人脱逃或者患有严重疾病的应当如何处理的批复 （2013年12月19日最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会 第十四次会议通过）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、人民检察院办理犯罪嫌疑人被羁押的审查起诉案件，应当严格依照法律规定的期限办结。未能依法办结的，应当根据[刑事诉讼法第九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-96)的规定予以释放或者变更强制措施。

**Article 1** I. When the People's Procuratorate handles a case for examination and prosecution where the criminal suspect is in custody, it shall strictly conclude the case within the time limit prescribed by law. If the case cannot be concluded according to law, the criminal suspect shall be released or the compulsory measures shall be changed in accordance with Article 96 of the Criminal Procedure Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、人民检察院对于侦查机关移送审查起诉的案件，如果犯罪嫌疑人脱逃的，应当根据《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则（试行）》第一百五十四条第三款的规定，要求侦查机关采取措施保证犯罪嫌疑人到案后再移送审查起诉。

**Article 2** II. For a case transferred by an investigation authority to the People's Procuratorate for examination and prosecution, if the criminal suspect escapes, the People's Procuratorate shall, in accordance with Article 154, paragraph 3 of the Criminal Procedure Rules of the People's Procuratorate (for Trial Implementation), require the investigation authority to take measures to ensure the criminal suspect appears before transferring the case for examination and prosecution.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、人民检察院在审查起诉过程中发现犯罪嫌疑人脱逃的，应当及时通知侦查机关，要求侦查机关开展追捕活动。  
人民检察院应当及时全面审阅案卷材料。经审查，对于案件事实不清、证据不足的，可以根据[刑事诉讼法第一百七十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-171)第二款、《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则（试行）》第三百八十条的规定退回侦查机关补充侦查。  
侦查机关补充侦查完毕移送审查起诉的，人民检察院应当按照本批复第二条的规定进行审查。  
共同犯罪中的部分犯罪嫌疑人脱逃的，对其他犯罪嫌疑人的审查起诉应当照常进行。

**Article 3** III. If during the process of examination and prosecution the People's Procuratorate discovers that a criminal suspect has escaped, it shall promptly notify the investigation authority and require the investigation authority to carry out pursuit and arrest activities.  
The People's Procuratorate shall promptly and thoroughly review the case files and materials. Upon review, if the facts of the case are unclear and the evidence is insufficient, it may, in accordance with Article 171, paragraph 2 of the Criminal Procedure Law and Article 380 of the Criminal Procedure Rules of the People's Procuratorate (for Trial Implementation), return the case to the investigation authority for supplementary investigation.  
After the investigation authority completes supplementary investigation and transfers the case for examination and prosecution, the People's Procuratorate shall conduct the examination in accordance with the provisions of Article 2 of this Reply.  
If some criminal suspects in a joint crime escape, the examination and prosecution of other criminal suspects shall proceed as normal.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、犯罪嫌疑人患有精神病或者其他严重疾病丧失诉讼行为能力不能接受讯问的，人民检察院可以依法变更强制措施。对实施暴力行为的精神病人，人民检察院可以商请公安机关采取临时的保护性约束措施。  
经审查，应当按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）经鉴定系依法不负刑事责任的精神病人的，人民检察院应当作出不起诉决定。符合[刑事诉讼法第二百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-284)规定的条件的，可以向人民法院提出强制医疗的申请；  
（二）有证据证明患有精神病的犯罪嫌疑人尚未完全丧失辨认或者控制自己行为的能力，或者患有间歇性精神病的犯罪嫌疑人实施犯罪行为时精神正常，符合起诉条件的，可以依法提起公诉；  
（三）案件事实不清、证据不足的，可以根据[刑事诉讼法第一百七十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-171)第二款、《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则（试行）》第三百八十条的规定退回侦查机关补充侦查。

**Article 4** IV. If a criminal suspect suffers from mental illness or other serious disease and loses the capacity to participate in legal proceedings and cannot be subjected to interrogation, the People's Procuratorate may change the compulsory measures according to law. For a mental patient who has committed violent acts, the People's Procuratorate may consult with the public security organ to take temporary protective restraint measures.  
Upon review, the following circumstances shall be dealt with separately:  
(1) If the criminal suspect is determined through expert evaluation to be a mental patient who does not bear criminal liability according to law, the People's Procuratorate shall make a decision not to prosecute. If the conditions stipulated in Article 284 of the Criminal Procedure Law are met, an application for compulsory medical treatment may be submitted to the People's Court;  
(2) If there is evidence proving that a criminal suspect suffering from mental illness has not completely lost the ability to recognize or control his or her behavior, or that a criminal suspect suffering from intermittent mental illness was in a normal mental state when committing the criminal act, and the conditions for prosecution are met, a public prosecution may be initiated according to law;  
(3) If the facts of the case are unclear and the evidence is insufficient, the case may be returned to the investigation authority for supplementary investigation in accordance with Article 171, paragraph 2 of the Criminal Procedure Law and Article 380 of the Criminal Procedure Rules of the People's Procuratorate (for Trial Implementation).

<a id="art-5"></a>五、人民检察院在审查起诉期间，犯罪嫌疑人脱逃或者死亡，符合[刑事诉讼法第二百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-280)第一款规定的条件的，人民检察院可以向人民法院提出没收违法所得的申请。  
此复。  
最高人民检察院  
2013年12月27日

**Article 5** V. If during the period of examination and prosecution, a criminal suspect escapes or dies and the conditions stipulated in Article 280, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Procedure Law are met, the People's Procuratorate may submit an application to the People's Court for confiscation of illegal gains.
