### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　违反国家金融管理法律规定，向社会公众（包括单位和个人）吸收资金的行为，同时具备下列四个条件的，除刑法另有规定的以外，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-176)规定的“非法吸收公众存款或者变相吸收公众存款”：  
（一）未经有关部门依法批准或者借用合法经营的形式吸收资金；  
（二）通过媒体、推介会、传单、手机短信等途径向社会公开宣传；  
（三）承诺在一定期限内以货币、实物、股权等方式还本付息或者给付回报；  
（四）向社会公众即社会不特定对象吸收资金。  
未向社会公开宣传，在亲友或者单位内部针对特定对象吸收资金的，不属于非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部关于办理非法集资刑事案件适用法律若干问题的意见.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部关于办理非法集资刑事案件适用法律若干问题的意见》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部关于办理非法集资刑事案件适用法律若干问题的意见.md#art-3" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部关于办理非法集资刑事案件适用法律若干问题的意见》第3条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 1** A person who, in violation of the provisions of the state financial management laws, absorbs funds from the public (including units and individuals) and meets all of the following four conditions shall, unless otherwise provided by the Criminal Law, be deemed to have committed "illegally absorbing public deposits or illegally absorbing public deposits in disguised form" as prescribed in Article 176 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Absorbing funds without approval according to law from the relevant department or by borrowing the form of legitimate business;  
(2) Publicly promoting through media, promotion meetings, leaflets, mobile phone text messages and other channels;  
(3) Accepting to repay principal and interest or give returns in the form of currency, material objects, equity, etc. within a certain period; and  
(4) Absorbing funds from the public, i.e., unspecified members of the society.  
Where funds are absorbed from specific targets such as relatives, friends, or within the internal scope of a unit without public promotion, such conduct shall not be deemed as illegally absorbing public deposits or illegally absorbing public deposits in disguised form.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　实施下列行为之一，符合[本解释第一条](#art-1)第一款规定的条件的，应当依照[刑法第一百七十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-176)的规定，以非法吸收公众存款罪定罪处罚：  
（一）不具有房产销售的真实内容或者不以房产销售为主要目的，以返本销售、售后包租、约定回购、销售房产份额等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（二）以转让林权并代为管护等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（三）以代种植（养殖）、租种植（养殖）、联合种植（养殖）等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（四）不具有销售商品、提供服务的真实内容或者不以销售商品、提供服务为主要目的，以商品回购、寄存代售等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（五）不具有发行股票、债券的真实内容，以虚假转让股权、发售虚构债券等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（六）不具有募集基金的真实内容，以假借境外基金、发售虚构基金等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（七）不具有销售保险的真实内容，以假冒保险公司、伪造保险单据等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（八）以投资入股的方式非法吸收资金的；  
（九）以委托理财的方式非法吸收资金的；  
（十）利用民间“会”、“社”等组织非法吸收资金的；  
（十一）其他非法吸收资金的行为。

**Article 2** Any person who commits one of the following acts and meets the conditions specified in Paragraph 1 of Article 1 of this Interpretation shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegally absorbing public deposits in accordance with Article 176 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Illegally absorbing funds by means of returning principal and selling, selling with leaseback, agreeing to repurchase, selling property shares, etc., without having the real content of real estate sales or without taking real estate sales as the main purpose;  
(2) Illegally absorbing funds by means of transferring forest rights and managing them on behalf of others, etc.;  
(3) Illegally absorbing funds by means of substitute planting (breeding), leased planting (breeding), joint planting (breeding), etc.;  
(4) Illegally absorbing funds by means of commodity repurchase, storage and consignment sale, etc., without having the real content of selling commodities or providing services, or without taking selling commodities or providing services as the main purpose;  
(5) Illegally absorbing funds by means of falsely transferring equities, issuing fictitious bonds, etc., without having the real content of issuing stocks or bonds;  
(6) Illegally absorbing funds by borrowing the name of overseas funds, issuing fictitious funds, etc., without having the real content of raising funds;  
(7) Illegally absorbing funds by means of counterfeiting insurance companies, forging insurance documents, etc., without having the real content of selling insurance;  
(8) Illegally absorbing funds by means of investment as shares;  
(9) Illegally absorbing funds by means of entrusted financial management; and  
(10) Illegally absorbing funds by utilizing private organizations such as "societies" or "associations"; or  
(11) Other acts of illegally absorbing funds.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，具有下列情形之一的，应当依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）个人非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，数额在20万元以上的，单位非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，数额在100万元以上的；  
（二）个人非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款对象30人以上的，单位非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款对象150人以上的；  
（三）个人非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，给存款人造成直接经济损失数额在10万元以上的，单位非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，给存款人造成直接经济损失数额在50万元以上的；  
（四）造成恶劣社会影响或者其他严重后果的。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百七十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-176)规定的“数额巨大或者有其他严重情节”：  
（一）个人非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，数额在100万元以上的，单位非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，数额在500万元以上的；  
（二）个人非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款对象100人以上的，单位非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款对象500人以上的；  
（三）个人非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，给存款人造成直接经济损失数额在50万元以上的，单位非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，给存款人造成直接经济损失数额在250万元以上的；  
（四）造成特别恶劣社会影响或者其他特别严重后果的。  
非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款的数额，以行为人所吸收的资金全额计算。案发前后已归还的数额，可以作为量刑情节酌情考虑。  
非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，主要用于正常的生产经营活动，能够及时清退所吸收资金，可以免予刑事处罚；情节显著轻微的，不作为犯罪处理。

**Article 3** The criminal liability shall be investigated according to law for any of the following circumstances of illegally absorbing public deposits or illegally absorbing public deposits in disguised form:  
(1) Where an individual illegally absorbs or illegally absorbs in disguised form public deposits of not less than RMB 200,000 yuan, or a unit illegally absorbs or illegally absorbs in disguised form public deposits of not less than RMB 1,000,000 yuan;  
(2) Where an individual illegally absorbs or illegally absorbs in disguised form public deposits from not less than 30 persons, or a unit illegally absorbs or illegally absorbs in disguised form public deposits from not less than 150 persons;  
(3) Where an individual illegally absorbs or illegally absorbs in disguised form public deposits, causing direct economic losses to depositors of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan, or a unit illegally absorbs or illegally absorbs in disguised form public deposits, causing direct economic losses to depositors of not less than RMB 500,000 yuan; or  
(4) Causing severe adverse social impact or other serious consequences.  
Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "a huge amount or other serious circumstances" as prescribed in Article 176 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Where an individual illegally absorbs or illegally absorbs in disguised form public deposits of not less than RMB 1,000,000 yuan, or a unit illegally absorbs or illegally absorbs in disguised form public deposits of not less than RMB 5,000,000 yuan;  
(2) Where an individual illegally absorbs or illegally absorbs in disguised form public deposits from not less than 100 persons, or a unit illegally absorbs or illegally absorbs in disguised form public deposits from not less than 500 persons;  
(3) Where an individual illegally absorbs or illegally absorbs in disguised form public deposits, causing direct economic losses to depositors of not less than RMB 500,000 yuan, or a unit illegally absorbs or illegally absorbs in disguised form public deposits, causing direct economic losses to depositors of not less than RMB 2,500,000 yuan; or  
(4) Causing particularly severe adverse social impact or other particularly serious consequences.  
The amount of illegally absorbed or illegally absorbed in disguised form public deposits shall be calculated based on the total amount of funds absorbed by the actor. The amount that has been repaid before or after the case is discovered may be taken into account as a sentencing circumstance.  
Where the illegally absorbed or illegally absorbed in disguised form public deposits are mainly used for normal production and business activities and the absorbed funds can be timely returned, the actor may be exempted from criminal punishment; if the circumstances are obviously minor, the act shall not be treated as a crime.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　以非法占有为目的，使用诈骗方法实施[本解释第二条](#art-2)规定所列行为的，应当依照[刑法第一百九十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-192)的规定，以集资诈骗罪定罪处罚。  
使用诈骗方法非法集资，具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为“以非法占有为目的”：  
（一）集资后不用于生产经营活动或者用于生产经营活动与筹集资金规模明显不成比例，致使集资款不能返还的；  
（二）肆意挥霍集资款，致使集资款不能返还的；  
（三）携带集资款逃匿的；  
（四）将集资款用于违法犯罪活动的；  
（五）抽逃、转移资金、隐匿财产，逃避返还资金的；  
（六）隐匿、销毁账目，或者搞假破产、假倒闭，逃避返还资金的；  
（七）拒不交代资金去向，逃避返还资金的；  
（八）其他可以认定非法占有目的的情形。  
集资诈骗罪中的非法占有目的，应当区分情形进行具体认定。行为人部分非法集资行为具有非法占有目的的，对该部分非法集资行为所涉集资款以集资诈骗罪定罪处罚；非法集资共同犯罪中部分行为人具有非法占有目的，其他行为人没有非法占有集资款的共同故意和行为的，对具有非法占有目的的行为人以集资诈骗罪定罪处罚。

**Article 4** Any person who, with the purpose of illegal possession, uses fraudulent methods to commit any of the acts listed in Article 2 of this Interpretation shall be convicted and punished for the crime of fund-raising fraud in accordance with Article 192 of the Criminal Law.  
Where fraudulent methods are used for illegal fund-raising and any of the following circumstances exists, the actor may be deemed to have "the purpose of illegal possession":  
(1) Not using the raised funds for production and business activities, or using them for such activities in a proportion that is obviously disproportionate to the scale of the raised funds, resulting in the inability to repay the raised funds;  
(2) Wantonly squandering the raised funds, resulting in the inability to repay them;  
(3) Absconding with the raised funds;  
(4) Using the raised funds for illegal or criminal activities;  
(5) Illegally withdrawing or transferring funds, concealing property, or evading the repayment of funds;  
(6) Concealing or destroying accounts, or engaging in false bankruptcy or false closure, to evade the repayment of funds;  
(7) Refusing to account for the whereabouts of the funds to evade repayment; or  
(8) Other circumstances that may establish the purpose of illegal possession.  
The purpose of illegal possession in the crime of fund-raising fraud shall be specifically determined based on the circumstances. Where part of the actor's illegal fund-raising acts is carried out with the purpose of illegal possession, the raised funds involved in that part shall be convicted and punished for the crime of fund-raising fraud; where, in a joint illegal fund-raising crime, some actors have the purpose of illegal possession while other actors do not have a common intent and conduct to possess the raised funds illegally, the actors with the purpose of illegal possession shall be convicted and punished for the crime of fund-raising fraud.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　个人进行集资诈骗，数额在10万元以上的，应当认定为“数额较大”；数额在30万元以上的，应当认定为“数额巨大”；数额在100万元以上的，应当认定为“数额特别巨大”。  
单位进行集资诈骗，数额在50万元以上的，应当认定为“数额较大”；数额在150万元以上的，应当认定为“数额巨大”；数额在500万元以上的，应当认定为“数额特别巨大”。  
集资诈骗的数额以行为人实际骗取的数额计算，案发前已归还的数额应予扣除。行为人为实施集资诈骗活动而支付的广告费、中介费、手续费、回扣，或者用于行贿、赠与等费用，不予扣除。行为人为实施集资诈骗活动而支付的利息，除本金未归还可予折抵本金以外，应当计入诈骗数额。

**Article 5** Where an individual commits fund-raising fraud, if the amount is not less than RMB 100,000 yuan, it shall be deemed as "a relatively large amount"; if not less than RMB 300,000 yuan, as "a huge amount"; and if not less than RMB 1,000,000 yuan, as "an especially huge amount".  
Where a unit commits fund-raising fraud, if the amount is not less than RMB 500,000 yuan, it shall be deemed as "a relatively large amount"; if not less than RMB 1,500,000 yuan, as "a huge amount"; and if not less than RMB 5,000,000 yuan, as "an especially huge amount".  
The amount of fund-raising fraud shall be calculated based on the amount actually defrauded by the actor, and the amount that has been repaid before the case is discovered shall be deducted. Advertising fees, intermediary fees, handling fees, rebates, or expenses used for bribery, gifts, etc. paid by the actor for carrying out the fund-raising fraud shall not be deducted. Interest paid by the actor for carrying out the fund-raising fraud shall be included in the fraudulent amount, except that if the principal has not been repaid, the interest may be offset against the principal.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　未经国家有关主管部门批准，向社会不特定对象发行、以转让股权等方式变相发行股票或者公司、企业债券，或者向特定对象发行、变相发行股票或者公司、企业债券累计超过200人的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-179)规定的“擅自发行股票、公司、企业债券”。构成犯罪的，以擅自发行股票、公司、企业债券罪定罪处罚。

**Article 6** Where, without approval from the relevant competent department of the state, stocks or corporate bonds are issued to unspecified members of the public or issued in disguised form by means of equity transfer, etc., or where stocks or corporate bonds are issued to specified objects or issued in disguised form to an aggregate of more than 200 persons, such conduct shall be deemed as "unauthorized issuance of stocks or corporate bonds" as prescribed in Article 179 of the Criminal Law. If a crime is constituted, the actor shall be convicted and punished for the crime of unauthorized issuance of stocks or corporate bonds.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　违反国家规定，未经依法核准擅自发行基金份额募集基金，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处罚。

**Article 7** Any person who, in violation of state regulations, issues fund shares without approval according to law to raise funds, if the circumstances are serious, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal business operations in accordance with Article 225 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　广告经营者、广告发布者违反国家规定，利用广告为非法集资活动相关的商品或者服务作虚假宣传，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第二百二十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-222)的规定，以虚假广告罪定罪处罚：  
（一）违法所得数额在10万元以上的；  
（二）造成严重危害后果或者恶劣社会影响的；  
（三）二年内利用广告作虚假宣传，受过行政处罚二次以上的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。  
明知他人从事欺诈发行股票、债券，非法吸收公众存款，擅自发行股票、债券，集资诈骗或者组织、领导传销活动等集资犯罪活动，为其提供广告等宣传的，以相关犯罪的共犯论处。

**Article 8** Any advertising operator or advertising publisher who, in violation of state regulations, uses advertisements to make false publicity for goods or services related to illegal fund-raising activities, and any of the following circumstances exists, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of false advertising in accordance with Article 222 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The amount of illegal gains is not less than RMB 100,000 yuan;  
(2) Causing serious harmful consequences or severe adverse social impact;  
(3) Having been subject to administrative penalties twice or more for using advertisements to make false publicity within the past two years; or  
(4) Other circumstances of serious nature.  
Where a person, knowing that another person is engaged in fund-raising criminal activities such as fraudulently issuing stocks or bonds, illegally absorbing public deposits, unauthorized issuance of stocks or bonds, fund-raising fraud, or organizing or leading pyramid schemes, provides advertising or other publicity for him/her, the person shall be punished as an accomplice to the relevant crime.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　此前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 9** In the event of any inconsistency between the previously issued judicial interpretations and this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
