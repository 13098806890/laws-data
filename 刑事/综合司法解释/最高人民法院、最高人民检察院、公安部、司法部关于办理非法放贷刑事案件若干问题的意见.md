# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、司法部关于办理非法放贷刑事案件若干问题的意见

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：0000-00-00  
**生效日期**：2019-10-21

> 第一条 违反国家规定，未经监管部门批准，或者超越经营范围，以营利为目的，经常性地向社会不特定对象发放贷款，扰乱金融市场秩序，情节严重的，依照刑法第二百二十五条第（四）项的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处罚。 前款规定中的“经常性地向社会不特定对象发放贷款”，是指2年内向不特定多人（包括单位和个人）以借款或其他名义出借资金10次以上。 贷款到期后延长还款期限的，发放贷款次数按照1次计算。 第二条 以超过36%的实际年利率实施符合本意见第一条规定的非法放贷行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于刑法第二百二十五条规定的“情节严重”，但单次非法放贷行为实际年利率未超过36%的，定罪量刑时不得计入： （一）个人非法放贷数额累计在200万元以上的，单位非法放贷数额累计在1000万元以上的； （二）个人违法所得数额累计在80万元以上的，单位违法所得数额累计在400万元以上的； （三）个人非法放贷对象累计在50人以上的，单位非法放贷对象累计在150人以上的； （四）造成借款人或者其近亲属自杀、死亡或者精神失常等严重后果的。 具有下列情形之一的，属于刑法第二百二十五条规定的“情节特别严重”： （一）个人非法放贷数额累计在1000万元以上的，单位非法放贷数额累计在5000万元以上的； （二）个人违法所得数额累计在400万元以上的，单位违法所得数额累计在2000万元以上的； （三）个人非法放贷对象累计在250人以上的，单位非法放贷对象累计在750人以上的； （四）造成多名借款人或者其近亲属自杀、死亡或者精神失常等特别严重后果的。 第三条 非法放贷数额、违法所得数额、非法放贷对象数量接近本意见第二条规定的“情节严重”“情节特别严重”的数额、数量起点标准，并具有下列情形之一的，可以分别认定为“情节严重”“情节特别严重”： （一）2年内因实施非法放贷行为受过行政处罚2次以上的；

### 正文

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 以超过36%的实际年利率实施符合本意见第一条规定的非法放贷行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)规定的“情节严重”，但单次非法放贷行为实际年利率未超过36%的，定罪量刑时不得计入：  
（一）个人非法放贷数额累计在200万元以上的，单位非法放贷数额累计在1000万元以上的；  
（二）个人违法所得数额累计在80万元以上的，单位违法所得数额累计在400万元以上的；  
（三）个人非法放贷对象累计在50人以上的，单位非法放贷对象累计在150人以上的；  
（四）造成借款人或者其近亲属自杀、死亡或者精神失常等严重后果的。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）个人非法放贷数额累计在1000万元以上的，单位非法放贷数额累计在5000万元以上的；  
（二）个人违法所得数额累计在400万元以上的，单位违法所得数额累计在2000万元以上的；  
（三）个人非法放贷对象累计在250人以上的，单位非法放贷对象累计在750人以上的；  
（四）造成多名借款人或者其近亲属自杀、死亡或者精神失常等特别严重后果的。

**Article 2** Where an act of illegal lending in accordance with Article 1 of this Opinion is committed at an actual annual interest rate exceeding 36%, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall constitute a "serious circumstance" as provided in Article 225 of the Criminal Law; provided that, if the actual annual interest rate of a single illegal lending act does not exceed 36%, such act shall not be counted in conviction and sentencing:  
(1) the total amount of illegal lending by an individual reaches or exceeds 2 million yuan, or by a unit reaches or exceeds 10 million yuan;  
(2) the total amount of illegal gains by an individual reaches or exceeds 800,000 yuan, or by a unit reaches or exceeds 4 million yuan;  
(3) the cumulative number of recipients of illegal lending by an individual reaches or exceeds 50 persons, or by a unit reaches or exceeds 150 persons;  
(4) causing serious consequences such as suicide, death, or mental disorder of the borrower or his/her close relatives.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, it shall constitute a "particularly serious circumstance" as provided in Article 225 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the total amount of illegal lending by an individual reaches or exceeds 10 million yuan, or by a unit reaches or exceeds 50 million yuan;  
(2) the total amount of illegal gains by an individual reaches or exceeds 4 million yuan, or by a unit reaches or exceeds 20 million yuan;  
(3) the cumulative number of recipients of illegal lending by an individual reaches or exceeds 250 persons, or by a unit reaches or exceeds 750 persons;  
(4) causing particularly serious consequences such as suicide, death, or mental disorder of multiple borrowers or their close relatives.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 非法放贷数额、违法所得数额、非法放贷对象数量接近本意见第二条规定的“情节严重”“情节特别严重”的数额、数量起点标准，并具有下列情形之一的，可以分别认定为“情节严重”“情节特别严重”：  
（一）2年内因实施非法放贷行为受过行政处罚2次以上的；  
（二）以超过72%的实际年利率实施非法放贷行为10次以上的。  
前款规定中的“接近”，一般应当掌握在相应数额、数量标准的80%以上。

**Article 3** Where the amount of illegal lending, the amount of illegal gains, or the number of recipients of illegal lending is close to the threshold standards of amount or quantity for "serious circumstance" or "particularly serious circumstance" as specified in Article 2 of this Opinion, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it may be determined as a "serious circumstance" or "particularly serious circumstance", respectively:  
(1) having been subjected to administrative penalties two times or more for committing illegal lending acts within two years;  
(2) committing illegal lending acts at an actual annual interest rate exceeding 72% for ten times or more.  
The term "close" in the preceding paragraph shall generally be understood as reaching or exceeding 80% of the corresponding amount or quantity standards.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 仅向亲友、单位内部人员等特定对象出借资金，不得适用本意见第一条的规定定罪处罚。但具有下列情形之一的，定罪量刑时应当与向不特定对象非法放贷的行为一并处理：  
（一）通过亲友、单位内部人员等特定对象向不特定对象发放贷款的；  
（二）以发放贷款为目的，将社会人员吸收为单位内部人员，并向其发放贷款的；  
（三）向社会公开宣传，同时向不特定多人和亲友、单位内部人员等特定对象发放贷款的。

**Article 4** Lending funds only to specific persons such as relatives, friends, or internal personnel of a unit shall not be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 1 of this Opinion. However, under any of the following circumstances, such acts shall be handled together with the act of illegal lending to unspecified persons in conviction and sentencing:  
(1) lending funds to unspecified persons through specific persons such as relatives, friends, or internal personnel of a unit;  
(2) for the purpose of lending funds, absorbing social persons as internal personnel of a unit and lending funds to them;  
(3) publicly advertising to the society while simultaneously lending funds to both unspecified multiple persons and specific persons such as relatives, friends, or internal personnel of a unit.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 非法放贷数额应当以实际出借给借款人的本金金额认定。非法放贷行为人以介绍费、咨询费、管理费、逾期利息、违约金等名义和以从本金中预先扣除等方式收取利息的，相关数额在计算实际年利率时均应计入。  
非法放贷行为人实际收取的除本金之外的全部财物，均应计入违法所得。  
非法放贷行为未经处理的，非法放贷次数和数额、违法所得数额、非法放贷对象数量等应当累计计算。

**Article 5** The amount of illegal lending shall be determined based on the actual principal amount lent to the borrower. Where the illegal lender collects interest under the names of referral fees, consulting fees, management fees, overdue interest, liquidated damages, or by deducting in advance from the principal, the relevant amounts shall all be included in calculating the actual annual interest rate.  
All property actually collected by the illegal lender other than the principal shall be included in the illegal gains.  
If the illegal lending act has not been subjected to any punishment, the number and amount of illegal lending, the amount of illegal gains, and the number of recipients of illegal lending shall be calculated cumulatively.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 为从事非法放贷活动，实施擅自设立金融机构、套取金融机构资金高利转贷、骗取贷款、非法吸收公众存款等行为，构成犯罪的，应当择一重罪处罚。  
为强行索要因非法放贷而产生的债务，实施故意杀人、故意伤害、非法拘禁、故意毁坏财物、寻衅滋事等行为，构成犯罪的，应当数罪并罚。  
纠集、指使、雇佣他人采用滋扰、纠缠、哄闹、聚众造势等手段强行索要债务，尚不单独构成犯罪，但实施非法放贷行为已构成非法经营罪的，应当按照非法经营罪的规定酌情从重处罚。  
以上规定的情形，刑法、司法解释另有规定的除外。

**Article 6** Where, for the purpose of engaging in illegal lending activities, acts such as establishing a financial institution without authorization, obtaining funds from a financial institution for high-interest relending, obtaining loans by deception, or illegally absorbing public deposits are committed and constitute a crime, the offender shall be punished for the most serious crime.  
Where, to forcibly collect debts arising from illegal lending, acts such as intentional homicide, intentional injury, illegal detention, intentional destruction of property, or creating disturbances are committed and constitute a crime, the offender shall be punished for multiple crimes in combination.  
Where a person assembles, instructs, or hires others to forcibly collect debts by means of harassment, pestering, uproar, or gathering a crowd to create momentum, and such acts do not independently constitute a crime, but the act of illegal lending has already constituted the crime of illegal business operation, the offender shall be given a heavier punishment within the discretion according to the provisions on the crime of illegal business operation.  
Except as otherwise provided by the Criminal Law or judicial interpretations, the above provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 有组织地非法放贷，同时又有其他违法犯罪活动，符合黑社会性质组织或者恶势力、恶势力犯罪集团认定标准的，应当分别按照黑社会性质组织或者恶势力、恶势力犯罪集团侦查、起诉、审判。  
黑恶势力非法放贷的，据以认定“情节严重”“情节特别严重”的非法放贷数额、违法所得数额、非法放贷对象数量起点标准，可以分别按照本意见第二条规定中相应数额、数量标准的50%确定；同时具有本意见第三条第一款规定情形的，可以分别按照相应数额、数量标准的40%确定。

**Article 7** Where illegal lending is organized and involves other illegal or criminal activities, and meets the criteria for determination as a mafia-style organization, an evil force, or an evil force criminal group, investigation, prosecution, and adjudication shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions on mafia-style organizations, evil forces, or evil force criminal groups, respectively.  
Where illegal lending is committed by a mafia-style organization or evil force, the threshold standards for the amount of illegal lending, the amount of illegal gains, and the number of recipients of illegal lending used to determine a "serious circumstance" or "particularly serious circumstance" may be determined as 50% of the corresponding amount or quantity standards specified in Article 2 of this Opinion, respectively; and where the circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of Article 3 of this Opinion also exist, such thresholds may be determined as 40% of the corresponding amount or quantity standards, respectively.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 本意见自2019年10月21日起施行。对于本意见施行前发生的非法放贷行为，依照最高人民法院《关于准确理解和适用刑法中“国家规定”的有关问题的通知》（法发〔2011〕155号）的规定办理。

**Article 8** This Opinion shall come into force as of October 21, 2019. For acts of illegal lending occurring before the coming into force of this Opinion, they shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Notice of the Supreme People's Court on Issues Concerning the Accurate Understanding and Application of "State Provisions" in the Criminal Law (Fa Fa [2011] No. 155).
