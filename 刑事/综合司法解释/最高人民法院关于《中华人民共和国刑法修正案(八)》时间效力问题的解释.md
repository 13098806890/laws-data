# 最高人民法院关于《中华人民共和国刑法修正案(八)》时间效力问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2011〕9号  
**公布日期**：2011-04-25  
**生效日期**：2011-05-01

> 最高人民法院《关于〈中华人民共和国刑法修正案（八）〉时间效力问题的解释》已于2011年4月20日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1519次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年5月1日起施行。 2011年4月25日 最高人民法院 关于《中华人民共和国刑法修正案（八）》 时间效力问题的解释 法释〔2011〕9号 （2011年4月20日最高人民法院审判委员会第1519次会议通过　2011年4月25日最高人民法院公告公布　自2011年5月1日起施行）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　对于2011年4月30日以前犯罪，依法应当判处管制或者宣告缓刑的，人民法院根据犯罪情况，认为确有必要同时禁止犯罪分子在管制期间或者缓刑考验期内从事特定活动，进入特定区域、场所，接触特定人的，适用修正后[刑法第三十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-38)第二款或者第七十二条第二款的规定。  
犯罪分子在管制期间或者缓刑考验期内，违反人民法院判决中的禁止令的，适用修正后[刑法第三十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-38)第四款或者第七十七条第二款的规定。

**Article 1** For crimes committed before April 30, 2011, where public surveillance shall be imposed or probation shall be declared according to law, if the People's Court, based on the circumstances of the crime, deems it truly necessary to simultaneously prohibit the criminal from engaging in specific activities, entering specific areas or premises, or contacting specific persons during the period of public surveillance or the probation test period, the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 38 or paragraph 2 of Article 72 of the amended Criminal Law shall apply.  
Where a criminal violates the restraining order in the judgment of the People's Court during the period of public surveillance or the probation test period, the provisions of paragraph 4 of Article 38 or paragraph 2 of Article 77 of the amended Criminal Law shall apply.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　2011年4月30日以前犯罪，判处死刑缓期执行的，适用修正前[刑法第五十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-50)的规定。  
被告人具有累犯情节，或者所犯之罪是故意杀人、强奸、抢劫、绑架、放火、爆炸、投放危险物质或者有组织的暴力性犯罪，罪行极其严重，根据修正前刑法判处死刑缓期执行不能体现罪刑相适应原则，而根据修正后刑法判处死刑缓期执行同时决定限制减刑可以罚当其罪的，适用修正后[刑法第五十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-50)第二款的规定。

**Article 2** For crimes committed before April 30, 2011, where a death sentence with a two-year reprieve is imposed, the provisions of Article 50 of the pre-amendment Criminal Law shall apply.  
Where the defendant has a recidivist circumstance, or the crime committed is intentional homicide, rape, robbery, kidnapping, arson, explosion, releasing dangerous substances, or organized violent crime, and the crime is extremely serious, and imposing a death sentence with a two-year reprieve under the pre-amendment Criminal Law would not reflect the principle of proportionality between crime and punishment, whereas imposing a death sentence with a two-year reprieve and concurrently deciding on restricting commutation under the amended Criminal Law could make the punishment fit the crime, the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 50 of the amended Criminal Law shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　被判处有期徒刑以上刑罚，刑罚执行完毕或者赦免以后，在2011年4月30日以前再犯应当判处有期徒刑以上刑罚之罪的，是否构成累犯，适用修正前[刑法第六十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-65)的规定；但是，前罪实施时不满十八周岁的，是否构成累犯，适用修正后[刑法第六十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-65)的规定。  
曾犯危害国家安全犯罪，刑罚执行完毕或者赦免以后，在2011年4月30日以前再犯危害国家安全犯罪的，是否构成累犯，适用修正前[刑法第六十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-66)的规定。  
曾被判处有期徒刑以上刑罚，或者曾犯危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪、黑社会性质的组织犯罪，在2011年5月1日以后再犯罪的，是否构成累犯，适用修正后[刑法第六十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-65)、第六十六条的规定。

**Article 3** For persons who have been sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment or a heavier punishment, after the completion of execution or remission of the punishment, and who commit another crime that shall be punished with fixed-term imprisonment or a heavier punishment before April 30, 2011, whether it constitutes recidivism shall be determined by applying the provisions of Article 65 of the pre-amendment Criminal Law; provided that if the person was under the age of 18 when committing the previous crime, whether it constitutes recidivism shall be determined by applying the provisions of Article 65 of the amended Criminal Law.  
Where a person previously committed a crime of endangering national security, and after the completion of execution or remission of the punishment, commits another crime of endangering national security before April 30, 2011, whether it constitutes recidivism shall be determined by applying the provisions of Article 66 of the pre-amendment Criminal Law.  
Where a person has been sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment or a heavier punishment, or has previously committed a crime of endangering national security, a crime of terrorist activities, or a crime of a mafia-type organization, and commits another crime after May 1, 2011, whether it constitutes recidivism shall be determined by applying the provisions of Articles 65 and 66 of the amended Criminal Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　2011年4月30日以前犯罪，虽不具有自首情节，但是如实供述自己罪行的，适用修正后[刑法第六十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-67)第三款的规定。

**Article 4** For crimes committed before April 30, 2011, where the person does not have the circumstance of voluntary surrender but truthfully confesses his or her own crime, the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 67 of the amended Criminal Law shall apply.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　2011年4月30日以前犯罪，犯罪后自首又有重大立功表现的，适用修正前[刑法第六十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-68)第二款的规定。

**Article 5** For crimes committed before April 30, 2011, where the person voluntarily surrenders after committing the crime and also has major meritorious service, the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 68 of the pre-amendment Criminal Law shall apply.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　2011年4月30日以前一人犯数罪，应当数罪并罚的，适用修正前[刑法第六十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-69)的规定；2011年4月30日前后一人犯数罪，其中一罪发生在2011年5月1日以后的，适用修正后[刑法第六十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-69)的规定。

**Article 6** Where a person commits multiple crimes before April 30, 2011, and shall be punished for multiple crimes concurrently, the provisions of Article 69 of the pre-amendment Criminal Law shall apply; where a person commits multiple crimes both before and after April 30, 2011, and one of the crimes occurs after May 1, 2011, the provisions of Article 69 of the amended Criminal Law shall apply.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　2011年4月30日以前犯罪，被判处无期徒刑的罪犯，减刑以后或者假释前实际执行的刑期，适用修正前[刑法第七十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-78)第二款、第八十一条第一款的规定。

**Article 7** For criminals who commit crimes before April 30, 2011 and are sentenced to life imprisonment, the actual term of imprisonment served after commutation or before parole shall be governed by the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 78 and paragraph 1 of Article 81 of the pre-amendment Criminal Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　2011年4月30日以前犯罪，因具有累犯情节或者系故意杀人、强奸、抢劫、绑架、放火、爆炸、投放危险物质或者有组织的暴力性犯罪并被判处十年以上有期徒刑、无期徒刑的犯罪分子，2011年5月1日以后仍在服刑的，能否假释，适用修正前[刑法第八十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-81)第二款的规定；2011年4月30日以前犯罪，因其他暴力性犯罪被判处十年以上有期徒刑、无期徒刑的犯罪分子，2011年5月1日以后仍在服刑的，能否假释，适用修正后[刑法第八十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-81)第二款、第三款的规定。

**Article 8** For criminals who commit crimes before April 30, 2011, who, due to having the recidivist circumstance or committing intentional homicide, rape, robbery, kidnapping, arson, explosion, releasing dangerous substances, or organized violent crime and are sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment, and who are still serving their sentences after May 1, 2011, whether they may be granted parole shall be determined by applying the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 81 of the pre-amendment Criminal Law; for criminals who commit crimes before April 30, 2011, who are sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment due to other violent crimes, and who are still serving their sentences after May 1, 2011, whether they may be granted parole shall be determined by applying the provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 81 of the amended Criminal Law.
