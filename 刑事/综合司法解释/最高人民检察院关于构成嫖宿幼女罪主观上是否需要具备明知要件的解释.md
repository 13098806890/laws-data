2001年6月11日  
关于构成嫖宿幼女罪  
主观上是否需要具备明知要件的解释  
（2001年6月4日最高人民检察院第九届检察委员会  
第八十九次会议通过）  
高检发释字〔2001〕3号  
为依法办理嫖宿幼女犯罪案件，对嫖宿幼女行为如何适用法律问题解释如下：  
行为人知道被害人是或者可能是不满十四周岁幼女而嫖宿的，适用[刑法第三百六十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-360)第二款的规定，以嫖宿幼女罪追究刑事责任。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Procuratorate  
June 11, 2001  
Interpretation on Whether the Subjective Element of Knowledge Is Required for the Crime of Whoring with a Minor Female Under the Age of Fourteen  
(Adopted at the 89th Session of the Ninth Procuratorial Committee of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on June 4, 2001)  
Gao Jian Fa Shi Zi [2001] No. 3  
For the purpose of handling criminal cases of whoring with a minor female under the age of fourteen according to law, the following interpretation is made on the application of law to the act of whoring with a minor female under the age of fourteen:  
Where an actor, knowing that the victim is or may be a minor female under the age of fourteen, engages in whoring with her, the provisions of Paragraph 2 of Article 360 of the Criminal Law shall apply, and criminal liability shall be investigated for the crime of whoring with a minor female under the age of fourteen.
