# 旅馆业治安管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2022-03-29  
**生效日期**：2022-05-01

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障旅馆业的正常经营和旅客的生命财物安全，维护社会治安，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated to ensure the normal operation of the hotel industry and the safety of life and property of guests, and to maintain public order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　凡经营接待旅客住宿的旅馆、饭店、宾馆、招待所、客货栈、车马店、浴池等（以下统称旅馆），不论是国营、集体经营，还是合伙经营、个体经营、外商投资经营，不论是专营还是兼营，不论是常年经营，还是季节性经营，都必须遵守本办法。

**Article 2** All hotels, restaurants, guesthouses, hostels, inns, lodging houses, bathhouses, etc. that operate to accommodate guests (hereinafter collectively referred to as "hotels"), whether state-owned, collectively-owned, partnership, individually-owned, or foreign-invested, whether specialized or part-time, whether operating year-round or seasonally, must comply with these Measures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　开办旅馆，要具备必要的防盗等安全设施。

**Article 3** To establish a hotel, it shall be equipped with necessary security facilities such as anti-theft devices.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　申请开办旅馆，应取得市场监管部门核发的营业执照，向当地公安机关申领特种行业许可证后，方准开业。  
经批准开业的旅馆，如有歇业、转业、合并、迁移、改变名称等情况，应当在市场监管部门办理变更登记后3日内，向当地的县、市公安局、公安分局备案。

**Article 4** An applicant for establishing a hotel shall obtain a business license issued by the market supervision and administration department, and apply to the local public security organ for a special industry permit before being permitted to commence business.  
Where a hotel approved to operate undergoes suspension, change of business, merger, relocation, change of name, or other such circumstances, it shall, within three days after completing the change registration with the market supervision and administration department, file a record with the local county or city public security bureau or public security sub-bureau.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　经营旅馆，必须遵守国家的法律，建立各项安全管理制度，设置治安保卫组织或者指定安全保卫人员。

**Article 5** Operating a hotel must comply with the laws of the State, establish various safety management systems, and set up a public security defense organization or designate security personnel.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　旅馆接待旅客住宿必须登记。登记时，应当查验旅客的身份证件，按规定的项目如实登记。  
接待境外旅客住宿，还应当在24小时内向当地公安机关报送住宿登记表。

**Article 6** Hotels must register guests for accommodation. When registering, they shall check the guest's identification documents and truthfully register according to the prescribed items.  
When accommodating overseas guests, they shall also submit the accommodation registration form to the local public security organ within 24 hours.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　旅馆应当设置旅客财物保管箱、柜或者保管室、保险柜，指定专人负责保管工作。对旅客寄存的财物，要建立登记、领取和交接制度。

**Article 7** Hotels shall set up safes, cabinets, or storage rooms, and strongboxes for the custody of guests' property, and designate specific personnel to be responsible for the custody. For property deposited by guests, a system of registration, retrieval, and handover shall be established.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　旅馆对旅客遗留的物品，应当妥为保管，设法归还原主或揭示招领；经招领3个月后无人认领的，要登记造册，送当地公安机关按拾遗物品处理。对违禁物品和可疑物品，应当及时报告公安机关处理。

**Article 8** Hotels shall properly keep items left behind by guests, and shall endeavor to return them to the owners or post a notice for claiming; if no one claims them after three months of notice, the items shall be registered and listed, and sent to the local public security organ for disposal as lost property. Contraband items and suspicious items shall be promptly reported to the public security organ for handling.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　旅馆工作人员发现违法犯罪分子，行迹可疑的人员和被公安机关通缉的罪犯，应当立即向当地公安机关报告，不得知情不报或隐瞒包庇。

**Article 9** If hotel staff discover lawbreakers, persons of suspicious behavior, or criminals wanted by public security organs, they shall immediately report to the local public security organ, and shall not fail to report with knowledge or conceal or shield them.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在旅馆内开办舞厅、音乐茶座等娱乐、服务场所的，除执行本办法有关规定外，还应当按照国家和当地政府的有关规定管理。

**Article 10** Where dance halls, music tea houses, or other entertainment and service venues are established within a hotel, in addition to implementing the relevant provisions of these Measures, they shall also be managed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and local governments.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　严禁旅客将易燃、易爆、剧毒、腐蚀性和放射性等危险物品带入旅馆。

**Article 11** Guests are strictly prohibited from bringing flammable, explosive, highly toxic, corrosive, radioactive, or other dangerous articles into hotels.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　旅馆内，严禁卖淫、嫖宿、赌博、吸毒、传播淫秽物品等违法犯罪活动。

**Article 12** Prostitution, whoring, gambling, drug abuse, dissemination of pornographic materials, and other illegal and criminal activities are strictly prohibited within hotels.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　旅馆内，不得酗酒滋事、大声喧哗，影响他人休息，旅客不得私自留客住宿或者转让床位。

**Article 13** Within hotels, it shall not be permitted to engage in drunken brawling, make loud noises, or disturb others' rest; guests shall not privately keep visitors for overnight accommodation or transfer beds.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　公安机关对旅馆治安管理的职责是，指导、监督旅馆建立各项安全管理制度和落实安全防范措施，协助旅馆对工作人员进行安全业务知识的培训，依法惩办侵犯旅馆和旅客合法权益的违法犯罪分子。  
公安人员到旅馆执行公务时，应当出示证件，严格依法办事，要文明礼貌待人，维护旅馆的正常经营和旅客的合法权益。旅馆工作人员和旅客应当予以协助。

**Article 14** The responsibilities of public security organs regarding the public security administration of hotels are: to guide and supervise hotels in establishing various safety management systems and implementing safety precautionary measures; to assist hotels in training staff on safety business knowledge; and to punish according to law those who violate the lawful rights and interests of hotels and guests.  
When public security personnel go to hotels to perform official duties, they shall present their credentials, strictly act according to law, treat people with civility and politeness, and safeguard the normal operation of hotels and the lawful rights and interests of guests. Hotel staff and guests shall provide assistance.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　违反[本办法第四条](#art-4)规定开办旅馆的，公安机关可以酌情给予警告或者处以200元以下罚款；未经登记，私自开业的，公安机关应当协助工商行政管理部门依法处理。

**Article 15** Where a hotel is established in violation of Article 4 of these Measures, the public security organ may, as it deems appropriate, give a warning or impose a fine of not more than 200 yuan; where a hotel commences business without registration, the public security organ shall assist the administrative department for industry and commerce in handling the matter according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　旅馆工作人员违反[本办法第九条](#art-9)规定的，公安机关可以酌情给予警告或者处以200元以下罚款；情节严重构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
旅馆负责人参与违法犯罪活动，其所经营的旅馆已成为犯罪活动场所的，公安机关除依法追究其责任外，对该旅馆还应当会同工商行政管理部门依法处理。

**Article 16** Where hotel staff violate the provisions of Article 9 of these Measures, the public security organ may, as it deems appropriate, give a warning or impose a fine of not more than 200 yuan; if the circumstances are serious enough to constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where the person in charge of a hotel participates in illegal or criminal activities and the hotel operated by him has become a venue for criminal activities, the public security organ shall, in addition to investigating his liability according to law, also handle the hotel jointly with the administrative department for industry and commerce according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　违反本办法第六、十一、十二条规定的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》有关条款的规定，处罚有关人员；发生重大事故、造成严重后果构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 17** Where the provisions of Articles 6, 11, and 12 of these Measures are violated, the relevant persons shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China; if a major accident occurs, causing serious consequences and constituting a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　当事人对公安机关的行政处罚决定不服的，按照[《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》第一百零二条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法.md#art-102)的规定办理。

**Article 18** If a party is dissatisfied with the decision on administrative penalty made by the public security organ, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 102 of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　省、自治区、直辖市公安厅（局）可根据本办法制定实施细则，报请当地人民政府批准后施行，并报公安部备案。

**Article 19** The public security departments (bureaus) of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with these Measures, formulate implementation rules, which shall come into force after being submitted to and approved by the local people's government, and shall be filed with the Ministry of Public Security for record.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本办法自公布之日起施行。1951年8月15日公布的《城市旅栈业暂行管理规则》同时废止。

**Article 20** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Interim Rules for the Administration of Urban Lodging Industry promulgated on August 15, 1951 shall be abolished simultaneously.
